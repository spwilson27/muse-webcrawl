Teodoro Obiang Nguema Mbasogo

Born: 5 June 1942, Acoacán, Spanish Guinea

Office: President of Equatorial Guinea (since 1979)

Party: Democratic Party of Equatorial Guinea

Teodoro Obiang Nguema Mbasogo (born 5 June 1942) is an Equatoguinean politician, former military officer and dictator who has served as the 2nd President of Equatorial Guinea since 1979. He is the longest-serving president of any country ever and the second-longest consecutively serving current non-royal national leader in the world.

Born into the Fang ethnic group, Obiang went to several schools before joining the military and being trained at the Zaragoza Military Academy in Spain. He held various positions under the colonial regime and the post-independence government of his uncle, Francisco Macías Nguema, including director of the notorious Black Beach Prison. He ousted Macías in a military coup on 3 August 1979, had him tried and executed, and assumed the presidency.

Presidency

Obiang's regime initially promised reforms, but he retained authoritarian control. A new constitution was adopted in 1982, and he was re-elected in single-candidate and later multi-party elections widely criticised by international observers as fraudulent. He founded the Democratic Party of Equatorial Guinea (PDGE) in 1987.

Under Obiang, Equatorial Guinea became a significant oil producer after offshore reserves were discovered in the 1990s, giving the country one of the highest per-capita GDP figures in Africa. Critics, however, note that the wealth has been concentrated among the ruling elite while most of the population remains in poverty, and the regime has been accused of corruption, human rights abuses and repression of the opposition and press.

Coup attempts and succession

In March 2004, a group of mercenaries was arrested in Zimbabwe and Equatorial Guinea in connection with an alleged plot to overthrow Obiang — an episode widely known as the "Wonga coup". Another attempted coup was reported in December 2017. Obiang's son, Teodoro Nguema Obiang Mangue, serves as Vice President and is widely regarded as a possible successor; he has been the subject of international corruption investigations and asset seizures abroad.
