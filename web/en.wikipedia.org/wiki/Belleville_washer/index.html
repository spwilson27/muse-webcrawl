Belleville washer
Type: Disc spring
Material: Steel, stainless steel

A Belleville washer, also known as a coned-disc spring, disc spring or Belleville spring, is a type of spring in the shape of a frusto-conical disc that can be loaded in the axial direction and acts as a spring.

Design

The disc is slightly conical, so when flattened it exerts a large force over a short deflection. Washers can be stacked in series to increase deflection or in parallel to increase load.

Applications

They are used as fasteners, in clutches, pressure relief valves, and to maintain tension in bolted joints subject to thermal expansion or vibration.

History

The type is named after Julien Belleville, who patented a disc-spring design in France in the 19th century.
