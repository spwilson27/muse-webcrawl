Elon Musk

Elon Reeve Musk (born June 28, 1971) is a businessman, investor, and engineer known for his leadership of SpaceX, Tesla, Inc., and X Corp. (formerly Twitter). He is the wealthiest individual in the world, with an estimated net worth, as of 2025, in the hundreds of billions of US dollars.

Musk was born in Pretoria, South Africa, and briefly attended the University of Pretoria before immigrating to Canada at age 18, acquiring citizenship through his Canadian-born mother. Two years later, he matriculated at Queen's University in Kingston, Ontario, then transferred to the University of Pennsylvania, earning bachelor's degrees in economics and physics. He moved to California in 1995 to pursue a PhD in applied physics at Stanford University, but dropped out after two days.

Early career

With his brother Kimbal, Musk co-founded the online city guide software company Zip2 in 1995. The startup was acquired by Compaq for $307 million in 1999. That same year, Musk co-founded X.com, an online bank. X.com merged with Confinity in 2000 to form PayPal. In 2002, eBay acquired PayPal for $1.5 billion in stock, of which Musk — the largest shareholder with 11.72% of shares — received $175.8 million.

SpaceX

In 2002, Musk founded Space Exploration Technologies Corp., commonly referred to as SpaceX. Its goal is to reduce space transportation costs to enable the colonization of Mars. SpaceX developed the Falcon rocket family and the Dragon spacecraft family, and in 2008 became the first privately developed liquid-fuel rocket to reach orbit with Falcon 1. SpaceX has since supplied the International Space Station under NASA contracts and developed the partially reusable Falcon 9 and Falcon Heavy launch vehicles, as well as the Starlink satellite internet constellation and the Starship vehicle.

Tesla

Musk joined Tesla Motors, Inc. (now Tesla, Inc.) in 2004 as the chairman of the board of directors after investing $6.5 million during the company's Series A round of investment. He became chief executive officer in 2008 and also served as product architect. Tesla built the Roadster sports car, then the Model S sedan, Model X SUV, Model 3 sedan, Model Y crossover, and the Cybertruck pickup truck, becoming the world's most valuable carmaker by market capitalization.

Other ventures

Musk co-founded OpenAI in 2015 as a nonprofit artificial intelligence research laboratory, leaving its board in 2018; in 2023 he founded the AI company xAI. He co-founded Neuralink in 2016, a neurotechnology company developing implantable brain–computer interfaces, and founded The Boring Company in 2017, a tunnel construction company. In 2022, he acquired the social media service Twitter for $44 billion, later renaming it X. In 2025, Musk served as a senior advisor to US President Donald Trump and the de facto head of the Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE), a role he left later that year.

Views and public image

Musk is known for his outspoken social media presence and controversial statements on topics including artificial intelligence, public transport, and politics, and his political activities and views have made him a polarizing figure.
