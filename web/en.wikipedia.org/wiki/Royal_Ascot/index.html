Royal Ascot

Royal Ascot is a five-day flat horse racing meeting held each June at Ascot Racecourse in Berkshire, England. Founded by Queen Anne in 1711, it is one of Britain's most prestigious race meetings and a centrepiece of the British social season, closely associated with the royal family.

The meeting

The meeting features eight Group One races, including the Gold Cup (established 1807), the Queen Anne Stakes, the King's Stand Stakes and the St James's Palace Stakes. Each day begins with the Royal Procession, in which the monarch and guests arrive by horse-drawn landau along the straight mile. Prize money is among the highest in British racing, totalling around £10 million in recent years.

Dress code

Royal Ascot is famous for its strict dress code — morning dress and hats in the Royal Enclosure — and for extravagant headwear, particularly on the Thursday, traditionally known as Ladies' Day.
