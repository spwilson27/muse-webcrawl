Districts of Israel

The six administrative districts of Israel.

Israel is divided into six main administrative districts (mehozot): Central, Haifa, Jerusalem, Northern, Southern and Tel Aviv. The districts are used for planning, statistics and the administration of government services such as the district offices of the Ministry of the Interior, district courts and district health offices. A seventh, the Judea and Samaria Area, is used for statistical purposes for Israeli settlements in the West Bank.

Sub-districts

The districts are further divided into fifteen sub-districts (nafot), which are in turn divided into natural regions. The Southern District is by far the largest by land area, covering much of the Negev desert, while the Tel Aviv District is the smallest but one of the most populous. The Jerusalem District includes the city of Jerusalem; the Central District, centred on Ramla and Rehovot, contains much of the country's population and industry.

Administration

Unlike federal systems, the districts have no elected regional government; they are administrative arms of the central government, each headed by a district commissioner appointed by the Minister of the Interior. Local government is exercised by municipalities, local councils and regional councils within the districts.
