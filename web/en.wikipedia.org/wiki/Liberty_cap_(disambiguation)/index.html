Liberty cap (disambiguation)

Liberty cap may refer to:

  • Liberty cap or Phrygian cap, a soft conical cap with the apex bent forward, worn in antiquity by freed slaves and later adopted as a symbol of liberty, notably during the American and French Revolutions.
  • Liberty cap, a common name for Psilocybe semilanceata, a species of psilocybin mushroom named for its resemblance to the cap.
  • Liberty Cap, a granite dome in Yosemite National Park, California.
  • Liberty Cap, a thermal feature in the Mammoth Hot Springs area of Yellowstone National Park.
