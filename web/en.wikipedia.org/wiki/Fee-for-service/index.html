Fee-for-service

Fee-for-service (FFS) is a payment model where services are unbundled and paid for separately. In health care, it gives providers an incentive to provide more treatments because payment is dependent on the quantity of care, rather than quality of care.

In health care

Under FFS, each test, procedure and visit generates a separate fee, whether paid out of pocket or reimbursed by an insurer. Fee-for-service is the dominant model in United States health care but is widely criticized for encouraging overutilization and contributing to high costs.

Alternatives

Alternative models include capitation (a fixed payment per patient), bundled payments, salaries, and value-based purchasing, under programs such as Medicare's shared savings arrangements. Fee-for-service is also used more generally in professional services such as law and consulting, contrasted with retainer or subscription pricing.
