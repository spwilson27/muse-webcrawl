The 2022 Atlantic hurricane season ran officially from 1 June to 30 November 2022. Although it produced a near-average number of named storms, it was one of the costliest seasons on record, principally because of Hurricane Ian in Florida.

Season summary

Fourteen named storms formed; eight became hurricanes and two — Fiona and Ian — reached major hurricane status. The season had an unusual lull in August, with no named storms between early July and September for the first time in decades, before activity surged in September.

Major storms

Hurricane Fiona caused catastrophic flooding in Puerto Rico in September and became the costliest and most intense storm ever recorded in Atlantic Canada. Hurricane Ian made landfall in southwest Florida near Cayo Costa on 28 September as a Category 4 hurricane, driving a devastating storm surge into Fort Myers Beach and Sanibel Island; with more than 150 deaths and roughly $113 billion in damage, it was the costliest hurricane in Florida's history. Hurricane Nicole struck Florida in November, an unusually late landfall.

Retirement

The names Fiona and Ian were retired after the season and replaced by Farrah and Idris for 2028.
