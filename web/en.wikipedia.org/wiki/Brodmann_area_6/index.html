Brodmann area 6

Brodmann area 6
Part of: frontal lobe
Components: premotor cortex, supplementary motor area

Brodmann area 6 is a region of the cerebral cortex in the frontal lobe, located anterior to the primary motor cortex (Brodmann area 4). It encompasses the premotor cortex laterally and the supplementary motor area medially, and is involved in the planning and coordination of movement.

Function

Area 6 neurons activate during the preparation of movements, in response to sensory cues, and during motor imagery and action observation. The ventral premotor region contains mirror neurons in primates. Stimulation of area 6 evokes coordinated movements of multiple joints rather than the simple twitches produced by area 4.

Subdivisions

The area is commonly divided into lateral premotor cortex (further split into dorsal and ventral parts) and the medial supplementary motor complex, each with distinct connections to the primary motor cortex, parietal cortex and spinal cord.
