TelevisaUnivision

TelevisaUnivision is a Mexican-American media company headquartered in Mexico City and New York City, formed on January 31, 2022, by the merger of the content and media assets of Mexico's Grupo Televisa with the United States–based Univision Communications. It is the world's largest producer of Spanish-language content.

Assets

The company operates the Univision and UniMás networks in the United States, the Las Estrellas and other broadcast channels in Mexico, numerous cable channels including TUDN for sports, and the ViX streaming service, launched in 2022 as the largest Spanish-language streaming platform. Televisa retains a major ownership stake and its chairman Emilio Azcárraga Jean has been a central figure in the company's leadership.

Programming

TelevisaUnivision is known for telenovelas, news (Noticias Univision and N+), sports coverage including Liga MX and international football, and variety programming for Spanish-speaking audiences in the Americas.
