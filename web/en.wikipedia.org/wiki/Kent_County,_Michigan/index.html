Kent County is a county in the western Lower Peninsula of the U.S. state of Michigan. Its county seat and largest city is Grand Rapids. As of the 2020 census the population was about 658,000, making it Michigan's fourth-most populous county.

History

The county was named after New York jurist James Kent. It was set off in 1831 and organised in 1836, growing around fur trading and later the furniture industry centred in Grand Rapids.

Geography and communities

The Grand River crosses the county. Besides Grand Rapids, cities include Wyoming, Kentwood, Walker and Grandville. Gerald R. Ford International Airport serves the region.
