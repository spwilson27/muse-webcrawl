List of Chinese administrative divisions by population

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This article lists the first-level administrative divisions of the People's Republic of China — its provinces, autonomous regions, direct-administered municipalities, and special administrative regions — ranked by population, based principally on the Seventh National Population Census of 2020.

Largest divisions

China has 34 claimed province-level divisions, of which the PRC administers 33 including Taiwan's administration being disputed. Guangdong, in the south, surpassed 126 million residents in 2020 and is the most populous province-level division, followed by Shandong and Henan, both above 98 million. The least populous is the Tibet Autonomous Region, with about 3.6 million.

Notes

The four direct-administered municipalities (Beijing, Tianjin, Shanghai, and Chongqing) govern large urban areas as province-level units; Chongqing, a vast municipality of about 32 million people, ranks among the top ten despite containing extensive rural territory. The special administrative regions of Hong Kong (about 7.5 million) and Macao (about 0.7 million) rank near the bottom of the list.
