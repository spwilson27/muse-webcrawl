The England cricket team represents England and Wales in international cricket and is administered by the England and Wales Cricket Board (ECB). It is a Full Member of the International Cricket Council and competes in Test, One Day International, and Twenty20 International cricket.

History

England played in the first ever Test match, against Australia at Melbourne in March 1877, and the rivalry between the two sides became The Ashes from 1882. For much of the game's history England was administered by the Marylebone Cricket Club, and its touring teams were known as the MCC until the later 20th century.

Honours

England won the Cricket World Cup for the first time in 2019, defeating New Zealand in a celebrated final at Lord's decided on boundary count after a tied match and Super Over. The team also won the ICC World Twenty20 in 2010 and again (as the T20 World Cup) in 2022, becoming the first men's side to hold both white-ball world titles simultaneously.
