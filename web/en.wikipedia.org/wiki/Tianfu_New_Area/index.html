Tianfu New Area

Tianfu New Area (Chinese: 天府新区) is a state-level new area in Sichuan Province, China, centred on the southern expansion of the provincial capital Chengdu. Approved by the State Council in October 2014, it is one of China's national-level new areas intended to drive development in the western interior.

Geography and development

The new area covers a planned area of roughly 1,500 square kilometres spanning parts of Chengdu and neighbouring Meishan, including districts such as Shuangliu and Longquanyi and counties such as Pengshan. Major projects include the Tianfu Software Park, the Chengdu Science City around Xinglong Lake, and Chengdu Tianfu International Airport, opened in 2021.

Economy

The area targets information technology, high-end manufacturing, biomedicine, and research institutions, and serves as a core of the Chengdu–Chongqing economic circle strategy.
