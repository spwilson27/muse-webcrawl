General Electric GE9X

The General Electric GE9X is a high-bypass turbofan aircraft engine developed by GE Aerospace exclusively for the Boeing 777X family. It is the largest and most powerful commercial jet engine, with a fan diameter of 128 inches (3.25 m) and a rated thrust of about 105,000 pounds-force in service; a test engine reached 134,300 lbf, a Guinness World Record.

Derived from the GE90 and GEnx, the GE9X uses a composite fan case and fan blades, ceramic matrix composites in the hot section, and 3D-printed parts including fuel nozzles. It first ran in 2016 and first flew under the wing of GE's 747 testbed in 2018, offering roughly 10% better fuel efficiency than the GE90-115B.
