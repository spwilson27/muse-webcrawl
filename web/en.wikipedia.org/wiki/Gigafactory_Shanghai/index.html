Gigafactory Shanghai

Location: Lingang, Pudong, Shanghai, China
Owner: Tesla, Inc.
Opened: 2019
Products: Model 3, Model Y

Gigafactory Shanghai (Chinese: 特斯拉上海超級工廠), also known as Giga Shanghai, is an electric vehicle manufacturing plant operated by Tesla, Inc. in the Lingang New Area of Pudong, Shanghai, China. It was Tesla's first factory outside the United States and the first car plant in China wholly owned by a foreign automaker.

History

Tesla signed an agreement with the Shanghai municipal government in July 2018, taking advantage of China's decision to lift foreign ownership caps for new-energy vehicle makers. Construction began in January 2019 on a site of roughly 86 hectares (210 acres), and the first Chinese-built Model 3 sedans were delivered to employees in December 2019 — less than a year after groundbreaking, a pace widely remarked upon as "Tesla speed". The second phase, building the Model Y crossover, followed in 2020–2021.

Production

The plant produces the Tesla Model 3 and Model Y for the Chinese market and serves as Tesla's principal export hub, shipping vehicles to Europe, Asia-Pacific and other markets. Annual installed capacity has been reported at more than 750,000 vehicles, and the factory passed cumulative production milestones of one million vehicles in 2022 and two million in 2023. It was Tesla's highest-volume plant in the early 2020s.

Megafactory

In 2023 Tesla announced an adjacent "Megafactory" for production of Megapack grid-scale battery storage units, its first such plant outside the United States; construction began in 2024 and production started in early 2025.
