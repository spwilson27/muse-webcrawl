Farmers' Almanac

The Farmers' Almanac is an annual American periodical that has been published continuously since 1818. It is known for its long-range weather predictions, astronomical data, planting charts, and a mixture of folklore, trivia, and advice.

History

Founded by Robert Bailey Thomas in 1818, the almanac is published in Lewiston, Maine. Thomas devised a secret formula for weather prediction, said to be kept locked away and passed from editor to editor; the publication claims the formula combines sunspot activity, lunar cycles, and other factors.

Content

Typical contents include calendars, best days for activities such as fishing and planting, tide tables, recipes, and articles on gardening and natural remedies. It is distinct from the older Old Farmer's Almanac (founded 1792).
