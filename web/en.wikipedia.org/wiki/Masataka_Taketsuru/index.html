Masataka Taketsuru (June 20, 1894 – August 29, 1979) was a Japanese chemist and businessman regarded as the father of Japan's whisky industry. He founded Nikka Whisky and established its Yoichi and Miyagikyo distilleries.

Training in Scotland

Taketsuru travelled to Scotland in 1918, studying organic chemistry at the University of Glasgow and apprenticing at distilleries including Longmorn and Hazelburn. He married Jessie Roberta 'Rita' Cowan in 1920 and returned to Japan with detailed notes on Scotch whisky production.

Suntory and Nikka

He helped build Japan's first whisky distillery at Yamazaki for Shinjiro Torii in 1923–24, then left in 1934 to found his own company in Yoichi, Hokkaido, chosen for a climate resembling Scotland's. Nikka grew into one of Japan's two great whisky houses.
