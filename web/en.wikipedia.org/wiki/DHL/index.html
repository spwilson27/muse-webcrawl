DHL is a German logistics company and a division of the DHL Group (formerly Deutsche Post DHL Group). Founded in the United States in 1969, it pioneered international air express delivery and is now one of the world's largest logistics brands, delivering well over a billion parcels a year.

History

DHL was founded in San Francisco by Adrian Dalsey, Larry Hillblom and Robert Lynn — whose surnames form the initials DHL — to ferry shipping documents between San Francisco and Honolulu, allowing cargo to be customs-cleared before ships arrived. The company expanded rapidly across the Pacific and then worldwide, becoming a pioneer of the international express industry and a rival to FedEx and UPS. Deutsche Post began acquiring shares in 1998 and took full ownership in 2002, making DHL the brand for its express, freight and supply-chain operations.

Operations

The brand covers several divisions: DHL Express (international courier and parcel), DHL Global Forwarding (air and ocean freight), DHL Freight (European road freight), DHL Supply Chain (contract logistics) and DHL eCommerce. Its major air hubs include Leipzig/Halle in Germany, Cincinnati/Northern Kentucky in the United States and Hong Kong in Asia.

Sponsorship

DHL is a prominent sponsor in sport, including Formula One, MotoGP, rugby and football, and its red-and-yellow livery is widely recognised. In 2023 Deutsche Post DHL Group renamed itself DHL Group, reflecting the dominance of the brand.
