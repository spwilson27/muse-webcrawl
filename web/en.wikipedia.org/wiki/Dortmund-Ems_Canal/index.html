Dortmund–Ems Canal

The Dortmund–Ems Canal is a 269-kilometre canal in Germany connecting the Ruhr industrial region at Dortmund with the North Sea port of Emden via Münster and the Ems river.

History

Opened in 1899 under Kaiser Wilhelm II, the canal let Ruhr coal and steel reach the sea without Dutch waterways. The Henrichenburg boat lift of 1899 became a landmark of industrial heritage.

Route

Branches link it to the Rhine via the Rhine–Herne Canal and to the Mittelland Canal. Modernised locks and bridges carry barge traffic through North Rhine-Westphalia and Lower Saxony.
