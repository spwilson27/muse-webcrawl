Davor Šuker (born 1 January 1968) is a Croatian former professional footballer who played as a striker. He was the top scorer of the 1998 FIFA World Cup, winning the Golden Boot as Croatia finished third in their first World Cup appearance, and he remains Croatia's all-time top goalscorer with 45 goals.

Club career

Šuker began his career at his hometown club NK Osijek before moving to Dinamo Zagreb in 1989. In 1991 he joined Spanish side Sevilla, where he became one of La Liga's most prolific forwards. In 1996 he signed for Real Madrid, winning the La Liga title in 1996–97 and the UEFA Champions League in 1998. He later played for Arsenal and West Ham United in England before ending his career with 1860 Munich in Germany.

International career

Šuker played for Yugoslavia at the 1990 World Cup and then became the focal point of the newly independent Croatian national team. At the 1998 World Cup in France he scored six goals, including the winner against the Netherlands in the third-place play-off, earning both the Golden Boot and the Silver Ball.

Later life

After retiring, Šuker founded a football academy in Zagreb and served as president of the Croatian Football Federation from 2012 to 2021.
