Bulacan, officially the Province of Bulacan, is a province in the Philippines located in the Central Luzon region, immediately north of Metro Manila. Its capital is the city of Malolos. Bulacan is one of the country's most populous and industrialized provinces, and it is especially rich in Philippine history.

History

Bulacan played a central role in the Philippine Revolution against Spain: the Republic of Kakarong was proclaimed in 1897, the Pact of Biak-na-Bato was signed there, and in 1898–99 Malolos hosted the revolutionary Congress that drafted the Malolos Constitution and inaugurated the First Philippine Republic at Barasoain Church.

Geography and economy

The province comprises 21 municipalities and three component cities (Malolos, Meycauayan, and San Jose del Monte). Its economy combines agriculture (rice and vegetables), jewellery making in Meycauayan, fireworks production centred in Bocaue, leather goods, and growing industrial estates. The planned New Manila International Airport in Bulakan is one of the Philippines' largest infrastructure projects.

Culture

Bulacan is known as a cradle of Filipino culture, home to heroes such as Marcelo H. del Pilar and Francisco Balagtas, the Obando Fertility Rites, and celebrated cuisine and sweets.
