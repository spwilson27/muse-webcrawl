Online dating service

An online dating service is a company or platform that provides services enabling individuals to find and introduce themselves to prospective partners over the Internet, usually with the goal of developing personal, romantic, or sexual relationships. Such services typically use websites or mobile applications and rely on searchable databases of personal profiles.

A typical online dating profile interface

History

Computerised matchmaking predates the public Internet: services such as Operation Match at Harvard in 1965 used questionnaires and mainframe computers to pair users. The modern online dating industry began in the mid-1990s with sites such as Match.com (founded 1995) and Kiss.com. The launch of eHarmony in 2000 popularised compatibility-based matching using long questionnaires, while free, user-driven sites such as OkCupid (2004) and Plenty of Fish grew rapidly in the following decade.

The release of location-based smartphone applications transformed the market. Grindr, launched in 2009, pioneered geolocation-based dating for gay men, and Tinder (2012) popularised the "swipe" mechanic of mutual interest. Subsequent apps such as Bumble (2014), which requires women to initiate contact in heterosexual matches, and Hinge further diversified the market.

Mechanisms

Services generally require users to create a profile with photographs, biographical details and stated preferences. Matching may be driven by user searches and filters, by proprietary compatibility algorithms, or by mutual-selection mechanics in which two users must each express interest before messaging is enabled. Revenue models include subscriptions, one-off fees for premium features, and advertising.

Criticism and safety

Online dating has been criticised for deceptive profiles, "catfishing", romance scams, and the collection of sensitive personal data. Studies have examined effects on self-esteem and the tendency toward superficial judgement based on photographs. At the same time, research indicates that couples who meet online are now among the most common ways relationships begin in many Western countries.
