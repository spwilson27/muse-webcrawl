Pace (transit)

Pace Suburban Bus
Founded: 1983
Headquarters: Arlington Heights, Illinois
Service area: Suburban Chicago, Illinois
Parent: Regional Transportation Authority

Pace is the suburban bus division of the Regional Transportation Authority (RTA) in the Chicago metropolitan area. Created under the RTA Act of 1983 and beginning operations in 1984, it provides fixed-route bus, paratransit and vanpool services across the suburbs of Cook County and the collar counties of northeastern Illinois.

Services

Pace operates several hundred fixed routes, many connecting with Metra commuter rail stations and CTA rapid transit terminals. It also runs ADA paratransit service for the suburbs (and, by contract, for Chicago), dial-a-ride services for municipalities, and a large vanpool programme. Its bus rapid transit service, Pulse, launched on Milwaukee Avenue in 2019.

Organisation

Pace is governed by a board of directors appointed from the suburban areas it serves, and is headquartered in Arlington Heights, Illinois. It operates multiple garages ("divisions") across the region and carries tens of millions of passengers a year.
