Commander Shepard is the player character and protagonist of the Mass Effect video game trilogy, developed by BioWare. Shepard is a veteran soldier of the Systems Alliance Navy, an N7 special-forces operative, and the first human Spectre, tasked with stopping the rogue Spectre Saren Arterius and, ultimately, the galaxy-wide threat of the Reapers.

Characterisation

Players customise Shepard's first name, gender, appearance, military background, and psychological profile before play, and shape the character's personality through dialogue and moral choices along "Paragon" (heroic) and "Renegade" (ruthless) paths. The male Shepard is voiced by Mark Meer and the female Shepard by Jennifer Hale; the default male appearance was modelled on Dutch model Mark Vanderloo. Shepard's given rank and backstory (such as surviving the massacre on Akuze, or service at the Skyllian Blitz) are selected at creation and referenced throughout the series.

Role in the series

In Mass Effect (2007) Shepard uncovers the Reaper threat; in Mass Effect 2 (2010) Shepard is killed, resurrected by the pro-human organisation Cerberus, and recruits a team for a "suicide mission" against the Collectors; in Mass Effect 3 (2012) Shepard unites the galaxy's species for a final stand against the Reapers. The character's fate is a major focus of the trilogy's much-discussed ending.

Reception

Shepard has been widely listed among the greatest video game characters, with particular praise for Hale's performance and for the character's role as a customisable yet coherent protagonist. The fan abbreviation "FemShep" for the female version became prominent enough that BioWare featured her prominently in marketing for Mass Effect 3.
