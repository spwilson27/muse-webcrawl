Maggi

Maggi is an international brand of seasonings, instant soups, bouillon cubes and noodles, founded in Switzerland in 1884 by Julius Maggi and owned by Nestlé since 1947. Its best-known product is Maggi seasoning sauce, a dark, savoury liquid with a flavour compared to soy sauce, sold in a distinctive bottle. In many countries the name Maggi is virtually synonymous with instant noodles.

History

Julius Maggi, a Swiss miller and entrepreneur, developed legume-based soups and seasonings in the 1880s to provide cheap, quick nutrition for working families. His company launched ready-made soups in 1886 and the famous seasoning sauce the same year; the bouillon cube followed in 1908. Branches and factories spread across Europe and beyond.

Products and reach

Maggi products are tailored to local tastes: Maggi noodles dominate the instant noodle market in India, Maggi cubes are a staple seasoning in West African cooking, and the sauce is ubiquitous in German-speaking households. Nestlé markets the brand in more than a hundred countries.
