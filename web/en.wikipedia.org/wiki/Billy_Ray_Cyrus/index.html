Billy Ray Cyrus (born August 25, 1961) is an American country singer, songwriter, and actor. He achieved stardom in 1992 with his debut album Some Gave All and its lead single "Achy Breaky Heart", which topped the country charts, became a crossover pop hit, and popularised line dancing around the world. More than 25 years later, his featured appearance on the remix of Lil Nas X's "Old Town Road" (2019) gave him the longest-running number-one single in Billboard Hot 100 history at the time, spending 19 weeks at the top.

Early life

Cyrus was born in Flatwoods, Kentucky, the son of Ron Cyrus, a steelworker who became a Kentucky state legislator, and Ruth Ann Cyrus. Surrounded by bluegrass and gospel music, he began singing young and later played in a band called Sly Dog while working various jobs before signing with Mercury Records.

Music career

Some Gave All (1992) debuted at number one on the Billboard 200 and sold more than nine million copies in the United States, driven by "Achy Breaky Heart". Follow-up albums such as It Won't Be the Last (1993) and Storm in the Heartland (1994) sustained his country career through the decade, and later albums included Home at Last (2007). The "Old Town Road" remix earned him two Grammy Awards in 2020, including Best Pop Duo/Group Performance and Best Music Video.

Acting career

Cyrus starred as the lead in the television drama Doc (2001–2004) and played Robby Ray Stewart, the father of his real-life daughter Miley Cyrus's character, on the Disney Channel series Hannah Montana (2006–2011). He later starred in the CMT sitcom Still the King (2016–2017).

Personal life

Cyrus was married to Cindy Smith and later to Tish Cyrus, with whom he has three children, including singer and actress Miley Cyrus and singer Noah Cyrus; he also adopted Tish's two children, including singer Trace Cyrus. He married Australian singer Firerose in 2023; the marriage ended in divorce in 2024.
