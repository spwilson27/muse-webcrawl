Babymetal

Babymetal (stylised in all caps) is a Japanese kawaii metal band formed in Tokyo in 2010 by producer Key Kobayashi ("Kobametal") as a sub-unit of the idol group Sakura Gakuin. The band fuses Japanese idol pop vocals and choreography with heavy metal instrumentation, performed live by the backing "Kami Band". Babymetal became the first Japanese act to headline Wembley Arena and has brought J-pop idol culture to metal festival audiences worldwide.

History

The group's debut album Babymetal (2014), containing the viral song "Gimme Chocolate!!", was followed by Metal Resistance (2016), which charted internationally and accompanied a world tour ending at the Tokyo Dome. After Yuimetal's departure in 2018, the band continued as a duo and released Metal Galaxy (2019), which topped the Billboard Top Rock Albums chart — a first for an Asian act. In 2023 Momoko Okazaki, formerly a rotating support dancer, became the official third member, and the band released the concept album The Other One, followed by Metal Forth (2025), featuring collaborations with artists including Electric Callboy, Poppy and Tom Morello.

Musical style

Babymetal's songs mix death and thrash metal riffing, blast beats and breakdowns with idol-pop melodies, electronic dance elements and choreographed dance, performed in fox-god ("kitsune") themed stage mythology. Their signature hand gesture is the kitsune sign rather than the traditional metal horns.
