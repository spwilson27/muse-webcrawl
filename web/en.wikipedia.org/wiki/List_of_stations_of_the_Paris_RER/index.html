List of stations of the Paris RER

The RER (Réseau Express Régional) is the commuter rail network serving Paris and its Île-de-France suburbs. Its five lines (A, B, C, D and E) serve about 257 stations over some 587 km of track. This article lists the stations by line; the busiest, such as Châtelet–Les Halles and Gare du Nord, are among the largest underground stations in the world.
