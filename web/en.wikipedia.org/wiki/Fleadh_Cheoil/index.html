The Fleadh Cheoil (Irish for "festival of music") is an Irish music festival and competition run by Comhaltas Ceoltóirí Éireann (CCÉ). The full title of the premier event is Fleadh Cheoil na hÉireann, the All-Ireland Fleadh. It is the world's largest festival of traditional Irish music, drawing hundreds of thousands of visitors.

Structure

Competitions are held at county and provincial level before the All-Ireland finals. Musicians compete in categories by instrument — including fiddle, tin whistle, flute, uilleann pipes, button accordion, banjo and harp — and by age group, as well as in céilí band and grúpaí ceoil (group) competitions.

History

The first national festival of traditional Irish music was held in Mullingar in 1951. It has since been hosted by towns across Ireland and, in 2016 and 2023, drew record crowds in Ennis and Mullingar. In 2024 the fleadh was held in Wexford, and Belfast was selected to host in 2026.
