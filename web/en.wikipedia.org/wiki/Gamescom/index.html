Gamescom (stylized as gamescom) is a trade fair for video games held annually at the Koelnmesse in Cologne, Germany. By attendance and exhibition space it is the world's largest gaming event, drawing several hundred thousand visitors. First held in 2009 as the successor to Leipzig's Games Convention, it combines public exhibition halls — where players try forthcoming games — with a business area, the Devcom developer conference, cosplay and esports events, and the Opening Night Live showcase hosted by Geoff Keighley.

Gamescom 2024 attracted about 335,000 visitors from over 120 countries and more than 1,400 exhibitors. Sister events include Gamescom Asia in Singapore and Gamescom Latam in São Paulo.
