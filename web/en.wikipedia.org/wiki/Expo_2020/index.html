Expo 2020

Expo 2020 (officially Expo 2020 Dubai) was a World Expo hosted by Dubai, United Arab Emirates, from 1 October 2021 to 31 March 2022. Originally scheduled for 2020–21, it was postponed due to the COVID-19 pandemic but retained the "2020" name. It was the first World Expo held in the Middle East, Africa and South Asia region.

The theme was "Connecting Minds, Creating the Future", with sub-themes of Opportunity, Mobility and Sustainability. The site, in the Dubai South district, was organised around the central Al Wasl Plaza with its vast projection dome. More than 190 countries participated, and the event recorded about 24 million visits.

After closing, the site was redeveloped as Expo City Dubai, retaining many pavilions and the UAE pavilion designed by Santiago Calatrava.
