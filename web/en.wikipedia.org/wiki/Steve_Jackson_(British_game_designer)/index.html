Steve Jackson (British game designer)

Born: May 20, 1951, Manchester, England

Occupation: Game designer, author

Known for: Games Workshop, Fighting Fantasy

Steve Jackson (born 20 May 1951) is a British game designer, writer, and video game industry figure. He co-founded Games Workshop in 1975 with Ian Livingstone and John Peake, and together with Livingstone created the Fighting Fantasy series of interactive gamebooks, beginning with The Warlock of Firetop Mountain (1982).

Jackson is frequently confused with the American game designer of the same name, Steve Jackson, founder of Steve Jackson Games; the two are unrelated. The American Jackson contributed a book to the Fighting Fantasy series, and the books carry disambiguating notes.

Career

Jackson began his career as a journalist before founding Games Workshop, which grew from importing and selling board games into a major publisher and retailer, securing the European rights to Dungeons & Dragons and later creating the Warhammer franchise.

As an author, Jackson wrote several Fighting Fantasy gamebooks, including Scorpion Swamp, Demons of the Deep, and the four-book Sorcery! epic (1983–85), notable for its spell system. He later worked in video games, co-founding Lionhead Studios with Peter Molyneux, and was appointed a professor at Brunel University, teaching game design.
