Randal Edward Brecker (born 27 November 1945) is an American trumpeter, flugelhornist, and composer in the jazz, jazz fusion, and funk idioms. The elder brother of saxophonist Michael Brecker, he is a seven-time Grammy winner and one of the most recorded session brass players in popular music.

Biography

Brecker grew up in a musical family in the Philadelphia area and studied at Indiana University before moving to New York in the mid-1960s. His early work included stints with Blood, Sweat & Tears, Horace Silver, and Art Blakey.

The Brecker Brothers

In 1975 he and Michael formed the Brecker Brothers, a jazz-funk band whose albums such as Back to Back (1976) and Don't Stop the Music (1977) became fusion landmarks. The band won Grammy Awards for the 1990s reunions Out of the Loop and Return of the Brecker Brothers.

Session work and solo career

As a session musician Brecker has appeared on hundreds of albums, including work with Steely Dan, Bruce Springsteen, Dire Straits, Parliament-Funkadelic, and Frank Sinatra. His solo albums include Score (1969), In the Idiom (1986), and Randy in Brazil (2008), which won the Grammy for Best Contemporary Jazz Album.
