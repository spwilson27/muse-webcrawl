Amadeus IT Group

Amadeus IT Group, S.A. is a Spanish multinational technology company that provides software and distribution services for the travel and tourism industry. Headquartered in Madrid, it operates one of the world's largest global distribution systems, linking airlines, hotels, rail operators, travel agencies and other suppliers with buyers of travel.

History

Amadeus was founded in 1987 by Air France, Lufthansa, Iberia and SAS as a European alternative to existing computer reservation systems. Its network grew through the 1990s as airlines moved from proprietary terminals to shared distribution platforms. The company later expanded into airline passenger service systems, airport operations, hospitality software and payments.

Business

The group's best known product family is the Amadeus Altéa suite, used by many airlines for reservations, inventory and departure control. Amadeus also supplies search, pricing, ticketing, disruption management and data analytics tools. Its scale makes it a central piece of infrastructure in commercial aviation, even though most travellers interact with it indirectly.
