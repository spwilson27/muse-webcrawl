Overview

Novi Sad is the second-largest city in Serbia and the capital of the autonomous province of Vojvodina, on the banks of the Danube. Founded in 1694 by Serb merchants across the river from the Habsburg fortress of Petrovaradin, it became known as the "Serbian Athens" for its cultural role in the 19th century.

Modern city

Petrovaradin Fortress hosts the EXIT music festival, founded in 2000 out of student protests, now one of Europe's largest festivals. In November 2024 the collapse of the railway station canopy killed 16 people, sparking sustained nationwide anti-corruption protests in Serbia. Novi Sad was a European Capital of Culture in 2022.
