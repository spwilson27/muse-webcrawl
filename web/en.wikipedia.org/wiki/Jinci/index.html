Jinci

Jinci (Chinese: 晋祠; pinyin: Jìncí, "Jin Ancestral Temple") is a historic temple complex and major tourist attraction located about 25 kilometres southwest of Taiyuan, the capital of Shanxi Province, China, at the foot of Xuanweng Mountain by the source of the Jin River.

History

The temple was established to commemorate Shu Yu, a son of King Wu of Zhou who was enfeoffed with the state of Tang, the predecessor of Jin, and his mother Yi Jiang. Buildings were added over many dynasties, creating a celebrated ensemble of temple architecture, gardens, and sculpture.

Notable features

The Hall of the Holy Mother (Shengmu Dian), built between 1023 and 1032 during the Song dynasty, is one of the oldest large timber structures in China; it houses 43 painted Song-dynasty clay statues of the Holy Mother and her attendants, regarded as masterpieces of Chinese sculpture. Other treasures include the Flying Bridge (Yuzhao Feiliang) over the spring-fed pond — a rare early stone cross-shaped bridge — the ever-flowing Nanlao Spring, ancient cypresses said to date from the Zhou dynasty, and iron statues from the Song period.
