List of Paris Métro stations

The Paris Métro is the rapid transit system of Paris, France, operated by the RATP. It comprises 16 lines and more than 300 stations, making it one of the densest metro networks in the world. The first line opened on 19 July 1900 for the Exposition Universelle. Below is a selection of stations by line; the full article lists every station alphabetically with its line connections, location and opening date.
