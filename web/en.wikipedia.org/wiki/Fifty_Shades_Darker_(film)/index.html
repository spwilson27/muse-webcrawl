Fifty Shades Darker is a 2017 American erotic romantic drama film directed by James Foley and written by Niall Leonard, based on the 2012 novel of the same name by E. L. James. It is the second instalment in the Fifty Shades film series, following Fifty Shades of Grey (2015) and preceding Fifty Shades Freed (2018). The film stars Dakota Johnson and Jamie Dornan as Anastasia Steele and Christian Grey.

Plot

After Anastasia Steele leaves Christian Grey, she begins work at a Seattle publishing house. Christian attempts to win her back and proposes a new arrangement without rules or punishments; as they rebuild their relationship, figures from Christian's past — including his former submissive Leila and Elena Lincoln, the woman who introduced him to his lifestyle — threaten the couple.

Production and release

The film was shot back-to-back with its sequel in Paris and Vancouver in 2016. It premiered in Hamburg on 7 February 2017 and was released in the United States on 10 February 2017 by Universal Pictures.

Reception

Like its predecessor, the film received generally negative reviews from critics, who criticised its script, acting, and narrative, though it was a major commercial success, grossing over $381 million worldwide against a $55 million budget.
