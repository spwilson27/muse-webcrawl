List of highest-grossing concert tours

Concert touring
The Eras Tour by Taylor Swift is the highest-grossing tour of all time, exceeding $2 billion.

This is a list of the highest-grossing concert tours of all time, based on reported box-office grosses, principally from Billboard Boxscore and Pollstar. Grosses are not adjusted for inflation unless noted.
