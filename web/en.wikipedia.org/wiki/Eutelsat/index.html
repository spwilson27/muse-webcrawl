Eutelsat Communications S.A. is a French satellite operator and one of the world's leading providers of satellite communications capacity. Originally founded in 1977 as an intergovernmental organisation — the European Telecommunications Satellite Organization — to develop a satellite infrastructure for Europe, it was privatised in 2001.

Operations

Eutelsat operates a fleet of geostationary satellites, including the well-known Hot Bird constellation at 13° East, a prime orbital position for television broadcasting to Europe, North Africa and the Middle East. Its services include video broadcasting to millions of cable and DTH households, broadband connectivity, maritime and in-flight connectivity, and data and government services. The company also offers high-throughput satellites such as KONNECT VHTS for broadband.

OneWeb merger

In 2023 Eutelsat completed its merger with the low Earth orbit constellation operator OneWeb, creating the Eutelsat Group — the first combined GEO–LEO satellite operator, positioning the group to compete in the growing market for low-latency satellite connectivity alongside constellations such as Starlink.
