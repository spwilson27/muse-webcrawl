Mediaset

Mediaset S.p.A., doing business as MFE – MediaForEurope, is an Italian mass media and television production and distribution company that is the largest commercial broadcaster in Italy and Spain. Founded in 1987 by Silvio Berlusconi as Fininvest's television arm, it is controlled by the Berlusconi family through the Fininvest holding company.

Operations

In Italy Mediaset operates the three national commercial channels Canale 5, Italia 1 and Rete 4, along with numerous free thematic digital channels and the streaming service Mediaset Infinity. In Spain it owns Telecinco and Cuatro. Advertising is sold through Publitalia '80 in Italy. Mediaset also has major film interests through Medusa Film.

Corporate history

Berlusconi built his television empire in the late 1970s and 1980s, taking advantage of the liberalisation of Italian broadcasting. Mediaset was carved out of Fininvest and listed on the Milan stock exchange in 1996. In 2021 the parent company Mediaset N.V. moved its legal seat to the Netherlands and adopted the name MediaForEurope (MFE), pursuing a pan-European strategy that included building a major stake in Germany's ProSiebenSat.1.
