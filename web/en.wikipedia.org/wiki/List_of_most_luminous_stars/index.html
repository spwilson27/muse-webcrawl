List of most luminous stars

The list of most luminous stars ranks stars by their bolometric luminosity — the total amount of energy they radiate per second across all wavelengths — expressed in units of the solar luminosity (L☉). The list is headed by extremely massive, short-lived stars such as Wolf–Rayet stars, luminous blue variables and rare hypergiants, most of which lie in the Large Magellanic Cloud or in dense star-forming regions of the Milky Way.


Some of the most luminous stars known, compared with the Sun.

Luminosity estimates for these objects carry large uncertainties, because most are heavily obscured by dust and lie at great distances. Historically the record was held by stars such as LBV 1806-20 and the Pistol Star, but modern surveys of the Magellanic Clouds have pushed the R136 cluster in the Tarantula Nebula to the top of the list.
