Rosso corsa

Rosso corsa (Italian for "racing red") is the traditional national racing colour of Italy. Cars entered by Italian teams in international motor racing were painted red, in accordance with the colour scheme assigned to nations in the early 20th century — alongside British racing green, French blue and German silver/white.

History

The custom dates to the Gordon Bennett Cup races of the 1900s, when colours were allocated by nationality. Red became inseparably associated with Italian marques such as Alfa Romeo, Maserati and above all Ferrari, whose Formula One cars have worn rosso corsa (in varying shades) throughout the team's history. The 1990s saw Ferrari shift to a brighter, more orange-red shade for television visibility before returning to deeper tones.

Usage

Rosso corsa remains the most popular colour for Ferrari road cars and a prestige colour for Italian sports cars generally. Its colour values are approximately hex #D40000.
