Edward Elric is a fictional character and the protagonist of Hiromu Arakawa's manga series Fullmetal Alchemist. Edward, titled the "Fullmetal Alchemist", is the youngest State Alchemist in the history of the fictional country of Amestris.

Character

Having lost his right arm and left leg in a failed attempt to resurrect his dead mother through alchemy—and having sacrificed his arm to bind his brother Alphonse's soul to a suit of armour—Edward uses steel prostheses called automail. Together with Alphonse, he searches for the Philosopher's Stone to restore their bodies.

Reception

Edward is consistently ranked among the most popular anime characters, praised for his determination, intelligence and sensitivity about his short stature, a running gag in the series.
