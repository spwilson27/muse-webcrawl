Geofencing

Geofencing is a location-based technology that creates a virtual geographic boundary (a geofence) around a real-world area, using GPS, RFID, Wi-Fi or cellular data. Software can then trigger an action — a notification, alert or automation — when a tracked device enters or exits the boundary.

Applications

Common uses include location-based marketing and advertising, fleet and asset tracking, parental and workforce monitoring, drone no-fly restrictions, and smart-home automation such as adjusting thermostats when residents arrive. Geofencing also raises privacy and legal questions, including its use in "geofence warrants" served on technology companies for device data near crime scenes.
