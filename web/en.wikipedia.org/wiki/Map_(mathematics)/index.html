Map (mathematics)

In mathematics, a map or mapping is a function, especially in contexts where the term emphasises a geometric or structural transformation, or where "function" is reserved for a specific type. The words are often used interchangeably.

Usage

"Map" is favoured in many branches: a linear map in algebra, a continuous map in topology, a smooth map in differential geometry, and a conformal map in complex analysis. In category theory, maps generalise to morphisms between objects. Mapping also denotes the process of associating each element of a domain with an element of a codomain, written x ↦ f(x).
