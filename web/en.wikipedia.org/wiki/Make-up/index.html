Make-up is an alternative spelling and common synonym for cosmetics: substances or products used to enhance or alter the appearance of the body, particularly the face. This article is a summary of cosmetics as commonly called make-up.

Types of make-up

  • Foundation and concealer — even out skin tone and cover blemishes.
  • Powder, blush and bronzer — set the base and add colour and contour.
  • Eye make-up — eyeshadow, eyeliner, mascara and eyebrow products.
  • Lip products — lipstick, lip gloss, lip liner and lip balm.
  • Nail polish — lacquers for fingernails and toenails.

History

Cosmetics have been used for thousands of years: ancient Egyptians lined their eyes with kohl, and Romans used chalk and lead-based powders (later recognised as poisonous). Make-up became a mass commercial industry in the 20th century, pioneered by entrepreneurs such as Max Factor, Elizabeth Arden and Helena Rubinstein, and spread through cinema, television and, more recently, social-media beauty culture.

Uses

Beyond everyday beautification, make-up is used in theatre and film — including prosthetic and special-effects make-up that transforms an actor's appearance — in fashion photography, and in rituals and performance traditions worldwide.
