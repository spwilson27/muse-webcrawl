Cortisol

Cortisol is the body's main glucocorticoid hormone, made by the adrenal cortex (Adrenal gland) under pituitary ACTH control. It regulates metabolism, immunity and the stress response, following a strong daily rhythm.

Actions

Cortisol mobilises energy: it promotes glucose production by the Liver, breaks down protein and fat, maintains blood pressure and dampens inflammation — the model for corticosteroid drugs (hydrocortisone, prednisone, dexamethasone). Levels peak near waking (the cortisol awakening response) and bottom out around midnight, timed by the Circadian rhythm.

Disorders

Excess (Cushing's syndrome) causes central obesity, hypertension, Diabetes and fragile skin; deficiency (Addison's disease) causes fatigue, weight loss and dangerous crises under stress. Chronic high cortisol from prolonged stress is linked to Depression and memory problems.
