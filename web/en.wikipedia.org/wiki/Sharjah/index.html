Sharjah

Country: United Arab Emirates
Status: Capital of the Emirate of Sharjah
Population: ~1.5 million (third most populous city in the UAE)

Sharjah (Arabic: الشارقة) is the third largest and third most populous city in the United Arab Emirates, forming part of the Dubai-Sharjah-Ajman metropolitan area. It is the capital of the Emirate of Sharjah, ruled by the Al Qasimi family, and the only emirate with land on both the Persian Gulf and the Gulf of Oman coasts.

Culture

Sharjah is regarded as the cultural capital of the UAE. UNESCO named it Cultural Capital of the Arab World in 1998, and it was World Book Capital for 2019. The city hosts the Sharjah International Book Fair, one of the largest in the world, the Sharjah Biennial art exhibition, and numerous museums, including the Sharjah Museum of Islamic Civilization and the Sharjah Art Museum. Its ruler since 1972, Sheikh Sultan bin Muhammad Al-Qasimi, has been a major patron of education and heritage; the city is home to the University of Sharjah and the American University of Sharjah.

Economy and society

Sharjah is an industrial and commercial centre and a popular residential base for people working in Dubai, to which it is connected by heavily used highways. It is socially more conservative than Dubai: alcohol is prohibited throughout the emirate, and dress and conduct codes are stricter. Sharjah International Airport is a major cargo hub and the home base of the low-cost carrier Air Arabia.
