The Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania is the business school of the University of Pennsylvania, a private Ivy League research university in Philadelphia. Founded in 1881 through a donation from industrialist Joseph Wharton, it is the world's oldest collegiate business school and consistently ranks among the very top business schools globally, particularly for finance.

Programmes

Wharton offers a Bachelor of Science in Economics for undergraduates, a full-time MBA programme and an executive MBA, plus doctoral programmes across ten academic departments, from finance and economics to marketing, management and statistics. Its main building is Jon M. Huntsman Hall, opened in 2002, and it also maintains a campus in San Francisco (Wharton San Francisco) and the Penn Wharton China Center in Beijing.

Notable alumni

Alumni include former U.S. President Donald Trump (B.S. 1968), Tesla and SpaceX CEO Elon Musk (B.S. in economics from the College of Arts and Sciences/Wharton programme, 1997), financier and investor Ronald Perelman, and former Johnson & Johnson CEO Alex Gorsky (MBA). Warren Buffett attended Wharton for two years before transferring to the University of Nebraska.
