The Mandalorian is an American space Western television series created by Jon Favreau for the streaming service Disney+. It is the first live-action series in the Star Wars franchise, set five years after the events of Return of the Jedi (1983). It stars Pedro Pascal as the title character, a lone bounty hunter hired to retrieve "the Child" (Grogu), whom he instead protects.

Premise

After the fall of the Galactic Empire, the Mandalorian bounty hunter Din Djarin operates in the lawless Outer Rim. Commissioned by Imperial remnants to capture a mysterious infant of Yoda's species, he instead becomes the child's protector, fleeing across the galaxy while uncovering his own people's creed and the child's significance to the future of the Jedi. Subsequent seasons follow their journeys to Mandalore, encounters with other Mandalorian factions including Bo-Katan Kryze, and confrontations with Moff Gideon.

Production

The series pioneered "StageCraft", an on-set virtual production technology developed by Industrial Light & Magic, in which LED video walls display real-time rendered environments behind physical sets, allowing in-camera visual effects. Directors across its seasons included Dave Filoni, Deborah Chow, Rick Famuyiwa, Bryce Dallas Howard, Taika Waititi, Peyton Reed, and Robert Rodriguez. Ludwig Göransson's score mixed orchestral themes with rock and electronic elements.

Reception

Premiering with the launch of Disney+ on 12 November 2019, the series was a critical and commercial success and a major driver of subscriptions. It received widespread praise for its writing, characters, visuals, and tone, and was nominated for numerous awards, including the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Drama Series, winning multiple Creative Arts Emmys for its effects, cinematography, and music. The character Grogu became a major popular-culture phenomenon. The series spawned the spin-offs The Book of Boba Fett and Ahsoka, and a feature film, The Mandalorian and Grogu, was announced for release in 2026.
