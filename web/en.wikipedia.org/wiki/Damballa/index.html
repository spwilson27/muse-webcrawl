Damballa

Damballa, also spelled Damballah, Dambala and Danbala, is one of the most important loa (spirits) in Haitian Vodou and related Afro-Caribbean traditions. Depicted as a great serpent, Damballa is a primordial creator spirit associated with purity, peace, fertility and the waters of creation.

Beliefs

Damballa is considered one of the oldest and most benevolent of the loa. In Vodou cosmology he is said to have created the waters of the earth and, with his consort Ayida-Weddo, to uphold the world. Because he is a serpent, he does not speak in possession rituals; instead, devotees possessed by him hiss, move sinuously and are cooled with water and offered eggs on mounds of flour. His purity means that offerings to him must be white and unseasoned, and his rites avoid blood sacrifice.

The name and serpent imagery derive from the Fon and Ewe deity Dan (Da) of Dahomey in West Africa, carried to Haiti through the Atlantic slave trade.
