Anjelica Huston

Anjelica Huston (born July 8, 1951) is an American actress and director. A member of the Huston acting family, she won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress for Prizzi's Honor (1985), directed by her father John Huston, and is known for playing Morticia Addams in The Addams Family (1991).

BornJuly 8, 1951, Santa Monica, California
OccupationActress, director
ParentsJohn Huston; Enrica Soma
SpouseRobert Graham (m. 1992; died 2008)

Life and career

Raised largely in Ireland and England, Huston modelled before acting in her father's films. Her breakthrough in Prizzi's Honor made the Hustons the first family with three generations of Oscar winners (Walter, John and Anjelica). She earned further Oscar nominations for Enemies, A Love Story (1989) and The Grifters (1990).

Notable roles

Her work includes The Witches (1990), Wes Anderson films The Royal Tenenbaums, The Life Aquatic and The Darjeeling Limited, and television's Smash and Transparent. She has directed films including Bastard Out of Carolina (1996) and written two memoirs, A Story Lately Told and Watch Me.
