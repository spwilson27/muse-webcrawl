Painesville is a city in and the county seat of Lake County, Ohio, United States, located along the Grand River about 27 miles northeast of Cleveland, a few miles south of Lake Erie. Its population was roughly 20,000 at the 2020 census.

History

The settlement was first known as Oak Openings. Edward Paine, a veteran of the American Revolutionary War who arrived from Connecticut in 1800, is regarded as its founder, and the town was renamed in his honour. Painesville was an early stop on the Underground Railroad, and Lake Erie College was founded there in 1856.

Geography and economy

The city covers about seven square miles. Its economy historically included nurseries and lake shipping; today it is a residential and commercial centre for Lake County and home to Lake Erie College.
