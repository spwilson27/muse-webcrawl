Kingdom of Libya

The Kingdom of Libya existed from 24 December 1951, when Libya declared independence as a federal monarchy under King Idris I, until 1 September 1969, when Idris was overthrown by a military coup led by Muammar Gaddafi. From 1951 to 1963 it was the United Kingdom of Libya, a federation of Cyrenaica, Tripolitania and Fezzan; in 1963 it became a unitary state, the Kingdom of Libya.

Idris, leader of the Senussi order, had been Emir of Cyrenaica. Independence under a UN-sponsored process made Libya one of Africa's first newly independent states. Discovery of oil in 1959 transformed its economy, though wealth distribution and pan-Arab politics created tensions. The 1969 coup abolished the monarchy and proclaimed the Libyan Arab Republic.
