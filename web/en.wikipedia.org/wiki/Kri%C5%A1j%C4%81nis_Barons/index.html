Krišjānis Barons (1835–1923) was a Latvian writer, folklorist and activist of the Latvian National Awakening, best known as the collector and systematiser of the dainas — Latvian folk songs.

The dainas project

From 1878 Barons devoted himself to gathering folk songs sent in by thousands of correspondents across Latvia. He classified more than 200,000 texts and published them in the monumental six-volume Latvju dainas (1894–1915).

Cabinet of Dainas

The songs were stored in a specially built cabinet, the Dainu skapis ("Cabinet of Dainas"), now preserved in Riga and inscribed in UNESCO's Memory of the World register. Barons is honoured as a founding figure of Latvian national culture.
