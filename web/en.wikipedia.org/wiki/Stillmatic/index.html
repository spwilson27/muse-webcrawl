Stillmatic

Stillmatic is the fifth studio album by American rapper Nas, released on December 18, 2001, by Ill Will Records and Columbia Records. Its title references his 1994 debut album Illmatic, and the album was widely seen as a return to form after the commercial orientation of his late-1990s releases.

The album addresses social and political themes, ghetto life, and Nas's feud with Jay-Z; the diss track "Ether" became one of the most celebrated battle records in hip hop. Other singles included "Got Ur Self a Gun", "One Mic", and "Rule". Production was handled by Large Professor, DJ Premier, Ron Browz, Salaam Remi, Trackmasters and others.

Stillmatic debuted at number five on the US Billboard 200 and was certified platinum. It received a rare five-microphone rating from The Source magazine and is frequently described as a comeback album that restored Nas's critical standing.
