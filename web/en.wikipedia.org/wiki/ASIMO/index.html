ASIMO

ASIMO
Manufacturer: Honda
Year of creation: 2000
Type: Humanoid robot
Height: 130 cm

ASIMO (Advanced Step in Innovative Mobility) is a humanoid robot developed by Honda, unveiled in 2000 after research beginning in 1986 with the E and P series. It was designed to operate in human environments, walking, running, climbing stairs and recognizing faces and gestures.

Development

Successive versions improved speed — up to 9 km/h running — dexterity and autonomy. ASIMO toured worldwide as a technology ambassador, conducting orchestras and meeting heads of state. Honda ended development in 2018 to focus on practical robotics applications.
