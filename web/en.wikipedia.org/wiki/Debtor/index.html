Debtor

A debtor is a person or entity legally or contractually obliged to provide a benefit or payment to another, the creditor. Debt arises from loans, bonds, purchases on credit and court judgments.

Types

Debtors include individuals, companies, municipalities and sovereign states. Failure to pay when due may constitute default, leading to enforcement, asset seizure, or bankruptcy proceedings that restructure or discharge debts.

History

Historically, debtors' prisons incarcerated those unable to pay, a practice largely abolished in the 19th century. Modern insolvency law balances creditor recovery with a fresh start for honest debtors.
