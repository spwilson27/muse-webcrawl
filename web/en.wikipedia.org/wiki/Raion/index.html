Raion

A raion (also spelled rayon) is a type of administrative unit of several post-Soviet states. The term comes from the French rayon ("honeycomb, department") and denotes a district, typically a second-level subdivision below an oblast, or a district within a large city.

Usage

Raions exist or existed in Russia, Ukraine, Belarus, Moldova, Georgia, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan and other successor states of the Soviet Union, and in Bulgaria the equivalent rayon is a subdivision of a municipality. In Ukraine, a major reform in 2020 consolidated the country's raions from 490 to 136 enlarged districts. In the Russian Federation raions are generally called municipal districts at the municipal level.
