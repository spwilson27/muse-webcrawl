What If...? is an American animated anthology television series created by A. C. Bradley for the streaming service Disney+, based on the Marvel Comics series of the same name. It is the fourth television series in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) produced by Marvel Studios, and its first animated series. The show explores alternate timelines in the multiverse in which major moments from the MCU films occur differently.

Premise

Following the creation of the multiverse in the first-season finale of Loki, the series imagines how events would unfold if key moments in MCU history had gone another way — Peggy Carter taking the Super Soldier Serum to become Captain Carter, T'Challa being abducted to become Star-Lord, the Avengers being murdered before they assemble, Doctor Strange destroying his universe in grief, and a zombie apocalypse overtaking the heroes. Jeffrey Wright voices Uatu the Watcher, who narrates each episode and is sworn never to interfere — a vow tested when the artificial intelligence Ultron, armed with the Infinity Stones, threatens the whole multiverse.

Episodes and production

The first season premiered on 11 August 2021 and ran for nine episodes as part of Phase Four of the MCU. A second season followed in December 2023, released daily over the Christmas period, and a third and final season concluded the series in December 2024. Many actors from the MCU films reprise their roles, and the animation, produced with a cel-shaded style modelled on classic American illustrators, was supplied by studios including Squeeze, Blue Spirit and Flying Bark Productions. The series was developed by head writer A. C. Bradley and director Bryan Andrews.

Reception

The series received generally positive reviews, with praise for its voice acting, inventive premises and animation, and some criticism of episode length and inconsistent tone. The episode "What If... Doctor Strange Lost His Heart Instead of His Hands?" won particular acclaim, and Chadwick Boseman's final performances as T'Challa earned a posthumous Emmy Award for Outstanding Character Voice-Over Performance.
