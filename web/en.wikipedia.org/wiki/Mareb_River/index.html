The Mareb River, also known as the Gash River in Sudan, is a seasonal river flowing from northern Eritrea into eastern Sudan. For much of its course it forms the border between Eritrea and Ethiopia.

Course

The river rises near Mount Sowayra east of Asmara and flows westward about 440 km. Below the Eritrean–Ethiopian border region it enters Sudan as the Gash, spreading into an inland delta around Kassala before dissipating in the sands; in very wet years it may reach the Atbara River.

Significance

The Mareb historically marked the frontier between the Ethiopian highlands and the lowlands, and the border along it was contested in the Eritrean–Ethiopian War (1998–2000). Its floodwaters support irrigation agriculture in the Gash Delta, especially cotton.

Hydrology

Flow is highly seasonal, confined to the summer rains; the channel is dry for much of the year.
