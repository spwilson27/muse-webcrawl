Tirano


The basilica of the Madonna di Tirano

Tirano is a town in the province of Sondrio in Lombardy, northern Italy, close to the Swiss border. It has a population of roughly 9,000 and lies in the Valtellina valley on the Adda River, at about 440 metres above sea level. The town is best known for its Renaissance sanctuary and as the Italian terminus of the scenic Bernina railway.

Sights

The Sanctuary of the Madonna di Tirano, begun in 1505 after a reported apparition of the Virgin Mary, is one of the most important Renaissance churches in Lombardy, with an elaborate organ and rich stucco work. The old town also preserves palazzi such as Palazzo Salis, with frescoed halls and a historic wine cellar reflecting the valley's wine trade.

Transport

Tirano is the terminus of the Bernina line of the Rhaetian Railway, whose Bernina Express climbs through the Brusio spiral viaduct and past glaciers to St. Moritz in Switzerland; the line is part of a UNESCO World Heritage listing. Italian state railways connect the town to Sondrio and Milan.
