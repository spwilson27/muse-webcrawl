SF Express
Native name: 顺丰速运
Industry: Courier / logistics
Founded: 1993, Shunde, Guangdong
Founder: Wang Wei
Parent: SF Holding

SF Express (Group) Co., Ltd. is a Chinese multinational delivery services and logistics company headquartered in Shenzhen. Founded by Wang Wei in Shunde in 1993, it grew from a small cross-border courier shuttling parcels between mainland China and Hong Kong into China's largest integrated logistics provider, known for fast, reliable, premium-priced delivery.

Operations

SF operates one of the world's largest cargo airline fleets through SF Airlines, centred on its hub at Ezhou Huahu Airport in Hubei — Asia's first dedicated cargo airport, which opened in 2022. The company listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange in 2017 through a reverse merger, making Wang Wei one of China's richest people. SF expanded internationally, including acquiring a controlling stake in Kerry Logistics in 2021, and listed in Hong Kong in 2024.
