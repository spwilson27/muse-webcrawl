Lake Qaraoun

Lake Qaraoun (Arabic: بحيرة القرعون) is an artificial lake in the Bekaa Valley of Lebanon, created by the Albert Naccache Dam on the Litani River. Completed in the early 1960s, it is the largest lake in Lebanon.

Uses

The reservoir stores water for irrigation of the southern Bekaa, supplies drinking water, and drives the Markaba hydroelectric station downstream via a tunnel system. The Qaraoun reservoir has a capacity of about 220 million cubic metres.

Environment

The lake is an important stopover for migratory birds, though it suffers from pollution caused by untreated sewage and agricultural runoff.
