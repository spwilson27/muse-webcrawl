Overview

The Shanghai Stock Exchange (SSE) is a stock exchange based in the city of Shanghai, China. It is one of the three stock exchanges operating independently in mainland China, the others being the Beijing Stock Exchange and the Shenzhen Stock Exchange. Re-established on 26 November 1990 and opened on 19 December 1990 — exchanges had existed in Shanghai from the 1860s and were closed after 1949 — it is among the world's largest exchanges by market capitalisation (several trillion US dollars, varying with markets).

Structure

The SSE lists A-shares (yuan-denominated, long restricted for foreigners, opened progressively through QFII and the Shanghai–Hong Kong Stock Connect, 2014) and B-shares, as well as bonds and funds. Its technology-focused STAR Market (Sci-Tech Innovation Board), launched in 2019 with a registration-based IPO system, was a flagship reform. The exchange operates under the China Securities Regulatory Commission. The SSE Composite Index, based to 100 in December 1990, is the most-quoted Chinese market gauge.
