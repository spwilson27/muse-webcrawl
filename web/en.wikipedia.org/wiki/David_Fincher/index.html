David Andrew Leo Fincher (born August 28, 1962) is an American film director known for psychological thrillers distinguished by meticulous visual control, desaturated palettes, fluid camera movement and storylines about obsession and alienation. His films have received numerous Academy Award nominations, and he is regarded as one of the most influential directors of his generation.

Career

Fincher worked at Industrial Light & Magic on films including Return of the Jedi before becoming a leading director of music videos and commercials through his company Propaganda Films, making acclaimed videos for Madonna ("Express Yourself", "Vogue") and Michael Jackson. His feature debut Alien 3 (1992) — the third film about Ellen Ripley — was a troubled production he later disowned, but he rebounded with the serial-killer thriller Se7en (1995) and The Game (1997).

Fight Club (1999) flopped theatrically but became a major cult film. Fincher went on to direct Panic Room (2002), the true-crime epic Zodiac (2007), The Curious Case of Benjamin Button (2008), The Social Network (2010) — for which he won the Golden Globe and BAFTA for Best Director — The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo (2011), Gone Girl (2014), the black-and-white Mank (2020) and The Killer (2023). For television he executive-produced and directed episodes of Netflix's House of Cards and Mindhunter.

Style

Fincher is famous for demanding dozens of takes, seamless digital effects, low-angle ceilings-in-frame compositions and a dark, precise visual signature that has shaped the modern thriller and crime film.
