List of post-nominal letters

Post-nominal letters, also called post-nominal initials or suffixes, are letters placed after a person's name to indicate office, honours, academic degrees, professional qualifications or membership of learned societies and orders. Conventions differ by country; in the United Kingdom the order of precedence generally runs: decorations and honours, appointments (such as QC/KC or JP), university degrees, professional qualifications, fellowships of learned societies, and membership of Parliament.

In academia, degrees are usually listed in ascending order of seniority, and only the highest degree in a subject is normally given. In the United States, professional credentials such as PE, CPA and AIA are common, while etiquette guides differ on whether academic post-nominals should be used outside professional contexts.
