Derek and the Dominos

Derek and the Dominos was an English–American blues rock band formed in the spring of 1970 by guitarist and singer Eric Clapton, keyboardist and singer Bobby Whitlock, bassist Carl Radle, and drummer Jim Gordon. Guitarist Duane Allman of the Allman Brothers Band contributed to their only studio album.

History

The members had played together in Delaney & Bonnie and backed George Harrison on All Things Must Pass. Their 1970 double album Layla and Other Assorted Love Songs, inspired by Clapton's unrequited love for Pattie Boyd, wife of Harrison, was only modestly received on release but is now regarded as one of the greatest rock albums ever made; its title track "Layla" became a classic, as did "Bell Bottom Blues." The band dissolved during sessions for a second album in 1971 amid Clapton's deepening heroin addiction and internal tensions. Whitlock and Radle, and Gordon, went on to other projects; Gordon's mental illness later led to tragedy, while Radle died in 1980.
