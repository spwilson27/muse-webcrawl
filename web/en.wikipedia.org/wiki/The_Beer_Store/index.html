The Beer Store

The Beer Store (legally Brewers Retail Inc.) is a Canadian privately owned chain of retail outlets selling beer and other malt beverages in the province of Ontario, Canada. Founded in 1927 as Brewers' Warehousing Company following the end of prohibition in Ontario, it long operated as the only retailer permitted to sell beer for off-premises consumption in the province, apart from the government-run Liquor Control Board of Ontario (LCBO) and brewery outlets.

Ownership and operations

The Beer Store is owned by a group of brewers; its largest shareholders historically were Molson Coors, Labatt (AB InBev), and Sleeman (Sapporo). Following a 2015 agreement with the Ontario government, ownership was opened to all Ontario brewers and the province expanded beer sales into grocery stores. The chain operates several hundred stores and runs Ontario's main bottle-return deposit program, accepting empty beer containers and, under agreement, wine and spirit containers for the LCBO.

Alcohol reform

In 2024 the Ontario government announced an early end to the Master Framework Agreement governing beer sales, expanding sales of beer, wine, cider, and ready-to-drink beverages to convenience stores and other retailers, with The Beer Store retaining a role in distribution and container recycling.
