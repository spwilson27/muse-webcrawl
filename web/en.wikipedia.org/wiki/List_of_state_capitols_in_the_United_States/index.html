List of state capitols in the United States

This is a list of the capitol buildings of the fifty U.S. states. Most are domed, Neoclassical structures modelled to some degree on the United States Capitol in Washington, D.C.; notable exceptions include Nebraska's tower design and the skyscraper capitols of Louisiana and North Dakota. The tallest is the Louisiana State Capitol (137 m), and the oldest still in use is the Maryland State House (1772).
