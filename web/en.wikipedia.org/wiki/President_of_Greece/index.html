The President of Greece, officially the President of the Hellenic Republic (Greek: Πρόεδρος της Ελληνικής Δημοκρατίας), is the head of state of Greece. The office was established upon the re-establishment of the republic in 1974, following the fall of the military junta and the referendum that abolished the monarchy. Greece is a parliamentary republic, and the president's role is largely ceremonial: executive power is exercised by the Prime Minister and the government.

The president is elected by the Hellenic Parliament for a five-year term, renewable once. Under the constitution as revised in 2019, election requires a majority that declines across successive ballots, and failure to elect no longer triggers dissolution of parliament. Any Greek citizen of at least forty years of age who has been a citizen for at least five years is eligible. The president resides at the Presidential Mansion on Herodou Attikou Street in Athens, formerly the Crown Prince's Palace.

Powers

The president formally appoints the prime minister and, on the prime minister's recommendation, the other members of government. The president convenes and dissolves parliament under specified conditions, promulgates laws, issues decrees on the government's countersignature, grants pardons, represents Greece internationally, and is the nominal commander-in-chief of the armed forces. Most presidential acts require ministerial countersignature, and the president has a limited suspensive veto, being able to return a bill to parliament once.

History

Earlier Greek republics existed in the 1820s and from 1924 to 1935. The modern office dates from 1974, when Michail Stasinopoulos served as provisional president before Phaedon Gizikis yielded to the civilian transition. Presidents of the Third Hellenic Republic include Konstantinos Tsatsos, Konstantinos Karamanlis, Christos Sartzetakis, Konstantinos Stefanopoulos, Karolos Papoulias, and Prokopis Pavlopoulos. In January 2020 parliament elected Katerina Sakellaropoulou, a senior judge who had presided over the Council of State, as the first female President of Greece; she took office on 13 March 2020.
