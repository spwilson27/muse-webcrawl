Military transport aircraft

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

A strategic airlifter loading cargo through its rear ramp.

A military transport aircraft, or military cargo aircraft, is a fixed-wing or rotary-wing aircraft used to carry troops, weapons, vehicles, and other military equipment to support operations in a war zone or forward area. Transport aircraft are essential to military logistics, enabling rapid deployment, resupply, airborne assault, aerial refuelling support, and medical evacuation.

Types

Transport aircraft are commonly divided into two broad classes:

  • Strategic airlifters — large, long-range aircraft such as the Lockheed C-5 Galaxy and Boeing C-17 Globemaster III, able to carry outsized cargo including main battle tanks and helicopters between theatres of operation.
  • Tactical airlifters — smaller, typically turboprop aircraft such as the Lockheed C-130 Hercules and Airbus A400M Atlas, designed to operate from short, unprepared airstrips close to the front line, delivering cargo by landing or by parachute airdrop.

Most military transports are high-wing designs with rear loading ramps, allowing vehicles to drive on and off. Many can also be configured for paratroop dropping, aeromedical evacuation, or — in tanker-transport variants — aerial refuelling.

History

Military airlift began in earnest during the Second World War, with aircraft such as the Douglas C-47 Skytrain (the military Dakota) carrying paratroops and towing gliders in operations including Normandy and Market Garden. The Berlin Airlift of 1948–49 demonstrated the strategic power of air transport, supplying a blockaded city entirely by air. The introduction of large turboprop and jet transports during the Cold War — the C-130 (1956), C-141 Starlifter, and C-5 Galaxy — gave major powers global airlift reach, demonstrated in operations from Vietnam to the Gulf War and in humanitarian relief worldwide.

Notable examples

  • Lockheed C-130 Hercules — the most widely used tactical transport in history
  • Boeing C-17 Globemaster III
  • Lockheed C-5 Galaxy
  • Airbus A400M Atlas
  • Antonov An-124 Ruslan and An-225 Mriya
  • Kawasaki C-2 and Shaanxi Y-20
