EPCR Challenge Cup

Sport: Rugby union
Founded: 1996 (as European Challenge Cup)
Organiser: European Professional Club Rugby (EPCR)
Region: Europe and South Africa
Related: European Rugby Champions Cup

The EPCR Challenge Cup is an annual rugby union competition organised by European Professional Club Rugby (EPCR). It is the second-tier competition below the European Rugby Champions Cup. First held in 1996 as the European Challenge Cup, the competition was restructured when EPCR took over European club rugby governance in 2014, and has since been known at various times under sponsor names including the Parker Pen Challenge Cup, the Amlin Challenge Cup, and the European Rugby Challenge Cup.

Format

The modern competition features clubs from the English Premiership, the French Top 14, and the multinational United Rugby Championship, together with invited clubs — in recent seasons including Georgian club Black Lion and the South African Cheetahs. In the current format, 18 teams are drawn into three pools of six; each club plays four pool matches against different opponents, and the top four in each pool advance to a knockout stage (round of 16, quarter-finals, semi-finals) culminating in a final at a neutral venue as part of EPCR's finals weekend. The competition also absorbs clubs that finish fifth in their Champions Cup pools, who drop into the Challenge Cup knockouts.

History

The tournament began in the 1996–97 season with 24 teams from England, France, Italy, Romania, Scotland, and Wales; Bourgoin were the inaugural winners. French clubs dominated the early years, while Harlequins became the most successful club in the competition's history with three titles (2001, 2004, 2011). Other notable winners include Sale Sharks, Wasps, Gloucester, Northampton Saints, Cardiff, Leinster (in 2013, having dropped down from the Heineken Cup), Toulon, and the Sharks — who in 2024 became the first South African club to win an EPCR title.
