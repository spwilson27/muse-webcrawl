Super Bowl XLVII was the championship game of the National Football League (NFL) for the 2012 season, played on February 3, 2013, at the Mercedes-Benz Superdome in New Orleans. The American Football Conference champion Baltimore Ravens defeated the National Football Conference champion San Francisco 49ers, 34–31.

Harbaugh Bowl

The game was the first Super Bowl in which the two head coaches were brothers: John Harbaugh of the Ravens and Jim Harbaugh of the 49ers, leading to nicknames such as the "Harbaugh Bowl" and "HarBowl". It was also the 49ers' first Super Bowl loss, after five victories.

Game summary

Baltimore built a 28–6 lead early in the third quarter, highlighted by Jacoby Jones's 108-yard kickoff return touchdown, tying the longest play in Super Bowl history. Moments later, a partial power outage in the Superdome halted play for 34 minutes. San Francisco then mounted a furious comeback behind Colin Kaepernick, closing to 31–29, but the Ravens held on a goal-line stand in the final minutes and took an intentional safety to run down the clock. Joe Flacco, who threw three first-half touchdowns, was named MVP, completing a postseason run of 11 touchdowns without an interception.
