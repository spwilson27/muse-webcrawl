Ganga (goddess)

Ganga
Devanagari: गंगा
Affiliation: Devi, river goddess
Abode: Shivaloka / earth (as the Ganges)
Mount (vahana): Makara
Festivals: Ganga Dussehra, Ganga Aarti

Ganga (Sanskrit: गङ्गा, Gaṅgā) is the personification of the river Ganges, worshipped as a goddess in Hinduism. She is held to purify the sins of the faithful and to liberate souls from the cycle of rebirth (moksha). The goddess is depicted as a beautiful woman riding the makara, a mythical water creature.


The descent of Ganga

Mythology

According to the Ramayana, Mahabharata and the Puranas, Ganga descended from heaven to earth at the request of King Bhagiratha, whose ancestors needed her purifying waters. To break her fall, Shiva caught the river in his matted hair, releasing her in gentle streams — hence Shiva's epithet Gangadhara, "bearer of the Ganga". Ganga is also the mother of Bhishma by King Shantanu in the Mahabharata.

Worship

Ganga is worshipped along the banks of the Ganges, especially at Varanasi, Haridwar, Prayagraj and Rishikesh. The daily Ganga Aarti ceremony offers fire and light to the goddess at dusk. The festival of Ganga Dussehra celebrates her descent to earth.
