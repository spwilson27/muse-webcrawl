"(Don't Fear) The Reaper" is a song by the American rock band Blue Öyster Cult from their 1976 album Agents of Fortune. Written and sung by lead guitarist Buck Dharma, it reached No. 12 on the Billboard Hot 100 and became the band's signature hit.

Composition and meaning

The song's narrator contemplates mortality and eternal love, suggesting death need not be feared because lovers may be reunited in an afterlife — a theme Dharma has said was inspired by his own thoughts on dying young, not by suicide, a common misreading. Its ringing guitar riff and ethereal harmonies made it a staple of classic rock radio.

Legacy

The track gained renewed fame from the 2000 Saturday Night Live sketch "More Cowbell", in which Christopher Walken's producer demands more cowbell from a fictional session. It frequently appears on lists of the greatest rock songs of all time.
