Stable Diffusion

Stable Diffusion is a deep learning, text-to-image model released in 2022 by the start-up Stability AI in collaboration with the CompVis group at Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich and Runway. It is a latent diffusion model capable of generating detailed images conditioned on text descriptions, and can also be used for image-to-image translation, inpainting, and outpainting. Unlike many contemporaneous models, Stable Diffusion was released with its weights publicly available, enabling it to run on consumer hardware and spurring a large ecosystem of fine-tuned derivatives and tools.

Technology

Stable Diffusion implements latent diffusion as described by Rombach et al. (2022): instead of diffusing in pixel space, the model operates in the compressed latent space of a variational autoencoder, dramatically reducing computation. A CLIP text encoder converts prompts into conditioning vectors, and a U-Net iteratively denoises random latent noise into an image, which the autoencoder decoder renders to pixels. The base model was trained on subsets of the LAION-5B dataset of image–caption pairs scraped from the web, filtered for resolution and aesthetics.

Releases

Version 1.4 became publicly available in August 2022, followed by 1.5 from Runway. Version 2.0 (November 2022) retrained the model with a different text encoder and filtered training data. SDXL (July 2023) enlarged the architecture and improved composition and photorealism; a Turbo variant enabled near-real-time generation. Stable Diffusion 3 (2024) adopted a multimodal diffusion transformer architecture.

Impact and controversy

Because its weights were open, Stable Diffusion could be run locally on a typical GPU, fine-tuned on custom datasets (via methods such as DreamBooth and LoRA), and integrated into tools like AUTOMATIC1111's web UI and ComfyUI. Its openness also made it central to debates over AI art: artists and stock-photo companies sued over the use of copyrighted images in training data; researchers documented bias and the model's capacity to generate disallowed content; and getty Images' litigation against Stability AI became a landmark case. Stable Diffusion is widely credited with democratising text-to-image generation and accelerating the generative AI boom.
