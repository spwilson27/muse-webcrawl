Silkroad Ensemble

The Silkroad Ensemble is a musical collective founded by cellist Yo-Yo Ma in 1998 as part of the Silkroad project (originally the Silk Road Project), inspired by the cultural exchange along the ancient Silk Road trade routes. The ensemble brings together musicians from dozens of countries and musical traditions — including Western classical, Chinese, Indian, Persian, Arab, and folk traditions — to create and perform new cross-cultural repertoire.

History and activities

Since its founding, the ensemble has performed throughout North America, Europe, and Asia, commissioned well over a hundred new works from composers around the world, and released numerous recordings. Its album Sing Me Home (2016), created with Ma, won the Grammy Award for Best World Music Album. The ensemble also became known for the documentary film The Music of Strangers: Yo-Yo Ma and the Silk Road Ensemble (2015), directed by Morgan Neville.

In 2020, composer and multi-instrumentalist Rhiannon Giddens succeeded Yo-Yo Ma as artistic director of Silkroad.
