Geneva International Motor Show

The Geneva International Motor Show was an annual auto show held in Geneva, Switzerland, first staged in 1905 and long regarded as one of the world's most important motor shows, a favourite venue for launching concept cars and supercars because Switzerland has no domestic car industry and was seen as neutral ground.

History

Held at the Palexpo exhibition centre near Geneva Airport from 1982, the March show hosted landmark debuts from the 1930 Bugatti Royale to modern hypercars. After cancellations during the COVID-19 pandemic and weak exhibitor interest, the organisers announced in 2024 that the Geneva show would be discontinued, while a related show continued in Qatar.
