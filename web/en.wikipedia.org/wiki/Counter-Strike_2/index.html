Counter-Strike 2

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Counter-Strike 2 (CS2) is a 2023 multiplayer tactical first-person shooter developed and published by Valve. It is the fifth main entry in the Counter-Strike series and replaced Counter-Strike: Global Offensive (2012), from which players' accounts and inventory items carried over.

Development and release

Built on Valve's Source 2 engine, the game was announced in March 2023, underwent a limited test period, and was released for Windows and Linux on 27 September 2023. Major changes included overhauled maps, volumetric smoke grenades that respond to light and gunfire, a sub-tick server architecture intended to make actions register more precisely, and a revised ranking system called CS Rating in the Premier mode.

Gameplay

Two teams — Terrorists and Counter-Terrorists — compete in rounds centred on planting or defusing a bomb, or eliminating the opposing team. Players earn money for kills and objectives, used to buy weapons and equipment in a persistent in-round economy. The game is free-to-play and supported by cosmetic weapon finishes traded on the Steam Community Market. It is one of the most prominent esports titles, with Major championships sponsored by Valve.
