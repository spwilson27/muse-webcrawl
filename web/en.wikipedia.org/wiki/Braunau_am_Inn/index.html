Braunau am Inn is a town on the Austrian border with Germany, in the state of Upper Austria. It lies on the south bank of the river Inn across from the Bavarian town of Simbach am Inn. The town is the administrative seat of the Braunau district and has a population of roughly 17,000.

History

Braunau developed as a fortified trading post on the Inn and received town rights in the 13th century. Historically part of Bavaria, it passed to Austria with the Innviertel in the Treaty of Teschen (1779). The town was the birthplace of Adolf Hitler in 1889; the house at Salzburger Vorstadt 15 now bears a memorial stone against war and fascism, and in 2023–2024 the building was repurposed to house police offices following a national debate over its future.

Landmarks

Sights include the late Gothic parish church of St. Stephen, with one of the tallest church towers in Austria, the preserved old town square, and the Inn bridge linking the town with Simbach in Bavaria.
