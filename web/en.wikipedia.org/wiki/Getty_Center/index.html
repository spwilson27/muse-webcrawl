Getty Center

The Getty Center, in Los Angeles, California, is a campus of the Getty Museum and other programs of the Getty Trust. The $1.3 billion center opened on December 16, 1997, and is famous for its architecture, gardens and views overlooking Los Angeles.

Getty Center
Location: 1200 Getty Center Drive, Los Angeles
Architect: Richard Meier
Opened: 1997
Owner: J. Paul Getty Trust

Architecture

Designed by Richard Meier, the complex is clad in Italian travertine and organised around a central courtyard, reached by a driverless tram from parking below. The Central Garden was designed by artist Robert Irwin.

Collections

The J. Paul Getty Museum at the Center houses European paintings, drawings, manuscripts, sculpture and photographs, including works by Van Gogh (Irises) and artists such as Georgia O'Keeffe's contemporaries. Admission is free. The Getty Research Institute and Getty Conservation Institute are also on site.
