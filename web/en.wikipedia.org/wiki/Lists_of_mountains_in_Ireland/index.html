Lists of mountains in Ireland

The mountains of Ireland are grouped into a number of lists based on height, prominence and other criteria used by hill walkers and mountaineers. The highest point is Carrauntoohil in County Kerry at 1,038.6 m (3,407 ft). Ireland's classification systems draw on the British and Irish traditions of the Furths, the Hewitts, the Marilyns and the Vandeleur-Lynams and Arderins compiled for Ireland itself.

Principal classifications

  • Furths — mountains over 3,000 ft in Ireland (the "Irish Furths" of the Scottish Munro tradition): Carrauntoohil, Beenkeragh, Caher, Cnoc na Péiste, The Bones, Brandon, Lugnaquilla and Galtymore, among others.
  • Vandeleur-Lynams — Irish mountains over 600 m with prominence over 15 m.
  • Arderins — Irish mountains over 500 m with prominence over 30 m; named from Arderin, "height of Ireland".
  • Hewitts — hills in England, Wales and Ireland over 2,000 ft with prominence over 30 m.
  • Marilyns — hills anywhere in Britain and Ireland with prominence over 150 m.
  • Carns — Irish mountains between 400 and 499 m with prominence over 30 m.
  • Binnions — Irish hills between 100 and 399 m.
