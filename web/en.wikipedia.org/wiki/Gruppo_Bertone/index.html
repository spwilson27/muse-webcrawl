Gruppo Bertone

Founded: 1912, Turin, Italy

Founder: Giovanni Bertone

Industry: Automotive design and coachbuilding

Gruppo Bertone was an Italian industrial design and coachbuilding company founded in Turin in 1912 by Giovanni Bertone. Led for decades by his son Nuccio Bertone, the firm became one of the great names of Italian car styling, designing landmark cars for Alfa Romeo, Lamborghini, Lancia, Citroën and many other manufacturers.

History

Bertone began as a carriage workshop and moved into car bodywork after the First World War. Under Nuccio Bertone from the 1950s, its designers — including Giorgetto Giugiaro and Marcello Gandini early in their careers — produced the Alfa Romeo BAT concepts, the Lamborghini Miura and Countach, the Lancia Stratos and the Fiat X1/9. The company also carried out small-series manufacturing for clients.

Decline

Financial difficulties led Bertone into bankruptcy proceedings in 2014; the Bertone name and design activities were later revived under new ownership.
