The Good Fight is an American legal and political drama television series created by Robert King, Michelle King and Phil Alden Robinson. A sequel and spin-off of The Good Wife, it premiered on CBS All Access (later Paramount+) in February 2017 and ran for six seasons until November 2022. It was the fledgling streaming service's first original scripted series.

Premise

One year after the events of The Good Wife, Diane Lockhart (Christine Baranski) loses her life savings in a Ponzi scheme run by the parents of her goddaughter and associate Maia Rindell (Rose Leslie). Forced out of her own firm, Diane joins the predominantly African-American Chicago firm Reddick, Boseman & Lockhart, alongside Lucca Quinn (Cush Jumbo) and Adrian Boseman (Delroy Lindo). The series became known for its fast-moving engagement with contemporary American politics, disinformation, and the law, and for surreal musical and animated interludes explaining subjects such as NDAs and microtargeting.

Reception

The series received widespread critical acclaim, with particular praise for Baranski's performance, and multiple Emmy nominations. Its final seasons dealt with civil unrest, January 6 and the erosion of shared reality, ending with Diane contemplating retirement abroad.
