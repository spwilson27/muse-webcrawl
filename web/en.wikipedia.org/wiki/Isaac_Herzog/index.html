Isaac "Bougie" Herzog (born 22 September 1960) is an Israeli politician and lawyer serving as the 11th president of Israel since 2021. He previously led the Israeli Labor Party and the parliamentary opposition, and chaired the Jewish Agency for Israel.

Background

Herzog comes from a prominent family: his father, Chaim Herzog, was the sixth president of Israel, his grandfather Yitzhak HaLevi Herzog was the first Chief Rabbi of Ireland and later of Israel, and his uncle Abba Eban was Israel's foreign minister. He served as a major in the IDF's Unit 8200 intelligence corps and studied law at Tel Aviv University and Cornell University.

Political career

Elected to the Knesset in 2003 for the Labor Party, Herzog held a succession of ministries — housing and construction, tourism, diaspora affairs, and welfare and social services — under prime ministers Sharon, Olmert and Netanyahu. He became Labor leader in 2013 and, with Tzipi Livni, formed the Zionist Union alliance, which finished second in the 2015 election; Herzog served as Leader of the Opposition until 2018, when he left the Knesset to chair the Jewish Agency.

Presidency

On 2 June 2021 the Knesset elected Herzog president with 87 votes, the largest margin in Israeli history, and he took office on 7 July 2021 for a seven-year term. As president — a largely ceremonial role — he has emphasised national unity, conducted state visits abroad, and, following the outbreak of the Gaza war in October 2023, acted as a prominent spokesman for Israel internationally.
