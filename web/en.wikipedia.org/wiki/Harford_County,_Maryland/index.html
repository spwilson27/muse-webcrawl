Harford County, Maryland

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Harford County is a county located in the U.S. state of Maryland, in the northeastern part of the state along the western shore of the Chesapeake Bay and the Susquehanna River. As of the 2020 census, the population was about 260,000. The county seat is Bel Air. Harford County is part of the Baltimore–Columbia–Towson metropolitan statistical area.

History

The county was created in 1773 from the eastern part of Baltimore County and named for Henry Harford, the last proprietary owner of Maryland. During the War of 1812, the town of Havre de Grace was raided by the British. In the nineteenth century the county was crossed by canals, railroads and the National Road corridor trade routes.

Geography and communities

The county covers roughly 527 square miles, of which about 437 are land. Its largest municipalities include Aberdeen, Bel Air and Havre de Grace, and it is home to much of Aberdeen Proving Ground, a major U.S. Army installation, which is one of the county's largest employers.
