King Fahd Causeway

The King Fahd Causeway is a 25-kilometre (16 mile) series of bridges and causeways connecting Khobar in Saudi Arabia with Bahrain across the Gulf of Bahrain. Opened in 1986, it is named after King Fahd of Saudi Arabia, who financed its construction.

Description

The four-lane highway runs across embankments and bridges, passing through a passport island (Middle Island) where border controls for both countries are located. It carries millions of vehicles annually and is a vital trade and tourism link, making Bahrain easily accessible by road from the Arabian mainland. A parallel rail causeway (the King Hamad Causeway) has been planned.
