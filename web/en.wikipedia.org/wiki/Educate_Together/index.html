Educate Together

Educate Together is an Irish charitable organisation that is the patron body of a network of equality-based primary and second-level schools in Ireland. Its schools are multi-denominational and co-educational, founded on principles of equality, democracy and child-centred education, in contrast to the denominational patronage that historically dominated Irish schooling.

History

Educate Together grew out of the Dalkey School Project, founded by parents and teachers in the 1970s who wanted a school open to children of all religions and none. The first school opened in Dalkey, County Dublin, in 1978. The organisation formally became the patron body for the growing network and has since opened dozens of primary schools across Ireland, as well as second-level schools beginning in the 2010s.

Ethos

Educate Together schools teach an ethical education curriculum called "Learn Together", covering moral and spiritual education, equality and justice, belief systems, and ethics and the environment, rather than the religious instruction of a single faith. No child is separated by religion during the school day. The schools are democratically run with strong parental involvement.
