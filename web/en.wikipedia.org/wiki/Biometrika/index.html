Biometrika

Biometrika is a peer-reviewed scientific journal published by Oxford University Press for the Biometrika Trust. It was established in 1901 and is one of the leading journals in theoretical and applied statistics.

Biometrika
Discipline: Statistics
Founded: 1901
Founders / first editors: Francis Galton, Karl Pearson, Walter Weldon
Publisher: Oxford University Press (Biometrika Trust)

History

The journal was founded by Francis Galton, Karl Pearson and Raphael Weldon to promote biometry — the statistical study of biological variation and heredity. Karl Pearson was its editor for many years. It published foundational work by figures including R. A. Fisher (early papers), Egon Pearson, Jerzy Neyman and later editors such as David Cox.

Scope

Today Biometrika publishes papers on statistical theory and methodology with emphasis on mathematical rigour and important applications, covering inference, probability, and modern computational statistics.
