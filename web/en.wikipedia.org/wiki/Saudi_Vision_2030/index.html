Saudi Vision 2030

Saudi Vision 2030 (Arabic: رؤية السعودية 2030) is a strategic framework launched by Saudi Arabia in 2016 to reduce the country's dependence on oil, diversify its economy, and develop public service sectors such as health, education, infrastructure, recreation and tourism.

Saudi Vision 2030
Launched: 25 April 2016
Led by: Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman
Themes: Vibrant society, thriving economy, ambitious nation

Programmes

The plan is implemented through Vision Realization Programs, including the National Transformation Program and the Public Investment Fund (PIF) programme. Goals include raising non-oil government revenue, increasing the private sector's share of GDP, and growing the PIF into one of the world's largest sovereign wealth funds.

Giga-projects

Flagship projects include NEOM (with The Line), the Red Sea tourism development, Qiddiya entertainment city, Diriyah Gate, and Green Riyadh.

Social reforms

Associated reforms included lifting the ban on women driving (2018), expanding entertainment, and promoting tourism visas, alongside continuing international scrutiny of human rights.
