Eiichiro Oda (Japanese: 尾田 栄一郎, born January 1, 1975) is a Japanese manga artist and the creator of One Piece, the best-selling manga series in history and one of the best-selling comic series worldwide, with over 500 million copies in circulation.

Early life

Born in Kumamoto, Oda decided at age four to become a manga artist, inspired by Dragon Ball creator Akira Toriyama and the anime Vicky the Viking. At 17 his work Wanted! won second place in the Tezuka Award.

Career

Oda worked as an assistant to Shinobu Kaitani, Masaya Tokuhiro, and Nobuhiro Watsuki (Rurouni Kenshin). He published two pirate one-shots titled Romance Dawn in 1996. One Piece began serialization in Weekly Shōnen Jump on July 22, 1997, following pirate Monkey D. Luffy.

Style and influence

Oda is known for intricate world-building, foreshadowing, expressive character designs, and an intense work schedule. He cites Toriyama, and works such as Dragon Ball and westerns, among influences.

Personal life

Oda married Chiaki Inaba, a former model who played Nami in a stage play, in 2004; they have two daughters.
