Wanda Film

Wanda Film Holding Co., Ltd. (万达电影) is a Chinese film production company and cinema operator, part of the Dalian Wanda Group. Through its cinema chains it has been one of the largest exhibitors in China, and its parent group formerly owned the American chain AMC Theatres. Wanda Film is involved in film production, distribution, and exhibition, and operates China's largest cinema circuit by number of screens.

History

Wanda's cinema business began in 2005, growing alongside the group's shopping malls across China. The company listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange in 2015 as Wanda Cinema Line, the first Chinese cinema operator to go public, and later reorganized around film production assets including Wanda Pictures and the Qingdao Movie Metropolis studio complex. In 2023–2024, Wanda Group sold a controlling stake in the film unit to China Ruyi Holdings amid the group's debt reduction efforts.
