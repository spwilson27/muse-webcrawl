Hinduism in Nepal

Followers~81% of the population (2021 census)
Major centrePashupatinath Temple, Kathmandu

Hinduism is the largest religion in Nepal, followed by about four-fifths of the population. Nepal was the world's only Hindu state until 2008, when it became a secular republic.

History

Hindu traditions in the Kathmandu Valley date back over two millennia, blending with Buddhism in the valley's Newar culture. The Shah dynasty, which unified Nepal in the 18th century, promoted Hinduism as a state religion.

Practices and festivals

Major festivals include Dashain, Tihar, Teej, and Maha Shivaratri at the Pashupatinath Temple. The king was traditionally regarded as an incarnation of Vishnu.
