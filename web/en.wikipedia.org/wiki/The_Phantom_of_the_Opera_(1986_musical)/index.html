The Phantom of the Opera is a musical with music by Andrew Lloyd Webber, lyrics by Charles Hart, and a book by Lloyd Webber and Richard Stilgoe. Based on the 1910 French novel by Gaston Leroux, it tells the story of Christine Daaé, a beautiful soprano who becomes the obsession of a mysterious, disfigured musical genius living beneath the Paris Opéra House.

Productions

The musical opened in London's West End in 1986, directed by Harold Prince and starring Michael Crawford as the Phantom and Sarah Brightman as Christine. It opened on Broadway in 1988 and became the longest-running show in Broadway history, closing in April 2023 after 35 years and 13,981 performances. The London production continues in a revised staging.

Music

Well-known songs include "The Music of the Night", "All I Ask of You", "The Phantom of the Opera", "Think of Me", "Angel of Music", "Masquerade" and "Wishing You Were Somehow Here Again". A film adaptation directed by Joel Schumacher was released in 2004.
