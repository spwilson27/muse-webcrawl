The Oregon State Beavers are the intercollegiate athletic teams representing Oregon State University in Corvallis, Oregon, United States. The Beavers compete in NCAA Division I and are one of the two remaining members of the historic Pac-12 Conference following the realignment of 2024, alongside Washington State. The school fields teams in sports including football, men's and women's basketball, baseball, softball, gymnastics, wrestling, soccer, and rowing.

History

Athletics at the university date to the 1890s; the "Beavers" name, referencing Oregon's state animal and the fur trade of the region, became established in the early 20th century. The school's colours are orange and black, and its fight song is "Hail to Old OSU". Its principal rival is the University of Oregon, against which it plays the annual football game formerly known as the Civil War.

Notable programmes

Baseball is the university's most successful programme, winning the College World Series in 2006, 2007 and 2018 under coach Pat Casey, and producing a string of high Major League Baseball draft picks. Gymnastics has a long tradition of national rankings and individual champions. The football team plays at Reser Stadium; highlights include the 1962 Heisman Trophy season of Terry Baker, the only Oregon State player to win the award, and an 11–1 season in 2000 capped by a Fiesta Bowl victory over Notre Dame. Men's basketball reached the Final Four in 1949 and 1963 and enjoyed a strong era under coach Ralph Miller; women's basketball under Scott Rueck reached the Final Four in 2016 and further Elite Eight appearances.

Facilities and traditions

Teams play at venues including Reser Stadium (football), Gill Coliseum (basketball, gymnastics, wrestling and volleyball), and Goss Stadium at Coleman Field (baseball), one of the oldest continuously used college baseball grounds in the United States. Students and alumni are collectively known as "Beaver Nation".
