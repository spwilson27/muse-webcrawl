Central Park is a 51-storey office tower in Perth, Western Australia. Located at 152–158 St Georges Terrace in the central business district, it was completed in 1994 and, at 249 metres (817 ft) including its pinnacle, is the tallest building in Perth. The tower rises from a landscaped parkland podium of about 5,000 square metres at its base — the largest landscaped area associated with an office development in the Perth CBD — from which it takes its name.

Design and construction

The building was designed by the architectural firm Forbes & Fitzhardinge. Its floor plates of roughly 2,000 square metres are among the largest of any Australian office tower, and the plan steps in as the building rises. The spire/pinnacle at the top brings the total height to 249 m; the roof is 226 m. Major tenants have included Rio Tinto and other resources-sector firms, reflecting Perth's role as a centre for the mining industry.

Site

The tower stands on the former site of the Foy & Gibson department store. The open plaza and gardens at its foot, planted with mature trees, are used by city workers and host public events, and the development preserved public open space on a site that had been earmarked for intensive development.
