Avicii

Tim Bergling (8 September 1989 – 20 April 2018), known professionally as Avicii, was a Swedish DJ, remixer, and record producer who pioneered the electronic dance music (EDM) boom of the early 2010s. His hits include "Levels", "Wake Me Up", "Hey Brother", and "Waiting for Love".

Career

Bergling took the name Avicii from the Buddhist hell Avīci, adding an extra 'i' because the name was taken on Myspace. "Levels" (2011), built around an Etta James sample, made him a global star. His debut album True (2013) fused EDM with country and soul; "Wake Me Up" with Aloe Blacc became one of the most-streamed songs ever at the time.

Health, retirement and death

Suffering anxiety, pancreatitis and exhaustion from relentless touring, he retired from live performance in 2016. He died by suicide in Muscat, Oman, in 2018. The posthumous album Tim (2019) and the Avicii Experience museum in Stockholm followed; his death prompted wide discussion of mental health in the music industry.
