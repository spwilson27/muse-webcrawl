Portugal national football team

The Portugal national football team represents Portugal in men's international football and is governed by the Portuguese Football Federation. It played its first match in 1921 and has grown into one of Europe's leading national sides.

History

Portugal's first golden generation, led by Eusébio, finished third at the 1966 World Cup in England. The team returned to prominence in the 2000s, reaching the final of Euro 2004 on home soil, where it lost to Greece, and the semi-finals of the 2006 World Cup.

Major trophies

Under captain Cristiano Ronaldo and coach Fernando Santos, Portugal won its first major trophy at UEFA Euro 2016 in France, defeating the hosts in the final with an extra-time goal by Éder. Portugal then won the inaugural UEFA Nations League in 2019, beating the Netherlands in the final in Porto, and won the competition again in 2025, defeating Spain on penalties in Munich under coach Roberto Martínez.

Players

Cristiano Ronaldo is the team's most capped player and the highest scorer in men's international football history, with over 130 international goals. Other notable players across generations include Eusébio, Luís Figo, Rui Costa, Deco, Pepe, Bernardo Silva and Bruno Fernandes.

Stadium

Portugal has no fixed national stadium but plays most home matches at Lisbon's Estádio da Luz and Estádio José Alvalade, or the Estádio do Dragão in Porto.
