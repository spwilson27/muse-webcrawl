CloverWorks Inc. is a Japanese animation studio founded on April 1, 2018. It was rebranded from the Kōenji Studio of A-1 Pictures and is a subsidiary of Aniplex, itself part of Sony Music Entertainment Japan. The studio is known for polished character-driven adaptations and high-profile seasonal hits.

Works

Selected television series and films include:

  • Darling in the Franxx (2018, with Trigger and A-1 Pictures)
  • Rascal Does Not Dream of Bunny Girl Senpai (2018)
  • The Promised Neverland (2019–2021)
  • Horimiya (2021)
  • My Dress-Up Darling (2022)
  • Spy × Family (2022–, with Wit Studio)
  • Bocchi the Rock! (2022)
  • The Elusive Samurai (2024)

History

Aniplex spun the Kōenji-based unit out of A-1 Pictures so that each studio could develop a distinct identity; the "Clover" name reflects a four-leaf motif shared with its sister studios. CloverWorks quickly became one of the most commercially successful anime studios of the early 2020s.
