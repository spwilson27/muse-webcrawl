Warcraft (film)

Warcraft is a 2016 American action fantasy film directed by Duncan Jones.

Director: Duncan Jones
Released: 2016
Budget: $160 million
Box office: $439 million

Warcraft (alternatively known as Warcraft: The Beginning) is a 2016 American action fantasy film directed by Duncan Jones and based on the Warcraft video game series by Blizzard Entertainment. It portrays the initial encounters between humans and orcs in the world of Azeroth, drawing principally on the first game, Warcraft: Orcs & Humans.

Plot

As the orc homeworld Draenor dies, the warlock Gul'dan opens a portal to the human realm of Azeroth, sending through a warband to pave the way for invasion. The orc chieftain Durotan questions Gul'dan's corrupting fel magic, while on the human side King Llane Wrynn, the knight Anduin Lothar and the mage Medivh must respond to the invasion. The half-orc Garona is caught between both worlds as the conflict escalates.

Production and reception

The film was produced by Legendary Pictures and released by Universal Pictures. It starred Travis Fimmel, Paula Patton, Ben Foster, Dominic Cooper and Toby Kebbell. Reviews were largely negative, criticising the script and pacing, though the visual effects were praised. Despite a modest performance in North America, the film grossed about $439 million worldwide — the largest share from China — making it the highest-grossing video game adaptation at the time, though it was still widely reported to have lost money after costs.
