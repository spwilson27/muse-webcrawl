The 2020–21 FA Cup was the 140th season of the FA Cup, the world's oldest football association cup competition. The tournament was heavily affected by the COVID-19 pandemic: replays were removed to ease fixture congestion, and most matches were played behind closed doors.

Final

The final at Wembley Stadium on 15 May 2021 was played before a crowd of about 20,000 as part of a government pilot event scheme. Leicester City beat Chelsea 1–0 through a spectacular long-range goal from Youri Tielemans in the 63rd minute, giving Leicester their first FA Cup triumph in five final appearances. A late Chelsea equaliser was disallowed by VAR for a marginal offside.

Semi-finals

Leicester defeated Southampton 1–0 in the semi-finals, while Chelsea beat Manchester City by the same scoreline, ending City's hopes of a quadruple.

Notable results

Marine, of the eighth tier, hosted Tottenham Hotspur in the third round, one of the largest league-position gaps in FA Cup history, while non-league Chorley defeated Championship side Derby County.
