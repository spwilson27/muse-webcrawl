RuPaul's Drag Race is an American reality competition television series produced by World of Wonder, in which drag queens compete in design, acting, comedy and performance challenges for the title of "America's Next Drag Superstar", a cash prize and a crown. Hosted and judged by RuPaul, the show premiered on Logo TV in 2009 and has become a global franchise and cultural phenomenon.

Format

Each episode typically features a mini-challenge and a main challenge, followed by a runway presentation. The two lowest-ranked contestants "lip sync for their lives", after which RuPaul eliminates one with the catchphrase "Sashay away"; winners are told "Shantay, you stay". Recurring elements include the Snatch Game celebrity-impersonation challenge, the reading challenge, makeover episodes, and the companion aftershow Untucked. Seasons conclude with a reunion and a finale, in recent series decided by a lip-sync tournament.

Impact and franchise

The series has won numerous Primetime Emmy Awards, including Outstanding Reality-Competition Program multiple times and repeated hosting awards for RuPaul, and is credited with bringing drag into mainstream culture and popularising drag slang. Spin-offs include All Stars and international versions in the UK, Canada, España, Down Under, France, Philippines, Thailand, Brazil, Mexico and elsewhere, plus the vs. the World and Global All Stars formats.
