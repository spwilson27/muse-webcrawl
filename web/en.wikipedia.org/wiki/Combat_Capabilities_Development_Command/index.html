Combat Capabilities Development Command

The United States Army Combat Capabilities Development Command (DEVCOM) is a major subordinate command of Army Futures Command, responsible for the Army's research, development, and engineering enterprise. Headquartered at Aberdeen Proving Ground, Maryland, it was activated in 2019 as the successor to the Research, Development and Engineering Command (RDECOM), and transferred from Army Materiel Command to the newly created Army Futures Command as part of a modernization reorganization.

Organization

DEVCOM comprises a headquarters, the Army Research Laboratory — the Army's corporate research laboratory for fundamental science — and a set of eight centers aligned with capability areas:

  • Aviation & Missile Center (C5ISR aviation and missile systems)
  • C5ISR Center (command, control, communications, computers, cyber, intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance)
  • Armaments Center (weapons and munitions, at Picatinny Arsenal)
  • Ground Vehicle Systems Center (at Detroit Arsenal)
  • Soldier Center (soldier equipment, clothing, and food science, at Natick)
  • Chemical Biological Center (at Edgewood, Aberdeen Proving Ground)
  • Data & Analysis Center
  • DEVCOM Analysis Center and the DEVCOM Americas organization, which manages international technology cooperation

The command employs on the order of 17,000 soldiers, civilians, and contractors, most of them scientists and engineers.

Mission

DEVCOM develops technologies and engineering solutions in support of Army Futures Command's modernization priorities — including long-range precision fires, next-generation combat vehicles, future vertical lift, air and missile defense, soldier lethality, and the network — and provides engineering support and technology assessment throughout a system's life cycle, from basic research to fielding and sustainment.
