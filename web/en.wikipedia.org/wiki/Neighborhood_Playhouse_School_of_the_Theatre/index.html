Neighborhood Playhouse School of the Theatre

The Neighborhood Playhouse School of the Theatre is a professional conservatory for actors located at 340 East 54th Street in New York City. It grew out of the Neighborhood Playhouse, one of the first "little theatres" in the United States, founded in 1915 by the philanthropist sisters Alice and Irene Lewisohn at the Henry Street Settlement on the Lower East Side.

History and the Meisner technique

The school is closely associated with Sanford Meisner, who joined the faculty in 1935 and developed there the approach to acting known as the Meisner technique, based on repetition exercises designed to make actors respond truthfully and spontaneously to their partners. The school's two-year certificate program and six-week summer intensive remain built around Meisner's work, alongside classes in voice, movement, dance and scene study.

Alumni

Notable alumni include Gregory Peck, Joanne Woodward, Robert Duvall, Diane Keaton, James Caan, Christopher Meloni, Jennifer Coolidge and many others who trained in the Meisner approach.
