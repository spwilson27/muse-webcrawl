Tomorrow Never Dies

Tomorrow Never Dies is a 1997 spy film and the eighteenth in the James Bond series, and the second to star Pierce Brosnan as the fictional MI6 agent James Bond. It was directed by Roger Spottiswoode.

Plot

Bond investigates media mogul Elliot Carver, who plans to provoke a war between Britain and China to secure broadcasting rights in China. With the help of Chinese agent Wai Lin, played by Michelle Yeoh, Bond infiltrates Carver's stealth ship in the South China Sea and foils the plot. Jonathan Pryce plays Carver, and Teri Hatcher plays Paris Carver, a former lover of Bond.

Production and reception

The film was shot in locations including Thailand, Germany, and the United Kingdom, and featured an elaborate remote-controlled BMW car chase in a Hamburg car park. It grossed over $333 million worldwide. Its theme song, "Tomorrow Never Dies", was performed by Sheryl Crow, while the score was composed by David Arnold.
