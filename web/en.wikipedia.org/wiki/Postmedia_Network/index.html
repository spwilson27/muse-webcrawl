Postmedia Network Canada Corp. is a Canadian media conglomerate consisting of the publishing properties of the former Canwest Global Communications, principally its chain of newspapers. The company was formed in 2010 when a group of creditors, led by the National Post's CEO Paul Godfrey, purchased Canwest's newspaper division out of bankruptcy protection for about $1.1 billion. Postmedia is headquartered in Toronto and is majority-owned by the American hedge fund Chatham Asset Management.

Publications

Postmedia owns many of Canada's major English-language broadsheet and tabloid newspapers, including the National Post, the Vancouver Sun, The Province, the Calgary Herald, the Edmonton Journal, the Regina Leader-Post, the Saskatoon StarPhoenix, the Windsor Star, the Ottawa Citizen, and the Montreal Gazette, along with the Sun tabloid chain (including the Toronto Sun) acquired from Quebecor in 2015, and numerous community newspapers. In 2022 it acquired Brunswick News, adding newspapers in New Brunswick. Its national news service supplies content across the chain, and its websites operate under a shared digital strategy built around the nationalpost.com and canoe.com brands.

History and controversies

Since its formation the company has repeatedly cut staff and centralised production in response to declining print advertising, moving printing and layout to shared hubs and reducing local newsroom employment. In 2016 it controversially merged newsrooms in cities where it owned two formerly competing papers. Postmedia's editorial direction is generally conservative, and the company's ownership by a US hedge fund, along with directives to endorse particular parties during elections, has drawn criticism from press-freedom advocates and competing outlets.

In 2018 Postmedia and Torstar announced a swap of dozens of community newspapers, most of which were promptly shut down; the transaction was investigated by the Competition Bureau, which raided the companies' offices, though no charges followed. The federal journalism support measures introduced from 2019 onward have provided the company with significant tax credits and subsidies.
