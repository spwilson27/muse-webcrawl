Doctor Octopus (Dr. Otto Octavius), also known as Doc Ock, is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Stan Lee and artist Steve Ditko, he first appeared in The Amazing Spider-Man #3 (July 1963) and is one of the superhero Spider-Man's most enduring and prominent foes, generally considered his second greatest archenemy after the Green Goblin.

Fictional biography

Otto Octavius was a brilliant but socially inept nuclear physicist who invented a harness of four powerful, telescoping mechanical arms to handle radioactive material from a safe distance. A laboratory explosion fused the harness to his body and damaged his brain, and he turned to crime, controlling the arms by mental command. He was the first villain to defeat Spider-Man decisively in battle, and later founded the Sinister Six. In the 2012 storyline Dying Wish, a terminally ill Octavius swapped minds with Peter Parker and briefly took over as the "Superior Spider-Man."

In other media

Alfred Molina portrayed Doctor Octopus in the film Spider-Man 2 (2004), a portrayal widely praised as one of the great comic-book movie villains, and reprised the role in Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021). A female version, Olivia "Liv" Octavius, appears in the animated film Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018). The character has appeared extensively in animated series and video games, including Marvel's Spider-Man (2018).
