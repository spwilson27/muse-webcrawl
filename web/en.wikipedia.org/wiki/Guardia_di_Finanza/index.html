Guardia di Finanza

The Guardia di Finanza (GdF) is an Italian law enforcement agency under the authority of the Minister of Economy and Finance. It is a militarised police force — part of the Italian Armed Forces — whose primary mission is combating financial crime, tax evasion, smuggling, drug trafficking and illegal immigration.

Its origins trace to the Royal Finance Guard established in the Kingdom of Sardinia in 1774. Members, known as finanzieri, wear distinctive grey-green uniforms with yellow flame grenade collar patches and yellow details on their caps.

The Guardia di Finanza maintains extensive naval and air components to patrol Italy's long coastline and borders, and it is heavily involved in operations against organised crime, counterfeit goods, money laundering and the financing of terrorism.
