Bonus

A bonus is a form of additional compensation given to an employee, executive, or other worker beyond their normal salary or wages, often as a reward for performance, as an incentive, or on special occasions such as holidays.

Types

Performance bonuses reward meeting targets; signing bonuses attract new hires; retention bonuses encourage staff to stay; holiday bonuses such as a Christmas bonus or 13th-month pay are customary in many countries. In finance, large banker bonuses have been controversial, especially after the 2008 financial crisis.

Taxation and regulation

Bonuses are generally taxed as ordinary income. Some jurisdictions capped or taxed banker bonuses heavily after 2008.

Other uses

The term also denotes extra content in media, bonus points in games, and bonus shares in investing.
