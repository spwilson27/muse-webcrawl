The Evil Queen, also known as the Wicked Queen or simply the Queen, and sometimes named Queen Grimhilde in Disney publications, is the main antagonist of Walt Disney Animation Studios' first animated feature film, Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937). She is the vain, jealous stepmother of the princess Snow White.

In the 1937 film

Obsessed with remaining "the fairest of them all", the Queen consults her Magic Mirror daily. When the Mirror names Snow White as fairest, she orders her huntsman to kill the girl and bring back her heart. Deceived by a pig's heart, the Queen learns the truth from the Mirror and, using dark magic, transforms herself into an old hag, creates a poisoned apple, and tricks Snow White into biting it. Fleeing the dwarfs after her crime, she is pursued to a precipice; lightning strikes the cliff and she falls to her death as a boulder crushes her.

Characterisation and legacy

The Queen was designed as a cold, regal beauty, influenced in part by Joan Crawford and by the character of Queen Hash-a-Motep in the 1935 film She. Lucille La Verne voiced both the Queen and her hag form. The character is frequently ranked among the greatest animated villains; the American Film Institute placed her tenth on its list of the 50 greatest screen villains. She has since appeared widely in Disney media, theme parks, the Kingdom Hearts games, and as the ancestor of the character Regina Mills in Once Upon a Time. Gal Gadot portrays her in the live-action remake Snow White (2025).
