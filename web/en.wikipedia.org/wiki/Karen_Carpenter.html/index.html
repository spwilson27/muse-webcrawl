Karen Carpenter

Karen Anne Carpenter (1950–1983) was an American singer and drummer who, with her brother Richard, formed the duo the Carpenters. Known for her warm three-octave contralto voice, she sang hits including '(They Long to Be) Close to You' and 'We've Only Just Begun'.

Career

The Carpenters signed to A&M Records in 1969 and became one of the best-selling acts of the 1970s, with Karen's drumming and singing at the centre of their sound. Their soft pop style won multiple Grammy Awards.

Death and legacy

Carpenter suffered from anorexia nervosa, a condition little understood at the time; she died of heart failure related to the illness in 1983, aged 32. Her death raised public awareness of eating disorders.
