Shrivenham

Shrivenham is a village and civil parish in the Vale of White Horse district of Oxfordshire, England (historically in Berkshire), about 5 miles (8 km) southwest of Faringdon and 7 miles (11 km) northeast of Swindon. The parish includes the hamlet of Bourton. The population was about 2,300 at the 2011 census.

History and landmarks

Shrivenham is an ancient settlement on the Icknield Way corridor; the parish church is St Andrew's. Beckett Hall and the Barrington estate are associated with the village. The Wilts & Berks Canal passed through the parish.

Defence Academy

Shrivenham is best known as the home of the Defence Academy of the United Kingdom, the Royal Military College of Science, and the Joint Services Command and Staff College, located on the Shrivenham station site, along with facilities of Cranfield University's defence and security teaching. The presence of service personnel and students strongly shapes the local community.
