Nava Raipur, formerly known as Naya Raipur, is a planned city and the capital of the Indian state of Chhattisgarh. It lies about 17 km south-east of the older city of Raipur, between National Highway 53 and National Highway 30, near Swami Vivekananda Airport. The city was developed by the Nava Raipur Development Authority after Chhattisgarh was carved out of Madhya Pradesh in 2000 and Raipur was designated its capital.

Development

Planned over an area of roughly 8,000 hectares, Nava Raipur was designed as a greenfield administrative and residential city with wide roads, sector-based planning, underground utilities, and extensive green cover. The state secretariat (Mantralaya) and other government offices moved there beginning in 2012, and the Chhattisgarh Legislative Assembly building is located in the city. Its name was changed from Naya Raipur to Nava Raipur in 2018 during the tenure of chief minister Raman Singh; "nava" and "naya" both mean "new".

Features

The city hosts the Shaheed Veer Narayan Singh International Cricket Stadium, one of India's largest cricket grounds, the Hidayatullah National Law University, an IIIT campus, and a jungle safari and zoo. Wide tree-lined avenues and planned sectors aim to accommodate a population of several hundred thousand as development proceeds.
