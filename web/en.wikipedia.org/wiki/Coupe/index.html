Coupe

A coupe or coupé is a passenger car with a sloping or truncated rear roofline, traditionally with two doors and two or four seats. The term derives from the French verb couper, "to cut", and was first applied to shortened horse-drawn carriages.

Definitions

Historically a coupe had a single row of seats or a fixed roof with less than a specified interior volume; standards bodies have used a rear-seat volume threshold to distinguish coupes from two-door sedans. Since the 2000s manufacturers have also marketed "four-door coupes" and coupe-styled SUVs with fastback rooflines.

See also: sedan; fastback; Berlinetta.
