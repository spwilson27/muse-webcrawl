Reynolds American, Inc. (RAI) is an American tobacco company and a wholly owned subsidiary of British American Tobacco (BAT). It was formed in 2004 by the merger of the US operations of Brown & Williamson with the R. J. Reynolds Tobacco Company.

Brands and history

Through its subsidiaries, Reynolds American sells cigarette brands including Camel, Newport, Natural American Spirit, and Pall Mall, as well as the Vuse vapour products and modern oral nicotine brands. In 2015 RAI acquired Lorillard, maker of Newport, in a deal worth about $27.4 billion, selling some brands to Imperial Tobacco to satisfy regulators.

Acquisition by BAT

In 2017 British American Tobacco, which had held a 42% stake since 2004, acquired the remaining shares for about $49 billion, making Reynolds American a wholly owned subsidiary and creating one of the world's largest listed tobacco groups.
