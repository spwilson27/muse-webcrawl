Vlad Tenev

Vladimir Tenev (born 13 February 1987) is a Bulgarian-American entrepreneur and billionaire, best known as the co-founder and chief executive officer of Robinhood, a financial technology company offering commission-free stock and cryptocurrency trading.

Early life and education

Tenev was born in Varna, Bulgaria, and emigrated to the United States with his parents as a child, growing up in Washington, D.C., where both parents worked at the World Bank. He studied mathematics at Stanford University, where he met his future co-founder Baiju Bhatt, and began a PhD in mathematics at UCLA before leaving to start a company.

Career

After working on high-frequency trading software in New York, Tenev and Bhatt founded Robinhood in 2013. The app's launch in 2015 popularised zero-commission trading in the United States. Tenev became sole CEO in 2020. He drew intense scrutiny during the January 2021 GameStop short squeeze, when Robinhood restricted purchases of certain stocks, and he testified before the U.S. House Financial Services Committee.
