AP NFL Most Valuable Player Award

AP NFL Most Valuable Player Award

Awarded for: Most valuable player in the NFL regular season

Presented by: Associated Press

First awarded: 1957

Most wins: Peyton Manning (5)

Recent winners: Patrick Mahomes (2022), Lamar Jackson (2023), Josh Allen (2024)

The AP NFL Most Valuable Player Award is presented annually by the Associated Press (AP) to a player in the National Football League (NFL) adjudged to have been the most valuable during the regular season. The winner is chosen by a nationwide panel of 50 sportswriters and broadcasters who cover the NFL, voting at the end of the regular season, before the playoffs. The award is announced during the NFL Honors ceremony on the eve of the Super Bowl.

History

The AP has given the award since 1957, making it the oldest and most prestigious of the several NFL MVP awards. Although defensive players and special teamers are eligible, the award has overwhelmingly gone to offensive players, especially quarterbacks. Only two defensive players have won: Alan Page (1971) and Lawrence Taylor (1986); kicker Mark Moseley won in the strike-shortened 1982 season. Peyton Manning holds the record with five awards. Running back Adrian Peterson (2012) was the most recent non-quarterback winner before quarterback dominance resumed. Cleveland Browns running back Jim Brown won the award three times in its early years.

Voting

Since 2022 voters submit ranked ballots of five players, scored on a weighted points system, replacing the earlier single-name ballot. Unanimous selections have been rare: Tom Brady in 2010 and Lamar Jackson in 2019 were the only unanimous winners under the old system.
