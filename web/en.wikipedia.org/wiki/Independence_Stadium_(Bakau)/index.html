Independence Stadium (Bakau)

Independence Stadium is a multi-purpose stadium in Bakau, The Gambia, near the capital Banjul. It is the national stadium and home of the Gambia national football team.

History

The stadium was built with Chinese assistance and opened in 1984, replacing the Box Bar Stadium as the country's principal venue. It hosts football matches, athletics, national celebrations and concerts.

Facilities

The complex includes the stadium bowl with an athletics track, a hostel, swimming pool and indoor sports facilities nearby. Periodic renovations have addressed pitch quality and seating to meet CAF and FIFA standards; at times the national team has played home fixtures abroad when the venue was deemed substandard.
