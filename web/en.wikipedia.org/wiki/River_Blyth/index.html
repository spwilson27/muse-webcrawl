River Blyth

The name River Blyth refers to several rivers in England, including rivers in Northumberland, Suffolk, and the East Midlands.

River Blyth, Northumberland

The Northumberland Blyth rises near Kirkheaton and flows east for about 42 km (26 mi) through Ponteland to the North Sea at the port of Blyth, the town from which it takes its name. Its estuary was a major coal-exporting harbour.

River Blyth, Suffolk

The Suffolk Blyth rises near Laxfield and flows through Halesworth and Blythburgh to an estuary shared with the River Wangford, reaching the North Sea at Southwold harbour. The estuary is known for its salt marshes and birdlife.

River Blyth, Warwickshire/Staffordshire

A smaller River Blyth rises in Warwickshire and flows through the Blythe Valley to join the River Tame near Coleshill; it gives its name to Solihull's ward of Blythe.
