CBeebies

CBeebies
Owner: BBC
Launched: 11 February 2002
Audience: Children under 7
Sister channel: CBBC

CBeebies is a British free-to-air television channel owned and operated by the BBC, aimed at children under seven years old. It launched on 11 February 2002 alongside its sister channel CBBC, and broadcasts daily with a mix of live-action and animated programming, presented in vision by a team of presenters.

Programming

Well-known CBeebies programmes include Bluey (in the UK), Hey Duggee, Bing, In the Night Garden, Teletubbies, Sarah & Duck and the bedtime story slot CBeebies Bedtime Stories, in which celebrity guests read a story at the end of the broadcast day. The channel's yellow "Bugbies" mascots and birthday card segments are long-running features.

International

International versions of CBeebies operate in regions including Latin America, Asia, Australia (until closure), Africa and Poland, and CBeebies content is also available through BBC iPlayer.
