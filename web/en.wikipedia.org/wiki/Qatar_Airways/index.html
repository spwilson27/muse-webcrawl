Qatar Airways is the state-owned flag carrier of Qatar, based in Doha.

Qatar Airways Company Q.C.S.C. (Arabic: الخطوط الجوية القطرية), operating as Qatar Airways, is the state-owned flag carrier of Qatar. Headquartered in Doha, the airline operates a hub-and-spoke network from Hamad International Airport, linking more than 160 international destinations across Africa, Asia, Europe, the Americas and Oceania. It is a member of the Oneworld alliance, which it joined in October 2013.

History

Qatar Airways was established on 22 November 1993 and began operations on 20 January 1994, initially serving Amman with a wet-leased Boeing 767. The airline was relaunched in 1997 under the leadership of chief executive Akbar Al Baker, who led the company for more than 26 years until stepping down in 2023, when Badr Mohammed Al-Meer succeeded him. From a small regional carrier, Qatar Airways grew into one of the largest long-haul airlines in the world, wholly owned by the Qatari government.

The airline was the launch customer for the Airbus A350-1000 and among the first operators of the A350-900. During the 2017–2021 diplomatic blockade of Qatar by neighbouring states, the airline lost access to surrounding airspace and many regional routes, incurring substantial losses but continuing operations through longer routings. Qatar Airways played a prominent role during the COVID-19 pandemic by maintaining services and repatriating passengers, and it was an official partner and airline of the 2022 FIFA World Cup held in Qatar.

Fleet

Qatar Airways operates an all-wide-body fleet for its mainline passenger services of more than 200 aircraft, built around the Airbus A350 family and Boeing 777 and 787 Dreamliner types, alongside Airbus A380s returned to service on high-density routes. Its cargo division, Qatar Airways Cargo, is among the world's largest air freight carriers, operating dedicated Boeing 777 freighters.

Services and reputation

The airline is known for its premium cabins, in particular the Qsuite business class product with closing doors, introduced in 2017. Its frequent-flyer programme, Privilege Club, adopted Avios as its reward currency in 2022. Qatar Airways has repeatedly been named "Airline of the Year" in the Skytrax World Airline Awards, winning the title a record number of times, most recently in 2024. The airline sponsors numerous sports teams and events; its logo has appeared on the shirts of FC Barcelona, Paris Saint-Germain and other clubs.

Subsidiaries and investments

The Qatar Airways Group includes the airport operations at Hamad International Airport, Qatar Duty Free, Qatar Aviation Services and the Al Maha ground-services and lounge businesses. The airline has held equity stakes in other carriers, including International Airlines Group (IAG), LATAM, Cathay Pacific and Air Italy (which ceased operations in 2020), and agreed in 2024 to acquire a stake in South Africa's Airlink and a major shareholding in RwandAir alongside investment in Kigali's new airport.
