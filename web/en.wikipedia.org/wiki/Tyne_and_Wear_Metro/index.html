Tyne and Wear Metro

The Tyne and Wear Metro is an urban light rail and rapid transit system serving Newcastle upon Tyne, Gateshead, North Tyneside, South Tyneside and Sunderland in Tyne and Wear, England. Opened in stages from 1980, it was the first modern light rail system in the United Kingdom.

Network

The system combines new tunnels beneath central Newcastle and Gateshead with converted suburban railway alignments, including the historic North Tyneside Loop. Its two lines share a core between South Gosforth and Pelaw. Extensions reached Newcastle Airport in 1991 and Sunderland in 2002. A fleet of new Stadler trains began replacing the original Metrocars in the 2020s.
