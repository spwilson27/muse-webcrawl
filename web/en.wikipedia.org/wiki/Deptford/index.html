Deptford

Deptford is an area of south-east London, England, within the London Borough of Lewisham. It is situated on the south bank of the River Thames and is named after a ford across the River Ravensbourne.

History

Deptford was historically a centre of shipbuilding: the Royal Navy's Deptford Dockyard was founded by Henry VIII in 1513 and operated for over 350 years. The playwright Christopher Marlowe was killed in Deptford in 1593. In the 19th century the area became associated with the meat trade and the Foreign Cattle Market.

Transport

Deptford is served by Deptford railway station and Deptford Bridge DLR station, with Deptford Market on the high street a longstanding local landmark.
