List of most watched television broadcasts in the United States

The most watched television broadcasts in the United States are dominated by the Super Bowl, which regularly draws over 100 million viewers. Nielsen ratings measure average audience across the broadcast.

Non-sport broadcasts

Beyond sport and the Moon landing, the largest audiences were for series finales (M*A*S*H, Cheers, Seinfeld), the "Who shot J.R.?" episode of Dallas, and special events such as the 1977 miniseries Roots and Richard Nixon's resignation speech.
