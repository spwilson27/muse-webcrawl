Efnysien

Efnysien fab Euroswydd
Appears inSecond Branch of the Mabinogi (Branwen ferch Llŷr)
FamilyMother Penarddun; twin Nisien; half-siblings Bran, Branwen, Manawydan

Efnysien fab Euroswydd is a destructive character in Welsh mythology, appearing in the Second Branch of the Mabinogion, Branwen ferch Llŷr. Twin brother of the peaceable Nisien, Efnysien is his opposite: quarrelsome, spiteful and violent.

Role in Branwen

When Matholwch, king of Ireland, comes to marry Branwen, sister of the giant king Brân the Blessed, Efnysien — insulted that he was not consulted — mutilates Matholwch's horses. Brân compensates Matholwch with gifts including a magic cauldron that resurrects the dead.

In Ireland, Efnysien later throws his nephew Gwern, son of Branwen and Matholwch, into a fire during a feast, sparking a devastating war between Britain and Ireland. In the final battle he redeems himself in part: hiding among Irish corpses, he is placed in the cauldron of rebirth and stretches himself to break it, bursting its sides — and his own heart — destroying the cauldron that gave the Irish their advantage.

Interpretation

Scholars read the twins Efnysien and Nisien as embodiments of strife and peace. Efnysien's name is sometimes connected with enmity or discord.
