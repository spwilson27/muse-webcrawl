Gender marker

A gender marker (or sex marker) is the designation of a person's sex or gender recorded on official documents such as passports, identity cards, driver's licences and birth certificates, conventionally shown as "M" (male) or "F" (female).

Non-binary markers

Many jurisdictions have introduced a third option, commonly "X", for non-binary, intersex or unspecified gender. The ICAO standard for machine-readable passports permits M, F or X. Countries offering X markers include the United States (since 2022 on passports), Canada, Australia, Germany and India (as "third gender"), though the procedures for changing a marker vary widely, from self-declaration to medical or court requirements.
