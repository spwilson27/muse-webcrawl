Bernard Montgomery

Field Marshal Bernard Law Montgomery, 1st Viscount Montgomery of Alamein (17 November 1887 – 24 March 1976), nicknamed "Monty", was a senior British Army officer who served in both world wars.

Bernard Montgomery
Born: 17 November 1887, London
Died: 24 March 1976
Rank: Field Marshal
Commands: Eighth Army, 21st Army Group
Battles: El Alamein, Normandy, Operation Market Garden

Second World War

Montgomery commanded the British Eighth Army in North Africa, defeating Rommel at the Second Battle of El Alamein in 1942, a turning point of the war. He later commanded Allied ground forces in the initial stages of the Normandy invasion and the 21st Army Group through north-west Europe, accepting the German surrender on Lüneburg Heath in May 1945. His ambitious Operation Market Garden in the Netherlands failed to end the war early.

Post-war

He served as Chief of the Imperial General Staff and later as Deputy Supreme Allied Commander Europe in NATO.
