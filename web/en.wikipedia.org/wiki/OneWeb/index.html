OneWeb (legally Network Access Associates Ltd, now part of Eutelsat Group as Eutelsat OneWeb) is a satellite communications company providing broadband internet from a constellation of satellites in low Earth orbit, principally serving governments, enterprises, aviation and maritime customers rather than selling directly to consumers.

Contents1 History · 2 Constellation · 3 Services · 4 See also

History

Founded by Greg Wyler in 2012 as WorldVu, OneWeb raised major investment from Virgin Group, Qualcomm, SoftBank and others, and began launching satellites in 2019. The company filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in March 2020 after SoftBank withdrew funding, but was rescued later that year by a consortium of the UK government and Bharti Global. Launches resumed, and following the loss of a Soyuz launch slot in 2022 amid the Russian invasion of Ukraine, OneWeb completed its first-generation constellation using SpaceX and Indian LVM3 rockets in March 2023. In September 2023 OneWeb merged with French geostationary operator Eutelsat, creating Eutelsat Group.

Constellation

The first-generation system comprises roughly 630 satellites in near-polar orbits at about 1,200 km altitude, using Ku- and Ka-band spectrum. Unlike some rivals, OneWeb satellites lack inter-satellite links and relay traffic via ground gateway stations.

Services

OneWeb sells wholesale capacity to telecom operators, governments and mobility markets (in-flight and maritime connectivity), emphasising lower latency than geostationary services.
