BYD Auto

BYD Auto Co., Ltd. is the automotive subsidiary of the Chinese multinational conglomerate BYD Company. Headquartered in Xi'an, Shaanxi, it produces battery electric vehicles and plug-in hybrid electric vehicles, together known in China as new energy vehicles (NEVs). Founded in 2003 following BYD's acquisition of Qinchuan Automobile, the company has grown to become one of the world's largest manufacturers of electric vehicles.

History and growth

BYD Auto's parent company began as a rechargeable battery manufacturer, and that vertical integration — building its own batteries, semiconductors and motors — became its signature advantage. In March 2022 BYD ended production of purely internal-combustion vehicles to focus entirely on NEVs. In the final quarter of 2023, BYD overtook Tesla as the world's best-selling maker of battery electric cars.

Products and technology

The company's range spans the Ocean series (including the Dolphin and Seal), the Dynasty series (including the Han and Tang), and the premium brands Denza, Fang Cheng Bao and Yangwang. Its Blade Battery, a lithium iron phosphate pack with an elongated cell design, is used across the range and supplied to other manufacturers.
