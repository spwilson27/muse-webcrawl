Law & Order: Special Victims Unit

Law & Order: Special Victims Unit (often abbreviated SVU) is an American police procedural drama television series created by Dick Wolf for NBC, premiered on 20 September 1999. It is the first spin-off of Law & Order and follows detectives of the NYPD's Special Victims Unit, which investigates sexually based offences.

Mariska Hargitay stars as Captain Olivia Benson, a role she has played since the first season, making her one of the longest-serving prime-time drama leads in US television. Christopher Meloni starred as Elliot Stabler for the first twelve seasons and returned in the spin-off Law & Order: Organized Crime.

SVU surpassed the original Law & Order in longevity, becoming the longest-running US prime-time live-action drama series, and has won multiple Emmy and other awards.
