Darkseid (/ˈdɑːrksaɪd/) is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer-artist Jack Kirby, the character first appeared in Superman's Pal Jimmy Olsen #134 in December 1970. He is the tyrannical ruler of the planet Apokolips and is considered one of Superman's greatest adversaries and the archenemy of the Justice League.

Character history

Born Uxas, second son of Yuga Khan, Darkseid seized the Omega Force when his father was imprisoned in the Source Wall, transforming himself into a rock-like being of immense power. He rules Apokolips through terror, aided by his uncle and military general Steppenwolf, the torturer Desaad, and Granny Goodness, who trains his Female Furies.

Powers and goals

Darkseid's most feared weapon is the Omega Effect, energy beams projected from his eyes that can disintegrate, teleport, or track targets around corners and through barriers. His driving ambition is to obtain the Anti-Life Equation, a mathematical proof that life is meaningless, which would allow him to extinguish free will throughout the universe and remake it in his own image.

In other media

Darkseid has appeared in numerous animated series, including Super Friends and the DC Animated Universe (voiced by Michael Ironside), and made his live-action film debut in Zack Snyder's Justice League (2021), portrayed by Ray Porter.
