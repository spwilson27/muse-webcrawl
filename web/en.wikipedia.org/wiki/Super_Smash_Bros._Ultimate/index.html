Super Smash Bros. Ultimate is a 2018 crossover fighting game developed by Bandai Namco Studios and Sora Ltd. and published by Nintendo for the Nintendo Switch. The fifth instalment of the Super Smash Bros. series, it was built on the premise "Everyone is Here": every playable character from all previous games returned, joined by newcomers and two waves of downloadable fighters that brought the roster to 89 characters.

Content

The game includes over 100 stages and roughly 1,000 music tracks drawn from dozens of game franchises. Its single-player modes include Classic Mode and the adventure mode World of Light, in which players rescue fighters and collect "spirits" — characters who grant stat boosts and abilities — from the entity Galeem. Third-party guests include Sonic the Hedgehog, Solid Snake, Cloud Strife, Steve from Minecraft, Sephiroth, Kazuya Mishima, and Sora from Kingdom Hearts, the final DLC fighter.

Reception

Ultimate received universal acclaim for its scale and polish and sold over 34 million copies, making it the best-selling fighting game of all time and one of the best-selling Nintendo Switch titles. It won awards including Best Fighting Game at The Game Awards 2019 and built one of the largest competitive scenes in esports.
