GBU-39 Small Diameter Bomb

The GBU-39/B Small Diameter Bomb (SDB) is a 250-pound (110 kg) precision-guided glide bomb developed by Boeing for the United States Air Force. Its small size lets aircraft carry more weapons per sortie, and folding wings extend its standoff range to over 60 nautical miles when released from altitude.

GBU-39 SDB
Type: Precision-guided glide bomb
Weight: 285 lb (129 kg)
Warhead: 36 lb (16 kg) explosive
Guidance: GPS/INS
In service: 2006–present

Design

The SDB combines GPS/inertial guidance with a penetrating multipurpose warhead in a compact body. Four bombs can be carried on a single BRU-61 rack in place of one 2,000-pound bomb.

Variants

The laser-guided GBU-39B/B adds terminal laser homing. The GBU-53/B StormBreaker (SDB II) uses a tri-mode seeker — millimeter-wave radar, infrared and semi-active laser — to strike moving targets in all weather.
