Orquesta Sinfónica de Tenerife

The Orquesta Sinfónica de Tenerife (Tenerife Symphony Orchestra) is a Spanish symphony orchestra based in Santa Cruz de Tenerife, in the Canary Islands. Founded in 1935 as the Orquesta de Cámara de Canarias, it is one of the oldest orchestras in Spain and is administered by the island government, the Cabildo de Tenerife. Its home concert venue is the Auditorio de Tenerife, the striking waterside concert hall designed by Santiago Calatrava that opened in 2003.

History and activities

The orchestra performs a subscription season in Tenerife, appears at the Festival Internacional de Música de Canarias, and has toured mainland Spain and abroad. It has made numerous recordings of Spanish and Latin American repertoire. Notable chief conductors have included Víctor Pablo Pérez, who led the orchestra for two decades and raised its national profile, and later music directors such as Lü Jia and Antonio Méndez.
