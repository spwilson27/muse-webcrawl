Knightfall (comics)

"Knightfall" is a 1993–1994 Batman comic book storyline published by DC Comics, spanning Batman, Detective Comics and related titles. It is divided into the arcs "Knightfall", "Knightquest" and "KnightsEnd".

Plot

The villain Bane systematically frees Batman's enemies from Arkham Asylum, exhausting Bruce Wayne before confronting and defeating him, breaking his back. Wayne chooses Jean-Paul Valley (Azrael) as his replacement, but Valley becomes increasingly violent and unstable. After recovering, Wayne reclaims the mantle after defeating Valley in "KnightsEnd".

Legacy

The story introduced Bane as a major villain and is one of the best-known Batman sagas of the 1990s; its central confrontation was adapted in the 2012 film The Dark Knight Rises.
