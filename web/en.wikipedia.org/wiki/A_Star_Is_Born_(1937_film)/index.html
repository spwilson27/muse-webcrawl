A Star Is Born (1937 film)

A Star Is Born is a 1937 American Technicolor romantic drama film produced by David O. Selznick and directed by William A. Wellman. It stars Janet Gaynor as Esther Blodgett, an aspiring Hollywood actress from North Dakota, and Fredric March as Norman Maine, a famous but fading, alcoholic movie star who helps launch her career as "Vicki Lester" — and whom she marries — while his own career collapses. The story has been remade three times: in 1954 with Judy Garland, in 1976 with Barbra Streisand, and in 2018 with Lady Gaga and Bradley Cooper.

Plot

Esther arrives in Hollywood with her grandmother's blessing and struggles through extra work before a chance meeting with idol Norman Maine at a concert. Maine, taken with her, arranges a screen test; rechristened Vicki Lester, she rises rapidly to stardom and an Academy Award. But Maine's alcoholism destroys his career, and after public humiliations and a failed attempt at recovery, he walks into the Pacific Ocean and drowns himself. Grieving, Esther resolves to quit — until her grandmother reminds her of the cost of his sacrifice, and she introduces herself at a premiere as "Mrs. Norman Maine".

Awards and legacy

The film won the Academy Award for Best Original Story and an honorary Oscar for its colour cinematography, and was nominated for Best Picture and both leading acting awards. In 2000 it was selected for preservation in the United States National Film Registry as culturally, historically, or aesthetically significant.
