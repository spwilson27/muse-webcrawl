National Commission on Indigenous Peoples
Agency overview
Formed: 1997
Jurisdiction: Philippines
Headquarters: Quezon City, Philippines

The National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP) is an agency of the government of the Philippines responsible for protecting and promoting the interests and well-being of the country's Indigenous Cultural Communities and Indigenous Peoples. It was created by the Indigenous Peoples' Rights Act of 1997 (Republic Act No. 8371), commonly known as IPRA.

Mandate

The NCIP is the primary government agency tasked with formulating and implementing policies for the recognition, protection, and promotion of the rights of Indigenous peoples. Its functions include issuing Certificates of Ancestral Domain Title (CADTs) recognizing communal ownership of ancestral domains, and ensuring that the free, prior and informed consent of Indigenous communities is obtained for projects affecting their lands.
