List of female state governors in the United States

As of 2025, there have been 51 women who have served as state governors in the United States. Women have served as governor in 33 of the 50 states. This article lists the female state governors in the history of the United States.

Nellie Tayloe Ross of Wyoming became the first woman to serve as governor of a U.S. state in 1925.

History

The first woman to act as governor was Carolyn B. Shelton of Oregon, who served briefly in 1909 as acting governor. The first woman elected governor in her own right was Nellie Tayloe Ross of Wyoming, inaugurated on 5 January 1925, followed fifteen days later by Miriam "Ma" Ferguson of Texas; both were widows of former governors. Ella T. Grasso of Connecticut, elected in 1974, was the first woman elected governor without having been the wife or widow of a former governor.
