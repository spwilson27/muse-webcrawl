Melee

A melee (French: mêlée, "a mixture") is a confused, close-quarters fight between multiple combatants, generally without formal structure or formation. The word comes from the French for a confused mingling of combatants. In medieval tournaments, the mêlée was the original form of the tourney: a mock battle in which groups of knights fought across open country, from which the later formalised joust developed.

Usage

In military usage, "melee" refers to hand-to-hand combat at close range after formations have broken down. The term is widely used in gaming, where "melee combat" denotes close-range fighting as opposed to ranged combat; characters or weapons specialising in such fighting are described as melee. In everyday language a melee can mean any noisy, confused crowd struggle or brawl.
