Close order drill

TypeMilitary exercise
PurposeTraining, ceremony, unit cohesion

Close order drill is a form of military drill in which soldiers march and manoeuvre in close formation, executing standardised movements on command. It is used in the armed forces of many countries for training, discipline and ceremonial purposes.

Purposes

Historically, close order formations were tactically essential for massed infantry. Today drill is retained to instil discipline, precision and unit cohesion, to move groups efficiently, and for public ceremonies, parades and funerals.

Commands and movements

Typical elements include falling in, dressing ranks, marching at quick and slow time, wheeling, saluting and presenting arms. Exhibition drill teams perform elaborate routines, sometimes with bayonets, beyond the standard manual.
