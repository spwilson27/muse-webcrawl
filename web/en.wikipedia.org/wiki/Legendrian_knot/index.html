Legendrian knot

In mathematics, a Legendrian knot is a knot or link in three-dimensional space that is everywhere tangent to the contact planes of a contact structure. Legendrian knots are central objects in the study of contact topology, the branch of geometry concerned with contact structures on manifolds.

Definitions

Consider the standard contact structure on three-dimensional Euclidean space, given at each point by a plane that twists as one moves. A smooth closed curve is Legendrian if its tangent vector at every point lies in the corresponding contact plane. Because of this constraint, a Legendrian knot is determined by certain projections: in the front projection (to the xz-plane), Legendrian knots appear with cusps and no vertical tangencies, while in the Lagrangian projection (to the xy-plane) they appear as immersed curves with no self-tangencies of a particular kind.

Classical invariants

Legendrian knots are distinguished not only by their ordinary knot type but by two classical invariants: the Thurston–Bennequin invariant (tb), which measures the twisting of the contact planes along the knot relative to a framing, and the rotation number (r), which measures the winding of the tangent vector relative to a trivialization of the contact plane field. Two Legendrian knots with the same topological type can fail to be Legendrian isotopic when these invariants differ. A classification programme asks whether tb and r completely determine Legendrian knots of a given type; this holds for some knot types (such knots are called Legendrian simple) but not others.

Stabilization and modern invariants

Any Legendrian knot can be stabilized, an operation that decreases tb by one and changes r, allowing a single knot type to admit infinitely many Legendrian representatives. Finer invariants coming from Legendrian contact homology, developed by Chekanov and others, can distinguish Legendrian knots with identical classical invariants, famously providing examples of Legendrian knots that are topologically identical with equal tb and r yet are not Legendrian isotopic.
