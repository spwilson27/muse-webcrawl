United Technologies

Company
Founded: 1929 (as United Aircraft and Transport Corporation)
Defunct: April 3, 2020 (merged into RTX)
Headquarters: Farmington, Connecticut
Industry: Conglomerate, aerospace

United Technologies Corporation (UTC) was an American multinational conglomerate headquartered in Farmington, Connecticut. It researched, developed, and manufactured products in numerous areas, including aircraft engines, aerospace systems, HVAC, elevators and escalators, fire and security, building automation, and industrial products.

History

UTC traced its origins to the United Aircraft and Transport Corporation, formed in 1929 from Boeing, Pratt & Whitney, and other aviation companies. Antitrust action under the Air Mail Act of 1934 broke the company apart, and United Aircraft continued as the eastern manufacturing arm. In 1975 it renamed itself United Technologies as it diversified beyond aerospace, acquiring Otis Elevator (1976) and Carrier (1979).

Businesses

Major units included Pratt & Whitney (jet engines), Collins Aerospace (avionics, after the 2018 acquisition of Rockwell Collins), Otis (elevators), and Carrier (heating and air conditioning). The Goodrich acquisition in 2012 made UTC a major aircraft components supplier.

Merger into RTX

On April 3, 2020, UTC merged with Raytheon Company to form Raytheon Technologies (now RTX Corporation). Otis and Carrier were spun off as independent companies as part of the transaction.
