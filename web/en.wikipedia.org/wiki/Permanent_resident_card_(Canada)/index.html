Permanent resident card (Canada)

The permanent resident card (PR card; French: carte de résident permanent) is an identification document and travel document for permanent residents of Canada. Introduced in 2002 under the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act, it is issued by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada.

Use

The card is the standard proof of permanent resident status in Canada. Permanent residents travelling abroad by commercial carrier must present a valid PR card, or a permanent resident travel document, to return to Canada. Inside Canada the card serves as official photo identification in many contexts, though it is not itself proof of citizenship.

Validity and renewal

Cards are normally valid for five years. Renewal requires that the holder meet the residency obligation — being physically present in Canada for at least 730 days in the preceding five years, with some exceptions for time spent abroad with a Canadian citizen spouse or working for a Canadian employer.
