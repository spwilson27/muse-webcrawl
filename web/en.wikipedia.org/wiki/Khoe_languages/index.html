Khoe languages

The Khoe languages are a family of click languages spoken in southern Africa, principally in Namibia, Botswana and South Africa. The largest member is Khoekhoe (Nama/Damara), the most widely spoken indigenous language of Namibia.

Classification

The family is generally divided into two branches: the Khoekhoe (or Central Khoisan) branch and the Kalahari Khoe branch, which includes languages such as Khwe, Naro and ǁGana. The Khoe languages were traditionally grouped with other click languages under the term "Khoisan", now considered an areal rather than genealogical grouping.
