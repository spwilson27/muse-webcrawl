The University of Oklahoma (OU) is a public research university in Norman, Oklahoma. Founded in 1890, seventeen years before Oklahoma statehood, it enrols roughly 30,000 students and is the state's flagship public university along with Oklahoma State University.

Academics and research

OU is noted for meteorology and severe-storms research, hosting the National Weather Center in partnership with NOAA, and for programs in petroleum engineering, law, and Native American studies. The Fred Jones Jr. Museum of Art and the Sam Noble Museum are major campus institutions.

Athletics

The Oklahoma Sooners are a college football power, with seven claimed national championships and numerous Heisman Trophy winners, and the softball program has won multiple national titles. OU joined the Southeastern Conference in 2024.
