London North Eastern Railway
Industry: Train operating company
Route: East Coast Main Line

London North Eastern Railway (LNER) is a British train operating company owned by the Department for Transport, which took over intercity services on the East Coast Main Line from Virgin Trains East Coast in June 2018.

Services

LNER runs services from London King's Cross to Leeds, York, Newcastle, Edinburgh and other destinations in Scotland and northern England, using Azuma trains built by Hitachi.

Name

The company revives the name of the London and North Eastern Railway, one of the Big Four railway companies of 1923-1948.
