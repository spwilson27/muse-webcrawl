Champ Car

Champ Car was the name for a class and specification of open-wheel racing cars used in American Championship car racing, and for the sanctioning bodies CART (1979–2003) and Champ Car World Series (2004–2008).

History

After the USAC/CART split, CART ran the premier Indy car series. The 1996 split with the Indy Racing League weakened the sport. CART went bankrupt in 2003; its successor Champ Car unified with IndyCar in February 2008, ending the split. Champions included Mario Andretti, Emerson Fittipaldi, Sébastien Bourdais (four consecutive titles 2004–2007).
