Port of Tianjin

The Port of Tianjin is the principal seaport of northern China and the maritime gateway to Beijing, located in the Binhai New Area of Tianjin municipality at the mouth of the Hai River on Bohai Bay. It is the largest port in northern China and consistently ranks among the world's ten busiest ports by both tonnage and container throughput, handling more than 22 million TEU per year.

Development

Tianjin was opened as a treaty port in 1860, and its modern harbour at Tanggu was developed in the twentieth century. Because the coast is shallow and silty, the port is built on dredged and reclaimed land protected by long breakwaters, with approach channels deepened to accept large bulk carriers and container ships.

Operations

The port's terminals handle containers, iron ore, coal, crude oil, vehicles and general cargo across the Beijiang, Dongjiang and Nanjiang port areas. It gained tragic prominence in August 2015, when massive explosions at a hazardous-chemicals warehouse in the port area killed more than 170 people and led to far-reaching reforms of dangerous-goods handling in Chinese ports.
