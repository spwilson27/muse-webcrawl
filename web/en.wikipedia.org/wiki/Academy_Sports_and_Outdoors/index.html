Academy Sports and Outdoors

Academy Sports and Outdoors, Inc. is an American sporting goods and outdoor recreation retailer headquartered in Katy, Texas. The company operates several hundred stores across the southern and central United States and sells footwear, apparel, sporting equipment, and hunting, fishing and camping gear.

History

The business traces its origins to 1938, when Arthur Gochman founded a tire shop in San Antonio, Texas. The company expanded into military surplus and then sporting goods, taking the name Academy after the Academy Tire Shop. The Gochman family sold a majority stake to the private equity firm Kohlberg Kravis Roberts in 2011, and the company completed an initial public offering on the Nasdaq stock exchange in 2020, trading under the ticker ASO.

Operations

Academy's stores are concentrated in Texas, the Gulf Coast, the Southeast and the Midwest. The company markets several private-label brands, including Magellan Outdoors, BCG and Outdoor Gourmet, alongside national brands.
