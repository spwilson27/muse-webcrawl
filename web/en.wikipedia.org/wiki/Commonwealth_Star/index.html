Commonwealth Star

The Commonwealth Star is a seven-pointed star symbolising the Federation of Australia, which came into force on 1 January 1901. Six points of the star represent the six original states of the Commonwealth of Australia, while the seventh point represents the federal territories and any future states. It appears on the flag of Australia below the Union Jack, and on the coat of arms of Australia, where a wreath of wattle surrounds the star and crest.

History

The Commonwealth Star was included on the Blue Ensign design chosen in a public competition in 1901. In its original form it had only six points, one for each state. A seventh point was added in 1908 to represent the Territory of Papua and any future Australian territories, and the revised design was gazetted on 19 December 1908.

Usage

Beyond the national flag and coat of arms, the Commonwealth Star appears on many Australian insignia, including the badges of the Australian Defence Force and federal police, the Order of Australia insignia, and the ensigns of government vessels. The seven points are also said to be commemorated in the design of Federation Mall and other features of Canberra, although such claims are debated. The star on the flag is rendered in white, while on the coat of arms it is gold.
