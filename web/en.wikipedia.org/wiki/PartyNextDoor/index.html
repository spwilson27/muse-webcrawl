Jahron Anthony Brathwaite (born July 3, 1993), known professionally as PartyNextDoor (stylized in all caps), is a Canadian singer, songwriter, and record producer. He was the first artist to sign with Canadian rapper Drake's record label OVO Sound, in 2013.

PartyNextDoor performing live.

PartyNextDoor released his debut extended play, the self-titled PartyNextDoor, in 2013 to critical praise. His debut studio album, PartyNextDoor Two (2014), was supported by the Billboard Hot 100-charting single "Recognize" (featuring Drake). His third album, PartyNextDoor 3 (2016), peaked at number three on the Billboard 200 and spawned the single "Come and See Me" (featuring Drake). His fourth album, Partymobile (2020), was supported by the single "Loyal" (featuring Drake). He released his fifth studio album, PartyNextDoor 4 (2024), followed by the collaborative album Some Sexy Songs 4 U (2025) with Drake.

Early life

PartyNextDoor was born Jahron Anthony Brathwaite in Mississauga, Ontario, to a Jamaican mother and a Trinidadian father. He was inspired to make music after seeing his father playing a film in a church choir. He grew up listening to Jodeci, Boyz II Men, Blackstreet and 112.

Career

2013–2014: Beginnings and PartyNextDoor Two

In 2013, Brathwaite became the first artist signed to Drake's OVO Sound label. His debut EP, PartyNextDoor, was released on July 1, 2013, and debuted at number six on the Billboard Heatseekers Albums chart. His debut studio album PartyNextDoor Two followed on July 29, 2014.

2016–2020: PartyNextDoor 3 and Partymobile

PartyNextDoor 3 was released on August 12, 2016, debuting at number three on the Billboard 200. Partymobile was released on March 27, 2020, after several delays.

2024–present: PartyNextDoor 4 and Some Sexy Songs 4 U

PartyNextDoor 4 was released in April 2024. In February 2025, he released the collaborative album Some Sexy Songs 4 U with Drake, which debuted atop the Billboard 200.

Songwriting

Outside of his own releases, PartyNextDoor has written songs for other artists, most notably co-writing Rihanna's hit single "Work" (2016), as well as tracks for DJ Khaled, Post Malone, and Drake.
