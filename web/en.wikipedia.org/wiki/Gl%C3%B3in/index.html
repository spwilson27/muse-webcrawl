Glóin is a fictional character in J. R. R. Tolkien's Middle-earth legendarium. A Dwarf of Durin's Folk, he was one of the twelve companions of Thorin Oakenshield on the Quest of Erebor recounted in The Hobbit (1937), and the father of Gimli, a member of the Fellowship of the Ring in The Lord of the Rings.

Appearances

In The Hobbit, Glóin accompanies Thorin and Bilbo Baggins to the Lonely Mountain to reclaim the Dwarf kingdom of Erebor from the dragon Smaug. He is initially sceptical of Bilbo. In The Fellowship of the Ring, an elderly Glóin attends the Council of Elrond at Rivendell as an emissary from Erebor, reporting the approaches of messengers from Mordor.

Background

Glóin was a descendant of Náin II and a veteran of the Battle of Azanulbizar. After the recovery of Erebor he grew wealthy, and he died in the Fourth Age at a great age.
