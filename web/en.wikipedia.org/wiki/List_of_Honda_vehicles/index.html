List of Honda vehicles

Honda Motor Co., Ltd. is a Japanese multinational manufacturer of automobiles, motorcycles and power equipment. This list of Honda vehicles covers cars sold under the Honda marque worldwide, past and present. Premium models are sold in some markets under the Acura brand.

Current models (selection)

Passenger cars: Civic (1972–present), Accord (1976–present), Fit/Jazz, City, Civic Type R, Prelude (revived), and the Amaze and Brio in Asian markets. SUVs and crossovers: CR-V (1995–present), HR-V/Vezel, Pilot, Passport, Odyssey (minivan, 1994–present), Ridgeline (pickup), ZR-V, WR-V and BR-V. Electrified models: the Honda e (2020–2024), the Prologue EV developed with General Motors, the e:N series in China, and hybrid versions of the Civic, Accord and CR-V.

Discontinued models

Notable past models include the S600 and S800 sports cars of the 1960s, the first-generation Civic CVCC, the Prelude coupé (1978–2001), the sporty CR-X and CR-X del Sol, the mid-engined NSX supercar (1990–2005; second generation 2016–2022), the S2000 roadster (1999–2009), the Element, the Insight hybrid (1999–2006, 2009–2014, 2019–2022), the Clarity fuel-cell and plug-in hybrid range, the Legend flagship sedan, the Integra (1985–2006, revived under Acura), the Elysion, the Stream and the kei cars such as the N360, Today, Beat and S660.

Motorcycles and other products

Honda is the world's largest manufacturer of motorcycles, with models such as the Super Cub (the best-selling motor vehicle in history), the Gold Wing touring bike, the Africa Twin and the CBR sport bike series. The company also produces the HondaJet light business jet, marine outboard engines, generators, lawn and garden equipment, and the ASIMO humanoid robot (retired 2018).
