William Joseph Baldwin (born February 21, 1963) is an American actor. A member of the Baldwin acting family, he is the second-youngest of the four Baldwin brothers, alongside Alec, Daniel, and Stephen. He first gained recognition for his roles in the early 1990s, including the firefighter drama Backdraft (1991) and the erotic thriller Sliver (1993).

Early life

Baldwin was born in Massapequa, New York, the son of Carol Martineau and Alexander Rae Baldwin Jr., a high school teacher and football coach. He graduated from Alfred G. Berner High School and studied political science at Binghamton University, where he was a varsity wrestler. Before acting, he worked as a model for Calvin Klein.

Career

Baldwin made his television debut playing a killer in the 1989 television film The Preppie Murder. His feature film breakthrough came with Ron Howard's Backdraft (1991), in which he played Chicago firefighter Brian McCaffrey alongside Kurt Russell and Robert De Niro. He went on to star in Flatliners (1990), Sliver (1993) opposite Sharon Stone, Fair Game (1995) with Cindy Crawford, and Virus (1999). He also appeared in The Squid and the Whale (2005) and had a recurring role as Patrick Darling IV on the ABC drama Dirty Sexy Money (2007–2009). Later television work included roles on Gossip Girl, Hawaii Five-0, and Too Old to Die Young.

Personal life

Baldwin married singer Chynna Phillips, of the group Wilson Phillips, in 1995. They have three children. He has been involved in environmental and political activism, and later relocated with his family to Santa Barbara, California.
