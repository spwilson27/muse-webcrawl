Fornost

Fornost is a fictional city in J. R. R. Tolkien's Middle-earth legendarium. Known in full as Fornost Erain, "Northern Fortress of the Kings", it stood on the North Downs in Eriador.

History in the legendarium

Fornost became the capital of the Dúnedain kingdom of Arthedain after the fall of Annúminas. In T.A. 1974 it was captured by the forces of the Witch-king of Angmar, and although a Gondorian–Lindon army destroyed Angmar's host at the Battle of Fornost the following year, the city was never reoccupied and fell into ruin, later known as "Deadmen's Dike". In The Lord of the Rings Aragorn recalls its fall; Barliman Butterbur mentions it as a place of ill repute in tales.
