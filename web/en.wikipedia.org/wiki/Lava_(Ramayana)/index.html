Lava (Ramayana)

The twins Lava and Kusha sing the epic to Rama.

Lava is a figure in the Hindu epic Ramayana. He is the younger twin son of Rama and Sita, and the twin brother of Kusha. Their story is told in the Uttara Kanda, the final book of the epic.

Story

After Sita was exiled from Ayodhya, she took refuge in the hermitage of the sage Valmiki, where she gave birth to twins. Raised and taught by Valmiki, the boys learned the Ramayana itself from its author and recited it so beautifully that they were invited to Rama's court, where the king recognised them as his sons. In later tradition, Lava founded the city of Lavapuri, identified with modern Lahore in Pakistan, while Kusha founded Kasur.
