Management information system
FieldInformation systems, management
FocusOrganisational decision support

A management information system (MIS) is an information system used for decision-making, and for the coordination, control, analysis and visualisation of information in an organisation. MIS also denotes the academic study of how people, technology and organisations interrelate in operating and managing such systems.

Overview

MIS uses computer hardware, software, data and procedures, together with people, to produce regular reports and summaries drawn from transaction-processing data. Managers use these reports to monitor performance, spot trends and make tactical decisions, distinct from operational systems that process individual transactions.

Types

Related system types include transaction processing systems (TPS), decision support systems (DSS), executive information systems (EIS), enterprise resource planning (ERP) and customer relationship management (CRM) platforms. Modern MIS implementations typically emphasise databases, dashboards, and business intelligence analytics.
