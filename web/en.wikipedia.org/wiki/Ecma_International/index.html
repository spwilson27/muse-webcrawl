Ecma International

Ecma International is a nonprofit standards organisation for information and communication systems, headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland. Founded in 1961 as the European Computer Manufacturers Association (ECMA) to standardise computer systems in Europe, it adopted its present name in 1994 to reflect its global role. Ecma is known for a fast-track process that submits its standards to ISO and IEC for adoption as international standards.

Standards

Ecma's best-known standard is ECMA-262, the specification of ECMAScript, the language standardised from JavaScript, maintained by Technical Committee 39 (TC39), which also standardises JSON (ECMA-404). Other Ecma standards include C# (ECMA-334) and the Common Language Infrastructure (ECMA-335) developed with Microsoft, the Office Open XML formats (ECMA-376), and standards for magnetic tape, optical discs and data interchange dating back to the 1960s.

Organisation

Membership consists of companies and organisations with interests in standardisation; ordinary members vote at the General Assembly, and technical work is done in committees and task groups. Ecma cooperates closely with ISO/IEC JTC 1.
