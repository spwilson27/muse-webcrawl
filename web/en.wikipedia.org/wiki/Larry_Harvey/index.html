Larry Harvey

Larry Harvey (January 11, 1948 – April 28, 2018) was an American artist, philanthropist, and activist. He was the main co-founder of the Burning Man event, along with Jerry James, and was its executive director and chief philosophical architect.

Burning Man

In 1986 Harvey and James burned an 8-foot wooden effigy of a man on Baker Beach in San Francisco. The annual ritual grew rapidly, moved to Nevada's Black Rock Desert in 1990, and developed into a temporary city of tens of thousands of participants. Harvey articulated the event's philosophy in its Ten Principles, written in 2004, including radical inclusion, gifting, decommodification, and radical self-expression.

Death

Harvey died in San Francisco on April 28, 2018, following a stroke earlier that month.
