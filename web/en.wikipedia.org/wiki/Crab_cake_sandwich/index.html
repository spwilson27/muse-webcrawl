Crab cake sandwich

A crab cake sandwich is a sandwich of the American Mid-Atlantic, especially Maryland, in which a fried or broiled crab cake is served on a bun or between slices of bread, typically with lettuce, tomato, tartar sauce or remoulade.

Background

Maryland crab cakes are traditionally made from blue crab meat with minimal filler, seasoned — famously with Old Bay seasoning — and bound with egg and crumbs. Served as a sandwich, the crab cake becomes a portable meal long popular at Baltimore restaurants, boardwalks and seafood houses around the Chesapeake Bay.

Variations

Regional styles differ in filler and seasoning: "boardwalk" style uses more cracker crumbs, while restaurant-style cakes emphasise lump crab meat. Soft-shell crab sandwiches are a related Chesapeake speciality.
