Yuji Naka (中 裕司, Naka Yūji; born 17 September 1965) is a Japanese video game designer and programmer. Often credited as the co-creator of the Sonic the Hedgehog series, he was the lead programmer of the original Sonic the Hedgehog (1991) and the head of Sonic Team at Sega, where he produced titles including Nights into Dreams (1996), Burning Rangers (1998), ChuChu Rocket! (1999), and the Phantasy Star Online series.

Career

Naka joined Sega in 1984 as a programmer and worked on arcade and Master System titles before leading development of the Genesis/Mega Drive technical demonstration that became Sonic the Hedgehog, working with artist Naoto Ohshima and designer Hirokazu Yasuhara. As head of Sonic Team he oversaw the franchise through the Dreamcast era.

He left Sega in 2006 to found the studio Prope, which developed games such as Let's Tap and Ivy the Kiwi?. In 2018 he joined Square Enix, where he directed Balan Wonderworld (2021), which was a commercial and critical failure; he later sued Square Enix over his removal from the project. In 2022–2023 he pleaded guilty in an insider-trading case related to Square Enix stock, receiving a suspended sentence and fines.
