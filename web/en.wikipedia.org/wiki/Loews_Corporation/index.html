Loews Corporation
Founded: 1946
Headquarters: New York City, U.S.
Type: Public (NYSE: L)
Industry: Conglomerate
Founders: Laurence and Robert Tisch

Loews Corporation is an American diversified conglomerate headquartered in New York City. Founded by brothers Laurence and Robert Tisch, who began by buying a hotel in New Jersey in 1946 and built the Loews hotel and theatre interests into a vast holding company, it is controlled by the Tisch family and known for disciplined, value-oriented capital allocation.

Businesses

Loews' principal subsidiaries have included:

  • CNA Financial — commercial property and casualty insurance (majority owned).
  • Boardwalk Pipelines — natural gas pipelines and storage.
  • Loews Hotels & Co — hotels and resorts.
  • Altium Packaging — rigid plastic packaging.

Historically Loews owned the Loews Theatres cinema chain (sold in 1985 under antitrust pressure), the Lorillard tobacco company (spun off in 2008), the Bulova watch company and Diamond Offshore Drilling.
