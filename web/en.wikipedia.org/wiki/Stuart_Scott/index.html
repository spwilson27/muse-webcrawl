Stuart Scott

Stuart Orlando Scott (July 19, 1965 – January 4, 2015) was an American sportscaster and anchor on ESPN, most notably on SportsCenter. He was known for his energetic hip-hop style of broadcasting and his catchphrases, including "Boo-yah!" and "As cool as the other side of the pillow."

Career

Scott joined ESPN in 1993 for the launch of ESPN2, where he hosted SportsNight, and soon became a prominent SportsCenter anchor, often paired with Rich Eisen. He also hosted NBA and NFL coverage and game shows such as Stump the Schwab. His style brought elements of African-American culture and hip-hop vernacular into mainstream sports broadcasting and influenced a generation of sportscasters.

Illness and legacy

Scott was diagnosed with cancer in 2007 and continued broadcasting through repeated recurrences. At the 2014 ESPY Awards he received the Jimmy V Award for Perseverance and delivered a widely remembered speech: "When you die, it does not mean that you lose to cancer. You beat cancer by how you live, why you live, and in the manner in which you live." He died in January 2015 at age 49.
