Cauvery Water Management Authority

Formed: 2018
Headquarters: New Delhi, India
Parent: Ministry of Jal Shakti, Government of India

The Cauvery Water Management Authority (CWMA) is a body constituted by the Government of India in June 2018 under the Inter-State River Water Disputes Act, 1956, to implement the final award of the Cauvery Water Disputes Tribunal, as modified by the Supreme Court of India's judgment of February 2018 on the sharing of the waters of the Cauvery (Kaveri) river.

Background

The sharing of Cauvery waters between the basin states of Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala and the union territory of Puducherry has been disputed for more than a century. The tribunal's final award of 2007 allocated shares of the river's yield among the parties; on appeal, the Supreme Court in 2018 slightly increased Karnataka's share and directed the central government to frame a scheme to implement the award, leading to the creation of the Authority and an associated Cauvery Water Regulation Committee.

Functions

The Authority oversees the apportionment and release of water from reservoirs in the basin, including the Krishna Raja Sagara, Kabini, Mettur and Bhavani Sagar dams, in accordance with the prescribed monthly schedule, and takes decisions on releases during years of distress when inflows fall short. Its meetings, often contentious in deficit years, are attended by representatives of the central government and the party states.
