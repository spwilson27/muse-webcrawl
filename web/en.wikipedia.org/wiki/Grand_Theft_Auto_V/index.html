Grand Theft Auto V

Grand Theft Auto V is a 2013 action-adventure game developed by Rockstar North and published by Rockstar Games. It is the seventh main entry in the Grand Theft Auto series, following Grand Theft Auto IV (2008). It was released in September 2013 for PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360, in 2014 for PlayStation 4 and Xbox One, in 2015 for Windows, and in 2022 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.

Gameplay and setting

The game is set in the fictional state of San Andreas, centred on the city of Los Santos (Los Angeles) and the surrounding Blaine County. The single-player story follows three playable protagonists — retired bank robber Michael De Santa, street gangster Franklin Clinton, and drug dealer and gunrunner Trevor Philips — whose lives intertwine as they plan and execute a series of heists under pressure from corrupt government agencies and rival criminals. Players can switch between the three characters, explore in first- or third-person view, and take part in activities from stock trading to triathlons.

Reception

Grand Theft Auto V received universal acclaim and generated $800 million in worldwide revenue on its first day and $1 billion in three days, the fastest-selling entertainment product in history at the time. With more than 200 million copies sold, it is the second-best-selling video game of all time, behind Minecraft. Its multiplayer component, Grand Theft Auto Online, launched shortly after release.
