Breguet range equation (disambiguation)

Breguet range equation may refer to:

  • The range equation for jet aircraft, relating range to the logarithm of the ratio of initial to final weight, lift-to-drag ratio, specific fuel consumption, and speed.
  • The corresponding equation for propeller-driven aircraft, expressed in terms of propeller efficiency and power-specific fuel consumption.

Both forms are named after French aviation pioneer Louis Breguet, who derived them in the early 1920s. The equations are fundamental in aircraft design for estimating how far an aircraft can fly on a given amount of fuel.
