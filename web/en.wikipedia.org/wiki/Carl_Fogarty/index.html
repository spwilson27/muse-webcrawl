Carl George Fogarty (born 1 July 1965), often known as Foggy, is an English former motorcycle racer and one of the most successful Superbike World Championship riders of all time. He won the world title four times — in 1994, 1995, 1998 and 1999 — all with Ducati, and recorded 59 race wins, a record at the time of his retirement.

Career

Fogarty rose through British racing and endurance events, winning the Formula TT world title and the Endurance World Championship before joining the Superbike World Championship full-time. His fierce rivalry with riders such as Aaron Slight and his aggressive, charismatic style made him hugely popular with British fans. A broken arm sustained at Phillip Island in 2000 ended his racing career.

Later life

After retiring, Fogarty ran the Foggy Petronas Racing team and became a television personality, winning the fourteenth series of ITV's I'm a Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here! in 2014. He was appointed MBE for services to motorcycling.
