Sari, Iran

Sari (Persian: ساری) is a city in the Central District of Sari County, Mazandaran province, Iran, serving as capital of the province, the county, and the district. It lies in the northern part of Iran, between the northern slopes of the Alborz Mountains and the southern coast of the Caspian Sea.

Sari is one of the largest and most populous cities in northern Iran. Excavations in the Hutto cave present evidence for the existence of settlements around Sari as far back as the 7th millennium BCE. The city was the capital of Tabaristan in early Islamic times and later became an important regional centre.

Geography and climate

Sari is located in the plain between the Caspian Sea and the Alborz range. It has a humid subtropical climate with hot, humid summers and cool, damp winters, receiving abundant rainfall.

Demographics

At the time of the 2016 National Census, the population of Sari was about 309,820 people. The inhabitants speak the Mazandarani language as well as Persian.

Economy

The city is known for citrus fruit production, rice, and wood and paper industries. Sari is connected by railway and highway to Tehran and is a gateway to the Caspian coast resorts.
