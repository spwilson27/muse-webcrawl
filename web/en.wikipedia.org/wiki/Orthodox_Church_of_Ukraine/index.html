Orthodox Church of Ukraine

St Michael's Golden-Domed Monastery in Kyiv, seat of the church's primate.

The Orthodox Church of Ukraine (OCU) is an Eastern Orthodox church in Ukraine that was established by a unification council in Kyiv on 15 December 2018 and received a tomos of autocephaly (independence) from the Ecumenical Patriarchate of Constantinople in January 2019. Its primate is the Metropolitan of Kyiv and All Ukraine, a position held since 2019 by Epiphanius I.

Formation

The church was formed by the merger of the Ukrainian Orthodox Church – Kyiv Patriarchate, the Ukrainian Autocephalous Orthodox Church and some parishes of the Moscow Patriarchate church in Ukraine, following the Ecumenical Patriarchate's decision in October 2018 to revoke the 1686 transfer of the Kyiv metropolia to Moscow. The grant of autocephaly led the Russian Orthodox Church to break communion with Constantinople.

Recognition and membership

The OCU has been recognised as autocephalous by the Ecumenical Patriarchate, the Patriarchate of Alexandria, and the Churches of Greece and Cyprus; other Orthodox churches have not formally recognised it, making it a subject of inter-Orthodox dispute. Surveys after Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022 showed a large shift of Ukrainian Orthodox believers toward identifying with the OCU, and many parishes transferred to it from the Moscow-linked Ukrainian Orthodox Church.
