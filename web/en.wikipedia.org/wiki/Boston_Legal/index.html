Boston Legal

Boston Legal is an American legal comedy-drama television series created by David E. Kelley. It aired on ABC from October 3, 2004, to December 8, 2008, running for five seasons and 101 episodes. A spin-off of The Practice, it follows the lawyers of the Boston firm Crane, Poole & Schmidt.

Boston Legal
Genre: Legal comedy-drama
Created by: David E. Kelley
Seasons: 5 (101 episodes)
Original run: 2004–2008

Cast

James Spader stars as Alan Shore, a brilliant but ethically flexible litigator, and William Shatner as Denny Crane, the firm's legendary, eccentric name partner who constantly reminds everyone of his name. Candice Bergen plays managing partner Shirley Schmidt. The closing balcony scenes, in which Alan and Denny share cigars and Scotch, became the show's signature.

Style and themes

The series mixed courtroom drama with farce and fourth-wall-breaking humour, and it frequently built episodes around topical issues — gun control, capital punishment, gay marriage, and the Iraq War — with Alan Shore's impassioned closing arguments arguing directly to the audience.

Reception

Boston Legal was nominated for numerous Emmy Awards; Spader won three Emmys for playing Alan Shore (one for The Practice and two for Boston Legal), and Shatner won an Emmy and a Golden Globe for Denny Crane.
