Carolina parakeet

KingdomAnimalia
FamilyPsittacidae
GenusConuropsis
SpeciesC. carolinensis (extinct)

The Carolina parakeet (Conuropsis carolinensis) was a small, brightly coloured parrot native to the eastern and central United States. It was the only parrot species native to the eastern United States and one of only three parrot species native to the country.

Description and range

The bird was mostly green, with a yellow head and orange cheeks. It lived in old-growth forests along rivers and swamps from the Ohio Valley to the Gulf Coast, feeding on seeds, fruit and the seeds of cockleburs.

Extinction

Numbers collapsed through the nineteenth century owing to habitat destruction, hunting, collection for the feather trade and possibly introduced disease. The last known wild specimen was killed in Florida in 1904, and the last captive bird, Incas, died at the Cincinnati Zoo in 1918. The species was declared extinct in 1939.
