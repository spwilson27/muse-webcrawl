FIFA Ballon d'Or

The FIFA Ballon d'Or was an annual association football award presented by FIFA from 2010 to 2015, merging France Football's Ballon d'Or with the FIFA World Player of the Year award, honouring the male player deemed to have performed best over the previous calendar year.

FIFA Ballon d'Or
Presented by: FIFA
Years: 2010–2015
Most wins: Lionel Messi (4 in this era)

History

Voting combined national team captains and coaches with journalists. Lionel Messi won in 2010, 2011, 2012 and 2015; Cristiano Ronaldo won in 2013 and 2014. A women's award ran in parallel. After the partnership ended in 2016, France Football revived the standalone Ballon d'Or and FIFA created The Best FIFA Football Awards.
