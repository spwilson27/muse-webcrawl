Bolshoi Ballet

The Bolshoi Ballet is an internationally renowned classical ballet company based at the Bolshoi Theatre in Moscow, Russia. Founded in 1776, it is among the world's oldest and most prestigious ballet companies, noted for its dramatic style, large corps de ballet, and productions on a grand scale.

History

The company began with dancing classes for Moscow orphans in 1773 and first performed in 1776. It rose to prominence in the Soviet era under choreographers such as Alexander Gorsky and Yuri Grigorovich, whose full-evening versions of Swan Lake, The Nutcracker and Spartacus became signature works. Celebrated dancers associated with the Bolshoi include Galina Ulanova, Maya Plisetskaya, Vladimir Vasiliev and Svetlana Zakharova.

Today

With around 250 dancers, the Bolshoi is one of the largest ballet companies in the world. Its school, the Moscow State Academy of Choreography, trains dancers in the Russian (Vaganova-influenced Bolshoi) style.
