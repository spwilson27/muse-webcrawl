Festa del Tricolore

The Festa del Tricolore (Tricolour Day), officially the Giornata Nazionale della Bandiera (National Flag Day), is Italy's national flag day, celebrated every year on 7 January. It commemorates the adoption of the first tricolour flag as the national banner of an Italian sovereign state in 1797.

History

On 7 January 1797 the congress of the Cispadane Republic, meeting in Reggio Emilia, adopted the green, white and red tricolour as its flag. The design drew on the flags of the Italian legions and the Jacobin tradition, and its colours came to symbolise the ideals of the Risorgimento and Italian unification.

Celebrations

The day was formally established by law in 1996 and first celebrated for the flag's bicentenary in 1997. Official ceremonies are held in Reggio Emilia, where the Tricolour Museum preserves the flag's story, and in Rome at the Quirinal Palace, with a ceremonial changing of the guard and the display of the historic flag.
