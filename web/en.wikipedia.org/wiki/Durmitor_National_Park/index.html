Durmitor National Park is a national park in northwestern Montenegro, centred on the Durmitor mountain massif and including the canyon of the Tara River. Established in 1952, the park was inscribed on UNESCO's World Heritage List in 1980. It is the largest national park in Montenegro.

Geography

The Durmitor massif contains 48 peaks above 2,000 metres, the highest being Bobotov Kuk at 2,523 metres. The landscape was shaped by glaciers and is dotted with 18 glacial lakes, known locally as "mountain eyes", the largest and most visited being the Black Lake (Crno Jezero) near the town of Žabljak. The park also encompasses the Tara River Canyon, the deepest river canyon in Europe at up to about 1,300 metres, and canyons of the Sušica, Draga and Komarnica rivers.

Flora and fauna

The park contains extensive forests of black pine, including a renowned stand of ancient pines, along with beech, fir and spruce woods and rich alpine meadows with many endemic species. Large mammals include brown bears, wolves and chamois, and the park is an Important Bird Area with species such as the golden eagle.

Tourism

Žabljak, the highest town in the Balkans, is the park's main gateway, offering hiking, mountaineering, skiing in winter and white-water rafting on the Tara in summer.
