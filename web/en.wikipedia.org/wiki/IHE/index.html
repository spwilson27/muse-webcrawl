IHE

Integrating the Healthcare Enterprise

The IHE logo

IHE (Integrating the Healthcare Enterprise) is an initiative by healthcare professionals and industry, founded in 1997, to improve the way computer systems in healthcare share information. IHE promotes the coordinated use of established standards such as DICOM and HL7 to address specific clinical needs in support of optimal patient care.

Profiles and connectathons

IHE publishes Integration Profiles — implementation guides that specify how existing standards should be used together to solve a defined interoperability task, such as scheduling imaging workflows or cross-enterprise document sharing (XDS). Systems are tested at annual "Connectathon" events, where vendors verify interoperability against the profiles, and results are showcased at demonstrations such as the HIMSS conference.

Domains

Work is organised into domains including radiology, cardiology, laboratory, pathology, pharmacy, patient care devices and IT infrastructure, with regional and national deployment committees adapting the profiles around the world.
