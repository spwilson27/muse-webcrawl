The National League South, known as the Vanarama National League South for sponsorship reasons, is an English football league covering the southern part of England. It forms the sixth tier of the English football league system, alongside the National League North, and sits below the National League.

History

The league was founded in 2004 as the Conference South, when the Football Conference restructured its divisions below the national Conference National. It was renamed National League South in 2015 as part of the rebranding of the Football Conference as the National League.

Promotion and relegation

The champions are promoted automatically to the National League, with a second promotion place decided by play-offs involving the teams finishing second to seventh. The bottom clubs are relegated to the Premier Divisions of the Isthmian League or the Southern League, depending on geography. Clubs enter the FA Cup in the qualifying rounds and also compete in the FA Trophy.
