Fenway Park

Location: Boston, Massachusetts

Opened: April 20, 1912

Tenants: Boston Red Sox (MLB)

Fenway Park is a ballpark in Boston, Massachusetts, near Kenmore Square. Since 1912 it has been the home of the Boston Red Sox, the city's Major League Baseball franchise, and it is the oldest ballpark still in use in the league.

Features

The park is famed for its idiosyncratic dimensions and features, above all the "Green Monster", the 37-foot (11.3 m) left field wall built to compensate for the short distance to the boundary. Other quirks include the triangular centre field area known as "the Triangle", the lone red seat marking the longest home run hit in the park, and Pesky's Pole in right field. Seats were added atop the Green Monster in 2003.

History

Fenway opened days before the sinking of the Titanic and hosted its first World Series that same year. Despite periodic threats of replacement, a major renovation programme in the 2000s preserved the park, which was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 2012. Besides baseball, Fenway has hosted football, soccer, hockey and concerts.
