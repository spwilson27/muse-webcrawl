Son Heung-min (Korean: 손흥민; born 8 July 1992) is a South Korean professional footballer who plays as a forward. Widely regarded as one of the best Asian footballers of all time, he is known for his explosive pace, finishing with both feet, and link-up play.

Early life and career

Son was born in Chuncheon, Gangwon Province. His father, Son Woong-jung, is a former professional footballer who ran a football academy. At age 16, Son moved to Germany to join the youth academy of Hamburger SV, making his Bundesliga debut in 2010. In 2013 he transferred to Bayer Leverkusen for a club-record fee, where he played in the UEFA Champions League.

Tottenham Hotspur

In August 2015, Son signed for Tottenham Hotspur in the Premier League for around £22 million, becoming the most expensive Asian player in football history at the time. He formed a prolific attacking partnership with Harry Kane, and the pair set a Premier League record for goal combinations. Son won the Premier League Golden Boot in the 2021–22 season, sharing the award with Mohamed Salah — the first Asian player to win it. He was appointed club captain in 2023 following the departures of Hugo Lloris and Harry Kane.

International career

Son has captained the South Korea national team and represented his country at multiple FIFA World Cups and AFC Asian Cups. He won a gold medal at the 2018 Asian Games, which earned him exemption from mandatory military service.

Playing style

Son is a versatile forward able to play across the front line. His two-footedness, speed in transition, and long-range shooting make him a constant threat on the counter-attack. He is also praised for his work rate and professionalism.
