Apparatjik (pronounced "apparat-chick") is a multi-media musical and artistic supergroup formed in 2008. Its members are bassist Guy Berryman of Coldplay, guitarist and keyboardist Magne Furuholmen of A-ha, singer and guitarist Jonas Bjerre of the Danish band Mew, and producer Martin Terefe. The band's name derives from the Russian word apparatchik, historically used for a functionary of the Communist Party apparatus, and the group performs in distinctive mirrored cube helmets and uniforms as part of an integrated visual identity.

Apparatjik performing live, wearing the cube helmets central to the project's visual identity.

Formation and early work

The four musicians first collaborated on "Ferreting", a track recorded for the BBC charity programme Amazon with Bruce Parry in 2008. Encouraged by the session, they continued recording in Furuholmen's studio in Oslo and developed an approach combining music with visual art, interactive web projects, and installations. Rather than operating as a conventional band, Apparatjik presents itself as an artistic collective, releasing material through bespoke formats and exhibitions.

Releases

The group's debut album, We Are Here, was released in 2010, preceded by a period in which individual tracks were issued through the band's website and a four-track EP, 4 Can Keep a Secret If 3 of Them Are Dead. Their second album, Square Peg in a Round Hole, first appeared in 2011 as an iPad app and in a drip-feed of fan-influenced drafts under the project name "Apparatjik Presents: Every Part of the Puzzle", before a standard release in 2012. The band has also created music for installations and exhibitions, including work connected with Furuholmen's art projects and the 2011 exhibition at the Neue Nationalgalerie in Berlin, where they performed inside a giant cube.

Art projects

Apparatjik's activities extend beyond recordings into participatory art. "The Apparatjik Light Space Modulator", a large-scale installation produced with light artist (and grandson of László Moholy-Nagy's collaborator tradition) was exhibited in Berlin. The collective also launched "Agricultural Module" experiments and other web-based participatory pieces inviting audiences to contribute samples, photographs, and data that the group incorporated into performances.

Discography

Studio albums: We Are Here (2010); Square Peg in a Round Hole (2012). Notable singles include "Electric Eye", "Datapop", and "Look Kids".
