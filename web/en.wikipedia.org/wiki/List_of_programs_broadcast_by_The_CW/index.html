List of programs broadcast by The CW

The CW is an American broadcast television network launched in 2006 from the merger of The WB and UPN. It became known for teen dramas, the Arrowverse superhero franchise, and supernatural series aimed at younger audiences.

Notable original programming

  • Drama: Supernatural, The Vampire Diaries and its spin-offs, Gossip Girl, One Tree Hill (from The WB), Riverdale, Jane the Virgin, Crazy Ex-Girlfriend.
  • Arrowverse: Arrow, The Flash, Supergirl, Legends of Tomorrow, Black Lightning, Batwoman, Superman & Lois.
  • Other: Smallville (inherited from The WB), Gilmore Girls (final season), America's Next Top Model, Whose Line Is It Anyway?, Walker, All American, The 100, iZombie, Nikita, Hart of Dixie, Reign.

Ownership changes

Originally a joint venture of CBS and Warner Bros., majority control of The CW passed to Nexstar Media Group in 2022, after which the network shifted toward broader, lower-cost programming including sports and acquired series.
