List of films based on DC Comics

DC Comics characters such as Superman, Batman and Wonder Woman have headlined films since the 1940s serials. Warner Bros. has been the principal distributor. The modern era includes the DC Extended Universe (DCEU, 2013–2023) and the rebooted DC Universe (DCU) launched under James Gunn and Peter Safran.
