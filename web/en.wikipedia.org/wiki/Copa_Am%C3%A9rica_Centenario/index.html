The Copa América Centenario was a special edition of the Copa América, held in the United States in June 2016 to celebrate the centenary of CONMEBOL and of the tournament itself. It expanded the field to sixteen teams: the ten South American nations plus six from CONCACAF.

Tournament

Matches were played in ten US cities. Chile won the tournament, defeating Argentina on penalties in the final at MetLife Stadium after a 0–0 draw — a repeat of the 2015 final result. Following the defeat, Lionel Messi briefly announced his retirement from international football before returning.

Significance

It was the first Copa América held outside South America and drew record attendances for the competition in the United States. Eduardo Vargas of Chile was top scorer with six goals.
