Penélope Cruz


Born: Penélope Cruz Sánchez, 28 April 1974, Alcobendas, Madrid, Spain
Occupation: Actress
Spouse: Javier Bardem (m. 2010)

Penélope Cruz Sánchez (born 28 April 1974) is a Spanish actress. One of the most acclaimed performers of her generation, she is the first Spanish actress to win an Academy Award, receiving the Oscar for Best Supporting Actress for Vicky Cristina Barcelona (2008).

Career

Cruz trained as a classical ballet dancer before turning to acting, debuting in Jamón Jamón (1992) opposite Javier Bardem. She became a muse of director Pedro Almodóvar, starring in Live Flesh (1997), All About My Mother (1999), Volver (2006)—which earned her an Oscar nomination—Broken Embraces (2009), Pain and Glory (2019), and Parallel Mothers (2021), for which she won the Volpi Cup at Venice and another Academy Award nomination.

In Hollywood she appeared in Blow (2001), Vanilla Sky (2001), Sahara (2005), the Pirates of the Caribbean film On Stranger Tides (2011), and Murder on the Orient Express (2017). On television she won praise and an Emmy nomination for playing Donatella Versace in The Assassination of Gianni Versace: American Crime Story (2018).

Personal life

Cruz married Javier Bardem in 2010; they have two children. She is involved in charitable work, including volunteering with Mother Teresa's missionaries in Calcutta early in her career, and has a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame.
