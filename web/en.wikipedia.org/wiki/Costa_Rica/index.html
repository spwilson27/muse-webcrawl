Costa Rica

[Image: Rainforest canopy and a hanging bridge in Costa Rica]
Rainforest canopy and a hanging bridge in Costa Rica

Costa Rica, officially the Republic of Costa Rica, is a country in Central America bordered by Nicaragua and Panama, with coastlines on the Caribbean Sea and the Pacific Ocean. Its population is about 5.1 million and its capital is San José.

Costa Rica abolished its army in 1948 and channels spending into education, health and conservation; it is famous for ecotourism and biodiversity — about 5 percent of the world's species on 0.03 percent of its land — with more than a quarter of its territory protected in national parks such as Corcovado and Tortuguero. The country runs almost entirely on renewable electricity in most years. Costa Ricans use the phrase 'pura vida' ('pure life') as a national motto. In contrast to its neighbours, Costa Rica has enjoyed stable democracy since 1949.
