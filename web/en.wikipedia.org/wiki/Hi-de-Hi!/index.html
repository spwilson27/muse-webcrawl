Hi-de-Hi! is a British sitcom written by Jimmy Perry and David Croft, broadcast on BBC1 from 1981 to 1988. Set in 1959–1960 at Maplins, a fictional holiday camp in Crimpton-on-Sea, Essex, it follows the entertainments staff — the Yellowcoats — led by entertainments manager Jeffrey Fairbrother (Simon Cadell) and featuring Su Pollard as Peggy Ollerenshaw, whose catchphrase greeting gave the show its title.

Background

Perry drew on his own experience as a Butlin's Redcoat. The show won a BAFTA for Best Comedy Series in 1984 and also starred Paul Shane, Ruth Madoc and Jeffrey Holland.

Legacy

It was adapted for the stage and remains a staple of British comedy repeats.
