Google Bard

Bard was a generative artificial intelligence chatbot developed by Google. Based initially on the LaMDA family of large language models and later on PaLM 2 and Gemini, it was announced in February 2023 in response to the rise of OpenAI's ChatGPT and released in March 2023. In February 2024, Bard was unified under the Gemini brand.

Background and launch

Google employees had nicknamed Bard the "code red" response to ChatGPT. CEO Sundar Pichai announced Bard on February 6, 2023; an error in its promotional demonstration — Bard incorrectly claimed the James Webb Space Telescope took the first picture of an exoplanet — coincided with a sharp fall in Alphabet's share price. Bard opened to waitlisted users in the United States and United Kingdom on March 21, 2023.

Development

Bard was upgraded to the PaLM 2 model at Google I/O in May 2023, gaining coding abilities, more languages, and multimodal features, and expanded to over 180 countries; EU release was delayed over privacy concerns. In December 2023 Google announced the Gemini model family, and Bard's most advanced tier moved to Gemini Ultra. On February 8, 2024, Bard was renamed Gemini, ending the Bard name after less than a year.
