Bandai Co., Ltd. is a Japanese toy manufacturer and distributor, a subsidiary of Bandai Namco Holdings. Founded in 1950 by Naoharu Yamashina, Bandai is one of the world's largest toy companies, best known for Gunpla plastic model kits of the Gundam franchise, the Tamagotchi virtual pet, and toys for franchises such as Power Rangers, Dragon Ball, Digimon and Kamen Rider.

History

Bandai began in 1950 in Asakusa, Tokyo, as Bandaiya, selling celluloid toys and rubber products. Through the 1960s and 1970s it expanded into die-cast vehicles (under the Popy subsidiary) and character merchandise tied to television anime and tokusatsu series. The 1978 Chogokin die-cast robot toys became iconic. In 1980 Bandai released the first 1/144 Gundam plastic model, launching the Gunpla phenomenon that has sold hundreds of millions of kits.

In 1996 Bandai launched the Tamagotchi, which became a global craze and sold tens of millions of units. In 2005, Bandai merged with video game company Namco to form Namco Bandai Holdings (now Bandai Namco Holdings), while Bandai continued as the group's toy and hobby company. Its Bandai Spirits division serves adult collectors with premium figures and model kits.

Products and franchises

Bandai produces toys for many of Japan's most durable character licences, including Mobile Suit Gundam, Ultraman, Kamen Rider, Super Sentai (adapted in the West as Power Rangers), Dragon Ball, One Piece, Digimon and Sailor Moon, as well as capsule toys (gashapon), trading cards, candy toys, apparel and model kits. The company is credited with pioneering the "character business" model of media-mix merchandising.
