Alnitak is a triple star system in the constellation of Orion. Together with Alnilam and Mintaka, it forms Orion's Belt, the three bright stars in a row that mark the hunter's waist. Its traditional name derives from the Arabic an-niṭāq, "the girdle". Alnitak is the easternmost of the three belt stars.

Components

The primary, Alnitak Aa, is a hot blue supergiant of spectral type O9.5, one of the brightest O-type stars in the night sky, with a close companion Ab that orbits it. A more distant B-type companion, Alnitak B, orbits the inner pair with a period of about 1,500 years. The system lies roughly 1,200 light-years away, though published distance estimates vary.

Surroundings

Alnitak sits in a rich star-forming region. The emission nebula NGC 2024 (the Flame Nebula) lies just east of the star, and the famous dark Horsehead Nebula (Barnard 33) is silhouetted against the emission nebula IC 434 a little to the south. The glare of Alnitak makes observing these neighbours a challenge for amateur astronomers.
