Covenant (Halo)

The Covenant is a fictional theocratic alliance of alien species in the Halo science fiction franchise, created by Bungie and later developed by 343 Industries. The Covenant serves as the principal antagonist of the original Halo trilogy, waging a genocidal war against humanity in the twenty-sixth century in the belief that this is the will of their gods, the Forerunners.

Structure and society

The Covenant is a caste-based empire governed by the High Council and led by the three Hierarchs — the Prophets of Truth, Mercy, and Regret. Its member species include the San'Shyuum (Prophets), who provide religious and political leadership; the Sangheili (Elites), long the military arm of the Covenant; the Jiralhanae (Brutes); the Unggoy (Grunts); the Kig-Yar (Jackals); the Yanme'e (Drones); the Mgalekgolo (Hunters); and the Huragok (Engineers), a species of biological constructs who maintain Covenant technology. Covenant society venerates the ancient Forerunners as gods and seeks to activate the Halo rings, which the Prophets falsely teach will begin the "Great Journey" to godhood.

Role in the series

First appearing in Halo: Combat Evolved (2001), the Covenant had by 2525 made first contact with humanity and begun a decades-long campaign of extermination, glassing human colony worlds from orbit. The later games depict the Covenant's internal collapse: the revelation that the Halo rings are weapons of mass destruction, the replacement of the Elites by the Brutes as the Prophets' guards — which triggers a civil war known as the Great Schism — and the alliance between the Arbiter's separatist faction and humanity. After the war, Covenant successor factions, including the Banished and the Storm Covenant, appear in later media.

Reception

The Covenant has been praised for the depth of its fictional culture and for the way subsequent games and novels complicated its initially villainous portrayal, particularly through the character of the Arbiter. Its member species, especially the Elites and Grunts, became some of the most recognisable elements of the franchise.
