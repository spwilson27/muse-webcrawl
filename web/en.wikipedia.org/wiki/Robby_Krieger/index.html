Robert Alan Krieger (born January 8, 1946) is an American guitarist and songwriter best known as the guitarist of the rock band the Doors. Krieger wrote or co-wrote many of the band's best-known songs, including "Light My Fire", "Love Me Two Times", "Touch Me" and "Love Her Madly".

Early life and the Doors

Born in Los Angeles, Krieger first studied flamenco and played a nylon-string guitar before moving to electric guitar, influenced by blues, jazz and Indian music. In 1965 he joined the band formed by Jim Morrison and Ray Manzarek, with John Densmore on drums. His fingerstyle playing — he rarely used a pick — and his slide guitar work gave the Doors a distinctive sound. "Light My Fire", largely Krieger's composition, became the band's breakthrough hit in 1967.

After the Doors

After Morrison's death in 1971, Krieger recorded two further albums with the surviving Doors, then formed the Butts Band, released solo jazz-fusion albums, and later toured with Manzarek in Manzarek–Krieger. He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame with the Doors in 1993. In 2021 he published the memoir Set the Night on Fire.
