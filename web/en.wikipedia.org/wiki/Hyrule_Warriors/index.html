Hyrule Warriors

Hyrule Warriors is a hack-and-slash action video game developed by Omega Force and Team Ninja and published by Nintendo and Koei Tecmo for the Wii U in 2014. It is a crossover between Nintendo's The Legend of Zelda series and Koei Tecmo's Dynasty Warriors series, combining large-scale battles against hundreds of enemies with characters, settings and music from Zelda.

Gameplay

Players control Link and other Zelda characters on open battlefields, capturing keeps, completing objectives and defeating waves of enemies with combo attacks, special "Musou" attacks and familiar items such as bombs and the Hookshot. Materials collected in battle are used to craft badges and upgrade weapons.

Versions

An expanded Nintendo 3DS port, Hyrule Warriors Legends (2016), added characters from The Wind Waker and Link's Awakening. A definitive edition combining all downloadable content was released for the Nintendo Switch in 2018. A prequel, Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity (2020), applied the formula to the world of Breath of the Wild.

Reception

The game received generally favourable reviews, with critics praising its fan service and combat but noting repetitive mission design. It sold over a million copies on Wii U, among the platform's better-performing third-party titles.
