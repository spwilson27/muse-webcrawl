Humble Pie are an English rock band formed in Moreton, Essex, in 1969 by guitarist and singer Steve Marriott, formerly of the Small Faces, together with Peter Frampton, Greg Ridley and Jerry Shirley. They were one of the first supergroups and achieved major success in the United States in the early 1970s.

History

The band's early albums combined folk, blues and rock. After Frampton left in 1971, Humble Pie adopted a harder sound; the live album Performance Rockin' the Fillmore (1971) and Smokin' (1972) became gold records in America, and the single "30 Days in the Hole" became a signature song. The original band split in 1975; Marriott died in a house fire in 1991.

Legacy

Humble Pie influenced hard rock and heavy metal bands with their powerful live performances and Marriott's soulful vocals. Frampton went on to solo stardom with Frampton Comes Alive!
