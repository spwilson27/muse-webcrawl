Jerusalem is a city in the Southern Levant, among the oldest cities in the world and holy to the three Abrahamic religions: Judaism (site of the ancient Temples, the Western Wall), Christianity (the Church of the Holy Sepulchre, site of Jesus's crucifixion and resurrection in Christian belief) and Islam (the Al-Aqsa Mosque compound / Haram al-Sharif with the Dome of the Rock, from which Muhammad is believed to have ascended to heaven).

Its status is one of the most contested issues in the Israeli–Palestinian conflict: Israel claims it as its (undivided) capital and houses its government there, while Palestinians claim East Jerusalem for their future state; most states keep embassies in Tel Aviv and treat the final status as subject to negotiation (the US moved its embassy in 2018). The walled Old City is divided into Jewish, Christian, Muslim and Armenian quarters and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Across history Jerusalem has been destroyed at least twice, besieged and captured dozens of times — by Egyptians, Babylonians, Persians, Greeks, Romans, Arabs, Crusaders (1099), Saladin (1187), Ottomans (1517) and the British (1917).
