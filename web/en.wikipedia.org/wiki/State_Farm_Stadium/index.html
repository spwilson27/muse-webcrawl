State Farm Stadium is a multi-purpose stadium in Glendale, Arizona, United States, west of Phoenix. It is the home of the Arizona Cardinals of the National Football League (NFL) and the annual Fiesta Bowl. It was known as University of Phoenix Stadium from 2006 to 2018.

Design

The stadium opened on August 1, 2006, after three years of construction. It is known for its retractable roof and its unique retractable playing field: the natural grass surface sits in an enormous tray that rolls out of the stadium to grow in the Arizona sun, one of the first such systems in North America. The exterior design, by architect Peter Eisenman together with HOK Sport (now Populous), evokes a barrel cactus and a coiled snake.

Major events

The stadium has hosted Super Bowls XLII (2008), XLIX (2015) and LVII (2023), the 2017 NCAA Men's Final Four, the College Football Playoff National Championship in 2016, and numerous concerts and soccer matches.
