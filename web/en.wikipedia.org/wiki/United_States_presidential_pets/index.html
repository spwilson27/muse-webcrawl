List of pets of United States presidents

Most United States presidents have kept pets while in office, or pets have been part of their families. Only James K. Polk, Andrew Johnson and Donald Trump (in his first term) did not have any presidential pets while in office; Johnson, however, did care for some mice he found in his bedroom.

History

Early presidents kept farm and working animals at the White House: George Washington had hounds and horses; John Adams kept horses in the first White House stables; Thomas Jefferson kept mockingbirds and two bear cubs given by Zebulon Pike. Abraham Lincoln's family kept dogs, cats, goats (Nanny and Nanko) and a turkey pardoned from Thanksgiving dinner, an origin of the modern turkey pardon tradition.

Notable pets

Famous presidential pets include Theodore Roosevelt's menagerie (including a one-legged rooster and a pony named Algonquin), Calvin Coolidge's raccoons Rebecca and Reuben and his collie Rob Roy, Franklin D. Roosevelt's Scottish terrier Fala, George H. W. Bush's spaniel Millie, Bill Clinton's cat Socks and dog Buddy, George W. Bush's Scottish terriers Barney and Miss Beazley, Barack Obama's Portuguese water dogs Bo and Sunny, and Joe Biden's German shepherds Champ and Major and cat Willow.
