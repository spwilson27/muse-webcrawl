Beijing West railway station

Beijing West railway station (Chinese: 北京西站), commonly referred to as Beijing West or Xike, is a major railway station in Fengtai District, Beijing, China. Opened in 1996, it was for a time the largest railway station in Asia, covering about 510,000 square metres, and it remains one of the busiest passenger stations in China, serving conventional and high-speed trains to western and southern destinations.

Services

The station is the Beijing terminus of the Beijing–Guangzhou railway, the Beijing–Kowloon (Jingjiu) railway — including through trains to Hong Kong — and the Beijing–Guangzhou high-speed railway, with services to cities such as Xi'an, Chengdu, Chongqing, Wuhan, Guangzhou and Shenzhen. It was also the starting point of trains to Lhasa on the Qinghai–Tibet railway. The underground Beijing West–Beijing station link, opened in 2015, allows through running across the city.

Transport connections

The station is served by Lines 7 and 9 of the Beijing Subway, which opened to the station in 2011 and 2014 respectively, as well as by numerous bus routes and long-distance coaches. Its huge scale and crowds, especially during the Spring Festival travel rush (Chunyun), made it a symbol of China's mass rail travel, and its opening helped shift traffic away from the older Beijing railway station.
