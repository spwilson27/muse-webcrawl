European Mobility Week
Dates16–22 September (annually)
Launched2002
OrganiserEuropean Commission
FeaturesCar-Free Day (22 September)

European Mobility Week is an annual awareness-raising campaign on sustainable urban mobility organised by the European Commission. Held every year from 16 to 22 September, it encourages towns and cities across Europe to introduce and test sustainable transport measures and invite people to try alternatives to private car use.

Activities

Participating municipalities organise activities such as car-free zones, cycling events, public transport promotion and walking tours. The week culminates in the well-known "In Town Without My Car!" car-free day on 22 September. Each edition has a theme, such as "Save Energy" (2023) and "Shared Public Space" (2024).

Participation

Thousands of towns and cities in over 40 countries take part each year, and the European Commission presents the EUROPEANMOBILITYWEEK Award to local authorities demonstrating excellence in sustainable mobility planning and campaigning.
