Anser (bird)

Anser is a genus of geese in the family Anatidae, often called the grey geese. It includes the greylag goose, the ancestor of most domestic geese, as well as the pink-footed goose, greater and lesser white-fronted geese, bean geese, bar-headed goose and swan goose in classifications that retain it within Anser.

Distribution and behaviour

Species breed mainly across northern Eurasia, with some extending into Central and East Asia, and migrate to temperate wintering grounds in Europe and Asia. They are strong fliers, form long-lived pair bonds and graze on grasses, sedges and agricultural crops. Flocks often use traditional migration routes and staging wetlands.

Taxonomy

The boundaries of the genus and the treatment of the bean goose complex have changed as genetic studies refined relationships among grey geese. Some authorities place the swan goose in the separate genus Cygnopsis, but it is frequently included in Anser in modern lists.
