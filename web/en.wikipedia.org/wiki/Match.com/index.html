Match.com

Match.com is an online dating service and website. It was launched on April 21, 1995, and is owned by Match Group, which also owns other dating properties such as Tinder, Hinge, OkCupid and PlentyOfFish. Match.com is headquartered in Dallas, Texas, and is one of the oldest and most widely recognized online dating services in the world.

History

Match.com was founded by Gary Kremen and Peng T. Ong and was originally launched in 1993 as Electric Classifieds before the first public version appeared in 1995. It was one of the earliest web-based dating services, predating the widespread adoption of social networking. The company went through several ownership changes: it was acquired by Cendant in the late 1990s and later sold to Ticketmaster Online-Citysearch (later IAC). Under IAC, Match.com became a cornerstone of a larger dating business that eventually consolidated into Match Group.

Over the years Match.com acquired or launched several related services, and in 2010 it acquired OkCupid. IAC later spun off Match Group as a public company in 2015.

Service

Match.com operates on a subscription model. Users create profiles with photos and biographical information, search for potential partners by criteria such as location, age, interests and lifestyle, and communicate with other members through messages and other interactive features. The service is available internationally in more than 190 countries and multiple languages.

Demographics and impact

Match.com has claimed credit for helping millions of users start relationships and marriages. It is frequently cited in academic research on online dating, including studies of communication patterns and the economics of dating markets.
