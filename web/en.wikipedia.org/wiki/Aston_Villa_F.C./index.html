Aston Villa Football Club is a professional football club based in Aston, Birmingham, England. Founded in 1874, the club has played at Villa Park since 1897. Aston Villa is one of England's oldest and most successful clubs: a founding member of the Football League in 1888 and of the Premier League in 1992, it has won seven league titles and seven FA Cups.

History

Villa dominated English football in the Victorian era under the influence of William McGregor, the club director who founded the Football League. The club won the Double in 1896–97. After decades of mixed fortunes, Villa enjoyed a golden period under manager Ron Saunders, winning the league title in 1980–81 and then, under Tony Barton, the European Cup in 1982, beating Bayern Munich 1–0 in Rotterdam — one of only a handful of English clubs to win Europe's premier trophy.

Recent years

The club was relegated from the Premier League in 2016, returned in 2019, and under manager Unai Emery (appointed 2022) qualified for the UEFA Champions League in 2024 for the first time in the competition's modern era. Villa's main rivalry is the Second City derby with Birmingham City.

Honours

Major honours include seven First Division/Premier League-era league titles (most recently 1980–81), seven FA Cups, five League Cups, and the 1982 European Cup and European Super Cup.
