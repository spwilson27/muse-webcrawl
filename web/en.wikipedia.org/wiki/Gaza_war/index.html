Gaza war
Date7 October 2023 – present (with ceasefires in Nov 2023 and from Jan 2025, and a further ceasefire agreement in Oct 2025)
LocationGaza Strip and Israel; spillover to Lebanon, the West Bank, Yemen and Iran
BelligerentsIsrael vs Hamas and allied Palestinian armed groups
Casus belliThe Hamas-led attack on southern Israel of 7 October 2023

The Gaza war is an armed conflict between Israel and Hamas-led Palestinian militant groups in the Gaza Strip that began on 7 October 2023, when Hamas and allied groups launched a surprise attack on southern Israel, killing about 1,200 people and taking some 250 hostages. Israel responded with a large-scale bombing campaign and a ground invasion of Gaza aimed at destroying Hamas and freeing the hostages.

Course of the war

The war caused mass casualties and displacement in Gaza, the destruction of much of the territory's housing and infrastructure, and a severe humanitarian crisis. A week-long ceasefire in late November 2023 exchanged hostages for prisoners. Fighting spread regionally, including an Israel–Hezbollah conflict in Lebanon in 2024 and direct exchanges between Israel and Iran. A second ceasefire agreed in January 2025 included staged hostage and prisoner releases but collapsed in March 2025 before a further agreement in October 2025, under which remaining living hostages were released.

Casualties and international response

The war killed tens of thousands of Palestinians in Gaza, according to the Gaza Health Ministry, and displaced most of the population. It drew worldwide protests, diplomatic crises, and legal proceedings, including a South African genocide case against Israel at the International Court of Justice, which Israel rejects, and International Criminal Court arrest warrants. Casualty figures, legal characterisations and responsibility remain widely contested.

Background

The conflict followed decades of Israeli–Palestinian conflict, Hamas's 2007 takeover of Gaza, and Israel's subsequent blockade of the Strip, punctuated by repeated earlier rounds of fighting in 2008–09, 2012, 2014 and 2021.
