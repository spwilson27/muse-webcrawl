Bayraktar Akıncı

Bayraktar Akıncı
Manufacturer: Baykar, Turkey
Type: UCAV (combat drone)
First flight: 6 December 2019
Status: In service

The Bayraktar Akıncı is a high-altitude long-endurance unmanned combat aerial vehicle developed by the Turkish company Baykar. Larger and more capable than the Bayraktar TB2, it can carry air-to-air and air-to-ground weapons and advanced sensors.

Development

Akıncı first flew in December 2019 and entered Turkish service in 2021. It uses turboprop engines, has a satellite communications link and an AESA radar.

Operational use

The type has been exported to several countries and used by Turkish forces in operations, and supplied to Ukraine.

Specifications

With a wingspan of about 20 metres and a payload of around 1,350 kg, Akıncı can carry cruise missiles, guided bombs and air-to-air missiles on internal and external stations.
