Möhne Reservoir

The Möhne Reservoir, or Lake Möhne (German: Möhnesee), is an artificial lake in North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany, formed by damming the Möhne river. The dam was built between 1908 and 1913 to help regulate water flow for the Ruhr region.

On the night of 16–17 May 1943, the dam was breached by RAF 617 Squadron in Operation Chastise, using bouncing bombs designed by Barnes Wallis. The resulting flood killed around 1,600 people. The dam was rebuilt the same year.

Today

The reservoir is a major recreation area and nature reserve, important for water supply, hydroelectric power, and overwintering birds.
