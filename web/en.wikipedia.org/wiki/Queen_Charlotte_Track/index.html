Queen Charlotte Track

Length~73.5 km (45.7 mi)
LocationMarlborough Sounds, New Zealand
TrailheadsShip Cove; Anakiwa
UseHiking, mountain biking

The Queen Charlotte Track is a 73.5 km (45.7 mi) hiking and mountain biking track in the Marlborough Sounds of the South Island of New Zealand. It runs from Ship Cove (Meretoto) in Queen Charlotte Sound / Tōtaranui to Anakiwa, near the head of the sound, following ridgelines and coastal forest with views over the bays of Queen Charlotte and Kenepuru Sounds.

Description

The track traverses a mixture of Department of Conservation land and private land (a track pass is required for private sections). Walkers typically take three to five days, staying at campsites, lodges, and resorts accessible by water taxi from Picton; luggage transfer by boat is a distinctive feature. Much of the route passes through regenerating and original native bush with abundant birdlife, including weka, bellbirds, and tūī.

Ship Cove, the start, was a favoured anchorage of Captain James Cook, who visited five times in the 1770s. Mountain biking is permitted year-round on most of the track, with a seasonal restriction on the Ship Cove to Punga Cove section during peak walking season.
