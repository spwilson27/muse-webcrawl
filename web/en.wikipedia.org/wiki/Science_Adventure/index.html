Science Adventure (科学アドベンチャー, Kagaku Adobenchā) is a Japanese series of science fiction visual novel video games developed by Mages (formerly 5pb.) in collaboration with Nitroplus, and created by Chiyomaru Shikura. The games share a near-future setting in which scientific concepts and conspiracy theories are central to the plot, and are connected by overlapping organisations, terminology and a shared timeline.

Games

  • Chaos;Head (2008) — set in Shibuya; a reclusive student witnesses a series of bizarre murders, the "New Generation Madness".
  • Steins;Gate (2009) — set in Akihabara; a group of students accidentally invent a means of sending messages into the past, attracting the attention of SERN. It became the series' breakthrough success.
  • Robotics;Notes (2012) — set on Tanegashima; a school robotics club builds a giant robot amid a wider conspiracy.
  • Chaos;Child (2014) — a sequel to Chaos;Head following a new generation of killings in Shibuya.
  • Steins;Gate 0 (2015) — a midquel to Steins;Gate exploring the worldline in which its protagonist failed.
  • Anonymous;Code (2023) — centred on hacking and a simulated Earth used to avert catastrophe.

Common elements

Entries feature recurring fictional organisations such as the Committee of 300, and in-universe concepts including worldlines and Reading Steiner. Gameplay typically centres on a "trigger" system unique to each title — such as the phone trigger in Steins;Gate — through which the player's choices alter the branching story. Each game has received anime, manga and light novel adaptations; the 2011 Steins;Gate anime is widely acclaimed.
