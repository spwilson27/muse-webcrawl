Chicken Run: Dawn of the Nugget is a 2023 British stop-motion animated comedy film produced by Aardman Animations and released by Netflix. It is the sequel to Chicken Run (2000), the highest-grossing stop-motion animated film of all time, arriving 23 years later.

Plot

After escaping Tweedy's farm, Ginger and Rocky live in an island sanctuary where their daughter Molly hatches. The adventurous Molly ventures to the mainland and is captured by Fun-Land Farms, a high-tech poultry factory run by Mrs Tweedy and Dr. Fry, which turns chickens into nuggets by fitting them with mind-controlling collars. Ginger must do the opposite of the first film: break into a farm to rescue her daughter and the flock.

Cast and production

Thandiwe Newton voices Ginger and Zachary Levi voices Rocky, with Bella Ramsey as Molly; Jane Horrocks, Imelda Staunton, and Lynn Ferguson return from the original. Miranda Richardson reprises Mrs Tweedy. The film was directed by Sam Fell and animated at Aardman's Bristol studios in the studio's signature claymation style.

Reception

The film received generally positive reviews for its animation and humour, and was nominated for awards including at the BAFTAs in the animated film category.
