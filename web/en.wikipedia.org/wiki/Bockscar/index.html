Type: Boeing B-29 Superfortress

Known for: Dropping the Fat Man bomb on Nagasaki, 9 August 1945

Bockscar, sometimes called Bock's Car, is the Boeing B-29 Superfortress that dropped the Fat Man nuclear weapon on Nagasaki, Japan, on 9 August 1945, the second and final nuclear attack of the Second World War.

The Nagasaki mission

Flown by Major Charles W. Sweeney, Bockscar's primary target was Kokura, but cloud cover and smoke forced diversion to the secondary target, Nagasaki, where the bomb was dropped at 11:02 local time. Low on fuel, the aircraft landed at Yontan Airfield, Okinawa.

Preservation

Bockscar is preserved at the National Museum of the United States Air Force near Dayton, Ohio. Its name is a pun on "boxcar", after its regular commander Captain Frederick C. Bock. See also Enola Gay.
