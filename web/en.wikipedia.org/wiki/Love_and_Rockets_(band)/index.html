Love and Rockets is an English alternative rock band formed in 1985 by former Bauhaus members Daniel Ash (vocals, guitar), David J (bass, vocals) and Kevin Haskins (drums), taking their name from the comic book series Love and Rockets by Los Bros Hernandez. The band fused psychedelia, glam rock, folk and electronic influences and was a formative act in alternative rock.

History

After Bauhaus split in 1983 and the short-lived Tones on Tail project ended, the three musicians regrouped as Love and Rockets. Their debut album Seventh Dream of Teenage Heaven (1985) established a shimmering psychedelic sound. Singles such as "Ball of Confusion" (a Temptations cover), "No New Tale to Tell" and "Yin and Yang (The Flowerpot Man)" brought college-radio success.

Their biggest hit came in 1989 with "So Alive", from their self-titled fourth album, which reached No. 3 on the US Billboard Hot 100. Later albums explored electronics (Sweet F.A., 1996) and the band split in 1999. They reunited for festival appearances in 2007–2009 and again toured in 2023.
