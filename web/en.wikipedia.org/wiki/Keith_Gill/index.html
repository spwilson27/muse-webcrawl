Keith Gill

Keith Patrick Gill (born June 8, 1986) is an American financial analyst and investor known for his posts on the subreddit r/wallstreetbets. His analyses of GameStop stock, published under the username DeepFuckingValue and the online name Roaring Kitty, were cited as a driving factor in the GameStop short squeeze of January 2021.

Early life and career

Gill grew up in Brockton, Massachusetts, and attended Stonehill College, where he was a track and field athlete. He worked in financial services and held roles including at MassMutual, where he worked as a chartered financial analyst and financial educator.

GameStop

Beginning in 2019, Gill posted screenshots of his long position in GameStop shares and call options, arguing the stock was undervalued. His posts gained a large following, and the January 2021 surge in GameStop's share price turned his roughly $53,000 position into tens of millions of dollars at its peak. Gill testified before the U.S. House Committee on Financial Services in February 2021 regarding the events.

Later activity

After several years of public silence, Gill resumed posting on social media in 2024, again drawing attention to GameStop and disclosing a substantial position in the stock.
