Aparthotel

An aparthotel, or apartment hotel, is a type of accommodation that combines features of a hotel with those of a self-catering apartment. Guests book units in a manner similar to hotel rooms, but each unit typically includes a kitchen or kitchenette, living space and laundry facilities, making aparthotels suitable for longer stays.

Features

Aparthotels usually provide reception, cleaning and other hotel services, though housekeeping may be less frequent than in a conventional hotel. Units range from studios to multi-bedroom apartments. They are popular with business travellers on extended assignments, families and groups who want more space and the ability to prepare meals.

Business model

Operators include hotel chains, serviced-apartment companies and independent owners. In some cities aparthotels and short-term apartment rentals raise regulatory questions similar to those surrounding hotels and residential letting, including zoning, taxation and minimum-stay rules.
