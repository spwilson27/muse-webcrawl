Tōmei Expressway

The Tōmei Expressway (東名高速道路, Tōmei Kōsoku-dōro) is a major expressway in Japan running from Tokyo southwest through Kanagawa, Shizuoka, and Aichi prefectures to Komaki, near Nagoya. It carries the route number E1 and is operated by the Central Nippon Expressway Company (NEXCO Central).

History and route

The expressway opened in stages between 1968 and 1969, in time for the growth era following the 1964 Tokyo Olympics, and forms the principal road link between Tokyo and Nagoya, paralleling the historic Tōkaidō corridor. It passes the southern foothills of Mount Fuji, which is visible from several sections in Shizuoka Prefecture. Chronic congestion led to the construction of the parallel Shin-Tōmei Expressway (E1A), opened in stages beginning in 2012. The Tōmei connects with the Meishin Expressway at Komaki, completing the Tokyo–Nagoya–Osaka expressway axis.
