Blue Öyster Cult (often abbreviated BÖC) is an American rock band formed in 1967 in Stony Brook, New York. The group is best known for the singles "(Don't Fear) The Reaper", "Burnin' for You", and "Godzilla", songs that have become staples of classic rock radio.

History

The band grew out of the group Soft White Underbelly, assembled by rock critic Sandy Pearlman, who became their manager and lyricist. Signing to Columbia Records, they released their self-titled debut album in 1972, developing a distinctive sound that combined heavy guitars with literate, occult-tinged lyrics.

Their commercial breakthrough came with the 1976 album Agents of Fortune, which contained "(Don't Fear) The Reaper", written and sung by guitarist Donald "Buck Dharma" Roeser. The band's live album Some Enchanted Evening (1978) was another major success, and 1981's Fire of Unknown Origin produced the hit "Burnin' for You".

Style and legacy

Blue Öyster Cult's music blends hard rock and heavy metal with science-fiction and supernatural themes, and their hook-and-cross logo, designed by Bill Gawlik, became one of rock's most recognisable symbols. The band has sold more than 25 million records worldwide and continues to tour, releasing the album The Symbol Remains in 2020.
