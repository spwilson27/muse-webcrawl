Q-Tip (musician)

Q-Tip (born Jonathan William Davis, April 10, 1970), also known as Kamaal Ibn John Fareed, is an American rapper, record producer, singer and actor. He is best known as the co-founder and frontman of the pioneering hip hop group A Tribe Called Quest.

Career

Q-Tip formed A Tribe Called Quest in Queens, New York, in the late 1980s with Phife Dawg, Ali Shaheed Muhammad and Jarobi White. As part of the Native Tongues collective, the group blended jazz samples with Afrocentric lyrics on acclaimed albums including The Low End Theory (1991) and Midnight Marauders (1993). Q-Tip was the group's principal producer, shaping its signature jazz-rap sound.

Solo work and production

His solo debut Amplified (1999) produced the hit "Vivrant Thing". Later solo albums include The Renaissance (2008) and Kamaal/The Abstract. As a producer he has worked with Nas, Mobb Deep, Mariah Carey, Kendrick Lamar and many others. Q-Tip also served as executive music producer on Tribe's final album, We Got It from Here... Thank You 4 Your Service (2016), and has mentored younger artists through his work at the Kennedy Center, where he was named artistic director for hip-hop culture.
