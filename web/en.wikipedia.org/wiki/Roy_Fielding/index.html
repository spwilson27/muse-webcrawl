Roy Fielding

Roy Thomas Fielding (born 1965) is an American computer scientist best known as the author of REST (Representational State Transfer) and a co-founder of the Apache HTTP Server project.

Work

Fielding's 2000 doctoral dissertation at the University of California, Irvine, Architectural Styles and the Design of Network-based Software Architectures, described REST, the architectural style underlying the World Wide Web. He was a lead author of the HTTP and URI specifications, co-founded the Apache Software Foundation, and worked at Day Software and Adobe. He also originated the "Waka" protocol proposal.
