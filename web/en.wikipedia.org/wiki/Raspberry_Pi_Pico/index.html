Raspberry Pi Pico

Raspberry Pi Pico
Released: January 2021
SoC: RP2040
CPU: Dual-core ARM Cortex-M0+ @ 133 MHz
Memory: 264 KB SRAM, 2 MB flash
Price at launch: US$4

The Raspberry Pi Pico is a low-cost microcontroller board developed by the Raspberry Pi Foundation (now Raspberry Pi Ltd), released in January 2021. Unlike the Linux-based Raspberry Pi single-board computers, the Pico is a microcontroller development board built around the RP2040 chip, designed in-house in the UK.

Hardware

The RP2040 features a dual-core Arm Cortex-M0+ processor running at up to 133 MHz, 264 KB of SRAM and support for up to 16 MB of off-chip flash (2 MB on the Pico). A distinctive feature is its Programmable I/O (PIO) blocks — two independent state-machine assemblies that can implement custom digital interfaces such as WS2812 LED control, DVI output or additional serial ports. The board has 26 exposed GPIO pins and castellated pads for surface mounting.

Software

The Pico can be programmed in C/C++ with the official SDK, or in MicroPython and CircuitPython; it is also supported by the Arduino ecosystem. Programs are loaded over USB mass storage by dragging a UF2 file onto the board.

Variants

The Pico W (2022) adds 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, and the Pico H adds pre-soldered headers. In 2024 the Pico 2 introduced the RP2350 chip with Cortex-M33 and RISC-V cores.
