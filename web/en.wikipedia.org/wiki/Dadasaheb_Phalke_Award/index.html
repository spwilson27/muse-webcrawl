Dadasaheb Phalke Award

The Dadasaheb Phalke Award is India's highest award in cinema, presented annually at the National Film Awards ceremony by the Directorate of Film Festivals for outstanding contribution to the growth and development of Indian cinema.

Instituted in 1969, the award honours Dadasaheb Phalke, director of Raja Harishchandra (1913), India's first full-length feature film, and regarded as the father of Indian cinema. The award comprises a Swarna Kamal (Golden Lotus) medallion, a shawl and a cash prize.

Recipients

The first recipient was actress Devika Rani (1969). Later honourees include Satyajit Ray, Lata Mangeshkar, Dilip Kumar, Amitabh Bachchan, Waheeda Rehman, Mithun Chakraborty and Mohanlal.
