Dion of Syracuse

Dion (408 – 354 BC) was a Syracusan statesman, politician and general, brother-in-law of the tyrant Dionysius I of Syracuse and a pupil of Plato. After falling out with Dionysius II, Dion was exiled to Athens, where he associated with Plato's Academy. In 357 BC he returned to Sicily with a small force of mercenaries, seized Syracuse and briefly ruled the city, attempting reforms in a Platonic spirit. His rule proved unpopular and he was assassinated in 354 BC by agents of the Athenian Calippus.
