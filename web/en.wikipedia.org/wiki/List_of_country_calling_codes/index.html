List of country calling codes

Country calling codes are telephone number prefixes for reaching subscribers in foreign countries or areas, defined by the International Telecommunication Union (ITU) in standard E.164. The world is divided into nine numbering zones, broadly continental. Zone 1 is the North American Numbering Plan; zone 2 is mostly Africa; zones 3 and 4 are Europe; zone 5 is Latin America; zone 6 is Southeast Asia and Oceania; zone 7 is Russia and Kazakhstan; zone 8 is East Asia and special services; zone 9 is West, South and Central Asia and the Middle East.
