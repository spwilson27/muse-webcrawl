Intercom

An intercom ("intercommunication device") is a stand-alone voice communication system for use within a building, small group of buildings, or portable equipment. Intercoms are generally mounted permanently in buildings and vehicles, though portable systems are also used on film sets, ships, and aircraft.

Traditional systems use dedicated wiring with a master station and substations; modern systems may use VoIP or wireless technology and can integrate with telephone systems, access control, and video door phones.

Uses

Intercoms are common in apartment buildings for visitor screening, in offices and schools for announcements, in hospitals, and in drive-through services. On aircraft and ships, crew intercoms (flight interphones) support internal communication.
