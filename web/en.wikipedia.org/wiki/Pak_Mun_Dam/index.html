Pak Mun Dam

LocationUbon Ratchathani Province, Thailand
RiverMun River
Opened1994
Installed capacity136 MW

The Pak Mun Dam is a hydroelectric dam on the Mun River in Ubon Ratchathani Province, northeastern Thailand, about 5.5 kilometres west of the river's confluence with the Mekong. Constructed by the Electricity Generating Authority of Thailand with World Bank financing and completed in 1994, it generates 136 megawatts of electricity.

Controversy

The dam became one of the world's most contested hydropower projects. Local villagers and the Assembly of the Poor protested that the dam blocked fish migration from the Mekong, destroying a fishery on which thousands of families depended, and that compensation was inadequate. A fish ladder proved largely ineffective for the Mun's diverse species.

Aftermath

Following sustained protests, the Thai government agreed in 2001 to open the dam's gates for a trial year, during which many fish species returned; the gates are now opened seasonally. The case became a landmark study for the World Commission on Dams.
