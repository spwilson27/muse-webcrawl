Black Canary is the name of two superheroines appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. The original Black Canary, Dinah Drake, debuted in Flash Comics #86 (August 1947) as a Golden Age character created by writer Robert Kanigher and artist Carmine Infantino. Her daughter, Dinah Laurel Lance, succeeded her in the Silver Age and is the best-known version of the character.

Fictional biography and abilities

Dinah Drake, a florist by day, fought crime in Gotham City relying on judo and hand-to-hand combat, becoming a mainstay of the Justice Society of America. Dinah Laurel Lance inherited her mother's mantle and added a metahuman power, the Canary Cry — an ultrasonic scream capable of shattering objects and incapacitating enemies. One of DC's premier martial artists, she has been trained by figures including Wildcat and Lady Shiva, served with the Justice League, and co-founded the all-female team Birds of Prey with Oracle (Barbara Gordon), with whom she is also closely associated as a field leader. She has a long-running romantic relationship with Green Arrow (Oliver Queen), whom she has married in several continuities.

In other media

Versions of Black Canary have appeared in the animated series Justice League Unlimited and Young Justice; in the CW's Arrowverse (notably Katie Cassidy as Laurel Lance and Juliana Harkavy as Dinah Drake); and in the DC Extended Universe film Birds of Prey (2020), played by Jurnee Smollett. The character is frequently ranked among comics' greatest female superheroes.
