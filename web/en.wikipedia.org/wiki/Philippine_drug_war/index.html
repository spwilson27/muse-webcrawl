Philippine drug war

The Philippine drug war is the intensified anti-drug campaign launched by the government of President Rodrigo Duterte after he took office on 30 June 2016. Officially known through police operations such as Oplan Tokhang and Oplan Double Barrel, the campaign aimed to eradicate illegal drugs in the Philippines within months.

Period2016–present (most intense 2016–2022)
Launched byRodrigo Duterte administration
Lead agencyPhilippine National Police; PDEA

Campaign

Police conducted buy-bust operations and house-to-house "knock and plead" visits (Tokhang) urging suspected users to surrender. Government figures recorded roughly 6,000 deaths in official police operations by 2022, while human rights groups estimated total killings, including by unidentified gunmen, at up to 30,000.

Reactions

The campaign drew strong domestic support in opinion surveys but international condemnation over alleged extrajudicial killings. The International Criminal Court opened an investigation into possible crimes against humanity, and in March 2025 Duterte was arrested and transferred to The Hague to face ICC charges.
