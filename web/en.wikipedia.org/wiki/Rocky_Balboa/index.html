Rocky Balboa

Rocky Balboa
Portrayed by: Sylvester Stallone
Nickname: The Italian Stallion
Born: July 6, 1947 (fictional)
From: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

Robert "Rocky" Balboa Sr. is a fictional character and the title protagonist of the Rocky film series, created by and portrayed by Sylvester Stallone. An Italian-American boxer from the slums of South Philadelphia, Rocky rises from small-time club fighter and debt collector to world heavyweight champion, becoming one of the most recognisable characters in American cinema and a symbol of the underdog.

Character biography

In the first film (1976), Rocky works as an enforcer for a loan shark while fighting in local clubs. Selected by champion Apollo Creed as a novelty opponent — a nobody given a shot at the title — Rocky trains under Mickey Goldmill and goes the full fifteen rounds, losing a split decision but winning public respect and the love of Adrian Pennino, whom he later marries. He wins the championship in the rematch in Rocky II (1979), defends it against Clubber Lang and befriends the dying Apollo in Rocky III (1982), avenges Apollo's death by defeating Ivan Drago in Rocky IV (1985), and is forced into retirement and financial ruin in Rocky V (1990). In Rocky Balboa (2006), the widowed, aging Rocky returns for an exhibition bout with champion Mason Dixon and again goes the distance. He later appears as trainer and mentor to Adonis Creed in the Creed films (2015–2018).

Creation

Stallone created the character after watching the 1975 Muhammad Ali–Chuck Wepner fight, in which the unknown Wepner knocked Ali down and lasted nearly fifteen rounds. Elements of Rocky's style and personality were also drawn from real fighters including Rocky Marciano (whose name inspired the character's) and Joe Frazier.

Cultural impact

The character's training montages, his run up the Philadelphia Museum of Art steps, and lines such as "Yo, Adrian!" entered popular culture. In 2011 Stallone was inducted into the International Boxing Hall of Fame in part for the cultural impact of the character. A statue of Rocky Balboa stands in Philadelphia as a tourist landmark.
