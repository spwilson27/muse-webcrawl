List of compositions for cello and orchestra

This is a list of notable compositions for solo cello and orchestra, principally cello concertos, together with concertante works such as variations, rondos, and double concertos for cello with another soloist.

Baroque and Classical

  • Antonio Vivaldi — 27 cello concertos, including the Concerto for Two Cellos in G minor, RV 531
  • Luigi Boccherini — 12 cello concertos; the Concerto No. 9 in B-flat major is the best known
  • Joseph Haydn — Cello Concerto No. 1 in C major (c. 1761–65) and No. 2 in D major (1783)
  • C. P. E. Bach — three cello concertos (Wq. 170–172)

Romantic

  • Robert Schumann — Cello Concerto in A minor, Op. 129 (1850)
  • Camille Saint-Saëns — Cello Concerto No. 1 in A minor, Op. 33 (1872); No. 2 in D minor, Op. 119
  • Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky — Variations on a Rococo Theme, Op. 33 (1876)
  • Antonín Dvořák — Cello Concerto in B minor, Op. 104 (1894–95), widely considered the greatest cello concerto
  • Johannes Brahms — Double Concerto for violin and cello in A minor, Op. 102 (1887)
  • Max Bruch — Kol Nidrei, Op. 47
  • Édouard Lalo — Cello Concerto in D minor (1876)

20th century and later

  • Edward Elgar — Cello Concerto in E minor, Op. 85 (1919)
  • Sergei Prokofiev — Symphony-Concerto in E minor, Op. 125
  • Dmitri Shostakovich — Cello Concerto No. 1 in E-flat major, Op. 107 (1959), written for Mstislav Rostropovich; No. 2, Op. 126
  • Benjamin Britten — Symphony for Cello and Orchestra, Op. 68 (1963)
  • William Walton — Cello Concerto (1956)
  • Henri Dutilleux — Tout un monde lointain... (1970)
  • Witold Lutosławski — Cello Concerto (1970)
  • György Ligeti — Cello Concerto (1966)
  • John Williams — Concerto for Cello and Orchestra (1994)
  • Philip Glass — Cello Concerto No. 1 (2001) and No. 2, "Naqoyqatsi" (2012)
