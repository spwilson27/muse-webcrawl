Scott Boras

Born: November 2, 1952, Sacramento, California, U.S.

Occupation: Sports agent

Organisation: Boras Corporation

Scott Dean Boras (born November 2, 1952) is an American sports agent, generally regarded as the most powerful agent in Major League Baseball. He is the founder and president of the Boras Corporation, a sports agency based in Newport Beach, California, which represents roughly 175 professional baseball clients.

Career

Boras played minor league baseball in the St. Louis Cardinals and Chicago Cubs organisations before knee injuries ended his playing career; he then earned a law degree from the University of the Pacific's McGeorge School of Law. As an agent he pioneered record-setting contracts and hard bargaining on behalf of free agents, negotiating deals including Alex Rodriguez's ten-year, $252 million contract with the Texas Rangers in 2000 — then the largest in sports history — and later the landmark contracts of Bryce Harper, Gerrit Cole and Juan Soto. His tactics, including taking top clients into free agency and detailed statistical "Boras binders" presented to clubs, have made him a controversial figure among team owners and fans, but his clients consistently rank among the highest-paid players in the game.
