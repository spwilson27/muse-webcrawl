Sweden national football team

The Sweden national football team (Swedish: Sveriges herrlandslag i fotboll) represents Sweden in men's international football and is controlled by the Swedish Football Association (SvFF), the governing body for football in Sweden. Sweden's home ground is Friends Arena in Solna, and the team's head coach has in recent years included Janne Andersson and, from 2024, Jon Dahl Tomasson.

History

Sweden played its first international match in 1908, beating Norway 11–3 in Gothenburg. Sweden has traditionally been a strong team in international football, with twelve World Cup appearances, finishing runners-up in 1958 when they hosted the tournament, losing 5–2 to Brazil in the final despite goals from Nils Liedholm and Agne Simonsson; Pelé famously announced himself to the world in that match. Sweden also finished third at the 1950 and 1994 World Cups and fourth in 1938.

At the European Championship, Sweden reached the semi-finals as hosts of Euro 1992, losing to Germany. The team won the gold medal at the 1948 Summer Olympics in London. Memorable modern players include Henrik Larsson, Zlatan Ibrahimović — Sweden's all-time top scorer — Freddie Ljungberg, Tomas Brolin, and Anders Svensson, the most-capped male player. Sweden's traditional colours are yellow shirts and blue shorts, and the team is nicknamed Blågult ("the Blue and Yellow").
