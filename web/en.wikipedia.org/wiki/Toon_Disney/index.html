Toon Disney

Toon DisneyLaunched: 18 April 1998
Closed: 13 February 2009
Owner: Disney Channels Worldwide

Toon Disney was an American pay television channel owned by the Walt Disney Company, broadcasting animated programming aimed at children. It launched on 18 April 1998, the fifteenth anniversary of Disney Channel.

Programming

The channel aired classic Disney animated series such as DuckTales, Chip 'n Dale: Rescue Rangers, Darkwing Duck and Gargoyles, along with newer shows and animated films. Its Jetix block, added in 2004, carried action-oriented animation and anime.

Closure

On 13 February 2009 Toon Disney was replaced by Disney XD, a channel aimed primarily at boys aged 6–14. International versions of Toon Disney continued in some markets for a time afterwards.
