Machine Head (album)

Machine Head is the sixth studio album by the English rock band Deep Purple, released on 25 March 1972 by Purple Records. Recorded in December 1971 at the Grand Hôtel in Montreux, Switzerland, using the Rolling Stones Mobile Studio, it is widely regarded as one of the most influential heavy metal albums of all time.

Background

The band had intended to record at the Montreux Casino, but the venue burned down during a Frank Zappa concert — an event immortalised in the album's most famous song, "Smoke on the Water". Other tracks include "Highway Star", "Space Truckin'" and "Lazy".

Legacy

Machine Head reached number one on the UK Albums Chart and is regularly listed among the greatest rock albums ever made; "Smoke on the Water" features one of the best-known guitar riffs in rock history.
