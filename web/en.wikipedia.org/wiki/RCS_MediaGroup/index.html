RCS MediaGroup S.p.A. is an Italian media and publishing company based in Milan. It publishes Italy's leading daily newspaper, Corriere della Sera, and the sports daily La Gazzetta dello Sport, and through its Spanish subsidiary Unidad Editorial publishes El Mundo and the sports paper Marca.

Activities

Alongside newspapers and magazines, the group operates in book publishing (including Rizzoli), advertising sales and sports events; its RCS Sport division organises the Giro d'Italia, one of cycling's three Grand Tours, and the Milan–San Remo classic.

History

The group's origins lie in the Rizzoli publishing house founded by Angelo Rizzoli in 1927, which acquired Corriere della Sera in 1974. After financial crises and restructurings, control of RCS passed in 2016 to Urbano Cairo's Cairo Communication following a takeover battle.
