List of mass shootings in Australia

This article lists mass shootings in Australia, generally defined as incidents in which several people are shot. Mass shootings were comparatively more frequent in the 1980s and early 1990s; the Port Arthur massacre of 1996 prompted sweeping national gun law reforms, after which firearm massacres became rare.

Gun law context

After Port Arthur, the federal and state governments agreed the National Firearms Agreement, which banned certain semi-automatic rifles and shotguns, introduced a buyback scheme that destroyed more than 650,000 firearms, and standardised licensing and registration. Studies have found that the rate of mass shootings fell sharply afterwards, while overall firearm deaths continued a pre-existing decline.
