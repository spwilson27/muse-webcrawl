James Harrison (American football)

Born: 4 May 1978, Akron, Ohio
Position: Linebacker
Teams: Pittsburgh Steelers, Baltimore Ravens, Cincinnati Bengals, New England Patriots

James Harrison Jr. (born 4 May 1978) is an American former professional football linebacker. He spent most of his National Football League career with the Pittsburgh Steelers, winning Super Bowls XL and XLIII, and was named the NFL Defensive Player of the Year in 2008.

Career

Harrison played college football at Kent State and went undrafted in 2002. He was signed and released several times by the Steelers and also spent time with the Baltimore Ravens and in NFL Europe (Rhein Fire) before earning a roster spot in Pittsburgh. He became a starter in 2007 following the release of Joey Porter, and recorded a dominant 2008 season with 16 sacks, earning Defensive Player of the Year honours — the first undrafted player to win the award.

In Super Bowl XLIII against the Arizona Cardinals, Harrison returned an interception 100 yards for a touchdown on the final play of the first half, the longest play in Super Bowl history at the time, in a game Pittsburgh won 27–23. Known for his intense playing style and heavy fines for hits deemed illegal by the league, he later played for the Cincinnati Bengals (2013) and the New England Patriots (2017) before retiring in 2018.

Legacy

Harrison is a five-time Pro Bowl selection and two-time first-team All-Pro, and is regarded as one of the most feared pass rushers of his era and one of the greatest undrafted players in NFL history.
