Aniplex Inc. (株式会社アニプレックス) is a Japanese entertainment company and subsidiary of Sony Music Entertainment Japan. Founded in 1995 as SPE Visual Works, it produces and distributes anime films and television series, video games, and music. Aniplex is involved in the majority of commercially successful anime franchises of the 2000s and 2010s, serving on production committees and as producer for series such as Fullmetal Alchemist, Fate/stay night and the Fate franchise, Sword Art Online, Puella Magi Madoka Magica, Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba, and Your Lie in April.

Studios

Aniplex owns the animation studios A-1 Pictures (founded 2005) and CloverWorks (spun off in 2018), and has worked closely with studios such as Ufotable and Shaft. Its subsidiary Aniplex of America distributes anime in North America.

Games

Aniplex published the hit mobile game Fate/Grand Order (developed by Delightworks/Lasengle), one of the highest-grossing mobile games worldwide, and operates the Aniplex Online store and the Aniplex Online Fest streaming events.
