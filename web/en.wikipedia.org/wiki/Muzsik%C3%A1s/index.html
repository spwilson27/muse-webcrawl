Muzsikás
OriginBudapest, Hungary
Formed1973
GenreHungarian folk music

Muzsikás is a Hungarian folk music ensemble formed in 1973 in Budapest. The group is credited with reviving interest in traditional Hungarian village music, particularly the string-band traditions of Transylvania, and with helping build the táncház (dance house) movement.

Career

The members learned repertoire directly from village musicians in Hungary and Transylvania. Long-time collaborators include the singer Márta Sebestyén, with whom the group recorded widely and contributed to the soundtrack of the film The English Patient (1996).

Recognition

Muzsikás received the WOMEX Artist Award in 2008 for their contribution to world music. Their albums, including The Bartók Album, explore the connections between village music and the folk sources used by Béla Bartók.
