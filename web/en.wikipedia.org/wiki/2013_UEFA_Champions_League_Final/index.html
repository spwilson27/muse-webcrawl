2013 UEFA Champions League Final

The 2013 UEFA Champions League Final was the final match of the 2012–13 UEFA Champions League, the 58th season of Europe's premier club football tournament organised by UEFA. It was played at Wembley Stadium in London on 25 May 2013 between German clubs Borussia Dortmund and Bayern Munich — the first time the final had been contested by two German teams. Bayern won 2–1 to claim their fifth European Cup.

Match details

Date: 25 May 2013
Venue: Wembley Stadium, London
Result: Borussia Dortmund 1–2 Bayern Munich
Scorers: Gündoğan (pen.); Mandžukić, Robben
Attendance: 86,298
Referee: Nicola Rizzoli (Italy)

Match summary

After a goalless first half in which both goalkeepers, Roman Weidenfeller and Manuel Neuer, made important saves, Mario Mandžukić put Bayern ahead on the hour mark from Arjen Robben's pass. Dortmund equalised seven minutes later through an İlkay Gündoğan penalty after Dante fouled Marco Reus. With the match heading for extra time, Robben latched onto Franck Ribéry's backheel in the 89th minute and rolled the ball past Weidenfeller for the winning goal.

Significance

Bayern, coached by Jupp Heynckes, went on to complete a treble by winning the DFB-Pokal a week later, adding to their Bundesliga title. The victory was a redemption for Robben, who had missed a penalty in the previous year's final, which Bayern lost to Chelsea.
