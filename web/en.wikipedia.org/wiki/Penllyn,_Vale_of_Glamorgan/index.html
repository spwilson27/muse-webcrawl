Penllyn, Vale of Glamorgan

Country: Wales, United Kingdom
Principal area: Vale of Glamorgan
Nearby town: Cowbridge

Penllyn is a village and community in the Vale of Glamorgan, south Wales, located just north of the market town of Cowbridge. The community includes the hamlets of Graig Penllyn and Llansannor among others.

Landmarks

The village is dominated by Penllyn Castle, a Norman castle later adapted as a fortified manor house, whose ruins stand on a ridge overlooking the River Thaw valley; the neo-Tudor Penllyn Court stands nearby. The parish church of St Brynach dates in part to the medieval period. Penllyn is surrounded by farmland typical of the Vale, known for its lowland pastures and historic estates.

Community

Penllyn community council covers a largely rural, low-population area of a few hundred residents. The village has a public house and a village hall, and lies on local routes between Cowbridge and the villages of the northern Vale.
