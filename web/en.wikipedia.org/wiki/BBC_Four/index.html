BBC Four is a British free-to-air television channel owned by the BBC. Launched on 2 March 2002, it broadcasts a wide variety of programming including arts, documentaries, drama, comedy and international film, with the remit to reflect a range of UK and international arts, music and culture.

Programming

The channel became known for intelligent factual series, music documentaries, subtitled European drama and innovative comedy. It commissioned or aired such programmes as The Thick of It, Detectorists, Twenty Twelve and the long-running quiz-adjacent comedy Only Connect (later moved to BBC Two). Imported dramas such as Danish series The Killing helped spark British interest in "Nordic noir".

Closure plans and reprieve

In 2022 the BBC announced plans to discontinue BBC Four as a broadcast channel as part of cost-saving and digital-first strategy, prompting public campaigns to save it. The closure was subsequently put on hold and the channel continued broadcasting.
