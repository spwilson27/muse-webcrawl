Snowdrop (South Korean TV series)

Snowdrop (Korean: 설강화; Hanja: 雪降花; RR: Seolganghwa) is a South Korean television series starring Jung Hae-in and Jisoo. It aired on JTBC from December 18, 2021, to January 30, 2022. It is also available for streaming on Disney+ in selected regions.

Plot

Set in 1987 Seoul during the June Democracy Movement, the series follows Eun Yeong-ro, a university student at a women's university, who hides and nurses Im Soo-ho, a graduate student she finds covered in blood after a group blind date. Soo-ho is secretly a North Korean agent on a mission in the South, and their relationship becomes entangled with the political machinations of the ruling party, the intelligence agency (ANSP), and the approaching presidential election.

Cast

  • Jung Hae-in as Im Soo-ho, a North Korean agent posing as a graduate student.
  • Jisoo (Kim Ji-soo) as Eun Yeong-ro, a first-year English literature student.
  • Yoo In-na as Kang Chung-ya, a surgeon and North Korean spy.
  • Jang Seung-jo as Lee Kang-mu, an ANSP team leader.
  • Yoon Se-ah as Pi Seung-hee, the housemother of the dormitory.

Controversy

Before and during its broadcast, the series faced accusations of historical distortion concerning the pro-democracy movement and the portrayal of the ANSP, prompting petitions and sponsor withdrawals. JTBC and the production maintained that the story was fictional and centred on individuals caught within the political situation of the time.
