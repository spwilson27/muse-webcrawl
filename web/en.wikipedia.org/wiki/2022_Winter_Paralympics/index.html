The 2022 Winter Paralympics, officially the XIII Paralympic Winter Games, were held in Beijing, China, from 4 to 13 March 2022, shortly after the 2022 Winter Olympics. Beijing became the first city to host both the Summer and Winter Paralympic Games.

Sports

Athletes competed in 78 medal events across six sports: alpine skiing, biathlon, cross-country skiing, ice sledge hockey, snowboarding and wheelchair curling. Ukraine's team, competing just days after Russia's invasion of their country began, delivered an inspired performance, finishing second in the medal table.

Russian and Belarusian exclusion

Following the invasion of Ukraine in February 2022, the International Paralympic Committee initially proposed allowing Russian and Belarusian athletes to compete as neutrals, then reversed the decision on the eve of the opening ceremony and excluded them entirely amid threats of boycotts.

Medal table

Hosts China topped the medal table for the first time at a Winter Paralympics, winning 18 gold medals and 61 medals overall, ahead of Ukraine and Canada.
