Tate Liverpool
Location: Royal Albert Dock, Liverpool, England
Established: 1988
Part of: Tate group of galleries

Tate Liverpool is an art gallery and museum located in Liverpool, England, on the Royal Albert Dock. It is part of the Tate group of galleries in England, which includes Tate Britain and Tate Modern in London and Tate St Ives in Cornwall. The gallery was created to display work from the Tate collection, which comprises national collections of British art from the year 1500 to the present day, and international modern art.

History

Tate Liverpool opened in 1988 in a converted warehouse on the Albert Dock, which had been designed by Jesse Hartley and Philip Hardwick and completed in 1846. The conversion of the former warehouse into a gallery was undertaken by the architect James Stirling. At the time of its opening, it was the largest gallery of modern and contemporary art in the United Kingdom outside London.

The Albert Dock complex had fallen into decline following the Second World War but was regenerated in the 1980s under the Merseyside Development Corporation. Tate Liverpool became a centrepiece of that regeneration and a symbol of the city's cultural revival. The gallery occupies several floors of the seven-storey dock warehouse.

Collections and exhibitions

The gallery presents rotating displays drawn from the Tate collection alongside temporary exhibitions of modern and contemporary art, often organised in collaboration with other venues. Admission to the permanent collection displays is free. Tate Liverpool has hosted major exhibitions devoted to artists such as Gustav Klimt, Pablo Picasso, and Keith Haring, and it staged the Turner Prize exhibition in 2007–08, the first time the prize was held outside London in its regular cycle.

Renovation and Tate Liverpool + RIBA North

The gallery closed temporarily in the 2020s for a major redevelopment of its gallery spaces. During the closure, Tate Liverpool partnered with RIBA North in the Mann Island building nearby, presenting a programme of exhibitions and events under the name Tate Liverpool + RIBA North.
