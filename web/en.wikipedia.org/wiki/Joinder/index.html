Joinder

Joinder is a legal procedure in which multiple claims or parties are joined together in a single lawsuit. Rules of civil procedure govern permissive and compulsory joinder of parties and claims, aiming to resolve related disputes in one proceeding.

Joinder of claims

A party may generally join as many claims as it has against an opposing party, whether related or not, subject to the court's jurisdiction. This avoids duplicative litigation over separate transactions.

Joinder of parties

Permissive joinder allows plaintiffs or defendants to be joined when claims arise from the same transaction or occurrence and share common questions of law or fact. Compulsory joinder requires certain indispensable parties to be included for a case to proceed fairly.
