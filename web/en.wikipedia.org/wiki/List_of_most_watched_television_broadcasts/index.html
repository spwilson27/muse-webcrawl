This article contains a list of the most watched television broadcasts in history. The largest television audiences are generally drawn by major international sporting events such as the FIFA World Cup final, the Super Bowl and the Olympic Games, together with events of historic significance such as the Apollo 11 Moon landing in 1969, which was watched by an estimated 600 million people worldwide. Reliable global figures are difficult to establish, and rankings typically rely on claims by broadcasters and ratings agencies such as Nielsen.

United States

In the United States, Nielsen ratings show the Super Bowl dominating the all-time list: the most watched broadcasts in American history are almost exclusively Super Bowls, interrupted only by the 1983 finale of M*A*S*H, which held the record until Super Bowl XLIV in 2010. Super Bowl XLIX (2015) drew an average of 114.4 million viewers, and subsequent editions regularly exceed 100 million.
