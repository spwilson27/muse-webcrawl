Heinz Prüfer

Born10 November 1896, Wilhelmshaven
Died7 April 1934, Münster
OccupationMathematician
Known forPrüfer sequences; Prüfer groups; Prüfer domains

Ernst Paul Heinz Prüfer (1896–1934) was a German mathematician who worked in abelian group theory, algebraic number theory, knot theory and Sturm–Liouville theory. He studied in Berlin and received his doctorate under Issai Schur.

Contributions

Prüfer introduced the Prüfer sequence, a bijective proof of Cayley's formula counting the labelled trees on n vertices; the Prüfer groups (quasicyclic groups); and the class of Prüfer domains in commutative algebra. He also developed the theory of Prüfer surfaces and worked on the structure of countable abelian groups. He became a lecturer at the University of Münster, where he died at the age of 37.
