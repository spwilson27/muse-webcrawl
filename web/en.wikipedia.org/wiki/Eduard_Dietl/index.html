Eduard Dietl (21 July 1890 – 23 June 1944) was a German general during World War II who commanded the 20th Mountain Army. He received the Knight's Cross of the Iron Cross with Oak Leaves and Swords.

Career

Dietl, a Bavarian, joined the army in 1909 and served in the First World War. In 1940 he commanded the German forces in the Norwegian campaign's northern sector, holding Narvik against Allied forces until the Allied withdrawal. He subsequently commanded German troops in northern Finland and Norway during Operation Barbarossa and the Lapland front.

Death

Dietl died on 23 June 1944 when his aircraft crashed near Rettenegg in Styria, Austria. He was among the most prominent German generals in the Arctic theatre of the war.
