Mie Prefecture
Country: Japan
Region: Kansai
Capital: Tsu
Area: ~5,774 km²

Mie Prefecture (三重県, Mie-ken) is a prefecture of Japan located in the Kansai region of Honshu, occupying much of the eastern Kii Peninsula. Its capital is the city of Tsu, and its largest city is Yokkaichi.

Highlights

Mie is home to Ise Grand Shrine (Ise Jingū), the most sacred Shinto shrine in Japan, rebuilt every twenty years in the Shikinen Sengū ceremony. The prefecture is also known for the ninja heritage of Iga, Matsusaka beef, cultured pearls from Ago Bay (pioneered by Mikimoto Kōkichi), and the Suzuka Circuit, long-time host of the Formula One Japanese Grand Prix. The 2016 G7 summit was held at Shima, in Mie.

Geography

Mie faces Ise Bay and the Pacific Ocean, borders Aichi, Gifu, Shiga, Kyoto, Nara and Wakayama prefectures, and includes the mountainous Kii Peninsula interior, partly protected as national parks.
