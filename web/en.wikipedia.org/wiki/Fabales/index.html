Fabales

Fabales is an order of flowering plants included in the rosid group of the eudicots in the Angiosperm Phylogeny Group (APG) systems. It contains four families: Fabaceae (the legumes), Quillajaceae, Surianaceae, and Polygalaceae.

The legume family Fabaceae dominates the order Fabales.

Description

The order is best known for the legume family Fabaceae, the third-largest family of flowering plants, with roughly 19,500 species including peas, beans, soybeans, peanuts, and many important forage and timber plants. Most legumes form symbiotic associations with nitrogen-fixing bacteria in root nodules, giving the group great ecological and agricultural significance.

Distribution

Fabales are distributed worldwide, from tropical rainforests to temperate grasslands and arid regions. The APG IV system recognises the order within the nitrogen-fixing clade of the rosids, alongside Rosales, Fagales, and Cucurbitales.
