Boris Belousov

Boris Pavlovich Belousov
Born: 19 February 1893, Moscow
Died: 12 June 1970, Moscow
Known for: Belousov–Zhabotinsky reaction

Boris Pavlovich Belousov (1893–1970) was a Soviet chemist and biophysicist who discovered the Belousov–Zhabotinsky (BZ) reaction, a classic example of non-equilibrium thermodynamics and oscillating chemical reactions.

Life

Belousov studied chemistry in Zurich, worked in military medicine and radiation research, and headed a laboratory at the Institute of Biophysics in Moscow. His work on the Krebs cycle led him to an oscillating mixture of citric acid, bromate and cerium ions.

Discovery

Around 1951 he observed periodic colour changes, contradicting the belief that such oscillations violated the second law. Journals rejected his papers; he published only a short abstract in 1959. Anatol Zhabotinsky later developed the work, and Belousov was posthumously awarded the Lenin Prize in 1980.
