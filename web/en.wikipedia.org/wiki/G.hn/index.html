G.hn

G.hn is an ITU-T home networking standard (Recommendation G.9960, with the data link layer in G.9961) that specifies high-speed networking over existing home wiring — power lines, telephone lines and coaxial cables — at data rates up to about 2 Gbit/s.

G.hn
Standard: ITU-T G.9960/G.9961
Approved: 2009–2010
Media: Powerline, coax, phone line
Promoter: HomeGrid Forum

Features

G.hn uses OFDM modulation and supports multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) operation over power lines. It aims to provide a single unified technology for distributing multimedia content throughout a home, with quality-of-service support and AES encryption.

Adoption

Promoted by the HomeGrid Forum, G.hn chipsets found use in extenders, set-top boxes and service-provider gateways, competing with HomePlug and MoCA technologies.
