Pompano Beach, Florida

Pompano Beach is a city in Broward County, Florida, United States, situated on the Atlantic coast just north of Fort Lauderdale. Its population was 112,046 at the 2020 census. The city takes its name from the pompano, a fish found in the local waters.

History

The area was settled in the 1890s following the arrival of Henry Flagler's Florida East Coast Railway. The community was incorporated in 1908, and the separate town of Pompano merged with the beach settlement in 1947 to form Pompano Beach. For much of the twentieth century it was an agricultural area and a winter resort; the nearby Hillsboro Inlet and lighthouse, completed in 1907, mark its northern boundary.

Economy and attractions

Tourism and boating are central to the economy. The city has some 5.5 kilometres of beach, an offshore coral reef popular with divers, the Fisher Family Pier (rebuilt and reopened in 2020), the Pompano Beach Airpark (home to a Goodyear Blimp base), and the Isle Casino at the Pompano Park harness racing track site, long billed as the "Winter Capital of Harness Racing".

Demographics

Pompano Beach is part of the Miami metropolitan area. It has significant African American, Haitian American and Brazilian communities, and its population swells in winter with seasonal residents.
