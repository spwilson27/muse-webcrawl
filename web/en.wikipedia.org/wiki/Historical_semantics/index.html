Historical semantics

Historical semantics is the branch of linguistics that studies how the meanings of words change over time. It is closely related to conceptual history and diachronic semantics.

Types of semantic change

Scholars classify changes such as widening (generalisation), narrowing (specialisation), pejoration, amelioration, metaphor, and metonymy. Classic examples include English nice, once meaning "ignorant", and meat, once meaning food in general.

Methods

The field draws on etymology, textual corpora, and the history of concepts (Begriffsgeschichte), associated with historians such as Reinhart Koselleck.
