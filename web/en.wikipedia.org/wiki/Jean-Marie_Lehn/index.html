Jean-Marie Lehn

Jean-Marie Lehn (born 30 September 1939) is a French chemist who shared the Nobel Prize in Chemistry in 1987 with Donald Cram and Charles Pedersen for the development and use of molecules with structure-specific interactions of high selectivity.

Born30 September 1939, Rosheim, France
FieldsSupramolecular chemistry
AwardsNobel Prize in Chemistry (1987)

Work

Lehn's synthesis of the cryptands—three-dimensional cage-like molecules that selectively bind ions—helped found the field of supramolecular chemistry, the chemistry of molecular assemblies held together by non-covalent interactions. He later developed concepts of self-organization and constitutional dynamic chemistry.

Career

He has been a professor at the University of Strasbourg and the Collège de France, and founded the Institute of Supramolecular Science and Engineering in Strasbourg.
