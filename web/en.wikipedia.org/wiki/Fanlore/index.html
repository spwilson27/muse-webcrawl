Fanlore is a wiki dedicated to preserving the history of fanworks and fan cultures, including fandoms, fan activities, terminology, and fan communities past and present. It is a project of the Organization for Transformative Works (OTW), the nonprofit organisation that also runs the fan fiction archive Archive of Our Own (AO3).

History

Fanlore entered beta testing in September 2008 and launched publicly in 2009. It was founded partly in response to concerns that fandom's early history — much of it in print fanzines, mailing lists, and archives that were disappearing — would be lost, and partly as a reaction to the commercial, advertising-driven direction of other fan platforms of the period. The wiki grew steadily to tens of thousands of pages documenting individual fans, zines, conventions, controversies, and fan terminology.

Content and policies

Unlike Wikipedia, Fanlore embraces a "plural point of view": pages may present multiple fan perspectives on events and disputes rather than a single neutral account, and first-person recollections are welcome. The wiki covers any topic connected to fandom, from media franchises and ships to glossary terms (such as canon, fanon, and squick) and community events like archive purges and platform migrations. Editing is open to registered users, and content is licensed under Creative Commons.

Significance

Fanlore is a primary source and reference for fan studies scholars, journalists, and fans documenting their own communities, and it is among the largest collaboratively built histories of online and pre-internet fan culture.
