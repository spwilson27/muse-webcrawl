Paramount+

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Paramount+ is an American subscription video on-demand over-the-top streaming service owned by Paramount Global. It launched on 28 October 2014 as CBS All Access, initially focused on streaming CBS programming, and was relaunched under the Paramount+ name on 4 March 2021 after the merger of CBS Corporation and Viacom, expanding its catalogue with content from Paramount Pictures, MTV, Nickelodeon, Comedy Central, BET and the Smithsonian Channel.

Content

The service is the streaming home of the Star Trek franchise's modern series, including Star Trek: Discovery, Star Trek: Picard and Star Trek: Strange New Worlds, and of Taylor Sheridan's dramas such as Yellowstone prequels 1883 and 1923, Tulsa King and Mayor of Kingstown. Other originals include Halo, The Good Fight, Frasier (2023 revival) and the children's series drawn from Nickelodeon's library. In the United States it carries live sports including NFL on CBS games, UEFA Champions League football and NCAA basketball.

International expansion

Paramount+ has rolled out across Latin America, Europe, Australia and elsewhere, sometimes in partnership with Sky as part of the SkyShowtime joint venture in certain European markets. In several territories it is bundled with the Showtime service following the 2023 integration of Showtime into the premium tier in the United States.

Subscribers

The service passed 60 million subscribers in 2023 and continued growing toward roughly 70 million by 2024, making it one of the larger general-entertainment streaming platforms, though smaller than Netflix, Disney+ and Amazon Prime Video.
