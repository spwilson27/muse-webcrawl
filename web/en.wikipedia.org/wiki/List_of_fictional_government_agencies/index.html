Fictional agencies
Invented intelligence, law-enforcement and scientific bodies appear frequently in film, television and literature.

This is a list of fictional government agencies from works of fiction, including intelligence services, law-enforcement bodies, military organisations and scientific agencies that do not exist in reality. Many have become enduring pop-culture icons.
