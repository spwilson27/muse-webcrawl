Croatoan

Croatoan was the name of an island and of the Native American people living there in what is now North Carolina, in the area of present-day Hatteras Island. The word is famous as the only complete word found carved at the abandoned site of the Roanoke Colony in 1590.

The Roanoke connection

When governor John White returned to Roanoke Island in 1590 after a three-year absence, the English colonists — the "Lost Colony" — had vanished. The letters "CRO" were carved on a tree and "CROATOAN" on a palisade post, with no cross indicating distress, suggesting the colonists had moved to Croatoan Island, home of the friendly chief Manteo's people.

The Croatoan people

The Croatoan (or Hatteras) were a small Algonquian-speaking group allied with the English. Later theories propose the surviving colonists were assimilated into the Croatoan or other local tribes; archaeological work on Hatteras continues to explore this.
