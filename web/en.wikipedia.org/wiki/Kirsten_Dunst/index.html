Kirsten Caroline Dunst (born April 30, 1982) is an American actress. She gained recognition for her portrayal of the child vampire Claudia in the horror film Interview with the Vampire (1994), which earned her a Golden Globe nomination for Best Supporting Actress. She also had roles in her youth in Little Women (1994) and Jumanji (1995).

Dunst transitioned to leading roles in teen films such as the drama The Virgin Suicides (1999), the cheerleading comedy Bring It On (2000), and the romantic comedy Get Over It (2001). She achieved international fame for her role as Mary Jane Watson in Sam Raimi's Spider-Man trilogy (2002–2007).

Career

Dunst's later work includes the romantic drama Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind (2004), the title role in Sofia Coppola's Marie Antoinette (2006), and Lars von Trier's science fiction drama Melancholia (2011), for which she won the Cannes Film Festival Award for Best Actress. She received critical acclaim for her performance in the second season of the FX series Fargo (2015), earning a Golden Globe nomination, and for her leading role in Jane Campion's western drama The Power of the Dog (2021), which earned her a nomination for the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress.

Personal life

Dunst began dating her Fargo co-star Jesse Plemons in 2016; they married in 2022 and have two sons. She has been open about her experiences with depression and has supported various charitable causes.
