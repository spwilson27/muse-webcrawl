Peter Jennings

Peter Jennings
Born29 July 1938, Toronto
Died7 August 2005, New York City
OccupationBroadcast journalist (ABC News)
[Image: Jennings at the World News Tonight desk]

Peter Jennings (1938–2005) was a Canadian-American journalist who anchored ABC's World News Tonight from 1983 until his death from lung cancer in 2005, one of the 'big three' American anchors of his era with Tom Brokaw and Dan Rather. A former foreign correspondent, he reported from the Middle East and anchored through the fall of the Berlin Wall, the Gulf War and 9/11.

Style

Jennings, who left high school in Canada and learned broadcasting from the ground up, was noted for international knowledge and composure in marathon coverage; his death mid-tenure marked the end of the long-anchor era.
