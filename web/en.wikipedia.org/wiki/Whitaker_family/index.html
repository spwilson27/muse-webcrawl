Whitaker family

The Whitaker family is an English family whose members were prominent in publishing, bookselling, and wine shipping in the nineteenth and twentieth centuries. The family is best known for founding Whitaker's Almanack and The Bookseller, reference works long central to the British book trade.

Joseph Whitaker

Joseph Whitaker (1820–1895) was a bookseller and publisher who founded The Bookseller in 1858, a trade journal still associated with the British publishing industry. In 1868 he launched Whitaker's Almanack, an annual reference book of statistics and general information that continued publication for more than 150 years. He also established Whitaker's Cumulative Book List (later the basis of The British National Bibliography), recording British publishing output.

Later members

The publishing business passed through generations of the family, with the firm of J. Whitaker & Sons based in London. Family members also became involved in the sherry trade in Spain; the Whitaker name is associated with the Marsala wine trade in Sicily, where the family established wine-making interests in the nineteenth century alongside families such as the Inghams and Woodhouses.
