Coastal Forces of the Royal Navy

The Coastal Forces were a division of the Royal Navy operating small, fast craft — motor torpedo boats (MTBs), motor gunboats (MGBs) and motor launches — for coastal defence and attack. They were at their largest during the Second World War.

Second World War

Coastal Forces fought German E-boats in the English Channel and North Sea, escorted convoys, landed agents and commandos, and operated in the Mediterranean and Far East. Largely manned by Royal Naval Volunteer Reserve officers and hostilities-only ratings, the forces earned the nickname "the little ships".

Post-war

Coastal Forces were disbanded in the 1950s as fast patrol boats were absorbed into the regular fleet organisation.
