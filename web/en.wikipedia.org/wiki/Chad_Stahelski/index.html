Chad Stahelski (born September 20, 1968) is an American stuntman, stunt coordinator, and film director. He is best known as the director of the John Wick film franchise, starring Keanu Reeves, which he has directed since the first film in 2014. Before directing, Stahelski worked extensively as a stunt performer and coordinator, and he co-founded the stunt design and production company 87eleven (later 87North Productions) with David Leitch.

Early life

Stahelski was born in Palmer, Massachusetts. He developed an interest in martial arts at a young age and trained in various disciplines, including kickboxing and Jeet Kune Do. He attended the Inosanto Academy, where he studied under Dan Inosanto, a student of Bruce Lee.

Career

Stunt work

Stahelski entered the film industry as a stunt performer in the early 1990s. His breakthrough came when he served as the stunt double for Brandon Lee on The Crow (1994); after Lee's accidental death on set, Stahelski, a friend of Lee's, helped complete the film. He later worked as Keanu Reeves's stunt double on The Matrix (1999) and served as a martial arts stunt coordinator on its sequels. His stunt and fight coordination credits include 300, The Hunger Games series, and numerous other action films.

Directing

In 2014, Stahelski made his directorial debut with John Wick, co-directed with David Leitch (though only Stahelski received credit). The film was a critical and commercial success and launched a franchise. Stahelski directed the sequels John Wick: Chapter 2 (2017), John Wick: Chapter 3 – Parabellum (2019), and John Wick: Chapter 4 (2023) alone. The series is praised for its long-take action choreography, a style often described as "gun fu".

Stahelski has also worked as a second-unit director and producer on other action productions, and his company 87North has produced films such as Atomic Blonde and Nobody.
