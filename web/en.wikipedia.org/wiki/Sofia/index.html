Sofia

Sofia is the capital and largest city of Bulgaria, with about 1.3 million residents. It lies in the Sofia Valley at the foot of Vitosha Mountain in western Bulgaria, at an elevation of about 550 metres.

Sofia is one of Europe's oldest capitals, inhabited since the Neolithic and known in antiquity as Serdica — a favourite city of Constantine the Great ('my Rome'). Its landmarks from successive eras cluster remarkably: the Roman Serdica ruins, the 4th-century St George Rotunda, the medieval Boyana Church (UNESCO), Ottoman mosques, the vast golden-domed Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, and Soviet-era monuments. Mineral springs in the centre have been used since Roman times. Sofia became Bulgaria's capital in 1879.
