Nicolas Cage

Nicolas Cage (born Nicolas Kim Coppola, January 7, 1964) is an American actor and film producer. A nephew of director Francis Ford Coppola, he changed his surname — after the Marvel Comics character Luke Cage — to make his own way in Hollywood.

Career

Cage rose to prominence in the 1980s with Valley Girl (1983), Birdy (1984), Raising Arizona (1987) and Moonstruck (1987). He won critical acclaim and the Academy Award for Best Actor playing an alcoholic in Leaving Las Vegas (1995), then became an action star in The Rock, Con Air and Face/Off (all 1996–97) and the National Treasure films. Other notable roles include Adaptation (2002), where he played twin brothers, Lord of War (2005) and Ghost Rider (2007).

Later work

After a period of financial difficulties and many direct-to-video productions, Cage enjoyed a critical resurgence with Mandy (2018), Color Out of Space (2019), Pig (2021), The Unbearable Weight of Massive Talent (2022), Dream Scenario (2023) and Longlegs (2024). Known for an intense, self-described "Nouveau Shamanic" acting style, he is regarded as one of the most distinctive performers of his generation.
