Vatsyayana

Vātsyāyana, also known as Mallanaga, was an ancient Indian philosopher and scholar traditionally credited as the author of the Kama Sutra, a Sanskrit text on human sexual behaviour, love, and the nature of relationships, probably composed between the 2nd and 4th centuries CE.

Little is known of his life; tradition places him in Varanasi. His name derives from the sage Vatsa. The Kama Sutra presents kama (pleasure and desire) as one of the goals of human life alongside dharma and artha, and discusses courtship, marriage, household life, and the arts expected of a cultured citizen, in addition to its famous sections on sexuality.

Another work, the Nyāya Sūtra Bhāṣya, a foundational commentary on logic, is also attributed to a Vatsyayana (Pakṣilasvāmin), though scholars debate whether this was the same person.
