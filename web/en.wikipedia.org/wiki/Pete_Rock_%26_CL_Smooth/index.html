Pete Rock & CL Smooth were an American hip hop duo from Mount Vernon, New York, consisting of producer/DJ Pete Rock (Peter Phillips) and rapper CL Smooth (Corey Penn). They are best known for their 1992 single "They Reminisce Over You (T.R.O.Y.)", widely regarded as one of the greatest hip hop songs of all time, and for their two studio albums Mecca and the Soul Brother (1992) and The Main Ingredient (1994).

Career

The duo debuted with the EP All Souled Out in 1991. Their first album, Mecca and the Soul Brother, showcased Pete Rock's jazz- and soul-inflected sampling and CL Smooth's fluid, reflective lyricism. "They Reminisce Over You (T.R.O.Y.)", written in memory of their friend Troy Dixon (Trouble T-Roy of Heavy D & the Boyz), became their signature song. The follow-up album The Main Ingredient (1994) was critically acclaimed, but creative and personal tensions led the pair to split in 1995.

Later work

Both members pursued solo careers; Pete Rock became one of hip hop's most celebrated producers. The duo reunited periodically for tours and festival performances, including appearances in the 2010s, though a long-discussed third album did not materialise.
