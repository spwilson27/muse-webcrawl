The Lion King

The Lion King is a 1994 American animated musical drama film produced by Walt Disney Feature Animation and released by Walt Disney Pictures. Directed by Roger Allers and Rob Minkoff, it is Disney's 32nd animated feature film.

Plot

Simba, a young lion cub and heir to King Mufasa, is tricked by his envious uncle Scar into believing he caused Mufasa's death in a wildebeest stampede — in fact engineered by Scar. Fleeing into exile, Simba grows up with the carefree meerkat Timon and warthog Pumbaa under the philosophy "hakuna matata." As an adult, reunited with his childhood friend Nala and guided by the mandrill Rafiki, Simba returns to Pride Rock to confront Scar and take his rightful place in the "Circle of Life."

Music

The film's songs — "Circle of Life," "I Just Can't Wait to Be King," "Be Prepared," "Hakuna Matata" and "Can You Feel the Love Tonight" — were written by Elton John and Tim Rice, with a score by Hans Zimmer drawing on African choral music arranged by Lebo M. The film won Academy Awards for Best Original Score and Best Original Song ("Can You Feel the Love Tonight").

Reception and legacy

The Lion King was the highest-grossing film of 1994 and the highest-grossing traditionally animated film of all time; it is widely regarded as one of the greatest animated films ever made. It spawned direct-to-video sequels, a long-running Broadway musical (1997) that became one of the most successful stage productions in history, television series, and a photorealistic remake in 2019 directed by Jon Favreau that grossed over $1.6 billion. In 2016 it was selected for the National Film Registry.
