Grand Tour (cycling)

In professional road cycling, a Grand Tour is one of the three major European stage races: the Tour de France, the Giro d'Italia, and the Vuelta a España. Each lasts three weeks and is considered among the most prestigious and demanding events in the sport.

Format

Each Grand Tour consists of 21 stages — flat, mountain, and time-trial stages — held over 23 days including two rest days. Riders compete for the general classification (the leader's distinctive jersey: yellow at the Tour, pink at the Giro, red at the Vuelta) as well as points, mountains, and young-rider classifications.

Records

Only seven riders have won all three Grand Tours in their careers. Eddy Merckx holds the record with eleven Grand Tour victories, and also holds the record for most stage wins across the three races. Winning all three in a single year has never been achieved by a male rider.
