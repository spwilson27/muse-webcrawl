Highfield Road was a football stadium in Coventry, England, and the home of Coventry City Football Club for 106 years, from 1899 until 2005. Opened on 9 September 1899 with a match against Shrewsbury Town, it was developed over the decades into a compact ground close to the city centre, with a record attendance of 51,455 for a match against Wolverhampton Wanderers in 1967.

History

Highfield Road witnessed Coventry's rise to the First Division under Jimmy Hill in 1967 and 34 consecutive seasons in the top flight, as well as the club's 1987 FA Cup triumph, celebrated elsewhere. In 1981 it controversially became England's first all-seater stadium, an experiment reversed within a few years. The ground's final match was a 6–2 Coventry victory over Derby County on 30 April 2005.

After closure

Coventry City moved to the new Coventry Building Society Arena (then the Ricoh Arena) in 2005. Highfield Road was demolished and the site redeveloped for housing, with street names and a small landscaped area commemorating the club's former home.
