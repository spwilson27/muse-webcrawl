Nelson Bunker Hunt (22 February 1926 – 21 October 2014) was an American oil company executive and billionaire. The son of oil tycoon H. L. Hunt, he became best known for an attempt, together with his brothers William Herbert Hunt and Lamar Hunt, to corner the world market in silver during the late 1970s, an episode that ended in a spectacular price collapse in 1980.

Early life and oil career

Hunt was born in El Dorado, Arkansas, and raised in Texas. Working in the family oil business, he developed oil fields internationally, most notably in Libya, where the discovery of the Sarir field made him enormously wealthy. The nationalisation of Libyan oil assets under Muammar Gaddafi in 1973 curtailed those holdings and turned Hunt toward commodity speculation as a hedge against inflation and currency risk.

Silver speculation

Believing silver was undervalued, Hunt and his brothers began accumulating the metal in the early 1970s, ultimately controlling, through physical holdings and futures contracts, an estimated 100 million troy ounces or more — a substantial share of the world's supply. The price of silver rose from about $6 per ounce in early 1979 to a record near $50 in January 1980.

Regulators responded: the COMEX exchange adopted "Silver Rule 7," restricting purchases to liquidation-only trading. On 27 March 1980 — "Silver Thursday" — the price collapsed, and the Hunts faced massive margin calls. A consortium of banks arranged a loan of roughly $1.1 billion to avert a broader financial crisis. The brothers were later found civilly liable for market manipulation, fined, and banned from commodity trading; Bunker Hunt declared bankruptcy in 1988.

Thoroughbred racing

Hunt was also a prominent thoroughbred racehorse owner and breeder, whose horses included Vaguely Noble, Dahlia, and Youth, winning major races in Europe and North America. The dispersal of his bloodstock in the late 1980s, following his bankruptcy, was among the largest in racing history.

Death

Hunt died in Dallas, Texas, on 21 October 2014, at the age of 88.
