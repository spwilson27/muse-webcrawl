Hishikawa Morohira

Hishikawa Morohira was a Japanese artist of the early Edo period and a pupil of Hishikawa Moronobu, the founder of the ukiyo-e school of woodblock printing and painting.

Work

Morohira worked in the style of his master, producing illustrations and paintings of beauties and scenes of everyday life in the early decades of the 18th century. Few signed works survive, and details of his life are little documented compared with his celebrated teacher.
