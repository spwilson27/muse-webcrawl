Home Fleet

The Home Fleet was a fleet of the Royal Navy that operated from the United Kingdom's territorial waters from 1902 with intervals until 1967. It guarded home waters and, in wartime, formed the main battle fleet against Germany.

History

Created amid the Anglo-German naval race, the Home Fleet fought at Jutland's supporting operations in the First World War era as the Grand Fleet's successor organisation took shape. In the Second World War, based at Scapa Flow, it covered Arctic convoys, hunted German surface raiders including the Bismarck and Tirpitz, and protected the Atlantic approaches.

Postwar

After 1945 the fleet became a NATO-committed force for the Eastern Atlantic before being merged into the Western Fleet in 1967, ending the name.
