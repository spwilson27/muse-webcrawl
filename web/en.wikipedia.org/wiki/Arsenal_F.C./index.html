Arsenal Football Club is a professional football club based in Islington, London, England. Arsenal compete in the Premier League, the top tier of English football. The club has won 13 league titles, a record 14 FA Cups, 2 League Cups, 17 FA Community Shields and the European Cup Winners' Cup. In terms of trophies won, it is the third-most successful club in English football.

History

The club was founded in 1886 by munitions workers at the Royal Arsenal armaments factory in Woolwich, south-east London, under the name Dial Square; it soon turned professional and joined the Football League in 1893, the first club from the south to do so. In 1913 the club crossed the city to Arsenal Stadium in Highbury, north London.

In the 1930s, under manager Herbert Chapman and his successors, Arsenal won five league championships and two FA Cups, becoming the dominant club in English football. After a leaner period, George Graham's sides won the league in 1989 — dramatically, at Anfield on the final day — and again in 1991, as well as the Cup Winners' Cup in 1994.

The appointment of French manager Arsène Wenger in 1996 transformed the club. Wenger revolutionised training, diet and scouting in English football and led Arsenal to league and cup doubles in 1998 and 2002. His 2003–04 side, nicknamed "The Invincibles", won the Premier League without a single defeat — the only team to do so in a 38-game season. In 2006 the club moved to the new Emirates Stadium and reached its only Champions League final, losing to Barcelona.

Recent years

Wenger retired in 2018 after 22 years. After a short spell under Unai Emery, former captain Mikel Arteta became manager in December 2019, winning the FA Cup in his first season and rebuilding the side into Premier League title challengers in the 2020s.

Identity

Arsenal's players wear red shirts with white sleeves, a design introduced by Chapman in 1933, and the club's nickname "The Gunners" and cannon crest reflect its origins in the Woolwich armaments works. Its fiercest rivalry is the North London derby with Tottenham Hotspur. The club motto is Victoria Concordia Crescit — "Victory Through Harmony".
