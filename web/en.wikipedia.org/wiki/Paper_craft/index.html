Paper craft
MediumPaper and card
TechniquesFolding, cutting, layering, moulding, modelling

Paper craft is the collection of art forms employing paper or card as the primary artistic medium for the creation of two- or three-dimensional objects. Paper has been used for craft since its invention in China in the second century AD, and paper crafts developed independently in many cultures.

Types

Major forms include origami (Japanese paper folding), kirigami (folding and cutting), paper cutting traditions such as Chinese jianzhi, German Scherenschnitte, and Polish wycinanki; quilling (paper filigree); papier-mâché; paper modelling and card models of buildings, vehicles, and figures; decoupage; scrapbooking and card making; and paper marbling and bookbinding as decorative crafts.

Materials and tools

Crafters work with construction paper, tissue, crepe paper, cardstock, and handmade papers, using scissors, craft knives, scoring tools, adhesives, and die-cutting machines. In education, paper craft is widely used to develop fine motor skills and spatial reasoning.
