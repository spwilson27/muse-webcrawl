New7Wonders of the World

The New7Wonders of the World was a campaign begun in 2000 to choose a new list of wonders from a selection of 200 existing monuments, echoing the ancient Seven Wonders of the World. Organized by the New7Wonders Foundation, the winners were decided by a global poll conducted by internet and telephone and were announced on 7 July 2007 in Lisbon, Portugal.

The winners

  • The Great Wall of China
  • Petra, Jordan
  • Christ the Redeemer, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil
  • Machu Picchu, Peru
  • Chichen Itza, Mexico
  • The Colosseum, Rome, Italy
  • The Taj Mahal, India

The Great Pyramid of Giza, the only surviving wonder of the ancient list, was granted honorary status and removed from the voting.

Reception

The campaign drew more than 100 million votes, but it was criticized by UNESCO, which distanced itself from the privately organized poll and noted that the selection reflected only the opinions of those with access to the voting. The foundation went on to run similar campaigns, including the New7Wonders of Nature.
