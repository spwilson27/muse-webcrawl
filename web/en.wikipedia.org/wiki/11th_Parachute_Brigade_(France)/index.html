11th Parachute Brigade (France)

The 11th Parachute Brigade (French: 11e brigade parachutiste, 11e BP) is an airborne infantry brigade of the French Army. Headquartered at Balma, near Toulouse in southwestern France, it is one of the French Army's rapid-reaction formations and groups most of the army's parachute regiments. Its soldiers wear the red beret (amaranth beret) common to French metropolitan paratroopers.

Country: France

Branch: French Army

Type: Airborne infantry

History

The brigade descends from the parachute divisions created after the Second World War, in which French paratroopers fought in Indochina and Algeria. The 11th Light Intervention Division was formed in 1961 and later became the 11th Parachute Division in 1971; in the army reorganisation of 1999 the division was reduced to brigade status as the 11th Parachute Brigade. Elements of the brigade have served in Lebanon, Chad, the Central African Republic, the Balkans, Afghanistan and Mali, as well as in numerous evacuation and humanitarian operations.

Composition

The brigade includes the 1st Parachute Chasseur Regiment (1er RCP) at Pamiers, the 1st Parachute Hussar Regiment (1er RHP) at Tarbes, the 2nd Foreign Parachute Regiment (2e REP) of the French Foreign Legion at Calvi in Corsica, the 3rd and 8th Marine Infantry Parachute Regiments (3e and 8e RPIMa) at Carcassonne and Castres, the 35th Parachute Artillery Regiment (35e RAP) at Tarbes, the 17th Parachute Engineer Regiment (17e RGP) at Montauban, and the 1st Parachute Train Regiment (1er RTP), together with a brigade headquarters company and the parachute school (ETAP) at Pau. The brigade works closely with the Army's airmobile and special forces brigades and with the air force transports that deliver it.

Role

The brigade is trained for parachute and air-landed assault, seizure of airheads, non-combatant evacuation operations and rapid reinforcement of overseas French forces. Its units maintain an airborne alert detachment (gendarmerie-free "Guépard" readiness posture) able to deploy at short notice.
