Jason Epstein

Jason Epstein
Born: 25 August 1928, Cambridge, Massachusetts, U.S.
Died: 4 February 2022, Sag Harbor, New York
Occupation: Editor, publisher

Jason Wolkow Epstein (August 25, 1928 – February 4, 2022) was an American editor and publisher. At Doubleday he launched Anchor Books in 1953, pioneering the quality trade paperback in the United States.

Career

Epstein was a co-founder of The New York Review of Books in 1963. He spent two decades as editorial director of Random House, where he edited writers including Norman Mailer, Gore Vidal, Philip Roth and Edmund Wilson, and created the Library of America in 1979 to keep classic American literature in print. Late in life he co-founded On Demand Books, maker of the Espresso Book Machine for print-on-demand.

Works

His books include Book Business: Publishing Past, Present, and Future (2001), a widely read account of the transformation of publishing.
