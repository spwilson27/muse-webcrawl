Television Broadcasts Limited, commonly known as TVB, is a television broadcasting company based in Hong Kong. Founded in 1967, it was the territory's first wireless (free-to-air) commercial television station and for decades dominated Cantonese-language television, producing hugely popular drama serials, variety shows and news through its flagship Jade channel (Cantonese) and Pearl channel (English).

History

TVB was co-founded by film magnate Sir Run Run Shaw, who steered its programme production with a studio system modelled on Shaw Brothers; its artist training classes launched the careers of stars such as Chow Yun-fat, Tony Leung and Andy Lau. TVB dramas became a cultural staple for Cantonese-speaking audiences worldwide, distributed through TVBI and overseas channels, while its annual events—the Miss Hong Kong Pageant and TVB Anniversary Awards—became institutions. The broadcaster's headquarters moved to the purpose-built TVB City in Tseung Kwan O in 2003.

Recent years

Since the 2010s TVB has faced declining local ratings, competition from streaming services, and the loss of its monopoly after rival free-to-air licences were granted (ViuTV launched in 2016). The company has invested in mainland Chinese co-productions and its myTV SUPER streaming platform, and undergone ownership and management changes while remaining listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange (SEHK: 511).
