His Majesty's Most Loyal Opposition (United Kingdom)

His Majesty's Most Loyal Opposition, typically shortened to His Majesty's Opposition, is the main opposition party in the United Kingdom's Parliament — the largest party not in government.

Role

The Leader of the Opposition is an official salaried post, and the opposition forms a shadow cabinet that scrutinises the government, challenges its legislation, and presents itself as an alternative administration.

History

The phrase "His Majesty's Opposition" was coined, reportedly in jest, by John Hobhouse in 1826, and captures the idea that opposition can be loyal to the Crown while opposing the ministry of the day.
