The Endless (comics)

The Endless are a family of seven anthropomorphic personifications of universal forces in Neil Gaiman's comic book series The Sandman, published by DC Comics and its Vertigo imprint. They are neither gods nor mortals but embodiments of the functions they represent, existing since the beginning of the universe and ranking among the most powerful beings in the DC Universe.

The seven Endless

In order of age, the siblings are Destiny, blind and chained to his book, which records all that happens; Death, depicted as a cheerful young woman in goth dress, who guides the dead; Dream (Morpheus), the protagonist of the series, lord of the Dreaming; Destruction, who abandoned his realm in the 17th century; Desire, beautiful and cruel, of indeterminate gender; Despair, Desire's twin, a squat figure with hooked ring; and Delirium, the youngest, once Delight, whose realm is madness. Their names in English all begin with the letter D.

Role in The Sandman

The Endless are bound by rules and responsibilities toward their functions and are forbidden to love mortals or spill family blood, on pain of dire consequences. Much of the series' drama turns on Dream's rigid adherence to these rules, his feuds with Desire, and Death's role as his confidante. The family appears in spin-offs including Death: The High Cost of Living and The Sandman: Overture, and in Netflix's television adaptation of The Sandman.
