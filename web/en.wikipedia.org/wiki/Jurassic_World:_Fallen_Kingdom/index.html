Jurassic World: Fallen Kingdom is a 2018 American science fiction action film directed by J. A. Bayona and written by Derek Connolly and Colin Trevorrow. It is the sequel to Jurassic World (2015) and the fifth instalment in the Jurassic Park franchise.

Plot

Three years after the destruction of the Jurassic World theme park, a volcanic eruption threatens the dinosaurs left roaming Isla Nublar. Claire Dearing and Owen Grady join a private rescue mission funded by Benjamin Lockwood, but discover the operation is a front to auction the animals and market a new hybrid predator, the Indoraptor, as a weapon. The dinosaurs are ultimately released into the wilds of California.

Cast

Chris Pratt and Bryce Dallas Howard reprise their roles as Owen Grady and Claire Dearing. The cast also includes Rafe Spall, Justice Smith, Daniella Pineda, James Cromwell, Toby Jones, Ted Levine, B. D. Wong, and Jeff Goldblum, returning as Ian Malcolm.

Reception

The film grossed over $1.3 billion worldwide, becoming the third-highest-grossing film of 2018. Reviews were mixed, praising Bayona's direction and the darker tone while criticising the screenplay. A sequel, Jurassic World Dominion, was released in 2022.
