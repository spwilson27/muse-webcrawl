DR (broadcaster)

DR, officially the Danish Broadcasting Corporation in English (Danish: Danmarks Radio), is a Danish public-service radio and television broadcasting company. Founded in 1925 as the first fully state-owned broadcasting company in Denmark, DR is one of the oldest and largest electronic media enterprises in the country and a founding member of the European Broadcasting Union.

DR is funded by a state media licence ("medielicensen"), historically collected from households owning televisions and other devices, and since 2019 increasingly funded through general taxation under a political agreement phasing out the licence fee.

History

Broadcasting began in 1925 under the name Statsradiofonien. Television broadcasts started in 1951, making DR for decades Denmark's only television provider until TV 2 launched in 1988. The corporation is headquartered at DR Byen in Ørestad, Copenhagen.

Services

DR operates television channels including DR1 (the flagship), DR2, and the children's channel DR Ramasjang, along with the streaming service DRTV. Its radio networks include DR P1 (speech and culture), DR P2 (classical music), DR P3 (youth and popular music) and DR P4 (regional stations). DR is internationally known for drama series such as The Killing, Borgen and The Bridge (co-produced with SVT), which spearheaded the global popularity of "Nordic noir".

Governance and remit

DR's activities are governed by the Danish Radio and Television Broadcasting Act and by a public service contract with the Ministry of Culture, obliging it to provide broad programming in news, culture, education and entertainment in Danish.
