December solstice

December solstice
Date20-22 December
HemisphereWinter (north), summer (south)

The December solstice occurs when the Sun reaches its southernmost declination of -23.4 degrees, around 21 December. It is the winter solstice in the Northern Hemisphere - the shortest day - and the summer solstice in the Southern Hemisphere.

Astronomy

At this moment the South Pole is tilted most toward the Sun; the Sun is directly overhead at noon on the Tropic of Capricorn. Dates vary between 20 and 22 December due to the calendar and orbital variations.

Culture

Festivals near it include Yule, Dongzhi, and historically Saturnalia and Inti Raymi observances in the south. Many Christmas customs absorbed midwinter traditions.
