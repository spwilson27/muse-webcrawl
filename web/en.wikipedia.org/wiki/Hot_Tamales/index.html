Hot Tamales

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Hot Tamales is an American brand of cinnamon-flavoured chewy candy introduced in 1950 by the Just Born candy company of Bethlehem, Pennsylvania. The oblong red candies — similar in shape and texture to Just Born's Mike and Ike — are flavoured with cinnamon to give a spicy "hot" taste, and are named after the Mexican tamale.

History and marketing

According to Just Born, Hot Tamales were developed as a way to use surplus production capacity and quickly became one of the best-selling cinnamon candies in the United States. The brand's mascot and advertising have long played on fire imagery; in 2012 the brand ran a campaign built around a character searching for the source of the candy's fictional "fire".

Varieties

Related products have included Hot Tamales Fire & Ice (a combination of the original candy with a cooling mint flavour, later reintroduced), Hot Tamales 3 Alarm (three levels of heat), and Hot Tamales Tropical Heat. The candy is sold in theatre boxes and bags throughout North America and is a popular cinema concession item.
