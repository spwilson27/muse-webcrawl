Defensible space (fire control)

Defensible space in wildfire protection is a managed zone around a building where vegetation and combustible materials are reduced to slow fire spread and give firefighters room to operate.

Zones

Guidance commonly describes an immediate zone near the house kept lean and green, an intermediate zone with spaced plantings, and an extended zone reducing dense fuels.

Effectiveness

Defensible space combined with fire-resistant construction improves survival during wildfires, but does not guarantee protection in extreme fire weather.

Regulation

Fire-prone jurisdictions in places such as California and Australia require property owners to maintain defensible space.
