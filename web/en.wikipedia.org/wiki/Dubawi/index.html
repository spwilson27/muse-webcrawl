Dubawi

Dubawi (foaled 7 February 2002) is a British-bred Thoroughbred racehorse and one of the world's most successful stallions. The only British-bred son of the great Dubai Millennium, he was named European champion two-year-old of 2004 and went on to win three Group 1 races before retiring to stud, where he has become the flagship sire of Darley and the Godolphin breeding operation.

Racing career

Trained by Saeed bin Suroor for Godolphin, Dubawi won all three of his starts as a two-year-old in 2004, including the National Stakes in Ireland and the Superlative Stakes. At three he finished third in the 2000 Guineas, won the Irish 2,000 Guineas, and then defeated older horses in the Prix Jacques Le Marois at Deauville. He was retired after injury later that season.

Stud career

Standing principally at Dalham Hall Stud in Newmarket, Dubawi rose to become one of the most expensive stallions in the world, with a stud fee reaching £350,000. His progeny include numerous Group 1 winners such as Ghaiyyath, Too Darn Hot, Zarak, New Bay, Postponed, Monterosso (winner of the Dubai World Cup), Night of Thunder, Space Blues, and Modern Games. He has been champion sire in Britain and Ireland, and his sons have themselves become leading stallions.

Pedigree

Dubawi is by Dubai Millennium out of Zomaradah, a Deploy mare who won the Oaks d'Italia. As Dubai Millennium died after a single season at stud, Dubawi has been essential to the survival of his sire line.
