Charlotte Guest

Lady Charlotte Elizabeth Guest (née Bertie; 19 May 1812 – 15 January 1895) was an English translator and businesswoman, best known for her English translation of the Mabinogion, the collection of medieval Welsh prose tales.

Life and work

Born into the Bertie family of Lincolnshire, she married John Josiah Guest, the Welsh ironmaster of the Dowlais Ironworks in Merthyr Tydfil, in 1833. She learned Welsh and, between 1838 and 1849, published her translation of the Mabinogion tales in parts, bringing Welsh medieval literature to a wide English-speaking audience for the first time.

After her husband's death in 1852, she took over the running of the Dowlais works, one of the largest ironworks in the world, managing it on behalf of her son until 1855, when she married Charles Schreiber and later built a famous collection of ceramics, fans, and playing cards, much of which she bequeathed to the Victoria and Albert Museum and the British Museum.
