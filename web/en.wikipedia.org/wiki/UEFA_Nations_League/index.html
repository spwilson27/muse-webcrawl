The UEFA Nations League is an international football competition played by the senior men's national teams of the member associations of UEFA, the sport's European governing body. The first tournament began in September 2018, following the 2018 FIFA World Cup. The competition largely replaces international friendly matches previously played on the FIFA International Match Calendar, giving them greater sporting significance.

History

The idea of a league of nations for UEFA members was discussed for several years before UEFA's executive committee approved the competition; the format was formally adopted in 2014. The stated aims were to improve the quality and standing of national team football, reduce the number of meaningless friendlies, and give teams competitive matches against similarly ranked opponents. All 55 UEFA national teams take part.

Format

Teams are divided into four leagues (A, B, C and D) based on their ranking, with promotion and relegation between leagues after each edition. Within Leagues A, B and C, teams are drawn into groups of three or four and play each other home and away. The four group winners of League A advance to the Nations League Finals, a knockout mini-tournament with semi-finals, a third-place match and a final, held the following year. The competition is also partly linked to qualification for the UEFA European Championship and the FIFA World Cup, offering play-off paths to teams that fail to qualify directly.
