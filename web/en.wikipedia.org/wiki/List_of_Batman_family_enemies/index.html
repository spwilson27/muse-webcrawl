List of Batman family enemies

The Batman family of characters created by DC Comics features one of the most famous rogues galleries in popular culture. The following is a list of prominent adversaries of Batman and his allies, with their first appearances in comics (unless otherwise noted).

CharacterFirst appearanceNotes
The JokerBatman #1 (1940)Batman's archenemy; a criminal mastermind with a clown-like appearance.
Catwoman (Selina Kyle)Batman #1 (1940)Cat burglar; sometimes anti-heroine and Batman's love interest.
Hugo StrangeDetective Comics #36 (1940)Mad scientist and psychologist obsessed with Batman.
ClayfaceDetective Comics #40 (1940)Identity used by several villains; the best known, Matt Hagen and Basil Karlo, can reshape their clay-like bodies.
The Penguin (Oswald Cobblepot)Detective Comics #58 (1941)Gentleman mobster who uses weaponised umbrellas and birds.
Scarecrow (Jonathan Crane)World's Finest Comics #3 (1941)Psychologist who weaponises fear.
Two-Face (Harvey Dent)Detective Comics #66 (1942)Disfigured former district attorney obsessed with duality and chance.
The Riddler (Edward Nigma)Detective Comics #140 (1948)Criminal who challenges Batman with riddles and puzzles.
Mad Hatter (Jervis Tetch)Batman #49 (1948)Mind-control technologist modelled on the Alice in Wonderland character.
Deadshot (Floyd Lawton)Batman #59 (1950)Expert marksman and assassin.
Mr. Freeze (Victor Fries)Batman #121 (1959, as Mr. Zero)Cryogenic scientist who must live at sub-zero temperatures.
Poison Ivy (Pamela Isley)Batman #181 (1966)Botanist eco-terrorist able to control plants and secrete toxins.
Man-Bat (Kirk Langstrom)Detective Comics #400 (1970)Zoologist transformed into a bat-like creature.
Ra's al GhulBatman #232 (1971)Centuries-old leader of the League of Assassins; father of Talia.
Talia al GhulDetective Comics #411 (1971)Daughter of Ra's al Ghul; sometime ally and adversary, mother of Damian Wayne.
Killer Croc (Waylon Jones)Detective Comics #523 (1983)Reptilian-skinned enforcer of great strength.
Black Mask (Roman Sionis)Batman #386 (1985)Sadistic Gotham crime lord.
Harley Quinn (Harleen Quinzel)Batman: The Animated Series (1992); comics 1993Former psychiatrist and the Joker's accomplice, later an anti-heroine in her own right.
BaneBatman: Vengeance of Bane #1 (1993)Venom-enhanced strategist who broke Batman's back in the Knightfall storyline.
Hush (Thomas Elliot)Batman #609 (2003)Childhood friend of Bruce Wayne turned vengeful surgeon-villain.
