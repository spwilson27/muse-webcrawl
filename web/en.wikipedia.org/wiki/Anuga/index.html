Anuga (short for Allgemeine Nahrungs- und Genussmittel-Ausstellung, "General Food and Beverage Exhibition") is the world's largest trade fair for food and beverages, held every two years at the Koelnmesse exhibition centre in Cologne, Germany. It is open to trade visitors only and brings together manufacturers, importers, wholesalers, and retailers from well over 100 countries, making it a principal marketplace and trend barometer for the global food industry.

Structure and history

The fair was first held in Stuttgart in 1919 and moved to Cologne in 1924; it has been staged biennially in its modern form since the post-war period. Anuga is organised as a set of specialised trade shows under one roof — covering fine food, drinks, meat, frozen food, dairy, bread and bakery, hot beverages, chilled and fresh food, culinary concepts, and food-service technology — so that exhibitors and buyers meet within their segment. Recent editions have each drawn roughly 7,000–8,000 exhibitors from about 100 countries and around 165,000–170,000 visitors, and themes such as sustainability, plant-based products, and alternative proteins have featured prominently.
