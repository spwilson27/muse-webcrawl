Roman Holiday

Roman Holiday is a 1953 American romantic comedy film directed and produced by William Wyler. It stars Gregory Peck as an American journalist and Audrey Hepburn as Princess Ann, a European princess who, while on a state visit to Rome, escapes her guardians and falls in love with the reporter who realises her identity but pretends not to.

Production

The screenplay was written by Dalton Trumbo and John Dighton; because Trumbo was blacklisted, Ian McLellan Hunter fronted for him, and Trumbo's credit was restored decades later. The film was shot entirely on location in Rome, and its Vespa sequences introduced the scooter to a worldwide audience.

Reception and legacy

Roman Holiday launched Hepburn to stardom and won her the Academy Award for Best Actress; the film also won awards for its story and Edith Head's costume design, and received ten nominations in total. In 1999 it was selected for preservation in the United States National Film Registry as culturally, historically, or aesthetically significant.
