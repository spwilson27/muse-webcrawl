InterContinental Hotels Group, legally incorporated as InterContinental Hotels Group plc and trading as IHG Hotels & Resorts, is a British multinational hospitality company headquartered in Denham, Buckinghamshire, England. It is one of the world's largest hotel companies, measured by number of rooms, and operates, franchises and leases hotels under a portfolio of brands across nearly 100 countries.

History

The company's origins lie in the Bass brewing business, which entered the hotel trade in 1969 with the founding of the Crest Hotels chain. Bass acquired Holiday Inn International in 1988 and the North American Holiday Inn business in 1990. In 1998 Bass launched the extended-stay Staybridge Suites brand and acquired the InterContinental Hotels & Resorts brand. After selling its brewing operations, Bass renamed itself Six Continents in 2000. On 15 April 2003 the hotel and soft drinks businesses were demerged, and InterContinental Hotels Group was created as a standalone company.

In 2005 the company sold its Britvic soft drinks interest. IHG launched Hotel Indigo in 2004 and the midscale avid hotels brand in 2017. In 2018 the company introduced voco hotels, and in 2019 it acquired the luxury Six Senses Hotels Resorts Spas brand. The Regent Hotels & Resorts brand was acquired in 2018. In 2021 the company rebranded its master identity as IHG Hotels & Resorts.

Operations

IHG reports more than 6,000 open hotels and roughly 880,000 guest rooms worldwide, with a development pipeline of around 2,000 additional hotels. Most properties operate under franchise agreements, with a smaller number managed by IHG and a few owned or leased. The largest market is the Americas, followed by Europe, the Middle East and Africa (EMEAA), and Greater China.

Loyalty programme

The company's guest loyalty programme, relaunched in 2022 as IHG One Rewards, has more than 130 million members. Members earn points for stays that can be redeemed for reward nights, merchandise and experiences.
