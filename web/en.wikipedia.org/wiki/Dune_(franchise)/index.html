Dune (franchise)

Dune is an American science fiction media franchise that originated with the 1965 novel Dune by Frank Herbert, widely considered one of the greatest and best-selling science fiction novels of all time. Herbert wrote five sequels before his death in 1986; his son Brian Herbert and Kevin J. Anderson have since expanded the series with numerous prequels and sequels. The franchise also encompasses films, television series, games and comics.

Premise

Set in the distant future amid a feudal interstellar empire, the series centres on the desert planet Arrakis — the only source of the spice melange, which extends life and enables space travel. The original novels follow Paul Atreides and his descendants, exploring themes of politics, religion, ecology and power.

Adaptations

David Lynch directed a 1984 film adaptation. Denis Villeneuve's Dune (2021) and Dune: Part Two (2024) were major critical and commercial successes, winning multiple Academy Awards. Television adaptations include the 2000 miniseries Frank Herbert's Dune and the prequel series Dune: Prophecy (2024). Influential games include the real-time strategy classic Dune II (1992).
