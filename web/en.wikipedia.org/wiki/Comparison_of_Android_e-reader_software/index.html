Comparison of Android e-reader software

[Image: E-book reader app on Android]

This article compares e-reader software applications for the Android operating system used to read e-books, documents and comics on phones, tablets and dedicated E Ink devices running Android. Applications differ in the file formats they support, their handling of digital rights management (DRM), annotation and synchronisation features, and whether they are tied to a commercial bookstore.

Format support

EPUB is the most widely supported open format, followed by PDF. Proprietary ecosystems (Kindle and Kobo) add their own variants and DRM schemes, while Adobe Content Server DRM is used by many library-lending and retail apps. Specialist applications extend support to comic archives (CBZ/CBR), DjVu scans, CHM help files and plain text, often with reflow, re-pagination and text-to-speech features.
