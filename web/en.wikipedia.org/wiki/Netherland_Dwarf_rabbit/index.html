Netherland Dwarf rabbit

The Netherland Dwarf is a breed of domestic rabbit that originated in the Netherlands. Weighing about 1.1–2.5 pounds (0.50–1.13 kg), it is one of the smallest rabbit breeds in the world and is widely kept as a pet and show animal.

OriginNetherlands

Appearance

The breed has a compact, rounded body, a large head relative to its size, and short upright ears typically 5–7.5 cm long. Its neotenic appearance — retaining a juvenile look into adulthood — is a large part of its popularity. Netherland Dwarfs are recognised in a wide range of colours by both the American Rabbit Breeders Association and the British Rabbit Council.

Temperament

The breed has a reputation for being more energetic and occasionally more skittish than larger breeds, though individual temperament varies and many are gentle pets. Because of their small size and delicate bones, they require careful handling.
