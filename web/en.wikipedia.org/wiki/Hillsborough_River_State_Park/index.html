Hillsborough River State Park

Hillsborough River State Park is a Florida State Park located northeast of Tampa, near Thonotosassa. It covers more than 2,990 acres along the Hillsborough River and is one of Florida's oldest state parks, developed by the Civilian Conservation Corps in the 1930s.

Features

The park is notable for its Class II rapids — a rarity in Florida — where the river flows over outcrops of limestone. It offers camping, hiking, biking, fishing, canoeing, and a large swimming pool. Fort Foster, a replica Second Seminole War fort, stands across the highway.

History

Established in 1936, the park preserves hardwood hammocks, pine flatwoods, and floodplain swamp along the river.
