National Library of Wales
Llyfrgell Genedlaethol Cymru
Established: 1907 (royal charter)
Location: Aberystwyth, Wales
Collection: Over 6 million books and much more

The National Library of Wales (Welsh: Llyfrgell Genedlaethol Cymru) is the national legal deposit library of Wales, located in Aberystwyth. It holds the largest collection of books and archival material in Wales and one of the largest bodies of Welsh-language material in the world.

History and collections

The Library was granted its royal charter in 1907 and opened in 1909; its present building on Penglais Hill was completed in stages from 1916. As a legal deposit library it is entitled to receive a copy of every work published in the United Kingdom and Ireland. Its treasures include the Black Book of Carmarthen, the Book of Taliesin, the Hengwrt Chaucer manuscript, and the Peniarth manuscripts, among the most important in the Welsh language.
