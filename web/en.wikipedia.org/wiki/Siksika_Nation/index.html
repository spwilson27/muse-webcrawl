Siksika Nation

The Siksika Nation (Siksika: Siksiká) is a First Nation in southern Alberta, Canada. The name Siksiká comes from the Blackfoot words sik (black) and ika (foot), referring to blackened moccasins. The Siksika are one of the four nations of the Blackfoot Confederacy (Niitsitapi), alongside the Kainai, Piikani and Northern Piikani.

Territory and people

The nation's reserve, Siksika 146, lies east of Calgary along the Bow River and is one of the largest reserves in Canada. The Siksika signed Treaty 7 in 1877. The nation has several thousand registered members, and its government is based at Gleichen, Alberta.
