MAPPA

MAPPA Co., Ltd. (Japanese: 株式会社MAPPA) is a Japanese animation studio founded in 2011 by Masao Maruyama, co-founder of Madhouse. The name is an acronym of Maruyama Animation Produce Project Association. MAPPA rapidly became one of the most prominent studios of the 2010s and 2020s, producing Yuri!!! on Ice, Kakegurui, Banana Fish, Dororo, Jujutsu Kaisen, Chainsaw Man, and the final seasons of Attack on Titan, though its heavy schedules have drawn scrutiny over working conditions.

History

Maruyama, a veteran producer who had co-founded Madhouse and worked since the era of Astro Boy's Mushi Production, left Madhouse at about age 70 to found MAPPA in 2011, completing Shinichiro Watanabe's Kids on the Slope (2012) and Terror in Resonance (2014). Yuri!!! on Ice (2016) was a breakout global hit. In the late 2010s MAPPA took on an unusually large slate of high-profile adaptations, often as production-committee investor itself — a business model distinguishing it from work-for-hire studios — including taking over Attack on Titan from Wit Studio for its final season (2020–2023) and animating blockbuster manga Jujutsu Kaisen (2020– ) and Chainsaw Man (2022).

Working-conditions debate

MAPPA's output and quality earned wide acclaim, but animators have publicly criticised tight deadlines and overtime on productions such as Jujutsu Kaisen season 2 (2023), fuelling industry-wide debate about sustainable anime production and comparisons with the staff-employment model of Kyoto Animation. The studio has expanded with offices in Osaka and Sendai and invested in CG production.
