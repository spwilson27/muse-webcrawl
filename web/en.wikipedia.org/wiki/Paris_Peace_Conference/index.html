Paris Peace Conference

Paris Peace Conference may refer to several diplomatic conferences held in Paris to settle wars and redraw international arrangements:

  • Paris Peace Conference (1919–1920) — the conference of the Allied victors after the First World War, which produced the Treaty of Versailles and the other treaties with the Central Powers, and created the League of Nations.
  • Paris Peace Conference of 1856 — the congress that ended the Crimean War with the Treaty of Paris.
  • Paris Peace Conference of 1946 — the conference of the victorious Allies of the Second World War, which drafted the Paris Peace Treaties of 1947 with Italy, Romania, Hungary, Bulgaria and Finland.
  • Paris Peace Accords of 1973 — the negotiations and agreement ending direct United States involvement in the Vietnam War.
