Scribus

Scribus is a free and open-source desktop publishing (DTP) application, released under the GNU General Public License. Available for Linux, Unix, macOS, Windows and other operating systems, it is designed for page layout, typesetting and preparing files for professional-quality image-setting equipment.

Features

Scribus supports most major bitmap formats, SVG vector graphics, CMYK and spot colours, ICC colour management, and versatile PDF creation including PDF/X-3 and PDF/X-4 export. Its native file format is XML-based (.sla). The application is built on the Qt toolkit and includes more than 200 colour palettes from commercial vendors and standards bodies.

History

Scribus was originally written by Franz Schmid and first released in 2001. The 1.4 series became the long-running stable branch; the 1.5 development series introduced a rewritten user interface and text engine, leading toward the 1.6 and later 1.7 releases. It is widely used for newsletters, brochures, small newspapers, posters and books, and is often cited as the leading open-source alternative to Adobe InDesign and QuarkXPress.
