Output device

A computer monitor is the most common visual output device

An output device is any piece of computer hardware used to communicate the results of data processing carried out by an information processing system (such as a computer) to the outside world. The results may be presented visually, audibly, or as a physical product.

Examples

  • Display devices — monitors and screens using LCD, LED, OLED and earlier CRT technologies; also video projectors and head-mounted displays.
  • Printers and plotters — produce hard copy on paper or other media.
  • Speakers and headphones — convert digital audio signals into sound.
  • Braille embossers and refreshable braille displays — tactile output for visually impaired users.
  • Actuators and machine tools — in embedded and industrial systems, output may take the form of motion or other physical action.

Output devices are contrasted with input devices, through which users and sensors supply data to the system. Some devices, such as touchscreens and storage drives, function as both input and output devices.
