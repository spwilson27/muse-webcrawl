WASH

WASH (or Watsan) is an acronym used in international development for "water, sanitation and hygiene". Access to safe water, adequate sanitation and hygiene education is a major public-health priority, embedded in Sustainable Development Goal 6.

Components

Water: safe drinking-water supply, water quality and quantity, and water resource management. Sanitation: safe containment, emptying, transport, treatment and disposal or reuse of human excreta and wastewater. Hygiene: handwashing with soap, menstrual hygiene management, food hygiene and safe water storage.

Health impact

Inadequate WASH is a leading risk factor for diarrhoeal disease, cholera, typhoid, trachoma, schistosomiasis and undernutrition in children. Diarrhoea linked to unsafe water and sanitation kills hundreds of thousands of children under five each year.

Monitoring

The WHO/UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme tracks global progress using "ladders" for drinking water, sanitation and hygiene, from open defecation and unimproved sources to safely managed services. Billions of people still lack safely managed services, especially in rural and low-income settings.
