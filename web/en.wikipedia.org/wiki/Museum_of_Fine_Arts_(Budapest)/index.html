Museum of Fine Arts (Budapest)

The Museum of Fine Arts (Hungarian: Szépművészeti Múzeum) is a museum in Heroes' Square, Budapest, Hungary, facing the Palace of Art. Built between 1900 and 1906 in Neoclassical style to designs by Albert Schickedanz and Fülöp Herzog, it houses Hungary's principal collection of international art.

Collections

The museum holds Egyptian art, classical antiquities, Old Master paintings — including works by Raphael, Titian, El Greco and Rembrandt, many from the Esterházy collection — 19th- and 20th-century art, prints and drawings. Its Egyptian collection is one of the richest in Central Europe.

The building underwent a major renovation and reopened in 2018, restoring spaces closed for decades.
