Ajax

Ajax may refer to several subjects. This article summarises the principal meanings.

Mythology

Ajax the Great and Ajax the Lesser were Greek heroes of the Trojan War in Homer's Iliad. Ajax the Great, son of Telamon, king of Salamis, was a towering warrior second only to Achilles; Ajax the Lesser, son of Oileus, was a swift Locrian leader. The tragedy Ajax by Sophocles dramatises the death of Ajax the Great.

Football

AFC Ajax is a Dutch professional football club based in Amsterdam, founded in 1900, one of the most successful clubs in the Netherlands and Europe, renowned for its youth academy and the 'total football' sides of the 1970s. Other clubs named Ajax exist in South Africa (Ajax Cape Town) and elsewhere.

Computing

Ajax (Asynchronous JavaScript and XML) is a set of web development techniques for creating asynchronous web applications that exchange data with a server behind the scenes without reloading the whole page.

Other uses

Ajax is also a town in Ontario, Canada; a brand of household cleaning products; the British Army's Ajax armoured fighting vehicle family; and a play by Sophocles. See also the disambiguation listings for ships, people and places bearing the name.
