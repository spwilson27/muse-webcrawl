LifeStraw

LifeStraw
Type: Water filter
Manufacturer: Vestergaard
Introduced: 2005
Purpose: Point-of-use water purification

LifeStraw is a brand of water filtration and purification devices developed by the Swiss-based company Vestergaard. The original LifeStraw, launched in 2005, is a portable water filter in the form of a straw designed to be used by a single person drinking directly from a water source.

Design and performance

The device uses hollow-fibre membrane microfiltration to remove bacteria (such as E. coli and Salmonella), waterborne protozoan parasites (such as Giardia and Cryptosporidium) and other particles, without chemicals, batteries or moving parts. Later versions of the product line include larger family units, bottles and gravity bags for households, schools and outdoor recreation, and some models add activated carbon to reduce chemicals and improve taste. The filters do not remove viruses or dissolved salts.

Humanitarian use

LifeStraw originated partly as a tool in the campaign against Guinea worm disease, for which Vestergaard developed simple pipe filters distributed in endemic regions. The consumer products are sold partly on a model that funds distribution of purifiers to schools and communities in developing countries, and the devices have been deployed widely in disaster relief and in regions lacking safe drinking water.
