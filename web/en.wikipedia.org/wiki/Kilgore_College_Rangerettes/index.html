Kilgore College Rangerettes

The Kilgore College Rangerettes, also known simply as the Rangerettes, are a precision dance team from Kilgore College in Kilgore, Texas. Founded in 1940 by Gussie Nell Davis, they are recognised as the first precision dance drill team in the United States.

History and performances

Davis created the team both to entertain football crowds and to encourage more women to attend the college. Wearing their distinctive red, white and blue uniforms with cowboy hats and boots, the Rangerettes perform high-kick routines in the style of the Rockettes. They have performed at presidential inaugurations, the Cotton Bowl, the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade and in numerous countries abroad.
