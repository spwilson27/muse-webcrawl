Pitfall!

Pitfall! is a platform video game designed by David Crane for the Atari 2600 and released in 1982 by Activision. The player controls Pitfall Harry, who must navigate a jungle, collecting 32 treasures within a 20-minute time limit while avoiding hazards such as pits, quicksand, crocodiles, scorpions, and rolling logs.

Gameplay

Harry can run left or right across 255 screens connected horizontally, swing on vines over pits, climb ladders between surface and underground passages, and jump over obstacles. Losing a contact with hazards costs points; falling into pits or being bitten costs one of three lives.

Development and reception

Crane programmed the game in about 1,000 hours, achieving unusually smooth character animation for the 2600 hardware. Pitfall! was a major commercial success, selling over four million copies and remaining at the top of the Billboard video game chart for 64 weeks. It is widely considered one of the greatest video games of all time and a landmark of the platform genre.

Legacy

The game spawned a sequel, Pitfall II: Lost Caverns (1984), an animated television series segment, and later re-imaginings on newer consoles. A perfect score of 199,000 points could be achieved only by collecting all treasures without losing any points.
