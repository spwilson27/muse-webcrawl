The Borough of Ashford is a local government district with borough status in Kent, England. Its council is based in the town of Ashford. It is the largest district in Kent by area.

Geography

The borough covers part of the North Downs and the Weald, including Tenterden, Wye and many villages. The rivers Stour and Rother flow through it.

History

The district was formed on 1 April 1974 under the Local Government Act 1972, merging Ashford Urban District with surrounding rural districts. Borough status followed in 1974.

Transport

Ashford International station provides Eurostar and high-speed Southeastern services via HS1; the M20 motorway crosses the borough.
