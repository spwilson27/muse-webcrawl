United States Ship

United States Ship (abbreviated USS or U.S.S.) is a ship prefix used to identify a commissioned ship of the United States Navy, applying only while the ship is in commission. Before commissioning, or after decommissioning, a vessel is referred to by name with no prefix.

Usage

The prefix, combined with the ship's name and hull classification symbol (for example, USS Enterprise (CVN-65)), uniquely identifies a commissioned warship. Civilian-crewed ships of the Military Sealift Command in active service use the prefix USNS (United States Naval Ship), while Coast Guard cutters use USCGC. In the early Navy, usage was inconsistent, and other styles such as "United States Frigate" were common; the USS prefix was standardised by executive order in 1907.
