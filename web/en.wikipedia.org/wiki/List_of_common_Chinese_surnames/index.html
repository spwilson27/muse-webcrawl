List of common Chinese surnames

Chinese surnames are used by Han Chinese and Sinicized ethnic groups in China, Taiwan, Korea, Vietnam, and among overseas Chinese communities. Although there are thousands of Chinese surnames in historical records, most are rare: the top 100 surnames cover the large majority of the Han Chinese population. The traditional verse text Baijiaxing ("Hundred Family Surnames"), compiled in the early Song dynasty, lists 504 surnames.

Most common surnames in Taiwan

In Taiwan the ranking differs somewhat because of distinct migration history. The most common are Chen (陳), Lin (林), Huang (黃), Zhang (張), Li (李), Wang (王), Wu (吳), Liu (劉), Tsai/Cai (蔡), and Yang (楊). Chen alone accounts for roughly 11% of Taiwan's population.

Compound surnames

Two-character compound surnames are much rarer. Well-known examples include Ouyang (歐陽), Situ (司徒), Sima (司馬), Zhuge (諸葛), Huangfu (皇甫), Dongfang (東方), and Murong (慕容, of Xianbei origin).
