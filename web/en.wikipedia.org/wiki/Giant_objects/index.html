Giant objects

Giant objects are novelty architectural constructions built to oversized scale, often as advertising, tourist attractions, or roadside landmarks. The phenomenon is particularly associated with North America and Australia, where large-scale replicas of animals, tools, food items, and everyday artefacts have been erected since the early 20th century.

History

Early examples include giant bottles, shoes, and dinosaurs built along American highways during the 1920s and 1930s motor-age boom. Such structures, examples of "mimetic architecture", served a commercial purpose: a building shaped like the product sold inside was unmistakable to passing motorists.

Australia's big things

Australia is famous for its "big things", of which over 150 are recorded, including the Big Pineapple in Queensland, the Big Banana at Coffs Harbour, the Big Merino at Goulburn, and the Big Lobster in South Australia. Community campaigns have sometimes formed around preserving or relocating these icons.
