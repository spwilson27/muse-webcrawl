San Luis Potosí, often called simply SLP, is the capital and largest city of the Mexican state of the same name. Founded in 1592, it was named after King Louis IX of France and the Bolivian silver city of Potosí, in expectation of comparable mineral wealth.

History

Gold and silver deposits discovered at Cerro de San Pedro in 1592 drew settlers to the site. The city grew rich on mining and became an important colonial centre; in 1863, during the French intervention, it briefly served as the seat of Benito Juárez's republican government.

Economy and culture

Today San Luis Potosí is a major industrial hub, especially for automotive manufacturing. Its historic centre, with the baroque cathedral and the Teatro de la Paz, forms part of the Camino Real de Tierra Adentro UNESCO World Heritage Site.
