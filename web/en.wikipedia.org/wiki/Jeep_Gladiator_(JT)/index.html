The Jeep Gladiator (JT) is a mid-size pickup truck manufactured by Jeep since 2019. Based on the fourth-generation Wrangler (JL), it revived the Gladiator nameplate last used on Jeep pickups in the 1960s–70s and marked Jeep's return to the pickup segment after the 1992 discontinuation of the Comanche.

Design

The Gladiator shares the Wrangler JL's front clip, interior, removable doors and roof, and solid-axle four-wheel-drive running gear, stretched onto a longer frame with a 5-foot steel cargo bed. Launch engines included a 3.6 L Pentastar V6 and a 3.0 L EcoDiesel V6; a plug-in hybrid 4xe version followed. Trims range from Sport to the off-road-focused Rubicon and Mojave, the latter tuned for high-speed desert running.

Reception

The Gladiator won MotorTrend Truck of the Year for 2020 and has sold strongly in North America as the only convertible pickup on the market.
