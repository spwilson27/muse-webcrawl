Categoría Primera A

Categoría Primera A, commonly known as Liga BetPlay Dimayor for sponsorship reasons, is the top professional football league in Colombia. Founded in 1948 and organised by DIMAYOR, it is contested by 20 clubs and operates on a system of promotion and relegation with Categoría Primera B.

Format

The season is split into two tournaments, Apertura and Finalización, each with its own champion. Clubs play a round-robin phase followed by play-offs or semi-final groups, and relegation is calculated from a multi-year points average.

History

The league began in 1948, and its early 1950s "El Dorado" era attracted star players from across South America and Europe, including Alfredo Di Stéfano at Millonarios. In the modern era Atlético Nacional, Millonarios and América de Cali have been the most successful clubs, and Colombian sides have won the Copa Libertadores, with Nacional triumphing in 1989 and 2016 and Once Caldas in 2004.
