Stockholm Central Station

Location: Norrmalm, Stockholm, Sweden
Opened: 1871
Passengers: approx. 200,000 daily (with metro/commuter)

Stockholm Central Station (Swedish: Stockholms centralstation) is the largest railway station in Sweden, located in the Norrmalm district of Stockholm beside the Cityterminalen bus station.

History

The station was designed by Adolf W. Edelsvärd and opened in 1871. The central hall, added in the 1920s, features murals depicting Swedish landscapes. It serves long-distance SJ trains, regional services, the Arlanda Express airport train, and connects to Stockholm Metro and the Stockholm commuter rail via Stockholm City station (opened 2017).

Services

Trains run to Gothenburg, Malmö, Uppsala, Oslo, Copenhagen, and northern Sweden, including overnight sleeper services.
