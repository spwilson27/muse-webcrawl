Ducati

Ducati Motor Holding S.p.A.
Industry: Motorcycle manufacturing
Founded: 1926, Bologna, Italy
Headquarters: Borgo Panigale, Bologna
Parent: Lamborghini / Audi / Volkswagen Group

Ducati Motor Holding S.p.A. is an Italian motorcycle manufacturer headquartered in Bologna, Italy. Founded in 1926 by Antonio Cavalieri Ducati and his three sons, the company originally produced radio components before turning to motorcycles after the Second World War. Ducati is renowned for its high-performance motorcycles characterised by large-capacity four-stroke, 90° V-twin engines (marketed as L-twins) with desmodromic valve actuation, and for its distinctive trellis frames and dry clutches.

History

The company began as Società Scientifica Radio Brevetti Ducati, making vacuum tubes and radio parts. Its Bologna factory was largely destroyed by Allied bombing in 1944. After the war Ducati produced the Cucciolo, a small auxiliary engine for bicycles, and in 1952 its first complete motorcycle. Through the 1950s and 1960s Ducati built a reputation with lightweight single-cylinder machines and racing success at events such as the Motogiro d'Italia.

In the 1970s the company introduced its large V-twin engines, beginning with the 750 GT, and achieved fame with the 750 Super Sport "Green Frame" and Mike Hailwood's victory at the 1978 Isle of Man TT. Financial difficulties led to a takeover by the Cagiva Group in 1985, then by Texas Pacific Group in 1996, Investindustrial in 2006, and finally acquisition by Audi through its Lamborghini subsidiary in 2012 for approximately €860 million.

Racing

Ducati Corse, the company's racing division, has been highly successful in the Superbike World Championship, winning numerous riders' and manufacturers' titles, and in MotoGP, where it won the riders' championship with Casey Stoner in 2007 and back-to-back titles with Francesco Bagnaia in 2022 and 2023.

Models

Current and notable model families include the Panigale superbikes, the Monster naked bikes, the Multistrada adventure tourers, the Scrambler sub-brand, the Diavel power cruiser, the Hypermotard and the Streetfighter.
