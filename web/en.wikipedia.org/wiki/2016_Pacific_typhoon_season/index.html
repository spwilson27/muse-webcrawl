2016 Pacific typhoon season

Last system dissipated: 28 December 2016
Strongest storm: Meranti — 890 hPa
Total storms: 26
Typhoons: 13
Total fatalities: 900+
Total damage: ~$14.5 billion

The 2016 Pacific typhoon season was a below-average but damaging season of tropical cyclone formation in the western North Pacific. It had the second-latest start on record, with the first named storm, Nepartak, forming in early July.

Season summary

Twenty-six named storms formed, thirteen reaching typhoon strength. The season was characterised by a slow start — influenced by the decaying 2015–16 El Niño — followed by a very active late summer and autumn.

Notable storms

Typhoon Nepartak struck Taiwan and Fujian in July. Typhoon Lionrock caused severe flooding in North Korea and Northeast China in late August. Typhoon Meranti, the strongest storm of the year worldwide, made landfall near Xiamen, China, in September after passing south of Taiwan, causing extensive destruction. Typhoon Haima struck the Philippines and China in October, and Typhoon Nock-ten (Nina) hit the Philippines around Christmas Day.

Retirement

The names Meranti, Sarika and Haima were retired following the season because of the damage they caused.
