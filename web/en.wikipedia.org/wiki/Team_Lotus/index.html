Team Lotus

Team Lotus, founded by Colin Chapman, is one of the most successful teams in Formula One history.

Team Lotus was the motorsport sister company of English sports car manufacturer Lotus Cars. The team ran cars in many motorsport categories, including Formula One, Formula Two, Formula Ford, and sports car racing. Between 1958 and 1994 the team competed in Formula One, winning seven Constructors' World Championships and six Drivers' Championships.

History

Team Lotus was founded by Colin Chapman in the early 1950s and entered Formula One in 1958. Under Chapman's direction the team pioneered innovations that reshaped the sport: the monocoque chassis (Lotus 25), the engine as a stressed structural member (Lotus 49), commercial sponsorship liveries, wings and aerodynamic downforce, and ground-effect aerodynamics (Lotus 78 and 79).

World championships were won by drivers Jim Clark (1963, 1965), Graham Hill (1968), Jochen Rindt (1970, posthumously), Emerson Fittipaldi (1972), and Mario Andretti (1978). Ayrton Senna won six Grands Prix for the team in the mid-1980s. Following financial difficulties, the team withdrew from Formula One after the 1994 season.

Legacy

Lotus's tally of 79 Grand Prix victories places it among the most successful constructors in the sport's history. The name returned to Formula One in various forms in the 2010s, though those later entries were not continuations of the original team.
