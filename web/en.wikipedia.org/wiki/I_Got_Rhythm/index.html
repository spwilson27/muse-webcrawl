I Got Rhythm

"I Got Rhythm"
Music: George Gershwin
Lyrics: Ira Gershwin
Published: 1930
From: Girl Crazy

"I Got Rhythm" is a piece composed by George Gershwin with lyrics by Ira Gershwin, written for the 1930 musical Girl Crazy, where it was introduced by Ethel Merman in her Broadway debut. It became one of the most famous standards of the Great American Songbook.

Rhythm changes

The song's chord progression — a 32-bar AABA form — became so ubiquitous in jazz that it acquired its own name, "rhythm changes". Hundreds of jazz compositions ("contrafacts") were built on it, including Charlie Parker's "Anthropology" and "Moose the Mooche" and the theme to The Flintstones. Gershwin himself reused the material in his orchestral Variations on "I Got Rhythm" (1934).

Notable recordings

The song has been recorded by countless artists, from Judy Garland and Ella Fitzgerald to Sarah Vaughan, and it was performed in the 1951 film An American in Paris by Gene Kelly.
