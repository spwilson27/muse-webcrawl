Aerovías de México, S.A. de C.V., operating as Aeroméxico, is the flag carrier airline of Mexico, headquartered in Mexico City. It operates scheduled services to Mexico, the United States, Canada, Central America, the Caribbean, Europe, and Asia from its hub at Mexico City International Airport. It is a founding member of the SkyTeam airline alliance and a partner of Delta Air Lines in a joint venture across the US–Mexico border.

History

Founded in 1934, Aeroméxico began operations as a domestic carrier and expanded internationally in the following decades. The airline was nationalized in 1972 and privatized again in the 1990s. It joined SkyTeam in 2000 as a founding member alongside Delta, Air France, and Korean Air.

In 2020 the airline filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in the United States after the COVID-19 pandemic devastated passenger demand; it emerged from restructuring in 2022 with a simplified fleet and a reorganized balance sheet. In 2024 it began trading on the New York Stock Exchange and announced plans for a new corporate campus at Mexico City's Felipe Ángeles International Airport area.

Fleet

Aeroméxico operates an all-Boeing fleet comprising 737 MAX aircraft for short- and medium-haul routes and 787 Dreamliners for long-haul services. The regional brand Aeroméxico Connect operates Embraer E190s.

Livery

The airline's livery features a white fuselage with a stylized dark blue and red design along the tail; the current design depicts a curved, flowing ribbon evoking the Mexican flag's colors and a pre-Hispanic eagle-warrior motif.
