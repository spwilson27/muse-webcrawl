Suriname

Rainforest covers most of Suriname's territory

Capital: Paramaribo
Official language: Dutch
Government: Unitary parliamentary republic
Area: 163,820 km²
Population: ~620,000 (2023 estimate)
Currency: Surinamese dollar (SRD)

Suriname, officially the Republic of Suriname, is a country in northeastern South America. It borders the Atlantic Ocean to the north, French Guiana to the east, Guyana to the west, and Brazil to the south. It is the smallest sovereign state in South America by both area and population. Its capital and largest city is Paramaribo.

History

The area was inhabited by Indigenous peoples, including the Arawak and Carib, long before European contact. The English established a colony in 1650, but in 1667, under the Treaty of Breda, the Dutch acquired Suriname in exchange for New Netherland (New York). The colony's plantation economy depended on enslaved Africans, whose descendants, along with Javanese, Hindustani, and Chinese labourers brought later, created Suriname's remarkably diverse society. Suriname gained independence from the Netherlands on 25 November 1975. A military coup in 1980 led by Dési Bouterse ushered in a period of authoritarian rule and the Interior War (1986–1992); democracy was restored in the 1990s.

Geography

Nearly 93% of Suriname is covered by tropical rainforest — the highest proportion of forest cover of any country in the world. A narrow coastal plain holds most of the population, while the interior rises to the Guiana Highlands, with Julianatop (1,230 m) the highest point. The Central Suriname Nature Reserve is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Demographics and culture

Suriname is ethnically diverse: Hindustanis (East Indians) form the largest group, followed by Maroons, Creoles, Javanese, Indigenous peoples, and Chinese. Dutch is the official language, while Sranan Tongo, an English-based creole, serves as a lingua franca. Hinduism, Christianity, and Islam are all widely practised, and Paramaribo's synagogue and mosque stand side by side as a symbol of tolerance.

Economy

Bauxite and alumina long dominated exports, supplemented by gold and oil. Significant offshore oil discoveries by TotalEnergies and APA Corporation in Block 58 have raised expectations of an oil boom in the later 2020s.
