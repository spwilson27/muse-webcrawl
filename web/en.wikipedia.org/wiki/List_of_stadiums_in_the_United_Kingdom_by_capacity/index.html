List of stadiums in the United Kingdom by capacity

This is a list of stadiums in the United Kingdom by capacity — the maximum number of spectators each stadium can accommodate. The list is dominated by association football grounds, together with the national stadiums used for rugby union and rugby league.

Notes

Capacities for league clubs can change with redevelopment and with safety rules requiring all-seater configurations in the top divisions. Wembley, opened in 2007 on the site of the original 1923 stadium, is the largest stadium in the UK and the second-largest in Europe.
