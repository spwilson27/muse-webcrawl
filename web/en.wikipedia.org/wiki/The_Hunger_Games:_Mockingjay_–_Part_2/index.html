The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 2 is a 2015 American dystopian science fiction film directed by Francis Lawrence. It concludes The Hunger Games film series and adapts the second half of Suzanne Collins' novel Mockingjay.

Plot

Katniss Everdeen joins a special unit infiltrating the booby-trapped Capitol to assassinate President Snow, while Peeta struggles with the Capitol's "hijacking" of his mind. In the final assault, Katniss's sister Prim is killed in a bombing Katniss suspects was ordered by President Coin. At Snow's scheduled execution, Katniss instead kills Coin, Snow dies in the ensuing chaos, and Katniss returns to District 12, eventually building a life with Peeta.

Reception

The film grossed over $658 million worldwide and received generally favourable reviews for its performances and its faithful, sombre conclusion to the series.
