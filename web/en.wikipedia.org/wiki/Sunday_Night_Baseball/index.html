Sunday Night Baseball

Sunday Night Baseball is an American television broadcast of Major League Baseball games that aired weekly on ESPN on Sunday nights from 1990 to 2025. It served as ESPN's flagship MLB telecast, featuring a single exclusive national game each week during the regular season.

Long-running voicesJon Miller and Joe Morgan (1990–2010)

History

The franchise premiered on 15 April 1990 with a game between the New York Mets and Montreal Expos. For two decades its signature announcing team was Jon Miller on play-by-play and Joe Morgan as analyst, a pairing that became synonymous with the broadcast. Later booths included Dan Shulman and, from 2022, Karl Ravech with David Cone and Eduardo Pérez.

End of the ESPN era

In February 2025, ESPN and MLB mutually opted out of the final years of their contract, ending Sunday Night Baseball on ESPN after the 2025 season; NBC subsequently acquired the Sunday-night package beginning in 2026. ESPN also carried the Spanish-language Béisbol de Domingo and radio coverage on ESPN Radio.
