Calendar Girls

Calendar Girls is a 2003 British comedy-drama film directed by Nigel Cole, starring Helen Mirren, Julie Walters, Linda Bassett, Annette Crosbie, Ciaran Hinds, Celia Imrie and Penelope Wilton. It is loosely based on the true story of a group of middle-aged women in Yorkshire who produced a nude calendar to raise money for leukaemia research.

Plot

Chris Harper and Annie Clarke are friends in the Women's Institute in Knapely, Yorkshire. When Annie's husband John dies of leukaemia, Chris proposes that the branch create a calendar featuring the members posing nude — tastefully obscured by flowers, cakes and crafts — to buy a sofa for the hospital relatives' room. Against local resistance and national press attention, the calendar becomes a phenomenon, selling hundreds of thousands of copies.

True story

The real calendar was made in 1999 by the Rylestone Women's Institute. It raised far more than expected, eventually millions of pounds for Blood Cancer UK (then the Leukaemia Research Fund). The women became known as the Calendar Girls.

Adaptations

Tim Firth adapted the film into a stage play in 2008 and later a musical, Calendar Girls: The Musical (2015), with music by Gary Barlow.

Reception

The film was a commercial success, grossing over $90 million worldwide, and received generally positive reviews for its warmth, humour and performances.
