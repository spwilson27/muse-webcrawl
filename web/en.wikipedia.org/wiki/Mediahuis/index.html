Mediahuis

Mediahuis is a Belgian media company active in newspaper, magazine and digital publishing in Belgium, the Netherlands, Ireland, Luxembourg and Germany. It is one of the largest media groups in the Benelux countries and Ireland, publishing news brands including De Standaard, Het Nieuwsblad, De Telegraaf, NRC and the Irish Independent.

History

Mediahuis was formed in 2013 as a joint venture between the Belgian publishers Corelio and Concentra, combining their newspaper activities, including the Flemish titles De Standaard, Het Nieuwsblad, Gazet van Antwerpen and Het Belang van Limburg. The joint venture took full effect at the beginning of 2014.

The group expanded into the Netherlands in 2015 with the acquisition of NRC Media, publisher of NRC Handelsblad and nrc.next. In 2017 Mediahuis acquired the Dutch Telegraaf Media Groep (TMG), publisher of De Telegraaf, the country's largest newspaper. The same period saw the group take control of the Dutch regional publisher Holland Media Combinatie.

In 2019 Mediahuis acquired Independent News & Media (INM), Ireland's largest newspaper group, publisher of the Irish Independent and Sunday Independent, for approximately €145.6 million. In 2020 the group bought the Dutch publisher NDC Mediagroep, and in 2022 it acquired the German newspaper Aachener Zeitung publisher Medienhaus Aachen, broadening its footprint into Germany.

Brands

Belgian brands include De Standaard, Het Nieuwsblad, Gazet van Antwerpen and Het Belang van Limburg, as well as classifieds and job platforms such as Jobat. In the Netherlands the group publishes De Telegraaf, NRC, and regional titles such as De Limburger and Dagblad van het Noorden. Irish brands include the Irish Independent, Sunday Independent, Sunday World and the Belfast Telegraph.
