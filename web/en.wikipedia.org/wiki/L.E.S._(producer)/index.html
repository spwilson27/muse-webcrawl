L.E.S. (producer)

Leshan David Lewis, professionally known as L.E.S., is an American hip hop record producer from Queens, New York. He is best known for his work with rapper Nas, having produced the opening track "N.Y. State of Mind" on Nas's landmark 1994 debut album Illmatic. His stage name derives from his first name shortened to initials.

Career

L.E.S. grew up in Queensbridge, the same housing project that produced Nas, and began producing in the early 1990s. Alongside "N.Y. State of Mind," built around a moody piano loop, he contributed production throughout Nas's discography, including tracks on It Was Written, I Am..., Nastradamus, Stillmatic, and later albums. His production style is rooted in East Coast boom bap, layering jazz and soul samples over hard drums.

Other work

Beyond Nas, L.E.S. has produced for artists such as Fat Joe, Big Pun, and AZ, and worked with Nas's group The Firm. He remains closely identified with the Queensbridge sound and the golden-age era of New York hip hop.

Legacy

"N.Y. State of Mind" is frequently cited among the greatest hip hop songs ever recorded, securing L.E.S. a lasting reputation despite a comparatively low public profile.
