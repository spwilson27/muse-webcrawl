Hectare

The hectare (symbol: ha) is a non-SI metric unit of area equal to a square with 100-metre sides (1 hm²), or 10,000 square metres, and is primarily used in the measurement of land. There are 100 hectares in one square kilometre. An acre is about 0.405 hectares, and one hectare contains about 2.47 acres.

Definition

The hectare was introduced in 1795 as part of the metric system, based on the are, a unit of 100 m²; the prefix "hecto-" denotes one hundred ares. Although the hectare is not an SI unit, it is the only named unit of area that is accepted for use with the SI. Its name is contracted from the Greek hekaton (one hundred) and are.

Usage

The hectare is the standard unit for measuring agricultural land, forests, farms and urban planning areas in most of the world. Sports venues are often compared with it: an international football pitch is roughly 0.7 hectares, and large wildfires are conventionally reported in hectares burned. The unit centiare (1 m²) and deciare (10 m²) belong to the same family but are rarely used.

Conversions

  • 1 hectare = 10,000 m²
  • 1 hectare = 100 ares = 0.01 km²
  • 1 hectare ≈ 2.471 acres
  • 1 hectare ≈ 107,639 square feet
