Ginger Rogers (born Virginia Katherine McMath; 1911–1995) was an American actress, dancer, and singer who achieved fame in film, radio, and television. She is best known for her partnership with Fred Astaire in ten Hollywood musicals, including Top Hat (1935) and Swing Time (1936).

Career

Rogers won the Academy Award for Best Actress for Kitty Foyle (1940) and starred in popular comedies such as Monkey Business with the Marx Brothers and The Major and the Minor. A famous remark holds that she did everything Astaire did, "backwards and in high heels".

Legacy

The American Film Institute ranked her 14th on its list of the greatest female stars of classic American cinema. She received a Kennedy Center Honors award in 1992.
