Ray Liotta

Raymond Allen Liotta (December 18, 1954 – May 26, 2022) was an American actor, best known for portraying Henry Hill in Martin Scorsese's crime film Goodfellas (1990). His intense, edgy screen persona made him a defining presence in crime and thriller films across four decades.

BornDecember 18, 1954, Newark, New Jersey
DiedMay 26, 2022, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic
OccupationActor

Career

Liotta broke through with a Golden Globe-nominated turn as the menacing ex-convict Ray Sinclair in Something Wild (1986) and played Shoeless Joe Jackson in Field of Dreams (1989) before Goodfellas made him a star. Later notable films include Cop Land (1997), Hannibal (2001), Narc (2002), Identity (2003), Killing Them Softly (2012), Marriage Story (2019), and The Many Saints of Newark (2021). He also voiced Tommy Vercetti, the protagonist of the video game Grand Theft Auto: Vice City (2002).

Death

Liotta died in his sleep in Santo Domingo on May 26, 2022, aged 67, while filming the thriller Dangerous Waters. His final released films included Cocaine Bear (2023).
