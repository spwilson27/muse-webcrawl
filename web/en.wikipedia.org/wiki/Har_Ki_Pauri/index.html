Har Ki Pauri

Har Ki Pauri
Location: Haridwar, Uttarakhand, India
River: Ganges
Known for: Ganga Aarti, Kumbh Mela

Har Ki Pauri is a famous ghat on the banks of the river Ganges in Haridwar, Uttarakhand, India. The name means "steps of Lord Shiva" (Har). It is believed to be the precise spot where the Ganges leaves the mountains and enters the plains.

Significance

According to tradition, the spot bears the footprint of Vishnu. Pilgrims believe bathing here washes away sins. The ghat is a principal site of the Kumbh Mela and Ardh Kumbh festivals.

Ganga Aarti

Each evening at sunset, priests perform the Ganga Aarti ceremony, offering fire to the river while thousands of small floating lamps (diyas) are set adrift by devotees. The ghat was built by King Vikramaditya in the 1st century BCE in memory of his brother Bharthari.
