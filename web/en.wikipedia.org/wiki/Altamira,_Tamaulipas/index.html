Altamira is a city and municipality in the Mexican state of Tamaulipas, on the Gulf of Mexico just north of Tampico. It forms part of the Tampico metropolitan area and hosts one of Mexico's most important industrial ports.

History

The city was founded in 1749 by José de Escandón as part of the colonization of Nuevo Santander. For much of its history it was a modest agricultural and fishing town.

Port and industry

From the 1980s the deep-water Port of Altamira and its surrounding petrochemical and industrial park transformed the municipality into a major centre for chemicals, plastics, energy, and container shipping, with extensive port infrastructure on the Gulf coast.

Geography

The municipality includes lagoons, wetlands, and the beach resort area of Playa Miramar's northern reaches near the Pánuco River mouth, and the Miramar lagoon system.
