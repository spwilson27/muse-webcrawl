Gävle

Gävle is a city in Sweden, the seat of Gävle Municipality and the capital of Gävleborg County. It had a population of about 77,000 in the city proper and is Sweden's oldest city in the historical region of Norrland, having received its city charter in 1446 from Christopher of Bavaria. Gävle lies on the Baltic Sea coast at the mouth of the Gavleån river, about 170 km north of Stockholm.

The city is famous for the Gävle goat, a giant straw goat erected each December since 1966, which has frequently been burned down in arson attacks despite security measures. Historically Gävle was a major port for iron and timber exports, and it is the birthplace of the Gevalia coffee brand.
