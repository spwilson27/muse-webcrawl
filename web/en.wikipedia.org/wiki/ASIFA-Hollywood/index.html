ASIFA-Hollywood

ASIFA-Hollywood, the International Animated Film Society, is a nonprofit organisation based in Los Angeles, California, dedicated to promoting the art of animation. It is the largest chapter of ASIFA (Association Internationale du Film d'Animation), the international association founded in France in 1960. ASIFA-Hollywood is best known for presenting the annual Annie Awards, the animation industry's principal honours.

History

ASIFA-Hollywood was founded in 1972 by voice actress June Foray and other animation professionals, including Bill Hanna and Bill Scott, as the Hollywood chapter of the international association. Its first project was the Animation Film Preservation Program, and in 1972 it established the Annie Awards (named for "animation", shortened to "Annie") to honour excellence in the field; early ceremonies recognised lifetime achievement for figures such as Walt Disney and Max Fleischer before competitive categories were added.

Activities

Besides the Annie Awards, the organisation maintains an animation archive, hosts screenings, panels and festivals, supports animation education, and works on the restoration and preservation of animated films. Membership is open to professionals, students and fans of animation.
