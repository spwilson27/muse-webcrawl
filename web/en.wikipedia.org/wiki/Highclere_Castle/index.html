Highclere Castle is a Grade I listed country house near Newbury, Hampshire, England, the seat of the Earl of Carnarvon. It stands on an estate of about 5,000 acres whose park was designed by Capability Brown. The present house was rebuilt in Jacobethan style by Sir Charles Barry (architect of the Houses of Parliament) for the 3rd Earl of Carnarvon between 1839 and 1842, around the core of an earlier Georgian house, and its interiors were completed to designs associated with Thomas Allom.

History

The site was a residence of the Bishops of Winchester in the Middle Ages before passing to the Herbert family; the 8th Earl's ancestors acquired it in the late 17th century. The 5th Earl of Carnarvon financed Howard Carter's excavation of Tutankhamun's tomb in 1922, and the castle houses an Egyptian exhibition drawn from that connection. During the First World War the house served as a hospital run by the 5th Countess, Almina.

Downton Abbey

Highclere achieved worldwide fame as the principal filming location of the television series and films Downton Abbey (2010–2015; films 2019 and 2022), created by Julian Fellowes, a friend of the family. Visitor numbers rose dramatically, funding extensive repairs to the house.
