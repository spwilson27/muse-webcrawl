Jaya Bachchan (née Bhaduri; born 9 April 1948) is an Indian actress and politician. Known for her work in Hindi cinema, she is regarded as one of the finest actresses of her generation, recognised for her naturalistic performances in films such as Guddi (1971), Abhimaan (1973), and Sholay (1975). Since 2004 she has served as a Member of Parliament in the Rajya Sabha from Uttar Pradesh, representing the Samajwadi Party.

Early life and debut

Born in Jabalpur into a Bengali family, Jaya Bhaduri made her screen debut as a teenager in Satyajit Ray's Mahanagar (1963). She trained at the Film and Television Institute of India in Pune before her Hindi film debut in Hrishikesh Mukherjee's Guddi (1971), playing a schoolgirl obsessed with cinema.

Career

Through the 1970s she starred in acclaimed films including Uphaar, Koshish, Abhimaan, Mili, and Sholay, winning multiple Filmfare Awards. She married co-star Amitabh Bachchan in 1973. After largely stepping back from acting to raise her children, she returned in films such as Fiza (2000), Kabhi Khushi Kabhie Gham... (2001), and Kal Ho Naa Ho (2003), and more recently Rocky Aur Rani Kii Prem Kahaani (2023).

Politics

Bachchan was first elected to the Rajya Sabha in 2004 and has been re-elected several times. She is known for her outspoken interventions in parliamentary debates on issues including film industry concerns and women's safety.
