Dries Buytaert (born 19 November 1978) is a Belgian computer programmer and entrepreneur, best known as the founder and project lead of Drupal, the open-source content management system used by millions of websites. He is the co-founder of Acquia, a company providing products and services for Drupal.

Career

Buytaert began Drupal in 2000 as a small message board for his student dormitory in Antwerp; the software was released as open source in 2001 and grew into one of the world's most widely used web platforms. He has remained Drupal's project lead and is recognised as its benevolent dictator for life. In 2007 he co-founded Acquia, serving as its chief technology officer, and he has also been involved with the search company Mollom and other ventures.

Recognition

Buytaert has been named among the top young innovators and entrepreneurs by various publications, and he speaks frequently on open source, digital experience platforms and the open web.
