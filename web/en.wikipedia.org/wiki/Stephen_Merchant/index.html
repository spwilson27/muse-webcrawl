Stephen Merchant

Stephen James Merchant (born 24 November 1974) is an English comedian, actor, director and writer. He was the co-star and co-writer of the sitcom The Office (2001–2003) alongside Ricky Gervais, and co-creator of Extras, An Idiot Abroad and The Outlaws.

Career

Merchant met Ricky Gervais at radio station Xfm, where their show — later a record-breaking podcast with Karl Pilkington — began. The Office, co-written and co-directed with Gervais, won Golden Globes and BAFTAs. He appeared on screen in Extras as agent Darren Lamb, and in films including Hot Fuzz, Logan (as Caliban, 2017) and Jojo Rabbit.

Solo work

He created the HBO series Hello Ladies (2013), voiced Wheatley in the video game Portal 2 (2011), co-wrote and directed Fighting with My Family (2019), and created the BBC/Amazon crime comedy The Outlaws (2021–), set in Bristol.
