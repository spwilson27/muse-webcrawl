Molson Canadian is a 5.0% abv lager beer sold in Canada and the United States by the Molson Coors Beverage Company (formerly the Molson Brewery, founded in Montreal in 1786 by John Molson). Introduced in 1959, it is one of Canada's best-selling beers and the flagship brand of the Molson portfolio.

Molson Canadian, with its red maple leaf branding.

History and marketing

Molson Canadian was launched in 1959, during a period when lager was displacing ale as Canada's preferred style of beer. The brand is strongly marketed around Canadian identity: its packaging features a red maple leaf and the slogan association with the nation's landscape and hockey culture. Molson has long been a major sponsor of the National Hockey League and of Hockey Night in Canada.

The brand is especially remembered for its "I Am Canadian" advertising campaign, launched in 2000, in which an ordinary Canadian, "Joe", delivers a humorous rant distinguishing Canada from the United States. The ad won international advertising awards, and the speech became a cultural touchstone frequently parodied and reprised.

Variants

  • Molson Canadian Light — a lower-alcohol, lower-calorie version.
  • Molson Canadian 67 — a 3.0% abv, 67-calorie-per-bottle variant introduced in 2009.
  • Molson Canadian Cold Shots — a higher-alcohol mini-bottle format.

Molson merged with the Adolph Coors Company in 2005 to form Molson Coors, and in 2016 the combined company acquired full ownership of the MillerCoors joint venture in the United States. Molson Canadian continues to be brewed in Canada at Molson Coors breweries including those in Montreal, Toronto (Etobicoke), Vancouver and Moncton.
