A contralto (Italian pronunciation: [konˈtralto]) is a type of classical female singing voice whose vocal range is the lowest female voice type. The contralto's tessitura lies lower than that of the mezzo-soprano and higher than that of the countertenor's typical range overlap.

A contralto vocalist in performance.

Range

The contralto voice type is generally divided into the coloratura, lyric, and dramatic contralto. True contraltos are comparatively rare.

Contralto in opera

Contralto roles include Ulrica in Verdi's Un ballo in maschera, Erda in Wagner's Ring, and Azucena is sometimes sung by contraltos though written for mezzo-soprano. Handel wrote many roles for contralto voices.

Notable contraltos
