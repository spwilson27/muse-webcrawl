WWV (radio station)

WWV is a shortwave ("high frequency") radio station, located near Fort Collins, Colorado. It has broadcast a continuous time signal since 1945, and implements an official United States Government time standard, with transmissions in the international medium wave and shortwave bands. WWV is operated by the U.S. Department of Commerce's National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST).

Broadcast content

WWV broadcasts time announcements in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) once per minute, in English, using a male voice. The announcements are preceded by 1000 Hz and 1200 Hz alternating tones, and each second is marked by a tick sound. Seconds 29 and 59 are omitted. The station also transmits the official warnings of the NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center regarding geomagnetic conditions.

History

The call sign WWV was assigned to the National Bureau of Standards in 1923. The station moved to its current site north of Fort Collins in 1966. In 2018 the station faced possible closure under a proposed NIST budget, but it received funding to continue operations. A sister station, WWVH, operates from Kauai, Hawaii.
