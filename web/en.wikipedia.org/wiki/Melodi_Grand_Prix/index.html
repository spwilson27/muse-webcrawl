Melodi Grand Prix, commonly known as MGP, is an annual music competition organised by the Norwegian public broadcaster NRK. The winning song represents Norway at the Eurovision Song Contest. The competition has been held almost every year since 1960 and is one of the longest-running national selections for Eurovision.

History

Norway debuted at the Eurovision Song Contest in 1960, and Melodi Grand Prix was created to select its entry. The format has varied from single-evening shows to multi-week festivals with semi-finals and heats around the country, usually culminating in a final in Oslo, often held at Oslo Spektrum.

Winners and Eurovision

Melodi Grand Prix winners who went on to win Eurovision for Norway include Bobbysocks! with "La det swinge" (1985), Secret Garden with "Nocturne" (1995), and Alexander Rybak with "Fairytale" (2009), the latter winning with a record points total at the time. The contest has also launched many prominent Norwegian artists and songwriters.

Format

In recent editions, the competition has combined auto-qualified finalists with songs advancing from semi-finals, and the result is decided by public voting together with international juries in the final. The show is a major television event in Norway, drawing large viewing shares.
