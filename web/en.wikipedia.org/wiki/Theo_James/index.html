Theo James

Theodore Peter James Kinnaird Taptiklis (born 16 December 1984), known professionally as Theo James, is an English actor. He gained international recognition for playing Tobias "Four" Eaton in The Divergent Series film adaptations (2014–2016). His other notable roles include Kemal Pamuk in Downton Abbey (2010), David in the Underworld films, Sidney Parker in Sanditon (2019), Cameron Sullivan in the HBO series The Time Traveler's Wife (2022), and Hal in the second season of The White Lotus (2022), as well as the lead role in the Netflix series The Gentlemen (2024).

Early life and education

James was born in High Wycombe, Buckinghamshire, the son of Jane (née Martin), who worked for the NHS, and Philip Taptiklis, a business consultant. His paternal grandfather was Greek, and his ancestry also includes English and Scottish roots. He was raised in Askett and attended Aylesford School and then the University of Nottingham, where he earned a degree in philosophy, before training at the Bristol Old Vic Theatre School.

Career

James made his television debut in the 2010 drama A Passionate Woman, and in the same year appeared in one memorable episode of Downton Abbey as Kemal Pamuk, the Turkish diplomat whose sudden death in Lady Mary's bed caused a scandal. He starred as the lead in the CBS police drama Golden Boy (2013) and joined the Underworld franchise as David in Underworld: Awakening (2012) and Underworld: Blood Wars (2016).

His breakthrough came as Four in Divergent (2014), opposite Shailene Woodley, a role he reprised in Insurgent (2015) and Allegiant (2016). Other film credits include The Benefactor (2015), War on Everyone (2016), How It Ends (2018) and Archive (2020). He voiced Hector in the animated Castlevania series, played the lead in ITV's Sanditon, and won acclaim — including Emmy and Golden Globe nominations for the ensemble of The White Lotus — for his role as the predatory Cameron in the show's Sicily-set second season. In 2024 he headlined Guy Ritchie's Netflix series The Gentlemen as Eddie Horniman.

Personal life

James married Irish actress Ruth Kearney in 2018; the couple have two children. He co-founded the production company Untold Stories and has served as a goodwill ambassador supporting refugee causes.
