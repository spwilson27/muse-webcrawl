The Annie Awards are accolades presented annually by the Los Angeles branch of the International Animated Film Association, ASIFA-Hollywood, recognising excellence in the field of animation. They are considered the animation industry's highest honour, sometimes described as "the Annies".

History

The awards were created in 1972 by voice actress June Foray to honour lifetime and career achievements in animation; the first trophy went to Max and Dave Fleischer. Individual achievement categories were added in the 1990s, including Best Animated Feature, and the ceremony grew into a major annual event. The Annie for Best Animated Feature has often predicted the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature, a category presented separately at the Annie Awards ceremonies.

Categories

Categories span production (feature, television, short subject, student film), individual achievement (directing, writing, storyboarding, character animation and design, voice acting, music, production design and editorial), and juried lifetime honours such as the Winsor McCay Award and the June Foray Award.
