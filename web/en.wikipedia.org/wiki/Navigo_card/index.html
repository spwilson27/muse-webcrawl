Navigo card

The Navigo card (carte Navigo) is a contactless smart card used for public transport fares in Paris and the Île-de-France region, managed by Île-de-France Mobilités. Introduced from 2001 (as the passe Navigo) for season ticket holders, it progressively replaced paper tickets.

Products

The card stores weekly and monthly Navigo passes giving unlimited travel on Métro, RER, buses, trams and most trains in the region. Variants include Navigo Easy for visitors (replacing paper carnets) and Liberté+ pay-as-you-go. The Navigo pass was famously priced at a flat monthly rate across all zones from 2015.
