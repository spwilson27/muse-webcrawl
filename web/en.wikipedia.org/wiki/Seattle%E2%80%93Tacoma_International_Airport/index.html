Seattle–Tacoma International Airport


IATA: SEA
Location: SeaTac, Washington, United States
Opened: 1944
Hub for: Alaska Airlines, Delta Air Lines

Seattle–Tacoma International Airport (IATA: SEA), commonly called Sea-Tac Airport, is the primary commercial airport serving the Seattle metropolitan area in the U.S. state of Washington. It is located in the city of SeaTac, roughly midway between Seattle and Tacoma.

History

The airport was built by the Port of Seattle in 1944 to serve the region after Boeing Field was taken over by the military during the Second World War. Successive expansions added parallel runways, satellite terminals connected by an underground transit system, and a third runway in 2008.

Facilities and airlines

Sea-Tac has one main terminal with four concourses and two satellite terminals. Alaska Airlines and Delta Air Lines operate major hubs there, with nonstop service across North America and to Europe and Asia. It is consistently ranked among the busiest airports in the United States.
