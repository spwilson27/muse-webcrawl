Balance board

A balance board is a device used for balance training, recreation, athletic conditioning, and physical therapy. Typically a flat board rests on an unstable base—a fulcrum, roller, sphere, or inflatable cushion—so that a person standing on it must continuously adjust their posture to remain balanced.

Types

The rocker board tilts on a fixed curved or angled base and is the simplest type, common in rehabilitation. The roller board (or rolla bolla) balances on a loose cylinder, allowing side-to-side sliding as well as rocking, and descends from a circus prop. Wobble boards sit on a hemisphere or ball, permitting movement in all directions. Sphere-and-ring boards and inflatable cushion boards offer still greater instability.

Uses

Balance boards are widely used to rehabilitate ankle and knee injuries, strengthen the core and proprioceptive system, and prevent falls among older adults. Surfers, snowboarders, skateboarders, and martial artists use them for off-season training. In the 1950s the Bongo Board became a popular American recreation device. Some workers use balance boards at standing desks to add movement to sedentary work. Skills progress from simply standing, to squats and single-leg stances, to juggling or catching while balancing.

Safety

Because falls are common, beginners are advised to practise near a support, on a non-slip surface, and without footwear that could slip; boards are not recommended on hard surfaces for inexperienced users.
