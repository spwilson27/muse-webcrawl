Jyn Erso

Jyn Erso is a fictional character in the Star Wars franchise, portrayed by Felicity Jones in the 2016 film Rogue One: A Star Wars Story. She is the daughter of Galen Erso, a scientist who was forced by the Galactic Empire to design the superlaser of the Death Star.

Separated from her parents as a child after her mother was killed and her father taken by Director Orson Krennic, Jyn was raised by the rebel extremist Saw Gerrera. As an adult, she is recruited by the Rebel Alliance to make contact with Gerrera and, ultimately, joins the mission to steal the Death Star plans from the Imperial archive on Scarif.

Jyn leads the ragtag "Rogue One" squad in the assault on Scarif, transmitting the plans to the Rebel fleet before she and Cassian Andor are killed when the Death Star destroys the base. Her actions directly set in motion the events of Star Wars: Episode IV – A New Hope.
