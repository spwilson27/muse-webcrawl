Mabey Bridge

Mabey Bridge is a British engineering company specialising in modular steel bridges, based in Lydney, Gloucestershire, England. The company is known for its prefabricated panel bridge systems, which are used by militaries, aid agencies and civil engineers around the world for both permanent and emergency crossings.

History

The business grew out of the Mabey Group, founded by Guy Mabey in 1923. After the Second World War the company became involved in the development of modular bridging derived from the wartime Bailey bridge. Its bridge division developed the Mabey Logistic Support Bridge and a range of panel bridges descended from that design.

Products

Mabey's best-known system is the Compact 200, a modular panel bridge that can be assembled by hand or with light equipment and launched across a gap without cranes working in the span. The system is widely used for military logistics, disaster relief and temporary works. Other products have included the Mabey Universal bridge for heavier permanent applications and floating bridge and ferry equipment.

Applications

Mabey bridges have been deployed after floods, earthquakes and other natural disasters to restore road access, and are stocked by armed forces including those of the United Kingdom and other NATO members. The company exports to well over a hundred countries.
