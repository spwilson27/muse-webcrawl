Chital

The chital (Axis axis), also called the spotted deer or axis deer, is a deer species native to the Indian subcontinent. Its golden-brown coat is marked with white spots throughout life, and males carry long, three-pronged antlers.

Habitat

Chital inhabit wooded regions, grasslands and forest edges across India, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh and Sri Lanka, usually near water. They form herds that can number over a hundred animals.

Ecology

Grazers and browsers, chital are important prey for tigers and leopards and often associate with langur monkeys, which drop fruit from the canopy and give alarm calls. The species has been introduced to Australia, the United States and elsewhere.
