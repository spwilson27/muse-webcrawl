Reki Kawahara

Reki Kawahara (川原 礫, Kawahara Reki, born 17 August 1974) is a Japanese novelist best known as the author of the light novel series Sword Art Online and Accel World, both of which have been adapted into manga, anime television series and films. His works, centred on virtual-reality games and their social consequences, are among the best-selling light novels of all time.

Career

Kawahara began writing Sword Art Online in 2001 for the Dengeki Novel Prize but, finding the manuscript too long for the competition's limits, published it instead as a web novel on his own site under the pen name Fumio Kunori from 2002. His other entry, Accel World, won the Grand Prize at the 15th Dengeki Novel Prize in 2008 and was published by ASCII Media Works' Dengeki Bunko imprint in February 2009; Sword Art Online followed in print from April 2009, with illustrations by abec. Both series received anime adaptations in 2012, and Sword Art Online grew into a major multimedia franchise with films including Ordinal Scale (2017) and the Progressive film series retelling the Aincrad arc.

Works

  • Sword Art Online (2009– ) — players trapped in a full-dive VR MMORPG in which death in the game means death in reality; followed by the retelling Sword Art Online: Progressive (2012– ) and spin-offs.
  • Accel World (2009– ) — a bullied schoolboy joins a secret accelerated-world fighting game in 2046 Tokyo.
  • The Isolator (2014–2020) — teenagers gain superhuman abilities from alien symbionts.
  • Sword Art Online Alternative Gun Gale Online — a spin-off written by Keiichi Sigsawa under Kawahara's supervision.
  • Demons' Crest (2022– ) — a new VR-game series.

The Sword Art Online series has sold more than 30 million copies worldwide, ranking among the best-selling light novel series ever, and its English translations by Yen Press helped popularise the format in North America. In 2021 Kawahara announced a final story arc for the main series, titled "Unital Ring".

Themes and style

Kawahara's fiction repeatedly explores the permeability between virtual and real worlds, the identity of players behind avatars, the ethics of immersive technology, and communities formed inside games. His afterwords and public appearances frequently discuss his own gaming experience, and he is an active user of social media where he interacts with fans and comments on adaptations of his work.
