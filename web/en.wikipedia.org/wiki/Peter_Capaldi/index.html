Peter Capaldi

Born: 14 April 1958, Glasgow, Scotland
Occupation: Actor, director, writer
Known for: The Thick of It, Doctor Who

Peter Dougan Capaldi (born 14 April 1958) is a Scottish actor, director and writer. He played the foul-mouthed spin doctor Malcolm Tucker in the BBC comedy The Thick of It (2005–2012) and its film spin-off In the Loop (2009), and the twelfth incarnation of the Doctor in Doctor Who (2013–2017).

Early life and career

Born in Glasgow, Capaldi studied at the Glasgow School of Art. His breakthrough film role came in Local Hero (1983). He won the Academy Award for Best Live Action Short Film as writer-director of Franz Kafka's It's a Wonderful Life (1993).

Doctor Who

A lifelong fan of the series, Capaldi succeeded Matt Smith as the Doctor, making his full debut in 2014's "Deep Breath" and departing in the 2017 Christmas special "Twice Upon a Time".

Other work

Later roles include the Thinker in The Suicide Squad (2021), voice work, and the series Criminal Record and The Devil's Hour. In 2021 he released the album St. Christopher.
