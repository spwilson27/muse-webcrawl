Jessica Biel

Born: Jessica Claire Biel, March 3, 1982, Ely, Minnesota, U.S.
Occupation: Actress
Years active: 1991–present
Spouse: Justin Timberlake (m. 2012)

Jessica Claire Timberlake (née Biel; born March 3, 1982) is an American actress. She began her career as a vocalist in musical theater and rose to fame as Mary Camden in the long-running family drama series 7th Heaven (1996–2006). She has since starred in films including The Rules of Attraction (2002), The Texas Chainsaw Massacre (2003), Blade: Trinity (2004), The Illusionist (2006), and Total Recall (2012), and earned critical acclaim and Emmy and Golden Globe nominations for producing and starring in the crime drama The Sinner (2017).

Early life

Biel was born in Ely, Minnesota, and raised in Boulder, Colorado. She trained as a singer and appeared in regional musical productions before being discovered by a modeling agency in 1994. She attended Tufts University briefly before leaving to focus on acting.

Career

Cast at fourteen as the eldest daughter in 7th Heaven, Biel became a teen star; she won a Young Artist Award for her film debut opposite Peter Fonda in Ulee's Gold (1997). Seeking more adult roles, she headlined the commercially successful Texas Chainsaw Massacre remake and appeared in action and romantic films throughout the 2000s. In 2017 she executive-produced and starred in USA Network's The Sinner, playing a woman who commits a shocking act of violence, to strong reviews. She later starred in the Facebook Watch series Limetown (2019) and the Hulu true-crime miniseries Candy (2022), portraying Candy Montgomery.

Personal life

Biel married singer Justin Timberlake in 2012 in Italy; they have two sons. She co-founded the wellness brand Kinderfarms and has been involved in charitable work on clean water access.
