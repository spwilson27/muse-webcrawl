Islam Ramazanovich Makhachev (Russian: Ислам Рамазанович Махачев; born October 27, 1991) is a Russian professional mixed martial artist. He competes in the Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC), where he is a former UFC Lightweight Champion and is widely regarded as one of the best pound-for-pound fighters in the world.

Background

Makhachev was born in Makhachkala, Dagestan, and grew up in the village of Burshi. He trained in combat sambo under Abdulmanap Nurmagomedov, the father of Khabib Nurmagomedov, and won the world combat sambo championship at 74 kg in 2016. He is a close friend and training partner of Khabib, who became his head coach after retiring.

Mixed martial arts career

Makhachev made his professional debut in 2010 and signed with the UFC in 2015. After an early loss to Adriano Martins, he built a long winning streak with victories over opponents including Arman Tsarukyan, Drew Dober, Thiago Moisés, Dan Hooker, and Bobby Green.

Lightweight championship

At UFC 280 in October 2022, Makhachev submitted former champion Charles Oliveira in the second round to win the vacant UFC Lightweight Championship. He defended the title twice against featherweight champion Alexander Volkanovski (UFC 284 and UFC 294), then submitted Dustin Poirier at UFC 302 and Renato Moicano at UFC 311, setting the record for the most consecutive title defenses in UFC lightweight history. In 2025 he vacated the lightweight title to move up to welterweight.

Fighting style

Makhachev is known for his pressure wrestling, top control, and submissions, combined with increasingly sharp striking, reflecting the Dagestani sambo-based approach popularized by his team.
