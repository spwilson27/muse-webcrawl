World record progression 110 metres hurdles men

Aries Merritt set the current record of 12.80 seconds in 2012.

The first world record in the men's 110 metres hurdles was recognised by the IAAF (now World Athletics) in 1912. The record has progressed from 15.0 seconds, run by Forrest Smithson of the United States at the 1908 London Olympics (ratified as the inaugural record), to the current mark of 12.80 seconds, set by Aries Merritt of the United States in Brussels on 7 September 2012.

History

Renaldo Nehemiah was the first man to break 13 seconds, running 12.93 in Zürich in 1981, a mark that stood for eight years. Colin Jackson's 12.91, set at the 1993 World Championships in Stuttgart, survived for more than a decade before Liu Xiang, Dominique Arnold and Dayron Robles each trimmed it by hundredths. Merritt's 12.80 came at the end of a remarkably consistent 2012 season in which he ran under 13 seconds eight times and won Olympic gold in London.
