The FV4034 Challenger 2 is a British main battle tank (MBT) in service with the armies of the United Kingdom, Oman, and, since 2023, Ukraine. It was designed and built by Vickers Defence Systems and entered service in 1998, replacing the Challenger 1.

Design

Challenger 2 is protected by second-generation Chobham armour (Dorchester) and is widely regarded as one of the best-protected tanks in the world. It is armed with a 120 mm L30A1 rifled gun — unusual among modern NATO tanks, most of which use smoothbore guns — firing HESH and APFSDS rounds, and powered by a Perkins CV12 diesel engine producing 1,200 hp, giving a road speed of about 59 km/h.

Service history

The tank saw operational service in Bosnia, Kosovo and Iraq, where in 2003 one Challenger 2 survived numerous RPG hits and a MILAN anti-tank missile strike. No Challenger 2 was lost to enemy fire until September 2023, when one of the tanks supplied to Ukraine was destroyed in combat. In 2003, in a friendly-fire incident near Basra, one Challenger 2 was destroyed by another.

Future

The Challenger 3 upgrade programme will rebuild much of the fleet with a new turret and the Rheinmetall 120 mm smoothbore gun, extending service life into the 2040s.
