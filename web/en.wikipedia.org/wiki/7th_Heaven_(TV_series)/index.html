7th Heaven (TV series)

7th Heaven
American family drama television series
The WB / The CW, 1996–2007
Created by Brenda Hampton.

7th Heaven is an American family drama television series created by Brenda Hampton. It premiered on The WB in 1996 and ran for eleven seasons, concluding on The CW in 2007. For much of its run it was The WB’s flagship family drama and one of the network’s longest-running series.

Premise

The series centres on the Reverend Eric Camden, a Protestant minister played by Stephen Collins, his wife Annie, played by Catherine Hicks, and their children in the fictional California town of Glenoak. The Camdens begin the series with five children—Matt, Mary, Lucy, Simon and Ruthie—and later welcome twins Sam and David. Episodes typically follow several family members through moral, romantic, school and community problems, usually resolved through conversation and parental guidance.

Themes and reception

The show addressed subjects including dating, drugs, pregnancy, grief, racism, faith and responsibility, generally from a wholesome, message-oriented perspective. Critics were often mixed about its sentimentality, but audiences made it a durable hit, particularly among family viewers. Jessica Biel, Beverley Mitchell, Barry Watson and Mackenzie Rosman were among the young cast members who became closely identified with the series.

Later history

The WB initially presented the tenth season as the last, but the newly formed CW network revived the programme for an eleventh season after strong ratings for the supposed finale. Discussion of the series later became inseparable from Collins’s 2014 admission of past sexual misconduct involving minors, which led networks to pull reruns for a time and reshaped public discussion of the show.
