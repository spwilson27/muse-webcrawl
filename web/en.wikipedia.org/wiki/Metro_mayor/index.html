Metro mayors are directly elected leaders of English combined authorities.

A metro mayor is a directly elected mayor who chairs a combined authority covering a city region in England. The role was created as part of devolution deals between central government and local areas, legislated for by the Cities and Local Government Devolution Act 2016. The first metro mayors were elected in May 2017.

Metro mayors exercise devolved powers over areas such as transport, housing, skills and economic development, and in Greater Manchester also oversee health and social care integration and the police and fire services through the mayor's role as police and crime commissioner.

History

The concept drew on the model of the Mayor of London, created in 2000, and on "devolution deals" negotiated from 2014 onwards under Chancellor George Osborne's "Northern Powerhouse" agenda. Elections in 2017 created mayors for Greater Manchester, the Liverpool City Region, the West Midlands, the West of England, Tees Valley, and the Cambridgeshire and Peterborough combined authority. Further mayors followed for the Sheffield City Region (South Yorkshire), the North of Tyne, the West Yorkshire Combined Authority, and others, with the number of mayoral combined authorities continuing to grow in the 2020s.

Notable metro mayors

  • Greater Manchester – Andy Burnham (Labour), elected 2017
  • West Midlands – Andy Street (Conservative), elected 2017; succeeded by Richard Parker (Labour) in 2024
  • Liverpool City Region – Steve Rotheram (Labour), elected 2017
  • Tees Valley – Ben Houchen (Conservative), elected 2017
  • West Yorkshire – Tracy Brabin (Labour), elected 2021
  • South Yorkshire – Dan Jarvis, then Oliver Coppard (Labour)

Powers

Powers vary by devolution deal but typically include control of local transport budgets and franchising of bus services, strategic planning and housing funds, adult education budgets, and economic development. Metro mayors can levy a precept on council tax and, in some areas, a mayoral development corporation can be established.
