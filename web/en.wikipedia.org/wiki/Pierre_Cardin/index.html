Pierre Cardin
BornJuly 2, 1922, San Biagio di Callalta, Italy
DiedDecember 29, 2020, Neuilly-sur-Seine, France
OccupationFashion designer

Pierre Cardin (born Pietro Costante Cardin; 2 July 1922 – 29 December 2020) was an Italian-born French fashion designer. He is known for what were his avant-garde style and Space Age designs, favouring geometric shapes and motifs and often experimenting with unisex fashions.

Career

Cardin moved to Paris in 1945, worked for Jeanne Paquin, Elsa Schiaparelli, and Christian Dior, and founded his own fashion house in 1950. His 1954 "bubble dress" brought international fame. He was a pioneer of ready-to-wear designer collections and of brand licensing, eventually lending his name to hundreds of products worldwide — a practice that built a fortune but diluted the label's prestige.

Later life

Cardin purchased the Paris restaurant Maxim's in 1981 and the Palais Bulles, a celebrated bubble-shaped house on the Côte d'Azur. He remained active into his nineties and died in 2020 at the age of 98.
