Hi Records


Hi Records logo

Hi Records is an American soul and blues record label founded in Memphis, Tennessee, in 1957 by singer Ray Harris, record store owner Joe Cuoghi, and partners Bill Cantrell and Quinton Claunch, with backing from Sam Phillips-connected investors. The label's name reportedly derived from the last syllable of Cuoghi's name.

History

Hi's early successes were largely instrumental rock and roll records by the Bill Black Combo and by Willie Mitchell, whose band became the label's house band at Royal Studios. In the 1970s, under producer Willie Mitchell, Hi became one of the defining labels of Memphis soul, distinguished by a smooth, string- and horn-laden sound built around the Hodges brothers (the "Hi Rhythm Section").

Artists

The label's most famous artist was Al Green, whose string of 1970s hits — including "Tired of Being Alone," "Let's Stay Together," "Love and Happiness," and "I'm Still in Love with You" — made Hi internationally famous. Other notable Hi artists included Ann Peebles ("I Can't Stand the Rain"), Syl Johnson, Otis Clay, O. V. Wright, and Don Bryant. In 1977 the label was sold to Al Bennett's Cream Records group; the catalogue later passed through several owners and is now handled by Fat Possum Records for much of the classic material.
