Segrate (Lombard: Segraa) is a town and comune (municipality) in the Metropolitan City of Milan, in the Lombardy region of northern Italy, located about 10 kilometres (6 mi) northeast of central Milan. It borders the municipalities of Milan, Pioltello, Peschiera Borromeo, Vimodrone and Cologno Monzese.

Geography and development

Once an agricultural village on the road from Milan toward the Adda river, Segrate grew rapidly during the post-war decades into a residential and logistics suburb. Within its territory lies part of the Idroscalo, the artificial lake created in the 1930s as a seaplane base and now a major recreational park for the Milan area. The planned residential district of Milano Due, built in the 1970s in neighbouring territory frequently associated with the Segrate area, was a landmark in Italian suburban development.

Transport and economy

Segrate hosts a major rail freight terminal and intermodal yard on the Milan–Venice line, one of the largest in northern Italy, together with warehouses and offices serving national media, publishing and technology companies. The area is served by Milan's suburban rail and bus networks and lies close to Linate Airport.
