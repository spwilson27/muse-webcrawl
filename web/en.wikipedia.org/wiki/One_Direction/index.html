One Direction, often shortened to 1D, are an English-Irish pop boy band formed in London in 2010 on the television series The X Factor. The group — Niall Horan, Liam Payne, Harry Styles, Louis Tomlinson, and, until 2015, Zayn Malik — became one of the best-selling boy bands of all time, selling over 70 million records worldwide.

History

The five members auditioned as soloists and were assembled into a group by the show's judges, finishing third. Their debut single "What Makes You Beautiful" (2011) topped charts internationally, and their first album Up All Night made them the first British group to debut at number one in the United States with a first album.

Albums and tours

Four further albums followed — Take Me Home (2012), Midnight Memories (2013), Four (2014), and Made in the A.M. (2015) — along with stadium tours including the Where We Are Tour, then the highest-grossing tour of 2014. Malik left in March 2015; the remaining four went on indefinite hiatus in 2016 and pursued solo careers.

Legacy

One Direction are credited with reviving the boy-band format in the social-media era, building a massive global fandom largely through Twitter and online platforms, and winning numerous awards including seven Brit Awards and seven American Music Awards. Liam Payne died in 2024.
