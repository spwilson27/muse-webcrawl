List of tallest buildings in the United States

This article lists the tallest buildings in the United States.

The tallest building in the United States is One World Trade Center in New York City, completed in 2014 and standing 1,776 feet (541.3 m) tall including its spire — a symbolic height referencing the year of American independence. New York City and Chicago have historically dominated the list: the first skyscrapers were built in Chicago in the 1880s, while New York's Empire State Building held the title of world's tallest building for four decades.

History

The Home Insurance Building in Chicago (1885), often regarded as the first skyscraper, introduced the steel-frame construction that made great heights possible. The title of tallest in the United States passed through a rapid succession of New York buildings in the early 20th century — the Singer Building, Metropolitan Life Tower, Woolworth Building, Bank of Manhattan Trust Building, Chrysler Building and finally the Empire State Building in 1931. Chicago's Sears (now Willis) Tower took the title in 1974 and held it until One World Trade Center was completed forty years later.
