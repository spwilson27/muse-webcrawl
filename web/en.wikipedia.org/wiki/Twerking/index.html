Twerking

Twerking is a dance move in which a person, usually a woman, shakes their hips and buttocks in an up-and-down bouncing motion, typically while squatting. The style originated in the bounce music scene of New Orleans in the late 1980s and 1990s, drawing on older African and African-American dance traditions.

Mainstream popularity

The word entered mainstream usage in the 2000s through hip hop lyrics and spread widely in 2013 after high-profile performances, notably by Miley Cyrus at the MTV Video Music Awards. "Twerk" was added to the Oxford English Dictionary in 2015, defined as dancing "in a sexually provocative manner involving thrusting hip movements and a low, squatting stance". Critics have debated whether its mainstream adoption constitutes cultural appropriation of Black dance culture.
