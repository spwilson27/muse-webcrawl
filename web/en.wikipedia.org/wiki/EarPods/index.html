EarPods are wired earbuds designed and marketed by Apple Inc. Introduced on September 12, 2012, alongside the iPhone 5, they replaced the round earbuds that Apple had bundled with its products since 2001. EarPods were included in the box with iPhones until 2020, and with iPods, and remain available as a standalone accessory.

Design

Unlike the earlier circular earbuds, EarPods have a teardrop shape designed from scans of human ears to fit the geometry of the ear more comfortably. They do not use silicone tips, resting instead in the outer ear. The design directs sound through ports intended to maximise airflow and audio output. A built-in remote on the cable of the right earbud allows the user to adjust volume, control music and video playback, and answer or end calls; a microphone is also built in.

Versions

EarPods were originally produced with a 3.5 mm headphone plug. Following the removal of the headphone jack from the iPhone 7 in 2016, a version with a Lightning connector was introduced. In 2023, alongside the iPhone 15 series, Apple released a USB-C version. The 3.5 mm version was discontinued, while the Lightning and USB-C versions continue to be sold.

Reception

Reviews generally praised EarPods as a notable improvement over the bundled earbuds they replaced, particularly in fit and bass response, while noting that sound isolation remained limited compared with in-ear designs using silicone tips. Their design served as the basis for the wireless AirPods introduced in 2016.
