This is a list of newspapers in Greece. The Greek press is centred on Athens and has historically been closely tied to political parties and movements, although circulations have declined sharply since the Greek government-debt crisis alongside the rise of online media. Leading titles include the centre-right Kathimerini, the centre-left Ta Nea and To Vima, and the left-wing Efimerida ton Syntakton.

English-language

Kathimerini publishes an English edition, formerly distributed with the International Herald Tribune. The Athens News, once the main English-language daily, ceased print publication in 2012.
