Dolby 3D

Dolby 3D is a brand of digital 3D cinema technology developed by Dolby Laboratories together with Philips, using wavelength-multiplex (interference filter) glasses derived from Infitec technology. Unlike polarised systems, Dolby 3D separates the red, green and blue primaries into slightly different wavelengths for each eye, allowing full-colour 3D projection on an ordinary white screen with a single digital projector fitted with a spinning filter wheel. The reusable glasses are more expensive than passive polarised glasses, and the system has been used in cinemas worldwide since the late 2000s.
