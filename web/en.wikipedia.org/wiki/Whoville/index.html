Whoville

Whoville is a fictional town created by American children's author Dr. Seuss. It is the home of the Whos, tiny people who appear in several of his books, most famously Horton Hears a Who! (1954) and How the Grinch Stole Christmas! (1957).

Whoville as depicted in Dr. Seuss's illustrations.

In Dr. Seuss's books

In Horton Hears a Who!, Whoville is a microscopic community living on a speck of dust, perched on a clover flower in the Jungle of Nool. The elephant Horton hears the Whos' cries for help and defends their existence against sceptical animals who cannot hear them, culminating in the book's moral: "A person's a person, no matter how small."

In How the Grinch Stole Christmas!, Whoville is a town of cheerful Whos living in the valley below Mount Crumpit, where the Grinch plots to steal their Christmas. The Whos' celebration of Christmas without presents teaches the Grinch that the holiday "doesn't come from a store". The two depictions differ in scale and setting, and are generally treated as separate versions of the town.

Adaptations

Whoville has appeared in numerous adaptations, including the 1966 animated television special How the Grinch Stole Christmas!, the 2000 live-action film How the Grinch Stole Christmas starring Jim Carrey, the 2008 animated film Horton Hears a Who!, and the 2018 animated film The Grinch. The town is also featured in the musical Seussical and in theme park attractions at Universal parks.
