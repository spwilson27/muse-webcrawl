Jonathan Nolan (born 6 June 1976) is a British-American screenwriter and producer. He is known for collaborating with his elder brother, director Christopher Nolan, and for creating the television series Person of Interest and Westworld.

Early life

Nolan was born in London to an English father and an American mother and was raised between London and Chicago. He studied English at Georgetown University, where he wrote the short story "Memento Mori" that became the basis of his brother's film Memento (2000), earning the pair an Academy Award nomination for Best Original Screenplay.

Career

Nolan co-wrote The Prestige (2006), The Dark Knight (2008), The Dark Knight Rises (2012) and Interstellar (2014) with his brother. For television, he created the CBS crime drama Person of Interest (2011–2016), about a surveillance system predicting violent crime, and, with his wife Lisa Joy, HBO's Westworld (2016–2022), a philosophical science-fiction series about android hosts in a theme park. He is an executive producer and director on the Amazon series Fallout (2024), adapted from the video game franchise.
