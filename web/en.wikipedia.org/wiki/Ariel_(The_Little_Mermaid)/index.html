Ariel is a fictional character in Walt Disney Pictures' animated film The Little Mermaid (1989). Voiced by Jodi Benson, she is the youngest daughter of King Triton, ruler of the undersea kingdom of Atlantica, and is distinguished by her long red hair, green tail, and seashell top. Fascinated by the human world, she collects human artefacts and trades her voice to the sea witch Ursula for three days as a human, falling in love with Prince Eric. Ariel is the fourth member of the Disney Princess franchise.

Development

The character adapts Hans Christian Andersen's 1837 fairy tale, softened for family audiences. Supervising animator Glen Keane based Ariel's design partly on his wife and on actress Alyssa Milano; her underwater hair movement drew on footage of astronaut Sally Ride in weightlessness. Songwriters Howard Ashman and Alan Menken gave her the "want song" "Part of Your World". Ariel's rebellious curiosity is credited with inaugurating the more active heroines of the Disney Renaissance.

Appearances

Ariel stars in the animated television series The Little Mermaid (1992–1994), the direct-to-video sequel The Little Mermaid II: Return to the Sea (2000), the prequel The Little Mermaid: Ariel's Beginning (2008), and Halle Bailey played her in the 2023 live-action remake. She appears throughout Disney parks, merchandise, and the Kingdom Hearts games.
