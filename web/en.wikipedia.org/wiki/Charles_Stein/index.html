Charles Stein

Charles Stein (born 1944) is an American poet and writer associated with the Language poetry movement and with the tradition of Charles Olson and the Black Mountain poets. His work combines long-form poetic composition with philosophical and critical inquiry into perception, language and myth.

Life and work

Stein studied at Yale University, where he encountered the work of Charles Olson, whose poetics of projective verse and whose writings on perception deeply influenced him. Stein became known both as a poet and as a critic and editor; he edited and introduced work on Olson's thought, including writings concerning Olson's studies of Alfred North Whitehead and of the pre-Socratic philosophers.

His books of poetry include long sequences and serial poems that interweave philosophical reflection, autobiography and mythic material. Among his works are The Hat Rack Tree, Parts and Other Parts, and the long poem From Mimir's Head. His critical and prose writings include Being = Space x Action: Searches for Freedom of Mind in Mathematics, Art and Mysticism and studies of Olson's poetics.

Stein's poetry is characterised by philosophical density, fragmentary and layered construction, and a sustained engagement with questions of consciousness and language. He has taught in workshops and programmes devoted to experimental writing, and his readings and lectures frequently address the intersection of poetry, philosophy and the contemplative traditions.
