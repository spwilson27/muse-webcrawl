The Unified Team (IOC code EUN, from the French Équipe unifiée) was the name used for the sports team of the former Soviet Union at the 1992 Winter Olympics in Albertville and the 1992 Summer Olympics in Barcelona. Following the dissolution of the Soviet Union in December 1991, athletes from twelve of the fifteen former Soviet republics competed together under the Olympic flag.

The Baltic states of Estonia, Latvia, and Lithuania competed independently, while the other twelve republics formed the Unified Team for one transitional Olympic cycle.

Results

At the Barcelona Summer Games, the Unified Team topped the medal table with 45 gold medals and 112 medals overall, ahead of the United States. At Albertville, the team finished second in the medal standings behind Germany. After 1992, the former republics began competing as independent nations, with Russia and the others forming their own National Olympic Committees.
