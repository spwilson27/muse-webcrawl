Yu-Gi-Oh! (Japanese: 遊☆戯☆王（ゆうぎおう）, Hepburn: Yū-Gi-Ō, lit. "King of Games") is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Kazuki Takahashi. It was serialized in Shueisha's Weekly Shōnen Jump magazine from 1996 to 2004. The plot follows a shy young boy named Yugi Mutou who solves the ancient Millennium Puzzle, awakening a gambling alter-ego — the spirit of an ancient Egyptian pharaoh — who helps him settle conflicts through various games.

Media franchise

The manga spawned a vast media franchise including multiple anime series, films, and the Yu-Gi-Oh! Trading Card Game, in which players duel using monster, spell, and trap cards. Two anime adaptations were produced by Toei Animation (1998) and Gallop (2000–2004), the latter becoming internationally popular, and numerous spin-off series followed, including Yu-Gi-Oh! GX, 5D's, Zexal, Arc-V, VRAINS, Sevens, and Go Rush!!.

The trading card game, published by Konami, was recognized by Guinness World Records as the best-selling trading card game in history. Creator Kazuki Takahashi passed away in July 2022.
