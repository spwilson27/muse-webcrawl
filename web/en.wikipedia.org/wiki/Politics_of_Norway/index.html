Politics of Norway

The politics of Norway take place in the framework of a parliamentary, representative democratic constitutional monarchy. Executive power is exercised by the King's Council, the cabinet, led by the Prime Minister. Legislative power is vested in the Storting, a unicameral parliament of 169 members elected for four-year terms by proportional representation.

Political parties

Norway has a multi-party system. Major parties include the Labour Party, the Conservative Party, the Progress Party, the Centre Party, and the Socialist Left Party. Coalition and minority governments are common.

Monarchy

The monarch, currently King Harald V, is head of state and formally appoints the government, though in practice the cabinet must command the confidence of the Storting.
