Outer automorphism group

In mathematics, the outer automorphism group of a group G, denoted Out(G), is the quotient group Aut(G) / Inn(G), where Aut(G) is the automorphism group of G and Inn(G) is the subgroup of inner automorphisms — those given by conjugation by an element of G.

Elements of Out(G) are equivalence classes of automorphisms that are not achievable by conjugation. The outer automorphism group measures the symmetries of a group that are not internal to it.

For example, the outer automorphism group of a free group on n generators plays a central role in geometric group theory and the study of Outer space, while most finite simple groups have small outer automorphism groups — the symmetric group S6 is a famous exception with Out of order 2.
