Wôlinak is an Abenaki First Nations community in the Centre-du-Québec region of Quebec, Canada. It is one of two Abenaki communities in Quebec, the other being Odanak. Wôlinak is situated near Bécancour, close to the confluence of the Bécancour River and the St. Lawrence River. The community's name means "land of the bay" (or "bay land") in the Abenaki language.

History

The Abenaki, part of the Wabanaki Confederacy, were displaced from their homelands in present-day New England during the colonial wars of the 17th and 18th centuries, with many families settling in the St. Lawrence valley at missions such as Saint-François-de-Sales (Odanak) and at Wôlinak, which developed in the early 18th century.

Present day

The community is governed by the Conseil des Abénakis de Wôlinak. Efforts to revitalise the Western Abenaki language and traditional crafts are central to community life, and Wôlinak participates in regional Wabanaki cultural gatherings.
