Gaelic League

The Gaelic League (Irish: Conradh na Gaeilge) is an Irish cultural organisation founded in Dublin on 31 July 1893 by Douglas Hyde, Eoin MacNeill and others to promote the Irish language and Irish culture. It spearheaded the Gaelic revival.


Founded: 31 July 1893, Dublin
Founders: Douglas Hyde, Eoin MacNeill
Purpose: Promotion of Irish language

Activities

The League ran Irish classes, published the newspaper An Claidheamh Soluis (edited for a time by Patrick Pearse), and organised the Oireachtas festival of music and literature on the model of the Welsh Eisteddfod.

Influence

Though officially non-political, the League radicalised many members who later led the Irish independence movement; Hyde resigned in 1915 over its politicisation. Conradh na Gaeilge still operates today, campaigning for Irish-language rights.
