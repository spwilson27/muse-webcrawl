The Aerospace Corporation

The Aerospace Corporation is an American nonprofit corporation that operates a federally funded research and development center (FFRDC) for the United States Space Force and other national-security space customers. Founded in 1960 and headquartered in El Segundo, California, it provides independent technical advice on space systems.

Role

Aerospace was created to give the Air Force systems-engineering and scientific support for ballistic missile and space programs free of the commercial conflicts of interest that contractors might have. Its staff of several thousand engineers and scientists work on launch assurance, satellite design review, space surveillance, and technology assessment, and it has supported virtually every US national-security launch.

Activities

The corporation runs laboratories and the Center for Space Policy and Strategy, publishes research on space debris, GPS, and launch reliability, and operates small experimental satellites to test new technologies.
