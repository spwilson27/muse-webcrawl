List of cheeses

This is a list of cheeses by place of origin. Cheese is a milk-based food made in wide varieties of flavours, textures and forms by coagulation of the milk protein casein. Types of cheese are grouped on the basis of the type of milk used, fat content, bacteria and moulds involved, texture (soft, semi-soft, semi-hard, hard), and ageing period. There are hundreds of recognised varieties — some counts of distinct cheeses exceed 1,800 worldwide — and cheesemaking traditions stretch back thousands of years.
