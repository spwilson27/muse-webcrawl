Christopher Nolan

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
[Image: Christopher Nolan beside an IMAX camera on the set of a large-scale practical stunt, suit jacket over a sweater as usual]

Sir Christopher Edward Nolan (born 30 July 1970) is a British and American filmmaker. Known for his Hollywood blockbusters with complex storytelling, he is considered a leading filmmaker of the 21st century. His films have grossed more than $6 billion worldwide, and he has received many accolades, including an Academy Award.

Following the backwards-told Memento (2000), Nolan revived Batman with the Dark Knight trilogy (2005–12), whose middle film reshaped superhero cinema, and made a series of high-concept blockbusters shot on film with practical effects: Inception (2010), Interstellar (2014), Dunkirk (2017) and Tenet (2020). His biopic Oppenheimer (2023) won seven Academy Awards including Best Picture and Best Director.
