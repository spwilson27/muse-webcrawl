Kim Possible

GenreAnimated action-comedy
Created byBob Schooley, Mark McCorkle
NetworkDisney Channel
Original run2002–2007

Kim Possible is an American animated action comedy-drama television series created by Bob Schooley and Mark McCorkle for Disney Channel. It follows high-school student Kimberly "Kim" Possible, who fights crime around the world with her best friend Ron Stoppable and computer genius Wade.

Reception

The series ran for four seasons and 87 episodes and was one of Disney Channel's most acclaimed shows, spawning television films, video games and a 2019 live-action film.
