Braskem is a Brazilian petrochemical company headquartered in São Paulo. It is the largest producer of thermoplastic resins in the Americas and a leading producer of polypropylene in the United States. Braskem operates industrial units in Brazil, the United States, Mexico and Germany, producing polyethylene, polypropylene, PVC and basic petrochemicals such as ethylene and propylene.

History

Braskem was created in 2002 through the integration of several Brazilian petrochemical companies controlled by the Odebrecht group (now Novonor) together with Petrobras. The company expanded internationally with the acquisition of assets in the United States and through the Braskem Idesa joint venture in Mexico, which operates the Ethylene XXI complex.

Green polyethylene

Braskem is the world's largest producer of biopolymers. Since 2010 it has manufactured "green" polyethylene from ethanol derived from Brazilian sugarcane at its plant in Triunfo, Rio Grande do Sul, marketed under the "I'm green" brand.
