Injury

Injury
DefinitionDamage to the body from external force, heat, chemicals or other agents
ContextsTrauma, sports, occupational, burns, poisoning
[Image: An emergency-room scene with a patient being wheeled in on a stretcher]

Injury is physiological damage to living tissue caused by external agents — mechanical trauma, heat, cold, electricity, chemicals or radiation. Injuries range from sprains and fractures (Bone) to life-threatening polytrauma treated in emergency medicine.

Prevention

Injuries are a leading cause of death in young people worldwide — road crashes, falls, drowning, violence — and largely preventable: seatbelts, helmets, swimming lessons and workplace safety are injury science's victories. First response is the first aid page's domain.
