Nikkei

Nikkei, Inc. (株式会社日本経済新聞社), formerly Nihon Keizai Shimbun, is a Japanese media company best known for publishing The Nikkei (Nikkei Shimbun), one of the world's largest financial newspapers, and for calculating the Nikkei 225 stock index.

History

The newspaper was founded in 1876 as the Chugai Bukka Shimpo (Domestic and Foreign Commodity Price Newspaper) and renamed Nihon Keizai Shimbun in 1946. Nikkei has published the Nikkei 225 average since 1950. In 2015 Nikkei acquired the British Financial Times from Pearson for £844 million, one of the largest purchases by a Japanese media group.

Activities

Beyond the flagship daily, Nikkei publishes the English-language Nikkei Asia, operates the QUICK financial information service, runs television interests in TV Tokyo, and organises data and index businesses. The term "Nikkei" also refers to Japanese emigrants and their descendants (Nikkeijin) — a separate usage.
