Virgin Plus

Virgin Plus (formerly Virgin Mobile Canada) is a Canadian mobile virtual network operator and flanker brand of Bell Mobility, providing postpaid and prepaid mobile phone and home internet services over the Bell network. It is targeted mainly at younger customers and markets a "Membership" programme of discounts and offers with entertainment and retail partners.

History

Virgin Mobile Canada was launched on 1 March 2005 as a joint venture between the Virgin Group and Bell Canada, reselling service on Bell's CDMA network. Richard Branson promoted the launch with a publicity stunt in Toronto. Bell acquired full ownership of the brand in 2009. The service moved from CDMA to HSPA and then LTE networks alongside Bell, and expanded into home internet in some provinces.

In July 2021 the service was rebranded from Virgin Mobile to Virgin Plus, aligning its name with a wider range of products (mobile and internet) and mirroring branding used by other Virgin telecom ventures. It competes with other Canadian flanker brands such as Rogers' Fido and Telus' Koodo.
