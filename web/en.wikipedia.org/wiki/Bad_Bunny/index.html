Bad Bunny

Benito Antonio Martínez Ocasio (born March 10, 1994), known professionally as Bad Bunny, is a Puerto Rican rapper, singer, and actor. Dubbed the "King of Latin Trap", he is regarded as one of the most influential Latin artists of all time and has been credited with helping Spanish-language music achieve worldwide mainstream popularity. His music spans reggaeton, Latin trap and numerous other genres.

Career

Bad Bunny rose to prominence in 2016 with songs uploaded to SoundCloud while he worked at a supermarket in Vega Baja, Puerto Rico. He broke through internationally with features on "Sensualidad" and Cardi B's chart-topping "I Like It" (2018). His debut album X 100pre followed in 2018, and YHLQMDLG (2020) became the most-streamed album globally on Spotify that year. Un Verano Sin Ti (2022) was the world's best-performing album of its year and made him the first non-English-language act to be Spotify's most-streamed artist three years running.

Other work and impact

Beyond music, he has appeared as a professional wrestler for WWE, including at WrestleMania, and acted in films including Bullet Train. He is an outspoken advocate on Puerto Rican social and political issues and for gender and LGBTQ+ causes. His stadium tours have been among the highest-grossing in history.
