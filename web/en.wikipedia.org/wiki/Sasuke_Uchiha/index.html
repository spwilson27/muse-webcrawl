Sasuke Uchiha (Japanese: うちは サスケ, Uchiha Sasuke) is a fictional character in the Naruto manga and anime franchise created by Masashi Kishimoto. He is a member of the Uchiha clan and, with the protagonist Naruto Uzumaki and Sakura Haruno, one of the three original members of Team 7 under Kakashi Hatake. As the deuteragonist and Naruto's chief rival, Sasuke's story — his quest to avenge his clan's massacre and his eventual redemption — forms one of the series' central arcs.

Creation and conception

Kishimoto designed Sasuke as a "cool genius" foil to the loud, underachieving Naruto, though he has said Sasuke's increasingly intricate clothing was difficult to draw repeatedly. The name comes from Sasuke Sarutobi, a famous fictional ninja of Japanese children's literature. Unlike Naruto, whose development Kishimoto planned early, Sasuke's trajectory grew more morally ambiguous over the series' run.

Character and story

Sasuke was the younger son of the Uchiha clan's head and initially lived in the shadow of his prodigious elder brother Itachi. When Sasuke was seven, Itachi slaughtered the entire clan, sparing only Sasuke and telling him to grow strong through hatred. At the ninja academy Sasuke graduated top of his class; in Team 7 his rivalry with Naruto pushed both to excel, and he began to value his teammates as a new family.

Consumed by the desire for power to kill Itachi, Sasuke defected from the Hidden Leaf to the renegade Orochimaru — the inciting conflict of the series' second half. After defeating a dying Itachi, he learned from Obito Uchiha that Itachi had massacred the clan under orders from the Leaf's leadership to avert a coup, and had spared Sasuke out of love. Sasuke turned his vengeance against the village itself, joining the criminal Akatsuki and later forming the team Taka/Hebi. In the Fourth Great Ninja War he fought alongside Naruto against Madara and Kaguya Ōtsutsuki, then clashed with Naruto one final time at the Valley of the End over his plan to revolutionise the ninja world by force. Defeated and reconciled, Sasuke left on a journey of atonement; as an adult in Boruto, he serves the village from the shadows as its "Supporting Shadow", and marries Sakura, with whom he has a daughter, Sarada.

Abilities

Sasuke inherited the Uchiha's Sharingan eye, which he advances to the Mangekyō Sharingan — granting techniques such as the black flames Amaterasu, the defensive apparition Susanoo, and genjutsu — and later the Rinnegan in his left eye, with space–time abilities. His signature techniques include the Chidori (developed from Kakashi's Lightning Blade) and its variants, fire-style jutsu, and mastery of the sword; as an adult he is, with Naruto, among the most powerful shinobi alive.

Reception

Sasuke is one of the most popular characters in the franchise, repeatedly winning or placing near the top of official popularity polls, and he is frequently cited among the most recognisable anime characters generally. His arc has drawn both praise for its ambition and criticism from readers who found his later actions difficult to sympathise with.
