Haskell Memorial Stadium is a stadium in Lawrence, Kansas, on the campus of Haskell Indian Nations University, a public tribal land-grant university serving Native American students. Opened in 1926, it is regarded as the first stadium built by and for Native Americans in the United States.

History

Funding for the stadium was raised from Native American tribes and individual donors across the country. It was dedicated on October 30, 1926, during Haskell's homecoming, which drew tens of thousands of visitors from hundreds of tribal nations and featured a football victory over Bucknell. The dedication remains one of the largest gatherings of Native Americans of its era.

The stadium's entrance features an arch honoring the Haskell students and alumni who served and died in World War I. The venue hosts the Haskell Fighting Indians football team and tribal and community events, including powwows. It was added to the National Register of Historic Places in recognition of its significance in Native American history.
