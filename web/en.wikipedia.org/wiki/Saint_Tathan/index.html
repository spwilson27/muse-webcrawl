Saint Tathan

Saint Tathan (Tathyw, Tatius)
Era: 5th–6th century
Region: Wales
Feast day: 26 December
Known for: Patron saint of St Athan

Saint Tathan (Latin: Tatius; Welsh: Tathyw) was an early Welsh saint, said to have been an Irish monk who crossed the Bristol Channel and founded a monastic school at the place now called St Athan in the Vale of Glamorgan, which derives its name from him.

Tradition

According to later Welsh tradition, Tathan withdrew to a hermit's life near Caerwent before settling at St Athan, where his church became a minor centre of teaching. Among his supposed disciples was St Cadoc. The parish church of St Tathan at St Athan preserves his association with the village, and he is commemorated on 26 December.
