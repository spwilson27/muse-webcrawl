List of Super Bowl MVPs

Super Bowl MVP Award
Sport: American football
League: NFL
First awarded: 1967 (Super Bowl I)
Most wins: Tom Brady (5)

The Super Bowl Most Valuable Player Award, or Super Bowl MVP, is presented annually to the most valuable player of the Super Bowl, the National Football League's championship game. The winner is chosen by a panel of 16 football writers and broadcasters, and, since Super Bowl XXXV, fans voting electronically; the media panel's ballots count for 80 per cent and the fans' for 20 per cent.

History and records

The award has been given since the first Super Bowl in 1967, when Bart Starr of the Green Bay Packers won; Starr repeated the following year. Quarterbacks dominate the list. Tom Brady holds the record with five MVP awards (XXXVI, XXXVIII, XLIX, LI and LV), followed by Joe Montana with three and by Bart Starr, Terry Bradshaw, Eli Manning and Patrick Mahomes with two each. Only one player from a losing team has won: linebacker Chuck Howley of the Dallas Cowboys in Super Bowl V. Defensive players and special cases are rare — safety Dexter Jackson, linebackers Ray Lewis, Von Miller and Malcolm Smith, and wide receivers including Lynn Swann, Jerry Rice, Deion Branch, Hines Ward, Santonio Holmes, Julian Edelman and Cooper Kupp are among the non-quarterback winners. Super Bowl XII produced co-MVPs, defensive linemen Harvey Martin and Randy White.

Selection

The award is sponsored and commonly associated with a vehicle or other prize for the winner, and in several years the MVP has famously declared "I'm going to Disney World!" (or Disneyland) in a long-running advertising campaign.
