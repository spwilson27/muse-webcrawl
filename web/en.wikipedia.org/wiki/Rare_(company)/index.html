Rare (company)

Rare Limited
Industry: Video games
Founded: 1985
Founders: Tim Stamper, Chris Stamper
Headquarters: Twycross, Leicestershire, England
Parent: Xbox Game Studios

Rare Limited is a British video game developer and a studio of Xbox Game Studios based in Twycross, Leicestershire. Rare's games span a wide range of genres, including platformers, first-person shooters, action-adventure, fighting, and racing.

The company was founded in 1985 by Tim and Chris Stamper, who had previously founded Ultimate Play the Game, a successful developer of games for the ZX Spectrum. Rare developed games for Nintendo systems for many years, creating acclaimed titles such as Donkey Kong Country, GoldenEye 007, Banjo-Kazooie, Perfect Dark and Conker's Bad Fur Day.

Microsoft acquisition

In 2002, Microsoft acquired Rare for US$375 million, making it a first-party developer for the Xbox. Under Microsoft, Rare developed Kameo: Elements of Power, Viva Piñata, the Kinect Sports series, and the pirate adventure game Sea of Thieves (2018), which became a long-running live service success.

Legacy

Rare is regarded as one of the most influential British game studios. Several of its Nintendo-era properties remain iconic, and its characters have appeared in crossover titles such as the Super Smash Bros. series.
