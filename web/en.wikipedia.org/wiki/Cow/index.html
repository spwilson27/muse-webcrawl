Cattle are large domesticated hoofed mammals raised worldwide for milk, meat (beef and veal), leather and labour. They descend from the wild aurochs, domesticated independently in the Near East and South Asia around 10,500 years ago as part of the Neolithic Revolution.

Importance and issues

Cattle numbers exceed a billion; dairy and beef industries are pillars of many economies and cuisines — Cheese among their products — and cattle are sacred in Hinduism, protected from slaughter in much of India, including Lahore's neighbouring culture (Karachi is in Pakistan). Their methane emissions and land use are major climate concerns in sustainability debates.
