The Samwer brothers — Marc (born 1970), Oliver (born 1972) and Alexander Samwer (born 1975) — are German entrepreneurs and investors known for founding companies that adapt successful American internet business models for other markets.

Career

The brothers founded the online auction site Alando in 1999 and sold it to eBay the same year; their mobile-content company Jamba! was sold to VeriSign in 2004. In 2007 they founded Rocket Internet, a Berlin-based company builder that launched ventures including Zalando and numerous e-commerce clones in emerging markets, and which went public in 2014. Their investment arm, Global Founders Capital, has backed companies worldwide.

Reception

The Samwers are praised for execution speed and operational discipline, and criticised — notably by some Silicon Valley founders — for a "clone" strategy of copying proven models. Oliver Samwer is the most public-facing of the three.
