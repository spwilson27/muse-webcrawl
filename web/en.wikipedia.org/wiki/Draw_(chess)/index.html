Draw (chess)

Draw
Result½–½ (each player scores half a point)
MethodsAgreement, stalemate, threefold repetition, fifty-move rule, insufficient material, perpetual check

In chess, a draw is the result of a game ending in a tie, with each player scoring half a point. Draws occur by several routes under the FIDE Laws of Chess, and at master level — where evenly matched players and thorough opening preparation make decisive results harder — a large proportion of games, often around half, end drawn.

Ways a game can be drawn

Draw by agreement: the players may agree a draw at any point, though some tournaments restrict this (for example the "Sofia rules", which prohibit offers before a set move or require the arbiter's consent). Stalemate: if the player to move has no legal move and is not in check, the game is drawn immediately. Threefold repetition: a player may claim a draw when the same position occurs for the third time with the same player to move; fivefold repetition ends the game automatically. Fifty-move rule: a player may claim a draw after fifty moves by each side without a pawn move or capture; seventy-five such moves end the game automatically. Insufficient material: the game is drawn when neither side can possibly checkmate, e.g. king against king, or king and minor piece against king. Perpetual check is no longer a separate rule but leads to a draw via repetition.

Draw offers and etiquette

A draw offer is made after playing a move and before pressing the clock, and it cannot be withdrawn once made. Offers are recorded on the scoresheet with the symbol (=). Repeated or frivolous offers may be penalised as distraction.

"Grandmaster draws" and reform

Short, bloodless agreed draws have long attracted criticism, and organisers have responded with Sofia rules, scoring systems such as 3–1–0, and play-off formats like Armageddon in rapid events. Defenders note that many top-level draws are hard-fought and that the draw is an intrinsic part of chess; the longest decisive and drawn games in world championship history — including the 136-move sixth game of the 2021 match — show how fine the line can be.
