Qzone

Qzone (Chinese: QQ空间; pinyin: QQ Kōngjiān) is a social networking website based in China which was created by Tencent in 2005. It allows users to write blogs, keep diaries, send photos, listen to music, and watch videos. Users can set their Qzone background and select accessories based on their preferences so that each Qzone is customized to the individual member's taste. Most Qzone accessories are free; however, some are not. Users can purchase premium accessories with "Yellow Diamond" membership.

History

Qzone started in April 2005 as an internal feature of the QQ instant messaging service. The name originally meant "QQ Zone". In 2008, Qzone was spun off by Zhu Liang. As China's social networking market grew, Qzone became one of the largest social platforms in the country, at times reporting hundreds of millions of users. It was particularly popular among younger users and in lower-tier cities where competing services had less reach.

Features

Qzone integrates tightly with QQ: users log in with QQ credentials and can share content, leave messages on friends' "message boards", publish photo albums, and post short updates known as "sayings" (说说). Games and third-party applications have also been hosted on the platform. Customization through virtual goods has been a significant revenue source.

Decline and status

With the rise of WeChat, also owned by Tencent, and microblogging and short-video platforms, Qzone's active user base declined during the 2010s, though it remains one of the largest social networks in China by registered accounts and continues to operate as part of Tencent's social media portfolio.
