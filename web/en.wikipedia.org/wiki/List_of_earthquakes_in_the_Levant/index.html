List of earthquakes in the Levant

This is a list of notable earthquakes that have taken place in the Levant — the historical region encompassing modern Israel, Palestine, Jordan, Lebanon, Syria, and adjoining areas. The region lies along the Dead Sea Transform, the fault system running from the Red Sea to Turkey, and has been struck by destructive earthquakes throughout recorded history.
