List of Harry Potter characters

The following is a list of the principal characters in J. K. Rowling's Harry Potter series of novels.

Main characters

CharacterDescription
Harry PotterThe protagonist; "the Boy Who Lived", marked by Lord Voldemort's failed killing curse
Ron WeasleyHarry's loyal best friend, youngest son of Arthur and Molly Weasley
Hermione GrangerMuggle-born witch, the cleverest student of their year
Albus DumbledoreHeadmaster of Hogwarts and leader of the Order of the Phoenix
Lord Voldemort (Tom Marvolo Riddle)The principal antagonist; a dark wizard seeking immortality and domination
Severus SnapePotions master and double agent whose loyalties are long ambiguous
Rubeus HagridHalf-giant Keeper of Keys and Grounds at Hogwarts
Draco MalfoyHarry's Slytherin rival
Ginny WeasleyRon's younger sister; later Harry's wife
Neville LongbottomA Gryffindor classmate who grows into a hero of the resistance
Luna LovegoodAn eccentric, perceptive Ravenclaw friend
Sirius BlackHarry's godfather, wrongly imprisoned in Azkaban
Remus LupinWerewolf and Defence Against the Dark Arts teacher
Minerva McGonagallTransfiguration professor and Deputy Headmistress

Hogwarts staff

  • Filius Flitwick (Charms)
  • Pomona Sprout (Herbology)
  • Horace Slughorn (Potions, returned)
  • Sybill Trelawney (Divination)
  • Quirinus Quirrell, Gilderoy Lockhart, Mad-Eye Moody (Barty Crouch Jr.), Dolores Umbridge and Amycus Carrow (Defence Against the Dark Arts, successively)
  • Argus Filch (caretaker), Madam Pomfrey (matron), Rolanda Hooch (flying instructor)

Order of the Phoenix and allies

  • Arthur and Molly Weasley; Bill, Charlie, Percy, Fred and George Weasley
  • Kingsley Shacklebolt, Nymphadora Tonks, Alastor "Mad-Eye" Moody
  • James and Lily Potter (deceased)
  • Fleur Delacour, Viktor Krum, Cedric Diggory, Cho Chang
  • Dobby, Kreacher, Griphook (non-human allies)

Death Eaters and antagonists

  • Bellatrix Lestrange, Lucius Malfoy, Narcissa Malfoy
  • Peter Pettigrew (Wormtail), Barty Crouch Jr.
  • Fenrir Greyback, Antonin Dolohov, Augustus Rookwood
  • Dolores Umbridge (Ministry antagonist)
  • Vernon, Petunia and Dudley Dursley (Harry's Muggle relatives)
