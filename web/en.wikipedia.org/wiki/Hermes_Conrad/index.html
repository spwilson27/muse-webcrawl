Hermes Conrad is a fictional character in the animated television series Futurama, voiced by Phil LaMarr. He is a Jamaican accountant and bureaucrat at Planet Express, the delivery company around which the series is set, and is known for his obsession with paperwork, his limbo skills, and his convoluted exclamations.

Character

Hermes is a workaholic "Grade 34" bureaucrat of the Central Bureaucracy (later demoted to Grade 36 and eventually reinstated), who handles Planet Express's finances, legal filings, and personnel discipline. He is married to LaBarbara, and their son Dwight is a friend of Professor Farnsworth's clone, Cubert. Hermes potently dislikes the company's physician, Dr. Zoidberg, who once ate his lunch — a running gag across the series.

In his youth Hermes was a champion limbo dancer who represented Earth at the 2980 Olympics; he retired after a young fan attempting to imitate him was killed, a trauma explored in the episode "The Deep South" follow-ups and "How Hermes Requisitioned His Groove Back", an episode centred on his breakdown and rediscovery of bureaucratic joy. He habitually spices his speech with elaborate comparisons ("like a green snake in a sugar cane field!") and enjoys extremely spicy food.
