List of rivers of Canada

Canada's rivers drain into three oceans — the Atlantic, Pacific and Arctic — as well as into Hudson Bay and the Gulf of Mexico (via the Mississippi system). The longest river entirely in Canada is the Mackenzie, the country's largest river system, which drains a vast area of the north-west into the Beaufort Sea.
