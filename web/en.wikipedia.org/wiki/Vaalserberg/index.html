Vaalserberg


Elevation: 322.4 m (1,058 ft)
Location: Limburg, Netherlands
Coordinates: 50°45′N 6°01′E

The Vaalserberg is a hill with a height of 322.4 metres (1,058 ft) above NAP and the highest point in mainland Netherlands. It is located in the province of Limburg, near the village of Vaals, after which it is named.

Three-country point

The Vaalserberg is famous as the location of the Drielandenpunt ("three-country point"), where the borders of the Netherlands, Belgium and Germany meet. Between 1830 and 1919 the point was actually a four-country point, also bordering the tiny neutral territory of Neutral Moresnet. The spot is a popular tourist attraction, with observation towers on both the Dutch and Belgian sides, a labyrinth, and restaurants.

Tourism

The Boudewijn Tower on the Belgian side and the Wilhelmina Tower on the Dutch side offer panoramic views over the surrounding hills of South Limburg, the Belgian province of Liège and the German city of Aachen, which lies only a few kilometres away.
