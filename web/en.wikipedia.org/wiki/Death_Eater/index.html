The Death Eaters are a fictional group of witches and wizards in J. K. Rowling's Harry Potter series who serve as the followers of the dark wizard Lord Voldemort. Devoted to Voldemort's ideology of blood purity, they seek to purge the wizarding world of Muggle-borns and to subjugate Muggles. Death Eaters are identified by the Dark Mark, a skull-and-serpent tattoo branded on their inner left forearms, which Voldemort uses to summon them.

History in the series

The Death Eaters waged the First Wizarding War against the Ministry of Magic and the Order of the Phoenix until Voldemort's fall in 1981, after which many claimed they had been under the Imperius Curse, were imprisoned in Azkaban, or went into hiding. They regrouped after Voldemort's return to power in Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire (1995 in-story), infiltrated and eventually seized the Ministry, and were defeated at the Battle of Hogwarts in Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows.

Notable members

Prominent Death Eaters include Bellatrix Lestrange, Voldemort's fanatical lieutenant; Lucius Malfoy and his son Draco; Severus Snape, who acted as a double agent for Albus Dumbledore; Peter Pettigrew, who betrayed Harry Potter's parents; Barty Crouch Jr.; Antonin Dolohov; and the werewolf Fenrir Greyback, an ally of the group. Antonin Dolohov and Thorfinn Rowle are among those shown duelling in the final battle.

Themes

Rowling has said the Death Eaters draw on the iconography and ideology of extremist movements, particularly in their obsession with racial purity, use of terror, torture, and murder, and employment of the Unforgivable Curses (the Killing Curse Avada Kedavra, the Cruciatus Curse, and the Imperius Curse).
