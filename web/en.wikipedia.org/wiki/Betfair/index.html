Betfair is a British online gambling company founded in 2000. It operates the world's largest online betting exchange, along with a conventional sportsbook, casino and other gaming products. It merged with Paddy Power in 2016 to form Paddy Power Betfair, now Flutter Entertainment.

The betting exchange

A betting exchange differs from a traditional bookmaker in that customers bet against each other rather than against the house: one customer "backs" an outcome (betting that it will happen) and another "lays" it (betting that it will not). The exchange matches the bets and takes a commission on net winnings. Betfair's exchange, launched in June 2000 by Andrew Black and Edward Wray, was the first commercially successful example and came to dominate the sector.

History

Betfair floated on the London Stock Exchange in 2010. It withdrew from various markets as gambling regulation evolved, introduced the "premium charge" on its most successful exchange customers, and expanded into a fixed-odds sportsbook in 2012. The 2016 merger with Paddy Power created one of the world's largest gambling groups.
