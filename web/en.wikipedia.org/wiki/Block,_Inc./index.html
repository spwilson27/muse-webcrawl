Block, Inc. (formerly Square, Inc.) is an American financial technology company founded by Jack Dorsey and Jim McKelvey in 2009. It operates the Square point-of-sale platform for merchants and the Cash App payment service for consumers.

History

Square began with a small card reader that plugged into a smartphone, letting small businesses accept card payments. The company expanded into point-of-sale hardware, business software and lending, and launched Cash App in 2013. In December 2021 it renamed itself Block to reflect its multiple business lines ("blocks"), including the music service Tidal and Bitcoin-focused projects.

Products

Its offerings include Square Register and Square Terminal, Cash App's peer-to-peer payments and investing features, and the Afterpay buy-now-pay-later service, acquired in 2022.
