List of craters on Mars

Mars bears hundreds of thousands of impact craters larger than one kilometre across; just over a thousand of them have been given official names by the International Astronomical Union. Martian craters are generally named after scientists and science-fiction authors (for craters larger than about 60 km) or small towns on Earth (for smaller craters). This list includes some of the most notable named craters, including several that have served as landing sites for robotic missions.

Many craters on Mars show distinctive "rampart" ejecta blankets with lobed, flow-like edges, interpreted as evidence that the impacts melted subsurface ice. Others preserve deltas, lake sediments, and alluvial fans that record a warmer, wetter early Mars.
