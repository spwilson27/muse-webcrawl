List of Olympic medalists

The Olympic medalists are the athletes who have won medals — gold, silver or bronze — at the Olympic Games, the leading international sporting event founded by Pierre de Coubertin and first held in modern form at Athens in 1896. This article summarises the most decorated Olympic medalists of all time and links to medalist lists by sport and Games.
