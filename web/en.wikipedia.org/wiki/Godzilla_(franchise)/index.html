Godzilla (franchise)

Godzilla is a Japanese monster (kaiju) media franchise created and owned by Toho Co., Ltd, centred on the giant monster Godzilla, who first appeared in the 1954 film Godzilla directed by Ishirō Honda. It is one of the longest-running film franchises in history, spanning dozens of Japanese films as well as American productions, television series, novels, comics, and video games.

History

The original 1954 film, a dark allegory for nuclear destruction, launched the kaiju genre. Toho produced films through Shōwa, Heisei, and Millennium eras, with later revivals including Shin Godzilla (2016) and Godzilla Minus One (2023), which won the Academy Award for Best Visual Effects. In the United States, Legendary Pictures' MonsterVerse began with Godzilla (2014) and continued with Kong: Skull Island, Godzilla: King of the Monsters, and Godzilla x Kong.

Cultural impact

Godzilla is a worldwide pop-culture icon and one of the most recognisable symbols of Japanese popular culture, recognised by Guinness World Records as the longest continuously running film franchise.
