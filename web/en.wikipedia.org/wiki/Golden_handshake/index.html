Golden handshake

A golden handshake is a clause in an executive employment contract that provides the executive with a significant severance package in the case that the executive loses their job through firing, restructuring, or even scheduled retirement. This can be in the form of cash, equity, and other benefits, and is often accompanied by an accelerated vesting of stock options.

Golden handshake
TypeExecutive severance arrangement
TriggerTermination, restructuring, or retirement
Related termsGolden parachute, golden handcuffs, golden boot

Usage

Golden handshakes are intended to compensate senior executives for the loss of office and may also serve to make a change of management smoother by securing the departing executive's agreement not to compete or to assist with the transition. In the public sector, payments to departing officials — particularly where performance has been poor — have drawn political criticism, and several governments have capped or banned large payoffs for public officials.

Criticism

Critics argue that generous exit payments can reward failure, since an executive may receive a large package even when the firm has performed badly during their tenure. Shareholder groups and governance codes in the United Kingdom, Australia, and elsewhere have pressed for limits, disclosure, and shareholder approval of termination payments.

Related concepts

  • Golden parachute – severance benefits triggered specifically by a change in control of the company.
  • Golden handcuffs – retention incentives that an executive forfeits on leaving.
  • Golden boot – an incentive to retire early.
