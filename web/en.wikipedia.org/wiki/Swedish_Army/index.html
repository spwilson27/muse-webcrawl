The Swedish Army (Swedish: Svenska armén) is the land force branch of the Swedish Armed Forces. It is responsible for land-based military operations and the defence of Sweden's territory. The Swedish Army traces its history to the early 16th century and the reign of Gustav Vasa, making it one of the oldest standing armies in the world, and it played a major role in European warfare during the Swedish Empire period of the 17th and early 18th centuries.

History

The modern Swedish Army emerged in the 1520s and 1530s under Gustav I (Gustav Vasa), who created a standing national force recruited through the indelningsverk (allotment system), under which groups of farms were responsible for providing and supporting a soldier. Reformed under Gustav II Adolf (Gustavus Adolphus), the army became renowned during the Thirty Years' War for its mobile artillery, combined-arms tactics and disciplined infantry. Under Charles XII it fought the Great Northern War (1700–1721), which ended the Swedish Empire. Sweden has not fought a war since 1814, and the army was progressively oriented toward territorial defence during the Cold War, when Sweden maintained a large conscript force despite its policy of neutrality.

Conscription was suspended in 2010 in favour of an all-volunteer force, but was reactivated in 2017–2018 in response to the deteriorating security situation in the Baltic region. Following Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, Sweden abandoned two centuries of military non-alignment and joined NATO in March 2024.

Organisation

The army is headed by the Chief of Army and organised into brigades, regiments and specialised units. Major formations and units have included the Life Guards (Livgardet) in Stockholm, the Norrbotten Regiment (I 19) in Boden, the Skaraborg Regiment (P 4) in Skövde, the South Scanian Regiment (P 7), the Göta Logistic Regiment, the Life Regiment Hussars (K 3), the Army Ranger Battalion in Arvidsjaur, and the Gotland Regiment (P 18), re-established in 2018 to defend the strategically important island of Gotland in the Baltic Sea.

Equipment

The army's principal equipment includes the Stridsvagn 122 main battle tank (a Swedish variant of the German Leopard 2), the Combat Vehicle 90 (Stridsfordon 90) infantry fighting vehicle — exported to several countries and donated to Ukraine — the Archer artillery system, and the Ak 5 and newer Ak 24 assault rifles. Sweden announced the purchase of Leopard 2A8 tanks (designated Stridsvagn 123) to modernise its armoured fleet.
