Beaujolais nouveau

Beaujolais nouveau
TypeVin de primeur (young wine)
GrapeGamay
ReleaseThird Thursday of November

Beaujolais nouveau is a red wine made from Gamay grapes produced in the Beaujolais region of France. It is the most popular vin de primeur, fermented for only a few weeks before being released for sale on the third Thursday of November. The arrival of the new vintage is celebrated worldwide with the slogan "Le Beaujolais nouveau est arrivé!"

Production

Beaujolais nouveau is produced using carbonic maceration, in which whole grapes ferment intracellularly, producing a light, fruity wine with low tannin that is intended for immediate drinking rather than ageing. By law, the grapes must be harvested by hand and the wine may not be released before the official release date.

History and marketing

The tradition began as a local celebration of the end of harvest, but in the second half of the 20th century négociants such as Georges Duboeuf turned the release into an international marketing phenomenon, with races to deliver the first bottles to Paris and export markets such as Japan and the United States. Critics have sometimes dismissed the wine as simple, but the event remains one of the best-known annual releases in the wine calendar.
