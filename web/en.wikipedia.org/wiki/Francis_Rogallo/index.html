Francis Rogallo

Born27 January 1912, Sanger, California, United States
Died1 September 2009, Kitty Hawk, North Carolina, United States
Known forRogallo wing; inventor of the flexible wing

Francis Melvin Rogallo (27 January 1912 – 1 September 2009) was an American aeronautical engineer and inventor who, with his wife Gertrude, developed the flexible self-inflating wing known as the Rogallo wing.

The flexible wing

Rogallo, an engineer at NASA's Langley Research Center, patented the flexible wing in the late 1940s and early 1950s. NASA studied the design (as the Paresev) for spacecraft recovery, and although the agency ultimately chose conventional parachutes, the wing found its true vocation in sport flying.

Legacy

The Rogallo wing became the basis of the modern hang glider and influenced ultralight aircraft and stunt kites. Rogallo is widely regarded as the father of hang gliding, and he spent his retirement near Kitty Hawk, where he flew hang gliders into his eighties.
