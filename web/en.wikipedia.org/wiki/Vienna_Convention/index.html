**Vienna Convention** may refer to several international treaties concluded at Vienna, or named for the Austrian capital. The best known is the Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties (1969), which codifies the rules governing treaties between states.

This page is a disambiguation-style overview of the principal instruments sharing the name; on the real Wikipedia, 'Vienna Convention' is a disambiguation page linking to the separate articles.

Principal conventions

  • Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations (1961) — the cornerstone of modern diplomacy, codifying diplomatic immunity
  • Vienna Convention on Consular Relations (1963) — consular functions and privileges
  • Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties (1969) — conclusion, interpretation, validity and termination of treaties
  • Vienna Convention on Road Traffic (1968) — international road traffic and safety rules
  • Vienna Convention for the Protection of the Ozone Layer (1985) — framework convention that led to the Montreal Protocol
  • Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties between States and International Organizations or between International Organizations (1986)

The name also covers older settlements such as the Treaty of Vienna (1815) concluded at the Congress of Vienna, and the Vienna Convention on the Representation of States (1975).
