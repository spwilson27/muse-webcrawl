Philip J. Fry, commonly known as Fry, is a fictional character and the protagonist of the animated television series Futurama. He is voiced by Billy West. Fry is a dim-witted but good-hearted pizza delivery boy from 20th-century New York City who is cryogenically frozen on New Year's Eve 1999 and wakes up on 31 December 2999, exactly one thousand years later.

Character biography

In the 31st century Fry finds work at Planet Express, the interplanetary delivery company owned by his distant descendant Professor Hubert J. Farnsworth, where he works alongside the one-eyed mutant captain Turanga Leela — with whom he has a long-running romantic relationship — and the bending robot Bender, who becomes his best friend and roommate.

A recurring storyline reveals that Fry's freezing was not an accident: because he lacks the delta brainwave (a consequence of being his own grandfather), Fry is uniquely able to resist the Brainspawn, giant brains bent on destroying the universe. The Nibblonians arranged his fall into the cryotube so he would be present in the 31st century to save the universe, making Fry, in their words, "the most important person in the universe".

Personality

Fry is lazy, slovenly and immature, but also loyal, kind and occasionally capable of unexpected emotional depth, most famously in episodes such as "Jurassic Bark", about his fossilised dog Seymour. He is a devotee of 20th-century popular culture and frequently fails to understand the customs of the future. His catchphrases include "Shut up and take my money!", which became a widely used internet meme.

Creation

Fry was created by Matt Groening, co-creator of Futurama with David X. Cohen. Groening named him Philip in honour of voice actor Phil Hartman, who was originally intended for a role in the series before his death in 1998. Fry's distinctive jacket and hairstyle were designed to make him instantly recognisable in silhouette.
