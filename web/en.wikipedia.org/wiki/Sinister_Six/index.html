The Sinister Six are a group of supervillains appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, primarily an enemy of the superhero Spider-Man. The original team was formed by Doctor Octopus, who gathered villains who had each been defeated by Spider-Man individually, reasoning that together they could overwhelm him. The group first appeared in The Amazing Spider-Man Annual #1 (1964), created by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko.

Fictional history

The founding line-up consisted of Doctor Octopus, Electro, Kraven the Hunter, Mysterio, Sandman, and the Vulture. In their first scheme they kidnapped Betty Brant and Aunt May to lure Spider-Man into a series of one-on-one battles; despite their numerical advantage, their refusal to fight as a true team led to their defeat. The group reunited in later storylines with changing membership, including the Hobgoblin replacing the deceased Kraven in the Return of the Sinister Six arc in 1990. A later incarnation, the "Sinister Twelve", was assembled by the Green Goblin.

Members

Rotating members over the decades have included Doctor Octopus, Vulture, Electro, Mysterio, Sandman, Kraven the Hunter, Hobgoblin, Beetle, Shocker, Rhino, Scorpion, Chameleon, Lizard, Hydro-Man, and others. Leadership is most often held by Doctor Octopus, though his arrogance frequently fractures the alliance.

Other media

The Sinister Six have appeared in animated series including Spider-Man: The Animated Series (as the "Insidious Six" for censorship reasons) and The Spectacular Spider-Man. A Sinister Six film was long in development at Sony Pictures; elements of the concept were explored in The Amazing Spider-Man 2 (2014), and several classic members — including Doctor Octopus, Electro, Sandman, Lizard, and the Green Goblin — appeared together in Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021).
