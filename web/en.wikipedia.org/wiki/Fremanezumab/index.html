Fremanezumab

Fremanezumab, sold under the brand name Ajovy, is a medication used to prevent migraine in adults. It is given by subcutaneous injection once monthly or once every three months. Fremanezumab is a humanized monoclonal antibody that binds to calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP) and blocks its function, preventing it from binding to its receptor.

Medical uses

Fremanezumab was shown to be effective for the preventive treatment of migraine in adults in clinical trials, reducing the number of migraine days per month. It was approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration in September 2018, becoming the second anti-CGRP preventive therapy for migraine to reach the market.

Side effects

The most common side effects are injection-site reactions, such as pain, redness, and itching. Constipation and hypersensitivity reactions have also been reported with drugs of this class.
