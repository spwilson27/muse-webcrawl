Islandora is an open-source software framework designed to help institutions manage, preserve and provide access to digital collections. Originally developed at the University of Prince Edward Island, it combines a digital repository with the Drupal content management system and is used by libraries, archives and museums worldwide.

Architecture

Islandora was originally built on the Fedora Commons repository software (later Fedora 3) and the Drupal CMS, with "solution packs" providing ingest, display and metadata tools for specific content types such as books, newspapers, images, audio and video. In 2019 the project began a transition to Islandora 8, built on Drupal 8/9 and a redesigned architecture using Fedora 4/5/6, with content modelled as linked data using RDF and JSON-LD.

Use

Islandora repositories host digitised books, newspapers, oral histories, photographs and research data. It is used by institutions including the University of Prince Edward Island, the University of Toronto, and the Smithsonian Institution. The Islandora Foundation, founded in 2013, coordinates development and community governance.
