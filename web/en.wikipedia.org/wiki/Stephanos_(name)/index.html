Stephanos (name)

Stephanos (Greek: Στέφανος) is a Greek masculine given name meaning "crown" or "garland" (literally "that which surrounds"). It is the original Greek form from which the name Stephen and its many international variants derive.

History

The name was borne in antiquity and spread widely in the Christian world through Saint Stephen (Protomartyr), whose Greek name is Stephanos. In the Byzantine Empire it was a common imperial and aristocratic name. Modern Greek usage retains the form Stephanos, while other languages adapted it: Stephen/Steven (English), Étienne (French), Stefan (German and Slavic languages), Stefano (Italian), Esteban (Spanish) and István (Hungarian).

People

Notable bearers include Stephanos of Byzantium, the 6th-century grammarian and author of the geographical lexicon Ethnica, and numerous Byzantine officials, scholars and saints.
