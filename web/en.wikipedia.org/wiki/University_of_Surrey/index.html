University of Surrey

The University of Surrey is a public research university in Guildford, Surrey, England. It traces its origins to the Battersea Polytechnic Institute founded in 1891, became a university in 1966, and moved to Stag Hill beside Guildford Cathedral.

Academics

Surrey enrolls around 17,000 students. It is known for the 5G/6G Innovation Centre, Surrey Space Centre, hospitality and tourism management, veterinary medicine, and its long-standing professional training year placements.

Campus

The university also runs the Surrey Research Park and the Surrey Sports Park, and incorporates the Guildford School of Acting.
