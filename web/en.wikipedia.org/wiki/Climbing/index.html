Climbing is the activity of ascending (and sometimes descending) a steep topographic object or man-made wall using one's hands, feet and equipment. It spans disciplines: bouldering (short routes without ropes, over crash pads), sport climbing (pre-placed bolts), traditional ('trad') climbing (removable protection), aid climbing, ice and mixed climbing, mountaineering, and competition climbing, which joined the Olympics at Tokyo 2020.

Climbers grade routes with systems such as the French sport grades, the US Yosemite Decimal System and British adjectival grades. Safety rests on belaying, anchors and falling technique; free soloing — climbing without any protection, most famously Alex Honnold's ascent of El Capitan's Freerider (2017, documented in Free Solo) — removes the safety margin entirely. Climbing gyms have turned an Alpine pursuit into a mass urban sport.
