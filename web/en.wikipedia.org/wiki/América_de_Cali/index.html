América de Cali, commonly known as América, is a Colombian professional football club based in Cali. Founded on 13 February 1927, it plays in the Categoría Primera A, the top division of Colombian football, and hosts its home matches at the Estadio Olímpico Pascual Guerrero. The club's traditional colours are red and white, and it is nicknamed Los Diablos Rojos (The Red Devils).

History

América is one of Colombia's oldest and most successful clubs, and was for decades burdened by the so-called "Curse of Garabato" — a supposed hex that kept the club from winning a league title until it finally won its first championship in 1979. Thereafter the club dominated Colombian football in the 1980s, winning five consecutive league titles from 1982 to 1986 under coach Gabriel Ochoa Uribe.

Internationally, América reached the final of the Copa Libertadores four times (1985, 1986, 1987 and 1996) but lost on each occasion. The club won the Copa Merconorte in 1999. In the 1990s the club was placed on the US "Clinton List" (Specially Designated Nationals) owing to alleged links of its owners to the Cali Cartel, which severely restricted its finances; it was removed from the list in 2013. The club was relegated for the first time in 2011 and returned to the top flight in 2016, winning further league titles in 2019 and 2020.

Rivalries

América's main rivalry is with Deportivo Cali, with whom it contests the Clásico Vallecaucano, one of the fiercest derbies in Colombia. It also has major rivalries with Atlético Nacional and Millonarios.

Honours

  • Categoría Primera A titles: 15 (including 1979, 1982–1986, 2019-II, 2020)
  • Copa Merconorte: 1999
  • Copa Libertadores: runners-up 1985, 1986, 1987, 1996
