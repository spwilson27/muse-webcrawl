Interserve


Interserve was a major UK support services and construction group.

Interserve plc was a British construction and support services business based in Reading, England. Once a member of the FTSE 250 Index, it provided facilities management, construction and public services to government departments, hospitals, schools and private clients in the United Kingdom and the Middle East, employing around 68,000 people at its peak.

History

The company's origins lay in the Tilbury Group, a dredging and construction firm founded in the 1880s. Renamed Interserve in 2001, it expanded aggressively into outsourcing, acquiring businesses including Initial Facilities and the facilities arm of Rentokil Initial, and winning large government contracts for probation services, cleaning, catering and building maintenance.

Financial difficulties and administration

From 2017 the group issued a series of profit warnings, burdened by losses on an energy-from-waste contract in Glasgow and rising debts. In March 2019, after shareholders rejected a rescue refinancing, the company entered a pre-pack administration; its operating businesses were sold to its lenders under a debt-for-equity arrangement, allowing services to continue while shareholders lost their investment. Parts of the group were subsequently sold or wound down, including the support services division's sale to Mitie in 2020.

Operations

Interserve worked across construction, equipment services (notably its RMD Kwikform formwork business), facilities management and citizen services such as probation and healthcare.
