Novorossiysk

Novorossiysk (Russian: Новоросси́йск) is a city in Krasnodar Krai, Russia, on the northeastern shore of the Black Sea. It is Russia's largest port and the principal Russian naval base on the Black Sea. The population is roughly 260,000–275,000.

History

The city was founded in 1838 as a Russian naval station and fortress on Tsemes Bay, on the site of the Ottoman fortress of Sujuk-Qale, and named after the Novorossiya (New Russia) region. It grew with the development of the port and a railway connection in 1888, becoming a centre for the cement industry using local marl deposits.

During the Second World War, German forces captured most of the city in 1942; Soviet marines held a small bridgehead at Malaya Zemlya from February 1943 until the city was liberated that September. Leonid Brezhnev's participation in the Malaya Zemlya landings was later heavily promoted in his memoirs. Novorossiysk was awarded the title Hero City in 1973.

Economy and port

The Port of Novorossiysk is the largest seaport in Russia by cargo volume, handling crude oil (via the Sheskharis terminal and the Caspian Pipeline Consortium terminal nearby), grain, containers, metals and cement. The city remains a major cement producer. Novorossiysk hosts a naval base of the Black Sea Fleet, whose importance increased after 2014. The climate is Mediterranean-like, though the city is known for the violent northeastern "bora" wind that can exceed hurricane force in winter.
