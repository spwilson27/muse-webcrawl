List of Argentina international footballers

The Argentina national football team has represented Argentina in international football since 1902. Argentina has won the FIFA World Cup three times (1978, 1986 and 2022) and the Copa América a record 16 times. This list covers the players with the most appearances and goals for the national team; figures are as commonly reported and may vary slightly between sources.

World Cup-winning captains

Daniel Passarella (1978), Diego Maradona (1986) and Lionel Messi (2022) captained Argentina's three World Cup-winning sides. Ubaldo Fillol, Jorge Valdano, Oscar Ruggeri and Javier Mascherano are among the other celebrated figures of the national team's history.
