Dont Look Back

Dont Look Back
Director: D. A. Pennebaker
Release: 1967
Starring: Bob Dylan, Joan Baez, Donovan, Alan Price
Genre: Documentary, cinéma vérité

Dont Look Back (stylised without apostrophe) is a 1967 American documentary film by D. A. Pennebaker chronicling Bob Dylan's 1965 concert tour in England, his last as an acoustic folk performer before going electric.

Content

The film follows Dylan on stage and off—dealing with journalists, fans, and fellow musicians Joan Baez and Donovan. It opens with the famous cue-card sequence for 'Subterranean Homesick Blues', shot in an alley behind London's Savoy Hotel, a precursor of the modern music video.

Legacy

A landmark of direct cinema, the film is preserved in the U.S. National Film Registry (1998) as culturally, historically, and aesthetically significant, and regularly ranks among the greatest documentaries ever made.
