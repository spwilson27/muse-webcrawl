DanceSport

DanceSport is the competitive form of ballroom dancing, governed internationally by the World DanceSport Federation (WDSF). Unlike social ballroom, DanceSport is judged as a sport, with couples competing in standardised styles before adjudicators who score technique, musicality, partnering and choreography.

Styles and competition

Competitions are divided into International Standard (ballroom) and International Latin divisions, each of five dances, as well as formation team events and, increasingly, other styles such as breaking — which, under the DanceSport umbrella, made its Olympic debut at Paris 2024. Competitors progress through graded classes from beginner to championship level, and world championships are held annually.

The term "DanceSport" was promoted from the 1990s to emphasise the athletic character of competitive ballroom and to pursue Olympic recognition.
