Fullmetal Alchemist (Japanese: 鋼の錬金術師, Hepburn: Hagane no Renkinjutsushi) is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Hiromu Arakawa. It was serialised in Square Enix's Monthly Shōnen Gangan magazine from July 2001 to June 2010 and collected in 27 tankōbon volumes. The steampunk-inspired story follows brothers Edward and Alphonse Elric, alchemists who search for the Philosopher's Stone to restore their bodies after a failed attempt to resurrect their mother cost Edward his limbs and Alphonse his entire body.

Plot

In the country of Amestris, alchemy is a widely practised science governed by the Law of Equivalent Exchange. As children, Edward and Alphonse Elric attempted the forbidden art of human transmutation to revive their dead mother. The attempt failed catastrophically: Alphonse's body was destroyed and Edward lost his left leg; Edward then sacrificed his right arm to bind Alphonse's soul to a suit of armour. Fitted with steel prosthetics ("automail"), Edward becomes a State Alchemist — the youngest in history, codenamed "Fullmetal" — and the brothers set out to find the Philosopher's Stone, a legendary amplifier that might restore their bodies. Their quest draws them into a vast conspiracy involving the homunculi, the military government of Amestris, and the true, terrible origin of the Stone.

Characters

The series features a large cast, including the brothers' childhood friend and automail mechanic Winry Rockbell; Colonel Roy Mustang, the "Flame Alchemist", who harbours ambitions to lead the country; Lieutenant Riza Hawkeye; the Xingese prince Ling Yao; and the seven homunculi named after the deadly sins, led by the mysterious Father.

Media

The manga has been adapted twice into anime television series by studio Bones: Fullmetal Alchemist (2003), which diverged into an original ending, and Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood (2009), which faithfully adapts the complete manga. The franchise also includes animated films (Conqueror of Shamballa, 2005; The Sacred Star of Milos, 2011), live-action films (2017 and 2022), light novels, video games and collectible card games.

Reception

Fullmetal Alchemist is one of the best-selling manga series of all time, with more than 80 million copies in circulation worldwide. It won the 49th Shogakukan Manga Award for the shōnen category in 2004 and the Seiun Award for best comic in 2011. Brotherhood is frequently ranked among the greatest anime series ever made, praised for its plotting, characterisation and thematic treatment of war, sacrifice and the ethics of science.
