The University of Tennessee System is the public university system of the U.S. state of Tennessee, governed by a board of trustees and a system president based in Knoxville. The system was formally created in 1968 and today serves more than 50,000 students.

Campuses

The flagship is the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, founded in 1794 as Blount College. Other campuses include UT Chattanooga, UT Martin, the UT Health Science Center in Memphis, and UT Southern in Pulaski. System institutes include the Institute of Agriculture and the Institute for Public Service, and UT co-manages Oak Ridge National Laboratory through UT-Battelle.

Role

As the state's land-grant system, UT operates extension offices in all 95 Tennessee counties, providing agricultural, family, and economic development services statewide.
