Annual growth rate

Annual growth rate (AGR) is the increase in the value of an investment, asset, individual, or population expressed as a percentage measured over one year. It is a key measure in economics, finance and demography, used to describe how quantities such as gross domestic product, revenue or population change over time.

Calculation

The simple annual growth rate for a value that changes from Vbegin to Vend over one year is:

AGR = (Vend − Vbegin) / Vbegin × 100%

Over multiple years, analysts usually prefer the compound annual growth rate (CAGR), which smooths fluctuations by computing the constant yearly rate that would produce the total observed change.

Uses

Governments report GDP growth on an annualised basis; companies report year-on-year revenue growth; demographers express population change per year. Because rates are sensitive to the base value, growth rates are often reported alongside absolute changes.
