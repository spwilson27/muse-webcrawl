2018 Major League Baseball season

2018 MLB season
Regular season: 29 March – 1 October 2018
Postseason: 2 – 28 October 2018
World Series champions: Boston Red Sox
AL MVP: Mookie Betts
NL MVP: Christian Yelich
AL Cy Young: Blake Snell
NL Cy Young: Jacob deGrom

The 2018 Major League Baseball season began on 29 March 2018 and ended on 1 October, with one-game tiebreakers required in both the National League Central and West divisions — the first time two tiebreaker games were needed in the same season. The Boston Red Sox won a franchise-record 108 games and went on to win the World Series.

Regular season

The Red Sox dominated the American League, while the Houston Astros (103 wins) and New York Yankees (100) also topped 100 wins. In the National League, the Milwaukee Brewers won the Central via tiebreaker, and the Los Angeles Dodgers won the West via tiebreaker. Notable individual feats included the Angels' Shohei Ohtani winning AL Rookie of the Year as a two-way player, and the Mets' Jacob deGrom posting a 1.70 ERA.

Postseason

In the League Championship Series, the Red Sox defeated the Astros in five games, and the Dodgers beat the Brewers in seven. In the World Series, Boston defeated Los Angeles in five games; Game 3 lasted 18 innings, the longest game in World Series history. Steve Pearce was named World Series MVP.
