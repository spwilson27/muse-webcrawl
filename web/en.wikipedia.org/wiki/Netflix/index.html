Netflix is an American subscription video-on-demand streaming service. Founded in 1997 as a DVD-by-mail rental business, it launched streaming in 2007, began producing original programming with series such as House of Cards in 2013, and expanded worldwide in 2016.

Netflix became the dominant global subscription streamer, known for full-season releases, data-driven recommendations and a large original film and series catalogue, and later added a cheaper advertising-supported plan and paid sharing rules. It competes with YouTube, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video and others.
