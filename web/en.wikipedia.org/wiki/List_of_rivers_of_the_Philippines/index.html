List of rivers of the Philippines

The Philippines has 412 principal river basins, of which 19 are classified as major. The longest river is the Cagayan River in northern Luzon, followed by the Rio Grande de Mindanao and the Agusan River in Mindanao.

Philippine rivers are heavily used for irrigation, transport and hydroelectric power; the Angat Dam supplies most of Metro Manila's water. Many rivers suffer from severe pollution, notably the Pasig River, long the subject of rehabilitation programmes, and flooding during the typhoon season is a recurring hazard in the Cagayan and Pampanga basins.
