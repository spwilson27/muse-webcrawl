List of closed London Underground stations

The London Underground has closed a number of stations over its history, for reasons including low usage, duplication after line extensions, wartime measures, and replacement by nearby stations. Some "ghost stations" remain visible from passing trains.

Reasons for closure

Many early closures arose when rival companies built stations close together; rationalisation under the Underground Group and London Transport removed little-used stops. wartime closures, such as Down Street's conversion into a protected headquarters, sometimes became permanent. Aldwych, a shuttle branch terminus, finally closed in 1994 because of lift-replacement costs; it is now used for filming and tours.
