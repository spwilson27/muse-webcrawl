The CONCACAF Nations League is an international association football competition contested by the senior men's national teams of the member associations of CONCACAF, the regional governing body of North America, Central America, and the Caribbean. Modelled on the UEFA Nations League, it was created to replace most international friendlies with meaningful competitive matches.

The CONCACAF Nations League trophy.

Format

All 41 CONCACAF member associations are divided into three leagues (A, B, and C) based on strength, with promotion and relegation between them. League A, the top tier, culminates in a Finals tournament between the group winners (later the quarter-final winners), played as single-leg semi-finals, a third-place match, and a final. Results also feed into qualification for the CONCACAF Gold Cup and into the FIFA World Rankings used for World Cup qualifying seedings.

History

The tournament was announced in 2017 and approved in 2018; qualifying for the inaugural edition was held in 2018–19, and the first Finals were played in June 2021 (delayed by the COVID-19 pandemic), with the United States defeating Mexico 3–2 after extra time in Denver. The United States also won the second and third editions, in 2023 and 2024.
