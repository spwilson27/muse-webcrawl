SMU Mustangs

The SMU Mustangs are the athletic teams that represent Southern Methodist University in Dallas, Texas, United States. The Mustangs participate in the NCAA Division I (FBS for football) as members of the Atlantic Coast Conference (ACC), which they joined in 2024. SMU fields teams in 17 varsity sports and its colours are red and blue.

SMU's football team competes in the NCAA Football Bowl Subdivision and plays its home games at Gerald J. Ford Stadium on campus. The basketball teams play at Moody Coliseum.

Football

SMU football claims three national championships (1935, 1981, 1982 in polls) and produced Doak Walker, winner of the 1948 Heisman Trophy. In 1987, the program received the NCAA's "death penalty" for repeated recruiting violations — the cancellation of its season — becoming the only football program ever to receive the sanction, from which it took decades to fully recover. Under head coach Rhett Lashlee, SMU joined the ACC in 2024 and reached the College Football Playoff in its first season in the conference.

Other sports

SMU is historically strong in swimming and diving, men's soccer, and equestrian; the women's equestrian team has won national championships. The men's basketball program has produced NBA players and has made multiple NCAA tournament appearances, and the men's golf program counts Bryson DeChambeau among its alumni.

Mascot

Peruna, a black Shetland pony, has served as SMU's mascot since 1932; the name comes from a popular patent medicine of the era advertised as a cure-all tonic.
