National Model Railroad Association

The National Model Railroad Association (NMRA) is a non-profit organisation for those involved in the hobby of model railroading. Founded in 1935 in the United States, it has around 20,000 members worldwide, organised into regions and divisions.

Standards

The NMRA is best known for setting standards and recommended practices for the hobby, covering track gauges, wheel profiles, couplers and electrical systems. Its standards for Digital Command Control (DCC), developed in the 1990s, enabled interoperable digital control of model trains across manufacturers.

Activities

The association holds an annual national convention, supports the Achievement Program recognising modelling skills, and maintains a library of railroad reference material.
