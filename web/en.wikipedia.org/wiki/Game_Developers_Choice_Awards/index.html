The Game Developers Choice Awards are awards annually presented at the Game Developers Conference (GDC), the largest annual gathering of professional video game developers. Presented since 2001, the awards celebrate the creativity, artistry and technical genius of the finest developers and games created in the previous calendar year. They are considered among the most prestigious honors in the industry because winners are selected by their peers through an open nomination and voting process among game developers.

History

The awards were first presented in 2001 at the Game Developers Conference in San Jose, California, replacing the earlier Spotlight Awards. The ceremony has traditionally been held on the Wednesday evening of GDC week in San Francisco, paired with the Independent Games Festival Awards. Hosts of the ceremony have included developers such as Tim Schafer, Robin Hunicke, and Kim Swift.

Nomination and voting

Any professional game developer may nominate games and vote through the International Choice Awards Network (ICAN), an invitation-based group of developers from around the world. Finalists are selected by an advisory committee, and winners are chosen by the broader developer community, distinguishing them from critic- or consumer-voted awards.

Categories

Regular categories include Game of the Year, Best Debut, Best Design, Best Visual Art, Best Audio, Best Narrative, Best Technology, the Innovation Award, and the Audience Award. Special honors include the Lifetime Achievement Award, the Pioneer Award, and the Ambassador Award, recognizing individuals who have advanced the medium.

Notable Game of the Year winners

Past Game of the Year winners include The Sims (2001), Grand Theft Auto III (2002), Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic (2004), Shadow of the Colossus (2006), Portal (2008), Uncharted 2: Among Thieves (2010), The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim (2012), The Last of Us (2014), The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt (2016), The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild (2018), God of War (2019), Hades (2021), Elden Ring (2023), and Balatro (2025).
