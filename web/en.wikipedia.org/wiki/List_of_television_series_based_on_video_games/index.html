List of television series based on video games

This is a list of television series adapted from video games, including animated series, anime, and live-action dramas. The earliest examples were Saturday-morning cartoons of the 1980s such as Pac-Man (1982). The genre expanded dramatically with the popularity of Pokémon (1997), and entered a prestige era in the 2020s with critically acclaimed adaptations such as Arcane, The Last of Us and Fallout.
