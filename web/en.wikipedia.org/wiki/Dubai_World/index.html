Dubai World

Dubai World (Arabic: دبي العالمية) is an investment company that manages and supervises a portfolio of businesses and projects for the Government of Dubai across a wide range of industry segments and projects, including transport and logistics, drydocks and maritime, urban development, and investment and financial services.

History

Dubai World was established in July 2006 by Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, Ruler of Dubai. It serves as a holding company for several government-owned enterprises, most notably DP World, one of the world's largest port operators, and Nakheel, the property developer behind the Palm Islands and The World archipelago.

Debt crisis

In November 2009, Dubai World requested a standstill on roughly US$26 billion of its debt, an announcement that shook global markets amid the Great Recession. A restructuring was eventually agreed with creditors, and Nakheel was separated from the group in 2011.

Subsidiaries

Notable subsidiaries have included DP World, Drydocks World, Istithmar World, and Limitless. The company's motto is "The sun never sets on Dubai World."
