Richard Taylor (filmmaker)

Sir Richard Leslie Taylor (born 1965) is a New Zealand special effects designer and co-founder of Weta Workshop, the Wellington-based company responsible for the creatures, armour, weapons, and prosthetics of Peter Jackson's The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit film trilogies.

Career

Taylor founded RT Effects with Tania Rodger while at art school, and later established Weta Workshop with Peter Jackson and Jamie Selkirk. He has won multiple Academy Awards, including for Makeup and Visual Effects on The Fellowship of the Ring (2001), The Return of the King (2003), and King Kong (2005), as well as Costume Design for The Return of the King.

Honours

Taylor was appointed a Knight Companion of the New Zealand Order of Merit in 2010 for services to film. Beyond feature films, Weta Workshop under his leadership has produced television series such as Thunderbirds Are Go, exhibitions including Gallipoli: The Scale of Our War at Te Papa, and the children's projects of Pukeko Pictures.
