Lists of earthquakes

Wikipedia maintains many lists of earthquakes, organized by period (by century and by individual year), by country and region, and by characteristics such as largest magnitude and deadliest events. Yearly lists cover earthquakes of magnitude 6 or above, or lower if damaging or deadly.
