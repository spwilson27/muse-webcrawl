The discography of American soul singer Otis Redding (1941–1967) comprises six studio albums released during his lifetime, numerous posthumous albums, and a string of singles for the Volt and Atco labels, subsidiaries of Stax Records and Atlantic Records.

Studio albums

  • Pain in My Heart (1964)
  • The Great Otis Redding Sings Soul Ballads (1965)
  • Otis Blue/Otis Redding Sings Soul (1965) — widely regarded as his masterpiece, including "Respect" and "I've Been Loving You Too Long"
  • The Soul Album (1966)
  • Complete & Unbelievable: The Otis Redding Dictionary of Soul (1966)
  • King & Queen (1967, with Carla Thomas)
  • The Dock of the Bay (1968, posthumous)
  • The Immortal Otis Redding (1968, posthumous)
  • Love Man (1969, posthumous)
  • Tell the Truth (1970, posthumous)

Notable singles

  • "These Arms of Mine" (1962)
  • "Pain in My Heart" (1963)
  • "Mr. Pitiful" (1965)
  • "I've Been Loving You Too Long" (1965)
  • "Respect" (1965)
  • "I Can't Turn You Loose" (1965)
  • "(I Can't Get No) Satisfaction" (1966)
  • "Try a Little Tenderness" (1966)
  • "Tramp" (1967, with Carla Thomas)
  • "(Sittin' On) The Dock of the Bay" (1968) — released after his death in a plane crash in December 1967, it became the first posthumous single to top the Billboard Hot 100.
