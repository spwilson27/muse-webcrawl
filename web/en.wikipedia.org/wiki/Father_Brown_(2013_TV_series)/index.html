Father Brown (2013 TV series)

Genre: Detective drama
Based on: Father Brown stories by G. K. Chesterton
Starring: Mark Williams
Country: United Kingdom
Original network: BBC One
First aired: 14 January 2013

Father Brown is a British detective drama television series loosely based on the Father Brown short stories by G. K. Chesterton, starring Mark Williams as the crime-solving Roman Catholic priest.

Premise

Set in the fictional Cotswolds village of Kembleford during the early 1950s, the series follows Father Brown of St Mary's Church, who uses his understanding of human nature to solve crimes, often to the exasperation of the local police inspector.

Cast

Mark Williams plays Father Brown. Supporting characters have included Mrs McCarthy (Sorcha Cusack), Lady Felicia (Nancy Carroll), Sid Carter (Alex Price), and later Mrs Devine and Brenda Palmer.

Production

The series is produced by BBC Studios and filmed around the Cotswolds, including Blockley, Winchcombe and Sudeley Castle. It has been broadcast internationally and has run for more than ten series.
