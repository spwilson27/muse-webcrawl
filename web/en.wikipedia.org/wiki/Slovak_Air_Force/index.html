Slovak Air Force

The Slovak Air Force (Slovak: Vzdušné sily Ozbrojených síl Slovenskej republiky) is the aviation and air defence branch of the Slovak Armed Forces. It was formed in 1993 following the dissolution of Czechoslovakia, inheriting part of the Czechoslovak Air Force's aircraft and bases.

Headquartered at Zvolen, the air force operates from bases including Sliač, Malacky-Kuchyňa and Prešov. After retiring its Soviet-era MiG-29 fighters — many transferred to Ukraine in 2023 — Slovakia selected the F-16 Block 70 as its new fighter, with deliveries beginning in the mid-2020s. The fleet also includes L-39 trainers, transport aircraft such as the C-27J Spartan, and helicopters including the UH-60 Black Hawk.

Role

As a NATO member since 2004, Slovakia contributes to allied air policing and integrated air defence.
