Museum Mile, New York City

Museum Mile is a stretch of Fifth Avenue on the Upper East Side of Manhattan, New York City, between 82nd and 110th Streets, bordering Central Park. It contains one of the densest concentrations of museums in the world.

Museums

Institutions along or near Museum Mile include the Metropolitan Museum of Art, the Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum, the Cooper Hewitt Smithsonian Design Museum, the Jewish Museum, the Museum of the City of New York, El Museo del Barrio, the Neue Galerie, the Africa Center and the Frick Collection nearby on 70th Street.

Museum Mile Festival

Each June the annual Museum Mile Festival closes part of Fifth Avenue to traffic, with the museums offering free admission for the evening.
