Dan Rather

Dan Rather
Born31 October 1931, Wharton, Texas
OccupationBroadcast journalist (CBS News)
[Image: Rather anchoring election-night coverage]

Dan Rather (born 1931) is an American journalist who anchored the CBS Evening News from 1981 to 2005, succeeding Walter Cronkite. He reported from Dallas on the Kennedy assassination, from Vietnam and the White House (including a famous exchange with President Nixon), before his tenure ended amid the 2004 'Killian documents' controversy over a 60 Minutes II report on President George W. Bush's National Guard service.

After CBS

Rather left CBS in 2006, anchored Dan Rather Reports on HDNet/AXS TV, and in later life built a large social-media following with commentary on journalism and democracy.
