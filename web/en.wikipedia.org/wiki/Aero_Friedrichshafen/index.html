AERO Friedrichshafen

LocationMesse Friedrichshafen, Friedrichshafen, Germany
FrequencyAnnual (spring)
First held1977

AERO Friedrichshafen is an annual trade fair for general aviation held in Friedrichshafen, Germany, on the shore of Lake Constance. Regarded as Europe's leading general aviation exhibition, it brings together manufacturers, suppliers, flight schools, and aviation associations, with several hundred exhibitors and tens of thousands of visitors from dozens of countries.

Scope

The fair covers the full spectrum of general aviation: piston and turboprop aircraft, business jets, helicopters, ultralights and light-sport aircraft, gliders, gyrocopters, electric aircraft, avionics, engines, and maintenance services. Friedrichshafen has a historic association with aviation as the home of the Zeppelin airships, and the adjacent Friedrichshafen Airport (Bodensee Airport) allows many visitors to fly in, with additional aircraft displayed on the airport grounds.

History

The event began in 1977 as a regional show for gliding and model aircraft and grew steadily in scale and international reach. It was held biennially for a period before becoming an annual event in the 2010s. Recent editions have emphasized electric and sustainable aviation, including dedicated showcases for electric flight and alternative propulsion.
