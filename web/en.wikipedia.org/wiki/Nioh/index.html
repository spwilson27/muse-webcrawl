Nioh

DeveloperTeam Ninja
PublisherSony Interactive Entertainment; Koei Tecmo
ReleaseFebruary 2017 (PlayStation 4)
GenreAction role-playing

Nioh (Japanese: 仁王, Niō, "benevolent king") is an action role-playing video game developed by Team Ninja and published in 2017. Set in a fictionalised, dark-fantasy version of Japan in the year 1600, it follows the Irish sailor William Adams as he pursues the alchemist Edward Kelley through a land ravaged by civil war and yōkai.

Gameplay

Nioh is noted for its demanding, stamina-based melee combat, drawing comparisons with the Souls series while adding its own systems: three weapon stances (high, mid, and low), the "Ki Pulse" mechanic for recovering stamina, guardian spirits granting special powers, and extensive loot with randomised attributes. Missions are selected from a map rather than forming a single open world, and players can summon visitors for cooperative play or fight computer-controlled "revenants" of fallen players.

Development and reception

The project had a long history: Koei first announced it in 2004 as a companion to an unfinished Akira Kurosawa script, and development restarted under Team Ninja years later. On release the game received generally positive reviews praising its combat and depth, and sold several million copies. A prequel sequel, Nioh 2, was released in 2020.
