Luxor Las Vegas

Luxor Las Vegas is a hotel and casino on the southern end of the Las Vegas Strip in Paradise, Nevada, owned and operated by MGM Resorts International. It opened on October 15, 1993, with an ancient Egyptian theme, and is named after the city of Luxor (ancient Thebes) in Egypt.

Design

The resort's main tower is a 30-story glass pyramid, among the largest pyramid structures in the world, fronted by a recreation of the Great Sphinx of Giza and an obelisk. Rooms line the pyramid's interior walls and are reached by "inclinators" travelling at an angle. At the pyramid's apex is the Luxor Sky Beam, an intense beam of light said to be visible from great distances. Two additional zigurrat-style towers were added later.

Attractions

The property has hosted long-running exhibitions including Bodies: The Exhibition and Titanic: The Artifact Exhibition, and shows such as the Blue Man Group (2000–2005) and Criss Angel's productions. A renovation in the late 2000s removed much of the original Egyptian interior theming in favour of more contemporary lounges and restaurants, though the exterior pyramid and sphinx remain.
