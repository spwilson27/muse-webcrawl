Use Your Illusion

Use Your Illusion is the name of two studio albums by the American rock band Guns N' Roses, released simultaneously on September 17, 1991: Use Your Illusion I and Use Your Illusion II. Together they mark the band's third and fourth studio albums.

Background

Following the success of Appetite for Destruction (1987) and G N' R Lies (1988), the band recorded a large body of material with producer Mike Clink. The two albums debuted at numbers 2 and 1 respectively on the Billboard 200, and together have sold millions of copies worldwide.

Content

Use Your Illusion I includes "November Rain", "Don't Cry" and a cover of Paul McCartney's "Live and Let Die"; Use Your Illusion II includes "You Could Be Mine", "Civil War", "Estranged" and a cover of Bob Dylan's "Knockin' on Heaven's Door". The albums showed a more ambitious, progressive sound with longer songs and piano-led ballads.

The cover art derives from Raphael's fresco The School of Athens. A combined compilation album, Use Your Illusion, was released in 1998. The band supported the albums with the lengthy Use Your Illusion Tour (1991–1993).
