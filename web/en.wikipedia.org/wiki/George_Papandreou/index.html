George Papandreou

George Papandreou (born 16 June 1952) is a Greek politician who served as Prime Minister of Greece from 2009 to 2011. He is the son of Andreas Papandreou and grandson of Georgios Papandreou, both also prime ministers, making him the third generation of the family to lead the country.

Leader of the Panhellenic Socialist Movement (PASOK) from 2004, he led the party to victory in the 2009 election. His premiership was consumed by the Greek government-debt crisis: after revealing the deficit was far larger than previously reported, his government accepted bailout packages from the EU and IMF with austerity measures.

His 2011 proposal to hold a referendum on the bailout provoked a political crisis and he resigned in November 2011, succeeded by a national unity government under Lucas Papademos. He had earlier served as Minister of Foreign Affairs (1999–2004) and later led the Movement of Democratic Socialists (KIDISO).
