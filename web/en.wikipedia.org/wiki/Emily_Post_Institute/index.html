The Emily Post Institute is an American organisation devoted to etiquette and manners, founded in 1946 by Emily Post (1872–1960), the author of the best-selling 1922 book Etiquette in Society, in Business, in Politics, and at Home. The institute is based in Burlington, Vermont, and remains run by members of the Post family.

Activities

The institute continues to publish updated editions of Emily Post's Etiquette, revised across generations by family members including Elizabeth L. Post, Peggy Post, and later Anna Post, Lizzie Post and Daniel Post Senning. It provides etiquette training and certification for businesses, wedding and business-etiquette advice, syndicated columns, and media commentary on manners in contemporary life — from table settings to email and social-media conduct.

Philosophy

The institute teaches that etiquette is grounded not in rigid rules but in consideration, respect and honesty toward others — principles Emily Post herself emphasised. Her name became so synonymous with good manners in American culture that "Emily Post" is still used generically to mean an authority on etiquette.
