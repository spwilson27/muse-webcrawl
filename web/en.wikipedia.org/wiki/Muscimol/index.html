Muscimol

Muscimol is one of the principal psychoactive compounds found in Amanita muscaria (fly agaric) and related mushrooms. It is a potent, selective agonist of the GABAA receptor and produces sedative-hypnotic, depressant and hallucinogenic effects.

Muscimol is formed in the mushroom by decarboxylation of ibotenic acid, a process promoted by drying or heating. Whereas ibotenic acid is an excitatory NMDA-receptor agonist, muscimol's GABAergic action accounts for the sedative, dream-like intoxication traditionally associated with fly agaric use in Siberian shamanic practice. Muscimol is also used in neuroscience research as a tool to reversibly inactivate brain regions, and its structural analogue gaboxadol (THIP) was investigated as a sleep drug.
