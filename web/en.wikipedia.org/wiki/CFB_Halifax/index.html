CFB Halifax

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Canadian Forces Base Halifax (CFB Halifax) is a Canadian Forces base located in Halifax, Nova Scotia. It is the homeport of the Royal Canadian Navy's Atlantic fleet, known formally as Maritime Forces Atlantic (MARLANT). CFB Halifax is Canada's largest naval base and one of the oldest, with naval operations at Halifax dating back to the founding of the town in 1749.

The base comprises several properties in the Halifax Regional Municipality, including HMC Dockyard on the western shore of Halifax Harbour, Stadacona (the former HMCS Stadacona shore establishment), Naval Radio Section Newport Corner, and the Canadian Forces Ammunition Depot in Bedford. CFB Shearwater, the heliport in Dartmouth, became part of CFB Halifax in the 1990s but retains its identity as 12 Wing Shearwater.

History

The Royal Navy established its North American station at Halifax in the 18th century, and the Halifax Naval Yard was formally established in 1759. Control of the dockyard passed to Canada in 1906, and it became the east coast base of the Naval Service of Canada upon the service's founding in 1910. The base supported the Royal Canadian Navy through both world wars and the Cold War; during the Second World War, Halifax was a principal convoy assembly port for the Battle of the Atlantic.

Today

CFB Halifax is home to the Halifax-class frigates, Kingston-class coastal defence vessels, and support ships of the Atlantic fleet, as well as Fleet Maintenance Facility Cape Scott, the Canadian Forces Naval Engineering School, and numerous lodger units. The base is one of the largest employers in the Halifax region.
