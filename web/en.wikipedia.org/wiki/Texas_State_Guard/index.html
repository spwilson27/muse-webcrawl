The Texas State Guard (TXSG) is the state defense force of Texas, one of the three branches of the Texas Military Forces alongside the Texas Army National Guard and Texas Air National Guard. It is an all-volunteer force organized under the authority of the Governor of Texas and the Adjutant General, and it may not be called into federal service.

Mission

The TXSG's primary mission is emergency management and disaster response within the state: it provides trained personnel to assist state and local authorities during hurricanes, floods, wildfires, and other emergencies, and augments the National Guard when it is deployed. Its members serve in components including Army and Air components, a medical brigade, and maritime elements.

History

State guards were raised when the National Guard was federalized during both World Wars. The modern Texas State Guard descends from the Texas Defense Guard formed in 1941 and was reconstituted under state law in the 1980s as an organized state military force. It has been activated for numerous hurricane responses, including Hurricanes Katrina, Ike, and Harvey, performing shelter operations, search and rescue support, and points-of-distribution staffing.
