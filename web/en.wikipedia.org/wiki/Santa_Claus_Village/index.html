Santa Claus Village

Santa Claus Village
Location: Rovaniemi, Lapland, Finland
Coordinates: On the Arctic Circle, ~8 km north of Rovaniemi
Opened: 1985 (Santa Claus Office)

Santa Claus Village (Finnish: Joulupukin Pajakylä) is an amusement park and tourist attraction in Rovaniemi, Finland, marketed as the official home of Santa Claus. It lies directly on the Arctic Circle, whose line is marked through the village grounds.

History

Rovaniemi's association with Santa began in the 1950s after Eleanor Roosevelt visited the city and a cabin was built for her at the Arctic Circle. Tourism grew around the Santa Claus legend, and in 1985 the Santa Claus Office opened in the village, allowing visitors to meet Santa year-round. Santa Claus Village developed into Lapland's best-known attraction, drawing hundreds of thousands of visitors annually before the COVID-19 pandemic, with numbers recovering strongly afterwards.

Attractions

Attractions include the Santa Claus Main Post Office, which receives hundreds of thousands of letters addressed to Santa each year and sends replies with a special Arctic Circle postmark; Santa's Office; reindeer and husky experiences; shops, restaurants, and accommodation including glass igloos for viewing the northern lights. The underlying claim that Santa lives in Finnish Lapland is linked to the older tradition placing his home at Korvatunturi ("Ear Fell").
