Humberside

Humberside was a non-metropolitan and ceremonial county in Northern England from 1 April 1974 until 1 April 1996. It was composed of land from either side of the Humber Estuary, created from portions of the East Riding of Yorkshire, West Riding of Yorkshire, and the northern part of Lindsey, Lincolnshire.

History

The county was unpopular, particularly north of the Humber, where many residents resented the loss of the historic East Riding identity. The Humber Bridge, opened in 1981, was built partly to bind the two halves together. In 1996 Humberside was abolished and replaced by four unitary authorities: Kingston upon Hull, the East Riding of Yorkshire, North Lincolnshire, and North East Lincolnshire.

Legacy

The name survives in bodies such as Humberside Police and BBC Radio Humberside, and "Humberside" remains a postal county designation in some usage.
