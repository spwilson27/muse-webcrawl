Bamm-Bamm Rubble is a fictional character in the animated television series The Flintstones, produced by Hanna-Barbera. He is the adopted son of Barney and Betty Rubble and is known for his immense, superhuman strength even as an infant, as well as his catchphrase "Bamm-Bamm!", which he shouts while pounding the ground with his club.

Character

Bamm-Bamm was introduced in the fourth-season episode "Little Bamm-Bamm" (1963), in which the Rubbles, having long wished for a child, find the baby abandoned on their doorstep and adopt him after a legal battle. The infant's strength is a running gag: he can lift boulders, shake the house, and hoist his father with one hand. He is the playmate of Pebbles Flintstone, the daughter of Fred and Wilma Flintstone.

Later appearances

In the 1970s series The Pebbles and Bamm-Bamm Show, the characters appear as teenagers; Bamm-Bamm works at the Bedrock radio station and plays in a band. In the television films I Yabba-Dabba Do! (1993) and Hollyrock-a-Bye Baby (1993), the adult Pebbles and Bamm-Bamm marry, move to Hollyrock, and have twins. Bamm-Bamm also appeared in spin-offs, specials, commercials, and the 1994 live-action film The Flintstones, in which he was played by child actors.
