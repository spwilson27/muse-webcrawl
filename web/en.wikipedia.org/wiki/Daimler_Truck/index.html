Daimler Truck

Daimler Truck Holding AG is a German multinational commercial vehicle manufacturer headquartered in Leinfelden-Echterdingen, near Stuttgart. It is one of the world’s largest makers of trucks and buses, employing more than 100,000 people. The company was created in 2021 when Daimler AG (now Mercedes-Benz Group) spun off its truck and bus division, and its shares began trading on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange on 10 December 2021; it joined Germany’s DAX index in 2022.

Brands

Daimler Truck’s products are sold under several regional brands: Mercedes-Benz Trucks and Setra buses in Europe and Latin America; Freightliner, Western Star, and Thomas Built Buses in North America, where its subsidiary Daimler Truck North America is the market leader in heavy-duty trucks; and Mitsubishi Fuso and BharatBenz in Asia. Well-known models include the Mercedes-Benz Actros and Freightliner Cascadia. The group also provides financial services through Daimler Truck Financial Services.

History and strategy

The truck business descended from both Benz and Daimler, which built early commercial vehicles in the 1890s; Gottlieb Daimler is credited with the first motor truck in 1896. After decades within Daimler-Benz and later Daimler AG, the division was separated to give both the car and truck businesses clearer strategic focus and separate stock listings. Since independence the company has pursued a dual strategy of optimising diesel drivetrains while developing battery-electric and hydrogen fuel-cell trucks, including through cellcentric, its fuel-cell joint venture with Volvo Group. It is also developing highly automated (Level 4) trucks through its subsidiary Torc Robotics. Martin Daum served as the first chief executive; Karin Rådström succeeded him in October 2024.
