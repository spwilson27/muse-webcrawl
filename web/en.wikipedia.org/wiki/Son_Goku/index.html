Son Goku

Son Goku (Japanese: 孫悟空, Hepburn: Son Gokū) is a fictional character and the main protagonist of the Dragon Ball manga series created by Akira Toriyama. He is a Saiyan raised on Earth, originally sent as an infant to conquer the planet but instead becoming its greatest defender. Goku made his debut in the first chapter of Dragon Ball, published in Weekly Shōnen Jump in 1984. The character is loosely based on Sun Wukong, the Monkey King of the Chinese novel Journey to the West, combined with elements of Hong Kong martial arts films.

Character and abilities

Goku is cheerful, energetic and fiercely competitive, with an almost single-minded love of training and fighting strong opponents. Though kind-hearted, he can be naive and socially simple. His signature technique is the Kamehameha energy wave, taught by Master Roshi. As a Saiyan he can transform into progressively more powerful forms, beginning with the golden-haired Super Saiyan state first achieved in battle against Frieza on Planet Namek, and later forms including Super Saiyan God, Super Saiyan Blue and Ultra Instinct.

Story

Goku's adventures begin as a young boy with a monkey tail, living alone in the mountains until he meets the teenage inventor Bulma and joins her quest for the seven Dragon Balls. Across Dragon Ball, Dragon Ball Z, Dragon Ball GT and Dragon Ball Super, he defends Earth and the universe against enemies including the Red Ribbon Army, King Piccolo, Vegeta, Frieza, Cell, Majin Buu, Beerus, Zamasu and Jiren.

Reception and legacy

Goku is one of the most recognisable anime and manga characters worldwide and a defining icon of the shōnen genre. His underdog origins and relentless self-improvement shaped countless later protagonists. In 2019 the Japanese government appointed Goku an ambassador for the Tokyo 2020 Olympics, and a giant Goku balloon has appeared in the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade since 2018.
