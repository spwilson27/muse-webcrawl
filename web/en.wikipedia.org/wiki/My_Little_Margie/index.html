My Little Margie

My Little Margie is an American television sitcom starring Gale Storm and Charles Farrell that ran from June 1952 to August 1955, first on CBS and then on NBC. Set in New York City, the series followed the comic misadventures of 21-year-old Margie Albright and her widowed father, Vern.

Premise and cast

Margie Albright (Gale Storm) lives in a fashionable apartment with her father, Vern Albright (former silent-film star Charles Farrell), a vice-president of the investment firm Honeywell and Todd. The plots typically turn on Margie's schemes — to help her father, advance her boyfriend Freddie Wilson's prospects, or meddle in Vern's love life — and on the schemes Vern and his girlfriend Roberta devise in return. Supporting characters include Mrs. Edna Odetts, the Albrights' neighbour; Freddie Wilson, Margie's boyfriend; and George Honeywell, Vern's boss.

Production and broadcast

The series was created as a summer replacement for I Love Lucy in 1952 and proved popular enough to earn a regular slot. It alternated between networks during its run and produced 126 episodes. A radio version with the same stars ran concurrently for part of the television run. After the series ended, Storm went on to star in The Gale Storm Show (1956–1960).

Legacy

My Little Margie was a durable favourite in syndication through reruns for decades, and its theme music and Storm's cheerful performance made it one of the fondly remembered domestic comedies of early American television.
