Group of Eight (Australian universities)

Formation: 1999

Type: Association of universities

Headquarters: Canberra, Australia

Membership: 8 universities

The Group of Eight (Go8) is a coalition of world-leading, research-intensive Australian universities. The group was founded in 1999 and functions as an incorporated company and as an advocacy body for its members' interests, particularly in higher education and research policy.

Members

The eight members are the University of Melbourne, the Australian National University, the University of Sydney, the University of Queensland, the University of Western Australia, the University of Adelaide, Monash University, and the University of New South Wales (UNSW Sydney). All are among Australia's oldest and largest universities, and they consistently feature in the upper ranks of international university league tables.

Profile

Go8 universities educate a large share of Australia's students and carry out a majority of the country's university research. Collectively they enrol over 400,000 students, including a substantial proportion of Australia's international students and research higher-degree students. Their alumni include many Australian prime ministers, Rhodes Scholars, and Nobel laureates.

Policy positions

The Group of Eight advocates for increased and sustainable investment in university research, deregulation of student fees, and policies supporting the international competitiveness of Australian higher education. It regularly publishes research briefs and submissions to government inquiries.
