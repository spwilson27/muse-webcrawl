The Democratic Party of Equatorial Guinea (Spanish: Partido Democrático de Guinea Ecuatorial, PDGE) is the ruling political party in Equatorial Guinea. It was founded in 1987 by President Teodoro Obiang Nguema Mbasogo, who has led the country since 1979.

Dominance

The PDGE has won every election held under the multiparty system introduced in the 1990s, typically with overwhelming majorities, amid widespread criticism from international observers regarding electoral fairness and human rights.
