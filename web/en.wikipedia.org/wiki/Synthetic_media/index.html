Synthetic media

Synthetic media (also called AI-generated media or generative media) is a term for media — text, images, audio and video — that is generated or substantially modified by artificial intelligence, especially by deep learning methods such as generative adversarial networks (GANs), diffusion models and transformer-based large language models. The category includes deepfakes, AI-generated images and artwork, synthetic voices, virtual influencers and algorithmically composed music.

History

Computer-generated imagery has existed since the mid-20th century, and photo manipulation predates digital tools. The field changed qualitatively in the 2010s with the arrival of GANs (2014), which could synthesise photorealistic faces of people who do not exist, and with neural style transfer and voice cloning. The term "deepfake" appeared in 2017 for face-swap videos. Text-to-image diffusion models such as DALL-E, Stable Diffusion and Midjourney, released in the early 2020s, brought high-quality image synthesis to the general public, while large language models made machine-written text ubiquitous.

Applications

  • Entertainment: visual effects, de-ageing of actors, virtual production and dubbing with synthesised voices.
  • Accessibility: text-to-speech voices for people who have lost the ability to speak.
  • Commerce: virtual influencers, personalised advertising and product imagery.
  • Education and research: simulated data, historical recreations and language learning.

Concerns

Synthetic media raises concerns about misinformation and disinformation, since fabricated video or audio of public figures can be difficult to distinguish from authentic recordings; about non-consensual imagery; about fraud using cloned voices; and about the "liar's dividend", in which genuine recordings are dismissed as fake. In response, researchers work on automated detection, and industry groups such as the Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity (C2PA) have developed cryptographic content credentials to attest a media file's origin and edit history. Several jurisdictions have introduced disclosure requirements or specific offences covering deceptive synthetic media, particularly around elections.
