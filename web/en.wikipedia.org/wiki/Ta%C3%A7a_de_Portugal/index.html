Taça de Portugal

The Taça de Portugal (Portuguese Cup) is the principal knockout football competition in Portugal, organized by the Portuguese Football Federation (FPF). Founded in 1938 (as the successor to the earlier Campeonato de Portugal), it is contested by clubs from the top divisions and regional leagues, with the final traditionally played at the Estádio Nacional in Oeiras, near Lisbon.

History

Benfica are the most successful club in the competition's history, with more than two dozen titles, followed by Porto and Sporting CP; the "Big Three" have dominated the cup. The winners qualify for the Supertaça Cândido de Oliveira and, historically, for European competition (formerly the UEFA Cup Winners' Cup, now the UEFA Europa League when not already qualified through the league).
