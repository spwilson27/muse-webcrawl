Big Brother is a British reality game show based on the Dutch format created by John de Mol. Contestants called "housemates" live together in a specially constructed house, isolated from the outside world, and are continuously monitored by cameras and microphones. Housemates are periodically evicted by public vote, with the last remaining housemate winning a cash prize.

History

The series premiered on Channel 4 in 2000 and became a defining reality TV phenomenon, hosted by Davina McCall. It moved to Channel 5 in 2011, hosted by Brian Dowling and then Emma Willis, and was axed in 2018. ITV revived the show on ITV2 in 2023, hosted by AJ Odudu and Will Best. A celebrity version, Celebrity Big Brother, has run alongside the civilian series.

Format

Housemates nominate each other for eviction; the public votes to evict (or, in later series, to save). Weekly tasks test cooperation and determine the shopping budget. The unseen voice of "Big Brother" instructs housemates and summons them to the Diary Room. The show has generated major public controversies and media debates, notably the 2007 Celebrity Big Brother racism row involving Shilpa Shetty, which prompted regulatory findings and diplomatic reaction.
