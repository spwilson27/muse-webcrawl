Padma Shri

Padma Shri
Country: India
Presented by: President of India
Established: 1954
Order of precedence: below Padma Bhushan

The Padma Shri is the fourth-highest civilian award of the Republic of India, after the Bharat Ratna, the Padma Vibhushan and the Padma Bhushan. Instituted on 2 January 1954, it is awarded for distinguished service in a wide range of fields.

Award

The awards are announced annually on Republic Day (26 January) and conferred by the President of India at ceremonial functions at Rashtrapati Bhavan. Recipients receive a certificate and a medallion bearing a lotus motif; the award does not carry a cash prize, and recipients may not use it as a title.

Fields and recipients

Padma Shri awards recognise contributions in art, social work, public affairs, science and engineering, trade and industry, medicine, literature and education, civil service, sports and other fields. Several thousand people have received the award since 1954, including a small number of foreign nationals; nominations are invited publicly and considered by a committee constituted by the Prime Minister.
