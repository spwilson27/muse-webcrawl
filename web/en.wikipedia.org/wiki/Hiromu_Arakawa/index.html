Hiromu Arakawa

Born8 May 1973, Hokkaido, Japan
OccupationManga artist
Notable worksFullmetal Alchemist, Silver Spoon

Hiromu Arakawa (born 1973) is a Japanese manga artist best known for Fullmetal Alchemist (2001–2010), one of the best-selling manga series of all time, and the farming slice-of-life series Silver Spoon.

Career

Raised on a dairy farm in Tokachi, Hokkaido, Arakawa moved to Tokyo in 1999. After assisting Hiroyuki Etō, she published Stray Dog and then launched Fullmetal Alchemist in Monthly Shōnen Gangan.

Style and persona

Arakawa famously depicts herself as a bespectacled cow in author notes, a nod to her farming background.
