List of countries by military expenditure

This is a list of countries by military expenditure, based principally on data from the Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (SIPRI) and the International Institute for Strategic Studies (IISS). World military expenditure reached about US$2.44 trillion in 2023 and rose further to roughly $2.72 trillion in 2024, the steepest increases since the end of the Cold War, driven largely by the war in Ukraine and rising spending in Europe and Asia.

Highest military burden (% of GDP)

Ukraine has carried by far the largest military burden in recent years (around a third of GDP), followed by Israel, Algeria, Saudi Arabia, Russia, Myanmar, Qatar, Poland, the United States and Armenia — most of which exceed the NATO guideline of 2% of GDP (raised to a 5% combined defence and related-infrastructure commitment by alliance members in 2025, to be reached over a decade).
