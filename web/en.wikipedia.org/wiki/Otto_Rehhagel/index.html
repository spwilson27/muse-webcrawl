Otto Rehhagel (born 9 August 1938) is a German former football coach and player, best known for guiding Greece to victory at UEFA Euro 2004, one of the greatest underdog triumphs in international football history. Nicknamed "King Otto", he is among the most successful coaches in Bundesliga history.

Playing career

Rehhagel played as a defender for Rot-Weiss Essen, Hertha BSC, and 1. FC Kaiserslautern, making over 200 appearances in the Bundesliga era before retiring in 1972.

Coaching career

Rehhagel's long association with SV Werder Bremen (1981–1995) brought two Bundesliga titles, two DFB-Pokals, and the 1992 European Cup Winners' Cup. He then coached Bayern Munich for one season, and in 1997 took newly promoted 1. FC Kaiserslautern to the Bundesliga title in 1997–98 — a feat unmatched by any other promoted side.

Greece

As coach of Greece from 2001 to 2010, Rehhagel built a disciplined, defensive side that won Euro 2004 in Portugal, defeating the hosts in both the opening match and the final. He later had a short spell with Hertha BSC and is remembered as a master of man-management and organisation.
