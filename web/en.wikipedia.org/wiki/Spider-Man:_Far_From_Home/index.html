Spider-Man: Far From Home

Spider-Man: Far From Home
Directed by: Jon Watts
Starring: Tom Holland, Samuel L. Jackson, Zendaya, Jake Gyllenhaal
Release date: 2 July 2019 (US)
Box office: $1.13 billion

Spider-Man: Far From Home is a 2019 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics character Spider-Man, co-produced by Columbia Pictures and Marvel Studios and distributed by Sony Pictures. It is the sequel to Spider-Man: Homecoming (2017) and the 23rd film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

Plot

Following the events of Avengers: Endgame, Peter Parker embarks on a school trip to Europe, hoping for a holiday from heroics. Nick Fury recruits him to fight the Elementals alongside Quentin Beck, "Mysterio", a supposed hero from another Earth. Peter discovers Beck is a fraud — a disgruntled former Stark Industries employee using drones and illusions — and defeats him in London, but a mid-credits scene reveals his identity to the world.

Reception

The film received positive reviews for its humour, action, and performances, particularly Gyllenhaal's, and grossed over $1.13 billion worldwide, becoming the first Spider-Man film to pass the billion-dollar mark. A sequel, Spider-Man: No Way Home, was released in 2021.
