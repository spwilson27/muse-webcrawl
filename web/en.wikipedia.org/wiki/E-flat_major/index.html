E-flat major

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The E-flat major scale: E♭ – F – G – A♭ – B♭ – C – D

E-flat major (or the key of E-flat) is a major scale based on E♭, consisting of the pitches E♭, F, G, A♭, B♭, C, and D. Its key signature has three flats: B♭, E♭ and A♭. Its relative minor is C minor, and its parallel minor is E-flat minor, whose key signature has six flats.

The key is well suited to brass and woodwind instruments, many of which are pitched in E♭ or B♭, and it has long been associated with a heroic or solemn character — Beethoven's Eroica Symphony (No. 3) and his Emperor Concerto are both in E-flat, as is Mozart's Horn Concerto No. 4 and the Sinfonia Concertante K. 364. Richard Strauss's Ein Heldenleben also uses the key for its hero theme.

Notable compositions

  • Beethoven — Symphony No. 3 "Eroica"; Piano Concerto No. 5 "Emperor"
  • Mozart — Symphony No. 39; Horn Concerto No. 4
  • Haydn — Trumpet Concerto
  • Chopin — Nocturne in E-flat major, Op. 9 No. 2
  • Liszt — Piano Concerto No. 1
