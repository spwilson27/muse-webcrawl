Timeline of the far future

While the future cannot be predicted with certainty, current understanding from fields such as astronomy, geology and particle physics allows a timeline of the far future for events from the 11th millennium onward.

Universe

The Milky Way–Andromeda collision is expected in ~4.5 billion years. Star formation ceases around 10¹⁴ years from now; thereafter the degenerate era of white dwarfs, neutron stars and black holes gives way—if protons decay—to a dark era, and ultimately to heat death, the leading scenario for the ultimate fate of the universe. Planned human artefacts figure too: time capsules, the Long Now clock, and messages meant to outlast civilisation.
