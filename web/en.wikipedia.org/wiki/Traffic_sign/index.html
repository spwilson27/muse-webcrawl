Traffic sign

A traffic sign or road sign is a sign erected at the side of or above roads to give instructions or provide information to road users. The earliest were milestones and fingerposts; motor traffic brought standardisation — internationally under the Vienna Convention on Road Signs and Signals (1968), used in most of Europe, and the MUTCD in the United States, whose signs differ in using more text and different shapes/colours.

Signs are categorised as regulatory (speed limits, prohibitions), warning (triangles in Europe, yellow diamonds in the US) and informational/guide signs. Pictograms predominate in Vienna-system countries to cross language barriers. Reflective sheeting and standardised fonts maximise legibility; modern practice adds variable-message signs and intelligent transport systems while fighting clutter and vandalism.
