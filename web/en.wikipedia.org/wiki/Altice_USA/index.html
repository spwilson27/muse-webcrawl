Altice USA, Inc. is an American cable television provider and telecommunications company headquartered in Long Island City, New York. It is one of the largest broadband communications and video services providers in the United States, serving customers under the Optimum brand across the New York metropolitan area and in 21 states in the south-central and western U.S.

History

Altice USA was formed when the Dutch-based multinational Altice, founded by Patrick Drahi, entered the U.S. market: it acquired Suddenlink Communications in 2015 for about $9.1 billion and Cablevision Systems in 2016 for about $17.7 billion. Cablevision's Optimum brand became the company's consumer-facing name, and Suddenlink was rebranded as Optimum in 2022.

In January 2018 Altice announced the spin-off of Altice USA as a separate publicly traded company, completed in June 2018; it trades on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker ATUS. The company acquired the news network assets of Cablevision, including News 12 Networks, and later sold the digital news outlet Cheddar. In 2024 Altice USA rebranded its parent identity as Optimum in many consumer contexts while retaining the Altice USA corporate name.

Services

Altice USA provides broadband internet (including fibre-to-the-home under Optimum Fiber), cable and streaming television, home phone, and mobile service, as well as business services and the advertising arm Optimum Media. It competes with Charter Communications, Comcast, Verizon Fios and satellite and streaming providers.
