The National Provincial Championship (NPC), known for sponsorship reasons as the Bunnings NPC, is New Zealand's premier domestic professional rugby union competition. It has been contested in its current single-division format since 2006, when the original National Provincial Championship — founded in 1976 — was restructured.

History of the format

Between 2006 and 2010 the competition was run as the Air New Zealand Cup with 14 professional teams; in 2011 it was reorganised as the ITM Cup with two tiers, a Premiership and a Championship, with promotion and relegation between them. In 2022 the competition returned to a single table of 14 teams, with all teams contesting the same title while odds/evens seeding pools were used in some seasons to structure the draw.

Teams and trophies

The fourteen provincial unions include Auckland, Canterbury, Wellington, Waikato, Otago and North Harbour, among others. Alongside the championship, teams contest the Ranfurly Shield, New Zealand rugby's famous challenge trophy, which is defended at the holder's home matches during the season. The NPC sits below Super Rugby Pacific in the New Zealand professional pathway and is a key development competition for future All Blacks.
