List of kings of Iraq

The King of Iraq (Arabic: ملك العراق, Malik al-ʿIrāq) was the head of state of the Kingdom of Iraq from its establishment in 1921 under the British Mandate until the monarchy was overthrown in the 14 July Revolution of 1958. All three kings belonged to the Hashemite dynasty, whose Iraqi branch was founded by Faisal I, son of Hussein bin Ali, Sharif of Mecca.

Regency

Because Faisal II was four years old at his accession, his maternal uncle Prince ʿAbd al-Ilāh served as regent from 1939 until the king came of age on 2 May 1953. ʿAbd al-Ilāh and Prime Minister Nuri al-Said remained the dominant figures of the kingdom until their deaths in 1958.

Abolition

On 14 July 1958 a military coup led by Abd al-Karim Qasim and Abdul Salam Arif stormed the royal palace in Baghdad. Faisal II, ʿAbd al-Ilāh and several other members of the royal family were killed, and the Republic of Iraq was proclaimed, ending 37 years of Hashemite rule.
