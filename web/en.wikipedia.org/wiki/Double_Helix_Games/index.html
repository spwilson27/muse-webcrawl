Double Helix Games

Double Helix Games was an American video game developer in Irvine, California, formed in 2007 from the merger of Foundation 9 studios The Collective and Shiny Entertainment.

Games

The studio developed Silent Hill: Homecoming, Front Mission Evolved, Killer Instinct (2013) and Strider (2014). Amazon acquired Double Helix in 2014, and it became part of Amazon Games' Orange County studio.

History

Its parent lineages traced to Shiny, founded by David Perry and known for Earthworm Jim and MDK, and The Collective, known for licensed titles.
