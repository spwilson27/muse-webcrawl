Nebula

Nebula
DefinitionInterstellar cloud of gas and dust
Famous examplesOrion Nebula, Eagle Nebula ('Pillars of Creation')
[Image: The Orion Nebula — swirling clouds of glowing gas with newborn stars]

A nebula is a distinct luminescent cloud of interstellar gas and dust. Some are star-forming regions (the Orion Nebula, the Eagle Nebula's 'Pillars of Creation' photographed by Hubble); others are the shells of dying stars (planetary nebulae) or supernova remnants like the Crab.

Observation

Nebulae are key targets of the Webb telescope, whose infrared vision penetrates their dust. Historically 'nebula' also meant galaxies beyond the Milky Way until the 1920s resolved the confusion — a reminder that classification evolves with instruments.
