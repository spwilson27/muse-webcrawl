Flue

A flue is a duct, pipe, or opening in a chimney for conveying exhaust gases from a fireplace, furnace, water heater, boiler, or generator to the outdoors.

Design

Flues are typically made of clay, ceramic, metal, or concrete liners inside a chimney structure, sized to create adequate draft while safely containing hot gases and sparks.

Dampers may regulate airflow, and flue liners protect masonry from corrosive combustion products.

Maintenance

Creosote buildup in wood-burning flues is a fire hazard, so regular inspection and chimney sweeping are recommended.

Carbon monoxide risks arise if flues become blocked or damaged.
