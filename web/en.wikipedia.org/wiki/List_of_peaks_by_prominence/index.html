List of peaks by prominence

Topographic prominence measures the height of a mountain or hill relative to the lowest contour line encircling it but containing no higher summit within it. This is a list of the most prominent mountain peaks in the world. Peaks with a prominence of over about 4,500 metres are listed; such peaks are sometimes called ultras (an ultra-prominent peak has prominence of 1,500 metres or more).
