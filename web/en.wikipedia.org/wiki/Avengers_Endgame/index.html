Avengers: Endgame

Avengers: Endgame is a 2019 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics superhero team the Avengers. Produced by Marvel Studios, it is the direct sequel to Avengers: Infinity War (2018) and the 22nd film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU). Directed by Anthony and Joe Russo and written by Christopher Markus and Stephen McFeely, the film features an ensemble cast including Robert Downey Jr., Chris Evans, Mark Ruffalo, Chris Hemsworth, Scarlett Johansson, Jeremy Renner, Josh Brolin and Brie Larson.

Plot

Five years after Thanos used the Infinity Stones to erase half of all life, the surviving Avengers discover a way to travel in time using the Quantum Realm. They journey to past events to collect the Stones before Thanos, reassemble a gauntlet and reverse the snap, restoring the vanished. A past version of Thanos follows them to the present, leading to a final battle in which Tony Stark wields the Stones to destroy Thanos and his army, dying from the injuries. Natasha Romanoff also sacrifices herself to obtain the Soul Stone.

Release and records

RecordHighest-grossing film of all time (2019–2021)

Endgame was widely anticipated and broke numerous box-office records, becoming the highest-grossing film of all time in July 2019 with about $2.8 billion worldwide, a record it held until Avatar reclaimed the top spot after a 2021 re-release. Critics praised its direction, acting, emotional weight and its function as a culmination of the MCU's first 22 films.
