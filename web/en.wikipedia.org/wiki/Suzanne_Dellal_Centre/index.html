Suzanne Dellal Centre

The Suzanne Dellal Centre for Dance and Theatre (Hebrew: מרכז סוזן דלל) is a cultural centre in the Neve Tzedek neighbourhood of Tel Aviv, Israel, and Israel's premier venue for contemporary dance. It occupies a restored historic school complex, originally the Alliance school and the Yehieli girls' school, dating from the early 20th century.

History

The centre was established in 1989 with support from the Dellal family of London, in memory of their daughter Suzanne, together with the Tel Aviv municipality and Israeli cultural authorities. Its opening helped drive the revival of Neve Tzedek as an arts district.

Activities

The centre is home to the Batsheva Dance Company and hosts the Inbal Dance Theater and many visiting companies. Its four performance halls and open-air courtyard stage festivals such as Tel Aviv Dance and DanceEuropa, residencies, and the biennial International Exposure showcase for Israeli dance.
