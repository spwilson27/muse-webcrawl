Spanish Formosa

Spanish Formosa was a Spanish colony on the northern coast of the island of Taiwan (Formosa) between 1626 and 1642. Established to protect Spanish trade with China and Japan and to counter Dutch influence from Dutch Formosa in the south, it centred on settlements at Keelung and Tamsui.

History

Spanish forces from Manila occupied Keelung in 1626, building Fort San Salvador on Heping Island, and in 1628 founded Santo Domingo near Tamsui. Dominican and Franciscan friars evangelised among the indigenous Basay and other peoples, while the colony served as an entrepôt for Chinese silk and Japanese trade. The colony never prospered: it suffered disease, supply problems, conflict with indigenous communities and the loss of the Japan trade after Japan's expulsion of the Spanish.

Fall

Weakened and under-supported by Manila, the Spanish garrisons were expelled by a Dutch expedition in 1642, giving the Dutch East India Company control of the whole island until its own expulsion by Koxinga in 1662.
