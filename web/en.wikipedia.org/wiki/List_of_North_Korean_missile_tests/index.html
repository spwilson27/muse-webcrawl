List of North Korean missile tests

North Korea has conducted a series of missile tests since the 1980s, developing short-, medium-, intermediate- and intercontinental-range ballistic missiles, as well as submarine-launched ballistic missiles and space launch vehicles. The tests are widely monitored by South Korea, Japan, the United States and the United Nations, and many have been condemned as violations of UN Security Council resolutions.

2017 escalation

2017 saw an intense campaign of testing, including the first flights of the Hwasong-12 intermediate-range missile, two overflights of Japan, and the first tests of the Hwasong-14 and Hwasong-15 intercontinental ballistic missiles, the latter assessed as capable of reaching the entire continental United States.

2019–present

After a moratorium during diplomatic talks in 2018, testing resumed in 2019 with new short-range solid-fuel systems such as the KN-23 and KN-24. In 2022 North Korea conducted a record number of launches, including the resumption of ICBM testing with the Hwasong-17, and in July 2023 it tested the solid-fuel Hwasong-18 ICBM. Launches have continued through 2024 and 2025, including hypersonic glide vehicle claims and the October 2024 test of the Hwasong-19.
