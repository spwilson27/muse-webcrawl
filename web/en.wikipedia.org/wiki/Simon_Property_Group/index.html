Simon Property Group, Inc. is an American real estate investment trust (REIT) that invests in shopping malls, outlet centres and other retail properties. It is the largest owner of shopping malls in the United States and one of the largest retail real estate companies in the world, with interests in more than 200 properties across North America, Europe and Asia.

History

The company traces its origins to 1960, when brothers Melvin and Herbert Simon founded a development firm in Indianapolis that built strip centres and enclosed malls across the Midwest. Simon Property Group was formed as a separate public company in 1993 in one of the largest REIT initial public offerings of its time. Through the 1990s and 2000s it expanded by acquiring rivals and portfolios, including DeBartolo Realty, Chelsea Property Group (operator of Premium Outlets) and Mills Corporation, building a portfolio of regional malls, Premium Outlet centres and international properties. A bid to acquire the bankrupt General Growth Properties in 2010 was rejected, though Simon later bought many assets from other struggling operators.

Operations

Simon's portfolio includes destination malls such as the King of Prussia Mall in Pennsylvania and Sawgrass Mills in Florida, one of the largest outlet and value retail centres in the United States. During the wave of US retail bankruptcies in 2020 the company, often in partnership with Brookfield or Authentic Brands Group, acquired distressed retailers including Forever 21, Brooks Brothers and JCPenney in order to keep anchor tenants and brands operating in its malls. The company is led by David Simon, son of co-founder Melvin Simon, and is headquartered in Indianapolis.
