Naturally aspirated engine

A naturally aspirated engine is an internal combustion engine in which air intake depends solely on atmospheric pressure and does not rely on forced induction through a turbocharger or supercharger.

Characteristics

Such engines typically produce less peak power per displacement than forced-induction equivalents, but offer simpler construction, linear throttle response and often greater reliability. Most aircraft piston engines and many performance cars historically used natural aspiration; modern downsized turbo engines have displaced them in many applications for efficiency and emissions reasons.
