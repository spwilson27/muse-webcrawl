Anthracene

Anthracene
FormulaC14H10
AppearanceColourless crystals, blue fluorescence
Melting point216 °C

Anthracene is a solid polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbon consisting of three fused benzene rings. It is obtained from coal tar and is a colourless crystalline solid that fluoresces blue under ultraviolet light.

Production and uses

Anthracene is recovered from the anthracene oil fraction of coal tar distillation. Its principal use is oxidation to anthraquinone, an important intermediate for dyes. It is also used in scintillation counters for radiation detection.

Reactions

The central ring of anthracene is the most reactive: it readily undergoes Diels–Alder reactions as a diene and photodimerises under UV light. Reaction occurs preferentially at the 9 and 10 positions.
