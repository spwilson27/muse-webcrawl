Overview

The Squad is an informal group of progressive members of the United States House of Representatives, initially composed of four women first elected in the 2018 midterms: Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez of New York, Ilhan Omar of Minnesota, Ayanna Pressley of Massachusetts, and Rashida Tlaib of Michigan. The name, drawn from a social media post by Ocasio-Cortez shortly after the election, was widely adopted by the press and by the members themselves.

History and membership

The group drew national attention in 2019 during a public clash with President Donald Trump, whose tweets attacking the four congresswomen were condemned in a House resolution. The Squad has advocated progressive policies including the Green New Deal, Medicare for All, and abolition or reform of immigration enforcement agencies.

After the 2020 elections, the label was extended to include newly elected allies such as Jamaal Bowman and Cori Bush; Summer Lee and Greg Casar were associated with the group after 2022. Bowman and Bush lost their 2024 primaries. The Squad functions as an informal bloc rather than an official caucus, coordinating votes and messaging on selected issues.
