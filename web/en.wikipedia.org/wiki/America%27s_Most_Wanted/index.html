America's Most Wanted

America's Most Wanted
Genre: Reality / true crime television
Created by: John Walsh
Original run: 1988–2013 (Fox, Lifetime); revived 2021

America's Most Wanted is an American television program that profiles fugitives and missing persons, asking viewers to phone in tips to help law enforcement capture them. It premiered on the Fox network on February 7, 1988, hosted by John Walsh, whose son Adam had been murdered in 1981 and who became a prominent victims' rights advocate.

Format and impact

Episodes combined reenactments, interviews, and a telephone hotline. The program is credited with helping capture more than 1,200 fugitives, including a number of FBI Ten Most Wanted Fugitives, and with locating missing children. It is regarded as a pioneering reality television series and one of the longest-running programs in Fox history.

History

Fox cancelled the show in 2011, after which it moved to Lifetime for a final run ending in 2013. Fox revived the series in March 2021 with journalist Elizabeth Vargas as host; John Walsh and his son Callahan Walsh returned as hosts for a further revival season in 2024.
