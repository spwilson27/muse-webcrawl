Karaiskakis Stadium

The Georgios Karaiskakis Stadium (Greek: Στάδιο Γεώργιος Καραϊσκάκης) is a football stadium in Piraeus, Greece. It is the home ground of Olympiacos F.C., Greece's most successful football club, and is named after Georgios Karaiskakis, a hero of the Greek War of Independence who was mortally wounded in the area.

History

A stadium has stood on the site since 1895, when it was built as a velodrome for the 1896 Summer Olympics. The original football stadium was constructed in the 1960s. The current stadium was built on the same site in 2004, in time for the Athens Olympic football tournament, with a capacity of about 33,000 spectators. It is a UEFA four-star stadium and hosted the 2023 UEFA Super Cup.

Karaiskakis Stadium disaster

On 8 February 1981, 21 Olympiacos supporters died in a crush at Gate 7 of the old stadium after a match against AEK Athens, the worst disaster in Greek football history. A memorial at the present stadium commemorates the victims, and the date is marked annually by the club and its fans.
