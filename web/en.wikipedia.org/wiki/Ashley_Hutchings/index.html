Ashley Hutchings
Born: 26 January 1945, Southgate, London
Genres: Folk rock, English folk
Instruments: Bass guitar, vocals
Associated acts: Fairport Convention, Steeleye Span, The Albion Band

Ashley Stephen Hutchings is an English folk musician, best known as a founder of Fairport Convention, of Steeleye Span, and of a long-running series of bands built around the Albion name. Often styled "the Guv'nor", he is regarded as one of the most influential figures in British folk rock.

Career

Hutchings co-founded Fairport Convention in 1967 and steered the band toward traditional English material on the landmark album Liege & Lief (1969). Leaving soon after, he formed Steeleye Span in 1969, then the Albion Country Band and its successors, including the Albion Band, while also producing numerous themed folk projects and recordings of traditional dance music.

Honours

In 2015 he received the Good Tradition Award at the BBC Radio 2 Folk Awards. His archive of folk recordings and writings is held by library collections in England.
