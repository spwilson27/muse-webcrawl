Stephanie Zimbalist

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Stephanie Zimbalist (born October 8, 1956) is an American actress best known for her role as detective Laura Holt in the NBC television series Remington Steele (1982–1987), opposite Pierce Brosnan. She comes from a family of noted performers: her father was actor Efrem Zimbalist Jr., star of 77 Sunset Strip and The F.B.I., and her grandfather was the Russian-born violin virtuoso Efrem Zimbalist.

Early life

Zimbalist was born in New York City and raised in Los Angeles, California. She studied at the Juilliard School in New York, preparing for a stage career before turning to television and film work in the late 1970s.

Career

Early television appearances included roles in The Love Boat, Family, and the television film The Gathering (1977). Her breakthrough came in 1982 with Remington Steele, in which she played Laura Holt, a private investigator who invents a fictitious male boss — "Remington Steele" — to lend her agency credibility, only to have a charming con man (Brosnan) assume the identity. The series ran for five seasons and made both leads stars.

Zimbalist narrowly missed the female lead in the film RoboCop (1987); she had been cast but was forced to withdraw when Remington Steele was unexpectedly renewed. In later years she appeared in numerous television films and guest roles, and she has worked extensively in theatre, including productions of classic plays, and as a narrator of audiobooks — she won an Audie Award for her narration work.

Personal life

Zimbalist has been private about her personal life. She has written about her family and faith, and she has taught acting. She is the half-sister of Efrem Zimbalist III.
