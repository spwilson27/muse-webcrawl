G major

G major is a major scale based on G, with the pitches G, A, B, C, D, E, and F♯. Its key signature has one sharp. Its relative minor is E minor and its parallel minor is G minor.

The G-major scale ascending and descending.

The G major scale is:

G, A, B, C, D, E, F♯, G

Characteristics

G major is a common key for guitar music, since the open strings of the guitar fit its scale well, and it is frequent in folk, rock, and popular music. Many of Bach's and Mozart's works use the key, and the national anthem of Italy, "Il Canto degli Italiani", is in G major in its most common arrangement.

Notable compositions

  • Bach: Goldberg Variations (BWV 988)
  • Mozart: Eine kleine Nachtmusik (K. 525)
  • Beethoven: Piano Sonata No. 25 (Op. 79)
  • Dvořák: String Quartet No. 13
