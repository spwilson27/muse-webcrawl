Raas lila

Raas lila, also spelled Ras lila, is a devotional dance-drama tradition in Hinduism centred on Krishna and the gopis of Vrindavan. It dramatizes the divine dance described in texts such as the Bhagavata Purana, in which Krishna multiplies himself so that each gopi feels he dances with her alone.

Themes

The performance expresses bhakti, loving devotion, and the longing of the soul for God. Radha often occupies a central place in later traditions, and the dance is read both as a joyful celebration and as a theological image of divine love.

Performance

Raas lila is especially associated with the Braj region and with Manipuri dance traditions, where the Manipuri Raas Leela is a major classical form. Performances combine circular dance, song, costume and narration, and are staged during festivals such as Janmashtami and Holi.

The tradition is closely connected with Krishna devotion in Vrindavan and Mathura.
