Ipswich Town F.C.

Ipswich Town Football Club is a professional football club based in Ipswich, Suffolk, England. Founded in 1878 and turning professional in 1936, the club — nicknamed the Tractor Boys — plays its home games at Portman Road and has a long-standing rivalry with Norwich City, contested in the East Anglian derby.

History

Under Alf Ramsey, Ipswich won the First Division title in 1961–62 in their first season in the top flight; Ramsey went on to manage England to the 1966 World Cup. Under Bobby Robson in the 1970s and early 1980s, the club enjoyed sustained success, winning the FA Cup in 1978 and the UEFA Cup in 1981.

Recent years

After a long spell outside the Premier League following relegation in 2002 and a drop into League One in 2019, Ipswich achieved back-to-back promotions under Kieran McKenna in 2023 and 2024, returning to the Premier League for the 2024–25 season.

Honours

Football League First Division champions (1961–62); FA Cup winners (1978); UEFA Cup winners (1981).
