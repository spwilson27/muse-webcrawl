Lists of stars

Stars are catalogued and listed by many criteria: brightness, distance, size, mass, variability and constellation. This article indexes the principal lists of stars on Wikipedia, including the lists of stars by constellation (one for each of the 88 constellations).

Principal lists

ListSubject
List of brightest starsStars by apparent magnitude; Sirius is brightest
List of nearest starsStars and brown dwarfs closest to the Sun, led by Proxima Centauri
List of largest starsStars by radius, such as UY Scuti and Stephenson 2-18
List of most massive starsStars by solar masses
List of most luminous starsStars by luminosity
List of traditional star namesProper names of individual stars
List of variable starsCatalogued variable stars
List of stars with exoplanetsStars with confirmed planetary systems
List of Arabic star namesStar names of Arabic origin

By constellation

Each constellation article carries a list of its notable stars with Bayer and Flamsteed designations, magnitudes and distances — for example, the list of stars in Orion, which includes Betelgeuse and Rigel, and the list of stars in Ursa Major, which includes the stars of the Plough.
