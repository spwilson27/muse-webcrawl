The Reapers' War (Catalan: Guerra dels Segadors), also known as the Catalan Revolt, was a conflict that lasted from 1640 to 1659, in which the Principality of Catalonia rose against the Spanish Monarchy of Philip IV during the Franco-Spanish War. Catalonia briefly became a republic and then accepted Louis XIII of France as its count, before being reconquered by Spain; the settlement in the Treaty of the Pyrenees (1659) nevertheless transferred the northern Catalan counties of Roussillon and part of Cerdanya to France.

The Corpus de Sang (7 June 1640), when reapers revolting in Barcelona killed the viceroy.

Background

Grievances accumulated under the Count-Duke of Olivares, whose Union of Arms project demanded that Catalonia contribute troops and money to the monarchy's wars, contrary to Catalan constitutional practice. During the war with France from 1635, Castilian and other royal troops were billeted in Catalonia, and their abuses — especially during the harvest season, when bands of itinerant reapers (segadors) came down to the plains — inflamed rural society.

Course of the war

On 7 June 1640, the feast of Corpus Christi, reapers entering Barcelona rose in revolt — the "Corpus de Sang" — and killed the viceroy, the Count of Santa Coloma. Under Pau Claris, president of the Generalitat, Catalonia proclaimed a republic in January 1641 and days later accepted Louis XIII as Count of Barcelona in return for French military support. French-Catalan forces defeated the Spanish at the Battle of Montjuïc (26 January 1641). Plague, war-weariness, and French occupation abuses eroded support, and in 1652 Barcelona surrendered to Don John of Austria; Philip IV pardoned the rebels and confirmed Catalonia's constitutions. The war formally ended with the Treaty of the Pyrenees in 1659, which detached Roussillon and northern Cerdanya from Catalonia and annexed them to France.

Legacy

The revolt is a cornerstone of Catalan historical memory. The song of the reapers, Els Segadors, which recounts the events of 1640, became the national anthem of Catalonia.
