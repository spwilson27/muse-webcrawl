Harmonyland

Harmonyland (ハーモニーランド) is an outdoor theme park in Hiji, Ōita Prefecture, Japan, operated by the Harmonyland Company, a subsidiary of Sanrio. It is the sister park of Sanrio Puroland, the indoor theme park in Tama, Tokyo. The park is themed around Sanrio characters such as Hello Kitty, My Melody, and Cinnamoroll.

Location: Hiji, Ōita, Japan

Opened: 26 April 1991

Owner: Sanrio

Theme: Sanrio characters

Sister park: Sanrio Puroland

History

Harmonyland opened on 26 April 1991, a few months after Sanrio Puroland's opening in December 1990. Set amid the hills of Hiji with views over Beppu Bay, the outdoor park offers attractions that the indoor Puroland could not, including a Ferris wheel, a carousel, boat rides, and open-air stages.

Attractions

Attractions include the Kitty Castle, character parades and live musical shows featuring costumed Sanrio characters, the Harmony Train, a Ferris wheel decorated with Hello Kitty gondolas, and seasonal events such as summer water shows and Christmas illuminations. Visitors can meet characters including Hello Kitty, Dear Daniel, My Melody, Kuromi, Cinnamoroll, Pompompurin, and the Little Twin Stars.

The park draws families from across Kyushu and international tourists visiting the nearby hot-spring resorts of Beppu and Yufuin.
