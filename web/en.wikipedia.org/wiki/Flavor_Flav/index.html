Flavor Flav

Born: William Jonathan Drayton Jr., March 16, 1959, Roosevelt, New York, U.S.
Genres: Hip hop
Occupations: Rapper, hype man, television personality
Years active: 1982–present

William Jonathan Drayton Jr. (born March 16, 1959), known by his stage name Flavor Flav, is an American rapper and television personality. Known for his yells of "Yeah, boyeeeeee!" when performing, he rose to prominence as the hype man and comic foil of the hip hop group Public Enemy, which he co-founded in 1985 with Chuck D. With Public Enemy he was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2013.

Flav is recognisable by the oversized clock he wears around his neck, a signature he adopted in the 1980s, together with flamboyant costumes and a Viking helmet. A classically trained pianist in childhood, he plays multiple instruments and contributed comic songs such as "911 Is a Joke" to Public Enemy albums.

Television career

After years away from the spotlight, Flav became a reality television star in the 2000s, appearing in VH1's The Surreal Life (2004), Strange Love (2005) with Brigitte Nielsen, and his own dating show Flavor of Love (2006–2008), which ran for three seasons. He opened short-lived fried chicken restaurants, including Flav's Fried Chicken in Iowa in 2011.

Later activities

Flav has publicly supported the United States women's water polo team in the 2020s, becoming a prominent sponsor and fan ambassador, including at the 2024 Paris Olympics. His relationship with Chuck D has at times been strained publicly, but the two have continued to perform together as Public Enemy.
