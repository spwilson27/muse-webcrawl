OriginVietnam War (USAF "Wild Weasel" programme, 1965)

Suppression of Enemy Air Defenses (SEAD) consists of air operations to neutralise, destroy or temporarily degrade enemy surface-based air defences — surface-to-air missile (SAM) systems, anti-aircraft artillery and their supporting radars and command networks — so that friendly aircraft can operate with acceptable risk. The closely related Destruction of Enemy Air Defenses (DEAD) emphasises physically destroying those systems. SEAD is typically a prerequisite for gaining air superiority in modern warfare.

History

The mission was born in the Vietnam War, when Soviet-supplied SA-2 missiles began downing US aircraft over North Vietnam. The US Air Force's "Wild Weasel" programme (from 1965) put radar-hunting crews in two-seat F-100s, F-105s and later F-4s, tasked with provoking SAM radars into emitting and then attacking them with bombs and the AGM-45 Shrike anti-radiation missile. The mission proved among the most hazardous of the war. Israel's 1982 Operation Mole Cricket 19, which dismantled Syria's SAM network in the Bekaa Valley, became a textbook SEAD campaign. In the 1991 Gulf War, coalition SEAD — including the opening Apache helicopter strike on Iraqi radar sites — rapidly collapsed Iraq's integrated air defence system; SEAD was likewise prominent in Kosovo (1999), Iraq (2003) and Libya (2011).

Methods

SEAD combines hard kill (anti-radiation missiles such as the AGM-88 HARM that home on radar emissions, precision-guided munitions, and standoff weapons) with soft kill (electronic jamming, chaff, towed and expendable decoys) and deception. Modern SEAD aircraft such as the F-16CJ and the EA-18G Growler electronic-attack jet cue their weapons with sensitive radar-warning and emitter-locating systems; stealth aircraft, cruise missiles, drones and loitering munitions increasingly share the mission. Because shutting down a radar suppresses it even without destroying it, operators may switch off emitters to survive — an outcome that still serves the attacker's purpose while the strike package passes.
