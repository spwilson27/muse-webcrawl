Christmas dinner is a meal traditionally eaten at Christmas, usually by a gathered family. The feast varies greatly by country, reflecting local cuisine and custom, but commonly features a large roasted centrepiece, rich side dishes and special desserts. In many countries the main meal is eaten on Christmas Eve; in the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Australia and New Zealand it is typically served in the early afternoon of Christmas Day.

United Kingdom and Commonwealth

The modern British Christmas dinner centres on roast turkey (historically goose), served with roast potatoes, parsnips, Brussels sprouts, carrots, stuffing, chipolata sausages wrapped in bacon ("pigs in blankets"), bread sauce, cranberry sauce and gravy. Dessert is Christmas pudding — a dense steamed fruit pudding, often flambéed with brandy — accompanied by brandy butter, followed by mince pies. Charles Dickens's A Christmas Carol (1843) helped popularise the festive roast-bird feast.

United States and Canada

American Christmas dinners often resemble Thanksgiving dinner: roast turkey or glazed ham, sometimes roast beef or tamales in the Southwest, with mashed potatoes, green beans, and pies such as pumpkin, pecan or apple. Eggnog is a traditional seasonal drink.

Europe

In much of continental Europe the feast falls on Christmas Eve. German families may serve roast goose or carp with potato salad; Poles observe the twelve-dish, meatless Wigilia supper beginning with barszcz; Italians hold the Feast of the Seven Fishes; Swedes lay out a julbord smörgåsbord of ham, herring and meatballs; the French réveillon may feature oysters, foie gras, roast capon and a bûche de Noël (yule log).

Other regions

In Japan, a popular modern custom is fried chicken and strawberry cream cake. In Brazil, Christmas Eve supper (ceia) features roast turkey or pork with rice dishes, while in the Philippines the Noche Buena follows midnight Mass with ham, queso de bola and bibingka. Australian and New Zealand dinners often adapt British traditions to summer, adding barbecued seafood and pavlova.
