Rebirth Brass Band
Origin: New Orleans, Louisiana, U.S.
Formed: 1983
Founders: Kermit Ruffins, Philip Frazier, Keith Frazier
Genre: Brass band, funk, jazz


Rebirth Brass Band performing in New Orleans.

The Rebirth Brass Band is an American New Orleans brass band founded in 1983 by brothers Philip "Tuba Phil" Frazier and Keith Frazier, along with trumpeter Kermit Ruffins and other classmates from Joseph S. Clark Senior High School in the Tremé neighbourhood.

Style and career

Rebirth revitalized the New Orleans brass band tradition by fusing traditional second-line parade music with funk, soul, hip-hop, and bebop influences. Their energetic style — chanting, heavy tuba bass lines, and contemporary repertoire — influenced a generation of brass bands. Long-running engagements at clubs such as the Maple Leaf Bar made them fixtures of the city's nightlife.

Recognition

The band won the Grammy Award for Best Regional Roots Music Album in 2012 for Rebirth of New Orleans. They have toured internationally, appeared in documentaries and television series including Treme, and remain central ambassadors of New Orleans musical culture.
