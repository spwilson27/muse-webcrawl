Maya Lin

Maya Ying Lin (born October 5, 1959) is an American designer and sculptor. She is known for creating large-scale environmental artworks, architectural works, and memorials, most famously the Vietnam Veterans Memorial in Washington, D.C.

Vietnam Veterans Memorial

In 1981, while an undergraduate at Yale University, Lin won the national design competition for the Vietnam Veterans Memorial. Her design — a polished black granite wall cut into the earth, inscribed with the names of American service members who died in the Vietnam War — was initially controversial but has become one of the most visited and revered memorials in the United States.

Other works

Her later works include the Civil Rights Memorial in Montgomery, Alabama, and the environmental installation series Wave Field. She received the National Medal of Arts in 2009 and the Presidential Medal of Freedom in 2016.
