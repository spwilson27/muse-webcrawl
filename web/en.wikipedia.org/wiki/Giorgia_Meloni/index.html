Giorgia Meloni (born 15 January 1977) is an Italian politician who has served as Prime Minister of Italy since October 2022, the first woman to hold the office. She leads the Brothers of Italy party, which she co-founded in 2012.

Early political career

Meloni joined the youth wing of the Italian Social Movement as a teenager and rose through the post-fascist National Alliance, becoming a deputy in 2006 and, at 31, the youngest minister in post-war Italian history as Minister of Youth in Silvio Berlusconi's government (2008–2011).

Premiership

After Brothers of Italy led the centre-right coalition to victory in the 2022 general election, Meloni formed a government with the League and Forza Italia. Her administration has pursued conservative social policies, restrictive migration measures, and close alignment with NATO and the European Union.
