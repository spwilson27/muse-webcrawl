Pages (word processor)

Pages is a word processor developed by Apple Inc. as part of the iWork productivity suite, alongside Keynote and Numbers. It runs on macOS, iOS and iPadOS, and through iCloud on the web.

History

Pages was first released in January 2005 as part of iWork '05. It combined word processing with page-layout features and a library of Apple-designed templates for letters, reports, posters and newsletters. Successive versions added change tracking, mail merge, collaboration via iCloud, and real-time co-editing. Since 2013 Pages has been free with new Apple devices, and later free for all users of the App Store.

Features

Pages supports two document modes: word-processing documents, where text flows from page to page, and page-layout documents, where text boxes and objects are placed freely. It can open and export Microsoft Word (.docx) files and export to PDF and EPUB formats. Documents use the .pages file format, a package containing the document data and previews.
