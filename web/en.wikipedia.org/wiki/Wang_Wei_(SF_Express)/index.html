Wang Wei (SF Express)

Wang Wei (born 1970) is a Chinese billionaire businessman, founder of SF Express.

Born: 1970, Shanghai, China
Occupation: Founder and chairman of SF Holding

Wang Wei (Chinese: 王卫; born 1970) is a Chinese billionaire entrepreneur best known as the founder of SF Express (Shunfeng), one of China's largest logistics and courier companies. Known for his low public profile, Wang built SF from a small delivery operation in Guangdong into a listed logistics giant with its own cargo airline.

Early life and career

Wang was born in Shanghai and moved to Hong Kong with his family as a child. After working in printing and dyeing factories in Shunde, Guangdong, he noticed the growing demand for cross-border deliveries between Hong Kong and the Pearl River Delta. In 1993 he founded Shunfeng in Shunde with a small team, initially carrying samples and documents between Shunde and Hong Kong.

SF Express

Under Wang's leadership, SF Express expanded nationwide, distinguished itself by directly owning its network rather than franchising, and established SF Airlines, China's largest cargo airline. SF Holding listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange in 2017 through a backdoor listing, briefly making Wang one of China's richest people. He has remained famously media-shy, rarely giving interviews or public speeches.
