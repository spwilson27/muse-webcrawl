Decree of Kutná Hora

The Decree of Kutná Hora, issued by King Wenceslaus IV of Bohemia in 1409, changed the voting balance at Charles University in Prague in favour of the Bohemian nation, prompting an exodus of German masters and students.

Background

At the university, nations voted as blocs. Czech reformers around Jan Hus sought greater influence amid disputes over Wycliffe's ideas.

Provisions

Bohemians received three votes while the three foreign nations together received one.

Consequences

German academics left and helped found the University of Leipzig. The decree strengthened the Hussite movement.
