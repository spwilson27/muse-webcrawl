Standpipe (street)

A standpipe is a free-standing public water tap, typically a vertical pipe with a faucet, that provides drinking water to a community where individual households lack piped water connections.

Use

Standpipes are common in parts of Africa, Asia, the Caribbean and Latin America, where residents collect water in containers for a small fee or free of charge. They are also found historically in cities of Europe and North America, and similar fixtures — fire hydrants — serve firefighting rather than domestic use.

Public health

Well-maintained standpipes greatly improve access to safe water compared with surface sources, but long queues, distance and intermittent supply remain challenges addressed by development programmes.
