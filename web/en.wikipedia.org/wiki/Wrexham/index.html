Wrexham is a city in north-east Wales and the administrative centre of Wrexham County Borough. It is the largest settlement in north Wales and was granted city status in 2022 as part of the Platinum Jubilee civic honours. Historically in Denbighshire, the town grew as a centre for coal mining, brewing and leather industries.

History

Wrexham appears in records from the 12th century and developed around its market and the parish church of St Giles, whose 16th-century tower is counted among the Seven Wonders of Wales. Industrialisation in the 18th and 19th centuries brought coal mines, ironworks and the famous lager brewery — Wrexham claims to be the birthplace of lager brewing in Britain. The 1934 Gresford Colliery disaster, which killed 266 men, remains a defining event in the town's history.

Sport

Wrexham A.F.C., founded in 1864, plays at the Racecourse Ground, the world's oldest international football stadium still in use. The club attracted worldwide attention after its 2020 purchase by actors Ryan Reynolds and Rob McElhenney and the documentary series Welcome to Wrexham, followed by back-to-back promotions in 2023 and 2024.
