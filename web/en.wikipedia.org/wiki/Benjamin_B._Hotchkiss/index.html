Benjamin B. Hotchkiss

Benjamin B. Hotchkiss
Born: 1826, Connecticut
Died: 1885, Paris

Benjamin Berkeley Hotchkiss (1 October 1826 – 14 February 1885) was an American ordnance engineer and founder of the Hotchkiss arms company, known for revolving cannon, machine guns and artillery.

Career

Working in France during the Franco-Prussian War era, Hotchkiss established factories near Paris producing the Hotchkiss revolving cannon and later machine guns widely adopted by armies and navies.

Legacy

His name survived in the Hotchkiss company and weapons such as the Hotchkiss M1909 and M1914 machine guns used in the First World War, and in automotive ventures by the French firm.
