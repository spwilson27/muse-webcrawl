Meredith Corporation was an American media company based in Des Moines, Iowa. Founded by Edwin Thomas Meredith in 1902, it grew from an agricultural publishing business into one of the largest magazine publishers and local television broadcasters in the United States.

History

Meredith began by publishing Successful Farming magazine. Its flagship title, Better Homes and Gardens, was launched in 1922 (originally as Fruit, Garden and Home), and became one of America's best-selling magazines. The company expanded into book publishing, other consumer magazines such as Ladies' Home Journal, Family Circle, Midwest Living and Traditional Home, and into television broadcasting from the 1940s onward.

In 2018 Meredith acquired Time Inc. for about $2.8 billion, gaining titles including Time, People, Sports Illustrated, and Fortune; several news and sports titles were subsequently sold. In 2021, Meredith was broken up: its local media group of television stations was sold to Gray Television, and its national media group was acquired by IAC's Dotdash and renamed Dotdash Meredith.

Publications

At its peak the company's brands reached well over 100 million American consumers, spanning magazines, websites, and syndicated television programmes such as Better and People Now.
