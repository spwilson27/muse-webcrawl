Sublime (band)

Members (classic): Bradley Nowell, Eric Wilson, Bud Gaugh

Sublime was an American rock band from Long Beach, California, formed in 1988. The band's line-up, unchanged during its original career, consisted of Bradley Nowell (vocals and guitar), Eric Wilson (bass), and Bud Gaugh (drums). Sublime blended ska, punk rock, reggae, and hip hop into a distinctive Southern California sound.

History

The band built a following through constant touring and independent releases, including the albums 40oz. to Freedom (1992) and Robbin' the Hood (1994). Their breakthrough came with the self-titled major-label album Sublime (1996), featuring the hit singles "What I Got", "Santeria", and "Wrong Way". Tragically, Nowell died of a heroin overdose on 25 May 1996, two months before the album's release, and the band dissolved. The album went on to sell millions of copies worldwide and remains a staple of American alternative radio.

Later incarnations

In 2009, Wilson and Gaugh performed with singer Rome Ramirez as "Sublime with Rome", a project that recorded new material and toured extensively until 2024. In December 2023, Wilson and Gaugh reunited as Sublime with Nowell's son, Jakob Nowell, on vocals and guitar, beginning a new chapter that continued into 2024 and beyond with festival appearances and touring.

Legacy

Sublime is credited with popularising the ska-punk and reggae-rock fusion that influenced numerous later acts, and songs such as "Santeria" and "What I Got" remain among the most played American alternative songs of the 1990s.
