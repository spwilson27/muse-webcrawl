Futebol Clube do Porto, commonly known as FC Porto, is a Portuguese professional sports club based in Porto, best known for its football team. Founded in 1893, it is one of Portugal's "Big Three" clubs, alongside Benfica and Sporting CP, and has never been relegated from the Primeira Liga.

History

Porto won its first European Cup in 1987 under coach Artur Jorge, and repeated the feat in the UEFA Champions League in 2004 under José Mourinho, having won the UEFA Cup the previous season. Domestically the club has won around thirty league titles and numerous Portuguese Cups, dominating Portuguese football for long stretches from the 1990s onward.

Stadium and identity

The club plays at the Estádio do Dragão, opened in 2003, and wears blue-and-white striped shirts. Its supporters are known as Super Dragões, and Porto is famed for a highly successful scouting and player-trading model.
