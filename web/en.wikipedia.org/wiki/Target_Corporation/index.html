Target Corporation is an American retail corporation headquartered in Minneapolis, Minnesota. It operates a chain of discount department stores and hypermarkets across the United States and is the second-largest discount retailer in the country after Walmart. Founded as the Dayton Dry Goods Company in 1902, it opened the first Target store in Roseville, Minnesota, in 1962, pioneering an upscale-discount format — jokingly pronounced "Tar-zhay" by affectionate shoppers — with design-led own brands and cleaner, brighter stores than its rivals. Products are sold through target.com and its stores.

History

George Dayton founded the company that became the Dayton-Hudson Corporation; the Target chain was its growth engine from the 1960s, and the parent renamed itself Target Corporation in 2000. Target expanded nationally, launched the SuperTarget hypermarket format, and built successful owned brands including Cat & Jack (children's clothing), Threshold and Hearth & Hand (home), and Good & Gather (food). A failed expansion into Canada (2013–2015) cost billions and was abandoned within two years. In 2013 Target suffered one of the largest retail data breaches then recorded, exposing payment-card data of tens of millions of customers.

Operations

Target operates roughly 1,900 stores in the US, most now also serving as fulfilment hubs for same-day services — in-store pickup, Drive Up and same-day delivery through Shipt, which it acquired in 2017. Its loyalty programme, Target Circle, and its small-format urban stores are key parts of its strategy to compete with Walmart and Amazon.
