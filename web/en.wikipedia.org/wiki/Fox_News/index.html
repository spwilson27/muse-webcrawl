Fox News (the Fox News Channel, FNC) is an American conservative cable news channel founded by Rupert Murdoch and launched on 7 October 1996, with Roger Ailes as its founding CEO. It became the most-watched cable news network in the United States in the early 2000s and is a central institution in American conservative politics and media.

History

Murdoch's News Corporation launched Fox News to compete with CNN, which had pioneered 24-hour cable news in 1980, and with the newly launched MSNBC. Ailes, a former Republican media consultant, built the channel around the slogan 'Fair and Balanced' and a mix of news and opinion programming.

Influence and controversies

Fox News' prime-time opinion hosts have been among the most influential voices on the American right. The channel has faced sustained criticism over accuracy and its role in election-related misinformation; in 2023 its parent company settled a defamation suit by Dominion Voting Systems for $787.5 million. Ailes resigned in 2016 amid sexual-harassment allegations and died the following year.

Programming

Daytime news blocks give way to opinion in prime time; well-known programmes have included Fox & Friends, Hannity and The Five. Its digital site and the Fox Business channel are sister operations.
