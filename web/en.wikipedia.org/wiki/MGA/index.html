MGA

An MGA roadster

The MGA is a sports car that was produced by MG from 1955 to 1962. The MGA replaced the MG TF 1500 and represented a complete styling break from the marque's earlier sports cars, featuring a modern, streamlined ponton body based on the 1951 Le Mans car driven by George Phillips.

History

Announced in 1955, the MGA was powered initially by a 1,489 cc BMC B-series inline-four engine producing 68–72 hp, driving the rear wheels through a four-speed manual gearbox. A Twin-Cam version with a high-compression DOHC cylinder head followed in 1958, and the later MGA 1600 and 1600 Mk II models brought larger engines and detail improvements. The car was offered as a roadster and a coupé.

Production and legacy

A total of 101,081 MGAs were built, the great majority exported, chiefly to the United States. In 1962 the model was succeeded by the MGB. The MGA remains a popular classic car, valued for its elegant styling and simple mechanics.

Other meanings

  • MGA may also refer to MGA Entertainment, the American toy company known for Bratz dolls.
  • MGA is the ISO 4217 currency code for the Malagasy ariary.
  • MGA is the IATA code for Augusto C. Sandino International Airport in Managua, Nicaragua.
