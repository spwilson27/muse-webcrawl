Chuncheon is the capital of Gangwon Province in northern South Korea, located in a basin formed by the Soyang and Han rivers about 80 km northeast of Seoul. Surrounded by lakes and mountains, it is a popular leisure destination and host city of festivals such as the Chuncheon Mime Festival.

History and culture

The city grew from an ancient settlement known in the Three Kingdoms period and became a provincial capital under the Joseon dynasty. It was heavily damaged during the Korean War and rebuilt afterwards. Chuncheon is nationally famous as the home of dak-galbi (spicy stir-fried chicken) and makguksu (buckwheat noodles), with a popular Dak-galbi Street. The Soyang Dam, completed in 1973, created Soyang Lake, one of Korea's largest artificial lakes.

Tourism

Nearby attractions include Nami Island (Namiseom), made famous by the drama Winter Sonata, the lakeside city park areas, and the Legoland Korea resort opened on Jungdo island in 2022. Rail links connect Chuncheon to Seoul in about an hour.
