Casa de Contratación

The Casa de Contratación de las Indias ("House of Trade of the Indies") was a Spanish government agency established in Seville in 1503 to control all trade and navigation between Spain and its overseas empire in the Americas and the Philippines.

Functions

The Casa licensed ships, captains and merchants, collected the royal fifth (quinto real) on precious metals, maintained the secret master map known as the Padrón Real, trained and examined navigators through its chief pilot (a post first held by Amerigo Vespucci), and settled commercial disputes. It moved to Cádiz in 1717 and was abolished in 1790 as Spain liberalised colonial trade.
