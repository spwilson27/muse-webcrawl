Lake Kremasta

Lake Kremasta (Greek: Λίμνη Κρεμαστών) is an artificial lake in western Greece, formed by the Kremasta Dam on the Achelous River. Completed in 1965, it is the largest artificial lake in Greece.

Description

The reservoir was created for hydroelectric power generation and flood control. Its construction submerged several villages and the historic monastery area of Episkopi; the bridge of Tatarna crosses one of its arms. The lake lies on the border of the regional units of Aetolia-Acarnania and Evrytania.

Environment

The lake and its surroundings form part of a Natura 2000 protected area and support significant birdlife.
