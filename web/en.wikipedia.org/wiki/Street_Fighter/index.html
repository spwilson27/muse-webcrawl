Street Fighter

Street Fighter is a Japanese media franchise centered on a series of fighting video and arcade games developed and published by Capcom. The first game in the series was released in 1987, followed by six main installments, numerous spin-offs and crossovers, and various appearances in other media. Its best-selling release, Street Fighter II (1991), established many of the conventions of the one-on-one fighting genre.

History

The original Street Fighter, designed by Takashi Nishiyama and Hiroshi Matsumoto, debuted in arcades in 1987. Its sequel, Street Fighter II, released in 1991, became a phenomenon: it popularized competitive, player-vs-player fighting games, introduced selectable characters with unique moves from different countries, and became one of the highest-grossing video games of all time. Successive series include Street Fighter Alpha, Street Fighter III, Street Fighter IV (2008), Street Fighter V (2016), and Street Fighter 6 (2023).

Gameplay

Players compete in one-on-one matches, using punches, kicks, throws, and special moves executed with joystick motions and button combinations, such as Ryu's Hadouken fireball and Shoryuken dragon punch. Recurring characters include Ryu, Ken Masters, Chun-Li, Guile, and the villain M. Bison.

Legacy

The franchise is considered one of the pioneers of the fighting game genre and remains a fixture of esports competition, including the Evolution Championship Series (EVO) and Capcom's own Capcom Cup.
