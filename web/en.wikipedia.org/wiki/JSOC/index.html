Joint Special Operations Command

Joint Special Operations Command (JSOC)

Active: 1980–present

Country: United States

Part of: United States Special Operations Command (USSOCOM)

Headquarters: Fort Bragg (Fort Liberty), North Carolina

Role: Joint special operations, counterterrorism

The Joint Special Operations Command (JSOC) is a component command of the United States Special Operations Command, charged with studying special operations requirements and techniques, ensuring interoperability, and planning and conducting joint special operations worldwide. It is headquartered at Fort Bragg (renamed Fort Liberty in 2023) in North Carolina.

History

JSOC was established in 1980 in the aftermath of Operation Eagle Claw, the failed attempt to rescue American hostages in Iran. The Holloway Commission's findings led to a permanent joint headquarters that could plan and execute complex counterterrorism missions without the inter-service coordination failures that had doomed the rescue. JSOC later became a sub-unified command of USSOCOM when that command was created in 1987.

Component units

JSOC exercises operational control over the military's most specialized units, often called special mission units. These include the Army's 1st Special Forces Operational Detachment–Delta (Delta Force), the Naval Special Warfare Development Group (DEVGRU, formerly SEAL Team Six), the Air Force's 24th Special Tactics Squadron, the Army's 75th Ranger Regiment in support roles, and intelligence elements such as the Intelligence Support Activity. The 160th Special Operations Aviation Regiment provides dedicated helicopter support.

Operations

JSOC units have conducted missions in Grenada, Panama, the Balkans, Afghanistan, and Iraq. During the Iraq and Afghanistan wars, JSOC under General Stanley McChrystal (2003–2008) expanded dramatically, running a rapid cycle of intelligence-driven raids known by the acronym F3EA (find, fix, finish, exploit, analyze). In May 2011, DEVGRU operators under JSOC command carried out Operation Neptune Spear, the raid in Abbottabad, Pakistan, that killed Osama bin Laden. JSOC has also been central to counterterrorism operations in Yemen and Somalia.
