Christy Carlson Romano

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Christy Carlson Romano (born March 20, 1984) is an American actress, podcaster, and singer. She is best known for playing Ren Stevens in the Disney Channel sitcom Even Stevens (2000–2003) and for voicing the title character in the animated series Kim Possible (2002–2007).

Career

Born in Milford, Connecticut, Romano began acting as a child, appearing in Broadway productions including Parade. Her role as the perfectionist older sister Ren Stevens earned her a Young Artist Award, and she starred in the Disney Channel films Cadet Kelly (2002, opposite Hilary Duff) and The Even Stevens Movie (2003). As the voice of Kim Possible she was nominated for an Emmy Award. She later played Belle in the Broadway production of Beauty and the Beast and appeared in independent films.

Later work

Romano graduated from Barnard College and has since become known for her YouTube channel and podcasting, including interviews with former child stars, and for commentary on the entertainment industry. She reprised the role of Kim Possible's voice in cameo form and has appeared in the live-action Kim Possible film (2019) in a supporting role.
