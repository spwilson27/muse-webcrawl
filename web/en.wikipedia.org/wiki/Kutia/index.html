Kutia

Kutia (Ukrainian: кутя; Belarusian: куцця; Polish: kutia) is a sweet grain pudding traditionally served in Ukraine, Belarus, Russia and parts of Poland as the first dish of the twelve-dish Christmas Eve supper (Sviata Vecheria).

Preparation

Kutia is made from wheat berries (sometimes barley or rice) simmered until tender and mixed with honey, ground poppy seeds, walnuts and raisins. The ingredients are symbolic: wheat for eternal life and abundance, honey for sweetness and good fortune, and poppy seeds for prosperity. In some families the head of the household throws a spoonful of kutia at the ceiling — if it sticks, the year will be prosperous.

Occasions

Besides Christmas Eve, kutia is served at funerals and memorial meals (kolyvo in some traditions), linking the dish to remembrance of the dead; a small portion may be left out overnight for the souls of ancestors.
