Paco de Lucía

Paco de Lucía
Born: Francisco Sánchez Gómez, 21 Dec 1947, Algeciras, Spain
Died: 25 Feb 2014, Playa del Carmen, Mexico
Genres: Flamenco, flamenco jazz

Paco de Lucía (1947–2014) was a Spanish flamenco guitarist and composer, widely regarded as one of the greatest guitarists of all time, who revolutionised flamenco with jazz harmony, new techniques, and the cajón.

Career

A child prodigy from Algeciras, he partnered with singer Camarón de la Isla on landmark recordings, achieved crossover fame with Entre dos aguas (1973), and toured in the Guitar Trio with Al Di Meola and John McLaughlin (Friday Night in San Francisco).

Legacy

His virtuoso picado, rasgueado, and harmonic innovations defined nuevo flamenco and influenced guitarists across genres; the Algeciras metro and concert halls bear his name.
