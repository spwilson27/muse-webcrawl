Shondaland is an American television production company founded by television writer and producer Shonda Rhimes. The company is known for producing a string of successful drama series for ABC, beginning with Grey's Anatomy (2005–present), and later for Netflix, following Rhimes's landmark overall deal with the streaming service in 2017.

History

Shondaland was founded in 2005 with the launch of Grey's Anatomy. Its spin-off Private Practice followed in 2007. The political thriller Scandal (2012) and legal thriller How to Get Away with Murder (2014, produced with Rhimes as executive producer) formed ABC's "Thank God It's Thursday" (TGIT) lineup. Other ABC series include Station 19, For the People, and The Catch.

In 2017, Rhimes signed a multi-year overall deal with Netflix, and Shondaland began producing streaming series, beginning with the period drama Bridgerton (2020), which became one of Netflix's most-watched series, followed by Inventing Anna (2022), Queen Charlotte: A Bridgerton Story (2023), and The Residence (2025).
