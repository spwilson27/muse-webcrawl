Guardians of the Galaxy (2008 team)

The Guardians of the Galaxy are a superhero team appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writers Dan Abnett and Andy Lanning, this incarnation of the team debuted in Guardians of the Galaxy vol. 2 #1 in May 2008, spinning out of the Annihilation: Conquest storyline. The team takes its name from the earlier, unrelated 1969 Guardians of the Galaxy created by Arnold Drake and Gene Colan.

Publication history

Following the cosmic events of Annihilation and Annihilation: Conquest, Star-Lord assembled a proactive team to prevent further galactic catastrophes rather than merely respond to them. The 2008 series ran for 25 issues and became the best-known version of the team, serving as the primary inspiration for the Marvel Cinematic Universe films beginning with Guardians of the Galaxy (2014).

Team roster

The founding line-up included Star-Lord (Peter Quill), Rocket Raccoon, Groot, Gamora, Drax the Destroyer, Adam Warlock, Phyla-Vell (as Quasar), and Mantis, with the telepathic dog Cosmo serving as the security chief of their base on Knowhere, the severed head of a Celestial. Later members included Jack Flag, Bug, Major Victory (Vance Astro of the original team) and Moondragon.

Fictional team history

Based on Knowhere, the Guardians intervened in conflicts across the universe, opposing the Universal Church of Truth, the Badoon, and threats arising from the Fault, a tear in space-time created by the War of Kings. The team's story continued through The Thanos Imperative and later relaunches of the title.

In other media

The 2008 team forms the basis of the Marvel Studios film trilogy directed by James Gunn, the animated series, and numerous video games and merchandise lines.
