Decorated Farmhouses of Hälsingland

The Decorated Farmhouses of Hälsingland are a UNESCO World Heritage Site in Sweden consisting of seven large timber farmhouses in Hälsingland noted for their richly painted interiors.

Description

Built mainly in the 19th century by prosperous independent farmers, the houses feature elaborate wall paintings and stencilled decoration by itinerant artists.

Heritage

They were inscribed in 2012. More than a thousand decorated farmhouses survive in the region, reflecting a unique folk tradition.

Architecture

The buildings combine local timber construction with fashionable influences, and often include separate festivity rooms used only on special occasions.
