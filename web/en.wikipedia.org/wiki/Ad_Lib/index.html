Ad Lib

Ad Lib was an American television talk show that aired on a local station in the Dallas–Fort Worth area. The name is unrelated to the Latin phrase ad libitum. The title is also shared by other works: Ad Lib is the name of a 2006 album by Italian rapper and singer-songwriter, and the term "ad lib" more broadly refers to improvisation in speech and performance.

Meaning

In general usage, "ad lib" (from Latin ad libitum, "at one's pleasure") describes something delivered impromptu or improvised, without preparation. In music, an ad lib section allows the performer freedom in timing or interpretation; in acting and broadcasting, ad-libbing means departing from the script.

Usage in performance

Comedians, talk-show hosts, and jazz musicians frequently ad lib to respond to audiences and fellow performers. On scripted television, ad-libbed lines are occasionally retained in final edits when they improve a scene.
