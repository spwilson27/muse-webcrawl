John Bull (composer)

John Bull (1562/63 – 1628) was an English composer, organist and virginalist, one of the most renowned keyboard performers of his age and a leading figure of the English virginalist school alongside William Byrd and Orlando Gibbons.

Career

Bull sang in the Chapel Royal, became organist of Hereford Cathedral and then of the Chapel Royal, and was the first professor of music at Gresham College, London (1597). He contributed to Parthenia (1612/13), the first printed collection of English virginal music.

Exile

Under a cloud of scandal and possibly recusancy suspicions, Bull left England in 1613 for the Spanish Netherlands, spending his last years as organist of Antwerp Cathedral. His output includes fantasias, pavans and galliards, and numerous works preserved in the Fitzwilliam Virginal Book.
