The Bratislava Region (Slovak: Bratislavský kraj) is one of the administrative regions of Slovakia. Its capital is Bratislava, which is also the national capital. It is the smallest of Slovakia's eight regions by area but the most densely populated and economically productive, with GDP per capita well above the national average and among the highest in the European Union by purchasing-power parity.

Geography

The region lies in southwestern Slovakia along the Danube, bordering Austria and Hungary. It comprises the districts of Bratislava I–V, Malacky, Pezinok and Senec. Landscapes range from the Danubian Lowland to the Little Carpathians, whose vineyards around Modra and Pezinok form Slovakia's best-known wine area.

Economy

Bratislava hosts the headquarters of Slovakia's major banks, IT and business-services employers, the Volkswagen Bratislava plant (in Devínska Nová Ves), and the Slovnaft refinery. Cross-border commuting and cooperation with Vienna — about 60 km away — make the region part of an emerging twin-city metropolitan area.
