Debut album

A debut album is the first studio album released by a musical artist or band. Debut albums often introduce the artist's signature sound and may be preceded by singles, demos or EPs.

Commercially successful and critically acclaimed debut albums are frequently cited among the greatest records of all time; examples frequently include Please Please Me by the Beatles, Are You Experienced by the Jimi Hendrix Experience, and Illmatic by Nas.

Sophomore slump

The pressure of following a successful debut with a second album is sometimes called the "sophomore slump".
