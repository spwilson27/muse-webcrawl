List of governors of Maryland

The governor of Maryland is the head of government of the U.S. state of Maryland and commander-in-chief of the Maryland National Guard. Maryland was one of the original Thirteen Colonies; its first state governor under the 1776 constitution was Thomas Johnson. Governors serve four-year terms and, since 1970, may serve no more than two consecutive terms.

Proprietary and colonial governors

Before independence, Maryland was a proprietary colony of the Lords Baltimore from 1632, governed by appointees such as Leonard Calvert, the colony's first governor, interrupted by periods of royal rule (1689–1715).
