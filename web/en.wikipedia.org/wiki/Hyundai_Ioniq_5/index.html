Hyundai Ioniq 5

ManufacturerHyundai
Production2021–present
ClassCompact crossover SUV (electric)
PlatformHyundai E-GMP

The Hyundai Ioniq 5 is a battery electric compact crossover SUV produced by Hyundai since 2021. It is the first model of the Ioniq sub-brand and the first built on the dedicated Electric Global Modular Platform (E-GMP), with styling inspired by the 45 EV concept and by the 1975 Hyundai Pony, featuring sharp lines and pixel-themed lighting.

Overview

The Ioniq 5 offers rear-wheel drive or dual-motor all-wheel drive, with battery packs of roughly 58 kWh and 77.4 kWh (later 84 kWh), giving ranges of around 380–480 km depending on specification. Its 800-volt architecture supports very rapid DC charging, taking the battery from 10 to 80 percent in about 18 minutes, and it provides vehicle-to-load (V2L) power output. The interior uses a flat floor, sliding console and sustainable materials. A high-performance Ioniq 5 N variant followed in 2023.

Reception

The Ioniq 5 was named 2022 World Car of the Year, also winning World Electric Vehicle of the Year and World Car Design of the Year at the New York International Auto Show.
