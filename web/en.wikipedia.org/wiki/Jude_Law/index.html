Jude Law

Jude David Law (born 29 December 1972) is an English actor. He has received numerous accolades, including a BAFTA Award and nominations for two Academy Awards and two Tony Awards.

Early life and breakthrough

Law was born in Lewisham, London, and began acting with the National Youth Music Theatre. After television work, he gained international attention as Dickie Greenleaf in The Talented Mr. Ripley (1999), winning a BAFTA for Best Supporting Actor and earning an Oscar nomination.

Career

Law starred in Enemy at the Gates (2001), A.I. Artificial Intelligence (2001), Road to Perdition (2002), and Cold Mountain (2003), the latter earning him a second Oscar nomination. He played Dr. Watson in Sherlock Holmes (2009) and its sequel, portrayed Albus Dumbledore in the Fantastic Beasts films, and played Yon-Rogg in Captain Marvel (2019). On television, he starred as Pope Pius XIII in The Young Pope (2016) and its sequel The New Pope.

Stage and personal life

On stage, Law has played Hamlet in the West End and on Broadway, as well as Faustus and Henry V. He was formerly married to actress Sadie Frost and has been a prominent figure in British popular culture since the 1990s.
