The South Indian International Movie Awards (SIIMA) are annual awards honouring artistic and technical achievements in the South Indian film industries: Telugu, Tamil, Malayalam, and Kannada cinema. Founded by Vishnu Vardhan Induri of Vibri Media, the first ceremony was held in Dubai in 2012, and subsequent editions have been staged in international venues including Sharjah, Malaysia, Singapore, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi, reflecting the large South Indian diaspora in the Gulf and Southeast Asia.

Format

Winners are chosen by public voting in most categories, with critics' choice awards in some. Awards are presented separately for each of the four language industries across categories such as Best Film, Best Director, Best Actor, and Best Actress, along with lifetime achievement honours.
