Mobico Group plc, formerly the National Express Group, is a British multinational public transport company headquartered in Birmingham, England. It operates bus, coach and contracted school transport services in the United Kingdom, North America, continental Europe, North Africa and the Middle East.

History

The National Express brand was created in 1972 by the state-owned National Bus Company to unite its scheduled coach services in England and Wales. The business was privatised in the 1980s and floated on the London Stock Exchange in 1992 as National Express Group. The group expanded into rail franchises in the UK, airport services, and overseas bus operations, including the Spanish operator ALSA, acquired in 2005, and North American school bus businesses.

After withdrawing from UK rail, the group concentrated on bus and coach operations. In 2023 shareholders approved renaming the parent company Mobico Group, while customer-facing brands such as National Express and ALSA were retained.

Operations

In the UK the group is best known for National Express coaches, the country's largest scheduled long-distance coach network, and for West Midlands bus services. In North America it operates school bus and transit contracts, and in Spain ALSA is one of the largest bus and coach operators. The group also runs services in Morocco, Bahrain and Qatar.
