William Price (physician)

William Price (4 March 1800 – 23 January 1893) was a Welsh physician known for his support of Welsh nationalism, Chartism, and his involvement with the Neo-Druidic religious movement. His cremation of his infant son in 1884 led to the legal recognition of cremation in Britain.

William Price (4 March 1800 – 23 January 1893) was a Welsh physician known for his support for Welsh nationalism, Chartism, and his involvement with the Neo-Druidic religious movement. He has been recognised as one of the most significant figures in the history of cremation in the United Kingdom.

Early life and career

Price was born at Tir-y-Sarn, near Caerphilly, Glamorgan, and trained in medicine in London, qualifying as a surgeon. Returning to Wales, he practised at Pontypridd, where he became known as a skilled and charitable doctor, an early advocate of vegetarianism and preventive medicine, and a campaigner against smoking. He was implicated in the Chartist Newport Rising of 1839 and fled briefly to France.

Druidism

Price styled himself an Archdruid, claimed that Welsh tradition preserved an ancient unbroken religious wisdom, wore distinctive white robes and a fox-skin headdress, and conducted ceremonies at sacred sites including the Rocking Stone at Pontypridd. He built a Druidic temple and school at Llantrisant that was never completed.

The cremation case

In January 1884, following the death of his five-month-old son Iesu Grist ("Jesus Christ"), Price attempted to cremate the body on a hillside at Llantrisant. Villagers intervened and Price was arrested and tried at Cardiff assizes for the supposed illegality of cremation. He successfully argued that although the law did not state cremation was legal, it did not state that it was illegal either, and he was acquitted. The case, R v Price (1884), set the precedent that led, through the Cremation Society's work, to the Cremation Act 1902.

Death

Price died at Llantrisant on 23 January 1893 and was himself cremated on the same hillside, before a crowd estimated at 20,000. A statue of him stands in Llantrisant's Bull Ring.
