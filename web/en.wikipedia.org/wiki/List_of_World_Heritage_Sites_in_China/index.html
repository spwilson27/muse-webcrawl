List of World Heritage Sites in China

China ratified the UNESCO World Heritage Convention in 1985, and its first sites were inscribed in 1987. With 59 properties on the World Heritage List (as of 2023), China is tied with Italy among the countries with the most sites, spanning cultural landmarks such as the Great Wall and the Forbidden City and natural areas such as the Sichuan Giant Panda Sanctuaries.
