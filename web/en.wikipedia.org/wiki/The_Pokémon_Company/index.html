The Pokémon Company (株式会社ポケモン, Kabushiki gaisha Pokémon) is a Japanese company responsible for brand management, production, publishing, and licensing of the Pokémon franchise, which consists of video games, a trading card game, an animated television series, films, manga, home entertainment, and merchandise. It was established in 1998 as a joint venture among Nintendo, Game Freak, and Creatures Inc., the three companies that hold the Pokémon copyright and trademarks.

History

The company was founded in April 1998 as Pokémon Center Co., Ltd. to operate Pokémon Center retail stores in Japan; it took its present name in October 2000 when its remit expanded to managing the whole franchise outside video game development (which remains with Game Freak and Nintendo). Subsidiaries include The Pokémon Company International (based in Bellevue, Washington, established 2001 as Pokémon USA) which manages the brand in the Americas, Europe, and Oceania, and Pokémon Korea.

Operations

The Pokémon Company publishes the Pokémon Trading Card Game outside Asia (printing tens of billions of cards), licenses the anime produced by OLM, operates Pokémon Center stores and the Pokémon GO partnership with Niantic, and oversees the Pokémon World Championships. Pokémon is the highest-grossing media franchise of all time, with lifetime revenues well in excess of US$100 billion, the bulk from licensed merchandise.
