Exposition Universelle (1889)

The Exposition Universelle of 1889 was a world's fair held in Paris from 5 May to 31 October 1889, celebrating the centenary of the French Revolution. Its entrance arch, the newly completed Eiffel Tower, became its enduring symbol.

Exposition Universelle (1889)
Location: Paris, France
Dates: 5 May – 31 Oct 1889
Visitors: ~32 million

Overview

The fair occupied the Champ de Mars, Trocadéro, and Esplanade des Invalides. The vast Galerie des Machines was the largest vaulted building of its time. Exhibits included Thomas Edison's phonograph, Buffalo Bill's Wild West show, Debussy's encounter with Javanese gamelan, and colonial villages that are now criticised as human zoos.

Legacy

The Eiffel Tower, intended as temporary, was retained for radio transmission and became Paris's icon.
