Formula D, officially Formula DRIFT, is an American professional drifting championship series, founded in 2003 with its first season in 2004. It is generally regarded as the premier drifting series in North America and helped establish drifting — a motorsport in which drivers intentionally oversteer to slide a car sideways through corners — as a professional discipline outside Japan.

Competition format

Events are judged rather than timed. After solo qualifying runs, the top drivers compete in head-to-head "tandem" battles: a lead driver sets the line while the chase driver attempts to follow closely while maintaining angle and style. Judges score line, angle, speed, and overall style, and ties may be settled by "One More Time" re-runs.

History and champions

Early stars included Rhys Millen, Samuel Hübinette (the first champion, in 2004), and Ken Gushi. Chris Forsberg became the series' most successful driver of its first decades with three championships, while drivers such as Vaughn Gittin Jr., Daigo Saito, Fredric Aasbø, and James Deane — who won three consecutive titles from 2017 to 2019 after arriving from Europe — also became prominent. The series runs on a mix of race tracks and temporary street-style courses, including the famous "House of Drift" at Irwindale Speedway in California.
