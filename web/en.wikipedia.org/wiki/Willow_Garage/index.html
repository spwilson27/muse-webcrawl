Willow Garage was a robotics research laboratory and technology incubator in Menlo Park, California, founded in 2006 by Scott Hassan. Despite its short life, it had an outsized influence on modern robotics through the open-source Robot Operating System (ROS) and the PR2 personal robot.

History

Willow Garage assembled a team of roboticists to pursue its vision of capable, affordable personal robots, while deliberately releasing its software as open source. Its flagship hardware, the PR2, was a mobile two-armed research platform given free to research groups around the world to seed a developer community.

Projects and spin-offs

Key outputs included ROS, which became a de facto standard in robotics research; the OpenCV computer-vision library, which Willow Garage maintained; and the TurtleBot low-cost platform. The company spun off several ventures, including Suitable Technologies and the Open Source Robotics Foundation, which took over stewardship of ROS.

Closure

Hassan wound down Willow Garage in 2013–2014 to focus on Suitable Technologies, but its staff and projects seeded much of the subsequent robotics industry, and ROS remains ubiquitous in research and industry.
