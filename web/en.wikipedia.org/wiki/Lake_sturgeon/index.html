Lake sturgeon

The lake sturgeon (Acipenser fulvescens), also known as the rock sturgeon, is a North American temperate freshwater fish, one of about 25 species of sturgeon. It is found in the Mississippi River, Hudson Bay, and Great Lakes basins.

Description

Lake sturgeon are large, long-lived fish that can exceed 2 metres in length and live well over 100 years. Like other sturgeons, they lack scales, having instead rows of bony scutes, and they feed on bottom-dwelling invertebrates and small fish.

Conservation

Populations collapsed in the late 19th and early 20th centuries from overfishing for caviar and meat, and from habitat loss. The species is now protected or listed as threatened across much of its range, and stocking programmes aim to restore it.
