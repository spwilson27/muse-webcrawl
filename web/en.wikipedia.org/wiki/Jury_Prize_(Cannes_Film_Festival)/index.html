Jury Prize (Cannes Film Festival)

The Jury Prize (Prix du Jury) is an award presented at the Cannes Film Festival by the festival jury to a film in the official competition. It is considered one of the festival's major prizes, ranking after the Palme d'Or and the Grand Prix.

History

The prize has been awarded, in various forms, since the early years of the festival and recognizes a film singled out by the jury for particular distinction. In some years it has been shared between two films.

Notable winners

Winners over the decades have included films by internationally acclaimed directors, and the award often signals strong critical reception and subsequent distribution success.
