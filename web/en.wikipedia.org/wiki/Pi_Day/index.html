Pi Day

Pi Day is an annual celebration of the mathematical constant π (pi), held on March 14 (3/14 in the month/day date format), since 3, 1, and 4 are the first three significant figures of π.

History

The earliest known large-scale Pi Day celebration was held in 1988 at the Exploratorium in San Francisco, organised by physicist Larry Shaw, where staff and visitors marched around a circular space and ate fruit pies. In 2009 the United States House of Representatives passed a resolution recognising March 14 as National Pi Day. In November 2019 UNESCO designated March 14 the International Day of Mathematics.

Observance

Pi Day is observed in many ways, including eating pie, throwing pies, memorising digits of π, and holding discussions about mathematics. March 14 was also the birthday of Albert Einstein (1879) and the date of the death of Stephen Hawking (2018). A special moment occurred on March 14, 2015 ("Super Pi Day"), when the date and time read 3/14/15 9:26:53, reflecting more digits of π.
