Quicksilver (Pietro Django Maximoff) is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Stan Lee and artist Jack Kirby, he first appeared in The X-Men #4 (March 1964).

Pietro is the twin brother of Wanda Maximoff, the Scarlet Witch. The twins were originally presented as mutants, the children of Magneto; later retcons redefined them as the products of experiments by the High Evolutionary who were given to believe they were mutants. Quicksilver possesses superhuman speed, able to run at velocities far exceeding the speed of sound, along with correspondingly accelerated thought processes — a trait often portrayed as the source of his impatience and arrogance.

Fictional character biography

Pietro and Wanda were raised by Romani foster parents, Django and Marya Maximoff, in Transia. As teenagers, after their powers manifested, they were recruited by Magneto into his Brotherhood of Evil Mutants, fighting the X-Men. Disillusioned with Magneto, the twins left and joined the Avengers in Avengers #16 (1965) as part of "Cap's Kooky Quartet" alongside Captain America and Hawkeye.

Quicksilver later married Crystal of the Inhumans, with whom he had a daughter, Luna. His storylines have included losing his powers on M-Day, stealing the Terrigen Mists to restore them, and serving on teams including X-Factor and the Avengers Unity Squad.

In other media

Quicksilver has appeared in two separate live-action film franchises due to shared character rights: Evan Peters portrayed a version named Peter Maximoff in 20th Century Fox's X-Men films, while Aaron Taylor-Johnson played Pietro Maximoff in the Marvel Cinematic Universe film Avengers: Age of Ultron (2015). Peters later reprised a version of the role in the Disney+ series WandaVision (2021).
