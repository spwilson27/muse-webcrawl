Global studies

Global studies (GS) is an interdisciplinary academic field that examines global processes and systems, such as globalization, transnational flows of people, goods, capital and ideas, and worldwide problems including climate change, pandemics, migration and inequality. It emerged in the late 20th century as universities created degree programmes distinct from international relations, which focuses on states, by placing greater emphasis on culture, civil society and non-state actors.

Scope and methods

Global studies draws on political science, economics, sociology, anthropology, history, geography and cultural studies. Common themes include global governance, development, human rights, global health and the environment. Scholars in the field often seek a "global" perspective that challenges nation-state and Eurocentric framings.

Programmes

Undergraduate and postgraduate global studies programmes exist at universities worldwide, including the University of California system, Leipzig University (with its Global and European Studies Institute) and many institutions in Asia and Australia. The Global Studies Consortium has coordinated graduate education in the field since 2007.
