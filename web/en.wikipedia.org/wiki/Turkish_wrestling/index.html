Turkish wrestling

Turkish oil wrestling
Turkish: Yağlı güreş
Famous festival: Kırkpınar, Edirne
Title: Başpehlivan (chief wrestler)

Turkish wrestling is best known internationally in its traditional form of oil wrestling (Turkish: yağlı güreş), in which wrestlers (pehlivan) douse themselves in olive oil and compete wearing only thick leather breeches called kıspet. Wrestling is regarded as Turkey's national and ancestral sport.

Kırkpınar tournament

The Kırkpınar oil-wrestling festival, held annually near Edirne since the 14th century, is considered the oldest continuously running sports competition in the world; it traces its origin to 1362. The winner of the top division earns the title of başpehlivan and holds the golden belt for a year; three consecutive titles confer permanent ownership. UNESCO inscribed Kırkpınar on its list of Intangible Cultural Heritage in 2010.

Other styles

Turkey also has strong traditions in freestyle and Greco-Roman wrestling, in which Turkish athletes have won numerous Olympic titles, and in regional folk styles such as aba güreşi and karakucak.
