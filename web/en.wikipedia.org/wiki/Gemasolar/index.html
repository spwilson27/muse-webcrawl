Gemasolar

Gemasolar is a concentrated solar power plant near Fuentes de Andalucía in the province of Seville, Spain. Owned by Torresol Energy, it was the first commercial plant to combine a central tower receiver with molten salt heat storage technology.

Design

An array of 2,650 heliostats spread over about 185 hectares focuses sunlight onto a receiver at the top of a 140-metre tower. Molten salts heated to over 500 °C store energy for up to 15 hours, allowing the plant to generate electricity around the clock, including through the night — a first for solar power.

Operation

The plant entered commercial operation in May 2011 and supplies roughly 110 GWh per year, enough for about 25,000 homes, while avoiding some 30,000 tonnes of CO2 emissions annually.
