Isfahan Province

Country: Iran

Capital: Isfahan

Population (2016): 5,120,850

Area: 107,029 km²

Isfahan Province is one of the 31 provinces of Iran, located in the center of the country. Its capital is the historic city of Isfahan, a major center of Persian art and architecture, especially from the Safavid era. The province is a significant industrial, agricultural, and tourist region.

Geography

The province lies on the central Iranian plateau, bordered by the Zagros Mountains in the west and arid deserts to the east. The Zayanderud River flows through the city of Isfahan, sustaining oasis agriculture before dissipating in the Gavkhouni wetland.

Economy and culture

Isfahan Province hosts steel production at Mobarakeh, textile and petrochemical industries, and renowned handicrafts including Persian carpets, miniature painting, and metalwork. Major attractions include Naqsh-e Jahan Square, the Shah Mosque, and the historic bridges over the Zayanderud, such as Si-o-se-pol and Khaju Bridge.
