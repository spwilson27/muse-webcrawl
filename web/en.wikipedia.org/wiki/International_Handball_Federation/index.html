International Handball Federation

IHF
Founded: 11 July 1946, Copenhagen
Headquarters: Basel, Switzerland
Type: Sports federation
Membership: 200+ national federations

The International Handball Federation (IHF) is the administrative and controlling body for handball and beach handball worldwide. Founded in Copenhagen in 1946 by representatives of eight nations, it now has more than 200 member federations across six continental confederations.

Competitions

The IHF organises the World Men's and Women's Handball Championships, held every two years, junior and youth world championships, and the beach handball world championships. Handball has been an Olympic sport for men since 1972 and for women since 1976, with the IHF working alongside the International Olympic Committee on the Olympic tournaments.

History

The federation's predecessor, the International Amateur Handball Federation, was founded in 1928 and oversaw field handball's Olympic appearance in 1936. The modern indoor game developed mainly in Scandinavia and Germany, and the IHF held the first men's world championship in 1954 and the first women's in 1957.
