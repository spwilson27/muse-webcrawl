Cassandra Cain is a superheroine appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, commonly in association with the superhero Batman. Created by Kelley Puckett and Damion Scott, Cassandra first appeared in Batman #567 (July 1999). The daughter of assassins David Cain and Lady Shiva, Cassandra was deprived of speech and human contact during her childhood as conditioning to become the world's greatest assassin. Consequently, she grew up to become an expert martial artist, developing an incredible ability to interpret body language as a substitute for spoken communication, while simultaneously remaining mute, developing limited social skills, and being illiterate.

Cassandra was the fourth character to assume the role of Batgirl, following the No Man's Land storyline of 1999, and she went on to star in the first ongoing Batgirl comic book series (2000–2006). She later adopted the names Black Bat and Orphan; the latter identity was introduced in the DC Rebirth relaunch after she was reintroduced into mainstream continuity.

Fictional character biography

Cassandra Cain is the daughter of David Cain and Sandra Wu-San (Lady Shiva), two of the world's deadliest assassins. Cain raised her in isolation, deliberately withholding speech so that the language centres of her brain would instead learn to read movement and body language, making her able to predict an opponent's actions. She was trained from birth as a living weapon. At the age of eight, Cain ordered her to kill a man; horrified by reading the man's dying emotions through his body language, she ran away.

Years later she surfaced in Gotham City during the No Man's Land crisis, working as a courier for Barbara Gordon (Oracle). After saving Commissioner Gordon's life, she was given the Batgirl costume with Batman's approval. Under Batman and Oracle's mentorship she slowly learned speech, reading and writing, and became one of the most skilled hand-to-hand fighters in the DC Universe — capable of holding her own against her mother, Lady Shiva, who is widely considered the greatest martial artist alive.

Powers and abilities

Like other members of the Batman family, Cassandra has no superhuman powers. Her childhood conditioning gave her an extraordinary ability to read body language and anticipate attacks, which, combined with a lifetime of martial-arts training, makes her one of the most formidable unarmed combatants in her fictional universe. She is also a skilled detective and acrobat.

In other media

Cassandra Cain made her live-action film debut in Birds of Prey (2020), played by Ella Jay Basco, depicted as a young pickpocket rather than a trained assassin. Versions of the character have also appeared in animated series and video games.
