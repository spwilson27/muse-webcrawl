FC Cincinnati

FC Cincinnati is an American professional soccer club based in Cincinnati, Ohio, that competes in Major League Soccer (MLS) as a member of the Eastern Conference. The club began play in the second-division United Soccer League in 2016, drawing record lower-division crowds at Nippert Stadium, and was awarded an MLS expansion franchise in 2018, joining the league in 2019.

History

After three difficult first MLS seasons, the club was rebuilt under general manager Chris Albright and head coach Pat Noonan. Cincinnati won the Supporters' Shield in 2023 as the team with the best regular-season record, powered by captain and league MVP Luciano Acosta. Its soccer-specific TQL Stadium opened in the West End in May 2021.

Rivalries and supporters

The club's fiercest rivalry is the "Hell Is Real" derby with the Columbus Crew, named after a roadside billboard on Interstate 71 between the two cities. The main supporters' group, The Bailey, occupies the stadium's north stand.
