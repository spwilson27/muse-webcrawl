List of world records in swimming

Olympic swimming in a 50 m pool

World records in swimming are ratified by World Aquatics (formerly FINA). Records are kept for long course (50 m pools) and short course (25 m pools) events. Many long-course records date from the 2008–2009 "supersuit" era, when polyurethane suits produced a wave of records before being banned in 2010.

History

FINA began recognising world records in 1908. The introduction of full-body polyurethane swimsuits in 2008–2009 led to an unprecedented 200-plus world records in two years; non-textile suits were banned from 1 January 2010. Long course records remain the most prestigious and are the standard for Olympic competition.
