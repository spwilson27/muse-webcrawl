Deutschlandticket

The Deutschlandticket (literally "Germany ticket"), also known as the 49-Euro-Ticket, is a subscription-based public transport ticket in Germany. Introduced on 1 May 2023, it provides unlimited travel on local and regional public transport across the entire country for a flat monthly fee.

History

The ticket was introduced as the successor to the temporary 9-Euro-Ticket, a heavily discounted fare offered during the summer of 2022 to relieve households of rising energy costs. After months of negotiation between the federal government and the states (Länder), the Deutschlandticket launched at a price of 49 euros per month.

Validity

The ticket is valid on all local and regional transport: buses, trams, U-Bahn and S-Bahn trains, and regional trains (RB, RE, IRE). It is generally not valid on long-distance trains such as ICE, IC and EC services, though some exceptions exist on specific routes. The subscription can be cancelled monthly.

Price changes

From 1 January 2025 the price rose to 58 euros per month, following disputes over the financing of the scheme between the federal and state governments.
