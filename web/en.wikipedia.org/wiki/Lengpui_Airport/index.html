Lengpui Airport

Lengpui Airport (IATA: AJL, ICAO: VELP) is a domestic airport serving Aizawl, the capital of Mizoram in northeastern India. It is located at Lengpui, roughly 32 kilometres (20 mi) from the city. The airport is unusual among major Indian airports for having been constructed by a state government rather than the Airports Authority of India, and it is one of the few airports in India built on a hilltop with a "table top" runway.

History

Construction of the airport began in the mid-1990s under the Government of Mizoram, and the airport was inaugurated in December 1998. Because of the hilly terrain, the runway was built on a raised plateau, requiring substantial earthworks, with streams flowing beneath the runway structure. Ownership and operation were later progressively transferred, with the Indian Air Force and the Airports Authority of India taking roles in its management and development.

Facilities and operations

The airport has a single asphalt runway of about 2,500 metres (8,200 ft) in length, equipped with an instrument landing system unusual for a table-top airfield in the country, which assists operations in the region's frequent poor weather. A passenger terminal handles domestic traffic, and the airport supports aircraft such as the Airbus A320 and ATR turboprops operated by Indian carriers.

Airlines and destinations

Lengpui is served by Indian domestic airlines including Air India, IndiGo, and Air India Express, with scheduled services connecting Aizawl to cities such as Delhi, Kolkata, Guwahati, and Imphal. The airport is a vital link for Mizoram, a landlocked hill state with limited road and rail connectivity.
