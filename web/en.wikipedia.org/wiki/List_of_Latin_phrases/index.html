List of Latin phrases

This page lists direct English translations of common Latin phrases. Some of the phrases are themselves translations of Greek phrases, as Greek rhetoric and literature reached its peak centuries before the Roman Empire. The full list is divided alphabetically on subpages; this article gives a selection of the best-known phrases.
