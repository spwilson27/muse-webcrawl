D'Alembert operator

The d'Alembert operator, also called the d'Alembertian, wave operator or box operator (denoted □ or □²), is a second-order differential operator in Minkowski spacetime, named after Jean le Rond d'Alembert.

Definition

In standard coordinates with metric signature (+,−,−,−), it is □ = (1/c²)∂²/∂t² − ∂²/∂x² − ∂²/∂y² − ∂²/∂z², the four-dimensional analogue of the Laplacian. It can be written covariantly as ∂μ∂μ.

Applications

The operator appears in the wave equation □φ = 0, the Klein–Gordon equation (□ + m²)φ = 0, and in the formulation of Maxwell's equations in Lorenz gauge, where □Aμ = μ0Jμ. Its Lorentz invariance makes it fundamental in relativistic field theory.
