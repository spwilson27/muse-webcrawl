Padmavati

Padmavati, also known as Padmini, is a legendary 13th–14th-century Indian queen, best known from Padmavat, the 1540 epic poem by the Sufi poet Malik Muhammad Jayasi. In the poem she is the exceptionally beautiful wife of Ratan Singh, the Rajput ruler of Mewar, whose kingdom is attacked by Alauddin Khalji, the Sultan of Delhi.

The legend

According to the story, Alauddin besieges Chittor Fort after hearing of Padmavati's beauty; when the fort falls, Padmavati and other women commit jauhar (mass self-immolation) to avoid capture. Historians debate whether Padmavati was a historical figure, as contemporary accounts of Alauddin's 1303 conquest of Chittor do not mention her.

Legacy

The tale became central to Rajput tradition and has been retold in many poems, plays, and films, including the 2018 film Padmaavat, whose release prompted protests in India.
