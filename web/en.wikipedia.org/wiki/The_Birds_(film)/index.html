The Birds (film)

The Birds
Director: Alfred Hitchcock
Year: 1963
Starring: Tippi Hedren, Rod Taylor
Based on: Daphne du Maurier short story

The Birds is a 1963 American horror-thriller directed by Alfred Hitchcock. Socialite Melanie Daniels (Tippi Hedren, in her debut) follows Mitch Brenner to Bodega Bay, where birds inexplicably begin attacking people in escalating waves.

Production

Combining real trained birds, mechanical birds and pioneering effects (including sodium vapour process work by Ub Iwerks), with an electronic sound score and no conventional music, the film ends unresolved as the family drives through a landscape of watching birds.

Legacy

A landmark of natural-horror cinema, in the National Film Registry since 2016.
