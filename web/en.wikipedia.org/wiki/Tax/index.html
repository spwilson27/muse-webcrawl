Tax

Tax
DefinitionCompulsory financial charge imposed by a government
TypesIncome, sales/VAT, property, corporate, excise, tariffs
[Image: A stack of tax forms, a calculator and coins on a desk]

A tax is a mandatory financial charge or levy imposed by a governmental organisation on individuals or businesses to fund public expenditures — infrastructure, defence, education, healthcare. Taxation is the primary revenue of modern states; 'no taxation without representation' was a slogan of the American Revolution (George Washington).

Politics

Tax design balances revenue, fairness and incentives: progressive income taxes, consumption taxes (VAT), property taxes and Pigouvian taxes on pollution. Evasion and avoidance drive international cooperation (global minimum corporate tax deals), while tax levels remain a defining left–right divide.
