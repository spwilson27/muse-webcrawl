Chunuk Bair

The New Zealand memorial on Chunuk Bair

Chunuk Bair (Turkish: Conk Bayırı) is a summit on the Sari Bair range of the Gallipoli Peninsula in Turkey. It was one of the main objectives of the Allied August Offensive during the Gallipoli campaign of the First World War, and the scene of fierce fighting between Ottoman forces and troops of the British Empire, particularly New Zealanders.

The battle

On the morning of August 8, 1915, the Wellington Battalion of the New Zealand Infantry Brigade, under Lieutenant-Colonel William Malone, seized the summit of Chunuk Bair — the only significant Allied success of the offensive. From the summit the New Zealanders could see the Dardanelles, the campaign's ultimate objective. Malone was killed, and the position was pounded by artillery and subjected to repeated counter-attacks.

After British battalions relieved the exhausted New Zealanders on the night of August 9–10, a massive Ottoman counter-attack led by Mustafa Kemal swept the summit on August 10, ending Allied hopes of breaking through from the heights. The ground was never retaken.

Commemoration

Chunuk Bair is central to New Zealand's memory of Gallipoli. The Chunuk Bair (New Zealand) Memorial, bearing the names of New Zealand soldiers with no known grave, stands on the summit, and the site hosts commemorative services each August and on Anzac Day.
