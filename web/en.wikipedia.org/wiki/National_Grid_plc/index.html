National Grid plc

Type: Public company
Industry: Utilities
Founded: 1990
Headquarters: London, United Kingdom
Listed: LSE: NG.; NYSE: NGG

National Grid plc is a British multinational electricity and gas utility company headquartered in London. It owns and operates energy transmission and distribution networks in the United Kingdom and the northeastern United States.

History

The company emerged from the breakup of the Central Electricity Generating Board during the privatisation of the UK electricity industry in 1990. It expanded into the United States through acquisitions including New England Electric System, Niagara Mohawk and KeySpan, becoming one of the largest investor-owned utilities in the US.

Operations

In Britain, National Grid owns the electricity transmission network in England and Wales and, following a major restructuring, sold its UK gas transmission business and transferred the electricity system operator role to the public National Energy System Operator in 2024. In the US it delivers electricity and gas to millions of customers in New York, Massachusetts and Rhode Island. The company invests heavily in grid modernisation to support renewable energy and electrification.
