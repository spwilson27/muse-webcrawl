Acquia

Acquia is an American software company that provides cloud hosting, support and digital experience products built around the open-source Drupal content management system. It was co-founded in 2007 by Dries Buytaert, the creator of Drupal, and Jay Batson, and is headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts.

History

Acquia was launched to provide commercial services for Drupal comparable to those Red Hat provided for Linux, including the Acquia Network subscription service and Acquia Cloud, a platform-as-a-service tuned for Drupal sites. Customers have included government agencies, universities and large media and consumer brands running high-traffic Drupal websites.

Products and acquisitions

The company expanded from hosting into a "digital experience platform" (DXP), acquiring the marketing automation company Mautic in 2019, the customer data platform AgilOne in 2019, and the digital asset management company Widen in 2021 (rebranded Acquia DAM). Other products include Site Factory for multisite management, Site Studio (from the Cohesion acquisition), and personalisation and campaign tools.

Ownership

In 2019 the private equity firm Vista Equity Partners acquired a majority stake in Acquia in a deal valuing the company at around $1 billion, making it one of the largest companies built on open-source web content management.
