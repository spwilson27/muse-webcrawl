Ambassador Theatre Group

FormerlyAmbassador Theatre Group (ATG)
Founded1992
FoundersHoward Panter, Rosemary Squire

The Ambassador Theatre Group (ATG), rebranded ATG Entertainment in 2024, is a British live entertainment company and the world's largest operator of theatres. Founded in 1992 by Howard Panter and Rosemary Squire, it owns or operates more than 70 venues across the United Kingdom, the United States and continental Europe, including many of London's West End theatres and several Broadway houses, and is also a major theatrical producer and ticketing business.

ATG's West End portfolio includes the Apollo Victoria, the Duke of York's, the Fortune, the Harold Pinter, the Lyceum, the Phoenix, the Piccadilly, the Playhouse and the Savoy theatres; regionally it runs venues such as the Manchester Opera House and the Edinburgh Playhouse. On Broadway it operates the Hudson Theatre and the Lyric Theatre (home to Harry Potter and the Cursed Child), and in 2015 it acquired the US venue operator ACE Theatrical Group. Through ATG Productions it produces and co-produces plays and musicals worldwide.

History

Panter and Squire began by acquiring the Duke of York's Theatre in 1992 and expanded steadily through the 1990s and 2000s. In 2009 ATG acquired the Live Nation UK theatre portfolio, making it the dominant West End operator. The company was sold to the private equity firm Providence Equity Partners in 2013. Founders Panter and Squire departed in 2016 to form Trafalgar Entertainment.

Venues

The group manages a mix of owned and leased historic theatres, presenting musicals, plays, opera, dance and comedy, and sells millions of tickets annually. Its venues host long-running productions such as Wicked (Apollo Victoria) and The Lion King (Lyceum).

Production and ticketing

ATG Productions has produced or co-produced numerous West End and Broadway transfers, and the group's ticketing operation, ATG Tickets, is one of the UK's largest theatre ticket sellers. The 2024 rebrand to ATG Entertainment reflected its expansion into broader live-entertainment promotion.
