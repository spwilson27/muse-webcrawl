Cleve Jones

Born1954, Lafayette, Indiana, U.S.
OccupationActivist, author, labour organiser
Known forNAMES Project AIDS Memorial Quilt

Cleve Jones (born 1954) is an American AIDS and gay rights activist and author. He conceived the NAMES Project AIDS Memorial Quilt, one of the world's largest community folk art projects, begun in 1985.

Early activism

Jones moved to San Francisco in the 1970s and worked in the office of Harvey Milk, the first openly gay man elected to public office in California. He was present in the movement circles around Milk before Milk's assassination in 1978.

The Quilt

In 1985 Jones conceived a memorial quilt in which each panel would commemorate a person who had died of AIDS. The quilt was first displayed on the National Mall in Washington, D.C., in 1987, and grew to tens of thousands of panels. Jones later worked as a union organiser and wrote the memoir When We Rise (2016).
