Pebble Island is one of the Falkland Islands, lying north of West Falkland across the Tamar Pass. It is one of the largest outlying islands of the archipelago and takes its name from the colourful pebbles found on its beaches.

Geography and settlement

The island is long and narrow, with hills in the east and west and a waist in the middle. The settlement, home to a small farming community, lies on the isthmus. The island is run as a sheep farm and has a lodge that receives tourists, who come for the wildlife, including rockhopper and gentoo penguins, sea lions and several species of waterfowl on the island's ponds.

Falklands War

During the Falklands War of 1982, Argentine forces established an airbase, Puerto Calderón, on the island's airstrip, operating Pucará ground-attack aircraft. On the night of 14–15 May 1982 the British Special Air Service raided the airfield in the Pebble Island raid, destroying eleven aircraft in one of the most successful special forces actions of the war. Memorials on the island commemorate both British and Argentine dead.
