Data is a fictional character in the Star Trek franchise, portrayed by actor Brent Spiner. A sentient android and member of Starfleet, he serves as second officer and chief operations officer aboard the starship USS Enterprise-D and its successor, the USS Enterprise-E, in the television series Star Trek: The Next Generation and the films that followed it.

[ Hero image: Lieutenant Commander Data at his operations station aboard the Enterprise ]

Character overview

Data was built by the cyberneticist Dr. Noonien Soong on the planet Omicron Theta. He is a fully functional android with a positronic brain, possessing superhuman strength, speed, and computational ability, and a memory capacity of 800 quadrillion bits. Although incapable of experiencing emotions for much of his career, Data aspires to become more human, and his gradual exploration of humanity — through art, music, humour, and friendship — is one of the central character arcs of The Next Generation. He keeps a cat named Spot and is an accomplished violinist, painter, and student of Sherlock Holmes, whom he frequently emulates on the holodeck alongside Geordi La Forge.

The emotion chip

Soong designed an "emotion chip" intended to grant Data feelings. The chip was stolen and implanted by Data's brother, the unstable android Lore, in the episode "Descent". Data later installed the chip himself in the film Star Trek Generations (1994), after which he experienced uncontrolled surges of emotion before learning to regulate them.

Death and legacy

Data sacrifices himself to save Captain Jean-Luc Picard at the climax of Star Trek: Nemesis (2002), destroying the Reman warbird Scimitar. His memories, downloaded shortly beforehand into the prototype android B-4, form the basis of his eventual restoration. In Star Trek: Picard, Data appears in Picard's dreams; a copy of his consciousness, preserved by Altan Inigo Soong, is ultimately allowed to die at Data's own request. In the third season, Data's personality is integrated into a new android body created by Daystrom Station researchers, alongside elements of Lore, B-4, and Lal.

Family

  • Lore — Data's older "brother", an earlier Soong android with emotions but an unstable, malevolent personality.
  • B-4 — a less advanced prototype discovered in Nemesis.
  • Lal — an android "daughter" created by Data in the episode "The Offspring"; she died of cascade failure shortly after activation.
  • Juliana Tainer — Soong's wife, recreated as an android unaware of her own nature.

Reception

Data is consistently ranked among the most popular characters in the Star Trek franchise. Spiner's performance won praise for conveying curiosity and innocence through an outwardly emotionless character, and Data's legal struggle for personhood in the episode "The Measure of a Man" is frequently cited as one of the finest hours of the series.
