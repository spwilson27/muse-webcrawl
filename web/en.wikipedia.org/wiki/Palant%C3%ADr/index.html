Palantír

A palantír (plural palantíri) is a fictional magical artefact in J. R. R. Tolkien's legendarium: an indestructible crystal sphere used for communication and for seeing events far away in space and time. The name is Quenya for "far-sighted" or "that which looks far away".

History in the legendarium

The seven Stones were made by the Elves of Valinor — tradition ascribes them to Fëanor — and given to the Men of Númenor, who brought them to Middle-earth before the island's fall. In the Third Age they were kept at key strongholds of Gondor and Arnor, including Minas Tirith, Orthanc, Osgiliath, and Annúminas. Several were lost; by the War of the Ring only a few remained in use.

Use and danger

Two users of Stones could communicate mind to mind, but a single user could be overpowered by a stronger will: Sauron, holding the Ithil-stone, used the Orthanc-stone to ensnare Saruman and to drive Denethor to despair with selectively shown visions. In The Lord of the Rings, Pippin Took's glimpse into the Stone alerts Sauron, while Aragorn later uses it deliberately to challenge him.
