Seattle Kraken

The Kraken at Climate Pledge Arena.

The Seattle Kraken are a professional ice hockey team based in Seattle, Washington. They compete in the National Hockey League (NHL) as a member of the Pacific Division of the Western Conference. The Kraken began play in the 2021–22 season as the league's 32nd franchise and play their home games at Climate Pledge Arena, on the Seattle Center campus.

Founded: 2021

Home arena: Climate Pledge Arena

Colours: Deep sea blue, ice blue, boundless blue, shadow blue, red alert

Conference: Western

Division: Pacific

First general manager: Ron Francis

Mascot: Buoy, a sea troll

History

The NHL Board of Governors voted unanimously on 4 December 2018 to award an expansion franchise to Seattle, the first top-level professional hockey team in the city since the Seattle Totems folded in 1975 (the Seattle Metropolitans had won the Stanley Cup in 1917). The name "Kraken" — after the mythical giant sea creature — was unveiled on 23 July 2020, along with a logo featuring an "S" in tribute to the Metropolitans.

Early seasons

After a difficult inaugural season finishing near the bottom of the league, the Kraken improved dramatically in 2022–23, reaching the Stanley Cup playoffs for the first time and upsetting the defending champion Colorado Avalanche in the first round before losing a seven-game second-round series to the Dallas Stars. The team hosted the 2024 NHL Winter Classic at T-Mobile Park, defeating the Vegas Golden Knights 3–0.
