Wasp (character)

The Wasp is the name of several superheroines appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. The original Wasp, Janet van Dyne, was created by Stan Lee, Ernie Hart, and Jack Kirby and first appeared in Tales to Astonish #44 (June 1963). She is a founding member of the Avengers and the partner of Hank Pym (Ant-Man/Giant-Man).

Powers and abilities

Using Pym Particles, the Wasp can shrink to insect size (typically about half an inch tall) while retaining her human strength, and sprouts bio-electric wings that allow flight at small size. She can fire bioelectric energy blasts known as "wasp's stings" and communicate with and influence insects. Janet van Dyne is also depicted as a skilled leader — she chaired the Avengers for extended periods — and as a successful fashion designer and businesswoman.

Other Wasps

After Janet was believed killed during the Secret Invasion storyline, Hank Pym took up the Wasp identity in her honour. In the Marvel Cinematic Universe, Janet's daughter Hope van Dyne becomes the Wasp, appearing in Ant-Man (2015) and taking the mantle in Ant-Man and the Wasp (2018). In the comics, Nadia van Dyne (adapted from Nadia Pym) later became a new Wasp.

In other media

Janet van Dyne is portrayed by Michelle Pfeiffer and Hope van Dyne by Evangeline Lilly in the Marvel Cinematic Universe films.
