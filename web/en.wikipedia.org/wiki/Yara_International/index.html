Yara International

Yara International ASA
Founded: 1905 (as Norsk Hydro); Yara spun off 2004
Headquarters: Oslo, Norway
Industry: Chemicals, fertilizers
Products: Nitrogen fertilizers, ammonia, nitrates, NPK
Listing: Oslo Stock Exchange (YAR)

Yara International ASA is a Norwegian chemical company and the world's largest producer of nitrogen-based mineral fertilizers. Headquartered in Oslo, Yara converts energy, natural minerals, and nitrogen from the air into crop nutrition products, and also supplies industrial chemicals and environmental solutions. It operates in more than 60 countries with roughly 17,000 employees.

History

Yara's roots lie in Norsk Hydro, founded in 1905 to exploit hydroelectric power for nitrogen fixation using the Birkeland–Eyde process. Hydro's fertilizer division, Hydro Agri, was demerged and listed as Yara International in 2004. The company expanded by acquisitions worldwide, becoming a dominant supplier of ammonia, urea, nitrates, and compound NPK fertilizers. The Norwegian state remains its largest shareholder, with a stake of about one third.

Activities

Beyond crop nutrition, Yara produces AdBlue (as Air1) for diesel emissions control, industrial nitrates for explosives and civil engineering, and CO₂ for industrial uses. In recent years it has invested in "clean ammonia" projects — green and blue ammonia produced with renewable hydrogen or carbon capture — including the ammonia-powered shipping fuel initiative and the Yara Eyde vessels, positioning ammonia as a future hydrogen carrier and maritime fuel.
