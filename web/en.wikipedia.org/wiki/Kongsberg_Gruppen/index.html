Kongsberg Gruppen

Kongsberg Gruppen ASA

Industry: Technology, defence, maritime

Founded: 1814 (as Kongsberg Weapons Factory)

Headquarters: Kongsberg, Norway

Major owner: Norwegian state (about 50%)

Kongsberg Gruppen ASA is an international technology group headquartered in Kongsberg, Norway. It supplies high-technology systems and solutions to customers within the defence, maritime, oil and gas, and digital industries.

Business areas

The group is organised into Kongsberg Defence & Aerospace, Kongsberg Maritime, and Kongsberg Digital. Its products range from missile and air-defence systems to dynamic positioning and automation systems for ships and offshore installations.

History

The company traces its origins to the Kongsberg Weapons Factory established in 1814. After financial difficulties in the 1980s, the group was restructured and listed on the Oslo Stock Exchange in 1993, with the Norwegian government retaining a majority stake. In 2019 it acquired the commercial marine business of Rolls-Royce.
