Banda Los Recoditos


Banda Los Recoditos in concert.

Banda Los Recoditos is a Mexican banda group from Mazatlán, Sinaloa, founded in 1989. It is the youth-oriented sister band of Banda el Recodo, one of Mexico's most famous and long-running bandas; the name "Los Recoditos" ("the little Recodos") reflects that its original members were the children and relatives of el Recodo's musicians. The group performs in the Sinaloan banda style — large brass and woodwind ensembles with tuba, trumpets, clarinets, trombones, and percussion — and is known for romantic ballads, cumbias, and corridos.

History

Los Recoditos was formed with the support of Banda el Recodo's leader Germán Lizárraga, initially as a way for the younger generation of the Lizárraga family and other Mazatlán musicians to perform. The band recorded for Fonovisa and built a following in Mexico and the United States through touring and regional Mexican radio.

The band's popularity rose sharply in the 2010s with the album A Bailar de Caballito (2010) and singles such as "Ando Bien Pedo", which topped Billboard's Regional Mexican charts and became a signature song of the modern banda movement in the United States. Subsequent hits included "Mi Último Deseo" (2013) and "Me Tocó Perder". The group has been nominated for Latin Grammy Awards in the Best Banda Album category and has performed at major US venues and festivals serving the Mexican-American audience.

Style and line-up

Like other Sinaloan bandas, Los Recoditos numbers more than a dozen musicians, with featured vocalists fronting the brass ensemble. Lead vocalists have included Luis Ángel "El Flaco" Franco, who left in 2020 to pursue a solo career, and Samuel Sarmiento. The band is credited with helping bring banda music to a younger, urban audience alongside acts such as Banda MS.
