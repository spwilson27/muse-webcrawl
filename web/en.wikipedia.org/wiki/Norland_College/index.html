Norland College

Norland College is a British higher-education institution in Bath, Somerset, founded in 1892 by Emily Ward to train professional childcarers. Its graduates, known as Norlanders or Norland nannies, are famous for their distinctive uniforms and for employment with royalty and celebrity families worldwide.

Norland College
Founded: 1892, by Emily Ward
Location: Bath, England
Known for: Training professional nannies

History

Emily Ward founded the Norland Institute in London in 1892, pioneering the idea of childcare as a trained profession for women. The college moved to Bath in 2003. Norlanders traditionally wore a brown uniform with a distinctive hat; a modernised uniform retains the classic styling for formal duties.

Training

Students complete a degree in Early Childhood Education and Care combined with the Norland diploma, covering child development, nutrition, practical care, self-defence, and defensive driving. Graduates famously include the nanny to the children of the Prince and Princess of Wales.
