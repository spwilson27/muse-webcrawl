Abhishek Amitabh Bachchan (born 5 February 1976) is an Indian actor and film producer known for his work in Hindi films. The son of actors Amitabh Bachchan and Jaya Bachchan, he debuted in Refugee (2000) and went on to win three consecutive Filmfare Awards for Best Supporting Actor.

Career

After a series of unsuccessful films, Bachchan's career turned in 2004 with Yuva, which won him the first of three consecutive Filmfare Best Supporting Actor awards, followed by Sarkar (2005) and Kabhi Alvida Naa Kehna (2006). He played the lead in the blockbuster action franchise Dhoom (2004–2013) and won critical acclaim for his title performance in Mani Ratnam's Guru (2007).

Later work

Bachchan produced the award-winning family drama Paa (2009), starring his father. In the streaming era he headlined the crime thriller series Breathe: Into the Shadows and films such as Ludo (2020), Bob Biswas (2021), Dasvi (2022), and Ghoomer (2023).

Personal life and ventures

He married actress Aishwarya Rai in 2007; their daughter Aaradhya was born in 2011. Bachchan owns the Jaipur Pink Panthers kabaddi team in the Pro Kabaddi League and co-owns the Chennaiyin FC football club.
