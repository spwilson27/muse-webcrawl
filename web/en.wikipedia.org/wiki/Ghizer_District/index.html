Ghizer District (Urdu: ضلع غذر) is a district in the Gilgit-Baltistan territory of Pakistan. Its administrative headquarters is the town of Gahkuch. The district occupies the western part of Gilgit-Baltistan and is traversed by the Ghizer River, a tributary of the Gilgit River, which in turn flows into the Indus River.

Geography

Ghizer District is bounded by the Wakhan Corridor of Afghanistan to the north, by China to the northeast, by the Diamer District to the south, and by the Upper Chitral District of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa to the west. The Shandur Pass, at an elevation of about 3,700 metres, connects Ghizer with Chitral and is famous for the annual Shandur Polo Festival, held on one of the highest polo grounds in the world.

The district is known for its mountain valleys, including the Phander Valley, Gupis Valley, Yasin Valley, Punial Valley and Ishkoman Valley. Phander Lake and Khalti Lake are among its notable lakes, and the region is popular for trout fishing and tourism.

History

Historically, the area formed part of the old princely states and mehtar-ruled territories of the northern areas. After the accession of the northern states to Pakistan in 1947 and the abolition of the princely states in 1974, the region was administered as part of the Gilgit Agency and later the Northern Areas. Ghizer was constituted as a separate district in 1974. In 2019 the district was divided, with the western tehsils of Gupis and Yasin forming the new Gupis-Yasin District, and Punial and Ishkoman remaining in Ghizer.

Demographics and culture

The population is ethnically and linguistically diverse. Shina is widely spoken in Gahkuch and Punial, Khowar (Chitrali) is spoken in the western valleys, Burushaski in parts of Yasin and Ishkoman, and Wakhi in the northern Ishkoman area bordering the Wakhan. The majority of the population follows Islam, with significant Ismaili communities alongside Sunni and Shia Muslims. Polo, played without rules in the traditional "freestyle" mountain style, is a beloved sport, and the district's teams compete in the Shandur festival against Chitral.
