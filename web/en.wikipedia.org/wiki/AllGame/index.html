AllGame (previously All Game Guide) was a commercial database of information about arcade, PC, and console video games, operated by the All Media Network alongside its sister databases AllMusic and AllMovie. The site provided game descriptions, credits, release information and reviews scored on a five-star scale.

History

The All Game Guide was launched in 1998 by Michael Erlewine's All Media Guide as a companion to the established AllMusic database. Its editorial content and database entries were widely syndicated to retailers and other websites. Like its sister sites, AllGame combined professional editorial reviews with a large catalogue of factual listings covering thousands of games across dozens of platforms.

In July 2013 the All Media Network's properties were sold by Rovi to All Media Network LLC. AllGame ceased operations on 12 December 2014, following the earlier shutdown of the AllMovie and AllMusic companion services' original formats; AllMusic itself continued under new ownership. Archived AllGame reviews remain a frequently cited source in video game history and reference works.
