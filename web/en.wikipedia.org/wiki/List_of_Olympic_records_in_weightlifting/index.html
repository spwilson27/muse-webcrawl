List of Olympic records in weightlifting

This article details the Olympic records in weightlifting. Records are maintained in each weight class for the snatch, the clean and jerk, and the total of both lifts. Because the International Weightlifting Federation restructured the weight classes in 1993, 1998 and again in 2018, many earlier records were retired; the records below reflect the classes in use at recent Games, principally Tokyo 2020 and Paris 2024. Records set with classes no longer contested (such as the pre-2018 divisions) are archived separately on the full article.

Historic highlights

Among retired classes, Hossein Rezazadeh of Iran held the super-heavyweight records with a 213 kg snatch and 263.5 kg clean and jerk (Athens 2004 era), while Halil Mutlu and Naim Süleymanoğlu set celebrated records in the lighter divisions in the 1990s. Women's weightlifting joined the Olympic program at Sydney 2000.
