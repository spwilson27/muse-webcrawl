Transport for Wales Rail Limited (Welsh: Trafnidiaeth Cymru Rheilffordd Cyfyngedig), trading as Transport for Wales Rail (TfW Rail), is a Welsh train operating company owned by Transport for Wales, an arm's-length body of the Welsh Government. It operates passenger services under the Wales and Borders franchise.

History

The Wales and Borders franchise was awarded in 2018 to KeolisAmey Wales, which traded as Transport for Wales Rail Services. After the collapse in passenger revenue during the COVID-19 pandemic, the Welsh Government took the franchise into public ownership, and the new publicly owned operator took over on 7 February 2021. It thus became the first train operator in Wales owned by the devolved government.

Services and fleet

TfW Rail operates local and regional services across Wales, long-distance services to Manchester, Birmingham and London, and the urban Valley Lines network around Cardiff, which is being upgraded as part of the South Wales Metro project. The company has introduced new fleets including Class 197 and Class 231 diesel units, Class 398 tram-trains and Class 756 tri-mode units, replacing the older trains inherited from previous operators.
