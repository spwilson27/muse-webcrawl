Simon McBurney

Born: 25 August 1957, Cambridge, England
Occupation: Actor, director, writer
Known for: Co-founding Complicité (Théâtre de Complicité)

Simon Montagu McBurney (born 25 August 1957) is an English actor, director, and writer. He is the co-founder — with Annabel Arden, Fiona Gordon, and Marcello Magni — of the Théâtre de Complicité, now known simply as Complicité, one of Britain's most internationally influential physical theatre companies.

Career

McBurney read English literature at Peterhouse, Cambridge, and trained in Paris at the École Jacques Lecoq. Complicité's productions, characterised by devised physical work and striking visual staging, include A Minute Too Late, The Visit, Mnemonic, The Elephant Vanishes, A Disappearing Number, and the Broadway and West End success The Encounter (2015), based on Petru Popescu's account of the Amazon, in which McBurney performed solo with binaural sound design.

As a screen actor he has appeared in films including The Manchurian Candidate (2004), The Last King of Scotland (2006), The Duchess (2008), Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 1 (2010, as the voice of Kreacher), Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy (2011), The Theory of Everything (2014), Mission: Impossible – Rogue Nation (2015), and The Conjuring 2 (2016).
