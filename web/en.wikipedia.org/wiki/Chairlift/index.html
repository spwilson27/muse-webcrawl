Chairlift

A chairlift is a type of aerial lift which consists of a continuously circulating steel cable loop strung between two end terminals and usually over intermediate towers, carrying chairs for passengers. Chairlifts are the primary on-mountain transport at most ski areas.

Types

Fixed-grip chairlifts keep chairs permanently attached to the cable; detachable chairlifts allow chairs to slow in stations for easier boarding and travel faster on the line. Capacities range from single to eight-person chairs, some with bubbles and heated seats.

Safety

Safety features include restraining bars, anti-rollback devices and auxiliary drives.
