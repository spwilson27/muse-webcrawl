Science, also widely referred to as Science Magazine, is the peer-reviewed academic journal of the American Association for the Advancement of Science (AAAS) and one of the world's top academic journals. It was first published in 1880, is published weekly, and has a print readership of around 130,000, with a much larger online audience. The journal is based in Washington, D.C., with an office in Cambridge, England.

History

Science was founded in 1880 by the journalist John Michels, with financial backing from Thomas Edison and later Alexander Graham Bell. The journal struggled financially in its early years; in 1900 it became the official journal of the AAAS after the association's secretary, Leland O. Howard, arranged for its purchase. Under editor James McKeen Cattell, who led the journal for the first four decades of the twentieth century, Science became the principal voice of American science.

Content

The journal publishes original research across the scientific disciplines, with an emphasis on work of broad significance, alongside news reporting on science policy and scientific developments, reviews, perspectives, and book reviews. Its news section is widely read for coverage of research funding, politics, and controversies. Papers in Science are heavily cited, and the journal consistently ranks among the highest-impact multidisciplinary journals, alongside its principal competitor, Nature.

Submissions undergo rigorous peer review, and acceptance rates are low — only a small fraction of submitted manuscripts are published. The AAAS also publishes a family of related journals, including Science Advances (an open-access journal launched in 2015), Science Immunology, Science Robotics, Science Signaling, and Science Translational Medicine.

Access

The journal's content is available by subscription, with much of its news content freely accessible. Since 1997 the journal has been published online at science.org. Each year, the editors select a "Breakthrough of the Year" recognising the most significant scientific advance.
