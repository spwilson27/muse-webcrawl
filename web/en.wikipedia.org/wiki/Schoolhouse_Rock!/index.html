Schoolhouse Rock!

Schoolhouse Rock! is an American series of animated musical educational short films that aired during Saturday morning children's programming on the ABC television network from 1973. The shorts teach grammar, science, economics, history, mathematics and civics through catchy songs and simple animation.

History

The series was created by advertising executive David McCall, whose son struggled to memorise multiplication tables but could remember popular song lyrics. Jazz musician Bob Dorough wrote and performed the first songs, beginning with "Three Is a Magic Number". The original run of Multiplication Rock was joined by Grammar Rock, America Rock — made for the United States Bicentennial and including the famous "I'm Just a Bill", about the legislative process, and "Conjunction Junction" — and Science Rock.

Legacy

The shorts were revived in the 1990s with Money Rock and have been repeatedly released on home video. They are widely remembered by generations of American schoolchildren, and "I'm Just a Bill" is frequently cited in civics education and popular culture. The songs have been covered by numerous artists and the property has been adapted for the stage as Schoolhouse Rock Live!
