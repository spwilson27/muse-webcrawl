Pan Trinbago

Pan Trinbago is the governing body for steelpan music in Trinidad and Tobago, recognised by the government as the official organisation of the steelband movement. It represents hundreds of steel orchestras, organises major competitions, and promotes the steelpan — the national instrument of Trinidad and Tobago, invented in the country in the twentieth century and widely described as the only new acoustic instrument of that century.

Role and events

Pan Trinbago organises Panorama, the flagship steelband competition held during Trinidad Carnival, in which large orchestras of a hundred or more players perform elaborate calypso arrangements at the Queen's Park Savannah. It also runs junior and single-pan competitions, supports member bands' administration, and coordinates Trinidad and Tobago's participation in international steelpan festivals.

History

The organisation grew out of the Steelband Association formed in the early 1950s to unite bands that had previously been fiercely rival street gangs, and it took the name Pan Trinbago in the early 1970s. It has periodically been at the centre of public debate over government funding, governance, and the development of the panyard system.
