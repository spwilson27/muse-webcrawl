1080p (1920 × 1080 progressively displayed pixels), also known as Full HD, is a set of HDTV high-definition video modes characterised by 1,080 horizontal lines of vertical resolution and progressive scan, in which each frame is drawn in full rather than as interlaced fields.

Usage

1080p24 is the format of Blu-ray Disc and most film-originated streaming content; 1080p30 and 1080p60 are common for video games, cameras and online video. Broadcast television historically favoured 1080i or 720p because of bandwidth, though streaming services made 1080p the mainstream consumer standard of the 2010s.

Successors

1080p has been succeeded at the high end by 1440p (QHD), 4K UHD (2160p) and 8K resolutions, but remains the most common display resolution worldwide across televisions, monitors and phones.
