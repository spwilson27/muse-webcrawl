Xiangxi Tujia and Miao Autonomous Prefecture

Xiangxi Prefecture
Country: China
Province: Hunan
Prefecture seat: Jishou
Area: about 15,500 km²
Population (2020): about 2.49 million

Xiangxi Tujia and Miao Autonomous Prefecture is an autonomous prefecture of the People's Republic of China, located in the northwestern corner of Hunan province. It borders Hubei province and Chongqing municipality to the north and Guizhou province to the west. Its name, Xiangxi, means "western Hunan." The prefecture is designated autonomous for its large populations of the Tujia and Miao ethnic minorities, who together form a majority of its roughly 2.5 million inhabitants.

Administration

The prefecture administers one county-level city, Jishou (the seat), and seven counties: Luxi, Fenghuang, Huayuan, Baojing, Guzhang, Yongshun, and Longshan.

Geography and culture

Xiangxi is mountainous, cut by the Yuan River and its tributaries, and is noted for the scenic Wuling Mountains. Its best-known attraction is Fenghuang Ancient Town, a remarkably preserved riverside town of stilt houses associated with the writer Shen Congwen, who was born there. The region is also celebrated for its Miao silverwork and festivals, Tujia culture, the Dehang Miao village, and as the home region of the writer Shen Congwen and painter Huang Yongyu. Local specialties include kiwi fruit and the austere, sour-spicy cuisine of western Hunan.
