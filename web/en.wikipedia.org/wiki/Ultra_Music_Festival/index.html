Ultra Music Festival (UMF) is an annual outdoor electronic music festival held in March in Miami, Florida, United States, during Miami Music Week. Founded in 1999 by Russell Faibisch and Alex Omes, it was named after the 1997 Depeche Mode album Ultra. The flagship event takes place at Bayfront Park in downtown Miami and draws about 165,000 attendees over three days, with performances across multiple stages by leading DJs and live acts.

Ultra's main stage in Miami. The festival pioneered large-scale live streaming of DJ sets.

History

The first Ultra was a one-day event on Miami Beach in 1999. It grew through the 2000s, moving between Bayfront Park and Bicentennial Park, and expanded to three days. The 2018 edition marked the festival's 20th anniversary, ending with the surprise reunion of Swedish House Mafia. In 2019 the festival temporarily moved to Virginia Key, returning to Bayfront Park in 2022 after a two-year pandemic hiatus.

International editions

Under the Ultra Worldwide banner, editions have been staged on every inhabited continent, including Ultra Europe (Croatia), Ultra Korea, Ultra Japan, Ultra Brasil, Ultra South Africa, and the smaller touring Road to Ultra events. The festival is known for elaborate stage production and for broadcasting sets to a large online audience.
