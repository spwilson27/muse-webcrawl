Cael Norman Sanderson (born June 20, 1979) is an American former folkstyle and freestyle wrestler and the head wrestling coach at Penn State University. He is widely considered one of the greatest American wrestlers of all time.

At Iowa State University, Sanderson completed his collegiate career undefeated at 159–0, winning four NCAA championships and four Outstanding Wrestler awards — an unprecedented feat. In 2004 he won the gold medal in freestyle wrestling at the Athens Olympic Games.

College career

Sanderson won NCAA titles from 1999 to 2002 at 184 and 197 pounds, and won the Dan Hodge Trophy three times as the nation's top college wrestler. Sports Illustrated named his undefeated run among the outstanding feats in college sports history.

Coaching

After a brief competitive return, Sanderson became head coach at Iowa State (2006–2009) and then at Penn State in 2009. Under him, the Nittany Lions became a dynasty, winning numerous NCAA team championships and producing many individual champions and Olympians, including his younger wrestlers in the programme he built into the sport's dominant force.

Family

Sanderson's brothers Cody and Cole were also Iowa State wrestlers, and his nephew-uncle wrestling lineage is a noted part of Utah wrestling history; he was coached in college by Bobby Douglas.
