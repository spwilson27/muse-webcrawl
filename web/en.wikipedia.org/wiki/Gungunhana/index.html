Gungunhana

Gungunhana (c. 1850 – 23 December 1906), also spelled Ngungunyane, was the last independent emperor (nkosi) of the Gaza Empire, a Nguni kingdom in what is now southern Mozambique. His defeat and capture by the Portuguese in 1895 ended Gaza resistance and consolidated colonial rule.

Reign

Grandson of Soshangane, founder of Gaza, Gungunhana succeeded his father Muzila in 1884 after a succession struggle. Pressured by Portugal and Britain — including the 1890 British Ultimatum over the region — he manoeuvred between treaties, but in 1895 rebelled against Portuguese demands. Portuguese forces under António Enes and Mouzinho de Albuquerque defeated his armies at Magul and Coolela.

Exile and legacy

Captured at Chaimite in December 1895 with his wives and family, he was paraded in Lisbon and exiled to the Azores, where he died in 1906. He is remembered in Mozambique as a hero of anti-colonial resistance. See Gaza Empire and Mozambique.
