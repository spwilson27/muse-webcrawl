Open Payments

Open Payments is an open protocol and API standard for setting up and making payments between accounts at different financial institutions and digital wallets, built on the Interledger Protocol (ILP) and the GNAP authorisation protocol. It was developed within the Interledger community, with major contributions from the Coil and Uphold teams and support from the Interledger Foundation.

Design

Accounts are identified by payment pointers and wallet addresses expressed as URLs. A client application, authorised by the account holder through GNAP, can create incoming payments, quotes and outgoing payments via standard HTTPS APIs, enabling interoperable micropayments, tipping and streaming payments on the web without bilateral integration between providers.

Context

Open Payments supports the web monetization ecosystem, in which browsers can stream small payments to creators as content is consumed. The term should not be confused with the unrelated US federal "Open Payments" database (Physician Payments Sunshine Act) that reports industry payments to physicians.
