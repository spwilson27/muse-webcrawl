William Jessop

William Jessop (23 January 1745 – 18 November 1814) was an English civil engineer, best known for canals, harbours and early railways of the Industrial Revolution. A pupil of John Smeaton, he became one of Britain's leading canal engineers.

Works

Major projects include the Cromford Canal, the Grand Canal of Ireland, the West India Docks in London (with Ralph Walker), the Surrey Iron Railway (1803, among the first public railways), and the Pontcysyllte Aqueduct in partnership overseeing Thomas Telford. He co-founded the Butterley Company ironworks. Professional organisation of his field later developed into bodies such as the Engineering Council.
