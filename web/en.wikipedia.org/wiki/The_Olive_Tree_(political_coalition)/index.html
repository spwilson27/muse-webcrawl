The Olive Tree (political coalition)

The Olive Tree (Italian: L'Ulivo) was a centre-left political coalition in Italy, founded in 1995 and led most prominently by Romano Prodi. It united post-communist, social-democratic, centrist and green parties against Silvio Berlusconi's centre-right.

LeaderRomano Prodi
Founded6 March 1995
IdeologyCentre-left; social democracy, Christian left, liberalism

History

The coalition won the 1996 general election, bringing Prodi to the premiership and Italy into the eurozone's founding group. After Prodi's government fell in 1998, the alliance continued under Massimo D'Alema and Giuliano Amato, lost the 2001 election, and returned to government with Prodi's victory in 2006 as part of the broader Union.

Dissolution

In 2007 the coalition's main parties merged into the Democratic Party, ending the Olive Tree as a separate alliance, though its symbol and name persisted locally for some years.
