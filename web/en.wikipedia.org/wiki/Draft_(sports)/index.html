Draft (sports)

A draft is a process used in some countries, primarily in North America, and in some other sports leagues elsewhere, to allocate certain players to teams. In a draft, teams take turns selecting from a pool of eligible players; the team selecting a player obtains exclusive rights to sign that player to a contract, preventing other teams from signing the player. Drafts are intended to promote competitive balance: the worst-performing teams typically pick first, often under a lottery system designed to discourage deliberate losing ("tanking").

Major drafts

The best-known drafts are the NFL Draft, the NBA draft, the NHL entry draft, and the MLB draft, which allocate amateur and college players to professional clubs. Drafts may also be used to distribute players in expansion drafts, dispersal drafts, and fantasy sports, where participants select players in a similar rotating order.
