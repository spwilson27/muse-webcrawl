List of lighthouses in England

Lighthouses of England
Most are maintained by Trinity House; harbour lights by local authorities.

This is a list of lighthouses in England. The majority of significant coastal lighthouses are maintained by Trinity House, the General Lighthouse Authority for England, Wales, the Channel Islands and Gibraltar. Many listed structures are now automated, and some are decommissioned.
