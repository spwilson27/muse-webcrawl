The Kingpin (Wilson Fisk) is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by Stan Lee and John Romita Sr., he first appeared in The Amazing Spider-Man #50 (July 1967) as an adversary of Spider-Man, and later became the arch-enemy of Daredevil and a recurring foe of the Punisher.

Characterisation

Fisk is a criminal mastermind who controls organised crime in New York City. Despite his enormous bulk — he stands about 6 feet 7 inches and weighs some 450 pounds — nearly all of it is muscle rather than fat, making him a formidable hand-to-hand combatant. His signature look is a white suit jacket and a diamond-tipped cane. His great weakness is his devotion to his wife, Vanessa Fisk.

In other media

The character was portrayed by Michael Clarke Duncan in the 2003 film Daredevil, and by Vincent D'Onofrio in the Marvel Cinematic Universe television series Daredevil, Hawkeye, and Daredevil: Born Again, a performance widely praised by critics.
