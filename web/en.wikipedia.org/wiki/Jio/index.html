Jio

Reliance Jio Infocomm Limited
Founded: 2007 (launched publicly 2016)
Headquarters: Navi Mumbai, India
Parent: Jio Platforms / Reliance Industries
Industry: Telecommunications

Reliance Jio Infocomm Limited, doing business as Jio, is an Indian telecommunications company and a subsidiary of Jio Platforms, itself part of Reliance Industries. Headquartered in Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra, it operates a national LTE and 5G network and is the largest mobile network operator in India and among the largest in the world by subscribers.

History

The company was incorporated in 2007 and acquired pan-India broadband wireless spectrum in 2010. After years of network construction, Jio launched commercially on 5 September 2016 with an extended free welcome offer of free voice calls and 4G data, an aggressive entry that triggered a price war, drove data prices in India to among the lowest in the world, and accelerated consolidation in the Indian telecom sector. Jio gained 100 million subscribers within its first 170 days.

Jio built an all-IP, LTE-only network without legacy 2G or 3G infrastructure, offering voice over LTE. Beyond mobile services it expanded into JioFiber home broadband, the JioPhone feature phone aimed at first-time internet users, and a suite of apps and digital services including JioCinema and JioSaavn. In 2020, Jio Platforms attracted major investments from companies including Meta (Facebook) and Google. Jio began rolling out its 5G service, Jio True 5G, from late 2022 using a standalone architecture.

Impact

Jio is widely credited with transforming internet access in India: cheap data sharply increased the number of Indians online, fuelled the growth of streaming, digital payments, and app-based services, and reshaped the competitive landscape of Indian telecommunications.
