Quake engine

The Quake engine is a game engine developed by id Software for the 1996 first-person shooter Quake. Written primarily in C by John Carmack with Michael Abrash, it introduced true 3D polygonal rendering, precomputed lightmaps, and networked multiplayer that helped popularise online deathmatch.

Technology

Unlike the ray-casting of earlier shooters, Quake rendered fully 3D worlds and characters, used BSP trees and potentially visible sets for efficiency, and supported hardware acceleration through GLQuake. Its source code was released under the GPL in 1999, spawning many derivative engines and source ports.

Legacy

The engine lineage continued through Quake II and Quake III engines (id Tech 2 and 3) and influenced the design of the GoldSrc and Source engines used by Valve.
