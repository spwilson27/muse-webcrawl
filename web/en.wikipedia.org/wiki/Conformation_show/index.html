Conformation show

A conformation show is a competitive event in which purebred animals — most prominently dogs, and also cats, horses, and livestock — are judged against the written breed standard for their breed, type, or species. Unlike performance events, a conformation show evaluates how closely each animal's physical structure, proportions, coat, movement, and temperament conform to the ideal described by the standard, which is taken to reflect the animal's suitability for its original function and for breeding.

Dog shows

In dog shows governed by bodies such as the American Kennel Club, the Fédération Cynologique Internationale, and The Kennel Club (UK), entered dogs are presented by handlers who stack (pose) the dog and gait it around the ring so the judge can examine its structure and movement. Judges inspect each dog by hand and in motion, awarding class placements; winners advance through successive levels — best of breed or variety, group judging (e.g. Sporting, Working, Terrier, Toy, Non-Sporting, Herding, Hound), and finally Best in Show. Dogs accumulate points or challenge certificates toward championship titles. Famous shows include the Westminster Kennel Club Dog Show in the United States and Crufts in the United Kingdom.

Other species

Cat shows judge pedigree cats against breed standards in "rings" run by individual judges. Horse halter and breed shows evaluate conformation in hand, distinct from ridden performance classes; livestock shows at agricultural fairs similarly judge cattle, sheep, pigs, and goats on structural correctness and breed character, often as part of youth programs such as 4-H and FFA.

Criticism

Conformation showing has been criticized for rewarding exaggerated physical traits that compromise health and welfare in some breeds — a debate brought to prominence by the 2008 documentary Pedigree Dogs Exposed — and for narrowing gene pools. Kennel clubs have responded by revising some breed standards, introducing veterinary checks for winning dogs at major shows, and promoting health testing.
