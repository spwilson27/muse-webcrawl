List of comic book supervillain debuts

This is a list of supervillains debuting in comic books, organized by era: the Golden Age (1938–1956), Silver Age (1956–1970), Bronze Age (1970–1985), and Modern Age (1985–present). First-appearance issues of major villains are among the most collectible comics.

Detective Comics #140 introduced the Riddler in 1948.
