Claw crane

A claw crane, also known as a claw machine, crane game, or UFO catcher (in Japan), is a type of arcade game in which the player operates a mechanical claw to attempt to grab prizes, typically plush toys or other merchandise, inside a glass-fronted cabinet.

Gameplay

The player inserts coins or tokens, moves the claw over a target prize using a joystick or buttons within a time limit, then drops the claw. The claw attempts to grip and carry the prize to a chute; success depends on grip strength and positioning.

Regulation

Because claw strength can be programmed to vary, making wins largely dependent on machine settings, claw machines are treated as games of chance in some jurisdictions and are subject to gambling or amusement-device regulations.

History

Crane games derive from early 20th-century steam-shovel novelty machines; they became hugely popular in Japan from the 1980s, where Sega's UFO Catcher (1985) gave the genre its Japanese name.
