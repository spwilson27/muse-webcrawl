μTorrent, or uTorrent (see pronunciation note), is a proprietary adware BitTorrent client owned and developed by Rainberry, Inc. The "μ" (Greek letter mu) in its name refers to the prefix meaning "micro-", alluding to the program's minimal resource footprint: the original client was designed to use as little CPU, memory, and disk space as possible while offering functionality comparable to larger clients such as Vuze and BitComet.

History

μTorrent was created in 2005 by Swedish programmer Ludvig Strigeus ("Ludde"). In December 2006, Strigeus sold the program to BitTorrent Inc., the company behind the official BitTorrent client, and μTorrent's code base came to underpin the official client. For many years it was the most widely used BitTorrent client worldwide, with well over 100 million users at its peak.

Features and criticism

The client supports magnet links, peer exchange, protocol encryption, RSS auto-downloading, bandwidth scheduling, and the Micro Transport Protocol (µTP). It has also attracted criticism for bundled advertising and optional third-party software offers, and in 2015 for the silent installation of a cryptocurrency miner with a partner offer, an episode after which the company apologised and removed the software. Since 2018 its development and distribution have been handled by Rainberry, Inc., a BitTorrent-affiliated company associated with the TRON cryptocurrency ecosystem.

Pronunciation

Strigeus has said he pronounces it "micro torrent", though it is commonly read as "you torrent".
