Combi steamer

A combi steamer (also called a combi-steam oven, combi oven, or combination oven) is a professional cooking appliance that combines the functions of a convection oven and a steam cooker in a single unit. Combi steamers are widely used in commercial kitchens, restaurants, hotels, hospitals, and catering operations because they can perform a large share of all cooking processes — roasting, steaming, blanching, poaching, baking, and reheating — in one device.

How it works

A combi steamer circulates hot air with a fan, like a convection oven, while also generating steam that is injected into the cooking chamber. The combination of heat and humidity can be regulated precisely: the appliance can operate in pure steam mode (around 100 °C), pure convection mode (up to about 250–300 °C), or a combined mode in which steam and hot air are used together. Combined mode prevents food from drying out, reduces cooking losses, and produces crisp surfaces while keeping interiors moist.

Steam generation

Steam is produced either by a built-in boiler (boiler-based systems) or by spraying water onto a heated surface or fan wheel inside the chamber (injection or "boilerless" systems). Modern units include automatic cleaning programs, core-temperature probes, and programmable cooking profiles that chain several steps with different temperatures and humidity levels.

Advantages

Because one combi steamer replaces separate steamers, convection ovens, and sometimes kettles or tilting pans, it saves kitchen space and energy. Gentle steam cooking preserves vitamins, colour, and weight in vegetables and fish, while Delta-T and low-temperature cooking modes allow slow overnight cooking of large roasts. Regeneration (reheating plated or bulk food to serving temperature without further cooking) is another common application in banqueting and cook-chill production.

History and manufacturers

The first combi steamer was developed in the 1970s by the German manufacturer Rational, which introduced the Combi-Steamer in 1976. Competitors such as Convotherm, Electrolux Professional, MKN, and Alto-Shaam subsequently developed their own combination ovens, and the technology became standard equipment in professional kitchens worldwide.
