National League North

National League North
Founded: 2004
Country: England (occasionally Wales)
Number of teams: 24
Level on pyramid: 6

The National League North, known for sponsorship purposes as the National League North, is a division of the National League in English football. Together with the National League South, it forms the sixth tier of the English football league system, immediately below the National League itself.

Structure

The division was created in 2004 as part of a restructuring of non-League football, originally under the name Conference North. It contains 24 clubs drawn from northern England and the Midlands, with the champion promoted automatically to the National League and a second promotion place decided by play-offs. Bottom clubs are relegated to the Northern Premier League or the Southern League depending on geography.
