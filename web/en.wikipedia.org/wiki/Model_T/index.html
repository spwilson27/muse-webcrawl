Ford Model T

The Ford Model T is an automobile that was produced by the Ford Motor Company from October 1, 1908, to May 26, 1927. It is generally regarded as the first mass-affordable automobile, which made car travel available to middle-class Americans. Nicknamed the "Tin Lizzie", about 15 million were built, making it the most widely sold car in history until the Volkswagen Beetle surpassed it in 1972.

Design

The Model T was designed by a team including Childe Harold Wills, Joseph Galamb and Eugene Farkas. It had a front-mounted 2.9-litre four-cylinder engine producing about 20 horsepower, driving the rear wheels through a two-speed planetary transmission. It featured a transverse leaf-spring suspension, a high ground clearance suited to poor roads, and extensive use of tough, light vanadium steel. Fuel consumption and ruggedness made it usable as a car, truck, tractor and stationary engine on farms across America.

Mass production

Ford revolutionised manufacturing with the moving assembly line at the Highland Park plant in 1913, cutting chassis assembly time from 12½ hours to about 93 minutes. Prices fell steadily: a touring car that cost $850 in 1908 sold for under $300 by the mid-1920s. In 1914 Ford introduced the "$5 day" wage, which both reduced worker turnover and enabled his own employees to buy the cars they built.

Colour

From 1914 to 1925 the Model T was famously available only in black, because black japan enamel dried fastest on the production line — the origin of Henry Ford's remark that a customer "can have any colour he wants, so long as it is black".

End of production

By the mid-1920s competition from Chevrolet and others, and consumer demand for comfort and style, eroded the Model T's dominance. Production ended in 1927 and Ford shut down for months to retool for its successor, the Model A.

Legacy

The Model T was named the most influential car of the 20th century in the 1999 Car of the Century competition. It put the world on wheels, transformed manufacturing, and reshaped American society and landscape.
