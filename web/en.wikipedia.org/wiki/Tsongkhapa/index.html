Tsongkhapa

Tsongkhapa (1357–1419), also known as Je Rinpoche, was a Tibetan Buddhist monk, philosopher and tantric yogi, founder of the Gelug school of Tibetan Buddhism.

Life and works

Born in Amdo, Tsongkhapa studied with masters of all Tibetan traditions, emphasising monastic discipline (vinaya), logic and the Madhyamaka philosophy of emptiness. His major work, the Lamrim Chenmo (Great Treatise on the Stages of the Path), and his founding of Ganden Monastery (1409) and the Monlam Chenmo festival in Lhasa shaped Tibetan Buddhism. His disciples' lineage produced the Dalai Lamas.
