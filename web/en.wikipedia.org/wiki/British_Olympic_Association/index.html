British Olympic Association
Code: GBR
Created: 1905
Headquarters: London

The British Olympic Association (BOA) is the National Olympic Committee for the United Kingdom, responsible for organising and overseeing the participation of athletes from Great Britain and Northern Ireland — competing as Team GB — at the Olympic Games and other events.

History

The BOA was founded in 1905 and helped organise the 1908 and 1948 London Olympic Games, and the London 2012 Games in partnership with LOCOG. It is funded entirely by private sponsorship and fundraising, receiving no government or lottery funding for its Olympic operations.

Role

The association selects, leads and supports Team GB delegations, promotes Olympic values in the UK, and works with national governing bodies on preparation for Summer, Winter and Youth Olympic Games.
