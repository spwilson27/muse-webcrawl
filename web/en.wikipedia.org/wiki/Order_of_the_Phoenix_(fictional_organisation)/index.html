Order of the Phoenix (fictional organisation)

The Order of the Phoenix is a fictional secret society in J. K. Rowling's Harry Potter series. Founded by Albus Dumbledore, it was dedicated to fighting the dark wizard Lord Voldemort and his followers, the Death Eaters, during both of Voldemort's campaigns.

History within the series

Dumbledore formed the original Order during the First Wizarding War (1970s–1981). Its members included James and Lily Potter, Sirius Black, Remus Lupin and Frank and Alice Longbottom; many were killed or incapacitated before Voldemort's first fall. When Voldemort returned in 1995 (in Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire), Dumbledore reconstituted the Order, headquartered at 12 Grimmauld Place, London, as revealed in Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix (2003).

Members and role

Members of the revived Order include Minerva McGonagall, Severus Snape (acting as a double agent), Molly and Arthur Weasley, Nymphadora Tonks, Kingsley Shacklebolt and Mad-Eye Moody. The Order guards prophecies and Harry Potter himself, escorts him between safe houses, and fights in the Battle of Hogwarts, where Voldemort is finally defeated. Membership is restricted to adult witches and wizards, which leads Harry and his friends to form the parallel student group Dumbledore's Army.
