Honda Civic Type R

The Honda Civic Type R (Japanese: ホンダ・シビックタイプR) is a series of high-performance hatchback models based on the Honda Civic, built by Honda since 1997. Type R vehicles are designed for a purer driving experience, typically featuring a lightened and stiffened body, a specially tuned engine, upgraded brakes and chassis, and Honda's red "H" badge.

First generation (EK9, 1997–2000)

The first Civic Type R was based on the sixth-generation Civic hatchback and sold only in Japan. Its hand-ported B16B 1.6-litre engine produced 182 hp (136 kW) at 8,200 rpm, among the highest specific outputs of any production naturally aspirated engine of its time.

Later generations

The EP3 (2001–2005) was built in Swindon, England, and exported to Japan. The FN2 and FD2 (2007–2011) split into European hatchback and Japanese sedan versions. The FK2 (2015–2017) introduced turbocharging with a 2.0-litre VTEC Turbo. The FK8 (2017–2021) was the first Type R sold globally, including North America, producing 306–316 hp, and set front-wheel-drive lap records at the Nürburgring.

FL5 (2022–present)

The sixth generation, based on the eleventh-generation Civic and built in Japan, produces 315 hp (US specification) with a six-speed manual transmission as the only gearbox, and continued the model's front-wheel-drive lap-record exploits.
