Bob Woodward (born 1943) is an American investigative journalist with the Washington Post since 1971, famed for his reporting with Carl Bernstein on the Watergate scandal, which won the Post the 1973 Pulitzer Prize for Public Service and led toward President Nixon's resignation. He became the archetype of the Washington insider-reporter and author of many bestselling books based on deep-background interviewing.

Books and method

From All the President's Men (1974, with Bernstein) through books on the Supreme Court, the CIA, and successive presidencies — including the Trump-era Fear, Rage and Peril — Woodward's volumes are events in Washington publishing, admired for access and criticised for credulity toward sources' self-presentations.
