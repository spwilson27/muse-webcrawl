Cook is a British comedian and podcaster.

Matthew Cook (born 1973) is a British comedian, actor and podcaster. He is best known for co-creating and presenting the podcast Pop Bitch-style entertainment and for stand-up appearances on the British comedy circuit. He has also worked as a writer and actor on television and radio comedy.

Career

Cook trained in the performing arts and began appearing on the stand-up circuit in the early 2000s. He co-founded the Pop Bitch podcast and has performed at venues including the Edinburgh Festival Fringe, where he presented solo shows. His credits include the sketch group The Committee and writing for television programmes such as Mock the Week and Have I Got News for You formats. He is also known for the Two Men Who Knew Too Much podcast, co-hosted with fellow comedian.
