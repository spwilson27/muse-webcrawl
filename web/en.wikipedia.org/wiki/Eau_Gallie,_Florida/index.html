Eau Gallie, Florida

Eau Gallie is a section of the city of Melbourne in Brevard County, Florida, United States, formerly an independent city. It was incorporated in 1860 and merged with Melbourne in 1969. Eau Gallie takes its unusual name from a coquina rock outcropping (in French, eau meaning "water" and gallie suggesting rocky water) at its site on the Indian River Lagoon.

History

The area was first settled in the 1850s, and the town grew around citrus groves, pineapple plantations and the river wharves. Kentucky Military Institute established a winter campus in Eau Gallie in the early twentieth century. Florida Institute of Technology was founded nearby in 1958 as Brevard Engineering College, and Eau Gallie's high school, opened in 1964, still bears the historic city's name. Following a referendum, Eau Gallie and Melbourne consolidated on 15 July 1969.

Eau Gallie Arts District

The historic downtown around Highland Avenue has been revitalised as the Eau Gallie Arts District (EGAD), with galleries, studios, murals, restaurants and the Foosaner Art Museum and Eau Gallie Public Library nearby. The district hosts art walks and festivals, and landmarks include the Eau Gallie Causeway across the Indian River and the old Eau Gallie Yacht Club.
