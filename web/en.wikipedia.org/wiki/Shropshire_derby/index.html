Shropshire derby

The Shropshire derby is a football rivalry in the English county of Shropshire, most notably contested between Shrewsbury Town and AFC Telford United, the county's two largest clubs, based about 13 miles apart.

History

Shrewsbury, a long-standing Football League club, have historically played at a higher level than Telford, so league meetings have been rare; the clubs have met mainly in the FA Cup, the Football League Trophy, and the Shropshire Senior Cup, which Shrewsbury have dominated. Earlier forms of the derby involved the original Telford United and, in non-league football, clubs such as Oswestry Town.

Other Shropshire fixtures

Matches between Shrewsbury and Wrexham are sometimes grouped with cross-border derbies, though Wrexham is in Wales.
