Glossary of general topology

This glossary of general topology lists definitions of terms used across topology, the branch of mathematics concerned with spaces, continuity, and closeness.

Selected terms

  • Topological space — a set equipped with a topology: a collection of "open" sets closed under arbitrary unions and finite intersections.
  • Continuous function — a map between spaces under which the preimage of every open set is open.
  • Compact — a space in which every open cover has a finite subcover.
  • Connected — a space that cannot be split into two disjoint nonempty open sets.
  • Hausdorff (T2) — a space in which any two distinct points have disjoint neighbourhoods.
  • Homeomorphism — a continuous bijection with a continuous inverse; two spaces related by one are topologically identical.
  • Basis — a collection of open sets from which every open set can be built by union.
  • Closure — the smallest closed set containing a given set.
