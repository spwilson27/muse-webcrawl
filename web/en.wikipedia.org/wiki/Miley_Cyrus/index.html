Miley Ray Cyrus (born Destiny Hope Cyrus; November 23, 1992) is an American singer, songwriter and actress. She rose to fame playing the title character in the Disney Channel series Hannah Montana (2006–2011), which made her a teen idol.

Career

Cyrus's solo music career began with the pop albums Meet Miley Cyrus (2007) and Breakout (2008). She shed her family-friendly image with the controversial album Bangerz (2013), featuring the hit singles "We Can't Stop" and "Wrecking Ball", her first number-one single on the Billboard Hot 100. Later albums include Younger Now (2017), Plastic Hearts (2020) and Endless Summer Vacation (2023), the latter containing "Flowers", which won the Grammy Award for Record of the Year.

Personal life

Cyrus is the daughter of country singer Billy Ray Cyrus. She founded the Happy Hippie Foundation, a non-profit supporting homeless and LGBTQ youth.
