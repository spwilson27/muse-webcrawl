Euclid (spacecraft)

Euclid is a wide-angle space telescope with a 600-megapixel camera to record visible light, a near-infrared spectrometer, and photometer, developed by the European Space Agency (ESA) and the Euclid Consortium. Launched on 1 July 2023 aboard a SpaceX Falcon 9, its mission is to explore the evolution of the dark universe and the nature of dark energy and dark matter.

Euclid
Operator: ESA
Launch: 1 July 2023
Orbit: Sun–Earth L2 halo
Telescope: 1.2 m Korsch
Mission: ~6 years

Mission

Euclid will survey more than a third of the sky, observing billions of galaxies out to 10 billion light-years, to map the geometry of the universe and measure weak gravitational lensing and baryon acoustic oscillations.

Instruments

VIS records high-resolution visible images for weak lensing; NISP measures near-infrared photometry and spectroscopy for galaxy redshifts.

Results

First images were released in November 2023, showing the Horsehead Nebula, the Perseus Cluster, and other targets in exceptional detail.
