Peter O'Toole

Born: Peter James O'Toole, 2 August 1932, Leeds, England

Died: 14 December 2013 (aged 81), London, England

Occupation: Actor

Known for: Lawrence of Arabia; Becket; The Lion in Winter

Peter James O'Toole (2 August 1932 – 14 December 2013) was an English actor. He achieved international stardom playing T. E. Lawrence in David Lean's Lawrence of Arabia (1962), receiving the first of eight Academy Award nominations for Best Actor — a record for nominations without a competitive win, which ended with an Honorary Academy Award in 2003.

Career

Raised in Leeds and trained at RADA, O'Toole made his name in Shakespeare at the Bristol Old Vic before Lawrence of Arabia made him a star. He played Henry II twice to great acclaim — in Becket (1964) and The Lion in Winter (1968) — and starred in What's New Pussycat? (1965), How to Steal a Million (1966), The Ruling Class (1972), The Stunt Man (1980) and My Favorite Year (1982). His later work included the voice of Anton Ego in Pixar's Ratatouille (2007) and a role in the television series The Tudors.

Famous for his charisma, voice and hell-raising lifestyle alongside peers such as Richard Burton and Richard Harris, O'Toole survived serious illness in the 1970s, giving up alcohol after surgery. He announced his retirement from acting in July 2012 and died in London the following year, aged 81.
