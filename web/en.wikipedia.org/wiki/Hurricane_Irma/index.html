Hurricane Irma was an extremely powerful Cape Verde hurricane that struck the Caribbean and Florida in September 2017. It was the strongest Atlantic hurricane ever recorded outside the Gulf of Mexico and Caribbean Sea, maintaining peak winds of 185 mph for 37 hours — the longest any tropical cyclone had sustained such intensity.

Impacts

Irma devastated Barbuda, Saint Martin and the British Virgin Islands as a Category 5 storm, then grazed Cuba before making landfall at Cudjoe Key, Florida, as a Category 4, and again near Marco Island. Storm surge, flooding and wind caused widespread destruction; more than 6.5 million Floridians were ordered to evacuate, one of the largest evacuations in US history.

Records

At the time, Irma was among the costliest Atlantic hurricanes on record. The name Irma was retired by the World Meteorological Organization and replaced with Idalia.
