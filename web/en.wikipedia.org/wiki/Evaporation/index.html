Evaporation

Evaporation is a type of vaporization that occurs on the surface of a liquid as it changes into the gas phase. The surrounding gas must not be saturated with the evaporating substance. Molecules at the surface with enough kinetic energy escape, so evaporation cools the remaining liquid — the principle behind sweating and evaporative coolers.

Factors

Rate of evaporation increases with temperature, surface area, airflow over the surface, and lower humidity and vapour pressure; it decreases with higher atmospheric pressure and stronger intermolecular forces. Evapotranspiration from land and plants is a major part of the water cycle.

Applications

Evaporation is used in drying, salt production from seawater pans, cooling towers, distillation, thin-film deposition and evaporative cooling of buildings and the human body.
