MessagePad

The MessagePad is a series of personal digital assistant (PDA) devices developed by Apple Computer, Inc. for the Newton platform. The first device, the MessagePad (later called the MessagePad 100), was released in 1993.

Features

The MessagePad devices ran the Newton OS and featured handwriting recognition via a stylus on a touch-sensitive screen. Later models, including the MessagePad 2000 and 2100, offered increased performance and expandability.

Discontinuation

The Newton platform and MessagePad line were discontinued in 1998 as part of Steve Jobs's streamlining of Apple's product line after his return to the company.
