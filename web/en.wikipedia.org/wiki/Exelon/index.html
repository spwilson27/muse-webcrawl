Exelon Corporation is an American Fortune 100 energy company headquartered in Chicago, Illinois. It is one of the largest electric utility holding companies in the United States by revenue and operates regulated electricity and natural gas utilities that serve approximately 10.7 million customers in Illinois, Pennsylvania, Maryland, New Jersey, Delaware, and the District of Columbia.

History

Exelon was formed on October 20, 2000, by the merger of PECO Energy Company of Philadelphia and Commonwealth Edison (ComEd) of Chicago, the energy subsidiaries of Unicom Corporation. The merger created the largest nuclear power plant operator in the United States.

In 2012, Exelon merged with Constellation Energy Group, expanding its retail and generation businesses. In 2016, Exelon completed its acquisition of Pepco Holdings, bringing the Washington, D.C.-area utility Pepco, Delmarva Power, and Atlantic City Electric into the company. In February 2022, Exelon separated its competitive power generation business, which was spun off as an independent company, Constellation Energy, leaving Exelon as a purely regulated transmission and distribution utility holding company.

Operations

Exelon's regulated utilities include Commonwealth Edison (ComEd) in northern Illinois, PECO Energy in southeastern Pennsylvania, Baltimore Gas and Electric (BGE) in Maryland, Pepco in the District of Columbia and Maryland, Delmarva Power in Delaware and Maryland, and Atlantic City Electric in southern New Jersey. Together these utilities operate hundreds of thousands of miles of electric distribution lines and natural gas pipelines.
