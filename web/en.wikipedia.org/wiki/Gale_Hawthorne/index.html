Gale Hawthorne is a fictional character in Suzanne Collins's The Hunger Games trilogy. He is Katniss Everdeen's best friend and hunting partner from District 12, and a central figure in the rebellion against the Capitol.

Character biography

Gale is the eldest of four children; his father died in the same mine explosion that killed Katniss's father. As a teenager he supports his family by hunting illegally in the woods outside District 12 and trading in the Hob, the district's black market. He and Katniss provide for their families together and share a deep bond, which becomes complicated by Katniss's relationship with Peeta Mellark during and after the Hunger Games.

In Catching Fire and Mockingjay, Gale becomes an active revolutionary. After District 12 is bombed he leads survivors to safety, then joins District 13's military, working with Beetee on weapons design. His willingness to use ruthless tactics — including a double-tap bombing strategy attributed with killing Katniss's sister Prim — estranges him from Katniss. After the war he takes a military position in District 2.

Portrayal and reception

Gale was portrayed by Australian actor Liam Hemsworth in the four-film adaptation series (2012–2015). Critics and fans frequently discussed the character as one vertex of the series' love triangle, while analyses of Mockingjay have focused on his arc as an exploration of radicalization and the moral costs of war.
