Sir Stelios Haji-Ioannou (Greek: Στέλιος Χατζηιωάννου; born 14 February 1967) is a Greek Cypriot entrepreneur widely known as the founder of the low-cost airline easyJet and the Stelmar shipping line, as well as the owner of the easyGroup brand, through which he licenses a range of "easy" branded ventures.

Born in Athens to shipping magnate Loucas Haji-Ioannou, he worked in his father's company before founding Stelmar Tankers in 1992. In 1995, aged 28, he founded easyJet with a loan from his father, initially operating two leased aircraft from London Luton. The airline grew into one of Europe's largest carriers; easyGroup retains a substantial shareholding and licenses the brand.

Business interests

Through easyGroup, Haji-Ioannou licenses brands including easyHotel, easyCar, easyGym, easyCoffee and easyStorage. He has had periodic disputes with easyJet's board over strategy, dividends and aircraft orders, notably during the COVID-19 pandemic, when he attempted to remove directors in 2020.

Philanthropy and honours

He was knighted in 2006 for services to entrepreneurship. He lives in Monaco and is a signatory of the Giving Pledge. His foundation funds scholarships and bi-communal reconciliation work in Cyprus.
