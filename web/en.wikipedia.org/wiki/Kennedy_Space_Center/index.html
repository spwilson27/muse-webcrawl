Kennedy Space Center

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Vehicle Assembly Building, Kennedy Space Center.

Kennedy Space Center (KSC) is one of NASA's ten field centres and, since 1968, the primary launch site for American human spaceflight. Located on Merritt Island, Florida, next to the Air Force's Cape Canaveral launch range, it is named for President John F. Kennedy, who set the goal of landing on the Moon.

KSC's Launch Complex 39 was built for the Saturn V: its Vehicle Assembly Building — one of the largest buildings in the world by volume — stacked Apollo, Skylab, every Space Shuttle and now assembles the Space Launch System for Artemis program missions. Pad 39A, from which Apollo 11 departed, is leased to SpaceX for Falcon 9 and Falcon Heavy launches. The centre sits within a wildlife refuge, and launches share the island with alligators, manatees and migratory birds.
