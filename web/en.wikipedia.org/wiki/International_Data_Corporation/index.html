International Data Corporation

IDC
Founded: 1964
Founder: Patrick J. McGovern
Headquarters: Needham, Massachusetts, US
Industry: Market research

International Data Corporation (IDC) is a global provider of market intelligence, advisory services and events for the information technology, telecommunications and consumer technology markets. Founded in 1964 by Patrick J. McGovern in Newtonville, Massachusetts, IDC is a wholly owned subsidiary of International Data Group (IDG).

Activities

IDC employs more than a thousand analysts worldwide who track technology markets in over 100 countries. Its quarterly trackers of shipments of personal computers, smartphones, servers and other products are widely cited in the press and used by manufacturers to measure market share. The firm is also known for its forecasts of worldwide IT spending and its "Digital Universe" studies of global data growth.
