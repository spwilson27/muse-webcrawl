Stanislaus County is a county in the San Joaquin Valley of California, United States. Its county seat and largest city is Modesto. The county is a major agricultural producer, known for almonds, dairy products, poultry and walnuts.

History

The county was formed in 1854 from part of Tuolumne County and named after the Stanislaus River, itself named for Estanislao, a Native American leader who resisted Mexican rule in the 1820s.

Communities

Cities include Modesto, Turlock, Ceres, Riverbank, Oakdale and Patterson. The county is crossed by the Tuolumne and Stanislaus rivers and by Highway 99.
