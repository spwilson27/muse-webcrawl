Catharina-Amalia Beatrix Carmen Victoria, Princess of Orange (born 7 December 2003) is the heir apparent to the throne of the Kingdom of the Netherlands. She is the eldest child of King Willem-Alexander and Queen Máxima, and a granddaughter of the former queen, Beatrix. She became heir apparent when her father ascended the throne on 30 April 2013, taking the title Princess of Orange, traditionally borne by the Dutch heir.

Early life and education

Catharina-Amalia was born in The Hague and baptised in 2004; her godparents include Crown Princess Victoria of Sweden and her uncle Prince Constantijn. She attended public primary school in Wassenaar and the Christelijk Gymnasium Sorghvliet in The Hague, graduating in 2021. After a gap year, she began studies at the University of Amsterdam in 2022, pursuing a bachelor's degree combining politics, psychology, law and economics (PPLE).

Public role

On turning eighteen in December 2021, she took her seat in the advisory division of the Council of State, as provided by the Dutch constitution. She undertook her first official foreign tour in early 2023, visiting the Dutch Caribbean islands with her parents. In 2024 it was disclosed that threats against her had forced her to live in Madrid for a period during her first university year, an episode that drew public sympathy and debate about royal security.

Titles

She is styled Her Royal Highness the Princess of Orange, Princess of the Netherlands, Princess of Orange-Nassau. If she succeeds, she would become the fourth consecutive reigning queen in the direct line since Wilhelmina.
