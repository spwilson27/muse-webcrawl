Marina Bay, Singapore

Marina Bay is a bay located in the Central Area of Singapore, surrounded by the perimeter of four planning areas: Downtown Core, Marina East, Marina South and Straits View. The area around the bay is a 360-hectare extension of the adjacent Central Business District, largely created by land reclamation from the 1970s onwards.

Development

Landmark developments on the bay include the Marina Bay Sands integrated resort (2010), the Marina Bay Financial Centre, Asia Square, Gardens by the Bay, and the ArtScience Museum. In 2008 the Marina Barrage was completed across the mouth of Marina Channel, converting the bay into a freshwater reservoir and a venue for water sports.

Events

The bay hosts Singapore’s National Day celebrations at The Float @ Marina Bay and the Marina Bay Street Circuit, home of the Singapore Grand Prix, the first night race in Formula One history. Annual events include the i Light Marina Bay sustainable light art festival and the Marina Bay Singapore Countdown.
