Port of Tanjung Perak

The Port of Tanjung Perak is the principal seaport of Surabaya, East Java, Indonesia, and the main gateway port for the eastern half of the Indonesian archipelago. It lies on the northern coast of Surabaya, facing the island of Madura across the Madura Strait.

History

Surabaya's traditional anchorage was in the Kali Mas river, but silting led the Dutch colonial government to build a modern deep-water harbour at Tanjung Perak in the early twentieth century; construction began in 1910 and the new wharves opened progressively from 1917, designed by the engineer Gerco Wijker. It quickly became one of the great ports of the Netherlands East Indies, serving the sugar industry of East Java.

Facilities

The port includes general-cargo and bulk terminals, the Jamrud, Mirah and Nilam wharves, the Terminal Petikemas Surabaya container terminal, and the newer Teluk Lamong terminal opened in 2014 to relieve congestion. Ferries and passenger ships connect Surabaya with Madura, Kalimantan, Sulawesi and eastern Indonesia.
