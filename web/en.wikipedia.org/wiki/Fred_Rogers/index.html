Fred Rogers

Fred McFeely Rogers (March 20, 1928 – February 27, 2003) was an American television host, author, producer, and Presbyterian minister. He was the creator, showrunner, and host of the preschool television series Mister Rogers' Neighborhood, which ran from 1968 to 2001.

Career

Rogers developed his gentle, direct approach to children's emotional and social education in early work at WQED Pittsburgh and in Canada before launching the Neighborhood on public television. He wrote nearly all the music for the program, including its theme "Won't You Be My Neighbor?".

Legacy

He received the Presidential Medal of Freedom in 2002 and a Lifetime Achievement Emmy. His 1969 testimony before the U.S. Senate in defense of public broadcasting funding is widely remembered. He was portrayed by Tom Hanks in the 2019 film A Beautiful Day in the Neighborhood.
