Trulia

Trulia provides property listings and neighbourhood information

Trulia is an American online real estate marketplace that provides information about homes for sale and rent, property values, and neighbourhood conditions in the United States. It is a subsidiary of the Zillow Group.

History

Trulia was founded in 2005 by Pete Flint and Sami Inkinen, and launched its website that year, initially providing map-based property search. The company grew through the late 2000s, acquired the rental platform Movity in 2010 and the real-estate software company Market Leader in 2013, and went public on the New York Stock Exchange in 2012.

In 2014 Zillow announced it would acquire Trulia for about US$3.5 billion in stock; the deal closed in February 2015, creating the Zillow Group, though the Trulia brand and website continued to operate alongside Zillow's.

Services

The site and apps let users search properties, view price histories and estimated values, and explore neighbourhood data such as crime statistics, school information, commute times and local amenities, including overlays for flood and wildfire risk.
