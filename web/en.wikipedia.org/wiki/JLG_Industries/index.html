JLG Industries

JLG Industries, Inc. is an American manufacturer of access equipment, including aerial work platforms (boom lifts and scissor lifts) and telehandlers. Headquartered in McConnellsburg, Pennsylvania, it is a subsidiary of Oshkosh Corporation.

History

The company was founded in 1969 by John L. Grove, who had left the Grove Manufacturing crane business and set out to build a safer way for workers to work at height. JLG sold its first aerial work platform in 1970 and grew to become the world's largest maker of such equipment. It acquired the Gradall and SkyTrak telehandler lines and in 2006 was bought by Oshkosh Truck Corporation for about $3.2 billion.

Products

JLG's range covers electric and engine-powered scissor lifts, telescopic and articulating boom lifts reaching over 185 feet, vertical masts, and telehandlers sold under the JLG and SkyTrak brands, together with the TRIPLE-L trailer line.
