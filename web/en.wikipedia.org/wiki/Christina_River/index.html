Christina River

The Christina River is a tributary of the Delaware River, approximately 35 miles (56 km) long, in northern Delaware and southeastern Pennsylvania in the United States.

Course and history

The river rises in Chester County, Pennsylvania, and flows southeast into Delaware through Newark, then east through Wilmington, where it is joined by Brandywine Creek, before emptying into the Delaware River. It is named after Christina, Queen of Sweden.

The mouth of the Christina was the site of Fort Christina, founded in 1638, the first permanent European settlement in the Delaware Valley and the capital of the New Sweden colony. The Old Swedes Church (Holy Trinity Church), built in 1698 near the site, remains one of the oldest churches in the United States still in use.
