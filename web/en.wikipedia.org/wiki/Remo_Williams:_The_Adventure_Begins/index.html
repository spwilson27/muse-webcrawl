Remo Williams: The Adventure Begins, also released as Remo: Unarmed and Dangerous in some territories, is a 1985 American action-adventure film directed by Guy Hamilton. It is based on The Destroyer series of pulp novels by Warren Murphy and Richard Sapir. The film stars Fred Ward as Remo Williams, a New York City police officer recruited into a secret government organization, and Joel Grey as Chiun, his Korean martial arts master.

Plot

Police officer Remo Williams is recruited by a covert organization called CURE after his death is faked during an undercover operation. CURE answers only to the President and exists to combat corruption beyond the reach of ordinary law. Remo is trained by Chiun, the master of the fictional Korean martial art Sinanju, and is sent to investigate a corrupt defense contractor, Grove Industries, which is developing a satellite weapon system known as HARP.

Production

The film was directed by Guy Hamilton, known for several James Bond films. A notable sequence was filmed at the Statue of Liberty, then undergoing restoration, with scaffolding used for a stunt set piece. Joel Grey, a non-Asian actor, portrayed the Korean character Chiun in makeup, a casting choice that drew criticism.

Reception

The film received mixed reviews and underperformed at the box office, ending plans for a franchise, although a television pilot for a series was produced in 1988. It later developed a cult following.
