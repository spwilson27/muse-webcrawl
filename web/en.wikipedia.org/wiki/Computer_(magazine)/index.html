Computer (magazine)

Computer is an academic journal of the IEEE Computer Society. Established in 1970, it is the society's flagship publication and is sent to all of its members. The magazine covers a wide range of topics across computer science and computer engineering, publishing peer-reviewed articles, columns, and special issues aimed at practitioners as well as researchers.

History

The publication traces its origins to journals sponsored by the IEEE Computer Society's predecessor groups. It was relaunched under the title Computer in 1970, succeeding earlier publications such as the IEEE Computer Group News. Over the decades it has become one of the most widely read computing magazines in the world.

Content

Computer publishes research articles, tutorials, survey papers, and commentary on topics including software engineering, artificial intelligence, computer security, networking, and emerging technologies. Special issues, guest-edited by experts, focus on particular themes. Regular columns cover industry news, standards, education, and the profession.

Editors

The magazine is led by an editor-in-chief appointed by the IEEE Computer Society, supported by associate editors and an editorial board drawn from academia and industry.
