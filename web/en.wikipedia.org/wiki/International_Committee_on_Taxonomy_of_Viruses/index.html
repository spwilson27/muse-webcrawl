International Committee on Taxonomy of Viruses

The International Committee on Taxonomy of Viruses (ICTV) is the body charged with the official classification and naming of viruses, under the International Union of Microbiological Societies.

ICTV
Formation1966
PurposeVirus classification and nomenclature
OutputICTV Report; Virus Metadata Resource

Work

Through study groups and ratified proposals, ICTV maintains a universal virus taxonomy, now headed by ranks such as realm, kingdom and phylum down to species, with binomial species names adopted from 2021. Its Master Species Lists catalogue thousands of recognised viruses, including SARS-CoV-2, which ICTV named in 2020.
