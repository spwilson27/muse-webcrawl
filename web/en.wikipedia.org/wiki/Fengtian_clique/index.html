Fengtian clique

The Fengtian clique was one of the warlord factions that split from the Beiyang Army during the Warlord Era of the Republic of China. Named after Fengtian Province (modern Liaoning), it was led by Zhang Zuolin and controlled Manchuria, with backing from the Empire of Japan.

History

The clique fought the Zhili clique in the Zhili–Anhui War's aftermath and the two Zhili–Fengtian Wars (1922 and 1924), seizing control of Beijing after the second. Its power peaked when Zhang Zuolin became head of the Beiyang government in 1927, but the Northern Expedition forced his retreat to Manchuria, and Japanese Kwantung Army officers assassinated him in the Huanggutun incident of June 1928. His son Zhang Xueliang succeeded him and pledged allegiance to the Nationalist government in the Northeast Flag Replacement of December 1928, ending the clique's independence.
