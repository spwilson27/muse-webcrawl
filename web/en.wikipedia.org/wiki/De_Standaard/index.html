De Standaard

De Standaard (Dutch pronunciation: [də ˈstɑndaːrt], The Standard) is a Flemish daily newspaper published in Belgium by Mediahuis. Founded in 1918, it was historically associated with the Flemish movement and Christian democratic politics, and it is one of Belgium's main Dutch-language quality newspapers.

History

The paper was first published on 4 December 1918, after a wartime predecessor had been suppressed. During the twentieth century it carried the Catholic and Flemish-national motto "Alles voor Vlaanderen, Vlaanderen voor Kristus" (AVV-VVK) on its masthead, a practice discontinued in 1999 as the paper broadened its outlook.

Today

De Standaard is published in tabloid format and is known for its political reporting, cultural coverage, and the weekend magazine dS Weekblad. Its sister publications within Mediahuis include Het Nieuwsblad and Gazet van Antwerpen.
