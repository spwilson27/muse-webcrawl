ISO 3166

ISO 3166 is a standard published by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) that defines codes for the names of countries, dependent territories, special areas of geographical interest, and their principal subdivisions (e.g., provinces or states). The official complete title is Codes for the representation of names of countries and their subdivisions.

Parts

  • ISO 3166-1: country codes—two-letter (alpha-2), three-letter (alpha-3), and three-digit numeric codes for 249 countries and territories
  • ISO 3166-2: codes for the principal subdivisions of all countries coded in part 1
  • ISO 3166-3: codes for country names that have been deleted from ISO 3166-1 since its first publication in 1974

Use

ISO 3166 codes are maintained by the ISO 3166 Maintenance Agency and are used in internet country-code top-level domains (based on alpha-2 codes, e.g. .uk, .de, .jp), vehicle registration plates, machine-readable passports, currency and bibliographic standards, and statistical datasets worldwide. Changes—such as additions for new countries or renamings like Eswatini and North Macedonia—are announced in agency newsletters and incorporated into the ISO Online Browsing Platform.
