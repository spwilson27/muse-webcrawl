ITIL (formerly the Information Technology Infrastructure Library) is a set of detailed practices for IT service management (ITSM) that focuses on aligning IT services with the needs of business. It provides a framework of processes and practices for managing the lifecycle of IT services.

History

ITIL was developed in the late 1980s by the British government's Central Computer and Telecommunications Agency. ITIL v3 (2007) organized practice around a service lifecycle of strategy, design, transition, operation and continual improvement. ITIL 4 (2019) reframed the guidance around a Service Value System and value co-creation, integrating ideas from agile, Lean and DevOps.

Practices

Well-known ITIL practices include incident management, problem management, change enablement, and service desk operation. Certification schemes for individuals run from Foundation to Master level.
