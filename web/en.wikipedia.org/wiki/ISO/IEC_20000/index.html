ISO/IEC 20000

ISO/IEC 20000 is an international standard for service management systems (SMS), published jointly by ISO and the IEC. It specifies requirements for an organisation to establish, implement, maintain and continually improve a service management system, and was the first worldwide standard specifically aimed at IT service management (ITSM).

History and structure

The standard grew out of the British standard BS 15000 and was first published in 2005. A major revision, ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018, restructured the requirements to follow the common high-level structure used by other ISO management system standards, making it easier to integrate with ISO 9001 and ISO/IEC 27001. Part 1 contains the auditable requirements used for certification; other parts give guidance on application, scope and process reference models.

Content

The standard covers service planning, design, transition, delivery and improvement, including processes such as incident management, problem management, change control, service level management, capacity management and information security management for services. It is closely aligned with the ITIL framework of service management practices.
