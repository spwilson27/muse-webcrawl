IT service management

IT service management (ITSM) is the set of activities performed by an organization to design, build, deliver, operate and control information technology services offered to customers. Unlike technology-centered management of individual components, ITSM is characterized by a process approach, a focus on services and customers, and continual improvement.

Frameworks and standards

The best-known ITSM framework is ITIL, a body of best-practice guidance. ITSM is also addressed by formal standards: ISO/IEC 20000 is the international standard for service management systems, against which organizations can be certified; it originated in the British standard BS 15000. Other related frameworks include COBIT and Microsoft Operations Framework.

Processes

Typical ITSM processes include incident and problem management, change and release management, service level management, capacity and availability management, and configuration management supported by a configuration management database (CMDB).
