Batwing is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, created by writer Grant Morrison and artist Chris Burnham. The identity was first held by David Zavimbe, a Congolese police officer recruited into Batman Incorporated as the Batman of Africa, and later by Luke Fox, son of Wayne Enterprises executive Lucius Fox, who operates in Gotham City.

Publication history

Batwing debuted in Batman Incorporated #5 (2011) during Morrison's storyline building Bruce Wayne's global network of Batmen. The character received an ongoing series as part of DC's "New 52" relaunch in September 2011, initially written by Judd Winick with art by Ben Oliver, exploring Zavimbe's fight against corruption and warlords in the fictional African city of Tinasha. In 2013 the mantle passed to Luke Fox, introduced by writers Justin Gray and Jimmy Palmiotti, and the series' focus shifted to Gotham. The title ran for 34 issues until 2014. Luke Fox has since appeared as a regular member of the Bat-family in titles such as Detective Comics and The Next Batman storylines.

Fictional biography

David Zavimbe, orphaned by AIDS and conscripted as a child soldier, became a police officer in Tinasha determined to fight lawlessness honestly; Batman supplied him with a powered, winged suit of armour. Luke Fox, an engineer and mixed martial artist, took up a redesigned Batwing suit built with his father's technology. Both incarnations are defined by the high-tech suit, which grants flight, enhanced strength and an array of sensors and non-lethal weapons.

In other media

Luke Fox appears in the television series Batwoman, played by Camrus Johnson, eventually adopting the Batwing identity in the series' later seasons. The character has also appeared in animated form in Batman: Bad Blood (2016).
