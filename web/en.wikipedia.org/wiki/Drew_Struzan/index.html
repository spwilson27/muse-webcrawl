Drew Struzan

Born 14 March 1947, Oregon City, Oregon, U.S.
Occupation Artist, illustrator
Known for Film poster artwork

Drew Struzan (born March 14, 1947) is an American artist and illustrator best known for his painted movie posters. Over a career spanning four decades he created more than 150 posters, becoming the signature poster artist for the films of George Lucas and Steven Spielberg.

Work

Struzan's posters — produced with acrylics and coloured pencil, often built around a glowing montage of the principal characters — include those for Star Wars, Indiana Jones, Back to the Future, E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial, The Goonies, The Shawshank Redemption, Blade Runner (special edition) and the Harry Potter films. He also painted album covers and collectibles art. As photographic and digital poster design displaced illustration, Struzan retired from regular poster work in 2008, returning occasionally for anniversary editions and documentaries about his career, such as Drew: The Man Behind the Poster (2013).
