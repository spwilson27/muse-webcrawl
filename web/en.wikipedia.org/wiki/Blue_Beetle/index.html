Blue Beetle is the name of three fictional superheroes appearing in American comic books published by Fox Comics, Charlton Comics and DC Comics. The original Blue Beetle, archaeologist Dan Garrett, debuted in 1939 and gained powers from a mystical scarab. His successor, Ted Kord, created by Steve Ditko for Charlton in 1966, had no powers and relied on technology and martial arts; he became a popular member of the Justice League, often paired with Booster Gold. The third Blue Beetle, teenager Jaime Reyes, debuted in 2006: the scarab — retconned as an alien weapon of the Reach — bonds to his spine and generates a powered suit of armour.

In other media

Jaime Reyes has appeared in animated series including Batman: The Brave and the Bold and Young Justice, and stars in the 2023 DC Extended Universe film Blue Beetle, directed by Ángel Manuel Soto and starring Xolo Maridueña — the first live-action superhero film led by a Latino character and actor in the DC franchise. Ted Kord's version remains a fan favourite for its comedic Justice League International era.
