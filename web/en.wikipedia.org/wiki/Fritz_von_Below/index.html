Fritz von Below

Fritz Wilhelm Theodor Karl von Below
Born: 23 September 1853, Danzig, Prussia
Died: 23 October 1918, Weimar, Germany
Allegiance: German Empire
Branch: Imperial German Army
Commands: 2nd Army; Army of the Niemen

Fritz Wilhelm Theodor Karl von Below (23 September 1853 – 23 October 1918) was a German general of infantry who commanded armies on both the Eastern and Western Fronts during the First World War.

First World War

Below began the war commanding XXI Corps, then took command of the 2nd Army on the Western Front. In 1915 he was transferred east to command the Army of the Niemen. In 1916 he returned west to lead the German 2nd Army during the Battle of the Somme, where his forces bore the initial brunt of the Anglo-French offensive. He later commanded the 1st Army during the German spring offensive of 1918.

Later life

Below was not related, except distantly, to fellow general Otto von Below. He died in Weimar in October 1918, shortly before the armistice.
