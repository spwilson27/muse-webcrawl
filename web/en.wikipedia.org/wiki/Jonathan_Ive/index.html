Jonathan Ive

Sir Jonathan Paul Ive (born 27 February 1967), known as Jony Ive, is a British-American industrial designer best known as Apple's chief design officer, where he oversaw the design of the iMac, iPod, iPhone, iPad, MacBook and Apple Watch, working closely with Steve Jobs.

Born in London and educated at Newcastle Polytechnic, Ive joined Apple in 1992 and became senior vice president of industrial design in 1997. His minimalist hardware designs, often in aluminium and glass, defined consumer electronics aesthetics for two decades. He left Apple in 2019 to found the design firm LoveFrom. He was knighted in 2012 and served as chancellor of the Royal College of Art.
