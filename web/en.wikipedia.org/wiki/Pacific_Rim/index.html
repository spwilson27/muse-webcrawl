Pacific Rim

Countries bordering the Pacific Ocean

The Pacific Rim comprises the lands around the rim of the Pacific Ocean. The Pacific Basin includes the Pacific Rim and the islands in the Pacific Ocean. The Pacific Rim roughly overlaps with the geological Pacific Ring of Fire.

Geography

The Pacific Rim includes regions in East Asia, Southeast Asia, Oceania, and the western coasts of North America and South America. Major metropolitan areas around the Pacific Rim include Bangkok, Beijing, Brisbane, Busan, Guayaquil, Ho Chi Minh City, Hong Kong, Jakarta, Kaohsiung, Kuala Lumpur, Lima, Los Angeles, Manila, Melbourne, Panama City, Perth, Port Moresby, San Diego, San Francisco, Santiago, Seattle, Seoul, Shanghai, Singapore, Sydney, Taipei, Tokyo, Vancouver, and Vladivostok.

Economics

The Pacific Rim is a major center of world trade and commerce. The Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) forum, founded in 1989, promotes free trade among its member economies around the Pacific. Ports such as Shanghai, Singapore, Hong Kong, Shenzhen, and Busan are among the busiest container ports in the world. Trade agreements such as the Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for Trans-Pacific Partnership (CPTPP) link economies across the region.

Geology

Much of the Pacific Rim coincides with the Ring of Fire, a horseshoe-shaped zone of intense seismic and volcanic activity resulting from plate tectonics, particularly the subduction of the Pacific Plate and surrounding plates beneath neighboring continental plates. The region experiences frequent earthquakes, tsunamis, and volcanic eruptions.
