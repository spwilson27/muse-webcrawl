OrderLepidoptera
FamilyTortricidae

Eidola is a small genus of moths belonging to the family Tortricidae, the leafroller moths. Species in the genus were described from southern Africa, including Madagascar, in the late 19th and early 20th centuries.

Taxonomy

The genus was erected by Edward Meyrick, one of the most prolific describers of microlepidoptera. Tortricidae is a large family of small moths whose larvae typically feed inside rolled leaves, giving the family its common name. Eidola species are small, inconspicuously coloured moths known from relatively few specimens.

Species

Recorded species include Eidola priscilla and Eidola ancylota, both described from Madagascar. As with many small tropical tortricid genera, the biology, larval host plants, and full distribution of these moths remain poorly documented.

See also

Leafroller moths occur worldwide, and Madagascar is noted for a highly endemic moth fauna. Many genera named by Meyrick and his contemporaries still await modern revision.
