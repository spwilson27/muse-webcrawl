Edward Abbey

Edward Abbey
Born: 29 January 1927, Pennsylvania
Died: 14 March 1989, Arizona
Occupation: Author, essayist, park ranger
Notable works: Desert Solitaire, The Monkey Wrench Gang

Edward Paul Abbey (1927–1989) was an American author, essayist and anarchist noted for his advocacy of environmental issues, criticism of public land policies, and views on anarchy. His best-known works are the novel The Monkey Wrench Gang and the non-fiction Desert Solitaire.

Life and work

Abbey worked as a seasonal park ranger, including at Arches National Monument in Utah, experiences recounted in Desert Solitaire (1968), a classic of nature writing about the American Southwest. The Monkey Wrench Gang (1975), about eco-saboteurs defending wilderness — 'monkey-wrenching' — inspired the radical environmental movement, including Earth First! (founded 1980).

Legacy

Controversial for his positions on immigration, industrial tourism and development, Abbey remains an icon of desert preservation and resistance writing. See also Environmentalism.
