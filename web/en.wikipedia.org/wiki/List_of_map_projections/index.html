List of map projections

Map projections
A systematic transformation of latitude and longitude into a plane; every projection distorts some property.

A map projection is a systematic transformation of the latitudes and longitudes of locations from the surface of a sphere or an ellipsoid into locations on a plane. All projections distort area, shape, distance or direction to some degree. This list catalogues projections by type: cylindrical, pseudocylindrical, conic, azimuthal and others.
