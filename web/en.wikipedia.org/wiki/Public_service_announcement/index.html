Public service announcement

A public service announcement (PSA) is a message broadcast or published in the public interest, free of charge, intended to raise awareness or change public behaviour on issues such as health, safety and the environment.

History and format

PSAs became prominent during the Second World War and were promoted in the United States by the Ad Council, founded in 1942, which produced famous campaigns on forest-fire prevention (Smokey Bear), road safety and drug abuse. Broadcasters traditionally donate airtime, and PSAs are usually short spots of 30 or 60 seconds, though print, outdoor and online versions are common.

Themes

Common subjects include vaccination, drink-driving, smoking cessation, disaster preparedness and recycling.
