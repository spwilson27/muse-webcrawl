Mammootty

Mammootty (born Muhammad Kutty Panaparambil Ismail, 7 September 1951) is an Indian actor and film producer who works predominantly in Malayalam cinema, with appearances in Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Hindi films. In a career spanning more than five decades he has acted in over 400 films and is regarded, alongside Mohanlal, as one of the two defining superstars of Malayalam cinema.

Career

Trained as a lawyer, Mammootty made his screen debut in Anubhavangal Paalichakal (1971) and gained prominence in the early 1980s with films such as Ahimsa (1981). His portrayal of a police officer in New Delhi (1987) revived his career and established his mass-hero image. Critically acclaimed performances followed in Oru Vadakkan Veeragatha (1989), Mathilukal (1990), Ponthan Mada (1994) and Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar (2000), in which he played the title role in English.

Awards

Mammootty has won three National Film Awards for Best Actor, seven Kerala State Film Awards and numerous Filmfare Awards South. He was awarded the Padma Shri by the Government of India in 1998 and received honorary doctorates from the University of Kerala and the University of Calicut.

Other work

He is the chairman of Malayalam Communications, which runs the Kairali TV channel, and has been associated with numerous charitable and social causes in Kerala. His son Dulquer Salmaan is also a prominent actor.
