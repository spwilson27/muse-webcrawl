Rita Lee

Rita Lee Jones de Carvalho (31 December 1947 – 8 May 2023), known as Rita Lee, was a Brazilian singer, songwriter, and multi-instrumentalist, widely regarded as the "Queen of Brazilian rock". She rose to fame as a founding member of the tropicália group Os Mutantes.

Born31 December 1947, São Paulo, Brazil
Died8 May 2023, São Paulo, Brazil
GenresRock, tropicália, pop
Associated actsOs Mutantes, Tutti Frutti

Career

Lee co-founded Os Mutantes in 1966, blending Brazilian music with psychedelic rock. After leaving the band in the early 1970s, she formed Tutti Frutti and then pursued a hugely successful solo career with hits such as "Lança Perfume" and "Mania de Você", becoming one of the best-selling Brazilian artists of all time.

Legacy

A bestselling author and animal-rights and environmental activist as well as a musician, Lee is regarded as one of the most influential figures in Brazilian popular music.
