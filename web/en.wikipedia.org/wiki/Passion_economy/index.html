Passion economy

The passion economy is a term, popularised by venture capitalist Li Jin around 2019, for economic activity in which individuals earn income by monetising their skills, knowledge, creativity and personal audiences directly online — through subscriptions, memberships, paid newsletters, courses, livestreams and digital products — rather than through traditional employment or advertising alone.

Features

It is distinguished from the earlier gig economy and influencer model by its emphasis on niche expertise, direct and recurring revenue from fans, and platforms that supply tools for payment, community and distribution — examples include Substack, Patreon, OnlyFans, Teachable and Twitch. Advocates argue it lets creators build sustainable businesses around small, devoted audiences ("1,000 true fans"). Critics note income concentration among top creators, dependence on platform policies and fees, precarity, and the blurring of work and personal life.
