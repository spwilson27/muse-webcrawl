DPG Media

DPG Media Group is a Belgian media company also active in the Netherlands and Denmark. It is owned by the Van Thillo family and is one of the largest media groups in the Benelux, publishing newspapers, magazines, and operating radio stations, television channels and online platforms.

History

The company traces its roots to the newspaper business of the Van Thillo family. It grew through acquisitions, notably the purchase of the Belgian publishing house De Persgroep, publisher of De Morgen and Het Laatste Nieuws. In 2019 De Persgroep changed its name to DPG Media. In the Netherlands the group acquired the newspaper business of TMG (Telegraaf Media Groep) titles including AD, de Volkskrant and Trouw (through Persgroep Nederland). In 2020 DPG Media acquired Sanoma Media Netherlands, adding magazines such as Libelle, Donald Duck and the news site NU.nl.

Activities

DPG Media publishes numerous national and regional newspapers, operates the commercial television channels of DPG Media Belgium (such as VTM), radio stations including Qmusic and Joe, and streaming and classified platforms. In Denmark it owns Berlingske Media, publisher of Berlingske and B.T.
