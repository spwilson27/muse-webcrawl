Oil platform

An oil platform (also called an oil rig, offshore platform, oil production platform, etc.) is a large structure with facilities to extract and process petroleum and natural gas that lie in rock formations beneath the seabed. Many oil platforms will also have facilities to accommodate the workers, often in separate accommodation modules.

Platform types include fixed platforms, compliant towers, semi-submersible platforms, jack-up rigs, drillships, floating production systems, and spar platforms, chosen according to water depth and conditions. The largest platforms operate in water depths of several hundred metres and support hundreds of personnel.
