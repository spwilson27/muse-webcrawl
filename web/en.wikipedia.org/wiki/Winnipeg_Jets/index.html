The Winnipeg Jets are a professional ice hockey team based in Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada. The Jets compete in the National Hockey League (NHL) as a member of the Central Division in the Western Conference. The team is owned by True North Sports & Entertainment and plays its home games at Canada Life Centre.

The franchise was founded in 1999 as the Atlanta Thrashers. In 2011, True North purchased the franchise and relocated it to Winnipeg, reviving the Jets name used by the city's original WHA and NHL franchise from 1972 to 1996, which had moved to Phoenix and become the Coyotes.

History

Atlanta Thrashers (1999–2011)

The Atlanta Thrashers joined the NHL as an expansion team in 1999. The team struggled for most of its existence in Atlanta, reaching the playoffs only once, in 2007, when it was swept in the first round. Star players included Ilya Kovalchuk and Dany Heatley. Persistent financial losses led ownership to seek a buyer who would relocate the team.

Return to Winnipeg (2011–present)

On May 31, 2011, the sale of the Thrashers to True North Sports & Entertainment was announced, and the NHL Board of Governors approved the relocation to Winnipeg. The announcement of the team's name — reviving the "Jets" identity — was made at the 2011 NHL Entry Draft. The Jets played their first regular season game on October 9, 2011, against the Montreal Canadiens, before a sold-out crowd. Fan enthusiasm was intense: season tickets for the inaugural season sold out in 17 minutes.

The Jets reached the playoffs for the first time in the new era in 2015. In 2017–18, led by players such as Mark Scheifele, Blake Wheeler, Patrik Laine, and goaltender Connor Hellebuyck, the Jets finished with the second-best record in the NHL and advanced to the Western Conference Final, the deepest playoff run in franchise history, before losing to the Vegas Golden Knights. Hellebuyck went on to win multiple Vezina Trophies as the league's top goaltender, as well as the Hart Memorial Trophy as league MVP. The "Winnipeg Whiteout" — the tradition of fans wearing white at home playoff games, inherited from the original Jets — remains one of the NHL's most recognizable fan traditions.

Original Jets

The original Winnipeg Jets were founded in 1972 as a charter member of the World Hockey Association, winning three Avco World Trophies. They joined the NHL in the 1979 merger and remained until 1996, when financial pressures in a small market with a weak Canadian dollar forced the sale and relocation of the team to Phoenix. The original Jets are remembered for European stars such as Anders Hedberg and Ulf Nilsson, signed in 1974 in a landmark move, and for Bobby Hull, whose million-dollar signing launched the WHA.
