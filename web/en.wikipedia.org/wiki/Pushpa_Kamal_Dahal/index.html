Pushpa Kamal Dahal (born 11 December 1954), commonly known by his nom de guerre Prachanda ("the fierce one"), is a Nepalese politician who has served three terms as Prime Minister of Nepal. He led the Communist Party of Nepal (Maoist) during the decade-long Nepalese Civil War (1996–2006).

Early life

Dahal was born in Dhikur Pokhari in Kaski District to a Brahmin family. He studied agriculture at the Institute of Agriculture and Animal Science in Rampur, Chitwan, and worked as a schoolteacher before entering underground communist politics in the late 1970s.

Insurgency and peace process

As chairman of the Maoist party, Dahal launched the "People's War" in February 1996, seeking to abolish the monarchy and restructure the Nepali state. The conflict ended with the Comprehensive Peace Accord of 2006.

Prime Minister

After the Maoists won the largest share of seats in the 2008 Constituent Assembly election, Dahal became Prime Minister, resigning in 2009 amid a dispute over the army chief. He served a second term from 2016 to 2017 and a third term beginning in December 2022.
