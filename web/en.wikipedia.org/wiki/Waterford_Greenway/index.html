Waterford Greenway

The Waterford Greenway is a 46 km (29 mi) off-road cycling and walking trail in County Waterford, Ireland, running from Waterford City to Dungarvan along the alignment of the disused Waterford–Mallow railway. It opened in stages between 2016 and 2017 and is Ireland's longest greenway.

Route

The trail follows the River Suir out of Waterford, crosses the countryside through Mount Congreve Gardens and Kilmacthomas, and includes eleven bridges, three viaducts — notably the Kilmacthomas Viaduct — and the Ballyvoyle Tunnel. Along the coast near Dungarvan it offers views over the Comeragh Mountains and the Copper Coast.

History

The railway line closed in 1967. Developed by Waterford City and County Council with national funding, the greenway transformed the corridor into one of Ireland's most popular visitor attractions, drawing several hundred thousand users annually and spurring cafés, bike-hire businesses and accommodation along the route.
