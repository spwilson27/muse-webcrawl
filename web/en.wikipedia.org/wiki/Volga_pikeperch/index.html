Volga pikeperch

The Volga pikeperch (Sander volgensis), also known as the Volga zander, is a species of freshwater fish in the perch family Percidae. It is the smallest of the Eurasian pikeperches.

Description

The Volga pikeperch reaches a maximum length of about 40 cm and lacks the large canine teeth of its close relative the zander (Sander lucioperca). Its body is deeper and its cheeks are scaled. Coloration is grey-green with dark vertical barring, fading with age.

Distribution and ecology

It inhabits the basins of the Caspian, Black and Azov seas — the Volga, Ural, Danube, Kuban and other rivers — preferring large, turbid lowland rivers and brackish estuaries. It feeds on small fish and invertebrates, and spawns in spring over vegetation. It supports local commercial and recreational fisheries but is generally less abundant than the zander.
