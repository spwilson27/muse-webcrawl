Mecca and the Soul Brother

Studio album by Pete Rock & CL Smooth

Released: June 9, 1992

Genre: East Coast hip hop, jazz rap

Label: Elektra

Producer: Pete Rock, DJ Eddie F, Nevelle Hodge

Mecca and the Soul Brother is the debut studio album by American hip hop duo Pete Rock & CL Smooth, released on June 9, 1992, by Elektra Records. It followed their EP All Souled Out (1991) and is widely regarded as one of the greatest hip hop albums of all time.

Background and music

The album is built on producer Pete Rock's dense, horn-driven sample-based production, layering jazz, soul, and funk records, paired with CL Smooth's smooth, reflective rapping. Its best-known track, "They Reminisce Over You (T.R.O.Y.)", is a tribute to the duo's friend Troy Dixon (Trouble T Roy of Heavy D & the Boyz), who died in 1990; the song, built around a saxophone sample from Tom Scott's "Today", is frequently cited among the finest hip hop songs ever recorded.

Reception and legacy

Though only a modest commercial success on release, peaking on the US Billboard charts in 1992, the album's critical reputation grew steadily; The Source and other publications have placed it on numerous "greatest albums" lists, and in 2022 it was among the recordings selected for preservation in the United States National Recording Registry by the Library of Congress.
