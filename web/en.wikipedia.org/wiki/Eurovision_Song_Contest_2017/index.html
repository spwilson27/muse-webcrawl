The Eurovision Song Contest 2017 was the 62nd edition of the annual Eurovision Song Contest. It took place in Kyiv, Ukraine, following the country's victory at the 2016 contest with the song "1944" by Jamala. The contest, hosted by the Public Broadcasting Company of Ukraine (UA:PBC) and the EBU, consisted of two semi-finals on 9 and 11 May and the final on 13 May 2017, presented by Oleksandr Skichko, Volodymyr Ostapchuk, and Timur Miroshnychenko — the first all-male presenting trio in the contest's history.

Controversy

Preparations were overshadowed by a dispute over Russia's entrant, Yulia Samoylova, who was banned from entering Ukraine for having performed in Crimea after its annexation by Russia. Russia ultimately withdrew from the contest. Portugal and Romania returned after a year's absence, while Bosnia and Herzegovina withdrew.

Portugal won for the first time, 53 years after its debut, with the jazz ballad "Amar pelos dois", written by the winner's sister Luísa Sobral. Its 758 points were the highest score ever recorded under the voting system introduced in 2016, topping both the jury and televote rankings.
