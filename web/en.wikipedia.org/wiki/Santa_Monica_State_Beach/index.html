Santa Monica State Beach

Santa Monica State Beach is a California State Park operated by the City of Santa Monica, located along the Pacific coast in Santa Monica, California. The beach stretches about 3.5 miles (5.6 km) along the coast.

Features

The park includes the famous Santa Monica Pier, opened in 1909, with its Pacific Park amusement park, carousel (a National Historic Landmark, built in 1922), and the end-of-the-trail sign for historic U.S. Route 66. North of the pier lies the original Muscle Beach, established in the 1930s and a birthplace of the modern physical fitness movement. The beach is also home to the Annenberg Community Beach House, on the site of Marion Davies's former estate.

Recreation

Facilities include volleyball courts, playgrounds, chess tables, picnic areas, and the Marvin Braude Bike Trail (The Strand), a paved path running along the beachfront from Torrance to Pacific Palisades. Lifeguards patrol the beach, which is popular for swimming, surfing, and beach volleyball.

Management

Although designated a state beach, it is operated by the City of Santa Monica under agreement with California State Parks. The beach hosts public events including outdoor concerts and film screenings.
