Kyūya Fukada

Born11 March 1903, Kaga, Ishikawa, Japan
Died21 March 1978
OccupationWriter, mountaineer, botanist

Kyūya Fukada (深田 久弥, 1903–1978) was a Japanese writer, botanist, and mountaineer. He is best known for Nihon Hyaku-meizan ("100 Famous Japanese Mountains"), published in 1964, which selected one hundred notable peaks across Japan and popularised the idea of climbing them all.

Life and work

Fukada was born in Kaga, Ishikawa Prefecture, and studied at the University of Tokyo. A passionate alpinist, he climbed extensively in the Japanese Alps and wrote essays combining mountaineering with studies of alpine plants. His list of one hundred mountains — chosen for their history, character, and prominence — became a classic of Japanese outdoor literature, and completing all 100 peaks remains a popular goal for Japanese hikers.
