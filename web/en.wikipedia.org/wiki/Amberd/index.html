Amberd

Amberd is a medieval Armenian fortress located on the slopes of Mount Aragats in Aragatsotn Province. Perched above the confluence of the Arkashen and Amberd rivers, it guarded routes across the Aragats foothills and is one of Armenia's best-known castle sites.

History

The site has earlier origins, but the surviving fortress is associated with the seventh century and with later rebuilding under the Pahlavuni family in the eleventh century. A church, commonly called Vahramashen Church, was built nearby in 1026. Amberd was captured and damaged during invasions in the later Middle Ages and eventually abandoned.

Site

Visitors can see sections of thick stone walls, towers, a gate, cisterns and the remains of domestic buildings within the fortress. The high elevation gives broad views over the Ararat plain. Amberd is often visited together with the Byurakan Observatory and other sites on Mount Aragats.
