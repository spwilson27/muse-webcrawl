Door County, Wisconsin

Door County
County seat: Sturgeon Bay
State: Wisconsin
Named for: Porte des Morts ("Door of the Dead")

Door County is the easternmost county in the U.S. state of Wisconsin, occupying the northern half of the Door Peninsula between Green Bay and Lake Michigan. Its county seat is Sturgeon Bay, and its population was about 30,000 in the 2020 census. The county is a popular tourist destination known as the "Cape Cod of the Midwest", with more than 300 miles of shoreline, five state parks, cherry orchards, lighthouses and villages such as Fish Creek, Ephraim and Sister Bay. The treacherous passage at its tip, Porte des Morts, gave the county its name.
