Green Goblin

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Green Goblin is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko, the character first appeared in The Amazing Spider-Man #14 (July 1964). He is the archenemy of Spider-Man and the alter ego most famously of Norman Osborn, the wealthy industrialist and father of Peter Parker's friend Harry Osborn.

Osborn was driven insane by an experimental strength-enhancing serum and adopted the goblin persona, using Halloween-themed weaponry: pumpkin bombs, razor bats, and a personal flying "goblin glider". His identity was a mystery in early stories until revealed in issue #39–40.

Key storylines

The Goblin is central to "The Night Gwen Stacy Died" (1973), in which he killed Spider-Man's girlfriend Gwen Stacy, a landmark in comics history. Norman Osborn apparently died in the same storyline but returned in the 1990s, later becoming a major power player as leader of the Thunderbolts, director of H.A.M.M.E.R., and the Iron Patriot during "Dark Reign". The Goblin mantle has also been taken by Harry Osborn and others.

In other media

Willem Dafoe portrayed Norman Osborn / Green Goblin in Sam Raimi's Spider-Man (2002) and reprised the role in Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021). The character has appeared in numerous animated series and video games.
