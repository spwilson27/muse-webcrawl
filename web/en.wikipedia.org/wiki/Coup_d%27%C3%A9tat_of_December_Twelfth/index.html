Coup d'état of December Twelfth

The Coup d'état of December Twelfth (Korean: 12·12 군사 반란) was a military coup that took place in South Korea on 12 December 1979, in which Major General Chun Doo-hwan and the Hanahoe faction of officers seized control of the armed forces following the assassination of President Park Chung-hee.

Events

Chun, head of the Defence Security Command, ordered the arrest of the martial law commander and Army Chief of Staff Jeong Seung-hwa without presidential authorisation, and troops loyal to the Hanahoe group moved into Seoul after a brief firefight. The coup consolidated Chun's power and led, after the Gwangju Uprising of May 1980, to his presidency under the Fifth Republic.
