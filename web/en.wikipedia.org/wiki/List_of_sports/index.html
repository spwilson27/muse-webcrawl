List of sports

The following is a list of sports, organised by category. A sport is a competitive physical activity governed by rules; the list below includes the world's most widely practised and watched sports, from association football — the most popular sport on Earth — to niche regional pastimes.

Team ball sports

  • Association football (soccer)
  • Basketball
  • Cricket
  • Volleyball and beach volleyball
  • Rugby union and rugby league
  • American football
  • Baseball and softball
  • Ice hockey and field hockey
  • Handball
  • Water polo
  • Australian rules football
  • Gaelic football and hurling
  • Lacrosse
  • Netball

Individual and racket sports

  • Tennis
  • Table tennis
  • Badminton
  • Squash
  • Golf
  • Bowling (tenpin)
  • Darts
  • Snooker and pool

Athletics and endurance

  • Athletics (track and field)
  • Marathon and road running
  • Triathlon
  • Road cycling
  • Cross-country running
  • Race walking
  • Orienteering

Aquatic sports

  • Swimming
  • Diving
  • Rowing
  • Canoeing and kayaking
  • Sailing
  • Surfing
  • Synchronised (artistic) swimming

Combat sports

  • Boxing
  • Judo
  • Wrestling (freestyle and Greco-Roman)
  • Fencing
  • Taekwondo and karate
  • Mixed martial arts (MMA)
  • Muay Thai and kickboxing

Winter sports

  • Alpine skiing
  • Cross-country skiing
  • Ski jumping
  • Snowboarding
  • Figure skating and speed skating
  • Bobsleigh, luge and skeleton
  • Curling
  • Biathlon

Other

  • Gymnastics
  • Weightlifting
  • Archery and shooting
  • Motorsport (Formula One, rallying, MotoGP)
  • Equestrian sports (dressage, show jumping, eventing)
  • Climbing
  • Skateboarding
  • Esports
