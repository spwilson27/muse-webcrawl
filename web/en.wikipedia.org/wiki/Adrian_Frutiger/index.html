Adrian Frutiger

Adrian Frutiger
Born: 24 May 1928, Unterseen, Switzerland
Died: 10 September 2015, Bremgarten, Switzerland
Occupation: Type designer
Notable work: Univers, Frutiger, Avenir

Adrian Frutiger (1928–2015) was a Swiss typeface designer who influenced the direction of digital typography in the second half of the 20th century. He is best known for the typefaces Univers, Frutiger and Avenir.

Career

Frutiger trained as a compositor and studied at the Kunstgewerbeschule Zürich. In 1952 he joined the Paris foundry Deberny & Peignot, where he created Univers (1957), one of the first type families launched simultaneously in a coordinated range of weights and widths. His Frutiger typeface (1976), designed for wayfinding signage at Charles de Gaulle Airport, became a benchmark for legible humanist sans-serifs. Avenir (1988) adapted the geometric sans style to a more humanist form.

Legacy

Frutiger's numbering system for Univers weights was widely adopted, and his signage systems were used in airports and metros worldwide.
