The Onassis Stegi (Greek: Στέγη του Ιδρύματος Ωνάση), formerly the Onassis Cultural Centre, is a cultural venue in Athens, Greece, operated by the Alexander S. Onassis Public Benefit Foundation. Opened in December 2010, it occupies a full city block on Syngrou Avenue.

Building

The building, designed by the French firm Architecture Studio, is clad in bands of white marble that act as a veil, glowing at night. Inside are two main auditoria — an 880-seat main stage and a 220-seat smaller hall — along with exhibition spaces, a lecture hall, and a rooftop restaurant.

Programme

Stegi presents theatre, dance, music, film, visual arts, and talks, mixing Greek productions with international touring work and festivals. It has become a central institution in Athens's contemporary arts scene, known for commissions addressing social and political themes.
