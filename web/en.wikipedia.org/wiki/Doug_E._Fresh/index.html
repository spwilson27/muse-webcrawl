Doug E. Fresh

Douglas E. Davis (born 17 September 1966), known professionally as Doug E. Fresh, is a Barbadian-born American rapper, record producer and beatboxer, nicknamed the "Human Beat Box" and the "Original Human Beatbox".

Doug E. Fresh
Born: 17 September 1966, Barbados
Origin: Harlem, New York
Genres: Hip hop, old-school
Known for: Beatboxing, "The Show", "La Di Da Di"

Career

Raised in Harlem, Doug E. Fresh pioneered beatboxing — imitating drum machines and effects entirely with the mouth. With the Get Fresh Crew and MC Ricky D (later Slick Rick), he released the landmark 1985 double-sided single "The Show" / "La Di Da Di", the latter one of the most sampled songs in hip-hop history. His debut album Oh, My God! followed in 1986.

Legacy

"La Di Da Di" has been sampled or referenced by hundreds of artists including Snoop Dogg and The Notorious B.I.G. Doug E. Fresh remains an active performer, mentor and health advocate, associated with Harlem institutions and hip-hop education.
