Martijn Gerard Garritsen (born 14 May 1996), known professionally as Martin Garrix, is a Dutch DJ and record producer. He rose to international fame in 2013 with his solo release "Animals", which topped charts in several countries and made him one of the youngest producers to have a number-one song on the electronic music platform Beatport. He was ranked number one on DJ Mag's Top 100 DJs list for three consecutive years (2016, 2017, and 2018), and again in later years.

Career

Garrix was inspired to become a DJ after seeing Tiësto perform at the 2004 Athens Olympic Games. He graduated from the Herman Brood Academy, a production school in Utrecht. After "Animals", he released successful collaborations including "Wizard" with Jay Hardway, "Tremor" with Dimitri Vegas & Like Mike, and "Don't Look Down" with Usher. In 2016 he founded his own record label, Stmpd Rcrds, and released a series of singles during Amsterdam Dance Event.

Later work

His melodic pop-leaning singles, including "Scared to Be Lonely" with Dua Lipa, "There for You" with Troye Sivan, and "Ocean" with Khalid, broadened his audience. In 2022 he released his debut studio album, Sentio. He also records more underground-oriented material under the alias Ytram and as part of the duo AREA21 with Maejor. Garrix has headlined major festivals including Ultra Music Festival, Tomorrowland, and Coachella.
