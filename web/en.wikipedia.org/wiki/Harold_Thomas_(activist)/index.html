Harold Thomas (activist)

Harold Joseph Thomas
Born: 1947, Sydney, Australia
Nationality: Australian (Lardil people)
Known for: Designing the Aboriginal flag

Harold Joseph Thomas (born 1947) is an Aboriginal Australian artist and activist, a member of the Lardil people of the Gulf of Carpentaria. He is best known as the designer of the Australian Aboriginal flag.

The Aboriginal flag

Thomas designed the flag in 1971: a black upper half representing Aboriginal people, a red lower half for the earth and ochre, and a yellow circle for the sun. It was first flown on National Aborigines Day in Adelaide in 1971 and was officially recognised by the Australian government in 1995 under the Flags Act.

Copyright

Thomas's copyright in the flag was upheld by the Federal Court in 1997. In 2022 the Australian government purchased the copyright for over A$20 million, making the flag freely available for public use.
