Experimental Aircraft Association

The Experimental Aircraft Association (EAA) is an international organisation of aviation enthusiasts based in Oshkosh, Wisconsin, United States. It was founded in 1953 by Paul Poberezny and a small group of aircraft builders in Milwaukee, and has grown to roughly 300,000 members in more than 100 countries, organised in over 1,000 local chapters.

EAA is best known for EAA AirVenture Oshkosh, the world's largest annual airshow and fly-in, held each summer at Wittman Regional Airport. The association advocates for recreational aviation, supports homebuilt and experimental aircraft, restoration and warbirds, and runs programmes such as Young Eagles, which has given millions of young people free introductory flights since 1992.

The EAA Aviation Museum in Oshkosh houses a large collection of aircraft. Membership is open to anyone interested in aviation, not only experimental-aircraft builders.
