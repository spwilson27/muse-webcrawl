ABET

Founded: 1932 (as Engineers' Council for Professional Development)

Headquarters: Baltimore, Maryland, United States

Type: Nonprofit accrediting organization

Scope: Engineering, engineering technology, computing, and applied and natural science programs

Former name: Accreditation Board for Engineering and Technology

ABET is a nonprofit, non-governmental organization that accredits post-secondary education programs in engineering, engineering technology, computing, and applied and natural sciences. Founded in 1932 as the Engineers' Council for Professional Development (ECPD), it adopted the name Accreditation Board for Engineering and Technology in 1980 and later shortened its official name to ABET.

Accreditation

ABET accredits individual programs rather than whole institutions. Reviews, conducted by teams of volunteer evaluators drawn from industry, government, and academia, assess curricula, faculty, facilities, and — since the adoption of the outcomes-based EC2000 criteria — the degree to which programs demonstrate that their students attain defined learning outcomes and engage in continuous improvement. ABET accredits several thousand programs at hundreds of colleges and universities in the United States and in dozens of other countries.

Significance

Graduation from an ABET-accredited engineering program is generally required or strongly expected for licensure as a Professional Engineer in the United States and for many engineering jobs. ABET is a founding signatory of the Washington Accord, an international agreement under which signatory bodies mutually recognize the substantial equivalence of accredited engineering degrees.
