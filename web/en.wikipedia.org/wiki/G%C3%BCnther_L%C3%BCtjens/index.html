Günther Lütjens

Johann Günther Lütjens (25 May 1889 – 27 May 1941) was a German admiral in the Kriegsmarine who served as fleet commander during the Second World War. He is best known for commanding the battleship Bismarck and the heavy cruiser Prinz Eugen during Operation Rheinübung.

Günther Lütjens
Born: 1889, Wiesbaden
Died: 1941, Atlantic Ocean
Rank: Admiral
Commands: Fleet Commander, Kriegsmarine

Career

Lütjens joined the Imperial German Navy in 1907 and served on torpedo boats in the First World War. Between the wars he rose steadily, commanding the cruiser Karlsruhe and serving as chief of personnel. In 1940 he led Operation Berlin, a successful Atlantic sortie by Scharnhorst and Gneisenau.

Death

After Bismarck sank HMS Hood on 24 May 1941, British forces hunted the battleship down; Lütjens went down with Bismarck on 27 May 1941.
