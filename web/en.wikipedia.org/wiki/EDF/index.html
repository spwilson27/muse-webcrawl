EDF

EDF most commonly refers to Électricité de France, a French multinational electric utility company largely owned by the French state, headquartered in Paris. It is one of the world's largest producers of electricity, operating a diverse portfolio including nuclear, hydroelectric, and renewable generation.

Électricité de France

Founded in 1946 from the nationalisation of numerous electricity producers, EDF operates France's large fleet of nuclear power stations and supplies electricity and gas to millions of customers in France, the United Kingdom (through EDF Energy), Italy, and elsewhere. In 2023 the French government completed a full takeover of the company, delisting it from the stock exchange.

Other uses

EDF may also refer to the European Development Fund, the earliest deadline first scheduling algorithm, or the Export Development Fund, among other terms.
