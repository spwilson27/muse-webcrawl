OkCupid

OkCupid (stylized as OkC) is a U.S.-based, internationally operating online dating, friendship, and formerly social networking website and application. It features multiple-choice questions in order to generate a match percentage between a pair of users.

The site was founded in 2004 by Harvard students Christian Rudder, Chris Coyne, Sam Yagan, and Max Krohn, growing out of TheSpark and SparkNotes. It was acquired by IAC's Match Group in 2011. OkCupid was notable for its data-driven OkTrends blog analysing user behaviour, published by Rudder in works including Dataclysm (2014).
