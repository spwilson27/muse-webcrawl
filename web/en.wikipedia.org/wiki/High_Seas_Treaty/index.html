High Seas Treaty

The High Seas Treaty, formally the Agreement under the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea on the Conservation and Sustainable Use of Marine Biological Diversity of Areas beyond National Jurisdiction and commonly known as the BBNJ Agreement, is an international treaty adopted on 19 June 2023 under the auspices of the United Nations.

Background

The high seas — waters beyond states' exclusive economic zones — cover nearly two-thirds of the ocean but were governed only fragmentarily under the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea. Negotiations on a new implementing agreement ran for nearly two decades, with a final intergovernmental conference reaching consensus in March 2023.

Provisions

The treaty provides for the creation of marine protected areas on the high seas, requires environmental impact assessments for activities that may harm the marine environment, regulates access to marine genetic resources and the sharing of benefits from them, and promotes capacity building and the transfer of marine technology to developing states. It supports the "30x30" goal of protecting 30% of the ocean by 2030.

Ratification

The agreement opened for signature in September 2023 and enters into force 120 days after the sixtieth instrument of ratification is deposited. The sixtieth ratification was reached in September 2025, setting entry into force for January 2026.
