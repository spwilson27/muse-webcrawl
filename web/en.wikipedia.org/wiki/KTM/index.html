KTM

KTM AG is an Austrian motorcycle and sports car manufacturer headquartered in Mattighofen, Upper Austria. Renowned for its off-road and enduro machines — identifiable by their orange livery and the slogan "Ready to Race" — KTM is among the world's largest motorcycle manufacturers, with brands including Husqvarna Motorcycles and Gas Gas, and a long record of dominance in rally raid racing, including many consecutive Dakar Rally victories.

History

The company's roots lie in a metalworking shop founded in 1934 by engineer Hans Trunkenpolz in Mattighofen. In 1953, industrialist Ernst Kronreif became a major shareholder, and the firm was renamed Kronreif & Trunkenpolz Mattighofen — the origin of the initials KTM. The company built its first motorcycle, the R100, in 1953, and motorcycles such as the Comet and the Ponny scooter followed. KTM began series production of off-road competition bikes in the 1970s and won its first motocross world championship in 1974. After financial difficulties and a bankruptcy in 1991, the motorcycle business was restructured under Stefan Pierer's Cross Industries (now Pierer Mobility), growing steadily through the 1990s and 2000s.

Products and racing

KTM's range spans motocross and enduro bikes (EXC and SX lines), the Duke naked street bikes, Adventure travel models, the RC sports bikes, and the X-Bow sports car. The company entered MotoGP's premier class in 2017. In rallying, KTM riders won the Dakar Rally motorcycle category every year from 2001 to 2019. Since 2013 KTM has partnered with India's Bajaj Auto, which manufactures small-displacement KTM and Husqvarna models.

In 2024 the group entered significant financial distress amid falling demand and high inventories, entered self-administration proceedings in Austria, and in 2025 Bajaj Auto agreed to take majority control.
