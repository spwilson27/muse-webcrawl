List of viceroys of Naples

This is a list of the viceroys who governed the Kingdom of Naples on behalf of foreign monarchs. After Ferdinand II of Aragon conquered Naples in 1503–1504, the kingdom was ruled from Spain by a succession of Spanish viceroys for two centuries. The War of the Spanish Succession transferred Naples to Austria in 1707; Austrian viceroys governed until Charles of Bourbon conquered the kingdom in 1734 and founded an independent cadet branch of the Spanish Bourbons.
