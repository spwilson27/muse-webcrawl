Erdős number

The Erdős number describes the "collaborative distance" between mathematician Paul Erdős and another person, as measured by joint authorship of mathematical papers. Erdős himself has Erdős number 0; his direct co-authors have number 1; their co-authors (other than those already counted) have number 2, and so on. The concept honours Erdős's extraordinary productivity and his roughly 500 collaborators.

Definitions and distribution

Erdős published around 1,500 papers in his lifetime, more than any other mathematician. His 509 direct co-authors have Erdős number 1, and thousands of mathematicians have number 2. The median Erdős number among publishing mathematicians is around 5. Only those who have co-authored a research paper along a chain leading to Erdős have a finite Erdős number.

Variations

The idea inspired similar measures, including the Bacon number for film actors connected to Kevin Bacon, the combined Erdős–Bacon number, and the Erdős–Bacon–Sabbath number for those connected through mathematics, acting and music. The American Mathematical Society maintains a collaboration-distance calculator based on MathSciNet data.
