The Welcome to Fabulous Las Vegas sign is a Las Vegas landmark funded in May 1959 and erected soon after by Western Neon. The sign was designed by Betty Willis at the request of Clark County; she intended it to welcome visitors to the city. It stands in the median of Las Vegas Boulevard, just south of the Strip, in Paradise, Nevada — technically outside the Las Vegas city limits.

Design

The 25-foot-tall sign is a horizontal stretched diamond shape, mounted on poles, with "Welcome" rendered in red letters inside white neon circles meant to evoke silver dollars — a nod to Nevada's nickname, the "Silver State", and to gambling. Below, "to Fabulous" is written in script and "Las Vegas" in large red capitals, with "Nevada" beneath. The reverse reads "Drive Carefully — Come Back Soon". Willis never copyrighted the design, considering it her gift to the city, and the image passed into the public domain.

History

The sign was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 2009, its 50th anniversary. A parking area for visitors was built in 2008 and expanded in 2012 to cope with the crowds who stop for photographs. After the 2017 Las Vegas shooting, the sign became a gathering point and backdrop for memorials, and it has periodically been lit or altered for civic causes and events.
