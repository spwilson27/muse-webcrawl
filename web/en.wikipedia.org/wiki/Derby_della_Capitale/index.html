The Derby della Capitale (English: Derby of the Capital City), also known as the Derby di Roma, is the local football derby in Rome, Italy, between AS Roma and SS Lazio. It is considered one of the fiercest rivalries in Italian and European football, characterised by passionate support, elaborate tifo choreography, and a history of tension between the fan bases.

History

Lazio was founded in 1900; Roma was created in 1927 by a merger of Roman clubs under the Fascist regime, which Lazio resisted joining — a founding grievance that still fuels the rivalry. The first derby was played on 8 December 1929, ending in a 1–0 Roma win. Both clubs share the Stadio Olimpico. The rivalry has also been shaped by social and geographic identities: Lazio traditionally draws support from Rome's northern districts and the surrounding region, Roma from the city's historic centre and southern quarters.

Notable matches

The 2013 Coppa Italia final was the first cup final contested between the two clubs; Lazio won 1–0 with a goal by Senad Lulic, a date and minute (the 71st) still celebrated by Lazio fans. Francesco Totti, Roma's captain and record appearance-maker in the derby, became its emblematic figure on the Giallorossi side.
