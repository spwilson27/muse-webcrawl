Winton is a town and locality in the Shire of Winton in Central West Queensland, Australia. It is a major service centre for the surrounding grazing country and lies on the Landsborough Highway about 178 kilometres (111 mi) north-west of Longreach. In the 2021 census the locality had a population of around 856 people. Winton is celebrated for three contributions to Australian identity: it is where Banjo Paterson's ballad "Waltzing Matilda" was first performed in 1895, it is associated with the founding of the airline Qantas in 1920, and it is a centre for dinosaur tourism in the surrounding Channel Country.

History

The town grew from a staging post on the coach routes of the 1870s and was surveyed in 1876, taking its name from the birthplace of the first postmaster, in the Dorset suburb of Winton near Bournemouth. The surrounding district supported sheep and cattle grazing on Mitchell grass downs watered by artesian bores. In April 1895 A. B. "Banjo" Paterson's song "Waltzing Matilda" was first performed at the North Gregory Hotel in Winton, an event commemorated by the Waltzing Matilda Centre, the first museum in the world dedicated to a song; the original centre burned down in 2015 and was rebuilt, reopening in 2018.

In November 1920 the Queensland and Northern Territory Aerial Services—Qantas—was registered and held its first board meetings in Winton (though its first operations were based in Longreach and Charleville). The town's musical and pastoral heritage is celebrated at the biennial Outback Festival, famous for events such as dunny races and the Australian championship of "cracking" whips.

Dinosaurs and attractions

Since the 1999 discovery of dinosaur remains on a local property, the region has become a key site of Australian palaeontology. The Australian Age of Dinosaurs museum, on a mesa called "The Jump-Up" east of town, houses major fossil collections and has described new sauropod species from the Winton Formation, including Diamantinasaurus and Savannasaurus. Nearby Lark Quarry Conservation Park preserves the world's only known evidence of a dinosaur stampede, thousands of small footprints made about 95 million years ago.

Climate

Winton has a hot semi-arid climate: summers are very hot, winters mild, and rainfall is low and variable, averaging roughly 400 mm (16 in), falling mostly in summer storms.
