The American Association of State Highway and Transportation Officials (AASHTO) is a nonprofit, nonpartisan association representing the departments of transportation of the fifty United States, the District of Columbia, and Puerto Rico. It is a standards-setting body whose technical guidance for the design, construction, and maintenance of highways and bridges is used throughout the United States and internationally.

History

Founded in 1914 as the American Association of State Highway Officials (AASHO), the association helped coordinate the numbered U.S. Highway System in the 1920s and later supported development of the Interstate Highway System. It adopted its current name in 1973 to reflect the broader responsibilities of state transportation agencies.

Standards and publications

AASHTO publishes widely adopted specifications, including A Policy on Geometric Design of Highways and Streets (the "Green Book"), the LRFD Bridge Design Specifications, and materials-testing standards through its AASHTO Materials Reference Laboratory. It also manages the numbering of U.S. routes and Interstate highways through its Special Committee on U.S. Route Numbering.
