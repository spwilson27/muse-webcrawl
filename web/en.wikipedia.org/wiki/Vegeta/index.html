Vegeta

Vegeta (Japanese: ベジータ, Hepburn: Bejīta), fully Vegeta IV and also known as Prince Vegeta, is a fictional character in the Dragon Ball media franchise created by Akira Toriyama. Vegeta first appeared in chapter #204 of the original manga, published in Weekly Shōnen Jump in 1988. He is the prince of the fallen Saiyan race and one of the main characters of the series.

Creation and design

Toriyama created Vegeta as a foil and rival to protagonist Son Goku. His name, like those of many Saiyans, is a pun on "vegetable". Vegeta is recognisable by his flame-like upswept black hair, prominent widow's peak, and the armoured bodysuit (battle armour) of Frieza's forces that he continues to wear. Toriyama considered ending the character several times, but Vegeta's popularity kept him in the story, evolving from villain to anti-hero to one of Earth's defenders.

Character

Vegeta is defined by immense pride in his royal Saiyan heritage, arrogance, and an obsessive rivalry with Goku, who repeatedly surpasses him. Despite his ruthless beginnings, his life on Earth — particularly his marriage to Bulma and fatherhood of Trunks — gradually humanises him. Signature techniques include the Galick Gun, Big Bang Attack, and Final Flash. He achieves the Super Saiyan transformation during the Android saga and later godly forms such as Super Saiyan Blue and, in Dragon Ball Super, Ultra Ego.

Appearances

Vegeta debuts as an antagonist arriving on Earth with Nappa to obtain the Dragon Balls, then becomes an uneasy ally against Frieza on Namek, where he is killed and later resurrected. In the Android/Cell and Majin Buu arcs he fights alongside the heroes — briefly falling under Babidi's control as "Majin Vegeta" and sacrificing himself in a failed attempt to destroy Buu. He is a central character throughout Dragon Ball GT (non-canon) and Dragon Ball Super, where he trains under Whis and Beerus and battles in the Tournament of Power.

Reception

Vegeta is consistently ranked among the most popular anime and manga characters of all time; his character arc from villain to hero is frequently cited as one of the finest in shōnen manga, and his rivalry with Goku is considered one of the most iconic in the medium.
