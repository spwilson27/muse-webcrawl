Falkland Sound

The Falkland Sound (Spanish: Estrecho de San Carlos) is a sea strait in the Falkland Islands in the South Atlantic Ocean. It runs southwest–northeast, separating West Falkland and East Falkland, the two main islands of the archipelago. It is roughly 80 km long and varies in width from a few kilometres to about 30 km.

Geography

Settlements on its shores include Port Howard and Fox Bay on West Falkland, and San Carlos, Port San Carlos, and Ajax Bay on East Falkland. Islands within the sound include Narrows Island and Great Island. The Spanish name derives from San Carlos Water, an inlet of East Falkland.

History

The sound saw fighting during the 1982 Falklands War: British forces landed at San Carlos Water in May 1982, and the surrounding waters were the scene of repeated Argentine air attacks during the landings, a period remembered in Britain as "Bomb Alley".
