Dorothy Gale

Dorothy Gale is a fictional character created by American author L. Frank Baum as the protagonist of many of his Oz novels. She first appears in Baum's classic children's novel The Wonderful Wizard of Oz (1900) and reappears in most of its sequels. Dorothy is an orphan raised by her Aunt Em and Uncle Henry on a farm in Kansas, from where a cyclone carries her and her little dog Toto to the magical Land of Oz.

Character

Dorothy is a level-headed, courageous and kind-hearted girl, usually depicted as around ten to twelve years old. In the novels she is an ordinary child whose resourcefulness and honesty win her the friendship of the Scarecrow, the Tin Woodman and the Cowardly Lion. In later books Dorothy moves to Oz permanently and is made a Princess of Oz by Princess Ozma.

In the books

Baum featured Dorothy in 10 of his 14 Oz books. In The Wonderful Wizard of Oz she seeks the Wizard's help to return to Kansas, travelling the yellow brick road to the Emerald City and defeating the Wicked Witch of the West. Subsequent adventures take her to Ev, the Nome Kingdom and across Oz, and in The Emerald City of Oz (1910) she brings Aunt Em and Uncle Henry to live in Oz with her.

Portrayals

Dorothy is most famously portrayed by Judy Garland in the 1939 MGM film The Wizard of Oz, in which she wears the iconic ruby slippers (silver shoes in Baum's book). Other portrayals include Diana Ross in The Wiz (1978) and Fairuza Balk in Return to Oz (1985).
