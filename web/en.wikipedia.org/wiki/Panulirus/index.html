Panulirus

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Panulirus is a genus of spiny lobsters in the family Palinuridae, including many of the species most commonly caught and marketed as rock lobsters. Unlike true lobsters (family Nephropidae), members of Panulirus lack large claws and instead possess long, spiny antennae and a heavily armoured carapace.

Description

Species of Panulirus are characterized by a cylindrical body, long whip-like antennae whose bases are thickened and spiny, and the absence of the large chelae found in clawed lobsters. Defence is provided by the spiny exoskeleton and by stridulating — producing sound by rubbing a file-like structure at the base of the antennae against the carapace. Many species are brightly coloured and patterned with spots or banded legs.

Distribution and ecology

The genus is distributed throughout warm seas worldwide, in tropical and subtropical waters of the Atlantic, Indian, and Pacific Oceans. Adults live in rocky reefs and crevices, emerging at night to feed on molluscs, other invertebrates, and carrion. Like other palinurids, Panulirus species have a long planktonic larval stage (the phyllosoma larva), which may drift for many months before settling to the bottom.

Fisheries

Several species support major commercial and artisanal fisheries and are highly prized as seafood. Important species include the Caribbean spiny lobster (Panulirus argus), the ornate rock lobster (P. ornatus), the mud spiny lobster (P. polyphagus), and the Japanese spiny lobster (P. japonicus). Many populations are heavily exploited, and some are subject to minimum size limits and closed seasons.

Species

Species in the genus include:

  • Panulirus argus — Caribbean spiny lobster
  • Panulirus interruptus — California spiny lobster
  • Panulirus japonicus — Japanese spiny lobster
  • Panulirus ornatus — ornate rock lobster
  • Panulirus polyphagus — mud spiny lobster
  • Panulirus regius — royal spiny lobster
  • Panulirus versicolor — painted rock lobster
  • Panulirus longipes — longlegged spiny lobster
  • Panulirus pascuensis — Easter Island spiny lobster
  • Panulirus echinatus — brown spiny lobster
