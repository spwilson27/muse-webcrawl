Ray Lee Hunt (born 1943) is an American billionaire businessman and oil executive. He is the chairman and chief executive officer of Hunt Oil Company, the Dallas-based energy company founded by his father, the legendary wildcatter H. L. Hunt. Through the broader Hunt family enterprises, he has also been involved in real estate development, refining, and liquefied natural gas projects around the world.

Ray Hunt, head of Hunt Oil Company.

Early life and education

Hunt was born in Shreveport, Louisiana, in 1943, the son of H. L. Hunt and his second wife, Ruth Ray. H. L. Hunt had become one of the wealthiest men in America through his East Texas oil discoveries of the 1930s. Ray Hunt attended Southern Methodist University in Dallas, graduating with a degree in economics, and went on to earn an MBA.

Career

Hunt joined the family business and rose to lead Hunt Oil Company, one of the largest privately held oil and gas companies in the United States. Under his leadership the company expanded internationally, with exploration and production operations in Yemen, Canada, Peru, and elsewhere. Hunt Oil's Yemen operations, conducted through the Yemen Hunt Oil Company, were among the most significant American investments in that country.

In the 2000s, Hunt Oil developed a major liquefied natural gas project in Peru, the Peru LNG plant at Pampa Melchorita, which began operations in 2010 as the first LNG export facility in South America. In 2007 the company signed a controversial oil exploration agreement with the Kurdistan Regional Government of Iraq, one of the first such deals by an American company in the region, drawing attention in Washington because of Hunt's personal ties to President George W. Bush.

Other activities

Hunt has served on numerous corporate and civic boards and has been a prominent figure in Dallas business and philanthropy, including involvement with Southern Methodist University. He was appointed by President George W. Bush to the President's Foreign Intelligence Advisory Board. The Hunt family's fortune, descended from H. L. Hunt's oil empire, is spread across energy, real estate (through Hunt Realty), and other investments.
