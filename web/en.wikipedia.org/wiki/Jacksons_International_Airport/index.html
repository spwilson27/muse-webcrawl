Jacksons International Airport

Jacksons International Airport (IATA: POM, ICAO: AYPY), also known as Port Moresby Airport, is the largest and busiest airport in Papua New Guinea. It lies about 8 kilometres from Port Moresby, the capital, and serves as the hub of Air Niugini and PNG Air.

History

The airfield originated in the Second World War, when the area around Port Moresby hosted several Allied airstrips named after their distance from the city; the field at Seven Mile became the basis of the postwar airport. It is named for Keith "Bluey" Jackson, an Australian pilot and airport manager. A new international terminal opened in 2015 ahead of the Pacific Games, with further expansion for the 2018 APEC summit.

Operations

Jacksons handles international services to Australia, Singapore, the Philippines, Fiji, and other Pacific destinations, and domestic flights throughout Papua New Guinea, where air travel is often the only practical link between the capital and remote provinces.
