Once Upon a Time in Mexico

Once Upon a Time in Mexico
Directed by: Robert Rodriguez
Starring: Antonio Banderas, Salma Hayek, Johnny Depp, Willem Dafoe
Released: 2003
Budget: $29 million; Box office: ~$98 million

Once Upon a Time in Mexico is a 2003 American neo-Western action film written, directed, produced, photographed, scored, and edited by Robert Rodriguez. It is the third and final installment in Rodriguez's Mexico Trilogy, following El Mariachi (1992) and Desperado (1995).

Plot

El Mariachi (Antonio Banderas), living in seclusion after the death of his wife Carolina (Salma Hayek), is recruited by corrupt CIA agent Sheldon Sands (Johnny Depp) to assassinate General Márquez, who is plotting a coup against the Mexican president with drug lord Armando Barillo (Willem Dafoe). The plot spirals into a web of double-crosses that erupts during the Day of the Dead celebrations.

Production

The film was shot on high-definition digital video, which Rodriguez championed for its speed and flexibility. It featured an ensemble cast including Mickey Rourke, Eva Mendes, Danny Trejo, and Rubén Blades. The title pays homage to Sergio Leone's Once Upon a Time in the West and Once Upon a Time in America.

Reception

The film received mixed-to-positive reviews, with praise for its visual style and Depp's performance, and grossed about $98 million worldwide against a $29 million budget.
