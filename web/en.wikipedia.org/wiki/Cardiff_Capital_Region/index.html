Cardiff Capital Region

The Cardiff Capital Region (CCR) is a city region in south-east Wales centred on Cardiff, covering ten local authorities in the former counties of Gwent and South and Mid Glamorgan. It is home to around 1.5 million people, roughly half the population of Wales.

Cardiff Capital Region
Country: Wales, United Kingdom
Principal city: Cardiff
Population: approx. 1.5 million
Local authorities: 10

Governance

The region is governed jointly by its ten councils through the Cardiff Capital Region Cabinet. A City Deal agreed with the UK and Welsh governments in 2016 provided £1.2 billion of investment funding over 20 years, supporting projects such as the South Wales Metro rail programme.

Economy

Cardiff's economy centres on finance, media, higher education, and creative industries, while the Valleys towns retain manufacturing and logistics. Regional priorities include compound semiconductors in Newport, transport electrification, and digital infrastructure.
