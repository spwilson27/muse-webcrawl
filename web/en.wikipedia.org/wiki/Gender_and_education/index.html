Gender and education

Gender and education is an interdisciplinary field of study examining how gender shapes educational access, experiences, and outcomes, and how education in turn reproduces or challenges gender inequalities. It covers disparities in enrollment and attainment, subject choice, classroom interaction, curriculum content, and the gender composition of the teaching profession.

Classrooms are a central site where gender norms are learned and negotiated

Global patterns

Historically, girls and women were excluded or underrepresented at many levels of education. Over the twentieth century, and especially since the 1970s, female enrollment expanded dramatically worldwide. In most developed countries women now constitute the majority of university students and graduates. However, in parts of Sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia, girls still face barriers to secondary schooling related to poverty, early marriage, distance to school, and inadequate sanitation facilities.

Performance and subject choice

International assessments such as PISA consistently find that girls outperform boys in reading in nearly all participating countries, while boys score slightly higher on average in mathematics in many countries, with wide variation. Gender gaps also appear in subject choice: women remain underrepresented in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM) fields and overrepresented in education, health, and humanities. Researchers debate the relative contributions of socialization, stereotype threat, teacher expectations, and differing interests to these patterns.

Policy responses

Governments and international bodies have promoted gender equality in education through initiatives such as the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goal 4, the Education for All campaign, scholarship and conditional cash transfer programs for girls, and efforts to recruit and train female teachers. Since the 1990s, concern has also grown in some countries about boys' underachievement and disengagement from schooling.
