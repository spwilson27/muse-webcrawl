Mukesh Dhirubhai Ambani (born 19 April 1957) is an Indian businessman who serves as chairman and managing director of Reliance Industries, one of India's largest conglomerates, with interests in petrochemicals, refining, oil and gas, retail, and telecommunications. He is frequently ranked among the wealthiest people in Asia and the world.

Early life and career

Ambani was born in Aden (now Yemen) to Dhirubhai Ambani, founder of Reliance, and was raised in Mumbai. He studied chemical engineering at the Institute of Chemical Technology, Mumbai, and began an MBA at Stanford University before leaving to join the family business in 1981. After his father's death in 2002, the Reliance empire was divided between Mukesh and his younger brother Anil, with Mukesh retaining the flagship Reliance Industries.

Business ventures

Under his leadership Reliance built the world's largest refining complex at Jamnagar, Gujarat. In 2016 the company launched Jio, a 4G telecommunications network that rapidly became India's largest mobile operator and reshaped the country's digital market. Reliance Retail has grown into India's largest retailer, and the group has expanded into new energy and technology ventures.

Personal life

Ambani is married to Nita Ambani; their children, Akash, Isha, and Anant, hold senior roles in the group. The family's Mumbai residence, Antilia, is one of the world's most expensive private homes.
