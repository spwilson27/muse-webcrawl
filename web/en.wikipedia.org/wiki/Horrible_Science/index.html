Horrible Science

Horrible Science is a series of children's science books written by Nick Arnold and illustrated by Tony De Saulles, published by Scholastic. The series began in 1996 and forms part of the wider Horrible Histories family of informal educational books.

Style and content

The books present scientific topics — biology, chemistry, physics, astronomy and earth science — with an emphasis on the gruesome, gory and surprising aspects of the subject, mixing cartoons, quizzes, experiments and jokes with factual text. Titles include Bulging Brains, Disgusting Digestion, Fatal Forces and Suffering Scientists.

Reception

The series has been a commercial success and has won awards, including the Royal Society's junior prize for science books. Arnold received the Aventis Prize for Science Books for The Terrible Truth About Time.

Adaptations

The books have been adapted for the stage and for a children's television series, and related titles such as Horrible Geography followed the same format.
