A&W Restaurants is an American fast-food chain distinguished by its draft root beer, served in frosted glass mugs. Founded in 1919, it is one of the oldest restaurant chains in the United States and pioneered franchising in the restaurant industry.

History

Roy W. Allen opened a root beer stand in Lodi, California in 1919, selling a recipe he had purchased from an Arizona pharmacist. In 1922 he partnered with Frank Wright; the initials of their surnames gave the chain its name, A&W. Allen began franchising the root beer in 1921, an early example of the franchise model, and A&W stands spread across the country. The chain became known for drive-in restaurants with carhop service and for its mascot, Rooty the Great Root Bear, introduced in the 1970s.

Ownership

The company passed through several owners, including a long period under Yum! Brands (2002–2011), before being bought by a consortium of franchisees in 2011. A separate Canadian operation, A&W Food Services of Canada, has been independently owned since 1995 and is among Canada's largest burger chains.

Menu

Signature items include root beer floats, the Papa Burger family of burgers, cheese curds, and Coney dogs. Some locations remain drive-ins with carhop service, evoking the chain's mid-century heyday.
