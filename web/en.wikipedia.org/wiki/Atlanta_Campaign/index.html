Atlanta Campaign
Dates7 May – 2 September 1864
LocationNorthwestern Georgia
ResultUnion victory

The Atlanta Campaign was a series of battles fought in the Western Theater of the American Civil War throughout northwest Georgia and the area around Atlanta during the summer of 1864. Union Major General William Tecumseh Sherman advanced from Chattanooga, Tennessee, against Confederate General Joseph E. Johnston's Army of Tennessee.

Course

Through a series of flanking manoeuvres and battles including Resaca, Kennesaw Mountain and Peachtree Creek, Sherman forced the Confederates back toward Atlanta. Johnston was replaced by the more aggressive John Bell Hood, whose costly attacks at Atlanta, Ezra Church and Jonesborough failed to break Sherman's grip. Hood evacuated Atlanta on 1 September 1864, and Union troops occupied the city the next day.

Significance

The capture of Atlanta, a major rail and industrial hub, greatly boosted Northern morale and helped secure Abraham Lincoln's re-election in November 1864. Sherman's subsequent March to the Sea began from the city that November.
