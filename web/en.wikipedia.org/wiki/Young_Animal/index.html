Young Animal (Japanese: ヤングアニマル, Hepburn: Yangu Animaru) is a Japanese seinen manga magazine published by Hakusensha. It is issued twice a month and is aimed primarily at young adult men. The magazine is best known internationally as the long-running home of Kentaro Miura's dark fantasy series Berserk.

History

The magazine began in 1989 under the title Animal House (アニマルハウス). It was relaunched and renamed Young Animal in 1992. Over the decades it has combined manga serials with gravure idol photography features, a format typical of seinen anthology magazines in Japan.

Notable series

The magazine's most famous serial is Berserk, which began in 1989 in the predecessor magazine and continued in Young Animal until author Kentaro Miura's death in 2021; the series later resumed under the supervision of Miura's friend Kouji Mori and Miura's studio, Studio Gaga. Other notable series serialised in the magazine include Chibi Maruko-chan spin-offs, Detroit Metal City, Air Master, Holyland, and Ubel Blatt, among many others.

Sister publications

Hakusensha has published related magazines such as Young Animal Zero and Young Animal Arashi (which ran from 2000 to 2018), carrying additional seinen titles and spin-off series.
