Astra (satellite)

Operator:SES S.A.
Launch date:11 December 1988 (Astra 1A)
Orbit:Geostationary
Main positions:19.2°E, 28.2°E, 23.5°E, 5°E

Astra is the brand name for a number of geostationary communication satellites, both individually and as a group, which are owned and operated by SES S.A., a global satellite operator based in Betzdorf, Luxembourg, in eastern Luxembourg.

Launched in 1988, Astra 1A was the first communication satellite vessel to deliver television and radio signals and data across Western and Eastern Europe. More than ten Astra satellites were launched in the 1990s, mostly at the 19.2°E position. At this point, the Astra 19.2°E position was the dominant source of satellite TV for Europe, most notably for Germany.

History

SES was founded in 1985 as Europe's first private satellite operator. The Astra system pioneered the concept of co-locating multiple satellites at a single orbital slot so that a fixed home dish could receive signals from all of them, and it helped create the direct-to-home television market in Europe.

Orbital positions

The main Astra positions are 19.2° east (serving continental Europe), 28.2° east (serving the United Kingdom and Ireland, in partnership with the Sky and Freesat platforms), 23.5° east and 5° east. Retired satellites are moved to graveyard orbits or repurposed, for example as part of the Eutelsat-adjacent backup fleet.
