Henry Moore Foundation

The Henry Moore Foundation is a registered charity established in 1977 by the English sculptor Henry Moore (1898–1986) to advance public appreciation of sculpture and the visual arts, and to preserve and promote Moore's own work. It is based at Perry Green in Hertfordshire, where Moore lived and worked for the last four decades of his life.

Activities

The Foundation runs the Henry Moore Studios & Gardens at Perry Green, where visitors can see the artist's studios, workshops, and monumental bronzes in the grounds, and operates the Henry Moore Institute in Leeds, a centre for the study of sculpture with a library, archive, and exhibition programme. It cares for a large collection of Moore's sculptures, drawings, and prints, lends works worldwide, and catalogues his oeuvre.

Grants

Funded originally by Moore's gift of works and assets, the Foundation makes grants supporting exhibitions, publications, and scholarship in sculpture, and supports conservation and research into Moore's art and archive.
