International recognition of the State of Palestine

The State of Palestine has been recognised as a sovereign state by a large majority of the member states of the United Nations since its declaration of independence on 15 November 1988 by the Palestine Liberation Organization.

Status at the United Nations

In 2012 the UN General Assembly granted Palestine non-member observer state status. Palestine is a member of UNESCO and other international bodies, though it is not a full UN member state, as membership requires Security Council recommendation.

Recognition history

Most states in Asia, Africa and Latin America recognised Palestine soon after 1988. A further wave of recognitions came in 2024, when several European and Caribbean states, including Spain, Ireland and Norway, extended recognition amid the Gaza war.

Opposition

Israel and a number of Western states have argued that Palestinian statehood should result from negotiations rather than unilateral recognition, and some states have not recognised Palestine.
