High School Musical

High School Musical is a 2006 American musical television film directed by Kenny Ortega and written by Peter Barsocchini. The first instalment of the High School Musical series, it stars Zac Efron as basketball captain Troy Bolton and Vanessa Hudgens as shy transfer student Gabriella Montez, who meet singing karaoke and, to the dismay of their cliques, audition together for East High School's musical.

Plot

Troy and Gabriella's audition threatens the school's social order: drama star Sharpay Evans (Ashley Tisdale) and her brother Ryan scheme to keep the leads, while Troy's teammates and Gabriella's scholastic decathlon team try to pull them back to their expected roles. After sabotage plots fail, Troy and Gabriella perform "Breaking Free" and win the lead roles, as their teams win the championship game and decathlon on the same afternoon.

Reception and legacy

Premiering on Disney Channel on 20 January 2006 to 7.7 million viewers, the film became a phenomenon: its soundtrack was the best-selling album of 2006 in the United States, featuring "We're All in This Together" and "Breaking Free". Two sequels followed — High School Musical 2 (2007) and the theatrical High School Musical 3: Senior Year (2008) — along with stage productions, a concert tour, and the later Disney+ series High School Musical: The Musical: The Series.
