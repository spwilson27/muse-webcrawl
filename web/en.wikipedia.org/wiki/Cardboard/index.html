Cardboard

Cardboard is a generic term for heavy paper-based products, including paperboard, corrugated fiberboard, and card stock. In common usage it usually refers to corrugated cardboard, the fluted material used for shipping boxes, though technically that is corrugated fiberboard.

Cardboard
Material: Paper pulp
Types: Paperboard, corrugated fiberboard, card stock
Uses: Packaging, boxes, displays

Types

Paperboard is a thick single-layer sheet used for cereal boxes and packaging cartons. Corrugated fiberboard sandwiches a wavy (fluted) inner layer between flat linerboards, giving strength with low weight. Card stock is a stiff paper used for cards and craft.

Recycling

Cardboard is one of the most widely recycled materials; old corrugated containers (OCC) are collected and repulped to make new board. Recycling rates exceed 90% in some countries, though waxed or food-soiled board is harder to process.
