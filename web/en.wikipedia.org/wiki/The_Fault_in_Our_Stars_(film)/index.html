The Fault in Our Stars (film)

The Fault in Our Stars is a 2014 American coming-of-age romantic drama film directed by Josh Boone, based on the 2012 novel of the same name by John Green. It stars Shailene Woodley and Ansel Elgort as Hazel Grace Lancaster and Augustus "Gus" Waters, two teenagers with cancer who fall in love.

Plot

Hazel Grace Lancaster, a sixteen-year-old with thyroid cancer that has spread to her lungs, reluctantly attends a cancer support group in Indianapolis, where she meets Augustus Waters, a charming seventeen-year-old in remission from osteosarcoma that cost him a leg. Bonded by their wit and a shared love of Hazel's favourite novel, An Imperial Affliction, they travel to Amsterdam to confront its reclusive author about the book's unresolved ending. Gus's cancer returns aggressively, and he dies eight days after a pre-funeral he arranged so he could hear his friends' eulogies; Hazel later finds the eulogy he wrote for her.

Production

Fox 2000 optioned the novel soon after publication. Filming began in Pittsburgh in August 2013, with additional scenes shot in Amsterdam. The film's title derives from Cassius's line in Shakespeare's Julius Caesar: "The fault, dear Brutus, is not in our stars, / But in ourselves".

Reception

The film received positive reviews, with particular praise for Woodley's performance, and was a major commercial success, topping the box office in its opening weekend. It won the Teen Choice Award for Choice Movie: Drama and numerous other audience awards, and its soundtrack single "All of the Stars" by Ed Sheeran became a hit.
