Good Morning America

Good Morning America (often abbreviated GMA) is an American morning television program broadcast on ABC. It premiered on November 3, 1975, and airs weekday mornings from a studio in Times Square, New York City, mixing news, interviews, weather, and entertainment features.

History

The program grew out of ABC's earlier The Morning Exchange format. Long-time hosts include David Hartman, Joan Lunden, Charles Gibson, and Diane Sawyer. In the current era the anchor team has featured Robin Roberts, George Stephanopoulos, and Michael Strahan. GMA has periodically led the morning ratings, surpassing NBC's Today in the 2010s after decades of competition.

Format

The two-hour broadcast leads with hard news and weather, then moves to lifestyle, health, entertainment, and consumer segments; a third hour, GMA3: What You Need to Know, airs in the early afternoon.
