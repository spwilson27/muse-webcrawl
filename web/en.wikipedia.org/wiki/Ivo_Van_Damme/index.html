Ivo Van Damme

Ivo Van Damme
Born: 21 February 1954, Brussels, Belgium
Died: 29 December 1976, Orange, France
Sport: Middle-distance running

Ivo Van Damme (21 February 1954 – 29 December 1976) was a Belgian middle-distance runner. He won silver medals in both the 800 metres and 1500 metres at the 1976 Summer Olympics in Montreal.

Career

Van Damme broke Roger Moens's long-standing Belgian 800 metres record in 1975 and won the European indoor title over 800 metres in 1976. At the Montreal Olympics he finished second in the 800 metres behind Alberto Juantorena and second in the 1500 metres behind John Walker, becoming one of Belgium's most celebrated athletes.

Death and legacy

Van Damme died in a car accident near Orange, France, in December 1976, aged 22. The Memorial Van Damme athletics meeting in Brussels, founded in his honour in 1977, became one of the world's leading track and field events and a fixture of the Diamond League.
