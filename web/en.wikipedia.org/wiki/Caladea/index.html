Caladea is a serif typeface designed by Polish type designer Łukasz Dziedzic and released by Google in 2013. It was created as a metric-compatible counterpart to Microsoft's Cambria typeface: Caladea shares Cambria's character widths and general proportions, allowing documents set in Cambria to be rendered with Caladea without reflowing text. Because of this compatibility, Caladea is often used as a free substitute for Cambria in open-source office software such as LibreOffice.

Design

Caladea is a transitional serif face with moderately high stroke contrast, sturdy serifs and a large x-height that make it legible at small sizes and on low-resolution screens. Although metrically matched to Cambria, its letterforms are visually distinct, with softer, more classical proportions. It supports Latin, Greek and Cyrillic scripts and includes regular, bold, italic and bold italic styles.

Licensing and use

Caladea is distributed under the Apache License 2.0 as part of Google's Croscore fonts project (alongside Carlito, the metric-compatible match for Calibri, and other liberation-oriented families). It is available through Google Fonts and is bundled with ChromeOS and many Linux distributions.
