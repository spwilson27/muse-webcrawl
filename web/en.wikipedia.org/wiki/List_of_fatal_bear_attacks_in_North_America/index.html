List of fatal bear attacks in North America

This is a list of fatal bear attacks in North America by decade and species. Fatal encounters are rare; most involve brown bears (grizzly bears) in the northwestern United States, Canada, and Alaska, or American black bears, which are responsible for a number of predatory attacks in Canada and the eastern United States.

Context

Fatalities average a few per decade from black bears and somewhat more from brown bears in remote regions. Wildlife agencies advise carrying bear spray, storing food securely, and making noise while hiking in bear country to reduce encounter risk.
