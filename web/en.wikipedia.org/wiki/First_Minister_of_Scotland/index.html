First Minister of Scotland

First Minister of Scotland
Style: First Minister (informal); The Right Honourable (formal)
Residence: Bute House, Edinburgh
Appointer: The monarch, on the nomination of the Scottish Parliament
Formation: 7 May 1999
First holder: Donald Dewar
Deputy: Deputy First Minister of Scotland

The First Minister of Scotland (Scottish Gaelic: Prìomh Mhinistear na h-Alba) is the head of the Scottish Government and is the keeper of the Great Seal of Scotland. The first minister chairs the Scottish Cabinet and is primarily responsible for the formulation, development and presentation of Scottish Government policy. The office was created by the Scotland Act 1998, which established the devolved Scottish Parliament and executive; the first holder, Donald Dewar, took office in May 1999. The first minister is nominated by vote of the Scottish Parliament and formally appointed by the monarch, and must normally be a Member of the Scottish Parliament (MSP).

Role and powers

The first minister leads the Scottish Government in all devolved matters — including health, education, justice and policing, housing, transport, environment, and (since the Scotland Acts 2012 and 2016) substantial taxation and welfare powers — appoints the other Scottish ministers and the law officers (the Lord Advocate and Solicitor General for Scotland), represents Scotland in relations with the UK Government, and presides over the Cabinet. The first minister answers questions in the Scottish Parliament at the weekly First Minister's Questions. The office is distinct from that of the Secretary of State for Scotland, a UK cabinet minister.

Election and term

Following a Scottish Parliament election, or a vacancy, the Parliament has 28 days to nominate a first minister; MSPs vote, and the Presiding Officer recommends the winner to the monarch for appointment. The first minister serves until resignation, death, or loss of the Parliament's confidence, there being no fixed statutory term apart from the five-year election cycle. The official residence is Bute House in Edinburgh's Charlotte Square, and the principal office is St Andrew's House.

List of first ministers

  • Donald Dewar (Labour), 1999–2000 — died in office.
  • Henry McLeish (Labour), 2000–2001 — resigned over the "Officegate" expenses affair.
  • Jack McConnell (Labour), 2001–2007.
  • Alex Salmond (SNP), 2007–2014 — led the government during the 2014 independence referendum.
  • Nicola Sturgeon (SNP), 2014–2023 — Scotland's first woman first minister and longest-serving holder of the office.
  • Humza Yousaf (SNP), 2023–2024 — the first Muslim to lead a UK national government.
  • John Swinney (SNP), from May 2024.
