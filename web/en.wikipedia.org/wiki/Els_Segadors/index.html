"Els Segadors" (Eastern Catalan: [əls səɣəˈðos]; "The Reapers") is the official national anthem of Catalonia, Spain. It is based on a Catalan folk story of the Corpus de Sang (the "Bloody Corpus" revolt) of 7 June 1640, an event during the Reapers' War.

History

The original song dates back to the 17th century, originating during the Reapers' War (1640–1659), in which Catalans revolted against the rule of Philip IV of Spain. The modern version of the anthem, with lyrics by Emili Guanyavents (1899) set to a melody standardized by Francesc Alió (1892), dates from the late 19th century. It was officially declared the national anthem of Catalonia by the Catalan Parliament's National Anthem Act of 1993.

The song narrates the June 1640 Corpus de Sang revolt in Barcelona, when Catalan peasants ("segadors", reapers) rose against the Castilian troops quartered in Catalonia during the Franco-Spanish War, an uprising that began the Reapers' War and led to the brief proclamation of a Catalan Republic under Pau Claris.

Lyrics

The best-known stanza and refrain run:

Catalunya triomfant,
tornarà a ser rica i plena.
Endarrera aquesta gent
tan ufana i tan superba.

Bon cop de falç,
bon cop de falç,
defensors de la terra!
Bon cop de falç!

(Catalonia triumphant shall again be rich and bountiful. Drive away these people, so conceited and so arrogant. Strike with the sickle, defenders of the land!)
