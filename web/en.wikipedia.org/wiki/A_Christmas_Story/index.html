A Christmas Story

A Christmas Story
Director: Bob Clark
Starring: Peter Billingsley, Melinda Dillon, Darren McGavin
Release: November 18, 1983

A Christmas Story is a 1983 American Christmas comedy film directed by Bob Clark, based on Jean Shepherd's semi-fictional anecdotes in In God We Trust: All Others Pay Cash. Narrated by Shepherd, it follows young Ralphie Parker in 1940 Indiana who wants a Red Ryder BB gun for Christmas.

Plot

Ralphie campaigns for the BB gun despite warnings "you'll shoot your eye out", while episodes include the leg lamp, a tongue frozen to a flagpole, and a Chinese restaurant Christmas dinner. Initially modest at the box office, it became a holiday classic through television marathons.

Legacy

TNT/TBS run 24-hour marathons annually. The film was selected for the National Film Registry in 2012.
