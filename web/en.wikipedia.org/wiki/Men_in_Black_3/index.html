Men in Black 3

Men in Black 3 (stylised as MIB³) is a 2012 American science fiction action comedy film directed by Barry Sonnenfeld and the third instalment in the Men in Black film series. It stars Will Smith and Tommy Lee Jones, with Josh Brolin as the younger Agent K and Jemaine Clement as the villain, Boris the Animal.

Plot

The alien criminal Boris the Animal escapes from a lunar prison after 40 years and travels back in time to 1969 to kill the young Agent K, who had captured him and deployed the ArcNet, a shield protecting Earth from his species, the Boglodites. When the adult K is erased from the present timeline, Agent J — the only one who remembers him — follows Boris back to 1969. Working with the young K, J races to deploy the ArcNet aboard the Apollo 11 launch at Cape Canaveral. The mission succeeds, but its commander, Colonel James Darrell Edwards Jr, is killed helping them; young J witnesses the scene — Edwards is J's father — and the young K neuralyzes the boy, choosing to protect him, a revelation that reframes J's whole relationship with his partner.

Reception

The film received generally positive reviews, with praise for Brolin's uncanny impersonation of Jones and for a more emotional storyline than the second film. It grossed $654 million worldwide, making it the highest-grossing film in the franchise and one of the top ten earners of 2012, though its high production cost made it only modestly profitable.
