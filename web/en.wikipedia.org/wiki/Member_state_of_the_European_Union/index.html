Member state of the European Union

The European Union (EU) is a political and economic union of 27 member states located primarily in Europe. Each member state is a party to the founding treaties of the union and is subject to the privileges and obligations of membership. Unlike other international organisations, the member states of the EU have transferred, or pooled, significant sovereign competences to common institutions through the treaties.

History of enlargement

The union began with the six founding members of the European Coal and Steel Community and the European Economic Community in the 1950s: Belgium, France, Italy, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, and West Germany. Successive enlargements added Denmark, Ireland, and the United Kingdom (1973); Greece (1981); Portugal and Spain (1986); Austria, Finland, and Sweden (1995); ten countries in the largest single enlargement of 2004 (Cyprus, the Czech Republic, Estonia, Hungary, Latvia, Lithuania, Malta, Poland, Slovakia, and Slovenia); Bulgaria and Romania (2007); and Croatia (2013). The United Kingdom withdrew from the union on 31 January 2020 following the Brexit referendum, reducing the membership from 28 to 27.

Rights and obligations

Member states participate in the European single market, guaranteeing free movement of goods, capital, services, and people. Citizens of member states hold EU citizenship in addition to their national citizenship. Member states are represented in the Council of the European Union and elect members of the European Parliament. Twenty member states use the euro as their currency, and most participate in the Schengen Area of passport-free travel. Membership requires meeting the Copenhagen criteria: stable democratic institutions, the rule of law, a functioning market economy, and acceptance of the acquis communautaire, the body of EU law.

List of member states

The 27 member states are: Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, the Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, the Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, and Sweden. Germany is the most populous member state, while Malta is the smallest.
