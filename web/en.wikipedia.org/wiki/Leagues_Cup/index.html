Leagues Cup

Founded2019
RegionNorth America (CONCACAF)
ParticipantsClubs from MLS (United States and Canada) and Liga MX (Mexico)

The Leagues Cup is an annual association football competition between clubs from Major League Soccer (MLS) and Liga MX of Mexico. It was first held in 2019 as a small knockout tournament and was radically expanded in 2023 to include every club from both leagues.

Format

In its expanded form, the tournament is played in the United States and Canada during a summer pause in both league seasons. Teams play a group stage followed by single-elimination knockout rounds; tied knockout matches go directly to penalties. The top finishers earn places in the CONCACAF Champions Cup.

History

Mexican clubs won the early editions, with Cruz Azul and León triumphing in 2019 and 2021. The first expanded edition in 2023 was won by Inter Miami, whose signing of Lionel Messi drew global attention to the tournament.
