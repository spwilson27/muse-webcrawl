Mount Semeru, also known as Mahameru ("The Great Mountain"), is an active stratovolcano in East Java, Indonesia. At 3,676 metres (12,060 ft), it is the highest mountain on the island of Java and one of the most active volcanoes in Indonesia.

Semeru lies within the Bromo Tengger Semeru National Park. In Hindu tradition on Java and Bali, the mountain is identified with Mount Meru, the axis of the universe, and is a sacred site; its name derives from Sumeru, the central mountain of Hindu and Buddhist cosmology.

Eruptive history

Semeru has been in a state of near-continuous eruption since 1967, with small explosive eruptions from the Jonggring Saloko crater occurring at intervals of tens of minutes. Larger eruptions have periodically devastated surrounding villages. A major eruption on 4 December 2021 produced pyroclastic flows that destroyed homes in Lumajang Regency, killed more than fifty people, and damaged the Gladak Perak bridge, a key transport link. Further significant eruptive episodes occurred in December 2022.

Climbing

Before its recent activity restricted access, Semeru was one of Indonesia's most popular climbs. The standard route from Ranu Pani village passes the lakeside camp at Ranu Kumbolo and the savanna of Oro-oro Ombo before the steep summit cone of loose scree.
