Haft-sin

Haft-sin
Persian: هفت‌سین
Observed for: Nowruz
Region: Iran and Persian diaspora

Haft-sin (Persian: هفت‌سین, "seven S's") is an arrangement of seven symbolic items whose names begin with the letter S (sin) in Persian, traditionally displayed at Nowruz, the Persian New Year celebrated at the vernal equinox.

The seven items

The traditional seven are: sabzeh (sprouted wheat or lentils, symbolising rebirth), samanu (sweet pudding, affluence), senjed (oleaster fruit, love), sir (garlic, health), sib (apple, beauty), somaq (sumac, the colour of sunrise), and serkeh (vinegar, patience and longevity).

Additional items

The table (sofreh) commonly also includes a mirror, candles, painted eggs, goldfish, hyacinth flowers, coins, and a book of poetry — often the Divan of Hafez — or, in Muslim families, the Qur'an. Families gather around the Haft-sin at the exact moment of the equinox.
