Electronic navigational chart (ENC)
Format: vector, IHO S-57 / S-101
Producer: national hydrographic offices
Display system: ECDIS

An electronic navigational chart (ENC) is an official digital nautical chart produced by or under the authority of a national hydrographic office. ENCs are vector charts conforming to International Hydrographic Organization standards and are designed for use in an Electronic Chart Display and Information System (ECDIS).

Features

Unlike a scanned raster chart, an ENC stores chart features — coastlines, depth contours, wrecks, buoys, traffic separation schemes — as a database of objects with attributes. Mariners can query objects, set safety contours, and receive alarms when a vessel's projected track approaches danger.

Carriage requirements

Under the SOLAS convention, most large commercial vessels are required to carry ECDIS with up-to-date ENCs as their primary means of navigation, with corrections distributed regularly by hydrographic offices and regional ENC centres.

Standards

The S-57 transfer standard long defined ENC data; the newer S-100 framework and its S-101 ENC product specification add richer data and interoperability with other digital maritime products such as bathymetry and weather layers.
