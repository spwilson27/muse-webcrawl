Bethesda Softworks LLC is an American video game publisher based in Rockville, Maryland. The company was founded by Christopher Weaver in 1986 as a division of Media Technology Limited, and it became a subsidiary of ZeniMax Media in 1999.

History

Bethesda was founded in Bethesda, Maryland, and its early output included sports games such as Gridiron! and titles in the Wayne Gretzky Hockey series. In 1990 the company moved into role-playing games, publishing The Terminator and later beginning The Elder Scrolls series with Arena in 1994.

In 1999 Weaver and Robert A. Altman founded ZeniMax Media as a parent company, and Bethesda became its publishing label, while game development was spun into the sister studio Bethesda Game Studios. In 2004 Bethesda acquired the rights to the Fallout series from Interplay Entertainment, releasing Fallout 3 in 2008.

Studios and acquisition

Under ZeniMax, Bethesda published games from studios including id Software, Arkane Studios, MachineGames, Tango Gameworks and ZeniMax Online Studios. In March 2021 Microsoft completed its $7.5 billion acquisition of ZeniMax Media, making Bethesda part of Xbox Game Studios.

Published games

Notable franchises published by Bethesda include The Elder Scrolls, Fallout, Doom, Wolfenstein, Dishonored, Prey, The Evil Within, Rage, Starfield and Hi-Fi Rush.
