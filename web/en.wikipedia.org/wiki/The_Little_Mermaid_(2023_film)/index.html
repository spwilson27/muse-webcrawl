The Little Mermaid is a 2023 American musical romantic fantasy film directed by Rob Marshall from a screenplay by David Magee. It is a live-action remake of Disney's 1989 animated film of the same name, itself based on the 1837 fairy tale by Hans Christian Andersen.

Halle Bailey stars as Ariel, a mermaid princess fascinated with the human world who falls in love with Prince Eric (Jonah Hauer-King) and makes a dangerous bargain with the sea witch Ursula (Melissa McCarthy) to become human.

Plot

Ariel, the youngest daughter of King Triton, longs to explore the world beyond the sea. After rescuing Prince Eric from a shipwreck, she trades her voice to Ursula for three days as a human, hoping to win Eric's love before the deadline. With the help of Sebastian the crab, Flounder and Scuttle the gannet, Ariel defeats Ursula and is permanently transformed by her father, who accepts her choice.

Music

Alan Menken returned to compose the score, with new songs written with Lin-Manuel Miranda, including "Wild Uncharted Waters", "For the First Time" and "The Scuttlebutt", alongside Howard Ashman's original songs such as "Part of Your World", "Under the Sea" and "Kiss the Girl".

Reception

The film received mixed-to-positive reviews, with praise for Bailey's performance and the musical numbers, and grossed $569.6 million worldwide against a production budget of roughly $240–265 million.
