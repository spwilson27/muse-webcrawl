Bene Gesserit

The Bene Gesserit are a fictional sisterhood in Frank Herbert's Dune universe, first appearing in Dune (1965). A secretive matriarchal order, its members train their bodies and minds through years of physical and mental conditioning to gain superhuman powers and abilities.

Bene Gesserit
Universe: Dune
Type: Sisterhood / school
Key figures: Gaius Helen Mohiam, Lady Jessica
Home: Wallach IX (Chapterhouse in later novels)

Abilities

Acolytes master prana-bindu control of muscles and nerves, the Voice (compelling obedience through vocal modulation), acute observation, truthsaying, and — after surviving the spice agony — access to Other Memory, the ancestral memories of their female forebears. Full members are Reverend Mothers.

Plans

For millennia the Bene Gesserit ran a secret breeding programme to produce the Kwisatz Haderach, a male who could access both male and female ancestral memory and see the future. The programme culminated unexpectedly in Paul Atreides, son of Lady Jessica.

Influence

The order is a major political force behind the scenes of the Imperium, placing wives, concubines and advisors in noble houses.
