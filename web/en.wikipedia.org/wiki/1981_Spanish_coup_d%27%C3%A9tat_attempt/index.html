1981 Spanish coup d'état attempt

Date23 February 1981
LocationMadrid and Valencia, Spain
ResultCoup failed; democracy preserved

The 1981 Spanish coup d'état attempt, known in Spain as 23-F, was a failed military coup carried out on 23 February 1981. Civil Guard officer Antonio Tejero led armed men into the Congress of Deputies during a vote to elect a new prime minister, taking the deputies hostage.

Events

Tejero stormed the chamber firing shots, while in Valencia General Jaime Milans del Bosch declared a state of emergency and sent tanks into the streets. The coup plotters hoped to install a military-led government and reverse Spain's democratic transition after Franco.

Failure

King Juan Carlos I, refusing to support the coup, appeared on television in uniform in the early hours of 24 February and ordered the armed forces to uphold the constitutional order. Loyal units stood down, Milans del Bosch surrendered, and Tejero released the deputies and was arrested. The leaders were tried and imprisoned, and the failure of 23-F consolidated Spanish democracy.
