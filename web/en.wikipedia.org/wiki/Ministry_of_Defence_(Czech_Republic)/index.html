Ministry of Defence (Czech Republic)

The Ministry of Defence of the Czech Republic (Czech: Ministerstvo obrany České republiky) is the central organ of state administration of the Czech Republic responsible for military defence of the country and for the Armed Forces of the Czech Republic.

Responsibilities

The ministry oversees defence planning, the budget of the armed forces, military intelligence cooperation, and the Czech Republic's participation in NATO, which the country joined in 1999. The Minister of Defence is a member of the Government of the Czech Republic appointed by the President on the proposal of the Prime Minister.
