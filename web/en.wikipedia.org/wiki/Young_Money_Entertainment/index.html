Young Money Entertainment is an American record label founded by rapper Lil Wayne in 2005. The label operated for many years as an imprint of Cash Money Records, distributed through Universal/Republic. Young Money achieved major commercial success in the late 2000s and 2010s by launching the careers of Drake and Nicki Minaj, two of the best-selling recording artists of their generation, alongside its founder.

History

Lil Wayne founded Young Money in 2005 while serving as president of Cash Money Records. The label built its roster through the late 2000s, signing Drake in 2009 — in a joint venture with Cash Money and Aspire Music Group — and Nicki Minaj the same year. The label's compilation album We Are Young Money (2009) produced the hit single "BedRock".

Young Money artists dominated popular music in the early 2010s: Drake's Thank Me Later and Take Care, Minaj's Pink Friday series, and Wayne's Tha Carter IV all debuted at number one in the United States. The label's roster also included Tyga, Gudda Gudda, Jae Millz, Lil Chuckee, Lil Twist, Mack Maine, Shanell, and T-Streets, among others.

Legal disputes and independence

From 2015, Lil Wayne was embroiled in a legal dispute with Cash Money over royalties and the delayed release of Tha Carter V. A settlement in 2018 resolved the dispute: Wayne exited Cash Money, became sole owner of Young Money, and released Tha Carter V through Young Money and Republic Records.

Artists

Notable current and former acts include Lil Wayne, Drake (whose later releases moved to his own OVO Sound and Republic arrangement), Nicki Minaj, Tyga, Cory Gunz, and Euro.
