List of countries by level of military equipment

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This article lists countries by the approximate scale of their major military equipment, including active-duty military personnel, main battle tanks, combat aircraft and major naval vessels, based principally on data published in The Military Balance by the International Institute for Strategic Studies (IISS). Equipment counts vary by source and by whether stored, reserve or obsolete items are included; figures below are approximate.

Nuclear weapons

Nine states possess nuclear weapons: Russia and the United States hold the great majority of the world's roughly 12,000 warheads, followed by China (expanding, estimated at several hundred), France, the United Kingdom, Pakistan, India, Israel and North Korea.
