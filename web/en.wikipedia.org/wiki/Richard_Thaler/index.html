Richard Thaler

Richard H. Thaler (born 12 September 1945) is an American economist and professor at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business, a founder of behavioural economics, and recipient of the 2017 Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences for contributions to behavioural economics.

Work

Thaler documented systematic deviations from rational-choice theory — the endowment effect, mental accounting, loss aversion and quasi-rational behaviour — in works including The Winner's Curse (1992) and Misbehaving (2015). With Cass Sunstein he co-authored Nudge (2008), promoting libertarian paternalism and choice architecture; ideas applied in auto-enrolment pensions (Save More Tomorrow, with Shlomo Benartzi) and government nudge units.
