Pea soup

Pea soup or split pea soup is a soup made typically from dried peas, such as the split pea. It is a part of the cuisine of many cultures and is usually green or yellow depending on the variety of pea used.

Varieties

In the Netherlands, erwtensoep (snert) is a thick stew of split peas, pork and vegetables eaten in winter. Swedish ärtsoppa is traditionally served on Thursdays with pancakes. In French Canada, soupe aux pois made with yellow peas and ham is a classic of habitant cuisine. Finnish pea soup is served with mustard and rye bread.

History

Peas have been cultivated since antiquity, and pea soup is mentioned in Aristophanes' comedy The Birds (414 BC). Dried peas kept well, making the soup a staple for sailors and armies; the dense London fogs of the 19th century were nicknamed "pea-soupers" after its colour.
