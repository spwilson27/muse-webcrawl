Education in India

Education in India is provided by public schools and private schools, regulated by the Union Government and the governments of each state. Under the Constitution of India, education is a concurrent subject shared by both levels of government. The Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2009 guarantees free education to children aged 6 to 14. The National Education Policy 2020 restructured schooling into a 5+3+3+4 design replacing the older 10+2 system.

Structure

The school system comprises pre-primary, primary, upper primary, secondary and higher secondary stages. Major national boards include the CBSE and CISCE, alongside state boards. Competitive entrance examinations such as JEE and NEET govern admission to prestigious engineering and medical institutions, including the Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs) and All India Institutes of Medical Sciences.

Higher education

India has one of the largest higher education systems in the world, with central universities, state universities, deemed universities, and institutions of national importance such as the IITs, IIMs and NITs. Literacy rose from about 18 percent at independence in 1947 to roughly three-quarters of the population in recent censuses, though challenges of quality, access and equity remain.
