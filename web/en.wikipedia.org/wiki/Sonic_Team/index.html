Sonic Team (Japanese: ソニックチーム, Sonikku Chīmu) is a Japanese video game developer owned by Sega. It is best known for the Sonic the Hedgehog series, one of the best-selling video game franchises of all time.

History

The team was formed in 1990 within Sega's AM8 division. Programmer Yuji Naka, artist Naoto Ohshima, and designer Hirokazu Yasuhara collaborated to create a mascot character and a fast-paced platform game to compete with Nintendo's Mario. The result, Sonic the Hedgehog (1991) for the Mega Drive/Genesis, was a major success that helped Sega gain a large share of the North American console market.

The division took the Sonic Team name in 1994. Members of the team relocated to the United States to form Sega Technical Institute, which developed Sonic the Hedgehog 2 and Sonic Spinball. Back in Japan, the team developed games for the Sega Saturn, including Nights into Dreams (1996) and Burning Rangers (1998).

Dreamcast era and restructuring

For the Dreamcast, Sonic Team produced Sonic Adventure (1998), the launch title that introduced fully 3D gameplay for the series, as well as ChuChu Rocket!, Samba de Amigo, and the pioneering online RPG Phantasy Star Online (2000). In 2000 the team became a semi-autonomous subsidiary, Sega subsidiary Sonicteam, Ltd., before being reintegrated into Sega in 2004 during corporate restructuring.

Later work

Sonic Team continues to develop the Sonic series, including Sonic Generations (2011), Sonic Mania in collaboration with independent developers (2017), Sonic Frontiers (2022), and Sonic Superstars (2023). The division also oversees the franchise's expansion and collaborations with Sega's other studios.
