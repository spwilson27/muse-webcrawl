Million Dollar Quartet

The Million Dollar Quartet is the name given to an impromptu jam session recorded on 4 December 1956 at Sun Studio in Memphis, Tennessee, featuring Elvis Presley, Jerry Lee Lewis, Carl Perkins and Johnny Cash — four of the biggest stars in early rock and roll and country music.

The session

Perkins was recording with Lewis on piano when Presley, by then an RCA star, dropped by; Cash also joined. Studio owner Sam Phillips had the tapes running and alerted the local press, whose photograph the next day carried the "Million Dollar Quartet" caption. The musicians sang mostly gospel and country favourites.

Legacy

The recordings were released decades later, and the session was dramatised in the stage musical Million Dollar Quartet, which opened on Broadway in 2010.
