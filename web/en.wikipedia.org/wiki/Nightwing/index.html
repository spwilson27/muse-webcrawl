Nightwing is a superhero identity used by several characters in DC Comics, most famously Dick Grayson, the original Robin, who adopted the name as an adult hero based in Blüdhaven.

Nightwing

Nightwing is the alias taken by a number of fictional superheroes appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. The name originated as a Kryptonian identity used by Superman in 1960s stories, but it is overwhelmingly associated with Dick Grayson, the first Robin, Batman's youthful partner.

Dick Grayson

Richard "Dick" Grayson was a circus acrobat whose parents were murdered; taken in by Bruce Wayne, he became Robin, the Boy Wonder (introduced 1940). As a young adult — in stories of the early 1980s, notably Marv Wolfman and George Pérez's New Teen Titans — Grayson stepped out of Batman's shadow and adopted the new identity of Nightwing, taking the name from the Kryptonian legend Superman had once told him. He subsequently starred in his own long-running solo series (1996–2009 and again from 2016), operating mainly in the crime-ridden city of Blüdhaven, and has served as leader of the Teen Titans and the Outsiders. During a period when Bruce Wayne was believed dead, Grayson himself took up the mantle of Batman.

In other media

Nightwing has appeared in animated series including The New Batman Adventures, Young Justice and Teen Titans, in video games such as the Batman: Arkham series, and in live action in the series Titans (2018–2023), played by Brenton Thwaites.
