Visakhapatnam

Country: India
State: Andhra Pradesh
Nickname: Vizag, "City of Destiny"
Known for: Major port, steel plant, Eastern Naval Command

Visakhapatnam (also known as Vizag) is the largest and most populous metropolitan city in the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh. It lies on the coast of the Bay of Bengal, between the Eastern Ghats and the sea, and is one of India's major port cities. Following the bifurcation of Andhra Pradesh in 2014, Visakhapatnam was designated the state's executive capital and seat of government under the three-capital plan proposed by the state government.

History

The region around Visakhapatnam formed part of the Kalinga kingdom and later came under the Eastern Ganga, Gajapati, Vijayanagara and Golconda rulers. The city's name is traditionally derived from Visakha, the Hindu god of valour. It developed as a trading port under British rule, and the natural harbour — sheltered by Dolphin's Nose headland — made it strategically important. Visakhapatnam Port was opened to ocean traffic in 1933. During the Second World War it was a naval base, and it later became headquarters of the Indian Navy's Eastern Naval Command.

Economy

Visakhapatnam is a major industrial city. Its economy rests on the port, the Visakhapatnam Steel Plant (Rashtriya Ispat Nigam), shipbuilding at Hindustan Shipyard, petroleum refining, fertilisers, and a growing information technology sector. The city is often called the "City of Destiny" and ranks among India's fastest-growing urban economies. The Port of Visakhapatnam handles bulk cargo including iron ore, coal and petroleum products, and a second major port operates at Gangavaram.

Geography and climate

The city occupies a coastal strip backed by hill ranges such as the Kailasagiri and Yarada hills. It has a tropical wet and dry climate, hot and humid in summer, with rainfall from both the southwest and retreating northeast monsoons. Cyclones occasionally strike the coast; Cyclone Hudhud made landfall directly over the city in October 2014, causing extensive damage.

Tourism

Attractions include the Ramakrishna and Rishikonda beaches, the INS Kursura submarine museum, Kailasagiri hill park, the Borra Caves and the Araku Valley in the nearby Eastern Ghats, served by a scenic railway line.

Transport

Visakhapatnam is connected by National Highway 16, a busy railway junction on the Howrah–Chennai main line, and Visakhapatnam International Airport. A new greenfield international airport at Bhogapuram is under development to serve the growing metropolitan region.
