PokerStars

The PokerStars online poker lobby

PokerStars is an online poker cardroom. Founded in 2001 by Isai and Mark Scheinberg, it grew to become the largest real-money online poker site in the world. It is owned by Flutter Entertainment, following Flutter's 2020 merger with The Stars Group.

History

PokerStars launched real-money games in December 2001 and expanded rapidly during the poker boom of the 2000s. Its profile rose dramatically when Chris Moneymaker, who had qualified through a PokerStars satellite tournament, won the 2003 World Series of Poker Main Event. The company sponsored live tours including the European Poker Tour and ran the World Championship of Online Poker (WCOOP) and Spring Championship of Online Poker (SCOOP).

On 15 April 2011 — "Black Friday" in the poker world — the United States Department of Justice seized the domain names of PokerStars and other major sites and charged their principals with bank fraud and money laundering. PokerStars settled with the Department of Justice in 2012, acquiring the assets of its rival Full Tilt Poker and repaying Full Tilt's non-US players. The site later re-entered regulated US markets in states such as New Jersey, Pennsylvania, and Michigan.

Games

The room offers variants including Texas hold 'em, Omaha, stud, and mixed games, in cash-game, tournament, and sit-and-go formats, and has set records for the largest online poker tournaments ever held.
