Magnus Carlsen

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Sven Magnus Øen Carlsen (born 30 November 1990) is a Norwegian chess grandmaster. He is a five-time World Chess Champion, the reigning five-time World Rapid Chess Champion and the reigning seven-time World Blitz Chess Champion. Carlsen has held the No. 1 position in the FIDE world chess rankings since 1 July 2011 and trails only Garry Kasparov in total time spent as the highest-rated player in the world. His peak rating of 2882 is the highest in history.

Career

A chess prodigy, Carlsen finished first in the C group of the Corus chess tournament shortly after turning 13 and earned the title of grandmaster a few months later. He won the Norwegian Chess Championship at 15 and finished joint first in the top group of Corus at 17. Carlsen defeated Viswanathan Anand in the World Chess Championship 2013 to become World Champion, and defended the title in 2014, 2016, 2018 and 2021. In 2023 he declined to defend the title again, citing a lack of motivation for the classical championship format, and was succeeded by Ding Liren; he remains widely regarded as the strongest player in the world and one of the greatest of all time.

Playing style

Carlsen is noted for his positional mastery, endgame technique and ability to grind down small advantages, as well as for his universal style and exceptional performance in rapid and blitz formats. He is also a co-founder of the Play Magnus Group, which merged with Chess.com in 2022.
