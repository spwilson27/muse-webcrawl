Junior Reserve Officers' Training Corps

The Junior Reserve Officers' Training Corps (JROTC) is a federal programme sponsored by the United States Armed Forces in high schools and some middle schools across the United States and at US military bases worldwide.

Purpose

Authorised by the National Defense Act of 1916 and expanded under the ROTC Vitalization Act of 1964, JROTC aims to instil values of citizenship, service, personal responsibility and accomplishment. It is a citizenship and leadership programme, not a recruiting or military training pipeline; cadets incur no service obligation.

Organisation

Each service branch — Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard and Space Force — sponsors its own JROTC units, taught by retired service members. Activities include drill, academics, community service and leadership labs.
