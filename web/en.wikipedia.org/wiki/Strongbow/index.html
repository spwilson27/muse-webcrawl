Strongbow is a dry cider brand produced by H. P. Bulmer in Hereford, England, and owned by Heineken. Launched in 1960, it is named after the Norman knight Richard de Clare, 2nd Earl of Pembroke, nicknamed "Strongbow" for his prowess with the longbow. It has long been the world's best-selling cider by volume and the leading cider in the United Kingdom.

Production and variants

Strongbow is made from a blend of bittersweet cider apples and culinary apples, fermented and sold on draught, in cans, and in bottles. Variant products have included Strongbow Dark Fruit, Cloudy Apple, and lower-alcohol versions, as well as differently formulated versions for export markets, where in some countries it is produced from apple juice concentrate.

Marketing

Early advertising featured the slogan of arrows thudding into a bar top; later campaigns emphasised refreshment and the brand's Herefordshire orchard heritage. Strongbow has sponsored music festivals and sporting properties in the UK.
