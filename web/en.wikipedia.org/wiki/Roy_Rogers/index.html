Roy Rogers

Roy Rogers (born Leonard Franklin Slye; 5 November 1911 – 6 July 1998) was an American singer, actor and television host, billed as the "King of the Cowboys".

Career

Rogers first found fame with the singing group Sons of the Pioneers, then starred in Western films for Republic Pictures from 1938, alongside his horse Trigger and his wife Dale Evans. The Roy Rogers Show ran on television from 1951 to 1957. His signature song with Evans was "Happy Trails". He appeared in more than 100 films and became a major popular-culture and merchandising figure.
