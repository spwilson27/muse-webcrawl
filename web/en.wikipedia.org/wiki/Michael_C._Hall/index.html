Michael Carlyle Hall (born 1 February 1971) is an American actor. He is best known for playing David Fisher in the HBO drama Six Feet Under (2001–2005) and the title role of forensic analyst and serial killer Dexter Morgan in Showtime's Dexter (2006–2013), a performance that won him a Golden Globe Award.

Career

Hall trained at New York University's Tisch School of the Arts and began on stage, including playing the Emcee in the Broadway revival of Cabaret (1999–2000). Cast by Alan Ball in Six Feet Under, he earned an Emmy nomination for his portrayal of David Fisher. Dexter made him one of television's most recognisable actors; he also served as an executive producer. He reprised the role in Dexter: New Blood (2021–2022) and voiced and appeared in later franchise entries.

On stage he originated the title role in Hedwig and the Angry Inch on Broadway (2014–2015) and played Thomas Jerome Newton in David Bowie's musical Lazarus. His film work includes Cold in July (2014) and Game Night (2018). He is also the singer of the band Princess Goes (formerly Princess Goes to the Butterfly Museum).

Personal life

Hall was treated for Hodgkin's lymphoma, announced in 2010, and subsequently became a spokesperson for the Leukemia & Lymphoma Society.
