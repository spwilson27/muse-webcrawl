FFP mask

An FFP mask is a type of filtering facepiece respirator regulated by the European Union under standard EN 149. FFP stands for "filtering facepiece". The masks protect against airborne particles such as dusts, aerosols and droplets, and are used in construction, industry, healthcare and during respiratory-disease outbreaks.

Classes

EN 149 defines three classes by filtration efficiency and maximum total inward leakage: FFP1 filters at least 80% of airborne particles; FFP2 at least 94%; and FFP3 at least 99%. FFP2 masks are broadly comparable to the American N95 and Chinese KN95 standards, while FFP3 offers the highest protection of the three classes.

Use

FFP masks are typically disposable, single-shift devices. A proper seal against the face is essential; facial hair can compromise fit. Some models include an exhalation valve, which improves comfort but does not filter exhaled air, limiting their usefulness for source control. During the COVID-19 pandemic, FFP2 masks were mandated in some European countries for public transport and shops.

Marking

Certified masks bear the CE mark together with the EN 149 standard number, the FFP class, and whether the mask is reusable (R) or non-reusable (NR).
