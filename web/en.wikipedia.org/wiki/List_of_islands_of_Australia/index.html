List of islands of Australia

Australia is surrounded by thousands of islands. The country has more than 8,000 islands within its maritime borders, ranging from the world's second-largest island state component, Tasmania, to tiny coral cays in the Great Barrier Reef. This list covers the most significant islands by area, population and geography.

Islands by state and territory

Queensland

Queensland's islands include the Whitsunday Islands (including Hamilton Island and Hayman Island), Magnetic Island off Townsville, Hinchinbrook Island, Great Keppel Island, and the Torres Strait Islands, which include Thursday Island and Horn Island. Moreton Island and North Stradbroke Island lie off Brisbane.

Western Australia

Notable islands include Rottnest Island near Perth, the Buccaneer Archipelago, Dirk Hartog Island, Barrow Island and the Montebello Islands.

South Australia

Besides Kangaroo Island, South Australia includes Neptune Island, the Investigator Group and Nuyts Archipelago.

Victoria

Phillip Island, French Island and Gabo Island are among Victoria's best-known islands.

New South Wales

Lord Howe Island, some 600 km offshore, is administered by New South Wales. Other islands include Montague Island and the Five Islands off Wollongong.

Tasmania

Tasmania is itself an island and is surrounded by more than 300 smaller islands, including Bruny Island, Maria Island, King Island and the Furneaux Group.

Northern Territory

The Territory's islands include the Tiwi Islands, Groote Eylandt, the Wessel Islands and the Sir Edward Pellew Group.

External territories

Australia's external territories include Christmas Island, the Cocos (Keeling) Islands, Norfolk Island, Heard Island and McDonald Islands, and the Ashmore and Cartier Islands.
