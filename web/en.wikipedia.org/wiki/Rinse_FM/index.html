Rinse FM is a London-based community radio station and music brand specialising in underground dance music. Founded in 1994 as a pirate radio station broadcasting from rooftops in East London, it became one of the most influential platforms for UK garage, grime and dubstep, and was awarded a legal FM licence by Ofcom in June 2010, nearly 16 years after its launch.

History

Rinse FM was founded in 1994 by the DJ and producer Geeneus, broadcasting initially from Ingram House in Tower Hamlets and later across East London rooftops. Like other pirate stations of the era, it repeatedly evaded raids by the authorities; its transmitters were confiscated on numerous occasions and DJs were given ASBO-style restrictions.

During the early 2000s Rinse was central to the emergence of grime, providing a regular platform for crews and artists such as Dizzee Rascal, Wiley and Roll Deep, and later for dubstep DJs including Skream, Benga, Hatcha and Youngsta. Its roster also extended to UK funky, house and drum and bass.

In June 2010 Ofcom granted Rinse a five-year community radio licence, and the station began legal broadcasting on 106.8 FM across London, as well as online. Rinse has also run a record label, Rinse Recordings, and a series of club nights and compilations.

Presenters

Notable DJs and shows on the station have included Skream, Benga, Oneman, Elijah & Skilliam, Sir Spyro, Plastician, Uncle Dugs and Josey Rebelle. The station's schedule is built around specialist shows in the evening and late-night slots, with daytime programming that includes breakfast and drivetime shows.

Sister stations

The Rinse brand expanded with the launch of Rinse France in Paris in 2014, run in partnership with Parisian promoters, reflecting the station's influence on the French underground scene.
