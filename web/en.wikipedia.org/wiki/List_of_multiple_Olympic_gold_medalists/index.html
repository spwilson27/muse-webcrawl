List of multiple Olympic gold medalists

This is a list of multiple Olympic gold medalists, listing athletes who have won multiple Olympic gold medals. American swimmer Michael Phelps is by far the most successful, with 23 gold medals won between 2004 and 2016 — more than twice the total of any other athlete in history.
