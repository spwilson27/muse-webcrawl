BAE Systems

BAE Systems plc

Founded: 30 November 1999

Headquarters: London, United Kingdom

Industry: Defence, aerospace, security

Employees: roughly 100,000 (2020s)

BAE Systems plc is a British multinational defence, aerospace and security company headquartered in London. It is the largest defence contractor in Europe and among the largest worldwide, with major operations in the United Kingdom, the United States (through BAE Systems Inc.), Saudi Arabia, Australia and Sweden.

Formation

BAE Systems was created on 30 November 1999 by the £7.7 billion merger of British Aerospace (BAe) with Marconi Electronic Systems, the defence electronics arm of GEC. The deal outmanoeuvred expectations that BAe would merge with Germany's DASA, and created a vertically integrated group spanning combat aircraft, warships, submarines, armoured vehicles, munitions and electronics.

Products and programmes

The company's flagship programmes include the Eurofighter Typhoon (built with Airbus and Leonardo), a major share of the F-35 Lightning II, the Royal Navy's Astute-class and Dreadnought-class submarines built at Barrow-in-Furness, Type 26 frigates, and armoured vehicles such as the CV90. BAE is a partner in the Global Combat Air Programme (Tempest) with Italy and Japan. Its US arm is a top supplier to the Pentagon across land, air, sea and electronic-warfare markets. Controversies have accompanied some exports, including the Al-Yamamah arms deal with Saudi Arabia, which was investigated by the Serious Fraud Office before the inquiry was halted in 2006 on national-security grounds.

Operations

BAE Systems reports its business through segments including Air, Maritime, Land, Electronic Systems, and Cyber & Intelligence. The United States accounts for the largest share of revenue, followed by the UK and Saudi Arabia.
