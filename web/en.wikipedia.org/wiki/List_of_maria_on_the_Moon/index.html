List of maria on the Moon

The lunar maria (singular: mare) are large, dark basaltic plains on Earth's Moon, formed by ancient volcanic eruptions. They were dubbed maria, Latin for "seas", by early astronomers who mistook them for actual seas. This article lists the maria on the Moon.

The dark patches visible on the Moon's near side are the maria.

Related features

Smaller dark plains are called lacus (lakes), sinus (bays) and palus (marshes), such as Sinus Iridum (Bay of Rainbows), Lacus Somniorum (Lake of Dreams) and Palus Epidemiarum (Marsh of Epidemics).
