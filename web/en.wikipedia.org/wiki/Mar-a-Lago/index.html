Mar-a-Lago is a resort and National Historic Landmark in Palm Beach, Florida. It was built from 1924 to 1927 by cereal heiress Marjorie Merriweather Post in the Hispano-Moorish Revival style. The name is Spanish for "sea to lake", reflecting that the estate spans the width of the island.

History

Post willed the estate to the United States government in 1973 as a presidential retreat, but it was returned to her foundation in 1981 because of maintenance costs. Businessman Donald Trump bought it in 1985 and converted it into a private members' club, the Mar-a-Lago Club, which opened in 1995.

Trump era

During his presidency (2017–2021), Trump used Mar-a-Lago as his "Winter White House". After leaving office it became his primary residence. In August 2022 the FBI executed a search warrant there in an investigation into government documents, an event that drew worldwide attention.

Architecture

The main house has 126 rooms and roughly 62,500 square feet of living space, decorated with Dorian stone, antique Spanish tiles and gold leaf. The grounds include a beach club, ballrooms added by Trump, and a tunnel under South Ocean Boulevard.
