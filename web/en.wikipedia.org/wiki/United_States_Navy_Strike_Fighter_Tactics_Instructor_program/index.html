United States Navy Strike Fighter Tactics Instructor program

The United States Navy Strike Fighter Tactics Instructor program (SFTI program), more popularly known as Top Gun (stylized as TOPGUN), is a United States Navy training program that teaches selected naval aviators and naval flight officers the tactics and techniques of air combat and strike warfare. The program is part of the Naval Aviation Warfighting Development Center (NAWDC) at Naval Air Station Fallon in Nevada.

History

The school was established on March 3, 1969, at Naval Air Station Miramar in California, in response to the findings of the Ault Report, which had identified poor air-to-air combat performance by Navy crews during the Vietnam War. Navy pilots had been achieving an exchange ratio of roughly 2.5 to 1 against North Vietnamese MiGs; after the school's tactics were disseminated, the Navy's kill ratio improved dramatically, to about 13 to 1 by some measures, while the Air Force (which lacked an equivalent program at the time) saw little improvement.

Originally housed in a surplus trailer at Miramar, the school trained crews flying aircraft such as the F-4 Phantom II against adversary aircraft flown to mimic opposing fighters. In 1996 the school moved to NAS Fallon and was merged into the Naval Strike and Air Warfare Center, later reorganized as NAWDC.

The course

The SFTI course lasts about thirteen weeks and covers air-to-air and air-to-ground tactics, including basic fighter maneuvers, section and division tactics, and strike planning. Students — experienced fleet pilots and weapon systems officers — fly against instructors acting as adversaries in aircraft painted in distinctive schemes. Graduates return to their squadrons as squadron training officers, spreading the tactics they learned throughout the fleet.

In popular culture

The school achieved worldwide fame through the 1986 film Top Gun, starring Tom Cruise, and its 2022 sequel Top Gun: Maverick. The films significantly boosted Navy recruiting and made "Top Gun" a household name.
