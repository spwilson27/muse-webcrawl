Root-mean-square deviation

The root-mean-square deviation (RMSD) or root-mean-square error (RMSE) is a measure of the differences between values predicted by a model or estimator and the values observed. It is the square root of the average of the squared differences, so it is expressed in the same units as the quantity measured and penalises large errors more heavily than the mean absolute error.

Formula

For a set of n predictions ŷi and observations yi: RMSE = √( (1/n) Σ (ŷi − yi)² ).

Applications

RMSE is widely used in meteorology, air-quality modelling, geostatistics and machine learning as a standard metric of model accuracy. In structural biology, RMSD denotes the average distance between the atoms of superimposed protein structures, used to quantify structural similarity and the accuracy of molecular dynamics simulations and structure predictions.
