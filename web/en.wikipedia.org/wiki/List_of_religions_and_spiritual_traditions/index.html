List of religions and spiritual traditions

This list of religions and spiritual traditions catalogues the principal religious movements of the world, organised by family and region of origin. Religions are commonly grouped into Abrahamic, Indian (Dharmic), Iranian, East Asian, indigenous and new religious movements. Adherent counts are approximate and vary widely by source.

Iranian religions

  • Zoroastrianism — founded by the prophet Zoroaster; once the state religion of Persia.
  • Yazdânism, Manichaeism (extinct as an organised religion), and the Yarsan faith.

Indigenous and folk religions

  • African traditional religions, including Yoruba religion, Vodun and Odinani.
  • Native American religions and Mesoamerican religions (Aztec, Maya).
  • Siberian and Arctic shamanic traditions; Tengrism of Central Asia.
  • Aboriginal Australian spirituality centred on the Dreaming.

New religious movements

  • The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints (Mormonism), founded 1830.
  • Jehovah's Witnesses; Seventh-day Adventism; Christian Science.
  • Tenrikyo, Oomoto and other Japanese new religions.
  • Modern Paganism, including Wicca, Heathenry and Druidry.
  • Scientology, the Unification Church, Falun Gong, and the Raëlian movement.
