Dame Barbara Windsor (born Barbara Ann Deeks; 6 August 1937 – 10 December 2020) was an English actress, known for her performances in the Carry On films and for playing Peggy Mitchell in the BBC One soap opera EastEnders. She joined the cast of EastEnders in 1994 and won the 1999 British Soap Award for Best Actress, before leaving the show in 2016.

Early life

Windsor was born in Shoreditch, London, the only child of John Deeks, a costermonger, and his wife Rose, a dressmaker. She was evacuated to Blackpool during the Second World War and attended dance and elocution lessons before training at the Aida Foster Theatre School in Golders Green.

Career

She made her stage debut at the age of 13 and her West End debut in the chorus of the musical Love From Judy in 1952. Her film debut came in The Belles of St Trinian's (1954). Windsor came to prominence in Joan Littlewood's Theatre Workshop productions, including Fings Ain't Wot They Used T'Be, and the film Sparrows Can't Sing (1963), for which she received a BAFTA nomination.

Between 1964 and 1974 she appeared in nine Carry On films, including Carry On Spying, Carry On Doctor and Carry On Camping, becoming one of the series' most recognisable stars, famed for her infectious giggle and comic timing.

EastEnders

In 1994 Windsor joined EastEnders as Peggy Mitchell, matriarch of the Mitchell family and landlady of The Queen Victoria pub. Her catchphrase "Get outta my pub!" became famous. She left the role in 2010, returned for guest appearances, and made her final appearance in 2016 when the character died by suicide in a storyline addressing terminal illness.

Personal life and death

Windsor was married three times, lastly to Scott Mitchell from 2000. She was appointed MBE in 2000 and DBE in 2016 for services to charity and entertainment. After being diagnosed with Alzheimer's disease in 2014, she and her husband campaigned to raise awareness of dementia. She died on 10 December 2020, aged 83.
