Chilika Lake

LocationOdisha, India
TypeBrackish water lagoon
Surface area~900–1,165 km² (seasonal)
DesignationRamsar site (1981)

Chilika Lake is a brackish water lagoon on the east coast of India, spread over the Puri, Khordha and Ganjam districts of Odisha at the mouth of the Daya River, flowing into the Bay of Bengal. It is the largest coastal lagoon in India and one of the largest in the world.

Ecology

Chilika is a biodiversity hotspot, famed for its wintering migratory birds — including flamingos at Nalabana Island — and for its population of Irrawaddy dolphins. It was designated a Ramsar wetland of international importance in 1981.

Fisheries

The lagoon supports a rich fishery that sustains over 150,000 fisherfolk in surrounding villages.
