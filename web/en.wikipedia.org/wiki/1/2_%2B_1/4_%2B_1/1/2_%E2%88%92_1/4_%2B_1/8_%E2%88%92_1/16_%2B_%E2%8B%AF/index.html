1/2 − 1/4 + 1/8 − 1/16 + ⋯

In mathematics, the infinite series 1/2 − 1/4 + 1/8 − 1/16 + ⋯ is a simple example of an alternating series that converges absolutely. It is a geometric series with first term 1/2 and common ratio −1/2.

Sum

Using the formula for the sum of a geometric series, the sum is (1/2)/(1 − (−1/2)) = (1/2)/(3/2) = 1/3. The partial sums oscillate around 1/3, converging to it as the number of terms increases.
