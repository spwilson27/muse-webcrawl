0.999...

0.999... (also written 0.9̅, 0.(9)) is a repeating decimal that, in mathematics, denotes the same real number as 1. The identity 0.999... = 1 is a standard result of real analysis, provable from the definition of the real numbers and the properties of infinite series.

Proofs

The number can be seen as the sum of the geometric series 9/10 + 9/100 + 9/1000 + ⋯ = 1. Elementary arguments include multiplying by 10 and subtracting, and the absence of any number lying strictly between 0.999... and 1. Reception

The equality surprises many students, and it is a popular topic in discussions of mathematical rigor and the completeness of the real numbers.
