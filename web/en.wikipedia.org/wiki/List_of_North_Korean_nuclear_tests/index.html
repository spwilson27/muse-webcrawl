List of North Korean nuclear tests

North Korea has conducted six underground nuclear tests between 2006 and 2017, all at the Punggye-ri Nuclear Test Site in Kilju County, North Hamgyong Province. The tests drew condemnation and successive rounds of United Nations Security Council sanctions.

Aftermath

In 2018 North Korea announced a moratorium on nuclear tests and the demolition of tunnel entrances at Punggye-ri in the presence of foreign journalists. Satellite imagery in later years suggested restoration activity at the site, though no seventh test had occurred as of subsequent reporting.
