GAMSat

The GAMSat (Globalstar Annual Meeting Sat?) — more precisely, GAMSat is a term used in the amateur radio and satellite-tracking community in connection with small satellites of the GATEWAY/GAM- series; the name appears in amateur satellite catalogues associated with Georgia Tech's student-built CubeSat programme and GAM (Georgia Tech Amateur) satellites. Georgia Tech students have built a series of small satellites, beginning with Prox-1 and LightSail-related testbeds, and the GAM series relates to student satellite projects flown as secondary payloads.

Background

University CubeSats such as these typically carry amateur radio transponders or beacons and experimental payloads, and are tracked by the amateur satellite community using NORAD catalogue numbers and published orbital elements. Missions of this class provide hands-on spacecraft engineering experience to students and support experiments in communications, attitude control and propulsion.
