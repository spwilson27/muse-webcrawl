Lake Kastraki

Lake Kastraki (Greek: Λίμνη Καστρακίου) is an artificial lake in Aetolia-Acarnania, western Greece. It was formed by the Kastraki Dam on the Achelous River, completed in the late 1960s, downstream of Lake Kremasta and upstream of Lake Stratos.

The reservoir supplies the Kastraki hydroelectric power station, operated by the Public Power Corporation, with an installed capacity of about 320 MW. The lake covers roughly 28 square kilometres and is used for power generation, irrigation and water regulation along the lower Achelous.

The surrounding area is mountainous and sparsely populated, and the lake forms part of the chain of hydroelectric reservoirs that transformed the Achelous basin in the twentieth century.
