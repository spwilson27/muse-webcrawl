Legion of Super-Pets

The Legion of Super-Pets is a fictional team of super-powered animals in the DC Comics universe. Modelled on the Legion of Super-Heroes, the team unites the animal companions of Superman and other heroes: Krypto the Super-Dog, Streaky the Supercat, Beppo the Super-Monkey and Comet the Super-Horse, later joined by Proty II, a shapeshifting protoplasmic creature.

Publication history

The team first appeared in Adventure Comics #293 (February 1962), in a story written by Jerry Siegel with art by Curt Swan, in which the super-animals band together to defeat an alien menace. They made occasional appearances during the Silver Age of Comic Books, often in tales told from the pets' point of view while their owners were away. Like many whimsical Silver Age concepts, the Super-Pets largely disappeared after the 1985–86 Crisis on Infinite Earths reboot, but members — especially Krypto — were later reintroduced, and the team returned in modern continuity and in all-ages publications.

Members

Krypto, a Kryptonian dog with Superman's powers; Streaky, Supergirl's cat, who gained powers from "X-Kryptonite"; Beppo, a Kryptonian monkey who stowed away on baby Kal-El's rocket; Comet, a centaur magically transformed into a horse with telepathic powers; and Proty II, an alien shapeshifter. Later members in various versions include Titus, the dog of Damian Wayne, and Flexi the Plastic Bird.

In other media

The team inspired the animated film DC League of Super-Pets (2022), in which Krypto (voiced by Dwayne Johnson) assembles a group of shelter animals who gain superpowers to rescue the Justice League. Members of the team also appear in the animated series Krypto the Superdog and Legion of Super Heroes.
