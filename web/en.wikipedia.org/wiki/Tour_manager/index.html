Tour manager

A tour manager (or concert tour manager) is an individual who organises the logistics of a musical act's concert tour, handling travel, accommodation, finances and day-to-day problem-solving on the road.

Responsibilities

  • Booking and coordinating transport, hotels and catering
  • Managing the tour budget and settling payments with venues and promoters
  • Producing itineraries and day sheets for the touring party
  • Liaising with venue staff, local crews and security
  • Handling emergencies, schedule changes and publicity obligations

Distinction from road manager

In smaller tours the roles of tour manager and road manager may be combined; on large productions the tour manager oversees overall administration while road managers handle specific aspects such as backline equipment and stage logistics.

Career

Tour managers typically work freelance for artists, management companies or promoters, and experienced managers are essential to the smooth running of arena and stadium tours.
