List of recurring The Simpsons characters

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

In addition to the Simpson family, the animated sitcom The Simpsons features one of the largest supporting casts in television, populating the town of Springfield. Many characters are voiced by the show's small core cast — principally Dan Castellaneta, Hank Azaria and Harry Shearer — and several have become as iconic as the family itself. This list covers recurring Springfield residents; one-off guest characters and the family itself are covered elsewhere.

Media and entertainment

  • Krusty the Clown (Dan Castellaneta) — a cynical, chain-smoking children's entertainer, born Herschel Krustofsky.
  • Sideshow Bob (Kelsey Grammer) — Krusty's former sidekick, an erudite would-be murderer of Bart.
  • Sideshow Mel (Dan Castellaneta) — Bob's silent, bone-haired replacement.
  • Kent Brockman (Harry Shearer) — Springfield's pompous Channel 6 news anchor.
  • Itchy and Scratchy — the hyper-violent cartoon cat and mouse within the show.
  • Rainier Wolfcastle (Harry Shearer) — an Arnold Schwarzenegger-like action star who plays McBain.
  • Bumblebee Man (Hank Azaria) — the slapstick star of a Spanish-language sitcom.

Antagonists and others

  • "Fat Tony" D'Amico (Joe Mantegna) — head of the Springfield Mafia.
  • Snake Jailbird (Hank Azaria) — Springfield's most frequent criminal.
  • Kang and Kodos (Harry Shearer, Dan Castellaneta) — Rigellian aliens appearing in every Treehouse of Horror episode.
  • Captain Horatio McCallister (Hank Azaria) — the Sea Captain, owner of The Frying Dutchman.
  • Cletus Spuckler (Hank Azaria) — the slack-jawed yokel with a huge hillbilly family.
  • Jasper Beardley (Harry Shearer) — a senior citizen, friend of Abe Simpson.
