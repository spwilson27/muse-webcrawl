Tokyo Bay Aqua-Line

The Tokyo Bay Aqua-Line (東京湾アクアライン) is a bridge–tunnel combination across Tokyo Bay in Japan, connecting Kawasaki in Kanagawa Prefecture with Kisarazu in Chiba Prefecture. Opened on 18 December 1997, it is 23.7 km long overall, including a 9.6 km undersea tunnel — among the world's longest — and a 14.1 km bridge.

At the junction sits Umihotaru, an artificial island with a rest area. The project drastically shortened travel between the two sides of the bay compared with driving around it.
