Cybill Lynne Shepherd (born February 18, 1950) is an American actress and former model. She made her film debut in Peter Bogdanovich's The Last Picture Show (1971) and went on to star in The Heartbreak Kid (1972) and Martin Scorsese's Taxi Driver (1976).

Career

After a period of less successful films, Shepherd achieved major television success as Maddie Hayes opposite Bruce Willis in the ABC comedy-drama Moonlighting (1985–1989), winning two Golden Globe Awards. She later headlined the sitcom Cybill (1995–1998), loosely based on her own life, winning another Golden Globe. Other credits include The L Word, Psych, and a recording career as a jazz and standards singer. Her memoir, Cybill Disobedience, was published in 2000.
