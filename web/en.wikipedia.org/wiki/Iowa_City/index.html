Iowa City is a city in and the county seat of Johnson County, Iowa, United States. It was the first capital of the state of Iowa (from 1846 until the capital moved to Des Moines in 1857) and is home to the University of Iowa. The city's Old Capitol building, with its gold-leafed dome, remains a landmark at the centre of the university's Pentacrest.

History

Iowa City was created by an act of the Legislative Assembly of Iowa Territory in 1839 to serve as the territory's new capital, replacing Burlington. Commissioners chose a site on bluffs above the Iowa River, and the town was platted the same year. When Iowa became a state in 1846, Iowa City became its first capital. The University of Iowa was founded in 1847, and the arrival of the railroad in 1855 brought waves of settlers, including many Czech and German immigrants.

Culture and education

Anchored by the university — including the celebrated Iowa Writers' Workshop — Iowa City has an outsized literary reputation and was designated a UNESCO City of Literature in 2008, the third city so honoured. The city is also a major medical centre through the University of Iowa Hospitals and Clinics.
