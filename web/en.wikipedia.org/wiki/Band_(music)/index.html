Band (music)

Band (music) (image placeholder)

A musical ensemble, also known as a music group or musical group, is a group of people who perform instrumental or vocal music. In classical music, ensembles are described by the number of performers — duet, trio, quartet, quintet, sextet, septet, octet — or by their instrumentation, such as a string quartet or wind quintet. In popular music, a group of musicians is commonly called a band.

Popular music bands

A typical rock or pop band consists of a lead vocalist, one or more guitarists, a bassist, a drummer, and often a keyboard player. Named forms include the power trio, and in jazz the big band of a dozen or more musicians arranged in brass, saxophone, and rhythm sections. Marching bands, brass bands, and concert bands feature wind and percussion instruments and are associated with schools, military units, and communities.

Organisation

Bands typically perform under a fixed name, write or select shared repertoire, and may be led by a bandleader; members' respective roles in songwriting, arrangements, and business arrangements vary widely and are frequent sources of both creative chemistry and disputes.
