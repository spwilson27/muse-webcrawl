Lonesome Dove
Author: Larry McMurtry
Published: 1985

Lonesome Dove is a 1985 Western novel by American writer Larry McMurtry. It follows retired Texas Rangers Augustus 'Gus' McCrae and Woodrow F. Call on a cattle drive from the Rio Grande to Montana.

Story

Leaving the small town of Lonesome Dove, the Hat Creek outfit drives cattle north through danger and loss, in a narrative that deconstructs and honours the myths of the American West.

Reception and adaptation

The novel won the Pulitzer Prize for Fiction in 1986 and was adapted into an acclaimed 1989 television miniseries starring Robert Duvall and Tommy Lee Jones. It spawned sequels and prequels including Streets of Laredo.
