Pottery

Pottery
DefinitionCeramic ware made by shaping and firing clay
AgeAmong humanity's oldest crafts (20,000+ years)
[Image: Hands shaping a clay pot on a spinning potter's wheel]

Pottery is the ceramic material and ware made by forming clay vessels and firing them at high temperature. Among the oldest human inventions — fragments in China date to about 20,000 years ago — pots stored grain (Wheat), carried water and became art from Greek vases to Ming porcelain.

Craft

The potter's wheel, glazes and kilns reached industrial scale with porcelain factories; studio pottery revived hand-making as art and hobby (see Sewing and Knitting for the textile kin). Archaeologists date cultures by their potsherds — pottery is history's most durable diary.
