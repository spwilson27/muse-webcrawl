Email marketing

Email marketing is the act of sending a commercial message, typically to a group of people, using email. In its broadest sense, every email sent to a potential or current customer could be considered email marketing; the term usually refers to messages intended to build loyalty, trust, or brand awareness, and to encourage purchases.

Types

Common forms include promotional campaigns, newsletters, transactional emails (order confirmations, receipts), and automated behavioural campaigns such as welcome series and abandoned-cart reminders. Permission-based "opt-in" marketing is considered best practice and is required by laws such as the EU's GDPR and ePrivacy rules and the US CAN-SPAM Act.

History

The first mass commercial email is often attributed to Gary Thuerk, who in 1978 sent an ARPANET message promoting DEC computers — an early example of what became known as spam.
