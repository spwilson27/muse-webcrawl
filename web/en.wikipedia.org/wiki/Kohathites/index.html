Kohathites

AncestorKohath, son of Levi
TribeLevi
DutyCarrying the most sacred objects of the Tabernacle

The Kohathites were one of the four main divisions among the Levites in biblical times, descended from Kohath, the second son of Levi. Together with the Gershonites, Merarites and Aaronites, they formed the Levitical families charged with the care and transport of the Tabernacle during Israel's wanderings in the wilderness.

Duties

According to the Book of Numbers, the Kohathites were responsible for the most holy furnishings of the sanctuary — the Ark of the Covenant, the Table of Showbread, the menorah, the altars and the sacred vessels. Because of the sanctity of these objects, the Kohathites were not permitted to touch or even look upon them directly; the priests first covered the items, and the Kohathites then carried them on their shoulders using poles. Unlike the other Levitical families, they were given no carts or oxen for transport.

Notable descendants

The family of Moses and Aaron belonged to the Kohathite line through Amram, Kohath's son, and the priestly Aaronites were a branch of the Kohathites. Other prominent Kohathite descendants include the prophet Samuel, the singer Heman, and Korah, whose rebellion against Moses is recounted in Numbers 16. In the settlement of Canaan, the Kohathites received cities in the territories of Judah, Simeon, Benjamin, Ephraim, Dan and Manasseh.
