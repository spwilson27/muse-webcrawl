Senate House is the administrative centre of the University of London, in Bloomsbury, London. Designed by Charles Holden in a monumental stripped-classical style clad in Portland stone, it was among London's earliest skyscrapers.

History

Built in 1932–1937 as the centrepiece of a never-completed master plan for the university precinct, it houses Senate House Library, one of the UK's major research libraries. During the Second World War it accommodated the Ministry of Information, inspiring the Ministry of Truth in George Orwell's Nineteen Eighty-Four, written while his wife worked there.

Architecture and culture

The symmetrical tower and courtyards combine modern steel-frame construction with classical massing. The building, Grade II* listed, is a popular filming location (e.g., Batman Begins, Fast & Furious 6) and hosts events including London Fashion Week shows and graduation ceremonies.
