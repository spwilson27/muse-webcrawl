Soho


Location: City of Westminster, London
Postcode district: W1
Known for: Entertainment, media, dining, nightlife

Soho is an area of the City of Westminster in the West End of London. Originally a fashionable district for the aristocracy, it has been one of London's main entertainment districts since the 19th century, known for its restaurants, clubs, theatres, media offices, and cosmopolitan character.

History

The name derives from a former hunting cry, first recorded in the 17th century when the area was developed from farmland. In the 18th and 19th centuries, successive waves of immigrants — Huguenots, Italians, Greeks, and Chinese — gave Soho a bohemian and international flavour. It became associated with the theatre, the sex industry in the mid-20th century, and later the British film and advertising industries centred on Wardour Street.

Culture

Soho contains Carnaby Street, famous for 1960s fashion; Berwick Street market; numerous theatres; and the centre of London's Chinatown around Gerrard Street. It is also the heart of London's LGBTQ+ scene, with Old Compton Street its focal point, and contains the parish church of St Anne and Soho Square, laid out in the 1680s.

Other districts named SoHo — most famously in New York City — took their names from the London original combined with local geographic references.
