SS Rona was a steam-powered merchant vessel. The prefix "SS" denotes a screw steamer — a ship propelled by steam power driving a propeller. Records bearing the name survive chiefly in shipping lists, inspection registers and casualty indexes of the age of steam, in which RONA appears among the vessels documented by maritime authorities.

Little consolidated narrative history is recorded for the vessel beyond such registry entries; the name Rona (after the Scottish island of Rona) was used for several ships in the 19th and 20th centuries, including later motor vessels and the yacht Rona associated with other owners.
