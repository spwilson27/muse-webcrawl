Kenneth Alfred Ham (born 20 October 1951) is an Australian-American young Earth creationist and founder, CEO and president of Answers in Genesis (AiG), a Christian apologetics organisation that operates the Creation Museum and the Ark Encounter theme park in Kentucky, United States.

Career

A former science teacher in Queensland, Ham became involved with creationist organisations in Australia in the 1970s before moving to the United States in 1987 to work with the Institute for Creation Research. In 1994 he founded what became Answers in Genesis–USA. The Creation Museum opened in Petersburg, Kentucky, in 2007, and the Ark Encounter — a full-size interpretation of Noah's Ark, about 155 m long — opened in Grant County in 2016.

Views and criticism

Ham advocates a literal reading of the Book of Genesis: creation in six days about 6,000 years ago, a global flood, and the rejection of evolution as incompatible with Christianity. In 2014 he publicly debated science communicator Bill Nye ("the Science Guy") at the Creation Museum, an event watched by millions online. His views are rejected by the scientific community, and the Ark Encounter's financing and hiring practices have attracted controversy and litigation.
