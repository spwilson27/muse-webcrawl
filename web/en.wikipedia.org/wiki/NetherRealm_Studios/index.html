NetherRealm Studios
Type: Subsidiary of Warner Bros. Games
Founded: 2010, Chicago, Illinois
Industry: Video games
Key people: Ed Boon (creative director)

NetherRealm Studios is an American video game developer based in Chicago, Illinois, and a subsidiary of Warner Bros. Games. It was founded in 2010 to replace Midway Games' Chicago studio, which Warner Bros. had acquired in bankruptcy, and it continues the work of the team behind the Mortal Kombat series under co-creator Ed Boon.

Games

The studio rebooted its flagship franchise with Mortal Kombat (2011), followed by Mortal Kombat X (2015), Mortal Kombat 11 (2019) and Mortal Kombat 1 (2023). It also developed the DC Comics fighting games Injustice: Gods Among Us (2013) and Injustice 2 (2017), along with mobile titles. Its games are noted for cinematic story modes unusual in the fighting genre.
