Margaret Scott (dancer)

Dame Margaret Scott AC DBE (1922 – 2019) was a South African-born Australian ballet dancer, choreographer, and educator, best known as the founding director of The Australian Ballet School. Born in Johannesburg, she trained in London and danced with Ballet Rambert and Sadler's Wells.

Career in Australia

Scott moved to Australia in the 1940s, performed with the Borovansky Ballet, and in 1964 became the first director of The Australian Ballet School in Melbourne, a post she held for 26 years. She shaped generations of Australian dancers and was made a Dame Commander of the Order of the British Empire in 1981 and a Companion of the Order of Australia in 2005.
