The National Academy of Television Arts and Sciences (NATAS) is an American professional service organization founded in 1955 for persons in the television industry. It is best known for presenting several of the Emmy Awards, the premier honors for American television.

NATAS presents several categories of the Emmy Awards.

Role

NATAS administers the Daytime Emmy Awards, the Sports Emmy Awards, the News and Documentary Emmy Awards, and technology and engineering honors. It operates through 19 regional chapters across the United States, which present regional Emmy Awards and run scholarship, student production and professional development programs.

Relationship with the Television Academy

The Emmys are divided between NATAS and the Los Angeles-based Television Academy (formally the Academy of Television Arts & Sciences), which presents the Primetime Emmy Awards. The two academies grew out of a single organization founded in 1946 and separated in 1977, dividing responsibility by daypart and genre.
