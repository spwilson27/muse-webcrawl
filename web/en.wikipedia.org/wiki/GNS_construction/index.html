Gelfand–Naimark–Segal construction

In functional analysis, the Gelfand–Naimark–Segal (GNS) construction associates to every state on a C*-algebra a representation of the algebra on a Hilbert space. It is named after Israel Gelfand, Mark Naimark and Irving Segal.

Construction

Given a C*-algebra A and a state φ (a positive linear functional of norm one), one forms the sesquilinear form (a, b) ↦ φ(a*b) on A, quotients out the null space, and completes to obtain a Hilbert space Hφ. Left multiplication then defines a representation πφ of A on Hφ, with the image of the identity acting as a cyclic vector.

Significance

The GNS construction shows that C*-algebras have enough states and representations to be faithfully embedded in bounded operators on a Hilbert space, and it is fundamental in the mathematical formulation of quantum mechanics and quantum field theory, where states on an observable algebra generate concrete Hilbert-space realisations.
