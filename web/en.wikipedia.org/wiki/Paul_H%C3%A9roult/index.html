Paul Héroult

Paul Louis-Toussaint Héroult (10 April 1863 – 9 May 1914) was a French scientist who independently invented the electrolytic process for producing aluminium and later developed the electric arc furnace for steel.

Born10 April 1863, Thury-Harcourt, France
Died9 May 1914 (aged 51)
Known forHall–Héroult process; electric arc furnace

Aluminium process

In 1886, at the age of 23, Héroult filed a French patent for producing aluminium by electrolysis of alumina dissolved in molten cryolite — the same method Charles Martin Hall patented in the United States the same year. The Hall–Héroult process made aluminium cheap and remains the basis of world production.

Electric steel furnace

Héroult also designed an electric arc furnace for steelmaking (patented around 1900), which became widely used in industry. He spent much of his later career developing aluminium production in France and died in 1914.
