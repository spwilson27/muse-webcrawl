John Garang de Mabior (June 23, 1945 – July 30, 2005) was a Sudanese politician and revolutionary leader. He founded and led the Sudan People's Liberation Army/Movement (SPLA/M) during the Second Sudanese Civil War, and after the 2005 Comprehensive Peace Agreement he became First Vice President of Sudan and President of the Government of Southern Sudan.

Early life and education

Born to a Dinka family in Wangulei, Garang was orphaned young and educated in Tanzania before winning a scholarship to Grinnell College in Iowa. He later earned a doctorate in agricultural economics from Iowa State University, with a thesis on agricultural development in southern Sudan.

The SPLA and the civil war

A former Anyanya officer who had been integrated into the Sudanese army, Garang was sent in 1983 to quell a mutiny of southern soldiers at Bor; instead he joined and reorganised them, founding the SPLA. For two decades he led a guerrilla war against successive Khartoum governments, advocating a united, secular, democratic "New Sudan" rather than outright southern secession.

Peace agreement and death

Garang negotiated the Comprehensive Peace Agreement signed in January 2005, ending Africa's longest-running civil war. Sworn in as First Vice President in July 2005, he died three weeks later in a helicopter crash in southern Sudan. He was succeeded by Salva Kiir, and South Sudan became independent in 2011 following the referendum he had secured.
