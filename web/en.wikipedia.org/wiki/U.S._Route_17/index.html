U.S. Route 17

U.S. Route 17 (US 17) is a north–south United States Numbered Highway running about 1,200 miles (1,900 km) along the Atlantic coast from Punta Gorda, Florida, to Winchester, Virginia. It passes through Jacksonville, Savannah, Charleston and Wilmington, largely paralleling Interstate 95.

Route highlights

In Florida the highway is known as the Coastal Highway; in South Carolina it forms much of the "Grand Strand" corridor through Myrtle Beach and crosses Charleston by the Arthur Ravenel Jr. Bridge. US 17 Business routes serve many coastal towns where the mainline has been bypassed.
