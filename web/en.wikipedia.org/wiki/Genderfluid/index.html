Genderfluid

Genderfluid describes a gender identity that shifts over time or in different situations: a genderfluid person may at times feel more male, more female, a combination of genders, or neither. It falls under the broader umbrellas of non-binary and transgender identities.

Usage

The term gained currency in the 1990s and 2000s within genderqueer communities. Genderfluid people may use different pronouns at different times, or consistently use gender-neutral pronouns such as singular they. The changes in identity may be gradual or sudden, and need not involve any change in gender expression.
