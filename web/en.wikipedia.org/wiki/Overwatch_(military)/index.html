Overwatch (military)

In modern warfare, overwatch is a force protection tactic in which one small unit or military vehicle supports another by providing covering fire while the latter moves. The overwatching element takes a position where it can observe the terrain ahead, especially likely enemy positions, and engage them if the moving element comes under fire.

Overwatch is a fundamental element of fire and movement and bounding overwatch, in which units alternately move and cover each other. It is used by infantry, armour and sniper teams, and a designated marksman or machine gun may be tasked with overwatch of a patrol or convoy.

The tactic reduces vulnerability during movement across exposed ground, at the cost of slower advance, and depends on reliable communication between the moving and overwatching elements.
