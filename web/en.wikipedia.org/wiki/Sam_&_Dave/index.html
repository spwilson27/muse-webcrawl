Sam & Dave

Origin: Miami, Florida, U.S.
Genres: Soul, R&B
Years active: 1961–1981
Members: Sam Moore, Dave Prater
Labels: Stax, Atlantic

Sam & Dave were an American soul and R&B duo who performed together from 1961 until 1981. The tenor (Sam Moore) and baritone/tenor (Dave Prater) voices gave them an immediately recognisable call-and-response sound rooted in gospel. Recording for Stax Records in Memphis with the house band Booker T. & the M.G.'s and songwriters Isaac Hayes and David Porter, they produced a run of hits including "Hold On, I'm Comin'" (1966) and "Soul Man" (1967), the latter winning the Grammy Award for Best Rhythm & Blues Performance.

Career

Sam Moore (1935–2025) and Dave Prater (1937–1988) met in Miami in 1961 and signed first to Roulette Records before joining Atlantic, which loaned them to Stax in 1965. Their Stax recordings of the later 1960s—among them "You Don't Know Like I Know", "When Something Is Wrong with My Baby", and "I Thank You"—made them one of the most successful soul acts of the era and an electrifying live attraction, earning them the nickname "Double Dynamite". When the Atlantic–Stax deal ended in 1968 their chart success faded, though they continued recording and touring through the 1970s.

Legacy

The duo's popularity revived after the Blues Brothers performed "Soul Man" on Saturday Night Live and in their 1980 film. Sam & Dave were inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 1992, and "Soul Man" entered the Grammy Hall of Fame. Dave Prater died in a car accident in Georgia in 1988; Sam Moore continued as a solo performer until his death in 2025.
