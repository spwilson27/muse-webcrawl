Enamel paint

Enamel paint is a paint that air-dries to a hard, usually glossy, finish, used for coating surfaces that are outdoors or otherwise subject to hard wear or temperature variation. The term is today applied mainly to oil-modified polyesters used for hard, wear-resistant coatings, but historically it referred to any paint giving a finish resembling vitreous enamel.

Types and uses

Enamel paints are commonly used on wood, metal, and masonry for applications such as kitchen and bathroom surfaces, outdoor furniture, model building, and signage. Varieties include floor enamel, fast-dry enamel, and high-temperature enamel used on engine blocks and exhaust systems. Although enamel paint can be removed with paint thinner, it is durable and resistant to moisture once cured.
