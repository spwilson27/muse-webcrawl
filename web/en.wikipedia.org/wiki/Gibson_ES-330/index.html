The Gibson ES-330 is a fully hollow archtop electric guitar manufactured by Gibson from 1959 to 1972, with numerous reissues since. It is visually similar to the semi-hollow ES-335, but unlike the 335 it has no solid center block, making it lighter and more resonant — and more prone to feedback at high volume.

Design

The ES-330 features a thinline laminated maple body with two f-holes, a glued-in mahogany neck, and two P-90 single-coil pickups. It was offered in one- and two-pickup versions (ES-330T and ES-330TD) and in sunburst, natural, and cherry finishes.

Players

Grant Green used an ES-330 extensively, and the model has been associated with jazz and blues players who favour its warm, woody tone. Gibson has reissued the guitar several times, including models built to 1959 and 1961 specifications.
