Overview

The Sky at Night is a British television programme about astronomy, broadcast by the BBC since 1957, making it one of the longest-running programmes with a largely consistent format in television history. The monthly show covers current developments in astronomy and space exploration, observing guides for amateur astronomers, and interviews with scientists.

Presenters

The programme was presented by Patrick Moore from its first edition on 24 April 1957 until his death in December 2012 — a run that made him the longest-serving presenter of a single television series. Since then the show has been hosted by a team including Chris Lintott and Maggie Aderin-Pocock.

The Sky at Night has featured interviews with many leading figures in astronomy and spaceflight and has inspired generations of amateur astronomers. A companion magazine was launched in 2005.
