Barbie is a 2023 American fantasy comedy film directed by Greta Gerwig from a screenplay she wrote with Noah Baumbach. Based on the Barbie fashion dolls by Mattel, it is the first live-action Barbie film after numerous animated predecessors. Margot Robbie stars as Barbie and Ryan Gosling as Ken, who journey from Barbieland to the real world after Barbie begins to experience an existential crisis.

Barbieland, the film's brightly coloured doll world.

Plot

In Barbieland, a matriarchal society of Barbies and Kens, "Stereotypical Barbie" begins having thoughts of death and develops flat feet and cellulite. Travelling to the real world with Ken to find the girl who owns her, she discovers the human world is patriarchal — an idea Ken enthusiastically imports back to Barbieland. Barbie ultimately works with her owner Gloria (America Ferrera) and Gloria's daughter Sasha to restore Barbieland, and chooses to become human herself.

Release and reception

The film premiered alongside Christopher Nolan's Oppenheimer, a coincidence that produced the viral "Barbenheimer" phenomenon, with many audiences watching both films as a double feature. Barbie grossed $1.447 billion worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing film of 2023 and the highest-grossing film ever directed solely by a woman. It received eight Academy Award nominations, including Best Picture, and won Best Original Song for "What Was I Made For?" by Billie Eilish and Finneas O'Connell. Gosling's performance of "I'm Just Ken" became a cultural touchstone.

Themes

Critics discussed the film's feminism, its satire of patriarchy and corporate culture — including jokes at the expense of its own producer, Mattel — and its treatment of gender roles and existential anxiety. Gerwig drew on classic Technicolor musicals such as The Wizard of Oz and The Umbrellas of Cherbourg for the practical, painted look of Barbieland.
