Kazakhstan

Kazakhstan, officially the Republic of Kazakhstan, is a landlocked country in Central Asia and Eastern Europe — the world's largest landlocked country and the ninth-largest country overall, at about 2.7 million square kilometres. Its population is about 20 million; the capital is Astana (called Nur-Sultan between 2019 and 2022), and the largest city is Almaty.

Kazakhstan's vast steppe is rich in oil, natural gas, uranium (it is the world's top producer) and minerals. It hosts Russia's Baikonur Cosmodrome, from which Yuri Gagarin launched in 1961. Independent from the Soviet Union in 1991, Kazakhstan was ruled by Nursultan Nazarbayev until 2019; his successor Kassym-Jomart Tokayev suppressed major unrest in January 2022 with Russian-led military assistance.
