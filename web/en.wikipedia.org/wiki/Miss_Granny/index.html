Miss Granny (Korean: Susanghan Geunyeo, "Suspicious Girl") is a 2014 South Korean fantasy-comedy drama film directed by Hwang Dong-hyuk. It stars Na Moon-hee as a woman in her 70s who magically finds herself in the body of her 20-year-old self (played by Shim Eun-kyung) after having her picture taken at a mysterious photo studio.

Plot

Oh Mal-soon, a 74-year-old widow who prides herself on having raised her son, a university professor, now lives with his family but is a constant source of friction with her daughter-in-law. After learning the family is considering sending her to a nursing home, she wanders the streets and steps into a mysterious photo studio, where she is astonished to emerge looking twenty years old again. Taking the new name Oh Doo-ri — after Audrey Hepburn, her favourite actress — she rents a room, joins her grandson's band as its lead singer, and draws the attention of a television music producer, while struggling to keep her secret from her family.

Reception

The film was a major box-office hit in South Korea, drawing over 8.65 million admissions. It was praised for balancing comedy with an emotional look at ageing, sacrifice, and the dreams deferred by motherhood.

Remakes

The film's story has been remade in numerous countries, including China (20 Once Again, 2015), Vietnam, Japan, Thailand, Indonesia, the Philippines (Miss Granny, 2018), and India, making it one of the most widely remade Korean films.
