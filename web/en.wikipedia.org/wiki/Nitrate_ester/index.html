Nitrate ester

A nitrate ester (organic nitrate) is an organic compound containing the nitrate functional group –O–NO2: the ester of nitric acid with an alcohol. Nitrate esters are best known as explosives and as vasodilator drugs.

Examples and uses

Nitroglycerin (glyceryl trinitrate) is both a powerful explosive (the active component of dynamite) and a medicine for angina. Other examples include nitrocellulose (gun cotton, a nitrate ester of cellulose), PETN (pentaerythritol tetranitrate), isosorbide dinitrate and isosorbide mononitrate (cardiac drugs), and ethylene glycol dinitrate.

Properties

The weak O–N bond gives nitrate esters their energy content and sensitivity. In medicine, they act as prodrugs releasing nitric oxide, relaxing vascular smooth muscle; tolerance develops with continuous use. Preparation is typically by nitration of the parent alcohol with mixed nitric and sulphuric acids.
