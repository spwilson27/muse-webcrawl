Otto III, Holy Roman Emperor

Otto III (June/July 980 – 23 January 1002) was Holy Roman Emperor from 996 until his death in 1002. A member of the Ottonian dynasty, Otto III was the only son of Emperor Otto II and his wife Theophanu, a Byzantine princess.

Otto III was crowned King of Germany in 983 at the age of three, shortly before his father's death. After a period of regency under his mother and then his grandmother Adelaide of Italy, he took up personal rule and was crowned emperor in Rome in 996 by Pope Gregory V, his cousin.

His reign pursued the ideal of renovatio imperii Romanorum, the restoration of the Roman Empire, centred on Rome. He installed his tutor Gerbert of Aurillac as Pope Sylvester II, opened the tomb of Charlemagne at Aachen in 1000, and made a pilgrimage to Gniezno in Poland. He died aged 21 during a revolt in Italy and was succeeded by Henry II.
