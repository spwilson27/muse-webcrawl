Nelson Piquet Jr.

Nelson Angelo Tamsma Piquet Souto Maior (born 25 July 1985), known as Nelson Piquet Jr. or Nelsinho Piquet, is a Brazilian racing driver. He is the son of three-time Formula One world champion Nelson Piquet. He raced in Formula One for Renault in 2008 and part of 2009, and became the first champion of Formula E in the 2014–15 season.

Early career

Born in Heidelberg, West Germany, Piquet Jr. began karting in Brazil before moving to single-seaters. He won the South American Formula Three Championship in 2002 and the British Formula 3 Championship in 2004, then finished runner-up in the 2006 GP2 Series to Lewis Hamilton.

Formula One and "crashgate"

Piquet joined Renault as a test driver in 2007 and race driver in 2008, scoring a podium at the German Grand Prix. He was dismissed mid-2009. Weeks later it emerged that he had deliberately crashed at the 2008 Singapore Grand Prix on team orders to trigger a safety car that benefited teammate Fernando Alonso — the "crashgate" scandal, which led to bans for team principal Flavio Briatore and engineer Pat Symonds (later reduced) and a suspended disqualification for Renault.

Later career

Piquet moved to NASCAR in the United States, winning races in the Truck and Nationwide series, then won the inaugural Formula E championship with Team China Racing (NEXTEV). He has also competed in rallycross, stock cars in Brazil, and endurance racing.
