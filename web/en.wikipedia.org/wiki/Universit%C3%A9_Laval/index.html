Université Laval is a public research university in Quebec City, Quebec, Canada. Founded in 1852 under a royal charter from Queen Victoria, it traces its origins to the Séminaire de Québec established by François de Laval in 1663, making it the oldest university in Canada and the first North American institution to offer higher education in French.

Campus and academics

The modern campus, built largely from the 1950s onward in the Sainte-Foy district, hosts faculties spanning medicine, law, science and engineering, agriculture, forestry, and the social sciences, with enrolment of roughly 47,000 students. Research strengths include northern studies, optics and photonics, and cardiology.

Athletics

The university's teams, the Rouge et Or, are among the most successful in Canadian university sport, particularly in football, where the program has won numerous Vanier Cup national championships.
