Sarasota County, Florida

Sarasota County
State: Florida
County seat: Sarasota
Population: about 430,000–450,000

Sarasota County is a county on the Gulf coast of Florida, United States. Its county seat and largest city is Sarasota. It forms part of the North Port–Sarasota–Bradenton metropolitan area.

Geography

The county fronts the Gulf of Mexico with barrier islands including Siesta Key, known for its quartz-sand beaches, Longboat Key and Casey Key. Inland lie the Myakka River and Myakka River State Park, one of Florida's largest state parks, noted for wetlands, alligators and birdlife.

History

Created in 1921 from Manatee County, Sarasota grew during the Florida land boom of the 1920s, when the Ringling brothers of circus fame invested heavily in the area; John Ringling's estate, Cà d'Zan, and the Ringling Museum of Art remain landmarks. Other towns include Venice, North Port and Englewood.

Economy

Tourism, retirement living, healthcare and real estate dominate the economy. The county is also a centre for arts institutions, including the Sarasota Orchestra and Sarasota Opera.
