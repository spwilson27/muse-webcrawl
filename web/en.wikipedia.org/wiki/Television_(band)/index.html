Television (band)

Television
Origin: New York City, US
Years active: 1973–1978, 1992, 2001–2023
Genres: Art punk, post-punk, art rock
Key members: Tom Verlaine, Richard Lloyd, Billy Ficca, Fred Smith

Television was an American rock band formed in New York City in 1973 by Tom Verlaine and Richard Hell, later joined by Richard Lloyd. A central act of the CBGB scene, they were pioneers of punk and post-punk despite limited commercial success.

History

Their debut album Marquee Moon (1977), with its interlocking guitars on the title track and "See No Evil", is widely ranked among the greatest albums ever made. Adventure (1978) followed before they split. They reunited for a self-titled album in 1992 and periodic tours until Verlaine's death in 2023.

Legacy

Television influenced The Strokes, R.E.M. and generations of alternative rock guitarists.
