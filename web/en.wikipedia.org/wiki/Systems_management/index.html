Systems management

Systems management refers to the enterprise-wide administration of distributed systems, including computer systems and networks. Systems management is strongly influenced by network management initiatives in telecommunications, and encompasses functions such as configuration management, performance monitoring, fault management, security administration, and software distribution.

Functions

Core systems management functions, often grouped under the FCAPS model (fault, configuration, accounting, performance, and security), include inventory and asset tracking, patch and update management, event and log monitoring, backup and recovery, and capacity planning. Modern practice increasingly relies on automation, infrastructure as code, and observability tooling.

Standards and frameworks

Relevant standards and frameworks include ITIL for IT service management, the Simple Network Management Protocol (SNMP) for device monitoring, and configuration management databases (CMDBs) for recording system components and their relationships.
