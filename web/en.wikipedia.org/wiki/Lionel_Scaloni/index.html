Lionel Sebastián Scaloni (born 16 May 1978) is an Argentine professional football manager and former player who is the manager of the Argentina national team. As manager, he led Argentina to victory at the 2021 Copa América, the 2022 Finalissima and the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar, as well as the 2024 Copa América.

Scaloni managing Argentina.

Playing career

Scaloni began his career with Newell's Old Boys and Estudiantes de La Plata in Argentina before moving to Europe, where he spent most of his playing days with Deportivo La Coruña in Spain, winning La Liga in 1999–2000 and the Copa del Rey in 2002. He later played for West Ham United (loan), Racing Santander, Lazio, Mallorca and Atalanta, retiring in 2015. He won seven caps for Argentina and was a member of the squad at the 2006 World Cup.

Managerial career

After coaching roles under Jorge Sampaoli with Sevilla and Argentina, Scaloni took charge of the national team in 2018, initially on an interim basis. Widely doubted at first, he rebuilt the side around captain Lionel Messi and a generation of younger players, ending Argentina's 28-year trophy drought at the 2021 Copa América in Brazil. At the 2022 World Cup in Qatar his team recovered from a shock opening defeat to Saudi Arabia to beat France on penalties in the final, regarded as one of the greatest ever. He was named The Best FIFA Men's Coach for 2022.
