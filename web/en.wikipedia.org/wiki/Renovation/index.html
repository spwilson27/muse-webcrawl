Renovation

Renovation (also called remodelling) is the process of improving a broken, damaged or outdated structure. Renovations are typically done on either a commercial or residential building. The work may range from cosmetic renewal — painting, flooring and fixtures — to structural repair, reconfiguration of internal space, and upgrades to plumbing, electrical and heating systems.

Types

Common residential renovations include kitchens and bathrooms, loft and basement conversions, and extensions. Commercial renovation covers office fit-outs and adaptive reuse of older buildings for new purposes. Renovation is distinguished from restoration, which aims to return a building to a documented earlier state, and from new construction; it often must comply with building codes and, for listed buildings, heritage protections.

Process and considerations

Projects typically proceed through assessment and design, obtaining permits, demolition, rough-in work, finishes and inspection. Energy-focused renovations add insulation, efficient glazing and heat pumps to cut running costs. Renovating an existing structure is often more sustainable than demolition and rebuilding, since it reuses the embodied carbon of the existing frame.
