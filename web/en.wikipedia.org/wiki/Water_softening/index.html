Water softening

Water softening is the removal of calcium, magnesium, and other metal cations from hard water, producing soft water that reduces scale and improves soap performance.

Methods

The most common method is ion exchange using resin beads regenerated with sodium chloride brine. Other methods include lime softening, reverse osmosis, and chelating agents.

Effects

Softening extends appliance life but adds sodium and removes beneficial minerals; environmental concerns include brine discharge.
