Anna Kay Faris (born 29 November 1976) is an American actress and podcaster. She rose to prominence as Cindy Campbell in the Scary Movie film series (2000–2006) and became known for her comic roles in films including The Hot Chick (2002), Just Friends (2005), My Super Ex-Girlfriend (2006), The House Bunny (2008) and What's Your Number? (2011). She also provided the voice of Sam Sparks in the Cloudy with a Chance of Meatballs films.

Early life

Faris was born in Baltimore, Maryland, and raised in Edmonds, Washington. She studied English literature at the University of Washington and acted in local theatre from a young age.

Career

From 2013 to 2020, Faris starred as Christy Plunkett in the CBS sitcom Mom, earning critical praise for her dramatic-comic performance. In 2015 she launched Anna Faris Is Unqualified, a comedy advice podcast, which became a bestseller in book form in 2017 with her memoir Unqualified. She has also starred in the Overboard remake (2018) and The Estate (2022).
