Timeline of Apple Inc. products

This timeline of Apple Inc. products lists the principal hardware products released by Apple Inc. (formerly Apple Computer, Inc.) since the company's founding in 1976, from the Apple I hobbyist computer through the Macintosh line, the iPod, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch, to the Apple silicon era.
