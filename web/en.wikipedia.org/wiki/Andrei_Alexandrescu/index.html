Andrei Alexandrescu (born 1969) is a Romanian-American computer scientist and author, recognised as an authority on the C++ and D programming languages. His 2001 book Modern C++ Design popularised policy-based design and template metaprogramming techniques that reshaped C++ library development.

Career

Alexandrescu studied electrical engineering at the Polytechnic University of Bucharest and later earned a PhD in computer science from the University of Washington (2009). He worked as a research scientist at Facebook (Meta), where he applied D to production systems, and has been a frequent speaker on programming language design.

Work on D

Together with Walter Bright, Alexandrescu is a principal designer of the D programming language. He designed D's ranges framework, which underlies its standard library Phobos, and described the language in The D Programming Language (2010). His talks on generic programming, allocators and error handling ("Expect the Expected") have been widely influential.
