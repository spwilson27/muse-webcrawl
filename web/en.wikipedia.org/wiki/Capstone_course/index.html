LevelFinal year of a degree programme
PurposeIntegration of programme learning outcomes
Typical outputMajor project, thesis or portfolio

A capstone course, also known as a capstone project or senior design project, is a culminating educational experience in the final year of a degree programme in which students integrate and apply the knowledge and skills acquired throughout their studies. The name derives from the capstone, the final stone placed at the top of a building or arch.

Characteristics

Capstone courses typically require students to work on a substantial, open-ended problem — often in teams and often for a real client, in engineering (senior design projects), business (consulting practica) and computing (software projects). Assessment centres on a major deliverable such as a designed artefact, research thesis, performance, exhibition or portfolio, frequently accompanied by a public presentation or defence.

Purpose

Educators use capstones to develop and assess programme-level learning outcomes: critical thinking, teamwork, communication, project management and professional judgement. Accrediting bodies in fields such as engineering (for example ABET) require a culminating major design experience. Capstones also serve as transition experiences, helping students move from study into employment or further research.
