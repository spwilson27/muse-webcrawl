October 18

October 18 is the 291st day of the year (292nd in leap years) in the Gregorian calendar; 74 days remain until the end of the year.

Events

1867 – The United States formally took possession of Alaska from Russia — Alaska Day. 1922 – The British Broadcasting Corporation (BBC) was founded. 1954 – Texas Instruments announced the first transistor radio, the Regency TR-1.

Births

1926 – Chuck Berry, American rock and roll musician, was born.
