Audi S4

Audi S4
Manufacturer: Audi AG
Production: 1991–1994; 1997–present
Class: Compact executive car
Layout: Front-engine, all-wheel drive
Related: Audi A4

The Audi S4 is the high-performance version of Audi's A4 compact executive car. The original S4, produced from 1991 to 1994, was based on the Audi 100; the nameplate was revived in 1997 on the A4 platform and has continued through successive A4 generations. All S4 models feature Audi's quattro permanent all-wheel drive.

History

The first S4 (C4) used a 2.2-litre turbocharged five-cylinder engine producing 230 PS. The B5 generation (1997–2001) introduced a 2.7-litre twin-turbo V6 with 265 PS in European specification, and became a cult favourite among enthusiasts. The B6 and B7 generations switched to a naturally aspirated 4.2-litre V8 of 344 PS. The B8 (2009–2016) returned to forced induction with a supercharged 3.0-litre V6 producing 333 PS, while the B9 (2016–present) uses a turbocharged 3.0-litre V6; in some markets late B9 cars were offered with a TDI diesel V6.
