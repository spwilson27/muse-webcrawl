Grêmio Recreativo Escola de Samba Estação Primeira de Mangueira, commonly known simply as Mangueira, is a Brazilian samba school based in the Mangueira neighbourhood of Rio de Janeiro. One of the oldest and most popular samba schools in the country, it takes its name from the Mangueira favela and its colours — green and pink — are among the most recognisable in Brazilian carnival.

History

Mangueira was founded on 28 April 1928 by musicians from the favela of the same name, including the legendary sambista Cartola (Angenor de Oliveira), Carlos Cachaça and Zé Espinguela. The name "Estação Primeira" ("First Station") refers to Mangueira's position as the first stop on the Central do Brasil railway line out of Rio's central station.

The school became famous for the quality of its composers and its bateria (percussion section). Cartola, together with Carlos Cachaça, produced many of the school's most celebrated sambas de enredo, and the composer's house in Mangueira became a gathering place for the leading figures of Brazilian popular music.

Carnival record

Mangueira is one of the most successful schools in the history of the Rio Carnival, having won the top-division championship twenty times, most recently in 2019 with a parade honouring Marielle Franco and Brazil's forgotten heroes. Its green-and-pink flag and its anthem-like songs are sung throughout the year, and the school's quadra (rehearsal hall), the Palácio do Samba, hosts weekly events attended by thousands.

Notable members

  • Cartola — founder and composer
  • Carlos Cachaça — composer
  • Jamelao — long-serving official interpreter (puxador)
  • Alcione — singer raised in the school's tradition
  • Beth Carvalho — singer closely associated with Mangueira
