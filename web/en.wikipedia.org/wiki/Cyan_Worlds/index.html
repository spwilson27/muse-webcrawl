Cyan Worlds, Inc., originally Cyan, Inc., is an American video game developer based in Mead, Washington, near Spokane. It was founded in 1987 by brothers Rand and Robyn Miller and is best known for creating the Myst series of adventure games.

History

Cyan first produced children's adventures such as The Manhole (1988). In 1993 it released Myst, a pre-rendered graphical adventure that became the best-selling PC game of the decade and a landmark of the CD-ROM era. Its sequel Riven followed in 1997. Later projects include the online experiment Uru: Ages Beyond Myst, the crowdfunded games Obduction (2016) and Firmament (2023), and remakes of Myst (2021) and Riven (2024) in real-time 3D with VR support.
