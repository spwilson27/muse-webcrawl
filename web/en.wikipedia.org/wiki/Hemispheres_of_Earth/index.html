Hemispheres of Earth

In geography and cartography, the hemispheres of Earth are any division of the globe into two equal halves (hemispheres). The most common divisions are by the Equator and by a meridian circle.

Equatorial division

The Equator divides the Earth into the Northern Hemisphere and Southern Hemisphere. The Northern Hemisphere contains about 68% of the planet's land and most of its population; seasons are reversed between the two halves, and the Coriolis effect deflects moving objects in opposite directions.

Meridional division

A great circle through the prime meridian and its antimeridian divides the Eastern Hemisphere from the Western Hemisphere. An alternative land-centred/water-centred division identifies the hemisphere containing the most land (centred near Nantes, France) and its opposite, the water hemisphere, centred in the South Pacific.
