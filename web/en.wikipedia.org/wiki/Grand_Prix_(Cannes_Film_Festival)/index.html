The Grand Prix is an award of the Cannes Film Festival bestowed by the jury of the festival on one of the competing feature films. It is the second-most prestigious prize of the festival after the Palme d'Or.

History

Prior to its creation in 1967, the second-most prestigious award at Cannes was the Jury Prize. The Grand Prix (officially "Grand Prix du Festival International du Film") was awarded from 1967, was renamed the "Grand Prix Spécial du Jury" in 1989, and took its current name, simply "Grand Prix", in 1995. Between 1967 and the present there have been years in which multiple films shared the award, and between 1979 and other years the hierarchy between the Grand Prix and the Jury Prize has occasionally shifted in form.

Selected winners

Notable Grand Prix winners include The Sweet Hereafter (1997), Life Is Beautiful (1998), Oldboy (2004), A Prophet (2009), Once Upon a Time in Anatolia (2011), Inside Llewyn Davis (2013), The Wonders (2014), Son of Saul (2015), Atlantics (2019), Compartment No. 6 and A Hero (2021, shared), Close and Stars at Noon (2022, shared), The Zone of Interest (2023), and All We Imagine as Light (2024).
