Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba (Japanese: 鬼滅の刃, "Blade of Demon Destruction") is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Koyoharu Gotouge. It was serialized in Shueisha's Weekly Shōnen Jump from February 2016 to May 2020, with its chapters collected in 23 tankōbon volumes. It follows teenage Tanjiro Kamado, who joins the Demon Slayer Corps after his family is slaughtered and his younger sister Nezuko is turned into a demon.

Plot

In Taishō-era Japan, Tanjiro Kamado earns a living selling charcoal. Returning home one day, he finds his family murdered by demons; his sister Nezuko has survived but been transformed into a demon who, remarkably, retains human emotions. Tanjiro trains under Sakonji Urokodaki, masters Water Breathing, and joins the Demon Slayer Corps to find a cure for Nezuko and to confront Muzan Kibutsuji, the progenitor of demons.

Media

An anime television series adaptation produced by Ufotable aired in 2019, followed by the film Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba – The Movie: Mugen Train (2020), which became the highest-grossing Japanese film of all time, and further seasons covering the Entertainment District, Swordsmith Village and Hashira Training arcs, with a film trilogy adapting the Infinity Castle arc announced.

Reception

By 2021 the manga had over 150 million copies in circulation, making it one of the best-selling manga series of all time; in 2020 it outsold all other series in Japan. The series has been praised for its emotional storytelling, fight choreography, and the anime's striking visuals.
