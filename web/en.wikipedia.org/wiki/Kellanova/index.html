Kellanova

Kellanova, formerly the Kellogg Company, is an American multinational food manufacturing company. Founded by Will Keith Kellogg in Battle Creek, Michigan, in 1906, it built its business on breakfast cereals — originating corn flakes with his brother, the physician John Harvey Kellogg — and grew into a global cereals and snacks group known for brands such as Corn Flakes, Frosted Flakes, Rice Krispies, Special K, Pringles (acquired from Procter & Gamble in 2012), Cheez-It, Pop-Tarts and Eggo.

2023 split and renaming

In October 2023 Kellogg Company split into two public companies: WK Kellogg Co, which took over the North American cereal business, and the remaining global snacking and international cereals business, which adopted the new name Kellanova. The name combines "Kellogg" with "nova" (Latin for new). Kellanova is headquartered in Chicago and retained the NYSE ticker "K".

Acquisition by Mars

In August 2024 Mars, Incorporated announced an agreement to acquire Kellanova for approximately $35.9 billion, one of the largest food-industry deals in years, combining Kellanova's snack brands with Mars's confectionery and snacking portfolio; the transaction ultimately closed in 2025 after regulatory review.
