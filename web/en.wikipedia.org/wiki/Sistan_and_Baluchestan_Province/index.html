Sistan and Baluchestan Province is one of the 31 provinces of Iran, located in the southeast of the country, bordering Pakistan and Afghanistan and the Gulf of Oman. It is Iran's largest province by area and one of its poorest, with a population composed mainly of Baloch people in the south (Baluchestan) and Sistani Persians in the north (Sistan). Its capital is Zahedan.

Geography

The province is arid and mountainous, with the Hamun wetlands in the north fed by the Helmand River from Afghanistan, subject to severe drought and dust storms driven by the '120-day wind'. The port of Chabahar on the Gulf of Oman is Iran's only oceanic port and a strategic trade hub.

History

Sistan corresponds to ancient Drangiana and later Sakastan, 'land of the Sakas'. The region features in Ferdowsi's Shahnameh as the homeland of the hero Rostam, said to come from Zabol.

Demographics

Most Baloch inhabitants are Sunni Muslims, unlike Iran's majority Shia population, and speak Balochi; the north around Zabol is predominantly Persian-speaking and Shia. The province has centres including Zahedan, Zabol, Iranshahr and Chabahar.
