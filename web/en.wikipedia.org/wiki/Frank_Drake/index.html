Frank Drake

Frank Donald Drake (1930–2022) was an American astrophysicist and astrobiologist. He conducted the first modern SETI search, Project Ozma (1960), formulated the Drake equation estimating the number of communicative extraterrestrial civilisations, and helped design the Pioneer plaque and the Arecibo message.

Frank Drake
Born: 28 May 1930, Chicago, Illinois
Died: 2 September 2022, Aptos, California
Known for: Drake equation, Project Ozma, SETI

Career

Educated at Cornell and Harvard, Drake worked at the National Radio Astronomy Observatory, where Project Ozma listened to stars Tau Ceti and Epsilon Eridani. He convened the 1961 Green Bank meeting where the Drake equation was presented. He later directed Arecibo Observatory, taught at Cornell and UC Santa Cruz, and co-founded the SETI Institute, serving as president of its board.

Legacy

Drake’s equation still frames debate about life in the universe. In 1974 he transmitted the Arecibo message toward globular cluster M13. He died in 2022 aged 92, widely honoured as the father of SETI as a scientific discipline.
