Spoiler (character)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Spoiler is a fictional superheroine appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, primarily in stories featuring Batman. The character was created by writer Chuck Dixon and artist Tom Lyle and first appeared in Detective Comics #647 (August 1992). Her real name is Stephanie Brown, the daughter of the minor supervillain Cluemaster (Arthur Brown). She adopted the Spoiler identity to "spoil" her father's criminal schemes by leaving clues for Batman and Robin.

Stephanie Brown later became the fourth Robin — the first female Robin in the mainstream DC Universe continuity — and subsequently the third Batgirl, before returning to the Spoiler identity during The New 52 relaunch. She is notable for her close relationship with Tim Drake, the third Robin, and for her persistence in seeking a place within the Batman family despite repeated rejection.

Publication history

Stephanie Brown debuted in the three-part storyline "Cluemaster" in Detective Comics #647–649 (1992). Initially intended as a minor supporting character, she proved popular and became a recurring ally of Tim Drake in the Robin ongoing series. In 2004, during the "War Games" crossover, Stephanie briefly served as Robin after Tim Drake stepped down; she was dismissed by Batman and apparently killed by the gangster Black Mask. Her death proved controversial, and in 2008 writer Grant Morrison revealed she had survived, having faked her death with the help of Dr. Leslie Thompkins. From 2009 to 2011 she starred as Batgirl in her own series written by Bryan Q. Miller. The character was absent from The New 52 relaunch until 2014, when she was reintroduced in Batman Eternal once again as Spoiler.

Fictional character biography

Stephanie Brown is the daughter of Arthur Brown, a failed game-show host turned criminal known as the Cluemaster, and Crystal Brown. When her father returned to crime after a prison term, Stephanie sewed herself a costume — an eggplant-purple hooded cape and mask — and set out to ruin his plans by planting clues that would lead Batman to him. She crossed paths with Robin (Tim Drake), and the two eventually began a long, complicated romantic relationship.

After a teenage pregnancy (the child was given up for adoption), Stephanie continued operating as Spoiler and trained intermittently with the Birds of Prey. When Tim Drake temporarily retired as Robin, Stephanie took up the mantle herself, but Batman fired her for disobeying orders. Seeking to prove herself, she stole one of Batman's contingency plans and inadvertently ignited a massive gang war in Gotham City. Captured and tortured by Black Mask, she was believed to have died of her injuries. In fact, Leslie Thompkins had switched her medication to fake her death and nursed her back to health abroad.

Returning to Gotham, Stephanie assumed the Batgirl identity after Cassandra Cain passed it to her, mentored by Barbara Gordon. Following the events of Flashpoint and later continuity reboots, she was reintroduced as a teenager whose father, again the Cluemaster, was involved in a conspiracy against Gotham's vigilantes; she became Spoiler and a member of a young team of heroes alongside Tim Drake.

Powers and abilities

Spoiler has no superhuman powers. She is a trained gymnast and acrobat and a capable hand-to-hand combatant, having received instruction from Robin, Batgirl, and the Birds of Prey. She employs a utility belt, grappling equipment, and various non-lethal gadgets standard to the Batman family.

In other media

Stephanie Brown appears as Spoiler in the animated series Young Justice, voiced by Vivica A. Fox's daughter in later seasons, and in the television series Batwoman and Gotham Knights, where she is a series regular portrayed by Anna Lore.
