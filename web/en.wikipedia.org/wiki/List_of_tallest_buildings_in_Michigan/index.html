TallestDetroit Marriott at the Renaissance Center (221.5 m)
Primary cityDetroit

This list of tallest buildings in Michigan ranks the state's skyscrapers by height. Nearly all of Michigan's tallest buildings are in downtown Detroit, whose skyline developed during the 1920s building boom and again in the 1970s with the Renaissance Center. The tallest building in the state since 1977 has been the central tower of the Renaissance Center, now the Detroit Marriott at the Renaissance Center.
