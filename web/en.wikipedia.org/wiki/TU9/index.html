TU9 German Universities of Technology e. V. is an association of the nine oldest and largest technical universities in Germany. Founded in 2003, it represents the interests of its members in higher education and research policy, promotes engineering education, and positions German technical universities internationally.

Members

The member institutions are:

  • RWTH Aachen University
  • Technische Universität Berlin
  • Technische Universität Braunschweig
  • Technische Universität Darmstadt
  • Technische Universität Dresden
  • Leibniz University Hannover
  • Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT)
  • Technical University of Munich (TUM)
  • University of Stuttgart

Activities

Together, the TU9 universities enrol roughly a quarter of a million students, employ a large share of Germany's engineering professors, and produce a majority of the country's engineering graduates and doctoral degrees in technical fields. The alliance coordinates joint marketing to international students, publishes position papers on research funding and the Bologna Process, and has taken common stances on issues such as tuition fees, excellence funding, and the recognition of engineering qualifications in Europe. Through its member universities' offices abroad, TU9 also promotes Germany as a destination for study and research.
