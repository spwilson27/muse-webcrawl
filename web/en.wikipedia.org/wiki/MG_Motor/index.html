MG Motor is an automotive brand owned by the Chinese state-owned carmaker SAIC Motor, which markets cars under the historic British MG marque. The original MG (Morris Garages) company, founded in Oxford in 1924, became famous for its two-seater sports cars.

History

After the collapse of MG Rover in 2005, the MG brand and the Longbridge factory were acquired by Nanjing Automobile, which was itself absorbed into SAIC in 2007. Production of MG cars resumed in China and, for a time, at Longbridge in Birmingham.

From the 2010s, MG Motor expanded rapidly in the United Kingdom, Australia, Europe, and other markets with affordable petrol and electric cars such as the MG ZS SUV, the MG3 hatchback, and the MG4 EV. The brand became one of the fastest-growing car brands in Britain, with electric models accounting for a large share of sales. In India, the marque is sold through JSW MG Motor India.

Products

Current models include the MG3, MG4 EV, MG ZS, MG HS, MG5 EV, and the Cyberster electric roadster launched to mark the marque's centenary in 2024.
