PDF/A

PDF/A is an ISO-standardised version of the Portable Document Format (PDF) designed specifically for the long-term archiving and preservation of electronic documents. It is defined by the ISO 19005 family of standards.

Overview

PDF/A differs from standard PDF by prohibiting features that are unsuitable for long-term preservation, such as encryption, audio and video content, JavaScript, and references to external content. Instead, everything needed to render the document identically in the future — fonts, colour information and metadata — must be embedded in the file itself.

Parts and conformance levels

PDF/A-1 (2005) is based on PDF 1.4; PDF/A-2 (2011) is based on PDF 1.7 and permits features such as JPEG 2000 compression and transparent elements; PDF/A-3 (2012) additionally allows arbitrary file formats to be embedded; and PDF/A-4 (2020) is based on PDF 2.0. Conformance levels distinguish fully conforming files (level A, with accessible structure), basic visual conformance (level B) and an intermediate level (U) requiring Unicode mapping for text.

Use

PDF/A is widely required or recommended by archives, libraries, courts and government agencies for records that must remain readable and authentic for decades.
