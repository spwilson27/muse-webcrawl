Sandra Bullock

Sandra Annette Bullock
Born: July 26, 1964, Arlington, Virginia, U.S.
Occupation: Actress, producer
Years active: 1987–present

Sandra Annette Bullock (born July 26, 1964) is an American actress and producer. The highest-paid actress in the world in 2010 and 2014, Bullock rose to fame through a series of starring roles in the 1990s, including the action thriller Speed (1994), the romantic comedy While You Were Sleeping (1995), and the drama A Time to Kill (1996).

Early life

Bullock was born in Arlington, Virginia, to Helga Meyer, a German opera singer, and John W. Bullock, an American voice coach and military contractor from Alabama. She spent much of her childhood in Nuremberg, Germany, where her mother performed, and became fluent in German. She studied ballet as a child and later attended East Carolina University, leaving before graduation to pursue acting in New York City.

Career

After early roles in television films, Bullock gained wide attention as the female lead in Demolition Man (1993) and achieved stardom with Speed (1994) opposite Keanu Reeves. She consolidated her success with While You Were Sleeping, Hope Floats, Practical Magic, and Miss Congeniality (2000). Her portrayal of Leigh Anne Tuohy in The Blind Side (2009) earned her the Academy Award for Best Actress. In 2013 she starred opposite George Clooney in Alfonso Cuarón's Gravity, one of the highest-grossing films of her career, and later appeared in Ocean's 8 (2018), Bird Box (2018), and The Lost City (2022).

Production work

Bullock founded the production company Fortis Films, which has produced several of her films, including Miss Congeniality and its sequel, as well as the sitcom George Lopez. She has been recognised with a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame and numerous honours for both her acting and philanthropy, notably her donations to the American Red Cross following Hurricane Katrina and the 2010 Haiti earthquake.
