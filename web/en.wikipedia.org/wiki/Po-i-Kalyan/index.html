Po-i-Kalyan

Po-i-Kalyan or Poi Kalan is an Islamic religious complex in Bukhara, Uzbekistan. Located beneath the Kalyan minaret, it consists of the Kalyan Mosque and Mir-i Arab Madrasah facing each other, around a plaza.

History

The Kalyan minaret, 45.6 m high, was built in 1127 by Qarakhanid ruler Arslan Khan. Genghis Khan spared it while destroying surroundings. The mosque (1514) and Mir-i Arab Madrasah (1535–36) were built under the Shaybanids.

Architecture

The complex is a landmark of Central Asian Islamic architecture, with turquoise domes, pishtaq portals and intricate tilework. It is part of the UNESCO World Heritage historic centre of Bukhara.
