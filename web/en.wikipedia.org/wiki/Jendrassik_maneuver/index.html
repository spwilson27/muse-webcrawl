Jendrassik maneuver

The Jendrassik maneuver is a medical manoeuvre used during neurological examination to enhance tendon reflexes, particularly when they are otherwise difficult to elicit.

Technique

The patient hooks the fingers of both hands together and pulls them apart as hard as possible while the examiner taps a tendon, such as the patellar tendon. The effort increases the excitability of spinal motor neurons and can make a weak reflex more apparent.

History

The manoeuvre is named after the Hungarian neurologist Ernő Jendrassik (1858–1921), who described it in the late nineteenth century. See also deep tendon reflex.
