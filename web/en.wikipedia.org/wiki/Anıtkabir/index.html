Anıtkabir

Anıtkabir
Location: Ankara, Turkey
Architects: Emin Onat, Ahmet Orhan Arda
Construction: 1944–1953
Dedicated to: Mustafa Kemal Atatürk

Anıtkabir (literally, memorial tomb) is the mausoleum of Mustafa Kemal Atatürk, founder and first President of the Republic of Turkey, located in Ankara. It also contains the grave of İsmet İnönü.

History

A design competition in 1941 selected Emin Onat and Ahmet Orhan Arda. Construction 1944-1953; Atatürk's remains were transferred on 10 November 1953, 15 years after his death.

Architecture

The complex blends ancient Anatolian and classical influences, in Second National Architectural Movement style. Features include the Road of Lions, Ceremonial Plaza, Hall of Honour with a 7-ton sarcophagus, Peace Park, and a museum of Atatürk's belongings and the War of Independence.
