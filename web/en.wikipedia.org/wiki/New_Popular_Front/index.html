The New Popular Front (French: Nouveau Front populaire, NFP) is a left-wing electoral alliance in France, founded on 10 June 2024 to contest the snap legislative elections called by President Emmanuel Macron after the far-right National Rally's victory in the European Parliament election. Its name consciously echoes the Popular Front that won the 1936 election under Léon Blum.

Formation

Within hours of Macron's dissolution of the National Assembly on 9 June 2024, France's main left-wing parties — La France Insoumise, the Socialist Party, The Ecologists (EELV) and the French Communist Party, together with smaller parties and unions — began negotiations. A common programme and single candidates in each constituency were agreed within four days, succeeding the earlier NUPES alliance of 2022.

Programme

The alliance's programme included repealing Macron's pension reform that raised the retirement age to 64, raising the minimum wage to €1,600 net per month, freezing prices of essential goods and energy, restoring a wealth tax, introducing a 90% marginal tax rate on the highest incomes, increasing public spending on services, and recognising a Palestinian state. The programme's cost and tax proposals were contested by political opponents and some economists.

2024 legislative election

In the first round on 30 June 2024 the NFP came second in the popular vote behind the National Rally. Through widespread tactical withdrawals — third-placed candidates standing down to block the far right in the second round on 7 July — the alliance emerged with the largest number of seats, about 182, ahead of Macron's Ensemble coalition and the National Rally, but far short of an absolute majority. The result produced a hung parliament; Macron eventually appointed the conservative Michel Barnier as prime minister in September 2024, over the NFP's protests that its candidate, Lucie Castets, should have been chosen. Barnier's government was brought down by a no-confidence vote in December 2024, in which NFP deputies voted alongside the National Rally.
