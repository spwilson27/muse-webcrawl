The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes is a 2023 American dystopian action film directed by Francis Lawrence. A prequel to the original Hunger Games series, it adapts Suzanne Collins' 2020 novel of the same name and is set 64 years before the first film.

Plot

Eighteen-year-old Coriolanus Snow, scion of a fallen Capitol family, is assigned to mentor Lucy Gray Baird, the defiant singing tribute from District 12, in the 10th Hunger Games. Under the eye of Head Gamemaker Dr. Volumnia Gaul and Dean Casca Highbottom, Snow cheats to help Lucy Gray survive, falls for her, and is exiled as a Peacekeeper to District 12, where their affair collapses into betrayal and murder — setting him on the path to becoming Panem's tyrannical president.

Reception

The film grossed about $349 million worldwide and received mixed-to-positive reviews, with praise for its cast, especially Rachel Zegler and Viola Davis.
