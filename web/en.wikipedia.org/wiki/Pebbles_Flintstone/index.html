Pebbles Flintstone is a fictional character in the animated television series The Flintstones by Hanna-Barbera. She is the red-haired infant daughter of Fred and Wilma Flintstone, recognisable by the bone she wears in her ponytail. Pebbles was born near the end of the show's third season, in the 1963 episode "The Surprise", after considerable publicity: the sex of Wilma's baby was the subject of a promotional campaign, and Pebbles's arrival was a television event of its era.

Appearances

As an infant, Pebbles's best friend is Bamm-Bamm Rubble, the super-strong adopted son of the Flintstones' neighbours Barney and Betty Rubble. She was voiced in most appearances by Jean Vander Pyl, who also voiced Wilma. A talking doll of Pebbles became a best-selling toy.

Later incarnations

In the 1971 series The Pebbles and Bamm-Bamm Show, Pebbles appears as a teenager attending Bedrock High School with Bamm-Bamm. Later television films, including I Yabba-Dabba Do! (1993), depict the two as adults who marry, and in Hollyrock-a-Bye Baby they become parents of twins, Chip and Roxy. Pebbles has appeared in most Flintstones spin-offs, films and commercials, including Fruity Pebbles and Cocoa Pebbles breakfast cereals, whose mascots feature her alongside Fred and Barney.
