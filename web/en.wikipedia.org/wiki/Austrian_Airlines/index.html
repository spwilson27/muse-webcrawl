Austrian Airlines AG, often shortened to Austrian, is the flag carrier of Austria and a subsidiary of the Lufthansa Group. The airline is headquartered at Vienna International Airport in Schwechat, where it maintains its hub. It is a member of the Star Alliance.

History

The airline was formed on 30 September 1957 by the merger of Air Austria and Austrian Airways, and commenced services on 31 March 1958 with a flight from Vienna to London using a leased Vickers Viscount. Transatlantic services to New York began in 1969 with a Boeing 707 operated in cooperation with Sabena.

Austrian joined the Star Alliance in 2000. After periods of financial difficulty, the Austrian government sold its stake to Lufthansa, and the takeover — approved by the European Commission subject to conditions — was completed in September 2009. The regional operations formerly flown under the Austrian arrows brand (originally Tyrolean Airways) were integrated into the mainline operation in 2015.

Destinations and fleet

Austrian serves a network concentrated on Central and Eastern Europe together with long-haul routes to North America and Asia. Its fleet has included Airbus A320 family aircraft and Embraer 195s on short-haul services, and Boeing 767-300ER and 777-200ER aircraft on long-haul routes, with Airbus A320neo aircraft joining the fleet from 2022.
