Miami Gardens, Florida

Miami Gardens
County: Miami-Dade, Florida
Incorporated: 13 May 2003
Known for: Hard Rock Stadium

Miami Gardens is a city in north-central Miami-Dade County, Florida, United States. It was incorporated on 13 May 2003 and is the third-largest city in the county by population, with roughly 110,000 residents. The city is predominantly African-American and is home to many former Miami Dolphins players.

Sport

Miami Gardens hosts Hard Rock Stadium, home of the NFL's Miami Dolphins, the University of Miami Hurricanes football team, and the Orange Bowl; the stadium has hosted multiple Super Bowls and is a venue of the 2026 FIFA World Cup. The Miami Open tennis tournament is also held at the stadium complex. Calder Casino lies within the city as well.

History

The area developed after the construction of the Palmetto Expressway and Interstate 95 spurred suburban growth in northern Miami-Dade in the 1960s and 1970s. Residents of unincorporated neighbourhoods such as Carol City, Scott Lake and Norland voted to incorporate in 2003, naming the new city after Miami Gardens Drive. The neighbourhood of Opa-locka Boulevard and the historic community of Bunche Park are among its constituent areas.
