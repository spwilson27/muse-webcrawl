Christen C. Raunkiær

Born: March 29, 1860, Lyngby, Denmark
Died: March 11, 1938, Copenhagen, Denmark
Fields: Botany, plant ecology

Christen Christensen Raunkiær (29 March 1860 – 11 March 1938) was a Danish botanist, who was a pioneer of plant ecology. He is mainly remembered for his plant life-form system.

Life-form system

Raunkiær classified plants according to the position of their perennating (survival) buds during unfavourable seasons. His main categories — phanerophytes, chamaephytes, hemicryptophytes, cryptophytes (including geophytes, helophytes and hydrophytes), and therophytes — remain standard in ecology. By computing the proportions of life forms in a flora (a "biological spectrum"), botanists can characterize the climate of a region.

Career

Raunkiær studied at the University of Copenhagen and became a professor of botany there and director of the Botanical Garden. He conducted fieldwork in the Danish West Indies, North Africa, and elsewhere, and published extensively on plant geography and the structure of plant communities.
