Nuclear magneton

The nuclear magneton (symbol μN) is a physical constant of magnetic moment, defined as μN = eħ / (2mp), where e is the elementary charge, ħ is the reduced Planck constant and mp is the proton mass. Its value in SI units is approximately 5.050 783 739 × 10−27 joules per tesla.

Use

The nuclear magneton is the natural unit for expressing the magnetic dipole moments of atomic nuclei, nucleons and other heavy particles, just as the Bohr magneton is used for electrons. Because the proton is about 1,836 times more massive than the electron, the nuclear magneton is smaller than the Bohr magneton by the same factor, reflecting the correspondingly smaller magnetic moments of nuclei. The magnetic moment of the proton is about 2.793 nuclear magnetons, and that of the neutron is about −1.913 nuclear magnetons; these values deviate from the simple Dirac predictions (1 and 0 respectively) because of the internal structure of nucleons, an effect explained by the quark model.
