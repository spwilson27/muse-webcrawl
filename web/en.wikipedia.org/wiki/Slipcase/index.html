Slipcase

A slipcase is a five-sided box, usually made of stiff cardboard or board covered with paper, cloth or leather, open on one side or at one end, into which a book or set of books slides for protection. The spine of the book remains visible when shelved.

Use

Slipcases protect fine bindings, limited editions and boxed sets from dust, light and shelf wear. Publishers often issue special editions in slipcases, and collectors commission custom cases for rare books. A more elaborate variant, closed on all sides with a hinged door, is called a clamshell box or Solander box.

Other media

The term is also used for protective sleeves for records, DVDs and video game boxes.
