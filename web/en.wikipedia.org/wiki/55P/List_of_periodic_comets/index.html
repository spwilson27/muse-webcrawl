List of periodic comets

Orbits of notable periodic comets.

Periodic comets are comets with orbital periods of less than 200 years (short-period comets) or that have been observed at more than one perihelion passage. Once a periodic comet's orbit is confirmed by observation at a second return, the International Astronomical Union assigns it a permanent number, prefixed to the name of its discoverer or discoverers — for example, 1P/Halley, the first comet whose return was predicted, by Edmond Halley in 1705.

Unnumbered periodic comets

Hundreds of additional periodic comets are known only from a single observed apparition and carry provisional designations beginning with P/ followed by the year of discovery. Many are faint Jupiter-family comets found by surveys such as LINEAR, Spacewatch, Pan-STARRS, and Catalina. Some periodic comets have been lost or have disintegrated, including 3D/Biela and 73P/Schwassmann–Wachmann, which fragmented into many pieces.
