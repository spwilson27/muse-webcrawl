Arsène Lupin is a fictional gentleman thief and master of disguise created in 1905 by French writer Maurice Leblanc. The character was first introduced in a series of short stories serialised in the magazine Je sais tout. Lupin appears in 17 novels and 39 novellas by Leblanc, as well as numerous adaptations for film, television, theatre and comics.

Character

Lupin is a master thief who steals only from the rich and the corrupt, frequently righting injustices in the process. He is a master of disguise, a brilliant escape artist, and a connoisseur of art and jewels. His adventures often pit him against the detective Herlock Sholmès — a thinly disguised version of Sherlock Holmes created after Leblanc's early, unauthorised use of Conan Doyle's detective drew legal objection.

Notable works

Among the best-known books are Arsène Lupin, Gentleman Burglar (1907), Arsène Lupin versus Herlock Sholmès (1908), The Hollow Needle (1909), in which Lupin hunts a treasure of the kings of France, and 813 (1910).

Legacy

Lupin is a central figure of French popular culture, comparable to Sherlock Holmes in the English-speaking world. The Netflix series Lupin (2021), starring Omar Sy as a thief inspired by the character, introduced him to a new global audience.
