Gnégnéri Yaya Touré (born 13 May 1983) is an Ivorian former professional footballer who played as a midfielder. Renowned for his combination of power, technique, and passing range, he is considered one of the greatest African footballers of all time.

Club career

Touré came through the ASEC Mimosas academy and moved to Europe with Beveren in Belgium, then Metalurh Donetsk, Olympiacos, and Monaco. At Barcelona (2007–2010) he won two La Liga titles and the 2009 UEFA Champions League as part of a treble-winning side. Joining Manchester City in 2010, he became central to the club's rise, scoring the winning goals in the 2011 FA Cup semi-final and final and scoring 20 league goals in the 2013–14 Premier League title season. He won three Premier League titles with City before leaving in 2018, later playing briefly for Olympiacos and Qingdao Huanghai.

International career and honours

Touré earned 101 caps for Ivory Coast and captained the team to victory at the 2015 Africa Cup of Nations. He was named African Footballer of the Year four times consecutively (2011–2014). His elder brother Kolo Touré was also a professional footballer.
