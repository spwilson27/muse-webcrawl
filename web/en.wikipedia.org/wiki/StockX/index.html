StockX is an online marketplace and clothing reseller, primarily of sneakers. The Detroit-based company was founded by Dan Gilbert, Josh Luber, and Greg Schwartz in 2015 and launched in February 2016. StockX operates a "stock market of things": buyers place bids, sellers place asks, and a transaction occurs when a bid matches an ask. The company verifies the authenticity of products before shipping them to buyers.

History

The startup was founded by Gilbert, Luber — who ran the sneaker price guide Campless — and Schwartz. StockX initially focused on the resale sneaker market, publishing live market prices based on real transactions. It later expanded into streetwear, handbags, watches, trading cards, electronics, and other collectibles. In 2019 the company reached a valuation of more than $1 billion, becoming Michigan's first tech "unicorn". In November 2019, Scott Cutler, formerly of eBay and the New York Stock Exchange, replaced Luber as CEO.

Business model

Sellers list items with an asking price or sell instantly to the highest bid; buyers similarly bid or buy at the lowest ask. Sold items are shipped to a StockX authentication center, where they are inspected for authenticity and condition before being forwarded to the buyer. StockX charges transaction and processing fees, and publishes historical price data for each product, functioning as a price index for the resale market.
