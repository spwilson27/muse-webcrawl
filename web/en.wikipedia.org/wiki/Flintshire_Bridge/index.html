The Flintshire Bridge is a cable-stayed bridge spanning the River Dee in Flintshire, north-east Wales. It carries the A548 across the Dee estuary between Connah's Quay and Flint, and forms part of the Deeside corridor linking the A55 expressway with the Wirral and the M56 motorway corridor in England.

Description

The bridge is an asymmetric cable-stayed structure with a single main tower. Its total length is about 954 metres, with a main span of approximately 194 metres over the navigable channel of the Dee. The deck carries a dual carriageway road with hard shoulders. The bridge was designed to relieve congestion through the towns of Connah's Quay and Shotton and to improve access to the Deeside Industrial Park, one of the largest industrial estates in Wales.

History

Construction took place in the mid-1990s as part of the A548 Deeside link improvements. The bridge was opened on 6 March 1998 by the Secretary of State for Wales. At the time of its opening it was one of the largest cable-stayed bridges in the United Kingdom. It is sometimes informally known locally as the "bridge to nowhere", a nickname dating from its early years when the connecting road improvements on the English side of the border were still incomplete.
