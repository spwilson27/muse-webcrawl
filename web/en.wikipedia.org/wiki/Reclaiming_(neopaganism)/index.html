Reclaiming (neopaganism)

Reclaiming is a tradition in modern Paganism and feminist Witchcraft, founded in the San Francisco Bay Area in the late 1970s, blending Goddess spirituality, environmentalism, and political activism. Its best-known co-founder is Starhawk, author of The Spiral Dance (1979).

Type: Feminist neopagan witchcraft

Origins

Emerging from the Reclaiming Collective (classes in magic and ritual in Berkeley/San Francisco in 1978–80), the tradition drew on Dianic Wicca, Anderson Feri tradition, feminism and anarchist politics. The Spiral Dance ritual, held annually at Samhain in San Francisco, became its signature public rite.

Beliefs and practice

Reclaiming is ecstatic, egalitarian and non-hierarchical: authority rotates, decisions use consensus, rituals are co-created, and deity is seen as immanent in nature and community. Activism — anti-nuclear, environmental, anti-globalisation — is treated as sacred work. Communities and "Witchcamps" exist across North America, Europe and Australia.
