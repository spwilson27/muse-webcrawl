Aromanticism

Aromanticism is a romantic orientation characterised by experiencing little or no romantic attraction to others. People who identify as aromantic may still experience sexual attraction, form deep platonic bonds, or seek non-romantic partnerships; others are also asexual (often shortened to "aroace").

Definition and spectrum

Aromanticism is commonly described as a spectrum. Terms used within the community include grey-romantic (rarely or weakly experiencing romantic attraction) and demiromantic (experiencing romantic attraction only after a strong emotional bond has formed). Romantic attraction is distinguished from aesthetic, sensual and platonic attraction.

Community and symbols

The aromantic pride flag consists of five horizontal stripes: two shades of green, white, grey and black, with green representing the aromantic spectrum. Aromantic Spectrum Awareness Week is observed in February, following Valentine's Day. Online communities such as AVEN and dedicated aromantic forums have played a central role in developing terminology since the early 2000s.
