Stanford Racing Team

The Stanford Racing Team is a team from Stanford University that competed in the DARPA Grand Challenge for autonomous vehicles. The team is best known for building Stanley, a modified Volkswagen Touareg that won the 2005 DARPA Grand Challenge.

DARPA Grand Challenge 2005

On 8 October 2005, Stanley completed the 131.6-mile (211.8 km) desert course in the Mojave Desert in 6 hours, 53 minutes and 58 seconds, an average speed of about 19.1 mph (30.7 km/h), winning the US$2 million prize. Stanley was equipped with LIDAR sensors, cameras, GPS, and inertial sensors, and its software used machine learning techniques, including a self-supervised system that learned to classify drivable terrain from camera images calibrated against laser data.

Later work

The team, led by Sebastian Thrun, went on to enter Junior, a modified Volkswagen Passat, in the 2007 DARPA Urban Challenge, finishing in second place. Members of the team subsequently played leading roles in the development of Google's self-driving car project, which later became Waymo.
