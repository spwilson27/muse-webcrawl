Pig butchering scam

A pig butchering scam (Chinese: 杀猪盘, shā zhū pán) is a type of long-term online fraud, typically combining romance and investment deception, in which scammers build a relationship with a victim over weeks or months — "fattening the pig" — before persuading them to invest escalating sums in fraudulent schemes, often fake cryptocurrency platforms, and then disappearing with the money.

Method

Contact is commonly made through dating apps, social media, or "wrong number" text messages. The scammer grooms the victim, demonstrates apparent investment profits on a convincing fake trading app, allows small withdrawals to build trust, then pressures the victim to invest more. When the victim tries to withdraw large sums, fees are demanded or the account is frozen, and the scammer vanishes.

Scale and trafficking

The schemes are frequently run by organised crime syndicates in Southeast Asia, including from scam compounds in Myanmar, Cambodia, and Laos, where investigators and the UN report many workers are themselves victims of human trafficking forced to carry out the fraud. Global losses have been estimated in the tens of billions of dollars; the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center regularly lists pig butchering among the most costly cybercrimes.
