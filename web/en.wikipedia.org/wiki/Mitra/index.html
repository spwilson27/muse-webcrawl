Mitra

Mitra (Sanskrit: Mitra; Avestan: Mithra) is an Indo-Iranian deity of covenant, oath, contract, friendship, and light. In the Rigveda, Mitra is closely paired with Varuna as a guardian of ṛta (cosmic order and truth). In Zoroastrianism, Mithra is a major yazata, judge of souls and protector of cattle, whose festival is Mihragan.

Indo-Iranian background

A treaty from the Mitanni kingdom (c. 14th century BC) invokes Mitra and Varuna alongside Indra and the Nasatyas, among the earliest attestations. The Roman god Mithras borrows the name, though the degree of continuity between the cults is disputed.

Hindu tradition

In later Hinduism Mitra is one of the Adityas, sons of Aditi, associated with the sun and friendship.
