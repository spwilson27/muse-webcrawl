Depression (mood)

Depression is a mood disorder causing a persistent feeling of sadness and loss of interest (anhedonia), accompanied by changes in sleep, appetite, energy, concentration and self-worth. Major depressive disorder affects hundreds of millions worldwide and is a leading cause of disability.

Understanding and care

Causes mix genetics, brain chemistry, life events and illness; the once-simple 'chemical imbalance' story is now considered oversimplified. Treatments — psychotherapy (especially CBT), antidepressants, exercise, and neuromodulation for resistant cases — help most people, though access lags need. Crisis lines and destigmatisation campaigns are major public-health efforts.
