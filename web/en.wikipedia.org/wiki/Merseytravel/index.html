Merseytravel is the passenger transport executive responsible for coordinating public transport in the Liverpool City Region in North West England. It is the executive body of the Liverpool City Region Combined Authority and oversees the Merseyrail rail network, the Mersey Ferries, the Mersey Tunnels, and the region's bus network and smart ticketing.

Transport services coordinated by Merseytravel

History

The organisation was established on 1 December 1969 as the Merseyside Passenger Transport Executive under the Transport Act 1968, taking over the municipal bus fleets of Liverpool, Birkenhead, and Wallasey. Guided by the Merseyside Area Land Use and Transportation Study (MALTS), the PTE developed the Merseyrail network, including the Liverpool underground Loop and Link lines that opened in 1977–78.

Following bus deregulation in 1986, the PTE's bus operations were separated into an arm's-length company, Merseybus (later MTL), which was eventually sold. The executive adopted the trading name Merseytravel in the early 1990s. In 2014 its functions passed to the Liverpool City Region Combined Authority, for which Merseytravel acts as the transport delivery body.

Responsibilities

  • Rail: Merseytravel lets and manages the Merseyrail concession, one of the most punctual rail networks in Britain, operated under contract (by Serco-Abellio Merseyrail from 2003 to 2028). It owns the Class 777 trains introduced from 2023.
  • Ferries: It owns and operates the Mersey Ferries, famous for the cross-river service between Liverpool's Pier Head and Wirral terminals at Seacombe and Woodside.
  • Tunnels: It manages the Queensway (Birkenhead) and Kingsway (Wallasey) road tunnels under the Mersey.
  • Buses: It coordinates bus services, infrastructure, and information across the region, and under the Bus Services Act 2017 the city region moved toward bus franchising, with franchised services planned to begin in 2026.
  • Ticketing: It administers concessionary travel and the MetroCard smartcard, successor to the Walrus card.
