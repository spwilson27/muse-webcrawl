Hațeg Island

Hațeg Island was a large offshore island in the Tethys Sea during the Late Cretaceous period, about 70 million years ago, corresponding to the Hațeg Basin in present-day Romania. It is famous for its endemic dinosaur fauna, many of which were unusually small — an example of insular dwarfism.

Fauna

Fossils described by Franz Nopcsa and later researchers include the dwarf sauropod Magyarosaurus, the hadrosaur Telmatosaurus, the rhabdodontid Zalmoxes, and the giant pterosaur Hatzegopteryx, one of the largest flying animals ever, which likely acted as an apex predator.
