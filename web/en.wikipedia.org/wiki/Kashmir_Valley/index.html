Kashmir Valley
Largest city: Srinagar
River: Jhelum
Region: Jammu and Kashmir, India

The Kashmir Valley is a fertile valley in the northern part of the Indian union territory of Jammu and Kashmir, lying between the Pir Panjal range to the south-west and the main Himalayan range to the north-east. The Jhelum River flows through it.

Geography

The valley is about 135 km long and 32 km wide, at an average elevation of roughly 1,850 metres. It contains Dal Lake and Wular Lake, and its largest city is Srinagar, the summer capital of the union territory.

Economy and culture

The valley is famed for saffron, apples, walnuts and handicrafts including pashmina shawls and papier-mâché. Tourism, centred on lakes, gardens and houseboats, has long been a major industry. The region has also been at the heart of the Kashmir conflict between India and Pakistan.
