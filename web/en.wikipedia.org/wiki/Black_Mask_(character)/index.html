Black Mask (Roman Sionis) is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Doug Moench and artist Tom Mandrake, the character first appeared in Batman #386 (August 1985). A brutal and sadistic crime lord of Gotham City, he is one of Batman's most enduring enemies, distinguished by the black skull-like mask carved from his father's coffin lid.

Fictional character biography

Roman Sionis was born into Gotham's wealthy elite, the son of neglectful, status-obsessed parents whose company, Janus Cosmetics, he inherited. After mismanaging the firm into ruin—and being forced into a humiliating takeover by Wayne Enterprises—Sionis murdered his parents and burned down the family mansion. From the charred remains of his father's ebony coffin lid he carved his signature mask, adopted the name Black Mask, and built a criminal empire through the False Face Society, a cult-like organization of masked henchmen.

Black Mask became notorious for his use of torture and for branding or disfiguring the faces of his victims. During the "War Games" storyline he seized control of Gotham's underworld, tortured and killed Stephanie Brown (the Spoiler/Robin). He was later killed by Catwoman, though the identity has since been revived and assumed by others, including Jeremiah Arkham.

In other media

The character has appeared in animated series, video games such as Batman: Arkham Origins (2013), and the film Birds of Prey (2020), portrayed by Ewan McGregor.
