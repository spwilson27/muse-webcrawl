Hungary

Hungary is a landlocked country in Central Europe, spanning the Carpathian (Pannonian) Basin, with a population of about 9.6 million and its capital at Budapest, on the Danube. It is bordered by Austria, Slovakia, Ukraine, Romania, Serbia, Croatia and Slovenia.

Hungarians (Magyars) speak a Uralic language unrelated to their Indo-European neighbours. Hungary was a great medieval kingdom and, from 1867, half of Austria-Hungary. Defeat in 1918 cost it two-thirds of its territory under the Treaty of Trianon — still a national grievance. Hungary rose against Soviet domination in 1956 and was a pioneer of the 1989 transitions. It joined the EU in 2004; under Viktor Orbán since 2010 it has clashed with Brussels over rule of law. Lake Balaton is Central Europe's largest lake; thermal baths and paprika are national icons.
