List of cities in India by population

The following is a list of the most populous cities in India. Figures are from the 2011 Census of India, the most recent completed census. The tables below use the population of the city proper (municipal corporation area); urban agglomeration populations, which include suburbs and satellite towns, are considerably larger — Greater Mumbai's urban agglomeration, for example, exceeded 18 million.

In 2011 India had 53 urban agglomerations with a population of over one million, up from 35 in 2001. India remains predominantly rural, with roughly 31 per cent of its population living in urban areas at the 2011 census, though urbanisation has accelerated since.
