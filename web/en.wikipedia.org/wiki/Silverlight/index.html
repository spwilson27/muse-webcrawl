Microsoft Silverlight

DeveloperMicrosoft
Initial release2007
StatusDiscontinued (support ended 2021)

Microsoft Silverlight is a discontinued application framework designed for writing and running rich internet applications, similar to Adobe Flash. It was first released in 2007; the final version, Silverlight 5, was released in 2011, and support ended in October 2021.

Overview

Silverlight applications were written in XAML for the interface combined with .NET languages such as C# or VB.NET for logic, and ran in a browser plug-in on Windows and macOS. It supported streaming media with digital rights management, vector graphics, animation and data binding, and was used for the websites streaming the 2008 and 2010 Olympic Games and, until 2015, Netflix streaming in browsers.

Decline

The rise of HTML5, the exclusion of plug-ins from mobile browsers, and Microsoft's shift to HTML5 and later Edge led to Silverlight's deprecation; it is supported only in legacy Internet Explorer.
