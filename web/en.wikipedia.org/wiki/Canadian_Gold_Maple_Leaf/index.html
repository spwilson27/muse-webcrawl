Canadian Gold Maple Leaf

The Canadian Gold Maple Leaf is a gold bullion coin issued annually by the Government of Canada and produced by the Royal Canadian Mint. Introduced in 1979, it was the first bullion coin to be struck in 99.99% pure gold.

Country: Canada
Composition: 99.99% gold
First issued: 1979
Producer: Royal Canadian Mint

Design

The obverse bears a portrait of the Canadian monarch, while the reverse features a maple leaf, Canada's national symbol. Later issues added radial lines and MintShield security features.

Denominations

The standard coin contains one troy ounce of gold with a face value of 50 Canadian dollars; fractional sizes include 1/2, 1/4, 1/10 and 1/20 ounce.
