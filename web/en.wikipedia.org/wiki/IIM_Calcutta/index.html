Indian Institute of Management Calcutta

Established1961
TypePublic business school
LocationJoka, Kolkata, West Bengal, India

The Indian Institute of Management Calcutta (IIMC) is a public business school located in Joka, Kolkata, West Bengal, India. It was the first Indian Institute of Management, established in November 1961 in collaboration with the MIT Sloan School of Management, the Government of West Bengal, and the Ford Foundation.

Campus

The 135-acre campus in Joka is known for its lakes — students traditionally speak of the "seven lakes" — and its white buildings set amid greenery.

Programmes and reputation

IIM Calcutta is particularly renowned for its finance teaching and research, and was the first Indian business school to achieve the "triple crown" of AACSB, AMBA and EQUIS accreditation.
