The Ministry of Home Affairs (गृह मंत्रालय) is a ministry of the Government of India responsible for internal security, domestic policy, centre–state relations, and the administration of Union Territories. It is headed by the Minister of Home Affairs, one of the senior-most offices in the Union Cabinet.

Responsibilities

The ministry oversees the Central Armed Police Forces, including the Border Security Force, Central Reserve Police Force, Central Industrial Security Force, Indo-Tibetan Border Police, and Sashastra Seema Bal, as well as the Intelligence Bureau, the National Investigation Agency, and the National Disaster Management Authority. Its departments deal with internal security, border management, centre–state relations, official language, and freedom fighters' welfare. It also administers Union Territories without legislatures and coordinates on matters such as the census (through the Registrar General of India) and inter-state councils.

History

The Home Department was created under British rule in the 19th century and was headed by Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel as the first Home Minister of independent India, in which role he oversaw the integration of the princely states. The ministry has since been central to India's response to insurgencies, communal violence, and terrorism, and to reforms of police and disaster management.
