Bibliothèque nationale de France

LocationParis, France
Established1461 (royal library origins)
CollectionOver 40 million items

The Bibliothèque nationale de France (BnF) is the national library of France and one of the largest libraries in the world. It is the legal deposit library for French publications and holds extensive historical collections.

Sites

Its historic Richelieu site in Paris houses manuscripts, prints, coins and performing-arts collections. The François-Mitterrand site, opened in 1996, consists of four glass towers shaped like open books around a sunken garden and holds the main reading rooms and print collections.

Digital library

The BnF's digital library, Gallica, provides free online access to millions of digitised books, manuscripts, maps, images and newspapers.
