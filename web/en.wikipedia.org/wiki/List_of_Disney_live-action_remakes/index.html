List of Disney live-action remakes of animated films

This is a list of live-action or photorealistic animated films produced by Walt Disney Pictures that are remakes, reimaginings or sequels of the studio's traditionally animated classics. The trend began in the 1990s but became a major strategy after the commercial success of Alice in Wonderland (2010) and Maleficent (2014).

Disney's photorealistic remakes have grossed billions of dollars worldwide.

Beauty and the Beast, Aladdin and The Lion King each grossed over $1 billion, with the latter becoming one of the highest-grossing films of all time. Critical reception has been mixed, with reviewers often praising the technical craft while questioning the need to remake beloved classics; several titles, including Dumbo (2019) and Snow White (2025), underperformed commercially.

Upcoming

Remakes of Moana and Tangled have been announced, and other classics such as Hercules and The Aristocats have been reported to be in development.
