Affective science
Field: Interdisciplinary study of emotion
Draws on: Psychology, neuroscience, philosophy, sociology
Key topics: Emotion, mood, affect, emotional regulation

Affective science is the interdisciplinary scientific study of affect, including emotions, moods and feelings, drawing on psychology, neuroscience, cognitive science, sociology and philosophy.

Scope

The field investigates how emotions arise, how they are expressed and regulated, their neural and physiological bases, their development across the lifespan, and their role in decision-making, mental health and social interaction. Methods range from self-report and behavioural experiments to neuroimaging, psychophysiology and computational modelling.

Debates

Central debates include whether emotions are discrete natural kinds or constructed from more basic processes, and the relative roles of appraisal, bodily feedback and culture in emotional experience.
