Paramount+ is an American subscription video on-demand streaming service owned by Paramount Global. It offers live and on-demand content from CBS and the wider Paramount library — including Paramount Pictures films and programming from MTV, Nickelodeon, BET, Comedy Central and the Smithsonian Channel — together with original series and live sports such as the NFL on CBS and UEFA club football.

History

The service launched on October 28, 2014, as CBS All Access, among the first over-the-top offerings from a U.S. broadcast network, built around live CBS streams, current-season episodes and originals such as Star Trek: Discovery and The Good Fight. Following the 2019 re-merger of CBS and Viacom, the service was rebranded as Paramount+ on March 4, 2021, broadening its catalog and expanding internationally across the Americas, Europe and Australia. A premium tier integration with Showtime followed in 2023.

Programming

Signature originals include the modern Star Trek series, Taylor Sheridan dramas such as Yellowstone spin-offs 1883 and 1923, Tulsa King, Halo, The Good Fight, and revivals and children's programming drawing on the Nickelodeon library. Subscribers typically choose between an ad-supported tier and a premium tier.
