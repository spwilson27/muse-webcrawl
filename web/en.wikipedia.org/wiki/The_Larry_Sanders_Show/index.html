The Larry Sanders Show is an American sitcom created by Garry Shandling and Dennis Klein, broadcast on HBO from 1992 to 1998. Set in the offices and studio of a fictional late-night talk show, it stars Shandling as the vain, insecure host Larry Sanders, Jeffrey Tambor as his sycophantic sidekick Hank Kingsley, and Rip Torn as his profane, fiercely loyal producer Artie.

Format

Episodes intercut the on-air talk show — shot on video with real celebrity guests playing warped versions of themselves — with behind-the-scenes footage shot on film, exposing the egos, feuds and anxieties of late-night television. The show's signature device was the contrast between Larry's warm studio persona and his neurotic private self; Hank's catchphrase "Hey now!" became a pop-culture staple.

Reception and legacy

The series won three Primetime Emmy Awards from 56 nominations and is routinely ranked among the greatest television programmes ever made. Its single-camera, laugh-track-free style and cringe-comedy realism directly influenced The Office, Curb Your Enthusiasm, 30 Rock and the modern half-hour comedy generally.
