Institute of technology

An institute of technology (also polytechnic, technological university, or technical university) is an institution of tertiary education that specializes in engineering, technology, applied science, and natural sciences.

Well-known examples include the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), the California Institute of Technology (Caltech), the Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs), and the Swiss Federal Institutes of Technology.

Unlike comprehensive universities, institutes of technology concentrate their teaching and research on science and engineering disciplines, though many have expanded into economics, management, and the humanities. Many grant doctoral degrees and are research-intensive.
