The United Rugby Championship (URC) is an annual rugby union competition involving professional teams from Ireland, Italy, Scotland, South Africa, and Wales. It is one of Europe's three major professional leagues, alongside England's Premiership Rugby and France's Top 14, and its teams qualify for the European Rugby Champions Cup and Challenge Cup.

History

The competition began in 2001 as the Celtic League, contested by Irish, Scottish, and Welsh sides. Sponsorship renamings produced the Magners League, and in 2011 two Italian teams (Benetton and Aironi, later Zebre) joined, creating the Pro12. A major expansion in 2017 added two South African franchises—the Cheetahs and Southern Kings—forming the Pro14. After the Kings folded and the pandemic disrupted play, the league relaunched in 2021 as the United Rugby Championship with the four leading South African sides (Bulls, Lions, Sharks, and Stormers) replacing the previous two.

Format

The 16 teams play an 18-round regular season: each team faces the others in its regional pool home and away, plus additional fixtures against teams from other pools. The top eight advance to single-elimination play-offs culminating in a Grand Final. Regional shields are awarded to the best team in each national pool. The Stormers won the inaugural URC title in 2022, followed by Munster in 2023 and Glasgow Warriors in 2024.
