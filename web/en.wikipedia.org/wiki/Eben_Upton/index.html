Eben Christopher Upton CBE (born 1978) is a British computer scientist and technology executive best known as a founder of the Raspberry Pi project, the series of inexpensive single-board computers designed to promote the teaching of computer science in schools and developing countries. He is chief executive of Raspberry Pi Ltd, the company that develops and markets the computers.

Early life and education

Upton was born in Pontypool, Wales, and grew up in the Leeds area. He studied physics and engineering at St John's College, University of Cambridge, and later completed a PhD in computer science at the Cambridge Computer Laboratory, followed by an MBA at the Cambridge Judge Business School. He worked as Director of Studies in Computer Science at St John's College and as a software engineer at Broadcom in Cambridge, where he worked on graphics processing units.

Raspberry Pi

Motivated by the decline in the number and skill level of applicants to Cambridge's computer science course, Upton and colleagues founded the Raspberry Pi Foundation in 2008, with Upton as a trustee. The first Raspberry Pi, a credit-card-sized computer priced at about US$35, was launched in February 2012 and sold far beyond expectations; tens of millions of units have since been sold, making it one of the best-selling British computers of all time. In 2024 the commercial arm, Raspberry Pi Ltd, was listed on the London Stock Exchange.

Honours

Upton was appointed Commander of the Order of the British Empire (CBE) in the 2016 Birthday Honours for services to business and education. He has received honorary degrees and engineering awards, and is a Fellow of the Royal Academy of Engineering.
