SAE International, originally the Society of Automotive Engineers, is a US-based professional association and standards-developing organisation for engineers in the automotive, aerospace and commercial vehicle industries. Founded in 1905, it has well over 100,000 members worldwide.

Standards

SAE publishes widely used technical standards, including the J3016 classification of driving automation levels (Levels 0–5), motor oil viscosity grades such as 5W-30, the J1772 electric vehicle charging connector, and numerous aerospace standards (AS) and recommended practices.

Activities

Beyond standards, SAE organises conferences, publishes journals and books, and runs collegiate design competitions including Formula SAE, Baja SAE and SAE Aero Design, which are major recruiting and training grounds for engineering students.
