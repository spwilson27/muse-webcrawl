Ocularist

An ocularist hand-painting a prosthetic eye.

An ocularist is a healthcare professional who specialises in the fabrication and fitting of ocular prostheses (artificial eyes) and conformers for people who have lost an eye or were born with a congenital defect such as anophthalmia or microphthalmia.

Practice

Ocularists are skilled technicians trained in the art and science of fitting, shaping and hand-painting prosthetic eyes to match the patient's remaining eye. Modern prostheses are usually made of acrylic plastic (PMMA), shaped from an impression of the eye socket. The work requires collaboration with ophthalmologists and oculoplastic surgeons.

Training

Training typically involves a lengthy apprenticeship; in the United States, certification is offered by the National Examining Board of Ocularists.
