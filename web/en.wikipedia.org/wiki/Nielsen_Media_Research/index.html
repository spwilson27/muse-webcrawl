Nielsen Media Research

Nielsen Media Research (NMR) is an American firm that measures media audiences, including television, radio and newspapers. It is best known for the Nielsen ratings, the audience measurement system that has determined television programme popularity and advertising rates in the United States since the 1950s.

History

The company grew out of the A.C. Nielsen Company, founded by Arthur C. Nielsen in 1923. Nielsen began measuring radio audiences in the 1930s–40s with the Audimeter, and television audiences from 1950. The media research business passed through ownership by Dun & Bradstreet and VNU before becoming part of Nielsen Holdings.

Methods

Nielsen recruits panels of households — historically about 40,000 US homes with "People Meters" — whose viewing is recorded and extrapolated to national estimates. Since the 2010s it has added streaming measurement, return-path data from set-top boxes, and cross-platform metrics such as Nielsen ONE.

Criticism

The ratings have long been criticised for small samples, undercounting younger and minority viewers, and slow adaptation to streaming; rival firms such as Comscore compete in the market. In 2021 the Media Rating Council suspended accreditation of Nielsen's national TV ratings, later restored.
