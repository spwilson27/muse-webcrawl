Subprefectures of Hokkaido

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Hokkaido, Japan's northernmost and second-largest island, is a single prefecture so large that it is subdivided into subprefectures (shinkō-kyoku, formerly shichō), branch administrations of the prefectural government. No other Japanese prefecture has this level of subdivision. There are fourteen subprefectures.

History

The subprefectural system dates to the Meiji-era colonisation and development of the island. Branch offices were established as the Hokkaido government expanded administration across the vast territory. In 2010, the fourteen subprefectural bureaus were reorganised: nine became "general subprefectural bureaus" with broader authority, and five became smaller "subprefectural promotion bureaus" administratively attached to neighbouring general bureaus.

The Ishikari subprefecture contains the prefectural capital, Sapporo, and its office functions within the main prefectural government. The Nemuro subprefecture also administers, from the Japanese perspective, municipalities covering the disputed southern Kuril Islands (Northern Territories).
