Virgin Money UK plc was a British banking group operating under the Virgin Money brand, combining the former Clydesdale Bank, Yorkshire Bank, and the original Virgin Money business. It served around 6.6 million customers with current accounts, savings, mortgages, credit cards, and business banking, and was listed on the London and Australian stock exchanges until its acquisition by Nationwide Building Society in 2024 for approximately £2.9 billion.

A Virgin Money store on a British high street.

History

The Virgin Money brand began in 1995 as Virgin Direct, Richard Branson's financial services venture. It expanded by acquiring the "good bank" portion of nationalized Northern Rock in 2012, taking on its branches and mortgages. In 2018, CYBG—the owner of Clydesdale and Yorkshire Banks—agreed a £1.7 billion takeover of Virgin Money, creating the UK's sixth-largest bank and adopting the Virgin Money name for the combined group, retiring the Clydesdale and Yorkshire brands in the following years.

Acquisition by Nationwide

In March 2024 Nationwide Building Society, the UK's largest building society, announced a £2.9 billion cash acquisition of Virgin Money UK—the largest UK bank takeover since the financial crisis. The deal completed in October 2024, making Nationwide the UK's second-largest provider of mortgages and deposits; Nationwide stated it would retain the Virgin Money brand in the medium term before eventual integration.
