Breanna Stewart

Born: August 27, 1994, Syracuse, New York, U.S.

Height: 6 ft 4 in (1.93 m)

Position: Forward

College: UConn (2012–2016)

Breanna Mackenzie Stewart (born August 27, 1994) is an American professional basketball player. Widely regarded as one of the greatest players in women's basketball history, she won four consecutive NCAA championships with the UConn Huskies, was the first overall pick in the 2016 WNBA draft, and has won multiple WNBA championships and Most Valuable Player awards.

College career

Stewart attended the University of Connecticut from 2012 to 2016, leading the Huskies to four straight national championships (2013–2016) — an unprecedented feat — and winning the NCAA Tournament Most Outstanding Player award four times. She finished her college career as one of the most decorated players in NCAA history, earning multiple national player of the year honours.

Professional career

Stewart was selected first overall by the Seattle Storm in the 2016 WNBA draft and was named Rookie of the Year. In 2018 she won both the league MVP award and her first WNBA championship, earning Finals MVP. She won a second championship with Seattle in 2020, again as Finals MVP. After missing the 2019 season with a torn Achilles tendon, she returned to elite form. In 2023 she signed with the New York Liberty as a free agent, won her second league MVP award that season, and helped the Liberty win the franchise's first WNBA championship in 2024.

International career

Stewart has been a core member of the United States women's national basketball team, winning Olympic gold medals at the 2016, 2020, and 2024 Summer Olympics, as well as FIBA World Cup titles. She was named MVP of the 2018 FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup.

Player profile

At 6 feet 4 inches with a 7-foot-1 wingspan, Stewart is known for her versatility — scoring inside and out, handling the ball, rebounding, and protecting the rim — making her one of the most complete two-way players in the game.
