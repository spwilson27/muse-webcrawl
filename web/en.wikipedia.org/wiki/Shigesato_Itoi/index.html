Shigesato Itoi (Japanese: 糸井 重里, born 10 November 1948) is a Japanese copywriter, essayist, and game designer. He is best known outside Japan as the creator of Nintendo's Mother video game series (released in the West as EarthBound), and in Japan as the founder of the popular website and company Hobonichi.

Career

Itoi first rose to prominence in the 1970s and 1980s as an advertising copywriter, writing celebrated catchphrases for clients including Seibu department stores. He worked with figures such as Hayao Miyazaki — he provided the voice of the father in the Japanese version of My Neighbor Totoro and wrote copy for Studio Ghibli films — and he has appeared as an actor in films including Juzo Itami's The Family Game.

The Mother series

A keen player of role-playing games, Itoi pitched a modern-day RPG to Nintendo and Shigeru Miyamoto. Mother (1989) for the Famicom was followed by Mother 2 (1994; EarthBound in the West), noted for its contemporary setting, humour, and emotional writing, and Mother 3 (2006) for the Game Boy Advance. The games have a devoted following, and Itoi has repeatedly stated there will be no Mother 4 from him.

Hobonichi

In 1998 Itoi founded the website Hobo Nikkan Itoi Shinbun ("Almost Daily Itoi Newspaper"), which grew into the Hobonichi company, known for the Hobonichi Techo planner and for its long-form interviews and essays. The company listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange in 2015.
