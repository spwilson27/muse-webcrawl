IIT Kanpur

Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur

The IIT Kanpur campus

The Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur (IIT Kanpur or IIT-K) is a public technical university in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India. Established in 1959, it is one of the first Indian Institutes of Technology and is designated an institute of national importance. Its roughly 1,055-acre residential campus is at Kalyanpur, about 15 km from Kanpur city.

History

IIT Kanpur was founded under the Indo-American Programme with assistance from a consortium of nine US universities, and it pioneered several practices in Indian technical education, including an early emphasis on computer science — it ran some of India's first computer courses in the 1960s — and a semester-and-credit system.

Academics

The institute offers BTech, MTech, MBA, MSc and PhD programmes across engineering, sciences, humanities and management, and is consistently ranked among India's top engineering schools (typically near the top of the NIRF engineering rankings). Undergraduate admission is through JEE Advanced. Notable features include the campus airstrip and flight laboratory, the Startup Incubation and Innovation Centre, and the annual cultural and technical festivals Antaragni and Techkriti.
