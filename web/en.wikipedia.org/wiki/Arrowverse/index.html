The Arrowverse is an American media franchise and shared universe centred on television series based on DC Comics characters, developed principally by Greg Berlanti and broadcast mainly on The CW. It began with Arrow in 2012 and expanded into one of the largest interconnected franchises in television history.


Heroes of the Arrowverse

Series

The core series are Arrow (2012–2020), The Flash (2014–2023), Supergirl (2015–2021, initially on CBS), Legends of Tomorrow (2016–2022), Black Lightning (2018–2021), Batwoman (2019–2022) and Superman & Lois (2021–2024), alongside animated web series such as Vixen. The multiverse framework allowed series set on parallel Earths to interact.

Crossovers

Annual crossover events — including "Elseworlds" (2018) and the five-part "Crisis on Infinite Earths" (2019–20) — merged the continuities, rebooted the multiverse and incorporated cameos from other DC adaptations. "Crisis" concluded Arrow with the death of Oliver Queen and consolidated the heroes on a single Earth-Prime.

End of the franchise

With the cancellation or conclusion of its remaining series, the Arrowverse effectively ended in 2023 with the final season of The Flash; Superman & Lois, later established as set on a separate Earth, concluded in 2024.
