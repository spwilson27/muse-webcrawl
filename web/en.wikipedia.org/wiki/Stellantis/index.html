Stellantis

Stellantis N.V. is a multinational automotive manufacturing company formed in January 2021 through the 50/50 merger of the Italian-American conglomerate Fiat Chrysler Automobiles (FCA) and the French PSA Group. Headquartered in Hoofddorp, Netherlands, for tax purposes, Stellantis is one of the world's largest automakers by sales, marketing vehicles under fourteen brands including Fiat, Peugeot, Citroën, Opel, Jeep, Ram, Dodge, Chrysler, Alfa Romeo, and Maserati. The name Stellantis derives from the Latin verb stello, "to brighten with stars".

History

FCA and PSA announced their merger agreement on 31 October 2019, intending to create the world's fourth-largest automaker by volume. After regulatory approvals in more than twenty jurisdictions, including the European Union—which required concessions regarding small commercial vans—the merger was completed on 16 January 2021, and Stellantis shares began trading in Milan, Paris, and New York on 18 January. Carlos Tavares, formerly PSA's chief executive, became CEO, while John Elkann of the Agnelli family became chairman. Exor, the Agnelli holding company, is the largest shareholder.

Strategy

Under its "Dare Forward 2030" plan announced in 2022, Stellantis targeted reaching 100% battery-electric vehicle sales in Europe and 50% in the United States by 2030, investing more than €30 billion in electrification and software. The group develops four multi-energy platforms (STLA Small, Medium, Large, and Frame) intended to underpin most of its future lineup. Tavares resigned abruptly in December 2024 amid disagreements with the board over strategy and falling sales and profits, particularly in North America; a successor process followed, with Antonio Filosa appointed CEO in 2025.

Operations

Stellantis operates manufacturing plants in more than 30 countries, with major hubs in France, Italy, Germany, Spain, Poland, the United States, Mexico, Brazil, and China (through joint ventures). It sold about 6.2 million vehicles in 2023 and about 5.7 million in 2024. The company also owns components and mobility businesses, including the car-sharing service Free2Move and, from 2023, a stake in the Chinese EV maker Leapmotor, with which it formed a joint venture to sell Leapmotor vehicles outside China.
