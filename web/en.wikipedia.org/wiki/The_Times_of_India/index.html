The Times of India (TOI) is an Indian English-language daily newspaper, founded in 1838 as The Bombay Times and Journal of Commerce, and the flagship of Bennett, Coleman & Co. (the Times Group). It has long claimed among the largest circulations of any English-language daily in the world.

Character

TOI is a mass-market broadsheet: metro city editions, heavy Bollywood and cricket coverage, and the 'masthead model' of advertising-led Indian newspaper economics associated with the Jain family ownership. Its Page 3 celebrity supplements helped define Indian media culture.

Digital

timesofindia.indiatimes.com is one of the most-visited news websites in the world, and the group's Times Internet operates a large portfolio of digital properties.
