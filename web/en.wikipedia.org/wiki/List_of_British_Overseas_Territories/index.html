British Overseas Territories

The British Overseas Territories (BOTs) are fourteen territories, all of which have a constitutional link with the United Kingdom, but do not form part of the United Kingdom itself. They are the remnants of the British Empire that have not been granted independence or have voted to remain British territories. This article lists the territories with their locations, capitals, and populations.

Governance

The territories are largely self-governing in internal affairs, with the United Kingdom responsible for defence and foreign relations. They are administered through the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office. Most have their own legislatures and legal systems, and their populations hold British Overseas Territories citizenship, which since 2002 has generally also conferred British citizenship.
