Peter J. Carroll

Peter James Carroll (born 1953) is a British occultist and author, best known as a co-founder of the chaos magic movement. With Ray Sherwin he founded the Illuminates of Thanateros (IOT) in the late 1970s.

Works

His books Liber Null (1978) and Psychonaut (1982), often published together as Liber Null & Psychonaut, are foundational texts of chaos magic, presenting magic as a technique based on belief-shifting rather than fixed symbolism. Later works include Liber Kaos (1992), PsyberMagick, and writings on the philosophy of science, including The Apophenion and The Octavo, which develop his ideas about probability and enchantment.

Influence

Carroll's pragmatic, results-oriented approach — including concepts such as sigils, gnosis, and paradigmatic flexibility — has influenced contemporary occultism widely, both within the IOT and beyond it.
