Marvel Entertainment

Marvel Entertainment, LLC was an American entertainment company founded in 1998 as the parent company of Marvel Comics and other businesses exploiting Marvel's library of more than 8,000 characters. Formed from the merger of Marvel Entertainment Group and Toy Biz after Marvel's 1996 bankruptcy, it was acquired by the Walt Disney Company in 2009 for approximately US$4 billion.

Business

Marvel Entertainment oversaw publishing (Marvel Comics), licensing of characters for toys, apparel, and other merchandise, and — through Marvel Studios, founded as Marvel Films — the film and television productions that became the Marvel Cinematic Universe, the highest-grossing film franchise in history. Disney's purchase brought characters such as Spider-Man, the X-Men, the Avengers, and Iron Man into the Disney portfolio, although some film rights were then held by other studios and were progressively recovered or shared.

Reorganisation

In 2023, Disney folded Marvel Entertainment's remaining operations — chiefly consumer products and licensing units not already elsewhere — into other Disney segments, and Marvel Entertainment ceased to exist as a separate subsidiary; Marvel Comics continues as an imprint of Marvel Worldwide, and Marvel Studios operates under Walt Disney Studios.
