20 Feet from Stardom is a 2013 American documentary film directed by Morgan Neville about the lives of backing vocalists who perform behind major recording artists. It premiered at the Sundance Film Festival and won the Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature.

Content

The film profiles singers including Darlene Love, Merry Clayton, Lisa Fischer, Judith Hill, Táta Vega and Claudia Lennear, tracing their contributions to landmark recordings — Clayton's vocal on the Rolling Stones' "Gimme Shelter" is a central episode — and their often frustrated attempts at solo careers. It features interviews with stars they supported, including Bruce Springsteen, Stevie Wonder, Mick Jagger and Sting.

Reception

The film was widely acclaimed for its music and storytelling, and it performed strongly at the box office for a documentary. Darlene Love sang "Lean on Me" during the Oscar ceremony when the film won Best Documentary Feature at the 86th Academy Awards.
