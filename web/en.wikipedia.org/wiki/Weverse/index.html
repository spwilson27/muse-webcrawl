Weverse

Weverse (Korean: 위버스; stylised in all caps) is a South Korean mobile app and web platform created by Hybe Corporation. The app specialises in hosting multimedia content and artist-to-fan communications for musical artists, operating as a fan community platform with posts, artist messages, live streams, and an integrated merchandise shop.

History

Weverse was launched in June 2019 by beNX, a subsidiary of Big Hit Entertainment (now Hybe). It first hosted communities for BTS and Tomorrow X Together, then expanded to most Hybe label artists and, later, to artists from other agencies in South Korea and abroad. In 2022 it merged with Naver's V Live streaming service, absorbing its live broadcasting features.

Features

Fans join individual artist communities to view posts and comments by artists, watch live broadcasts via Weverse Live, and buy official merchandise through Weverse Shop. Paid memberships offer exclusive content. The platform supports automatic translation of posts into multiple languages, reflecting the global K-pop audience; by the early 2020s it had tens of millions of monthly users.
