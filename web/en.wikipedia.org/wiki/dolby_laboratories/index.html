Dolby Laboratories

Dolby Laboratories, Inc. is an American technology corporation specialising in audio noise reduction, audio encoding and compression, and immersive entertainment technologies. It was founded by Ray Dolby in London in 1965 and later moved to the United States, where it is headquartered in San Francisco, California.

History

Ray Dolby developed the Dolby noise-reduction system for professional tape recorders (Dolby A) in the 1960s, followed by consumer versions (Dolby B and C) widely used on cassette tapes. The company then moved into cinema sound with Dolby Stereo in the 1970s, which brought optical surround sound to films such as Star Wars (1977). Dolby Digital (AC-3) brought digital 5.1 surround to cinemas, DVDs, and television in the 1990s.

Technologies

Dolby licenses its technologies to manufacturers of consumer electronics, cinemas, and content producers. Major products include Dolby Atmos, an object-based immersive audio format; Dolby Vision, a high-dynamic-range video format; Dolby Cinema, a premium cinema concept combining both; and voice and conferencing technologies through its Dolby.io platform.
