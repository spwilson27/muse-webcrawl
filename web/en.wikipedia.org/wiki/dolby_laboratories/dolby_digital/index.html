Dolby Digital

Dolby Laboratories, Inc. is an American technology corporation headquartered in San Francisco, California, that develops audio and imaging technologies. Its best-known product family includes Dolby Digital, the digital multichannel audio format used in cinemas, DVDs, Blu-ray discs, and broadcast television.

History

Ray Dolby founded the company in London in 1965, introducing Dolby noise reduction for professional and later consumer tape recording. The company moved to San Francisco in 1976. Dolby Digital surround sound debuted in cinemas in 1992 and went on to become a dominant standard in home entertainment.

Technologies

Beyond Dolby Digital and Dolby Digital Plus, the company's portfolio includes Dolby Atmos for object-based immersive audio, Dolby Vision high-dynamic-range imaging, Dolby Voice for conferencing, and the Dolby Cinema premium theatre concept.
