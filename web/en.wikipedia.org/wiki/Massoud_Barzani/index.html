Massoud Barzani (born 16 August 1946) is a Kurdish politician, leader of the Kurdistan Democratic Party since 1979 and President of the Kurdistan Region of Iraq from 2005 to 2017. He is also catalogued in some sources under the spelling "Massoud", an alternative transliteration of his Kurdish name.

Biography

Born in Mahabad during his father Mustafa Barzani's exile, Barzani spent part of his childhood in the Soviet Union before returning to Iraq. He joined the Peshmerga and, on his father's death in 1979, assumed leadership of the KDP. He played a central role in the establishment of the autonomous Kurdistan Region after 1991, served on the Iraqi Governing Council in 2003–04, and was twice elected regional president. His tenure ended on 1 November 2017, following the fallout from the September 2017 independence referendum and Baghdad's retaking of Kirkuk; he remains the KDP's president and the dominant figure of the Barzani family in Kurdish politics.
