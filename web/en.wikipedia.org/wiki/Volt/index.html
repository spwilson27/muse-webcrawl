Volt

The volt (symbol: V) is the SI unit of electric potential, potential difference, and electromotive force, named after Alessandro Volta.

Definition

One volt is the potential difference across a conductor when a current of one ampere dissipates one watt of power: 1 V = 1 W/A = 1 J/C. Since 2019 it is defined via the Josephson effect and fixed elementary charge.

Common voltages

AA batteries supply 1.5 V, car batteries 12 V, and household mains 120 V or 230 V depending on country.
