List of Formula One circuits

This is a list of circuits that have hosted a Formula One World Championship race since the championship began in 1950. More than seventy different venues have been used, ranging from purpose-built road courses to street circuits and, in the early years, public-road tracks.

Historic circuits

Notable former venues include the Nürburgring (including the 22.8 km Nordschleife), Watkins Glen in the United States, the Österreichring, Brands Hatch, Kyalami in South Africa, the Adelaide street circuit, Sepang in Malaysia, the Buddh International Circuit in India, Istanbul Park in Turkey and the Indianapolis Motor Speedway, which hosted the United States Grand Prix from 2000 to 2007 and the Indianapolis 500 as a championship round in the 1950s. Monza is the most-used circuit, having hosted the Italian Grand Prix in almost every season since 1950.
