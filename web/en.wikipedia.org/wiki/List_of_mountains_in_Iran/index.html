List of mountains in Iran

Iran is one of the most mountainous countries in the world. Two great ranges dominate: the Alborz along the Caspian coast in the north and the Zagros running from the northwest to the south. Mount Damavand, a dormant volcano in the Alborz, is the highest peak in the Middle East and the highest volcano in Asia.

Major ranges

Alborz

The Alborz stretches about 900 km along the southern shore of the Caspian Sea, separating the humid Caspian lowlands from the arid central plateau. It contains Damavand and the popular Tochal ski area above Tehran.

Zagros

The Zagros extends roughly 1,600 km from Kurdistan to the Strait of Hormuz. Folded sedimentary ridges give it a distinctive parallel-ridge landscape; its highest summits are Zard-Kuh and Dena.
