List of hairstyles

Hairstyles vary widely by culture, era, fashion and personal identity. This list of hairstyles collects named styles, from ancient and ceremonial forms to 20th- and 21st-century fashion cuts. For men's facial hair, see the separate list of facial hairstyles.

Short styles

StyleDescription
Buzz cutHair clipped very short all over with electric clippers; military standard.
Crew cutShort on top, slightly longer at the front, tapered sides.
Caesar cutShort, horizontally straight fringe, named after depictions of Julius Caesar.
Pixie cutVery short women's style, short at back and sides with slightly longer top; popularised in the 1950s–60s.
Bob cutShort-to-medium blunt cut at jaw level, emblematic of 1920s flappers.
PageboyBob-like style turned under at the ends, with a fringe.
Eton cropVery short, slicked women's cut of the 1920s.
UndercutLonger top with closely shaved sides and back; popular in the 2010s.
Fade / taper fadeGraduated blend from skin to longer hair; low, mid, high and skin fades.
Flat topTop hair cut to stand up and form a level plane.
Hi-top fadeTall flat top with faded sides, iconic in 1980s–90s hip hop.

Medium and long styles

StyleDescription
PonytailHair gathered and tied at the back; variants include side and high ponytails.
BunHair coiled into a knot on top or back of the head; includes chignon, top knot and space buns.
Braids / plaitsInterwoven strands: French braid, Dutch braid, fishtail braid, crown braid.
CornrowsTight braids plaited close to the scalp in rows; ancient African style.
Box braidsIndividual plaits divided into square sections, often with extensions.
DreadlocksRope-like strands formed by matting or coiling hair; associated with Rastafari but far older globally.
AfroNatural hair grown out into a rounded halo; a symbol of Black pride in the 1960s–70s.
Perm (permanent wave)Chemically set curls; the tight "poodle" perms of the 1980s are distinctive.
Jheri curlGlossy loosened curls popular in the 1980s.
BeehiveTall, conical upswept style of the 1960s.
BouffantHair teased and padded for volume.
ShagLayered, feathered cut of the 1970s and its revivals (wolf cut, modern mullet-shag).
MulletShort at front and sides, long at the back; 1980s icon and 2020s revival.
PompadourHair swept up and back from the forehead; from Madame de Pompadour to Elvis Presley.
QuiffVolume at the front fringe, merging pompadour and flat-top elements.
Ducktail1950s greaser style combed back to meet at the centre.
Mop topThe Beatles' fringe style of the 1960s.
DevilockLong front lock with shaved/short sides and back, created by the Misfits.
MohawkShaved sides with a central strip of upright hair; also fauxhawk and liberty spikes.
RattailA single thin tail of long hair at the nape.
Hime cutJapanese style with blunt-cut sidelocks and fringe.
Feathered hairLayered, swept-back style popularised by Farrah Fawcett in the 1970s.
The RachelLayered, voluminous style worn by Jennifer Aniston in Friends, much copied in the 1990s.

Historical and cultural styles

  • Tonsure — monastic shaving of the crown.
  • Queue — braided ponytail mandated for Han Chinese men under the Qing dynasty.
  • Chonmage — Japanese samurai topknot.
  • Sidelocks (payot) — worn by Orthodox Jewish men and boys.
  • Powdered wigs — 17th–18th-century European fashion (peruke, periwig).
  • Odango — Chinese double buns; Sikh dastar covers uncut hair as an article of faith.
  • Flattop, DA and conk — mid-20th-century styles, the conk using chemical straighteners.
