List of United States combatant commands

The United States Department of Defense maintains eleven combatant commands (COCOMs): joint military commands headed by four-star officers that exercise operational control of U.S. forces. Six are geographic commands with defined areas of responsibility (AOR); five are functional commands with worldwide responsibilities. Combatant commanders report to the President through the Secretary of Defense.
