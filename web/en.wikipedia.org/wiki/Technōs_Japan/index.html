Technōs Japan

Technōs Japan Corp. was a Japanese video game developer and publisher, best known for the Kunio-kun series, the Double Dragon series and the creation of the Renegade franchise. The company was founded in 1981 by Kunio Taki, a former employee of Data East, and was headquartered in Kiyose, Tokyo.

History

Technōs began as a small team that later established its own arcade hardware and publishing operation. Its early arcade hits included Mysterious Stones and the wrestling game Exciting Hour (released overseas as Mat Mania). In 1986 the company released Renegade (in Japan, Nekketsu Kōha Kunio-kun), one of the first belt-scrolling beat 'em up games, which launched the long-running Kunio-kun series of school-delinquent action games and sports titles.

Notable games

Double Dragon (1987) became Technōs' most internationally successful title and a landmark of the beat 'em up genre, popularizing two-player cooperative play. Other notable releases include Super Dodge Ball, River City Ransom, Crash 'n the Boys: Street Challenge and the World Cup Soccer Kunio-kun games for the Nintendo Entertainment System.

Bankruptcy and legacy

Facing financial difficulties in the mid-1990s, Technōs Japan closed in 1996. Former staff founded the studio Million Co., which later acquired rights to many Technōs properties. In 2015, Arc System Works purchased the intellectual property rights to Technōs Japan's catalogue, including Double Dragon and Kunio-kun, and has continued to publish new entries and re-releases in those series.
