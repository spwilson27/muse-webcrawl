Gun dog

Gun dogs, or bird dogs, are types of hunting dogs developed to assist hunters in finding and retrieving game, usually birds. They are traditionally divided into three primary groups according to their working method.

Types

Retrievers

Retrievers, such as the Labrador Retriever and Golden Retriever, specialise in finding and bringing back shot game, often from water, with a soft mouth that does not damage the bird.

Flushing dogs

Spaniels, such as the English Springer Spaniel and Cocker Spaniel, work close to the hunter, quartering ground cover to flush game into the air within gunshot range.

Pointing breeds

Pointers and setters range widely ahead of the hunter and freeze in a distinctive "point" stance on locating game by scent, allowing the hunter to approach before the birds are flushed.

Training

Gun dog training emphasises steadiness to shot, whistle and hand signals, marking fallen game, and delivery to hand. Many gun dog breeds also serve as popular family pets, though their energy and retrieving instincts require exercise and mental work.
