Pernod Ricard SA is a French company best known as a producer of wine and spirits, and one of the world's largest alcoholic beverage groups, second only to Diageo. Its flagship products are the anise-based spirits Pernod Anise and Ricard pastis, from which the group takes its name.

History

The company was formed in 1975 by the merger of two French anise producers: Pernod, founded in 1805 by Henri-Louis Pernod in Pontarlier as a maker of absinthe and later of anise liqueur after the absinthe ban, and Ricard, founded in Marseille in 1932 by Paul Ricard, creator of the Ricard pastis brand. Under Patrick Ricard (chairman from 1978 until his death in 2012) the group expanded internationally by acquisition, buying, among others, Irish Distillers (Jameson, 1988), parts of Seagram (2000, including Chivas Regal and Martell), Allied Domecq (2005, including Ballantine's, Beefeater, Kahlúa and Mumm champagne), and Vin & Sprit (2008), owner of Absolut Vodka. Alexandre Ricard, Paul Ricard's grandson, became chairman and CEO in 2015.

Brands

Major brands include Absolut vodka, Jameson Irish whiskey, Chivas Regal, The Glenlivet and Ballantine's Scotch whiskies, Martell cognac, Beefeater gin, Havana Club rum, Malibu, Kahlúa, Ricard pastis, and the Mumm and Perrier-Jouët champagnes, as well as wines such as Jacob's Creek and Campo Viejo.

Operations

Pernod Ricard operates a decentralised model of brand companies and market companies, sells in roughly 160 countries and is a component of the CAC 40 index of the Paris stock exchange. The company promotes "responsible drinking" programmes and has announced sustainability targets under its "Good Times from a Good Place" roadmap.
