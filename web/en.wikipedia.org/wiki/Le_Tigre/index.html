Le Tigre is an American electronic rock band formed in New York City in 1998 by Kathleen Hanna (formerly of Bikini Kill), Johanna Fateman, and later JD Samson. The band fused punk and riot grrrl politics with dance beats, samplers, and lo-fi electronics, making explicitly feminist and queer-positive music designed for the dance floor as much as the protest.

History

Hanna began Le Tigre as a live backing project for her solo work (as "Julie Ruin") before it became a full band. The self-titled debut album (1999) included "Hot Topic," a roll-call of feminist and queer artists, and "Deceptacon," which became a durable club favourite. Feminist Sweepstakes (2001) followed, and in 2004 the band signed to a major label for This Island, containing a cover of the Pointer Sisters' "I'm So Excited." After an extended hiatus from 2011, the band reunited for the single "I'm With Her" (2016) in support of Hillary Clinton's presidential campaign, and resumed touring in the 2020s.

Style and influence

Le Tigre's sound layers drum machines, cheap synths, guitar, and shouted/sung vocals; its live shows used synchronised choreography and slide projections. The band is credited with helping define electroclash and dance-punk and with carrying riot grrrl's politics into electronic pop.
