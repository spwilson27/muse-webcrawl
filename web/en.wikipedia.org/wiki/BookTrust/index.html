BookTrust

BookTrust
Type: Charity
Founded: 1921 (as the Book Trade Benevolent Society lineage via the National Book League, 1925)
Headquarters: London, England
Purpose: Promoting children's reading
Known programme: Bookstart

BookTrust is a British charity dedicated to getting children reading, particularly children from disadvantaged backgrounds. It is best known for Bookstart, the world's first national book-gifting programme for babies and young children, which provides free book packs through health visitors, libraries, and early-years settings.

Programmes

The charity's flagship programme, Bookstart, began in 1992 in Birmingham in partnership with health and library services and was later rolled out nationally following research showing that babies given books early read more and performed better at school. BookTrust also supplies book packs to school starters (Booktime traditions and successor schemes), runs reading programmes for children in care such as the Letterbox Club, and administers prizes and initiatives that celebrate children's books and illustrators.

Work and funding

BookTrust works with publishers, local authorities, libraries, and health professionals across England, Wales, and Northern Ireland, and is largely funded by Arts Council England and government departments alongside charitable donations. Its research campaigns, including work on bedtime reading and reading habits during the COVID-19 pandemic, aim to evidence the benefits of shared reading for children's development and wellbeing.
