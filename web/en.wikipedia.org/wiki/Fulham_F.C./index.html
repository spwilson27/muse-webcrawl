Fulham F.C.

Fulham Football Club is a professional football club based in Fulham, London, England. Founded in 1879, it is the oldest football club in London still playing professionally. The club has played at Craven Cottage, on the banks of the River Thames, since 1896, and its colours are white shirts and black shorts.

History

Fulham joined the Football League in 1907 and spent much of the 20th century in the lower divisions, with a golden period in the 1950s and 60s around star player Johnny Haynes, the first £100-a-week footballer. After near-bankruptcy in the 1990s, investment by Mohamed Al-Fayed brought rapid promotion to the Premier League in 2001. Fulham reached the 1975 FA Cup final and, most famously, the 2010 UEFA Europa League final under Roy Hodgson, losing to Atlético Madrid after extra time. Since 2013 the club has been owned by Shahid Khan and has alternated between the Premier League and the Championship before re-establishing itself in the top flight under Marco Silva.
