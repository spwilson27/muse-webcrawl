IAC Inc.

American holding company

The IAC Building in New York City, designed by Frank Gehry

IAC Inc. is an American holding company that owns brands in media and internet businesses, headquartered at the IAC Building in New York City. Led for decades by chairman Barry Diller, IAC has a long history of acquiring internet companies, growing them, and spinning them off to shareholders as independent public companies.

History

The company's roots lie in the television and home-shopping businesses of the 1980s and 1990s; IAC grew out of Silver King Communications and the Home Shopping Network. Under the name InterActiveCorp from 2003, and IAC/InterActiveCorp, it accumulated properties including Ticketmaster, Expedia, Hotels.com, Match.com, LendingTree, Citysearch and Ask Jeeves.

Spin-offs and portfolio

IAC spun off Expedia (2005), Ticketmaster/Live Nation and other units (2008), the Match Group (completed 2020), and Vimeo (2021). Its best-known remaining businesses have included Dotdash Meredith in publishing and Angi Inc. in home services. The company's strategy has often been summarised as building and incubating internet brands and then distributing them to shareholders.
