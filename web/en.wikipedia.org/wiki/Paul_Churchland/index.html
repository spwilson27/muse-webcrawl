Paul Churchland

Paul Montgomery Churchland (born 21 October 1942) is a Canadian philosopher known for his work in philosophy of mind and neurophilosophy. He taught for many years at the University of California, San Diego, where he was Valtz Family Professor of Philosophy. He is married to the philosopher Patricia Churchland; together they are leading proponents of eliminative materialism.

Eliminative materialism

In papers such as "Eliminative Materialism and the Propositional Attitudes" (1981) and books including Scientific Realism and the Plasticity of Mind (1979) and A Neurocomputational Perspective (1989), Churchland argued that our everyday "folk psychology" — talk of beliefs and desires — is a stagnant, possibly false theory that may be eliminated, rather than reduced, by a mature neuroscience, much as phlogiston was eliminated by modern chemistry.

Other work

His The Engine of Reason, the Seat of the Soul (1995) popularised connectionist, neural-network models of cognition for a general audience. Churchland has also written on scientific realism, conceptual change, and the application of network dynamics to knowledge, perception and consciousness.
