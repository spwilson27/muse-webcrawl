The Common Admission Test (CAT) is a computer-based entrance examination in India used for admission to postgraduate management programmes, principally those of the Indian Institutes of Management (IIMs). It is conducted annually by one of the IIMs on a rotating basis.

Format

The test assesses verbal ability and reading comprehension, data interpretation and logical reasoning, and quantitative ability. It is held over a limited number of sessions in a single period each year, and scores are reported as percentiles, which the IIMs and many other business schools use for shortlisting candidates for interviews.

Scale

CAT is among the most competitive graduate admissions tests in India, with well over two hundred thousand candidates registering in recent years for a few thousand IIM seats.
