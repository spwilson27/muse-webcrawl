Oklahoma City Thunder

The Oklahoma City Thunder are an American professional basketball team based in Oklahoma City. The Thunder compete in the National Basketball Association (NBA) as a member of the Western Conference Northwest Division. The team plays its home games at the Paycom Center.

History

The franchise was established in 1967 as the Seattle SuperSonics. The SuperSonics won the NBA championship in 1979. In 2008, after a dispute over a new arena in Seattle, the franchise relocated to Oklahoma City and was renamed the Thunder.

Led by Kevin Durant, Russell Westbrook, and James Harden, the Thunder reached the NBA Finals in 2012, losing to the Miami Heat. Westbrook won the league MVP award in 2017. After a rebuilding period around Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, the Thunder returned to contention and won their first NBA championship in Oklahoma City in 2025, defeating the Indiana Pacers in the Finals, with Gilgeous-Alexander named both league and Finals MVP.

Rivalries and identity

The team's colours are Thunder blue, sunset red-orange, and yellow. Its mascot is Rumble the Bison. The Thunder developed a strong local following, and the franchise's departure from Seattle remains a contentious episode in NBA history.
