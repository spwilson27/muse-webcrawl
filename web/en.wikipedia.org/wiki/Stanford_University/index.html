Stanford University, officially the Leland Stanford Junior University, is a private research university in Stanford, California, United States. Founded in 1885 by railroad magnate Leland Stanford and his wife Jane in memory of their only child, Leland Stanford Jr., who died of typhoid fever at 15, it opened in 1891. Stanford is among the world's most prestigious universities and is closely associated with Silicon Valley: its alumni and faculty founded companies including Google, Hewlett-Packard, Nvidia, Yahoo, Cisco and Instagram, and Apple co-founder Steve Jobs delivered its famous 2005 commencement address. Its website is stanford.edu.

History

The university was endowed with the Stanfords' Palo Alto stock farm. Early leaders included first president David Starr Jordan. After the 1906 San Francisco earthquake damaged the campus, Stanford rebuilt around its sandstone Main Quad with red-tile roofs. Under provost Frederick Terman — often called "the father of Silicon Valley" — Stanford encouraged faculty and graduates to start companies nearby and developed the Stanford Industrial Park (1951), leasing land to firms such as HP and Varian, a model that helped seed the region's technology industry.

Academics

Stanford has seven schools, including Engineering, Law, Medicine, Business (the Graduate School of Business) and the Doerr School of Sustainability, opened in 2022. Admission is extremely selective. Its research centres include the SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory, operated for the US Department of Energy, and the Hoover Institution, a public-policy think tank. Stanford faculty and alumni include numerous Nobel laureates and Turing Award winners, and its teams — the Cardinal — have won more NCAA team championships than any other university.

Campus

The campus, at about 8,180 acres, is one of the largest in the United States, featuring Palm Drive, the Main Quad, Memorial Church with its mosaics, the 285-foot Hoover Tower, and an extensive collection of Rodin sculptures. The university owns much of the surrounding land, including the Stanford Shopping Center and Research Park.
