Pet Avengers

The Pet Avengers are a fictional team of animal superheroes appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. The team brings together the animal companions of established heroes.

Members

The founding lineup, assembled in the 2009 limited series Lockjaw and the Pet Avengers, includes Lockjaw, the teleporting Inhuman dog; Lockheed, Kitty Pryde's dragon companion; Redwing, Falcon's hawk; Zabu, Ka-Zar's sabre-toothed tiger; Hairball (Niels), Speedball's cat; and Ms. Lion, Aunt May's dog, guided by Throg, a frog wielding a sliver of Thor's hammer Mjolnir.

Story

In their debut series the animals secretly gather the Infinity Gems before Thanos can claim them, operating unnoticed by their owners. The team returned in sequels including Lockjaw and the Pet Avengers Unleashed and various anthology appearances.
