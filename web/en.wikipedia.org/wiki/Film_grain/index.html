Film grain

Film grain or film granularity is the random optical texture of processed photographic film due to the presence of small particles of a metallic silver, or dye clouds, developed from silver halide that have received enough photons.

Characteristics

Grain is influenced by film stock speed (ISO), development, and exposure; faster films have larger silver halide crystals and produce more visible grain. While grain can be aesthetically desirable as a cinematic texture, excessive grain from underexposure is considered a defect.

Digital grain

Digital noise is a distinct phenomenon arising from sensor electronics. Filmmakers shooting digitally sometimes add synthetic grain in post-production to emulate the look of photochemical film.
