SIMBAD

SIMBAD (the Set of Identifications, Measurements and Bibliography for Astronomical Data) is an astronomical database of objects beyond the Solar System. It is maintained by the Centre de données astronomiques de Strasbourg (CDS), France.

SIMBAD was created by merging the Catalog of Stellar Identifications (CSI) and the Bibliographic Star Index as they existed in 1979. The first online interactive version, known as Version 2, was made available in 1981. Version 3, developed in the C language and running on Unix stations, was released in 1990. Version 4, with a fully relational database and a Java-based client, followed in 2006.

Contents

As of recent releases, SIMBAD contains tens of millions of astronomical objects — stars, galaxies, nebulae, clusters, quasars and more — with multiple identifiers cross-referenced for each object, basic observational data (coordinates, magnitudes, proper motions, parallaxes, spectral types), and bibliographic references linking objects to the scientific literature.

Use

Astronomers query SIMBAD by object name or coordinates to identify objects and gather measurements from heterogeneous catalogues and publications. It forms one of the core services of the Virtual Observatory, alongside the VizieR catalogue service and the Aladin sky atlas, also maintained by the CDS.
