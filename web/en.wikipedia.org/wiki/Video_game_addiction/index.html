Video game addiction

Video game addiction, also called gaming disorder, is characterized by impaired control over gaming, increasing priority given to gaming over other activities, and continuation despite negative consequences.

Classification

The World Health Organization included gaming disorder in ICD-11 in 2018. The DSM-5 lists Internet Gaming Disorder as a condition for further study.

Symptoms and treatment

Symptoms include withdrawal, tolerance, and loss of interests. Debate continues over prevalence and diagnostic boundaries. Treatment often involves cognitive behavioural therapy and limiting access.
