Alliance Boots GmbH was a multinational pharmacy-led health and beauty retailer and pharmaceutical wholesaler headquartered in Bern, Switzerland. It was formed in 2006 by the merger of the British high-street chain Boots Group and the pan-European wholesaler Alliance UniChem.

History

Boots, founded in Nottingham in 1849 by John Boot, was one of Britain's best-known retailers. Alliance UniChem, built up by Stefano Pessina, was a major drug wholesaler. Their merger created a group operating over 3,000 stores and supplying pharmacies across Europe.

In 2007 Alliance Boots was taken private by Kohlberg Kravis Roberts and Pessina in an £11.1 billion leveraged buyout, then the largest in Europe. In 2012 the American chain Walgreens bought a 45% stake, and in December 2014 it acquired the remainder, forming Walgreens Boots Alliance.

Operations

Its best-known retail brand was Boots in the UK and Ireland, and it conducted pharmaceutical wholesaling in more than 20 countries.
