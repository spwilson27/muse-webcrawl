List of dates predicted for apocalyptic events

This article lists dates predicted for apocalyptic events such as the end of the world, the Second Coming of Christ, or other eschatological catastrophes. Predictions of doomsday have been made for at least two thousand years, by religious figures, astrologers, scientists and cranks; none has come to pass. Failed predictions often generate the phenomenon known as the Great Disappointment, after the Millerite movement of the 1840s, and are studied by psychologists interested in cognitive dissonance.

Future predictions

Future dates occasionally proposed include various years in the 2020s–2060s (Isaac Newton calculated 2060 as a not-before date), while scientific forecasts place genuinely existential events — asteroid impacts, supervolcanoes, the Sun's red-giant phase — on timescales of thousands to billions of years.
