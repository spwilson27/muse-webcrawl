Dress code (Western)

Western dress codes categorise clothing by formality for different social occasions, ranging from ceremonial and formal to informal and casual. The main traditional categories are, in descending order of formality:

  • Formal wear — white tie for evening events, black tie for evening dinners and balls; morning dress for formal daytime occasions.
  • Semi-formal wear — the dinner jacket (tuxedo) for evening, the stroller or lounge suit for day.
  • Informal wear — suits for business and social occasions.
  • Casual wear — everyday clothing, including "smart casual" and "business casual" codes used by employers.

History

The modern hierarchy grew out of 19th- and early 20th-century court and social etiquette, codified by etiquette writers and later simplified; since the late 20th century dress codes have steadily relaxed in most workplaces and social settings.
