Uplifting trance

Uplifting trance emphasizes soaring melodies and euphoric breakdowns

Uplifting trance, also known as euphoric trance or anthem trance, is a subgenre of trance music characterized by long melodic build-ups, euphoric major-key chord progressions, extended breakdowns, and energetic climaxes. It developed out of the progressive and epic trance sounds of the late 1990s and is closely associated with the emotional, melody-driven side of the genre, in contrast to harder or more minimal styles.

Characteristics

Uplifting trance tracks typically run at tempos around 138–142 BPM in a 4/4 time signature. Signature features include a driving offbeat or rolling bassline, lush supersaw pad chords, arpeggiated lead melodies, and a long breakdown in which the beat drops away and the main melody is introduced by piano, strings, or plucked synths before building back into a climax. Tracks often follow a long-form structure suited to DJ mixing, with intros and outros of several minutes.

History

The sound emerged in the late 1990s and early 2000s from producers seeking a return to the melodic sensibilities of classic trance. Labels such as Anjunabeats (founded by Above & Beyond), Armada Music, Enhanced Music, and Future Sound of Egypt (founded by Aly & Fila) became central outlets. Artists strongly associated with the genre include Armin van Buuren, Above & Beyond, Aly & Fila, Sean Tyas, Daniel Kandi, Andy Blueman, and Aly & Fila, among many others. The annual radio show and compilations under the "A State of Trance" banner helped define and popularize the style.

Related styles

Uplifting trance overlaps with orchestral trance, which incorporates classical instrumentation, and with vocal trance, in which a sung vocal line carries the melody. Its faster, more energetic offshoot is sometimes called "138 trance", while tech-influenced variants blend in techno elements.
