Robinhood Markets, Inc. is an American financial services company headquartered in Menlo Park, California. It operates a popular mobile app offering commission-free trading of stocks, exchange-traded funds, options, and cryptocurrencies, and is credited with popularising app-based investing among younger retail investors.

History

Founded in 2013 by Vladimir Tenev and Baiju Bhatt, Robinhood launched its app in 2015. Its zero-commission model pressured established brokerages to drop their own trading fees. The company grew rapidly during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Controversies

Robinhood drew intense scrutiny during the January 2021 GameStop short squeeze, when it restricted purchases of heavily traded stocks amid clearing-house collateral demands. It has also faced regulatory fines over outages, options-trading practices, and its payment-for-order-flow revenue model. The company went public in July 2021.
