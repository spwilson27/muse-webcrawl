VRIO

VRIO is a business analysis framework that forms part of a firm's strategic planning. It asks four questions about a resource or capability to determine its competitive potential: Is it Valuable? Is it Rare? Is it costly to Imitate? And is the firm Organized to capture its value?

Framework

The framework builds on the resource-based view of the firm associated with Jay Barney, whose earlier VRIN criteria it refined. A resource that is valuable but not rare yields competitive parity; one that is valuable and rare yields a temporary competitive advantage; one that is valuable, rare, and costly to imitate yields a sustained advantage only if the firm is organized to exploit it.

Application

Managers use VRIO analysis to audit a firm's tangible and intangible resources—brands, patents, human capital, processes, and culture—and to focus investment on the capabilities most likely to sustain superior performance.
