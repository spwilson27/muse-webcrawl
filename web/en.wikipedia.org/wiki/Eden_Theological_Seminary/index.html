Eden Theological Seminary is a seminary of the United Church of Christ located in Webster Groves, Missouri, a suburb of St. Louis. Founded in 1850 by German Evangelical pastors, it is one of the oldest seminaries in the American Midwest and is known for a progressive theological outlook.

History

The seminary began in 1850 in Marthasville, Missouri, to train pastors for German immigrant congregations of the German Evangelical Church Society of the West (Kirchenverein). Instruction was originally in German. The school moved to St. Louis in 1883 and adopted the name Eden, after the Garden of Eden, when it relocated to its present Webster Groves campus in 1925. Through the mergers that produced the Evangelical and Reformed Church and then the United Church of Christ in 1957, Eden became a seminary of the UCC.

Academics

Eden offers the Master of Divinity and other graduate degrees and certificates, preparing students for ordained ministry, chaplaincy and community leadership. Famous alumni include the theologians Reinhold Niebuhr and H. Richard Niebuhr, who both studied and taught in the Evangelical tradition Eden served; Reinhold Niebuhr was ordained in the Evangelical Synod in 1915.

Campus

The Webster Groves campus includes Press Hall and the Luhr Library, which holds significant collections on Evangelical and Reformed history and on the Niebuhrs. Eden shares its neighbourhood with Webster University.
