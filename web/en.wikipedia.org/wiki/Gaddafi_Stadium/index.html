Gaddafi Stadium is a cricket stadium in Lahore, Pakistan, and the headquarters of the Pakistan Cricket Board. It was designed by the architect Nasredeen Murat-Khan, built in 1959, and originally known as Lahore Stadium before being renamed in 1974 in honour of the Libyan leader Muammar Gaddafi.

History

The ground hosted matches of the 1987 and 1996 Cricket World Cups, including the 1996 final. It was comprehensively renovated under the direction of the PCB in the 1990s. The stadium is the home ground of the Lahore Qalandars franchise in the Pakistan Super League.

Return of international cricket

After international cricket largely moved away from Pakistan following the 2009 attack on the Sri Lankan team in Lahore, Gaddafi Stadium hosted the 2015 Zimbabwe tour matches, the 2017 PSL final, and subsequent international fixtures as cricket returned to the country.
