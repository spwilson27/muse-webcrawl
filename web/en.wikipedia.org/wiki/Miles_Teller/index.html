Miles Alexander Teller (born 20 February 1987) is an American actor. He made his feature film debut in Rabbit Hole (2010) and gained critical acclaim for The Spectacular Now (2013) and Damien Chazelle's Whiplash (2014), in which he played an obsessive young jazz drummer. He went on to star in the Divergent series and achieved blockbuster success as Lt. Bradley "Rooster" Bradshaw in Top Gun: Maverick (2022).

Early life

Teller was born in Downingtown, Pennsylvania, and grew up in Florida, where he played drums, piano and saxophone in school and church groups — skills that later informed his role in Whiplash. In 2007 he survived a serious car accident that left facial scars. He graduated from New York University's Tisch School of the Arts in 2009.

Career

After Rabbit Hole, Teller appeared in Footloose (2011) and Project X (2012). His Sundance double success in 2013 — The Spectacular Now, for which he shared a special jury prize with Shailene Woodley, and Whiplash the following year — established him as a leading actor of his generation. He played Peter Hayes in the Divergent films (2014–2016), Reed Richards in Fantastic Four (2015), boxer Vinny Paz in Bleed for This (2016) and David Packouz in War Dogs (2016). In 2022 he co-starred in the miniseries The Offer and in Top Gun: Maverick, the highest-grossing film of his career.
