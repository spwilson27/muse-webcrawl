Keshō-mawashi

A keshō-mawashi is the ornate, heavily embroidered apron-like garment worn by top-division sumo wrestlers during the dohyō-iri ring-entering ceremony. Richly decorated, often with images or the names of sponsors and fan clubs, it is worn over the wrestler's mawashi.

Description

Keshō-mawashi are made of silk and embroidered, sometimes with gold thread; they can be extremely costly and are typically gifts from supporters or sponsors. During the ceremony, sekitori wrestlers wear them in a coordinated set provided to the stable or individually.

Use

They are worn only for the ring-entering ceremony, not for bouts, during which wrestlers wear the plain sagari and competition mawashi.
