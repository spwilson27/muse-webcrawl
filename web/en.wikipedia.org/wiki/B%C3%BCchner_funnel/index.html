Büchner funnel

A Büchner funnel is a piece of laboratory equipment used in vacuum filtration. It is a cylindrical funnel with a perforated plate on which filter paper is laid, and is fitted through a rubber stopper into a Büchner flask connected to a vacuum source.

Use

Solid–liquid mixtures are poured onto the wetted filter paper; the vacuum draws the filtrate through quickly, leaving the solid (often a precipitate or crystals) on the paper, where it can be washed and partly dried by the airflow. The technique is named after the German chemist Ernst Büchner.

Materials

Funnels are most commonly porcelain, though glass and plastic versions exist, and they are a standard item in organic chemistry teaching and research laboratories.
