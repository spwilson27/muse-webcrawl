Platinum jubilee

Street decorations for the Platinum Jubilee of Elizabeth II, 2022

A platinum jubilee is a celebration held to mark a 70th anniversary, most commonly the 70th year of a monarch's reign. In the hierarchy of jubilee anniversaries it follows the silver (25th), ruby (40th), golden (50th) and diamond (60th) jubilees; in some traditions a 75th anniversary is also called a platinum jubilee.

Notable platinum jubilees

The most widely celebrated example was the Platinum Jubilee of Elizabeth II in 2022, marking 70 years since her accession in 1952. She was the first British monarch to reach the milestone, and the occasion was marked by a four-day bank holiday weekend in the United Kingdom in June 2022, with street parties, a Trooping the Colour parade, a thanksgiving service at St Paul's Cathedral, and a pageant in London. Other long-reigning monarchs to pass 70 years include Louis XIV of France and Bhumibol Adulyadej of Thailand, though formal platinum jubilee celebrations in the modern style are largely a British and Commonwealth practice.

Other uses

The term is also used for 70th anniversaries of institutions, marriages and other events, platinum being associated with rarity, strength and endurance.
