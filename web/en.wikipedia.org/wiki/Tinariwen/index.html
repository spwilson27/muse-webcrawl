Tinariwen

Tinariwen (Tamasheq for "deserts") is a group of Tuareg musicians from the Sahara region of northern Mali, formed around 1979 by Ibrahim Ag Alhabib and fellow exiles in Algeria and Libya. Their music blends traditional Tuareg and West African styles with electric guitar-driven rock, often called "desert blues".

History

Members were involved in the Tuareg rebellion of the early 1990s; their songs of exile, political struggle and desert life circulated on cassettes. International recognition came with albums such as The Radio Tisdas Sessions (2001), Amassakoul (2004) and Tassili (2011), the latter winning the Grammy Award for Best World Music Album.

Style

Clapping rhythms, call-and-response vocals in Tamasheq, and interlocking guitars characterise their sound. Tinariwen have toured worldwide and influenced many desert-blues acts, though instability in northern Mali has repeatedly shaped the group's circumstances.
