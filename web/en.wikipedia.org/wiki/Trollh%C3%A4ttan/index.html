Trollhättan

Trollhättan is a city in Västra Götaland County, Sweden, situated on the Göta älv river at the Trollhättan Falls. It is the seat of Trollhättan Municipality and has a population of roughly 50,000 in the urban area. The city developed around the falls, whose power drove early industry, and around the locks of the Trollhätte Canal, which let shipping bypass the falls between Gothenburg and Lake Vänern.

Industry

Trollhättan was the home of Saab Automobile, whose car factory operated in the city from the late 1940s until the company's bankruptcy in 2011; the site later hosted National Electric Vehicle Sweden (NEVS). The aerospace and engineering firm GKN Aerospace (formerly Volvo Aero) also has a major presence. The city is home to University West (Högskolan Väst).

Film

Trollhättan hosts Film i Väst, one of Scandinavia's largest film production centres, nicknamed "Trollywood"; a large share of Swedish feature films are shot in or around the city.
