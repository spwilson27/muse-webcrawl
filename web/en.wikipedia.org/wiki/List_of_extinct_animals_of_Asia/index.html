Fish

AnimalRangeExtinction
Chinese paddlefishYangtze River, ChinaDeclared extinct 2022

Causes of extinction in Asia include habitat loss, hunting, river damming and introduced species. The baiji was the first cetacean driven to extinction by human activity, while the Chinese paddlefish, one of the world's largest freshwater fish, was lost to overfishing and habitat fragmentation.
