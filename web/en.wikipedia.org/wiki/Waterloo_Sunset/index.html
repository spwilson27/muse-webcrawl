"Waterloo Sunset" is a song by English rock band the Kinks. It was released as a single in 1967 and featured on their album Something Else by the Kinks. Written and produced by Ray Davies, the song reached number 2 on the British charts in mid-1967 and became one of the band's best-known and most acclaimed recordings.

Lyrics and meaning

The lyrics describe a solitary narrator watching the sun set over the River Thames at Waterloo in London, observing two lovers, Terry and Julie, crossing over the river on a Friday night. Davies has said the song was inspired by watching the city from a hospital window as a child and by the view from Waterloo Bridge, near where the band's early gigs took place.

Reception and legacy

"Waterloo Sunset" is frequently cited among the greatest songs of all time; it appeared in Rolling Stone's list of the 500 greatest songs. It has been covered by numerous artists, including Cathy Dennis, who had a UK hit with it in 1997, and Def Leppard. The song was performed by Ray Davies at the closing ceremony of the 2012 London Olympics.
