Born: October 31, 1926, Leeds, England
Died: October 29, 2011 (aged 84), Leeds
Occupation: DJ, television and radio presenter

Jimmy Savile

Sir James Wilson Vincent Savile (31 October 1926 – 29 October 2011) was an English disc jockey and television and radio personality who hosted BBC shows including Top of the Pops and Jim'll Fix It. After his death, he was revealed to have been one of Britain's most prolific sexual offenders.

Career

Savile worked as a dance-hall manager and pop DJ before joining Radio Luxembourg and then BBC Radio 1 in 1968. He presented the first edition of Top of the Pops in 1964 and fronted the children's wish-fulfilment show Jim'll Fix It from 1975 to 1994. He was famous for his charity fundraising, claiming to have raised over £40 million, and received unrivalled access to hospitals including Stoke Mandeville, Broadmoor, and Leeds General Infirmary. He was knighted in 1990.

Sexual abuse scandal

In October 2012, an ITV documentary broadcast allegations that Savile had sexually abused underage girls. The ensuing police investigation, Operation Yewtree, concluded that Savile had committed offences against hundreds of victims — the youngest aged five — over six decades, including on BBC premises and in hospitals. Inquiries severely criticized the institutions that had given him access and honours, and his knighthood, though it lapsed at his death, became a symbol of institutional failure.
