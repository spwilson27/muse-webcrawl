The Haunted Manor

The Haunted Manor (Polish: Straszny dwór) is an opera in four acts by the Polish composer Stanisław Moniuszko, set to a libretto by Jan Chęciński. Composed between 1861 and 1864, it premiered at the Teatr Wielki in Warsaw on 28 September 1865 and is regarded as one of the finest Polish operas.

Acts4

Synopsis

Two brothers, Stefan and Zbigniew, return from war and swear to remain bachelors so they can always answer the call of duty. Visiting the manor of the Miecznik (Sword-bearer), they fall in love with his daughters Hanna and Jadwiga. The manor is rumoured to be haunted, but the supposed ghosts are a trick; the brothers overcome their vow and the couples are united.

Music

The score blends Polish dance rhythms such as the mazurka and polonaise with virtuoso arias, including Stefan's famous aria with the chiming clock melody. Despite its comic tone, the opera carries patriotic undertones that resonated under the partitions of Poland; performances were shut down by the Russian authorities after only a few shows.
