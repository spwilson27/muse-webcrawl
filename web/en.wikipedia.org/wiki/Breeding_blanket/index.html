Breeding blanket

A breeding blanket is a key component proposed for fusion power reactors, surrounding the plasma-facing first wall. Its purpose is to breed tritium fuel from lithium using neutrons produced by the fusion reaction, while also absorbing neutron energy to be converted into electricity and shielding other reactor components.

Function

In a deuterium–tritium reactor, tritium is scarce and must be produced in situ. Neutrons striking lithium-6 in the blanket generate tritium and helium. Candidate blanket designs include liquid lithium-lead eutectic and solid ceramic breeders such as lithium orthosilicate, often with beryllium as a neutron multiplier.

Development

Test blanket modules are a major element of the ITER programme, where several competing blanket concepts are being evaluated for the future DEMO reactor.
