Marylebone station

Marylebone station is a Central London railway terminus and connected London Underground station in the Marylebone area of the City of Westminster. It is the southern terminus of the Chiltern Main Line to Birmingham and is served by Chiltern Railways.

History

The station opened on 15 March 1899 as the London terminus of the Great Central Railway's London Extension, the last main line railway to be built into London until High Speed 1. It was designed by Henry Wilson Braddock.

Underground station

The Underground station, served by the Bakerloo line, opened on 27 March 1907 as Great Central, and was renamed Marylebone in 1917.
