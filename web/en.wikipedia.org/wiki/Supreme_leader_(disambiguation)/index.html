Supreme leader (disambiguation)

"Supreme leader" is a title used by several heads of state and fictional rulers.

Supreme leader may refer to:

Political offices

  • Supreme Leader of Iran — the highest political and religious authority of the Islamic Republic of Iran, a position held by Ruhollah Khomeini (1979–1989) and Ali Khamenei (from 1989).
  • Supreme Leader of North Korea — an informal title for the paramount leader of the Democratic People's Republic of Korea, held successively by Kim Il-sung, Kim Jong-il, and Kim Jong-il's son Kim Jong-un.
  • Paramount leader of China — sometimes translated as supreme leader, the informal designation for the most powerful political figure in the People's Republic of China.
  • Supreme Leader of the Islamic Emirate of Afghanistan — the title of the Taliban's head of state.

Fiction

  • Supreme Leader Snoke — a fictional character in the Star Wars sequel trilogy, leader of the First Order, succeeded by Kylo Ren as Supreme Leader.
