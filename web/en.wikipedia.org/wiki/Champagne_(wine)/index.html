Champagne (wine)

Champagne is a sparkling wine produced in the Champagne region of France under appellation rules requiring secondary fermentation in the bottle (méthode champenoise), specific grapes – chiefly Pinot Noir, Pinot Meunier and Chardonnay – and minimum ageing.

Production

Still base wine is blended, bottled with yeast and sugar for a second fermentation creating the bubbles, aged on lees, riddled and disgorged, then topped with dosage. Styles range from ultra brut to doux, plus blanc de blancs, blanc de noirs and rosé.

Protection

The name Champagne is legally protected in most countries; other sparkling wines must use other terms.
