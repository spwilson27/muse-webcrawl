Jalen Hurts

Jalen Alexander Hurts (born August 7, 1998) is an American professional football quarterback for the Philadelphia Eagles of the National Football League (NFL). He played college football for the Alabama Crimson Tide and the Oklahoma Sooners.

College career

Hurts became Alabama's starting quarterback as a true freshman in 2016, leading the Crimson Tide to the national championship game. In the 2018 title game against Georgia he was benched at halftime for Tua Tagovailoa, who led a comeback win — but Hurts famously returned in the 2018 SEC Championship to rally Alabama past Georgia after Tagovailoa was injured. He transferred to Oklahoma for 2019, finishing as runner-up for the Heisman Trophy behind Joe Burrow.

NFL career

The Eagles drafted Hurts in the second round (53rd overall) of the 2020 NFL Draft; he became their starter late in his rookie season. In 2022 he led Philadelphia to Super Bowl LVII, finishing second in MVP voting, and signed a five-year, $255 million extension in 2023. Known for the Eagles' short-yardage "tush push" play and his exceptional strength — he could squat over 600 pounds — Hurts led the Eagles to victory in Super Bowl LIX in February 2025, where he was named Super Bowl MVP after a win over the Kansas City Chiefs.
