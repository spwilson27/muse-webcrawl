Dendrite (disambiguation)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

A dendrite (from Greek dendron, "tree") is a branched, tree-like structure. Dendrite may refer to:

Science

  • Dendrite, a branched projection of a neuron (nerve cell) that receives signals from other neurons
  • Dendrite (crystal), a crystal that develops with a typical multi-branching tree-like form, as in snowflakes and frost patterns
  • Dendrite (metal), a characteristic tree-like structure of crystals that grows during the solidification of metals and alloys
  • Dendritic drainage, a tree-like pattern of river channels in drainage basins
  • Dendritic cell, an antigen-presenting cell of the mammalian immune system
  • Dendritic spine, a small protrusion from a neuron's dendrite that receives synaptic input

Other uses

  • Dendrite, a fictional substance; the dendrite in the novel The Chrysalids and other works
  • Dendrites, jewellery and decorative objects made from dendritic agate and similar stones showing tree-like inclusions
