Jean de Béthencourt

Jean de Béthencourt (1362–1425) was a French explorer who in 1402 led an expedition to the Canary Islands, landing first on Lanzarote. Undertaken with Gadifer de la Salle under the patronage of Henry III of Castile, the expedition began the European conquest and colonization of the archipelago.

Born1362, Grainville-la-Teinturière, Normandy
Died1425, Normandy
Known forConquest of the Canary Islands

Expedition

Béthencourt's forces subdued Lanzarote, Fuerteventura, and El Hierro, establishing settlements such as Betancuria. He received the title King of the Canary Islands as a vassal of Castile, though his authority was contested by Gadifer and later by his own nephew Maciot.

Legacy

The chronicle Le Canarien records the expedition. Place names in the Canaries, including Betancuria, preserve his memory.
