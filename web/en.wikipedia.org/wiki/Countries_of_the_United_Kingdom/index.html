Countries of the United Kingdom

The United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland is composed of four countries: England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. Each has its own distinct culture, legal traditions and sporting teams; Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland have devolved governments and legislatures, while England is governed directly by the UK Parliament.

Terminology

The four are variously described as countries, nations or constituent countries. England is by far the largest by population, containing around 84 per cent of the UK total. Scotland and Wales were unified with England by the Acts of Union of 1707 and 1535–1542 respectively; Ireland was partitioned in 1921.
