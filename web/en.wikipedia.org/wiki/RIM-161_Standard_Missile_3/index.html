RIM-161 Standard Missile 3

The RIM-161 Standard Missile 3, usually called SM-3, is a ship-based interceptor used in the Aegis ballistic missile defense system. Unlike many surface-to-air missiles, it is designed to destroy ballistic missiles outside the atmosphere during their midcourse phase.

Mechanism

SM-3 is launched from the Mk 41 vertical launching system. After booster and sustainer stages place it on an intercept trajectory, a lightweight exoatmospheric projectile separates and uses an infrared seeker and divert thrusters to collide directly with the target. The kill vehicle destroys the target by kinetic energy rather than by an explosive warhead.

Variants and use

Block IA, IB and IIA variants progressively improved the seeker, divert system and velocity. The missile has been deployed by United States and Japanese Aegis ships and in the Aegis Ashore configuration. It is distinct from the earlier RIM-24 Tartar generation of naval missiles and from SM-2 and SM-6, which are primarily atmospheric interceptors.
