Bohai Sea

Bohai Sea
Location: Northeast China
Area: ~77,000 km²
Max. depth: ~30 m
Inflow: Yellow River

The Bohai Sea is a marginal sea of the Pacific Ocean, the innermost gulf of the Yellow Sea on the coast of northeastern China. It is bounded by the Liaodong and Shandong peninsulas and comprises Liaodong Bay, Bohai Bay and Laizhou Bay.

Geography

The Yellow River discharges into the Bohai Sea, carrying large quantities of silt. Major ports include Tianjin, Dalian, Yantai and Qinhuangdao. The region around it, the Bohai Economic Rim, is one of China's major industrial zones.

Environment and resources

The shallow sea supports fisheries and offshore oil fields, but faces pollution from industrial runoff and periodic oil spills, including the 2011 Penglai spill. See also Bohai Bay.
