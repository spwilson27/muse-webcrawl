Weasley family

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Burrow, home of the Weasley family.

The Weasley family is a fictional family in J. K. Rowling's Harry Potter series. The family consists of Arthur and Molly Weasley and their seven children: Bill, Charlie, Percy, Fred, George, Ron, and Ginny. They live in a ramshackle, magically expanded house called the Burrow in Devon, near the fictional village of Ottery St Catchpole. Ron Weasley is one of Harry Potter's two best friends, and the family effectively adopts Harry, who has no real home of his own.

Members

Arthur Weasley, the patriarch, works in the Ministry of Magic's Misuse of Muggle Artefacts Office and is fascinated by Muggle technology. Molly Weasley, the matriarch, is a formidable homemaker and a member of the Order of the Phoenix; she kills Bellatrix Lestrange in the Battle of Hogwarts.

The children include Bill, a curse-breaker for Gringotts; Charlie, a dragon researcher in Romania; Percy, an ambitious Ministry official; twins Fred and George, founders of Weasleys' Wizard Wheezes, a joke shop in Diagon Alley; Ron, Harry's best friend and later husband of Hermione Granger; and Ginny, the youngest and only daughter, who becomes Harry's wife.

Significance

The Weasleys are the series' most prominent "pure-blood" family who reject blood purity ideology, welcoming Muggle-borns and outsiders. Their poverty contrasts with the wealthy, prejudiced Malfoy family and with the Dursleys' neglect of Harry. The family embodies the series' values of loyalty, warmth, and generosity, and serves as the emotional anchor of the series' later books.
