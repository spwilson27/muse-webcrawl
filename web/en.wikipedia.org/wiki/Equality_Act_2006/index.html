Equality Act 2006

Citation2006 c. 3
JurisdictionUnited Kingdom
Royal assent16 February 2006

The Equality Act 2006 (c. 3) is an Act of the Parliament of the United Kingdom covering the United Kingdom. The 2006 Act is a precursor to the Equality Act 2010, which combines all of the equality enactments within Great Britain and provides comparable protections across all equality strands.

Provisions

The Act established the Equality and Human Rights Commission (EHRC), which took over the functions of the Commission for Racial Equality, the Equal Opportunities Commission, and the Disability Rights Commission. It also made discrimination on the grounds of religion or belief in the provision of goods and services unlawful, prohibited discrimination on the grounds of sexual orientation in similar terms, and introduced a gender equality duty for public bodies.
