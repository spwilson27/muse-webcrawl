Amsterdam

[Image: Canal houses along a canal in Amsterdam]
Canal houses along a canal in Amsterdam

Amsterdam is the capital and most populous city of the Netherlands, with about 900,000 residents in the city and 2.5 million in the metropolitan area. It lies in the province of North Holland at the mouth of the Amstel River, largely below sea level, protected by dikes and pumps.

Amsterdam grew from a 13th-century fishing village into the world's leading trade centre during the Dutch Golden Age of the 17th century, when the Dutch East India Company made it arguably the richest city on Earth. Its concentric canal belt (the Grachtengordel) is a UNESCO World Heritage site. The city is home to the Rijksmuseum and the Van Gogh Museum, the Anne Frank House, a famous cycling culture, and liberal social policies. The port and Schiphol Airport are European hubs.
