Kalshi

Kalshi Inc. is an American financial exchange and prediction market platform, founded in 2018 by Tarek Mansour and Luana Lopes Lara, that offers trading in "event contracts" — yes/no derivatives whose payoff depends on the outcome of specific events, from Federal Reserve interest-rate decisions and inflation statistics to weather, elections and popular culture.

Regulation

KalshiEX LLC is regulated by the US Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) as a designated contract market, a status it received in November 2020, allowing it to list event contracts legally across the United States. Its contracts trade between $0.01 and $0.99, with prices interpretable as market-implied probabilities of the event occurring.

Election markets litigation

Kalshi's application to offer contracts on US congressional elections was initially blocked by the CFTC, but in September 2024 a federal judge ruled in Kalshi's favour, and the platform subsequently listed markets on the 2024 US presidential election, handling billions of dollars in volume. The case became a landmark in the legal recognition of election prediction markets in the United States, and the company later expanded into sports event contracts, provoking further disputes with state gaming regulators.
