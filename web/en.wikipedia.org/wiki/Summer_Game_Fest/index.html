Summer Game Fest is an annual live video game event organised and hosted by game journalist Geoff Keighley. The event takes the form of a season of digital announcements, anchored by a major live showcase stream featuring new game reveals, trailers and updates, followed by partner presentations from publishers and platforms. It was established in 2020 after the cancellation of the E3 expo and the Gamescom trade fair as physical events during the COVID-19 pandemic, and subsequently positioned itself as E3's successor following that expo's permanent discontinuation in 2023.

History

Keighley, who had previously produced E3's Coliseum programming and hosts The Game Awards, launched Summer Game Fest as a distributed, digital-first "festival" running across the summer of 2020, incorporating demo festivals on Xbox and Steam and streams from major publishers. From 2022, the event was consolidated around a flagship June kickoff show. In 2023, Summer Game Fest held its first in-person media component, "Play Days", in Los Angeles, and the main showcase moved to the YouTube Theater in Inglewood, California, with a live audience.

Format

The centrepiece is a roughly two-hour cross-industry showcase broadcast live on YouTube, Twitch and other platforms, featuring world premieres and guest appearances by developers and celebrities. Around it sit partner events such as the Day of the Devs indie showcase, publisher-specific streams (Xbox Games Showcase, Ubisoft Forward, Capcom Showcase) and platform demo festivals that let players try upcoming games. The event is frequently noted for high-profile premieres of major titles and for its role in the mid-year announcement calendar of the games industry.
