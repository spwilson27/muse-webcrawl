ISO pallet

An ISO pallet is a pallet that meets the sizes specified by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) for use in global supply chains. ISO originally approved six pallet sizes in ISO Standard 6780, chosen because they relate to the internal dimensions of ISO shipping containers and to common door widths and packaging modules.

Standard sizes

The six dimensions in ISO 6780 are: 1219 × 1016 mm (48 × 40 in), common in North America; 1000 × 1200 mm (39.37 × 47.24 in), common in Europe and Asia; 1165 × 1165 mm (45.9 × 45.9 in), used in Australia; 1067 × 1067 mm (42 × 42 in); 1100 × 1100 mm (43.3 × 43.3 in), common in Asia; and 800 × 1200 mm (31.5 × 47.24 in), the EUR-pallet size used throughout Europe.

Use

The 48 × 40 inch pallet accounts for the largest share of pallets in North America, while the EUR-pallet (EUR/EPAL) dominates European logistics. Standardisation allows forklifts, racking, trucks and intermodal containers to be designed around predictable footprints, simplifying handling and exchange pools.
