Weekly Shōnen Jump (週刊少年ジャンプ, Shūkan Shōnen Janpu) is a weekly shōnen manga anthology published in Japan by Shueisha since 1968. It is the best-selling manga magazine in history and the launchpad of many of the world's most successful comics franchises.

System and history

Series are serialised weekly and governed by reader survey postcards: the editorial department ranks the chapters and unpopular series are cancelled quickly ("axed"), while hits run for years and are collected in tankōbon volumes. Editors famously pair closely with artists under the principles "friendship, effort, victory". Circulation peaked at 6.53 million copies with a 1995 New Year issue, a Guinness-recognised record, before settling around 1–2 million print copies as reading shifted to the digital Shōnen Jump+ app (2014) and simultaneous English releases on the Shonen Jump app and Manga Plus.

Famous series

Landmark titles include Dragon Ball, Slam Dunk, Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece (the best-selling manga of all time, running since 1997), Naruto, Bleach, Death Note, My Hero Academia, Demon Slayer and Jujutsu Kaisen. The magazine's legacy also includes the Tezuka and Akatsuka newcomer awards and Jump Festa conventions.
