Gorkhaland Territorial Administration

Type: Autonomous administrative body
Established: 2012 (Act passed 2011)
Headquarters: Lal Kothi, Darjeeling
Region: Darjeeling and Kalimpong districts, West Bengal
Predecessor: Darjeeling Gorkha Hill Council

The Gorkhaland Territorial Administration (GTA) is a semi-autonomous council for the Darjeeling and Kalimpong districts of West Bengal in India. The GTA replaced the Darjeeling Gorkha Hill Council, which was formed in 1988. The GTA presently consists of three hill subdivisions — Darjeeling, Kurseong, and Mirik — together with Kalimpong district and some areas of Siliguri subdivision.

Formation

The GTA was formed through a tripartite agreement signed on 18 July 2011 at Pintail Village near Siliguri between the Government of India, the Government of West Bengal, and the Gorkha Janmukti Morcha (GJM), following years of agitation for a separate state of Gorkhaland. The Gorkhaland Territorial Administration Act was passed by the West Bengal Legislative Assembly in 2011 and elections to the body were held in July 2012.

Structure and powers

The GTA Sabha (assembly) has 45 elected members and five members nominated by the state government. Its executive body is headed by a Chief Executive. The council administers a wide range of subjects transferred from the state, including agriculture, horticulture, tourism, education (primary through college), public health, water supply, and development of the tea gardens region, though land, law and order, and certain financial powers remain with the state.

Developments

The GJM swept the first GTA elections in 2012 and Bimal Gurung became Chief Executive. Relations with the state government frequently soured over the council's powers and finances. In 2017, during the 104-day Gorkhaland agitation, Gurung resigned and went underground; the state government appointed administrators and, later, elections were held in 2022 after a decade, with the Bharatiya Gorkha Prajatantrik Morcha winning control of the GTA.
