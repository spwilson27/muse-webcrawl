Rowan Atkinson

Born: 6 January 1955, Consett, County Durham, England
Occupation: Actor, comedian, writer

Rowan Sebastian Atkinson (born 6 January 1955) is an English actor, comedian and writer. He is best known for the sitcoms Blackadder (1983–1989) and Mr. Bean (1990–1995), and for the Johnny English film series — characters that made him one of Britain's most internationally recognised comedians.

Career

Atkinson studied electrical engineering at Newcastle and Oxford, where he began performing sketches and met writer Richard Curtis and composer Howard Goodall. He came to prominence in the sketch show Not the Nine O'Clock News (1979–1982). Blackadder, co-written with Curtis and Ben Elton, is routinely ranked among the greatest British sitcoms. Mr. Bean, a nearly wordless physical comedy, was sold worldwide; the films Bean (1997) and Mr. Bean's Holiday (2007) were major hits, and Atkinson voiced the character in an animated series. His other work includes The Thin Blue Line, Four Weddings and a Funeral, The Lion King (voice of Zazu), Love Actually, Maigret, Man vs. Bee and a stage Oliver!.

Personal life

Atkinson is a noted car enthusiast and collector who has written for car magazines and raced competitively. He was appointed CBE in 2013.
