Built: 1967–1970
In commission: 1970–1992
Completed: 3

The Churchill class were a class of three fleet (nuclear-powered attack) submarines of the British Royal Navy. They were a development of the preceding Valiant class, incorporating greater internal volume, improved habitability, and enhanced equipment. All three were built by Vickers in Barrow-in-Furness.

Boats

  • HMS Churchill (S46) — commissioned 15 July 1970; named for Winston Churchill.
  • HMS Conqueror (S48) — commissioned 9 November 1971.
  • HMS Courageous (S50) — commissioned 16 October 1971.

Design

The boats were powered by a single Rolls-Royce pressurised water reactor driving geared steam turbines, giving a submerged speed of around 28 knots. They were armed with six bow torpedo tubes for Tigerfish and Spearfish torpedoes and could also fire Sub-Harpoon anti-ship missiles and lay mines. The class carried a complement of about 103 officers and men.

Service

HMS Conqueror achieved lasting fame during the Falklands War in 1982, when on 2 May she torpedoed and sank the Argentine cruiser ARA General Belgrano — the only nuclear-powered submarine ever to sink an enemy warship in combat, and the first such sinking since the Second World War. All three boats were decommissioned in the early 1990s (1990–1992) and await disposal at Rosyth and Devonport.
