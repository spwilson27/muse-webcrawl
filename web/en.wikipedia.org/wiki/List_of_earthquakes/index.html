Deadliest earthquakes

The deadliest earthquake in recorded history was the 1556 Shaanxi earthquake in China, which killed an estimated 830,000 people. Other catastrophic events include the 1976 Tangshan earthquake, the 1920 Haiyuan earthquake, the 2004 Indian Ocean earthquake and tsunami (about 228,000 deaths), and the 2010 Haiti earthquake.
