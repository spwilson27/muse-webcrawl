The European System of Central Banks (ESCB) comprises the European Central Bank (ECB) and the national central banks of all 27 member states of the European Union, including those that have not adopted the euro. It was established by the Treaty of Maastricht and began operating in 1998.

Objectives and tasks

The primary objective of the ESCB is to maintain price stability. Its basic tasks are to define and implement the monetary policy of the euro area (carried out by the Eurosystem), conduct foreign-exchange operations, hold and manage official foreign reserves, and promote the smooth operation of payment systems.

Eurosystem

The Eurosystem is the narrower body consisting of the ECB and the national central banks of euro-area countries, which actually conducts the single monetary policy.
