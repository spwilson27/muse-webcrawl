MirrorMask is a 2005 fantasy film directed by Dave McKean and written by Neil Gaiman and McKean, based on a story they developed together. It was produced by the Jim Henson Company. The film stars Stephanie Leonidas as Helena, a fifteen-year-old circus performer who longs for a normal life.

Plot

After an argument with her mother, who then falls ill, Helena is drawn into a surreal dream world divided between a City of Light and a Dark Lands, populated by masked inhabitants. There she learns that a princess from the dark side — her own double — has taken her place in the real world, and that she must find the MirrorMask, a powerful charm, to restore balance and wake the White Queen.

Reception

The film premiered at the Sundance Film Festival and received generally favourable reviews for its distinctive visual style, which combines live action with extensive digital imagery based on McKean's artwork, though its narrative drew comparisons to Labyrinth and Gaiman's other works.
