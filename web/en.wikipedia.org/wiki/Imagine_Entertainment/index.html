Imagine Entertainment

Imagine Entertainment
Founded: 1986
Founders: Brian Grazer, Ron Howard
Headquarters: Beverly Hills, California
Industry: Film and television production

Imagine Entertainment, formerly Imagine Films Entertainment, is an American film and television production company founded in 1986 by producer Brian Grazer and director Ron Howard. The company grew out of the pair's collaboration on films such as Night Shift (1982) and Splash (1984).

Productions

Imagine's films include Parenthood (1989), Apollo 13 (1995), A Beautiful Mind (2001), which won the Academy Award for Best Picture, The Da Vinci Code (2006) and Frost/Nixon (2008). Its television division produced series including Arrested Development, 24, Friday Night Lights and Empire.

Divisions

The company has expanded into documentaries, kids and family programming, and international content through divisions and partnerships.
