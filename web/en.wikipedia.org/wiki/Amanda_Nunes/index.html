Amanda Nunes

Amanda Lourenço Nunes (born 30 May 1988) is a Brazilian former professional mixed martial artist. She competed in the Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC), where she is a former two-time UFC Women's Bantamweight Champion and a former UFC Women's Featherweight Champion — the first woman to hold UFC titles in two divisions simultaneously and the first openly lesbian UFC champion. Widely regarded as the greatest female mixed martial artist of all time, she retired in June 2023 after defending her bantamweight title against Irene Aldana at UFC 289.

Career

Nunes grew up in Bahia, training in karate, boxing, and Brazilian jiu-jitsu, in which she holds a black belt. She fought in Strikeforce and Invicta FC before joining the UFC in 2013. In July 2016 she submitted Miesha Tate at UFC 200 to win the bantamweight championship, then famously knocked out Ronda Rousey in 48 seconds at UFC 207. In December 2018 she knocked out Cris Cyborg in 51 seconds to win the featherweight title, becoming a simultaneous two-division champion. Defences included wins over Holly Holm, Germaine de Randamie, and Megan Anderson.

Upset and final fights

In December 2021 Nunes lost the bantamweight title in a major upset, submitting to Julianna Peña at UFC 269; she regained it decisively in the rematch at UFC 277 in July 2022. After defeating Irene Aldana in June 2023 she announced her retirement, leaving both titles vacant.

Personal life

Nunes is married to former UFC fighter Nina Nunes (née Ansaroff); they have two children. In 2023 she was inducted into discussions of the sport's all-time greats and remains a prominent figure in Brazilian sport.
