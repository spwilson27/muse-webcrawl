742 Evergreen Terrace
Location: Springfield
Residents: the Simpson family
From the animated series The Simpsons.

742 Evergreen Terrace is the fictional home address of the Simpson family in the animated television series The Simpsons. The detached suburban house in Springfield is one of the programme’s most recognisable recurring locations. Its layout is familiar but deliberately inconsistent: rooms, corridors and even the size of the house change when a joke or plot requires it.

Description

The house is usually drawn as a two-storey suburban home with an attached garage, a front lawn and a back garden. Inside are a living room with the famous couch used in the opening sequence, a kitchen and dining area, and bedrooms for Homer and Marge and for Bart, Lisa and Maggie. A basement, attic, rumpus room and treehouse appear in various episodes. The Flanders family live next door at 744 Evergreen Terrace.

Continuity

The address is generally given as 742 Evergreen Terrace, though episodes and related media have occasionally used different numbers or street names for comic effect. The programme treats the house less as a fixed architectural plan than as a flexible stage. Stories have shown it damaged, rebuilt, expanded, sold, foreclosed upon and restored, usually returning to the familiar design by the next episode.

Cultural references

A real house in Henderson, Nevada was built as a replica and given away in a 1997 promotional contest; its exterior was later repainted to conform to local homeowner rules. The fictional address has become shorthand for the Simpsons’ domestic setting and is frequently referenced in merchandise, games and location-based episodes.
