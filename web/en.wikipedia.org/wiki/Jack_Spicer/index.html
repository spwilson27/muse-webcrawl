Jack Spicer

Jack Spicer (January 30, 1925 – August 17, 1965) was an American poet often identified with the San Francisco Renaissance and the Berkeley Renaissance.

Life and work

Born John Lester Spicer in Los Angeles, he studied at the University of California, Berkeley, where he met Robert Duncan and Robin Blaser. He refused to sign the California loyalty oath and lost his teaching assistantship. In San Francisco he co-founded the 6 Gallery, site of the famous 1955 Six Gallery reading.

Poetics

Spicer developed a theory of poetry as "dictation" — the poet as a radio receiving transmissions from the "Outside". His books include After Lorca (1957), Admonitions (1958), and the serial poem The Holy Grail. He died in San Francisco aged 40.
