The China National Space Administration (CNSA) is the civil space agency of the People's Republic of China, responsible for the national space program, space policy and international cooperation.

History

CNSA was established in 1993 when the former Ministry of Aerospace Industry was split into CNSA and the China Aerospace Corporation. Since then, China has achieved crewed spaceflight with the Shenzhou program (beginning with Yang Liwei in 2003), soft landings on the Moon with the Chang'e program — including the first far-side landing in 2019 — construction of the Tiangong space station, and the Tianwen-1 Mars mission.

Organization

Launches are conducted from four spaceports: Jiuquan, Taiyuan, Xichang and Wenchang. Much development work is carried out by state-owned enterprises under the China Aerospace Science and Technology Corporation.
