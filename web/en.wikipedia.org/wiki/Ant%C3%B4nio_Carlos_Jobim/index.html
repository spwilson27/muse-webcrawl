Antônio Carlos Brasileiro de Almeida Jobim (1927–1994), also known as Tom Jobim, was a Brazilian composer, pianist, guitarist and singer who was a central figure in the creation of the bossa nova style.

Career

Working with poet Vinicius de Moraes, Jobim composed the music for the play Orfeu da Conceição (1956). With Luiz Bonfá he scored the film Black Orpheus (1959). His songs "Garota de Ipanema" ("The Girl from Ipanema"), "Chega de Saudade", "Desafinado", "Wave" and "Corcovado" ("Quiet Nights of Quiet Stars") became international standards, popularised worldwide by the 1962 album Getz/Gilberto.

Legacy

Jobim is regarded as one of the most important songwriters of the 20th century; his harmonically sophisticated songs reshaped Brazilian music and influenced generations of jazz and pop musicians.
