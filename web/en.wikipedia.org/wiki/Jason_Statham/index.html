Jason Statham (born 26 July 1967) is an English actor and producer. He is known for portraying tough, gritty, and violent characters in action thriller films, frequently performing his own stunts. Statham has been credited with leading a resurgence of action films during the 2000s and 2010s, and his films have grossed over $8.4 billion worldwide.

Early life

Statham was born in Shirebrook, Derbyshire, and grew up in Great Yarmouth, Norfolk. He developed an interest in diving and was a member of Britain's National Swimming Squad for twelve years, competing at the 1990 Commonwealth Games. He was discovered by a modelling agency while training, and worked as a model for brands including French Connection and Tommy Hilfiger, while also selling goods on street markets.

Career

Director Guy Ritchie cast Statham in his breakthrough film Lock, Stock and Two Smoking Barrels (1998), drawing on his market-stall background. He reunited with Ritchie for Snatch (2000). His first major Hollywood leading role was Frank Martin in The Transporter (2002), which established his action-hero persona. He went on to star in The Italian Job (2003), Crank (2006), The Bank Job (2008), and The Mechanic (2011).

Statham joined The Expendables ensemble series from 2010 and the Fast & Furious franchise as Deckard Shaw, first appearing in Fast & Furious 6 (2013) and co-headlining the spin-off Hobbs & Shaw (2019). Other major roles include Spy (2015), The Meg (2018), and Wrath of Man (2021).

Personal life

Statham has been in a relationship with model Rosie Huntington-Whiteley since 2010; they have two children. He is known for his interest in martial arts, including kickboxing and Brazilian jiu-jitsu.
