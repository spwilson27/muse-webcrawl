Giacinto Morera (18 August 1856 – 8 February 1909) was an Italian engineer and mathematician. He is remembered for Morera's theorem in complex analysis and for his work in the theory of linear elasticity and rational mechanics.

Life and career

Morera was born in Novara. He studied engineering and mathematics at Turin, and pursued further studies at Pavia, Pisa and Leipzig. He obtained a degree in engineering and a doctorate in pure mathematics, and in 1886 he won a competition for the chair of rational mechanics at the University of Genoa, where he also served as dean of the faculty of science. In 1900 he moved to the University of Turin as professor of higher mechanics, and he remained there for the rest of his life, becoming dean of the faculty of science. He died in Turin in 1909.

Work

Morera's theorem, published in 1886, gives a criterion for a continuous function on an open set in the complex plane to be holomorphic: if the integral of the function around every closed piecewise-smooth curve within the domain vanishes, then the function is holomorphic. It is a standard converse to Cauchy's integral theorem and remains a fundamental tool in complex analysis courses.

In mathematical physics, Morera worked on the equilibrium of elastic bodies, the theory of harmonic functions, and hydrodynamics. In elasticity he is associated with the stress function approach for torsion; the Morera stress functions generalise the Prandtl stress function to multiply connected cross-sections. He also worked on thermodynamics and on the analytical mechanics of holonomic and non-holonomic systems.
