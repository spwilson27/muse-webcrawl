MoD Corsham

Location: Corsham, Wiltshire, England
Owner: Ministry of Defence
Opened: 2011 (as a unified site)

MoD Corsham is a major Ministry of Defence site at Corsham in Wiltshire, England. It is the United Kingdom's primary hub for defence communications and information systems, home to Defence Digital (formerly Information Systems and Services) and the headquarters of the Global Operations and Security Control Centre.

Site

The modern campus, redeveloped under a project completed around 2011, consolidated staff from several older sites onto a single location above the former Bath stone quarries. Around 2,000 military and civilian personnel work there on military satellite communications (including Skynet), computer networks, and operational IT support for the British Armed Forces.

Underground history

Corsham is famous for the vast underground complexes excavated in the worked-out stone quarries beneath and around the site. These include the Central Government War Headquarters at Burlington — a vast Cold War bunker intended to house the government in the event of nuclear war — as well as wartime shadow factories and ammunition depots. Parts of the underground estate remain in defence use or storage.
