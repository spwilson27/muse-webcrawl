Royal Academy Schools

The Royal Academy Schools form the postgraduate art school of the Royal Academy of Arts in London. Founded in 1768, they are the oldest art school in Britain.

History and course

The Schools were established when the Royal Academy was founded, with teaching in the life room at their heart. Today they offer a three-year postgraduate diploma in fine art — painting, sculpture, print, photography and moving image — with tuition free and a small, highly competitive intake. Students exhibit in the annual Premiums and final RA Schools Show.

Notable alumni include J. M. W. Turner and many leading British artists.
