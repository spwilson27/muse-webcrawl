The Jungle Book is a 2016 American adventure film directed by Jon Favreau, combining live action with photorealistic computer-generated animals. It is a remake of Walt Disney's 1967 animated classic, itself based on Rudyard Kipling's 1894 collection of stories.

Plot

Mowgli, a boy raised by wolves in the Indian jungle, is threatened by the scarred tiger Shere Khan, who hates men after being burned by fire. Guided by the panther Bagheera and befriended by the carefree bear Baloo, Mowgli journeys toward the man-village, encountering the python Kaa and the gigantopithecus King Louie along the way, before confronting Shere Khan with the "red flower" — fire — and choosing to remain in the jungle.

Production and reception

Filmed almost entirely against bluescreen in Los Angeles with newcomer Neel Sethi as the only major live-action performer, the film was acclaimed for its visual effects and grossed $966.6 million worldwide. It won the Academy Award for Best Visual Effects at the 89th Academy Awards.
