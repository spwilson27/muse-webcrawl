List of knots

The List of knots is an index of the named knots used in ropework, sailing, climbing, fishing, and decorative knotting. Knots are organised by type: bends join two ropes, hitches attach a rope to an object, loops form fixed or sliding eyes, and stoppers prevent a rope from slipping through an opening. Well over a thousand named knots, bends, hitches, and splices are recorded in references such as The Ashley Book of Knots (1944).

Terminology

Knot nomenclature follows the standing end, working end, and bight of the rope. Knots differ in strength, security, ease of untying after loading, and resistance to capsizing; the "king of knots", the bowline, is prized because it holds under load yet unties easily.
