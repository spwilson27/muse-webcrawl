Sara Ahmed

Sara Ahmed (born 1969) is a British-Australian scholar and writer whose work works at the intersection of feminist theory, queer theory, critical race theory, and postcolonial studies. She is known for her work on affect and emotion, orientation, and diversity work in institutions.

Career

Ahmed was professor of race and cultural studies at Goldsmiths, University of London, where she directed the Centre for Feminist Research. In 2016 she resigned in protest at the university's handling of sexual harassment allegations, an action that drew wide attention. She has since worked as an independent scholar and writer.

Works

Her books include Differences That Matter (1998), The Cultural Politics of Emotion (2004), Queer Phenomenology (2006), The Promise of Happiness (2010), On Being Included (2012), Willful Subjects (2014), and Living a Feminist Life (2017), which introduced her widely cited figure of the "feminist killjoy". Complaint! (2021) examines how complaints about harassment and inequality are handled by universities.
