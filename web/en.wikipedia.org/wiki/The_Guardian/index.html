The Guardian

The Guardian is a British daily newspaper, founded in Manchester in 1821 as the Manchester Guardian, renamed in 1959, and owned since 1936 by the Scott Trust, a structure designed to protect its editorial independence.

Digital era

The Guardian became one of the world's most-read English news websites, funded in part by voluntary reader contributions, with US and Australian editions, while its print edition continues from London.
