Dr. Henry Jonathan "Hank" Pym is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by Stan Lee, Larry Lieber and Jack Kirby, he first appeared in Tales to Astonish #27 (January 1962). Pym is a scientist who discovered the subatomic "Pym Particles" that allow size-changing, and became the first Ant-Man.

Publication history

Pym debuted as a scientist in a standalone story, then returned as Ant-Man in issue #35, becoming a founding member of the Avengers. He has adopted several identities including Giant-Man, Goliath and Yellowjacket.

Fictional biography

A biochemist, Pym developed particles that could shrink objects and later restore them. He married Janet van Dyne, who became the Wasp. His creation of the robot Ultron, which turned against humanity, haunted his career. In the Marvel Cinematic Universe he is portrayed by Michael Douglas.

Powers and abilities

Using Pym Particles stored in his suit, Pym can shrink to insect size while retaining human strength, or grow to giant size with proportionally increased strength and durability. He communicates with insects via a cybernetic helmet.
