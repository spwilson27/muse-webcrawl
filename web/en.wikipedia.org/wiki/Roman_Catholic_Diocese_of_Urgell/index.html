Roman Catholic Diocese of Urgell

The Diocese of Urgell (Catalan: Bisbat d'Urgell) is a Latin Church diocese of the Catholic Church in Catalonia, Spain, with its seat at La Seu d'Urgell and its cathedral dedicated to Saint Mary. It is a suffragan of the Archdiocese of Tarragona.

One of the oldest dioceses in the Iberian Peninsula, it is traditionally dated to the 5th–6th centuries. Its territory includes much of the Catalan Pyrenees and, uniquely, the entire Principality of Andorra.

Co-prince of Andorra

Since the paréages of 1278 and 1288, the Bishop of Urgell serves ex officio as Co-Prince of Andorra, jointly with the head of state of France (now the French President). The bishop is thus one of two heads of state of a sovereign country, a role formalised in Andorra's 1993 constitution.
