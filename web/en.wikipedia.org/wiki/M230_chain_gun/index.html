The M230 chain gun is a 30 mm, single-barrel electrically operated chain gun developed by Hughes Helicopters in the 1970s. It is best known as the chin-mounted armament of the AH-64 Apache attack helicopter.

Design

Unlike most automatic weapons, which use recoil or gas from the fired cartridge to cycle the action, the M230 is driven by an external electric motor connected to the bolt by a continuous roller chain. This "chain gun" principle makes the weapon largely indifferent to dud rounds: a misfired cartridge is simply extracted and ejected as the cycle continues.

The gun fires 30×113mm ammunition, principally the M789 high-explosive dual-purpose (HEDP) round, whose shaped-charge warhead can penetrate light armour while also producing fragmentation against personnel and materiel.

Use

On the Apache, the M230 is mounted in a turret beneath the forward fuselage and can be slaved to the pilot's or gunner's helmet-mounted sight. The helicopter carries up to 1,200 rounds in a flexible ammunition handling system. A lightweight version, the M230LF, has been offered for ground and naval mounts.
