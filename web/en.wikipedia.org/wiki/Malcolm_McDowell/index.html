Malcolm McDowell (born Malcolm John Taylor; 13 June 1943) is an English actor. He first became known for portraying Mick Travis in Lindsay Anderson's films if.... (1968), O Lucky Man! (1973) and Britannia Hospital (1982). His performance in Stanley Kubrick's A Clockwork Orange (1971), in which he played the delinquent Alex DeLarge, cemented his status as a leading actor of his generation and earned him a Golden Globe nomination.

Career

Born in Horsforth, Leeds, and raised in Liverpool, McDowell worked as a coffee salesman and trained at the London Academy of Music and Dramatic Art before joining the Royal Shakespeare Company. After his breakthrough with Anderson and Kubrick, he starred in films including Time After Time (1979), playing H. G. Wells, and Caligula (1979). He later took roles in Star Trek Generations (1994), as the villain Dr Tolian Soran, Rob Zombie's Halloween remakes as Dr Samuel Loomis, and numerous television series, including Entourage, Heroes, Franklin & Bash and Mozart in the Jungle. His extensive voice work includes animation and video games.

Personal life

McDowell has been married three times, including to actress Margot Bennett and to Mary Steenburgen, with whom he has two children, among them director Charlie McDowell. He was appointed an honorary figure in various film institutions and received a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame in 2012.
