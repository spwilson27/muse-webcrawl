Associazione Calcio Milan, commonly referred to as AC Milan or simply Milan, is a professional football club based in Milan, Lombardy, Italy. Founded in 1899 by English expatriate Herbert Kilpin, the club wears red-and-black striped shirts and plays its home matches at the San Siro, which it shares with city rivals Inter Milan.

History

Milan won its first Italian championship in 1901 and has accumulated 19 Serie A titles, 5 Coppa Italia trophies and 7 European Cup/Champions League titles, making it one of the most successful clubs in world football. Its golden eras include Arrigo Sacchi's pressing side of the late 1980s, featuring the Dutch trio of Gullit, Van Basten and Rijkaard, and Carlo Ancelotti's team of the 2000s. Long owned by Silvio Berlusconi (1986–2017), the club passed through Chinese ownership before being acquired by the American fund RedBird Capital in 2022.

Rivalries and honours

The Derby della Madonnina against Inter is one of world football's great derbies. Milan won its most recent Scudetto in the 2021–22 season. The club's retired numbers include Paolo Maldini's number 3 and Franco Baresi's number 6.
