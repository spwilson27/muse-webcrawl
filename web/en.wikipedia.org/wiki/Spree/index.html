Spree

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Spree (German: Spree, pronounced [ʃpʁeː]; Sorbian: Sprjewja) is a river in Germany flowing through the states of Saxony, Brandenburg and Berlin. About 400 kilometres long, it is the principal river of Berlin, joining the Havel at Spandau.

Course

The Spree rises in the Lusatian highlands near the Czech border, flows north through Cottbus and the Spreewald biosphere reserve — where it divides into a network of small channels — then through Berlin, where it passes Museum Island, the Reichstag district and the East Side Gallery, before joining the Havel in the west of the city.

Significance

Berlin grew up around a crossing of the Spree, and the river remains central to the city's geography, transport and recreation, linked by canals such as the Landwehr Canal and the Spree-Oder Waterway to the wider European inland waterway network.
