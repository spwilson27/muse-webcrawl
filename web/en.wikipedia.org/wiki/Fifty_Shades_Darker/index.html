Fifty Shades Darker

Fifty Shades Darker is a 2011 erotic romance novel by British author E. L. James. It is the second instalment in the Fifty Shades trilogy, which traces the deepening relationship between college graduate Anastasia Steele and wealthy young businessman Christian Grey. The trilogy began as fan fiction before being reworked and published.

Plot

After breaking up with Christian because of his sadomasochistic practices, Ana starts work at a Seattle publishing house. Christian persuades her to resume their relationship on new terms — no rules and no punishments — and the couple grow closer as figures from Christian's past, including his former submissive Leila and the older Elena Lincoln, complicate their lives. The novel ends with a marriage proposal.

Reception and adaptation

The trilogy became a publishing phenomenon, selling well over 100 million copies worldwide despite mixed critical reviews. A film adaptation of Fifty Shades Darker, directed by James Foley and starring Dakota Johnson and Jamie Dornan, was released in 2017. James later published Darker (2017), retelling the story from Christian's point of view.
