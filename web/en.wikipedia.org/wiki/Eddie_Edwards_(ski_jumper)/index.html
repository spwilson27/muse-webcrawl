Michael David Edwards (born 5 December 1963), better known as Eddie the Eagle, is an English ski jumper and Olympian who in 1988 became the first competitor to represent Great Britain in Olympic ski jumping since 1928.

1988 Winter Olympics

At the Calgary Winter Olympics, Edwards finished last in both the 70 m and 90 m events, but his enthusiastic underdog spirit made him an international celebrity. His British records and personal story embodied the Olympic ideal of participation.

Legacy

The International Olympic Committee subsequently tightened qualification rules, informally known as the "Eddie the Eagle rule". His life story was dramatised in the 2016 film Eddie the Eagle, starring Taron Egerton.
