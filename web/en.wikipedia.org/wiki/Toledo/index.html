Toledo

Toledo most commonly refers to:

  • Toledo, Spain, a historic city in central Spain, former capital of the Visigothic kingdom and later of Spain, famous for its medieval old town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
  • Toledo, Ohio, a city in the United States on Lake Erie, known as the "Glass City" for its glass industry.

Toledo, Spain

The Spanish city, perched above the Tagus River, was a major centre where Christian, Muslim and Jewish cultures coexisted in the Middle Ages, producing the Toledo School of Translators. It is associated with the painter El Greco, who lived and worked there.

Toledo, Ohio

The American city grew as a Great Lakes port and manufacturing centre, especially for glass and automobiles, and is home to the Toledo Museum of Art.

Other places named Toledo exist in Belize, Brazil, Colombia, the Philippines, Uruguay and elsewhere in the United States.
