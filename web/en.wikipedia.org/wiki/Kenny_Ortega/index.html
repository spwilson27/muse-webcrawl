Kenneth John Ortega (born 18 April 1950) is an American director, producer, and choreographer. He is best known for directing the High School Musical trilogy and the Descendants films, and for his choreography on films such as Dirty Dancing and Ferris Bueller's Day Off. He also directed Michael Jackson's This Is It concert film.

Career

Ortega began his career as a performer and choreographer, working with Gene Kelly — who became his mentor — on the film Xanadu (1980). He choreographed music videos and stage shows throughout the 1980s, including Madonna's "Material Girl" video, and choreographed Dirty Dancing (1987) and Ferris Bueller's Day Off (1986). He moved into directing with the Disney film Newsies (1992) and directed Hocus Pocus (1993).

Ortega directed and choreographed the opening and closing ceremonies of the 1996 Atlanta and 2002 Salt Lake City Olympic Games ceremonies. His greatest commercial success came with Disney Channel's High School Musical (2006), which became a global phenomenon and spawned two sequels, the third released theatrically. He later directed the Descendants trilogy for Disney Channel and the Netflix series Julie and the Phantoms.

Work with Michael Jackson

Ortega was a longtime collaborator of Michael Jackson, choreographing and directing his Dangerous and HIStory world tours. In 2009 he was directing Jackson's planned This Is It London concerts; after Jackson's death, Ortega directed the concert documentary film Michael Jackson's This Is It, assembled from rehearsal footage.
