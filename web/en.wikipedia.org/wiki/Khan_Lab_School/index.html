Khan Lab School is an independent, non-profit laboratory school founded in 2014 by Sal Khan, founder of Khan Academy. Located in Palo Alto, California (originally in Mountain View), the school was created to test and develop new educational practices centered on mastery learning, mixed-age grouping, and student agency, and to share those practices with the wider education community.

Model

The school does not assign traditional grade levels by age; instead, students are grouped in mixed-age "Independence Levels" and advance in each subject upon demonstrating mastery. There are no conventional letter grades or bells, and the day includes extended blocks for projects, one-on-one tutoring, and self-directed study supported by Khan Academy's online resources.

History

Khan Lab School opened in fall 2014 with 30 students in Mountain View, in the same building as Khan Academy's offices. It grew into a full K–12 program and relocated to Palo Alto, California. Sal Khan described the school's philosophy in his book The One World Schoolhouse and in writings on the "lab school" concept, in which the school functions as a research and development site for educational innovation.
