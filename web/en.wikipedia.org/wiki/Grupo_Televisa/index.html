Grupo Televisa

Grupo Televisa, S.A.B. is a Mexican media and telecommunications company headquartered in Mexico City. For decades it was the largest Spanish-language media company in the world, famous for its telenovelas, news broadcasts and sports coverage.

Televisa's roots go back to the radio and television ventures of Emilio Azcárraga Vidaurreta; the modern company took shape in 1973 with the merger of Telesistema Mexicano and Televisión Independiente de México. Under Emilio Azcárraga Jean, who led the company from 1997, Televisa expanded internationally. In 2022 its content and media assets were combined with Univision Communications to form TelevisaUnivision, while the remaining Grupo Televisa retained businesses including the cable operator Izzi, the satellite service Sky México (later sold), publishing and the Club América football team and Estadio Azteca (spun off as Ollamani).
