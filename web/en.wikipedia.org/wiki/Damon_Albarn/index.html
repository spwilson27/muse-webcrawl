Damon Albarn

Damon Albarn (born 23 March 1968) is an English musician, singer, songwriter and composer. He is the frontman and primary songwriter of the rock band Blur and the co-creator, with artist Jamie Hewlett, of the virtual band Gorillaz, and has pursued numerous collaborative and solo projects spanning rock, hip hop, electronic and world music.

Career

Albarn formed Blur in 1988 with Graham Coxon, Alex James and Dave Rowntree. The band became central to the 1990s Britpop movement with albums such as Parklife (1994) and hits including "Country House," "Song 2" and "Tender." Blur reunited periodically after 2009 and released The Ballad of Darren in 2023.

In 1998 Albarn and Jamie Hewlett created Gorillaz, a virtual band whose members are animated characters; its albums, from Gorillaz (2001) to Demon Days (2005) and beyond, blend hip hop, dub and pop and feature dozens of guest artists. Albarn's other work includes the supergroup The Good, the Bad & the Queen, the Africa Express collective, two operas (Monkey: Journey to the West and Dr Dee), and the solo albums Everyday Robots (2014) and The Nearer the Fountain, More Pure the Stream Flows (2021).
