ASCII Media Works

ASCII Media Works, Inc. (株式会社アスキー・メディアワークス) is a Japanese publisher and a brand of Kadokawa Corporation. It was formed on 1 April 2008 from the merger of ASCII Corporation and MediaWorks, two Kadokawa Group companies. The company publishes magazines, light novels, manga, video games and other media, largely under the Dengeki imprint family.

History

MediaWorks, founded in 1992, published the Dengeki line of magazines and the Dengeki Bunko light novel imprint. The original ASCII Corporation, founded in 1977, was a pioneer of Japanese personal computing publishing and hardware. After both became Kadokawa subsidiaries, they merged in 2008 to form ASCII Media Works. In 2013 the company was absorbed into Kadokawa Corporation and continued as an internal brand company and later as a publishing brand.

Publishing

ASCII Media Works publishes magazines such as Dengeki Daioh, Dengeki G's Magazine and Dengeki Maoh, and light novels under Dengeki Bunko, including series such as Sword Art Online, A Certain Magical Index, Durarara!!, The Irregular at Magic High School and Spice and Wolf. It also organises the annual Dengeki Novel Prize and has developed video games based on its properties.
