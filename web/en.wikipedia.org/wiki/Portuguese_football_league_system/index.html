Portuguese football league system

A Primeira Liga stadium in Portugal.

The Portuguese football league system is a series of interconnected leagues for association football clubs in Portugal. It is organised by the Portuguese Football Federation (FPF) and the Liga Portuguesa de Futebol Profissional (LPFP), which runs the top two professional divisions.

Structure

  • Level 1 — Primeira Liga: 18 clubs; the national championship, dominated historically by Benfica, Porto and Sporting CP.
  • Level 2 — Liga Portugal 2: 18 clubs, including some reserve ("B") teams of top clubs, which cannot be promoted to the same division as their parent club.
  • Level 3 — Liga 3: introduced in 2021, a national third tier of two geographic series.
  • Level 4 — Campeonato de Portugal: a national fourth tier in several series.
  • Levels 5 and below — District championships organised by the regional football associations.

Promotion and relegation

Clubs are promoted and relegated between levels at the end of each season, with play-offs at some boundaries. The bottom clubs of the Primeira Liga drop to Liga Portugal 2, and the champions of the lower divisions move up in turn, subject to licensing requirements.

Cup competitions

Alongside the leagues, Portuguese clubs compete in the Taça de Portugal (the main national cup, open to clubs across the system, including district sides) and the Taça da Liga (league cup, for professional clubs).
