Sirtaki

Sirtaki is a popular dance of Greek origin, created for the 1964 film Zorba the Greek. Despite often being mistaken for an ancient folk dance, it was choreographed by Giorgos Provias for the film, combining slow hasapiko movements with a faster hasaposerviko section, to music by Mikis Theodorakis.

Description

Dancers stand in a line or circle with hands on each other's shoulders. The dance begins slowly and accelerates, culminating in leaps and quick steps as the tempo rises — a structure designed so that Anthony Quinn, who had an injured foot and limited dancing ability, could perform it on screen.

"Zorba's Dance" became internationally famous, and the sirtaki is now performed worldwide as an emblem of Greek culture and at Greek celebrations.
