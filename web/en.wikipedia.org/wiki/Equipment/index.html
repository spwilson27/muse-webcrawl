Equipment

Equipment most commonly refers to a set of tools or other objects commonly used to achieve a particular objective. Different jobs require different kinds of equipment. The term covers a wide range of items, from simple hand tools and machines to complete systems designed for industrial, scientific, military, medical, or recreational purposes.

Types

Equipment may be categorised by function: heavy equipment used in construction and mining; protective equipment such as personal protective equipment (PPE) worn to minimise exposure to hazards; medical equipment used in diagnosis and treatment; laboratory equipment; sports equipment; and office equipment. In accounting, equipment is a class of fixed assets, depreciated over its useful life, and its acquisition is a common form of capital expenditure.

Management

Organisations track equipment through inventories and maintenance programmes covering procurement, calibration, repair, and eventual disposal or replacement. Total cost of ownership includes purchase price, operating costs, and downtime.
