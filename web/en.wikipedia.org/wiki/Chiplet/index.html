Chiplet

A chiplet is a small integrated circuit ("die") designed to be combined with other chiplets in a single package to form a larger, more complex processor or system. Chiplet-based designs are a form of heterogeneous integration and modular chip architecture.

Advantages

Splitting a design into smaller dies improves manufacturing yield, lets each function be built on the most suitable process node, and allows mixing and matching of components such as CPU cores, I/O and memory interfaces across product lines.

Industry adoption

AMD popularised chiplets with its Zen 2 and later processors, combining compute dies with an I/O die. The Universal Chiplet Interconnect Express (UCIe) consortium, formed in 2022, standardises die-to-die interconnects between vendors.
