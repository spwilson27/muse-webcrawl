Cittaslow

Founded: 15 October 1999
Founded at: Orvieto, Italy
Type: Organisation / network of towns
Symbol: An orange snail bearing a skyline

Cittaslow is an organisation founded in Italy and known as the Slow City movement. Its mission is to spread the philosophy of the Slow Food movement to local communities and governments of towns, applying the concepts of ecogastronomy and quality of life in everyday town practice, and to support local cultures and traditions.

History

Cittaslow was founded on 15 October 1999 in Orvieto by Paolo Saturnini, mayor of Greve in Chianti, together with the mayors of Orvieto, Bra, and Positano, and with Carlo Petrini, founder of Slow Food. The founding towns adopted a charter of some 55 criteria grouped into areas such as environmental policy, infrastructure, quality of urban fabric, safeguarding local produce and traditions, hospitality, and community awareness. Membership is restricted to towns with fewer than 50,000 inhabitants.

Membership and aims

To join, a town must be assessed against the Cittaslow criteria, which include reducing traffic and noise, increasing green space, supporting local farmers and shops, preserving historic centres, promoting local food and craftsmanship, and involving residents in decision-making. The network has grown to several hundred towns across around thirty countries, including towns in the United Kingdom, Germany, South Korea, Turkey, Australia, and China. The movement's logo is an orange snail carrying a crown of modern and ancient buildings.
