Old City of Sanaa

CountryYemen
Altitude~2,300 m
StatusUNESCO World Heritage (in danger)
[Image: Tower houses of the Old City of Sanaa]
Tower houses of the Old City of Sanaa

The Old City of Sanaa, in Yemen's capital, is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world, a UNESCO World Heritage site famed for its tower houses of rammed earth and burnt brick decorated with white gypsum tracery.

Character

Sanaa stands at about 2,300 m in the Yemeni highlands. Its old city preserves over a hundred mosques — including the Great Mosque, among the oldest in Islam — thousands of multi-storey tower houses, hammams and the Bab al-Yaman gate, in an urban fabric substantially intact since before the 11th century. The war in Yemen since 2015 has damaged parts of the old city and placed the site on the List of World Heritage in Danger.
