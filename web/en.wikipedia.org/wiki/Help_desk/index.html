Help desk

A help desk is a department or service that provides information and support to users, typically regarding an organisation's products, services or internal information technology. It serves as a single point of contact for reporting problems, requesting services and obtaining assistance.

Functions

Help desks receive requests by telephone, email, chat or ticketing systems; log and categorise them (incident, problem, service request); resolve simple issues directly; and escalate complex cases to specialist teams. Common metrics include first-response time, resolution time and user satisfaction.

IT help desks

In information technology, the help desk is a central element of IT service management frameworks such as ITIL, handling password resets, hardware faults, software installation and network problems. Larger organisations distinguish tiers of support, from first-line generalists to third-line engineers.
