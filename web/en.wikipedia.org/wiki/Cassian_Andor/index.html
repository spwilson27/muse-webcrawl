Cassian Andor

Cassian Jeron Andor is a fictional character in the Star Wars franchise, portrayed by Mexican actor Diego Luna. He first appeared in the film Rogue One (2016) as a Rebel Alliance intelligence officer who leads the mission to steal the plans for the Death Star, and is the protagonist of the Disney+ prequel series Andor (2022–2025).

Character

Cassian was born Kassa on the planet Kenari and, after losing his home and family, grew up on Ferrix, adopted by Maarva and Clem Andor. Drawn into theft, smuggling, and eventually rebellion, he becomes a captain and intelligence officer in the Rebel Alliance, operating with his reprogrammed Imperial security droid K-2SO. In Rogue One he leads the extraction of Jyn Erso and the mission to Scarif, where he dies alongside her as the Death Star plans reach Princess Leia.

The series Andor

Created by Tony Gilroy, Andor traces Cassian's transformation from a cynical thief to a committed rebel over the five years before Rogue One, encompassing his imprisonment in the Narkina 5 labor facility, the Aldhani heist, and the uprising on Ferrix. The series was widely praised for its political themes and grounded depiction of rebellion.

Reception

Luna's portrayal has been acclaimed, and Cassian is noted among Star Wars heroes for his moral ambiguity, including killing an informant in his first scene.
