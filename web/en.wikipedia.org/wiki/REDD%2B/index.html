REDD+

REDD+
Stands forReducing Emissions from Deforestation and forest Degradation, plus conservation and sustainable forest management
FrameworkUNFCCC
AdoptedWarsaw Framework (2013); Paris Agreement (2015)

REDD+ is a climate change mitigation framework developed under the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC) that creates financial value for the carbon stored in forests. It offers incentives to developing countries to reduce emissions from deforestation and forest degradation, conserve and enhance forest carbon stocks, and manage forests sustainably — the activities denoted by the "+" in the name.

Mechanism

Under REDD+, countries establish forest reference emission levels, measure and report avoided emissions, and may receive results-based payments from donor countries and climate funds. Implementation proceeds through readiness, demonstration and full results-based phases, with requirements for safeguards protecting biodiversity and the rights of indigenous peoples and local communities.

Criticism

Debate around REDD+ concerns measurement difficulties, leakage of logging to other regions, insecure land tenure, and the equity of carbon-market financing. It was formally encouraged in Article 5 of the 2015 Paris Agreement.
