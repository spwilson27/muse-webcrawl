Oshki

Oshki is a village in the Vayots Dzor Province of Armenia. Small rural settlements of this kind are typical of the mountainous southern regions of the country, where agriculture, animal husbandry and seasonal work form the basis of the local economy.

Villages in Vayots Dzor are known for their proximity to historic monasteries, medieval churches and the Arpa River valley, as well as for wine production, the region being one of the oldest wine-making areas in the world, as evidenced by the Areni-1 winery complex dated to around 4100 BC.
