The Last of Us Part II

Developer: Naughty Dog
Publisher: Sony Interactive Entertainment
Directors: Neil Druckmann, Anthony Newman, Kurt Margenau
Released: 19 June 2020 (PlayStation 4)
Genre: Action-adventure, survival horror

The Last of Us Part II is a 2020 action-adventure game developed by Naughty Dog and published by Sony Interactive Entertainment. Set four years after the events of The Last of Us (2013), the game follows two playable characters in a post-apocalyptic United States: Ellie, who sets out on a quest for vengeance after Joel is murdered, and Abby, the soldier who killed him and becomes entangled in a war between her militia and a religious cult.

Gameplay

The game is played from a third-person perspective. Players traverse environments in Seattle and Jackson using firearms, improvised weapons, crafting and stealth; new mechanics include crawling prone, dodging, squeezing through narrow gaps and using a companion dog-like tracking threat from enemies. An extensive accessibility menu, unusually comprehensive for a major release, was widely praised.

Development

Development began in 2014, shortly after the release of The Last of Us Remastered. Neil Druckmann returned as creative director and co-wrote the story with Halley Gross, centred on themes of revenge, cycles of violence and empathy. Ashley Johnson and Troy Baker reprised their roles as Ellie and Joel, and Laura Bailey joined as Abby; Gustavo Santaolalla returned as composer. The game was subject to a major leak of footage and plot details in April 2020, and reporting on "crunch" working conditions at Naughty Dog drew criticism.

Reception

The game received critical acclaim for its gameplay, graphics, performances and narrative ambition, winning more than 300 Game of the Year awards, though its story proved divisive among players and sparked online controversy and review bombing. It sold over four million copies in its first weekend and more than ten million by 2022, becoming one of the best-selling PlayStation 4 games. A remastered version for PlayStation 5 was released in January 2024, adding a roguelike survival mode, "No Return".
