Twitch Plays Pokémon

Twitch Plays Pokémon was a social experiment and phenomenon on the video game live streaming website Twitch, in which thousands of viewers collectively played Game Freak's and Nintendo's Pokémon video games by entering commands into the stream's chat.

The experiment

Launched on 12 February 2014 by an anonymous Australian programmer, the channel fed chat commands (up, down, a, b, start, and so on) into an emulator of Pokémon Red. Despite constant contradictory input and self-sabotage, the crowd completed the game in just over 16 days of continuous play, with peak concurrent participation above 100,000 and tens of millions of total views.

Culture and legacy

The stream generated elaborate fan lore — the "Helix Fossil" worshipped as a god, the false prophet Flareon, and the anarchist-versus-democracy voting system added to manage input. It won awards as an example of crowd gaming and went on to play through many further Pokémon titles and other games.
