Genre: Children's animated fantasy
Network: Disney Junior
Original run: 2013–2018

Sofia the First is an American computer-animated children's television series that aired on Disney Junior from 2013 to 2018. It follows Sofia, a young village girl whose mother marries the king of Enchancia, making Sofia a princess.

Plot

Sofia attends the Royal Preparatory Academy and learns what it means to be a princess, aided by the magical Amulet of Avalor, which grants abilities and can summon Disney princesses in times of need. Characters include her step-siblings Amber and James, the royal sorcerer Cedric, and animal friends such as Clover the rabbit.

Production

The series was created by Craig Gerber and debuted with the television film Sofia the First: Once Upon a Princess (2012). Ariel Winter voiced Sofia. The show spawned the spin-off Elena of Avalor.
