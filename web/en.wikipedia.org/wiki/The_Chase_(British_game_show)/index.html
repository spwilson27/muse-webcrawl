The Chase (British game show)

The Chase is a British television quiz show broadcast on ITV and hosted by Bradley Walsh. Contestants play against a professional quizzer, known as the "Chaser", who attempts to prevent them from winning a cash prize. The programme premiered on 29 June 2009 and has become one of ITV's most successful daytime shows.

Gameplay

A team of four contestants, who do not know each other, each take part in a "Cash Builder" round, answering as many questions as possible in one minute, each correct answer earning £1,000. In the head-to-head chase, the contestant chooses a starting position on a seven-step board and whether to play for the amount earned, a higher offer, or a lower offer, while the Chaser answers the same multiple-choice questions and tries to catch them.

Contestants who outrun the Chaser advance to the "Final Chase", in which the team builds up a set of steps by answering questions for two minutes and the Chaser attempts to match that total; if the Chaser passes or answers incorrectly, the team may push them back one step. If the Chaser fails to catch the team, the prize fund is split equally among the surviving players.

The Chasers

The regular Chasers include Mark Labbett ("The Beast"), Shaun Wallace ("The Dark Destroyer"), Anne Hegerty ("The Governess"), Paul Sinha ("The Sinnerman"), Jenny Ryan ("The Vixen"), and Darragh Ennis ("The Menace"). Several of them were accomplished quiz competitors before joining the show.

International versions

The format has been licensed in numerous countries, including Australia, Germany (Gefragt – Gejagt), Spain (El cazador), and the United States, where an ABC version began airing in 2021 with former Jeopardy! champions among the Chasers.
