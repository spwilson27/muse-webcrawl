Be'eri
Type: Kibbutz
Country: Israel
Region: Northwestern Negev
Founded: 1946

Be'eri is a kibbutz in southern Israel, in the northwestern Negev near the border with the Gaza Strip. Founded in 1946 as one of the eleven points in the Negev, it is named after Berl Katznelson, whose first name was Be'eri.

History

The kibbutz developed agriculture and a major printing house, Be'eri Printers. On 7 October 2023, Hamas militants attacked the kibbutz during the October 7 attacks, killing over one hundred residents and taking hostages, one of the deadliest attacks on a single community that day.

Aftermath

Survivors were evacuated; the community has since worked toward rebuilding and returning, and investigations examined the military response during the attack.

Economy

Be'eri is known for its printing works, agriculture and a popular cycling area in the surrounding countryside.
