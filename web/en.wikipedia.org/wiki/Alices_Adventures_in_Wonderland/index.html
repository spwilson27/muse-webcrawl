Alice's Adventures in Wonderland is an 1865 novel by English author Lewis Carroll. It details the adventures of a girl named Alice in a dreamlike underground world populated by peculiar creatures, and is considered a founding work of the literary nonsense genre.

Characters

Notable characters include Alice herself, the White Rabbit, the Cheshire Cat, the Mad Hatter, the March Hare, the Dormouse, the Caterpillar, the Duchess, the Mock Turtle, the Gryphon, and the Queen of Hearts.

Themes and influence

The novel plays with logic, language, and the conventions of Victorian society and education. Its poems and songs parody well-known verses of the day. The book has never been out of print and has inspired numerous adaptations, including Walt Disney's 1951 animated film and Tim Burton's 2010 film, as well as references throughout mathematics, psychology, and popular culture.
