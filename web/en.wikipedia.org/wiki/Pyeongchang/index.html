Pyeongchang (Korean: 평창) is a county in Gangwon Province, South Korea, located in the Taebaek Mountains. It is best known internationally as the host of the 2018 Winter Olympic Games and the 2018 Winter Paralympics.

Geography

The county is mountainous, with an average elevation of around 750 metres, and includes the ski resorts of Alpensia and Yongpyong. The town of Daegwallyeong is noted for its cold winters and wind farms.

2018 Winter Olympics

After unsuccessful bids for the 2010 and 2014 Games, Pyeongchang was awarded the 2018 Winter Olympics in 2011. Events were split between the Pyeongchang mountain cluster and the coastal cluster in Gangneung. The Games featured a joint Korean women's ice hockey team and a North Korean delegation, giving them a notable diplomatic dimension.
