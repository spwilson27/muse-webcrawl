Trekkie

A Trekkie or Trekker is a fan of the Star Trek franchise, particularly of the original series and its spin-offs. The term is sometimes used humorously or pejoratively, though many fans embrace it; some prefer "Trekker", and a long-running (usually good-natured) debate distinguishes the two labels.

History

Star Trek's devoted fandom emerged in the late 1960s and is credited with helping revive the franchise: a letter-writing campaign secured a third season of the original series in 1968, and sustained fan enthusiasm led to the first feature film in 1979. The first Star Trek convention was held in New York in 1972.

Culture

Trekkies are known for fan conventions, costumes (including Starfleet uniforms and Klingon dress), fan fiction, and learning fictional languages such as Klingon. The 1997 documentary film Trekkies, narrated by Denise Crosby, examined the phenomenon. See Star Trek and Klingon language.
