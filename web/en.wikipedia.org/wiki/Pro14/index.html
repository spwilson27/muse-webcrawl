Pro14

The Pro14 (known for sponsorship reasons at various times as the Guinness Pro14 and the Guinness Pro12) was an annual rugby union competition involving teams from Ireland, Scotland, Wales, Italy, and South Africa. It was one of the three major professional leagues in Europe, alongside the English Premiership and the French Top 14.


The Pro14 brought together Celtic, Italian, and South African teams.

History

The competition began as the Celtic League in 2001, contested by teams from Ireland, Scotland, and Wales. It was rebranded as the Magners League, then as the Pro12 when two Italian teams joined in 2010. In 2017, two South African teams, the Cheetahs and the Southern Kings, joined following their removal from Super Rugby, and the competition became the Pro14, organised in two conferences. In 2021 the league was restructured and rebranded as the United Rugby Championship, with four South African teams participating.

Format

In its Pro14 form, the fourteen teams were split into two conferences of seven. Teams played the others in their conference home and away, and teams in the other conference once, with additional derby fixtures. The top teams advanced to play-off quarter-finals, semi-finals, and a final to decide the champion. Leinster were the most successful side of the Pro14 era, winning four consecutive titles from 2018 to 2021.
