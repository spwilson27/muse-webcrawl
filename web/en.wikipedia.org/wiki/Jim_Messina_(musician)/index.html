Jim Messina (musician)

James Messina
Born: 5 December 1947, Maywood, California, US
Instruments: Guitar, vocals, bass
Associated acts: Buffalo Springfield, Poco, Loggins and Messina

James Messina (born 5 December 1947) is an American musician, songwriter, singer and record producer. He was a member of Buffalo Springfield in its final phase, a founding member of the country rock band Poco, and one half of the successful 1970s duo Loggins and Messina with Kenny Loggins.

Career

Messina began as a recording engineer and producer, working on Buffalo Springfield's later recordings before joining the band as bassist in 1968. After its breakup he formed Poco with Richie Furay, pioneering country rock on albums such as Pickin' Up the Pieces (1969). Leaving Poco in 1970, he produced a solo album for the young Kenny Loggins; the collaboration became the duo Loggins and Messina, whose albums — including Sittin' In (1971), Full Sail (1973) and Mother Lode (1974) — produced hits such as "Your Mama Don't Dance" and "Thinking of You".

Later work

After the duo split in 1976, Messina released solo albums including Oasis (1979), Messina (1981) and Watching the River Run. He has reunited periodically with Loggins and with Poco, and runs songwriting and performance workshops.
