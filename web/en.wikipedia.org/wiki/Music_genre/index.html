Music genre
TypesRock, jazz, classical, hip hop, electronic, folk, etc.

A music genre is a conventional category that identifies some pieces of music as belonging to a shared tradition or set of conventions. Genres may be defined by instrumentation, rhythmic structure, cultural context, or lyrical themes, and are often divided into subgenres.

Major genres

Broad categories include classical, jazz, blues, rock, pop, hip hop, electronic dance music, country, folk, and metal. Many modern genres emerged through the fusion of earlier traditions and through developments in recording and broadcast media.

Criticism

Classification by genre has been criticised as commercially driven and imprecise, since artists frequently cross boundaries. Streaming platforms now supplement genre labels with algorithmic micro-genres and mood-based playlists.
