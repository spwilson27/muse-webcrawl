Believe (Cher song)

Artist: Cher

Album: Believe

Released: 19 October 1998

Genre: Dance-pop, Eurodance

Length: 3:52

"Believe" is a song recorded by American singer Cher for her twenty-second album, Believe (1998). It was released as the album's lead single in October 1998. Written by Brian Higgins, Stuart McLennen, Paul Barry, Steven Torch, Matthew Gray, and Timothy Powell, and produced by Mark Taylor and Brian Rawling, the song became one of the best-selling singles of all time, with sales of over 11 million copies worldwide.

Background and production

The song is notable for its pioneering use of Auto-Tune as a deliberate vocal effect: Cher's voice is processed so that pitch corrections produce a distinctive robotic, warbling sound, often called the "Cher effect". The producers initially tried to disguise the process, but the effect became the song's signature and was widely imitated in pop music.

Commercial performance

"Believe" reached number one in more than 23 countries. In the United Kingdom it was the best-selling single of 1998 and made Cher the oldest female artist to top the UK chart. In the United States it reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100 in March 1999, making Cher the oldest woman to have a number-one single there and giving her number-one hits across four decades. The song won the Grammy Award for Best Dance Recording.

Legacy

"Believe" is credited with reviving Cher's career and with popularising the creative, audible use of pitch-correction software in popular music, an influence heard in hip hop, R&B, and electronic music for decades afterwards.
