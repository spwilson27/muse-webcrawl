Hine-nui-te-pō

Hine-nui-te-pō ("Great Woman of the Night") is the goddess of night and death in Māori mythology. She rules over the underworld and receives the spirits of the dead.

Mythology

She was originally Hine-tītama, daughter of Tāne and Hine-ahuone. On learning that Tāne was both her father and her husband, she fled in shame to the underworld, becoming Hine-nui-te-pō.

Māui

The demigod Māui attempted to defeat death by crawling through her body while she slept, but a fantail's laughter woke her and she crushed him, making him the first man to die and ensuring mortality for humankind.
