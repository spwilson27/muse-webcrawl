Ramblers is the trading name of the Ramblers Association, a Great Britain organisation for walkers. It is a charity which promotes walking and campaigns to keep the British countryside open to all and to protect the network of public paths. The Ramblers is Britain's largest walking organisation, with hundreds of local groups that organise group walks across England, Scotland and Wales.

History

The association was formally founded on 1 January 1935, bringing together regional federations of rambling clubs that had formed in the early 20th century in the industrial north of England. Its background lay in the access campaigns of the 1920s and early 1930s, in which working-class walkers clashed with landowners over access to moorland — most famously in the mass trespass of Kinder Scout in 1932, organised by the British Workers' Sports Federation, which helped build momentum for the access movement.

The Ramblers campaigned for the National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act 1949, which established national parks in England and Wales and required local authorities to record public rights of way on definitive maps. Later campaigns contributed to the Countryside and Rights of Way Act 2000 (the "right to roam" in England and Wales), the creation of the England Coast Path, and the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2003, which established general access rights in Scotland.

Activities

Ramblers volunteers:

  • Lead thousands of group walks each week through local groups and areas;
  • Check and report problems on public rights of way, and carry out practical path maintenance and clearance;
  • Campaign on planning, access and countryside policy at national and local level;
  • Promote the health benefits of walking, including through walking-for-health schemes and the promotion of routes such as the England Coast Path and Britain's national trails.

Organisation

The charity operates through local groups grouped into areas across Great Britain, with separate national offices for England, Scotland (Ramblers Scotland) and Wales (Ramblers Cymru). It dropped the word "Association" from its trading name in 2009, rebranding as simply "Ramblers".
