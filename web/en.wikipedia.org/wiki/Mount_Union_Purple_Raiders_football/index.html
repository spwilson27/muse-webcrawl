Mount Union Purple Raiders football

The Purple Raiders at Mount Union Stadium in Alliance, Ohio.

The Mount Union Purple Raiders football team represents the University of Mount Union in Alliance, Ohio, in NCAA Division III college football. The Purple Raiders compete in the Ohio Athletic Conference (OAC) and are widely regarded as the most successful programme in Division III history.

Dominance

Under head coach Larry Kehres (1986–2012), Mount Union won 11 national championships and compiled a record of 332–24–3; Kehres was succeeded by his son Vince Kehres, who added further titles, bringing the school's total to 13 Stagg Bowl championships. The programme produced two of the longest winning streaks in college football history — 54 games (1996–1999) and 55 games (2000–2003) — and a record 110 consecutive regular-season victories. The Raiders' Stagg Bowl rivalry with Wisconsin–Whitewater, which produced seven consecutive championship-game meetings from 2005 to 2011, defined an era of Division III football.

Programme

Mount Union plays its home games at Mount Union Stadium (Kehres Stadium), one of the oldest college football venues in the United States, in use since 1913. Despite Division III's prohibition on athletic scholarships, the programme has sent players to the NFL, most notably wide receivers Pierre Garçon and Cecil Shorts III. The team's colours are purple and white.
