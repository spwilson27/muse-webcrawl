EarthBound

EarthBound
Developer: Ape Inc. / HAL Laboratory
Publisher: Nintendo
Director: Shigesato Itoi
Platform: Super Nintendo Entertainment System
Release: 1994 (Japan), 1995 (North America)
Genre: Role-playing

EarthBound, known in Japan as Mother 2, is a role-playing video game developed by Ape Inc. and HAL Laboratory and published by Nintendo for the Super Nintendo Entertainment System. It is the second entry in the Mother series, designed by writer Shigesato Itoi.

Ness and his companions explore a quirky, modern-day America threatened by the alien Giygas.

Gameplay and story

Unlike most role-playing games of its era, EarthBound is set in a contemporary, suburban America-inspired world of towns, drugstores and hamburger shops. The player controls the boy Ness, who is chosen to gather melodies at eight "Your Sanctuary" locations and defeat the cosmic destroyer Giygas. He is joined by Paula, Jeff and Poo. Combat is turn-based with a rolling odometer-style HP meter, visible enemies on the map rather than random encounters, and an offbeat tone mixing humour with moments of genuine darkness.

Development and release

The game was written by copywriter Shigesato Itoi, with music by Keiichi Suzuki and Hirokazu Tanaka. Its North American release in 1995 was accompanied by an unusual marketing campaign ("This game stinks", with scratch-and-sniff cards) and a bundled strategy guide, but sold poorly at the time.

Legacy

EarthBound has since become a cult classic, frequently cited among the greatest video games ever made. Ness became a playable fighter in the Super Smash Bros. series. The game was re-released on the Wii U Virtual Console in 2013—its first release in Europe—and on Nintendo Switch Online in 2022. Its sequel, Mother 3 (2006), was released only in Japan, and the game has influenced many independent RPGs, including Undertale.
