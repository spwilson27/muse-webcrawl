Beechcraft

The Beechcraft King Air family is among the company's best-known products.

Beechcraft is an American brand of civil and military aircraft owned by Textron Aviation since 2014. Founded in Wichita, Kansas in 1932 by Walter Beech and his wife Olive Ann Beech as the Beech Aircraft Corporation, the company became one of the most prominent manufacturers of general aviation aircraft in the world.

History

The company's first design, the classic Model 17 Staggerwing biplane, set the tone for high-performance business aircraft. After World War II, the V-tailed Bonanza (1947) became one of the longest-produced aircraft in history. Other notable models include the twin-engine Baron, the King Air turboprop family (1964 onwards), the T-6 Texan II military trainer and the AT-6 light attack aircraft. The company passed through ownership by Raytheon (1980), Hawker Beechcraft, and bankruptcy in 2012, before being acquired by Textron and merged into Textron Aviation alongside Cessna.

Products

Current production under the Beechcraft name centres on the King Air 260 and 360 turboprops, the Baron G58 and Bonanza G36 piston aircraft, and the T-6 Texan II trainer.
