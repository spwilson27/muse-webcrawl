Greg Rucka

Greg Rucka (born November 29, 1969) is an American author of novels and comic books, known for character-driven crime and espionage fiction and for acclaimed runs on major superhero titles, frequently centred on strong female protagonists.

Novels

Rucka's prose work includes the Atticus Kodiak series, about a professional bodyguard, beginning with Keeper (1996), and the Tara Chace espionage series published as tie-ins to his comic Queen & Country. He has also written standalone thrillers such as The Last Run and novelisations and tie-in fiction, including work in the Star Wars universe.

Comics

In comics Rucka wrote the spy series Queen & Country, the crime series Stumptown (later adapted for television), and the science fiction series Lazarus with artist Michael Lark. For DC Comics he wrote extended runs on Detective Comics — including the introduction of the modern Batwoman, Kate Kane — Gotham Central (co-written with Ed Brubaker), Wonder Woman, for which he created a widely praised modern interpretation of the character, and Lois Lane. For Marvel he wrote Punisher and Wolverine. His original graphic novel The Old Guard, with artist Leandro Fernández, was adapted into a 2020 Netflix film, for which Rucka wrote the screenplay. He has won multiple Eisner Awards.
