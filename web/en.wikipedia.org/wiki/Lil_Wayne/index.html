Dwayne Michael Carter Jr. (born September 27, 1982), known professionally as Lil Wayne, is an American rapper and record executive. He is regarded as one of the most influential hip hop artists of his generation, and one of the best-selling music artists of all time, with more than 120 million records sold worldwide.

Carter signed with Cash Money Records as a teenager and became the label's flagship artist. He first gained attention with the group Hot Boys in the late 1990s, then as a solo artist with albums including Tha Block Is Hot (1999). His reputation grew with Tha Carter (2004) and Tha Carter II (2005), and a torrent of acclaimed mixtapes.

Early life

Carter was born in New Orleans and grew up in the Hollygrove neighbourhood. He wrote his first rap song at age eight and was discovered by Birdman, co-founder of Cash Money Records, joining the label as its youngest member at age eleven.

Career

Wayne's sixth album, Tha Carter III (2008), sold over one million copies in its first week in the United States and won the Grammy Award for Best Rap Album; it included the singles "Lollipop", "A Milli" and "Got Money". He experimented with rock music on Rebirth (2010). After a long dispute with Cash Money delayed it for years, Tha Carter V was released in 2018 to commercial success. His thirteenth album, Funeral, followed in 2020.

Artistry

Known for his distinctive raspy delivery, dense wordplay, free-associative punchlines and prolific output, Wayne influenced a generation of rappers including Drake, Nicki Minaj, Kendrick Lamar and Young Thug.

Young Money

In 2005 Carter founded Young Money Entertainment, which launched the careers of Drake, Nicki Minaj and Tyga. He served as the label's president before stepping back from executive duties.

Personal life

Carter has served an eight-month prison sentence in 2010 on a weapons charge, and has had well-publicised health problems including seizures. He is an avid skateboarder and sports fan.

Discography

Selected studio albums: Tha Block Is Hot (1999), Tha Carter (2004), Tha Carter II (2005), Tha Carter III (2008), Rebirth (2010), Tha Carter IV (2011), Tha Carter V (2018), Funeral (2020).
