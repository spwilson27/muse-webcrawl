Essilor International is a French company that designs, manufactures and distributes ophthalmic lenses and optical equipment. Long the world's largest manufacturer of corrective lenses, it is known for inventing the Varilux progressive lens. In October 2018 Essilor merged with the Italian eyewear group Luxottica to form EssilorLuxottica, a vertically integrated eyewear giant, with Essilor continuing as a subsidiary and brand.

History

Essilor was formed in 1972 by the merger of Essel and Silor, two French optical firms whose roots reach back to 1849. Essel's optician Bernard Maitenaz invented the Varilux progressive lens in 1959, replacing bifocals with a lens of continuously varying power; Silor pioneered lightweight plastic lenses under the Orma name. The combined company became the global leader in prescription lenses, expanding through acquisitions of laboratories and lens makers worldwide.

Products

Flagship brands include Varilux progressive lenses, Crizal anti-reflective coatings, Transitions photochromic lenses (through a joint venture), and Eyezen and Xperio lines. Essilor also supplies opticians with edging and measuring instruments and supports vision-access programmes in developing countries through its social initiatives.
