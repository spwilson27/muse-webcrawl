Mammoplasty
SpecialtyPlastic surgery
TypesAugmentation, reduction, reconstruction

Mammoplasty (also spelled mammaplasty) is a group of surgical procedures whose goal is to reshape or otherwise change the appearance of the breast. The term most commonly refers to breast augmentation, breast reduction, and breast reconstruction after mastectomy.

Types

Augmentation mammoplasty enlarges the breast using implants or fat transfer. Reduction mammoplasty removes excess tissue and skin to relieve symptoms such as back and neck pain caused by very large breasts. Reconstructive mammoplasty rebuilds the breast, commonly after cancer treatment, using implants or autologous tissue flaps such as the DIEP flap.

Considerations

As with any surgery, mammoplasty carries risks including infection, scarring, changes in nipple sensation, and complications related to implants, such as capsular contracture. Outcomes and techniques have been refined over more than a century of practice, from early paraffin and silicone injections to modern structured implants and microsurgical reconstruction.
