Siege of Fort Zeelandia

The Siege of Fort Zeelandia (1661–1662) was a military conflict between the forces of Koxinga (Zheng Chenggong), the Ming loyalist warlord, and the Dutch East India Company (VOC), fought at Fort Zeelandia on the island of Formosa (Taiwan). After a nine-month siege, the Dutch governor Frederick Coyett surrendered the fort on 1 February 1662, ending 38 years of Dutch colonial rule in Taiwan and establishing Zheng rule under the Kingdom of Tungning.

Background

The Dutch had ruled southern Taiwan since 1624, building Fort Zeelandia at Tayouan (modern Anping, Tainan). Koxinga, driven from mainland China by the Qing dynasty, needed a secure base and crossed the Taiwan Strait with some 400 junks and 25,000 men in the spring of 1661, landing through the Luermen Channel at a moment when the Dutch were unprepared. The Dutch stronghold of Fort Provintia surrendered within days, and Koxinga blockaded Zeelandia.

The siege

Koxinga initially expected the fort to fall quickly but Coyett held out, organising active defence and awaiting relief. A Dutch relief fleet from Batavia under Jacob Caeuw arrived in September 1661 but its attacks on the Zheng fleet and attempts to break through failed; Caeuw eventually sailed away, abandoning the garrison. In January 1662 a German sergeant, Hans Jurgen Radis, defected and revealed a weak point in the defences — the Utrecht redoubt commanding the fort. Zheng cannon captured the redoubt on 25 January 1662, making the fort untenable.

Surrender and aftermath

Coyett negotiated terms and surrendered on 1 February 1662; the Dutch were allowed to leave with their personal belongings and sufficient provisions, marching out with flags flying. Coyett was later scapegoated in the Netherlands, tried, and exiled to the Banda Islands before being pardoned; he published a defence, Neglected Formosa (1675). Koxinga made Zeelandia (renamed Anping) his capital, but died only months later, in June 1662. His son Zheng Jing consolidated the Kingdom of Tungning, which lasted until the Qing conquest of Taiwan in 1683.
