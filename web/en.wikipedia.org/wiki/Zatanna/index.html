Zatanna

Zatanna Zatara is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Gardner Fox and artist Murphy Anderson, she first appeared in Hawkman #4 (November 1964). Zatanna is both a stage magician and a real sorceress, the daughter of the magician Giovanni "John" Zatara.

Fictional character biography

Zatanna's mother, Sindella, was a member of the Homo magi, a hidden race of humans with natural magical ability, giving Zatanna innate power in addition to the craft she learned from her father. Her introductory storyline, "Zatanna's Search," ran across several DC titles as she sought her missing father, aided by heroes including Hawkman, the Atom, Green Lantern and Batman.

Powers and abilities

Zatanna casts spells by speaking the desired effect backwards — a system called logomancy. Speaking her commands in reverse concentrates her power, though she is capable of magic without speech in emergencies. Her abilities include telekinesis, healing, elemental manipulation, teleportation and reality alteration, making her one of the most powerful magic users in the DC Universe.

In other media

Zatanna has appeared in animated series including Batman: The Animated Series, Justice League Unlimited and Young Justice, and was portrayed by Serinda Swan in the television series Smallville. She is a recurring member of Justice League Dark, DC's team of occult heroes.
