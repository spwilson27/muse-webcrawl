Religion

Religion is a range of social-cultural systems — including designated behaviours and practices, morals, beliefs, worldviews, texts, sanctified places and organisations — that relate humanity to supernatural, transcendental or spiritual elements.

Major religions

By number of adherents, the largest religions are Christianity, Islam, Hinduism and Buddhism, alongside Judaism, Sikhism, folk religions and many others; a growing share of the world population is religiously unaffiliated.

Study of religion

Religion is studied in disciplines including theology, comparative religion, anthropology and sociology of religion.
