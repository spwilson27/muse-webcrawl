Sonic the Hedgehog

Sonic the Hedgehog is a Japanese video game series and media franchise created by Naoto Ohshima and Yuji Naka for Sega. The franchise follows Sonic, an anthropomorphic blue hedgehog who battles the evil Doctor Eggman, a mad scientist. The main Sonic the Hedgehog games are platformers mostly developed by Sonic Team; other games, developed by various studios, include spin-offs in the racing, fighting, party and sports genres. The franchise also incorporates printed media, animations, feature films, and merchandise.

History

Sega developed the first Sonic game, released in 1991 for the Sega Genesis, to compete with Nintendo's mascot Mario. Its success helped Sega become one of the leading video game companies during the fourth generation of video game consoles in the early 1990s. Sega Technical Institute developed the next three Sonic games, plus the spin-off Sonic Spinball (1993). A number of Sonic games were also developed for Sega's 8-bit consoles, the Master System and Game Gear. After a hiatus during the unsuccessful Saturn era, the first major 3D Sonic game, Sonic Adventure, was released in 1998 for the Dreamcast.

Gameplay

Sonic games are known for their speed: Sonic runs fast, curls into a ball to attack enemies, and collects golden rings as a form of health. Levels are filled with springs, slopes, bottomless pits, and loop-de-loops. Dr. Eggman (Dr. Robotnik in early Western localizations) serves as the recurring antagonist, often imprisoning animals in robots or seeking the powerful Chaos Emeralds.

Characters

Alongside Sonic, the series features Miles "Tails" Prower, a two-tailed fox who flies by spinning his tails; Knuckles the Echidna, guardian of the Master Emerald; Amy Rose; and the antihero Shadow the Hedgehog, introduced in Sonic Adventure 2 (2001).

Other media

The franchise has expanded into animated series including The Adventures of Sonic the Hedgehog, Sonic Boom, and Sonic Prime, a long-running comic book series, and a film series beginning with Sonic the Hedgehog (2020), which was a commercial success and spawned sequels.

Reception and legacy

The Sonic franchise is one of the best-selling video game franchises, with over 140 million copies sold by 2022, and grossed over $5 billion by 2014. Sonic is one of the most recognizable video game characters in the world and Sega's flagship mascot.
