Historic Sites and Monuments Board of Canada

The Historic Sites and Monuments Board of Canada (French: Commission des lieux et monuments historiques du Canada) is a federal advisory body that recommends places, persons, and events of national historic significance to the Government of Canada.

History

The board was established in 1919. Its recommendations lead to designations as National Historic Sites, National Historic Persons, and National Historic Events, usually marked with federal plaques.

Composition

Membership includes representatives from each province and territory, along with the Librarian and Archivist of Canada and a representative of the Canadian Museum of History.
