Liane Moriarty

Liane Moriarty
Born: 1966, Sydney, Australia
Occupation: Novelist
Notable works: Big Little Lies; Apples Never Fall

Liane Moriarty (born 1966) is an Australian author of novels and children's books. She has written ten novels, including the New York Times best sellers Big Little Lies, Nine Perfect Strangers, and Apples Never Fall, which together have sold millions of copies worldwide.

Career

Moriarty worked in advertising before publishing her first novel, Three Wishes (2003), written for a master's degree at Macquarie University. Her breakthrough came with The Husband's Secret (2013) and Big Little Lies (2014), both topping bestseller lists. She also writes the Nicola Berry children's series under the name L. M. Moriarty.

Adaptations

Big Little Lies was adapted by HBO into an acclaimed limited series (2017–2019) starring Reese Witherspoon and Nicole Kidman; Nine Perfect Strangers and Apples Never Fall were likewise adapted for television. Her novel Here One Moment was published in 2024.
