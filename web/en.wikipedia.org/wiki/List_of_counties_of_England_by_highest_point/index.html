List of counties of England by highest point

This is a list of the ceremonial counties of England by their highest point. The highest county top in England is Scafell Pike in Cumbria at 978 metres (3,209 ft), which is also the highest mountain in England. Completing all of the county tops is a popular challenge among British hillwalkers; the list is sometimes known as the "county tops". Heights are given in metres above sea level.
