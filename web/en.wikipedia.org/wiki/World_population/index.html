The world population is the total number of humans currently living on Earth. It reached 8 billion in November 2022 according to the United Nations, and continues to grow by roughly 70–80 million per year (approximate), though the growth rate has been falling since the 1960s peak of over 2% per year.

Milestones: 1 billion (~1804), 2 billion (1927), 3 billion (1960), then roughly one billion every 12–14 years through the late 20th century. The most populous countries are India (which passed China around 2023) and China, each about 1.4 billion, followed by the United States (~335 million). UN medium projections see a peak of about 10.3–10.4 billion in the 2080s before a slow decline, with more than half of growth to 2050 concentrated in a handful of countries, mostly in sub-Saharan Africa.
