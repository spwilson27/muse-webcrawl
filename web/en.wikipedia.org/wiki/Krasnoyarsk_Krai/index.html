Krasnoyarsk Krai is a federal subject of Russia and the second-largest by area, stretching from the Sayan Mountains in the south to the Taymyr Peninsula on the Arctic Ocean — nearly 3,000 km north to south, roughly 13% of Russia's territory.

Geography

The Yenisei River, one of the world's longest, divides the krai. Landscapes range from steppe and taiga forest to tundra and arctic desert. The region contains the Putorana Plateau, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and borders the Arctic Ocean along a vast, sparsely populated coastline.

Economy

The krai is rich in natural resources: nickel and platinum-group metals around Norilsk, aluminium smelted at Krasnoyarsk, gold, timber and the huge hydropower of the Krasnoyarsk and Sayano-Shushensk dams.

History

Founded in 1934, the krai absorbed the Taymyr and Evenk autonomous okrugs in 2007. Its population of about 2.8 million is concentrated in the south around Krasnoyarsk city.
