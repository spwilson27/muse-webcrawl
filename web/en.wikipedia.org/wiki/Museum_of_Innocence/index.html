Museum of Innocence

The Museum of Innocence (Turkish: Masumiyet Müzesi) is a novel by the Turkish Nobel laureate Orhan Pamuk, published in 2008. It tells the story of Kemal, a wealthy Istanbul businessman, and his obsessive love for his distant cousin Füsun, whose everyday objects he collects over decades.

In 2012 Pamuk opened a real Museum of Innocence in the Çukurcuma neighbourhood of Istanbul, displaying in vitrines the objects described in the novel, arranged by chapter. The novel itself contains a ticket that admits the reader to the museum.

Themes

The work explores love, obsession, memory, collecting, and the tension between tradition and Westernisation in Istanbul society from the 1970s onward. The museum received the European Museum of the Year Award in 2014.
