Martin Beck

Martin Beck is a fictional Swedish police detective created by Maj Sjöwall and Per Wahlöö in their ten-novel series The Story of a Crime (1965–1975), foundational works of Scandinavian crime fiction and the police procedural.

Created byMaj Sjöwall & Per Wahlöö
First appearanceRoseanna (1965)
OccupationPolice detective, Stockholm

The novels

The series — including Roseanna, The Man Who Went Up in Smoke, The Laughing Policeman (Edgar Award winner), and The Terrorists — follows Beck and colleagues such as Lennart Kollberg and Gunvald Larsson, combining crime plots with social critique of Swedish society.

Adaptations

Beck has been played on screen by Gösta Ekman and, in the long-running TV films since 1997, Peter Haber.
