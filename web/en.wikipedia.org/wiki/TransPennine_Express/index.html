TransPennine Express

TransPennine Express (TPE) is a British train operating company, owned by the Department for Transport's operator of last resort since 28 May 2023, providing inter-city and regional services across Northern England and Scotland. It runs services from Liverpool and Manchester to Leeds, York, Newcastle, Edinburgh and Glasgow.

History

The TransPennine franchise was created in 2004, operated first by First Keolis TransPennine and, from 2016, by FirstGroup alone. Persistent poor performance and cancellations led the government in May 2023 not to renew the contract, transferring operations to the public operator of last resort.

Services

TPE's network has three main corridors: the North TransPennine route via the Standedge Tunnel between Manchester and Leeds; the South TransPennine route via Sheffield to Cleethorpes; and Anglo-Scottish services up the West Coast Main Line. Services also link Liverpool with Newcastle and Saltburn.

Rolling stock

From 2019 the franchise introduced the "Nova" fleets: Class 802 bi-mode multiple units (Nova 1), Class 68 locomotives with Mark 5A coaches (Nova 3) and Class 397 electric units (Nova 2), alongside Class 185 diesel units dating from the original franchise. The locomotive-hauled Nova 3 sets were withdrawn in December 2023.
