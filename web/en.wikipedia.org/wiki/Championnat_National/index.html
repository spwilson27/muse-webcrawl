Championnat National

The Championnat National, commonly referred to as simply the National, is the third division of the French football league system, behind Ligue 1 and Ligue 2. It is contested by 18 clubs and operates on a system of promotion and relegation with Ligue 2 and the Championnat National 2.

Third-tier French football, mixing professional, semi-professional and amateur clubs.

History

The league was created in 1993 by the French Football Federation as National 1, replacing the former third-level structure, and was renamed Championnat National in 1997. Unlike Ligue 1 and Ligue 2, which are run by the Ligue de Football Professionnel, the National is administered directly by the FFF.

Format

Clubs play each other home and away over a season running from August to May. The top two clubs are promoted to Ligue 2 (with a play-off introduced for the third-placed club in recent reforms), while the bottom clubs are relegated to National 2. The division has a mixed character: fully professional clubs relegated from Ligue 2 play alongside semi-professional and amateur sides, and reserve teams of professional clubs are not permitted in the division.

Notable clubs and players

Many well-known French clubs have passed through the National during rebuilding periods, and the division has served as a proving ground for young players and managers before moves to the professional leagues.
