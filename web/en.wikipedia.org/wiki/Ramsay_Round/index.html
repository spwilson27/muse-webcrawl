Ramsay Round

LocationLochaber, Scottish Highlands
DistanceAbout 56 miles (90 km)
AscentAbout 28,500 ft (8,700 m)
Summits24 peaks (23 Munros and one Corbett)
First completed1978, by Charlie Ramsay

The Ramsay Round, also known as the Charlie Ramsay Round, is a long-distance fell running challenge in the Lochaber region of the Scottish Highlands. It is Scotland's equivalent of the Bob Graham Round in the Lake District and the Paddy Buckley Round in Wales, and links 24 peaks — 23 Munros and one Corbett — including Ben Nevis, the highest mountain in the British Isles.

Route

The round starts and finishes at the Glen Nevis Youth Hostel and traverses the Mamores, the Grey Corries, the Aonachs, Ben Nevis and the Loch Treig hills. The standard challenge is to complete the circuit on foot within 24 hours. Charlie Ramsay completed the first round in July 1978 with support from fellow runners, extending an earlier 24-peak route by Philip Tranter.

Records

Fewer people have completed the Ramsay Round than the Bob Graham Round, reflecting its remoteness. The record times have fallen steadily, with leading fell runners completing the round in well under 15 hours in recent years.
