List of newspapers

This article indexes lists of newspapers by country and region, and summarises the largest-circulation newspapers in the world. Daily circulation is highest in Asia, where Japanese and Indian papers dominate readership figures.

By region

Separate lists exist for newspapers in each country, for defunct newspapers, student newspapers, and for newspapers by language. Print circulation has declined in most Western markets since the 2000s as audiences moved online.
