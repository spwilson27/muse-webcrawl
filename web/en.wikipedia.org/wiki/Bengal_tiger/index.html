Bengal tiger
Scientific name: Panthera tigris tigris
Status: Endangered

The Bengal tiger (Panthera tigris tigris) is a tiger population native to the Indian subcontinent, found mainly in India, Bangladesh, Nepal and Bhutan, recognised by its orange coat with black stripes.

Habitat

Bengal tigers inhabit mangrove forests such as the Sundarbans, tropical rainforests, grasslands and deciduous forests, usually near water.

Behaviour

They are solitary apex predators hunting deer, wild boar and buffalo, mostly at night. Males maintain large territories.

Conservation

Classified as endangered, the population is threatened by poaching and habitat loss, though India's Project Tiger reserves have supported recovery.
