Inspector Montalbano (TV series)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The series is filmed in south-eastern Sicily, around Ragusa and Scicli.

Inspector Montalbano (Italian: Il commissario Montalbano) is an Italian police procedural television series based on the detective novels of Andrea Camilleri. It stars Luca Zingaretti as Salvo Montalbano, police chief of the fictional Sicilian town of Vigàta. First broadcast by Rai 2 and later Rai 1 in 1999, the series ran until 2021, producing 37 feature-length episodes, and became one of Italy's most successful television exports.

Setting and characters

Filmed in the Baroque towns of Ragusa Ibla, Scicli, and Modica — with Montalbano's seaside house at Punta Secca — the series blends murder investigations with comedy, Sicilian food, and the detective's long-distance relationship with Livia, his Genoese girlfriend. His team includes the faithful deputy Mimì Augello, the comic desk sergeant Catarella, and the methodical Fazio. The prequel series The Young Montalbano (2012–2015) starred Michele Riondino.

Reception

Episodes regularly drew over ten million viewers in Italy, and the subtitled series reached more than 60 countries, airing on BBC Four in the United Kingdom and MHz in the United States. It is credited with boosting tourism to south-east Sicily and with spreading Camilleri's blend of Italian and Sicilian dialect to a mass audience.
