Novelty item

A novelty item is an object designed to be ingenious, humorous or unusual, whose appeal lies mainly in its novelty rather than practical function.

Examples include lava lamps, whoopee cushions, drinking birds, executive ball clickers and novelty songs and dances. Novelties are often sold as gifts, souvenirs or gag items, and many become collectible fads such as the Pet Rock of 1975.
