List of largest banks

The following list of largest banks in the world is based on total assets as reported in the S&P Global Market Intelligence ranking of the world's 100 largest banks, supplemented by rankings by market capitalisation and by Tier 1 capital (as in The Banker magazine's Top 1000 World Banks). Chinese banks have occupied the top four positions by assets since the mid-2010s.

By market capitalisation

JPMorgan Chase is by a wide margin the largest bank in the world by market capitalisation (over US$600 billion in 2024–2025), followed by Bank of America, ICBC, Wells Fargo, Morgan Stanley, and Goldman Sachs. Rankings by market value differ markedly from asset rankings because investors discount the low returns on equity and perceived asset quality of the large Chinese state banks.

Systemic importance

The Financial Stability Board publishes an annual list of global systemically important banks (G-SIBs), which face extra capital surcharges. JPMorgan Chase has repeatedly been placed alone in the highest surcharge bucket, reflecting its size, complexity, and interconnectedness.
