List of nuclear weapons tests

Nuclear weapons tests are experiments carried out to determine the effectiveness, yield and explosive capability of nuclear weapons. Between 1945 and 2017, more than 2,000 nuclear test explosions were conducted by at least eight nations: the United States (about 1,030 tests), the Soviet Union (about 715), France (210), the United Kingdom (45), China (45), India (6 announced detonations), Pakistan (6) and North Korea (6). Testing began with the Trinity test in New Mexico on 16 July 1945 and peaked in the late 1950s and early 1960s during the Cold War arms race.

The Partial Test Ban Treaty (1963) banned atmospheric, underwater and space tests for its signatories, driving testing underground. The Comprehensive Nuclear-Test-Ban Treaty (CTBT) was opened for signature in 1996; while not formally in force, a global moratorium has largely held since the 1990s, broken only by India and Pakistan (1998) and North Korea (2006–2017).

Test sites included the Nevada Test Site, Bikini and Enewetak atolls, Semipalatinsk (Kazakhstan), Novaya Zemlya, Maralinga and Monte Bello (Australia), Mururoa and Fangataufa (French Polynesia), Lop Nur (China), Pokhran (India), Chagai (Pakistan) and Punggye-ri (North Korea). Israel is widely believed to have been involved in the 1979 Vela incident, which remains officially unresolved.
