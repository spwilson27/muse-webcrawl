List of rivers by length

This is a list of the longest rivers on Earth. River lengths are approximate: sources differ depending on where a river's source and mouth are defined, and whether tributary systems are counted. The Nile and the Amazon are the two longest, at roughly 6,650 and 6,400–7,000 km respectively; the Amazon discharges far more water than any other river.

Measurement difficulties

Length figures vary because rivers braid, meander and shift; the "source" may be a seasonal stream; and delta mouths are ambiguous. The Nile-versus-Amazon question is the most famous dispute: some surveys extending the Amazon's source to the Mantaro or Apurímac headwaters produce figures exceeding the Nile's.
