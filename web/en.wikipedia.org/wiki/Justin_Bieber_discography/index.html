Justin Bieber discography

Canadian singer Justin Bieber has released seven studio albums, two remix albums, three compilation albums, five extended plays, and dozens of singles. Discovered through his YouTube covers in 2008 and signed by Usher and Scooter Braun, Bieber became one of the best-selling music artists of all time, with estimated sales of over 150 million records worldwide.

Selected singles

Among Bieber's most successful singles are "Baby" (2010), "What Do You Mean?" (2015), "Sorry" (2015), "Love Yourself" (2015), "Despacito" (remix, with Luis Fonsi and Daddy Yankee, 2017) — which tied the record for most weeks at number one on the Billboard Hot 100 at the time — "Peaches" (2021), and the collaboration "Stay" with the Kid Laroi (2021), one of the most-streamed songs of all time. His 2015 album Purpose made him the first artist to occupy the top three positions of the UK Singles Chart simultaneously.

Other releases

Bieber's first EP, My World (2009), introduced the singles "One Time" and "One Less Lonely Girl". Compilation and remix releases include My Worlds: The Collection (2010), Never Say Never: The Remixes (2011), and the acoustic album Believe Acoustic (2013). The EP Freedom followed in 2021.
