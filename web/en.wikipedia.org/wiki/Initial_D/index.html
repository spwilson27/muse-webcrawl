Initial D

Initial D
Japanese manga series by Shuichi Shigeno
Serialised 1995–2013, 48 volumes
Genres: action, sports (street racing)

Initial D is a Japanese street racing manga series written and illustrated by Shuichi Shigeno, serialised in Kodansha's Weekly Young Magazine from 1995 to 2013 and collected in 48 volumes. It was adapted into several anime television series and films, arcade and console games, and a 2005 Hong Kong live-action film.

Plot

The story centres on Takumi Fujiwara, a quiet teenager who delivers tofu in the early morning for his father's shop, driving a Toyota Sprinter Trueno AE86 over the mountain passes of Gunma. Unbeknownst to most, his years of deliveries have made him a prodigiously skilled downhill racer. Drawn into the world of illegal street racing teams, Takumi defeats a succession of rivals and eventually joins the expedition team Project D, led by Ryosuke Takahashi.

Reception and influence

The series is credited with popularising drifting and the AE86 worldwide; its anime adaptations are also famous for their Eurobeat soundtracks. A sequel series, MF Ghost, began in 2017.
