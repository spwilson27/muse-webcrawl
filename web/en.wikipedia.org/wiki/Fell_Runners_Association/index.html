Fell Runners Association

The Fell Runners Association (FRA) is the governing body for fell running in England, affiliated to UK Athletics. The sport involves running and racing over upland country, often off marked paths, and emphasises navigation, endurance and self-reliance in mountainous terrain.

History

The association was formed in 1970 to promote and regulate the traditional sport of fell racing, which has roots in the Lake District and other upland areas of northern England. It publishes an annual calendar of races, sets safety and environmental guidelines, and organises the English Fell Running Championships.

Race categories

FRA races are graded by ascent and distance, from category A (the most severe) to category C, and by length as long, medium or short. Well-known events include the Ben Nevis Race, the Three Peaks Race and the Borrowdale Fell Race.
