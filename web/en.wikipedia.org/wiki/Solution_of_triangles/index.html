Solution of triangles


Solution of triangles is the determination of all sides and angles of a triangle from partial data.

The solution of triangles (Latin: solutio triangulorum) is the main trigonometric problem of finding all the sides and angles of a triangle when some of these are known. It has applications in astronomy, surveying, navigation, and engineering.

Plane triangles

A plane triangle has six main elements: three sides and three angles. Standard solution cases are: three sides (SSS), two sides and the included angle (SAS), one side and two angles (ASA), two sides and a non-included angle (SSA, which may be ambiguous), and a right triangle with known parts. The tools are the law of sines, the law of cosines, and the fact that the angles sum to 180 degrees.

Spherical triangles

On a sphere, triangles are solved with spherical trigonometry, using the spherical law of sines, the spherical law of cosines, and Napier's rules for right-angled spherical triangles. Spherical solutions were historically central to celestial navigation and geodesy.
