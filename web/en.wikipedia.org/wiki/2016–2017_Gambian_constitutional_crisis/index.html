2016–2017 Gambian constitutional crisis

The 2016–2017 Gambian constitutional crisis followed the December 2016 presidential election, in which opposition candidate Adama Barrow defeated long-time president Yahya Jammeh. Jammeh initially conceded, then rejected the result a week later and refused to step down.


Date: 9 December 2016 – 21 January 2017
Location: The Gambia
Outcome: Jammeh exiled; Barrow inaugurated

Course

The Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS) mediated and, after the UN Security Council backed its position, threatened military intervention. Barrow was sworn in at the Gambian embassy in Dakar, Senegal, on 19 January 2017. ECOWAS troops entered the country largely unopposed, and Jammeh left into exile in Equatorial Guinea on 21 January.

Significance

The crisis is often cited as a successful regional defence of an election result, restoring constitutional order without major bloodshed.
