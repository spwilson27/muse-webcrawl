TQL Stadium is a soccer-specific stadium in Cincinnati, Ohio, United States. It is the home of FC Cincinnati, a Major League Soccer (MLS) club. The stadium opened on May 16, 2021, with a regular-season match between FC Cincinnati and Inter Miami CF. It is located in the West End neighborhood of Cincinnati, just northwest of downtown, on the site of the former Stargel Stadium.

The stadium's naming rights are held by Total Quality Logistics (TQL), a freight brokerage and third-party logistics company headquartered in the Cincinnati area. The naming rights agreement was announced in April 2021, shortly before the stadium opened.

History

FC Cincinnati began play in the United Soccer League in 2016 at Nippert Stadium, on the campus of the University of Cincinnati, where the club drew record crowds for a lower-division American soccer team. As part of its successful bid to join Major League Soccer as an expansion team, the club committed to building a soccer-specific stadium. After a contentious site selection process involving several neighborhoods, the West End site was chosen in 2018, and the club was awarded an MLS expansion franchise in May 2018.

Construction began in December 2018. The stadium was designed by Populous, the Kansas City-based architecture firm responsible for numerous sports venues worldwide. The total project cost was approximately $250 million, privately financed by the club's ownership group.

Design

TQL Stadium has a capacity of 26,000, making it one of the largest soccer-specific stadiums in the United States. Its most distinctive feature is the exterior facade, composed of vertical fins fitted with LED lighting that can display moving imagery and patterns, including the club's orange and blue colors. The design was intended to evoke the motion of a lion's mane, referencing FC Cincinnati's lion crest.

The stadium includes a safe-standing supporters' section known as The Bailey in the north end, which holds about 3,100 fans and is among the steepest and largest single-tier supporters' sections in MLS. A continuous seating bowl wraps the pitch, and roughly half of the seats are covered by a canopy roof. The stadium also features wide concourses, several premium club spaces, and a grand staircase entrance on its east side.

Events

In addition to FC Cincinnati matches, TQL Stadium has hosted international soccer, including matches of the United States men's and women's national teams. It was a venue for the 2023 CONCACAF Gold Cup, hosting matches including a quarterfinal. The stadium has also hosted concerts and community events.

FC Cincinnati won the Supporters' Shield, awarded to the MLS team with the best regular-season record, in 2023 while playing its home matches at TQL Stadium.
