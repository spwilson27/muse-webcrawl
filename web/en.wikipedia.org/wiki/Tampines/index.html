Tampines

Tampines, a major residential town in the East Region of Singapore

Tampines is a planning area and residential town located in the East Region of Singapore. It is the third largest new town in Singapore by area and the third most populous, after Bedok and Jurong West. Tampines serves as the regional centre for the East Region, housing commercial, retail and transport facilities that serve the eastern part of the island.

History

In the early 1900s, Tampines was a military training area with forests, swamps and sand quarries. The area took its name from the tempinis tree (Streblus elongatus), which grew abundantly there. Development of the new town began in 1978 under the Housing and Development Board (HDB), and residents moved into the first flats in the early 1980s. The town was developed in phases as neighbourhoods, each centred on local amenities.

In 1992, Tampines New Town received the World Habitat Award from the Building and Social Housing Foundation of the United Nations, in recognition of its town planning and design.

Geography and demographics

Tampines is bordered by Pasir Ris to the north, Bedok to the south, Paya Lebar to the west and Changi to the east. The planning area covers roughly 2,089 hectares. Its population numbers in the hundreds of thousands, making it one of Singapore's largest residential concentrations.

Amenities and transport

The town centre is anchored by shopping malls such as Tampines Mall, Century Square and Tampines 1, together with Our Tampines Hub, Singapore's first integrated community and lifestyle hub, which opened progressively from 2016 and houses a stadium, library, swimming pools and hawker centre. Tampines is served by the East West MRT line at Tampines station, the Downtown line at Tampines West, Tampines East and Tampines North stations, and a bus interchange. The Tampines Expressway (TPE) and Pan Island Expressway (PIE) pass near the town.

Education

Educational institutions in Tampines include Temasek Polytechnic, the Singapore University of Technology and Design is nearby in Changi, and numerous primary and secondary schools including St. Hilda's, Poi Ching School and Tampines Junior College (now part of Meridian Junior College arrangements historically).
