Logan is a 2017 American superhero film featuring the Marvel Comics character Wolverine, played by Hugh Jackman. Directed by James Mangold, it is the tenth film in the X-Men film series and the third and final Wolverine solo film, following X-Men Origins: Wolverine (2009) and The Wolverine (2013).

Plot

In 2029, no new mutants have been born for 25 years. An ageing Logan, his healing powers fading, works as a limousine driver on the Texas–Mexico border while caring for the ailing Professor Charles Xavier, whose seizures are dangerously uncontrolled. Logan is persuaded to escort a young girl, Laura — a mutant created from his DNA by the Transigen corporation — to a supposed sanctuary called Eden in North Dakota, pursued by the cyborg Donald Pierce and the Reavers. The journey ends at the Canadian border, where Logan dies defending Laura and a group of escaped mutant children.

Reception

The film was acclaimed for its performances, emotional depth and Western-inflected tone, and became the first superhero film nominated for the Academy Award for Best Adapted Screenplay. It marked Jackman's and Stewart's farewell to their roles after 17 years.
