Hacettepe University

Hacettepe University is a leading state university in Ankara, Turkey. It was established on 8 July 1967 and consistently ranks among the top Turkish universities, particularly for medicine and health sciences. Its motto is "Daha ileriye... En iyiye..." ("Further ahead... Towards the best...").

History

The university grew out of the Child Health Institute founded in 1957 by Professor İhsan Doğramacı within Ankara University, followed by a school of medicine in 1963 and a children's hospital. In 1967 these institutions were combined into Hacettepe University. Doğramacı was its founding rector.

Campuses and structure

The university operates principally from two campuses: the Sıhhiye campus in central Ankara, housing the medical faculties and hospitals, and the Beytepe campus to the west of the city, housing most other faculties. Hacettepe comprises numerous faculties — including medicine, engineering, literature, economics and administrative sciences, fine arts, and health sciences — along with graduate institutes, vocational schools, and a major teaching hospital system serving tens of thousands of students.
