ArmaLite

ArmaLite is an American small-arms engineering company founded in 1954 as a division of the Fairchild Engine and Airplane Corporation. It is best known for designs by Eugene Stoner, including the AR-10 and AR-15 rifles.

History

Working in Hollywood, California, Stoner's team developed rifles using aircraft-style aluminium alloys and synthetic materials unusual for the time. ArmaLite sold the rights to the AR-10 and AR-15 to Colt in 1959; the AR-15 was adopted by the US military as the M16. The original company changed hands several times and ceased operating in the 1980s; the brand was revived in 1996 by Mark Westrom, and the company now produces rifles for civilian, law-enforcement, and military markets.

Products

Historically significant designs include the AR-5 and AR-7 survival rifles, the AR-10 battle rifle, the AR-15, and the AR-18. The "AR" in these names stands for ArmaLite Rifle.
