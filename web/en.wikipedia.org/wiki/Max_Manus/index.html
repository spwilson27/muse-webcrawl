Max Manus

Max Manus (9 December 1914 – 20 September 1996) was a Norwegian resistance fighter during the Second World War, specialising in sabotage in occupied Norway and a founding member of the Norwegian Independent Company 1 (Kompani Linge).

Resistance work

After fighting as a volunteer in the Winter War in Finland, Manus returned to Norway and became one of the most effective saboteurs in the resistance. He co-founded the Oslogjengen (the Oslo Gang) and carried out attacks on German ships and installations, including the sinking of ships in Oslo harbour using limpet mines.

Later life

After the war Manus wrote several bestselling memoirs, including Det blir alvor and Det er alvor. His life was dramatised in the 2008 film Max Manus: Man of War.
