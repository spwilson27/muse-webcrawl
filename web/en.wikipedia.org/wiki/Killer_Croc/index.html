Killer Croc, real name Waylon Jones, is a fictional supervillain appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, most commonly as an adversary of Batman. Created by writer Gerry Conway and artist Gene Colan, he first appeared in 1983. Born with a rare genetic condition giving him reptilian skin, Jones was abused and mocked as a child, grew into a life of crime in Gotham City, and over later depictions mutated further into a huge, crocodile-like brute dwelling in the city's sewers.

Character biography

Waylon Jones was born with a form of regressive atavism aggravated by a retrovirus, leaving him with thick scaly skin, sharpened teeth and immense strength. Raised by an abusive, alcoholic aunt in a Florida swamp town, he killed her as a teenager and drifted into sideshow wrestling as "Killer Croc" before moving to Gotham, where he sought to become the city's crime kingpin — a campaign that put him in conflict with Batman and saw him incarcerated in Arkham Asylum. In later storylines a virus administered by the villains Hush and the Riddler further mutated him, increasing his size, strength and savagery at the cost of his humanity. Despite his brutality, stories occasionally stress his longing for acceptance and his occasional role as protector of Gotham's outcasts.

In other media

Killer Croc is a recurring villain in Batman: The Animated Series and numerous animated films and video games, including the Batman: Arkham series. He made his live-action film debut in Suicide Squad (2016), played by Adewale Akinnuoye-Agbaje under heavy prosthetic make-up.
