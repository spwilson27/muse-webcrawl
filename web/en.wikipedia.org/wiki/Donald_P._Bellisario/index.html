Donald P. Bellisario

Donald Paul Bellisario (born 1935) is an American television producer and screenwriter who created or co-created series including Magnum, P.I., Quantum Leap, JAG and NCIS.

Career

After service in the US Marine Corps and work in advertising, Bellisario joined television in the 1970s, writing for Battlestar Galactica and working with Glen A. Larson before forming Belisarius Productions.

Series

Magnum, P.I. (1980), Airwolf, Quantum Leap (1989), JAG (1995) and its spin-off NCIS (2003) made his shows among the longest-running American dramas; many feature military settings reflecting his service.

Personal life

Bellisario's daughter Troian Bellisario is an actress; he endowed programmes at Penn State, his alma mater.
