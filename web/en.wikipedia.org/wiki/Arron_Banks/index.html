Arron Banks

Arron Fraser Andrew Banks (born 22 March 1966) is a British businessman and political donor. He co-founded the insurance company Brightside Group and was the largest financial backer of the Brexit campaign group Leave.EU during the 2016 EU referendum.

Business career

Banks built his fortune in insurance, founding or acquiring ventures including Southern Rock Insurance in Gibraltar and Eldon Insurance in the UK. His business and political activities have attracted regulatory scrutiny, including investigations by the Electoral Commission and the National Crime Agency into the source of his referendum donations; the NCA concluded in 2019 that there was no evidence of criminal offences.

Politics

Formerly a Conservative donor, Banks switched to UKIP in 2014 and funded Leave.EU, co-founded with Richard Tice. He co-wrote The Bad Boys of Brexit, an account of the campaign.
