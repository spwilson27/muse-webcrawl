List of prime ministers of Italy

The Prime Minister of Italy (President of the Council of Ministers) is the head of government. The office dates from the Kingdom of Italy (1861); since 1946 its holders have led the Italian Republic, often for short periods because of coalition politics.

Facts

Italy has had more than 60 governments since 1946. The prime minister is appointed by the President of the Republic and must win confidence votes in both houses of Parliament.
