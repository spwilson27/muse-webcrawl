Marge Simpson

Marjorie Jacqueline "Marge" Simpson (née Bouvier) is a fictional character in the American animated sitcom The Simpsons. Voiced by Julie Kavner, she is the wife of Homer Simpson and the mother of Bart, Lisa, and Maggie, known for her distinctive tall blue beehive hairstyle, green strapless dress, and gravelly voice.

Marge debuted with the rest of the family in The Tracey Ullman Show short "Good Night" on April 19, 1987, and became a central figure of the half-hour series from 1989. Creator Matt Groening named her after his mother, Margaret Groening. Within the show she serves as the family's moral center and a devoted homemaker, though storylines have explored numerous jobs and interests, including police officer, realtor, pretzel franchisee, painter, and bodybuilder.

Reception

Marge is one of the most widely recognized television mothers; Kavner won a Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Voice-Over Performance for the role in 1992. The character has been featured on extensive merchandise and appeared in a 1990 cover story in People and a playful 2009 Playboy cover marking the show's twentieth anniversary.
