Duke of Sutherland is a title in the Peerage of the United Kingdom, created in 1833 for George Leveson-Gower, 2nd Marquess of Stafford, shortly before his death. The dukes were among the wealthiest families in 19th-century Britain, owning vast estates in Staffordshire, Shropshire and the Scottish Highlands.

History

The 1st Duke's wife, Elizabeth Sutherland, Countess of Sutherland, inherited the Sutherland estates in Scotland. The family is associated with the Highland Clearances, during which many tenants were removed from the Sutherland estates in the early 19th century — a lasting source of controversy. Their seats included Trentham Hall, Stafford Park and Dunrobin Castle in Sutherland.

The current holder is Francis Egerton, 7th Duke of Sutherland, who succeeded his godfather's line in 2000; he is also Earl of Ellesmere. The heir apparent is James Granville Egerton, Marquess of Stafford.
