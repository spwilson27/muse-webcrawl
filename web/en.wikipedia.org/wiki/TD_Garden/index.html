TD Garden

TD Garden is a multi-purpose arena in Boston, Massachusetts. It is the home arena of the Boston Celtics of the National Basketball Association and the Boston Bruins of the National Hockey League.

History

The arena opened on September 30, 1995, replacing the Boston Garden, which had stood since 1928. It is named after its sponsor, TD Bank, and sits atop the North Station rail terminal in downtown Boston.

Features

The arena has a seating capacity of about 19,156 for basketball and 17,850 for hockey. It hosts concerts, conventions, and other sporting events, and is connected to a large mixed-use development known as The Hub on Causeway.
