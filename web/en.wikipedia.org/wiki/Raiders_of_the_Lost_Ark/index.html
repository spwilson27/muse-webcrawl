Raiders of the Lost Ark

Raiders of the Lost Ark is a 1981 American action-adventure film directed by Steven Spielberg from a screenplay by Lawrence Kasdan, based on a story by George Lucas and Philip Kaufman. It stars Harrison Ford as archaeologist Indiana Jones, who in 1936 races Nazi agents to recover the Ark of the Covenant, believed to make an army invincible. Karen Allen co-stars as Marion Ravenwood, with Paul Freeman as the rival archaeologist René Belloq.

Plot

Hired by US Army intelligence, Jones travels from Nepal to Cairo to find the headpiece of the Staff of Ra, which reveals the location of the Well of Souls where the Ark is buried. Pursued by Gestapo agent Toht and opposed by Belloq, Jones recovers the Ark in Egypt, loses it to the Nazis, and finally witnesses its opening on an Aegean island, where its supernatural power destroys the Germans. The Ark is then crated away into a vast government warehouse.

Production

Lucas conceived the film as an homage to the serials of the 1930s and 1940s. Principal photography took place in England, France, Tunisia and Hawaii on a budget of about $20 million. John Williams composed the score, including the famous "Raiders March".

Reception and legacy

The film was the highest-grossing release of 1981 and won five Academy Awards. It launched the Indiana Jones franchise and is widely regarded as one of the greatest adventure films ever made; in 1999 it was selected for the US National Film Registry. It was later marketed as Indiana Jones and the Raiders of the Lost Ark.
