Rogaland

Rogaland is a county in Western Norway, bordering the North Sea to the west and the counties of Vestland, Telemark, and Agder. Its administrative centre is Stavanger, Norway's fourth-largest city. Rogaland has a population of roughly 480,000 and covers about 9,400 square kilometres.

Geography

The county includes the flat agricultural district of Jæren, the fjords of Ryfylke — including the Lysefjord with the famous Preikestolen (Pulpit Rock) and Kjeragbolten — and the islands of Boknafjorden, such as Karmøy. Hafrsfjord, near Stavanger, is traditionally the site of the naval battle around 872 in which Harald Fairhair united Norway into one kingdom.

Economy

Stavanger is Norway's petroleum capital, hosting the headquarters of Equinor and major oil service companies serving the North Sea fields. The county is also a major agricultural area — Jæren is among Norway's most productive farming districts — and has significant hydropower, fishing, and aquaculture industries.
