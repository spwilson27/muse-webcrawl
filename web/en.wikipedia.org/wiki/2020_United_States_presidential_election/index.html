2020 United States presidential election

The 2020 United States presidential election was held on 3 November 2020. Democrat Joe Biden, running with Kamala Harris, defeated incumbent Republican President Donald Trump and Vice President Mike Pence.


Date: 3 November 2020
Winner: Joe Biden / Kamala Harris (D)
Electoral vote: Biden 306, Trump 232
Popular vote: Biden 81,283,501 (51.3%), Trump 74,223,975 (46.8%)
Turnout: ~66.6%, highest since 1900

Campaign

Held during the COVID-19 pandemic, the election saw record mail-in and early voting. Biden campaigned on the pandemic response, healthcare and restoring norms; Trump emphasised the pre-pandemic economy, law and order and deregulation.

Aftermath

Trump refused to concede, alleging widespread fraud without evidence; dozens of lawsuits failed. On 6 January 2021, during Congress's certification of Biden's victory, a mob of Trump supporters attacked the US Capitol. Biden was inaugurated on 20 January 2021; Harris became the first woman, first Black and first South Asian vice president.
