ISO 216

ISO 216
Subject: Paper sizes
Based on: DIN 476 (1922)
Key series: A, B

ISO 216 is an international standard for paper sizes, used in most countries except the United States, Canada and parts of Latin America. It was first published in 1975 and is based on the German DIN 476 standard of 1922.

The A series

The A series is built on an aspect ratio of 1:√2, so that cutting a sheet in half parallel to its shorter side yields two sheets of the same proportions. A0 has an area of exactly one square metre (841 × 1189 mm); each step halves the area: A1, A2, A3, and A4 (210 × 297 mm), the most widely used office paper size in the world. The B series provides intermediate sizes as geometric means of adjacent A sizes, and ISO 269 defines the C series for envelopes.

Adoption

A sizes are standard for letters, documents, books and printing in Europe, Asia and most other regions; North America instead uses Letter, Legal and related ANSI/ASME sizes.
