Black Russian

TypeCocktail
Base spiritVodka
IngredientsVodka, coffee liqueur
ServedOn the rocks, in an old fashioned glass

The Black Russian is a cocktail of vodka and coffee liqueur. It is traditionally made by pouring the two ingredients over ice cubes in an old fashioned glass and stirring gently.

History

The drink was created in 1949 by Gustave Tops, a bartender at the Hotel Metropole in Brussels, in honour of Perle Mesta, the United States ambassador to Luxembourg. The name combines "Black" from the dark coffee liqueur and "Russian" from the vodka. It belongs to the family of after-dinner coffee cocktails and is listed among the International Bartenders Association's official cocktails.

Variations

Adding cream produces a White Russian. Other variations adjust the liqueur or add cola (a "Dirty Black Russian") or stout. The usual proportions are five parts vodka to two parts coffee liqueur, though many recipes use equal parts.
