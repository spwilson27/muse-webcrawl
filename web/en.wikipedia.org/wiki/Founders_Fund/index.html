Founders Fund is an American venture capital firm based in San Francisco, California. Founded in 2005 by PayPal co-founders Peter Thiel, Ken Howery and Luke Nosek, the firm is known for backing ambitious technology companies at early stages and for a contrarian investment philosophy — summarised in its manifesto "What Happened to the Future?" — that favours transformative engineering over incremental consumer internet products.

Investments

Founders Fund was the first institutional investor in SpaceX and an early backer of companies including Facebook, Palantir Technologies, Airbnb, Spotify, Stripe, Lyft, Neuralink, Anduril Industries and Affirm. The firm's partners — several of them members of the so-called "PayPal Mafia" — raised a succession of ever-larger funds, from a $50 million first fund in 2005 to multi-billion-dollar growth funds in the 2020s, and the firm has managed well over $10 billion. Investments span aerospace, defence technology, biotechnology, artificial intelligence and energy.

Approach

The firm emphasises founder-friendly terms, stage-agnostic investment and willingness to fund sectors other venture firms avoid, most notably defence and space. Its partners include Keith Rabois, Brian Singerman, Delian Asparouhov and Trae Stephens.
