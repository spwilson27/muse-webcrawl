Overview

FRELIMO (the Mozambique Liberation Front) is a political party and former liberation movement that has governed Mozambique since independence from Portugal in 1975. Founded in Dar es Salaam in 1962 by the merger of exile groups under Eduardo Mondlane, it launched the Mozambican War of Independence in 1964, fighting from bases in Tanzania and Zambia.

From movement to party

After Mondlane's assassination by parcel bomb (1969), Samora Machel led FRELIMO to power under the Lusaka Accord following Portugal's 1974 revolution. As a Marxist-Leninist vanguard party it ran a one-party state through the civil war against RENAMO (to 1992), then won every multiparty election since 1994, amid opposition allegations of fraud — most sharply after the disputed 2024 election, which brought Daniel Chapo to the presidency and triggered mass protests and violence. FRELIMO remains a member of the Socialist International and of the Former Liberation Movements network of southern Africa.
