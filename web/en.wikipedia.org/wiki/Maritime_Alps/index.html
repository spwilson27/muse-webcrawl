Maritime Alps

The Maritime Alps (French: Alpes Maritimes; Italian: Alpi Marittime) are a mountain range in the southwestern part of the Alps, forming the border between France and Italy near the Mediterranean coast. They extend from the Col de Tende toward the Ligurian Alps.

Geography

The highest summit is Monte Argentera at 3,297 metres in Italy. The range is protected by Mercantour National Park in France and the Maritime Alps Natural Park in Italy, home to ibex, chamois, wolves, and the rock engravings of the Vallée des Merveilles. The climate grades from Alpine summits to Mediterranean slopes within a short distance of the Côte d'Azur.
