Creatures Inc. is one of the co-owners of the Pokémon franchise.

Creatures Inc. is a Japanese video game development company affiliated with Nintendo and The Pokémon Company. It was founded on 8 November 1989 by Shigesato Itoi as Ape Inc., and is best known as one of the three co-owners of the Pokémon franchise, alongside Nintendo and Game Freak.

History

As Ape Inc., the company developed the Mother series of role-playing games for Nintendo, beginning with Mother (1989) and including the internationally released EarthBound (1994). In 1995 the company was renamed Creatures Inc. and became involved in the planning and development of Pokémon products.

Activities

Creatures is responsible for producing the Pokémon Trading Card Game, modelling Pokémon in 3D for various titles in the series, and developing spin-off games such as Pokémon Ranger and Detective Pikachu. Together with Nintendo and Game Freak, it is a co-owner of The Pokémon Company, founded in 1998 to manage the franchise.
