Almere is a planned city and municipality in the province of Flevoland, Netherlands, across the IJmeer from Amsterdam. It is the largest city in Flevoland and one of the largest municipalities in the Netherlands by population. Almere was built entirely on land reclaimed from the IJsselmeer in the 20th century, making it one of the youngest cities in the country.

History

The land on which Almere stands was reclaimed as part of the Zuiderzee Works. The first houses were completed in 1976 in what is now Almere Haven, and the city was formally established as a municipality on 1 January 1984. It was named after the early medieval name for the Zuiderzee, Almere or Aelmere, a large lake mentioned by Boniface in the 8th century.

Almere was planned as a polynuclear city: instead of one centre, it was designed with several distinct districts, each with its own centre, separated by green belts. The districts include Almere Stad, Almere Haven, Almere Buiten, Almere Hout, Almere Poort and Almere Pampus.

Geography

Almere lies on the southern Flevoland polder, roughly 5 metres below sea level. It is bordered by the Markermeer to the northwest, the Gooimeer and IJmeer to the west, and the province of North Holland across the Hollandse Brug bridge. Waterways such as the Lage Vaart and Hoge Vaart cross the municipality.

Architecture

Because the city is so young, Almere became a showcase for contemporary Dutch architecture. Notable buildings include the Kunstlinie Almere (formerly Schouwburg Almere) by SANAA, the wave-shaped residential blocks of Het Carré, and experimental self-build housing in the Homeruskwartier. Each year the city hosts a Floriade horticultural exhibition site once a decade; the Floriade 2022 was held in Almere.

Transport

Almere is served by six railway stations on the Flevolijn and Weesp–Lelystad lines, with direct trains to Amsterdam Centraal and Lelystad. The A6 and A27 motorways pass the city. An extensive separate bus network connects the districts.

Sports

The city's professional football club is Almere City FC, which earned promotion to the Eredivisie for the first time in 2023. The city is also known for triathlon events, including the Challenge Almere-Amsterdam, one of the oldest long-distance triathlons in the world.
