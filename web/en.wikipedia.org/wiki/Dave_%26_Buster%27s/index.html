Dave & Buster's Entertainment, Inc. is an American restaurant and entertainment business headquartered in Coppell, Texas. Each Dave & Buster's location combines a full-service restaurant and bar with a large video arcade and midway-style games, aimed primarily at adults. The company operates well over 150 locations across the United States and Canada.

History

The first Dave & Buster's opened in Dallas, Texas, in 1982, founded by David Corriveau, who ran an arcade business, and James "Buster" Corley, who operated a restaurant nearby. The two combined their businesses into a single large venue offering food, drinks, and games under one roof. The company expanded through the 1990s and 2000s, went through private-equity ownership, and returned to public markets with an initial public offering in 2014, trading on Nasdaq under the symbol PLAY.

Concept

Locations typically feature a dining area and sports bar alongside the "Million Dollar Midway" arcade, where guests play video games and redemption games using rechargeable Power Cards. Tickets won can be exchanged for prizes in the Winner's Circle prize shop. The venues also host parties, corporate events, and sports viewing, screening major pay-per-view and league events.
