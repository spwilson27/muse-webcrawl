Grue (monster)

The grue is a fictional predatory creature that dwells in darkness, originating in the Zork series of interactive fiction games by Infocom in the early 1980s. In Zork I (1980), players who wander into pitch-black areas without a light source are warned: "It is pitch black. You are likely to be eaten by a grue." Lingering in darkness results in the grue devouring the player.

Origins

The term was invented by the Zork implementers, drawing loosely on the word "gruesome". The creature's habits are described in the fictional Encyclopedia Frobozzica: grues are sinister, lurking creatures whose jaws and claws threaten travellers in darkness, but which flee from light, so that a lit lantern suffices to keep them at bay.

Cultural legacy

The grue and its warning message became one of the most famous elements of text-adventure gaming. The phrase "You are likely to be eaten by a grue" has been widely referenced in games, internet culture, and programming humour; grues have appeared or been alluded to in later games including NetHack tributes, Dungeons & Dragons-inspired works, and numerous homage titles.
