BMW i5

BMW i5 (G60/G61)
Manufacturer: BMW
Production: 2023–present
Class: Executive car
Body styles: Saloon, Touring estate
Powertrain: Battery electric

The BMW i5 is the battery electric version of the eighth-generation BMW 5 Series (G60), launched in 2023. It is offered as a saloon and, from 2024, as a Touring estate — the first electric 5 Series wagon.

Variants

The range includes the rear-wheel-drive eDrive40 (340 hp), the dual-motor xDrive40 and the M60 xDrive flagship with 601 hp. The battery has about 81 kWh usable capacity, giving WLTP ranges of roughly 500–580 km depending on variant, with DC charging up to 205 kW.

Technology

The i5 shares BMW's flexible CLAR platform with petrol, diesel and plug-in hybrid 5 Series models, and features the BMW Curved Display, iDrive 8.5/9 and optional hands-free motorway driving assistants in some markets.
