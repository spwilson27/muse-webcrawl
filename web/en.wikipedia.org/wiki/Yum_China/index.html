Yum China

Yum China Holdings, Inc. is a Chinese Fortune 500 restaurant company, headquartered in Shanghai, that operates the KFC, Pizza Hut and Taco Bell brands in mainland China under exclusive licence, together with domestic brands including Little Sheep, Huang Ji Huang, East Dawning and COFFii & JOY. It is the largest restaurant company in China by restaurant count.

History

KFC entered China in 1987, opening its first restaurant near Tiananmen Square in Beijing, and Pizza Hut followed in 1990. The China division of Yum! Brands grew into by far the group's largest market. On 1 November 2016 Yum China was spun off from Yum! Brands as an independent, publicly traded company, accompanied by investments from Primavera Capital Group and Ant Financial. The company obtained a primary listing on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in 2020.

Operations

Yum China operates well over ten thousand restaurants across more than 1,900 cities and towns, the great majority under the KFC brand, which it has adapted heavily to local tastes with items such as congee, youtiao and rice dishes. Pizza Hut is the leading Western casual-dining brand in China, and Taco Bell re-entered the market in 2016. The company has emphasised digital ordering, membership programmes and delivery, and operates its own logistics network of distribution centres.
