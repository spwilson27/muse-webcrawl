My Little Pony: The Movie is a 2017 animated musical fantasy film based on the animated television series My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic, which was developed as part of the fourth incarnation of Hasbro's My Little Pony franchise. The film was directed by Jayson Thiessen and written by Meghan McCarthy and Rita Hsiao.

Plot

The ponies of Equestria prepare for their first Friendship Festival in Canterlot, overseen by Princess Twilight Sparkle. The celebration is interrupted by an invasion of airships commanded by Tempest Shadow, a unicorn with a broken horn working for the Storm King, who seeks to steal the magic of the four princesses to empower his staff and conquer Equestria. The princesses are turned to stone with obsidian orbs, but Twilight escapes with her friends — Applejack, Rainbow Dash, Pinkie Pie, Fluttershy, Rarity, and the dragon Spike.

Following a hint from Princess Celestia, the group journeys beyond Equestria seeking the help of the hippogriffs of Mount Aris. Along the way they escape the desert town of Klugetown with the help of a con-artist cat named Capper, are rescued from pirates by Captain Celaeno's crew, and reach the undersea kingdom of Seaquestria, where Queen Novo transforms them into seaponies. When Twilight's desperate attempt to steal Novo's transformation pearl fails, the group is expelled, and Twilight is captured by Tempest.

Her friends return to Canterlot during the Storm King's assault. Tempest, betrayed by the Storm King, is rescued by Twilight and turns against him; the Storm King is defeated and petrified by his own staff, and the princesses are restored. Tempest reveals her real name, Fizzlepop Berrytwist, and the Friendship Festival resumes.

Voice cast

The film features the series' regular voice cast — Tara Strong (Twilight Sparkle), Ashleigh Ball (Applejack and Rainbow Dash), Andrea Libman (Pinkie Pie and Fluttershy), Tabitha St. Germain (Rarity), and Cathy Weseluck (Spike) — alongside guest stars Emily Blunt (Tempest Shadow), Kristin Chenoweth (Princess Skystar), Liev Schreiber (the Storm King), Michael Peña (Grubber), Sia (Songbird Serenade), Taye Diggs (Capper), and Zoe Saldana (Captain Celaeno).

Music

Songs were composed by Daniel Ingram, including "We Got This Together", "I'm the Friend You Need", "Time to Be Awesome", "One Small Thing", and "Open Up Your Eyes". Sia contributed the end-credits song "Rainbow".

Reception

The film grossed $61.3 million worldwide against a $6.5 million budget. Reviews were mixed, with praise for the animation and voice performances and criticism of the plot.
