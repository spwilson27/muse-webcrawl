Dog walking

TypePet care service / recreational activity

Dog walking is the act of a person walking with a dog, typically from the dog's residence and then returning. Leashes are commonly used for this purpose. It is a widely practised activity with one's own pet, and a professional service offered by dog walkers for owners who are at work or otherwise unavailable.

Benefits

Walking provides dogs with exercise, mental stimulation through new sights and smells, opportunities for socialisation with other dogs and people, and regular toilet breaks. For owners, dog walking is a form of moderate physical exercise associated with lower blood pressure and improved cardiovascular health. The required frequency and duration of walks vary by breed, age, and health; active breeds may need substantially more exercise than sedentary or elderly dogs.

Professional dog walking

Professional dog walkers may walk dogs individually or in groups, and are often employed through pet-care businesses or app-based platforms. Some municipalities regulate commercial dog walking in public parks, limiting the number of dogs per walker or requiring permits. Pet sitting and dog walking form part of the growing pet services industry in many countries.

Laws and etiquette

Many jurisdictions require dogs to be leashed in public places and require owners or walkers to clean up after their dogs. Designated off-leash dog parks provide enclosed spaces where dogs may run freely.
