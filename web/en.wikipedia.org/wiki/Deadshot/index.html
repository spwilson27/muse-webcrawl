Deadshot (Floyd Lawton) is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by Bob Kane, David Vern Reed and Lew Schwartz, he first appeared in Batman #59 (1950) as a would-be vigilante in Gotham City, and was later reimagined in the 1970s as a world-class assassin and marksman who "never misses".

Character biography

Lawton, the black-sheep son of wealthy parents, became the world's deadliest assassin-for-hire, haunted by a death wish and the accidental shooting connected to his family history. He is a core member of the Suicide Squad, serving under Amanda Waller to reduce his sentence, and of the mercenary team the Secret Six. His devotion to his young daughter Zoe is his defining redeeming trait.

In other media

Deadshot was portrayed by Will Smith in the film Suicide Squad (2016), and has appeared in the Arrow television series (Michael Rowe), the animated Justice League, and the Batman: Arkham video games.
