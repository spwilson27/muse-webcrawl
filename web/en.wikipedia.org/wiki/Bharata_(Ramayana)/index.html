Bharata (Ramayana)

Bharata is a character in the Hindu epic Ramayana, the younger brother of the hero Rama and the son of King Dasharatha of Ayodhya and his second queen, Kaikeyi. He is revered as an exemplar of brotherly devotion, loyalty and selfless duty.

Role in the epic

When Kaikeyi, manipulated by her maid Manthara, demanded that Bharata be crowned king and Rama exiled for fourteen years, Bharata was absent from Ayodhya. On his return he was horrified by his mother's actions, refused the throne, and journeyed to the forest to beg Rama to return. When Rama declined, bound by his father's word, Bharata took Rama's sandals (paduka) back to Ayodhya, placed them on the throne, and ruled as regent from the village of Nandigrama for the fourteen years of exile.

Significance

Bharata's regency, in which he governed in Rama's name without claiming kingship, is one of the most celebrated episodes of the Ramayana and a central theme in devotional tradition. On Rama's return, Bharata joyfully surrendered the kingdom and served as his brother's devoted counsellor.
