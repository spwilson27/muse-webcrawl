Alexander Igorevich Rybak (born 13 May 1986) is a Norwegian singer, songwriter, violinist and actor of Belarusian origin. He won the Eurovision Song Contest 2009 for Norway with his song "Fairytale", achieving the highest score in the contest's history at the time under the voting system then in use (387 points).

Early life

Rybak was born in Minsk and emigrated to Norway with his parents at the age of four. Trained in violin and piano from childhood, he performed with orchestras across Europe and won the Norwegian talent show Kjempesjansen in 2004 and the Anders Jahre Culture Prize in 2006.

Eurovision and career

"Fairytale", which Rybak wrote and performed with fiddle playing and folk dancers, topped charts across Europe in 2009. His debut album Fairytales followed, and he went on to release further albums including No Boundaries (2010) and Christmas Tales (2012). He returned to Eurovision in 2018 with "That's How You Write a Song", winning Norway's Melodi Grand Prix and finishing 15th in the final in Lisbon. Rybak has also acted (notably in the film Yohan: The Child Wanderer), voiced Hiccup in the Norwegian dub of How to Train Your Dragon, and written a children's book, Trolle og den magiske fela (2015).
