Santa Fe Complex

The Santa Fe Complex (SFC) was a community art space and non-profit organisation in Santa Fe, New Mexico, dedicated to art, science and technology collaborations, digital media, and community engagement. Founded around 2007–2008 in the Santa Fe Railyard area, it hosted exhibitions, workshops, lectures and interdisciplinary projects.

Santa Fe Complex
Location: Santa Fe, New Mexico, US
Type: Non-profit arts / media lab
Focus: Art, science, technology

Activities

The Complex organised talks and events on complex systems, visualisation, and digital fabrication, drawing on the intellectual community around the nearby Santa Fe Institute. It ran youth programmes in media and technology and community projects such as digital storytelling.

Context

Santa Fe, the New Mexico state capital, is noted for its art market and research institutions, including Los Alamos National Laboratory nearby.
