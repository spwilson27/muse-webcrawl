The Knights Templar (Poor Knights of Christ and of the Temple of Solomon) were a Catholic military order founded about 1119 to protect pilgrims to the Holy Land after the First Crusade. They became an international military and financial power, building castles and running an early banking network.

Fall

After the fall of the Crusader states (1291), the order lost its purpose; King Philip IV of France, deep in debt to them, had the Templars arrested in 1307 on heresy charges, and Pope Clement V dissolved the order in 1312. Grand Master Jacques de Molay was burned in 1314. Legends of Templar treasure and curses have flourished ever since.
