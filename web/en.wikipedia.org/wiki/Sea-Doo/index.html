Sea-Doo is a Canadian brand of personal watercraft (PWC) and boats manufactured by Bombardier Recreational Products (BRP). Sea-Doo is a leading brand in the personal watercraft market, competing with Kawasaki's Jet Ski and Yamaha's WaveRunner lines.

History

The original Sea-Doo was launched in 1968 by Bombardier, based on an invention developed with Laurentian investor Clayton Jacobson II, who went on to create the stand-up Jet Ski for Kawasaki. The early sit-down craft used an air-cooled engine and jet drive but was discontinued in 1970. Bombardier relaunched the Sea-Doo in 1988 with a water-cooled Rotax engine and modern hull, helping to create the mass recreational PWC market of the 1990s.

Models and technology

The range has included two- and three-seat runabouts, performance models such as the RXP and RXT series, touring craft, and the entry-level Spark introduced in 2014. Sea-Doo pioneered features such as the iBR intelligent brake and reverse system, which allows the craft to stop under jet thrust and improves low-speed handling.
