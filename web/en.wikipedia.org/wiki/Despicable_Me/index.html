Despicable Me is a 2010 American animated comedy film produced by Illumination Entertainment and distributed by Universal Pictures. It was directed by Pierre Coffin and Chris Renaud and stars the voices of Steve Carell as the supervillain Gru, alongside Jason Segel, Russell Brand, Julie Andrews, Will Arnett, Kristen Wiig and Miranda Cosgrove.

Gru with his army of small, yellow, goggle-eyed Minions.

Plot

Gru, a supervillain who delights in all things wicked, hatches a plan to steal the Moon. Encouraged by his elderly assistant Dr. Nefario and his army of Minions, he adopts three orphaned girls — Margo, Edith and Agnes — as part of the scheme to steal a shrink ray from his rival Vector. But the girls gradually melt his heart, forcing him to choose between his greatest heist and his newfound family; in the climax he rescues them and gives up the Moon, embracing fatherhood.

Reception and legacy

The film was a major commercial success, grossing about US$543 million worldwide on a US$69 million budget, and received positive reviews for its humour and heart. It launched one of the most successful animated franchises of all time: sequels Despicable Me 2 (2013), Despicable Me 3 (2017) and Despicable Me 4 (2024), plus the Minions spin-off films, and made the gibberish-speaking Minions global pop-culture icons.
