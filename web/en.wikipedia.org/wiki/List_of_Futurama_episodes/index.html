Futurama is an American animated science fiction sitcom created by Matt Groening. The series follows Philip J. Fry, a pizza delivery boy cryogenically frozen in 1999 and thawed in the year 2999, who joins the Planet Express delivery company in New New York. It originally aired on Fox from 1999 to 2003, was revived by Comedy Central from 2008 to 2013 following four direct-to-video films, and was revived again by Hulu in 2023.
