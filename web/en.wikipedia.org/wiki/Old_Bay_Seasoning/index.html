Old Bay Seasoning

Old Bay Seasoning is an American blend of herbs and spices, strongly associated with the Chesapeake Bay region and seafood cookery. Marketed by McCormick & Company, it is traditionally sprinkled on steamed blue crabs, shrimp, and other seafood, and has become a regional culinary icon of Maryland.

History

The blend was created in 1939 in Baltimore by Gustav Brunn, a German Jewish immigrant who had fled Nazi Germany and founded the Baltimore Spice Company. It was named after the Old Bay Line, a passenger steamship line that operated on the Chesapeake Bay. McCormick acquired the brand in 1990.

Ingredients and uses

The mixture's listed ingredients include celery salt, black pepper, crushed red pepper flakes, paprika, and a variety of other spices such as mustard, cardamom, and bay leaf. Beyond seafood, it flavors fried chicken, corn on the cob, potato chips, popcorn, and even beer and ice cream in Maryland-area promotions.
