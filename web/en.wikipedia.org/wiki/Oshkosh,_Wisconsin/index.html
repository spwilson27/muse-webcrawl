Oshkosh, Wisconsin

Oshkosh is a city in Winnebago County, Wisconsin, United States, situated on the western shore of Lake Winnebago where the Fox River enters the lake. It is the county seat and had a population of about 67,000 at the 2020 census. The city was named for Chief Oshkosh of the Menominee Nation and grew in the nineteenth century as a lumber centre — its sawmills earned it the nickname "Sawdust City" — before a devastating fire in 1875 led to rebuilding in brick.

Economy and culture

Modern Oshkosh is known as the headquarters of the Oshkosh Corporation, a manufacturer of speciality and military trucks founded in 1917, and of OshKosh B'gosh, the children's clothing company, which originated in the city in 1895. The University of Wisconsin–Oshkosh, founded in 1871, is one of the larger campuses in the UW system.

EAA AirVenture

Each summer Oshkosh hosts EAA AirVenture Oshkosh, the world's largest general aviation gathering, organised by the Experimental Aircraft Association at Wittman Regional Airport. The week-long fly-in draws more than 600,000 visitors and over 10,000 aircraft, temporarily making the airport's control tower the busiest in the world.
