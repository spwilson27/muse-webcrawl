The 1992 Summer Olympics, officially the Games of the XXV Olympiad, were held in Barcelona, Catalonia, Spain, from July 25 to August 9, 1992. They were the first Summer Games after the end of the Cold War and were widely praised for their organisation, urban regeneration and festive atmosphere. The opening ceremony featured archer Antonio Rebollo lighting the Olympic cauldron by firing a flaming arrow over it.

Sporting highlights

The United States men's basketball “Dream Team”, the first Olympic team of NBA stars, won gold with ease and transformed global basketball. Former Soviet republics competed as the Unified Team and topped the medal table. South Africa returned after the end of apartheid-era exclusion, Germany competed as a reunified nation, and Linford Christie, Derartu Tulu and Hassiba Boulmerka produced memorable athletics victories.

Legacy

Barcelona used the Games to rebuild waterfront districts, ring roads and Montjuïc facilities, becoming a model—admired and debated—for Olympic-led urban transformation. The Games are often remembered as the start of the fully professional, commercially mature modern Olympics.
