List of countries by Global Militarization Index

The Global Militarization Index (GMI) is an index compiled by the Bonn International Center for Conflict Studies (BICC) that measures the relative weight of a country's military apparatus in relation to its society as a whole. It combines indicators such as military expenditure as a share of GDP and of government spending, military and paramilitary personnel relative to population and to physicians, and heavy weapons per capita. Scores are scaled so that a higher value indicates a more militarised society; the index typically covers more than 150 countries.

Methodology

The GMI weighs three categories of indicators: expenditure (military spending as a proportion of GDP and of total government expenditure, compared with health spending), personnel (active military and paramilitary personnel and reserves relative to population, and relative to the number of physicians), and heavy weapons (numbers of tanks, armoured vehicles, combat aircraft, helicopters, submarines and major naval vessels per capita) held by the armed forces.
