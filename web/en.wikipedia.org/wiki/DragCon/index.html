RuPaul's DragCon is an annual expo and convention celebrating drag culture, the television series RuPaul's Drag Race, and LGBTQ popular culture. Produced by World of Wonder, the production company behind Drag Race, the first DragCon was held in Los Angeles in May 2015; a New York City edition launched in 2017. It is billed as the world's largest celebration of drag culture.

Events

The convention brings together hundreds of drag performers — principally queens from the many international editions of Drag Race — who meet fans at booths along the show floor. Programming includes panel discussions on drag, make-up, fashion, activism and queer history; make-up and merchandise exhibitors; a "Queen's Walk" entrance and pink carpet; and main-stage performances. Panels with political figures and discussions of LGBTQ rights and representation are a regular feature, and the event markets itself as family-friendly, with a kids' zone and drag story hours.

Attendance and expansion

Attendance grew rapidly, with tens of thousands of visitors across each multi-day event and an associated economic footprint in the tens of millions of dollars. A digital edition, Digital DragCon, was streamed in 2020 when in-person events were cancelled due to the COVID-19 pandemic. In 2024 the franchise expanded to the United Kingdom with DragCon UK in London.
