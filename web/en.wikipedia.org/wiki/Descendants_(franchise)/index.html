Descendants (franchise)

Descendants is an American media franchise centred on a series of musical fantasy television films produced by Disney Channel. The films follow the teenage children of classic Disney heroes and villains in the kingdom of Auradon and the Isle of the Lost. Directed and choreographed by Kenny Ortega, the franchise stars Dove Cameron as Mal (daughter of Maleficent), Cameron Boyce as Carlos (son of Cruella de Vil), Booboo Stewart as Jay (son of Jafar), and Sofia Carson as Evie (daughter of the Evil Queen).

Films

  • Descendants (2015) — Ben, son of Belle and the Beast, invites four villain kids to attend Auradon Prep; the film was watched by millions on its premiere and launched hit songs such as "Rotten to the Core" and "If Only".
  • Descendants 2 (2017) — Mal returns to the Isle of the Lost, where Uma (China Anne McClain), daughter of Ursula, has risen to power.
  • Descendants 3 (2019) — the villain kids confront Audrey and Hades; the film is dedicated to Cameron Boyce, who died in July 2019 shortly before its release.
  • Descendants: The Rise of Red (2024) — a new chapter for Disney+ and Disney Channel starring Kylie Cantrall as Red, daughter of the Queen of Hearts, and Malia Baker as Chloe Charming.

Other media

The franchise includes the animated short-form series Descendants: Wicked World (2015–2017), the prequel novel The Isle of the Lost by Melissa de la Cruz and its sequels, junior novelisations, a live-action short Under the Sea: A Descendants Short Story (2018), and the animated special Descendants: The Royal Wedding (2021). Its soundtracks have topped the Billboard charts, and the franchise became a staple of Disney Channel programming for a generation of viewers.
