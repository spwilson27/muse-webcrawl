CAF Awards

CAF Awards
Presented by: Confederation of African Football
First awarded: 1992
Top honour: African Footballer of the Year

The CAF Awards are an annual awards ceremony presented by the Confederation of African Football (CAF) recognising the best African footballers, coaches, clubs and national teams of the year. The headline prize is the African Footballer of the Year for men and women.

History

The African Footballer of the Year award was first presented by CAF in 1992, succeeding the France Football award given since 1970. Samuel Eto'o and Yaya Touré each won the men's prize four times; Nigeria's Asisat Oshoala has won the women's award a record number of times.

Categories

Categories include men's and women's player, young player, goalkeeper, coach, club and national team of the year, as well as goal of the year. Winners are chosen by votes from coaches, captains, technical experts and media.
