FIS

FIS most commonly refers to the International Ski and Snowboard Federation (French: Fédération Internationale de Ski et de Snowboard), the international governing body for skiing and snowboarding, founded in 1924 and headquartered in Oberhofen am Thunersee, Switzerland.

The FIS governs international skiing and snowboarding competition.
