Saint Stephen's Day

Saint Stephen's Day is a Christian saint's day commemorating Saint Stephen, the first Christian martyr, celebrated on 26 December in the Latin Church and 27 December in Eastern Christianity. In many countries it is a public holiday falling on the day after Christmas Day.

Traditions

In Ireland the day is known as Lá Fhéile Stiofáin and is traditionally associated with the Wren Hunt, in which costumed "wrenboys" parade through towns. In the United Kingdom and Commonwealth countries the same date is celebrated as Boxing Day.

Regional observances

Saint Stephen's Day is a public holiday in, among others, Ireland, Austria, Germany (as Zweiter Weihnachtsfeiertag), the Czech Republic (First and Second Christmas holidays), Croatia, Serbia, Finland and parts of Spain such as Catalonia, where it is called Sant Esteve.
