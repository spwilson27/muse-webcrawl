TuneIn is an American audio streaming service and media company based in San Francisco, California. It provides live and on-demand radio, sports, news, music and podcasts to listeners worldwide through its website, mobile applications, smart speakers, connected cars and hundreds of other devices. The service aggregates streams from well over 100,000 radio stations and millions of podcast programmes.

History

The company was founded in Dallas, Texas, in 2002 by Bill Moore as RadioTime, originally a guide and tuner technology for radio station streams. It rebranded as TuneIn and relocated to the San Francisco Bay Area, growing rapidly with the spread of smartphones. TuneIn offers a free, advertising-supported tier and a premium subscription that adds commercial-free news and music stations, premium sports coverage such as live play-by-play, and fewer advertisements.

Content and legal issues

TuneIn's catalogue spans local AM/FM stations, international broadcasters, and online-only stations, alongside live coverage of leagues such as the NFL, MLB, NBA and English Premier League in certain territories. The service has faced litigation over music licensing: in a widely followed United Kingdom case brought by major record labels, courts ruled in 2019 that TuneIn required licences for linking to foreign radio stations carrying recorded music to UK users, a judgment largely upheld on appeal, leading TuneIn to restrict access to many international stations in the UK.
