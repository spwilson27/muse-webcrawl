The Daily Telegraph is a British daily broadsheet newspaper published in London, founded in 1855 as the Daily Telegraph and Courier. Long Britain's best-selling quality paper, it is the flagship of Telegraph Media Group and is editorially centre-right; its 2009 MPs' expenses investigation is one of the great modern British scoops.

History and scoops

The Telegraph pioneered the penny quality paper and later the modern sports and obituaries pages. The 2009 expenses disk story forced resignations across Parliament. The Barclay brothers bought the paper in 2004; a contested sale process in the 2020s, involving a UAE-backed bid blocked by new foreign-state-ownership rules, kept its future in the news.
