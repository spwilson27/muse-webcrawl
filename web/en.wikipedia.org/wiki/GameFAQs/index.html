GameFAQs

GameFAQs is a website that hosts user-contributed FAQs, walkthroughs, guides, cheat codes, reviews and save files for video games, as well as discussion boards. It was created in 1995 by Jeff "CJayC" Veasey and has been owned by a succession of media companies, currently Fandom.

History

Veasey started the site in 1995 as the Video Game FAQ Archive, originally hosted as a hobby project. Renamed GameFAQs, it grew on the strength of plain-text walkthroughs written by volunteers — a format that remained the site's hallmark long after illustrated guides became standard elsewhere. In 2003 the site was sold to CNET Networks; Veasey stepped down in 2007, succeeded by Allen "SBAllen" Tyner, who ran the site for over 16 years.

GameFAQs passed through CBS Interactive to Red Ventures (2020) and then to Fandom (2022), when Fandom acquired a group of entertainment and gaming sites including GameSpot, Giant Bomb and TV Guide.

Content and community

The site's database covers tens of thousands of games. Users contribute FAQs and guides, maps, cheat codes, reviews and user ratings, and vote in the long-running daily "Poll of the Day". The message boards are among the oldest large gaming communities on the web; the secret board LUE and the current-events board 261 became notorious parts of early internet culture.

Since 2002 GameFAQs has run a popular "Character Battle" popularity contest; the 2003 winner was Sephiroth and the 2013 contest famously won by the Dota/Long-time-underdog Draven, while the site also runs an annual "Best. Game. Ever." bracket. GameFAQs data — release dates, user scores and guides — is deeply embedded in gaming culture, and the site remains one of the most visited video-game resources on the internet.
