Radiator Springs Racers is a slot car dark ride attraction at Disney California Adventure at the Disneyland Resort in Anaheim, California. The attraction is based on Pixar's Cars franchise and is the centrepiece of Cars Land, which opened on June 15, 2012.

Ride experience

Guests board six-passenger convertible vehicles for a scenic tour through Ornament Valley before entering the town of Radiator Springs, encountering characters including Lightning McQueen, Mater, Doc Hudson and Luigi. At Luigi's Casa Della Tires or Ramone's paint shop, riders are prepared for a race; two vehicles then compete side-by-side over outdoor hills and banked turns, reaching about 40 mph.

Development

The ride uses a ride system derived from Epcot's Test Track, combined with extensive Audio-Animatronics and a large rockwork mountain range modelled on Cadillac Range peaks. It was the most expensive attraction in the Cars Land expansion, itself the largest part of the park's billion-dollar redevelopment.
