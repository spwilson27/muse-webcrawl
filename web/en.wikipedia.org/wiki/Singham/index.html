Singham is a 2011 Indian Hindi-language action film directed by Rohit Shetty and produced by Reliance Entertainment. A remake of the 2010 Tamil film Singam, it stars Ajay Devgn in the title role of Inspector Bajirao Singham, alongside Kajal Aggarwal and Prakash Raj, who plays the antagonist.

Plot

Bajirao Singham is an honest and upright police inspector posted in the border village of Shivgad. He is confronted by the corrupt crime lord and politician Jaikant Shikre, who terrorises the region of Goa. When a fellow officer is driven to suicide after being falsely accused by Shikre, Singham vows to bring him to justice. The conflict escalates into a battle between Singham's integrity and Shikre's network of bribery and intimidation, culminating in Singham exposing Shikre's crimes before the public.

Reception

The film was a commercial success at the box office and was praised for its action sequences and Devgn's performance. It spawned the sequel Singham Returns (2014) and became the starting point of Shetty's Cop Universe franchise, later joined by Simmba (2018) and Sooryavanshi (2021).
