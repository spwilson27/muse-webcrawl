Alberto Juantorena Danger (born 3 December 1950) is a Cuban former track and field athlete and athletics administrator. At the 1976 Montreal Olympics he became the first and still the only athlete to win both the 400 metres and 800 metres Olympic titles at the same Games.

Athletics career

A tall, long-striding runner nicknamed "El Caballo" ("The Horse"), Juantorena had originally played basketball before turning to athletics in his twenties. In Montreal he won the 800 m in a world record of 1:43.50, then returned days later to win the 400 m in 44.26. He lowered the 800 m world record to 1:43.44 in Sofia in 1977, and also won silver medals in the 4 × 400 m relay at the 1976 Games and golds at the Pan American Games.

Administration

After retiring in the mid-1980s Juantorena rose to senior posts in Cuban sport, serving as president of the Cuban Athletics Federation and as a vice-president of the Cuban Olympic Committee. Internationally he served on the council of the IAAF (now World Athletics) and as one of its vice-presidents, becoming a prominent figure in global athletics governance.
