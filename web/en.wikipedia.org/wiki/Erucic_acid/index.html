Erucic acid

Erucic acid
IUPAC name(Z)-Docos-13-enoic acid
FormulaC22H42O2
Molar mass338.57 g/mol
TypeMonounsaturated omega-9 fatty acid

Erucic acid is a monounsaturated omega-9 fatty acid with 22 carbons, abundant in the seeds of Brassicaceae plants such as rapeseed and mustard. Its name derives from Eruca, the genus of rocket (arugula).

Sources and uses

Traditional rapeseed oil contained up to 50% erucic acid; concerns over myocardial lipidosis in animal studies led breeders to develop low-erucic cultivars—commercially canola (under 2%). High-erucic rapeseed oil remains valuable industrially as a lubricant and as feedstock for erucamide slip agents in plastic films.

Health regulation

The European Food Safety Authority set a tolerable daily intake of 7 mg/kg body weight; regulators cap erucic acid levels in edible oils (e.g. 5% in the EU, historically). Erucic acid is also a component of Lorenzo's oil, used experimentally for adrenoleukodystrophy.
