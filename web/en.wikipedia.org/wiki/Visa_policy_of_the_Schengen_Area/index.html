Visa policy of the Schengen Area


Area: 29 European countries (Schengen Area)
Instrument: Common Schengen visa
Typical stay: Up to 90 days in any 180-day period

The visa policy of the Schengen Area is the common set of rules governing entry for short stays in the European states that have abolished internal border controls. A visa issued by any Schengen member is generally valid for travel throughout the area.

Framework

Nationals of listed "Annex II" countries may enter visa-free for short stays, while nationals of "Annex I" countries must obtain a uniform Schengen visa in advance. Freedom of movement within the area applies to EU/EEA and Swiss citizens and their family members under separate rules.

Schengen visa

The standard short-stay (type C) visa permits stays of up to 90 days within any 180-day period for tourism, business or family visits, but not employment. Applications are lodged with the member state of main destination, and entries are recorded under the Entry/Exit System being rolled out across the area.
