The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt is a 2015 action role-playing game developed and published by Polish studio CD Projekt Red. It is the sequel to The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings and is based on the fantasy novel series by Andrzej Sapkowski.

Gameplay and plot

Players control Geralt of Rivia, a monster hunter for hire known as a Witcher, in an open world based on Slavic mythology. Geralt searches for his adopted daughter Ciri, who is pursued by the spectral Wild Hunt, while the Northern Kingdoms are invaded by the Nilfgaardian Empire. The game features branching choices with lasting consequences, two large expansions (Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine), and the card game Gwent.

Reception

The game received universal acclaim, won over 250 Game of the Year awards, and has sold more than 50 million copies, making it one of the best-selling video games of all time. It is widely regarded as one of the greatest video games ever made.
