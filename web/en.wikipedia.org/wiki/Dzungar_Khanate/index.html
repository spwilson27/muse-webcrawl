Dzungar Khanate

Dzungar Khanate
Existed: 1634–1758
Capital: Ghulja (Ili)
People: Oirat Mongols
Religion: Tibetan Buddhism

The Dzungar Khanate, also written Zunghar Khanate, was an Inner Asian khanate of Oirat Mongol origin. At its greatest extent it covered present-day Xinjiang, Kyrgyzstan, eastern Kazakhstan, and parts of Mongolia and Siberia, and was the last great nomadic empire in Asia.

History

Founded in the 1630s by Erdeni Batur, the khanate consolidated the Dzungar and other Oirat tribes in the Ili valley and Dzungaria. Under Galdan Boshugtu Khan it expanded into the Tarim Basin and fought the Qing dynasty and the Khalkha Mongols. Under Tsewang Rabtan and Galdan Tseren it remained a major power contesting Tibet, notably intervening in Lhasa in 1717.

Fall

Succession struggles after 1745 weakened the khanate. The Qianlong Emperor launched campaigns in 1755–1758; Amursana's revolt failed, and Qing conquest was accompanied by disease and mass killing — the Dzungar genocide — after which Dzungaria was repopulated and incorporated into Xinjiang.
