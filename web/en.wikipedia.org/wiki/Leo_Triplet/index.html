Leo Triplet

The Leo Triplet (also known as the M66 Group) is a small group of galaxies located in the constellation Leo, about 35 million light-years from Earth. It consists of the spiral galaxies M65, M66, and NGC 3628.

ConstellationLeo
Distanceabout 35 million light-years
MembersM65, M66, NGC 3628
Other nameM66 Group

Members

M66 is the largest and brightest member, an intermediate spiral galaxy showing asymmetric spiral arms distorted by gravitational interaction. M65 is a tightly wound spiral that appears less disturbed. NGC 3628, sometimes called the Hamburger Galaxy, is seen edge-on and displays a prominent dust lane and a long tidal tail of stars drawn out by past encounters.

Interactions

The three galaxies are gravitationally interacting. The tidal tail of NGC 3628 and the warped structure of M66 are evidence of close passages, and the group forms the core of the larger M66 Group of galaxies.
