XL Recordings

XL Recordings is a British independent record label founded in 1989 by Tim Palmer, Nick Halkes and Richard Russell. Originally an offshoot of Beggars Group's Citybeat label devoted to dance music, it became one of the most successful independent labels of its era, known for a small roster and a string of critically acclaimed, commercially successful releases.

History

XL launched as a dance and rave imprint, releasing early records by artists including SL2 and The Prodigy, whose albums Experience (1992) and Music for the Jilted Generation (1994) made the label a force in British electronic music. Richard Russell took sole charge of the label in the 1990s and broadened its scope. In the 2000s XL released landmark albums by Dizzee Rascal, The White Stripes (in the UK), Basement Jaxx, M.I.A. and Radiohead, whose In Rainbows (2007) was licensed to XL for its physical release.

Adele and later years

The label's biggest commercial success came with Adele, whose albums 19 (2008), 21 (2011) and 25 (2015) — the latter two among the best-selling albums of the 21st century — were released through XL and its partner Columbia in some territories. Other notable artists on the roster have included The xx, Vampire Weekend, Tyler, the Creator (for some releases), King Krule, Jai Paul, Sampha and Peggy Gou. XL is noted for maintaining a compact roster of around a handful of active artists at a time, and for Richard Russell's own music project, Everything Is Recorded.
