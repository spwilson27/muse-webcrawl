Warcraft

Warcraft is a franchise of video games, novels, and other media created by Blizzard Entertainment. The series began with the real-time strategy game Warcraft: Orcs & Humans in 1994 and is set primarily on the world of Azeroth, where the human-led Alliance and the orc-led Horde contend with each other and with cosmic threats.

Games

The real-time strategy trilogy comprises Warcraft: Orcs & Humans (1994), Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness (1995) and Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos (2002), the last with its expansion The Frozen Throne. In 2004 Blizzard launched the massively multiplayer online role-playing game World of Warcraft, which became the most successful MMORPG of all time and has received numerous expansions. The digital card game Hearthstone (2014) is also set in the Warcraft universe.

Other media and legacy

The franchise includes dozens of novels and comics and the 2016 feature film Warcraft, directed by Duncan Jones. Warcraft III's modding community produced the custom map Defense of the Ancients, which founded the multiplayer online battle arena genre. The series is noted for its influence on real-time strategy design, online gaming culture and transmedia storytelling.
