Mylonite

Mylonite is a fine-grained, compact metamorphic rock produced by dynamic recrystallization of the constituent minerals resulting in a reduction of the grain size of the rock. Mylonites can have many different mineralogical compositions; classification is based on texture and deformation.

Mylonites form deep in the crust where temperature and pressure allow ductile deformation, typically within shear zones associated with major faults. They are characterised by a strongly developed foliation and porphyroclasts — surviving fragments of the original rock.

Formation

Strain accumulates under high strain rates; grain-size reduction occurs by crystal-plastic processes. With increasing strain, protomylonite passes to mylonite and ultramylonite.
