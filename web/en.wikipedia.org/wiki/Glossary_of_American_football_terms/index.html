Glossary of American football terms

The following terms are used in American football, both conventional and in American football slang.

Blitz: A defensive play in which more players than usual rush the quarterback in an attempt to sack him. Down: One of a series of four attempts (downs) by the offense to advance the ball at least ten yards. End zone: The area at each end of the field into which the ball must be carried or caught to score a touchdown.

Fumble: Loss of possession of the ball by a player who had control of it. Hail Mary pass: A very long forward pass, typically thrown in desperation near the end of a game. Line of scrimmage: The imaginary line through the ball, parallel to the goal lines, from which each play begins. Pocket: The protected area behind the offensive line within which the quarterback sets up to pass. Sack: Tackling the quarterback behind the line of scrimmage while he still holds the ball. Snap: The act of the center handing or passing the ball backward to the quarterback to begin a play. Two-point conversion: An attempt after a touchdown to score two points by advancing the ball into the end zone from close range instead of kicking an extra point.
