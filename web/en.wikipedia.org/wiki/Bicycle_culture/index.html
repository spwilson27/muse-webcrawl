Bicycle culture

ScopeSocial attitudes and practices around cycling
Notable centresNetherlands, Denmark

Bicycle culture refers to the social norms, infrastructure, fashions and communities that develop around cycling in a place. In countries such as the Netherlands and Denmark, cycling is an ordinary, mainstream mode of transport for all ages.

Elements

Strong bicycle cultures typically combine protected cycle networks, secure parking, traffic laws that protect riders, and cultural acceptance of cycling in everyday clothing. Advocacy movements, critical-mass rides, bike-sharing schemes and cargo-bike use all reinforce the culture.

History

The safety bicycle boom of the 1890s first made cycling a mass cultural phenomenon, influencing women's emancipation and dress reform. After mid-20th-century decline in many car-oriented countries, urban cycling revived from the 1970s onward, driven by environmental and health concerns.
