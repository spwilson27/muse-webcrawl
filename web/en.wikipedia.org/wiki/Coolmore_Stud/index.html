Coolmore Stud

Coolmore Stud, in Fethard, County Tipperary, Ireland, is the headquarters of one of the world's largest breeding operations of thoroughbred racehorses. The operation, founded in the 1970s by John Magnier in partnership with trainer Vincent O'Brien and Robert Sangster, now encompasses stud farms in Ireland, the United States (Ashford Stud, Kentucky), and Australia.

History

Coolmore was originally a small farm owned by Tim Vigors. The partnership of Magnier, O'Brien, and Sangster transformed it through the purchase of expensive yearlings at Keeneland sales, whose progeny justified very high stud fees. Stallion Sadler's Wells, a son of Northern Dancer, became the dominant European sire of his era and cemented Coolmore's standing.

Operation

Coolmore stallions shuttle between hemispheres so they can cover mares in both the northern and southern breeding seasons. Famous sires have included Danehill, Galileo, and Montjeu. The associated racing stable, Ballydoyle, trained by Aidan O'Brien, campaigns horses in the colours of the Magnier–Tabor–Smith partnership.
