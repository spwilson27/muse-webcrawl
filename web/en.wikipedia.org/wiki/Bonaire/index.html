Bonaire

Bonaire is an island in the Leeward Antilles in the southern Caribbean Sea, lying about 80 kilometres off the coast of Venezuela. Together with Aruba and Curaçao it forms the ABC islands. Since 10 October 2010 Bonaire has been a special municipality of the Netherlands, part of the Caribbean Netherlands along with Sint Eustatius and Saba (the BES islands).

Geography

The island covers about 288 square kilometres and has a dry, sunny climate tempered by trade winds. Its capital is Kralendijk. Bonaire is ringed by a fringing coral reef that has been protected as the Bonaire National Marine Park since 1979, making it a world-renowned shore-diving destination. The uninhabited islet of Klein Bonaire lies just off the western coast.

Economy and nature

Tourism, particularly diving, is the main industry; solar salt production at the Pekelmeer pans in the south is also significant, and the pink-stained salt pans are a feeding habitat for American flamingos, whose sanctuary in the south of the island hosts one of the Caribbean's largest breeding colonies. The population is about 20,000, and Papiamentu, Dutch, English and Spanish are widely spoken.
