The National Hockey League (NHL) is a professional ice hockey league in North America comprising 32 teams, 25 in the United States and 7 in Canada, and is the world's premier professional ice hockey league. Founded in Montreal in 1917, it evolved from the "Original Six" era (1942–1967) through expansion to today's league, whose players come predominantly from Canada, the US, Sweden, Finland, Russia and Czechia.

Teams play an 82-game regular season; sixteen qualify for the Stanley Cup playoffs, four best-of-seven rounds that many consider the gruelling highlight of the sporting calendar. The Montreal Canadiens' 24 Cups include five straight (1956–1960) and four straight (1976–1979); Wayne Gretzky's Edmonton Oilers won four in the 1980s. No Canadian team won the Cup between Montreal in 1993 and the present drought — a recurring national storyline. The Florida Panthers won their first Cup in 2024 and repeated in 2025, both times beating Edmonton.
