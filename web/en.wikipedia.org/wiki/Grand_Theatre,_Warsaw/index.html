Grand Theatre, Warsaw

[Image: Grand Theatre, Warsaw]

The Grand Theatre (Polish: Teatr Wielki) in Warsaw, Poland, is a theatre and opera complex situated on the historic Theatre Square (Plac Teatralny). It is home to the Polish National Opera and the Polish National Ballet.

History

The theatre was designed by Antonio Corazzi and built between 1825 and 1833 in the neoclassical style, inaugurated on 24 February 1833 with Rossini's The Barber of Seville. Stanisław Moniuszko's operas Halka and The Haunted Manor premiered here. The building was burned during the Warsaw Uprising of 1944; only the façade survived. Rebuilt after the war under Bohdan Pniewski, it reopened on 19 November 1965 as one of the largest stages in Europe.

Today

The theatre houses the Stanisław Moniuszko Grand Auditorium (seating over 1,700), the Emil Młynarski Chamber Hall, and the Polish Theatre Museum.
