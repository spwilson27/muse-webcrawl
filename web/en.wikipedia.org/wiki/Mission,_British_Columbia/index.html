Mission, British Columbia

Mission viewed from across the Fraser River

Mission is a city in the Lower Mainland of British Columbia, Canada, located on the north bank of the Fraser River east of Vancouver, across from Abbotsford. It had a population of about 41,500 at the 2021 census.

History

The townsite grew from St. Mary's Mission, an Oblate mission founded in 1861, in the traditional territory of the Stó:lō peoples. Westminster Abbey, a Benedictine monastery established in Mission in 1939, is a local landmark. Mission is known for the Mission Raceway Park drag strip and for hosting the historic West Coast Express commuter rail terminus.

Geography

The city is set on a forested hillside above the Fraser, with the Stave River and Hatzic Prairie to the east and views south to Mount Baker.
