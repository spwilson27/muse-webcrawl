Juana

Juana is a Spanish feminine given name, the feminine form of Juan, and the Spanish equivalent of Joanna, Jane and Joan. It has been borne by numerous queens, princesses, saints and notable women throughout the Spanish-speaking world.

Notable bearers

Prominent historical figures named Juana include Joanna of Castile (1479–1555), queen of Castile and Aragon, remembered in Spanish as Juana la Loca; Juana Inés de la Cruz (1648–1695), the Mexican nun, poet and scholar regarded as one of the foremost writers of the Spanish Golden Age; and Juana Azurduy (1780–1862), a guerrilla military leader who fought for independence in Upper Peru.

In popular culture

The name appears in literature, music and film across Latin America and Spain, and forms part of many compound names such as Juana María and María Juana. People with the name, and fictional characters, are listed on the related disambiguation and name pages.
