Australian War Memorial

LocationCampbell, Canberra, ACT
Opened11 November 1941
FounderCharles Bean (conceived)

The Australian War Memorial (AWM) is a national war memorial, museum and archive in Canberra, Australia, commemorating Australian service members who have died or participated in wars involving the Commonwealth of Australia. It opened in 1941.

Features

The Byzantine-influenced building contains the Hall of Memory with the Tomb of the Unknown Australian Soldier (interred 1993), the Roll of Honour listing over 103,000 names, a commemorative courtyard and Pool of Reflection, and extensive galleries covering the First and Second World Wars and later conflicts. The Last Post ceremony is held daily.

History

Official historian Charles Bean conceived a memorial-museum after the First World War. The Memorial sits at the northern end of Anzac Parade, aligned in the Parliament House vista. A major redevelopment was undertaken in the 2020s.
