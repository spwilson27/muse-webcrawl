The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air is an American television sitcom created by Andy and Susan Borowitz for NBC. It aired from September 10, 1990, to May 20, 1996, and starred rapper and actor Will Smith as a fictionalised version of himself.

Premise

Will, a street-smart teenager from West Philadelphia, is sent by his mother to live with his wealthy aunt and uncle in their Bel Air mansion after getting into a fight on a local basketball court. The Banks household consists of the stern judge Philip Banks (James Avery), his wife Vivian, their children Hilary, Carlton and Ashley, and their dry-witted English butler Geoffrey (Joseph Marcell). Will's working-class mannerisms clash comically with the family's upper-class lifestyle, while the show also addressed serious topics including race, class, gun violence and absent parents.

Cast and legacy

The role of Vivian was played by Janet Hubert for the first three seasons and by Daphne Maxwell Reid thereafter. Alfonso Ribeiro's Carlton became famous for the "Carlton dance" performed to Tom Jones's "It's Not Unusual". The series made Smith a major television star and launched his film career. A dramatic reimagining, Bel-Air, premiered on Peacock in 2022, and the original cast reunited for a reunion special in 2020.
