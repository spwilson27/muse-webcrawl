List of Parliamentary constituencies in East Sussex

The ceremonial county of East Sussex, which includes the unitary authority of Brighton and Hove, is divided into eight parliamentary constituencies for elections to the House of Commons of the United Kingdom. Following the 2023 Periodic Review of Westminster constituencies, implemented for the 2024 general election, the county's seats were redrawn, with Sussex Weald replacing Wealden and boundary changes affecting several other seats.

History

Historically the county returned members for boroughs including Hastings, Lewes, Seaford, Winchelsea and the notoriously rotten boroughs abolished by the Reform Act 1832. The Cinque Ports of Hastings, Rye and Winchelsea each returned two members before reform. Brighton first returned MPs in 1832 and grew to three seats (Pavilion, Kemptown and, briefly, others). Eastbourne became a seat in 1885. The 2024 election saw the Liberal Democrats win several seats in the county, including Eastbourne and Lewes, while Labour took the Brighton and Hove seats and Hastings and Rye.
