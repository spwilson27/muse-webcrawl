Vlogbrothers

Vlogbrothers is a video blog channel on YouTube. The Internet-based show is created and hosted by brothers John Green and Hank Green. The first incarnation of the brothers' online broadcasting was the "Brotherhood 2.0" project, preceding the establishment of the pair's regular vlogging activity through their shared channel.

Brotherhood 2.0

On 1 January 2007, the Greens launched a year-long experiment in which they communicated only through daily video blogs, abandoning textual communication entirely. The project, called Brotherhood 2.0, built a substantial audience and gave rise to the fan community known as Nerdfighteria, whose motto includes "Don't Forget To Be Awesome" (DFTBA).

Format and content

After 2007, the brothers continued posting videos on alternating weekdays, addressing each other and their audience on topics ranging from current events and science to personal reflections and complexly imagined jokes. Typical videos are under four minutes long, a rule dating to the original project, though longer videos have become more common.

Community and philanthropy

The Vlogbrothers audience, Nerdfighteria, is noted for its charitable activity. The annual Project for Awesome, founded by the Greens in 2007, raises money for charities chosen by the community. The brothers have also co-founded educational channels such as Crash Course and SciShow, the podcast Dear Hank & John, and the e-commerce company DFTBA Records.
