National Football Museum

The National Football Museum is England's national museum of association football. Founded in 2001 and originally based at Deepdale in Preston, it moved to the Urbis building in Manchester city centre in 2012.

Collections

The museum holds major collections of football memorabilia, including items from the FIFA collection and the Football Association collection: the ball from the 1930 World Cup final, the Jules Rimet Trophy replica, and the shirt worn by Geoff Hurst in the 1966 World Cup final. It also maintains the English Football Hall of Fame.

Exhibitions

Permanent and temporary exhibitions trace the history of the game from its folk origins through the formation of the FA in 1863 to the modern Premier League era.
