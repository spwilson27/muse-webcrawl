Bluff

Bluff may refer to several distinct topics:

Places

  • Bluff, New Zealand, a town and seaport in the Southland region of the South Island
  • Bluff, Utah, a census-designated place in San Juan County, Utah, United States
  • Bluff, Queensland, a town in Central Queensland, Australia

Landforms

A bluff is a type of broad, rounded cliff or steep bank, often formed by erosion along a river or coastline. River bluffs line many valleys, notably along the Mississippi River, where towns such as Memphis, Tennessee ("the Bluff City") take their names from them.

Games and deception

  • Bluffing, in poker and other games, the practice of betting or acting as though one holds a stronger position than one actually does
  • Bluff (card game), also known as Cheat, a card game in which players shed cards while making possibly false claims about them

Other uses

  • Bluff (magazine), a former American magazine devoted to poker
  • Bluff Island, several islands in various countries
