Spider (solitaire)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Spider is a type of patience game (solitaire) played with two decks of playing cards. It is one of the most popular two-deck patience games and gained wide fame through its inclusion in Microsoft Windows. The game takes its name from a spider's eight legs, corresponding to the eight foundation piles that must be filled.

Rules

104 cards are dealt into ten tableau piles, with the top card of each pile face up. Cards are built down in descending order regardless of suit, but only sequences of the same suit can be moved together. A complete running sequence from King to Ace of one suit is removed to a foundation. The stock is dealt one card onto each tableau pile at a time, with the restriction that no tableau pile may be empty. The game is won when all eight suit-sequences have been removed. Easier variants use one or two suits instead of four.

Computer versions

A version of Spider has shipped with Windows since the late 1990s, and it remains widely available in card-game collections on many platforms, where its one-, two- and four-suit difficulty levels are standard.
