Superannuation Guarantee

The Superannuation Guarantee (SG) is the part of the Australian compulsory retirement savings system under which employers are required to make superannuation contributions on behalf of their employees, on top of wages and salaries. Administered under the Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992, the system is enforced by the Australian Taxation Office (ATO).

History

Compulsory employer superannuation contributions were introduced in Australia in 1992, following the earlier establishment of award-based superannuation under the Accord agreements of the 1980s. The SG began at a modest rate of 3% or 4% of earnings (depending on the size of the employer) and was legislated to rise progressively. It reached 9% in 2002–03 and remained there until 2013–14, when it began rising again, reaching 12% on 1 July 2025.

Operation

Employers must generally contribute a prescribed percentage of each employee's "ordinary time earnings" to a complying superannuation fund or retirement savings account. Contributions must be paid at least quarterly, by the 28th day following the end of the quarter. Most employees aged 18 or over are covered, as are employees under 18 who work more than 30 hours per week. The former $450-per-month earnings threshold was removed from 1 July 2022, extending SG coverage to lower-paid workers.

Superannuation Guarantee Charge

Where an employer fails to pay the minimum amount by the due date, the shortfall becomes subject to the Superannuation Guarantee Charge (SGC), which is payable to the ATO and consists of the SG shortfall amount, nominal interest, and an administration component. The SGC is deliberately more costly than paying the contribution on time, and unlike SG contributions it is not tax-deductible for the employer.

Maximum contribution base

Employers are not required to pay SG on earnings above a quarterly maximum contribution base, which is indexed annually. Employees may also face concessional contribution caps that limit the amount of tax-advantaged contributions that can be made each year.
