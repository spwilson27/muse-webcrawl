Communes of the Loire department

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Loire department in the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region of France is divided into communes — the smallest administrative division in France. The department, whose prefecture is Saint-Étienne, has around 320 communes grouped into three arrondissements: Montbrison, Roanne and Saint-Étienne.

The department

Loire takes its name from the River Loire, which flows through it from south to north. Its largest city and prefecture is Saint-Étienne, a major industrial and university centre; other significant towns include Roanne, Saint-Chamond, Firminy, Montbrison (a former prefecture and historic capital of the Forez), Rive-de-Gier, Le Chambon-Feugerolles, Andrézieux-Bouthéon, Riorges and Veauche.

Representative communes

The following are among the communes of the department, listed alphabetically:

  • Ambierle, Andrézieux-Bouthéon, Apinac, Arthun
  • Balbigny, Belmont-de-la-Loire, Bourg-Argental, Briennon
  • Charlieu, Chazelles-sur-Lyon, Commelle-Vernay, Cottance
  • Feurs, Firminy, Fraisses
  • La Grand-Croix, Le Coteau, L'Étrat
  • Montbrison, Montrond-les-Bains
  • Noirétable
  • Pélussin, Pouilly-les-Nonains
  • Renaison, Riorges, Rive-de-Gier, Roanne, Roche-la-Molière
  • Saint-Chamond, Saint-Étienne, Saint-Galmier, Saint-Just-Saint-Rambert, Saint-Paul-en-Jarez, Sorbiers
  • Unieux, Veauche, Villars, Violay

The number and boundaries of communes occasionally change through mergers, in which smaller communes combine into a single new commune, a pattern encouraged by national policy across France.
