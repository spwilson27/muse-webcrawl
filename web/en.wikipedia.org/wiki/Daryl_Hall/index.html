Daryl Hall

Daryl Franklin Hohl (born October 11, 1946), known professionally as Daryl Hall, is an American rock, R&B and soul singer and musician. He is best known as the co-founder and principal lead vocalist of Hall & Oates, with guitarist and songwriter John Oates. Hall & Oates are one of the best-selling music duos of all time, with hits including "Rich Girl", "Kiss on My List", "Private Eyes", "Maneater" and "Out of Touch".

Early life

Hall was born in Pottstown, Pennsylvania, into a musical family of German descent, and grew up in the Philadelphia area, absorbing the city's soul and doo-wop traditions. He studied music at Temple University and recorded with groups including the Temptones and the Electric Indians before meeting John Oates at Temple in 1967.

Hall & Oates

The duo signed to Atlantic Records in 1972; after early albums with modest success (though "She's Gone" became a hit on re-release), they moved to RCA and broke through with "Sara Smile" (1976) and the number-one "Rich Girl" (1977). Their peak came in the early 1980s with the albums Voices, Private Eyes and H2O, yielding six US number-one singles. Billboard named them the most successful duo of the rock era, ahead of the Everly Brothers. They were inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2014.

Solo work

Hall's solo albums include Sacred Songs (recorded 1977 with Robert Fripp, released 1980), Three Hearts in the Happy Ending Machine (1986, produced by Dave Stewart, with the hit "Dreamtime"), Soul Alone (1993) and Laughing Down Crying (2011). Since 2007 he has hosted the acclaimed web/TV series Live from Daryl's House, performing with guest artists at his home studio and club in Pawling, New York.

Personal life

Hall is also known for restoring historic houses, a passion documented in the series Daryl's Restoration Over-Hall. In 2023 he filed a lawsuit and obtained a restraining order against John Oates over a proposed sale of their joint business interests, and the duo's active partnership effectively ended, though Hall has continued touring solo.
