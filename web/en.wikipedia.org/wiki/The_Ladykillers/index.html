The Ladykillers

The Ladykillers is a 1955 British black comedy film made by Ealing Studios, directed by Alexander Mackendrick and starring Alec Guinness. It is regarded as one of the finest Ealing comedies and one of the greatest British films.

DirectorAlexander Mackendrick
WriterWilliam Rose
StarringAlec Guinness, Cecil Parker, Herbert Lom, Peter Sellers, Katie Johnson
Release date8 December 1955

Plot

Professor Marcus and his gang of criminals rent rooms from the sweet elderly Mrs Wilberforce, posing as an amateur string quintet while planning a security-van robbery. When she discovers the crime, the gang resolves to kill her — but their attempts unravel into farce, and one by one the criminals dispose of each other, leaving Mrs Wilberforce with the money.

Legacy

William Rose's screenplay won a BAFTA and was Oscar-nominated. The Coen brothers remade the film in 2004 with Tom Hanks, transferring the setting to Mississippi.
