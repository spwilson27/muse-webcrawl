Hydroxyquinol

Hydroxyquinol, systematically benzene-1,2,4-triol, is an organic compound with the formula C6H3(OH)3. It is one of three isomeric benzenetriols, the others being phloroglucinol (1,3,5) and pyrogallol (1,2,3). Hydroxyquinol is a colorless-to-pale solid that is readily oxidized in air and in alkaline solution, turning dark.

Chemistry and occurrence

Hydroxyquinol is the aglycone and hydrolysis product of some plant glycosides and arises in the biodegradation of aromatic compounds: several bacteria funnel diverse plant-derived aromatics through hydroxyquinol on the way to ring cleavage and eventual mineralization. It can be prepared by hydrolysis of its triacetate or from p-benzoquinone chemistry. Like other polyphenols it is a reducing agent and has been studied as an intermediate in organic synthesis and as a metabolite in pathways degrading chloroaromatics and nitrophenols.
