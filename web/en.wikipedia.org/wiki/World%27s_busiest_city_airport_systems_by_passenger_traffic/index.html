World's busiest city airport systems by passenger traffic

The world's busiest city airport systems by passenger traffic ranking aggregates the total passenger numbers of all commercial airports serving a single metropolitan area, rather than of any individual airport. On this measure, multi-airport cities such as London, New York and Tokyo typically lead the world.

Leading systems

London's system—Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted, Luton, London City and Southend—has long been the world's largest by combined passengers, exceeding 170 million in peak pre-pandemic years. The New York system (JFK, Newark, LaGuardia and satellite airports) and Tokyo's (Haneda and Narita) have each handled on the order of 120–130 million passengers annually, followed by systems such as Paris, Shanghai, Los Angeles, Chicago, Beijing and Dallas/Fort Worth. Figures collapsed in 2020–2021 during the COVID-19 pandemic and subsequently recovered at different rates by region.

Measurement

Totals count enplaned and deplaned passengers, domestic and international, across every airport assigned to the metropolitan system; definitions of which airports belong to a city vary between sources such as Airports Council International and national statistics, so rankings differ slightly between publications.
