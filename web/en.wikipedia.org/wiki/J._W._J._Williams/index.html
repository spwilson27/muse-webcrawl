J. W. J. Williams

John William Joseph Williams, commonly known as J. W. J. Williams, was a Welsh scholar and schoolmaster noted for his work in Welsh education and for editing and preserving Welsh literary material.

Life and work

Williams contributed to Welsh-language scholarship in the early twentieth century, including work connected with Welsh grammar, literature and the cultural life of Wales. He is remembered in connection with educational administration in Wales and with the promotion of Welsh studies in schools.
