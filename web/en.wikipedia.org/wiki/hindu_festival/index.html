Hindu festival

Hindu festivals are celebrations in Hinduism that occur throughout the year, generally calculated according to the Hindu lunisolar calendar. Festivals mark seasonal cycles, harvests, and mythological events, and typically involve prayer, fasting, feasting, processions, and acts of charity.

Major festivals

Among the most widely observed are Diwali, the festival of lights symbolizing the victory of light over darkness; Holi, the spring festival of colours; Navaratri and Durga Puja, honouring the goddess; Raksha Bandhan; Krishna Janmashtami; Ganesh Chaturthi; and Pongal and Makar Sankranti, harvest festivals. The Kumbh Mela, held in a twelve-year cycle at four river-bank sites, is the largest religious gathering in the world.

Observance

Dates vary by region because communities follow different regional calendars (solar, lunar, or lunisolar). Observances commonly include temple visits, special pujas, decorated homes, exchange of sweets and gifts, and community gatherings.
