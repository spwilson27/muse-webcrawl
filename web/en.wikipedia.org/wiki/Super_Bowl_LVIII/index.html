Super Bowl LVIII

Super Bowl LVIII was an American football game played on February 11, 2024, at Allegiant Stadium in Paradise, Nevada, to determine the champion of the National Football League (NFL) for the 2023 season. The American Football Conference (AFC) champion Kansas City Chiefs defeated the National Football Conference (NFC) champion San Francisco 49ers, 25–22 in overtime.

Background

The game was the first Super Bowl held in Nevada and the Las Vegas area. It was a rematch of Super Bowl LIV four years earlier, which Kansas City had also won. The Chiefs, led by quarterback Patrick Mahomes and head coach Andy Reid, were seeking to become the first team to repeat as champions since the New England Patriots in the 2003–2004 seasons.

Game summary

San Francisco led 10–0 in the second quarter and held a 10–3 advantage at half-time. Kansas City rallied in the second half, and the teams finished regulation tied 19–19. It was the second Super Bowl in history to go to overtime. The 49ers kicked a field goal on the first possession of overtime; the Chiefs responded with a drive ending in Mahomes' game-winning touchdown pass to Mecole Hardman with seconds left in the extra period.

Aftermath

Mahomes was named Super Bowl MVP for the third time. The Chiefs' victory cemented their status as the league's reigning dynasty, with three titles in five seasons. The halftime show was headlined by Usher, and the broadcast — carried by CBS and a family-oriented simulcast on Nickelodeon — became the most-watched telecast in American television history up to that point, drawing an average audience of about 123 million viewers.
