Treebeard

Treebeard, or Fangorn in Sindarin, is a fictional character in J. R. R. Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings. He is an Ent, a giant tree-like being, said to be the oldest living thing in Middle-earth. Treebeard is the shepherd of Fangorn Forest, home of the Ents.

Role in the story

In The Two Towers, the hobbits Merry and Pippin, escaping from Orcs, meet Treebeard in Fangorn Forest. After long deliberation, Treebeard convenes an Entmoot, which decides the Ents will march on Isengard to confront the wizard Saruman, whose forces have been felling the forest's trees. The Ents destroy Isengard's works and flood it, effectively ending Saruman's power.

Character

Treebeard is famously slow and deliberate — Ents "do not say anything unless it is worth taking a long time to say". His speech incorporates long, booming sounds and lists of living things. Tolkien based Treebeard's deep, slow voice in part on that of his friend C. S. Lewis. See Ent and The Lord of the Rings.
