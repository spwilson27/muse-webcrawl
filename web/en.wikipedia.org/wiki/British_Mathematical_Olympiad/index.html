British Mathematical Olympiad
Organiser: United Kingdom Mathematics Trust
First held: 1965
Participants: school students, by invitation/qualification

The British Mathematical Olympiad (BMO) is a national mathematics competition for school students in the United Kingdom, forming the main route for selection of the UK team for the International Mathematical Olympiad. It consists of two rounds of proof-based problems.

Format

Students usually qualify for BMO Round 1 through high scores in the UKMT Senior Mathematical Challenge. The paper contains several long problems to be solved in three and a half hours. The top scorers advance to Round 2, a harder paper, after which a training squad is selected for further mentoring and international competitions.

History

The competition began in 1965 as the National Mathematical Contest and adopted its present name and role as the British Mathematical Olympiad as the UK began regular participation in the IMO.
