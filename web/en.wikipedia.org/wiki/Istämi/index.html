Istämi

Istämi (died 575) was the first khagan of the Western Turkic Khaganate and a younger brother of Bumin Qaghan, founder of the First Turkic Khaganate. He is known in Greek sources as Silziboulos (Dizabul) and played a key role in opening relations between the Turks, Sogdiana, and the Byzantine Empire.

Reign

When the Göktürk empire was divided, Istämi received its western half, governing the rich Silk Road oasis states of Sogdiana. He destroyed the Hephthalites in alliance with the Sasanian Empire around 557–565, then fell out with Persia over the silk trade.

Byzantine diplomacy

In 568 Istämi sent the Sogdian merchant Maniah as envoy to Constantinople, proposing direct silk trade and an alliance against Persia; the Byzantine envoy Zemarchus returned the visit in 569. These exchanges are a landmark in steppe–Byzantine diplomacy and in the history of the Silk Road.
