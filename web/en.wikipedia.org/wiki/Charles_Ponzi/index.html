Charles Ponzi

Born3 March 1882, Lugo, Italy
Died18 January 1949, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil
Known forThe Ponzi scheme

Charles Ponzi (born Carlo Pietro Giovanni Guglielmo Tebaldo Ponzi; 1882–1949) was an Italian swindler who became notorious in the United States in 1920 for a fraudulent investment scheme that promised investors enormous returns from arbitraging international reply coupons. His name gave rise to the term "Ponzi scheme".

The scheme

Ponzi's Securities Exchange Company promised 50% returns in 45 days. In reality, early investors were paid with money from later investors. At its peak the scheme took in millions of dollars before collapsing in August 1920, ruining many investors and several banks.

Aftermath

Ponzi pleaded guilty to federal mail fraud and served prison time in the United States before further convictions in Massachusetts. He was deported to Italy in 1934 and died in poverty in Rio de Janeiro.
