Caitlyn Jenner

Caitlyn Marie Jenner
Born: 28 October 1949, Mount Kisco, New York
Sport: Track and field (decathlon)
Known for: 1976 Olympic decathlon gold medal; television personality

Caitlyn Marie Jenner (born 28 October 1949) is an American media personality and former Olympic gold medal-winning decathlete. Jenner won the men's decathlon at the 1976 Summer Olympics in Montreal, setting a world record and earning wide fame in the United States.

Athletic career

Jenner took up the decathlon after competing in the 1972 Munich Olympics. At Montreal in 1976 Jenner scored 8,618 points, a world record, defeating defending champion Mykola Avilov. The victory, celebrated with an impromptu flag-waving lap, made Jenner a national celebrity and led to endorsements, including the Wheaties box, and roles in film and television.

Television and public life

Jenner later appeared in the reality series Keeping Up with the Kardashians (2007–2021) as part of the Kardashian–Jenner family. In 2015 Jenner came out as a trans woman in a widely viewed television interview and on the cover of Vanity Fair, subsequently starring in the documentary series I Am Cait and receiving the Arthur Ashe Courage Award.

Politics

Jenner ran as a Republican candidate in the 2021 California gubernatorial recall election, finishing among the replacement candidates with a small share of the vote.
