Football tactics and skills

Football tactics and skills covers the strategies, formations and individual techniques used in association football. Tactics concern how a team is organised in and out of possession, while skills are the technical abilities — passing, dribbling, shooting, tackling and heading — that players use to execute those plans.

Formations

A formation describes how players are distributed on the pitch, conventionally written as the numbers of defenders, midfielders and forwards, such as 4-4-2, 4-3-3 or 3-5-2. Early football used extremely attacking arrangements such as the 2-3-5 pyramid; over the twentieth century formations became more balanced, and modern systems often change between attacking and defensive shapes during a match.

Styles of play

Teams may emphasise possession and short passing, as in the tiki-taka style associated with Barcelona and Spain, or high pressing to win the ball back quickly, as in gegenpressing. Other approaches include direct long-ball play, counter-attacking from a compact defensive block, and wing play built around crosses.

Skills

Core skills include first touch and ball control, short and long passing, dribbling past opponents, finishing, heading, tackling and, for goalkeepers, shot-stopping and distribution. Set pieces — corners, free kicks and penalties — are rehearsed like other tactical plays.
