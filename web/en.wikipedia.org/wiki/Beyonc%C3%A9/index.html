Beyoncé Giselle Knowles-Carter (born 4 September 1981) is an American singer, songwriter, and actress. Born and raised in Houston, Texas, she rose to fame in the late 1990s as the lead singer of Destiny's Child, one of the best-selling girl groups of all time. Her solo debut album, Dangerously in Love (2003), established her as a solo star.

Career

After Destiny's Child disbanded in 2006, Beyoncé released a string of acclaimed albums including B'Day (2006), I Am... Sasha Fierce (2008), 4 (2011), the surprise visual album Beyoncé (2013), Lemonade (2016), Renaissance (2022), and the country-influenced Cowboy Carter (2024). Her hits include "Crazy in Love", "Irreplaceable", "Single Ladies (Put a Ring on It)", "Halo", "Formation", "Break My Soul", and "Texas Hold 'Em". Her headlining performance at Coachella in 2018 was widely hailed as one of the greatest concert performances ever.

Legacy and ventures

Nicknamed "Queen Bey", Beyoncé is regarded as one of the greatest entertainers of her generation, praised for her vocal ability, choreography, and meticulous visual presentation. She is the most awarded individual artist in the history of the Grammy Awards, with 32 wins. Her business ventures include the fashion line Ivy Park and the production company Parkwood Entertainment. She is married to rapper Jay-Z, with whom she has three children.
