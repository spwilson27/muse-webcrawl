Country's most famous supergroup

The Highwaymen were an American country music supergroup composed of four of the genre's biggest stars: Johnny Cash, Waylon Jennings, Kris Kristofferson, and Willie Nelson. Active between 1985 and 1995, they were leading figures of the outlaw country movement.

Career

The group's debut album, Highwayman (1985), yielded a number-one country single with its title track, written by Jimmy Webb, in which the four singers voice reincarnated souls across history. Two further studio albums followed, Highwayman 2 (1990) and The Road Goes On Forever (1995), along with extensive touring and a 1990 television film, Stagecoach-era collaborations aside, that cemented their collective image as country's elder statesmen.

Legacy

The Highwaymen are frequently cited as the definitive country supergroup, and their recordings remain staples of the outlaw country canon.
