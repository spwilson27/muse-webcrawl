Han Solo

Han Solo
First appearance: Star Wars (1977)
Portrayed by: Harrison Ford; Alden Ehrenreich (young)
Occupation: Smuggler, captain of the Millennium Falcon, Rebel/Resistance general
Spouse: Leia Organa

Han Solo is a fictional character in the Star Wars franchise created by George Lucas. The character first appeared in the 1977 film Star Wars, portrayed by Harrison Ford. A Corellian smuggler and captain of the Millennium Falcon, Han is initially a cynical, self-interested loner who is drawn into the Rebel Alliance's struggle against the Galactic Empire.

Character overview

Han Solo is introduced in the Mos Eisley cantina, where he accepts a charter to transport Luke Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi. His Wookiee co-pilot and first mate is Chewbacca. Over the original trilogy he becomes a leader in the Alliance, falls in love with Princess Leia Organa, and is famously frozen in carbonite in The Empire Strikes Back (1980) before being rescued in Return of the Jedi (1983).

Later appearances

Ford returned as Han in The Force Awakens (2015), in which the character is killed by his son, Ben Solo (Kylo Ren). The standalone film Solo: A Star Wars Story (2018), starring Alden Ehrenreich as a young Han, depicts his early adventures, including winning the Millennium Falcon from Lando Calrissian in a game of sabacc.

Cultural impact

Han Solo is consistently ranked among the greatest film heroes; the American Film Institute placed him 14th on its list of the 100 greatest heroes. The character helped cement Harrison Ford's stardom.
