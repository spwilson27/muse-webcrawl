Overview

The Third Man Theme (also known as 'The Harry Lime Theme') is an instrumental piece written and performed on the zither by Anton Karas for Carol Reed's 1949 film noir The Third Man. Reed discovered Karas, a Viennese zither player performing in a wine garden, and had him compose and perform the film's entire score on the zither, an unconventional choice that became one of the most celebrated film scores of all time.

Success

Released as a single, Karas's recording topped the American charts for eleven weeks in 1950 and sold in the millions, briefly making the zither fashionable and Karas an international celebrity. A version by Guy Lombardo was also a major hit. The jaunty, bittersweet theme, associated in the film with the entrance of Orson Welles's character Harry Lime, remains instantly recognizable and is a fixture of 'greatest film music' lists.
