Dengeki Bunko (電撃文庫) is a light novel imprint affiliated with the Japanese publishing company ASCII Media Works, now part of the Kadokawa Corporation. Established in 1993, it is one of the largest and best-known light novel labels in Japan, publishing titles aimed mainly at a young male audience, many of which have been adapted into anime, manga and video games.

History and activities

The imprint grew out of MediaWorks, founded in 1992, and launched its first titles in 1993. Dengeki Bunko discovers new authors chiefly through the annual Dengeki Novel Prize (Dengeki Shōsetsu Taishō), one of Japan's most prominent newcomer awards for light novels, held since 1994. Winning and shortlisted works — including early successes such as Kino's Journey and Shakugan no Shana — built the label's reputation. The related magazine Dengeki Bunko Magazine (successor to Dengeki hp) serialised stories and news about the imprint until its closure in 2020.

Notable titles

Dengeki Bunko series include many of the best-selling light novels of the 2000s and 2010s: Sword Art Online by Reki Kawahara, A Certain Magical Index by Kazuma Kamachi, Durarara!! and Baccano! by Ryohgo Narita, Spice and Wolf by Isuna Hasekura, The Irregular at Magic High School by Tsutomu Satō, Toradora! by Yuyuko Takemiya, Accel World, Oreimo, Re:Zero's rival long-runners of the era, Eromanga Sensei, and 86—Eighty-Six— by Asato Asato. Cumulative sales of the imprint's major franchises run to tens of millions of copies; Sword Art Online alone has sold over 30 million copies worldwide.

Related media

The label's works anchor the "Dengeki" brand alongside sister imprints such as Dengeki G's Bunko and the Media Works Bunko line for general readers. ASCII Media Works has staged large fan events, including the Dengeki Bunko Autumn Festival, and the imprint's characters appeared in the crossover fighting game Dengeki Bunko: Fighting Climax (2014). English translations of many series are published in North America, principally by Yen On.
