Generations of leadership

Chinese political discourse groups PRC leaders into "generations": Mao's first generation, Deng's second, Jiang's third, Hu's fourth, and Xi's fifth. Since 2012 the roles of party general secretary, state president, and Central Military Commission chairman have been held concurrently by Xi Jinping.
