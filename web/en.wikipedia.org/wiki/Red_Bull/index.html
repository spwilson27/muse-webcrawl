Red Bull

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Red Bull is an energy drink created and owned by the Austrian company Red Bull GmbH. First sold in Austria in 1987, it is the best-selling energy drink in the world, with around 12 billion cans sold in 2023. The brand is also known for its extensive sports sponsorship and ownership, including Formula One teams and football clubs, and for its slogan "Red Bull gives you wings."

History

Red Bull was founded by Austrian entrepreneur Dietrich Mateschitz, inspired by the Thai energy drink Krating Daeng ("red bull" in Thai), which had been created by Chaleo Yoovidhya. Mateschitz partnered with Chaleo, adapted the formula and marketing for Western markets, and launched Red Bull GmbH in 1984 with Chaleo's family; each side held 49%, with 2% for Chaleo's son. The drink launched in Austria in 1987 and expanded across Europe and the United States in the 1990s.

Ingredients and health

A standard 250 ml can contains 80 mg of caffeine, taurine, B-group vitamins, sucrose and glucose (with sugar-free variants using artificial sweeteners), and water sourced from springs near the Austrian production sites. Health authorities including EFSA have concluded that the drink's ingredients at normal consumption levels are not a safety concern, while advising moderation in caffeine intake overall.

Sports and marketing

Red Bull owns the Formula One teams Red Bull Racing and RB Formula One Team, football clubs including RB Leipzig and FC Red Bull Salzburg, and the New York Red Bulls. It sponsors extreme sports events such as Red Bull Cliff Diving and the Red Bull Air Race, and in 2012 sponsored Felix Baumgartner's stratospheric freefall jump, Red Bull Stratos.
