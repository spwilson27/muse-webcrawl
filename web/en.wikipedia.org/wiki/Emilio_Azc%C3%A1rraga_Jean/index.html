Emilio Fernando Azcárraga Jean (born 21 February 1968) is a Mexican businessman who led Grupo Televisa, the largest Spanish-language media company in the world, for more than two decades. He became president of the company in 1997, at age 29, following the death of his father, Emilio Azcárraga Milmo.

Career at Televisa

Azcárraga Jean modernised Televisa, expanding its interests in cable, satellite television (Sky México), publishing and telecommunications. In 2021 Televisa merged its content business with the US broadcaster Univision to form TelevisaUnivision, one of the largest Spanish-language streaming and broadcast ventures. He also owned Club América, one of Mexico's most popular football teams, and the Estadio Azteca.

Stepping back

In October 2024 Azcárraga Jean took a leave of absence as executive chairman of Grupo Televisa while a United States Department of Justice investigation related to FIFA bribery allegations proceeded; Televisa denied wrongdoing by the company.
