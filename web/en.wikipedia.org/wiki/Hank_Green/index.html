William Henry "Hank" Green II (born May 5, 1980) is an American YouTuber, science communicator, author, musician and entrepreneur. He is best known for co-creating, with his older brother John Green, the YouTube channel vlogbrothers (launched in 2007 under the "Brotherhood 2.0" project), and for founding the educational media company Complexly, which produces channels such as SciShow and Crash Course. The brothers' online community is known as Nerdfighteria, and their slogan is "Don't Forget To Be Awesome" (DFTBA).

Career

Green created the environmental technology blog EcoGeek before turning to video. Through Crash Course and SciShow he became one of the most prominent science educators on YouTube, reaching tens of millions of subscribers across channels. He co-founded the crowdfunding platform Subbable (later merged into Patreon), the online video conference VidCon (founded 2010, later acquired by Viacom), and the Project for Awesome charity fundraiser. He co-hosts the podcasts Dear Hank & John and SciShow Tangents.

Writing and music

Green's debut novel, An Absolutely Remarkable Thing (2018), became a New York Times bestseller, followed by the sequel A Beautifully Foolish Endeavor (2020). As a musician he has released several albums of comedic and science-themed songs, including So Jokes and Incongruent, and performed with his band Hank Green and the Perfect Strangers. In 2023 he was diagnosed with Hodgkin lymphoma and documented his treatment publicly; he later reported being in remission.
