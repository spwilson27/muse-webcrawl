Dhaka

Dhaka is the capital and largest city of Bangladesh, with about 10 million residents in the city and more than 23 million in the metropolitan area — one of the world's largest and most densely populated urban regions. It stands on the Buriganga River in the Ganges-Brahmaputra delta.

Dhaka was a Mughal provincial capital famed for muslin cloth, became the capital of East Pakistan in 1947 and of independent Bangladesh in 1971. It is known as the 'City of Mosques' and the rickshaw capital of the world, with several hundred thousand cycle rickshaws. The garment industry around Dhaka powers Bangladesh's exports but was scarred by the 2013 Rana Plaza collapse in nearby Savar, which killed over 1,100 workers. The low-lying delta city faces severe flooding and waterlogging in the monsoon.
