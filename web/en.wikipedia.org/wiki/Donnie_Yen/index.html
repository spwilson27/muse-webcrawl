Donnie Yen Chi-tan (Chinese: 甄子丹; born July 27, 1963) is a Hong Kong actor, filmmaker, martial artist, and action director. Regarded as one of the greatest martial arts stars of his generation, Yen is credited with popularising Wing Chun through his portrayal of Bruce Lee's master in the Ip Man film series (2008–2019), and with bringing mixed martial arts elements into Hong Kong action cinema. He has worked across Asian and Hollywood productions, including Hero (2002), Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (2016), and John Wick: Chapter 4 (2023).

Yen's portrayal of Ip Man made him one of the most bankable action stars in Asia.

Early life and training

Yen was born in Guangzhou and moved with his family to Hong Kong and then to Boston, Massachusetts. His mother, Bow-sim Mark, is a renowned wushu and tai chi master who founded the Chinese Wushu Research Institute in Boston; his father was a newspaper editor and amateur musician. Yen trained in wushu in Beijing with the Beijing Wushu Team before being discovered by director Yuen Woo-ping, who cast him in Drunken Tai Chi (1984).

Career

Yen gained fame in the 1990s with roles in Once Upon a Time in China II (1992), opposite Jet Li, and Iron Monkey (1993). His fight choreography for films such as SPL: Sha Po Lang (2005) and Flash Point (2007) won multiple Hong Kong Film Awards. The success of Ip Man (2008) and its sequels elevated him to superstar status across Asia. In Hollywood he played the blind warrior Chirrut Îmwe in Rogue One and the blind assassin Caine in John Wick: Chapter 4. He reportedly became one of the highest-paid actors in Asia.
