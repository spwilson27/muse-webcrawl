Harry Osborn

Harold "Harry" Osborn is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko, he first appeared in The Amazing Spider-Man #31 (December 1965). He is the best friend of Peter Parker (Spider-Man) and the son of Norman Osborn, the original Green Goblin.

Fictional biography

Introduced as a wealthy classmate of Peter Parker at Empire State University, Harry — initially snobbish — becomes part of Peter's circle of friends including Gwen Stacy and Mary Jane Watson. Neglected by his distant father and strained by academic and romantic pressures, Harry becomes addicted to drugs in a landmark 1971 storyline, one of the first depictions of drug abuse in mainstream comics.

After Norman Osborn's apparent death, Harry blames Spider-Man, discovers his father's Goblin equipment and identity, and takes up the mantle of the Green Goblin himself. His mental state deteriorates; he dies in The Spectacular Spider-Man #200 (1993) after briefly overcoming his madness to save Peter's life. Following the events of "One More Day", Harry was resurrected, and later storylines reinvented aspects of his return as part of the Kindred saga.

In other media

Harry Osborn was played by James Franco in Sam Raimi's Spider-Man trilogy (2002–2007) and by Dane DeHaan in The Amazing Spider-Man 2 (2014), and appears in numerous animated series and video games.
