Democratic Party of Korea

Founded2014 (as NPAD); renamed 2015
HeadquartersSeoul, South Korea
IdeologyLiberalism

The Democratic Party of Korea (DPK) is a liberal political party in South Korea and one of the country's two major parties alongside the People Power Party. It traces its roots to earlier democratic parties and was formed in 2014 as the New Politics Alliance for Democracy, adopting its current name in 2015.

History

The party won the presidency with Moon Jae-in in 2017 and secured a large National Assembly majority in 2020. It lost the 2022 presidential election narrowly but remained the largest party in the legislature, and won the 2024 legislative elections.
