Kinross (Scottish Gaelic: Ceann Rois) is a burgh in Perth and Kinross, Scotland, situated on the southern shore of Loch Leven. It was formerly the county town of the small historic county of Kinross-shire. The town lies close to the M90 motorway, about 13 miles south of Perth and 30 miles north-west of Edinburgh.

History and attractions

Kinross developed as a market and coaching town on the Great North Road. Its best-known neighbour, just offshore in Loch Leven, is Loch Leven Castle, where Mary, Queen of Scots was imprisoned in 1567–68 and from which she escaped in May 1568. The loch itself is a National Nature Reserve famous for its breeding wildfowl and its trout fishery, and is ringed by the Loch Leven Heritage Trail.

From 2008 to 2014 Kinross House and its estate hosted the T in the Park music festival's successor events after the festival moved from Balado, near Kinross, where it had been held since 1997 at a disused airfield. The town serves today largely as a commuter settlement for Perth and Edinburgh.
