Willem-Alexander of the Netherlands

Willem-Alexander (Willem-Alexander Claus George Ferdinand; born 27 April 1967) is the King of the Netherlands, having acceded to the throne on 30 April 2013 following the abdication of his mother, Queen Beatrix. He is the first Dutch king since the death of his great-great-grandfather William III in 1890.

Early life and education

Willem-Alexander was born in Utrecht, the eldest child of Princess Beatrix and Prince Claus. He was educated in the Netherlands and at Atlantic College in Wales, served in the Royal Netherlands Navy, and read history at Leiden University, graduating in 1993. He is a qualified pilot and has flown commercial aircraft as a guest pilot for KLM to maintain his licence. Before his accession he was active in water management, serving as chairperson of the UN Secretary-General's Advisory Board on Water and Sanitation, and was a member of the International Olympic Committee.

Marriage and reign

In 2002 he married Máxima Zorreguieta, an Argentine investment banker, who became Queen Máxima. The marriage was controversial because her father had served in the Argentine military government, but it won parliamentary approval. As king, Willem-Alexander heads a largely ceremonial monarchy; his reign has emphasised accessibility and national unity, and his eldest daughter, Catharina-Amalia, is heir apparent as Princess of Orange.
