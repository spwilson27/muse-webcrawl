Manipal is a university town in Udupi district in the Indian state of Karnataka, situated on a plateau about 5 kilometres inland from the Arabian Sea coast and adjoining the temple town of Udupi. It is best known as the home of the Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE), one of India's largest private universities.

History

The modern town grew out of the educational vision of Dr T. M. A. Pai, who founded the Kasturba Medical College in Manipal in 1953 — India's first private medical college — transforming a barren laterite hillock into a major educational centre. The Manipal Academy was granted deemed university status in 1993.

Education and life

Manipal hosts institutions in medicine, engineering, dentistry, pharmacy, management and communication, drawing tens of thousands of students from across India and abroad. Local landmarks include Manipal Lake, End Point overlooking the Swarna River valley, and the Hasta Shilpa heritage village museum.
