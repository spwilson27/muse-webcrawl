Poly(methyl methacrylate) (PMMA)
Also known as: Acrylic, Perspex, Plexiglas
Density: ~1.18 g/cm³
Transmission: up to 92% of visible light

Poly(methyl methacrylate) (PMMA) is a transparent thermoplastic polymer, often used in sheet form as a lightweight, shatter-resistant alternative to glass. It is sold under trade names including Perspex, Plexiglas, Lucite, and simply "acrylic".

Properties

PMMA transmits up to 92 percent of visible light and is more transparent than glass in thick sections. It is lighter and more impact-resistant than glass but scratches more easily. It softens at around 100 °C and can be thermoformed, cast, or extruded.

Uses

Applications include windows and aircraft canopies, aquariums, lenses, medical devices and bone cement, paints, and signage. PMMA is produced by polymerisation of methyl methacrylate.
