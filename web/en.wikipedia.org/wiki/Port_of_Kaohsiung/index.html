Port of Kaohsiung

The Port of Kaohsiung is the largest seaport in Taiwan, located in Kaohsiung in the south of the island. It handles a large share of Taiwan's total shipping and is among the busiest container ports in the world.

History

The natural harbour was known during the Dutch colonial period and developed substantially under Japanese rule in the early 20th century, when it became the island's principal port. It was heavily bombed during the Second World War and rebuilt afterwards. In the 1970s and 1980s container terminals expanded rapidly, and the port briefly ranked among the world's top three container ports.

Facilities

The port comprises several container terminals, bulk and general cargo wharves, and an intercontinental terminal. It is operated by the Taiwan International Ports Corporation. The harbour entrance is divided by Qijin Island, a popular tourist area.
