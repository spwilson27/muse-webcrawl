Business communication

FieldBusiness, communication studies
FocusInformation sharing within and between organisations
ChannelsMeetings, email, reports, presentations, social media

Business communication encompasses internal and external exchanges of information.

Business communication is the process of sharing information between people within an organisation and between the organisation and external audiences such as customers, investors, regulators, and the media. Effective business communication supports coordination, decision-making, marketing, and reputation management, and it is studied as a field within business schools and communication studies.

Types

Internal communication flows downward (instructions, policies), upward (reports, feedback), and horizontally (collaboration between peers and departments). External communication includes advertising, public relations, customer service, investor relations, and business-to-business correspondence. Common channels include face-to-face meetings, video conferences, email, instant messaging, reports and proposals, presentations, and corporate social media.

Principles

Standard guidance emphasises the "7 Cs" — clarity, conciseness, concreteness, correctness, coherence, completeness, and courtesy. Barriers to effective communication include jargon, information overload, hierarchical filtering, cultural and language differences, and noise in the channel. Modern practice also stresses listening skills, nonverbal communication, cross-cultural competence, and the legal and ethical constraints on corporate disclosures.
