November Uprising

The November Uprising (1830–31), also known as the Polish–Russian War, was an armed rebellion in Congress Poland against the Russian Empire.

Course

It began on 29 November 1830 in Warsaw when young Polish officers rose against Grand Duke Constantine. The Sejm dethroned Tsar Nicholas I as King of Poland, but after early successes the larger Russian army under Ivan Paskevich retook Warsaw in September 1831.

Aftermath

Defeat brought repression, abolition of Polish autonomy, and the Great Emigration of thousands of Poles to Western Europe.
