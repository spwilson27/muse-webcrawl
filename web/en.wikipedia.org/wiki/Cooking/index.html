Cooking

Cooking
DefinitionPreparing food by applying heat, combining and seasoning ingredients
MethodsBoiling, frying, roasting, baking, steaming, grilling
[Image: A chef tossing a flaming pan in a professional kitchen]

Cooking, also known as cookery, is the art, science and craft of using heat to prepare food for eating. Control of fire was a turning point in human evolution — cooked food yields more energy and is safer — and cooking techniques are a core of every culture, from bread ovens to wok hei.

Styles and science

Cuisines differ by ingredients, climate and history; the Maillard reaction and caramelisation create flavour chemistry now studied as gastronomy. Home cooking declined with processed food and rose again with television chefs (Television) and online recipes; cooking skills correlate with healthier diets (see Health).
