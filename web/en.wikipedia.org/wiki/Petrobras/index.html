Petróleo Brasileiro S.A., better known as Petrobras, is a Brazilian multinational petroleum corporation headquartered in Rio de Janeiro. It is the largest company in Brazil and one of the largest oil and gas producers in the world, with the Brazilian federal government as its controlling shareholder.

Petrobras was created in 1953 under President Getúlio Vargas, following the popular nationalist campaign summed up by the slogan "O petróleo é nosso" ("The oil is ours"). It held a legal monopoly on oil exploration and production in Brazil until the market was opened in 1997.

Operations

The company is internationally recognised for its expertise in deep-water and ultra-deep-water exploration. Beginning in 2006, Petrobras announced a series of giant "pre-salt" discoveries — oil reservoirs beneath thick layers of salt in the Santos and Campos Basins offshore south-eastern Brazil — which transformed Brazil into one of the world's top ten oil producers.

Corruption scandal

From 2014, Petrobras was at the centre of the Operation Car Wash (Lava Jato) investigation, which revealed a vast scheme of kickbacks and over-billing involving company executives, contractors, and politicians. The scandal led to billions of dollars in write-downs, a collapse in the company's share price, and far-reaching political consequences in Brazil.
