Confederation of African Football

The Confederation of African Football (CAF; French: Confédération Africaine de Football) is the administrative and controlling body for association football, futsal and beach soccer in Africa. It is one of FIFA's six confederations and represents 54 member associations.

History

CAF was founded in 1957 by Egypt, Sudan, Ethiopia and South Africa; the first Africa Cup of Nations was held that year in Khartoum. The organisation grew with decolonisation as new nations joined, and African football gained global prominence through club competitions and World Cup performances.

Competitions

CAF organises the Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON), the African Nations Championship, the CAF Champions League and Confederation Cup for clubs, women's tournaments including the Women's Africa Cup of Nations, and youth and futsal competitions.
