Kristopher Lee Bryant (born 4 January 1992) is an American professional baseball third baseman and outfielder. He has played in Major League Baseball (MLB) for the Chicago Cubs, San Francisco Giants and Colorado Rockies. Bryant played college baseball at the University of San Diego, where he was a two-time All-American, and was selected by the Cubs with the second overall pick in the 2013 MLB draft.

Chicago Cubs

Bryant debuted in April 2015 and won the National League Rookie of the Year Award after hitting 26 home runs. In 2016 he won the NL Most Valuable Player Award and helped the Cubs win the World Series — their first championship in 108 years — scoring and fielding prominently in Game 7, where he fielded the final out's ground ball. He was named an All-Star in 2015, 2016, 2019 and 2021.

Later career

After filing a widely discussed service-time grievance early in his career, Bryant was traded to the San Francisco Giants at the 2021 trade deadline. In March 2022 he signed a seven-year, $182 million contract with the Colorado Rockies, though his tenure there has been repeatedly interrupted by back and foot injuries.
