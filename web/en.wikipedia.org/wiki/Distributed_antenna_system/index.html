Distributed antenna system

A distributed antenna system (DAS) is a network of spatially separated antenna nodes connected to a common source via a transport medium, providing wireless service within a geographic area or structure. DAS installations are used where conventional single-antenna coverage is impractical because of terrain or building materials, such as stadiums, airports, hospitals, tunnels and dense urban areas.

Design

A DAS distributes radio signals from a central hub over coaxial cable or optical fibre (radio-over-fibre) to remote units, dividing the total transmitted power among many low-power antennas. Systems may be passive or active, and may be dedicated to a single carrier or neutral host, shared among multiple operators.
