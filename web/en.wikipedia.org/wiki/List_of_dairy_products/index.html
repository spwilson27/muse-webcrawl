List of dairy products

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

A dairy product is food produced from the milk (or mammary secretions) of mammals, principally cattle but also goats, sheep, buffalo, camels, yaks and horses. This article lists the principal categories of dairy products consumed around the world.

Milk and cream products

Half and halfA mixture of milk and cream, common in North America.
EggnogA sweetened milk-and-egg drink, traditionally served at Christmas.

Butter and related products

  • Butter — churned cream or milk; salted and unsalted varieties.
  • Ghee — clarified butter simmered to remove water and milk solids, essential in South Asian cooking.
  • Clarified butter / beurre noisette — butter with milk solids removed or browned.
  • Cultured butter — made from fermented cream, common in Europe.
  • Smen — a fermented, salted clarified butter from North Africa.
  • Yak butter — used in Tibetan butter tea.

Cheeses

There are well over a thousand named cheeses. Major types include:

  • Fresh: mozzarella, ricotta, cottage cheese, cream cheese, mascarpone, feta, halloumi, queso fresco, paneer.
  • Soft-ripened: Brie, Camembert.
  • Semi-soft: Gouda, Edam, Havarti, Port Salut, Monterey Jack.
  • Hard: Cheddar, Parmigiano-Reggiano (Parmesan), Grana Padano, Gruyère, Emmental, Comté, Manchego, Pecorino.
  • Blue: Roquefort, Gorgonzola, Stilton, Danish Blue, Cabrales.
  • Washed-rind: Époisses, Munster, Limburger, Taleggio.
  • Processed: processed cheese slices, cheese spreads, and products such as Dairylea.

Frozen and other products

  • Ice cream and gelato — frozen desserts of sweetened milk and cream.
  • Kulfi — dense Indian frozen dessert made from slowly reduced milk.
  • Frozen yogurt — a lower-fat frozen dessert.
  • Custard — milk or cream thickened with egg.
  • Whey — the liquid by-product of cheese making, dried for protein powders.
  • Casein — the principal milk protein, used in foods and industry.
  • Dulce de leche / cajeta — caramelized sweetened milk of Latin America.
  • Khoya (mawa) — reduced milk solids used in Indian sweets such as gulab jamun.
  • Clotted cream — thick cream from Devon and Cornwall, served with scones.
