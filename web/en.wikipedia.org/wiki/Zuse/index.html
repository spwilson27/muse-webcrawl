Zuse

Zuse may refer to several people and things, most notably the German computer pioneer Konrad Zuse and the company he founded.

Konrad Zuse

Konrad Zuse (1910–1995) was a German civil engineer, inventor and computer pioneer. Working largely in isolation in Berlin, he built the Z1 (1938) and the Z3 (1941), the latter the first working programmable, fully automatic digital computer, and the Z4, later installed at ETH Zürich. He also designed Plankalkül, an early high-level programming language, though it was not implemented in his lifetime. In 1949 he founded Zuse KG, one of the earliest computer companies, which built a series of machines for scientific and commercial customers before being absorbed into Siemens in the late 1960s.
