Marketing management

Marketing management is the organisational discipline of planning, implementing and controlling marketing programmes to create exchanges that satisfy individual and organisational goals. Drawing on economics, psychology and statistics, it covers market research, strategy, and management of the marketing mix.

Process

The process typically involves analysing the marketing environment and opportunities, researching and selecting target markets, designing marketing strategies, planning marketing programmes (product, price, place and promotion), and organising, implementing and controlling the marketing effort.

Orientations

Firms may adopt a production, product, selling, marketing or societal marketing orientation. Philip Kotler's textbooks helped establish marketing management as an academic discipline in the second half of the 20th century.
