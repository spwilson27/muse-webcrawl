Tehri Garhwal is a district in the Indian state of Uttarakhand, in the Himalayan foothills of the Garhwal region. Its administrative headquarters is New Tehri, a planned town built to replace old Tehri.

Tehri Dam

The district is dominated by the Tehri Dam on the Bhagirathi River, one of the tallest dams in the world. Completed in phases from 2006, it generates hydroelectric power and supplies irrigation and drinking water, but its reservoir submerged the historic town of Tehri and dozens of villages, prompting decades of protest led by figures such as Sunderlal Bahuguna.

Geography

The district spans steep river valleys of the Bhagirathi and Bhilangna rivers, rising toward the Greater Himalaya. Pilgrims pass through it on routes toward the Char Dham shrines, and Devprayag, where the Bhagirathi and Alaknanda meet to form the Ganges, lies at its southern edge.
