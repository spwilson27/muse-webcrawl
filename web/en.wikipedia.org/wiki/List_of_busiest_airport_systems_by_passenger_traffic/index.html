List of busiest airport systems by passenger traffic

An airport system comprises all airports serving a metropolitan area. This list ranks the world's busiest city airport systems by total passenger traffic across their airports (figures approximately as of recent pre- and post-pandemic years; totals vary by year). London is typically the busiest system in the world, followed by New York.
