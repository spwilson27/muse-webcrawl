Tandoori chicken

Tandoori chicken
Course: Appetiser / main
Region: Punjab, Delhi
Main ingredients: Chicken, yogurt, spices

Tandoori chicken is a dish made from chicken marinated in yogurt and spices and roasted in a tandoor, a cylindrical clay oven. It is a staple of Punjabi cuisine and of Indian restaurants worldwide.

History

Chicken roasted in tandoors has precedents in the Indus Valley, but the modern dish is credited to Kundan Lal Gujral and the Moti Mahal restaurant in Peshawar and then Delhi in the 1940s, where it was popularised after Partition. It later inspired butter chicken, created from leftover tandoori chicken in a tomato-butter sauce.

Preparation

Chicken pieces, often skinless and scored, are marinated in yogurt with Kashmiri chilli, garam masala, ginger, garlic, cumin, and lemon, giving the characteristic red-orange colour (sometimes deepened with food colouring). They are then skewered and roasted at high heat until charred at the edges.

Serving

The dish is served hot, often on a sizzling platter with sliced onions, lemon wedges, and mint chutney, accompanied by naan.
