Monarchy

Monarchy
DefinitionForm of government with a monarch at its head
TypesAbsolute, constitutional, elective
[Image: A crown resting on a velvet cushion]

A monarchy is a form of government in which a person, the monarch, reigns as head of state for life or until abdication, usually by hereditary succession. Once the default constitution of states from Egypt to China, monarchy survives in about forty countries.

Modern monarchies

Most are constitutional (UK, Japan, Spain, the Netherlands): the monarch reigns ceremonially while governments rule. Absolute monarchies persist in a few states (Saudi Arabia, Eswatini, Brunei). Debate weighs continuity and national symbolism against democratic principle.
