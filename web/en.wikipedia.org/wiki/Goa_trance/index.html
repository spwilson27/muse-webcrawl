Goa trance

Goa trance is an electronic dance music style that originated in the early 1990s in the Indian state of Goa. Goa trance often has funky, drone-like basslines similar to those of techno with more organic, "squiggly" textures of synthesizer sound. Popular elements are samples of topics such as science fiction and space travel.

The style grew out of the beach parties held by Western travellers in Goa from the late 1960s onwards, where DJs such as Goa Gil developed all-night sets blending rock, dub and industrial with early electronic music. By the early 1990s a distinct sound emerged, characterised by layered melodies, prominent use of the Roland TB-303 and Eastern scales, at tempos around 130–150 BPM.

Key acts included Hallucinogen, Astral Projection, Man With No Name, The Infinity Project and Juno Reactor. Goa trance later evolved into the harder, darker and faster psychedelic trance (psytrance), which eclipsed it commercially while retaining its party culture in Goa and at festivals worldwide.
