COBie

Full name: Construction Operations Building Information Exchange
Developed by: Bill East, U.S. Army Corps of Engineers
First published: 2007
Type: Data exchange standard for building information modelling

COBie (Construction Operations Building Information Exchange) is an international standard for the exchange of information about buildings, used to deliver asset data from construction projects to the owners and facilities managers who will operate the completed building. It is a subset of the data defined by building information modelling (BIM) and is most often delivered as a structured spreadsheet.

Development and content

COBie was developed by Dr Bill East of the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, with a pilot version published in 2007 and version 2.24 issued as part of the U.S. National BIM Standard. The exchange focuses on the information needed to operate and maintain a facility: spaces, floors, equipment (assets), systems, spare parts, documents, jobs (planned maintenance), and the contacts responsible for them. Data is organised into linked worksheets (such as Facility, Floor, Space, Equipment, Component, System, Job and Contact) that can be delivered in spreadsheet form, in STEP format, or as an ifcXML/IFC deliverable.

Adoption

COBie deliverables are required on many public building projects. In the United Kingdom, a COBie data drop was a requirement of the government's BIM Level 2 programme for centrally procured public projects, and the standard is incorporated into BS 1192-4. In the United States it is widely specified on military and federal construction contracts.
