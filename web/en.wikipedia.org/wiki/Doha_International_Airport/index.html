Doha International Airport (IATA: DIA, ICAO: OTBD) was the principal international airport of Qatar until it was replaced by Hamad International Airport in 2014. It remains in use for military, VIP, charter and general aviation purposes.

History

The airport opened in 1959 and grew with Qatar's oil-funded development and the expansion of Qatar Airways. By the 2000s it was operating far beyond its design capacity — handling over 20 million passengers a year in a terminal intended for a fraction of that — leading to the construction of its replacement on reclaimed land to the east. All scheduled commercial flights transferred to Hamad International Airport on 27 May 2014.

Later use

The airport hosted arrivals during the 2022 FIFA World Cup alongside Hamad International, and its site adjoins the Al Udeid-linked military facilities and the Amiri (royal) terminal. The runway, at about 4,570 metres, was once among the longest in civilian use.
