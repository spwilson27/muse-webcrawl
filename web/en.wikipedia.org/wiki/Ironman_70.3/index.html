Ironman 70.3

An Ironman 70.3, also known as a Half Ironman, is one of a series of long-distance triathlon races organised by the World Triathlon Corporation (WTC). The "70.3" refers to the total distance in miles (113.0 km) covered in the race: a 1.2-mile (1.9 km) swim, a 56-mile (90 km) bike ride, and a 13.1-mile (21.1 km) run — exactly half the distance of a full Ironman triathlon.

History

The first Half Ironman race was held in the United Kingdom in 2001. The series was rebranded as Ironman 70.3 in 2006, when the first Ironman 70.3 World Championship was held in Clearwater, Florida. The world championship has since rotated among venues including Las Vegas, Mont-Tremblant, Zell am See, the Sunshine Coast, Chattanooga, St. George, Lahti and Taupō.

Format

Races are held worldwide throughout the year and serve as qualifiers for the Ironman 70.3 World Championship. Like the full Ironman, events attract professional triathletes, age-group competitors and relay teams.
