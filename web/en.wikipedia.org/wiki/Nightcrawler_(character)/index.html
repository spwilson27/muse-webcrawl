Nightcrawler (real name Kurt Wagner) is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, commonly in association with the X-Men. Created by artist Dave Cockrum and writer Len Wein, the character first appeared in Giant-Size X-Men #1 (May 1975). Nightcrawler is a mutant with the ability to teleport, accompanied by a puff of smoke, a sulfurous "bamf" sound, and a faint smell of brimstone.

Publication history

Nightcrawler was originally designed by Dave Cockrum for a proposed series called "The Outsiders" for DC Comics, but Cockrum brought the character with him when he moved to Marvel. He debuted as part of the "all-new, all-different" X-Men assembled by Professor Charles Xavier to rescue the original team from the living island Krakoa. He became one of the most popular members of the team during the Chris Claremont era and has remained a core X-Men character for decades.

Fictional character biography

Kurt Wagner was born in Bavaria, Germany, the son of the mutant shapeshifter Mystique (Raven Darkhölme) and, in later retcons, Azazel. Abandoned as an infant because of his demonic appearance — blue fur, three-fingered hands, a prehensile tail, and glowing yellow eyes — he was found and raised by the fortune teller Margali Szardos in a traveling circus, where he performed as an acrobat. A devout Catholic despite his appearance, Kurt was eventually recruited by Professor X. He later learned that Mystique was his mother, and he served on the British team Excalibur before returning to the X-Men.

Powers and abilities

Nightcrawler's primary power is teleportation: he can transport himself, his clothing, and a limited amount of additional mass by passing through an alternate dimension (the "Brimstone Dimension"). He also possesses superhuman agility and reflexes, can cling to walls and ceilings, becomes nearly invisible in deep shadow, and is a master fencer and hand-to-hand combatant.

In other media

Nightcrawler was portrayed by Alan Cumming in the film X2: X-Men United (2003), widely praised as a highlight of the movie, and by Kodi Smit-McPhee in X-Men: Apocalypse (2016), Dark Phoenix (2019), and a cameo in Deadpool 2 (2018). He has also appeared in numerous animated X-Men series and video games.
