List of North American Numbering Plan area codes

The North American Numbering Plan (NANP) is a telephone numbering plan serving twenty-five countries and territories, including the United States, Canada, Bermuda and most of the Caribbean. Each numbering plan area (NPA) is identified by a three-digit area code. The system was devised by AT&T in 1947, when 86 area codes were originally assigned; today there are several hundred, including overlay codes serving the same geographic area.

Originally, area codes with the lowest digits were assigned to the largest cities — 212 for New York City, 213 for Los Angeles, 312 for Chicago — because rotary telephones dialled low numbers fastest. Middle digit 0 indicated a state or province with a single area code; middle digit 1 indicated one of several.
