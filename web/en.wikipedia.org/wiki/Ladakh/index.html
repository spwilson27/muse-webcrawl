Ladakh is a region administered by India as a union territory and constitutes a larger portion of the wider Kashmir region, which has been the subject of dispute between India, Pakistan, and China since 1947. A high-altitude cold desert between the Karakoram and the Great Himalaya, it is one of the most sparsely populated regions in India and is sometimes called "Little Tibet" for its Tibetan-influenced culture and predominantly Buddhist heritage in the east.

Geography

Ladakh is the highest plateau in India, with much of it over 3,000 metres. The Indus River is its lifeline, flowing between the Ladakh and Zanskar ranges. The region includes the districts of Leh and Kargil, the Nubra Valley beyond the Khardung La pass (one of the world's highest motorable roads), and the salt lake Pangong Tso, whose eastern end lies in Chinese-administered territory. The world's second-highest battlefield, the Siachen Glacier, lies to the northwest.

History

Ladakh was an independent kingdom from the 10th century, ruled first by a dynasty descended from the Tibetan empire, and reached its greatest extent under Sengge Namgyal in the 17th century. In 1834 the Dogra general Zorawar Singh conquered it for the Sikh Empire's vassal Gulab Singh, after which it passed to the princely state of Jammu and Kashmir under British paramountcy. At partition in 1947 it acceded to India; its western areas were fought over in 1947–48, and the eastern Aksai Chin plateau was occupied by China and contested in the 1962 war. On 31 October 2019, following the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, Ladakh became a union territory administered directly by New Delhi, without a legislature.

Culture and tourism

Vajrayana Buddhist monasteries — Hemis, Thiksey, Lamayuru, Alchi — anchor the cultural landscape, while Kargil district is predominantly Shia Muslim. Tourism, army employment, and apricot and pashmina production drive the economy; Leh's airport and the Manali–Leh and Srinagar–Leh highways connect the region seasonally. Ladakh has also become a major destination for adventure tourism and for Bollywood location filming.
