List of popes

The pope is the bishop of Rome and head of the worldwide Catholic Church. According to Catholic tradition, the line of popes descends from Saint Peter, regarded as the first pope. The official Vatican list, the Annuario Pontificio, counts 266 popes through Francis. This article lists the popes by era, noting the most significant pontificates.
