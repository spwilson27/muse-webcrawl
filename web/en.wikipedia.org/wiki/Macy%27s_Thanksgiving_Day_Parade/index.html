Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade

The Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade is an annual parade in New York City presented by the department store chain Macy's. First held in 1924, it takes place on Thanksgiving morning and is famous for its giant helium balloons of cartoon characters, along with floats, marching bands and Broadway performances.

History

The first parade was organised by Macy's employees, many of them immigrants who wanted to celebrate with the kind of festivals they remembered from Europe; it featured animals from the Central Park Zoo. Giant balloons replaced the live animals in 1927, with Felix the Cat among the first characters. The parade was suspended from 1942 to 1944 because of wartime rubber and helium shortages.

The parade today

The roughly 2.5-mile route runs from the Upper West Side down to Herald Square. The event is televised nationally and traditionally ends with the arrival of Santa Claus, marking the start of the Christmas season.
