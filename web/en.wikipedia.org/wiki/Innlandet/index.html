Innlandet

Country: Norway
County seat: Hamar
Established: 1 January 2020
Area: approx. 52,000 km²

Innlandet is a county in Norway, created on 1 January 2020 by the merger of the former counties of Oppland and Hedmark. The name means "the inland". It is Norway's only landlocked county, bordering Sweden to the east. The administrative centre is Hamar, while Lillehammer is another major town.

Geography

Innlandet contains Norway's highest mountains, including Galdhøpiggen in the Jotunheimen range, its largest lake, Mjøsa, and long agricultural valleys such as Gudbrandsdalen and Østerdalen. Forestry and farming are important industries, alongside tourism and winter sports.

Municipalities

The county comprises 46 municipalities, including Lillehammer, Gjøvik, Elverum, Kongsvinger, and Ringsaker. Lillehammer hosted the 1994 Winter Olympics.
