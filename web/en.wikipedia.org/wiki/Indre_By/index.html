Indre By
Neighbourhood of Copenhagen, Denmark
The historic city centre inside the former ramparts

Indre By (Danish for "Inner City") is the central neighbourhood of Copenhagen, Denmark, corresponding to the historic city centre within the line of the old fortifications. It is the oldest part of the city and contains many of its best-known landmarks and institutions.

Geography and landmarks

The district is bounded by the Copenhagen Lakes to the west and the harbour to the east, and includes Christianshavn across the harbour in the wider City Centre administrative district. Within Indre By stand Christiansborg Palace, the seat of the Danish Parliament; the Strøget pedestrian shopping streets; Nyhavn's coloured harbour houses; the Round Tower; Rosenborg Castle with the Danish Crown Jewels; and the Latin Quarter around the University of Copenhagen, founded in 1479.

Character

Indre By mixes medieval street patterns near the old centre with the regular streets laid out after the fires of the eighteenth century. It is the commercial, cultural and administrative heart of the capital, with a relatively small resident population compared with the numbers who work, study and visit there daily.
