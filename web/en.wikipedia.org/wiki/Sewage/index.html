Sewage

Sewage (or domestic wastewater) is a type of wastewater that is produced by a community of people. It typically consists of the liquid waste discharged from toilets, baths, showers, kitchens, and sinks in homes, businesses, and institutions, carried away in sewers or drains. Sewage is more than 99% water by weight, but contains dissolved and suspended organic matter, nutrients, pathogens, and trace chemicals that make its safe collection, treatment, and disposal a central concern of sanitation and public health.

Composition

Sewage contains human excreta, food scraps, soaps and detergents, and paper products. Its strength is commonly measured by biochemical oxygen demand (BOD), chemical oxygen demand (COD), and suspended solids. Untreated sewage carries bacteria, viruses, protozoa, and helminths capable of causing cholera, typhoid, dysentery, hepatitis, and other diseases.

Collection

In sewered areas, sewage is collected in networks of pipes — either sanitary sewers carrying only wastewater, or combined sewers also carrying stormwater — and conveyed, by gravity and pumping stations, to a treatment plant. Where sewers are absent, on-site systems such as septic tanks and pit latrines are used.

Treatment and disposal

Sewage treatment removes solids and reduces organic matter and pathogens through primary (physical), secondary (biological), and sometimes tertiary (advanced) processes before the effluent is discharged to water bodies or reused for irrigation and groundwater recharge. Solids separated in treatment form sewage sludge (biosolids), which may be applied to land, incinerated, or landfilled.

Environmental and health impacts

Untreated or poorly treated sewage discharged to rivers and coastal waters depletes dissolved oxygen, spreads disease, and contributes excess nitrogen and phosphorus that fuel eutrophication and harmful algal blooms. The UN estimates that globally a large fraction of wastewater is still released untreated, making improved sewage management a target of Sustainable Development Goal 6.
