Jewel in the Palace

Jewel in the Palace (Korean: 대장금, Dae Jang-geum) is a 2003 South Korean historical drama television series, directed by Lee Byung-hoon and starring Lee Young-ae in the title role. It tells the story of Jang-geum, an orphaned kitchen cook in the Joseon royal court who becomes the first female royal physician in Korean history. The series aired on MBC from September 2003 to March 2004.

Lee Young-ae as Jang-geum in the royal kitchen.

Plot

Set in the early 16th century, the story follows Seo Jang-geum, who enters the palace kitchen as a young girl after her parents are killed. She survives court intrigue, learns court cuisine and medicine, and rises from cook to court lady and finally to royal physician, serving King Jungjong. The drama weaves together palace politics, the rivalries of the kitchen and the medical bureau, and Jang-geum's romance with the court official Min Jeong-ho.

Reception

Jewel in the Palace was one of the highest-rated Korean dramas of its era, with its finale reaching over 50% of the audience in South Korea. It became a landmark of the Korean wave (Hallyu), exported to more than 90 countries and especially popular in China, Japan, Taiwan and the Middle East. Its success boosted tourism to filming locations and renewed interest in traditional Korean cuisine and medicine.

Legacy

The series spawned a 2005 animated spin-off and a 2024 sequel project, and it remains a reference point for the sageuk (historical drama) genre. The real Jang-geum is attested in the Annals of the Joseon Dynasty, which record a female physician of that name serving the king in 1515.
