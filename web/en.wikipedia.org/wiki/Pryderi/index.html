Pryderi

Pryderi fab Pwyll is a central figure of Welsh mythology and the only character to appear in all Four Branches of the Mabinogi. He is the son of Pwyll and Rhiannon, lord of Dyfed, and is associated with the otherworld kingdom of Annwn through his father's adventures there.

In the First Branch

In Pwyll Pendefig Dyfed, Pryderi is born to Pwyll and Rhiannon but vanishes on the night of his birth; the six women attending Rhiannon smear dog's blood on her and accuse her of eating the child. Rhiannon does penance until the boy is restored by Teyrnon Twrf Liant, who had found him after rescuing him from a monstrous claw that stole foals from his stable. Teyrnon names him Gwri Wallt Euryn ("Gwri of the Golden Hair") and returns him to court, where Rhiannon names him Pryderi — from pryder, "care/anxiety" — for the worry she had suffered.

In the later branches

Pryderi appears briefly in the Second Branch as one of the seven survivors of Brân's expedition to Ireland, and in the Third Branch he rules Dyfed with his mother and his wife Cigfa; when a magical mist empties the land, he is trapped in an enchanted fort by a golden bowl and must be freed by Manawydan. In the Fourth Branch, Pryderi — now a prosperous lord famed for his swine, a gift from Arawn of Annwn — is tricked by Gwydion, who steals the pigs, and is killed in single combat by Gwydion at the battle that follows. He is buried at Maen Tyriawg.

Legacy

Scholars have linked Pryderi with the figure of Mabon ap Modron and with a presumed earlier British god; his swineherd origins and otherworld livestock connect him to older Celtic traditions of the Otherworld's bounty.
