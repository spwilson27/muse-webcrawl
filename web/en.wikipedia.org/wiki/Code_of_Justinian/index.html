Code of Justinian

The Code of Justinian (Codex Justinianus) is the first part of the Corpus Juris Civilis, the great codification of Roman law ordered by the Byzantine emperor Justinian I in the 6th century CE. A first version appeared in 529; a revised edition followed in 534.

Content

Compiled by a commission under the jurist Tribonian, the Code collected and edited imperial constitutions from the time of Hadrian onward, resolving contradictions. Together with the Digest, the Institutes and the Novels, it preserved Roman law and became the foundation of the civil-law tradition in continental Europe.
