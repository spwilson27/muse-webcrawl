Juvenile (rapper)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

[Image: Juvenile performing]

Juvenile (born Terius Gray; March 25, 1975) is an American rapper from New Orleans, Louisiana. He rose to fame in the late 1990s as a member of the Hot Boys and as a solo artist on Cash Money Records, and is credited with helping popularise the New Orleans bounce-influenced Southern hip-hop sound.

Career

After early recordings including Being Myself (1995) and Solja Rags (1997), Juvenile achieved mainstream success with the album 400 Degreez (1998), which featured the hit singles "Ha" and "Back That Azz Up" (also known as "Back That Thang Up"). With the Hot Boys he released Guerrilla Warfare (1999), spawning the hit "We on Fire."

In 2004, his single "Slow Motion" (featuring Soulja Slim) reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100. He continued releasing albums including Reality Check (2006) and Beast Mode (2010), and gained renewed attention from a widely praised Tiny Desk Concert performance in 2023.
