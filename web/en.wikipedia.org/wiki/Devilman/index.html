Devilman is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Go Nagai, serialized from 1972 to 1973. It follows Akira Fudo, a kind-hearted teenager who merges with the demon Amon to become Devilman — a being with a demon's body and a human heart — in order to fight an ancient race of demons threatening humanity.

Story and themes

Warned by his friend Ryo Asuka of the coming demon invasion, Akira undergoes a ritual at a Black Sabbath to unite with Amon. The story escalates into paranoia, witch hunts, and apocalyptic war between demons and humans, ending in tragedy. The manga is noted for its graphic violence, bleak anti-war message, and influence on later dark fantasy works.

Adaptations

An anime television series aired in 1972–1973 with a more superhero-oriented tone. Later adaptations include OVA series, the crossover Cyborg 009 VS Devilman (2015), and the acclaimed Netflix anime Devilman Crybaby (2018), directed by Masaaki Yuasa.
