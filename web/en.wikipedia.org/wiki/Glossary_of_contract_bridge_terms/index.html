Glossary of contract bridge terms

This glossary of contract bridge terms defines words and phrases used in the card game of contract bridge, including terms for bidding, play, scoring, and tournament organisation.

Selected terms

  • Auction — the bidding phase in which partnerships compete to name the contract.
  • Declarer — the player who first bid the suit (or no trump) of the final contract and who plays both their own hand and dummy's.
  • Dummy — declarer's partner, whose cards are laid face up on the table after the opening lead.
  • Finesse — a play that attempts to win a trick with a lower card by exploiting the position of a higher opposing card.
  • Ruff — to play a trump on a trick when unable to follow suit.
  • Slam — a contract to win twelve tricks (small slam) or all thirteen (grand slam).
  • Stayman — a conventional 2♣ response to a 1NT opening asking for a four-card major.
  • Vulnerable — a partnership's scoring status, alternating during a rubber, which increases bonuses and penalties.
