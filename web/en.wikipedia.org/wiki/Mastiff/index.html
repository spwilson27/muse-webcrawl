Mastiff

Mastiff is a type of large domestic dog, characterised by a massive head, heavy bone, and a short muzzle. The term also refers specifically to the English Mastiff breed. Mastiff-type dogs were historically used as guardians, war dogs, and hunters of large game.

Breeds

Mastiff-type breeds include the English Mastiff, Bullmastiff, Neapolitan Mastiff, Tibetan Mastiff, Dogue de Bordeaux, Cane Corso, Spanish Mastiff, and Pyrenean Mastiff, among others recognised by kennel clubs.

History and temperament

Ancient depictions suggest mastiff-type dogs existed in antiquity in Britain and Asia. Despite their size, modern mastiffs are generally described as gentle, dignified, and affectionate with family, though they require socialisation and are prone to health issues common to giant breeds, including hip dysplasia and bloat.
