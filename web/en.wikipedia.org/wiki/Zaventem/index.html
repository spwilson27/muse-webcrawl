Zaventem
Country: Belgium
Region: Flanders
Province: Flemish Brabant
Population: about 36,000
Known for: Brussels Airport

Zaventem is a municipality in the Belgian province of Flemish Brabant, just northeast of Brussels. It is best known internationally as the location of Brussels Airport, Belgium's main international airport, which lies partly within the municipality and is often simply called "Zaventem" by travellers.

Geography

The municipality comprises the sub-municipalities of Zaventem proper, Nossegem, Sint-Stevens-Woluwe and Sterrebeek. It sits on the northeastern edge of the Brussels agglomeration and is a Dutch-speaking municipality in Flanders, though it has a substantial international population connected with the airport, NATO and the European institutions.

Brussels Airport

The airport site began as a military airfield used during the Second World War and was developed for civil aviation in the 1950s, expanding greatly for Expo 58. The airport came to international attention on 22 March 2016, when suicide bombers attacked its departure hall in the Brussels bombings, killing sixteen people at the airport.

Economy and transport

Beyond the airport, Zaventem hosts logistics firms, offices and business parks along the Brussels ring road and the A201 and E40 corridors. The municipality is served by Zaventem and Nossegem railway stations and by the airport's own station.
