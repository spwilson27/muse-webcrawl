Shanghai Hongqiao railway station

Shanghai Hongqiao railway station (Chinese: 上海虹桥站) is one of the major railway stations of Shanghai, China, located in Minhang District adjacent to Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport. Opened on 1 July 2010, it is among the largest railway stations in Asia and the principal western terminus of China's high-speed rail network for the Yangtze River Delta.

Facilities

The station covers a total floor area of about 1.3 million square metres within the Hongqiao comprehensive transport hub, which integrates the railway station, airport Terminal 2, a metro interchange, long-distance bus station and taxi and parking facilities. The station has 16 platforms serving 30 tracks, designed to handle more than 10,000 departing passengers per hour at peak. Its vast waiting hall, among the largest of any station in China, sits directly above the platforms in the manner of modern airport terminals.

Services

Hongqiao is the Shanghai terminus of the Beijing–Shanghai high-speed railway, the Shanghai–Nanjing intercity railway, the Shanghai–Hangzhou high-speed railway and lines toward Kunming, Wuhan and the southeast coast. Hundreds of G- and D-series trains depart daily. The station is served by Shanghai Metro lines 2, 10 and 17, and is directly connected to Hongqiao Airport Terminal 2, allowing air–rail transfers.
