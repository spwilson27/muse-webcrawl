Santiago Bernabéu Stadium

Santiago Bernabéu
Location: Madrid, Spain
Opened: 1947
Home team: Real Madrid CF

The Santiago Bernabéu Stadium is a football stadium in Madrid, Spain, home of Real Madrid CF since its completion in 1947. It is named after Santiago Bernabéu, the club's legendary president. With a capacity of over 80,000, it is one of the world's most famous football venues.

History

Construction began in 1944 on land beside the old Chamartín stadium; the new ground opened on 14 December 1947. It hosted the 1982 FIFA World Cup final, four European Cup/Champions League finals, and the second leg of the 2018 Copa Libertadores final. Successive expansions added a third tier and a roof.

Renovation

A major remodelling begun in 2019 wrapped the stadium in a futuristic metallic shell with a retractable roof, a 360-degree video scoreboard, and an underground storage system allowing the pitch to be swapped for other events, turning the Bernabéu into a year-round entertainment venue.
