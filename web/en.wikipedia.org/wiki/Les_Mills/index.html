Les Mills

Les Mills International is a New Zealand company that creates and licenses choreographed group fitness programmes to gyms and instructors in more than 100 countries. Its flagship classes include BODYPUMP (barbell-based strength training), BODYCOMBAT (martial arts-inspired cardio), BODYBALANCE/BODYFLOW (yoga and Pilates fusion), indoor cycling formats RPM and THE TRIP, and the high-intensity programme GRIT.

History

The company traces to Les Mills (1934–2003), a New Zealand athlete who represented his country at the Olympic Games and Commonwealth Games in track and field and discus, and later served as mayor of Auckland. He opened the first Les Mills gym in Auckland in 1968. His son Phillip Mills, returning from the United States in the 1980s, developed choreographed classes set to music; BODYPUMP, launched in 1991, became the breakthrough format exported worldwide from 1997 under Les Mills International.

Business model

Les Mills licenses its programmes to clubs and certifies instructors, releasing new choreography and music quarterly for each programme. The company claims millions of weekly participants worldwide and has expanded into on-demand streaming (LES MILLS+), virtual classes, and equipment such as the SMARTBAR. Headquartered in Auckland, it remains one of New Zealand's best-known consumer brands.
