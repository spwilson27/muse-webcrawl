List of NASA missions

The National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) has conducted many uncrewed and crewed spaceflight missions since its founding in 1958. This list covers major programs and notable missions.

Crewed programs

ProgramYearsNotes
Mercury1958–1963First U.S. crewed program; Alan Shepard first American in space (1961); John Glenn first American to orbit Earth (1962)
Gemini1961–1966Developed rendezvous, docking and EVA techniques
Apollo1961–1972Apollo 11 landed the first humans on the Moon, July 20, 1969
Skylab1973–1974First U.S. space station
Apollo–Soyuz1975First joint U.S.–Soviet crewed mission
Space Shuttle1981–2011135 missions; orbiters Columbia, Challenger, Discovery, Atlantis, Endeavour
International Space Station1998–presentContinuously crewed since November 2000
Commercial Crew2020–presentSpaceX Crew Dragon and Boeing Starliner flights
Artemis2022–presentReturn of crew to the Moon; Artemis I uncrewed flight 2022

Notable robotic missions

MissionLaunchDestination / achievement
Explorer 11958First U.S. satellite; discovered Van Allen belts
Pioneer 10 / 111972 / 1973First traversals of the asteroid belt; Jupiter and Saturn flybys
Voyager 1 / 21977Grand Tour of the outer planets; now in interstellar space
Viking 1 / 21975First successful Mars landers
Galileo1989First Jupiter orbiter
Hubble Space Telescope1990Orbiting observatory, deployed from Discovery
Cassini–Huygens1997Saturn orbiter; Huygens landed on Titan (2005)
Mars Pathfinder / Sojourner1996First Mars rover
Mars Exploration Rovers (Spirit, Opportunity)2003Long-lived Mars rovers
New Horizons2006First Pluto flyby (2015); Arrokoth flyby (2019)
Mars Science Laboratory (Curiosity)2011Nuclear-powered Mars rover
Juno2011Jupiter polar orbiter
Mars 2020 (Perseverance, Ingenuity)2020Sample caching rover and first powered flight on another planet
James Webb Space Telescope2021Infrared observatory at Sun–Earth L2
DART2021First planetary defense asteroid deflection test (2022)
Europa Clipper2024En route to study Jupiter's moon Europa

NASA missions are operated by centers including the Jet Propulsion Laboratory, Goddard Space Flight Center, and Johnson Space Center.
