List of TCP and UDP port numbers

The List of TCP and UDP port numbers catalogues the port numbers used by the Transmission Control Protocol (TCP) and User Datagram Protocol (UDP) to direct traffic to specific processes or services on a host. Port numbers are 16-bit unsigned integers from 0 to 65535, administered by the Internet Assigned Numbers Authority (IANA).

Ranges

  • Well-known ports: 0–1023, assigned to the most common services and requiring administrative privilege to bind on many systems.
  • Registered ports: 1024–49151, assigned by IANA on request.
  • Dynamic/private ports: 49152–65535, intended for ephemeral client-side use.
