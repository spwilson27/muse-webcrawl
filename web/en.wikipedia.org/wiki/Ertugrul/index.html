Ertuğrul (Ottoman Turkish: ارطغرل; died c. 1280), also rendered Ertugrul, was a 13th-century Turkmen leader of the Kayı tribe and the father of Osman I, the founder of the Ottoman Empire. According to Ottoman tradition, Ertuğrul led his tribe westward into Anatolia and settled at Söğüt, where his son would establish the principality that grew into the Ottoman state.

Historical evidence

Very little is known with certainty about Ertuğrul's life. No contemporary sources mention him by name; the earliest references appear in Ottoman chronicles written more than a century after his death, such as the works of Aşıkpaşazade and Neşri. A coin attributed to Osman I names his father as Ertuğrul, which is among the earliest material evidence for his existence. Historians debate the details of his genealogy: Ottoman tradition makes him the son of Süleyman Şah, the legendary leader whose tomb lies in Syria, while other sources name Gündüz Alp as his father.

Legend and tradition

Ottoman narratives recount that Ertuğrul and his followers migrated from Central Asia through Iran and the Caucasus before entering Anatolia. In one famous episode, Ertuğrul intervened in a battle between the Seljuks of Rum and a Mongol or Byzantine force, turning the tide for the Seljuks; in gratitude, Sultan Alaeddin Kayqubad I granted him lands around Söğüt, near the Byzantine frontier. From this base the Kayı expanded at Byzantine expense, a process continued by Osman.

Death and tomb

Ertuğrul is said to have died around 1280 at an advanced age and was buried at Söğüt, where his tomb remains a place of commemoration. The annual Ertuğrul Gazi commemoration ceremonies at Söğüt celebrate him as the progenitor of the Ottoman dynasty.

In popular culture

Ertuğrul became internationally known through the Turkish television series Diriliş: Ertuğrul ("Resurrection: Ertuğrul", 2014–2019), in which he was portrayed by Engin Altan Düzyatan. The drama, though heavily fictionalised, sparked wide interest in Ottoman history across Turkey and abroad, and its sequel series Kuruluş: Osman follows the career of his son.
