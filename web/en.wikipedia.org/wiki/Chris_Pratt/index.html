Chris Pratt

Chris Pratt (born 1979) is an American actor who rose to prominence as Andy Dwyer on the sitcom Parks and Recreation (2009–2015) before becoming a leading man in major film franchises.

Career

Pratt starred as Peter Quill / Star-Lord in Marvel's Guardians of the Galaxy films and as Owen Grady in the Jurassic World trilogy, and voiced Mario in The Super Mario Bros. Movie (2023) and Emmet in The Lego Movie (2014). His other roles include Moneyball (2011), Zero Dark Thirty (2012), Passengers (2016) and the series The Terminal List (2022).

Personal life

Pratt was married to actress Anna Faris, with whom he has a son; he later married Katherine Schwarzenegger, with whom he has children.
