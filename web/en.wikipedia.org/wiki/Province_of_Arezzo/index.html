The Province of Arezzo (Italian: Provincia di Arezzo) is a province in the Tuscany region of central Italy. Its capital is the city of Arezzo. The province is the easternmost of Tuscany's provinces, bordering Umbria, Marche and Emilia-Romagna, and comprises 36 municipalities.

Geography

The territory is largely hilly and mountainous, shaped by the upper valleys of four rivers: the Arno (Valdarno), the Tiber (Valtiberina), the Marecchia and the Casentino valley of the upper Arno, enclosed by the Apennines and the Pratomagno range. The Chiana valley (Valdichiana) in the south is a fertile agricultural plain.

Main towns

Other important towns include Cortona, Sansepolcro (birthplace of Piero della Francesca), Montevarchi, Bibbiena, Poppi, San Giovanni Valdarno and Anghiari, site of the famous 1440 battle depicted by Leonardo da Vinci in a lost fresco.

History and culture

Arezzo was one of the twelve cities of the Etruscan League. The province is rich in Renaissance art: Piero della Francesca's Legend of the True Cross frescoes are in Arezzo's church of San Francesco, and the artist was born in Sansepolcro. The poet Petrarch and the artist and writer Giorgio Vasari were also born in Arezzo. The province is known for goldworking, furniture and fashion manufacturing.
