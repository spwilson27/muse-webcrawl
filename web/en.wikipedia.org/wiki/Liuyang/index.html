Liuyang

Liuyang (simplified Chinese: 浏阳; traditional Chinese: 瀏陽; pinyin: Liúyáng) is a county-level city in Hunan Province, China, administered by the prefecture-level city of Changsha. It is world-famous as the historic home of the Chinese fireworks industry.

Fireworks industry

According to local tradition, Li Gong, a monk of the Tang dynasty era, invented firecrackers in the Liuyang area by filling bamboo tubes with gunpowder. The city became the centre of China's fireworks trade, and today Liuyang produces a large share of the world's consumer and display fireworks. An annual international fireworks festival is held on the banks of the Liuyang River, and the "Liuyang fireworks" brand is a protected geographical indication.

Geography and administration

The Liuyang River flows through the city toward Changsha, where it joins the Xiang River. The city administers numerous towns and subdistricts and borders Jiangxi Province to the east.
