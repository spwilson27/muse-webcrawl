Bilawal Bhutto Zardari (born 21 September 1988) is a Pakistani politician who has served as chairman of the Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP) since 2007. The son of former prime minister Benazir Bhutto and former president Asif Ali Zardari, and the grandson of former prime minister Zulfikar Ali Bhutto, he is a scion of the Bhutto political dynasty.

Early life and education

Bilawal was born in Karachi and spent much of his childhood abroad during his mother's periods of exile. He studied modern history and politics at Christ Church, University of Oxford. Following the assassination of Benazir Bhutto on 27 December 2007, the nineteen-year-old Bilawal was named chairman of the PPP, with his father as co-chairman; he added "Bhutto" to his name at that time.

Political career

After completing his studies, Bilawal formally launched his political career in 2012. He was elected to the National Assembly of Pakistan from Larkana in the 2018 general election. Following the removal of Imran Khan in April 2022, he became Minister of Foreign Affairs in the coalition government of Shehbaz Sharif, serving until August 2023 — among the youngest foreign ministers in Pakistan's history. In that role he undertook high-profile diplomacy, including addressing Pakistan's position after the 2022 floods and engaging with India, China, and the United States.

Bilawal led the PPP into the February 2024 general election, after which his father Asif Ali Zardari returned to the presidency and the PPP supported the PML-N-led coalition government while Bilawal remained outside the cabinet.
