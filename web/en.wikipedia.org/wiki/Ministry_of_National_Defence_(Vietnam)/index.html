Ministry of National Defence (Vietnam)

Formed1945
HeadquartersBa Đình District, Hanoi
JurisdictionVietnam
Parent bodyGovernment of Vietnam

The Ministry of National Defence (Vietnamese: Bộ Quốc phòng) is the governmental ministry of Vietnam responsible for the organization, building, and command of the country's armed forces, the People's Army of Vietnam (PAVN). Headquartered in Hanoi, it manages military affairs, national defence policy implementation, and the defence industry.

Role and structure

The ministry operates under the leadership of the Central Military Commission of the Communist Party of Vietnam and the authority of the National Assembly and the Government. Its subordinate bodies include the General Staff, the General Political Department, and general departments for logistics, technology, and defence industry. The Minister of National Defence, traditionally a senior general, sits on the Politburo. The ministry oversees the army, navy, air force, border guard, and coast guard, as well as military-run enterprises such as the telecommunications group Viettel.

History

Founded in 1945 in the Democratic Republic of Vietnam, the ministry directed the armed forces through the First Indochina War and the Vietnam War; after reunification in 1976 it became the defence ministry of the unified Socialist Republic of Vietnam.
