The O2

Location: Greenwich Peninsula, London, England
Opened: 2000 (as the Millennium Dome); reopened as The O2 in 2007
Arena capacity: 20,000
Named for: O2 (Telefónica's mobile brand)

The O2 is a large entertainment district on the Greenwich Peninsula in south-east London, centred on the former Millennium Dome, one of the largest single-roofed structures in the world. Reopened in 2007 after redevelopment by Anschutz Entertainment Group, it takes its name from the O2 mobile network under a naming-rights agreement.

The arena

Inside the dome sits The O2 Arena, a 20,000-capacity multi-purpose venue that is among the busiest music arenas in the world by ticket sales. It has hosted residencies and concerts by artists including Prince, Beyoncé, and Elton John; the ATP Finals in tennis from 2009 to 2020; boxing, darts, and gymnastics at the 2012 Summer Olympics (when it was temporarily called the North Greenwich Arena). Michael Jackson's cancelled This Is It residency was to have been staged there.

The district

Around the arena, the dome houses restaurants, bars, a cinema, the smaller indigo at The O2 venue, and ICON Outlet shopping. Since 2012 visitors have been able to climb over the dome's roof on the "Up at The O2" guided experience.
