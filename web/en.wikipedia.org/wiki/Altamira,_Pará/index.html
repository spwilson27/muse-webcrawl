Altamira is a municipality in the state of Pará in northern Brazil, located on the Xingu River. By area it is one of the largest municipalities in the world, covering about 159,000 square kilometres — larger than many countries — though most of its territory is Amazon rainforest and protected or Indigenous land.

History

Founded as a Jesuit mission area in the 18th century, Altamira grew with the rubber trade and expanded rapidly after the Trans-Amazonian Highway reached it in the 1970s, drawing settlers and ranching into the region.

Belo Monte Dam

Altamira is the gateway to the Belo Monte Dam on the Xingu River, one of the world's largest hydroelectric plants, completed in the late 2010s. Its construction brought a population boom and fierce controversy over displacement and impacts on Indigenous peoples and river ecosystems.
