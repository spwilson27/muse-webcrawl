Anthropic PBC is an American artificial intelligence company founded in 2021 by former members of OpenAI, including siblings Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei. Structured as a public benefit corporation, the company develops large language models under the name Claude and conducts research on AI safety and interpretability.

Anthropic's founders left OpenAI over disagreements about safety and the direction of commercialisation. The company has raised large investments from technology firms including Google and Amazon, and positions itself as focused on the reliable, interpretable and steerable deployment of AI systems.

Claude

Claude is a family of large language models first released in 2023. Models are trained with a technique Anthropic calls Constitutional AI, in which a model is supervised and critiqued against an explicit set of principles ("a constitution") rather than solely by human feedback, a variant of reinforcement learning from AI feedback. Successive generations — including Claude 2, the Claude 3 family (Haiku, Sonnet and Opus), and later releases — have expanded context length, multimodal ability and computer-use capabilities. Claude is offered through a web assistant, an API and a command-line coding tool.

Research and governance

Anthropic publishes research on mechanistic interpretability — reverse-engineering the internal features of neural networks — and on alignment and evaluation of frontier models. Its Responsible Scaling Policy commits the company to evaluate models for dangerous capabilities and to apply safeguards scaled to model capability, categorised by "AI Safety Levels". A Long-Term Benefit Trust holds a governance role intended to align the company's decisions with its public-benefit mission.

Funding

The company has raised billions of dollars in funding rounds, including a strategic partnership with Amazon worth several billion dollars and an earlier investment from Google, valuing the company in the tens of billions of dollars by 2024–2025.
