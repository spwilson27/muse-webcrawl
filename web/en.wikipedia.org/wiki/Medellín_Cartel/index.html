The Medellín Cartel (Spanish: Cartel de Medellín) was a powerful and highly organised Colombian drug cartel and narcoterrorist organisation originating in the city of Medellín, Colombia, that was founded and led by Pablo Escobar. Operating from the mid-1970s to the early 1990s, it controlled the majority of the world's cocaine trade at its height and waged a violent campaign of assassinations and bombings against the Colombian state in an effort to block extradition of its members to the United States.

History

The cartel emerged in the mid-1970s as cocaine became a lucrative export to the United States. Escobar built a vertically integrated organisation that bought coca paste in Bolivia and Peru, processed it in jungle laboratories in Colombia, and smuggled finished cocaine by aircraft — notably through Norman's Cay in the Bahamas, run by trafficker Carlos Lehder — into the United States. At its peak the organisation was estimated to supply around 80 percent of the cocaine entering the United States and to earn billions of dollars per year.

The narcoterrorist campaign

When the Colombian government moved toward extraditing traffickers to the United States, the cartel's "Extraditables" faction responded with a campaign of terror. It was responsible for, or implicated in, the 1985 Palace of Justice siege (in cooperation with the M-19 guerrillas, according to later investigations), the assassination of Justice Minister Rodrigo Lara Bonilla in 1984 and of presidential candidate Luis Carlos Galán in 1989, and the bombing of Avianca Flight 203 and the DAS headquarters in Bogotá in 1989.

Downfall

Escobar surrendered in 1991 under a deal forbidding extradition and was confined in his self-built prison, La Catedral, from which he escaped in July 1992. A manhunt by the Search Bloc of the Colombian police, aided by the vigilante group Los Pepes and a rival campaign by the Cali Cartel, culminated in Escobar's death on a Medellín rooftop on 2 December 1993. The cartel fragmented thereafter, and its surviving leaders were killed or imprisoned.
