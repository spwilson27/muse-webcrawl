Governors Awards

Presented byAcademy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences
First awarded14 November 2009

AwardsHonorary Award; Irving G. Thalberg Memorial Award; Jean Hersholt Humanitarian Award

The Governors Awards is an annual awards ceremony hosted by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences (AMPAS) in Hollywood, at which the Academy presents its honorary awards — the Honorary Award, the Irving G. Thalberg Memorial Award and the Jean Hersholt Humanitarian Award.

History

Before 2009, honorary Oscars were presented during the main televised Academy Awards ceremony. To allow recipients fuller tributes and to shorten the Oscar telecast, the Academy's Board of Governors created a separate, untelevised ceremony; the first Governors Awards dinner was held on 14 November 2009, honouring Lauren Bacall, Roger Corman, Gordon Willis and John Calley.

Ceremony

The event is a black-tie dinner held each autumn, generally November, at the Ray Dolby Ballroom in Hollywood. Recipients are selected by the Board of Governors, and their statuettes are presented by colleagues in extended tributes; edited highlights are later shown during the Academy Awards broadcast.
