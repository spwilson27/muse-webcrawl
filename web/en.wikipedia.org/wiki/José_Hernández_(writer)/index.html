José Hernández (writer)

José Rafael Hernández y Pueyrredón (November 10, 1834 – October 21, 1886) was an Argentine journalist, poet and politician best known as the author of Martín Fierro, the epic poem regarded as the masterpiece of gaucho literature and a cornerstone of Argentine national identity.

Life

Hernández was born at the Chacra Pueyrredón, in present-day San Martín, Buenos Aires Province. Raised partly on rural estancias, he knew gaucho life at first hand. He worked as a journalist, fought in the civil wars of the period, lived in exile in Brazil, and later served as a provincial and national legislator, defending the rights of rural labourers and advocating the federalisation of Buenos Aires.

Martín Fierro

El Gaucho Martín Fierro (1872) and its sequel, La vuelta de Martín Fierro (1879), narrate the life of a gaucho conscripted into frontier military service who becomes an outlaw and later returns to society. Written in the payada style of rural balladeers, the poem protested the treatment of the gauchos and became the best-selling work of nineteenth-century Argentina. Argentina's Tradition Day is celebrated on Hernández's birthday.
