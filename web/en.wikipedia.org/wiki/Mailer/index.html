Mailer

Mailer may refer to several things:

  • A mailing envelope or padded shipping envelope used to send items through postal services.
  • A person or organization that sends mail; in direct marketing, a business that sends advertising mail.
  • Mailer (computer), software for sending email or managing electronic mailing lists.
  • Norman Mailer (1923–2007), American novelist, journalist, and essayist.
  • Mailer, a Demon in the Japanese novel and media series.

Shipping mailers

In packaging, a mailer is a protective envelope or lightweight box designed for sending goods by post or courier. Common forms include padded mailers lined with bubble wrap or paper cushioning, poly mailers made of flexible plastic film for soft goods, rigid cardboard mailers for documents and photographs, and corrugated mailer boxes for small parcels. They are widely used in e-commerce because they are lighter and cheaper than full shipping cartons, and many are made with recycled or recyclable materials.

Norman Mailer

The surname is most famously borne by Norman Mailer, a leading figure of the New Journalism, author of The Naked and the Dead (1948), The Armies of the Night (1968, Pulitzer Prize), and The Executioner's Song (1979, Pulitzer Prize).
