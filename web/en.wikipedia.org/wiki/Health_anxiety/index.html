Health anxiety

Health anxiety is a condition in which a person worries excessively that they are or may become seriously ill, interpreting normal bodily sensations as signs of severe disease. It encompasses what was historically called hypochondriasis or hypochondria; in DSM-5, most such cases are classified as somatic symptom disorder or illness anxiety disorder.

Features

People with health anxiety may repeatedly check their bodies, seek reassurance from doctors, undergo unnecessary tests, or conversely avoid medical care entirely for fear of bad news. Reassurance provides only brief relief, and the cycle of worry is amplified by searching symptoms online — sometimes called "cyberchondria."

Treatment

Cognitive behavioural therapy adapted for health anxiety is the best-supported treatment, helping patients test catastrophic interpretations and reduce checking and reassurance-seeking. Antidepressant medication, particularly SSRIs, may also help, especially when anxiety or depression co-occurs.
