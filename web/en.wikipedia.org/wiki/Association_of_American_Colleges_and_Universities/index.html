The Association of American Colleges and Universities (AAC&U) is a national association in the United States dedicated to advancing the vitality and public standing of liberal education. Founded in 1915 as the Association of American Colleges, it counts roughly a thousand member institutions, from small liberal arts colleges to large research universities.

Work

AAC&U is known for its Liberal Education and America's Promise (LEAP) initiative, which promotes "essential learning outcomes" and high-impact educational practices such as first-year seminars, undergraduate research, capstone projects and community-based learning. It publishes the journals Liberal Education and Peer Review.

History

The association was created by a group of college presidents to represent liberal arts institutions; in 1995 it added "Universities" to its name to reflect its broader membership.
