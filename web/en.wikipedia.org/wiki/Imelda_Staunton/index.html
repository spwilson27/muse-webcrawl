Imelda Mary Philomena Bernadette Staunton (born 9 January 1956) is an English actress. Known for her work on stage and screen, she has won four Olivier Awards and a BAFTA Award, and received an Academy Award nomination. She played Dolores Umbridge in the Harry Potter films and Queen Elizabeth II in the final two seasons of The Crown.

Early life and training

Staunton was born in Archway, north London, to Irish immigrant parents. She trained at the Royal Academy of Dramatic Art (RADA) and began her career in repertory theatre.

Career

Staunton earned acclaim in musicals including Into the Woods, Sweeney Todd, Gypsy and Hello, Dolly!. Her breakthrough film role as the title character in Mike Leigh's Vera Drake (2004) won her the BAFTA Award for Best Actress and an Oscar nomination. Other notable film work includes Sense and Sensibility (1995), Shakespeare in Love (1998), Nanny McPhee (2005), Pride (2014) and the voice of Aunt Lucy in the Paddington films. From 2022 she portrayed Queen Elizabeth II in The Crown.

Honours

Staunton was appointed a Dame Commander of the Order of the British Empire (DBE) in 2024 for services to drama and charity.
