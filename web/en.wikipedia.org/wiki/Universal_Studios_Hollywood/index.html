Universal Studios Hollywood is a film studio and theme park in the San Fernando Valley area of Los Angeles County, California. About 70% of the studio lies within the unincorporated county island known as Universal City, while the rest lies within the city limits of Los Angeles. It is one of the oldest and most famous Hollywood film studios still in use, and its official marketing headline is "The Entertainment Capital of LA".

History

Universal founder Carl Laemmle opened Universal City in 1915 and originally charged visitors 25 cents to watch films being made. The modern theme park began on July 15, 1964, when the Studio Tour — a tram ride through the working backlot — opened to the public. Over the decades the park grew to include attractions based on films such as Jaws, King Kong, Jurassic Park, Transformers, and The Simpsons. The park is divided by a steep hillside into the Upper Lot and Lower Lot, connected by a series of escalators known as the Starway.

Attractions

The Studio Tour remains the signature attraction, passing working soundstages and sets including the Bates Motel from Psycho and the crashed airliner set built for War of the Worlds. The Wizarding World of Harry Potter opened in 2016, and Super Nintendo World opened in February 2023, headlined by the Mario Kart: Bowser's Challenge ride. Adjacent to the park is Universal CityWalk, a shopping and entertainment district.
