Kane Tanaka

Born: Kane Ota, 2 January 1903, Wajiro, Fukuoka, Japan
Died: 19 April 2022 (aged 119), Fukuoka, Japan
Known for: World's oldest living person (2018–2022); second-oldest verified person in history

Kane Tanaka (田中 力子, Tanaka Kane, née Ota; 2 January 1903 – 19 April 2022) was a Japanese supercentenarian who, at the age of 119 years and 107 days, was the second-oldest verified person in history, behind only Jeanne Calment of France. She was the world's oldest living person from the death of Chiyo Miyako on 22 July 2018 until her own death in April 2022, and the oldest Japanese person ever recorded.

Life

Tanaka was born in the village of Wajiro (now part of Fukuoka) in Fukuoka Prefecture, the seventh of nine children. In 1922 she married her cousin Hideo Tanaka, and the couple ran a shop selling sticky rice and udon noodles. She continued working in the family store until she was 103.

She survived several serious illnesses, including colon cancer in her forties, and at the age of 103 underwent surgery. In later life she lived in a nursing home in Higashi-ku, Fukuoka, where she enjoyed playing the board game Othello, doing calligraphy and solving arithmetic problems. She credited her longevity to faith, family, sleep and hope, and enjoyed carbonated drinks and chocolate.

Longevity records

On 22 July 2018, following the death of Chiyo Miyako, Tanaka became the world's oldest living person at 115. Guinness World Records formally recognised her as the oldest living person on 9 March 2019. On 18 September 2020 she surpassed Nabi Tajima to become the oldest Japanese person ever, and in January 2021 she became only the third person in history verified to reach the age of 118.

Tanaka was selected as a torchbearer for the Tokyo 2020 Olympic torch relay, but withdrew because of concerns about COVID-19. In September 2021 she was vaccinated against COVID-19, making her one of the oldest vaccinated people. She celebrated her 119th birthday on 2 January 2022 and died on 19 April 2022 in a hospital in Fukuoka. Her death left Lucile Randon of France as the world's oldest living person.
