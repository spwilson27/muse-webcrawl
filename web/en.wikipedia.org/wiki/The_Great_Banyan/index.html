The Great Banyan, which resembles a small forest

The Great Banyan is a giant banyan tree (Ficus benghalensis) located in the Acharya Jagadish Chandra Bose Indian Botanic Garden in Howrah, near Kolkata, India. It is widely considered the largest tree in the world by canopy area, forming what looks like a dense forest from a single organism.

Description and history

The tree is estimated to be more than 250 years old and appears in travel accounts from the nineteenth century. Its main trunk, diseased after being struck by lightning and damaged by cyclones, was removed in 1925, but the tree survives and continues to grow through its thousands of aerial prop roots, which descend from the branches and root in the ground as secondary trunks. A road was built around its circumference, and the tree remains one of the principal attractions of the botanic garden.
