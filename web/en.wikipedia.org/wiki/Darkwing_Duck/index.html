Darkwing Duck

Darkwing Duck is an American animated television series produced by Walt Disney Television Animation that aired from 1991 to 1992 as part of The Disney Afternoon. Created by Tad Stones, it is a superhero parody set in the fictional city of St. Canard and is a spin-off of DuckTales.

Premise

The series follows Drake Mallard, an ordinary suburban duck who secretly fights crime as the flamboyant, ego-driven hero Darkwing Duck — "the terror that flaps in the night". He is aided by his sidekick and pilot Launchpad McQuack (carried over from DuckTales) and his adopted daughter Gosalyn. Recurring villains include Negaduck, Megavolt, Quackerjack, Bushroot and Liquidator, collectively known as the Fearsome Five, and the organisation F.O.W.L.

Production and legacy

The show mixed slapstick comedy with affectionate parodies of pulp heroes and comic-book superheroes such as Batman and the Shadow. It ran for 91 episodes and has remained a cult favourite; the character later appeared in comics and in the 2017 DuckTales reboot, where Darkwing Duck exists as a fictional show-within-a-show.
