edX is an online learning platform that hosts massive open online courses (MOOCs) and degree programmes from universities and other institutions around the world. It was founded in 2012 by Harvard University and the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, each contributing $30 million, as a non-profit venture intended to offer high-quality education to anyone with an internet connection and to research how students learn.

History

edX grew out of MIT's MITx initiative, whose first course, "Circuits and Electronics," attracted about 155,000 students in 2012. Harvard joined the effort and the platform launched publicly in May 2012. The open-source software behind edX, Open edX, was released in 2013 and powers other learning sites. Over time the platform expanded from individual courses to MicroMasters programmes, professional certificates and full online master's degrees.

In 2021 the non-profit edX organisation and brand were sold to the for-profit education technology company 2U for about $800 million, with proceeds funding a new non-profit foundation. The Open edX platform was transferred to a Harvard- and MIT-affiliated non-profit.

Courses

Courses on edX cover subjects including computer science, engineering, humanities, business, data science and public health. Many courses are free to audit, with payment required for graded assignments and verified certificates. Well-known offerings include Harvard's CS50 introduction to computer science.
