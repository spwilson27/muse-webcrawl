Megatron

Megatron is a fictional character and the main antagonist of the Transformers franchise, created by Hasbro in 1984. He is the supreme leader of the Decepticons, the tyrannical rival faction to Optimus Prime's Autobots, and one of the most recognised villains in popular culture. In the original Generation 1 continuity, Megatron transformed into a Walther P38 pistol; later incarnations transform into tanks, jets, and other war machines. In many continuities he is rebuilt by Unicron into the more powerful Galvatron.

Character

Megatron is typically depicted as a former gladiator or miner on Cybertron who rose to lead a revolution and became a ruthless conqueror bent on dominating Cybertron and the universe and harvesting Earth's energy. His signature weapon is the arm-mounted fusion cannon; his motto is often rendered as "Peace through tyranny!" He is brilliant but arrogant, treating even loyal Decepticons such as Starscream — whose treachery he repeatedly tolerates — as expendable.

Appearances

Megatron has appeared in virtually every Transformers series: the original 1984 animated series and The Transformers: The Movie (1986), Beast Wars (as a descendant namesake), Transformers: Animated, Transformers: Prime, the live-action film series directed by Michael Bay and successors, and numerous comics (Marvel, Dreamwave, IDW) and games. Frank Welker originated the voice in Generation 1 and has reprised it in later media; Leonard Nimoy and Frank Welker voiced Galvatron in the 1986 film and subsequent series respectively, and Hugo Weaving voiced Megatron in the first three live-action films.

Reception

Megatron is regularly ranked among the greatest cartoon and toy-line villains of the 1980s and among the most iconic robot characters in fiction. The name derives from a portmanteau of "megaton" and "electronic".
