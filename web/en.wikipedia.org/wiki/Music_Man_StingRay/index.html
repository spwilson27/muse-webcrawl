Music Man StingRay
ManufacturerMusic Man (Ernie Ball)
Introduced1976
TypeElectric bass guitar

The Music Man StingRay is an electric bass guitar introduced by Music Man in 1976. Designed with input from Leo Fender, it was one of the first production basses to feature active electronics, with a built-in preamp powered by a 9-volt battery.

Design

The StingRay features a single large humbucking pickup positioned near the bridge, a distinctive 3+1 tuning machine headstock arrangement, and an oval control plate. Its bright, punchy tone made it popular in funk, rock, and pop.

Players

Famous users include Flea of the Red Hot Chili Peppers, Louis Johnson, Bernard Edwards, and Tony Levin. Variants include the StingRay 5, introduced in 1987 as the company's first five-string bass.
