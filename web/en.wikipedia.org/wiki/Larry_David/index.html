Larry David

Born: Lawrence Gene David, July 2, 1947, Brooklyn, New York, U.S.
Occupation: Comedian, writer, actor, producer
Known for: Seinfeld, Curb Your Enthusiasm

Lawrence Gene David (born July 2, 1947) is an American comedian, writer, actor, director, and television producer. Along with Jerry Seinfeld, he co-created the NBC sitcom Seinfeld, serving as its head writer and executive producer during its first seven seasons. He went on to create and star in the HBO series Curb Your Enthusiasm, playing a semi-fictionalised version of himself in a largely improvised comedy about social conventions and everyday grievances. Curb Your Enthusiasm ran for twelve seasons between 2000 and 2024.

Early life and career

David was born in Brooklyn, New York, to a Jewish family, and graduated from the University of Maryland, College Park with a degree in history. He worked odd jobs — including store clerk, limousine driver, and historian — while pursuing stand-up comedy, and wrote for ABC's Fridays and NBC's Saturday Night Live, where he famously got only one sketch on the air. A story from his SNL tenure — quitting his job in frustration and returning days later as if nothing had happened — later became the basis of the Seinfeld episode "The Revenge".

Seinfeld

In 1989 David and Jerry Seinfeld created The Seinfeld Chronicles, which became Seinfeld, one of the most successful sitcoms of all time. David based the character George Costanza substantially on himself and provided the voice of George Steinbrenner. Guided by his rule of "no hugging, no learning," the show's observational humour about the trivial rules of daily life made David wealthy when the series entered syndication.

Curb Your Enthusiasm and later work

Beginning with a 1999 HBO special, Curb Your Enthusiasm followed "Larry David" through social disasters of his own making, improvised from detailed outlines. The show earned numerous Emmy and Golden Globe nominations and concluded with its twelfth season in 2024. David also wrote and starred in the HBO film Clear History (2013) and the Broadway play Fish in the Dark (2015), and gained renewed attention for his impression of Senator Bernie Sanders on Saturday Night Live.
