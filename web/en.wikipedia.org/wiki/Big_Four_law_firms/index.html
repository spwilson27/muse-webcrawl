Big Four law firms

CountrySouth Korea
FirmsKim & Chang; Bae, Kim & Lee; Shin & Kim; Yulchon

The term Big Four law firms is used in several jurisdictions for the dominant group of large law firms. In South Korea it commonly refers to Kim & Chang, Bae, Kim & Lee (BKL), Shin & Kim and Yulchon, the country's largest firms by revenue and lawyer numbers.

Usage

Similar groupings exist elsewhere: Japan has a "Big Four" of major firms (Nishimura & Asahi, Mori Hamada & Matsumoto, Nagashima Ohno & Tsunematsu and Anderson Mori & Tomotsune), while other markets use terms such as the Magic Circle in London.

Practice

Big Four firms typically handle the largest corporate, finance, dispute-resolution and cross-border matters in their markets and recruit from the top of the legal profession.
