The World DanceSport Federation (WDSF) is the international governing body for DanceSport — competitive ballroom dancing — including the International Standard and Latin styles, and for related disciplines such as breaking (breakdancing), rock 'n' roll and boogie woogie through its affiliated bodies. It has around ninety national member federations and is recognised by the International Olympic Committee.

History

The federation was founded on 12 May 1957 in Wiesbaden, West Germany, as the International Council of Amateur Dancers (ICAD). It was renamed the International DanceSport Federation (IDSF) in 1990 as the sport professionalised, and adopted its present name, World DanceSport Federation, in 2011. The IOC granted the federation full recognition in 1997, and DanceSport appeared as a demonstration feature of the closing ceremony era of Olympic sport development; its discipline of breaking was included in the programme of the 2018 Summer Youth Olympics in Buenos Aires and made its full Olympic debut, under the WDSF's governance, at the Paris 2024 Olympic Games.

Competitions and disciplines

The WDSF sanctions world and continental championships, the GrandSlam series and the World Open circuit in the ten International dances: the five Standard dances (waltz, tango, Viennese waltz, slow foxtrot and quickstep) and the five Latin dances (samba, cha-cha-cha, rumba, paso doble and jive). Formation (team) dancing, ten-dance combined events, and competitions for senior, youth and professional division couples are also governed by the federation. It maintains world ranking lists, adjudicator licensing, anti-doping rules in compliance with the World Anti-Doping Agency, and the judging framework used across member competitions.

Relations with other bodies

Throughout its history the WDSF has existed alongside rival or complementary organisations, most notably the World Dance Council (WDC), which historically governed the professional circuit; the two bodies have at times competed for the allegiance of dancers and national associations. The WDSF is a member of the Association of IOC Recognised International Sports Federations (ARISF) and of SportAccord (GAISF).
