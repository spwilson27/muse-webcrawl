National day

A national day is a day on which celebrations mark a nation's statehood or sovereignty. Most national days are public holidays. They often mark independence from a colonial power, the formation of a state, the signing of a constitution, or another significant historical event such as the birthday of a monarch or a national patron saint's feast day.

Significance

National days are typically celebrated with parades, fireworks, flag displays, speeches by heads of state, and cultural events. Some countries have more than one national day, and a few — notably the United Kingdom — have no single official national day, instead marking events such as the monarch's official birthday.

Examples

Well-known national days include 4 July in the United States (Independence Day), 14 July in France (Bastille Day), 26 January in India (Republic Day) and Australia (Australia Day), and 1 October in China (National Day of the People's Republic of China).
