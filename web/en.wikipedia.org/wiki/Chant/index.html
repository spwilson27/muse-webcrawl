Chant

A chant is the rhythmic speaking or singing of words or sounds, often on a limited number of pitches, performed individually or by a group. Chanting is used in many religious traditions, in ritual, and in collective settings such as protests and sporting events.

Religious chant

Forms of chant include Gregorian chant in the Roman Catholic tradition, Byzantine chant, Vedic chanting of Sanskrit hymns in Hinduism, Buddhist chanting of sutras, and the cantillation of scripture in Judaism and Islam. Such chants are typically monophonic and unaccompanied, with melodies shaped by the ritual text.

Secular uses

Outside religion, chants are used by crowds at football matches, by demonstrators repeating slogans, in military cadence calls, and in children's games. Repetition and simple rhythm make chants easy to learn and powerful as expressions of group identity.
