Stop the War Coalition

The Stop the War Coalition (StWC) is a British campaigning organisation founded in September 2001 to oppose the war in Afghanistan and subsequently the 2003 invasion of Iraq.

The 15 February 2003 march

With the Campaign for Nuclear Disarmament and the Muslim Association of Britain, the coalition organised the march of 15 February 2003 in London, estimated at between one and two million people — the largest political demonstration in British history — as part of a worldwide day of protest against the Iraq War.

Later campaigns

The coalition has campaigned against military intervention in Libya, Syria, and Yemen, and against the expansion of NATO. Prominent figures have included Tony Benn (its founding president), Jeremy Corbyn (chair 2011–2015), Andrew Murray, and Lindsey German.
