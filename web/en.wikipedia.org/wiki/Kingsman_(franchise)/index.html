Kingsman (franchise)

Kingsman is a British-American action-comedy media franchise based on the comic book series The Secret Service (2012–13) by Mark Millar and Dave Gibbons. The franchise centres on the fictional Kingsman agency, a private intelligence service whose agents operate out of a Savile Row tailor shop and combine gentlemanly manners with high-technology espionage. The film series is directed by Matthew Vaughn.

The first film follows Gary "Eggsy" Unwin, a working-class Londoner recruited into Kingsman's training programme as the agency confronts a plan by billionaire Richmond Valentine to cull humanity via a rage-inducing signal. Noted for its stylised, violent action sequences, the film was a commercial success and launched the franchise.

Other media

The franchise also includes sequel comic books by Millar and others, a short animated tie-in, and video games. The Kingsman tailor aesthetic — bespoke suits, the motto "Manners maketh man," and gadgets concealed in umbrellas, lighters and briefcases — became the series' signature.
