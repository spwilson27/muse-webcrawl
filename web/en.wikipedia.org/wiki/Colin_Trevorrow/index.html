Colin Trevorrow

Colin Trevorrow
Born: 13 September 1976, San Francisco, California, U.S.
Occupation: Film director, screenwriter
Known for: Safety Not Guaranteed, Jurassic World

Colin Trevorrow (born 13 September 1976) is an American film director and screenwriter. He is best known for the independent science-fiction comedy Safety Not Guaranteed (2012) and for directing the blockbuster Jurassic World (2015) and its sequel Jurassic World Dominion (2022).

Career

Trevorrow studied film and television at New York University's Tisch School of the Arts. After writing and directing short films and the documentary Reality Show, he co-wrote and directed Safety Not Guaranteed, which premiered at the Sundance Film Festival and won the Waldo Salt Screenwriting Award. The film's success led Universal Pictures to hire him to revive the Jurassic Park franchise.

Jurassic World

Jurassic World (2015), co-written with Derek Connolly, became one of the highest-grossing films of all time on release. Trevorrow co-wrote the sequel Jurassic World: Fallen Kingdom (2018) and returned to direct Jurassic World Dominion (2022), which reunited the casts of both trilogies.

Other work

Trevorrow directed the drama The Book of Henry (2017). He was originally attached to direct Star Wars: Episode IX but departed the project in 2017 over creative differences, while retaining a story credit on the finished film, The Rise of Skywalker.
