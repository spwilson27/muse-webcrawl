Bill Istvan Günther Skarsgård (born 9 August 1990) is a Swedish actor. He is best known internationally for portraying Pennywise the Dancing Clown in the supernatural horror films It (2017) and It Chapter Two (2019), based on Stephen King's novel.

Career

A member of the Swedish Skarsgård acting family, Bill Skarsgård began acting as a child and broke through in Swedish cinema with Simple Simon (2010). He gained wider attention in the Netflix series Hemlock Grove (2013–2015) and in films including Allegiant (2016), Atomic Blonde (2017) and the Hulu series Castle Rock (2018–2019), in another Stephen King-connected role.

Following the success of the It films, he appeared in Barbarian (2022), John Wick: Chapter 4 (2023) as the Marquis de Gramont, Boy Kills World (2023), The Crow (2024) as Eric Draven, and Nosferatu (2024) as Count Orlok.
