Jamie Richard Vardy (born 11 January 1987) is an English professional footballer who plays as a striker. He is best known for his years at Leicester City, whom he helped win the Premier League in 2015–16 in one of the greatest upsets in sporting history, rising from non-League football to become one of the Premier League's most prolific scorers.

Early career

Released by Sheffield Wednesday at 16, Vardy rebuilt his career at Stocksbridge Park Steels while working as a factory technician. Prolific spells at FC Halifax Town and Fleetwood Town — where his goals won the Conference Premier title in 2012 — earned him a move to Leicester City for a non-League record fee of around £1 million.

Leicester City

Vardy helped Leicester win the Championship in 2013–14. In the 2015–16 season he scored in a record eleven consecutive Premier League matches, surpassing Ruud van Nistelrooy's mark, and finished with 24 league goals as Leicester, 5,000–1 outsiders at the season's start, won the title. He was named the FWA Footballer of the Year and Premier League Player of the Season.

Vardy won the Premier League Golden Boot in 2019–20 with 23 goals, becoming the oldest player to win the award, and lifted the FA Cup with Leicester in 2021. He reached 100 Premier League goals in July 2020 and remained the club's talisman through relegation in 2023 and immediate promotion as Championship champions in 2024, before departing in 2025 as one of the club's greatest-ever players; he subsequently joined Italian side Cremonese.

International career

Vardy made his England debut in June 2015 and scored his first international goal against Germany in March 2016. He was selected for UEFA Euro 2016 and the 2018 FIFA World Cup, retiring from international football after the latter with 26 caps and 7 goals.

Style of play

A high-energy striker noted for his pace, relentless pressing, and clinical finishing on the counter-attack, Vardy typically played on the shoulder of the last defender and attacked space behind defences.
