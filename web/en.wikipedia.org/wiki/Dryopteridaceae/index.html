Dryopteridaceae

The Dryopteridaceae are a family of leptosporangiate ferns in the order Polypodiales. They are known as the wood ferns and include about 26 genera and around 2,100 species, with a cosmopolitan distribution, most diverse in temperate and montane tropical forests, especially in eastern Asia.

Description

Members are mostly terrestrial or epipetric, with creeping to erect rhizomes, pinnate leaves and round sori covered by kidney-shaped or round indusia. Well-known genera include Dryopteris (wood ferns), Polystichum (shield ferns), Cyrtomium, Arachniodes and Elaphoglossum.

Classification

The circumscription of the family has varied considerably between systems; the Pteridophyte Phylogeny Group classification of 2016 (PPG I) recognises three subfamilies: Dryopteridoideae, Polybotryoideae and Elaphoglossoideae.
