Gävleborg County

Gävleborg County (Swedish: Gävleborgs län) is a county on the Baltic Sea coast of Sweden. It comprises most of the provinces of Gästrikland and Hälsingland, plus a small part of Dalarna, and borders the counties of Uppsala, Västmanland, Dalarna, Jämtland and Västernorrland.

Gävleborg County
Capital: Gävle
Country: Sweden
Area: ~18,200 km2
Population: ~287,000

Administration

The County Administrative Board, led by a governor, represents the national government, while the elected Region Gävleborg is responsible for healthcare and regional development. The county was formed in 1762 when it was split from Västernorrland County.

Municipalities

Its ten municipalities include Gävle, Sandviken, Hudiksvall, Bollnäs, Söderhamn, Ljusdal, Ovanåker, Nordanstig, Hofors and Ockelbo.

Heritage

The county contains the Decorated Farmhouses of Hälsingland, a UNESCO World Heritage Site of large painted wooden farmhouses.
