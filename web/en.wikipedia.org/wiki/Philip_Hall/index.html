Philip Hall

Philip Hall FRS (11 April 1904 – 30 December 1982) was an English mathematician. His major work was on group theory, notably on finite groups and solvable groups.

Hall was educated at King's College, Cambridge, and spent most of his career there. He is known for Hall's marriage theorem in combinatorics, for Hall subgroups and Hall's collection formula, and for work on commutators. He served as president of the London Mathematical Society from 1955 to 1957 and received the Sylvester Medal in 1961.

Contributions

His results influenced the classification and study of finite soluble groups and p-groups, and his name is attached to several concepts including the Hall–Higman theorem and Hall polynomials.
