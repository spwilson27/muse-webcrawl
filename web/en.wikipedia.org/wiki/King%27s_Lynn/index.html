King's Lynn

CountryEngland
CountyNorfolk
Populationc. 42,800
Historic namesLynn; Bishop's Lynn

King's Lynn, known until 1537 as Bishop's Lynn and locally as Lynn, is a market town and seaport in Norfolk, England, on the River Great Ouse near the Wash.

History

The town grew in the Middle Ages into one of England's most important ports as a member of the Hanseatic trading network; its Hanseatic warehouse, built in 1475, is the only surviving Hanseatic League building in England.
