Krzysztof Kiełowski

Krzysztof Kiełowski is a retired Polish chess player who held the title of Grandmaster. He was one of Poland's leading players in the 1990s and 2000s, representing his country in international team competition.


Kiełowski during a tournament game.

Career

Kiełowski achieved the International Master title before being awarded the Grandmaster title by FIDE in 1996. He competed in the Polish Chess Championship on numerous occasions and represented Poland at the Chess Olympiad, including the 2000 Olympiad in Istanbul. He recorded tournament successes in Polish and European open events and played league chess for Polish clubs.
