Ramzan Kadyrov

Ramzan Akhmadovich Kadyrov (born 5 October 1976) is a Russian politician and the Head of the Chechen Republic, a federal subject of Russia, a position he has held since 2007. He is the son of Akhmad Kadyrov, the republic's first president, who was assassinated in 2004.

Career

During the First Chechen War the Kadyrov family fought against Russia; in the Second Chechen War they switched sides, and Ramzan commanded a pro-Moscow Chechen militia (the "Kadyrovtsy"). After serving as prime minister of Chechnya from 2005, he became president of the republic in 2007 with the support of Vladimir Putin, and was later re-elected under the title of Head of the Chechen Republic.

Criticism

Kadyrov's rule has been widely criticised by international human-rights organisations, which accuse his security forces of extrajudicial killings, torture, forced disappearances, and persecution of LGBT people in Chechnya. He has been placed under sanctions by the United States and other countries. He is known for a highly publicised personal style, patronage of mixed martial arts through the Akhmat fight club, and the deployment of Chechen units in support of Russia's invasion of Ukraine.
