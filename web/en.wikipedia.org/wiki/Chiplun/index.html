Chiplun

Chiplun is a city in Ratnagiri district in the state of Maharashtra, India. It lies in the Konkan region, on the banks of the Vashishti River, roughly midway between Mumbai and Goa along National Highway 66.

Geography

Chiplun is situated in the foothills of the Western Ghats and receives very heavy monsoon rainfall. The surrounding area is known for paddy fields, mango (especially Alphonso) and cashew cultivation, and laterite plateaus. The Koyna Dam and its reservoir are located to the east in the Ghats.

Economy and transport

The town has chemical and pharmaceutical industrial estates at Lote Parshuram. Chiplun railway station on the Konkan Railway connects it to Mumbai, Mangaluru, and Goa. Nearby attractions include the Parshurama Temple, Guhagar beach, and the Sawatsada waterfall.
