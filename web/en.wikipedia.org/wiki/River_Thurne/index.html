The River Thurne is a river in Norfolk, England, within the Broads National Park. It rises close to Martham Broad and flows past the villages of Potter Heigham, Ludham and Thurne before joining the River Bure. Several of the northern Norfolk Broads — Hickling Broad, Horsey Mere, Martham Broad and Heigham Sound — drain into it.

Navigation

The Thurne is navigable from its junction with the Bure as far as Potter Heigham and, for smaller craft, beyond towards Hickling. The medieval bridge at Potter Heigham is famously low and is one of the most challenging passages on the Broads for hire cruisers. The river passes some of the best-known drainage windmills in Norfolk, including Thurne Mill and St Benet's Level Mill.

Wildlife

The surrounding marshes and broads support bitterns, marsh harriers, swallowtail butterflies and otters. Hickling Broad, the largest of the Broads, is a National Nature Reserve managed by the Norfolk Wildlife Trust.
