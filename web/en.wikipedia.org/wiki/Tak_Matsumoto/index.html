Takahiro Matsumoto (松本 孝弘, born March 27, 1961), better known as Tak Matsumoto, is a Japanese musician, songwriter and record producer. He is best known as the guitarist and lead composer of the rock duo B'z, the best-selling music act in Japanese history, formed with vocalist Koshi Inaba in 1988. In 1999 he became the first Asian guitarist to receive a signature-model Les Paul from Gibson, and he is the first Japanese artist to have his handprints and signature on display at Hollywood's RockWalk.

Career

Before B'z, Matsumoto worked as a session guitarist and supported acts including Mari Hamada and TM Network. With B'z he has written and produced a string of consecutive number-one singles and albums in Japan; the duo was inducted into Hollywood's RockWalk in 2007, the first Asian artists so honoured.

Solo work

Matsumoto has maintained a parallel solo career. His solo debut, Thousand Wave, appeared in 1988. In 2004 he formed the Tak Matsumoto Group (TMG) with Eric Martin, Jack Blades and others. His 2010 instrumental collaboration with American guitarist Larry Carlton, Take Your Pick, won the Grammy Award for Best Pop Instrumental Album at the 53rd Grammy Awards. Later albums explored jazz and fusion (New Horizon, 2014) and hard rock (Electric Island, Acoustic Sea, 2017, with Daniel Ho). He has released numerous signature Gibson and Epiphone guitar models.

Style

Matsumoto's playing blends blues phrasing with precise, melodic rock technique; his compositional hallmark is compact, hook-heavy arrangements combining Western hard-rock guitar with Japanese pop songcraft.
