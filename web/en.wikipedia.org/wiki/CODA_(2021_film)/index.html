CODA is a 2021 coming-of-age comedy-drama film written and directed by Sian Heder. An English-language remake of the 2014 French film La Famille Bélier, it stars Emilia Jones as Ruby Rossi, the only hearing member of a deaf family (a child of deaf adults, or CODA) in Gloucester, Massachusetts, who is torn between her family's struggling fishing business and her love of singing.

CODA premiered at the 2021 Sundance Film Festival.

Plot

Ruby Rossi serves as interpreter for her deaf parents, Frank and Jackie, and her deaf older brother Leo, while helping on the family fishing boat. When she joins her school choir and discovers a talent for singing, her choir director encourages her to audition for Berklee College of Music. Ruby must choose between her obligation to her family — especially as new fishing regulations threaten their livelihood and require a hearing crew member — and her own ambitions.

Reception and awards

The film premiered at the 2021 Sundance Film Festival, where it won four awards including the Grand Jury Prize and was acquired by Apple for a festival-record $25 million. At the 94th Academy Awards it won all three of its nominations: Best Picture, Best Adapted Screenplay, and Best Supporting Actor for Troy Kotsur, who became the first deaf man to win an acting Oscar. Marlee Matlin, who plays Jackie, had previously been the only deaf Oscar winner in an acting category.
