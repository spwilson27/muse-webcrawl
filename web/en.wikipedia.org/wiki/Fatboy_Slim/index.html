Fatboy Slim

Birth name: Norman Quentin Cook
Born: 31 July 1963, Bromley, London
Genres: Big beat, electronic, acid house
Occupations: DJ, musician, record producer
Years active: 1981–present

Norman Quentin Cook (born 31 July 1963), better known as Fatboy Slim, is an English musician, DJ, and record producer who helped popularise the big beat genre in the 1990s. His style combines elements of hip hop, funk, house, and rock, built largely from samples, and his albums and singles achieved worldwide success.

Career

Cook first found success as the bassist of the indie rock band the Housemartins, who scored a UK number-one in 1986 with an a cappella cover of "Caravan of Love". He subsequently fronted Beats International, whose single "Dub Be Good to Me" (1990) also reached number one in the UK. Throughout the early 1990s Cook worked under numerous aliases, including Pizzaman and Mighty Dub Katz, and was a member of the group Freak Power.

As Fatboy Slim he released the album Better Living Through Chemistry (1996), but his breakthrough came with You've Come a Long Way, Baby (1998), which included the hit singles "The Rockafeller Skank", "Praise You", and "Right Here, Right Now". The video for "Praise You", directed by Spike Jonze, won multiple awards. The follow-up Halfway Between the Gutter and the Stars (2000) featured "Weapon of Choice", whose Christopher Walken dance video became iconic. Later albums include Palookaville (2004). In 2002 Cook staged the free Big Beach Boutique concert on Brighton beach, attended by an estimated 250,000 people — far beyond expectations — and he has continued to perform huge DJ shows, including sets at Glastonbury and on the Great Wall of China. He also composed, with David Byrne, the musical Here Lies Love.

Personal life

Cook lives in Hove, East Sussex. He was married to the broadcaster Zoe Ball from 1999 until their separation in 2016. He has spoken publicly about his recovery from alcohol dependence, having entered rehabilitation in 2009.
