Lagrangian submanifold

A Lagrangian submanifold is a submanifold of a symplectic manifold on which the symplectic form vanishes identically, and whose dimension is exactly half that of the ambient manifold.

Definition

Let (M, ω) be a symplectic manifold of dimension 2n. A submanifold L ⊂ M is Lagrangian if dim L = n and the restriction of ω to L is zero at every point. Lagrangian submanifolds are the maximal-dimensional submanifolds on which the symplectic form can vanish.

Examples

The zero section of any cotangent bundle is a Lagrangian submanifold, as are graphs of exact one-forms. In symplectic topology, Lagrangian submanifolds play a central role in Floer homology and mirror symmetry.

Nearby Lagrangian conjecture

A famous open problem asks whether every closed exact Lagrangian submanifold of a cotangent bundle must be Hamiltonian isotopic to the zero section.
