Croatia

[Image: Dubrovnik's old town walls and the Adriatic Sea]
Dubrovnik's old town walls and the Adriatic Sea

Croatia, officially the Republic of Croatia, is a country in Central and Southeast Europe on the eastern Adriatic Sea, with a long, island-dotted coastline — over 1,000 islands and islets — and an inland Pannonian region. It borders Slovenia, Hungary, Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina and Montenegro. Its population is about 3.9 million and its capital is Zagreb.

Croatia was part of Yugoslavia until declaring independence in 1991, followed by the Croatian War of Independence (1991-1995). It joined the EU in 2013 and adopted the euro and entered Schengen in 2023. Tourism on the Dalmatian coast — Dubrovnik (a Game of Thrones filming location), Split with Diocletian's Palace, Hvar and the Plitvice Lakes — is the economic mainstay. Croatia's football team reached the 2018 World Cup final.
