GameWorks

GameWorks is a chain of entertainment centers in the United States featuring video arcade games, prize redemption, bowling, billiards, and full-service restaurants and bars. The concept was originally developed in the mid-1990s as a joint venture involving Sega, Universal Studios and DreamWorks SKG, with creative input from Steven Spielberg, aiming to create large-scale "urban entertainment centers" combining arcade gaming with dining.

History

The first GameWorks location opened in Seattle, Washington, in March 1997. The chain expanded to cities including Las Vegas, Chicago (Schaumburg), Columbus, Tampa and Ontario, California. After the original venture wound down, the brand passed through several owners, including Sega Sammy Holdings, and a number of locations closed during the 2000s and 2010s. In 2018 the remaining chain was acquired by ExWorks Capital. GameWorks announced the permanent closure of its remaining locations in December 2021, citing the financial impact of the COVID-19 pandemic.

Attractions

Locations typically occupy 20,000–50,000 square feet and feature hundreds of arcade and redemption games, esports lounges, prize stores, and restaurant brands such as The Works Kitchen. The Las Vegas location was notable for its large esports arena.
