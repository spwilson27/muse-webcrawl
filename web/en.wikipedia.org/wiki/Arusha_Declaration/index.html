Arusha Declaration

The Arusha Declaration (Swahili: Azimio la Arusha) and TANU's Policy on Socialism and Self-Reliance (1967), referred to as Arusha Declaration, is known as Tanzania's most prominent political statement on African socialism, or ujamaa.

Arusha Declaration
Date5 February 1967
AuthorJulius Nyerere / TANU
PlaceArusha, Tanzania

Content

Issued under President Julius Nyerere, the declaration committed TANU to socialism and self-reliance, nationalisation of major means of production, a leadership code barring officials from capitalist enrichment, and emphasis on agriculture and rural development.

Implementation

Banks, mills and major industries were nationalised; ujamaa villagisation in the 1970s moved millions into collective villages. Economic results were poor, and Tanzania later liberalised under successors, though the declaration remains symbolically important.
