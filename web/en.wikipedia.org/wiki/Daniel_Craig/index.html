Daniel Wroughton Craig (born 2 March 1968) is an English actor. He gained international fame playing James Bond in five films, from Casino Royale (2006) to No Time to Die (2021), and is also known for playing detective Benoit Blanc in the Knives Out films.

Early life and career

Craig was born in Chester and raised on the Wirral Peninsula. He joined the National Youth Theatre and graduated from the Guildhall School of Music and Drama in 1991. He first attracted attention in the BBC drama Our Friends in the North (1996), and appeared in films including Elizabeth (1998), Lara Croft: Tomb Raider (2001), Road to Perdition (2002), Layer Cake (2004) and Munich (2005).

James Bond

Announced as the sixth Bond in October 2005 — to initial scepticism from some fans and press — Craig won widespread acclaim for a grittier, more physical portrayal in Casino Royale (2006), then the highest-grossing Bond film. He returned in Quantum of Solace (2008), Skyfall (2012, the first Bond film to gross over $1 billion), Spectre (2015) and No Time to Die (2021), which concluded his tenure with the character's death — a first for the series.

Other work

Craig played journalist Mikael Blomkvist in The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo (2011), starred in Logan Lucky (2017), and created the drawling detective Benoit Blanc in Rian Johnson's Knives Out (2019) and Glass Onion (2022). On stage he has appeared in A Steady Rain (2009), Betrayal (2013), Othello (2016) and, as the title character, Macbeth on Broadway in 2022. He was appointed CMG in the 2022 New Year Honours — the same honour held by the fictional Bond.
