Sail Training International (STI) is an international sail training organisation and registered charity, based in Gosport, England, with members in many countries. It exists to develop and educate young people through the challenge of crewing tall ships and other sailing vessels at sea, regardless of nationality, culture, religion, gender or social background.

Activities

STI is best known for organising the annual Tall Ships Races, a series of races and cruises-in-company for sail training vessels, mostly in European waters, in which thousands of young people (typically aged 15–25) take part as trainees aboard square-rigged ships and smaller vessels. It also organises or licenses other races and regattas, including transatlantic events such as Rendez-Vous 2017 Tall Ships Regatta.

The organisation coordinates the activities of national sail training associations, maintains conferences and publications for the sail training community, and presents awards for sail training achievement.

History

The modern tall ships races began in 1956, when a race from Torbay to Lisbon was organised to mark the passing of the great commercial sailing ships; the event proved so popular that it became a regular series. The Sail Training International Race Committee grew out of these events, and the organisation eventually took the name Sail Training International, administering the races on behalf of its member nations.
