Crisis on Infinite Earths

Crisis on Infinite Earths is a 12-issue American comic book limited series published by DC Comics from April 1985 to March 1986, written by Marv Wolfman and pencilled by George Pérez. It is regarded as one of the most influential crossover events in comics history.

Plot

The cosmic being known as the Monitor recruits heroes from across DC's multiverse to battle his counterpart, the Anti-Monitor, who is destroying parallel universes with antimatter. The story features the deaths of major characters including Supergirl and Barry Allen, the Flash.

Impact

The series rebooted DC continuity, collapsing its multiverse into a single universe and prompting line-wide relaunches of titles such as Superman and Wonder Woman. It established the template for later "Crisis" events and was adapted as a television crossover in the Arrowverse (2019–2020) and in animated films.
