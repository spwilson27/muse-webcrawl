The Canada women's national soccer team represents Canada in international women's soccer and is governed by the Canadian Soccer Association. The team's greatest triumph came at the Tokyo 2020 Olympic Games, where it won the gold medal, defeating Sweden on penalties in the final.

History

Canada played its first official international match in 1986 and qualified for the FIFA Women's World Cup for the first time in 1995. At the 2003 World Cup the team achieved a fourth-place finish. Under coach John Herdman, Canada won bronze medals at the 2012 and 2016 Olympics, and hosted the 2015 Women's World Cup, reaching the quarter-finals.

Olympic champions

At the delayed Tokyo 2020 Olympics, Canada beat the United States in the semi-final — its first win over its rivals in twenty years — and then defeated Sweden in a shootout in the gold-medal match, with Julia Grosso scoring the winning penalty.

Players

The team's long-time captain Christine Sinclair is the all-time leading international goalscorer, male or female, with 190 goals for her country. Other prominent players have included Diana Matheson, Sophie Schmidt, Ashley Lawrence, Jessie Fleming and Kadeisha Buchanan.
