The Israeli Public Broadcasting Corporation (Hebrew: תאגיד השידור הישראלי), known by its brand name Kan (כאן, "Here"), is Israel's state-funded public broadcaster. It replaced the Israel Broadcasting Authority (IBA) in May 2017 under the Public Broadcasting Law of 2014.

Services

Kan operates the television channels Kan 11 (main, Hebrew), Makan (Arabic) and Kan Educational (children and youth), along with eight radio stations inherited from Kol Israel — including Kan Bet (news), Kan Gimel (Israeli music), Kan Kol Hamusika (classical), Kan REKA (immigrant languages) and Kan 88 (music and culture). It runs an extensive digital and streaming operation, and its podcasts and YouTube channels became prominent in Israeli media.

History

The corporation was established to replace the IBA, which had been criticised for bloated staffing and government interference through the television licence fee. Kan's launch was repeatedly delayed by political disputes over its news division. It began television broadcasts on 15 May 2017. Kan is a member of the European Broadcasting Union and has produced Israel's Eurovision entries; Israel hosted the Eurovision Song Contest 2019 in Tel Aviv under its production.
