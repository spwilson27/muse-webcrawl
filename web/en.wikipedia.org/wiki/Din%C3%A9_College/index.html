Diné College

TypePublic tribal college
Established1968
LocationTsaile, Arizona, United States

Diné College is a public tribal college in Tsaile, Arizona, serving the Navajo Nation. Founded in 1968 as Navajo Community College, it was the first tribally controlled and accredited college in the United States, a landmark in the movement for Native American self-determination in education.

History and mission

The college was chartered by the Navajo Tribal Council and opened in 1968, initially on the campus of a federal boarding school in Many Farms before moving to its purpose-built campus at Tsaile, whose central octagonal Ned Hatathli Center is a landmark of Navajo-inspired architecture. It took the name Diné College in 1997.

Academics

Diné College offers associate and bachelor's degrees, with a curriculum grounded in Diné philosophy — the Diné Educational Philosophy based on the concepts of learning and living associated with Sa'ąh Naagháí Bik'eh Hózhóón. It operates additional centres across the Navajo Nation in Arizona and New Mexico.
