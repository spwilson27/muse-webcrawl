United States Munitions List

The United States Munitions List (USML) is a list of articles, services, and related technology designated as defence- and space-related under the Arms Export Control Act. Export and temporary import of items on the list are controlled by the Department of State's Directorate of Defense Trade Controls under the International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR).

Categories

The list, at 22 CFR 121.1, is organised into 21 categories, including firearms and ammunition (Category I–III), launch vehicles and missiles (IV), explosives (V), surface vessels (VI), ground vehicles (VII), aircraft (VIII), military training equipment (IX), military electronics (XI), spacecraft (XV), and nuclear weapons design (XVI), among others.

Reform

Under the Export Control Reform initiative begun in 2010, many less sensitive items were moved from the USML to the Commerce Control List administered by the Department of Commerce under the Export Administration Regulations, while genuinely military items remained under State Department control.
