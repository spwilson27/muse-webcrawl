Vegreville

Vegreville is a town in central Alberta, Canada, about 103 kilometres east of Edmonton along the Yellowhead Highway. It was incorporated as a town in 1906 and has a population of roughly 5,700. The town serves an agricultural region settled heavily by Ukrainian immigrants beginning in the 1890s.

History

The community was named after Father Valentin Vegreville, an Oblate missionary. Settlement grew with the arrival of the Canadian Northern Railway in 1905. Vegreville became a service centre for the surrounding Ukrainian-Canadian farming bloc settlement, one of the largest in Canada.

Landmarks

Vegreville is best known for the Vegreville egg, a giant sculpture of a pysanka (Ukrainian Easter egg) erected in 1975 to commemorate the centennial of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police. Built of aluminium in a complex computer-designed geometry, it is one of the largest Easter eggs in the world and anchors the town's annual Pysanka Festival celebrating Ukrainian culture.
