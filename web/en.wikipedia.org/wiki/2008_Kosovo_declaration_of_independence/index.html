2008 Kosovo declaration of independence

Date17 February 2008
LocationPristina, Kosovo
Declared fromSerbia

The 2008 Kosovo declaration of independence was adopted on 17 February 2008 by the Assembly of Kosovo, unilaterally declaring Kosovo an independent and sovereign state. It was the second declaration of independence by Kosovo's Albanian-majority institutions.

Background

Kosovo had been administered by the United Nations since the 1999 Kosovo War ended with NATO intervention against Yugoslavia. Years of negotiations over final status, including the Ahtisaari Plan proposing supervised independence, failed to win agreement from Serbia or a UN Security Council resolution.

Reaction

The United States and most European Union member states promptly recognized Kosovo; Serbia, Russia, China, and five EU states did not. In 2010 the International Court of Justice advised that the declaration itself did not violate international law. Kosovo's statehood remains disputed, and it is not a UN member.
