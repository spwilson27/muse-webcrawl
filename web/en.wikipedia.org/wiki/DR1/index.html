DR1


Image placeholder: DR1

DR1 is the first channel of Danish public broadcaster DR, and one of the oldest television channels in Denmark. Broadcasting began on 2 October 1951 as the first television channel in the country, then known simply as DR TV.

Programming

DR1 is DR's flagship generalist channel, carrying news (TV Avisen), drama, entertainment, documentaries, and major national events. Its original dramas, including internationally acclaimed series, have played a major role in the global reputation of Danish television. The channel adopted its current name, DR1, in 1996 when DR2 was launched.
