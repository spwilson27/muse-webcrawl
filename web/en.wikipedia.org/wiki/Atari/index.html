Atari

Atari is a brand name owned by several entities since its founding in 1972. The original Atari, Inc. was founded by Nolan Bushnell and Ted Dabney in Sunnyvale, California, and pioneered the arcade video game industry with the release of Pong in 1972.

History

Atari's Video Computer System (later renamed the Atari 2600), launched in 1977, popularised the home video game console with interchangeable cartridges. Under Warner Communications, which bought the company in 1976, Atari grew rapidly but was severely hit by the video game crash of 1983. The company was split in 1984: the home computer and console division became Atari Corporation under Jack Tramiel, producing the Atari ST line, while the arcade division continued as Atari Games.

Legacy

The Atari name passed through several owners, including Hasbro Interactive and the French publisher Infogrames, which rebranded itself Atari SA. Atari's machines and games, including Asteroids, Centipede and Missile Command, remain landmarks of early video game history.
