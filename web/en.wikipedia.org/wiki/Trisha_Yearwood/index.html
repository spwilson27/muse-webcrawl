Patricia Lynn Yearwood (born September 19, 1964) is an American country music singer. She rose to fame with her 1991 debut single "She's in Love with the Boy", which became a number-one hit on the Billboard Hot Country Songs chart. Her debut album was certified double-platinum, and she became one of the best-selling female country artists of the 1990s.

Career

Yearwood signed with MCA Nashville in 1990. Her hits include "Walkaway Joe", "The Song Remembers When", "Thinkin' About You", "XXX's and OOO's (An American Girl)" and "How Do I Live" (1997), the latter featured in the film Con Air and winning her a Grammy Award for Best Female Country Vocal Performance. She has won three Grammy Awards in total and was inducted into the Grand Ole Opry in 1999.

Other work

Yearwood is also a successful cookbook author; her books include Georgia Cooking in an Oklahoma Kitchen (2008) and Home Cooking with Trisha Yearwood (2010). From 2012 she hosted the Emmy-winning Food Network programme Trisha's Southern Kitchen.

Personal life

Yearwood was born in Monticello, Georgia. She married country singer Garth Brooks in 2005. The couple are noted philanthropists, including work with Habitat for Humanity.
