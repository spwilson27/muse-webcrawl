Daredevil (Marvel Comics)

Daredevil is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer-editor Stan Lee and artist Bill Everett, with input from Jack Kirby, the character first appeared in Daredevil #1 (April 1964). Daredevil is the alias of Matthew Murdock, a blind lawyer from New York's Hell's Kitchen whose remaining senses were heightened to superhuman levels by a radioactive accident.

Fictional biography

As a boy, Matt Murdock was blinded when radioactive material spilled into his eyes while he saved a pedestrian from an oncoming truck. The accident greatly enhanced his other senses and gave him a "radar sense". His father, boxer "Battlin' Jack" Murdock, was murdered for refusing to throw a fight. As an adult, Murdock co-founded the law firm Nelson and Murdock with his friend Foggy Nelson, and by night fights crime as Daredevil — known as "the Man Without Fear".

Characters and legacy

Key supporting characters and villains include Karen Page, Elektra, the Kingpin (Wilson Fisk), Bullseye, and the Punisher. Frank Miller's acclaimed 1980s run redefined the character with noir tones and introduced Elektra and the ninja clan the Hand. Daredevil has been adapted widely, including the 2003 film and the Marvel Television/Netflix series Daredevil (2015–2018) starring Charlie Cox, who reprised the role in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, including Daredevil: Born Again.
