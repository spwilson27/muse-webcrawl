Hackney Central is a railway station on the North London line in the London Borough of Hackney, East London. It is served by London Overground trains between Stratford and Richmond/Clapham Junction, and lies in Travelcard Zone 2. A footpath link connects it with Hackney Downs station on the Lea Valley lines.

History

The first station on the site opened in 1850 on the North London Railway. It was replaced by a grander station in 1870, whose buildings partly survive as a venue and retail space. The station closed in 1944 after bomb damage curtailed passenger services on the line's eastern section, and the present station opened in 1985 when passenger services were restored between Richmond and the newly opened Stratford link. In 2010 the station joined the extended London Overground network's core, and a new southern entrance on Amhurst Road opened in 2016.

Services

Trains run frequently throughout the day, typically every few minutes at peak times, westbound to Highbury & Islington, Gospel Oak, Willesden Junction, Richmond and Clapham Junction, and eastbound to Stratford.
