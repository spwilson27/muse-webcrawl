Blur are an English rock band formed in London in 1988. The group consists of vocalist Damon Albarn, guitarist Graham Coxon, bassist Alex James and drummer Dave Rowntree. Their debut album Leisure (1991) incorporated influences from Madchester and shoegazing, and they went on to become one of the central bands of the Britpop movement.

Career

With Modern Life Is Rubbish (1993), Parklife (1994) and The Great Escape (1995), Blur chronicled British life and rivalled Oasis in the much-publicised "Battle of Britpop" of 1995, which Blur won when "Country House" outsold Oasis's "Roll with It". From Blur (1997), featuring the hit "Song 2", the band absorbed American lo-fi and indie influences; 13 (1999) and Think Tank (2003) were more experimental. Coxon left in 2002, and the band went on hiatus.

Reunions

Blur reunited with Coxon in 2008, playing acclaimed festival shows and releasing The Magic Whip in 2015, recorded in Hong Kong. In 2023 they released The Ballad of Darren and played two sold-out shows at Wembley Stadium.
