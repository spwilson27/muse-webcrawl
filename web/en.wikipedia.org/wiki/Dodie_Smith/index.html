Dodie Smith

Dorothy Gladys "Dodie" Smith (3 May 1896 – 24 November 1990) was an English novelist and playwright, best known for The Hundred and One Dalmatians (1956) and her debut novel I Capture the Castle (1948).

Career

Smith began as an actress and wrote a number of successful plays in the 1930s under the pen name C. L. Anthony. During the Second World War she lived in the United States, where homesickness inspired I Capture the Castle, narrated by 17-year-old Cassandra Mortmain and famous for its opening line, "I write this sitting in the kitchen sink."

The Hundred and One Dalmatians, inspired by her own Dalmatian dogs, was adapted by Disney into the 1961 animated film One Hundred and One Dalmatians and subsequent remakes, including the live-action 101 Dalmatians (1996) and Cruella (2021).
