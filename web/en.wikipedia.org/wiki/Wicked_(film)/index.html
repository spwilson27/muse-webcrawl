Wicked (film)

Wicked
Directed by Jon M. Chu
Starring Cynthia Erivo, Ariana Grande
Released 2024

Wicked, marketed as Wicked: Part I, is a 2024 American musical fantasy film directed by Jon M. Chu, adapted from the first act of the stage musical Wicked (2003) by Stephen Schwartz and Winnie Holzman, itself based on Gregory Maguire's 1995 novel.

Plot

In the Land of Oz, Elphaba Thropp, a young woman born with green skin, enrols at Shiz University, where she rooms with the popular Galinda Upland. Initially rivals, the two become friends. Elphaba's magical talent draws the attention of the Wizard of Oz, but she discovers his regime oppresses talking Animals and is built on propaganda. She defies him, is branded the "Wicked Witch of the West," and flees, while Galinda — now Glinda the Good — remains within the establishment.

Cast

Cynthia Erivo stars as Elphaba and Ariana Grande as Galinda/Glinda, with Jonathan Bailey as Fiyero, Michelle Yeoh as Madame Morrible, Jeff Goldblum as the Wizard, Ethan Slater as Boq, and Marissa Bode as Nessarose.

Production and release

Universal Pictures developed the adaptation for over a decade before splitting it into two films. Principal photography took place in England. The film premiered in November 2024 and became a major box-office success, earning Academy Awards for costume and production design. Its sequel, Wicked: For Good, adapts the second act.

Music

Songs include "The Wizard and I," "What Is This Feeling?," "Popular," "Dancing Through Life," and the climactic "Defying Gravity."
