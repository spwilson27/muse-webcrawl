Melatonin

Melatonin
TypeHormone produced by the pineal gland
RoleSignals darkness; regulates sleep–wake timing
UsesSupplement for jet lag and some sleep disorders
[Image: A small bottle of melatonin tablets beside an alarm clock at night]

Melatonin is a hormone made by the pineal gland in response to darkness, signalling night to the body's circadian system and promoting sleepiness. Levels rise in the evening, peak at night and fall by morning.

Supplements

Melatonin is sold widely (prescription-only in some countries) for jet lag and delayed sleep phase; evidence for general insomnia is modest, and doses in shops vary widely from labels. Light exposure remains the stronger clock-setter; melatonin also has antioxidant roles studied in many tissues.
