Yann LeCun

Yann André LeCun (born 8 July 1960) is a French-American computer scientist working primarily in machine learning, computer vision, mobile robotics and computational neuroscience. He is a co-recipient of the 2018 ACM Turing Award, with Geoffrey Hinton and Yoshua Bengio, for work on deep learning.

Career

LeCun received his PhD from Université Pierre et Marie Curie in 1987, proposing an early form of the backpropagation algorithm, and did postdoctoral work with Hinton in Toronto. At Bell Labs (1988–2002) he developed convolutional neural networks (such as LeNet) for reading handwritten digits and cheques, now fundamental to computer vision.

He became a professor at New York University, founded the NYU Center for Data Science, and in 2013 became the founding director of Facebook AI Research (FAIR); he serves as Vice-President and Chief AI Scientist at Meta.

Research and views

LeCun has advocated self-supervised learning and energy-based models, and proposed the Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA). He is a prominent, often sceptical voice on claims about imminent artificial general intelligence and large language models' limitations.
