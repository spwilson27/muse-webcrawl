Eastern Ghats

The Eastern Ghats are a discontinuous range of mountains along India's eastern coast, running from Odisha through Andhra Pradesh to Tamil Nadu, cut through by the Godavari, Mahanadi, Krishna and Kaveri rivers.

Eastern Ghats
Highest point: Jindhagada Peak, 1,690 m
States: Odisha, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka
Geology: Khondalite, charnockite

Geography

Unlike the continuous Western Ghats, the Eastern Ghats are broken into hills including the Nallamala, Velikonda, Palar and Javadi ranges. Major peaks include Jindhagada and Arma Konda.

Ecology

The range hosts tropical forests, the Papikonda and Nagarjunsagar-Srisailam protected areas nearby, and endemic species, though mining for bauxite and deforestation threaten it.
