County Kildare (Irish: Contae Chill Dara) is a county in Ireland, in the province of Leinster and the Eastern and Midland Region. It is named after the town of Kildare and borders Dublin to the east; its county town is Naas.

Features

Kildare is known for horse breeding and racing: the Curragh plain hosts Ireland's premier racecourse, and the Irish National Stud is near Kildare town. The county also contains the Bog of Allen, the Grand Canal and Royal Canal, and large commuter towns such as Celbridge, Leixlip and Maynooth, home to Maynooth University.
