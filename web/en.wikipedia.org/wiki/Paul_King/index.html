Paul King

Born: Kent, England
Occupation: Film director, writer
Known for: Paddington, Paddington 2, Wonka

Paul King is a British writer and film director who works in television, film and theatre. He is best known for directing the family films Paddington (2014) and Paddington 2 (2017), and the musical fantasy Wonka (2023).

Career

King began in television comedy, serving as associate director on Garth Marenghi's Darkplace and directing episodes of the surreal sitcom The Mighty Boosh. His feature directorial debut was Bunny and the Bull (2009).

Paddington and later work

Paddington, adapted from Michael Bond's books, was a critical and commercial success, and its sequel Paddington 2 was widely acclaimed, frequently cited among the best-reviewed films of its decade. King co-wrote and directed Wonka, a prequel origin story for Willy Wonka starring Timothée Chalamet.

Style

King's work is noted for handcrafted visual inventiveness, theatrical staging, warmth and meticulous comic timing.
