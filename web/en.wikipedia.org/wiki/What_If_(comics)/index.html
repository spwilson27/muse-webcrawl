What If (comics)

What If...?
PublisherMarvel Comics
First series1977–1984
NarratorUatu the Watcher

What If...? is a comic book anthology series published by Marvel Comics whose stories explore how the Marvel Universe might have unfolded if key moments in its history had not occurred as they did in mainstream continuity. The stories are narrated by Uatu the Watcher, who observes alternate realities without interfering.

Premise and history

The first issue (1977) asked "What If Spider-Man Had Joined the Fantastic Four?" Subsequent volumes revisited famous storylines with a single changed event, often ending heroically or tragically. The series has run in multiple volumes since 1977, and its premises frequently introduced ideas and character variants later adapted elsewhere.

Adaptation

The concept was adapted into the animated series What If...? (2021–2024) for Disney+, part of the Marvel Cinematic Universe, with Jeffrey Wright voicing the Watcher.
