This Land Is Your Land

"This Land Is Your Land"
Song byWoody Guthrie
Written23 February 1940
RecordedApril 1944
GenreFolk

"This Land Is Your Land" is one of the United States' most famous folk songs. Its lyrics were written by American folk singer Woody Guthrie in 1940 in critical response to Irving Berlin's "God Bless America". Its melody is based on a Carter Family tune, itself derived from a Baptist hymn.

History

Guthrie wrote the song in New York City on 23 February 1940, originally titled "God Blessed America for Me", with verses commenting on poverty, private property and inequality during the Great Depression. He recorded it in 1944 for Moses Asch; the version with the famous chorus became a staple of school songbooks, often without the radical verses.

Lyrics and meaning

The celebrated verses travel "from California to the New York island, from the redwood forest to the Gulf Stream waters". The omitted verses question the 'private property' sign and celebrate relief-office crowds, making the song a populist anthem as well as a patriotic one.

Legacy

The song has been performed at inaugurations, protests and concerts by Pete Seeger, Bruce Springsteen and many others, was inducted into the National Recording Registry, and was the subject of long-running copyright disputes before entering broad public use.
