Nasser Al-Attiyah

Nasser Saleh Nasser Abdullah Al-Attiyah (born 21 December 1970) is a Qatari rally driver and sport shooter. He is one of the most successful competitors in rally raid history, having won the Dakar Rally five times (2011, 2015, 2019, 2022 and 2023), and is also an Olympic medallist in skeet shooting, winning bronze at the London 2012 Games. He is a member of the Qatari ruling family.

Rally career

Al-Attiyah began in conventional rallying, dominating the Middle East Rally Championship, which he has won many times, and competing in the World Rally Championship in the Production and WRC2 categories, winning the WRC2 title in 2014 and 2015. In marathon rallying he won the FIA World Cup for Cross-Country Rallies repeatedly. His first Dakar victory came in 2011 with Volkswagen in South America, followed by wins with Mini (2015) and Toyota (2019), and two further victories for Toyota Gazoo Racing in the Saudi Arabian editions of 2022 and 2023, generally co-driven by Mathieu Baumel. He later joined the Dacia programme for the Dakar.

Shooting career

Alongside motorsport, Al-Attiyah has represented Qatar in skeet shooting at six Olympic Games, from Atlanta 1996 to Tokyo 2020. His best result came at London 2012, where he won the bronze medal after a shoot-off, becoming one of Qatar's few Olympic medallists. He has also won medals at the Asian Games and Asian Shooting Championships.

Personal life

Born in Doha, Al-Attiyah is the second cousin of the Emir of Qatar. He is celebrated in Qatar, where his Dakar triumphs and Olympic medal have made him one of the country's most prominent sportsmen, and he has business interests in motorsport and hospitality.
