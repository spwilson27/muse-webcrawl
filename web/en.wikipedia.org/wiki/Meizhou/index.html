Meizhou

Meizhou (Chinese: 梅州; Hakka: Mòi-chû) is a prefecture-level city in the mountainous north-east of Guangdong Province, China, bordering Fujian and Jiangxi. It is widely regarded as the cultural heartland of the Hakka people and is often called the "capital of Hakka culture".

Geography and administration

The prefecture administers the Meijiang and Meixian districts and several counties, including Dabu, Fengshun, Wuhua, Pingyuan, and Jiaoling, plus the county-level city of Xingning. The Mei River flows through the city. The surrounding hills are planted with pomelo orchards, and Meizhou is famous for its golden pomelos.

Culture

Residents predominantly speak the Hakka dialect. Notable local traditions include Hakka walled houses (weilongwu), Hakka folk songs, and a strong tradition of emigration: many overseas Chinese trace their ancestry to Meizhou. The city is also noted in Chinese football history as the home of pioneering teams and players, and Marshal Ye Jianying was a native of Meixian. The region was the birthplace of Lee Kuan Yew's ancestors, commemorated by an ancestral home museum in Dabu.
