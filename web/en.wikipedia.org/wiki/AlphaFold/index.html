AlphaFold

AlphaFold is an artificial intelligence system developed by Google DeepMind that predicts a protein's three-dimensional structure from its amino acid sequence. AlphaFold 2 effectively solved the protein folding problem at CASP14 in 2020, and its creators shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.

DeveloperGoogle DeepMind
ReleasedAlphaFold 1 (2018); AlphaFold 2 (2020); AlphaFold 3 (2024)
TypeDeep learning, protein structure prediction
Key peopleDemis Hassabis, John Jumper

History

AlphaFold 1 won CASP13 in 2018. AlphaFold 2, using an attention-based "Evoformer" architecture trained on known structures and sequence alignments, achieved median accuracy rivalling experimental methods at CASP14, a breakthrough widely hailed as solving a 50-year grand challenge.

Database and impact

With EMBL-EBI, DeepMind released the AlphaFold Protein Structure Database, growing to over 200 million predicted structures covering nearly all catalogued proteins. The predictions have accelerated work in drug discovery, enzymology and disease research, though predictions carry per-residue confidence scores (pLDDT) and disordered regions remain unreliable.

AlphaFold 3

Released in 2024, AlphaFold 3 extends prediction to complexes of proteins with DNA, RNA, ligands and ions using a diffusion-based model.
