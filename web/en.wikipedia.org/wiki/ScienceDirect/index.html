ScienceDirect


Producer: Elsevier
Launched: 1997
Type: Bibliographic database / full-text platform

ScienceDirect is a website that provides access to a large bibliographic database of scientific and medical publications of the Dutch publisher Elsevier. Launched in 1997, it hosts over 4,000 academic journals and more than 40,000 e-books, encompassing some 18 million pieces of content from about 4,000 journals and 30,000 books. The platform is organized into four major subject areas: physical sciences and engineering; life sciences; health sciences; and social sciences and humanities.

Access

Journal articles and book chapters that are not open access generally require a subscription or pay-per-view purchase. Access is typically provided through institutional subscriptions at universities and research organizations, and the platform supports authentication methods such as IP recognition and federated login. ScienceDirect is one of the largest scholarly full-text platforms in the world, and its pricing and bundling practices have been a significant focus of debate in debates over open access and academic publishing economics.
