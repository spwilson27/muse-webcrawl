Donald L. Shell

Donald Lee Shell (1924–2015) was an American computer scientist best known for inventing the Shellsort sorting algorithm, published in 1959.

Shellsort

Shellsort generalises insertion sort by comparing elements separated by a diminishing gap. Shell's original gap sequence halved the interval each pass; later sequences by Hibbard, Pratt and Ciura improved performance.

Career

Shell worked at General Electric and later founded a company in data processing; his algorithm remains a standard teaching example of an in-place, non-stable sort that bridges simple and advanced methods.
