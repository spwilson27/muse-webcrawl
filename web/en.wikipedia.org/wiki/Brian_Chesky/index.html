Brian Chesky, chief executive of Airbnb.

Brian Joseph Chesky (born August 29, 1981) is an American businessman and industrial designer who is the co-founder and chief executive officer of the short-term rental platform Airbnb. He founded the company in 2008 with Joe Gebbia and Nathan Blecharczyk.

Early life and founding of Airbnb

Chesky grew up in Niskayuna, New York, and studied industrial design at the Rhode Island School of Design. After moving to San Francisco, he and Gebbia, unable to afford rent, rented out air mattresses in their apartment to attendees of a design conference in October 2007 — the origin of "AirBed & Breakfast". The company passed through the Y Combinator accelerator in 2009 and grew into one of the world's largest accommodation platforms; it went public in December 2020.

Philanthropy

Chesky has signed The Giving Pledge and, with his co-founders, committed substantial equity to host and community programmes. He is a billionaire, with his wealth derived mainly from his stake in Airbnb.
