The 2004–05 FA Premier League (known as the FA Barclays Premiership) was the 13th season of the Premier League. Chelsea, under new manager José Mourinho and bankrolled by owner Roman Abramovich, won their first league championship in 50 years, setting a then-record points total of 95 and conceding only 15 goals — still a Premier League record.

Season summary

Chelsea led the table from November and never relinquished first place, clinching the title at Bolton on 30 April 2005. Defending champions Arsenal, whose record 49-match unbeaten run was ended by Manchester United in October 2004, finished second, with United third. Everton surprisingly took fourth place, but Liverpool, who finished fifth, also qualified for the Champions League after winning the competition in Istanbul in one of the most famous finals of all time.

The relegation battle was uniquely dramatic: for the first time in Premier League history, no team was relegated before the final day. West Bromwich Albion, bottom at Christmas, completed the "Great Escape" by beating Portsmouth and surviving as all three rivals — Southampton, Norwich City and Crystal Palace — lost or drew.
