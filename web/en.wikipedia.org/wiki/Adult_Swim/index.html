Adult Swim (stylised as [adult swim]) is an American adult-oriented television programming block that airs on Cartoon Network during the evening and overnight hours. Operated by the Williams Street studio in Atlanta, it is known for surreal, experimental and often low-budget comedy series aimed at viewers aged 18 and over, and it is treated by ratings services as a separate network from Cartoon Network's daytime programming.

History

The block grew out of Cartoon Network's late-1990s experiments with mature programming, most notably Space Ghost Coast to Coast. Adult Swim launched on September 2, 2001, initially on Sunday nights, with early originals including Aqua Teen Hunger Force, Sealab 2021, The Brak Show and Harvey Birdman, Attorney at Law. The block expanded across the week and gained a reputation for ironic "bumper" cards, lo-fi aesthetics and championing independent animators.

Programming

Signature original series include Robot Chicken, The Venture Bros., Metalocalypse, Tim and Eric Awesome Show, Great Job!, The Eric Andre Show, Rick and Morty and Smiling Friends. The block also airs acquired animation such as Family Guy and Bob's Burgers reruns, and anime through its Toonami strand, which was revived under the Adult Swim banner in 2012. Adult Swim has also released music (notably the Metalocalypse-linked Dethklok albums and hip-hop singles programs), games and live events such as the Adult Swim Festival.
