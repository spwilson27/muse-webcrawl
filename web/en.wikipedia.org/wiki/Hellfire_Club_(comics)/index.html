Hellfire Club (comics)

The Hellfire Club is a fictional society of wealthy elites and mutants appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, most often as adversaries of the X-Men. Created by writer Chris Claremont and artist John Byrne, the Club debuted in Uncanny X-Men #129 (1980), modelled on the historical Hellfire Clubs and the television episode "A Touch of Brimstone" from The Avengers.

Organisation

The Club's ruling Inner Circle uses chess-piece titles — Black King, White Queen and so on. Notable members include Sebastian Shaw (Black King), Emma Frost (White Queen), Selene (Black Queen), Donald Pierce and Harry Leland. The Club figured centrally in the "Dark Phoenix Saga", in which Jean Grey was manipulated into becoming the Black Queen before transforming into the Dark Phoenix. The Hellfire Club has appeared in films such as X-Men: First Class (2011) and in the television series The Gifted.
