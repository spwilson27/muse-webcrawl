Nail gun

A nail gun, or nailer, is a power tool used to drive nails into wood or other materials. It is usually driven by compressed air (pneumatic), electromagnetism, a small explosive charge, or a combustible gas.

Types

Framing nailers drive large nails for timber framing; finish and brad nailers drive smaller fasteners for trim; roofing and flooring nailers are specialised for those trades. Nails are supplied in strips or coils collated with wire, paper or plastic.

Safety

Nail guns cause tens of thousands of injuries annually, commonly puncture wounds to hands and feet. Contact-trip triggers, which fire whenever the nose is depressed, are more dangerous than sequential-trip mechanisms.
