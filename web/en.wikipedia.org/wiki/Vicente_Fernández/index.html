Vicente Fernández

Died12 December 2021 (aged 81), Guadalajara, Jalisco, Mexico
GenresRanchera, mariachi, bolero
OccupationSinger, songwriter, actor, film producer

Vicente Fernández Gómez (17 February 1940 – 12 December 2021) was a Mexican singer, songwriter, actor, and film producer. Nicknamed "Chente" (short for Vicente), "El Charro de Huentitán" (The Charro from Huentitán), and "El Rey de la Música Ranchera" (The King of Ranchera Music), Fernández started his career as a busker and went on to become a cultural icon, recording more than 50 albums and appearing in more than 30 films.

His best-known songs include "Volver Volver", "Por Tu Maldito Amor", "Qué de Raro Tiene", "Las Llaves de Mi Alma", and "Guadalajara". Fernández's work earned him three Grammy Awards, nine Latin Grammy Awards, fourteen Lo Nuestro Awards, and a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame. In 1998, he was inducted into the International Latin Music Hall of Fame.

Early life

Fernández was born in the village of Huentitán El Alto, Jalisco, to a rancher father and a homemaker mother. After his family fell into poverty, he left school and worked as a shoeshiner, bricklayer, and dishwasher, singing in restaurants and at mariachi contests in Guadalajara. His breakthrough came in the mid-1960s in Mexico City: after the death of leading ranchera singer Javier Solís in 1966, radio station XEX gave Fernández a chance, and he signed with CBS Records the same year.

Career and retirement

His recording of "Volver Volver" (1976) became his signature song and consolidated his status throughout Latin America. Fernández also starred in dozens of ranchera films during the 1970s and 1980s. He announced his retirement from live performing in 2012, playing his farewell concert "Un Azteca en el Azteca" at the Estadio Azteca in April 2016, though he continued to record music. He died in December 2021 following complications from a fall at his ranch, Los Tres Potrillos, earlier that year; Mexico observed a period of national mourning, and he was honoured with a public wake at the Arena VFG in Guadalajara.
