Mardavij ibn Ziyar (died 935) was an Iranian military leader from Gilan who founded the Ziyarid dynasty. A Zoroastrian who aspired to restore the Persian Empire of the Sasanians, he conquered much of north-western Iran between 930 and 935 before being murdered by his own Turkish slave-soldiers.

Rise to power

Mardavij began his career in the service of the Alavid rulers of Tabaristan and then the warlord Asfar ibn Shiruya. In 930 he broke with Asfar, seized Gorgan and Tabaristan, and rapidly extended his power over Ray, Qazvin, Hamadan and Isfahan, defeating Abbasid forces sent against him.

Rule and death

Mardavij celebrated the Zoroastrian festival of Sadeh with great pomp at Isfahan and reportedly planned to conquer Baghdad and revive the Sasanian Empire. In January 935 he was assassinated in his bath by Turkish ghulams. His brother Vushmgir succeeded him, though the Ziyarid realm contracted sharply.
