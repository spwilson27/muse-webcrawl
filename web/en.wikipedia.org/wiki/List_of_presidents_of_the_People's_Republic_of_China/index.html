IncumbentXi Jinping (since 2013)

The president of the People's Republic of China is the ceremonial head of state of China, elected by the National People's Congress. The office was established by the 1954 constitution with Mao Zedong as its first holder (styled Chairman of the People's Republic), abolished under the 1975 constitution, and restored in 1982. Since the 1990s the presidency has normally been held concurrently by the general secretary of the Chinese Communist Party, the country's paramount leader.
