Peru

Machu Picchu citadel in the Peruvian Andes

Peru, officially the Republic of Peru, is a country in western South America bordered by Ecuador, Colombia, Brazil, Bolivia, Chile and the Pacific Ocean. Its population is about 34 million and its capital is Lima.

Peru was the heartland of the Inca Empire, the largest state in pre-Columbian America, and of earlier civilisations including Caral, one of the oldest in the world, and the Nazca, famous for the Nazca Lines. Spanish conquest began in 1532 under Francisco Pizarro. Machu Picchu, a 15th-century Inca citadel rediscovered by the outside world in 1911, is a UNESCO site and one of the New7Wonders of the World. Peru's geography spans Pacific desert coast, high Andes and Amazon rainforest; its cuisine, from ceviche to Nikkei fusion, is internationally celebrated. Political instability has been chronic, with frequent changes of president.
