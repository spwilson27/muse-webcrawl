Akihito

Akihito (Japanese: 明仁; born 23 December 1933) is a member of the Imperial House of Japan who reigned as the 125th emperor of Japan from 1989 until his abdication in 2019. He succeeded his father, Emperor Shōwa (Hirohito), upon his death, and presided over the Heisei era. On 30 April 2019 he became the first Japanese emperor to abdicate in over two centuries, and he is styled Emperor Emeritus. He was succeeded by his eldest son, Naruhito.

Early life and marriage

Akihito was born at the Tokyo Imperial Palace, the fifth child and eldest son of Emperor Shōwa and Empress Kōjun. As a child he was evacuated from Tokyo during the Second World War and was educated at the Gakushūin schools, also studying English with his American tutor Elizabeth Gray Vining. He was invested as Crown Prince in 1952. In 1959 he broke with precedent by marrying Michiko Shōda, a commoner — the daughter of a wealthy industrialist — whom he had met on a tennis court in Karuizawa; the match, celebrated as a symbol of the modernisation and democratisation of postwar Japan, made the couple enormously popular.

Reign

As emperor, Akihito developed the role of "symbol of the State" prescribed by the postwar constitution into an active programme of public engagement. He and Empress Michiko travelled to every Japanese prefecture and visited disaster zones — notably after the 1995 Great Hanshin earthquake and the 2011 Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami, when he made an unprecedented televised address to the nation — comforting victims in a style that won widespread affection. Abroad, he made carefully worded statements of remorse for Japan's wartime actions on visits to China, South Korea, Saipan, Palau, and the Philippines. An accomplished marine biologist, he published scientific papers on goby fish and maintained research throughout his reign.

Abdication

In August 2016 Akihito addressed the nation by video and hinted at his wish to retire, citing his age and declining health — he had undergone heart bypass surgery and treatment for prostate cancer. Since the Imperial Household Law made no provision for abdication, the Diet passed special one-off legislation in 2017 permitting it. He abdicated on 30 April 2019, ending the Heisei era, and took the title of Emperor Emeritus (Jōkō).
