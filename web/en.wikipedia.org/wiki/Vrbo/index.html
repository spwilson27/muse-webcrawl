Vrbo (pronounced "VER-boh") is an American online marketplace for vacation rentals, originally an acronym for Vacation Rentals by Owner. It is owned by Expedia Group and headquartered in Austin, Texas. Unlike its principal competitor Airbnb, Vrbo has traditionally listed only entire homes rather than shared spaces.

History

Vrbo was founded in 1995 by David Clouse in Breckenridge, Colorado, initially as a website where condominium owners could advertise ski rentals. It grew into one of the largest vacation-rental listing services in the United States. In 2006 Vrbo was acquired by HomeAway, the Austin-based vacation rental company, which itself was acquired by Expedia in 2015 for about $3.9 billion. In 2019 Expedia rebranded the combined business around Vrbo, folding the HomeAway brand into it in most markets, and in 2020 the company dropped the full "Vacation Rentals by Owner" name in favour of the initialism alone, with a new pronunciation and logo.

Business model

Property owners and professional managers list homes on the platform and pay either an annual subscription or a per-booking commission; travellers typically also pay a service fee. Listings number in the millions across more than 190 countries, concentrated in the United States, and skew toward family and group travel to beach, mountain, and lake destinations. Vrbo brands within Expedia Group work alongside sister travel brands such as Hotels.com and Expedia.com.
