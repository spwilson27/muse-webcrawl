Nativity scene

A Nativity scene, also known as a crib, crèche, or manger scene, is a depiction of the birth of Jesus as described in the Gospels of Matthew and Luke, displayed especially at Christmas. It typically includes Mary, Joseph, the infant Jesus in a manger, shepherds, angels, the Magi, and animals such as an ox and donkey, set in a stable or cave in Bethlehem.

History

St Francis of Assisi is credited with popularising the living Nativity at Greccio in 1223. Traditions vary worldwide: Provençal santons, Polish szopki, Mexican nacimientos and Neapolitan presepi are elaborate folk-art forms. Nativity scenes appear in churches, homes and public spaces, sometimes controversially in secular public settings.
