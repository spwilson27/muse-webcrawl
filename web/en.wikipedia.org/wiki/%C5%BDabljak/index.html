Žabljak is a small town in northern Montenegro, the centre of the Durmitor mountain region and Durmitor National Park. At 1,456 metres above sea level it is the highest town in the Balkans.

Tourism

Žabljak is Montenegro's principal mountain resort. In summer it serves as the gateway to Durmitor National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site known for glacial lakes such as the Black Lake (Crno jezero), deep canyons including the Tara River Canyon — the second deepest in the world — and peaks like Bobotov Kuk (2,523 m). In winter, its Savin Kuk ski area attracts regional visitors.

History

The town was heavily damaged during the Balkan Wars and the Second World War; it was burned during the Italian occupation in 1941 and rebuilt after the war. Its name derives from the Slavic word for frogs (žabe), said to have croaked in the local ponds.
