Infosys Foundation

Infosys Foundation
Founded: 1996
Headquarters: Bengaluru, India
Type: Charitable trust
Focus: Education, healthcare, rural development, arts and culture

The Infosys Foundation is the philanthropic and corporate social responsibility arm of Infosys, the Indian information-technology company. Established in 1996 and based in Bengaluru, the Foundation supports projects in education, healthcare, rural development, destitute care, and the preservation of arts and culture across India.

Activities

The Foundation has built libraries, classrooms, and computer centres in government schools, funded hospital wards and medical equipment, supported midday-meal and sanitation programmes, and restored heritage structures and traditional art forms. It responded to natural disasters such as floods and the COVID-19 pandemic with relief and medical infrastructure funding. For many years it was chaired by Sudha Murty, author and wife of Infosys co-founder N. R. Narayana Murthy. A separate Infosys Foundation USA, founded in 2015, supports computer-science education in the United States.
