Masala (spice mix)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Masala (from Hindi मसाला, ultimately from Arabic maṣāliḥ, "ingredients, materials") is a term for a mixture of ground spices used in South Asian cooking. A masala can be a blend of dry-roasted and ground spices, or a paste made with spices combined with wet ingredients such as onions, garlic, ginger, chillies, tomatoes, vinegar or coconut milk. Because households and regions blend their own, there is no single recipe: masalas vary by dish, region, community and family tradition.

Common types

  • Garam masala — a "warming" blend of spices such as black and green cardamom, cinnamon, cloves, cumin, coriander and black pepper, typically added late in cooking or sprinkled over finished dishes, especially in North Indian cuisine.
  • Curry powder — a British-era commercial blend, often turmeric-heavy, loosely modelled on South Indian spice mixtures; not a traditional single category in India itself.
  • Chaat masala — a tangy blend including dried mango powder (amchoor), cumin, black salt (kala namak) and asafoetida, sprinkled on snacks and fruit.
  • Tandoori masala — a reddish blend used in marinades for tandoor-cooked meats.
  • Regional blends — such as Maharashtrian goda masala and kala masala, Bengali panch phoron (a whole-spice mixture of five seeds), Sri Lankan roasted curry powder, and the spice pastes of Thai and Indonesian cooking, which are also called masalas in some contexts (e.g., in Malaysian and Indonesian usage).
  • Masala chai — tea brewed with a spiced blend (cardamom, ginger, cinnamon, cloves and pepper).

Use

Dry masalas are often toasted before grinding to release aromatic oils and are best used fresh; wet masala pastes form the flavour base of many curries, fried with oil or ghee at the start of cooking. The word "masala" has also spread into general usage in South Asia — giving rise to terms like "masala film" for a Bollywood movie mixing genres — and dishes described as "masala" (e.g., masala dosa) usually mean they are seasoned with a spiced mixture or filling.
