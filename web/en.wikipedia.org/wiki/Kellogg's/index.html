Kellogg's

Kellogg's
Founded: 1906, Battle Creek, Michigan
Founder: Will Keith Kellogg
Products: Breakfast cereals, snacks
Parent: Kellanova (from 2023)

Kellogg's is a brand of the American multinational food company founded by Will Keith Kellogg in 1906 in Battle Creek, Michigan. It became famous for ready-to-eat breakfast cereals such as Corn Flakes, Rice Krispies, Frosted Flakes (Frosties), Special K and Coco Pops.

History

W. K. Kellogg and his brother John Harvey Kellogg developed flaked cereals at the Battle Creek Sanitarium in the 1890s. After a dispute, Will founded his own company in 1906. Kellogg's expanded internationally through the 20th century and acquired brands including Pringles in 2012.

Restructuring

In 2023 the Kellogg Company split into Kellanova (global snacking and international cereals, retaining the Kellogg's brand) and WK Kellogg Co (North American cereals).

Marketing

Well-known mascots include Tony the Tiger, Toucan Sam and Snap, Crackle and Pop.
