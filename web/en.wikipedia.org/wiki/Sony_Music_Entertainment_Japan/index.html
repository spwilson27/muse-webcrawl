Sony Music Entertainment Japan

Sony Music Entertainment (Japan) Inc. (SMEJ) is a Japanese entertainment company wholly owned by Sony Group Corporation. It is independent of the United States–based Sony Music Entertainment and reports directly to Sony's headquarters in Tokyo. SMEJ is one of the largest music companies in Japan and also owns the animation studio Aniplex.

History

The company was founded in 1968 as CBS/Sony Records, a joint venture between CBS and Sony, and issued the first compact discs manufactured in Japan. It became Sony Music Entertainment (Japan) in 1991. Its labels include Sony Music Labels (formed from several historic imprints such as Sony Records, Epic Japan, Ki/oon Music, Ariola Japan and Sacra Music), which handles domestic and licensed international repertoire.

Aniplex and visual business

Through Aniplex, SMEJ is a major producer of anime, responsible for series such as Fullmetal Alchemist, Sword Art Online, Fate/stay night, and Demon Slayer. SMEJ also operates artist management (Sony Music Artists), live venues (Zepp halls, in partnership), and the streaming and visual content businesses.

Notable artists

Artists associated with SMEJ labels include YOASOBI, Kenshi Yonezu, Official HIGE DANdism, Nogizaka46, and many of Japan's leading domestic acts.
