United States Secret Service

U.S. Secret Service
Founded5 July 1865
Parent agencyDepartment of Homeland Security (since 2003)
MissionsProtection; financial crimes investigation

The United States Secret Service (USSS) is a federal law enforcement agency with two distinct missions: protecting national leaders and investigating financial crimes. It is best known for protecting the President and Vice President of the United States, their families, former presidents, and visiting heads of state.

The agency was founded in 1865 within the Department of the Treasury to combat widespread counterfeiting of U.S. currency after the Civil War. Presidential protection was added following the assassination of President William McKinley in 1901.

Organisation

The Secret Service employs special agents and a Uniformed Division that guards the White House and other sites in Washington, D.C. In 2003, the agency was transferred from the Treasury Department to the new Department of Homeland Security. Its financial crimes role now includes counterfeit currency, financial institution fraud, and cyber-enabled fraud investigations.
