Haruomi Hosono

Haruomi Hosono (細野 晴臣, Hosono Haruomi, born July 9, 1947), sometimes credited as Harry Hosono, is a Japanese musician, singer, songwriter, and producer. A founding member of the folk-rock band Happy End and the electronic group Yellow Magic Orchestra (YMO), he is regarded as one of the most influential figures in modern Japanese popular music.

Career

Hosono began his career as bassist in the psychedelic rock band Apryl Fool before co-founding Happy End in 1969 with Takashi Matsumoto, Eiichi Ohtaki, and Shigeru Suzuki; the band pioneered Japanese-language rock. His 1973 solo debut, Hosono House, drew on American country and folk influences, while mid-1970s albums such as Tropical Dandy explored exotica and tropical styles.

In 1978 he formed Yellow Magic Orchestra with Ryuichi Sakamoto and Yukihiro Takahashi. Pioneers of synth-pop and electronic dance music, YMO achieved international success with songs such as "Rydeen" and "Behind the Mask", and their use of samplers, drum machines, and video-game sounds influenced hip hop, techno, and ambient music worldwide.

Later work

Alongside YMO's periodic reunions, Hosono has released numerous solo albums spanning ambient music (including early video game soundtracks), swing, boogie-woogie, and electronica, and has produced artists across Japanese pop. Compilations and reissues of his 1970s work in the 2010s brought him renewed international attention, and he continues to perform live.
