Export control

Export controls are laws and regulations by which a country controls the export of goods, software, and technology for reasons of national security, foreign policy, or protection of scarce resources. Controlled items typically include weapons, military equipment, dual-use goods, and sensitive technologies such as advanced semiconductors, encryption, and nuclear-related materials.

In the United States, the main regimes are the International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR), administered by the State Department, and the Export Administration Regulations (EAR), administered by the Bureau of Industry and Security in the Department of Commerce. Licences may be required depending on the item, destination, end user, and end use.

Multilateral arrangements such as the Wassenaar Arrangement coordinate controls among participating states. Violations can carry heavy civil and criminal penalties.
