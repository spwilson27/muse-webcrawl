Zoologischer Garten (Berlin U-Bahn)

Zoologischer Garten is a Berlin U-Bahn station located beside the Berlin Zoological Garden, in the Charlottenburg district. It is served by lines U2 and U9, and connects with the Berlin Zoologischer Garten railway station above, served by S-Bahn and regional trains.

History

The U2 station opened on 11 March 1902 as part of Berlin's first U-Bahn line. The station was heavily damaged in the Second World War and was rebuilt. The U9 level, designed with striking orange tiling, opened on 28 August 1961, just weeks after the Berlin Wall divided the city.

Significance

During the division of Berlin, "Bahnhof Zoo" was West Berlin's most important railway station and a notorious social hotspot, later made famous internationally by the book and film Christiane F. – Wir Kinder vom Bahnhof Zoo. The station remains a major interchange for western Berlin.
