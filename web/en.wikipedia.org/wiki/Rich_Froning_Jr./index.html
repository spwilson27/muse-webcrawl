Rich Froning Jr.

Rich Froning Jr. (born July 21, 1987) is an American professional CrossFit athlete known for winning the men's title at the CrossFit Games in 2011, 2012, 2013, and 2014, earning the title "Fittest Man on Earth". He is widely regarded as one of the most successful and influential competitors in the sport's history.

Career

Froning, from Cookeville, Tennessee, debuted at the 2010 CrossFit Games, finishing second after a costly failure on a rope-climb event. He then won four consecutive individual championships. After 2014 he moved to team competition with CrossFit Mayhem Freedom, winning multiple Affiliate Cup titles, and later won further team championships with CrossFit Mayhem.

Other ventures

Froning owns and trains at CrossFit Mayhem in Cookeville, has published a memoir, First: What It Takes to Win (2013), and has been featured in documentary films about the CrossFit Games. He played baseball at Tennessee Tech before becoming a firefighter and discovering CrossFit.
