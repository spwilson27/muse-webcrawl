The Flag Institute is a British membership organisation devoted to vexillology, the study of flags. Founded in 1971 by the vexillologist William Crampton, it is one of the largest organisations of its kind in the world and is a member of the International Federation of Vexillological Associations (FIAV).

Activities

The Institute provides advice on flag design, protocol and usage to government bodies, local authorities and the public. It publishes the journal Flagmaster, considered a leading publication in vexillology, and maintains a library and archives on flags. It holds conferences and lectures, and its members have advised on the design and adoption of flags for British counties, cities and institutions.

UK Flag Registry

The Institute maintains the UK Flag Registry, a record of flags associated with British counties, regions and communities. Its registration process played a prominent role in the wave of county flag competitions held in the United Kingdom in the 2000s and 2010s, in which many historic counties adopted officially recognised flags.
