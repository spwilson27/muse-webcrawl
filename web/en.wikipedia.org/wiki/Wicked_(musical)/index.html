Wicked is a musical with music and lyrics by Stephen Schwartz and a book by Winnie Holzman. It is based on Gregory Maguire's 1995 novel Wicked: The Life and Times of the Wicked Witch of the West, a retelling of L. Frank Baum's The Wonderful Wizard of Oz from the perspective of the witches of Oz. The story follows Elphaba, a misunderstood young woman with green skin who becomes the Wicked Witch of the West, and her unlikely friendship with the popular Glinda, the Good Witch.

Synopsis

At Shiz University, the earnest, green-skinned Elphaba forms an unlikely bond with her roommate Galinda ("Glinda"). Summoned to the Emerald City, Elphaba discovers the Wizard is a fraud persecuting Oz's talking Animals; refusing to serve him, she is branded "wicked" and becomes a fugitive, while Glinda rises as the regime's public face. The show reframes familiar Oz events — the formation of the Tin Woodman, Scarecrow, and Cowardly Lion, and Dorothy's arrival — through Elphaba's story, climaxing in her staged melting and secret escape with Fiyero. Signature numbers include "Popular", "For Good", and "Defying Gravity".

Productions and film

The original Broadway production, starring Idina Menzel and Kristin Chenoweth, opened in October 2003, won three Tony Awards among ten nominations, and became one of the highest-grossing musicals of all time, with long-running productions worldwide. A two-part film adaptation directed by Jon M. Chu, starring Cynthia Erivo and Ariana Grande, began with Wicked in 2024, followed by Wicked: For Good in 2025.
