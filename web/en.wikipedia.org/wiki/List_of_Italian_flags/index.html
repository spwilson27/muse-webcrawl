List of Italian flags

This is a list of flags used in Italy, including the national flag, military and naval ensigns, regional flags, and historical flags of the Italian states.

National flags

  • Flag of Italy — a vertical tricolour of green, white, and red (il Tricolore), first adopted by the Cispadane Republic in 1797 and made the national flag of the Kingdom of Italy in 1861 and of the Italian Republic in 1946. The current version was formally specified in 2006.
  • Civil ensign — the tricolour with the arms of the maritime republics (Venice, Genoa, Pisa, and Amalfi) in the white stripe.
  • Naval ensign — the tricolour with the naval coat of arms, incorporating the four maritime republics' arms and a naval crown.
  • War flag of the armed forces, the standard of the President of the Republic, and the flags of the Army, Navy, Air Force, and Carabinieri.

Regional flags

Italy's twenty regions each have an official flag or gonfalon, most of which are recent adoptions:
Piedmont (red cross and lambello on white), Lombardy (the green field with the silver Curvilinear Cross of the Rosa Camuna), Veneto (the Lion of Saint Mark), Sicily (the Trinacria on a red-and-yellow diagonal field), Sardinia (the Four Moors), Tuscany (a silver Pegasus), Campania, Lazio, Emilia-Romagna, and others.

Historical flags

The list also covers the flags of the Napoleonic-era Italian republics and kingdoms (Cispadane and Cisalpine Republics, Kingdom of Italy 1805–1814), the pre-unification states (Kingdom of Sardinia, Kingdom of the Two Sicilies, Papal States, Grand Duchy of Tuscany, Republic of Venice), the Kingdom of Italy (1861–1946) with the Savoy arms in the centre, and the Italian Social Republic (1943–1945).
