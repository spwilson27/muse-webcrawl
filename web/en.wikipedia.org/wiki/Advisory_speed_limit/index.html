Advisory speed limit
Type: Traffic sign / recommended speed
Enforcement: Generally non-mandatory
Used for: Curves, ramps, hazards, work zones

An advisory speed limit is a speed recommendation posted by a traffic authority for a particular stretch of road or feature, such as a sharp curve, exit ramp or temporary hazard. Unlike a regulatory speed limit, exceeding an advisory speed is not by itself an offence in most jurisdictions.

Use

Advisory speeds are typically displayed on supplementary plaques beneath warning signs — for example, yellow diamond signs in the United States showing a recommended speed for a bend. They are usually set through engineering studies of the safe speed for the feature under good conditions.

Legal status

Although not strictly enforceable as limits, advisory speeds can be cited as evidence of careless driving after a crash, and some jurisdictions treat gross exceedance under basic speed laws.
