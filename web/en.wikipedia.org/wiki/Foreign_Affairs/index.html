Foreign Affairs is an American magazine of international relations and U.S. foreign policy published by the Council on Foreign Relations, a nonprofit think tank specialising in foreign policy. Founded in 1922, it is widely considered one of the most influential foreign policy journals.

History

The journal succeeded the short-lived Journal of International Relations. Its first editor was Harvard historian Archibald Cary Coolidge. Under longtime editor Hamilton Fish Armstrong (1928–1972) it became a central forum of the American foreign-policy establishment.

Notable articles

George F. Kennan's 1947 "X Article", "The Sources of Soviet Conduct", publicly set out the doctrine of containment of the Soviet Union. Samuel P. Huntington's 1993 article "The Clash of Civilizations?" and Francis Fukuyama's 1989 essay "The End of History?" also debuted in its pages, as did many statements of policy by presidents and secretaries of state.
