The Gibson Explorer is a solid-body electric guitar introduced by Gibson in 1958 as part of a futuristic "Modernistic" series alongside the Flying V. Its radical angular body was too far ahead of its time, and the original korina-wood run was discontinued by 1963 after small sales.

Revival

When other makers began selling Explorer-shaped copies in the 1970s, Gibson reissued the model in 1976 in mahogany. The reissue found favour with hard rock and heavy metal guitarists, and the Explorer became a staple of the genre.

Notable players

Players associated with the Explorer include James Hetfield of Metallica, The Edge of U2, and Matthias Jabs of the Scorpions. Original 1958–59 korina Explorers are among the most valuable vintage electric guitars.
