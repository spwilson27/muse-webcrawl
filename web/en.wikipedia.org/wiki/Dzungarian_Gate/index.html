Dzungarian Gate

Dzungarian Gate
Location: Kazakhstan–China border
Between: Dzungarian Alatau and Barlik ranges
Elevation: c. 460 m

The Dzungarian Gate, also known as the Alashankou pass, is a historically significant mountain pass on the border between Kazakhstan and Xinjiang, China, between the Dzungarian Alatau to the north and the Barlik range to the south.

Geography and history

The narrow valley connects the Balkhash basin and the Kazakh steppe with Dzungaria, funnelling strong winds. For centuries it was a principal route for trade and invasion between Central Asia and China, used by nomadic peoples including the Dzungars of the Dzungar Khanate.

Modern transport

Today the pass carries a highway and the railway linking Kazakhstan to China via Alashankou and Dostyk, a key Eurasian land bridge for freight, though gauge differences require transshipment. See also Dzungaria.
