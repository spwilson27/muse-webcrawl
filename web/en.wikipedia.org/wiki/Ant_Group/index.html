Ant Group Co., Ltd. is a Chinese financial technology company, an affiliate of Alibaba Group. It operates Alipay, the world's largest mobile and online payment platform, and offers digital banking, lending, wealth management and insurance products to over a billion users.

History

Ant Group began as Alipay, launched by Alibaba in 2004 as an escrow payment service for Taobao. In 2011 Alipay was spun out of Alibaba into a domestically owned entity controlled by Jack Ma, a move that caused a dispute with shareholders Yahoo and SoftBank, later settled. The company was renamed Ant Financial in 2014 and Ant Group in 2020. Its money-market fund Yu'ebao became the world's largest, and its online bank MYbank serves small businesses.

Suspended IPO and restructuring

In November 2020, days before a planned dual listing in Shanghai and Hong Kong expected to raise about US$34 billion — the largest IPO in history — Chinese regulators suspended the offering after Jack Ma publicly criticized financial regulators. Ant was subsequently ordered to restructure as a financial holding company, separate its lending businesses, and reduce Yu'ebao's scale. In 2023 the company was fined 7.1 billion yuan, and changes to voting rights ended Ma's control of the group.

Services

Beyond payments, Ant operates the Zhima (Sesame) Credit scoring system, lending platforms Huabei and Jiebei, insurance marketplace Ant Insurance, and the blockchain unit AntChain.
