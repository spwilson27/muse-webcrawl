Zack Snyder

Zachary Edward Snyder (born March 1, 1966) is an American filmmaker. He made his feature film debut in 2004 with Dawn of the Dead, a remake of the 1978 horror film of the same name. Since then, he has directed or produced a number of comic book and superhero films, including 300 (2006), Watchmen (2009), Man of Steel (2013), Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice (2016) and Justice League (2017), as well as the director's cut Zack Snyder's Justice League (2021).

Career

Born in Green Bay, Wisconsin, and raised in Connecticut, Snyder studied painting and filmmaking, attending the Art Center College of Design in Pasadena. He first became known directing commercials and music videos. His visually distinctive style, often employing slow-motion action sequences and a desaturated color palette, has been widely discussed by critics.

Snyder co-founded the production company The Stone Quarry (formerly Cruel and Unusual Films) with his wife Deborah Snyder and producing partner Wesley Coller. His later work includes the Netflix films Army of the Dead (2021) and the space opera Rebel Moon (2023) and its sequel (2024).
