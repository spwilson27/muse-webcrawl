Sik Sik Yuen Wong Tai Sin Temple

LocationWong Tai Sin, Kowloon, Hong Kong
DeityWong Tai Sin
Established1921

The Sik Sik Yuen Wong Tai Sin Temple is a well-known shrine and tourist attraction in Hong Kong, located in the Wong Tai Sin district of Kowloon. It is dedicated to Wong Tai Sin, the Great Immortal Wong, a deity revered for healing and for granting requests — summed up in the saying that the temple makes "every wish come true upon request."

Features

The temple complex includes halls and pavilions in traditional Chinese style, a Nine-Dragon Wall, and the Good Wish Garden. It is home to Taoist, Buddhist and Confucian elements. Worshippers practise kau chim, shaking a cylinder of bamboo fortune sticks until one falls out, whose number corresponds to a fortune to be interpreted.

History

The temple was established in 1921 by the Taoist organisation Sik Sik Yuen, which also operates schools and clinics. It is especially busy during the Chinese New Year and on Wong Tai Sin's birthday.
