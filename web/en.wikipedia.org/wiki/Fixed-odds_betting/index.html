Fixed-odds betting

TypeWagering
Contrasts withParimutuel betting; spread betting

Fixed-odds betting is wagering on an event at odds agreed when the bet is placed: the payout is fixed at that moment and does not change if the odds subsequently move. It is the standard form of sports betting offered by bookmakers, in contrast to parimutuel betting, where payouts are determined by the total pool staked.

Odds formats

Bookmakers express odds in three main formats: fractional odds (e.g. 5/1), traditional in the United Kingdom and Ireland; decimal odds (e.g. 6.0), common in Europe, Canada and Australia; and moneyline odds (e.g. +500 or −200), standard in the United States. The bookmaker's margin — the overround — is built into the odds so that the implied probabilities sum to more than 100%.

Fixed odds betting terminals

The term also became associated in Britain with fixed odds betting terminals (FOBTs), electronic gaming machines in betting shops offering roulette and other casino-style games. Following concern about high stakes and problem gambling, the UK government reduced the maximum FOBT stake from £100 to £2 in 2019.
