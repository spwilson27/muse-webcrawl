Flutter Entertainment plc is an Irish-American international sports betting and gambling company, the world's largest online betting operator by revenue. It was formed by the 2016 merger of Irish bookmaker Paddy Power and betting exchange Betfair, and renamed Flutter Entertainment in 2019 after the original name of the company that became Betfair. The group is a constituent of major stock indices and in 2024 moved its primary listing to the New York Stock Exchange, reflecting the importance of the United States market.

Operations

Flutter's portfolio includes FanDuel, the leading U.S. sportsbook and daily-fantasy operator, in which it acquired a majority stake in 2018 and later raised to about 95%; Paddy Power (retail and online bookmaking in Ireland and the UK); the Betfair betting exchange and sportsbook; PokerStars, acquired through the 2020 merger with Canada's The Stars Group in a deal creating the world's largest online gambling company; Sky Betting & Gaming; Sportsbet in Australia; and Italy's Sisal, acquired in 2022.

History

Paddy Power was founded in 1988 by the merger of three Irish bookmakers, and Betfair in 2000 as the pioneering betting exchange. Their 2016 combination created Paddy Power Betfair. The Stars Group acquisition closed in May 2020. Flutter later sold Oddschecker, settled long-running litigation with Fox Corporation over the Fox Bet joint venture (closed in 2023) and the FanDuel option, and expanded in Italy by acquiring Snaitech in 2025.
