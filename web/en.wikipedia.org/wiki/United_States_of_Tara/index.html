United States of Tara

United States of Tara is an American comedy-drama television series that aired on Showtime from January 18, 2009, to June 20, 2011, running for three seasons and 36 episodes. The series was created by Diablo Cody and stars Toni Collette as Tara Gregson, a suburban wife and mother living with dissociative identity disorder.

Premise

Tara Gregson, a Kansas artist and mother of two teenagers, transitions between several distinct alternate personalities ("alters") when stressed. Her alters include Alice, a 1950s-style housewife; Buck, a loud, gun-loving male persona; T, a flirtatious teenager; and others that emerge over the course of the series, including a child alter named Chicken, a therapist alter named Shoshana, and the self-destructive Gimme. Her husband Max (John Corbett) and her family cope with the unpredictability of her condition while Tara seeks to uncover the trauma in her past that caused it.

Cast

  • Toni Collette as Tara Gregson and her alters
  • John Corbett as Max Gregson
  • Keir Gilchrist as Marshall Gregson
  • Brie Larson as Kate Gregson
  • Rosemarie DeWitt as Charmaine Craine, Tara's sister

Reception

Collette won the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Comedy Series in 2009 and a Golden Globe Award in 2010 for her performance. The series was cancelled by Showtime in May 2011 after its third season.
