Rungrado May Day Stadium
Location:Rungra Island, Pyongyang, North Korea
Opened:1 May 1989
Capacity:114,000

The Rungrado May Day Stadium (Korean: 릉라도 5월1일 경기장) is a multi-purpose stadium on Rungra Island, Pyongyang, North Korea. It was opened on 1 May 1989 and is one of the largest stadiums in the world by seating capacity, currently able to hold around 114,000 spectators after a 2014 renovation. The stadium covers a total floor area of over 207,000 m² across eight storeys, with a distinctive scalloped roof of sixteen arches arranged like a parachute or magnolia blossom.

Uses

The stadium is used for football matches of the North Korea national team and, most famously, for the Arirang Festival Mass Games, a huge gymnastics and artistic performance involving tens of thousands of performers. In 1995 it hosted Collision in Korea, a professional wrestling event held jointly by WCW and NJPW, which drew some of the largest crowds in wrestling history.

Renovation

The stadium was renovated in 2014 with new seating, an improved pitch and modern facilities, reducing the capacity from an often-quoted 150,000 to about 114,000.
