October 17

October 17 is the 290th day of the year (291st in leap years) in the Gregorian calendar; 75 days remain until the end of the year.

Contents: Events · Births · Deaths · Holidays and observances

Events

  • 1604 – Kepler's Supernova, the most recent supernova observed in the Milky Way, was first sighted.
  • 1814 – Eight people died in the London Beer Flood after a large vat of porter ruptured at a brewery.
  • 1931 – Al Capone was convicted of income tax evasion in Chicago.
  • 1933 – Albert Einstein arrived in the United States as a refugee from Nazi Germany.
  • 1979 – Mother Teresa was awarded the Nobel Peace Prize.
  • 1989 – The 6.9 Mw Loma Prieta earthquake struck the San Francisco Bay Area, killing 63 people, during the 1989 World Series.
  • 2003 – Taipei 101, in Taiwan, was topped out, becoming at the time the world's tallest building under construction.

Births

  • 1915 – Arthur Miller, American playwright (d. 2005)
  • 1918 – Rita Hayworth, American actress (d. 1987)
  • 1972 – Eminem, American rapper

Deaths

  • 1849 – Frédéric Chopin, Polish composer and pianist (b. 1810)
  • 1967 – Puyi, last emperor of China (b. 1906)

Holidays and observances

  • International Day for the Eradication of Poverty
