Cheech & Chong are an American comedy duo consisting of Cheech Marin and Tommy Chong. They found commercial and cultural success in the 1970s and early 1980s with stand-up routines, comedy albums and feature films based on the hippie and free-love era, especially the countercultural trope of the perpetually stoned slackers "Cheech" and "Chong".

Career

The pair met in Vancouver, Canada, in the late 1960s and developed their act in improvisational theatre before moving to Los Angeles. Their debut album Cheech and Chong (1971) and follow-ups such as Big Bambu and the Grammy-winning Los Cochinos (1973) made them stars of comedy records. Sketches like "Dave's Not Here" became catchphrases.

Films

Their first film, Up in Smoke (1978), was a surprise box-office hit and essentially created the stoner film genre. Sequels included Cheech and Chong's Next Movie (1980), Nice Dreams (1981) and Still Smokin (1983). The duo split in 1985 amid creative differences.

Later years

Marin went on to a mainstream acting career, while Chong became a prominent cannabis activist. After Chong's 2003 prosecution and imprisonment in a paraphernalia case, the pair reunited for comedy tours beginning in 2008 and the animated film Cheech & Chong's Animated Movie! (2013).
