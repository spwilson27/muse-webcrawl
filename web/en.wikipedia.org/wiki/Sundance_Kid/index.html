Sundance Kid

Harry Alonzo Longabaugh (1867 – November 7, 1908?), better known as the Sundance Kid, was an American outlaw and member of Butch Cassidy's Wild Bunch gang in the American Old West. He took his nickname from the town of Sundance, Wyoming, the only place where he was ever imprisoned — jailed at age 20 for horse theft in 1887 and pardoned after 18 months by Governor Francis E. Warren.

Life and career

Longabaugh was born in Mont Clare, Pennsylvania, and travelled west as a teenager, working as a ranch hand. With Robert LeRoy Parker, known as Butch Cassidy, he formed the Wild Bunch, which robbed trains and banks across Wyoming, Montana, South Dakota, and Nevada during the 1890s. The gang's last American train robbery was near Wagner, Montana, in July 1901. A famous 1900 group portrait of the gang in Fort Worth, Texas — the "Fort Worth Five" photograph — was recognized by a Pinkerton detective and helped drive the outlaws from the country.

South America and death

In 1901 Longabaugh sailed to South America with Cassidy and Etta Place, Longabaugh's companion, whose real identity remains unknown. They posed as ranchers in Argentine Patagonia before returning to bank and payroll robberies in Bolivia and Argentina. Pinkerton agents and local law enforcement pursued them; Etta Place returned to the United States, possibly for medical treatment, around 1906.

In November 1908, two North American bandits who held up a payroll near San Vicente, Bolivia, were cornered by soldiers and local police; after a gunfight, both died — one apparently by suicide, the other killed. The men were widely identified as Cassidy and Longabaugh, and were buried in the San Vicente cemetery. A 1991 forensic exhumation and DNA comparison against relatives found no match, and the pair's exact fate has remained a durable Western mystery, though most historians accept the San Vicente identification.

Legacy

The 1969 film Butch Cassidy and the Sundance Kid, starring Robert Redford as the Sundance Kid, cemented his image as the taciturn, fast-drawing partner to Cassidy's schemer. The Sundance Film Festival and Sundance Institute take their names, via Redford, from the character.
