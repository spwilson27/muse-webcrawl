Cuius regio, eius religio

Cuius regio, eius religio is a Latin phrase which literally means 'whose realm, his religion' — i.e. the religion of the ruler dictates the religion of the ruled. It summarises a principle of the Peace of Augsburg (1555).

Peace of Augsburg

The 1555 settlement between Charles V and Lutheran princes allowed each prince of the Holy Roman Empire to choose either Lutheranism or Catholicism for his territory. Subjects who dissented could emigrate (ius emigrandi). Calvinism was not recognised, and the ecclesiastical reservation barred prince-bishops who converted from taking their lands with them.

Later development

The Peace of Westphalia (1648) extended recognition to Calvinism and set a normative year of 1624. The phrase, coined later (attributed to Joachim Stephani, 1586), became shorthand for early-modern confessional state-building.
