Dame Elizabeth Jane Campion DNZM (born 30 April 1954) is a New Zealand film director, screenwriter and producer. She is the first woman to win the Palme d'Or at Cannes, for The Piano (1993), and the third woman to win the Academy Award for Best Director, for The Power of the Dog (2021).

Career

Campion studied at the Australian Film, Television and Radio School. Her early features Sweetie (1989) and An Angel at My Table (1990) won international acclaim. The Piano (1993), starring Holly Hunter and Harvey Keitel, won the Palme d'Or and three Academy Awards, including Best Original Screenplay for Campion. Her later films include The Portrait of a Lady (1996), Holy Smoke! (1999), In the Cut (2003) and Bright Star (2009), about the poet John Keats.

Television

Campion co-created the acclaimed television miniseries Top of the Lake (2013) and its sequel China Girl (2017), set in New Zealand and Australia.
