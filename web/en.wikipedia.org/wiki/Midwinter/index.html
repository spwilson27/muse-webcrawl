Midwinter

Midwinter is the name given to the period around the winter solstice, the middle of the winter season in each hemisphere. In the Northern Hemisphere it falls around 21 December, and in the Southern Hemisphere around 21 June. The winter solstice itself is the shortest day and longest night of the year, when the Sun reaches its lowest maximum daily elevation in the sky at one of Earth's poles. "Midwinter" is also used, less precisely, for the coldest part of winter, which in many temperate climates lags the solstice by several weeks, falling in January and February in the north.

Solstice astronomy

At the December solstice the Sun is at its southernmost declination (−23.44°), directly overhead at noon on the Tropic of Capricorn; at the June solstice it stands over the Tropic of Cancer. In polar regions midwinter brings the polar night: the Sun does not rise at all near the winter pole, a fact that makes the solstice a dramatic turning point — after it, days begin to lengthen in the north.

Cultural observances

Midwinter has been celebrated since Neolithic times, and monuments such as Stonehenge and Newgrange are aligned on the solstice sunrise or sunset. Festivals around midwinter include the Germanic and Norse Yule, the Roman Saturnalia and Sol Invictus, the Iranian Shab-e Yalda, the Chinese Dongzhi Festival, the Japanese Tōji, and modern Neopagan celebrations of the solstice as one of the eight sabbats. Christmas, fixed at 25 December, falls within the midwinter season in the north, while in the Southern Hemisphere midwinter festivals such as the Inti Raymi of the Andes mark the June solstice, and Antarctic research stations celebrate Midwinter Day (21 June) as their principal holiday of the polar night, with feasts and messages from home.

In other uses

"Midwinter" also appears in literature and popular culture — most famously Shakespeare's A Midsummer Night's Dream draws its contrast with the summer solstice season — and as the title of works including John Buchan's novel Midwinter (1923) and the 1989 computer game Midwinter.
