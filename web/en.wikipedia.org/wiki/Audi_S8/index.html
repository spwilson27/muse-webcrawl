The Audi S8 is a high-performance version of the Audi A8 full-size luxury saloon, produced by Audi since 1996. Combining the A8's aluminium space-frame construction and understated luxury with powerful engines and quattro all-wheel drive, the S8 is the flagship of Audi's S performance range and a competitor to cars such as the Mercedes-AMG S-Class and BMW Alpina B7.

First generation (D2, 1996–2002)

The original S8 used a 4.2-litre V8 producing 340 PS (later 360 PS), paired with a six-speed manual or five-speed automatic transmission. It gained fame as the hero car of the 1998 film Ronin, whose chase scenes showcased the car's abilities.

Second generation (D3, 2006–2010)

The D3 S8 adopted a 5.2-litre V10 engine, closely related to the Lamborghini Gallardo's powerplant, producing 450 PS, with a six-speed automatic transmission and a 40:60 rear-biased quattro system.

Third generation (D4, 2012–2017)

The D4 S8 switched to a 4.0-litre twin-turbocharged V8 producing 520 PS, accelerating from 0–100 km/h in about 4.2 seconds; the later S8 plus raised output to 605 PS. Cylinder-on-demand technology deactivated four cylinders at light loads for improved economy.

Fourth generation (D5, 2019–present)

The current S8 pairs the 4.0-litre twin-turbo V8 with a 48-volt mild-hybrid system for about 571 PS, and adds predictive active suspension, all-wheel steering, and the full suite of A8 luxury and driver-assistance technology.
