Work exchange

A work exchange, also known as work travel or workaway, is an arrangement in which a traveller works for a host in return for accommodation, food and cultural experience, rather than wages.

Practice

Hosts are commonly farms, hostels, vineyards, sailing boats and households. Networks such as WWOOF (founded 1971, linking volunteers with organic farms), Workaway and HelpX match travellers with hosts. Typical work is a few hours a day, several days a week.

Issues

Because participants often enter on tourist visas, work exchanges sit in a legal grey area in some countries; insurance, hours and expectations are negotiated informally between host and guest.
