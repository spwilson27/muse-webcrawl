Mexican oil expropriation

The Mexican oil expropriation on 18 March 1938 was the nationalisation of Mexico's oil reserves and expropriation of equipment from foreign oil companies by President Lázaro Cárdenas.

Background

Article 27 of the 1917 Constitution claimed subsoil resources for the nation. A labour dispute between oil workers and foreign firms (Mexican Eagle, Standard Oil, Royal Dutch Shell) escalated when companies refused a Supreme Court-backed wage award.

Aftermath

Cárdenas created Pemex (Petróleos Mexicanos) in June 1938. Britain broke diplomatic relations; boycotts followed, but compensation was eventually negotiated. 18 March is a national civic holiday in Mexico.
