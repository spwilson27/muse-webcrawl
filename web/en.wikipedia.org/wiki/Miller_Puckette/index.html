Miller Puckette

Miller Smith Puckette (born 1959) is an American mathematician and computer music researcher, best known as the creator of the Max and Pure Data (Pd) programming environments for interactive computer music and multimedia.

Career

Puckette developed the original Max at IRCAM in Paris in the mid-1980s. After moving to the University of California, San Diego, he created Pure Data in 1996 as a freely available, open-source successor. His textbook The Theory and Technique of Electronic Music (2007) is a standard reference in the field. He has served as associate director of the Center for Research in Computing and the Arts (CRCA) at UCSD.
