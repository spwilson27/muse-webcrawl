List of tallest structures

The tallest human-made structure in the world is the Burj Khalifa in Dubai, at 829.8 metres (2,722 ft). This list ranks the tallest completed structures of all types — buildings, towers, masts, chimneys and offshore platforms.

History

For most of history the Great Pyramid of Giza (146.6 m) was the tallest human-made structure, a record it held for more than 3,800 years until Lincoln Cathedral surpassed it around 1311. The Eiffel Tower (1889) reached 330 m, and the twentieth century saw masts and then skyscrapers pass 600 m.
