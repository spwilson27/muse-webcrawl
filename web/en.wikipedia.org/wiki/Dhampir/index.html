Dhampir

In Balkan folklore, a dhampir is a creature that is the result of a union between a vampire and a human. The word is derived from the Albanian language, where it is traditionally understood to mean "tooth drinker" or similar.

Folklore

According to tradition, dhampirs were believed to possess the ability to see and identify vampires, which are normally invisible to others. This made them valued as vampire hunters in Serbian, Macedonian and Bulgarian folklore. A dhampir was often hired by villages to find and destroy vampires troubling the community.

Traits

Folklore attributes various traits to dhampirs: a jelly-like body without bones in some tales, a short life, dark hair, and an agitated temperament. In some traditions the dhampir's powers were hereditary, passing to descendants.

In popular culture

The dhampir has become a common figure in modern fantasy fiction, films and games, often depicted as a half-vampire with some vampiric powers but without their weaknesses, frequently serving as a vampire hunter.
