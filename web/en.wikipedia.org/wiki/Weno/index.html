Weno


Weno is the largest island in Chuuk Lagoon.

Weno (formerly Moen) is an island municipality in Chuuk State of the Federated States of Micronesia. It is the largest island in Chuuk Lagoon (formerly Truk Lagoon), covering about 20 square kilometres, and its main town is the state capital and the largest settlement in the country outside the national capital. The island's highest point is Mount Tonoken, at about 364 metres.

History

During the Second World War, Chuuk Lagoon was a major Imperial Japanese Navy base — sometimes called the "Gibraltar of the Pacific" — and Weno hosted airfields, anchorages, and supply facilities. In February 1944, the United States Navy's Operation Hailstone devastated the base, sinking dozens of ships and destroying hundreds of aircraft; the wrecks in the lagoon now form a renowned scuba diving destination. After the war the islands were administered by the United States as part of the Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands before Micronesian independence.

The island today

Weno contains the state government offices, Chuuk International Airport, the main commercial port, hospitals, and schools serving the lagoon's many islands. Most residents are Chuukese, and Chuukese is the principal language alongside English. Transport within the lagoon is mainly by boat, as the outlying islands have no road connections to Weno.
