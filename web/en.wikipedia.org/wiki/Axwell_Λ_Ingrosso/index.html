Axwell Λ Ingrosso (pronounced "Axwell and Ingrosso") was a Swedish electronic dance music duo consisting of Axwell (Axel Hedfors) and Sebastian Ingrosso, two of the three members of the supergroup Swedish House Mafia. Formed in 2014 after Swedish House Mafia's initial breakup, the duo released a string of hit singles and performed as headliners at major festivals worldwide before pausing the project in 2018 when Swedish House Mafia reunited.

History

Following Swedish House Mafia's farewell tour in 2013, Axwell and Ingrosso announced their joint project in 2014, debuting at the Governors Ball in New York. Early singles included "Something New" (2014) and "On My Way" (2015). Their biggest hits were "Sun Is Shining" (2015), a number-one single in Sweden used in an H&M advertising campaign, and "I Love You" (2017) featuring Kid Ink. Other releases included "This Time", "Thinking About You", "Renegade", "Dancing Alone" (2018, with RØMANS), and "Dreamer" featuring Trevor Guthrie.

The duo headlined festivals such as Ultra Music Festival, Tomorrowland, and Summerburst, and held a residency at Ushuaïa in Ibiza. At Ultra Music Festival in March 2018 they closed the festival with a surprise Swedish House Mafia reunion set, and the Axwell Λ Ingrosso project was put on hold as the trio resumed activity.
