Stealth game

A stealth game is a genre of video game in which the player must avoid detection by enemies, using concealment, timing and observation to complete objectives rather than direct combat. Enemies typically patrol predictable routes and respond to sight, sound and disturbances.

Gameplay

Core mechanics include vision cones, light and shadow, noise propagation, hiding bodies, disguises and non-lethal takedowns. Many stealth games reward players for finishing missions unseen, sometimes ranking them as a "ghost".

History

The genre was pioneered by Castle Wolfenstein (1981) and popularised by Hideo Kojima's Metal Gear (1987) and especially Metal Gear Solid (1998). Influential series include Thief, Splinter Cell, Hitman, Dishonored and Deus Ex, and stealth elements have spread widely into action and role-playing games.
