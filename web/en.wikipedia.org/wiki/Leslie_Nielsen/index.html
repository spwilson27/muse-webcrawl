Leslie Nielsen

Leslie William Nielsen OC (11 February 1926 – 28 November 2010) was a Canadian-American actor and comedian. With a career spanning 60 years, he appeared in more than 100 films and 150 television programs, portraying more than 220 characters.

Nielsen was born in Regina, Saskatchewan. He made his acting debut in 1950, appearing in 46 live television programs a year. He made his film debut in 1956, with supporting roles in several dramas, westerns and romance films. His breakthrough came with leading roles in the science fiction film Forbidden Planet (1956) and adventure film The Poseidon Adventure (1972).

Comedy career

Although Nielsen's acting career crossed a variety of genres in both television and film, his deadpan delivery in the comedy film Airplane! (1980) marked a turning point in his career, making him one of the most recognizable comedic actors in Hollywood. Nielsen later gained further acclaim for the Naked Gun film series, based on his portrayal of Detective Frank Drebin in Police Squad!.

Personal life and death

Nielsen married four times and had two daughters. He died in his sleep of pneumonia on 28 November 2010 in Fort Lauderdale, Florida, at age 84.
