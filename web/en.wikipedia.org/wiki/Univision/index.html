Univision is an American Spanish-language free-to-air television network owned by TelevisaUnivision. It is the United States' largest provider of Spanish-language content, and its programming is aimed primarily at the Hispanic and Latino American audience. The network's studios, production facilities and business operations are based in Doral, Florida, with major offices in New York City and Los Angeles.

History

Univision traces its origins to the Spanish International Network (SIN), launched in 1962 by Mexico's Telesistema Mexicano (later Televisa) together with American partners, built around station KWEX-TV in San Antonio, Texas. SIN was the first television network in the United States to broadcast in a language other than English. Following a Federal Communications Commission ruling on foreign ownership, the network was sold in 1986 to a group headed by Hallmark Cards and was relaunched as Univision in 1987.

In 2007 the network was acquired by Broadcasting Media Partners, a consortium led by Saban Capital Group, in a deal valued at about $13.7 billion. In 2022 Univision merged with Televisa's content business to form TelevisaUnivision, combining the two largest Spanish-language content producers in the world.

Programming

Univision's schedule is dominated by telenovelas produced by Televisa, along with variety shows such as Sábado Gigante (which ran from 1962 to 2015), news programming under the Noticiero Univision banner, sports coverage including Liga MX and FIFA World Cup matches, and reality and awards programming such as Nuestra Belleza Latina and Premio Lo Nuestro. Its prime-time telenovela block has frequently made Univision competitive with — and at times ahead of — the major English-language broadcast networks among younger demographics.

Affiliates and related networks

Univision programming is distributed through more than 50 owned-and-operated and affiliate stations across the continental United States and Puerto Rico. Sister networks include UniMás, Galavisión, TUDN and the streaming service ViX, launched in 2022.
