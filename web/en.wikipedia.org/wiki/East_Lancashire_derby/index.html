The East Lancashire derby, also known as the Cotton Mills derby and the M65 derby, is the football rivalry between Blackburn Rovers and Burnley, two clubs from the former mill towns of East Lancashire in North West England, separated by about 11 miles. Both were founder members of the Football League in 1888 and are among the oldest professional clubs in the world. The derby is considered one of the fiercest in English football.

History

The rivalry dates to the 1880s, when Blackburn and Burnley were leading centres of the cotton industry whose mills and workers sustained the clubs. The first competitive meeting took place on 3 November 1888 at Turf Moor in the inaugural Football League season, Blackburn winning 7–1. Through the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries the fixture was one of the League's marquee events, deciding championships and cup ties, and matches were major occasions in the mill towns' social calendar.

The clubs followed divergent paths after the Second World War: Burnley won the league title in 1959–60, while Blackburn were Premier League champions in 1994–95 under Kenny Dalglish. Because the clubs spent long stretches in different divisions, meetings became rare and intense; between 1983 and 2000 the teams did not meet at all in the league, and the 2000–01 season reunion in the First Division was marked by heavy policing. Premier League meetings followed in 2009–10, when Burnley were promoted, and again in the Championship through the 2010s and 2020s.

Culture

The nickname "Cotton Mills derby" reflects the towns' shared industrial heritage; "El Lanclasico" is a humorous journalistic label. Local bragging rights have spawned enduring folk traditions, including the legend that the losing town's fans must endure the victors' colours displayed on landmarks. Matches are usually scheduled early in the day on police advice, and away fans have at times been required to travel on official club coaches ("bubble trips").

Notable matches

  • 1888 — the first league meeting, a 7–1 Blackburn win at Turf Moor.
  • 1913–14 FA Cup — Burnley won the cup, beating Blackburn on the way in the semi-final season of their triumph.
  • 2000 — Blackburn won 2–0 at Turf Moor in the first league derby for 17 years.
  • 2010 — Blackburn completed the double over Burnley in the Premier League, including a 1–0 win at Turf Moor settled by a David Dunn penalty.
  • 2014 — Burnley ended a 35-year wait for a win over Blackburn, coming from behind to win 2–1 at Ewood Park.
