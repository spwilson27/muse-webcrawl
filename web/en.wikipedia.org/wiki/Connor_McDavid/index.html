Connor Andrew McDavid (born January 13, 1997) is a Canadian professional ice hockey centre and captain of the Edmonton Oilers of the National Hockey League (NHL). Widely regarded as one of the best players in the world, he is noted for exceptional speed, skating, and playmaking.

Career

McDavid was granted "exceptional player" status by Hockey Canada and was selected first overall in the 2012 OHL draft by the Erie Otters. The Oilers chose him first overall in the 2015 NHL draft, and he was named team captain in 2016, the youngest captain in NHL history at the time. He has won the Art Ross Trophy as the league's scoring leader multiple times, the Hart Memorial Trophy as most valuable player, and the Ted Lindsay Award as outstanding player as voted by his peers. In the 2022–23 season he scored 153 points, the highest total of the salary-cap era to that point; in 2024 he won the Conn Smythe Trophy as playoff MVP even as Edmonton lost the Stanley Cup Final, and in 2024 he reached 1,000 career points, among the fastest ever to do so.

International play

McDavid has represented Canada at the World Junior Championships (gold in 2015) and the World Championship (gold in 2016), and captained Team North America at the 2016 World Cup of Hockey.
