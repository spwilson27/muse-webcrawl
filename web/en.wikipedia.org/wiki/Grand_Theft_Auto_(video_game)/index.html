Grand Theft Auto (video game)

Grand Theft Auto is a 1997 action-adventure game developed by DMA Design and published by BMG Interactive. It is the first entry in the Grand Theft Auto series. The game was released for MS-DOS and Windows in November 1997, for PlayStation in 1998, and for Game Boy Color in 1999.

Gameplay

The game is played from a top-down perspective in open cities — Liberty City, San Andreas, and Vice City — where the player, a criminal working for various gangs, completes missions such as assassinations, robberies, and car thefts. Points are earned for crimes and completed jobs, with a multiplier system rewarding riskier play; enough points unlock the next level. The player can also freely roam, stealing vehicles and causing mayhem, pursued by police under a wanted-level system.

Development and reception

The game was created by David Jones and Mike Dailly at DMA Design in Dundee, Scotland. It was originally conceived as a cops-and-robbers game called Race 'n' Chase before the focus shifted to the criminal. Reviews were mixed on its graphics and technical performance but praised its gameplay freedom and humour; it was a commercial success and spawned one of the most successful media franchises in history. Two expansion packs set in London were released in 1999.
