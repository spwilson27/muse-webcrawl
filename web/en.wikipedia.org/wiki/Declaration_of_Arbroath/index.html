Declaration of Arbroath
Date6 April 1320
LocationArbroath Abbey, Scotland
AddresseePope John XXII

The Declaration of Arbroath is a 1320 declaration of Scottish independence: a letter in Latin from the barons and freeholders of Scotland to Pope John XXII, asserting Scotland's status as an independent kingdom and Robert the Bruce as its king.

Content

It recounts Scotland's ancient history, complains of English aggression under Edward I, and declares the Scots would depose even Robert if he submitted them to England: "It is not for glory, nor riches, nor honours that we are fighting, but for freedom - for that alone, which no honest man gives up but with life itself."

Legacy

Sent during the Wars of Scottish Independence, it helped persuade the Pope to recognise Scottish independence. It is celebrated as an early statement of popular sovereignty and national self-determination.
