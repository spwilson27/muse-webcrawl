Modane is a commune in the Savoie department in the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region of south-eastern France. It lies in the Maurienne valley on the river Arc, close to the Italian border, and is best known as the French end of the Fréjus railway and road tunnels beneath the Alps.

Transport

Modane station is the international border station on the Culoz–Modane railway (the Fréjus line) between France and Italy; trains pass through the 13.7 km Fréjus Rail Tunnel, opened in 1871, to Bardonecchia. The parallel Fréjus Road Tunnel, opened in 1980, carries motorway traffic. The planned Turin–Lyon high-speed line's Mont d'Ambin base tunnel will pass nearby.

History and geography

The town grew with the railway in the 19th century and suffered heavy flooding in 1957 when the Arc burst its banks. Above the town stands the 19th-century Fort du Replaton, built to defend the tunnel entrance. The ski resort of Valfréjus lies within the commune.
