The Comoros, officially the Union of the Comoros, is an island country in the Indian Ocean, located at the northern end of the Mozambique Channel off the eastern coast of Africa. It shares maritime borders with Madagascar, Mozambique, Tanzania, and the French department of Mayotte, which the Comoros claims as part of its territory.

The archipelago consists principally of three islands: Grande Comore (Ngazidja), Mohéli (Mwali), and Anjouan (Nzwani). The capital and largest city is Moroni on Grande Comore. The country is a member of the Arab League and the African Union, and its population is predominantly Sunni Muslim.

History

The islands were first settled by Austronesian and Bantu-speaking peoples, later joined by Arab traders who brought Islam and established sultanates. France colonised the islands in the 19th century. In a 1974 referendum, three islands voted for independence while Mayotte voted to remain French; the Comoros unilaterally declared independence on 6 July 1975.

The country's post-independence history was marked by political instability, including numerous coups and attempted coups, notably involving the French mercenary Bob Denard. A new constitution in 2001 created the Union of the Comoros with a rotating presidency among the three islands.

Geography

The Comoros are volcanic islands. Mount Karthala on Grande Comore is an active shield volcano and the country's highest point at 2,361 metres. The climate is tropical marine, with a rainy season from November to April.

Economy

The economy is based largely on subsistence agriculture and fishing. The Comoros is one of the world's leading producers of ylang-ylang essence used in perfumes, and a significant producer of vanilla and cloves. Remittances from the large Comorian diaspora, especially in France, are an important source of income.

Demographics

The Comorian people are of mixed African, Arab, Malagasy, and Austronesian ancestry. The Comorian languages (Shikomori) are Bantu languages closely related to Swahili, written in both Arabic and Latin scripts. Islam is the dominant religion, practised by about 98% of the population.
