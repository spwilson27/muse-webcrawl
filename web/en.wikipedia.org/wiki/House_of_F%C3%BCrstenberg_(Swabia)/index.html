House of Fürstenberg (Swabia)

The House of Fürstenberg is a German noble family from Swabia, first documented in the 11th century as counts of Urach. The family took its name from Fürstenberg castle and the landgraviate of Fürstenberg in the southern Black Forest region.

History

Through inheritance and purchase the counts assembled a patchwork principality in south-western Germany. The family was raised to princely rank in 1716, and its territories were mediatised to Baden, Württemberg and Hohenzollern-Sigmaringen in 1806.

Holdings

The family's seat is Donaueschingen, where the source of the Danube is traditionally placed in the palace gardens. The princely house remains one of Germany's largest private landowners and owns the Fürstenberg brewery, founded in 1283.
