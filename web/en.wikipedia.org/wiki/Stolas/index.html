Stolas

Stolas, also known as Stolos, is a Great Prince of Hell in demonological grimoires including the Pseudomonarchia Daemonum (1577) and the Ars Goetia (first part of the Lesser Key of Solomon). He commands twenty-six legions of demons.

Description

Stolas is depicted as a crowned owl with long legs, or as a raven, and later in human form. He is said to teach astronomy and knowledge of poisonous plants, herbs, and precious stones.

In popular culture

A stylised aristocratic owl version of Stolas is a main character in the adult animated web series Helluva Boss (2020–), voiced by Bryce Pinkham, which greatly raised the figure's public profile.
