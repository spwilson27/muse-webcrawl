Mitsubishi Motors

Mitsubishi Motors Corporation is a Japanese multinational automobile manufacturer headquartered in Minato, Tokyo. It was formed in 1970 from the automotive division of Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, whose first car, the Model A, dated to 1917. Since 2016 it has been part of the Renault–Nissan–Mitsubishi Alliance, with Nissan holding a controlling stake.

History

Mitsubishi built a strong reputation in rallying with the Lancer Evolution and Pajero, winning the Dakar Rally many times. A partnership with Chrysler in the 1970s–1990s included the Diamond-Star Motors plant in Illinois. In 2016 the company admitted falsifying fuel-economy data for Japanese kei cars, leading to Nissan's investment.

Products

Current and notable models include the Outlander (including a plug-in hybrid version), Pajero/Montero, Triton/L200 pickup, Eclipse Cross, and the i-MiEV electric car.
