Turn-based tactics

Turn-based tactics (TBT), or tactical turn-based (TTB), is a subgenre of strategy video games in which players take turns issuing orders to a small squad of units on a battlefield, as opposed to the larger armies of turn-based strategy games. The genre emphasises positioning, cover, line of sight and the individual abilities of each unit.

Gameplay

Games typically grant each unit action points or a fixed set of moves and attacks per turn. Common mechanics include overwatch, flanking bonuses, destructible cover, morale, and permadeath of named soldiers. Many titles add a strategic layer — base building, research or a world map — between tactical missions.

History

Early examples include Laser Squad (1988) and UFO: Enemy Unknown (1994, also known as X-COM: UFO Defense). Japanese tactical role-playing games such as Fire Emblem and Final Fantasy Tactics developed the genre in parallel. XCOM: Enemy Unknown (2012) revived the genre for a wide audience, followed by titles such as Into the Breach, Wargroove and Gears Tactics.
