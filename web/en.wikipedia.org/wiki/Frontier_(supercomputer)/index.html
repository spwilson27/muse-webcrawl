Frontier (supercomputer)

Frontier, officially the OLCF-5, is a supercomputer located at the Oak Ridge Leadership Computing Facility of Oak Ridge National Laboratory in Tennessee, United States. Delivered in 2021–2022, it was the world's first supercomputer to break the exascale barrier, achieving over one quintillion floating-point operations per second on the LINPACK benchmark (about 1.1–1.2 exaFLOPS), and it topped the TOP500 list from June 2022 until it was succeeded by El Capitan in November 2024.

Design

Built by Hewlett Packard Enterprise using its Cray EX architecture, Frontier consists of more than 9 million cores across AMD 3rd-generation EPYC CPUs and over 37,000 AMD Instinct MI250X accelerators, connected by the Slingshot interconnect. The machine occupies about 680 square metres of floor space, consumes roughly 22–24 megawatts of power, and is cooled by millions of litres of water circulated per minute.

Use

Frontier supports open scientific research selected through DOE programmes — simulations in astrophysics, materials science, fusion energy, climate science, biology, and artificial intelligence — as well as classified work for national security. Its procurement cost was approximately US$600 million.
