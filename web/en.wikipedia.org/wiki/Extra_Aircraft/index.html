Extra Aircraft (Extra Flugzeugproduktion- und Vertriebs- GmbH) is a German aircraft manufacturer based at Dinslaken/Hünxe airfield, specialising in aerobatic aeroplanes. It was founded in 1980 by Walter Extra, a German aerobatic competitor who began designing aircraft to improve on existing competition types.

Aircraft

The company's best-known models include the Extra 200, Extra 230, the Extra 300 series (including the two-seat 300L and single-seat 300S), and the high-performance Extra 330 family powered by Lycoming engines. The carbon-fibre Extra 330SC is widely used in unlimited aerobatic competition, and Extra aircraft have been flown by many Red Bull Air Race pilots. The company also produces the Extra EA-400 and EA-500 turboprop business aircraft.

Reputation

Extra aeroplanes are noted for their composite construction, high roll rates, and certification to demanding aerobatic load factors, and have won numerous world and national aerobatic championships.
