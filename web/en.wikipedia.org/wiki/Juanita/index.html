Juanita

Juanita is a Spanish feminine given name, the diminutive of Juana, the feminine form of Juan (John). Literally "little Juanita" in form but used as a full name in its own right, it has been widely used in Spanish-speaking countries and the United States.

Usage

The name gained currency in the English-speaking world partly through the popular 19th- and early 20th-century song "Juanita" ("Soft o'er the fountain..."), often described as the first American popular song written by a woman, Caroline Norton having composed the music in England. Juanita was a moderately popular name for girls in the United States in the early twentieth century.

Other uses

"Juanita" is also the popular name given to the remarkably preserved Inca mummy discovered on Mount Ampato in Peru in 1995 — see Juanita mummy. The word appears as the title of several films and songs in Spanish and English.
