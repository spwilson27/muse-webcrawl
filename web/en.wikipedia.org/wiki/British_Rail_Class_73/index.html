British Rail Class 73

British Rail Class 73
Type: electro-diesel locomotive
Built: 1962–1967
Number built: 49

The British Rail Class 73 is a British electro-diesel locomotive. It operates from a 750 V DC third rail where available, or from an onboard diesel engine of about 600 hp when away from the electrified network. The type was built for the Southern Region between 1962 and 1967.

Design and service

The locomotives combined electric traction equipment in a compact body able to work within the restricted Hastings line loading gauge. They worked passenger, parcels and freight services across the Southern Region and were famously used on boat trains and the Gatwick Express. Many were later operated by freight and charter companies, and some remain in use, including on Caledonian Sleeper empty-stock workings.

Variants

The 73/0 series were the original production batch, while the 73/1 and 73/2 sub-classes incorporated modifications including multiple-working equipment for push-pull operation.
