London Stadium

London Stadium (formerly and also known as the Olympic Stadium and officially as the Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park Stadium) is a multi-purpose outdoor stadium at Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park in the Stratford district of London, in the London Borough of Newham. It was originally built for the 2012 Summer Olympics and 2012 Summer Paralympics, serving as the athletics venue and as the site of their opening and closing ceremonies. Following the Games, it was renovated for multi-purpose use and now serves primarily as the home of West Ham United of the Premier League.

London Stadium
Location: Stratford, London E20
Opened: 2011
Owner: Greater London Authority (E20Stadium LLP)
Operator: London Stadium 185 / Vinci Stadium
Capacity: 62,500 (football); 80,000 (2012 Olympics)
Tenants: West Ham United (2016–present); UK Athletics
Surface: Grass (Desso GrassMaster)

History

Construction began in May 2008 on the site of former industrial land between the River Lea and City Mill River; the stadium was designed by Populous with an emphasis on a demountable upper tier, allowing capacity to be reduced after the Games. It hosted the athletics events and ceremonies of London 2012, including the "Super Saturday" of 4 August 2012. After the Games it hosted the 2013 Anniversary Games, matches of the 2015 Rugby World Cup, the 2017 World Championships in Athletics and World Para Athletics Championships, and concerts.

Conversion and West Ham

Conversion works — including a new roof, retractable seating, and corporate facilities — cost several hundred million pounds amid public controversy. West Ham United moved from the Boleyn Ground in 2016 under a 99-year lease, with UK Athletics retaining annual use for summer events. Major League Baseball's London Series between the New York Yankees and Boston Red Sox was also staged at the stadium in 2019.
