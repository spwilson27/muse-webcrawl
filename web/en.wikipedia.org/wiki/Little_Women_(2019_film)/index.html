Little Women (2019 film)

Little Women is a 2019 American coming-of-age period drama film written and directed by Greta Gerwig. It is the seventh film adaptation of Louisa May Alcott's 1868 novel, following the lives of the four March sisters in 1860s Massachusetts.

Plot

The film interweaves two timelines seven years apart. In 1868, Jo March works as a writer in New York while her sister Amy studies painting in Paris; in flashbacks to 1861, the sisters grow up in Concord during the Civil War, befriending their wealthy neighbour Laurie. The narrative follows Jo's struggle to publish her novel, Meg's marriage, Beth's illness, and Amy's eventual marriage to Laurie.

Reception

The film received widespread critical acclaim for Gerwig's direction, its nonlinear structure, performances and costume design. It was nominated for six Academy Awards, including Best Picture, and won Best Costume Design for Jacqueline Durran.
