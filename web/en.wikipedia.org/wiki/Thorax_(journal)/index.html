Thorax is a monthly peer-reviewed medical journal specialising in respiratory medicine. It is the official journal of the British Thoracic Society and is published by the BMJ Group.

Scope and history

Founded in 1946, the journal publishes research on all aspects of respiratory medicine and thoracic surgery, including asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), lung cancer, interstitial lung disease, cystic fibrosis, respiratory infections, sleep medicine, and critical care. It carries original research articles, reviews, guidelines, and correspondence.

Standing

Thorax is ranked among the leading international respiratory journals, alongside the American Journal of Respiratory and Critical Care Medicine and the European Respiratory Journal, and is widely cited in clinical guidelines on respiratory disease.
