Rule of law

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The rule of law is the principle that all people and institutions are subject to, and accountable to, law that is fairly applied and enforced; the principle of government by law rather than by arbitrary rule. It implies that no one is above the law — including officials and rulers — and that laws should be general, public, prospective, clear and stable, applied by independent courts.

History

The idea is ancient: Aristotle held that law should govern rather than any individual; Roman law and medieval thinkers developed related ideas. Clause 39 of Magna Carta (1215) promised that no free man would be punished except by the lawful judgment of his peers or the law of the land. The English jurist A. V. Dicey popularised the modern phrase in Introduction to the Study of the Law of the Constitution (1885), identifying three meanings: the absence of arbitrary power, equality before the law, and the constitution as the result of ordinary law developed by courts.

Elements

Formulations differ, but common elements include: laws that are publicly promulgated and equally enforced; an independent judiciary; access to justice; legal certainty and non-retroactivity in criminal law; protection of fundamental rights; and constraints on the exercise of official discretion. Procedural expressions include Due process and Habeas corpus, which requires that a detained person be brought before a court. "Thin" (formal) accounts focus on procedure and clarity, while "thick" accounts add substantive commitments to rights and democracy.

Measurement and significance

Indices such as the World Justice Project's Rule of Law Index attempt to measure adherence across countries, covering constraints on government powers, corruption, open government, fundamental rights, order and security, and civil and criminal justice. The rule of law is widely treated as a precondition of economic development and a core requirement for membership of bodies such as the European Union — contrasted with its opposite, rule by decree or rule of man.
