Sage Gateshead

Sage Gateshead
Location: Gateshead, Tyne and Wear, England
Opened: 17 December 2004
Architect: Foster and Partners
Capacity: 1,640 (Hall One), 450 (Hall Two)
Type: Concert hall / music centre

Sage Gateshead is a concert venue and centre for music education, located in Gateshead on the south bank of the River Tyne, in North East England. It opened on 17 December 2004 and is occupied by the Royal Northern Sinfonia. The centre takes its name from the software company Sage Group, which has been a major sponsor of the project.

Architecture

The building was designed by architects Foster and Partners and constructed by Laing O'Rourke. Its distinctive curved glass and steel shell shelters three separate performance spaces: Hall One, a 1,640-seat wood-panelled concert hall with adjustable acoustics; Hall Two, a flexible 450-capacity space; and the Northern Rock Foundation Hall, a rehearsal space for the orchestra. A concourse running the length of the building offers views across the Tyne to Newcastle.

Programming

Sage Gateshead hosts performances across classical, jazz, folk, rock and electronic music, and runs an extensive programme of music classes and workshops for all ages through its learning and participation department.
