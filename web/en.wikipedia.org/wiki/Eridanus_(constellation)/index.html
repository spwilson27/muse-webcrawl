Eridanus (constellation)

Eridanus
MeaningThe River (Po)
Brightest starAchernar (α Eri), mag 0.46
Area1,138 sq. deg. (6th largest)
Notable objectsEpsilon Eridani, Witch Head Nebula, Eridanus Supervoid

Eridanus is a constellation in the southern celestial hemisphere, one of the 48 listed by Ptolemy and among the 88 modern constellations. It is represented as a river, winding from near Orion southward, and is the sixth-largest constellation in the sky.

Stars

Its brightest star, Achernar ("end of the river"), is a hot blue star notably flattened by rapid rotation. Epsilon Eridani, only 10.5 light-years away, is a Sun-like star with a debris disk and was long a favourite candidate in searches for extraterrestrial intelligence. The star 40 Eridani (Keid) is the fictional home of the planet Vulcan in Star Trek.

Mythology

The Eridanus was identified with the river Po in Italy and with the myth of Phaethon, who fell into the river after losing control of the sun god's chariot; his sisters, the Heliades, became poplar trees weeping amber tears.

Deep-sky features

The constellation contains the Witch Head Nebula and the vast Eridanus Supervoid, associated with the CMB cold spot.
