Jagged Little Pill is a jukebox musical with music by Alanis Morissette and Glen Ballard, lyrics by Morissette, and a book by Diablo Cody. Inspired by Morissette's 1995 album Jagged Little Pill, the musical tells an original story about the Healy family, a seemingly picture-perfect suburban Connecticut household grappling with addiction, gender and sexual identity, race, and sexual assault.

Productions

The musical premiered at the American Repertory Theater in Cambridge, Massachusetts, in May 2018, directed by Diane Paulus with choreography by Sidi Larbi Cherkaoui. It transferred to Broadway, opening at the Broadhurst Theatre on December 5, 2019. The Broadway production, like others, was suspended in March 2020 due to the COVID-19 pandemic; it reopened in October 2021 and closed in December 2021, again amid an Omicron-variant outbreak.

Reception and awards

The production received generally positive reviews for its energy and Cody's topical book. It led the 2020 Tony Award nominations with 15, winning two: Best Book of a Musical for Cody and Best Featured Actress in a Musical for Lauren Patten ("You Oughta Know"). The original Broadway cast recording won the Grammy Award for Best Musical Theater Album. The show also attracted controversy around the gender identity of the character Jo, which some audiences felt had been erased in the transfer from Cambridge to Broadway; the producers and creatives issued apologies and commitments regarding casting and representation as the production reopened.

Subsequent productions

A North American tour launched in 2022, and an Australian production opened in Sydney in December 2021 before touring.
