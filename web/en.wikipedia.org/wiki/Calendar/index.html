A calendar is a system of organising days for social, religious, administrative and commercial purposes, based on astronomical cycles — chiefly the solar year and, historically, lunar months.

Systems

The Gregorian calendar, promulgated by Pope Gregory XIII in 1582 to correct the Julian calendar's drift, is now the world's civil standard, with leap years keeping it aligned to the seasons. Other calendars persist in religious and cultural life: Islamic (lunar), Hebrew, Chinese, Hindu and more. Days of the year anchor this crawl's seed page 'On this day' feature and the histories from Byzantium to the French Revolution, whose republic briefly imposed its own revolutionary calendar.
