House of Windsor

House of Windsor
Country: United Kingdom and Commonwealth realms
Founded: 17 July 1917
Founder: George V
Current head: Charles III
Previous name: House of Saxe-Coburg and Gotha

The House of Windsor is the reigning house of the United Kingdom and the other Commonwealth realms. It was founded on 17 July 1917, when King George V changed the name of the British royal house from the German Saxe-Coburg and Gotha to the English Windsor, owing to anti-German sentiment during the First World War.

Monarchs

Windsor monarchs are George V, Edward VIII (who abdicated in 1936), George VI, Elizabeth II — Britain's longest-reigning monarch (1952–2022) — and Charles III, who acceded on 8 September 2022.

Name and surname

In 1960 Elizabeth II and Prince Philip decreed that their male-line descendants without royal titles would bear the surname Mountbatten-Windsor. The house name Windsor was retained despite speculation it might change on Charles III's accession.
