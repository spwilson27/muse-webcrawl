Carbon/Silicon was a British rock band formed in London in 2002 by Mick Jones, formerly of the Clash and Big Audio Dynamite, and Tony James, formerly of Generation X and Sigue Sigue Sputnik. The two had first played together in the 1970s in the proto-punk band London SS.

History

The band's name reflects its philosophy: "carbon" for old, organic rock-and-roll values and "silicon" for the new digital technology. True to that ethos, Carbon/Silicon embraced internet distribution from the start, making early recordings freely available for download and encouraging fans to record and share live performances — a notably open approach for musicians of their generation.

The band released a series of albums beginning with A.T.O.M. (2006), followed by Western Front (2006), The Last Post (2007) — their first conventional commercial release — The Carbon Bubble (2009) and the EP The News (2010). Song themes combined rock and roll celebration with commentary on war, technology and modern media.
