Tony James (born 12 April 1953) is an English musician who first came to prominence as the bass guitarist and co-founder of the punk band Generation X, alongside Billy Idol. A Cambridge mathematics graduate, he was a central figure in the early London punk scene around the Bromley Contingent.

Career

After a brief spell in the proto-punk band London SS — whose ranks also passed through future members of The Clash and The Damned — James formed Generation X in 1976; the band scored UK hits including "Your Generation" and "King Rocker". In 1981 he conceived the flamboyant, media-savvy Sigue Sigue Sputnik, whose debut single "Love Missile F1-11" (produced by Giorgio Moroder) was a UK hit in 1986 amid a blaze of self-generated hype. James later joined The Sisters of Mercy for the Vision Thing album and tour (1990–91), and in 2002 reunited with his London SS contemporary Mick Jones of The Clash to form the internet-focused rock duo Carbon/Silicon, which released its music free online.
