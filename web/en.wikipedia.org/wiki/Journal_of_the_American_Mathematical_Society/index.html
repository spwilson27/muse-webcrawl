Journal of the American Mathematical Society

The Journal of the American Mathematical Society (often abbreviated JAMS) is a quarterly peer-reviewed mathematical journal published by the American Mathematical Society. Founded in 1988, it is regarded as one of the most prestigious journals in pure mathematics.

Scope and standing

JAMS publishes research articles in all areas of pure mathematics, with an emphasis on papers of broad interest presenting major advances. It was established to give the American Mathematical Society a flagship journal comparable to the leading European titles, alongside its older journals, the Transactions and Proceedings of the AMS and the Bulletin, which carries surveys and research announcements.

Notable papers

Among landmark results published in JAMS is Grigori Perelman's colleagues' exposition work associated with the Poincaré conjecture era, and the journal has carried many influential papers in geometry, number theory, representation theory and dynamical systems. Its impact factor consistently places it among the top mathematics journals worldwide.

Access

Articles are available through the AMS website; following the society's policies, papers become freely accessible on the journal site after a delay of several years, and preprints of most published papers are available on the arXiv.
