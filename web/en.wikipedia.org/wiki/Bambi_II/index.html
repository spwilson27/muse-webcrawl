Bambi II (also known as Bambi and the Great Prince of the Forest) is a 2006 American animated drama film produced by DisneyToon Studios. It is a midquel to Disney's 1942 film Bambi: the story takes place between the death of Bambi's mother and Bambi's adulthood, a segment not covered by the original film. Patrick Stewart voices the Great Prince, and Alexander Gould voices the young Bambi.

Plot

After the death of his mother, the young fawn Bambi is left in the care of his distant father, the Great Prince, who is unsure how to raise a fawn and considers asking Friend Owl to find a doe to take him in. Over the winter and spring, father and son gradually bond as Bambi, aided by Thumper and Flower, tries to prove himself brave. When Bambi is endangered by Man's hunting dogs and apparently killed, the Great Prince finally shows open affection, and he decides to raise Bambi himself.

Production and release

The film was produced at DisneyToon Studios in Australia with animation that deliberately imitated the painted backgrounds of the 1942 original, using digital ink and paint. Although released direct-to-video in the United States, it received theatrical releases in a number of other countries, including Argentina, Spain and the United Kingdom. It was among the last direct-to-video Disney sequels, before the studio shifted away from the line.

Reception

The film received generally favourable reviews for a direct-to-video sequel, with praise for its animation, Stewart's voice performance and its emotional treatment of the father–son relationship; it won the Annie Award for Best Home Entertainment Production.
