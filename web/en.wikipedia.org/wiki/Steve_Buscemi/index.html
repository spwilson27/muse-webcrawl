Steve Buscemi

Steven Vincent Buscemi (born December 13, 1957) is an American actor, director and producer. A regular collaborator of the Coen brothers and a fixture of independent cinema, he is known for roles in Reservoir Dogs (1992), Fargo (1996), Con Air (1997), The Big Lebowski (1998), and Ghost World (2001), and for starring as Enoch "Nucky" Thompson in the HBO series Boardwalk Empire (2010–2014), for which he won a Golden Globe Award.

Early life

Buscemi was born in Brooklyn, New York City, the son of Dorothy (née Wilson) and John Buscemi, a sanitation worker and Korean War veteran. He graduated from Valley Stream Central High School in 1975 and later, from 1980 to 1984, served as a New York City firefighter with Engine Company 55 in Little Italy. After the September 11 attacks, he rejoined his former company as a volunteer, working twelve-hour shifts for several days searching for survivors.

Career

Buscemi's early film roles included Parting Glances (1986) and Jim Jarmusch's Mystery Train (1989). His breakthrough came as Mr. Pink in Quentin Tarantino's Reservoir Dogs (1992), followed by performances for the Coen brothers in Miller's Crossing (1990), Barton Fink (1991), The Hudsucker Proxy (1994), Fargo (1996) and The Big Lebowski (1998). He voiced Randall Boggs in Pixar's Monsters, Inc. (2001) and played recurring roles on The Sopranos — for which he also directed episodes — and 30 Rock. His five-season run as the lead of Boardwalk Empire earned him a Golden Globe and two Screen Actors Guild Awards. As a director, his films include Trees Lounge (1996), Animal Factory (2000), Lonesome Jim (2005) and Interview (2007).
