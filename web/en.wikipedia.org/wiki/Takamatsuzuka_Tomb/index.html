Takamatsuzuka Tomb

The Takamatsuzuka Tomb is an ancient kofun burial mound in Asuka, Nara Prefecture, Japan, dating to the late 7th or early 8th century. It was discovered in 1972.

Murals

The tomb is famous for its vivid wall paintings, including groups of courtiers in colorful dress and the Four Deities of Chinese cosmology — the Azure Dragon, White Tiger, Vermilion Bird, and Black Tortoise — beneath a ceiling painted with stars. The murals are designated National Treasures of Japan.
