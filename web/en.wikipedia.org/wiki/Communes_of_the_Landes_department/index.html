Communes of the Landes department

The Landes department in the Nouvelle-Aquitaine region of France.

The following is a list of the communes of the Landes department of France. Landes, in the Nouvelle-Aquitaine region of southwestern France, is one of the largest French departments by area and is covered to a great extent by the Landes forest, one of the largest man-made forests in Western Europe. The department's prefecture is Mont-de-Marsan, and its other major town is Dax, a well-known spa town. The department contains roughly 330 communes.

Organisation

The communes of Landes are grouped into intercommunal bodies (communautés de communes and the two communautés d'agglomération of Mont-de-Marsan and Grand Dax) and into the two arrondissements of Mont-de-Marsan and Dax. INSEE codes for Landes communes begin with 40, the departmental number.
