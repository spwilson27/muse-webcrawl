Numbers (spreadsheet)

Numbers is a spreadsheet application developed by Apple Inc. as part of the iWork productivity suite, alongside Keynote and Pages. It is distributed for macOS, iOS and iPadOS, and is also available as a web application through iCloud. Numbers was first released in August 2007.

Approach and features

Unlike traditional spreadsheets that present a single vast grid, Numbers uses a freeform canvas on which multiple independent tables, charts, images and text boxes can be placed; each table functions as its own spreadsheet with its own rows, columns and formulas. Formulas can reference cells by named rows and columns, and the application includes hundreds of functions, data categories, interactive charts, and templates aimed at personal and small-business tasks such as budgets, invoices and checklists. Files can be exported to Microsoft Excel and CSV formats, and real-time collaboration is supported through iCloud.

Reception

Numbers is praised for its presentation-quality output and ease of use for casual users, but has historically been considered less powerful than Microsoft Excel for very large datasets and advanced analysis, lacking features such as pivot tables for many years (categories and smart categorisation partially address this). It is provided free with Apple devices.
