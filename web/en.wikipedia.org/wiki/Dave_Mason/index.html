David Thomas Mason (born 10 May 1946) is an English singer-songwriter and guitarist from Worcester, who first found fame with the rock band Traffic. His song "Feelin' Alright?", written for Traffic in 1968, became a standard after Joe Cocker's hit version, and he wrote the Traffic classic "Hole in My Shoe".

Career

Mason co-founded Traffic with Steve Winwood, Jim Capaldi and Chris Wood in 1967, leaving and rejoining the band several times. As a session musician he played on Jimi Hendrix's Electric Ladyland (acoustic guitar on "All Along the Watchtower"), the Rolling Stones' Beggars Banquet and George Harrison's All Things Must Pass. His debut solo album Alone Together (1970) was critically acclaimed, and his 1977 album Let It Flow produced his biggest solo hit, "We Just Disagree". In the mid-1990s he briefly joined Fleetwood Mac. Mason was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2004 as a member of Traffic.
