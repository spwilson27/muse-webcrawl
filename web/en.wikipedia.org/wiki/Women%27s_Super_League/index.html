Women's Super League (WSL)
Founded: 2010 (first season 2011)
Country: England
Teams: 12

The Women's Super League (WSL) is the highest league of women's football in England. Established in 2010, it is run by the Football Association and currently features twelve fully professional teams. The league replaced the FA Women's Premier League National Division as the top tier of the English women's game.

History

The WSL began play in April 2011 as a summer league before switching to a winter calendar in 2017–18. Arsenal were the dominant early champions; Chelsea became the leading force in the late 2010s and 2020s, winning multiple consecutive titles, while Manchester City have also won the league. The second tier is the Women's Championship.

Growth

Since England's victory at UEFA Women's Euro 2022, attendances and broadcast deals have grown rapidly, with matches at major stadiums drawing record crowds. In 2024, governance of the league began transferring from the FA to an independent company owned by the clubs.
