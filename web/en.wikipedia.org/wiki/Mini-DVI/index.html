Mini-DVI

Mini-DVI is a miniature video connector used on certain Apple computers in the 2000s as a compact alternative to the standard Digital Visual Interface (DVI) connector. It carries the same signals as DVI and, with adapters, can output DVI, VGA, composite or S-Video.

Usage

Mini-DVI appeared on the 12-inch PowerBook G4, Intel-based iMacs, the MacBook, Mac mini and the MacBook Air (first generation interfaces aside), typically requiring adapters to connect external displays or projectors. It is physically smaller than Mini-VGA, which it replaced on some models.

Replacement

From 2008–2009 Apple transitioned to Mini DisplayPort, and Mini-DVI disappeared from new machines.
