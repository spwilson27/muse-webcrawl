Independent News & Media

Independent News & Media plc (INM) was an Irish media organisation headquartered in Dublin. For much of its history it was Ireland's largest newspaper group, publishing the Irish Independent, the Sunday Independent, the Evening Herald, the Sunday World and a number of regional titles. The company adopted the trading name Mediahuis Ireland after its acquisition by the Belgian group Mediahuis in 2019.

History

The company traces its origins to Independent Newspapers, founded by William Martin Murphy in 1904 and publisher of the Irish Independent from 1905. Control passed to Tony O'Reilly in 1973, and under his leadership the group expanded internationally, acquiring stakes and titles in the United Kingdom, South Africa, Australia and New Zealand, including The Independent of London (acquired outright in 1998) and a controlling interest in APN News & Media.

After years of heavy borrowing, the group underwent major restructuring. In 2009 it sold The Independent and Independent on Sunday to Alexander Lebedev. Irish businessman Denis O'Brien became the largest shareholder in the early 2010s, and in 2019 Mediahuis acquired the group for approximately €145.6 million, taking it private and delisting it from the Irish and London stock exchanges. The business was subsequently rebranded Mediahuis Ireland.

Operations

INM published Ireland's best-selling national newspapers and operated the news website Independent.ie. Its regional titles included the Kerryman, the Wicklow People and the Belfast Telegraph group in Northern Ireland (sold to Mediahuis as part of the deal). The group also held a distribution business and a stake in radio.
