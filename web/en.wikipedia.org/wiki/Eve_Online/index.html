Eve Online (stylised EVE Online) is a space-based, persistent-world massively multiplayer online role-playing game (MMORPG) developed and published by the Icelandic company CCP Games. Players pilot customisable spaceships across a galaxy of more than 7,800 star systems, most of which are connected by a series of stargates.

Gameplay

Players create characters and explore, trade, mine, manufacture, and fight in a single shared virtual universe known as New Eden. Unlike most MMORPGs, all players inhabit the same shard, Tranquility, making Eve famous for enormous player-versus-player battles involving thousands of participants. The game's economy is almost entirely player-driven, and corporations and alliances of players contest sovereignty over lawless "null security" space.

History

Eve Online was released in May 2003 and has received numerous free expansions. In 2016 CCP adopted a hybrid free-to-play model, dividing accounts into limited "Alpha" clones and subscription-based "Omega" clones. Notable events in the game's history include the "Bloodbath of B-R5RB" in 2014, one of the largest virtual battles ever fought, which destroyed in-game assets valued at hundreds of thousands of real-world dollars.

Reception

The game has been praised for its depth, player-driven narrative, and economic simulation, while criticised for its steep learning curve. Its player stories have been exhibited at the Museum of Modern Art in New York.
