Lake Gruyère

Lake Gruyère (French: Lac de la Gruyère) is an artificial lake in the canton of Fribourg, Switzerland, formed by the Rossens Dam on the Saane (Sarine) River. It was filled in 1948.

Description

The lake is about 13.5 km long and is one of the larger reservoirs in Switzerland. The island of Île d'Ogoz, with the ruins of a medieval castle and chapel, becomes a peninsula at low water and is a popular walking destination.

Hydropower

The Rossens Dam provides hydroelectric power for the canton of Fribourg. The viaduct of the A12 motorway crosses the lake near its southern end.
