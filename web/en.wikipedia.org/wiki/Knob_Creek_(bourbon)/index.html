Knob Creek is a brand of Kentucky straight bourbon whiskey produced by Beam Suntory at the Jim Beam distillery in Clermont, Kentucky. It is one of the four brands marketed as part of Jim Beam's "Small Batch Bourbon Collection", alongside Booker's, Baker's and Basil Hayden's.

History

The brand was introduced in 1992 by master distiller Booker Noe, who named it after Knob Creek, a water source in Kentucky said to have been near the childhood home of Abraham Lincoln. Noe designed the whiskey to evoke the style of pre-Prohibition bourbon, with a full-bodied flavour, higher proof of 100 (50% alcohol by volume) and, originally, a nine-year age statement.

Expressions

In addition to the flagship bourbon, the Knob Creek line includes a straight rye whiskey, single barrel bourbon and rye expressions bottled at higher proofs, cask-strength releases, and age-stated bottlings such as 12- and 15-year-old editions. The nine-year age statement, dropped in 2016 amid supply pressures, was restored in 2019–2020 as stocks recovered.

Production

Knob Creek is distilled from a traditional bourbon mash of corn, rye and malted barley, aged in deeply charred new American oak barrels in Kentucky warehouses, and bottled at the Clermont distillery.
