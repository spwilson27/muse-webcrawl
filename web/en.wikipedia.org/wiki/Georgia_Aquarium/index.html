Georgia Aquarium is a public aquarium in Atlanta, Georgia, United States. It opened in 2005, funded largely by a $250 million gift from Home Depot co-founder Bernie Marcus, and was for many years the largest aquarium in the world. It anchors the northern edge of Centennial Olympic Park.

Exhibits

The aquarium's centrepiece, Ocean Voyager, holds 6.3 million US gallons of water and is one of the largest single aquarium exhibits in the world, home to whale sharks — the aquarium is one of very few outside Asia to keep them — as well as manta rays, groupers and thousands of other fish, viewed through a 100-foot underwater tunnel and a vast viewing window. Other galleries include Cold Water Quest (beluga whales, sea otters, African penguins), Tropical Diver (living coral reefs), River Scout (freshwater animals), and a dolphin presentation and sea lion gallery.

Conservation and research

Georgia Aquarium conducts research and conservation work on whale sharks, coral restoration, and marine animal health, and operates a veterinary services and conservation centre. It welcomes more than two million visitors a year.
