SCORE International (Southern California Off Road Enthusiasts) is an off-road racing sanctioning body in the United States, best known for organising desert races on Mexico's Baja California peninsula. SCORE organises the SCORE World Desert Championship, which includes the famous Baja 1000.

History

SCORE was founded in 1973 by the racer and promoter Mickey Thompson, who took over the organisation of the Baja 1000 from NORRA. Long-time owner Sal Fish purchased SCORE in the mid-1980s and ran the organisation for decades, expanding its profile internationally. In 2012 Fish sold SCORE to Roger Norman, under whom the series continued with races including the Baja 1000, Baja 500, San Felipe 250 and Baja 400.

Races and classes

SCORE events feature a wide range of classes for cars, trucks, motorcycles, ATVs and UTVs. The premier class is the SCORE Trophy Truck, purpose-built unlimited desert racing trucks, alongside Class 1 buggies and numerous production-based classes. Races are point-to-point or loop events held over punishing desert terrain, often running through the night.
