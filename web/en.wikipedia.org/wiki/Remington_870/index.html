Remington 870

The Remington Model 870 is a pump-action shotgun manufactured by Remington Arms. Introduced in 1950, it became one of the best-selling shotguns of all time, with more than 11 million produced. It is widely used by sportsmen, police forces and military units around the world.

The 870 features a bottom-loading, side-ejecting receiver milled from a solid billet of steel, twin action bars, and an internal hammer. Variants include the Wingmaster, Express, Marine Magnum and Police models, in gauges including 12, 20, 28 and .410 bore.
