Jaden Christopher Syre Smith (born July 8, 1998) is an American rapper, singer, and actor. The son of actors Will Smith and Jada Pinkett Smith, he made his film debut alongside his father in The Pursuit of Happyness (2006) and later starred in The Karate Kid (2010) and After Earth (2013).

Career

Smith's performance as Christopher Gardner Jr. in The Pursuit of Happyness earned him critical praise and a Breakthrough Performance award at the 2007 MTV Movie Awards. He headlined the remake of The Karate Kid (2010) with Jackie Chan, one of the highest-grossing films of his career, and sang on Justin Bieber's hit single "Never Say Never" for the film. He again co-starred with his father in the science-fiction film After Earth (2013), which was a critical and commercial disappointment.

As a musician, Smith released the mixtape The Cool Cafe (2012) and his debut studio album Syre in 2017, followed by Erys (2019) and CTV3: Cool Tape Vol. 3 (2020). His single "Icon" became his signature song. He also appeared in the Netflix series The Get Down (2016–2017) and voiced a character in Neo Yokio (2017).

Other ventures

Smith co-founded the fashion label MSFTSrep and the water company Just Water, an eco-friendly bottled water venture launched with his father. He has been noted for his gender-fluid fashion sense and philanthropy, including work on water filtration systems for Flint, Michigan.
