Old Bushmills Distillery

The Old Bushmills Distillery is an Irish whiskey distillery in Bushmills, County Antrim, Northern Ireland, owned by Proximo Spirits. The company claims a long heritage: a licence to distil in the area was granted in 1608, making Bushmills one of the oldest licensed whiskey distilleries in the world.

History

The Old Bushmills Distillery Company was formally registered in 1784, taking its name from the mills on the River Bush. The distillery survived a fire in 1885 and the challenges of Prohibition in the United States, its largest export market. In modern times it passed through the ownership of Irish Distillers and Diageo before being acquired by the Beckmann family's Proximo Spirits in 2014.

Whiskeys

Bushmills produces a range of single malts (including 10-, 16-, and 21-year-old expressions) and blends such as Original and Black Bush. Its whiskeys are triple-distilled from unpeated malted barley and are known for a smooth, honeyed style.

Visitor centre

The distillery is a popular tourist attraction, drawing visitors touring the nearby Giant's Causeway.
