Kevin De Bruyne (born 28 June 1991) is a Belgian professional footballer who plays as a midfielder. Widely regarded as one of the best playmakers of his generation, he is celebrated for his passing range, vision, crossing and long-range shooting with either foot.

Club career

De Bruyne began his career at Genk, winning the Belgian Pro League in 2010–11, before joining Chelsea in 2012. After a successful loan at Werder Bremen, he returned to Germany with VfL Wolfsburg, where he was named Bundesliga Player of the Year. In 2015 he signed for Manchester City for a club-record fee, becoming the creative centre of Pep Guardiola's side. With City he won multiple Premier League titles, FA Cups, League Cups and the UEFA Champions League in 2023 as part of a continental treble. In 2025 he left City at the end of his contract and joined Napoli in Italy's Serie A.

International career

De Bruyne has earned over 100 caps for Belgium and was a key member of the "golden generation" that finished third at the 2018 FIFA World Cup, Belgium's best World Cup result.

Honours and awards

He has twice been named PFA Players' Player of the Year and has repeatedly been selected in the UEFA Team of the Year and the FIFA FIFPRO World 11.
