Mumbai

[Image: The Gateway of India and Mumbai harbour]
The Gateway of India and Mumbai harbour

Mumbai, formerly Bombay, is the capital city of the Indian state of Maharashtra and the most populous city in India, with about 12.5 million residents in the city and over 20 million in the metropolitan region. It lies on the west coast of India on a group of islands reclaimed into a single landmass around a natural deep-water harbour.

Mumbai is India's financial, commercial and entertainment capital: home to the Bombay Stock Exchange, the Reserve Bank of India, the Hindi film industry (Bollywood), the Gateway of India arch (1924), Victorian Gothic and Art Deco ensembles listed by UNESCO, the Bandra-Worli Sea Link, and Dharavi, one of Asia's largest informal settlements. The city was attacked in November 2008 by terrorists who killed 166 people across sites including the Taj Mahal Palace Hotel and Chhatrapati Shivaji railway station.
