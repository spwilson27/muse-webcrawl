Weltklasse Zürich

Weltklasse Zürich is an annual track and field meeting held at the Letzigrund Stadium in Zürich, Switzerland. Founded in 1928, it is one of the most prestigious athletics meetings in the world and forms part of the Diamond League. The meeting is regularly referred to as the "one-day Olympics" because of the depth and quality of its fields.

History

The first meeting was staged on 12 August 1928. After the Second World War the event grew in stature, and from the 1950s onwards it became famous for world records and high-quality middle-distance racing. Numerous world records have been set at Letzigrund, including Armin Hary's 10.0-second 100 metres in 1960 and Sebastian Coe's mile world record in 1981. The stadium was rebuilt and reopened in 2007.

Diamond League

Since 2010 Weltklasse Zürich has been one of the finals venues of the Diamond League, hosting the season-ending event in alternating years and, in 2022, staging the final across two evenings, including a street athletics event at Sechseläutenplatz.
