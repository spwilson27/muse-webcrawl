List of Narcissus species

Narcissus is a genus of predominantly spring-flowering perennial plants of the amaryllis family, Amaryllidaceae. Various common names including daffodil, narcissus and jonquil are used to describe all or some members of the genus. The number of defined species ranges from about 36 to over 100 depending on the classification. The following is a selection of accepted species, organized by section in the widely used classification of John Blanchard and the Royal Horticultural Society.
