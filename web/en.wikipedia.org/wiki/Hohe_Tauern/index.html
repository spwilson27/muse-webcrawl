Hohe Tauern

The Hohe Tauern (Italian: Alti Tauri) is a major mountain range of the Central Eastern Alps in Austria, stretching about 130 km along the main Alpine divide through the states of Salzburg, Carinthia and Tyrol. It contains Austria's highest peak, the Großglocker (3,798 m).

Geography

The range is heavily glaciated and includes the Pasterze, Austria's largest glacier. Much of the range is protected within Hohe Tauern National Park, established in 1981 and one of the largest protected areas in Central Europe, home to ibex, chamois, marmots and golden eagles.

Crossings and tourism

Historic passes such as the Felbertauern and the Großglocker High Alpine Road cross the range. The area is a major centre for mountaineering, skiing and hiking.
