Aetna

Aetna Inc. is an American managed health care company that sells traditional and consumer-directed health care insurance and related services, such as medical, pharmaceutical, dental, behavioral health, long-term care, and disability plans. Founded in Hartford, Connecticut, in 1853 as the Aetna Life Insurance Company, it took its name from Mount Etna in Sicily.

History

Aetna grew through the twentieth century into one of the largest US insurers, at times also writing property and casualty lines, which it sold to Travelers in 1996 to concentrate on health benefits. In 2000 it spun off its financial services arm to ING.

Acquisition by CVS

In December 2017 CVS Health announced it would acquire Aetna for about $69 billion, combining a pharmacy chain and pharmacy benefit manager with a major insurer; the deal closed in November 2018 after regulatory review, and Aetna has since operated as part of CVS Health's health benefits segment, serving tens of millions of members.
