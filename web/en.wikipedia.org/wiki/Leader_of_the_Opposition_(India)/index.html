Leader of the Opposition (India)

The Leader of the Opposition in India is the politician who leads the official opposition in either House of the Parliament of India. The position is held in the Lok Sabha and separately in the Rajya Sabha by the leader of the largest party not in government.

Role and recognition

Recognition requires the opposition party to hold at least one-tenth of the seats in the House. The Leader of the Opposition has the status of a Cabinet Minister and sits on selection committees for bodies such as the Central Vigilance Commission, Central Bureau of Investigation director and Lokpal.

History

There was no recognised Leader of the Opposition until 1969. The post was vacant in the Lok Sabha from 2014 to 2024 because no opposition party met the threshold; in 2024 Rahul Gandhi of the Indian National Congress became Leader of the Opposition in the Lok Sabha.
