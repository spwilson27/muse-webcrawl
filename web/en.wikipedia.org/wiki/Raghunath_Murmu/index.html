Died1 February 1982 (aged 76)

Pandit Raghunath Murmu (5 May 1905 – 1 February 1982) was an Indian writer, linguist and playwright from Odisha, best known as the inventor of the Ol Chiki script, the writing system used for the Santali language.

Life

Murmu was born in the village of Dandbus in Mayurbhanj district, Odisha, into a Santal family, and worked as a teacher. According to Santal tradition, the idea for a script suited to the sounds of Santali came to him in 1925 while meditating on a hill; the script he devised, Ol Chiki ("writing of the script"), has 30 letters whose shapes are inspired by natural forms and everyday objects.

Works and legacy

Murmu wrote textbooks, grammars, poetry, and plays in Santali using Ol Chiki, including the play Bidu Chandan, and founded organisations to promote Santali language and culture. His birth anniversary, called Guru Gomke Janam Maha, is celebrated by Santal communities. Santali, written in Ol Chiki among other scripts, was added to the Eighth Schedule of the Indian Constitution in 2003, a milestone widely credited to the script and movement Murmu began.
