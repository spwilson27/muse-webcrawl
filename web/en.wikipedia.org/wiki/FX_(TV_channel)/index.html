FX is an American pay television channel owned by FX Networks, LLC, a subsidiary of the Walt Disney Television unit of the Walt Disney Company. Launched on June 1, 1994, the channel originally broadcast from a studio apartment in New York City with an emphasis on live, interactive programming, before relaunching in 1997 as "Fox Gone Cable" with reruns of Fox series. It subsequently reinvented itself as a home for edgy, adult-oriented original drama and comedy.

History

FX's breakthrough into prestige television came with The Shield (2002–2008), followed by Nip/Tuck, Rescue Me, and Damages. Under network president John Landgraf, FX became known for auteur-driven limited series and anthology formats, including Sons of Anarchy, Justified, The Americans, Fargo, American Horror Story, Atlanta, and Shōgun, which in 2024 won a record haul of Emmy Awards for a single season of a drama series. Disney acquired FX as part of its 2019 purchase of 21st Century Fox assets.

Programming and brand

FX's slogan "Fearless" (from 2017) reflects a brand built on risk-taking original series aimed at adult audiences. Since 2020, new FX originals have also premiered on the "FX on Hulu" hub within the Hulu streaming service. Sister channel FXX, launched in 2013, carries much of the network's comedy output, including It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia and the animated Archer.
