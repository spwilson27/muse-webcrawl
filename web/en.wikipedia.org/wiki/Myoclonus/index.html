Myoclonus

Myoclonus is a brief, involuntary, irregular twitching of muscles. It involves sudden muscle contractions (positive myoclonus) or brief lapses of contraction (negative myoclonus). The most common form, hiccups, and hypnic jerks on falling asleep, occur in healthy people.

Pathological myoclonus can occur in epilepsy, metabolic disorders, neurodegenerative diseases, infections, and as a reaction to medication. Classification is by cause, distribution, and physiological origin (cortical, subcortical, spinal, peripheral).

Treatment

Treatment addresses the underlying cause; symptomatic therapy may include clonazepam, levetiracetam, piracetam, or sodium valproate, often in combination.
