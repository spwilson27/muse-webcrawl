Woodwind instrument

A woodwind instrument is a musical instrument in which sound is produced by air vibrating inside a tube, usually by blowing across an edge or through a reed. Examples include the flute, oboe, clarinet, bassoon, and saxophone.

Classification: Aerophones
Examples: Flute, oboe, clarinet, bassoon, saxophone

Types

Flutes produce sound at an edge tone; reed instruments use single reeds (clarinet, saxophone) or double reeds (oboe, bassoon). Despite the name, modern woodwinds may be made of metal or plastic — classification follows sound production, not material.

In the orchestra

The woodwind section sits centrally in the orchestra, providing colour and agility. See Flute, Clarinet, Oboe, Bassoon.
