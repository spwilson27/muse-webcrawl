Hassan Nasrallah

Hassan Nasrallah Born: 31 August 1960, Beirut, Lebanon
Died: 27 September 2024, Beirut, Lebanon
Office: Secretary-General of Hezbollah, 1992–2024

Hassan Nasrallah (31 August 1960 – 27 September 2024) was a Lebanese Shia cleric and politician who served as the third secretary-general of Hezbollah from 1992 until his assassination by Israel in 2024. Under his leadership, Hezbollah grew from a militia into a dominant political and military force in Lebanon, widely designated a terrorist organisation by Western governments.

Nasrallah led Hezbollah for more than three decades.

Early life and rise

Born in the Beirut suburb of Bourj Hammoud, Nasrallah studied at the Shia seminary in Tyre and later in Baalbek and Najaf, Iraq, before being expelled during Saddam Hussein's crackdown on Shia activists. He joined Hezbollah after Israel's 1982 invasion of Lebanon and rose through its ranks. After his predecessor Abbas al-Musawi was killed by an Israeli airstrike in 1992, Nasrallah, then 31, was elected secretary-general.

Leadership

Nasrallah led Hezbollah through the end of the Israeli occupation of southern Lebanon in 2000, the 2006 Lebanon War, and the group's intervention in the Syrian civil war on the side of Bashar al-Assad. He built close ties with Iran, and Hezbollah developed an extensive social-welfare network alongside its armed wing. Known for lengthy televised speeches, he lived largely in hiding after 2006. On 27 September 2024, an Israeli airstrike on Hezbollah's headquarters in the Dahieh suburb of Beirut killed him; he was succeeded by Naim Qassem.
