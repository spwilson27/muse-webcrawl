It's a Small World

It's a Small World (stylised "it's a small world") is a water-based dark ride at Disneyland and other Disney theme parks around the world. Created for the 1964 New York World's Fair, it features hundreds of Audio-Animatronic children in national dress singing the song "It's a Small World (After All)", written by Robert B. and Richard M. Sherman.

DesignerWED Enterprises; Mary Blair (art direction)
Opened1964 (World's Fair); 1966 (Disneyland)
TypeBoat dark ride
Sponsor (original)UNICEF / Pepsi-Cola

History

Walt Disney commissioned the attraction for the UNICEF pavilion at the 1964–65 New York World's Fair. Mary Blair's colourful, flat graphic style defined its look, while costumer Alice Davis designed the dolls' costumes. After the fair, the ride was moved to Disneyland in California.

The attraction

Boats carry guests through scenes representing Europe, Asia, Africa, the Americas, and the South Pacific, ending in a grand finale room where all the dolls sing together in white costumes. Versions now exist at Magic Kingdom, Tokyo Disneyland, Disneyland Paris, and Hong Kong Disneyland.
