Chaos;Child

Chaos;Child (Japanese: カオスチャイルド, Hepburn: Kaosu Chairudo) is a Japanese visual novel video game developed by 5pb. and Nitroplus. It is the fourth main entry in the Science Adventure series, following Chaos;Head, Steins;Gate and Robotics;Notes, and is set in Shibuya, Tokyo, in 2015, six years after the events of Chaos;Head.

The story follows Takuru Miyashiro, a high school student and president of his school's newspaper club, who investigates a series of bizarre murders known as the "Return of the New Generation Madness" killings, which echo a string of murders from six years earlier. Players use the "delusion trigger" system, choosing whether Takuru experiences positive (pleasant) or negative (disturbing) delusions at key moments, which branches the story toward different routes and endings.

Gameplay

Chaos;Child is a visual novel in which the player reads text accompanied by character sprites and backgrounds, making occasional choices. Its signature mechanic is the delusion trigger: at certain points the player may push the story toward a positive delusion, a negative delusion, or remain in reality. Combined with a "mapping trigger" system in which the player organizes evidence on a board, these choices determine which character route and ending the player reaches, including the game's true ending.

Plot

In 2015, Shibuya is shaken by a series of gruesome, theatrical murders occurring on the same dates as the "New Generation Madness" killings of 2009. Takuru Miyashiro, an orphan raised in a care facility after the Shibuya earthquake, pursues the story for his newspaper club alongside his foster sister Nono Kurusu and friends. As the investigation deepens, Takuru uncovers connections to psychic powers known as "Gigalomania", the shadowy Committee of 300, and his own buried past.

Development and release

The game was developed by 5pb. in collaboration with Nitroplus, with Chiyomaru Shikura as executive producer and Tatsuya Matsubara as producer. Character designs were by sasakimikako, and the scenario was written by Eiji Umehara and others. It was first released for Xbox One in Japan in June 2015, with PlayStation ports following in December 2015. An English localization by PQube was released in North America and Europe in October 2017. A Nintendo Switch version was released as part of a double pack with Chaos;Head Noah in 2022.

Adaptations

A manga adaptation was serialized in Kadokawa's Dengeki G's Magazine, and a twelve-episode anime television series adaptation by Silver Link aired from January to March 2017, directed by Masato Jinbo.

Reception

The game was generally well received by critics, who praised its dark atmosphere, writing and the delusion system, though some noted its slow opening chapters. Japanese magazine Famitsu gave the Xbox One version a score of 31 out of 40.
