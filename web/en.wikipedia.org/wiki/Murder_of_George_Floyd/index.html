Murder of George Floyd

George Floyd, a 46-year-old African-American man, was murdered on May 25, 2020, in Minneapolis, Minnesota, by Derek Chauvin, a white police officer, who pressed his knee onto Floyd's neck for over nine minutes while Floyd was handcuffed and lying face-down, despite Floyd repeatedly saying "I can't breathe." The murder, recorded on video by bystanders, sparked worldwide protests against police brutality and racism.

Events

Police responded to a report that Floyd had used a counterfeit $20 bill at a Cup Foods store at 38th and Chicago Avenue. During the arrest Chauvin knelt on Floyd's neck for 9 minutes 29 seconds while officers Kueng and Lane restrained him and officer Thao kept bystanders away. Floyd became unresponsive and was pronounced dead at hospital. The Hennepin County medical examiner ruled the death a homicide.

Trials

Chauvin was convicted in April 2021 of second-degree unintentional murder, third-degree murder, and second-degree manslaughter, and sentenced to 22.5 years in state prison, plus a federal civil rights sentence of 21 years. The other three officers were convicted on federal civil rights charges, and Kueng and Lane pleaded guilty in state court.

Protests and legacy

The George Floyd protests spread across the United States and internationally, becoming one of the largest protest movements in US history, prompting policing reforms, chokehold bans, and debates about racial justice. The intersection where he died became George Floyd Square.
