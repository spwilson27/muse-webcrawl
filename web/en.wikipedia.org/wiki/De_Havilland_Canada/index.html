de Havilland Canada

De Havilland Aircraft of Canada Limited (DHC) is a Canadian aircraft manufacturer that has produced numerous widely used aircraft, particularly STOL (short takeoff and landing) bush planes. Founded in 1928 as a subsidiary of the British de Havilland company, it is based in the Toronto area.

Aircraft

Its best-known designs include the DHC-2 Beaver (1947), DHC-3 Otter, DHC-4 Caribou, DHC-5 Buffalo, DHC-6 Twin Otter, and the DHC-7 and DHC-8 (Dash 8) regional airliners. The company also produces the Canadair CL-415 water bomber under the revived brand.

Ownership

The company passed through Crown ownership, Boeing, and Bombardier before Longview Aviation Capital acquired the Dash 8 program and the de Havilland brand in 2019, reviving the de Havilland Canada name. The Twin Otter is produced by sister company Viking Air.
