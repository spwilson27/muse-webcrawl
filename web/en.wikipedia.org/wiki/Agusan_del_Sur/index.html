Agusan del Sur, officially the Province of Agusan del Sur, is a landlocked province in the Caraga region of Mindanao, Philippines. Its capital is the municipality of Prosperidad, and its only city is Bayugan. It is the largest province in Caraga by land area and one of the largest in the country.

History

The province was created on 17 June 1967 when the old province of Agusan was divided into Agusan del Norte and Agusan del Sur under Republic Act No. 4979. The valley had been inhabited for centuries by Manobo and other indigenous peoples along the Agusan River, and the area was a center of the pre-colonial trade networks served by the river; the famous Golden Tara image was found in Esperanza in 1917.

Geography

Agusan del Sur occupies the middle and upper Agusan Valley between the Diwata mountain range to the east and hills to the west, bordering Agusan del Norte to the north, Surigao del Sur to the east, Davao de Oro and Davao del Norte to the south and southeast, Bukidnon to the west, and Misamis Oriental to the northwest. Much of the province is covered by the Agusan Marsh Wildlife Sanctuary, a vast wetland in the river's floodplain. The province comprises 13 municipalities and one component city.

Economy

The economy is based on agriculture — rice, corn, banana, coconut, and oil palm — along with forestry and small-scale gold mining. Despite its resources, Agusan del Sur has historically recorded among the higher poverty rates in the Philippines.
