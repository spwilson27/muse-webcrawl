Hugh Grant


Actor Hugh Grant.

Hugh John Mungo Grant (born 9 September 1960) is an English actor. He established himself early in his career as a charming and vulnerable romantic leading man, and has since transitioned into a character actor. He has received several accolades including a British Academy Film Award and a Golden Globe Award as well as nominations for two Primetime Emmy Awards.

Career

Grant made his feature film acting debut in Privileged (1982), followed by Maurice (1987), for which he gained recognition. He then achieved international stardom after appearing in the Richard Curtis–penned romance Four Weddings and a Funeral (1994), for which he won the BAFTA Award and the Golden Globe for Best Actor. He furthered this success starring in Notting Hill (1999), Bridget Jones's Diary (2001) and its sequels, About a Boy (2002), Two Weeks Notice (2002), Love Actually (2003), and Music and Lyrics (2007).

In the 2010s and 2020s, Grant reinvented his career with acclaimed turns in Cloud Atlas (2012), Florence Foster Jenkins (2016), Paddington 2 (2017), A Very English Scandal (2018), The Undoing (2020), and Heretic (2024), moving into darker and more villainous character roles.

Personal life and activism

Grant has been a prominent campaigner against the practices of some British tabloid newspapers, notably through the campaign group Hacked Off, and gave evidence to the Leveson Inquiry into press ethics.
