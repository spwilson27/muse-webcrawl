The University of Alaska is the public university system of the U.S. state of Alaska, comprising three separately accredited universities — the University of Alaska Fairbanks (UAF), the University of Alaska Anchorage (UAA), and the University of Alaska Southeast (UAS) in Juneau — along with community campuses across the state.

History

The system began in 1917 with the Alaska Agricultural College and School of Mines in Fairbanks, opened in 1922 and renamed the University of Alaska in 1935. It expanded with the growth of Anchorage and the addition of campuses statewide.

Research

UAF is the system's research flagship, known for Arctic and northern research, geophysics, and climate studies, including the Geophysical Institute and the Poker Flat Research Range. The system faced severe budget cuts beginning in 2019, prompting restructuring debates and, in 2020, a merger of some programs.
