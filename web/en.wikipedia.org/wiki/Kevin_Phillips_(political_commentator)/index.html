Kevin Phillips (political commentator)

Kevin P. Phillips (November 30, 1940 – October 9, 2023) was an American writer, political commentator and former Republican Party strategist. As an aide to Richard Nixon, he helped develop the "Southern strategy" that realigned American politics. His first book, The Emerging Republican Majority (1969), was highly influential in Republican circles.

Career

Phillips later became a critic of the Republican Party's direction. In works such as American Theocracy (2006), he warned about the intertwining of radical religion, oil politics and borrowed money in American public life. Earlier books, including The Politics of Rich and Poor (1990) and Wealth and Democracy (2002), examined rising economic inequality. He was a regular commentator for the Los Angeles Times, NPR and other outlets.
