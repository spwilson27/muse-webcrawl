Oral-B

Oral-B is a brand of oral hygiene products, including toothbrushes, toothpaste, dental floss and mouthwash, owned by Procter & Gamble. The brand traces its origins to 1950, when California dentist Robert W. Hutson designed and patented a toothbrush with soft, end-rounded nylon bristles, sold under the name Oral-B.

The company grew through sales to dental professionals and was acquired by the Gillette Company in 1984; it passed to Procter & Gamble with the 2005 acquisition of Gillette. Oral-B became known for electric toothbrushes using oscillating-rotating technology developed with Braun, as well as manual brushes, interdental products and children's ranges.

The brand is marketed in most countries and is frequently promoted as widely used or recommended by dentists.
