Release Me (Engelbert Humperdinck album)

ArtistEngelbert Humperdinck
Released1967
LabelDecca
GenreEasy listening, pop

Release Me is the debut studio album by British pop singer Engelbert Humperdinck, released in 1967 by Decca Records. The album takes its name from Humperdinck's breakthrough single "Release Me", which reached number one on the UK Singles Chart in 1967 and famously kept the Beatles' double A-side "Strawberry Fields Forever" / "Penny Lane" from the top spot.

Background

Humperdinck, born Arnold George Dorsey, had struggled for years before his manager Gordon Mills relaunched him under the distinctive stage name borrowed from the German composer of Hansel and Gretel. The success of "Release Me" made him an international star and launched a long career as a crooner.

Track listing

The album collects the title song alongside other contemporary ballads and standards in the easy-listening style that became Humperdinck's trademark, including material associated with his early singles.

Legacy

The album established Humperdinck as one of the leading male vocalists of the late 1960s and began a string of successful albums for Decca.
