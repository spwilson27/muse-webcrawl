Josef Dobrovský (1753–1829) was a Czech philologist and historian, regarded as the founder of Slavic studies and a central figure of the Czech National Revival. A Jesuit-educated scholar, he applied rigorous critical methods to the history of the Czech and Slavic languages.

Works

His Geschichte der böhmischen Sprache und Literatur (1792) surveyed Bohemian language and literature, and his grammar Ausführliches Lehrgebäude der böhmischen Sprache (1809) codified modern literary Czech, deliberately modelling it on the language of the 16th century. He also studied Old Church Slavonic and edited early manuscripts, although he famously remained sceptical about the capacity of Czech to serve as a modern literary language — a pessimism his successors in the National Revival overcame.
