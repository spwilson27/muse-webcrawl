Formication

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Formication is the sensation resembling that of small insects crawling on or under the skin that has no physical cause. It is a specific type of paresthesia and takes its name from the Latin formica, meaning "ant". Formication may occur as a symptom of underlying conditions or as an effect of certain substances.

Causes

Causes include menopause, diabetic neuropathy, skin cancers, syphilis, and other neurological or psychiatric conditions. It is also associated with stimulant intoxication — classically cocaine and methamphetamine use, where the sensation (sometimes called "coke bugs") may contribute to delusional parasitosis — and with withdrawal states and certain medications.
