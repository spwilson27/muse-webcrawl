SABMiller plc was a multinational brewing and beverage company headquartered in London, England. It was the world's second-largest brewer measured by revenues (after Anheuser-Busch InBev) and was also a major bottler of Coca-Cola. Its brands included Fosters, Miller, Peroni Nastro Azzurro, Grolsch and Pilsner Urquell.

History

The company originated in 1895 as South African Breweries (SAB), founded in Johannesburg to serve thirsty gold miners — famously delivering its first beer, Castle Lager, to miners at the Barberton gold fields. After the end of apartheid, SAB expanded aggressively abroad, listing in London in 1999. In 2002 it acquired the American Miller Brewing Company, becoming SABMiller, and in 2005 it bought Bavaria, South America's second-largest brewer. Further deals included the 2011 acquisition of Australia's Foster's Group for about A$11.5 billion.

Acquisition by AB InBev

In September 2015 Anheuser-Busch InBev announced its intention to buy SABMiller in a deal valued at about £69 billion (US$104 billion), the largest takeover of a British company at the time. To win antitrust approval, SABMiller's stake in MillerCoors was sold to Molson Coors, and the Peroni, Grolsch and Meantime brands were sold to Japan's Asahi. The acquisition closed on 10 October 2016; SABMiller was delisted and ceased trading as a separate entity, and AB InBev discontinued the SABMiller name.

Operations

Before its takeover, SABMiller operated in more than 80 countries, with particularly strong positions in Africa, Latin America, Eastern Europe and China (through the CR Snow joint venture with China Resources, whose Snow brand was the world's best-selling beer by volume).
