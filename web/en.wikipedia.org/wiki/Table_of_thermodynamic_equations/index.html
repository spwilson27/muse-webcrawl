Table of thermodynamic equations

This article is a summary of common equations and quantities in thermodynamics, the branch of physics concerned with heat, work, temperature and energy. The notation follows the conventions used in the main thermodynamics articles: T temperature, p pressure, V volume, U internal energy, H enthalpy, S entropy, G Gibbs free energy, A Helmholtz free energy, and n amount of substance.

Fundamental relations

NameEquation
First law of thermodynamicsdU = δQ − δW (closed system; sign conventions vary)
Fundamental relationdU = T dS − p dV + μ dn
EnthalpyH = U + pV;   dH = T dS + V dp + μ dn
Helmholtz free energyA = U − TS;   dA = −S dT − p dV + μ dn
Gibbs free energyG = H − TS;   dG = −S dT + V dp + μ dn
Heat capacity at constant pressureCp = (∂H/∂T)p
Heat capacity at constant volumeCV = (∂U/∂T)V
