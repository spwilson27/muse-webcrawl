Basketball at the 1992 Summer Olympics was held in Barcelona, Spain, from 25 July to 9 August 1992. The tournament is remembered above all for the debut of the United States men's team of NBA professionals — the "Dream Team" — widely regarded as the greatest basketball team ever assembled.

Men's tournament

Following a FIBA rule change in 1989 allowing professional players, the United States fielded a roster including Michael Jordan, Magic Johnson, Larry Bird, Charles Barkley and Karl Malone. Coached by Chuck Daly, the team won its eight games by an average margin of about 44 points, defeating Croatia 117–85 in the gold medal game. Lithuania, playing its first Olympics since independence and famous for its tie-dyed uniforms, took the bronze medal over the Unified Team.

Women's tournament

The United States women's team defeated China in the final to win the gold medal, continuing American dominance of the event, while the Unified Team took bronze.

Legacy

The Dream Team's popularity is credited with globalising basketball, inspiring a generation of international players who later starred in the NBA.
