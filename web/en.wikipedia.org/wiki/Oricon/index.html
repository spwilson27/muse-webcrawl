Oricon provides the standard music sales charts of Japan.

Oricon Inc. (株式会社オリコン, Kabushiki-gaisha Orikon), established in 1999, is the holding company at the head of the Japanese Oricon Group. The group is famous for providing statistics and information on music and the music industry in Japan and Western entertainment. It started as Original Confidence Inc., established by Sōkō Koike in 1967, which quickly became known for its music charts. Oricon Inc. was established as a subsidiary of Original Confidence. The name "Oricon" is derived from the English words Original Confidence.

Charts

The Oricon Charts are the standard Japanese music sales charts, ranking singles, albums, DVDs, and, more recently, combined physical and digital releases. Data are compiled daily and weekly from thousands of reporting retail outlets across Japan, as well as digital download and streaming services. Weekly charts are traditionally considered the definitive measure of commercial success in the Japanese music industry, the world's second-largest recorded-music market.

Unlike charts in some other countries, Oricon's rankings long counted physical sales only, which famously favoured acts with highly organised fan bases; idol groups such as AKB48 and Johnny's artists achieved enormous first-week sales partly through multi-copy purchases linked to handshake events and voting ballots. Oricon introduced rules against excessive bundling in 2016 and launched a combined singles and albums chart in December 2018, incorporating downloads and streams.

History and scope

Original Confidence began publishing chart data in 1967 and its first official singles chart in January 1968. Beyond record charts, the Oricon group publishes entertainment news, consumer satisfaction rankings, and the Oricon Style magazine and website. Its annual "year-end" rankings and the designation of million-seller certifications are widely reported in Japanese media.
