Simeon (Gospel of Luke)

Simeon is a figure in the Gospel of Luke (Luke 2:25–35), described as a righteous and devout man in Jerusalem who had been promised by the Holy Spirit that he would not die before seeing the Messiah.

The Presentation

When Mary and Joseph brought the infant Jesus to the Temple for the Presentation, Simeon took the child in his arms and spoke the prayer known as the Nunc dimittis ("Now you dismiss your servant in peace"), calling Jesus "a light for revelation to the Gentiles". He also prophesied to Mary that a sword would pierce her soul. Simeon is venerated as a saint, and the meeting is commemorated in the feast of Candlemas in many churches.
