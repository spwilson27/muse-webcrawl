Dragon Age is a media franchise centred on a series of fantasy role-playing video games developed by BioWare and published by Electronic Arts. The games are set in the fictional world of Thedas, a continent of rival kingdoms, ancient elven ruins, the Chantry religion, and recurring invasions of monstrous darkspawn known as Blights. The franchise has expanded into novels, comics, an anime film and an animated series.

Games

Dragon Age: Origins (2009) introduced the player as a Grey Warden fighting the Fifth Blight in Ferelden, and was acclaimed as a spiritual successor to Baldur's Gate. An expansion, Awakening, followed in 2010. Dragon Age II (2011) told the story of Hawke, a refugee who becomes Champion of the city-state of Kirkwall amid tensions between mages and templars. Dragon Age: Inquisition (2014), in which the player leads the Inquisition against a rift in the sky, won numerous Game of the Year awards. Dragon Age: The Veilguard, released in October 2024 after a long development, follows Rook and a team of companions against unleashed elven gods in northern Thedas.

Setting and themes

Thedas mixes high fantasy with dark and political elements: magic is drawn from the spirit realm of the Fade, mages are confined by the templar-supervised Circles, elves are an oppressed underclass, and dwarven society is bound by caste. Player choices — including character origin, romances and major political decisions — carry between games through save imports and the Dragon Age Keep application.

Reception

The series has sold well over ten million copies and is among BioWare's flagship franchises alongside Mass Effect. Origins and Inquisition in particular are frequently listed among the best role-playing games ever made.
