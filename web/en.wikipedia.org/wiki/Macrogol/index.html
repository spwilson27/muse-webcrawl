Macrogol

Macrogol, also known as polyethylene glycol (PEG), is used as a medication to treat constipation in children and adults. It is also used to empty the bowels before a colonoscopy. It is taken by mouth, commonly dissolved in water.

Medical uses

Macrogol is an osmotic laxative: the large polymer molecules are not absorbed from the gut and hold water in the bowel by osmosis, softening the stool and increasing its volume. Preparations are identified by the average molecular weight of the polymer, such as macrogol 3350 or macrogol 4000. Formulations often include electrolytes to reduce the risk of electrolyte disturbance during bowel preparation.

Side effects

Common side effects include bloating, nausea, abdominal cramping and diarrhoea. Macrogols are generally considered safe in pregnancy and breastfeeding.
