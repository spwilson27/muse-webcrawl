Muscle tone

Muscle tone (also called tonus) is the continuous, passive partial contraction of muscles at rest, and the resistance felt when a joint is moved passively. It helps maintain posture and readiness for action and is regulated by the nervous system, particularly spinal reflex arcs and descending pathways.

Abnormalities

Hypertonia is abnormally increased tone, seen as spasticity or rigidity in conditions such as cerebral palsy, stroke and Parkinson's disease. Hypotonia is reduced tone, observed in some neuromuscular disorders and in infants as "floppy baby syndrome". Assessment of tone is a standard part of the neurological examination.
