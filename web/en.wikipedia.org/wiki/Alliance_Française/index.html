Alliance Française

The Alliance Française is an international organisation that promotes the French language and francophone culture around the world. Founded in Paris in 1883 — with early supporters including Ferdinand de Lesseps, Louis Pasteur and Jules Verne — it grew into a network of local associations teaching French and hosting cultural events.

Network

There are more than 800 Alliances Françaises in well over a hundred countries, each typically run as an independent, non-profit local association under French law principles set by the Fondation des Alliances Françaises, established in 2007 to coordinate the network. Together they teach French to hundreds of thousands of students each year and administer official examinations such as the DELF and DALF.

Activities

Beyond language courses, local Alliances organise concerts, film screenings, exhibitions, lectures and festivals, and many maintain French-language libraries and resource centres. The network is one of the principal instruments of French cultural diplomacy, alongside the Institut français.
