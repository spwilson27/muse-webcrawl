Puss in Boots (Shrek)

Puss in Boots
First appearance: Shrek 2 (2004)
Voiced by: Antonio Banderas
Species: Cat
Occupation: Assassin, hero, outlaw

Puss in Boots is a fictional character in the Shrek franchise, voiced by Antonio Banderas. Based on the fairy-tale character of the same name, he is a smooth-talking, swashbuckling orange tabby cat — a parody of Zorro and other sword-wielding heroes — complete with a Spanish accent, a rapier, a cavalier's hat, and, of course, boots.

Appearances

Puss first appears in Shrek 2 (2004), hired by King Harold to assassinate Shrek; instead he becomes a loyal ally after Shrek spares his life. He returns in Shrek the Third (2007) and Shrek Forever After (2010), the latter featuring an alternate-timeline version of Puss as Princess Fiona's pampered, overweight pet. He is famous for his "cute kitten eyes" routine, in which he widens his eyes and removes his hat to disarm opponents.

Spin-offs

The character received his own films: Puss in Boots (2011), a prequel exploring his origins alongside Humpty Dumpty and Kitty Softpaws, and Puss in Boots: The Last Wish (2022), in which, having burned through eight of his nine lives, he quests for the Wishing Star. He also starred in the animated series The Adventures of Puss in Boots (2015–2018).

Reception

Puss in Boots became one of the most popular characters of the franchise, with Banderas's performance widely praised and The Last Wish receiving an Academy Award nomination for Best Animated Feature.
