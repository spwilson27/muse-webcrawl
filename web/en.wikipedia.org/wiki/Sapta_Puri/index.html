Sapta Puri

Varanasi, one of the seven sacred cities
ReligionHinduism
Meaning"Seven cities"

The Sapta Puri (Sanskrit: सप्त पुरी, "seven cities") are seven holy pilgrimage centres in India revered in Hinduism. Tradition holds that these cities are especially sacred and that pilgrimage to them grants moksha (liberation from the cycle of rebirth). They are also known as the Sapta Moksha Puri.

The seven cities

  • Ayodhya — birthplace of Rama
  • Mathura — birthplace of Krishna
  • Haridwar (Maya) — where the Ganges descends to the plains
  • Varanasi (Kashi) — the city of Shiva on the Ganges
  • Kanchipuram (Kanchi) — temple city in Tamil Nadu
  • Ujjain (Avanti) — site of the Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga and the Kumbh Mela
  • Dwarka — Krishna's capital on the Gujarat coast

The Sapta Puri are among the oldest continuously inhabited sacred sites in India and figure prominently in the Puranas and other Hindu scriptures. Several of them host major festivals, including the Kumbh Mela at Haridwar and Ujjain.
