Audi R8

The Audi R8 is a mid-engine, two-seater sports car produced by the German manufacturer Audi from 2006 to 2024. It was Audi's first mid-engined production car and served as the brand's flagship performance model, sharing much of its platform and drivetrain with the Lamborghini Gallardo and later the Lamborghini Huracán.

First generation (Type 42; 2006–2015)

Unveiled at the 2006 Paris Motor Show, the first-generation R8 was based on the Audi Le Mans quattro concept car of 2003. It launched with a 4.2-litre V8 producing 420 PS, followed in 2009 by a 5.2-litre V10 derived from the Lamborghini Gallardo's engine. Audi's quattro all-wheel-drive system was standard, and a convertible Spyder version followed. The high-performance R8 GT and the electric R8 e-tron prototypes were notable variants; the car also became widely known as the car driven by Tony Stark in the Iron Man films.

Second generation (Type 4S; 2015–2024)

The second-generation R8 debuted at the 2015 Geneva Motor Show, offered only with the 5.2-litre naturally aspirated V10 in standard (540 PS) and plus/performance (up to 620 PS) forms, driving through a seven-speed dual-clutch gearbox. Rear-wheel-drive versions were added later. The R8's V10 made it one of the last naturally aspirated supercars on sale. Production ended in March 2024 as Audi shifted its performance range toward electrification.

Motorsport

The R8 LMS GT3 racing version became one of the most successful GT3 cars ever built, winning multiple 24-hour races at the Nürburgring, Spa and Daytona, as well as numerous national and international championships.
