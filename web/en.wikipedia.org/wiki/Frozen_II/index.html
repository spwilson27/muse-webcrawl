Frozen II is a 2019 American animated musical fantasy film produced by Walt Disney Animation Studios and the sequel to Frozen (2013). Directed by Chris Buck and Jennifer Lee, it follows Queen Elsa, Princess Anna, Kristoff, Sven and Olaf as they journey to an enchanted forest to discover the origin of Elsa's magical powers and save their kingdom of Arendelle.

Plot

Three years after her coronation, Elsa is drawn by a mysterious siren-like voice calling from the north. When Arendelle is threatened by the elemental spirits of earth, fire, water and air, the sisters travel to the Enchanted Forest, where the indigenous Northuldra people and a trapped troop of Arendellian soldiers have been cut off by a magical mist for decades. Elsa ventures onward to Ahtohallan, a river of memory, learns that she is herself the fifth spirit uniting the others, and — with Anna's help — undoes a historic wrong by destroying the dam that had harmed the forest. Anna becomes queen of Arendelle and Elsa remains in the forest as its protector.

Reception

The film grossed $1.453 billion worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing animated film of all time at the end of its run. It received generally positive reviews for its animation, music and themes, though some critics considered it less effective than the original. Its songs include "Into the Unknown", which was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Original Song.
