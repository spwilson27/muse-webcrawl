Google Duo was a mobile-oriented video calling application developed by Google. Announced at Google I/O in May 2016 alongside the messaging app Allo, it was released on 16 August 2016 for Android and iOS. Registration was based on phone numbers, and calls were end-to-end encrypted.

Features

Duo was designed for simplicity and call quality on poor connections, using the QUIC protocol and adapting resolution automatically. Its signature feature, "Knock Knock", showed the recipient a live video preview of the caller before answering. The app later added audio-only calls, group video calls (eventually up to 32 participants), video messages, a web client, and low-light and data-saving modes.

Merger with Google Meet

In 2022 Google announced that Duo would be merged into Google Meet: the Duo app was updated and rebranded as Meet, combining Duo's consumer calling features with Meet's meeting functionality, and the standalone Duo brand was retired.
