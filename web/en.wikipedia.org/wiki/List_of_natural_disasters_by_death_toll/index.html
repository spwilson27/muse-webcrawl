List of natural disasters by death toll

The following is a list of natural disasters by death toll. The deadliest natural disasters in recorded history are floods and earthquakes in China and great famines caused or worsened by drought. Historical death tolls are estimates and often vary widely between sources, particularly for events before the 20th century. The single deadliest event is generally considered to be the 1931 China floods, with estimates ranging from about 400,000 to several million deaths (including subsequent disease and famine). The deadliest earthquake was the 1556 Shaanxi earthquake, which killed an estimated 830,000 people.

Notes on famines and epidemics

Drought-related famines (for example the Great Famine of 1876–1878 in India and the Northern Chinese Famine of 1876–1879, each killing on the order of 9–13 million) and pandemics (such as the 1918 influenza pandemic, with estimates of 17–100 million deaths) are sometimes counted among "natural disasters" and would top this list; they are usually catalogued separately because human policy contributed heavily to their tolls.
