Kärnan

Kärnan is a medieval tower in Helsingborg, Sweden, and the only part remaining of a larger Danish fortress that once controlled the entrance to the Øresund. Built in the 1310s, the brick tower stands about 35 metres tall.

History

The fortress was an important Danish stronghold until Scania passed to Sweden under the Treaty of Roskilde in 1658. Most of the castle was demolished in the late 17th century, leaving the central keep, Kärnan, standing.

Today

Surrounded by terraced gardens in central Helsingborg, Kärnan is a protected historic monument and one of the city's best-known landmarks.
