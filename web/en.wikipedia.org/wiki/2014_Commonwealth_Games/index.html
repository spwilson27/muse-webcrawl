The 2014 Commonwealth Games, officially the XX Commonwealth Games and commonly known as Glasgow 2014, were held in Glasgow, Scotland, from 23 July to 3 August 2014. They were the third Commonwealth Games staged in Scotland (after Edinburgh 1970 and 1986) and the largest multi-sport event ever held in the country.

Overview

Glasgow defeated Abuja, Nigeria, in the 2007 host vote. Venues included Celtic Park (opening ceremony), Hampden Park (athletics and closing ceremony), the SSE Hydro, the Sir Chris Hoy Velodrome and the Emirates Arena. Para-sport events were fully integrated into the programme, the largest such programme of any Commonwealth Games to that date.

Highlights

England topped the medal table for the first time since 1986, ahead of Australia. Notable performances included Jamaican sprint wins by Kemar Bailey-Cole and Shelly-Ann Fraser-Pryce's relay team (Usain Bolt anchored Jamaica to 4×100 m relay gold), Scotland's most successful Games ever with 53 medals, and Welsh gymnast Claudia Fragapane's four golds. Kiribati won its first ever Commonwealth medal, a weightlifting gold for David Katoatau.

Legacy

The Games were widely praised for organisation and atmosphere, and the Athletes' Village in Dalmarnock was converted to housing. The event took place weeks before Scotland's independence referendum, adding political resonance to the home team's success.
