Australian Convict Sites

Australian Convict Sites
UNESCO World Heritage Site
Inscribed: 2010
Components: 11 sites
States: NSW, Tasmania, WA, Norfolk Island

The Australian Convict Sites is a World Heritage listing of eleven remnant penal sites originally built within the British Empire during the 18th and 19th centuries on the fertile coastal strip of Australia. They were inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 2010.

The sites

The eleven properties are: Kingston and Arthur's Vale Historic Area (Norfolk Island); Old Government House and Domain (Parramatta); Hyde Park Barracks and the Old Great North Road (New South Wales); Brickendon and Woolmers Estates, the Cascades Female Factory, Coal Mines Historic Site, Darlington Probation Station and Port Arthur (Tasmania); and Fremantle Prison (Western Australia). Together they represent the forced migration of some 166,000 convicts transported to Australia and the colonial penal system that supported it.
