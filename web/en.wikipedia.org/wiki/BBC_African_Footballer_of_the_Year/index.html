BBC African Footballer of the Year

The BBC African Footballer of the Year was an annual award given to the best African footballer of the year, as voted by fans and organised by the BBC through BBC Sport and the BBC World Service. It began in 1992 as the BBC African Sports Star of the Year and took its football-specific form from 2001.

Format

Journalists across Africa compiled a shortlist of five players, and supporters voted online and by text message. Winners were announced on BBC television and radio programmes including Focus on Africa.

Winners

Notable winners include Abedi Pele, George Weah, Jay-Jay Okocha, Didier Drogba, Michael Essien, Yaya Touré (twice), Mohamed Salah (2017 and 2018) and Sadio Mané. The award was discontinued after the 2018 edition, with the BBC citing plans to refresh its sports coverage.
