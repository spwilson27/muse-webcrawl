Viken (county)

Viken was a county in Eastern Norway that existed from 1 January 2020 to 1 January 2024, created by the merger of Akershus, Buskerud and Østfold counties under the Solberg government's regional reform. It surrounded, but did not include, the capital Oslo, and had about 1.25 million inhabitants.

History

The name revived the historic region of Viken around the Oslofjord. The merger was controversial and locally unpopular; after the 2021 parliamentary election the Støre government permitted its reversal. On 1 January 2024 Viken was dissolved and Akershus, Buskerud and Østfold were re-established as separate counties.

Geography

The county stretched from the Swedish border and Oslofjord to the Hardangervidda plateau, with its administrative seats in Oslo, Drammen, Sarpsborg and Moss.
