Colombia, one of the world's most biodiverse countries, protects a large share of its territory through the National Natural Parks System (Sistema de Parques Nacionales Naturales), administered by Parques Nacionales Naturales de Colombia. The system includes around 60 protected areas covering Amazon rainforest, Andean páramo, Caribbean coast and Pacific lowlands. The largest, Serranía de Chiribiquete, was expanded in 2018 to roughly 4.3 million hectares and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
