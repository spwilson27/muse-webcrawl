Alina Ilnazovna Zagitova (born 18 May 2002) is a Russian former competitive figure skater. She is the 2018 Olympic champion in women's singles, the 2019 World champion, the 2018 European champion, and the 2017–18 Grand Prix Final champion, one of the few skaters to have won all major international titles in the same period.

Career

Zagitova rose rapidly through the junior ranks, winning the 2017 World Junior Championships. In her first senior season she won the gold medal at the 2018 Winter Olympics in Pyeongchang at age 15, defeating teammate Evgenia Medvedeva with a free skate notable for placing all of its jumps in the second half to maximise bonus points — a layout that prompted a rule change limiting such back-loading. In 2019 she won the World Championships in Saitama, completing a career sweep of major titles. She announced a break from competition in December 2019 and later worked as a television presenter in Russia.
