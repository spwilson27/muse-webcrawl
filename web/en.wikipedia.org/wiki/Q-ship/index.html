Q-ship

Q-ships were heavily armed merchant ships with concealed weaponry, designed as decoys to lure submarines into making surface attacks. They were used by the Royal Navy during the First World War and, to a lesser extent, by several navies in the Second World War.

Operation

A Q-ship would feign damage or abandonment, with a "panic party" taking to boats, so that a U-boat would approach on the surface; hinged panels then dropped to reveal naval guns and the White Ensign was hoisted. The tactic lost effectiveness as submarine commanders grew wary and attacked submerged without warning.
