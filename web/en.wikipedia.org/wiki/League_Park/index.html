League Park

LocationCleveland, Ohio, United States
Opened1891
TenantsCleveland Indians and predecessors
StatusDemolished 1951; site now a public park and museum

League Park was a baseball stadium in Cleveland, Ohio, that served as the home of Cleveland's major league teams, including the Indians, from 1891 until the club moved away full-time after the 1946 season.

History

The wooden park opened in 1891 and was rebuilt in concrete and steel in 1910, with an unusually deep centre field and a towering right-field wall close to home plate. League Park hosted the 1920 World Series, won by Cleveland, and saw notable events including Babe Ruth's 500th home run in 1929 and Joe DiMaggio's 56-game hitting streak being stopped in 1941.

Legacy

The stadium was largely demolished in 1951, but the ticket office and a section of wall survived. The site was restored as a public park with a baseball field on the original diamond, and the Baseball Heritage Museum now occupies the former ticket house.
