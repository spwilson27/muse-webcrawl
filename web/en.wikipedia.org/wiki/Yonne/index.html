Yonne

Image: Yonne

Yonne (French pronunciation: [jɔn]) is a department in the Bourgogne-Franche-Comté region of east-central France. It is named after the river Yonne, a tributary of the Seine. Its prefecture is Auxerre.

Geography

Yonne is part of the Paris Basin. The Yonne river flows northward through the department, passing Joigny, Sens and Auxerre. The south-east of the department touches the Morvan hills.

History

The department was created during the French Revolution on 4 March 1790 from parts of the former provinces of Burgundy, Champagne and Orléanais. It is known for its white wine, including Chablis, produced in the north of the department.
