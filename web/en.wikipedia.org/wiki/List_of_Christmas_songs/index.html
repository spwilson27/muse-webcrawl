List of Christmas songs

This is a list of well-known Christmas songs, including traditional carols and popular modern songs associated with the Christmas season.

Traditional carols

TitleOrigin / notes
"O Come, All Ye Faithful" (Adeste Fideles)Attributed to John Francis Wade, c. 1743
"Silent Night"Franz Gruber and Joseph Mohr, Oberndorf, Austria, 1818
"Hark! The Herald Angels Sing"Words by Charles Wesley (1739), music adapted from Mendelssohn
"Joy to the World"Isaac Watts (1719), based on Psalm 98
"O Holy Night"Adolphe Adam, 1847
"Away in a Manger"First published 1885
"The First Noel"Traditional English, published 1823
"God Rest You Merry, Gentlemen"Traditional English, 18th century
"We Three Kings"John Henry Hopkins Jr., 1857
"What Child Is This?"William Chatterton Dix, 1865; tune "Greensleeves"
"O Little Town of Bethlehem"Phillips Brooks and Lewis Redner, 1868
"Carol of the Bells"Based on "Shchedryk" by Mykola Leontovych, 1914
"Deck the Halls"Welsh melody; English lyrics by Thomas Oliphant, 1862
"The Twelve Days of Christmas"Traditional English cumulative song, c. 1780
