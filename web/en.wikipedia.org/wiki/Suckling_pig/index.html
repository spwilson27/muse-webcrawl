Suckling pig
Main ingredientYoung pig, 2–6 weeks old

A suckling pig is a piglet fed only on its mother's milk, slaughtered between two and six weeks old and traditionally cooked whole, often roasted, producing tender meat and crisp skin.

Traditions

Famous preparations include Spanish cochinillo asado of Segovia, Portuguese leitão da Bairrada, Chinese Cantonese roasted suckling pig with crackling skin, Filipino lechon, German Spanferkel and Hawaiian kalua pig celebrations.

Preparation

The pig is seasoned, sometimes stuffed, and slow-roasted until the skin blisters and crisps while the milk-fed flesh stays unusually tender and mild.
