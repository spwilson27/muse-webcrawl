Tinder

Tinder
Type: Online dating application
Launched: 2012
Developer: Tinder, Inc. (Match Group)
Platforms: iOS, Android, web

Tinder is an online dating and geosocial networking application launched in 2012. Users create profiles with photos and short biographies, and are shown other users within a chosen geographic radius. The app's signature mechanic is swiping: a user swipes right to express interest or left to pass; if two users both swipe right on each other, they "match" and can exchange messages.

History

Tinder was founded within the IAC incubator Hatch Labs and launched at the University of Southern California in 2012. It spread rapidly on college campuses and then internationally, becoming one of the world's most popular dating apps and a major cultural phenomenon associated with casual dating and hookup culture.

Impact

The swipe-based interface was widely imitated by other apps. Tinder's parent company, Match Group, also owns services such as Hinge and OkCupid. The app has introduced paid tiers and features such as boosts and extended matching filters.
