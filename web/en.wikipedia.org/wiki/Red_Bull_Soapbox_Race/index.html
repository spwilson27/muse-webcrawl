Red Bull Soapbox Race

The Red Bull Soapbox Race is an international series of amateur gravity-racing events sponsored by the energy drink company Red Bull. Competitors race homemade, human-powered, non-motorized soapbox carts down a downhill course, and are judged on speed, creativity of design, and showmanship.

History

The first Red Bull Soapbox Race was held in Brussels, Belgium, in 2000. Since then, the event has travelled to dozens of cities around the world, including Los Angeles, London, and Mumbai, drawing large crowds and teams whose carts take forms ranging from giant food items to replicas of film scenes.

Rules and judging

Carts must be powered only by gravity and the initial push of the crew; no engines or stored energy sources are allowed. Vehicles must steer and brake, and are subject to weight and size limits and safety inspections. Teams typically perform a short skit before their run. Winners are determined by a combination of course time, the originality of the craft, and the crowd-pleasing performance.
