NCR Corporation

Founded: 1884 (as National Manufacturing Company), Dayton, Ohio
Founder: John Henry Patterson
Headquarters: Atlanta, Georgia
Industry: Software, technology services; ATMs, point of sale

NCR Corporation, previously known as National Cash Register, is an American software, consulting and technology company providing services and products for banks, retailers, restaurants and small businesses, including automated teller machines, point-of-sale terminals and self-service kiosks.

History

The company began in Dayton, Ohio, when John Henry Patterson bought the National Manufacturing Company in 1884 and renamed it the National Cash Register Company. Patterson built it into the dominant maker of cash registers, pioneering aggressive sales techniques, territorial quotas and modern employee welfare programmes. NCR later expanded into accounting machines and computers. It was acquired by AT&T in 1991 and spun off again as an independent company in 1997, acquiring firms such as Teradata (later spun off) and Radiant Systems.

Recent developments

In the 2020s NCR shifted toward software and services with recurring revenue. In 2023–24 the company split into two publicly traded companies: NCR Voyix, focused on digital commerce for retail and restaurants, and NCR Atleos, focused on ATMs and banking infrastructure.
