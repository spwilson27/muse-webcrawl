Fayzabad

Country: Afghanistan
Province: Badakhshan
Elevation: ~1,200 m
Population: approx. 80,000 (est.)

Fayzabad (also spelled Faizabad) is the capital and largest city of Badakhshan Province in northeastern Afghanistan, situated on the Kokcha River in the Hindu Kush foothills.

Geography

The city lies in a mountainous valley near the border with Tajikistan, at roughly 1,200 metres elevation. The surrounding region is known for lapis lazuli mining, practised since antiquity.

History

Fayzabad grew as a trading post on routes between Central Asia and South Asia. It served as an administrative centre under the Afghan monarchy, and during the late 20th century conflicts it remained largely under Northern Alliance control rather than the Taliban. It is served by Fayzabad Airport.
