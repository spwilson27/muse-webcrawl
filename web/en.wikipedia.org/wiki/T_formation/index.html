T formation

The T formation is a formation in American football in which the quarterback lines up directly behind the center, with three running backs positioned in a row behind him, forming the shape of the letter T.

History

One of the oldest formations in football, the T was popularized at the professional level by the Chicago Bears under coach Clark Shaughnessy and George Halas, culminating in the Bears' 73–0 victory in the 1940 NFL Championship Game. It remained the dominant formation until the rise of the I formation and shotgun sets.
