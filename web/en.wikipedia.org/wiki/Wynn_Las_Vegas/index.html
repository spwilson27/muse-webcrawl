Wynn Las Vegas, often simply Wynn, is a luxury resort and casino on the Las Vegas Strip in Paradise, Nevada, United States. Developed by casino mogul Steve Wynn through his company Wynn Resorts, it opened on 28 April 2005 on the site of the former Desert Inn. The resort is consistently ranked among the finest hotels in Las Vegas and has received more Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star Awards than any other resort in the world.

History

Steve Wynn purchased the Desert Inn in 2000 and closed it that year to make way for his new flagship project, originally announced as "Le Rêve" after the Picasso painting. The resort, ultimately named after himself, was built at a cost of about $2.7 billion, making it the most expensive resort ever constructed in Las Vegas at the time. Its curved, 45-storey bronze tower is topped by Wynn's signature.

Features

The resort includes a 111,000-square-foot casino, a convention centre, luxury retail, numerous restaurants and nightclubs, a spa, and the Lake of Dreams, a three-acre lake with a 90-foot waterfall and a pine-topped artificial mountain facing the Strip. It was formerly home to an art gallery displaying works from Wynn's private collection, a Ferrari/Maserati dealership, and the aquatic stage show Le Rêve. The adjoining sister property Encore Las Vegas opened in December 2008.

Recognition

Wynn Las Vegas and its restaurants have received numerous accolades, including Forbes Five-Star ratings for the hotel, spa and several restaurants, and AAA Five Diamond status.
