The Lotus Evija is a limited-production electric sports car manufactured by British automobile manufacturer Lotus Cars. Unveiled in July 2019 in London, it is the company's first all-electric vehicle and its first model developed under the ownership of Chinese group Geely. The name is pronounced "eh-VYE-ah" and is derived from "Eve", signifying "the first in existence".

Specifications

The Evija uses four electric motors, one at each wheel, with a targeted combined output of around 2,000 PS (1,500 kW) and 1,700 N·m of torque, making it one of the most powerful production cars ever announced. Lotus targeted acceleration from 0–100 km/h in under three seconds and a top speed above 320 km/h. Its battery pack is mounted amidships behind the passenger compartment, and dramatic Venturi tunnels through the rear bodywork generate downforce while reducing drag.

Production

Production was planned at the Lotus headquarters in Hethel, Norfolk, with a run limited to 130 cars — a nod to the car's Type 130 project designation — at a price of around £2 million each. Deliveries, delayed by the COVID-19 pandemic and development work, began in the mid-2020s.
