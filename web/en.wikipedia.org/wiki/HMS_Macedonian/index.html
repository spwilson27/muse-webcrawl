HMS Macedonian

HMS Macedonian was a 38-gun Lively-class frigate of the Royal Navy, launched in 1810 at Woolwich. She is best known for her capture by the American frigate USS United States on 25 October 1812 during the War of 1812.

Capture and US service

After a fierce action in the Atlantic in which she was dismasted, Macedonian was taken into the United States Navy as USS Macedonian. She later served on anti-piracy and diplomatic missions, including the Perry expedition era, and was broken up in 1834. A successor ship of the same name served thereafter.
