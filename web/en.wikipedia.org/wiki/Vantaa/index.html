Vantaa

Vantaa (Finnish: [ˈʋɑntɑː]; Swedish: Vanda) is a city in Finland, located immediately north of Helsinki in the Uusimaa region. With a population of roughly 250,000, it is the fourth most populous city in Finland and part of the Helsinki metropolitan area. Vantaa is home to Helsinki Airport, the country's largest and busiest airport.

History

The area along the Vantaa River (Vantaanjoki) has been inhabited since the Stone Age. The parish, known as Helsinge, was first mentioned in documents in the 14th century. The rural municipality grew rapidly after the Second World War as suburbs were built to serve Helsinki; it became a market town in 1972 and a city in 1974, taking the name of the river.

Geography and districts

Vantaa is divided into districts including Tikkurila, the administrative centre; Myyrmäki in the west; Hakunila; Korso; Koivukylä; and Aviapolis, the airport city surrounding Helsinki Airport. The city is bisected by the Vantaa River, which flows to the Baltic Sea at Helsinki.

Economy and transport

Helsinki Airport, in the Aviapolis area, is a major employer and a hub for Finnair. The city is served by the Ring III highway, the Helsinki commuter rail network including the Ring Rail Line opened in 2015 connecting the airport to Helsinki Central Station, and the Vantaa light rail line, whose construction began in 2023. The Heureka science centre in Tikkurila is one of Finland's most visited attractions.
