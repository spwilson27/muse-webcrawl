Mary Louise Curtis Bok Zimbalist (August 6, 1876 – January 4, 1970) was an American philanthropist and music patron. In 1924 she founded the Curtis Institute of Music in Philadelphia, one of the world's leading conservatories, which has charged no tuition since 1928.

Philanthropy

The daughter of publishing magnate Cyrus Curtis and wife of Ladies' Home Journal editor Edward Bok, she used her fortune to endow the Curtis Institute and support musicians, orchestras, and settlement music schools. She stipulated that the institute's students be admitted on merit alone.

Legacy

Curtis graduates have included Leonard Bernstein, Lang Lang, and generations of orchestral principals. Bok's philanthropic model—full scholarships funded by endowment—remains the institute's defining feature a century after its founding.
