Monotype Imaging Holdings Inc. is an American company specialising in type design, font technology, and branding. It owns and licenses a large library of well-known typefaces, including Helvetica, Times New Roman (in its Monotype form), Arial, Gill Sans, and Franklin Gothic. The company is based in Woburn, Massachusetts.

History

The company descends from the Lanston Monotype Machine Company, founded in 1887 by Tolbert Lanston, inventor of the Monotype typesetting machine, and from the British Monotype Corporation, which became famous for its typefaces created under Stanley Morison, including Times New Roman (1931), Gill Sans (designed by Eric Gill), and Perpetua.

After a series of corporate reorganisations, Agfa-Compugraphic acquired Monotype in 1998, forming Agfa Monotype Corporation. In 2004 the type business was sold to TA Associates and relaunched as Monotype Imaging. The company acquired several competitors and font businesses, including Linotype (2006), Bitstream's font business (2012), FontShop (2014), and Hoefler&Co (2021). In 2019, Monotype was acquired by the private equity firm HGGC and taken private.

Products and business

Monotype earns revenue from licensing fonts to publishers, advertisers, software companies, and device manufacturers, and from its Monotype Fonts subscription platform for creative professionals. It also sells custom type design and brand services. Its library includes designs distributed through MyFonts, which it also operates.
