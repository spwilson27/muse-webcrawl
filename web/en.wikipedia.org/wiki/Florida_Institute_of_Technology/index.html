Florida Institute of Technology

Florida Institute of Technology (Florida Tech) is a private research university in Melbourne, Florida, founded in 1958 as Brevard Engineering College to provide education for professionals working at what became NASA's Kennedy Space Center.

Academics

The university is known for engineering, aeronautics, sciences, and psychology, offering the first university-based flight training and strong programmes in ocean engineering and space sciences.

Its mascot is the Panther, and it operates research facilities including an offshore marine laboratory.

Campus

Located near Florida's Space Coast, Florida Tech has close ties to the aerospace industry and counts several astronauts among its alumni.
