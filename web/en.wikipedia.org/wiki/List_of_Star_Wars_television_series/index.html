List of Star Wars television series

The List of Star Wars television series covers the animated and live-action series set in the Star Wars galaxy. Television expansions of the saga began in the 1980s with the animated Droids and Ewoks, grew substantially with Star Wars: The Clone Wars (2008–2020), and expanded into flagship live-action streaming series on Disney+ beginning with The Mandalorian in 2019.
