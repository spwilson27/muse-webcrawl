Bösendorfer is an Austrian piano manufacturer founded in Vienna in 1828 by Ignaz Bösendorfer, and one of the oldest luxury piano brands in the world. Its grand pianos are noted for their warm, rich tone and for extra keys beyond the standard 88 — the Imperial Grand has 97 keys, extending the bass range to C.

History

The company won the title of imperial and royal court piano manufacturer and became emblematic of Viennese musical culture, favoured by Franz Liszt, who reputedly could not destroy them in performance. After financial difficulties in the 20th century, Bösendorfer was acquired by the Yamaha Corporation in 2008, while production remained in Austria.

Models

Flagship models include the 214VC, the 230VC and the 290 Imperial Grand, one of the largest concert grands in production at 290 cm.
