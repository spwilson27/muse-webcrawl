The Big Bang Theory is an American television sitcom created by Chuck Lorre and Bill Prady, both of whom served as executive producers on the series, along with Steven Molaro. It aired on CBS from September 24, 2007, to May 16, 2019, running for 12 seasons and 279 episodes, making it the longest-running multi-camera sitcom in television history at the time of its finale.

The show is centered on five characters living in Pasadena, California: Leonard Hofstadter (Johnny Galecki) and Sheldon Cooper (Jim Parsons), both physicists at Caltech, who share an apartment; Penny (Kaley Cuoco), a waitress and aspiring actress who lives across the hall; and Leonard and Sheldon's friends and coworkers, aerospace engineer Howard Wolowitz (Simon Helberg) and astrophysicist Raj Koothrappali (Kunal Nayyar). Later main characters include neuroscientist Amy Farrah Fowler (Mayim Bialik), microbiologist Bernadette Rostenkowski (Melissa Rauch), and comic book store owner Stuart Bloom (Kevin Sussman).

Premise

The geekiness and intellect of the four male characters are contrasted for comic effect with Penny's social skills and common sense. Over time, supporting characters were promoted to starring roles, and the series explored the group's romantic relationships, careers, and friendships.

Production

The show was filmed in front of a live studio audience and was produced by Warner Bros. Television and Chuck Lorre Productions. The theme song, "The History of Everything", was written and recorded by the Canadian rock band Barenaked Ladies. Science consultant David Saltzberg, a professor of physics and astronomy at UCLA, checked scripts and provided mathematical equations and diagrams used as props.

Reception and awards

The series was a ratings success, ranking as one of the most-watched comedies on American television for most of its run. Jim Parsons won four Primetime Emmy Awards for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Comedy Series for his portrayal of Sheldon Cooper, and the show won the Emmy for Outstanding Comedy Series' associated honors among a total of ten Emmy wins from 46 nominations. A prequel spin-off, Young Sheldon, premiered in 2017 and ran until 2024.
