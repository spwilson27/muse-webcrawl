COVID-19 apps
Purpose: Contact tracing, exposure notification, health passes
Technologies: Bluetooth LE, GPS, QR codes
Peak use: 2020–2022

COVID-19 apps are mobile software applications developed during the COVID-19 pandemic to support public-health responses, most prominently digital contact tracing and exposure notification, but also symptom reporting, quarantine enforcement, information provision and vaccine or test certification.

Exposure notification

Many countries deployed apps using Bluetooth Low Energy to log proximity to other app users anonymously. In April 2020 Apple and Google jointly released an Exposure Notification framework, adopted by many national apps because it worked across iOS and Android while preserving privacy through decentralised, rotating identifiers.

Debate and effectiveness

The apps raised debates over privacy, centralised versus decentralised data storage, uptake thresholds and interoperability across borders. Studies of systems such as the NHS COVID-19 app in England and Wales estimated they averted hundreds of thousands of cases; most tracing apps were retired as the pandemic phase ended in 2022–2023.
