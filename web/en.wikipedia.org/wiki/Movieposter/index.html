Movieposter

Movieposter (also Movie Poster) is an artwork by the British artist Banksy. Dated to 2008, it depicts the famous image of Vincent Vega and Jules Winnfield — the two hitmen played by John Travolta and Samuel L. Jackson in Quentin Tarantino's 1994 film Pulp Fiction — pointing bananas instead of guns, in the artist's characteristic stencilled style.

The original mural was painted near the Old Street tube station in London; Transport for London eventually painted over it, following its policy on graffiti. Early prints of the image nonetheless circulated widely, and Movieposter became one of Banksy's better-known reworkings of cinema imagery, part of his long-running practice of subverting pop-cultural icons with comic substitutions.

Screen prints of the work in signed and unsigned editions have been sold at auction, and the image is often cited as an example of the crossover between street art and the commercial print market in the 2000s.
