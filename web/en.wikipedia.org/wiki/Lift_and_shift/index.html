Lift and shift

Lift and shift, also known as rehosting, is a strategy for migrating an application to the cloud by moving it from one environment to another with minimal or no changes to its architecture or code. It is one of the common migration strategies sometimes summarised among the "R" strategies of cloud migration, such as rehost, replatform, and refactor.

Approach

In a lift-and-shift migration, servers, virtual machines, data, and dependencies are copied to cloud infrastructure, typically using automated migration tools. The application continues to run much as it did on premises, but on cloud compute, storage, and networking resources.

Advantages and disadvantages

The approach is fast and comparatively low-risk because little redesign is required, making it attractive for organisations under time pressure, for example when a data centre lease is ending. However, because the application is not redesigned for cloud-native features, it may not gain the elasticity, managed services, or cost optimisation of a refactored application, and it may carry over inefficient resource usage, sometimes called "lift and shift, then drift".

Follow-on optimisation

Many organisations treat lift and shift as a first phase, replatforming or refactoring workloads after they are running in the cloud. Cost management, rightsizing instances, and adopting managed databases are common second steps.
