M-Tel Masters

The M-Tel Masters was an annual international chess tournament held in Sofia, Bulgaria, from 2005 to 2009. Sponsored by the Bulgarian mobile operator Mobiltel, it brought together some of the world's strongest grandmasters in a double round-robin format.

M-Tel Masters
Location: Sofia, Bulgaria
Years: 2005–2009
Format: Double round-robin
Sponsor: Mobiltel

History

The inaugural edition in 2005 was won by Veselin Topalov, who went on to become FIDE World Champion later that year. Topalov also won the 2006 and 2007 editions, while Vassily Ivanchuk triumphed in 2008 and Alexei Shirov in 2009.

Rules

The tournament was notable for its "Sofia rules", which discouraged short draws: players were not permitted to agree a draw without the arbiter's approval, a measure intended to promote fighting chess.
