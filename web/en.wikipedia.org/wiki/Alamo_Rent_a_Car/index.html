Alamo Rent a Car

Alamo Rent a Car is an American car rental agency headquartered in Clayton, Missouri. Founded in 1974 in Orlando, Florida, it became one of the largest rental brands in North America, oriented particularly toward leisure travellers, and is today owned by Enterprise Holdings, which also operates the Enterprise Rent-A-Car and National Car Rental brands.

History

Alamo opened its first locations in Florida in 1974 and expanded through airport concessions aimed at vacation travellers, popularising innovations such as unlimited mileage and pioneering online booking and self-service check-in kiosks. In 1996 it was acquired by Republic Industries' Automotive Rental Group (later ANC Rental), which also owned National Car Rental. ANC's 2001 bankruptcy led to the brands being sold in 2003 to an investment group, and in 2007 Enterprise's parent, the Taylor family of St. Louis, acquired Alamo and National, forming Enterprise Holdings.

Operations

Alamo serves major airports and tourist destinations in the United States, Canada, Mexico, the Caribbean and Latin America, and through partners in Europe and elsewhere. It is the official rental car brand of Walt Disney World and Disneyland, and markets to families and leisure customers, complementing National's business-travel focus within the Enterprise portfolio.
