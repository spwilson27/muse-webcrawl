Mark Millar (born 24 December 1969) is a Scottish comic book writer known for his work for DC Comics and Marvel Comics, and for his creator-owned Millarworld titles, many of which have been adapted into films.

Career

Millar began writing for the British anthology 2000 AD in the early 1990s. He gained prominence at DC Comics with Superman Adventures and, with artist Frank Quitely, The Authority. At Marvel he co-created the Ultimate Marvel line's flagship titles, writing Ultimate X-Men and The Ultimates, and later the crossover event Civil War (2006–2007), one of Marvel's best-selling comics of the decade.

Millarworld

Millar's creator-owned imprint Millarworld produced Wanted, Kick-Ass, Kingsman: The Secret Service, Superior and Jupiter's Legacy, all adapted for film or television. In 2017 Netflix acquired Millarworld, its first acquisition of a comic book company.

Style

Millar's writing is noted for cinematic pacing, widescreen action and provocative, satirical takes on superheroes.
