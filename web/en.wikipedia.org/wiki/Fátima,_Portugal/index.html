Fátima is a city and parish in the municipality of Ourém, in the district of Santarém in central Portugal. It is one of the most important centres of Catholic pilgrimage in the world, owing to the Marian apparitions reported there in 1917. Millions of pilgrims visit the Sanctuary of Fátima each year, above all on 13 May and 13 October, the anniversaries of the first and last apparitions.

The apparitions of 1917

Between 13 May and 13 October 1917, three shepherd children — Lúcia dos Santos and her cousins Francisco and Jacinta Marto — reported that a lady "brighter than the sun" appeared to them at the Cova da Iria, a field outside the village, on the thirteenth day of six consecutive months. The figure, later identified as Our Lady of Fátima, is said to have entrusted the children with prayers and with three secrets concerning war, Russia, and the fate of the Church. The final apparition on 13 October 1917 was accompanied by the so-called Miracle of the Sun, witnessed by a crowd estimated in the tens of thousands and reported in the press of the day. The Catholic Church recognised the apparitions as worthy of belief in 1930; Francisco and Jacinta Marto were canonised by Pope Francis in 2017, the centenary year.

The sanctuary

The Sanctuary of Fátima comprises the Basilica of Our Lady of the Rosary (built 1928–1953), crowned by a 65-metre tower, which contains the tombs of the three seers; the Chapel of the Apparitions, built on the site of the Cova da Iria appearances; the vast open esplanade used for processions and candlelit vigils; and the Basilica of the Holy Trinity, consecrated in 2007, one of the largest churches in the world, seating nearly 9,000 people. A section of the Berlin Wall is preserved at the sanctuary entrance.

Pilgrimage

Pilgrims traditionally approach the Chapel of the Apparitions on their knees along the esplanade's marble path, attend the nightly candlelight procession, and visit nearby Aljustrel, the children's home village, and the Via Sacra through the Valinhos, where a further apparition is reported to have occurred in August 1917. Popes John Paul II, Benedict XVI and Francis have all visited Fátima; John Paul II credited Our Lady of Fátima with saving his life in the 1981 assassination attempt, which took place on 13 May.
