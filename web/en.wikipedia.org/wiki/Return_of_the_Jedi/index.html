Return of the Jedi (also known as Star Wars: Episode VI – Return of the Jedi) is a 1983 American epic space opera film directed by Richard Marquand from a screenplay by Lawrence Kasdan and George Lucas. It is the third film in the original Star Wars trilogy and the sixth chronological chapter of the saga.

Plot

A year after the events of The Empire Strikes Back, Luke Skywalker and his friends rescue Han Solo from the crime lord Jabba the Hutt on Tatooine. The Rebel Alliance learns that the Empire is building a second Death Star near the forest moon of Endor. While a strike team led by Han disables the station's shield generator with the help of the native Ewoks, Luke confronts Darth Vader and Emperor Palpatine aboard the station; Vader, redeemed by his son, destroys the Emperor at the cost of his own life.

Reception

The film was a major box-office success, grossing over $475 million worldwide on release, and won an Academy Award for Best Visual Effects. Reviews were positive though some critics considered it weaker than its predecessors; the Ewoks proved divisive, but the film's climax is widely praised.
