Pauri

Pauri is a town and municipal board in Pauri Garhwal district in the Indian state of Uttarakhand. It serves as the headquarters of the Garhwal division and is noted for its views of the snow-covered Himalayan peaks.

Geography

Perched on a ridge at roughly 1,800 metres, Pauri overlooks a panorama stretching from Chaukhamba to Trishul and Nanda Devi on clear days. Oak, pine and rhododendron forests surround the town.

Tourism

Nearby attractions include the Kandoliya temple, the Chaukhamba viewpoint, Kyunkaleshwar Mahadev temple and the hill station of Khirsu, about 19 km away.
