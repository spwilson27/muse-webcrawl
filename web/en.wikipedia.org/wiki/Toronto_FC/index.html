Toronto FC

Toronto FC is a Canadian professional soccer club based in Toronto, Ontario, that competes in Major League Soccer (MLS) as a member of the Eastern Conference. The club joined MLS in 2007 as its first Canadian expansion franchise and plays its home matches at BMO Field on the grounds of Exhibition Place.

History

Owned by Maple Leaf Sports & Entertainment, Toronto FC began play in the 2007 season and quickly developed one of the league's most passionate supporter bases, with groups such as the Red Patch Boys and Inebriatti. The early years on the pitch were difficult, but the club's fortunes changed dramatically in the mid-2010s with the signings of designated players including Jermain Defoe, Michael Bradley, Jozy Altidore, and Italian international Sebastian Giovinco.

The 2017 season was the finest in club history: Toronto won the Supporters' Shield with a then-record 69 points, captured the Canadian Championship, and defeated the Seattle Sounders 2–0 in the MLS Cup Final at BMO Field to complete the first domestic treble in MLS history. The club also reached the final of the 2018 CONCACAF Champions League, losing to Chivas Guadalajara on penalties.

Stadium

BMO Field opened in 2007 as a soccer-specific stadium and was expanded to roughly 30,000 seats for the 2016 season. It has hosted MLS Cups, the 2016 Grey Cup, and international fixtures.

Honours

MLS Cup (2017), Supporters' Shield (2017), and multiple Canadian Championship titles (Voyageurs Cup).
