Honda Integra

The Honda Integra is an automobile produced by the Japanese manufacturer Honda from 1985 to 2006 and again from 2021. It was sold in North America under the Acura brand, first as the Acura Integra and, in its revived form, as a liftback successor to the Acura ILX.

ManufacturerHonda
Production1985–2006; 2021–present
ClassCompact car

History

The original Integra was a sporty compact offered as a hatchback, coupé and sedan, and the high-performance Type R version of the third generation is widely regarded as one of the finest front-wheel-drive sports cars ever made. The fourth-generation model was sold in Japan as the Integra and in North America as the Acura RSX. In 2021 Honda revived the Integra name in China, and in 2022 Acura relaunched the Integra in North America as a five-door liftback related to the Honda Civic.
