The Eras Tour was the sixth headlining concert tour by the American singer-songwriter Taylor Swift. Conceived as a retrospective journey through the "musical eras" of her career, the tour began on March 17, 2023, in Glendale, Arizona, and concluded on December 8, 2024, in Vancouver, Canada, after 149 shows across five continents. With a gross exceeding US$2 billion, it became the highest-grossing concert tour of all time and the first tour ever to surpass $1 billion and then $2 billion in revenue.

Conception and show

Swift announced the tour in November 2022, following the release of her tenth studio album Midnights. Because her previous tours had been curtailed by the COVID-19 pandemic and she had released several albums since, including Folklore, Evermore and her re-recorded "Taylor's Version" albums, the Eras Tour was structured as a sequence of acts each representing one album or "era", with distinctive staging, costumes and choreography. The concert ran for over three and a half hours with a set list of around 44 songs, including a rotating "surprise songs" acoustic section. In 2024 the set list was restructured to add an act for her eleventh album, The Tortured Poets Department.

Ticketing and impact

The tour's North American on-sale in November 2022 crashed Ticketmaster's systems amid unprecedented demand, prompting a U.S. Senate hearing and renewed scrutiny of the Live Nation–Ticketmaster merger. Economists and journalists described the tour's local economic effects as "Swiftonomics": host cities reported surges in hotel occupancy, transport use and consumer spending, and the U.S. Federal Reserve's Beige Book noted the tour's effect on regional economies. Cultural phenomena around the tour included fan friendship-bracelet trading and seismic activity measured at concerts in Seattle from dancing crowds.

Film

A concert film, Taylor Swift: The Eras Tour, released in October 2023 through an unusual direct distribution deal with AMC Theatres, became the highest-grossing concert film of all time, earning over $260 million worldwide; an extended version later streamed on Disney+.
