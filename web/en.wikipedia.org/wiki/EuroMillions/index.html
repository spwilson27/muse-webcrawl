EuroMillions is a transnational lottery held across several European countries. The first draw took place on 7 February 2004 in Paris, organised initially by the French, Spanish and British national lotteries; six further countries joined in October 2004, bringing the total of participating nations to nine, with tickets also sold by licensed operators elsewhere.

Game format

Players select five main numbers from 1 to 50 and two "Lucky Star" numbers from 1 to 12. Draws take place every Tuesday and Friday evening in Paris. Matching all seven numbers wins the jackpot, which starts at €17 million and rolls over when not won, subject to a cap that rises in increments after each capped jackpot is reached or rolls.

History

The concept of a pan-European lottery with very large jackpots was developed in the late 1990s. The United Kingdom, France and Spain launched the game in February 2004, followed by Austria, Belgium, Ireland, Luxembourg, Portugal and Switzerland. Special "Superdraw" events periodically guarantee enhanced jackpots of €130 million or more.

Records

The largest jackpots have exceeded €230 million under the raised cap. A United Kingdom ticket holder won £195 million in July 2022, then the largest single prize in the game's history. Winnings are paid tax-free in most participating countries, although Switzerland and Spain apply taxes to large prizes.
