Glen Canyon

LocationUtah / Arizona, USA
StatusPartly submerged by Lake Powell
[Image: Smooth sandstone walls of Glen Canyon]
Smooth sandstone walls of Glen Canyon

Glen Canyon is the canyon of the Colorado River in southern Utah and northern Arizona, largely submerged beneath Lake Powell since the completion of Glen Canyon Dam in 1966, and the centrepiece of Glen Canyon National Recreation Area.

History

Before flooding, Glen Canyon was celebrated as a serene counterpart to the Grand Canyon: smooth sandstone walls, hanging gardens, side canyons such as the Escalante arm, and Ancestral Pueblo sites. The Sierra Club's 1963 book The Place No One Knew, with Eliot Porter's photographs, elegised it after the dam decision, taken as a compromise to save Dinosaur National Monument. Drought-driven falls in Lake Powell since 2000 have re-exposed reaches of the canyon, reviving debate about its restoration.
