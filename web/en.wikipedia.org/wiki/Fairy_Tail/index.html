Fairy Tail

Fairy Tail is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Hiro Mashima. It was serialized in Kodansha's Weekly Shōnen Magazine from August 2006 to July 2017, with its chapters collected in 63 tankōbon volumes. The story follows the adventures of Natsu Dragneel, a member of the popular wizard guild Fairy Tail, as he searches for the dragon Igneel.

Plot

In the fictional world of Earth Land, magic is commonplace and wizards join guilds to take on jobs. Celestial wizard Lucy Heartfilia joins the rambunctious Fairy Tail guild and teams with fire dragon slayer Natsu Dragneel, the flying cat Happy, ice wizard Gray Fullbuster, and requip wizard Erza Scarlet.

Media

The manga was adapted into an anime series by A-1 Pictures and Satelight, running from 2009 to 2019 with 328 episodes, plus two animated films and a sequel manga, Fairy Tail: 100 Years Quest, launched in 2018.
