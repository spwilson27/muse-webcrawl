Tullu Demtu

Tullu Demtu
Elevation: 4,377 m
Location: Bale Mountains, Oromia, Ethiopia
Parent range: Sanetti Plateau

Tullu Demtu is the second-highest mountain in Ethiopia, rising to 4,377 metres (14,360 ft) above sea level. It stands on the Sanetti Plateau in the Bale Mountains of the Oromia Region in the southeast of the country, within Bale Mountains National Park.

Geography

Only Ras Dashen in the Simien Mountains exceeds Tullu Demtu in height. The mountain is an eroded volcanic massif; the Sanetti Plateau around it is the largest area of afroalpine habitat on the African continent and is home to the endangered Ethiopian wolf. A gravel road crossing the plateau, among the highest all-weather roads in Africa, passes close beneath the summit, making Tullu Demtu one of the most accessible high peaks on the continent.
