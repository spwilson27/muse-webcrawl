Nguyễn Văn Linh

Nguyễn Văn Linh (1 July 1915 – 27 April 1998) was a Vietnamese revolutionary and politician who served as General Secretary of the Communist Party of Vietnam from 1986 to 1991. He is credited as the principal architect of Đổi Mới, the economic renovation reforms that introduced market mechanisms into Vietnam's socialist economy.

Early life and resistance

Born Nguyễn Văn Cúc in Hưng Yên Province, he joined the communist movement as a teenager, was imprisoned on Côn Đảo by the French, and later became a leading Viet Cong organiser in South Vietnam during the Vietnam War, heading the party organisation in Saigon.

Đổi Mới

After reunification he led Ho Chi Minh City and experimented with pragmatic economic policies. Elevated to General Secretary at the Sixth Party Congress in December 1986 amid economic crisis, he launched Đổi Mới: price liberalisation, encouragement of private enterprise and foreign investment, and greater openness in the press. He retired in 1991 and remained an influential party adviser until his death in 1998.
