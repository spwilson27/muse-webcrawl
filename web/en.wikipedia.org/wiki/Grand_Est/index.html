Grand Est

Grand Est ("Great East") is an administrative region of north-eastern France, created on 1 January 2016 by the merger of the former regions of Alsace, Lorraine, and Champagne-Ardenne. Its capital and largest city is Strasbourg, seat of the European Parliament.

Geography

The region borders Belgium, Luxembourg, Germany, and Switzerland, and includes the Vosges mountains, the Rhine plain, and the Champagne vineyards. It comprises ten departments, among them Bas-Rhin, Haut-Rhin, Moselle, Meurthe-et-Moselle, Marne, and Ardennes.

History

The merger, part of France's 2014 territorial reform, was initially unpopular in Alsace, where a European Collectivity of Alsace was created in 2021 to recover some competencies. The provisional name "Grand Est" was adopted in 2016 following an online consultation.
