Oil reserves in Iraq

Oil reserves in Iraq are considered the world's fifth-largest proven oil reserves, with approximately 145 billion barrels. As a result of military occupation and civil unrest, development of Iraq's reserves has been delayed, and detailed geological surveys are incomplete.

Major fields include Rumaila, West Qurna, Majnoon, Kirkuk, and Zubair, mostly in the south and north of the country. Oil accounts for the overwhelming majority of Iraqi government revenue. The industry is overseen by the Ministry of Oil and state companies, with international oil companies operating under technical service contracts.
