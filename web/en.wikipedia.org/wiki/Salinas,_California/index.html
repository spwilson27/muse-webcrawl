Salinas is a city in the US state of California and the county seat of Monterey County. It is the largest municipality in the county and the principal city of the Salinas Valley, one of the most productive agricultural regions in the United States. Its population was 163,542 at the 2020 census. Salinas is widely known as the birthplace of Nobel Prize-winning author John Steinbeck, who set many of his novels in the surrounding valley.

History

The Salinas area was inhabited by the Ohlone and Esselen peoples before Spanish colonisation; the name derives from the nearby Salinas River and from salinas, Spanish for salt marshes. Rancho lands granted in the Mexican era were subdivided after American annexation, and the town was laid out in 1868 on the stagecoach route; it incorporated in 1874 and became the Monterey county seat in 1872, supplanting Monterey. The arrival of the Southern Pacific Railroad and the growth of the sugar beet and lettuce industries — Salinas shipped the valley's crops by refrigerated rail car across the country — made it a boomtown of California agriculture in the early 20th century.

Agriculture

The Salinas Valley produces the majority of the United States' lettuce supply along with strawberries, broccoli, celery, cauliflower, wine grapes, and artichokes, earning the region its title of "Salad Bowl of the World". Agriculture and food processing dominate the local economy, and the industry relies heavily on a predominantly Latino farm workforce, whose labour struggles — including those associated with the United Farm Workers movement — form an important part of the city's history.

Culture

Steinbeck's presence pervades the city: his childhood home is preserved as a museum and restaurant, the National Steinbeck Center opened in Oldtown Salinas in 1998, and murals and festivals commemorate his works, including East of Eden, set largely in Salinas itself. The California Rodeo Salinas, held since 1911, is one of the largest rodeos in the state. Oldtown Salinas, centred on Main Street, contains the Fox California Theater and a cluster of historic buildings and has undergone periodic revitalisation. The city is also known in motorsport through WeatherTech Raceway Laguna Seca, located between Salinas and Monterey.

Geography and climate

Salinas lies 18 miles (29 km) inland from Monterey Bay, in the lower Salinas Valley between the Gabilan Range to the east and the Santa Lucia Range to the west. It has a mild Mediterranean climate strongly moderated by marine air from the bay, with cool summers, frequent afternoon winds, and most of its roughly 15 inches (380 mm) of annual rain falling in winter.
