The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom is a 2023 action-adventure game developed and published by Nintendo for the Nintendo Switch. The sequel to Breath of the Wild (2017), it expands that game's Hyrule with sky islands floating above the kingdom and a vast subterranean region known as the Depths.

Gameplay

As Link, the player explores the three-layered world after the cataclysmic "Upheaval" unleashed by the revived Demon King Ganondorf. New abilities granted by Link's replacement right arm define the design: Ultrahand and the Zonai device system let players build vehicles, machines and contraptions; Fuse combines weapons with materials; Ascend passes through ceilings; and Recall reverses an object's motion. Link allies with the Sages of the Hyrulean peoples and pieces together, through Princess Zelda's dragon-transformed memories, a war fought in Hyrule's founding age.

Reception

The game received universal acclaim, with reviewers praising its construction systems and the scope of its world while debating its similarities to its predecessor. It sold more than 10 million copies in its first three days and over 20 million within its first year, won numerous awards, and was a Game of the Year nominee at The Game Awards 2023, winning Best Action/Adventure.
