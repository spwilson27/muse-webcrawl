Chang and Eng Bunker

Chang Bunker and Eng Bunker (11 May 1811 – 17 January 1874) were Siamese-American conjoined twins born in Siam (Thailand), joined at the sternum by cartilage and fused livers. Their fame as exhibition performers gave rise to the term "Siamese twins".

Life

Brought to the United States in 1829, they toured for years before settling in North Carolina, becoming naturalised US citizens, farmers and slaveholders. In 1843 they married sisters Adelaide and Sarah Ann Yates and had a total of 21 children. They died on the same night in 1874, Eng a few hours after Chang.
