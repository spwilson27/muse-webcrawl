Ffestiniog power station

The Ffestiniog power station is a pumped-storage hydroelectric power station near Ffestiniog in Gwynedd, north Wales, United Kingdom. Commissioned in 1963, it was the first major pumped-storage scheme in the UK, with an installed capacity of about 360 megawatts from four generating units.

Description

The scheme uses two reservoirs at different elevations. The upper reservoir, Llyn Stwlan, was created by damming a mountain lake high above the lower reservoir at Tanygrisiau. When electricity demand is high, water flows downhill through tunnels and turbines to generate power; at times of low demand, surplus electricity pumps the water back up to be used again. The station can reach full output within about a minute, making it valuable for responding to sudden surges in demand.

History

Built by the Central Electricity Generating Board, the station was officially opened in 1963. Today it is operated by First Hydro Company. A visitor centre was formerly located at Tanygrisiau. Part of the Ffestiniog Railway, a narrow-gauge heritage line, was diverted during construction of the lower reservoir.
