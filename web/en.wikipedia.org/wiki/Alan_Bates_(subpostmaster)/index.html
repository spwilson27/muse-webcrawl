Alan Bates (born 1954) is a former British subpostmaster who led the campaign for justice for subpostmasters wrongly blamed for accounting shortfalls caused by the Post Office's Horizon computer system. He founded the Justice for Subpostmasters Alliance in 2009.

Background

Bates ran a post office in Craig-y-Don, Llandudno, Wales, from 1998. After refusing to accept responsibility for unexplained shortfalls shown by the Horizon system, his contract was terminated in 2003. He spent two decades campaigning, bringing together hundreds of affected subpostmasters, and was the lead claimant in the group litigation Bates & Others v Post Office Ltd, whose 2019 High Court judgments established that bugs in Horizon could cause the apparent losses. The case paved the way for criminal convictions to be overturned and compensation schemes to be established.

Recognition

His story was dramatised in the 2024 ITV series Mr Bates vs The Post Office, which brought the scandal to mass public attention. Bates declined an OBE in 2024 while former Post Office chief executive Paula Vennells retained her CBE (later returned), and was subsequently knighted.
