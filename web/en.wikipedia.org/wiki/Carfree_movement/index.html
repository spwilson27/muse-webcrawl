Carfree movement

The carfree movement is a network of individuals, organisations, and cities advocating reduced dependence on private automobiles in favour of walking, cycling, and public transport. It promotes car-free places, car-free days, and urban design that limits car use.

Carfree movement
Focus: Reduced car dependence
Key events: World Carfree Day (22 September)
Concepts: Car-free city, low-traffic neighbourhood

History

Ideas of pedestrianised city centres gained ground in Europe from the 1960s, with cities such as Groningen and Copenhagen redesigning streets for cycling. Writers including J.H. Crawford (Carfree Cities) and organisations such as the World Carfree Network helped link local campaigns internationally.

Forms

The movement spans car-free housing developments (for example in Freiburg's Vauban district), car-free islands and old towns, ciclovía open-streets events, critical mass rides, and policies such as congestion charging and parking restraint.
