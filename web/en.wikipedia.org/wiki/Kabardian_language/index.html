Kabardian language

Kabardian (Kabardian: Къэбэрдейбзэ, Qabardeybza), also known as East Circassian, is a Northwest Caucasian language spoken by the Kabardian subgroup of the Circassian people. It is an official language of the Kabardino-Balkaria and Karachay-Cherkessia republics of Russia and is also spoken by diaspora communities in Turkey, Jordan and elsewhere.

Kabardian is known for its extremely small vowel inventory — often analysed as just two or three vowels — contrasted with one of the largest consonant inventories of any language, with numerous ejective, pharyngealised and labialised consonants.

Together with Adyghe (West Circassian), it forms the Circassian branch of the Northwest Caucasian family. It is written in a Cyrillic-based alphabet in Russia and in Latin or Arabic scripts historically in the diaspora.
