Satoru Nakajima

Satoru Nakajima (中嶋 悟, Nakajima Satoru, born 23 February 1953) is a Japanese former racing driver. He is a five-time Japanese Formula Two champion and was the first Japanese driver to score points in Formula One, driving for Lotus and Tyrrell between 1987 and 1991.

Career

Nakajima dominated Japanese Formula Two (later Formula 3000) in the early 1980s, winning five consecutive championships from 1981 to 1986 (with the series title sequence spanning those seasons). His success, backed by Honda, led to a Formula One seat with Team Lotus in 1987 as teammate to Ayrton Senna.

He scored his first championship points with a sixth place at the 1987 San Marino Grand Prix and recorded a best finish of fourth at the 1989 Australian Grand Prix in heavy rain, setting the fastest lap. Nakajima moved to Tyrrell for 1990–1991 before retiring from Formula One at the end of 1991.

Later life

After retiring, Nakajima founded his own team, Nakajima Racing, which competes in Japanese series such as Super Formula and Super GT. His son Kazuki Nakajima also became a racing driver, winning the 24 Hours of Le Mans three times with Toyota. He is also familiar to many through the classic video game F-1 Hero / Nakajima Satoru Kanshū F1 Hero.
