Jurassic Park (film)

Film information
Directed by: Steven Spielberg
Based on: Jurassic Park by Michael Crichton
Starring: Sam Neill, Laura Dern, Jeff Goldblum, Richard Attenborough
Music by: John Williams
Released: 1993

Jurassic Park is a 1993 American science fiction film directed by Steven Spielberg and produced by Kathleen Kennedy and Gerald R. Molen, based on Michael Crichton's 1990 novel. Set on the fictional Isla Nublar near Costa Rica, it tells of a theme park of cloned dinosaurs created by billionaire John Hammond (Richard Attenborough), whose preview tour for scientists Alan Grant (Sam Neill), Ellie Sattler (Laura Dern) and Ian Malcolm (Jeff Goldblum) descends into chaos when sabotage shuts down the park's power and security systems.

Production

The film's dinosaurs were created through a landmark combination of computer-generated imagery by Industrial Light & Magic and life-size animatronics by Stan Winston Studio, marking a revolution in visual effects. John Williams composed the celebrated score.

Reception and legacy

Jurassic Park grossed over $1 billion worldwide following its 2013 re-release, becoming the highest-grossing film of its time, and won three Academy Awards. It is regarded as a landmark in computer-generated imagery and animatronics, was selected for preservation in the United States National Film Registry in 2018, and launched a franchise of sequels beginning with The Lost World: Jurassic Park (1997).
