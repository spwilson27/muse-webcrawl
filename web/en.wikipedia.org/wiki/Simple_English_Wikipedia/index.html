The Simple English Wikipedia is an English-language edition of Wikipedia written primarily in Basic English and in a simplified form of English. It is intended for readers who need simple language — including children, students, people learning English, and readers with learning difficulties.

Characteristics

Articles on the Simple English Wikipedia use shorter sentences, a restricted vocabulary (based on the word lists of Basic English and Voice of America's Special English), and explain technical terms carefully. The project generally avoids idioms and complex grammar. Many articles are simplified versions of articles from the English Wikipedia, though the project also has original content and follows the same core policies of neutrality and verifiability, adapted to its audience.

Readership

The Simple English Wikipedia is widely used in schools and by English-language learners, and its articles frequently appear prominently in search-engine results for basic reference queries.
