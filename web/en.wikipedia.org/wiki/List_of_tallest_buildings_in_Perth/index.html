List of tallest buildings in Perth

Perth, the capital of Western Australia, has a compact high-rise skyline on the north bank of the Swan River. Its tallest building is Brookfield Place (the former City Square), completed in 2012 at 244.8 metres, followed by Central Park (249 metres to its spire, 226 metres to roof), for many years the city's tallest after its completion in 1992.
