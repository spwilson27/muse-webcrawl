Discernment

Discernment is the ability to obtain sharp perceptions or to judge well (or the activity of so doing). In the case of judgment, discernment can be psychological, moral or aesthetic in nature. The term is used in several contexts, including philosophy, religion, and everyday decision-making.

In Christianity

In Christian spirituality, discernment of spirits refers to the practice of distinguishing between influences believed to come from God, from evil spirits, or from human nature, particularly in making decisions about one's vocation or course of action. Ignatius of Loyola set out influential rules for discernment in his Spiritual Exercises. Discernment is also described in the New Testament as a spiritual gift (1 Corinthians 12:10).

Decision-making

More generally, discernment involves careful evaluation of information, motives, and consequences before reaching a judgment. It is associated with wisdom, prudence, and critical thinking, and is cultivated in fields such as law, medicine, and leadership.
