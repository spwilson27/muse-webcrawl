Hérault (Occitan: Erau) is a department in the Occitanie region of southern France, on the Mediterranean coast. It is named after the Hérault River, and its prefecture is Montpellier.

Geography

The department stretches from the Mediterranean beaches and the Étang de Thau lagoon (famous for oysters) inland to the Cévennes foothills and the Haut-Languedoc mountains. Other major towns include Béziers, Sète, Agde, and Lunel.

Economy and culture

Hérault is one of France's largest wine-producing departments, part of the Languedoc wine region. It is a popular tourist destination, with historic sites including the Canal du Midi, Saint-Guilhem-le-Désert, and the Romanesque churches of the interior. Montpellier is a major university and research city.
