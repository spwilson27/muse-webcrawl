Moana 2 (titled Vaiana 2 in most European countries) is a 2024 American animated musical adventure film produced by Walt Disney Animation Studios and released by Walt Disney Pictures. It is the sequel to Moana (2016), and stars Auliʻi Cravalho and Dwayne Johnson reprising their roles as Moana and Maui. The film was directed by David Derrick Jr., Jason Hand and Dana Ledoux Miller from a screenplay by Jared Bush, Miller and Bek Smith.

Plot

Three years after her first voyage, Moana is a celebrated wayfinder of Motunui. Receiving an unexpected call from her ancestors, she journeys to the far seas of Oceania with a crew — the young engineer Loto, the storyteller Moni, the grumpy farmer Kele, her rooster Heihei and pig Pua — to break the curse of the god Nalo on the hidden island of Motufetu, which once connected all the peoples of the ocean. Maui joins the quest; together they confront the bat-like guardian Matangi and Nalo's storm, and Moana, briefly killed and revived as a demigoddess by the ocean and her ancestors, raises Motufetu and reconnects the peoples.

Production and reception

The project began as a long-form streaming series for Disney+ before being redeveloped into a theatrical feature, announced by Disney CEO Bob Iger in February 2024. Songs were written by Abigail Barlow and Emily Bear, replacing the first film's Lin-Manuel Miranda. Reviews were mixed — praising the animation and music but finding the story episodic, reflecting its television origin — yet the film was a major commercial success, grossing over $1 billion worldwide, among the highest-grossing films of 2024. A live-action remake of the original Moana was separately announced with Johnson returning as Maui.
