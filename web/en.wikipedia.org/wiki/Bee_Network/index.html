The Bee Network is the integrated transport network for Greater Manchester, England, overseen by Transport for Greater Manchester (TfGM). It brings together bus services, the Manchester Metrolink tram system, and active travel (cycling and walking) routes under a single brand, recognisable by its yellow livery and bee symbol — the worker bee being a long-standing emblem of Manchester. Local rail services within Greater Manchester are planned to join the network later in the decade.

History

The Bee Network name was first used in 2018 for Greater Manchester's planned network of cycling and walking routes (the "Beelines"). Under Mayor Andy Burnham, the brand was extended to the whole public transport system as Greater Manchester became the first English city-region outside London to bring buses back under local public control through franchising, using powers in the Bus Services Act 2017.

Bus franchising

A legal challenge by bus operators was dismissed by the Court of Appeal in 2022, allowing franchising to proceed. Franchised Bee Network bus services began on 24 September 2023 in Bolton, Wigan and parts of Salford and Bury, followed by a second tranche in March 2024 covering Oldham, Rochdale, the remainder of Bury and Salford, and north Manchester. The third and final tranche, covering Stockport, Tameside, Trafford and south Manchester, began on 5 January 2025, completing the franchising of all bus services in Greater Manchester. Under franchising, TfGM sets routes, timetables and fares and contracts operators to run services, replacing the deregulated market that had operated since 1986.

Branding and fares

Franchised buses and Metrolink trams carry a yellow livery. The network introduced capped, integrated ticketing, including a £2 single bus fare cap (later £3 in line with the national scheme), "hopper" fares allowing unlimited bus travel within an hour, and combined bus-and-tram daily caps introduced in March 2025. A tap-in, tap-out contactless system operates across buses and trams.

Active travel

The Bee Network also encompasses a planned network of segregated cycling routes and pedestrian improvements across the city region, including cycle hire bikes branded under the same identity.
