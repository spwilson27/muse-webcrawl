State Council of the People's Republic of China

Formed27 September 1954
HeadquartersZhongnanhai, Beijing
HeadPremier of China
ParentNational People's Congress

The State Council of the People's Republic of China, also known as the Central People's Government, is the chief administrative authority and the executive organ of the National People's Congress, the highest organ of state power in China. It is chaired by the Premier and includes the heads of each governmental department and agency.

Organisation

The State Council consists of the Premier, Vice Premiers, State Councillors, ministers in charge of ministries and commissions, the Auditor-General, and the Secretary-General. Its executive meeting is composed of the Premier, Vice Premiers, State Councillors and the Secretary-General. The State Council directly oversees provincial-level governments and in practice maintains membership interlocking with the top levels of the Chinese Communist Party.

Functions

The council's functions include implementing laws enacted by the National People's Congress and its Standing Committee, drafting administrative regulations, formulating economic and social development plans and the state budget, and directing the work of local governments. It is the highest executive body of state administration in China.
