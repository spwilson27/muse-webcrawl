Suddenlink Communications was an American telecommunications subsidiary of Altice USA, trading in cable television, broadband, IP telephony, home security, and advertising services. It served mainly smaller and mid-sized communities in states including Texas, West Virginia, Louisiana, Arkansas, Oklahoma, Arizona, and North Carolina.

History

The company was founded in 2003 as Cebridge Connections, assembling cable systems divested by larger operators. It adopted the Suddenlink name in 2006 and grew by acquisition to become the seventh-largest cable operator in the United States, with roughly 1.4 million residential customers at its peak.

In 2015, the European telecommunications group Altice, controlled by Patrick Drahi, agreed to acquire a controlling stake in Suddenlink in a deal valuing the company at about $9.1 billion; Altice USA subsequently also acquired Cablevision (Optimum). In August 2022, Altice USA retired the Suddenlink brand, rebranding its services under the Optimum name to unify its footprint.
