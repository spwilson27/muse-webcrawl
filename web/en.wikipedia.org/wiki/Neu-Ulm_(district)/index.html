Neu-Ulm (district)

Neu-Ulm is a Landkreis (district) in Swabia, Bavaria, Germany. It is bounded by the districts of Günzburg, Unterallgäu and Oberallgäu, by the Austrian state of Vorarlberg in the far south, and by the state of Baden-Württemberg across the Iller river. The district capital is the city of Neu-Ulm.

Geography

The district lies east of the Danube and Iller rivers, opposite the city of Ulm in Baden-Württemberg. Its northern part is comparatively flat and urbanised, while the south rises toward the foothills of the Allgäu.

Towns and municipalities

Towns include Neu-Ulm, Illertissen, Vöhringen, Weißenhorn and Senden. The district was established in 1972 by merging the former districts of Neu-Ulm and Illertissen with parts of neighbouring districts.
