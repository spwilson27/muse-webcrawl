Declaration of Astana

The Declaration of Astana is a political declaration adopted in Astana, Kazakhstan, associated with international discussions on security, cooperation and regional stability in Central Asia. Such declarations typically reaffirm commitments to sovereignty, territorial integrity and peaceful settlement of disputes.

Background

Astana has hosted numerous diplomatic summits, including talks on Syria, Eurasian integration and interfaith dialogue. Declarations issued there reflect Kazakhstan's role as a mediator.

Content

The text generally emphasises multilateralism, non-interference, economic cooperation and respect for international law.

Significance

Declarations of this kind are non-binding but signal shared positions among participating states.
