Rockschool

Rockschool, officially RSL Awards Ltd, is an examination board specialising in contemporary music and the performing arts. Founded in 1991 by Norton York, it was the first exam board to offer graded qualifications in rock and pop instruments — electric guitar, bass, drums, vocals and keyboards — alongside grades in music production, musical theatre, dance and acting.

Qualifications

Rockschool offers graded exams from Debut to Grade 8, performance certificates, and vocational qualifications and diplomas for the creative industries. Its qualifications are regulated in the United Kingdom and recognised internationally, with examinations administered in dozens of countries. Higher grades carry UCAS tariff points for university entry in the UK. In 2016 the organisation rebranded its awarding activities under the name RSL Awards, retaining Rockschool as the name of its music qualifications.
