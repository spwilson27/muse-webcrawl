Nel Noddings

Nel Noddings (January 19, 1929 – August 25, 2022) was an American feminist philosopher and educator best known for her work on the ethics of care and on moral education. She held positions at Stanford University, where she was the Jacks Professor of Child Education, and later at Teachers College, Columbia University.

Ethics of care

In her influential book Caring: A Feminine Approach to Ethics and Moral Education (1984), Noddings argued that ethics should be grounded in natural caring — the receptive, responsive relation between the "one-caring" and the "cared-for" — rather than in abstract principles and rules. Ethical caring, on her account, grows out of and must remain answerable to this natural caring. Her work, alongside that of Carol Gilligan, helped establish care ethics as a major field within feminist philosophy.

Education

Drawing on her early career as a school mathematics teacher, Noddings wrote extensively on education, including The Challenge to Care in Schools (1992) and Educating Moral People (2002), arguing that schools should organise around themes of care and that caring relations between teachers and students are a precondition of effective teaching.
