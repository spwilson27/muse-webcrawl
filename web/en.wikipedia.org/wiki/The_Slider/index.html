The Slider

Artist: T. Rex
Released: 21 July 1972
Producer: Tony Visconti

The Slider is the seventh studio album by the English rock band T. Rex, and the third since the band shortened its name from Tyrannosaurus Rex. Released in July 1972 and produced by Tony Visconti, it is considered a classic of glam rock and the peak of "T. Rextasy" in Britain.

Background

The album was largely recorded at the Château d'Hérouville in France, with sessions also in Copenhagen and Los Angeles. Marc Bolan wrote all the songs; the cover photograph of Bolan in a top hat was taken by Ringo Starr. Two UK hit singles preceded the album: "Telegram Sam" and "Metal Guru," both reaching number one.

Legacy

Although a comparatively modest success in the United States, The Slider is consistently ranked among the greatest glam rock albums; "Ballrooms of Mars" and the title track are enduring Bolan compositions, and the album has been reissued in expanded editions.
