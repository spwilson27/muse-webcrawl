Pain tolerance

Pain tolerance is the maximum level of pain that a person is able to tolerate. It differs from the pain threshold, the point at which a stimulus first becomes painful.

Factors

Tolerance varies with genetics, sex, age, prior experience, psychological state, culture, expectations, and context. It can be increased by training, distraction, and some medications, and decreased by fatigue, anxiety, and sensitisation after injury.

Measurement

Clinicians and researchers measure tolerance with tools such as the cold pressor test, pressure algometers, and dolorimeters, interpreting results alongside self-reported pain scales.
