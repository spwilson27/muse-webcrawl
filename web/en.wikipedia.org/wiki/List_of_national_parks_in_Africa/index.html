List of national parks in Africa

This is a list of national parks in Africa. The continent's protected areas include some of the world's most famous wildlife reserves, protecting savanna, rainforest, desert and mountain ecosystems and species such as elephants, lions, gorillas and rhinoceroses. The oldest, including South Africa's Kruger National Park (proclaimed 1926, with game reserves dating to 1898) and the Congo's Virunga National Park (1925), were created in the colonial era.
