Jana Gana Mana

LyricsRabindranath Tagore, 1911
MusicRabindranath Tagore, 1911
Adopted24 January 1950 (India)

"Jana Gana Mana" is the national anthem of India. Written in Bengali by the poet Rabindranath Tagore, it was first sung publicly on 27 December 1911 at the Calcutta session of the Indian National Congress. The Constituent Assembly adopted the first stanza of the five-stanza hymn as the national anthem on 24 January 1950.

Text and meaning

The anthem addresses a divine "ruler of the minds of all people" and names the regions and peoples of India — Punjab, Sindh, Gujarat, Maratha, Dravida, Utkala (Odisha), Bengal, the Vindhya and Himalaya mountains, and the Yamuna and Ganga rivers. A formal rendition takes approximately 52 seconds.

History

The song was published in the journal Tatwabodhini Patrika in January 1912 under the title "Bharoto Bhagyo Bidhata". The tune was set down with the help of the composer Jadunath Bhattacharya. In 1919 Tagore himself sang an English rendering, "The Morning Song of India", at Madanapalle. Before independence it was a leading song of the freedom movement, and the Indian National Army adopted a version as its anthem.
