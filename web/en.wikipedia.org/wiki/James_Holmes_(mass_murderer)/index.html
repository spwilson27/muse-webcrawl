James Eagan Holmes (born 13 December 1987) is an American mass murderer who carried out the 2012 Aurora theater shooting in Aurora, Colorado, killing 12 people and injuring 70 others during a midnight screening of the film The Dark Knight Rises. He was convicted in 2015 and sentenced to life imprisonment without parole.

Background

Holmes grew up in San Diego, California, and graduated from the University of California, Riverside, in 2010 with a degree in neuroscience. In 2011 he enrolled in a doctoral programme in neuroscience at the University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus in Aurora. His academic performance declined and he withdrew from the programme in June 2012, weeks before the attack. During this period he legally purchased several firearms and thousands of rounds of ammunition, and he booby-trapped his apartment with explosives, which were later safely neutralised by police.

The shooting

On 20 July 2012, Holmes entered Theater 9 of the Century 16 multiplex in Aurora during a midnight premiere, propped open an exit door, and returned wearing tactical gear. He released tear gas canisters and opened fire on the audience. Twelve people were killed and 70 injured, the largest number of casualties in a single mass shooting in modern American history at that time. Holmes was arrested in the parking lot minutes later without resistance.

Trial and sentencing

Holmes pleaded not guilty by reason of insanity. At his 2015 trial, court-appointed psychiatrists diagnosed schizotypal personality disorder and schizoaffective disorder, but the jury concluded he was legally sane at the time of the attack. He was convicted on all counts, including 24 counts of first-degree murder (two for each victim under different theories). The jury could not reach a unanimous decision on the death penalty, so he was sentenced to 12 consecutive life terms plus 3,318 years in prison. He is incarcerated in the federal prison system.
