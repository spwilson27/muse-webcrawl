Gutter

A gutter is a narrow channel, typically fixed below the edge of a roof, that collects rainwater and discharges it through downpipes (downspouts) away from a building's foundation. In British English the system of roof channels and downpipes is called guttering; roof gutters may be made of aluminium, galvanised steel, copper, zinc, uPVC or cast iron.

The term also denotes the open drainage channel at the side of a street (the kerbside gutter), the space between facing pages in bookbinding, the trough beside a bowling lane, and the blank space between columns or panels in graphic design and comics. Roof gutters require periodic clearing of leaves and debris to prevent overflow and ice dams.
