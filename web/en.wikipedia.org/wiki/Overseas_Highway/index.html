Overseas Highway

The Seven Mile Bridge, part of the Overseas Highway

Length: 113 mi (182 km)

Route: U.S. Route 1

From: Florida City, Florida

To: Key West, Florida

Bridges: 42

The Overseas Highway is a 113-mile (182 km) highway carrying U.S. Route 1 through the Florida Keys. It runs from Florida City on the mainland to Key West, crossing 42 bridges between the islands, including the famous Seven Mile Bridge. Most of the highway follows the former roadbed of the Overseas Railroad, the Key West Extension of the Florida East Coast Railway completed in 1912 and destroyed by the Labor Day hurricane of 1935.

History

After the hurricane, the railroad was not rebuilt; the state of Florida purchased the right-of-way and converted the surviving bridges for automobile use. The highway opened in 1938, originally as State Road 4A and a toll road (the Overseas Road and Toll Bridge District); tolls were removed in 1954. Major bridge replacements were completed in the 1980s, including the modern Seven Mile Bridge (1982), while many of the old railroad bridges remain alongside, some serving as fishing piers and pedestrian ways.

The highway is an All-American Road in the National Scenic Byways Program and is sometimes called "The Highway That Goes to Sea".
