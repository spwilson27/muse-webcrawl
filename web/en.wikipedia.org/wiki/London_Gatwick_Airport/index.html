London Gatwick Airport

London Gatwick Airport
IATA: LGW
Location: West Sussex, England

London Gatwick Airport is a major international airport south of London, near Crawley in West Sussex. It is the second-busiest airport in the United Kingdom by passenger traffic after Heathrow.

History

Gatwick developed from a 1930s aerodrome and was rebuilt as a London airport in the 1950s. It has North and South Terminals linked by a shuttle, and for many years operated with a single main runway.

Transport

The airport has a railway station on the Brighton Main Line with Gatwick Express and other services to London Victoria and London Bridge, and lies near the M23 motorway.
