GRACE-FO (Gravity Recovery and Climate Experiment Follow-On) is a partnership between NASA and the German Research Centre for Geosciences (GFZ) that continues the work of the original GRACE mission, measuring monthly changes in Earth's gravity field to track the movement of water mass around the planet.

Mission

The mission consists of two identical satellites flying about 220 kilometres apart in a polar orbit roughly 490 kilometres above Earth. Precise measurements of the changing distance between them — by microwave ranging, supplemented by an experimental laser ranging interferometer — reveal variations in gravity caused by shifting water, ice, and solid Earth.

Science

GRACE-FO data are used to monitor groundwater depletion, ice-sheet and glacier mass loss in Greenland and Antarctica, sea-level change, drought, and ocean currents.
