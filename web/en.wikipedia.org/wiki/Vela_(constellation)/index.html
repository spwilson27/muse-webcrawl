Vela (constellation)

Vela is a constellation in the southern sky, representing the sails of the ship Argo Navis. It was once part of the larger constellation Argo Navis, which was divided by Nicolas-Louis de Lacaille in the 1750s into Vela, Carina (the keel), and Puppis (the stern).

Vela lies in the Milky Way and contains several notable objects, including the Vela Supernova Remnant and the Vela Pulsar, one of the brightest pulsars in the sky, and the open cluster IC 2391 (the Omicron Velorum Cluster). Its brightest star is Gamma Velorum, a complex multiple system containing one of the brightest Wolf–Rayet stars known.

Neighbours and visibility

Vela borders Antlia, Pyxis, Puppis, Carina, and Centaurus. It is best visible from southern latitudes in late summer evenings, though parts can be seen from the southern United States and Mediterranean.
