Gödel metric

The Gödel metric, also known as the Gödel solution, is an exact solution of the Einstein field equations of general relativity in which the stress–energy tensor contains dust and a negative cosmological constant. It was published by the logician Kurt Gödel in 1949 as a gift for Albert Einstein's 70th birthday.

The solution describes a rotating universe. Its most startling feature is the existence of closed timelike curves through every point, which would in principle permit forms of time travel and which challenge notions of causality. The Gödel universe does not expand and does not match observations of our universe, but it demonstrated that general relativity does not by itself exclude time travel into the past.
