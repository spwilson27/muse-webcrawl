Aeroflot — Russian Airlines (Russian: Аэрофлот — Российские авиалинии), commonly known as Aeroflot, is the flag carrier and the largest airline of Russia. Founded in 1923 as the civil air fleet of the Soviet Union, it is one of the world's oldest airlines. During the Soviet era Aeroflot was the national airline of the USSR and grew into the largest airline in the world, operating both civil aviation and, in effect, all Soviet air transport.

History

Aeroflot traces its origins to Dobrolyot, founded in 1923; the name Aeroflot was adopted in 1932. After the Second World War it operated an enormous network of Soviet-built aircraft, including the Tupolev Tu-104, one of the first jet airliners in scheduled service, and the Ilyushin Il-62 long-haul jet. Following the dissolution of the Soviet Union in 1991, Aeroflot was restructured as a joint-stock company and re-equipped largely with Airbus and Boeing aircraft.

Operations

The airline is majority state-owned, is based at Sheremetyevo International Airport in Moscow, and was a member of the SkyTeam alliance until its membership was suspended in 2022. The Aeroflot Group includes the low-cost carrier Pobeda and the regional airline Rossiya.
