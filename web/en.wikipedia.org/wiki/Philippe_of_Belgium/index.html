Philippe of Belgium

Reign: 21 July 2013 – present
Born: April 15, 1960, Laeken, Brussels, Belgium
Predecessor: Albert II
Spouse: Mathilde d'Udekem d'Acoz
Heir apparent: Princess Elisabeth, Duchess of Brabant

Philippe has been King of the Belgians since 2013.

Philippe (born 15 April 1960) is King of the Belgians. He is the eldest child of King Albert II and Queen Paola and ascended the throne on 21 July 2013, following his father's abdication for health reasons. As heir apparent he held the title Duke of Brabant.

Early life and education

Philippe was born at the Belvédère Castle in Laeken. He was educated at the Collège Saint-Michel in Brussels, the Royal Military Academy, Oxford University (Trinity College), and Stanford University, where he earned a master's degree in political science in 1985. He qualified as a fighter pilot and paratrooper in the Belgian Armed Forces. Before his accession he led more than seventy Belgian economic missions abroad in his role as honorary chairman of the Belgian Foreign Trade Board.

Reign

His reign has included government-formation crises typical of Belgian politics, the aftermath of the 2016 Brussels terror attacks, and growing debate over the colonial past: in 2020 Philippe expressed "deepest regrets" for the wounds of Belgium's colonial rule in Congo, the first such acknowledgement by a Belgian monarch, and in 2022 he repeated the regret during a state visit to the Democratic Republic of the Congo. He is married to Queen Mathilde, and their eldest daughter Princess Elisabeth is heir apparent — destined, if she succeeds, to be Belgium's first reigning queen.
