List of sheep breeds

More than a thousand breeds of domestic sheep have been developed worldwide, usually classified by their primary purpose: wool, meat, milk, or dual purpose. Others are grouped by physical traits such as hair sheep (which shed their coat) and fat-tailed sheep of southwest Asia.

Conservation organizations such as the Rare Breeds Survival Trust in Britain and the Livestock Conservancy in the United States monitor heritage breeds whose numbers have declined as commercial farming has concentrated on a few high-yielding types.
