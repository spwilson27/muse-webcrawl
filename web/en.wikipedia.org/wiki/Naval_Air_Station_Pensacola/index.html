Naval Air Station Pensacola (NAS Pensacola), also known as Forrest Sherman Field, is a United States Navy base located in the Warrington area of Pensacola, Florida, southwest of the city centre. It is famous as the initial primary training base for all U.S. Navy, Marine Corps and Coast Guard naval aviators and naval flight officers, earning it the nickname the "Cradle of Naval Aviation".

History

The site, an old Spanish presidio and later a navy yard established in 1826, was selected in 1913 as the Navy's first aeronautic station; the first flight unit arrived in January 1914 with Curtiss seaplanes. During the First World War it grew into a major training station, and in the interwar years it became the sole primary flight training base for naval aviation. The station also served as the long-time shore home of the Blue Angels flight demonstration squadron.

Tenants and attractions

Major tenant commands include the Naval Aviation Schools Command, Training Air Wing Six, the Naval Aerospace Medical Institute, and the Center for Information Warfare Training. The National Naval Aviation Museum, one of the largest aviation museums in the world, is located aboard the station, as is the historic Pensacola Lighthouse (1859) and the Barrancas National Cemetery.

On 6 December 2019 a Saudi aviation trainee carried out a shooting at the station that killed three people and wounded eight, an incident investigated as an act of terrorism.
