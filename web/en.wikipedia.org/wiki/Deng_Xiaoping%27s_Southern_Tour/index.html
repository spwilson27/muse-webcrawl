Deng Xiaoping's Southern Tour

Deng Xiaoping's Southern Tour (鄧小平南巡) was a political tour of southern China by retired paramount leader Deng Xiaoping from January to February 1992. Deng visited Shenzhen, Zhuhai, Guangzhou and Shanghai, delivering speeches calling for renewed economic reform and opening.

Significance

After the 1989 Tiananmen Square protests, reform had stalled amid conservative backlash. Deng's tour, widely reported despite his having no formal office, broke the deadlock: his remarks — including the sentiment that "development is the absolute principle" — relaunched market reforms and underpinned China's rapid growth in the following decades.
