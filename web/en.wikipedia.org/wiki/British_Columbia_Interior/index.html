British Columbia Interior
Country: Canada
Province: British Columbia
Largest city: Kelowna

The British Columbia Interior, popularly the BC Interior or simply the Interior, is the largely mountainous and plateau region of the Canadian province of British Columbia lying inland from the Coast Mountains. It includes the Okanagan, Thompson, Cariboo, Kootenays and Northern Interior regions.

Geography

The Interior is dominated by the Interior Plateau and by mountain ranges including the Monashees, Selkirks, Purcells and the eastern slopes of the Rockies. Rivers such as the Fraser, Thompson and Columbia drain the region. Climate ranges from semi-arid sagebrush country around Kamloops to wet interior rainforest in the Columbias.

Economy

Forestry, mining, ranching, agriculture — including Okanagan fruit and wine production — and tourism are the main industries. Principal cities include Kelowna, Kamloops, Prince George and Vernon.
