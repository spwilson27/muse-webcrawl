Ray Tomlinson

Raymond Samuel Tomlinson
Born: 23 April 1941, Amsterdam, New York, US
Died: 5 March 2016 (aged 74)
Known for: Invention of networked email; the @ sign in email addresses

Raymond Samuel Tomlinson (23 April 1941 – 5 March 2016) was an American computer programmer who implemented the first email program on the ARPANET system, the precursor to the Internet, in 1971. It was the first system able to send mail between users on different host computers connected to ARPANET.

The @ sign

Working at Bolt Beranek and Newman (BBN), Tomlinson combined the SNDMSG and CPYNET programs to send messages between machines. He chose the @ sign to separate the user name from the name of their machine, a scheme that has been used in email addresses ever since. He later recalled that the first networked messages were "something like QWERTYUIOP" — test strings, entirely forgettable.

Recognition

Tomlinson was inducted into the Internet Hall of Fame in 2012. He also contributed to the TENEX operating system, network protocols, and standards for email fields such as "From", "Subject" and date formats.
