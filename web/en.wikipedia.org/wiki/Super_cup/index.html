Super cup

Super cups traditionally open the domestic season, pitting league champions against cup winners.

A super cup is a competition in association football, typically a one-off match (or occasionally a two-legged tie) contested between the winners of a country's league championship and its main domestic cup from the previous season. Super cups usually serve as the curtain-raiser to a new season. Equivalent competitions exist in many other sports, including basketball, handball, futsal, ice hockey, and motorsport.

Format

If one team wins both the league and the cup (a "double"), the super cup place is often awarded to the cup runner-up or the league runner-up, depending on the competition's rules. Some super cups have expanded into multi-team final-four tournaments, frequently staged abroad for commercial reasons, as with the Supercopa de España since 2019–20 and the Supercoppa Italiana since 2023.

Notable super cups

  • UEFA Super Cup — between the winners of the UEFA Champions League and the UEFA Europa League
  • FA Community Shield — England's season opener, played at Wembley Stadium since 1908 in various forms
  • Supercopa de España — Spain's super cup, a four-team tournament since 2020
  • DFL-Supercup — Germany's super cup, revived in 2010
  • Trophée des Champions — France's super cup, frequently staged outside France
  • Supercoppa Italiana — Italy's super cup
  • Copa de Campeones / Supercopa competitions across Latin America

Status

Super cups are generally considered minor honours compared with league titles and major cups, and managers sometimes field rotated squads. Nevertheless victories are frequently counted in club trophy tallies and in the records of decorated players and managers, and the matches can draw large television audiences, particularly when staged between major rivals.
