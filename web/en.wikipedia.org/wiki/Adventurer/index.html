Adventurer
Definition: One who seeks unusual, risky experiences
Related concepts: Exploration, travel, heroism
In fiction: A stock character and protagonist type

An adventurer is a person who seeks out unusual, exciting and often risky experiences, particularly through travel and exploration of unfamiliar places.

History

The figure of the adventurer recurs throughout history, from ancient seafarers and mercenaries to explorers of the Age of Discovery and 19th-century travellers who combined exploration with writing. In some eras the term carried a pejorative sense of a fortune-seeker or soldier of fortune; in others it connoted heroism and curiosity.

In fiction

Adventurers are central protagonists of adventure literature and film, and form a basic character archetype in role-playing games, where "adventurer" often denotes the player's profession outright.
