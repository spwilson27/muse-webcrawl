List of flags of Norway

Flag of Norway
Red, blue and white Nordic cross, designed by Fredrik Meltzer and adopted in 1821.

This is a list of flags used in Norway, including the national flag, royal and military standards, and the flags of counties and municipalities. The national flag of Norway is red with an indigo blue Scandinavian cross fimbriated in white, extending to the edges of the flag, designed by Fredrik Meltzer and officially adopted in 1821.

County flags

Norway's counties each have a flag based on their coat of arms, including:

  • Agder, Akershus, Buskerud, Finnmark, Innlandet, Møre og Romsdal, Nordland, Oslo, Rogaland, Telemark, Troms, Trøndelag, Vestfold, Vestland and Østfold.

Other flags

  • Sami flag — adopted 1986; red, green, yellow and blue with a circle symbolising the sun and moon.
  • Postal flag and Customs flag — state flag variants with service emblems in the canton.
  • Historical flags — the union flags with Sweden (1814–1905) bearing the "herring salad" union mark; Kalmar Union banner (1397–1523); Danish-era flags.
