Joker (2019 film)

Joker
Directed by: Todd Phillips
Starring: Joaquin Phoenix, Robert De Niro, Zazie Beetz
Release: 2019
Box office: over $1 billion worldwide

Joker is a 2019 American psychological thriller film directed by Todd Phillips, who co-wrote the screenplay with Scott Silver. Based on the DC Comics character, it provides an alternative origin story for the Joker, starring Joaquin Phoenix as Arthur Fleck, a mentally ill, impoverished stand-up comedian whose gradual descent into madness and nihilism inspires a violent popular uprising in Gotham City.

Plot

In 1981 Gotham City, Arthur Fleck works as a party clown and lives with his mother, Penny, while suffering from a neurological disorder that causes uncontrollable laughter. Marginalised and repeatedly beaten down — cut off from social services, fired from his job, and disillusioned about his parentage — Fleck kills three men who attack him on a subway, sparking protests by the city's poor against the rich. His deterioration culminates in a live television appearance on Murray Franklin's talk show, where he murders the host on air amid citywide riots, and is embraced by the mob as the Joker.

Production and release

Phillips conceived the film as a character study inspired by 1970s cinema, particularly Martin Scorsese's Taxi Driver and The King of Comedy — the latter influence reflected in Robert De Niro's casting as the talk-show host. Phoenix lost a large amount of weight for the role. The film premiered at the Venice International Film Festival in August 2019, where it won the Golden Lion, the festival's top prize.

Reception

Joker grossed over $1 billion worldwide, becoming the first R-rated film to reach that mark. It led the 92nd Academy Awards with eleven nominations; Phoenix won the Academy Award for Best Actor and Hildur Guðnadóttir won for Best Original Score. Critical response praised Phoenix's performance and the film's craft, while its depiction of violence and mental illness generated substantial public debate and security concerns around its release. A sequel, Joker: Folie à Deux, was released in 2024.
