Double top and double bottom

The double top and double bottom are chart patterns used in technical analysis of financial markets. A double top resembles the letter M and signals a possible bearish reversal after an uptrend; a double bottom resembles W and signals bullish reversal.

Interpretation

Traders watch for price to break the neckline between the two peaks or troughs, ideally with volume confirmation. Measured-move targets project the pattern's height from the breakout.

Criticism

Like other chart patterns, their predictive value is debated, and false breakouts are common.
