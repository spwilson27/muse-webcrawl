Sword of Damocles (disambiguation)

Sword of Damocles may refer to:

  • The sword of Damocles, an anecdote from ancient Greek culture associated with the court of Dionysius II of Syracuse, in which a sword hung by a single hair above the throne symbolizes the peril that accompanies power and good fortune.
  • "The Sword of Damocles", a song from the 1975 musical film The Rocky Horror Picture Show.
  • The Sword of Damocles, an early head-mounted display system for virtual reality developed by Ivan Sutherland in 1968, named for its ceiling-suspended apparatus.
  • Various books, episodes, and works of fiction taking their title from the anecdote.
