Barangaroo is a waterfront locality on the northwestern edge of the Sydney central business district, in New South Wales, Australia. Formerly a container port known as the Hungry Mile, the 22-hectare site has been redeveloped since the 2010s into a mixed-use precinct of commercial towers, apartments, parkland and the Crown Sydney casino and hotel. It is named after Barangaroo, a Cammeraygal woman who was a prominent figure in the early years of the Sydney colony and the wife of Bennelong.

Redevelopment

The site comprises three parts: Barangaroo South, a financial and commercial district anchored by the three International Towers completed in 2015–16 and by Crown Sydney (One Barangaroo), opened in 2020; Barangaroo Central, a low-rise cultural and residential zone; and Barangaroo Reserve, a 6-hectare headland park opened in 2015 that re-creates the natural shoreline and headland using sandstone excavated from the site. Barangaroo railway station on the Sydney Metro City & Southwest line opened in 2024.

History

The area was a fishing and hunting ground for the Cadigal people before colonisation, then became a hub of wharves, bond stores and shipping — its "Hungry Mile" nickname recalling waterside workers seeking shifts during the Depression. Containerisation moved port activity to Port Botany, clearing the way for redevelopment announced in the 2000s; the project drew sustained public debate over its scale, casino licence and public access to the foreshore.
