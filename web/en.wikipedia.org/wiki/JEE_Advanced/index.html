Joint Entrance Examination – Advanced

JEE Advanced
Type: Computer-based test
Purpose: Admission to the Indian Institutes of Technology
Conducted by: One of the IITs (rotational), under the Joint Admission Board

The Joint Entrance Examination – Advanced (JEE-Advanced) is an academic examination held annually in India that tests the skills and knowledge of candidates in physics, chemistry, and mathematics. It is conducted by one of the Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs) on a rotational basis under the guidance of the Joint Admission Board (JAB).

Eligibility

Only candidates who rank among the top performers in JEE Main — approximately the top 250,000 candidates — are eligible to appear for JEE Advanced, subject to additional criteria on age, number of attempts, and qualifying examination performance.

Format

The examination consists of two compulsory papers, each of three hours' duration, held on the same day in computer-based mode. The papers include multiple-choice, numerical-answer, and matching questions with negative marking in some sections, and the question pattern varies from year to year.

Admission

Ranks in JEE Advanced determine admission to undergraduate programmes at the 23 IITs through the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA). The examination succeeded the IIT-JEE, which was replaced in 2013 by the two-tier JEE system.
