National Film School in Łódź
Polish: Państwowa Wyższa Szkoła Filmowa, Telewizyjna i Teatralna im. Leona Schillera
Founded: 1948
Location: Łódź, Poland
Type: Public film school

The Leon Schiller National Film School in Łódź (Polish: Państwowa Wyższa Szkoła Filmowa, Telewizyjna i Teatralna im. Leona Schillera w Łodzi, PWSFTvit) is a Polish academy for future actors, directors, cinematographers, photographers and television staff. Founded in 1948, it is one of the oldest and most prestigious film schools in Europe.

History

The school was established in Łódź, then the center of the Polish film industry, and its early students and teachers shaped the Polish Film School movement of the 1950s and 1960s. The famous steps and corridors of its main building on Targowa Street became an emblem of Polish cinema.

Notable alumni

Graduates include directors Andrzej Wajda, Roman Polanski, Jerzy Skolimowski and Krzysztof Kieślowski, as well as internationally renowned cinematographers. The school offers programs in directing, cinematography, acting, screenwriting, editing, photography and production management.
