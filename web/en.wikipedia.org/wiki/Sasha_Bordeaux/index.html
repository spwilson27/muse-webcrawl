Sasha Bordeaux

Sasha Bordeaux is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Greg Rucka and artist Shawn Martinbrough, she debuted in Detective Comics #751 (December 2000) as Bruce Wayne's bodyguard, and became a significant figure in Batman storylines of the 2000s before evolving into an agent — and eventually a cyborg operative — of the intelligence organisation Checkmate.

Fictional biography

Hired to protect Bruce Wayne, Bordeaux gradually deduced that her employer was Batman and was drawn into his war on crime. During the Bruce Wayne: Murderer? and Fugitive arcs she was framed for the murder of Vesper Fairchild and imprisoned, refusing to betray Wayne's secret. Later recruited by Checkmate, she was critically injured and rebuilt with OMAC technology, gaining enhanced strength and a cybernetic eye, and rose to become the organisation's "Black Queen" in the post-Infinite Crisis Checkmate series written by Rucka.

Character

Bordeaux is portrayed as a disciplined, pragmatic professional soldier whose loyalty and moral compromises — between duty to the law, to Checkmate and to Batman — drive much of her character arc.
