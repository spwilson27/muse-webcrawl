Porsche Cayenne

A third-generation Porsche Cayenne.

The Porsche Cayenne is a series of automobiles manufactured by the German company Porsche since 2002. It is a luxury crossover SUV, and it has been described as both a full-size and a mid-size vehicle. It was the first V8-engined vehicle built by Porsche since 1995, when the Porsche 928 was discontinued, and Porsche's first off-road variant vehicle since its Super and Junior tractors of the 1950s.

Development

The Cayenne was developed jointly with Volkswagen, sharing its platform with the Volkswagen Touareg and later the Audi Q7. The decision to build an SUV was controversial among Porsche purists but proved commercially decisive: the Cayenne became the company's best-selling model and generated the profits that secured Porsche's independence and funded its sports car lines.

Generations

  • First generation (955/957), 2002–2010: included the Cayenne S and the powerful Turbo models; a GTS variant followed.
  • Second generation (958), 2010–2017: lighter, more efficient, and introduced hybrid power with the Cayenne S Hybrid.
  • Third generation (9Y0), 2017–present: added a coupé body style (2019) and progressively more electrified variants, including plug-in hybrids and the Turbo GT.

Performance

Top versions have performance rivalling sports cars; the Cayenne Turbo GT set an SUV lap record at the Nürburgring Nordschleife in 2021.
