The Venetian Las Vegas

The Venetian Las Vegas is a luxury hotel and casino resort on the Las Vegas Strip in Paradise, Nevada, United States. Themed after Venice, Italy, it features replicas of Venetian landmarks — including St Mark's Campanile, the Rialto Bridge and the Doge's Palace — along with canals on which gondolas carry guests.

History

Developed by Sheldon Adelson's Las Vegas Sands on the site of the demolished Sands Hotel and Casino, The Venetian opened on May 3, 1999, with a ceremony featuring Sophia Loren and white doves. Its all-suite hotel tower offered some of the largest standard rooms on the Strip. The adjoining Palazzo tower opened in 2007, making the combined complex one of the largest hotels in the world by room count. The resort connects to the Venetian Expo convention centre (formerly Sands Expo). In 2022, Las Vegas Sands sold the property's operations to Apollo Global Management and the real estate to Vici Properties, while the Venetian name was retained.

Attractions

The Grand Canal Shoppes line an indoor canal beneath a painted-sky ceiling; costumed performers entertain in "St. Mark's Square." The resort has hosted long-running theatrical productions and, in 2023, the adjoining Sphere entertainment venue opened behind the property. Madame Tussauds Las Vegas is located at the resort's front.
