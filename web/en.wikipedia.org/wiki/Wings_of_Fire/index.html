Wings of Fire

Wings of Fire may refer to several works. The best known is Wings of Fire: An Autobiography (1999), the life story of A. P. J. Abdul Kalam, the Indian aerospace scientist who became the 11th President of India, written with Arun Tiwari. The book recounts Kalam's early life in Rameswaram, his work on India's satellite launch vehicle and missile programs, and his philosophy of national development through technology; it has been translated into many languages and has sold millions of copies.

The fantasy series

Wings of Fire is also a bestselling middle-grade fantasy book series by Tui T. Sutherland, published by Scholastic beginning in 2012. Set in the dragon-inhabited continents of Pyrrhia and Pantala, the series follows young dragonets caught in wars and prophecies among the dragon tribes. It comprises multiple arcs of five novels each, along with companion books, graphic novel adaptations, and a "Legends" prequel line, and has sold more than 20 million copies worldwide.

Other uses

The title has also been used for a 1967 American television film (also released as The Man Who Landed) about a pilot, and for various songs, albums, and episodes in other media.
