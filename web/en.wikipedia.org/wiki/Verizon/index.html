Verizon

Verizon Communications Inc. is an American multinational telecommunications company headquartered in New York City. It is one of the largest telecommunications companies in the world and the largest wireless carrier in the United States.

History

Verizon was formed in 2000 from the merger of Bell Atlantic — one of the "Baby Bells" created by the 1984 breakup of AT&T — and GTE. The name combines veritas (Latin for truth) and horizon. Its wireless division, begun as a joint venture with Vodafone, became wholly owned in 2014 in a $130 billion buyout. Verizon built the FiOS fibre-optic broadband network, acquired AOL (2015) and Yahoo (2017) into a media unit later sold in 2021, and acquired prepaid carrier TracFone in 2021.

Operations

The company provides mobile service under the Verizon brand, 5G and LTE networks, wireline broadband and enterprise network services through its Consumer and Business segments. It has been involved in public debates over net neutrality, spectrum policy and data privacy.
