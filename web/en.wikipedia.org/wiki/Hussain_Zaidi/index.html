Hussain Zaidi

S. Hussain Zaidi is an Indian author and former investigative journalist known for his books on the Mumbai underworld and organised crime. He worked for newspapers including The Asian Age, Mumbai Mirror, Mid-Day and Indian Express.

Career

Zaidi spent many years reporting on crime in Mumbai, covering figures and gangs associated with the city's underworld. His reporting formed the basis of several bestselling non-fiction books.

Books

His works include Black Friday (2002), about the 1993 Bombay bombings, later adapted into a film by Anurag Kashyap; Dongri to Dubai: Six Decades of the Mumbai Mafia (2012), adapted as the film Shootout at Lokhandwala in part and the basis for other adaptations; Mafia Queens of Mumbai (2011), co-authored with Jane Borges, which inspired the film Gangubai Kathiawadi; and Headley and I, with Rahul Bhatt. He has also written on Dawood Ibrahim, Abu Salem, and the 26/11 Mumbai attacks.
