Trend line (technical analysis)

In finance, a trend line is a bounding line for the price movement of a security, drawn on a price chart in technical analysis. An uptrend line is drawn along successive rising troughs (support), while a downtrend line is drawn along successive falling peaks (resistance).

Use

Trend lines are among the most basic tools of technical analysis. Traders use them to identify the prevailing direction of a market and potential points of support and resistance. A decisive break of a trend line, especially on heavy volume, may signal that the trend is reversing.

Drawing

A valid trend line typically connects at least two, and preferably three or more, significant pivot points without cutting through price bars between them. Lines may be drawn on linear or logarithmic scales. Trend lines underpin related concepts such as channels, which pair a trend line with a parallel return line.
