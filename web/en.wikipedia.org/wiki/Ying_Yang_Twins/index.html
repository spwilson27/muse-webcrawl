The Ying Yang Twins, an Atlanta crunk duo.

The Ying Yang Twins are an American hip hop duo from Atlanta, Georgia, composed of Kaine (born Eric Jackson) and D-Roc (born Deongelo Holmes). Despite their stage name, the two are not twins and are not related. The duo rose to prominence in the early 2000s as leading figures of the crunk movement, known for call-and-response party records built around shouted hooks, whispered verses and heavy bass.

Career

The duo debuted in 2000 with the album Thug Walkin', featuring the single "Whistle While You Twurk". Their breakthrough came through collaborations with Atlanta producer Lil Jon, including appearances associated with the crunk scene, and with their 2003 album Me & My Brother, which produced the hit "Salt Shaker" featuring Lil Jon & the East Side Boyz. In 2005 they released U.S.A. (United State of Atlanta), which included the singles "Wait (The Whisper Song)" and "By Yourself". The whispered delivery of "Wait" became one of their signature styles.

The Ying Yang Twins also appeared on Britney Spears' 2003 album In the Zone on the track "(I Got That) Boom Boom". Subsequent releases included Chemically Imbalanced (2006), produced largely by Mr. Collipark, and later independent albums and mixtapes. Their music has remained a staple of 2000s crunk retrospectives and party playlists.

Style

The duo's sound combines minimal, bass-heavy production with energetic chants and sexually explicit party lyrics. Along with Lil Jon, they helped define crunk's mainstream moment in the mid-2000s, when the style dominated Southern hip hop and club music across the United States.
