List of 20th Century Studios films

This is a list of films produced and distributed by the American film studio 20th Century Studios, founded in 1935 as 20th Century-Fox Film Corporation through the merger of Fox Film Corporation and Twentieth Century Pictures. Following its acquisition by The Walt Disney Company in 2019, the studio was renamed 20th Century Studios in 2020.

Notable films by decade

  • 1950s: All About Eve (1950), The King and I (1956), The Diary of Anne Frank (1959).
  • 1960s: The Sound of Music (1965), Planet of the Apes (1968), Butch Cassidy and the Sundance Kid (1969).
  • 1970s: Patton (1970), The French Connection (1971), Star Wars (1977), Alien (1979).
  • 1980s: The Empire Strikes Back (1980), Raiders of the Lost Ark (distribution, 1981), Aliens (1986), Die Hard (1988).
  • 1990s: Home Alone (1990), Independence Day (1996), Titanic (1997, co-production with Paramount), Fight Club (1999).
  • 2000s: X-Men (2000), Ice Age (2002), Avatar (2009).
  • 2010s: The Martian (2015), Deadpool (2016), Bohemian Rhapsody (2018).
  • 2020s (as 20th Century Studios): The King's Man (2021), Death on the Nile (2022), Avatar: The Way of Water (2022), Kingdom of the Planet of the Apes (2024).

The complete filmography runs to thousands of titles across more than nine decades and includes films released under the 20th Century Fox, Fox 2000 Pictures, and 20th Century Studios banners.
