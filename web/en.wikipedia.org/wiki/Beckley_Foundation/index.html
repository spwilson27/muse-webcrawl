Beckley Foundation

The Beckley Foundation is a British think tank and non-governmental organisation dedicated to the study of consciousness and the reform of drug policy. Founded in 1998 by Amanda Feilding, the Foundation is based at Beckley Park in Oxfordshire and operates both a scientific research programme and a policy programme aimed at developing evidence-based, health-oriented drug policies.

Scientific programme

The Foundation has sponsored and co-organised pioneering research into psychedelic substances and cannabis. Through the Beckley/Imperial Research Programme, a collaboration with Imperial College London involving researchers including David Nutt and Robin Carhart-Harris, it supported some of the first modern neuroimaging studies of LSD and psilocybin, including the first brain-imaging study of the LSD experience, published in 2016. Its research has explored the effects of psychedelics on cerebral blood flow, brain connectivity, and conditions such as depression and addiction.

Policy programme

The Foundation's policy work has included convening expert seminars, producing reports on the costs and consequences of prohibition, and drafting model frameworks for the legal regulation of cannabis and psychedelics. It has held consultative status with the United Nations Economic and Social Council, and its Global Initiative for Drug Policy Reform brought together former heads of state and other public figures to advocate reform ahead of the 2016 UN General Assembly Special Session on drugs.
