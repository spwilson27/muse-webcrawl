Efficient estimator

In statistics, an efficient estimator is an estimator that estimates the quantity of interest in some optimal sense—generally having the smallest possible variance among all unbiased estimators, or more generally achieving the best possible performance in a given class.

Cramér–Rao bound

The Cramér–Rao lower bound states that the variance of any unbiased estimator of a parameter is at least the inverse of the Fisher information. An unbiased estimator that achieves this bound is called efficient. Maximum likelihood estimators are asymptotically efficient under regularity conditions.

Asymptotic efficiency

Because exact efficiency is rare outside specific models, statisticians often consider asymptotic efficiency: an estimator whose asymptotic variance equals the inverse Fisher information as the sample size tends to infinity.
