Instituto Ayrton Senna
Founded: 1994
Headquarters: São Paulo, Brazil
Type: Non-profit organization
Focus: Education

The Instituto Ayrton Senna is a Brazilian non-profit organization founded in 1994 by Viviane Senna, sister of three-time Formula One world champion Ayrton Senna, shortly after his death at the 1994 San Marino Grand Prix.

Working in partnership with public school systems, companies, and universities, the institute develops educational solutions aimed at improving public education in Brazil, including teacher training, school management programs, and socio-emotional learning initiatives. It reports reaching millions of students across Brazil.
