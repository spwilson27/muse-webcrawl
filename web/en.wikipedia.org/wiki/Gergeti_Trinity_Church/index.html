Gergeti Trinity Church

LocationGergeti, near Stepantsminda, Mtskheta-Mtianeti, Georgia
DenominationGeorgian Orthodox Church
Built14th century
Elevation2,170 m (7,120 ft)

Gergeti Trinity Church (Georgian: წმინდა სამება, Tsminda Sameba) is a popular name for the Holy Trinity Church built in the 14th century near the village of Gergeti in northern Georgia. Standing at an elevation of about 2,170 metres beneath the glaciated peak of Mount Kazbek, its isolated silhouette against the mountains has made it one of the best-known symbols of Georgia.

History

The church was built in the 14th century, in the cross-cupola style typical of medieval Georgian architecture, accompanied by a separate bell tower of the same period. In times of danger, precious relics from Mtskheta, including Saint Nino's cross, were brought here for safekeeping. During the Soviet era religious services were prohibited, though the church remained a popular destination for travellers; it is now an active place of worship of the Georgian Orthodox Church.

Setting and access

The church overlooks the town of Stepantsminda (Kazbegi) on the Georgian Military Road. A paved road completed in recent years leads up to the church, and a hiking trail of about three hours round trip climbs from the village through Gergeti. The dramatic setting beneath the 5,054-metre Mount Kazbek draws large numbers of visitors.
