Q is a fictional character in the James Bond films and novelisations. Q is the head of Q Branch (later Q Division), the fictional research and development arm of the British Secret Service (MI6). His real name is Major Boothroyd, a name taken from a real firearms expert Geoffrey Boothroyd, who advised Ian Fleming.

Role

Q supplies Bond with his equipment — gadgets, weapons, and modified vehicles such as the Aston Martin DB5 — usually in a briefing scene in which Q earnestly urges Bond to return the equipment "in one piece". The character appears in nearly every Bond film from From Russia with Love (1963) onward.

Actors

Peter Burton played the role (as Major Boothroyd) in Dr. No (1962). Desmond Llewelyn then played Q in 17 films from 1963 to 1999, becoming one of the longest-serving actors in the series. John Cleese played Q's assistant "R" and then Q in Die Another Day (2002). Ben Whishaw took over as a younger, computer-savvy Q beginning with Skyfall (2012).
