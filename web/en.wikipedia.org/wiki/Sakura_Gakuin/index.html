Sakura Gakuin (Japanese: さくら学院, "Cherry Blossom Academy") was a Japanese idol group formed by the talent agency Amuse in 2010. Built around a fictional school theme, the group consisted of girls of elementary and junior-high-school age who "graduated" upon finishing junior high school each spring, with new younger members transferring in. Its concept centred on school subjects and extracurricular "club activities", each club forming a sub-unit with its own musical style.

History

The group debuted in 2010 with the single "Yume ni Mukatte / Hello! IVY". Members were organised into clubs such as the Twinklestars (baton twirling), Minipati (cooking), the Science Club Kagaku Kyumei Kiko Logica?, and the Heavy Music Club, whose unit became Babymetal. Suzuka Nakamoto, Yui Mizuno and Moa Kikuchi of Babymetal all began in Sakura Gakuin. Annual rituals included entrance ceremonies, transfer ceremonies and graduation concerts, documented on the group's albums, each titled by school year (e.g. Sakura Gakuin 2010 Nendo: Message).

Over eleven years the group released numerous singles and studio albums, staged regular "Sakura Gakuin Festival" concerts, and produced a long line of graduates who went on to careers in music and acting. In 2020 Amuse announced the group would conclude activities; Sakura Gakuin held its final concert, "The Road to Graduation 2020", and disbanded on 31 August 2021.

Legacy

Sakura Gakuin is regarded as a formative training ground within the idol industry; its most famous offshoot, Babymetal, achieved international success fusing idol pop with heavy metal.
