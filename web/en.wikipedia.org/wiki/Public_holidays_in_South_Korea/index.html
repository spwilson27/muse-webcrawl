Public holidays in South Korea

Public holidays in South Korea are prescribed by the Regulations on Holidays of Government Offices and the Act on Public Holidays (2021), which also introduced substitute holidays when certain holidays fall on a weekend. South Korea observes a mix of national patriotic days, traditional lunar festivals and religious holidays.

Seollal and Chuseok are the two great family festivals, marked by travel to ancestral homes, the charye ancestral rite, traditional foods such as tteokguk and songpyeon, and folk games. Election days for national elections are also statutory holidays. Constitution Day (17 July) is a national day but ceased to be a public holiday in 2008.
