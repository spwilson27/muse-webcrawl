Kif Kroker

Lieutenant Kif Kroker is a fictional character in the animated television series Futurama, voiced by Maurice LaMarche. Kif is an amphibious alien from the planet Amphibios 9 and serves as the long-suffering fourth lieutenant and personal assistant to the pompous Captain Zapp Brannigan aboard the Democratic Order of Planets (DOOP) starship Nimbus. Gentle, soft-spoken and perpetually exasperated, Kif is the show's chief foil to Brannigan's bluster: he does the actual work, absorbs the blame, and punctuates his captain's announcements with weary sighs.

Character

Kif has a green, boneless body supported by fluid-filled bladders, three stomachs, and the ability to shed his skin and inflate with air; Amphibiosans pass through a tadpole-like life cycle and reproduce when in a state of deep love, absorbing a partner's DNA through skin contact. Kif's biology becomes a plot point when he unexpectedly becomes "pregnant" — with Leela identified as the genetic donor — and returns to his home swamp to give birth in the episode "Kif Gets Knocked Up a Notch".

Relationships

Despite being set up with her as a joke by Zapp, Kif develops a sincere romance with Amy Wong, which becomes one of the series' recurring emotional threads; the two eventually become fonfon ru (an Amphibiosan form of marriage). His relationship with Brannigan is one of resigned servitude, though episodes occasionally show Kif's contempt breaking through, as in "War Is the H-Word", and his brief promotion when Brannigan is disgraced.
