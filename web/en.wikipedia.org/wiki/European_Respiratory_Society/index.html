European Respiratory Society

The European Respiratory Society (ERS) is an international non-profit medical organisation for professionals working in respiratory medicine, including clinicians, scientists, nurses, and allied health workers. Founded in 1990 from the merger of the Societas Europaea Physiologiae Clinicae Respiratoriae and the European Society of Pneumology, it has grown to a membership of more than 30,000 drawn from over 160 countries. Its head office is in Lausanne, Switzerland, with additional offices in Brussels and Sheffield.

Activities

The ERS organises the annual ERS International Congress, one of the largest respiratory medicine meetings in the world, and publishes a portfolio of journals headed by the European Respiratory Journal, alongside ERJ Open Research, European Respiratory Review, and Breathe. It also issues clinical practice guidelines, runs educational programmes and examinations such as the European Diploma in Adult Respiratory Medicine (HERMES), and funds research fellowships.

Advocacy

Through its advocacy work the society promotes lung health policy in Europe, including tobacco control, air quality, and vaccination. It established the European Lung Foundation to involve patients and the public in respiratory health.
