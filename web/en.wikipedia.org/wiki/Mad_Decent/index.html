Mad Decent is an American record label founded in 2006 in Philadelphia by DJ and producer Diplo. The label became known for championing global club sounds — baile funk, dancehall, moombahton, Jersey club and EDM trap — and for its irreverent, internet-savvy branding. It later relocated to Los Angeles.

History

Diplo founded Mad Decent after the success of his work popularising Brazilian baile funk and Baltimore club. The label released music by artists including Major Lazer (Diplo's own project), Dillon Francis, DJ Snake, Flosstradamus, Baauer and Rusko. Baauer's "Harlem Shake", released through the Mad Decent sublabel Jeffree's in 2012, became a viral phenomenon in early 2013 and topped the Billboard Hot 100. The label helped launch moombahton after Dave Nada's slowed-down house experiments were released and promoted through its network.

Mad Decent Block Party

From 2008 the label ran the Mad Decent Block Party, a touring festival that grew from a free Philadelphia street party into a multi-city North American event, with an edition in India in 2016. The festival was discontinued after 2016, when two drug-related deaths at the 2014 Maryland edition and subsequent scrutiny contributed to its end; in 2019 a successor event, the "Super Mega Ultra Giant Mad Decent Block Party Festival", was held in Massachusetts.

Sublabels and releases

Mad Decent operated the free-release imprint Jeffree's from 2012 and later the imprint Good Enuff. Its catalogue is notable for Diplo's Express Yourself EP, Major Lazer's early records, and releases bridging underground club music and mainstream pop.
