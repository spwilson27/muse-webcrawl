Bergerac, Dordogne

Bergerac, Dordogne
Region: Nouvelle-Aquitaine
Department: Dordogne

Bergerac is a commune and subprefecture in the Dordogne department in Nouvelle-Aquitaine in southwestern France, situated on the Dordogne River, known for its wines and old town.

Geography and economy

Bergerac is the centre of a wine region producing red, white and sweet wines including Monbazillac nearby. Tobacco and tourism have also been important.

Culture

The town is associated in literature with Cyrano de Bergerac, Edmond Rostand's fictional hero, whose statues stand in the town, although the historical Cyrano came from Paris.
