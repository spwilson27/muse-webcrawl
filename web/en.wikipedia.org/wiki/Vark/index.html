Vark

Vark (also varak or warq) is a fine filigree foil sheet of pure metal, typically silver but sometimes gold, used to decorate South Asian sweets and food. The silver and gold are edible, though flavourless, and are commonly placed on mithai, rice dishes, and paan for festive presentation.

Vark is made by pounding metal into sheets a few micrometres thick, traditionally between layers of hide or paper. Because the foil is extremely delicate, it is handled with the backing paper and applied directly to the food. Concerns have been raised about hygiene in traditional production and about adulteration with cheaper metals, leading to food-safety regulations in India.
