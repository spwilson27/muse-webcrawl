China–Pakistan Economic Corridor

AbbreviationCPEC
CountriesChina and Pakistan
LaunchedApril 2015
ValueInitially ~$46 billion, later announced above $60 billion
Key nodeGwadar Port

The China–Pakistan Economic Corridor (CPEC) is a collection of infrastructure and energy projects under construction throughout Pakistan, forming a flagship component of China's Belt and Road Initiative. It links Gwadar Port in Balochistan with Kashgar in Xinjiang via a network of highways, railways and pipelines.

Projects

Early phases emphasized power generation — coal, hydro, wind and solar plants that eased Pakistan's chronic electricity shortages — alongside motorways, the upgrading of the Karakoram Highway, and the development of Gwadar's port, airport and free zone. Special economic zones were planned for a later phase.

Significance and debate

Supporters hail CPEC as a "game changer" for Pakistan's economy and connectivity; critics raise concerns over debt sustainability, security for Chinese workers, and the corridor's route through Gilgit-Baltistan, territory disputed with India.
