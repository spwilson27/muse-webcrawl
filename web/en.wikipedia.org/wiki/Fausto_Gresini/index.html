Fausto Gresini

Fausto Gresini (23 January 1961 – 23 February 2021) was an Italian Grand Prix motorcycle racing road racer and team manager. He won the 125cc World Championship twice, in 1985 and 1987, and later founded the Gresini Racing team, which competed in MotoGP and the lower classes for more than two decades.

Racing career

Gresini was born in Imola and began competing in the Italian championship before moving into the world championship in the early 1980s. Riding for the Garelli team, he won the 125cc world title in 1985, and after finishing runner-up in 1986 he regained the crown in 1987, a season in which he won ten of the eleven races. He remained a front-runner in the class into the early 1990s, finishing second in the championship again in 1990, 1991 and 1992, before retiring from racing at the end of 1994. In total he won 21 Grands Prix.

Team management

In 1997 Gresini founded his own team, Gresini Racing, initially in the 500cc class with Honda machinery and rider Alex Barros. The team went on to become one of the most successful independent outfits in the paddock, winning the 250cc world championship with Daijiro Kato in 2001 and later titles in Moto3 and MotoE. Kato's death at the 2003 Japanese Grand Prix and Marco Simoncelli's fatal accident in 2011 were defining tragedies for the team, which Gresini led until his death.

Death

Gresini was hospitalised in Bologna in late December 2020 after contracting COVID-19 during the COVID-19 pandemic in Italy. He died on 23 February 2021, aged 60, after nearly two months in intensive care.
