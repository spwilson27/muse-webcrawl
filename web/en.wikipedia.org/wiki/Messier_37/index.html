Messier 37

Messier 37 (also known as M37 or NGC 2099) is the brightest and richest open cluster in the constellation Auriga. It was discovered by the Italian astronomer Giovanni Battista Hodierna before 1654.

Characteristics

The cluster is located roughly 4,500 light-years from Earth and contains several hundred stars, including numerous red giants. Its age is estimated at between 350 and 550 million years, and it spans about 24 light-years.

Observation

M37 is the most spectacular of the three Messier open clusters in Auriga (with M36 and M38) and is a popular target for amateur astronomers.
