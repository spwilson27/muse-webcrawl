Delco Electronics

Delco Electronics Corporation was an American automotive electronics design and manufacturing company, long a subsidiary of General Motors. Based in Kokomo, Indiana, it produced radios, engine controls and other electronic systems for GM vehicles.

History

The name Delco derives from the Dayton Engineering Laboratories Company, founded by Charles Kettering and Edward Deeds, inventors of the electric self-starter and points ignition system. Through the 20th century Delco divisions made everything from car radios to guidance systems. In the 1990s it was folded into Delphi Automotive Systems when GM spun off its components operations.
