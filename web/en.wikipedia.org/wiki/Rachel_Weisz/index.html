Rachel Hannah Weisz (born 7 March 1970) is a British actress. She has received several accolades, including an Academy Award, a BAFTA Award and a Laurence Olivier Award. Weisz began acting in British stage and television in the early 1990s and made her film debut in Death Machine (1994). She won critical acclaim for her performances in the theatre productions of Noël Coward's Design for Living (1994) and Tennessee Williams' Suddenly Last Summer (1999).

Her breakthrough came with the role of Evelyn Carnahan in the Hollywood action films The Mummy (1999) and The Mummy Returns (2001). She won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress for playing an activist in the thriller The Constant Gardener (2005). Her career continued with roles in The Fountain (2006), The Lovely Bones (2009), The Deep Blue Sea (2011), The Bourne Legacy (2012), and Yorgos Lanthimos' films The Lobster (2015) and The Favourite (2018), the latter earning her the BAFTA Award for Best Supporting Actress and another Oscar nomination. She later played Melina Vostokoff in the Marvel film Black Widow (2021) and dual lead roles in the series Dead Ringers (2023).

Early life

Weisz was born in Westminster, London, to a Hungarian Jewish inventor father, George Weisz, and an Austrian Jewish mother, Edith Ruth, a teacher turned psychotherapist, both of whom had come to Britain as refugees before the Second World War. She was educated at St Paul's Girls' School and read English at Trinity Hall, Cambridge, where she co-founded the student drama group Talking Tongues.

Personal life

Weisz was previously in a long-term relationship with American filmmaker Darren Aronofsky, with whom she has a son. In June 2011 she married actor Daniel Craig in a small private ceremony in New York; they have a daughter, born in 2018. She became a naturalised American citizen in 2011.
