Scotland

[Image: Edinburgh Castle above the Old Town]
Edinburgh Castle above the Old Town

Scotland is a country that is part of the United Kingdom, occupying the northern third of the island of Great Britain, with a population of about 5.5 million. Its capital is Edinburgh and its largest city is Glasgow. Scotland shares a border with England and is surrounded by the Atlantic Ocean, the North Sea and the Irish Sea, with more than 790 islands including the Hebrides, Orkney and Shetland.

The Kingdom of Scotland emerged in the 9th century and preserved its independence through wars with England until the Acts of Union of 1707 created Great Britain — though Scotland kept its own legal system, church and education. The Scottish Enlightenment profoundly influenced modern thought. Devolution restored a Scottish Parliament in 1999; voters rejected independence in a 2014 referendum by 55 to 45 percent. North Sea oil, whisky, salmon and renewables are key economic sectors.
