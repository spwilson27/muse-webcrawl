Zaxxon

Zaxxon
Developer: Sega
Publisher: Sega
Release: 1982 (arcade)
Genre: Scrolling shooter
Platforms: Arcade, ColecoVision, and many home systems

Zaxxon is a 1982 arcade video game developed and published by Sega. It is an isometric scrolling shooter in which the player pilots a fighter spacecraft through a heavily defended fortress, over open space, and into a second fortress to destroy the robot Zaxxon.

Gameplay

Zaxxon was notable for its pseudo-3D isometric graphics at a time when most shooters used flat top-down or side views. The player must manage altitude as well as position, judging height from the ship's shadow and an altimeter, in order to clear walls, dodge missiles and pass through narrow gaps. Fuel is consumed continuously and replenished by destroying fuel tanks.

Reception and legacy

The game was a major commercial success and one of the highest-grossing arcade games of its year. The ColecoVision home version was particularly praised for reproducing the arcade experience and helped sell the console. Ports appeared for numerous computers and consoles, and the years following saw a sequel, Super Zaxxon (1984), a spin-off, Future Spy, and much later the isometric shooter Zaxxon Motherbase 2000 for the 32X. Zaxxon is frequently cited among the pioneering titles of the shoot 'em up genre.
