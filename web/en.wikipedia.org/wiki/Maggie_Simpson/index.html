Margaret Lenny "Maggie" Simpson is a fictional character in the animated television series The Simpsons, the youngest child of Homer and Marge Simpson and the baby sister of Bart and Lisa. Created by cartoonist Matt Groening, she first appeared in the short "Good Night" on The Tracey Ullman Show in 1987. Perpetually one year old, Maggie almost never speaks and communicates by sucking her red pacifier — a sound effect originally performed by series creator Groening and producer James L. Brooks.

Character

Although a baby, Maggie is frequently portrayed as the most competent and resourceful Simpson: she has escaped danger unaided, driven a car, and displays surprising strength and cunning, including leading a baby uprising at the Ayn Rand School for Tots in the Oscar-shortlisted short film The Longest Daycare (2012). In the famous two-part episode "Who Shot Mr. Burns?" (1995), Maggie was revealed as the (accidental) shooter of Mr. Burns, one of the series' most celebrated storylines. When she does speak — usually in fantasy or future-set episodes — her voice has been provided by guest stars including Elizabeth Taylor (her first word, "Daddy"), Jodie Foster and Amy Sedaris.

In popular culture

Maggie is named after Groening's youngest sister. Her supermarket price-scan gag in the opening sequence — the register reading "$847.63" — became a long-running joke, the figure having once been cited as the monthly cost of raising a baby in the United States. In 2009 her scan readout was changed to mirror episode themes. Despite her silence she is one of the show's most recognisable characters and a fixture of its merchandising.
