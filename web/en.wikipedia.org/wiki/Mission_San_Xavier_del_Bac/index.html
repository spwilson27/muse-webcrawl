Mission San Xavier del Bac

Mission San Xavier del Bac is a historic Spanish Catholic mission near Tucson, Arizona, on the Santa Cruz River within the Tohono O'odham Nation. Nicknamed the "White Dove of the Desert", it is renowned for its brilliant white stucco, Moorish-influenced architecture and richly painted interior.

History

A mission at Bac was founded by the Jesuit Eusebio Francisco Kino in 1692 among the Tohono O'odham. The present church was built between 1783 and 1797 under Franciscan direction, by O'odham labourers, after the Jesuits' expulsion from Spanish territories.

Architecture and art

The church combines Moorish, Byzantine and Mexican baroque elements; one tower was left unfinished. Inside are original murals and statuary, restored in the 1990s by international conservators. The mission remains an active parish church for the Tohono O'odham community and a major pilgrimage and tourist site.
