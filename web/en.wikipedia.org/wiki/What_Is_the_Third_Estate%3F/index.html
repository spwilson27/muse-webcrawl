What Is the Third Estate?

What Is the Third Estate? (French: Qu'est-ce que le tiers état?) is a political pamphlet written in January 1789 by the French clergyman and political theorist Emmanuel Joseph Sieyès during the period immediately before the outbreak of the French Revolution.

Argument

The pamphlet opens with three famous questions: "What is the Third Estate? — Everything. What has it been hitherto in the political order? — Nothing. What does it desire? — To be something." Sieyès argued that the Third Estate — the commoners, who made up the vast majority of the population — constituted the true nation, and that the privileged orders (the clergy and nobility) were superfluous burdens. He demanded that the Third Estate's representation in the Estates-General be doubled and that voting proceed by head rather than by order.

Influence

The pamphlet was enormously influential, selling tens of thousands of copies and shaping the political demands that led to the formation of the National Assembly in June 1789.
