Of Mice & Men is an American metalcore band from Orange County, California, formed in 2009 by former Attack Attack! vocalist Austin Carlile and bassist Jaxin Hall. The band's name comes from John Steinbeck's 1937 novella of the same name.

History

Their self-titled debut album appeared in 2010, followed by The Flood (2011) and Restoring Force (2014), the latter reaching the top five of the Billboard 200 and marking a shift towards a more mainstream metal sound. Carlile, whose health was affected by Marfan syndrome, left the band at the end of 2016; bassist Aaron Pauley took over as lead vocalist. Subsequent albums include Defy (2018), Earthandsky (2019), the lockdown-written Echo (2021), and Tether (2023).

Musical style

The band's early material combined breakdowns and alternating screamed and sung vocals typical of metalcore; later work incorporates nu metal and hard rock influences with an emphasis on choruses.

Members

The current line-up consists of Aaron Pauley (vocals, bass), Phil Manansala (lead guitar), Alan Ashby (rhythm guitar) and Valentino Arteaga (drums).
