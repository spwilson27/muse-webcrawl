Nara Prefecture

Nara Prefecture (奈良県) is a prefecture of Japan located in the Kansai region of Honshu. Its capital is the city of Nara, which served as Japan's capital in the eighth century during the Nara period (710–794).

Geography

Nara is a landlocked prefecture bordered by Osaka, Kyoto, Wakayama and Mie prefectures. The north is dominated by the Nara Basin, while the south is mountainous, including the Kii Mountains, part of a UNESCO World Heritage pilgrimage landscape.

Tourism and culture

The prefecture is famed for Tōdai-ji temple and its Great Buddha, Kasuga-taisha shrine, and the free-roaming sika deer of Nara Park. The Historic Monuments of Ancient Nara are a World Heritage Site.
