Paul of Greece

Paul (Greek: Παῦλος; 14 December 1901 – 6 March 1964) was King of Greece from 1947 until his death in 1964. He succeeded his elder brother George II.

Early life

Paul was the third son of Constantine I of Greece and Sophia of Prussia. He served as a naval officer and spent periods of exile with his family during Greece's political upheavals of the 1920s and the Second World War.

Reign

Paul came to the throne during the Greek Civil War (1946–1949) and reigned through Greece's post-war reconstruction, its entry into NATO in 1952 and the Cyprus dispute with Britain and Turkey. He was known for extensive foreign tours that promoted Greece internationally. He died in Athens in 1964 and was succeeded by his son Constantine II, the last king of Greece.
