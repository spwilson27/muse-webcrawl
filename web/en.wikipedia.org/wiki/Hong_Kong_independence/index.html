Hong Kong independence

Hong Kong independence is a political movement that advocates Hong Kong becoming an independent sovereign state. It emerged from localist movements in the 2010s, particularly after the 2014 Umbrella Movement, within debates over the city's autonomy under "one country, two systems" following the 1997 handover from Britain to China.

Development

Groups such as the Hong Kong Indigenous and the short-lived Hong Kong National Party (founded 2016, banned in 2018 — the first party banned under the Societies Ordinance) promoted independence or self-determination. The Chinese and Hong Kong governments hold that independence violates the Basic Law and China's constitution.

After 2020

The Hong Kong national security law of 2020 criminalised secession, and advocacy of independence largely moved overseas among diaspora groups, while public discussion within Hong Kong ceased.
