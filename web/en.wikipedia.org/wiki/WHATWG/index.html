WHATWG

The Web Hypertext Application Technology Working Group (WHATWG) is a community of people interested in evolving HTML and related technologies. It was founded in 2004 by individuals from Apple, Mozilla and Opera in response to the slow development of web standards at the W3C and its focus on XHTML.

Founded2004
FoundersApple, Mozilla, Opera contributors
ProductsHTML Living Standard, DOM, Fetch, URL standards

Work

The WHATWG developed Web Forms 2.0 and the specification that became HTML5. It maintains the HTML specification as a continuously updated "living standard" rather than a sequence of versioned snapshots, alongside standards for DOM, Fetch, URL, Streams and other core web platform technologies. Its specifications are implemented by all major browser engines.

Relationship with the W3C

After years of parallel publication of HTML specifications by both bodies, the W3C and WHATWG signed a memorandum of understanding in 2019 under which the WHATWG's living standards became the single canonical versions of HTML and DOM.
