American Buffalo (coin)

The American Buffalo, also called the American Buffalo gold bullion coin, is a 24-karat gold coin issued by the United States Mint beginning in 2006. It was the first widely issued U.S. coin struck in .9999 fine gold and was authorised by the Presidential $1 Coin Act of 2005.

Design

The coin adapts James Earle Fraser's Buffalo nickel design: a Native American profile on the obverse and an American bison on the reverse. Fraser's early twentieth-century imagery was chosen both for its popularity with collectors and for its distinctly American subject matter.

Issues

The standard one-ounce coin contains one troy ounce of gold and has a face value of 50 dollars, far below its metal value. Proof and burnished collector versions have been issued, and in 2008 the Mint briefly offered fractional sizes and a special set. Demand has varied with gold prices and collector interest.
