Laskarina Bouboulina

Laskarina Pinotsi, commonly known as Bouboulina (1771 – 22 May 1825), was a Greek naval commander, a heroine of the Greek War of Independence of 1821, and one of the first women in modern history to hold senior military command.

Life and war

Born in a Constantinople prison where her father was held by the Ottomans, Bouboulina was raised on Hydra and Spetses and twice widowed by husbands killed fighting Barbary pirates, inheriting fortunes she spent on ships and arms. A member of the secret Filiki Eteria, she built the brig Agamemnon, among the largest Greek warships, and commanded a fleet in the sieges of Nafplion and Monemvasia and the blockade of Ottoman forts.

Death and legacy

Bouboulina was killed in 1825 in a family feud shooting on Spetses. Russia posthumously honoured her with the rank of Admiral, and she appears on Greek coins, stamps and monuments; her Spetses home is now a museum.
