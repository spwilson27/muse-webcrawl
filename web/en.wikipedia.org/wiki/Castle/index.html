A castle is a type of fortified structure built during the Middle Ages predominantly by the nobility or royalty and by military orders. Scholars distinguish genuine castles from other fortifications by their dual role: a castle was both a defensible stronghold and the private fortified residence of a lord or noble, in contrast to a palace, which is not fortified, or a fortress, which was not necessarily a residence. The term derives from the Latin castellum, a diminutive of castrum (fort).

History

Castles originated in the 9th and 10th centuries, in the fragmented political landscape following the fall of the Carolingian Empire, when local lords fortified their homes to control surrounding territory. Early castles, such as the Norman motte-and-bailey, were built of earth and timber; stone construction became common from the 11th and 12th centuries. Contact with Byzantine and Islamic military architecture during the Crusades encouraged concentric designs with layered defences. From the late Middle Ages, the spread of gunpowder artillery undermined the castle's defensive value, and from the 16th century castles increasingly gave way to artillery fortresses on the one hand and unfortified country houses on the other. Romantic revivals in the 19th century produced picturesque sham castles such as Neuschwanstein in Bavaria.

Features

Characteristic elements include the keep (a strong central tower), curtain walls linking mural towers, a gatehouse, battlements and machicolations, and often a moat. Castles housed a garrison, the lord's household, stables, workshops and stores, enabling them to withstand sieges. Their siting — on hilltops, at river crossings and along borders — combined military advantage with a display of lordship.

Social role

Beyond defence, castles served as administrative centres for estates and districts, symbols of authority, and bases for controlling trade routes and populations. The building of castles was sometimes licensed by monarchs (licence to crenellate) in an effort to control private fortification.
