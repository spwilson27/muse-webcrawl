Fingerstyle guitar

Fingerstyle guitar is the technique of playing the guitar or bass guitar by plucking the strings directly with the fingertips, fingernails, or picks attached to fingers, as opposed to flatpicking (plucking individual notes with a single plectrum, commonly called a "pick").

Technique

Fingerstyle players use independent thumb and finger movements to sound bass lines, chords, and melodies simultaneously. Styles range from classical guitar technique to Travis picking, Piedmont blues fingerpicking, and percussive modern fingerstyle that incorporates tapping and body percussion.

History

The technique underpins classical, flamenco, folk, country blues, and ragtime guitar traditions, and was popularised by players such as Chet Atkins, Merle Travis, and Mississippi John Hurt.
