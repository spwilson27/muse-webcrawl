Eurovision Song Contest 1994

The Eurovision Song Contest 1994 was the 39th edition of the Eurovision Song Contest, held on 30 April 1994 at the Point Theatre in Dublin, Ireland, following Niamh Kavanagh's victory the previous year. It was presented by Cynthia Ní Mhurchú and Gerry Ryan and organised by RTÉ for the European Broadcasting Union. Twenty-five countries took part: Ireland's win made it the first country to win three contests in a row, and a record seven countries — Estonia, Hungary, Lithuania, Poland, Romania, Russia and Slovakia — made their debuts as the contest expanded eastwards, while relegation removed several western regulars including Italy and Luxembourg.

The Irish entry "Rock 'n' Roll Kids", written by Brendan Graham and performed by Paul Harrington and Charlie McGettigan, won with 226 points. Poland's Edyta Górniak finished second on the country's debut, despite controversy when she sang part of her song in English at the jury rehearsal.

Riverdance

The contest is remembered above all for its interval act: a seven-minute Irish dance performance called Riverdance, choreographed by Michael Flatley and Jean Butler, with music by Bill Whelan and the voices of the choir Anúna. Watched by some 300 million viewers, the performance was expanded the following year into a full stage show that became a worldwide phenomenon.
