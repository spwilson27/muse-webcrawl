Hiroyuki Nishimura
Born: 16 November 1976, Sagamihara, Kanagawa, Japan
Known for: Founder of 2channel (2ch); administrator of 4chan (2015– )

Hiroyuki Nishimura (西村 博之, born 16 November 1976), often known simply as Hiroyuki, is a Japanese internet entrepreneur. He founded the textboard website 2channel (2ch) in 1999 while studying in the United States, and it became one of Japan's most influential online forums.

Career

Nishimura was involved in the founding of Niwango, the company behind the video-sharing site Niconico, and has written best-selling books in Japan on work and argumentation. In September 2015, 4chan founder Christopher Poole announced that Nishimura had acquired the site and would succeed him as its administrator.

Legal matters

His tenure at 2channel involved numerous defamation lawsuits in Japan, many of which he declined to contest, and a long-running dispute over the site's ownership and domain that led to the 2ch/5ch split in 2017.
