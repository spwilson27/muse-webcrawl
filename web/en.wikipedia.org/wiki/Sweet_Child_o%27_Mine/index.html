Sweet Child o' Mine

"Sweet Child o' Mine" is a song by the American rock band Guns N' Roses, released in 1988 as the third single from their debut album Appetite for Destruction (1987). It became the band's only number-one single on the US Billboard Hot 100.

Background

The song grew out of a string exercise guitarist Slash performed during a jam session, which he initially considered a joke. Vocalist Axl Rose wrote the lyrics for his girlfriend, Erin Everly. The track is noted for Slash's opening guitar riff, frequently ranked among the greatest of all time, and for its extended guitar solo. The single topped the Billboard Hot 100 in September 1988, helping propel Appetite for Destruction to become the best-selling debut album in US history. The song has since been covered by artists including Sheryl Crow and has featured prominently in films and popular culture.
