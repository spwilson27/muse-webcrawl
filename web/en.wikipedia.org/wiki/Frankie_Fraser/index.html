Francis Davidson "Frankie" Fraser (13 December 1923 – 26 November 2014), better known as "Mad" Frankie Fraser, was an English gangster who spent 42 years in prison for his crimes, including work as an enforcer for Charlie Richardson's south London gang.

Criminal career

Fraser began stealing as a boy and served his first prison sentence at fourteen. During the 1960s he joined the Richardson Gang, rivals of the Krays, and was convicted in the notorious "Torture Trial" of 1967, receiving ten years. His later sentences included five years for leading the Parkhurst prison riot of 1969.

Later life

Released finally in 1989, Fraser became a minor celebrity, giving guided tours of London gangland, appearing on television and publishing an autobiography. He died in 2014, aged 90.
