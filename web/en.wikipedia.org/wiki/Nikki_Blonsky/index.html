Nikki Blonsky

BornNovember 9, 1988, Great Neck, New York, U.S.
OccupationActress, singer
Years active2007–present

Nikki Blonsky (born November 9, 1988) is an American actress and singer. She is best known for playing Tracy Turnblad in the 2007 film adaptation of the musical Hairspray, a role for which she received a Golden Globe nomination for Best Actress in a Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy.

Early life and breakthrough

Blonsky was raised in Great Neck, Long Island, New York. She saw the Broadway production of Hairspray as a teenager and dreamed of playing Tracy. While working at an ice cream parlour, she answered an open casting call for the film version and was selected from hundreds of applicants by director Adam Shankman, despite having no professional film experience. Her performance in Hairspray (2007), opposite John Travolta, Zac Efron, and Queen Latifah, won critical praise and several ensemble awards.

Career

Blonsky subsequently starred in the Lifetime film Queen Sized (2008) and the ABC Family series Huge (2010), playing Willamena Rader at a weight-loss camp. She has also appeared in films including Waiting for Forever and The Last Movie Star, made guest appearances on television, and performed in stage productions and her own one-woman cabaret shows. She has been an outspoken advocate for body positivity.
