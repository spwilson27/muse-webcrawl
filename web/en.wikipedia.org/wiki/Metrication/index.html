Metrication

Metrication is the act or process of converting a country's system of measurement to the metric system, the International System of Units (SI).

History

The metric system originated in Revolutionary France in the 1790s. Most countries adopted it during the 19th and 20th centuries. The United Kingdom began metrication in 1965 but retains miles and pints in some uses; the United States remains the major country not fully metricated, though metric is standard in science and industry.

Process

Conversion involves legislation, education, dual labelling, and changing road signs, packaging and engineering standards.
