Kevin Norwood Bacon (born July 8, 1958) is an American actor. He first gained prominence with the drama film Footloose (1984) and has since appeared in a wide range of films including JFK (1991), A Few Good Men (1992), Apollo 13 (1995), Mystic River (2003) and Frost/Nixon (2008). Bacon won a Golden Globe Award for his performance in the television film Taking Chance (2009) and starred in the drama series The Following (2013–2015) and City on a Hill (2019–2022).

Early life

Bacon was born in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, the youngest of six children of Ruth Hilda, an elementary school teacher and social activist, and Edmund Bacon, a prominent urban planner. At 16 he won a full scholarship to the Pennsylvania Governor's School for the Arts, and at 17 left home to pursue theatre in New York City, studying at the Circle in the Square Theatre School.

Career

Bacon made his film debut in National Lampoon's Animal House (1978) and appeared in Friday the 13th (1980) and Barry Levinson's Diner (1982) before his breakthrough as Ren McCormack in Footloose. Rather than rely on teen-idol roles, he built a reputation as a versatile character actor, playing both heroes and villains in films such as Tremors (1990), Flatliners (1990), Sleepers (1996), Hollow Man (2000), X-Men: First Class (2011) and Patriots Day (2016). With his brother Michael he forms the musical duo The Bacon Brothers.

"Six Degrees of Kevin Bacon"

Bacon is the namesake of the trivia game "Six Degrees of Kevin Bacon", based on the idea that any actor can be linked to him through film roles in no more than six steps, an application of the small-world phenomenon. The resulting "Bacon number" parallels the Erdős number in mathematics. Bacon initially disliked the game but embraced it, founding the charitable network SixDegrees.org in 2007.

Personal life

Bacon has been married to actress Kyra Sedgwick since 1988; they have two children, including actress Sosie Bacon. He was a prominent victim of Bernie Madoff's Ponzi scheme, having invested and lost money with Madoff.
