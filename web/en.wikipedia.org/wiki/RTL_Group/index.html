RTL Group is a Luxembourg-based international media conglomerate, with another corporate office in Cologne, Germany. It is the leading European entertainment network, operating television channels and radio stations in several countries, together with the global production company Fremantle. The group is majority-owned by the German media conglomerate Bertelsmann.

History

The company's roots lie in Radio Luxembourg, which began broadcasting in 1933 under the Compagnie Luxembourgeoise de Radiodiffusion (CLR), becoming famous across Europe for its English-language pop music service. Television followed with Télé Luxembourg in 1955. In 2000, the merger of CLT-UFA with Pearson TV created RTL Group; the production arm, renamed Fremantle, produces formats such as Idols, Got Talent and The X Factor.

Operations

RTL Group's interests include RTL Deutschland, Germany's largest commercial broadcaster; a controlling stake in France's Groupe M6; RTL Nederland; RTL Hungary; and streaming services RTL+ and Videoland. In 2024–2025 the group agreed to sell RTL Nederland to DPG Media and adjusted its portfolio as European broadcasters consolidated in response to global streaming competition.
