Public holidays in the Netherlands

The Netherlands has a relatively small number of official public holidays. There is no general statutory right to a day off on all of them; entitlements depend on employment contracts and collective agreements, though schools and many offices close on the main holidays.

List

  • New Year's Day – 1 January
  • Good Friday (optional for many employers)
  • Easter Monday
  • King's Day (Koningsdag) – 27 April, celebrated on 26 April if the 27th is a Sunday
  • Liberation Day – 5 May, an official holiday every year but a day off in many sectors only every five years
  • Ascension Day
  • Whit Monday (Pentecost Monday)
  • Christmas Day – 25 December
  • Boxing Day / Second Christmas Day – 26 December

King's Day, marking the birthday of King Willem-Alexander, is famed for street markets (vrijmarkt), orange dress and canal festivities, especially in Amsterdam.
