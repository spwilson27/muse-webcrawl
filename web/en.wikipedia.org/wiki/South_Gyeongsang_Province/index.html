South Gyeongsang Province

South Gyeongsang Province (Korean: 경상남도, Gyeongsangnam-do) is a province in the southeast of South Korea. The provincial capital is at Changwon. The province surrounds the metropolitan cities of Busan and Ulsan, which were separated from it administratively, and borders North Gyeongsang Province to the north and the Korea Strait to the south.

Geography and cities

The province includes the lower Nakdong River delta, mountainous interior, and a deeply indented coastline with islands such as Geoje. Major cities include Changwon (a planned industrial city and the capital since 1983), Gimhae, Jinju, Yangsan, Tongyeong, and Sacheon. The province administers 8 cities and 10 counties.

Economy

South Gyeongsang is one of Korea's industrial heartlands: shipbuilding (Geoje and Tongyeong yards), machinery and defence production in Changwon, aerospace at Sacheon (Korea Aerospace Industries), and agriculture and fisheries along the coast. Historic sites include the Haeinsa temple, storing the Tripitaka Koreana, and Tongdosa.

History

The province was formed in 1896 when Gyeongsang Province was divided into north and south. Busan was separated in 1963 and Ulsan in 1997 as metropolitan cities.
