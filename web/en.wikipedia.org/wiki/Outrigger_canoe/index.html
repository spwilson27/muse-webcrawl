Outrigger canoe

The outrigger canoe is a type of canoe featuring one or more lateral support floats known as outriggers, fastened to one or both sides of the main hull. Smaller canoes often employ a single outrigger; larger sailing vessels may use double outriggers (trimarans).

Developed by Austronesian peoples, outrigger canoes enabled the settlement of the Pacific Islands and Madagascar. They remain central to Pacific cultures and to the modern sport of outrigger canoe racing, popular in Hawaii and Tahiti.
