Crystal Palace F.C.

Full name: Crystal Palace Football Club
Nickname: The Eagles
Founded: 1905
Ground: Selhurst Park
Colours: Red and blue

Crystal Palace Football Club is a professional football club based in Selhurst in the London Borough of Croydon, England. Nicknamed "the Eagles", the club was founded in 1905 at the Crystal Palace exhibition building and has played its home games at Selhurst Park since 1924. Palace traditionally play in red and blue stripes, a kit adopted in the 1970s under Malcolm Allison.

History

The club joined the Football League in 1920 and spent much of its history moving between divisions. Its most successful era came under Steve Coppell, reaching the 1990 FA Cup final — losing to Manchester United after a replay — and finishing third in the First Division in 1990–91, the club's highest ever league placing. After administration in 1998 and again in 2010, Palace were promoted to the Premier League in 2013 and have remained there since, reaching a second FA Cup final in 2016.

Supporters and rivalries

Palace's main rivals are Brighton & Hove Albion, a rivalry dating from the 1970s, and south London neighbours Millwall and Charlton Athletic. The club's supporters, including the Holmesdale Fanatics ultras group, are known for the atmosphere at Selhurst Park. See also Selhurst Park and Premier League.
