Spaceship Earth is a dark ride attraction at the Epcot theme park at the Walt Disney World Resort in Bay Lake, Florida. It is also the name of the geodesic sphere that houses the attraction, which has served as the symbolic structure of Epcot since the park opened in 1982.

Structure

The structure is a complete geodesic sphere, similar in design to a Buckminster Fuller geodesic dome, composed of 11,324 silvered facets. It stands 180 feet (55 m) tall and rests on six legs. The sphere's outer skin is made of alucobond panels, and a gutter system collects rainwater and channels it into the World Showcase Lagoon so that no water pours off the sphere onto guests below.

The ride

Inside, an Omnimover system carries guests on a journey through the history of human communication, from prehistoric cave paintings through the invention of the alphabet, the printing press, and the telecommunications of the modern era, ending with a vision of the future. Scenes feature Audio-Animatronic figures, including Phoenician sailors, Egyptian scribes, Roman soldiers, Islamic scholars, Johannes Gutenberg, and Renaissance artists. Narration has been provided over the years by Vic Perrin, Walter Cronkite, Jeremy Irons, and, since 2008, Judi Dench. The attraction was sponsored by Bell System, AT&T, and Siemens at various times.

Legacy

The term "Spaceship Earth" was popularized by Buckminster Fuller, who used it to describe the planet as a vessel whose resources must be managed by its crew. The Epcot sphere remains one of the most recognized theme park icons in the world.
