Turn: Washington's Spies

Turn: Washington's Spies (originally titled Turn) is an American historical drama television series that aired on AMC from April 6, 2014, to August 12, 2017, running for four seasons and 40 episodes. It is based on Alexander Rose's book Washington's Spies: The Story of America's First Spy Ring (2007).

Plot

Set during the American Revolutionary War, the series follows Abe Woodhull (Jamie Bell), a cabbage farmer in Setauket, Long Island, who is recruited into the Culper Ring, a network of spies reporting to General George Washington on British movements in occupied New York. The story interweaves real figures such as Major John André, Peggy Shippen and Benedict Arnold with fictionalised drama.

Reception

The series received generally favourable reviews for its atmosphere and performances, though critics noted its slow early pacing and loose relationship to the historical record.
