List of World Heritage Sites in Africa

The United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) has designated over 140 World Heritage Sites in Africa. These sites include cultural landmarks such as ancient cities and rock art, and natural sites such as national parks and fossil localities. A selection of the best-known sites by country follows.
