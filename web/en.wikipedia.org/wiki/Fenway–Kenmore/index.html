Fenway–Kenmore

Fenway–Kenmore is an officially recognized neighborhood of Boston, Massachusetts, in the United States. The neighborhood consists of the two sub-neighborhoods of Fenway and Kenmore, divided by the Massachusetts Turnpike. It is known for Fenway Park, home of the Boston Red Sox, and for a dense concentration of colleges, universities, and cultural institutions.

Geography and institutions

The area encompasses Kenmore Square, the Back Bay Fens (part of Frederick Law Olmsted's Emerald Necklace park system), and the Museum of Fine Arts, Boston; the Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum; Boston University; Northeastern University; Berklee College of Music; and the Longwood Medical Area are in or adjacent to the neighborhood. Much of the Fenway was built on reclaimed land along the Muddy River in the late 19th and early 20th centuries.

Landmarks

Landmarks include Fenway Park (opened 1912), the Citgo sign in Kenmore Square, and Symphony Hall, home of the Boston Symphony Orchestra.
