Wreck-It Ralph is a 2012 American animated comedy film produced by Walt Disney Animation Studios and directed by Rich Moore. It is the 52nd Disney animated feature film. The story follows Ralph, the villain of a fictional arcade game, who sets out to become a hero.

Plot

For 30 years, Ralph has played the bad guy in the arcade game Fix-It Felix Jr., wrecking a building that Felix repairs. Ostracised by his game's characters, Ralph "game-jumps" to win a medal in the shooter Hero's Duty and then crashes into the kart-racing game Sugar Rush, where he befriends Vanellope von Schweetz, a "glitch" racer excluded from competition by King Candy. Ralph discovers King Candy is secretly Turbo, a rogue racer who took over the game, and helps Vanellope race, restoring her rightful place as the game's ruler. Ralph returns home reconciled to his role.

Voice cast

John C. Reilly voices Ralph, Sarah Silverman voices Vanellope, Jack McBrayer voices Fix-It Felix Jr., and Jane Lynch voices Sergeant Calhoun. The film features cameo appearances by numerous real video game characters, including Bowser, Doctor Eggman, Q*bert and Sonic the Hedgehog.

Reception and sequel

The film was a critical and commercial success, grossing about $471 million, and was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature. It won the Annie Award for Best Animated Feature. A sequel, Ralph Breaks the Internet, was released in 2018.
