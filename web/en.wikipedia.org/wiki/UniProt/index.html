UniProt (Universal Protein Resource) is a freely accessible database of protein sequence and functional information, maintained by an international consortium of the European Bioinformatics Institute (EMBL-EBI), the SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics, and the Protein Information Resource (PIR) in the United States. It is a central hub for the biomedical research community, linking protein sequences to literature, structures, pathways and other databases.

Components

UniProtKB

The UniProt Knowledgebase consists of two sections. UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot contains manually curated, reviewed entries with rich annotation, and descends from the Swiss-Prot database founded by Amos Bairoch in 1986. UniProtKB/TrEMBL contains computationally analysed, automatically annotated records awaiting manual curation, translated from nucleotide sequences in the EMBL-EBI/GenBank/DDBJ archives.

Other resources

UniRef provides clustered sets of sequences at 100%, 90% and 50% identity to speed up similarity searches. UniParc is a comprehensive, non-redundant archive of all publicly available protein sequences. UniProt also supplies reference proteomes and extensive cross-references to resources such as the Protein Data Bank, Gene Ontology and Ensembl.

History

UniProt was created in 2002 by merging the Swiss-Prot, TrEMBL and PIR databases under a consortium funded principally by the US National Institutes of Health and the European Commission. The database passed 200 million entries in the 2020s, driven by genome and metagenome sequencing. Predicted structures from AlphaFold are linked from UniProt entries.
