Cuomo family

Placeholder image: Cuomo family

The Cuomo family is an American political family of Italian descent based in New York. Its most prominent members are Mario Cuomo, who served as the 52nd governor of New York from 1983 to 1994, and his son Andrew Cuomo, who served as the 56th governor of New York from 2011 to 2021. The family has been a significant force in New York and national Democratic politics for decades.

Origins

Mario Cuomo's parents, Andrea and Immacolata Cuomo, immigrated to the United States from southern Italy. The family ran a grocery store in South Jamaica, Queens. Mario Cuomo (1932–2015) studied law at St. John's University and built a career as a lawyer and mediator before entering politics, serving as secretary of state of New York and then lieutenant governor before winning the governorship in 1982.

Mario Cuomo

Mario Cuomo served three terms as governor (1983–1994) and gained national prominence with his keynote address at the 1984 Democratic National Convention, the "Tale of Two Cities" speech. He was frequently mentioned as a presidential candidate but declined to run. He was married to Matilda Cuomo (née Raffa), who as First Lady of New York championed mentoring programs and founded Mentoring USA.

Andrew Cuomo

Andrew Cuomo (born 1957), Mario and Matilda's eldest son, served as U.S. Secretary of Housing and Urban Development under President Bill Clinton (1997–2001), then as attorney general of New York (2007–2010), before his three terms as governor. His tenure included the legalization of same-sex marriage in New York in 2011 and a high-profile role during the early COVID-19 pandemic. He resigned in 2021 amid a sexual harassment scandal and controversies over nursing home data.

Other members

Chris Cuomo (born 1970), the youngest son, became a television journalist, hosting programs on ABC and CNN before his dismissal from CNN in 2021. Margaret Cuomo, a daughter, is a radiologist and health advocate; Maria Cuomo Cole is a film producer; Madeline Cuomo is an attorney. Andrew Cuomo was previously married to Kerry Kennedy, a member of the Kennedy family, with whom he has three daughters.
