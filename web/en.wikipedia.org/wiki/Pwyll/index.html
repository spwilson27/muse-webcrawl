Pwyll

Pwyll Pen Annwn is a prince of Dyfed and the central figure of the First Branch of the Mabinogi, Pwyll Pendefig Dyfed ("Pwyll, Prince of Dyfed"). He is the husband of Rhiannon and father of Pryderi, the only character to appear in all Four Branches. His byname Pen Annwn ("Head of Annwn") refers to his year ruling the Otherworld.

The exchange with Arawn

While hunting at Glyn Cuch, Pwyll sets his hounds on a stag already brought down by a strange pack of white, red-eared hounds — the hounds of Arawn, king of Annwn. To make amends, Pwyll agrees to exchange shapes and kingdoms with Arawn for a year and a day, and at the term's end defeats Arawn's enemy Hafgan in single combat with one blow, as instructed. He rules Annwn wisely and refrains from Arawn's wife, earning the king's lasting friendship and his own epithet Pen Annwn.

Marriage to Rhiannon

Seated on the magical mound of Gorsedd Arberth, Pwyll sees the lady Rhiannon riding past on a white horse that no rider can overtake, though it seems to amble. He wins her from her unwanted betrothal to Gwawl ap Clud through her own stratagem involving a small bag that can never be filled. Their wedding feast is interrupted by Gwawl's demand, but Rhiannon's cleverness turns the tables.

The birth of Pryderi

After three childless years, Rhiannon bears a son, who vanishes on the night of his birth; her attendants accuse her of devouring the child. As penance she sits by the horse-block at Arberth telling her story to travellers. The boy is found by Teyrnon, lord of Gwent Is Coed, raised under the name Gwri Gwallt Euryn ("Gwri of the Golden Hair"), and eventually restored to his parents, receiving the name Pryderi from his mother's words of relief (pryder, "care/anxiety").
