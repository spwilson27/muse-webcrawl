Dr. Emmett Lathrop "Doc" Brown is a fictional character in the Back to the Future franchise, portrayed by Christopher Lloyd. An eccentric scientist and inventor, he creates the first time machine out of a DeLorean sports car, and serves as mentor and friend to the teenage protagonist Marty McFly.

Character

Doc Brown is characterised by wild white hair, manic energy, and catchphrases such as "Great Scott!" He conceived the flux capacitor — the device that makes time travel possible — after slipping in his bathroom and hitting his head on the sink in 1955. His time machine requires 1.21 gigawatts of power and a speed of 88 miles per hour to travel through time.

Appearances

Brown appears in all three films of the trilogy (1985, 1989, 1990), the animated series, and numerous games and cameos. Across the films he encounters his younger self in 1955, is stranded in 1885 where he falls in love with schoolteacher Clara Clayton, and ultimately settles in the nineteenth century, returning briefly in a time-travelling locomotive. Lloyd's performance made Doc one of the most recognisable scientists in popular cinema; the character was ranked among the greatest movie characters of all time in several polls.
