Image

An image is a visual representation of something. Images can be two-dimensional, such as a drawing, painting or photograph, or three-dimensional, such as a statue. They may be captured by optical devices — cameras, mirrors, lenses, telescopes and microscopes — or produced by the eye and visual system.

Types

A still image is a single static picture, as opposed to a moving image (film or video). Photographs, prints, digital graphics and screenshots are common still images. In computing, an image may also mean a complete copy of a storage medium, such as a disk image.

Characteristics

Images can be analogue or digital. Digital images are stored as raster graphics (grids of pixels) or vector graphics (geometric descriptions), and are characterised by resolution, colour depth and file format.

Mental imagery

In psychology, a mental image is an experience that resembles perceiving an object or scene without the object being present to the senses.
