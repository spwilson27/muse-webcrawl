List of Major League Baseball career hits leaders

The following is a list of Major League Baseball career hits leaders. Pete Rose holds the all-time record with 4,256 hits over 24 seasons, all but the first 880 of them with the Cincinnati Reds. The 3,000-hit club is one of baseball's most exclusive milestones, with 33 members.
