List of peace activists

This is a list of notable peace activists — people who have campaigned for the abolition of war, nuclear disarmament, conscientious objection, and nonviolent resolution of conflict.

Historical figures

  • Mahatma Gandhi — leader of Indian independence through nonviolence (satyagraha).
  • Martin Luther King Jr. — civil rights leader and anti-war campaigner.
  • Bertrand Russell — philosopher; anti-nuclear campaigner, Russell–Einstein Manifesto.
  • Leo Tolstoy — writer whose pacifist works influenced Gandhi.
  • Jane Addams — founder of the Women's International League for Peace and Freedom; Nobel Peace Prize 1931.
  • Albert Einstein — signatory of the 1955 Russell–Einstein Manifesto.
  • A. J. Muste, Dorothy Day, Bayard Rustin — American pacifist organisers.
  • Thich Nhat Hanh — Vietnamese monk who campaigned to end the Vietnam War.
  • Daniel Ellsberg — released the Pentagon Papers.
  • Mairead Maguire and Betty Williams — Peace People, Northern Ireland.
  • Archbishop Desmond Tutu — anti-apartheid and peace campaigner.
  • Jody Williams — led the campaign to ban landmines.

Organisations

Activists have worked through bodies such as the Campaign for Nuclear Disarmament, Greenpeace, War Resisters' International, and the International Campaign to Abolish Nuclear Weapons (ICAN), Nobel laureate in 2017.
