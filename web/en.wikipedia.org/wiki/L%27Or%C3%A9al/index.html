L'Oréal

L'Oréal Groupe
Founded: 1909 by Eugène Schueller, Paris
Headquarters: Clichy, France
Industry: Cosmetics; world's largest cosmetics company

L'Oréal is a French multinational personal care corporation and the world's largest cosmetics company, headquartered in Clichy, Hauts-de-Seine.

History

Chemist Eugène Schueller founded the company in 1909 around a hair dye formula he sold to Parisian hairdressers. His daughter Liliane Bettencourt became the principal shareholder; the Bettencourt Meyers family remains the largest shareholder, with Nestlé also holding a major stake historically.

Brands

The group markets brands across price tiers: L'Oréal Paris, Garnier, Maybelline, Lancôme, Kiehl's, CeraVe, La Roche-Posay, and luxury perfumes under licence for houses such as Giorgio Armani and Yves Saint Laurent. Its slogan "Because You're Worth It" dates to 1973. See Cosmetics.
