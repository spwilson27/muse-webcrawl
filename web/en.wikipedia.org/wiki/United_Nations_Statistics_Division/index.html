The United Nations Statistics Division (UNSD), part of the UN Department of Economic and Social Affairs, is the central office for statistics within the United Nations system. It compiles and disseminates global statistical information, develops standards and methods for statistical activities, and supports countries' efforts to strengthen their national statistical systems.

UNSD serves as the secretariat to the UN Statistical Commission and maintains major databases including UN Comtrade (international trade statistics), the Demographic Yearbook, and national accounts data.

Standards

The Division maintains the standard country and area codes known as M49, used throughout the UN system, and coordinates international classifications such as the System of National Accounts. It also supports statistical monitoring of the Sustainable Development Goals through the Inter-Agency and Expert Group on SDG Indicators.
