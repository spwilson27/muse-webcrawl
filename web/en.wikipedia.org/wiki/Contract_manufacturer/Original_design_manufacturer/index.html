Original design manufacturer

An original design manufacturer (ODM) is a company that designs and manufactures a product, as specified, that is eventually rebranded by another firm for sale. Such companies allow the brand firm to produce (either as a supplement or solely) without having to engage in the organization or running of a factory. ODMs have grown in size in recent years and, as of 2015, many have the scale to handle production in-house for products that are branded by the buying firm. This is in contrast to a contract manufacturer (CM), which manufactures a product according to the customer's own design.

Business model

This model is especially used in international trade, where a local ODM is used to produce goods for a foreign company that sees some advantage in the transaction, such as low labour-inputs, transport links or proximity to markets. Innovative and/or patented technologies developed by the ODM are also a factor driving this business model. The products designed by the ODM are protected as the intellectual property of the ODM, similar to the products designed by the service-requesting companies being their intellectual property.

ODM arrangements are common in the electronics, personal computer and appliance industries. Taiwanese companies such as Compal, Quanta and Wistron design and manufacture laptop computers sold under many global brands; similar arrangements exist for smartphones, televisions and white goods.

Comparison with OEM

An original equipment manufacturer (OEM) traditionally produces parts or products according to the buyer's specifications or design, whereas an ODM supplies its own design which the buyer brands as its own. In practice the terms are often used loosely, and a single company may act as OEM, ODM and contract manufacturer for different customers.
