Lucid Group, Inc., trading as Lucid Motors, is an American electric vehicle manufacturer headquartered in Newark, California. Founded in 2007 as Atieva, it originally supplied battery packs and powertrains before pivoting to complete vehicles.

Vehicles

Its first car, the Lucid Air luxury sedan, entered production in Casa Grande, Arizona in 2021 and achieved EPA ranges exceeding 500 miles in some versions — among the longest of any production EV. The Lucid Gravity SUV followed, with production beginning in late 2024. Lucid emphasises efficiency, miniaturised drive units developed from Formula E battery supply experience.

Financing

Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund invested over $1 billion in 2018 and became majority owner. Lucid went public in 2021 via a SPAC merger with Churchill Capital Corp IV, valued at about $24 billion. A second plant, AMP-2, opened in King Abdullah Economic City, Saudi Arabia in 2023.
