E. Remington and Sons

E. Remington and Sons
Founded: 1816, Ilion, New York
Founder: Eliphalet Remington
Products: Firearms, typewriters, sewing machines

E. Remington and Sons (1816–1896) was a manufacturer of firearms and typewriters founded in Ilion, New York, by Eliphalet Remington. It was the original form of the company that became the Remington Arms Company.

Firearms

Remington began by forging rifle barrels and grew into one of America's largest arms makers, supplying rifles, carbines and revolvers to the Union during the Civil War and exporting the Remington Rolling Block rifle worldwide. The company also produced shotguns, pistols and ammunition.

Typewriters and other products

In 1873 Remington began manufacturing the Sholes and Glidden typewriter, the first commercially successful typewriter and the origin of the QWERTY keyboard layout. The typewriter business was sold in 1886 and became the Remington Typewriter Company, later part of Remington Rand. Remington also made sewing machines, agricultural implements and bridges. Financial difficulties led to receivership in 1886 and reorganisation; the firearms business continued as Remington Arms.
