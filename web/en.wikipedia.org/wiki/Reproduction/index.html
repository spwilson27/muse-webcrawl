Reproduction

Reproduction is the biological process by which new organisms are produced from parents. It is a fundamental feature of Life, occurring asexually (one parent) or sexually (two parents, genetic recombination).

Asexual reproduction

Bacteria divide by fission; many plants reproduce vegetatively; some animals bud, fragment, or reproduce by parthenogenesis. Offspring are (mostly) clones — fast and dependable, but genetically uniform, which Population genetics shows can be risky in changing environments.

Sexual reproduction

Meiosis halves chromosome number to make gametes; Fertilisation restores it, shuffling alleles into new combinations — a major engine of the variation Natural selection acts on. Its costs (finding mates, halved gene transmission) versus its benefits remain a classic question of Evolution. In humans and other mammals development proceeds through the Embryo and fetal stages to birth.
