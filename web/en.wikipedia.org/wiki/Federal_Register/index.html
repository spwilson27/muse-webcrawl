Federal Register

The Federal Register (FR or Fed. Reg.) is the official journal of the federal government of the United States that contains government agency rules, proposed rules, and public notices.

History and contents

It was established under the Federal Register Act of 1935, with the first issue published on 14 March 1936, and is published every business day by the National Archives and Records Administration (NARA). Contents include executive orders and proclamations, documents with general applicability and legal effect, proposed rules open for public comment, and notices of meetings.

Access

The Federal Register is available in print, via the Government Publishing Office, and online at federalregister.gov, which provides machine-readable data and improved search. Final rules are subsequently codified in the Code of Federal Regulations (CFR).
