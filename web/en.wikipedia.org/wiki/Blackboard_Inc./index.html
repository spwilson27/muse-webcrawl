Blackboard Inc.

Type: Private
Industry: Educational technology
Founded: 1997
Headquarters: Reston, Virginia, United States
Products: Blackboard Learn, Blackboard Collaborate

Blackboard Inc. is an American educational technology company best known for its learning management system, Blackboard Learn. Founded in 1997, the company provides software and services to schools, universities, businesses and government agencies in more than 100 countries.

History

Blackboard grew out of CourseInfo, a company formed by Stephen Gilfus and Daniel Cane at Cornell University, and Blackboard LLC, founded by Matthew Pittinsky and Michael Chasen. The two merged in 1997. Blackboard went public in 2004 and was taken private by Providence Equity Partners in 2011.

Products

The Blackboard Learn platform allows instructors to post course materials, assignments, discussions and grades online. Blackboard Collaborate provides virtual classroom and web-conferencing tools. In 2021 Blackboard merged with Anthology, another higher-education software firm.
