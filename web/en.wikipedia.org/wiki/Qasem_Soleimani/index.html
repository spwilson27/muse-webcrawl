Qasem Soleimani (Persian: قاسم سلیمانی; 11 March 1957 – 3 January 2020) was an Iranian military officer who served as commander of the Quds Force of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps from 1998 until his assassination by the United States in a drone strike near Baghdad International Airport.

Career

Soleimani joined the Revolutionary Guards after the 1979 revolution and fought in the Iran–Iraq War. As Quds Force commander he directed Iran's support for allied militias and governments across the Middle East, including Hezbollah in Lebanon, the Assad government in Syria, and Shia militias in Iraq, and he coordinated operations against the Islamic State in Iraq and Syria.

Assassination and aftermath

His killing, ordered by US President Donald Trump, sharply escalated tensions between the United States and Iran; Iran retaliated with missile strikes on US bases in Iraq. Millions attended his funeral ceremonies in Iran, and he is officially regarded there as a martyr.
