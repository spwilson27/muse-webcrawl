Miss Universe

Miss Universe is an annual international beauty pageant run by the Miss Universe Organization. Founded in 1952 by the California clothing company Pacific Mills, it is one of the most widely watched pageants in the world, with national winners competing in interview, swimsuit and evening gown rounds.

History

The first Miss Universe, Armi Kuusela of Finland, was crowned in Long Beach, California in 1952. The pageant passed through several owners, including Donald Trump, who co-owned it from 1996 to 2015, and the talent agency IMG/Endeavor, before its purchase in 2022 by Thai businesswoman Anne Jakrajutatip's JKN Global Group.

Format and titleholders

Contestants are selected in national pageants in more than ninety countries. The winner undertakes a year of travel and advocacy for the organisation's charitable causes. Venezuela, the United States, the Philippines and Puerto Rico are among the most successful countries in the competition's history.
