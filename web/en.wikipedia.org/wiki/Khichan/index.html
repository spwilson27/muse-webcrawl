Khichan is a village in Phalodi district of Rajasthan, India. It is internationally known as a wintering ground for large flocks of migratory demoiselle cranes, which are fed daily by the local community.

Demoiselle cranes

Each winter, thousands of demoiselle cranes arrive from Central Asia and Mongolia. Villagers, following a tradition associated with the Jain community, scatter grain in designated feeding enclosures (chugga ghars). The feeding practice dates to the 1970s and has grown into a major birdwatching attraction.
