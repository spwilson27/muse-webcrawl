Burger wars

The burger wars are a series of off-and-on advertising campaigns by competing fast-food hamburger chains in the United States, beginning in the late 1970s. The principal rivals are McDonald's, Burger King, and Wendy's, whose comparative and often confrontational campaigns sought to win customers through taste claims, size comparisons, and price promotions.

Burger wars
PeriodLate 1970s–present
Main rivalsMcDonald's, Burger King, Wendy's
ArenaUS fast-food advertising

History

Burger King's 1982 "Battle of the Burgers" ads claimed taste-test victories and provoked a lawsuit from McDonald's. Wendy's 1984 "Where's the beef?" campaign mocked competitors' small patties and became a cultural catchphrase. Later skirmishes included value-menu price wars in the 1990s and 2000s, and social-media sparring in the 2010s, notably Wendy's provocative Twitter presence.

Impact

The campaigns are a classic case study in comparative advertising, credited with sharpening brand identities and driving product launches such as flame-grilled positioning for Burger King.
