Media buying

Media buying is the process of purchasing advertising space and time on media platforms — television, radio, print, outdoor, cinema, and digital media — on behalf of an advertiser. It follows media planning, in which the target audience, objectives, and channel mix are decided; media buyers then negotiate rates and placements to obtain the desired reach and frequency at the lowest effective cost.

Process

Buyers evaluate media using audience measurement data (ratings, circulation, impressions, CPM), negotiate discounts and value-added placements, book inventory, and verify that advertisements actually ran as ordered (through affidavits, post-buy analysis, and ad verification). In digital media, much buying is now automated through programmatic advertising platforms and real-time bidding, although direct deals and upfront television commitments remain significant.

Industry structure

Most large advertisers delegate media buying to specialized agencies, including the media networks of holding groups such as WPP's GroupM (now WPP Media), Omnicom Media Group, and Publicis Media, whose combined billings give them substantial negotiating leverage with media owners.
