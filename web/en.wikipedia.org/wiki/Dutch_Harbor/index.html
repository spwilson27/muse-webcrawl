Dutch Harbor

Dutch Harbor is a harbour on Amaknak Island in Unalaska, Alaska. It was a natural port for vessels of the Russian-American Company and later the most important fishing port in the United States by volume of seafood landed. The harbour lies within the city of Unalaska, which covers parts of both Unalaska Island and Amaknak Island, connected by bridge.

History

The name arose because a Dutch ship is recorded as the first to enter the harbour. Dutch Harbor was a United States naval operating base and fortified port in the Second World War; on 3–4 June 1942 Japanese carrier aircraft bombed it during the Aleutian Islands campaign, one of the few attacks on the continental United States. The navy departed in the 1990s, leaving bunkers and the aerodrome as legacies.

Fishing industry

Since 1989 the port of Dutch Harbor has ranked first in the United States in quantity of seafood landed, including pollock and king crab, thanks to the huge Bering Sea fisheries. The crab fleet based here became famous worldwide through the television series Deadliest Catch, which is filmed largely in and around the port.
