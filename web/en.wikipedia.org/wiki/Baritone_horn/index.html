Baritone horn

Baritone horn
ClassificationBrass, aerophone
RangePitched in B♭, same as tenor trombone and euphonium
RelatedEuphonium, tenor horn, tuba

The baritone horn, or simply baritone, is a low-pitched brass instrument in the saxhorn family. It is pitched in B♭ and sounds a major ninth below written pitch. The baritone is distinguished from the euphonium by its smaller, predominantly cylindrical bore, which gives it a brighter, lighter tone; the euphonium's largely conical bore produces a darker, rounder sound.

Use

Baritones are a fixture of British-style brass bands, where the section typically comprises first and second baritones covering middle harmonies, and they also appear in concert bands, particularly in American school ensembles where a compact "American baritone" with a forward-facing bell was long common. The instrument reads both treble and bass clef in brass band and concert band practice.
