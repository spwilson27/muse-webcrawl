Xyzzy (computing)

Xyzzy
OriginColossal Cave Adventure (1976–77)

Xyzzy is a magic word in the classic text adventure game Colossal Cave Adventure (1976–77). Typing it inside the game's starting building instantly transports the player to a chamber deep in the cave, and vice versa, making it one of the earliest and most famous cheat-like shortcuts in video games.

Origin

Colossal Cave Adventure, written by Will Crowther and expanded by Don Woods, was set in a cave system modelled on Kentucky's Mammoth Cave. The word XYZZY worked as a teleport command, and the game also featured its companion magic word PLUGH. Generations of players attempted XYZZY in later programs, and many programmers hid responses to it as an Easter egg.

Use in computing culture

Because of the game's influence, "xyzzy" became a metasyntactic variable in hacker culture, alongside foo and bar, and is used as a placeholder password, filename or test string. The word survives widely: it appears in software documentation, in jokes embedded in operating systems and games, and as the name of the annual XYZZY Awards for interactive fiction. A common mnemonic for the spelling is as a mnemonic for crossing (X) the Y and Z axes.
