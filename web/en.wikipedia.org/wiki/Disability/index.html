Disability

Disability
DefinitionPhysical or mental condition limiting movement, senses or activities; shaped by environment
ModelsMedical model vs social model; UN CRPD (2006)
[Image: A wheelchair user entering a building via a ramp beside stairs]

Disability is any condition of body or mind that makes it harder for a person to do certain activities or interact with the world — with the social model emphasising that inaccessible environments disable as much as impairments do. Over a billion people live with significant disability.

Rights

The UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (2006) reframed disability as a rights issue — access, inclusion, independent living. Paralympic sport (Swimming, Running), assistive technology and universal design flow from the same shift.
