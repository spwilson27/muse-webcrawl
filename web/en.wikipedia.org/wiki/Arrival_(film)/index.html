Arrival is a 2016 American science fiction drama film directed by Denis Villeneuve and written by Eric Heisserer. Based on the 1998 short story "Story of Your Life" by Ted Chiang, it stars Amy Adams as Louise Banks, a linguist enlisted by the United States Army to discover how to communicate with extraterrestrials who have arrived on Earth, before tensions lead to war. Jeremy Renner and Forest Whitaker co-star.

Plot

Twelve alien spacecraft appear at locations around the world. Linguist Louise Banks is recruited to lead an investigative team at the landing site in Montana, alongside theoretical physicist Ian Donnelly. Inside the craft, the seven-limbed aliens — dubbed "heptapods" — communicate through complex circular written symbols. As Banks learns their language, she begins to perceive time non-linearly, experiencing visions of her future life, including a daughter who will die young. When global tensions escalate toward war, Banks uses her newfound perception of time to avert catastrophe by communicating with a Chinese general at a key moment eighteen months in the future.

Production

The film was produced by FilmNation Entertainment, Lava Bear Films and 21 Laps Entertainment, and distributed by Paramount Pictures. Jóhann Jóhannsson composed the score, Bradford Young served as cinematographer, and the heptapod language's circular logograms were designed with input from linguists. Principal photography took place in Montreal, Canada.

Reception

Arrival premiered at the Venice Film Festival on September 1, 2016, and was widely acclaimed for its acting, direction, screenplay, score and thoughtful treatment of language and time. It grossed over $203 million worldwide and was nominated for eight Academy Awards, including Best Picture, Best Director and Best Adapted Screenplay, winning Best Sound Editing. Adams's performance was singled out for particular praise, and the film is frequently cited among the best science fiction films of the 21st century.
