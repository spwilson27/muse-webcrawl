Wizarding World

The Wizarding World (previously known as J. K. Rowling's Wizarding World) is a fantasy media franchise and shared fictional universe centred on the Harry Potter series of novels by J. K. Rowling. The franchise is owned and administered by Warner Bros. Entertainment in partnership with Rowling.

Overview

The core of the franchise is the eight Harry Potter films (2001–2011), adapted from Rowling's seven novels, which collectively grossed over US$7.7 billion worldwide. The franchise expanded with the Fantastic Beasts prequel film series, beginning in 2016, the stage play Harry Potter and the Cursed Child (2016), theme park lands at Universal parks, exhibitions, video games such as Hogwarts Legacy (2023), and the Pottermore/Wizarding World digital platform.

The fictional world

The wizarding world is a society of witches and wizards hidden in secret alongside the non-magical ("Muggle") world, governed by bodies such as the British Ministry of Magic and the Magical Congress of the United States. Central locations include Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, Diagon Alley and the village of Hogsmeade.
