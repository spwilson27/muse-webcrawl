The list of state parks of the United States is organized by state. The United States has more than 6,500 state park units, covering roughly 18 million acres (about 7.3 million hectares) and receiving several hundred million visitors a year — far more visits in aggregate than the national parks. Lists for each state include state parks proper as well as, in separate articles, state recreation areas, state forests and state historic sites where these are managed by the same agencies.

Every U.S. state operates a state park system; the remaining state-by-state lists follow the same format in the complete article series.

Largest and oldest

Alaska's Wood-Tikchik State Park, at about 1.6 million acres, is the largest state park in the United States. Niagara Falls State Park in New York, established in 1885, is generally regarded as the oldest state park in the country, while Mackinac Island in Michigan was first a national park before becoming a state park in 1895.
