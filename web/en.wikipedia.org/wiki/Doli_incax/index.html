Doli incax

Doli incax is a species of small predatory sea snail, a marine gastropod mollusk in the family Cancellariidae, the nutmeg snails.

Scientific classification
Kingdom: Animalia
Phylum: Mollusca
Class: Gastropoda
Family: Cancellariidae
Genus: Doli
Species: D. incax

Description and distribution

The shell of Doli incax is small, ovate and cancellate (sculptured with intersecting axial and spiral ribs), typical of the nutmeg snails. The species is recorded from marine waters in the Indo-Pacific region. Like other cancellariids, it is a carnivore, feeding on other marine invertebrates.

Taxonomy

The species was described in the 19th century during the period of extensive conchological description of Indo-Pacific molluscan faunas, and has at times been placed in the genus Cancellaria sensu lato before the splitting of that genus.
