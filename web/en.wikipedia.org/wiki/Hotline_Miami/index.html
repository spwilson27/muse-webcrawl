Hotline Miami is a top-down shooter video game developed by Dennaton Games and published by Devolver Digital. Set in Miami in 1989, the game follows an unnamed protagonist, dubbed "Jacket" by fans, who receives cryptic messages on his answering machine instructing him to commit massacres against the local Russian mafia. The game blends top-down perspective with stealth, extreme violence and surreal storytelling, along with a soundtrack and visuals influenced by 1980s culture.

Gameplay

Hotline Miami is divided into chapters, in each of which the player takes the role of a mostly unnamed protagonist carrying out hits against criminal hideouts. Before each mission, the protagonist selects an animal mask to wear, each granting a different ability, such as faster movement or starting with a specific weapon. Combat is fast and lethal: a single hit usually kills both enemies and the player, encouraging careful planning and rapid restarts. Weapons include melee items such as baseball bats and crowbars, as well as firearms that attract attention from across the map.

Plot

The story begins on 3 April 1989, when Jacket receives a voicemail asking him to deliver "cookies" to an address that turns out to be a Russian mob hideout. As the messages continue, he encounters other masked figures, a mysterious video store clerk named Beard, and a dying woman he rescues and nurses back to health. The narrative grows increasingly surreal, blurring the line between reality and hallucination, and a parallel storyline follows a vigilante known as Biker, whose investigation intersects with Jacket's.

Development and reception

The game was created by Jonatan "Cactus" Söderström and Dennis Wedin, who had previously collaborated on the game Keyboard Drumset Fucking Werewolf. Hotline Miami received critical acclaim for its soundtrack, which features artists such as M|O|O|N, Perturbator and Scattle, its neon-soaked aesthetic, and its tight, demanding gameplay. A sequel, Hotline Miami 2: Wrong Number, was released in 2015 and concluded the series' storyline.
