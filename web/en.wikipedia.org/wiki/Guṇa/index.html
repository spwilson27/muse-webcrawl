Guṇa

Guṇa
Tradition: Samkhya, Hindu philosophy
The three guṇas: Sattva, Rajas, Tamas
Meaning: "Quality, strand"

Guṇa (Sanskrit: गुण, "quality, peculiarity, attribute") is a concept in Hindu philosophy, central to Samkhya, which holds that all of nature (prakriti) is composed of three fundamental qualities or tendencies.

The three guṇas

Sattva is purity, light, harmony, and goodness; Rajas is passion, activity, and movement; Tamas is darkness, inertia, and ignorance. All material phenomena and mental states are mixtures of these three in varying proportions.

Applications

The guṇa framework is applied in the Bhagavad Gita, Ayurveda, and Yoga philosophy to classify foods, actions, personalities, and modes of worship. Liberation involves transcending the guṇas, becoming trigunatita (beyond the three qualities).
