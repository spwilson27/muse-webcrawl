The University of Calgary (UCalgary) is a public research university in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. It began in 1945 as the Calgary branch of the University of Alberta and gained independence in 1966. It now enrols more than 33,000 students in 14 faculties.

Research

The university is a member of the U15 group of Canadian research universities, with strengths in energy and environmental research, health sciences through the Cumming School of Medicine, engineering, and veterinary medicine. It operates research facilities including the Rothney Astrophysical Observatory and a campus in Doha, Qatar.

Campus and sport

The main campus in northwest Calgary adjoins facilities built for the 1988 Winter Olympics, including the Olympic Oval speed-skating venue, which remains a training and research site. The varsity teams are known as the Dinos.
