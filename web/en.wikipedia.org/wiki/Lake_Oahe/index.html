Lake Oahe

Lake Oahe is a large reservoir behind Oahe Dam on the Missouri River in South Dakota and North Dakota, United States. It stretches 231 miles (372 km) from Pierre, South Dakota, to Bismarck, North Dakota, making it one of the largest reservoirs in the United States by volume.

History

Oahe Dam was constructed by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers under the Pick–Sloan Plan; construction began in 1948 and the dam was dedicated in 1962. Flooding for the reservoir displaced residents of the Standing Rock and Cheyenne River reservations and inundated fertile bottomlands.

Recreation

The lake is a major walleye fishery and offers numerous recreation areas along its shores.
