European route E4

European route E4 is a north–south European route running from Helsingborg in southern Sweden to Tornio in northern Finland, with a length of about 1,590 kilometres (990 mi). It passes through Stockholm and along the Swedish coast of the Gulf of Bothnia before crossing into Finland.

Route

From Helsingborg the road runs northeast through Jönköping and Norrköping to Stockholm, then north via Uppsala, Gävle, Sundsvall, Örnsköldsvik (crossing the High Coast Bridge), Umeå, Skellefteå, and Luleå to Haparanda at the Finnish border, continuing a short distance to Tornio. The Swedish section forms the backbone of the country's road network and is built largely to motorway or expressway standard in the south, with narrower sections in the far north.

History

Under the original 1950 numbering the E4 ran from Lisbon to Stockholm, but in the 1992 renumbering of the E-road network the route was reassigned to the Swedish–Finnish corridor. Sweden, unusually, did not fully renumber its national signage, and the E4 designation in Sweden retains strong national recognition.
