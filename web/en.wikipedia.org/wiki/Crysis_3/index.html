Crysis 3 is a 2013 first-person shooter video game developed by Crytek and published by Electronic Arts. It is the third main instalment in the Crysis series and was released for Microsoft Windows, PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360 in February 2013. A remastered version was released in 2021 as part of Crysis Remastered Trilogy.

Plot and gameplay

The game is set in 2047, twenty-four years after Crysis 2. The player controls Prophet, whose consciousness persists in the Nanosuit after the death of Alcatraz, as he returns to a New York City encased in a giant "Nanodome" by the corrupt CELL Corporation, where the city has decayed into an overgrown urban rainforest. Prophet seeks revenge against the Alpha Ceph, the leader of the alien Ceph invasion. Gameplay retains the series' Nanosuit abilities — armour and cloaking modes — and adds a signature composite bow, hackable enemy technology, and larger, more open levels than its predecessor, mixing the jungle environments of the first game with the urban setting of the second. A multiplayer mode supports up to 12 players on consoles and 16 on PC.

Reception

Crysis 3 received generally favourable reviews, with praise for its graphics — powered by CryEngine 3, it was widely used as a PC hardware benchmark — and criticism for its short campaign and story. Sales were considered disappointing by EA despite selling over three million copies across the series' contemporaneous titles, and the franchise remained dormant until the announcement of a fourth entry in 2022.
