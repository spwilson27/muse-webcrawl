Shallow (Lady Gaga and Bradley Cooper song)

"Shallow" is a song performed by Lady Gaga and Bradley Cooper for the 2018 film A Star Is Born. It was written by Gaga with Mark Ronson, Anthony Rossomando and Andrew Wyatt, and produced by Gaga with Benjamin Rice. Released on 27 September 2018 as the lead single from the film's soundtrack, it is performed in the film's pivotal scene in which Cooper's character, Jackson Maine, invites Gaga's Ally onstage to sing it before a crowd.

Composition

The song is a folk-pop and country-rock power ballad that builds from a quiet, fingerpicked opening to a soaring final chorus, mirroring the lyric's movement from self-doubt toward emotional risk: "I'm off the deep end, watch as I dive in." Its call-and-response structure turns Jackson's verses and Ally's answers into the film's central conversation about fame, addiction and love.

Reception and awards

"Shallow" topped charts in more than twenty countries, including the US Billboard Hot 100, and became one of the best-selling digital singles of all time. It won the Academy Award for Best Original Song, the Golden Globe for Best Original Song, two Grammy Awards including Song of the Year, and a BAFTA. The live performance by Gaga and Cooper at the 91st Academy Awards was widely praised.

Legacy

Beyond its awards, the song became a defining pop-culture moment of 2018–19, widely covered and parodied, and it anchored the soundtrack album, which topped the Billboard 200 and was certified multi-platinum.
