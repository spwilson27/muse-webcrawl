A hand is a prehensile, multi-fingered appendage located at the end of the forearm or forelimb of primates such as humans, chimpanzees, monkeys, and lemurs. A few other vertebrates, such as the koala, also have two opposable thumbs and functionally similar hands. The human hand normally has five digits: four fingers plus one thumb, which is opposable in humans and old-world monkeys, allowing the hand to grip and manipulate objects with precision.

Structure

The hand comprises the wrist (carpus) of eight carpal bones; the palm (metacarpus) of five metacarpal bones; and the digits, each consisting of phalanges — three per finger and two in the thumb. Movement is produced by intrinsic muscles lying within the hand and extrinsic muscles of the forearm acting through long tendons. Sensation and motor control are supplied mainly by the median, ulnar, and radial nerves, and blood by the radial and ulnar arteries, which form superficial and deep palmar arches.

Function

Hands serve both motor and sensory roles. The precision grip between thumb and fingers and the power grip of the whole hand underlie tool use, writing, and manual work. The palmar skin is rich in mechanoreceptors, making the hand a principal organ of touch; the fingers are central to Braille reading and to sign languages. Handedness — a preference for one hand — is present in most humans, with a large majority right-handed.

Evolution

The pentadactyl forelimb of tetrapods evolved into fins, wings, and limbs of many forms; in primates, arboreal life favoured grasping hands with flattened nails rather than claws. Human hand proportions, with a relatively long, robust, fully opposable thumb, are adapted for both forceful and precise manipulation and are closely connected with the evolution of stone tool manufacture.

Clinical significance

Common conditions of the hand include fractures, carpal tunnel syndrome, Dupuytren's contracture, arthritis, trigger finger, and infections such as felons and flexor tenosynovitis. Hand surgery is a subspecialty of orthopaedic and plastic surgery; hand transplantation has been performed since the late 1990s.
