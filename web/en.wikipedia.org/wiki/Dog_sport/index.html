Dog sport

Dog sports are competitive and recreational activities in which dogs participate, usually together with their handlers. Sports test qualities such as speed, agility, obedience, scenting ability, retrieving and herding instinct.

Types

Popular dog sports include agility, in which dogs negotiate an obstacle course against the clock; flyball, a relay race over hurdles; obedience trials; rally obedience; dock jumping (dock diving); disc dog; herding trials; tracking and nose work; lure coursing; and canicross and bikejoring, in which dogs pull runners or cyclists. Historically, races such as greyhound racing and sled dog racing are also dog sports, though they raise distinct welfare debates.

Organisation

Competitions are sanctioned by kennel clubs and dedicated bodies such as the American Kennel Club, The Kennel Club (UK), the Fédération Cynologique Internationale and sport-specific associations.
