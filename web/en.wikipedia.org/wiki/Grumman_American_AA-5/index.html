Grumman American AA-5

RoleFour-seat light aircraft
ManufacturerAmerican Aviation / Grumman American
First flight1970

The Grumman American AA-5 series is a family of American all-metal, four-seat, light aircraft used for touring and training. Developed from the two-seat AA-1 Yankee, the AA-5 was first produced by American Aviation and later by Grumman American, Gulfstream American, and most recently Tiger Aircraft.

Variants

The original AA-5 Traveler (1971) was powered by a 150 hp Lycoming O-320 engine. The AA-5A Cheetah (1975) added aerodynamic refinements, and the AA-5B Tiger (1975), with a 180 hp Lycoming O-360, became the best-known variant, with a cruising speed around 139 knots. Production by Tiger Aircraft in the 2000s revived the type as the AG-5B Tiger.

Design

The aircraft features bonded aluminium honeycomb construction, a sliding canopy, and a distinctive steerable nosewheel. It is noted for responsive handling and good visibility.
