Syria

Syria, officially the Syrian Arab Republic, is a country in West Asia, in the Levant, bordered by Turkey, Iraq, Jordan, Israel and Lebanon, with a Mediterranean coast. Its population is about 23 million (including refugees abroad, estimates vary) and its capital is Damascus, often called the world's oldest continuously inhabited capital.

Syria was a crossroads of empires — Palmyra, Aleppo and Damascus are ancient Silk Road cities. The French mandate gave way to independence in 1946 and Ba'ath Party rule from 1963; Hafez al-Assad ruled from 1970, succeeded by his son Bashar in 2000. The 2011 uprising became a catastrophic civil war involving Russia, Iran, Turkey, the US and ISIS, killing over 500,000 people and displacing half the population. In December 2024 a rebel offensive led by Hay'at Tahrir al-Sham toppled the Assad regime in eleven days, ending 53 years of Assad family rule; a transitional government under Ahmed al-Sharaa took power amid sanctions relief and continuing sectarian violence.
