The Suicide Squad, also known as Task Force X, is a fictional team in DC Comics publications, consisting of incarcerated supervillains who undertake high-risk black-ops missions for the United States government in exchange for reduced sentences. The team is directed by Amanda Waller, who maintains control through explosive implants placed in the members' necks or brains.

Publication history

The original Suicide Squad, a team of non-powered adventurers led by Rick Flag, debuted in 1959. The modern incarnation was created by writer John Ostrander in the 1986–87 Legends miniseries and starred in its own acclaimed series from 1987 to 1992. Recurring members have included Deadshot, Harley Quinn, Captain Boomerang, Bronze Tiger, Count Vertigo, and Killer Croc. The concept has been repeatedly revived, including in the New 52 reboot (2011) and subsequent series.

In other media

The Squad has appeared in animated series such as Justice League Unlimited and in the DC Extended Universe films Suicide Squad (2016), which won an Academy Award for makeup and hairstyling despite poor reviews, and James Gunn's The Suicide Squad (2021), which was critically acclaimed. The video game Suicide Squad: Kill the Justice League was released in 2024.
