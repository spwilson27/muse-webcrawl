Mile run

The mile run (1,760 yards, or exactly 1,609.344 metres) is a middle-distance foot race. It was historically the premier middle-distance event, though metric distances such as the 1500 metres are now standard in most international competition.

History

The quest for the first four-minute mile culminated on 6 May 1954, when Roger Bannister ran 3:59.4 at Oxford, paced by Chris Brasher and Chris Chataway. John Landy broke the record weeks later. The mile remains a featured event in meetings in Britain and the United States.

Records

The men's world record of 3:43.13 was set by Hicham El Guerrouj in Rome in 1999. The women's record was lowered by Faith Kipyegon to 4:07.64 in 2023, breaking a long-standing mark.
