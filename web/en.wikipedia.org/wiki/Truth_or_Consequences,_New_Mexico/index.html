Truth or Consequences is a city in New Mexico, United States, and the county seat of Sierra County. In 2020, the population was 6,052. It has frequently been noted on lists of unusual place names for having been renamed after a popular radio quiz show. The city is built around hot springs along the Rio Grande and is often shortened to "T or C".

History

The area was first settled around the Geronimo Springs, used for centuries by Apache peoples, and the town developed in the early twentieth century as the health resort of Hot Springs, New Mexico, following construction of Elephant Butte Dam (completed 1916), which created a nearby reservoir for recreation and irrigation.

Name change

In 1950, Ralph Edwards, host of the NBC radio quiz show Truth or Consequences, announced that he would broadcast the program's 10th anniversary episode from the first town to rename itself after the show. Hot Springs officials won the competition, and the town officially changed its name to Truth or Consequences on 31 March 1950; the show broadcast from there the following evening. Edwards visited annually for the town's Fiesta for the next fifty years. Voters twice rejected proposals to revert to the name Hot Springs in referendums.

Geography and attractions

The city lies on the Rio Grande between Elephant Butte Reservoir and Caballo Lake. Its historic bathhouse district contains numerous hot springs spas. Spaceport America, the commercial spaceport from which Virgin Galactic operates, is located in the desert about 30 miles southeast of the city, which serves as its gateway community.
