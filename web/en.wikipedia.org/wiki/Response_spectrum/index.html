Response spectrum

FieldEarthquake engineering, structural dynamics
InputGround motion record
OutputPeak response vs. natural period

A typical acceleration response spectrum

A response spectrum is a plot of the peak response (displacement, velocity, or acceleration) of a series of single-degree-of-freedom oscillators, each with a different natural period and typically the same damping, subjected to the same ground motion. It is a central tool of earthquake engineering.

Use

Given a recorded or design ground acceleration, engineers compute the maximum response of oscillators across a range of periods. The resulting spectrum lets a designer estimate the peak force and deformation demands on a real structure from its period and mode shapes, without performing a full time-history analysis.

Design spectra

Building codes present smoothed design response spectra that represent the expected seismic hazard at a site, scaled by soil conditions and importance factors. The elastic spectrum may be reduced by a response-modification factor to account for ductility and overstrength.
