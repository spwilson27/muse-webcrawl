Polyploidy

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Polyploidy is a condition in which the cells of an organism have more than two complete sets of chromosomes. Such organisms are called polyploids: triploid (3 sets), tetraploid (4 sets), pentaploid (5 sets), hexaploid (6 sets), and so on. Polyploidy is especially common in plants — it has occurred in the ancestry of all seed plants — and also occurs in some amphibians, fish, invertebrates, and fungi. It is rare in mammals, where it is usually lethal, though some polyploid mammal species exist.

Mechanisms

Polyploidy arises mainly through the production of unreduced (diploid) gametes or through somatic chromosome doubling, often caused by failure of cytokinesis. Two broad types are distinguished. In autopolyploidy, all chromosome sets come from a single species, typically by doubling of its own genome. In allopolyploidy, hybridization between two species is followed by doubling, which restores fertility by giving each chromosome a pairing partner in meiosis. The chemical colchicine, which disrupts the spindle, is used experimentally and in plant breeding to induce polyploidy.

Evolutionary significance

Polyploidy can create new species in a single generation, especially in plants, because crosses between a polyploid and its diploid parents usually fail (the "triploid block"). Whole-genome duplication events punctuate plant evolution — most flowering plants descend from ancient polyploids — and two rounds of whole-genome duplication occurred early in vertebrate evolution (the "2R hypothesis"). Duplicated genes can diverge and take on new functions, providing raw material for evolution.

Examples

  • Bread wheat (Triticum aestivum) is an allohexaploid combining genomes of three species.
  • Cotton, potatoes (tetraploid), coffee, and oats (hexaploid) are polyploid crops.
  • Strawberries are octoploid; cultivated bananas and seedless watermelons are triploid and therefore sterile.
  • Salmonid fish and the frog Xenopus laevis descend from polyploid ancestors; certain whiptail lizards reproduce as triploid parthenogens.
  • The sturgeon family includes species with very high ploidy levels.

Advantages and disadvantages

Polyploid plants are often larger, more vigorous ("gigas" effects), and better able to tolerate environmental stress and inbreeding, since duplicate gene copies mask deleterious recessive alleles. Disadvantages include disrupted meiosis and reduced fertility in odd-ploidy individuals, larger cell size with slower development, and genomic instability following genome doubling. In humans, polyploid conceptions (triploidy, tetraploidy) occur in a small percentage of pregnancies and are almost always lethal.
