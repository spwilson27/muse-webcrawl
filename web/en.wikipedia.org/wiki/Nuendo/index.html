Nuendo

Nuendo is a digital audio workstation (DAW) developed by the German company Steinberg for music recording, arranging, editing, and post-production. First released in 2000, it shares much of its audio engine and interface with Steinberg's Cubase, but adds features aimed at film, television, video game audio, and immersive sound production.


Developer: Steinberg
Initial release: 2000
Operating system: Windows, macOS
Type: Digital audio workstation

Features

Nuendo supports multitrack recording and mixing, MIDI sequencing, VST plug-ins, and surround and immersive formats including Dolby Atmos production workflows. Its post-production toolset includes automatic dialogue replacement (ADR) tools, field recorder workflows, loudness metering and normalisation, and direct integration with game audio middleware such as Audiokinetic Wwise. It also offers advanced video playback, reconforming tools for tracking picture edits, and extensive networking and collaboration functions.

Reception and use

Nuendo is widely used in film and television post-production houses, game studios, and broadcast facilities, where its ADR and game-audio features distinguish it from music-oriented workstations. Major version releases have progressively expanded its immersive audio, dialogue editing, and AI-assisted tools such as voice separation and speech-to-text transcription for subtitle and dialogue workflows.
