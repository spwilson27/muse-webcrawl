Miss World 1994

Date19 November 1994
VenueSun City Entertainment Centre, Sun City, South Africa
Entrants87
WinnerAishwarya Rai (India)

Miss World 1994, the 44th edition of the Miss World pageant, was held on 19 November 1994 at the Sun City Entertainment Centre in Sun City, South Africa. Eighty-seven contestants competed, and at the end of the event Lisa Hanna of Jamaica crowned Aishwarya Rai of India as her successor.

Results

The first runner-up was Basetsana Makgalemele of South Africa, and the second runner-up was Irene Ferreira of Venezuela. Rai's victory, following her runner-up finish at Femina Miss India earlier that year, launched her career as one of India's most celebrated actresses and international film stars.

Context

The 1994 pageant was the third consecutive edition hosted in Sun City. It took place in the year of South Africa's first democratic elections, and the presence of a South African runner-up was widely noted in coverage of the event.
