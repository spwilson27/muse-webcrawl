String Theory in Anti-de Sitter Space

String theory in anti-de Sitter (AdS) space is the gravitational side of the AdS/CFT correspondence: type IIB string theory on AdS5 × S5, type IIA strings on AdS4 × CP3, and M-theory on AdS4 × S7 or AdS7 × S4 are each dual to conformal field theories. These backgrounds are among the few in which string theory can be studied beyond supergravity approximation.

From branes to geometry

A stack of N coincident D3-branes has two descriptions: open strings on the branes give N = 4 super Yang–Mills theory, while the branes' gravitational back-reaction curves spacetime into AdS5 × S5 near the horizon. Equating the two descriptions is the AdS/CFT duality; the AdS radius in string units is set by the 't Hooft coupling, so weak coupling on the gauge side is strong curvature physics — elementary strings — on the gravity side.

Integrability

Strings on AdS5 × S5 form an integrable worldsheet theory: both the string sigma model and the dual gauge theory's dilatation operator can be attacked with Bethe ansatz techniques. This "AdS/CFT integrability" has produced exact results for operator dimensions, such as the cusp anomalous dimension, interpolating between weak and strong coupling.

Beyond supergravity

Because the duality is exact, AdS backgrounds let physicists ask how stringy α′ corrections and quantum gs corrections deform classical geometry, and test proposals — from Wilson loops computed by minimal surfaces to holographic entanglement entropy — at every order. Related constructions extend to AdS3/CFT2, where string theory is comparatively solvable.
