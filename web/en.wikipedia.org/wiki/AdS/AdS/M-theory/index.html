M-theory in the AdS/CFT correspondence

AdS / M-theory

Field: String theory, M-theory
Related duality: AdS/CFT correspondence
Key objects: M2-branes, M5-branes, AdS4×S7, AdS7×S4

M-theory in the context of the AdS/CFT correspondence describes how eleven-dimensional M-theory on anti-de Sitter backgrounds is holographically dual to lower-dimensional conformal field theories. Whereas the original 1997 duality of Juan Maldacena related type IIB string theory on AdS5×S5 to N=4 supersymmetric Yang–Mills theory, its M-theory analogues involve membranes rather than strings.

AdS4 × S7 and ABJM theory

M-theory on AdS4×S7, sourced by a stack of M2-branes, is dual to a three-dimensional superconformal Chern–Simons–matter theory. In 2008 Aharony, Bergman, Jafferis and Maldacena (ABJM) gave a concrete Lagrangian description for coincident M2-branes, opening precision tests of holography in three dimensions, including Wilson loops, free energies and the localisation of the S3 partition function.

AdS7 × S4

M-theory on AdS7×S4, sourced by M5-branes, is dual to the six-dimensional (2,0) superconformal theory, a theory with no conventional Lagrangian description. Holography predicts its entropy, anomalies and the behaviour of its surface operators, providing one of the main windows into this otherwise mysterious theory.

Significance

These dualities extend holography beyond string theory proper, show that geometry and gravity can emerge from non-gravitational quantum field theories without strings, and connect M2- and M5-brane physics to exactly solvable quantities in conformal field theory.
