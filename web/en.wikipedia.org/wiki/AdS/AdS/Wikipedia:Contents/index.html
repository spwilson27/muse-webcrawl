Wikipedia: Contents — Anti-de Sitter (AdS) section

This contents portal gathers the articles in this recreation devoted to anti-de Sitter space (AdS) and the AdS/CFT correspondence — the duality between gravity in an AdS bulk and a conformal field theory on its boundary.

Core topics

  • Anti-de Sitter space — geometry, coordinates, and causal structure
  • Conformal field theory
  • AdS/CFT correspondence
  • Black hole information paradox

Related areas

  • String theory and M-theory backgrounds such as AdS5×S5
  • Holographic principle
  • Supergravity
  • Quantum gravity

Applications

AdS methods are applied to strongly coupled gauge theories, quark–gluon plasma, condensed-matter systems, and black hole thermodynamics. Browse the topics above to navigate the section.
