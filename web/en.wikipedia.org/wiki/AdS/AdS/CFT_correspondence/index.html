AdS/CFT Correspondence

The anti-de Sitter/conformal field theory correspondence, frequently abbreviated as AdS/CFT, is a conjectured duality in theoretical physics stating that a theory of quantum gravity in anti-de Sitter (AdS) space is equivalent to a conformal field theory (CFT) living on the boundary of that space. Proposed by Juan Maldacena in 1997, it is the most successful realization of the holographic principle.

In AdS/CFT, gravitational physics in the bulk of AdS space is encoded in a field theory on its boundary.

Overview

In the original and best-understood example, type IIB string theory on the product space AdS5 × S5 is equivalent to N = 4 supersymmetric Yang–Mills theory, a quantum field theory on the four-dimensional boundary. Because one side involves gravity and the other does not, the duality gives a non-perturbative definition of string theory in AdS backgrounds and a powerful computational tool: hard strongly-coupled gauge-theory problems map to easier classical gravity problems, and vice versa.

Evidence and applications

Precision tests compare symmetries, spectra of operators, and correlation functions on both sides; results agree in every case where both sides are computable. The correspondence has been applied to quark–gluon plasma physics (predicting a famously low shear-viscosity-to-entropy ratio), condensed matter systems such as strange metals and superconductors, and the quantum information theory of spacetime, including the Ryu–Takayanagi formula relating entanglement entropy to minimal surfaces in the bulk.

Relation to black holes

Thermal states of the boundary CFT correspond to black holes in the AdS bulk, and Hawking–Page transitions in the bulk mirror confinement transitions in the gauge theory. The duality underlies modern approaches to the black hole information problem and to reconstructing bulk spacetime from boundary entanglement.
