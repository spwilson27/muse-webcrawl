Supersymmetric Yang–Mills theory

Supersymmetric Yang–Mills
Type: Gauge theory with supersymmetry
Key example: N = 4 SYM in four dimensions

Supersymmetric Yang–Mills theory is Yang–Mills gauge theory extended with supersymmetry, pairing gauge bosons with fermionic superpartners (gauginos) and often including scalar fields. The most studied example is N = 4 supersymmetric Yang–Mills theory, a conformal field theory in four dimensions with gauge group SU(N).

Role in AdS/CFT

In 1997 Juan Maldacena conjectured that N = 4 SYM is exactly dual to type IIB string theory on AdS5 × S5. Calculations of correlation functions, Wilson loops, scattering amplitudes, and black hole thermodynamics via Hawking radiation in AdS have provided extensive checks of this duality.

Properties

N = 4 SYM is finite and conformally invariant, its beta function vanishing to all orders. It is a leading laboratory for integrability, amplitude methods, and non-perturbative dualities such as S-duality inherited from string theory.

Other versions

Supersymmetric Yang–Mills theories exist in various dimensions, with different amounts of supersymmetry, and arise from dimensional reduction of ten-dimensional SYM, the worldvolume theory of branes in related settings.
