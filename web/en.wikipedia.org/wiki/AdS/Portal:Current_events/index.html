Portal: Anti-de Sitter space — Current events

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This portal page collects recent developments in research on anti-de Sitter space and holography. The field remains one of the most active areas of theoretical physics, with ongoing work connecting the AdS/CFT correspondence to quantum information theory, condensed matter physics, and quantum error correction.

Recent themes

  • Islands and the black hole information problem: computations using "island" formulas in AdS gravity reproduced the Page curve for evaporating black holes, a major step in reconciling Hawking radiation with unitarity.
  • Holographic quantum error correction: researchers continue to develop the picture of bulk spacetime emerging from boundary entanglement, including tensor-network models such as HaPPY codes.
  • AdS condensed matter: holographic models of strange metals, superconductors, and quantum critical points remain an active testing ground for strongly coupled systems.
  • Precision holography: bootstrap and localization techniques have produced exact results for correlation functions in maximally supersymmetric versions of the duality, checked against supergravity calculations.
