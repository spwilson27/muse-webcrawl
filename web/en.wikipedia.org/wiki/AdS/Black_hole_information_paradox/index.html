Black hole information paradox — in AdS/CFT

The black hole information paradox arises from the apparent conflict between quantum mechanics, which requires information to be preserved, and Stephen Hawking's 1974 calculation that black holes emit thermal radiation and evaporate, seemingly destroying information. Within anti-de Sitter space, the AdS/CFT correspondence offers a framework in which the paradox can be addressed, because evaporation in the bulk corresponds to unitary evolution in the boundary conformal field theory.

Background

Hawking radiation appears purely thermal, so a black hole formed from a pure quantum state would end in a mixed state — violating unitarity. Proposed resolutions include information escaping in subtle correlations in the radiation, remnants, complementarity, and firewalls.

The AdS perspective

In AdS/CFT, a black hole in the bulk is dual to a thermal state of the boundary CFT, whose evolution is manifestly unitary; therefore bulk evaporation must also be unitary. Computations of the Page curve using "islands" and replica wormholes (2019 onwards) reproduce the expected rise and fall of radiation entropy, suggesting information does escape. Holographic entanglement entropy (the Ryu–Takayanagi formula) underpins these results.
