Notice of readiness

A notice of readiness (NOR) is a notification given by the master of a ship to the charterer (or other relevant party under a charterparty) that the vessel has arrived at the agreed destination and is ready in all respects to load or discharge its cargo. Tendering a valid notice of readiness normally starts the running of laytime — the period allowed for loading or unloading — after which demurrage may become payable if the charterer exceeds the time allowed.

Requirements

For a notice of readiness to be valid, the vessel must generally be an "arrived ship" within the meaning of the charterparty (under a port charter, she must have reached the port; under a berth charter, the berth), must be physically and legally ready to work the cargo, and the notice must be tendered in the manner and within the hours the charterparty prescribes. A notice given when the vessel is not in fact ready is generally invalid, and disputes over the validity and timing of NORs are a frequent subject of shipping arbitration.
