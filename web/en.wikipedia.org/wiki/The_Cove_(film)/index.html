The Cove (film)

The Cove
Director: Louie Psihoyos
Year: 2009
Subject: Dolphin drive hunting in Taiji, Japan
Award: Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature

The Cove is a 2009 American documentary film directed by Louie Psihoyos, following former dolphin trainer Ric O'Barry and the Oceanic Preservation Society as they covertly film the annual dolphin drive hunt in a guarded cove at Taiji, Wakayama, Japan.

Content

Using hidden cameras and divers, the team documents the killing of dolphins for meat (with high mercury levels) and capture of others for marine parks, criticising Japan's whaling policy and the International Whaling Commission.

Reception

It won the Oscar for Best Documentary Feature and audience awards at Sundance, sparking international controversy and debate in Japan.
