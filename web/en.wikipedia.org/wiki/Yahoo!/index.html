Yahoo! (styled yahoo!) is an American web services provider. It provides a web portal, the search engine Yahoo Search, and related services including Yahoo Mail, Yahoo News, Yahoo Finance, Yahoo Sports, and the advertising platform Yahoo Native. It is operated by the namesake company Yahoo Inc., which is owned 90% by Apollo Global Management and 10% by Verizon.

History

Yahoo was founded by Jerry Yang and David Filo in January 1994 while they were graduate students at Stanford University. It began as "Jerry and David's guide to the World Wide Web", a human-edited directory of websites, and was renamed Yahoo! — an acronym for "Yet Another Hierarchically Organized Oracle", though the founders insisted the name was chosen for its slang meaning of a rude, uncouth person. The company went public in April 1996 and became one of the defining companies of the dot-com boom; at its peak in January 2000 its shares traded at an all-time high that valued the company at more than $125 billion.

Yahoo was a pioneer of the early internet era and at its height was the most popular starting point for web users. It survived the dot-com collapse but struggled against the rise of Google in search and later social media platforms. In 2008 Microsoft made an unsolicited bid of $44.6 billion to acquire the company, which Yahoo rejected; the decision was widely criticised by shareholders.

Under CEO Marissa Mayer (2012–2017), Yahoo acquired the blogging platform Tumblr in 2013 for $1.1 billion. In 2016 Yahoo disclosed two major data breaches affecting more than a billion user accounts, the largest known breaches of their time, which reduced the price of its pending sale. In 2017 Verizon Communications acquired Yahoo's core internet business for $4.48 billion, combining it with AOL into a subsidiary initially called Oath Inc. and later Verizon Media. In 2021 Verizon sold the media group to Apollo Global Management for about $5 billion, and the business was renamed Yahoo.

Services

Yahoo Mail, launched in 1997 after the acquisition of RocketMail, remains one of the largest free email services. Yahoo Finance and Yahoo Sports are leading destinations in their categories, and Yahoo Search continues to operate, powered for many years by Microsoft's Bing under a 2009 search partnership.
