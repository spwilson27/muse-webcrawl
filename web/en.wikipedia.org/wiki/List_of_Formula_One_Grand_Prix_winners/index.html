List of Formula One Grand Prix winners

Formula One, abbreviated to F1, is the highest class of open-wheeled auto racing sanctioned by the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA). The World Championship season has been held since 1950. This article lists every driver to have won a World Championship Grand Prix.

Milestones

Giuseppe Farina won the first World Championship race, the 1950 British Grand Prix at Silverstone. Lewis Hamilton surpassed Michael Schumacher's long-standing record of 91 victories at the 2020 Portuguese Grand Prix and became the first driver to reach 100 wins at the 2021 Russian Grand Prix. Michael Schumacher and Lewis Hamilton share the record for wins at a single circuit (eight) and the most wins in a season was set by Max Verstappen with 19 in 2023. The youngest Grand Prix winner is Max Verstappen (18 years, 228 days, 2016 Spanish Grand Prix); the oldest is Luigi Fagioli (53 years, 1951 French Grand Prix, a shared drive).

By constructor

Ferrari is the most successful constructor with more than 240 wins, followed by McLaren, Mercedes, Williams and Red Bull Racing.
