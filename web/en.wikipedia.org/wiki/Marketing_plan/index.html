Marketing plan

A marketing plan is a comprehensive document or blueprint that outlines a business's advertising and marketing efforts for the coming year. It describes business activities involved in accomplishing specific marketing objectives within a set time frame, and usually forms part of an overall business plan.

Contents

A typical marketing plan includes a situation analysis (often a SWOT analysis of strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats), a description of target markets and customer segments, measurable objectives, the marketing strategy expressed through the marketing mix — product, price, place and promotion (the "four Ps") — together with budgets, timelines, responsibilities and the metrics by which success will be evaluated.

Purpose

The plan coordinates an organisation's promotional activities, allocates resources, and provides a benchmark against which actual performance can be measured. It links the firm's marketing function to its financial goals and helps ensure that campaigns, product launches and pricing decisions reinforce one another rather than conflict.

Types

Plans may be strategic (covering several years), annual, or specific to a product, brand, market segment or campaign. Digital marketing plans address channels such as search, social media and email, increasingly written and shared in lightweight formats like Markdown. Effective plans respond to changing conditions such as a shifting market trend rather than remaining fixed documents.
