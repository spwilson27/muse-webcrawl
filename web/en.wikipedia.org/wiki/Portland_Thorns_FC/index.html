Portland Thorns FC

Portland Thorns FC is an American professional women's soccer team based in Portland, Oregon, that competes in the National Women's Soccer League (NWSL). Founded in 2012 as one of the league's eight charter teams, the Thorns play their home matches at Providence Park and have consistently drawn the largest crowds in the NWSL — and for several years, larger average attendances than many top-flight men's teams worldwide.

History

The Thorns were announced in November 2012, taking their name from Portland's nickname, the Rose City. Allocated stars included Tobin Heath, Alex Morgan, and Canadian captain Christine Sinclair, who became the club's defining player and the all-time leading goalscorer in international football. Portland won the inaugural NWSL Championship in 2013, defeating the Western New York Flash. Under coach Mark Parsons, the club won the NWSL Shield in 2016 and a second championship in 2017.

In 2021 the Thorns won the NWSL Challenge Cup, the Shield, and the Women's International Champions Cup. They captured a third star in 2022, beating the Kansas City Current 2–0 in the final, with Sophia Smith named league MVP. The club was sold by Peregrine Sports to the Bhathal family's RAJ Sports in early 2024.

Supporters

The Rose City Riveters, the club's supporters' group, are renowned for their tifo displays and for helping establish a culture of large-scale support for women's club soccer, regularly drawing crowds above 15,000 and peaking over 25,000.

Rivalries

The Thorns' fiercest rival is Seattle Reign FC (now OL Reign / Seattle Reign), echoing the Cascadia rivalry seen in the men's game in the Portland Timbers–Seattle Sounders rivalry.
