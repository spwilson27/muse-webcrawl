Okoye (Marvel Cinematic Universe)

Okoye is a fictional character in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU), portrayed by Danai Gurira. She is the general of the Dora Milaje, the all-female special forces of the African nation of Wakanda, and one of King T'Challa's most trusted advisors and warriors. The character is based on the Marvel Comics figure of the same name, who first appeared in Black Panther vol. 3 #1 (1998).

Appearances

Okoye debuted in the MCU in Black Panther (2018), in which she helps T'Challa reclaim the throne from Erik Killmonger, remaining loyal to the throne even when it means opposing her husband, W'Kabi. She reprised the role in Avengers: Infinity War (2018), fighting in the Battle of Wakanda; Avengers: Endgame (2019), in which she coordinates with the Avengers during the five years after the Blip; and Black Panther: Wakanda Forever (2022), in which she is stripped of her rank after failing to protect Princess Shuri and later returns wearing the new Midnight Angel armour. Gurira has also voiced the character in the animated series What If...? (2021).

Characterisation

Okoye is characterised by absolute devotion to Wakanda and its throne, formidable combat skill with her vibranium spear, dry wit, and adherence to Dora Milaje tradition — symbolised by her bald, tattooed head and her distaste for the wig she wears as a disguise in a casino sequence in Black Panther. Critics praised Gurira's performance and the character's representation of African female strength and loyalty.
