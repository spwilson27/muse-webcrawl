Nasdaq Nordic

Nasdaq Nordic is the common name for the subsidiaries of Nasdaq, Inc. that provide securities trading services in the Nordic and Baltic countries of northern Europe. It operates the regulated exchanges in Stockholm, Copenhagen, Helsinki, Iceland, Tallinn, Riga, and Vilnius, as well as the Nasdaq First North growth markets.

History

The group descends from OMX, the Swedish-Finnish exchange operator formed in 2003 by the merger of OM and HEX, which progressively consolidated the Nordic and Baltic exchanges. In 2008 OMX was acquired by the American Nasdaq Stock Market, forming NASDAQ OMX Group; the Nordic operations were subsequently rebranded as Nasdaq Nordic (and Nasdaq Baltic for the three Baltic exchanges).

Exchanges and indices

Principal marketplaces include Nasdaq Stockholm (whose benchmark is the OMXS30), Nasdaq Copenhagen (OMXC25), Nasdaq Helsinki (OMXH25), and Nasdaq Iceland (OMXI), together with the Baltic exchanges of Tallinn, Riga, and Vilnius. The group also runs Nasdaq First North, a multilateral trading facility aimed at smaller growth companies, and offers derivatives, commodities, and clearing services through related Nasdaq entities in the region.
