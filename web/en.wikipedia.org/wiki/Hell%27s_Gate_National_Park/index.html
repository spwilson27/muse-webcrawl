Hell's Gate National Park

LocationRift Valley Province, Kenya
Nearest townNaivasha
Area68.25 km²
Established1984
Governing bodyKenya Wildlife Service

Hell's Gate National Park is a national park in Kenya, south of Lake Naivasha and north-west of Nairobi. Named for a narrow break in its red cliffs — once a channel of a prehistoric lake — the park is noted for its dramatic scenery of towering cliffs, gorges, rock towers and geothermal steam vents.

Wildlife and activities

The park protects buffalo, zebra, eland, hartebeest, gazelles, baboons and over 100 bird species, including lammergeiers nesting on the cliffs. Unusually for a Kenyan national park, visitors may walk and cycle among the wildlife, and the Ol Njorowa Gorge and Fischer's Tower are popular for hiking and rock climbing. The park borders the Olkaria geothermal power stations. Hell's Gate is said to have inspired the scenery of the 1994 film The Lion King.
