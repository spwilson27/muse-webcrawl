Piedmont Triad

The Piedmont Triad (or simply the Triad) is a metropolitan region in north-central North Carolina formed by the three cities of Greensboro, Winston-Salem, and High Point. The region, lying in the Piedmont plateau between the mountains and the coastal plain, is home to roughly 1.7 million people in its combined statistical area.

Economy and transport

The Triad was historically a centre of textiles, furniture (High Point is known as the "Furniture Capital of the World"), and tobacco (Winston-Salem). Its economy has diversified into logistics, healthcare, education, and finance. Piedmont Triad International Airport serves the region, and the area sits at the junction of several interstate highways, including I-40 and I-85.

Education

Major institutions include Wake Forest University, the University of North Carolina at Greensboro, North Carolina A&T State University, Winston-Salem State University, and High Point University.
