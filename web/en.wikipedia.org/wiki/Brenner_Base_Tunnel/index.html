Brenner Base Tunnel

Location: Austria–Italy border, Eastern Alps

Length: 55 km (64 km with link tunnels)

Status: Under construction

The Brenner Base Tunnel (German: Brenner Basistunnel; Italian: Galleria di base del Brennero) is a planned 55-kilometre-long railway tunnel through the Eastern Alps beneath the Brenner Pass, connecting Innsbruck in Austria with Fortezza in Italy. Together with existing bypass tunnels it forms part of the Berlin–Palermo corridor of the Trans-European Transport Network.

Project

The twin-bore tunnel, with an exploratory tunnel beneath, is intended to shift freight from the heavily congested Brenner motorway to rail and cut passenger travel times between Innsbruck and Bolzano sharply. Construction began in earnest in the 2010s and completion is expected in the early 2030s.
