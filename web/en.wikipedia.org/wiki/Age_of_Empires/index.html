Age of Empires

Age of Empires is a series of historical real-time strategy video games, originally developed by Ensemble Studios and published by Xbox Game Studios. The first game, Age of Empires, was released in 1997 and followed humanity from the Stone Age to the Iron Age. The series is known for its historical campaigns, civilisation-specific units and technologies, and the progression of settlements through successive "ages." Seven main titles have been released, along with spin-offs such as Age of Mythology.

Games

  • Age of Empires (1997) — Stone Age to Iron Age; expansion The Rise of Rome (1998).
  • Age of Empires II: The Age of Kings (1999) — the Middle Ages; expansion The Conquerors (2000). Widely regarded as one of the greatest strategy games, it received an HD edition (2013) and Definitive Edition (2019).
  • Age of Mythology (2002) — a spin-off featuring Greek, Egyptian, Norse, and later Atlantean and Chinese mythologies.
  • Age of Empires III (2005) — European colonisation of the Americas; expansions The WarChiefs and The Asian Dynasties.
  • Age of Empires IV (2021) — developed by Relic Entertainment; returns to the medieval setting with documentary-style campaign presentation.

Gameplay

Players gather resources (food, wood, gold, and stone), construct buildings, research technologies, and train civilian and military units to defeat opponents. Most titles structure progress around advancing through ages—such as the Dark, Feudal, Castle, and Imperial Ages in Age of Empires II—unlocking stronger units and buildings. Single-player campaigns follow historical figures and events, including Joan of Arc, Genghis Khan, William Wallace, and Saladin. Multiplayer matches and a scenario editor are longstanding features.

Development and reception

Ensemble Studios, founded in Dallas, Texas, created the series using its Genie engine. The games were critical and commercial successes, praised for balancing historical education with accessible strategy gameplay; Age of Empires II in particular sustains an active competitive scene decades after release. After Ensemble's closure in 2009, stewardship passed to studios including Forgotten Empires and World's Edge, producing remastered "Definitive Editions" of the first three games between 2018 and 2020 and the new mainline entry Age of Empires IV in 2021. A mobile title, Age of Empires Mobile, followed in 2024.

Legacy

The series has sold tens of millions of copies and helped popularise historical real-time strategy. Its campaigns are frequently credited with sparking players' interest in history.
