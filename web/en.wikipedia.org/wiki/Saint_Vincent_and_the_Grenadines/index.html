Saint Vincent and the Grenadines is an island country in the Caribbean Sea, located in the southern portion of the Windward Islands of the Lesser Antilles. It consists of the main island of Saint Vincent and the northern two-thirds of the Grenadines, a chain of 32 smaller islands and cays, some of which are inhabited, including Bequia, Mustique, Canouan, Union Island, Mayreau, Petit St Vincent and Palm Island. Its capital and largest city is Kingstown, situated on the main island.

History

The island of Saint Vincent was originally inhabited by the Island Caribs (Kalinago), who resisted European colonisation longer than the peoples of most neighbouring islands. The Caribs called the island Hairouna, meaning "land of the blessed". Following the wreck of a slave ship in 1675, a community of Africans came to live alongside the Caribs, giving rise to the Garifuna people. French settlers began colonising the island in the early 18th century, but Britain gained control under the Treaty of Paris in 1763. Resistance by the Garifuna under Joseph Chatoyer ended with their defeat in 1796, after which thousands were deported to Roatán off Honduras. Saint Vincent became a British crown colony, passed through various federations including the West Indies Federation, and gained associated statehood in 1969 before full independence on 27 October 1979.

Geography

Saint Vincent is a volcanic island, dominated by La Soufrière, an active stratovolcano reaching 1,234 metres. The volcano erupted explosively in April 2021, displacing roughly a fifth of the population. The Grenadines are low-lying coral and volcanic islands famed for their beaches, reefs and yachting. The country has a tropical climate with a rainy season from June to November and lies within the Atlantic hurricane belt.

Politics

Saint Vincent and the Grenadines is a parliamentary democracy and a Commonwealth realm, with the British monarch as head of state represented by a Governor-General. Executive power rests with the Prime Minister. The unicameral House of Assembly has 15 elected members plus appointed senators. The Unity Labour Party under Ralph Gonsalves, prime minister since 2001, has dominated recent politics.

Economy

The economy depends on tourism, agriculture (historically bananas, once the mainstay under preferential EU access), and offshore financial services. The Grenadine islands, particularly Mustique and Bequia, are well-known luxury and sailing destinations. Kingstown's port serves cruise ships and inter-island ferries.

Demographics

The population of around 100,000 is mostly of African descent, with minorities of mixed, East Indian, European and Carib heritage. English is the official language; Vincentian Creole is widely spoken. Christianity is the predominant religion.
