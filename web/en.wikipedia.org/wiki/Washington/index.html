Washington is a state in the Pacific Northwest region of the United States. It borders the Pacific Ocean to the west, Oregon to the south (along the Columbia River), Idaho to the east, and the Canadian province of British Columbia to the north. Washington is the 18th-largest state by area but the 13th-most populous, with most residents concentrated in the Puget Sound region around Seattle. To avoid confusion with the national capital, the city Washington, D.C., the state is often called "Washington state".

Geography and economy

The Cascade Range splits the state into a wet, forested west — temperate rainforest on the Olympic Peninsula, among the rainiest places in the continental U.S. — and a dry, agricultural east irrigated by the Columbia and its dams, which produce most of America's apples and much of its hops and wheat. Mount Rainier (4,392 m), an active volcano, dominates the Seattle skyline; Mount St. Helens erupted catastrophically in 1980. The state's economy rests on aerospace (Boeing's main plants), software (Microsoft, Amazon), trade with Asia, agriculture and timber. Washington has no state income tax, legalised cannabis in 2012 (a US first, with Colorado), and was the last stop of the westward journeys begun by expeditions such as Lewis and Clark's.
