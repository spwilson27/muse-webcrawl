Flight of Nagorno-Karabakh Armenians

The flight of Nagorno-Karabakh Armenians was the exodus of ethnic Armenians from Nagorno-Karabakh following Azerbaijan's offensive on 19–20 September 2023 against the self-proclaimed Republic of Artsakh. Within about a week, nearly the entire Armenian population of the region — over 100,000 people, the overwhelming majority of its inhabitants — fled to Armenia.

Background

Nagorno-Karabakh had been governed by ethnic Armenian authorities since the First Nagorno-Karabakh War, though internationally recognized as part of Azerbaijan. After the Second Nagorno-Karabakh War in 2020, Azerbaijan controlled surrounding districts, and from December 2022 the Lachin corridor, the region's only road link to Armenia, was blockaded, producing severe shortages of food, fuel and medicine.

Exodus

Following the September 2023 offensive and the capitulation of Artsakh authorities, the corridor reopened and a mass departure began. By early October 2023, Armenian government figures reported more than 100,000 arrivals. The Republic of Artsakh announced it would dissolve by 1 January 2024. International observers and officials debated whether the exodus constituted ethnic cleansing; Azerbaijan stated that Armenians were free to stay and reintegrate, an offer the population overwhelmingly did not take up amid fears for their safety.
