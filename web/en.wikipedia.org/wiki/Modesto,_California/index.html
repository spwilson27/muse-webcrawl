Modesto is a city in and the county seat of Stanislaus County, California, in the San Joaquin Valley. With a population of about 218,000 at the 2020 census, it is among the twenty largest cities in California. The city's official motto is "Water, Wealth, Contentment, Health."

History

Modesto was founded in 1870 as a stop on the Central Pacific Railroad. According to local tradition, the town was to be named for banker William C. Ralston, who declined the honour out of modesty, inspiring the Spanish word modesto. The city grew as an agricultural centre for the surrounding valley, shipping fruit, nuts, and dairy products. The Modesto Arch, erected in 1912, bears the city motto.

Economy and culture

Agriculture and food processing remain central to the economy; Modesto is home to the E & J Gallo Winery, the largest winery in the world, and to major almond and dairy producers. Filmmaker George Lucas was born in Modesto, and his film American Graffiti (1973) was inspired by the city's cruising culture, commemorated annually by the Graffiti Summer festival. The city is also associated with the Scott Peterson murder case of 2002, which drew extensive national coverage.
