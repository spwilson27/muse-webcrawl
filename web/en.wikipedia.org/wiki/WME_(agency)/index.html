WME

Type: Talent and entertainment agency

Founded: April 2009 (merger of Endeavor and the William Morris Agency)

Headquarters: Beverly Hills, California, United States

Parent: Endeavor Group Holdings (now TKO Group ecosystem)

WME, formerly known as William Morris Endeavor and WME-IMG, is an American talent agency based in Beverly Hills, California. It is one of the largest talent agencies in the world, representing artists and content creators across film, television, music, theatre, digital media, books and licensing, as well as athletes, chefs and other public figures.

History

WME was formed in 2009 by the merger of the Endeavor Agency, founded in 1995 by Ari Emanuel, Rick Rosen, Tom Stricklin, David Greenblatt and others, with the William Morris Agency, the oldest talent agency in Hollywood, founded in 1898. The merged company was initially called William Morris Endeavor, later shortened to WME. Ari Emanuel and Patrick Whitesell served as co-chief executives.

In 2014 WME led the acquisition of IMG Worldwide, the sports, events, fashion and media company, forming WME-IMG. The parent company subsequently rebranded as Endeavor and went public in 2021. Following Endeavor's take-private transaction with Silver Lake in 2025, WME continued as the group's core representation business, while the sports assets were combined with TKO Group Holdings, the parent of UFC and WWE.

Operations

The agency's clients have included major film and television stars, directors, writers, recording artists and touring musicians. WME operates offices in Beverly Hills, New York, Nashville, London, Miami and Sydney, and its music division is among the largest booking operations in live entertainment.
