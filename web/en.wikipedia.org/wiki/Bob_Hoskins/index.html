Robert William Hoskins (26 October 1942 – 29 April 2014) was an English actor. His work included lead roles in Pennies from Heaven (1978), The Long Good Friday (1980), Mona Lisa (1986), Who Framed Roger Rabbit (1988), Mermaids (1990) and Hook (1991), in which he played Smee. He won the Cannes Film Festival Best Actor Award and a BAFTA for Mona Lisa, and was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Actor.

Career

Hoskins came to acting almost by accident, accompanying a friend to an audition and being mistaken for a candidate. After theatre work he broke through on television as Dennis Potter's Arthur Parker in Pennies from Heaven. As Harold Shand in The Long Good Friday he created one of British cinema's defining gangster performances. His turn as private detective Eddie Valiant opposite animated characters in Who Framed Roger Rabbit brought international stardom. Later roles included Twenty Four Seven (1997), Felicia's Journey (1999) and Mrs Henderson Presents (2005); he played Mario in the poorly received Super Mario Bros. (1993), a role he cheerfully disowned.

Later life

Hoskins retired in 2012 following a diagnosis of Parkinson's disease, and died of pneumonia in April 2014.
