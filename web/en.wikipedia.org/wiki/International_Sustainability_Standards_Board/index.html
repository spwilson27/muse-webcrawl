The International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB) is a standard-setting board established in 2021 by the IFRS Foundation to develop a global baseline of sustainability disclosure standards for capital markets.

Formation

The ISSB was announced at the COP26 climate conference in Glasgow in November 2021, alongside the consolidation of the Climate Disclosure Standards Board and the Value Reporting Foundation into the IFRS Foundation.

Standards

In June 2023 the ISSB issued its first two standards: IFRS S1, covering general sustainability-related financial disclosures, and IFRS S2, covering climate-related disclosures. Both took effect for annual reporting periods beginning on or after 1 January 2024, with adoption left to individual jurisdictions.
