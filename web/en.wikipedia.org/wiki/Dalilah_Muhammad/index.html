Dalilah Muhammad (born 7 February 1990) is an American track and field athlete who specializes in the 400 metres hurdles. She is the 2016 Olympic champion and the 2019 World champion in the event, and a former world record holder.

Career

Muhammad won the 2007 World Youth title and competed collegiately for the USC Trojans. She took silver at the 2013 and 2017 World Championships before winning Olympic gold at Rio 2016, becoming the first American woman to win the Olympic 400 m hurdles title.

In 2019 she broke the long-standing world record, running 52.20 seconds at the US championships and then 52.16 at the World Championships in Doha, defeating Sydney McLaughlin. McLaughlin broke the record in 2021, when Muhammad won Olympic silver in Tokyo in 51.58, then the second-fastest time in history. She also earned gold with the US 4×400 m relay team in Tokyo.

Personal life

Muhammad was born in Jamaica, Queens, New York City, to Muslim parents, and attended Benjamin Cardozo High School in Bayside.
