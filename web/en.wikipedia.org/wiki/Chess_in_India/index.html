Chess in India

Chess in India traces its origins to chaturanga, the ancient Indian strategy game from around the 6th century CE that is widely regarded as the ancestor of chess. The modern game returned to India under British influence, and the All India Chess Federation, founded in 1951, governs the sport.

Modern era

India's modern chess boom is closely associated with Viswanathan Anand, who became the country's first grandmaster in 1988 and won multiple world championships. In the 21st century India has produced a large cohort of young grandmasters, including Gukesh Dommaraju, who won the World Chess Championship in 2024, and the Indian teams have won medals at Chess Olympiads, including double gold in the open and women's events at the 2024 Olympiad.
