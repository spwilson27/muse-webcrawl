Tysons, Virginia

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Tysons (formerly Tysons Corner) is a census-designated place and edge city in Fairfax County, Virginia, United States, within the Washington metropolitan area. Long known as a suburban office and retail hub, it is being redeveloped as a walkable urban centre.

Development

The opening of Tysons Corner Center in 1968 and the later Tysons Galleria made the area one of the largest retail destinations on the US East Coast. It grew into one of America's classic "edge cities", with more office space than many downtowns, housing the headquarters of companies such as Capital One, Hilton Worldwide and Freddie Mac nearby.

Urbanisation plan

Fairfax County's Comprehensive Plan for Tysons, adopted in 2010, aims to transform the area over several decades into a high-density urban district with a grid of streets and a population of up to 100,000. The arrival of four Washington Metro Silver Line stations in 2014 was a key catalyst.

Demographics

The resident population was around 20,000 at the 2020 census, though the daytime workforce is far larger, historically exceeding 100,000 jobs.
