King County International Airport

King County International Airport (IATA: BFI, ICAO: KBFI), commonly known as Boeing Field, is a public airport owned by King County, five miles south of downtown Seattle, Washington. It is one of the busiest general aviation and cargo airports in the United States.

History and use

Opened in 1928, it was Seattle's main commercial airport until Seattle–Tacoma International Airport opened in the 1940s. Boeing has long used the field for test flights and deliveries of aircraft built in the region; the Museum of Flight stands on its west side. The airport serves cargo operators, charter and corporate aviation, flight training and limited scheduled passenger service.
