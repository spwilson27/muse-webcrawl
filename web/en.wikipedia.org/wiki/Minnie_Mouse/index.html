Minnie Mouse is an American cartoon character created by Walt Disney and Ub Iwerks. An anthropomorphic mouse, she is the longtime sweetheart of Mickey Mouse and debuted alongside him in Steamboat Willie on November 18, 1928. Her full name is Minerva Mouse, though it is rarely used.

Character

Minnie is traditionally drawn wearing a polka-dot dress or skirt, oversized high heels, white gloves, and a large bow on her head — a look based on 1920s flapper fashion that has become her trademark. Sweet, musical, and resourceful, she starred in a dozen shorts of the classic era, often as a singer or dancer rescued by Mickey, but increasingly as an independent character in later television series such as Mickey Mouse Clubhouse and the 2013 Mickey Mouse shorts. Her pet cat Figaro and her nieces Millie and Melody appear in various stories.

Legacy

Minnie received her own star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame in 2018, forty years after Mickey's. Russi Taylor voiced her for over three decades, from 1986 until her death in 2019, and was married to Wayne Allwine, the voice of Mickey Mouse. In 2022 Minnie's attire was refreshed with a blue pantsuit designed by Stella McCartney for Disneyland Paris's 30th anniversary, while the classic polka dots remain her standard look.
