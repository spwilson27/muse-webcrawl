Clairefontaine-en-Yvelines

Clairefontaine-en-Yvelines is a commune in the Yvelines department in the Île-de-France region of north-central France, about 40 kilometres southwest of Paris. It lies on the edge of the forest of Rambouillet and had a population of roughly 900 in recent censuses. The commune is best known internationally for the Centre Technique National Fernand Sastre, usually called Clairefontaine, the national football centre of France.

Clairefontaine is home to France's national football training centre.

INF Clairefontaine

The Institut national du football de Clairefontaine, opened in 1988, is one of the elite academies of the French Football Federation. It selects talented young players, generally aged 13 to 15, mostly from the Paris region, for intensive coaching while they continue their schooling. Numerous French internationals — including Thierry Henry, Nicolas Anelka, William Gallas and Kylian Mbappé — passed through Clairefontaine. The broader Fernand Sastre national technical centre on the same site serves as the training base and headquarters camp of the French national teams.

Geography and the village

The commune is divided between the village proper and scattered hamlets within woodland. Its territory includes the Château de Clairefontaine grounds on which the football centre was built; the château's estate was chosen for the academy for its seclusion and green surroundings. The village church and older buildings cluster along the main road towards Rambouillet.
