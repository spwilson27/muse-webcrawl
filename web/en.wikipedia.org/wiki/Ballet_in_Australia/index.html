Ballet in Australia

Ballet in Australia developed during the twentieth century, strongly influenced by touring European companies and by the establishment of a national company, The Australian Ballet, in 1962. Australia now supports several professional ballet companies and a major training institution, The Australian Ballet School in Melbourne.

Early history

Ballet was performed in Australia from the colonial era, but a permanent professional culture emerged after tours by companies such as the Ballets Russes in the 1930s, which inspired local dancers including Edouard Borovansky. Borovansky founded the Borovansky Ballet in Melbourne in 1939, which became the direct forerunner of The Australian Ballet.

The Australian Ballet

The Australian Ballet was founded in 1962 under artistic director Dame Peggy van Praagh, with financial support from the Australian Elizabethan Theatre Trust. Its first performance was Tchaikovsky's Swan Lake in Sydney. The company tours internationally and performs at the Sydney Opera House and Arts Centre Melbourne.

Other companies

State companies include Queensland Ballet, West Australian Ballet, and the contemporary company Australian Dance Theatre (founded in Adelaide in 1965 by Elizabeth Cameron Dalman).
