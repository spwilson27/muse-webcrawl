Nayi Kavita

Nayi Kavita (Hindi: नई कविता, "New Poetry") was a movement in modern Hindi literature that emerged in the 1950s, following the experimental Prayogvad of the Tar Saptak anthologies. It emphasised the experience of the individual in post-independence India, intellectual honesty and a plainer, more direct poetic diction.

Features

The poets of Nayi Kavita turned away from the romanticism of Chhayavad and the collective rhetoric of the Progressive movement toward irony, urban experience, doubt and self-examination. Their verse made extensive use of free verse and everyday imagery.

Major poets

Prominent figures associated with the movement include Agyeya (Sachchidananda Hirananda Vatsyayan), Gajanan Madhav Muktibodh, Raghuvir Sahay, Sarveshwar Dayal Saxena and Kunwar Narayan. The journal Nayi Kavita, edited by Jagdish Gupta, gave the movement its name and platform.
