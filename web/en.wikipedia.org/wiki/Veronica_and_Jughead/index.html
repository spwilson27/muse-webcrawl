Veronica and Jughead

Veronica and Jughead was an American comic book series published by Archie Comics, launched in 2023 as part of the publisher's line of horror and genre one-shots and miniseries under the Archie Horror imprint's expanded output. The series pairs two of the company's best-known characters — Veronica Lodge, the wealthy Riverdale heiress, and Jughead Jones, Archie Andrews's food-loving best friend — in a story outside the traditional all-ages Archie continuity.

Background

Archie Comics began experimenting with alternative takes on its characters with the success of Afterlife with Archie (2013) and follow-ups such as Jughead: The Hunger and Vampironica, in which classic characters were placed in horror settings. The pairing of Veronica and Jughead — normally characterised in the classic comics by their mutual antagonism, her wealth and glamour contrasting with his slacker indifference — provided the premise for a fresh team-up storyline.

Characters

Veronica Lodge is the rich, fashionable daughter of Hiram Lodge and a member of Archie's core group of friends in Riverdale. Jughead Jones, recognisable by his crown-shaped whoopee cap, is depicted as a wisecracking, perpetually hungry teenager with little interest in romance in classic continuity. The series plays the two against one another as reluctant partners.
