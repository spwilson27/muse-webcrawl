Walt Disney

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Walt Disney at his drawing board with an early Mickey Mouse sketch, model trains and storyboards behind him

Walter Elias Disney (5 December 1901 – 15 December 1966) was an American animator, film producer, voice actor and entrepreneur. A pioneer of the American animation industry, he introduced several developments in the production of cartoons. As a film producer, he holds the record for most Academy Awards earned and nominations by an individual.

With Ub Iwerks he created Mickey Mouse (1928) and the first cartoon with synchronised sound, Steamboat Willie. His studio invented the multiplane camera and produced the first feature-length animated film, Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937), followed by Pinocchio, Fantasia (both 1940), Dumbo and Bambi. In 1955 he opened Disneyland in California, pioneering the modern theme park; Walt Disney World in Florida opened in 1971, five years after his death.
