World Vegetarian Day

World Vegetarian Day
Date1 October
Established1977, North American Vegetarian Society
Endorsed1978, International Vegetarian Union
RelatedWorld Vegan Day (1 November)

World Vegetarian Day is observed annually on 1 October to promote the benefits of vegetarianism and to raise awareness of the ethical, environmental and health arguments for a meat-free diet. It also kicks off Vegetarian Awareness Month, which runs through October.

History

The day was established by the North American Vegetarian Society (NAVS) in 1977 and endorsed by the International Vegetarian Union in 1978. Vegetarian societies worldwide mark the day with festivals, cooking demonstrations, talks and information stalls.

Related observances

World Vegan Day, founded by Louise Wallis of the UK Vegan Society, falls on 1 November. Other related observances include Meatless Monday campaigns and World Day for Farmed Animals (2 October).
