Omar Sy (born 20 January 1978) is a French actor and comedian. Best known in his native country for his sketches with Fred Testot on the Service après-vente des émissions television show on Canal+, he gained international recognition for his role in the 2011 comedy-drama film The Intouchables, which earned him the César Award for Best Actor, making him the first Black recipient of the award.

Career

Sy was born in Trappes, in the Yvelines department of France, to West African immigrant parents from Senegal and Mauritania. He began his career in radio and television comedy in the late 1990s, forming the duo Omar et Fred. After a string of French film roles, his performance as Driss, a street-smart ex-convict hired to care for a wealthy quadriplegic aristocrat in The Intouchables, became a worldwide phenomenon; the film became one of the highest-grossing French films of all time.

He subsequently appeared in Hollywood productions including X-Men: Days of Future Past (2014), Jurassic World (2015), Burnt (2015) and Inferno (2016). Since 2021 he has starred as Assane Diop in the Netflix series Lupin, inspired by the adventures of Arsène Lupin, which became one of the platform's most-watched non-English-language series.
