Diablo Canyon Power Plant

The Diablo Canyon Power Plant is a nuclear power station near Avila Beach in San Luis Obispo County, California, operated by Pacific Gas and Electric Company (PG&E). Its two pressurised water reactors together generate about 2,240 megawatts, supplying roughly 9% of California's electricity.

History

Construction began in 1968 amid significant public protest over seismic safety, as the plant sits near several earthquake faults. The two units entered service in 1985 and 1986. Diablo Canyon is California's last operating nuclear power plant following the closure of San Onofre in 2013.

Planned closure and extension

In 2016 PG&E announced plans to close the plant when its licences expired in 2024–2025. In 2022, amid concerns about grid reliability, California passed legislation to extend operations, and PG&E applied to the Nuclear Regulatory Commission for licence renewal to 2044/2045.

Cooling

The plant uses a once-through cooling system drawing seawater from the Pacific Ocean.
