EXPTIME

In computational complexity theory, the complexity class EXPTIME (sometimes called EXP or DEXPTIME) is the set of all decision problems that are solvable by a deterministic Turing machine in exponential time, i.e., in O(2p(n)) time, where p(n) is a polynomial function of n.

EXPTIME
Class: Complexity
Contains: PSPACE, NP, P
Proven distinct from: P, via time hierarchy theorem

Relations to other classes

P ⊆ NP ⊆ PSPACE ⊆ EXPTIME ⊆ NEXPTIME ⊆ EXPSPACE. By the deterministic time hierarchy theorem, P is strictly contained in EXPTIME, one of the few proven separations between major classes.

EXPTIME-complete problems

Problems complete for EXPTIME include generalised chess, checkers and Go (judging a position), and certain succinct circuit and logic problems. Such problems are provably not in P.
