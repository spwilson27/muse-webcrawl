Hawaii

Lava flowing into the sea on Hawaii's Big Island

Hawaii is an island state of the United States in the Pacific Ocean, about 3,200 kilometres southwest of the mainland, consisting of 137 volcanic islands spanning 2,450 kilometres, of which eight are considered the 'main islands'. It is the only US state outside North America and the only one composed entirely of islands. Its capital and largest city is Honolulu, on O'ahu, and its population is about 1.4 million.

Polynesian voyagers settled Hawaii by about 1200 CE; Kamehameha I unified the islands into a kingdom in 1795-1810. US business interests overthrew Queen Lili'uokalani in 1893; the US annexed Hawaii in 1898, and the attack on Pearl Harbor (7 December 1941) brought the US into the Second World War. Statehood came in 1959. Hawaii is a major tourist destination famed for Waikiki, active volcanoes Kīlauea and Mauna Loa, surfing and hula, and it is a biodiversity hotspot with many endemic species.
