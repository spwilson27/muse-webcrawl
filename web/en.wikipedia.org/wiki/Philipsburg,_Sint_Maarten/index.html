Philipsburg, Sint Maarten

Philipsburg is the main town and capital of Sint Maarten, the Dutch constituent country occupying the southern part of the Caribbean island of Saint Martin. It lies on a narrow strip of land between Great Bay and the Great Salt Pond.

CountrySint Maarten (Netherlands)
Founded1763 (by John Philips)
Population~1,900

History

The area was noted by Columbus in 1493. Philipsburg was founded in 1763 by John Philips, a Scottish captain in the Dutch navy, and grew as a centre of the salt trade and later of tourism.

Tourism

Front Street is lined with duty-free shops catering to cruise passengers who arrive at the Port St. Maarten terminal, while Boardwalk promenade runs along Great Bay Beach. The town was heavily damaged by Hurricane Irma in September 2017 and has since rebuilt.
