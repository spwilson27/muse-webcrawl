Christians

Christians are people who follow Christianity, the Abrahamic monotheistic religion centred on the life and teachings of Jesus of Nazareth, whom they regard as Christ (Messiah) and Son of God. With about 2.3–2.4 billion adherents, Christians are the world's largest religious group, roughly a third of humanity.

Beliefs

Core shared beliefs include one God as Trinity, the incarnation, crucifixion and resurrection of Jesus, and salvation. Major families are Roman Catholic (about half of all Christians), Protestant, Eastern Orthodox, Oriental Orthodox, and others.

History and distribution

The name "Christian" was first used at Antioch (Acts 11). Christianity spread through the Roman Empire, then worldwide through missions and migration. Today the largest populations are in the Americas, Europe, sub-Saharan Africa, and Asia-Pacific.
