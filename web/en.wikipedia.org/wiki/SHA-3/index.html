SHA-3

BasisKeccak sponge construction
Standardised2015 (FIPS 202)
DesignersGuido Bertoni, Joan Daemen, Michaël Peeters, Gilles Van Assche

SHA-3 (Secure Hash Algorithm 3) is the latest member of the Secure Hash Algorithm family of cryptographic hash functions, standardised by NIST in FIPS 202 in August 2015. Unlike SHA-1 and SHA-2, which share the Merkle–Damgård design, SHA-3 is based on the Keccak algorithm's sponge construction.

Competition and design

Keccak, designed by Guido Bertoni, Joan Daemen, Michaël Peeters and Gilles Van Assche, won the NIST hash function competition in 2012. The sponge construction absorbs input into a large internal state (a 1600-bit permutation) and squeezes out the hash, giving a structure quite different from earlier SHA designs and resistant to length-extension attacks.

Variants

FIPS 202 specifies four fixed-length functions (SHA3-224, SHA3-256, SHA3-384, SHA3-512) and two extendable-output functions, SHAKE128 and SHAKE256, which can produce hashes of arbitrary length. SHA-3 was standardised as a complement and fallback to SHA-2 rather than a replacement.
