The Independent is a British online newspaper, founded in 1986 as a national broadsheet by Andreas Whittam Smith and partners, which pioneered a design-led, non-partisan-positioned quality paper. It ceased print in March 2016 — the first major British title to go digital-only — under owner Evgeny Lebedev.

History

The 'Indy' launched against the Murdoch-era press, championed poster-style front pages in the 2000s under Simon Kelner, and passed through Independent News & Media and Alexander Lebedev's ownership. Its digital-only incarnation built large UK and US audiences on a free, advertising-funded model.
