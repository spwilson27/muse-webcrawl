Chess variant

A chess variant is a game related to, derived from, or inspired by chess, differing in the board, the pieces, or the rules. Thousands of variants exist, from slight rule tweaks to wholesale redesigns. National and regional forms such as shogi (Japan), xiangqi (China) and makruk (Thailand) are sometimes classed with variants but descend in parallel from the same ancestor, chaturanga.

Well-known variants

Popular variants include Chess960 (Fischer Random Chess), which randomises the back-rank arrangement; Crazyhouse and bughouse, which reuse captured pieces; four-player chess; and fairy chess problems employing unorthodox "fairy pieces" such as the nightrider or the grasshopper.
