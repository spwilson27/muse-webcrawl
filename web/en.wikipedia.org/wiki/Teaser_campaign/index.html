Teaser campaign

A teaser campaign is an advertising campaign that offers only a small, cryptic hint of a product or event before its full launch, withholding the brand or details to build curiosity and anticipation. Teasers typically run for days or weeks before the "reveal" phase identifies the advertiser.

Characteristics

Teaser advertisements often consist of a striking image, slogan or countdown without naming the product. The technique is common in film marketing (teaser trailers), automotive launches and technology product announcements, where early speculation and media coverage amplify the eventual reveal.

Risks

If the reveal disappoints, or the teaser attracts little attention, the campaign may fail; teasers can also frustrate audiences if the mystery seems manipulative. Nevertheless, successful campaigns — such as mysterious billboards that later reveal a film or car — are staples of modern marketing.
