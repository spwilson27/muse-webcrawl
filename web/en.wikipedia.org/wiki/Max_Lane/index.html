Max Lane

Max Lane (Maxwell Ronald Lane, born 1949) is an Australian scholar, writer and translator specialising in Indonesian politics and society. He is known in particular for his English translations of the Buru Quartet novels of the Indonesian writer Pramoedya Ananta Toer, and for his analyses of the fall of the Suharto regime and of modern Indonesian protest movements.

Career

Lane studied at the University of Sydney and became involved in Indonesia-related activism during the Suharto era. He later held research positions at institutions including the University of Sydney and Singapore's ISEAS–Yusof Ishak Institute, and taught at universities in Australia and Indonesia. His book Unfinished Nation: Indonesia Before and After Suharto (2008) examines the country's democratic transition and the forces constraining it.

Translations

Lane's translations of Pramoedya's This Earth of Mankind, Child of All Nations, Footsteps and House of Glass introduced one of Indonesia's greatest novelists to a wide English-language readership. He has also translated works by the poet and playwright W. S. Rendra and other Indonesian writers.
