King of the Hill

King of the Hill is an American animated sitcom created by Mike Judge and Greg Daniels that aired on Fox from January 12, 1997, to May 6, 2010, running for 13 seasons and 259 episodes. A revival premiered on Hulu in 2025.

Premise

Set in the fictional town of Arlen, Texas, the series centres on the Hill family: propane salesman Hank Hill, his wife Peggy, their son Bobby, and Peggy's niece Luanne, along with Hank's friends Dale, Bill and Boomhauer, who gather in the alley drinking beer. The show satirises suburban American life with a relatively realistic, character-driven style, and Hank's earnest, traditional values often clash with a changing world.
