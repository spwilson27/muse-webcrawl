Big Brother (American TV series)

NetworkCBS
PremieredJuly 5, 2000
HostJulie Chen Moonves

Big Brother is an American reality television series, based on the Dutch format created by John de Mol, that premiered on CBS in 2000. Strangers live together in a purpose-built house under constant camera surveillance, competing to be the last remaining houseguest.

Format

Each week houseguests compete for the Head of Household title and the Power of Veto. The Head of Household nominates two houseguests for eviction, and the house votes to evict one. The finalists face a jury of evicted houseguests, who choose the winner of the grand prize (US$750,000 in recent seasons).

Broadcast

Hosted by Julie Chen Moonves since its debut, the show airs several episodes a week in summer and is accompanied by live feeds of the house.
