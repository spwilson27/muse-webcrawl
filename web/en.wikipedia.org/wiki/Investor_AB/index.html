Investor AB is a Swedish investment and holding company, often considered the flagship of the Wallenberg family enterprises. Founded in 1916 when new Swedish legislation made it harder for banks to own shares in industrial companies, Investor received the equity holdings of Stockholms Enskilda Bank and has since acted as a long-term, engaged owner of major Swedish and international companies.

Holdings

Investor's portfolio is organised in three areas: Listed Companies, with major stakes in firms such as ABB, AstraZeneca, Atlas Copco, Electrolux, Ericsson, Epiroc, Husqvarna, Nasdaq, Saab and SEB; Patricia Industries, which holds wholly owned subsidiaries including Mölnlycke Health Care, Laborie, BraunAbility and Permobil; and Investments in EQT, holding stakes in funds managed by the private equity firm EQT. Investor is typically the largest shareholder in its core holdings and exercises influence through board representation.

Ownership and governance

The Wallenberg foundations, principally the Knut and Alice Wallenberg Foundation, are Investor's largest owners, and members of the Wallenberg family have chaired the company across generations, including Marcus Wallenberg, Peter Wallenberg and Jacob Wallenberg. The foundations use dividends from Investor to fund scientific research in Sweden. Investor is one of the most widely held stocks in Sweden, popular with retail savers.
