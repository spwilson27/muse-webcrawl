Peter Wright (choreographer)

Sir Peter Wright CBE (born 25 November 1926) is a British ballet teacher, choreographer, director and former professional dancer. He is renowned for his productions of the classical ballets, especially The Nutcracker, Giselle, and Swan Lake.

Career

Wright danced with companies including Kurt Jooss's Ballets Jooss and the Sadler's Wells Theatre Ballet, then turned to teaching and directing, serving as ballet master and choreographer for Stuttgart Ballet. He became associate director of The Royal Ballet and, from 1977 to 1995, director of Sadler's Wells Royal Ballet, which under him became Birmingham Royal Ballet in 1990.

Productions

His staging of Giselle (1966) and his Nutcracker for The Royal Ballet (1984, revised 1999) and Birmingham Royal Ballet (1990) are regarded as classics, praised for musicality, clarity of storytelling, and fidelity to the 19th-century sources. He was knighted in 1993.
