Avatar: The Way of Water

Avatar: The Way of Water is a 2022 American epic science fiction film directed and produced by James Cameron, who co-wrote the screenplay with Rick Jaffa and Amanda Silver. It is the sequel to Avatar (2009) and the second instalment in the Avatar franchise. Sam Worthington, Zoe Saldaña, Stephen Lang, Sigourney Weaver and Kate Winslet star.

Plot

More than a decade after the events of the first film, Jake Sully and Neytiri have formed a family on Pandora. When the human RDA returns to colonise the moon and a resurrected Colonel Quaritch hunts them, the family seeks refuge with the oceanic Metkayina clan, where they must learn the way of water while the conflict follows them to the reef.

Production and release

The film was shot back-to-back with the third Avatar film, pioneering underwater performance capture in a purpose-built tank in Manhattan Beach, California, with additional photography in New Zealand. Repeatedly delayed, it premiered in London on 6 December 2022 and was released in the United States on 16 December. With a production budget estimated at $350–460 million, it was among the most expensive films ever made.

Reception

The film grossed over $2.3 billion worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing film of 2022 and the third-highest-grossing film of all time. Critics praised its visual effects and technical achievements. It won the Academy Award for Best Visual Effects and was nominated for Best Picture.
