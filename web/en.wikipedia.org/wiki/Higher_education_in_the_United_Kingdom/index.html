Higher education in the United Kingdom

Higher education in the United Kingdom is provided principally by universities and other institutions awarding degrees at bachelor's, master's and doctoral level. The UK has some of the world's oldest and highest-ranked universities, including Oxford and Cambridge.

Structure

Undergraduate degrees typically take three years in England, Wales and Northern Ireland and four in Scotland. Admission is through UCAS, funding through tuition fees and student loans (with differing regimes in the devolved nations), and quality is overseen by the Office for Students and QAA. The sector expanded greatly after the 1992 reforms converted polytechnics into universities.

Students

UK institutions enrol large numbers of international students, and research is concentrated through exercises such as the Research Excellence Framework.
