Alexander J. Honnold (born August 17, 1985) is an American rock climber best known for his free solo ascents of big walls, in particular his free solo climb of El Capitan in Yosemite National Park on June 3, 2017, via the Freerider route—a 2,900-foot (884 m) granite wall completed in 3 hours and 56 minutes. His ascent, without ropes or protective equipment, was widely described as one of the greatest athletic feats of any kind. It is the subject of the documentary film Free Solo (2018), which won the Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature.

Early life

Honnold was born in Sacramento, California, to community college professors Dierdre Wolownick and Charles Honnold. He began climbing in gyms at age five and was climbing "many times a week" by age ten. He enrolled at the University of California, Berkeley, to study civil engineering but dropped out at 19 to climb full time, living for years in a van.

Career

Honnold first gained attention in 2007 by free soloing the Astroman and Rostrum routes in Yosemite in a single day. In 2008 he free soloed the Regular Northwest Face of Half Dome, and in 2012 he climbed the "Yosemite Triple"—Mount Watkins, El Capitan, and Half Dome—mostly free solo, in under 19 hours. With Hans Florine he set a speed record on the Nose of El Capitan in 2012 (2 hours 23 minutes), a record he later lowered with Tommy Caldwell in 2018 to 1 hour 58 minutes.

On June 3, 2017, Honnold completed the first free solo ascent of El Capitan via Freerider (5.13a), a climb he had prepared for over years of rehearsal on rope. The feat was filmed by Elizabeth Chai Vasarhelyi and Jimmy Chin for Free Solo.

Other notable achievements include the Honnold–Florine link-ups in Yosemite, free solo ascents of routes in Zion and at El Sendero Luminoso in Mexico (2014), and alpine ascents in Patagonia, including the Torre traverse with Colin Haley in 2016. In 2022 he led a climbing and research expedition to Ingmikortilaq in Greenland.

Philanthropy

In 2012 Honnold founded the Honnold Foundation, which supports solar energy access projects for underserved communities worldwide. Drawing on his minimalist lifestyle—he lived for years in a Ford Econoline van and later a Sprinter—he has committed a substantial share of his income to the foundation. His memoir Alone on the Wall (2015), written with David Roberts, recounts his early career.

Personal life

Honnold is known for his vegetarian diet and measured, analytical approach to risk; brain imaging research on his amygdala response to alarming images attracted scientific interest in his fear processing. He married Sanni McCandless in 2020, and they have two daughters. The family lives in Las Vegas, Nevada.
