Rostec

Rostec (Russian: Ростех, Rostekh), formerly Rostekhnologii, is a Russian state corporation established in 2007 to consolidate state-owned defence, aerospace and high-technology industrial assets. Headquartered in Moscow, it controls hundreds of enterprises and holding companies employing several hundred thousand people.

Founded: 2007

Headquarters: Moscow, Russia

CEO: Sergey Chemezov

Owner: Government of Russia

Holdings

Rostec's portfolio includes Russian Helicopters, the United Aircraft Corporation (consolidated into Rostec in 2018), United Engine Corporation, the Kalashnikov Concern, the arms exporter Rosoboronexport, the truck maker Kamaz, the car maker AvtoVAZ (until its sale in 2022), and firms in electronics, optics, ammunition and pharmaceuticals. It was created to promote the development, production and export of high-tech industrial products and to restructure inefficient Soviet-era enterprises.

Sanctions

Following Russia's annexation of Crimea in 2014, and more comprehensively after the full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, Rostec and its chief executive Sergey Chemezov were placed under sanctions by the United States, the European Union and other countries, and the corporation became a central instrument of Russia's wartime defence production.
