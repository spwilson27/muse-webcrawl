Christopher Isaac "Biz" Stone (born March 10, 1974) is an American entrepreneur, investor and author who co-founded Twitter, among other technology ventures including Xanga, Blogger-related projects at Google, Medium and Jelly.

Early life and education

Stone was born in Boston and raised in Wellesley, Massachusetts. He attended Northeastern University and the University of Massachusetts Boston, leaving both without graduating in order to work in the technology industry. He took the nickname "Biz" as a child, from a mispronunciation of his surname by his younger brother.

Career

Stone began his career in book publishing and web design before joining Xanga, a blogging community. In 2003 he joined Google, where he worked on Blogger following its acquisition from Pyra Labs. In 2006 he left with Jack Dorsey, Evan Williams and others from the podcasting company Odeo to form the company that launched Twitter, the microblogging service that grew into one of the world's largest social networks.

Stone left Twitter in 2011. He co-founded the search start-up Jelly in 2013, which was acquired by Pinterest in 2017. He returned to Twitter in 2017 in an advisory role focused on company culture, and departed again after the company's acquisition by Elon Musk in 2022. His book Things a Little Bird Told Me (2014) recounts lessons from his time building Twitter.
