Kathleen Genevieve Ledecky (born 17 March 1997) is an American competitive swimmer. She is the most decorated female swimmer in history and is widely regarded as the greatest distance freestyle swimmer of all time. Across four Olympic Games she has won nine gold medals and fourteen overall, surpassing Jenny Thompson as the most decorated American woman in Olympic history at Paris 2024.

Ledecky announced herself at London 2012, winning the 800 m freestyle at 15. At Rio 2016 she won four golds, then at Tokyo 2020 became the first women's 1500 m freestyle Olympic champion. Her dominance of the 800 m was absolute: she held the world record from 2013 and won the event at four consecutive Olympics before Australia's Ariarne Titmus era reshaped the 400 m. At Paris 2024 she retained the 800 m and 1500 m titles, winning the 1500 m by over ten seconds. In May 2025 she broke her own 800 m world record — her first long-course world record in seven years — at a meet in Florida. Like Michael Phelps, she combined a metronomic training base (at Stanford, then Florida) with unmatched aerobic capacity.
