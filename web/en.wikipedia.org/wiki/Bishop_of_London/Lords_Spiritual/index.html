Lords Spiritual

BodyHouse of Lords, Parliament of the United Kingdom
Number26 bishops of the Church of England
Automatic seatsArchbishops of Canterbury and York; Bishops of London, Durham, Winchester

The Lords Spiritual are the bishops of the Church of England who sit in the House of Lords of the Parliament of the United Kingdom. Twenty-six bishops hold seats, alongside the much larger number of Lords Temporal.

Composition

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York and the Bishops of London, Durham, and Winchester always hold seats. The remaining twenty-one seats go to diocesan bishops by seniority of consecration, subject to provisions introduced in 2015 that for ten years gave priority to women bishops as they were appointed. Bishops retire from the Lords when they retire from their sees.

Role

The Lords Spiritual take part in debates and votes, with a daily place in the chamber's prayers, but by convention they act independently of party politics and often speak on moral, social, and community matters. Their presence reflects the established status of the Church of England; reform proposals periodically suggest altering or removing the seats.
