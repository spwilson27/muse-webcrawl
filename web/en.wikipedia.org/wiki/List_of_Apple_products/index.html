List of Apple products

Apple products

Manufacturer: Apple Inc.

First product: Apple I (1976)

Main lines: Mac, iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, AirPods

The following is a list of products offered by the American company Apple Inc., organized by release year and type. Apple's product history begins with the Apple I personal computer in 1976 and includes the Apple II, the Macintosh line (from 1984), the iPod (2001), the iPhone (2007), the iPad (2010), the Apple Watch (2015), AirPods (2016), and services and software alongside the hardware.

Computers

This section lists Apple's computers: the Apple I, II, and III; the Lisa; the Macintosh family including the iMac, MacBook (Air and Pro), Mac mini, Mac Studio, and Mac Pro; and earlier lines such as the Power Macintosh, PowerBook, and the Newton handheld (listed under other devices).

Mobile and other devices

This section lists the iPod family (classic, nano, shuffle, touch), the iPhone models by generation, the iPad family (standard, mini, Air, Pro), the Apple Watch series, Apple TV, AirPods and other audio products (HomePod), and accessories such as the Apple Pencil and Magic Keyboard.

Software and services

Apple's software includes the operating systems macOS, iOS, iPadOS, watchOS, and tvOS; applications such as Safari, iMovie, and Final Cut Pro; and services including the App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, and Apple TV+.
