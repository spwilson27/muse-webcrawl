Community of Madrid

Community of Madrid
Capital: Madrid
Area: 8,028 km²
Population: ~6.8 million
Status: Autonomous community and province of Spain

The Community of Madrid (Spanish: Comunidad de Madrid) is one of the seventeen autonomous communities of Spain. Located in the centre of the Iberian Peninsula, it is coextensive with the province of Madrid and contains the national capital, the city of Madrid, which also serves as the regional capital. With roughly 6.8 million inhabitants it is the third most populous autonomous community, and by far the most densely populated, despite being the smallest by area after the Balearic Islands and the Basque Country.

Geography

The region occupies the southern Meseta plateau, bordered by the Sierra de Guadarrama mountains to the northwest — whose highest peak, Peñalara (2,428 m), is the region's highest point — and by the Tagus river valley in the south. Its territory is drained by tributaries of the Tagus, including the Jarama, Manzanares, Henares, and Tajuña rivers. The climate is continental Mediterranean, with hot, dry summers and cold winters.

History

Madrid became capital of Spain under Philip II in 1561, and its province grew around the court. During Spain's transition to democracy, the province was constituted as a single-province autonomous community; its Statute of Autonomy was approved on 1 March 1983. The decision reflected both the capital's distinct identity and the aim of balancing the surrounding region of Castile.

Government and economy

Regional institutions are the Assembly of Madrid (legislature) and the Government of the Community of Madrid, headed by a president. Madrid is Spain's economic engine: the region contributes about a fifth of national GDP, concentrates corporate headquarters, finance, and the country's main transport hub (Adolfo Suárez Madrid–Barajas Airport), and hosts major universities and museums. Municipalities in the metropolitan ring — including Móstoles, Alcalá de Henares, Fuenlabrada, Leganés, and Getafe — are among Spain's most populous cities.
