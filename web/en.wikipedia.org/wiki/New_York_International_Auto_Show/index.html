New York International Auto Show

The New York International Auto Show is an annual auto show held in New York City, typically in late March or early April around Easter weekend, at the Jacob Javits Convention Center in Manhattan. First held in 1900, it is North America's oldest and one of its largest auto shows, drawing around a million visitors.

History

The first show took place in November 1900 at the original Madison Square Garden, organised by the Automobile Club of America. It later moved to the Grand Central Palace and then the New York Coliseum before settling at the Javits Center in 1987. The show is run by the Greater New York Automobile Dealers Association.

The show

Manufacturers use the New York show for world and North American vehicle debuts and concept car unveilings, and the World Car Awards — including World Car of the Year — are announced there. In addition to new-car exhibits, the show features classic cars, aftermarket displays and test-drive experiences. The 2020 show was postponed and ultimately cancelled because of the COVID-19 pandemic, and the 2021 show was also cancelled, with the event returning in 2022.
