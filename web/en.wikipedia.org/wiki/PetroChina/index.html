PetroChina Company Limited is the listed arm of state-owned China National Petroleum Corporation (CNPC) and is China's largest oil and gas producer and distributor, playing a dominant role in the country's oil and gas industry. The company engages in exploration, development, production and sale of crude oil and natural gas; refining and marketing; chemicals; and the operation of oil and gas pipelines.

History

PetroChina was established in 1999 as part of the restructuring of CNPC, which transferred most of its exploration, production, refining and distribution assets to the new listed company. It listed in Hong Kong and New York in 2000, and in Shanghai in 2007; its Shanghai debut briefly made it the world's first trillion-dollar company by market capitalisation, before its shares fell sharply. In 2022 PetroChina delisted from the New York Stock Exchange amid wider tensions over audit rules.

Operations

The company operates major oilfields including Daqing, China's largest, and extensive natural gas fields and pipeline networks, including parts of the West–East Gas Pipeline. It runs refineries and a large network of service stations across China, and has overseas assets in Central Asia, the Middle East, Africa and the Americas.
