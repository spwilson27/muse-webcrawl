Station Casinos

Station Casinos, LLC is an American hotel and casino company based in Las Vegas, Nevada. It is a major operator of casinos aimed primarily at local residents of the Las Vegas Valley rather than tourists, with properties spread across the metropolitan area.

History

The company was founded by Frank Fertitta Jr., who opened a small bingo hall called The Casino in 1976; it later became Palace Station, near the Las Vegas Strip. Under his sons Frank Fertitta III and Lorenzo Fertitta, the company expanded through the 1990s and 2000s with properties such as Boulder Station, Texas Station, Sunset Station, Santa Fe Station and Red Rock Resort, which opened in Summerlin in 2006.

Operations

Station Casinos operates numerous casinos, hotels and entertainment venues across the Las Vegas Valley, including the Red Rock, Green Valley Ranch and Palms properties acquired later. Its parent company is Red Rock Resorts, Inc., which trades on the Nasdaq.
