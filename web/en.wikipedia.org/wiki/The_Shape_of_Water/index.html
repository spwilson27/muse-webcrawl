The Shape of Water

The Shape of Water is a 2017 American romantic fantasy film directed and co-written by Guillermo del Toro. Set in Baltimore, Maryland, in 1962, the film follows a mute cleaner at a high-security government laboratory who falls in love with a captured humanoid amphibian creature and resolves to help him escape. The film stars Sally Hawkins, Michael Shannon, Richard Jenkins, Doug Jones, Michael Stuhlbarg, and Octavia Spencer.

Plot

Elisa Esposito, rendered mute by a childhood injury, works as a janitor at the Occultra research facility in Baltimore. Colonel Richard Strickland brings a strange amphibious creature, captured in South America, to the facility for study in the Cold War race against the Soviets. Elisa secretly communicates with the creature through sign language, music, and food, and the two form a bond. When she learns the creature is to be vivisected, she enlists her friend Giles and co-worker Zelda to free him, hiding him in her apartment until the rains can carry him to the sea. A sympathetic scientist, Dr. Hoffstetler — secretly a Soviet agent — helps the escape succeed.

Production

Del Toro conceived the story as an homage to Creature from the Black Lagoon, reimagining it as a romance told from the creature's side. Principal photography took place in Toronto and Hamilton, Ontario, in 2016. Doug Jones performed the Amphibian Man in a prosthetic suit refined from designs del Toro had developed over several years.

Reception and awards

The film premiered at the 74th Venice International Film Festival, where it won the Golden Lion. At the 90th Academy Awards it received thirteen nominations and won four: Best Picture, Best Director, Best Original Score, and Best Production Design. It also won two Golden Globe Awards and was named one of the top ten films of 2017 by the American Film Institute. See also Guillermo del Toro and Academy Award for Best Picture.
