Edd Byrnes (born Edward Byrne Breitenberger; 30 July 1932 – 8 January 2020) was an American actor best known for his role as Gerald Lloyd "Kookie" Kookson III, the jive-talking, hair-combing parking attendant, in the television detective series 77 Sunset Strip (1958–1964).

Career

After small film roles, Byrnes was cast in a single episode of 77 Sunset Strip as a contract killer — but audience response led Warner Bros. to bring him back as a regular, reinvented as the hip valet who parked cars outside the heroes' office. Kookie became a teen idol; fan mail poured in, and Byrnes's duet with Connie Stevens, "Kookie, Kookie (Lend Me Your Comb)" (1959), reached No. 4 on the Billboard chart. Contract disputes and typecasting hampered his later career; he worked in European Westerns in the 1960s and struggled with alcoholism, which he described in his 1996 autobiography Kookie No More. Younger audiences know him as dance-show host Vince Fontaine in the 1978 film Grease. From 1981 to 1996 he hosted NBC's Wheel of Fortune-era pilot appearances and made numerous guest appearances on television into the 1990s.
