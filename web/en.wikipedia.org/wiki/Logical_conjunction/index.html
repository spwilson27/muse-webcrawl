Logical conjunction
Symbol: AND, ∧
Field: Logic

Logical conjunction is the truth-functional operation that is true only when both of its operands are true. It corresponds to 'and' in natural language and is denoted ∧ or AND.

Truth table

For propositions p and q, p ∧ q is true in the single case where p and q are both true, and false in the other three cases.

Use

Conjunction is fundamental to Boolean algebra, digital logic gates, set intersection and formal reasoning.
