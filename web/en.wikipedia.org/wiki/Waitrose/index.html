Waitrose
Founded: 1904 (as Waite, Rose & Taylor)
Owner: John Lewis Partnership
Headquarters: Bracknell, Berkshire, England
Industry: Supermarket retail

Waitrose Limited, trading as Waitrose & Partners, is a British chain of supermarkets forming the food retail division of the John Lewis Partnership. It is known for its upmarket positioning, emphasis on food quality, provenance and customer service, and its employee-ownership model in which staff are "Partners".

History

The business began in 1904 as a small grocery shop called Waite, Rose & Taylor in Acton, London. It was acquired by the John Lewis Partnership in 1937 with ten shops. The chain grew steadily through the twentieth century, pioneered self-service formats in the 1950s and by the 2020s operated over 300 shops, including convenience formats and shops inside motorway service areas.

Ranges and services

Own-label ranges include Essential Waitrose, Waitrose Duchy Organic and the No.1 premium line, alongside services such as home delivery through Waitrose.com. See also John Lewis Partnership.
