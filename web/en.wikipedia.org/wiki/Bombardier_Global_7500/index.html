The Bombardier Global 7500 and Global 8000 are ultra long-range business jets developed by Bombardier Aviation (formerly Bombardier Aerospace) and derived from the Global Express series. Announced in 2010 as the Global 7000, the 7500 first flew on 4 November 2016 and entered service on 20 December 2018. At its introduction it was the largest, longest-range and roomiest purpose-built business jet in production.

The aircraft features a four-zone cabin (club suite, conference suite, entertainment suite and a private suite with an en-suite shower option), a dedicated crew rest area and a full-size galley. Its General Electric Passport engines and advanced wing give it a top speed of Mach 0.925 and a range of 7,700 nautical miles, enabling non-stop flights such as New York–Hong Kong and Singapore–Tucson; in October 2019 a Global 7500 flew 8,225 nmi from Sydney to Detroit, the longest city-pair flight by a purpose-built business jet.

Global 8000

The Global 8000, announced in 2022, is a variant with a range increased to 8,000 nmi and the same Mach 0.94 maximum operating speed demonstrated during flight testing; it also set a record as the fastest civil aircraft since Concorde during a 2021 test flight in which it briefly exceeded the speed of sound in a shallow dive. Existing Global 7500s can be upgraded to the 8000 configuration.
