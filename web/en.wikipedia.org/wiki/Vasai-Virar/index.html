Vasai-Virar is a municipal corporation in Palghar district, Maharashtra, India, formed in 2009 by merging the municipal councils of Vasai (historically Bassein), Virar, Nalasopara and Navghar-Manikpur along with surrounding villages. It forms the northern part of the Mumbai Metropolitan Region, connected to Mumbai by the Western Railway suburban line and the Mumbai–Ahmedabad highway. Its population exceeded 1.2 million at the 2011 census.

History

Vasai, under the name Bassein, was a major Portuguese port and trading centre in the 16th and 17th centuries, ceded by the Sultan of Gujarat in 1534 and captured by the Maratha army of Chimaji Appa in 1739 after the Battle of Vasai. The ruins of the Portuguese Vasai Fort remain a major heritage site. Virar, at the end of the suburban rail line, grew rapidly from the 1970s as affordable housing for Mumbai commuters, and the combined city is now one of the fastest-growing urban areas in Maharashtra.
