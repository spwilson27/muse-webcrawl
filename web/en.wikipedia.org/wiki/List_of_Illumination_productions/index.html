List of Illumination productions

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This is a list of films and other productions by Illumination (formerly Illumination Entertainment), an American animation studio founded by Chris Meledandri in 2007. The studio is a division of Universal Pictures, and all of its feature films are distributed by Universal. Illumination is best known for the Despicable Me franchise, whose installments have made it one of the highest-grossing animation studios in the world.

Short films

Illumination has produced numerous short films, many released with home-video editions of its features, frequently starring the Minions. Examples include Home Makeover, Orientation Day and Banana (2010, with Despicable Me), Puppy (2013), Mower Minions (2016, released theatrically with The Secret Life of Pets), The Secret Life of Kyle (2017), Santa's Little Helpers (2019), Minion Scouts (2019) and Post Modern Minion (2022).

Franchises

  • Despicable Me — six films (four Despicable Me films and two Minions spin-offs); the highest-grossing animated franchise of all time.
  • The Secret Life of Pets — two films.
  • Sing — two films.
  • Dr. Seuss adaptations — The Lorax (2012) and The Grinch (2018).
  • Nintendo — The Super Mario Bros. Movie (2023), produced with Nintendo.
