Düren is a Kreis (district) in the west of North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany, in the region between Aachen and Cologne. Its capital is the city of Düren.

Geography

The district stretches from the lignite-mining lowlands of the Jülich Börde in the north to the foothills of the Eifel in the south. The river Rur flows through it, impounded in the south by the Rur dam (Rurtalsperre), one of Germany's largest reservoirs. Neighbouring districts include Aachen, Euskirchen, Rhein-Erft-Kreis and Heinsberg.

Towns and municipalities

The district comprises 15 municipalities, including the towns of Düren and Jülich — the latter known for its large research centre, Forschungszentrum Jülich.
