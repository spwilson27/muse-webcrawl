Napalm Death

Napalm Death
Origin: Birmingham, England
Genres: Grindcore, death metal, crust punk
Years active: 1981–present
Labels: Earache, Century Media

Napalm Death are an English grindcore band formed in Meriden, West Midlands, in 1981. None of the original members remain in the band, which is widely credited with defining the grindcore genre through the fusion of hardcore punk and extreme metal. Their debut album Scum (1987) is a landmark of extreme music, and their song "You Suffer" is recognized by Guinness World Records as the shortest song ever recorded, at 1.316 seconds.

History

The band was founded by Nicholas Bullen and Miles Ratledge as teenagers, initially playing anarcho-punk. Through a series of line-up changes the sound hardened into the ultra-fast, abrasive style captured on Scum, whose two sides feature almost entirely different personnel. From Enslavement to Obliteration (1988) consolidated their reputation.

With vocalist Mark "Barney" Greenway, guitarist Mitch Harris, bassist Shane Embury (the longest-serving member, having joined in 1987), and drummer Danny Herrera, the 1990s albums Harmony Corruption (1990) and Utopia Banished (1992) moved toward death metal before later releases such as Enemy of the Music Business (2000) and Apex Predator – Easy Meat (2015) returned to a grindcore-hybrid sound.

Style and themes

Napalm Death's music is characterized by extremely fast tempos, blast beats, downtuned guitars, and guttural vocals. Lyrically the band addresses social and political themes including anti-racism, animal rights, and criticism of capitalism and authoritarianism.

Legacy

The band is regarded as a founding influence on grindcore and a major influence on death metal and other extreme genres; their name for the genre itself derives in part from their description of their music.
