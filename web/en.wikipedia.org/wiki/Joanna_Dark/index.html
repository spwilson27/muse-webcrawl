Joanna Dark

Joanna Dark is a fictional character and the protagonist of the Perfect Dark video game series, developed by Rare. An agent of the Carrington Institute, she first appeared in Perfect Dark (2000) for the Nintendo 64 and returned in the prequel Perfect Dark Zero (2005) for the Xbox 360.

Joanna Dark, agent of the Carrington Institute.

Character

Joanna Dark is depicted as an exceptionally skilled operative — marksman, pilot and hand-to-hand fighter — raised and trained by her father Jack Dark, a bounty hunter, before joining Daniel Carrington's research institute. Codename "Perfect Dark" derives from her flawless performance in training. In the original game she uncovers a conspiracy involving the dataDyne corporation and two warring alien races, the Maians and the Skedar.

Appearances

Besides the two main games, Joanna appears in the Game Boy Color Perfect Dark (2000), in novels and comics expanding the series' backstory, and as a referenced character elsewhere in Rare's catalogue. A reboot of the series, announced in 2020 with Joanna again as protagonist, was in development at The Initiative before its cancellation in 2025.

Reception

Joanna Dark was one of the more prominent female protagonists in games of her era and has frequently appeared on lists of notable video game heroines. Critics praised Perfect Dark as a spiritual successor to GoldenEye 007, with Joanna at the centre of its story.
