Chitral

Chitral is a region and district in the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province of Pakistan, in the Hindu Kush mountains along the Afghan border. The town of Chitral is its administrative centre.

Geography

The Chitral valley is drained by the Chitral River (also called the Kunar) and is dominated by Tirich Mir, at 7,708 metres the highest peak of the Hindu Kush. The region is connected to the rest of Pakistan by the Lowari Tunnel and to Afghanistan via high passes such as the Dorah Pass.

Culture

Chitral is home to the Kho people, speakers of Khowar, and to the Kalash valleys, whose inhabitants preserve a distinctive polytheistic culture. The area is famous for polo played at Shandur, the world's highest polo ground, and was a princely state until 1969.
