Herstal is a municipality in the province of Liège, Wallonia, Belgium, located just north-east of the city of Liège on the River Meuse. It is best known internationally as the home of FN Herstal, the firearms manufacturer founded in 1889.

History

The area was settled in early medieval times and was associated with Pippin of Herstal, mayor of the palace of Austrasia and grandfather of Charlemagne. Industrialisation in the 19th century brought coal mining, steel and arms manufacture to the Meuse valley.

Economy

FN Herstal (Fabrique Nationale) has been the dominant employer, producing military and sporting firearms exported worldwide. Other engineering and logistics firms cluster along the Albert Canal.
