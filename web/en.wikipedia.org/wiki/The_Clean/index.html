The Clean

The Clean were a New Zealand indie rock band formed in Dunedin in 1978 by brothers Hamish and David Kilgour and bassist Robert Scott. They are regarded as pioneers of the Dunedin sound and one of the most influential bands on the Flying Nun Records label.

History

The Clean's debut single "Tally Ho!" (1981), recorded for very little money, reached number 19 on the New Zealand charts and was one of the first releases on the newly formed Flying Nun label. The EPs Boodle Boodle Boodle (1981) and Great Sounds Great, Good Sounds Good, So-so Sounds So-so, Bad Sounds Bad, Rotten Sounds Rotten (1982) established their distinctive droning organ, pounding drums and jangling guitar sound.

After a long hiatus the band reformed repeatedly, releasing full-length albums including Vehicle (1990), Modern Rock (1994), Unknown Country (1996), Getaway (2001), Anthology (2003 compilation), Mister Pop (2009), and the album Hello Tomorrow.

Legacy

The Clean's do-it-yourself aesthetic and lo-fi recordings influenced indie rock bands internationally, including Pavement and Yo La Tengo. The compilation Anthology, released in the United States on Merge Records, introduced the band to a new generation of listeners. Following the death of drummer Hamish Kilgour in 2022, the band's remaining members wound down the group.
