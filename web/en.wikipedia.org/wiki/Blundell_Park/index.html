Blundell Park is a football ground in Cleethorpes, North East Lincolnshire, England, and the home of Grimsby Town Football Club. Opened in 1899, it is one of the oldest grounds in the Football League still in use.

History

Grimsby Town moved to Blundell Park in 1899 from Abbey Park. The ground's record attendance of 31,651 was set for an FA Cup tie against Wolverhampton Wanderers in 1937. The Main Stand, dating from 1901, is among the oldest stands in English football. Floodlights were installed in 1958, funded partly by supporters.

Stands

The ground comprises the Main Stand, the Pontoon Stand behind one goal (home of the club's most vocal supporters), the Findus Stand (later the Young's Stand), and the Osmond Stand for away supporters.

Future

The club has long explored relocating to a new stadium, though Blundell Park remains its home. See also Cleethorpes.
