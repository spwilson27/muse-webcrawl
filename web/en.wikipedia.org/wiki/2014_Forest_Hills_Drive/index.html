2014 Forest Hills Drive is the third studio album by American rapper J. Cole, released on 9 December 2014 by Dreamville Records, Roc Nation and Columbia Records. The album's title refers to the address of the childhood home in Fayetteville, North Carolina, that Cole repurchased in 2014.

Background

The album was announced with minimal promotion and no preceding singles, an unusual strategy that generated considerable attention. Its themes trace Cole's journey from his upbringing in Fayetteville to fame and his reflections on success, family and materialism.

Commercial performance

The album debuted at number one on the US Billboard 200. It became the best-selling hip hop album of 2015 in the United States and was notable for reaching triple platinum certification without any featured artists — every track is performed solely by Cole.

Track listing

Tracks include "Intro", "January 28th", "Wet Dreamz", "03' Adolescence", "Fire Squad", "G.O.M.D.", "No Role Modelz", "Love Yourz" and the fourteen-minute closing credits track "Note to Self".

Reception

The album received generally positive reviews and was nominated for Best Rap Album at the 58th Grammy Awards. "Apparently" earned a nomination for Best Rap Performance.
