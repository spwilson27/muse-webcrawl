Original design manufacturer

ODMs design and manufacture products sold under other companies' brands.

An original design manufacturer (ODM) is a company that designs and manufactures a product, as specified, that is eventually rebranded by another firm for sale. Such companies allow the brand firm to expand its range of products without having to engage in the organisation or running of a factory. ODMs have grown in size in recent years and, as of 2015, many have the scale to handle production in-house for products such as mobile phones, laptops, televisions, and home appliances ordered by the brand company in large volumes.

Comparison with OEMs

An ODM differs from an original equipment manufacturer (OEM). In the OEM model, the brand company supplies the product design and the contractor manufactures it to specification. In the ODM model, the contractor itself designs the product — often offering a catalogue of reference designs that customers can customise cosmetically — and owns much of the underlying intellectual property. A related term is white-label manufacturing, in which generic products are sold to many brands with minimal modification.

Business model and geography

The ODM model is especially prominent in the electronics industry of Taiwan, China, and Southeast Asia. Major ODMs include Compal, Quanta Computer, Wistron, Inventec, and Pegatron (Taiwan), long known for producing notebook computers for global brands, and contract manufacturers such as Foxconn, which combine OEM and ODM work. Because the ODM bears the cost of research and development and spreads it across multiple customers, brand firms — including start-ups — can bring products to market quickly and cheaply. The trade-off is limited product differentiation, since rival brands may sell near-identical devices based on the same reference design.
