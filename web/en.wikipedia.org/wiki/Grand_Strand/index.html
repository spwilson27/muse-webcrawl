Grand Strand

LocationSouth Carolina (and partly North Carolina), United States
LengthAbout 60 miles (97 km)
Major cityMyrtle Beach

The Grand Strand is an arc of beach land on the Atlantic Ocean in South Carolina, United States, extending more than 60 miles (97 km) from Cape Fear, North Carolina, to Winyah Bay in Georgetown County, South Carolina. The Grand Strand is home to an estimated 400,000 full-time residents (2020 census) and hosts millions of tourists each year, centred on the city of Myrtle Beach.

Communities

Communities along the Strand include North Myrtle Beach, Atlantic Beach, Surfside Beach, Garden City, Murrells Inlet, Pawleys Island, and Georgetown, as well as inland Conway, the seat of Horry County. The region is one of the major tourist destinations on the U.S. East Coast, known for beaches, golf courses (more than 100 in the area), seafood restaurants, and attractions such as the Myrtle Beach Boardwalk and SkyWheel.

History and name

The name "Grand Strand" was coined in the mid-20th century as the area developed into a resort destination; the term "strand" refers to the long stretch of beach. Resort development accelerated after the 1954 Hurricane Hazel and the subsequent rebuilding, and the area later became a year-round retirement and second-home market.
