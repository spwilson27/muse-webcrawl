Dr. John A. Zoidberg is a fictional character in the animated television series Futurama, created by Matt Groening and David X. Cohen and voiced by Billy West. Zoidberg is a lobster-like alien from the planet Decapod 10 who works as the staff physician of the Planet Express delivery company, despite being almost comically ignorant of human anatomy and perpetually impoverished. The character is one of the show's principal sources of comic relief and has become a popular internet meme.

Dr. Zoidberg, Planet Express's resident physician, a Decapodian with claws and an ink pouch.

Character

Zoidberg is a Decapodian: a crustacean-like humanoid with claws, a beak-like mouth of fleshy tendrils, an ink pouch for escaping embarrassment, and a habit of scuttling sideways while making a "woop-woop-woop" sound — a homage to the Three Stooges' Curly Howard. Although he holds the title of doctor, he routinely confuses human internal organs, at one point insisting that humans have multiple mouths and that the heart is in the head. His incompetence is explained in part by his specialisation in alien medicine rather than human physiology.

Despite working for a delivery company for decades, Zoidberg is destitute, eating from dumpsters and living in poverty while his co-workers mock or ignore him. His friendship with Professor Farnsworth dates to a military mission in their youth. Episodes exploring his past include "Why Must I Be a Crustacean in Love?", in which he returns home to mate, and "Stench and Stenchibility", in which he finds love with a flower seller who lacks a sense of smell.

Reception

Zoidberg is consistently ranked among the most popular Futurama characters. His catchphrase of self-pity — "Why not Zoidberg?" — originating in the episode "A Taste of Freedom", became a widely used internet meme, typically paired with an image of the character to express being overlooked. Critics have praised Billy West's vocal performance and the character's combination of grotesque comedy and pathos.
