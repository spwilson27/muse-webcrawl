Sanctuary of Fátima

Sanctuary of Fátima
Location: Fátima, Ourém, Portugal
Denomination: Roman Catholic
Dedication: Our Lady of Fátima
Status: Major Marian shrine

The Sanctuary of Fátima (Portuguese: Santuário de Fátima), officially the Sanctuary of Our Lady of the Rosary of Fátima, is a Marian shrine dedicated to Our Lady of Fátima, located in Fátima, in the municipality of Ourém, in Portugal. It consists of a group of Catholic religious buildings and structures built at the Cova da Iria, where in 1917 three shepherd children — Lúcia dos Santos and her cousins Francisco and Jacinta Marto — reported apparitions of the Virgin Mary, culminating in the "Miracle of the Sun" witnessed by a large crowd on 13 October 1917. The sanctuary is one of the most important Catholic pilgrimage destinations in the world, receiving millions of pilgrims annually, especially on the 13th of May and October.

Buildings

The site is dominated by a vast rectangular prayer area flanked by colonnades. At its head stands the Basilica of Our Lady of the Rosary (1928–53), a neo-baroque church with a 65-metre tower crowned by a great bronze crown and cross; inside are the tombs of the three visionaries. Near the site of the apparitions stands the Chapel of the Apparitions (1919, rebuilt after being dynamited in 1922), enclosing the pedestal where the Virgin is said to have appeared above a holm oak. Across the esplanade, the Basilica of the Holy Trinity, consecrated in 2007, is one of the largest churches in the world, seating about 8,600 people.

Pilgrimage

Pilgrims gather for candlelight processions on the eve of the 13th, recitation of the rosary, and the emotional "Adeus" (farewell) ceremony in which white handkerchiefs are waved. Some cross the esplanade on their knees in fulfilment of vows. Popes Paul VI, John Paul II (who credited Our Lady of Fátima with saving his life in the 1981 assassination attempt), Benedict XVI, and Francis have all visited; Francis canonised Francisco and Jacinta Marto at the sanctuary during the centenary in May 2017.
