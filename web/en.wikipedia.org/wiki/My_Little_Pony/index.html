My Little Pony is a toy line and media franchise owned by the American company Hasbro. The toys are colourful plastic ponies, each with a distinctive symbol ("cutie mark") on its flank and a long, brushable mane and tail. First launched in 1982 (following the 1981 "My Pretty Pony"), the line has been relaunched in several generations accompanied by animated television series, films, comics and other merchandise, and has become one of Hasbro's flagship brands.

Generations

The first generation (1982–1995) featured Earth ponies, unicorns and pegasi, supported by the animated series My Little Pony (1986–87) and a feature film. A smaller second generation appeared from 1997, and the third generation (2003) emphasised a handful of core characters in direct-to-video animation. The fourth generation, launched in 2010 with the animated series My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic, developed by Lauren Faust, was a major commercial and critical success; unusually, it attracted a large adult fan following known as "bronies", generating conventions, fan art and academic study. A fifth generation began in 2021 with the film My Little Pony: A New Generation and follow-up series on streaming platforms.

Cultural impact

The franchise's themes of friendship and kindness, collectible toy design, and extensive character roster have kept it prominent in popular culture for over four decades. See also Hasbro and My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic.
