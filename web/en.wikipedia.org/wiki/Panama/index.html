Panama

Panama, officially the Republic of Panama, is a country in Central America and the Caribbean, bordered by Costa Rica and Colombia, with coasts on the Caribbean Sea and the Pacific Ocean. Its capital is Panama City, and its population is about 4.5 million.

Panama's defining feature is the Panama Canal, built by the United States (opened 1914) and transferred to Panama in 1999, which together with banking, the Colón Free Zone and shipping registry makes Panama a global logistics hub. The country seceded from Colombia in 1903 with US support precisely to enable the canal. Panama City's skyline of towers fronts the bay, while the old town, Casco Viejo, is a UNESCO site. The Darién Gap, a roadless jungle on the Colombian border, became a major and dangerous migration route in the 2020s.
