Dunedin Railways

Dunedin Railways (formerly the Taieri Gorge Railway) is the trading name of Dunedin Railways Limited, a tourist railway operator based at Dunedin railway station in Dunedin, New Zealand. It is owned by the Dunedin City Council and operates scenic train journeys through the Taieri Gorge and along the Otago coast.

History

The line through the Taieri Gorge was built in the late 19th century as part of the Otago Central Railway. When the regular passenger service to Central Otago ended, the Dunedin-based Taieri Gorge Railway trust took over the Dunedin–Pukerangi section in 1990 to preserve it as a tourist attraction, using vintage carriages and diesel locomotives. The operation became a council-controlled company in 2014 under the Dunedin Railways name.

Services

The flagship journey runs from Dunedin's ornate Edwardian station through the rugged Taieri River gorge, crossing wrought-iron viaducts and passing through tunnels to Pukerangi and, on some trips, Middlemarch. The "Seasider" runs north along the cliffs of the Otago coast toward Palmerston. Trains use heritage DJ class diesel locomotives and carriages dating from the early 20th century.

Recent history

The operation was severely affected by the loss of cruise-ship and international visitors during the COVID-19 pandemic and was mothballed in 2020 with reduced services resuming later; debates over its funding and future became a major local issue for the Dunedin City Council.
