PC World (stylised PCWorld) is a global computer magazine and website published by IDG. It was founded in 1983 and for three decades was one of the most widely read computer publications, offering news, reviews, benchmarks and how-to advice on personal computers, peripherals and software.

History

The magazine's first issue appeared in March 1983, timed to the early growth of the IBM PC market. PC World maintained test labs that published comparative reviews of PCs, printers and components. The US print edition ceased with the August 2013 issue as publishing moved online; international editions appeared in many countries and languages, and the brand continues as a technology website under Foundry, IDG's successor.
