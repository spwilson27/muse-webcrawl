Districts of Tanzania

Tanzania is administratively divided into thirty-one regions (mkoa). In turn, it is subdivided into 195 districts (wilaya), which form the country's local government authorities. Districts are further subdivided into divisions, wards (kata), and villages (kijiji); urban districts are administered as city, municipal, or town councils.

Administration

Districts are governed by district councils under the Local Government (District Authorities) Act, with an elected council and an appointed district executive director. Urban authorities (such as the city councils of Dar es Salaam, Mwanza, Arusha, Dodoma, and Mbeya) administer Tanzania's major cities. New districts have been created repeatedly by splitting existing ones, both to bring services closer to citizens and for political representation; the number of districts has grown steadily since independence, and several regions — most recently in the 2010s — gained additional districts.

Examples by region

  • Arusha Region — Arusha City, Arusha, Karatu, Longido, Meru, Monduli, Ngorongoro
  • Dar es Salaam Region — Ilala, Kinondoni, Temeke, Kigamboni, Ubungo
  • Dodoma Region — Dodoma Municipal, Bahi, Chamwino, Chemba, Kondoa, Kongwa, Mpwapwa
  • Kilimanjaro Region — Hai, Moshi Municipal, Moshi Rural, Mwanga, Rombo, Same, Siha
  • Mwanza Region — Ilemela, Nyamagana, Kwimba, Magu, Misungwi, Sengerema, Ukerewe, Buchosa
  • Zanzibar — districts include Kaskazini A and B, Kati, Kusini, Magharibi A and B, Mjini, Micheweni, Wete, Chake Chake, and Mkoani
