Hannah Montana: The Movie is a 2009 American musical comedy-drama film based on the Disney Channel television series Hannah Montana. Directed by Peter Chelsom and produced by Walt Disney Pictures, it stars Miley Cyrus as Miley Stewart / Hannah Montana, alongside Billy Ray Cyrus, Emily Osment, Jason Earles, Mitchel Musso, Lucas Till and Vanessa Williams. The film was released in the United States on April 10, 2009.

Plot

As Miley's fame as pop star Hannah Montana begins to overwhelm her life in Los Angeles — including a public feud and a missed family commitment — her father Robby Ray takes her back to their hometown of Crowley Corners, Tennessee, for her grandmother's birthday, hoping to reconnect her with her roots. There Miley reconnects with childhood friend Travis, helps save the town from a developer, and struggles to keep her double identity secret before a climactic concert where she reveals the truth to the crowd, who promise to keep her secret.

Music and reception

The soundtrack includes Cyrus's hit "The Climb", which became a top-five single in the United States, and Taylor Swift's "Crazier"; Swift also makes a cameo performance. The film was a commercial success, grossing about $155 million worldwide on a budget of roughly $30 million, though reviews were mixed, with critics finding it a pleasant extension of the series for its young audience.
