Stilgar is a fictional character in the Dune universe created by Frank Herbert. He appears in the novels Dune (1965) and Dune Messiah (1969), and is mentioned in Children of Dune (1976). The naib (leader) of Sietch Tabr, a Fremen community on the desert planet Arrakis, Stilgar becomes a close ally and follower of Paul Atreides.

Role in the novels

Stilgar first encounters Duke Leto Atreides as an emissary of the Fremen, spitting on the council table—a gesture of respect in Fremen custom, offering moisture to a guest. After House Atreides falls, Stilgar shelters Paul and Lady Jessica, initially intending to sacrifice Jessica but accepting the pair into the sietch after Paul's victories and Jessica's demonstration of her "weirding" abilities. Stilgar becomes one of Paul's chief lieutenants in the guerrilla war against the Harkonnens and the Emperor, and later a pillar of Paul's empire and regency council in the sequels.

Portrayals

Everett McGill played Stilgar in David Lynch's 1984 film Dune. Javier Bardem portrays him in Denis Villeneuve's Dune (2021) and Dune: Part Two (2024), where the character is depicted as a fervent believer in Paul as the prophesied Lisan al Gaib.
