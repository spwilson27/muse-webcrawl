The Rolex Grand Slam of Eventing is a prize in the equestrian sport of eventing, sponsored by Rolex, awarded to any rider who consecutively wins the three premier four-star (now five-star) three-day events: the Kentucky Three-Day Event in the United States, the Badminton Horse Trials and the Burghley Horse Trials, both in England.

Cross-country day at one of the Grand Slam events.

History

The Grand Slam was created in 1999, offering a large cash prize — originally $250,000, later $350,000 — for the unprecedented feat of winning all three competitions in a row, in any order, with the sequence continuing across seasons. Each event is among the most demanding in the sport, requiring dressage, cross-country and show jumping phases over successive days.

Winners

Only two riders have completed the Grand Slam. Britain's Pippa Funnell became the first in 2003, winning Kentucky with Primmore's Pride, Badminton with Supreme Rock and Burghley with Primmore's Pride. Germany's Michael Jung completed the Slam in 2016, winning Burghley (2015) and Kentucky and Badminton (2016) with La Biosthetique-Sam FBW.
