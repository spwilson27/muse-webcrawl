List of Scottish Gaelic given names

This is a list of Scottish Gaelic given names, with their Anglicised forms and meanings where known. Scottish Gaelic names derive from native Gaelic roots, from Norse influence in the Highlands and Islands, and from Gaelicised forms of Norman, Latin and biblical names.
