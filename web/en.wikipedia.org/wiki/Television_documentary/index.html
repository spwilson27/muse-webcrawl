Television documentary

A television documentary is a documentary film or programme produced for broadcast on television, as distinct from one made for theatrical release. Television documentaries may be single programmes or multipart series, and cover subjects such as nature, history, science, current affairs, biography, and the arts.

History

The documentary found a mass audience with the growth of television after World War II. Landmark series include the BBC's nature output associated with David Attenborough, such as Life on Earth (1979) and Planet Earth (2006); ITV's current-affairs series World in Action; and American works such as The Civil War (1990) by Ken Burns, a hallmark of PBS documentary style.

Formats

Common formats include the presenter-led film, observational or fly-on-the-wall filming, the investigative report, the docudrama, and the documentary series. Cable channels such as Discovery Channel, National Geographic, and the History Channel, and later streaming services, greatly expanded the market for television documentaries.
