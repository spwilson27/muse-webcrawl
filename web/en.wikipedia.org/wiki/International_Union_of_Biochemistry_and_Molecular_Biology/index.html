International Union of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology

The International Union of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology (IUBMB) is an international non-governmental organisation concerned with biochemistry and molecular biology. Founded in 1955, it brings together national societies representing biochemists and molecular biologists in dozens of countries.

Activities

The IUBMB is best known for its role in standardising nomenclature. Together with IUPAC, it maintains the recommendations on enzyme nomenclature through the Nomenclature Committee (NC-IUBMB), which assigns EC numbers classifying enzyme-catalysed reactions.

Publications and meetings

The union sponsors journals including IUBMB Life and Biochemistry and Molecular Biology Education, and organises international congresses and smaller focused meetings, fellowships and training initiatives, particularly supporting early-career scientists in developing countries.
