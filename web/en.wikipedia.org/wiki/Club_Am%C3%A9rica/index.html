Club de Fútbol América, commonly known as Club América, is a Mexican professional football club based in Mexico City. Founded in 1916, it competes in Liga MX and is the most successful club in Mexican football history, holding records for league championships and international titles.

History

América was founded by a group of students who named the club after the continent, the team having been formed on the anniversary of Columbus's arrival in the Americas. The club was a founding member of the professional league in 1943. Its modern era of dominance began after the media company Televisa acquired the club in 1959, providing the resources to sign star players; the Estadio Azteca, one of the largest stadiums in the world, opened as its home in 1966.

Rivalries

América's fiercest rivalry is with Guadalajara (Chivas), the Súper Clásico, a clash between Mexico's two most popular clubs. It also contests the Clásico Capitalino with Pumas UNAM and the Clásico Joven with Cruz Azul.

Honours

América has won a record number of Mexican league titles (15 by 2024, including three consecutive Apertura/Clausura crowns in 2023–24 under André Jardine), multiple Copa México trophies, and a record seven CONCACAF Champions Cup/Champions League titles, making it the most decorated club in North American football.
