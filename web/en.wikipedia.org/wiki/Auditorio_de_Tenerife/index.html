Auditorio de Tenerife

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Auditorio de Tenerife "Adán Martín" is an arts complex and concert hall in Santa Cruz de Tenerife, Canary Islands, Spain. Designed by the architect Santiago Calatrava and opened in 2003, its sweeping sail-like concrete shell has become the most recognisable symbol of the city and of Tenerife, frequently compared to the Sydney Opera House.

Architecture

The building stands on the waterfront between the marine park and the port. Its dominant feature is a great curved wing of white concrete that rises from the base and sweeps over the auditorium like a wave or sail, reaching about 58 metres at its peak before tapering to a point. The exterior is clad in white trencadís (broken-tile mosaic).

Facilities and programme

Inside are the Symphony Hall, with vineyard-style seating around the stage and a large pipe organ whose pipes emerge from the walls, and a smaller Chamber Hall seating 422. The complex is home to the Tenerife Symphony Orchestra and hosts the Ópera de Tenerife season, the Festival de Música de Canarias, and the annual Premios Cadena Dial music awards.
