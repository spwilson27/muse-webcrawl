Dictionnaire Infernal

The Dictionnaire Infernal ("Infernal Dictionary") is a French book on demonology written by Jacques Collin de Plancy, first published in 1818. It catalogues demons, occult beings and related subjects, organising the hierarchy of Hell and describing dozens of named demons.

The 1863 edition

The work is most famous for its sixth edition of 1863, illustrated with 69 engravings by Louis Le Breton, whose imaginative portraits of demons — including Astaroth as an ugly angel riding a dragon, and Belphegor — have become iconic images in popular culture and are reproduced widely today.

Author's stance

Collin de Plancy wrote the early editions as a sceptic mocking superstition, but after his return to Catholicism he revised the work to affirm the reality of its subject matter; the later editions were endorsed by the Church.
