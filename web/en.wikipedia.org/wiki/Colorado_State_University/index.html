Colorado State University (Colorado State or CSU) is a public land-grant research university in Fort Collins, Colorado. Founded in 1870 as the Colorado Agricultural College under the Morrill Act, it opened to students in 1879. It is the flagship institution of the Colorado State University System and is classified among universities with very high research activity.

Academics and research

CSU offers degrees through eight colleges, including well-regarded programs in veterinary medicine, atmospheric science, agriculture, engineering and natural resources. Its College of Veterinary Medicine and Biomedical Sciences is consistently ranked among the top in the United States, and the university is a leading centre for atmospheric and environmental research in the Rocky Mountain region. The tree-lined Oval, with its historic American elm trees, anchors the main campus.

Student life and athletics

The university's sports teams, the Colorado State Rams, compete in the Mountain West Conference; the football team plays at Canvas Stadium, opened in 2017. CSU's mascot is CAM the Ram. The annual Rocky Mountain Showdown football game against the University of Colorado is a highlight of the sporting calendar.
