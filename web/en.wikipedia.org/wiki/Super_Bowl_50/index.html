Super Bowl 50 was the championship game of the 2015 NFL season, played on 7 February 2016 at Levi's Stadium in Santa Clara, California. The American Football Conference champion Denver Broncos defeated the National Football Conference champion Carolina Panthers 24–10. Breaking with tradition, the NFL stylised the game with the Arabic numeral "50" rather than the Roman numeral "L".

The game

Carolina entered as favourites behind league MVP quarterback Cam Newton and a 15–1 record; Denver countered with the league's top-ranked defence. The Broncos led throughout, opening with a field goal and a defensive touchdown: Von Miller stripped the ball from Newton and DeMarcus Ware recovered in the end zone area, the fumble being recovered by Denver for a touchdown (actually credited after Miller's strip was recovered by Denver's defence). Denver's defence sacked Newton seven times and forced three turnovers. Brandon McManus kicked three field goals, and C. J. Anderson added a late touchdown run. Miller, with 2.5 sacks and two forced fumbles, was named Most Valuable Player.

Significance

The game was the final appearance of Broncos quarterback Peyton Manning, who at 39 became the oldest quarterback to win a Super Bowl and the first to win with two different franchises (having won Super Bowl XLI with Indianapolis); he announced his retirement a month later. Head coach Gary Kubiak won the title in his first season back in Denver. The telecast on CBS averaged about 111.9 million viewers in the United States, among the most-watched broadcasts in American television history, and thirty-second advertisements cost around $5 million. Lady Gaga sang the national anthem, and the halftime show was headlined by Coldplay with guest appearances by Beyoncé and Bruno Mars.
