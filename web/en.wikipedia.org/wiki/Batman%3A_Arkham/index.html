Batman: Arkham

GenreAction-adventure
DeveloperRocksteady Studios; WB Games Montréal
PublisherWarner Bros. Interactive Entertainment
First releaseBatman: Arkham Asylum (2009)

Batman: Arkham is a series of action-adventure video games based on the DC Comics character Batman, developed principally by Rocksteady Studios. The series is celebrated for its free-flow combat system, stealth "predator" encounters, detective mechanics and its dark interpretation of Gotham City and the rogues' gallery, with Kevin Conroy and Mark Hamill voicing Batman and the Joker in most entries.

Main games

Batman: Arkham Asylum (2009) confined the action to Arkham Island and won wide acclaim, including a Guinness World Record as the most critically acclaimed superhero game. Batman: Arkham City (2011) expanded into an open-world prison district of Gotham. Batman: Arkham Origins (2013), a prequel by WB Games Montréal, and Batman: Arkham Knight (2015), which introduced the drivable Batmobile, completed the main console arc. A virtual-reality entry, Arkham Shadow, followed in 2024.

Gameplay

Core mechanics include rhythm-based melee combat rewarding uninterrupted counters, silent takedowns from gargoyles and vents, and "Detective Vision" highlighting clues and enemies. Gadgets such as the grapnel gun, batarangs and explosive gel open new paths in a Metroidvania-like structure.

Reception and influence

The series has sold tens of millions of copies and influenced combat design across the action-adventure genre.
