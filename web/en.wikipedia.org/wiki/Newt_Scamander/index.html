Newt Scamander

Newton Artemis Fido "Newt" Scamander is a fictional character in J. K. Rowling's Wizarding World franchise. A magizoologist — a wizard who studies magical creatures — he is the in-universe author of the textbook Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them (1927), a real companion book Rowling published in 2001. Newt is the protagonist of the Fantastic Beasts film series, in which he is portrayed by Eddie Redmayne.

Character biography

Born in 1897, Newt attended Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, where he was sorted into Hufflepuff, but was expelled after an incident involving a magical creature endangered another student — an episode in which his friend Leta Lestrange was implicated. He later worked for the Ministry of Magic, including in the Beast Division of the Department for the Regulation and Control of Magical Creatures, and travelled the world researching creatures for his book. His efforts helped establish the 1965 Ban on Experimental Breeding.

Films

In Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them (2016), Newt arrives in New York with a suitcase of magical creatures and becomes entangled in the hunt for the dark wizard Gellert Grindelwald and an Obscurus. He returns in The Crimes of Grindelwald (2018) and The Secrets of Dumbledore (2022), working with Albus Dumbledore against Grindelwald. In the original Harry Potter books his textbook is a set text for first-year students.
