Microsurgery

Microsurgery is a general term for surgery requiring an operating microscope. The procedures are performed on very small structures of the body, such as blood vessels and nerves typically 1–3 millimetres in diameter, using specialised instruments and fine sutures.

Applications

Microsurgical techniques are central to reconstructive plastic surgery (free tissue transfer), replantation of amputated digits and limbs, peripheral nerve repair, vascular anastomosis, ophthalmic surgery and infertility procedures such as vasectomy reversal.

History

The field developed in the mid-20th century with improved microscopes and instruments, enabling successful replantation and free flap surgery from the 1960s and 1970s onward.
