Sky Sports is a group of British subscription sports television channels operated by the satellite pay television company Sky Group (a division of Comcast). Sky Sports is the dominant subscription television sports brand in the United Kingdom and Ireland, and played a major role in the commercialisation of British sport since the 1990s, notably through its association with the Premier League, whose creation in 1992 was driven in part by Sky's broadcast money.

Channels and coverage

Following a 2017 rebranding, the network's channels are themed by sport, including Sky Sports Premier League, Sky Sports Football, Sky Sports Cricket, Sky Sports Golf, Sky Sports F1, Sky Sports Tennis, Sky Sports Action and the flagship Sky Sports Main Event, alongside the free-to-air rolling news channel Sky Sports News. Sky holds exclusive UK rights to Formula One and the majority of Premier League matches, and covers Test cricket, The Open and Ryder Cup golf, PDC darts and the NFL.

History

Sky's sports coverage began with the Eurosport venture and the BSB Sports Channel; after the 1990 merger forming BSkyB, Sky Sports launched in 1991. Its £304 million deal for the first five seasons of Premier League rights in 1992 transformed English football broadcasting. The service is available via satellite, cable, IPTV and the NOW streaming service.
