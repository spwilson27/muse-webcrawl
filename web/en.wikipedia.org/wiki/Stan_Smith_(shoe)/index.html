The Adidas Stan Smith is a tennis shoe made by Adidas, first introduced in 1965 and named for American tennis champion Stan Smith. One of the best-selling sneakers of all time — with sales reported in the tens of millions of pairs — its minimalist white leather design has made it a fashion staple far beyond the tennis court.

History

Adidas developed the shoe in 1963–65 as its first leather tennis shoe, originally endorsed by French player Robert Haillet. In 1973, Adidas signed the American star Stan Smith, then world number one and a Wimbledon and US Open champion; from 1978 the shoe bore his name and a sketch of his face on the tongue, notably without the usual three-stripes branding on the side — instead three rows of perforations serve as ventilation and a subtle stripe motif. A green heel tab and green lettering became the classic colourway.

Fashion status

The shoe was periodically discontinued and reissued; a widely publicized 2014 relaunch, supported by designer collaborations (including with Raf Simons and Pharrell Williams), turned it into a ubiquitous fashion item worn by celebrities and featured in style media. Guinness World Records once listed it among the best-selling athletic shoes ever made.
