In physics, a force is an influence that can cause an object to change velocity — to accelerate. Newton's laws relate force, mass and acceleration (F = ma) and govern the motions analysed from projectiles to planets.

Fundamental forces

Nature has four fundamental interactions: gravity, electromagnetism (see Magnetism), and the strong and weak nuclear forces behind fission and alpha decay. Everyday forces — friction, tension, the normal force — are electromagnetic in origin. Einstein's relativity recast gravity as spacetime curvature, probed by the Hubble and Webb telescopes.
