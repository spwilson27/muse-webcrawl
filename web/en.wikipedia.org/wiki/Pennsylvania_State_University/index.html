The Pennsylvania State University (Penn State) is a public research university with campuses throughout Pennsylvania, founded in 1855 as the Farmers' High School of Pennsylvania. Its administrative and largest campus is University Park.

History and organisation

Penn State became Pennsylvania's land-grant institution in 1863 and grew into one of the largest universities in the United States, with some 100,000 students across its campuses, including World Campus online. It is classified among research universities with very high research activity and operates the Penn State Health system and Dickinson Law.

Campus life and athletics

The Nittany Lions compete in the Big Ten Conference; Beaver Stadium is one of the largest stadiums in the world. Penn State is also known for THON, the largest student-run philanthropy in the world, a 46-hour dance marathon raising money for childhood cancer care. The university was profoundly affected by the 2011 child sex abuse scandal involving former coach Jerry Sandusky, leading to major sanctions and reforms.
