Todd McFarlane

Todd McFarlane (born March 16, 1961) is a Canadian comic book creator, artist, writer, and entrepreneur best known for his work on The Amazing Spider-Man and as the creator of the supernatural antihero Spawn.

Early life and career

Born in Calgary, Alberta, McFarlane studied at Eastern Washington University, where he played baseball and hoped for a professional sports career before injuries redirected him toward art. After hundreds of rejection letters, he broke into comics at Marvel in the mid-1980s, drawing The Incredible Hulk and then The Amazing Spider-Man, where his dynamic, detail-heavy style — including a spider symbol redesign and the full figure treatment of Venom, whom he co-created with writer David Michelinie — made him one of the industry's best-selling artists.

Image Comics and Spawn

In 1992 McFarlane co-founded Image Comics with six other prominent artists in a landmark push for creator-owned work. His own series Spawn debuted that year and became one of the best-selling independent comics ever; its first issue sold over a million copies. A live-action film followed in 1997, along with an acclaimed HBO animated series.

Other ventures

McFarlane founded McFarlane Toys in 1994, known for highly detailed action figures, and McFarlane Entertainment. He is also an avid collector of baseball memorabilia, famously purchasing Mark McGwire's record 70th home-run ball. In 2020–2021 he launched an expanded "Spawn's Universe" line of interconnected titles.
