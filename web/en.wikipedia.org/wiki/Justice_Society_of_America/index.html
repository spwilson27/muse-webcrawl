The Justice Society of America (JSA) is a superhero team appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Gardner Fox and editor Sheldon Mayer, the team first appeared in All Star Comics #3 (Winter 1940–41). The Justice Society was the first team of superheroes in comic books, and its members were the flagship characters of the Golden Age of Comic Books. Most of DC's later teams, including the Justice League, were built on its example.

Publication history

All Star Comics, conceived as an anthology in which the publisher's heroes appeared side by side, introduced the Society in its third issue with a frame story of the heroes exchanging tales around a table. The founding line-up featured the Flash (Jay Garrick), Green Lantern (Alan Scott), Hawkman (Carter Hall), Doctor Fate, Hourman, the Atom, Sandman and the Spectre; Superman and Batman were honorary members, and Wonder Woman joined as the team's secretary — later an active member — becoming one of the few heroines of the era. Johnny Thunder and Doctor Mid-Nite also joined during the run.

As superhero sales fell, All Star Comics became All-Star Western in 1951 and the team faded away. In the Silver Age revival, DC explained the Society as the heroes of a parallel world, Earth-Two, and the annual Justice League/Justice Society team-ups begun in 1963 — including the landmark "Crisis on Earth-One!" — kept the characters alive. The team returned in its own revivals in the 1970s (introducing Power Girl) and in the acclaimed JSA series of 1999, which teamed the ageing originals with legacy heroes. After subsequent reboots, the JSA's history was progressively restored in the mainstream DC continuity.

Concept and legacy

The Society pioneered the superhero team as a format: a shared meeting place, a chairman, combined operations against menaces too great for one hero. Its later portrayals emphasise mentorship and legacy — the passing of names and ideals from the wartime generation to their children and successors — distinguishing it from the Justice League's gathering of peers. The team appeared in live action in the film Black Adam (2022) and in television series including Smallville, Legends of Tomorrow and Stargirl.
