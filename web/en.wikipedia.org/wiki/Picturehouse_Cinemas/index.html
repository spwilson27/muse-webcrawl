Picturehouse Cinemas

Picturehouse Cinemas is a British network of cinemas specialising in arthouse, independent, and world cinema, combined with bars, cafés, and event screenings. It was founded in 1989 by Lyn Goleby and Tony Jones, beginning with the Phoenix Cinema in Oxford.

History

The chain grew through acquiring and restoring historic cinemas, including the Ritzy in Brixton, the Cameo in Edinburgh, and the Duke of York's in Brighton, one of Britain's oldest cinemas. In December 2012 Picturehouse was acquired by Cineworld for £47.3 million, continuing to operate as a distinct brand. Its flagship, Picturehouse Central, opened on Shaftesbury Avenue in London in 2015.

Programming

Picturehouse screens mainstream releases alongside independent and foreign-language films, plus live broadcasts of theatre, opera, and ballet, Q&A events, and community programmes such as parent-and-baby screenings and dementia-friendly shows. The company also operates the distribution arm Picturehouse Entertainment.
