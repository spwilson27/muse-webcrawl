Zagato

Founded: 1919, Milan, Italy

Founder: Ugo Zagato

Industry: Automotive coachbuilding and design

Zagato is an Italian coachbuilding and design company founded in Milan in 1919 by Ugo Zagato. It is renowned for lightweight, aerodynamic sports-car bodies, frequently distinguished by the "double bubble" roof profile.

History

Ugo Zagato applied techniques learned in the aircraft industry to automobile bodies, producing light aluminium coachwork for Alfa Romeo racing cars in the 1920s. The firm went on to create celebrated designs for Maserati, Lancia, Fiat, Aston Martin and other marques, including the Aston Martin DB4 GT Zagato of 1960.

Design

Zagato bodies emphasize low weight and aerodynamic efficiency. Signature elements include the double-bubble roof, panoramic windscreens and truncated "coda tronca" tail treatments.
