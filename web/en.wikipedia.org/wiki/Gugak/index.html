Gugak

Gugak (Korean: 국악, "national music") is the traditional music of Korea, encompassing court music, folk music, and ceremonial and religious repertoires developed over many centuries.

Categories

Gugak is conventionally divided into jeongak (court and aristocratic music), including the Confucian ritual music aak and the native court tradition hyangak, and minsogak (folk music), which includes farmers' dance music (nongak), the narrative singing of pansori, and the shamanic accompaniment sinawi. Instruments include the geomungo and gayageum zithers, the daegeum flute, and the janggu hourglass drum.

Preservation

The National Gugak Center in Seoul, founded in 1951, is the principal institution for the preservation, research and performance of gugak. Many gugak genres are designated Important Intangible Cultural Properties of Korea, and several are inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.
