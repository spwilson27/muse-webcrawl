NAM Atlântico (A140) is a multipurpose helicopter carrier (Navio Aeródromo Multipropósito) and the flagship of the Brazilian Navy. She was originally built for the British Royal Navy as HMS Ocean, an amphibious assault ship (LPH) commissioned in 1998, and served as the Royal Navy's helicopter carrier and fleet flagship for twenty years.

Service

As HMS Ocean, the ship took part in operations off Sierra Leone (2000), the Iraq War (2003), the NATO intervention in Libya (2011), and in the security operation for the London 2012 Olympic Games, when she was moored on the River Thames at Greenwich. She was decommissioned from Royal Navy service in March 2018.

Brazil purchased the ship for £84.6 million. Renamed Atlântico, she was commissioned into the Brazilian Navy on 29 June 2018 at Devonport and arrived in Rio de Janeiro in August 2018, replacing the aircraft carrier São Paulo as the fleet's capital ship. Her designation was changed from PHM (multipurpose helicopter carrier) to NAM in 2020.

Characteristics

The ship can operate up to 18 helicopters from her flight deck and hangar, embark up to 800 marines with vehicles, and carry landing craft in her stern dock. In Brazilian service she operates Helibras/Sikorsky S-70B, Super Lynx and other rotary-wing aircraft, and serves in amphibious, humanitarian and disaster-relief roles.
