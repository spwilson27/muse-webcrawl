Divergent is a series of young adult science fiction novels by American author Veronica Roth set in a post-apocalyptic dystopian Chicago. The trilogy consists of Divergent (2011), Insurgent (2012) and Allegiant (2013), supplemented by the short-story collection Four: A Divergent Collection (2014), told from the perspective of Tobias "Four" Eaton.

Setting and plot

In the society of the novels, the population is divided into five factions dedicated to particular virtues: Abnegation (the selfless), Amity (the peaceful), Candor (the honest), Dauntless (the brave) and Erudite (the intelligent). At sixteen, every citizen chooses a faction, guided by an aptitude test. Beatrice "Tris" Prior discovers she is Divergent — suited to more than one faction — a trait suppressed by the authorities. The series follows her as tensions between the factions erupt into conflict and the truth about her society's origins is revealed.

Reception and adaptations

The books were commercial successes and were compared with The Hunger Games series. A film series, The Divergent Series, was released by Summit Entertainment beginning in 2014, starring Shailene Woodley as Tris and Theo James as Four; the first three films adapted the first two novels and part of the third, but the planned concluding film was never completed theatrically.
