TNF inhibitor

A TNF inhibitor is a pharmaceutical drug that suppresses the physiologic response to tumor necrosis factor (TNF), a cytokine involved in inflammation and immune system regulation.

Uses

TNF inhibitors are used to treat autoimmune and inflammatory conditions including rheumatoid arthritis, Crohn's disease, ulcerative colitis, psoriasis, psoriatic arthritis, and ankylosing spondylitis.

Examples

Major agents include infliximab, adalimumab, etanercept, certolizumab pegol, and golimumab. Because they suppress the immune system, they carry risks of serious infection, including reactivation of tuberculosis.
