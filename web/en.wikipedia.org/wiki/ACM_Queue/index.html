ACM Queue

ACM Queue (stylised acmqueue) is a magazine published by the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) aimed at practising software engineers. Launched in 2003, it publishes articles, interviews and case studies written by practitioners about the challenges of building large-scale, real-world software systems.

Content

The magazine is known for its in-depth technical features and for republishing selected articles in Communications of the ACM. Recurring topics include distributed systems, performance engineering, and post-mortems of major production incidents. Its editorial board and contributors include engineers from companies such as Google and Netflix.

Relationship to Communications of the ACM

Since 2010 a curated selection of Queue articles has appeared in Communications of the ACM under the "Queue" heading. See also Association for Computing Machinery.
