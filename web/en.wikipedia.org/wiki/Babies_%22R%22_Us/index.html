Babies "R" Us

Babies "R" Us was an American retail chain specialising in products for infants and young children, including furniture, clothing, toys, feeding and care items. It was a sister brand of Toys "R" Us.

History

The chain was founded in April 1996, expanding from the company's earlier Kids "R" Us clothing format. At its peak it operated several hundred stores in the United States, often as side-by-side or combined locations with Toys "R" Us, and offered baby registries as a major service.

Closure and revival

Following the bankruptcy of Toys "R" Us in September 2017, all US Babies "R" Us stores closed in 2018. International operations continued under licensees in some markets. In 2023 the brand returned to the United States with a flagship store at the American Dream mall in New Jersey, under the brand's new owner WHP Global, in partnership with Kohl's for shop-in-shop locations announced subsequently.
