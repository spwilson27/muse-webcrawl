Colombia

Colombia, officially the Republic of Colombia, is a country in South America with coastlines on both the Pacific Ocean and the Caribbean Sea — the only South American country with both. It borders Panama, Venezuela, Brazil, Ecuador and Peru. Its population is about 52 million and its capital is Bogotá, a high-altitude metropolis of more than 8 million.

Colombia is one of the world's most biodiverse countries, spanning Amazon rainforest, Andes ranges (split into three cordilleras), Caribbean and Pacific coasts, and the coffee region. It is the world's top source of emeralds and a leading coffee producer. A half-century internal conflict with the FARC guerrillas killed over 220,000 people and displaced millions; a peace accord was signed in 2016, though violence by other armed groups continues. Cartagena's walled colonial city is a UNESCO site. Famous Colombians include Gabriel García Márquez and Shakira.
