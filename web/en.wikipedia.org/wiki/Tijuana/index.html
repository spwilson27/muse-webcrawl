Tijuana

Tijuana is the largest city in Baja California and the second-most populous city in Mexico, with about 1.9 million residents, forming a binational metropolis with San Diego, California. The San Ysidro Port of Entry between Tijuana and San Diego is among the busiest land border crossings in the world.

Founded in 1889, Tijuana grew through US Prohibition tourism, then maquiladora manufacturing, and is now a hub of medical tourism, aerospace and electronics. Avenida Revolución, the Tijuana Cultural Center (CECUT, 'La Bola'), Caesar salad (invented here in the 1920s) and lucha libre define its culture. Tijuana is also a principal arrival point for migrants and deportees at the US border, and was scarred by cartel violence in the late 2000s. Its airport uniquely straddles the border via the Cross Border Xpress bridge.
