Canonical (company)

Canonical Ltd. is a British software company founded in 2004 by South African entrepreneur Mark Shuttleworth. It is best known as the developer and commercial sponsor of the Ubuntu Linux distribution.

Products and services

Canonical develops Ubuntu and related technologies including the Snap package format and Snap Store, the Juju orchestration tool, MAAS for bare-metal provisioning, LXD container hypervisor, and the Mir display server. It sells enterprise support (Ubuntu Pro), managed services and consulting for cloud, Kubernetes and OpenStack deployments.

History

Canonical was founded as M R Shuttleworth's vehicle to support Ubuntu's development, with early offices on the Isle of Man and later headquarters in London. The company has backed projects such as Launchpad, Bazaar, Upstart and Unity, some of which were later discontinued. Canonical remains privately held and employs staff distributed across many countries.

Business model

Ubuntu itself is free and open-source software; Canonical earns revenue from support subscriptions, services, and partnerships with hardware makers and cloud providers.
