Souk

A souk (Arabic: سوق, also souq) is a marketplace or commercial quarter in Western Asian and North African cities. Traditionally an open-air market held weekly on a designated day, the term now covers permanent covered markets in historic medinas.

Souk
Type: Marketplace
Region: Middle East, North Africa
Related: Bazaar, Medina, Caravanserai

History

Souks arose along trade routes where caravans unloaded goods. Many were organised by trade — gold souks, spice souks, textile souks — often near the main mosque. In cities such as Aleppo, Damascus, Marrakech and Fez, covered souks formed the economic heart of the city.

Organisation

Stalls are grouped by product, with workshops behind shops. Bargaining is customary. Souks are often located within or near a kasbah or medina walls.

Notable souks

The Al-Madina Souq in Aleppo was among the largest covered historic markets; Khan el-Khalili in Cairo, the Grand Bazaar in Istanbul (technically a bazaar) and the souks of Marrakech remain major tourist and commercial sites.
