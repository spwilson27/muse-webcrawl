List of Game of the Year awards

Game of the Year (GotY) is a title awarded annually by various publications, shows and organisations to the video game they judge the best of that year. Dozens of outlets bestow such awards; the most prominent include The Game Awards, the Golden Joystick Awards, the BAFTA Games Awards, the D.I.C.E. Awards and the Game Developers Choice Awards, alongside major magazines and websites.

The Game Awards ceremony, the most widely watched source of Game of the Year honours.

Other major awards

The British Academy Games Award for Best Game and the D.I.C.E. Award for Game of the Year frequently agree with The Game Awards but sometimes diverge: BAFTA honoured Vampire Survivors for 2022 while most outlets chose Elden Ring. Tracking sites aggregate hundreds of critics' and readers' picks each year; titles such as The Witcher 3, Breath of the Wild, Elden Ring and Baldur's Gate 3 rank among the most-awarded games in history.
