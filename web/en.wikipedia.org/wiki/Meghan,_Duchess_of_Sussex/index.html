Meghan, Duchess of Sussex (born Rachel Meghan Markle; 4 August 1981) is an American member of the British royal family and a former actress. She is married to Prince Harry, Duke of Sussex, the younger son of King Charles III. Before her marriage she was best known for playing Rachel Zane in the American legal drama series Suits (2011–2018).

Early life and education

Markle was born and raised in Los Angeles, California, the daughter of Doria Ragland, a social worker and yoga instructor, and Thomas Markle, a retired television lighting director. She attended Hollywood Schoolhouse and the Immaculate Heart High School before studying at Northwestern University in Illinois, where she graduated in 2003 with a double major in theatre and international studies. She also interned at the U.S. embassy in Buenos Aires.

Acting career

Markle worked as a freelance calligrapher while auditioning for roles, appearing in small parts in television series such as General Hospital, Fringe and CSI: NY and in films including Remember Me (2010) and Horrible Bosses (2011). Her breakthrough came in 2011 when she was cast as paralegal-turned-lawyer Rachel Zane in Suits, a role she played for seven seasons. From 2014 to 2017 she ran the lifestyle blog The Tig.

Marriage and royal life

Markle began a relationship with Prince Harry in 2016; their engagement was announced in November 2017 and they married on 19 May 2018 at St George's Chapel, Windsor Castle, whereupon she became Duchess of Sussex. Their son Archie was born in May 2019. She undertook royal duties and patronages, including serving as vice-president of the Queen's Commonwealth Trust, and published the children's book The Bench (2021).

Withdrawal from royal duties

In January 2020 the couple announced their intention to step back as senior working royals, dividing their time between the United Kingdom and North America; the arrangement, completed in early 2020, became known as "Megxit". They settled in Montecito, California, founded the Archewell organisation, and signed production deals including the Netflix documentary series Harry & Meghan (2022) and the Spotify podcast Archetypes (2022). Their daughter Lilibet was born in June 2021. In March 2021 a televised interview with Oprah Winfrey drew worldwide attention to the couple's accounts of their treatment within the royal household.
