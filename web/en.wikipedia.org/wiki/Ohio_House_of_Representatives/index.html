The Ohio House of Representatives is the lower house of the Ohio General Assembly, the state legislature of the U.S. state of Ohio; the other house is the Ohio Senate. The House convenes at the Ohio Statehouse in Columbus, Ohio.

The House consists of 99 members elected from single-member districts across the state, each representing a district of approximately equal population. Members serve two-year terms and, under term limits adopted in 1992, may serve no more than four consecutive terms (eight years). The House is led by the Speaker, elected by the members, and its presiding officers and committees control the flow of legislation, including appropriations bills, which must originate in the House under the state constitution for revenue measures.

History

The first General Assembly met in Chillicothe in 1803 following Ohio's statehood. The modern apportionment of 99 House districts, each nested within 33 Senate districts, dates from a 1966 constitutional amendment effective with the 1968 elections.
