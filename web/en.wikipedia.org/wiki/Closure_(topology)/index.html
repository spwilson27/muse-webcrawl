Closure (topology)

FieldTopology
Notationcl(A) or Ā

In topology, the closure of a subset A of a topological space is the smallest closed set containing A. It consists of A together with all of its limit points, and is often denoted cl(A) or Ā.

Definitions

Equivalently, the closure of A is the set of points x such that every open neighbourhood of x intersects A, or the intersection of all closed sets containing A. A set is closed exactly when it equals its closure, and dense when its closure is the whole space.

Examples

In the real line, the closure of the open interval (0, 1) is the closed interval [0, 1], and the closure of the rational numbers is the set of all real numbers, since the rationals are dense in the reals.
