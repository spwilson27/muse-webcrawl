Systems ecology

Systems ecology is an interdisciplinary field of ecology that takes a holistic approach to the study of ecological systems, especially ecosystems. It applies concepts from systems theory — feedback, energy flow, and network analysis — to the interactions among organisms and their environment.

History

The field is closely associated with Howard T. Odum and his brother Eugene Odum, whose work on energy systems language and ecosystem energetics in the mid-twentieth century laid its foundations. Howard Odum introduced the "energy circuit language" (energese) for diagramming energy and material flows, and developed concepts such as emergy and maximum power.

Concepts

Systems ecologists model ecosystems as networks of producers, consumers, storages, and flows, and use simulation to explore questions of sustainability, nutrient cycling, and ecosystem services. Related areas include ecological modelling, ecological engineering, and ecological economics.
