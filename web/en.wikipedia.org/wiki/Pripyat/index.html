Pripyat (Ukrainian: При́п'ять) is a ghost city in northern Ukraine, near the border with Belarus. It was founded in 1970 to house the workers of the nearby Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant and was evacuated on 27 April 1986, the day after the Chernobyl disaster.

History

Pripyat was one of the Soviet Union's model "atom cities" (atomograds), built with broad avenues, a cultural palace, hotels, and an amusement park whose Ferris wheel became an emblem of the disaster. Its population of nearly 50,000 was young, with an average age of about 26.

Evacuation and abandonment

Residents were told the evacuation would last three days; it became permanent. The city lies inside the Chernobyl Exclusion Zone. Buildings have decayed and been colonised by forest, and the site is now visited by controlled tours. Wildlife, including wolves and reintroduced Przewalski's horses, thrives in the surrounding zone.
