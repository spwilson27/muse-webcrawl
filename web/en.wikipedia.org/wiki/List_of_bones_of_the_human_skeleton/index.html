List of bones of the human skeleton

The human skeleton of an adult consists of 206 bones. Infants are born with around 270–300 bones, many of which fuse during growth. The skeleton is divided into the axial skeleton (80 bones) and the appendicular skeleton (126 bones).

Axial skeleton (80 bones)

Skull (22 bones)

  • Cranial (8): frontal, parietal (2), temporal (2), occipital, sphenoid, ethmoid
  • Facial (14): maxilla (2), zygomatic (2), palatine (2), nasal (2), lacrimal (2), inferior nasal conchae (2), mandible, vomer

Auditory ossicles (6)

  • Malleus (2), incus (2), stapes (2) — the three smallest bones, in the middle ears

Hyoid (1)

  • Hyoid bone — supports the tongue; the only bone not articulating with another

Vertebral column (26)

  • Cervical vertebrae (7, C1–C7: atlas, axis, …)
  • Thoracic vertebrae (12, T1–T12)
  • Lumbar vertebrae (5, L1–L5)
  • Sacrum (1, fused from 5)
  • Coccyx (1, fused from 4)

Thoracic cage (25)

  • Ribs (24, in 12 pairs: 7 true, 3 false, 2 floating per side)
  • Sternum (1, comprising manubrium, body, xiphoid process)

Appendicular skeleton (126 bones)

Pectoral girdle (4)

  • Clavicle (2), scapula (2)

Upper limbs (60)

  • Arm: humerus (2)
  • Forearm: radius (2), ulna (2)
  • Wrist (carpals, 16): scaphoid, lunate, triquetrum, pisiform, trapezium, trapezoid, capitate, hamate (each side)
  • Hand (metacarpals, 10)
  • Fingers (phalanges, 28): proximal, middle, distal (thumb has 2 per hand)

Pelvic girdle (2)

  • Hip bones (2), each formed of fused ilium, ischium, and pubis

Lower limbs (60)

  • Thigh: femur (2) — the longest and strongest bone
  • Kneecap: patella (2)
  • Leg: tibia (2), fibula (2)
  • Ankle (tarsals, 14): talus, calcaneus, navicular, cuboid, medial/intermediate/lateral cuneiforms (each side)
  • Foot (metatarsals, 10)
  • Toes (phalanges, 28)
