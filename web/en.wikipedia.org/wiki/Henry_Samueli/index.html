Henry Samueli (born September 20, 1954) is an American businessman, engineer, and philanthropist. He is a co-founder of the semiconductor company Broadcom and its longtime chairman and chief technical officer. He owns the Anaheim Ducks of the National Hockey League and, with his wife Susan, runs one of the largest philanthropic foundations in Orange County, California.

Early life and education

Samueli was born in Buffalo, New York, to Polish Jewish immigrant parents who survived the Holocaust and ran a small liquor store in Los Angeles. He built his first radio from a kit as a teenager and studied electrical engineering at the University of California, Los Angeles, earning his bachelor's (1975), master's (1976) and PhD (1980) there. He later became a professor in UCLA's electrical engineering department, whose engineering school now bears his name.

Broadcom

In 1991 Samueli and his former PhD student Henry Nicholas left TRW and founded Broadcom in the garage of Nicholas's Redondo Beach home. Samueli served as the company's chief technical officer and guided its work in digital signal processing for broadband communications — cable modems, set-top boxes, Ethernet and Wi-Fi chips — growing Broadcom into one of the world's largest semiconductor companies. In 2016 Broadcom was acquired by Avago Technologies in a $37 billion deal; Samueli remained chairman of the board of the combined company, Broadcom Inc.

In 2008 Samueli pleaded guilty to a federal charge of making a false statement to the SEC in connection with the stock-options backdating investigation at Broadcom; the charge was dismissed by the judge in 2009.

Sports and philanthropy

In 2005 Samueli bought the Mighty Ducks of Anaheim (renamed the Anaheim Ducks) from the Walt Disney Company, and in 2007 the team won the Stanley Cup — the first California team to do so. He also owns the Honda Center arena's operating company.

Through the Samueli Foundation, Henry and Susan Samueli have given hundreds of millions of dollars to education, health and Jewish causes, including major gifts to UCLA, UC Irvine (whose Samueli School of Engineering and Samueli Institute bear the family name), and the Samueli Academy public charter school in Santa Ana.
