Megurine Luka

Megurine Luka (巡音ルカ) is a humanoid persona voiced by a singing synthesizer application developed by Crypton Future Media. She is the third release in Crypton's Character Vocal Series and was released on 30 January 2009 for the Vocaloid 2 engine. Her voice was provided by the Japanese voice actress and singer Yū Asakawa. Luka was the first Vocaloid designed to sing in both Japanese and English, being marketed as a bilingual voicebank.

Concept and design

Luka was designed to evoke a cool, moody adult vocal, in contrast to the younger-sounding Hatsune Miku. Her character illustration was drawn by the artist Kei, who had designed the earlier Crypton characters. She is depicted as a 20-year-old with long pink hair, and her design incorporates elements such as a detached sleeve and asymmetrical costume details intended to reflect her bilingual nature.

Development

Crypton developed Luka's voicebank to handle both Japanese and English lyrics, which was unusual for Vocaloid products of the time. She later received updated releases, including an append-style expansion and a Vocaloid 4 version, "Megurine Luka V4X," which added features such as the E.V.E.C. (Enhanced Voice Expression Control) system for adjusting vocal expression.

Reception and cultural impact

Luka quickly became one of the most popular Vocaloid characters. Songs featuring her include "Just Be Friends" by Dixie Flatline, "Double Lariat" by Agoaniki, and "Luka Luka ★ Night Fever." A particular internet meme associates her with tuna (maguro) and the Tako Luka (octopus Luka) mascot derived from her hairstyle. Like other Crypton characters, she appears in the Hatsune Miku: Project DIVA rhythm game series and in live holographic concerts.
