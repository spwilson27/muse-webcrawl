Galgalatz (Hebrew: גלגלצ) is an Israeli radio station operated by the Israel Defense Forces' Galei Tzahal (Army Radio). It broadcasts mainly pop and rock music, together with traffic reports, and is one of the most listened-to stations in Israel. Its name is a portmanteau of Gal ("wave") and Galei Tzahal.

History

Galgalatz began broadcasting on 31 October 1993 as a station dedicated to road safety, funded partly by the National Road Safety Authority. Over time its format broadened toward a mainstream contemporary-hit playlist determined weekly by listener voting, while retaining frequent traffic updates. While the station broadcasts 24 hours a day, some of those hours are automated and not live.

Programming

The station's famous playlist is compiled each week and voted on in the "Safe Journey" and chart programmes; its annual and decade-end Hebrew and international song charts are major cultural events in Israel. Presenters, many of them IDF soldiers doing national service, combine music with news bulletins from Galei Tzahal and traffic reporting tailored to drivers.
