Figure skating at the 1968 Winter Olympics in Grenoble, France, consisted of three events: men's singles, ladies' singles and pair skating. The competition was held at the Palais des Sports. Skaters were judged on compulsory figures and free skating, with figures carrying heavy weight in the final standings.

Highlights

American Peggy Fleming won the ladies' title by a wide margin, the only gold medal won by the United States at the Grenoble Games, and helped revive American skating after the 1961 plane crash that killed the entire US team. Wolfgang Schwarz of Austria upset expectations to win the men's event, while the veteran Soviet pair Lyudmila Belousova and Oleg Protopopov successfully defended their Olympic title.
