Valiant-class submarine


Operators: Royal Navy
Built: 1960s
In commission: 1966–1994
Completed: 2 (Valiant, Warspite)

The Valiant class were a class of two nuclear-powered fleet submarines in service with the Royal Navy from the mid-1960s until the early 1990s. They were the first fully British nuclear attack submarines: HMS Dreadnought had used an American reactor, whereas the Valiants were powered by the British Rolls-Royce PWR1 pressurised water reactor.

Design

The class comprised HMS Valiant (launched 1963, commissioned 1966) and HMS Warspite (commissioned 1967). Displacing about 4,900 tonnes submerged, they were armed with six bow torpedo tubes and, later, Sub-Harpoon anti-ship missiles, and were notably quieter than their predecessors thanks to raft-mounted machinery.

Service

Both boats served through the Cold War on patrols in the Atlantic and Arctic. Warspite collided with a Soviet Echo II-class submarine in the Barents Sea in 1968. Valiant served in the 1982 Falklands War. The class was retired in the 1990s as the Swiftsure and Trafalgar classes took over.
