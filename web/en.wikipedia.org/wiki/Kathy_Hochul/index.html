Kathy Hochul

Kathleen Courtney Hochul (née Courtney; born August 27, 1958) is an American politician and lawyer who has served since August 24, 2021, as the 57th governor of New York. A member of the Democratic Party, she is the first woman to serve as governor of New York. Hochul previously served as lieutenant governor under Andrew Cuomo, as a US representative for New York's 26th congressional district, and as Erie County clerk.

Early life and career

Hochul was born in Buffalo to an Irish Catholic family and grew up in Hamburg, New York. She studied political science at Syracuse University and law at the Columbus School of Law of the Catholic University of America. She worked as an aide to Representative John LaFalce and Senator Daniel Patrick Moynihan, then served on the Hamburg town board and as Erie County deputy clerk before being appointed county clerk in 2007 and winning election in her own right.

US House of Representatives

In a May 2011 special election, Hochul won the heavily Republican 26th district, defeating Jane Corwin in a race widely read as a referendum on Republican proposals to convert Medicare to a voucher system. Redistricting placed her in an unfavourable district, and she lost the 2012 general election to Chris Collins.

Lieutenant governor and governorship

Andrew Cuomo selected Hochul as his running mate in 2014; the ticket was re-elected in 2018. When Cuomo resigned amid sexual-harassment allegations in August 2021, Hochul succeeded him, becoming New York's first female governor. She won a full term in November 2022, defeating Republican Lee Zeldin by about six points — the closest New York gubernatorial race in decades.

As governor she has emphasized public safety legislation (including a concealed-carry law enacted after Bruen, parts later modified by courts), abortion-rights protections, housing production (though her ambitious 2023 housing plan was scaled back by the legislature), congestion pricing in Manhattan (which she paused in June 2024 before allowing a reduced-toll version to proceed in 2025), and economic development deals including a state package for a new Buffalo Bills stadium. Her administration has also dealt with the arrival of large numbers of asylum seekers in New York City.
