St Martin's Lane

St Martin's Lane is a street in the City of Westminster, London, running from St Martin-in-the-Fields church and Trafalgar Square northward toward Covent Garden and Seven Dials. It lies at the heart of London's Theatreland.

Description

The street takes its name from the parish church of St Martin-in-the-Fields at its southern end. It is lined with theatres, including the London Coliseum — home of English National Opera — the Duke of York's Theatre, and the Noël Coward Theatre. In the eighteenth century the lane was a centre for artists and craftsmen: the St Martin's Lane Academy, an early art school attended by William Hogarth, stood here and helped shape British art. Today the street is known for its cafés, hotels, and theatre ticket trade.
