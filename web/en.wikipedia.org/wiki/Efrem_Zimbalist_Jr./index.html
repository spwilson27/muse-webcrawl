Efrem Zimbalist Jr.

Efrem Zimbalist Jr. (November 30, 1918 – May 2, 2014) was an American actor best known for his starring roles in the television series 77 Sunset Strip (1958–1964) and The F.B.I. (1965–1974). He was also known as a recurring character in Maverick and as the voice of Alfred Pennyworth in Batman: The Animated Series and other animated productions.

Early life

Zimbalist was born in New York City, the son of the Russian-born concert violinist Efrem Zimbalist and the Romanian-born soprano Alma Gluck. He was educated at St. Paul's School in Concord, New Hampshire, and attended Yale University before serving in the United States Army during World War II, in which he was wounded and awarded the Purple Heart. After the war he studied acting at the Neighborhood Playhouse and worked in theatre and early television.

Career

Zimbalist gained fame playing private detective Stu Bailey in 77 Sunset Strip, one of the most popular American television series of its era. He followed this with a nine-season run as Inspector Lewis Erskine in The F.B.I., a role endorsed by FBI director J. Edgar Hoover. His film appearances included Band of Angels (1957), Wait Until Dark (1967), and Airport 1975 (1974). In the 1990s he voiced Alfred Pennyworth in the DC animated universe, and Doctor Octopus in Spider-Man: The Animated Series.

Personal life and death

Zimbalist was known for his Christian faith and his narration of television versions of the Bible. He died of natural causes at his ranch in Solvang, California, on May 2, 2014, aged 95. His daughter, Stephanie Zimbalist, is an actress best known for the series Remington Steele.
