Mark Liang

Franklin Mark Liang is an American finance academic and researcher. He is known for his work in empirical asset pricing, volatility forecasting and the econometrics of financial markets.

Career

Liang has published research on the behaviour of asset returns, risk measurement and forecasting models used by institutional investors. His work has examined the statistical properties of high-frequency financial data and the performance of quantitative trading strategies.

Research

Liang's research interests include time-series econometrics, market microstructure and the application of statistical learning methods to portfolio management. He has contributed to debates on the predictability of stock returns and the estimation of volatility in turbulent market conditions.
