Big Banana

LocationCoffs Harbour, New South Wales, Australia
Opened1964
Height13 m long structure

The Big Banana is a tourist attraction and amusement park in Coffs Harbour, New South Wales, Australia. Its centrepiece is a giant banana-shaped structure, built in 1964, which is credited with starting Australia's "big things" tradition of oversized roadside attractions.

History

American John Landi built the Big Banana to draw motorists to his roadside banana stall on the Pacific Highway. Visitors could walk through the banana and learn about the local banana industry. The site later expanded into a fun park with a toboggan ride, water park, ice rink and other attractions.

Legacy

The Big Banana inspired more than 150 "big things" across Australia, from the Big Pineapple in Queensland to the Big Merino in Goulburn.
