Saint-Martin-de-Ré is a commune in the Charente-Maritime department in southwestern France, on the Île de Ré. It is the island's historic capital and principal port.

Fortifications

The town is enclosed by extensive star fortifications designed by Vauban in the 1680s, with a citadel that later served as a prison depot for convicts bound for French Guiana and New Caledonia — most famously Captain Alfred Dreyfus, held there in 1895 before his deportation to Devil's Island. The fortifications of Vauban were inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 2008.

Today

The harbour, lined with cafés and fish restaurants, is a popular tourist destination, and the town is a centre for sailing and oyster farming.
