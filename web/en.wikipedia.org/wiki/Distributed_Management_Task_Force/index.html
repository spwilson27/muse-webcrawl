The Distributed Management Task Force (DMTF) is a computer industry standards organization that creates open manageability standards spanning diverse emerging and traditional IT infrastructures including cloud, virtualization, network, servers and storage. Member companies and organizations represent both IT product manufacturers and academic and government bodies.

Standards

DMTF standards include the Common Information Model (CIM), an extensible object-oriented schema for describing managed elements; Web-Based Enterprise Management (WBEM); the Desktop and mobile Architecture for System Hardware (DASH); the Systems Management Architecture for Server Hardware (SMASH); and the Redfish standard for scalable management of servers and data-center infrastructure. Its Alert Standard Format (ASF) and the SMBIOS specification for system firmware tables are also widely implemented.

History

Founded in 1992 as the Desktop Management Task Force, the organization renamed itself as its remit grew beyond desktops. Its specifications underpin the management interfaces built into most enterprise servers, PCs, and virtualization platforms.
