The HEXACO model of personality structure is a six-dimensional model of human personality that was created by Ashton and Lee and explained in their book The H Factor of Personality (2012). The six factors, or domains, are:

  • Honesty-Humility (H)
  • Emotionality (E)
  • Extraversion (X)
  • Agreeableness (A)
  • Conscientiousness (C)
  • Openness to Experience (O)

The model emerged from lexical studies of personality-descriptive adjectives conducted in numerous languages, which repeatedly recovered a sixth factor — Honesty-Humility — not separate in the five-factor ("Big Five") model. Each HEXACO domain is measured by the HEXACO Personality Inventory and is subdivided into four facets, plus an interstitial facet of altruism.

The six dimensions

Honesty-Humility

Persons high in Honesty-Humility are sincere, fair, modest and uninterested in manipulating others for personal gain; those low in the trait tend to be deceitful, greedy and status-seeking. This dimension predicts workplace integrity, low delinquency, and the absence of the "Dark Triad" traits (narcissism, Machiavellianism and psychopathy, which correlate strongly and negatively with H).

Emotionality

High scorers experience fear, anxiety, dependence and sentimentality. Unlike Big Five Neuroticism, HEXACO Emotionality excludes anger (which loads on Agreeableness); it shows a substantial sex difference, with women scoring higher on average.

Extraversion, Agreeableness, Conscientiousness and Openness

These four domains broadly resemble their Big Five counterparts, though HEXACO Agreeableness incorporates (lack of) anger and patience, and Extraversion emphasises social self-esteem and liveliness. Conscientiousness covers organisation, diligence, perfectionism and prudence; Openness covers aesthetic appreciation, inquisitiveness, creativity and unconventionality.

Applications

The HEXACO model has been applied in industrial and organisational psychology (predicting job performance, integrity and counterproductive work behaviour), criminology, consumer research, and the study of ethical and political attitudes. Honesty-Humility in particular adds predictive power beyond the Big Five for ethically relevant criteria.
