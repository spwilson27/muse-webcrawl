Magic Wand (vibrator)

The Magic Wand, formerly known as the Hitachi Magic Wand, is an AC-powered wand vibrator that was originally manufactured by Hitachi. It was introduced as a body massager in the United States in 1968, and it became widely known for its use as a sex toy. It consists of a handle about 30 cm (12 in) long with a soft rubber spherical head, and provides two speeds of vibration.

History

Hitachi registered the trademark and marketed the device as a personal massager for muscles. During the 1970s, sex educator Betty Dodson popularized it as a vibrator and masturbation aid for women in her workshops, and it acquired an iconic status in feminist and sex-positive culture. Sales surged after appearances in popular media, including a 2002 episode of Sex and the City in which it was recommended in a shop.

In 2013, Hitachi, concerned about the association of its brand with a sex product, removed its name from the device, which was thereafter marketed simply as the Magic Wand (and later the Magic Wand Original) by distributor Vibratex. A rechargeable cordless version was introduced in 2015.

Medical use and research

Clinicians and researchers have studied and used the device for provoking orgasm in women with anorgasmia or spinal cord injury, and it has been used in studies of vibratory stimulation more broadly, including in the management of chronic pelvic pain. The Magic Wand has received numerous awards from the adult industry and consumer publications.
