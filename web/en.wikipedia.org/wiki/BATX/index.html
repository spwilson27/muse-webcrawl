BATX

BATX is an acronym used for the four largest Chinese technology companies: Baidu, Alibaba, Tencent and Xiaomi. The term parallels the American groupings FAANG / Big Tech and is used in business and financial journalism to discuss China's internet sector.

Members

Baidu dominates Chinese web search and develops artificial intelligence and autonomous driving. Alibaba Group operates e-commerce platforms Taobao and Tmall, Alibaba Cloud and Ant Group affiliates. Tencent operates WeChat, QQ and a major video games business. Xiaomi is a consumer electronics maker known for smartphones, smart home devices and electric vehicles.

Usage and criticism

Analysts use BATX to frame competition, regulation and investment in Chinese tech. The grouping is sometimes criticised as outdated as ByteDance, Meituan, JD.com and Pinduoduo's parent PDD Holdings grew to comparable scale, leading to alternative lists. The companies were significantly affected by China's 2020–2022 regulatory crackdown on platform monopolies, fintech and data practices.
