Payment system

A payment system is any system used to settle financial transactions by transferring monetary value. It includes the institutions, instruments, rules, procedures and technical means that make payments between parties possible.

Types

Modern payment systems range from cash and cheques to card networks, automated clearing houses (ACH), real-time gross settlement (RTGS) systems operated by central banks — such as Fedwire in the United States — and newer instant-payment and mobile-money platforms.

Clearing and settlement

Payments typically pass through authorisation, clearing (the exchange of payment information and calculation of obligations) and settlement (the final transfer of funds). Large-value systems settle in central bank money to minimise systemic risk, which is why systemically important payment systems are overseen by central banks and international standards such as the CPMI-IOSCO Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures.
