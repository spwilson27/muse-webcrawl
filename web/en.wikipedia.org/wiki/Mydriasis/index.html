Mydriasis

Mydriasis is the dilation of the pupil, usually having a non-physiological cause, or sometimes a physiological pupillary response. Non-physiological causes include disease, trauma, or the use of certain types of drug.

Normally, as part of the pupillary light reflex, the pupil dilates in the dark and constricts in the light to protect the retina. A mydriatic is an agent that induces dilation of the pupil, such as tropicamide used in eye examinations.

Causes

Causes include anticholinergic and sympathomimetic drugs, head injury, increased oxytocin, and certain neurological conditions. The opposite condition, constriction of the pupil, is miosis.
