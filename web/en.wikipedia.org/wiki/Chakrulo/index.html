Chakrulo

Chakrulo (Georgian: ჩაკრულო, literally "woven together" or "intertwined") is a traditional Georgian polyphonic song from the Kakheti region of eastern Georgia. It is a ceremonial table song (supra) performed by male voices and is considered one of the masterpieces of Georgian polyphonic singing. In 2001, UNESCO proclaimed Georgian polyphonic singing, with Chakrulo among its foremost examples, a Masterpiece of the Oral and Intangible Heritage of Humanity; it was inscribed on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2008.

Musical structure

Chakrulo is sung in three parts. Two high solo voices (mtkmeli and modzakhili) weave elaborate, ornamented melodic lines — including the yodel-like falsetto cry known as krimanchuli — over a slow-moving choral bass (bani) that sustains a drone or moves in measured steps. The solo lines interlace with each other and with the choir's chordal responses in sharp, striking dissonances (seconds, fourths, and fifths) that resolve to unisons or octaves, a signature of the Kakhetian table-song style. The song is characteristically performed in a slow, majestic tempo over the course of a feast.

Text and occasion

The lyrics of Chakrulo are philosophical and ceremonial, greeting guests, honouring the village, and reflecting on life, labour, and the bond between host and guest. Table songs belong to the Georgian supra (feast) tradition, led by a toastmaster (tamada), at which songs and toasts proceed in a prescribed, reverent order.

Voyager Golden Record

Chakrulo achieved worldwide renown when a recording of it was selected for the Voyager Golden Record, the phonograph record of sounds and music of Earth carried aboard NASA's Voyager 1 and Voyager 2 spacecraft, launched in 1977. It appears in the record's "Sounds of Earth" music section as one of 27 musical selections representing the world's cultures — making the song, in a literal sense, the first Georgian music sent to the stars. The inclusion is a source of great national pride in Georgia.
