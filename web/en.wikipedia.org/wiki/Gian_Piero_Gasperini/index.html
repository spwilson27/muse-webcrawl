Gian Piero Gasperini (born 26 January 1958) is an Italian football manager and former player. He is best known for his long tenure at Atalanta, whom he transformed from a mid-table Serie A club into regular Champions League participants and winners of the 2023–24 UEFA Europa League, the club's first major European trophy.

Playing and early coaching career

A product of the Juventus youth system, Gasperini played as a midfielder mainly for Pescara, Palermo and Salernitana. He began coaching in Juventus's youth sector, winning the Torneo di Viareggio, then took Crotone into Serie B before two spells at Genoa, where his attacking 3-4-3 side finished as high as fifth in Serie A. A brief, unsuccessful stint at Inter Milan in 2011 and a turbulent period at Palermo followed.

Atalanta

Appointed by Atalanta in 2016, Gasperini built one of Europe's most admired teams on modest resources, favouring intense man-oriented pressing, a back three and fluid attacking play. Atalanta finished fourth in his first season and later recorded three consecutive third-place finishes, reached the Champions League quarter-finals in 2020 and the Coppa Italia final three times. On 22 May 2024 his side beat the previously unbeaten Bayer Leverkusen 3–0 in Dublin to win the Europa League, ending a 61-year trophy drought for the Bergamo club. Gasperini has been named Serie A Coach of the Year multiple times.
