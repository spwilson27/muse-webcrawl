BNSF Railway is one of the largest freight railroad networks in North America, operating about 32,500 miles of track in 28 US states and three Canadian provinces. It was created in 1995 by the merger of the Burlington Northern Railroad and the Atchison, Topeka and Santa Fe Railway.

History

BNSF's predecessor lines include some of the oldest railroads in the western United States, among them the Great Northern and Northern Pacific. The combined company's parent, Burlington Northern Santa Fe Corporation, was acquired by Warren Buffett's Berkshire Hathaway in 2010 in a deal valued at about 4 billion — Berkshire's largest acquisition at the time.

Operations

Headquartered in Fort Worth, Texas, BNSF hauls intermodal containers, coal, agricultural products, automobiles and industrial goods, and hosts Amtrak passenger services on parts of its network.
