Rahul Gandhi (born 19 June 1970) is an Indian politician and a leading figure of the Indian National Congress. A member of the Nehru–Gandhi family, he is the son of former prime minister Rajiv Gandhi and Congress leader Sonia Gandhi, the grandson of Indira Gandhi, and the great-grandson of Jawaharlal Nehru. He has served as Leader of the Opposition in the Lok Sabha since 2024.

Political career

Gandhi entered politics in 2004, winning the Amethi constituency in Uttar Pradesh previously held by his parents. He became Congress vice-president in 2013 and party president in December 2017, leading the party into the 2019 general election, in which he lost Amethi but won from Wayanad in Kerala; he resigned the presidency after the party's defeat. He undertook the Bharat Jodo Yatra, a roughly 4,000 km march from Kanyakumari to Kashmir in 2022–23, followed by the Bharat Jodo Nyay Yatra in 2024. In the 2024 general election he won both Wayanad and Rae Bareli (retaining the latter) and, with Congress nearly doubling its seat count, became Leader of the Opposition.

In 2023 Gandhi was convicted of defamation over a 2019 campaign remark and disqualified from Parliament; the Supreme Court stayed the conviction later that year, restoring his membership. His career has been marked by debate over dynastic politics in India and by his campaigns centred on unemployment, social justice, and a caste census.
