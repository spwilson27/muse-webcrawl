Dream season

A dream season is a term used in sports to describe a season in which a team or individual athlete far exceeds expectations, often achieving an unexpectedly successful run of results, a championship, or an undefeated or near-undefeated record. The term is commonly applied retrospectively by journalists, fans and broadcasters to underdog teams that capture public imagination.

Usage

Examples frequently described as dream seasons include league title wins by heavy outsiders, cup runs by lower-division clubs, and breakthrough seasons by young players. In association football, Leicester City's 2015–16 Premier League title, won at pre-season odds of 5000–1, is one of the most widely cited dream seasons. The phrase is also used more loosely for any season in which a team achieves its best-ever results.
