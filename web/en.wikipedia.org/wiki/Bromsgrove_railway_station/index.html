Bromsgrove railway station

Bromsgrove
Location: Bromsgrove, Worcestershire, England
Managed by: West Midlands Railway
Line: Birmingham to Worcester via Bromsgrove; Cross-City line services

Bromsgrove railway station serves the town of Bromsgrove in Worcestershire, England. It stands at the foot of the Lickey Incline, the steep gradient between Bromsgrove and Barnt Green on the route from Birmingham to Worcester and the South West.

History

The original station opened in 1840 on the Birmingham and Gloucester Railway. Because of the Lickey Incline, Bromsgrove became associated with banking engines that assisted heavy trains up the gradient. The station was rebuilt and relocated slightly in the twenty-first century, with a new station opening in 2016 providing longer platforms, a footbridge with lifts, and expanded parking.

Services

Trains run north to Birmingham New Street, with many services continuing on the Cross-City line toward Lichfield, and south to Worcester, Hereford, Cheltenham Spa, and destinations in the South West. The station is a gateway for passengers travelling between Worcestershire and the West Midlands conurbation.
