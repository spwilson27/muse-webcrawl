Radio

Radio
TechnologyElectromagnetic waves (radio waves)
PioneersMaxwell (theory), Hertz, Marconi (practical systems)
UsesBroadcasting, communications, radar, astronomy
[Image: A 1930s wooden-cabinet radio receiver glowing in a living room]

Radio is the technology of communicating using radio waves — electromagnetic radiation of longer wavelength than infrared light. Predicted by Maxwell's equations and demonstrated by Hertz (1880s), practical wireless telegraphy followed in the 1890s (Marconi), then voice broadcasting in the 1920s.

Uses

Radio carries broadcast entertainment and news, two-way communications, radar and navigation, and the faint signals of spacecraft — the Voyagers are heard by giant dishes using radio alone. Radio astronomy opened a new sky, discovering pulsars (see Neutron star). Podcasting and streaming are radio's internet-era heirs.
