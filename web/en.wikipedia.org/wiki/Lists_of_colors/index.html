Lists of colors

Wikipedia's colour lists are organized as "List of colors" articles, historically split alphabetically (for example, List of colors: A–F, G–M, N–Z) plus a compact single-page list. Each entry gives a colour's name, a swatch, and its hexadecimal and RGB values as used in web colours. The lists draw in part on standard references such as the ISCC–NBS system of colour names and the X11 colour names.
