"Uptown Funk" (stylised in all caps) is a song by British record producer Mark Ronson featuring American singer Bruno Mars. It was released on 10 November 2014 as the lead single from Ronson's fourth studio album, Uptown Special (2015). The song was written by Ronson, Mars, Philip Lawrence, and Jeff Bhasker, among others, with additional credits later granted to the Gap Band's writers over similarities to "Oops Up Side Your Head".

Composition

The track is a funk-pop and disco-pop homage to the Minneapolis sound of Prince and the funk of acts such as the Gap Band, Zapp, and Earth, Wind & Fire. It grew out of a jam session in Mars's Los Angeles studio and was refined over many months and dozens of takes in studios in Los Angeles, Toronto, London, and Memphis. Its arrangement features horn riffs, a deep funk bassline, vocoder touches, and Mars's playful call-and-response vocals, including the hook "Don't believe me, just watch".

Commercial performance and reception

"Uptown Funk" became one of the best-selling singles of all time, spending 14 consecutive weeks at number one on the US Billboard Hot 100 — among the longest runs in chart history — and topping charts in dozens of countries, including the United Kingdom, where it was the best-selling song of 2015. The official music video, filmed in Los Angeles, became one of the most-viewed videos on YouTube.

At the 58th Annual Grammy Awards in 2016, the song won Record of the Year and Best Pop Duo/Group Performance. Critics praised its exuberant recreation of early-1980s funk, and it was widely ranked among the best songs of 2014 and of the 2010s decade.

Copyright disputes

The song's success prompted several copyright claims and settlements, including credits for the writers of the Gap Band's "Oops Up Side Your Head" (1979) and, later, a share arising from similarities to the Sequence's "Funk You Up" (1979), reflecting the heavily referential nature of its composition.
