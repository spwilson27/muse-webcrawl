The Indian Institute of Management Bangalore (IIMB) is a public business school and Institute of National Importance located in Bengaluru, India. Established in 1973, it was the third IIM to be founded, after IIM Calcutta and IIM Ahmedabad.

Campus

The 100-acre campus on Bannerghatta Road was designed by Pritzker Prize-winning architect B. V. Doshi, and is known for its stone buildings, pergolas, and lush gardens.

Programmes

IIMB offers the two-year MBA (PGP), executive MBA programmes, a doctoral programme, and the NSRCEL entrepreneurship centre, one of India's best-known startup incubators.
