Charleston is the most populous city in the U.S. state of South Carolina, the county seat of Charleston County, and the principal city in the Charleston metropolitan area. The city lies just south of the geographical midpoint of South Carolina's coastline on Charleston Harbor, an inlet of the Atlantic Ocean formed by the confluence of the Ashley, Cooper, and Wando rivers. Charleston was founded in 1670 as Charles Town, in honor of King Charles II, at Albemarle Point on the west bank of the Ashley River (now Charles Towne Landing). In 1680 its site was moved to its present location on the peninsula, where it became the fifth-largest city in North America within ten years.

History

Charleston grew into a wealthy colonial port trading rice, indigo, and deerskins, and it was the principal entry point for enslaved Africans in mainland North America: historians estimate that roughly 40 percent of enslaved people brought to the colonies passed through its harbor, many through the quarantine station on Sullivan's Island. The city figured centrally in the American Revolution — the repulse of a British fleet at the Battle of Sullivan's Island in June 1776, and the siege and capture of Charleston in 1780 — and in the coming of the Civil War: the first shots were fired on Fort Sumter in Charleston Harbor on April 12, 1861.

The city endured a devastating earthquake in 1886 and, after relative economic decline, preserved its historic architecture through America's first historic-district zoning ordinance in 1931. In June 2015, a gunman murdered nine worshippers at Emanuel African Methodist Episcopal Church, an event that prompted the removal of the Confederate flag from the statehouse grounds.

Geography and culture

Known as "The Holy City" for its many church steeples and tradition of religious tolerance, Charleston is celebrated for its preserved antebellum mansions, Rainbow Row of pastel houses, cobblestone streets, and the Battery promenade. Its cuisine — Lowcountry dishes such as shrimp and grits and she-crab soup — and the annual Spoleto Festival USA make it a major tourist destination. Joint Base Charleston and the Port of Charleston, one of the busiest container ports on the East Coast, anchor the economy, though the low-lying city faces chronic tidal flooding.
