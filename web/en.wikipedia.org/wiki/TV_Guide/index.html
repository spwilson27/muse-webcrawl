TV Guide is an American digital media brand and former weekly magazine that provides television program listings and entertainment news. Founded by Walter Annenberg, the magazine's first national issue appeared on April 3, 1953, with Lucille Ball and her newborn son Desi Arnaz Jr. on the cover. At its peak in the 1970s, TV Guide was one of the most widely read magazines in the United States, with circulation approaching 20 million copies, and its digest size became iconic in American households.

History

Annenberg's Triangle Publications assembled TV Guide from several regional television listings magazines it had acquired. The national magazine published localized editions with listings tailored to each broadcast market. Its small digest format (roughly 5 by 7.5 inches) remained standard for more than five decades, and the magazine became known for its covers, feature journalism, and the annual Fall Preview issue.

The spread of cable television strained the listings format: as channel lineups ballooned, complete grid listings became impractical. In 2005 the magazine was relaunched in a larger format emphasizing features over comprehensive listings, and print frequency later declined from weekly to biweekly. The TV Guide brand and its digital properties passed through several owners; the magazine business was sold separately from the online listings business, and the brand continues online as a source of TV news, reviews, and streaming guidance.

Cultural significance

TV Guide shaped American television culture for decades. Its critics, including longtime reviewer Cleveland Amory, influenced viewing tastes, and its cover art is collected by museums — the magazine's archive of cover photographs was donated to and exhibited by cultural institutions. The phrase "TV Guide" became so generic for listings magazines that it is a frequently cited example in trademark discussions. The magazine also produced the TV Guide Awards and published influential rankings, such as its lists of the greatest television episodes and shows of all time.
