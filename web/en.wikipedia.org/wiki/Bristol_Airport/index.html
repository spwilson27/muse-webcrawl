Bristol Airport (IATA: BRS) is an international airport serving the city of Bristol and the West of England. It is located at Lulsgate Bottom in North Somerset, about 13 km (8 miles) south-west of Bristol city centre, on a plateau at 190 metres above sea level — one of the highest airports in England, which occasionally exposes it to fog and snow.

History

Bristol's first airport opened at Whitchurch in 1930. After wartime use, it became too small for modern aircraft, and operations moved in 1957 to the former RAF Lulsgate Bottom airfield. A new terminal opened in 2000, and traffic grew rapidly with low-cost airlines; easyJet made the airport one of its largest bases, alongside Ryanair, Jet2 and TUI. The airport handled around ten million passengers a year by the mid-2020s, ranking among the busiest in the United Kingdom.

Facilities and transport

The airport has a single 2,011-metre runway (09/27) and one main terminal. Plans to expand capacity to 12 million passengers annually were approved after a legal challenge in 2023. The airport is linked to Bristol Temple Meads station by the Airport Flyer express bus; a rail link has been proposed but not built.
