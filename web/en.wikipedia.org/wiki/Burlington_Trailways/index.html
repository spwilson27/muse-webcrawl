Burlington Trailways

Burlington Trailways is an intercity bus company based in West Burlington, Iowa, United States. It serves the US Midwest and West as part of the Trailways Transportation System, a network of independent bus companies that cooperate on through-routes, ticketing, and branding.

History

The company traces its origins to 1929, when it was organized as the Burlington Transportation Company, taking its name from the Chicago, Burlington and Quincy Railroad, whose subsidiary it initially was. Like several other railroad-affiliated bus lines of the era, it was created to operate bus routes that complemented and fed the parent railroad's passenger trains.

Following the divestiture of railroad-owned bus operations, the company became an independent carrier. It subsequently joined the Trailways cooperative association and adopted the Burlington Trailways name. Over the decades the company expanded through acquisitions of smaller regional carriers and route authorities, extending its network from the Chicago area westward across Iowa, Nebraska, Colorado, and beyond.

Operations

Burlington Trailways operates scheduled intercity service connecting cities and towns across the Midwest and Mountain West, including routes linking Chicago, Des Moines, Omaha, Lincoln, Denver, and points in Wyoming and Montana. It also provides charter services and participates in interline ticketing arrangements with other Trailways members and partner carriers, allowing passengers to travel across multiple operators on a single ticket.

The company has also operated as a contractor on state-supported rural intercity routes, helping maintain bus connections for smaller communities.
