Dudley George

Anthony Dudley George (17 March 1957 – 6 September 1995) was an Ojibwa protester from the Kettle and Stony Point First Nation who was shot and killed by Ontario Provincial Police (OPP) Acting Sergeant Kenneth Deane during the Ipperwash Crisis, a land dispute over Ipperwash Provincial Park in Ontario, Canada. He remains one of a small number of Indigenous protesters killed by Canadian police during a land claims action.

Background

The land around Ipperwash, including the provincial park and the adjacent Stony Point reserve, had been appropriated by the federal government in 1942 under the War Measures Act for a military camp, Camp Ipperwash, with a promise that it would be returned after the Second World War. Members of the Stony Point band, whose burial grounds lie within the park, occupied the camp in 1993 and, on 4 September 1995, occupied Ipperwash Provincial Park after it closed for the season, asserting their claim to the land.

The shooting

On the night of 6 September 1995, a heavily armed OPP riot unit confronted the occupiers near the park. In the confrontation, Deane fired on a car and a school bus leaving the park, fatally wounding Dudley George, who was unarmed. George was driven to hospital by family members and died shortly after midnight. Deane was convicted of criminal negligence causing death in 1997; he testified that he believed he had seen George with a rifle, a claim the other officers' evidence did not support.

Aftermath and inquiry

George's family and First Nations organizations campaigned for years for a public inquiry. The Ipperwash Inquiry, established by the Ontario government in 2003 under Commissioner Sidney Linden, reported in 2007, concluding that George's death resulted from a chain of failures including inadequate government handling of the land claim and culturally insensitive policing, and made 98 recommendations. The federal government returned the Camp Ipperwash land to the Kettle and Stony Point First Nation in 2016, and the provincial park land was also transferred, with a financial settlement.
