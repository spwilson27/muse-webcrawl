A Room with a View is a 1908 novel by English writer E. M. Forster, set in Italy and England, about a young woman in the restrained culture of Edwardian era England. The story is both a romance and a humorous critique of English society at the beginning of the 20th century.

Plot

Lucy Honeychurch, travelling in Florence with her chaperone Charlotte Bartlett, is offered a room with a view by the unconventional Mr Emerson and his son George. In Italy, George's free spirit challenges Lucy's upbringing; back in England she becomes engaged to the priggish Cecil Vyse but ultimately rejects him and accepts her love for George.

Themes

The novel contrasts the repressive, convention-bound world of English middle-class life (symbolised by rooms without views and medieval darkness) with the passion, honesty, and light of Italy and of the Emersons. It is among the most popular of Forster's novels and was ranked among the Modern Library's 100 best English-language novels of the 20th century.

Adaptations

The best-known adaptation is the 1985 Merchant Ivory film starring Helena Bonham Carter, Julian Sands, and Daniel Day-Lewis, which won three Academy Awards.
