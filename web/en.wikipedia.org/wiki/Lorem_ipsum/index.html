Lorem ipsum

Lorem ipsum is placeholder text commonly used in publishing, graphic design and web development to demonstrate the visual form of a document or typeface without relying on meaningful content. Its standard passage begins: "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit, sed do eiusmod tempor incididunt ut labore et dolore magna aliqua."

Origin

The text is a scrambled corruption of sections 1.10.32–33 of Cicero's De finibus bonorum et malorum (45 BC), a treatise on ethics discussing pleasure and pain. The phrase itself comes from "dolorem ipsum" ("pain itself") in the sentence "Neque porro quisquam est qui dolorem ipsum quia dolor sit amet, consectetur, adipisci velit…" — "There is no one who loves pain itself, who seeks after it and wants to have it, simply because it is pain." The classicist Richard McClintock is credited with identifying the source in the 1980s.

Use

Lorem ipsum has been used by typesetters since the 16th century and became ubiquitous with Letraset transfer sheets in the 1960s and desktop publishing software such as Aldus PageMaker in the 1980s. Because the text has a roughly normal distribution of letters and word lengths, it approximates the look of real copy while remaining unreadable enough not to distract the viewer. Variants and humorous generators ("greeking") abound, and modern CSS frameworks and editors can insert the passage with a single command.

Criticism

Some designers argue that dummy text encourages designs detached from content, and advocate drafting real content first; others defend lorem ipsum as a neutral tool that keeps clients focused on layout rather than wording.
