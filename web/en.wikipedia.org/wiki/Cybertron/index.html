Cybertron

Cybertron, the metallic homeworld of the Transformers.

Cybertron is a fictional planet in the Transformers franchise created by Hasbro. It is the homeworld of the Transformers — the sentient robot species that includes the heroic Autobots and the villainous Decepticons — depicted as a vast mechanical planet of metal cities, energon seas, and ancient machinery, often said to be the transformed body of the godlike being Primus.

Depiction

Across the many Transformers continuities — the original 1980s animated series and Marvel comics, later series such as Beast Machines, Transformers: Prime, and the live-action film series — Cybertron's details differ, but common elements recur: the capital city of Iacon, the Decepticon stronghold of Kaon, the Council and its Primes, and the civil war that devastated the planet and scattered its inhabitants to Earth and beyond. The planet's energy resource is energon, and its heart contains Vector Sigma and the Matrix of Leadership in several versions.

History in the fiction

In most tellings, Cybertron enjoyed a golden age before Megatron's uprising plunged it into a civil war lasting millions of years. The war drained the planet's energy, prompting expeditions — including Optimus Prime's crew aboard the Ark — that brought the conflict to Earth. Restoring Cybertron is a recurring goal in later series, sometimes at moral cost.
