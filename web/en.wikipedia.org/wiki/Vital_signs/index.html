Vital signs

Vital signs are measurements of the body's essential functions: body temperature, pulse rate, respiration rate, and blood pressure. Pain and oxygen saturation are sometimes included.

Normal ranges

Typical adult resting ranges: temperature about 36.5–37.5 °C, pulse 60–100 bpm, respiration 12–20 breaths/min, blood pressure around 120/80 mmHg.

Clinical use

Vital signs are recorded at triage and during monitoring to detect deterioration, using early warning scores.
