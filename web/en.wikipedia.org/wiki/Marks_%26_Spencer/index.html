Marks & Spencer

Marks and Spencer Group plc, commonly abbreviated to M&S, is a major British multinational retailer headquartered in London. It was founded in 1884 by Michael Marks and Thomas Spencer in Leeds, beginning as a penny bazaar market stall.

Business

M&S specialises in clothing, home products, and food under its own labels, historically including the St Michael brand. Its food halls are known for premium own-brand groceries. The company long resisted selling other brands and was known for a generous returns policy. In the 21st century it has restructured store estates, expanded online sales and the Ocado food joint venture, and operates stores and franchises in dozens of countries.
