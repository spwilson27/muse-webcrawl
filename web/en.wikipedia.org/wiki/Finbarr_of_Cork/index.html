Finbarr of Cork

Saint Finbarr or Finnbarr (c. 550 – 25 September 623), often styled Finbarr of Cork, was Bishop of Cork and abbot of a monastery in what is now the city of Cork, Ireland. He is the patron saint of the city and Diocese of Cork.

Life

According to tradition, Finbarr was born near Bandon and educated at Kilmacahil. He founded a monastic school at Eirce in Cork, at the site now occupied by Saint Fin Barre's Cathedral, which became a noted centre of learning.

Veneration

His feast day is 25 September. Numerous churches and schools in Ireland and the Irish diaspora bear his name.
