Stray (video game)

Developer: BlueTwelve Studio
Publisher: Annapurna Interactive
Release: 19 July 2022
Genre: Adventure
Platforms: PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Windows, Xbox One, Xbox Series X/S, Nintendo Switch, macOS

Stray is a 2022 adventure game developed by BlueTwelve Studio and published by Annapurna Interactive. It was released for PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, and Windows in July 2022, and later for Xbox and Nintendo Switch. The player controls a stray cat who falls into a walled city populated by robots, machines, and mutant bacteria, and sets out to return to the surface with the help of a drone companion, B-12.

Gameplay

The game is a third-person cat adventure presented from a feline perspective. Players traverse platforming sequences, solve environmental puzzles, and evade hostile creatures known as Zurks and Sentinels. The cat can meow, leap across rooftops, knock objects from ledges, and interact with robot inhabitants, who communicate through dialogue translated by B-12.

Development

BlueTwelve Studio, a small team based in Montpellier, France, began development in 2015 under the working title HK. The game was inspired by the founders' cats and by Kowloon Walled City. The cat's movement and animation were based on close observation of real cats.

Reception

Stray received generally favorable reviews, with critics praising its art direction, world design, and faithful depiction of cat behaviour, though some noted its relatively short length and simple puzzles. It won Best Independent Game and Best Debut Indie at The Game Awards 2022, and was nominated for several other awards. The game also became a viral hit among cat owners, with videos of real cats reacting to it widely shared online.
