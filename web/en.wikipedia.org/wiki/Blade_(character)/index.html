Blade (Eric Brooks) is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Marv Wolfman and penciller Gene Colan, he first appeared in The Tomb of Dracula #10 (1973) as a supporting character — a human–vampire hybrid ("dhampir") who hunts vampires, including Deacon Frost, the vampire who attacked his mother during his birth and gave him vampiric traits without their weaknesses, earning him the name "Daywalker".

Character history

Born in London in 1929 in most accounts, Brooks was raised and trained by the vampire hunter Abraham Whistler (in later retellings) and by the secretive order of vampire hunters. Initially written with wooden knives and an immunity to vampire bites and mesmerism, Blade gained the full powers of vampires — superhuman strength, speed, healing and senses — after being bitten by Morbius in the 1990s. He has headlined several ongoing series, led the Nightstalkers, joined the supernatural Midnight Sons, and become sheriff of the vampire nation in recent storylines.

Abilities and weapons

Blade is an expert martial artist and swordsman and a master of edged weapons and firearms. He traditionally fights with a katana-like sword (often silver or titanium with an acid-etched edge), teakwood daggers, mahogany stakes, and specially designed guns firing silver and garlic-filled ammunition, and he wears a Kevlar-reinforced long coat with bandoliers of stakes.

In other media

Wesley Snipes portrayed Blade in the film trilogy Blade (1998), Blade II (2002) and Blade: Trinity (2004), whose success is credited with helping launch the modern era of comic-book films, and reprised the role in Deadpool & Wolverine (2024). Sticky Fingaz played the character in the 2006 television series. Mahershala Ali was cast as Blade for the Marvel Cinematic Universe, making a voice cameo in Eternals (2021).
