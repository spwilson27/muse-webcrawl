Fazioli

Fazioli Pianoforti is an Italian manufacturer of high-end grand pianos, founded in 1981 by engineer and pianist Paolo Fazioli. The company is based in Sacile, in the province of Pordenone in north-eastern Italy. Fazioli produces only around 140 pianos a year, each built largely by hand, and its instruments are prized by many concert pianists for their tonal clarity and craftsmanship.

History

Paolo Fazioli, who held degrees in mechanical engineering and piano performance, set out in the late 1970s to build a piano combining scientific design with traditional craftsmanship. The first Fazioli instruments were shown at trade fairs in 1981, and the company gradually won the admiration of leading pianists. The factory in Sacile occupies some 12,000 square metres and includes a concert hall with seating for about 200 people, used for testing and recitals.

Models

Fazioli makes seven grand piano models, from the F156 (156 cm) up to the F308, at 308 cm one of the longest pianos in production. The F308 features a fourth pedal, patented by the company, which brings the hammers closer to the strings and reduces the volume without altering the timbre, allowing softer playing. Fazioli instruments are also known for elaborate special editions, often with exotic veneers and custom artwork.

Reputation

Fazioli pianos are offered as a choice in many concert halls and competitions, and have been selected by artists including Angela Hewitt, who performs on her own Fazioli F278. The instruments are among the most expensive new pianos in the world.
