Hulu is an American subscription video on-demand streaming service. It was launched in 2008 as a joint venture of media companies including NBCUniversal and News Corporation (later joined by Disney and Time Warner), initially offering free, ad-supported streams of current television episodes. Hulu is named, per its founders, after a Mandarin word evoking a "holder of precious things" (a carved gourd).

History

Hulu launched publicly in March 2008 and quickly became a leading destination for legal online TV streaming in the United States. A subscription tier, Hulu Plus, debuted in 2010. The service launched a live-TV bundle (Hulu + Live TV) in 2017. In 2019 Disney acquired 21st Century Fox's stake and then operational control of Hulu; in 2023–24 Disney bought out Comcast's remaining stake to take full ownership. Hulu content was also integrated into Disney+ as a content hub, while remaining a distinct US service with roughly 50 million subscribers in the mid-2020s.

Programming

Hulu offers next-day streams of broadcast and cable series, a library of films and shows, and acclaimed originals including The Handmaid's Tale (the first streaming series to win the Emmy for Outstanding Drama Series, in 2017), Only Murders in the Building, Shōgun (via FX on Hulu) and The Bear. Its FX on Hulu hub (from 2020) premieres FX productions.
