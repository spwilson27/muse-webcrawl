Enola Holmes

Enola Holmes is a fictional character created by American author Nancy Springer in a series of young adult mystery novels. She is the younger sister of the famous detective Sherlock Holmes and of Mycroft Holmes, and becomes a detective in her own right in Victorian-era London.

The book series

The Enola Holmes Mysteries comprise six novels published between 2006 and 2010, beginning with The Case of the Missing Marquess. When Enola's mother disappears on her fourteenth birthday, Enola flees the finishing school her brothers intend for her and sets up in London as a "scientific perditorian" — a finder of lost things — using wordplay, ciphers, and disguises, frequently escaping her brothers' attempts to retrieve her. Her first name, "Enola," reads "alone" in reverse, a device emphasised throughout the books.

Films

The books were adapted into the Netflix film Enola Holmes (2020), starring Millie Bobby Brown in the title role, with Henry Cavill as Sherlock Holmes and Helena Bonham Carter as their mother, Eudoria. A sequel, Enola Holmes 2, followed in 2022. The first film centres on Enola's search for her missing mother and her encounter with the runaway young Lord Tewkesbury.

Lawsuit

The Conan Doyle Estate sued Netflix and other parties over the first film, arguing that its portrayal of Sherlock Holmes as emotional infringed copyright in the later Holmes stories still under protection at the time. The suit was dismissed later in 2020 following an agreement between the parties.
