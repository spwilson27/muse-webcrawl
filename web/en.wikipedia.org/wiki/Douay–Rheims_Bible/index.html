Douay–Rheims Bible

The Douay–Rheims Bible is an English translation of the Latin Vulgate made by English Catholic exiles: the New Testament at Reims in 1582 and the Old Testament at Douai in 1609–1610.

History

Produced during the English Reformation to serve recusant Catholics, it influenced later English Bible language. Bishop Richard Challoner's extensive 18th-century revision made the text read much like the King James Version.

Use

For centuries it was the standard Bible of English-speaking Catholics, and modern editions and the Douay–Rheims-based translations remain in traditionalist use.
