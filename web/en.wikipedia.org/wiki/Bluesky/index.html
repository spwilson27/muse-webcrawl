Bluesky is a decentralized social microblogging platform and the company that develops it. Created as an initiative within Twitter in 2019 to develop an open, decentralized standard for social media, it became an independent public benefit corporation in 2021 and launched its social app publicly in early 2024.

History

Twitter CEO Jack Dorsey announced the Bluesky initiative in December 2019, funding a small independent team to develop a decentralized protocol. Jay Graber was appointed to lead the project in August 2021. The AT Protocol (Authenticated Transfer Protocol) was unveiled in 2022, and the Bluesky app entered invite-only beta in early 2023. Registration opened to the public in February 2024, after which the service grew rapidly, surpassing 20 million users by late 2024.

Technology

Bluesky runs on the AT Protocol, which allows user data portability, algorithmic choice through custom feeds, and composable moderation via labelling services. Unlike centralized platforms, accounts can migrate between hosting providers without losing followers.

Features

Users post short messages called "posts" (informally "skeets"), follow accounts, create custom feeds and starter packs, and apply content filters. See also Twitter and Mastodon.
