Medical record

The medical record — also called a health record or medical chart — is the systematic documentation of a patient's medical history and care across time. It includes notes on examinations, diagnoses, treatments, test results, medications and correspondence between providers.

Purposes

Records support continuity of care, communication among clinicians, billing, quality review, medico-legal documentation and research. Historically kept on paper, they are now predominantly electronic health records (EHRs), which enable legibility, remote access and decision support but raise privacy and usability concerns.

Contents

A typical record contains identifying information, presenting complaints, past medical and family history, physical examination findings, investigations, diagnoses, treatment plans and progress notes, often structured in formats such as SOAP (subjective, objective, assessment, plan).
