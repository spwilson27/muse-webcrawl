Lynn Ahrens

Lynn Ahrens (born October 1, 1948) is an American writer and lyricist for the musical theatre, television and film, best known for her long collaboration with composer Stephen Flaherty.

Career

Ahrens grew up in New York and worked in advertising before entering musical theatre through the BMI Lehman Engel Musical Theatre Workshop, where she met Flaherty in 1983. Their Broadway successes together include Once on This Island (1990), My Favorite Year (1992), Ragtime (1998), for which they won the Tony Award for Best Original Score, Seussical (2000), Rocky (2014) and Anastasia (2017). Their songs for the 1997 animated film Anastasia, including "Journey to the Past", earned Academy Award and Golden Globe nominations.

Other work

Before the theatre, Ahrens wrote and performed many of the songs for ABC's educational cartoon series Schoolhouse Rock!, including "I'm Just a Bill" material and numerous grammar and science numbers; she also created jingles and themes for television. She and Flaherty were inducted into the Theater Hall of Fame in 2010.
