T-Mobile (UK) was a mobile network operator in the United Kingdom. It began life as Mercury One2One, launched in 1993 as the world's first GSM 1800 network, and was acquired by Deutsche Telekom in 1999. In 2002 the network was rebranded as T-Mobile, in line with Deutsche Telekom's international mobile brand.

Merger into EE

In September 2009, Deutsche Telekom and France Télécom announced plans to merge T-Mobile UK and Orange UK into a joint venture, initially called Everything Everywhere, which launched in 2010 and was rebranded as EE in 2012 with the UK's first 4G network. The T-Mobile and Orange brands were withdrawn from new sales in 2015, and remaining customers were migrated to EE, which BT acquired in 2016.
