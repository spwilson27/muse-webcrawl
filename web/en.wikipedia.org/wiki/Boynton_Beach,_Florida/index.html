Boynton Beach is a city in Palm Beach County, Florida, United States. The population was approximately 80,000 at the 2020 census. Boynton Beach is a principal city of the Miami metropolitan area, which was home to an estimated 6.2 million people in 2018.

History

The area that became Boynton Beach was first settled in 1894 by Nathan Boynton, a former Civil War major from Port Huron, Michigan. Boynton built the Boynton Hotel on the oceanfront and directed much early development. By 1898, a community that became known as Boynton existed near the Intracoastal Waterway. The town was incorporated in 1920.

Geography

According to the United States Census Bureau, the city has a total area of 16.5 square miles (42.8 km²), of which 16.1 square miles is land and 0.4 square miles is water. Boynton Beach lies on Florida's Atlantic coast, south of West Palm Beach and north of Delray Beach.

Demographics

As of the 2020 census, Boynton Beach had a population of about 80,000. The city is one of the most diverse in Palm Beach County and is a popular destination for retirees and families alike.
