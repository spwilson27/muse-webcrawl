St. Louis Cardinals

St. Louis Cardinals League: National League (Central Division)
Established: 1882 (as the St. Louis Brown Stockings)
Ballpark: Busch Stadium, St. Louis, Missouri
World Series titles: 11 (1926, 1931, 1934, 1942, 1944, 1946, 1964, 1967, 1982, 2006, 2011)
Colours: Cardinal red, navy, white

The St. Louis Cardinals are an American professional baseball team based in St. Louis, Missouri. They compete in Major League Baseball as a member club of the National League Central Division. With 11 World Series championships, the Cardinals are the most successful National League franchise and second only to the New York Yankees overall.

Busch Stadium, home of the Cardinals since 2006

History

Founded in 1882 as the Brown Stockings, the club adopted the Cardinals name in 1900. The 1930s "Gashouse Gang" teams, Branch Rickey's pioneering farm system, and post-war stars such as Stan Musial built a powerhouse. Later eras featured Bob Gibson and the 1960s champions, Lou Brock's basestealing, Ozzie Smith's defence in the 1980s, and the Tony La Russa teams of the 2000s led by Albert Pujols.

Rivalries and culture

The Cardinals' principal rivals are the Chicago Cubs and, historically, the Kansas City Royals ("Show-Me Series"). The club is famed for high attendance and the "Cardinal Nation" fan base across the Midwest and South.
