Sophia Gardens is a public park and sports area on the west bank of the River Taff in Cardiff, Wales. The name is also used for the cricket stadium within it, officially the Sophia Gardens Cardiff stadium, the home of Glamorgan County Cricket Club and a venue for international cricket, including Test matches and games in the 2019 Cricket World Cup.

History

The gardens were named after Sophia Rawdon-Hastings, Marchioness of Bute, and opened to the public in the 19th century as one of Cardiff's first public parks, laid out on land donated by the Bute estate. Glamorgan moved its home ground there from Cardiff Arms Park in 1967.

Cricket stadium

The stadium was extensively redeveloped in the 2000s, raising capacity to about 16,000, and hosted its first Test match in 2009, the opening match of the Ashes series between England and Australia. It also hosts Welsh Fire in The Hundred and houses the National Cricket Centre and Sport Wales National Centre nearby.
