Monthly Review Press

Monthly Review Press is an independent publishing house based in New York City, associated with the socialist magazine Monthly Review. Founded in 1951, it publishes books on Marxist theory, political economy, imperialism, ecology, and social movements.

History and publications

The press grew out of Monthly Review, founded in 1949 by Leo Huberman and Paul Sweezy. Its book program became influential with titles such as Paul A. Baran and Paul Sweezy's Monopoly Capital (1966), Harry Braverman's Labor and Monopoly Capital (1974), and Samir Amin's works on dependency and unequal development. In later decades the press developed a strong list in Marxist ecology, including John Bellamy Foster's Marx's Ecology (2000) and Ian Angus's Facing the Anthropocene.
