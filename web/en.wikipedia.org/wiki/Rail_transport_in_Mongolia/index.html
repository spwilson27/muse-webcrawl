Rail transport in Mongolia is dominated by the Ulaanbaatar Railway joint venture (UBTZ), shared between Mongolia and Russia. Railways carry the majority of Mongolia's freight, especially coal, copper and other mineral exports to China and Russia.

Trans-Mongolian Railway

The Trans-Mongolian Railway (completed 1956) runs from the Russian border at Sükhbaatar through Ulaanbaatar to the Chinese border at Zamyn-Üüd, forming the shortest rail link between Moscow and Beijing as part of the Trans-Siberian corridor. It uses the 1,520 mm gauge of Russia, with bogie exchange or transshipment at the Chinese standard-gauge border.

Development

A branch to the Erdenet copper mine opened in the 1970s. In the 2020s Mongolia opened new lines connecting the Tavan Tolgoi coal field to the Chinese border (2022), reducing dependence on trucking and on the older route.
