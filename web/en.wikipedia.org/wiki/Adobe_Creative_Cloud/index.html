Adobe Creative Cloud

Adobe Creative Cloud is a set of applications and services from Adobe giving subscribers access to software for graphic design, video editing, web development, photography and cloud services.

Applications

Flagship apps include Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, Premiere Pro, After Effects, Lightroom, Acrobat Pro, Dreamweaver and XD. Subscriptions are sold in plans for individuals, businesses, students and schools, replacing the earlier perpetual-licence Creative Suite.

History

Creative Cloud was announced in 2011 and became subscription-only in 2013, a shift that drew criticism but produced steady recurring revenue. Services include cloud storage, Adobe Fonts, Behance integration, and generative AI features under the Firefly brand (added 2023).
