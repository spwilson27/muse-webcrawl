Merzouga is a small village in southeastern Morocco, about 35 km (22 mi) southeast of Rissani and 55 km (34 mi) from Erfoud. It lies on the edge of Erg Chebbi, a large sea of dunes formed by wind-blown sand, the largest in Morocco.

Tourism

Merzouga is a major destination for desert tourism. Visitors come to ride camels into the dunes, spend nights in desert camps, watch sunrise and sunset over the sand sea, and try sandboarding. The erg's highest dunes reach about 150 metres. Local tradition holds that bathing in the hot sand in summer can relieve rheumatism and other ailments.

Geography and climate

The village has a hot desert climate with very high summer temperatures. Nearby Lake Dayet Srji, when filled by rain, attracts seasonal birds including flamingos. Occasional flash floods have affected the area, notably in 2006.
