Duke of Roxburghe is a title in the Peerage of Scotland, created in 1707 for John Ker, 5th Earl of Roxburghe, the last Secretary of State of the Kingdom of Scotland, in recognition of his support for the Union. The dukes' seat is Floors Castle near Kelso, the largest inhabited castle in Scotland.

History

John Ker, 3rd Duke of Roxburghe, was a celebrated bibliophile whose library sale in 1812 led to the founding of the Roxburghe Club of bibliophiles. The dukedom passed through the Ker family and, by marriage and name change, to the Innes-Ker family.

The current holder is Charles Innes-Ker, 11th Duke of Roxburghe, who succeeded in 2019. The heir apparent is Charles Innes-Ker, Marquess of Bowmont and Cessford.
