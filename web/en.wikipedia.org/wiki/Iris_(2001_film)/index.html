Iris (2001 film)

Iris is a 2001 biographical drama film directed by Richard Eyre, based on John Bayley's memoirs Elegy for Iris and Iris: A Memoir. It tells the story of novelist Iris Murdoch and her husband John Bayley, from their early romance at Oxford to Murdoch's decline with Alzheimer's disease.

Cast

Judi Dench plays the older Iris Murdoch, with Kate Winslet as her younger self; Jim Broadbent plays the older John Bayley and Hugh Bonneville the younger. The film intercuts between the exuberant young couple and the elderly pair coping with illness.

Reception

The film was critically acclaimed. Jim Broadbent won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor for his performance, while Dench and Winslet both received Oscar nominations, marking a rare case of two actors nominated for playing the same character in one film.
