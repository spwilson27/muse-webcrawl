Defensive medicine

Defensive medicine is the practice of ordering tests, procedures or referrals primarily to reduce legal liability rather than because they are clinically necessary.

Forms

Positive defensive medicine adds low-value care; negative defensive medicine avoids high-risk patients or procedures.

Causes and effects

Fear of malpractice suits is a major driver. It can raise costs, expose patients to radiation and false positives, and strain health systems.

Debate

Estimates of its cost vary widely, and tort reform alone has shown mixed effects on practice.
