National Martyrs' Memorial

Location: Savar, Bangladesh
Completed: 1982
Architect: Syed Mainul Hossain
Commemorates: Martyrs of the Bangladesh Liberation War

The National Martyrs' Memorial (Bengali: জাতীয় স্মৃতিসৌধ, Jatiya Sriti Shoudho) is the national monument of Bangladesh, located in Savar, about 35 kilometres northwest of Dhaka. It honours those who died in the Bangladesh Liberation War of 1971.

Design

Designed by architect Syed Mainul Hossain, the monument consists of seven pairs of triangular folded concrete walls of graduated height, rising to a peak of about 46 metres (150 feet). The seven stages symbolise seven significant movements in Bangladesh's history, from the 1952 Language Movement to the Liberation War. The complex includes mass graves, a mosque, a museum, lakes and landscaped grounds planted with trees by visiting heads of state.

Significance

Construction began in 1972 and the main structure was completed in 1982. The memorial is the focal point of Victory Day (December 16) and Independence Day ceremonies, when the President and Prime Minister lay wreaths at its altar.
