A14 road (England)

The A14 is a major trunk road in England, running roughly 127 miles (204 km) from the Port of Felixstowe in Suffolk, through Ipswich, Bury St Edmunds, Newmarket, Cambridge, and Huntingdon, to join the M1 and M6 motorways near Catthorpe in Leicestershire. It is one of the United Kingdom's most important freight corridors, carrying container traffic from Britain's busiest container port into the Midlands.

Route

Most of the road is dual carriageway. Notable sections include the Orwell Bridge south of Ipswich and the elevated Huntingdon viaduct. Between 2016 and 2020 National Highways carried out a major upgrade between Cambridge and Huntingdon, including a new bypass south of Huntingdon, one of the largest road construction projects in Britain at the time, costing about £1.5 billion.

History

The A14 designation was created in the 1990s unification of several routes (parts of the former A45 and A604) to provide a single strategic corridor from the Haven ports to the motorway network.
