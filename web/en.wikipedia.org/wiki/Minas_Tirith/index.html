Minas Tirith

Minas Tirith (Sindarin for "Tower of the Guard") is the capital city of Gondor in J. R. R. Tolkien's Middle-earth legendarium, prominent in The Lord of the Rings. Originally called Minas Anor, "Tower of the Sun", it was renamed Minas Tirith after its sister city Minas Ithil fell to the Nazgûl.

Description

The city is built on a hill spur of Mount Mindolluin in seven concentric tiers of white stone, each ringed by a wall, with the Citadel and the White Tower of Ecthelion on the summit, where the White Tree of Gondor grows. In the War of the Ring it was besieged by the armies of Mordor in the Battle of the Pelennor Fields.
