Nestlé

Nestlé S.A.
Founded: 1866
Founder: Henri Nestlé
Headquarters: Vevey, Switzerland
Industry: Food and beverage
Products: Nescafé, Kit Kat, Maggi, Purina

Nestlé S.A. is a Swiss multinational food and drink processing conglomerate, headquartered in Vevey, Vaud. It is the largest publicly held food company in the world. It was founded in 1866 by Henri Nestlé, who developed an infant cereal, and merged in 1905 with the Anglo-Swiss Condensed Milk Company.

Brands

Nestlé owns over 2,000 brands including Nescafé, Nespresso, Kit Kat, Smarties, Maggi, Milo, Perrier, San Pellegrino, Purina pet foods, Gerber baby food and Nestlé Toll House cookies. It holds a major stake historically associated with L'Oréal.

History

The company expanded through both world wars, acquiring Maggi (1947), Libby's, Carnation, Rowntree Mackintosh (Kit Kat), Perrier, Buitoni and many others, becoming a global leader in coffee, water, confectionery, dairy and nutrition.

Criticism and controversies

Nestlé has faced long-running criticism over marketing of infant formula in developing countries — prompting a boycott from 1977 — as well as over water extraction, cocoa supply chain child labour, and plastic pollution. The company states it complies with the WHO code and invests in sustainability and responsible sourcing.
