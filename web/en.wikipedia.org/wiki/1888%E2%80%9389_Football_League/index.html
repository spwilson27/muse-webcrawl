The 1888–89 Football League was the first season of the Football League in England, the world's first national association football league competition. Twelve clubs from the Midlands and Northern England competed between September 1888 and April 1889.

Season summary

Preston North End won the championship without losing a single match — the original "Invincibles" — and also won the FA Cup without conceding a goal, completing the first "Double". Aston Villa finished second and Wolverhampton Wanderers third. Each team played the other eleven home and away; two points were awarded for a win.

Foundation

The League was founded by Aston Villa director William McGregor to give clubs regular, organised fixtures in the wake of professionalism. Its success led to the addition of a Second Division in 1892 and provided the template for league systems worldwide.
