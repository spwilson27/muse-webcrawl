Harnai is a coastal town in the Dapoli taluka of Ratnagiri district in the Indian state of Maharashtra. It is situated on the Konkan coast of the Arabian Sea and is known for its fishing harbour, fish auctions, and the sea fort of Suvarnadurg lying just offshore.

Geography

Harnai lies near the mouth of a small creek south of Dapoli and north of Anjarle. The coast here is rocky and dotted with small beaches. The town's natural harbour is protected by the island fort of Suvarnadurg and smaller fortified outcrops such as Kanakadurg.

Suvarnadurg Fort

Suvarnadurg, the "golden fort", was built by the Bijapur Sultanate in the 16th century and later captured and strengthened by the Maratha ruler Shivaji. Together with nearby forts it guarded the Konkan coastline and served as a base for the Maratha navy under admirals such as Kanhoji Angre.

Economy

Fishing is the main occupation, and the daily fish auction at Harnai port is among the busiest on this stretch of coast. Tourism is growing, drawn by the beach, boat trips to the fort, and dolphin watching. The surrounding region produces Alphonso mangoes, cashews, and coconuts.
