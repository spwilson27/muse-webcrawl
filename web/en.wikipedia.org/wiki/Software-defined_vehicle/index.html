Software-defined vehicle

A software-defined vehicle (SDV) is an automobile whose features, functions, and performance are primarily enabled and controlled through software rather than fixed hardware. The concept parallels software-defined networking and reflects the automotive industry's shift toward centralised computing and connectivity.

Characteristics

Software-defined vehicles typically consolidate many electronic control units into a few powerful central computers, support over-the-air software updates that can add or improve features after purchase, and collect data enabling new services and subscription-based functions.

Industry

Tesla popularised the approach with its centrally computed, updateable cars, and most major manufacturers have since announced SDV programmes, often partnering with technology companies on vehicle operating systems.
