A Lot Like Birds

A Lot Like Birds is an American post-hardcore band from Sacramento, California, formed in 2009. The band is known for its technically intricate instrumentation, dual-vocalist dynamic and lengthy, concept-driven compositions, and is associated with the "swancore" scene centred on guitarist Will Swan's Blue Swan Records and related acts such as Dance Gavin Dance.

OriginSacramento, California, United States

History

The band released its debut album, Plan B, in 2010, followed by the concept album Conversation Piece in 2011 after signing with Equal Vision Records. Its third album, No Place (2013), is a concept record in which each song represents a different room of a house, and it charted on the Billboard 200. Vocalist Kurt Travis, formerly of Dance Gavin Dance, joined for Conversation Piece and No Place. After line-up changes, including the departure of Travis and the arrival of Cory Lockwood as sole lead vocalist alongside screamer/lyricist duties, the band released DIVISI in 2017, a more personal album dealing with divorce and loss.

Musical style

A Lot Like Birds' music combines the aggressive dynamics of post-hardcore with progressive and math-rock influences: frequent time-signature changes, tapped and layered guitar work, and shifts between clean singing and harsh vocals. Lyrics are frequently narrative or conceptual, with recurring characters and story arcs across albums.
