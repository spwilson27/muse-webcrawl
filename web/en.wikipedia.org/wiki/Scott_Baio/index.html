Scott Baio

Born: September 22, 1960, New York City, U.S.

Occupation: Actor, television director

Years active: 1976–present

Scott Vincent James Baio (born September 22, 1960) is an American actor and television director. He became a teen idol in the late 1970s playing Chachi Arcola on the sitcom Happy Days (1977–1984) and its spin-off Joanie Loves Chachi, and went on to star as the title character in the sitcom Charles in Charge (1984–1990).

Career

Baio made his film debut as the title character in Alan Parker's children's gangster musical Bugsy Malone (1976). On Happy Days his character Chachi, the cousin of Fonzie, became popular enough to receive his own spin-off. After Charles in Charge he played Dr. Jack Stewart in the medical mystery series Diagnosis: Murder and later appeared in the reality series Scott Baio Is 45...and Single and its sequel, as well as a recurring role in Arrested Development as lawyer Bob Loblaw. He has also directed episodes of television series.

Personal life and politics

Baio has been an outspoken supporter of the Republican Party, speaking at the 2016 Republican National Convention in support of Donald Trump.
