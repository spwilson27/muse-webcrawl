Sewing

Sewing
DefinitionFastening objects using stitches made with needle and thread
ToolsHand needle, sewing machine (1840s+)
[Image: A sewing machine stitching colourful fabric, spools of thread nearby]

Sewing is the craft of fastening or attaching objects using stitches made with a sewing needle and thread — among the oldest textile arts, predating woven cloth itself. The sewing machine (Howe, Singer, 1840s–50s) industrialised garment-making and home dressmaking alike.

Industry

Sewing underpins the global garment industry, concentrated in Asia and a focus of labour-rights campaigning; at home it survives as hobby, repair culture and cosplay craft beside knitting. Surgical sewing — sutures — is its medical branch (see Surgery).
