P'yŏngsŏng (Korean: 평성) is a city in North Korea, the capital of South Pyongan Province in the western part of the country. It is located about 32 kilometres northeast of Pyongyang. The city was established in December 1969.

Geography and economy

Pyongsong lies in a basin near the Taedong River. It serves as a centre of science and light industry for the surrounding province, hosting research institutes affiliated with the Academy of Sciences of North Korea, including facilities associated with atomic energy research. Local industry includes food processing, textiles, and the manufacture of consumer goods; the city is also a distribution centre for wholesale trade, with large markets noted by outside observers.

Transport

The city is served by Pyongsong station on the Pyongra line of the Korean State Railway, connecting it to Pyongyang and the north of the country.
