Beyblade

Beyblade (Japanese: ベイブレード, Beiburēdo) is a line of spinning-top toys originally developed by Takara, first released in Japan in July 1999, along with its debut manga series. Beyblades are launched into a stadium where they collide with opponents' tops; battles are won by knocking the opposing top out of the stadium, causing it to stop spinning, or causing it to burst apart, depending on the generation.

Gameplay and system

A Beyblade is assembled from several parts — typically an attack wheel/layer, a weight or metal component, and a performance tip or driver — allowing players to customize attack, defense, stamina, and balance types. A launcher (often a ripcord type) spins the top into the "Beystadium." In the Burst generation, tops can also win by "bursting" the opponent, when the opponent's top breaks into its component pieces mid-battle.

Generations

  • First generation (1999–2005) — plastic-generation Beyblades, accompanied by the manga and anime Bakuten Shoot Beyblade (simply Beyblade internationally), about Tyson Granger and the Bladebreakers.
  • Metal Fight Beyblade (2008–2012) — also known as Beyblade: Metal Fusion internationally; introduced mostly metal wheels and a new cast led by Gingka Hagane.
  • Beyblade Burst (2015–2023) — introduced the burst mechanic; anime follows Valt Aoi and successors.
  • Beyblade X (2023–present) — emphasizes high-speed battles using a gear-and-rail "Xtreme Gear System" in the stadium.

Media

The franchise includes several manga serialized in CoroCoro Comic, numerous anime television series and films, video games, and licensed merchandise. Hasbro distributes Beyblade products in many markets outside Japan. Beyblade has been a commercial success worldwide, selling hundreds of millions of units, and organized tournaments — including world championships — are held for competitive players.
