Domestic worker

A domestic worker is a person who works within a residence, performing services such as cleaning, cooking, laundry, childcare, elder care and driving. Domestic work is one of the world's oldest and largest occupations, employing tens of millions of people, predominantly women and frequently migrants. Historically excluded from many labour protections, domestic workers gained international recognition through the International Labour Organization's Domestic Workers Convention (No. 189) of 2011, which sets standards for decent work, rest, minimum wage coverage and protection from abuse.
