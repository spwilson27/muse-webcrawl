Trophy Truck

A Trophy Truck, officially called a Trick Truck in some series and designated Class 1-derived "TT", is the fastest and most capable class of off-road racing vehicle, built for desert races such as the Baja 1000, Baja 500, and the Mint 400. Trophy Trucks debuted as a class in the SCORE series in 1994.

Design

Trophy Trucks are purpose-built tube-chassis machines with fiberglass or carbon-fibre bodies resembling production pickup trucks. They typically use front or mid-mounted naturally aspirated V8 engines producing around 900 horsepower or more, rear-wheel drive (with all-wheel drive permitted in an "AWD" subclass), and enormous long-travel suspension — up to about 24 inches of wheel travel in front and 36 inches in the rear — allowing speeds over 130 mph across rough desert terrain. Costs commonly exceed half a million US dollars per truck.

Racing

As the premier unlimited class, Trophy Trucks start first and usually take overall honours at desert events. Famous drivers include Robby Gordon, Ivan "Ironman" Stewart (whose career predates the class), B. J. Baldwin, Andy McMillin, and Bryce Menzies. A spec-engine variant, Trophy Truck Spec (6100 class), limits costs with a sealed production-based engine.
