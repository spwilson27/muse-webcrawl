Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine is the highest body of state executive power in Ukraine. It is responsible to the President of Ukraine and the Verkhovna Rada (parliament), and directs the work of ministries and other central executive bodies.

Composition

The Cabinet consists of the Prime Minister of Ukraine, the First Vice Prime Minister, vice prime ministers, and ministers. The Prime Minister is appointed by the Verkhovna Rada upon the proposal of the President, who in turn considers the candidate proposed by the parliamentary coalition. Other members are appointed by parliament on the Prime Minister's submission — except the Minister of Defence and the Minister of Foreign Affairs, who are nominated by the President.

Powers

Under the Constitution of Ukraine, the Cabinet ensures state sovereignty and economic independence, implements domestic and foreign policy, executes the Constitution and laws, prepares the draft state budget, and directs and coordinates the work of ministries. It issues resolutions and orders that are binding for execution.

Seat

The Cabinet meets in the Government Building on Hrushevsky Street in Kyiv, a monumental 1930s structure facing the Verkhovna Rada building.
