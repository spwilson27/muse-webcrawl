Transformers

"Robots in disguise": the Autobots of the Transformers franchise.

Transformers is a media franchise produced by American toy company Hasbro and Japanese toy company Takara Tomy. It primarily follows the heroic Autobots and the villainous Decepticons, two warring factions of alien robots who can transform into other forms, such as vehicles and animals. The franchise encompasses toys, animation, comic books, video games and films.

History

The franchise began in 1984 with a toy line comprising transforming mecha toys from Takara's Diaclone and Microman lines, rebranded by Hasbro. The taglines "More Than Meets The Eye" and "Robots in Disguise" framed the story of the war between Optimus Prime's Autobots and Megatron's Decepticons, continued from their homeworld of Cybertron to Earth. A Marvel comic and the animated series The Transformers (1984–1987) established the fiction for "Generation 1".

Films and later incarnations

An animated film appeared in 1986. The brand was repeatedly relaunched — Beast Wars (1996), Robots in Disguise (2000), the Unicron Trilogy and Transformers: Prime — and in 2007 Michael Bay's live-action Transformers began a film series that became one of the highest-grossing in cinema history, grossing billions of dollars worldwide. The franchise remains one of the most commercially successful toy-based properties ever, spanning dozens of television series and thousands of toys.
