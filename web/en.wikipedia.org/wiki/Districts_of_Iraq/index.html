Districts of Iraq

The districts of Iraq are the second-level administrative divisions of the country, below the governorates (provinces). Iraq is divided into 18 governorates, which are subdivided into districts (qadha), and districts are further divided into subdistricts (nahiya). There are roughly 120 districts. Baghdad Governorate contains districts including Adhamiyah, Karkh, Rusafa, Sadr City, Kadhimiyah and Mahmudiyah; other large districts include Mosul, Basra, Kirkuk and Erbil. District boundaries and numbers have changed over time, particularly in the Kurdistan Region.
