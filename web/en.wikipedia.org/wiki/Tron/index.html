Tron

A light cycle, the iconic vehicle of the Tron franchise

Tron is a 1982 American science fiction action film produced by Walt Disney Productions, written and directed by Steven Lisberger and starring Jeff Bridges, Bruce Boxleitner, Cindy Morgan and David Warner. It tells the story of Kevin Flynn, a computer programmer and arcade owner who is transported inside the software world of a mainframe computer, where he must team up with security programs to escape.

Plot

Flynn, a former employee of the technology corporation ENCOM, tries to hack into its mainframe to prove that executive Ed Dillinger stole his video game programs. Aided by colleagues Alan Bradley and Lora Baines, Flynn is digitised by an experimental laser and pulled into the computer's "Grid". There he meets Tron, Bradley's security program, and together they battle the tyrannical Master Control Program and its enforcer Sark to free the system.

Production and legacy

The film was one of the first to make extensive use of computer animation, combined with backlit hand-drawn effects; although it was only a modest box-office success on release, it became a cult classic and is credited with pioneering CGI in cinema. It spawned the sequel Tron: Legacy (2010), the animated series Tron: Uprising, video games, and the further sequel Tron: Ares (2025). The light cycle sequence is among the most recognisable images in science fiction film.
