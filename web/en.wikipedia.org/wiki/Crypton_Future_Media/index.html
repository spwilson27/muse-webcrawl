Crypton Future Media

Crypton Future Media, Inc. (クリプトン・フューチャー・メディア株式会社), commonly abbreviated as Crypton, is a Japanese media company based in Sapporo, Japan. The company is best known as the developer of the Hatsune Miku singing voice synthesizer character and other Vocaloid voicebanks.

Crypton Future Media, Inc.
Founded: July 1995
Headquarters: Sapporo, Hokkaido, Japan
Industry: Sound media, software

History

Founded in 1995 by Hiroyuki Itoh, Crypton originally operated as an importer and distributor of sound libraries, sample CDs and music software. In the 2000s it became the main Japanese distributor for Yamaha's Vocaloid engine and released voicebanks including Meiko (2004) and Kaito (2006).

Character Vocal Series

In 2007 Crypton released Hatsune Miku, the first of its "Character Vocal Series", combining a sampled singing voice (provided by voice actress Saki Fujita) with an illustrated anime-style character designed by Kei. Miku became a cultural phenomenon, spawning tens of thousands of user-created songs, concerts performed with a projected 3D avatar, and the collaborative content platform Piapro. Follow-ups included Kagamine Rin and Len (2007) and Megurine Luka (2009). Crypton later moved its characters to its own Piapro Studio software based on the NT engine.
