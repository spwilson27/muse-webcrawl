The General Lee is a fictional automobile featured in the American television series The Dukes of Hazzard. It is a 1969 Dodge Charger driven by cousins Bo and Luke Duke, painted bright orange with the number "01" on its doors, its name lettered above the windows, and a Confederate battle flag painted on the roof. The car is named after Confederate general Robert E. Lee.

Characteristics

The General Lee is known for its signature horn, which plays the first twelve notes of the song "Dixie", its welded-shut doors — requiring the Duke boys to enter through the windows — and its spectacular jumps, a staple of the series. Hundreds of Chargers were used during production; stunt jumps wrecked cars at a high rate, and surviving examples are prized collectors' items.

Legacy and controversy

The car remains one of the most recognisable vehicles in television history. Its roof flag, however, has made it controversial: following wider debates over Confederate symbols, Warner Bros. announced in 2015 that it would no longer manufacture merchandise depicting the flag on the car, and some surviving display cars have had the roof graphic altered or covered.
