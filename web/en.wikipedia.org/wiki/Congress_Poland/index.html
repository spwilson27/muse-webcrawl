Congress Poland, formally the Kingdom of Poland, was a Polish polity created in 1815 by the Congress of Vienna as a client state of the Russian Empire, in personal union with Russia through the Russian emperor, who ruled as King of Poland. It was formed from most of the territory of Napoleon's Duchy of Warsaw.

History

The kingdom initially enjoyed considerable autonomy, with its own constitution (one of the most liberal in Europe at the time), parliament (Sejm), army, currency and administration, with Polish as the language of government. After the failed November Uprising of 1830–31 against Russian rule, its autonomy was progressively curtailed: the constitution was replaced by the Organic Statute, the army dissolved, and the Sejm abolished. Following the January Uprising of 1863–64, the kingdom was stripped of its remaining separate institutions and incorporated more directly into the Russian Empire, being informally renamed the "Vistula Land". Russian became compulsory in administration and education, and a policy of Russification followed. The territory remained under Russian control until the First World War, when it was occupied by Germany and Austria-Hungary in 1915.
