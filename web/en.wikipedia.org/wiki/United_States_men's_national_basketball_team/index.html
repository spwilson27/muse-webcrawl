The United States men's national basketball team represents the United States in international basketball and is the most successful national team in the sport's history. Governed by USA Basketball, the team has won the Olympic gold medal 17 times and the FIBA Basketball World Cup five times. For most of the 20th century the team was drawn from amateur and college players; since 1992 it has been selected from the stars of the NBA.

The Dream Team and after

The 1992 Barcelona Olympic team — featuring Michael Jordan, Magic Johnson, Larry Bird and Charles Barkley — is widely considered the greatest team ever assembled in any sport, winning its games by an average of nearly 44 points and transforming basketball's global popularity. Defeats in the 2004 Olympics and the 2006 and 2019/2023 World Cups, where the US finished off the podium in 2023 as Germany won the title, demonstrated how the rest of the world has closed the gap. The team responded by sending star-laden squads to win Olympic gold at Beijing 2008 (the "Redeem Team"), London, Rio, Tokyo and Paris 2024, where a veteran side led by LeBron James, Stephen Curry and Kevin Durant prevailed.
