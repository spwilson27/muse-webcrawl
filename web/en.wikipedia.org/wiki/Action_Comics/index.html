Action Comics is an American comic book series published by DC Comics since 1938. Its first issue introduced Superman, the first modern superhero, created by writer Jerry Siegel and artist Joe Shuster — an event usually taken to mark the beginning of the Golden Age of Comic Books and of the superhero genre itself.

History

Action Comics #1 (cover-dated June 1938) was originally an anthology featuring characters such as Zatara the magician and Tex Thomson, but Superman quickly became its star and cover feature. The series ran monthly for decades, reaching issue #904 in 2011 before being relaunched with new numbering as part of DC's "New 52". Original numbering was restored in 2016, and issue #1000 was published in April 2018 with a celebratory anthology of stories. Along the way the title introduced or developed figures such as Lex Luthor, Lois Lane's solo features, Supergirl, the Fortress of Solitude and the Legion of Super-Heroes' first appearance.

Collectibility

Action Comics #1 is among the most valuable comic books in the world; high-grade copies have sold at auction for several million dollars, reflecting both its rarity and its foundational place in popular culture.
