Luciano Acosta

Full name: Luciano Federico Acosta
Born: 31 May 1994, Rosario, Argentina
Height: 1.60 m (5 ft 3 in)
Position: Attacking midfielder
Youth career: Boca Juniors

Luciano Federico Acosta (born 31 May 1994) is an Argentine professional footballer who plays as an attacking midfielder. A product of the Boca Juniors academy, he is noted for his dribbling, vision and playmaking despite his small stature, and he won the Landon Donovan MLS MVP Award in 2023 with FC Cincinnati.

Club career

Boca Juniors and Estudiantes

Acosta made his professional debut for Boca Juniors in 2014. He spent a season on loan at Estudiantes de La Plata in 2015, where regular playing time accelerated his development.

D.C. United

In 2016 Acosta joined D.C. United of Major League Soccer, initially on loan and then on a permanent, club-record transfer. Playing behind and alongside Wayne Rooney from 2018, he formed one of the league's most productive attacking partnerships, earning the nickname "Lucho" and MLS Best XI honours.

Atlas

Acosta moved to Liga MX club Atlas in 2020, spending a single season in Guadalajara before returning to MLS.

FC Cincinnati

In 2021 Acosta signed with FC Cincinnati as a Designated Player. As club captain and creative hub, he led the league in assists and in 2023 guided Cincinnati to the Supporters' Shield, winning the MLS Most Valuable Player award and a place in the MLS Best XI. In 2025 he was traded within MLS to FC Dallas.

Style of play

A classic Argentine enganche, Acosta plays between the lines, receiving on the half-turn and creating chances through short passing, feints and close control in tight spaces.

Honours

  • Supporters' Shield: 2023 (FC Cincinnati)
  • Landon Donovan MLS MVP Award: 2023
  • MLS Best XI: 2018, 2023
