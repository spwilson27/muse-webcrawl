Hannover Messe

The Hannover Messe (Hanover Fair) is one of the world's largest trade fairs for industrial technology. It is held on the Hanover Fairground in Hanover, Lower Saxony, Germany, typically every spring. It showcases developments in industrial automation, energy, robotics, software and research and development.

History

The fair was founded in 1947 as the Exportmesse Hannover, created to promote economic activity in post-war Germany under the British military government. It proved so successful that it became an annual event. Over the decades it grew into the Hanover Fairground, the largest exhibition centre in the world.

Profile

The modern Hannover Messe typically hosts several thousand exhibitors and attracts well over 100,000 visitors. Each year a partner country is featured. The concept of "Industry 4.0" (Industrie 4.0) — the digitalisation of manufacturing — was publicly launched at the fair in 2011 and became a flagship theme. The fair is organised by Deutsche Messe AG.
