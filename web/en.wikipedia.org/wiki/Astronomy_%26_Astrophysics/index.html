Astronomy & Astrophysics is a peer-reviewed scientific journal covering theoretical, observational, and instrumental astronomy and astrophysics. It was established in 1969 and is published by EDP Sciences on behalf of the European Southern Observatory (ESO) as a journal of the European astronomical community.

The journal's sponsoring countries are European countries plus Argentina, Brazil and Chile. The editor-in-chief is Thierry Forveille. It is often abbreviated as A&A. The journal publishes papers on all aspects of astronomy, divided into sections covering astrophysical processes, cosmology, galactic and extragalactic astronomy, stellar structure and evolution, planetary systems, and instrumentation.

History

Astronomy & Astrophysics was formed in 1969 by the merger of several national journals of European countries into a common publication: the French Annales d'Astrophysique, the German Zeitschrift für Astrophysik, the Dutch Bulletin of the Astronomical Institutes of the Netherlands, and others. A supplement series, Astronomy and Astrophysics Supplement Series, published extensive catalogues and data tables; it merged into the main journal in 2001. Since 2022 the journal has been published fully open access under the Subscribe to Open model.

Abstracting and indexing

The journal is abstracted and indexed in major databases including the Astrophysics Data System (ADS), Scopus, the Science Citation Index and INSPEC.
