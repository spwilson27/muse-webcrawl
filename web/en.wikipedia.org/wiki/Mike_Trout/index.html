Mike Trout

Michael Nelson Trout (born 7 August 1991) is an American professional baseball center fielder for the Los Angeles Angels of Major League Baseball (MLB). Widely regarded as one of the greatest players of his generation, Trout is a three-time American League Most Valuable Player (2014, 2016, 2019), an eleven-time All-Star, and the 2012 AL Rookie of the Year.

Career

Trout was drafted by the Angels 25th overall in 2009 out of Millville Senior High School in New Jersey — a famous draft snub, as 21 teams passed on him. He debuted in July 2011 and, in his first full season of 2012, posted a landmark campaign combining power, speed and defence, leading the league in runs and stolen bases and becoming the youngest player in the 30–30 club.

In 2019 he signed a 12-year, $426.5 million contract extension with the Angels, then the largest contract in North American sports history. Injuries limited his playing time in several seasons of the early 2020s, including a calf strain in 2021 and back and meniscus injuries thereafter, though his per-game production remained elite. Trout has combined a career batting average around .300 with over 380 home runs and more than 200 stolen bases, and he captained Team USA at the 2023 World Baseball Classic.

Playing style

Trout is a five-tool player noted for exceptional plate discipline, raw power, sprint speed and centre-field defence; by wins above replacement he accumulated value faster than almost any player in history through his twenties.
