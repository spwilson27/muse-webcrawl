Bang Si-hyuk (born 9 August 1972), also known professionally as "Hitman" Bang, is a South Korean music producer, songwriter, and entertainment executive. He is the founder of Big Hit Music and the chairman of Hybe Corporation, the entertainment conglomerate behind BTS, and is widely credited as one of the architects of K-pop's global expansion.

Early career

Bang graduated from Seoul National University, where he studied aesthetics. He began his career in the mid-1990s and joined Park Jin-young's JYP Entertainment as a composer and producer, writing hits for artists including g.o.d, Rain, and the Wonder Girls. His songwriting earned him numerous awards in South Korea, and he became known for emotionally direct lyrics and polished pop production.

Big Hit and BTS

In 2005 Bang founded his own company, Big Hit Entertainment. After years of modest results with groups such as 8Eight and 2AM (co-managed with JYP), he formed the hip-hop-influenced idol group BTS, which debuted in 2013. Bang's willingness to let the members write about their own anxieties and to communicate directly with fans through social media helped BTS grow into the best-selling musical act in South Korean history and a global stadium act, topping the Billboard Hot 100 and addressing the United Nations.

Hybe

In 2021 Big Hit Entertainment rebranded as Hybe Corporation, expanding into a multi-label structure (Big Hit Music, Pledis, Source Music, ADOR, KOZ and others), the fan platform Weverse, and overseas acquisitions including Ithaca Holdings in the United States. Bang stepped back from the CEO role in 2021 to focus on music production and his position as chairman of the board. He has appeared on Bloomberg and Forbes lists of the world's billionaires, and in 2022 was named to the Time 100.
