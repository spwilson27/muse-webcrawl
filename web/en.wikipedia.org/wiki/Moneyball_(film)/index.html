Moneyball (film)

Moneyball is a 2011 American sports drama film directed by Bennett Miller and written by Steven Zaillian and Aaron Sorkin, based on Michael Lewis's 2003 nonfiction book Moneyball: The Art of Winning an Unfair Game. Brad Pitt stars as Billy Beane, the general manager of the Oakland Athletics baseball team.

The film dramatizes the Athletics' 2002 season, in which Beane, unable to compete financially with wealthy clubs, teams up with Yale economics graduate Peter Brand (Jonah Hill, in a role loosely based on Paul DePodesta) to build a competitive team using sabermetric statistical analysis — valuing players by undervalued metrics such as on-base percentage rather than by traditional scouting judgments. The team goes on to set an American League record with a 20-game winning streak, though it loses in the playoffs.

The film was a commercial and critical success, grossing over $110 million worldwide, and received six Academy Award nominations, including Best Picture, Best Actor for Pitt, and Best Supporting Actor for Hill.
