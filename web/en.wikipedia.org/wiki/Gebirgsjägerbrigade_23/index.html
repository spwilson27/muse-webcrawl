Gebirgsjägerbrigade 23 "Bayern" is a mountain infantry brigade of the German Army (Bundeswehr). Headquartered in Bad Reichenhall in Bavaria, it is the Bundeswehr's principal formation specialised in warfare in mountains and difficult terrain, and in winter conditions.

Organisation

The brigade comprises three mountain infantry battalions (Gebirgsjägerbataillone 231, 232 and 233) stationed in Bad Reichenhall, Bischofswiesen and Mittenwald, together with a mountain reconnaissance battalion, a mountain engineer battalion, a mountain signals company and a mountain supply battalion. Its soldiers wear the edelweiss badge on their caps, a tradition of German and Austrian mountain troops.

Role and deployments

The brigade trains for operations in high mountains, extreme weather and rough terrain, including military skiing and climbing. Its units have deployed on Bundeswehr missions abroad, including in Kosovo and Afghanistan, and support civil authorities during alpine emergencies and natural disasters in Bavaria.
