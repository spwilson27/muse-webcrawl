List of moments of inertia

The moment of inertia of a rigid body is a quantity that determines the torque needed for a given angular acceleration about a rotational axis. The following is a list of moments of inertia (mass moments of inertia) for common shapes, where m is the mass of the body and the axis passes through the centre of mass unless otherwise noted.

Standard shapes and their axes of rotation.

List

Point massAt distance r from axisI = mr²
Thin rod (length L)Perpendicular, through centreI = (1/12) mL²

Thin hoop / ring (radius R)Through centre, perpendicular to planeI = mR²
Thin hoop / ring (radius R)DiameterI = (1/2) mR²
Solid cylinder / disk (radius R)Central axisI = (1/2) mR²
Hollow cylinder (radii R₁, R₂)Central axisI = (1/2) m(R₁² + R₂²)
Solid sphere (radius R)DiameterI = (2/5) mR²
Hollow sphere (thin shell, radius R)DiameterI = (2/3) mR²
Solid cone (radius R, height h)Central (symmetry) axisI = (3/10) mR²
Rectangular plate (sides a, b)Perpendicular, through centreI = (1/12) m(a² + b²)
Rectangular plate (sides a, b)Along side b in the plane (through centre)I = (1/12) ma²
Solid cuboid (width w, height h, depth d)Through centre, perpendicular to w–h faceI = (1/12) m(w² + h²)
Torus (tube radius r, ring radius R)Central axisI = m(R² + (3/4)r²)
Ellipsoid (semi-axes a, b, c)Along c axisI = (1/5) m(a² + b²)
Regular polygon (n sides, circumradius R)Perpendicular, through centreI = (mR²/2)(1 − (2/3) tan²(π/n)) approximately for large n; varies by shape

Parallel axis theorem

If the moment of inertia about an axis through the centre of mass is Icm, then the moment of inertia about a parallel axis a distance d away is I = Icm + md². This theorem allows many entries above to be adapted to other axes.
