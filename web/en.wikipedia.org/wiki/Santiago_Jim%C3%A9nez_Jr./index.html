Santiago Jiménez Jr.

Santiago Jiménez Jr. (born 1944) is an American accordionist and singer from San Antonio, Texas, and a leading figure in conjunto and Tejano music. He is the son of conjunto pioneer Santiago Jiménez Sr. and the brother of Leonardo "Flaco" Jiménez.

Career

Jiménez Jr. began playing accordion in his father's tradition, championing the older two-row button accordion style of traditional conjunto at a time when many younger musicians were modernising the sound. He formed his own band and recorded extensively, keeping close to the roots style pioneered by his father and Narciso Martínez.

Recognition

He received a National Heritage Fellowship from the National Endowment for the Arts, the United States' highest honour in folk and traditional arts, and was inducted into halls of fame honouring Tejano and conjunto music. His recordings for labels such as Arhoolie helped bring traditional conjunto to wider audiences.
