List of the busiest airports in Australia

This is a list of the busiest airports in Australia by passenger traffic. Australia's aviation market is dominated by the "golden triangle" routes between Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane; the Sydney–Melbourne route has long been among the busiest air routes in the world.
