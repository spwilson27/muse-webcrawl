Double (baseball)

In baseball, a double is a hit in which the batter reaches second base safely without the benefit of an error or a fielder's choice. Doubles are counted among extra-base hits.

Rules

A ball bouncing over the outfield fence is an automatic or ground-rule double. League leaders in doubles are recorded seasonally; Tris Speaker holds the career record with 792.

Strategy

Doubles frequently score runners from first and are a key component of slugging percentage.
