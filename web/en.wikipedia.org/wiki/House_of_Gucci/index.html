House of Gucci

House of Gucci is a 2021 American biographical crime drama film directed by Ridley Scott, based on Sara Gay Forden's 2001 book The House of Gucci: A Sensational Story of Murder, Madness, Glamour, and Greed. It stars Lady Gaga as Patrizia Reggiani and Adam Driver as Maurizio Gucci.

Plot

The film follows Patrizia Reggiani, who marries into the Gucci family in the 1970s and becomes embroiled in the family's internal power struggles over control of the fashion house. Her ambition and betrayals ultimately lead her to hire a hitman, who murders Maurizio Gucci outside his Milan office in 1995. Reggiani was convicted of arranging the murder in 1998.

Cast

  • Lady Gaga as Patrizia Reggiani
  • Adam Driver as Maurizio Gucci
  • Al Pacino as Aldo Gucci
  • Jared Leto as Paolo Gucci
  • Jeremy Irons as Rodolfo Gucci
  • Salma Hayek as Giuseppina "Pina" Auriemma

Reception

The film grossed over $150 million worldwide. The Gucci family criticised the portrayal of its members, while performances — particularly those of Lady Gaga and Jared Leto — received awards attention.
