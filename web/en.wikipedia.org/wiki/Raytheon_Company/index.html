The Raytheon Company was an American defence contractor and industrial corporation with manufacturing concentrations in weapons and military and commercial electronics. Founded in 1922, it was one of the largest producers of guided missiles in the world, including the Patriot, Sidewinder, Tomahawk and Stinger systems. In April 2020 Raytheon merged with United Technologies Corporation to form Raytheon Technologies, now RTX, with the Raytheon name surviving in its missiles and defence business.

History

The company began in Cambridge, Massachusetts, as the American Appliance Company, founded by MIT engineer Vannevar Bush, Laurence K. Marshall and Charles G. Smith to commercialise a gaseous rectifier tube that let radios run on household current. Renamed Raytheon ("light of the gods"), it became a major wartime supplier of magnetron tubes for radar; its engineer Percy Spencer discovered the heating effect of microwaves and invented the microwave oven, marketed from 1947 as the Radarange. Raytheon built the guidance computer for the Apollo programme, produced the shipboard computers of the US Navy, and through acquisitions — including Beech Aircraft, Hughes Aircraft's defence businesses, Texas Instruments' defence arm and E-Systems — assembled a portfolio spanning missiles, radars, sensors and space systems.

Products and business areas

Before the merger, Raytheon's major segments were Integrated Defense Systems (Patriot and naval radars), Intelligence, Information and Services, Missile Systems (Tomahawk, AMRAAM, Sidewinder, Stinger, Javelin co-production), Space and Airborne Systems, and Forcepoint, its cybersecurity joint venture. At its final independence it employed roughly 70,000 people and reported annual revenues of about US$29 billion, overwhelmingly from United States government contracts.

Merger

Announced in June 2019 as a "merger of equals", the combination with United Technologies closed on 3 April 2020, creating Raytheon Technologies, headquartered in Waltham, Massachusetts; the company renamed itself RTX in 2023. The former Raytheon businesses were reorganised mainly into the Raytheon (missiles and defence) segment.
