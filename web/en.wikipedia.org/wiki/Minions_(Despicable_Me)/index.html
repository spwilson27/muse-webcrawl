Minions are a fictional species of small, yellow, capsule-shaped creatures who are the signature characters of Illumination's Despicable Me franchise. They wear blue overalls and silver goggles, speak an unintelligible language mixing fragments of many real languages (often called "Minionese" or "Banana Language"), and are driven by an instinct to serve the most despicable master they can find. Originally comic-relief henchmen of the supervillain Gru, the Minions became breakout stars and headline their own spin-off films, beginning with Minions (2015).

Characteristics

Minions are typically one to three and a half feet tall, with one or two eyes, three fingers on each hand, and sprouts of black hair or bald heads. Named Minions include Kevin, Stuart, Bob, Dave, Jerry, Tim, and Phil. They are childlike, impulsive, easily distracted — especially by bananas — and apparently functionally immortal: the spin-off film establishes that they have existed since the dawn of life on Earth, serving a succession of masters from a Tyrannosaurus rex to Napoleon.

Language

The Minions' speech, largely performed by director Pierre Coffin, is a gibberish that interleaves genuine words from French, English, Spanish, Italian, Indonesian, Japanese, and other languages. Familiar words such as "banana" and phrases like "pwede na" and "para tu" recur frequently, and context and tone generally make their meaning clear.

Appearances

The Minions appear in all of the main Despicable Me films (2010, 2013, 2017, 2024), star in the prequel spin-offs Minions (2015) and Minions: The Rise of Gru (2022), and feature in numerous short films, video games, and theme-park attractions. Minions grossed over $1.1 billion worldwide, making it one of the highest-grossing animated films of all time.

Cultural impact

The Minions became a major merchandising and internet phenomenon in the 2010s: their image was licensed onto a vast range of consumer products, and they became ubiquitous as memes and reaction images. The characters are the official mascots of Illumination.
