Weill Cornell Medicine - Qatar (WCM-Q) is a branch campus of Cornell University's medical school, located in Education City in Doha, Qatar. Established in 2001 through a partnership between Cornell University and the Qatar Foundation, it was the first American medical school to open a campus offering a full medical degree outside the United States.

History

The college was founded in 2001 and admitted its first students to the pre-medical programme in 2002; the first cohort began the medical programme in 2004 and graduated in 2008, receiving Cornell University M.D. degrees identical to those awarded in New York. The campus building, designed by the Japanese architect Arata Isozaki, opened in 2003 and is noted for its monumental geometric forms in Education City.

Programmes

WCM-Q offers a six-year programme combining a two-year pre-medical curriculum with the four-year medical curriculum of Weill Cornell Medicine in New York, as well as a standalone four-year graduate-entry M.D. programme and a two-year pre-medical programme. Teaching is in English, and clinical training takes place largely at Hamad Medical Corporation facilities and other affiliated hospitals in Qatar. The college also runs research programmes in areas such as metabolic disease, population health and biomedical sciences, and continuing professional development for healthcare practitioners.
