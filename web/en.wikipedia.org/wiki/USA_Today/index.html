USA Today is an American daily newspaper founded by Al Neuharth and launched on 15 September 1982 by the Gannett Company. Famous for its colour graphics, short stories, national scope and free distribution in hotels, it pioneered the modern national general-interest paper and for decades had among the largest circulations in the United States.

History and style

USA Today was designed for the television age: colour weather maps, sports statistics and brief national stories, sold in distinctive rounded blue vending boxes. Critics dubbed it 'McPaper'; its design and national model influenced newspapers worldwide.

Network era

As print declined, Gannett built the USA Today Network of local papers feeding the national site; usatoday.com became one of America's most-visited news sites, and the print edition shrank in frequency and reach.
