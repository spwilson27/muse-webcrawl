Staurakios (Greek: Σταυράκιος, romanized: Staurákios; died 11 January 812) was a Byzantine emperor who reigned for a few months in 811. The son of Emperor Nikephoros I, he had been crowned co-emperor in December 803 and succeeded his father after Nikephoros was killed at the catastrophic Battle of Pliska against the Bulgars under Krum on 26 July 811.

Staurakios himself was gravely wounded in the battle—reportedly paralysed by a neck wound—and was carried back to Constantinople, where he was proclaimed sole emperor. His incapacity, and machinations by courtiers and his wife Theophano, led to his deposition by his brother-in-law Michael Rangabe on 2 October 811. Staurakios retired to a monastery and died of his wounds a few months later, on 11 January 812.
