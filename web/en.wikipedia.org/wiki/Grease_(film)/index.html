Grease is a 1978 American musical romantic comedy film directed by Randal Kleiser and based on the 1971 musical of the same name by Jim Jacobs and Warren Casey. The film depicts the lives of greaser Danny Zuko (John Travolta) and Australian transfer student Sandy Olsson (Olivia Newton-John), who develop an attraction for each other during a summer romance in 1958.

Plot

In the summer of 1958, local boy Danny Zuko and vacationing Sandy Olsson meet at the beach and fall in love. When summer ends, Sandy unexpectedly enrols at Rydell High School, where Danny is the leader of the T-Birds greaser gang. After a troubled reunion in which Danny tries to maintain his tough image, the two gradually reconcile, culminating in the school carnival, where Sandy transforms her look to win Danny over as he likewise makes an effort to change for her.

Music

The film's soundtrack was a major success. "You're the One That I Want" and "Hopelessly Devoted to You" became hit singles, and the title song "Grease", written by Barry Gibb and performed by Frankie Valli, also topped the charts. The soundtrack album was one of the best-selling albums of all time.

Reception

Grease was the highest-grossing film of 1978 in the United States and, for decades, the highest-grossing musical film worldwide. It was nominated for an Academy Award for Best Original Song for "Hopelessly Devoted to You". A sequel, Grease 2, was released in 1982, and a prequel television series, Grease: Rise of the Pink Ladies, premiered in 2023.
