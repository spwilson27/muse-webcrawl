Shakti

Shakti (Sanskrit: "power, ability") in Hinduism is the primordial cosmic energy and the personification of the divine feminine creative power, often embodied as the Great Goddess (Devi or Mahadevi). Shakti is the dynamic energy through which the universe is created, sustained and destroyed.

Theology

In Shaktism, one of the major Hindu traditions, the goddess is supreme. Goddesses such as Durga, Kali, Lakshmi, Saraswati and Parvati are all forms of Shakti; each male deity's power is likewise understood as his shakti, his consort. Philosophically, Shakti is paired with Shiva as energy with consciousness.

Practice

Shakti is honoured at Shakti Pithas — pilgrimage sites linked to the fallen body parts of Sati — and in festivals such as Navaratri and Durga Puja. Kundalini yoga describes shakti coiled at the base of the spine.
