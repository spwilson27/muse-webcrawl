"I Want to Know What Love Is" is a power ballad by the British-American rock band Foreigner. It was released in November 1984 as the lead single from their album Agent Provocateur and became the band's biggest hit, reaching number one in both the United Kingdom and the United States, and topping charts in many other countries.

Background and recording

Written by guitarist Mick Jones, the song features lead vocals by Lou Gramm and backing vocals from the New Jersey Mass Choir, whose gospel contribution gives the recording its distinctive climax; Jennifer Holliday and Tom Bailey of the Thompson Twins also sang backing vocals. Jones has described the song as arising from his personal relationships and a search for a more enduring kind of love.

Reception and legacy

The single spent several weeks at number one on the Billboard Hot 100 in early 1985 and remains one of the best-known rock ballads of the 1980s. It has been covered by many artists, most successfully by Mariah Carey, whose 2009 version was a major international hit, particularly in Brazil and Europe. The song is regularly listed among the greatest ballads of all time and has accumulated billions of streams and radio plays.
