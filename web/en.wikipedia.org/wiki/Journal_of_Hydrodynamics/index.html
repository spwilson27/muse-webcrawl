The Journal of Hydrodynamics is a peer-reviewed scientific journal covering research in hydrodynamics and fluid mechanics. Founded in 1989, it is the English-language edition associated with the Chinese journal 水动力学研究与进展 (Journal of Hydrodynamics, Ser. A) and is one of China's principal outlets for fundamental and applied research on the motion of liquids. Since 2008 the journal has been published internationally by Elsevier.

Scope

The journal publishes original theoretical, experimental and numerical studies in areas including ship and ocean hydrodynamics, cavitation, multiphase and free-surface flows, hydraulic engineering, environmental fluid mechanics, wave mechanics, computational fluid dynamics applied to water flows, and fluid–structure interaction. It has been a prominent venue for research on high-speed hydrofoil craft, supercavitating vehicles and hydrodynamic noise.

Publication and indexing

The journal appears bimonthly and is abstracted and indexed in services including Science Citation Index Expanded, EI Compendex and Scopus. Its editorial office is in Shanghai, and the journal is sponsored by the China Ship Scientific Research Center and the Chinese Society of Theoretical and Applied Mechanics.
