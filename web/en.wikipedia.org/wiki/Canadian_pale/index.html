Canadian pale

In heraldry, a Canadian pale is a central vertical band (a pale) occupying half the width of a shield or flag, rather than the traditional one third. It was devised for the national flag of Canada adopted in 1965.

Type: Heraldic ordinary
Introduced: 1965
Associated with: Flag of Canada

Origin

George Stanley's design for the Canadian flag used a widened pale to give prominence to the central maple leaf emblem. The term was recognised by heraldic authorities when the flag was granted.

Use

The Canadian pale appears in many Canadian civic and institutional arms and flags granted by the Canadian Heraldic Authority.
