Home insurance

Home insurance, also commonly called homeowners insurance, is a type of property insurance that covers losses and damage to an individual's house and assets in the home. It usually also provides liability coverage against accidents in the home or on the property.

Coverage

Standard policies typically combine several protections: insurance of the dwelling itself against perils such as fire, wind and theft; coverage of personal property; liability protection; and additional living expenses if the home becomes uninhabitable. In the United States, standardised policy forms (such as HO-3) are widely used, while perils like flood and earthquake usually require separate policies or endorsements.

Requirements

Mortgage lenders generally require borrowers to maintain homeowners insurance for at least the value of the loan. Premiums depend on location, construction, the value insured, the deductible chosen and the policyholder's claims history.
