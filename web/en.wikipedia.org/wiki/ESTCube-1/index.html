ESTCube-1

ESTCube-1 is a form factor CubeSat spacecraft built by students at the University of Tartu in Estonia. It was launched on 7 May 2013 aboard a Vega rocket from Guiana Space Centre, becoming Estonia's first satellite.

ESTCube-1
Operator: University of Tartu
Launch: 7 May 2013, Vega
Mass: 1.05 kg
Mission: Test electric solar wind sail

Mission

The satellite was designed to test a 10-metre electric solar wind sail (E-sail) tether developed by Finnish inventor Pekka Janhunen, and to photograph Earth. The tether deployment did not succeed as planned.

End of mission

ESTCube-1 operated for about two years; its last signal was received on 19 May 2015. It remains in low Earth orbit as debris until it re-enters.
