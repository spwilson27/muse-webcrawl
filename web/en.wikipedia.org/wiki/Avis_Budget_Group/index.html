Avis Budget Group

Avis Budget Group, Inc. is an American car rental holding company headquartered in Parsippany, New Jersey. It is the parent company of Avis Car Rental, Budget Rent a Car, Budget Truck Rental and Zipcar, and operates or licenses its brands in more than 180 countries, directly running over 11,000 rental locations worldwide. The company is listed on the Nasdaq stock exchange under the ticker symbol CAR.

History

Warren Avis founded Avis Rent a Car in 1946 at Willow Run Airport near Detroit — the first rental company located at an airport — and built its identity around the famous "We Try Harder" campaign of 1962, which embraced its number-two position behind Hertz. Budget was founded in Los Angeles in 1958 by Morris Mirkin, targeting cost-conscious renters. Both brands passed through a succession of owners, including Beatrice Companies and, from 1987 and 2002 respectively, the hospitality and travel conglomerate Cendant Corporation.

Formation of the group

In 2006 Cendant split into four companies; its vehicle rental businesses became Avis Budget Group. The group acquired Avis Europe in 2011, reuniting the Avis and Budget brands globally, and bought the carsharing service Zipcar in 2013 for about $500 million. In 2015 it acquired Maggiore, an Italian rental company. The group licences its brands in some territories — in southern Africa, for example, operations were historically run by the Barloworld group under licence.

Operations

Avis is positioned as the premium brand and Budget as the value brand; Budget Truck rents light commercial vehicles for household moves. Fleet purchases, typically of several hundred thousand vehicles per year, are largely under manufacturer repurchase ("program car") agreements that shift residual-value risk. The COVID-19 pandemic severely depressed rentals in 2020, after which a shortage of new vehicles drove record used-car prices and unexpectedly large profits in 2021.
