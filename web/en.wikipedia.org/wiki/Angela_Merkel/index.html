Angela Merkel

Angela Dorothea Merkel (born 17 July 1954) is a German retired politician and scientist who served as Chancellor of Germany from 2005 to 2021 — the first woman to hold the office — and as leader of the Christian Democratic Union (CDU) from 2000 to 2018. She was widely described as the de facto leader of the European Union and the most powerful woman in the world.

Early life

Born in Hamburg, she grew up in East Germany, where her father was a Lutheran pastor. She studied physics at Leipzig and worked as a research chemist in quantum chemistry in Berlin. After the fall of the Berlin Wall she entered politics, becoming a spokeswoman for the last East German government.

Chancellorship

Entering the Bundestag in 1990 and serving as a minister under Helmut Kohl, she became CDU leader in 2000 and Chancellor after the 2005 election. Over four terms she steered Germany through the eurozone crisis, the 2015 European migrant crisis — marked by her phrase Wir schaffen das — the Energiewende away from nuclear power after Fukushima, and the COVID-19 pandemic.

Retirement

She stood down in 2021 and was succeeded by Olaf Scholz. Her memoir Freedom appeared in 2024.
