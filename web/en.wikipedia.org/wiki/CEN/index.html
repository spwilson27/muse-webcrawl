CEN

CEN most commonly refers to the European Committee for Standardization (French: Comité européen de normalisation), one of the three European Standardization Organizations recognized by the European Union. The abbreviation may also refer to other topics.

Organisations

  • European Committee for Standardization (CEN), the Brussels-based body that develops European Standards (ENs) with its national member bodies
  • Centre d'études nucléaires and other French research institutes
  • Corporación Estatal Petrolera Ecuatoriana and other state enterprises

Other uses

  • Cen (surname), a Chinese surname
  • CEN, a railway station code and airport-related codes in various countries
  • Cen., an abbreviation for century in some citation styles

The European Committee for Standardization was founded in 1961 and, together with CENELEC (electrotechnical standardization) and ETSI (telecommunications), forms the European standardization system. Its members are the national standardization bodies of 34 European countries.
