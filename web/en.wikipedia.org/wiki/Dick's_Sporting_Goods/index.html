Dick's Sporting Goods, Inc. is an American sporting goods retail company based in Coraopolis, Pennsylvania. The company was established by Richard "Dick" Stack in 1948 and today operates several hundred stores across the United States, making it one of the largest sporting goods retailers in the country.

History

In 1948, 18-year-old Dick Stack, working in an Army surplus store in Binghamton, New York, was asked to prepare a list of fishing tackle products for a new line; when his suggestions were dismissed by the store's owner, his grandmother gave him $300 from her cookie jar savings to open his own bait and tackle shop. The business expanded into general sporting goods over the following decades. Edward W. Stack, Dick's son, took over in 1984 and led the company's rapid expansion beyond upstate New York.

Operations

The company operates stores under several banners, including Dick's Sporting Goods, Golf Galaxy and Field & Stream, as well as the experiential Dick's House of Sport concept stores. In 2018, following the Stoneman Douglas High School shooting, the company announced it would stop selling assault-style rifles and high-capacity magazines and would not sell firearms to anyone under 21.
