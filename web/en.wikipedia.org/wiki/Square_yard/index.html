Square yard

The square yard (abbreviated sq yd or yd²) is an imperial and US customary unit of area, defined as the area of a square with sides of one yard (three feet) in length. One square yard equals nine square feet, or 1,296 square inches, or approximately 0.8361 square metres.

Conversions

One square yard is equal to:

  • 9 square feet
  • 1,296 square inches
  • 0.83612736 square metres (exactly, given the international yard of 0.9144 m)
  • 1/4,840 of an acre

Usage

The square yard is used in the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom for measuring medium-sized areas such as rooms, and traditionally for pricing carpet, flooring, and other floor coverings, which are commonly sold by the square yard. It is also used for some land measurements and for materials such as concrete and topsoil surfaces, though the square metre is now preferred in most countries that have adopted the metric system.
