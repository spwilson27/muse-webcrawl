Spotify is a Swedish audio-streaming service founded in 2006 and launched in 2008. It gives access to a large catalogue of recorded music, podcasts and audiobooks from record labels and media companies, on a freemium model: a free ad-supported tier and a paid Premium tier without ads and with offline listening.

Spotify became the largest music-streaming service by subscribers, popularised playlist culture (such as Discover Weekly and personalised mixes), and has been debated over royalty payments to artists. It competes with services including YouTube Music.
