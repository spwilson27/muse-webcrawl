Big Muff

ManufacturerElectro-Harmonix
TypeFuzz / distortion pedal
Introducedc. 1970

The Big Muff Pi is a fuzz distortion effects pedal produced by the Electro-Harmonix company of New York City. Known for its thick, sustaining, violin-like distortion, it became a defining sound of alternative rock, grunge and shoegaze.

History

Designed by Mike Matthews and Bob Myer around 1970, the Big Muff uses a four-transistor circuit with two clipping stages. A Soviet-made version was produced in Russia in the 1990s by Sovtek. Numerous variants exist, including the Ram's Head, Triangle and Op-Amp versions.

Users

Notable users include David Gilmour, Carlos Santana, J Mascis of Dinosaur Jr., Billy Corgan of the Smashing Pumpkins and Jack White. Its scooped-midrange sustain is a staple of heavy guitar tones.
