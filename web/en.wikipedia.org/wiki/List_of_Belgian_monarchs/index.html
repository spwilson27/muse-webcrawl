List of Belgian monarchs

This is a list of the monarchs of Belgium since the country's independence in 1830. Belgium is a constitutional monarchy; its rulers bear the title King (or Queen) of the Belgians, indicating a popular monarchy tied to the people rather than the territory. All Belgian monarchs have belonged to the House of Saxe-Coburg and Gotha (later called the House of Belgium).

Leopold III1934–1951Abdicated after the Royal Question

Albert II1993–2013Abdicated in favour of his son
Philippe2013–presentCurrent King of the Belgians

Regents

Before Leopold I took the oath in 1831, Erasme Louis Surlet de Chokier served as regent. Prince Charles, Count of Flanders, served as regent from 1944 to 1950 during the Royal Question.
