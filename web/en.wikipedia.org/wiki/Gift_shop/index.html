Gift shop

A gift shop or souvenir shop is a store primarily selling souvenirs, memorabilia, and other items relating to a particular topic or theme. The items often include coffee mugs, stuffed animals, toys, t-shirts, postcards, and handmade collections.

Locations

Gift shops are commonly found at tourist attractions such as museums, zoos, aquariums, national parks, historic sites, airports, and hotels. Museum shops often sell reproductions, books, and educational items related to their collections, providing an important source of revenue for nonprofit institutions.

History

The trade in souvenirs grew with mass tourism in the nineteenth century, when railways and steamships made travel widely accessible. Resorts and exhibitions sold commemorative items, and the modern gift shop developed alongside the museum and heritage industries in the twentieth century.

Operations

Gift shops typically stock a mix of locally made goods and mass-produced memorabilia. Pricing frequently carries a premium over comparable items elsewhere, reflecting the captive audience of visitors and the commemorative value of the purchases.
