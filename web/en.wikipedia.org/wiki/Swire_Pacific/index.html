Swire Pacific

Swire Pacific Limited (Chinese: 太古股份有限公司) is a Hong Kong-based conglomerate and the publicly traded arm of the British-headquartered Swire Group. Founded on the Swire trading business established by John Samuel Swire in Liverpool in 1816 and in Shanghai and Hong Kong in the 1860s–70s, Swire Pacific is listed on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong and is controlled by the Swire family through John Swire & Sons.

Businesses

The group's core businesses are organised into divisions: property (through its majority stake in Swire Properties, developer of Pacific Place and Taikoo Place in Hong Kong), aviation (it is the largest shareholder of Cathay Pacific Airways, and owns HAECO, the aircraft engineering group), beverages (Swire Coca-Cola, one of the largest Coca-Cola bottlers in the world, with franchises in mainland China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Vietnam, Cambodia and the western United States), marine services, and trading and industrial operations, including a significant shareholding in Hong Kong Aircraft Engineering and interests in retail, food and automotive distribution.
