A&M Records was an American record label founded as an independent company by Herb Alpert and Jerry Moss in 1962. The initials stand for the founders' surnames, Alpert and Moss. The label became known for artists such as the Carpenters, Cat Stevens, Peter Frampton, Sting and The Police, Janet Jackson, and Sheryl Crow.

History

Alpert and Moss formed the label after the success of Alpert's instrumental recordings with the Tijuana Brass. A&M operated from the historic Charlie Chaplin Studios lot in Hollywood. In 1989 the company was sold to PolyGram, and in 1999, following PolyGram's merger into Universal Music Group, the label was folded into Interscope Geffen A&M Records, though the A&M name has continued in various forms.

Notable artists

  • The Carpenters
  • Peter Frampton, whose Frampton Comes Alive! (1976) was one of the best-selling live albums of its era
  • The Police and Sting
