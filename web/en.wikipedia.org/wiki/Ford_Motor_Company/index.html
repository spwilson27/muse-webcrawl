Ford Motor Company

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

[Image: A blue Ford F-150 pickup truck on a road]
A Ford F-Series pickup.

The Ford Motor Company is an American multinational automobile manufacturer founded by Henry Ford in 1903 and headquartered in Dearborn, Michigan. Ford revolutionised manufacturing with the moving assembly line for the Model T (1908), which put car ownership within reach of ordinary families. Today Ford is among the world\'s largest automakers, known for the F-Series pickup — America\'s best-selling vehicle line for decades — the Mustang, and a major pivot toward electric vehicles under its Ford+ plan.

History

After two failed ventures, Ford Motor Company was incorporated in 1903; the Model T (1908–27, over 15 million built) and the Highland Park and River Rouge plants defined mass production (\'Fordism\'). The $5 day (1914) doubled wages to reduce turnover. Ford built aircraft and jeeps in the Second World War and launched icons such as the Thunderbird, Mustang (1964) and F-Series trucks.

Modern era

Ford survived the 2008 financial crisis without a government bailout (unlike GM and Chrysler), mortgaging assets beforehand under CEO Alan Mulally. In the 2020s it split into Ford Blue (combustion) and Ford Model e (EVs), building the Mustang Mach-E and F-150 Lightning while investing heavily in batteries and software.

Global operations

Ford sells in over 100 markets, with major operations in Europe, China and South America. Its performance arm races from NASCAR to Formula 1 (returning in 2026 with Red Bull Powertrains) and the World Rally Championship.
