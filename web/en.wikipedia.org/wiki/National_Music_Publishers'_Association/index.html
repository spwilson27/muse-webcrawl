National Music Publishers' Association (NMPA)
Type: Trade association
Founded: 1917
Headquarters: Washington, D.C., United States
Focus: Music publishing and songwriter rights

The National Music Publishers' Association (NMPA) is a trade association for the American music publishing industry. Founded in 1917, it represents music publishers and their songwriting partners, working to protect and promote copyright interests in music.

Activities

The NMPA lobbies on copyright legislation, participates in rate proceedings before the Copyright Royalty Board, and pursues litigation and licensing settlements involving mechanical and digital royalties. It has negotiated landmark settlements with digital services and platforms over the use of music in streaming, video and social media services, and it works against online piracy and unlicensed lyric and sheet-music sites.

Members and affiliations

Its members range from the major global music publishing companies to small independent publishers. The NMPA works closely with the Harry Fox Agency, historically its licensing subsidiary for mechanical rights, and publishes industry revenue statistics and advocacy on behalf of songwriters.
