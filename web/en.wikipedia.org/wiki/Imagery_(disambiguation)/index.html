Imagery (disambiguation)

Imagery may refer to several concepts:

Uses

  • Imagery (literature) — descriptive language that appeals to the senses in literary works.
  • Mental imagery — quasi-perceptual experience in the absence of stimuli, such as visualising a scene.
  • Satellite imagery and remote sensing — images of Earth or other planets collected by imaging satellites.
  • Medical imagery — see medical imaging.
  • Guided imagery — a mind-body intervention using visualisation for relaxation or therapy.
