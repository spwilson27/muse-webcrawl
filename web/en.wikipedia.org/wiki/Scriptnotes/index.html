Scriptnotes

Scriptnotes is a weekly podcast about screenwriting and the film industry, hosted by screenwriters John August and Craig Mazin. Launched in 2011, the show discusses craft topics — structure, dialogue, character — alongside the business of film and television, and answers listener questions.

Format

A recurring feature is the "Three Page Challenge", in which the hosts critique the opening three pages of listener-submitted screenplays. The hosts also analyse current industry news, from guild negotiations to box-office trends, drawing on their own credits: August wrote Big Fish and Charlie and the Chocolate Factory; Mazin wrote comedies before creating the HBO series Chernobyl and co-creating The Last of Us.

Influence

Scriptnotes is among the most influential screenwriting podcasts, frequently recommended to aspiring writers, and its transcripts and live shows have made it a durable reference on professional writing practice.
