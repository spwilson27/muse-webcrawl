Vocaloid

Vocaloid (stylised VOCALOID) is a singing voice synthesiser technology and software developed by the Yamaha Corporation. It enables users to synthesise singing by typing lyrics and a melody: a "voice bank" recorded from a human singer is sliced into phonetic fragments and reassembled to produce a vocal performance. Commercial voice banks are marketed with illustrated character personas, most famously Hatsune Miku.

Technology

The Vocaloid engine works by concatenative synthesis in the frequency domain: a voice provider records a script of phonemes and transitions, which are analysed and stored as a library. When the user enters lyrics and pitch, the software selects and blends fragments, applying pitch and dynamics curves, vibrato and expression parameters drawn in the editor's piano-roll-like interface. Successive versions — Vocaloid 2 (2007), 3 (2011), 4 (2014) and 5 (2018) — added realism, cross-synthesis (growl, blending voices) and additional languages.

History and culture

The technology grew out of a Yamaha research project (codenamed "Daisy") with Pompeu Fabra University in Spain. The first products, Leon and Lola (English) and Meiko and Kaito (Japanese), appeared in 2004. The breakthrough came in 2007 when Crypton Future Media released Hatsune Miku for Vocaloid 2 with a character design by Kei Garō; user-generated songs exploded on the video site Nico Nico Douga, and Miku became a virtual idol performing "live" concerts via projection, with a vast fan-created repertoire. Vocaloid culture — producers such as wowaka, Kenshi Yonezu (as Hachi) and Deco*27 among them — has been a major strand of Japanese internet music, and songs written for Vocaloid voices frequently chart in Japan.

Later developments

Competing and successor synthesisers, including UTAU, CeVIO and AI-based systems such as Synthesizer V, expanded the field; Crypton moved its characters to its own Piapro Studio engine in 2019 while Yamaha continued Vocaloid with new releases. In 2024 Yamaha released Vocaloid 6-era developments emphasising AI voice banks.
