Shizuka Minamoto, known simply as Shizuka, is a fictional character in the Japanese manga and anime series Doraemon, created by Fujiko Fujio. She is the main female character of the series and the love interest of the protagonist, Nobita Nobi.

Character

Shizuka is depicted as a kind, gentle and studious girl who lives in Nobita's neighbourhood and attends the same school. She is talented at the violin — though her playing is comically terrible in the stories — enjoys baking, and is famous for her frequent baths, a running gag in which Nobita accidentally intrudes via Doraemon's gadgets. Unlike her boisterous friends Gian and Suneo, Shizuka is compassionate and often defends Nobita.

Role in the series

Future storylines consistently show that Shizuka eventually marries Nobita despite his flaws, a destiny Doraemon's intervention helps secure. She appears in virtually all Doraemon films and media, and is one of the most recognisable characters in Japanese popular culture.
