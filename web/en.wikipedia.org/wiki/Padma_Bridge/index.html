Padma Bridge

The Padma Multipurpose Bridge, commonly known as the Padma Bridge, is a two-level road-rail bridge across the Padma River in Bangladesh. It is the longest bridge in Bangladesh and the longest over the Ganges (in terms of both span and total length). It was inaugurated on 25 June 2022.

CrossesPadma River
LocaleLouhajong, Munshiganj – Zajira, Shariatpur, Bangladesh
Total length6.15 km (3.82 mi)
Opened25 June 2022
CarriesRoad vehicles (upper deck), railway (lower deck)

Design and construction

The bridge is a steel truss structure with a concrete deck, carried on deep pile foundations driven into the riverbed — among the deepest piling of any bridge in the world. The upper level carries a four-lane highway and the lower level a single-track railway. Construction was carried out by China Major Bridge Engineering Company, after Bangladesh decided to finance the project from its own resources following the withdrawal of the World Bank in 2012 over corruption allegations that were not substantiated in court.

Significance

The bridge connects the south-western districts of Bangladesh directly with Dhaka and the east of the country, replacing long ferry crossings. It is expected to raise national GDP growth by roughly one percentage point and to transform transport to the port of Mongla.
