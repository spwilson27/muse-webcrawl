Marcus Johnson

Johnson, American jazz musician and entrepreneur

Marcus Johnson is the name of several notable people. This article concerns Marcus Johnson (born 1971), the American jazz pianist, composer, and entrepreneur from Washington, D.C.

Career

Johnson studied music and law, earning both a law degree and an MBA from Georgetown University; he initially practised law while performing jazz. He founded Marmel Records (later Three Keys Music) and built a career as an independent smooth-jazz artist, releasing a steady series of albums beginning in the late 1990s, including Lessons in Love and the charting Phoenix (2007). His live-album project In Person: Live at Blues Alley and holiday and tribute releases widened his audience.

Entrepreneurship

Beyond recording, Johnson became known for combining music with business ventures. He founded FLO Brands (For the Love Of), a lifestyle company whose FLO Wine label markets wines paired with his music, and has written and lectured on independent artistry and brand building. He has hosted radio in the Washington area and taught as an adjunct in music business.
