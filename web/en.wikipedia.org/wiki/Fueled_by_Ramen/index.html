Fueled by Ramen LLC is an American record label owned by Atlantic Records, part of the Warner Music Group. The label was founded in Gainesville, Florida, in 1996 by John Janick and Vinnie Fiorello, drummer of the ska punk band Less Than Jake. Its name is a reference to the inexpensive instant ramen noodles on which the founders subsisted after investing their money in the label.

History

Fueled by Ramen grew out of the Florida punk and ska scene of the mid-1990s, releasing early records by acts such as Less Than Jake. In 2004 the label became affiliated with Warner Music Group, operating as an imprint distributed through Atlantic Records. Its commercial breakthrough came with the pop punk and emo wave of the 2000s: Fall Out Boy's From Under the Cork Tree (2005), Panic! at the Disco's A Fever You Can't Sweat Out (2005), and Paramore's Riot! (2007) all achieved major success.

Under Janick's leadership the label became home to a roster that blended alternative rock with pop, later signing acts including fun., Twenty One Pilots, Gym Class Heroes, Cobra Starship, The Academy Is..., and A Day to Remember (via a distribution arrangement). Twenty One Pilots' album Blurryface (2015) became one of the label's biggest sellers. In 2018 the label celebrated milestones including Paramore's continued success and expanded into a broader pop-alternative roster.

Artists

Notable current and former artists include Fall Out Boy, Paramore, Panic! at the Disco, Twenty One Pilots, fun., Gym Class Heroes, Cobra Starship, The Hush Sound, This Providence, Cute Is What We Aim For, and The Cab.
