Franklin half dollar

The Franklin half dollar is a coin that was minted by the United States Mint from 1948 to 1963. The fifty-cent piece pictures Benjamin Franklin on the obverse and the Liberty Bell on the reverse.

Design and history

Designed by John R. Sinnock, the coin was the first regular-issue American coin to depict a non-president, and the first to show Franklin. A small eagle was added to the reverse to satisfy the legal requirement that half dollars display an eagle. The series ended when the Kennedy half dollar was issued in 1964 following President Kennedy's assassination.

Collecting

Coins were struck at Philadelphia, Denver, and San Francisco. Well-struck examples showing "full bell lines" on the Liberty Bell command premiums.
