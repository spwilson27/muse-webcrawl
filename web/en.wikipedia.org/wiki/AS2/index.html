AS2
Full name: Applicability Statement 2
Type: Communications protocol
Standard: RFC 4130

AS2 (Applicability Statement 2) is a specification for transporting data securely and reliably over the Internet, widely used for Electronic Data Interchange (EDI). It is defined in RFC 4130 and uses HTTP, S/MIME encryption and digital signatures, with Message Disposition Notifications (MDNs) as receipts.

Overview

AS2 was popularized by Walmart's requirement that suppliers use it for EDI in the early 2000s. Unlike older value-added networks, it runs over the public internet with encryption and non-repudiation.
