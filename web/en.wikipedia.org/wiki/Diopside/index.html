Diopside

CategoryInosilicate (pyroxene)
FormulaCaMgSi2O6
Hardness5.5 – 6.5 (Mohs)
ColourWhite, yellow, pale to dark green, black
Crystal systemMonoclinic

Diopside is a monoclinic pyroxene mineral with composition CaMgSi2O6. It forms a complete solid solution series with hedenbergite (CaFeSi2O6) and augite, and partial solutions with many other pyroxenes. The name derives from the Greek di, "two", and opsis, "appearance", referring to the two ways its prism can be oriented.

Occurrence

Diopside occurs in mafic and ultramafic igneous rocks such as kimberlite and peridotite, and is abundant in high-grade metamorphosed dolostones and skarns. Chrome diopside, a vivid green chromium-bearing variety, is a characteristic indicator mineral in kimberlite exploration for diamonds.

Varieties and uses

Transparent green chrome diopside and the dark green "black star" variety are cut as gemstones, as is violane, a rare violet manganese-bearing variety from Italy. Diopside is also of geological interest because its presence in mantle xenoliths constrains the depth and temperature at which those rocks formed.
