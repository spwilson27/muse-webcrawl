Antique shop

An antique shop or antiques shop is a retail store specialising in the sale of antiques — collectable objects that are valued because of their age, craftsmanship, rarity or historical interest. Antiques are commonly defined as objects at least 100 years old, though shops also stock later "vintage" and retro goods. Items typically sold include furniture, silver, porcelain, glass, clocks, paintings, jewellery, books and militaria.

Trade

Antique dealers acquire stock from auctions, estate sales, house clearances and private sellers, and may restore items before resale. Antique shops are often clustered in market towns or districts — such as London's Portobello Road or the Lanes of Brighton — and dealers may band together in antique centres or malls where many traders rent space under one roof. The trade is governed by conventions on describing age and authenticity, and international rules, such as CITES, restrict trade in items containing ivory and other protected materials.

Online era

Since the 1990s, online auction sites and marketplaces have shifted much antique dealing to the internet, though physical shops remain important for high-value items that buyers wish to inspect.
