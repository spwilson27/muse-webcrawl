José Millán-Astray y Terreros (1879–1954) was a Spanish general and the founder of the Spanish Legion. A veteran of campaigns in the Philippines and Morocco, he cultivated an image of fearless devotion to a cult of death, having lost an arm and an eye in combat.

Spanish Legion

Modelled in part on the French Foreign Legion, the Spanish Legion (Tercio de Extranjeros) was founded in 1920 with Millán-Astray as its first commander; Francisco Franco served as his deputy. During the Spanish Civil War, Millán-Astray became head of the Nationalists' press and propaganda office. He is remembered for the notorious 1936 confrontation at the University of Salamanca with the philosopher Miguel de Unamuno, during which the cry "¡Muera la inteligencia!" ("Death to intelligence!") was attributed to him or his entourage.
