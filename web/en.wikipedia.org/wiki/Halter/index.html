Halter

A halter is a type of headgear used to lead or tie up livestock and other animals. It fits behind the ears and around the muzzle, and a lead rope is attached to it. Halters are distinct from bridles, which are used for riding and use a bit in the mouth.

Use and construction

Horse halters are commonly made of leather or nylon webbing with metal fittings, adjusted by buckles. They are used for catching, leading, grooming, veterinary care and tying, but should not be left on unattended or turned-out animals because they can snag. Variants include rope halters favoured in natural horsemanship, and halters for cattle, sheep, goats, llamas and dogs.

Other meanings

"Halter" also refers to the halter top, a garment tied behind the neck. For the fly anatomy term, see haltere.
