Overview

Siem Reap is a city in northwestern Cambodia, the capital of Siem Reap Province, best known as the gateway to the ruins of Angkor, the seat of the Khmer Empire from the 9th to the 15th centuries. Its name, "Siam defeated", commemorates a Khmer victory over Ayutthaya (in local tradition).

Today

Tourism around Angkor Archaeological Park transformed the once-sleepy town into Cambodia's fastest-growing city, with hotels along the road to Angkor Wat and the Pub Street nightlife district. The floating villages of Tonlé Sap lake (Kampong Phluk, Chong Kneas) are nearby excursions. Tourism collapsed during the COVID-19 pandemic and has been recovering since.
