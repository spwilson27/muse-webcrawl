Colchester is a city in Essex, England. It is widely claimed to be the oldest recorded town in Britain, mentioned by Pliny the Elder in AD 77. As Camulodunum, it was the first Roman town in Britain and briefly the first Roman capital of the province, before being destroyed in Boudica's revolt in AD 60/61. Colchester was granted city status in 2022 as part of the Platinum Jubilee honours.

History

Roman Camulodunum

Camulodunum was a major stronghold of the Trinovantes and the Catuvellauni; its name derives from the Celtic god of war, Camulus. After the Roman invasion of AD 43, a fortress was established, and the settlement became the first colonia in Britain, Colonia Claudia Victricensis. The Temple of Claudius — the largest classical temple in Britain — stood at its heart. In AD 60 or 61, Boudica's Iceni rebels destroyed the town. Afterwards the town was rebuilt with extensive defensive walls, much of which survive, and a Roman circus — the only known chariot-racing track in Britain — was constructed nearby.

Medieval and later

Colchester Castle, begun around 1076, is the largest Norman keep in England, built partly from reused Roman brick. The town became known for cloth manufacturing, particularly bay and say cloth woven by Dutch and Flemish refugees in the 16th century. During the English Civil War, it endured the Siege of Colchester (1648), after which the Royalist commanders Sir Charles Lucas and Sir George Lisle were executed at the castle. Colchester later became a garrison town, home to Colchester Garrison and the 16 Air Assault Brigade.

Landmarks

  • Colchester Castle — Norman keep housing a museum of the town's Roman and later history.
  • Colchester Zoo — one of the UK's most popular zoos, founded in 1963.
  • The Dutch Quarter — historic streets of Tudor and Georgian merchant houses.
  • University of Essex — founded 1964, with its main campus at Wivenhoe Park.

Transport

The town is served by the A12 London–Ipswich road and by Colchester station on the Great Eastern Main Line, with trains to London Liverpool Street in under an hour.
