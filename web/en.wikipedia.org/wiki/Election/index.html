Election

Election
DefinitionFormal decision-making process by which a population chooses office-holders
TypesGeneral, primary, local, by-elections, referendums
[Image: A polling station with voters queuing in the morning]

An election is a formal group decision-making process by which a population chooses an individual or multiple individuals to hold public office. Elections are the standard mechanism of modern representative democracy (see Democracy in the wider crawl), filling legislatures and executives from villages to the presidency.

Integrity

Free elections require universal suffrage, secret ballots, honest counts and acceptance of results; observers monitor elections worldwide. Campaigning runs on the Television and social channels recreated in this crawl, and misinformation and foreign interference are the era's integrity threats.
