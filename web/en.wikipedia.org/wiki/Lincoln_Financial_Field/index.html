Lincoln Financial Field is an American football stadium in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. It is the home stadium of the Philadelphia Eagles of the National Football League (NFL) and the Temple Owls football team of Temple University. Located in the South Philadelphia Sports Complex, it opened in 2003 to replace Veterans Stadium. Lincoln Financial Group holds the naming rights.

History

Ground was broken in May 2001, and the stadium hosted its first event, a soccer match between Manchester United and FC Barcelona, on August 3, 2003. The Eagles played their first regular-season game there on September 8, 2003, against Tampa Bay. The stadium hosted the Eagles' victory in the NFC Championship Game following the 2017 season on the way to their Super Bowl LII triumph, and again for the 2022 season. It is a regular venue for the Army–Navy game, international soccer matches including the 2016 Copa América Centenario, WrestleMania XL in 2024, and concerts. A major renovation in 2013–2014 added seating, larger video boards, and expanded concourses, while solar panels and wind turbines made it one of the greenest NFL stadiums. The stadium is scheduled to host matches of the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

Features

The seating bowl is designed with the majority of seats along the sidelines, and the north end features an open plaza. Temple University has played its home games at the stadium since 2003.
