Ghostwriter

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia


A ghostwriter produces work credited to another person.

A ghostwriter is a person hired to write literary or journalistic works, speeches, or other texts that are officially credited to another person as the author. Celebrities, executives, and political leaders often hire ghostwriters to produce or edit autobiographies, memoirs, magazine articles, and books. Ghostwriters typically receive either no credit or a limited acknowledgment, and their involvement is often bound by confidentiality agreements.

Role and practice

Ghostwriters generally work from interviews, recordings, and research material supplied by or gathered about the credited author, shaping the material into the credited author's voice and style. In publishing, a ghostwritten book may credit the ghostwriter only as a collaborator ("with..." or "as told to..."), or not at all. Editors at publishing houses sometimes function as partial ghostwriters when they heavily restructure a manuscript.

Fields

Nonfiction and memoirs

A large share of celebrity memoirs and business books are ghostwritten. The practice is considered standard in the industry, though it occasionally draws criticism when the credited author's authorship is publicly emphasized.

Music

In music, the term ghostwriter is used for songwriters who write lyrics or songs credited to performers, a practice especially discussed in hip hop, where writing one's own lyrics is traditionally valued.

Politics

Political speechwriters are a form of ghostwriter, drafting speeches delivered by officeholders. Autobiographies of politicians are also frequently ghostwritten.

Ethics

The ethics of ghostwriting vary by field. It is broadly accepted in commercial publishing and politics, but considered academic dishonesty when used to produce student work, and controversial in journalism when the credited author did not substantially participate. Some ghostwriters have later become authors in their own right or publicly disclosed their roles.

In popular culture

The profession is the subject of the 2010 film The Ghost Writer, directed by Roman Polanski, based on the novel The Ghost by Robert Harris, about a ghostwriter hired to complete the memoirs of a former British prime minister.
