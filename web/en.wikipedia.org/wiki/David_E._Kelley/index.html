David Edward Kelley (born 4 April 1956) is an American television writer and producer, known for creating a string of acclaimed legal and medical dramas including Picket Fences, Chicago Hope, The Practice, Ally McBeal, Boston Public and Boston Legal, and the HBO limited series Big Little Lies.

Career

Kelley, a former Boston lawyer, was hired as a writer on L.A. Law in 1986 and rose to showrunner. He won numerous Emmy Awards; in 1999 his shows The Practice and Ally McBeal won the Emmys for Outstanding Drama Series and Outstanding Comedy Series in the same year, a unique double. His series are noted for quirky characters, courtroom storylines addressing contemporary social issues, and his practice of writing most episodes himself.

Later work

In the streaming era Kelley created or adapted Big Little Lies (2017), The Undoing (2020), Big Sky and Presumed Innocent (2024). He has been married to actress Michelle Pfeiffer since 1993.
