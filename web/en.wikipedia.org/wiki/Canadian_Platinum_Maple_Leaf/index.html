Canadian Platinum Maple Leaf

The Canadian Platinum Maple Leaf is a platinum bullion coin issued by Canada and produced by the Royal Canadian Mint. First issued in 1988, it is struck in 99.95% pure platinum.

Country: Canada
Composition: 99.95% platinum
First issued: 1988

History

The coin was issued from 1988 to 2002 in several denominations, discontinued, and reintroduced in 2009 as a one-ounce coin with a face value of 50 dollars.

Design

Like the gold Maple Leaf, it bears the monarch's portrait on the obverse and a maple leaf on the reverse.
