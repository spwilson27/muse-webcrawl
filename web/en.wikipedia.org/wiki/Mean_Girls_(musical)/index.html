Mean Girls is a musical with a book by Tina Fey, music by Jeff Richmond, and lyrics by Nell Benjamin. Based on the 2004 Paramount Pictures film of the same name, it premiered at the National Theatre in Washington, D.C. in October 2017 and opened on Broadway at the August Wilson Theatre on April 8, 2018, directed and choreographed by Casey Nicholaw.

Synopsis

Cady Heron, raised on an African savanna by scientist parents, moves to suburban Illinois and enters public high school for the first time. She befriends Janis Sarkisian and Damian Hubbard, who introduce her to the school's social order and warn her about the Plastics: Regina George, Gretchen Wieners, and Karen Smith. When the Plastics invite Cady to sit with them, Janis persuades her to infiltrate the group and get revenge for past cruelty. Cady complicates the plan by falling for Regina's ex, Aaron Samuels, and gradually becomes as manipulative as Regina herself. The story culminates with the Burn Book scandal, the Spring Fling dance, and Cady's redemption.

Reneé Rapp later played Regina on Broadway and reprised the role in the 2024 film adaptation.

Productions and reception

The musical received twelve Tony Award nominations in 2018, including Best Musical, though it won none. It closed on March 11, 2020, when Broadway shut down for the COVID-19 pandemic, after 805 regular performances, and did not reopen. A U.S. national tour began in 2019. A revised production opened in London's West End at the Savoy Theatre in June 2024. The musical was adapted into a 2024 feature film.
