List of mountains of the British Isles by height

The following is a list of the highest mountains in the British Isles, ranked by height above sea level. The summit of Ben Nevis in Scotland, at 1,345 metres (4,413 ft), is the highest point in the archipelago. Scottish summits dominate the upper part of the list; the highest peaks outside Scotland are Snowdon (Yr Wyddfa) in Wales at 1,085 m and Carrauntoohil in Ireland at 1,039 m.

Summits over 3,000 ft (914.4 m) in Scotland are called Munros; there are 282 of them. In England and Wales equivalent lists include the Hewitts and Nuttalls, while Ireland uses the Vandeleur-Lynams and Arderins.
