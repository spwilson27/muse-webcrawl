Prince Rahim al-Hussaini (born 12 October 1971), known as Aga Khan V, is the 50th hereditary Imam of the Nizari Ismaili Muslims. He succeeded his father, Prince Karim al-Hussaini, Aga Khan IV, on 5 February 2025, following his father's death in Lisbon on 4 February 2025 and the designation in his father's will.

Early life and work

The eldest son of Aga Khan IV and Princess Salimah, Rahim was educated at Phillips Academy Andover and Brown University, graduating in 1995 in comparative literature. Before his accession he worked for two decades across the Aga Khan Development Network (AKDN), particularly in economic development, microfinance and environmental sustainability: he chaired the AKDN's Environment and Climate Committee and was closely involved with the Aga Khan Agency for Habitat and the network's institutions in Central and South Asia and East Africa. He is based at the Ismaili Imamat's seat in Lisbon, Portugal.

Accession

Under Nizari Ismaili doctrine the Imam designates his successor by nass (explicit designation). Aga Khan IV's will, read in the presence of the family and community leaders on 5 February 2025 in Lisbon, designated Rahim as the 50th Imam. Ceremonies of homage (bay'a) by the worldwide Ismaili community followed. In his first addresses he emphasised continuity with his father's work, climate action, and improving quality of life in the regions where Ismailis live.
