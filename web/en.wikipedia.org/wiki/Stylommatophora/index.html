Stylommatophora

Stylommatophora is an order of air-breathing land snails and slugs, terrestrial pulmonate gastropod molluscs. The great majority of land snails and slugs belong to this group, which is characterised by having eyes at the tips of a pair of retractable tentacles.

KingdomAnimalia
PhylumMollusca
ClassGastropoda
OrderStylommatophora

Anatomy

Stylommatophorans have two pairs of retractable tentacles on the head, with the eyes located at the tips of the longer upper pair — the feature that gives the group its name, from Greek roots meaning "stalk-eye". As pulmonates, they breathe air through a vascularised mantle cavity (a "lung") opening through a pneumostome. They are hermaphroditic, each individual producing both eggs and sperm.

Diversity and habitat

The order contains the familiar garden snails and slugs of the families Helicidae, Limacidae and Arionidae, among many others — tens of thousands of described species distributed on every continent except Antarctica. Most are herbivorous or detritivorous and play important roles in soil ecology; a few are significant agricultural pests or intermediate hosts of parasites.
