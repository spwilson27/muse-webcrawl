Directorate of Film Festivals

The Directorate of Film Festivals (DFF) was an organisation of the Government of India, under the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, established in 1973 to promote Indian cinema. It was best known for organising the National Film Awards and the International Film Festival of India (IFFI) in Goa, as well as India's participation in film festivals abroad and the organisation of foreign film festivals and retrospectives in India.

Activities

The Directorate administered the annual National Film Awards, including the Dadasaheb Phalke Award, and organised the Indian Panorama section of IFFI. It also managed cultural exchange programmes under which film weeks were held in other countries, maintained an archive and publication programme, and coordinated the selection of Indian entries for international festivals.

Merger

As part of a rationalisation of the ministry's media units, the Directorate was merged into the National Film Development Corporation (NFDC); the merger was completed in 2022–2023, after which the NFDC took over its functions, including the National Film Awards and IFFI.
