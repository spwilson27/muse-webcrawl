Melbourne Cup hurdle

The Melbourne Cup hurdle is a term associated with jumps racing held in Melbourne, Australia, and in particular the hurdle races run during the Melbourne spring racing period at courses such as Flemington and Sandown, where the famous flat Melbourne Cup is the centrepiece of the carnival.

Jumps racing in Victoria

Hurdle races are contests in which horses jump a series of lower, brush-topped obstacles, distinct from the taller fences of steeplechases. Victoria and South Australia are the only Australian states where jumps racing has continued into the modern era, and its feature events include the Grand Annual Steeplechase at Warrnambool and the Grand National Hurdle.

Debate

Jumps racing in Australia has been controversial because of horse falls and fatalities, leading to reviews, safety modifications and periodic threats of prohibition, balanced against its long tradition and regional employment.
