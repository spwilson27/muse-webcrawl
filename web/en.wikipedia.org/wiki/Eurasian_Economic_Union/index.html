Eurasian Economic Union

Founded: 1 January 2015

Members: Armenia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Russia

Headquarters: Moscow (Commission)

The Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU) is an economic union of post-Soviet states providing free movement of goods, services, capital and labour, with coordinated policies in areas such as customs and transport.

History

The idea was proposed by Kazakhstan's president Nursultan Nazarbayev in 1994. It grew from the Eurasian Economic Community and the 2010 customs union of Russia, Belarus and Kazakhstan. The founding treaty was signed in Astana in May 2014; Armenia and Kyrgyzstan joined in 2015.

Institutions

Bodies include the Supreme Eurasian Economic Council (heads of state), the Eurasian Economic Commission in Moscow, and a court in Minsk. Russia accounts for the great majority of the union's population and GDP.

Relations

The EAEU has signed trade agreements with countries including Vietnam, Iran and Serbia. It developed from the looser Commonwealth of Independent States.
