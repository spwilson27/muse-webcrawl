Danjaq

Danjaq, LLC is an American film production company and holding company responsible for the copyright and trademarks of the characters, elements, and other material associated with the James Bond film series. Together with its subsidiary Eon Productions, it has controlled the production of the official Bond films since the series began.

History

Danjaq was founded in 1962 by Albert R. Broccoli and Harry Saltzman, around the time of the first Bond film, Dr. No. Its name combines the names of Broccoli's and Saltzman's wives, Dana and Jacqueline. Saltzman sold his share of the company to United Artists in the mid-1970s, after which the Broccoli family exercised sole control. Following Albert R. Broccoli's death in 1996, production passed to his daughter Barbara Broccoli and stepson Michael G. Wilson.

Role in the franchise

Through Eon Productions, based at Pinewood Studios in England, Danjaq produces the official James Bond films, which are distributed in partnership with Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer (and, internationally, other distributors). The company jealously guards the Bond intellectual property and has been involved in notable legal disputes, including with Kevin McClory over the rights to Thunderball and the SPECTRE organisation — issues ultimately resolved in 2013.
