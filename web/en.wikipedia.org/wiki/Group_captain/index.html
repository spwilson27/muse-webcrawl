Group captain

Group captain is a senior commissioned officer rank in the Royal Air Force and in the air forces of many Commonwealth countries. It has a NATO rank code of OF-5 and is equivalent to a colonel in the British Army or a captain in the Royal Navy. The rank is immediately above wing commander and below air commodore.

History

The rank was introduced on 1 April 1919 with the formation of the RAF's own rank structure. Its name derives from the officer's typical command of a "group" of stations or squadrons. Group captains commonly command RAF stations.

Insignia

The insignia consists of four broad bands of black braid with sky-blue stripes worn on the sleeve or shoulder. Famous holders include Leonard Cheshire and Douglas Bader, both of whom held the rank during the Second World War.
