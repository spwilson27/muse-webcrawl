List of works by Michelangelo

The following is a list of works of painting, sculpture and architecture by the Italian Renaissance artist Michelangelo Buonarroti (1475–1564). Michelangelo considered himself primarily a sculptor; his paintings include two of the most famous works in Western art, the Sistine Chapel ceiling and The Last Judgment.
