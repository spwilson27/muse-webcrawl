Final Doom is a 1996 first-person shooter game developed and published by id Software. It consists of two 32-level megawads for Doom II, TNT: Evilution and The Plutonia Experiment, and is an official, commercially released sequel to Doom II running on the same engine.

Contents

TNT: Evilution was created by the fan group TeamTNT, whose free megawad project was acquired by id Software shortly before release. In it, the UAC experiments with teleportation technology on Io, only to have its base overrun by a demon "ship" and its forces. The Plutonia Experiment, created by brothers Dario and Milo Casali, sends the player to stop a demonic invasion after UAC experiments open gates to Hell; it is noted for its very high difficulty, with dense placement of powerful monsters.

Gameplay

Gameplay is identical to Doom II: the player fights demons through maze-like levels with the same weapons and enemies, with new levels, textures, and music providing the novelty. No new gameplay mechanics were added.

Release and reception

The game was released for MS-DOS in June 1996 and later ported to PlayStation (combining levels with Master Levels for Doom II) and other platforms. It has been re-released in various Doom collections and digital storefronts.
