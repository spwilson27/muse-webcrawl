Tokimeki Memorial (Japanese: ときめきメモリアル, "Heartbeat Memorial") is a dating simulation video game series developed and published by Konami. The first game, released in 1994 for the PC Engine Super CD-ROM², is credited with popularising the dating sim genre; its lead heroine, Shiori Fujisaki, became one of the most famous virtual characters in Japan.

Gameplay

The player controls a male student at Kirameki High School over three school years, balancing study, sports, clubs and social activities to raise parameters such as academics, appearance and fitness, while meeting and dating a cast of female students. The goal is to receive a confession of love under the school's legendary "Tree of Legend" at graduation. The series is non-erotic, focusing on school-life romance and stat management.

Reception and legacy

The original game was ported to many platforms, including PlayStation, Sega Saturn and Super Famicom, and sold over a million copies. It spawned sequels, spin-offs (including the Girl's Side series aimed at female players), drama CDs, anime adaptations and extensive merchandise. Shiori Fujisaki became a cultural phenomenon, appearing in advertisements and other Konami games.
