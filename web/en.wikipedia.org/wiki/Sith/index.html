The Sith are an order of Force users in the Star Wars universe who draw on the dark side of the Force, driven by passion, ambition and the pursuit of power. They are the principal antagonists of the Jedi, whom they regard as weak for suppressing emotion. Notable Sith include Darth Vader, Darth Sidious (Emperor Palpatine) and Darth Maul.

History in the fiction

In Star Wars lore the Sith originated with Jedi who embraced the dark side and were exiled, settling among the Sith species of Korriban. Millennia of warfare with the Jedi culminated in the apparent destruction of the order; Darth Bane then instituted the Rule of Two, under which only a master and an apprentice exist at any time, working in secret.

Philosophy

The Sith Code proclaims: 'Peace is a lie, there is only passion.' Where the Jedi seek detachment and harmony, the Sith channel fear, anger and hatred as sources of strength, and characteristically bear the title Darth and wield red-bladed lightsabers.

Appearances

The Sith first appeared on screen in the original trilogy through Darth Vader and the Emperor, and were greatly expanded in the prequel films, animated series, novels and games of the expanded universe.
