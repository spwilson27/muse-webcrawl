Bellator MMA

Founded: 2008

Founder: Bjorn Rebney

Headquarters: Hollywood, California (later Santa Monica)

Acquired by: Professional Fighters League (2023)

Bellator MMA (formerly Bellator Fighting Championships) was an American mixed martial arts promotion founded in 2008 by Bjorn Rebney. For most of its existence it was regarded as the second-largest MMA promotion in the United States, behind the UFC. Its name derives from the Latin word bellator, meaning "warrior".

History

Bellator originally distinguished itself with a season-long tournament format, in which fighters competed in single-elimination brackets for a title shot — promoted with the slogan that title shots were "earned, not given". Events aired first on ESPN Deportes and later on MTV2 and Spike TV (Paramount Network). Viacom acquired a controlling stake in 2011. In 2014, Rebney was replaced by former Strikeforce founder Scott Coker, who moved the promotion away from rigid tournaments toward more traditional matchmaking, star-driven cards, and grand prix tournaments.

Bellator held events internationally, crowned champions such as Patrício "Pitbull" Freire, Michael Chandler, Ilima-Lei Macfarlane, Ryan Bader, and Cris Cyborg, and staged notable crossover events with Japan's Rizin FF. In November 2023 the Professional Fighters League (PFL) acquired Bellator; its roster was folded into the PFL, and the Bellator brand was retired in early 2025.
