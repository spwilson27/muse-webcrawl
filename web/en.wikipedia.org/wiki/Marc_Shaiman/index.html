Marc Shaiman

Marc Shaiman (born October 22, 1959) is an American composer and lyricist for films, television and theatre. He is known for his collaborations with director Rob Reiner and with actor and writer Billy Crystal, and for co-writing the Broadway musical Hairspray with Scott Wittman.

Career

Shaiman began his career in New York, working as a musical director and arranger, including for Bette Midler. He moved into film scoring in the late 1980s and 1990s with scores for films such as When Harry Met Sally... (1989), Misery (1990), Sister Act (1992), Sleepless in Seattle (1993), The American President (1995), The First Wives Club (1996) and South Park: Bigger, Longer & Uncut (1999).

On Broadway, Shaiman and Wittman wrote the music and lyrics for Hairspray (2002), which won the Tony Award for Best Musical, and later collaborated on Catch Me If You Can (2011), Charlie and the Chocolate Factory (2013) and Some Like It Hot (2022). For television, the pair contributed songs to the series Smash.

Shaiman has been nominated for multiple Academy Awards, including for his scores to Sleepless in Seattle, The American President, The First Wives Club, Patch Adams and Mary Poppins Returns (2018), and he has won Tony, Grammy and Emmy awards, making him one of a small number of artists to have won all three.
