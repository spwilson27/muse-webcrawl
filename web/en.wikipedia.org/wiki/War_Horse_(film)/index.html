War Horse (film)

War Horse is a 2011 American war drama film directed and produced by Steven Spielberg, from a screenplay by Lee Hall and Richard Curtis. It is based on Michael Morpurgo's 1982 novel of the same name and its 2007 stage adaptation. The film follows Joey, a horse bought by the British Army at the outbreak of the First World War, and his young owner Albert Narracott, who enlists to find him.

Plot and production

Set in Devon and on the battlefields of France, the film traces Joey's journey through the hands of British and German soldiers and a French farmer and his granddaughter. It was filmed in England in 2010 and released to generally positive reviews, grossing about $177 million worldwide.

Accolades

The film was nominated for six Academy Awards, including Best Picture, and won the American Film Institute's award for movie of the year.
