Atlanta United FC

Founded:2014
Stadium:Mercedes-Benz Stadium, Atlanta, Georgia
Owner:Arthur Blank
League:Major League Soccer (Eastern Conference)

Atlanta United FC is an American professional soccer club based in Atlanta, Georgia, that competes in Major League Soccer (MLS) as a member of the league's Eastern Conference. The club was founded in 2014 and joined MLS as an expansion team in 2017.

History

Home Depot co-founder and Atlanta Falcons owner Arthur Blank was awarded the franchise in April 2014. Under first head coach Gerardo "Tata" Martino and stars such as Josef Martínez and Miguel Almirón, Atlanta immediately became one of MLS's best-supported clubs, breaking league attendance records. In only its second season the club defeated the Portland Timbers 2–0 to win MLS Cup 2018 before a crowd of 73,019.

Honours

The club won the MLS Cup in 2018, the U.S. Open Cup and the Campeones Cup in 2019. Its reserve team, Atlanta United 2, and academy have been prominent in developing American talent.

Supporters

Atlanta United's supporters' groups — including Terminus Legion, the Resurgence and Footie Mob — are known for the "tifo" displays and the golden spike ceremony held before matches.
