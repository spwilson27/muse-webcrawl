Disney Channel is an American pay television channel that serves as the flagship property of Disney Branded Television, a unit of Disney Entertainment, a division of The Walt Disney Company. Launched on April 18, 1983, as a premium channel, it later shifted to basic cable and now targets children and pre-teens, particularly viewers aged 6–14, with preschool programming airing under the Disney Junior block.

Programming

The channel is known for its original live-action sitcoms and animated series, many of which launched major entertainment careers. Live-action successes include Lizzie McGuire, That's So Raven, The Suite Life of Zack & Cody, Hannah Montana, Wizards of Waverly Place, Good Luck Charlie, and Jessie; animated hits include Kim Possible, Phineas and Ferb, Gravity Falls, Star vs. the Forces of Evil, Amphibia, and The Owl House. Its Disney Channel Original Movies — more than one hundred since the 1990s — include the High School Musical, Camp Rock, Descendants, and Zombies franchises, with High School Musical 2 (2007) drawing 17.2 million viewers, a record for basic cable at the time.

International versions and decline of linear TV

Disney Channel expanded worldwide from the mid-1990s, reaching dozens of local feeds. Since 2019, as Disney prioritized the Disney+ streaming service, many international Disney Channel feeds have closed and programming shifted online; the US channel continues as a basic cable network alongside Disney Junior and Disney XD.
