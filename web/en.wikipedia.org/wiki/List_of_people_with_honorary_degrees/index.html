List of people with honorary degrees

An honorary degree is an academic degree for which a university has waived the usual requirements, conferred honoris causa ("for the sake of honour") to recognise distinguished achievement. This list records notable recipients who have received honorary doctorates from many institutions.

Revoked and refused degrees

Honorary degrees are occasionally revoked — as happened to several honorees implicated in scandal — and sometimes refused: the University of Oxford's congregation voted in 1985 not to award an honorary degree to Prime Minister Margaret Thatcher, a widely reported break with tradition.
