Robert Cialdini

Robert Beno Cialdini (born April 27, 1945) is an American psychologist and author. He is a professor emeritus of psychology and marketing at Arizona State University and was a visiting professor of marketing and business at Stanford University. He is best known for his 1984 book Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion.

Influence

Influence, based on three years of undercover study among salespeople, fundraisers and advertisers, sets out six principles of persuasion: reciprocity, commitment and consistency, social proof, authority, liking, and scarcity. A seventh principle, unity, was added in the expanded edition Influence, New and Expanded (2021). The book has sold millions of copies and is widely taught in marketing and psychology courses.

Other work

Cialdini's later books include Yes! 50 Scientifically Proven Ways to Be Persuasive (2008, with Noah Goldstein and Steve Martin) and Pre-Suasion: A Revolutionary Way to Influence and Persuade (2016), which argues that persuasion depends heavily on directing attention in the moments before a message is delivered. He has advised organisations and election campaigns on applying principles of ethical persuasion.
