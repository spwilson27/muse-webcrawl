Commercial agriculture

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Commercial agriculture is a large-scale form of agriculture in which crops and livestock are produced primarily for sale in markets rather than for the farmer's own subsistence. It is characterised by high capital investment, mechanisation, purchased inputs such as fertilisers, pesticides and improved seed, hired labour, and specialisation — often in a small number of commodities for regional or global markets. In the classification of farming types used in human geography, commercial agriculture is contrasted with subsistence agriculture and includes intensive and extensive forms.

Types

Commercial grain farming

Extensive production of wheat, maize and other cereals on the mid-latitude plains of North America, Eurasia and Australia, with large fields, heavy machinery and low labour input per hectare.

Plantation agriculture

Tropical and subtropical estates producing cash crops such as tea, coffee, rubber, sugar, bananas and palm oil, historically established under colonial systems and typically reliant on large labour forces and processing facilities on site.

Mixed crop and livestock farming

The dominant commercial system of the eastern United States and western Europe, integrating forage and feed crops with beef, dairy and pig production so that manure and crop residues cycle within the farm.

Mediterranean and market gardening

Mediterranean agriculture specialises in vines, olives, citrus and winter vegetables for distant urban markets; market gardening (truck farming) around cities and in favoured climates such as California and the Netherlands supplies fresh produce, increasingly from large glasshouse and irrigated operations. Commercial ranching and dairying are the livestock analogues.

Impacts

Commercialisation has raised yields and lowered food prices but is criticised for soil erosion, water depletion, pesticide pollution, greenhouse-gas emissions, monoculture vulnerability and pressure on farm labour and smallholders. Responses include precision agriculture, integrated pest management and certification schemes.
