Country: Georgia

Capital: Mtskheta

Area: 6,786 km² (2,620 sq mi)

Municipalities: Akhalgori, Dusheti, Kazbegi, Mtskheta, Tianeti

Mtskheta-Mtianeti (Georgian: მცხეთა-მთიანეთი) is a region (mkhare) in eastern Georgia comprising the ancient capital Mtskheta and the mountainous historical provinces of Mtianeti, Khevi, Khevsureti and Pshavi. Its administrative centre is the city of Mtskheta. The region borders Russia to the north along the ridge of the Greater Caucasus.

Geography

The region stretches from the outskirts of Tbilisi northward to the Caucasus watershed. It includes the Georgian Military Road corridor through Kazbegi municipality, with the town of Stepantsminda beneath Mount Kazbek (5,047 m), and the remote highland valleys of Khevsureti and Pshavi, famous for their medieval defensive tower villages such as Shatili and Mutso.

Subdivisions

Mtskheta-Mtianeti is divided into five municipalities: Akhalgori (Leningori; partly outside Georgian government control since 2008), Dusheti, Kazbegi, Mtskheta and Tianeti. The region contains the UNESCO-listed Historical Monuments of Mtskheta and numerous monasteries, fortresses and alpine settlements, making it one of Georgia's main destinations for cultural and mountain tourism.
