Microsoft 365, launched in 2011 as Office 365, is a subscription service from Microsoft bundling productivity applications and cloud services. It includes the Office applications (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and others), the OneDrive cloud storage service, Microsoft Teams for collaboration, and, in business plans, Exchange Online and SharePoint Online.

History

Office 365 was introduced in 2010 and released in June 2011, initially aimed at business and enterprise customers as the successor to Microsoft's Business Productivity Online Suite. Consumer subscription plans followed in 2013 alongside Office 2013. In April 2020 Microsoft renamed the consumer and small-business plans from Office 365 to Microsoft 365, a name previously used for an enterprise bundle, and the whole product line subsequently adopted the Microsoft 365 name.

Plans and features

Plans are sold for personal, family, business and enterprise use, with per-user monthly or annual subscriptions. Subscribers install desktop applications on multiple devices, receive 1 TB of OneDrive storage per user in most plans, and get continuous feature updates rather than periodic major releases. Later additions include the Copilot AI assistant, offered as an add-on and in premium tiers.

Reception

The subscription model shifted Microsoft from one-time licence sales to recurring revenue and has hundreds of millions of users across consumer and commercial plans.
