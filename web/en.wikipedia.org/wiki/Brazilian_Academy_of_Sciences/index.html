The Brazilian Academy of Sciences (Portuguese: Academia Brasileira de Ciências, ABC) is the national academy of Brazil. It is headquartered in the city of Rio de Janeiro and was founded on 3 May 1916. It plays a role in promoting scientific research and advising the Brazilian government on science policy.

History and organisation

Founded as the Brazilian Society of Sciences (Sociedade Brasileira de Sciências), it took its present name in 1921. Members are elected in ten sections covering mathematical, physical, chemical, earth, biological, biomedical, health, agrarian, engineering and human/social sciences, plus corresponding foreign members and affiliates (younger researchers). The Academy publishes the journal Anais da Academia Brasileira de Ciências (Annals), founded in 1929 and one of Brazil's oldest scientific periodicals.

Activities

The ABC organises symposia, produces policy reports on topics from Amazon science to science education, and represents Brazil in international bodies such as the InterAcademy Partnership and TWAS (The World Academy of Sciences), whose secretariat cooperation it has long supported.
