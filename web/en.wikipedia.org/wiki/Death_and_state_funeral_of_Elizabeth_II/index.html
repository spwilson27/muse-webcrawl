Death and state funeral of Elizabeth II

Date of death: 8 September 2022
Place: Balmoral Castle, Scotland
State funeral: 19 September 2022, Westminster Abbey
Burial: King George VI Memorial Chapel, Windsor

Elizabeth II, Queen of the United Kingdom and the other Commonwealth realms, died on 8 September 2022 at Balmoral Castle in Scotland, aged 96, after a reign of 70 years and 214 days — the longest of any British monarch. Her death set in motion long-standing plans codenamed Operation London Bridge (and, for Scotland, Operation Unicorn). She was succeeded by her eldest son, Charles III.

Lying in state and funeral

The Queen's coffin rested first at St Giles' Cathedral in Edinburgh, where the King and his siblings held a vigil, and was then flown to London to lie in state in Westminster Hall. An enormous queue — at its peak some 25 miles of waiting mourners, with waits of up to 24 hours — formed to file past. The state funeral at Westminster Abbey on 19 September 2022 was attended by some 2,000 guests, including hundreds of heads of state and government, and was widely described as the largest ceremonial event in modern British history; an estimated audience of hundreds of millions watched worldwide. A national holiday was declared in the United Kingdom.

Committal and burial

After the funeral, the coffin was taken in procession to Wellington Arch and by hearse to Windsor, where a committal service was held in St George's Chapel. In a moment of high symbolism, the crown, orb and sceptre were removed from the coffin and the Lord Chamberlain broke his wand of office. The Queen was interred privately that evening in the King George VI Memorial Chapel alongside her husband, Prince Philip, her parents and her sister.
