Solid Snake

Solid Snake (real name David) is a fictional character and the principal protagonist of Konami's Metal Gear series of stealth games, created by Hideo Kojima. A former Green Beret and operative of the special forces unit FOXHOUND, Snake is a clone of the legendary soldier Big Boss, created in the fictional "Les Enfants Terribles" project alongside his twin Liquid Snake and later Solidus Snake.

Appearances

Snake debuted in Metal Gear (1987), infiltrating the fortress Outer Heaven to destroy the walking nuclear tank Metal Gear. He returned in Metal Gear 2: Solid Snake (1990) and achieved wide fame in Metal Gear Solid (1998), set on Alaska's Shadow Moses Island, where he confronts his twin Liquid. In Metal Gear Solid 2: Sons of Liberty (2001) he appears as a supporting character and co-founder, with Otacon, of the anti–Metal Gear group Philanthropy. In Metal Gear Solid 4: Guns of the Patriots (2008), suffering accelerated ageing from his cloning, he undertakes a final mission as "Old Snake".

Characterisation

Snake is depicted as a hardened, laconic soldier, fond of cigarettes and famously fond of hiding in cardboard boxes, yet increasingly weary of war and manipulation by the Patriots, the clandestine system controlling information and politics in the series' world. In English-language versions he is voiced by David Hayter; in Japanese by Akio Ōtsuka.

Reception

Solid Snake is widely regarded as one of the most iconic video game characters, appearing in crossover titles such as Nintendo's Super Smash Bros. Brawl and helping to popularise the stealth game genre.
