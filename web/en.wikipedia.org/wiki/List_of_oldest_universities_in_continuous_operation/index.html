List of oldest universities in continuous operation

This is a list of the oldest universities in continuous operation in the world. Inclusion depends on the definition of a university. The University of al-Qarawiyyin in Fez, Morocco, founded in 859 as a mosque school (madrasa), is recognised by UNESCO and Guinness World Records as the oldest existing educational institution, though it became a university in the modern sense only in 1963. Al-Azhar University in Cairo (founded 970–972) has a similar history. The first institution founded as a university in the European sense, with a corporate charter and faculties, is the University of Bologna (1088).

Criteria and disputes

Whether an institution counts depends on whether it was founded as a university, whether it has operated without interruption, and whether splits or refoundations (as with Paris in 1970, or Alcalá) break continuity. Medieval studia generalia that closed for long periods are generally excluded.
