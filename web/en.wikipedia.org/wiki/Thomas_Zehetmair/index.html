Thomas Zehetmair

Thomas Zehetmair (born 23 November 1961 in Salzburg, Austria) is an Austrian violinist and conductor. He is internationally acclaimed both as a soloist and as the founder of the Zehetmair Quartet, and he has held chief conductor posts with orchestras in the United Kingdom and Germany.

Career

Zehetmair studied at the Mozarteum in Salzburg and took masterclasses with Nathan Milstein and Max Rostal. He won first prize at the International Mozart Competition in Salzburg in 1979. His recording of Paganini's 24 Caprices and his interpretations of the Bach solo sonatas and partitas, played with a Baroque bow, have been widely praised. He has served as music director of the Northern Sinfonia (now Royal Northern Sinfonia) and as chief conductor of the Stuttgarter Kammerorchester.

Zehetmair Quartet

Founded in 1994, the Zehetmair Quartet is known for performing from memory and for award-winning recordings of Schumann, Bartók, and Hartmann for ECM Records.
