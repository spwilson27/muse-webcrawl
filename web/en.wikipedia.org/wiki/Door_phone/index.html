Door phone

A door phone or door bell phone is a set of electrical and electronic elements used to handle communication between a resident in a house, apartment or villa and a guest outside. The visitor presses a button at an outdoor panel, and the resident can communicate by voice — and, in video door phone systems, see the visitor on a monitor — before deciding whether to open the door, usually by remotely releasing an electric lock.

Operation

Modern door phone systems use intercom technology and may be connected to the building's telephone system or to a smartphone application, allowing the resident to answer the door remotely. Outdoor panels often include a call button, microphone, speaker, nameplate, and sometimes a keypad or card reader for access control. Video door phones add a camera at the entrance and a display at the indoor station.
