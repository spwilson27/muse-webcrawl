Orlando Pride

The Orlando Pride is a professional women's soccer team based in Orlando, Florida, that competes in the National Women's Soccer League (NWSL). Founded in 2015 as the league's tenth team and affiliated with Orlando City SC of Major League Soccer, the Pride began play in the 2016 season. The club plays its home matches at Inter&Co Stadium (formerly Exploria Stadium), which it shares with Orlando City.

History

The Pride set an NWSL attendance record of 23,403 in their first home match in April 2016. Early squads featured United States international Alex Morgan and Brazilian star Marta, widely regarded as one of the greatest women's players of all time, who joined in 2017. After several seasons of mixed results, the club enjoyed a breakthrough 2024 campaign under head coach Seb Hines, going unbeaten through the regular season to win the NWSL Shield and then the NWSL Championship, the club's first major trophies.

Colours and identity

The club's colours are purple, inherited from the Orlando City brand, and the name "Pride" refers both to female lions and to a collective of lions. The team's supporters' group is known as the Black Swans.
