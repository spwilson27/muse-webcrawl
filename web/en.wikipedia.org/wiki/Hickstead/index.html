Hickstead

Hickstead (March 2, 1996 – November 6, 2011) was a Dutch Warmblood gelding and one of the most successful show jumping horses of all time. Ridden by Canadian rider Eric Lamaze, he won individual gold and team silver at the 2008 Beijing Olympic Games, along with numerous grand prix titles.

Career

Bred in the Netherlands, Hickstead was purchased by Torrey Pines Stable and developed by Lamaze. Standing about 1.61 m (16 hands), he was relatively small for a top show jumper but renowned for his carefulness, speed and enormous jump. His victories included the 2007 Pan American Games team silver and individual bronze, the $1 million CN International at Spruce Meadows (2007 and 2011), the Grand Prix of Aachen (2010), and individual bronze at the 2010 World Equestrian Games in Kentucky, where he was named Best Horse after four clear rounds in the final-four rotation.

Death and legacy

On 6 November 2011, immediately after completing a round at a World Cup event in Verona, Italy, Hickstead collapsed and died of an aortic rupture. He was fifteen years old. He was inducted into Canada's Sports Hall of Fame in 2012, and a statue of Hickstead and Lamaze was erected at the Angelstone equestrian facility in Ontario.
