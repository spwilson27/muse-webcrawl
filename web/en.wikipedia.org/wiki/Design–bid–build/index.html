Design–bid–build

Design–bid–build (DBB), also known as the traditional method or hardbid, is a project delivery method in which the owner contracts separately with a designer or architect and with a construction contractor.

Process

The project proceeds in three sequential phases: the design phase, in which the architect produces complete construction documents; the bidding phase, in which contractors submit competitive bids; and the build phase, in which the selected contractor constructs the project. The method contrasts with design–build, where a single entity is responsible for both design and construction.

Advantages and disadvantages

DBB gives the owner substantial control over design and typically competitive pricing, but the sequential phases lengthen schedules and the separation of designer and builder can produce disputes over errors in the documents.
