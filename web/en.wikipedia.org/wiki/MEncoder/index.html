MEncoder is a free command line video decoding, encoding, and filtering tool released under the GNU General Public License. It is a companion to MPlayer and shares its codebase and library of demuxers and codecs, allowing it to read virtually any format MPlayer can play.

Features

MEncoder can convert between a wide variety of audio and video formats, apply filters such as scaling, cropping, deinterlacing, denoising, and rotation, and extract or hard-code subtitles. It supports encoding with codecs provided through libraries such as x264, Xvid, and libavcodec, and output into containers including AVI, MPEG, and Matroska.

Because it is driven entirely from the command line, MEncoder became popular for scripted and batch conversion jobs. Its development slowed as the FFmpeg project grew to offer similar capabilities with a more actively maintained codebase, and many users migrated to ffmpeg-based workflows.
