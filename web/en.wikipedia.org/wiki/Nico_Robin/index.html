Nico Robin, the "Devil Child".

Nico Robin is a fictional character in the One Piece manga and anime franchise created by Eiichiro Oda. The archaeologist of the Straw Hat Pirates, she is the sole survivor of the island of Ohara and one of the few people in the world able to read the ancient Poneglyph stones — a skill that makes her a target of the World Government, which branded her the "Devil Child" and placed a bounty on her head when she was eight years old.

Character

Robin is calm, intelligent and reserved, with a morbid sense of humour that unnerves her crewmates. She ate the Hana Hana no Mi, a Paramecia-type Devil Fruit that lets her sprout replicas of her limbs — and eventually her whole body — from any surface within range. Introduced as an antagonist under the codename Miss All Sunday, vice-president of the criminal organisation Baroque Works, she defected to the Straw Hats and formally joined the crew after the Alabasta saga.

Story role

Robin's lifelong goal is to find the Rio Poneglyph and learn the true history of the world, including the forbidden Void Century. Her backstory — the destruction of Ohara by a Buster Call and her two decades on the run — is revealed in the Enies Lobby arc, culminating in her declaration that she wants to live, a turning point in the series. During the two-year timeskip she worked with the Revolutionary Army, and in later arcs she becomes central to the race for the Road Poneglyphs that lead to the final island, Laugh Tale.

Reception

Robin consistently ranks among the most popular One Piece characters in reader polls, and her Enies Lobby scenes are frequently cited among the series' emotional highlights.
