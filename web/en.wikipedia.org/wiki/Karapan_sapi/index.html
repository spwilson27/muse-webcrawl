Karapan sapi

Karapan sapi is a traditional bull racing festival on the Indonesian island of Madura. Pairs of bulls, yoked together and steered by a jockey standing on a wooden sled, race over a course of about 100 metres, cheered on by large crowds.

History

The tradition is said to have originated as a celebration of the rice harvest, when farmers raced their plough bulls across harvested fields. Over time it developed into a prestigious competitive event held in the dry season, culminating in finals that draw competitors from across Madura.

The race

Two bulls are harnessed side by side to a light wooden frame. The jockey, or tokang karapan, balances on the frame and urges the animals on, sometimes biting their tails or using other traditional methods to spur them. Races are run in elimination heats between pairs of teams.

Culture

Winning bulls bring great prestige and can command high prices. Owners decorate their animals, and the festival is accompanied by gamelan music, food stalls and gambling on the outcomes.
