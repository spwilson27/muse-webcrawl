Autodesk

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Autodesk, Inc. is an American multinational software corporation that makes software products and services for the architecture, engineering, construction, manufacturing, media, education and entertainment industries. Autodesk is headquartered in San Francisco, California, and has offices worldwide. Its best-known product is AutoCAD, the computer-aided design (CAD) program that helped popularise CAD on personal computers.

History

The company was founded in 1982 in Mill Valley, California, by a group of programmers including John Walker and Michael Riddle, around Riddle's Interact CAD program, which became AutoCAD. Launched for the then-new IBM PC, AutoCAD was one of the first CAD programs to run on personal computers and grew into the industry standard for 2D drafting. Autodesk went public in 1985 and expanded through the 1990s and 2000s by acquiring products including Revit (building information modelling), 3ds Max and Maya (3D animation), Inventor (mechanical design) and Alias.

Products and business

Major products include AutoCAD, Revit, Civil 3D, Fusion (formerly Fusion 360), Inventor, Maya, 3ds Max, and the construction management platform Autodesk Construction Cloud. In the 2010s the company shifted from perpetual licences to subscription and cloud-based services, a transition led by CEO Carl Bass and continued under Andrew Anagnost, who became chief executive in 2017. Autodesk software is widely used in film visual effects, video game development, architecture and product design, and the company provides free licences to students and educators.
