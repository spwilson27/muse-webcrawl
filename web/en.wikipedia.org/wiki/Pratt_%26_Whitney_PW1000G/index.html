TypeGeared turbofan
ManufacturerPratt & Whitney

Major applicationsAirbus A320neo family, A220, Embraer E-Jet E2

The Pratt & Whitney PW1000G family, marketed as the GTF (geared turbofan) engine, is a series of high-bypass turbofan aircraft engines produced by Pratt & Whitney. Its defining feature is a reduction gearbox between the fan and the low-pressure spool, allowing each to rotate at its optimal speed.

Development

Developed from demonstrator work begun in the 1990s, the engine entered service in 2016 on the Airbus A320neo with Lufthansa. Variants power the Airbus A220, the Embraer E-Jet E2 family, and were selected for the Mitsubishi SpaceJet before that programme's cancellation.

Issues

The fleet experienced durability problems in the 2020s; in 2023 Pratt & Whitney announced accelerated inspections of PW1100G engines after a defect in powdered-metal manufacturing was found, grounding many aircraft for extended periods.
