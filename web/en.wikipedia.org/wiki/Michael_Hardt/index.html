Michael Hardt

Michael Hardt (born 1960) is an American political philosopher and literary theorist, best known for his collaborations with the Italian philosopher Antonio Negri, including the books Empire (2000), Multitude: War and Democracy in the Age of Empire (2004), and Commonwealth (2009).

Work

Hardt is a professor of literature at Duke University. His work with Negri analyses globalisation, sovereignty, and contemporary forms of resistance, arguing that a new form of global sovereignty — "Empire" — has succeeded modern imperialism, and that the creative productive capacities of the "multitude" offer possibilities for democratic transformation.
