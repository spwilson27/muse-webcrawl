Global Media & Entertainment

Global Media & Entertainment Limited, trading as Global, is a British media company formed in 2007. It is the owner of the largest commercial radio company in the United Kingdom. The company was founded by Ashley Tabor-King (formerly Ashley Tabor), who in 2008 led its acquisition of GCap Media, creating the group that now operates the radio brands Heart, Capital, Smooth, LBC, Classic FM, Capital Xtra, Radio X, and Gold.

Global is headquartered at 30 Leicester Square in London. Beyond radio broadcasting, the company expanded into outdoor advertising with the acquisition of Primesight, Outdoor Plus, and Exterion Media (renamed Global Outdoor) in 2018, and operates DAX, a digital advertising exchange. Its charitable arm, Global's Make Some Noise, supports small charities across the UK.

History

Global Radio was formed in 2007 and acquired Chrysalis Radio's stations (including Heart and Galaxy) the same year. The 2008 purchase of GCap Media brought Capital, Classic FM, and XFM into the group. The company rebranded as Global in 2013 and has since grown into one of Europe's largest radio and outdoor media groups.
