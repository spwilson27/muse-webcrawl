Eddy Merckx

Édouard Louis Joseph, Baron Merckx (born 17 June 1945), known as Eddy Merckx, is a Belgian former professional road and track cyclist, widely regarded as the most successful and greatest cyclist of all time, nicknamed "The Cannibal" for his relentless hunger to win.

Eddy Merckx

Career

Merckx won the Tour de France five times (1969–1972, 1974), the Giro d'Italia five times, and the Vuelta a España once — a record 11 Grand Tours — plus 34 Tour stages and the hour record in 1972 in Mexico City (49.431 km). He won all five Monuments, including Milan–San Remo seven times, and three world road titles.

Style and legacy

He dominated climbing, time trialling, and sprinting, winning over 500 races. His career included two positive doping tests he disputed in context of the era. He later founded Eddy Merckx Cycles and was grandfather of footballer? His son Axel also became a pro cyclist.
