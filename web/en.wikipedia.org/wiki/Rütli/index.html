Rütli

Rütli
Location: Lake Lucerne, canton of Uri, Switzerland
Significance: Legendary site of the Rütlischwur, the founding oath of the Swiss Confederacy

Rütli (German pronunciation: [ˈryːtli]) is a mountain meadow on the western shore of Lake Lucerne in the canton of Uri, Switzerland. It is famous as the legendary site of the Rütlischwur, the oath sworn by representatives of the three founding cantons — Uri, Schwyz, and Unterwalden — which in Swiss national tradition marks the founding of the Old Swiss Confederacy, conventionally dated to 1 August 1291, the date now celebrated as Swiss National Day.

The founding legend

According to the tradition recorded by the chronicler Aegidius Tschudi in the 16th century, the three confederates — Werner Stauffacher of Schwyz, Walter Fürst of Uri, and Arnold von Melchthal of Unterwalden — met in secret on the Rütli meadow to swear mutual assistance against the Habsburg bailiffs. The story is closely associated with the legend of William Tell. Friedrich Schiller's play William Tell (1804) immortalised the oath scene, with its famous vow: "We will be a single people of brothers."

History

The meadow was purchased in 1859 by the Schweizerische Gemeinnützige Gesellschaft (Swiss Society for Public Welfare), partly through donations raised by Swiss schoolchildren, in order to preserve it from development, and it was given to the Swiss Confederation. It is administered today on behalf of the Confederation. In 1940, during the Second World War, General Henri Guisan assembled the senior officers of the Swiss Army at the Rütli to announce the strategy of resistance from the National Redoubt — the famous "Rütli Report" (Rütlirapport).

Access and commemorations

The Rütli is accessible only on foot or by boat from Lake Lucerne; a boat landing connects it to the hiking trail known as the Swiss Path (Weg der Schweiz), created in 1991 for the 700th anniversary of the Confederacy. Celebrations are held on the meadow each year on Swiss National Day, 1 August, sometimes including an address by the President of the Swiss Confederation.
