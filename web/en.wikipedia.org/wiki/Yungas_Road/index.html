Yungas Road

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Yungas Road is a 64-kilometre (40 mi) road leading from La Paz to Coroico, 56 kilometres (35 mi) northeast of La Paz in the Yungas region of Bolivia. It is legendary for its extreme danger and was dubbed "Death Road" (Camino de la Muerte). In 1995 the Inter-American Development Bank named it the "world's most dangerous road".

Description

The road descends from La Cumbre pass at about 4,650 metres (15,260 ft) above sea level down to Coroico at about 1,200 metres (3,900 ft). The mostly single-lane, unpaved track clings to mountainsides with sheer drops of up to 600 metres, almost no guardrails, and frequent rain, fog, landslides, and cascading waterfalls. In Bolivia vehicles drive on the right, but on the Yungas Road drivers customarily keep left to better see the edge. An estimated 200–300 travellers were killed yearly on the road in its heyday.

A modern paved alternative route opened in 2006, and the old road is now used mainly by mountain bikers; downhill cycling tours from La Paz have become a major tourist attraction, though fatalities among cyclists have also occurred.
