2015–2016 protests in Moldova

Protest details
DateApril 2015 – 2016
LocationChișinău, Moldova
CausesBanking fraud scandal, corruption, economic hardship

The 2015–2016 protests in Moldova were a series of mass demonstrations in Chișinău and other cities sparked by the revelation of a massive banking fraud — the theft of roughly one billion US dollars, about an eighth of the country's GDP, from three Moldovan banks in 2014.

Background

The disappearance of the funds, revealed publicly in early 2015, triggered a currency crisis, soaring inflation and a collapse in living standards. The civic platform Dignity and Truth (Demnitate și Adevăr) organised large rallies from April 2015, demanding the resignation of the government and the prosecution of those responsible, widely believed to include powerful oligarchic figures.

Course of the protests

Tens of thousands gathered in the Great National Assembly Square, and in September 2015 protesters established a tent encampment in central Chișinău. Demonstrations continued into 2016, escalating in January when the appointment of Pavel Filip as prime minister provoked clashes outside parliament.

Outcome

The movement helped force constitutional change restoring direct presidential elections, held in late 2016, and kept the bank fraud — and the demand to recover the stolen assets — at the centre of Moldovan politics.
