Scarlett Ingrid Johansson (born 22 November 1984) is an American actress and singer. The world's highest-paid actress in 2018 and 2019, she has featured multiple times on the Forbes Celebrity 100 list. Her films have grossed over $15 billion worldwide, making her one of the highest-grossing box office stars of all time. She has received various accolades, including a Tony Award and a BAFTA Award, as well as two Academy Award nominations.

Early life

Johansson was born in the Manhattan borough of New York City. Her father, Karsten Olaf Johansson, is an architect originally from Copenhagen, Denmark; her mother, Melanie Sloan, is a producer from the Bronx. She holds dual American and Danish citizenship. She attended PS 41 in Greenwich Village and the Professional Children's School, and trained at the Lee Strasberg Theatre Institute.

Career

Johansson made her film debut in the fantasy comedy North (1994) and gained early recognition for roles in Manny & Lo (1996), The Horse Whisperer (1998), and Ghost World (2001). Her breakthrough came with Sofia Coppola's Lost in Translation (2003), for which she won the BAFTA Award for Best Actress, and Girl with a Pearl Earring (2003).

She achieved global stardom playing Natasha Romanoff / Black Widow in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, beginning with Iron Man 2 (2010) and concluding with the standalone Black Widow (2021). Other notable films include Her (2013, voice role), Under the Skin (2013), Lucy (2014), Marriage Story (2019), and Jojo Rabbit (2019), the latter two earning her Academy Award nominations in the same year. On stage, she won a Tony Award for her Broadway debut in A View from the Bridge (2010).

Music and other ventures

Johansson has released two studio albums: Anywhere I Lay My Head (2008), consisting largely of Tom Waits covers, and Break Up (2009) with Pete Yorn. In 2022 she launched The Outset, a skincare brand. She has been a global ambassador for Oxfam and supports various charitable causes.

Public image

Frequently described by the media as a sex symbol, Johansson has nonetheless been praised for her versatility across independent and blockbuster cinema, and has repeatedly spoken about typecasting and equal pay in Hollywood.
