Route des Crêtes

The Route des Crêtes (Ridge Road) is a scenic mountain road in the Vosges mountains of eastern France, running roughly 89 kilometres from Sainte-Marie-aux-Mines in the north to Cernay in the south, largely along the main ridge.

History

The road was built by the French Army during the First World War to supply front-line positions along the ridge while remaining sheltered from German observation on the eastern slope.

Route

It passes near the Grand Ballon, the highest point in the Vosges, and the Hohneck, and crosses passes including the Col de la Schlucht. It is popular with motorists, cyclists and hikers in summer and often closed by snow in winter.
