Acura is the luxury and performance vehicle division of the Japanese automaker Honda, based in the United States. Launched on 27 March 1986 with the Legend sedan and Integra sports coupe, Acura was the first luxury marque created by a Japanese manufacturer, predating Toyota's Lexus and Nissan's Infiniti by several years.

History

Acura's early success rested on Honda's reputation for reliability combined with higher trim and performance; the Legend, developed with input from the Rover Group, sold strongly. In 1990 Acura launched the NSX, a mid-engined aluminium sports car developed with input from Ayrton Senna, which redefined the everyday supercar. Through the 1990s and 2000s the range expanded to SUVs such as the MDX and RDX and sedans such as the TL and RL. A second-generation NSX hybrid supercar was sold from 2016 to 2022. The brand has since re-emphasised performance with Type S models and returned to names such as the Integra (2023).

Motorsport

Acura competes in North American sports car racing, winning multiple IMSA championships; its ARX prototypes have won the 24 Hours of Daytona overall, and Acura-powered cars have been successful in the Pikes Peak International Hill Climb.

Markets

Acura is sold primarily in the United States and Canada; it was introduced in Mexico and China, though Chinese sales ended in the early 2020s. Many models are manufactured in Ohio.
