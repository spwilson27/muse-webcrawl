Knots Landing is an American prime time television soap opera that aired on CBS from December 27, 1979, to May 13, 1993. A spin-off of Dallas, it was set in a fictitious coastal suburb of Los Angeles and initially centred on the lives of four married couples living in a cul-de-sac, Seaview Circle. With 14 seasons and 344 episodes, it is one of the longest-running prime-time dramas in American television history.

Premise and characters

The series followed Gary Ewing (Ted Shackelford), the middle son of the Ewing family of Dallas, and his wife Valene (Joan Van Ark), who moved to California for a fresh start after Gary's alcoholism and failures in Texas. Their neighbours on Seaview Circle included Karen Fairgate (Michele Lee, the only cast member to appear in all 344 episodes) and her husbands Sid and Mack MacKenzie, and the Avery and Cunningham families. Donna Mills joined in 1980 as the scheming Abby Cunningham, becoming one of television's most celebrated villains, and William Devane joined in 1983 as politician-businessman Greg Sumner. Unlike the opulent Dallas, Knots Landing emphasised middle-class domestic life, touching on social issues alongside its melodrama.

Reception and legacy

The show was a consistent ratings performer for CBS, peaking in its middle seasons, and won praise for its writing and acting, including an Emmy for its music and multiple nominations for its cast. A reunion miniseries, Knots Landing: Back to the Cul-de-Sac, aired in 1997. Characters crossed over with Dallas in the early years; after Dallas's famous "dream season" retcon, the two series' continuities diverged and Knots Landing continued to treat Bobby Ewing as dead.
