Microchip Technology Inc. is an American semiconductor manufacturer headquartered in Chandler, Arizona. The company is a leading supplier of microcontrollers, mixed-signal, analog and Flash-IP integrated circuits. Its products are used in embedded control applications across the industrial, automotive, consumer, aerospace and communications markets.

History

Microchip began as a division of General Instrument and was spun out as a wholly owned subsidiary in 1987, becoming an independent company in 1989. It held its initial public offering in 1993. The company grew through numerous acquisitions, most notably Atmel Corporation in 2016 (for about $3.56 billion) and Microsemi Corporation in 2018 (for about $8.35 billion), which expanded its portfolio into aerospace, defence and timing products.

Products

Microchip's flagship products are its PIC microcontrollers, a family of 8-bit, 16-bit and 32-bit devices widely used by hobbyists and industry alike. The company also sells AVR microcontrollers (inherited from Atmel), 32-bit SAM and PIC32 processors, serial EEPROM and Flash memory, analog and interface components, USB and Ethernet connectivity chips, FPGA products (from Microsemi), and timing and synchronization systems. Its MPLAB X development environment supports programming and debugging of its devices.
