Justin Portal Welby (born 6 January 1956) is a British Anglican bishop who served as the 105th Archbishop of Canterbury from 2013 until his resignation in January 2025. As primate of the Church of England and symbolic head of the worldwide Anglican Communion, he crowned — anointed and crowned — Charles III in May 2023.

Early life and ministry

Educated at Eton and Cambridge, Welby worked in the oil industry for eleven years, largely in Paris and Nigeria, before training for ordination. He served at Coventry Cathedral, where he worked in reconciliation ministry, as Dean of Liverpool (2007–2011), and as Bishop of Durham. His background informed his interventions on banking reform — he sat on the Parliamentary Commission on Banking Standards — payday lending, and business ethics.

Archbishop

Welby prioritised evangelism, reconciliation, and prayer communities — founding the Community of St Anselm at Lambeth Palace. He navigated deep divisions over women's bishops (legislated in 2014), sexuality, and the Living in Love and Faith process, and pronounced blessings for same-sex couples while the Church retained its marriage doctrine, straining relations with conservative provinces such as those grouped in GAFCON.

In November 2024, following the Makin Review's findings on failures to act on abuse by John Smyth, Welby announced his resignation, taking responsibility for institutional failures; he formally stood down in January 2025.
