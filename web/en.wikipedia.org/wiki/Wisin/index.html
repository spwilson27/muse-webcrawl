Juan Luis Morera Luna (born December 19, 1978), known professionally as Wisin, is a Puerto Rican reggaeton rapper, singer, and producer. He is best known as one half of the duo Wisin & Yandel, one of the most successful acts in reggaeton history, and for a solo career that began in earnest with El Regreso del Sobreviviente (2014).

Career

Wisin formed Wisin & Yandel with Llandel Veguilla in the late 1990s; the duo rose to international fame with albums such as Pa'l Mundo (2005) and Los Extraterrestres (2007), winning a Grammy Award and multiple Latin Grammy Awards. His solo albums include Victory (2017), and he has collaborated with artists including Jennifer Lopez, Ricky Martin, Enrique Iglesias, and Daddy Yankee. The hit "Adrenalina" (2014) and his production company La Base Music Group have kept him central to the genre; Wisin & Yandel reunited for a final album and farewell tour, La Última Misión, in 2022.
