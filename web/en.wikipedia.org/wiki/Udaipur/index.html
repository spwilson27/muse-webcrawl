Overview

Udaipur is a city in southern Rajasthan, India, founded in 1559 by Maharana Udai Singh II as the new capital of the Mewar kingdom, chosen for its defensible position among the Aravalli hills and lakes. It is known as the City of Lakes for its system of interconnected lakes, chiefly Pichola, Fateh Sagar and Swaroop Sagar.

Landmarks

The City Palace complex, among Rajasthan's largest, overlooks Lake Pichola, in which stand the Lake Palace (Jag Niwas, 1746, now a hotel — famous from the film Octopussy) and Jag Mandir. Udaipur is often ranked among the world's most romantic cities and is a major wedding and film location.
