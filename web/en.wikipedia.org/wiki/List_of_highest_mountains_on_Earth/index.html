List of highest mountains on Earth

There are at least 108 mountains on Earth with elevations of 7,200 metres (23,622 ft) or greater above sea level. All of them are located in central and southern Asia, in the Himalaya, Karakoram, Hindu Kush, Pamir, Kunlun, and related ranges. The fourteen peaks above 8,000 metres are known as the eight-thousanders.

Climbing history

Annapurna I was the first eight-thousander climbed, in 1950 by Maurice Herzog and Louis Lachenal. Reinhold Messner became the first person to climb all fourteen, completing the set in 1986 without supplemental oxygen.
