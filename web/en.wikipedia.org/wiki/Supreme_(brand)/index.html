Supreme (brand)

IndustryRetail, fashion
FoundedApril 1994; New York City, U.S.
FounderJames Jebbia
ProductsClothing, shoes, accessories, skateboards

Supreme's red-and-white box logo is one of streetwear's most recognizable marks.

Supreme is an American clothing brand and skateboarding shop established in New York City in April 1994 by James Jebbia. The brand is known worldwide by fans of streetwear and skateboarding culture, and its products — released in limited weekly "drops" — frequently sell out within minutes and command high prices on resale markets.

History

The first Supreme store opened on Lafayette Street in downtown Manhattan, staffed by skateboarders and designed with open space in the centre so skaters could ride in. The brand's box logo, rendered in white Futura Heavy Oblique type on a red rectangle, was inspired by the work of artist Barbara Kruger. Supreme grew slowly for a decade before collaborations and scarcity-driven marketing made it a global phenomenon in the 2010s.

Business

In 2017 the private equity firm The Carlyle Group acquired a reported 50 percent stake valuing the company at about $1 billion. In 2020, VF Corporation, owner of Vans and The North Face, acquired Supreme for $2.1 billion. VF later agreed to sell the brand to EssilorLuxottica in 2024 for $1.5 billion.

Collaborations and culture

Supreme has collaborated with brands including Nike, Louis Vuitton, The North Face, and Oreo, producing items ranging from sneakers to a branded brick. Its drop model and resale culture made it a defining example of hype-driven fashion and the streetwear economy.
