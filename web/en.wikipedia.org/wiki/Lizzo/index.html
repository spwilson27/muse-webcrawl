Melissa Viviane Jefferson (born April 27, 1988), known professionally as Lizzo, is an American singer, rapper and songwriter. Born in Detroit and raised in Houston, Texas, she began her career in Minneapolis, Minnesota, releasing the independent albums Lizzobangers (2013) and Big Grrrl Small World (2015) before signing with Atlantic Records. A classically trained flautist, she incorporates flute playing into her performances — her flute is nicknamed "Sasha Flute" — and her music blends pop, R&B and hip hop with themes of self-love and body positivity.

Breakthrough and success

Lizzo achieved mainstream breakthrough with her third studio album, Cuz I Love You (2019), which reached number four on the Billboard 200; its sleeper-hit single "Truth Hurts", originally released in 2017, topped the Billboard Hot 100 after going viral, and "Good as Hell" also became a major hit. At the 2020 Grammy Awards she received eight nominations, the most of any artist that year, winning Best Urban Contemporary Album, Best Pop Solo Performance ("Truth Hurts") and Best Traditional R&B Performance. Her fourth album, Special (2022), produced the number-one single "About Damn Time", which won Record of the Year at the 2023 Grammys, making her the first Black woman to win the award in the twenty-first century. She also headlined the Emmy-winning reality competition series Lizzo's Watch Out for the Big Grrrls (2022). In 2023, three former dancers filed a lawsuit alleging a hostile work environment, which Lizzo denied; the case drew extensive media coverage.
