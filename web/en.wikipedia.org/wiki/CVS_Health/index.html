CVS Health

Industry: Healthcare, retail pharmacy
Founded: 1963 (as Consumer Value Stores)
Headquarters: Woonsocket, Rhode Island, U.S.
Divisions: CVS Pharmacy, CVS Caremark, Aetna

CVS Health Corporation (formerly CVS Corporation and CVS Caremark Corporation) is an American healthcare company that owns CVS Pharmacy, a retail pharmacy chain; CVS Caremark, a pharmacy benefits manager; and Aetna, a health insurance provider, among many other brands. The company is headquartered in Woonsocket, Rhode Island.

History

The company was founded in Lowell, Massachusetts, in 1963 by brothers Stanley and Sidney Goldstein and partner Ralph Hoagland as Consumer Value Stores, initially a chain of health and beauty aid stores. It added pharmacies in the late 1960s and grew through acquisitions including Peoples Drug (1990), Revco (1997), Eckerd (2004), and the pharmacy operations of Albertsons (2006). In 2007, CVS merged with pharmacy benefits manager Caremark Rx in a roughly $21 billion deal.

In 2014 the company renamed itself CVS Health and removed tobacco products from its stores. In 2018 it completed its $69 billion acquisition of health insurer Aetna, one of the largest healthcare mergers in history, creating a vertically integrated company spanning insurance, pharmacy benefit management, retail pharmacies, and MinuteClinic walk-in clinics.
