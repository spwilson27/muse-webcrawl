Comcast Technology Center

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Comcast Technology Center is a supertall skyscraper in Center City, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, United States. Completed in 2018, it rises to about 1,121 feet (342 m) with 60 floors, making it the tallest building in Philadelphia and one of the tallest buildings in the United States outside New York and Chicago. It serves as the technology and innovation headquarters of the Comcast Corporation.

Design and construction

The tower was designed by Foster + Partners, with interiors involving Gensler, and stands adjacent to the earlier Comcast Center (completed 2008). Its form features a stepped silhouette rising to a central spine and lantern-like top. The building contains offices for Comcast's technology and engineering staff, studios for NBC 10 and Telemundo 62, and, in its upper floors, the Four Seasons Hotel Philadelphia, which opened in 2019. A top-floor restaurant and a multi-storey sky lobby offer panoramic views over the city.

Features

The building includes a public ground-floor plaza and concourse with access to Suburban Station, public art installations, and large column-free loft-style workspaces designed for engineering teams. It was reported to have cost around $1.5 billion, and it achieved LEED Platinum certification. The tower surpassed the Comcast Center as Philadelphia's tallest building and extended the headquarters campus of the cable and media company in its home city.
