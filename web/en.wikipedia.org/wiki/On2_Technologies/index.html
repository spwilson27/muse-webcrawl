On2 Technologies

On2 Technologies
Founded: 1992 (as The Duck Corporation)
Headquarters: Clifton Park, New York, US
Industry: Video compression
Fate: Acquired by Google (2010)

On2 Technologies, formerly known as The Duck Corporation, was an American company that developed video compression technology, notably the VP3, VP6, VP7 and VP8 codecs.

History

The Duck Corporation gained early recognition for its TrueMotion video codecs used in 1990s CD-ROM games. Renamed On2 Technologies, the company released its VP series of codecs; VP3 was donated to the open-source community in 2001 and became the basis of the Theora codec. VP6 was widely used for video in Adobe Flash Player, and VP7 powered video in Skype.

Acquisition by Google

In August 2009 Google announced it would acquire On2; the deal, valued at roughly $133 million in stock after adjustment, closed in February 2010. In May 2010 Google released the VP8 codec as open source under the WebM project, aiming to provide a royalty-free standard for web video.
