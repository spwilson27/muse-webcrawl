The Khwarezmian Empire (also spelled Khwarazmian) was a Persianate Sunni Muslim empire of Turkic mamluk origin, ruled by the Anushteginid dynasty of Khwarazmshahs from about 1077 to 1231. At its zenith in the early 13th century it ruled most of Persia, Transoxiana, and parts of Afghanistan — a territory of some 2.3 to 3.6 million square kilometers — before being annihilated in the Mongol invasion of 1219–1221, one of the most devastating conquests in history.

Rise

The dynasty began with Anushtegin Gharchai, a Turkic slave commander appointed governor of Khwarazm by the Seljuk sultan in 1077. His descendants progressively threw off Seljuk overlordship: Atsiz expanded Khwarazmian power in the 1130s–40s (though he suffered subjugation to the Qara Khitai), and Il-Arslan and Tekish consolidated independence. Tekish (r. 1172–1200) conquered Khorasan and western Persia, and extinguished the last Seljuk sultan of Iraq in 1194.

Zenith under Muhammad II

Ala al-Din Muhammad II (r. 1200–1220) defeated the Ghurids, annexed Transoxiana from the Qara Khitai by 1210, and subdued most of Persia, making his realm the dominant power of the eastern Islamic world. Yet the empire was fragile: power rested on often-disloyal Qangli Turkic military elites, the shah feuded with his mother Terken Khatun's parallel court at Gurganj, and he quarreled with the Abbasid caliph al-Nasir.

Destruction

In 1218 the governor of Otrar seized a Mongol-sponsored trade caravan and massacred its merchants; when Genghis Khan demanded redress, Muhammad II had the Mongol envoys humiliated and one killed. The Mongol invasion of 1219 destroyed the empire city by city — Bukhara, Samarkand, Gurganj, Merv, and Nishapur suffered catastrophic massacres and population losses that in some regions persisted for centuries. Muhammad II died in flight on a Caspian island in 1220. His son Jalal al-Din won a celebrated victory at Parwan in 1221 before his defeat at the Battle of the Indus; he waged a decade of further resistance across Persia and the Caucasus until his death in 1231.
