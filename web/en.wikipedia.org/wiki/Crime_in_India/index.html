Crime in India

Crime in India is recorded annually by the National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB), which publishes the "Crime in India" report covering offences under the Indian Penal Code (now the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita) and special and local laws.

Overview

Millions of cognizable crimes are registered each year, with offences such as theft, assault and offences against women forming large shares. States such as Uttar Pradesh typically record the highest absolute numbers, while crime rates per capita vary widely across states and union territories.

Issues

Key concerns include crimes against women, communal violence, organised crime, cybercrime, and the large backlog of cases in the court system, which contributes to a high proportion of undertrial prisoners. Under-reporting is widely acknowledged, so survey-based estimates often exceed official registrations.
