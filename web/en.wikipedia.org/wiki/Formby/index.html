Formby
Location: Sefton, Merseyside, England
Population: approx. 23,000
Postcode district: L37

Formby is a town and civil parish in the Metropolitan Borough of Sefton, Merseyside, England. It lies on the Irish Sea coast north of Liverpool and is known for its extensive sand dunes, pine woods and beach.

History

The name Formby derives from the Old Norse Fornebei, the village of a man named Forni. The area was historically part of Lancashire. Formby grew as a residential and holiday area after the arrival of the railway in the nineteenth century.

Geography and nature

The National Trust manages much of Formby's coastline, where mobile sand dunes back onto pinewoods planted in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries. The woods are a stronghold of the red squirrel, and the dunes support rare species including natterjack toads. Prehistoric human and animal footprints preserved in the shore sediments are periodically exposed by the tides.

Transport

Formby railway station is on the Northern Line of the Merseyrail network, with services to Liverpool and Southport.
