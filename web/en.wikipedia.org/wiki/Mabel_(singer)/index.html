Mabel Alabama-Pearl McVey (born 19 February 1996), known mononymously as Mabel, is a British singer and songwriter. Born in Spain to English record producer Cameron McVey and Swedish singer Neneh Cherry, she grew up in Stockholm and moved to London to pursue music. In 2017 she had her mainstream breakthrough with the single "Finders Keepers" and was nominated for the Critics' Choice Award at the 2019 Brit Awards; she won the Brit Award for British Female Solo Artist in 2020.

Career

Mabel uploaded her debut track "Know Me Better" to SoundCloud in 2015 and released her first single, "My Boy My Town", that year. Her 2017 single "Finders Keepers", featuring Kojo Funds, reached the UK top ten. She opened for Harry Styles on tour and released the mixtape Ivy to Roses (2017). The 2019 single "Don't Call Me Up" became a major international hit, peaking at number three in the UK and charting across Europe and Australia, and preceded her debut studio album High Expectations (August 2019), which debuted at number three on the UK Albums Chart. Singles from the album included "Mad Love" and "Boyfriend".

Her second studio album, About Last Night... (2022), a concept record about the course of a night out, drew on dance and house influences and included the singles "Let Them Know" and "Good Luck" (with Jax Jones and Galantis). In 2021 she contributed the single "Take It Home" to the Pokémon 25th anniversary album. Later releases leaned back toward R&B, including the 2024 single "Vitamins" and collaborations in UK dance and garage circles.

Personal life and style

Mabel has spoken openly about anxiety and dyslexia and is an ambassador for mental-health awareness. Musically she cites Destiny's Child, Lauryn Hill and her mother's trip-hop era as influences; her sound blends contemporary pop with 1990s–2000s R&B.
