Measles

Measles
CauseMeasles virus ( airborne, extremely contagious)
SignsFever, cough, Koplik spots, blotchy rash
PreventionMMR vaccine (highly effective)
[Image: A child with the characteristic red blotchy measles rash]

Measles is a highly contagious airborne viral disease causing fever, cough, runny nose, red eyes and a characteristic blotchy rash, with Koplik spots inside the cheeks. Complications — pneumonia, encephalitis, blindness in malnourished children — killed millions historically.

Elimination efforts

The measles vaccine (1963, now in MMR) is one of medicine's great bargains: two doses are about 97% effective. Measles was eliminated from many regions, but vaccination gaps — worsened by misinformation and the pandemic's disruption — have allowed resurgences, making measles a tracer of immunisation system health.
