Raunkiær plant life-form

Raunkiær life-forms
Devised byChristen C. Raunkiær
Published1904–1907
CriterionPosition of perennating buds

The Raunkiær system is a classification of plant life-forms developed by Danish botanist Christen C. Raunkiær. It categorises plants by the position of their perennating (survival) buds relative to the ground during the unfavourable season.

Main categories

The principal classes are: phanerophytes, with buds high on aerial shoots (trees and shrubs); chamaephytes, with buds close to the ground; hemicryptophytes, with buds at the soil surface; cryptophytes, with buds below ground or under water; and therophytes, annuals surviving only as seeds.

Biological spectrum

The percentage of a flora in each class — its biological spectrum — characterises climate: phanerophytes dominate the wet tropics, therophytes deserts, and hemicryptophytes cold temperate regions.
