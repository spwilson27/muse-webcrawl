Honda Civic


A Honda Civic sedan

The Honda Civic is a series of automobiles manufactured by Honda since 1972. Originally a subcompact car, the Civic has grown through successive generations into the compact class. Along with the Accord, it is one of Honda's core global models and one of the best-selling cars of all time, with more than 27 million sold worldwide.

History

The first Civic was introduced in July 1972 as a two-door coupe, followed by a three-door hatchback. Its transverse-mounted engine and front-wheel-drive layout gave it generous interior space for its size, and its CVCC engine met 1970s United States emissions standards without a catalytic converter—a notable engineering achievement. The Civic's fuel efficiency made it popular during the 1973 oil crisis.

Generations and variants

The Civic is now in its eleventh generation (introduced in 2021). Body styles have included sedans, coupes, hatchbacks, and station wagons. Performance variants have included the sporty Si and the high-performance Type R, the latter notable for setting front-wheel-drive lap records at the Nürburgring. Hybrid versions have been offered in several generations, including the Civic Hybrid and the e:HEV models sold in Europe and Japan.

Motorsport and culture

The Civic has a long history in touring car racing and amateur motorsport, and it became an icon of the 1990s and 2000s tuner culture in North America and Japan. It is consistently among the best-selling cars in the United States and Canada.
