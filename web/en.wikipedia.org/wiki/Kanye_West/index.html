Ye (born Kanye Omari West, 8 June 1977), commonly known as Kanye West, is an American rapper, record producer and fashion designer. One of the most influential and controversial figures in popular music, he first gained recognition as a producer for Roc-A-Fella Records — notably on Jay-Z's The Blueprint (2001) — before launching a solo career with The College Dropout (2004).

Career

His albums Late Registration (2005), Graduation (2007), 808s & Heartbreak (2008), My Beautiful Dark Twisted Fantasy (2010), Yeezus (2013), The Life of Pablo (2016), Ye (2018), Jesus Is King (2019) and Donda (2021) repeatedly reshaped hip hop and pop, moving between soul-sampling, orchestral rap, electronic and gospel sounds. He has sold well over 100 million records and won more than twenty Grammy Awards. In fashion he founded the Yeezy brand, whose partnership with Adidas ended in 2022.

Public life

West legally changed his name to Ye in 2021. His outspoken public statements — including a 2020 presidential campaign and a series of widely condemned antisemitic remarks in 2022 that cost him major business partnerships — have been extensively covered and have significantly affected his career and reputation.
