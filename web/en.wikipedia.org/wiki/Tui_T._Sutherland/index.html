Tui T. Sutherland

Tui Tamara Sutherland (born July 31, 1978) is a Venezuelan-born American children's book author. She is best known for writing the Wings of Fire series of dragon fantasy novels, and for her work under the collective pseudonym Erin Hunter on the Warriors and Seekers series.

Early life

Sutherland was born in Caracas, Venezuela, to a New Zealand father and a mother who had lived in Paraguay, Miami, and the Dominican Republic. The family moved frequently during her childhood, including to Paraguay, Florida, and the Dominican Republic, before she attended high school in New Jersey. She graduated from Williams College in 1998. Her first name, Tui, comes from the tui, a New Zealand bird.

Career

Sutherland began her career as an editor of children's books in New York before turning to writing. She was part of the team behind the name "Erin Hunter," contributing to the Warriors series about feral cat clans and the Seekers series about bears. She also wrote the Pet Trouble books under the name T. T. Sutherland and co-authored The Menagerie trilogy with her sister Kari Sutherland.

Wings of Fire

Her most successful work is the Wings of Fire series (2012–present), published by Scholastic, set in the dragon continents of Pyrrhia and Pantala. The series follows young dragonets prophesied to end a war of royal succession, and later arcs explore a new continent and threats such as the mind-controlling WaspWing queen. The books have appeared on the New York Times bestseller list, and graphic novel adaptations illustrated by Mike Holmes began in 2018.

Personal life

Sutherland lives in Massachusetts with her husband and children. She has spoken frequently about how animals — especially her dogs — inspire her animal-centred fiction.
