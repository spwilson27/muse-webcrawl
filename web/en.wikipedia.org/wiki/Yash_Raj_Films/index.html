Yash Raj Films (YRF) is an Indian film production and distribution company founded by filmmaker Yash Chopra in 1970. Based in Mumbai, it is one of the largest and most successful studios in Hindi cinema.

History

Yash Chopra founded the company after splitting from his brother B. R. Chopra's studio; its first production was Daag (1973). YRF became synonymous with lavish romantic films, including Silsila (1981), Chandni (1989), Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge (1995) — one of the longest-running films in Indian cinema — and Veer-Zaara (2004). Under Aditya Chopra the studio diversified with the Dhoom action franchise, Chak De! India (2007), and the YRF Spy Universe films such as Ek Tha Tiger, War, and Pathaan (2023).

Operations

The company operates YRF Studios in Mumbai, a music label (YRF Music), home entertainment and international distribution arms, and the talent-management division YRF Talent. It also runs the Yash Raj Films Foundation supporting industry workers.
