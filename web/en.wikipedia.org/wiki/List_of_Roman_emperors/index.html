List of Roman emperors

The Roman emperors were the rulers of the Roman Empire from the granting of the name and title Augustus to Octavian in 27 BC until the fall of Constantinople in AD 1453. The principate established by Augustus was, in form, a continuation of the Republic, with the emperor holding a collection of republican powers; the later dominate was openly monarchical.

Severan dynasty and third-century crisis

After the Year of the Five Emperors (193), Septimius Severus founded the Severan dynasty (193–235), including Caracalla (211–217), who granted universal citizenship in 212, and Elagabalus (218–222), ending with Severus Alexander (222–235). The Crisis of the Third Century (235–284) saw dozens of emperors and claimants, including Maximinus Thrax, Philip the Arab, Valerian (captured by the Persians), Gallienus, Claudius Gothicus and Aurelian, the "restorer of the world".

From 395 the empire was permanently divided. The Western line ended with Romulus Augustulus, deposed in 476. The Eastern (Byzantine) emperors continued for nearly a thousand years, through the Heraclian, Isaurian, Macedonian, Komnenian, Angelid, Laskarid and Palaiologan dynasties, ending with Constantine XI, who died defending Constantinople against the Ottoman Turks on 29 May 1453.
