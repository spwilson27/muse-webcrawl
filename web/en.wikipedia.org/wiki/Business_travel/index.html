Business travel

Business travel is travel undertaken for work or business purposes, as opposed to other types of travel, such as for leisure purposes or commuting between home and workplace. According to the Global Business Travel Association, global business travel spending exceeded US$1.4 trillion in the mid-2020s, with the United States and China the largest markets.

Purposes

Common reasons include attending meetings, conferences and trade shows, sales visits, client relations, training, internal company meetings, and travel by specialists to install or repair equipment. Jobs in consulting, sales, engineering and aviation involve especially frequent travel.

Management

Companies commonly manage business travel through travel policies, preferred suppliers and travel management companies (TMCs), and may use corporate travel booking tools and expense-reporting software. Employers have a duty of care toward travelling employees, covering health, safety and security risks. Business travel is a major source of airline and hotel revenue and of carbon emissions, and substitution by videoconferencing expanded sharply after the COVID-19 pandemic.
