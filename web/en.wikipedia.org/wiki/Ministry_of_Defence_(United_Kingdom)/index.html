The Ministry of Defence (MoD) is the department of the Government of the United Kingdom responsible for implementing the defence policy set by His Majesty's Government and for the administration of the British Armed Forces. It is headed by the Secretary of State for Defence and is one of the largest departments of state, overseeing the Royal Navy, the British Army and the Royal Air Force.

History

The modern ministry grew out of the separate service ministries — the Admiralty, the War Office and the Air Ministry — whose coordination during the Second World War exposed the need for unified direction. A Minister of Defence was appointed in 1940, and in 1947 a small Ministry of Defence was created. The decisive step came in 1964, when the three service ministries were merged into a single unified Ministry of Defence under a Secretary of State for Defence, with the individual services retaining their own ministers and professional heads.

Organisation

The ministry's senior leadership pairs the Permanent Under Secretary, the senior civil servant, with the Chief of the Defence Staff, the professional head of the armed forces. Below them sit the single-service chiefs, the Vice-Chief of the Defence Staff, and functional organisations covering procurement and support (Defence Equipment and Support), infrastructure, and the Defence Nuclear Organisation, which manages the United Kingdom's nuclear deterrent based on Trident-carrying Vanguard-class and successor Dreadnought-class submarines.

Strategic direction is reviewed periodically through exercises such as the Strategic Defence and Security Review and the Integrated Review, which set force structure and spending priorities within a defence budget that is among the largest in NATO.

Main Building

The ministry is headquartered in Main Building on Whitehall in London, a monumental Portland stone structure completed in the 1950s on the site of the Palace of Whitehall. It was extensively refurbished under a private finance initiative in the early 2000s.

Responsibilities

The MoD's responsibilities include defending the United Kingdom and its overseas territories, maintaining the continuous at-sea nuclear deterrent, contributing to NATO collective defence, supporting international peacekeeping and humanitarian operations, and providing military aid to the civil authorities, together with the procurement of equipment and the welfare of service personnel and veterans.
