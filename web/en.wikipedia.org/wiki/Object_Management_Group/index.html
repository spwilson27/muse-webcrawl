The Object Management Group (OMG) is a computer industry standards consortium founded in 1989. OMG develops enterprise integration standards for a wide range of technologies. Its best-known specifications include the Unified Modeling Language (UML), the Common Object Request Broker Architecture (CORBA), the Business Process Model and Notation (BPMN), and the Data Distribution Service (DDS).

History and standards

OMG was founded by eleven companies, including IBM, Hewlett-Packard and Sun Microsystems, with the goal of promoting the object model as the basis for distributed application integration. Its specifications are developed by member organizations through task forces and adopted after a formal voting process. In addition to UML and CORBA, the OMG maintains the Systems Modeling Language (SysML), the Meta Object Facility (MOF), and standards for model-driven architecture (MDA).
