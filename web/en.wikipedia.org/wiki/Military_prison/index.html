Military prison

A military prison is a prison operated by a military organisation for the confinement of service personnel convicted under military law, and in some cases enemy combatants or prisoners of war. Military prisons differ from prisoner-of-war camps and from civilian prisons in administration and legal basis.

Examples

In the United States, the principal maximum-security facility is the United States Disciplinary Barracks at Fort Leavenworth, Kansas, while regional confinement facilities handle shorter sentences. In the United Kingdom, the Military Corrective Training Centre at Colchester combines detention with retraining, and many inmates return to duty on release.

Legal framework

Offences are tried under codes such as the Uniform Code of Military Justice in the United States or the Armed Forces Act in Britain. International humanitarian law, including the Geneva Conventions, sets standards for the treatment of prisoners of war held by military authorities.
