Silk Sonic is an American musical superduo composed of Bruno Mars and Anderson .Paak. The duo released their debut single, "Leave the Door Open", in March 2021, followed by their album An Evening with Silk Sonic in November 2021. Their music revives the lush soul, funk and R&B sounds of the 1970s, drawing on Motown, Philadelphia soul and artists such as Stevie Wonder and Earth, Wind & Fire.

Formation

Mars and .Paak met in 2017 when .Paak opened for Mars on the 24K Magic World Tour; informal studio jams developed into a full collaborative project, recorded largely at Shampoo Press & Curl Studios. Funk legend Bootsy Collins, who coined the name Silk Sonic, introduces the album as its host.

An Evening with Silk Sonic

The album's singles — "Leave the Door Open", "Skate" and "Smokin out the Window" — all reached the top of the charts; "Leave the Door Open" hit number one on the Billboard Hot 100. At the 64th Grammy Awards in 2022, Silk Sonic won all four of their nominations for the song, including Record of the Year and Song of the Year, and the album was widely acclaimed by critics. In 2022 the duo performed a Las Vegas residency, "An Evening with Silk Sonic at Park MGM". They later withdrew the album from Album of the Year consideration at the 2023 Grammys, saying it had already been amply honoured.

Style

Silk Sonic's sound is a meticulous homage to 1970s soul: live instrumentation, orchestral strings, falsetto harmonies and vintage production, paired with contemporary wit in the lyrics.
