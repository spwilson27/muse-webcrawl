Rajinikanth

Shivaji Rao Gaikwad (born 12 December 1950), known professionally as Rajinikanth, is an Indian actor who works primarily in Tamil cinema. In a career spanning over five decades, he has appeared in more than 170 films, including works in Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, and Malayalam. Widely regarded as one of the most popular and influential actors in Indian cinema, he is popularly called the "Superstar" by fans and media.

Early life

Rajinikanth was born in Bangalore, Karnataka, into a Marathi family. Before entering films, he worked as a bus conductor for the Bangalore Transport Service. He joined the Madras Film Institute and was discovered by director K. Balachander, who gave him his screen name and cast him in his debut, Apoorva Raagangal (1975).

Career

Rajinikanth began his career playing antagonistic and supporting roles before becoming a leading man in the late 1970s. Films such as Bairavi (1978), Mullum Malarum (1978), Thillu Mullu (1981), and later Baashha (1995), Muthu (1995), Padayappa (1999), Chandramukhi (2005), Sivaji (2007), and Enthiran (2010) cemented his mass-hero image, famous for stylised mannerisms, dialogue delivery, and signature gestures. His science-fiction film 2.0 (2018) was among the most expensive Indian films ever made. He has received numerous honours, including the Padma Bhushan (2000), the Padma Vibhushan (2016), and the Dadasaheb Phalke Award (2019), India's highest film honour.

Public image and politics

Rajinikanth commands an enormous and devoted fan following across South India and the Indian diaspora, and his films' releases are often treated as festivals. Though long speculated to enter politics, he announced a political party in late 2020 before withdrawing months later, citing health reasons.
