Colin Jackson

Colin Jackson
Born: 18 February 1967, Cardiff, Wales
Nationality: Welsh
Sport: Athletics (110 m hurdles)
Olympic medals: Silver, Seoul 1988

Colin Ray Jackson CBE (born 18 February 1967) is a Welsh former sprint and hurdling athlete who specialised in the 110 metres hurdles. One of Britain's most successful hurdlers, he won two World Championship gold medals, four European Championship titles, and an Olympic silver medal at the 1988 Seoul Games.

Career

Jackson set the world record for the 110 metres hurdles in 1993 at the World Championships in Stuttgart, running 12.91 seconds; the record stood for more than a decade. He also held the 60 metres hurdles world record of 7.30 seconds for nearly 27 years. Remarkably consistent, he won the European title at four consecutive championships from 1990 to 2002 and remained unbeaten at the European Championships throughout his career.

Style and legacy

Known for an exceptionally fast start and technical precision over the hurdles, Jackson was coached for much of his career by Malcolm Arnold. Injuries frequently interrupted his Olympic campaigns, and despite his dominance elsewhere he never won Olympic gold, finishing second in 1988 and suffering setbacks in 1992 and 1996.

Later life

After retiring in 2003, Jackson became a familiar athletics commentator and television presenter with the BBC, and has appeared in entertainment programmes including Strictly Come Dancing. He was appointed CBE for services to sport.
