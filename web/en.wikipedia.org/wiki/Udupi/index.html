Udupi

Udupi is a city in the Indian state of Karnataka, on the Arabian Sea coast. It is the administrative headquarters of Udupi district and is renowned as a centre of pilgrimage, education and cuisine.

Religion and culture

Udupi is famous for the Sri Krishna Matha, a temple and monastery founded in the 13th century by the Dvaita philosopher Madhvacharya, who established the eight Ashta Mathas that administer the temple in rotation through the Paryaya festival. The Kanakana Kindi, a small window through which the devotee Kanaka Dasa is said to have viewed the deity, remains a beloved feature.

Cuisine

Udupi cuisine, developed in the temple's kitchens, is a celebrated style of South Indian vegetarian cooking. Udupi restaurants spread across India and abroad, popularizing dishes such as the masala dosa and idli served with sambar and chutney. The nearby town of Manipal is a major education hub.
