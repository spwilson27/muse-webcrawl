Damian Wayne is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, commonly in association with Batman. He is the son of Bruce Wayne and Talia al Ghul, and thus the grandson of Ra's al Ghul, Batman's enemy. Raised and trained by the League of Assassins, Damian became the fifth character to assume the role of Robin, Batman's crime-fighting partner, serving alongside his father and later Dick Grayson (during Grayson's period as Batman).

Publication history

The character originated as the unnamed infant in the 1987 graphic novel Batman: Son of the Demon, a story long treated as outside continuity. Grant Morrison reintroduced the boy as Damian in Batman #655 (2006), beginning the "Batman and Son" storyline. Following Bruce Wayne's apparent death, Damian became Robin to Dick Grayson's Batman in the acclaimed Batman and Robin series (2009), and starred with Jon Kent (Superboy) in Super Sons. Damian was killed by his clone, the Heretic, in Batman Incorporated in 2013, and resurrected in 2014 during the "Robin Rises" storyline.

Characterisation

Damian is depicted as precocious, arrogant and initially willing to kill, reflecting his assassin upbringing; a recurring arc is his gradual adoption of his father's moral code and his struggle for Bruce's approval. He is a vegetarian in some depictions and is known for adopting animals, including the dog Titus and the cow "Bat-Cow".

In other media

Damian appears as Robin in animated films including Son of Batman (2014) and its sequels, in the DC Animated Movie Universe, and in series such as Teen Titans: The Judas Contract and Harley Quinn, as well as in video games in the Injustice series, where an adult Damian serves as Nightwing and later Robin under Superman's regime.
