Administrative divisions of the Philippines

The Philippines is divided into four levels of administrative divisions: regions, provinces, cities and municipalities, and barangays. The country has 82 provinces, 149 cities, 1,493 municipalities, and 42,001 barangays, the smallest unit of local government. Metro Manila is governed separately as the National Capital Region.

Regions

There are 17 administrative regions, which group provinces for administrative purposes; most have no separate political representation, except the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao, which has an elected parliament and chief minister. Regions include Ilocos, Cagayan Valley, Central Luzon, Calabarzon, Bicol, Western and Central Visayas, and the regions of Mindanao.

Local government

The Local Government Code of 1991 devolved significant powers to local government units. Provinces are led by elected governors; cities and municipalities by mayors; and each barangay by a barangay captain and council (sangguniang barangay). Highly urbanized cities are independent of provincial government.
