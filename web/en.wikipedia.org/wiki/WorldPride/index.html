WorldPride is a series of international LGBT pride events coordinated by InterPride, an international association of pride organisers, and hosted by a member city. The festival typically includes a parade, concerts, parties, human rights conferences and other cultural programming, and is intended to promote lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender and queer issues on an international stage.

History

The first WorldPride was held in Rome in 2000, despite opposition from the Vatican and local politicians, and coincided with the Great Jubilee year of the Catholic Church. Subsequent editions were held in Jerusalem (2006), London (2012, coinciding with the Summer Olympics), Toronto (2014), Madrid (2017) and New York City (2019, WorldPride NYC / Stonewall 50, marking the fiftieth anniversary of the Stonewall riots and drawing millions of visitors).

Copenhagen and Malmö jointly hosted WorldPride 2021, combined with the EuroGames. Sydney hosted WorldPride in 2023, the first edition in the Southern Hemisphere. Washington, D.C. was selected to host the 2025 edition, and Amsterdam was chosen for 2026, coinciding with the 25th anniversary of the first same-sex marriages in the Netherlands.

Selection

Host cities are selected by InterPride member organisations at annual general meetings, several years in advance. Bidding cities present campaigns emphasising their local LGBT communities, infrastructure and human rights record.
