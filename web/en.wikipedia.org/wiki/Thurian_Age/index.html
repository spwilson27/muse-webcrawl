The Thurian Age is a fictional prehistoric era created by American author Robert E. Howard as the setting for his stories of Kull of Atlantis, an Atlantean barbarian who becomes king of Valusia. In Howard's invented history, the Thurian Age precedes the Hyborian Age of his later hero Conan the Barbarian, from which it is separated by a great cataclysm that reshaped the world.

Setting

The civilisation of the Thurian Age is centred on the continent of Thuria, whose dominant power is the ancient, decadent empire of Valusia, the "Land of Enchantment". Other kingdoms include Commoria, Grondar, Kamelia, and snow-bound Thule. To the west lie the isles of Atlantis, whose people are regarded as barbarians by the Thurian kingdoms, and to the east the islands of Lemuria.

The Thurian peoples share the world with older, non-human races. The serpent-men, survivors of a pre-human civilisation, secretly infiltrate human society using illusion, and are the chief enemies of Kull. Elder horrors such as the wizard Thulsa Doom also feature in the tales.

The Cataclysm

Howard's essay and story fragments describe a Great Cataclysm that ends the Thurian Age: Atlantis and Lemuria sink beneath the sea, the Pictish Isles rise to become mountains, and the Thurian kingdoms are destroyed. Survivors of the catastrophe found the very different world of the Hyborian Age thousands of years later.

Stories

The Kull stories set in the Thurian Age were mostly published in Weird Tales from 1929, beginning with "The Shadow Kingdom", in which Kull, newly crowned king of Valusia, battles the serpent-men. Other tales include "The Mirrors of Tuzun Thune", "By This Axe I Rule!", and "Exile of Atlantis", Howard's account of Kull's youth before the kingship.
