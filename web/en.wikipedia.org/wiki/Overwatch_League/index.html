Overwatch League

The Overwatch League (OWL) was a professional esports league for the video game Overwatch, run by Blizzard Entertainment from 2018 to 2023. Modelled on traditional North American sports leagues, it featured city-based franchise teams with permanent spots, player minimum salaries and benefits.

History

Announced at BlizzCon 2016, the league's inaugural 2018 season fielded 12 teams and concluded with the London Spitfire winning the first Grand Finals at Barclays Center in Brooklyn. Expansion brought the league to 20 teams in 2019. After 2019 the league moved to a homestand model, which was quickly disrupted by the COVID-19 pandemic and a shift to online play; tournaments in later seasons were split between regions.

Dissolution

Following declining viewership and sponsorship, franchise owners voted in 2023 to exit the league, which ended after the 2023 season. Competitive Overwatch continued from 2024 under the Overwatch Champions Series (OWCS), an open-format circuit run with ESL FACEIT Group.
