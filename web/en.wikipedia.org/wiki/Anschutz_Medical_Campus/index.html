The University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus is the academic health sciences campus of the University of Colorado, located in Aurora, Colorado. It houses the university's School of Medicine, Skaggs School of Pharmacy and Pharmaceutical Sciences, College of Nursing, Colorado School of Public Health and School of Dental Medicine, together with the UCHealth University of Colorado Hospital and Children's Hospital Colorado.

History

The campus occupies the site of the former Fitzsimons Army Medical Center, which closed in the 1990s. Beginning in 1999, the university relocated its health sciences programs from Denver to the redeveloped Fitzsimons site. The campus was renamed in 2006 in recognition of major philanthropic gifts from Philip Anschutz and the Anschutz Foundation, among the largest donations then made to a medical campus in the United States.

Research and care

The campus is a major centre for biomedical research and clinical care in the Rocky Mountain region, with strengths in cancer, diabetes, neuroscience and child health. It is home to the Barbara Davis Center for Diabetes and the Linda Crnic Institute for Down Syndrome, and supports a large graduate and professional student population.
