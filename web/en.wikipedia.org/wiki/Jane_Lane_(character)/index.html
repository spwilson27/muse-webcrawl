Jane Lane is a fictional character from the MTV animated series Daria (1997–2002). Voiced by Wendy Hoopes, she is the best friend of the title character, Daria Morgendorffer, and a central character throughout the series and its two television films, Is It Fall Yet? (2000) and Is It College Yet? (2002).

Character

Jane is an aspiring artist attending Lawndale High School. Sardonic and creative, she shares Daria's contempt for the school's popularity culture but is more outgoing and socially adaptable than her friend. She lives in a chaotic household of artists: her largely absent parents, Vincent and Amanda, and her older siblings, including her slacker musician brother Trent, the frontman of the band Mystik Spiral, on whom Daria has a long-running crush.

Jane's storylines include her relationship with Tom Sloane — whose later romance with Daria caused the series' most serious rift between the two friends — her summer at an artists' colony, and her decision in the finale to attend the fictional Boston Fine Arts College (BFAC).

Reception

The friendship between Jane and Daria is widely regarded as the emotional core of Daria and as a notable portrayal of female friendship in animation. Jane's deadpan manner, red lipstick, and triangle-shaped hairstyle made her one of the show's most recognisable characters.
