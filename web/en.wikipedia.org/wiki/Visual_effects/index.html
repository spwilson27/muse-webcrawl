Visual effects

Visual effects (VFX) are effects created or manipulated in post-production to create environments, objects, or creatures that cannot be filmed practically.

Techniques

Techniques include CGI, compositing, matte painting, motion capture, and digital set extension. VFX differs from special effects (SFX), which are done on set.

Industry

Major studios include Industrial Light & Magic, Weta FX, and DNEG. The Academy Award for Best Visual Effects recognizes achievements since 1963.
