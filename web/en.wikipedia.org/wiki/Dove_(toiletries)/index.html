Dove (toiletries)

Dove
Owner: Unilever
Introduced: 1957 (United States)
Products: Soap bars, body wash, shampoo, deodorant
Campaign: Dove Campaign for Real Beauty (2004–)

Dove is a personal care brand owned by British consumer goods company Unilever. Its products are sold in more than 150 countries and are offered for women, men, and babies.

History

The Dove beauty bar, developed from a mild cleansing formula created for treating burns during the Second World War era research, launched in the United States in 1957, marketed as containing 'one-quarter cleansing cream' and not drying skin like ordinary soap. The line later expanded to body washes, lotions, hair care, and deodorants, and a Dove Men+Care range in 2010.

Real Beauty campaign

The 2004 Campaign for Real Beauty, featuring non-model women and films such as Evolution and Real Beauty Sketches, became a widely discussed case study in purpose marketing and body image, attracting both praise and criticism of Unilever's wider portfolio.
