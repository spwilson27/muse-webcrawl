Temple of the Moon

The Temple of the Moon (Huaca de la Luna) is a large adobe structure built by the Moche culture of northern Peru, near modern Trujillo. Together with the adjacent Huaca del Sol it forms the Huacas de Moche complex, the ceremonial centre of the Moche southern polity between roughly AD 100 and 800.

CultureMoche
LocationLa Libertad, Peru
MaterialAdobe brick

Description

Unlike the Huaca del Sol, which served administrative functions, the Huaca de la Luna was primarily religious. It consists of stepped platforms built over successive construction phases, enclosing earlier temples. Its polychrome murals depict the deity Ai Apaec and scenes of combat, capture and sacrifice, and excavations have found the remains of sacrificed warriors in its plazas.
