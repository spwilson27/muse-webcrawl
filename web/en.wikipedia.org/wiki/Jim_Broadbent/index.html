James Broadbent (born 24 May 1949) is an English actor. One of Britain's most versatile and prolific character actors, he won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor for playing John Bayley in Iris (2001), and is known to international audiences as Horace Slughorn in the Harry Potter films and Archmaester Ebrose in Game of Thrones.

Early life and career

Broadbent was born in Lincoln, the son of a furniture maker and amateur actor who co-founded a theatre company. He trained at the London Academy of Music and Dramatic Art, graduating in 1972, and worked widely in theatre, including with the National Theatre and the Royal Shakespeare Company, before building a screen career in the 1980s in films by Stephen Frears and Mike Leigh.

Film and television

Broadbent's collaborations with Mike Leigh — including Life Is Sweet (1990), Topsy-Turvy (1999), in which he played W. S. Gilbert, and Another Year (2010) — are among his most acclaimed work. He won the 2001 Oscar and a Golden Globe for Iris, a BAFTA for Moulin Rouge! (2001), and appeared in Bridget Jones's Diary, Hot Fuzz, The Iron Lady, Cloud Atlas and the Paddington films. On television he starred in Longford (2006) and War & Peace (2016).

Personal life

Broadbent married painter Anastasia Lewis in 1987. In 2017 he publicly declined to discuss an honour he had previously turned down, having refused an OBE in 2002 on the grounds that actors should not be fêted by the honours system.
