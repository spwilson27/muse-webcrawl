Adolf Hitler was an Austrian-born German politician who was dictator of Germany from 1933 until his suicide in 1945. He rose to power as leader of the Nazi Party, becoming Chancellor in 1933 and Führer in 1934, and initiated the Second World War in Europe by invading Poland in 1939.

Crimes

Hitler's regime perpetrated the Holocaust — the genocide of six million Jews — and the murder of millions of others, alongside a war that killed tens of millions. His racial ideology and expansionism make him a byword for evil; historians study how a democracy collapsed into totalitarianism, a warning running through the government and communism pages' contrasts.
