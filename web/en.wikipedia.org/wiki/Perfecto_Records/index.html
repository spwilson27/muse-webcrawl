Perfecto Records is a British electronic music record label founded in 1989 by DJ and producer Paul Oakenfold. One of the most influential labels of the trance and progressive house era, Perfecto launched and developed the careers of artists including Carl Cox (in his early years), Timo Maas, Quivver, and BT, and became especially famous for its remix work and for Oakenfold's own productions and compilations.

History

Oakenfold founded Perfecto after his success as a remixer and as resident DJ at London clubs during the acid house explosion. The label scored early crossover hits, including remixes for U2, Massive Attack, The Cure and Happy Mondays, and released Oakenfold's production projects such as Grace, whose "Not Over Yet" (1995) became a trance anthem. The label's sound — melodic, euphoric, club-focused — was central to the late-1990s global trance boom.

Perfecto operated through various partnerships, including with East West/Warner Music in the UK and later with Armada Music, and established a Las Vegas presence through Oakenfold's long-running "Perfecto" residency at Rain nightclub in the Palms Casino Resort, beginning in 2008. Sub-labels and series included Perfecto Fluoro, aimed at harder and psy-influenced trance, and the long-running Perfecto Presents mix compilation series.
