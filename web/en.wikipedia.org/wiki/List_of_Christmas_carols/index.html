List of Christmas carols

This is a list of Christmas carols — songs or hymns on the theme of Christmas, traditionally sung at Christmas itself or during the surrounding Christmas and holiday season. The list is organised by origin and includes both sacred carols and widely sung secular songs commonly grouped with them.

Carols from other languages
