The culinary arts are the creative and technical practices involved in preparing and presenting food. They encompass the work of professional cooks and chefs in restaurants, hotels and other food-service establishments, as well as the body of knowledge — techniques, traditions and aesthetic principles — that underlies cooking as a craft and an art form.

History

Cooking as a profession dates to antiquity: cooks served in the households of rulers in Egypt, Greece and Rome, and medieval guilds regulated the training of cooks and bakers in Europe. The modern restaurant emerged in Paris in the late 18th century, and in the 19th and early 20th centuries Auguste Escoffier systematised professional kitchen organisation (the brigade de cuisine) and codified French haute cuisine in Le Guide Culinaire (1903).

In the 20th century, television chefs such as Julia Child brought professional techniques to home audiences, while movements such as nouvelle cuisine and, later, molecular gastronomy reshaped fine dining. The culinary arts today combine classical technique with global influences, local sourcing and scientific approaches to flavour and texture.

Techniques

Core culinary techniques include knife skills, stock- and sauce-making, and the principal cooking methods: dry-heat methods (roasting, grilling, sautéing, frying, baking), moist-heat methods (boiling, simmering, steaming, braising, poaching) and combination methods. Mastery of heat control, seasoning and timing is considered fundamental. Presentation — the arrangement and visual composition of food on the plate, known as plating — is treated as an integral part of the discipline.

Education and careers

Formal training is offered by culinary schools such as Le Cordon Bleu (founded in Paris in 1895) and the Culinary Institute of America (founded 1946). Many chefs still train through apprenticeships, progressing through the ranks of the kitchen brigade from commis to chef de partie, sous-chef and executive chef. Culinary professionals also work in catering, product development, food writing and education.
