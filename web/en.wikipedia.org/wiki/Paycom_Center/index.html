Paycom Center is an arena located in Downtown Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, United States. It opened on June 8, 2002, at a cost of $89.2 million, and is the home venue of the Oklahoma City Thunder of the National Basketball Association (NBA).

History

The arena was built as part of Oklahoma City's Metropolitan Area Projects (MAPS) initiative, a capital improvement program funded by a temporary sales tax. Originally known as the Ford Center, it was built without an anchor major-league tenant, but was designed to NBA and NHL standards in hopes of attracting a franchise.

That hope was first realized after Hurricane Katrina, when the New Orleans Hornets temporarily relocated to Oklahoma City for the 2005–06 and 2006–07 seasons, playing as the New Orleans/Oklahoma City Hornets. In 2008 the Seattle SuperSonics moved to the city permanently and became the Oklahoma City Thunder. A major renovation in 2010–2011 upgraded suites, concessions, and locker rooms.

Naming rights

The venue took the Chesapeake Energy Arena name in 2011 under a naming-rights deal with Chesapeake Energy. After that agreement ended, Oklahoma City-based payroll and human-resources software company Paycom acquired the naming rights in 2021 under a 15-year agreement.

Events

Beyond Thunder games, the arena has hosted NCAA basketball tournament games, major concerts, bull riding, and UFC events. City voters approved funding in December 2023 for a new arena to replace Paycom Center, with the Thunder expected to move when it opens later in the decade.
