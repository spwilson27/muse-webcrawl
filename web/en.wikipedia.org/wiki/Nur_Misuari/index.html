Nurallaj Misuari (born March 3, 1939) is a Moro Filipino revolutionary and politician, founder and leader of the Moro National Liberation Front (MNLF). He led the group's armed struggle for Moro self-determination in Mindanao and later signed the 1996 Final Peace Agreement with the Philippine government, after which he served as governor of the Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (ARMM) from 1996 to 2001.

Early life and education

Misuari was born in Tapul, Sulu, to Tausug parents. He studied political science at the University of the Philippines Manila, initially entering on a scholarship from the Commission on National Integration, and became a student activist. He taught at the university before going underground to organize the Moro separatist movement in the early 1970s.

MNLF leadership

Misuari founded the MNLF, which waged an armed campaign for an independent Moro homeland. The movement signed the Tripoli Agreement in 1976 and, after years of negotiation, the 1996 peace accord. As ARMM governor, Misuari was criticized for poor governance and was removed in 2001; he was later arrested in connection with a failed uprising in Sulu. In 2013 his faction was implicated in the Zamboanga City siege, leading to charges of rebellion.
