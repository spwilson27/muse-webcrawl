The Atlantic is an American magazine and multi-platform publisher founded in Boston in 1857 as The Atlantic Monthly, by a circle including Ralph Waldo Emerson and Henry Wadsworth Longfellow. Known for long-form essays on politics, ideas and culture ('The Battle Hymn of the Republic' and 'The American Scholar' tradition), it is owned by Laurene Powell Jobs' Emerson Collective.

Modern era

From a loss-making literary monthly, the Atlantic became a digital-first success under editors James Bennet and Jeffrey Goldberg (editor from 2016), winning general-excellence National Magazine Awards and Pulitzers for feature writing; its 2020 'The Case for Reparations'-style agenda essays and its Ideas section continue its founding mission.
