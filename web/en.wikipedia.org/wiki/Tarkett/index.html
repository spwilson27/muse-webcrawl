Tarkett

Tarkett S.A. is a French multinational company specialising in flooring and sports surfaces. Headquartered in Paris La Défense, it is one of the world's largest producers of resilient flooring, manufacturing vinyl, linoleum, carpet, wood, laminate, and artificial turf products for residential, commercial, healthcare, education, and sports markets. The company traces its origins to 1886 and to the Sommer and Allibert businesses in France and to Tarkett AB in Sweden, whose lineage reaches back to a Swedish wood-products firm founded in the 1880s.

History

The group's Swedish arm descends from a Malmö woodworking business that began producing parquet and, in the 1940s, pioneered plastic flooring with the "Tarkett" product launched in 1947, from which the company took its name. The French arm grew from Sommer, a felt and floor-covering maker. The modern Tarkett group was formed in 1997 through the merger of Sommer-Allibert's flooring activities with Tarkett AB. Tarkett was listed on Euronext Paris in 2013; in 2021 the Deconinck family, with investor Wendel, took the company private in ownership terms through a majority holding while it remained publicly listed.

Operations

Tarkett sells in more than 100 countries and operates dozens of production sites across Europe, North America, and elsewhere. Its sports division, built around FieldTurf (artificial turf for stadiums and schools), is a major supplier of playing surfaces in North America. The acquisition of Dutch carpet maker Desso in 2015 strengthened its carpet-tile business.

Sustainability

Tarkett markets circular-economy initiatives including take-back and recycling programmes for vinyl and carpet, and Cradle to Cradle certification for some product lines, citing the long-standing linoleum tradition of natural raw materials.
