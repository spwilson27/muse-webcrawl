FAI World Grand Prix

The FAI World Grand Prix is a series of international air sports competitions organised under the auspices of the Fédération Aéronautique Internationale (FAI), the world air sports federation. The Grand Prix format brings elite pilots together in spectacular, spectator-friendly events, principally in aerobatics and air racing.

History

The World Grand Prix of aerobatics was launched in the mid-1990s as an invitational series for the world's top competition aerobatic pilots, with events staged at venues around the world. Competitors flew sequences in powerful unlimited-class aerobatic aircraft such as the Sukhoi Su-26 and Extra 300.

Format

Events typically combine compulsory and freestyle programmes, with the freestyle — often flown to music with smoke — judged on technical merit and artistic impression. The format was designed to make aerobatics accessible to television audiences and large live crowds.

Related events

The FAI also sanctions world championships in numerous air sports disciplines, including gliding, ballooning, parachuting and aeromodelling.
