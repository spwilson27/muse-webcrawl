Glory (kickboxing)

Glory, formerly Glory World Series, is an international kickboxing promotion founded in 2012. It is one of the largest kickboxing organisations in the world and has promoted events across Europe, North America, Asia and the Middle East, featuring many of the sport's top-ranked fighters.

The promotion was formed after the decline of K-1 and It's Showtime, when investors behind Glory Sports International acquired It's Showtime and assembled a roster that included stars such as Peter Aerts, Remy Bonjasky, Giorgio Petrosyan and Semmy Schilt. Its first event, Glory 1: Stockholm, took place in May 2012.

Glory crowned champions across weight divisions from heavyweight to featherweight; long-reigning heavyweight champion Rico Verhoeven became the promotion's most recognisable figure. The organisation has used formats including one-night tournaments, "Collision" superfight events and the Glory Grand Slam heavyweight tournament.
