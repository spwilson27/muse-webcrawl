List of rivers of Norway

Most of Norway's rivers are relatively short because of the country's narrow, mountainous terrain, but several in the east and north are among Scandinavia's longest. Norwegian rivers are important for hydroelectric power — Norway generates most of its electricity from hydropower — and for salmon fishing.
