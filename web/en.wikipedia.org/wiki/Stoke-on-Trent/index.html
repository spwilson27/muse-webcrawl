Stoke-on-Trent

County: Staffordshire, England
Population: approx. 258,000 (2021)
Nickname: The Potteries

Stoke-on-Trent is a city in Staffordshire, England, formed in 1910 from the federation of six towns: Stoke, Hanley, Burslem, Tunstall, Longton, and Fenton. Known as the Potteries, it was the world centre of ceramic production.

Pottery industry

Abundant coal and clay made the area ideal for pottery from the 17th century. Josiah Wedgwood founded his famous company here in 1759; Spode, Minton, and Royal Doulton followed. Bottle ovens dotted the skyline, of which dozens survive.

Culture

The city is the setting of Arnold Bennett's novels. Notable natives include Robbie Williams, Lemmy Kilmister, and Stanley Matthews. Its football clubs are Stoke City and Port Vale. The local delicacy is the oatcake.
