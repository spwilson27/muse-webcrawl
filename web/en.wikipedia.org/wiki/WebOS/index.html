webOS

webOS
Developer: LG Electronics
Original developer: Palm, Inc.
Initial release: 2009
Platforms: smartphones, tablets, smart TVs, IoT

webOS, also known as LG webOS, is a Linux-based operating system originally developed by Palm, Inc. for smartphones. It debuted in 2009 on the Palm Pre and was praised for its card-based multitasking interface and gesture navigation.

History

Palm launched webOS as the successor to Palm OS. After Hewlett-Packard acquired Palm in 2010, it released the HP TouchPad tablet running webOS in 2011, but discontinued its webOS hardware only weeks later. HP open-sourced parts of the system as Open webOS in 2012. In 2013 LG Electronics acquired the webOS source code and team from HP, repositioning it for smart televisions.

LG era

Under LG, webOS became the software platform for its smart TVs, with a redesigned launcher bar interface, and was later extended to projectors, monitors, signage and appliances. LG opened the platform to other TV brands through licensing. Features such as the card metaphor and Synergy contact integration in the original Palm version were widely regarded as ahead of their time and influenced later mobile interfaces.
