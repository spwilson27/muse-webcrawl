Punch-up in Piestany

The Punch-up in Piestany was a bench-clearing brawl at the 1987 World Junior Ice Hockey Championships in Piešťany, Czechoslovakia, during the final game between Canada and the Soviet Union on 4 January 1987.

Incident

The fight erupted in the second period with Canada leading 4–2, a score that put Canada on course for the gold medal. After a Soviet player slashed a Canadian, both benches emptied and fighting continued for around 20 minutes; officials at one point turned off the arena lights in an attempt to stop it. The International Ice Hockey Federation disqualified both teams from the tournament, costing Canada a medal and leading to the Soviet Union also losing its standing. The incident led to stricter IIHF rules on fighting and became one of the most famous events in junior hockey history, boosting the tournament's profile in Canada thereafter.
