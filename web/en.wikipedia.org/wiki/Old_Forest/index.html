Old Forest

The Old Forest is a fictional woodland in J. R. R. Tolkien's Middle-earth, appearing in The Fellowship of the Ring. It lies east of the Shire, between the Brandywine River and the Barrow-downs, and is a remnant of the vast ancient forests of Eriador.

Description

The Old Forest is a malevolent, semi-sentient wood: its trees watch travellers, paths shift, and the forest drives intruders toward the valley of the River Withywindle, ruled by Old Man Willow, a great and evil-hearted tree. The hobbits of Buckland keep a cleared strip, the Hay, between their hedge and the forest, and burn bonfires against its encroachment.

In the narrative

Frodo Baggins and his companions pass through the Old Forest on their way out of the Shire. They are lulled to sleep by Old Man Willow and rescued by Tom Bombadil, the forest's enigmatic master, who lives there with Goldberry.
