Seoul

Seoul, officially the Seoul Special City, is the capital and largest metropolitan city of South Korea, with about 9.4 million residents in the city and over 26 million in the Seoul Capital Area — almost half the country's population. It lies on the Han River in the northwest of the Korean Peninsula, only about 50 kilometres from the North Korean border.

Founded as the capital of Joseon in 1394 (as Hanseong), Seoul preserves five grand palaces, including Gyeongbokgung and Changdeokgung (UNESCO), amid one of the world's most advanced technology landscapes: Samsung and Hyundai are headquartered here, broadband and 5G are ubiquitous, and districts from Gangnam to Hongdae drive global K-pop culture. The city was devastated in the Korean War (1950-53), changing hands four times, then rebuilt in the 'Miracle on the Han River'. It hosted the 1988 Olympics and the 2002 FIFA World Cup (with Japan).
