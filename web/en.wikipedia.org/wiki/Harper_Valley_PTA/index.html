Harper Valley PTA

"Harper Valley PTA"
Single by Jeannie C. Riley (1968)
Songwriter: Tom T. Hall
Genre: Country
Awards: Grammy Award for Best Female Country Vocal Performance

"Harper Valley PTA" is a country song written by Tom T. Hall and recorded by Jeannie C. Riley in 1968. It became a number-one hit on both the Billboard Hot 100 and the US country charts, a rare cross-over success.

Story

The song tells of Mrs. Johnson, a widowed mother reprimanded by the local PTA for wearing short skirts and socialising; in response she attends a PTA meeting and exposes the members' own hypocrisies and scandals, declaring them "Harper Valley hypocrites".

Legacy

Riley won the Grammy Award for Best Female Country Vocal Performance. The song inspired a 1978 film of the same name starring Barbara Eden and a 1981–82 television sitcom.
