Liberty Island

Liberty Island is a federally owned island in Upper New York Bay in the northeastern United States, best known as the location of the Statue of Liberty. It lies within the territorial jurisdiction of New York City, close to Liberty State Park in New Jersey, and is accessible only by ferry.

History

Known to Native Americans and later called Bedloe's Island, the island hosted Fort Wood, a star-shaped fort whose base became the statue's pedestal. The Statue of Liberty, a gift from France designed by Frédéric Auguste Bartholdi, was dedicated in 1886. The island was renamed Liberty Island in 1956 and, with Ellis Island, forms Statue of Liberty National Monument. The Statue of Liberty Museum opened in 2019.
