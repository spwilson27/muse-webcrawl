List of fictional metals

This is a list of fictional metals, alloys and metalloid materials appearing in mythology, literature, film, comics and games. Such materials typically possess extraordinary properties — extreme strength, lightness, magical virtue or resistance to magic.

Some fictional metals, such as mithril and orichalcum, originate in myth and legend, while others were invented for modern science-fiction and fantasy franchises. The names adamantium and vibranium have occasionally been used informally by materials scientists for real alloys and metamaterials.
