Novo Nordisk A/S is a Danish multinational pharmaceutical company headquartered in Bagsværd, Denmark, with production facilities in nine countries and affiliates or offices in five countries. Novo Nordisk manufactures and markets pharmaceutical products and services, specifically diabetes care medications and devices. It is a world leader in diabetes care and also involved in haemophilia care, growth hormone therapy and hormone replacement therapy.

History

The company was created in 1989 through a merger of two Danish companies which date back to the 1920s. Nordisk Insulinlaboratorium was founded in 1923 by August Krogh, Marie Krogh and Hans Christian Hagedorn, after Krogh obtained permission to produce insulin in Denmark based on the Canadian discovery of insulin at the University of Toronto. A rival firm, Novo Terapeutisk Laboratorium, was founded in 1925 by former Nordisk employees. The two companies merged in 1989 to form Novo Nordisk.

Products

Novo Nordisk's major products include the diabetes medicines insulin aspart (NovoLog/NovoRapid), insulin glargine competitors, and the GLP-1 receptor agonists liraglutide (Victoza, Saxenda) and semaglutide (Ozempic, Rybelsus, Wegovy). Semaglutide became one of the best-selling drugs in the world in the 2020s, used for type 2 diabetes and obesity treatment.
