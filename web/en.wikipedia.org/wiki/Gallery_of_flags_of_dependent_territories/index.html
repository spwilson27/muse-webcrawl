Gallery of flags of dependent territories

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This is a gallery of flags of dependent territories — overseas territories, dependencies and constituent parts of sovereign states that possess distinctive flags of their own, whether officially adopted by law or in established civil use. It complements the gallery of sovereign state flags and lists flags grouped by the administering state, including both inhabited territories and uninhabited possessions.

Overview

Many dependent territories use defaced ensigns of the metropolitan flag. British Overseas Territories and Crown Dependencies historically use the Red or Blue Ensign charged with the territory's arms or badge (examples include Bermuda, the Cayman Islands, Gibraltar and the Falkland Islands), while others, such as the Isle of Man (triskelion) and Jersey, have unique designs. United States insular areas such as Puerto Rico, Guam, American Samoa and the U.S. Virgin Islands have their own official flags. French overseas collectivities (French Polynesia, New Caledonia, Saint Pierre and Miquelon in unofficial use), Dutch Caribbean municipalities and special municipalities (Bonaire, Sint Eustatius, Saba, Aruba, Curaçao, Sint Maarten), Danish territories (Greenland and the Faroe Islands), New Zealand's associated states (Cook Islands, Niue, Tokelau) and Australian external territories also appear in the gallery.

Organisation of the gallery

The full gallery arranges the flags by sovereign state — Australia, China (Hong Kong and Macau as special administrative regions), Denmark, France, the Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway (Bouvet Island, Queen Maud Land and Peter I Island), Spain, the United Kingdom and the United States — with the name of each territory, its flag's adoption year, and a short description of the design and any badge or arms.
