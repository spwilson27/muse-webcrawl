Persona 5 is a 2016 role-playing video game developed by Atlus. It is the sixth main instalment in the Persona series, part of the larger Megami Tensei franchise. The game takes place in modern-day Tokyo and follows a high school student known by the codename Joker who, after being falsely accused of assault and expelled, transfers to a new school and awakens to the power of Persona.

Story

Along with fellow students, Joker forms the Phantom Thieves of Hearts, a vigilante group that enters the Metaverse — a supernatural realm born of human cognition — to infiltrate the "Palaces" of corrupt adults and steal the distorted desires at their hearts, forcing them to confess their crimes in reality. A procedural dungeon, Mementos, complements the handcrafted Palaces. The narrative unfolds over a school year structured by a daily calendar, mixing social simulation with dungeon exploration.

Gameplay

Players balance school life and Confidant relationships with turn-based battles against Shadows. Personas are negotiated with, captured and fused in the Velvet Room. The game refined the series' combat with mechanics such as Baton Pass, Technical hits, and gun attacks.

Reception

Persona 5 was acclaimed for its visual style, acid-jazz soundtrack, gameplay and narrative, and is regarded as one of the greatest role-playing games of all time. An enhanced version, Persona 5 Royal (2019), added a new character, Kasumi Yoshizawa, a third school term and additional story content. Spin-offs include Persona 5 Strikers and Persona 5 Tactica.
