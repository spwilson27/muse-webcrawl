Roll Deep

Roll Deep are a British grime crew and record label founded in Bow, East London, in 2001 by the rapper and producer Wiley. They are considered one of the pioneering collectives of the grime genre, which emerged from the UK garage scene on pirate radio stations such as Deja Vu FM.

History

The crew's fluid membership at various times included Dizzee Rascal, Tinchy Stryder, Skepta, Jme, Flowdan, Breeze, Brazen, Scratchy, Manga Saint Hilare and DJ Target. Roll Deep's debut album, In at the Deep End (2005), won the Urban Music Award for best album. After moving toward a more commercial dance-pop sound, they scored two UK number-one singles in 2010, "Good Times" and "Green Light", both featuring uncredited vocals from Jodie Connor, followed by the top-ten single "Take Control" with Alesha Dixon.

Legacy

Roll Deep played a central role in the development of grime, and several former members became major solo artists, notably Wiley, Dizzee Rascal and Skepta.
