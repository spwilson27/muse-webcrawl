Miami Beach Architectural District

The Miami Beach Architectural District (also known as the Old Miami Beach Historic District, the Miami Art Deco District, or the Art Deco Historic District) is a U.S. historic district located in the South Beach neighborhood of Miami Beach, Florida. It contains the largest concentration of 1920s–1940s resort architecture in the United States, with some 960 historic buildings, most of them in the Art Deco, Mediterranean Revival, and Miami Modernism (MiMo) styles.

History

The district was added to the National Register of Historic Places on 14 May 1979 — the first 20th-century district so designated — following a campaign led by preservationist Barbara Baer Capitman, who founded the Miami Design Preservation League in 1976. The listing helped halt the demolition of the aging hotels along Ocean Drive, Collins Avenue, and Washington Avenue and sparked the revival of South Beach in the 1980s and 1990s.

Architecture

The district's Art Deco hotels and apartment buildings, built largely between the 1926 hurricane reconstruction and the Second World War, are characterized by pastel colors, symmetry, stepped-back rooflines, "eyebrow" sunshades over windows, neon signage, terrazzo floors, and nautical and tropical ornament — including porthole windows, ship railings, palm fronds, flamingos, and ocean-liner motifs sometimes called "Nautical Moderne" or Streamline Moderne. Prominent architects included Henry Hohauser, L. Murray Dixon, and Albert Anis. Later buildings in the district reflect postwar Miami Modernism, exemplified by Morris Lapidus's Fontainebleau and Eden Roc hotels further north on Collins Avenue.

Preservation and tourism

Today the district is the centerpiece of South Beach tourism, centered on the Ocean Drive strip facing Lummus Park and the beach. The Miami Design Preservation League operates the Art Deco Welcome Center and runs walking tours. The area faces ongoing challenges from sea-level rise and flooding, as well as development pressure, and the city has adopted adaptation measures including raised streets and pumps.
