Gwyllgi

Gwyllgi
Type: Mythical creature
Origin: Welsh folklore
Form: Black dog / mastiff
Meaning: "Dog of Darkness"

The gwyllgi (from Welsh gwyll, "twilight, darkness" and ci, "dog") is a mythical black dog in Welsh folklore, appearing as a huge, fearsome mastiff with baleful breath and blazing red eyes.

Folklore

The gwyllgi is said to haunt lonely roads and lanes at night, and its appearance was considered an omen of death. Travellers reported it stalking them silently before vanishing. It belongs to the wider tradition of black dog apparitions across Britain, comparable to the barghest and Black Shuck.

Cultural references

The creature appears in Welsh folk-tale collections and modern fantasy works drawing on Welsh mythology.
