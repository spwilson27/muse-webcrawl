League Championship Series

The League Championship Series is the semifinal round of the Major League Baseball postseason, contested in both the American League (ALCS) and the National League (NLCS). The winners of the two series meet in the World Series.

History

The LCS was created in 1969 when both leagues split into divisions, and it originally pitted the two division champions of each league against one another in a best-of-five series. The format expanded to best-of-seven in 1985, and later rounds of playoffs (wild card and Division Series) were added in the 1990s and 2010s.
