Isaac "Ike" Perlmutter (born December 1, 1942) is an Israeli-American businessman and financier. He gained control, through Toy Biz, of Marvel Entertainment following its bankruptcy in the 1990s, became its chief executive and later chairman, and sold it to Disney in 2009 for about $4 billion, receiving a large share of the consideration in Disney stock and becoming one of Disney's largest individual shareholders.

Early life and career

Born in Mandatory Palestine, Perlmutter served in the Israel Defense Forces during the Six-Day War and emigrated to the United States. He worked in retail and liquidation businesses, moved into toy distribution, and with Avi Arad took control of the toy company Toy Biz, which in 1998 merged with Marvel after its Chapter 11 reorganisation.

Marvel and Disney

As Marvel's chief executive, Perlmutter was known for extreme frugality and tight cost control. He remained chairman of Marvel Entertainment after the Disney acquisition, overseeing publishing, television, and (until a 2015 reorganisation) film-related decisions through the Marvel Creative Committee. In March 2023, Disney laid him off as part of a cost-cutting reorganisation that folded Marvel Entertainment into other Disney units. In 2023 he also waged an unsuccessful proxy fight, alongside activist investor Nelson Peltz, for seats on Disney's board, and later sold most of his Disney shares.

Philanthropy and politics

Perlmutter and his wife Laura are major philanthropists, notably endowing cancer research at NYU Langone Medical Center, and he has been a prominent political donor and informal adviser at Mar-a-Lago on veterans' affairs.
