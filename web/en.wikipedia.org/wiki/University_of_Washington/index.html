The University of Washington (UW) is a public research university in Seattle, Washington. Founded in 1861, it is one of the oldest universities on the U.S. West Coast and enrols more than 47,000 students across its Seattle campus and branch campuses in Bothell and Tacoma.

Academics and research

UW is among the largest recipients of federal research funding of any American university, with particular strengths in medicine, oceanography, computer science, and engineering. Its medical school and the UW Medical Center are leading research and teaching institutions. The campus is famous for its Collegiate Gothic architecture, the Suzzallo Library reading room, and cherry blossoms in the Quad.

Athletics

The Washington Huskies compete in the Big Ten Conference (joining in 2024); the football team plays at Husky Stadium, and the crew program has a storied history dating to the 1936 Olympic gold-medal eight.
