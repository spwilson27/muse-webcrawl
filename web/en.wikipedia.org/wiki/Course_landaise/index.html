Course landaise

The course landaise is a traditional form of bull spectacle practised in the Landes and neighbouring departments of southwestern France. Unlike Spanish bullfighting, the animals — cows of the Brave breed — are not harmed, and the spectacle centres on the athleticism of the participants.

Format

Performers called écarteurs dodge the charging cow at the last moment, while sauteurs leap over it in somersaults and other acrobatic jumps. The cow is guided by a rope held by the cordier. Competitions are scored, with championships held through the season; the tradition is recognised as part of the cultural heritage of Gascony.
