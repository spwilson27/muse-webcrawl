The 2024 UEFA European Football Championship, commonly referred to as UEFA Euro 2024 or simply Euro 2024, was the 17th edition of the UEFA European Championship, the quadrennial international football championship organised by UEFA for the European men's national teams. Germany hosted the tournament, which took place from 14 June to 14 July 2024 and was contested by 24 teams. Georgia, one of the 24 teams, made their European Championship debut.

Tournament summary

Spain won the tournament for a record fourth time after defeating England 2–1 in the final at the Olympiastadion in Berlin. Nico Williams opened the scoring early in the second half; Cole Palmer equalised for England, before Mikel Oyarzabal scored the winning goal in the 86th minute. Spain won all seven of their matches, the first team to do so at a European Championship since France in 1984.

Hosts Germany were eliminated by Spain in the quarter-finals in a match noted for a late equaliser by Florian Wirtz and an extra-time winner by Mikel Merino. Defending champions Italy were knocked out by Switzerland in the round of 16.

Venues

Matches were played at ten stadiums in ten cities: Berlin, Munich, Dortmund, Stuttgart, Gelsenkirchen, Hamburg, Leipzig, Cologne, Frankfurt, and Düsseldorf. The opening match, Germany's 5–1 win over Scotland, was held at the Munich Football Arena.

Awards

Rodri of Spain was named Player of the Tournament, and his teammate Lamine Yamal, who turned 17 the day before the final, was named Young Player of the Tournament. Six players shared the top scorer honours with three goals each.
