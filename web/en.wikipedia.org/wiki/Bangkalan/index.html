Bangkalan

Bangkalan is a town in East Java, Indonesia, and the capital of Bangkalan Regency on the western end of the island of Madura. It lies across the Madura Strait from the port of Surabaya and serves as the principal gateway to Madura from Java.

Geography

Bangkalan occupies the south-western coastal plain of Madura, facing the Madura Strait. Since 2009 the Suramadu Bridge, Indonesia's longest bridge, has connected the town's vicinity directly with Surabaya, replacing the ferry crossing that previously carried most traffic.

History

The area was historically the seat of a Madurese princely house under the influence of the Mataram Sultanate and later the Dutch East India Company. The town's Grand Mosque and the Arosbaya area's carved red-stone mosque reflect its long Islamic heritage.

Economy

Local livelihoods include fishing, salt production, batik making, and agriculture. Bangkalan is also known for its spicy duck dish, bebek sinjay, which draws culinary tourists from Surabaya.
