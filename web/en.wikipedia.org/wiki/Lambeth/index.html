Lambeth

Lambeth is a district of south London, England, in the London Borough of Lambeth, historically in the county of Surrey. It is situated on the south bank of the River Thames, opposite Westminster.

History

Lambeth is recorded in the Domesday Book. Lambeth Palace, the London residence of the Archbishops of Canterbury since the 13th century, stands by the river. The area grew rapidly in the 19th century with the arrival of the railways and the development of industry along the Thames, including the Doulton pottery works.

Landmarks

Notable landmarks include Lambeth Palace, St Thomas' Hospital, the former County Hall, and Waterloo station nearby. The district gives its name to the Lambeth Walk, made famous by the musical Me and My Girl.
