Wood Green

Wood Green is a suburban district in the London Borough of Haringey in north London, England. Centred on a busy section of the High Road, it is one of the metropolitan centres identified in the London Plan.

History

Wood Green was historically a wooded hamlet in the parish of Tottenham. Development accelerated after the opening of the Great Northern Railway in the 1850s and Alexandra Palace in 1873. It became an urban district and later a municipal borough in Middlesex before joining Haringey in 1965.

Transport

Wood Green tube station on the Piccadilly line opened in 1932 and is a listed Charles Holden design. Alexandra Palace station provides National Rail services, and numerous bus routes serve the High Road and Turnpike Lane.

Culture and amenities

The Mall (formerly Shopping City) is the main retail centre. Nearby are Alexandra Palace and Park, the Green Rooms arts hotel, Haringey Civic Centre, and several cinemas. The area has notable Greek Cypriot, Turkish and other communities reflected in its shops and restaurants.

Governance

Wood Green is represented in Parliament as part of the Hornsey and Friern Barnet constituency (formerly Hornsey and Wood Green) and locally by Haringey Council wards including Noel Park and Woodside.
