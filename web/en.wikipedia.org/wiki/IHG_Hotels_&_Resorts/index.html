IHG Hotels & Resorts

British multinational hospitality company

An InterContinental Hotels & Resorts property

InterContinental Hotels Group plc, trading as IHG Hotels & Resorts, is a British multinational hospitality company headquartered in Windsor, England. It is one of the world's largest hotel groups by number of rooms, with several thousand hotels across roughly one hundred countries, most owned and operated by third parties under franchise or management agreements.

Brands

IHG's brand portfolio spans luxury, premium, mainstream and extended-stay segments and includes InterContinental, Six Senses, Regent, Kimpton, Hotel Indigo, Crowne Plaza, voco, Holiday Inn, Holiday Inn Express, Staybridge Suites, Candlewood Suites, avid hotels, Garner and Iberostar Beachfront Resorts. Its loyalty programme is IHG One Rewards.

History

The company traces its roots to the Bass brewery business and its Holiday Inn acquisition, and to the InterContinental brand founded by Pan American Airways in the 1940s. The hotels business was demerged from Six Continents (the former Bass) in 2003, creating InterContinental Hotels Group as an independent listed company.
