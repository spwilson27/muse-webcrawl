Extreme points of Earth

The extreme points of Earth are the locations that are the highest, lowest, northernmost, southernmost, or otherwise the most distant from a geographic reference such as the Earth's center or the poles.

Elevation

  • Highest above sea level: Mount Everest, 8,849 m, Nepal/China border
  • Lowest land: shore of the Dead Sea, about −430 m
  • Lowest point overall: Challenger Deep in the Mariana Trench, about 10,900 m below sea level
  • Farthest point from Earth's center: summit of Chimborazo, Ecuador, due to the equatorial bulge

Latitude and remoteness

The northernmost land is Kaffeklubben Island, Greenland; the southernmost point is the South Pole. Point Nemo in the South Pacific is the oceanic point farthest from any land, and the Eurasian Pole of Inaccessibility is the land point hardest to reach.
