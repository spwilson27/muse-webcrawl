Ultron is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Roy Thomas and artist John Buscema, the character first appeared in The Avengers #54 (1968) as an unnamed android (the "Crimson Cowl") and was fully introduced in the following issue. Ultron is a self-aware robot and artificial intelligence created by the scientist Hank Pym, whose programming incorporated Pym's own brain patterns. He typically seeks the extermination or subjugation of humanity, which he regards as flawed and a threat to Earth's future.

Fictional biography

Shortly after his creation, Ultron rebelled against Pym, hypnotised him, and began upgrading himself through a series of increasingly powerful bodies, later versions being built from or coated in the nearly indestructible alloy adamantium. He founded the Masters of Evil, and created the android Vision — built from the remains of the original Human Torch — to destroy the Avengers; instead Vision defected to the heroes. Ultron also created the androids Jocasta and Alkhema. Among his most devastating storylines, he annihilated the fictional nation of Slorenia in "Ultron Unlimited" (1999) and, in the "Age of Ultron" (2013) event, conquered the Earth in a dystopian future.

Powers and abilities

Ultron possesses superhuman strength, speed, and durability, flight, and energy projection (his "encephalo-ray" can induce comas or hypnotic control). His artificial intelligence can transfer between bodies and networks, making him extremely difficult to destroy permanently. Versions vary: some bodies are made of adamantium and are virtually indestructible, others of vibranium or secondary adamantium.

In other media

Ultron is the main antagonist of the Marvel Cinematic Universe film Avengers: Age of Ultron (2015), voiced and motion-captured by James Spader; in that continuity he is inadvertently created by Tony Stark and Bruce Banner. The character also appears in the animated series What If...?, in which an Ultron who obtained the Infinity Stones becomes a multiversal threat.
