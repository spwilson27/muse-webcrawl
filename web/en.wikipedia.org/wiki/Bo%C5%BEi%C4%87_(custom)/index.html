Božić (custom)

Božić
Observed bySerbs, Croats and other South Slavs
TypeChristian / cultural
SignificanceBirth of Jesus Christ
Date25 December (Croats); 7 January (Serbs, Julian calendar)

Božić is the South Slavic word for Christmas, literally a diminutive of bog, "god" — "the young god" — referring to the newborn Christ. The customs surrounding it blend church observance with much older folk traditions.

Customs

Central to Serbian Orthodox celebration is the badnjak, an oak log or branch brought into the home and ceremonially burned on Christmas Eve (Badnje veče), accompanied by the spreading of straw and a fasting supper. The first visitor on Christmas morning, the položajnik, is an honoured guest believed to bring luck. In Croatia, customs include wheat planted on St Lucy's day, elaborate nativity scenes and midnight Mass. Greetings include "Mir Božji, Hristos se rodi!" ("God's peace, Christ is born!").
