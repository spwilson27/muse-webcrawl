New Providence

New Providence
Country: The Bahamas
Capital on island: Nassau
Population: roughly 250,000 (about 70% of the Bahamas)

New Providence is the most populous island in the Bahamas, containing the capital city of Nassau and more than 70 percent of the country's population, although it is only the archipelago's eleventh largest island by area. It lies between Andros to the west and Eleuthera to the east.

History

English settlers from Bermuda established a colony on the island in the 17th century. In the early 1700s Nassau became a notorious haven for pirates — the self-declared Republic of Pirates associated with figures such as Blackbeard — until royal governor Woodes Rogers arrived in 1718 to restore order. During the American Revolutionary War the island was briefly captured by American forces, and it later received Loyalist settlers after the war.

Today

New Providence is the commercial and governmental centre of the Bahamas. Cable Beach and Paradise Island, joined to the island by bridges, host large resort developments, and the port of Nassau receives large numbers of cruise ship visitors. Lynden Pindling International Airport, the country's main gateway, is in the island's west.
