List of largest oil and gas companies

The largest oil and gas companies in the world can be ranked by revenue, by production volume, or by reserves. National oil companies (NOCs) such as Saudi Aramco, the National Iranian Oil Company and CNPC dominate reserves and production, while the publicly traded "supermajors" — ExxonMobil, Shell, BP, Chevron, TotalEnergies and ConocoPhillips — are among the largest by revenue and market value.

By production

By barrels of oil equivalent per day, Saudi Aramco is the largest producer (~12–13 million boe/d including gas), followed by the National Iranian Oil Company, CNPC, ExxonMobil (around 4.5–5 million boe/d after its 2024 acquisition of Pioneer Natural Resources), Rosneft, PetroChina, Kuwait Petroleum, ADNOC, Chevron and Shell.
