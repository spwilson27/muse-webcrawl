DoorDash

DoorDash, Inc. is an American company that operates an online food ordering and delivery platform. Founded in 2013 in Palo Alto, California, by Tony Xu, Stanley Tang, Andy Fang, and Evan Moore, it is the largest food delivery company in the United States. Deliveries are made by independent contractors known as "Dashers".

History

The service began as PaloAltoDelivery.com in 2013 before being renamed DoorDash, and it was part of the Y Combinator accelerator programme. The company expanded rapidly across US cities, competing with Uber Eats and Grubhub, and grew dramatically during the COVID-19 pandemic as demand for restaurant delivery surged. DoorDash held its initial public offering on the New York Stock Exchange in December 2020.

Expansion and acquisitions

DoorDash acquired the rival service Caviar in 2019 and the Finnish delivery company Wolt in a deal announced in 2021, extending its business into Europe and other international markets. It has also expanded beyond restaurant food into groceries, convenience goods, alcohol and retail delivery, and offers a subscription service, DashPass.

Criticism

The company has faced criticism and litigation over its tipping and pay practices for Dashers, commissions charged to restaurants, and the classification of delivery workers as independent contractors, an issue central to California's Proposition 22 in 2020, which DoorDash supported.
