BBC Cymru Wales

BBC Cymru Wales is the national broadcaster of Wales within the BBC. Broadcasting began in Wales in 1923, when the Cardiff station 5WA opened, and a distinct Welsh service developed through the BBC Welsh Region. A dedicated television service, BBC Wales, was launched on 1 February 1964, shortly before the opening of dedicated studios at Broadcasting House in Llandaff, Cardiff.

Services

BBC Cymru Wales operates the BBC One Wales and BBC Two Wales television services with Welsh news and programming opt-outs, the English-language BBC Radio Wales and the Welsh-language BBC Radio Cymru, and online services in both languages. It is a major supplier of programmes to the Welsh-language channel S4C, including the long-running soap Pobol y Cwm, made since 1974.

Production in Wales

Cardiff has become a major centre of BBC network drama production. The revived Doctor Who (from 2005), Torchwood, Sherlock, and Casualty have been made in Wales, with drama production concentrated from 2012 at the Roath Lock studios in Cardiff Bay. In 2020 staff moved from Llandaff to a new headquarters at Central Square in central Cardiff, one of the first BBC buildings designed around file-based production.
