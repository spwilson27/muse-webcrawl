Sussex Spaniel

The Sussex Spaniel is a breed of spaniel dog originating in Sussex, England, in the late 18th and 19th centuries. It is a low, compact, slow-moving gundog developed to work in the dense undergrowth of the Sussex Weald, and it is unusual among spaniels for giving tongue (barking) while working game.

Appearance

The breed has a long, low body, a massive head with a frowning expression, and a thick coat of a rich golden liver colour. Dogs stand about 15–16 inches at the shoulder and weigh roughly 20–23 kg.

History and temperament

The breed was developed around 1795 onward by breeders in the Hastings area and was exhibited at the first dog shows in the 1860s. Numbers fell sharply in the 20th century, and the Sussex remains a rare breed. In temperament it is calm, steady and friendly, slower and more deliberate than other spaniels, and it makes a placid companion.
