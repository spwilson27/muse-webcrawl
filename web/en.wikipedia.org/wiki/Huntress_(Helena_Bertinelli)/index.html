Huntress (Helena Bertinelli)

The Huntress is a superheroine appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. The modern version, Helena Bertinelli, is the daughter of a murdered Mafia family who becomes a violent anti-hero and vigilante in Gotham City, often clashing with Batman over her willingness to kill. She is associated with the Birds of Prey and the Justice League.

Publication history

Helena Bertinelli was introduced in 1989, replacing the earlier Golden Age/Earth-Two Huntress, Helena Wayne, daughter of Batman and Catwoman. The Bertinelli version's origin was retold in Batman/Huntress: Cry for Blood (2000). She became a core member of Birds of Prey under writer Gail Simone.

Fictional biography

Born into the Bertinelli crime family, Helena witnessed her family's murder at age eight and was raised in Sicily, training in combat and weaponry. Returning to Gotham, she wages war on the Mafia. Her lethal methods bring her into conflict with Batman, though she has served with the Justice League and as a member of the Birds of Prey alongside Black Canary and Oracle.

Other media

The character has appeared in animated series including Justice League Unlimited, and was portrayed by Mary Elizabeth Winstead in the film Birds of Prey (2020).
