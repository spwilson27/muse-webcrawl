List of multinational festivals and holidays

This is a list of multinational festivals and holidays — festivals, observances and public holidays that are celebrated in many countries around the world, whether for religious, cultural, historical or commercial reasons. Some, such as New Year's Day and Christmas, are public holidays in dozens of states; others, such as Halloween and Valentine's Day, are widely observed culturally without official status in most places.

Fixed-date observances

DateFestival / holidayNotes
1 JanuaryNew Year's DayFirst day of the Gregorian calendar year; a public holiday in most countries.

1 MayInternational Workers' Day (May Day / Labour Day)Public holiday in most countries, honouring workers.
31 OctoberHalloweenEve of All Saints' Day; trick-or-treating and costume parties in many countries.
25 DecemberChristmas DayBirth of Jesus; public holiday across the Christian world and beyond.
26 DecemberBoxing Day / St Stephen's DayPublic holiday in Commonwealth countries and parts of Europe.
