Greece

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

[Image: The Parthenon on the Acropolis of Athens at sunset]

Greece, officially the Hellenic Republic, is a country in Southeast Europe at the southern tip of the Balkans, with thousands of Aegean and Ionian islands. Its capital and largest city is Athens. Greece is regarded as the cradle of Western civilisation — birthplace of democracy, Western philosophy, theatre, the Olympic Games and much mathematics and science.

Contents1. History

History

Ancient Greek city-states such as Athens and Sparta flourished in the 5th century BC; Alexander's conquests spread Hellenistic culture, followed by Roman, Byzantine and Ottoman rule. Modern Greece won independence in the 1820s–30s, and is today an EU and NATO member whose economy rests heavily on shipping and tourism.
