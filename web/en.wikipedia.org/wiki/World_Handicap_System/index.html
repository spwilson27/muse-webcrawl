World Handicap System

The World Handicap System (WHS) is a unified system for handicapping golfers, introduced in 2020 by The R&A and the United States Golf Association (USGA) together with golf's national authorities. It replaced the six pre-existing handicap systems used around the world.

Overview

The WHS is based on the average of the best eight of a player's most recent 20 score differentials, adjusted for course rating, slope rating and daily playing conditions. It aims to be portable across countries and formats, allow golfers of differing abilities to compete equitably, and incorporate safeguards against manipulation and exceptional scores.
