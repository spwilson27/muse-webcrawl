Education in Bangladesh

Education in Bangladesh is administered by the Ministry of Education and the Ministry of Primary and Mass Education. The system has three main tiers: primary education (grades 1–5), secondary education (grades 6–12) and tertiary education at universities and colleges. Primary education is compulsory and free in government schools.

Structure

Secondary education is divided into lower secondary (grades 6–8), secondary (grades 9–10), ending with the Secondary School Certificate (SSC) examination, and higher secondary (grades 11–12), ending with the Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC). Alongside the general stream there are madrasa (religious) and technical-vocational streams. Bangladesh has several boards of intermediate and secondary education that conduct the public examinations.

Higher education

The oldest university is the University of Dhaka, founded in 1921. Other major public universities include Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), the University of Rajshahi, the University of Chittagong and Jahangirnagar University, alongside a large private university sector that grew from the 1990s onward. The medium of instruction at primary and secondary level is mainly Bengali, with an English-medium stream offered by some private schools.
