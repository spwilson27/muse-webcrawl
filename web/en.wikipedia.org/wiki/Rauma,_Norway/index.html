Rauma, Norway

Rauma is a municipality in Møre og Romsdal county, Norway, located in the traditional district of Romsdal. The administrative centre is the town of Åndalsnes. The municipality is named after the Rauma River, which flows through the Romsdalen valley.

Rauma is known for dramatic alpine scenery including Trollveggen (the Troll Wall), the highest vertical rock face in Europe, and the Rauma Line railway, which crosses the Kylling Bridge. Tourism, agriculture and manufacturing are important local industries.
