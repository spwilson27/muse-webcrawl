The Shuto Expressway (首都高速道路, Shuto Kōsoku-dōro) is a network of toll expressways in the Greater Tokyo Area of Japan, operated by the Metropolitan Expressway Company Limited. The first section opened in 1962 ahead of the 1964 Tokyo Olympics.

Network

The system comprises radial routes numbered 1–11 radiating from central Tokyo, inner and central circular routes (C1 and C2), and the Bayshore Route along Tokyo Bay, famed for the Rainbow Bridge. Much of the network is elevated, threading between buildings and over canals, with notably tight curves and low speed limits of 50–60 km/h on older sections.

Culture

The expressway's Wangan (bayshore) route became famous through the manga and anime Wangan Midnight and street-racing culture centred on rest areas such as Daikoku Futo.
