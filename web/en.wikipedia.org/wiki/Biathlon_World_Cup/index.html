Biathlon World Cup

[Image placeholder: Biathlon World Cup competition]

The Biathlon World Cup is a top-level biathlon season-long competition series. It has been held since the winter seasons of 1977–78 for men and 1982–83 for women. The competitions for the season are organized by the International Biathlon Union (IBU) and take place at venues across Europe and occasionally North America and Asia.

Points are awarded in each race — sprint, pursuit, individual, mass start and relays — and the athlete with the highest total at the end of the season wins the overall World Cup, symbolized by the crystal globe. Separate globes are awarded for each discipline.

Records

Norway's Ole Einar Bjørndalen won six overall titles, while Frenchman Martin Fourcade won seven consecutive titles between 2012 and 2018. Magdalena Forsberg of Sweden won six consecutive women's overall titles. The yellow bib is worn by the current overall leader in each race.
