LCOS

LCOS or LCoS may refer to:

  • Liquid crystal on silicon, a micro-projection and display technology that combines liquid crystal layers with a silicon backplane, used in projectors and near-eye displays.
  • LCOS, the abbreviation for the League of Conservation Voters' environmental scorecard in some contexts.

Liquid crystal on silicon

Liquid crystal on silicon (LCoS) is a miniaturized reflective liquid-crystal display technology. Unlike transmissive LCD panels, an LCoS device forms its image on a silicon chip coated with a reflective aluminium layer; light is modulated by a nematic liquid crystal layer and reflected back through polarizing optics. LCoS panels are used in high-resolution projection televisions, video projectors, head-mounted displays, and optical switching equipment such as wavelength-selective switches in fibre-optic networks.

Because the electronics sit behind the reflective surface rather than between pixels, LCoS devices achieve a high aperture ratio and smooth, film-like images. Commercial variants include JVC's D-ILA and Sony's SXRD technologies.
