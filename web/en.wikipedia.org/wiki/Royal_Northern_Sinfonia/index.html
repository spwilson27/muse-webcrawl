Royal Northern Sinfonia

The Royal Northern Sinfonia is a chamber orchestra based at Sage Gateshead (now The Glasshouse International Centre for Music), on the south bank of the River Tyne in Gateshead, England. Founded in 1958 by Michael Hall, it was the only full-time professional chamber orchestra in England for much of its history and is the resident orchestra of The Glasshouse. It was granted the title "Royal" in 2013, becoming Royal Northern Sinfonia on its 55th anniversary.

History

Michael Hall, a former BBC producer, founded the orchestra in Newcastle upon Tyne in 1958, with an initial ensemble of around 39 players. As the Northern Sinfonia it built a reputation for performances of Baroque, Classical and contemporary repertoire, and toured extensively throughout Europe, Asia and the Americas. Early artistic leaders included clarinettist and conductor figures closely associated with the ensemble's formative years. In 2004 the orchestra moved into the newly opened Sage Gateshead concert hall complex.

In June 2013, during its 55th birthday season, the orchestra was granted the title "Royal" by Elizabeth II, an honour recognising its national and international standing.

Artistic leadership

Thomas Zehetmair served as music director from 2002 to 2014, combining the roles of conductor and violinist, and led notable cycles of Beethoven, Brahms and Schumann symphonies. Lars Vogt succeeded him as music director in 2015; the German pianist, a close collaborator of the orchestra, died in September 2022 while in post. Dinis Sousa, previously principal guest conductor, took up the role of principal conductor and artistic partner. The orchestra's leader is violinist Maria Włoszczowska in recent seasons.

Repertoire and recordings

The orchestra performs a broad repertoire from the Baroque era to newly commissioned works, and has given premieres of pieces by composers including John Casken, Sally Beamish and Erkki-Sven Tüür. It has recorded extensively, including cycles of Mozart piano concertos with Lars Vogt and recordings for labels such as Avie, BIS and Chandos. The orchestra also tours throughout the North of England and appears at festivals including the BBC Proms.
