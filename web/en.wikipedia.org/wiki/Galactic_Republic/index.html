The Galactic Republic is a fictional state in the Star Wars universe that predates the formation of the Galactic Empire. Comprising the galaxy's democratic union of star systems for roughly a thousand years before the events of the films, the Republic is governed by the Galactic Senate on the capital world of Coruscant, presided over by an elected Supreme Chancellor, and is protected by the Jedi Order, which serves as its peacekeepers.

Depiction

The Republic is the principal government depicted in the prequel trilogy (1999–2005). In The Phantom Menace, Chancellor Valorum is ousted amid a trade dispute engineered by Senator Palpatine, who is elected in his place. In Attack of the Clones and Revenge of the Sith, the separatist crisis and the Clone Wars lead the Senate to grant Palpatine sweeping emergency powers; the Republic acquires a Grand Army of clone troopers and becomes increasingly authoritarian. At the war's end Palpatine — secretly the Sith Lord Darth Sidious — destroys the Jedi through Order 66 and proclaims the Republic's transformation into the Galactic Empire.

Background

In the expanded fiction, the Old Republic is said to have been founded some 25,000 years before the films, and to have been reformed following the Ruusan Reformation about a millennium before them, after which it maintained no standing army and relied on the Jedi. Story material distinguishes the Old Republic, the High Republic era, and the late Republic of the prequel films.
