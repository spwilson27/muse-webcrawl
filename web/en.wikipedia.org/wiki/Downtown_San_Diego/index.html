Downtown San Diego

Downtown San Diego is the central business district of San Diego, California, the eighth-largest city in the United States. Located on San Diego Bay, downtown comprises several distinct neighbourhoods, including the Core, the Gaslamp Quarter, East Village, Little Italy, the Marina District, and Cortez Hill. The Gaslamp Quarter, a historic district of Victorian-era buildings, is the centre of the downtown nightlife and dining scene.

Features

Downtown landmarks include Petco Park, home of the San Diego Padres, which opened in 2004 in East Village; the San Diego Convention Center along the waterfront; Horton Plaza; and the Embarcadero, with the USS Midway museum ship. One America Plaza is among the tallest buildings in the city. Because of the proximity of San Diego International Airport, building heights downtown are limited to about 500 feet by federal aviation regulations.
