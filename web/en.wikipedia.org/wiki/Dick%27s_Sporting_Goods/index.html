Dick's Sporting Goods, Inc. is an American sporting goods retail company headquartered in Coraopolis, Pennsylvania. It is the largest sporting goods retailer in the United States, operating hundreds of stores under the Dick's Sporting Goods name as well as the Golf Galaxy, Public Lands, and House of Sport banners.

History

The company was founded in 1948 by Richard Stack as a small bait-and-tackle shop in Binghamton, New York, financed with a loan from his grandmother. It expanded steadily across the eastern United States and went public in 2002. In 2019 the company moved into a new headquarters campus near Pittsburgh International Airport.

Operations

Stores sell athletic apparel, footwear, exercise equipment, and outdoor gear. Dick's also owns the Field & Stream trademark rights and has taken public positions on firearms sales policy, notably restricting sales after 2018.
