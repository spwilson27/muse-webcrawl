Cassius was a male saltwater crocodile held at Marineland Melanesia, a zoo on Green Island, Queensland, Australia. Measuring 5.48 metres (18 ft) and weighing about a tonne, he was recognised by Guinness World Records as the world's largest crocodile living in captivity after the death of the Philippine crocodile Lolong in 2013.

Life

Cassius was captured in 1984 in the Finniss River region of Australia's Northern Territory, after he was blamed for attacks on boats and livestock; the tip of his snout was damaged and part of his left front leg and tail were missing, attributed to fights with other crocodiles. He was purchased by crocodile collector George Craig and transported to Green Island in 1987, where he became the star attraction of Marineland Melanesia for nearly four decades.

Age and death

His exact age was unknown; keepers estimated he may have been around 110–120 years old, which would make him among the oldest known crocodiles. Cassius died in November 2024 after a decline in health following the death of his long-time keeper George Craig the previous month.
