1889 FA Cup Final

Event1888–89 FA Cup
Date30 March 1889
VenueKennington Oval, London
RefereeMajor Francis Marindin
Attendance27,000

Preston North End 3–0 Wolverhampton Wanderers

The 1889 FA Cup Final was contested by Preston North End and Wolverhampton Wanderers at the Kennington Oval. Preston won 3–0, with goals from Fred Dewhurst, Jimmy Ross and Sam Thomson. The victory completed the league and cup Double for Preston, who had already won the inaugural Football League championship without losing a match; their side became immortalised as "The Invincibles". Remarkably, Preston won the cup without conceding a single goal in any round of the competition.

Route to the final

Preston's run to the final included victories over Bootle, Grimsby Town, Birmingham St George's and, in the semi-final, West Bromwich Albion, whom they defeated 1–0 at Bramall Lane. Wolves defeated Old Carthusians, Walsall Town Swifts, Sheffield Wednesday, Accrington and, in their semi-final at Crewe, Blackburn Rovers by 3–1.

Match summary

A crowd of about 27,000 — a record for an FA Cup Final at the time — watched Preston dominate the match. Fred Dewhurst opened the scoring after about 15 minutes, heading in a cross, and Jimmy Ross doubled the lead before half-time with a powerful shot. In the second half Sam Thomson added a third goal, sealing a comfortable victory. Preston's goalkeeper, Robert Mills-Roberts, had so little to do that his clean sheet for the whole tournament was never seriously threatened.

The trophy was presented to Preston captain Robert Holmes amid scenes of celebration. Preston's achievement of winning the Double while remaining unbeaten in the league and conceding no goals in the cup stood as a unique feat in English football history.
