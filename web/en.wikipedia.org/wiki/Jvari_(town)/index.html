Jvari (town)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

[Image: Jvari, Samegrelo, Georgia]

Jvari (Georgian: ჯვარი) is a town in the Samegrelo-Zemo Svaneti region of western Georgia, located in the Tsalenjikha Municipality near the Enguri River. Its name means "cross" in Georgian. The town had a population of roughly 800 in the 2014 census.

Geography

Jvari lies at the foot of the Greater Caucasus foothills, close to the Enguri Dam — one of the world's tallest arch dams — and near the administrative boundary with Abkhazia. The surrounding area is known for its humid subtropical climate and tea and hazelnut cultivation.

People

Jvari is the birthplace of Meliton Kantaria (1920–1993), the Georgian Red Army sergeant famed as one of the soldiers who raised the Soviet Victory Banner over the Reichstag in Berlin in 1945. A museum in the town is dedicated to him.
