UCI World Championships
Governing bodyUnion Cycliste Internationale (UCI)
DisciplinesRoad, track, mountain bike, cyclo-cross, BMX, trials, indoor, para-cycling, gravel, esports

The UCI World Championships are a family of annual (and, in some disciplines, periodic) world championship events in cycling organised by the Union Cycliste Internationale (UCI), the sport's international governing body. Each discipline crowns world champions who are entitled to wear the rainbow jersey.

Championships by discipline

  • UCI Road World Championships
  • UCI Track World Championships
  • UCI Mountain Bike World Championships
  • UCI Cyclo-cross World Championships
  • UCI BMX World Championships
  • UCI Trials World Championships
  • UCI Indoor Cycling World Championships
  • UCI Para-cycling Road and Track World Championships
  • UCI Gravel World Championships
  • UCI Cycling Esports World Championships

Combined championships

In 2023, the UCI inaugurated the UCI Cycling World Championships in Glasgow and across Scotland, a combined mega-event held every four years in the year before the Summer Olympic Games, bringing together 13 world championships including road, track, mountain bike, BMX, trials, indoor cycling, and para-cycling, with some 2,600 athletes competing for more than 200 rainbow jerseys. The next combined edition is scheduled for 2027 in Haute-Savoie, France.

Rainbow jersey

Across all disciplines, world champions wear the distinctive rainbow jersey—white with the five UCI colours (blue, red, black, yellow, and green) banded around the chest—until the next championship in their event.
