Tang Museum

The Frances Young Tang Teaching Museum and Art Gallery, known as the Tang Museum, is an art museum on the campus of Skidmore College in Saratoga Springs, New York. It opened in 2000 and is internationally recognised for its interdisciplinary exhibitions and its role as a teaching museum.

Building and collection

Designed by architect Antoine Predock, the building's intersecting wings and central tower echo the region's landscape. The Tang's collection numbers more than 18,000 objects, with strengths in modern and contemporary art, photography, and works on paper, alongside African, Asian, and European art.

Programme

True to its "teaching museum" mission, the Tang invites faculty and students from all disciplines to curate exhibitions and use its galleries as a laboratory for ideas. Landmark shows have addressed subjects from genetics to sound to the Hudson River School, and the museum publishes scholarly catalogues. Its rooftop and events programme, including the long-running summer concert series "UpBeat on the Roof", make it a cultural hub for the Saratoga region.
