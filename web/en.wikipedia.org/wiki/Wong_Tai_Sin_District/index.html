Wong Tai Sin District

RegionKowloon, Hong Kong
Area~9.3 km²
Population~406,000 (approx.)

Wong Tai Sin District is one of the 18 districts of Hong Kong, located in Kowloon. It is the only fully landlocked district in Hong Kong, bordered by Kwun Tong District to the southeast, Kowloon City District to the southwest, Sham Shui Po District to the west, and Sha Tin District of the New Territories to the north across the Lion Rock range.

Name and landmarks

The district takes its name from the Wong Tai Sin Temple, dedicated to the Taoist deity Wong Tai Sin (Huang Daxian), famous for the saying "what you request is what you get" (yau kau bit ying). The temple is one of Hong Kong's most popular shrines, drawing huge crowds at Lunar New Year. Other landmarks include the Chi Lin Nunnery and Nan Lian Garden in Diamond Hill, built in Tang dynasty style.

Areas

The district includes Wong Tai Sin, Diamond Hill, San Po Kong, Tsz Wan Shan, Wang Tau Hom, Lok Fu, Chuk Yuen, Fung Wong and parts of Hammer Hill. It is largely residential, with many public housing estates built from the 1950s onward to resettle squatter areas, and the industrial area of San Po Kong.

Transport

The district is served by the MTR Kwun Tong line (Wong Tai Sin, Diamond Hill and Lok Fu stations) and the Tuen Ma line at Diamond Hill. Lung Cheung Road and other major roads cross the district.
