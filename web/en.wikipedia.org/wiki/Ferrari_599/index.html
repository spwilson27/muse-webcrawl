Ferrari 599

Production: 2006–2012

Class: Grand tourer (S)

Engine: 6.0 L V12

The Ferrari 599 GTB Fiorano (internal code F141) is a grand tourer produced by the Italian automobile manufacturer Ferrari between 2006 and 2012. It replaced the 575M Maranello as the company's flagship V12 two-seater and took its name from its 5,999 cc engine displacement and the Fiorano test circuit.

Specifications

The 599's Tipo F140C V12 produced 612 horsepower (456 kW), giving a top speed of over 330 km/h (205 mph) and 0–100 km/h acceleration in about 3.7 seconds. It featured an aluminium chassis, magnetorheological suspension and Ferrari's fast-shifting F1 automated manual gearbox in most examples.

Variants

Special versions included the track-focused 599 GTO of 2010 — the fastest road-going Ferrari of its time around the Fiorano circuit — the SA Aperta roadster, and the one-off and limited hybrid concept HY-KERS. The 599 was succeeded by the F12berlinetta in 2012.
