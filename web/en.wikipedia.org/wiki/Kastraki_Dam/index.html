The Kastraki Dam is an earth-fill embankment dam on the Achelous River in Aetolia-Acarnania, western Greece, near the village of Kastraki. Completed in the late 1960s, it forms Lake Kastraki and houses a hydroelectric power station with an installed capacity of about 320 MW, operated by the Public Power Corporation (PPC).

Description

The dam is roughly 95 metres high and impounds a reservoir used for power generation and irrigation of the Achelous plain downstream. It sits between two other major Achelous projects: the Kremasta Dam upstream and the Stratos Dam downstream, forming a cascade that regulates the river's flow toward the Ionian Sea.

History

Construction formed part of Greece's post-war programme of harnessing the Achelous, the country's largest river by discharge. The filling of Lake Kastraki submerged agricultural land and small settlements along the river valley. In recent decades the Achelous projects have been at the centre of long-running legal and environmental disputes over plans to divert part of the river's water to the Thessaly plain.
