Shipping

Shipping is the transportation of goods and cargo, and also refers, in a fandom context, to the desire for two fictional characters or celebrities to be in a romantic relationship. This article focuses on the transportation sense; the industry of moving freight by sea, land, and air forms the backbone of international trade.

Maritime shipping

Maritime shipping carries the great majority of world trade by volume. Cargo moves in bulk carriers, tankers, and container ships; the introduction of the standard shipping container in the 1950s and 1960s dramatically reduced the cost of international freight. Major shipping lanes connect ports in Asia, Europe, and North America.

Freight and logistics

Beyond ocean transport, shipping encompasses rail freight, trucking, air cargo, and parcel delivery services. Modern supply chains integrate warehousing, inventory management, and last-mile delivery. Terms such as FOB and Incoterms govern the responsibilities of buyers and sellers in international shipments.

Environmental impact

International shipping accounts for roughly 2–3% of global greenhouse gas emissions. The International Maritime Organization has adopted measures to improve energy efficiency and reduce sulfur emissions from marine fuels.
