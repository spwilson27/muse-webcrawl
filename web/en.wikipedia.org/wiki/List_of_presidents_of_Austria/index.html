List of presidents of Austria

The Federal President of Austria (Bundespräsident) is the head of state of the Republic of Austria, directly elected by popular vote for a six-year term, renewable once. The office was created after the First World War; the presidency was interrupted by the authoritarian Ständestaat (1934–1938) and by Austria's annexation into Nazi Germany (1938–1945).

Notes

Chancellor Arthur Seyß-Inquart briefly acted as head of state on 13 March 1938 before Austria's incorporation into the German Reich. Between the death of Thomas Klestil on 6 July 2004 and Heinz Fischer's inauguration on 8 July, the three presidents of the National Council acted collectively as head of state, as the constitution provides.
