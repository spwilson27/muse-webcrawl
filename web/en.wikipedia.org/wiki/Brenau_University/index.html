Brenau University

Motto: "As Gold Refined by Fire"

Established: 1878

Type: Private university

Location: Gainesville, Georgia, U.S.

Mascot: Golden Tigers

Brenau University is a private university in Gainesville, Georgia, United States. Founded in 1878, it comprises a historic women's college — the Brenau Women's College — together with coeducational undergraduate and graduate programs. The university also has campuses in other Georgia cities and in Jacksonville, Florida, and offers extensive online programs.

History

The institution was founded in 1878 as the Georgia Baptist Female Seminary. It was renamed Brenau College in 1900; the name "Brenau" was coined from the German word brennen ("to burn") and the Latin aurum, rendered in the university motto as gold refined by fire. In 2011 the institution officially became Brenau University, reflecting its expanded graduate offerings.

Academics

Brenau is organized into colleges of business and communication, education, fine arts and humanities, and health sciences. The Sydney O. Smith Graduate School offers master's and doctoral programs, including nursing, occupational therapy, physical therapy, and education specialist degrees. The Women's College continues the university's residential liberal arts tradition for undergraduate women.

Campus

The main campus in Gainesville covers about 57 acres and includes buildings listed on the National Register of Historic Places, such as Pearce Auditorium, built in 1897. The campus is known for its historic architecture and its collection of art galleries, including the Simmons Visual Arts Center.

Athletics

Brenau's teams, the Golden Tigers, compete in the NAIA, primarily in the Appalachian Athletic Conference, in sports including basketball, soccer, softball, swimming, tennis, track and field, and competitive cheer.
