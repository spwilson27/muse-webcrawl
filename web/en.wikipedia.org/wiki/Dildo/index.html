Dildo

A dildo is a sex toy, often phallic in appearance, intended for sexual penetration or other sexual activity during masturbation or sex with a partner. Dildos have existed since antiquity and are made in a wide variety of shapes, sizes, and materials, including silicone, glass, metal, and rubber compounds.

History

Objects interpreted as dildos are known from the Upper Palaeolithic, and phallic artefacts occur in ancient Greek, Roman, and other cultures; Greek vase painting and literature, including Herodas's mimes, refer to leather dildos (olisbos). Historical materials included wood, leather, stone, and ceramic.

Types and safety

Modern designs include strap-on harnesses, double-ended models, and forms intended for specific stimulation. Health guidance generally favours non-porous, body-safe materials that can be fully cleaned, and notes that toys with a flared base are safer for anal use.
