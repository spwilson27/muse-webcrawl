Homer Jay Simpson is a fictional character and the main protagonist of the American animated sitcom The Simpsons. The bumbling, gluttonous and good-hearted father of the Simpson family, he works as a safety inspector at the Springfield Nuclear Power Plant. Homer is one of the most recognisable characters in television history, and his annoyed grunt "D'oh!" has entered the Oxford English Dictionary.

Homer Simpson, Springfield's most famous resident, famed for his love of doughnuts and Duff Beer.

Character

Homer is overweight, balding and lazy, with a short attention span and a fondness for food, television and Moe's Tavern. He frequently endangers his family and town through impulsive schemes, yet episodes typically reveal his devotion to his wife Marge and his children Bart, Lisa and Maggie. Matt Groening named the character after his own father, Homer Groening. Over the show's long run, commentators have noted a tendency towards "Homerisation" or flanderisation of his stupidity, while his role as a satire of the American working-class father endures.

History in the series

The Simpsons debuted as shorts on The Tracey Ullman Show in 1987 before the half-hour series premiered on Fox on 17 December 1989. Homer's backstory, developed across episodes, includes growing up on a farm with his father Abe after his mother Mona fled the law, failing to graduate fully from high school, a brief career in the barbershop quartet the Be Sharps, work as a mascot, astronaut (in "Deep Space Homer"), and countless other jobs. He and Marge met at high school and married after she became pregnant with Bart.

Cultural impact

Homer has been voted among the greatest television and cartoon characters of all time; The Sunday Times called him "the greatest comic creation of the modern age". His catchphrases — "D'oh!", "Mmm..." followed by a food, and "Woo-hoo!" — are globally recognised. Philosophers and sociologists have written extensively on the character as an everyman, and a bronze statue and a street sign, among many tributes, attest to his reach. Dan Castellaneta has won multiple Emmy Awards for voicing him.
