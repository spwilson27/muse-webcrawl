Global Mercy is a hospital ship operated by the international charity Mercy Ships. Purpose-built in China by the China State Shipbuilding Corporation (Tianjin Xingang yard) and completed with outfitting in Belgium, she is the world's largest civilian hospital ship and the first purpose-built vessel in the Mercy Ships fleet, joining — and later succeeding — the converted ferry Africa Mercy.

Design and facilities

The 174-metre, 37,000 GT vessel has two hospital decks with six operating rooms, a CT scanner, X-ray and laboratory facilities, 102 acute-care beds and accommodation for over 600 crew and volunteers. An onboard training centre is designed to build local surgical capacity in host countries. The ship has an expected service life of around 50 years.

Service

Global Mercy was unveiled at a ceremony in Rotterdam in 2022, then sailed to Senegal for her first field service in Dakar in 2022–2023, providing free surgeries and training in partnership with the Senegalese government. Subsequent field services have included Sierra Leone (Freetown) from 2023 and Madagascar (Toamasina) from 2024.
