Operation Maslenica

Operation Maslenica was a Croatian Army offensive conducted from 22 January to 1 February 1993 during the Croatian War of Independence. Its objective was to retake territory in northern Dalmatia and Lika held by the self-proclaimed Republic of Serbian Krajina, and to reopen the land connection between Zagreb and the Dalmatian coast.

Croatian forces recaptured the area around the Maslenica strait, Novsko Ždrilo and the Zemunik airfield near Zadar, allowing construction of a pontoon bridge that temporarily restored road traffic to Dalmatia until the new Maslenica Bridge was rebuilt.

The offensive drew international criticism because it breached a UN-brokered ceasefire, and fighting continued intermittently thereafter; Serb forces retaliated with artillery attacks on Zadar and other coastal towns. The gains were largely held despite a Serb counteroffensive in the following months.
