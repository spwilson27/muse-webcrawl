Champion Beer of Britain

The Champion Beer of Britain (CBOB) is an award presented by the Campaign for Real Ale (CAMRA) at the Great British Beer Festival, held annually since 1978. It is one of the most prestigious cask ale awards in the United Kingdom.

Selection

Beers are nominated by CAMRA members and tasting panels, judged at regional competitions, then blind-tasted in category rounds at the festival. Categories include Mild, Bitter, Best Bitter, Strong Bitter, Golden Ale, Speciality and others; category winners compete for Supreme Champion. Notable winners include Taylor's Landlord and Greene King Abbot Ale.
