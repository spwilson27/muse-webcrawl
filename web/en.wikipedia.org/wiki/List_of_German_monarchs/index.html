List of German monarchs

This is a list of monarchs who ruled over East Francia, and the Kingdom of Germany (the regnum Teutonicum), from the division of the Frankish Empire at the Treaty of Verdun in 843 until the collapse of the German Empire during the November Revolution of 1918. It also includes the heads of the various confederations of German states that existed after the dissolution of the Holy Roman Empire in 1806, including the Confederation of the Rhine, the German Confederation and the North German Confederation.

The monarchs of the Kingdom of Germany started as kings of East Francia, and many were crowned Holy Roman Emperor by the pope. After the fall of the Carolingian dynasty the kings were elected, first by the rulers of the stem duchies and later by the prince-electors.

Habsburg and rival kings

After the Great Interregnum (1254–1273), the throne alternated among several houses, including the counts of Habsburg, Nassau, Luxembourg and Wittelsbach. Notable monarchs of this period include Rudolf I (1273–1291), Adolf of Nassau (1292–1298), Albert I (1298–1308), Henry VII of Luxembourg (1308–1313), Louis IV the Bavarian (1314–1347), Charles IV (1346–1378), Sigismund (1410–1437) and Albert II (1438–1439).

Habsburg dynasty (1438–1806)

From 1438 the imperial throne was held almost continuously by the House of Habsburg until the dissolution of the Holy Roman Empire in 1806. Monarchs included Frederick III (1440–1493), Maximilian I (1493–1519), Charles V (1519–1556), Ferdinand I (1556–1564), Maximilian II (1564–1576), Rudolf II (1576–1612), Matthias (1612–1619), Ferdinand II (1619–1637), Ferdinand III (1637–1657), Leopold I (1658–1705), Joseph I (1705–1711), Charles VI (1711–1740), Maria Theresa (1740–1780, queen in her own right in the hereditary lands), Joseph II (1780–1790), Leopold II (1790–1792) and Francis II (1792–1806). The sole interruption was the Wittelsbach emperor Charles VII (1742–1745).

Confederation period

Following the Confederation of the Rhine (1806–1813), headed by Napoleon as protector, the German Confederation (1815–1866) was presided over by the Emperor of Austria. The North German Confederation (1867–1871) was headed by Wilhelm I of Prussia.
