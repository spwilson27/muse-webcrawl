List of solar deities

A solar deity is a god or goddess who represents the Sun, or an aspect of it. Solar cults have existed in most cultures: the sun has been worshipped as a source of light, warmth, fertility and divine kingship. This list collects notable sun deities by culture.

African

  • Ra (Re) — ancient Egyptian sun god, depicted with a solar disk; also Amun-Ra, Aten (the disk itself, exalted under Akhenaten), Khepri (morning sun), Atum (evening sun)
  • Magec — Guanche (Tenerife) sun god
  • Nyame — Akan sky god with solar aspects

Asian

  • Amaterasu — Japanese Shinto sun goddess, ancestress of the imperial line
  • Surya — Hindu sun god, one of the Adityas; also Vivasvant, Savitr, Mitra
  • Xihe — Chinese mother of the ten suns; Yi the Archer shot down nine of them
  • Marici — Buddhist goddess of light and dawn
  • Inti — Inca sun god (Americas)
  • Malakbel — Palmyrene sun god
  • Shamash (Utu) — Mesopotamian sun god of justice
  • Shapash — Canaanite/Ugaritic sun goddess

European

  • Helios — Greek Titan/god of the sun, driving his chariot across the sky; Apollo acquired solar functions later
  • Sol Invictus — Roman "Unconquered Sun", promoted by Aurelian; also Sol Indiges, Apollo, Mithras (in his solar aspect)
  • Sól (Sunna) — Norse goddess of the sun, chased by the wolf Sköll
  • Saulė — Baltic sun goddess
  • Dazhbog — Slavic sun god
  • Belenus, Grannus — Celtic gods with solar associations; Áine in Irish tradition
  • Ekhi — Basque personification of the sun

Americas

  • Inti — sun god of the Inca Empire, honoured at the Inti Raymi festival
  • Tonatiuh — Aztec sun god, the fifth sun, requiring nourishment
  • Kinich Ahau — Maya sun god
  • Wi — Lakota sun god
  • Jóhonaaʼéí — Navajo sun god, the Sun Bearer

Oceania

  • Tama-nui-te-rā — Māori personification of the sun, slowed by Māui
  • Bila — Adnyamathanha (Australian Aboriginal) sun goddess
  • Wala — Aboriginal sun goddess of Arnhem Land
