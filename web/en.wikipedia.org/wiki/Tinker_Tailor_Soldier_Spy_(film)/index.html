Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy (film)

Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy
Director: Tomas Alfredson
Based on: novel by John le Carré
Starring: Gary Oldman, Colin Firth, Tom Hardy, John Hurt, Mark Strong
Release: 2011

Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy is a 2011 Cold War espionage film directed by Tomas Alfredson, adapted from John le Carré's 1974 novel of the same name. Gary Oldman stars as George Smiley, a retired British intelligence officer brought back to uncover a Soviet mole within MI6, known as "the Circus".

Plot

After a disastrous operation in Budapest, Smiley is forced into retirement. When suspicion arises that a double agent sits at the highest level of the service, he is secretly tasked with investigating his former colleagues — codenamed Tinker, Tailor, Soldier and others — to expose the traitor.

Reception

The film was critically acclaimed for its direction, acting and atmospheric restraint, and received three Academy Award nominations, including Best Actor for Oldman.
