Bullfrog Productions Limited was a British video game developer based in Guildford, England. Founded in 1987 by Peter Molyneux and Les Edgar, the company gained a reputation for innovative strategy and simulation games, several of which effectively defined the "god game" genre. Electronic Arts acquired Bullfrog in 1995, and the studio was merged into EA UK in 2004.

History

Bullfrog's breakthrough came with Populous (1989), widely regarded as the first successful god game, in which the player manipulates terrain and worshippers to defeat a rival deity. The studio followed with acclaimed titles including Powermonger, Syndicate, Theme Park, Magic Carpet, Theme Hospital and the Dungeon Keeper series, which inverted fantasy conventions by casting the player as the villain defending a dungeon.

Decline and legacy

After the EA acquisition, Molyneux served as a vice-president at EA before leaving in 1997 to found Lionhead Studios, taking several Bullfrog staff with him. Bullfrog continued with titles such as Populous: The Beginning and Dungeon Keeper 2 before being absorbed into EA UK in 2004. Many former staff later formed studios including Mucky Foot Productions and Media Molecule, and Bullfrog's catalogue remains highly influential in the strategy genre.
