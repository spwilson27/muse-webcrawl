Rail transport is the conveyance of passengers and freight by wheeled vehicles running on rails. Beginning with horse-drawn wagonways and the steam railways of 1820s Britain, railways industrialised continents, standardised time, and built fortunes.

Modern rail

Diesel and electric traction, container freight, urban metros and high-speed trains (Japan's Shinkansen from 1964; China's vast network) keep rail central — and comparatively low-carbon — transport, competing with roads and airplanes. Maglev trains float on magnets at over 400 km/h in trials.
