France 24
Launched6 December 2006
LanguagesFrench; English; Arabic; Spanish
OwnerFrance Médias Monde (French state)
[Image: The France 24 studios near Paris]

France 24 is a French state-owned international news television network, launched on 6 December 2006, broadcasting in French, English, Arabic and (from 2017) Spanish from studios in Issy-les-Moulineaux near Paris. It is part of the France Médias Monde group alongside RFI radio and Monte Carlo Doualiya.

Mission

France 24 was created to give a French perspective on world news to global audiences — a long-discussed project of President Jacques Chirac — and mixes rolling news with magazines on culture, business, sport and its Observers citizen-verification programme.
