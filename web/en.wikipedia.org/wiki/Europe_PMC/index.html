Europe PMC

Europe PMC (Europe PubMed Central) is an open-access repository of life-science research literature, providing free access to millions of journal articles, preprints, books and other research outputs. It is developed by the European Molecular Biology Laboratory's European Bioinformatics Institute (EMBL-EBI) in Hinxton, United Kingdom, and forms the European node of the worldwide PubMed Central network.

Scope and services

The service began in 2007 as UK PubMed Central, funded by a consortium of UK research funders, and was renamed Europe PMC in 2012 as its funder base broadened to include European agencies coordinated through Science Europe. It mirrors the article content of the US PubMed Central and adds material required by its funders' open-access mandates, including author manuscripts and grant-linked outputs. Beyond search, Europe PMC offers full-text mining tools, links from articles to underlying data, genes, proteins and diseases, programmatic APIs, and a preprint layer that indexes servers such as bioRxiv and medRxiv. Its database contained more than 40 million abstracts and over 9 million full-text articles by the mid-2020s.

Role

Europe PMC is a core infrastructure for open science in Europe: funders use it to track the outputs of grants they support, and researchers use it as a single searchable corpus of the biomedical literature, complementary to the US National Library of Medicine's PubMed.
