Legio XIV Gemina

Legio XIV Gemina ("Twinned Fourteenth Legion") was a legion of the Imperial Roman army, levied by Julius Caesar in 57 BC for his Gallic Wars. The name Gemina ("twinned") reflects its reconstitution under Augustus from the remnants of two legions. Its emblem was the Capricorn.

Britain

The Fourteenth was one of the four legions of the invasion of Britain in AD 43. In AD 60/61, under Suetonius Paulinus, it won its lasting fame at the Battle of Watling Street, where some 10,000 Romans routed the vastly larger army of Boudica. Nero afterwards called it his "most efficacious" legion.

Later service

Withdrawn from Britain in 67, the legion fought in the civil wars of 69, then served on the Rhine and Danube frontiers. From the reign of Trajan it was based at Carnuntum in Pannonia, where it remained for three centuries, fighting in the Marcomannic Wars under Marcus Aurelius and proclaiming Septimius Severus emperor in 193. The legion was still at Carnuntum in the early 5th century.
