The Autobots are a faction of sentient robots in the Transformers franchise, created by the American toy company Hasbro and the Japanese company Takara Tomy. Usually portrayed as the heroic protagonists of the series, the Autobots are generally depicted as honourable, freedom-loving robots who oppose the tyrannical Decepticons. In most continuities their leader is Optimus Prime, and their story is bound up with the civil war that devastated their home planet of Cybertron and eventually brought the conflict to Earth.

Fictional history

In the original Generation 1 continuity, the Autobots and Decepticons were created on Cybertron, in some versions by the Quintessons as consumer goods and military hardware respectively, and in others as the descendants of the Thirteen Primes created by the god Primus. The Decepticons, led by Megatron, launched a war for control of the planet. The Autobot leader Optimus Prime took a crew aboard the starship Ark in search of new energy sources; attacked by the Decepticons, both ships crashed on Earth, where the crews lay dormant for four million years until reawakening in 1984. From then on the Autobots defended humanity and Earth while continuing their war against the Decepticons and seeking a way to restore Cybertron.

Later continuities repeatedly retell this basic conflict: in Beast Wars, the Autobots' descendants are the Maximals; in the live-action film series, the Autobots arrive on Earth searching for the AllSpark; in Transformers: Animated and Transformers: Prime, smaller Autobot teams protect Earth from Decepticon incursions. The 1986 animated film famously killed Optimus Prime and introduced Rodimus Prime as his successor, a decision that generated lasting controversy among fans.

Members

Well-known Autobots include Optimus Prime, Bumblebee, Ironhide, Ratchet, Jazz, Prowl, Wheeljack, Grimlock and the Dinobots, Arcee, Ultra Magnus, Hot Rod/Rodimus Prime, and the combiner teams such as the Aerialbots and Protectobots. Membership varies widely between continuities, and many characters have been reinvented across the franchise's many toy lines, comics, animated series and films.

Character and themes

The Autobots are characterised by their respect for life, their code of honour, and their mottoes "Freedom is the right of all sentient beings" and "Till all are one." Optimus Prime in particular is portrayed as a wise, self-sacrificing leader whose defining trait is compassion even toward enemies. The faction's insignia, a stylised red robotic face, is one of the franchise's most recognisable symbols and is used extensively in merchandising.

In other media

The Autobots have appeared in comic books from Marvel, Dreamwave and IDW Publishing, in numerous animated television series beginning with The Transformers (1984–1987), in the live-action film series directed initially by Michael Bay beginning in 2007, and in many video games. The toys, originally adapted from Takara's Diaclone and Micro Change lines, remain the commercial foundation of the franchise.
