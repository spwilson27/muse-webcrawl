Gavril Radomir

Gavril Radomir (died 1015) was the emperor (tsar) of the First Bulgarian Empire from October 1014 to August or September 1015. He was the son of Emperor Samuel.

Gavril Radomir
Reign: 1014–1015
Predecessor: Samuel
Successor: Ivan Vladislav
Died: 1015, near Lake Ostrovo
Father: Samuel of Bulgaria

Life

During his father's long war with the Byzantine emperor Basil II, Gavril Radomir distinguished himself as a commander. In 996 he saved Samuel's life after the disastrous Battle of Spercheios. He reportedly rescued his father and carried him to safety. Byzantine sources describe him as brave and capable, though less energetic than Samuel.

Reign

After Samuel died — traditionally of shock after seeing his blinded soldiers return from the Battle of Kleidion in 1014 — Gavril Radomir succeeded him. He continued resistance against Basil II but his authority was undermined by defections. In 1015 he was murdered while hunting near Lake Ostrovo by his cousin Ivan Vladislav, whom he had once spared, and who seized the throne.

Family

Gavril Radomir was married twice: first to a Hungarian princess, whom he repudiated, and then to Irene of Larissa. His son Peter Delyan later led a major uprising against Byzantine rule in 1040–1041, claiming descent from him.
