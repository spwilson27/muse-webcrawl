Earthly Branches

The Earthly Branches (also called the Terrestrial Branches) are a Chinese system of twelve ordered symbols used historically for reckoning time, directions and years, together with the ten Heavenly Stems forming the sexagenary cycle.

Earthly Branches
Chinese: 地支 (dìzhī)
Number: 12
Paired with: Heavenly Stems
Associated: Chinese zodiac animals

The twelve branches

The branches are Zi 子, Chou 丑, Yin 寅, Mao 卯, Chen 辰, Si 巳, Wu 午, Wei 未, Shen 申, You 酉, Xu 戌 and Hai 亥. Each corresponds to a zodiac animal (Rat through Pig), a two-hour period of the day, a month and a compass direction.

Uses

Combined with the Heavenly Stems, the branches form the 60-year sexagenary cycle used in calendars, Chinese astrology, feng shui, and traditional naming of years, days and hours.
