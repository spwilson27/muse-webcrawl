Ricardo Izecson dos Santos Leite (born 22 April 1982), commonly known as Kaká, is a Brazilian former professional footballer who played as an attacking midfielder. Renowned for his pace, dribbling, passing, and long-range shooting, he is regarded as one of the finest players of his generation.

Career

Kaká began his career at São Paulo before joining AC Milan in 2003, where he won Serie A, the UEFA Champions League in 2007, and both the Ballon d'Or and FIFA World Player of the Year that year — the last player other than Lionel Messi or Cristiano Ronaldo to win the Ballon d'Or before their decade of dominance. In 2009 he moved to Real Madrid for a fee of €67 million, then a world record. He later returned to Milan and finished his career with Orlando City in Major League Soccer, retiring in 2017.

International career

With Brazil, Kaká won the 2002 FIFA World Cup and the Confederations Cup in 2005 and 2009, earning 92 caps and scoring 29 goals.
