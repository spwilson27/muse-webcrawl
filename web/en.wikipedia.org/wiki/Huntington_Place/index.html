Huntington Place is a convention centre in downtown Detroit, Michigan, on the bank of the Detroit River. Opened in 1960 as Cobo Hall (later Cobo Center), it is one of the largest convention facilities in the United States and is named, since December 2021, for Huntington Bank.

History and events

The centre was named for Kermit G. Cobo, a mayor of Detroit in the 1950s. It hosted the North American International Auto Show for decades and was the site of the 1980 Republican National Convention. The adjacent arena was the scene of the 1994 attack on figure skater Nancy Kerrigan. A major renovation and expansion completed in 2015 added a grand riverfront ballroom and atrium.
