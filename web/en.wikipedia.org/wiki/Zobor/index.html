Zobor

Zobor is a mountain in the Tribeč range in western Slovakia, rising to 587 metres above the city of Nitra. It is a prominent landmark of the city and a popular recreational area.

Features

The slopes of Zobor are partly covered by protected nature reserves with oak-hornbeam forest and thermophilous plant communities. A television and telecommunications transmitter stands near the summit. The hill is accessible by hiking paths from Nitra and by a chairlift that formerly operated from the Zobor district.

History

Zobor and its surroundings are archaeologically significant: the area was settled in prehistoric times, and nearby stood Zobor Abbey, a Benedictine monastery founded in the early 11th century, from which the Zobor documents (1111–1113) — among the oldest surviving written records from the territory of present-day Slovakia — originate. A Slavic hillfort stood on the summit area in the early medieval period.
