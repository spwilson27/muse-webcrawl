Galei Tzahal (Hebrew: גלי צה"ל, "IDF Waves"), known by its acronym Galatz, is a nationwide Israeli radio network operated by the Israel Defense Forces. Founded in 1950, it broadcasts news, current affairs, talk, and music, and is staffed largely by soldiers doing their mandatory service, alongside civilian employees.

History

The station began broadcasting on 24 September 1950, initially intended to serve soldiers. Over the decades it evolved into a general public broadcaster with a prominent news department, and its journalists — many recruited as teenage soldier-reporters — became a famous training ground for the Israeli media industry. Its sister station Galgalatz, launched in 1993, focuses on music and traffic reports.

Programming and status

Galei Tzahal carries hourly news bulletins, current-affairs magazines, interviews, and cultural programming. Its status as a military-operated but editorially journalistic station has repeatedly been debated in Israeli politics, with periodic proposals to privatise, restructure, or close it; in the mid-2020s the government advanced plans to shut the station down, prompting public and legal controversy.
