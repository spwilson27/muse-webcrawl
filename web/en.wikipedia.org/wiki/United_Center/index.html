United Center

The United Center is an indoor arena on the Near West Side of Chicago, Illinois. It is home to the Chicago Bulls of the National Basketball Association (NBA) and the Chicago Blackhawks of the National Hockey League (NHL). It is the largest arena by capacity in the NBA and one of the largest in the NHL.

History

The arena opened on 18 August 1994, replacing the Chicago Stadium, which stood across the street. It is named for its corporate sponsor, United Airlines. The Bulls won three NBA championships (1996–1998) in the building during the Michael Jordan era; a statue of Jordan, "The Spirit", stands outside the east entrance. The arena has also hosted the Democratic National Convention (1996 and 2024), NCAA basketball, and numerous concerts.

Capacity

Seating capacity is approximately 20,900 for basketball and 19,700 for hockey, with standing room pushing attendance higher. The building is owned jointly by the Bulls and Blackhawks organisations through the United Center Joint Venture.
