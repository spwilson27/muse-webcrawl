Telegram (software)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Telegram is a cloud-based instant messaging service founded in 2013 by brothers Pavel and Nikolai Durov. Available on mobile and desktop, it emphasises speed and large-scale features — channels, groups of up to 200,000 members, bots and file sharing of very large files. Standard chats are cloud-stored and encrypted in transit and at rest on Telegram's servers; end-to-end encryption is offered only in one-to-one 'Secret Chats'.

History and structure

The Durovs, who previously founded the Russian social network VK, launched Telegram in 2013; its operations have been based variously in Berlin and Dubai. Telegram reported passing 900 million monthly users in 2024 and over 1 billion in 2025, funded partly by advertising, premium subscriptions and the TON cryptocurrency ecosystem.

Controversies

Telegram's light moderation has made it both a tool for protesters and a platform criticised over extremist and illicit content. Founder Pavel Durov was arrested in France in August 2024 in an investigation concerning the platform's moderation; the case drew wide debate about platform liability.
