The Scarecrow is a supervillain published by DC Comics and an enemy of Batman. Created by Bill Finger and Bob Kane, he first appeared in World's Finest Comics #3 (Fall 1941). The character is Dr. Jonathan Crane, a former professor of psychology who uses specially designed drugs and toxins that induce phobias to exploit the fears of his victims, delighting in studying and manipulating fear itself.

Fictional character biography

Crane, in most versions, was a bullied, awkward youth who developed an obsession with fear. As a professor at Gotham University he conducted unethical experiments on human subjects and was fired, turning to crime as the Scarecrow. He wears a costume resembling a farm scarecrow and weaponises a "fear toxin" (or fear gas), which causes those exposed to hallucinate their worst fears — a weapon that frequently turns Batman's own traumas against him.

In other media

The Scarecrow has appeared in many adaptations. Cillian Murphy plays Crane in Christopher Nolan's Dark Knight trilogy (2005–2012); the character also appears in the television series Gotham, in animated series such as Batman: The Animated Series, and as a major antagonist in the Batman: Arkham video games, where his nightmare sequences are notable set pieces.
