The Mountain (French: La Montagne) is a 2022 French drama film directed by Thomas Salvador, who also co-wrote the screenplay with Naïla Guiguet and stars in the leading role alongside Louise Bourgoin. It premiered in the Directors' Fortnight section of the 2022 Cannes Film Festival, where it won the SACD Prize, and was released theatrically in France in early 2023.

Plot

Pierre, a robotics engineer, travels to Chamonix in the French Alps to deliver a sales presentation on industrial exoskeletons. During the trip he feels an inexplicable pull toward the high mountains visible from the valley. On an impulse he cancels his return to Paris, buys camping equipment, and hikes up to a glacier, where he pitches his tent and simply stays, watching the landscape and the lights that appear in the night sky. He forms a tentative friendship with Léa, a chef working in an alpine restaurant, who is first puzzled and then drawn by his quiet obsession. As Pierre retreats further from his former life — ignoring calls from his family and his employer — the film drifts from social realism into gentle fantasy, suggesting a strange symbiosis between the man and the living rock and ice around him.

Production

Salvador, whose previous feature Vincent (2014) blended a superhero premise with understated realism, developed The Mountain in the same register of low-key magic realism. The film was shot on location in the Mont Blanc massif, with extended sequences filmed at altitude on glaciers and in high refuges. Its sparse dialogue and long landscape takes contrast with the banal corporate setting of the opening act.

Reception

Critics praised the film's serenity, its deadpan humour and its evocation of humanity's attraction to wilderness, with several reviewers comparing its tone to a contemporary fairy tale. At Cannes it received the SACD Award for best French-language film in the Directors' Fortnight. The film subsequently circulated on the international festival circuit and was released in a number of European territories.
