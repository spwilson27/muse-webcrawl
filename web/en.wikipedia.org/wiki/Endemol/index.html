Endemol was a Dutch-based media company that produced and distributed multiplatform entertainment content. Founded in 1994 through the merger of the television production companies of Joop van den Ende and John de Mol, its name is a portmanteau of the surnames of its two founders. Endemol became one of the largest independent television production groups in the world, with operations in more than 30 countries.

History

Endemol was formed in 1994 when Joop van den Ende TV Productions and De Mol's John de Mol Produkties merged. The company rapidly expanded internationally, acquiring production companies and launching local versions of its formats around the world. In 2000, the Spanish telecommunications company Telefónica acquired Endemol for €5.5 billion. The company was later sold in 2007 to a consortium including Mediaset, Goldman Sachs, and De Mol's investment vehicle Cyrte Investments.

In 2014, Endemol merged with Shine Group and CORE Media Group to form the Endemol Shine Group, a joint venture between 21st Century Fox and Apollo Global Management. In 2020, the French company Banijay acquired Endemol Shine Group for approximately $2.2 billion, after which the Endemol brand was largely retired in favour of the Banijay name.

Formats and productions

Endemol is best known for creating and producing globally successful formats including Big Brother, Deal or No Deal, Who Wants to Be a Millionaire? licensor of formats such as Fear Factor, Wipeout, The Money Drop, and Your Face Sounds Familiar. Through Endemol Shine, it also produced scripted series such as Peaky Blinders and Black Mirror.
