Just Born, Inc. is a privately held American confectionery company based in Bethlehem, Pennsylvania. It is best known as the maker of Peeps marshmallow candies, Mike and Ike, Hot Tamales, and Goldenberg's Peanut Chews.

History

The company was founded in 1923 in Brooklyn, New York, by Samuel Born, a Russian Jewish immigrant and candymaker who invented the machine that inserts sticks into lollipops. The name "Just Born" advertised the freshness of the candies, said to be made daily — "just born". In 1932 the company moved to Bethlehem, Pennsylvania.

In 1953 Just Born acquired the Rodda Candy Company, maker of marshmallow Peeps — chick-shaped marshmallows that were then produced by hand, each taking hours to make. Sam Born's son Bob Born developed a mechanised process that cut production time dramatically, earning him the nickname "Father of Peeps". Peeps became the company's signature product and the best-selling non-chocolate Easter candy in the United States.

Products

  • Peeps — marshmallow animals and shapes, originally Easter chicks and bunnies, now produced for many holidays in many flavours.
  • Mike and Ike — fruit-flavoured chewy candies, introduced in 1940.
  • Hot Tamales — cinnamon-flavoured chewy candies, introduced in 1950.
  • Goldenberg's Peanut Chews — a Philadelphia brand acquired in 2003.
