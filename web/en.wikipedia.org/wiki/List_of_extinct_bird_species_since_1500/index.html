List of extinct bird species since 1500

Around 160 bird species are known to have become extinct since the year 1500, the baseline used by the IUCN Red List for "recently extinct" species. The overwhelming majority were island endemics, driven to extinction by introduced predators, habitat destruction and hunting.

The passenger pigeon, once numbering in the billions, is the most famous example of a superabundant species hunted to extinction. Island rails and flightless birds were especially vulnerable: dozens of Pacific rail species disappeared after the arrival of rats, cats and other introduced predators.
