Canvas is a web-based learning management system (LMS) developed by the American education technology company Instructure. It is used by learning institutions, educators and students to access and manage online course materials and communicate about skill development and learning achievement.

History

Instructure was founded in 2008 in Salt Lake City, Utah, by two University of Utah graduate students, and Canvas was launched in 2011. The company positioned Canvas as a modern, cloud-hosted alternative to established systems such as Blackboard and Moodle, emphasising ease of use and an open API. Canvas grew rapidly in North American higher education and by the late 2010s had become the market leader in US and Canadian universities, also expanding into K–12 schools and corporate training.

Features

Canvas provides course content pages, assignments, quizzes, discussion boards, a gradebook (with the "SpeedGrader" annotation tool), learning outcomes and rubrics, video conferencing integration, mobile apps for teachers and students, and support for interoperability standards such as LTI, which allows third-party learning tools to plug into courses. An open-source version of Canvas is available under the AGPL licence for self-hosting institutions, though most institutions use Instructure's cloud service.
