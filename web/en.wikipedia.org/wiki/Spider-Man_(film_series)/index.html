Spider-Man (film series)

The Spider-Man film series consists of American superhero films based on the Marvel Comics character Spider-Man, produced by Columbia Pictures and distributed by Sony Pictures. The property has been rebooted several times, making it one of the highest-grossing film franchises of all time.

The series

Sam Raimi's trilogy — Spider-Man (2002), Spider-Man 2 (2004) and Spider-Man 3 (2007) — starred Tobey Maguire. Marc Webb's The Amazing Spider-Man (2012) and its 2014 sequel relaunched the character with Andrew Garfield. Under a deal between Sony and Marvel Studios, Tom Holland's Spider-Man joined the Marvel Cinematic Universe, headlining Homecoming (2017), Far from Home (2019) and No Way Home (2021), the latter bringing all three live-action Spider-Men together through the multiverse and grossing over $1.9 billion. Sony has also produced the animated Spider-Verse films, beginning with Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018), which won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature.
