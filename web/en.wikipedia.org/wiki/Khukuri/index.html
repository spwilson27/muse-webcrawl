Khukuri

The khukuri (also spelled kukri) is a distinctive curved knife originating in Nepal. It serves both as a tool and a weapon, and is famously carried by the Gurkha regiments of the British and Indian armies.

Design

The blade is forward-curved with the cutting edge on the concave side, broadening towards the tip. A traditional khukuri is often accompanied by two small knives, the karda and the chakmak, carried in the same scabbard.

Use

In Nepal the khukuri is an everyday agricultural and household tool as well as a symbol of the Gurkha soldier. Ceremonial versions are used in rituals such as the Dashain festival.
