Louis II of Hungary

Louis II (1 July 1506 – 29 August 1526) was King of Hungary, Croatia and Bohemia from 1516 until his death at the Battle of Mohács against the Ottoman Empire led by Suleiman the Magnificent.

Reign

Born prematurely and succeeding his father Vladislaus II as a child, Louis's kingdom was weakened by magnate factionalism and financial crisis. He married Mary of Habsburg under the Habsburg–Jagiellonian alliance.

Mohács and aftermath

At Mohács the Hungarian army was annihilated; Louis died, reportedly drowning fleeing the field. With no heir, Hungary was partitioned between the Ottomans, Habsburg Royal Hungary and Transylvania, ending medieval Hungary.
