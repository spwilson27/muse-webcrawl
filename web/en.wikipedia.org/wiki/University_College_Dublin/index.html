University College Dublin

University College Dublin (UCD) is a public research university in Dublin, Ireland, and the largest university in the country, with over 30,000 students. It descends from the Catholic University of Ireland founded in 1854, whose first rector was John Henry Newman.

History

UCD became a constituent college of the National University of Ireland in 1908. In the 1960s it moved from Earlsfort Terrace to a modern campus at Belfield in south Dublin.

Academics

The university has colleges of arts and humanities, business, engineering, health, science, social sciences and law. Alumni include writers James Joyce and Seán O'Casey connections through its predecessor, and several Irish taoisigh and presidents.
