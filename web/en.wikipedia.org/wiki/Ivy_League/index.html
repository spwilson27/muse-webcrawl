Ivy League

The Ivy League is an American collegiate athletic conference comprising eight private research universities in the Northeastern United States. The term also denotes the schools' academic excellence, selectivity and social prestige.

History

Seven of the eight schools were founded in the colonial era. The athletic agreement was formalised in 1954. The Ivies do not award athletic scholarships and emphasise academics in admissions.

Reputation

Ivy League universities consistently rank among the world's top institutions, have large endowments, and admit a small percentage of applicants. Their alumni include many US presidents, Nobel laureates and business leaders.
