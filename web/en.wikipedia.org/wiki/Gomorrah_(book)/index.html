Gomorrah (book)

Gomorrah (Italian: Gomorra) is a 2006 non-fiction investigative book by Italian journalist Roberto Saviano exposing the criminal empire of the Camorra, the Neapolitan mafia, and its infiltration of business and politics.

Gomorrah
Author: Roberto Saviano
Published: 2006 (Mondadori)
Subject: Camorra organised crime
Adaptations: Film (2008), TV series (2014–2021)

Content

Saviano, who worked undercover in a Camorra-linked textile workshop, documents the System's grip on fashion counterfeiting, waste dumping in Campania, drug trafficking through the port of Naples, and clan wars in Scampia and Secondigliano.

Impact

The book sold millions of copies and was translated worldwide. After death threats, Saviano has lived under police protection since 2006. It was adapted into Matteo Garrone's Grand Prix-winning film Gomorrah (2008) and the acclaimed television series Gomorrah (2014–2021).
