Sudha Murty

Sudha Murty (née Kulkarni; born 19 August 1950) is an Indian educator, author, and philanthropist. She is the chairperson of the Infosys Foundation and was a member of the Rajya Sabha, the upper house of India's Parliament, nominated in 2024. She is married to N. R. Narayana Murthy, co-founder of Infosys.

Early life and career

Born in Shiggaon, Karnataka, Murty earned a degree in electrical and electronics engineering from B.V.B. College of Engineering and Technology — where she was the only woman in her class — topping her university, and then completed a master's degree in computer science at the Indian Institute of Science, again standing first. She became the first woman engineer hired at Tata Motors (then TELCO), an episode she has recounted as beginning with a postcard she wrote to J. R. D. Tata protesting a "men only" job advertisement. She provided the initial seed capital of ₹10,000 that helped her husband found Infosys in 1981.

Writing and philanthropy

Murty is a prolific writer of novels, short stories, travelogues, and children's books in Kannada and English, including Wise and Otherwise, Mahashweta, Dollar Bahu, and the Gopi Diaries series. Through the Infosys Foundation she has supported education, libraries, hospitals, and rural development across India. She received the Padma Shri in 2006 and the Padma Bhushan in 2023.
