Charlotte Linlin (Japanese: シャーロット・リンリン), better known as Big Mom, is a fictional character and major antagonist in the manga and anime series One Piece created by Eiichiro Oda. She was one of the four Emperors of the Sea (Yonko) and the captain of the Big Mom Pirates, ruling the archipelago of Totto Land.

Character

A giantess with a prodigious appetite and an obsessive dream of building a utopia where every race lives equally, Linlin suffers from a trauma-driven disorder: as a child she destroyed her caretaker Mother Carmel in a moment of ecstatic hunger and repressed the memory. As an adult her mood swings between maternal generosity and murderous hunger pangs that compel her to demand soul payments from her subjects. Her "Soul Pocus" ability extracts fragments of a subject's lifespan; those who feel no fear are immune.

She ate the Soru Soru no Mi, which lets her manipulate souls, infusing them into objects and animals to create loyal homies such as Zeus the thundercloud, Prometheus the sun, Napoleon the bicorne hat, and Hera. Her immense physical strength, Haki, and near-invulnerability made her one of the series' most formidable fighters.

Story arc

Introduced in the Fish-Man Island saga and prominent in the Whole Cake Island arc, Linlin pursues an alliance with the protagonist Monkey D. Luffy's crew through a marriage plot involving her daughter Pudding and Sanji, whose destruction the Straw Hats sabotage. In the Wano Country arc she joins Kaido in an alliance of Emperors, but the two are defeated in the Raid on Onigashima — Big Mom being brought down by Eustass Kid and Trafalgar D. Water Law — after which she is presumed dead beneath Wano's volcano.
