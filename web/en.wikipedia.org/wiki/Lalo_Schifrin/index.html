Lalo Schifrin

Lalo Schifrin (born Boris Claudio Schifrin, 21 June 1932 – 26 June 2025) was an Argentine-American pianist, composer, arranger and conductor, best known for his film and television scores, most famously the theme from Mission: Impossible with its distinctive 5/4 time signature.

Born in Buenos Aires, Schifrin studied classical piano and played jazz before joining Dizzy Gillespie's big band as pianist and arranger in 1960. His scores include The Cincinnati Kid, Cool Hand Luke, Bullitt, Enter the Dragon, Dirty Harry and the Rush Hour films, blending jazz, funk and orchestral writing.

Schifrin was nominated for six Academy Awards and won four Grammy Awards. In 2018 he received an Honorary Academy Award for his contributions to film music.
