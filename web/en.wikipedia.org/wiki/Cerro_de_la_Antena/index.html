Cerro de la Antena

Cerro de la Antena ("Antenna Hill") is a name borne by several hills and peaks in the Spanish-speaking world, typically given to summits used as sites for radio, television, or telecommunications antennae. Hills with this name are found in Mexico and Spain, where their elevated positions make them natural locations for broadcast transmitters serving the surrounding valleys and towns.

Broadcast use

Like other transmitter hills — such as Cerro del Cubilete in Guanajuato or the many "Cerro de la Antena" sites in provincial Mexico — these summits are commonly topped by masts carrying FM radio, television, and mobile telephony equipment, and they are frequently visible landmarks identifiable from a distance by their antenna arrays. Access tracks and small service buildings usually accompany the installations.
