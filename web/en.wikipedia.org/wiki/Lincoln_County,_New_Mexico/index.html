Lincoln County is a county in the U.S. state of New Mexico. Its county seat is Carrizozo, and its largest community is the resort village of Ruidoso. The county was formed in 1869 and named for President Abraham Lincoln; it was once the largest county by area in the United States before being reduced by the creation of neighbouring counties.

History

Lincoln County is best known for the Lincoln County War of 1878–1881, a violent conflict between rival mercantile and ranching factions — the Murphy-Dolan "House" and the Tunstall-McSween Regulators — that drew in the young gunman Henry McCarty, better known as Billy the Kid, as well as Sheriff Pat Garrett. The town of Lincoln preserves many buildings from the period as the Lincoln Historic Site. The county also includes Fort Stanton, established in 1855, and part of the Mescalero Apache Reservation.

Geography and communities

The county covers about 12,500 km² of south-central New Mexico, spanning the Sacramento and Capitan mountain ranges, including Sierra Blanca (3,652 m), whose slopes host the Ski Apache resort. Communities include Ruidoso, Ruidoso Downs (home to Ruidoso Downs Race Track, where the All American Futurity quarter-horse race is run), Capitan (birthplace of the fire-prevention bear Smokey Bear), Carrizozo, Corona, and Lincoln. Large tracts fall within the Lincoln National Forest.
