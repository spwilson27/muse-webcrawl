Central Saint Martins
Type: Art and design college
Established: 1989 (merger of schools founded 1854 and 1910)
Parent: University of the Arts London
Location: King's Cross, London, England

Central Saint Martins (often abbreviated CSM) is a public art university in London, England, and a constituent college of the University of the Arts London. It is one of the world's best-known centres for art and design education, particularly in fashion.

History

The college was formed in 1989 by the merger of the Central School of Art and Design (founded 1896 as the Central School of Arts and Crafts) and Saint Martin's School of Art (founded 1854). The Central School had pioneered industrial design education in Britain; Saint Martin's was noted for sculpture and painting. The Byam Shaw School of Art and the Drama Centre London were later absorbed into CSM.

Campus

In 2011 the college consolidated at a new campus in the Granary Building, a converted Victorian granary at King's Cross, designed by Stanton Williams and winner of several architectural awards.

Alumni

Famous graduates include fashion designers Alexander McQueen, John Galliano and Stella McCartney; artists Gilbert & George and Antony Gormley; musicians Jarvis Cocker and M.I.A.; and actors including Colin Firth and Pierce Brosnan (Drama Centre).
