Down payment

A down payment (also called a deposit in British English or a downpayment) is an initial up-front partial payment for the purchase of expensive items or services such as a house or car. It is usually paid in cash or an equivalent at the time of finalising the transaction, with the remainder of the purchase price financed by a loan. A larger down payment reduces the amount borrowed, the interest paid over the life of the loan, and often the interest rate offered.

Real estate

In home purchases, down payments are typically expressed as a percentage of the purchase price. In the United States, a 20 percent down payment has traditionally avoided the need for private mortgage insurance, while government-backed programmes such as FHA loans permit much smaller down payments, and VA loans may require none. Lenders require down payments to reduce their risk and to demonstrate the borrower's financial commitment.
