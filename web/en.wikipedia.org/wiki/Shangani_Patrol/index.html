Shangani Patrol

Shangani Patrol
Date: 4 December 1893
Location: north of the Shangani River, Matabeleland
Result: Patrol annihilated

The Shangani Patrol was a detachment of the British South Africa Company, led by Major Allan Wilson, that was wiped out by Ndebele (Matabele) warriors on 4 December 1893 during the First Matabele War in present-day Zimbabwe.

Events

Sent across the Shangani River in pursuit of the Ndebele king Lobengula, Wilson's 37 men were cut off after Major Patrick Forbes's main column failed to cross the flooded river. Surrounded by thousands of warriors, the patrol fought to the last man. Their stand became a celebrated legend of Rhodesian settler mythology, often compared to Custer's Last Stand.

Legacy

The patrol's members were buried at Great Zimbabwe and later reinterred at the Matopos Hills near Cecil Rhodes's grave.
