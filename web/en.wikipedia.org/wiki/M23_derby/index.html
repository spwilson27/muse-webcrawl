M23 derby

The M23 derby is the name given to football matches between Brighton & Hove Albion and Crystal Palace, two clubs based in southern England and connected by the M23 motorway and the A23 road between Brighton and Croydon, London.

M23 derby
Teams: Brighton & Hove Albion, Crystal Palace
Region: Sussex / South London
First meeting: 25 December 1920
Named after: M23 motorway

Origins of the rivalry

Although the clubs are about 40 miles apart, the rivalry intensified during the 1970s when both sides, managed by Terry Venables (Palace) and Alan Mullery (Brighton), contested promotions and met frequently in league and cup competition, including a famously contentious FA Cup tie in 1976.

Later meetings

The fixture has been played in the top flight since both clubs reached the Premier League, and is known for a partisan atmosphere despite the geographic distance between the teams.
