Wan Chai District

Wan Chai District is one of the 18 districts of Hong Kong. It is located on the northern shore of Hong Kong Island.

Population: approx. 180,000
Area: approx. 10.6 km²

Wan Chai District is one of the 18 districts of Hong Kong. Situated on the northern coast of Hong Kong Island, it is bordered by Eastern District to the east, Southern District to the south, and Central and Western District to the west. The district takes its name from the area of Wan Chai, once a small bay ("wan chai" means "small bay" in Cantonese).

Geography

The district includes the neighbourhoods of Wan Chai, Causeway Bay, Happy Valley, Tai Hang, So Kon Po and Jardine's Lookout. The coastline was substantially extended by land reclamation, most recently for the Central–Wan Chai Bypass. Victoria Park, one of Hong Kong's largest urban parks, lies in Causeway Bay.

Landmarks

Major landmarks include the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre, the Golden Bauhinia Square, the Happy Valley Racecourse, the Hong Kong Stadium, and the historic Blue House cluster of tong lau shophouses. The district is a major commercial and entertainment hub, known for its nightlife, restaurants and the annual Hong Kong Book Fair.

Transport

Wan Chai District is served by the Island line of the MTR, with stations at Wan Chai, Causeway Bay and Tin Hau, as well as trams, buses and the Wan Chai Ferry Pier, which offers services across Victoria Harbour.
