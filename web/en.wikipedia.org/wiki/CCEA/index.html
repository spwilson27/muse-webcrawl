CCEA

Full name: Council for the Curriculum, Examinations & Assessment
Formed: 1994
Jurisdiction: Northern Ireland
Headquarters: Belfast

The Council for the Curriculum, Examinations & Assessment (CCEA) is a non-departmental public body in Northern Ireland responsible for developing the curriculum, setting examinations and regulating qualifications.

Functions

CCEA advises the Department of Education on the Northern Ireland Curriculum, develops teaching resources and learning materials, and operates as an awarding organisation offering GCSEs, A-levels and vocational qualifications taken mainly in Northern Ireland.

History

CCEA was established on 1 April 1994, bringing together the former Northern Ireland Curriculum Council and the Northern Ireland Schools Examinations and Assessment Council.
