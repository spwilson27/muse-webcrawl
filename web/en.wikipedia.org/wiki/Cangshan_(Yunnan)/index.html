Cangshan (Yunnan)

LocationDali, Yunnan, China
Highest peakMalong Peak
Elevation4,122 m (13,524 ft)
LengthAbout 50 km (north–south)

Cangshan (Chinese: 苍山; pinyin: Cāngshān), also known as the Diancang Range, is a mountain range in Yunnan province, China, rising west of the city of Dali and overlooking Erhai Lake. The range runs roughly north–south for about 50 kilometres and forms part of the dramatic landscape for which the Dali region is famous.

Geography

Cangshan has nineteen peaks over 3,500 metres, the highest being Malong Peak at 4,122 metres. Eighteen streams flow down its eastern slopes into Erhai Lake. The mountains are noted for their marble — the word for marble in Chinese, dàlǐshí, literally means "Dali stone" — which has been quarried locally for centuries.

Nature and tourism

The range supports rich plant diversity, including azaleas, camellias and conifer forests, and is protected as part of a national nature reserve and geopark. A paved "Jade Belt" path runs along the mountainside at about 2,600 metres, and cable cars carry visitors from Dali to temples and viewpoints on the slopes. Snow on Cangshan's peaks, the "wind, flowers, snow and moon" of local Bai tradition, is one of the celebrated scenic emblems of Dali.
