Williamsburg, Virginia

Williamsburg is an independent city in Virginia, United States, founded in 1632 as Middle Plantation. It was the capital of the Colony of Virginia from 1699 to 1780 and is home to the College of William & Mary.

State: Virginia
Founded: 1632
Population: ~15,000
Known for: Colonial Williamsburg, William & Mary

History

After Jamestown burned, the capital moved here and was renamed for William III. Williamsburg was central to the American Revolution — Patrick Henry's speeches, the Gunpowder Incident — before the capital moved to Richmond in 1780. In the 1920s–30s John D. Rockefeller Jr. funded restoration of Colonial Williamsburg, now a major living-history museum.

Today

Part of the Historic Triangle with Jamestown and Yorktown, linked by the Colonial Parkway. See also College of William & Mary.
