Hypebeast (person)

A hypebeast is a slang term, originating in streetwear and sneaker culture, for a person who avidly collects and wears fashionable, heavily hyped clothing, shoes, and accessories — particularly limited-edition items from brands such as Supreme, Nike, Off-White, and Yeezy — primarily to display status and keep up with trends. The term is often used pejoratively, implying an obsession with hype and brand prestige over personal style.

Origin and culture

The word took off with the Hong Kong-based streetwear blog Hypebeast, founded by Kevin Ma in 2005, which became a leading media platform for sneaker and fashion "drops". Hypebeast culture revolves around limited releases: queuing for drops, raffles, resale markets such as StockX, and social-media display of rare items. It is closely tied to youth, hip-hop, and skate culture and to the economics of artificial scarcity in fashion marketing.

Criticism

Commentators criticize hypebeast consumerism for materialism, inflated resale prices, bot-driven purchasing, and waste, while others defend it as a creative youth subculture and an accessible form of entrepreneurship through resale. The term has broadened in casual use to describe anyone who conspicuously follows any over-hyped trend.
