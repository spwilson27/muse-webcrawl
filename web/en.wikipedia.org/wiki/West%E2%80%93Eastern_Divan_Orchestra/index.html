The West–Eastern Divan Orchestra is a youth orchestra based in Seville, Spain, bringing together young musicians from Israel, Palestine and other Arab countries of the Middle East. It was founded in 1999 by the conductor Daniel Barenboim and the Palestinian scholar Edward Said.

Name and mission

The orchestra takes its name from the West-östlicher Divan, a collection of poems by Goethe inspired by the Persian poet Hafez. Its founding idea was to create a forum in which young musicians from across the region's divides could rehearse, perform and talk together, with music-making as a model for dialogue.

Activities

The ensemble tours internationally, has performed at major festivals and concert halls, and has made numerous recordings under Barenboim. Its alumni number in the thousands, and the project also inspired the Barenboim–Said Academy in Berlin.
