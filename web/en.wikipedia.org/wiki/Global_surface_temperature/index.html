Global surface temperature

Global surface temperature (GST) is the average temperature of Earth's surface, typically calculated from measurements of sea surface temperature and air temperature over land. It is a key indicator of climate change.

Instrumental records from organisations such as NASA's GISS, NOAA, Berkeley Earth and the HadCRUT series show that global surface temperature has risen by roughly 1.1–1.2 °C since the late 19th century, with the rate of warming accelerating since the 1970s.

Measurement

Anomalies relative to a baseline period (often 1951–1980 or pre-industrial 1850–1900) are usually reported rather than absolute temperatures. Satellite microwave sounding measurements of the lower troposphere complement surface records since 1979.
