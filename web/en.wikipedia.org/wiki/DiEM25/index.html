DiEM25

The Democracy in Europe Movement 2025, generally shortened to DiEM25, is a pan-European political movement launched in Berlin in February 2016 by the former Greek finance minister Yanis Varoufakis and others. It campaigns for the democratisation of the European Union.

Manifesto

DiEM25's founding manifesto argues that the EU will either be democratised or it will disintegrate. Its demands include full transparency in European decision-making, a constitutional assembly to draft a democratic constitution for Europe, and a "European New Deal" addressing debt, investment, migration and poverty.

Electoral activity

The movement has supported and founded national parties, such as MeRA25 in Greece, which entered the Greek parliament in 2019. DiEM25 organises through local volunteer collectives (DSCs) across Europe and beyond, and decides policy through internal all-member votes.
