Charles Michel (born 21 December 1975) is a Belgian politician who served as President of the European Council from 2019 to 2024 and as Prime Minister of Belgium from 2014 to 2019. A member of the French-speaking liberal Reformist Movement (MR), he was the youngest Belgian prime minister since 1845 when he took office at the age of 38.

Early life and career

Michel was born in Namur, the son of Louis Michel, a former Belgian foreign minister and European Commissioner. He studied law at the Université libre de Bruxelles and the University of Amsterdam, and practised as a lawyer. He entered politics young, becoming a provincial councillor and then, in 1999, a member of the Belgian Chamber of Representatives. He served as mayor of Wavre from 2006 and as a minister in the Walloon government, and in 2011 became leader of the MR.

Prime Minister of Belgium

After the 2014 federal election, Michel formed an unprecedented centre-right coalition — the "Swedish coalition" of Flemish nationalists (N-VA), Flemish and French-speaking liberals and Flemish Christian democrats — in which his party was the only Francophone partner. His government pursued pension, tax and labour-market reforms. The coalition collapsed in December 2018 over the UN Global Compact for Migration; Michel resigned and led a caretaker government through the 2019 elections.

President of the European Council

In July 2019 Michel was chosen to succeed Donald Tusk as President of the European Council, chairing summits of EU heads of state and government from 1 December 2019. His term covered the COVID-19 pandemic and the agreement of the Next Generation EU recovery fund, Brexit's final phase, and the coordination of the EU's response to Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022. He was succeeded by António Costa on 1 December 2024.
