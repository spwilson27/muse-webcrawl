Moro Islamic Liberation Front

Founded1977 (split from MNLF); formally organised 1984
HeadquartersCamp Darapanan, Sultan Kudarat, Maguindanao, Philippines
IdeologyMoro self-determination, Islamic democracy
LeadersHashim Salamat (founder); Murad Ebrahim

The Moro Islamic Liberation Front (MILF; Arabic: جبهة تحرير مورو الإسلامية) is a group based in Mindanao, Philippines, which sought self-determination for the Moro (Muslim) peoples of the southern Philippines. It broke away from the Moro National Liberation Front (MNLF) under Hashim Salamat, favouring an Islamic-oriented state over the MNLF's more secular line.

History

The MILF emerged in 1977 and was formally established in 1984. For decades it waged an insurgency against the Philippine government from camps in central Mindanao. Peace negotiations began in the late 1990s and, after periods of renewed war — notably in 2000 and 2008 — culminated in the Framework Agreement on the Bangsamoro (2012) and the Comprehensive Agreement on the Bangsamoro (2014).

Bangsamoro region

The peace process led to the Bangsamoro Organic Law of 2018 and the creation of the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM) in 2019, with MILF chairman Murad Ebrahim appointed interim chief minister of the Bangsamoro Transition Authority. Under the agreements, MILF combatants have been progressively decommissioned.
