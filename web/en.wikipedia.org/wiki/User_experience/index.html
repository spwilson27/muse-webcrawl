User experience

User experience (UX) is how a user interacts with and experiences a product, system, or service. It includes a person's perceptions of utility, ease of use, and efficiency. Improving user experience is important to most companies, designers, and creators when creating and refining products because negative user experience can diminish use of the product and, therefore, revenue.

Definition and scope

User experience is a broad concept encompassing all aspects of the end-user's interaction with a company, its services, and its products. It draws on fields such as human–computer interaction, psychology, industrial design, and information architecture. The term was popularised by Don Norman in the 1990s.

User experience design

User experience design (UX design) is the process of defining the experience a user would go through when interacting with a product. It involves user research, information architecture, interaction design, usability testing, and visual design. Practitioners aim to create products that are useful, usable, desirable, findable, accessible, and credible.

Evaluation

UX is evaluated through methods such as usability testing, surveys, interviews, analytics, and A/B testing. Because user experience is subjective and dynamic, its measurement combines qualitative observation with quantitative metrics.
