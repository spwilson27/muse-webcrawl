Doklam

Doklam (known in India as Doka La and in China as Donglang) is a high-altitude plateau region in the Himalayas, at the trijunction of Bhutan, China, and India. The territory is claimed by both Bhutan and China and is strategically significant to India because of its proximity to the Siliguri Corridor.

2017 standoff

In June 2017, Chinese construction crews began extending a road on the plateau, prompting Indian troops to intervene in support of Bhutan. The resulting military standoff between India and China lasted 73 days, ending on 28 August 2017 with both sides withdrawing. The incident was one of the most serious border crises between the two countries in decades and led to renewed attention on the unresolved Bhutan–China boundary negotiations.
