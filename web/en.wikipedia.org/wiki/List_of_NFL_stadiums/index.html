List of NFL stadiums

The National Football League (NFL) comprises 32 teams, most of which play in dedicated stadiums. As of the 2024 season there are 30 stadiums in use, with the New York Giants and New York Jets sharing MetLife Stadium, and the Los Angeles Rams and Los Angeles Chargers sharing SoFi Stadium. The oldest stadium is Soldier Field in Chicago (opened 1924, heavily rebuilt 2002–03); the largest by seating capacity is MetLife Stadium (82,500).
