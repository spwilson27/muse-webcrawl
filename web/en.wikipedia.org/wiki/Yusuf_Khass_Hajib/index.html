Yusuf Khass Hajib

Yusuf Khass Hajib (also Yusuf Balasaguni) was an 11th-century Turkic poet and courtier of the Karakhanid Khanate, author of the Kutadgu Bilig, completed at Kashgar in about 1070. The name "Khass Hajib" is not a family name but a court title—"privy chamberlain" or "special adviser"—bestowed on him by the Karakhanid khan Tabgach Bughra Khan in reward for the presentation of his poem. He is known by this title across Central Asia, and by his nisba Balasaguni from his birthplace, Balasagun.

References in culture

Under the name Yusuf Khass Hajib he is commemorated in the names of universities and cultural institutions in Kyrgyzstan and Xinjiang, and he features on Kyrgyz currency. UNESCO-associated Turkic bodies marked 2016–2017 in his honour.
