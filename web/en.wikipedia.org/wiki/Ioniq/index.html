Ioniq

Manufacturer: Hyundai
Type: Electric sub-brand
Introduced: 2020 (as sub-brand)

Ioniq is a nameplate used by the South Korean manufacturer Hyundai for electrified vehicles. It was first applied to a compact liftback produced from 2016 to 2022 that was offered with hybrid, plug-in hybrid, and fully electric powertrains — the first production car sold with all three electrified options and no conventional petrol-only version. In August 2020 Hyundai relaunched Ioniq as a dedicated electric sub-brand.

Original model (2016–2022)

The original Ioniq was unveiled in South Korea in January 2016 and shown at the Geneva and New York motor shows that year. Built on a dedicated platform with extensive use of lightweight materials, the electric version offered a range that grew from about 200 km (124 miles) in early versions to roughly 294 km (183 miles) after a 2019 update with a 38.3 kWh battery.

Electric sub-brand

As a sub-brand, Ioniq's models are built on Hyundai's E-GMP platform. The first, the Ioniq 5 crossover (2021), was followed by the Ioniq 6 streamlined saloon (2022) and the larger Ioniq 9 SUV. The Ioniq 5 won the 2022 World Car of the Year award, along with World Electric Vehicle and World Car Design of the Year.
