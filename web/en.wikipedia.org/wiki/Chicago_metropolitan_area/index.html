The Chicago metropolitan area, often called Chicagoland, is the metropolitan area centred on the city of Chicago in northeastern Illinois, extending into northwest Indiana and southeast Wisconsin. It is the third-largest metropolitan area in the United States, with roughly 9.5 million residents in the metropolitan statistical area and about 10 million in the wider combined statistical area.

Geography

The region lies on the southwestern shore of Lake Michigan and is organised around Cook County and the Illinois "collar counties" of DuPage, Kane, Lake, McHenry, and Will, plus Lake and Porter counties in Indiana and Kenosha County in Wisconsin. Historically the plain around the Chicago Portage was a critical link between the Great Lakes and the Mississippi River watershed.

Economy and transport

Chicagoland is a major centre of finance, manufacturing, logistics, and higher education. It is one of the world's great rail and freight hubs, served by O'Hare International Airport — among the busiest airports in the world — and Midway International Airport. Commuter rail (Metra) and the CTA rail and bus network serve the region.
