Phinehas

Phinehas (also spelled Phineas) is a figure in the Hebrew Bible, the son of Eleazar and grandson of Aaron. In the Book of Numbers, Phinehas zealously intervenes during the incident at Baal-Peor, killing an Israelite man and a Midianite woman, which stops a plague; in reward, God grants him and his descendants a covenant of perpetual priesthood.

Phinehas later appears in the Book of Joshua and in Judges, and his descendants served as high priests. His name is of Egyptian derivation, often interpreted as "the Nubian" or "the bronze-coloured one".
