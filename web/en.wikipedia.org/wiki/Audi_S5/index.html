The Audi S5 is the high-performance variant of Audi's A5 model range, manufactured by the German automaker Audi since 2007. It sits above the regular A5 models in the line-up and below the more extreme RS 5. The S5 is offered as a coupé, a cabriolet, and a five-door Sportback.

First generation (B8, 2007–2016)

The original S5 coupé, launched alongside the A5 in 2007, was powered by a 4.2-litre naturally aspirated FSI V8 engine producing 354 PS (260 kW; 349 hp), paired with either a six-speed manual or six-speed Tiptronic automatic transmission. The S5 Cabriolet and Sportback models instead used a 3.0-litre supercharged V6 TFSI engine with 333 PS (245 kW).

For the 2013 model year facelift, the coupé also adopted the 3.0-litre supercharged V6. All versions featured Audi's quattro all-wheel drive, sport suspension, and subtle styling differences from the A5, including aluminium-look mirror housings and quad exhaust outlets.

Second generation (B9, 2016–2024)

The second-generation S5, built on the MLB Evo platform, is powered by a new 3.0-litre turbocharged V6 TFSI engine producing 354 PS (260 kW; 349 hp) and 500 N·m of torque, mated to an eight-speed Tiptronic automatic. Audi quotes a 0–100 km/h time of 4.7 seconds for the coupé. In European markets, from 2019 the S5 was sold with a 3.0-litre V6 TDI diesel engine producing 347 PS and 700 N·m of torque, with a 48-volt mild-hybrid system.

Third generation (B10, 2024–present)

With the introduction of Audi's new naming strategy, the third-generation model continues as the S5 within the merged A5 line (replacing both the previous A4 and A5 ranges), offered as a liftback sedan and Avant wagon. It uses a 3.0-litre V6 TFSI with mild-hybrid technology producing around 367 PS.
