Lists of United States state symbols

The states of the United States have adopted official state symbols — birds, flowers, trees, songs, mottos and many other emblems — usually by act of the state legislature. This article is an index of the lists of those symbols, by state and by category.

By category

CategoryExamples
State birdsNorthern cardinal (seven states), western meadowlark (six states)
State flowersRose (New York), orange blossom (Florida), forget-me-not (Alaska)
State treesSugar maple (several north-eastern states), ponderosa pine (Montana)
State songs"Yankee Doodle" (Connecticut), "My Old Kentucky Home" (Kentucky)
State mottos"Live Free or Die" (New Hampshire), "Eureka" (California)
State foods and beveragesMilk (many states), blueberry muffin (Massachusetts)
State minerals, rocks and gemsAgate (Minnesota), turquoise (New Mexico and Arizona)

By state

Each state maintains its own set of lists — for example, "List of Alabama state symbols", "List of California state symbols", "List of Texas state symbols" and so on for all fifty states, plus symbols of the District of Columbia and the US territories.
