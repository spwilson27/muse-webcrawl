Bølling–Allerød Interstadial

Bølling–Allerød Interstadial
Periodc. 14,690 – 12,890 years BP
Named forBølling lake and Allerød, Denmark
Preceded byOldest Dryas
Followed byYounger Dryas

The Bølling–Allerød Interstadial was an abrupt warm and moist period during the final stages of the last glacial period, lasting roughly from 14,690 to 12,890 years before present. It is recorded clearly in Greenland ice cores and North Atlantic sediment records.

Climate

Temperatures in Greenland rose sharply at the start of the Bølling oscillation, meltwater pulses raised global sea level, and forests expanded across northern Europe. A brief cooler episode, the Older Dryas, separates the Bølling from the Allerød within the interstadial.

End

The warm interval ended abruptly with the onset of the Younger Dryas cold reversal around 12,900 years ago, after which the Holocene began.
