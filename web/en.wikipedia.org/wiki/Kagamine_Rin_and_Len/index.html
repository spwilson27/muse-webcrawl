Kagamine Rin and Len

Kagamine Rin & Len
Developer: Crypton Future Media
Release: 27 December 2007
Engine: Vocaloid 2 (later V4X)
Voice provider: Asami Shimoda

Kagamine Rin and Len are a pair of Japanese Vocaloid voicebanks developed by Crypton Future Media. Released in December 2007 as the second entry in the Character Vocal Series after Hatsune Miku, they are voiced by Asami Shimoda and personified as a pair of 14-year-old characters — the female Rin and the male Len.

Development

Crypton designed the pair as mirror images: their surname Kagamine combines kagami (mirror) with ne (sound). A single voice actress recorded both a feminine and a masculine voice. An "Append" expansion followed in 2010, and updated V4X banks in 2015.

Usage and culture

Rin and Len star in thousands of user-produced songs distributed on video sites such as Niconico and YouTube, and appear as holographic performers in Vocaloid concerts alongside Miku. Their character designs — blonde hair, black and yellow outfits, and signature accessories — are staples of cosplay and fan art.
