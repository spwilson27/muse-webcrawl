List of active duty United States four-star officers

In the United States Armed Forces, four-star officers hold the grade of admiral (Navy and Coast Guard) or general (Army, Air Force, Marine Corps, and Space Force). Four-star billets are established by law (10 U.S.C. § 601 and related provisions), and the number of officers who may serve on active duty in four-star grade is strictly capped; appointments are nominated by the President and confirmed by the Senate. There are typically around three dozen four-star officers on active duty at any one time.

Statutory notes

Four-star grade is tied to the position held rather than being a permanent personal rank; officers generally revert to two-star grade if not reassigned, unless retired in grade by law. By statute, the services each maintain limited numbers of four-star billets, with additional joint billets allocated by the Secretary of Defense. The Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff is the highest-ranking officer in the armed forces, though by law exercises no operational command over the services.
