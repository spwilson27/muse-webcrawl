List of dog diseases

This is a list of dog diseases. Dogs are susceptible to a wide range of infectious, hereditary, metabolic and neoplastic disorders, many of which are covered by routine vaccination and parasite control. The list below groups common conditions by category.

Infectious diseases

  • Canine distemper — morbillivirus infection affecting respiratory, gastrointestinal and nervous systems; vaccine-preventable.
  • Canine parvovirus — highly contagious virus causing severe haemorrhagic enteritis, especially in puppies.
  • Rabies — invariably fatal viral encephalitis, zoonotic; controlled by vaccination and law in many countries.
  • Infectious canine hepatitis — canine adenovirus 1.
  • Kennel cough (infectious tracheobronchitis) — complex including Bordetella bronchiseptica and parainfluenza virus.
  • Leptospirosis — bacterial, zoonotic, spread via urine-contaminated water.
  • Canine influenza — H3N8 and H3N2 subtypes.
  • Lyme disease — Borrelia burgdorferi, tick-borne.
  • Ehrlichiosis and anaplasmosis — tick-borne rickettsial diseases.
  • Heartworm disease — Dirofilaria immitis, mosquito-borne.
  • Canine brucellosis — Brucella canis, reproductive disease.
  • Coccidiosis, giardiasis — intestinal protozoal infections.
  • Ringworm (dermatophytosis) — fungal skin infection, zoonotic.

Hereditary and congenital conditions

  • Hip dysplasia and elbow dysplasia
  • Progressive retinal atrophy and hereditary cataracts
  • Degenerative myelopathy
  • Intervertebral disc disease (especially in chondrodystrophic breeds)
  • Patellar luxation
  • Von Willebrand disease (bleeding disorder)
  • Collie eye anomaly
  • Deafness associated with merle and piebald coat genes
  • Brachycephalic obstructive airway syndrome

Metabolic, endocrine and organ diseases

  • Diabetes mellitus
  • Hypothyroidism and hyperadrenocorticism (Cushing's disease)
  • Hypoadrenocorticism (Addison's disease)
  • Chronic kidney disease
  • Pancreatitis
  • Gastric dilatation-volvulus ("bloat")
  • Dilated cardiomyopathy and mitral valve disease
  • Epilepsy (idiopathic)
  • Canine cognitive dysfunction

Cancers

  • Lymphoma, mast cell tumour, osteosarcoma, haemangiosarcoma, melanoma, mammary tumours, transmissible venereal tumour.

Skin and ear conditions

  • Atopic dermatitis, flea allergy dermatitis, demodectic and sarcoptic mange, otitis externa, hot spots (acute moist dermatitis), pyoderma.
