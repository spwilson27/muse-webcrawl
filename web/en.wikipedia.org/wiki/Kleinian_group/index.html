Kleinian group

A Kleinian group is a discrete subgroup of PSL(2, C), the group of Möbius transformations of the Riemann sphere, named for Felix Klein. Kleinian groups act on hyperbolic 3-space, and their quotients are hyperbolic 3-manifolds, making them central to three-dimensional geometry and topology.

Limit set

The group's action divides the sphere into the limit set — typically a fractal on which the action is chaotic — and the domain of discontinuity, where the action is properly discontinuous and the quotient is a conformal surface or orbifold. Finitely generated Kleinian groups were the subject of the tameness and ending lamination theorems, key steps toward Thurston's geometrisation programme.
