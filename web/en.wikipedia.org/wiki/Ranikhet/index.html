Ranikhet is a hill station and cantonment town in Almora district in the Indian state of Uttarakhand. Situated at an elevation of about 1,869 metres in the Kumaon hills, it offers views of the western Himalayan peaks including Nanda Devi. Its name means "Queen's Meadow" in Hindi.

History

According to local legend, Raja Sudhardev's queen, Rani Padmini, chose the site for a palace, giving the place its name. The British established a cantonment here in 1869, and Ranikhet became the headquarters of the Kumaon Regiment of the Indian Army, whose regimental centre and museum remain major landmarks.

Places of interest

Attractions include the Chaubatia orchards and botanical gardens, the Upat golf course — one of the highest in Asia — the Jhula Devi and Haidakhan temples, and the nearby village of Majkhali, known for Himalayan views.
