University Park, Texas

University Park is a city in Dallas County, Texas, United States, completely surrounded by the city of Dallas together with Highland Park. It is home to Southern Methodist University and had a population of about 25,000 at the 2020 census.

History

The city grew around SMU, founded in 1911; homes were built for faculty and staff, and University Park incorporated in 1924. Together with Highland Park it forms the Park Cities enclave.

Education

Most of the city is served by the Highland Park Independent School District, noted for high-performing schools; a small portion lies in the Dallas district.
