Culture war
Term from: German Kulturkampf
Popularised (US): James Davison Hunter (1991)
Arenas: Religion, education, media, sexuality

A culture war is a struggle between social groups for dominance of their values, beliefs and practices in public life. The term echoes Bismarck's Kulturkampf against the Catholic Church in 1870s Germany.

United States

Sociologist James Davison Hunter's Culture Wars (1991) described conflict between orthodox and progressive impulses across issues such as abortion, gun laws, separation of church and state, sexuality, education and the arts. Pat Buchanan's 1992 Republican convention speech declared a "religious war" for the soul of America. Scholars debate how polarised ordinary voters are versus elites and media.

Elsewhere

Analogous conflicts occur in Australia, Canada, the UK and elsewhere, over immigration, historical memory, language and national identity, amplified by social media.
