Alrosa

Alrosa (Russian: Алроса) is a Russian group of diamond mining companies that specialises in the exploration, mining, manufacture, and sale of diamonds. Headquartered in Mirny, Sakha Republic, with its main sales office in Moscow, it is the world's largest diamond mining company by volume, accounting for roughly a quarter to a third of global rough diamond production in many years. The Russian federal government and the Sakha Republic are major shareholders.

History

Alrosa was created in 1992 from the Soviet diamond monopoly Yakutalmaz following the dissolution of the USSR. Diamonds had been discovered in Yakutia in the 1950s, leading to the development of the Mir and Udachnaya kimberlite pipes and the town of Mirny. The company held an initial public offering on the Moscow Exchange in 2013, selling a minority stake.

Operations

The company's principal mining divisions operate in western Yakutia (Mirny, Aikhal, Udachny, Nyurba) and in Arkhangelsk Oblast (the Grib and Lomonosov deposits). It also holds a stake in Angola's Catoca mine, one of the world's largest kimberlite pipes. Famous stones recovered include large coloured diamonds sold through its polishing subsidiary and auctions.

Sanctions

Following Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, Alrosa and its chief executive were designated under United States and allied sanctions, and the G7 and European Union moved to restrict imports of Russian-origin diamonds, including through third-country tracing requirements.
