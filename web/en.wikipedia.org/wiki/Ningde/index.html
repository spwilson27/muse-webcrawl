Ningde

Ningde (宁德, Níngdé) is a prefecture-level city in north-eastern Fujian province, China, on the coast of the East China Sea opposite Taiwan. Its population is roughly 3.1 million. The urban core is the district of Jiaocheng.

Geography and economy

The prefecture is mountainous, with indented bays and islands including the Sanduao harbour area. Historically one of Fujian's poorer regions, Ningde has grown rapidly: it hosts the Fuqing and Ningde nuclear power stations and is the headquarters of CATL, the world's largest maker of electric-vehicle batteries, founded there in 2011. Aquaculture — especially large yellow croaker — is a major industry.

Administration

Ningde administers Jiaocheng District, the county-level city of Fu'an and Fuding, and the counties of Shouning, Xiapu, Zherong, Pingnan, Gutian and Zhouning. The region is home to a significant She ethnic minority population.
