Aurora, Colorado

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Aurora is a home rule municipality located in Arapahoe, Adams, and Douglas counties, Colorado, United States. The city's population was 386,261 at the 2020 United States census, a 20.91% increase since the 2010 census. Aurora is the third most populous city in the State of Colorado and the 51st most populous city in the United States. Aurora is a principal city of the Denver–Aurora–Lakewood metropolitan statistical area and a major city of the Front Range urban corridor.

History

Aurora originated in the 1880s as the town of Fletcher, taking its name from Denver businessman Donald Fletcher, who saw it as a real estate opportunity. Fletcher and his partners staked out four square miles east of Denver. The town was renamed Aurora in 1907, after the Roman goddess of dawn. Growth was slow until the mid-twentieth century, when Fitzsimons Army Hospital, Lowry Air Force Base and Buckley Air Force Base (now Buckley Space Force Base) drove rapid expansion.

On July 20, 2012, a mass shooting occurred at a Century movie theatre during a midnight screening of The Dark Knight Rises, killing 12 people and injuring 70 others — at the time the largest number of casualties in one shooting in modern U.S. history. The city has since grown into one of the most diverse in Colorado, with large immigrant communities along East Colfax Avenue and South Havana Street.

Geography and economy

Aurora lies on the high plains east of Denver, at elevations rising above 5,800 feet in its southeastern districts. Major employers include the Anschutz Medical Campus, Children's Hospital Colorado, Buckley Space Force Base and defence contractors. The city spans three counties and is known for its extensive parks, reservoirs and golf courses.

Government

Aurora is governed by a council–manager system with a ten-member city council and a mayor elected at large. It is one of the largest cities in the United States by land area among its population class, covering roughly 160 square miles.
