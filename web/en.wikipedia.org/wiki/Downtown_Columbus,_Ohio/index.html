Downtown Columbus, Ohio

Downtown Columbus is the central business district of Columbus, Ohio, situated near the confluence of the Scioto and Olentangy rivers. It contains the Ohio Statehouse, completed in 1861 and one of the oldest working state capitols in the United States, as well as the city's tallest building, the Rhodes State Office Tower, and the historic LeVeque Tower, a 1927 Art Deco skyscraper that was the tallest building between New York and Chicago when completed.

Features

Downtown landmarks include the Greater Columbus Convention Center, Nationwide Arena in the adjacent Arena District, the Columbus Museum of Art, and the Scioto Mile park system along the riverfront. The district is home to Columbus State Community College, Capital University Law School, and the main branch of the Columbus Metropolitan Library. Since the 2000s, residential development has grown substantially in the downtown core.
