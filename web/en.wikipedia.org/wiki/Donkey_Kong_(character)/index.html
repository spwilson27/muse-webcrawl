Donkey Kong, often shortened to DK, is a fictional gorilla character in Nintendo's Donkey Kong and Mario franchises. He first appeared as the barrel-throwing antagonist of the 1981 arcade game Donkey Kong, who had kidnapped Mario's girlfriend Pauline. The modern, heroic Donkey Kong — grandson of the original (now Cranky Kong) — was introduced in Donkey Kong Country (1994), wearing only a red necktie with his initials.

Role in games

In the Country series, DK defends his banana hoard from King K. Rool and the Kremlings, teaming with Diddy Kong, Dixie Kong and the wider Kong family. He is a regular in Mario spin-offs — Mario Kart, Mario Party, tennis and golf titles, and the Super Smash Bros. series — as a heavyweight character. His name, chosen by Miyamoto, was intended to convey "stubborn ape": "donkey" for obstinacy and "Kong" for gorilla.

Legacy

Donkey Kong is one of gaming's most recognisable mascots, anchoring a franchise that has sold tens of millions of copies. He appeared in The Super Mario Bros. Movie (2023), voiced by Seth Rogen.
