Tony Gilroy

Anthony Joseph Gilroy (born September 11, 1956) is an American screenwriter, producer, and director. He wrote the screenplays for the first four films of the Bourne series and directed Michael Clayton and Duplicity, as well as The Bourne Legacy.

Gilroy was born in Manhattan, New York City, into a filmmaking family — his father was the playwright and filmmaker Frank D. Gilroy, and his brothers Dan and John Gilroy are also screenwriters and filmmakers. He first gained notice for The Cutting Edge and Dolores Claiborne, then adapted Robert Ludlum's novels for The Bourne Identity (2002), which launched a successful franchise.

Michael Clayton and Star Wars work

His directorial debut, Michael Clayton (2007), starring George Clooney, was nominated for seven Academy Awards, including Best Picture, Director, and Original Screenplay, and won for Tilda Swinton's supporting performance. Gilroy later co-wrote and performed extensive reshoot work on Rogue One (2016) and created the acclaimed Star Wars series Andor (2022–2025) for Disney+, which he ran as showrunner.
