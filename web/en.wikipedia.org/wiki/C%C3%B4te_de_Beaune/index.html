Côte de Beaune
Region: Burgundy, France
Main town: Beaune
Famous wines: Corton-Charlemagne, Meursault, Puligny-Montrachet

The Côte de Beaune is the southern half of the Côte d'Or escarpment in Burgundy, France, running roughly from Ladoix-Serrigny south to the Maranges. It is world-renowned for its white wines made from Chardonnay, though it also produces fine Pinot noir.

Wines

Villages such as Meursault, Puligny-Montrachet and Chassagne-Montrachet produce white Burgundies regarded among the finest dry white wines in the world. The hill of Corton yields both the red grand cru Corton and the white Corton-Charlemagne. Pommard and Volnay are noted for red wines.

Beaune

The town of Beaune is the wine capital of Burgundy, home to the famous Hospices de Beaune, whose annual charity wine auction each November sets a benchmark for Burgundy prices.
