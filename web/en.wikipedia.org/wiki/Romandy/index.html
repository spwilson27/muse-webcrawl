Romandy

Romandy (French: la Romandie, la Suisse romande) is the French-speaking part of western Switzerland. It covers roughly the western third of the country and is home to about two million people, around a quarter of the Swiss population.

Geography

Romandy comprises the cantons of Geneva, Vaud, Neuchâtel and Jura, and the French-speaking parts of the bilingual cantons of Fribourg, Valais and Bern. Its largest cities are Geneva and Lausanne. The region stretches from Lake Geneva and the Jura mountains to the Alps of the Valais.

Language and culture

Swiss French differs only slightly from standard French of France, mainly in vocabulary and some numbers (for example septante for seventy and nonante for ninety). Historically, Franco-Provençal dialects were spoken in much of the region but have largely died out. The cultural and political divide between Romandy and German-speaking Switzerland is sometimes referred to as the Röstigraben. The public broadcaster for the region is RTS (Radio Télévision Suisse).
