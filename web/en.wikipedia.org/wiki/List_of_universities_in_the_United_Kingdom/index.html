This is a list of universities in the United Kingdom. University status in the UK is granted by the Privy Council on the advice of the Office for Students (England), or the corresponding bodies in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. The list includes only institutions with full university status, ordered alphabetically within each constituent country; university colleges and other higher education institutions are listed separately.
