President pro tempore of the California State Senate

President pro tempore of the California State Senate
IncumbentMike McGuire (since 5 February 2024)
AppointerThe California State Senate (elected by its members, in practice chosen by the majority party caucus)
Term lengthTwo years (per legislative session); serves at the pleasure of the Senate

The president pro tempore of the California State Senate (often shortened to president pro tem) is the leader of the California State Senate and its highest-ranking officer after the lieutenant governor, who serves ex officio as its president. Under the California Constitution, the lieutenant governor presides over the Senate only nominally and may cast a tie-breaking vote; the day-to-day leadership of the chamber rests with the president pro tempore, elected by the senators from among their number.

Role and functions

The president pro tempore is effectively the Senate's floor leader and presiding officer. The office holder chairs the Senate Rules Committee, which assigns bills to committees, appoints committee chairs and members, and controls much of the chamber's business, staff and budget. The president pro tem is also the leader of the majority party in the Senate, negotiates the state budget with the governor and the Assembly Speaker, and is a central figure in California politics — generally considered, along with the Speaker of the Assembly, one of the two most powerful legislators in the state.

Succession

The president pro tempore stands in the line of succession to the governorship, after the lieutenant governor, and serves as acting governor whenever both the governor and lieutenant governor are absent from the state or incapacitated.

Notable office holders

  • James D. Phelan — later U.S. Senator and mayor of San Francisco
  • Hugh M. Burns — served as president pro tem in the 1950s–1960s era
  • James R. Mills (1971–1980)
  • David Roberti (1981–1994) — among the longest-serving modern holders
  • Bill Lockyer (1994–1998)
  • John Burton (1998–2004)
  • Don Perata (2004–2008)
  • Darrell Steinberg (2008–2014)
  • Kevin de León (2014–2018) — first Latino president pro tem in over a century
  • Toni Atkins (2018–2024) — first woman and first openly LGBTQ person to lead the Senate
  • Mike McGuire (2024–present)
