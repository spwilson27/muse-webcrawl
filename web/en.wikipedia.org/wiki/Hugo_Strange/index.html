Professor Hugo Strange is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, commonly as an adversary of the superhero Batman. Created by Bill Finger and Bob Kane, he debuted in Detective Comics #36 (1940), making him one of Batman's first recurring villains—predating both the Joker and Catwoman.

Fictional character biography

Strange is a brilliant but deranged psychologist and chemist who becomes obsessed with Batman. In his earliest appearances he was a mad scientist who used a fog machine to cover robberies and later created giant "Monster Men" through a growth serum. In modern continuity he is often portrayed as a psychiatrist at Arkham Asylum who deduces Batman's secret identity as Bruce Wayne and becomes fixated on replacing him. His obsession, genius-level intellect, and psychological insight make him one of the few villains to challenge Batman intellectually rather than physically.

Notable storylines include "Prey" (Legends of the Dark Knight), in which he manipulates Gotham's police task force against Batman, and the "Strange Apparitions" run by Steve Englehart and Marshall Rogers. He has a significant role in the video game Batman: Arkham City (2011), where, voiced by Corey Burton, he runs the Arkham City prison under "Protocol 10" in league with Ra's al Ghul. In television, BD Wong portrayed a younger Hugo Strange as a central antagonist in the series Gotham.
