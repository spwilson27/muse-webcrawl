Joaquim Alberto Chissano (born 22 October 1939) is a Mozambican politician who served as the second President of Mozambique from 1986 to 2005. A founding member of the liberation movement FRELIMO, he had earlier been prime minister of the transitional government (1974–75) and foreign minister after independence from Portugal.

Presidency

Chissano succeeded Samora Machel after Machel's death in a 1986 plane crash. He steered Mozambique away from Marxism-Leninism toward a market economy, oversaw the signing of the Nkomati Accord framework's aftermath and, most importantly, negotiated the Rome General Peace Accords of 1992, which ended the Mozambican Civil War with RENAMO. He won multiparty elections in 1994 and 1999.

Chissano declined to seek a third term and handed power peacefully to Armando Guebuza in 2005. In 2007 he became the first winner of the Mo Ibrahim Prize for Achievement in African Leadership, recognised for ending the civil war, rebuilding the economy and observing constitutional term limits.
