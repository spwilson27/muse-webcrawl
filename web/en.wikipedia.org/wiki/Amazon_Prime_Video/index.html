Amazon Prime Video, or simply Prime Video, is an American subscription video on-demand over-the-top streaming and rental service of Amazon, offered both as a standalone service and as part of Amazon's Prime subscription. The service primarily distributes films and television series produced by Amazon MGM Studios or licensed to Amazon, branded as Amazon Originals, alongside content from other providers, content add-ons, live sporting events, and video rental and purchasing options.

History

The service debuted on September 7, 2006, as Amazon Unbox in the United States. It grew through a series of rebrandings — Amazon Video on Demand, Amazon Instant Video, and finally Amazon Prime Video in 2015. Initially available only to residents of the United States, Germany, Japan, and the United Kingdom, the service expanded worldwide in November 2016, launching in more than 200 countries and territories. In 2024 the service introduced limited advertising on its standard tier in several markets, with an ad-free option available for an additional fee.

Original programming

Amazon began distributing original content in 2013 with the launch of Amazon Studios. Acclaimed originals include The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel, Fleabag (co-produced), The Boys, Jack Ryan, Reacher, Fallout, and the big-budget fantasy series The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power. Amazon's 2022 acquisition of Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer for $8.5 billion substantially expanded the service's library of films and series.

Sports

Prime Video has become a significant broadcaster of live sports, most notably holding exclusive rights to Thursday Night Football NFL games in the United States from 2022, as well as Premier League football in the UK, UEFA Champions League matches in several markets, and NBA coverage beginning with the 2025–26 season.

Availability

Prime Video is accessible via web browsers and dedicated apps on smart TVs, mobile devices, streaming sticks, and game consoles. Membership is bundled with Amazon Prime in most markets, though standalone subscriptions are offered in some countries.
