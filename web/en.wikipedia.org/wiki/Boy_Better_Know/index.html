Boy Better Know, often abbreviated to BBK, is an English grime collective and independent record label based in North London. Founded in 2005 by the MC and producer Jme (Jamie Adenuga), the crew grew out of the Meridian Walk estate in Tottenham and the pirate radio scene, and became one of the defining acts of grime's second wave and its mainstream breakthrough in the 2010s.

History

Jme founded Boy Better Know as a label in 2005, releasing his brother Skepta's early material and his own. The collective formed around a core of MCs including Skepta, Wiley, Jammer, Shorty, Frisco, and DJ Maximum, broadcasting on pirate stations such as Deja Vu 92.3 from rooftops in East and North London. BBK developed a strong independent business model—selling T-shirts, staging raves, and self-releasing music—at a time when grime had little mainstream support.

The label's profile rose sharply in 2015–2016. Skepta's single "Shutdown" became an anthem of grime's international resurgence, and his album Konnichiwa (2016), released through Boy Better Know, won the Mercury Prize. In 2017 the collective released the group album Greatest Hits-era material and took over London's O2 Academy Brixton, and in 2018 BBK was honoured with the Innovation Award at the NME Awards. Members have continued to pursue solo careers while collaborating under the BBK banner, and the label is widely credited with demonstrating that grime artists could achieve global success while retaining ownership and independence.
