Book cover

[Image placeholder: Book cover]

A book cover is any protective covering used to bind together the pages of a book. Beyond protection, covers serve to promote the book, summarise its content and identify its author and publisher. Covers may be hard (case-bound) or soft (paperback), and typically consist of a front cover, spine and back cover.

History

Early books were bound in leather, wood or precious materials, sometimes richly decorated. Printed dust jackets appeared in the 1820s–30s, initially as disposable protection; by the early 20th century they had become the principal advertising surface of trade books, carrying blurbs and artwork.

Design

The front cover usually bears the title, author's name and imagery; the spine carries title, author and publisher's colophon for shelf identification; the back cover may include a synopsis, reviews, barcode and ISBN. Cover design is a specialised field of graphic design, balancing typography, imagery and marketing considerations.
