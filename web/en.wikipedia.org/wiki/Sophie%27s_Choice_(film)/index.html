Sophie's Choice (film)

Directed byAlan J. Pakula
StarringMeryl Streep, Kevin Kline, Peter Macon
Release date1982
Based onSophie's Choice by William Styron

Sophie's Choice is a 1982 American drama film directed by Alan J. Pakula, adapted from William Styron's 1979 novel of the same name. It stars Meryl Streep as Sophie Zawistowska, a Polish immigrant and Holocaust survivor living in Brooklyn, Kevin Kline as her unstable lover Nathan, and Peter Macon as Stingo, the young Southern writer who narrates the story.

Plot

In 1947, Stingo moves to a boarding house in Brooklyn where he befriends Sophie and Nathan. Through flashbacks Sophie gradually reveals her past: her imprisonment at Auschwitz, her father's death, and the impossible "choice" forced on her by a Nazi officer between her two children — a trauma that haunts her and drives the film's tragic conclusion.

Reception and awards

Streep's performance, delivered partly in Polish and German-accented English, is widely regarded as one of the greatest in film history. She won the Academy Award for Best Actress, and the film received additional Oscar nominations including for its screenplay, cinematography, costume design, and score by Marvin Hamlisch.

Legacy

The phrase "Sophie's choice" entered common usage to describe an agonising decision between two equally unbearable alternatives. The film is frequently listed among the American Film Institute's greatest American films.
