Owens Valley

The Owens Valley is an arid valley of the Owens River in eastern California, between the Sierra Nevada to the west and the White and Inyo Mountains to the east. It is one of the deepest valleys in the United States.

The Los Angeles Aqueduct, completed in 1913, diverted Owens River water to Los Angeles, leading to the California Water Wars and the desiccation of Owens Lake.
