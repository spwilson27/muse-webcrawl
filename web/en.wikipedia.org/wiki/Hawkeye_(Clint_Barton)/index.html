Hawkeye (Clint Barton)

Hawkeye (Clinton Francis "Clint" Barton) is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Stan Lee and artist Don Heck, he first appeared as a reluctant villain in Tales of Suspense #57 (September 1964) before joining the Avengers in The Avengers #16 (1965). A master archer with no superpowers, Hawkeye fights with a bow, trick arrows, and extraordinary marksmanship.

Hawkeye, the Avengers' master archer.

Fictional character biography

Orphaned young, Clint Barton ran away to a carnival, where he was trained by the Swordsman and Trick Shot. Initially manipulated by Black Widow into fighting Iron Man, he soon reformed and became a mainstay of the Avengers, leading the West Coast Avengers and mentoring later heroes. Barton has at times operated under other identities, including Goliath (using Pym particles) and Ronin. After his death and resurrection in the events of Avengers Disassembled and House of M, he resumed the Hawkeye mantle, sharing it with his protégée Kate Bishop in acclaimed runs such as Matt Fraction and David Aja's series (2012–2015).

In other media

Jeremy Renner portrays Clint Barton in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, debuting in Thor (2011) and appearing in the Avengers films, Captain America: Civil War, and the Disney+ series Hawkeye (2021), which introduces Kate Bishop, played by Hailee Steinfeld.
