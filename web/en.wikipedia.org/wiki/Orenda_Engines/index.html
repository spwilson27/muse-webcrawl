Orenda Engines

Orenda Engines
Industry: Aerospace
Founded: 1946 (as part of Avro Canada)
Headquarters: Mississauga, Ontario, Canada
Products: Gas turbine engines

Orenda Engines was a Canadian aircraft engine manufacturer, originally the gas-turbine division of Avro Canada, based at Malton, Ontario. It is best known for the Orenda jet engine that powered Canadian-built Canadair Sabres and CF-100 Canuck interceptors, and for the powerful Iroquois engine developed for the Avro Arrow.

History

The company's turbojet work began during the Second World War under Turbo Research, which became part of A.V. Roe Canada in 1946. The Orenda axial-flow engine first ran in 1949 and went on to power thousands of aircraft. Development of the Iroquois, one of the most powerful engines of its era, ended with the cancellation of the Arrow programme in 1959.

Later years

Orenda continued in engine overhaul, repair and components manufacture, later becoming part of Magellan Aerospace, which continues to operate the Orenda name in Mississauga.
