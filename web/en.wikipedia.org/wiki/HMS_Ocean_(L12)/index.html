HMS Ocean was an amphibious assault ship of the United Kingdom's Royal Navy, designated a Landing Platform Helicopter (LPH). She was launched in 1995 and commissioned in 1998 as the sole member of her class, and for much of her career she was the largest ship in the Royal Navy. Ocean was designed to support amphibious landing operations, embarking Royal Marine Commandos and delivering them ashore by helicopter and landing craft.

Design

Ocean was based on the hull design of the Invincible-class aircraft carriers, adapted by Vickers Shipbuilding and Engineering at Barrow-in-Furness. Her flight deck, some 170 metres long, could operate up to a dozen medium support helicopters simultaneously, with hangar space for twelve more. She was optimised for the carriage of helicopters such as the Sea King, Merlin and later the Apache attack helicopter, and could also embark Chinooks on deck. Four LCVP Mk 5 landing craft were carried in davits, allowing her to land troops and vehicles directly.

The ship normally embarked an air group and a headquarters staff, and could accommodate around 480 troops in addition to her crew, with surge capacity to 800 for short periods. She also carried extensive command, control and communications facilities for directing joint operations.

Service history

Following commissioning in 1998, Ocean deployed widely. She provided disaster relief in Central America after Hurricane Mitch in 1998, supported operations over Sierra Leone in 2000, and took part in the 2003 invasion of Iraq, launching helicopter assaults against the Al-Faw Peninsula. She later deployed to the Mediterranean during the 2011 Libyan civil war, from which her Apaches conducted strike missions — the first time British Apache helicopters flew combat sorties from a ship at sea.

In 2012 Ocean was moored in the River Thames at Greenwich as part of the security operation for the London Olympic Games. She subsequently served as the Royal Navy's flagship and undertook exercises and patrols from the Baltic to the Caribbean, including hurricane relief work in 2017.

Decommissioning and sale

Under the 2010 Strategic Defence and Security Review Ocean's withdrawal was announced; she was decommissioned at HMNB Devonport on 27 March 2018, with the Queen attending the ceremony. She was subsequently sold to Brazil for about £84 million and commissioned into the Brazilian Navy as Atlântico (A140), arriving in Brazil in August 2018 and becoming the fleet's flagship.
