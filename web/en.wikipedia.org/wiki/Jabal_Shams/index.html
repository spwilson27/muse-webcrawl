Jabal Shams

Elevation: approx. 3,009 m (9,872 ft)
Location: Al Hajar Mountains, Oman
Parent range: Al Hajar

Jabal Shams (Arabic: جبل شمس, "Mountain of the Sun") is the highest mountain in Oman and in the Arabian Peninsula's Hajar range, rising to about 3,009 metres (9,872 ft) above sea level. It forms part of the Al Jabal Al Akhdar (Green Mountain) region of the Al Hajar Mountains in the Ad Dakhiliyah Governorate of northeastern Oman.

Geography

The mountain's summit area hosts a military radar installation and is closed to the public; the highest accessible point for visitors is a nearby secondary summit. Jabal Shams overlooks Wadi Ghul, a deep canyon often described as the "Grand Canyon of Arabia", with cliffs dropping roughly a thousand metres. The popular "Balcony Walk" trail runs along the canyon rim to the abandoned village of Sap Bani Khamis.

Climate and tourism

Because of its altitude, Jabal Shams enjoys much cooler temperatures than the Omani lowlands, with winter nights that can fall below freezing and occasional snow. Resorts and camps near the rim cater to hikers and sightseers, and the mountain is a notable stop on tourist routes through the Al Hajar range, reached by graded and partly paved roads from the town of Al Hamra.
