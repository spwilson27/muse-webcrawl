2009 L'Aquila earthquake

Date6 April 2009
LocationL'Aquila, Abruzzo, Italy
Magnitude6.3 M
Deaths308

The 2009 L'Aquila earthquake occurred in the Abruzzo region of central Italy on 6 April 2009, with a moment magnitude of 6.3. Its epicentre was near the city of L'Aquila, whose historic centre was devastated.

Damage and casualties

The quake struck at 03:32 local time, killing 308 people, injuring more than 1,500, and leaving tens of thousands homeless. Medieval buildings, churches, and a student dormitory collapsed in L'Aquila; the medieval village of Onna was almost entirely destroyed.

Aftermath

Reconstruction of L'Aquila proceeded slowly over many years amid corruption investigations. In a controversial trial, six scientists and an official were convicted of manslaughter in 2012 for allegedly misleading risk communication before the quake; the scientists were acquitted on appeal and the acquittals upheld in 2015.
