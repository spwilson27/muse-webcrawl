Ryan Daniel Montgomery (born 5 July 1977), known professionally as Royce da 5'9", is an American rapper from Detroit, Michigan. He is known for his long association with Eminem, with whom he forms the duo Bad Meets Evil, and as a member of the supergroup Slaughterhouse and of PRhyme with DJ Premier.

Career

Royce first gained attention through guest appearances alongside Eminem in the late 1990s, including the original "Renegade". His debut album Rock City (2002) was followed by Death Is Certain (2004) and Independent's Day (2005). After a period of personal difficulties, documented on the Bar Exam mixtapes, he joined Slaughterhouse, whose major-label album Welcome to: Our House appeared in 2012. Bad Meets Evil's Hell: The Sequel (2011) debuted at number one in the United States.

Later work

With DJ Premier he released PRhyme (2014) and PRhyme 2 (2018). His solo albums Layers (2016), Book of Ryan (2018) and The Allegory (2020) were widely praised; The Allegory, which he also produced, was nominated for the Grammy Award for Best Rap Album. Royce is frequently cited among the most technically accomplished lyricists of his generation.
