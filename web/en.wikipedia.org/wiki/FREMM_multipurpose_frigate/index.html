FREMM multipurpose frigate

The FREMM (Frégate Européenne Multi-Mission in French; Fregata Europea Multi-Missione in Italian) is a class of multi-purpose frigates designed jointly by France and Italy, built by Naval Group and Fincantieri. It is one of the largest European naval shipbuilding programmes, and variants have been exported widely; the United States Navy's Constellation-class frigate is derived from the Italian FREMM design.

Variants

France built anti-submarine warfare (ASW) and land-attack variants (the Aquitaine class), while Italy built general-purpose and ASW variants (the Bergamini class). The ships displace about 6,000–6,700 tonnes, carry Aster surface-to-air missiles in Sylver vertical launchers, a naval gun of 76 mm or 127 mm, torpedoes, anti-ship missiles and a helicopter.

Operators and exports

Beyond France and Italy, FREMM frigates serve with Morocco and Egypt, and the design was selected by Indonesia and adapted for the U.S. Navy's Constellation class. Greece has also pursued acquiring FREMM ships. The programme emphasised commonality while allowing each navy to fit national combat systems and weapons.
