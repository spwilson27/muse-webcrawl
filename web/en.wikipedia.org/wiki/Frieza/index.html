Frieza (Japanese: フリーザ, Hepburn: Furīza) is a fictional character and one of the main antagonists of the Dragon Ball manga and anime franchise, created by Akira Toriyama. A galactic tyrant of the Frieza Race, he rules an interstellar empire of conquered planets, buying and selling worlds through the Frieza Force and employing the Saiyans until he destroyed their home planet, Vegeta, fearing the legend of the Super Saiyan.

Role in the series

Frieza serves as the principal villain of the Namek/Frieza Saga of Dragon Ball Z, seeking the Dragon Balls of planet Namek to wish for immortality. His cruelty — including the destruction of Namek and the murder of Krillin — triggers Goku's first transformation into a Super Saiyan, and the ensuing battle is one of the longest and most famous in shōnen anime. Defeated, Frieza is rebuilt as a cyborg ("Mecha Frieza") but is killed by the time-travelling Trunks.

Frieza returns in the 2015 film Dragon Ball Z: Resurrection 'F' and in Dragon Ball Super, revived by his subordinates and achieving a new "Golden Frieza" form. He later fights alongside Universe 7 in the Tournament of Power and continues as a recurring antagonist, eventually attaining the "Black Frieza" form in the manga.

Creation and reception

Toriyama modelled Frieza's appearance partly on creatures from his nightmares and his polite, soft-spoken manner on real-estate speculators, whom he considered "the worst kind of people". Frieza is consistently ranked among the greatest villains in anime, and Ryūsei Nakao's Japanese performance is particularly acclaimed.
