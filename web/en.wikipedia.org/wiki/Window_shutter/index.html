Window shutter

A window shutter is a solid and stable window covering usually consisting of a frame of vertical stiles and horizontal rails (top, centre and bottom) within which may be set louvres—fixed or operable, horizontal or vertical—or solid panels, fabric, glass or other items.

Interior and exterior shutters

Exterior shutters were historically functional, protecting windows from storms, weather and intruders, and providing shade in hot climates; many are now purely decorative. Interior shutters, including "plantation shutters" with wide louvres common in the southern United States, serve for light control, privacy and insulation. Materials include wood, vinyl, composite and aluminium.
