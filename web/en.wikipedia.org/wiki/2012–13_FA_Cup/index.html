2012–13 FA Cup

The 2012–13 FA Cup (known as the FA Cup with Budweiser for sponsorship reasons) was the 132nd season of the FA Cup, the main domestic cup competition in English football. A total of 758 clubs were accepted into the tournament. The competition was won by Wigan Athletic, who defeated Manchester City 1–0 in the final at Wembley Stadium on 11 May 2013, one of the biggest upsets in the competition's history.

2012–13 FA Cup

Country: England, Wales
Teams: 758
Champions: Wigan Athletic (1st title)
Runners-up: Manchester City
Matches played: 156
Top scorer: Danny Hylton (8 goals)

Final

Wigan, managed by Roberto Martínez, were fighting relegation from the Premier League, while Manchester City were the holders of the Premier League title race's runners-up spot and heavy favourites. The match remained goalless until stoppage time, when substitute Ben Watson headed in a corner from Shaun Maloney in the 91st minute to give Wigan their first major trophy. City defender Pablo Zabaleta had been sent off late in the game, becoming only the third player to be dismissed in an FA Cup final.

Aftermath

Three days after the final, Wigan were relegated from the Premier League after losing to Arsenal, becoming the first club to win the FA Cup and be relegated in the same season. As cup winners they qualified for the group stage of the 2013–14 UEFA Europa League.
