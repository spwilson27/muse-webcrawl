List of cities in Japan

A city (市, shi) in Japan is a local administrative unit with a population of at least 50,000, of which at least 60% of households are in a central urban area. Japan has around 790 cities. Tokyo's 23 special wards are administered as separate municipalities but are not formally classed as a single city; Tokyo Metropolis (Tokyo-to) is a prefecture.

Tokyo is the center of the world's largest metropolitan economy.

Designated cities

Cities with over 500,000 inhabitants may be designated by government ordinance (seirei shitei toshi), gaining expanded administrative powers. There are 20 designated cities, including Yokohama, Osaka, Nagoya, Sapporo, Fukuoka, Kobe, Kyoto, and Hiroshima. Below them rank core cities (chūkakushi) and special cities.

History

The modern city system dates to the Local Autonomy Law of 1947. Waves of municipal mergers, notably the "Great Heisei Mergers" of the 2000s, reduced Japan's municipalities from over 3,200 to about 1,700.
