Recaíto

Recaíto is a Puerto Rican cooking base and seasoning sauce made from puréed fresh herbs and vegetables. Bright green in color, it is closely related to — and often used interchangeably with — sofrito, though recaíto traditionally contains no tomato or pork products. It forms the flavor foundation of many Puerto Rican dishes.

Ingredients

The essential ingredients are culantro (recao, or sawtooth coriander), cilantro, cubanelle or ají dulce sweet peppers, onion, and garlic, sometimes with green bell pepper and olive oil. The ingredients are blended into a coarse purée, which can be used fresh, refrigerated, or frozen in portions (commonly in ice cube trays) for later use.

Use

Recaíto is typically sautéed in oil or lard — often annatto oil — at the start of cooking to season rice dishes such as arroz con gandules, stews (guisos), beans, soups, and mofongo fillings. Together with sofrito and adobo, it is one of the pillars of Puerto Rican criollo cooking.
