The OICA (French: Organisation Internationale des Constructeurs d'Automobiles, "International Organization of Motor Vehicle Manufacturers") is a federation of automobile manufacturer associations founded in Paris in 1919. It brings together national trade associations representing the motor industry in countries around the world.

Activities

The OICA coordinates the international harmonisation of motor vehicle regulations, working closely with the United Nations World Forum for Harmonization of Vehicle Regulations (WP.29) in Geneva. It represents the global auto industry in discussions on safety, emissions and environmental standards, and publishes worldwide statistics on vehicle production and sales. The organisation also coordinates the calendar and accreditation of major international motor shows, including those held in Paris, Geneva, Frankfurt (IAA), Detroit and Tokyo.
