The National Music Publishers' Association (NMPA) is a trade association for the American music publishing industry. Founded in 1917, it represents music publishers and songwriters before legislative, regulatory and judicial bodies, and works to protect copyright interests across the music business.

Activities

The NMPA advocates on copyright legislation, licenses works through its affiliated Harry Fox Agency (founded in 1927), and pursues litigation against unlicensed uses of music, including landmark actions against file-sharing services, karaoke companies and, more recently, digital platforms and generative-AI firms.

Certifications

Since 1958 the NMPA has sponsored the gold and platinum single certification programme later administered with the Recording Industry Association of America, honouring recordings that reach major sales milestones.

Leadership

The association is led by a president and chief executive officer and a board drawn from major and independent publishers. Since 2005 its president and CEO has been David Israelite, a former U.S. Department of Justice official.
