Aorta

Aorta
Source: Left ventricle
Branches: Coronary, brachiocephalic, carotid, subclavian, abdominal branches
Precursor: Truncus arteriosus

The aorta is the largest artery in the human body, originating from the left ventricle of the heart and distributing oxygenated blood to systemic circulation.

Structure

Divided into ascending aorta, aortic arch, descending thoracic aorta, and abdominal aorta, ending at bifurcation into common iliac arteries at L4. Wall layers: intima, media, adventitia.

Function and disease

Elastic recoil (Windkessel effect) smooths pulsatile flow. Diseases include aortic aneurysm, aortic rupture, dissection, coarctation, and aortitis.
