Air Force Materiel Command (AFMC) is a major command of the United States Air Force, created on 1 July 1992 through the merger of Air Force Systems Command and Air Force Logistics Command. Headquartered at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio, it conducts research, development, test and evaluation, and provides acquisition management and logistics support for Air Force weapon systems.

Mission

AFMC's mission is to power the world's greatest Air Force through sustaining and producing affordable, capable and trusted Air and Space Forces. It manages installation support, supplies and maintains aircraft and missiles, and oversees the Air Force's science and technology enterprise.

Organization

The command includes the Air Force Research Laboratory, the Air Force Life Cycle Management Center, the Air Force Sustainment Center, the Air Force Test Center, the Air Force Nuclear Weapons Center, and the Air Force Installation and Mission Support Center, among other units.

History

Its lineage traces to the Materiel Division of the Army Air Corps and the post-war research and development and logistics commands whose merger in 1992 unified weapon system acquisition and sustainment under a single command.
