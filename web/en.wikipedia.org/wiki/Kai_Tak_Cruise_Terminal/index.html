Kai Tak Cruise Terminal

Location33 Shing Fung Road, Kowloon, Hong Kong
Opened12 June 2013
ArchitectFoster + Partners
Berths2

The Kai Tak Cruise Terminal is a cruise ship terminal in Hong Kong, built on the southern tip of the former runway of the old Kai Tak Airport at Kowloon Bay. Designed by Foster + Partners, it opened in June 2013 and can berth two large cruise ships simultaneously, including the largest Oasis-class vessels.

Development

After Kai Tak Airport closed in 1998, its runway area was earmarked for redevelopment as the Kai Tak Development. With the Ocean Terminal in Tsim Sha Tsui unable to berth the newest generation of megaships, the Hong Kong government chose the former runway tip for a purpose-built terminal. Construction began in 2010.

Facilities

The terminal building has a large rooftop park open to the public, with views across Victoria Harbour. Its processing hall can handle several thousand passengers per hour. The building incorporates energy-saving features including district cooling and extensive natural lighting.

The first ship to berth was the Mariner of the Seas in June 2013. The terminal has since hosted homeported ships of lines such as Royal Caribbean and Dream Cruises, though operations were disrupted during the COVID-19 pandemic, when the building was temporarily used for other purposes.
