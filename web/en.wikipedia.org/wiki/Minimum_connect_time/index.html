Minimum connect time

The minimum connect time (MCT) is the shortest time period required for a passenger to make a connection between an arriving flight and a departing flight at an airport. It is used by airlines, airports, and reservation systems when constructing bookable itineraries, and it varies by airport, terminal, airline combination, and whether the connection is domestic or international.

How MCT is determined

Minimum connect times are normally set by individual airports in cooperation with the airlines that operate there, and they are published in airline reservation systems and the Official Airline Guide. Factors include the size and layout of the terminal complex, walking distances, the need to pass through security or passport control, baggage transfer times, and whether passengers must change terminals or be re-checked at a landside transfer desk.

Typical values

At small airports an MCT may be as short as 20–30 minutes for domestic-to-domestic connections. At large hub airports, international connections commonly require 60 minutes or more, and some airport–airline combinations publish minimum connect times of two hours or longer when a terminal change or immigration processing is involved. If a proposed itinerary falls below the applicable MCT, reservation systems will not offer it as a single booking, and the airline is generally not responsible for rebooking passengers who miss self-constructed connections below MCT.
