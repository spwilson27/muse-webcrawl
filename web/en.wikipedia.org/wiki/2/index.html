2 (two) is a number, numeral and digit. It is the natural number following 1 and preceding 3. It is the smallest and the only even prime number.

Mathematics

Two is the first prime number and the only even one, which is why it is sometimes jokingly called "the oddest prime". An integer is called even if divisible by 2. Two is the base of the binary number system, which is widely used in computing. In Euclidean geometry, two points determine a unique line. The number two is also the atomic number of helium, and powers of two recur throughout mathematics and computer science.

Evolution of the glyph

The modern digit 2 derives from the Brahmi numeral, which was written with two horizontal strokes. Through the Gupta, Nagari and Arabic traditions the strokes were joined into a single curved character, giving the form used today.
