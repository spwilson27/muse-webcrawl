Concord (entertainment company)

Concord is an American independent music and entertainment company headquartered in Nashville, Tennessee, with additional offices in Los Angeles, New York, London, Berlin, and Miami. Founded in 1973 as Concord Records by Carl Jefferson, the company grew from a jazz label into one of the world's largest independent music companies, spanning recorded music, music publishing, and theatrical licensing.

Divisions

Concord Label Group oversees labels including Concord Records, Fantasy Records, Rounder Records, Fearless Records, Craft Recordings, and the classical and kids' divisions. Concord Music Publishing administers a large catalogue of songs and represents songwriters and catalogues; Concord Theatricals, formed in 2018 from Samuel French and R&H Theatricals, licenses plays and musicals, including the Rodgers & Hammerstein catalogue.

Growth

The company expanded through numerous acquisitions, including the Bicycle Music Company, Razor & Tie, Savoy Label Group, Independiente, and the publishing catalogue of Imagem Music. In 2022 Concord acquired the catalogue of Phil Collins and Genesis band members, and it has repeatedly been reported among bidders for major music rights catalogues.
