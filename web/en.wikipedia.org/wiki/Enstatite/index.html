Enstatite

CategorySilicate mineral (pyroxene)
FormulaMgSiO3
Crystal systemOrthorhombic
Hardness5–6 (Mohs)
ColorWhite, grey, greenish, brown

Enstatite is a mineral; the magnesium endmember of the pyroxene silicate mineral series enstatite (MgSiO3) – ferrosilite (FeSiO3). The magnesium-rich members of the solid solution series are common rock-forming minerals found in igneous and metamorphic rocks.

Properties

The pure magnesium and iron endmembers are known as enstatite and ferrosilite. Enstatite crystals are usually white, grey, greenish or brown in colour and have a vitreous to pearly lustre. When weathered, the mineral can take on a bronze-like submetallic lustre, a variety known as bronzite.

Occurrence

Enstatite is a common constituent of mafic and ultramafic igneous rocks such as peridotite and pyroxenite, and it also occurs in meteorites, including the enstatite chondrites and enstatite achondrites, which are named after it. It is also found in some metamorphic rocks and in the Earth's upper mantle.
