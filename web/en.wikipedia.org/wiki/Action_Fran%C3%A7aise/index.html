Action Française

Action française is a French far-right monarchist and nationalist political movement founded in 1899 in the wake of the Dreyfus Affair. Its associated newspaper, L'Action française, directed by Charles Maurras and Léon Daudet, became the principal organ of integral nationalism in France.

Maurras developed the doctrine he called nationalisme intégral, favouring a hereditary monarchy, decentralisation, Catholicism as a social bond, and the exclusion of what he termed 'anti-France'. The movement supported the Vichy regime in 1940; the paper was banned after the Liberation and Maurras was imprisoned for collaboration.

The movement survived in reduced form after the war and continues to exist as a small monarchist organisation; in 2023 the French government moved to dissolve affiliated violent groups such as the GUD milieu around it, while Action française itself retained its publishing activity.
