Indiana Senate

Type: Upper house of the Indiana General Assembly

Seats: 50

Term length: 4 years

Meeting place: Indiana Statehouse, Indianapolis

The Indiana Senate is the upper house of the Indiana General Assembly, the state legislature of Indiana. It works alongside the Indiana House of Representatives.

Composition

The Senate consists of 50 members elected from single-member districts. Senators serve staggered four-year terms, with half the seats up for election every two years. Candidates must be at least 25 years old and have lived in Indiana for at least two years.

Leadership

The Lieutenant Governor of Indiana serves as President of the Senate but votes only to break ties. Day-to-day leadership rests with the President pro tempore and the majority and minority leaders.

Powers

Like other state senates, the Indiana Senate shares lawmaking power with the House and confirms certain gubernatorial appointments.
