Concert promotion

Concert promotion is the business of organising, financing, marketing, and presenting live music events. A concert promoter takes on the financial risk of a show: renting the venue, hiring the artist through a booking agent, arranging production, security, and staffing, advertising the event, and selling tickets, in return for a share of the proceeds.

How it works

Promoters typically pay the artist a guaranteed fee or a percentage of ticket revenue ("versus" deals combine both), and cover show costs from gross receipts. Profit depends on accurate budgeting and ticket sales; a poorly attended show can leave the promoter with substantial losses. Deals may also divide ancillary income such as merchandise.

Industry structure

Historically, independent regional promoters booked tours market by market. Since the 1990s the industry has consolidated: companies such as Live Nation and AEG Presents promote tours nationally and internationally and also own or operate venues and ticketing (notably Ticketmaster, which merged with Live Nation in 2010). Critics have raised concerns about competition, ticket prices, and service fees, prompting regulatory scrutiny in several countries.
