Glacier National Park is a national park in the Selkirk and Purcell mountains of British Columbia, Canada, established in 1886 — one of Canada's oldest national parks. It contains Rogers Pass, the route of the Canadian Pacific Railway and the Trans-Canada Highway through the Selkirks.

Geography and climate

The park covers 1,349 km² of rugged, heavily glaciated terrain with some of the heaviest snowfall in Canada; more than 400 glaciers lie within its boundaries. Its wet interior rainforest of western redcedar and hemlock is globally rare habitat.

Avalanche control

Rogers Pass is the site of the world's largest mobile avalanche control programme, run by Parks Canada with the Canadian Armed Forces, which fire artillery to trigger controlled slides and keep the highway and railway open.
