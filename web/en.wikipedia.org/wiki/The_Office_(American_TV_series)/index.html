The Office is an American mockumentary sitcom television series that depicts the everyday work lives of office employees at the Scranton, Pennsylvania, branch of the fictional Dunder Mifflin Paper Company. It aired on NBC from March 24, 2005, to May 16, 2013, spanning nine seasons and 201 episodes. It is an adaptation of the British series of the same name created by Ricky Gervais and Stephen Merchant, developed for American television by Greg Daniels.

Premise and cast

The series is filmed in a single-camera setup without a studio audience or laugh track, as if a documentary crew were following regional manager Michael Scott (Steve Carell) and his staff, including salesman Jim Halpert (John Krasinski), receptionist Pam Beesly (Jenna Fischer), and assistant regional manager Dwight Schrute (Rainn Wilson). Carell left after the seventh season; the series continued with a rotating set of managers before concluding with a widely praised ninth season and finale.

Reception and legacy

The show won a Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Comedy Series (2006) and a Peabody Award. In the streaming era it became one of the most-watched series in the world — the most-streamed show in the United States in 2020 — and remains a durable cultural touchstone, frequently quoted and memed.
