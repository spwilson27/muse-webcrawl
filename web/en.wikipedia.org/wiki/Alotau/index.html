Alotau is the capital of Milne Bay Province in Papua New Guinea, located on the northern shore of Milne Bay in the southeastern tip of the island of New Guinea. It is a port town and the gateway to the province's many islands.

History

Alotau became the provincial capital in 1968, replacing Samarai. During the Second World War the Milne Bay area was the scene of the Battle of Milne Bay (1942), in which Australian and American forces defeated a Japanese landing — the first major defeat of Japanese land forces in the Pacific war.

Geography and economy

The town lies at the eastern end of the Papua New Guinea mainland highway system and is served by Gurney Airport. Its economy rests on copra, palm oil, fishing, small-scale trade, and tourism, including cruise ship visits and the annual Kenu and Kundu Festival celebrating local canoe and drum culture.
