Kentucky Wildcats

The Kentucky Wildcats are the men's and women's intercollegiate athletic teams representing the University of Kentucky in Lexington, Kentucky. The Wildcats compete in the Southeastern Conference (SEC) and field teams in sports including football, basketball, baseball, and softball. The programme is most famous for its men's basketball team, one of the most successful in college basketball history.

Men's basketball

Kentucky men's basketball has won eight NCAA national championships (1948, 1949, 1951, 1958, 1978, 1996, 1998, and 2012) and ranks among the all-time leaders in victories. Legendary coaches include Adolph Rupp and John Calipari. The team plays at Rupp Arena, one of the largest arenas in the United States.

Football

The Wildcats football team plays at Kroger Field and competes in the SEC Eastern Division. The programme's notable moments include the 1950 national championship season under Bear Bryant and victories in the Sugar Bowl and Cotton Bowl.
