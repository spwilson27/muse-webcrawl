Wolong National Nature Reserve

Wolong National Nature Reserve is a protected area located in Wenchuan County, in the Ngawa Tibetan and Qiang Autonomous Prefecture of Sichuan Province, China. Established in 1963, the reserve covers about 2,000 square kilometres (770 sq mi) in the Qionglai Mountains and is best known as a centre for the protection and captive breeding of the giant panda.

Giant pandas

The reserve is home to a wild population of giant pandas as well as the China Conservation and Research Center for the Giant Panda, founded in 1980 with the support of WWF. Wolong pioneered captive breeding techniques for pandas and has supplied pandas for zoos around the world through loan agreements. Following damage in the 2008 Sichuan earthquake, the breeding centre's operations were largely relocated to the Bifengxia base near Ya'an, with a new facility later developed at Shenshuping in the reserve.

Biodiversity

Beyond pandas, the reserve protects red pandas, snow leopards, golden monkeys and a rich flora across an altitude range from about 1,200 to 6,250 metres. It forms part of the Sichuan Giant Panda Sanctuaries UNESCO World Heritage Site, inscribed in 2006.
