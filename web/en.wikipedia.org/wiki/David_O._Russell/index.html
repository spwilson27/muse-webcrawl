David Owen Russell (born August 20, 1958) is an American film director, screenwriter, and producer. His early work includes the black comedy Spanking the Monkey (1994), the comedy Flirting with Disaster (1996), the Gulf War satire Three Kings (1999), and the existential comedy I Heart Huckabees (2004).

Russell received widespread acclaim in the 2010s for a run of films including the boxing drama The Fighter (2010), the romantic comedy-drama Silver Linings Playbook (2012), and the crime comedy American Hustle (2013). His films have earned numerous Academy Award nominations, and he is known for an improvisational, actor-driven directing style and for frequent collaborations with actors such as Christian Bale, Jennifer Lawrence, Bradley Cooper, Amy Adams, and Robert De Niro.

Early life and education

Russell was born in New York City to Maria (née Muzio), who worked for Macmillan Publishers, and Bernard Russell, a vice president at Simon & Schuster. He grew up in Larchmont, New York, and graduated from Amherst College in 1981 with a degree in English and political science. Before filmmaking, he worked as a community organizer and taught literacy in Nicaragua.

Career

Russell's feature debut, Spanking the Monkey, won the Audience Award at the Sundance Film Festival. Three Kings, starring George Clooney, Mark Wahlberg, and Ice Cube, was a critical success. After the troubled production of I Heart Huckabees, Russell returned with The Fighter, which won Academy Awards for Christian Bale and Melissa Leo. Silver Linings Playbook became one of the few films to receive Oscar nominations in all four acting categories, and Jennifer Lawrence won Best Actress. American Hustle received ten Academy Award nominations. His later films include Joy (2015) and Amsterdam (2022).

Directing style and controversies

Russell's sets have been noted for their intense, chaotic energy, and he has been involved in widely reported on-set altercations, including with George Clooney during Three Kings and with Lily Tomlin during I Heart Huckabees. He has also been praised by many collaborators for eliciting spontaneous, emotionally raw performances.
