Aluminium nitride

Aluminium nitride
Formula: AlN
Molar mass: 40.989 g/mol
Appearance: white to pale yellow solid
Density: 3.255 g/cm3
Melting point: 2,200 °C (decomposes)

Aluminium nitride (AlN) is a solid nitride of aluminium. It has a high thermal conductivity of up to 321 W/(m·K) and is an electrical insulator. Its wurtzite phase (w-AlN) has a band gap of about 6 eV at room temperature, which gives it potential applications in deep ultraviolet optoelectronics.

Properties

Aluminium nitride is a covalent material with a hexagonal wurtzite crystal structure. It is notable for combining high thermal conductivity with strong electrical insulation, low thermal expansion similar to silicon, and good resistance to molten metals. It hydrolyses slowly in water and aqueous acids, and oxidises in air above about 700 °C.

Applications

Because of its thermal properties, AlN is used as a substrate and heat sink material in power electronics and LED devices, as a component in surface acoustic wave sensors, and as a piezoelectric thin film in MEMS resonators and RF filters. It is also used in crucibles for handling molten metals and salts.

Synthesis

Aluminium nitride is synthesised by the carbothermal reduction of aluminium oxide in the presence of nitrogen gas or ammonia, or by direct nitridation of aluminium powder at high temperature.
