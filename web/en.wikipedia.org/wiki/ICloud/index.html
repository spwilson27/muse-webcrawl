iCloud

DeveloperApple Inc.
Launched12 October 2011
TypeCloud storage and computing service

iCloud is a cloud computing and storage service from Apple Inc., launched on 12 October 2011 as the successor to MobileMe. It synchronises data — including photos, files, device backups, mail, contacts, calendars and passwords — across a user's iPhone, iPad, Mac and other Apple devices, and provides services such as Find My, iCloud Drive, iCloud Photos and iCloud Mail.

Features

Every Apple account includes 5 GB of free storage, with paid iCloud+ tiers adding larger storage, Private Relay, Hide My Email and custom mail domains. Features include iCloud Keychain for credentials, Family Sharing, device backup and restore, and end-to-end encryption for much data, extended by the optional Advanced Data Protection introduced in 2022. In 2014, a high-profile incident in which attackers obtained celebrities' photos from accounts via targeted password attacks prompted Apple to expand two-factor authentication and security notifications.
