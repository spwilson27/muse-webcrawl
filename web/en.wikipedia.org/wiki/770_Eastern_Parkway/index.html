770 Eastern Parkway, known simply as "770", is the world headquarters of the Chabad-Lubavitch Hasidic movement, located in the Crown Heights neighbourhood of Brooklyn, New York City. The Gothic Revival red-brick building became the centre of the movement under the sixth Rebbe, Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn, who purchased it in 1940, and his successor, Menachem Mendel Schneerson, the seventh Rebbe.

Significance

For Lubavitch Hasidim, 770 holds immense spiritual significance as the Rebbe's synagogue, study hall and office, from which the movement's global network of emissaries (shluchim) was directed. The building has been replicated in detail at Chabad centres around the world — copies, known as "770" replicas, exist in Israel, Australia, and numerous other countries. After the Rebbe's death in 1994, control of the synagogue became the subject of a long-running legal dispute between factions of the movement.

Building

The structure houses a large main synagogue (the "shul") that is filled with worshippers and students, particularly during the High Holidays, when thousands gather. An underground expansion houses additional study and prayer space.
