Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport

Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport (IATA: DFW) is the primary international airport serving the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex in Texas, United States. Opened in 1974, it is among the busiest airports in the world by passenger traffic and aircraft movements, and it is the largest hub for American Airlines.

History

DFW was conceived in the 1960s as a joint project of Dallas and Fort Worth after the two cities failed to agree on separate facilities; the airport sits on land in the cities of Grapevine, Irving, Euless and Coppell. It opened in 1974 with four semicircular terminals and has since added the international Terminal D (2005) and the Skylink automated people mover connecting all terminals.

Operations

With seven runways and service to more than 250 destinations, DFW handles over 70 million passengers in a typical recent year. American Airlines accounts for the large majority of its traffic, making DFW one of the world's largest single-airline hubs. The airport is also a significant cargo facility and has its own ZIP code and postal designation.
