Maharashtra (Marathi: महाराष्ट्र, romanized: Mahārāṣṭra) is a state in the western peninsular region of India, occupying a substantial portion of the Deccan Plateau. It is the second-most populous state and the third-largest state by area in India, with an area of 307,713 km2 (118,809 sq mi). Maharashtra is bordered by the Arabian Sea to the west, the states of Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh to the north, Chhattisgarh to the east, Karnataka to the south, and Telangana and Goa to the southwest. Its capital, Mumbai, is India's financial and commercial centre and the centre of the Bollywood film industry.

History

The region was ruled successively by the Satavahanas, Chalukyas, Rashtrakutas, and the Deccan sultanates, and the Maratha Empire founded by Shivaji in the 17th century made Maharashtra the nucleus of the last major Indian power to resist British expansion. Under British rule the area was divided among the Bombay Presidency and princely states. After independence, the bilingual Bombay State was reorganized along linguistic lines, and Maharashtra was created on 1 May 1960 with Marathi as its official language; the date is celebrated as Maharashtra Day.

Geography

The state's geography divides into the narrow Konkan coastal strip, the Western Ghats (Sahyadri) mountain range, and the Deccan Plateau to the east. Major rivers include the Godavari and Krishna, which rise in the Ghats and flow eastward. The climate is tropical monsoon, and large tracts of the interior are rain-shadow country prone to drought.

Economy

Maharashtra is India's richest state by gross state domestic product, contributing an outsized share of the national industrial output, exports, and tax revenue. Mumbai hosts the Bombay Stock Exchange, the Reserve Bank of India, and the headquarters of most major Indian banks and corporations. Pune is a centre for automobiles and information technology, while agriculture — including sugarcane, cotton, and oranges from Vidarbha — remains a major employer. The state is a leader in electricity generation, film production, and higher education.

Culture

Marathi literature and theatre have long traditions, and the Wari pilgrimage to Pandharpur, Ganesh Chaturthi celebrations popularized by Bal Gangadhar Tilak, and the cave temples of Ajanta and Ellora (UNESCO World Heritage Sites) are major cultural landmarks.
