Tobias Lütke

Tobias Lütke
Born: 1981, Koblenz, West Germany
Occupation: Entrepreneur, programmer
Known for: Co-founder and CEO of Shopify

Tobias "Tobi" Lütke (born 1981) is a German-Canadian entrepreneur and programmer, best known as the co-founder and chief executive officer of Shopify, the e-commerce platform headquartered in Ottawa, Canada.

Career

Lütke taught himself to program as a child in Koblenz, Germany. After moving to Canada, he and partners set out in 2004 to build an online snowboard shop; dissatisfied with existing software, Lütke built a new e-commerce framework using Ruby on Rails, which became Shopify, launched in 2006. He contributed significantly to the Ruby on Rails ecosystem, including the Liquid template language.

Recognition

Under Lütke, Shopify grew into one of Canada's most valuable companies. He has been recognised with honours including being named to the Order of Canada, and is known for his advocacy of immigration and technology talent in Canada.
