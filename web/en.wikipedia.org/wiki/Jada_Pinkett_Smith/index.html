Jada Koren Pinkett Smith (born September 18, 1971) is an American actress, talk show host, and businesswoman. She is known for her roles in films such as Menace II Society (1993), Set It Off (1996), The Nutty Professor (1996), and as Niobe in The Matrix franchise. She co-hosted the Facebook Watch talk show Red Table Talk (2018–2023) alongside her daughter Willow Smith and her mother Adrienne Banfield-Norris.

Early life

Pinkett Smith was born in Baltimore, Maryland, the daughter of Adrienne Banfield, a nurse, and Robsol Pinkett Jr., who ran a construction company. She attended the Baltimore School for the Arts, where she majored in dance and theatre and befriended classmate Tupac Shakur. She later studied at the North Carolina School of the Arts but left before graduating to pursue acting in Los Angeles.

Career

She began her acting career in 1990 with a guest appearance on True Colors and rose to prominence with a role on the sitcom A Different World (1991–1993). Her film breakthrough came with the Hughes brothers' Menace II Society (1993). She starred opposite Eddie Murphy in The Nutty Professor (1996) and appeared in Scream 2 (1997). In 2003 she played Niobe in The Matrix Reloaded and The Matrix Revolutions, reprising the role in The Matrix Resurrections (2021). She voiced Gloria the hippopotamus in the Madagascar animated films and played Fish Mooney on the television series Gotham (2014–2017). She also fronted the nu-metal band Wicked Wisdom as lead singer under the name Jada Koren.

Personal life and ventures

Pinkett Smith married actor Will Smith in 1997; they have two children, Jaden and Willow. The couple later revealed they had been separated since 2016. Together they founded the production company Westbrook Inc. and the Will & Jada Smith Family Foundation. Her memoir, Worthy, was published in 2023. At the 94th Academy Awards in 2022, her husband slapped presenter Chris Rock on stage after Rock joked about Pinkett Smith's shaved head, which she had attributed to alopecia areata, a condition she has spoken about publicly.
