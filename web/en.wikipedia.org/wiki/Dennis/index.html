Dennis

Dennis is a masculine given name derived from the Greek name Dionysios, meaning "follower of Dionysus", the god of wine. The name was popularised in the English-speaking world through Saint Denis, the third-century patron saint of France, and spread through forms such as Denis, Denys and Denise (the feminine form). It was common in medieval England and enjoyed renewed popularity in the 20th century, particularly in the United States, Germany and the Netherlands.

People with the name

  • Dennis Hopper (1936–2010), American actor and filmmaker
  • Dennis Quaid (born 1954), American actor
  • Dennis Rodman (born 1961), American basketball player
  • Dennis Ritchie (1941–2011), American computer scientist, co-creator of the C programming language and Unix
  • Dennis Wilson (1944–1983), American musician, drummer of the Beach Boys
  • Dennis Bergkamp (born 1969), Dutch footballer

Other uses

  • Dennis the Menace, name of a British comic strip character (in The Beano) and an unrelated American comic strip, both debuting in 1951
  • Hurricane Dennis (2005), a major Atlantic hurricane
  • Dennis, Massachusetts, a town on Cape Cod
