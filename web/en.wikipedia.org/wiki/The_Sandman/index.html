The Sandman

The Sandman is a comic book series written by Neil Gaiman and published by DC Comics under its Vertigo imprint. It ran for 75 issues from 1989 to 1996 and is widely regarded as one of the greatest graphic novels of all time, credited with bringing literary respectability to the comic medium.

Premise

The series follows Dream, also known as Morpheus, the anthropomorphic personification of dreams and one of the seven Endless. Captured in an occult ritual in 1916, Dream escapes decades later into a world much changed, and sets out to recover his stolen tools of power — a helm, a pouch of sand, and a ruby — before rebuilding his kingdom, the Dreaming, and confronting the consequences of his past.

Themes and storylines

Across arcs such as Preludes & Nocturnes, The Doll's House, Dream Country, Season of Mists, A Game of You, Brief Lives and The Kindly Ones, the series blends mythology, folklore, horror and history. Dream Country includes "A Midsummer Night's Dream", the only comic ever to win the World Fantasy Award for Best Short Fiction.

Adaptations

A television adaptation premiered on Netflix in 2022, with Tom Sturridge as Dream.
