Hilton Worldwide

Hilton Worldwide Holdings Inc. is an American multinational hospitality company that manages and franchises a broad portfolio of hotels, resorts and timeshare properties. Founded by Conrad Hilton in May 1919, the corporation is headquartered in Tysons, Virginia.

History

Conrad Hilton bought his first hotel, the Mobley in Cisco, Texas, in 1919. Hilton expanded across Texas and internationally, acquiring the Waldorf Astoria New York in 1949. Blackstone acquired Hilton in 2007 for $26 billion; it returned to public listing in 2013 in a major IPO.

Brands and loyalty

Hilton operates more than 20 brands from luxury (Waldorf Astoria, LXR, Conrad) to focused-service (Hampton, Tru). Its loyalty programme Hilton Honors has over 190 million members. Properties number more than 7,500 in over 120 countries.
