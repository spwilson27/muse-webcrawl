Tel Aviv District

The Tel Aviv District (Hebrew: מחוז תל אביב) is one of Israel's six administrative districts. Its capital is Tel Aviv-Yafo, and with about 1.4 million inhabitants it is the smallest but most densely populated district in the country. The district forms the core of the Gush Dan metropolitan area.

Cities and geography

Besides Tel Aviv-Yafo, the district includes the cities of Ramat Gan, Bnei Brak, Bat Yam, Holon, Givatayim, Herzliya, Ramat HaSharon, Kiryat Ono, and Or Yehuda. It stretches along the Mediterranean coast in central Israel, covering roughly 186 square kilometres, and is bordered by the Central District on three sides.

Economy

The district is Israel's economic and cultural hub, containing the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange, the headquarters of most Israeli banks and technology companies, and a large share of the country's "Silicon Wadi" high-tech industry.
