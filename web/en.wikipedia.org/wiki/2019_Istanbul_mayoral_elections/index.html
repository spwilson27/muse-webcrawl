2019 Istanbul mayoral elections

The 2019 Istanbul mayoral elections took place twice: an initial election on 31 March 2019, held as part of the Turkish local elections, and a repeat election on 23 June 2019 after the Supreme Electoral Council annulled the first result. Both were won by Ekrem İmamoğlu of the opposition Republican People's Party (CHP).

March election

In the March vote, İmamoğlu narrowly defeated Binali Yıldırım of the ruling Justice and Development Party (AKP) by roughly 13,000–14,000 votes out of more than 8 million cast, ending 25 years of Islamist-rooted party control of Istanbul. İmamoğlu took office on 17 April after recounts.

Annulment and re-run

The AKP appealed, alleging irregularities in the appointment of polling station officials. On 6 May 2019, the Supreme Electoral Council voted 7–4 to annul the Istanbul mayoral result and order a re-run — a decision widely criticised domestically and internationally. In the 23 June re-run, İmamoğlu won by a much larger margin of around 800,000 votes (about 54% to 45%), and took office as Mayor of Istanbul on 27 June.

Significance

The elections were seen as a major political turning point in Turkey, demonstrating the opposition's ability to win the country's largest city and economic hub, and the re-run result was interpreted as a voter backlash against the annulment.
