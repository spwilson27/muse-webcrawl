Pharrell Williams

Pharrell Lanscilo Williams (/fəˈrɛl/; born April 5, 1973) is an American singer, rapper, songwriter, record producer, and fashion designer. With Chad Hugo he formed the production duo the Neptunes, shaping the sound of 2000s pop and hip hop through hits for Jay-Z, Britney Spears, Nelly, and Snoop Dogg, and the band N.E.R.D. As a solo artist he is known for the albums In My Mind (2006) and Girl (2014) and the global number-one single "Happy" (2013). He has won 13 Grammy Awards and, since 2023, has served as men's creative director of Louis Vuitton.

Career

Williams met Chad Hugo at a summer camp for the arts in Virginia Beach; discovered by Teddy Riley, they wrote for Wreckx-N-Effect and Blackstreet before the Neptunes' breakthrough with Jay-Z's "Frontin'" and Nelly's "Hot in Herre". Their sparse, off-kilter production defined early-2000s chart music. With Shay Haley they formed N.E.R.D. (In Search Of…, 2001). Williams sang the hook on Snoop Dogg's "Drop It Like It's Hot" (2004), his first Billboard number one.[1]

In 2013 he achieved a rare triple success: Daft Punk's "Get Lucky" and Robin Thicke's "Blurred Lines", both featuring him, and "Happy", written for Despicable Me 2, which topped charts in over 20 countries and was Oscar-nominated. He was a coach on The Voice and narrated the 2018 film The Grinch. In fashion, he co-founded Billionaire Boys Club and Ice Cream (2003), collaborated with Adidas and Chanel, and in February 2023 succeeded Virgil Abloh as Louis Vuitton's men's creative director, debuting on the Pont Neuf in Paris.
