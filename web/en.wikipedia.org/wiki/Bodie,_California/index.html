Bodie, California

Bodie is a ghost town in the Bodie Hills east of the Sierra Nevada in Mono County, California. It is a genuine Wild West boomtown preserved as Bodie State Historic Park, a National Historic Landmark. Visitors walk the deserted streets where some 170 buildings remain in a state of "arrested decay".

History

Gold was discovered nearby in 1859 by the prospector W. S. Bodey. Following a rich strike in 1876, Bodie boomed: by 1879 it had a population of 7,000–10,000, dozens of saloons, a red-light district, and a reputation for lawlessness epitomised by the fictional "Bad Man from Bodie". Output from its mines totalled some $34 million before decline set in; the last mine closed in 1942.

Park

The California State Parks system took over the town in 1962. Interiors remain as they were left, stocked with goods. The park is reached by a partly unpaved road and is closed by snow for much of the year.
