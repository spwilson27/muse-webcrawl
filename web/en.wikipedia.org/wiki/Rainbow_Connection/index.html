"Rainbow Connection" is a song written by Paul Williams and Kenneth Ascher for the 1979 film The Muppet Movie. It is performed by Kermit the Frog, played by Jim Henson, at the opening of the film as he sits on a log in a swamp playing the banjo. The song became one of the most famous works associated with the Muppets and is often regarded as Kermit's signature song.

Background

Williams and Ascher were asked to write the songs for The Muppet Movie. The melody's gentle, questioning tone and its lyrics about "the lovers, the dreamers and me" made it an enduring standard. It reached number 25 on the Billboard Hot 100 in 1979 and was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Original Song.

Legacy

The song has been recorded by numerous artists, including Willie Nelson, the Carpenters, Sarah McLachlan, Jason Mraz, and Weezer with Hayley Williams. In 2020, the recording was selected for preservation in the United States National Recording Registry by the Library of Congress as being "culturally, historically, or aesthetically significant."
