Electric Daisy Carnival, commonly known as EDC, is an electronic dance music festival organised by Insomniac Events. Held annually in Las Vegas, Nevada, since 2011, and in editions around the world including Mexico City, Orlando, Tokyo, Shanghai and the United Kingdom, it is one of the largest electronic music festivals in North America. The flagship Las Vegas event runs dusk-to-dawn across three nights at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, drawing more than 160,000 attendees per day.

History

The first Electric Daisy Carnival was held in 1997 at the Shrine Exposition Hall in Los Angeles, growing out of Southern California's rave scene and Insomniac's earlier parties. The event moved through Los Angeles venues to the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum, where the 2010 edition drew enormous crowds. After controversy in Los Angeles, Insomniac moved the flagship festival to Las Vegas in 2011, where its overnight format, carnival rides, art installations, fireworks and themed stages — kineticFIELD, cosmicMEADOW, neonGARDEN, bassPOD and others — became its signature.

Culture

EDC promotes the rave ethos of PLUR (peace, love, unity, respect), encourages elaborate costumes and "kandi" bracelet exchanges, and describes attendees as "headliners". Its stages feature large-scale production with pyro, lasers and LED art, and its line-ups span house, techno, trance, dubstep, drum and bass and hard dance. The festival has also raised harm-reduction and safety measures after early incidents, and Insomniac markets the event as an immersive carnival experience rather than a conventional concert.
