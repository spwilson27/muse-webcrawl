Dornoch Firth

The Dornoch Firth is a firth on the east coast of Highland, Scotland, where the River Shin and other rivers meet the Moray Firth near Dornoch and Tain.

Geography

Designated a national scenic area and protected for its estuarine habitats, the firth is crossed by the Dornoch Firth Bridge, opened in 1991, which shortened the A9 route north.

Wildlife

Sandflats and saltmarsh support wintering wildfowl and waders, seals and otters; the area forms part of a Special Protection Area and Ramsar site.
