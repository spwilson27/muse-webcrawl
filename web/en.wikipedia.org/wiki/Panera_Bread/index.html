Panera Bread

Panera Bread is an American chain of bakery-café fast casual restaurants with locations throughout the United States and Canada. Founded in 1987, the company is known for its artisan breads, sandwiches, soups, salads, and baked goods, and it helped popularize the fast-casual dining segment. Its headquarters are in St. Louis, Missouri.

History

The company traces its history to the Saint Louis Bread Company, founded by Ken Rosenthal in Kirkwood, Missouri, in 1987. Au Bon Pain Co., led by Ron Shaich, acquired Saint Louis Bread in 1993 and rebranded many locations under the name Panera — a portmanteau suggesting "bread basket" (from Spanish pan, bread). In 1999, Shaich sold the Au Bon Pain division to focus entirely on Panera, which expanded rapidly through franchising and company stores in the 2000s. In 2017, Panera was acquired by JAB Holding Company for about $7.5 billion and taken private. Panera later became part of Panera Brands, alongside Caribou Coffee and Einstein Bros. Bagels.

Operations and initiatives

Panera pioneered features such as free Wi-Fi in cafés, calorie posting on menus, and a "clean ingredients" pledge removing artificial additives. Its Panera Cares community cafés experimented with pay-what-you-can pricing between 2010 and 2019. The company operates fresh dough facilities that supply its cafés daily, and runs a large loyalty program (MyPanera) and subscription offerings such as the Unlimited Sip Club for drinks.
