Record chart

A record chart, in the music industry, also called a music chart, is a ranking of recorded music according to certain criteria during a given period. Many different criteria are used in worldwide charts, often in combination. These include record sales, the amount of radio airplay, the number of downloads, and the amount of streaming activity.

History

Charts began with sheet-music bestseller lists and gained prominence with Billboard magazine's rankings in the 1940s and 1950s, including the Billboard Hot 100 (launched 1958). Charts in the streaming era blend sales with on-demand streams and video views.

Chart types

Charts may rank singles or albums, and may be general or genre-specific (e.g., country, R&B, dance). Notable chart providers include Billboard (US), the Official Charts Company (UK), and national bodies such as ARIA and Oricon.
