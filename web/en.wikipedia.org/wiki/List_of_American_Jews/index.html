List of American Jews

This is a list of American Jews: notable Jewish Americans, organized by field of activity. Jewish Americans have made significant contributions to American culture, science, politics, business, and the arts since the colonial period; the first Jewish settlers arrived in New Amsterdam in 1654. The list includes figures from entertainment, literature, science, politics, business, sports, and religion, drawn from all periods of American history.

Arts and entertainment

This section covers notable Jewish American actors, directors, musicians, comedians, writers, and artists. Examples spanning the twentieth and twenty-first centuries include figures from Hollywood's studio era through the present day, reflecting the central role of Jewish Americans in the American entertainment industry.

Science and academia

Jewish American scientists and scholars have been prominently represented among Nobel laureates and university faculties, particularly in physics, medicine, mathematics, and economics. This section lists notable academics and researchers.

Politics and public life

This section includes Jewish Americans who served in the United States Congress, the Cabinet, the federal judiciary (including the Supreme Court), and as governors and mayors, as well as noted activists and jurists.

Business and sports

These sections list notable Jewish American businesspeople and entrepreneurs, and athletes and coaches across major American sports.
