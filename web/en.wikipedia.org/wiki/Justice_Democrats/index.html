Justice Democrats

Political action committee
Formation: January 2017
Founders: Kyle Kulinski, Cenk Uygur, Saikat Chakrabarti, Corbin Trent
Position: Left-wing, progressive
Affiliation: Democratic Party (primary challengers)

Justice Democrats is an American progressive political action committee founded in January 2017 by former staffers of Bernie Sanders's 2016 presidential campaign together with Cenk Uygur of The Young Turks and commentator Kyle Kulinski. It recruits and supports progressive candidates running in Democratic Party primary elections, generally against more centrist incumbents, on a platform including Medicare for All, a Green New Deal, tuition-free public college and rejecting corporate PAC money.

Elections

In the 2018 midterms, Justice Democrats endorsed dozens of candidates; its most famous recruit, Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, defeated House Democratic Caucus chair Joe Crowley in a New York primary and went on to win the general election, alongside winners such as Ayanna Pressley. In 2020 the group helped elect challengers including Jamaal Bowman, Cori Bush and Marie Newman. Its endorsed members of Congress have been associated with the informal progressive bloc known as "the Squad".
