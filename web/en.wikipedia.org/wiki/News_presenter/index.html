News presenter

[Image: A presenter at a modern news desk with a video wall behind]

A news presenter (newsreader, newscaster, or anchor in American usage) is the journalist who presents television or radio news programmes in the studio, introducing reports and interviewing guests. The role blends journalism with performance; the American 'anchor' archetype — managing editor of the broadcast — was defined by Walter Cronkite at CBS.

Traditions

British newsreaders were long expected to be neutral announcers (the BBC's Alvar Lidell and successors), while US anchors became network stars. The modern presenter often reports, writes and fronts special coverage, and the role has extended to rolling-news and streaming presenters.
