The Bislett Games is an annual track and field meeting held at the Bislett Stadium in Oslo, Norway. It is one of the premier athletics meetings in the world and has been part of the Diamond League since the series began in 2010. Previously it was a fixture of the IAAF Golden League and the Grand Prix circuit.

History

Athletics meetings at Bislett date back to 1924, when they were promoted as the "American Meetings" because of the touring American athletes who competed. The Bislett Games in their modern form grew to prominence in the 1960s and 1970s, earning a reputation as a "world record factory": the compact stadium's fast track and knowledgeable crowd have witnessed more than sixty world records across all events, in both athletics and, historically, speed skating.

Dream Mile

The meeting's signature event is the Dream Mile, a mile race instituted in 1978 that has attracted the world's leading middle-distance runners. Famous winners include Sebastian Coe, Steve Ovett, Steve Cram, Hicham El Guerrouj, Jakob Ingebrigtsen and Josh Kerr. Other famous Bislett moments include world records by Haile Gebrselassie in the 10,000 metres and the first sub-13-minute 5,000 metres by Saïd Aouita in 1985.
