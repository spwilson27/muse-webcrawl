2024 United Kingdom general election

The 2024 United Kingdom general election was held on 4 July 2024. The Labour Party under Keir Starmer won a landslide majority, ending 14 years of Conservative government under Prime Minister Rishi Sunak.


Date: 4 July 2024
Labour: 411 seats, 33.7%
Conservatives: 121 seats, 23.7%
Liberal Democrats: 72 seats
Turnout: 59.7%

Results

Labour won 411 of 650 seats on about a third of the vote — a highly efficient landslide shaped by first-past-the-post. The Conservatives fell to 121 seats, their worst result ever. Reform UK won 5 seats with 14.3% of the vote, the Liberal Democrats won 72 seats, and the SNP fell to 9 seats in Scotland.

Aftermath

Starmer became prime minister on 5 July, promising change and national renewal after years of political turmoil including Brexit, COVID-19 and the cost-of-living crisis.
