Lanzón

The Lanzón (Spanish for "great lance" or "spear", referring to its shape) is a granite monolith, about 4.5 metres (15 ft) tall, carved by the pre-Columbian Chavín culture of Peru. It stands in its original position within a cruciform gallery of the Old Temple at Chavín de Huántar, high in the Peruvian Andes.

Description

The sculpture depicts an anthropomorphic deity with feline features: one hand raised and the other lowered, with snakes and carved motifs spreading from its head. The figure is thought to represent the principal god of the Chavín cult, a mediator between opposing cosmic forces, and the gallery around it may have functioned as an oracle chamber.
