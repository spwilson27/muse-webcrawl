Peterborough United F.C.

Peterborough United Football Club is a professional association football club based in Peterborough, Cambridgeshire, England. Nicknamed "The Posh", the club plays its home matches at London Road (the Weston Homes Stadium) and competes in EFL League One, the third tier of English football. Founded in 1934, Peterborough United were elected to the Football League in 1960 and are known for developing young players and for high-scoring attacking football.

History

The club was formed in 1934 after the collapse of Peterborough & Fletton United, and played in the Midland League, which it dominated in the 1950s, winning five consecutive titles. Elected to the Football League's Fourth Division in 1960, United won the division in their first season with a record 134 league goals, Terry Bly scoring 52. The club reached the FA Cup quarter-finals in 1965. Financial irregularities led to demotion to the Fourth Division in 1968 despite a mid-table Third Division finish.[1]

Under managers including Barry Fry and Darren Ferguson, the club yo-yoed between the second and fourth tiers. Peterborough won the Football League Trophy (EFL Trophy) in 2014 and again in 2024, and were promoted to the Championship via the League One play-offs in 2011. The club's academy and recruitment model, buying young lower-league talent such as Ivan Toney and selling at a profit, has brought national attention.

Nickname

"The Posh" is said to derive from a remark by former Fletton United manager Pat Tirrell in 1921 that he wanted "posh players for a posh team"; Victoria Beckham's use of "Posh Spice" led to a light-hearted trademark dispute in 2002, which the club ultimately did not press.
