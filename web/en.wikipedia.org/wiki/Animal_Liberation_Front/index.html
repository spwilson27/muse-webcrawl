The Animal Liberation Front (ALF) is an international, leaderless resistance movement that engages in direct action on behalf of animals. It originated in Britain in 1976, founded by activist Ronnie Lee out of the earlier Band of Mercy and Hunt Saboteurs Association.

Activities

ALF activists — operating anonymously in small cells — have rescued animals from laboratories, farms and fur facilities, carried out acts of economic sabotage and property damage, and gathered undercover evidence of animal cruelty. The movement's guidelines state that activists should take all reasonable precautions not to harm any animal, human or otherwise.

Criticism and legal status

Governments and industry groups have characterised some ALF activity as extremist or eco-terrorism; the FBI in the United States has listed animal-rights extremism among domestic threats, while supporters argue the movement has injured no one and acts to expose cruelty.
