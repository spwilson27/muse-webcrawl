Hakusensha

Hakusensha, Inc. (株式会社白泉社, Kabushiki-gaisha Hakusensha) is a Japanese publishing company headquartered in Chiyoda, Tokyo. Founded on December 1, 1973, it is best known for publishing shōjo manga magazines and the manga serialised in them. The company is a member of the Hakuhodo DY Group alongside Shueisha and Shogakukan, with which it shares historical ownership ties.

Magazines and notable titles

Hakusensha's flagship magazine is Hana to Yume (founded 1974), alongside LaLa, LaLa DX, Melody, and the former Bessatsu Hana to Yume. Notable series published by the company include Berserk by Kentaro Miura (serialised in Young Animal), Fruits Basket by Natsuki Takaya, Vampire Knight, Ouran High School Host Club, Skip Beat!, and Natsume's Book of Friends. Collected volumes are released under imprints such as Hana to Yume Comics and Jets Comics.
