List of costliest Atlantic hurricanes

This is a list of the costliest Atlantic hurricanes by total economic damage. The three costliest — Katrina (2005), Harvey (2017), and Ian (2022) — are also among the costliest natural disasters in United States history, each causing well over US$100 billion in damage.

Hurricane Katrina (2005) remains the costliest Atlantic hurricane on record.

Normalized damage

Adjusted for inflation, wealth, and population growth along coastlines, older storms rank higher: studies normalizing U.S. hurricane damage estimate that the 1926 Great Miami hurricane would cause the most damage if repeated today, followed by storms such as Katrina and the 1900 Galveston hurricane.

Retired names

Names of exceptionally deadly or costly hurricanes are retired by the World Meteorological Organization; retired names include Katrina, Sandy, Harvey, Irma, Maria, Ian, Andrew, and Ike.
