Max Stahl (born Christopher Max Stahl Wenner; 3 December 1954 – 28 October 2021) was a British journalist and filmmaker best known for filming the Santa Cruz massacre in Dili, East Timor, on 12 November 1991. His footage, smuggled out of the Indonesian-occupied territory, exposed the massacre to the world and is credited with shifting international opinion toward East Timorese independence.

Santa Cruz footage

Stahl travelled to East Timor posing as a tourist and filmed Indonesian troops opening fire on a peaceful procession at the Santa Cruz cemetery, killing more than 250 people. He buried the videotape in a grave to evade arrest, retrieved it later and smuggled it out with the help of Dutch journalist Saskia Kouwenberg. Broadcast internationally, the film became one of the most consequential pieces of war reporting of the decade.

Later career

Stahl covered conflicts in the Balkans, the Middle East and elsewhere, and settled in East Timor after its independence, establishing the Max Stahl Audiovisual Centre in Dili to preserve the nation's recorded memory. He became an East Timorese citizen and received the Order of Timor-Leste. He died in Brisbane in 2021 after treatment for cancer.
