Fujiko Fujio A (Japanese: 藤子不二雄Ⓐ), born Motoo Abiko (安孫子 素雄, 10 March 1934 – 7 April 2022), was a Japanese manga artist. With his school friend Hiroshi Fujimoto he formed the duo Fujiko Fujio, working under the shared pen name from 1951 to 1987; Fujimoto subsequently became Fujiko F. Fujio, and Abiko took the name Fujiko Fujio A.

Career

Abiko's best-known solo works tend toward the humorous and the macabre. They include Ninja Hattori-kun, about a boy ninja living with an ordinary family; Kaibutsu-kun (The Monster Kid), about a monster prince; Pro Golfer Saru; and Manga Michi, an autobiographical account of the duo's early years. His darkest and most celebrated series is Warau Salesman (The Laughing Salesman), whose salesman Fukuzō Moguro offers desperate people Faustian bargains that invariably end badly — a work frequently cited as a classic of black-humour manga.

The two artists, friends since elementary school in Toyama, drifted apart professionally as their styles diverged — Fujimoto toward children's science fiction such as Doraemon, Abiko toward satire and adult themes — and they dissolved the partnership in 1987. Abiko died in 2022 at the age of 88.
