Akers Mekaniske Verksted

Akers Mekaniske Verksted (Akers Mechanical Workshop), often known simply as Akers Mekaniske or Akers Mek., was a Norwegian shipyard and engineering company located at Aker Brygge in Oslo. Founded in 1841, it was for more than a century Norway's largest shipyard and industrial employer.

History

The company began as a workshop on the Aker River before moving to the harbor site at Pipervika in the 1850s, where it built iron-hulled ships, steam engines, and later naval vessels, tankers, and offshore equipment. Its wharves and dry docks dominated Oslo's waterfront, and the firm merged with Nylands Verksted in the Kværner group in the later twentieth century.

Closure and redevelopment

Shipbuilding at the yard ended in 1982, and the site was redeveloped during the 1980s into Aker Brygge, a mixed-use district of offices, shops, restaurants, and apartments that has become one of Oslo's best-known waterfront areas, retaining some historic industrial buildings and cranes as landmarks.
