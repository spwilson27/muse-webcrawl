Lomb–Scargle periodogram
Field: Time-series analysis
Use: Unevenly sampled data

The Lomb–Scargle periodogram is a method for detecting and characterising periodic signals in unevenly sampled time-series data, widely used in astronomy for variable stars and exoplanet searches.

Method

Developed from work by Nicholas Lomb and Jeffrey Scargle, it effectively performs a least-squares fit of sinusoids at each trial frequency, handling gaps in observations without interpolation.

Applications

It is a standard tool in astrophysics, geophysics and other fields where sampling is irregular, with significance assessed via false-alarm probabilities.
