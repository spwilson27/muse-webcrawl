International Energy Agency

The International Energy Agency (IEA) is a Paris-based intergovernmental organisation founded in 1974 within the OECD framework, after the 1973 oil crisis, to coordinate energy policy and emergency oil stockpiles.

International Energy Agency
Founded18 November 1974
HeadquartersParis, France
Members31 member countries plus association countries
Executive DirectorFatih Birol (since 2015)

Role

Members must hold strategic petroleum reserves equal to 90 days of net imports and can release them collectively, as in 1991, 2005, 2011 and 2022. The IEA publishes the influential World Energy Outlook and, increasingly, analysis on clean-energy transitions, critical minerals and net-zero pathways.

Criticism

It has been criticised historically for underestimating solar and wind growth and for scenarios seen as inconsistent with Paris climate goals, prompting revised modelling.
