To Err Is Human (report)

To Err Is Human: Building a Safer Health System
Publisher: Institute of Medicine (National Academies)
Published: 1999
Subject: Medical error, patient safety

To Err Is Human: Building a Safer Health System is a landmark 1999 report by the Institute of Medicine of the United States National Academies. The report concluded that between 44,000 and 98,000 people die each year in American hospitals as a result of preventable medical errors, and it called for a comprehensive national effort to improve patient safety.

Findings and recommendations

The report argued that most medical errors arise from faulty systems and processes rather than individual negligence, and recommended a mandatory nationwide reporting system, safety standards, and a culture of safety modelled partly on other high-risk industries such as aviation.

Impact

The publication attracted enormous media attention, spurred legislation and new safety organisations, and is widely credited with launching the modern patient-safety movement in healthcare.
