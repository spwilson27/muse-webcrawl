Mega Man X

Developer: Capcom
Release: 1993 (Japan)
Platform: Super Nintendo Entertainment System
Genre: Action, platform

Mega Man X (known in Japan as Rockman X) is an action-platform game developed by Capcom for the Super NES. It is the first game in the Mega Man X sub-series, a darker continuation of the original Mega Man story set about a century later.

Story and gameplay

The protagonist is X, an advanced robot built by Dr. Light with the capacity for free will, awakened by Dr. Cain and serving as a Maverick Hunter against rogue Reploids led by Sigma. The game refined the series formula with a wall-jump, a dash move, armour upgrades hidden in capsules, and heart tanks and sub-tanks that expand X's abilities — features that became hallmarks of the X series.

Reception

Mega Man X was a commercial and critical success, praised for its graphics, music and gameplay evolution, and it spawned numerous sequels and remakes, including the PSP remake Mega Man Maverick Hunter X.
