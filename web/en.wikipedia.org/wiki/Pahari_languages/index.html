Pahari languages

The Pahari languages are a group of Indo-Aryan languages spoken in the lower ranges of the Himalayas, from eastern Nepal through the Indian states of Uttarakhand and Himachal Pradesh to Jammu. The name derives from pahar, "mountain".

Classification

Linguists commonly divide them into Eastern Pahari (represented by Nepali), Central Pahari (Kumaoni and Garhwali), and Western Pahari (including Dogri, Kangri, and other varieties of Himachal Pradesh and Jammu). Nepali and Dogri are official scheduled languages of India, and Nepali is the national language of Nepal.

Status

Many smaller Pahari varieties are endangered as speakers shift to Hindi, Nepali, and other regional standards for education and administration.
