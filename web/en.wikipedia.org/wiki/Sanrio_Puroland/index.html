Sanrio Puroland (サンリオピューロランド, Sanrio Pyūrorando) is an indoor theme park in Tama New Town, Tokyo, Japan, operated by Sanrio. Opened in 1990, it is devoted to Sanrio's character universe — Hello Kitty, My Melody, Cinnamoroll, Pompompurin, Kuromi, and others — and draws roughly a million and a half visitors a year, many of them adults devoted to kawaii culture.

Attractions

Puroland is entirely indoors and organised around "Puro Village", a fantasy townscape beneath an artificial sky. Attractions include elaborate musical revues and parades performed by costumed characters and live actors (its Kabuki-inflected Hello Kitty theatre productions are particularly noted), character greeting houses such as Lady Kitty House, a boat ride past Sanrio character tableaux, seasonal events, and character-themed restaurants and shops. Sanrio's second park, Harmonyland in Oita Prefecture, is its outdoor counterpart.

Cultural role

The park is a pilgrimage site for Hello Kitty fans worldwide and has leaned into its adult fanbase with events such as cosplay days and evening parties. Character popularity rankings announced by Sanrio — the annual Sanrio Character Award — receive substantial attention at the park each year.
