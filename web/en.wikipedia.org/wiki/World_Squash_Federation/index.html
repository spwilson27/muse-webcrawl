The World Squash Federation (WSF) is the international governing body for the sport of squash. Founded in 1967 as the International Squash Rackets Federation, it adopted its present name in 1992. The federation is recognised by the International Olympic Committee and is a member of the Association of IOC Recognised International Sports Federations (ARISF).

History

The federation was formed by representatives of seven founding nations and grew steadily as squash spread worldwide. It sanctions the World Squash Championships, the World Team Squash Championships, the World Junior Squash Championships and the World Doubles Squash Championships, and it maintains the official rules of the game and world ranking regulations in cooperation with the Professional Squash Association (PSA).

Olympic inclusion

Squash campaigned for Olympic inclusion for several decades. In October 2023, the IOC approved squash as one of the additional sports for the Los Angeles 2028 Olympic Games, marking the sport's Olympic debut.

Member nations

The WSF has well over one hundred member national federations, organised into five regional federations covering Africa, Asia, Europe, Oceania and the Pan-American region.
