Steve Jackson (American game designer)

Born: 1953, Tulsa, Oklahoma, U.S.
Occupation: Game designer, publisher
Known for: GURPS, Illuminati, Munchkin
Company: Steve Jackson Games

Steve Jackson (born 1953) is an American game designer and founder of Steve Jackson Games. He is best known as the creator of the GURPS role-playing system, the card game Illuminati, and the best-selling Munchkin card game series.

Career

Jackson worked for Metagaming Concepts in the late 1970s, designing games including Ogre and The Fantasy Trip. In 1980 he founded Steve Jackson Games in Austin, Texas, initially publishing his own designs such as Car Wars and Killer.

The company became famous in 1990 when the United States Secret Service raided its offices in connection with a hacker investigation, an incident that helped spur the founding of the Electronic Frontier Foundation and led to the landmark case Steve Jackson Games, Inc. v. United States Secret Service.

Major works

GURPS (Generic Universal RolePlaying System), first published in 1986, is a flexible point-based role-playing system used for hundreds of supplements. Munchkin (2001), illustrated by John Kovalic, parodies dungeon-crawl role-playing and became one of the most successful card games of its era.

Honours

Jackson was inducted into the Academy of Adventure Gaming Arts and Design Hall of Fame. Many of his games have won Origins Awards.
