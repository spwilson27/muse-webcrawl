Gavril Radomir (Greek: Γαβριὴλ Ρωμανός, Gavriil Romanos) was the emperor (tsar) of the First Bulgarian Empire from October 1014 to August 1015. The son of Tsar Samuel, he succeeded his father in the midst of the empire's losing war against the Byzantine emperor Basil II.

Reign

As crown prince, Gavril Radomir had distinguished himself as a capable commander; in 1014 he saved his father's life at the Battle of Kleidion, the catastrophic defeat after which Samuel died, reportedly of grief at the sight of his blinded army. Gavril Radomir's brief reign was marked by continued Byzantine advances and internal division. Basil II reportedly addressed him by the name "Romanos", a claim to an imperial lineage. In August 1015 Gavril Radomir was murdered while hunting by his cousin Ivan Vladislav, whom he had once saved from death, and who seized the throne as the last ruler of the First Bulgarian Empire.
