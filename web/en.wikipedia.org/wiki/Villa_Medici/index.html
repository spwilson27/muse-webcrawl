Villa Medici

The Villa Medici (Italian: Villa Medici) is a Mannerist villa and architectural complex on the Pincian Hill in Rome, next to Trinità dei Monti. Built for Cardinal Ricci da Montepulciano and acquired by Cardinal Ferdinando de' Medici in 1576, its garden facade is decorated with antique reliefs and statues.

French Academy

Napoleon transferred the French Academy in Rome to the villa in 1803; the Academy, founded in 1666, hosts Prix de Rome fellows — artists, writers, composers and researchers in residence. Directors have included Ingres and Balthus.

Architecture

The austere street facade contrasts with the open, sculpture-filled garden front overlooking Rome. The grounds contain studios built for fellows, including those erected for the composer Debussy era residents, and the famous painted views by Velázquez of the villa garden.
