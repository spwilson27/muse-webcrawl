List of Scottish monarchs

The monarch of Scotland was the head of state of the Kingdom of Scotland. According to tradition, the first King of Scots was Kenneth I MacAlpin (Cináed mac Ailpín), who founded the kingdom in 843. The monarchy continued until the Acts of Union 1707 united Scotland with England to form the Kingdom of Great Britain under Queen Anne, the last queen of Scotland.

Interregnum and House of Balliol

After the death of Margaret, Maid of Norway (1290), thirteen competitors claimed the throne. John Balliol was chosen and reigned 1292–1296 before being deposed by Edward I of England, beginning the Wars of Scottish Independence.
