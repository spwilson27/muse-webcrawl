Robertson Panel

DateJanuary 14–18, 1953
ChairHoward P. Robertson
SponsorCentral Intelligence Agency
SubjectUnidentified flying objects

The Robertson Panel was a scientific committee convened by the CIA in January 1953 to review the US Air Force's files on unidentified flying objects, at a time when UFO reports — notably the 1952 Washington, D.C. sightings — were raising concern about clogged intelligence channels and public panic in a Cold War attack scenario.

Conclusions

Chaired by physicist Howard P. Robertson, with members including Luis Alvarez and Samuel Goudsmit, the panel concluded after reviewing dozens of cases that UFOs posed no direct physical threat and showed no evidence of extraterrestrial technology. Its most controversial recommendation was a public "debunking" and education campaign, using mass media and even Walt Disney productions, to strip UFOs of their "aura of mystery" and reduce public reporting. The panel's recommendations shaped official US handling of UFO reports for years and remain a touchstone in debates about government secrecy around the subject.
