Eclipso

Eclipso is a fictional supervillain appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Bob Haney and artist Lee Elias, the character first appeared in House of Secrets #61 (August 1963). Eclipso was originally scientist Bruce Gordon, transformed by a black diamond during a solar eclipse; later retcons established Eclipso as an ancient spirit of wrath and vengeance imprisoned within the diamond, known as the Heart of Darkness.

Fictional character biography

In early stories, solar eclipses triggered Gordon's transformation into the powerful, malevolent Eclipso, while other light returned him to normal — a Jekyll-and-Hyde premise. Later continuity recast Eclipso as the original Spirit of God's Wrath, replaced in that role by the Spectre, who seeks hosts through shards of the black diamond. Host Jean Loring featured prominently in storylines such as Countdown to Mystery, and a female successor, using the diamond's powers, appeared in the Eclipso ongoing material of the 1990s.

Powers and abilities

Eclipso possesses vast magical powers including superhuman strength, flight, energy projection from the diamond, and the ability to possess and corrupt others touched by diamond shards. The character served as the main antagonist of the 1992 DC summer crossover Eclipso: The Darkness Within and the JSA storyline "Black Adam and the Eclipso" arcs, among others.

In other media

Eclipso appears as a major villain in the second season of the television series Stargirl, portrayed through host Bruce Gordon and effects.
