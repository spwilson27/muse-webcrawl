Emblem of Ethiopia

The Emblem of Ethiopia has been in its current form since 1996. It contains a radiant star with rays of light on a blue circular background. The star represents the unity of the people and the diversity of the nationalities of Ethiopia, while the rays of light symbolize the bright future of the country.

History

Earlier Ethiopian emblems reflected the country's imperial and socialist periods. Under the Ethiopian Empire, the heraldic symbol was the Lion of Judah bearing a banner, derived from the Solomonic dynasty's claimed descent. During the Derg regime (1974–1991), a new socialist-style emblem featured a rising sun, a plough, and the lion's head elements replaced with state socialist imagery. The transitional government adopted an interim emblem before the present design was specified under the 1995 constitution of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia.

In 2009 the emblem was slightly modified: the rays of the star were adjusted and the blue disk lightened, following legislation to standardize the national symbols.

Symbolism

According to the official description, the radiant star signifies the equality of all Ethiopian nations, nationalities, and peoples, and the light rays represent the prosperity and future of the country. The blue background stands for peace.
