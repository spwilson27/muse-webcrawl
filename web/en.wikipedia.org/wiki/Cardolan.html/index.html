Cardolan

Cardolan is a fictional region in J. R. R. Tolkien's Middle-earth legendarium. It was one of the three successor kingdoms of Arnor, the great kingdom of the Dúnedain in the North, after Arnor was divided in T.A. 861 among the sons of King Eärendur.

History

Cardolan occupied the lands between the Brandywine (Baranduin) river in the west, the river Hoarwell (Mitheithel), and the Great South Road. Its first king was Tarastor. Cardolan, together with Rhudaur and Arthedain, inherited the divided realm, and the three kingdoms frequently warred over the strategic tower of Amon Sûl (Weathertop) and its palantír.

In T.A. 1409 Cardolan was overrun by the forces of Angmar; its last prince was slain defending the Barrow-downs, and the Dúnedain of Cardolan were destroyed or scattered. The remnant took refuge in the Barrow-downs and the Old Forest, but a great plague in T.A. 1636 emptied the land, and evil spirits sent by Angmar came to dwell in the barrows. By the time of the War of the Ring, Cardolan was a desolate, empty land.
