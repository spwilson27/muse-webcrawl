Adolfo Suárez González, 1st Duke of Suárez (1932–2014), was a Spanish politician and lawyer who served as Prime Minister of Spain from 1976 to 1981. He was the first democratically elected prime minister after the Franco dictatorship and a central figure of the Spanish transition to democracy.

Premiership

Appointed by King Juan Carlos I in July 1976, Suárez pushed through the Political Reform Act, which dismantled the Francoist Cortes and legalized political parties, including the Communist Party. He won the first free general election of 1977 and presided over the drafting of the democratic Constitution of 1978.

Later life

Suárez resigned in January 1981 amid party infighting; weeks later he famously stood his ground in Congress during the attempted coup of 23 February 1981. He later founded the Democratic and Social Centre party. In 2008 he was admitted to the Order of the Golden Fleece. Madrid's airport was renamed in his honour shortly after his death.
