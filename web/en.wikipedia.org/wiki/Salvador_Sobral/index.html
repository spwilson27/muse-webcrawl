Salvador Sobral

Born: Salvador Vilar Braamcamp Sobral, 28 December 1989, Lisbon, Portugal
Genres: Jazz, alternative rock, soul, bossa nova
Occupation: Singer
Known for: Winner of the Eurovision Song Contest 2017

Salvador Vilar Braamcamp Sobral (born 28 December 1989) is a Portuguese singer, who won the Eurovision Song Contest 2017 for Portugal with the song "Amar pelos dois" ("To Love for the Both of Us"), written by his sister Luísa Sobral. The victory was Portugal's first ever in the contest, and the song scored 758 points, the highest total in Eurovision history under the voting system then in use.

Early life and career

Sobral was born in Lisbon and spent part of his youth in the United States and Barcelona, where he studied at the Taller de Músics and performed in bars and clubs. He first came to public attention in Portugal in 2009 as a contestant on the third season of Ídolos, the Portuguese version of Pop Idol, finishing seventh. He released his debut album Excuse Me in 2016.

Eurovision and health

Sobral won the Portuguese selection Festival da Canção in March 2017. Unable to attend the first Eurovision rehearsals in Kyiv because of a serious heart condition, he was represented there by his sister, who also joined him on stage for the winner's reprise. His understated performance and acceptance speech—"music is not fireworks, music is feeling"—were widely noted. In December 2017 he received a heart transplant, and he returned to performing in 2018.

Later work

His subsequent albums include Paris, Lisboa (2019), bpm (2021), and Timbre (2023), reflecting his interests in jazz and Brazilian music. He has recorded in Portuguese, English, Spanish, and French.
