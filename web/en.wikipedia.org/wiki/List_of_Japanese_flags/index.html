List of Japanese flags

This is a list of flags of Japan, including the national flag, imperial and military flags, prefectural and municipal flags, and historical banners.

National flag

The National Flag of Japan is a white rectangular banner bearing a crimson-red disc at its centre, known as the Nisshōki ("sun-mark flag") or Hinomaru ("circle of the sun"). It was officially adopted in its present statutory form by the Act on National Flag and Anthem of 1999, though the design had been in use as the civil ensign since 1870. The sun symbol reflects Japan's name, "origin of the sun".

Imperial flags

The Emperor's standard is a deep red banner bearing a golden 16-petalled chrysanthemum, the Imperial Seal of Japan. Other members of the Imperial House have personal standards distinguished by smaller chrysanthemums and borders.

Military flags

The Japan Self-Defense Forces use the Hachijō-Kyokujitsuki-style flag adopted in 1954. The Japan Ground Self-Defense Force flag features a gold cherry blossom and red sun rays; the Japan Maritime Self-Defense Force ensign retains the Rising Sun design with 16 rays offset to the hoist, used by the Imperial Japanese Navy before 1945. Regimental colours of the Ground Self-Defense Force are purple banners on pikes crowned with a golden chrysanthemum.

Prefectural flags

Each of Japan's 47 prefectures has a flag, usually a stylised monogram of the prefecture's name in a symbolic colour on a plain field, such as Tokyo's stylised "T" sun emblem (1989) and Hokkaido's blue field with a white seven-pointed star (1967).

Historical flags

Historical banners include the white banners of the Taira and the "seven-star" standards of medieval warlords, the kamon banners (nobori) of the samurai in the Sengoku period, the Dutch-trade era flags of the Edo period, and the war flag of the Imperial Japanese Army (a centred 16-ray Rising Sun with a white border).
