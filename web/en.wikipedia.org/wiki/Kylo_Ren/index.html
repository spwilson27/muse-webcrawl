Kylo Ren (born Ben Solo) is a fictional character in the Star Wars franchise, portrayed by Adam Driver. Introduced in Star Wars: The Force Awakens (2015), he is the son of Han Solo and Leia Organa, trained as a Jedi by his uncle Luke Skywalker before being seduced to the dark side of the Force by Supreme Leader Snoke. He appears in all three films of the sequel trilogy.

Character

Ben Solo was born to Leia Organa and Han Solo shortly after the Battle of Endor. Strong in the Force, he trained with Luke Skywalker, but Snoke's manipulation and Luke's momentary fearful reaction — igniting his lightsaber over the sleeping Ben — drove him to destroy the Jedi temple and fall to the dark side, taking the name Kylo Ren as master of the Knights of Ren. Idolising his grandfather Darth Vader, Ren wields an unstable red crossguard lightsaber of his own construction with a cracked kyber crystal.

Appearances

In The Force Awakens, Ren hunts the map to Luke Skywalker, captures the scavenger Rey, and kills his father Han Solo on Starkiller Base in an attempt to extinguish the light in himself. In The Last Jedi (2017), he forms a Force bond with Rey, kills Snoke, and — after Rey refuses to join him — declares himself Supreme Leader. In The Rise of Skywalker (2019), a vision of his father and his mother's final act redeem him; as Ben Solo he aids Rey against the resurrected Emperor Palpatine and gives his life to resurrect her, dying in her arms.

Reception

Driver's performance was widely praised, and the character is often cited among the best-received elements of the sequel trilogy for his internal conflict and volatility.
