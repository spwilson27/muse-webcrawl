Vimeo

Vimeo, Inc. is an American online video platform headquartered in New York City. Launched in November 2004 by Jake Lodwick and Zach Klein, Vimeo provides tools for creating, sharing, and hosting high-quality video, and has positioned itself toward professional creators and businesses rather than general consumer uploads.

History

Vimeo — its name a blend of "video" and "me" — grew out of Connected Ventures in New York and was acquired by IAC in 2006. It was among the first video-sharing sites to support high-definition playback, and it cultivated a community of filmmakers and animators, showcased through its curated "Staff Picks." In 2021 Vimeo was spun off from IAC and became a publicly traded company listed on Nasdaq.

Services

The platform operates on a subscription model rather than advertising: users pay for increased storage, advanced analytics, customizable players, live streaming, and video marketing tools. Vimeo also operates an over-the-top (OTT) service enabling creators to launch subscription video channels and apps, and it has expanded into AI-assisted video creation and enterprise video communications.
