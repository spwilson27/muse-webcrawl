Suzanne Brigit "Sue" Bird (born October 16, 1980) is an American former professional basketball player who spent her entire 21-season career with the Seattle Storm of the Women's National Basketball Association (WNBA). Widely regarded as one of the greatest point guards in the history of the sport, Bird won four WNBA championships (2004, 2010, 2018, 2020), five Olympic gold medals with the United States national team, and four FIBA World Cup titles.

Bird played college basketball for the UConn Huskies, winning two NCAA championships in 2000 and 2002 and earning the Wade Trophy and Naismith Award as the nation's top player in her senior season. She was selected first overall in the 2002 WNBA draft by Seattle.

Professional career

With the Storm, Bird formed celebrated partnerships with Lauren Jackson and later Breanna Stewart, leading the franchise to all four of its championships. She was named to the WNBA All-Star Game 13 times, a league record, and retired as the WNBA's all-time leader in assists and games played. She also played overseas during WNBA offseasons, winning multiple EuroLeague titles with Russian clubs including Spartak Moscow Region and UMMC Ekaterinburg.

International career

Bird is the only basketball player, male or female, to win five Olympic gold medals, achieved consecutively from Athens 2004 through Tokyo 2020. With Diana Taurasi, she also won four World Cup golds. Her five golds made her one of the most decorated team-sport athletes in Olympic history.

Retirement and later work

Bird announced in June 2022 that she would retire at the end of the season. She has since worked as a broadcaster and analyst with ESPN, co-founded the media company Togethxr, and became a part-owner of the Seattle Storm. She is engaged to former soccer player Megan Rapinoe.
