Star Wars: The Force Awakens

Star Wars: The Force Awakens (also known as Star Wars: Episode VII – The Force Awakens) is a 2015 American epic space opera film directed by J. J. Abrams, who co-wrote the screenplay with Lawrence Kasdan and Michael Arndt. It is the first instalment of the Star Wars sequel trilogy and the seventh instalment of the main Skywalker Saga, set thirty years after Return of the Jedi.

Plot

Thirty years after the destruction of the second Death Star, Luke Skywalker, the last Jedi, has vanished. From the ashes of the Empire has risen the First Order, opposed by the Resistance backed by the Republic. Scavenger Rey, defecting stormtrooper Finn, and Resistance pilot Poe Dameron race to deliver a map fragment leading to Luke, pursued by the dark warrior Kylo Ren — the son of Han Solo and Leia Organa. Old heroes return: Han Solo and Chewbacca help the newcomers assault the planet-sized Starkiller Base, where Han is killed by his son. Rey, awakening to the Force, duels Kylo Ren before escaping; the film ends with her finding Luke in exile on Ahch-To and offering him his father's lightsaber.

Production

The film followed Disney's 2012 acquisition of Lucasfilm for $4.05 billion. Announced as the start of a new trilogy, it was produced by Lucasfilm and Abrams's Bad Robot, filmed mainly at Pinewood Studios in England beginning in May 2014, with a deliberate emphasis on practical effects and real locations.

Reception

The film received positive reviews, with praise for its action, effects, and performances, though some noted its structural similarities to A New Hope. It grossed over $2 billion worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing film of 2015 and, at the time, the third-highest-grossing film ever. It was followed by The Last Jedi (2017) and The Rise of Skywalker (2019).
