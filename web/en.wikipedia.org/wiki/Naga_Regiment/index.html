Naga Regiment

The Naga Regiment is an infantry regiment of the Indian Army, raised in 1970 at Ranikhet. It recruits primarily from the Naga people of Nagaland in north-eastern India.

History

The regiment was raised shortly after statehood for Nagaland, partly to provide employment for Naga youth, including former insurgents who had laid down arms. Its soldiers are noted for their hardiness in mountain and jungle terrain.

Service

Battalions of the regiment have served in counter-insurgency operations, on the Line of Control, and in the Kargil War of 1999, earning battle honours and gallantry awards.
