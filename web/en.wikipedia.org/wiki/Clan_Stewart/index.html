Clan Stewart

Clan Stewart (Gaelic: Stiùbhart) is a Scottish Highland and Lowland clan. The clan is recognised by the Court of the Lord Lyon; however, it does not have a Clan Chief recognised by the Lord Lyon King of Arms. Because the clan has no chief it is considered an armigerous clan.

Origins

The Stewarts descend from Walter fitz Alan, a Breton-descended knight who became High Steward of Scotland in the 12th century; the surname derives from that office. His descendant Walter Stewart married Marjorie Bruce, daughter of Robert the Bruce, and their son became King Robert II, founding the royal House of Stewart.

History

The royal Stewarts (later spelled Stuart) ruled Scotland and then Great Britain until 1714. Branches of the clan include the Stewarts of Appin, Atholl, and Galloway, and the family gave rise to numerous Jacobite supporters during the risings of 1715 and 1745.
