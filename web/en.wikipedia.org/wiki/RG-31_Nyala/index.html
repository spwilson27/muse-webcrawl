RG-31 Nyala

The RG-31 Nyala is a South African mine-protected armoured personnel carrier. It uses a V-shaped hull and raised chassis to deflect blast away from the crew compartment, a design philosophy developed from South African experience with land mines.

Design

The vehicle is a 4×4 armoured carrier that can be configured for troop transport, command, ambulance and route-clearance support roles. Its protection is aimed mainly at mines and small-arms fire, and variants have been fitted with remote weapon stations and additional armour packages.

Service

RG-31 vehicles have been used by several armed forces and by United States units in Iraq and Afghanistan, where improvised explosive devices made mine-protected vehicles a priority. The Nyala became one of the recognizable early MRAP-type vehicles before larger purpose-built fleets were fielded.

Its name, Nyala, refers to the antelope native to southern Africa.
