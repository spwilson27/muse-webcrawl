John Harry Martin Gosden OBE (born 30 March 1951) is a British racehorse trainer. He has trained over 3,000 winners worldwide, including winners of the Derby, the Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe, the King George VI and Queen Elizabeth Stakes, the Eclipse Stakes and the Breeders' Cup Classic, and has been British flat racing Champion Trainer several times. Since 2021 he has trained in partnership with his son Thady as John & Thady Gosden.

Early career

Gosden, the son of trainer Towser Gosden, was educated at Eastbourne College and Emmanuel College, Cambridge, where he read economics and land economy. He worked as assistant to Vincent O'Brien in Ireland and Sir Noel Murless in England before taking out a licence in California in 1979, where he trained Bates Motel to win the Santa Anita Handicap and an Eclipse Award.

Return to England

Returning to Britain in 1988, Gosden trained at Stanley House and then Clarehaven Stables in Newmarket. His stars have included Raven's Pass (Breeders' Cup Classic, 2008), Nathaniel, Kingman, Golden Horn (Derby and Arc, 2015), Cracksman, and above all Enable, who won the Arc twice and a record three King George VI and Queen Elizabeth Stakes, and Stradivarius, the champion stayer who won four Ascot Gold Cups' worth of staying honours including three Gold Cups themselves.

Training partnership

In 2021 Gosden took his son Thady into partnership on the licence, one of the first such joint licences in Britain; their early successes together included Mishriff's international victories.
