Trevor Noah

Born20 February 1984, Johannesburg, South Africa
OccupationComedian, television host, author

Trevor Noah (born 20 February 1984) is a South African comedian, writer, producer, political commentator, actor, and former television host. He hosted The Daily Show, an American satirical news program on Comedy Central, from 2015 to 2022, succeeding Jon Stewart.

Early life

Noah was born in Johannesburg to a Black Xhosa mother and a Swiss-German father; under apartheid, his parents' relationship was illegal, a theme he explores in his bestselling 2016 memoir Born a Crime: Stories from a South African Childhood.

Career

He built a stand-up career in South Africa before moving to the United States, joining The Daily Show as a correspondent in 2014 and becoming host the following year. He has hosted the Grammy Awards multiple times and tours internationally as a stand-up comedian. See The Daily Show.
