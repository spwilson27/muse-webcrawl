FIFA World Rankings

The FIFA World Rankings (officially FIFA/Coca-Cola World Ranking for men) are a ranking system for men's national association football teams, published by FIFA, currently led in recent years by teams such as Argentina, France and Spain.

FIFA World Rankings
Introduced: December 1992
System: Elo-based (from Aug 2018)
Teams: 211 member associations

Method

Since August 2018 the rankings use an Elo-based formula: points are added or subtracted after each match based on result, opponent strength and match importance, replacing the previous averaging system criticised for anomalies. A separate FIFA Women's World Ranking, older and also Elo-like, ranks women's teams.

Uses and criticism

Rankings seed World Cup draws and decide awards. Critics note incentives to avoid friendlies and distortions from differing confederation strengths.
