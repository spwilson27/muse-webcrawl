Hooker (rugby union)

Hooker
SportRugby union
Squad number2
UnitForwards (front row)
Key skillsScrummaging, lineout throwing, open-field play

The hooker is a position in rugby union, normally wearing the number 2 jersey. The hooker plays in the middle of the front row of the scrum, between the two props, and is responsible for winning possession by "hooking" the ball back after it is fed into the scrum.

Role

Besides scrummaging, the hooker usually throws the ball into the lineout, requiring accuracy under pressure. In open play the hooker performs duties similar to both forwards and backs—carrying the ball, tackling, and competing at the breakdown. Because of these combined demands, the position requires strength, mobility, and throwing skill.

Notable hookers

Celebrated hookers include Sean Fitzpatrick (New Zealand), Keith Wood (Ireland), John Smit (South Africa), Dane Coles (New Zealand), and Bismarck du Plessis (South Africa).
