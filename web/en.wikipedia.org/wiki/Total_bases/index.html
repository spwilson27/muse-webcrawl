Total bases

SportBaseball
TypeBatting statistic
AbbreviationTB

In baseball statistics, total bases (TB) is the number of bases a player has gained through hits: one for a single, two for a double, three for a triple and four for a home run. It is calculated as TB = 1B + 2×2B + 3×3B + 4×HR. Bases gained by walks, being hit by a pitch, stolen bases or errors do not count toward total bases. The statistic is the numerator of slugging percentage (TB divided by at-bats).

Records

Hank Aaron holds the Major League Baseball career record with 6,856 total bases. The single-season record is 457, set by Babe Ruth in 1921. The single-game record is 19 total bases, achieved by Shawn Green in 2002 with four home runs, a single and a double.
