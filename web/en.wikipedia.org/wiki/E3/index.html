E3 (short for the Electronic Entertainment Expo) was a trade event for the video game industry in the United States, organized by the Entertainment Software Association (ESA). For more than two decades it was the industry's premier annual showcase, where console makers and publishers unveiled new hardware, games and trailers to press, retailers and, later, the public.

History

The first E3 was held in May 1995 in Los Angeles after game companies grew dissatisfied with their treatment at the Consumer Electronics Show; the debut featured the launch rivalry between the Sega Saturn and Sony's PlayStation. The show became famous for lavish press conferences by Sony, Microsoft and Nintendo. Nintendo shifted to pre-recorded Nintendo Direct videos in 2013, and Sony withdrew from the show entirely after 2018. E3 was cancelled in 2020 because of the COVID-19 pandemic, held digitally in 2021, and cancelled again in 2022 and 2023; in December 2023 the ESA announced the event was permanently finished. Its role has largely been taken over by events such as Summer Game Fest and publisher-run digital presentations.
