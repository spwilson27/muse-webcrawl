Jenna Marie Ortega (born 27 September 2002) is an American actress and producer. She began her career as a child actress, receiving recognition for her role as young Jane in The CW comedy-drama Jane the Virgin (2014–2019) and starring as Harley Diaz in the Disney Channel series Stuck in the Middle (2016–2018).

Career

Ortega transitioned to mainstream film with roles in the horror films The Babysitter: Killer Queen (2020), X and Scream (both 2022), and Scream VI (2023), establishing her as a "scream queen". She achieved international stardom playing Wednesday Addams in the Netflix series Wednesday (2022–present), earning Golden Globe and Emmy nominations. She later starred in Beetlejuice Beetlejuice (2024).
