Blankety Blank

Blankety Blank is a British comedy game show based on the American programme Match Game. Contestants and a panel of celebrities attempt to match answers to fill-in-the-blank questions. It first aired on BBC1 from 18 January 1979 to 12 March 1990, hosted by Terry Wogan and later Les Dawson, and was revived for ITV by Paul O'Grady (as Lily Savage) in the late 1990s. A further revival fronted by Bradley Walsh began on BBC One in 2020.

Format

Two contestants compete. The host reads a statement with a missing word — the "blank" — and contestants try to match the answers written independently by six celebrity panellists. Points are scored for matches; the winner proceeds to the "Supermatch" round for prizes. The show was famous for its deliberately modest prizes, culminating in the consolation prize of a "Blankety Blank cheque book and pen", which became a catchphrase in itself.

Hosts

Terry Wogan hosted from 1979 to 1983 with a famously thin, wand-like microphone that he joked about on air. Les Dawson took over in 1984 and brought a lugubrious, self-deprecating style, mocking the show's cheap prizes. Paul O'Grady hosted an ITV revival from 1997 in character as Lily Savage, and a later series under his own name. Bradley Walsh has hosted the BBC revival since a 2020 Christmas special.

Legacy

Blankety Blank is remembered as a fixture of British light entertainment, notable for the chemistry between host and panellists — regulars included Kenny Everett, Lorraine Chase and Roy Hudd — rather than for its gameplay or prizes.
