Regal Cinemas

Image placeholder: a Regal multiplex theatre.

Regal Cinemas (branded as Regal) is an American movie theatre chain headquartered in Knoxville, Tennessee. It operates theatres under the Regal, United Artists and Edwards names and is one of the largest cinema circuits in the United States, with several hundred locations and several thousand screens. Regal is a subsidiary of the British company Cineworld Group, which acquired its parent, Regal Entertainment Group, in 2018.

History

Regal Cinemas was founded in 1989 by Mike Campbell in Knoxville, growing rapidly through acquisitions of regional chains during the multiplex-building boom of the 1990s. After a period of financial restructuring in the early 2000s, it combined with United Artists Theatres and Edwards Theatres under the control of the Anschutz Company to form Regal Entertainment Group. Cineworld's acquisition made the combined group the world's second-largest cinema operator. Cineworld and Regal entered Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings in 2022 amid post-pandemic debts, emerging in 2023 after restructuring.

Features

Many Regal locations offer premium formats such as IMAX, RPX (Regal Premium Experience), ScreenX and 4DX, recliner seating and the Regal Unlimited subscription programme.
