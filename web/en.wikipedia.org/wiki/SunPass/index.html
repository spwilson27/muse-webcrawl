SunPass

SunPass is an electronic toll collection system used in the U.S. state of Florida, operated by the Florida Department of Transportation's Florida's Turnpike Enterprise. Launched in 1999, it allows drivers to pay tolls automatically without stopping at toll booths, using a transponder affixed to the vehicle's windshield linked to a prepaid account.

How it works

As a vehicle passes under an overhead gantry or through a toll plaza lane, the transponder is read and the toll is deducted from the account; where no transponder is detected, cameras photograph the license plate and the registered owner is billed under the Toll-by-Plate system, usually at a higher rate. SunPass users generally receive a discount of about 25 percent compared with plate billing on Florida's Turnpike Enterprise facilities.

Transponders

The original SunPass Portable is a hard-case transponder that can be moved between vehicles. The SunPass Mini, introduced in 2008, is a lower-cost sticker tag that adheres permanently to the windshield and cannot be transferred. The SunPass PRO, introduced in 2021, is interoperable with E-ZPass and works in most states in the eastern United States.

Usage

SunPass is accepted on Florida's Turnpike, express lanes in Miami-Dade and Broward counties, and most other toll roads and bridges in Florida, as well as at some airport parking facilities including Orlando International Airport.
