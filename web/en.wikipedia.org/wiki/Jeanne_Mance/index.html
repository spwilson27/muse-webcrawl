Jeanne Mance

Jeanne Mance (November 12, 1606 – June 18, 1673) was a French nurse and settler of New France. She arrived at the future site of Montreal in 1642 and founded the Hôtel-Dieu de Montréal, the city's first hospital, in 1645. She is regarded as a co-founder of Montreal alongside Paul de Chomedey de Maisonneuve.

Born12 November 1606, Langres, France
Died18 June 1673, Montreal, New France
Known forCo-founder of Montreal; founder of Hôtel-Dieu

Life

Mance sailed with the Société Notre-Dame expedition in 1641 and helped establish Ville-Marie in May 1642. She administered the colony's funds and repeatedly returned to France to secure support and recruit settlers and hospital sisters.

Legacy

A monument in Montreal honours Mance with Maisonneuve, and she is commemorated as one of the city's founders.
