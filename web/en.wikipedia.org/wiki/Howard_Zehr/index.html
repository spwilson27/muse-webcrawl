Howard Zehr

Howard Zehr
Born: July 2, 1944, Freeport, Illinois, U.S.
Known for: Restorative justice
Notable work: Changing Lenses (1990)

Howard Zehr (born 1944) is an American criminologist and a leading figure in the modern restorative justice movement, sometimes described as its grandfather. He is associated with Eastern Mennonite University in Virginia, where he co-founded the Center for Justice and Peacebuilding programmes in restorative justice.

Work

In Changing Lenses: A New Focus for Crime and Justice (1990), Zehr contrasted a retributive lens — crime as a violation of law, with the state as victim — with a restorative lens: crime as harm to people and relationships, creating obligations to make things right, involving victims, offenders, and communities. His Little Book of Restorative Justice (2002) became a widely used introduction. Zehr is also a noted photographer and has written on photography and contemplative practice.
