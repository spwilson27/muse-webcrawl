FEI World Equestrian Games

The FEI World Equestrian Games were the major international championships for equestrianism, administered by the Fédération Equestre Internationale (FEI). Held every four years, midway between sets of consecutive Summer Olympic Games, the Games brought together the world championships of all of the FEI's recognised disciplines in a single event.

Dressage was one of the core disciplines contested at the Games.

History

The first World Equestrian Games were held in Stockholm, Sweden, in 1990. Subsequent editions took place in The Hague (1994), Rome (1998), Jerez de la Frontera (2002), Aachen (2006), Lexington, Kentucky (2010) — the first held outside Europe — Normandy (2014), and Tryon, North Carolina (2018). The 2018 edition was intended to be the last under the combined format; afterwards the FEI reverted to separate world championships for each discipline, beginning in 2022.

Disciplines

Disciplines contested at the Games included dressage, para-equestrian dressage, eventing, show jumping, driving, endurance, vaulting, and reining. Medals were awarded to individuals and national teams in each discipline.
