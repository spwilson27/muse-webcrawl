Sean-nós dance

Sean-nós dance (Irish for "old-style dance") is an older, vernacular form of Irish solo step dancing, distinct from the codified competitive style of Irish dance. It is performed low to the ground, with relaxed upper body, arms held loosely, and improvised percussive steps that follow the rhythm of the music closely.

Style and regions

The best-known tradition comes from Connemara in County Galway, with related styles in County Clare and elsewhere. Dancers traditionally performed on small raised surfaces such as a half-door, tabletop or the top of a barrel, showcasing intricate battering steps. Steps are passed down through families and local teachers rather than a central syllabus, and competitions are held at festivals such as the Oireachtas.
