AR-15 style rifle
Type: Semi-automatic rifle
Place of origin: United States
Based on: ArmaLite AR-15

AR-15 style rifles are semi-automatic rifles based on the design of the ArmaLite AR-15 and the Colt AR-15. After Colt's patents expired in the 1970s, many manufacturers produced their own versions, making the platform one of the most popular rifles in the United States.

Characteristics

They are gas-operated, magazine-fed, modular rifles typically chambered in .223 Remington / 5.56×45mm NATO, with many caliber variants. Modularity in barrels, handguards, stocks and optics is a defining feature.

Usage and debate

The rifles are used for sport shooting, hunting, home defense and by some law enforcement agencies, and have been central to debates over gun control in the United States.
