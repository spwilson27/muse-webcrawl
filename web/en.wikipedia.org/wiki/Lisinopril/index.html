Lisinopril

ClassACE inhibitor
Trade namesPrinivil, Zestril, others
RoutesBy mouth
Elimination half-life~12 hours
CAS number83915-83-7

Lisinopril is a medication belonging to the angiotensin-converting enzyme (ACE) inhibitor class, used to treat high blood pressure, heart failure, and after heart attacks. It is also used to prevent kidney problems in people with diabetes mellitus. It is taken by mouth.

Medical uses and side effects

Common side effects include headache, dizziness, tiredness, cough, nausea, and rash. Serious side effects may include low blood pressure, liver problems, high blood potassium, and angioedema. Use during pregnancy is not recommended as it may harm the baby. Lisinopril works by inhibiting the renin–angiotensin–aldosterone system.

History

Lisinopril was patented in 1978 and approved for medical use in the United States in 1987. It is a lysine analogue of enalaprilat, the active metabolite of enalapril, and unlike enalapril it is not a prodrug — it is active as administered. It is on the World Health Organization's List of Essential Medicines and is available as a generic medication.
