Troposphere

The troposphere is the lowest layer of Earth's atmosphere, extending from the surface to about 12 km on average — higher in the tropics (about 18 km) and lower at the poles (about 8 km). It contains about 75–80% of the atmosphere's mass and almost all its water vapour and aerosols.

BoundaryTropopause (above)

Characteristics

Temperature generally decreases with height at about 6.5 °C per km (the lapse rate). Nearly all weather occurs in the troposphere because of convection and water vapour. It is bounded above by the tropopause, above which lies the stratosphere.
