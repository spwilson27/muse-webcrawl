Wikipedia:Contents/Portals

This page lists Wikipedia's portals — subject gateway pages that highlight featured content and organise articles in a topic area. The main portals, linked from the Main Page, are:

PortalSubject
ArtsArt, architecture, film, literature, music, theatre
BiographyPeople and their lives
GeographyPlaces, countries, cities, landforms
HistoryPast events, periods and civilisations
MathematicsNumbers, algebra, geometry, logic
ScienceNatural and physical sciences
SocietyPolitics, law, economics, education, culture
TechnologyComputing, engineering, transport, inventions
Current eventsNews and ongoing events

Beyond these are hundreds of more specific portals, for individual countries, sports, religions, scientific fields and many other subjects, maintained by WikiProjects and other editor groups. Many smaller portals were retired or merged in community clean-ups, leaving the most active ones.

See also Wikipedia:Contents.
