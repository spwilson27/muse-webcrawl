Portuguese Football Federation

The Portuguese Football Federation (Portuguese: Federação Portuguesa de Futebol, FPF) is the governing body of football in Portugal. Founded in 1914 as the Portuguese Football Union, it organises the national teams and domestic cup competitions.

History

The FPF was a founding member of FIFA in 1923-era expansion and joined UEFA in 1954. Portugal hosted and won UEFA Euro 2016, the national team's first major trophy, followed by the inaugural UEFA Nations League title in 2019.

Competitions

The federation runs the Taça de Portugal and the Supertaça, while the Primeira Liga is operated by the Liga Portugal. Its headquarters and the Cidade do Futebol training complex are in Oeiras, near Lisbon.
