Global Financial Centres Index

The Global Financial Centres Index (GFCI) is a ranking of the competitiveness of financial centres based on over 29,000 assessments from an online questionnaire together with more than 100 indices from organisations such as the World Bank, the OECD, and the Economist Intelligence Unit. It is compiled by the Z/Yen Group in London and published twice a year, in March and September. The first index was published in March 2007.

The GFCI evaluates financial centres across five areas of competitiveness: business environment, human capital, infrastructure, financial sector development, and reputation. New York and London have traded the top position for most of the index's history, with centres such as Hong Kong, Singapore, San Francisco, Chicago, Los Angeles, Shanghai, and Shenzhen also ranking near the top in recent editions.

Methodology

Ratings combine instrumental factors (external indices) with responses from financial services professionals to an online questionnaire, using a factor assessment model to combine them into a single rating on a 1,000-point scale.
