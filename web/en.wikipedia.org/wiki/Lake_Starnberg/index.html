Lake Starnberg

Lake Starnberg (German: Starnberger See) is a lake in Bavaria, Germany, southwest of Munich. It is the second-largest lake in Bavaria by surface area and, owing to its great depth, the largest by water volume.

Geography

The lake is about 21 km long and up to 128 metres deep. The town of Starnberg lies at its northern tip; other shore towns include Berg, Tutzing, and Seeshaupt. Roseninsel (Rose Island) is its only island.

History

King Ludwig II of Bavaria died in the lake near Berg Castle in 1886 under circumstances that remain disputed; a chapel and memorial cross on the shore commemorate the event. The lakeshore has long been a favoured residence for Munich's wealthy, with many grand 19th-century villas.
