Nationwide Building Society is a British mutual financial institution, the largest building society in the world, with its headquarters in Swindon, England. It provides mortgages, savings, current accounts, credit cards, personal loans, and insurance products. Unlike a bank, Nationwide is owned by its members — customers with a mortgage, savings, or current account — rather than by shareholders.

History

Nationwide's origins trace to the Provident Union Building Society of Wiltshire, founded in 1846, and the Northampton Town & County Freehold Land Society (1848). The modern society arose through a series of mergers, most importantly the 1970 merger forming the Nationwide Building Society from the Co-operative Permanent and other societies, and the 1987 merger with the Anglia Building Society, which created the Nationwide Anglia (the Anglia name was dropped in 1991). Nationwide absorbed the Portman Building Society in 2007 and took on the savings business and branches of the Dunfermline Building Society in 2009.

Mutual status

During the wave of demutualisations in the 1980s–1990s, when many building societies converted to banks, Nationwide's members voted to remain mutual. In 1998, carpetbagger-led conversion resolutions were narrowly defeated, and the society has since emphasised its member ownership. As a mutual, it is not required to pay dividends to external shareholders, which it says allows it to offer better value to members.

Operations

Nationwide is one of the largest mortgage lenders and savings providers in the UK, with several hundred branches and total assets in excess of £270 billion in the mid-2020s. It is a member of the Building Societies Association. Its long-running advertising in the 2010s featured the "Voices" campaign of spoken-word poets.

Virgin Money acquisition

In March 2024 Nationwide agreed to acquire Virgin Money UK for approximately £2.9 billion, the largest bank takeover since the 2008 financial crisis; the deal completed on 1 October 2024, making the combined group the UK's second-largest provider of mortgages and savings, with around 24.5 million customers. Nationwide stated that the Virgin Money brand would continue in the near term.
