Law & Order (franchise)

The Law & Order franchise is a group of American police procedural and legal drama television series created by Dick Wolf and produced by Wolf Entertainment for NBC. Beginning with the original Law & Order in 1990, the franchise became one of the longest-running and most successful in television history, spawning numerous spin-offs sharing characters, settings, and frequent crossovers.

Series in the Law & Order franchise.

Series

The principal series are Law & Order (1990–2010, revived 2022), Law & Order: Special Victims Unit (1999–present), Law & Order: Criminal Intent (2001–2011), Law & Order: Trial by Jury (2005–2006), Law & Order: LA (2010–2011), and Law & Order: Organized Crime (2021–present). International adaptations have been produced in the United Kingdom, France, and Russia.

Format and influence

The shared format — a crime investigated in the first half and prosecuted in the second — together with the distinctive two-note "dun-dun" sound effect, made the franchise a cultural fixture. Collectively the series have produced well over a thousand episodes.
