Amateur sports

Amateur sports are sporting activities pursued without payment for participation, whether for recreation, fitness, education or competition. Amateurism has historically carried social and moral meanings beyond money: in nineteenth-century Britain and the Olympic movement it was often associated with fair play, character and participation for its own sake.

History

Strict amateur rules once barred many working-class athletes and anyone who had accepted prizes, expenses or employment connected with sport. These rules shaped the early Olympic Games and university athletics. Over the twentieth century, state-supported athletes, scholarships, sponsorship and open competition eroded the distinction between amateur and professional status.

Modern forms

Today amateur sport includes school and college programmes, community leagues, masters events, youth clubs and mass-participation races. Governing bodies may still use “amateur” categories in sports such as golf and boxing, while in North American college athletics recent name, image and likeness reforms have further blurred older boundaries.
