Wirral Waters is a large-scale regeneration project developed by the Peel Group on the derelict docklands of Birkenhead and Wallasey, on the Wirral Peninsula in Merseyside, England. First announced in 2006, the scheme covers around 500 acres of former dockland around the Great Float and the Birkenhead Docks, on the western bank of the River Mersey opposite Liverpool.

The project was presented as a counterpart to Peel's Liverpool Waters scheme, with the two together forming a "Mersey Waters" enterprise zone. Proposals included tens of thousands of square feet of office space, thousands of new homes, leisure and cultural facilities, and a projected creation of tens of thousands of jobs over a development period of several decades.

The Birkenhead docks within the Wirral Waters site

Development

Planning permission for the East Float portion of the scheme was granted in 2012. Early phases focused on infrastructure and smaller developments, including office buildings at Tower Wharf, educational facilities, and the Hythe building, and later residential schemes. The Maritime Knowledge Hub, based around the former Birkenhead hydraulic tower and engine house, was developed as a centre for maritime businesses and training. Progress has at times been slower than originally projected, and the scheme has been delivered in phases with support from local enterprise zone status and public funding.
