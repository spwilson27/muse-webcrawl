List of Major League Baseball no-hitters

A no-hitter is a game in which one team was not able to record a single hit through at least nine innings. More than 300 no-hitters have been thrown in Major League Baseball history. Nolan Ryan holds the record with seven; Sandy Koufax threw four. Twenty-four pitchers have thrown perfect games, in which no batter reached base at all.
