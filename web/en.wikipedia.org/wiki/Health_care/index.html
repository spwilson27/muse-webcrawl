Health care

Health care, or healthcare, is the improvement or maintenance of health via the prevention, diagnosis, treatment, amelioration, or cure of disease, illness, injury, and other physical and mental impairments in people. Health care is delivered by health professionals and allied health fields, including medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, nursing, midwifery, psychology, and many therapy professions.

Levels of care

Health systems are commonly organised into primary care (first-contact, generalist care), secondary care (specialist care, often in hospitals), tertiary care (highly specialised treatment in referral centres), and quaternary care (advanced experimental interventions). Public health addresses population-level prevention, sanitation, and health promotion.

Systems and access

Countries finance health care through varying mixes of public funding, insurance, and out-of-pocket payment; universal health coverage is a stated goal of the World Health Organization. Access, cost, quality, and workforce shortages are persistent challenges, and outcomes vary widely between countries. Global health expenditure accounts for roughly a tenth of world economic output.
