Nephroid

In geometry, a nephroid (from the Greek nephros, kidney, after its shape) is a specific plane curve whose name means "kidney-shaped". It is a type of epicycloid, generated by a circle of radius a rolling around the outside of a fixed circle of radius 2a.

Equations

With the fixed circle centred at the origin, parametric equations of the nephroid are x = 3a·cos t − a·cos 3t and y = 3a·sin t − a·sin 3t. It has two cusps and total arc length 24a, enclosing an area of 12πa².

Properties

The nephroid can also be defined as a caustic: it is the curve of light reflected inside a circle (or appearing on the surface of coffee in a cylindrical cup) when the light source is at infinity in a suitable direction. It is the catacaustic of a circle for rays from a point on the circumference, and it can be generated as the envelope of circles centred on a given circle and tangent to a diameter.
