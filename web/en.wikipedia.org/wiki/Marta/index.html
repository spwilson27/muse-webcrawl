Marta

Marta Vieira da Silva (born 19 February 1986), known mononymously as Marta, is a Brazilian professional footballer who plays as a forward. Widely regarded as one of the greatest female footballers of all time, she was named FIFA World Player of the Year six times — five of them consecutively between 2006 and 2010, and again in 2018.

Career

Born in Dois Riachos, Alagoas, Marta was discovered playing youth football and joined Vasco da Gama before moving to Sweden's Umeå IK in 2004, where she won the UEFA Women's Cup and became a star of the Damallsvenskan. She later played in the United States (Los Angeles Sol, FC Gold Pride, Western New York Flash), for Santos and Rosengård, and from 2017 for the Orlando Pride in the NWSL.

International career

With Brazil, Marta is the all-time top scorer at the FIFA Women's World Cup, with 17 goals — a record for any player, male or female, at World Cup finals. She won silver medals at the 2004 and 2008 Olympic Games and at the 2007 World Cup, where she also won the Golden Ball and Golden Boot. She became Brazil's all-time leading scorer, surpassing Pelé's national team tally.

Legacy

Known as "Pelé in skirts" early in her career, Marta is celebrated for her dribbling, close control, and finishing, and as an advocate for equality in women's football. In 2018 she was appointed a UN Goodwill Ambassador for women and girls in sport.
