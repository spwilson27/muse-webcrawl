Cambridge Assessment International Education

Cambridge Assessment International Education (CAIE), often known simply as Cambridge International, is an international qualifications provider affiliated with the University of Cambridge. It is part of Cambridge University Press & Assessment.

Qualifications

CAIE provides the Cambridge Pathway for learners aged 3 to 19, including Cambridge Primary, Lower Secondary, IGCSE, O Level, and International AS & A Level qualifications. Its syllabuses are offered in more than 10,000 schools in over 160 countries, making it the world's largest provider of international education programmes for school students.

History

The organisation descends from the University of Cambridge Local Examinations Syndicate, founded in 1858. It adopted the name Cambridge Assessment International Education in 2017 following the restructuring of Cambridge Assessment.
