CME Group

CME Group Inc. (Chicago Mercantile Exchange Group) is an American financial services company operating the world's largest derivatives marketplace. Headquartered in Chicago, it runs the CME, CBOT, NYMEX, and COMEX exchanges, offering futures and options on interest rates, equity indexes, foreign exchange, energy, metals, and agricultural commodities.

History

The Chicago Mercantile Exchange was founded in 1898 as the Chicago Butter and Egg Board. CME demutualised and went public in 2002, merged with the Chicago Board of Trade in 2007 to form CME Group, and acquired NYMEX (including COMEX) in 2008. Most trading now occurs electronically on the CME Globex platform; open-outcry floors have largely closed, with options pits remaining longer.

Operations

CME Clearing clears and guarantees trades, and the group co-owns the S&P Dow Jones Indices joint venture. Benchmark products include E-mini S&P 500 futures, Eurodollar and SOFR futures, and WTI crude oil futures.
