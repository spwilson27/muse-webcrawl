IEEE 802.15

Standards for wireless personal area networks

A wireless personal area network (WPAN)

IEEE 802.15 is a working group of the IEEE 802 standards committee that specifies standards for wireless personal area networks (WPANs) — short-range, low-power radio networks for devices in and around a person's workspace. Its task groups have produced standards underlying several widely used technologies.

Task groups

802.15.1 standardised the Bluetooth physical and link layers. 802.15.4 specifies low-rate WPANs (LR-WPANs) and forms the basis for Zigbee, Thread, WirelessHART and 6LoWPAN; numerous amendments (such as 802.15.4g for smart utility networks and 802.15.4z for secure ranging) have extended it. Other groups addressed high-rate WPANs (802.15.3), ultra-wideband and body-area networks (802.15.6), and visible light communication (802.15.7).

Applications

WPAN standards are used in home automation, industrial sensing, medical monitoring, smart metering and the Internet of Things, where low cost and long battery life matter more than raw speed.
