Ecosystem services

Ecosystem services are the many and varied benefits to humans provided by the natural environment and healthy ecosystems. The Millennium Ecosystem Assessment (2005) grouped them into provisioning, regulating, cultural, and supporting services.

Categories

Provisioning: food, fresh water, timber, fibre, medicines. Regulating: climate regulation, flood control, disease regulation, water purification, pollination. Cultural: recreation, aesthetic, spiritual, educational values. Supporting: nutrient cycling, soil formation, primary production.

Valuation

Economists attempt to value services — e.g., Costanza et al. estimated global services in tens of trillions of dollars annually — to inform policy, payments for ecosystem services (PES), and natural capital accounting, though valuation is contested.

Threats

Biodiversity loss, land-use change, and climate change degrade services; the IPBES global assessment (2019) warned many are declining.
