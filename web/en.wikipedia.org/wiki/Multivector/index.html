Multivector

In mathematics, a multivector is an element of the exterior algebra of a vector space, or equivalently of a geometric algebra. Multivectors generalise vectors: just as a vector can be viewed as a directed line segment, a bivector represents an oriented plane element, a trivector an oriented volume element, and so on.

Grades

A multivector can be decomposed into parts of different grade: a scalar part (grade 0), a vector part (grade 1), a bivector part (grade 2), and higher grades up to the dimension of the space. A multivector of a single grade k is called a k-vector.

Products

Multivectors can be combined with the exterior (wedge) product, which raises grade, and in geometric algebra with the geometric product, which underlies applications to rotations, electromagnetism and relativistic physics.
