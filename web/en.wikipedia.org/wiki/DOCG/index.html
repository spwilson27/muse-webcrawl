DOCG

DOCG
Full name: Denominazione di origine controllata e garantita
Country: Italy
Level: Highest Italian wine classification

DOCG — Denominazione di origine controllata e garantita — is the highest quality classification for Italian wines, denoting controlled and guaranteed designation of origin.

Requirements

DOCG wines must meet all the rules of the DOC category (defined production area, grape varieties, yields and methods) and additionally pass stricter controls, including a government tasting and analysis, with individually numbered seals on each bottle. The first DOCG wines, including Barolo, Barbaresco, Brunello di Montalcino and Chianti, were recognised in 1980.

Context

Italy classifies wines in ascending tiers as IGT, DOC and DOCG; by the 2020s there were more than 70 DOCG appellations across the country's regions.
