Sognefjord
Location: Vestland, Norway
Length: approx. 205 km
Max. depth: over 1,300 m

The Sognefjord is the largest and deepest fjord in Norway and the second-longest in the world, stretching about 205 kilometres (127 mi) inland from the North Sea coast to the village of Skjolden, beneath the Jotunheimen mountains.

Geography

The fjord reaches depths exceeding 1,300 metres (4,300 ft), while its threshold near the mouth is shallow. Its branches include the Lustrafjord and the Nærøyfjord, a UNESCO World Heritage Site noted for its dramatic scenery. Villages along the fjord include Balestrand, Vik, and Flåm, the terminus of the famous Flåm Railway.

Tourism and transport

The Sognefjord is a major tourist destination, served by express boats from Bergen and crossed by one of the world's longest free-spanning power-line crossings. Fruit farming, especially apples and berries, is traditional along its sheltered shores.
