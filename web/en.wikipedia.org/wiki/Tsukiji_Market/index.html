Tsukiji Market

Tsukiji Market
Location: Tsukiji, Chuo, Tokyo, Japan
Opened: 1935
Inner market closed: 6 October 2018

Tsukiji Market was a major wholesale food market in the Tsukiji district of Chuo, Tokyo, Japan. Operated by the Tokyo Metropolitan Government, it was the largest wholesale fish and seafood market in the world, handling over 2,000 tonnes of marine products per day at its peak and employing tens of thousands of people.

History

The market was established in 1935, replacing an earlier market in Nihonbashi that had been destroyed in the Great Kanto earthquake of 1923. It consisted of an inner market (jonai) where licensed wholesalers conducted the famous early-morning tuna auctions, and an outer market (jogai) of small retail shops and restaurants open to the public.

Relocation

On 6 October 2018 the inner wholesale market closed and its operations moved to the new Toyosu Market on an artificial island in Tokyo Bay. The outer market at Tsukiji remained in place and continues to operate as a popular food and tourist destination.
