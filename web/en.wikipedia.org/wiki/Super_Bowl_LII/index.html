Super Bowl LII

Super Bowl LII was an American football game played on February 4, 2018, at U.S. Bank Stadium in Minneapolis, Minnesota, to determine the champion of the National Football League (NFL) for the 2017 season. The National Football Conference (NFC) champion Philadelphia Eagles defeated the American Football Conference (AFC) champion and defending champion New England Patriots, 41–33, to win their first Super Bowl title and first NFL championship since 1960.

Game summary

The game set a Super Bowl record for total yards gained (1,151 combined) and, famously, featured only one punt. Backup quarterback Nick Foles, who had taken over after starter Carson Wentz tore his ACL in December, threw for 373 yards and three touchdowns and caught a one-yard touchdown pass on the "Philly Special" trick play just before halftime — one of the most celebrated calls in Super Bowl history. Foles was named Super Bowl MVP. Patriots quarterback Tom Brady passed for a Super Bowl-record 505 yards in defeat.

Philadelphia's defense finally broke through late: Brandon Graham strip-sacked Brady with just over two minutes remaining, and the Eagles recovered to run down the clock. A last-second Hail Mary by Brady fell incomplete, sealing the upset.

Entertainment

Pink sang the national anthem and Justin Timberlake performed the halftime show, his third Super Bowl halftime appearance. The broadcast on NBC drew about 103 million U.S. viewers. The Eagles' victory parade in Philadelphia drew massive crowds along Broad Street to the Art Museum steps.
