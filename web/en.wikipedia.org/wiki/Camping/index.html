Camping

Camping
DefinitionOutdoor recreational activity away from home in a tent or shelter
SettingsNational parks, forests, mountains, coasts
[Image: A glowing tent under a starry sky beside a campfire in the mountains]

Camping is a form of outdoor recreation in which participants take temporary residence in a tent, caravan or primitive structure in natural areas — forests, mountains, lakeshores. It ranges from survivalist minimalism to 'glamping'.

Practice

Camping skills (fire, knots, navigation — historically taught by scouts) and ethos (Leave No Trace) protect wild places for the conservation system of parks. Millions camp yearly; equipment from lightweight plastics to GPS satellites reshaped a pastime once defined by canvas and woodsmoke.
