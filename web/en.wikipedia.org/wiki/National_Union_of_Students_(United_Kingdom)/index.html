National Union of Students (United Kingdom)

The National Union of Students (NUS) is a confederation of students' unions in the United Kingdom. Founded in 1922, it represents students in further and higher education, campaigning on issues such as tuition fees, student funding, accommodation and welfare.

History

The NUS was established in 1922 following the Inter-Varsity Association. Through the 20th century it grew into the principal national voice of British students. It played a visible role in campaigns against the introduction and later increases of tuition fees in England, including the student protests of 2010.

Structure

Individual students' unions affiliate to the NUS, which is governed by its national conference and national executive. Separate organisations exist in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland (NUS-USI). The NUS also runs commercial services, including the TOTUM discount card (formerly NUS Extra).
