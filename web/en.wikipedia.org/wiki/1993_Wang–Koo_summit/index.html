1993 Wang–Koo summit
Date27–29 April 1993
LocationSingapore
ParticipantsWang Daohan (ARATS) and Koo Chen-fu (SEF)

The 1993 Wang–Koo summit was a meeting held in Singapore from 27 to 29 April 1993 between Wang Daohan, chairman of mainland China's Association for Relations Across the Taiwan Straits (ARATS), and Koo Chen-fu, chairman of Taiwan's Straits Exchange Foundation (SEF). It was the first high-level, quasi-official meeting between the two sides of the Taiwan Strait since 1949.

Background

The summit followed the informal understandings reached in Hong Kong in 1992 between the two organizations, later known as the "1992 Consensus". Although the talks deliberately avoided political questions about sovereignty, they represented a significant thaw in Cross-Strait relations after four decades of separation.

Agreements

The two sides signed four agreements concerning the verification of documents, the compensation of lost registered mail, and the establishment of regular contact mechanisms between ARATS and the SEF. The summit set a precedent for later Cross-Strait negotiations, including a second Wang–Koo meeting in Shanghai in 1998.
