Le Monde is a French daily newspaper founded in 1944 by Hubert Beuve-Méry at the request of Charles de Gaulle, and France's newspaper of record. An evening paper printed in Paris, it is known for sober design, long analytical articles, foreign reporting and investigative work, and is the flagship of the Groupe Le Monde.

Character

Le Monde's journalists historically held a stake and a say in the paper's governance through their society of editors. Its investigations (often with the ICIJ consortium) and its 'Les Décodeurs' fact-checking desk make it central to French public life; a successful digital subscription business rebuilt its finances in the 2010s.
