Escape (Journey album)

Genre: Rock, hard rock

Length: 42:46

Label: Columbia

Producer: Kevin Elson, Mike Stone

Escape (stylised as E5C4P3) is the seventh studio album by the American rock band Journey, released on July 17, 1981, by Columbia Records. It is the band's most commercially successful studio album, reaching number one on the Billboard 200 and producing four hit singles, including "Don't Stop Believin'", which became one of the best-selling digital tracks of the twentieth century.

Background

Escape was the first album to feature keyboardist Jonathan Cain, who replaced founding member Gregg Rolie. Cain, formerly of The Babys, brought a pop-oriented songwriting sensibility that, combined with guitarist Neal Schon's playing and singer Steve Perry's vocals, defined the album's sound. The record was co-produced by Kevin Elson and Mike Stone and recorded at Fantasy Studios in Berkeley, California.

Singles

The album produced the singles "Who's Crying Now" (No. 4 on the Billboard Hot 100), "Still They Ride" (No. 19), "Open Arms" (No. 2 for six weeks, the band's biggest hit), and "Don't Stop Believin'" (No. 9 on release, later achieving enormous renewed popularity through its use in film and television, including the final episode of The Sopranos in 2007).
