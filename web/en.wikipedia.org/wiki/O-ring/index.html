O-ring

O-rings of various sizes.

An O-ring, also known as a packing or a toric joint, is a mechanical gasket in the shape of a torus — a loop of elastomer with a round cross-section — designed to be seated in a groove and compressed during assembly between two or more parts, creating a seal at the interface.

Design and use

O-rings are among the simplest and most versatile seals, used in static and dynamic applications throughout machinery, plumbing and hydraulics. They are typically made of nitrile rubber, silicone, fluorocarbon (Viton) or other elastomers chosen for temperature and chemical resistance.

Failures

The catastrophic failure of an O-ring seal in a solid rocket booster joint, worsened by cold weather, caused the Space Shuttle Challenger disaster in 1986.
