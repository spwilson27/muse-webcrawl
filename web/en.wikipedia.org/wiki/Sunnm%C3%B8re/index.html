Sunnmøre

Sunnmøre is a traditional district in the southernmost part of Møre og Romsdal county in Western Norway. It comprises the municipalities surrounding the town of Ålesund — the district's principal urban centre — including Hareid, Ulstein, Ørsta, Volda, Sykkylven, Stranda, Norddal, Stordal, Skodje, Giske, Haram, and Sula, among others.

Geography

Sunnmøre is characterized by a rugged coastline of fjords and islands, and by the Sunnmørsalpene (Sunnmøre Alps), a range of sharp alpine peaks rising directly from the fjords, which is popular for skiing and hiking. The Hjørundfjorden and Geirangerfjorden — the latter a UNESCO World Heritage Site — are among its best-known fjords. The island bird sanctuary of Runde is famous for its puffin colonies.

Economy

Historically the district's economy rested on fishing and fish processing; in modern times the maritime and offshore industries — shipbuilding, ship design, and equipment manufacture around Ålesund, Ulsteinvik, and Fosnavåg — are dominant, alongside tourism.
