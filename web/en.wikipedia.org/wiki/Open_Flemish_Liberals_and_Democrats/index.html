Open Flemish Liberals and Democrats

Open Flemish Liberals and Democrats (Dutch: Open Vlaamse Liberalen en Democraten, Open Vld) is a Flemish liberal political party in Belgium. It is the Flemish counterpart of the francophone Reformist Movement (MR) and a member of the European ALDE/Renew Europe family. Its colour is blue.

History

The party descends from the unitary Liberal Party and its Flemish successor, the PVV. In 1992, under Guy Verhofstadt, it relaunched as the VLD with an economically liberal, reformist platform; Verhofstadt served as Prime Minister of Belgium from 1999 to 2008. The party adopted the "Open Vld" name in 2007 after merging with smaller liberal groupings. Alexander De Croo, party leader from 2009 and later Prime Minister (2020–2025), is among its leading figures.

Ideology

Open Vld advocates free markets, lower taxes, individual liberty, social liberalism on ethical questions, and pro-European integration, combined with Flemish autonomy within a federal Belgium. The party suffered heavy losses in the 2024 elections and moved into opposition at the federal level.
