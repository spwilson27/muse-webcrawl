List of nuclear reactors

This is a list of nuclear reactors worldwide — power reactors, research reactors, and naval propulsion reactors. Around 440 power reactors operate in some 30 countries, supplying about a tenth of the world's electricity.

Types

Most power reactors are pressurised water reactors (PWR), followed by boiling water reactors (BWR), pressurised heavy-water reactors (CANDU/PHWR), and smaller numbers of gas-cooled and fast breeder designs. The IAEA's PRIS database maintains the authoritative register of power reactors.

Research reactors

More than 200 research reactors in about 50 countries produce isotopes and support experiments; notable examples include the High Flux Isotope Reactor (US) and OPAL (Australia).
