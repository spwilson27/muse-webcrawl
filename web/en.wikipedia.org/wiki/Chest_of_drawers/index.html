Chest of drawers

A chest of drawers, also called (especially in North American English) a dresser or a bureau, is a type of cabinet that has multiple parallel, horizontal drawers generally stacked one above another. Chests of drawers have traditionally been used for storing clothing and other personal items, and are commonly placed in bedrooms.

History

The form developed from the medieval chest: by the late 17th century, chests fitted with drawers became common in Europe, evolving into tallboys, lowboys, and commodes in successive furniture styles.

Construction

Traditional examples are made of wood with dovetailed drawer boxes; modern versions may be flat-packed from manufactured boards. Safety standards now address tip-over risks, especially for children's furniture.
