Avis Car Rental

Avis Car Rental, LLC is an American car rental agency headquartered in Parsippany, New Jersey. Together with Budget Rent a Car, Budget Truck Rental and Zipcar, it is part of the Avis Budget Group. Avis operates rental locations in North America, South America, Europe, Africa, Asia and Australasia, and is one of the largest car rental companies in the world.

History

Avis was founded in 1946 by Warren Avis, who opened the first rental location at Willow Run Airport in Ypsilanti, Michigan, with three cars. It is credited as the first company to operate car rentals from airport locations. The company grew rapidly in the 1950s through franchising and acquisitions.

In 1963 Avis launched its famous advertising campaign built around the slogan "We Try Harder", created by the agency Doyle Dane Bernbach after Avis acknowledged it was only number two in the industry behind Hertz. The campaign is regarded as one of the most successful in advertising history and was used for decades.

Operations

Avis markets itself primarily to the premium commercial and leisure segments of the rental market, while its sister brand Budget targets value-oriented customers. The company offers a loyalty programme, Avis Preferred, and services such as Avis Prestige for luxury vehicles. Avis Budget Group acquired the car-sharing company Zipcar in 2013 for approximately $500 million.
