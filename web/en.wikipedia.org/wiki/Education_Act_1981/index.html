Education Act 1981

The Education Act 1981 is an act of the Parliament of the United Kingdom that reformed the provision of education for children with special educational needs in England and Wales. It implemented many of the recommendations of the Warnock Report of 1978.

Provisions

The Act introduced the term "special educational needs" (SEN) in place of earlier categories of handicap, and required local education authorities to identify and assess children who might need special provision. For children with significant needs, the authority had to issue a formal "statement" specifying the provision to be made — the origin of the system of statementing. The Act established a presumption that children with SEN should be educated in mainstream schools where parents wished it and where it was compatible with efficient education for other children, promoting the principle of integration.

Legacy

The 1981 Act's framework was consolidated in later legislation, including the Education Act 1993 and the Special Educational Needs and Disability Act 2001. In England, statements were eventually replaced by Education, Health and Care (EHC) plans under the Children and Families Act 2014.
