5.1 surround sound

5.1 surround sound is the common name for surround sound audio systems with five full-bandwidth channels and one low-frequency effects channel (the ".1"). It is the standard layout for home theatre systems and was long the dominant format for digital cinema sound.

Channel layout

The five main channels are front left, centre, front right, surround left, and surround right. The centre channel carries most dialogue, while the LFE channel feeds a subwoofer for low-frequency effects such as explosions.

Formats and applications

5.1 audio is delivered by codecs including Dolby Digital (AC-3), DTS, and AAC, and is standard on DVDs, Blu-ray discs, broadcast television, and streaming services. It was first used theatrically in the early 1990s and became widespread with the DVD era.
