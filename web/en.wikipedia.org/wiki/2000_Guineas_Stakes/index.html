The 2000 Guineas Stakes is a Group 1 flat horse race in Great Britain open to three-year-old thoroughbred colts and fillies. It is run on the Rowley Mile at Newmarket over a distance of one mile, usually in late April or early May. It is the first of Britain's five Classic races and the first leg of the English Triple Crown, followed by the Derby and the St Leger.

History

The race was first run in 1809 and takes its name from the original prize fund of 2,000 guineas. Its fillies' counterpart, the 1000 Guineas, followed in 1814. Together the two races open the British Classics season and are centrepieces of Newmarket's Guineas Festival.

Notable winners

Famous winners include Brigadier Gerard (1971), Nijinsky (1970, the last horse to complete the English Triple Crown), Dancing Brave (1986), Sea the Stars (2009) and Frankel (2011), whose fifteen-length demolition of the field is often called the greatest Guineas performance of all. Fillies rarely contest the race, preferring the 1000 Guineas, but a few, such as Sceptre (1902), have won it.

Records

The leading jockey is Jem Robinson with nine wins in the nineteenth century; in modern times Lester Piggott won the race five times. Trainers Aidan O'Brien and John Gosden have both enjoyed repeated success in the twenty-first century.
