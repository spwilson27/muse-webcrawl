Sir Russell Coutts (born 1 March 1962) is a New Zealand sailor who is one of the most successful competitors in the history of the America's Cup and an Olympic gold medallist. He won the Finn class gold medal at the 1984 Los Angeles Olympic Games and has won the America's Cup multiple times as skipper and later as a team chief executive.

Career

Coutts skippered Team New Zealand's Black Magic to victory in the 1995 America's Cup in San Diego, winning 5–0, and successfully defended the Cup in Auckland in 2000. He then famously moved to the Swiss challenger Alinghi, winning the Cup again in 2003, and later joined Oracle Team USA as CEO, overseeing Cup wins in 2010 and the dramatic comeback victory of 2013 in San Francisco.

Later ventures

After leaving Oracle, Coutts co-founded the SailGP international racing series, launched in 2019, featuring national teams racing identical foiling F50 catamarans. He was appointed a Knight Companion of the New Zealand Order of Merit for services to yachting.
