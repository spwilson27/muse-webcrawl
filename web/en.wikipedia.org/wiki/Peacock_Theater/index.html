The Peacock Theater (formerly the Nokia Theatre L.A. Live and Microsoft Theater) is a music and theatre venue in the L.A. Live complex in downtown Los Angeles, California, at the intersection of Chick Hearn Court and Figueroa Street. The theater seats 7,100 and hosts concerts, award shows, and special events.

History

The venue opened on 18 October 2007 with concerts by the Eagles and the Dixie Chicks, as the Nokia Theatre L.A. Live, part of the L.A. Live entertainment district developed by AEG adjacent to the Staples Center (now Crypto.com Arena). In June 2015, the naming rights passed to Microsoft and the venue became the Microsoft Theater. In 2023, NBCUniversal's streaming service Peacock acquired the naming rights, and the theater was renamed the Peacock Theater; the adjoining plaza became Peacock Place.

Events

The theater is a regular home of televised award ceremonies, including the Primetime Emmy Awards, the ESPY Awards, the American Music Awards, the BET Awards, and the People's Choice Awards, as well as video game events associated with E3 and The Game Awards. It also hosts concerts, boxing, and the graduation ceremonies of local institutions.
