Arc System Works

Arc System Works Co., Ltd. is a Japanese video game developer and publisher based in Yokohama. Founded in 1988 by Minoru Kidooka, the company is best known for its 2D fighting game franchises Guilty Gear and BlazBlue.

Founded: 1988
Headquarters: Yokohama, Japan
Industry: Video games
Known for: Guilty Gear, BlazBlue, Dragon Ball FighterZ

History and works

Arc System Works spent its early years doing contract work for other publishers before gaining recognition with Guilty Gear (1998). In the 2010s the company earned acclaim for combining hand-drawn-style 3D graphics with fast-paced gameplay in Guilty Gear Xrd and Guilty Gear Strive, and for developing Dragon Ball FighterZ (2018) with Bandai Namco and Granblue Fantasy: Versus. It also acquired the rights to Technōs Japan series such as Double Dragon and River City.
