Morrisons

Wm Morrison Supermarkets Limited, trading as Morrisons, is the fifth largest supermarket chain in the United Kingdom. Founded in 1899 by William Morrison, the company began as an egg and butter merchant in Rawson Market, Bradford, England, and remained based in Bradford, with its head office in the city's Thornbury area.

For most of its history Morrisons concentrated on northern England, but it expanded nationally following its £3 billion takeover of Safeway in 2004, which roughly tripled its store estate, especially in southern England and Scotland. Morrisons was listed on the London Stock Exchange and was a constituent of the FTSE 100 Index until it was taken over by the American private equity firm Clayton, Dubilier & Rice (CD&R) in October 2021 for about £7 billion.

The chain distinguishes itself from rivals by its "Market Street" format, featuring in-store butchers, fishmongers and bakers, and by an unusually high degree of vertical integration: Morrisons owns abattoirs, food-processing plants, bakeries and farms, processing much of its fresh food itself. The company operates several hundred supermarkets and, after acquiring the convenience chain McColl's in 2022, a large estate of Morrisons Daily convenience stores.
