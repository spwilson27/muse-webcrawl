Ineos 1:59 Challenge

Ineos 1:59 Challenge
Date: 12 October 2019
Venue: Vienna, Austria
Athlete: Eliud Kipchoge
Result: 1:59:40.2 for the marathon distance

The Ineos 1:59 Challenge was a staged marathon-distance run held in Vienna, Austria, on 12 October 2019, in which Kenyan long-distance runner Eliud Kipchoge became the first person to cover the marathon distance of 42.195 kilometres in under two hours, finishing in 1 hour, 59 minutes and 40.2 seconds.

Background

The event, sponsored by the chemicals company Ineos, followed Kipchoge's earlier attempt at the distance with Nike's Breaking2 project in Monza in 2017, where he had run 2:00:25. Conditions in Vienna were optimised in every controllable respect: a flat, tree-lined course in the Prater park chosen for mild October weather, a rotating team of world-class pacemakers running in an aerodynamic formation, a laser guide projected from a pace car, drinks delivered by bicycle, and prototype carbon-plated racing shoes.

Status

Because of the rotating pacemakers and other assistance, the performance was not eligible as a world record under World Athletics rules, and Kipchoge's official marathon world record at the time remained his 2:01:39 from Berlin in 2018. The run was nevertheless widely regarded as a landmark in endurance sport.
