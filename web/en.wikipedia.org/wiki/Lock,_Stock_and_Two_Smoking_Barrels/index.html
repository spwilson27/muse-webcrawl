Lock, Stock and Two Smoking Barrels

Lock, Stock and Two Smoking Barrels
Director: Guy Ritchie
Released: 1998
Genre: Crime comedy
Running time: 107 minutes
Country: United Kingdom

Lock, Stock and Two Smoking Barrels is a 1998 British black comedy crime film written and directed by Guy Ritchie in his feature debut. The film tells the story of a young London card sharp who loses £500,000 to a powerful crime lord in a rigged game of three-card brag, and plots with his friends to rob a neighbouring gang to pay the debt within a week.

Plot

Eddy, a talented card player, is staked by his friends Tom, Bacon and Soap in a high-stakes game run by "Hatchet" Harry Lonsdale; cheated, he leaves owing half a million pounds. Overhearing their neighbours planning to rob a marijuana-growing gang, the four decide to ambush the robbers. The scheme entangles them with debt collectors, Harry's enforcer Big Chris, a pair of incompetent thieves who have stolen Harry's prized antique shotguns, and rival criminals — with the storylines converging in farcical violence.

Cast and reception

The ensemble cast includes Jason Flemyng, Dexter Fletcher, Nick Moran and Jason Statham in his film debut, with Vinnie Jones as Big Chris and Sting in a supporting role. Made for about £800,000, the film was a commercial and critical success, launched Ritchie's career, and was followed in a similar style by Snatch (2000).
