Cheese

Cheese
Made fromMilk (cow, goat, sheep, buffalo)
MethodCurdling milk, pressing and ripening curds
VarietiesThousands — Cheddar, Brie, Parmesan, mozzarella, etc.
[Image: A cheese board with wedges of hard, soft and blue cheeses]

Cheese is a dairy product produced in wide ranges of flavours and textures from milk. Curds are separated from whey by acid or rennet, salted, shaped and often aged, during which microbes and moulds develop flavour.

History and varieties

Cheesemaking is ancient — possibly as old as animal husbandry with cattle, goats' kin the goat and sheep — and monasteries refined many European classics. Fresh cheeses (mozzarella, paneer) are eaten young; hard, aged cheeses (Parmigiano-Reggiano) keep for years. Cheese anchors cuisines atop bread and wine pairings.
