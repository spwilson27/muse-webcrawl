Chamois leather

Chamois leather (pronounced "shammy") is a type of porous, absorbent leather traditionally made from the skin of the chamois goat-antelope, now usually from sheepskin split. It is oil-tanned, giving exceptional softness and water absorption.

Uses

Chamois is used for drying and polishing cars, windows and optical lenses, for gloves and orthopaedic products, and historically for filtering petrol. Synthetic chamois cloths mimic its properties.
