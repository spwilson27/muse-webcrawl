Indian Armed Forces

The Indian Armed Forces are the military forces of the Republic of India, comprising the Indian Army, Indian Navy and Indian Air Force, supported by paramilitary and strategic commands.

Indian Armed Forces
Founded15 August 1947
Service branchesArmy, Navy, Air Force
HeadquartersMinistry of Defence, New Delhi
Commander-in-ChiefPresident of India
Active personnelover 1.4 million

Organisation

Supreme command vests in the President; administration rests with the Ministry of Defence. The Chief of Defence Staff, created in 2019, coordinates jointness. India maintains one of the world's largest standing forces and a nuclear triad under the Strategic Forces Command.

History

The forces descend from the British Indian military, partitioned in 1947. They have fought wars with Pakistan in 1947–48, 1965, 1971 and 1999 (Kargil), and with China in 1962, and undertake extensive UN peacekeeping and disaster relief.
