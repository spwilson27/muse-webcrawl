List of forts in Maharashtra

The Indian state of Maharashtra contains more than 350 forts, most of them built on the hills of the Sahyadri (Western Ghats) range. Many are associated with the Maratha king Shivaji, who captured, built, and held dozens of forts as the backbone of his kingdom in the seventeenth century.

Classification

Maharashtra's forts are traditionally classed as hill forts (giridurg), sea forts (jaldurg), and land forts (bhuikot). Many are protected monuments of the Archaeological Survey of India and popular trekking destinations.
