Goffredo Mameli

Goffredo Mameli (5 September 1827 – 6 July 1849) was an Italian patriot, poet, and writer who played a central role in the Risorgimento. He is best known as the author of the lyrics of "Il Canto degli Italiani", Italy's national anthem, also known as "L'Inno di Mameli".

Life

Born in Genoa, Mameli was drawn to Mazzinian republicanism as a youth and wrote poetry celebrating Italian unification. In 1847, aged twenty, he composed the hymn that Michele Novaro set to music; it was first sung in Genoa that December. He joined the defence of the Roman Republic in 1849, serving as an aide to Giuseppe Garibaldi, and was wounded during the French siege of Rome; he died of an infection following amputation at the age of 21.

The anthem

"Il Canto degli Italiani" became the provisional anthem of the Italian Republic in 1946 and was formally recognised by law in 2017.
