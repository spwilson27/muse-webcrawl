The Shin-Meishin Expressway (新名神高速道路, Shin Meishin Kōsokudōro), or "New Meishin Expressway", is an expressway in the Kansai region of Japan, running roughly parallel to the older Meishin Expressway between Nagoya and Kobe. It carries route number E1A under Japan's expressway numbering system and is operated by the Central Nippon Expressway Company (NEXCO Central) and the West Nippon Expressway Company (NEXCO West).

Route

The expressway begins at Yokkaichi Junction in Mie Prefecture, where it branches from the Isewangan Expressway, and runs west through Shiga, Kyoto, and Osaka prefectures to Kobe Junction in Hyōgo Prefecture, where it joins the Sanyō Expressway. It passes north of Kyoto and Osaka, relieving congestion on the Meishin Expressway and improving links between the Chūkyō and Keihanshin metropolitan areas.

History

The first sections opened in 2008, between Kusatsu-Tanakami and Kameyama. Subsequent sections opened progressively, including the stretch between Kameyama and Yokkaichi and between Takatsuki and Kobe (2017–2018). The section between Ōtsu and Jōyō remained under construction into the 2020s, with delays pushing its planned opening later in the decade.
