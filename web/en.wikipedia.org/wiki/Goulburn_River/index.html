Goulburn River

[Image: Goulburn River]

The Goulburn River is a major inland river of the Goulburn Broken catchment, part of the Murray–Darling basin, located in the Alpine, Northern Country/North Central, and Southern Riverina regions of Victoria, Australia. It rises in the Victorian Alps near Woods Point and flows generally north-west for about 654 kilometres to join the Murray River near Echuca.

Course and features

The river is impounded by Lake Eildon, Victoria's second-largest water storage, and by the Goulburn Weir near Nagambie, which diverts water for irrigation. Tributaries include the Delatite, Rubicon, Acheron, Yea, and Broken rivers, and Seven Creeks. Towns along its length include Alexandra, Seymour, Nagambie, Murchison, Shepparton, and Echuca.

Uses

The Goulburn is one of Australia's most heavily regulated rivers, supplying irrigation water to the Goulburn Valley, one of the country's most productive agricultural regions. It is also valued for recreational fishing, particularly for Murray cod and golden perch, and for environmental flows.
