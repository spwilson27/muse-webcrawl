Overview

Lilongwe is the capital and largest city of Malawi, in the country's Central Region. It takes its name from the Lilongwe River, and grew from a small settlement into an administrative centre under British colonial rule. In 1975 President Hastings Kamuzu Banda moved the capital here from Zomba, favouring its central position, and built the Capital Hill government complex and a new city layout.

The city divides between the modern Capital City area — ministries, embassies and the National Assembly — and the congested Old Town with its markets. Lilongwe is the hub of Malawi's tobacco trade, one of the world's largest burley tobacco markets, and is served by Kamuzu International Airport.
