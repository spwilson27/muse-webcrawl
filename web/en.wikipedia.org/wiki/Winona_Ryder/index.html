Winona Ryder (born Winona Laura Horowitz; October 29, 1971) is an American actress. A defining star of late-1980s and 1990s cinema, she rose to prominence with roles in Beetlejuice (1988), Heathers (1989), and Edward Scissorhands (1990), and received Academy Award nominations for The Age of Innocence (1993) and Little Women (1994).

Career

Ryder debuted in Lucas (1986) and became known for portraying quirky, intelligent young women. Her 1990s work included Bram Stoker's Dracula (1992), Reality Bites (1994), and Girl, Interrupted (1999), which she also executive-produced. After a career slowdown in the 2000s, she returned to prominence playing Joyce Byers in the Netflix series Stranger Things (2016–2025), earning a Golden Globe nomination.

Personal life

Named after her birthplace of Winona, Minnesota, she grew up partly in a commune in Mendocino County, California. She has been an advocate for various causes, including the search for Polly Klaas, a kidnapping victim from her hometown of Petaluma. Ryder received a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame in 2000.
