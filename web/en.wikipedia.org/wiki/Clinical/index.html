Clinical

Clinical may refer to:

Medicine

In medicine, clinical describes the direct observation and treatment of patients, as opposed to laboratory or theoretical work. The term derives from the Greek klinikos, pertaining to a bed. Related uses include:

  • Clinical medicine — the practice of medicine based on the direct examination of patients.
  • Clinical trial — a research study in human volunteers designed to evaluate medical, surgical, or behavioural interventions.
  • Clinical endpoint — an outcome measure in a clinical trial that reflects how a patient feels, functions, or survives.
  • Clinical pathology and clinical laboratory science — the diagnosis of disease through laboratory analysis.
  • Clinical psychology — the psychological specialty concerned with assessment and treatment of mental illness.
  • Clinical skills — the skills of history-taking, physical examination, and communication used at the bedside.

Other uses

In general usage, "clinical" can mean precise, detached, or unemotionally efficient, and in sport it describes finishing performed with cold accuracy, as in a "clinical finish". A clinic is a healthcare facility or an instructional session, such as a coaching clinic.
