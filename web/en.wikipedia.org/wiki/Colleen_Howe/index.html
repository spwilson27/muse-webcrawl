Colleen Howe

Colleen Joffa Howe (February 17, 1933 – March 6, 2009) was a pioneering ice hockey executive and sports agent, and the wife of hockey legend Gordie Howe. Known as "Mrs. Hockey", she was one of the first female sports agents and a driving force behind the creation of the World Hockey Association's Houston Aeros, for whom her husband and sons played.

Career

Howe managed her husband's business affairs from the 1950s onward and founded the Detroit Junior Red Wings, the first junior hockey team of its kind in the United States. In 1973 she negotiated the landmark contracts that brought Gordie Howe out of retirement to play alongside sons Mark and Marty with the Houston Aeros of the WHA.

Honours

In 2000 she received the Wayne Gretzky International Award from the United States Hockey Hall of Fame, honouring her contributions to the growth of hockey in the US. The Colleen J. Howe Arena in Sandusky, Michigan is named for her.
