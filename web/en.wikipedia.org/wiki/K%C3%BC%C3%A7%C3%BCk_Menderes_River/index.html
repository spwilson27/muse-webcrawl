The Küçük Menderes ("Little Maeander") is a river in western Turkey, flowing through İzmir Province to the Aegean Sea. In antiquity it was known as the Cayster (Greek: Κάϋστρος, Kaystros), and its plain was part of the ancient region of Lydia and later the Roman province of Asia.

Course

The river rises in the Bozdağ mountains and flows westward past the towns of Ödemiş, Tire, and Bayındır through a fertile alluvial plain used intensively for agriculture, before reaching the Aegean coast near Selçuk, close to the ruins of Ephesus.

History

Alluvium carried by the Cayster progressively silted up the harbour of Ephesus in antiquity, contributing to the city's decline; the ancient coastline now lies several kilometres inland from the archaeological site. The river's name distinguishes it from the larger Büyük Menderes (the ancient Maeander) to the south.
