Amblin Television

Amblin Television is an American television production company and the television division of Amblin Partners/Amblin Entertainment, founded by Steven Spielberg. It develops scripted drama, comedy, limited series and family programming for broadcast networks, cable channels and streaming services.

Productions

Amblin's television credits have ranged from animated series connected with Spielberg's film world to long-running broadcast dramas. Notable productions associated with the banner include ER in its production lineage, Animaniacs and later revivals, The Americans through related Amblin production credits, Halo, Masters of the Air with partner companies, and numerous Steven Spielberg executive-produced series.

Role in the industry

The company illustrates the migration of major film storytellers into prestige television and streaming. It packages literary adaptations, historical subjects and genre concepts with established showrunners, often in co-production with studios and networks.
