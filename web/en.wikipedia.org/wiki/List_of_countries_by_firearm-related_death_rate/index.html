List of countries by firearm-related death rate

This is a list of countries by firearm-related death rate, typically expressed as deaths per 100,000 population per year. Figures combine homicides, suicides and unintentional deaths involving firearms. Rates vary widely between countries, reflecting differences in gun ownership, regulation, and broader social conditions.

Overview

The highest firearm death rates are generally found in parts of Latin America and the Caribbean, where homicide dominates the figures. The United States has the highest rate among high-income countries, driven by both homicide and suicide. Many European and East Asian countries report rates below 1 per 100,000.

Methodology

Data are compiled from sources such as the Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation's Global Burden of Disease study and the Small Arms Survey. Comparability between countries is limited by differences in death registration and classification of undetermined-intent deaths.
