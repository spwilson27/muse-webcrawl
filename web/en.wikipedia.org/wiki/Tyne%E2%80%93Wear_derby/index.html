Tyne–Wear derby

The Tyne–Wear derby, also known as the North East derby, is a local football derby in North East England between Newcastle United and Sunderland, two clubs separated by about 12 miles, with the cities of Newcastle upon Tyne and Sunderland situated on the rivers Tyne and Wear respectively.

History

The rivalry reflects a much older antagonism between the two cities dating back to the English Civil War and the Jacobite risings. The clubs first met in an FA Cup tie in 1888, and their first league meeting took place in 1898. Matches are fiercely contested and the fixture has occasionally been marred by crowd disorder, most notoriously in 1990 and 2013.

Honours and records

Newcastle have generally been the more successful club in league football, while Sunderland famously won 9–1 at St James' Park in 1908, the record margin in the fixture. Sunderland won six consecutive derbies between 2013 and 2015, equalling Newcastle's earlier record run.
