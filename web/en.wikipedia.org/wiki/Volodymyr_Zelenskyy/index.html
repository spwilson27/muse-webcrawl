Volodymyr Oleksandrovych Zelenskyy (born 25 January 1978) is a Ukrainian politician and former comedian and actor who has served as the sixth president of Ukraine since 2019. He gained worldwide prominence as the wartime leader of Ukraine during the Russian invasion that began in February 2022.

Early life and entertainment career

Zelenskyy was born in Kryvyi Rih to a Jewish family; his father was a professor of cybernetics and his mother an engineer. He studied law at the Kyiv National Economic University but never practised as a lawyer. Instead he built a career in comedy: as a student he joined the KVN comedy competition, and his troupe, Kvartal 95, became one of Ukraine's most successful comedy acts. He co-founded the Kvartal 95 production studio, starred in popular films such as Love in the Big City, and voiced Paddington Bear in the Ukrainian dubs of the Paddington films. From 2015 he starred in the television satire Servant of the People, playing a schoolteacher unexpectedly elected president of Ukraine after a rant against corruption goes viral.

Presidency

Zelenskyy announced his candidacy for the real presidency on New Year's Eve 2018, running on an anti-corruption, anti-establishment platform under a party named after his show. He defeated incumbent Petro Poroshenko in the April 2019 run-off with about 73 percent of the vote. His early presidency pursued peace negotiations with Russia over the Donbas war and domestic reforms, while also becoming entangled in the events leading to the first impeachment of U.S. President Donald Trump following a July 2019 phone call.

Russian invasion

When Russia launched its full-scale invasion on 24 February 2022, Zelenskyy refused offers of evacuation, reportedly replying "I need ammunition, not a ride." His nightly video addresses, speeches to foreign parliaments, and visible presence in Kyiv rallied domestic resistance and international military and financial support for Ukraine. He was named Time Person of the Year for 2022. His five-year term formally expired in May 2024, but elections cannot be held under martial law, and he remains in office.
