Senior master sergeant

Senior master sergeant (SMSgt) is the second-highest non-commissioned officer rank in the United States Air Force and United States Space Force, designated pay grade E-8, above master sergeant (E-7) and below chief master sergeant (E-9).

Duties

Senior master sergeants serve as superintendents and senior enlisted leaders of flights, squadrons and staff sections, managing operations, resources and personnel while mentoring junior NCOs and advising officers. The grade was created, together with chief master sergeant, by the Career Compensation Act amendments of 1958, with the first promotions effective in 1959 as the Air Force developed its "supergrade" senior NCO tier.

Promotion

Promotion is by a central board using a whole-person evaluation of performance reports, decorations, fitness and records; by law, only a small percentage of the enlisted force (about 2.5 percent in the E-8 and E-9 grades combined in the Air Force's planning factors) may hold the top two grades. The insignia features five inverted chevrons (rockers) below and two above a central star within a wreath-adorned design distinguishing the senior NCO grades.
