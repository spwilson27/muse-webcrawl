KB Toys

KB Toys (stylised K·B Toys, and formerly Kay Bee Toys) was an American chain of mall-based retail toy stores. Founded in 1922 in Pittsfield, Massachusetts, by the Kaufman brothers as a wholesale candy business, it grew into one of the largest toy retailers in the United States, operating over 1,300 stores at its peak.

The company was known for its small mall outlets and closeout pricing. It filed for bankruptcy in 2004, emerged under new ownership, and filed again in December 2008, liquidating its remaining stores in early 2009.

The brand name was later acquired by Strategic Marks, which announced plans to revive it; in 2018 the name was used to open pop-up stores in malls following the closure of Toys "R" Us.
