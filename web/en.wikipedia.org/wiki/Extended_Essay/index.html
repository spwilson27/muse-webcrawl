Part ofIB Diploma Programme core

The Extended Essay (EE) is a mandatory component of the International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme. It is an independent research paper of up to 4,000 words on a topic of the student's choice, chosen from the list of available Diploma subjects, undertaken with the guidance of a supervisor.

Requirements

Students formulate a focused research question, gather and evaluate sources, and present a structured, referenced argument. The process includes three mandatory reflection sessions, the last of which is a short concluding interview (viva voce); reflections are recorded on the Reflections on Planning and Progress Form.

Assessment

The essay is externally assessed against criteria covering focus and method, knowledge and understanding, critical thinking, and presentation, for a maximum of 34 marks converted to a grade from A to E. Combined with Theory of Knowledge, the Extended Essay contributes up to three bonus points toward the Diploma total; a grade of E in either is a failing condition.

Purpose

The IB intends the essay to prepare students for undergraduate research by developing skills in academic writing, citation, time management, and intellectual engagement with a subject in depth.
