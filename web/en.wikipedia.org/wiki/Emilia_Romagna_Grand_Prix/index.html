The Emilia Romagna Grand Prix is a Formula One motor race held at the Autodromo Internazionale Enzo e Dino Ferrari in Imola, Italy. It was introduced to the World Championship calendar in 2020, when the COVID-19 pandemic calendar reshuffle brought Formula One back to Imola for the first time since 2006, and is named after the Emilia-Romagna region.

History

Imola previously hosted the Italian Grand Prix in 1980 and the San Marino Grand Prix from 1981 to 2006; the circuit is also remembered for the deaths of Ayrton Senna and Roland Ratzenberger at the 1994 San Marino Grand Prix. The Emilia Romagna Grand Prix was first held on 1 November 2020 as a two-day event, won by Lewis Hamilton. Subsequent editions were held in 2021 (won by Max Verstappen), 2022 (Verstappen) and 2024 (Verstappen). The 2023 race was cancelled because of severe flooding in the region. Major sponsorship has seen the race billed with the "Made in Italy" name in its official title.
