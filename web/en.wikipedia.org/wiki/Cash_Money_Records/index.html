Cash Money Records, Inc. is an American record label founded in New Orleans, Louisiana, in 1991 by brothers Bryan "Birdman" Williams and Ronald "Slim" Williams. It became one of the most successful independent hip hop labels of the late 1990s and 2000s.

History

The Williams brothers built the label around local New Orleans bounce music before signing a landmark distribution deal with Universal Records in 1998, worth a reported $30 million, under which Cash Money retained ownership of its masters. The label's Hot Boys — Juvenile, Lil Wayne, B.G. and Turk — brought national success, with Juvenile's 400 Degreez (1998) going multi-platinum, driven by the single "Back That Azz Up".

Young Money and later years

In the 2000s, Lil Wayne rose to superstardom, and his imprint Young Money Entertainment, launched in 2005 as a joint venture with Cash Money, signed Drake and Nicki Minaj, who became two of the biggest recording artists in the world. The label's roster also included producer Mannie Fresh, whose sound defined its early era. Cash Money has been involved in numerous artist disputes over royalties and contracts, including high-profile litigation with Lil Wayne, settled in 2018.
