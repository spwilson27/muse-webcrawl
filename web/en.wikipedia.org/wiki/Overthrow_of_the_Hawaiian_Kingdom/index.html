Overthrow of the Hawaiian Kingdom

The overthrow of the Hawaiian Kingdom began on January 16, 1893, with a coup d'état by the Committee of Safety, a group of Hawaiian subjects and foreign nationals, against Queen Liliʻuokalani, ending on January 17 with her abdication, under protest, in response to landing of U.S. Marines.

The Provisional Government of Hawaii was established, followed by the Republic of Hawaii, and annexation by the United States in 1898. In 1993 the U.S. Congress passed the Apology Resolution acknowledging the role of U.S. agents.
