Ernie Ball Inc. is an American manufacturer of electric and acoustic guitar strings and musical instrument accessories. It was founded by musician and entrepreneur Ernie Ball (born Roland Sherwood Ball, 1930–2004) and is famous for its Slinky brand of guitar strings.

History

Ball opened one of the first shops in the United States devoted exclusively to guitars, in Tarzana, California, in 1957. Noticing that students struggled with the heavy string gauges then standard, he had custom sets made with a lighter third string; by the early 1960s he was selling "Slinky" string sets by mail order nationwide, and endorsements from players such as Jimmy Page and Eric Clapton spread the brand.

Music Man and later ventures

In 1984 the company acquired Music Man, the instrument maker co-founded by Leo Fender, and produces its guitars and basses — including the StingRay — in San Luis Obispo, California. Ernie Ball also runs the annual Battle of the Bands and long sponsored stages on touring festivals. After Ernie Ball's death in 2004, the company remained family-owned under his son Sterling Ball.
