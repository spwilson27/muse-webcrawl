Apple Pencil

The Apple Pencil is a line of stylus pens developed by Apple Inc. for use with its iPad tablet computers. It is designed for drawing, note-taking, and precise input, offering pressure sensitivity, tilt detection, and low latency.

Developer: Apple Inc.
Type: Stylus
Introduced: 2015 (first generation)
Compatible with: iPad

Generations

The first-generation Apple Pencil was announced in September 2015 alongside the iPad Pro. It charges via a Lightning connector and pairs over Bluetooth. The second-generation model, released in 2018, attaches magnetically to the side of compatible iPads for charging and pairing, and supports a double-tap gesture to switch tools. An Apple Pencil with USB-C was introduced in 2023 as a lower-cost option, and the Apple Pencil Pro followed in 2024 with additional features such as squeeze gestures, barrel roll, and haptic feedback.

Features

Apple Pencil supports palm rejection, allowing users to rest their hand on the screen while drawing. Later models support hover previews on some iPads, showing where the tip will land before it touches the display.
