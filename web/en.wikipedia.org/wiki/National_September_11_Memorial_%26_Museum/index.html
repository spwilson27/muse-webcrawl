National September 11 Memorial & Museum

Location: World Trade Center site, New York City
Opened: Memorial 2011; Museum 2014
Design: Michael Arad, Peter Walker

The National September 11 Memorial & Museum (the 9/11 Memorial & Museum) commemorates the September 11, 2001 terrorist attacks and the 1993 World Trade Center bombing. It stands at the World Trade Center site in Lower Manhattan, where the Twin Towers once stood.

The memorial

The memorial, titled Reflecting Absence, consists of two immense waterfall pools set within the footprints of the Twin Towers, surrounded by bronze panels inscribed with the names of the 2,977 victims of the 2001 attacks and the six victims of the 1993 bombing. A plaza of swamp white oak trees surrounds the pools.

The museum

The underground museum, opened in 2014, exhibits artifacts including damaged steel, the "Survivors' Stairs," and personal effects, and presents historical and commemorative exhibitions about the attacks and their aftermath.
