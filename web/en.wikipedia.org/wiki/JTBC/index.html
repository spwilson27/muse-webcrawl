JTBC
Country: South Korea
Launched: 1 December 2011
Owner: JoongAng Group
Headquarters: Seoul

JTBC (short for Joongang Tongyang Broadcasting Company) is a South Korean nationwide pay television network. It was launched on 1 December 2011 as one of four new generalist cable networks, and is owned by the JoongAng Group, the media conglomerate behind the JoongAng Ilbo newspaper.

Programming

JTBC is known for its news programme JTBC Newsroom, variety shows such as Knowing Bros, and highly rated television dramas. Its drama SKY Castle (2018–19) achieved some of the highest ratings in Korean cable television history at the time, and The World of the Married (2020) surpassed it, becoming the highest-rated drama in Korean cable history.

News coverage

The network's reporting in 2016 on the tablet computer evidence in the Choi Soon-sil scandal contributed to the events leading to the impeachment of President Park Geun-hye, and significantly raised the channel's profile and credibility.
