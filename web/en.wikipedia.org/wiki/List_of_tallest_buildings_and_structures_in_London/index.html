List of tallest buildings and structures in London

This is a list of the tallest buildings and structures in London, the capital of the United Kingdom. London's skyline was historically dominated by church spires and then by St Paul's Cathedral; since the 1960s, and especially since 2000, clusters of skyscrapers have grown in the City of London, Canary Wharf, and increasingly in districts such as Vauxhall, Elephant and Castle and Croydon. The tallest building is The Shard, completed in 2012, at 310 metres (1,016 ft).
