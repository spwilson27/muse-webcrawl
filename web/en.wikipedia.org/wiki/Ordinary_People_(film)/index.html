Ordinary People (film)

Ordinary People
Directed by: Robert Redford
Written by: Alvin Sargent (from the novel by Judith Guest)
Starring: Donald Sutherland, Mary Tyler Moore, Timothy Hutton, Judd Hirsch
Release: 1980 (Paramount Pictures)

Ordinary People is a 1980 American drama film directed by Robert Redford in his directorial debut, adapted by Alvin Sargent from the 1976 novel by Judith Guest. It stars Donald Sutherland, Mary Tyler Moore, Timothy Hutton, and Judd Hirsch.

Plot

The Jarretts are an affluent family in Lake Forest, Illinois, shattered by the death of their elder son, Buck, in a sailing accident. The younger son, Conrad, who survived the accident, is consumed by guilt and has recently returned home after a suicide attempt and psychiatric hospitalisation. Working with a psychiatrist, Dr. Berger, Conrad slowly confronts his feelings, while his mother Beth — who favoured Buck — retreats into cold denial, and his father Calvin struggles to hold the family together.

Reception and awards

The film was a major critical success and won four Academy Awards, including Best Picture, Best Director for Redford, Best Supporting Actor for Hutton — at 20, the youngest winner in that category — and Best Adapted Screenplay. Moore's performance as the emotionally withholding Beth was widely praised as a dramatic departure from her television persona.
