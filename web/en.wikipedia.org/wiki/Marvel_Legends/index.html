Marvel Legends

Marvel Legends is an action figure line based on the characters of Marvel Comics, initially produced by Toy Biz, then by Hasbro. The line initially began in 2002 as a spin-off of the Spider-Man Classics line and followed the same paradigm: highly articulated 6-inch-scale figures with detailed sculpts, packaged with a comic book or poster and, in later assortments, with parts to assemble a larger "Build-A-Figure".

History

Toy Biz launched Marvel Legends in 2002 with a first series including Captain America, Iron Man, the Hulk, and Toad. The line was credited with revitalizing the collector-oriented superhero toy market through its emphasis on articulation (often more than 30 points), character breadth spanning A-list heroes and obscure villains, and chase variants. Popular Toy Biz Build-A-Figures included Galactus, a Sentinel, and Apocalypse.

In 2006 Hasbro acquired the Marvel toy licence, and from January 2007 the line continued under Hasbro after a transitional period that fans sometimes view as a decline in paint and plastic quality; the line was relaunched with renewed collector focus in 2014 as the Marvel Legends Infinite Series, tying assortments to Marvel Studios films and comic themes, each wave including a Build-A-Figure. Packaging was redesigned several times, including plastic-free windowless packaging in 2022–23 that was partly reversed in response to collector criticism.

Build-A-Figure

The Build-A-Figure (BAF) concept, in which each figure in a wave includes a limb or component of a much larger figure, became the line's signature. It encouraged completion of entire waves and was widely imitated across the action-figure industry. Larger deluxe and "HasLab" crowdfunded items, such as a 26-inch Sentinel and Galactus, have extended the line's scale beyond retail assortments.
