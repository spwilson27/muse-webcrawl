Alan Michael Sugar, Baron Sugar (born 24 March 1947) is a British business magnate, media personality, author and politician. He founded the consumer electronics company Amstrad and has hosted the BBC reality series The Apprentice since 2005.

Amstrad

Sugar founded Amstrad (Alan Michael Sugar Trading) in 1968, initially as an importer and wholesaler of electrical goods. The company became a major British success in the 1980s with affordable hi-fi systems and the Amstrad CPC home computer range, followed by word processors that undercut rivals. Amstrad was floated on the London Stock Exchange in 1980; Sugar sold the business to BSkyB in 2007.

Football and television

Sugar was chairman of Tottenham Hotspur Football Club from 1991 to 2001, a sometimes controversial tenure during the formation of the Premier League. As the frontman of The Apprentice, adapted from the American series, his blunt boardroom style — summed up by his catchphrase "You're fired!" — made him one of British television's best-known business figures.

Politics

Sugar was elevated to the House of Lords as a life peer in 2009 and served as the Labour government's enterprise champion. He later resigned from the Labour Party in 2015 while remaining a crossbench-affiliated peer in public debate.
