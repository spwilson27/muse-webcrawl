Harris Corporation was an American technology company, defense contractor and information technology services provider headquartered in Melbourne, Florida. It produced wireless equipment, tactical radios, electronic systems, night vision equipment and both terrestrial and spaceborne antennas for use in the government, defense and commercial sectors.

History

The company traced its origins to the Harris Automatic Press Company of Niles, Ohio, founded in 1895, which made lithographic printing presses. It entered electronics through acquisitions, including Radiation, Inc. of Melbourne, Florida, in 1967, and sold the printing business in the 1980s. In 2015 Harris acquired Exelis Inc. for about $4.75 billion, significantly expanding its defense electronics and geospatial businesses. The StingRay phone-surveillance devices made by Harris attracted public attention and litigation over police use of cell-site simulators.

Merger

In October 2018 Harris and L3 Technologies announced an all-stock "merger of equals"; the combination was completed on 29 June 2019, creating L3Harris Technologies, one of the largest defense contractors in the United States, with headquarters in Melbourne.

Products

Harris was best known for its Falcon family of tactical military radios, for public-safety and professional communications systems, for air traffic control communications, and for large deployable space antennas flown on communications and surveillance satellites.
