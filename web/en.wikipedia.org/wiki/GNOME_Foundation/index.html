GNOME Foundation

The GNOME Foundation is a non-profit organization based in San Francisco, California, United States, which coordinates the efforts of the GNOME project — the free and open-source desktop environment for Linux and other Unix-like systems. It was founded in 2000 to provide organizational, financial, and legal support.

The foundation is governed by a board of directors elected annually by its members, who are contributors to GNOME. It employs staff including an executive director, organizes conferences such as GUADEC, holds the GNOME trademarks, and receives funding from companies including Red Hat, Google, and from individual donations.
