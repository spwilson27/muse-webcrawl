Jardine Matheson

Image placeholder: Jardine Matheson

Jardine Matheson Holdings Limited is a Hong Kong–based Bermuda-incorporated diversified conglomerate. Founded in Canton (Guangzhou) in 1832 by Scotsmen William Jardine and James Matheson, the firm was one of the original Hong Kong trading houses, or hongs, and remains controlled by the Keswick family, descendants of Jardine's nephew.

History

Jardine, Matheson & Co. was founded on 1 July 1832 in Canton, trading in tea, silk, and — most lucratively — opium smuggled into China. The firm played a central role in lobbying the British government before the First Opium War, and after the Treaty of Nanking (1842) it established itself at East Point in Hong Kong. Jardines became the colony's pre-eminent tai-pan house, with interests extending into shipping, insurance, railways, and textiles across Asia.

In the 20th century the group expanded and diversified, listing in Hong Kong and later restructuring with primary listing in London and incorporation in Bermuda in 1984 amid uncertainty ahead of Hong Kong's handover.

Businesses

The Jardine Matheson Group today includes Jardine Pacific, Jardine Motors, Jardine Lloyd Thompson interests historically, Hongkong Land, Dairy Farm (DFI Retail Group), Mandarin Oriental hotels, Jardine Cycle & Carriage, and a major stake in Astra International of Indonesia. Its principal listed holding structure is organized through a cross-shareholding with Jardine Strategic Holdings.
