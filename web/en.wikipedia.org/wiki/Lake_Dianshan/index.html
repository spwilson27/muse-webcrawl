Lake Dianshan

Dianshan Lake (Chinese: 淀山湖; pinyin: Diànshān Hú) is a freshwater lake west of Shanghai in eastern China. Most of the lake lies in the Qingpu District of Shanghai, with its western shore in Jiangsu Province.

Geography

The lake covers about 62 square kilometres and is the source of the Huangpu River, Shanghai's principal waterway. It is a major drinking-water source for the metropolis and is surrounded by water towns such as Zhujiajiao.

Recreation

Dianshan Lake is popular for sailing, rowing, and lakeside resorts, and hosts water-sports training facilities.
