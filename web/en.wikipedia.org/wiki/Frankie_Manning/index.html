Frankie Manning

Frankie Manning (May 26, 1914 – April 27, 2009) was an American dancer, instructor, and choreographer. Considered one of the founding fathers of Lindy Hop, he is widely credited with introducing the first aerial (or "airstep") to the dance in 1935 and with leading the Savoy Ballroom troupe Whitey's Lindy Hoppers to international fame.

Early life

Manning was born in Jacksonville, Florida, and moved with his family to Harlem, New York, as a child. His mother was a dancer, and Manning began attending dances at the Savoy Ballroom as a teenager, where he quickly became one of the venue's most respected dancers.

Career

In 1935, Manning and his partner Frieda Washington performed the first aerial step in a dance competition at the Savoy, reportedly back-flipping over Manning's back to defeat reigning champions Shorty George Snowden and Big Bea. The move revolutionized social partner dancing.

Manning joined Whitey's Lindy Hoppers, the professional troupe organized by Herbert "Whitey" White, and served as its choreographer. The troupe toured internationally and appeared in films including A Day at the Races (1937) and Hellzapoppin' (1941), whose Lindy Hop sequence is regarded as one of the finest dance performances ever filmed.

Later life and revival

After serving in the U.S. Army during World War II, Manning worked for the U.S. Postal Service for about thirty years. In the mid-1980s, as interest in swing dancing revived, he was persuaded to return to teaching. He spent the rest of his life travelling the world teaching Lindy Hop, and served as a consultant and choreographer for the film Malcolm X (1992). His autobiography, Frankie Manning: Ambassador of Lindy Hop, co-written with Cynthia Millman, was published in 2007.

Legacy

Manning received a Tony Award in 1989 for his choreography of the Broadway musical Black and Blue. The annual Frankie Manning Foundation and worldwide celebrations of his birthday (Frankie Fest) continue his legacy. He died in Manhattan on April 27, 2009, one month before his 95th birthday.
