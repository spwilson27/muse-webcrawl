The Color Purple

The Color Purple
Author: Alice Walker
Published: 1982
Awards: Pulitzer Prize for Fiction & National Book Award (1983)
Adaptations: 1985 Spielberg film; stage musical; 2023 film

The Color Purple is a 1982 epistolary novel by American author Alice Walker. It tells, through letters to God and to her sister Nettie, the story of Celie, a poor Black woman in rural Georgia surviving abuse and finding selfhood, faith and independence in the early 20th century.

Themes and reception

Exploring racism, sexism, domestic and sexual violence, and spirituality, it won the Pulitzer Prize — Walker was the first Black woman to win for fiction — but has frequently been challenged and banned for its content.

Adaptations

Steven Spielberg's 1985 film starred Whoopi Goldberg and Oprah Winfrey; a Broadway musical (2005) led to the 2023 musical film.
