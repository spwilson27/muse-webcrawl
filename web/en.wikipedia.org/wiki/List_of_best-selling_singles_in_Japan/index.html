List of best-selling singles in Japan

This is a list of the best-selling singles in Japan, based on figures from Oricon, which has tracked physical single sales since 1968. Japan was for decades the world's largest market for physical singles, and numerous releases have sold over two million copies; idol groups such as AKB48 dominated the charts in the 2010s with multiple million-selling singles.

Japan's physical singles market remained unusually strong into the streaming era.

The AKB48 era

AKB48 and its sister groups achieved a string of million-selling singles in the 2010s, including "Sayonara Crawl" (2013), "Koi Suru Fortune Cookie" (2013), "Labrador Retriever" (2014), "Kibouteki Refrain" (2014), and "Teacher Teacher" (2018), aided by handshake-event tickets bundled with CDs. By Oricon's count, AKB48 became the best-selling female group in Japanese singles history.

Digital era

From the late 2010s, digital downloads and streaming grew; Kenshi Yonezu's "Lemon" (2018) became the first song certified triple-million by the RIAJ for downloads, and Official HIGE DANdism, YOASOBI, and Ado achieved major digital hits.
