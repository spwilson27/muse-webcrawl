Service desk

A service desk is the single point of contact between a service provider, such as an IT department, and its users. It handles incidents, service requests, problems, and general communication, aiming to restore normal service as quickly as possible while minimising impact on the organisation.

Functions

Typical service desk responsibilities include logging and categorising tickets, first-line diagnosis and resolution, escalation to specialist teams, password resets, access requests, and keeping users informed of progress. Many operate tiered support models (levels 1–3) and track performance through service-level agreements (SLAs).

Frameworks

The service desk is a core function described by IT service management frameworks such as ITIL, which distinguishes the service desk from the narrower technical help desk by its broader, business-focused scope. Modern service desks increasingly use self-service portals, chatbots, and automation.
