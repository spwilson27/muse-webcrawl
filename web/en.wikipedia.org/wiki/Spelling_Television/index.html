Spelling Television

Spelling Television Inc. was an American television production company founded by Aaron Spelling and the successor to Aaron Spelling Productions. Headquartered in Los Angeles, it produced many of the most successful American prime-time series of the 1980s and 1990s.

History

Aaron Spelling built his production empire through Spelling-Goldberg Productions and Aaron Spelling Productions, producing hits such as Charlie's Angels, The Love Boat, Dynasty, Fantasy Island, and T.J. Hooker. In the early 1990s the company, then publicly traded, was renamed Spelling Entertainment and its television arm became Spelling Television, producing Beverly Hills, 90210, Melrose Place, 7th Heaven, and Charmed.

Later years

Viacom acquired control of Spelling Entertainment in 1994 after buying Blockbuster, and the company was later folded into Paramount Television; after the CBS–Viacom split of 2005, Spelling Television became part of CBS Paramount Television (now CBS Studios), which retains its library.
