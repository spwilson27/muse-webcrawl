Kyle Broflovski

Kyle Broflovski is a fictional character in the animated television series South Park, created by Trey Parker and Matt Stone and voiced by Stone. One of the show's four main child protagonists, Kyle is distinguished by his green ushanka-style hat, orange jacket, strong moral sense and frequent clashes with Eric Cartman, particularly over Kyle's Jewish identity.

Character

Kyle is generally the most thoughtful and conscientious of the four boys, often delivering the show's concluding moral ("I learned something today..."). He is intelligent, sceptical and quick-tempered, and his friendship with Stan Marsh — the pair are "best friends forever" — forms the emotional core of many episodes. His parents, Gerald (an attorney) and Sheila (an activist prone to moral panics), and his adopted Canadian baby brother Ike feature frequently.

Background

The character is partly based on co-creator Matt Stone, who is Jewish and raised in Colorado. Kyle's catchphrases include "Oh my God, they killed Kenny!" (shared with Stan) and indignant responses to Cartman's antisemitic taunts, which long-running storylines treat as a constant of his character.

In popular culture

A 2016 episode cycle gave Kyle a storyline as the internet persona "Skankhunt42"'s chief pursuer... later seasons developed his father's double life as the troll. Kyle remains one of the most recognisable figures of the franchise across its films, including South Park: Bigger, Longer & Uncut (1999).
