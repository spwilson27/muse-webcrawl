Pakistan movement

Pakistan Movement
Key body: All-India Muslim League
Leaders: Muhammad Ali Jinnah, Allama Iqbal, Liaquat Ali Khan
Outcome: Partition of India, Pakistan created 14 Aug 1947

The Pakistan movement was the political movement in British India that sought a separate Muslim homeland, culminating in the creation of Pakistan in 1947 under the two-nation theory.

Origins

Roots lie in Syed Ahmad Khan's Aligarh movement, the founding of the Muslim League (1906), separate electorates (1909), and Iqbal's 1930 Allahabad Address. Chaudhry Rahmat Ali coined "Pakistan" in 1933. The Lahore Resolution (1940) demanded independent Muslim-majority states.

Towards Partition

Under Muhammad Ali Jinnah, the League triumphed in 1945–46 elections. Failed Cabinet Mission talks and communal violence led to the Mountbatten Plan and the Partition of India, accompanied by mass migration and killings.
