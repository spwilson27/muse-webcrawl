Federal Council (Switzerland)

The Federal Council (German: Bundesrat) is the federal cabinet of the Swiss Confederation: a seven-member executive council that constitutes the federal government and serves as the collective head of state of Switzerland.

Composition and election

Councillors are elected by the United Federal Assembly for four-year terms, traditionally distributed among the major parties under the informal "magic formula" (1959–2003) and among language regions. Each councillor heads one of the seven federal departments. The President of the Swiss Confederation, elected annually from among the seven, chairs the Council as first among equals.

Decision-making

The Council decides collectively; decisions are defended publicly by all members under the principle of collegiality, regardless of personal views.
