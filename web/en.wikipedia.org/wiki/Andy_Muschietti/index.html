Andy Muschietti

Andrés Muschietti (born August 26, 1973), professionally known as Andy Muschietti, is an Argentine filmmaker. He is best known for directing the horror films Mama (2013), It (2017) and It Chapter Two (2019), the former two of which rank among the highest-grossing horror films of all time, and the DC superhero film The Flash (2023). He frequently collaborates with his sister, producer Barbara Muschietti.

Early life and early career

Muschietti was born in Vicente López, a suburb of Buenos Aires, Argentina. He worked in the Argentine film industry and made the short films Nostalgia en la mesa 8 (1999) and Mamá (2008), the latter a three-minute horror short that attracted the attention of Guillermo del Toro.

Breakthrough: Mama and It

With del Toro producing, Muschietti expanded his short into the feature Mama (2013), starring Jessica Chastain and Nikolaj Coster-Waldau, which grossed over $146 million worldwide. He was then hired by Warner Bros. and New Line Cinema to direct It (2017), based on Stephen King's 1986 novel. Made for about $35 million, It grossed over $700 million and became the highest-grossing horror film of all time at its release. The sequel, It Chapter Two (2019), grossed a further $473 million.

Later work

Muschietti directed The Flash (2023) for DC Films, with Ezra Miller as Barry Allen and Michael Keaton returning as Batman. He and his sister run the production company Nocturna. Warner Bros. announced Muschietti as director of the Batman film The Brave and the Bold for the DC Universe franchise, and he served as an executive producer of the HBO prequel series It: Welcome to Derry (2025), having directed several episodes.
