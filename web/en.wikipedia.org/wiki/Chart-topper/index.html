Chart-topper

A chart-topper is a song or album that reaches number one on a music popularity chart, such as the Billboard Hot 100 in the United States or the UK Singles Chart. The term may also be used more broadly for any recording that leads a published chart.

Usage

Chart-toppers are a common measure of commercial success in the music industry. Some songs hold the top position for many weeks; others reach number one only briefly. The phrase is also used figuratively in other media for works that lead sales or popularity rankings.
