Rate of return regulation

Rate of return regulation is a system of pricing for public utilities, such as electricity and gas networks, in which the regulator sets prices so that the utility can recover its operating costs and capital costs while earning a fair return on its asset base (rate base).

Critique

The Averch–Johnson effect predicts that rate-of-return regulation can encourage over-investment in capital, since profit scales with the rate base. Alternatives include price cap regulation (RPI−X), which gives stronger efficiency incentives, and revenue cap approaches. Rate cases before commissions determine allowed returns based on cost of debt and equity.
