Zvartnots International Airport (Armenian: Զվարթնոց միջազգային օդանավակայան) (IATA: EVN, ICAO: UDYZ) is located near Zvartnots, 15 km west of Yerevan, the capital city of Armenia. It is the main international airport of Armenia and Yerevan's main international transport hub, the busiest airport in the country.

History

The airport was opened in 1961. The original terminal, designed in the Soviet modernist style with a distinctive circular concourse and control tower, won a USSR architects' competition in 1970. After Armenian independence, a new cargo terminal opened in 1998, and in 2001 the government signed a 30-year concession with Armenia International Airports CJSC, operated by Corporación América Airports.

A new arrivals hall opened in 2006 and a new departures and arrivals building in 2011, bringing capacity to about 3.5 million passengers per year. The old circular terminal was closed in 2011 and its fate has been debated; despite demolition plans, the concessionaire's proposal was rejected by Yerevan's urban planning council, and the building — an icon of Soviet Armenian architecture — remains standing but unused.

Facilities and operations

The airport has a single runway, 09/27, 3,850 metres long, equipped with an ILS CAT II system allowing operations in low visibility. It serves as the hub for Armenian carrier Armenia Aircompany and hosts flights by airlines including Air France, Lufthansa, Austrian Airlines, LOT Polish Airlines, Qatar Airways, flydubai, and Wizz Air, connecting Yerevan with destinations across Europe, Russia, and the Middle East.

Passenger traffic passed 3 million for the first time in 2023, driven by growth in transit traffic and new routes. Ground transport includes buses and taxis to central Yerevan; the airport takes its name from the nearby seventh-century Zvartnots Cathedral, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
