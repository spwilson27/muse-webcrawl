The 2016 NFL season was the 97th season of the National Football League. It began on 8 September 2016 with a Super Bowl 50 rematch between the Denver Broncos and Carolina Panthers, and concluded with Super Bowl LI on 5 February 2017 at NRG Stadium in Houston.

Season highlights

The season marked the Rams' return to Los Angeles after two decades in St. Louis. On the field, the Dallas Cowboys — led by rookie quarterback Dak Prescott and rookie running back Ezekiel Elliott — went 13–3 to take the NFC's top seed, while the Oakland Raiders returned to the playoffs for the first time since 2002. Atlanta Falcons quarterback Matt Ryan won league MVP, and Colin Kaepernick's national-anthem protests became a major national story from the preseason onward.

Super Bowl LI

The New England Patriots met the Atlanta Falcons in the championship game. Trailing 28–3 late in the third quarter, the Patriots produced the greatest comeback in Super Bowl history, scoring 25 unanswered points to force the first overtime in Super Bowl history, then winning 34–28 on a James White touchdown run. Tom Brady, who had missed the first four games through the Deflategate suspension, was named Super Bowl MVP for a record fourth time, and the title was the Patriots' fifth.
