Blue Sky Studios, Inc. was an American visual effects and computer animation studio based in Greenwich, Connecticut. Founded in 1987 by staff from the defunct MAGI/Synthavision, it was a subsidiary of 20th Century Fox from 1997. It is best known for the Ice Age and Rio franchises, and for films including Robots (2005), Horton Hears a Who! (2008), The Peanuts Movie (2015), Ferdinand (2017) and Spies in Disguise (2019).

History

Blue Sky pioneered its proprietary renderer CGI Studio, notable for ray-traced lighting. Its short film Bunny (1998) won the Academy Award for Best Animated Short Film. The studio's first feature, Ice Age (2002), directed by Chris Wedge and co-directed by Carlos Saldanha, was a major success and spawned four sequels, making Ice Age one of the highest-grossing animated franchises of all time. The studio was also famous for Scrat, the hapless sabre-toothed squirrel whose acorn-chasing shorts opened most Ice Age films.

Closure

Following Disney's acquisition of 21st Century Fox in 2019, Blue Sky became part of Walt Disney Studios. In February 2021 Disney announced the studio's closure, citing the economic impact of the COVID-19 pandemic, and it ceased operations on April 10, 2021, with about 450 employees affected. Its final completed feature was Spies in Disguise (2019); the unfinished Nimona was later completed by Annapurna Pictures and DNEG and released by Netflix in 2023.
