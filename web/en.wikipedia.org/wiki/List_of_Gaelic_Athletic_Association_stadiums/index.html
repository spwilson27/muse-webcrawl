List of Gaelic Athletic Association stadiums

This is a list of stadiums used by the Gaelic Athletic Association (GAA) for Gaelic games — Gaelic football, hurling, and camogie — in Ireland. County grounds host inter-county championship matches. The largest, and the association's headquarters stadium, is Croke Park in Dublin, with a capacity of 82,300, making it one of the largest stadiums in Europe.
