List of Marvel Comics villains

This is a list of villainous characters appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Many of these characters have also appeared in films, television series, and video games of the Marvel Cinematic Universe and other adaptations.

Marvel's rogues galleries are among the most extensive in comics.

Spider-Man villains

Spider-Man's rogues gallery includes the Green Goblin, Doctor Octopus, Venom, Carnage, Mysterio, the Vulture, Electro, Sandman, the Lizard, Kraven the Hunter, the Rhino, Scorpion, Shocker, Hobgoblin, and Morbius.

X-Men villains

The X-Men's adversaries include Magneto, Apocalypse, Mystique, Sabretooth, Juggernaut, the Sentinels, Mister Sinister, the Hellfire Club, and Dark Phoenix.
