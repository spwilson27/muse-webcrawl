Richards Bay Coal Terminal

The Richards Bay Coal Terminal (RBCT) is a coal export terminal in the port of Richards Bay, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa. It is one of the largest single coal export terminals in the world and the principal outlet for South African steam coal exports.

History and operations

The terminal opened in 1976 with an initial capacity of 12 million tonnes per annum. Successive expansion phases raised its design capacity to about 91 million tonnes per year. Coal arrives by rail from mines in Mpumalanga and neighbouring provinces on Transnet's coal line, is stockpiled and blended, and is loaded onto bulk carriers by shiploaders for export mainly to Asia and Europe.

Ownership

RBCT is owned by a consortium of South African coal mining companies, whose export allocations are proportional to their shareholdings. In recent years actual throughput has been constrained below nameplate capacity by bottlenecks and derailments on the rail corridor.
