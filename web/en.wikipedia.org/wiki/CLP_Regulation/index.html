CLP Regulation

The CLP Regulation (Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008) is a European Union regulation on the classification, labelling and packaging of substances and mixtures. It entered into force on 20 January 2009 and implements in the EU the United Nations' Globally Harmonized System of Classification and Labelling of Chemicals (GHS).

Scope and requirements

Under the CLP Regulation, manufacturers, importers and downstream users must classify substances and mixtures according to harmonized hazard classes and categories (physical, health, and environmental hazards), label them with hazard pictograms, signal words ("Danger" or "Warning"), hazard statements (H-statements) and precautionary statements (P-statements), and package them safely. Suppliers must notify the European Chemicals Agency (ECHA) of classifications for the Classification and Labelling Inventory.

The regulation gradually replaced the earlier Dangerous Substances Directive (67/548/EEC) and Dangerous Preparations Directive (1999/45/EC), becoming fully applicable to mixtures on 1 June 2015. It works alongside the REACH Regulation, which governs registration and evaluation of chemicals.
