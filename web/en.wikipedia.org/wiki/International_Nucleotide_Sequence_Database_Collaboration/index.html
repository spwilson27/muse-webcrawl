International Nucleotide Sequence Database Collaboration

The International Nucleotide Sequence Database Collaboration (INSDC) is a long-standing collaboration between the three principal public repositories of DNA and RNA sequence data: the DNA Data Bank of Japan (DDBJ), the European Nucleotide Archive (ENA) at the European Molecular Biology Laboratory's European Bioinformatics Institute, and GenBank at the US National Center for Biotechnology Information.

Function

The three databases exchange data daily, so a sequence submitted to any one of them appears in all three under a shared accession number system. Scientific journals generally require authors to deposit new sequences in an INSDC database before publication, making the collaboration the central archive of the world's publicly funded sequencing output.

Policy

Data in the INSDC databases are freely available without restriction, a policy rooted in the Bermuda Principles and Fort Lauderdale agreements on rapid release of genome sequence data.
