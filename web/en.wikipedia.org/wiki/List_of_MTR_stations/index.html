List of MTR stations

The Mass Transit Railway (MTR) is the rapid transit system of Hong Kong, operated by the MTR Corporation. The network comprises heavy rail lines, the Airport Express, the Light Rail in the north-west New Territories, and an intercity connection at Hong Kong West Kowloon station. This article lists the stations of the heavy rail network.

Interchanges and boundary stations

Major interchange stations include Admiralty (four lines), Central–Hong Kong (pedestrian subway link), Kowloon Tong, Kim Xi (Diamond Hill) and Tai Wai. East Rail line trains cross the boundary with mainland China at Lo Wu and Lok Ma Chau stations. The network carries around five million passenger journeys on a typical weekday, and the MTR Corporation also operates the Light Rail network of 68 stops in Tuen Mun, Yuen Long and Tin Shui Wai.
