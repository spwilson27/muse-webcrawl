International Cinematographers Guild

The International Cinematographers Guild (ICG, IATSE Local 600) is a US labour union representing cinematographers, camera operators, camera assistants, still photographers and other camera professionals in film and television.

History

Local 600 was formed in 1996 by merging camera locals from New York, Chicago and Los Angeles/Hollywood into one national local within the IATSE. It negotiates contracts, safety standards and training, and presents the Emerging Cinematographer Awards.

Safety advocacy

The guild has campaigned on set safety and long working hours, issues highlighted by the death of camera assistant Sarah Jones in 2014 and the 2021 Rust shooting.
