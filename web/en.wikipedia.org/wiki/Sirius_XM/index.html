Sirius XM Holdings Inc. is an American broadcasting company headquartered in Midtown Manhattan, New York City, that provides satellite radio and online radio services across the United States and Canada. It was formed by the 2008 merger of Sirius Satellite Radio and XM Satellite Radio, the two licensed satellite radio operators in the United States, creating a monopoly provider of satellite radio in North America.

History

XM launched its service in 2001 and Sirius in 2002; both companies spent heavily on content, signing deals with sports leagues, news networks, and celebrities — most famously Howard Stern, who joined Sirius in 2006 in a landmark $500 million agreement. Neither company reached profitability competing against the other, and in February 2007 they announced a merger. After a lengthy regulatory review, the FCC approved the combination in July 2008. The merged company later acquired the streaming service Pandora in 2019 for $3.5 billion and invested in podcasting, acquiring Stitcher and Conan O'Brien's podcast network. In 2024 Sirius XM completed a restructuring transaction with Liberty Media that simplified its ownership.

Service

SiriusXM broadcasts more than 400 channels of music (mostly commercial-free), sports (including NFL, MLB, NBA, NHL, and NASCAR play-by-play), news, talk, comedy, and entertainment via geostationary satellites and a network of terrestrial repeaters, supplemented by internet streaming apps. Receivers are factory-installed in the majority of new vehicles sold in North America, and the company has roughly 33 million self-pay and promotional subscribers. Its best-known personalities include Howard Stern, whose show anchors its talk lineup.
