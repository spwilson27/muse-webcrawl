Indo-Pakistani War of 1947–1948

Date: 22 October 1947 – 1 January 1949

Location: Princely state of Jammu and Kashmir

Result: UN-mediated ceasefire; Kashmir divided along the Line of Control

The Indo-Pakistani War of 1947–1948, also called the First Kashmir War, was fought between India and Pakistan over the princely state of Jammu and Kashmir following the partition of British India in 1947.

Background

Kashmir's Hindu maharaja, Hari Singh, initially sought independence, but after tribal militias from Pakistan invaded in October 1947 he signed the Instrument of Accession to India, and Indian troops were airlifted to Srinagar.

Course

Fighting spread across the Kashmir Valley, Jammu, Ladakh, and Gilgit-Baltistan. India referred the dispute to the United Nations, and a UN-brokered ceasefire took effect on 1 January 1949.

Outcome

The ceasefire line — later the Line of Control — left India controlling about two-thirds of the former princely state, including the Kashmir Valley, Jammu, and Ladakh, and Pakistan the remainder. Kashmir remains disputed.
