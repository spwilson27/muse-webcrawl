Nazareth is a borough in Northampton County, Pennsylvania, United States, in the Lehigh Valley region. Founded in 1740 by Moravian settlers on land purchased from George Whitefield, it was named after the biblical town of Nazareth. The borough is known as the home of Martin Guitar and as the hometown of the Andretti racing family.

History

The Moravian Church purchased the 5,000-acre Barony of Nazareth tract in 1740, and the community was restricted to Moravian residents for more than a century. Nazareth Hall, a manor house built in 1756, became a Moravian boarding school. The borough was incorporated in 1858. Cement production became a major industry in the surrounding area in the late nineteenth and twentieth centuries.

Economy and culture

C. F. Martin & Company, founded in 1833 and headquartered in Nazareth since 1839, manufactures Martin acoustic guitars, among the most respected in the world; its factory offers public tours. Nazareth Speedway, a paved oval, hosted major auto racing events until its closure in 2004. Mario Andretti, the Formula One and IndyCar champion, has long lived in the borough.
