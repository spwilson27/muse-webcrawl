The Dewan Rakyat (English: House of Representatives; literally "People's Hall") is the lower house of the Parliament of Malaysia. Its 222 members, known as Members of Parliament (MPs), are elected by single-member constituencies using the first-past-the-post system for terms of up to five years.

Functions

The Dewan Rakyat is the dominant chamber: the prime minister and most ministers are drawn from it, and it originates money bills and most legislation. Bills passed by the Dewan Rakyat go to the Dewan Negara (Senate) for review before receiving royal assent from the Yang di-Pertuan Agong. The chamber is presided over by a Speaker elected from among members or qualified outsiders.

Membership

Candidates must be Malaysian citizens at least 18 years old following the 2019 Undi18 constitutional amendment, which also lowered the voting age to 18. The Dewan Rakyat is dissolved by the King on the prime minister's advice, precipitating a general election.
