Louisville Presbyterian Theological Seminary

Louisville Presbyterian Theological Seminary (LPTS), also known as Louisville Seminary, is a seminary affiliated with the Presbyterian Church (USA) and located in Louisville, Kentucky. It is one of ten theological institutions connected to the denomination and is accredited by the Association of Theological Schools.

History

The seminary traces its origins to 1853, when classes began in Danville, Kentucky, as the Danville Theological Seminary. A second institution, Louisville Theological Seminary, was founded in Louisville in 1893, and the two merged in 1901 on the Louisville campus. The seminary's present campus in the Crescent Hill neighbourhood, centred on Gardencourt, a former estate, dates from the early twentieth century.

Academics

Louisville Seminary offers the Master of Divinity, Master of Arts in Religion, Master of Arts in Marriage and Family Therapy, and Doctor of Ministry degrees. The institution is known for its work in marriage and family therapy education and for pastoral care, and it has emphasised inclusivity and interfaith engagement in its programming. Students come from Presbyterian and other denominations. See also Presbyterian Church (USA).
