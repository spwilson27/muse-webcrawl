Sun 'n Fun

The SUN 'n FUN Aerospace Expo is a nonprofit airshow and fly-in held annually each spring at Lakeland Linder International Airport in Lakeland, Florida. One of the largest aviation events in the world, it is often described as "spring break for pilots" and is second in scale in the United States only to EAA AirVenture Oshkosh.

History

The first Sun 'n Fun fly-in was held in January 1975, organized by members of the local Experimental Aircraft Association chapter and other aviation groups in central Florida. The event grew steadily, moving to its permanent site on the southwest side of Lakeland Linder Airport, and was shifted to spring dates. It now draws more than 200,000 visitors and thousands of aircraft from dozens of countries over six days.

The event

Attractions include daily airshows with aerobatic performers and military demonstration teams such as the U.S. Navy Blue Angels and the Air Force Thunderbirds, hundreds of commercial exhibitors, educational forums and workshops, and aircraft judging in categories including homebuilt, warbird, vintage and light-sport aircraft. Fly-in camping under the wing is a signature feature.

Aerospace Center for Excellence

Proceeds support the Aerospace Center for Excellence (ACE), an aviation education campus on the grounds that includes the Florida Air Museum and programs introducing young people to aviation and STEM careers, including flight scholarships through the James C. Ray Flight School.
