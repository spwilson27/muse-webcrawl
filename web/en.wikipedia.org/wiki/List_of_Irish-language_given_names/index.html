List of Irish-language given names

This is a list of given names in the Irish language. Many Irish names were historically anglicised — sometimes phonetically, sometimes by substitution with an unrelated English name — during centuries of English-language administration in Ireland. The late 20th century saw a revival of Irish-language naming, and names such as Saoirse, Aoife, and Oisín are now among the most popular baby names in Ireland.
