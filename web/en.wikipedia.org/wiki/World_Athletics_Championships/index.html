World Athletics Championships

The World Athletics Championships are a biennial global athletics competition organised by World Athletics (formerly the IAAF), ranking alongside the Olympic Games as the sport's premier championship.

History

The first edition was held in Helsinki in 1983, after the IAAF decided to stage its own world championships separate from the Olympics. Early editions were quadrennial (Rome 1987, Tokyo 1991); from Stuttgart 1993 the event settled into a two-year cycle. Hosts have included Paris, Osaka, Berlin, Beijing, London, Doha, Oregon (2022), Budapest (2023) and Tokyo (2025).

Programme

Athletes compete in track and field, road running and race walking events for men and women, plus a mixed 4×400 m relay introduced in 2019. The United States tops the all-time medal table.
