Ajay Devgn

Ajay Devgn (born Vishal Veeru Devgan; 2 April 1969) is an Indian actor, film director and producer who works primarily in Hindi cinema. One of India's most prolific leading actors, he has appeared in over 100 films and won numerous accolades, including two National Film Awards and four Filmfare Awards. He was awarded the Padma Shri in 2016.

Career

The son of stunt choreographer Veeru Devgan, Devgn debuted as an action hero in Phool Aur Kaante (1991), winning the Filmfare Award for Best Male Debut. Through the 1990s he starred in successful action and romantic films such as Jigar, Dilwale and Ishq. He won National Film Awards for Best Actor for Zakhm (1998) and The Legend of Bhagat Singh (2002), and critical acclaim for Company (2002), Gangaajal (2003) and Omkara (2006), in which he played the Othello figure. His comedy successes include the Golmaal franchise, and his portrayal of Bajirao Singham in the Singham films made him a central figure of Rohit Shetty's "Cop Universe". He made his directorial debut with U Me Aur Hum (2008) and later directed Shivaay (2016) and Runway 34 (2022); Tanhaji (2020) became one of his biggest commercial successes and earned him a third National Film Award for Best Actor.

Personal life

Devgn married actress Kajol in 1999; they have two children. Through his company Ajay Devgn FFilms he has produced numerous films and co-founded a visual-effects studio.
