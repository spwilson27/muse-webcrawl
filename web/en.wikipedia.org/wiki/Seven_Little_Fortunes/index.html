Seven Little Fortunes

The Seven Little Fortunes (Chinese: 七小福) were a troupe of child performers trained at the China Drama Academy, a Peking opera school in Hong Kong run by the strict master Yu Jim-yuen. The troupe took its name from an opera in which seven of Yu's students performed, and it is best known today for producing several of the most famous action film stars in Hong Kong cinema history, including Jackie Chan, Sammo Hung and Yuen Biao.

Background

Peking opera schools in Hong Kong typically took in children from poor families under a ten-year contract. Students, called sifu's disciples, lived at the school, rose before dawn and trained for many hours a day in acrobatics, martial arts, singing, acting and dance, with discipline enforced by corporal punishment. In return the master received the children's performance earnings.

The "Seven Little Fortunes" were the seven students selected to headline the troupe's stage performances. The original line-up consisted of Yuen Lung (who later took the stage name Sammo Hung), Yuen Lou (Jackie Chan), Yuen Biao, Yuen Kwai, Yuen Wah, Yuen Tak and Yuen Mo. Membership in stage programmes varied, and other academy students such as Yuen Woo-ping and Corey Yuen later became celebrated action choreographers and directors.

Careers

As television and film eroded the audience for traditional opera, the academy closed in the early 1970s and its graduates moved into the film industry, first as child actors, extras and stuntmen. The rigorous acrobatic training of the school became the foundation of the distinctive Hong Kong action style of the 1970s and 1980s. Sammo Hung, Jackie Chan and Yuen Biao — the "Three Dragons" — starred together in a series of popular films including Project A (1983), Wheels on Meals (1984) and Dragons Forever (1988).

Legacy

The Seven Little Fortunes are remembered as a bridge between the centuries-old Peking opera tradition and modern action cinema. Jackie Chan's semi-autobiographical film Painted Faces (1988), directed by Alex Law and starring Sammo Hung as Master Yu, dramatised life at the China Drama Academy.
