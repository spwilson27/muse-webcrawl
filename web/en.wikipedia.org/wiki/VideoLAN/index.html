VideoLAN

Formation1996 (project); 2009 (non-profit organisation)
TypeNon-profit organisation
HeadquartersParis, France
ProductsVLC media player, x264, dav1d, libdvdcss

VideoLAN is a non-profit organisation and open-source project which develops software for playing video and other media formats across a local area network (LAN). It is best known for the VLC media player, one of the most widely used media players in the world, recognizable by its orange traffic-cone icon.

History

The project began in 1996 as an academic project of students at École Centrale Paris, aiming to stream video across the campus network using a client–server architecture: VideoLAN Server and VideoLAN Client, the latter becoming VLC. The code was released under the GNU General Public License on 1 February 2001, after the school's permission, and development expanded into an international volunteer community. The non-profit VideoLAN organisation was formally created in 2009 to hold the project's funds and legal affairs.

Projects

In addition to VLC, VideoLAN hosts or supports major multimedia libraries including x264 (an H.264 encoder), x265, dav1d (an AV1 decoder developed with the Alliance for Open Media), libdvdcss, and libbluray. Its software is free of charge, advertising, and user tracking, funded by donations.
