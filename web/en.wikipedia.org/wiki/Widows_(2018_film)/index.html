Widows is a 2018 heist thriller film directed by Steve McQueen, co-written with Gillian Flynn, and based on the 1983 British television series of the same name by Lynda La Plante, transplanted to contemporary Chicago.

The widows plan the robbery their husbands left behind

Plot

When career criminal Harry Rawlings (Liam Neeson) and his crew are killed in a botched robbery that also destroys $2 million belonging to crime boss Jamal Manning, Manning demands repayment from Harry's widow Veronica (Viola Davis). Finding Harry's notebook with plans for another heist, Veronica recruits the other widows — Linda, Alice, and Belle — to pull off the robbery themselves, against the backdrop of a corrupt local election campaign involving Manning and the dynastic Mulligan family.

Reception

The film premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival and was released by 20th Century Fox. It received widespread critical acclaim for McQueen's direction and the ensemble's performances, particularly Davis and Elizabeth Debicki, though its box-office returns were considered disappointing relative to its reception.
