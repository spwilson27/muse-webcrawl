USNS Comfort (T-AH-20) is a Mercy-class hospital ship of the United States Navy. She was originally built as the oil tanker SS Rose City in 1976 and was acquired by the Navy in 1986 and converted into a hospital ship, entering service in 1987. Her sister ship is USNS Mercy.

Capabilities

Comfort carries around 1,000 hospital beds, 12 operating rooms, a digital radiological service, a medical laboratory, and intensive care units, making her one of the largest floating medical treatment facilities in the world. She is operated by civilian mariners of the Military Sealift Command with a Navy medical crew that embarks when the ship is activated.

Deployments

The ship has deployed for Operations Desert Shield and Desert Storm, to New York City after the September 11 attacks, to the Gulf Coast after Hurricane Katrina, to Haiti after the 2010 earthquake (Operation Unified Response), and on recurring humanitarian missions in Latin America and the Caribbean under Operation Continuing Promise. In March 2020 she deployed to New York City during the COVID-19 pandemic to relieve pressure on local hospitals.
