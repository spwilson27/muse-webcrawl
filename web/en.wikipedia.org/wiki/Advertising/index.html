Advertising

[Image: a Times Square scene filled with illuminated advertising screens]

Advertising is the practice of drawing public attention to a product, service or cause through paid announcements in media — print, broadcast, outdoor, cinema, and now predominantly digital (search, social, video and display). It is the most visible branch of Marketing promotion and funds much of the free media economy.

Industry and effects

Advertisers brief agencies (creative, media-buying and now data specialists, consolidated into holding groups such as WPP and Omnicom) to build brands and shift demand. Global advertising spending runs to several hundred billion dollars a year, with the majority digital and the largest share captured by Google, Meta and Amazon. Economic views divide: advertising as information (reducing search costs, signalling quality — the favourable view in Microeconomics) versus advertising as persuasion that entrenches market power and manufactures wants (Galbraith's 'dependence effect'). Regulation bans or restricts categories (tobacco in many countries), polices misleading claims and protects children, while ad-blocking, privacy law and the decline of third-party cookies reshape digital practice.
