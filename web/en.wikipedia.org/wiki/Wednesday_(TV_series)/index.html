Wednesday is an American supernatural mystery comedy-drama television series based on the character Wednesday Addams from The Addams Family, created by cartoonist Charles Addams. Created by Alfred Gough and Miles Millar, with Tim Burton as executive producer and director of the first four episodes, the series premiered on Netflix on 23 November 2022 and became one of the platform's most-watched English-language series of all time.

Premise

Expelled from her mainstream school after avenging her brother Pugsley with live piranhas, Wednesday Addams (Jenna Ortega) is enrolled at Nevermore Academy in Jericho, Vermont — the boarding school for "outcasts" (werewolves, sirens, vampires, gorgons and psychics) that her parents attended. There she develops her emerging psychic ability, investigates a monstrous killing spree and a decades-old mystery involving her parents, and reluctantly forms friendships, notably with her sunny werewolf roommate Enid Sinclair (Emma Myers).

Production and reception

The first season was filmed in Romania, including at Cantacuzino Castle. Ortega's deadpan performance and the school-dance sequence — in which Wednesday dances to the Cramps' "Goo Goo Muck" — became viral cultural phenomena, spiking interest in goth fashion and Lady Gaga's "Bloody Mary". The series received positive reviews for Ortega's performance and Burton's visual style, and won four Primetime Emmy Awards from twelve nominations for its first season. A second season, filmed in Ireland, was released in two parts in August and September 2025, and the series has been renewed for a third season.
