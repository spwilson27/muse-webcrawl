Get Carter is a 1971 British crime film written and directed by Mike Hodges, based on Ted Lewis's 1970 novel Jack's Return Home. It stars Michael Caine as Jack Carter, a London gangster who returns to his home city of Newcastle to investigate the suspicious death of his brother, embarking on a cold, methodical quest for vengeance.

Plot

Carter, an enforcer for London crime bosses, travels north against his employers' wishes. Moving through Newcastle's pubs, clubs and betting shops, he uncovers a web of corruption linking his brother's death to local crime boss Cyril Kinnear and a pornography ring. His pursuit ends in violence on the Durham coast, where Carter himself is shot by a distant sniper as he disposes of his final victim.

Reception and legacy

Made for around £550,000 and shot on location in north-east England, the film was initially received as a stylish but brutal thriller. Its reputation has grown steadily; in 1999 the British Film Institute ranked it 16th among the greatest British films of the 20th century, and in 2004 Total Film named it the greatest British film of all time. A Hollywood remake starring Sylvester Stallone appeared in 2000.
