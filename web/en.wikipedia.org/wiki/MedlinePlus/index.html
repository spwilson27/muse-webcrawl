MedlinePlus

MedlinePlus is an online information service produced by the United States National Library of Medicine (NLM). The service provides curated consumer health information in English and Spanish, with subsets in other languages. It brings together information from the National Institutes of Health (NIH), other US government agencies, and health-related organisations, and is a key component of the NLM's outreach to the general public.

Contents

MedlinePlus provides encyclopaedic summaries for more than 1,000 diseases and conditions, a medical encyclopedia (the A.D.A.M. Illustrated Encyclopedia), drug and supplement information, medical tests information, health news, directories of health professionals and facilities, and interactive tutorials. Its "Health Topics" pages organise authoritative links by condition, body system, and demographic group. A Spanish version, MedlinePlus en español, provides equivalent consumer information.

History

The service launched in 1998, at which time it provided access to a small number of health topic pages. The medical encyclopedia was added in later years. In 2010, MedlinePlus Connect was introduced, allowing electronic health record systems to link patient portals directly to relevant MedlinePlus information by diagnosis, medication, or laboratory test codes. The site receives hundreds of millions of page views per year.

MedlinePlus and PubMed

MedlinePlus is distinct from PubMed and MEDLINE, which are aimed at researchers and clinicians: MedlinePlus content is written for consumers in plain language, while PubMed indexes the biomedical research literature.
