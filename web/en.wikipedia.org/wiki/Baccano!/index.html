Baccano! is a Japanese light novel series written by Ryohgo Narita and illustrated by Katsumi Enami. The title is Italian for "ruckus" or "commotion". Set largely in Prohibition-era America, the series follows an ensemble cast of mobsters, thieves, alchemists and immortals whose storylines interweave across several decades, told in a non-linear narrative style.

Plot

The central plot revolves around an elixir of immortality first created in 1711 by alchemists aboard the ship Advena Avis, and rediscovered during the 1930s by Mafia families and bootleggers in New York and Chicago. Key storylines include the transcontinental train journey of the Flying Pussyfoot in 1931 and the feud between the Gandor and Martillo crime families.

Prominent characters include the cheerful immortal thieves Isaac Dian and Miria Harvent, the assassin Ladd Russo, the homunculus Ennis, and the rail tracer Czeslaw Meyer.

Adaptations

The novels were adapted into a 13-episode anime television series by Brain's Base in 2007, with three additional episodes released on DVD. The anime is noted for its large ensemble cast, jazz-influenced score and fragmented, multi-timeline structure. A manga adaptation and a sequel manga set in 2002 have also been published. Narita is also the author of Durarara!!, which shares a similar ensemble style.
