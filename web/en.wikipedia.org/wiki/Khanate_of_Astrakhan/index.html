Khanate of Astrakhan

The Khanate of Astrakhan was a Tatar feudal state that existed from 1466 to 1556, centred on the city of Astrakhan near the mouth of the Volga River. It emerged from the disintegration of the Golden Horde and was one of its successor khanates, alongside Kazan, Crimea, Sibir and the Nogai Horde.

History

Founded by Mahmud bin Küchük, the khanate controlled an important trading point where routes from Persia, Central Asia and Europe met, though it was militarily weak and frequently dependent on its neighbours. Its khans were often installed or deposed through the interventions of the Crimean Khanate and the Nogais. In 1554 Russian forces under Ivan IV placed a client khan on the throne; in 1556 Russia annexed the khanate outright, completing its conquest of the Volga region begun with Kazan in 1552.
