Wren Library

The Wren Library is the library of Trinity College, Cambridge, designed by Sir Christopher Wren and completed in 1695. It houses many of the college's rarest books and manuscripts.

Location: Nevile's Court, Trinity College, Cambridge
Architect: Christopher Wren
Completed: 1695

Architecture and collections

Raised on a loggia above Nevile's Court so books sit above damp, the long single room is lit by large windows, lined with limewood carvings by Grinling Gibbons and marble busts. Treasures include Newton's annotated Principia, the Capgrave manuscript, A. A. Milne's Winnie-the-Pooh manuscripts, and a first folio tradition of college treasures.
