Vladikavkaz

Vladikavkaz is a city in southern Russia, the capital of the Republic of North Ossetia–Alania. It lies in the foothills of the Caucasus Mountains on the Terek River, near the Georgian border.

History

Founded in 1784 as a Russian fortress guarding the Georgian Military Road through the Darial Gorge, its name means "ruler of the Caucasus". It grew with the railway in the late 19th century. In the Soviet period the city was known as Ordzhonikidze (1931–1944 and 1954–1990), after Sergo Ordzhonikidze, and as Dzaudzhikau in 1944–1954, before reverting to Vladikavkaz.

Geography and economy

The city is an industrial and transport hub for the North Caucasus, with metallurgy, food processing and education among its sectors, and a mixed Ossetian, Russian, Armenian and Georgian population.
