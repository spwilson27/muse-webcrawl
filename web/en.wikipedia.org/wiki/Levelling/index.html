Levelling

Levelling or leveling (American English) is a branch of surveying, the object of which is to establish or verify points at the same horizontal elevation (level surface) or to determine differences in elevation between points.

Methods

The most common method is differential levelling, using a levelling instrument (a telescope with a spirit level or automatic compensator) and graduated staffs. The surveyor reads a backsight and a foresight and computes the height difference. Other methods include trigonometric levelling, barometric levelling and GPS-based height determination.

Instruments

Instruments include the dumpy level, tilting level, automatic level and digital level, along with the Philadelphia rod and other levelling staffs. Modern digital levels read bar-coded staffs electronically.

Applications

Levelling is used in construction, road and railway building, drainage and flood control, and in establishing vertical datums and benchmarks for mapping.
