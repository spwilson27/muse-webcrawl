Das Erste ("The First") is the flagship national television channel of the ARD association of public broadcasters in Germany. It is jointly operated by the nine regional broadcasting corporations that make up ARD and is funded principally through the German broadcasting licence fee (Rundfunkbeitrag).

History

Regular television broadcasts began on 25 December 1952 from Hamburg under the NWDR. The service was renamed Deutsches Fernsehen in 1954 when it became a joint programme of the regional broadcasters, and for decades it was simply "the" German television channel; after the launch of ZDF in 1963 it became known as the first channel, and it adopted the name Das Erste in 1984. Following German reunification in 1990, the channel's coverage extended across the whole country.

Programming

Das Erste broadcasts the flagship Tagesschau news bulletin — Germany's most-watched news programme — at 20:00 each evening, together with the Tagesthemen late-night news magazine. Its drama output includes the crime series Tatort, running since 1970, and long-running soap operas such as Lindenstraße (1985–2020). The channel carries major sports events including the FIFA World Cup, the Olympic Games, and German national football team matches, shared with ZDF.
