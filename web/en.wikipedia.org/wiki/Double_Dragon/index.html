Double Dragon

Double Dragon is a beat 'em up video game series initially developed by Technōs Japan and released as an arcade game in 1987. The series features twin martial artists, Billy and Jimmy Lee, as they fight against various adversaries and rescue the shared love interest, Marian. The original game is credited with popularising the cooperative two-player beat 'em up genre and was a major hit, spawning sequels, home console ports, a film adaptation in 1994, and an animated television series.

Gameplay

Players control one of the Lee brothers, progressing through side-scrolling levels while fighting gangs of enemies with punches, kicks, throws, and improvised weapons such as baseball bats, knives, and oil drums. The arcade original introduced features such as the ability to take weapons from defeated enemies and cooperative play in which the two players could also fight each other at the finale over Marian.

Legacy

Double Dragon influenced later beat 'em ups including Final Fight and Streets of Rage. The franchise was revived by Arc System Works, which acquired the rights in 2015, and has continued with titles such as Double Dragon IV (2017).
