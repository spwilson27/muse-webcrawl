This list of space telescopes (astronomical space observatories) is grouped by major frequency ranges: gamma ray, X-ray, ultraviolet, visible, infrared, microwave, radio, and particle detection. Space telescopes avoid the filtering and distortion of Earth's atmosphere and can observe wavelengths that never reach the ground.

Microwave and radio

Microwave observatories include COBE (1989), WMAP (2001) and Planck (2009), which mapped the cosmic microwave background. The HALCA/VSOP spacecraft (1997) carried a radio antenna for space very-long-baseline interferometry, followed by RadioAstron (2011).

Particle detection

Particle detectors in orbit include PAMELA, the Alpha Magnetic Spectrometer (AMS-02) aboard the International Space Station, and DAMPE, which study cosmic rays and search for dark matter signatures.
