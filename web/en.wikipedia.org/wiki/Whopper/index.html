The Whopper is the signature hamburger of the international fast-food chain Burger King. Introduced in 1957 by Burger King co-founder James McLamore in Gainesville, Florida, it was created to compete with larger burgers sold by rival restaurants, and was priced at 37 cents. It is one of the best-known products in the fast-food industry, and Burger King markets itself as "Home of the Whopper".

Description

The standard Whopper consists of a quarter-pound flame-grilled beef patty served in a sesame seed bun with mayonnaise, ketchup, lettuce, tomato, sliced onion, and pickles. Variants include the Double and Triple Whopper, the Whopper Jr., cheese and bacon versions, and limited-time regional varieties. Nutritional values for a standard Whopper are roughly 660 calories.

History and marketing

The sandwich has been at the centre of many advertising campaigns, including the "Have It Your Way" slogan, introduced in 1974, which emphasised customisation. In 2017 Burger King won a Grand Prix at Cannes Lions for the "Google Home of the Whopper" campaign, which deliberately triggered voice-assistant devices.
