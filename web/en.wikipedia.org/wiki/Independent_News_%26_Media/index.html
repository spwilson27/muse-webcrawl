Independent News & Media plc (INM) is an Irish media organisation based in Dublin that publishes national newspapers, including the Irish Independent and the Sunday Independent. Founded from the newspaper interests built up around the Irish Independent, the company expanded internationally in the 1990s and 2000s under Tony O'Reilly, acquiring interests in South Africa, Australia, and New Zealand.

History

The Irish Independent was founded in 1905 by William Martin Murphy. The modern company took shape in 1973 when Tony O'Reilly began building a stake, and grew into a multinational group holding titles such as South Africa's The Star and a large shareholding in APN News & Media. Heavy debts after the 2008 financial crisis led to restructuring, and businessman Denis O'Brien became the largest shareholder.

Sale

In 2019 INM was acquired by the Belgian group Mediahuis. Its Irish titles include the Irish Independent, Sunday Independent, Sunday World, the Belfast Telegraph, and regional newspapers, along with digital outlets such as Independent.ie.
