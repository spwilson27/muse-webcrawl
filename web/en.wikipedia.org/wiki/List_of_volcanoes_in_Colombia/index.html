List of volcanoes in Colombia

Colombia lies above the subduction zone where the Nazca Plate sinks beneath South America, and has around a dozen active volcanoes, mostly in the Central Cordillera of the Andes. They are monitored by the Colombian Geological Service (SGC). The deadliest eruption in Colombian history was that of Nevado del Ruiz in 1985, whose lahars destroyed the town of Armero and killed about 23,000 people.
