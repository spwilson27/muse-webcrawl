Holiday Inn Express is an American-based mid-priced hotel chain within the IHG Hotels & Resorts family of brands. As a "limited service" hotel brand, Holiday Inn Express properties offer free breakfast and free Wi-Fi, but generally lack full-service restaurants, room service, and large meeting or recreational facilities. The brand was one of the fastest-growing in the hotel industry when it launched, and it has since grown to be IHG's largest brand by number of hotels.

History

The brand was launched in 1990 by Bass plc, then owner of Holiday Inn, with the first hotels opening in the United States in 1991. It was designed to compete in the growing limited-service segment against chains such as Hampton Inn and Fairfield Inn. In Europe the brand expanded rapidly, often under the name "Express by Holiday Inn" before being rebranded under the Holiday Inn Express name.

Features

Holiday Inn Express hotels are known for their complimentary "Express Start" hot breakfast bar, which is standardized across the brand, and for the "Stay Smart" advertising campaign that ran from the late 1990s, featuring the tagline "But I did stay at a Holiday Inn Express last night"—a phrase that entered popular culture. Newer properties follow the brand's "Formula Blue" design standards, introduced in 2014, which standardise room layouts, public spaces, and signage.

Most properties are franchised and independently owned and operated under licence from IHG. The brand is targeted at both business travellers and leisure guests seeking value accommodation in urban, airport, and roadside locations.
