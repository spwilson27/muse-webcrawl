Deutsche Post is a German postal and courier company, the mail division of the Deutsche Post DHL Group. It is the successor to the German federal postal authority Deutsche Bundespost, which was privatised in 1995. Under its best-known brand, DHL, the group is one of the largest logistics companies in the world.

History

The Deutsche Bundespost was a state-owned postal administration. As part of Germany's postal reform, it was split in 1995 into three companies: Deutsche Post (mail), Deutsche Telekom (telecommunications) and Postbank (banking). Deutsche Post was floated on the stock market in 2000 and progressively acquired logistics firms, most notably the express carrier DHL, which it fully owned by 2002. In 2015 the group rebranded its mail division as "Deutsche Post DHL Group", and in 2023 shortened the corporate name to DHL Group, while "Deutsche Post" remains the brand for domestic German mail.

Operations

Deutsche Post delivers letters and parcels throughout Germany, operating a network of post offices, parcel shops, Packstation automated lockers and delivery vehicles — many of them electric, including the purpose-built StreetScooter. Its Post & Parcel Germany division handles billions of letter items and over a billion parcels annually, driven by the growth of e-commerce.
