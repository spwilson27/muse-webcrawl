Emily Olivia Laura Blunt (born 23 February 1983) is a British actress. She is known for her versatility across drama, comedy, action and musical films, and has received numerous accolades, including a Golden Globe Award and a Screen Actors Guild Award, as well as an Academy Award nomination.

Early life

Blunt was born in Wandsworth, London, the daughter of a barrister and a former actress and teacher. She developed a stutter as a child and has credited acting — taking on a different voice — with helping her overcome it; she later became a board member of the American Institute for Stuttering. She was educated at Ibstock Place School and at Hurtwood House, where she was discovered by an agent.

Career

After stage and television work, Blunt made her film debut in My Summer of Love (2004). Her breakthrough came as the sharp-tongued assistant Emily Charlton in The Devil Wears Prada (2006), opposite Meryl Streep. Leading roles followed in The Young Victoria (2009), the science-fiction films The Adjustment Bureau (2011), Looper (2012) and Edge of Tomorrow (2014), and the musical Into the Woods (2014).

Blunt starred in the thriller The Girl on the Train (2016) and played Mary Poppins in Mary Poppins Returns (2018). With her husband John Krasinski she co-starred in the horror film A Quiet Place (2018) and its sequel, winning a Screen Actors Guild Award. She received an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actress for her portrayal of Kitty Oppenheimer in Christopher Nolan's Oppenheimer (2023).
