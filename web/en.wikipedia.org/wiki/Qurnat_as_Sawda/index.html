Qurnat as Sawda

Qurnat as Sawda is the highest mountain in Lebanon and the Levant, rising to about 3,088 metres above sea level. Its name means the “black horn” or “black peak”, and it forms part of the Mount Lebanon range near the country&rsquo;s northern highlands.

Geography

The summit area is remote and exposed, with long snow cover in winter and wide views across Lebanon and toward Syria on clear days. The mountain stands near the Qadisha valley region and the cedar forests that are emblematic of Lebanon&rsquo;s high country.

Access and landscape

Approaches are rugged and usually undertaken by experienced hikers or with local guidance. The slopes combine bare rock, seasonal pasture and sparse vegetation adapted to altitude. Because of its elevation and isolation, weather can change quickly around the summit.

Qurnat as Sawda is the high point of Lebanon, a country whose geography also includes Mediterranean coast, the Beqaa Valley and the Anti-Lebanon mountains.
