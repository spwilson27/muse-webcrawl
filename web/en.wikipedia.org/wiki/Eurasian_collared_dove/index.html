Eurasian collared dove

The Eurasian collared dove (Streptopelia decaocto) is a dove species native to Europe and Asia which has become established across North America. It is named for the black half-collar on the back of its neck.

KingdomAnimalia
FamilyColumbidae
GenusStreptopelia
SpeciesS. decaocto
ConservationLeast Concern (IUCN)

Description and call

A medium-sized, pale grey-buff dove, about 32 cm long, with red eyes and a repetitive cooing song often rendered "goo-GOO-goo". Sexes are similar.

Range expansion

Originally from Asia, the species spread spectacularly across Europe during the 20th century, reaching Britain in the 1950s. Escaped/released birds in the Bahamas in the 1970s spread to Florida and then across North America. It thrives in human-altered habitats, feeding on seeds and grain, and breeds several times a year, usually laying two white eggs.
