Ward W. Willits House

The Ward W. Willits House is a house designed by architect Frank Lloyd Wright in Highland Park, Illinois, completed in 1901. It is widely considered the first house of Wright's mature Prairie style.

Design

The house was built for Ward Winfield Willits, an executive of the Adams and Westlake brass foundry. Its plan is cruciform, radiating from a central fireplace mass, with a low-pitched hip roof, broad overhanging eaves, and horizontal bands of art-glass windows that dissolve the box-like form of the Victorian house. The living room occupies a raised main floor above a ground-level basement, an arrangement Wright used to give privacy and views over the surrounding prairie landscape.

Significance

Architectural historians regard the Willits House as the first fully realised Prairie house and a landmark in the development of modern residential architecture. It remains a private residence and is listed on the National Register of Historic Places.
