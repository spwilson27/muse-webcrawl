The 1948 Winter Olympics, officially the V Olympic Winter Games, were held in St. Moritz, Switzerland, from 30 January to 8 February 1948. They were the first Olympic Games held after the Second World War, following a twelve-year break since 1936; St. Moritz had previously hosted in 1928.

Highlights

Switzerland's neutrality made St. Moritz an acceptable host when much of Europe lay in ruins. Germany and Japan were not invited. Skeleton returned to the programme for the last time until 2002, on the Cresta Run. Canada and Sweden dominated ice hockey and skiing events respectively; the hockey gold came down to a tie between Canada and Czechoslovakia resolved on goal average.

Medal table

Norway, Sweden, and Switzerland topped the medal table with ten medals each, Norway and Sweden each winning four golds. The United States placed fourth overall.
