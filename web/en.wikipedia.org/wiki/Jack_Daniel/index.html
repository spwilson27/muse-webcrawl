Jack Daniel

Jasper Newton "Jack" Daniel (c. 1849 – October 9, 1911) was an American distiller and businessman, best known as the founder of the Jack Daniel's Tennessee whiskey distillery.

Life

Born in or around Lynchburg, Tennessee, Daniel was apprenticed as a boy to preacher and distiller Dan Call, where he learned distilling from Nathan "Nearest" Green, an enslaved man who became his mentor and later the distillery's first master distiller. Daniel took over the business and registered his distillery with the US government in 1866, among the oldest registered distilleries in the United States.

Death

Daniel died in 1911 from blood poisoning, said to have followed an infection after he injured his toe kicking his safe in frustration.
