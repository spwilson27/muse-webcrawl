GURPS

GURPS
Designer: Steve Jackson
Publisher: Steve Jackson Games
First published: 1986
System: Generic, universal, point-buy; 3d6 resolution

GURPS is a role-playing game system published by Steve Jackson Games. The acronym originally stood for "Generic Universal RolePlaying System": it is designed as a single rules framework usable in any setting or genre, from fantasy and science fiction to historical and horror campaigns.

System

Characters are built with a point-buy system across four basic attributes (Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Health) plus advantages, disadvantages, and skills. Task resolution uses three six-sided dice rolled against a target number of the relevant skill or attribute, low being success. The system is noted for its emphasis on realism, detailed tactical combat, and an enormous library of sourcebooks adapting it to specific worlds and genres; famous supplements include GURPS Traveller, GURPS Discworld, and the post-apocalyptic GURPS materials that influenced the Fallout video game series (originally intended to use GURPS itself).

Editions

The first edition appeared in 1986, growing out of Jackson's The Fantasy Trip. The fourth edition, released in 2004, consolidated the rules into two core volumes, Characters and Campaigns.
