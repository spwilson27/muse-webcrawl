List of Columbus Blue Jackets seasons

The Columbus Blue Jackets are a professional ice hockey team based in Columbus, Ohio, and members of the Metropolitan Division of the Eastern Conference of the National Hockey League (NHL). The franchise was founded as an expansion team in 2000 and plays its home games at Nationwide Arena.

The Blue Jackets struggled through most of their first decade, reaching the playoffs for the first time in 2009. Their 2019 upset sweep of the Presidents' Trophy-winning Tampa Bay Lightning was the franchise's first series victory. Franchise records and full season-by-season standings are maintained in the team's official records.
