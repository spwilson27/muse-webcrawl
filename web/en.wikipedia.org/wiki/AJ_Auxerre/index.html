AJ Auxerre

Association de la Jeunesse Auxerroise, commonly known as AJ Auxerre, is a French professional football club based in Auxerre, Burgundy. Founded in 1905, the club plays its home matches at the Stade de l'Abbé-Deschamps.

Auxerre rose to prominence under long-serving coach Guy Roux, who managed the club for more than four decades and led it to the Ligue 1 title in 1995–96, completing a league and cup double that season. The club also won the Coupe de France four times and became known for its academy, which developed players such as Eric Cantona, Basile Boli and Djibril Cissé.

The club has spent periods in both Ligue 1 and Ligue 2 in the twenty-first century, earning promotion back to the top flight in 2024.
