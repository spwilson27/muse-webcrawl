A Question of Sport was a British sports quiz show produced and broadcast by the BBC. It was the longest-running sports quiz in the world and one of the BBC's longest-running programmes overall, airing from 1970 until 2023.

Format

Two teams, each captained by a well-known sports personality and joined by sporting guests, competed in rounds including the Picture Board (identifying sportspeople from partially revealed photographs), the Mystery Guest, and "What Happened Next?", in which a clip of sporting action was paused before an unusual outcome. Long-serving hosts included David Coleman (1979–1997) and Sue Barker, who presented from 1997 to 2021 alongside team captains Matt Dawson and Phil Tufnell. In 2021 Paddy McGuinness took over as host with captains Sam Quek and Ugo Monye.

History

The programme was first broadcast on 5 January 1970 with David Vine as host. The BBC announced in 2023 that production would cease, citing funding pressures and declining viewing figures.
