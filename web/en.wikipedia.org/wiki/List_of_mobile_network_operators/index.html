List of mobile network operators

This is a list of mobile network operators (MNOs) — companies that own wireless network infrastructure and spectrum licences and provide mobile telephony and data services to subscribers. The largest operators serve hundreds of millions of customers, mainly in China and India. Subscriber figures are approximate and change frequently.
