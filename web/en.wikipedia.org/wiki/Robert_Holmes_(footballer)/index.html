Robert Holmes (footballer)

Born: 1867, Preston, Lancashire, England
Died: November 17, 1955
Position: Full-back
Clubs: Preston North End
National team: England (7 caps)

Robert Holmes (1867 – 17 November 1955), commonly known as Bob Holmes, was an English professional footballer who played as a full-back for Preston North End and the England national team. He was a member of Preston's famous "Invincibles" side that won the inaugural Football League championship in 1888–89 without losing a match.

Career

Holmes joined Preston North End in the mid-1880s and played in the 1888 FA Cup Final, which Preston lost to West Bromwich Albion. The following season, Preston completed the League and FA Cup "Double," remaining unbeaten in the league and winning the 1889 FA Cup Final against Wolverhampton Wanderers 3–0 without conceding a goal in the entire cup run. Holmes's dependable defending alongside fellow full-back and captain Bob Howarth formed the backbone of the team. Preston retained the league title in 1889–90, and Holmes remained the club's regular left-back or right-back through much of its early Football League era.

International career

Holmes won seven caps for England between 1888 and 1895, and captained his country on at least one occasion. He was one of the last surviving members of the Invincibles and, at his death in 1955 at the age of 88, a living link to the very first season of league football.

Honours

  • Football League First Division: 1888–89, 1889–90
  • FA Cup: 1888–89; runner-up 1887–88
