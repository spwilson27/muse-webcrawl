Yellowstone (American TV series)

Yellowstone is an American neo-Western drama television series created by Taylor Sheridan and John Linson. It premiered on the Paramount Network on June 20, 2018, and concluded in December 2024 after five seasons. The series stars Kevin Costner as John Dutton, the patriarch of a powerful ranching family in Montana.

Premise

The series follows the Dutton family, owners of the Yellowstone Dutton Ranch — the largest ranch in Montana — and their conflicts with land developers, a neighbouring Indian reservation, and others seeking to control the land. Members of the family include John's children Beth, Kayce and Jamie, and ranch foreman Rip Wheeler.

Cast

The principal cast includes Kevin Costner (John Dutton), Luke Grimes (Kayce Dutton), Kelly Reilly (Beth Dutton), Wes Bentley (Jamie Dutton) and Cole Hauser (Rip Wheeler).

Reception and franchise

Yellowstone became one of the most-watched series on American cable television, with its later seasons drawing record audiences for the Paramount Network. Its success launched a franchise of prequel and spin-off series, including 1883 and 1923, also created by Sheridan, which trace the Dutton family's history.
