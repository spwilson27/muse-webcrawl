Warner Bros. Animation Inc. is an American animation studio that is part of Warner Bros. Discovery. It is the successor to the classic Warner Bros. Cartoons studio that produced the Looney Tunes and Merrie Melodies theatrical shorts, and it is best known for its animated television series based on DC Comics characters and other Warner properties.

History

The original Warner Bros. cartoon studio closed in 1969. Warner Bros. reopened its animation division in 1980 to produce television specials and series. Its modern era began with Tiny Toon Adventures (1990), produced with Steven Spielberg's Amblin Entertainment, followed by Animaniacs and the acclaimed Batman: The Animated Series (1992), which launched the DC Animated Universe overseen by Bruce Timm and others.

Productions

The studio's output includes Superman: The Animated Series, Justice League, Teen Titans, The Batman, Batman: The Brave and the Bold, Young Justice and numerous direct-to-video DC films, as well as Looney Tunes revivals and, since 2013, the library of Hanna-Barbera productions through its sister studio. It remains one of the principal American television animation studios.
