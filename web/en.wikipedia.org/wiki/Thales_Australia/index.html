Thales Australia

Type: Subsidiary

Industry: Defence, aerospace, transport

Parent: Thales Group

Headquarters: Sydney, Australia

Thales Australia is the Australian arm of the French multinational Thales Group, and one of the largest defence contractors in Australia. It designs, manufactures and supports a range of military and civil systems, including protected vehicles, weapons and ammunition, naval systems, avionics, air traffic management and communications.

History

The company's Australian lineage includes the former government-owned Australian Defence Industries (ADI), which Thales acquired from the Australian government in 1999 (initially as a joint venture, and fully in 2006), and Rockwell/Boeing Australia assets. Operations are centred in Sydney with major sites in Bendigo and Lithgow in New South Wales and Victoria, and the naval centre at Garden Island, Sydney.

Products and activities

Thales Australia is best known for the Bushmaster protected mobility vehicle, designed and built at Bendigo, which has served with the Australian Army and several export customers, and for the Hawkei light protected vehicle. The Lithgow facility produces small arms, including the F88 Austeyr rifle and its EF88 (F90) successor. Other activities include munitions manufacture at Benalla and Mulwala, sonar and underwater systems, optronics, air traffic control systems exported worldwide, and support of Royal Australian Navy vessels.
