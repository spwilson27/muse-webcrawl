TWA Hotel

The TWA Hotel is an airport hotel at John F. Kennedy International Airport in New York City, opened in 2019 within the landmark former TWA Flight Center terminal, designed by Eero Saarinen and opened in 1962.

Features

The hotel has 512 rooms in two new wings flanking the restored terminal head house, which serves as its lobby. Amenities include a rooftop pool overlooking the runways and a museum devoted to Trans World Airlines and the Jet Age.
