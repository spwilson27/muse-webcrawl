Christopher Edward Martin (born March 21, 1966), known professionally as DJ Premier (also known as Preemo and Primo), is an American record producer and DJ. He was the instrumental half of the hip hop duo Gang Starr, alongside rapper Guru. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest hip hop producers of all time, known for a signature sound built on chopped samples from jazz, funk and soul records, scratching of short vocal fragments in choruses, hard drums, and sparse, gritty loops.

DJ Premier performing live.

Career

Martin was born in Houston, Texas, and raised in Prairie View, where he attended Prairie View A&M University. He formed Gang Starr with Guru in the late 1980s after moving to Brooklyn, New York. The duo released a string of acclaimed albums including Step in the Arena (1991), Daily Operation (1992), Hard to Earn (1994), and Moment of Truth (1998), with Premier producing every track.

In parallel, Premier became one of the most in-demand producers in hip hop, crafting landmark songs for Nas ("N.Y. State of Mind"), the Notorious B.I.G. ("Unbelievable", "Kick in the Door"), Jay-Z ("D'Evils", "So Ghetto"), Mos Def, Jeru the Damaja, and many others. He formed the group PRhyme with Royce da 5'9", releasing albums in 2014 and 2018, and has continued production work, DJ sets, and archival Gang Starr releases, including the posthumous album One of the Best Yet (2019).

Production style

Premier's productions typically combine a short looped or chopped sample with heavy boom-bap drums and a chorus constructed entirely from scratched vocal snippets of other rappers — a technique that became one of the most recognisable signatures in rap music. Music publications including The Source and Rolling Stone have consistently ranked him among the best producers in hip hop history.
