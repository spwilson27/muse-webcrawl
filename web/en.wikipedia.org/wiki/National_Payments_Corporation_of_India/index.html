National Payments Corporation of India

The National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) is an umbrella organisation for operating retail payments and settlement systems in India. It was incorporated in 2008 as a not-for-profit company promoted by the Reserve Bank of India and the Indian Banks' Association.

Products

NPCI operates the Unified Payments Interface (UPI), which enables instant mobile payments and has become one of the world's largest real-time payment systems by volume. Its other products include the RuPay card network, the Immediate Payment Service (IMPS), the National Financial Switch for ATMs, Aadhaar-enabled payment systems (AePS), Bharat BillPay, and FASTag electronic toll collection.
