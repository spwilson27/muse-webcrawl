Multiple Launch Rocket System

A multiple launch rocket system (MLRS) is a type of rocket artillery system consisting of a launcher mounted on a vehicle that can fire multiple unguided or guided rockets in rapid succession at ground targets. Such systems are used to deliver heavy volumes of fire over a wide area.

History

Early examples include the Soviet Katyusha launchers of the Second World War. During the Cold War, both NATO and Warsaw Pact forces developed large-calibre rocket artillery, such as the American M270 Multiple Launch Rocket System and the Soviet BM-21 Grad, BM-27 Uragan and BM-30 Smerch.

Modern systems

Modern MLRS platforms increasingly fire precision-guided rockets and can also launch tactical ballistic missiles. The wheeled M142 HIMARS, derived from the M270 family, has seen extensive use, including by Ukraine following Russia's invasion in 2022.
