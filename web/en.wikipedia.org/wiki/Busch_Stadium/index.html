Busch Stadium

Busch Stadium (also referred to informally as "New Busch Stadium" or "Busch Stadium III") is a baseball stadium located in St. Louis, Missouri. It is the home of the St. Louis Cardinals of Major League Baseball. The stadium opened on April 4, 2006, replacing the previous Busch Memorial Stadium (Busch Stadium II), which stood partly on the same site. It offers views of the downtown skyline and the Gateway Arch from inside the park.

History

The Cardinals began pressing for a new ballpark in the 1990s, seeking a baseball-only retro-classic facility to replace the multipurpose Busch Memorial Stadium (1966–2005). After financing was arranged — largely private, with the team funding most of the roughly $365 million construction cost — the new stadium rose immediately south of the old one. The first regular-season game was played on April 10, 2006, against the Milwaukee Brewers. In its inaugural season the Cardinals won the 2006 World Series, defeating the Detroit Tigers; the club also won the 2011 World Series at Busch Stadium and hosted the 2009 MLB All-Star Game.

Ballpark Village

Adjacent to the stadium, on the site of the old stadium, the Cardinals developed Ballpark Village, a mixed-use entertainment district of restaurants, bars, offices, and a Cardinals museum and Hall of Fame, whose rooftop seating overlooks the playing field. The first phase opened in 2014, with a second phase — including a residential tower and office building anchored by PwC — completed around 2020.

Other events

Beyond baseball, Busch Stadium has hosted concerts, soccer matches, and outdoor NHL hockey (the 2017 Winter Classic between the St. Louis Blues and Chicago Blackhawks).
