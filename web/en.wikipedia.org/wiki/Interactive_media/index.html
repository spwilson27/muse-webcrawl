Interactive media

Interactive media normally refers to products and services on digital computer-based systems which respond to the user's actions by presenting content such as text, moving image, animation, video, and audio.

Unlike passive media, interactive media allow a two-way flow of information: the user can navigate, manipulate, and contribute content. Examples include video games, websites, social media, virtual reality environments, interactive installations, and educational software.

Interactivity is a central concept in media studies and human–computer interaction, distinguishing new media from broadcast media such as radio and television.
