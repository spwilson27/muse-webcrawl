The 2006 Commonwealth Games, officially the XVIII Commonwealth Games and branded Melbourne 2006, were held in Melbourne, Australia, from 15 to 26 March 2006. With about 4,500 athletes from 71 Commonwealth nations and territories, they were the largest sporting event held in Melbourne since the 1956 Summer Olympics and coincided with the 150th anniversary of the Melbourne Cricket Ground's first major events era.

Highlights

Hosts Australia dominated the medal table with 84 gold medals and 221 medals overall, ahead of England and Canada. The opening ceremony featured the Yarra River and a giant platypus, and the Queen's Baton Relay had travelled through all 71 nations. South African swimmer Natalie du Toit won gold in elite-athlete-with-a-disability events while also competing in open swimming finals, a Commonwealth first. India enjoyed a breakthrough in shooting and weightlifting, while Kenya again dominated distance running.

Organization

The Games used venues across Melbourne, including the MCG for athletics and ceremonies, the Melbourne Sports and Aquatic Centre, and the State Netball and Hockey Centre. They were praised for strong ticket sales, volunteer turnout, and integration of para-sport events into the main program as medal events.
