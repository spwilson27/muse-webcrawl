Spice mix

A spice mix or spice blend is a mixture of two or more spices or herbs combined to achieve a balanced, reproducible flavour. Spice mixes may be prepared fresh at home, by spice merchants, or manufactured commercially, and they form the seasoning foundation of many regional cuisines.

Characteristics

Blending allows cooks to combine pungent, sweet, hot, and aromatic elements in fixed proportions so that a dish can be seasoned quickly and consistently. Whole spices are often toasted to release their volatile oils, then ground and combined. Proportions vary widely by region, household, and intended dish, so named blends are better understood as styles than as exact formulas.

Commercial production

Industrial blends are ground, mixed, and packaged at scale, sometimes with added salt, anti-caking agents, or dehydrated garlic and onion. Freshly ground mixtures lose potency over months as volatile aromatics dissipate, which is why many cooks prefer to grind small batches at home.
