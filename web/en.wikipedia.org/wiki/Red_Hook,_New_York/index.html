Red Hook, New York

Red Hook is a town in Dutchess County, New York, United States. The population was about 9,400 at the 2020 census. The town contains the villages of Red Hook and Tivoli, as well as the hamlets of Annandale-on-Hudson, home to Bard College, and Barrytown.

Geography

Red Hook lies in the northwestern corner of Dutchess County in the Hudson Valley. Its western border is the Hudson River, across from the city of Kingston and the Catskill Mountains. The town is largely rural, with farmland, orchards and estates, and is crossed north-to-south by U.S. Route 9.

History

The name derives from Dutch, after the red clay soil and a hook of land in the Hudson River near Tivoli, or alternatively after a tavern painted red at a crossroads. The town was formed from the town of Rhinebeck in 1812. Historic estates in the area include Montgomery Place, a Federal-style mansion in Annandale-on-Hudson.
