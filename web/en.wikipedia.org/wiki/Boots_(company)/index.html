Boots (company)

Boots
Type: Pharmacy and health/beauty retail chain
Founded: 1849, Nottingham, England
Founder: John Boot
Headquarters: Nottingham (Beeston), England
Parent: Walgreens Boots Alliance (from 2014)

Boots is a British health and beauty retailer and pharmacy chain, and one of the best-known names on the British high street. Founded in Nottingham in 1849 by John Boot as a herbal medicine shop, the company grew under his son Jesse Boot into the largest chemist chain in Britain, with its own manufacturing, research, and brands such as No7 cosmetics.

History

Jesse Boot transformed the family business in the late 19th century by selling proprietary medicines at low prices and opening hundreds of stores; the Boots factory site at Beeston, Nottingham, became a landmark of industrial architecture and mass production. The company developed the painkiller ibuprofen at its Nottingham research laboratories in the 1960s. In 2006 Boots merged with the European pharmacy group Alliance UniChem to form Alliance Boots, which was acquired by Walgreens in stages culminating in the creation of Walgreens Boots Alliance in 2014.

Operations

Boots operates around 2,000 stores in the United Kingdom and Ireland (figures have varied with restructuring), dispensing NHS prescriptions and selling medicines, toiletries, cosmetics, and optical and hearing services. Its own-brand ranges, including No7, Soap & Glory, and Boots Pharmaceuticals, are also sold internationally.
