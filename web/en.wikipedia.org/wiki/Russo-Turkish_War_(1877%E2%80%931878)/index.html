Russo-Turkish War (1877–1878)

Date: 1877–1878
Belligerents: Russian Empire and allies vs. Ottoman Empire
Result: Russian victory; Treaty of San Stefano, Congress of Berlin

The Russo-Turkish War of 1877–1878 was a conflict between the Ottoman Empire and a coalition led by the Russian Empire, including Romania, Serbia, Montenegro, and Bulgarian volunteers. It arose from Balkan nationalism and Ottoman suppression of uprisings in Bosnia and Bulgaria.

Course

Russian forces crossed the Danube, fought the costly Siege of Plevna, crossed the Balkan Mountains in winter, and advanced to the outskirts of Constantinople. A second front operated in the Caucasus, where Kars was captured.

Settlement

The Treaty of San Stefano created a large autonomous Bulgaria, but the great powers revised the settlement at the Congress of Berlin (1878). Romania, Serbia, and Montenegro gained full independence, and the war became a milestone in the liberation of the Balkans — Bulgaria celebrates 3 March, the San Stefano signing date, as Liberation Day.
