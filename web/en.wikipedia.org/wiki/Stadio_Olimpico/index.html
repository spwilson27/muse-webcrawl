The Stadio Olimpico is the largest sports facility in Rome, Italy, seating more than 68,000 spectators. It is located within the Foro Italico sports complex north of the city centre and is owned by the Italian National Olympic Committee. It serves as the home stadium of both of Rome's principal football clubs, Lazio and Roma, and hosts the Coppa Italia final.

History

A stadium first stood on the site in 1911 (the Stadio Nazionale del PNF). The present structure arose from reconstruction under architect Annibale Vitellozzi and was inaugurated on 17 May 1953 with a match between Italy and Hungary. It was the centrepiece of the 1960 Summer Olympics, hosting the opening and closing ceremonies and the athletics events.

Major events

The stadium hosted the 1968 and 1980 European Championship finals, four European Cup/Champions League finals (1977, 1984, 1996 and 2009), and the final of the 1990 FIFA World Cup, in which West Germany defeated Argentina. It has also staged the World Championships in Athletics (1987) and European Championships (1974 and 2024), the annual Golden Gala athletics meeting, and Italy's home matches in the Six Nations rugby championship since 2012. Extensive renovations, including the addition of a roof, were carried out for the 1990 World Cup.

Concerts

The Olimpico is also one of Italy's principal open-air concert venues, having hosted artists including U2, Madonna, Bruce Springsteen, Coldplay and Ed Sheeran.
