Eric Norman Thompson (4 November 1929 – 30 November 1982) was an English actor, television presenter and producer. He is best remembered as the narrator of the BBC children's television series The Magic Roundabout, for which he wrote the English scripts and voiced all the characters, including Dougal the dog, Zebedee, Brian the snail and Ermintrude the cow.

Career

Thompson studied at the Old Vic acting school and worked in repertory theatre before joining the BBC. In 1965 he was asked to rework the French stop-motion series Le Manège enchanté for British audiences; rather than translate the scripts, he watched the films with the sound off and invented entirely new storylines, which became beloved for their gentle wit aimed at adults as much as children. Some 500 five-minute episodes were produced.

He also presented programmes and appeared in television dramas and films. Thompson died of a heart attack in 1982, aged 53.

Family

He was married to the Scottish actress Phyllida Law; their daughters are the actresses Emma Thompson and Sophie Thompson.
