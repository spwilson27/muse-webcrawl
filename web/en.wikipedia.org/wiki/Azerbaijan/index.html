Azerbaijan

Azerbaijan, officially the Republic of Azerbaijan, is a country in the South Caucasus bordered by Russia, Georgia, Armenia, Iran and the Caspian Sea. Its population is about 10 million and its capital is Baku, a major Caspian port famous for its medieval walled Old City, the Maiden Tower and the modern Flame Towers.

Azerbaijan is a major oil and gas producer — Baku's fields were among the world's first and most productive in the late 19th century — and pipelines through Georgia and Turkey supply Europe. The country is majority Shia Muslim but officially secular, governed since 2003 by Ilham Aliyev, who succeeded his father. After decades of conflict with Armenia over Nagorno-Karabakh, Azerbaijan recaptured the region in 2020 and 2023. The exclave of Nakhchivan borders Turkey. Azerbaijan hosted the COP29 climate summit in Baku in 2024.
