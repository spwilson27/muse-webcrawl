SportsCenter

SportsCenter is an American daily sports news television program that serves as the flagship show of ESPN. It premiered on September 7, 1979, the day ESPN itself launched, and has since aired tens of thousands of editions — more episodes than any other program in American television history. The show presents highlights, scores, analysis, and interviews from across the sporting world.

History

Originally conceived by ESPN executives Chet Simmons and Scotty Connal and first anchored by George Grande and Lee Leonard, SportsCenter grew from a modest digest of scores into the centrepiece of sports television. Through the 1990s its blend of highlights and humour, delivered by anchor teams such as Dan Patrick and Keith Olbermann, Chris Berman, and later Stuart Scott, made it a cultural institution; its catchphrases and the long-running "This Is SportsCenter" advertising campaign (begun in 1995) became widely imitated. The program is produced from ESPN's studios in Bristol, Connecticut, with additional editions from Los Angeles and other locations.

Format

Editions air many times daily across ESPN's networks, ranging from concise morning updates to the late-night flagship show. Signature segments include the "Top 10" plays of the day and its inverse, the "Not Top 10". In the streaming and social-media era, the program has adapted with personality-led editions such as SportsCenter with Scott Van Pelt and dedicated digital versions.
