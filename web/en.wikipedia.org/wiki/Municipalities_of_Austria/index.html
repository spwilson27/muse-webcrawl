Municipalities of Austria

Municipalities (Gemeinden) are the smallest administrative divisions of Austria. There are roughly 2,100 municipalities, each governed by a municipal council and a mayor. They form the third tier of administration below the federal states (Bundesländer) and districts (Bezirke).

Categories

Municipalities may hold special designations. A Marktgemeinde (market municipality) is a town with historic market rights, while a Stadtgemeinde is a city. Fifteen cities are statutory cities (Statutarstädte), which administer their own district-level affairs in addition to municipal ones; Vienna is simultaneously a municipality, a statutory city, and a federal state.

Responsibilities

Municipalities are responsible for local services such as water supply, waste disposal, kindergartens, local roads, building permits, and civil registration. They are financed through shares of federal taxes, local taxes such as the municipal tax on business payrolls, and fees.

Variation

Austrian municipalities vary enormously in size, from Vienna with about two million inhabitants to tiny Alpine villages with fewer than a hundred residents.
