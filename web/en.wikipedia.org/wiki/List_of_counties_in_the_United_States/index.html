List of counties in the United States

This is a list of the 3,143 counties and county-equivalents in the United States. Counties are the primary administrative divisions of most U.S. states; two states, Louisiana and Alaska, use parishes and boroughs respectively, and several independent cities and federal districts are treated as county-equivalents by the Census Bureau.

Overview

Texas has the most counties of any state, with 254, while Delaware has the fewest, with only three (Kent, New Castle, and Sussex). The average county population is roughly 100,000, although Los Angeles County, California, with more than ten million residents, is more populous than most states, while Loving County, Texas, has fewer than 100 residents.

County-equivalents

County-equivalents include independent cities such as Baltimore, Maryland, and Carson City, Nevada; the boroughs and census areas of Alaska; the parishes of Louisiana; the District of Columbia; and the municipalities of Puerto Rico. Eight states are divided entirely into county-equivalents of a special type, such as Connecticut's planning regions.
