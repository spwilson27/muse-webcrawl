Krating Daeng

Krating Daeng, the original Thai energy drink

Krating Daeng (Thai: กระทิงแดง, "red gaur") is a sweetened, non-carbonated energy drink created in Thailand by Chaleo Yoovidhya and introduced in 1976. Marketed initially to Thai truck drivers and labourers, it later served as the basis for the Austrian drink Red Bull, developed after Dietrich Mateschitz encountered Krating Daeng in 1982; the two products remain distinct and are sold side by side in many markets.

Composition

Krating Daeng contains caffeine, taurine, B-group vitamins, sugar or sweeteners and other ingredients. It is typically sold in small 150 ml brown glass bottles with a label depicting two red gaurs charging at one another before a sun. The original Thai formulation is sweeter and uncarbonated compared with the Western Red Bull version.

History and business

Chaleo founded the T.C. Pharmaceutical company and launched Krating Daeng in 1976; it became Thailand's market-leading energy drink. His licensing partnership with Mateschitz led to the founding of Red Bull GmbH in 1984, in which the Yoovidhya family retained a majority stake. After Chaleo's death in 2012, his family continued to control the business.

Marketing

In Thailand the brand sponsors Muay Thai boxing, concerts and festivals, and its two-bull logo is one of the country's most recognisable trademarks. The drink is exported across Asia and beyond.
