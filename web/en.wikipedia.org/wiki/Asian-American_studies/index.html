Asian-American studies
Field: Ethnic studies
Origins: 1968-69 Third World Liberation Front strikes
Key institutions: San Francisco State University, UC Berkeley

Asian-American studies is an academic discipline that studies the history, politics, and culture of Asian Americans. It emerged from the student activism of the late 1960s, when the Third World Liberation Front strikes at San Francisco State University and the University of California, Berkeley led to the creation of the first ethnic studies programmes in the United States.

Scope

The field examines immigration, exclusion laws, internment, community formation, labour, literature and identity among Americans of East, Southeast and South Asian descent. Scholars debate the inclusiveness of the term "Asian American" and the field's relationship to Pacific Islander studies.

Organisations

The Association for Asian American Studies, founded in 1979, is the field's main professional body, publishing the Journal of Asian American Studies.
