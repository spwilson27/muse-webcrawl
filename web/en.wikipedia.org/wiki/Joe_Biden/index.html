Joe Biden

Joseph Robinette Biden Jr. (born 20 November 1942) is an American politician who served as the 46th president of the United States from 2021 to 2025. A member of the Democratic Party, he was vice president under Barack Obama (2009–2017) and a US senator from Delaware (1973–2009).

Early life and Senate

Born in Scranton and raised in Delaware, Biden studied at the University of Delaware and Syracuse Law School. Elected to the Senate at 29 in 1972, weeks before his wife Neilia and daughter died in a car crash, he served 36 years, chairing the Judiciary and Foreign Relations committees. He ran for president in 1988 and 2008.

Vice presidency

As Obama's vice president he oversaw stimulus implementation, advised on Iraq and Ukraine, and championed the Cancer Moonshot after his son Beau died in 2015.

Presidency

Biden defeated Donald Trump in 2020. His administration enacted the American Rescue Plan, the bipartisan Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act, the CHIPS and Science Act and the Inflation Reduction Act, rejoined the Paris Agreement, withdrew from Afghanistan, and led support for Ukraine after Russia's 2022 invasion. Amid concerns about his age, he withdrew from the 2024 race in July 2024, endorsing Kamala Harris; Trump won the election and succeeded him in January 2025.
