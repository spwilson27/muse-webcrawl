Bruce Schneier (born January 15, 1963) is an American cryptographer, computer security professional, privacy specialist, and writer. He is the author of the widely read 1994 book Applied Cryptography and of the long-running blog and newsletter "Schneier on Security".

Work

Schneier designed or co-designed the block ciphers Blowfish and Twofish, the stream cipher Solitaire, and the Skein hash function, a finalist in the NIST SHA-3 competition. He founded the security firm Counterpane (later BT Counterpane) and has testified before the US Congress on security and surveillance issues. He coined the term "security theater" for measures that provide the feeling of security without real benefit, and "Schneier's law" observes that anyone can invent a security system they themselves cannot break.

Books

His books include Beyond Fear (2003), Liars and Outliers (2012), Data and Goliath (2015), Click Here to Kill Everybody (2018), and A Hacker's Mind (2023).
