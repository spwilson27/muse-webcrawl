BMW M6

BMW M6
ManufacturerBMW M GmbH
Production1983–1989; 2005–2018
ClassGrand tourer

The BMW M6 is the high-performance version of the BMW 6 Series grand tourer, developed by BMW M GmbH. The first generation, sold as the M635CSi in Europe (and M6 in North America and Japan), was produced from 1983 to 1989 and used the M88 straight-six engine derived from the BMW M1 supercar.

Generations

The nameplate returned in 2005 with the E63/E64 M6, powered by the 5.0-litre S85 V10 shared with the E60 M5. The third generation (F06/F12/F13, 2012–2018) switched to a 4.4-litre twin-turbocharged V8 and added a four-door Gran Coupé. The M6 was effectively succeeded by the BMW M8 in 2019.
