Måns Petter Albert Sahlén Zelmerlöw (born 13 June 1986) is a Swedish pop singer, television presenter and actor. He is best known internationally as the winner of the Eurovision Song Contest 2015 with the song "Heroes", which became a chart success across Europe.

Early life

Zelmerlöw was born in Lund, Skåne, to Birgitta Sahlén, a professor of speech therapy at Lund University, and surgeon Sven-Olof Zelmerlöw. He grew up in Lund and studied music at Lars-Erik Larsson-gymnasiet, singing in the school choir and taking part in a school production of Joseph and the Amazing Technicolor Dreamcoat.

Career

Zelmerlöw first came to public attention in 2005 as a contestant on the Swedish version of Idol, finishing in fifth place. In 2006 he won the first season of the Swedish edition of Dancing with the Stars (Let's Dance) and starred as Danny Zuko in a Malmö production of Grease.

He competed in Melodifestivalen, Sweden's Eurovision selection, three times: in 2007 with "Cara Mia" (third place), in 2009 with "Hope & Glory", and in 2015 with "Heroes", which won the contest and went on to triumph at the Eurovision Song Contest in Vienna with 365 points — at the time the third-highest score in the contest's history. The song's staging, featuring an animated stick-figure companion, became one of the contest's most memorable performances.

Zelmerlöw has released several studio albums, including Stand by For… (2007), MZW (2009), Barcelona Sessions (2014), Perfectly Damaged (2015), Chameleon (2016) and Time (2019). His singles include "Brother Oh Brother", "Miss America" and "Should've Gone Home".

Presenting

Alongside his music career, Zelmerlöw has become one of Sweden's most familiar television presenters, hosting the long-running sing-along show Allsång på Skansen from 2011 to 2013. He co-hosted the Eurovision Song Contest 2016 in Stockholm with Petra Mede, an edition widely praised for its humour, including the interval number "Love Love Peace Peace". He has also provided commentary for SVT's Eurovision broadcasts and co-hosted the BBC's Eurovision: You Decide.

Personal life

Zelmerlöw married English actress Ciara Janson in 2019; the couple have children and divide their time between Sweden and the United Kingdom. He is a supporter of charitable causes relating to children's health and has taken part in charity performances following the Eurovision victory.
