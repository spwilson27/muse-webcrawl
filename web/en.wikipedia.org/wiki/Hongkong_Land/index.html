Hongkong Land

Hongkong Land (Chinese: 香港置地) is a property investment, management and development group and a member of the Jardine Matheson Group. Founded in 1889 as The Hongkong Land Investment and Agency Co. Ltd. by Sir Paul Chater and James Johnstone Keswick, it is one of Asia's leading property groups.

Holdings

In Hong Kong's Central district the company owns and manages some 450,000 square metres of prime office and luxury retail space — including Exchange Square, Jardine House, Alexandra House, Chater House, Prince's Building and the Landmark complex — making it the largest landlord in Central. These buildings are linked by an extensive footbridge network.

Regional portfolio

Beyond Hong Kong, Hongkong Land develops premium residential and mixed-use properties across Asia, including in Singapore, Beijing, Shanghai, Bangkok, Jakarta and Manila. The company is incorporated in Bermuda and has primary listings in London and Singapore, with a secondary listing in Bermuda.

History

Founded during the era of land reclamation that created much of modern Central, the company was central to Hong Kong's commercial development throughout the twentieth century and survived the property crises of the 1980s and 1990s as part of the Jardine group.
