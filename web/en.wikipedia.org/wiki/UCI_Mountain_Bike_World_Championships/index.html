UCI Mountain Bike World Championships

SportMountain biking
Governing bodyUCI
First held1990
FrequencyAnnual

The UCI Mountain Bike World Championships are the annual world championship events for mountain bike racing, organized by the Union Cycliste Internationale (UCI). The championships award the coveted rainbow jersey to the winner of each discipline, who wears it in competition for the following year.

Disciplines

The championships include cross-country Olympic (XCO), cross-country short track (XCC), cross-country marathon (XCM), cross-country eliminator (XCE), downhill (DHI), and team relay events, across elite, under-23, and junior categories. Four-cross was contested in earlier years before being discontinued. Since 2000 the championships have also incorporated the UCI Trials World Championships in some editions, and from 2017 the mountain bike championships were combined with other cycling disciplines in later "super" world championship formats.

History

The first Mountain Bike World Championships were held in 1990 in Durango, Colorado, United States, featuring cross-country and downhill events. The event has since been hosted at venues around the world, including Mont-Sainte-Anne in Canada, Val di Sole in Italy, and Les Gets in France, which are among the most frequent hosts.
