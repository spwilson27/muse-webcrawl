Viz Media, LLC is an American entertainment company headquartered in San Francisco, California, focused on publishing manga and distributing and licensing Japanese anime, films and television series. It was founded in 1986 as Viz Communications by Seiji Horibuchi and is jointly owned by the Japanese publishing companies Shogakukan and Shueisha.

Viz Media is one of the largest publishers of manga in North America.

History

Viz began by publishing translated manga for the American market, initially in a left-to-right "flopped" format before adopting the Japanese right-to-left reading order in the early 2000s. Its magazine Shonen Jump (2002–2012) introduced series such as Naruto, Bleach and One Piece to a mass North American readership; the digital successor, the Shonen Jump subscription service, launched in 2018.

Imprints and distribution

Viz publishes under imprints including Shonen Jump, Shojo Beat, Signature, Haikasoru (science fiction and fantasy literature) and Perfect Square (children's books). It is also a major licensor of anime home video, including Pokémon, Sailor Moon and JoJo's Bizarre Adventure.
