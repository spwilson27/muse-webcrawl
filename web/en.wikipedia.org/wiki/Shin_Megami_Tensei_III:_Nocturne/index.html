Shin Megami Tensei III: Nocturne

Shin Megami Tensei III: Nocturne is a role-playing video game developed and published by Atlus. It is the third numbered entry in the Shin Megami Tensei series, itself a part of the larger Megami Tensei franchise. It was released for the PlayStation 2 in Japan in 2003, and internationally in 2004–2005.

Story

The game is set in modern-day Tokyo, which is destroyed in an apocalyptic event called the Conception and transformed into the Vortex World, a spherical world overrun by demons. The protagonist, a high school student who survives and becomes a half-demon known as the Demi-fiend, must navigate factions competing to recreate the world according to their own philosophies, called Reasons, or choose other endings.

Gameplay

Nocturne uses the turn-based "Press Turn" battle system, in which hitting an enemy's weakness or landing a critical hit grants extra turns while missing or having attacks repelled costs turns. The player negotiates with demons to recruit them and fuses them to create new ones, a hallmark of the series.

Versions and legacy

An expanded Japanese director's cut, Maniax, added new content including Dante from Capcom's Devil May Cry series as a guest character; this version became the basis for the international release. A remaster, Shin Megami Tensei III Nocturne HD Remaster, was released in 2020–2021 for modern platforms. The game is acclaimed for its atmosphere, challenge, and philosophical story.
