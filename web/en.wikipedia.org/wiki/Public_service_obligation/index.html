Public service obligation

A public service obligation (PSO) is a requirement imposed by a government on a transport operator or other service provider to maintain services that would not be commercially viable but are considered socially or economically necessary, usually in exchange for subsidy or exclusive rights.

Air transport

In the European Union, PSOs are widely used to sustain air links to remote and island regions, under Regulation (EC) No 1008/2008. Member states may impose minimum frequencies, capacity and maximum fares on specified routes and tender them to carriers. The United States operates a similar scheme, Essential Air Service, for small communities.

Other sectors

Comparable obligations exist in rail franchising, ferry services, postal universal service and telecommunications, ensuring minimum service levels in uneconomic areas.
