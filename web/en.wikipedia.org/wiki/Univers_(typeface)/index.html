Univers

ClassificationNeo-grotesque sans-serif
DesignerAdrian Frutiger
FoundryDeberny & Peignot
Date released1957

Univers is a neo-grotesque sans-serif typeface designed by Adrian Frutiger and released by the French foundry Deberny & Peignot in 1957. It was one of the first type families conceived as a systematic range of weights and widths, marked by a rational two-digit numbering system (for example, Univers 55 for the regular weight and 67 for bold condensed).

Design

Univers shares the Swiss International Style heritage of Helvetica, released the same year, but Frutiger's design has subtler modulation and more open apertures; it avoids the "quirks" of earlier grotesques in favor of a consistent, modular family of 21 variants at launch. The numbering scheme later influenced Frutiger's own eponymous typeface and other families.

Usage

Univers has been widely used for corporate identity and signage: it was long the corporate typeface of companies such as Swiss International Air Lines and Deutsche Bank (in a custom version), was used extensively for the Munich 1972 Olympic Games identity, and featured in the Montreal Metro and many airport wayfinding systems.
