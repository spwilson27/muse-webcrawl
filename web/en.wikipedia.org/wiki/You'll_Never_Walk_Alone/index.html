You'll Never Walk Alone

"You'll Never Walk Alone" is a show tune from the 1945 Rodgers and Hammerstein musical Carousel. In the musical, Nettie Fowler, the cousin of the protagonist Julie Jordan, sings it to comfort Julie after her husband Billy Bigelow dies. It is reprised in the final scene as Billy is given a chance to redeem himself, and the graduating class joins in the song.

Football anthem

The song became famous beyond the theatre as the anthem of Liverpool Football Club. A 1963 recording by the Liverpool group Gerry and the Pacemakers topped the UK charts, and supporters on the Kop at Anfield adopted it; the words "You'll Never Walk Alone" appear on the club crest and above the Shankly Gates at the ground. The song is sung before every home match and has become a symbol of solidarity, notably after the Hillsborough disaster of 1989. It has since been adopted by other clubs, including Celtic and Borussia Dortmund.

Recordings

Many artists have recorded the song, among them Frank Sinatra, Elvis Presley, Johnny Cash, Judy Garland and Barbra Streisand. In 2020, a version by Captain Tom Moore, Michael Ball and the NHS Voices of Care choir reached number one in the UK during the COVID-19 pandemic.
