European route E35 is a north–south Class A intermediate road in the International E-road network, running approximately 1,647 km from Amsterdam in the Netherlands through Germany and Switzerland to Rome in Italy.

Route

From Amsterdam the route follows Dutch motorways to the German border, then German autobahns through Cologne and Frankfurt to Basel. In Switzerland it follows the A2 motorway through Lucerne and the Gotthard Tunnel to the Italian border at Chiasso, then continues via Milan, Bologna and Florence to Rome.

Significance

The E35 is one of the principal north–south freight corridors of Europe, linking the port of Rotterdam's hinterland with northern Italy across the Alps; the Gotthard section is among the most heavily used Alpine crossings.
