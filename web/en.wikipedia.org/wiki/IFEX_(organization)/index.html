IFEX (organization)

Global network defending freedom of expression

The IFEX logo

IFEX, formerly the International Freedom of Expression Exchange, is a global network of more than one hundred independent non-governmental organisations that defend and promote freedom of expression as a fundamental human right. Founded in 1992 and based in Toronto, Canada, its secretariat coordinates joint advocacy, monitoring and campaigns among member groups.

Work

IFEX circulates alerts and reports from its members about attacks on journalists, writers and media outlets, runs global campaigns such as the International Day to End Impunity for Crimes Against Journalists (2 November), and co-ordinates advocacy at the United Nations and regional bodies. Prominent members include press-freedom and free-expression groups from every region of the world.

History

The network grew out of a 1992 meeting in Montreal of freedom-of-expression organisations that wanted a rapid system for exchanging information about violations; the "exchange" model of sharing alerts became the network's hallmark before it shortened its name to IFEX.
