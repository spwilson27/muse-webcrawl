Mockingjay is a 2010 dystopian young adult novel by American author Suzanne Collins. It is the third and final instalment of The Hunger Games trilogy, following The Hunger Games (2008) and Catching Fire (2009). The novel continues the story of Katniss Everdeen, who, after twice surviving the Capitol's Hunger Games, becomes the symbolic leader — the "Mockingjay" — of a full-scale rebellion by the districts of Panem against the tyrannical Capitol led by President Coriolanus Snow.

Plot

After the destroyed arena of the Quarter Quell, Katniss is taken to District 13, long believed destroyed, which is preparing open war. District 12 has been firebombed. Katniss reluctantly agrees to serve as the rebels' propaganda figurehead while Peeta Mellark, captured by the Capitol, is "hijacked" through fear conditioning to hate her. As the rebellion advances on the Capitol, Katniss joins a military unit fighting through the booby-trapped capital. The war ends with Snow's capture, but at devastating personal cost, including the death of Katniss's sister Prim. Facing a new leader, Alma Coin, who proposes a final Hunger Games using Capitol children, Katniss assassinates Coin instead of Snow at the execution, and Snow dies shortly afterward. Katniss eventually returns to a ruined District 12, where she and Peeta slowly rebuild their lives together.

Themes

The novel explores the costs of war, propaganda and media manipulation, trauma and post-traumatic stress, the moral equivalence of opposing regimes that use the same brutal methods, and the cycle of vengeance. Collins has cited the Iraq War and reality television as influences on the trilogy's treatment of spectacle and conflict.

Reception

Mockingjay debuted at number one on bestseller lists and sold over 450,000 copies in its first week in the United States. Reviews were generally positive, though some critics found the pacing uneven and the ending bleak; its unflinching depiction of the psychological toll of war was widely praised.

Film adaptations

The novel was adapted into two films, The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 1 (2014) and Part 2 (2015), directed by Francis Lawrence and starring Jennifer Lawrence as Katniss. Together they grossed over $1.4 billion worldwide.
