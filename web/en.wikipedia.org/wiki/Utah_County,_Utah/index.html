Utah County, Utah

County seat: Provo

Largest city: Provo

Population: approx. 660,000 (2020 census)

Named for: Ute people / Utah Lake

Utah County is a county in the U.S. state of Utah, located in north-central Utah along the Wasatch Front. Its county seat and largest city is Provo. As of the 2020 census it had a population of about 660,000, making it the second-most populous county in the state after Salt Lake County.

Geography

The county is centred on Utah Lake, the largest freshwater lake in Utah, and the Provo River valley, bounded by the Wasatch Range to the east — including Mount Timpanogos — and the Lake Mountains to the west. Fast-growing cities such as Lehi have made its northern end part of the "Silicon Slopes" tech corridor.

Demographics and culture

Utah County is home to Brigham Young University in Provo and Utah Valley University in Orem, two of the largest universities in the state. It has one of the highest proportions of members of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints of any large U.S. county and is known for consistently conservative voting patterns, young median age and high birth rates.
