Chord (disambiguation)

Chord may refer to:

Music

  • Chord (music), a combination of three or more notes sounded simultaneously
  • Chord, an archaic term for a string of a musical instrument, as in "vocal chords" (a common misspelling of vocal cords)

Mathematics and engineering

  • Chord (geometry), a line segment joining two points on a curve
  • Chord (aeronautics), the width of a wing or airfoil from leading to trailing edge
  • Chord (graph theory), an edge joining two non-consecutive vertices of a cycle
  • Chord (truss construction), the top or bottom horizontal member of a truss

Computing

  • Chord (peer-to-peer), a distributed hash table protocol for peer-to-peer networks
  • Chord, a concurrency construct in the language Polyphonic C# and in Join Java
  • Chording, entering characters by pressing several keys at once, as on a chorded keyboard

Other uses

  • Chord (astronomy), a line crossing a body used in occultation measurements
  • Chord, a former railway line — the Chord of the Dartford Loop and other routes
  • Honda Chord, a mid-size car sold in Japan
