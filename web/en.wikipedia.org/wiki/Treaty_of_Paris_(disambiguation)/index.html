Treaty of Paris (disambiguation)

Many treaties, conventions and agreements have been signed in or named after Paris, France. Treaty of Paris may refer to:

13th–18th centuries

  • Treaty of Paris (1259), between Henry III of England and Louis IX of France
  • Treaty of Paris (1303), between England and France
  • Treaty of Paris (1657), between England and France against Spain
  • Treaty of Paris (1718), forming the Quadruple Alliance
  • Treaty of Paris (1763), ending the Seven Years' War
  • Treaty of Paris (1782), between Britain and the United States
  • Treaty of Paris (1783), ending the American Revolutionary War
  • Treaty of Paris (1796), ending the war between France and Sardinia

19th century

  • Treaty of Paris (1801), between France and Russia
  • Treaty of Paris (1810), ending the war between France and Sweden
  • Treaty of Paris (1814) and Treaty of Paris (1815), after the Napoleonic Wars
  • Treaty of Paris (1856), ending the Crimean War
  • Treaty of Paris (1898), ending the Spanish–American War

20th century

  • Treaty of Paris (1900), between Spain and France over Río Muni
  • Paris Peace Treaties of 1919–1920 following the First World War, including the Treaty of Versailles
  • Treaty of Paris (1947), peace treaties with Italy, Romania, Hungary, Bulgaria and Finland after the Second World War
  • Treaty of Paris (1951), establishing the European Coal and Steel Community
  • Paris Peace Accords (1973), ending United States involvement in the Vietnam War

21st century

  • Paris Agreement (2015), the international climate agreement under the UNFCCC
