East Renfrewshire

East Renfrewshire (Scots: Aest Renfrewshire; Scottish Gaelic: Siorrachd Rinn Friù an Ear) is one of 32 council areas of Scotland. It borders Glasgow to the north and takes its name from the historic county of Renfrewshire. Its administrative centre is Giffnock, and its largest town is Newton Mearns.

The council area was created in 1996 from the former Eastwood district and parts of Renfrew district. Much of the area is affluent suburban housing for Glasgow commuters, including the towns of Newton Mearns, Clarkston, Giffnock and Eaglesham, alongside the more rural and historically industrial town of Barrhead and the village of Neilston.

Education and demographics

East Renfrewshire is frequently ranked among the best-performing local authority areas in Scotland for school attainment. It has a population of around 96,000 and includes a significant Jewish community, historically centred on Newton Mearns and Giffnock, which is home to Scotland's only Jewish primary school.
