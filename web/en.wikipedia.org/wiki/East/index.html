East

East is one of the four cardinal directions or points of the compass. It is the opposite direction from west and is perpendicular to north and south. By convention, east is to the right on most maps. East is the direction toward which the Earth rotates about its axis, and is therefore the general direction from which the Sun appears to rise.

The word "east" is formed from the Old English ēast and is cognate with words in other Germanic languages; it is commonly believed to derive from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "dawn" or "to shine." Similarly, the Latin word for east, oriens, means "rising" (as in the rising sun), giving rise to the term "the Orient."

Cultural uses

In many cultures and religions, east is symbolically associated with sunrise, birth, and renewal. Churches were traditionally oriented with the altar at the eastern end. In geopolitical usage, "the East" historically referred to Asia and the Middle East from a European perspective, in contrast to "the West."
