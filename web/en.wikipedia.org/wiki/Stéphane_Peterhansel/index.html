Stéphane Peterhansel (born 6 August 1965) is a French rally driver. He is the most successful competitor in the history of the Dakar Rally, with a record 14 overall victories — six in the motorcycle category with Yamaha and eight in the car category with Mitsubishi, Mini, Peugeot, and Audi. His dominance earned him the nickname "Monsieur Dakar".

Career

Peterhansel began his career in enduro racing, winning French national titles before entering the Paris-Dakar Rally in 1988. He won the motorcycle class in 1991, 1992, 1993, 1995, 1997, and 1998. After Yamaha withdrew from the event he switched to cars, initially with Nissan, and began his car-winning streak with Mitsubishi in 2004, adding wins in 2005 and 2007. He later won with the X-raid Mini team in 2012 and 2013, with Peugeot in 2016 and 2017, and again with Mini in 2021 in Saudi Arabia. In 2022 he joined Audi's hybrid RS Q e-tron programme.

Other achievements

Outside the Dakar, Peterhansel has won the Abu Dhabi Desert Challenge and other rounds of the World Rally-Raid Championship multiple times, and with his wife Andrea Peterhansel (née Mayer), herself a former Dakar competitor, he contested the 2019 Abu Dhabi event as a husband-and-wife crew. He is widely regarded as the greatest rally raid driver of all time for his combination of speed, navigation judgement, and mechanical sympathy over the sport's longest and harshest events.
