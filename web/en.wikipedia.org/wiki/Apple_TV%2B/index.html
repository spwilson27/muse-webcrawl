Apple TV+

Apple TV+ is a subscription streaming television service owned and operated by Apple Inc. Launched on November 1, 2019, it offers a selection of original production films and television series, branded as Apple Originals.

Owner: Apple Inc.
Type: Streaming service
Launched: November 1, 2019
Original programming: Apple Originals

Programming

Apple TV+ launched with series such as The Morning Show, See, For All Mankind, and Dickinson. The comedy series Ted Lasso became one of its most acclaimed titles, winning multiple Emmy Awards. The film CODA won the Academy Award for Best Picture in 2022, making Apple TV+ the first streaming service to win that honour.

Availability

The service is available through the Apple TV app on Apple devices, smart televisions, streaming sticks, game consoles, and on the web. It is also bundled in Apple One subscription packages.
