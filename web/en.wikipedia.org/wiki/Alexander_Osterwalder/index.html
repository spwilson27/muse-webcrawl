Alexander Osterwalder (born 1974) is a Swiss business theorist, author and entrepreneur best known as the inventor of the Business Model Canvas, a widely used visual template for developing and documenting business models.

Career

Osterwalder studied political science and earned a PhD in management information systems at the University of Lausanne, where his doctoral research formalised a "business model ontology" — the nine building blocks (customer segments, value propositions, channels, customer relationships, revenue streams, key resources, key activities, key partnerships and cost structure) that became the Canvas. He is a co-founder of the consultancy Strategyzer, which develops tools and training around business model innovation.

Books

His book Business Model Generation (2010), co-created with Yves Pigneur and hundreds of practitioners worldwide, became an international bestseller and popularised the Canvas among startups and large companies alike. He followed it with Value Proposition Design (2014), Testing Business Ideas (2019) and The Invincible Company (2020). Osterwalder has appeared on management-thinker rankings such as the Thinkers50.
