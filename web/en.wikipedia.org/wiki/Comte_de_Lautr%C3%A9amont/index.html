Comte de Lautréamont

Comte de Lautréamont was the pen name of Isidore-Lucien Ducasse (4 April 1846 – 24 November 1870), a French poet born in Montevideo, Uruguay. He is known mainly for the prose poem Les Chants de Maldoror (The Songs of Maldoror) and the Poésies.

Life and work

Ducasse was raised in Uruguay and sent to France for his education. He published the first canto of Maldoror anonymously in 1868 and the complete work in 1869 under the Lautréamont pseudonym; the book, a nightmarish, violent epic centred on the misanthropic Maldoror, was scarcely noticed in his lifetime. He died in Paris aged 24 during the Siege of Paris.

Legacy

Rediscovered by the Surrealists in the 20th century, who celebrated its dreamlike imagery and revolt against convention, Les Chants de Maldoror became a classic of French literature and a forerunner of modernism.
