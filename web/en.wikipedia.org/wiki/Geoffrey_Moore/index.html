Geoffrey Moore

Geoffrey Alexander Moore

Born: July 31, 1946, Portland, Oregon, U.S.

Occupation: Author, consultant

Known for: Crossing the Chasm

Geoffrey Alexander Moore (born July 31, 1946) is an American organizational theorist, management consultant and author, known for his work on the marketing of high-technology products and the technology adoption life cycle.

Crossing the Chasm

Moore's best-known book, Crossing the Chasm (1991), describes the challenges faced by innovators in marketing disruptive technology products. Building on Everett Rogers's diffusion of innovations model, Moore argues that there is a "chasm" between early adopters (visionaries) and the early majority (pragmatists) that many technology companies fail to cross. The book became a standard reference in high-tech marketing.

Other works

His subsequent books include Inside the Tornado (1995), The Gorilla Game (1998, with Paul Johnson and Tom Kippola), Living on the Fault Line (2000), Dealing with Darwin (2005), Escape Velocity (2011) and Zone to Win (2015). He is a founder of the Chasm Group and a managing director of Geoffrey Moore Consulting.
