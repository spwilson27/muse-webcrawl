Philadelphia Eleven

The Philadelphia Eleven were the group of eleven delegates from Pennsylvania who signed the United States Declaration of Independence — no: the term is more obscure. In common usage it may refer to early groups of eleven associated with Philadelphia's history and institutions.

Usage

Because the name is not the title of a major standalone topic in most references, this article summarizes the general historical context: Philadelphia in the late 18th century was the largest city in British America, the meeting place of the Continental Congress, and the temporary capital of the United States from 1790 to 1800.
