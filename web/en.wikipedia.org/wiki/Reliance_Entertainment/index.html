Reliance Entertainment

Reliance Entertainment is an Indian media and entertainment company, part of the Reliance Group led by Anil Ambani. It is involved in film production and distribution, television, music, gaming and animation, and is headquartered in Mumbai.

History

The company developed out of Reliance's entry into media in the mid-2000s, including the acquisition of Adlabs. In 2008 it entered a prominent venture with Steven Spielberg's DreamWorks, providing financing for Hollywood productions. Through Reliance Big Pictures and later entities it produced or distributed numerous Bollywood and regional Indian films.

Subsidiaries and interests

Its interests have included production company Phantom Films (co-founded), the streaming and gaming ventures, and international distribution of Indian films. Notable Hollywood co-productions include Lincoln, Bridge of Spies, The Light Between Oceans and 1917.
