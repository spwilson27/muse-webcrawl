Puebla

Capital: Puebla (city)
Population: about 6.6 million
Area: about 34,300 km²
Country: Mexico

Puebla (Spanish pronunciation: [ˈpweβla]), officially the Free and Sovereign State of Puebla, is one of the 32 states of Mexico, located in east-central Mexico. Its capital and largest city is Puebla. The state is bordered by Veracruz to the north and east, Hidalgo, Tlaxcala, and Mexico (state) to the west, and Morelos, Guerrero, and Oaxaca to the south.

Geography

Much of Puebla lies on the Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt. The volcanoes Popocatépetl and Iztaccíhuatl stand on its western border, while Citlaltépetl (Pico de Orizaba), Mexico's highest mountain, straddles the Veracruz border. The state's terrain ranges from high valleys around the capital to the tropical lowlands of the north and the dry Mixteca region in the south.

History and economy

Before the Spanish conquest the region was home to peoples including the Nahua, Mixtec, and Popoloca, and the great pyramid city of Cholula. The state is named after its capital, founded in 1531. The 1862 Battle of Puebla, in which Mexican forces defeated a French army on 5 May, is commemorated as Cinco de Mayo. Economically, Puebla is a major manufacturing centre, notably for automobiles — Volkswagen's Puebla plant is one of the company's largest — as well as textiles and food processing. Its cuisine, including mole poblano and chiles en nogada, is celebrated throughout Mexico.
