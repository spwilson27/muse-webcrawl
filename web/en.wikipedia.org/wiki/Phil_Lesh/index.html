Phil Lesh

Philip Chapman Lesh (March 15, 1940 – October 25, 2024) was an American musician, best known as a founding member and bassist of the Grateful Dead. With a background in classical music and trumpet, Lesh brought an innovative, melodic approach to the electric bass, treating it as a counterpoint instrument rather than only a rhythm anchor.

After the Grateful Dead disbanded following Jerry Garcia's death in 1995, Lesh performed with his own bands, including Phil Lesh and Friends, and later with Furthur alongside Bob Weir. He also co-founded the Terrapin Crossroads venue in San Rafael, California.

Musical style

Lesh's playing was influenced by classical composers and jazz, featuring improvisation, complex harmonies and frequent use of the upper register of the bass.
