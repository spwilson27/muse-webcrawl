Po (river)

CountryItaly
Length~652 km
MouthAdriatic Sea (Po Delta)
[Image: The Po river in the Po Valley]
The Po river in the Po Valley

The Po is the longest river in Italy, flowing eastward about 652 km from Monte Viso in the Cottian Alps across the whole of northern Italy to a wide delta in the Adriatic Sea south of Venice.

Course

From its Alpine source the Po crosses Piedmont past Turin, then traverses the broad Po Valley (Pianura Padana) — Italy's industrial and agricultural heartland — passing Piacenza, Cremona and Ferrara before splitting into distributaries across its delta. Tributaries from the Alps and Apennines, including the Ticino, Adda, Oglio, Mincio and Tanaro, swell the river along its course.

Issues

The valley's intensive farming and industry make heavy demands on the river, and in 2022 the Po suffered its worst drought in decades, with saline water intruding far up the delta. Flooding has historically been the opposite danger; embankments line much of the lower river.
