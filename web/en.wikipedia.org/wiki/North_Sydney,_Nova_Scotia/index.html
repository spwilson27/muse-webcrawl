Overview

North Sydney is a community in the Cape Breton Regional Municipality of Nova Scotia, Canada, on the northern shore of Sydney Harbour in Cape Breton Island. It is the Atlantic terminus of the ferry service to Newfoundland, one of the community's defining roles.

History

Settled in the late eighteenth and early nineteenth centuries, North Sydney grew with shipbuilding, fishing, and trade, and by the late nineteenth century it was one of the busiest ports in Canada. During both world wars it served as a convoy assembly and naval port. In 1995 the town was amalgamated into the Cape Breton Regional Municipality.

Ferry terminal

Marine Atlantic operates year-round ferries from North Sydney to Port aux Basques, Newfoundland, and a seasonal service to Argentia. The route forms part of the Trans-Canada Highway system, linking the island of Newfoundland to the mainland road network.
