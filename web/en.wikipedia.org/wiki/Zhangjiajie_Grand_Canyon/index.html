Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon

Location: Cili County, Zhangjiajie, Hunan, China

Feature: Zhangjiajie Glass Bridge

Bridge opened: 20 August 2016

Bridge length: 430 m; height above canyon floor: about 300 m

The Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon (Chinese: 张家界大峡谷) is a scenic canyon area in Cili County, near the city of Zhangjiajie in Hunan Province, China. It is best known internationally as the site of the Zhangjiajie Glass Bridge, one of the world's longest and highest glass-bottomed bridges.

The canyon

The scenic area encompasses cliffs, waterfalls, streams, and dense subtropical forest, along with a glass walkway, a zip line, and one of the world's longest bungee jumps from the glass bridge. It lies near, but is separate from, the famous quartzite pillar landscape of Zhangjiajie National Forest Park and the Wulingyuan Scenic Area.

Glass bridge

Designed by Israeli architect Haim Dotan, the bridge spans 430 metres between two cliffs, suspended about 300 metres above the canyon floor, with a deck of transparent glass panels 6 metres wide. When it opened in August 2016 it set records as the longest and tallest glass-bottomed bridge in the world; it temporarily closed two weeks after opening because visitor numbers far exceeded expectations, then reopened with crowd-control measures. The bridge has since been joined by other record-setting glass bridges across China.
