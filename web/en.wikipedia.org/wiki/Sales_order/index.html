Sales order

A sales order is a commercial document prepared by a seller and issued to a customer confirming the sale of goods or services. It records the products or services ordered, quantities, agreed prices, delivery details and payment terms. The sales order is generated internally by the seller in response to a customer's purchase order, and it forms the basis for the subsequent fulfilment, shipping and invoicing processes.

Contents

A sales order usually includes the seller's and buyer's names and addresses, an order number and date, item descriptions and quantities, unit prices and totals, applicable taxes, shipping terms and method, and payment terms. In enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems, the sales order is a central object: it reserves inventory, triggers picking and packing in the warehouse, schedules production in make-to-order environments, and ultimately leads to the delivery note and the invoice.

Process

The typical order-to-cash cycle flows from quotation to purchase order (issued by the buyer), then to the sales order (created by the seller), followed by order fulfilment, delivery, invoicing and payment. A sales order acknowledgement may be sent to the customer as confirmation. Discrepancies between the purchase order and sales order—price, quantity or date—are resolved before fulfilment proceeds.
