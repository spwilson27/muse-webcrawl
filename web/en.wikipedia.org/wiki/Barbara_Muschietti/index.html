Barbara Muschietti

Barbara Muschietti (born December 22, 1971) is an Argentine film producer. She frequently collaborates with her younger brother, director Andy Muschietti, and together they founded the production company Nocturna. Born in Buenos Aires, she worked in production roles in Spain and Argentina before moving into producing features.

Career

Muschietti produced her brother's short film Mamá (2008), which attracted the attention of Guillermo del Toro and was expanded into the feature Mama (2013), which she also produced. She served as a producer on It (2017) and It Chapter Two (2019), the adaptations of Stephen King's novel that became the highest-grossing horror films of their time, and produced the DC film The Flash (2023), directed by her brother. Through Nocturna she has developed horror and genre projects for film and television, including the It prequel series Welcome to Derry.
