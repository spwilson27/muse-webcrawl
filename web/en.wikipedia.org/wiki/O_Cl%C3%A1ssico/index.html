O Clássico

The Estádio da Luz in Lisbon, home of Benfica.

O Clássico ("The Classic") is the name given to the football rivalry and matches between Portugal's two most successful clubs, S.L. Benfica of Lisbon and FC Porto. It is the biggest club match in Portuguese football.

History

The rivalry began in 1912 and intensified as the two clubs came to dominate the Primeira Liga. It also reflects a broader cultural and regional divide between the capital, Lisbon, and the northern city of Porto.

Honours comparison

Benfica hold the record for league titles, while Porto have been more successful in European competition, winning the European Cup/Champions League twice. Matches between the sides frequently decide the league championship.
