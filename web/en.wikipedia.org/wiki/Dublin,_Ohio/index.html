Dublin, Ohio

Dublin is a city in Franklin, Delaware, and Union counties in the U.S. state of Ohio. A suburb of Columbus, it is located on both banks of the Scioto River in the northwest of the Columbus metropolitan area. The population was 49,328 at the 2020 census. Dublin is home to the headquarters of several major companies and to the Memorial Tournament, an annual PGA Tour golf event hosted by Jack Nicklaus at Muirfield Village Golf Club.

History

Native American peoples including the Hopewell, Adena, and Shawnee inhabited the Scioto River valley long before European settlement. The village of Dublin was surveyed in 1810 and named by landowner John Sells after his birthplace, Dublin, Ireland. It remained a small farming community for more than a century. Dublin's growth accelerated after 1970 with suburban development around Columbus, the construction of Interstate 270, and the arrival of corporate headquarters; it incorporated as a city in 1987.

Economy

Dublin hosts the corporate headquarters of Cardinal Health, the Wendy's Company, and OCLC, as well as major facilities of other employers. The city is known for its corporate office parks, high-performing schools (Dublin City Schools), and extensive parkland along the Scioto River.

Events and culture

The Dublin Irish Festival, held each August since 1988, is one of the largest Irish cultural festivals in the United States. The Memorial Tournament has been played at Muirfield Village since 1976 and attracts leading professional golfers. The city also maintains whimsical public art, including the "Field of Corn" sculpture of 109 concrete ears of corn in Sam and Eulalia Frantz Park.

Geography

Dublin sits on the glaciated Allegheny Plateau along the Scioto River, which has carved limestone gorges through parts of the city. Indian Run Falls and numerous parks line the river corridor.
