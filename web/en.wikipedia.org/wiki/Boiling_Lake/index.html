Boiling Lake

The Boiling Lake is a flooded fumarole located in Morne Trois Pitons National Park near Laudat in Dominica, in the Caribbean. The lake is approximately 200 feet (61 m) across, and its greyish-blue water is usually obscured by a cloud of vapour. It is the world's second-largest hot lake, after Frying Pan Lake in New Zealand.

Description

The water along the lake's edges measures 82–92 °C, while the centre actively boils as gases — mainly steam, carbon dioxide and sulphur dioxide — escape from molten magma below. The lake was first documented in 1870. Its water level and activity fluctuate; in 1901 and occasionally since, it has partly drained before refilling.

Access

The lake is reached by a strenuous 13 km round-trip hike from Laudat through the Valley of Desolation, forming part of a UNESCO World Heritage Site. There is no direct road access.
