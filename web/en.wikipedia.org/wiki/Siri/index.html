Siri

Siri is a virtual assistant developed by Apple Inc., built into iOS, macOS, watchOS, tvOS and other Apple platforms. It uses voice queries, gesture-based control and natural-language processing to answer questions, make recommendations and perform actions such as sending messages, setting reminders and searching the web.

History

Siri originated as an app from SRI International's CALO project, a DARPA-funded artificial intelligence research effort. Apple acquired Siri Inc. in 2010 and launched the assistant as a flagship feature of the iPhone 4S in October 2011. The original American voice was recorded by Susan Bennett.

Features and development

Siri supports dozens of languages and can be activated by voice ("Hey Siri" / "Siri"). Apple has progressively integrated on-device processing and, more recently, generative AI features under the Apple Intelligence programme, alongside competitor assistants such as Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant.
