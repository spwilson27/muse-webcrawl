Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse is a 2023 American animated superhero film featuring the Marvel Comics character Miles Morales / Spider-Man, produced by Columbia Pictures and Sony Pictures Animation in association with Marvel Entertainment, and distributed by Sony Pictures Releasing. It is the sequel to Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018) and the second film in the Spider-Verse franchise.

Plot

Miles Morales, Brooklyn's Spider-Man, reunites with Gwen Stacy and is catapulted across the multiverse, where he encounters the Spider-Society, a team of Spider-People led by Miguel O'Hara (Spider-Man 2099) charged with protecting the multiverse's stability. When Miles discovers that his own origin disrupted a "canon event" — including the impending death of his father, police captain Jefferson Davis — he clashes with the Society over whether such events must be allowed to happen, and escapes across dimensions as they pursue him. The film ends on a cliffhanger, with Miles trapped on Earth-42 confronting a version of himself who became the Prowler. The principal antagonist, the Spot, grows from a comic-relief villain into a multiversal threat.

Production

The film was directed by Joaquim Dos Santos, Kemp Powers, and Justin K. Thompson from a screenplay by Phil Lord, Christopher Miller, and David Callaham. Its animation combined multiple distinct visual styles for each universe visited, including a watercolor-inspired look for Gwen's world and a punk collage aesthetic for Spider-Punk. Daniel Pemberton composed the score, and Metro Boomin produced the soundtrack album.

Reception

Released on June 2, 2023, the film grossed $690.9 million worldwide and received widespread critical acclaim for its animation, story, and voice acting. It was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature. A sequel, Spider-Man: Beyond the Spider-Verse, was announced as the concluding chapter of the trilogy.
