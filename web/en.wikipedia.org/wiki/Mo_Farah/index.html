Sir Mohamed Muktar Jama Farah (born Hussein Abdi Kahin, 23 March 1983) is a Somali-born British retired long-distance runner. He is the most successful British track athlete in modern Olympic history, winning the 5000 metres and 10,000 metres at both the 2012 London Olympics and the 2016 Rio Olympics — the "double-double".

Career

Farah was born in Mogadishu, Somalia, and was trafficked to the United Kingdom as a child, a history he revealed publicly in 2022. Spotted by a school PE teacher in London, he rose through junior athletics and won his first major senior title at the 2010 European Championships. Moving to train under Alberto Salazar in Oregon in 2011, he won world titles at 5000 m and 10,000 m, completing a run of ten consecutive global championship golds in distance finals between 2011 and 2017.

His victory celebrations popularised the "Mobot" gesture. In 2017 he moved to road racing, winning the Chicago Marathon in 2018 in a European record, before retiring in 2023. He was knighted in 2017.
