Ryzen

ManufacturerAMD
LaunchedMarch 2017
ArchitectureZen family (Zen, Zen+, Zen 2, Zen 3, Zen 4, Zen 5)
MarketDesktop, laptop, workstation

Ryzen is a brand of multi-core x86-64 microprocessors designed and marketed by AMD. First released in March 2017, Ryzen processors are built on the Zen microarchitecture and its successors, and marked AMD's return to competitiveness in the high-performance CPU market after years of lagging behind Intel.

History

The first-generation Ryzen 7, 5 and 3 chips, codenamed "Summit Ridge", launched in 2017 on the 14 nm process. Subsequent generations — Pinnacle Ridge, Matisse, Vermeer, Raphael and Granite Ridge — progressively increased core counts, clock speeds and instructions per clock. The high-end Ryzen Threadripper line targets workstations and enthusiasts with very high core counts.

Product lines

Ryzen products are segmented into Ryzen 3, 5, 7 and 9 tiers for consumers, Ryzen PRO for business, and mobile Ryzen parts (with integrated Radeon graphics) for laptops. Many models include AMD's Precision Boost dynamic frequency scaling and are unlocked for overclocking.

Reception

Ryzen was widely praised for its multi-threaded performance and value, forcing industry-wide increases in mainstream core counts.
