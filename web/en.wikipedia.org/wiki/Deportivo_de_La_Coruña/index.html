Deportivo de La Coruña

Real Club Deportivo de La Coruña (lit. Royal Sporting Club of La Coruña), commonly known as Deportivo La Coruña or simply Dépor, is a Spanish professional football club based in the city of A Coruña, Galicia. Founded in 1906, the club plays at the Estadio Riazor.

History

Deportivo's golden era came in the 1990s and early 2000s, when the "Super Dépor" sides of Arsenio Iglesias and Javier Irureta challenged Spain's giants. The club narrowly missed the La Liga title in 1994, losing it on the final day, before finally winning its only league championship in the 1999–2000 season. Dépor also won the Copa del Rey in 1995 and 2002 — the latter, the famous "Centenariazo," beating Real Madrid at the Bernabéu on Madrid's centenary — and reached the UEFA Champions League semi-finals in 2004 after a celebrated comeback against AC Milan.

Decline

Financial problems and relegations followed; the club dropped from La Liga in 2011, 2013 (after one season back), 2018, and in 2020 fell to the third tier for the first time in decades, before beginning a rebuilding process aimed at returning to professional football.
