List of United States visa types by purpose

A United States visa is a travel document issued by the U.S. Department of State that allows a foreign national to seek entry to the United States for a specific purpose. Visas are broadly divided into nonimmigrant visas (temporary stays) and immigrant visas (permanent residence). This article lists visa classifications grouped by purpose of travel.

Immigrant visas

Immigrant visas are issued in categories including immediate relatives of U.S. citizens (IR), family-sponsored preference categories (F1–F4), employment-based preferences (EB-1 through EB-5, including the EB-5 investor program), the Diversity Visa lottery (DV), and special immigrant categories (SI), such as religious workers and certain employees of the U.S. government abroad.
