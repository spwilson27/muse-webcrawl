Jason Isbell

Michael Jason Isbell (born 1 February 1979) is an American singer-songwriter and guitarist from Alabama, known for his work with the Drive-By Truckers and as frontman of Jason Isbell and the 400 Unit, earning multiple Grammy Awards in the Americana genre.

Career

Isbell joined the Drive-By Truckers at 21, writing songs such as "Decoration Day" and "Outfit", before leaving in 2007 amid struggles with alcohol. His solo breakthrough came with Southeastern (2013), recorded after achieving sobriety and featuring the much-covered song "Cover Me Up".

Acclaim

Albums including Something More Than Free (2015), The Nashville Sound (2017) and Weathervanes (2023) won Grammy Awards for Best Americana Album, and Isbell is widely regarded as one of his generation's leading songwriters. He appeared in the film Killers of the Flower Moon (2023).
