Stade de France

The Stade de France is the national stadium of France, located just north of Paris in Saint-Denis. With a capacity of 81,338, it is among the largest stadiums in Europe and serves as home to the France national football and rugby union teams.

Stade de France
Location: Saint-Denis, Paris, France
Opened: 28 January 1998
Capacity: 81,338
Tenants: France football and rugby teams

History

Built for the 1998 FIFA World Cup, it was inaugurated on 28 January 1998 with a friendly between France and Spain, and hosted the World Cup final in which France beat Brazil 3–0. It also hosted the 2003 World Athletics Championships, Rugby World Cups in 2007 and 2023, the UEFA Euro 2016 final, and athletics at the Paris 2024 Olympic Games.

Design

Architects Michel Macary, Aymeric Zublena, Michel Rémon and Claude Costantini designed a distinctive elliptical white roof suspended above the stands, with a movable lower tier that can retract to reveal an athletics track.

Events

The stadium hosts major concerts and the finals of the Coupe de France. In November 2015 it was targeted during the Paris attacks, with explosions outside during a France–Germany friendly.
