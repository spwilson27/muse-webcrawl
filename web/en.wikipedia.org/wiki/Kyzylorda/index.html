Overview

Kyzylorda is a city in southern Kazakhstan on the Syr Darya River, founded in 1820 as the Kokand fortress of Ak-Mechet. Captured by Russian forces under Vasily Perovsky in 1853, it became a stop on the Orenburg–Tashkent railway.

History

From 1925 to 1929, as Kzyl-Orda, it was the first capital of the Kazakh ASSR within Soviet Russia before the capital moved to Alma-Ata. The region is tied to the Kazakh epic hero and musician Korkyt Ata, whose mausoleum complex stands near Karmakshy. The Baikonur Cosmodrome lies in Kyzylorda Region, though it is administered separately and leased by Russia.
