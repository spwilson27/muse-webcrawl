Haseena Parkar (c. 1959 – 6 July 2014) was an Indian woman alleged by Mumbai police and the media to have been a central figure in the Mumbai operations of the organised crime syndicate D-Company, run by her elder brother, the fugitive gangster Dawood Ibrahim. Dubbed the "Godmother of Nagpada" in press accounts, she was accused of running extortion and land-grab operations in south Mumbai after Dawood fled India following the 1993 Bombay bombings, though she was never convicted.

Life

Parkar was one of twelve children of Ibrahim Kaskar, a Mumbai police head constable, and Amina Bi. She lived in the Gordon Hall Apartments in Nagpada, Mumbai. Her husband, Ismail Parkar, was shot dead in 1991 by members of a rival gang, an event often cited as drawing her deeper into her brother's network.

Allegations and cases

Mumbai police accused her of extorting builders, settling disputes for a fee and holding assets on behalf of D-Company. In 2007 she was arrested in a widely reported extortion case involving a real-estate dispute; she was later released on bail and the case was still unresolved at her death. In media coverage she was frequently described as managing her brother's interests in Mumbai, a characterisation her family denied.

Death and in popular culture

Parkar died of a heart attack in Mumbai on 6 July 2014, aged about 55. Her life was dramatised in the 2017 Hindi film Haseena Parkar, starring Shraddha Kapoor in the title role, and she features prominently in journalistic accounts of the Mumbai underworld, including Hussain Zaidi's book Queens of Crime.
