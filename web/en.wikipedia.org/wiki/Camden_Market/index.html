Camden Market

Camden Market
Location: Camden Town, London, England
Opened: 1974 (Camden Lock Market)
Visitors: about 28 million per year

Camden Market is a group of adjoining retail markets in Camden Town, London, around Camden Lock on the Regent's Canal. One of London's most popular visitor attractions, drawing roughly 250,000 people each weekend, the markets are famous for fashion, crafts, antiques, and street food, and were a hub of alternative and punk culture from the 1970s onward.

Component markets

The complex includes the original Camden Lock Market (opened 1974 in a former timber yard and gin warehouse), the Stables Market in former horse stables and a horse hospital serving the canal trade, the Camden Lock Village, Inverness Street Market, and the Electric Ballroom venue. The Buck Street and Hawley Wharf developments added further retail and food halls.

History

Starting as a small crafts fair in 1974, the markets grew with Camden's reputation for music and counterculture. Major fires in 2008 and 2017 damaged parts of the site, which were rebuilt. Since 2014 the markets have been owned by the Market Tech / LabTech group associated with businessman Teddy Sagi.
