Palawan

Palawan is an island province of the Philippines in the Mimaropa region, comprising the long main island of Palawan and surrounding islands between the South China Sea and the Sulu Sea. Its capital is Puerto Princesa.

Geography and nature

Palawan is renowned for limestone karst landscapes, lagoons, and rich marine life. Attractions include the Puerto Princesa Subterranean River National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and the island groups of El Nido and Coron. It is home to endemic species such as the Palawan peacock-pheasant and the Philippine mouse-deer.

History and people

The Tabon Caves in Palawan yielded some of the oldest human remains in the Philippines. Indigenous groups include the Tagbanwa, Palawano, and Batak peoples.
