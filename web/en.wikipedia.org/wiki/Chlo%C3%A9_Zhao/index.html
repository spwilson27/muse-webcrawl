Chloé Zhao

Chloé Zhao (born 1982) is a Chinese filmmaker known for naturalistic dramas shot on location with non-professional actors. She won the Academy Award for Best Director for Nomadland (2020), which also won Best Picture; she was the first woman of colour to win the directing Oscar.

Career

Zhao's earlier films Songs My Brothers Taught Me (2015) and The Rider (2017) premiered to acclaim at Sundance and Cannes. She subsequently directed the Marvel film Eternals (2021).

Style

Her work blends documentary techniques with fiction, favouring real locations, available light and stories drawn from marginal American landscapes.
