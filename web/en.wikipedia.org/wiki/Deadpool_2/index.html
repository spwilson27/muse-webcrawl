Deadpool 2

Deadpool 2 is a 2018 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics character Deadpool. It is the sequel to Deadpool (2016) and the eleventh installment in the X-Men film series. The film was directed by David Leitch and stars Ryan Reynolds alongside Josh Brolin, Morena Baccarin, Julian Dennison, Zazie Beetz, and T. J. Miller.

Plot

Deadpool forms the team X-Force to protect a young mutant, Russell Collins, from the time-traveling soldier Cable. The film introduces Domino and features a post-credits sequence in which Deadpool uses Cable's device to alter the timeline.

Reception

The film grossed over $785 million worldwide. A PG-13 version, Once Upon a Deadpool, was released later in 2018. A third film, Deadpool & Wolverine, followed in 2024 within the Marvel Cinematic Universe.
