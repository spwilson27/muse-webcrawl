List of airports in Cyprus

This is a list of airports in Cyprus, sorted by location. The island's civil aviation reflects the island's division since 1974: the Republic of Cyprus operates Larnaca and Paphos international airports, while Ercan International Airport in the north is served only by flights via Turkey. Nicosia International Airport has been closed since 1974 and lies in the UN buffer zone.
