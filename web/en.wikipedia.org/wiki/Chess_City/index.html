Chess City

Chess City (Russian: Сити-Чесс, Siti-Chess) is a large chess-themed complex in Elista, the capital of the Republic of Kalmykia, Russia. It was built under the presidency of Kirsan Ilyumzhinov, who also served as president of FIDE, the international chess federation, and hosted major chess events including the 1998 Chess Olympiad.

Description

The complex centres on the Chess Palace, a glass-domed building containing tournament halls and a chess museum. The surrounding district, sometimes called Chess Town, includes cottages named after chess pieces and public chess-themed sculpture and artwork.

Events

Besides the 1998 Olympiad, Chess City hosted FIDE Grand Prix events and other international tournaments, reflecting Kalmykia's unusual official promotion of chess, including chess in school curricula.
