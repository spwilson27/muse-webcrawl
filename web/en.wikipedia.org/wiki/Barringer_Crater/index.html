Barringer Crater (Meteor Crater)
LocationCoconino County, Arizona, US

Depth170 m
Age~50,000 years

Barringer Crater, also called Meteor Crater, is a meteorite impact crater about 59 km east of Flagstaff, Arizona. It was formed about 50,000 years ago by a nickel-iron meteorite roughly 50 metres across striking at high speed.

History of study

Mining engineer Daniel Barringer argued from 1903 that the crater was impact-formed and sought the buried meteorite mass, which had largely vaporized. Eugene Shoemaker's work in the 1960s, finding shocked minerals coesite and stishovite, definitively proved impact origin and made the crater a model for planetary science. Apollo astronauts trained here.

Geology

The crater rim rises 45 m above the plain. The surrounding desert climate has preserved it exceptionally well. It is privately owned by the Barringer Crater Company and is a National Natural Landmark with a visitor centre.
