Natural minor scale

The natural minor scale is a diatonic scale also known as the Aeolian mode. Its pattern of whole and half steps is W–H–W–W–H–W–W, giving the sequence of intervals 1, 2, ♭3, 4, 5, ♭6, ♭7 relative to the major scale. On the piano, the A natural minor scale consists of the white keys from A to A: A, B, C, D, E, F, G.

Relation to other minor scales

The natural minor is distinguished from the harmonic minor scale, which raises the seventh degree to create a leading tone, and from the melodic minor scale, which raises the sixth and seventh degrees when ascending. The key signature of a natural minor scale is that of its relative major, a minor third above the tonic.

Use

Natural minor is widely used in folk, rock and popular music, as well as in classical music, where passages in minor keys frequently move between the natural, harmonic and melodic forms.
