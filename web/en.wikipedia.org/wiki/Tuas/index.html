Tuas

Tuas is a planning area and industrial estate located in the West Region of Singapore. Situated at Singapore's south-western tip, bordering the Straits of Johor, it is one of the country's principal heavy-industry zones.

History and development

The area was developed from reclaimed land beginning in the later 20th century. It hosts shipyards, power stations, waste-management facilities, chemical and pharmaceutical plants, and the Tuas Checkpoint, the second land crossing between Singapore and Malaysia via the Malaysia–Singapore Second Link, opened in 1998.

Tuas Port

The Tuas Mega Port, developed by the Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore, is intended to consolidate the country's container port operations. Its first berths opened in 2021–2022, and when fully completed in the 2040s it is planned to be the world's largest fully automated container terminal, with an annual capacity of about 65 million TEUs.

Transport

Tuas is served by the East–West MRT line's Tuas extension, opened in 2017, with stations including Tuas Link, Tuas West Road, Tuas Crescent and Gul Circle.
