CIEDE2000

The CIEDE2000 (ΔE00) color-difference formula is a method published by the International Commission on Illumination (CIE) in 2001. It is an extension of the earlier CIE94 formula and is intended to provide a better correlation with human visual perception of color differences than the simple Euclidean distance in the CIELAB color space.

Description

The formula computes a color difference between two colors given in CIELAB coordinates (L*, a*, b*). It introduces weighting functions for lightness, chroma, and hue differences, a correction for the interaction between chroma and hue differences, and a rotation term that improves performance in the blue region of color space, where CIELAB's perceptual uniformity is particularly poor. The weighting factors depend on the average lightness, chroma, and hue angle of the two samples, making the formula considerably more computationally involved than ΔE*ab.

CIEDE2000 is widely used in color-critical industries, including printing, textiles, paints, and display calibration, and it is implemented in color-management software and the International Color Consortium workflows.
