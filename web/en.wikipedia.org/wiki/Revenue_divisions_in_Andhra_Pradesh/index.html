Revenue divisions in Andhra Pradesh

Revenue divisions are the administrative divisions in the districts of the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh. A revenue division is headed by a Revenue Divisional Officer (RDO) or Sub Collector. Each revenue division comprises several mandals, which are the next level of administration below the division and above the village.

Administration

The revenue division is part of the state's civil administration, responsible for land records, revenue collection, maintenance of law and order at the divisional level, and implementation of government schemes. After the reorganization of districts in Andhra Pradesh in April 2022, which increased the number of districts from 13 to 26, the number of revenue divisions rose correspondingly; the state subsequently had over 70 revenue divisions.

Structure

Examples include the Vijayawada revenue division in NTR district, the Tirupati revenue division in Tirupati district, and the Anantapur revenue division in Anantapuramu district. Each division groups a set of mandals, whose day-to-day revenue administration is handled by tahsildars (mandal revenue officers).
