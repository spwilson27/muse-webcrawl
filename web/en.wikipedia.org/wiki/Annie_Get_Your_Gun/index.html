Annie Get Your Gun

Annie Get Your Gun is a musical with lyrics and music by Irving Berlin and a book by Dorothy Fields and her brother Herbert Fields. The story is a fictionalised version of the life of Annie Oakley (1860–1926), a sharpshooter who starred in Buffalo Bill's Wild West, and her romance with sharpshooter Frank E. Butler.

Productions

The musical premiered on Broadway at the Imperial Theatre on 16 May 1946 and ran for 1,147 performances, starring Ethel Merman as Annie. It was produced by Richard Rodgers and Oscar Hammerstein II. A 1950 film version starred Betty Hutton.

Songs

The score includes standards such as "There's No Business Like Show Business", "Doin' What Comes Naturally", "You Can't Get a Man with a Gun", "They Say It's Wonderful" and "Anything You Can Do (I Can Do Better)".

Synopsis

Annie Oakley, an uncouth but supremely talented sharpshooter, joins Buffalo Bill's Wild West Show and falls for its star marksman, Frank Butler. Their rivalry and romance culminate in a final shooting match in which Annie deliberately loses, winning Frank's heart.
