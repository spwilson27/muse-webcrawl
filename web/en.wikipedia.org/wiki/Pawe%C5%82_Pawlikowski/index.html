Paweł Pawlikowski

Paweł Aleksander Pawlikowski (born 15 September 1957) is a Polish filmmaker. He won the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film for Ida (2013) and the Best Director prize at Cannes for Cold War (2018).

Born15 September 1957, Warsaw, Poland
OccupationFilm director, screenwriter
Notable worksIda; Cold War; Last Resort; My Summer of Love

Career

Pawlikowski emigrated to Britain as a teenager and began by making documentaries for the BBC in the late 1980s. His features Last Resort (2000) and My Summer of Love (2004) won BAFTA awards.

Ida and Cold War

Returning to Polish subjects, Ida — a black-and-white drama about a young nun in 1960s Poland — became the first Polish film to win the foreign-language Oscar. Cold War, a doomed love story across the Iron Curtain, earned him an Academy Award nomination for Best Director.
