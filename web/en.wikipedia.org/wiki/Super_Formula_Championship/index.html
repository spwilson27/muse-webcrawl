Super Formula Championship

The Super Formula Championship is the top-level single-seater racing championship in Japan, succeeding championships contested under the names Formula 2000, Formula Two, Formula 3000, and Formula Nippon. It is organised by Japan Race Promotion and sanctioned by the Japanese Automobile Federation.

Championship format

The season typically consists of rounds at major Japanese circuits including Suzuka, Fuji, Motegi, Autopolis, and Sugo. Drivers accumulate points across the season's races, with separate titles awarded to the drivers' champion and the winning team.

Notable champions

Champions of the series and its predecessors include Satoru Nakajima, Kazuyoshi Hoshino, Pedro de la Rosa, Ralf Schumacher, André Lotterer, and Stoffel Vandoorne, several of whom went on to compete in Formula One.
