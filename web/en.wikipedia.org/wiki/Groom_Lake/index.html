Groom Lake is a dry lake, also known as a playa, in the Great Basin desert of southern Nevada, United States. It lies at the southern end of the Groom Range, within the Nevada National Security Site region, and is best known as the location of the highly secretive United States Air Force installation commonly called Area 51, which occupies the lake's southern shore.

The lakebed is roughly 6 kilometres (3.7 miles) long and 4.4 kilometres (2.7 miles) wide, sitting at an elevation of about 1,344 metres (4,410 feet) above sea level. Like other playas, it is a flat expanse of dried mud and salt that occasionally holds shallow water after rains before evaporating.

History

The Groom mining district was established in the 19th century; lead and silver were mined in the area from the 1860s, and the Groom Mining District was organised in 1885. During the Second World War the area saw limited military use, and in 1955 the Central Intelligence Agency and the Lockheed Corporation selected the remote lakebed as the test site for the U-2 reconnaissance aircraft. The flat, hard surface of the dry lake provided a natural emergency landing area, and runways were subsequently built along its shore, growing into the permanent base that became known as Area 51 (after its designation on Atomic Energy Commission maps).

Geography

Groom Lake lies in the Groom Lake Valley, part of the Tikaboo region of the Basin and Range Province. It is surrounded by the Groom Range to the east and the Bald Mountain Range to the west. Nevada State Route 375, the "Extraterrestrial Highway", passes some distance to the east of the restricted area around the lake. The entire basin is closed to the public, and the airspace above it is restricted.

In popular culture

Because of the secrecy surrounding the base on its shore, Groom Lake has become a fixture of UFO folklore and conspiracy theories, with claims — unsupported by evidence — that recovered extraterrestrial spacecraft are stored there. The lake and base have appeared in numerous films, television series, and video games.
