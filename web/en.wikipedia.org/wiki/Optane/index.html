Optane

ManufacturerIntel
TypeNon-volatile memory / solid-state drives
Introduced2017
Discontinued2022

Intel Optane is a brand name for a system of storage and memory products based on 3D XPoint technology, developed jointly by Intel and Micron. Announced in 2015 and first sold in 2017, Optane products included consumer cache modules (Optane Memory), high-endurance data centre solid-state drives (such as the P4800X), and persistent memory DIMMs that provided large-capacity non-volatile memory for servers.

Products

The first Optane product, released in March 2017, was a small M.2 NVMe cache drive intended to accelerate hard-disk-based systems. Data-centre SSDs followed, offering very low latency and high endurance. Optane Persistent Memory (the 100, 200, and 300 series) plugged into DDR DIMM slots on Xeon servers, offering capacities of 128 GB to 512 GB per module.

Discontinuation

Micron exited the 3D XPoint business in 2021, selling its Lehi, Utah fabrication plant to Texas Instruments. In July 2022, Intel announced during its second-quarter earnings that it would wind down the Optane business entirely, taking a write-off of over $500 million.
