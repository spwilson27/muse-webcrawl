G. Harry Stine

George Harry Stine (March 26, 1928 – November 2, 1997) was an American science fiction author, model rocketry pioneer and engineer. Writing as Lee Correy, he contributed stories to the science fiction magazines of the 1950s.

Stine worked at White Sands Missile Range and later became a leading figure in model rocketry, helping to found the National Association of Rocketry and writing the influential Handbook of Model Rocketry. He is often called the 'father of model rocketry' alongside Orville Carlisle and Vernon Estes.

Writing

His novels include Starship Through Space (as Lee Correy), Contraband Rocket and the Warbots series. He also wrote extensively on space exploration and the future, including The Third Industrial Revolution and works on space colonies.
