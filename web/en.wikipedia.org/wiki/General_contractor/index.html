General contractor

Also calledPrime contractor, main contractor
IndustryConstruction
Contracts withProject owner (client)
EngagesSubcontractors

A general contractor, also known as a prime contractor or main contractor, is a party engaged by a client to carry out construction work and who takes overall responsibility for the day-to-day oversight of a construction site, management of vendors and trades, and communication of information to all parties throughout the course of a building project.

Responsibilities

The general contractor typically provides all materials, labour, equipment and services necessary for the construction of the project, hires specialised subcontractors for portions of the work, applies for building permits, keeps accurate records, and ensures the site is safe and secure. The general contractor is usually responsible for the price and schedule agreed with the owner, and for coordination between the design team and the trades.

Licensing and contracting

Many jurisdictions require general contractors to be licensed, bonded and insured, with requirements varying by project type and size. Common delivery arrangements include lump-sum contracts awarded by competitive bid, cost-plus contracts, and construction-management arrangements; in design-build projects the contractor also assumes responsibility for design.
