Hannah Montana is an American teen sitcom created by Michael Poryes, Rich Correll and Barry O'Brien that aired on Disney Channel from 24 March 2006 to 16 January 2011. The series centres on Miley Stewart (played by Miley Cyrus), a teenager who lives a double life: an average schoolgirl by day and the famous pop singer Hannah Montana by night, concealing her identity with a blonde wig.

Premise and cast

Miley lives in Malibu with her widowed father Robby Ray Stewart (played by Cyrus's real-life father, country singer Billy Ray Cyrus), a former country star who acts as her manager, and her older brother Jackson (Jason Earles). Her best friends Lilly Truscott (Emily Osment) and Oliver Oken (Mitchel Musso) are among the few who know her secret; Moisés Arias joined as the scheming neighbour Rico Suave. The comedy derives from Miley's efforts to keep her two worlds apart.

Music and franchise

The series produced a string of successful soundtrack albums featuring Cyrus as Hannah Montana, the concert film Hannah Montana & Miley Cyrus: Best of Both Worlds Concert (2008), and the feature film Hannah Montana: The Movie (2009). The Best of Both Worlds Tour (2007–08) was one of the highest-grossing concert tours of its time, and the franchise generated extensive merchandising.

Reception and legacy

Hannah Montana was one of Disney Channel's most successful series, earning high ratings, four Emmy nominations, and launching Miley Cyrus's career as a pop star. Its final season was marketed as Hannah Montana Forever.
