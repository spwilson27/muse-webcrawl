Basil Glen Ballard Jr. (born May 1, 1953) is an American songwriter and record producer. He is best known for his collaborations with singer Alanis Morissette, co-writing and producing her diamond-certified 1995 album Jagged Little Pill, one of the best-selling albums of all time. He was inducted into the Songwriters Hall of Fame in 2023.

Career

Born in Natchez, Mississippi, Ballard moved to Los Angeles and worked as a staff writer and arranger, including for producer Quincy Jones. He wrote songs for Michael Jackson, including "Man in the Mirror" (1988) with Siedah Garrett, and produced hits for Wilson Phillips, whose debut album he produced. With Morissette, he developed a fast, intuitive co-writing method — many Jagged Little Pill songs were written in a day — yielding hits such as "You Oughta Know", "Ironic", and "Hand in My Pocket"; the album won the Grammy Award for Album of the Year.

Other work

Ballard later worked with Dave Matthews Band (Everyday), Aerosmith, No Doubt, and Christina Aguilera, and co-created the musical Ghost and the Netflix series The Eddy (2020) with Damien Chazelle. He also co-wrote the music for Jagged Little Pill, the Broadway jukebox musical based on Morissette's album.
