List of bioterrorism incidents

This is a list of bioterrorism incidents — deliberate releases, or attempted releases, of biological agents intended to cause disease, death, or fear. Documented incidents are rare compared with conventional terrorism, but several have been historically significant.

Major incidents

Other cases and hoaxes

Ricin letters sent to U.S. officials in 2003, 2013, and later years caused disruptions but no infections. Numerous white-powder hoaxes followed the 2001 attacks. Earlier, during the Second World War, Japan's Unit 731 conducted biological attacks in China, and several states maintained offensive bioweapons programs until the Biological Weapons Convention of 1972 banned them.

Preparedness

Incidents such as Amerithrax spurred investment in biosurveillance, stockpiles of antibiotics and vaccines, and mail-screening systems in the United States and allied countries.
