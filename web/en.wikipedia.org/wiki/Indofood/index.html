Indofood

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

PT Indofood Sukses Makmur Tbk, commonly known as Indofood, is an Indonesian food company headquartered in Jakarta. Founded in 1990 by Sudono Salim's Salim Group, it is one of the world's largest instant noodle producers, best known for the Indomie brand, and operates across the food chain from raw-material production to branded consumer goods.

Operations

Indofood's business groups include Consumer Branded Products (through the separately listed Indofood CBP, maker of Indomie noodles, Indomilk dairy products, snacks and seasonings), Bogasari (Indonesia's largest flour miller), Agribusiness (palm oil and other plantations through Indofood Agri Resources), and Distribution. The company's Bogasari flour mills in Jakarta and Surabaya supply a large share of Indonesia's wheat flour market.

Indomie

Indomie, launched in 1972 by the Salim Group's earlier noodle business and folded into Indofood, is the group's flagship. Its Mi Goreng flavour is sold in more than 100 countries, with production plants in countries including Nigeria—where Indomie is a household staple—Egypt, Turkey, Saudi Arabia and Serbia.

History

The Salim Group's food businesses were consolidated into Indofood in 1990 and listed on the Jakarta and Surabaya stock exchanges in 1994. Control is exercised through Hong Kong-listed First Pacific Company. In 2020 Indofood CBP acquired full ownership of Pinehill Company, the maker of Indomie for markets in Africa, the Middle East and Southeast Europe, in a deal valued at about US$3 billion.
