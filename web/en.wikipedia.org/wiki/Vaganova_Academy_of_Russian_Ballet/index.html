Vaganova Academy of Russian Ballet


Established: 1738
Location: St Petersburg, Russia
Named for: Agrippina Vaganova

The Vaganova Academy of Russian Ballet is a school of dance in St Petersburg, Russia, associated with the Mariinsky Ballet. Founded in 1738 by decree of Empress Anna as the Imperial Theatrical School, it is one of the oldest and most prestigious ballet schools in the world.

History and method

The school took its present name from Agrippina Vaganova (1879–1951), a former Mariinsky dancer whose teaching system — codified in her 1934 book Basic Principles of Classical Ballet — became the foundation of the Vaganova method, the dominant style of ballet training in Russia and widely taught internationally.

Alumni

Graduates include Anna Pavlova, Vaslav Nijinsky, George Balanchine, Galina Ulanova, Rudolf Nureyev, Natalia Makarova, Mikhail Baryshnikov and Diana Vishneva. Admission is highly competitive, with children auditioned from around age ten for an eight-year course of study.
