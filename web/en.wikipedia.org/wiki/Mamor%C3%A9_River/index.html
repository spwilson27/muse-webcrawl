CountriesBolivia, Brazil
Lengthc. 1,930 km (1,200 mi)
MouthMadeira River

The Mamoré River is a large river in Bolivia and Brazil, one of the principal headstreams of the Madeira River and thus of the Amazon. It rises in the Bolivian Andes near Cochabamba and flows northward for roughly 1,930 kilometres, forming part of the Bolivia–Brazil border before joining the Beni River to form the Madeira.

Course

Formed by tributaries including the Chapare and Ichilo rivers, the Mamoré drains the vast Llanos de Moxos savanna plains of the Beni Department. Near Guayaramerín it is joined by the Guaporé (Iténez) River, and downstream it meets the Beni at Villa Bella, where their confluence marks the start of the Madeira River.

Fauna

The river system is home to rich Amazonian biodiversity, including the Bolivian river dolphin (bufeo), a distinct population of the Amazon river dolphin found in the upper Madeira basin.
