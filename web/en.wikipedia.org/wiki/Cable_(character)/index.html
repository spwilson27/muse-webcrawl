Cable (character)

Cable (Nathan Christopher Charles Summers) is a character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, commonly in association with X-Force and the X-Men. The character first appeared as the adult Cable in The New Mutants #87 (March 1990), created by writer Louise Simonson and artist/co-writer Rob Liefeld; the infant Nathan Summers had appeared earlier in Uncanny X-Men #201 (1986).

Fictional character biography

Nathan Summers is the son of Scott Summers (Cyclops) and Madelyne Pryor (a clone of Jean Grey). As an infant he was infected by Apocalypse with a techno-organic virus and sent roughly 2,000 years into the future to be raised by the Clan Askani, where the virus could be held in check by his developing telekinetic powers — which permanently convert much of his left side into living metal. Raised as a soldier in a war against Apocalypse's future regime, Cable travels back to the present day to prevent that future, organizing the New Mutants into the paramilitary X-Force and repeatedly clashing with his clone, Stryfe. His adopted daughter Hope Summers, the first mutant born after M-Day, is central to later storylines such as Messiah Complex and Avengers vs. X-Men.

Powers and abilities

Cable is an omega-level telepath and telekinetic whose powers are mostly consumed containing his techno-organic infection; when freed of the virus (as in Cable & Deadpool) he displayed near-limitless psionic ability. He is also a veteran soldier, tactician, and hand-to-hand combatant, and his cybernetic eye and arm grant enhanced vision and strength.

In other media

Cable was portrayed by Josh Brolin in the film Deadpool 2 (2018) and has appeared in X-Men animated series and numerous video games.
