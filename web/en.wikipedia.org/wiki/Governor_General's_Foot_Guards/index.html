Governor General's Foot Guards

The Governor General's Foot Guards (GGFG) is the senior infantry regiment of the Canadian Army's Primary Reserve, based in Ottawa, Ontario. Raised in 1872, it is styled as a guards regiment and performs the ceremonial duties associated with the Governor General of Canada, including mounting the guard at Rideau Hall and on Parliament Hill.

The regiment wears a scarlet tunic and bearskin cap modelled on the British Grenadier Guards, reflecting its alliance with that regiment. Its members form a major part of the Ceremonial Guard seen by visitors to the capital each summer.

Beyond ceremonial tasks, the GGFG is a trained infantry regiment whose soldiers have served in both World Wars — in the Second World War as part of the 21st Canadian Armoured Regiment — and in modern operations including Afghanistan. Its headquarters is Cartier Square Drill Hall in Ottawa.
