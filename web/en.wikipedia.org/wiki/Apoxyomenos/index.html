Apoxyomenos

Apoxyomenos
Artist: Lysippos (original)
Date: c. 330 BC original
Subject: Athlete scraping oil with strigil

Apoxyomenos (the Scraper) is one of the conventional subjects of ancient Greek votive sculpture: an athlete scraping sweat, dust and oil from his body with a strigil. The most famous version was by Lysippos of Sikyon (c. 330 BC).

Copies

The original bronze is lost. A Roman marble copy is in the Vatican Museums. A remarkably preserved bronze, the Croatian Apoxyomenos, was recovered from the Adriatic Sea near Lošinj in 1999 and is now in Zagreb/Lošinj museum display.

Significance

Lysippos's version broke from Polykleitos's canon with a more slender figure and an arm extended into the viewer's space.
