GeForce RTX

ManufacturerNvidia

GeForce RTX is a line of graphics processing units (GPUs) developed by Nvidia, introduced in 2018 with the GeForce RTX 20 series based on the Turing architecture. The RTX brand denotes hardware support for real-time ray tracing via dedicated RT cores and AI acceleration via Tensor cores.

Features

RTX GPUs combine conventional CUDA cores with RT cores that accelerate bounding-volume-hierarchy traversal for ray-traced reflections, shadows and global illumination, and Tensor cores used for DLSS (Deep Learning Super Sampling), which upscales lower-resolution frames using machine learning. Subsequent generations — Ampere (RTX 30 series, 2020), Ada Lovelace (RTX 40 series, 2022) and Blackwell (RTX 50 series, 2025) — improved ray-tracing throughput and added features such as DLSS frame generation.

Professional line

The RTX name is also used for Nvidia's professional workstation GPUs (formerly Quadro), such as the RTX A6000 and RTX 6000 Ada Generation, aimed at rendering, simulation and AI workloads.
