ICE (art group)

Underground ASCII and ANSI art group

ANSI art in the style of the ICE group

iCE (Insane Creators Enterprise) is an underground computer art group that published monthly artpacks of ANSI, ASCII and VGA artwork during the bulletin board system (BBS) era of the 1990s. Along with rival groups such as ACiD Productions, iCE helped define the ANSI art scene, with artists drawing elaborate coloured text-mode illustrations using extended character sets and shading blocks.

History

Founded in the early 1990s, iCE released packs — typically named in the pattern ICE-YYMM — containing artwork, loader programs and member lists, distributed through BBS networks and later the early internet. The group's productions are preserved by digital archives of the demoscene and text-art communities, and former members have periodically produced anniversary releases.

Context

ANSI art groups operated alongside the warez and demo scenes, producing logos and screens for bulletin boards. Their work is now exhibited and archived by projects devoted to preserving computer art history.
