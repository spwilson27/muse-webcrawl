EuroBasket, also called the European Basketball Championship, is the main international basketball competition for men's national teams in Europe, organised by FIBA Europe. First held in Geneva in 1935 and won by Latvia, it was for decades played every two years before moving to a four-year cycle after 2017. The Soviet Union dominates the historical roll of honour with 14 titles, while Yugoslavia and Spain have each won multiple championships; Spain, champions in 2009, 2011, 2015 and 2022, is the leading power of the modern era.

Format and history

Twenty-four teams contest the finals, usually across multiple host countries since 2015, after qualification through FIBA windows. The tournament doubles as part of the European pathway toward the FIBA Basketball World Cup and the Olympic Games. Famous editions include the Soviet Union's long dominance under coach Alexander Gomelsky, Yugoslavia's golden generations, Greece's shock 1987 triumph on home soil in Athens — a landmark in Greek sport — and Lithuania's basketball-obsessed runs to the title, most recently in 2003. EuroBasket Women is the corresponding women's championship, first played in 1938.
