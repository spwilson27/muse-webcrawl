Science and technology

Autoregressive moving-average model (ARMA), a statistical model for time series data, combining autoregressive (AR) and moving-average (MA) components; the basis of the ARIMA and Box–Jenkins methods of forecasting.

Anti-reflective, magnetic, and antiviral coatings and other technical uses appear in specialised literature.

Other uses

Arma (Latin for "arms" or "weapons") is a Finnish firearms manufacturer name element (Valmet/Sako heritage), the setting name of the ARMA series of military simulation video games by Bohemia Interactive (stylised Arma, from Armed Assault), and part of place and personal names.
