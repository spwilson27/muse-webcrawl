Nordic cross flag

A Nordic cross flag is a flag bearing the design known as a Nordic cross, a cross with its vertical bar offset toward the hoist. The design originates with the Dannebrog of Denmark and is used by all five sovereign Nordic states and by numerous autonomous territories, regions, cities and organisations connected with Northern Europe.

Other flags

Autonomous territories within the Nordic states — Åland (Finland), the Faroe Islands and Greenland (though Greenland's flag is not a cross design) use or have used Nordic crosses. Outside the region, the flags of Orkney and Shetland (Scotland), adopted in 2007 and 1969 respectively, deliberately use the design to recall their Norse heritage. Numerous historical, proposed, military and regional flags in Scandinavia also employ the offset cross, including Norwegian and Swedish naval ensigns with swallowtails and the former flag of the Kalmar Union revival proposals.
