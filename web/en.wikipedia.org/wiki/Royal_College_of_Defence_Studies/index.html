Royal College of Defence Studies

The Royal College of Defence Studies (RCDS) is a British defence college in Belgravia, London, part of the Defence Academy of the United Kingdom. It provides strategic education for senior officers and officials.

History and course

Founded in 1927 as the Imperial Defence College, it was renamed in 1970. Its flagship one-year course brings together senior British military officers, civil servants, diplomats, police officers and international participants to study international security and strategy at the grand strategic level.

The college motto is "To prepare for war, to preserve peace".
