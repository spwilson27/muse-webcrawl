List of FA Cup winning managers

This is a list of FA Cup winning managers. The FA Cup is an annual knockout football competition in English football, first played in the 1871–72 season. The manager who has won the cup most often is Arsène Wenger, who guided Arsenal to seven victories between 1998 and 2017.

Managers with multiple wins

Arsène Wenger leads with seven wins. George Ramsay won six with Aston Villa in the late 19th and early 20th centuries; Alex Ferguson won five with Manchester United. Other multiple winners include Thomas Mitchell, Bob Crompton, Bill Nicholson, Bob Paisley and Pep Guardiola.
