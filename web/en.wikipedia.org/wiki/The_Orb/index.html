The Orb

Origin: London, England
Genres: Ambient house, dub, electronica
Years active: 1988–present

The Orb is an English electronic music group founded in 1988 by Alex Paterson and Jimmy Cauty (later of The KLF). The group pioneered the genre of ambient house, blending ambient textures, dub basslines, and samples drawn from science fiction, space exploration broadcasts, and comedy records.

History

Paterson, a former roadie and A&R man, began DJing chill-out sets at London clubs, mixing ambient music with house beats. The Orb's breakthrough single "Little Fluffy Clouds" (1990) famously sampled Rickie Lee Jones. Their debut album, The Orb's Adventures Beyond the Ultraworld (1991), and its follow-up U.F.Orb (1992) — which reached number one in the UK — established them as leading figures in early-1990s electronic music.

Style and legacy

Long-time collaborator Thomas Fehlmann became Paterson's principal partner. The Orb are known for long, immersive live performances and for playful, sample-laden soundscapes. Later works include Moonbuilding 2703 AD (2015) and Abolition of the Royal Familia (2020).
