Storm is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Len Wein and artist Dave Cockrum, the character first appeared in Giant-Size X-Men #1 (1975). Ororo Munroe is a mutant with the ability to perceive and manipulate weather and atmospheric phenomena, and a long-standing member—and frequent leader—of the X-Men.

Fictional biography

Ororo was born in Manhattan to an American photojournalist father and a Kenyan tribal princess mother, and orphaned as a child in Cairo when a plane crashed into their home; she survived as a street thief under the master thief Achmed el-Gibar. As a young woman she travelled to her ancestral homeland in Kenya, where her emerging powers led local tribes to worship her as a rain goddess, before Professor Charles Xavier recruited her to the new X-Men.

Storm has served as leader of the X-Men (famously defeating Cyclops for leadership while depowered in the 1980s), queen consort of Wakanda through her marriage to the Black Panther (later annulled), and a member of the Avengers and Fantastic Four. She suffers from severe claustrophobia stemming from her childhood trauma.

In other media

Halle Berry portrayed Storm in the 20th Century Fox X-Men films (2000–2014), with Alexandra Shipp playing a younger version in X-Men: Apocalypse (2016) and Dark Phoenix (2019). The character is widely regarded as one of the most prominent Black superheroes in comics.
