Philippine Trench

The Philippine Trench (also Philippine Deep, Mindanao Trench, or Mindanao Deep) is a submarine trench to the east of the Philippines. It stretches about 1,320 kilometres long and 30 kilometres wide from the centre of the island of Luzon southeast to the northern Maluku island of Halmahera in Indonesia.

Its deepest point, the Emden Deep (Galathea Depth), reaches more than 10,500 metres, making it among the deepest oceanic trenches in the world. The trench formed by subduction of the Philippine Sea Plate beneath the Philippine Mobile Belt and is associated with significant seismic activity.
