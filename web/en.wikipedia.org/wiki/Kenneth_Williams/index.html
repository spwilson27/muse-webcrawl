Kenneth Williams

Kenneth Charles Williams (22 February 1926 – 15 April 1988) was an English actor and comedian. He was best known for his performances in the Carry On films, in which he appeared in 26 of the series' 31 films, and for his regular appearances on the BBC radio comedy programmes Hancock's Half Hour and Round the Horne.

Williams was renowned for his distinctive nasal voice, his flair for innuendo-laden delivery, and his use of Polari. His diaries, published posthumously, revealed a lonely and troubled private life that contrasted sharply with his flamboyant public persona.

Early life

Williams was born in Kings Cross, London, the son of a hairdresser. He was educated at Lyulph Stanley School and served with the Royal Engineers during the Second World War, including in Burma.

Career

Williams joined the Combined Services Entertainment Unit, performing in revues in the Far East, and on returning to Britain worked in repertory theatre. His radio work brought him national fame, and his first Carry On film was Carry On Sergeant (1958). Later in his career he became a popular chat show guest and storyteller, and he narrated the children's programme Willo the Wisp.
