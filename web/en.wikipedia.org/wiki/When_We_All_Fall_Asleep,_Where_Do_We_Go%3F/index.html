When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go? (stylised in all lowercase) is the debut studio album by American singer-songwriter Billie Eilish, released on 29 March 2019 by Darkroom and Interscope Records. Written and produced almost entirely with her brother Finneas O'Connell in his small bedroom studio in Los Angeles, the album blends electropop, avant-pop and trap with whispered, ASMR-like vocals and dark lyrical themes drawn from Eilish's lucid dreams, night terrors and experience of synesthesia.

Background and singles

Eilish first gained attention with the viral single "Ocean Eyes" (2015) and the EP Don't Smile at Me (2017). The album was preceded by singles including "You Should See Me in a Crown", "When the Party's Over" and "Bury a Friend". The single "Bad Guy" became a worldwide number one — making Eilish the first artist born in the 21st century to top the Billboard Hot 100 — and won Record of the Year and Song of the Year at the Grammy Awards. Other tracks include "Wish You Were Gay", "Xanny", "All the Good Girls Go to Hell" and "Ilomilo".

Critical reception and awards

The album debuted at number one on the Billboard 200 and in many other countries, and was met with widespread critical acclaim for its production, songwriting and Eilish's vocal performance. At the 62nd Grammy Awards in January 2020 it won Album of the Year, Best Pop Vocal Album and Best Engineered Album, Non-Classical, while Eilish swept the "Big Four" categories — the first woman, and only the second artist ever, to do so in one night. The album has been certified multi-platinum in numerous countries and frequently appears on lists of the best albums of 2019 and of the decade.
