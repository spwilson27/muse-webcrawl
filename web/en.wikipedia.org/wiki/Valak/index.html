Valak

Valak (also spelled Ualac, Valac, Valax, Valu, Valic) is a demon described in the goetic grimoires The Lesser Key of Solomon (in the Ars Goetia), Johann Weyer's Pseudomonarchia Daemonum, and Jacques Collin de Plancy's Dictionnaire Infernal. He is ranked as a President of Hell, commanding legions of demons.

Description in the grimoires

In the Ars Goetia, Valak is the sixty-second spirit, a mighty President who appears in the likeness of a boy with angel's wings, riding on a two-headed dragon. His office is to give true answers about hidden treasures and to reveal where serpents may be seen, delivering them harmlessly to the exorcist. He is said to govern 30 legions of spirits (38 in some texts). Weyer's Pseudomonarchia Daemonum gives a similar account under the name Volac.

In popular culture

Valak appears as the principal antagonist of the horror films The Conjuring 2 (2016) and The Nun (2018) and its sequel The Nun II (2023), where the demon is reimagined as a sinister entity taking the form of a demonic nun, played by Bonnie Aarons. This portrayal departs substantially from the grimoire descriptions.
