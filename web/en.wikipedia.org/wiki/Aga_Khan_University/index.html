Aga Khan University (AKU)
Founded: 1983, Karachi, Pakistan
Founder: Aga Khan IV
Type: Private research university

The Aga Khan University is a private research university, with its founding campus in Karachi, Pakistan, and campuses and programmes in Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Afghanistan, and the United Kingdom. Founded in 1983 by Prince Karim Aga Khan IV, it is part of the Aga Khan Development Network.

History and structure

AKU began with a medical college and teaching hospital (Aga Khan University Hospital, Karachi) and expanded to nursing, midwifery, education (Institute for Educational Development), media and communications, and the study of Muslim civilisations (ISMC, London). It received the first university charter granted to a private university in Pakistan.

Mission

The university emphasises quality, relevance, impact, and access, serving predominantly Muslim and developing-world populations, with need-based financial assistance and research in health sciences, education, and East African development.
