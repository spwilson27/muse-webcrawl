List of hamburgers

This is a list of hamburgers — notable varieties of the hamburger sandwich, regional styles, and famous branded burgers sold by restaurant chains. A hamburger consists of a cooked patty of ground meat (usually beef) placed inside a sliced bread roll or bun, with varied toppings.
