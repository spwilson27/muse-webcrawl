Alibaba Group

Alibaba Group Holding Limited is a Chinese multinational technology company specialising in e-commerce, retail, internet and technology. Founded in Hangzhou in 1999 by Jack Ma and 17 others, it operates marketplaces including Taobao, Tmall and Alibaba.com, and is one of the world's largest retailers and technology companies.

History

Alibaba began as a B2B marketplace connecting Chinese manufacturers with overseas buyers. Taobao (2003) beat eBay in China; Alipay launched in 2004 and later became Ant Group. Alibaba Cloud was founded in 2009. Its 2014 New York IPO raised about US$25 billion, then the largest in history; it dual-listed in Hong Kong in 2019.

Businesses

Core commerce spans Taobao and Tmall in China and AliExpress, Trendyol and Lazada internationally. Other arms include Alibaba Cloud, logistics network Cainiao, local services (Ele.me), entertainment (Youku, Alibaba Pictures) and the annual Singles' Day (Double 11) shopping festival, originated in 2009.

Regulation and restructuring

In 2020 regulators halted Ant Group's IPO, and in 2021 Alibaba was fined ¥18.2 billion in an antitrust case. In 2023 the group announced a split into six business groups under a holding-company structure.
