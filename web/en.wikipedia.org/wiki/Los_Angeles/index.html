Los Angeles

[Image: Downtown Los Angeles with the San Gabriel Mountains behind]
Downtown Los Angeles with the San Gabriel Mountains behind

Los Angeles, often called LA, is the most populous city in California and the second-most populous in the United States, with about 3.8 million residents in the city and 12 to 13 million in its metropolitan area. It lies in a basin in Southern California, bordered by the Pacific Ocean and the San Gabriel and Santa Monica mountains.

Los Angeles is the centre of the American film and television industry — Hollywood became synonymous with cinema after studios moved west in the 1910s. The city is famously sprawling and car-oriented, culturally diverse (with large Latino and Asian populations), and a leader in entertainment, aerospace, technology and trade through the twin ports of LA and Long Beach, among the busiest in the world. LA hosted the Olympic Games in 1932 and 1984 and will host again in 2028. Its Mediterranean climate, beaches, Griffith Observatory and the Hollywood Sign are iconic.
