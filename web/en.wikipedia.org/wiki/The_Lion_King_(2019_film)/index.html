The Lion King (2019 film)

Directed by: Jon Favreau
Voices: Donald Glover, Beyoncé, Seth Rogen, James Earl Jones
Released: 2019
Box office: $1.66 billion

The Lion King is a 2019 American musical drama film directed and produced by Jon Favreau, written by Jeff Nathanson, and produced by Walt Disney Pictures. It is a photorealistic computer-animated remake of Disney's traditionally animated 1994 film of the same name.

Plot

The film retells the story of Simba, a young lion prince of the Pride Lands. After his father Mufasa is murdered by his uncle Scar, Simba flees into exile, growing up with the carefree meerkat Timon and warthog Pumbaa. As an adult, reminded of his responsibilities by his childhood friend Nala and the mandrill Rafiki, Simba returns to challenge Scar and take his place in the "Circle of Life."

Production and reception

Favreau built on the virtual production techniques he had developed for The Jungle Book (2016), filming in a virtual environment while animators created the animals with photorealistic CGI. The voice cast includes Donald Glover as Simba, Beyoncé as Nala, and James Earl Jones reprising his role as Mufasa. The soundtrack features songs from the original, reworked with Hans Zimmer, Elton John, and Beyoncé's companion album The Lion King: The Gift.

Critics praised the visual effects but were divided on the emotional expressiveness of the photorealistic animals. The film grossed over $1.66 billion worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing animated film of all time at its release. A prequel, Mufasa: The Lion King, followed in 2024.
