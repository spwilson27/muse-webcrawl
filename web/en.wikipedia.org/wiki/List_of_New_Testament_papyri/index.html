List of New Testament papyri

A New Testament papyrus is a copy of a portion of the New Testament written on papyrus. To date, over 140 such papyri are known, and they are among the earliest witnesses to the text of the New Testament. In the Gregory–Aland numbering system they are designated by a blackletter P (𝔓) followed by a superscript number. Most were discovered in Egypt, many at Oxyrhynchus, where the dry climate preserved them.
