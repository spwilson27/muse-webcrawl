Address (geography)

An address is a collection of information, presented in a mostly fixed format, used to give the location of a building, apartment, or other structure or a plot of land, generally using political boundaries and street names as references.

Format

Address formats vary by country: many place the recipient name first, then street number and name, locality, region and postal code, with the country last for international mail. Some systems, such as Japan's, order from largest to smallest unit; Western systems usually go smallest to largest.

Purposes

Addresses enable mail delivery, emergency services, navigation, census and taxation, and legal identification of property. Standardisation and geocoding link addresses to coordinates in geographic information systems.
