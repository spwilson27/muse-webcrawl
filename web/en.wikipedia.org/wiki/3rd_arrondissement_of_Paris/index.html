3rd arrondissement of Paris

The 3rd arrondissement of Paris (IIIe arrondissement, the "Temple" arrondissement) is one of the 20 arrondissements of the capital of France. Located on the Right Bank of the Seine, it covers the northern part of the historic Marais district and is one of the smallest arrondissements, at about 1.17 km², with a population of roughly 34,000.

Landmarks

The arrondissement contains the Musée Picasso, the Musée Carnavalet (museum of the history of Paris), the Conservatoire national des arts et métiers, and the Archives nationales, housed in the Hôtel de Soubise. Its medieval streets preserve some of the oldest houses in Paris, including the house of Nicolas Flamel (1407), traditionally cited as the city's oldest stone house.

Quarters

Administratively it is divided into the quarters of Arts-et-Métiers, Enfants-Rouges, Archives, and Sainte-Avoye. Since 2020 the first four arrondissements have shared a single mayor under the Paris Centre grouping, though each retains its own identity.
