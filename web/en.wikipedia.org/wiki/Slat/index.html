Slat

A slat is a thin, narrow strip of wood, metal or other material. Slats are used in furniture (such as the supporting slats of a bed frame), fences, blinds and agricultural equipment. The word is also used in several specialised senses.

Uses

Venetian blinds are made of horizontal slats that can be tilted to control light. Bed slats support a mattress in place of a box spring. In furniture making, slat-backed chairs and benches are common vernacular forms.

In aviation

In aeronautics, a slat is a small aerodynamic surface on the leading edge of a wing that, when extended, allows the wing to operate at a higher angle of attack, improving low-speed lift. Leading-edge slats are fitted to many airliners and retract for cruise flight.
