Ciudad Juárez, commonly referred to as Juárez, is a city in the Mexican state of Chihuahua. It is commonly considered the fifth-largest city in Mexico by population, and forms a transborder agglomeration with El Paso, Texas, United States, across the Rio Grande.

History

Founded as El Paso del Norte in 1659 by Franciscan friars, the city grew around a mission and river crossing. It took the name of Benito Juárez in 1888, honouring the president who made it his wartime capital during the French intervention. During the Mexican Revolution it was the scene of major battles, including the 1911 capture by Pancho Villa's forces.

Economy

Juárez is a major manufacturing centre, dominated by maquiladora assembly plants serving the US market, and its economy is deeply integrated with that of El Paso.
