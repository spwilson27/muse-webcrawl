Question Time (TV programme)

Question Time

Network: BBC One
First broadcast: 25 September 1979
Presenter: Fiona Bruce (since 2019)

Question Time is a British topical debate programme broadcast by the BBC, first aired in 1979. Each week, a panel of politicians and public figures answers questions posed by a studio audience in a different town or city across the United Kingdom.

Format

The panel typically includes representatives of the main political parties plus journalists, commentators, or celebrities. Audience members submit questions in advance, and selected questioners put them live; discussion follows, moderated by the chair. The programme's register of debate reflects the week's news agenda.

Presenters

The programme was chaired by Peter Sissons (1989–1993), by David Dimbleby for 25 years (1994–2019), and since January 2019 by Fiona Bruce, its first female presenter. Sir Robin Day presented the earliest editions.

Impact

Question Time is a fixture of British political broadcasting and a frequent source of news stories, notable editions including the 2009 episode featuring BNP leader Nick Griffin and special editions during general elections and the Brexit referendum campaign.
