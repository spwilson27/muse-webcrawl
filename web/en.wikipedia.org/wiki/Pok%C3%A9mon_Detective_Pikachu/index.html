Pokémon Detective Pikachu
Directed by: Rob Letterman
Released: 2019
Starring: Ryan Reynolds (voice), Justice Smith, Kathryn Newton
Box office: about US$450 million

Pokémon Detective Pikachu is a 2019 fantasy film based on the Pokémon franchise and loosely adapted from the 2016 video game Detective Pikachu. Directed by Rob Letterman, it is the first live-action Pokémon film, blending human actors with computer-animated Pokémon. Ryan Reynolds voices Pikachu, a wisecracking detective who can be understood only by the film's protagonist, Tim Goodman (Justice Smith), a former Pokémon trainer searching for his missing father.

Plot

In Ryme City, where humans and Pokémon live side by side and battling is outlawed, Tim teams up with his father's amnesiac Pikachu partner to uncover a conspiracy involving a mysterious gas that drives Pokémon into frenzies and a plot centred on the legendary Mewtwo.

Reception

The film grossed roughly $450 million worldwide, becoming one of the highest-grossing video game adaptations of its time, and received generally positive reviews praising its visual effects and Reynolds's performance.
