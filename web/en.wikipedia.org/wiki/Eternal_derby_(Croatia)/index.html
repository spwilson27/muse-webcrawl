Eternal derby (Croatia)

Eternal derby (Vječni derbi)
Teams: GNK Dinamo Zagreb and HNK Hajduk Split
First meeting: 1946 (unofficial matches from 1911 era clubs)
Stadiums: Stadion Maksimir (Zagreb); Stadion Poljud (Split)
Most wins: Dinamo Zagreb

The Eternal derby (Croatian: Vječni derbi) is the name given to matches between Croatia's two most successful football clubs, Dinamo Zagreb and Hajduk Split. It is the biggest rivalry in Croatian football, dividing the capital Zagreb in the north and the Dalmatian city of Split in the south.

History

Hajduk was founded in 1911 and Dinamo in 1945 (inheriting the tradition of Građanski Zagreb). The two clubs dominated Yugoslav football alongside the Belgrade giants and have continued their dominance in the independent Croatian league since 1991, winning the overwhelming majority of league titles between them. Dinamo has been the more successful side in the modern era.

Supporters

The derby is also a clash of ultras groups: Dinamo's Bad Blue Boys and Hajduk's Torcida, one of Europe's oldest organised supporter groups, founded in 1950. Matches are intensely contested on and off the pitch, and the fixture is considered one of the most passionate derbies in European football.
