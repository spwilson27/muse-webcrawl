Rolling admission

Rolling admission is a policy used by many colleges and universities to admit freshmen. Under rolling admission, candidates are invited to submit their applications to the university anytime within a large window. The university then reviews the application and notifies the applicant of their decision within a few weeks of submission, rather than waiting for a fixed deadline.

Comparison with regular admission

Unlike early action or regular decision plans with a single fixed deadline, rolling admission spreads decisions across the admissions season. This gives applicants the possibility of receiving an admissions decision months before regular decisions go out. However, because places may fill as the season progresses, applicants are usually advised to apply as early as possible.

Large public universities in the United States, such as Pennsylvania State University and the University of Pittsburgh, are among the institutions commonly associated with rolling admissions.
