China State Railway Group

Headquarters of China State Railway Group, Beijing

China State Railway Group Co., Ltd. (中国国家铁路集团有限公司), commonly known as China Railway or CR, is a state-owned enterprise that operates passenger and freight rail services in the People's Republic of China. Headquartered in the Haidian District of Beijing, it is one of the largest railway operators in the world, employing roughly two million people.

History

The company originated in China's Ministry of Railways, which for decades both regulated and operated the national rail system. In March 2013, as part of a broad restructuring of the State Council, the ministry was dissolved: its policy and planning functions went to the Ministry of Transport and the new National Railway Administration, while its commercial operations were transferred to the newly created China Railway Corporation. On June 18, 2019, the corporation was restructured into China State Railway Group Co., Ltd., a limited liability company wholly owned by the central government and supervised by the Ministry of Finance.

Structure

The group oversees 18 regional bureaus (such as China Railway Beijing Group, Shanghai Group, and Guangzhou Group) as well as specialized subsidiaries for freight, express services, and international operations. It works alongside, but is distinct from, China Railway Construction Corporation (CRCC) and CRRC, which build infrastructure and manufacture rolling stock respectively.
