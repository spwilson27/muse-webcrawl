MSNBC is an American cable news channel launched on 15 July 1996 as a partnership between NBC News and Microsoft (the name combines 'MS' and 'NBC'). Microsoft divested its stake in the channel in 2005 and in the website in 2012. Known for liberal-leaning prime-time opinion programming, it has been the second major American cable news channel by audience for much of its life.

History

MSNBC began as a straight-news rival to CNN, pivoted toward opinion in the 2000s, and found success with hosts such as Keith Olbermann and Rachel Maddow, whose programme became its flagship. Morning Joe, hosted by Joe Scarborough and Mika Brzezinski, anchors its mornings.

Rebrand

As part of Comcast's spin-off of its cable networks into a new company (Versant), MSNBC was required to drop the NBC name and peacock branding; a rebrand to 'MS NOW' was announced in 2025, with the change taking effect in the mid-2020s while NBC News journalists continued to supply its news coverage under arrangement during transition.
