Gewiss Stadium, known historically as the Stadio Atleti Azzurri d'Italia and originally as the Stadio Mario Brumana, is a football stadium in Bergamo, Italy. It is the home ground of Serie A club Atalanta BC. The stadium opened in 1928 and has been extensively redeveloped since Atalanta purchased it from the municipality in 2017.

History

Built in the late 1920s, the ground was long known as the Stadio Comunale and then the Atleti Azzurri d'Italia. For many years it was regarded as outdated, with an athletics track separating fans from the pitch. In 2017 Atalanta bought the stadium, beginning a phased reconstruction in which each stand was demolished and rebuilt close to the pitch while matches continued.

Redevelopment and naming

The rebuilt north stand (Curva Pisani) opened in 2019, followed by the south stand, with work on the remaining stands completing the transformation in the mid-2020s and raising capacity to roughly 25,000. In 2019 the naming rights were sold to the electrical equipment company Gewiss, giving the stadium its current name. The redeveloped ground has hosted Atalanta's Champions League fixtures, which had previously been played at San Siro in Milan.
