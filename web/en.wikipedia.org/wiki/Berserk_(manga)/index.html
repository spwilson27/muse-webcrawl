Berserk (Japanese: ベルセルク, Hepburn: Beruseruku) is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Kentaro Miura. Set in a medieval Europe-inspired dark fantasy world, the story centres on the characters of Guts, a lone swordsman, and Griffith, the leader of a mercenary band called the "Band of the Hawk".

Plot

Guts, known as the "Black Swordsman", is a wandering mercenary marked by a cursed brand that draws demonic Apostles to him. Once a member of the Band of the Hawk, he seeks revenge against his former friend Griffith, who sacrificed the band during the cataclysmic event known as the Eclipse in order to ascend to the godlike God Hand. The narrative moves between the earlier "Golden Age" arc and Guts's later journey with a new company of companions.

Publication

Miura first published a prototype of Berserk in 1988. The series began serialization in Hakusensha's Monthly Animal House in 1989 and moved to Young Animal in 1992. Miura died suddenly in May 2021; the series resumed in June 2022 under the supervision of his friend Kouji Mori and Miura's assistants at Studio Gaga, based on Miura's notes and plans.

Media and reception

Berserk has been adapted into a 1997 anime television series, a trilogy of films (2012–2013), a second anime series (2016–2017), and several video games. By 2023 the manga had over 60 million copies in circulation. It is widely acclaimed for its detailed artwork, dark themes, and characterization, and is considered one of the most influential manga of all time.
