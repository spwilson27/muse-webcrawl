Iowa Hawkeyes

University: University of Iowa
Conference: Big Ten
Location: Iowa City, Iowa
Colours: Black and gold

The Iowa Hawkeyes are the athletic teams representing the University of Iowa in Iowa City, Iowa. A member of the Big Ten Conference, the university fields teams in more than twenty intercollegiate sports. The Hawkeye nickname is drawn from the state of Iowa's identity as the Hawkeye State, itself named after the Sauk leader Black Hawk.

Football

The football team plays at Kinnick Stadium, named for Nile Kinnick, the 1939 Heisman Trophy winner who died in service during the Second World War. Games there are known for the "Iowa Wave", begun in 2017, in which fans and players turn and wave to patients at the adjacent children's hospital after the first quarter.

Other sports

Iowa's wrestling programme is among the most successful in the United States, having won numerous NCAA team championships, particularly under coach Dan Gable. The women's basketball team drew national attention in the early 2020s behind guard Caitlin Clark, reaching consecutive NCAA championship games in 2023 and 2024. The field hockey team has also won a national title.
