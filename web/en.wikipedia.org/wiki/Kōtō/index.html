Kōtō

Kōtō (江東区, Kōtō-ku) is a special ward of Tokyo, Japan. Located east of the Sumida River on the western shore of Tokyo Bay, it is largely built on reclaimed land in the bay, including areas such as Toyosu and Ariake.

The ward was formed in 1947 from the merger of the former Fukagawa and Jōtō wards. Historically a centre of lumber storage and canals, Kōtō has been extensively redeveloped since the late twentieth century; the Toyosu Market, which replaced the Tsukiji fish market in 2018, is located here, as are the Tokyo Big Sight exhibition centre and several venues built for the Tokyo 2020 Olympic Games.

As of recent estimates the ward has a population of around 525,000 residents and an area of about 43 square kilometres. Major transport links include the Yurikamome and Rinkai lines and the Tokyo Gate Bridge.
