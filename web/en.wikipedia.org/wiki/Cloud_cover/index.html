Cloud cover

FieldMeteorology
UnitsOktas, percent

Cloud cover (also cloudiness or cloud amount) is the fraction of the sky covered by clouds when observed from a particular location. It is a standard element of weather observations and forecasts.

Measurement

Trained observers and automated instruments estimate cover in oktas — eighths of the sky obscured — while satellites provide global measurements. Descriptive terms correspond to ranges of cover: clear, scattered, broken and overcast in aviation weather reports (METARs).

Role in climate

Clouds strongly affect Earth's energy budget: low clouds tend to cool by reflecting sunlight, while high thin clouds can warm by trapping infrared radiation. Cloud feedback is a major source of uncertainty in climate sensitivity estimates.
