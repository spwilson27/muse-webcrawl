2016 Summer Olympics

The 2016 Summer Olympics, officially the Games of the XXXI Olympiad and branded as Rio 2016, were held in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, from 5 to 21 August 2016. They were the first Summer Olympic Games held in South America. More than 11,000 athletes from 207 National Olympic Committees took part in 306 events across 28 sports, with rugby sevens and golf returning to the programme.

Highlights

Usain Bolt completed a "triple-triple" of sprint titles, winning the 100 m, 200 m and 4×100 m relay for the third consecutive Games. Michael Phelps won five golds and one silver to retire with 23 Olympic gold medals. American gymnast Simone Biles won four golds, and host nation Brazil won its first Olympic football gold, defeating Germany on penalties in the men's final at the Maracanã. A ten-athlete Refugee Olympic Team competed under the Olympic flag for the first time.

Issues and legacy

The build-up was overshadowed by Brazil's political and economic crisis, the Zika virus outbreak, polluted venues in Guanabara Bay and the Russian doping scandal, which saw Russia's track and field team banned. Despite these concerns the Games were staged successfully, though several purpose-built venues fell into disuse afterwards, fuelling debate about Olympic costs and legacy.
