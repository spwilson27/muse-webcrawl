Human sexual activity

Human sexual activity, human sexual practice or human sexual behaviour is the manner in which humans experience and express their sexuality. People engage in a variety of sexual acts, ranging from activities done alone (e.g., masturbation) to acts with another person (e.g., sexual intercourse, oral sex, anal sex) in varying patterns of frequency, for a wide variety of reasons.

Sexual response

William H. Masters and Virginia E. Johnson described the human sexual response cycle in four phases: excitement, plateau, orgasm, and resolution. Sexual activity generally plays an important role in pair bonding and in reproduction, and is influenced by biological, psychological, social, and cultural factors.

Health and safety

Sexual activity carries risks including sexually transmitted infections and unintended pregnancy, which can be reduced by safer sex practices such as the use of condoms. Public health guidance also emphasizes consent as a fundamental aspect of healthy sexual behaviour.
