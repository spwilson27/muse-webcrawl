Non-tariff barriers to trade

Non-tariff barriers to trade (NTBs) are trade barriers that restrict imports or exports through mechanisms other than tariffs. They include quotas, import licensing, technical standards, sanitary rules, customs procedures, subsidies and local-content requirements.

Types

Quantitative restrictions cap the volume of goods directly. Technical barriers to trade (TBT) and sanitary and phytosanitary (SPS) measures set product, health and safety requirements that can raise compliance costs or exclude foreign goods. Other measures include rules of origin, government procurement restrictions, embargoes and voluntary export restraints.

Regulation

World Trade Organization agreements — the TBT and SPS Agreements and GATT Article XI — discipline NTBs while recognising members' rights to protect health, safety and the environment. As average tariffs have fallen, NTBs have become the more significant focus of trade negotiations and disputes.
