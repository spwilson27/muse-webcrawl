Capcom Co., Ltd. is a Japanese video game company. It has created a number of critically acclaimed and multi-million-selling game franchises, including Resident Evil, Monster Hunter, Street Fighter, Mega Man, Devil May Cry, Ace Attorney, and Dead Rising. Founded in 1979, it has become an international enterprise with subsidiaries in East Asia, Europe, and North America.

History

The company was founded by Kenzo Tsujimoto as I.R.M. Corporation in 1979, originally a manufacturer and distributor of electronic game machines; the name Capcom is a clipped compound of "Capsule Computers", reflecting the founders' aim of producing machines like capsules packed with gaming fun. Its first arcade games appeared in 1984, including 1942. Capcom entered the home market with the NES version of 1942 and the influential Mega Man (1987).

In the 1990s, Street Fighter II (1991) ignited the fighting-game boom and Resident Evil (1996) defined the survival-horror genre. In the 2000s and 2010s the company built major successes with Devil May Cry, Monster Hunter (whose Monster Hunter: World, 2018, became its best-selling game), and acclaimed Resident Evil remakes built on its in-house RE Engine.

Structure and business

Beyond consumer games, Capcom operates amusement arcades and arcade machines in Japan, licenses its characters for film and merchandise (including Hollywood adaptations of Resident Evil and Monster Hunter), and has pursued a "single content multiple usage" strategy for its intellectual property.
