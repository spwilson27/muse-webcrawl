Sainsbury's

J Sainsbury plc
Founded: 1869, Drury Lane, London
Founder: John James Sainsbury
Headquarters: Holborn, London
Industry: Retail (supermarkets)

J Sainsbury plc, trading as Sainsbury's, is a British supermarket chain and one of the largest retailers in the United Kingdom. Founded in 1869 by John James Sainsbury with a shop in Drury Lane, London, it grew to become the UK's largest grocery retailer for much of the 20th century before being overtaken by Tesco in 1995.

History

The company pioneered self-service shopping in Britain, opening its first self-service store in Croydon in 1950. It floated on the London Stock Exchange in 1973 in what was then the largest flotation ever. The group also owns Argos (acquired 2016) and Habitat, and operates Sainsbury's Bank.

Operations

Sainsbury's operates supermarkets and convenience stores (Sainsbury's Local) across the UK and runs the Nectar loyalty scheme in partnership with other brands.
