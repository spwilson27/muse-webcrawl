Wang Jinxi

Wang Jinxi (Chinese: 王进喜; pinyin: Wáng Jìnxǐ; 8 October 1923 – 15 November 1970) was a Chinese oil worker at the Daqing Oil Field and a celebrated "model worker" of the People's Republic of China. Nicknamed "Iron Man" Wang (铁人王), he became a national symbol of dedication, endurance, and self-sacrifice during the development of China's petroleum industry.

Life

Wang was born into a poor peasant family in Yumen, Gansu. He began working at the Yumen oil field as a young man and joined the Communist Party in 1956. In 1960 he led drilling team No. 1205 to the newly discovered Daqing oil field in Heilongjiang, where workers struggled under primitive conditions. In a famous episode, when a blowout threatened and there was no equipment to mix the drilling mud, Wang jumped into the mud pit and stirred the mud with his own body—an act credited with saving the well and cementing his "Iron Man" image.

Legacy

Wang was honored at national model-worker conferences and became a national deputy at the 1969 Ninth Party Congress. He died of stomach cancer in 1970, aged 47. The "Iron Man spirit" (大庆精神/铁人精神) remains officially promoted in China, and his memorial hall stands in Daqing; drilling team 1205 still bears his name.
