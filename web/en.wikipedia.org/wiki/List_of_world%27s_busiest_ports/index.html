List of world's busiest ports

The world's busiest ports are ranked by cargo tonnage and by container traffic measured in twenty-foot equivalent units (TEU). By both measures the list is dominated by ports in China and East Asia. The Port of Shanghai has been the world's busiest container port since 2010.

By cargo tonnage

By total cargo tonnage, Ningbo-Zhoushan is the world's busiest port, handling well over a billion tonnes per year, driven by iron ore, crude oil and coal bulk trades. Shanghai, Singapore and Tangshan also rank among the largest by tonnage. The largest non-Asian port by tonnage is Rotterdam, Europe's biggest port.
