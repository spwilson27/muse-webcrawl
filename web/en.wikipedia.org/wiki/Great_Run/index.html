Great Run

[Image: Great Run]

Great Run is a British mass-participation sporting events company, best known for organising the Great North Run, the world's largest half marathon, held annually in North East England between Newcastle upon Tyne and South Shields.

History

The company grew out of the success of the first Great North Run in 1981, founded by former Olympic medallist Brendan Foster. The event attracted over 12,000 runners in its first year and now draws around 60,000 participants.

Events

Great Run organises a series of running events across the United Kingdom, including the Great South Run in Portsmouth, the Great Manchester Run, the Great Birmingham Run, the Great Bristol Run, and the Great Scottish Run in Glasgow, as well as junior and mini events and the Great Swim series of open-water swims.
