MacBook Pro

The MacBook Pro is a line of Mac laptop computers developed and manufactured by Apple. It is the higher-end model of the MacBook family, positioned above the MacBook Air, and is currently sold with 14-inch and 16-inch screens. Introduced in January 2006, the MacBook Pro was the first Mac laptop powered by an Intel processor and replaced the PowerBook G4 line.

Generations

  • First generation (2006) — aluminium design carried over from the PowerBook, first with Intel Core Duo chips.
  • Unibody (2008) — enclosure milled from a single block of aluminium; 13, 15 and 17-inch sizes; later models added Retina displays (2012), SSDs, and Thunderbolt.
  • Touch Bar generation (2016) — thinner chassis, USB-C/Thunderbolt 3 only, Touch Bar on most models, and controversial butterfly keyboards, later revised.
  • Apple silicon (2020– ) — transition to Apple's own M-series chips: M1 Pro/Max 14- and 16-inch models in 2021 restored HDMI, SD card reader and MagSafe, followed by M2-, M3- and M4-generation updates with brighter mini-LED ProMotion displays.

Reception

The MacBook Pro line is aimed at professional users in software development, media production and research, and is consistently among the best-selling premium laptops. The Apple silicon generation was widely praised for performance-per-watt and battery life relative to the Intel models it replaced.
