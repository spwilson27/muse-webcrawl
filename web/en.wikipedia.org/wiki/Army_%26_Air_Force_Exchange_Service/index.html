Army & Air Force Exchange Service

A Main Exchange operated by AAFES

The Army & Air Force Exchange Service (AAFES, also known as The Exchange) is the retailer on U.S. Army and Air Force installations worldwide. Founded in 1895, it operates department stores (post exchanges and base exchanges), convenience stores, restaurants, theatres, gas stations and other services on military bases, and an online store. AAFES is a non-appropriated fund instrumentality of the Department of Defense, jointly serving the Army and the Air Force (and now Space Force).

History

The exchange system traces its origins to the sutlers and post traders of the nineteenth century. On 25 July 1895 the War Department issued General Orders No. 46 directing post commanders to establish exchanges, replacing private traders and canteens with a regulated system whose profits supported soldiers' welfare. After the creation of the independent Air Force in 1947, the services combined their exchange systems in 1948 under the Army & Air Force Exchange Service name.

Operations

AAFES operates thousands of facilities in the United States and in dozens of countries and territories, including deployed locations supporting troops in combat zones. Its operations include main exchanges, Express convenience stores, food courts and name-brand fast food outlets, Class Six (package liquor) stores, barber and beauty shops, and movie theatres. Sales are generally free of state sales tax and priced to be competitive with off-base retailers.

Two-thirds of AAFES's earnings are typically paid out as dividends to Army and Air Force Morale, Welfare and Recreation programs, funding fitness centres, recreation and family services; the remainder funds capital improvements. Access to exchange privileges is limited by law and regulation principally to service members, retirees, their dependants and certain other authorised categories.
