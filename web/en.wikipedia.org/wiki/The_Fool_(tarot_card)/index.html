The Fool (tarot card)

The Fool is one of the 78 cards in a tarot deck and one of the 22 Major Arcana. In most traditional decks it is unnumbered or numbered 0, placing it outside the sequence of the other trumps. In the Rider–Waite deck the Fool is shown as a young traveller standing at a cliff's edge, gazing at the sky, accompanied by a small dog.

In the game of tarot

In tarot card games, the Fool is the "Excuse", a special card that can be played to any trick, excusing the player from following suit. Its role is unlike that of the other trumps.

Divinatory meanings

In cartomancy the Fool is associated with beginnings, spontaneity, innocence, faith in the future, and taking a leap into the unknown; reversed, it may signify recklessness or folly. Occultists such as those of the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn linked the card to the Hebrew letter Aleph and the element of air, and placed it at the start of the Major Arcana's spiritual journey — the "Fool's journey" through the remaining trumps.
