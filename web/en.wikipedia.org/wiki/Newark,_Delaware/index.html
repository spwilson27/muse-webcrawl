Newark is a city in New Castle County, Delaware, United States. It is best known as the home of the University of Delaware, whose campus dominates the centre of the city. Newark lies near the borders with Maryland and Pennsylvania, about 12 miles southwest of Wilmington.

History

Newark grew from a crossroads settlement chartered in 1758. The Newark Academy, founded in 1743, evolved into the University of Delaware. In the nineteenth century the town became a small industrial centre making paper, fibre (vulcanised fibre), and organ pipes, served by the Philadelphia, Wilmington and Baltimore Railroad.

Economy and culture

Today the university is the largest employer, and Main Street's restaurants and shops serve a large student population. The city is surrounded by White Clay Creek State Park and is a hub for cycling, hosting national-level road races.
