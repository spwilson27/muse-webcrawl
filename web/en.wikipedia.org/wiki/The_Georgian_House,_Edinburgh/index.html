No. 7 Charlotte Square, the Georgian House

The Georgian House is a historic house museum at 7 Charlotte Square in Edinburgh, Scotland, operated by the National Trust for Scotland. The house is part of Robert Adam's Charlotte Square, completed in the 1790s and regarded as a masterpiece of Georgian town planning within Edinburgh's New Town.

Description

The house has been restored and furnished to show life in a wealthy New Town home around the early nineteenth century. The principal rooms include a drawing room, dining room, and bedroom dressed with period furniture, paintings, porcelain, and silver, while the basement kitchens and servants' quarters illustrate the working life below stairs. The house's first owner was John Lamont of Lamont, a Highland chief, who bought the property in 1796.

Visitor information

The Georgian House is open to the public seasonally and is one of the National Trust for Scotland's principal Edinburgh properties.
