Monty Don

Montagu Denis Wyatt Don OBE DL (born 8 July 1955) is a British horticulturist, broadcaster, and writer who is best known as the lead presenter of the BBC gardening television series Gardeners' World since 2003.

Born in Germany and raised in Hampshire, Don studied at Magdalene College, Cambridge, where he met his future wife Sarah. The couple moved to London, where they ran a successful costume jewellery business; the collapse of that business in the early 1990s left them nearly bankrupt, an experience Don later wrote about in his memoir.

Don began his broadcasting career as the gardening presenter on Channel 4's The Real Gardens and went on to present numerous programmes, including Fork to Fork, Italian Gardens, French Gardens, Japanese Gardens and American Gardens, as well as the series Big Dreams, Small Spaces.

His home, Longmeadow, in Herefordshire, has been the setting for Gardeners' World since 2011 and is the subject of several of his books. Don is a prolific author, with works including The Complete Gardener, The Jewel Garden (written with Sarah Don), Nigel: My Family and Other Dogs, and Down to Earth. He has written about his struggles with depression and his belief in the therapeutic value of gardening, and has campaigned for organic gardening practices.
