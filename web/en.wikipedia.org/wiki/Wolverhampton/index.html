Wolverhampton

Wolverhampton is a city and metropolitan borough in the West Midlands of England. It lies northwest of Birmingham and forms part of the West Midlands conurbation, with a population of around 264,000 at the 2021 census. It was granted city status in 2000.

History

The town's name derives from Lady Wulfrun, who founded a church in 985 (Heantune). Wolverhampton grew as a market town specialising in the woollen trade, and in the Industrial Revolution became a major centre for coal mining, steel, locks, japanning and the manufacture of motorcycles and cars. Wolverhampton Wanderers F.C., founded in 1877 and based at Molineux Stadium, are the city's professional football club.
