The Big Mac is a hamburger sold by the international fast food restaurant chain McDonald's. It was introduced in the Greater Pittsburgh area in 1967 and nationwide in 1968. It is one of the company's flagship products and signature food items. The Big Mac consists of two beef patties separated by an additional middle bun slice, along with iceberg lettuce, American cheese, pickles, chopped onions, and a Thousand Island-type dressing called "Big Mac sauce" — all on a three-part sesame seed bun.

History

The Big Mac was created by Jim Delligatti, an early Ray Kroc franchisee who operated several McDonald's restaurants in the Pittsburgh area. It was first offered at Delligatti's Uniontown, Pennsylvania restaurant in 1967, priced at 45 cents. The sandwich was designed to compete with the Big Boy hamburger from the Big Boy restaurant chain. After proving popular, it was added to menus nationwide in 1968.

The name "Big Mac" was created by Esther Glickstein Rose, a 21-year-old advertising secretary working at McDonald's corporate headquarters in Oak Brook, Illinois. The famous advertising jingle listing the ingredients — "Two all-beef patties, special sauce, lettuce, cheese, pickles, onions on a sesame seed bun" — debuted in 1974.

Product description

The Big Mac is made with two 1.6 oz (45.4 g) beef patties, a three-piece sesame seed bun, a slice of American cheese, shredded iceberg lettuce, sliced pickles, diced onions, and Big Mac sauce. Outside the United States, its formulation varies somewhat: in some markets the patties are larger, and halal or kosher versions are produced where required.

Big Mac index

The sandwich has become an informal economic indicator. The Big Mac index, published by The Economist since 1986, compares the price of a Big Mac in various countries as a playful measure of purchasing power parity between currencies.

Variants

Variants have included the Mega Mac (with four patties), the Chicken Big Mac, and limited regional editions. In India, where beef is not served at McDonald's, the Chicken Maharaja Mac fills the equivalent role on the menu.
