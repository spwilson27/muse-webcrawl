Wedding favor

A wedding favor is a small gift given as a gesture of appreciation or gratitude to guests from the bride and groom during a wedding ceremony or a wedding reception.

History

The tradition of distributing wedding favors is hundreds of years old. It is believed that the first wedding favors, common amongst European aristocrats, were known as bonbonniere — small decorated boxes made of crystal, porcelain, or precious stones containing bonbons or sugar cubes, which were then a symbol of wealth and royalty, as sugar was an expensive commodity. As sugar became more affordable, the tradition spread to the general population.

In many cultures around the world, including Italy and Greece, Jordan almonds (sugared almonds known as confetti in Italian tradition) became the classic favor: five almonds in a small pouch or box represented five wishes for the newlyweds — health, wealth, happiness, fertility, and longevity. The bitterness of the almond and the sweetness of the candy coating also symbolized the bitter and sweet aspects of marriage.

Modern favors

Contemporary wedding favors range widely and often reflect the couple's personalities or the wedding theme: chocolates, candles, seeds, miniature bottles, personalised keepsakes, edible treats, or charitable donations made in guests' names. Favors are typically placed at each guest's place setting at the reception.
