List of Pokémon

The Pokémon franchise revolves around fictional species of creatures, each with unique designs, skills and powers. Created by Satoshi Tajiri and developed by Game Freak and Nintendo, the franchise began with Pokémon Red and Green in Japan in 1996, which introduced the original 151 species, known as Generation I. As of Generation IX (Pokémon Scarlet and Violet, 2022), there are 1,025 known species, numbered in the National Pokédex.

Each generation is set in a new region and introduces new species alongside new mechanics. Species were primarily designed by Ken Sugimori and a team of artists.
