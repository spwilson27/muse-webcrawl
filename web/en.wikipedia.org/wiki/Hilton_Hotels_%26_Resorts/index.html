Hilton Hotels & Resorts (formerly known as Hilton Hotels) is a global brand of full-service hotels and resorts and the flagship brand of American multinational hospitality company Hilton Worldwide. The original company was founded by Conrad Hilton in 1919, when he bought the Mobley Hotel in Cisco, Texas.

History

Conrad Hilton expanded across Texas and beyond, opening the first hotel to bear the Hilton name in Dallas in 1925. The company grew internationally after the Second World War, and for much of the twentieth century Hilton's international and American operations were run by separate companies. The two were reunited in 2006 when Hilton Hotels Corporation acquired Hilton International.

Properties

The brand operates hundreds of hotels in more than 90 countries, generally positioned at the upscale end of the market, including landmark properties such as the Hilton Hawaiian Village, the London Hilton on Park Lane, and the Waldorf Astoria New York (managed under a sister brand). Hilton Hotels & Resorts was an early innovator in amenities such as air conditioning, airport hotels, and centralised reservations.
