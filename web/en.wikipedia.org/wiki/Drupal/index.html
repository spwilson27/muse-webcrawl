Drupal

Drupal is a free and open-source web content management system (CMS) written in PHP and distributed under the GNU General Public License. Drupal provides an open-source back-end framework for at least 14% of the top 10,000 websites worldwide and 1.2% of the top 10 million websites, ranging from personal blogs to corporate, political, and government sites.

History

Drupal was originally written by Dries Buytaert as a message board for students at the University of Antwerp. The name derives from the Dutch word druppel, meaning "drop", itself a reference to the project's original site, Drop.org. Buytaert released Drupal as open source in 2001. The Drupal community grew steadily, and the Drupal Association, a non-profit organization, was founded in 2006 to support the project.

Features

Drupal core includes basic features common to content management systems: user account registration and maintenance, menu management, RSS feeds, taxonomy, page layout customization, and system administration. Thousands of community-contributed modules extend its functionality, and themes control the presentation of content. Drupal is known for its powerful taxonomy system, its flexible content types and fields, and its strong support for multilingual sites and accessibility. It is frequently used for complex, content-rich websites and for headless (decoupled) architectures, in which Drupal serves content to separate front-end applications through APIs.

Community

The Drupal project is maintained by a large international community of contributors. The Drupal Association organizes DrupalCon conferences, and local communities hold Drupal camps and meetups worldwide. Security is handled by a dedicated security team that coordinates vulnerability disclosures and releases.

Criticism

Drupal has been criticized for a steep learning curve compared with simpler systems such as WordPress, and major-version upgrades (notably from Drupal 7 to Drupal 8) historically required substantial rebuilding of sites. From Drupal 8 onward, the project adopted a continuous upgrade path based on the Symfony framework components.
