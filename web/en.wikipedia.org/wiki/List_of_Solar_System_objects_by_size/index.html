List of Solar System objects by size

This article lists Solar System objects by mean radius, from the Sun down to bodies a few hundred metres across. It includes the planets, dwarf planets, their larger moons, and the largest asteroids and trans-Neptunian objects.

Smaller bodies

Below Ceres, the list continues with large asteroids such as Vesta (263 km), Pallas (256 km) and Hygiea (217 km), Kuiper belt objects such as Orcus and Quaoar, and the larger irregular satellites, down to objects of roughly 100 km radius and below, where measurements grow increasingly uncertain.
