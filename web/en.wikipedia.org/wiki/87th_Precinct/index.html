87th Precinct

The 87th Precinct is a series of police procedural novels by Ed McBain (the pen name of Evan Hunter), published from 1956 to 2005 — nearly sixty books — and widely credited with popularising the ensemble police procedural.


Author: Ed McBain
First book: Cop Hater (1956)
Last book: Fiddlers (2005)
Setting: Isola (fictional New York City)

Series

Set in the fictional city of Isola, the novels rotate an ensemble of detectives — notably Steve Carella, Bert Kling, Meyer Meyer, Cotton Hawes and the deaf criminal mastermind known as 'the Deaf Man' — emphasising forensic realism, multiple subplots and the city itself as a character.

Influence and adaptations

The series influenced television procedurals from Hill Street Blues onward and was itself adapted for TV (1961–62), several films including Japanese and American versions, and Akira Kurosawa's High and Low drew on McBain's King's Ransom.
