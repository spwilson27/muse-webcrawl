Daqing


Image placeholder: Daqing

Daqing (Chinese: 大庆; pinyin: Dàqìng) is a prefecture-level city in Heilongjiang province, China, with a population of roughly 2.8 million. It is famous as the site of the Daqing Oil Field, China's largest oil field, discovered in 1959.

History

The city grew around the development of the oil field, whose discovery was hailed as a triumph of self-reliance; the model worker Wang Jinxi, the "Iron Man", became a national hero of the campaign to develop Daqing. The city was officially established in 1979 from the former Anda special district.

Economy

Daqing's economy remains dominated by petroleum extraction and petrochemicals, operated by PetroChina's Daqing Oilfield Company. Output has declined from its peak as the field matures, and the city has sought to diversify into other industries and services. Daqing is also known for its wetlands and nature reserves for cranes.
