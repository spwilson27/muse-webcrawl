Energy in Australia

Energy in Australia is the production in Australia of energy and electricity, and its consumption and trade. Australia is a net energy exporter, with abundant reserves of coal and natural gas, and is one of the world's largest exporters of liquefied natural gas and coal. Electricity generation has historically been dominated by coal-fired power stations, but renewable energy — particularly wind and rooftop solar — has grown rapidly since the 2010s.

Electricity

The National Electricity Market interconnects the eastern and southern states, while Western Australia and the Northern Territory operate separate grids. Australia has among the highest per-capita uptake of rooftop solar photovoltaic systems in the world. Energy policy is shaped by the country's international commitments on greenhouse gas emissions and ongoing debate over the pace of the transition away from fossil fuels.
