Howards End (film)

Howards End
Directed by: James Ivory
Screenplay by: Ruth Prawer Jhabvala
Based on: Howards End by E. M. Forster
Starring: Anthony Hopkins, Emma Thompson, Vanessa Redgrave, Helena Bonham Carter
Release date: 1992
Running time: 142 minutes

Howards End is a 1992 period romantic drama film by Merchant Ivory Productions, directed by James Ivory, written by Ruth Prawer Jhabvala, and based on E. M. Forster's 1910 novel of the same name. It explores class relations in Edwardian England through the intersecting lives of three families.

Plot

The intellectual, middle-class Schlegel sisters, Margaret (Emma Thompson) and Helen (Helena Bonham Carter), befriend Ruth Wilcox (Vanessa Redgrave), the ailing wife of wealthy businessman Henry Wilcox (Anthony Hopkins). Before her death Ruth leaves a note bequeathing her beloved country house, Howards End, to Margaret — a note the Wilcox family suppresses. Margaret later marries Henry, while Helen's involvement with the impoverished clerk Leonard Bast brings the class tensions of the story to a tragic climax at Howards End itself.

Reception and awards

The film premiered at the 1992 Cannes Film Festival, where it won the 45th Anniversary Prize, and received wide critical acclaim. At the 65th Academy Awards it was nominated for nine Oscars and won three: Best Actress for Emma Thompson, Best Adapted Screenplay, and Best Art Direction. It is frequently ranked among the finest Merchant Ivory productions.
