List of Indianapolis 500 winners

The Indianapolis 500, held annually at the Indianapolis Motor Speedway since 1911, is one of the most prestigious automobile races in the world and forms part of the Triple Crown of Motorsport. This is a list of its winners.

Multiple winners

Four drivers have won the race four times: A. J. Foyt (1961, 1964, 1967, 1977), Al Unser Sr. (1970, 1971, 1978, 1987), Rick Mears (1979, 1984, 1988, 1991) and Hélio Castroneves (2001, 2002, 2009, 2021). Three-time winners include Louis Meyer, Wilbur Shaw, Mauri Rose, Johnny Rutherford, Bobby Unser, Emerson Fittipaldi and Dario Franchitti.

Records

The fastest race was the 2021 edition, won by Castroneves at an average speed of 190.690 mph. Rick Mears holds the record for pole positions with six. The winner traditionally drinks a bottle of milk in Victory Lane, a custom begun by Louis Meyer in 1936, and the winner's likeness is sculpted in sterling silver on the Borg-Warner Trophy.
