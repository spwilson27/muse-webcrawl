Johannes Hendrikus Hubert "John" de Mol Jr. (born April 24, 1955) is a Dutch media tycoon and television producer, one of the founders of the modern reality-television format business. He co-founded the production company Endemol in 1994 and later founded Talpa.

Career

The son of singer John de Mol Sr., he began in radio and television production in the 1970s. His company John de Mol Produkties merged with Joop van den Ende's to form Endemol, which created the reality show Big Brother (1999) and the game show Deal or No Deal — formats exported worldwide. Endemol was sold to Telefónica in 2000 for a sum that made de Mol a billionaire.

Talpa and The Voice

Through his later company Talpa, de Mol created the singing competition format The Voice, launched in the Netherlands in 2010 and licensed internationally. He has also been a major shareholder in Dutch commercial broadcasting, including the SBS6 and Net5 channels and the Talpa Network.
