Apocalypse (En Sabah Nur) is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Louise Simonson and artist Jackson Guice, he first appeared in X-Factor #5 in 1986 and is one of the principal antagonists of the X-Men. He is depicted as one of the world's first mutants, born in ancient Egypt some five thousand years ago, and enhanced by alien Celestial technology.

Fictional character biography

Abandoned as an infant and raised by nomads, En Sabah Nur — "the First One" — embraced a philosophy of survival of the fittest after encounters with the time traveller Kang and the Celestials. Over millennia he periodically emerged to test humanity, recruiting four followers as his Horsemen (War, Famine, Pestilence and Death). Major storylines include his transformation of Angel into the Horseman Death (Archangel) and the alternate-reality saga Age of Apocalypse (1995), in which he conquered North America.

Powers and abilities

Apocalypse possesses complete molecular control over his body, granting him superhuman strength, shapeshifting, size alteration, energy projection and near-immortality, further augmented by his Celestial armour and his ability to transfer his consciousness into new host bodies.

In other media

The character was portrayed by Oscar Isaac in the film X-Men: Apocalypse (2016) and appeared in X-Men: The Animated Series and other adaptations.
