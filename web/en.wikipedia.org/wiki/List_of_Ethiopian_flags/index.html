List of Ethiopian flags

This is a list of flags used in Ethiopia, including the national flag, historical imperial and military banners, and the flags of the country's regional states.

National flag

The flag of Ethiopia consists of horizontal stripes of green, yellow, and red, the colours of the Ethiopian pennants used since the 19th century and of the banner of the Ethiopian Empire. The green-yellow-red tricolour was formally adopted on 6 February 1897 under Emperor Menelik II, and has influenced the flags of many other African nations, giving rise to the term "Pan-African colours".

The current version, adopted on 31 October 1996, bears the national emblem in the centre: a radiant star with rays on a blue disc, representing the diversity and unity of the peoples of Ethiopia. Earlier versions carried the Lion of Judah under the Empire (–1974), the socialist emblem under the Derg and the People's Democratic Republic (1975–1991), and a plain tricolour in the transitional period (1991–1996).

Historical flags

The list also covers the pennants and banners of the Ethiopian Empire, the flag flown during the Italian occupation (1936–1941), the flags of the Federation of Ethiopia and Eritrea (1952–1962), and military, naval, and imperial standards, including the personal standard of the Emperor.
