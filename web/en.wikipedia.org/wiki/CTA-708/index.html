CTA-708

CTA-708 (formerly EIA-708 and also known as CEA-708) is a standard developed by the Consumer Technology Association (CTA) for closed captioning of digital television (DTV) streams in the United States and other countries using the ATSC standard. It was designed to replace the older EIA-608 captions used with analog television.

Features

CTA-708 captions are transmitted within the MPEG-2 video stream of a DTV broadcast. Unlike the character-based EIA-608 system, CTA-708 provides up to 63 caption services, supports multiple languages and character sets (including Latin, Cyrillic, Chinese, Korean, and Japanese characters), and allows viewers to control text size, font, color, background, and opacity. Caption windows can be positioned more flexibly on screen, and the standard supports both native 708 captions and compatibility bytes carrying 608 data.

The standard defines the caption channel data structure, service blocks, command sets for pen attributes, window definitions, and predefined anchor positions. In the United States, the FCC requires digital television programming to carry closed captions, and 708 is the delivery format for HD broadcasts.
