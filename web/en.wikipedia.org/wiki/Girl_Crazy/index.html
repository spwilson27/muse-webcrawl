Girl Crazy

MusicGeorge Gershwin
LyricsIra Gershwin
BookGuy Bolton and John McGowan
Premiere1930, Alvin Theatre, Broadway

Girl Crazy is a 1930 musical with music by George Gershwin, lyrics by Ira Gershwin, and a book by Guy Bolton and John McGowan. The story follows Danny Churchill, a wealthy New York playboy sent to a dude ranch in Arizona, where he turns the ranch into a lively resort and falls for the local postmistress, Molly Gray.

Productions

The original Broadway production opened on 14 October 1930 at the Alvin Theatre and ran for 272 performances. It starred Ginger Rogers and marked the Broadway debut of Ethel Merman, whose rendition of "I Got Rhythm" made her a star. The pit orchestra included musicians such as Benny Goodman, Glenn Miller, and Gene Krupa.

Songs and adaptations

The score produced several standards, including "I Got Rhythm", "Embraceable You", "But Not for Me", and "Bidin' My Time". The musical was adapted into films in 1932 and 1943, the latter starring Judy Garland and Mickey Rooney, and was later reworked as the 1992 musical Crazy for You.
