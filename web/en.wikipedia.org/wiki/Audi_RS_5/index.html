Audi RS 5

The Audi RS 5 is a high-performance version of the Audi A5 range, produced by Audi Sport GmbH (formerly quattro GmbH), the performance subsidiary of the German manufacturer Audi. Introduced in 2010 as a coupé, it is the flagship of the A5 line, positioned above the S5, and combines a powerful engine with the quattro all-wheel-drive system. A five-door Sportback version joined the range in the second generation.

First generation (2010–2015)

The first RS 5 Coupé debuted at the 2010 Geneva Motor Show. It used a naturally aspirated 4.2-litre V8 with FSI direct injection, derived from the RS 4's engine and shared in lower tune with the R8 supercar, producing 450 PS (331 kW) at 8,250 rpm and revving to 8,500 rpm. Power went through a seven-speed S tronic dual-clutch gearbox to a quattro system with a crown-gear centre differential and torque vectoring. The 0–100 km/h sprint took 4.6 seconds, with an optional top speed of 280 km/h. A cabriolet followed in 2013. The RS 5 also served as the basis of Audi's DTM silhouette racing cars in the early 2010s.

Second generation (2017–2024)

The second-generation RS 5, shown at Geneva in 2017, abandoned the V8 for a 2.9-litre twin-turbocharged V6 developed with Porsche, again producing 450 PS but with substantially more torque (600 Nm) and lower emissions; it used an eight-speed automatic transmission. Performance improved to 3.9 seconds for 0–100 km/h. The five-door RS 5 Sportback followed in 2018, and a facelift arrived for 2020. Limited "Competition" packages late in the production run sharpened the chassis with adjustable coilover suspension and reduced sound insulation.
