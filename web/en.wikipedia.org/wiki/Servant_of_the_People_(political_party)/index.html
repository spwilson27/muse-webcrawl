Servant of the People (Ukrainian: Слуга народу, romanized: Sluha narodu) is a liberal, centrist and pro-European political party in Ukraine. It was formed in late 2017 and registered on 31 March 2018, taking its name from the Ukrainian TV series Servant of the People, in which Volodymyr Zelenskyy played a schoolteacher who unexpectedly becomes President of Ukraine.

History

The party was first registered as the Party of Decisive Changes in 2016 and was renamed in 2017–2018. Volodymyr Zelenskyy, star of the eponymous television series produced by his studio Kvartal 95, announced his candidacy for the 2019 Ukrainian presidential election on 31 December 2018 and won the election in April 2019 with about 73 percent of the vote in the second round against incumbent Petro Poroshenko.

In the snap parliamentary election of July 2019, Servant of the People won 43.16 percent of the party-list vote and a total of 254 of 450 seats in the Verkhovna Rada — the first single-party majority in post-Soviet Ukrainian history. The party campaigned on anti-corruption, economic liberalisation, and digitalisation of public services, and many of its deputies were political newcomers selected partly through an open application process.

Ideology and policies

The party describes its ideology as "Ukrainian centrism" and has been characterised by observers as populist, liberal, and pro-European. Its programme has included anti-corruption reforms, a land market reform, large-scale privatisation, tax reform, and support for Ukraine's integration with the European Union and NATO. Since Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, the party has governed under martial law, with Zelenskyy remaining in office as wartime president.
