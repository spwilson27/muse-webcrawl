Minions is a 2015 American animated comedy film produced by Illumination Entertainment and distributed by Universal Pictures. A spin-off prequel to the Despicable Me franchise, it tells the origin story of the Minions — small yellow creatures who exist to serve the most despicable masters they can find — following three of them, Kevin, Stuart, and Bob, on a quest in the 1960s that leads them to the supervillain Scarlet Overkill.

Plot

Having accidentally destroyed every master from the T. rex to Napoleon, the Minions fall into depression in an ice cave. Kevin leads Stuart and Bob to 1968 New York and then to Villain-Con in Orlando, where they are hired by Scarlet Overkill, the world's first female supervillain, who orders them to steal Queen Elizabeth II's crown from the Tower of London. After Bob is accidentally crowned king, Scarlet turns on them; the trio ultimately defeats her with the help of a giant-sized Kevin, and the Minions take up service with a young Gru.

Reception

The film received mixed reviews, with praise for its humour and animation but criticism of its plot and the Minions' ability to carry a feature. It grossed $1.159 billion worldwide, becoming the second-highest-grossing animated film of 2015 and, at the time, the second-highest-grossing animated film ever. A sequel, Minions: The Rise of Gru, was released in 2022.
