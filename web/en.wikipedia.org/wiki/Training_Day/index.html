Training Day

Training Day is a 2001 American crime thriller film directed by Antoine Fuqua and written by David Ayer. It stars Denzel Washington as Alonzo Harris, a corrupt Los Angeles narcotics detective, and Ethan Hawke as Jake Hoyt, a rookie officer spending his first day under evaluation in Alonzo's unit.

Plot

Over a single day, Hoyt is drawn into Alonzo's brutal and criminal methods, forced to confront a series of moral compromises as the veteran detective's debts and schemes close in on him. The film culminates in a confrontation between the two men in the gang-controlled neighbourhood that Alonzo has exploited for years.

Reception

Washington won the Academy Award for Best Actor for his performance, and Hawke was nominated for Best Supporting Actor. The film was a commercial success and has been widely cited among the best crime films of its era. It later inspired a television series of the same name in 2017.
