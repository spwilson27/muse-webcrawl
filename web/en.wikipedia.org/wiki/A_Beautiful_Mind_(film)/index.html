A Beautiful Mind (film)

A Beautiful Mind
Director: Ron Howard
Starring: Russell Crowe, Jennifer Connelly
Release: 2001
Awards: Academy Award for Best Picture

A Beautiful Mind is a 2001 American biographical drama film directed by Ron Howard, inspired by Sylvia Nasar's biography of mathematician John Nash, played by Russell Crowe. It depicts Nash's brilliance at Princeton, his work on game theory, and his struggle with paranoid schizophrenia.

Plot

Nash develops his governing dynamics concept, joins MIT, and believes he is recruited for codebreaking work that proves delusional. Supported by his wife Alicia, he learns to live with his illness and receives the 1994 Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences.

Reception

The film won four Academy Awards including Best Picture, Best Director, Best Adapted Screenplay and Best Supporting Actress for Connelly.
