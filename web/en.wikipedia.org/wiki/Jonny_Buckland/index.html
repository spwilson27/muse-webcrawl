Jonny Buckland

Jonathan Mark Buckland (born 11 September 1977) is a Welsh guitarist and songwriter, best known as the lead guitarist and co-founder of the rock band Coldplay. His melodic, echoing guitar style, influenced by U2's the Edge and My Bloody Valentine, is a defining element of the band's sound.

Early life

Buckland was born in London and raised in Mold, Wales, where he attended Alun School. He began playing guitar at age 11, encouraged by his older brother. He went on to study astronomy and mathematics at University College London, where in 1996 he met Chris Martin; the two began writing songs together and formed the band that became Coldplay, with Guy Berryman and Will Champion.

Coldplay

Buckland co-writes Coldplay's songs with the other band members; the band splits all songwriting credits equally. His guitar work features prominently on albums from Parachutes (2000) and A Rush of Blood to the Head (2002) through Viva la Vida (2008), Music of the Spheres (2021), and Moon Music (2024). Signature riffs include those on "Yellow", "Clocks", "In My Place", "Speed of Sound", and "Viva la Vida". With Coldplay he has won seven Grammy Awards and sold over 100 million records worldwide.

Personal life

Buckland is married to Chloe Evans, a jewellery designer, and they have children. He is godfather to Chris Martin's daughter Apple. A supporter of charitable causes with the band, he has participated in numerous benefit performances.
