Wikipedia

Wikipedia is a free online encyclopedia, written and maintained by a community of volunteers known as Wikipedians. Founded on 15 January 2001, it is the largest and most-read reference work in history, with editions in more than 300 languages. Most of its content is freely reusable under a Creative Commons licence.

Wikipedia is hosted by the Wikimedia Foundation, a non-profit organisation based in San Francisco, California. It was founded by Jimmy Wales and Larry Sanger as a companion to Nupedia, and its articles are written collaboratively by anonymous and registered editors following policies of neutral point of view and verifiability.

History

Wikipedia grew extremely rapidly from its 2001 launch, overtaking established encyclopedias in size within a few years. The English edition passed one million articles in 2006. Growth in article creation later slowed, while the project's influence, readership and use by search engines and voice assistants continued to increase.

Content and editing

Articles are written in a summary style, citing reliable published sources. Anyone can edit most pages; edits are recorded in a public page history, and vandalism is usually reverted quickly by volunteers and automated bots. Content disputes are resolved by discussion and consensus on talk pages.

Reception

Studies comparing Wikipedia with traditional encyclopedias have generally found comparable accuracy in scientific entries, while critics have raised concerns about systemic bias, uneven quality, and the reliability of articles on controversial topics. Wikipedia itself advises readers to use it as a starting point and to check its references.
