A first-generation Golf

The Volkswagen Golf Mk1 is the first generation of the small family car manufactured and marketed by Volkswagen. It was noteworthy for signalling Volkswagen's shift of its major car lines from rear-wheel drive and rear-mounted air-cooled engines to front-wheel drive with front-mounted, water-cooled engines that were often transversely mounted.

Development

Introduced in May 1974 as a modern replacement for the ageing Beetle, the Golf's crisp hatchback body was styled by Giorgetto Giugiaro. In North America it was sold as the Volkswagen Rabbit; a sedan derivative was marketed as the Jetta.

Variants

The range included economical diesel models and, from 1976, the Golf GTI, whose blend of a fuel-injected 1.6-litre engine and hatchback practicality created the hot hatch formula. A convertible, the Golf Cabriolet, and the Caddy pickup extended the platform's life; South Africa built the Mk1 as the Citi Golf until 2009.
