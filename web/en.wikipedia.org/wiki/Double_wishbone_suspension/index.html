Double wishbone suspension

Double wishbone suspension is an independent wheel suspension design using two wishbone-shaped arms to locate each wheel, allowing engineers to control camber and geometry precisely through wheel travel.

Design

Upper and lower A-arms connect the wheel hub to the chassis; a coil spring and damper act on one arm. The layout is common on sports and racing cars and many front-engine production cars.

Advantages

It offers strong handling and tuning flexibility at the cost of space and complexity compared with MacPherson struts.
