Avanti West Coast is a British train operating company owned by FirstGroup (70%) and Trenitalia (30%) that operates long-distance inter-city services on the West Coast Main Line. It took over from Virgin Trains on 8 December 2019 as the operator of the West Coast Partnership franchise, which also was originally intended to include the development of services on High Speed 2.

Services and fleet

Avanti runs trains from London Euston to the West Midlands, North West England, North Wales, and Scotland, calling at cities including Birmingham, Coventry, Manchester, Liverpool, Preston, Carlisle, Glasgow, and Edinburgh. It inherited the Class 390 Pendolino tilting electric trains and Class 221 Super Voyager diesels from Virgin Trains. From 2024, new Class 805 and 807 Evero trains built by Hitachi began replacing the Voyagers, enabling all-electric operation under the wires and releasing capacity.

Performance and contract

The company faced sustained criticism over cancellations, timetable reductions, and onboard service during 2022–2024, driven partly by driver shortages and industrial relations issues. Its contract was placed on short-term extensions while the government pursued rail reform; under the Passenger Railway Services (Public Ownership) Act 2024, Avanti's services are expected to transfer to public ownership when its current contract terms allow, with the date announced as part of the rolling renationalisation programme.
