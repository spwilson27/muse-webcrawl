MLS Cup 2015 was the 20th edition of the MLS Cup, the championship match of Major League Soccer (MLS), contested on December 6, 2015, between Columbus Crew SC and the Portland Timbers at Mapfre Stadium in Columbus, Ohio. The Timbers won the match 2–1 to claim their first MLS Cup title.

Portland struck almost immediately: goalkeeper Steve Clark's error in the opening minute allowed Diego Valeri to score after just 27 seconds, one of the fastest goals in MLS Cup history. Rodney Wallace headed in a second goal in the seventh minute. Kei Kamara pulled one back for Columbus in the 18th minute after a goalmouth scramble, but the Timbers held on despite a controversial incident in the second half in which the ball appeared to cross the byline before a Crew attack that ended with the ball striking the post.

Road to the final

Columbus Crew SC, led by coach Gregg Berhalter, finished second in the Eastern Conference and defeated the Montreal Impact and the Supporters' Shield-winning New York Red Bulls in the playoffs. The Portland Timbers, coached by Caleb Porter, finished third in the Western Conference, beat Sporting Kansas City in a knockout-round shootout remembered for its dramatic "double post" finish, then eliminated rivals Seattle Sounders FC and top seed FC Dallas.

Match summary

The final is remembered for its frantic opening: Valeri's goal, officially timed at 27 seconds, came when Clark took a heavy touch on a back pass and the Argentine midfielder slid in to deflect the clearance into the net. Wallace's header from a Jorge Villafaña cross made it 2–0 within seven minutes. Kamara's reply kept the match alive, and Columbus pressed late, but Portland's defense, anchored by Nat Borchers and Liam Ridgewell, preserved the lead. Valeri was named the MLS Cup Most Valuable Player.
