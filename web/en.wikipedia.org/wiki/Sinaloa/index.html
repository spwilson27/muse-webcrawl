Sinaloa

Sinaloa is one of the 31 states of Mexico, located in northwestern Mexico on the coast of the Gulf of California. Its capital and largest city is Culiacán; other major cities include Mazatlán, Los Mochis and Guasave.

Geography and economy

The state stretches from coastal plains up into the Sierra Madre Occidental. Sinaloa is one of Mexico's most important agricultural states — sometimes called the breadbasket of Mexico — producing tomatoes, maize, wheat and other crops, and it has significant fishing and tourism, centred on the port and resort city of Mazatlán.

History

Before Spanish colonisation the region was inhabited by indigenous peoples including the Cahita. Sinaloa became a state in 1830. In the modern era the state has also become widely associated in public discussion with the Sinaloa Cartel, an organised-crime group named after it.
