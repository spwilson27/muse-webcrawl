Ronald Ulysses "Ron" Swanson is a fictional character portrayed by Nick Offerman in the NBC comedy series Parks and Recreation (2009–2015), created by Greg Daniels and Michael Schur. He is the director of the Parks and Recreation Department of the fictional town of Pawnee, Indiana, and the superior and counterpoint of the show's protagonist, Leslie Knope.

Character

Ron is a staunch libertarian who believes in minimal government and works, paradoxically, to make his own department as ineffective as possible. He is famously deadpan, private and masculine in a deadpan, old-fashioned style: he loves meat, woodworking, hunting, whisky (particularly Lagavulin), breakfast food and the music of Willie Nelson, and despises his two ex-wives, both named Tammy. His woodworking skills reflect Offerman's own off-screen craft as a woodworker.

Storylines

Across the series Ron reluctantly becomes a mentor and friend to his staff, especially Leslie and April Ludgate. Running gags include his alter ego "Duke Silver", a smooth-jazz saxophonist he plays in secret; his extreme secrecy (including a gold hoard and undisclosed birthday); and his horror of his ex-wife Tammy II (played by Megan Mullally, Offerman's real-life wife), a librarian whose appearances send him into panic. In later seasons he marries Diane Lewis, becomes a father, leaves government to found the Very Good Building Company, and in the finale becomes superintendent of the Pawnee National Park.

Reception

Ron Swanson became the show's breakout character and a popular-culture icon; his face and quotations ("Never half-ass two things. Whole-ass one thing") spread widely as internet memes, and Offerman's performance was widely praised by critics.
