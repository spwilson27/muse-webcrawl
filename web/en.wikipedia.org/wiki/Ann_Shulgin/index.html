Laura Ann Shulgin (née Gotlieb; 22 March 1931 – 9 July 2022) was a New Zealand-born American author and therapist. With her husband, the chemist Alexander "Sasha" Shulgin, she co-authored the books PiHKAL (1991) and TiHKAL (1997), semi-autobiographical works documenting the synthesis and subjective effects of phenethylamines and tryptamines.

Work

Ann Shulgin contributed the experiential and narrative portions of the books, developing "shamanic" approaches to psychedelic experience and working as a lay therapist with MDMA before its prohibition in 1985. She lectured widely on the psychological and spiritual dimensions of psychedelic use, the role of the "shadow," and the importance of set and setting, and was a founding figure in the modern psychedelic community.
