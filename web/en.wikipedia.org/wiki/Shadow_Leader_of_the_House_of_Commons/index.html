Shadow Leader of the House of Commons

The Shadow Leader of the House of Commons is a member of the Official Opposition Shadow Cabinet in the United Kingdom, responsible for shadowing the Leader of the House of Commons. The post-holder manages the Opposition's business strategy in the Commons and represents the Shadow Cabinet on matters of parliamentary procedure and the scheduling of business.

Role

The Shadow Leader questions the Leader of the House at the weekly Business Questions, pressing the government on its forthcoming programme and demanding debates on issues of opposition concern. The role involves negotiating through the "usual channels" over the timing of opposition days, private members' business and the handling of controversial legislation. The post-holder also typically leads the opposition response to procedural reforms and to reports of the Procedure Committee and the Committee on Standards.

Office holders

The office has been held by senior opposition figures, often former cabinet ministers. Holders in recent decades have included Margaret Beckett, Robin Cook (before becoming Foreign Secretary in government), Eric Forth, Oliver Heald, Theresa May (in opposition), Chris Grayling, Harriet Harman, Angela Eagle, Valerie Vaz, Thangam Debbonaire and Lucy Powell, the last serving until Labour's return to government in 2024, when the post passed to the Conservative opposition front bench.
