AFI's 100 Years...100 Movies is a list of the 100 best American films, as determined by the American Film Institute from a poll of artists and leaders in the film industry. The list was first unveiled in a 1998 CBS television special and was the first of the AFI 100 Years... series of lists celebrating the centennial of American cinema.

The list

In the original 1998 list, Citizen Kane (1941) ranked first, followed by Casablanca (1942) and The Godfather (1972). A tenth-anniversary edition aired in 2007; Citizen Kane retained the top spot, while The Godfather rose to second and Casablanca fell to third. Films were judged on criteria including critical recognition, historical significance, and cultural impact.
