Duke Farms

Duke Farms is a 2,700-acre (1,100 ha) estate in Hillsborough Township, Somerset County, New Jersey, United States. The property was created by tobacco and electric power magnate James Buchanan Duke and further developed by his daughter, Doris Duke. Today it is managed by the Duke Farms Foundation as a model of environmental stewardship, conservation and agroecology, and is open to the public.

History

Beginning in 1899, James Buchanan Duke purchased farms along the Raritan River and transformed the land into an estate of lakes, waterfalls, meadows and woodlands, with over 45 miles (72 km) of roads and more than 2 million trees planted. After his death in 1925, his daughter Doris Duke continued work on the property, creating display gardens including a conservatory modelled on those of Longwood Gardens.

Environmental centre

The Duke Farms Foundation opened the estate to the public in 2012. The site includes the Farm Barn Orientation Center, an orchid range in a geothermal greenhouse, habitats for grassland birds, and extensive trails. The foundation's work focuses on habitat restoration, sustainable agriculture research and environmental education.
