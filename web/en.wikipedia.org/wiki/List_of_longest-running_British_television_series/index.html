List of longest-running British television series

Longest-running
Panorama, first broadcast in 1953, is the world's longest-running television news magazine.

This is a list of the longest-running television programmes broadcast in the United Kingdom, ordered by years on air. It includes programmes still in production as well as long-running completed series.
