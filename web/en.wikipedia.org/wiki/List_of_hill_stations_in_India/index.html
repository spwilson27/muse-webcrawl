List of hill stations in India

Hill stations are towns at higher elevations than the surrounding plains, originally developed in British India as retreats from the summer heat and as sanatoria — notably Shimla, the summer capital of British India. India has several hundred hill stations across the Himalaya, the Western and Eastern Ghats, the Nilgiri and Aravalli ranges and the hills of the northeast. Many remain major tourist destinations; several are served by the UNESCO-listed mountain railways (Darjeeling, Kalka–Shimla and Nilgiri lines).
