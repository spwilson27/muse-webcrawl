V. P. Singh

Vishwanath Pratap Singh
Born: 25 June 1931, Allahabad
Died: 27 November 2008, New Delhi
Office: 7th Prime Minister of India (1989–1990)

Vishwanath Pratap Singh (25 June 1931 – 27 November 2008), often shortened to V. P. Singh, was an Indian politician who served as the seventh Prime Minister of India from 1989 to 1990. A scion of the royal family of Manda in Uttar Pradesh, he entered politics with the Indian National Congress and rose through the ranks of the Uttar Pradesh state government before moving to national office.

Early life and career

Singh was born in Allahabad and educated at Allahabad University and Fergusson College, Pune. He was adopted into the ruling family of the Manda estate and became its raja. He first entered the Uttar Pradesh Legislative Assembly in 1969 as a Congress candidate, later serving as the state's Chief Minister from 1980 to 1982, during which he campaigned against dacoity in the Chambal region.

Finance and defence minister

Under Prime Minister Rajiv Gandhi, Singh served as Finance Minister (1985–1987), where his raids on tax evaders and pursuit of investigations such as the Bofors scandal made him both popular and controversial. Transferred to the Defence Ministry, his inquiries into defence procurement deepened the rift with Gandhi, and he was expelled from the Congress party in 1987.

Prime ministership

Singh founded the Janata Dal and led the National Front coalition to victory in the 1989 general election, becoming Prime Minister on 2 December 1989. His government's most consequential act was implementing the recommendations of the Mandal Commission, reserving 27 per cent of central government jobs for Other Backward Classes — a decision that triggered widespread protests but permanently reshaped Indian politics. His government fell in November 1990 after the Bharatiya Janata Party withdrew support over the Ram Janmabhoomi agitation.

Later life

Singh remained a respected elder statesman, active in anti-corruption and social justice movements. He died in New Delhi on 27 November 2008 after a long illness.
