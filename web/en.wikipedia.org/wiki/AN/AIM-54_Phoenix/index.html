AIM-54 Phoenix

AIM-54 Phoenix
Designation (Joint Electronics Type Designation System): AN/AIM-54
Type: Long-range air-to-air missile
In service: 1974–2004 (US Navy)
Range: over 100 nmi (190 km)

The AIM-54 Phoenix is an American radar-guided, long-range air-to-air missile developed for the Grumman F-14 Tomcat fleet air defence fighter. Its designation under the Joint Electronics Type Designation System is written AN/AIM-54. Carried in clusters of up to six, it was the United States' only long-range air-to-air missile for decades.

Guidance

The Phoenix uses semi-active radar homing in midcourse with track-while-scan guidance from the F-14's AWG-9 radar — which could engage six targets simultaneously — switching to active radar homing for the terminal phase. It flies a lofted trajectory to ranges beyond 100 nautical miles at speeds above Mach 5.

Service

The US Navy retired the Phoenix in 2004 ahead of the F-14's own retirement in 2006; Iran, which received the missile before 1979, was its only export operator and used it in the Iran–Iraq War. See also F-14 Tomcat.
