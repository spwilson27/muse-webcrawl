The Grumman F-14 Tomcat is an American carrier-based, twin-engine, two-seat, variable-sweep wing fighter aircraft. This page is filed under the AN/ designation path (AN/Grumman F-14 Tomcat) used by some designation listings for the type.

Design

The F-14 was developed for the US Navy's fleet air defence mission to replace the F-4 Phantom II. It combined the AWG-9 radar and AIM-54 Phoenix missile — able to engage six targets at once — with variable-geometry wings that swept from 20° to 68° for both low-speed carrier approaches and high-speed interception.

Service

Entering service in 1974, the Tomcat flew combat air patrols, reconnaissance (TARPS) and, later, precision strike missions. It was retired by the US Navy in 2006 and replaced by the F/A-18E/F Super Hornet; Iran remains the sole operator of the type. The aircraft achieved wide cultural fame through the film Top Gun.
