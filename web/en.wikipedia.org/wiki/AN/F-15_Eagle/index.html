The McDonnell Douglas F-15 Eagle is an American twin-engine, all-weather tactical fighter aircraft designed by McDonnell Douglas (now part of Boeing). This page sits under the AN/ designation path (AN/F-15 Eagle) used by some joint electronics designation listings to reference the aircraft type.

Overview

First flown in 1972 and entering service in 1976, the F-15 was designed as a dedicated air superiority fighter with a thrust-to-weight ratio greater than one, a large wing for manoeuvrability, and a powerful pulse-Doppler radar able to look down and shoot down targets over cluttered terrain.

Record and variants

The Eagle has a combined record of more than 100 aerial victories with no losses in air-to-air combat, earned in Israeli, American and Saudi service. Variants include the two-seat F-15E Strike Eagle strike fighter and the modernised F-15EX Eagle II. See also F-15E Strike Eagle.
