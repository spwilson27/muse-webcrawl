MIL-STD-196

MIL-STD-196
Title: Joint Electronics Type Designation System (JETDS)
Issuing authority: United States Department of Defense
System name: "AN/" designations

MIL-STD-196 is a United States military standard that defines the Joint Electronics Type Designation System (JETDS), the system of "AN/" designations used to name United States military electronics equipment.

The designation system

A JETDS designation begins with "AN/", followed by three letters and a number, such as AN/APG-77. The letters encode, in order, where the equipment is installed (for example A for piloted aircraft, S for water surface craft), the type of equipment (P for radar, R for radio), and its purpose (G for fire control, Q for special or combination purposes). The number identifies the specific model within that series.

Background

The system descends from the Joint Army–Navy (JAN) nomenclature introduced during the Second World War; the "AN" prefix originally meant "Army/Navy". MIL-STD-196, issued in successive revisions, governs how new designations are assigned across the US armed services so that equipment names are unique and descriptive.
