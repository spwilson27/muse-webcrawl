HMS Goliath (1781)

HMS Goliath was a 74-gun third-rate ship of the line of the Royal Navy, launched on 19 October 1781 at Deptford Dockyard. She was built to the Arrogant-class design.

Service

Goliath fought at the Battle of the Saintes in 1782 and, most famously, at the Battle of the Nile in 1798, where she was among the first British ships to engage the French fleet in Aboukir Bay. She also served during the French Revolutionary and Napoleonic Wars before being broken up in 1815.
