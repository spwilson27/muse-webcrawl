List of ER episodes

ER is an American medical drama television series created by Michael Crichton that aired on NBC from September 19, 1994, to April 2, 2009, with a total of 331 episodes across 15 seasons. Set in the emergency room of the fictional County General Hospital in Chicago, the series starred an ensemble cast including Anthony Edwards, George Clooney, Julianna Margulies, Eriq La Salle, Noah Wyle, and Sherry Stringfield in its early years. This article lists the episodes by season.

Notable episodes

Notable episodes include the pilot "24 Hours" (1994), the live episode "Ambush" (1997), "Love's Labor Lost" (1995) — frequently cited among the greatest television episodes — and the series finale "And in the End..." (2009), which revisited the show's opening day format.
