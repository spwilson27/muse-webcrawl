Asda Stores Ltd.
Industry: Retail
Founded: 19 February 1949, Wakefield
Headquarters: Leeds, England
Products: Groceries, general merchandise

Asda Stores Ltd., trading as Asda, is a British supermarket chain headquartered in Leeds, West Yorkshire. The company was founded in 1949 when the Asquith family merged their retail business with the Associated Dairies company of Yorkshire. It expanded into the south of England during the 1970s and 1980s.

History

Asda was the UK's second-largest supermarket chain by market share for many years. In 1999 it was purchased by the American retailer Walmart for £6.7 billion. In 2020 Walmart agreed to sell a majority stake to the Issa brothers and TDR Capital, a deal completed in 2021 valuing Asda at £6.8 billion.

Operations

The company operates superstores, supermarkets and smaller Asda Express convenience stores, along with petrol filling stations and the George clothing brand, one of the largest clothing retailers in the UK by volume.

Branding

Asda is known for its "Asda Price" slogan and the promotional image of staff patting their back pockets. Its green livery is a longstanding feature of its stores.
