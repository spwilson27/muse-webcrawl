[Image: A carved dharmachakra (wheel of dharma) with eight spokes on a temple pillar]

Dharma (Sanskrit; Pali: dhamma) is a key concept of Indian religions with no single English equivalent. In Hinduism it is the cosmic law and the duty, righteousness and proper conduct that uphold it — varying by caste, stage of life and circumstance, and exemplified in the epics by figures torn between conflicting dharmas. In Buddhism, 'the Dharma' is the Buddha's teaching itself, one of the Three Jewels of refuge, and dharmas in Abhidharma philosophy are the irreducible constituents of experience.

Range

Jain and Sikh uses extend the range (dharma as the medium of motion in Jain cosmology; righteous duty in Sikh ethics). The wheel of dharma (dharmachakra) symbolises the teaching's turning and stands at the centre of India's national flag, adopted from Ashoka's pillars — a reminder that dharma once named a political ideal too.
