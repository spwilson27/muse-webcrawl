RAF St Athan

Image placeholder: MOD St Athan in the Vale of Glamorgan, Wales.

Royal Air Force St Athan, commonly abbreviated RAF St Athan, is a large Ministry of Defence unit near the village of St Athan in the Vale of Glamorgan, Wales. Opened in 1939, it was for much of its history the RAF's principal aircraft maintenance station, home to maintenance units and schools of technical training.

History

The station opened shortly before the Second World War and grew into the largest RAF station in terms of personnel, with major engineering and training functions, including the No. 4 School of Technical Training. In later decades it hosted the Defence Aviation Repair Agency (DARA) "super-hangar" for fast-jet maintenance and No. 71 (Inspection and Repair) Squadron, which provides forward repair in operational theatres.

Current use

Following defence restructuring, the site became known as MOD St Athan and now hosts units including No. 4 School of Technical Training (aircraft engineering training) and elements of 71 Squadron, while adjoining land has been developed as an aerospace business park and the neighbouring Bro Tathan enterprise zone. The airfield is also used by civil aviation-related businesses and by the South Wales Aviation Museum.
