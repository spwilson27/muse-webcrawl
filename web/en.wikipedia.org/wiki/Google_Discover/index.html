Google Discover

[Image: Google Discover]

Google Discover is a mobile feed developed by Google that proactively surfaces content — including articles, videos, and other web material — tailored to a user's interests, based on their search history, app activity, and location. It appears on the Google homepage in the Google app and on many Android devices.

History

Discover evolved from Google Feed, which was introduced in December 2016 as an evolution of Google Now. In September 2018 Google renamed the service Google Discover and redesigned it with a greater emphasis on visuals and topics that remain relevant over time.

Features

Users can follow topics, control how much of a given subject they see, and view content in multiple languages. Publishers' content can appear in Discover without any special action, and performance is reported in Google Search Console. Unlike search, Discover does not respond to a query; it predicts what a user may find interesting.
