Mercer (consulting firm)

Mercer is one of the world's largest human-resources and investment consulting firms.

Mercer is an American global human-resources and financial-services consulting firm headquartered in New York City. A subsidiary of Marsh McLennan, it is among the world's largest HR consulting firms, advising clients in more than 130 countries on health and benefits, workforce strategy, pensions and investments, with over 20,000 employees worldwide.

History

The firm began in Canada in 1945 as William M. Mercer Limited, an actuarial and employee-benefits practice founded by William Manson Mercer. Marsh & McLennan acquired it in 1959, and it grew through the twentieth century by absorbing pension and investment consultancies internationally, including the 1997 merger with the US firm A. Foster Higgins & Co. Mercer is one of the "Big Four" businesses of Marsh McLennan alongside Marsh, Guy Carpenter and Oliver Wyman.

Services

Mercer's business is organised around three areas: Health (employee health and benefits design and brokerage advice), Wealth (retirement and pension consulting, investment advisory and outsourced chief investment officer services, managing delegated assets for pension funds), and Career (workforce planning, talent strategy, compensation data and rewards consulting). The firm publishes widely used research, including the annual Quality of Living city ranking, cost-of-living surveys for expatriate assignments, the Mercer CFA Institute Global Pension Index, and remuneration surveys.
