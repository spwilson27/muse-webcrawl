David Allen (author)

David Allen (born 28 December 1945) is an American author and productivity consultant best known for creating the Getting Things Done (GTD) time-management method, described in his 2001 book of the same name.


Born: 28 December 1945, Shreveport, Louisiana, US
Known for: Getting Things Done (GTD)

Career

Allen worked in diverse jobs before becoming a management consultant and executive coach. His decades of consulting distilled into GTD: capture everything into a trusted system, clarify next actions, organise, reflect weekly, and engage — achieving what he calls "mind like water".

Works

Books include Getting Things Done (2001), Ready for Anything (2003), Making It All Work (2008), and a revised GTD edition (2015). The method spawned a large ecosystem of apps, training, and communities, and influenced personal-productivity culture worldwide.
