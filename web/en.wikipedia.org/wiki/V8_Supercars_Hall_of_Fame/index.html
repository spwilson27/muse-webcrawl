V8 Supercars Hall of Fame

The V8 Supercars Hall of Fame honours drivers, team figures and other contributors who have made an outstanding contribution to Australian touring car racing and to the Supercars Championship (formerly the V8 Supercars Championship and Australian Touring Car Championship). Inductees are announced periodically, often in connection with major events such as the Bathurst 1000.

Inductees

The inaugural inductions in 1999 recognised foundational figures of Australian touring car racing. Early inductees included multiple championship and Bathurst winners such as Peter Brock, Allan Moffat, Dick Johnson, Jim Richards and Mark Skaife, alongside pioneers of the sport's earlier eras. Subsequent inductions have added later champions and significant non-driving contributors, including team owners, engineers and administrators who shaped the category's growth into a professional, nationally televised series.

Notable inductees across the years include:

  • Peter Brock — nine-time Bathurst 1000 winner
  • Allan Moffat — four-time Australian Touring Car champion
  • Dick Johnson — five-time champion and team owner
  • Jim Richards — seven-time Bathurst winner
  • Mark Skaife — five-time champion
  • Craig Lowndes — three-time champion and multiple Bathurst winner
