Law Enforcement Support Office

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Law Enforcement Support Office (LESO) is a division of the United States Department of Defense's Defense Logistics Agency (DLA) that administers the transfer of surplus military equipment to civilian law enforcement agencies. The transfers are commonly known as the 1033 Program, after Section 1033 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 1997, which authorised the Secretary of Defense to transfer excess Department of Defense property to federal, state, and local law enforcement agencies.

History

The program traces its origins to the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Years 1990 and 1991, whose Section 1208 allowed transfers of surplus equipment for counterdrug activities. The 1997 act broadened the authority to law enforcement purposes generally, with preference for counterdrug and counterterrorism activities. LESO, headquartered within DLA Disposition Services, coordinates enrollment of participating agencies, allocation of property, and compliance oversight.

Equipment

Transferred property ranges from office equipment, clothing, and generators to armoured vehicles such as the MRAP, helicopters, and rifles. Since the program's expansion, billions of dollars' worth of equipment has been transferred to thousands of agencies. Certain "controlled" items, including weapons and armoured vehicles, remain federal property on permanent loan and must be accounted for annually.

Criticism and restrictions

The program drew national attention after the 2014 protests in Ferguson, Missouri, where heavily armoured police vehicles were widely photographed. In 2015, an executive order prohibited the transfer of certain equipment, including tracked armoured vehicles, weaponised aircraft, and bayonets; those restrictions were rescinded in 2017 and partially reinstated in modified form in 2022. Critics argue the program contributes to the militarisation of police, while supporters say it provides needed equipment at little cost to taxpayers.
