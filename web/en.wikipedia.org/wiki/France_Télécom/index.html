France Télécom

France Télécom was the main telecommunications company in France, formed in 1988 from the state postal and telecommunications administration (PTT) and becoming an autonomous operator in 1991. It was partially privatised from 1997–98, acquired the mobile operator Orange in 2000, and progressively adopted the Orange name for its services. On 1 July 2013, France Télécom itself was renamed Orange S.A.

History

French state telecommunications long operated within the PTT (Postes, Télégraphes et Téléphones). Under the Direction Générale des Télécommunications, France built the Minitel videotex system (launched 1982), a pioneering online service. France Télécom was created as a separate public operator in 1988, became a société anonyme in 1996, and was floated in 1997. After acquiring Orange plc in 2000 it unified its mobile activities under the Orange brand. A wave of employee suicides in 2008–2009 led to a major public scandal and, in 2019, a court judgment finding the company and former executives guilty of institutional moral harassment related to restructuring. The group took the Orange name in 2013.
