List of One Day International cricket hat-tricks

A hat-trick in cricket occurs when a bowler takes three wickets from three consecutive deliveries. Hat-tricks are rare in One Day Internationals: the first was taken by Pakistan's Jalal-ud-Din in 1982, and Sri Lanka's Lasith Malinga remains the only bowler to have taken three ODI hat-tricks, including four wickets in four balls against South Africa at the 2007 World Cup.
