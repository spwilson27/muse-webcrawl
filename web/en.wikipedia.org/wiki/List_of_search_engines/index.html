Historical search engines

Early web search engines included Archie (1990, for FTP archives), W3Catalog (1993), WebCrawler, Lycos and Infoseek (1994), AltaVista and Excite (1995), and Ask Jeeves (1996). Most were displaced by Google in the early 2000s.

Metasearch engines

Metasearch engines such as Dogpile, MetaCrawler and SearXNG aggregate results from multiple engines rather than crawling the web themselves.
