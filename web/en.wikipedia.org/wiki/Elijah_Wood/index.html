Elijah Wood

Born: January 28, 1981, Cedar Rapids, Iowa, U.S.
Occupation: Actor, producer
Years active: 1988–present

Elijah Jordan Wood (born January 28, 1981) is an American actor and producer. He made his film debut with a minor part in Back to the Future Part II (1989) and achieved recognition as a child actor with roles in films such as Avalon (1990), Paradise (1991), Radio Flyer (1992), The Good Son (1993), North (1994), and Flipper (1996).

Career

Wood is best known for portraying Frodo Baggins in Peter Jackson's The Lord of the Rings film trilogy (2001–2003) and reprising the role in The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey (2012). His later film work includes Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind (2004), Sin City (2005), Green Street (2005), and Everything Is Illuminated (2005).

On television, Wood starred as Ryan Newman in the FX comedy Wilfred (2011–2014) and as Todd Brotzman in Dirk Gently's Holistic Detective Agency (2016–2017). He has also done extensive voice work, including Mumble in Happy Feet (2006) and Wirt in the animated miniseries Over the Garden Wall (2014).

Production work

In 2010, Wood co-founded the production company SpectreVision (originally The Woodshed), which produces horror and genre films such as A Girl Walks Home Alone at Night (2014) and Mandy (2018).
