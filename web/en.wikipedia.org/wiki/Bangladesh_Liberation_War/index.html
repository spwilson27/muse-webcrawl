The Bangladesh Liberation War (Bengali: মুক্তিযুদ্ধ, Muktijuddho) was a revolution and armed conflict in 1971 that led to the independence of Bangladesh from Pakistan. It began on 26 March 1971, when the Pakistani military launched Operation Searchlight against Bengali nationalists in East Pakistan, and ended on 16 December 1971 with the surrender of Pakistani forces in Dhaka — now celebrated as Victory Day in Bangladesh.

Background

Since partition in 1947, East Pakistan (East Bengal) had been politically marginalized despite its majority population: the capital, the military, and economic investment were concentrated in West Pakistan, and the attempt to impose Urdu as the sole state language sparked the Bengali Language Movement of 1952. In the general election of December 1970, Sheikh Mujibur Rahman's Awami League won an outright majority of seats in the National Assembly on a program of regional autonomy (the Six Points), but Pakistan's military rulers refused to transfer power.

The war

On the night of 25 March 1971 the army cracked down in Dhaka, arresting Mujib and attacking the university and Hindu neighborhoods. The ensuing nine months saw mass atrocities against Bengali civilians — estimates of the death toll range from several hundred thousand to three million — and some ten million refugees fled into India. Bengali soldiers and guerrillas of the Mukti Bahini fought a resistance war with Indian training and support. After Pakistani air strikes on Indian airfields on 3 December, India intervened openly; its forces, advancing with the Mukti Bahini, compelled the Pakistani eastern command to surrender at Dhaka on 16 December 1971.

Aftermath

Bangladesh became an independent republic; Mujib, released from prison in Pakistan, returned as its leader. The war produced one of the largest refugee crises of the century and remains central to Bangladeshi national identity; the extent and characterization of the 1971 killings as genocide continues to be debated internationally. The conflict also completed the breakup of the Pakistan created by Muhammad Ali Jinnah's movement a generation earlier.
