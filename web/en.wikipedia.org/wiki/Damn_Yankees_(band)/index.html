Damn Yankees was an American rock supergroup formed in 1989, consisting of Tommy Shaw of Styx (vocals and guitar), Jack Blades of Night Ranger (vocals and bass), Ted Nugent (guitar and vocals), and Michael Cartellone (drums).

History

The group's self-titled debut album, Damn Yankees (1990), went double platinum in the United States, driven by the power ballad "High Enough" — a number 3 hit on the Billboard Hot 100 — along with "Coming of Age" and "Come Again". Their second album, Don't Tread (1992), produced the hit "Where You Goin' Now" but sold less well as grunge reshaped rock radio.

A third album, often referred to by fans as The Third Album, was recorded in the late 1990s but never officially released, though tracks circulated. The members returned to their primary bands — Shaw to Styx, Blades to Night Ranger, and Nugent to his solo career — while Cartellone later joined Lynyrd Skynyrd. The four have reunited sporadically for performances over the years.
