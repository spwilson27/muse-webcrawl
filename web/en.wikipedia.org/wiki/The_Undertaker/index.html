The Undertaker

Mark William Calaway (born March 24, 1965), better known by his ring name The Undertaker, is a retired American professional wrestler. Widely regarded as one of the greatest professional wrestlers of all time, he performed for WWE for three decades, from 1990 until his retirement in 2020.

Career

Calaway began his career in 1987, wrestling in World Class Championship Wrestling and the Continental Wrestling Association, and briefly in WCW as "Mean" Mark Callous, before debuting in the WWF at Survivor Series in November 1990 as The Undertaker — a macabre, undead mortician character accompanied by manager Paul Bearer. The persona evolved through phases including the supernatural "Deadman," the biker "American Bad Ass" (2000–2003), and back to a hybrid Deadman.

The Streak and legacy

The Undertaker is famed for The Streak: 21 consecutive victories at WrestleMania, WWE's flagship event, from 1991 to 2013, ended by Brock Lesnar at WrestleMania XXX in 2014. A multiple-time world champion, he headlined numerous WrestleManias, won the 2007 Royal Rumble, and was known for signature matches such as Hell in a Cell and the casket match. His "Final Farewell" took place at Survivor Series 2020, thirty years after his debut, and he was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2022. His last match was the cinematic "Boneyard Match" against AJ Styles at WrestleMania 36.
