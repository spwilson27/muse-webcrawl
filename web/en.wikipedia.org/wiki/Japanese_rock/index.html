Japanese rock

Japanese rock (日本のロック), often abbreviated J-rock, is rock music originating in Japan, sung (usually) in Japanese and spanning folk rock, new wave, punk, heavy metal and the theatrical visual kei movement.

History

Japanese rock emerged in the 1960s with Group Sounds bands copying Western rock and pop. The folk-rock band Happy End proved Japanese-language rock viable in the early 1970s. The 1980s boogied with hard rock and metal acts, and gave rise to visual kei through bands like X Japan, Buck-Tick and Luna Sea.

1990s onwards

The 1990s "Being" boom and indie scene produced acts like B'z, Mr. Children and Spitz, while alternative bands such as Number Girl influenced a generation. In the 2000s, anime tie-ins internationalised J-rock audiences, and acts like One Ok Rock and Babymetal toured globally.
