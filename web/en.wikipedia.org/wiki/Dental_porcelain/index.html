Dental porcelain

Dental porcelain is a ceramic material used in dentistry to fabricate crowns, veneers, inlays and bridges. Valued for its lifelike translucency and stain resistance, it is made primarily from feldspathic glass containing silica and alumina, fired at high temperatures.

Uses

Porcelain can be fused to a metal substructure (porcelain-fused-to-metal crowns) or used all-ceramically, increasingly with high-strength cores such as lithium disilicate and zirconia for better aesthetics and durability.
