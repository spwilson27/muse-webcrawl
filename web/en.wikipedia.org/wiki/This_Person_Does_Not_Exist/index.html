This Person Does Not Exist

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

A StyleGAN-generated face of a person who does not exist

This Person Does Not Exist is a website, launched in February 2019 by software engineer Phillip Wang, that displays a photorealistic portrait of a nonexistent person each time it is refreshed. The images are produced by StyleGAN, a generative adversarial network developed by researchers at Nvidia, trained on a large dataset of real portraits.

Impact

The site drew widespread attention as a demonstration of how far generative image synthesis had come and fuelled public debate about deepfakes, fake social-media profiles, and the reliability of photographic evidence. Imitator sites followed for generated cats, artworks, rental properties, and other subjects. Tell-tale artefacts in early GAN faces — asymmetric earrings, smeared backgrounds, distorted teeth — became popular guides for spotting synthetic portraits, although later models greatly reduced such flaws.
