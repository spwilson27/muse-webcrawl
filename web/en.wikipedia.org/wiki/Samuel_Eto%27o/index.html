Samuel Eto'o Fils (born 10 March 1981) is a Cameroonian former professional footballer who played as a striker, widely regarded as one of the greatest African players of all time. Since December 2021 he has served as president of the Cameroonian Football Federation (FECAFOOT).

Club career

After early years at Real Madrid and Mallorca, Eto'o starred at Barcelona (2004–2009), scoring over 100 goals and winning two UEFA Champions League titles. He then moved to Inter Milan, where in 2010 he became the only player to win consecutive European trebles with two different clubs. Later spells included Anzhi Makhachkala, Chelsea, Everton, Sampdoria and clubs in Turkey and Qatar.

International career

Eto'o is Cameroon's all-time top scorer, won the Africa Cup of Nations in 2000 and 2002, and claimed Olympic gold at Sydney 2000. He was named African Player of the Year four times.
