Nacional

Nacional is a Spanish and Portuguese word meaning "national". It appears in the names of numerous institutions, sports clubs, companies and places across the Spanish- and Portuguese-speaking world. This article lists some of the most notable uses.

Gran Parque Central, home of Club Nacional de Football, Montevideo.

Sports clubs

  • Club Nacional de Football — a Uruguayan sports club founded in 1899 in Montevideo, one of the two giants of Uruguayan football alongside Peñarol. Nacional has won three Copa Libertadores titles (1971, 1980, 1988) and three Intercontinental Cups, and plays at the Gran Parque Central, a venue of the first World Cup in 1930.
  • Clube Desportivo Nacional — a Portuguese football club from Funchal, Madeira, founded in 1910, long a regular member of the Primeira Liga.
  • Club Nacional — a Paraguayan football club from Asunción, founded in 1904, Copa Libertadores finalist in 2014.
  • Atlético Nacional — a Colombian football club from Medellín, founded in 1947, two-time Copa Libertadores champion (1989, 2016).
  • Club Deportivo El Nacional — an Ecuadorian football club from Quito, historically the team of the Ecuadorian armed forces.
  • Nacional Potosí — a Bolivian football club from Potosí.
  • Amazonas Futebol Clube aside, other "Nacional" clubs include Nacional Fast Clube and Nacional Futebol Clube of Manaus, Brazil.

Other uses

  • Nacional (cacao) — a prized variety of fine-flavour cacao beans from Ecuador, also called Arriba cacao.
  • Banco Nacional and similarly named national banks in several countries.
  • Nacional media — newspapers and broadcasters, e.g., Radio Nacional in Spain and Argentina.
  • As a common noun, nacional denominates "domestic" products or citizens ("nationals") in legal and commercial language.
