The Comcast Center is a skyscraper in Center City Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Completed in 2008, the 58-storey, 297-metre (974 ft) tower was the tallest building in Philadelphia until the completion of the Comcast Technology Center in 2018. It serves as the headquarters of the media company Comcast.

Design

Designed by Robert A. M. Stern Architects, the tower has an obelisk-like form with a glass curtain wall and a large winter garden at its base. It was among the tallest buildings in the United States to achieve LEED Gold certification at the time of its completion.

Features

The lobby contains The Comcast Experience, a large high-definition LED video wall, and the restaurant and public spaces at its base connect to the Suburban Station concourse below.
