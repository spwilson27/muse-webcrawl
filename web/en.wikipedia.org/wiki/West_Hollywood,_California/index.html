West Hollywood is a city in Los Angeles County, California, United States. Incorporated in 1984, it lies directly between the cities of Los Angeles and Beverly Hills and is one of the most densely populated and walkable cities in California. It is widely known by its nickname "WeHo".

Character and culture

West Hollywood is home to the Sunset Strip, famous for its music venues, nightclubs, hotels and giant billboards, and to a celebrated design district and restaurant scene. The city has long been a centre of LGBTQ life in Southern California: its population includes a large gay community, and it hosts one of the largest annual Halloween street parties in the United States, as well as major Pride celebrations. It is also home to a significant Russian-speaking immigrant community.

Government

Since incorporation, the city's progressive council has enacted rent control, some of the earliest domestic-partnership and animal-protection laws in the United States, and in 1985 it became one of the first cities to create a same-sex domestic partnership registry.
