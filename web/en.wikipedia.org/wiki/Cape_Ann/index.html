Cape Ann

Cape Ann is a rocky cape on the Atlantic coast of northeastern Massachusetts, about 30 miles northeast of Boston. It comprises the city of Gloucester and the towns of Rockport, Essex and Manchester-by-the-Sea.

Location: Essex County, Massachusetts
Communities: Gloucester, Rockport, Essex, Manchester-by-the-Sea

History

Named by Captain John Smith for his patroness Anne of Denmark, Cape Ann became an important fishing and granite-quarrying centre and a noted artists' colony, especially in Rockport.

Tourism

Visitors come for beaches, seafood, whale watching and historic lighthouses such as Thacher Island's twin lights.
