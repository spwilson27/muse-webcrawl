The Estadio Akron, formerly known as Estadio Omnilife and Estadio Chivas, is a football stadium in Zapopan, in the Guadalajara metropolitan area of Jalisco, Mexico. It is the home of Liga MX club C.D. Guadalajara, popularly known as Chivas.

Design and opening

Designed by the French architects Jean-Marie Massaud and Daniel Pouzet, the stadium was conceived as an artificial volcano: a grass-covered cone rising from the landscape, with the pitch set inside. It opened on 30 July 2010 with a friendly between Chivas and Manchester United, in which Javier "Chicharito" Hernández — newly transferred from Chivas to United — scored the first goal at the new ground.

Events

The stadium hosted matches of the 2011 FIFA U-17 World Cup and ceremonies of the 2011 Pan American Games. It is one of the Mexican venues selected for the 2026 FIFA World Cup, during which it is referred to as "Estadio Guadalajara" under FIFA naming rules.
