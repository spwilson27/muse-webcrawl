Mohamed al-Menfi

Mohamed Yunus al-Menfi (born 3 March 1976) is a Libyan politician and diplomat who has served as chairman of the Presidential Council of Libya since March 2021, in which capacity he is Libya's head of state. He was selected for the role by the Libyan Political Dialogue Forum as part of a UN-backed interim unity government intended to lead the country to elections.

Early life and career

Al-Menfi was born in Tobruk in eastern Libya. He pursued doctoral studies in France and entered the diplomatic service, eventually serving as Libya's ambassador to Greece. His posting in Athens ended abruptly in December 2019 when he was expelled amid the diplomatic dispute that followed the signing of a maritime memorandum between Libya's Tripoli-based government and Turkey.

Presidential Council

In February 2021, the Libyan Political Dialogue Forum, convened under UN auspices in Geneva, elected a joint ticket headed by al-Menfi as chairman of a restructured three-member Presidential Council, with Abdul Hamid Dbeibah as prime minister of a Government of National Unity. The interim authorities took office in Tripoli in March 2021, unifying institutions divided since 2014. Elections planned for December 2021 were postponed indefinitely, and al-Menfi remained in post as rival administrations contested legitimacy.
