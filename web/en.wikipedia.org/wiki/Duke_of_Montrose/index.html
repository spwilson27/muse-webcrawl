Duke of Montrose is a title in the Peerage of Scotland, held by the Graham family. It was first created in 1488 for David Lindsay, 5th Earl of Crawford, but was forfeited within months. The Graham dukedom dates from 1707, when James Graham, 4th Marquess of Montrose, was elevated for his support of the Union.

History

The family's most famous figure is James Graham, 1st Marquess of Montrose, the Royalist general of the Civil War in Scotland, executed in 1650. The 2nd Duke was a prominent politician; the 3rd Duke obtained the repeal of the Act proscribing Highland dress in 1782. The 6th Duke founded De Beers Consolidated Mines in Rhodesia-adjacent ventures and served in government in Southern Rhodesia. The 7th Duke was a minister in the Rhodesian government.

The current holder is James Graham, 8th Duke of Montrose. The heir apparent is James Alexander Norman Graham, Marquess of Graham.
