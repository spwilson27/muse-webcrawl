Paris 2024 Paralympic Games

The Paris 2024 Paralympic Games, officially the XVII Paralympic Games, were an international multi-sport event for athletes with disabilities held in Paris, France, from 28 August to 8 September 2024, shortly after the 2024 Summer Olympics. It was the first Summer Paralympics hosted by Paris.

Overview

Around 4,400 athletes from 168 delegations competed in 549 medal events across 22 sports, including para athletics, para swimming, wheelchair basketball, sitting volleyball, blind football and para cycling. The opening ceremony, the first Paralympic opening held outside a stadium, took place on the Place de la Concorde and the Champs-Élysées.

Medal table

China topped the medal table, followed by Great Britain and the United States. Host nation France finished in the top ten, its best result in decades.

Legacy

The Games were praised for strong ticket sales, accessible venues and increased visibility for para sport in France.
