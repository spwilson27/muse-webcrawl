800 metres world record progression

The following table shows the world record progression in the men's and women's 800 metres, as ratified by World Athletics (formerly the IAAF). The 800 metres is a middle-distance running event contested at the Olympic Games since 1896 for men and, after an early appearance in 1928, continuously for women since 1960.

Men

The first men's world record recognized by the IAAF was set by Ted Meredith of the United States in 1912. The record stood for long periods in the hands of Rudolf Harbig, Sebastian Coe, and Wilson Kipketer. At the 2012 London Olympics, Kenya's David Rudisha ran 1:40.91 in the final, becoming the first man to break the 1:41 barrier; that mark remains the current men's world record.

Women

The women's record was first ratified in 1928. The current record of 1:53.28 was set by Jarmila Kratochvílová of Czechoslovakia in Munich in 1983 and is the longest-standing individual world record in women's track and field. Other notable record holders include Nadezhda Olizarenko, who ran 1:53.43 at the 1980 Moscow Olympics, and Ana Fidelia Quirot of Cuba.
