Arsinoe III

DynastyPtolemaic
SpousePtolemy IV Philopator (brother)

Arsinoe III (died 204 BC) was Queen of Egypt as the wife and sister of Ptolemy IV Philopator, whom she married around 220 BC. She was the mother of Ptolemy V Epiphanes.

Life

Arsinoe accompanied her husband during the Fourth Syrian War and, according to Polybius, personally encouraged the troops before the Battle of Raphia in 217 BC, a major Ptolemaic victory over Antiochus III. Coins and inscriptions honoured her as a goddess alongside her husband in the dynastic cult.

Death

When Ptolemy IV died in 204 BC, his ministers Sosibius and Agathocles concealed the death and had Arsinoe murdered before announcing the accession of the young Ptolemy V. News of her killing provoked a popular uprising in Alexandria in which Agathocles and his family were lynched by the mob.
