GMC Hummer EV

GMC Hummer EV
Manufacturer: General Motors
Production: 2021–present
Class: Full-size pickup truck / SUV
Layout: Dual- or tri-motor all-wheel drive
Platform: GM Ultium / BT1

The GMC Hummer EV (badged as the Hummer EV) is a line of battery electric vehicles produced by General Motors under its GMC marque. It was formally announced on 30 January 2020 and unveiled on 20 October 2020, reviving the Hummer nameplate that had been discontinued in 2010. The line comprises a pickup truck (SUT) and a sport utility vehicle (SUV), both built at GM's Factory Zero plant in Detroit–Hamtramck, Michigan.

Specifications

The Hummer EV is built on the Ultium platform with battery packs of up to roughly 200 kWh in the launch Edition 1 pickup. The tri-motor Edition 1 produces about 1,000 horsepower (746 kW) and an estimated 11,500 lb-ft of wheel torque as quoted by GM, with a claimed 0–60 mph time of about 3 seconds in "Watts to Freedom" launch mode. EPA-estimated range exceeds 300 miles depending on configuration. Distinctive features include four-wheel steering with a diagonal-drive "CrabWalk" mode, an adaptive air suspension with Extract Mode, removable roof panels ("Infinity Roof"), and the Super Cruise driver-assistance system.

Production and sales

Production of the Pickup began in late 2021; the Edition 1 sold out its first-year reservations within hours of the reveal. The SUV variant entered production in early 2023. The Hummer EV is notably heavy — the pickup's curb weight exceeds 9,000 lb (about 4,100 kg) — which drew comment about the efficiency and safety implications of very large electric trucks.

Reception

Reviews praised the vehicle's off-road technology and performance while criticizing its weight, size, and efficiency. The Hummer EV has been a halo vehicle for GM's electrification programme.
