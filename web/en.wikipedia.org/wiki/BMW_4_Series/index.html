BMW 4 Series

BMW 4 Series
ManufacturerBMW
Production2013–present
ClassCompact executive car

The BMW 4 Series is a range of compact executive cars manufactured by BMW since 2013. The 4 Series was created when BMW split the coupé and convertible models of the 3 Series into a separate series, continuing the naming convention in which even-numbered series denote coupé-style body styles.

Generations

The first generation (F32 coupé, F33 convertible, F36 Gran Coupé fastback) was produced from 2013 to 2020 and closely followed the F30 3 Series mechanically. The second generation (G22) arrived in 2020, distinguished by a large vertical kidney grille, with the battery-electric i4 Gran Coupé sharing its body. High-performance M4 models are produced by BMW M GmbH.
