Diablo (series)

Diablo is an action role-playing dungeon crawler video game series developed by Blizzard Entertainment (and Blizzard North). The series is set in the dark fantasy world of Sanctuary, where heroes battle the forces of Hell led by the Prime Evils, foremost among them Diablo, the Lord of Terror.

Games

The original Diablo (1997) sent players beneath the town of Tristram into a gothic cathedral's depths. Diablo II (2000) and its expansion Lord of Destruction became landmark titles in the action RPG genre. Diablo III followed in 2012, Diablo Immortal in 2022, and Diablo IV in 2023.

Gameplay

Players choose a character class — such as Barbarian, Sorcerer, Necromancer, Rogue or Druid — and fight through procedurally arranged dungeons, collecting randomly generated loot. The series is credited with popularising the loot-driven action RPG formula.
