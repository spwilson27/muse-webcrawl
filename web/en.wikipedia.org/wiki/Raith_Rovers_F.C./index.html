Raith Rovers F.C.

Raith Rovers Football Club is a Scottish professional football club based in Kirkcaldy, Fife. Founded in 1883 and named after the former Raith estate, the club plays at Stark's Park and competes in the Scottish league system, most recently in the Scottish Championship.

History

Raith's finest era came in the late 1930s, when the club set a British goalscoring record of 142 league goals in the 1937–38 season on the way to the Second Division title. The club's most famous modern achievement was winning the Scottish League Cup in 1994, defeating Celtic on penalties in the final at Ibrox, which earned a UEFA Cup run that included taking the lead against Bayern Munich. Gordon Brown, the former British prime minister, is a well-known supporter and former shareholder.
