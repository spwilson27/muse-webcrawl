Pamela Dawber (born 18 October 1951) is an American actress best known for her lead television sitcom roles as Mindy McConnell in Mork & Mindy (1978–1982) and as Samantha Russell in My Sister Sam (1986–1988).

Dawber in her breakthrough role opposite Robin Williams

Career

Dawber worked as a model before being cast, with no prior acting experience, opposite Robin Williams in Mork & Mindy, the Happy Days spin-off that became an immediate hit and made both leads stars. She went on to star in My Sister Sam; the series ended after co-star Rebecca Schaeffer was murdered by a stalker in 1989, an event that prompted Dawber to advocate for gun control measures. Dawber appeared in television films and voiced characters in animation, and in 2021 joined her husband Mark Harmon's series NCIS for a short arc as journalist Marcie Warren.

Personal life

Dawber married actor Mark Harmon in 1987; they have two sons and are known for keeping their family life private.
