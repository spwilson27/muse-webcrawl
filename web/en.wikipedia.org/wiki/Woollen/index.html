Woollen

Woollen (American spelling woolen) is a type of yarn made from carded wool, and the fabric woven from it. Woollen yarn is soft, light, stretchy and full of air, and is thus a good insulator, used for warm clothing such as jumpers, blankets and tweeds.

Manufacture

Unlike worsted yarn, woollen yarn is spun from fibres of mixed lengths that have been carded but not combed, so the fibres lie in many directions and trap air. The resulting fabric has a fuzzy surface, less defined weave and greater bulk.

History

Woollen cloth production was a foundation of the medieval English economy, centred on regions such as East Anglia, the West Country and Yorkshire. Mills later industrialised carding, spinning and weaving during the Industrial Revolution.

Uses

Woollens are used for blankets, flannel, tweed, and knitwear where warmth and softness matter more than a smooth finish.
