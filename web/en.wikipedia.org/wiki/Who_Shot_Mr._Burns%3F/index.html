Who Shot Mr. Burns?

Who Shot Mr. Burns?
Series: The Simpsons
Episodes: Season 6, episode 25; Season 7, episode 1
Original air dates: 21 May 1995; 17 September 1995

Who Shot Mr. Burns? is a two-part episode of the animated television series The Simpsons, serving as the sixth-season finale and the seventh-season premiere. It is a parody of the "Who shot J.R.?" storyline from Dallas.

Plot

After Mr. Burns steals oil discovered beneath Springfield Elementary and then blocks out the sun over Springfield to force consumption of his electricity, nearly every resident has a motive to shoot him. Part One ends with Burns shot in the town square. A summer-long publicity campaign invited viewers to guess the culprit; Part Two reveals that Maggie Simpson accidentally shot Burns when he tried to steal her lollipop and his gun fell into her hands.

Reception

The episodes are among the most famous of the series and were accompanied by an alternate ending (in which Smithers was the shooter) produced to foil leaks.
