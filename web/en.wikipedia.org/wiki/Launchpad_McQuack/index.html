Launchpad McQuack is a fictional character created by Walt Disney Television Animation as one of the main characters of the animated series DuckTales (1987–1990). He is an able-bodied pilot with a powerful build and a cheerful, dim-witted personality, employed by Scrooge McDuck. His surname, McQuack, is a humorous reference to his signature trait: despite his skill in the air, nearly every flight he makes ends in a crash landing — his famous catchphrase being "Any crash you can walk away from is a good crash!"

Character

Launchpad is kind-hearted, brave, and fiercely loyal, but prone to taking instructions literally and to comical overconfidence. Within the original DuckTales he serves as Scrooge's pilot and chauffeur and as an occasional big-brother figure to Huey, Dewey, and Louie. In Darkwing Duck (1991–1992) he crosses over as the title hero's devoted sidekick and biggest fan, helping him establish his crime-fighting career in St. Canard.

DuckTales reboot

In the 2017 DuckTales reboot, voiced by Beck Bennett, Launchpad is reimagined as the McDuck family's limousine driver and pilot, an enthusiastic Darkwing Duck fan who later becomes the hero's friend in person. The reboot gives him an extended family of daredevils and a recurring flying instructor past, and portrays his crashes as a matter of family pride.

In other media

Launchpad appears in the DuckTales video games and remasters, in comics, and in the video game QuackShot (as a non-playable pilot). He is one of the few characters created for the Disney Afternoon television block to become a durable part of the wider Donald Duck universe.
