State Russian Museum

The State Russian Museum in Saint Petersburg is the world's largest depository of Russian fine art, housing a collection spanning from 10th-century icons to contemporary works.

History

The museum was established by decree of Nicholas II in 1895 and opened in 1898 in the Mikhailovsky Palace. It grew from works transferred from the Hermitage, the Academy of Arts, and imperial palaces, and today occupies several buildings including the Stroganov Palace, the Marble Palace, and St Michael's Castle.

Collection

Holdings include roughly 400,000 items: icons by Andrei Rublev's school, portraits by Karl Bryullov, landscapes by Ivan Aivazovsky, and major works by Ilya Repin, Kazimir Malevich (including versions of the Black Square), and Wassily Kandinsky.
