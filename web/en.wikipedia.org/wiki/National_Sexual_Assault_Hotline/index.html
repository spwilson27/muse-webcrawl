National Sexual Assault Hotline

Operator: RAINN
Founded: 1994
Phone: 1-800-656-4673 (1-800-656-HOPE)
Online: online.rainn.org
Availability: 24 hours, free and confidential

The National Sexual Assault Hotline is a free, confidential, 24-hour telephone and online hotline in the United States for survivors of sexual assault and their friends and family. It is operated by RAINN (the Rape, Abuse & Incest National Network) and was created in 1994.

How it works

Callers dialling 1-800-656-HOPE are automatically routed, by their area code or location, to one of more than a thousand local affiliated sexual assault service providers across the United States. Trained support specialists offer crisis intervention, emotional support, information about medical care and reporting options, and referrals. RAINN launched the companion online hotline in 2006, offering one-to-one chat support.

Impact

The hotline has helped millions of survivors since its founding and is frequently cited as the largest anti-sexual-violence support service in the United States. Call volume typically rises sharply when sexual assault is prominent in the news, for example during the #MeToo movement. RAINN also operates the DoD Safe Helpline for the U.S. Department of Defense under a separate contract.
