Port Elizabeth, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines

Port Elizabeth
Country: Saint Vincent and the Grenadines
Island: Bequia
Parish: Grenadines

Port Elizabeth is a town on the island of Bequia, which forms part of the country of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines. Located on Admiralty Bay, it is the main town and harbour of Bequia, the largest of the Grenadine islands in the Vincentian archipelago.

The town is a popular anchorage for yachts cruising the Grenadines. Its waterfront is lined with shops, restaurants, and small hotels, and a walkway runs along the bay from the harbour toward Princess Margaret Beach. Ferries connect Port Elizabeth with the capital, Kingstown, on Saint Vincent.
