Puzzle

A puzzle is a game, problem or toy that tests a person's ingenuity or knowledge. In a puzzle, the solver is expected to put pieces together (or take them apart) in a logical way in order to arrive at the correct or an intended solution.

Types

Puzzles are commonly classified into categories such as logic puzzles (including Sudoku and nonograms), word puzzles (crosswords, anagrams), mechanical puzzles (jigsaw puzzles, disentanglement puzzles, the Rubik's Cube), mathematical puzzles, and lateral-thinking puzzles or riddles.

History

Puzzles are ancient: riddles appear in Babylonian and Egyptian texts, and Archimedes is credited with the Stomachion dissection puzzle. The jigsaw puzzle is traced to the London engraver John Spilsbury around 1760, who mounted maps on wood and cut them into pieces for teaching geography. The twentieth century saw mass-market puzzle crazes, from the crossword boom of the 1920s to the Rubik's Cube in the 1980s.
