Park

A park is an area of natural, semi-natural, or planted space set aside for human enjoyment and recreation, or for the protection of wildlife and natural habitats. Parks may consist of grassy areas, rocks, soil, and trees; they often include amenities such as playgrounds, sports fields, gardens, walking and cycling paths, benches, fountains, and monuments.

Types

Urban parks are green spaces within cities and towns, providing recreation and relief from the built environment. Famous examples include Central Park in New York City and Hyde Park in London. National parks are large protected areas, usually owned and managed by national governments, established to conserve scenery, wildlife, and ecosystems; Yellowstone, established in 1872, is generally regarded as the world's first national park. Other categories include state and provincial parks, regional parks, country parks, pocket parks, dog parks, and amusement or theme parks, the latter being commercial entertainment complexes rather than open green space.

History

The earliest parks were deer parks and hunting reserves maintained by aristocracy. In the nineteenth century, industrialisation and urban crowding prompted the public parks movement, which argued that open green space was essential to public health. Municipal parks proliferated across Europe and North America, designed in landscaped styles associated with figures such as Frederick Law Olmsted, co-designer of Central Park.

Uses and benefits

Parks support recreation, exercise, social gathering, cultural events, and tourism. Ecologically, they provide habitat, improve air quality, moderate urban temperatures, and assist stormwater management. Studies associate access to parks with improved physical and mental health outcomes for nearby residents.

Management

Parks are typically maintained by municipal or national park services, though some are operated by trusts, charities, or private owners. Funding, conservation, safety, and balancing recreation with habitat protection are continuing management concerns.
