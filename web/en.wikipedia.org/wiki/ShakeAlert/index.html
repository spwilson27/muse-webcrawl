ShakeAlert

ShakeAlert is an earthquake early warning system operated by the United States Geological Survey (USGS) for the West Coast of the United States. It detects significant earthquakes so quickly that alerts can reach many people before damaging shaking arrives, typically providing seconds to tens of seconds of warning.

How it works

A network of seismic sensors across California, Oregon and Washington detects the fast-moving, less-damaging P waves that precede the slower, destructive S waves. Algorithms estimate the earthquake's location and magnitude within seconds, and alerts are distributed through partners including wireless emergency alerts, mobile apps and automated systems that can slow trains, open fire-station doors and stop elevators.

Deployment

ShakeAlert-powered public alerting began in California in 2019, followed by Oregon in 2021 and Washington in 2021. The system builds on earlier research systems developed with university partners including Caltech, UC Berkeley, the University of Oregon and the University of Washington.
