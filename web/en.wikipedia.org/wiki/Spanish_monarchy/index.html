The Spanish monarchy is the institution of the Crown of Spain, headed by the King of Spain, who serves as head of state under the Spanish Constitution of 1978. It is one of the oldest monarchies in Europe, tracing a continuous line through the kingdoms of Castile and Aragon to the present day, with interruptions during the two Spanish republics and the Franco era.

Origins

The monarchy emerged from the medieval Christian kingdoms of the Iberian Peninsula. The marriage of Ferdinand II of Aragon and Isabella I of Castile in 1469 united the two largest crowns dynastically, and their grandson Charles I inherited both in 1516, along with an overseas empire in the Americas and possessions in Europe.

Dynasties

  • House of Trastámara — Ferdinand and Isabella and their immediate heirs.
  • House of Habsburg (1516–1700) — Charles I through Charles II; Spain's Golden Age and global empire.
  • House of Bourbon (1700–1808, 1813–1868, 1874–1931, 1975–present) — beginning with Philip V, grandson of Louis XIV of France.
  • House of Bonaparte (1808–1813) — Joseph Bonaparte, imposed by Napoleon.
  • House of Savoy (1870–1873) — Amadeo I, elected by the Cortes.

Interruptions and restoration

The monarchy was abolished during the First Republic (1873–1874) and the Second Republic (1931–1939). Following the Spanish Civil War, Francisco Franco ruled as dictator; in 1947 he declared Spain a kingdom, and in 1969 he named Juan Carlos de Borbón as his successor. The monarchy was restored on Franco's death in 1975, and Juan Carlos I oversaw the transition to democracy. His son Felipe VI succeeded him upon his abdication in 2014.

The monarchy today

Under the 1978 constitution the King is head of state and symbol of national unity, sanctions laws, formally appoints the prime minister, and is commander-in-chief of the armed forces. Executive power rests with the government. Public opinion surveys have historically shown majority support for the monarchy, though republican sentiment persists, particularly among younger voters and in Catalonia and the Basque Country.
