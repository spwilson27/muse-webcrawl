DataCite

DataCite is an international not-for-profit organisation that provides persistent identifiers, specifically Digital Object Identifiers (DOIs), for research data and other research outputs. Founded in London in 2009, its aim is to help the research community locate, identify and cite research data and other scholarly resources with the same ease as traditional publications.

Services

DataCite's member organisations — libraries, data centres, universities and research institutions — register DOIs accompanied by standardised metadata described by the DataCite Metadata Schema. The registered metadata are made freely available through services such as DataCite Commons, a search index of DOIs and other persistent identifiers, and APIs that support linking between datasets, articles, people (via ORCID) and organisations (via ROR).

Role in scholarly communication

By making data citable, DataCite supports the FAIR principles of findability, accessibility, interoperability and reusability, and allows data producers to receive credit for their work. DataCite is one of the founding organisations behind initiatives on open research infrastructure and works alongside Crossref, the registration agency for scholarly publications.
