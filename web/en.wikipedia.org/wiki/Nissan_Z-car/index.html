Nissan Z-car

The Nissan Z-car is a series of sports cars manufactured by Nissan. The original Z was first sold in October 1969 in Japan as the Nissan Fairlady Z and in other markets under the name Datsun 240Z. Since then, Nissan has manufactured six generations of the Z, culminating in the seventh-generation model introduced for 2023, simply called the Nissan Z.

ManufacturerNissan

History

The first generation, the S30 (sold as the 240Z, 260Z and 280Z), established the formula: a long bonnet, short rear deck, a straight-six engine and an affordable price that undercut European rivals such as the Porsche 911. The second generation S130 (280ZX) was more luxurious. The Z31 and Z32 generations of the 300ZX brought turbocharging and, in the Z32, advanced technology such as four-wheel steering. After a hiatus, the 350Z (Z33) revived the line in 2002, followed by the 370Z (Z34) in 2009.

Seventh generation

Revealed in production form in August 2021, the seventh-generation Nissan Z (RZ34) uses a 3.0-litre twin-turbocharged V6 producing 400 horsepower, offered with a six-speed manual or nine-speed automatic transmission. Its styling quotes the original 240Z at the front and the 300ZX at the rear.

Motorsport

Z-cars have a long competition history, including SCCA championships in the United States, rally success with the 240Z in the East African Safari Rally, and IMSA racing with the 300ZX, which won the 24 Hours of Daytona and the 12 Hours of Sebring in its class.
