DaVinci Resolve is a proprietary application for non-linear video editing, color correction, color grading, visual effects and audio post-production developed by Australian company Blackmagic Design. It is available for macOS, Windows, Linux and iPadOS in a free edition and a paid version, DaVinci Resolve Studio.

History

The software descends from the da Vinci Systems color-correction hardware used in film post-production since the 1980s. Blackmagic Design acquired da Vinci Systems in 2009, sharply reduced the price, and progressively added editing, Fusion visual effects and Fairlight audio tools, combining previously separate applications into a single program.

Features

Resolve is organized into pages: Media, Cut, Edit, Fusion, Color, Fairlight and Deliver. The Color page is widely regarded as an industry standard for grading feature films and television. The free version includes most functionality; Studio adds features such as higher resolutions, higher frame rates, neural-engine tools, HDR grading and stereoscopic 3D support.

Reception

Resolve is used on major film and broadcast productions and has become popular with independent creators because of its capable free tier.
