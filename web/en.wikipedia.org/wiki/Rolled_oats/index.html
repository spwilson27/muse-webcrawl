Rolled oats

Rolled oats are a type of lightly processed whole-grain food made from oat groats that have been dehusked, steamed and flattened between rollers into flakes, then lightly toasted. They are a staple breakfast food, most commonly cooked with water or milk to make porridge (oatmeal), and are also used in muesli, granola, flapjacks and oatcakes.

Types and preparation

Regular ("old-fashioned") rolled oats take about five minutes to cook; thinner "quick" and "instant" oats are rolled finer or pre-cooked so they prepare faster, while steel-cut oats are chopped rather than rolled and take longer. Rolled oats are a good source of soluble fibre, notably beta-glucan, which is associated with lowered cholesterol, and can be soaked overnight without cooking to make "overnight oats".
