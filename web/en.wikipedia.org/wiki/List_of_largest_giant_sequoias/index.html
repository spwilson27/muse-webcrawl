List of largest giant sequoias

The giant sequoia (Sequoiadendron giganteum) is the world's largest tree species by volume. Giant sequoias occur naturally only in groves on the western slopes of the Sierra Nevada of California. This list ranks individual living specimens by the volume of their trunks, the standard measure used because heights and diameters alone do not capture the trees' massive, tapered trunks.

Measurement

Trunk volumes are calculated from detailed measurements of height, basal diameter and diameters at intervals up the trunk, treating sections as truncated cones. The General Sherman Tree, with a volume of about 1,487 cubic metres, is the largest known living single-stem tree on Earth. Several named giants have been lost to fire and storms, most notably in the severe Sierra Nevada wildfires of 2020–2021, which killed an estimated 13–19 per cent of all large giant sequoias.
