Amon Leopold Göth (11 December 1908 – 13 September 1946) was an Austrian SS functionary and war criminal. As commandant of the Kraków-Płaszów concentration camp in German-occupied Poland from February 1943 to September 1944, he personally murdered prisoners and directed the liquidation of the Kraków and Tarnów ghettos. He was tried by the Polish Supreme National Tribunal after the war, convicted, and hanged. Göth became widely known through his portrayal by Ralph Fiennes in the 1993 film Schindler's List.

Early life and SS career

Göth was born in Vienna to a prosperous publishing family. He joined the Austrian Nazi Party and the SS in the early 1930s, when both were illegal in Austria, and after the Anschluss served in SS administrative postings. With the start of Operation Reinhard he worked in Lublin under Odilo Globocnik on the construction of extermination camps before being sent to Kraków in 1943.

Płaszów commandant

Under Göth's command, the Płaszów forced-labor camp was converted into a concentration camp and expanded to hold tens of thousands of Jewish and Polish prisoners. He supervised the March 1943 liquidation of the Kraków Ghetto, in which thousands were murdered or deported to extermination camps, and the liquidation of the Tarnów Ghetto. Survivors testified that Göth routinely shot prisoners from the balcony of his villa and set his dogs on inmates; he is held responsible for the deaths of thousands. He also profited from confiscated prisoner property. A protected group of prisoners worked in the enamel factory of Oskar Schindler, who bribed Göth to preserve his workers.

Arrest, trial, and execution

In September 1944 the SS itself arrested Göth for stealing Jewish property and abusing prisoners; the war ended before his court martial. Captured by American forces, he was extradited to Poland, where the Supreme National Tribunal tried him in Kraków in the summer of 1946. He was sentenced to death and hanged on 13 September 1946 near the former Płaszów camp. His granddaughter Jennifer Teege later wrote about discovering her family history in the memoir My Grandfather Would Have Shot Me (2013).
