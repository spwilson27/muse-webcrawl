Pet Shop Boys are an English synth-pop duo formed in London in 1981. Consisting of primary vocalist Neil Tennant and keyboardist Chris Lowe, they have sold more than 100 million records worldwide and are listed in the Guinness Book of Records as the most successful duo in UK music history.

Three-time Brit Award winners and six-time Grammy Award nominees, Pet Shop Boys have achieved 42 Top 30 singles, including 22 Top 10 hits on the UK Singles Chart, including four number ones: "West End Girls", "It's a Sin", "Always on My Mind" (always credited in its full form with "(In My House)"), and "Heart".

History

Tennant and Lowe met by chance in an electronics shop on the King's Road, Chelsea, in August 1981. Discovering a shared interest in dance music, they began writing together, initially calling themselves West End before settling on Pet Shop Boys, a name taken from friends who worked in a pet shop in Ealing.

Their debut single, "West End Girls", was first released in 1984 and became a club hit in Belgium and France. After the duo signed with Parlophone in 1985, a re-recorded version produced by Stephen Hague topped the charts in the United Kingdom and the United States in 1986.

Musical style and influence

The duo's sound combines synth-pop and dance music with literate, often ironic lyrics. Tennant's detached delivery and Lowe's largely silent stage persona became trademarks. They have been cited as an influence on numerous electronic and pop acts, and their work has been noted for its collaborations with producers such as Trevor Horn and Stuart Price.

Discography

Studio albums include Please (1986), Actually (1987), Introspective (1988), Behaviour (1990), Very (1993), Bilingual (1996), Nightlife (1999), Release (2002), Fundamental (2006), Yes (2009), Elysium (2012), Electric (2013), Super (2016), Hotspot (2020) and Nonetheless (2024).
