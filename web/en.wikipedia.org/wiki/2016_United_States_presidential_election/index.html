2016 United States presidential election

The 2016 United States presidential election was held on 8 November 2016. Republican businessman Donald Trump, running with Mike Pence, defeated Democratic former Secretary of State Hillary Clinton, running with Tim Kaine, in the Electoral College despite Clinton winning the popular vote.


Date: 8 November 2016
Winner: Donald Trump / Mike Pence (R)
Electoral vote: Trump 304*, Clinton 227* (*after faithless electors; pledged 306–232)
Popular vote: Clinton 65,853,514 (48.2%), Trump 62,984,828 (46.1%)

Campaign

Clinton won the Democratic nomination over Bernie Sanders; Trump prevailed in a crowded Republican primary. Issues included trade, immigration, healthcare and the Supreme Court. Controversies included Clinton's private email server, Trump's Access Hollywood tape, FBI Director James Comey's late letter on Clinton emails, and Russian interference in the election, later documented by US intelligence agencies.

Results

Trump flipped Michigan, Pennsylvania and Wisconsin by narrow margins, breaking the Democrats' 'blue wall'. It was the fifth election in US history where the popular-vote loser won the presidency.
