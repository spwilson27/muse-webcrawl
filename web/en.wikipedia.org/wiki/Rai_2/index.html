Rai 2 is an Italian free-to-air television channel owned and operated by RAI, the country's national public broadcasting company. Launched in 1961 as Italy's second television network (originally called Secondo Programma), it was created to break the monopoly of Rai 1's generalist schedule and to offer lighter, more experimental, and youth-oriented programming.

Programming

Rai 2 has traditionally been RAI's channel for variety and talk shows, satire, reality television, and imported series, alongside live sports coverage and a strong schedule of drama and documentary strands. Notable long-running programmes have included Quelli che il calcio, Dribbling, and various late-night satirical shows. The channel is aimed at a younger demographic than Rai 1 and competes directly with the Mediaset networks Canale 5 and Italia 1.

Identity

Over the decades Rai 2's branding has been updated several times, with the modern orange "2" logo introduced in the 2010s. The channel broadcasts in high definition as Rai 2 HD and is carried on digital terrestrial television, satellite, and streaming platforms.
