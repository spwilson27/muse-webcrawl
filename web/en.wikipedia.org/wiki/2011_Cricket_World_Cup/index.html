The 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup was the tenth Cricket World Cup, co-hosted by India, Sri Lanka and Bangladesh from 19 February to 2 April 2011. Fourteen teams competed in a round-robin group stage followed by knockouts. India defeated Sri Lanka by six wickets in the final at Wankhede Stadium, Mumbai, to win their second World Cup title.

Tournament summary

The tournament returned to Asia for the first time since 1996; Pakistan, originally a co-host, had been stripped of its matches in 2009 following the attack on the Sri Lanka team in Lahore. Notable group-stage results included Ireland's famous chase of 328 against England, powered by Kevin O'Brien's 50-ball century — the fastest in World Cup history. India eliminated three-time defending champions Australia in the quarter-finals and then defeated arch-rivals Pakistan in a charged semi-final at Mohali.

The final

Sri Lanka, batting first, posted 274 for 6, built around Mahela Jayawardene's unbeaten 103. India lost Virender Sehwag and Sachin Tendulkar early, but Gautam Gambhir's 97 and an unbeaten 91 from captain MS Dhoni steadied the chase. Dhoni finished the match with a towering six over long-on, sparking celebrations across India and giving Tendulkar a World Cup title at his sixth attempt. Yuvraj Singh was named player of the tournament for his all-round performances.
