Paphos International Airport

Paphos International Airport (IATA: PFO, ICAO: LCPH) is a joint civil-military public airport in Cyprus, located about 6.5 km (4 mi) southeast of the city of Paphos. It is the country's second-largest airport, after Larnaca International Airport, and serves mainly tourists visiting western Cyprus and the Paphos resort region. The airport is operated by Hermes Airports.

History

The airport opened in 1982 to serve the growing tourist trade of western Cyprus, which previously relied on Larnaca. A new passenger terminal, part of a nationwide airport development program under Hermes Airports, opened in November 2008; the old terminal was converted to other uses. Traffic grew strongly in the 2010s as low-cost carriers, particularly Ryanair — which opened a base at Paphos in 2012 — expanded services. The airport also hosts facilities used by the Cyprus National Guard.

Facilities and airlines

The single terminal handles international scheduled and charter flights, with airlines linking Paphos to the United Kingdom, Germany, Greece, Israel, and other European and Middle Eastern destinations, many operating seasonally. The airport has 28 check-in counters, seven gates, and parking for several thousand cars. Ground transport is by bus, taxi, and rental car via the A6 motorway.
