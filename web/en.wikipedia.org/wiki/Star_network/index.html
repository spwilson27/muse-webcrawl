Star network

A star network is a computer network topology in which each host is connected to a central hub, such as a switch or router, and all communication passes through that central node. It is the most common topology in modern local area networks.

Advantages and disadvantages

Star networks are easy to install, manage, and troubleshoot: a failed cable affects only one device, and devices can be added or removed without disturbing the rest. The central hub is, however, a single point of failure, and cabling costs can be higher than in a bus topology. Ethernet networks using switches are physical stars even when they behave logically like a bus.

Variations

An extended star links several hubs together, and distributed star topologies form the basis of many enterprise networks.
