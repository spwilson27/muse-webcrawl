The Transporter

The Transporter (French: Le Transporteur) is a 2002 action-thriller film directed by Corey Yuen and Louis Leterrier, written by Luc Besson and Robert Mark Kamen, and starring Jason Statham in the role that made him an action star. It also stars Shu Qi, Matt Schulze and François Berléand.

Plot

Frank Martin, a British ex-special forces operative living quietly on the French Mediterranean coast, hires himself out as a mercenary "transporter" who moves goods — human or otherwise — for a fee, under three strict rules: never change the deal, no names, and never open the package. When he breaks the third rule and finds a bound and gagged woman, Lai, in his boot, he is drawn into fighting a human-smuggling ring, aided reluctantly by French police inspector Tarconi.

Sequels

The film spawned a franchise: Transporter 2 (2005), Transporter 3 (2008), the reboot The Transporter Refueled (2015) with Ed Skrein, and a television series (2012–2014) starring Chris Vance.
