Adriana Caselotti

Adriana Elena Loreta Caselotti
Born: 6 May 1916, Bridgeport, Connecticut
Died: 18 January 1997, Los Angeles
Known for: Voice of Snow White (1937)

Adriana Caselotti (May 6, 1916 – January 18, 1997) was an American actress and singer best known as the voice of Snow White in Walt Disney's Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937).

Career

From a musical family — her father taught voice and her mother sang at the Royal Opera in Rome — Caselotti was chosen by Disney from around 150 candidates. She was paid modestly and kept largely uncredited to preserve the illusion, limiting her later career. She also had small voice roles in The Wizard of Oz (1939, the Tin Man's "Wherefore art thou Romeo" line) and It's a Wonderful Life (1946).

Legacy

Caselotti promoted the film for decades, re-recorded Snow White's voice for Disneyland attractions, and was named a Disney Legend in 1994.
