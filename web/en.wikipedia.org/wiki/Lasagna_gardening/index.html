Lasagna gardening

Lasagna gardening, also called sheet mulching or sheet composting, is a no-dig gardening method in which a bed is built by layering organic materials — like the layers of a lasagna — directly on the ground, without tilling.

A typical bed starts with cardboard or newspaper to smother weeds, followed by alternating "brown" carbon-rich layers (straw, leaves, shredded paper) and "green" nitrogen-rich layers (grass clippings, kitchen scraps, manure), topped with compost or soil. The layers decompose in place, improving soil structure and moisture retention.

The method was popularised by Patricia Lanza's 1998 book Lasagna Gardening. Planting can begin immediately into the top layer or after the bed has partly composted.
