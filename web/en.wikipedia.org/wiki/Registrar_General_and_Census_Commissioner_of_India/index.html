Registrar General and Census Commissioner of India

The Registrar General and Census Commissioner of India is a senior official in the Government of India, heading the Office of the Registrar General and Census Commissioner under the Ministry of Home Affairs. The office is responsible for conducting the decennial Census of India, maintaining the National Register of Citizens-related registers, and administering the Civil Registration System for births and deaths.

Functions

India has conducted a census every ten years since 1881 (the 2021 census was postponed). The Registrar General organises the census through directors of census operations in each state, overseeing the enumeration of more than a billion people. The office also produces vital statistics based on the registration of births and deaths and the Sample Registration System. The position is normally held by an officer of the Indian Administrative Service.
