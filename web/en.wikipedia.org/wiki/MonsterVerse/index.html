MonsterVerse

The MonsterVerse is an American multimedia franchise and shared fictional universe featuring Godzilla, King Kong, and other giant monsters (Titans) owned by Toho and other studios. The franchise is produced by Legendary Pictures in partnership with Warner Bros. Pictures, which distributes the films, and is centered on a series of monster films in which the secretive organization Monarch studies and responds to the creatures.

Films

The series began with Godzilla (2014), directed by Gareth Edwards, followed by Kong: Skull Island (2017), Godzilla: King of the Monsters (2019), Godzilla vs. Kong (2021), and Godzilla x Kong: The New Empire (2024). Television expansions include the animated series Skull Island (2023) and the live-action series Monarch: Legacy of Monsters (2023) on Apple TV+. The franchise has grossed well over two billion dollars worldwide and is credited with reviving both flagship monsters for global audiences.
