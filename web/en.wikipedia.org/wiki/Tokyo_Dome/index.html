Tokyo Dome (Japanese: 東京ドーム) is an indoor baseball stadium in Bunkyo, Tokyo, Japan. Nicknamed "The Big Egg", it opened in 1988 as Japan's first domed stadium and is the home field of the Yomiuri Giants of Nippon Professional Baseball.

Design

The stadium's roof is an air-supported structure: a flexible membrane held up by slightly pressurising the interior of the building. It was modelled on the Hubert H. Humphrey Metrodome in Minneapolis. The field uses artificial turf, and the arena forms part of the larger Tokyo Dome City entertainment complex, which includes an amusement park, a spa, and hotels.

Events

Beyond baseball — including opening-round games of the World Baseball Classic and MLB season openers — Tokyo Dome hosts major concerts, professional wrestling (notably New Japan Pro-Wrestling's annual January 4 show, now Wrestle Kingdom), boxing (including Mike Tyson's 1990 loss to Buster Douglas), and K-1 kickboxing events. Performers such as Michael Jackson, the Rolling Stones, and many K-pop and J-pop acts have played to sold-out crowds there.
