[Image: A wall of classic red-bordered Time covers]

Time (stylised TIME) is an American news magazine founded on 3 March 1923 by Henry Luce and Briton Hadden, which more or less invented the weekly newsmagazine format — a brisk, opinionated summary of the week — and its famous red border. Its Person of the Year (begun 1927 as Man of the Year) and TIME100 franchises remain global media events.

Luce era and after

Under Luce, Time Inc. built a magazine empire (Fortune, Life, Sports Illustrated). Time merged into AOL Time Warner and later spin-offs; Meredith bought Time Inc. in 2018 and sold Time itself to Marc and Lynne Benioff, who own it as a digital-first brand. Print frequency declined to monthly-ish/fortnightly schedules in the 2020s.
