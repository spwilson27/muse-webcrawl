Tottenham Hotspur Stadium

The Tottenham Hotspur Stadium is a football stadium in Tottenham, north London, and the home ground of Tottenham Hotspur Football Club. Opened in April 2019 on a site immediately north of the club's former White Hart Lane ground, it has a capacity of 62,850, making it one of the largest club stadiums in England.

Design and features

Designed by Populous, the stadium is notable for a retractable grass pitch that slides away to reveal a synthetic American football field beneath, making it the first stadium purpose-built to host both Premier League football and NFL games under a long-term agreement. Its single-tier South Stand holds 17,500 spectators, among the largest in Europe, and the stadium includes extensive premium areas, an in-house brewery, and the "Goal Line Bar", reputedly the longest bar in Europe. Construction costs were widely reported at around £1 billion.

Events

Besides Spurs matches and NFL International Series games, the stadium hosts concerts, boxing — including the heavyweight championship fight between Anthony Joshua and Oleksandr Usyk in 2021 — and rugby league's Challenge Cup final. Naming rights had not been sold as of the mid-2020s, so the ground retains the club's name.
