Girder

A girder is a primary horizontal support beam in construction that carries loads from smaller beams (joists) and transfers them to columns or foundations. Girders are typically larger and stiffer than the beams they support.

Types

Common forms include the steel I-girder (I-beam), the box girder with a hollow rectangular or trapezoidal section, the plate girder built up from welded or riveted plates, and prestressed concrete girders used in bridges. Trussed girders combine elements in a triangular framework for long spans.

Bridges

Girder bridges are the most common bridge type worldwide for short and medium spans, with decks carried on parallel girders of steel or concrete.
