Hideaki Anno is a Japanese animator and filmmaker, co-founder of Gainax and founder of Studio Khara. He is best known as creator of the anime series Neon Genesis Evangelion (1995), whose psychological depth reshaped the medium.

Career

Anno began as an animator on Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind (1984), animating the God Warrior sequence, and directed Gunbuster and Nadia: The Secret of Blue Water before Evangelion. After its success he directed the live-action-influenced Kare Kano, then the Rebuild of Evangelion film tetralogy (2007–2021). He co-directed Shin Godzilla (2016), a major box-office and critical hit, and directed Shin Ultraman (2022) and Shin Kamen Rider (2023).
