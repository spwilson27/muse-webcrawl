Legio IV Macedonica

Legio IV Macedonica ("Fourth Macedonian Legion") was a legion of the Imperial Roman army founded by Augustus in 48 BC, or possibly inherited from the armies of the late Republic, and disbanded by Vespasian in AD 70. Its emblem was the bull, and later also the Capricorn, the sign of Augustus.

Service

The legion took its name from service in Macedonia during the civil wars, fighting for Mark Antony and then Octavian. Under the Empire it was stationed for decades in Hispania Tarraconensis, campaigning against the Cantabri, before transferring to Mogontiacum (Mainz) in Germania Superior around AD 43.

Year of the Four Emperors and disbandment

In the civil wars of AD 69 the Fourth supported Vitellius against Vespasian, and detachments fought at Bedriacum. During the Batavian revolt the legion's conduct at Mainz was judged disgraceful, and on his victory Vespasian disbanded it in AD 70, reconstituting its soldiers into the new Legio IV Flavia Felix.
