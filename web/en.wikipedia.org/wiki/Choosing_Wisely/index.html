Choosing Wisely

Choosing Wisely is a health campaign launched in 2012 by the ABIM Foundation to reduce unnecessary medical tests, treatments and procedures. Medical specialty societies publish lists of "Things Physicians and Patients Should Question" — commonly five recommendations each.

Goals

The campaign aims to spark conversations between clinicians and patients about overuse, avoiding interventions whose harms or costs outweigh their benefits, such as imaging for uncomplicated low back pain or antibiotics for viral infections.

Spread

Choosing Wisely campaigns have been adopted in dozens of countries, coordinated internationally as Choosing Wisely International, and the lists now number hundreds of recommendations.
