J. K. Simmons

Jonathan Kimble Simmons (born January 9, 1955) is an American actor. Regarded as one of the most prolific and accomplished character actors of his generation, he won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor for his portrayal of the abusive jazz instructor Terence Fletcher in Whiplash (2014).

Career

Simmons built his reputation on television as Dr. Emil Skoda on Law & Order, neo-Nazi inmate Vernon Schillinger on HBO's Oz (1997–2003), and Assistant Chief Will Pope on The Closer. In film he is widely known as J. Jonah Jameson in Sam Raimi's Spider-Man trilogy (2002–2007), a role he reprised in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. His terrifying turn in Whiplash swept the major supporting-actor awards of 2014–15.

Other work

Simmons has extensive voice credits, including the Yellow M&M, Tenzin in The Legend of Korra, Mayor Lionheart in Zootopia, and Omni-Man in Invincible. He has also been the familiar face of Farmers Insurance advertising campaigns since 2010.
