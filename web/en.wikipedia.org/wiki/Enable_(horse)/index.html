Enable (foaled 12 February 2014) is a retired British Thoroughbred racehorse, widely regarded as one of the greatest fillies and mares in racing history. Trained by John Gosden at Newmarket and ridden in most of her major races by Frankie Dettori, she won 15 of her 19 races, including eleven Group 1 events, for her owner-breeder Khalid Abdullah's Juddmonte operation.

Racing career

Enable won her only start as a two-year-old in 2016, then blossomed in the summer of 2017: she won the Cheshire Oaks, the Epsom Oaks by five lengths, the Irish Oaks, and then beat older colts in the King George VI and Queen Elizabeth Stakes at Ascot. After a facile win in the Yorkshire Oaks she crowned the season in the Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe at Chantilly, becoming European Horse of the Year.

Injury delayed her 2018 campaign, but she returned to win a second Arc at Longchamp — only the eighth dual winner — and then became the first Arc winner to succeed at the Breeders' Cup in the same season, taking the Breeders' Cup Turf at Churchill Downs. In 2019 she added the Eclipse Stakes, an epic third King George (edging out Crystal Ocean), and a third Yorkshire Oaks, before her bid for an unprecedented third Arc ended in second place behind Waldgeist on heavy ground. Kept in training as a six-year-old, she won a third King George VI and Queen Elizabeth Stakes in 2020 — the first horse to do so — and was retired that autumn after finishing sixth in the Arc, her final start, with career earnings of over £10.7 million.

Breeding career

Enable was retired to Juddmonte's Banstead Manor Stud, where her first matings included Kingman and Dubawi. She was named Cartier Champion Three-Year-Old Filly (2017) and Cartier Horse of the Year in 2017 and 2019, and her peak official rating of 134 placed her among the highest-rated mares of the modern era.
