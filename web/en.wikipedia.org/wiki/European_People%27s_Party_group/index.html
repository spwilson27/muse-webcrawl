The European People's Party Group (EPP Group) is the political group of the European People's Party in the European Parliament, composed of Members of the European Parliament (MEPs) from Christian democratic, conservative and liberal-conservative national parties. It has been the largest group in the Parliament since the 1999 European elections.

History

The group traces its origins to the Christian Democratic Group formed in the Common Assembly of the European Coal and Steel Community in 1953. It adopted its present name in 1979, when the first direct European elections were held, and grew through the accession of new member states and the affiliation of parties such as the British Conservatives (who later left to form the ECR group in 2009).

Organisation

The group elects a chairman and bureau, and its MEPs coordinate voting positions in parliamentary committees and plenary sessions.
