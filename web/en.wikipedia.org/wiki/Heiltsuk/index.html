Heiltsuk

The Heiltsuk (Haíɫzaqv) are an Indigenous people of the Central Coast of British Columbia, Canada, centred on the community of Bella Bella (Waglisla) on Campbell Island. Their traditional territory spans a large area of inlets, islands and mainland coast, and their history in the region extends back at least 14,000 years — among the earliest evidence of human habitation in the Americas, at sites such as Triquet Island.

Culture and language

The Heiltsuk speak Heiltsuk-Oowekyala, a Northern Wakashan language, and traditionally organised society in extended family groups (núák̓) with hereditary chiefs, potlatching and a rich ceremonial and artistic tradition tied to the salmon- and ocean-based economy.

Modern nation

The Heiltsuk Nation is a self-governing First Nation active in resource stewardship, fisheries and land-use planning in its territory, and played a prominent role in the Great Bear Rainforest agreements.
