The BYU Cougars are the intercollegiate athletic teams representing Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah. BYU fields teams in the NCAA Division I, joining the Big 12 Conference in 2023 after a period as an independent in football and membership of the West Coast Conference in most other sports.

Football

The Cougars football team, playing at LaVell Edwards Stadium, won the national championship in 1984 under coach LaVell Edwards, and quarterback Ty Detmer won the Heisman Trophy in 1990. The programme is known for prolific passing offences and older rosters due to many players serving Latter-day Saint missions.

Other sports

BYU has strong traditions in men's and women's volleyball, track and field, cross country, rugby (club) and basketball, playing at the Marriott Center. The university's honor code shapes recruiting and athlete conduct across all teams.
