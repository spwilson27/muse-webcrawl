Herod (horse)

Herod (foaled 1758, originally named King Herod) was a Thoroughbred racehorse and one of the most influential sires in the history of the breed. Bred by Prince William, Duke of Cumberland, he was by Tartar out of Cypron, and traced in the male line to the Byerley Turk, one of the three foundation sires of the Thoroughbred.

Racing career

Herod raced in the colours of the Duke of Cumberland and later Sir John Moore. In the match-racing style of the period he contested a small number of long-distance events, winning several valuable matches at Newmarket and elsewhere before being retired to stud.

Stud record

At stud Herod proved transformative. He was leading sire in Great Britain and Ireland eight times between 1777 and 1784, and his sons and daughters dominated racing and breeding. His most important offspring included Highflyer (himself thirteen times champion sire), Woodpecker, and Florizel. The Herod sire line, through Highflyer, became one of the principal branches of the Thoroughbred pedigree, though the direct male line has since become rare compared with that of Eclipse. Herod died in 1780.
