Haleon plc is a British multinational consumer healthcare company with headquarters in Weybridge, Surrey. It was created in July 2022 when GSK (formerly GlaxoSmithKline) demerged its consumer healthcare business into a separately listed company. Haleon is one of the world's largest consumer health businesses, selling oral health, pain relief, vitamins, respiratory and digestive-health products in more than 170 markets.

History

The business combines the consumer healthcare operations of GSK and Pfizer, which were merged into a joint venture in 2019 in which GSK held a controlling stake, along with products originating in Novartis's consumer arm, which GSK had acquired in 2015. In January 2022 GSK disclosed that it had rejected an unsolicited £50 billion bid from Unilever for the division. The demerger was completed on 18 July 2022, with Haleon listing on the London Stock Exchange and the New York Stock Exchange; at debut it was the largest new listing in London for a decade and entered the FTSE 100 index. After the separation, both GSK and Pfizer progressively sold down their retained shareholdings.

Brands

Haleon's portfolio is organised around five categories. Oral health includes Sensodyne, Aquafresh and Polident/Poligrip denture care. Pain relief includes Panadol (paracetamol), Advil (ibuprofen) and Voltaren gels. Its vitamins, minerals and supplements range is led by Centrum multivitamins, Emergen-C and Caltrate. Respiratory brands include Theraflu, Otrivin and Flonase Sensimist allergy relief, acquired in some markets. Digestive health and other brands include ENO antacid, Tums and Nicorette smoking-cessation products in certain territories. The company has described itself as "purpose-driven", emphasising everyday health and health inclusivity, and its name combines "Hale" (an old English word meaning in good health) with "Leon" (associated with strength).

Operations

Haleon employs around 24,000 people and operates research and development centres and manufacturing sites across Europe, the Americas, Africa and Asia. Its chief executive since the demerger has been Brian McNamara, who previously ran the GSK consumer healthcare business.
