MiHoYo

Founded: 13 February 2012, Shanghai, China
Founders: Cai Haoyu, Liu Wei, Luo Yuhao
Industry: Video games
Products: Genshin Impact, Honkai series, Zenless Zone Zero
Brand overseas: HoYoverse

miHoYo Co., Ltd. is a Chinese video game development and publishing company headquartered in Shanghai. Outside mainland China it operates under the brand HoYoverse. The company is best known for the action role-playing game Genshin Impact (2020), one of the highest-grossing mobile games in the world.

History

miHoYo was founded in 2012 by three computer science students at Shanghai Jiao Tong University — Cai Haoyu, Liu Wei and Luo Yuhao — who shared a passion for anime, comics and games (ACG) culture; the company's motto is "Tech Otakus Save the World". Early titles included FlyMe2theMoon and the Honkai series, of which Honkai Impact 3rd (2016) became a major hit in Asia.

Genshin Impact and expansion

Released in September 2020, Genshin Impact, an open-world action RPG with gacha mechanics, earned hundreds of millions of dollars within its first months and billions in its first years, and won wide critical attention for its production values. In 2022 the company launched HoYoverse as its global publishing brand, and in 2023 released the turn-based RPG Honkai: Star Rail, followed in 2024 by the action game Zenless Zone Zero. Other titles include Tears of Themis and the virtual companion Lumi. In 2023 Cai Haoyu stepped down as chairman, with Liu Wei taking a leading role, while Cai focused on research and new projects.
