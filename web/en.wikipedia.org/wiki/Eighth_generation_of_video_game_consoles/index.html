Eighth generation of video game consoles

The eighth generation of video game consoles began in 2012 with the release of Nintendo's Wii U, and was followed in 2013 by Sony's PlayStation 4 and Microsoft's Xbox One. The generation also includes handheld systems such as the Nintendo 3DS (launched in 2011) and Sony's PlayStation Vita, as well as Nintendo's hybrid Nintendo Switch, released in 2017 — a machine whose classification is debated because it also ushered in the following generation. The PlayStation 4 was by far the best-selling home console of the generation, selling more than 117 million units.

Home consoles

Nintendo's Wii U, released in November 2012, featured a GamePad controller with a built-in touchscreen, but confused marketing and weak third-party support limited it to about 13.5 million units. Sony's PlayStation 4 (November 2013) emphasised gaming performance, an x86 architecture friendly to developers, and social sharing features, and it dominated the generation commercially. Microsoft's Xbox One (November 2013) recovered from a troubled launch centred on DRM and an always-required Kinect, and later introduced mid-generation upgrades with the Xbox One X. Sony likewise released the more powerful PlayStation 4 Pro in 2016.

Handhelds and hybrids

The Nintendo 3DS, with its glasses-free stereoscopic display, sold roughly 76 million units across several hardware revisions. The PlayStation Vita, launched in 2011–2012, was critically praised but sold poorly outside Japan. The Nintendo Switch (March 2017), a hybrid home/handheld console, became one of the best-selling consoles of all time, blurring the boundary between the eighth and ninth generations.

Characteristics

The generation saw the consolidation of digital distribution, subscription services such as PlayStation Plus and Xbox Game Pass, streaming and sharing functions built into hardware, and the first mass-market virtual reality headset for consoles, PlayStation VR (2016). Microconsoles and streaming devices, such as the Ouya and Nvidia Shield, also appeared at the margins of the market.
