American Israel Public Affairs Committee

The American Israel Public Affairs Committee (AIPAC) is a United States lobbying group that advocates for pro-Israel policies to Congress and the executive branch. Founded in the 1950s and taking its present name in 1959, it is one of the best known foreign-policy advocacy organisations in Washington.

Activities

AIPAC promotes military aid to Israel, sanctions against shared adversaries, opposition to boycotts, and close strategic cooperation. Its annual policy conference has drawn senior American and Israeli officials, while its legislative work focuses on bipartisan support in Congress. In the 2020s affiliated political action committees and a super PAC began endorsing and spending for or against candidates, a change from the organisation's traditional model of not directly rating candidates.

Debate

Supporters describe AIPAC as an expression of democratic advocacy by American citizens. Critics across the political spectrum argue that it narrows debate over U.S. policy toward Israel and the Palestinians. Its influence is therefore frequently discussed in broader debates about lobbying, campaign finance and American Middle East policy.
