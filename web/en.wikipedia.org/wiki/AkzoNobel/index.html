AkzoNobel

Akzo Nobel N.V.
Type: Public (N.V.)
Industry: Chemicals, paints and coatings
Founded: 1994 (roots to 1792)
Headquarters: Amsterdam, Netherlands
Products: Decorative paints, performance coatings, specialty chemicals (historical)

Akzo Nobel N.V., commonly known as AkzoNobel, is a Dutch multinational company which creates paints and performance coatings for both industry and consumers worldwide. Headquartered in Amsterdam, the company has activities in more than 80 countries and employs about 33,000 people. Its brand portfolio includes Dulux, International, Sikkens and Interpon.

History

AkzoNobel has a long and varied history. The company can trace its roots to 1646, when Bofors was founded in Sweden, and to companies such as the Swedish explosives firm founded by Alfred Nobel. The modern company was formed in 1994 by the merger of Akzo and Nobel Industrier. Akzo itself was formed in 1969 by the merger of Algemene Kunstzijde Unie (AKU) and Koninklijke Zout Organon (KZO).

In 2008, AkzoNobel acquired the British company Imperial Chemical Industries (ICI), bringing the Dulux paint brand into the group. In 2018, the company sold its Specialty Chemicals business to the Carlyle Group for €10.1 billion, sharpening its focus on paints and coatings.

Operations

AkzoNobel is one of the world's largest manufacturers of paints and coatings. Its products range from decorative paints sold to homeowners, to protective and marine coatings, powder coatings, and vehicle refinish paints. The company is a constituent of the AEX index on Euronext Amsterdam.
