List of chancellors of Austria

The Chancellor of Austria is the head of government of the Republic of Austria. The office was established in 1918 with the founding of the republic. The list below covers the First Republic (1918–1938) and the Second Republic (1945–present); between 1938 and 1945 Austria was annexed into Nazi Germany (Anschluss) and had no chancellor of its own.
