Kōtō (Japanese: 江東区, Kōtō-ku) is a special ward of Tokyo, Japan. Located east of the Sumida River between Tokyo Bay and the Arakawa River, it is a largely waterfront district of canals, reclaimed land, and major event venues.

Geography and districts

The ward includes the older neighbourhoods of Fukagawa and Kameido as well as extensive newer reclaimed-land areas such as Toyosu, Ariake, and Odaiba-adjacent waterfronts. The Toyosu Market, which replaced the Tsukiji fish market in 2018, is located here, and Ariake hosted several venues for the Tokyo 2020 Olympic and Paralympic Games, including the Ariake Arena and the Tokyo Aquatics Centre.

History

Kōtō ward was formed in 1947 by the merger of Fukagawa and Jōtō wards. Its low-lying land has historically been prone to flooding, and the ward contains many temples and shrines, including Tomioka Hachimangū, famous for its summer festival, and the Fukagawa Edo Museum.
