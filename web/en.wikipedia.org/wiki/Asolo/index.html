Asolo

Country: Italy

Region: Veneto

Province: Treviso

Population: approx. 9,000

Asolo is a town and comune in the province of Treviso, in the Veneto region of northern Italy. Set among hills at the foot of the Dolomites foothills, it is known as "the pearl of the province of Treviso" and has long been a retreat for artists and writers.

History

Asolo was a settlement of the Veneti and later the Roman town of Acelum. In the Middle Ages it was held by the Ezzelini and then by Venice. In 1489 the Republic of Venice granted Asolo to Caterina Cornaro, the former queen of Cyprus, whose court there attracted figures such as Pietro Bembo, who set his dialogue Gli Asolani (1505) at her court. The town's castle and the Rocca fortress (12th–13th century) stand above the old centre.

Notable residents

  • Caterina Cornaro (1454–1510), last queen of Cyprus, held court here.
  • Eleonora Duse (1858–1924), the celebrated actress, is buried in Asolo's cemetery.
  • Robert Browning set Asolando (1889), his last book of poems, here.
  • Freya Stark (1893–1993), travel writer, made Asolo her home.
  • The composer Gian Francesco Malipiero lived in Asolo.

Sights

Sights include the cathedral with Lorenzo Lotto's Assumption, the castle of Caterina Cornaro with its theatre named for Eleonora Duse, the civic museum, and the villa of Freya Stark.
