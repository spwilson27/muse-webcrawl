List of Mexican heads of state

The head of state of Mexico is the President of the United Mexican States, who is also head of government and commander-in-chief of the armed forces. The following is a list of the individuals who have held the highest office in Mexico since independence from Spain in 1821, including the emperors of the First and Second Mexican Empires and the presidents of the republic.
