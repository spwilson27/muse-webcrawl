Crysis 2

Crysis 2 is a first-person shooter video game developed by Crytek and published by Electronic Arts in March 2011 for Microsoft Windows, PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360. It is the second main instalment in the Crysis series and the first to use the CryEngine 3.

Plot

Set in 2023, three years after the first game, the story follows Alcatraz, a Force Recon Marine whose life is saved by the dying Prophet, who gives him the Nanosuit 2.0. New York City is under martial law amid an outbreak of the alien "Manhattan virus" and an invasion by the Ceph; the private military company CELL, under Crynet Systems, hunts Alcatraz for the suit. Alcatraz ultimately uses the suit to disperse a biological agent that destroys the Ceph in the city.

Gameplay and reception

The gameplay refines the original's formula with a more urban, vertical environment: the Nanosuit's armour and stealth modes are toggled alongside power jumps, sliding, and ledge grabs. Critics praised the visuals, suit gameplay and Hans Zimmer and Borislav Slavov's score, though some PC players criticised the narrower level design compared with the first game. A remastered version was released in 2021. See also Crysis 3.
