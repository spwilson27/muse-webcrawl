House of X and Powers of X

House of X and Powers of X (pronounced "Powers of Ten") are two interconnected American comic book limited series published by Marvel Comics in 2019. Written by Jonathan Hickman with art by Pepe Larraz, R. B. Silva and Marte Gracia, the twin six-issue series relaunched the X-Men line and established the "Krakoan Age", a new status quo in which Marvel's mutants found a sovereign nation-state on the living island of Krakoa.

Plot

House of X depicts Professor Charles Xavier's plan to secure mutantkind's future: the establishment of Krakoa as a mutant homeland offering amnesty to all mutants, resurrection through the combined powers of five mutants ("The Five") overseen by Cerebro's backups of mutant minds, and diplomatic leverage through Krakoan miracle drugs that extend human life. Powers of X interleaves four timelines — Year One, Year Ten, Year One Hundred and Year One Thousand — centred on the revelations that Moira MacTaggert is a mutant whose power is reincarnation with memory, and that every mutant future risks ending in machine ascendance via the Phalanx and the anti-mutant organisation Orchis.

Publication and reception

The series ran from July to October 2019 and concluded with the two series' final issues interlocking into a single narrative. Both were critical and commercial successes, praised for re-conceptualising the X-Men franchise around mutant culture, language and nationhood. They launched the "Dawn of X" line of titles written under Hickman's direction as Head of X.
