Beta Pictoris

Beta Pictoris (β Pic) is the second-brightest star in the constellation Pictor, about 63 light-years from the Solar System. Only about 20 million years old, it is famous for its large disk of dust and gas — a protoplanetary / debris disk — and its directly imaged exoplanets.

Beta Pictoris
Constellation: Pictor
Apparent magnitude: 3.86
Distance: ~63 ly
Spectral type: A6V
Age: ~20 million years
Planets: Beta Pictoris b and c

The disk and planets

An infrared excess detected by IRAS in 1983 revealed the disk, the first debris disk imaged around a star. The giant planet Beta Pictoris b, about 13 Jupiter masses orbiting at ~9 AU, was directly imaged in 2008; a second planet, Beta Pictoris c, was announced in 2019. Falling evaporating bodies (exocomets) are also observed.
