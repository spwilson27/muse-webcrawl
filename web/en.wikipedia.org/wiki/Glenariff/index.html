Glenariff

LocationCounty Antrim, Northern Ireland
RiverGlenariff River

Glenariff (from Irish Gleann Aireamh, "glen of the ploughman") is one of the Glens of Antrim in County Antrim, Northern Ireland. It is sometimes called the "Queen of the Glens" and was celebrated in song by the poet William Thackeray, who compared its scenery to Switzerland.

Geography

The glen runs from the Antrim Plateau down to the sea at Waterfoot (Glenariff village). The Glenariff River flows through a steep gorge in Glenariff Forest Park, where a waymarked waterfall walk passes a series of falls including the Ess-na-Larach waterfall.

Forest park

Glenariff Forest Park covers about 1,185 hectares and is managed as a national nature reserve in its river gorge section. The park's trails, opened in their scenic form in the nineteenth century, are among the most visited attractions on the Antrim coast.
