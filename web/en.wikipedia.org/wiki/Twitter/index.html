Twitter, officially renamed X since 2023, is an American social networking service for short posts originally limited to 140 characters, later 280. It was created in 2006 within the podcast company Odeo and spun out as its own company.

Elon Musk acquired Twitter in October 2022 for about US$44 billion, as widely reported, took it private, and in 2023 rebranded it to X, replacing the bird logo. The service remains influential in news, politics and live events, and has been debated over moderation, verification changes and advertiser relations since the takeover.

Features

Posts (formerly tweets), replies, reposts/retweets, likes, hashtags, trending topics, Spaces audio rooms, long-form posts and video for paying subscribers, and the Premium subscription tier that replaced much of the old verification system.
