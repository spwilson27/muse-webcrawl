Nathaniel Woodard

OccupationMusician; keyboardist and organist
GenresGospel, R&B, jazz, soul
InstrumentsHammond organ, piano, keyboards
BaseRichmond, Virginia, United States

Nathaniel Woodard is an American gospel keyboardist and organist, widely regarded among church musicians as a master of the Hammond organ in the African-American gospel tradition. Based in Richmond, Virginia, he has served for decades as a church musician and has accompanied numerous gospel choirs and artists.

Career

Woodard built his reputation within the church music community of Virginia and the wider gospel world, where his command of traditional hymns, quartets and contemporary gospel arranging made him a sought-after accompanist and musical director. Recordings and broadcasts of his playing, including widely shared performances of classic gospel repertoire from behind the organ console, have circulated among musicians studying the gospel organ style, and he is frequently cited by younger keyboardists as an influence on their approach to service playing and improvisation.

Style

His playing is characterised by the idioms of the Black gospel organ tradition: rich Hammond drawbar registrations, expressive use of the Leslie speaker, hymn harmonisations enriched with passing chords, and the driving "shout" rhythms used to accompany preaching and congregational singing.
