Electric Global Modular Platform

The Electric Global Modular Platform (E-GMP) is a dedicated battery-electric vehicle platform developed by Hyundai Motor Group for use across its Hyundai, Kia and Genesis brands. Introduced in 2020, it underpins a family of vehicles beginning with the Hyundai Ioniq 5, Kia EV6 and Genesis GV60.

Design

E-GMP uses a flat "skateboard" layout with the battery pack mounted low in the floor, giving a long wheelbase, short overhangs and a flat cabin floor. Its 800-volt architecture supports ultra-fast DC charging, allowing a 10–80% charge in about 18 minutes under suitable conditions, and it supports vehicle-to-load (V2L) bidirectional power output. Rear-wheel drive is standard, with an optional front motor for all-wheel drive. The platform integrates the battery, motor and inverter into a compact drive unit and is designed to span segments from compact cars to larger SUVs.

Vehicles

Models on E-GMP include the Hyundai Ioniq 5, Ioniq 6 and Ioniq 9, the Kia EV3, EV6 and EV9, and the Genesis GV60 and electrified GV70/G80 derivatives use related technology. A successor high-volume architecture, eM, was announced for later models.
