Allianz SE is a German multinational financial services company headquartered in Munich. Founded in 1890 in Berlin by Carl von Thieme and Wilhelm von Finck, it is one of the world's largest insurance groups and asset managers, serving more than 120 million customers in nearly 70 countries.

Operations

Allianz operates through property-casualty insurance, life and health insurance, and asset management segments. Its asset management division includes PIMCO and Allianz Global Investors, which together manage several trillion euros. The company also owns Euler Hermes (Allianz Trade), a leading credit insurer.

History

Allianz moved its headquarters to Munich in 1949. It expanded internationally through acquisitions, including RAS in Italy, AGF in France, and Fireman's Fund in the United States. In 2006 it converted to a Societas Europaea (SE). Allianz is a component of the DAX and Euro Stoxx 50 indices and sponsors the Allianz Arena football stadium in Munich.
