New World is a massively multiplayer online role-playing game (MMORPG) developed by Amazon Games Orange County and released by Amazon Games in September 2021. Set on the fictional supernatural island of Aeternum in the 17th century, players explore, gather resources, craft and fight for territory.

Gameplay

Players wash up on Aeternum, an island infused with a magical substance called Azoth, and choose among three factions — Marauders, Syndicate and Covenant — that compete to control settlements. Combat is action-based and classless, with character builds determined by weapons and attributes.

The game features player-driven crafting and trading, expeditions (dungeons), large-scale 50-versus-50 territory wars and open-world player-versus-player combat.

Reception and development

New World launched to very large player numbers, peaking at over 900,000 concurrent players on Steam, and generally favourable-to-mixed reviews praising its visuals and combat while criticising repetitive quests and endgame content. Its population later declined sharply, and Amazon released expansions including Brimstone Sands (2022) and Rise of the Angry Earth (2023).

In 2024 the game was relaunched in updated form as New World: Aeternum, including versions for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.
