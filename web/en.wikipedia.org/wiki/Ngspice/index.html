Ngspice

Ngspice is a free, open-source mixed-level/mixed-signal circuit simulator. It is based on three open-source packages: Spice3f5, originally from UC Berkeley, Cider1b1 for device simulation, and Xspice for mixed-signal and event-driven simulation.

Features

Ngspice performs DC, AC, transient, noise, and other analyses of analogue and digital circuits described by netlists. It includes models for transistors, diodes, and other components, supports Verilog-A models via OpenVAF, co-simulation interfaces, and a shared-library mode allowing it to be embedded in other applications. It is the simulation engine used by the KiCad EDA suite.

History and licensing

The project began in 1999 to modernise and maintain SPICE3 for modern operating systems. It is maintained by a volunteer team and released under open-source licences (chiefly BSD-style terms inherited from its components), running on Linux, Windows, and macOS.
