Reverse-Flash

The Reverse-Flash is the name of several supervillains appearing in DC Comics, each an archenemy of a different incarnation of the Flash. The best known is Eobard Thawne, also called Professor Zoom, the nemesis of Barry Allen. Other Reverse-Flashes include Hunter Zolomon (Zoom), the enemy of Wally West, and Daniel West.

Eobard Thawne

Created by John Broome and Carmine Infantino, Thawne first appeared in The Flash #139 (1963). A scientist from the 25th century obsessed with Barry Allen, he replicated the accident that gave Allen super-speed, and after discovering he was destined to become the Flash's greatest enemy, embraced the role. He wears a yellow inversion of the Flash's costume. Thawne is infamous in the comics for murdering Barry Allen's mother — an act central to the 2011 Flashpoint storyline — and for killing Allen's wife Iris West. His powers derive from the "Negative Speed Force", and his existence is described as a living paradox, making him notoriously difficult to kill permanently.

In other media

Thawne, played by Tom Cavanagh and Matt Letscher, is the principal antagonist of the first season of the television series The Flash (2014), and versions of the character have appeared across the Arrowverse and animated DC productions.
