Mobipocket

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia


An e-book in Mobipocket Reader

Mobipocket was a French company founded in 2000 that produced software and a file format for e-books. Its Mobipocket format (files with the .mobi or .prc extension) became one of the most widely used e-book formats of the 2000s, and the company's Mobipocket Reader software ran on PDAs, smartphones and desktop computers.

History

Mobipocket was founded in Paris by Thierry Brethes and Nathalie Ting. The format was based on the Open eBook standard, using XHTML markup with optional digital rights management. In 2005, Amazon acquired Mobipocket. Amazon's Kindle devices, launched in 2007, used a closely related format (AZW) derived from Mobipocket, and for many years Kindle books were essentially Mobipocket files with Amazon's DRM.

Publishers could produce Mobipocket titles with the free Mobipocket Creator tool, and the format supported features such as hyperlinks, images, tables of contents and JavaScript-like interactivity on some devices. Support gradually declined as Amazon moved the Kindle ecosystem to the KF8/AZW3 and later KFX formats; in 2022 Amazon's "Send to Kindle" service dropped support for sending MOBI files.
