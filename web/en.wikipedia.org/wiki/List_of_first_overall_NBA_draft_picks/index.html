List of first overall NBA draft picks

The first overall pick in the NBA draft is selected by the team that wins the NBA draft lottery (introduced in 1985). Many first picks have become Hall of Famers, including Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Magic Johnson, Shaquille O'Neal, Tim Duncan, and LeBron James.

Notes

Before the lottery era, the first pick alternated between the worst teams of each division, with a coin flip used from 1966. The Cleveland Cavaliers have held the first pick a record six times. No first overall pick has ever been selected from a high school outside the United States and Europe's professional leagues in the modern era, though several international players have been chosen first overall.
