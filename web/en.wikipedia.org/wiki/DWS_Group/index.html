DWS Group (DWS Group GmbH & Co. KGaA) is a German asset management company headquartered in Frankfurt am Main. It operates as a largely standalone entity controlled by Deutsche Bank, which retained a majority stake following DWS's initial public offering on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange in March 2018.

History

DWS traces its history to 1956, when it was founded as the Deutsche Gesellschaft für Wertpapiersparen. It grew into one of Europe's largest fund managers, best known for its retail mutual fund business under the DWS Investments brand. In 2012, Deutsche Bank integrated its asset and wealth management divisions, and in 2017 announced the partial IPO of the unit under the DWS name.

Business

DWS manages assets across active strategies (equities, fixed income, multi-asset), passive products such as Xtrackers exchange-traded funds, and alternatives including real estate and infrastructure. It serves retail and institutional clients worldwide, with assets under management of roughly €900 billion in the mid-2020s.

Controversies

In 2021, whistleblower allegations accused DWS of overstating its use of ESG criteria in investment products. German and US regulators investigated; in 2023 DWS agreed to pay $19 million to settle US SEC charges relating to ESG misstatements, and €10.5 million in Germany in 2025.
