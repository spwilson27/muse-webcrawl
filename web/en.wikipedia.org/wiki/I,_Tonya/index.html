I, Tonya

I, Tonya is a 2017 American biographical sports film directed by Craig Gillespie and written by Steven Rogers. It follows the life of American figure skater Tonya Harding and her connection to the 1994 attack on her rival Nancy Kerrigan. The film stars Margot Robbie as Harding, Sebastian Stan as her ex-husband Jeff Gillooly, and Allison Janney as her mother LaVona Golden.

Plot

Framed by mockumentary interviews, the film traces Harding's rise from a rough childhood in Portland, Oregon, through becoming the first American woman to land a triple Axel in competition, to the infamous attack on Kerrigan planned by Gillooly and Shawn Eckardt. It ends with Harding's lifetime ban from figure skating and her later careers in boxing and other work.

Reception

The film premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival and was widely praised for the performances of Robbie and Janney and for its sympathetic yet unreliable portrait of Harding. Janney won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress, and the film also received Oscar nominations for Best Actress and Best Film Editing.
