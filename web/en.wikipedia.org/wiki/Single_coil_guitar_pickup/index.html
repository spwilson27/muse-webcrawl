Single coil guitar pickup

TypeMagnetic pickup
Used onFender Stratocaster, Telecaster
Known forBright tone, mains hum

A single-coil pickup is a type of magnetic transducer for electric guitars and basses consisting of a single coil of wire wrapped around a set of pole pieces or a bar magnet. It converts string vibration into an electrical signal and is known for its bright, clear and articulate tone.

Design

In classic designs such as those on the Fender Stratocaster and Telecaster, six alnico magnet pole pieces sit beneath the strings inside a coil of thousands of turns of fine copper wire. The vibrating steel string disturbs the magnetic field, inducing a voltage in the coil.

Sound and noise

Single coils produce a crisp, bell-like sound with strong treble response, favoured in country, funk, blues and surf music. Their main drawback is susceptibility to electromagnetic hum from mains wiring and lighting, a problem the dual-coil humbucker was invented to solve.

Variants

Stacked and rail designs fit hum-cancelling coils into a single-coil footprint, while P-90 pickups use a wider, shorter coil for a fatter, more midrange-heavy sound.
