Yasumi Matsuno (松野 泰己, born 24 October 1965) is a Japanese video game designer and writer known for creating the fictional world of Ivalice and for strategy role-playing games noted for their political themes and mature storytelling.

Career

Matsuno began his career at Quest Corporation, where he directed Ogre Battle (1993) and Tactics Ogre (1995). He joined Square in 1995 and directed Final Fantasy Tactics (1997), Vagrant Story (2000), and initially Final Fantasy XII (2006), stepping down during its production for health reasons. He later worked with Level-5 and founded his own studio, Algebra Factory. He returned to collaborate with Square Enix on content for Final Fantasy XIV, including the "Return to Ivalice" raids and the Save the Queen storyline.

Style

His work draws on historical and political conflicts — the Wars of the Roses, the Yugoslav wars, and medieval European succession disputes — and frequently features morally ambiguous protagonists and church or state conspiracies.
