Honbasho

A honbasho (本場所) is an official professional sumo tournament. Since 1958, six honbasho have been held each year: three in Tokyo (January, May and September) and one each in Osaka (March), Nagoya (July) and Fukuoka (November). Each tournament lasts fifteen days.

Format

Wrestlers in the top two divisions — makuuchi and jūryō — fight one bout per day, fifteen in total. Lower-division wrestlers fight seven bouts. The wrestler with the best record in each division wins the championship (yūshō); ties are resolved by playoffs on the final day, known as senshūraku.

Significance

Results in honbasho determine the official rankings (banzuke) published before each tournament. Performance in the top division can lead to promotion to the champion ranks of ōzeki and yokozuna. Only honbasho results count toward official career records; exhibition tournaments (jungyō) do not.

History

The six-tournament system was established in 1958, when the January tournament in Tokyo was added. Earlier systems varied in number and format, and tournaments were shortened during wartime.
