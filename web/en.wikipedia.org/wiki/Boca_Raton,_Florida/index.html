Boca Raton, Florida

Boca Raton is a city in Palm Beach County, Florida, United States. It was first incorporated on August 2, 1924, as "Bocaratone", and then incorporated as "Boca Raton" on May 26, 1925. Its population was 97,422 in the 2020 census. The name Boca Ratón means "mouse mouth" in Spanish, likely referring to the shape of the inlet or to submerged rocks in the area.

History

The area was sparsely settled until the Florida land boom of the 1920s, when architect Addison Mizner arrived and designed the resort town in the Mediterranean Revival style. During the Second World War the Boca Raton Army Air Field trained radar operators. After the war, IBM established a major facility there, where its personal computer was developed in 1981.

Geography and economy

Boca Raton sits on Florida's Atlantic coast, between Delray Beach and Deerfield Beach. It is home to Florida Atlantic University, Lynn University, extensive office parks, and affluent gated communities.
