Maritime Mobile Service Identity

A Maritime Mobile Service Identity (MMSI) is a nine-digit number that uniquely identifies a ship station, coast station, or other maritime radio user. MMSIs are administered nationally under the framework of the International Telecommunication Union.

Format and use

The first three digits are the Maritime Identification Digits (MID), indicating the country of registration. MMSIs are transmitted by Digital Selective Calling on VHF and MF/HF radios and by the Automatic Identification System (AIS), allowing rescuers and other vessels to identify a station and retrieve its details from registration databases. Distinct formats exist for ship stations, group calls, coast stations, search-and-rescue aircraft, and navigational aids.
