Purple Mountain (Zijin Shan)
LocationEastern Nanjing, Jiangsu, China
Elevation448.9 m (North Peak)
SitesSun Yat-sen Mausoleum, Ming Xiaoling, Purple Mountain Observatory

Purple Mountain (Chinese: 紫金山, Zijin Shan, "Purple-Gold Mountain") is a mountain in eastern Nanjing, Jiangsu province, China, named for the purple and golden clouds said to gather around its summit.

Sites

The densely wooded mountain is one of China's most historically significant scenic areas. It contains the Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum of the Hongwu Emperor, founder of the Ming dynasty, the Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum, the Linggu Temple, and the Purple Mountain Observatory, founded in 1934 and important in Chinese astronomy. The area is a national scenic and historic site visited by millions.
