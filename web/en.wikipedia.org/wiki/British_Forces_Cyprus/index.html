British Forces Cyprus

GarrisonAkrotiri and Dhekelia Sovereign Base Areas, Cyprus
RoleUK military presence in the Eastern Mediterranean
Personnel~2,300 service personnel plus civilian staff and dependants
Major unitsRAF Akrotiri; resident infantry battalion; Episkopi garrison

British Forces Cyprus (BFC) is the name given to the British Armed Forces stationed in the UK Sovereign Base Areas of Akrotiri and Dhekelia on the island of Cyprus and at related facilities in the Republic of Cyprus. The bases were retained by Britain under the 1960 Treaty of Establishment when Cyprus became independent, and together cover about 254 km² (98 sq mi), roughly 3% of the island.

Role

Cyprus occupies a strategic position in the Eastern Mediterranean, close to the Middle East. British Forces Cyprus provides a forward operating base and staging post for operations in the region, including air operations flown from RAF Akrotiri — a large airfield on the western Akrotiri Peninsula — and supports intelligence facilities such as the listening station at Ayios Nikolaos in the Dhekelia area. The bases also serve as a location for acclimatisation, training and recuperation, including the Bloodhound Camp adventure training facility in the Troodos Mountains.

Organisation

The garrison includes a resident infantry battalion, Royal Navy, Royal Air Force and supporting units, the Cyprus Joint Police Unit, and the Sovereign Base Areas administration, customs and police, which govern the territories separately from the military command. The commander of British Forces Cyprus also serves as administrator of the Sovereign Base Areas. British personnel and their families live in the garrisons of Episkopi and Dhekelia, with schools, medical facilities and other amenities.

History

British forces have been present in Cyprus since Britain took over administration of the island from the Ottoman Empire in 1878. During the 1950s emergency against EOKA the island hosted large forces; after independence in 1960 the garrison contracted into the two Sovereign Base Areas. The bases were used intensively during the 1974 crisis, the Gulf wars and operations over Libya, Iraq and Syria in the 2010s.
