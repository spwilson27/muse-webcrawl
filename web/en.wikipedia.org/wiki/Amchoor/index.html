Amchoor

Amchoor or aamchur (Hindi: आमचूर), also called mango powder, is a fruity spice powder made from dried unripe green mangoes. It is used in Indian cuisine as a citrus-like souring agent, particularly in North Indian dishes, chutneys, chaat, marinades, and vegetable curries, where it adds tartness without additional liquid.

Production

To prepare amchoor, unripe mangoes are harvested while still green, peeled, sliced thinly, and dried in the sun until brittle. The dried slices are sold whole or ground into a pale beige powder, sometimes with a small amount of turmeric to inhibit pests. Because ripe mangoes are too sweet and moist, only tart, firm green fruit is used.

Culinary use

Amchoor is a key ingredient of chaat masala and is sprinkled over snacks such as pakoras, samosas, and fruit chaat. It is used in chole (chickpea curry), stuffed vegetables, and lentil dishes, and as a tenderising, sour component in marinades for tandoori meats and kebabs. Outside India it serves as a shelf-stable substitute for lemon juice or tamarind in dry spice blends.

Properties

The powder retains much of the sour fruit acids of the green mango and modest amounts of vitamins A and C. Its acidity is milder and fruitier than that of vinegar or citrus juice.
