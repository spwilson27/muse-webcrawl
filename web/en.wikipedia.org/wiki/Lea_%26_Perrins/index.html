Lea & Perrins

Lea & Perrins is a United Kingdom-based food manufacturer, best known as the maker of Worcestershire sauce. Founded in Worcester in 1837 by chemists John Wheeley Lea and William Henry Perrins, the company is now owned by Kraft Heinz.

History

Lea and Perrins, who ran a pharmacy in Worcester, devised the fermented sauce reportedly from a recipe brought from Bengal. After finding a batch aged and improved in their cellar, they began commercial production. The sauce became a global export and an ingredient in dishes and drinks such as the Bloody Mary.

Worcestershire sauce

The original recipe includes vinegar, molasses, sugar, salt, anchovies, tamarind extract, onions and garlic. The exact formula remains a trade secret. See Worcestershire sauce.
