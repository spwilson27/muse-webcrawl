FA Vase

The FA Vase, officially the Isuzu FA Vase for sponsorship reasons, is an annual English football tournament for semi-professional clubs playing at levels 9 and 10 of the English football league system (Steps 5 and 6 of the National League System).

History

The competition was founded in 1974 after the FA abolished the distinction between amateur and professional players, ending the FA Amateur Cup. In its first season, 1974–75, Hoddesdon Town won the inaugural final, played at Wembley. The final returned permanently to the new Wembley Stadium in 2007, and since 2016 it has shared "Non-League Finals Day" with the FA Trophy final.

Eligibility

Clubs in the lower levels of the National League System enter the Vase, while higher-level non-League clubs enter the FA Trophy. More than 500 clubs typically enter each season.

Notable records

Whitley Bay won the Vase three years running between 2009 and 2011, a record run of consecutive victories. Billericay Town won the competition three times in the 1970s.
