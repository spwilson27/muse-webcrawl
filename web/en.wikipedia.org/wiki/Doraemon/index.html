Doraemon

Manga1969–1996 (45 volumes)
Anime series1973; 1979–2005; 2005–present
GenreComedy, science fiction, slice of life

Doraemon (Japanese: ドラえもん) is a Japanese manga and anime series created by Fujiko Fujio (the pen name of Hiroshi Fujimoto, writing solo as Fujiko F. Fujio after 1987). It tells the story of Doraemon, a blue robotic cat from the 22nd century who travels back in time to aid Nobita Nobi, a kind-hearted but hapless schoolboy. Using futuristic gadgets pulled from his four-dimensional pocket — such as the Anywhere Door, the Take-copter (bamboo helicopter), and the Time Machine — Doraemon tries to steer Nobita toward a better future, usually with comically mixed results.

Doraemon is one of the best-selling manga series of all time and a cultural icon across Asia.

Premise

In the 22nd century, Nobita's descendant Sewashi sends the robot cat Doraemon back in time to improve Nobita's life: as an adult, Nobita is destined for business failure, debt, and an unhappy marriage. The episodic stories typically begin with Nobita facing a problem — bullies Gian and Suneo, schoolwork, or his crush Shizuka — borrowing a gadget from Doraemon, and misusing it until the plan backfires. Doraemon, who lost his ears to robot mice, is terrified of mice and loves the sweet snack dorayaki.

Media and legacy

The manga ran from 1969 to 1996 and sold over 170 million copies worldwide. Three anime series (1973, 1979, and 2005) and more than 40 animated feature films have been produced, including the 3D film Stand by Me Doraemon (2014). In 2008, Japan's Foreign Ministry appointed Doraemon as the country's first "anime ambassador." Doraemon is especially beloved across East and Southeast Asia, and has been inducted into popular culture in China, Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia, and India.
