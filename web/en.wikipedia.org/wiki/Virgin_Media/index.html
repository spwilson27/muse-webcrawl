Virgin Media

Virgin Media is a British telecommunications company providing telephone, television and internet services in the United Kingdom. Since June 2021 its services have been operated by Virgin Media O2, a 50:50 joint venture between Liberty Global and Telefónica that merged Virgin Media with the mobile network O2.

History

The company was formed in 2006–2007 from the merger of cable operators NTL and Telewest, together with Virgin Mobile UK, and adopted the Virgin brand under licence from Richard Branson's Virgin Group. It operated the UK's principal cable network, built largely during the 1990s cable boom, and became known for high-speed DOCSIS broadband marketed under the slogan of fibre-optic speed. Liberty Global acquired Virgin Media in 2013 in a deal valued at about $23 billion.

The Virgin Media O2 merger, cleared in 2021, combined the cable network with O2's mobile business to create one of Britain's largest telecoms groups, competing with BT/EE and Vodafone. The company has been upgrading its cable footprint to full-fibre (XGS-PON) technology and has wholesale and network-expansion ventures aimed at extending gigabit broadband coverage.
