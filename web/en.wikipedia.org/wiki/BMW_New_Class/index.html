BMW New Class

BMW New Class
German: Neue Klasse
Production: 1962–1977
Manufacturer: BMW
Successor themes: BMW 3 Series, 5 Series

The BMW New Class (German: Neue Klasse) was a line of sedans and coupés produced from 1962 to 1977 that rescued BMW from near-bankruptcy and established the company's modern identity as a maker of premium sports sedans.

History

Launched with the BMW 1500 at the 1961 Frankfurt Motor Show, the range grew to include the 1600, 1800 and 2000 saloons, the 2000C/CS coupés and the compact two-door 02 Series (1602, 2002). The 2002, especially in turbo and tii forms, became an icon and direct ancestor of the 3 Series.

Legacy

The name was revived in the 2020s for BMW's Neue Klasse electric vehicle platform, debuting with the iX3 and i3, citing the original range's role as a company-defining reinvention.
