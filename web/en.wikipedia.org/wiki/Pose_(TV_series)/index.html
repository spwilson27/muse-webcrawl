Pose (TV series)

Pose

Genre: Drama
Created by: Ryan Murphy, Brad Falchuk, Steven Canals
Network: FX
Original run: 2018–2021
Seasons: 3; Episodes: 26

Pose (stylized as POSE) is an American drama television series about New York City's ballroom culture scene in the 1980s and early 1990s. Created by Ryan Murphy, Brad Falchuk and Steven Canals, it premiered on FX on 3 June 2018 and concluded on 6 June 2021 after three seasons.

Premise

Set beginning in 1987, the series follows Blanca Rodriguez (Mj Rodriguez), a trans woman who founds her own "house" — the House of Evangelista — taking in homeless LGBTQ youth including the dancer Damon and the sex worker Angel. The houses compete in balls judged on dance (including voguing), costume and "realness" categories, presided over by emcee Pray Tell (Billy Porter), while the characters face the HIV/AIDS crisis, poverty and discrimination.

Cast and significance

The series featured the largest cast of transgender actors in series-regular roles in television history, including Michaela Jaé Rodriguez, Indya Moore, Dominique Jackson, Hailie Sahar and Angelica Ross. Billy Porter won the 2019 Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama Series — the first openly gay Black man to win in that category — and Rodriguez became the first trans woman nominated for a lead acting Emmy in 2021.

Reception

Pose was widely praised by critics for its performances, writing and celebration of ballroom culture, which had earlier been documented in the film Paris Is Burning (1990).
