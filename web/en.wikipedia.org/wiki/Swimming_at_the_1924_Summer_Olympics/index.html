Swimming at the 1924 Summer Olympics

At the 1924 Summer Olympics in Paris, eleven swimming events were contested: six for men and five for women. The competitions were held from Sunday 13 July to Sunday 20 July 1924 at the Piscine des Tourelles, a newly built outdoor pool in the 20th arrondissement of Paris — the first Olympic swimming venue to be a purpose-built pool with marked 50-metre lanes.

Highlights

The United States dominated the medal table. Johnny Weissmuller won three gold medals (100 m freestyle, 400 m freestyle, and the 4 × 200 m freestyle relay) plus a bronze in water polo; he set an Olympic record of 59.0 seconds in the 100 m freestyle, becoming the first person to swim the distance in under a minute at the Olympics. His teammate Duke Kahanamoku, the defending champion, took silver, and Duke's younger brother Samuel took bronze. In the women's events, Ethel Lackie won the 100 m freestyle, and Lucy Morton of Great Britain won the 200 m breaststroke, becoming the first British woman to win an Olympic swimming title. A total of 169 swimmers from 23 nations competed.
