The Astronomical Journal (AJ) is a peer-reviewed monthly scientific journal published by the American Astronomical Society. Founded in 1849 by Benjamin Apthorp Gould, it is one of the oldest astronomy journals in the world; publication was suspended during the American Civil War and resumed in 1886.

Scope

The journal publishes papers on observational and theoretical astronomy, with an emphasis on work connected to observations — astrometry, celestial mechanics, exoplanets, stellar and galactic astronomy — complementing the more theory-oriented Astrophysical Journal.

History

Gould modelled the journal on the European Astronomische Nachrichten. Editorship passed through Benjamin Peirce and later to Yale and other observatories before the journal came under the American Astronomical Society in the 1940s.
