1976 French Open

The 1976 French Open was a tennis tournament that took place on the outdoor clay courts at the Stade Roland Garros in Paris, France, from 31 May to 13 June 1976.

Champions

Italy's Adriano Panatta won the men's singles title, defeating Harold Solomon in the final; it remains Italy's only French Open men's singles title of the Open Era. Britain's Sue Barker won the women's singles, beating Renáta Tomanová in the final. It was Barker's only Grand Slam singles title.

Context

Defending champion Björn Borg was eliminated in the quarterfinals by Panatta—the only player to beat Borg at Roland Garros, doing so twice. Panatta followed his Paris triumph with victory at the Italian Open the same season.
