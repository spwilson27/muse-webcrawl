The Ford Bronco is a model line of sport utility vehicles manufactured and marketed by Ford. The first SUV model developed by the company, five generations of the Bronco were produced from 1966 to 1996, followed by a sixth generation launched for the 2021 model year after a 25-year hiatus.

History

Introduced for 1966 as a compact off-road vehicle competing with the Jeep CJ-5 and International Scout, the original Bronco was offered as a roadster, wagon or half-cab pickup, famously rugged and popularised in desert racing at Baja. For 1978 the Bronco grew into a full-size SUV based on the F-Series pickup, a format it kept through further generations in 1980, 1987 and 1992 until production ended in 1996, when it was replaced by the four-door Expedition. A white 1993 Bronco became notorious as the vehicle of the O. J. Simpson slow-speed chase in 1994.

The revived 2021 Bronco returned to a mid-size, body-on-frame design with removable doors and roof, turbocharged engines and extensive off-road equipment, alongside the smaller crossover-based Bronco Sport.
