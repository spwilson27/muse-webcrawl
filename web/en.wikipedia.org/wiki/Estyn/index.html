Full titleHis Majesty's Inspectorate for Education and Training in Wales

Estyn is the office of His Majesty's Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales. It is a Crown body, and though independent of the Welsh Government it is funded by it under Section 104 of the Government of Wales Act 1998. Estyn inspects quality and standards in education and training providers in Wales, and provides advice to the Welsh Government on educational standards.

Remit

Estyn inspects maintained and independent schools, pupil referral units, further education colleges, work-based learning, adult community learning, local authority education services, teacher education and training, and Welsh-medium and Welsh for Adults provision. It also inspects some education provision in prisons and other settings in conjunction with other inspectorates.

Inspection framework

Inspections judge providers across inspection areas including learning, well-being and attitudes to learning, teaching and learning experiences, care, support and guidance, and leadership and management. Since a framework reform, inspection reports use summative judgements and identify providers causing significant concern, which may be placed in categories requiring follow-up activity such as "special measures" or "significant improvement".

History

School inspection in Wales dates to the nineteenth century; Estyn in its modern form was created in 1992 as the Welsh arm of the schools inspectorate and became a distinct body under devolution. Its name is a Welsh word meaning "to extend" or "to stretch".
