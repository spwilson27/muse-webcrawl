Freightliner Trucks

Freightliner Trucks is an American truck manufacturer. Founded in 1929 as the truck-manufacturing division of Consolidated Freightways in Portland, Oregon, it established itself as a separate corporation, Freightliner Inc., in the 1940s. The company was acquired by Daimler-Benz in 1981 and is now part of Daimler Truck North America. Freightliner is known largely for heavy-duty Class 8 diesel trucks, commonly referred to as "semis", but also produces a range of other vehicles.

History

Freightliner began when Consolidated Freightways, dissatisfied with the trucks available on the market, began building its own vehicles under the name "Freightliner" — a combination of "freight" and "liner". In 1942 the company sold its first trucks to outside customers. Freightliner became a subsidiary of Daimler in 1981. The flagship model line, the Cascadia, was introduced in 2007 and has become one of the best-selling heavy trucks in North America.

Products

Current and recent models include the Cascadia, the M2 medium-duty truck, the Coronado, and the electric eCascadia and eM2. Freightliner vehicles are also produced for export markets.
