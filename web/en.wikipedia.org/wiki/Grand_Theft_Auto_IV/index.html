Grand Theft Auto IV

Grand Theft Auto IV is a 2008 action-adventure game developed by Rockstar North and published by Rockstar Games. It is the sixth main entry in the Grand Theft Auto series, following Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas (2004), and was released for PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360 in April 2008, and for Windows in December 2008.

Gameplay and setting

The game is set in a redesigned Liberty City based on New York City, comprising four boroughs and the neighbouring state of Alderney (New Jersey). The story follows Niko Bellic, a war veteran from Eastern Europe who emigrates to the United States seeking the American Dream, and becomes entangled with loan sharks, mobsters, and his own past. The game introduced the RAGE engine with Euphoria physics, cover-based combat, a mobile phone interface for activities and relationships, and an online multiplayer mode for up to 32 players on Windows.

Reception and expansions

Grand Theft Auto IV received universal acclaim, with particular praise for its narrative and world design, and broke industry sales records, earning over $500 million in its first week. Two episodic expansions followed in 2009: The Lost and Damned and The Ballad of Gay Tony, later bundled as Episodes from Liberty City. It has sold over 25 million copies.
