"The Girl from Ipanema" (Portuguese: "Garota de Ipanema") is a Brazilian bossa nova and jazz song written in 1962, with music by Antônio Carlos Jobim and Portuguese lyrics by Vinícius de Moraes; the English lyrics were written later by Norman Gimbel. It is one of the most recorded songs in popular music history.

History

The song was inspired by Heloísa Eneida Menezes Paes Pinto (later Helô Pinheiro), a young woman from the Ipanema neighbourhood of Rio de Janeiro whom Jobim and de Moraes saw passing the Veloso bar on her way to the beach. The 1964 recording by Stan Getz and João Gilberto, featuring Astrud Gilberto's English vocal, became a worldwide hit, reaching the top five of the Billboard Hot 100 and winning the Grammy Award for Record of the Year in 1965. The bar was later renamed Garota de Ipanema in the song's honour.
