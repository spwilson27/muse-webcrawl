Proteus (Kevin MacTaggert), also known as Mutant X, is a character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Chris Claremont and artist John Byrne, he first appeared in The Uncanny X-Men #125 (September 1979). The son of Scottish geneticist Moira MacTaggert, Proteus is a mutant whose psionic energy form can possess other people's bodies and warp reality around him.

Fictional character biography

Kevin MacTaggert was the son of Moira MacTaggert and her abusive husband Joseph. His reality-warping powers manifested in childhood and steadily burned out his body; to protect others, his mother kept him in a containment cell at her Mutant Research Centre on Muir Island for ten years. When the cell was damaged during a battle, his energy form escaped and began possessing a succession of hosts, each of which quickly withered and died.

The Proteus Saga

In the classic "Proteus Saga" (Uncanny X-Men #125–128), Proteus possessed the body of his father Joseph and confronted the X-Men in Edinburgh. He proved nearly unstoppable, warping space and matter at will, but shared the classic weakness of his energy form to metal; Colossus, in his steel form, dispersed Proteus's energy and apparently killed him — one of the few times the young Colossus was forced to take a life, which haunted him afterwards.

Later appearances

Proteus's essence survived and resurfaced several times, including in New Excalibur and during the Necrosha storyline. In the Krakoan era he was resurrected and became one of "The Five", the group of mutants who together resurrect the dead: his reality-warping powers transform inert eggs into viable biological vessels for reborn mutants, with a rotating stock of cloned bodies grown by Mister Sinister to house his energy form.
