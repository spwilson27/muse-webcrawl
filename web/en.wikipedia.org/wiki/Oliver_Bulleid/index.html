Oliver Bulleid

Oliver Vaughan Snell Bulleid (1882–1970) was a British railway engineer, best known as the Chief Mechanical Engineer of the Southern Railway from 1937 to nationalization in 1948, and later of Ireland's CIÉ. He was renowned for innovative, sometimes unconventional locomotive designs.

Southern Railway designs

Bulleid introduced the Merchant Navy and West Country/Battle of Britain classes of air-smoothed "spam can" Pacific locomotives, featuring chain-driven valve gear and all-steel welded fireboxes, and the unusual Q1 0-6-0 austerity freight engine. He also pioneered electric multiple-unit design, double-decker suburban trains, and the ill-fated Leader class.

Ireland

Moving to Ireland in 1950, Bulleid led CIÉ's modernization, introducing diesel locomotives and the experimental turf-burning CC1 locomotive.
