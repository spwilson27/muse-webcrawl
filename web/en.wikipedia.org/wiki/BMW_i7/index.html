BMW i7

The BMW i7 is a battery electric full-size luxury sedan produced by BMW. It is the electric variant of the seventh-generation BMW 7 Series (G70), and was unveiled in April 2022 alongside its internal combustion and plug-in hybrid counterparts. The i7 serves as BMW's flagship electric sedan, competing with the Mercedes-Benz EQS and the Tesla Model S.

Overview

The launch model, the i7 xDrive60, pairs two electric motors for a combined output of 536 horsepower, powered by a 101.7 kWh usable battery pack giving a range of up to roughly 625 kilometres under the WLTP cycle. A high-performance M70 xDrive variant followed, producing 659 horsepower and making it the most powerful all-electric BMW at its launch. A rear-wheel-drive eDrive50 model was also offered in some markets.

Features

The i7 shares the G70's emphasis on rear-seat luxury, including an optional 31.3-inch 8K "BMW Theatre Screen" that folds down from the headliner for rear passengers, a curved dashboard display running iDrive 8, and executive lounge seating. Charging supports up to 195 kW DC fast charging.
