Newmarket Racecourse

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Newmarket Racecourse is a British racecourse located in Newmarket, Suffolk, comprising two individual racecourses: the Rowley Mile and the July Course. Newmarket is often referred to as the headquarters of British horse racing and is home to the largest racehorse training centre in Britain, the National Stud, the National Horse Racing Museum and two of the country's five Classic races: the 2,000 Guineas and the 1,000 Guineas.

History

Racing at Newmarket dates to the early 17th century; James I held court there, and Charles II made the town the centre of the sport after the Restoration, riding in races himself and instituting the Town Plate in 1666 — among the oldest races run under rules. The Jockey Club, founded around 1750, has its headquarters at Newmarket and long governed British racing. The Rowley Mile takes its name from "Old Rowley", Charles II's favourite stallion (and the king's nickname).

The courses

Rowley Mile

The Rowley Mile is used for the spring and autumn meetings, including the Guineas Festival in early May (2,000 and 1,000 Guineas) and Champions Day racing in October with the Dewhurst Stakes and the Cesarewitch. It is a broad, straight, largely undulating course with a dip before the final furlong climb to the finish — a famously testing configuration for two-year-olds and milers.

July Course

The July Course hosts the summer meetings, including the July Festival with the July Cup, one of Britain's most prestigious sprint races, and the Falmouth Stakes. It has its own charming garden-party character and a separate grandstand complex.

Training centre

Around 3,000 racehorses are trained in and around the town by dozens of trainers, using some 2,500 acres of training grounds, including Warren Hill and the Limekilns gallops. The Tattersalls sales at Newmarket are Europe's premier bloodstock auctions, and the National Stud stands stallions on the July Course side of town.

Major races

  • 2,000 Guineas Stakes (Rowley Mile, May)
  • 1,000 Guineas Stakes (Rowley Mile, May)
  • July Cup (July Course, July)
  • Dewhurst Stakes (Rowley Mile, October)
  • Cesarewitch Handicap (Rowley Mile, October)
  • Cheveley Park Stakes and Middle Park Stakes
