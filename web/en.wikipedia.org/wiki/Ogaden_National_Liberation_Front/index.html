Ogaden National Liberation Front

The Ogaden National Liberation Front (ONLF; Somali: Jabhadda Xoreynta Ogaadeenya) is a Somali nationalist political and formerly armed movement in the Somali Region of Ethiopia. Founded in 1984, the ONLF sought self-determination for ethnic Somalis in the Ogaden region.

The group waged a low-level insurgency against the Ethiopian government from 1994 until a peace agreement in 2018, following political reforms under Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed, after which the ONLF disarmed and entered legal politics.
