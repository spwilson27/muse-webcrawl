The Lucid Air is a battery electric luxury sedan produced by American electric vehicle manufacturer Lucid Motors. Unveiled in production form in September 2020, with first customer deliveries in October 2021, the Air was noted at launch for the longest EPA-rated range of any electric vehicle — up to 520 miles (837 km) for the Dream Edition Range — and for very high efficiency from its in-house motors and 900-volt-plus electrical architecture.

Variants and performance

The range includes the Pure, Touring, Grand Touring, and the tri-motor Sapphire. The Sapphire produces 1,234 hp, accelerating from 0–60 mph in about 1.89 seconds, making it among the quickest production sedans in the world. The Air was named the 2022 MotorTrend Car of the Year. It is built at Lucid's AMP-1 factory in Casa Grande, Arizona.
