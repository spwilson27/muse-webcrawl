Triad

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Triad or triads may refer to a group of three. The word derives from the Greek trias (τριάς), a group of three.

Crime

A triad is a transnational organised crime syndicate based in Greater China and with outposts in various countries with significant overseas Chinese diaspora populations. Triads originated in secret societies and resistance movements of Qing-dynasty China and evolved into criminal organisations involved in drug trafficking, extortion, illegal gambling and other rackets. Well-known triads include the 14K and Sun Yee On in Hong Kong, and the Bamboo Union in Taiwan. The name "triad" was coined by British colonial authorities in Hong Kong, from the triangular motif in the societies' flags and iconography, reflecting the union of heaven, earth and man.

Music

In music theory, a triad is a set of three notes (or pitch classes) that can be stacked vertically in thirds and forms the basic building block of tertian harmony. The four principal types are the major, minor, augmented and diminished triads, each determined by the quality of the two intervals above the root.

Other uses

  • The nuclear triad: the three components of a strategic nuclear weapons delivery system — land-based intercontinental ballistic missiles, submarine-launched ballistic missiles, and strategic bombers.
  • The Triad region of North Carolina (Greensboro–Winston-Salem–High Point), distinct from the Triangle.
  • Triadic colour schemes in art and design, using three colours evenly spaced on the colour wheel.
  • The Dark Triad in psychology: narcissism, Machiavellianism and psychopathy.
  • Triads in medicine, such as Beck's triad and the classic triads of symptoms used in diagnosis.
