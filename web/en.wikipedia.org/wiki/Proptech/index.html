Proptech

Property technology, commonly called proptech, is the application of information technology and platform economics to real estate markets. It encompasses software and online marketplaces that help individuals and businesses research, buy, sell, rent and manage property, as well as construction technology and smart-building systems.

Sectors

Proptech businesses include property portals and listing services, digital brokerages, mortgage and conveyancing platforms, property-management software, short-stay and co-living platforms, automated valuation models, and building technologies such as access control, energy management and sensor networks. The term is modelled on fintech and is sometimes extended as "real estate tech" or, for construction, "contech".

Development

The sector expanded rapidly in the 2010s with venture-capital funding and the digitisation of property transactions, producing large platforms in listings, iBuying (algorithmic home purchase) and property management, though the iBuying model suffered high-profile reversals when firms such as Zillow exited the business in 2021.
