Charles McKeown

Charles McKeown (born 1949) is a British writer and actor. He is best known for his screenplays for films directed by Terry Gilliam, including Brazil (1985), which he co-wrote with Gilliam and Tom Stoppard, and The Adventures of Baron Munchausen (1988) and The Imaginarium of Doctor Parnassus (2009), both co-written with Gilliam.

Career

McKeown began his career as an actor, appearing in British television and film, including a small role in Monty Python's Life of Brian (1979) and in the television series Ripping Yarns. He moved into writing, and his collaboration with Terry Gilliam began with Brazil, the dystopian satire whose screenplay was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay.

He went on to co-write Gilliam's The Adventures of Baron Munchausen (1988). Two decades later he again collaborated with Gilliam on The Imaginarium of Doctor Parnassus (2009), the film completed after the death of its star Heath Ledger during production.
