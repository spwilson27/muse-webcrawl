Death Eaters

The Death Eaters are a fictional group of witches and wizards in J. K. Rowling's Harry Potter series: the followers of the dark wizard Lord Voldemort, committed to purging the wizarding world of Muggle-born witches and wizards and establishing Voldemort's rule. They are branded with the Dark Mark, a skull-and-serpent tattoo on the inner left forearm, by which Voldemort summons them.

History in the series

The Death Eaters terrorised Britain during the First Wizarding War, murdering opponents including members of the Order of the Phoenix. After Voldemort's fall in 1981, many claimed to have been under the Imperius Curse and escaped punishment — most notoriously Lucius Malfoy — while others, such as Bellatrix Lestrange, were imprisoned in Azkaban. With Voldemort's return in Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire, the Death Eaters regrouped, broke their comrades out of Azkaban, infiltrated the Ministry of Magic, and were finally defeated at the Battle of Hogwarts in Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows.

Notable members

Named Death Eaters include Bellatrix Lestrange, Lucius, Draco and Narcissa Malfoy, Peter Pettigrew, Barty Crouch Jr., Antonin Dolohov, Augustus Rookwood, the Carrows, Fenrir Greyback (an ally), and the double agent Severus Snape, whose true loyalty to Dumbledore is one of the series' central revelations.
