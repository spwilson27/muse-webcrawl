Jorge Paulo Agostinho Mendes (born 7 January 1966) is a Portuguese football agent, founder of the agency Gestifute, and one of the most influential figures in the modern transfer market. He is commonly described as a "super-agent".

Career

Mendes abandoned ambitions of a professional playing career and, after running businesses including a video rental shop and a nightclub, negotiated his first transfer in 1996, moving goalkeeper Nuno Espírito Santo from Vitória Guimarães to Deportivo La Coruña. In the same year he founded Gestifute in Porto. His breakthrough came with the moves of Portuguese players and coaches to England in the early 2000s, above all José Mourinho's appointment at Chelsea and Cristiano Ronaldo's transfer from Sporting CP to Manchester United in 2003.

Influence

Mendes has brokered many of the most expensive transfers in football history, including those of Ronaldo, James Rodríguez, Ángel Di María, João Félix and Bernardo Silva, and has held close relationships with clubs such as Wolverhampton Wanderers, Valencia and Atlético Madrid. He has repeatedly been named Agent of the Year at the Globe Soccer Awards. His reach has also attracted scrutiny over conflicts of interest and third-party ownership, including investigations in Portugal and reporting based on the Football Leaks documents.
