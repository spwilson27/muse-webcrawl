Pomona Valley

The Pomona Valley is located in the eastern part of Los Angeles County and the western part of San Bernardino County in Southern California, United States. The valley lies east of the San Gabriel Valley and west of the Inland Empire, with the city of Pomona as its principal centre.

Geography

The valley is bordered by the San Gabriel Mountains to the north and the Puente Hills and Chino Hills to the south and west. Major communities include Pomona, Claremont, La Verne, San Dimas, Diamond Bar, and Chino. The valley is traversed by Interstate 10, State Route 60, and State Route 57.

History

Originally inhabited by the Tongva people, the area became part of Spanish and Mexican ranchos. In the late 19th century Pomona was founded and the valley became famous for citrus groves. The Claremont Colleges were established beginning in 1887, making the area a notable centre of education.

Economy and culture

The Pomona Valley hosts California State Polytechnic University, Pomona (Cal Poly Pomona), the Fairplex fairgrounds — home to the LA County Fair — and the NHRA Winternationals drag racing event.
