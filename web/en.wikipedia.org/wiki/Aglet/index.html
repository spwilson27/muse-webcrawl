Aglet

An aglet (or aiglet) is the small sheath, often made of plastic or metal, that binds the end of a shoelace, cord or drawstring to prevent fraying and to make threading through eyelets easier. The word derives from the Old French aiguillette, a small needle.

Aglets have existed since ancient times, fashioned from metal, glass or stone; in the modern era most are formed by heat-sealing or by applying plastic film or acetate around the lace tip. Harvey Kennedy is often credited with early shoelace-related patents in the nineteenth century.

Beyond function, aglets appear decoratively on aiguillettes and livery cords, and the humble component gained cultural fame through a well-known episode of the animated series Phineas and Ferb devoted to spreading awareness of the word.
