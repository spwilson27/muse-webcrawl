Abersoch

Abersoch is a village and seaside resort in the community of Llanengan on the Llŷn Peninsula in the Welsh county of Gwynedd. It lies at the southern end of the peninsula, on the A499 between Pwllheli and Llanbedrog, facing east into Cardigan Bay. Originally a fishing port, Abersoch became a popular holiday destination in the 20th century and is now noted for sailing, watersports and its beaches.

Geography

The name derives from the River Soch, which flows into the sea at Abersoch ("aber" meaning estuary or mouth of a river in Welsh). The main beach is sheltered and faces south-east, giving views toward St Tudwal's Islands and the mountains of Snowdonia and mid-Wales across the bay. A harbour lies at the northern end of the village.

Tourism

Abersoch is a centre for dinghy sailing and hosts regattas and sailing weeks; the South Caernarvonshire Yacht Club, founded in the 1920s, organises racing in the bay. The village also attracts visitors for windsurfing, paddleboarding, powerboating and coasteering, and it has hosted music events including the Wakestock festival in nearby locations. Tourism has driven very high property prices, and a large proportion of homes are second homes or holiday lets, a matter of longstanding local debate about the effect on the Welsh-speaking community.

Community

Facilities include a primary school (Ysgol Abersoch, which closed in 2021 amid controversy), shops, pubs and an RNLI lifeboat station. The nearby hamlet of Bwlchtocyn and the headland of Mynytho overlook the village, and the Wales Coast Path passes through.
