Walter Extra is a German aircraft designer, aerobatic pilot and founder of Extra Aircraft (Extra Flugzeugbau), the manufacturer of some of the world's most successful competition aerobatic aeroplanes, including the Extra 200, Extra 300 and Extra 330 series.


An Extra aerobatic aircraft in flight

Career

Extra began as a competitive aerobatic pilot, flying as a member of the German national team. Dissatisfied with the aircraft available to him, he began designing his own machines in the late 1970s, building his first designs around wooden construction before moving to advanced composite structures. He founded his company at Hünxe/Dinslaken in Germany, and in the 1980s the Extra 230 and then the Extra 300 — an unlimited-class monoplane with exceptional roll rate and strength — became dominant in world aerobatic competition.

Subsequent models include the two-seat Extra 200 and Extra 300L trainers, the Extra 330SC single-seat competition aircraft, and the Extra 330SX, as well as the pressurised touring Extra 400 and the Extra 500 turboprop. Extra aircraft have won numerous World Aerobatic Championships titles and are popular in the Red Bull Air Race era air racing, airshows and upset-recovery training.
