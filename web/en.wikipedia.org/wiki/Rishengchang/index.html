Rishengchang

Rishengchang (Chinese: 日昇昌) was a pioneering Chinese financial institution based in Pingyao, Shanxi, generally regarded as the first piaohao — a draft bank — in China. Founded in the early 1820s (often dated 1823), it allowed merchants to remit money across the country using paper drafts rather than transporting silver.

Foundedc. 1823, Pingyao, Shanxi
TypePiaohao (draft bank)
FounderLei Litai

History

Rishengchang began as a dye business, Xiyucheng, whose owner and manager Lei Litai developed a system of exchange drafts for merchants moving funds between cities. Converted into a piaohao, it grew to dozens of branches across China and had dealings in Mongolia, Russia, and Japan.

Legacy

The rise of Shanxi piaohao foreshadowed modern Chinese banking; the system declined in the early 20th century with the fall of the Qing dynasty and the rise of modern banks. Rishengchang's Pingyao premises are now a museum within the UNESCO-listed Ancient City of Pingyao.
