Skylanders is a toys-to-life action-adventure video game franchise published by Activision. Players place physical toy figures on a peripheral called the Portal of Power; an NFC chip in each figure summons the corresponding character into the game, and the figure itself stores the character's progress and upgrades.

Games

The series began with Skylanders: Spyro's Adventure (2011), developed by Toys for Bob and loosely spun off from the Spyro franchise. Annual sequels followed: Giants (2012), SWAP Force (2013), Trap Team (2014), SuperChargers (2015), which added vehicles, and Imaginators (2016), which let players create their own characters. Each instalment introduced new figures while remaining compatible with earlier ones.

Gameplay and figures

Games combine platforming, puzzle-solving, and combat in the floating realm of Skylands, defended against the villain Kaos. Characters belong to elemental classes such as Fire, Water, Tech, and Undead, and certain areas require a Skylander of a matching element. Over 300 distinct figures were released, making the franchise a major commercial success and a pioneer of the toys-to-life category alongside competitors such as Disney Infinity and Lego Dimensions.

Other media

The Netflix animated series Skylanders Academy ran from 2016 to 2018. Following Imaginators, no new mainline games have been released, though characters have appeared in later Activision titles.
