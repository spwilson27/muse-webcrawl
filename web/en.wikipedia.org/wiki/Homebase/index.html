Homebase is a British home improvement and garden retailer, founded in 1979 as the home products division of Sainsbury's. It sells DIY products, paint, tools, kitchens, bathrooms and garden plants and furniture.

History

The chain expanded under various owners, including Home Retail Group. It was sold to Australian retailer Wesfarmers in 2016, whose troubled attempt to convert stores to the Bunnings format led to a sale to Hilco Capital in 2018 and the closure of many stores, before further ownership changes and administration in 2024, with the brand and some stores acquired by CDS (The Range).
