Party of Growth

The Party of Growth (Russian: Партия Роста, Partiya Rosta) was a liberal, pro-business political party in Russia. It was founded in 2016 as the successor to the Right Cause party and was led by Boris Titov, the presidential commissioner for entrepreneurs' rights.

History and platform

The party advocated market reforms, support for small and medium business, lower taxes, an independent judiciary and reduced state interference in the economy, while remaining broadly loyal to the political system. Titov stood as its candidate in the 2018 presidential election, winning under one per cent of the vote. The party held a small number of regional and municipal seats but never won representation in the State Duma on its own.

In 2024 the Party of Growth merged into the New People party.
