Big Boss (Metal Gear)

Big Boss, real name John and also known by the codenames Naked Snake and Jack, is a fictional character in Konami's Metal Gear video game series, created by Hideo Kojima. He is depicted as a legendary soldier, the founder of the mercenary states Outer Heaven and Zanzibar Land, and the genetic father of Solid Snake and Liquid Snake.

Character biography

As the young operative Naked Snake, he is the protagonist of Metal Gear Solid 3: Snake Eater (2004), where he earns the title "Big Boss" after defeating his mentor, The Boss, during the Cold War. Disillusioned with governments, he founds the mercenary company Militaires Sans Frontières in Peace Walker and later becomes the antagonist of the original Metal Gear games, opposed by his clone-son Solid Snake.

Concept and reception

Big Boss was introduced as the villain of the 1987 Metal Gear and reimagined as a tragic protagonist in the prequel games. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest video game characters, praised for his complex arc from hero to anti-villain.
