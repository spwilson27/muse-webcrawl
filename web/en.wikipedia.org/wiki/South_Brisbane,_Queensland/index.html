South Brisbane

Location: South bank of the Brisbane River, opposite the CBD

South Brisbane is an inner-city suburb of Brisbane, Queensland, Australia, situated on the southern bank of the Brisbane River directly across from the central business district. It is a major cultural and recreational precinct.

Features

South Brisbane contains the South Bank Parklands, created on the site of World Expo 88 and featuring the Streets Beach artificial lagoon, the Wheel of Brisbane, and riverside promenades. The Queensland Cultural Centre in the suburb includes the Queensland Museum, the State Library of Queensland, the Queensland Art Gallery and the Gallery of Modern Art (QAGOMA), and the Queensland Performing Arts Centre (QPAC). The Brisbane Convention & Exhibition Centre is also located here.

History

One of Brisbane's oldest suburbs, South Brisbane was a separate municipality (the City of South Brisbane) from 1888 until amalgamation into Greater Brisbane in 1925. It was historically a working-class and migrant area, and retains landmarks such as the former South Brisbane Town Hall and South Brisbane railway station.
