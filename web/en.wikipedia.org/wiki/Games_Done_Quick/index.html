Games Done Quick

Games Done Quick (GDQ) is a series of charity video game speedrunning marathons organised in the United States. Volunteers play games at high speed ("speedruns") while commentating, and viewers are encouraged to donate to charity, often in exchange for choosing file names, character names or extra showcase runs. GDQ events have raised over US$50 million for charities including the Prevent Cancer Foundation and Doctors Without Borders.

Events

The two flagship week-long marathons are Awesome Games Done Quick (AGDQ), held in January in aid of the Prevent Cancer Foundation, and Summer Games Done Quick (SGDQ), held in June or July in aid of Doctors Without Borders (Médecins Sans Frontières). The first event, Classic Games Done Quick, was held in January 2010 in the basement of organiser Mike Uyama's mother, raising about $10,000. By the late 2010s single events routinely raised over $3 million, with AGDQ 2020 topping $3.13 million.

Smaller events include Frame Fatales, a speedrunning community and marathon showcasing women and femme runners, and special one-off marathons for disaster relief.

Format

Runs run around the clock for the full week, streamed live on Twitch to hundreds of thousands of viewers and archived on YouTube. Donations are read out by hosts between and during runs, and donation incentives ("bid wars") let donors vote on run variants or trigger bonus content. A large on-site audience, and signature crowd chants such as "HYPE!" and "Save the animals / Kill the animals" (a famous Super Metroid donation choice), are fixtures of the events.

The organisation was formalised as Games Done Quick, LLC in 2015; founder Mike Uyama stepped back from the company in 2023.
