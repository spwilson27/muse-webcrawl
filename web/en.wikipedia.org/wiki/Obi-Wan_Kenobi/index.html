Obi-Wan Kenobi

Obi-Wan Kenobi is a fictional character in the Star Wars franchise, created by George Lucas. A Jedi Knight of the Galactic Republic, he serves as the mentor of Anakin Skywalker and later of Anakin's son Luke. He was portrayed by Alec Guinness in the original trilogy and by Ewan McGregor in the prequel trilogy and the 2022 television series Obi-Wan Kenobi.

Story

As a young Jedi, Obi-Wan was apprenticed to Qui-Gon Jinn and, after Qui-Gon's death at the hands of Darth Maul, took Anakin Skywalker as his own Padawan. During the Clone Wars he became a general and a member of the Jedi High Council. When Anakin fell to the dark side and became Darth Vader, Obi-Wan defeated him on Mustafar and went into exile on Tatooine, watching over the infant Luke under the name "Ben Kenobi". Nineteen years later he began Luke's training, and was struck down by Vader aboard the Death Star, becoming one with the Force and continuing to guide Luke as a spirit.

Reception

Obi-Wan is consistently ranked among the most popular characters in the franchise; Guinness received an Academy Award nomination for the role, and McGregor's portrayal is widely credited as a highlight of the prequel films.
