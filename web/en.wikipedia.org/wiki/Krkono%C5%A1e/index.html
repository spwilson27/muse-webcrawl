The Krkonoše (German: Riesengebirge; Polish: Karkonosze) are a mountain range on the border between the Czech Republic and Poland, the highest part of the Sudetes. Their highest summit, Sněžka (1,603 m), is the highest point in the Czech Republic.

Nature

The range features subarctic tundra on its plateaus, glacial cirques, waterfalls and rare alpine flora. Both the Czech and Polish sides are protected as national parks and together form a UNESCO biosphere reserve.

Tourism and folklore

The Krkonoše are a popular area for hiking and skiing, with resorts such as Špindlerův Mlýn and Karpacz. In folklore the mountains are home to Krakonoš (Rübezahl), a legendary mountain spirit.
