Instant payment

An instant payment is a method of exchanging money and processing payments that allows for near-instant transfer of money between bank accounts, instead of the typical one to three business days. Such systems are available around the clock, every day of the year, and the recipient can use the funds immediately.

Systems

Notable instant payment schemes include the SEPA Instant Credit Transfer in Europe, the Faster Payments Service in the United Kingdom, FedNow and the RTP network in the United States, Pix in Brazil, and the Unified Payments Interface (UPI) in India. Many systems support addressing by mobile phone number or alias rather than full account details, sometimes via QR codes.

Benefits and risks

Instant payments improve cash flow for businesses, support e-commerce and gig-economy payouts, and can reduce reliance on cash and cards. Their speed and finality, however, also make recovery of fraudulent or mistaken transfers difficult, prompting safeguards such as confirmation-of-payee checks and transaction limits.
