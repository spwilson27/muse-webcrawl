Bollock dagger

The bollock dagger or ballock knife is a type of dagger with a distinctively shaped hilt, with two oval swellings at the guard resembling male anatomy. It was popular in Scandinavia, Flanders, England and Scotland from the 13th to the 18th centuries, and was worn by both soldiers and civilians, including merchants and knights.

Description

Most bollock daggers have a single-edged blade, often with a reinforced point for thrusting. The hilt was usually carved from a single piece of wood, such as boxwood or root, sometimes with metal mounts. The form evolved into the Scottish dirk in the early 17th century.

Use

Worn at the front of the belt, the bollock dagger served as a utility and fighting knife and a status object. Many examples survive in museum collections. See also Dirk and Dagger.
