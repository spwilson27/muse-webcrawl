Nadia Comăneci

Nadia Elena Comăneci (born 12 November 1961) is a Romanian former artistic gymnast and a five-time Olympic gold medallist. At the 1976 Summer Olympics in Montreal, aged 14, she became the first gymnast to be awarded a perfect score of 10.0 at the Olympic Games, for her routine on the uneven bars — the scoreboard, unprepared for a ten, displayed 1.00. She went on to receive six more perfect 10s in Montreal and won three gold medals there.

Career

Spotted by coach Béla Károlyi as a young child, Comăneci won her first Romanian national title at nine and dominated the 1975 European Championships. After Montreal she won further titles at the 1979 World Championships and two more golds at the 1980 Moscow Olympics. She retired from competition in 1984.

Later life

In November 1989 Comăneci defected from Communist Romania to the United States. She later married American gymnast Bart Conner, with whom she runs gymnastics schools and charitable work; she is widely credited with popularising gymnastics worldwide and transforming it into a sport dominated by young athletes.
