Dead Set is a British television horror drama series written and created by Charlie Brooker. Set during a fictional series of the reality show Big Brother, it follows the housemates and production staff who initially fail to notice that a zombie outbreak has devastated the outside world.

Plot

On eviction night, rioting outside the studio turns out to be the start of a fast-moving zombie outbreak. Runner Kelly, the housemates and the show's producer Patrick barricade themselves inside the Big Brother house, watching society collapse through the programme's camera feeds as supplies dwindle and the group fractures.

Production and reception

The series was produced by Zeppotron for E4 and aired over five consecutive nights in October 2008, with former Big Brother presenter Davina McCall appearing as herself. It was nominated for a BAFTA for Best Drama Serial and praised for combining satire of reality television with genuine horror; a Brazilian remake, Reality Z, followed in 2020.
