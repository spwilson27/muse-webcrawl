Dunkin' Donuts, now usually branded simply as Dunkin', is an American multinational coffee and doughnut company founded by William Rosenberg in Quincy, Massachusetts, in 1950. It is one of the largest coffee and doughnut shop chains in the world, with thousands of franchised locations across the United States and dozens of other countries.

History

Rosenberg opened his first shop, the Open Kettle, in 1948, selling doughnuts and coffee; in 1950 it was renamed Dunkin' Donuts. Franchising began in 1955 and the chain grew rapidly, becoming known for its pink-and-orange branding and slogans such as "America Runs on Dunkin'". For many years it was owned alongside Baskin-Robbins under Dunkin' Brands.

Rebranding and ownership

In 2019 the company shortened its name to Dunkin' to emphasise its beverage-led menu, though doughnuts remained a core product. In 2020 Inspire Brands acquired Dunkin' Brands for about $11.3 billion, making the chain part of the same group as Arby's and Buffalo Wild Wings.

Products

The menu centres on hot and iced coffee, espresso drinks, doughnuts (including the bite-sized Munchkins doughnut holes), bagels, muffins and breakfast sandwiches.
