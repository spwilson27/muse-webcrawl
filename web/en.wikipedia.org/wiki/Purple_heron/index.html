Purple heron

The purple heron (Ardea purpurea) is a wide-ranging wading bird in the heron family, breeding in Africa, southern and eastern Asia and southern Europe, with northern populations migrating south in winter.

Description

It is a large bird, 78–97 cm tall with a wingspan of about 120–152 cm, but slender for its size. Adults have a chestnut-red head and neck with black stripes, grey upper wings and back, and a long yellow-brown bill. It is more secretive than the grey heron, favouring dense reedbeds and marshes, where it hunts fish, amphibians, insects and small mammals with a strike of its dagger-like bill.

Breeding

Purple herons usually nest colonially in reedbeds or trees near water, laying clutches of about three to five pale blue-green eggs.
