MGM Resorts International is an American global hospitality and entertainment company operating destination resorts in the United States and Macau, including the Bellagio, MGM Grand, Mandalay Bay, Luxor, and the MGM Macau. The company also offers online sports betting and gaming through BetMGM, a joint venture with Entain. It is one of the largest employers on the Las Vegas Strip.

History

The company's origins lie with investor Kirk Kerkorian, who had built the original MGM Grand Hotel and Casino in 1973. MGM Grand, Inc. was formed in 1986, and in 1993 opened the current MGM Grand Las Vegas, then the largest hotel in the world. In 2000 the company acquired Mirage Resorts — owner of the Mirage, Treasure Island and Bellagio — in a deal worth about $6.4 billion, and renamed itself MGM Mirage. A second transformational acquisition followed in 2005, when it bought the Mandalay Resort Group for roughly $7.9 billion, adding Mandalay Bay, Luxor, Excalibur and Circus Circus to the portfolio.

In 2010 the company adopted its present name, MGM Resorts International, reflecting ambitions beyond gaming. During 2016 most of its real estate was transferred to MGM Growth Properties, a real estate investment trust later acquired by Vici Properties in 2022, with MGM retaining operations under long-term leases. MGM also pursued international expansion through MGM China Holdings, and entered legal US sports betting after 2018 through BetMGM. In September 2023 the company suffered a major ransomware attack that disrupted systems across its properties for several days.

Properties and operations

The company's Las Vegas portfolio includes Bellagio, Aria (in the CityCenter complex it developed with Dubai World), MGM Grand, Mandalay Bay, Park MGM, Luxor, New York-New York, Excalibur, The Cosmopolitan of Las Vegas (acquired in 2022) and others. Regional US properties include Borgata in Atlantic City, MGM National Harbor in Maryland, MGM Grand Detroit, Beau Rivage in Mississippi, and Empire City Casino in New York. In Asia, MGM China operates MGM Macau and MGM Cotai.
