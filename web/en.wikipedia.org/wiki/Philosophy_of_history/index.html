Philosophy of history

Philosophy of history is the philosophical study of history and its discipline. The term was coined by Voltaire. It covers two broad kinds of inquiry: speculative philosophy of history, which seeks a pattern, direction or meaning in the historical process as a whole (as in the work of Hegel, Marx or Spengler), and analytical or critical philosophy of history, which examines the concepts, methods and explanations used by historians.

Questions in the field include whether history progresses or repeats, the role of individuals versus structures, historical explanation and narrative, objectivity, and how knowledge of the past is possible.
