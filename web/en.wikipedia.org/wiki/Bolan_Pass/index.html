Bolan Pass
Elevation: 1,793 m (5,884 ft)
Location: Balochistan, Pakistan
Length: ~89 km

The Bolan Pass is a mountain pass through the Toba Kakar range in Balochistan, Pakistan, about 120 km south of the border with Afghanistan. Together with the Khyber Pass, it has been one of the two great gateways into South Asia for traders and invading armies for millennia.

History

The pass links Sibi with Quetta and was used by the Army of the Indus during the First Anglo-Afghan War in 1839. The nearby Neolithic site of Mehrgarh shows the route's antiquity.

Transport

A railway line, part of Pakistan Railways, winds through the pass with numerous tunnels and bridges, alongside the N-65 highway. See also Quetta.
