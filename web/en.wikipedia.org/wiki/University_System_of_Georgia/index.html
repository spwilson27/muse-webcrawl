University System of Georgia

The University System of Georgia (USG) is the government agency that includes 26 public institutions of higher learning in the U.S. state of Georgia. It is governed by the Georgia Board of Regents, a body created by the Georgia state constitution in 1932, whose eighteen members are appointed by the governor.

Institutions

The system includes four research universities — the University of Georgia (the flagship and the system's oldest institution, founded 1785), Georgia Tech, Georgia State University, and Augusta University — along with comprehensive universities, state universities, and state colleges. A series of consolidations beginning in 2013 reduced the number of institutions from 35 to 26.

Enrollment and administration

The system enrolls more than 340,000 students and employs roughly 48,000 faculty and staff. Its central office is in Atlanta, headed by a chancellor appointed by the Board of Regents. The system office also oversees the Georgia Public Library Service and the Georgia Archives.
