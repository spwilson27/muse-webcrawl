Nuggets (compilation album)

Nuggets: Original Artyfacts from the First Psychedelic Era, 1965–1968
Compilation album by various artists
Released: 2 October 1972
Genre: Garage rock, psychedelic rock, proto-punk
Label: Elektra
Compiler: Lenny Kaye

Nuggets: Original Artyfacts from the First Psychedelic Era is a compilation album of American garage rock singles released in the mid-to-late 1960s. It was assembled by musician and writer Lenny Kaye and released as a double album by Elektra Records on 2 October 1972. The album's subtitle, Original Artyfacts from the First Psychedelic Era, 1965–1968, frames its 27 tracks as artefacts of a lost era.

Content

The collection gathers regional hits and obscurities by bands including the 13th Floor Elevators ("You're Gonna Miss Me"), the Seeds ("Pushin' Too Hard"), the Standells ("Dirty Water"), the Count Five ("Psychotic Reaction"), the Electric Prunes ("I Had Too Much to Dream (Last Night)"), the Leaves ("Hey Joe"), ? and the Mysterians ("96 Tears") and the Blues Magoos ("(We Ain't Got) Nothin' Yet"). Kaye's liner notes popularised the term "punk rock" in reference to 1960s garage bands.

Influence

Nuggets is credited with reviving interest in American garage rock and influencing the 1970s punk movement; Kaye himself soon became guitarist in the Patti Smith Group. Rhino Records expanded the concept with the four-CD box set Nuggets (1998) and the sequel Nuggets II: Original Artyfacts from the British Empire and Beyond (2001). The original album was preserved in the US National Recording Registry in 2005 for its cultural, historical and aesthetic significance.
