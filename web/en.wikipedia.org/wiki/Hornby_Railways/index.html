Hornby Railways

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

A Hornby OO gauge model train

Hornby Railways is a British model railway brand, founded in 1901 in Liverpool by Frank Hornby, the inventor of Meccano. It is now part of Hornby Hobbies Ltd, based in Margate, Kent. Hornby's models are primarily made to OO gauge (1:76 scale on 16.5 mm track), the most popular scale in the United Kingdom.

History

Frank Hornby's company first produced clockwork tinplate trains in O gauge (1:43) from 1920 under the Hornby name. In 1938 the company launched Hornby Dublo, a half-O (HO/OO) electric system with three-rail track, which became a staple of British train sets. After the parent company, Meccano Ltd, collapsed in 1964, the brand was acquired by Tri-ang, and the merged range was sold as Tri-ang Hornby, later renamed Hornby Railways. Production moved from Liverpool to Margate, and from the late 1990s manufacturing shifted to China, enabling far more detailed models.

Products

The range includes locomotives, coaches, wagons, train sets, track, and accessories spanning British steam, diesel, and electric prototypes, along with digital command control (DCC) systems such as the Select, Elite, and later app-based HM7000 Bluetooth decoders. Hornby has also held licences for tie-in ranges including Thomas & Friends (earlier years) and Harry Potter Hogwarts Express sets. The company marked its centenary in 2020 with special models and a television documentary series about its design and production process.
