Dow Jones & Company

Dow Jones & Company is an American publishing company owned by News Corp since 2007. It publishes The Wall Street Journal, Barron's, MarketWatch and Dow Jones Newswires, and created the Dow Jones Industrial Average.

History

Founded in 1882 by Charles Dow, Edward Jones and Charles Bergstresser, the firm began with hand-delivered news bulletins to Wall Street subscribers before launching the Journal in 1889.

Indices

Its index business, including the Dow Jones Industrial Average first published in 1896, is now operated through S&P Dow Jones Indices, a joint venture majority-owned by S&P Global.
