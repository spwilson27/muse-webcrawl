Mount Ōmine

Mount Ōmine (Ōmine-san), also known as Mount Sanjō, is a mountain in Nara Prefecture, Japan, sacred to the Shugendō tradition of mountain asceticism. Its summit, Mount Sanjōgatake (1,719 m), has for centuries been a site of religious training for male practitioners (yamabushi).

The mountain is traditionally associated with En no Gyōja, the legendary founder of Shugendō. Controversially, the summit area maintains a centuries-old prohibition on women climbers, defended by local religious authorities as a religious tradition; it forms part of the UNESCO World Heritage listing "Sacred Sites and Pilgrimage Routes in the Kii Mountain Range" (inscribed 2004) and of the long Omine Okugakemichi pilgrimage route to Kumano.
