Fishing

Fishing
DefinitionActivity of catching fish
FormsSubsistence, commercial, recreational
[Image: Fishermen hauling a net full of fish aboard a small boat at dawn]

Fishing is the activity of trying to catch fish, practised for at least tens of thousands of years with spears, hooks, traps and nets. It remains a livelihood for millions and the primary protein source for many coastal peoples.

Sustainability

Industrial trawling and longlining depleted many stocks in the 20th century; management by quotas, seasons and marine protected areas aims to rebuild them, while illegal fishing persists. Recreational fishing is a major pastime. Aquaculture now supplies over half of fish eaten, easing — and sometimes adding — pressure on wild stocks.
