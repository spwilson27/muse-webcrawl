Sports Car Club of America

The Sports Car Club of America (SCCA) is a non-profit American automobile club and sanctioning body supporting road racing, rallying, and autocross in the United States. Founded in 1944, it is one of the largest amateur motorsport organisations in the world, with tens of thousands of members organised into regional divisions.

History

The SCCA was founded in Boston in 1944 by enthusiasts of imported sports cars. It organised early American road races at venues such as Watkins Glen and became the dominant organiser of amateur road racing in the post-war decades. Its professional arm later sanctioned series including Can-Am and Trans-Am.

Programmes

Club Racing is the SCCA's flagship programme, culminating each year in the National Championship Runoffs, one of the largest single motorsport events in the United States. Solo (autocross) events are held in parking lots and airfields, while RallyCross and RoadRally programmes extend the club's reach off the circuit. The club is headquartered in Topeka, Kansas.
