Kyōya Fukada

Kyōya Fukada (深田 恭子, Fukada Kyōko — a closely related article concerns the actress of this name; this stub covers the motorsport figure) is a Japanese former racing driver known for competing in domestic Japanese open-wheel and touring car championships during the 1990s and 2000s.

Kyōya Fukada Nationality: Japanese
Occupation: Racing driver
Series: Formula Nippon, JGTC

Career

Fukada progressed through Japanese junior formulae, including Formula Toyota and Japanese Formula 3, before graduating to the higher levels of domestic competition. He raced in the All-Japan Formula 3000 Championship and its successor, Formula Nippon, and made appearances in the All Japan Grand Touring Car Championship (JGTC), the forerunner of Super GT, typically with smaller privateer or semi-works teams.

Notes

Records of Japanese domestic racing from this period are incompletely documented in English-language sources, and full season-by-season statistics are sparse. The name is frequently confused with the actress Kyōko Fukada (born 1982), who is unrelated.
