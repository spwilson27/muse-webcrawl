List of Japanese Nobel laureates

Since 1949, when Hideki Yukawa became the first Japanese Nobel laureate, Japanese nationals have won Nobel Prizes in Physics, Chemistry, Physiology or Medicine, Literature and Peace, making Japan one of the most successful countries in the science categories.

Chemistry

Kenichi Fukui (1981), Hideki Shirakawa (2000), Ryōji Noyori (2001), Kōichi Tanaka (2002), Osamu Shimomura (2008, green fluorescent protein), Ei-ichi Negishi and Akira Suzuki (2010, cross-coupling reactions), and Akira Yoshino (2019, lithium-ion batteries).

Physiology or Medicine

Susumu Tonegawa (1987), Shinya Yamanaka (2012, induced pluripotent stem cells), Satoshi Ōmura (2015, avermectin), Yoshinori Ohsumi (2016, autophagy) and Tasuku Honjo (2018, PD-1 immunotherapy).

Literature and Peace

Literature: Yasunari Kawabata (1968) and Kenzaburō Ōe (1994); the British novelist Kazuo Ishiguro (2017) was born in Nagasaki. Peace: Eisaku Satō (1974), the organisation Nihon Hidankyo (2024), and the International Campaign to Abolish Nuclear Weapons (2017), led by Japanese-born executive director Beatrice Fihn's predecessor Setsuko Thurlow's campaigning; ICAN's award is associated with hibakusha advocacy.
