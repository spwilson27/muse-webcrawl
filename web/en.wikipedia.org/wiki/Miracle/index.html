Miracle

A miracle is an event that is inexplicable by natural or scientific laws and accordingly gets attributed to a supernatural or praeternatural cause. Various religions, including Christianity, Islam, Judaism and Hinduism, describe miracles performed by God, deities, prophets or saints. Informally, the word "miracle" is also used of any statistically unlikely but beneficial event, such as the survival of a natural disaster or simply a "wonderful" occurrence, regardless of any supernatural explanation.

Definitions and explanations

Philosophers have defined miracles in several ways. For Augustine of Hippo, a miracle was not contrary to nature but contrary to what is known of nature. Thomas Aquinas distinguished miracles performed by God beyond, above, or against the order of nature. David Hume, in An Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding (1748), defined a miracle as a "transgression of a law of nature by a particular volition of the Deity, or by the interposition of some invisible agent," and argued famously that testimony could never be sufficient to establish one, since the falsehood of the testimony would always be the lesser miracle. Later philosophers and statisticians, invoking Littlewood's law, point out that individually improbable events are statistically expected to occur in any large population.

In religion

In the Hebrew Bible and the New Testament, miracles — healings, exorcisms, control over nature, and the resurrection of the dead — authenticate the message of prophets and, pre-eminently, of Jesus. The Catholic Church investigates claimed miracles, especially cures attributed to saints' intercession, through medical and theological commissions; a verified miracle is generally required for beatification and canonisation. In Islam, the Quran itself is regarded as the supreme miracle (i'jaz) of Muhammad, while saints' wonders (karamat) are distinguished from prophets' miracles (mu'jizat). Hindu traditions recount miracles (siddhis and divine lila) of deities and gurus; Buddhist texts likewise describe supernormal powers attained through meditation.
