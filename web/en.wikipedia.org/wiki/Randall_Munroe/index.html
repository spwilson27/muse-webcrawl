Randall Patrick Munroe (born 17 October 1984) is an American cartoonist, author and engineer best known as the creator of the webcomic xkcd. A former roboticist at NASA's Langley Research Center, he left to write and draw full time. His books — What If? Serious Scientific Answers to Absurd Hypothetical Questions (2014), Thing Explainer (2015), How To (2019) and What If? 2 (2022) — have been bestsellers.

Early life and NASA

Munroe grew up in the Richmond, Virginia area and studied physics at Christopher Newport University, graduating in 2006. Before and after graduation he worked at NASA Langley as a programmer and roboticist, work that ended when he made his living from xkcd merchandise and licensing.

xkcd

Munroe began posting stick-figure drawings online in 2005; the name xkcd is a deliberately unpronounceable string of letters. The comic, subtitled "a webcomic of romance, sarcasm, math, and language", became widely popular in science and technology culture. Its strips have influenced real-world practice: a comic about password strength popularised the "correct horse battery staple" passphrase idea, and the comic's title-text (alt text) is a signature feature.

Books and other work

What If? grew out of a blog series answering absurd hypothetical questions with rigorous physics; it debuted at number one on the New York Times bestseller list. Thing Explainer explains complex systems using only the thousand most common English words. In 2015 the International Astronomical Union named asteroid 4942 Munroe in his honour. Munroe has also contributed to public-science projects including a climate-change comic commissioned for a magazine anniversary.
