The ArtScience Museum is a museum in Singapore, part of the Marina Bay Sands integrated resort at Marina Bay. Opened on 17 February 2011, it was billed as the world's first museum dedicated to the intersection of art and science.

Architecture

Designed by architect Moshe Safdie, the building resembles a lotus flower — or, in Safdie's description, a welcoming hand with ten "fingers". Each finger contains gallery space topped by skylights, and the roof channels rainwater into a central waterfall and reflecting pool. The structure stands at the waterfront promenade of the resort, opposite the central business district skyline.

Exhibitions

The museum hosts major international touring exhibitions spanning art, design, media and technology, alongside its permanent exhibition Future World: Where Art Meets Science, created with the Tokyo-based art collective teamLab, which features large-scale interactive digital installations. Past touring shows have covered subjects from Titanic artefacts and Harry Potter to Leonardo da Vinci and deep-sea exploration. The museum's galleries total roughly 50,000 square feet (about 4,600 m²) across several levels.
