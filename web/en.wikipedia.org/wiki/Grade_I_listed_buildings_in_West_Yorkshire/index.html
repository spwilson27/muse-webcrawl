Grade I listed buildings in West Yorkshire

There are over 9,000 Grade I listed buildings in England. This page is a list of Grade I listed buildings in the county of West Yorkshire, organised by metropolitan borough. Grade I is the highest of the three grades of listed building status in England and Wales, reserved for buildings of "exceptional interest."

Overview

West Yorkshire's Grade I buildings include medieval churches and monastic ruins, great country houses such as Harewood House and Nostell Priory, civic landmarks of the Victorian era, and industrial structures reflecting the county's textile heritage. Listing is administered by Historic England under the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990.
