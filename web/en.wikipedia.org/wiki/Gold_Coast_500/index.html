Gold Coast 500

The Gold Coast 500 is an annual motor racing event for the Supercars Championship, held on the Surfers Paradise Street Circuit on the Gold Coast, Queensland, Australia. Staged each October, it continues a tradition of street racing at Surfers Paradise dating back to the IndyCar events of the 1990s. The current format features a 500-kilometre endurance race with two drivers per car, under a sponsorship name most recently the Boost Mobile Gold Coast 500.

History

Racing on the streets of Surfers Paradise began with the CART IndyCar Gold Coast Indy 300 in 1991, a hugely popular event that continued under various open-wheel banners until 2008. V8 Supercars raced as a support category from 2002 and took over as the headline act from 2010, initially with an international-driver format (the "Gold Coast 600") in which each car paired a regular driver with an overseas star. After a one-driver sprint format interrupted by COVID-era cancellations, the event was reinstated in 2022 as a 500 km endurance race with co-drivers, echoing the championship's traditional enduros at Sandown and Bathurst.

The circuit, shortened over the years to about 2.98 km, is notorious for its aggressive kerbs at the beachfront chicanes, which launch cars into the air and frequently feature in incidents. Multiple winners of the Surfers endurance races include drivers such as Jamie Whincup, Sébastien Bourdais (as an international co-driver) and, in the 500 era, pairings from Triple Eight Race Engineering and other leading teams.
