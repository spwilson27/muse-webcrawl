Weekly Shōnen Jump

Weekly Shōnen Jump (Japanese: 週刊少年ジャンプ) is a weekly shōnen manga anthology published in Japan by Shueisha under the Jump line of magazines. It is the best-selling manga magazine and one of the longest-running, with the first issue released with a cover date of 1 August 1968. Its manga have been collected in tankōbon volumes under the Jump Comics imprint; many of the best-selling manga series in history originated in its pages.

History

Launched in 1968 as a fortnightly, then weekly, magazine, Jump rose under editor Kazuhiko Torishima and others by aggressively recruiting new talent and using reader surveys to rank series — unpopular titles are cancelled quickly. Its 1980s–90s "golden age" brought circulation above six million with Fist of the North Star, Dragon Ball and Slam Dunk. Its stated editorial pillars are friendship, effort and victory.

Famous series

Series serialised in the magazine include Yu Yu Hakusho, Rurouni Kenshin, One Piece (from 1997, the best-selling manga ever), Naruto, Bleach, Death Note, My Hero Academia, Demon Slayer and Jujutsu Kaisen. Since 2014 the digital edition has been published simultaneously in English through Viz Media's service and Shueisha's Manga Plus, greatly reducing scanlation delays.

Influence

Jump's survey-driven competition, annual one-shot contests (the Tezuka Award) and the iconic Jump Pirate logo shaped the whole shōnen industry. Circulation has declined with print media generally, to around 1.2–1.4 million copies in recent years, while digital readership grows.
