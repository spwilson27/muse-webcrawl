Zenyatta

Image: Zenyatta

Zenyatta (foaled April 1, 2004) is a retired American Thoroughbred racehorse mare, widely regarded as one of the greatest female racehorses of all time. She won 19 of her 20 career starts.

Racing career

Trained by John Shirreffs and ridden principally by Mike Smith, Zenyatta was famous for her dramatic come-from-behind running style, typically trailing the field before unleashing a powerful late run. She won the 2008 Breeders' Cup Ladies' Classic and the 2009 Breeders' Cup Classic, becoming the first mare to win the Classic.

Retirement

Her only defeat came in her final start, the 2010 Breeders' Cup Classic, where she finished second to Blame. She was named American Horse of the Year for 2010 and was inducted into the National Museum of Racing and Hall of Fame in 2016.
