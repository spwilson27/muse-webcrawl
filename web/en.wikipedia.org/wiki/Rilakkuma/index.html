Rilakkuma

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Rilakkuma (Japanese: リラックマ) is a Japanese character franchise created by Aki Kondo and produced by the company San-X. The name is a portmanteau of the Japanese pronunciation of "relax" and kuma (bear), i.e. "relax bear". Rilakkuma first appeared in 2003 in picture books and on stationery, and became one of the most popular character goods franchises in Japan and internationally, generating billions of yen in retail sales.

Characters

  • Rilakkuma — a lazy, food-loving bear who mysteriously appeared one day in the apartment of the office worker Kaoru. Its costume has a zipper on the back, hinting it may not be a real bear; its real contents are unknown. It loves pancakes, omurice, pudding and dango, and spends its days napping and relaxing.
  • Korilakkuma — a smaller white bear cub, mischievous and fond of playing pranks and remote-control toys; "ko" means small/young.
  • Kiiroitori — Kaoru's pet yellow chick, tidy and hardworking, who often scolds the bears for lazing about; its name means "yellow bird".
  • Chairoikoguma — a darker-furred bear cub introduced in 2016, living in the Honey Forest, with fangs and a bear mark on its chest.

Media and merchandise

Beyond plush toys and stationery, the characters appear in picture books, video games, cafés and extensive licensed merchandise. In 2019 Netflix released the stop-motion animated series Rilakkuma and Kaoru, produced by Dwarf Studio, which was widely praised; a follow-up series, Rilakkuma's Theme Park Adventure, appeared in 2022.

Cultural impact

Rilakkuma is frequently cited as an emblem of Japan's kawaii (cute) culture and of a cultural valorization of rest and slow living aimed at stressed working adults. It consistently ranks among the top character franchises in Japan.
