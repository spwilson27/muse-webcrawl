Ferrero

Industry: Confectionery

Founded: 1946, Alba, Piedmont, Italy

Founder: Pietro Ferrero

Ferrero International SpA is an Italian multinational manufacturer of branded chocolate and confectionery products, and one of the largest chocolate producers and confectionery companies in the world. It was founded in 1946 in Alba, Piedmont, by Pietro Ferrero.

Products

The company's best-known brands include Nutella hazelnut spread, Ferrero Rocher pralines, Kinder chocolate products (including Kinder Surprise eggs), Tic Tac mints, Raffaello and Mon Chéri. Through acquisitions it has added brands such as Butterfinger, Crunch and Thorntons to its portfolio.

History

Pietro Ferrero developed an early hazelnut-and-cocoa paste during post-war cocoa shortages; his son Michele Ferrero refined it into Nutella in 1964 and built the firm into a global business. The family-owned group remains controlled by the Ferrero family and is headquartered in Luxembourg, with production plants worldwide.
