Jedi

The Jedi are characters in the Star Wars franchise created by George Lucas. They are members of the Jedi Order, an ancient monastic order of Force-sensitive individuals who serve as guardians of peace and justice in the Galactic Republic, wielding lightsabers and drawing on the light side of the Force.

The Order

The Jedi Order, headquartered at the Jedi Temple on Coruscant, recruits Force-sensitive children and trains them from youngling to Padawan to Jedi Knight and Master, governed by the Jedi High Council. Prominent Jedi include Yoda, Obi-Wan Kenobi, Luke Skywalker, Mace Windu, and Anakin Skywalker before his fall to the dark side.

Fall and legacy

The Order was nearly exterminated by Order 66 at the end of the Clone Wars, with survivors such as Obi-Wan and Yoda going into exile. Luke Skywalker's later efforts to rebuild the Order feature in subsequent films and expanded media. The Jedi have also inspired a real-world movement of people identifying their religion as Jediism in censuses.
