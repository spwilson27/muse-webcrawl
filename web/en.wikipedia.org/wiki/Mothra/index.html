Mothra

Mothra (Japanese: モスラ, Hepburn: Mosura) is a fictional monster, or kaiju, that first appeared in the 1961 film Mothra, produced and distributed by Toho. Mothra has appeared in several Toho tokusatsu films, most often as a recurring character in the Godzilla franchise, and also appears in the American MonsterVerse film Godzilla: King of the Monsters (2019).

Mothra is depicted as a colossal sentient larva (caterpillar) that metamorphoses into a giant moth. She is accompanied by two tiny priestesses, the Shobijin, who communicate on her behalf and sing the song "Mothra's Song" to summon or appease her. Unlike most other kaiju, Mothra is usually portrayed as a benevolent guardian — a protector of Earth, of her island home of Infant Island, or of humanity.

The character is one of Toho's most popular creations, rivalled only by Godzilla in frequency of appearances, and is unusual as a female-presenting, heroic monster. She starred in her own trilogy, Rebirth of Mothra (1996–1998), featuring her offspring Mothra Leo. Mothra's life cycle — dying in battle and being reborn from an egg laid beforehand — is a recurring element of her stories.
