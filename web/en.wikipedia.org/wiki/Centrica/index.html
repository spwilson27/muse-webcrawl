Centrica plc is a British multinational energy and services company with its headquarters in Windsor, Berkshire. Its principal activity is the supply of electricity and gas to businesses and consumers in the United Kingdom and Ireland, and it also provides energy-related services. It is the largest supplier of gas to domestic customers in the UK, trading under the British Gas brand, and one of the largest domestic electricity suppliers.

History

Centrica was created on 17 February 1997, when British Gas plc demerged into two separate companies: Centrica, which took over gas supply, trading, services and retail businesses and the Morecambe gas fields, and BG plc (later BG Group), which took the gas exploration, production, transmission and distribution businesses. The demerger was designed to separate the competitive supply business from the regulated Transco monopoly.

Operations

The group's best-known business is British Gas, which supplies energy to millions of homes and businesses and provides central heating installation and repair, including through its HomeCare cover products. Centrica has also owned upstream oil and gas assets (including a majority stake in the Spirit Energy venture formed with Stadtwerke München in 2017), power stations, a stake in the UK's nuclear fleet through a 20% share in EDF Energy's nuclear generation business, and the Rough gas storage facility in the North Sea, the UK's largest, which was partially reopened in 2022 amid the European gas crisis. In North America it formerly owned Direct Energy, sold in 2020–21.

Criticism

Centrica and British Gas have faced recurring political and public criticism over profits during periods of high consumer energy prices and over practices such as the forced installation of prepayment meters in vulnerable customers' homes, which led to regulatory intervention in 2023.
