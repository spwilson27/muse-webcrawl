Sleep No More

Sleep No More is an immersive theatre production created by the British company Punchdrunk, loosely based on Shakespeare's Macbeth with elements of film noir and the Paisley witch trials. Audience members wear masks and wander freely through the performance space, following performers at will.

Creator: Punchdrunk
Premiere: 2009 (London); New York production opened 2011
Venue (NYC): The McKittrick Hotel, Chelsea

Format

The show contains almost no dialogue. Instead, the story unfolds simultaneously in dozens of rooms across several floors of a transformed warehouse styled as the 1930s McKittrick Hotel. A one-hour loop of scenes is performed three times per evening; individual audience members construct their own experience by choosing whom to follow, and a few are singled out for one-on-one encounters.

Reception and influence

The New York production, opened in 2011, became a long-running hit and is widely credited with popularising large-scale immersive theatre in the United States. Productions were also staged in Shanghai and Seoul.
