The Baseball Tonight studio

Baseball Tonight is an American television program that airs on ESPN and serves as the network's flagship nightly Major League Baseball highlights and analysis show. The program premiered in 1990 and typically airs following ESPN's baseball telecasts, including Sunday Night Baseball.

Format

The show combines game highlights, scores, standings updates, and analysis from former players, managers, and baseball journalists. Recurring segments have included home run calls, web gems highlighting outstanding defensive plays, and breakdowns of pitching mechanics. During the postseason, extended editions provide in-depth coverage of the Division Series, League Championship Series, and the World Series.

Personalities

Over its run, the program has been hosted by a rotating cast of ESPN anchors, with analysts including former major leaguers and longtime baseball writers. The show has also featured contributions from reporters stationed at ballparks around the league.

Related broadcasts

Baseball Tonight is part of ESPN's broader baseball coverage, which has included game telecasts since the network's early years and special programming around events such as the Home Run Derby and the MLB trade deadline.
