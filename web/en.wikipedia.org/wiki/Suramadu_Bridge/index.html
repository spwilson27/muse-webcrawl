The Suramadu Bridge (Indonesian: Jembatan Suramadu), also known as the Surabaya–Madura Bridge, is a cable-stayed bridge between Surabaya on the island of Java and the town of Bangkalan on the island of Madura in Indonesia. Opened in June 2009, the 5.4-km bridge is the longest in Indonesia and was the first bridge to cross the Madura Strait.

Construction

The cable-stayed portion has three spans with lengths 192 m, 434 m and 192 m. The bridge has two lanes in each direction plus an emergency lane and a dedicated lane for motorcycles. Construction began on 20 August 2003 and was carried out by Indonesian state-owned contractors together with Chinese firms including the China Road and Bridge Corporation. The bridge was inaugurated by President Susilo Bambang Yudhoyono on 10 June 2009.

Significance

The bridge was built to accelerate economic development on Madura, which had long been connected to Java only by ferry services from Tanjung Perak to Kamal. The crossing reduced travel time between Surabaya and Bangkalan from roughly half an hour by ferry to about ten minutes by road. The name "Suramadu" is a portmanteau of Surabaya and Madura.
