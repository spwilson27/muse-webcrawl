Ghost Rider

Publisher: Marvel Comics
First appearance: Marvel Spotlight #5 (August 1972)
Created by: Roy Thomas, Gary Friedrich, Mike Ploog
Principal hosts: Johnny Blaze, Danny Ketch, Robbie Reyes

Ghost Rider is the name of several antiheroes appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. The character is a supernatural "Spirit of Vengeance": a human host bonded to a demonic entity, who transforms into a skeletal figure with a flaming skull, rides a hellfire-infused motorcycle (or, in later incarnations, a muscle car), and punishes the wicked — most famously with the Penance Stare, which forces victims to experience all the pain they have ever inflicted on others.

Johnny Blaze

The first and best-known Ghost Rider is Johnny Blaze, a motorcycle stunt performer who made a deal with the demon Mephisto to save his adoptive father from cancer, and was instead bound to the spirit Zarathos. Debuting in 1972, Blaze headlined his own series through the 1970s and early 1980s and remains the character most associated with the name. (The name had earlier been used by a Western hero, later renamed the Phantom Rider.)

Successors

In 1990 a new Ghost Rider, teenager Danny Ketch, revived the franchise, introducing the Penance Stare and a chain-wielding, spike-jacketed redesign that proved highly popular. A third major incarnation, Robbie Reyes, debuted in 2014: a Los Angeles mechanic possessed by the spirit of his murderous uncle, who drives a black muscle car dubbed the Hell Charger instead of a motorcycle.

In other media

Nicolas Cage starred as Johnny Blaze in the films Ghost Rider (2007) and Ghost Rider: Spirit of Vengeance (2011). Gabriel Luna portrayed Robbie Reyes in the television series Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. The character has appeared across Marvel animation, video games, and crossover events.
