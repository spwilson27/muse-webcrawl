RER D

RER D is one of the five lines of the Réseau Express Régional, the hybrid suburban and rapid transit system serving Paris and the Île-de-France region. It runs broadly north–south through central Paris, linking northern suburbs, the city centre and southern destinations including Melun and Malesherbes branches.

Route and operation

The line shares the busy central tunnel between Châtelet–Les Halles and Gare de Lyon with RER A, a constraint that strongly affects capacity and reliability. Services are operated by SNCF and use double-deck and regional rolling stock suited to long suburban runs.

Importance

RER D is among the busiest commuter routes in the Paris region, carrying passengers from dense suburbs into major interchange stations. Its performance is closely watched because delays in the shared tunnel can affect two of the network&rsquo;s principal lines at once.

The line is part of the same regional express family as RER A, B, C and E.
