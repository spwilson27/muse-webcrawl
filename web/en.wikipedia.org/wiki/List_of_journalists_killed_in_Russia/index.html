List of journalists killed in Russia

The dangers to journalists in Russia have been well documented since the early 1990s. Dozens of journalists have been killed in Russia since the beginning of the 1990s, many of them while investigating corruption, organised crime, human rights abuses and the wars in Chechnya. Press freedom organisations such as the Committee to Protect Journalists (CPJ) and the Glasnost Defense Foundation have maintained records of these deaths; a striking feature of many cases is that the killings remain unsolved and those who ordered them are rarely brought to justice.

Particularly dangerous periods were the First and Second Chechen Wars and the years 1993–2000, although high-profile murders continued into the 2000s.

Chechnya

Numerous journalists, cameramen and fixers were killed during the two Chechen wars (1994–96 and 1999–2009), including foreign correspondents. Among them were the German cameraman Jochen Piest (1995), the Russian photographer Vladimir Yatsina (1999), and the Italian journalist Antonio Russo, found dead in Georgia in 2000 after reporting from Chechnya.

Statistics and impunity

The CPJ has repeatedly ranked Russia among the deadliest countries for journalists and among the worst in its Impunity Index, which measures unsolved journalist murders as a share of population. Russian authorities dispute some characterisations, noting convictions in a number of cases, including those of the killers of Politkovskaya (gunmen convicted in 2014) and Baburova.
