Norah Jones

[Image: Norah Jones performing]

Norah Jones (born Geethali Norah Jones Shankar; March 30, 1979) is an American singer, songwriter, and pianist. She has won multiple Grammy Awards and as of 2023 had sold more than 50 million records worldwide. Billboard named her the top jazz artist of the 2000s decade.

Jones is the daughter of Indian sitarist and composer Ravi Shankar and American dancer and producer Sue Jones. She was raised in New York City and Grapevine, Texas, and attended the University of North Texas, where she studied jazz piano and sang with the UNT Jazz Singers before leaving to pursue a music career in New York.

Career

After signing with Blue Note Records, Jones released her debut album Come Away with Me in 2002. The record, a blend of jazz, country, blues, folk, and pop, achieved critical and commercial success, reaching number one on the Billboard 200 and winning eight Grammy Awards in 2003, including Album of the Year and Record of the Year for "Don't Know Why".

Her subsequent studio albums include Feels Like Home (2004), Not Too Late (2007), The Fall (2009), Little Broken Hearts (2012), produced by Danger Mouse, Day Breaks (2016), Begin Again (2019), Pick Me Up Off the Floor (2020), the holiday album I Dream of Christmas (2021), and Visions (2024). She has also collaborated with the country band The Little Willies and the trio Puss n Boots, and acted in the 2007 film My Blueberry Nights.

Musical style

Jones's music is noted for her warm, smoky contralto voice and understated piano playing, drawing on influences such as Billie Holiday, Nina Simone, Aretha Franklin, and Willie Nelson.
