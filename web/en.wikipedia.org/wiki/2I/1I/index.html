2I/Borisov (chain page)

2I/1I is a composite link path in the chain of references concerning the interstellar object 2I/Borisov, the first observed interstellar comet and the second detected interstellar visitor after 1I/ʻOumuamua.

2I/Borisov

Borisov was discovered on 30 August 2019 by amateur astronomer Gennadiy Borisov using a telescope he built himself. Its hyperbolic trajectory and speed confirmed an origin outside the Solar System; its coma and tail showed that, unlike the enigmatic ʻOumuamua, it was an active comet broadly similar to Solar System comets. The leaf segment "1I" refers to 1I/ʻOumuamua, the first interstellar object, through which this chain routes the Borisov discussion.

Observations

Borisov made its closest approach to the Sun in December 2019 and was observed by the Hubble Space Telescope and large ground-based observatories before leaving the planetary region.
