Project Lyra

Project Lyra
OrganisationInitiative for Interstellar Studies
Founded2017
ObjectiveAssess missions to interstellar objects

Project Lyra is a feasibility study run by the Initiative for Interstellar Studies (i4is) examining whether a spacecraft could be sent to intercept an interstellar object passing through the Solar System. It was launched in October 2017, shortly after the discovery of 1I/ʻOumuamua.

Mission concepts

Because ʻOumuamua was already receding at high speed when discovered, reaching it requires extreme velocities. Studied concepts include solar Oberth manoeuvres, Jupiter gravity assists, chemical and nuclear propulsion, and solar or laser sails. Some trajectories could reach the object in a few decades.

Legacy

Project Lyra helped establish the scientific case for keeping a spacecraft ready to chase future interstellar visitors, an idea embodied by the European Space Agency's Comet Interceptor mission, which will wait in space for a suitable target.
