Solar System (in the 1I context)

The Solar System is the gravitationally bound system of the Sun and the objects that orbit it, formed 4.6 billion years ago. It comprises eight planets, their moons, dwarf planets, and small bodies such as asteroids and comets.

Interstellar visitors

This page is reached through the chain 2I → 1I, the first two identified interstellar objects to pass through the Solar System: 1I/ʻOumuamua (2017) and 2I/Borisov (2019). Both arrived on hyperbolic trajectories from interstellar space, demonstrating that material from other planetary systems can transit our own.

Structure

The inner Solar System contains the terrestrial planets Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars; the outer system holds the gas giants Jupiter and Saturn and the ice giants Uranus and Neptune, beyond which lie the Kuiper belt and the Oort cloud.
