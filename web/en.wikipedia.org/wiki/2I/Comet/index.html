2I

2I may refer to 2I/Borisov, the second observed interstellar object and the first interstellar comet, which passed through the Solar System in 2019.

2I/Borisov

2I/Borisov is a comet discovered by Crimean amateur astronomer Gennady Borisov on 30 August 2019. With a hyperbolic trajectory and an eccentricity of about 3.36, it is not bound to the Sun, confirming its interstellar origin. It was the first interstellar comet and second interstellar object observed, after 1I/ʻOumuamua in 2017.
