Comet Interceptor
AgencyEuropean Space Agency (with JAXA)
Mission typeComet flyby
Planned launch2029 (with ARIEL)
Waiting positionSun–Earth L2 point

Comet Interceptor is a European Space Agency mission designed to visit a pristine long-period comet — or possibly an interstellar object — entering the inner Solar System for the first time. Unusually, the spacecraft will be launched before its target is known: it will wait at the Sun–Earth L2 Lagrange point until a suitable comet is discovered.

Spacecraft

The mission comprises a main spacecraft and two small probes, one contributed by the Japanese space agency JAXA. At the encounter the three components will separate to observe the comet's nucleus and coma from multiple vantage points simultaneously.

Goals

By studying a dynamically new comet that has never been heated by the Sun, scientists hope to learn about the primordial materials from which the planets formed. An interstellar target like 2I/Borisov would be a bonus of exceptional value.
