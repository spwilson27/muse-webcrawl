An asteroid is a minor planet — a rocky, airless body smaller than a planet — that orbits the Sun, composed mainly of rock and metal. Millions of asteroids exist, most of them in the main asteroid belt between the orbits of Mars and Jupiter.

Classification

Asteroids are grouped by composition into carbon-rich C-types, siliceous S-types and metallic M-types, among others. Near-Earth asteroids have orbits that bring them close to Earth and are monitored for impact risk.

Exploration

Spacecraft have orbited, landed on and returned samples from several asteroids, including Eros, Itokawa, Ryugu and Bennu. In 2017 the first known interstellar asteroid-like object, 1I/ʻOumuamua, passed through the Solar System from another star.
