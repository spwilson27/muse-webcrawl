The BlackBerry Classic is a smartphone developed by BlackBerry Limited, released in December 2014. It was designed to evoke the company's classic Bold series, combining a physical QWERTY keyboard, an optical trackpad, and hardware navigation keys with the BlackBerry 10 operating system.

Design and features

The Classic features a 3.5-inch square touchscreen above a full physical keyboard, a return to the form factor that had defined BlackBerry's earlier success. It runs BlackBerry 10 with the BlackBerry Hub, supports Android applications through an included runtime and the Amazon Appstore, and includes the BlackBerry Assistant voice control.

Reception and legacy

The phone was aimed at loyal keyboard users and enterprise customers. BlackBerry ended production of the Classic in 2016 as the company shifted away from manufacturing its own hardware, making it one of the last devices in the traditional BlackBerry mould.
