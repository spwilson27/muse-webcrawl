List of Arrested Development episodes

Arrested Development is an American television sitcom created by Mitchell Hurwitz. The series originally aired on Fox from November 2, 2003, to February 10, 2006, running for three seasons and 53 episodes. Netflix revived the series for a fourth season of 15 episodes in 2013 and a fifth season released in two parts in 2018–2019, bringing the total to 84 episodes.

Selected episodes

Season 1 begins with "Pilot" and includes "Pier Pressure", "Marta Complex", and "Let 'Em Eat Cake". Season 2 includes "The One Where Michael Leaves", "Afternoon Delight", "Motherboy XXX", and "The Righteous Brothers". Season 3 includes "The Cabin Show", "Mr. F", "S.O.B.s", and the finale "Development Arrested". Season 4 episodes are titled by character ("Michael I", "George Sr. I", "Lindsay I"...), and Season 5 includes "Family Leave", "Self-Deportation", and the series finale "The Fallout".
