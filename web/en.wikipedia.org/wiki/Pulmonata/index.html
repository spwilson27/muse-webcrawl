Pulmonata is an informal group of snails and slugs characterised by the ability to breathe air using a pallial lung instead of gills. It includes most land snails and slugs as well as many freshwater and some marine gastropods.

Characteristics

Pulmonates have a vascularised mantle cavity that functions as a lung, opening to the outside through a pneumostome. Most are hermaphroditic. Familiar members include garden snails, pond snails and terrestrial slugs.

Classification

Traditional taxonomy treated Pulmonata as an order or subclass, but modern phylogenetic work shows the group is not a single natural clade in its old sense, and its members are now distributed within the Eupulmonata and related groups of Heterobranchia.
