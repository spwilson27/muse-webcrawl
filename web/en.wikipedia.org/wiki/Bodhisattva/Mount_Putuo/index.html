Mount Putuo

Mount Putuo (Chinese: 普陀山; pinyin: Pǔtuóshān) is an island in the East China Sea, in Zhoushan, Zhejiang province, China. It is one of the Four Sacred Mountains of Chinese Buddhism, venerated as the bodhimaṇḍa (abode) of Guanyin, the bodhisattva of compassion (Sanskrit: Avalokiteśvara), and a major pilgrimage destination for over a thousand years.

History

The mountain's name derives from Mount Potalaka, the legendary island abode of Avalokiteśvara described in the Avataṁsaka Sūtra. According to legend, in 863 CE the Japanese monk Egaku (Huì'è) was carrying a statue of Guanyin back to Japan when storms repeatedly blocked his ship near the island; taking this as a sign, he left the statue ashore, and a local resident donated his house as the "Guanyin Temple of Refusing to Leave" (不肯去觀音院), the seed of the island's cult. Imperial patronage in the Song dynasty made Putuo the pre-eminent Guanyin pilgrimage center in China, drawing monks and pilgrims from Japan, Korea, and Southeast Asia.

Temples

The island hosts dozens of temples and monasteries. The three great temples are Puji Temple (founded as the Temple of Guanyin Refusing to Leave), Fayu Temple, and Huiji Temple on the summit of Foding Mountain, reached by cable car or a stairway of over a thousand steps. Other sites include the Cave of the Tidal Sound (Chaoyin), where Guanyin is said to have appeared, the Thousand-Step Beach, and the 33-metre bronze Nanhai Guanyin statue, completed in 1997, which has become the island's landmark.

Pilgrimage

Putuo receives millions of visitors a year, especially on Guanyin's three feast days (her birth, enlightenment, and renunciation in the lunar calendar). The island is car-free in its temple areas and is administered partly as a scenic area; ferry services connect it with Zhoushan's main island, Ningbo, and Shanghai. Along with Mount Wutai (Mañjuśrī), Mount Emei (Samantabhadra), and Mount Jiuhua (Kṣitigarbha), it completes the set of China's four great Buddhist mountains, each the seat of a great bodhisattva.
