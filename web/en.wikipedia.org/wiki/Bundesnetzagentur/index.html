Bundesnetzagentur

Bundesnetzagentur
English: Federal Network Agency
Headquarters: Bonn, Germany
Founded: 1998 (as RegTP); renamed 2005
Sectors: Electricity, gas, telecommunications, post, rail

The Bundesnetzagentur für Elektrizität, Gas, Telekommunikation, Post und Eisenbahnen (Federal Network Agency for Electricity, Gas, Telecommunications, Posts and Railway), abbreviated BNetzA, is the German federal regulatory agency for network industries. It operates under the Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Climate Action and is headquartered in Bonn.

History

The agency originated in 1998 as the Regulatory Authority for Telecommunications and Posts (RegTP), created alongside the liberalization of German telecommunications and postal markets. In 2005 it assumed responsibility for electricity and gas network regulation and took its present name; railway infrastructure regulation followed.

Responsibilities

The agency oversees network access and charges, consumer protection, frequency allocation (including spectrum auctions for mobile networks), approval of electricity grid expansion needed for the energy transition (Energiewende), and monitoring of energy markets. During the 2022 energy crisis, it assumed a prominent public role in gas supply management, and its president Klaus Müller became a familiar public figure. The agency also acts against illegal products and prohibited transmitters through its radio monitoring service.
