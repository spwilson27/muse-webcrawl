Enterprise architecture framework

An enterprise architecture framework (EA framework) is a structured scheme for creating, organising and using an enterprise architecture. Frameworks define what kinds of models and descriptions ("artefacts" and "deliverables") should be produced, from which viewpoints, and often what process to follow in developing them. They provide a common vocabulary that lets architects, executives and engineers discuss a complex organisation in manageable parts.

Structure and use

Most frameworks organise architecture descriptions into layers or domains — business, data (information), application and technology — and into states: current ("as-is"), target ("to-be") and the transition roadmaps between them. Surveys of the industry regularly find that a majority of large organisations use a framework, most often TOGAF, frequently tailored and combined with internal standards. Critics note that frameworks can encourage over-documentation, and that none of them guarantees good architecture by itself; the framework is a tool, while the outcome depends on the practice built around it.
