Rhein II

Rhein II
Artist: Andreas Gursky
Year: 1999
Medium: C-print photograph
Subject: The River Rhine near Düsseldorf

Rhein II is a photograph made by the German artist Andreas Gursky in 1999. It depicts the River Rhine near Düsseldorf, horizontally bisected by bands of water, grassy bank, footpath and an overcast sky. In 2011 a print sold at auction for $4.3 million, at the time the most expensive photograph ever sold.

Description

The image shows a minimalist, almost abstract composition: parallel horizontal strips of grey river, green embankment and grey sky. Gursky digitally removed distracting details from the scene, including a factory building, passers-by and a dog walker, explaining that a "parallel existence" of the elements interested him and that the picture could not have been taken without such intervention.

Production

Rhein II was the second and largest of a set of six photographs of the Rhine made by Gursky in the 1990s. It was printed as a chromogenic colour print face-mounted to acrylic glass, measuring about 156 cm by 308 cm including the frame. An edition of six prints was made.

Sale

On 8 November 2011, a print was sold at Christie's in New York for $4,338,500 (about £2.7 million), a world record for a photograph at auction. It surpassed Cindy Sherman's Untitled #96, which had held the record at $3.9 million. The Rhein II record itself was later surpassed by other photographic works in the 2020s.
