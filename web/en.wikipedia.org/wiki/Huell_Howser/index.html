Huell Howser

Huell Burnley Howser
Born: 18 October 1945, Gallatin, Tennessee, U.S.
Died: 7 January 2013, Palm Springs, California, U.S.
Occupation: Television host, producer
Known for: California's Gold

Huell Burnley Howser (October 18, 1945 – January 7, 2013) was an American television personality, actor and voice artist, best known for hosting California's Gold, a public-television travel series celebrating the people, places and history of California, produced by KCET in Los Angeles.

Early life

Howser was born and raised in Gallatin, Tennessee, and graduated from the University of Tennessee, where he served as student body president. After military service in the U.S. Marine Corps and work as an aide to Senator Howard Baker, he began his broadcasting career in Nashville.

California's Gold

After moving to Los Angeles and working as a reporter for KCBS-TV, Howser joined KCET and launched California's Gold in 1991. Travelling with a single cameraman, he visited state parks, small towns, family businesses and little-known historical sites, greeting nearly everything with his signature enthusiasm — "That's amazing!" The series ran for 18 seasons and more than 440 episodes, alongside spin-offs such as Visiting... with Huell Howser and Road Trip.

Legacy

Howser retired in 2012 and died the following year. He donated his papers and episode tapes to Chapman University, which maintains the Huell Howser Archives, and endowed a scholarship for California students. He voiced himself in an episode of The Simpsons ("O Brother, Where Bart Thou?") as "Huell Howser" parody figure.
