International Towers
Location: Barangaroo, Sydney, Australia
Status: Complete
Towers: Three commercial skyscrapers
Completed: 2015–2016

International Towers is a commercial skyscraper complex of three buildings in Barangaroo, Sydney, Australia. Known during construction as C3, C4 and C5 and afterwards as One, Two and Three International Towers, the development forms the commercial core of the Barangaroo South precinct on the western edge of Sydney's central business district.

Design

The towers were designed by Rogers Stirk Harbour + Partners. Tower Two, the largest, rises 217 metres and was among the largest commercial buildings in Australia by floor area on completion. The buildings were designed to high environmental standards, with the precinct targeting carbon-neutral and water-positive outcomes, and their lobbies and podium incorporate extensive public art and retail space.

Tenants

Anchor tenants have included major banks, accounting and professional-services firms and technology companies, making the complex one of Sydney's principal financial centres alongside the established eastern CBD.
