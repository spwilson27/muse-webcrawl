Daria Morgendorffer

Daria Morgendorffer is a fictional character and the protagonist of the MTV animated series Daria (1997–2002). Voiced by Tracy Grandstaff, she first appeared as a recurring character on Beavis and Butt-Head before receiving her own spin-off series created by Glenn Eichler and Susie Lewis Lynn.

Character

Daria is a highly intelligent, sarcastic and sardonic teenage girl living in the fictional suburb of Lawndale with her workaholic lawyer mother Helen, her stress-prone father Jake, and her popularity-obsessed younger sister Quinn. Dressed in glasses, a green jacket, black skirt and combat boots, she observes the absurdities of high school and suburban life with deadpan wit. Her best friend is the artistic Jane Lane; their misadventures and conversations anchor the show.

Reception and legacy

Daria has become an iconic figure of 1990s popular culture, frequently cited as a feminist and countercultural heroine celebrated for her intellect, dry humour and refusal to conform. The character headlines two television films, Is It Fall Yet? (2000) and Is It College Yet? (2002), and has been the subject of renewed interest through reboots and spin-offs such as Jodie.
