2K is an American video game publisher based in Novato, California. It is a publishing label of Take-Two Interactive, founded in January 2005 following Take-Two's acquisition of the developer Visual Concepts and Sega's sports game properties.

Franchises

2K publishes several major franchises through its studios, including the NBA 2K basketball series, the WWE 2K wrestling series, and internally developed series such as BioShock, Borderlands, Mafia, Civilization (through Firaxis), and XCOM. Its studios have included 2K Marin, Irrational Games, Firaxis Games, Hangar 13, Gearbox Software, and Visual Concepts.

History

After acquiring Visual Concepts for about 4 million, Take-Two consolidated its publishing under the 2K Games and 2K Sports labels. The label grew into one of the largest third-party publishers, balancing annual sports titles with large narrative and strategy franchises.
