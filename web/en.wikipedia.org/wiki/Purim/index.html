Purim

Purim is a Jewish holiday that commemorates the saving of the Jewish people from annihilation at the hands of the Achaemenid Empire, as recounted in the Book of Esther. It is celebrated on the 14th day of Adar (15th in walled cities) with feasting, gifts, charity, and the public reading of the Megillah.

Story

According to Esther, the royal vizier Haman planned to kill all Jews in Persia. Esther, the Jewish queen of King Ahasuerus, and her cousin Mordecai foiled the plot; Haman was executed and the Jews were granted the right to defend themselves.

Customs

Observances include listening to the Megillah, giving mishloach manot (food gifts), matanot la'evyonim (charity to the poor), a festive meal, wearing costumes, and eating hamantaschen, triangular filled pastries. Noisemakers called groggers are shaken when Haman's name is read.
