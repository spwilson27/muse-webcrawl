Armin Jozef Jacobus Daniël van Buuren (born 25 December 1976) is a Dutch DJ and record producer, one of the most prominent figures in trance music. He was ranked the world's No. 1 DJ by DJ Mag a record five times and has hosted the weekly radio show A State of Trance since 2001, broadcast to tens of millions of listeners.

Career

Van Buuren began producing while studying law at Leiden University, releasing early tracks under names such as Gaia and Rising Star. Singles including "Communication" (1999) and collaborations such as "Exhale" built his reputation; his studio albums include 76 (2003), Shivers (2005), Imagine (2008), Mirage (2010), Intense (2013) — whose single "This Is What It Feels Like" was Grammy-nominated — Embrace (2015), and Balance (2019).

A State of Trance and Armada

His radio show grew into the ASOT festival brand with large arena events worldwide. In 2003 he co-founded the label Armada Music, which became one of the largest independent dance music labels. Van Buuren's marathon DJ sets, including the Armin Only shows, are a hallmark of his career.
