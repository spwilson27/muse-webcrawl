Medal table

A medal table is a tabulation of the number of medals won by each competing team or nation during a sporting event, most prominently the Olympic Games. Medal tables typically list gold, silver and bronze medals, together with a total.

Ranking methods

The International Olympic Committee does not publish an official ranking, but media outlets commonly rank nations first by gold medals won, then silver, then bronze — the system used in most of the world. An alternative, once common in the United States, ranks by total medals. The choice of method can change which nation appears at the top of the table.

History

The modern three-medal system dates to the 1904 Summer Olympics in St. Louis. Medal tables have since become a standard summary device for multi-sport events including the Paralympics, Commonwealth Games and Asian Games, and a subject of national prestige and debate.
