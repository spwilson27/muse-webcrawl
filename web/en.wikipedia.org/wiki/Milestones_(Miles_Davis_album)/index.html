Milestones (Miles Davis album)

Milestones is a studio album by the American jazz trumpeter Miles Davis, released in September 1958 by Columbia Records. It features Davis's sextet with saxophonists John Coltrane and Cannonball Adderley, pianist Red Garland, bassist Paul Chambers, and drummer Philly Joe Jones.

Music

The album is a landmark of modal jazz: the title track "Milestones" and "Sid's Ahead" explore improvisation over scales rather than fast-changing chords, pointing toward the approach Davis would fully develop on Kind of Blue the following year. The set also includes Thelonious Monk's "Straight, No Chaser", "Two Bass Hit", "Billy Boy" (a trio feature for Garland, Chambers, and Jones), and "Dr. Jackle".

Legacy

Milestones is widely regarded as one of Davis's essential recordings and a bridge between hard bop and modal jazz.
