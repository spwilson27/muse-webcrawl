Eddie Gay Robinson Sr. (February 13, 1919 – April 3, 2007) was an American football coach. For 56 years, from 1941 to 1942 and again from 1945 to 1997, he was the head coach at Grambling State University, a historically black university in Louisiana.

Career

Robinson retired with a record of 408 wins, 165 losses and 15 ties, at the time the most wins by any coach in NCAA football history. He coached more than 200 players who went on to professional football, including NFL Hall of Famers Buck Buchanan, Willie Brown, Charlie Joiner and Willie Davis.

Legacy

Robinson won numerous black college national championships and SWAC titles. The Football Writers Association of America's Eddie Robinson Award is named in his honour, as is the Eddie Robinson Museum in Grambling.
