Wikipedia:In the news/Criteria

In the news is the section of Wikipedia's Main Page presenting short summaries of current events. Items are proposed by editors and selected against criteria intended to ensure the underlying article is updated, verifiable and of encyclopaedic significance.

How items are chosen

Editors nominate stories at the In the news candidates page, discuss whether the event is significant and the target article adequately updated, and post blurred, neutral one-line summaries linking to the article rather than to external news sources.
