List of grape varieties

This is a list of grape varieties, covering cultivars of Vitis vinifera and other Vitis species used for wine, table grapes and raisins. Thousands of varieties exist; the most planted wine grape in the world is generally considered to be Cabernet Sauvignon, while Airén of Spain and Sultanina (Thompson Seedless) cover enormous areas for brandy, table and raisin production.
