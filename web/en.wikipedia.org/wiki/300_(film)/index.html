300 (film)

300 is a 2006 American epic period action film co-written and directed by Zack Snyder, based on the 1998 comic series of the same name by Frank Miller and Lynn Varley. The film is a fictionalized retelling of the Battle of Thermopylae (480 BC) within the Greco-Persian Wars. It stars Gerard Butler as King Leonidas of Sparta, who leads 300 Spartans into battle against the Persian king Xerxes (Rodrigo Santoro) and his vast invading army.

Production

Like Sin City (2005), the film was shot almost entirely against bluescreen at Icestorm Studios in Montreal, with digital environments composited to reproduce the look of Miller's graphic novel — desaturated, high-contrast imagery with stylized blood and slow-motion combat.

Reception

300 grossed over $456 million worldwide on a budget of about $65 million, becoming a major box-office success and a touchstone of mid-2000s pop culture ("This is Sparta!" became a widespread meme). Critics praised its visuals while debating its stylized violence and portrayal of Persians. A prequel/sequel, 300: Rise of an Empire, followed in 2014.
