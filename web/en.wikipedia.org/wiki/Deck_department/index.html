Deck department

The deck department is an organisational unit aboard a naval or merchant vessel, comprising members of the ship's crew whose primary responsibilities involve navigation, cargo handling, maintenance of the hull and deck equipment, and watchkeeping. It is headed by the chief mate (chief officer), who reports to the ship's master.

Roles

Officers of the deck department include the chief mate, second mate, third mate, and deck cadets, responsible for safe navigation, cargo planning and stability. Ratings include the boatswain (bosun), able seamen and ordinary seamen, who perform mooring, anchoring, cleaning, painting and maintenance tasks. On naval vessels, the deck department (or deck division) additionally handles seamanship evolutions such as underway replenishment and small-boat operations.
