Refugee

Refugee
DefinitionPerson forced to flee their country by persecution, war or violence
Law1951 Refugee Convention; UNHCR protection
ScaleOver 100 million forcibly displaced people worldwide (2020s, UNHCR)
[Image: A refugee camp of white tents stretching to the horizon]

A refugee is a person who has been forced to flee their country because of persecution, war or violence, and who cannot safely return. The 1951 Refugee Convention, administered by the UNHCR, forbids returning refugees to danger (non-refoulement) and defines the status distinct from economic migration.

Crises

Syria, Afghanistan, Ukraine, Sudan and others produced the largest displacement since the Second World War; over 100 million people are forcibly displaced. Host states (often poor neighbours) bear most of the burden while asylum politics hardens in wealthy countries.
