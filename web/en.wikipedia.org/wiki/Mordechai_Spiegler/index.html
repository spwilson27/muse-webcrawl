Mordechai Spiegler (Hebrew: מרדכי שפיגלר; born 19 August 1944) is an Israeli former footballer who played as a forward. He is best known as the scorer of Israel's only goal at a FIFA World Cup, at the 1970 tournament in Mexico, and is considered one of the greatest Israeli footballers of all time.

Career

Spiegler was born in Sochi in the Soviet Union and immigrated to Israel as a child, growing up in Netanya. He spent most of his club career with Maccabi Netanya, and also played in France for Paris FC and Paris Saint-Germain, and in the United States for the New York Cosmos, where he played alongside Pelé.

International career

Spiegler won 83 caps for the Israel national team, scoring 24 goals. He was part of the team that won the 1964 AFC Asian Cup, and captained Israel at the 1970 FIFA World Cup, where he scored in a 1–1 draw with Sweden — Israel's first and, to date, only goal at a World Cup finals.
