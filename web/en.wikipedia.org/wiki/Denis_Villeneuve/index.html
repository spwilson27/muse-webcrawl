Denis Villeneuve

Born3 October 1967, Bécancour, Quebec, Canada
OccupationFilm director, screenwriter

Denis Villeneuve (born 1967) is a Canadian filmmaker. He first gained acclaim for French-language films including Maelström (2000) and Incendies (2010), then moved to English-language cinema with Prisoners (2013), Sicario (2015) and Arrival (2016), the latter earning him an Academy Award nomination for Best Director.

Later work

Villeneuve directed Blade Runner 2049 (2017) and the blockbuster adaptations Dune (2021) and Dune: Part Two (2024), which together won multiple Academy Awards and established him as one of the leading directors of large-scale science fiction.
