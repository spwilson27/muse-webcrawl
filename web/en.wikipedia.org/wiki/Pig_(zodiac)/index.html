Pig (zodiac)

The Pig (豬) is the twelfth and final sign of the Chinese zodiac, a twelve-year cycle of animal signs used in Chinese astrology and adopted across East and Southeast Asia. In Japanese and some other traditions the sign is represented by the wild boar (猪, inoshishi) rather than the domestic pig. The Pig is associated with the earthly branch hài (亥) and the hours 9–11 in the evening.

Years

Recent and upcoming Pig years include 1971 (metal), 1983 (water), 1995 (wood), 2007 (fire), 2019 (earth), and 2031 (metal). The year of the Pig begins at Chinese New Year, so people born in January or February may fall under the preceding sign, the Dog. Each sign cycles through the five elements — wood, fire, earth, metal and water — giving a sixty-year cycle.

Personality traits

In popular astrology, people born in Pig years are said to be sincere, honest, trusting, generous and diligent, with a love of comfort and good food; their weaknesses are said to include naivety and credulity. Compatibility traditions hold the Pig to be most compatible with the Rabbit and the Goat, and least compatible with the Snake.

Legend

Folklore explaining the order of the zodiac tells that in the Great Race held by the Jade Emperor, the Pig overslept or stopped to eat and feast, arriving last — hence its twelfth place. In Chinese tradition the pig also symbolises wealth and abundance, and the character for "home" (家) combines the roof radical with the pig.
