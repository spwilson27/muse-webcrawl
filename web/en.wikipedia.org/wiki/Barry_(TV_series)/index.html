Barry is an American black comedy-drama television series created by Bill Hader and Alec Berg for HBO. Hader stars as Barry Berkman, a former U.S. Marine turned Cleveland hitman who, while travelling to Los Angeles to carry out a killing, stumbles into an acting class run by Gene Cousineau (Henry Winkler) and decides he wants to become an actor — a wish that collides disastrously with his handler Monroe Fuches (Stephen Root), the Chechen mob, and his talented but self-absorbed classmate Sally Reed (Sarah Goldberg).

Premise and production

The series ran for four seasons of eight episodes each between 2018 and 2023. Hader directed many episodes, including all of the final season. Barry is noted for its abrupt tonal shifts between farce and harrowing violence, its formally daring direction — such as the largely wordless motorcycle chase of the third season and the single-take shootout sequences — and for growing progressively darker as Barry's attempts to escape his past cause ever more damage.

Reception

Barry received widespread critical acclaim, appearing on many critics' lists of the best television series of its era. It won multiple Primetime Emmy Awards, including Outstanding Lead Actor in a Comedy Series for Hader (twice) and Outstanding Supporting Actor for Winkler, and was nominated for Outstanding Comedy Series for each of its first three seasons. Anthony Carrigan's turn as the unfailingly polite Chechen mobster NoHo Hank became a fan favourite.
