Cyclops (Scott Summers) is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby, he first appeared in The X-Men #1 (1963) as a founding member of the X-Men.

Powers and character

Cyclops is a mutant who emits powerful optic blasts of concussive force from his eyes. Because he cannot control the blasts — a consequence of a childhood head injury in most accounts — he wears a visor or glasses of ruby quartz, the only material that blocks them. Depicted as the X-Men's field leader and Professor Xavier's first student, he is characterized by discipline and tactical skill. His long-running relationships with Jean Grey, and his family ties to Havok and Vulcan, are central X-Men storylines. In film he has been portrayed by James Marsden and by Tye Sheridan.
