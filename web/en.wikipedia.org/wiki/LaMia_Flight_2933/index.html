LaMia Flight 2933 was a charter flight operated by the Bolivian airline LaMia that crashed on 28 November 2016 near Medellín, Colombia, killing 71 of the 77 people on board. The Avro RJ85 was carrying the Brazilian football team Chapecoense, which was travelling to play the first leg of the 2016 Copa Sudamericana Finals against Atlético Nacional.

Accident

The flight departed Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia, with insufficient fuel for the journey, the flight plan having dispensed with a refuelling stop. While holding near its destination awaiting landing clearance, the aircraft exhausted its fuel; its engines flamed out in sequence, electrical power was lost, and the aircraft struck the Cerro El Gordo mountain near La Unión, Antioquia, at about 21:59 local time.

Survivors and aftermath

Six people survived: three Chapecoense players (Alan Ruschel, Jackson Follmann, and Neto), two crew members, and one journalist. The final report of the Colombian aviation authority Aerocivil attributed the accident to fuel exhaustion caused by the operator's inadequate planning and the flight crew's decisions. Atlético Nacional asked CONMEBOL to award the Copa Sudamericana title to Chapecoense, which was done; the disaster became one of the deadliest accidents involving a sports team.
