List of Meryl Streep performances

Meryl Streep (born 1949) is an American actress regarded as one of the greatest performers of her generation. She made her film debut in Julia (1977) and rose to prominence with The Deer Hunter (1978) and Kramer vs. Kramer (1979). She holds the record for the most Academy Award nominations of any actor, with 21 nominations and three wins.

Stage

Early stage work included productions at the Yale Repertory Theatre and the New York Shakespeare Festival, including Trelawny of the "Wells" (1975), A Memory of Two Mondays / 27 Wagons Full of Cotton (1976), The Cherry Orchard (1977), and Mother Courage and Her Children (2006, Delacorte Theatre).
