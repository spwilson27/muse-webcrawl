ICOMOS

The International Council on Monuments and Sites (ICOMOS) is an international non-governmental organisation dedicated to the conservation of cultural heritage sites and monuments worldwide. Founded in Warsaw in 1965 following the adoption of the Venice Charter in 1964, it is based in Charenton-le-Pont, near Paris, and has national committees in more than 100 countries.

Role

ICOMOS is one of the three formal advisory bodies to the UNESCO World Heritage Committee, evaluating nominations of cultural properties to the World Heritage List and monitoring the state of conservation of listed sites. It develops doctrinal texts and charters, including guidance on restoration, cultural landscapes, historic towns and heritage impact assessments.

Structure

Its membership includes architects, archaeologists, historians, planners and conservators organised in national committees and international scientific committees devoted to topics such as earthen architecture, rock art, underwater heritage and risk preparedness. ICOMOS coordinates the annual International Day for Monuments and Sites on 18 April.
