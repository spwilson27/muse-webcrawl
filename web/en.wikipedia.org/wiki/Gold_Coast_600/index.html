Gold Coast 600

The Gold Coast 600 (formally known as the Boost Mobile Gold Coast 500 and previously the Gold Coast 600 under its earlier format) is an annual motor racing event for Supercars, held at the Surfers Paradise Street Circuit in Surfers Paradise, Queensland, Australia. The event has been a part of the Supercars Championship since 2010, though the street circuit itself hosted IndyCar and Champ Car races from 1991 as the Gold Coast Indy 300.

From 2010 to 2012, the event ran as an endurance race in which each Supercars driver was paired with an international guest driver. From 2013 it adopted the "Pirtek Enduro Cup" two-driver 600 km format, and later became a pair of sprint-length races.

The narrow concrete-lined circuit, famous for its fast chicanes along the beachfront, is regarded as one of the most demanding on the calendar, and the event is a major fixture of the Gold Coast sporting calendar.
