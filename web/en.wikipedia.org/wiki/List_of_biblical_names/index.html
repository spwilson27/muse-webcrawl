List of biblical names

The List of biblical names is an index of the proper names of persons mentioned in the Bible — more than three thousand in total, ranging from the primordial figures of Genesis to the minor officials of the Persian and Roman periods. Many of these names, such as John, Mary, David, and Sarah, remain among the most common given names in the world.

Scope

The list spans the Hebrew Bible (Old Testament) and the New Testament. It includes patriarchs and matriarchs, kings of Israel and Judah, prophets, priests, apostles, and numerous otherwise unknown individuals named only in genealogies and legal records. Some names, like "Jesus" (Yeshua/Joshua), are borne by several different figures.
