The Founder is a 2016 American biographical drama film directed by John Lee Hancock and written by Robert Siegel. It tells the story of Ray Kroc, the travelling salesman who partnered with brothers Richard and Maurice McDonald and ultimately took control of their hamburger business, building it into the McDonald's restaurant empire.

Plot

In 1954, struggling milkshake-mixer salesman Ray Kroc (Michael Keaton) discovers the McDonald brothers' San Bernardino restaurant and its revolutionary "Speedee Service System." He becomes their franchising agent and expands the chain, but clashes with the brothers over quality control, costs, and the terms of their contract. Advised that the real money lies in owning the land beneath the franchises, Kroc founds a real-estate company, breaks with the brothers, and in 1961 buys them out for $2.7 million — later reneging on a handshake agreement for ongoing royalties.

Reception

The film received generally positive reviews, with particular praise for Keaton's performance, and grossed about $24 million worldwide.
