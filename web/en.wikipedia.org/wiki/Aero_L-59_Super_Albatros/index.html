Aero L-59 Super Albatros

The Aero L-59 Super Albatros is a military jet trainer developed by Aero Vodochody of Czechoslovakia (later the Czech Republic) as a more powerful development of the successful L-39 Albatros. It first flew on 30 September 1986 under the original designation L-39MS.

Design

Compared with the L-39, the L-59 has a strengthened airframe, a longer nose, an updated cockpit, and a more powerful Lotarev DV-2 turbofan engine of about 21.6 kN thrust, raising maximum speed to roughly 865 km/h. It can carry stores on four underwing hardpoints for weapons training or light attack.

Service

Production was limited: the Czech and Slovak air forces took a small number as the L-39MS, while export L-59E and L-59T variants went to Egypt and Tunisia, each ordering 48 and 12 aircraft respectively. The type's development fed into the more extensively redesigned L-159 ALCA.
