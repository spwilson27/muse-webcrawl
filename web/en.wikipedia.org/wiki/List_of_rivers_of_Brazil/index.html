List of rivers of Brazil

Brazil contains a large share of the world's fresh water, most of it in the Amazon Basin, which covers much of the north of the country. Other great drainage systems are the Paraná–Paraguay basin in the south, the São Francisco in the east, and the Tocantins–Araguaia basin.

Amazon basin

  • Amazon River — the largest river in the world by discharge; enters Brazil from Peru
  • Negro — largest left-bank tributary of the Amazon; famous "Meeting of Waters" near Manaus
  • Madeira — largest right-bank tributary; formed by the Beni and Mamoré
  • Tapajós
  • Xingu
  • Japurá (Caquetá)
  • Juruá
  • Purus
  • Solimões — the Brazilian name for the upper Amazon above the Negro confluence
  • Branco, Trombetas, Jari, Paru

Tocantins–Araguaia basin

  • Tocantins
  • Araguaia

Paraná–Paraguay basin

  • Paraná — site of the Itaipu Dam, on the Paraguay border
  • Paraguay River
  • Iguaçu (Iguazu) — famous for Iguazu Falls
  • Paranaíba, Grande, Tietê, Paranapanema, Ivaí

Other Atlantic-draining rivers

  • São Francisco — about 2,900 km, the longest river entirely within Brazil
  • Parnaíba
  • Doce
  • Jequitinhonha
  • Paraíba do Sul
  • Uruguay River (border with Argentina and Uruguay)
  • Jaguaribe, Mearim, Itapicuru
