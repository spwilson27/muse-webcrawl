Maleficent (film)

Directed by: Robert Stromberg

Screenplay by: Linda Woolverton

Starring: Angelina Jolie, Sharlto Copley, Elle Fanning

Music by: James Newton Howard

Release date: May 30, 2014

Box office: $758.5 million

Maleficent is a 2014 American fantasy film directed by Robert Stromberg in his directorial debut, produced by Walt Disney Pictures. A live-action retelling of Disney's 1959 animated film Sleeping Beauty, it portrays the story from the perspective of the antagonist, Maleficent, played by Angelina Jolie, with Elle Fanning as Princess Aurora and Sharlto Copley as King Stefan.

Plot

Maleficent is a powerful fairy of the Moors, a magical realm bordering a human kingdom. As a young woman she befriends and falls in love with Stefan, a human orphan, who betrays her by cutting off her wings to secure the throne. Embittered, she curses Stefan's infant daughter Aurora to prick her finger on a spinning-wheel spindle and fall into a death-like sleep on her sixteenth birthday, broken only by true love's kiss. Aurora is raised in hiding by three pixies, and Maleficent, watching over her, grows to love the girl and becomes the agent of the curse's undoing — her own maternal kiss breaking the spell. Aurora unites the two realms, and Maleficent's wings are restored.

Production and reception

The film was noted for its production design and Jolie's performance and received an Academy Award nomination for Best Costume Design. Reviews were mixed, with praise for the visuals and lead performance and criticism of the screenplay. It grossed over $758 million worldwide, becoming one of the highest-grossing films of 2014. Lana Del Rey's recording of "Once Upon a Dream" accompanied the marketing campaign. A sequel, Maleficent: Mistress of Evil, was released in 2019.
