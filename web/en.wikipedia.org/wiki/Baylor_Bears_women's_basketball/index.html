Baylor Bears women's basketball

The Baylor Bears women's basketball team (historically known as the Lady Bears) represents Baylor University in Waco, Texas, in NCAA Division I women's basketball. Competing in the Big 12 Conference, the programme has won three NCAA national championships, in 2005, 2012, and 2019.

Mulkey era

Kim Mulkey, a former Louisiana Tech star, became head coach in 2000 and transformed Baylor into a national power. In 2005 the team won its first national championship, defeating Michigan State in the final. The 2011–12 team, led by centre Brittney Griner and guard Odyssey Sims, went 40–0 — the first 40-win season in NCAA basketball history — and won the national title by beating Notre Dame. Baylor won a third championship in 2019, defeating Notre Dame 82–81 in a final remembered for an injury to star Lauren Cox and a decisive late performance by Chloe Jackson.

Mulkey departed for LSU in 2021 after 21 seasons, twelve Big 12 regular-season titles, and more than 600 wins at Baylor.

Recent years

Nicki Collen, formerly head coach of the WNBA's Atlanta Dream, succeeded Mulkey in 2021. The team, which dropped the "Lady" prefix from its name in 2021, moved into the new Foster Pavilion alongside the men's team in 2024 and remains a perennial Big 12 contender and NCAA Tournament participant.
