Royal Mail Ship

Royal Mail Ship (prefix RMS) is a ship prefix used for seagoing vessels that carry mail under contract with the British Royal Mail. Ships bearing the prefix historically had to meet standards of speed and reliability.

History

The designation dates from the era of Admiralty packet ships and the great 19th-century mail contracts with companies such as Cunard and the Peninsular and Oriental Steam Navigation Company. Famous holders include RMS Titanic, RMS Lusitania and RMS Queen Mary. The prefix is an honour rather than a requirement, and its use declined with airmail. RMS St Helena and RMS Queen Mary 2 are among its last holders.
