Ministry of Defence (India)

The Ministry of Defence (MoD) is the Government of India ministry charged with coordinating and supervising all agencies and functions of the government relating directly to national security and the Indian Armed Forces. The President of India is the ceremonial commander-in-chief, while executive responsibility rests with the Minister of Defence. The ministry is headquartered in New Delhi, in the South Block and Sena Bhavan.

Organisation

The ministry comprises five departments: the Department of Defence, the Department of Military Affairs (created in 2019, headed by the Chief of Defence Staff), the Department of Defence Production, the Department of Defence Research and Development (overseeing DRDO), and the Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare, together with a Finance Division. The three services — the Indian Army, Indian Navy, and Indian Air Force — as well as the Indian Coast Guard and various inter-service organisations, function under it.

History

A Military Department was created under the East India Company in the 18th century; after independence in 1947 it became the Ministry of Defence of the Dominion, then Republic, of India. The ministry's budget is among the largest of any Indian ministry, reflecting India's position among the world's top military spenders.
