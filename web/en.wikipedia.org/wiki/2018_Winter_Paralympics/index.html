The 2018 Winter Paralympics, officially the XII Paralympic Winter Games, were held in Pyeongchang, South Korea, from 9 to 18 March 2018, following the 2018 Winter Olympics at the same venues. A record 49 National Paralympic Committees sent 569 athletes to compete in 80 medal events across six sports: alpine skiing, biathlon, cross-country skiing, ice hockey, snowboarding and wheelchair curling. Georgia, North Korea and Tajikistan made their Winter Paralympic debuts.

Highlights

The United States topped the medal table with 36 medals, including 13 golds, ahead of the Neutral Paralympic Athletes team — Russian athletes cleared to compete following the suspension of the Russian Paralympic Committee over doping — and Canada. Hosts South Korea won their first Winter Paralympic gold medal, in cross-country skiing through Sin Eui-hyun, and took bronze in ice hockey. China won its first-ever Winter Paralympic medal, a gold in wheelchair curling. Snowboarding expanded to ten events in its second Games, and ice hockey's final saw the United States defeat Canada in overtime, as in the two previous Games.

North Korean participation

North Korea sent two cross-country skiers, the country's first Winter Paralympic athletes, continuing the inter-Korean thaw begun at the Olympics the previous month, although the two Koreas marched separately at the opening ceremony over a dispute concerning the unified flag.
