Banda MS


Banda MS performing in concert.

Banda MS — formally Banda Sinaloense MS de Sergio Lizárraga — is a Mexican banda group from Mazatlán, Sinaloa, formed in 2003. The initials "MS" stand for Mazatlán, Sinaloa. Founded by brothers Sergio and Alberto Lizárraga, the band grew from regional popularity in Mexico to become one of the most successful regional Mexican acts in the world, noted for romantic banda ballads sung with large brass arrangements.

History

Banda MS was founded in 2003 in Mazatlán; early members included vocalist Alan Ramírez and, from 2004, Oswaldo "Walo" Silvas, whose voice became central to the band's sound. After years of recording and touring in Mexico, the band broke through internationally with the 2015 album Qué Bendición (2016), whose single "Solo con Verte" spent weeks atop Billboard's Regional Mexican Airplay chart.

The band's ballad "No Me Pidas Perdón", "Tengo que Colgar", "Tu Postura", and the duet "La Mejor Versión de Mí" further consolidated its audience. Banda MS became one of the most-streamed regional Mexican artists globally, appearing among Spotify's most-played Mexican acts, and in 2020 it performed at the Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival — a landmark for a banda act — with a return appearance in 2022. It also collaborated with rapper Snoop Dogg on the single "Qué Maldición" (2020), a widely noted cross-genre pairing.

Touring incident

In July 2016 lead singer Alan Ramírez was shot in the neck by an unknown assailant while leaving a sold-out concert at the Auditorio Nacional in Mexico City; he survived the attack and returned to performing after recovery.
