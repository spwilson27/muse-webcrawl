The Boston Bruins are a professional ice hockey team based in Boston, Massachusetts. They compete in the National Hockey League as a member of the Atlantic Division in the Eastern Conference. Founded in 1924, they are one of the Original Six teams.

History

Founded by Charles Adams, the Bruins won their first Stanley Cup in 1929. The Bobby Orr era brought Cups in 1970 and 1972, famous for 'The Goal' in 1970. After a long drought, they won again in 2011 against Vancouver, and set the NHL single-season wins and points records in 2022–23.

Players

Notable players include Bobby Orr, Ray Bourque, Cam Neely, Patrice Bergeron, Brad Marchand and David Pastrňák.
