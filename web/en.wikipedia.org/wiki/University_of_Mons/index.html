University of Mons

The University of Mons (French: Université de Mons, abbreviated UMONS) is a public university in the French-speaking Community of Belgium, located in Mons, Hainaut. It was created on 1 January 2009 by the merger of the University of Mons-Hainaut and the Faculty of Engineering of Mons (Faculté Polytechnique de Mons, founded 1837).

Structure

UMONS has around 10,000 students and seven faculties: Architecture and Urban Planning; Medicine and Pharmacy; Engineering (the Polytechnique); Psychology and Education Sciences; Science; Translation and Interpretation; Law and Economics and Management. It also hosts an institute of language sciences and numerous research institutes, with recognised strengths in materials science, energy, and information technology.

History

The engineering school founded in 1837 as the École des Mines is one of the oldest in Belgium. The University of Mons-Hainaut traces its origins to a university centre created in the 1960s. The merged university has played a central role in the economic regeneration of the Mons region, emphasising research partnerships and technology transfer.
