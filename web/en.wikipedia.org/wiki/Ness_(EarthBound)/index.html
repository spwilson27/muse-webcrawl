Ness (EarthBound)

Ness (Japanese: ネス, Hepburn: Nesu) is a fictional character in the Mother series of role-playing video games published by Nintendo. He is the protagonist of the 1994 Super Nintendo Entertainment System game EarthBound (known as Mother 2 in Japan). Ness is a young boy from the town of Onett in Eagleland who possesses psychic abilities known as PSI. He has appeared as a playable fighter in every installment of the Super Smash Bros. series.

Character design

Ness was created by Shigesato Itoi. He is depicted as an ordinary suburban boy of about thirteen, wearing a red baseball cap, a yellow-and-blue striped shirt, blue shorts, and a yellow backpack. His signature weapons are baseball bats, including the Cracked Bat he starts with and the legendary Gutsy Bat. His name is an anagram of "NES" / "SNES", the consoles associated with the series.

Appearances

EarthBound

In EarthBound, a meteorite crashes near Ness's home in Onett. Investigating, he meets Buzz Buzz, a time-traveling insect from ten years in the future, who tells him of the cosmic destroyer Giygas and the prophecy that Ness and three friends will save the world. Ness journeys across Eagleland and beyond, visiting the eight "Your Sanctuary" locations to record the melodies of the Sound Stone, and is joined by Paula, Jeff, and Poo. In the end, the party travels back in time and defeats Giygas with the power of prayer.

Super Smash Bros.

Ness has been a playable character in all Super Smash Bros. games since the original 1999 Nintendo 64 title, where he was an unlockable character. His moveset draws on PSI powers actually used by Paula and Poo in EarthBound, including PK Fire, PK Thunder, and PSI Magnet, along with his bat and yo-yo.

Reception

Ness is regarded as one of Nintendo's most beloved protagonists; his silent, everyman character and the game's contemporary American setting have been praised. His inclusion in Super Smash Bros. helped introduce the Mother series to audiences outside Japan.
