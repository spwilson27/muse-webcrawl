Ada (programming language)

Ada is a structured, statically typed, imperative, object-oriented programming language with strong support for real-time and embedded systems. It was designed by a team led by Jean Ichbiah of CII Honeywell Bull under contract to the United States Department of Defense, and standardised as Ada 83; later revisions include Ada 95, Ada 2005, Ada 2012 and Ada 2022. The language is named after Ada Lovelace, regarded as the first computer programmer.

Features

Ada emphasises reliability and maintainability: strong typing, explicit concurrency through tasks and protected objects, packages for modularity, generics, exception handling and contract-based programming (pre- and postconditions in Ada 2012). The SPARK subset supports formal verification of programs.

Use

Ada is used in safety- and mission-critical systems: avionics (including Boeing and Airbus aircraft), air traffic control, railway signalling, space systems and defence applications, where its emphasis on early error detection and code correctness is highly valued.
