Zootopia 2 (titled Zootropolis 2 in some regions) is a 2025 American animated buddy cop comedy film produced by Walt Disney Animation Studios and released by Walt Disney Pictures. It is the sequel to Zootopia (2016) and the studio's 64th animated feature. The film is directed by Jared Bush and Byron Howard, written by Bush, and produced by Yvett Merino.

Premise

Rookie police partners Judy Hopps, a rabbit, and Nick Wilde, a red fox, go undercover in the new district of Marsh Market to track a mysterious reptile — a pit viper named Gary De'Snake — whose arrival in the mammal metropolis of Zootopia throws the city into turmoil, testing the partners' relationship as they unravel a conspiracy reaching back to the city's founding.

Production

A sequel to Zootopia was announced by Disney CEO Bob Iger in February 2023. The first film had grossed over $1 billion worldwide and won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature. The sequel expands the world to include reptiles and semi-aquatic animals, with new districts designed for the sequel's undercover storyline.

Release

Zootopia 2 was released in the United States on November 26, 2025, over the Thanksgiving holiday weekend.
