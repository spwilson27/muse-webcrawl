Dihedral group

In mathematics, a dihedral group is the group of symmetries of a regular polygon, including both rotations and reflections. The dihedral group of a regular n-gon, written Dn (or Dihn), has order 2n.

Definition

The group is generated by a rotation r through 2π/n and a reflection s, subject to the relations rn = 1, s2 = 1, and srs = r−1. It can be presented as Dn = ⟨r, s | rn = s2 = 1, srs = r−1⟩. Dihedral groups are among the simplest examples of non-abelian groups (for n ≥ 3).

Examples and applications

D1 and D2 correspond to the symmetries of a digon and a rectangle-like figure; D3 is isomorphic to the symmetric group S3. The infinite dihedral group D∞ acts on the integers. Dihedral symmetry appears throughout geometry, chemistry, and the classification of two-dimensional symmetry groups.
