Telematics

Telematics is an interdisciplinary field encompassing telecommunications, vehicular technologies, electrical engineering (sensors and instrumentation), and computer science. The term combines "telecommunications" and "informatics" and was coined in a 1978 French report by Simon Nora and Alain Minc.

Applications

The best-known applications are in vehicles: GPS navigation, automatic emergency calling (eCall), usage-based insurance, fleet management, vehicle tracking, and remote diagnostics. Telematics units typically combine a GNSS receiver, an engine-interface, and a cellular modem to send data to a central server.

Other uses

Telematics is also applied in healthcare (telemedicine data transfer), environmental monitoring, and the remote management of industrial equipment, and the term is sometimes used broadly for any system that transmits measurement data over a network.
