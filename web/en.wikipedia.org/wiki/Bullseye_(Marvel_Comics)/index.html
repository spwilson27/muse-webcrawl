Bullseye is a fictional supervillain appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. A psychopathic assassin, Bullseye uses the exceptional aim granted by his innate ability and training to make almost any object a lethal weapon. He is one of the archenemies of Daredevil and a frequent adversary of the Punisher.

Publication history

Bullseye was created by writer Marv Wolfman and artist John Romita Sr., and first appeared in Daredevil #131 (March 1976). He became central to Frank Miller's acclaimed run on the title, in which he murdered Elektra with her own sai — one of the most famous deaths in Marvel history — before Daredevil let him fall from a height, shattering his spine. His bones were later laced with adamantium, restoring his mobility.

Powers and abilities

Bullseye has no true superhuman powers, but possesses an uncanny, seemingly preternatural accuracy: he can throw playing cards, paper clips, peanuts and other mundane objects with enough force and precision to kill. He is an expert marksman and hand-to-hand combatant, and his skeleton, reinforced with adamantium, is unusually durable. His true name and past remain deliberately obscure and contradictory.

In other media

Colin Farrell played Bullseye in the 2003 film Daredevil, with a bullseye scar carved into his forehead. Wilson Bethel portrayed a version of the character, Benjamin "Dex" Poindexter, in the third season of the Netflix series Daredevil (2018).
