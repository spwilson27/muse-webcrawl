Mozambique

Mozambique, officially the Republic of Mozambique, is a country in Southeast Africa bordered by Tanzania, Malawi, Zambia, Zimbabwe, South Africa and Eswatini, facing Madagascar across the Mozambique Channel. Its capital and largest city is Maputo, and its population is about 33 million.

A Portuguese colony until 1975, Mozambique then endured a devastating civil war (1977-1992) between FRELIMO and RENAMO. It is one of the world's poorest countries despite rich resources in coal, natural gas and gemstones, and is frequently struck by cyclones, including Idai in 2019. Since 2017 an Islamist insurgency in the northern province of Cabo Delgado has displaced hundreds of thousands.
