Wilmersdorf is an inner-city locality (Ortsteil) of Berlin in the borough of Charlottenburg-Wilmersdorf. Before Berlin's 2001 administrative reform it was a separate borough in the former West Berlin. Until 1920, Wilmersdorf was an independent city, known as Deutsch-Wilmersdorf, that developed as a prosperous residential suburb (Millonenviertel) during the Gründerzeit period of the late 19th century.

Geography

Wilmersdorf lies immediately southwest of Berlin's city centre, bordering Charlottenburg to the northwest, Schöneberg to the east, Friedenau and Steglitz to the south, and Halensee, Grunewald and Schmargendorf to the west. The locality includes the neighbourhoods around the historic village green and the Wilmersdorfer Straße, one of West Berlin's main shopping streets. The Volkspark Wilmersdorf and the adjoining Fennsee provide green space in the densely built-up district.

History

The village was first mentioned in documents in 1293 as Wilmerstorff. For centuries it remained a small agricultural settlement near the Electoral hunting lodge at Grunewald. In the 19th century, wealthy Berliners began building villas and apartment houses there, and the village grew rapidly after the opening of railway connections. Wilmersdorf received town privileges in 1906 and grew into one of the largest cities in Brandenburg by population. On 1 October 1920 it was incorporated into Berlin under the Greater Berlin Act.

During the Weimar era Wilmersdorf was a centre of intellectual and artistic life, home to writers and publishers, and it had a large Jewish community. Under Nazi rule the community was destroyed; a memorial at the site of the former synagogue commemorates its members. After 1945 Wilmersdorf lay in the British sector of divided Berlin and developed into an affluent commercial and residential district of West Berlin.

Education and culture

The locality is home to several schools and to cultural institutions including the Schaubühne theatre company's former venue and numerous cinemas along the Kurfürstendamm fringe. The Universität der Künste and the Technical University are located in neighbouring Charlottenburg.

Transport

Wilmersdorf is served by U-Bahn lines U3, U7 and U9, including the stations Hohenzollernplatz, Konstanzer Straße, Blissestraße and Güntzelstraße, as well as by numerous bus routes.
