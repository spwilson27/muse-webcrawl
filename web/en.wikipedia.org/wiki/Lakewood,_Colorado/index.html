Lakewood, Colorado

Lakewood is a home rule municipality in Jefferson County, Colorado, United States, and part of the Denver metropolitan area. With a population of about 155,000 at the 2020 census, it is the fifth most populous city in Colorado.

History

Lakewood was founded in 1889 by Charles Welch and W.A.H. Loveland as a summer resort area west of Denver. It remained unincorporated until 1969, when it became a city — at the time one of the largest newly incorporated cities in the United States. The Denver Federal Center, a large complex of federal offices, is located in Lakewood.

Geography

The city lies at the foot of the Rocky Mountain Front Range and includes the Bear Creek Lake Park recreation area.
