Aubrey Drake Graham (born 24 October 1986), known mononymously as Drake, is a Canadian rapper, singer and actor. Regarded as one of the most influential figures in popular music, he is credited with popularising melodic singing and emotional vulnerability in hip hop. Drake first gained recognition as an actor on the teen drama series Degrassi: The Next Generation (2001–2008) before leaving to pursue music.

Career

After signing with Lil Wayne's Young Money Entertainment, Drake released his debut studio album Thank Me Later (2010), which debuted atop the US Billboard 200. His subsequent albums — Take Care (2011), Nothing Was the Same (2013), If You're Reading This It's Too Late (2015), Views (2016), More Life (2017), Scorpion (2018), Certified Lover Boy (2021), Honestly, Nevermind (2022) and For All the Dogs (2023) — have made him one of the best-selling music artists of all time, with record-breaking streaming numbers; he was the most-streamed artist globally on Spotify for much of the 2010s and the first artist to surpass 50 billion streams on the platform.

Business and honours

Drake co-founded the OVO Sound label in 2012 and the OVO fashion and festival brands, and has served as a global ambassador for the Toronto Raptors NBA team. Among his numerous awards are multiple Grammy Awards and a record haul of Billboard Music Awards. His 2024 public feud with Kendrick Lamar, conducted through a rapid exchange of diss tracks, became one of the most discussed events in hip hop history.
