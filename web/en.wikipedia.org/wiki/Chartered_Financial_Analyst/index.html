Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) is a professional designation awarded by the CFA Institute to investment and financial professionals who complete a series of three examinations and meet work-experience and ethical requirements.

Program

The CFA Program curriculum covers ethical and professional standards, quantitative methods, economics, financial reporting, corporate finance, equity and fixed-income analysis, derivatives, alternative investments and portfolio management. Candidates must pass Level I, II and III exams, typically over several years, and complete qualifying professional work experience.

Recognition

The CFA charter is regarded as one of the most prestigious credentials in the investment industry, with charterholders working in portfolio management, research, consulting and risk management worldwide.
