Mastiff (armoured vehicle)

TypeMRAP / protected patrol vehicle
Place of originUnited States / United Kingdom
Crew2 + 8 troops
Based onCougar 6×6

The Mastiff is a heavily armoured, six-wheeled patrol vehicle used by the British Armed Forces. It is based on the American Cougar MRAP (Mine Resistant Ambush Protected) vehicle built by Force Protection, Inc., and was procured as an urgent operational requirement for service in Iraq and Afghanistan, with the first vehicles delivered in 2006–2007.

Design

The Mastiff features a V-shaped hull designed to deflect mine and improvised explosive device (IED) blasts away from the crew compartment. British-specific modifications include additional armour, electronic countermeasures, and Bowman communications equipment. The vehicle carries a crew of two plus up to eight troops, and is typically armed with a 7.62 mm general-purpose machine gun, a 12.7 mm heavy machine gun, or a 40 mm grenade machine gun.

Service

Mastiffs were used extensively by British forces in Afghanistan, where their protection was credited with saving many lives. Variants include the Mastiff 2 with improved suspension and axles, the smaller four-wheeled Ridgback, and the Wolfhound tactical support vehicle. After the end of combat operations in Afghanistan, the Mastiff remained in service with the British Army as part of its protected mobility fleet.
