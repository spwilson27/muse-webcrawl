Jean Anthelme Brillat-Savarin

Jean Anthelme Brillat-Savarin (2 April 1755 – 2 February 1826) was a French lawyer and politician, who gained fame as an epicure and gastronome.

The Physiology of Taste

His celebrated work Physiologie du goût (The Physiology of Taste), published in December 1825 shortly before his death, is a witty meditation on food, dining, and the senses, mixing recipes, anecdotes, and philosophy. It contains the famous aphorism "Tell me what you eat, and I will tell you what you are."

Life

Born in Belley, Brillat-Savarin practised law, sat in the Estates-General of 1789, fled France during the Terror, lived in the United States, and later served as a judge of the Court of Cassation. See also gastronomy.
