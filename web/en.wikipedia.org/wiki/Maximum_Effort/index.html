Maximum Effort
Type: Film and television production company / marketing agency
Founded: 2018
Founder: Ryan Reynolds (with George Dewey)
Headquarters: United States

Maximum Effort is an American film production company and digital marketing agency founded by actor Ryan Reynolds together with George Dewey. Its name is drawn from the catchphrase of Reynolds's character Deadpool. The company gained attention for fast, humorous, viral marketing campaigns, often produced within days to ride current events.

Work

Maximum Effort produced or marketed projects including the Deadpool films' campaigns, Free Guy, The Adam Project and Aviation American Gin, in which Reynolds held a stake. Its advertising arm created widely shared spots for brands such as Mint Mobile and Match.com. In 2021 the production side was developed in partnership with Paramount Pictures, and the marketing business was acquired by MNTN, with Reynolds becoming the company's chief creative officer.

Style

The company's hallmark is self-aware, meme-literate humour and speed — "fastvertising" — turning around culturally tuned adverts in response to news events.
