George Floyd protests

The George Floyd protests were a series of protests, demonstrations and riots against police brutality and racism, beginning in Minneapolis, Minnesota, on May 26, 2020, the day after George Floyd, a 46-year-old Black man, was murdered during a police arrest.

George Floyd protests
Date: May 26, 2020 – 2021 (major phase)
Location: United States and worldwide
Cause: Murder of George Floyd; police brutality; racism

Background

On May 25, 2020, Minneapolis officer Derek Chauvin knelt on Floyd's neck for over nine minutes during an arrest over a suspected counterfeit $20 bill, while bystander video spread worldwide. Chauvin was later convicted of murder. The killing came after other high-profile deaths including Breonna Taylor and Ahmaud Arbery.

Spread

Protests spread to more than 2,000 cities and towns across the United States and to many countries, becoming possibly the largest protest movement in U.S. history, with estimates of 15–26 million participants. Most protests were peaceful; some cities saw rioting, looting, arson and clashes, curfews, and deployment of the National Guard. The Minneapolis 3rd Precinct police station was burned.

Responses and impact

The movement, closely associated with Black Lives Matter, prompted police reforms in some jurisdictions, bans or limits on chokeholds, corporate and cultural reckonings, removal of Confederate monuments, and debates over "defund the police". A Minneapolis ballot measure to replace the police department failed in 2021. Chauvin was sentenced to 22.5 years in prison.
