The Virginia Tech Hokies are the intercollegiate athletic teams representing Virginia Polytechnic Institute and State University (Virginia Tech) in Blacksburg, Virginia. The Hokies compete in NCAA Division I as members of the Atlantic Coast Conference (ACC), which the university joined in 2004. The school fields 22 varsity sports — 11 men's and 11 women's.

Nickname and colours

The name "Hokie" derives from the "Old Hokie" spirit yell written in 1896 by student O. M. Stull for a contest to create a new cheer after the school changed its name. The official colours, Chicago maroon and burnt orange, were adopted in 1896 and are unusual among American college teams. The mascot is the HokieBird, which evolved from earlier gobbler (turkey) mascots; teams were historically known as the "Fighting Gobblers", and a bronze Hokie Bird statue and the gobbling sound at games preserve that heritage.

Football

The football team plays at Lane Stadium and is the school's most prominent programme. Under head coach Frank Beamer (1987–2015), the Hokies became a national power, reaching the 2000 Sugar Bowl — the BCS National Championship Game for the 1999 season — led by quarterback Michael Vick. The team's entrance to Metallica's "Enter Sandman" is widely regarded as one of the most dramatic in college football.

Other sports

Virginia Tech has won national team championships in wrestling-related competition and individual titles across several sports; the men's basketball team plays at Cassell Coliseum, and the women's basketball team reached the Final Four in 2023. The baseball team plays at English Field, and the softball programme has been a regular NCAA tournament participant.
