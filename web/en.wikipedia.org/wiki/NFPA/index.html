National Fire Protection Association

Founded: 1896
Headquarters: Quincy, Massachusetts, United States
Type: Nonprofit standards organisation
Known for: NFPA 1 Fire Code, NFPA 70 (National Electrical Code), NFPA 704

The National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) is a U.S.-based international nonprofit organisation devoted to eliminating deaths, injuries, property and economic loss due to fire, electrical and related hazards. Founded in 1896, it develops and publishes more than 300 consensus codes and standards.

Codes and standards

Well-known NFPA publications include NFPA 1, the Fire Code; NFPA 70, the National Electrical Code; NFPA 101, the Life Safety Code; NFPA 13 on sprinkler installation; and NFPA 704, the "fire diamond" system for identifying hazardous materials. NFPA standards are developed by committees of volunteers and are widely adopted by governments as regulations.

Other activities

The NFPA conducts research, data collection and public education, including Fire Prevention Week, observed in October since 1922. Its mascot, Sparky the Fire Dog, was created in 1951. The organisation has tens of thousands of members worldwide and is headquartered in Quincy, Massachusetts.
