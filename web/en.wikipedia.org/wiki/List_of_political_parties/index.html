List of political parties

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This article provides an overview of the principal political parties in selected countries around the world. Full listings are maintained in country-specific articles (for example, "List of political parties in India"). Parties are named with their usual English names, with positions described on the conventional left–right spectrum where applicable.
