List of goose breeds

Domestic goose breeds
Domestic geese descend from the greylag goose and the swan goose.

This list of breeds of domestic goose is divided by the two species from which domestic geese descend: the western domestic goose, derived from the greylag goose (Anser anser), and the eastern (Chinese) domestic goose, derived from the swan goose (Anser cygnoides). Breeds are kept for meat, eggs, down, guarding and exhibition.
