Hirohata Merc

Hirohata Merc
Base car: 1951 Mercury Eight Coupe
Owner/commissioned by: Bob Hirohata
Built by: Barris Kustoms (George and Sam Barris), 1952

The Hirohata Merc is a celebrated American custom car: a 1951 Mercury Eight coupe restyled in 1952 by George and Sam Barris of Barris Kustoms for owner Bob Hirohata. It is widely regarded as one of the most influential custom cars of the post-war era and helped define the classic "lead sled" style.

Design

The car was chopped, its B-pillars removed to create a hardtop profile, and fitted with a 1952 Lincoln front end treatment, Buick side trim, frenched headlights, and a distinctive two-tone seafoam-green and organic-green paint scheme. It won numerous show awards in the early 1950s.

Later history

After years out of public view and a long ownership by Jim McNiel, who restored it, the car returned to prominence in exhibitions of custom-car history. In January 2022 it sold at auction in Kissimmee, Florida, for $2.145 million, a record for a Barris custom.

References: Mecum Auctions (2022); Hot Rod Magazine histories of Barris Kustoms.
