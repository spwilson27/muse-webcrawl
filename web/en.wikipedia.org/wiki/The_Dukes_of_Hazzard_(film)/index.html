The Dukes of Hazzard (film)

The Dukes of Hazzard is a 2005 American action comedy film directed by Jay Chandrasekhar and based on the television series of the same name (1979–1985). It stars Johnny Knoxville as Luke Duke, Seann William Scott as Bo Duke, and Jessica Simpson as Daisy Duke, with Burt Reynolds as Boss Hogg and Willie Nelson as Uncle Jesse. The film was produced by Warner Bros. Pictures and Village Roadshow Pictures.

Plot

In fictional Hazzard County, Georgia, cousins Bo and Luke Duke run moonshine for their Uncle Jesse in their orange 1969 Dodge Charger, the General Lee. When corrupt county commissioner Boss Hogg schemes to strip-mine the county by seizing local farms, the Dukes, aided by Daisy Duke and mechanic Cooter, race to expose the plan at a rally race and before a county vote in Atlanta.

Reception

The film grossed about $111 million worldwide against a budget of roughly $50 million. Reviews were generally negative, criticising the script and humour, though the car stunts were praised. The film was followed by a direct-to-video prequel, The Dukes of Hazzard: The Beginning (2007). Several members of the original television cast declined to participate and publicly criticised the film's script.
