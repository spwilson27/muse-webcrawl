Physician

Physician
Also calledDoctor of medicine; medical practitioner
TrainingMedical school plus residency (varies by country)
RolesDiagnosis, treatment, prevention, research
[Image: A doctor in a white coat with a stethoscope listening to a patient's chest]

A physician, medical practitioner or (informally) doctor is a professional who practices medicine — diagnosing, treating and preventing disease and injury. Training is long: medical school (University) followed by supervised residency, with specialisation from general practice to neurosurgery.

Practice

Doctors work in hospitals, clinics and research, guided by evidence-based medicine, ethics codes descending from Hippocrates, and increasingly by decision-support software. Shortages and burnout are chronic concerns; the title 'doctor' is shared with PhD holders, to occasional confusion.
