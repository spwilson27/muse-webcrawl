Microsoft Corporation is an American multinational technology company headquartered in Redmond, Washington. Founded by Bill Gates and Paul Allen on April 4, 1975, it became dominant in personal computer software with MS-DOS and then the Microsoft Windows operating system. Microsoft is one of the "Big Five" American technology companies alongside Alphabet, Amazon, Apple and Meta, and is among the world's most valuable companies. Its best-known products are the Windows operating system, the Microsoft 365 productivity suite (formerly Office), the Azure cloud computing platform, the Xbox video game consoles, and the professional network LinkedIn, which it acquired in 2016.

History

Gates and Allen founded Microsoft — a portmanteau of "microcomputer" and "software" — to sell a BASIC interpreter for the Altair 8800 microcomputer. In 1980 IBM contracted Microsoft to provide the operating system for its first personal computer; Microsoft bought and adapted an existing system, released as PC DOS/MS-DOS, and licensed it to other PC makers, building the business that made it the leading PC software company. Windows, first released in 1985 as a graphical shell for MS-DOS, came to dominate the desktop operating system market in the 1990s, alongside the Office suite.

In 2000 Steve Ballmer succeeded Gates as CEO. Microsoft was the defendant in the landmark antitrust case United States v. Microsoft Corp., which alleged monopolistic practices in tying the Internet Explorer browser to Windows; the company reached a settlement with the Department of Justice in 2001. Under Ballmer, Microsoft expanded into gaming with the Xbox (2001) and search with Bing, but struggled to compete in mobile phones against Apple's iPhone and Google's Android.

Satya Nadella became CEO in 2014 and refocused the company on cloud computing and cross-platform services. Major acquisitions under Nadella include LinkedIn (2016), GitHub (2018) and the game publisher Activision Blizzard (2023), the company's largest acquisition. Microsoft also became a central player in artificial intelligence through its multi-billion-dollar partnership with OpenAI, integrating "Copilot" assistants based on large language models into Windows, Microsoft 365 and Bing.

Business areas

Microsoft reports in three segments: Productivity and Business Processes (Microsoft 365, LinkedIn); Intelligent Cloud (Azure and server products); and More Personal Computing (Windows licensing, Surface devices, Xbox gaming and search advertising). Azure is generally regarded as the second-largest cloud platform after Amazon Web Services.

Corporate affairs

Microsoft's main campus in Redmond, Washington, covers a large suburban site of office buildings. The company has frequently topped most-valuable-company rankings and was among the first to reach a $3 trillion market capitalisation.

Criticism

Microsoft has been criticised over its historical antitrust conduct, the security vulnerabilities of Windows, forced software updates and telemetry/privacy practices, and the market power of its cloud and gaming acquisitions.
