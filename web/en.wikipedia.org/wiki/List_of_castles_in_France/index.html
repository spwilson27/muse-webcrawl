List of castles in France

France has an estimated 45,000 castles and châteaux, ranging from medieval fortresses to Renaissance palaces and fortified farmhouses. This list includes some of the most notable examples by region.

Loire Valley

  • Château de Chambord – the largest Loire château, built by Francis I.
  • Château de Chenonceau – spanning the River Cher, known as the "Ladies' Château".
  • Château d'Amboise – royal residence; Leonardo da Vinci is buried in its chapel.
  • Château de Villandry – famous for its ornamental gardens.
  • Château d'Azay-le-Rideau – built on an island in the Indre.
  • Château de Blois – home of several kings of France.
  • Château d'Angers – medieval fortress housing the Apocalypse Tapestry.

Île-de-France

  • Palace of Versailles – the great palace of Louis XIV.
  • Château de Fontainebleau – royal residence from the 12th to 19th centuries.
  • Château de Vaux-le-Vicomte – the model for Versailles.
  • Château de Vincennes – medieval royal fortress with the tallest keep in Europe.
  • Château de Saint-Germain-en-Laye – birthplace of Louis XIV.
  • Château de Chantilly – home of the Musée Condé.
