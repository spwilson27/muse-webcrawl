Stephanie Brown is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Chuck Dixon and artist Tom Lyle, she first appeared in Detective Comics #647 (1992) as the amateur vigilante the Spoiler, the daughter of the minor Batman villain Cluemaster, determined to "spoil" her father's crimes. She went on to become the first female Robin in mainstream DC continuity (briefly, in 2004) and the third Batgirl, starring in her own acclaimed Batgirl series (2009–2011).

Publication history and biography

As Spoiler, Stephanie became a recurring ally of Batman and the girlfriend of Tim Drake, the third Robin, joining the cast of the Robin ongoing series. In the 2004 storyline "War Games", she briefly replaced Drake as Robin, was fired for disobeying orders, and was apparently killed after torture by Black Mask — a death controversial with readers, especially as the character was for years denied the memorial accorded other fallen Robins. In 2008 it was revealed that Dr Leslie Thompkins had faked Stephanie's death to protect her; she returned, and in 2009 assumed the mantle of Batgirl under the mentorship of Barbara Gordon, in a series written by Bryan Q. Miller.

After DC's 2011 New 52 reboot erased her from continuity, sustained fan campaigning led to her reintroduction as Spoiler in Batman Eternal (2014). She later co-headlined Batgirls alongside Cassandra Cain, with whom she is depicted as best friends and partners.

Characterisation

Stephanie Brown is portrayed as an optimistic, wisecracking working-class Gothamite whose heroism is self-made — she lacks the wealth, training and pedigree of other members of the Batman family — and her stories frequently address class, her father's criminality, and her determination to prove herself.
