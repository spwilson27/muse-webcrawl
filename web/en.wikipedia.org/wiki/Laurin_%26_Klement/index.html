Laurin & Klement

Founded1895, Mladá Boleslav, Bohemia
FoundersVáclav Laurin and Václav Klement
ProductsBicycles, motorcycles, automobiles
FateAcquired by Škoda Works in 1925

Laurin & Klement was a Czech manufacturer of bicycles, motorcycles, and automobiles founded in 1895 in Mladá Boleslav, in what was then Bohemia, Austria-Hungary. It was the predecessor of the present-day automobile company Škoda Auto.

History

Bookseller Václav Klement and mechanic Václav Laurin began by repairing and then manufacturing bicycles under the Slavia brand. They moved into motorcycles around 1899 and produced their first automobile, the Voiturette A, in 1905. Laurin & Klement cars gained a reputation for quality and competition success, and the company became one of the largest car makers in the Austro-Hungarian Empire.

Škoda takeover

After the First World War, economic difficulties led the firm to seek a stronger partner. In 1925 Laurin & Klement was acquired by the Škoda Works industrial conglomerate, and its cars were thereafter sold under the Škoda name. The Laurin & Klement nameplate survives today as the designation of the highest trim level on some Škoda models.
