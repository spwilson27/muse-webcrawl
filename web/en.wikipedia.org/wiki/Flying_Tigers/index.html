The Flying Tigers were the members of the 1st American Volunteer Group (AVG) of the Republic of China Air Force, formed to help oppose the Japanese invasion of China. Led by Claire Lee Chennault and composed of volunteer pilots from the United States services, the group flew Curtiss P-40s painted with distinctive shark-mouth nose art.

Operations

Training in Burma in late 1941, the AVG entered combat over Rangoon on 20 December 1941, days after Pearl Harbor. In seven months of fighting over Burma, China and Thailand the group was credited with destroying some 296 enemy aircraft while losing only 14 pilots in combat — a record that made it famous in the United States.

Legacy

On 4 July 1942 the AVG was disbanded and absorbed into the U.S. Army Air Forces as the China Air Task Force, ancestor of the Fourteenth Air Force. The Flying Tigers' insignia — a winged Bengal tiger — was designed by the Walt Disney studios.
