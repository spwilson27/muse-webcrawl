Reinhard Tristan Eugen Heydrich (1904–1942) was a high-ranking German SS and police official during the Nazi era and a principal architect of the Holocaust. He headed the Reich Security Main Office (RSHA), which combined the Gestapo, Kripo and SD, and chaired the January 1942 Wannsee Conference, which coordinated the 'Final Solution to the Jewish Question'.

Career

Dismissed from the Reichsmarine in 1931, Heydrich joined the SS and built its intelligence service, the Sicherheitsdienst (SD), under Heinrich Himmler. He helped organise Kristallnacht in 1938 and established the Einsatzgruppen, mobile killing units that murdered over a million people in occupied Eastern Europe.

Assassination

As acting Reich Protector in Prague, Heydrich was ambushed on 27 May 1942 by Czechoslovak agents Jozef Gabčík and Jan Kubiš in Operation Anthropoid and died of his wounds on 4 June. Nazi reprisals included the destruction of the villages of Lidice and Ležáky.
