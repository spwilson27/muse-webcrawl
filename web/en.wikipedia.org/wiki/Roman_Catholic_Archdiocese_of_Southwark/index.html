Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Southwark

The Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Southwark (Latin: Archidioecesis Southvarcensis) is a Latin Church archdiocese of the Catholic Church in England. It covers the London boroughs south of the River Thames and the county of Kent, together with the Medway unitary authority area. Its cathedral is St George's Cathedral in Southwark, London.

Country: England

Ecclesiastical province: Southwark

Cathedral: St George's Cathedral, Southwark

Suffragan dioceses: Arundel and Brighton, Plymouth, Portsmouth

History

The Diocese of Southwark was erected in 1850 when Pope Pius IX restored the Catholic hierarchy in England and Wales with the bull Universalis Ecclesiae. It was one of the original twelve dioceses, and its first bishop was Thomas Grant. On 28 May 1965 the diocese was raised to the status of a metropolitan archdiocese, with the dioceses of Arundel and Brighton, Plymouth and Portsmouth as its suffragans.

Archbishops

Archbishops of Southwark have included Cyril Cowderoy, Michael Bowen, Kevin McDonald and Peter Smith. John Wilson became the tenth Archbishop of Southwark in 2019.
