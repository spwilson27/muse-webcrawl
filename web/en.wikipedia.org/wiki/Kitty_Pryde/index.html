Kitty Pryde

Katherine Anne 'Kitty' Pryde, later Kate Pryde, is a fictional superhero in Marvel Comics, created by Chris Claremont and John Byrne and first appearing in The Uncanny X-Men #129 (1980). A mutant with the ability to phase through solid matter, she joined the X-Men as a teenager under codenames Sprite and Shadowcat.

Kitty is a computer genius, trained martial artist, and longtime moral centre of the X-Men, companion to the alien dragon Lockheed. Major stories include the Days of Future Past timeline where her older self sends her mind back in time, her time with Excalibur, and later leadership as headmistress and as the Red Queen / Captain Kate Pryde of the Marauders in the Krakoan era. She was played by Elliot Page in films.
