Resolute (Inuktitut: ᖃᐅᓱᐃᑦᑐᖅ, Qausuittuq, "place with no dawn") is an Inuit hamlet on Cornwallis Island in Nunavut, Canada. Located at Resolute Bay at about 74°41′N, it is one of Canada's northernmost communities and a common staging point for expeditions to the North Pole.

History

The settlement was established in 1947 alongside a Royal Canadian Air Force base and weather station. In 1953, during the High Arctic relocation, Inuit families from Inukjuak, Quebec, and Pond Inlet were moved to Resolute and Grise Fiord by the Canadian government; the relocations later became the subject of official apologies. The community is named after HMS Resolute, a British Arctic exploration ship.

Transport

Resolute Bay Airport is among the busiest airports in Nunavut and supports Arctic research, including the Polar Continental Shelf Program, as well as military operations such as Operation Nanook.
