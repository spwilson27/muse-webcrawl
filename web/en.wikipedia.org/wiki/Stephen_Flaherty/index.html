Stephen Flaherty

Stephen Flaherty (born September 18, 1960) is an American composer of musical theatre and film. He works most often in collaboration with the lyricist–book writer Lynn Ahrens, with whom he is best known for writing the songs for the animated film Anastasia (1997) and the Broadway musicals Once on This Island, Ragtime, Seussical and Anastasia.

Career

Flaherty was born in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and studied musical composition at the Cincinnati College-Conservatory of Music. After moving to New York, he joined the BMI Lehman Engel Musical Theatre Workshop, where he met Lynn Ahrens in 1982; the two became one of the most durable songwriting partnerships in American musical theatre.

Their breakthrough came with Once on This Island (1990), followed by My Favorite Year (1992), the Tony Award-winning Ragtime (1998), Seussical (2000), A Man of No Importance (2002), Dessa Rose (2005), The Glorious Ones (2007), Rocky the Musical (2014), and the stage adaptation of Anastasia (2017). For the 1997 animated film Anastasia they wrote songs including "Journey to the Past", which was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Original Song. Flaherty has also composed film scores, including for the documentary After the Storm.

Awards

With Ahrens, Flaherty won the Tony Award, Drama Desk Award and Outer Critics Circle Award for Best Score for Ragtime, and the team has received multiple additional Tony, Grammy and Olivier nominations, including a Tony nomination for the score of Anastasia.
