The Simpsons is an American animated sitcom created by Matt Groening for the Fox Broadcasting Company. It is the longest-running American animated series and the longest-running American sitcom. The series debuted on December 17, 1989, with the Christmas special "Simpsons Roasting on an Open Fire" and has aired more than 780 episodes across over 36 seasons.

Treehouse of Horror

The annual Halloween anthology episodes, beginning with "Treehouse of Horror" (season 2, 1990), present three non-canonical horror stories per episode and are among the series' most popular traditions.
