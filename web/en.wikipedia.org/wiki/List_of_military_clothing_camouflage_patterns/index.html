List of military clothing camouflage patterns

This is a list of camouflage patterns used by military forces around the world for clothing and equipment. Patterns range from hand-drawn designs of the Second World War to modern digitally generated pixelated patterns.

PatternCountry / originNotes
Woodland (M81)United StatesFour-colour green/brown/black pattern standard from 1981; copied worldwide
DPM (Disruptive Pattern Material)United KingdomIn service from the 1960s to 2010s, replaced by MTP
MTP (Multi-Terrain Pattern)United KingdomDerived from Crye MultiCam, adopted 2010
MultiCam / OCPUnited States (Crye Precision)Adopted by the U.S. Army as Operational Camouflage Pattern
MARPATUnited States Marine CorpsPixelated woodland/desert pattern introduced 2002
ACU / UCPUnited States ArmyGrey-based Universal Camouflage Pattern, 2004–2019
FlecktarnGermanyFive-colour speckled pattern, in Bundeswehr service since the 1990s
Centre-Europe (CE)FranceFour-colour woodland variant used by French forces
EMR (Digital Flora)RussiaPixelated green pattern standard for Russian forces
Type 07 / Type 21 (Xingkong)ChinaPLA digital and later "starry sky" patterns
CADPATCanadaFirst computer-generated pixelated pattern, from 1997
Auscam (DPCU)Australia"Bunny ears" disruptive pattern clothing uniform
SplittertarnGermany (WWII)Splinter pattern used by the Wehrmacht
ErbsenmusterGermany (WWII)"Pea dot" pattern of the Waffen-SS
Tiger stripeSouth Vietnam / USABrush-stroke pattern used in the Vietnam War
Chocolate-chipUnited StatesSix-colour desert pattern of the Gulf War era

Modern pattern design uses computer modelling of target environments and human vision; the trend since the 2000s has been toward multi-environment patterns intended to work acceptably across many terrains rather than optimally in one.
