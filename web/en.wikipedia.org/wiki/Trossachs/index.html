Trossachs

The Trossachs (Scottish Gaelic: Na Tròiseachan) is a wooded glen and area of hills and lochs in the Stirling council area of Scotland, north of Glasgow and east of Loch Lomond, now at the heart of Loch Lomond and The Trossachs National Park.

Geography

The core glen runs between Loch Katrine and Aberfoyle, with peaks such as Ben Venue and Ben A'an. Loch Katrine supplies Glasgow's water and is sailed by the steamer SS Sir Walter Scott.

Tourism history

The area was popularised by Sir Walter Scott's poem The Lady of the Lake (1810) and novel Rob Roy, making it one of Scotland's earliest tourist destinations. See Loch Lomond.
