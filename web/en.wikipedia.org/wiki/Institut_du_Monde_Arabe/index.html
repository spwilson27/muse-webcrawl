Institut du Monde Arabe

The Institut du Monde Arabe (Arab World Institute), abbreviated IMA, is a cultural institute in Paris, France, founded in 1980 by France and Arab League states to promote understanding of Arab culture.

Location5th arrondissement, Paris
ArchitectJean Nouvel and Architecture-Studio
Inaugurated30 November 1987

Architecture

Designed by Jean Nouvel, the building on the Seine's Left Bank is famous for its south façade of 240 photosensitive mashrabiya apertures that open and close with sunlight — a landmark of high-tech architecture. It houses a museum, library, exhibitions, concerts and a rooftop terrace overlooking Notre-Dame.
