Energy Institute

The Energy Institute (EI) is a professional membership body and learned society for the energy industry, based in London, United Kingdom. It was formed in 2003 from the merger of the Institute of Petroleum and the Institute of Energy, whose histories stretch back to 1913 and 1927 respectively. The EI aims to promote the safe, environmentally responsible and efficient supply and use of energy in all its forms and applications.

The Energy Institute, New Cavendish Street, London.

Activities

The Institute accredits professional qualifications, awards Chartered Energy Engineer and Chartered Energy Manager status, publishes technical guidance and the journal Petroleum Review, and organises events including International Petroleum Week. Since 2023 it has published the Statistical Review of World Energy, which it took over from BP after more than 70 years.

Membership

Membership grades range from student and affiliate through to Member (MEI) and Fellow (FEI), with members working across oil and gas, renewables, nuclear and energy management.
