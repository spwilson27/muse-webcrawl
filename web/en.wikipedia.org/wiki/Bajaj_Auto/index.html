Bajaj Auto Limited is an Indian multinational automotive manufacturing company based in Pune. It manufactures motorcycles, scooters, and auto rickshaws, and is the world's largest manufacturer of three-wheelers and among the largest motorcycle producers globally, exporting to dozens of countries across Latin America, Africa, and Asia.

History

The company was founded in 1945 as Bachraj Trading Corporation by Jamnalal Bajaj, initially importing and selling two- and three-wheelers. In 1948 it began selling imported Vespa scooters, and in 1959 obtained a licence from Piaggio to manufacture them in India, incorporating as Bajaj Auto in 1960. The Bajaj Chetak scooter, launched in 1972, became an icon of Indian middle-class mobility, with waiting lists stretching for years. After the Piaggio licence expired, Bajaj developed its own motorcycles, notably the Pulsar (2001) and Discover ranges. In 2007 it acquired a stake in Austria's KTM, later becoming its largest shareholder, and partnered with Triumph Motorcycles to develop mid-capacity bikes.

Products

Motorcycle brands include Pulsar, Dominar, Avenger, Platina, and CT, alongside KTM and Husqvarna models produced in India. Its three-wheeler range, sold under names such as RE, dominates the auto rickshaw market. The Qute quadricycle, launched in 2018, targeted urban low-cost transport. Bajaj phased out scooter production in 2009 to focus on motorcycles, but revived the Chetak nameplate in 2020 as an electric scooter.

Operations

Manufacturing plants are located at Akurdi and Chakan near Pune, Waluj near Aurangabad, and Pantnagar in Uttarakhand. The company is part of the Bajaj Group founded by Jamnalal Bajaj, a close associate of Mahatma Gandhi.
