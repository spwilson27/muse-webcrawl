List of castles in Wales

Wales has more castles per square mile than any other country in Europe — over 600 have been identified, built by Welsh princes, Norman and Marcher lords, and the English kings who conquered Wales in the 13th century. The Castles and Town Walls of King Edward in Gwynedd (Beaumaris, Caernarfon, Conwy, and Harlech) are a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Many castles are in the care of Cadw.
