Product Sans

Product Sans

Classification: Geometric sans-serif

Commissioned by: Google

Released: 1 September 2015

Licence: Proprietary

Product Sans is a geometric sans-serif typeface created by Google for branding purposes. It replaced the old Google logo, which had used the Catull serif typeface, on 1 September 2015 as part of the company's new visual identity.

Design

The typeface is based on the classic proportions of geometric sans-serifs such as Futura, but with distinctive touches: the uppercase "G" reflects the circular form of the logo, and the slanted terminal of the lowercase "e" echoes the tilt of the previous wordmark. Google described the design as combining "the mathematical purity of geometric forms with the optical proportions of a humanist sans."

Usage

Product Sans is used in the Google logo and in product wordmarks across services such as Google Maps and Google Drive. A variant, Google Sans, was later developed for user-interface text in Google products, and Google Sans Text further adapted the design for smaller sizes and body text. Product Sans is proprietary and is not released for general public licensing.
