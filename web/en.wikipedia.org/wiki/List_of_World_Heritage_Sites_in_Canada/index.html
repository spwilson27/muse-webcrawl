List of World Heritage Sites in Canada

The United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) designates World Heritage Sites of outstanding universal value to cultural or natural heritage. Canada accepted the World Heritage Convention in 1976, and its first sites — L'Anse aux Meadows and Nahanni National Park — were inscribed in 1978. As of 2023, Canada has 22 World Heritage Sites, comprising 10 cultural, 11 natural and one mixed site.
