Li Shufu

Li Shufu (Eric Li)
Born: 25 June 1963, Taizhou, Zhejiang, China
Occupation: Businessman
Known for: Founder and chairman of Geely

Li Shufu (Chinese: 李书福; born 25 June 1963), also known as Eric Li, is a Chinese billionaire businessman. He is the founder and chairman of Zhejiang Geely Holding Group, the parent company of the car manufacturer Geely Automobile, and is credited with building Geely into one of China's largest private automakers.

Career

Li founded Geely in 1986, initially manufacturing refrigerator parts and later aluminium panels and motorcycles, before entering automobile production in the late 1990s despite lacking a state licence at first. Under his leadership Geely acquired the Swedish carmaker Volvo Cars from Ford in 2010 for about US$1.8 billion, in what was then the largest overseas acquisition by a Chinese automaker. Geely has since acquired stakes or control in Proton, Lotus Cars, the London EV Company (maker of London black cabs), smart (with Mercedes-Benz), and flying-car company Terrafugia. In 2018 Li became the largest single shareholder of Daimler AG with a stake of about 9.7 per cent.

Other activities

Li has served as a member of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference and is frequently ranked among China's wealthiest individuals by Forbes and the Hurun Report.
