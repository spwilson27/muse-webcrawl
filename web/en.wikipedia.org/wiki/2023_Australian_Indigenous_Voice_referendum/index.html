2023 Australian Indigenous Voice referendum

The 2023 Australian Indigenous Voice referendum was held on 14 October 2023. Voters rejected a proposal to amend the Constitution to recognise Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples by establishing an Indigenous Voice to Parliament, an advisory body.


Date: 14 October 2023
Result: No — 60.06% nationally
Yes: 39.94%; majority in no state

Background

The proposal stemmed from the 2017 Uluru Statement from the Heart, which called for Voice, Treaty and Truth. Prime Minister Anthony Albanese committed to the referendum after Labor's 2022 election victory.

Campaign and result

The Yes campaign argued recognition and listening would improve outcomes; the No campaign, backed by the Coalition, argued it was risky, divisive and lacking detail. The proposal required a double majority — nationally and in a majority of states — and failed both tests, with No winning all six states; only the Australian Capital Territory voted Yes.
