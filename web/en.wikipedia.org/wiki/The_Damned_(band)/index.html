The Damned (band)

The Damned
Origin: London, England
Formed: 1976
Genres: Punk rock, gothic rock, post-punk
Members: Dave Vanian, Captain Sensible, Rat Scabies

The Damned are an English rock band formed in London in 1976 by Dave Vanian, Brian James, Captain Sensible and Rat Scabies. They were the first British punk band to release a single ("New Rose", 1976) and an album (Damned Damned Damned, 1977), and first to tour the US.

Career

Evolving toward gothic rock on Machine Gun Etiquette (1979), The Black Album (1980) and Strawberries, they scored a UK No. 3 hit with "Eloise" in 1986. Vanian's baritone and horror persona made them pioneers of goth.
