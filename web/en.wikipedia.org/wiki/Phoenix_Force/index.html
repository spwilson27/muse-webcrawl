Phoenix Force

The Phoenix Force is a fictional cosmic entity appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. An immortal manifestation of the universal force of life and passion, it is most famously associated with the X-Men character Jean Grey, who became its host in the landmark "Dark Phoenix Saga" storyline (1980) by Chris Claremont and John Byrne.

Depicted as a vast raptor of cosmic fire, the Phoenix Force is linked to psionic energy, resurrection, and the cycle of death and rebirth across the universe. It has bonded with multiple hosts in later stories and played a central role in numerous X-Men and Avengers events.
