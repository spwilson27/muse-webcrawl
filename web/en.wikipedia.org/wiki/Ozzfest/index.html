Ozzfest

Ozzfest
Founded: 1996, by Sharon and Ozzy Osbourne
Genre: Heavy metal, hard rock
Years active: 1996–2018 (intermittent)

Ozzfest was an annual American heavy-metal festival tour founded by Sharon Osbourne after Ozzy Osbourne was rejected from Lollapalooza. It became the leading metal package tour, launching and showcasing nu-metal and metalcore acts.

History

Headliners and regulars included Black Sabbath, Slipknot, Korn, Marilyn Manson, Pantera, Tool, and Iron Maiden — whose 2005 feud with Sharon Osbourne became notorious. A 2007 edition was free ("FreeFest"). The last full tour was 2008; later editions were one-off festivals, merging with Knotfest as Ozzfest Meets Knotfest (2016–17), ending with a New Year's Eve 2018 show.
