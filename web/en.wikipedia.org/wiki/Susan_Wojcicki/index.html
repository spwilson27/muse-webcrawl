Susan Wojcicki

Susan Diane Wojcicki (July 5, 1968 – August 9, 2024) was an American business executive who was the chief executive officer of YouTube from 2014 to 2023. She was one of Google's earliest employees — famously, Google was started in the garage of her Menlo Park home, which she rented to Larry Page and Sergey Brin in 1998 — and she joined the company in 1999 as its first marketing manager.

Career at Google

Wojcicki worked on Google's advertising products, including AdSense, and led the development of Google Video. She advocated strongly for Google's acquisition of YouTube in 2006 for $1.65 billion and later oversaw the acquisition of DoubleClick. In February 2014 she became CEO of YouTube, growing it into a platform with more than two billion monthly users and expanding YouTube Premium, YouTube TV and the creator economy.

Later life

She stepped down as YouTube CEO in February 2023, citing a wish to focus on family, health and personal projects, and moved into an advisory role at Google and Alphabet. Wojcicki died of non-small-cell lung cancer in August 2024, aged 56.
