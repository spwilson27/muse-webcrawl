The Kunming–Singapore railway, also known as the Pan-Asia Railway, is a network of rail lines intended to connect Kunming in China's Yunnan province with Singapore, passing through Laos, Thailand and Malaysia.

Development

The idea dates to colonial-era proposals and was revived by ASEAN in the 1990s. The central corridor took shape with the China–Laos railway, opened in December 2021, linking Kunming to Vientiane. Thailand has been constructing a high-speed line from Bangkok toward Nong Khai, while Malaysia's existing line from Kuala Lumpur to the Thai border at Padang Besar is electrified double track.

Gaps

Completion of a continuous standard-gauge route depends on the Thai sections reaching the Lao border and on connections at the Malaysian end; freight services between China and Laos have grown rapidly since 2021, transforming landlocked Laos into a regional land link.
