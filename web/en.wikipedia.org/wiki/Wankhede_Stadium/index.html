Wankhede Stadium

Wankhede Stadium is an international cricket stadium in Mumbai, India, owned by the Mumbai Cricket Association (MCA). Opened in 1974, it has a capacity of about 33,000 and is the home ground of the Mumbai Indians of the Indian Premier League and of the Mumbai cricket team.

History

The stadium was built after a dispute between the MCA and the Cricket Club of India over ticket allocations at Brabourne Stadium. It was named after S. K. Wankhede, a politician and MCA president who drove the project. The first Test match, between India and the West Indies, was played in January 1975. The stadium was extensively redeveloped ahead of the 2011 Cricket World Cup, with new stands and improved facilities.

Memorable matches

Wankhede hosted the final of the 2011 Cricket World Cup, in which India defeated Sri Lanka, with captain M. S. Dhoni finishing the match with a famous six. Other landmarks include Ravi Shastri hitting six sixes in an over in a Ranji Trophy match in 1985 — only the second such feat in first-class cricket — and Sachin Tendulkar's final international match, a Test against the West Indies in November 2013. The stadium hosted matches of the 2023 Cricket World Cup, including a semi-final.
