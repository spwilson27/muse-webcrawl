Diva is the debut solo studio album by Scottish singer Annie Lennox, released on 6 April 1992 by RCA Records. The album entered the UK Albums Chart at number one and reached the top 10 in numerous countries, including the United States, where it peaked at number 23 on the Billboard 200. It has sold over six million copies worldwide.

Background and reception

Following the end of her partnership with Dave Stewart in Eurythmics, Lennox worked principally with producer Stephen Lipson on Diva, writing all but one of the songs herself. Critics praised the album's songwriting and Lennox's vocals, and at the 1993 Brit Awards Diva won British Album of the Year while Lennox won British Female Solo Artist. The album was also nominated for Album of the Year at the 35th Grammy Awards.

Singles

Diva produced the hit singles "Why", "Walking on Broken Glass", "Little Bird", "Precious", and "Cold". "Walking on Broken Glass" reached number one in Canada and the top 10 in the United Kingdom and the United States, while "Little Bird" was released as a double A-side with Lennox's Eurythmics-era recording of "Love Song for a Vampire", written for the film Bram Stoker's Dracula. The accompanying video album, Totally Diva, won a Grammy Award for Best Long Form Music Video.
