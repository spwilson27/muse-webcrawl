Tottenham Hotspur Football Club, commonly referred to as Tottenham or Spurs, is a professional football club based in Tottenham, London, England. It competes in the Premier League, the top flight of English football. The club was founded in 1882 by schoolboys from St John's School and Tottenham Grammar School, initially as Hotspur Football Club.

History

Tottenham won the FA Cup in 1901, becoming the first non-League club to do so since the formation of the Football League in 1888, and remain the only non-League winners of the trophy. Under manager Bill Nicholson, Spurs became the first club in the twentieth century to achieve the league and FA Cup Double, in the 1960–61 season. They won the European Cup Winners' Cup in 1963, the first European trophy won by a British club, and the inaugural UEFA Cup in 1972.

The club played at White Hart Lane from 1899 until 2017, when the ground was demolished and replaced on the same site by the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, which opened in April 2019.

Honours

Tottenham have won two league titles, eight FA Cups, four League Cups, the European Cup Winners' Cup, and two UEFA Cups (1972 and 1984), with the club also winning the UEFA Europa League in 2025, defeating Manchester United in the final in Bilbao.

Rivalries

The club's fiercest rivalry is with Arsenal, contested as the North London derby; Arsenal's relocation to Islington in 1913 and a controversial promotion decision in 1919 seeded the enmity. Spurs also share rivalries with Chelsea and West Ham United.
