Polypharmacy

Polypharmacy is the concurrent use of multiple medications by one person, commonly defined as the regular use of five or more medicines. It is most prevalent among older adults with multiple chronic conditions.

Risks

Polypharmacy increases the risk of drug interactions, adverse drug reactions, falls, hospitalisation, and medication errors, and can reduce adherence. "Problematic polypharmacy" refers to use where the harms outweigh the benefits; regular medication review and deprescribing aim to reduce it.

Management

Guidelines recommend reviewing each medicine's indication, dose, and continuing benefit, using tools such as the Beers criteria and STOPP/START criteria to identify potentially inappropriate prescribing in older people.
