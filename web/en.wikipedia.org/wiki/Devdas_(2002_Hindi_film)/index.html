Devdas is a 2002 Indian Hindi-language romantic drama film directed by Sanjay Leela Bhansali. It is based on the 1917 Bengali novel of the same name by Sarat Chandra Chattopadhyay. The film stars Shah Rukh Khan in the title role, alongside Aishwarya Rai as Paro and Madhuri Dixit as Chandramukhi.

Plot

Devdas Mukherjee returns from London to his wealthy family's estate, rekindling his childhood love with Parvati "Paro" Chakraborty. When his family rejects the match on grounds of status, Paro is married off to an older widowed landlord. Devastated, Devdas descends into alcoholism, finding refuge and unrequited devotion at the Calcutta establishment of the courtesan Chandramukhi. His decline ends in tragedy as he dies at the gate of Paro's marital home.

Reception

The film was one of the most expensive Indian productions of its time, noted for its opulent sets and costumes. It premiered at the Cannes Film Festival and was India's submission for the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film. It won numerous Filmfare Awards and National Film Awards.
