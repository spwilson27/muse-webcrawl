The Gulfstream G650 is a large, ultra-long-range business jet produced by Gulfstream Aerospace. Together with its extended-range variant, the G650ER, it was for several years the company's flagship and among the fastest and longest-ranging civil aircraft in service.

Development

Gulfstream announced the G650 in March 2008. It introduced a new wide cabin — the largest in its class at the time — with large oval windows, a fly-by-wire flight control system, and Rolls-Royce BR725 engines. The aircraft first flew on 25 November 2009 and received FAA type certification in September 2012.

Performance

The G650 can carry up to 19 passengers and cruise at Mach 0.90, with a maximum speed of Mach 0.925 and a service ceiling of 51,000 ft. The G650ER, announced in 2014, extended the range to 7,500 nautical miles, enabling routes such as New York–Hong Kong non-stop.

Successors

The G650 family was succeeded in Gulfstream's line-up by the G700 and G800, although the G650 remained in production for over a decade with more than 500 aircraft delivered across the family.
