Row cover

Row cover is a lightweight fabric used in gardening and agriculture to protect plants from insects, frost, wind and excessive sun. Made typically of spunbonded polypropylene or polyester, it is laid loosely over crop rows — hence also called floating row cover or garden fleece — and secured at the edges with soil, stones or staples.

Uses

Row covers act as a physical barrier against pests such as flea beetles, cabbage root fly and carrot fly without insecticides, and extend the growing season by providing a few degrees of frost protection and a warmer, more humid microclimate. Lightweight grades (around 17 g/m²) exclude insects; heavier frost fleece (30 g/m² and up) protects overwintering crops. Because the fabric transmits light, air and rain, plants can be left covered for weeks, though pollinator access must be allowed for fruiting crops such as strawberries and squash.
