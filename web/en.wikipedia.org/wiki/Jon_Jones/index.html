Jon Jones

Jonathan Dwight Jones (born July 19, 1987) is an American former professional mixed martial artist who competed in the Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC), where he held the UFC Heavyweight Championship and was a two-time UFC Light Heavyweight Champion. Widely regarded as one of the greatest mixed martial artists of all time, Jones became the youngest champion in UFC history when he won the light heavyweight title at age 23.

Early life

Jones was born in Rochester, New York, and raised in Endicott, New York. His father was a pastor and his mother a nurse. His older brother Arthur and younger brother Chandler both played in the National Football League. Jones was a state wrestling champion at Union-Endicott High School and went on to win a national junior college wrestling championship at Iowa Central Community College before leaving to pursue mixed martial arts.

Mixed martial arts career

Jones made his professional debut in April 2008 and signed with the UFC later that year, quickly gaining attention for his unorthodox striking, creative elbows, and dominant wrestling. On March 19, 2011, he defeated Maurício "Shogun" Rua at UFC 128 to become the youngest champion in UFC history. He went on to defend the light heavyweight title a record number of times, defeating former champions including Quinton Jackson, Lyoto Machida, Rashad Evans, Vitor Belfort, Chael Sonnen, Alexander Gustafsson, Glover Teixeira, and Daniel Cormier.

His only career loss came via disqualification for illegal downward elbows against Matt Hamill in 2009, a result widely disputed. His career was also marked by controversies outside the cage, including a hit-and-run arrest in 2015, failed drug tests, and title strippings, though several findings were later attributed to contaminated supplements and "pulsing" metabolites.

Heavyweight career and retirement

After vacating the light heavyweight title in 2020, Jones returned in March 2023 at UFC 285, defeating Ciryl Gane by first-round submission to win the vacant UFC Heavyweight Championship. He defended the title against Stipe Miocic at UFC 309 in November 2024, winning by knockout. In June 2025, UFC CEO Dana White announced that Jones had retired, and Jones was subsequently stripped of the heavyweight title as Tom Aspinall was promoted to undisputed champion.

Championships and accomplishments

  • Two-time UFC Light Heavyweight Champion (eleven title defenses across two reigns)
  • UFC Heavyweight Champion (one defense)
  • Youngest champion in UFC history (age 23)
  • Longest unbeaten streak in UFC history
