The Boy and the Heron

Japanese title: 君たちはどう生きるか (Kimi-tachi wa Dō Ikiru ka)
Directed by: Hayao Miyazaki
Studio: Studio Ghibli
Released: July 14, 2023 (Japan)

The Boy and the Heron is a 2023 Japanese animated fantasy film written and directed by Hayao Miyazaki and produced by Studio Ghibli. Its Japanese title, Kimi-tachi wa Dō Ikiru ka ("How Do You Live?"), is borrowed from the 1937 novel by Genzaburo Yoshino, though the film's story is original. It is Miyazaki's first feature film in a decade, following The Wind Rises (2013).

Plot

In 1943, during the Pacific War, young Mahito Maki loses his mother in a Tokyo hospital fire. After his father remarries his late mother's younger sister, Natsuko, Mahito moves to her rural estate, where a strange grey heron taunts him and leads him to an abandoned tower built by his eccentric grand-uncle. Entering the tower, Mahito journeys through a surreal other world of life, death, and creation, confronting his grief and the question of how to live in a world marked by conflict.

Production and release

Miyazaki came out of retirement to make the film, which was produced semi-autobiographically over roughly seven years largely by hand-drawn animation. Ghibli famously released it in Japan with virtually no marketing — no trailers or synopses — relying entirely on audience curiosity. The Japanese voice cast includes Soma Santoki as Mahito and Masaki Suda as the Grey Heron; the English dub features Luca Padovan, Robert Pattinson, Christian Bale, Dave Bautista, and Florence Pugh.

Reception

The film was acclaimed by critics and grossed over $290 million worldwide, becoming one of the highest-grossing Japanese films ever. It won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature at the 96th Academy Awards — Miyazaki's second Oscar after Spirited Away — as well as the Golden Globe and BAFTA in the category.
