Shandong (17; Chinese: 山东舰; pinyin: Shāndōng Jiàn) is the second aircraft carrier of the People's Liberation Army Navy (PLAN) Surface Force and the first domestically built aircraft carrier in China. Constructed at Dalian Shipyard, she was launched in April 2017 and commissioned on 17 December 2019 at Yulin Naval Base on Hainan Island.

Shandong is a STOBAR carrier broadly derived from the Liaoning design but with significant improvements: a larger hangar, a modified island, and refined weapons fit, allowing a larger air group of up to about 44 aircraft, principally Shenyang J-15 fighters. At roughly 70,000 tonnes full load she is larger than Liaoning, though still smaller than U.S. Navy supercarriers and, like Liaoning, lacking catapults.

Construction and service

The carrier was developed with extensive domestic engineering, reflecting experience gained refitting Liaoning. She completed eight sea trials before commissioning, the first fleet carrier ever homeported on Hainan, giving China a second carrier alongside Liaoning at Qingdao. Shandong has since undertaken training and patrols in the South China Sea and Western Pacific, and in 2023 conducted exercises east of Taiwan.

She is named for Shandong province, continuing the PLAN practice of naming carriers after Chinese provinces. A third carrier, Fujian, was launched in 2022 with electromagnetic catapults, marking a generational step beyond Shandong's ski-jump design.
