Association Sportive de Monaco Football Club, commonly referred to as AS Monaco, is a professional football club based in Fontvieille, Monaco. The club was founded in 1924 and plays in Ligue 1, the top tier of French football. Despite not being a French club, it is a member of the French Football Federation and competes in the French league system.

History

Monaco is one of the most successful clubs in French football, having won eight league titles and five Coupe de France trophies. The club has also competed in European competitions, reaching the final of the UEFA Cup Winners' Cup in 1992 and the UEFA Champions League in 2004, losing to FC Porto. Under Russian billionaire Dmitry Rybolovlev, who acquired a majority stake in 2011, Monaco won the 2016–17 Ligue 1 title with a young, attacking side.

Stadium

The club has played at the Stade Louis II since 1985. The stadium, built on reclaimed land in the Fontvieille district, is recognisable for the nine arches at its southern end.
