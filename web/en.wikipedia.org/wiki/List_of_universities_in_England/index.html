List of universities in England

This is a list of universities in England. England is home to more than 100 universities, from the medieval University of Oxford (teaching from 1096) and University of Cambridge (founded 1209) to the "plate glass" universities of the 1960s and the former polytechnics granted university status in 1992.

Groupings

Many English universities belong to mission groups such as the Russell Group of research-intensive universities, the University Alliance and MillionPlus. The majority are represented by Universities UK.
