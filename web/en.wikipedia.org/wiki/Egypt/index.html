Egypt

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

[Image: The Great Sphinx and pyramids of Giza on the desert plateau]

Egypt, officially the Arab Republic of Egypt, is a transcontinental country spanning northeast Africa and the Sinai Peninsula in Asia, linked by the Suez Canal. Its capital and largest city is Cairo, one of the largest cities in Africa and the Middle East. With well over 100 million inhabitants, most living along the Nile, Egypt has one of the longest recorded histories of any country, from Ancient Egypt and the pharaohs to the present republic.

Contents1. Geography and history

Geography and history

The Nile valley and delta are Egypt's lifeblood amid surrounding desert. After pharaonic, Greek, Roman, Byzantine and Islamic caliphate periods, Egypt became a British protectorate, then a kingdom, then a republic in 1953. The 2011 revolution and its aftermath reshaped modern politics.
