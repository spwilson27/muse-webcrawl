Rooster (zodiac)

The Rooster (雞 / 鸡) is the tenth of the 12-year cycle of animals that appear in the Chinese zodiac, in the Chinese calendar. The Year of the Rooster is associated with the Earthly Branch symbol 酉. In the Japanese zodiac and the Tibetan zodiac, the animal is the bird; in the Vietnamese zodiac, the Rooster is also used (unlike the Rabbit, which is replaced by the Cat).

Years and the Five Elements

People born within these date ranges can be said to have been born in the "Year of the Rooster", while bearing the following elemental sign:

  • 5 February 1921 – 24 January 1922: Metal Rooster
  • 23 January 1933 – 10 February 1934: Water Rooster
  • 9 February 1945 – 28 January 1946: Wood Rooster
  • 28 January 1957 – 15 February 1958: Fire Rooster
  • 13 February 1969 – 5 February 1970: Earth Rooster
  • 1 February 1981 – 24 January 1982: Metal Rooster
  • 23 January 1993 – 9 February 1994: Water Rooster
  • 9 February 2005 – 28 January 2006: Wood Rooster
  • 28 January 2017 – 15 February 2018: Fire Rooster
  • 13 February 2029 – 2 February 2030: Earth Rooster

Traits

In Chinese astrology, people born in the Year of the Rooster are said to be observant, hardworking, resourceful, courageous and talented, but also potentially vain, proud and outspoken. Roosters are thought to be most compatible with the Ox and the Snake, and least compatible with the Rabbit.
