A4

A4 is a designation used in several contexts, most commonly for the standard paper size defined by ISO 216 and for roads and other numbered routes.

Paper size

A4 paper measures 210 × 297 millimetres (8.27 × 11.69 inches). It is the most widely used paper size in the world for letters, documents, and printing, standard in most countries except the United States, Canada, and parts of Latin America, where Letter size predominates. The A-series is based on a height-to-width ratio of the square root of two, so that folding a sheet in half along its long edge yields the next smaller size (A5) with the same proportions; A0 has an area of one square metre.

Other uses

"A4" also names major roads in several European countries, including the A4 road in England from London to Avonmouth and the A4 motorways of France, Germany, Italy, Poland, and other states. The designation is further used for vehicles (the Audi A4), aircraft, musical pitch notation contexts, and the Apple A4 processor.
