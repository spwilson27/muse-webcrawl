Shamakhi Astrophysical Observatory

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Shamakhi Astrophysical Observatory (Azerbaijani: Şamaxı Astrofizika Rəsədxanası), named after Nasreddin Tusi, is an observatory in the Caucasus Mountains in Azerbaijan. It is located on the eastern slope of Pirqulu, near the city of Shamakhi, at an altitude of about 1,435–1,500 m above sea level, in an area chosen for its large number of clear nights.

The observatory was founded in 1957 under the Azerbaijan Academy of Sciences and began regular observations around 1960. Its principal instrument is a 2-metre reflecting telescope manufactured by the Carl Zeiss Jena company of Germany, commissioned in 1966, supplemented by smaller instruments including a 70 cm Cassegrain reflector and solar telescopes. Research at the observatory has covered solar physics, stellar astrophysics, comets and asteroids, and the physics of non-stable stars.
