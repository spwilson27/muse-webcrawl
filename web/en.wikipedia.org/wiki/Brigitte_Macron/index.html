Brigitte Marie-Claude Macron (née Trogneux, previously Auzière; born 13 April 1953) is a French former teacher and the wife of Emmanuel Macron, President of France since 2017. As spouse of the president she plays a public role in French civic life, although France has no official title of "First Lady."

Early life and career

Brigitte Trogneux was born in Amiens, the youngest of six children in a family of chocolatiers who run the Trogneux chocolate business, known for its macarons. She studied literature and worked as a teacher of French and Latin, teaching at the Lycée Saint-Louis de Gonzague in Paris and, earlier, at La Providence, a Jesuit high school in Amiens.

Marriage

At La Providence in the early 1990s she supervised a theatre workshop attended by the teenage Emmanuel Macron. Their relationship, a frequent subject of media attention because of their 24-year age difference, became public years later. She divorced her first husband, banker André-Louis Auzière, with whom she has three children, and married Macron in 2007.

Role as presidential spouse

Brigitte Macron played an active part in her husband's 2017 presidential campaign. After his election, a proposed official "First Lady" statute was dropped following public petition, and instead a "transparency charter" set out her role and resources. She focuses on issues including education, disability, children's welfare, culture and the fight against bullying and online harassment of young people. In 2019 she returned to teaching by co-founding an adult education institute (L'Institut des Vocations pour l'Emploi) in Clichy-sous-Bois aimed at helping adults without qualifications return to work.
