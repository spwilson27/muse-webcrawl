Women's National Basketball Association

The Women's National Basketball Association (WNBA) is a women's professional basketball league based in the United States. Founded in 1996 as the women's counterpart to the NBA, it began play in 1997 with eight teams and now consists of 13 teams, with further expansion announced.

History

The league launched on the momentum of the United States women's national team's gold medal at the 1996 Atlanta Olympics. The Houston Comets, led by Cynthia Cooper, Sheryl Swoopes and Tina Thompson, won the first four championships. Stars such as Lisa Leslie, Diana Taurasi, Sue Bird, Breanna Stewart and A'ja Wilson defined successive eras, and the arrival of Caitlin Clark in 2024 drove record attendance and television ratings.

Format

Teams play a regular season from May to September, followed by playoffs culminating in the WNBA Finals. The league also stages the mid-season Commissioner's Cup tournament.
