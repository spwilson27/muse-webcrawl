Cotangent bundle

In differential geometry, the cotangent bundle of a smooth manifold M is the vector bundle of all the cotangent spaces at every point in the manifold. It is usually denoted T*M. The elements of the cotangent bundle are cotangent vectors: linear maps on the tangent space at a point, also known as differential 1-forms evaluated at a point.

Structure

If M has dimension n, the cotangent bundle has dimension 2n and is itself a smooth manifold. Local coordinates on M together with the components of a covector give natural coordinates (x1, …, xn, p1, …, pn) on T*M. Unlike the tangent bundle, the cotangent bundle carries a canonical symplectic structure: the exterior derivative of the tautological one-form is a symplectic form.

Role in physics

The cotangent bundle is the natural phase space of classical mechanics: position coordinates come from the base manifold and the covector components are the generalized momenta. Hamilton's equations are formulated in terms of Hamiltonian vector fields on this symplectic manifold, and in geometric quantization and microlocal analysis the cotangent bundle plays a central role.
