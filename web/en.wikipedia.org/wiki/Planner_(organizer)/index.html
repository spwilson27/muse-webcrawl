Planner (organizer)

A planner (also called a personal organizer, personal diary, or appointment book) is a book or binder designed to help an individual organise their schedule, tasks, contacts, and notes. Planners typically contain calendar pages (daily, weekly, or monthly), address-book sections, and blank or ruled pages, often in a refillable ring binder so inserts can be replaced each year.

History and formats

Commercial appointment diaries date to the 19th century. Loose-leaf organizer systems became widely popular in the 1980s, with brands such as Filofax and Day-Timer marketing planners as professional accessories. Common sizes range from pocket to desk (A4/letter). Many planners incorporate productivity methods such as to-do lists, goal-setting pages, and time-blocking layouts.

Digital successors

From the 1990s, personal digital assistants and later smartphone calendar and task apps took over much of the planner's role, though paper planners retain a following for handwriting, journaling, and methods such as bullet journaling.
