OASIS (organization)

Full nameOrganization for the Advancement of Structured Information Standards
Founded1993 (as SGML Open)
TypeNon-profit standards consortium
HeadquartersBurlington, Massachusetts, United States

OASIS, the Organization for the Advancement of Structured Information Standards, is a global non-profit consortium that works on the development, convergence and adoption of open standards for cybersecurity, computing, the Internet of Things and other areas. It was founded in 1993 as SGML Open, a consortium of vendors promoting the Standard Generalized Markup Language, and renamed in 1998 as its focus broadened to XML and related technologies.

Standards

OASIS technical committees have produced many widely used specifications, including:

  • DocBook and DITA — XML formats for documentation
  • OpenDocument (ODF) — the ISO-standard office document format
  • SAML, XACML and WS-Security — security standards
  • MQTT and AMQP — messaging protocols
  • UBL — the Universal Business Language for electronic business documents
  • STIX and TAXII — cyber threat intelligence sharing formats, and the CAP emergency alerting protocol

Organisation

OASIS counts hundreds of member organisations — companies, governments, universities and individuals — and develops standards through open technical committees whose work is publicly accessible. In recent years the consortium has also embraced open-source-style "open projects" alongside its traditional standards track, and operates as OASIS Open.
