Internet in Germany

The Internet in Germany is among the largest national segments of the global Internet. Germany's country code top-level domain, .de, is one of the most popular ccTLDs in the world by number of registered domains.

Infrastructure

Broadband access is provided mainly over DSL and cable networks, with Deutsche Telekom the largest provider. Fibre-optic coverage expanded more slowly than in many comparable countries, prompting government broadband strategies. DE-CIX in Frankfurt is one of the world's largest internet exchange points by traffic.

Usage

The great majority of the German population is online. Regulation of the sector is led by the Bundesnetzagentur, and German law has historically placed strong emphasis on data protection and privacy.

History

Early German networking centred on the research network DFN, which connected universities and research institutes and linked Germany to the emerging Internet in the 1980s.
