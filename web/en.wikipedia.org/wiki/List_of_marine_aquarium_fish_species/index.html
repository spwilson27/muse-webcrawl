List of marine aquarium fish species

Marine aquarium fish
Mostly wild-caught reef species, though captive breeding is expanding.

This is a list of fish species commonly kept in marine (saltwater) aquaria, especially reef aquariums. Marine fishkeeping demands stable salinity, temperature and water chemistry, and many species have specialised feeding requirements; suitability ratings vary between guides.
