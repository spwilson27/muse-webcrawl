Fantastic Beasts

Fantastic Beasts is a film series directed by David Yates and written by J. K. Rowling, serving as a prequel to the Harry Potter film series. Produced and distributed by Warner Bros. Pictures, the series is the second instalment of the Wizarding World shared media franchise. It stars Eddie Redmayne as Newt Scamander, a magizoologist travelling the world decades before the events of Harry Potter.

Films

The series consists of three films: Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them (2016), Fantastic Beasts: The Crimes of Grindelwald (2018), and Fantastic Beasts: The Secrets of Dumbledore (2022). The story follows Scamander's adventures and the rising conflict with the dark wizard Gellert Grindelwald, portrayed by Johnny Depp in the first two films and by Mads Mikkelsen in the third.

Origin

The series takes its name from Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them, a 2001 book Rowling wrote for the charity Comic Relief under the pseudonym Newt Scamander, presented as a textbook used by students at Hogwarts describing magical creatures. Rowling made her screenwriting debut with the first film.

Reception

The first film was a commercial and critical success, grossing over $814 million worldwide and winning the Academy Award for Best Costume Design — the first Oscar win for the Wizarding World franchise. The later films received more mixed reviews and declining box office returns, and although the series was originally announced as five films, no further instalments have been produced.
