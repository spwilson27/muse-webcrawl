Keswick family

Placeholder image: Jardine House, Hong Kong.

The Keswick family (pronounced "Kezzick") is a Scottish business family of Dumfriesshire origin that has controlled the Hong Kong–based conglomerate Jardine Matheson for much of its history. Descended from the marriage of Jean Johnstone to William Jardine's niece's family line through Margaret Keswick, the family became connected to the firm in the 19th century and has produced several of its taipans (chairmen).

History with Jardine Matheson

Thomas Keswick joined Jardine, Matheson & Co. in the 1850s, and his son William Keswick (1834–1912) became a partner and later Member of Parliament in Britain. In the 20th century, John Keswick and Sir William "Tony" Keswick led the firm through the Second World War and the post-war transformation of Hong Kong. Henry Keswick served as chairman from 1972 to 1975 and again, after Simon Keswick's tenure in the 1980s–2010s the family retained control through Jardine Matheson's cross-shareholding structure with Jardine Strategic.

Estates and philanthropy

The family's British base centres on estates in Dumfries and Galloway, including East Park. Members have been involved in philanthropy and the arts, notably Maggie Keswick Jencks, co-founder of the Maggie's Centres for cancer care, and her husband, the architectural critic Charles Jencks.

Notable members

  • William Keswick (1834–1912) — partner of Jardine Matheson, MP for Epsom
  • Sir William Johnstone Keswick (1903–1990) — wartime intelligence officer and Jardine chairman
  • Henry Keswick (born 1938) — chairman of Jardine Matheson
  • Simon Keswick (born 1942) — chairman 1983–2012 (with interruption)
  • Maggie Keswick Jencks (1941–1995) — writer, garden designer, founder of Maggie's Centres
