Claude (language model)

Claude is a family of large language models developed by Anthropic.

Claude is a family of large language models developed by Anthropic. The first model was released in March 2023. Claude is named after Claude Shannon, a pioneer of information theory. Like its competitors, Claude is trained to perform a wide variety of text and code tasks, and is available through a chat interface, an API, and integrations with cloud platforms such as Amazon Bedrock and Google Cloud's Vertex AI.

Training

Anthropic trains Claude using techniques it calls constitutional AI: the model is given a set of principles (a "constitution") and is trained, via reinforcement learning from AI feedback (RLAIF), to critique and revise its own outputs according to those principles, with the goal of making its behaviour helpful and harmless. Anthropic has published research on interpretability and on methods for evaluating model behaviour alongside the model releases.

Models

The original Claude and Claude Instant were followed by Claude 2 in July 2023, which expanded the context window to 100,000 tokens. The Claude 3 family, released in March 2024, comprised three models of increasing capability — Haiku, Sonnet, and Opus — and added multimodal image input. Subsequent releases included Claude 3.5 Sonnet (June 2024), notable for strong performance in coding tasks and for introducing agentic computer use; Claude 3.7 Sonnet (February 2025), a hybrid reasoning model with an extended-thinking mode; and the Claude 4 generation (May 2025), including Claude Opus 4 and Sonnet 4, aimed at advanced coding and agent workflows. Anthropic also offers Claude Code, a command-line agentic coding tool released in 2025.

Usage and reception

Claude has been adopted for software development, document analysis, customer support and research assistance. Commentators have noted its comparatively cautious refusal behaviour and its long context windows. Anthropic operates under a Responsible Scaling Policy that ties model capability evaluations to deployment safeguards, and Claude's releases have featured prominently in debates about AI safety and the pace of frontier model development.
