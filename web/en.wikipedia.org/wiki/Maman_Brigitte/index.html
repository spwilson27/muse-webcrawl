Maman Brigitte

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Manman Brigitte (also spelled Maman Brigitte or Gran Brigitte) is a loa of death in Haitian Vodou and Louisiana Voodoo. She is the wife of Baron Samedi and, with him, rules over the dead and the cemeteries; she is a member of the Guédé family of loa.

Character

Maman Brigitte is one of the few loa depicted as white-skinned, traditionally with red hair and green eyes, an inheritance from her syncretisation with the Celtic goddess Brigid and Saint Brigid of Ireland. She is foul-mouthed, fond of rum steeped with hot peppers, and speaks in a gravelly voice, yet she is also a fierce protector of women and a powerful healer.

Worship

She guards tombstones and the graves of properly buried dead, and the first female grave in a new cemetery is consecrated to her. Her feast day is associated with Fèt Gede, the Vodou festival of the dead held around 1–2 November. Offerings include rum with hot peppers, black coffee, and purple flowers.
