Square foot

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

A square foot is the area of a square with sides one foot long

The square foot (plural square feet; abbreviated sq ft, sf, or ft²) is an imperial and U.S. customary unit of area, used mainly in the United States and partially in Canada, the United Kingdom, and other countries with historical ties to the British Imperial system. It is defined as the area of a square with sides of one foot (12 inches, 1/3 yard, or exactly 0.3048 metres).

Conversions

  • 1 square foot = 144 square inches
  • 1 square foot = 1/9 square yard ≈ 0.1111 square yards
  • 1 square foot = 0.09290304 square metres (exact, since the foot is defined as exactly 0.3048 m)
  • 1 square foot = 929.0304 square centimetres
  • 1 acre = 43,560 square feet

Usage

The square foot is the standard unit for measuring the floor area of homes, offices, and other buildings in the United States, and it is widely used in real-estate listings in Canada and elsewhere. It is also used for materials such as flooring, carpeting, and roofing. In scientific and engineering contexts it has largely been replaced by the square metre.

Symbols

Common symbols include ft², sq ft, sq. ft., sf, and SF. Care is required not to confuse the square foot with the board foot, a unit of lumber volume, or with the cubic foot, a unit of volume.
