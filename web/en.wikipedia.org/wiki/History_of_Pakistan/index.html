History of Pakistan

The history of Pakistan traces one of the world's oldest civilisations — the Indus Valley Civilisation (c. 3300–1300 BCE) — through Vedic, Achaemenid, Greek, Maurya, Gupta, and Islamic eras, including the Delhi Sultanate and Mughal Empire.

Creation

Under British rule, the All-India Muslim League, led by Muhammad Ali Jinnah, demanded a separate Muslim state. Pakistan achieved independence on 14 August 1947 at Partition, comprising West Pakistan and East Bengal.

Modern history

East Pakistan seceded as Bangladesh in 1971 after civil war. Pakistan's history since has alternated between civilian and military rule, with wars with India over Kashmir, a nuclear programme (tests in 1998), and a frontline role in regional geopolitics.
