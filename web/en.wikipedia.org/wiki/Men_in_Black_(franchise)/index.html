Men in Black (franchise)

The Men in Black franchise is an American media franchise centred on a secret organisation that monitors extraterrestrial life on Earth, inspired by the men in black conspiracy lore of UFOlogy and originating in Lowell Cunningham's comic book series The Men in Black (1990–1991). The franchise is best known for its four science fiction comedy films, beginning with Men in Black in 1997.

Films

  • Men in Black (1997) — Agents K (Tommy Lee Jones) and J (Will Smith) stop an alien Bug seeking "the Galaxy". Grossed $589 million.
  • Men in Black II (2002) — J restores K's erased memory to avert a new alien threat; grossed $441 million.
  • Men in Black 3 (2012) — J travels back to 1969 and works with the young K (Josh Brolin); the franchise's highest grosser at $654 million.
  • Men in Black: International (2019) — a spin-off with Chris Hemsworth and Tessa Thompson as Agents H and M; grossed $254 million.

Other media

The franchise includes Men in Black: The Series (1997–2001), an animated television series continuing the adventures of K and J, plus video games, theme park attractions (Men in Black: Alien Attack at Universal Studios Florida, opened 2000), and music — notably Will Smith's hit songs "Men in Black" and "Black Suits Comin' (Nod Ya Head)". Signatures of the franchise include the black suits and sunglasses, the memory-erasing neuralyzer, and the concept of Earth as a neutral zone secretly hosting some 1,500 aliens, many disguised as humans in New York City.
