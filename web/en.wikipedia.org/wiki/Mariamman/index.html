Mariamman

Mariamman, often abbreviated to Amman or Aatha, is a Hindu goddess of weather, mainly venerated in the rural areas of South India, especially Tamil Nadu. She is associated with rain, fertility, and protection from diseases such as smallpox, cholera, and chickenpox.

Worship

Mariamman is regarded as a form of the goddess Parvati or Durga and as a village mother goddess (gramadevata). Her festivals, notably Aadi Thiruvizha in the Tamil month of Aadi, include fire-walking, processions, and offerings of pongal and neem leaves. Temples such as Samayapuram Mariamman Temple near Tiruchirappalli draw millions of devotees. Her worship spread with the Tamil diaspora to Sri Lanka, Malaysia, Singapore, South Africa, Fiji, and the Caribbean.
