Creditor

A creditor is a party — a person, organisation, company or government — that has a claim on the services of a second party. The first party has provided some property or service to the second party under the assumption that the second party will return an equivalent property or service.

Types

A secured creditor has a security interest, such as a mortgage or lien, over the debtor's property and is paid first in bankruptcy. An unsecured creditor has no such claim on specific assets. Creditors may also be classified as personal or real, and preferential or concurrent in insolvency law.

Creditor and debtor

The counterpart is a debtor, who owes the debt. The relationship is governed by contract and by insolvency and bankruptcy law, which determines the order in which creditors are repaid when a debtor cannot pay. See also credit risk.

In accounting

In accounting, creditors are amounts owed by a business, shown as liabilities — trade creditors or accounts payable — on the balance sheet.
