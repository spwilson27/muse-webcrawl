Defence Academy of the United Kingdom

Established: 2002

Location: Shrivenham, Oxfordshire

Parent: Ministry of Defence

The Defence Academy of the United Kingdom provides higher education and professional training for personnel of the British Armed Forces, civil servants in the Ministry of Defence, and students from allied nations. It is headquartered at Shrivenham in Oxfordshire, on the site of the former Royal Military College of Science.

History

The Defence Academy was established in 2002, bringing together several existing institutions of military education under a single organisation to promote joint, rather than single-service, education. Academic support has been provided in partnership with Cranfield University, which delivers much of the postgraduate teaching at Shrivenham.

Component institutions

  • Joint Services Command and Staff College (JSCSC) — delivers command and staff training, including the Advanced Command and Staff Course.
  • Royal College of Defence Studies (RCDS) — based in London, for senior officers and officials studying strategy at the highest level.
  • Defence Academy for Training and Education and specialist centres, including the Defence Centre of Training Support and the Armed Forces Chaplaincy Centre.
  • Defence Technical Undergraduate Scheme and Shrivenham's technology schools, successors to the Royal Military College of Science.

Role

The Academy's courses range from strategic studies and defence management to engineering and languages, serving several thousand students each year.
