List of circulating currencies

There are about 180 currencies recognised as legal tender around the world. This list gives the currency of each country and territory, its ISO 4217 code where one exists, and its fractional unit.

Shared and pegged currencies

Beyond the euro, currency unions include the two CFA franc zones in Africa and the East Caribbean dollar. Many smaller currencies are pegged to the dollar or euro; some countries use another state's currency outright (dollarisation), such as Ecuador and El Salvador using the U.S. dollar. A few states, such as Zimbabwe in various periods, have used multi-currency systems.
