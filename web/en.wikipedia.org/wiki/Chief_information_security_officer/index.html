Chief information security officer

AbbreviationCISO
DomainInformation security, cybersecurity, risk management
Reports toOften the CIO, CTO, or CEO

A chief information security officer (CISO) is the senior-level executive within an organization responsible for establishing and maintaining the enterprise vision, strategy, and program to ensure information assets and technologies are adequately protected.

Responsibilities

The CISO oversees security architecture, incident response, identity and access management, vulnerability management, security awareness training, and compliance with regulations such as GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI DSS. The role emerged in the mid-1990s as organizations began digitizing sensitive data; one of the first CISOs was appointed at Citicorp in 1994.

Role in the organization

Reporting lines vary: many CISOs report to the chief information officer, while others report directly to the chief executive or the board, reflecting the elevation of cyber risk to a board-level concern.
