Cameco Corporation (formerly Canadian Mining and Energy Corporation) is a Canadian uranium mining company based in Saskatoon, Saskatchewan. It is the world's largest publicly traded uranium company and one of the largest global producers of uranium fuel for nuclear power.

Operations

Cameco's principal mines are in the Athabasca Basin of northern Saskatchewan, including Cigar Lake — the world's largest high-grade uranium mine, a joint venture in which Cameco is the majority owner and operator — and McArthur River/Key Lake, among the largest high-grade deposits ever mined. It operates the Blind River refinery and the Port Hope conversion facilities in Ontario, and holds a 49% stake in Westinghouse Electric Company, acquired in partnership with Brookfield in 2023.

History

The company was formed in 1988 by the merger of the federal Crown corporation Eldorado Nuclear Limited and the provincial Saskatchewan Mining Development Corporation, and was progressively privatised through the 1990s. Its name is an acronym of Canadien mining and energy corporation.

In 2007 flooding at Cigar Lake delayed its start-up for years, and the McArthur River operation was suspended from 2018 to 2022 amid weak uranium prices before restarting as prices recovered.
