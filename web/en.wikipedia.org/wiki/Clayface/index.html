Clayface

Clayface is an alias used by several fictional supervillains appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Most versions are enemies of the superhero Batman, possessing clay-like bodies and shapeshifting abilities. The most famous incarnation, Matt Hagen, and the original, Basil Karlo, have recurred across comics, animation, and games; the character was ranked among the notable members of Batman's rogues' gallery.

Incarnations

Basil Karlo, the original Clayface of the Golden Age, was a deranged B-movie actor who committed murders in the persona of a character he had played. Matt Hagen, introduced in 1961, was a treasure hunter transformed by a radioactive protoplasm pool into a mass of living clay, needing periodic re-exposure to retain his powers. Preston Payne (1978), suffering from hyperpituitarism, built an exo-suit and could melt flesh with a touch; Sondra Fuller (1987) was given shapeshifting powers by the terrorist Kobra. In Batman: The Animated Series (1992), a celebrated two-part episode combined Karlo and Hagen into a tragic former actor disfigured and transformed by an experimental cosmetic.

In other media

Clayface has appeared in numerous animated series, the video games of the Batman: Arkham series, and in live action in Gotham; a solo film centered on the character was developed for DC Studios in the 2020s.
