Memphre

Memphre is the nickname given to a legendary lake monster said to inhabit Lake Memphremagog, a long, narrow lake straddling the border between Vermont, United States, and Quebec, Canada. Described as a serpentine creature with a horse-like or dragon-like head, Memphre is part of the local folklore in much the same way as "Champ" of Lake Champlain or the Loch Ness Monster in Scotland.

Sightings

Reports of a large creature in the lake date back to the 19th century, with accounts by settlers and later by swimmers, boaters and fishermen describing humps, a long neck, or a large animal moving through the water. The name "Memphre" was popularised by Quebec folklorist and lake advocate who promoted the legend in the late 20th century; the creature has since become a mascot and tourism emblem for the Memphremagog region, with its likeness appearing on signs and souvenirs around Newport, Vermont, and Magog, Quebec. No scientific evidence for the creature's existence has been produced, and reported sightings are generally attributed to waves, logs, otters, or large fish such as sturgeon.
