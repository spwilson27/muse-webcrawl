IEEE Software

Magazine of the IEEE Computer Society

A cover of IEEE Software

IEEE Software is a bimonthly magazine published by the IEEE Computer Society for software practitioners and researchers. First published in 1984, it focuses on practical software engineering: design, development, management, requirements, testing, tools and processes, with articles intended to be accessible to working developers as well as academics.

Content

Each issue combines peer-reviewed feature articles around themes — such as software architecture, agile methods, DevOps, or AI engineering — with regular columns and departments, including long-running columns by well-known practitioners. The magazine's mission statement distinguishes it from research journals: it aims to transfer proven practice and emerging ideas into the hands of developers and managers.

Publication details

IEEE Software is published in print and online via the IEEE Xplore digital library, and members of the IEEE Computer Society receive it as part of their membership.
