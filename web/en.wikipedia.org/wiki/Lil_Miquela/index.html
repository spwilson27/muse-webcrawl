Lil Miquela

Created by: Brud, Inc. (Trevor McFedries and Sara DeCou)
First appearance: April 2016 (Instagram)
Character: Miquela Sousa, a fictional 19-year-old Brazilian-American model and singer

Miquela Sousa, or Lil Miquela, is a fictional American character, singer and social media personality. She is a computer-generated imagery (CGI) "virtual influencer" created by Trevor McFedries and Sara DeCou of the Los Angeles startup Brud, and she first appeared on Instagram in April 2016. Miquela is presented as a 19-year-old Brazilian-American model, singer, and social media influencer living in Los Angeles.

History

Miquela's Instagram account accumulated millions of followers with photographs that mixed selfies and fashion imagery, initially leaving audiences unsure whether she was a real person. In April 2018 her account was temporarily "hacked" by Bermuda, another Brud-created character, in a storyline that publicly revealed her nature as a fictional creation. She has fronted campaigns for fashion brands including Prada, Calvin Klein, and Samsung, and appeared in a controversial 2019 Calvin Klein video alongside model Bella Hadid.

Music

Miquela debuted as a singer with the single "Not Mine" in 2017 and subsequently released songs including "Overnight", "Right Back", "Sleeping In", and "Hard Feelings". In 2018 Time named her one of the 25 most influential people on the internet.
