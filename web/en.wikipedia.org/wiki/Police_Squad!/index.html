Police Squad!
Genre: Comedy, parody
Created by: David Zucker, Jim Abrahams, Jerry Zucker
Starring: Leslie Nielsen
Network: ABC
Original run: 1982 (6 episodes)

Police Squad! is an American television comedy series created by David Zucker, Jim Abrahams, and Jerry Zucker, first broadcast in 1982. It parodies police procedurals, starring Leslie Nielsen as the deadpan detective Frank Drebin and Alan North as his boss Ed Hocken. The show packed episodes with sight gags, puns, and non sequiturs in the style of the creators' film Airplane!

Running gags

Each episode opened by announcing a "special guest star" who was immediately killed during the credits, and closed with a freeze-frame gag in which the actors froze while the world around them continued to move. Episode titles were announced in voice-over that deliberately mismatched the on-screen titles.

Cancellation and legacy

ABC cancelled the show after six episodes, with executives famously reasoning that viewers had to actually watch it to appreciate the jokes. It gained a cult following and directly spawned the successful Naked Gun film series (1988–1994), which reunited Nielsen as Drebin.
