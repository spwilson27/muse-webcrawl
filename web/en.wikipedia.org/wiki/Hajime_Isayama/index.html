Hajime Isayama

Hajime Isayama (諫山 創, Isayama Hajime, born August 29, 1986) is a Japanese manga artist. His first series, Attack on Titan (2009–2021), became one of the best-selling manga series of all time, with more than 140 million copies in circulation worldwide, and won awards including the Kodansha Manga Award and the Harvey Award.

Biography

Isayama was born in Ōyama, Ōita Prefecture (now part of Hita). He began drawing manga in high school and, after graduating, attended a manga design programme in Fukuoka before moving to Tokyo. A one-shot version of Attack on Titan won a prize in Kodansha's Magazine Grand Prix in 2006; he turned down an offer to serialise it at Weekly Shōnen Jump and instead brought it to Kodansha, where the series launched in Bessatsu Shōnen Magazine in 2009. The series concluded in April 2021 after 139 chapters.

Works

Following the end of Attack on Titan, Isayama contributed a one-shot, Soyogi Fractal (2024), written in collaboration with voice actor Yūki Kaji. He has cited influences including the visual novel Muv-Luv Alternative and films such as Jurassic Park. Isayama has also been involved with a sentō (public bathhouse) business in his home town of Hita.
