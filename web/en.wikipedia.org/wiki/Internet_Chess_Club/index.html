Internet Chess Club

The Internet Chess Club (ICC) is a commercial Internet chess server devoted to the play and discussion of chess. It was one of the first and longest-running internet chess servers.

History

The ICC grew out of the free Internet Chess Server (ICS) developed in the early 1990s. When the ICS organisers began charging in 1995, the service was relaunched commercially as the Internet Chess Club, while a volunteer fork continued as the Free Internet Chess Server (FICS).

Features

The server hosts games at many time controls, including bullet and blitz, and offers automated tournaments, lectures, and broadcasts of major events. Many titled players, including grandmasters, have played on the ICC, often under pseudonyms.
