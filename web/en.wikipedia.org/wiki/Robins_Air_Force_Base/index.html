Robins Air Force Base (IATA: WRB, ICAO: KWRB) is a major United States Air Force installation located in Houston County, Georgia, adjacent to the city of Warner Robins. It is the home of the Warner Robins Air Logistics Complex, one of three such depots in the Air Force, and is the largest single-site industrial complex in the state of Georgia.

Aerial view of Robins AFB, Georgia.

History

The base was established in 1941, when the Army Air Corps selected a site near the small community of Wellston, Georgia, for an aviation depot. It was named in honour of Brigadier General Augustine Warner Robins, often called the "father of logistics" in the Air Force. The surrounding town, incorporated in 1943, took the name Warner Robins in his honour.

During the Second World War the depot maintained and repaired aircraft for the war effort, and after the war it became a centre for the storage and maintenance of the Air Force's bomber and transport fleets.

Role and units

Today the Warner Robins Air Logistics Complex performs programmed depot maintenance on aircraft including the C-130 Hercules, C-5 Galaxy, C-17 Globemaster III and F-16 Fighting Falcon. The base hosts Headquarters Air Force Reserve Command, the 78th Air Base Wing, the 116th Air Control Wing (flying the E-8C JSTARS) and the 461st Air Control Wing. The Museum of Aviation, adjacent to the base, is one of the largest museums of the United States Air Force.
