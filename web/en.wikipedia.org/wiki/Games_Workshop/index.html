Games Workshop Group PLC is a British manufacturer of miniature wargames, based in Nottingham, England. Its best-known products are Warhammer and Warhammer 40,000. Founded in 1975 by John Peake, Ian Livingstone and Steve Jackson, the company began by manufacturing wooden boards for games such as backgammon and became an early importer of Dungeons & Dragons into the United Kingdom.


A Warhammer store

History

The company launched the magazine White Dwarf in 1977 and opened its first retail stores. In 1983 it published the first edition of Warhammer Fantasy Battle, followed by Warhammer 40,000 in 1987. Through its Citadel Miniatures subsidiary it shifted to producing its own plastic and metal miniatures. Games Workshop floated on the London Stock Exchange in 1994.

Products and licensing

Besides its core tabletop games, the company licenses its Warhammer properties for video games, novels (through the Black Library imprint), and screen adaptations, and sells through hundreds of its own Warhammer-branded stores worldwide as well as independent retailers.
