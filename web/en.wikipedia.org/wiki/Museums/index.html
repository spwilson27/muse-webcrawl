Museum

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

[Image: The grand neoclassical facade of the British Museum in London]
The grand neoclassical facade of the British Museum in London

A museum is an institution that cares for a collection of artefacts and other objects of artistic, cultural, historical or scientific importance, and exhibits them to the public. The modern definition adopted by the International Council of Museums (2022) describes museums as not-for-profit, permanent institutions in the service of society that research, collect, conserve, interpret and exhibit tangible and intangible heritage. The world's most visited museums include the Louvre, the National Museum of China and the Vatican Museums.

History

The word derives from the Greek Mouseion, a shrine to the Muses — Alexandria's Mouseion with its great library was an early research institute. Renaissance cabinets of curiosities and princely collections preceded the public museum: the Ashmolean Museum at Oxford (1683) and the British Museum (founded 1753, opened 1759) were among the first national public museums, and the Louvre's opening as a museum in 1793 embodied the Enlightenment and revolutionary ideal of heritage held for all citizens.

Types and functions

Museums span art, history, natural history, science, war, transport, childhood and almost anything collectable; ecomuseums and community museums invert the model, keeping heritage in place. Their core work — acquisition, conservation, research, exhibition and education — is governed by professional ethics (ICOM's Code) covering provenance, deaccessioning and the treatment of human remains.

Controversies

Museums face restitution claims for colonial-era and Nazi-looted objects (Benin Bronzes, the Parthenon Sculptures), debates over repatriation of Indigenous remains, sponsorship ethics (fossil fuels, opioid fortunes), and audience relevance. Visitor numbers in strong years number in the tens of millions for the largest institutions; the museum sector globally counts somewhere between 55,000 and 100,000 institutions depending on definition.
