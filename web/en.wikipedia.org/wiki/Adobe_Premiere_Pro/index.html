Adobe Premiere Pro

DeveloperAdobe Inc.
Initial releaseDecember 1991 (as Premiere)
TypeVideo editing software
PlatformWindows, macOS

Adobe Premiere Pro is a timeline-based, nonlinear video editing application developed by Adobe and published as part of Adobe Creative Cloud. It is widely used for editing feature films, television, commercials, and online video, and supports high-resolution video editing, multi-camera workflows, effects, and extensive third-party plug-ins.

History

The software was originally launched in 1991 as Adobe Premiere, one of the first computer-based nonlinear editing systems. It was completely rewritten and re-released as Premiere Pro in 2003. Later versions added native support for modern camera formats, GPU-accelerated rendering via the Mercury Playback Engine, and tight integration with other Adobe applications such as After Effects (via Dynamic Link), Audition, and Photoshop.

Features

Premiere Pro offers multi-track timelines, trimming tools, colour correction and grading (including Lumetri Color), audio editing, captioning, and motion graphics templates. Notable films edited with Premiere Pro include Deadpool (2016), Gone Girl (2014), and Everything Everywhere All at Once (2022). The program is available by subscription on Windows and macOS.
