Skyfall is a 2012 spy film and the twenty-third in the James Bond series produced by Eon Productions. Directed by Sam Mendes and starring Daniel Craig in his third outing as Bond, it was released during the 50th anniversary of the franchise. In the film, Bond investigates attacks on MI6 orchestrated by Raoul Silva (Javier Bardem), a former MI6 agent seeking revenge against M (Judi Dench).

Plot

After a mission in Istanbul goes wrong and Bond is presumed dead, a cyberattack on MI6 forces him back into service. The trail leads to Silva, a cyberterrorist and former agent betrayed by M in Hong Kong. Bond lures Silva to his ancestral Scottish home, Skyfall Lodge, where he, M and gamekeeper Kincade mount a last stand; M is fatally wounded, and Bond kills Silva. The film ends with Gareth Mallory (Ralph Fiennes) installed as the new M.

Reception

Skyfall received critical acclaim, particularly for Mendes's direction, Roger Deakins's cinematography and Bardem's performance. It grossed over $1.1 billion worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing Bond film and one of the highest-grossing films of all time at its release. Adele's title song won the Academy Award for Best Original Song.
