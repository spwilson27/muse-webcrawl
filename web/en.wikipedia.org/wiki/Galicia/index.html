Galicia is an autonomous community of Spain in the northwest of the Iberian Peninsula, bordered by Portugal to the south and by the Atlantic Ocean and the Bay of Biscay. Its capital is Santiago de Compostela, the destination of the Camino de Santiago pilgrimage.

Geography

Galicia's coast is deeply indented by the rías, flooded river valleys — the Rías Baixas in the south and Rías Altas in the north — which support important fishing and shellfish industries. The green, rainy interior is one of the wettest parts of Spain.

Language and culture

Galician, a Romance language closely related to Portuguese, is co-official with Spanish and widely spoken. Galicia has a distinct cultural identity, with Celtic associations, bagpipe (gaita) music, and a large diaspora in Latin America. Major cities include Vigo, A Coruña, Ourense, Lugo, and Pontevedra.
