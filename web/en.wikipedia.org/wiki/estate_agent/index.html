Estate agent

An estate agent is a person or business that arranges the selling, renting, or management of properties and other buildings. An agent that specialises in renting is often called a letting or management agent. The term is chiefly used in the United Kingdom and Commonwealth countries; the equivalent role in the United States is generally called a real estate agent or broker.

Services

Estate agents market properties, arrange viewings, negotiate between buyers and sellers or landlords and tenants, and may provide valuations. They typically charge a percentage of the sale price or rent as commission.

Regulation

In the UK, estate agents are regulated under the Estate Agents Act 1979 and must belong to an approved redress scheme; in the United States, agents must be licensed by the state in which they operate.

History

The profession grew with the expansion of the housing market in the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, and has been reshaped in recent decades by online property portals and hybrid or online-only agencies.
