Decanter

A decanter is a vessel used to hold liquids, typically wine, after they are decanted from their original bottle. Decanting separates wine from sediment and exposes it to oxygen, which can soften tannins and open up aromas.

Decanters are usually made of glass or crystal, with a wide base maximising the wine's surface area. Ship's decanters have an especially broad base for stability at sea. Decanters are also used for spirits such as whisky and cognac, mainly for presentation.
