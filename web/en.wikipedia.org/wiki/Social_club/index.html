Social club

A social club is a group of people and the place where they meet, generally formed around a common interest, occupation, or activity. Social clubs range from informal hobby groups to exclusive private clubs with dedicated clubhouses.

Types

Examples include gentlemen's clubs, which arose in Britain from the 17th century onward; working men's clubs; country clubs centred on golf and recreation; ethnic and immigrant mutual-aid societies; and university societies. Many clubs historically restricted membership by class, gender, or ethnicity, a practice that has declined under legal and social pressure.

Activities

Clubs typically provide dining, drinking, socialising, and recreational facilities for members, and often host events, lectures, and charitable work. Membership usually requires nomination by existing members and payment of dues.
