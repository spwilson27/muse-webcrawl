Štrbské Pleso

Štrbské Pleso is a picturesque mountain lake of glacial origin in the High Tatras of Slovakia, and also the name of the surrounding resort settlement, part of the village of Štrba. At 1,346 metres above sea level, it is the second-largest glacial lake on the Slovak side of the Tatras.

The lake and resort

The lake covers about 19.8 hectares and reaches a depth of some 20 metres, remaining frozen roughly 155 days a year. A tourist settlement grew around it from the 1870s, connected by the Štrbská Pleso rack railway to Štrba below and by the Tatra Electric Railway along the mountain foothills.

Sport

Štrbské Pleso is Slovakia's premier ski centre, hosting the 1970 FIS Nordic World Ski Championships and regular World Cup cross-country events. Its ski jumps, built for 1970, still dominate the southern shore. The resort is a year-round base for hiking in the High Tatras.
