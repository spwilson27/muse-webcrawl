The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 2

The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 2 is a 2015 American dystopian action film directed by Francis Lawrence, the fourth and final instalment of The Hunger Games film series, concluding the adaptation of Suzanne Collins's Mockingjay.

DirectorFrancis Lawrence
Based onMockingjay by Suzanne Collins
StarringJennifer Lawrence, Josh Hutcherson, Liam Hemsworth, Julianne Moore
Release date20 November 2015

Plot

Katniss joins a special unit infiltrating the booby-trapped Capitol to assassinate President Snow, but discovers the rebellion's leader, President Coin, may be no better. The assault costs many lives, and Katniss must decide what kind of peace Panem will have.

Reception

The film grossed over $650 million worldwide and was generally praised as a satisfying, sombre conclusion to the series, with Jennifer Lawrence's performance singled out.
