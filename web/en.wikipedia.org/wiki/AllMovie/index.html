AllMovie (previously All Movie Guide) is an online database of information about films, television programmes and actors, including reviews, synopses, cast and crew credits, and biographies. It is operated by the All Media Network, alongside the music database AllMusic and the former video game database AllGame.

History

The All Movie Guide was founded by Michael Erlewine, who had earlier founded the All Music Guide, and was launched in 1994, initially as a licensed database for retailers and video stores before becoming a consumer website. Its editorial reviews, rated on a five-star scale, were syndicated widely.

In 2007 the parent company, All Media Guide, was acquired by Macrovision (later Rovi Corporation). In 2011 the AllMovie site was relaunched, and in 2013 Rovi sold the consumer-facing properties to the newly formed All Media Network LLC. In 2015 the company was acquired by Blinkx (later RhythmOne). The site continues to publish new reviews and database entries, and its historical catalogue covers several hundred thousand films and programmes.

Content

Each AllMovie entry typically includes a plot synopsis, an editorial review and star rating, production credits, release information, genre and theme classifications ("moods" and "keywords"), and links between related works and people, making the database a standard reference source for film information.
