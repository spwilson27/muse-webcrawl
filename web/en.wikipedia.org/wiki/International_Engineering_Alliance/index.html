International Engineering Alliance

AbbreviationIEA
TypeInternational alliance of engineering accreditation and licensing bodies
PurposeMutual recognition of engineering qualifications and professional competence standards

The International Engineering Alliance (IEA) is an international organization that brings together engineering bodies responsible for accrediting engineering education programmes and registering or licensing professional engineers. Through a set of multilateral agreements, the IEA promotes the mutual recognition of accredited qualifications and agreed benchmarks of graduate attributes and professional competence, supporting the international mobility of engineers.

Accords and agreements

The IEA administers several accords covering different levels of engineering education:

  • The Washington Accord (1989), recognizing substantial equivalence of accredited engineering degree programmes.
  • The Sydney Accord (2001), covering engineering technologist education.
  • The Dublin Accord (2002), covering engineering technician education.
  • Agreements on professional competence, including the International Professional Engineers Agreement (IPEA) and the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Engineer Agreement, which maintain international registers of professional engineers.

Benchmarks

The IEA publishes Graduate Attributes and Professional Competencies reference documents that define internationally recognized benchmarks for the outcomes of accredited programmes and the competence expected of practising engineers, technologists and technicians.
