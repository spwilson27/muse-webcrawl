Payot is a Swiss bookselling and publishing name originating in Lausanne, in French-speaking Switzerland (Romandy). The Payot bookshops are the best-known booksellers in Romandy, while Éditions Payot, based in Paris, is known for publishing in the humanities and social sciences.

History

The business traces its origins to 1875, when the Payot family took over a Lausanne bookshop. Under Fritz Payot and his brothers, the firm grew into a chain of bookstores across French-speaking Switzerland and also developed a publishing house. In the 20th century the Paris publishing arm came under separate ownership, while the Swiss bookshop network remained based in Lausanne.

Éditions Payot

The Paris-based publishing house Éditions Payot is noted for scholarly and trade non-fiction. It published French editions of works by Sigmund Freud and other psychoanalysts, as well as historians, anthropologists, and geographers. Its paperback imprint, the Petite Bibliothèque Payot, founded in the early 1960s, made classic texts in the human sciences widely available. In the 1990s the publisher merged with Rivages to form Payot & Rivages.

Payot Libraire

The Swiss bookshop business, Payot Libraire, operates stores in cities including Lausanne, Geneva, Neuchâtel, Fribourg, and Montreux. After a long association with the French Lagardère group, the chain returned to Swiss ownership in the 2010s and remains a leading cultural retailer in Romandy, selling books, stationery, and media.
