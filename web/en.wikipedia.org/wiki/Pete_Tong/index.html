Peter Michael Tong (born 30 July 1960) is an English disc jockey and radio presenter who has worked for BBC Radio 1 since 1991. A central figure in the popularisation of house and electronic dance music in Britain, he is known for the shows The Essential Selection and the Essential Mix, and his name entered Cockney rhyming slang — "it's all gone Pete Tong" (wrong) — popularised by the 2004 film It's All Gone Pete Tong.

Career

Tong began DJing in his teens and worked in music journalism and A&R, founding the FFRR label at London Records, which released key house and rave records. On Radio 1 he championed dance music from its acid-house era onward; the Essential Mix, launched in 1993, became an institution featuring mixes by leading DJs. A long-running fixture of the Ibiza club scene, he has held residencies at venues including Pacha.

Later work

In the 2010s Tong — by then based in Los Angeles — co-founded the International Music Summit in Ibiza and presented the orchestral "Ibiza Classics" concerts with the Heritage Orchestra, reinterpreting dance anthems with classical instrumentation. He was appointed MBE in 2014 for services to broadcasting and music.
