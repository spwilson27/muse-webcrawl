Teen pop

Teen pop is a subgenre of pop music created, marketed and performed primarily for teenagers and pre-teens. It features catchy, uptempo songs with simple melodies and lyrics centred on love, friendship and growing up, typically sung by young performers with carefully managed images.

History

Idols marketed to teenagers date to the 1950s, but teen pop boomed in the late 1990s with acts such as the Spice Girls, Backstreet Boys, *NSYNC, Britney Spears and Christina Aguilera, whose polished dance-pop sold tens of millions of records. The genre revived in the 2000s and 2010s through Disney Channel stars and boy bands such as One Direction.

Characteristics

Production emphasises hooks, harmonies and choreography, while marketing exploits merchandising and fan devotion. Critics sometimes dismiss teen pop as manufactured, though many of its stars have matured into respected adult artists.
