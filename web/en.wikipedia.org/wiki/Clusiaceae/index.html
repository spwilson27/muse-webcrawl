Clusiaceae

The Clusiaceae or Guttiferae are a family of plants including 13 genera and about 750 species. Many are trees and shrubs with milky white or yellow latex, opposite leaves, and characteristic flowers. The family is widespread in the tropics.

Genera and uses

Well-known members include Clusia, widely planted as an ornamental and hedge plant; Garcinia, which includes the mangosteen (Garcinia mangostana), prized as a fruit; and Mammea. Some species yield timber, resins (such as gamboge from Garcinia), and edible fruits; the oil from Calophyllum seeds has traditional and industrial uses.

Classification

In the APG IV system the family is placed in the order Malpighiales. The related family Hypericaceae (St John's wort family) was formerly included within the Clusiaceae as a subfamily.
