Transcaucasian Sejm

The Transcaucasian Sejm was a representative and legislative body of the Transcaucasus, convened in Tiflis (Tbilisi) in early 1918 after the dissolution of the Russian Constituent Assembly following the October Revolution. It was composed of Transcaucasian deputies who had been elected to the Constituent Assembly.

History

The Sejm first met in February 1918, succeeding the Transcaucasian Commissariat as the region's governing authority. Under pressure from the advancing Ottoman armies and the Treaty of Brest-Litovsk, the Sejm proclaimed the Transcaucasian Democratic Federative Republic on 22 April 1918.

The federation quickly fractured over differing national aims and the question of peace with the Ottoman Empire. Georgia declared independence on 26 May 1918, followed by Armenia and Azerbaijan, and the Sejm dissolved itself the same day, ending the brief experiment in Transcaucasian unity.
