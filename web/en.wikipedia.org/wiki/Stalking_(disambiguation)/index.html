Stalking (disambiguation)

Stalking may refer to several different topics; this page lists the principal meanings.

Meanings

  • Stalking — the criminal or unwanted behaviour of repeatedly following, watching or harassing another person, an offence in many jurisdictions, which may include cyberstalking conducted online.
  • Stalking (hunting) — the pursuit of game animals by stealth on foot, as in deer stalking in the Scottish Highlands; see Deer stalking and Hunting.
  • Stalking (1979 film) — see Stalker, the Soviet science-fiction film directed by Andrei Tarkovsky, based on the novel Roadside Picnic.
  • Courtship stalking — in animal behaviour, the stealthy approach behaviour of predators toward prey, and analogous display behaviours in some species.
  • Stalking (botany) — having or producing a stalk, the stem that supports a leaf, flower or fruit; see Plant stem.
  • Stalking horse — a figurehead or decoy used to test reactions or conceal the identity of the true actor, and, in hunting, a horse (real or artificial) behind which a hunter approaches game; see Stalking horse.
