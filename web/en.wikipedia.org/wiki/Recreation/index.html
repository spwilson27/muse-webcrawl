Recreation

Recreation is an activity of leisure, leisure being discretionary time. The need to do something for recreation is an essential element of human biology and psychology. Recreational activities are often done for enjoyment, amusement, or pleasure and are considered to be "fun".

Forms

Recreation spans outdoor pursuits such as hiking, fishing, and sport; indoor pastimes including games, reading, and crafts; and organized activities in parks, community centres, and recreation programs. Public provision of recreation became a municipal responsibility in many countries during the 19th and 20th centuries.

Health effects

Regular recreational activity is associated with physical fitness, stress reduction, and social connection. Therapeutic recreation uses structured activities to support rehabilitation and well-being.
