The Federal Parliament (Dutch: Federaal Parlement; French: Parlement fédéral; German: Föderales Parlament) is the bicameral legislature of Belgium. It consists of the Chamber of Representatives, the primary legislative body with 150 members elected by proportional representation, and the Senate, which since the sixth state reform of 2014 has 60 members, most appointed by the community and regional parliaments.

Functions

The Federal Parliament exercises federal legislative power together with the King, votes the federal budget, and holds the federal government to account. The Chamber alone grants and withdraws confidence in the government and has the final word on most legislation; the Senate acts mainly as a chamber of reflection and represents the federated entities. Constitutional revision requires declarations and special majorities in both houses.

Seat

Both houses meet in the Palace of the Nation in Brussels, opposite the Royal Palace across Brussels Park.
