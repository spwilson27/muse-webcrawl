Hale Koa Hotel

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Image placeholder: Hale Koa Hotel

The Hale Koa Hotel is a United States Armed Forces recreation centre and hotel located on Waikīkī Beach in Honolulu, Hawaii. Its name means "House of the Warrior" in Hawaiian. Opened in 1975, the hotel is operated by the Department of Defense as part of the Armed Forces Recreation Centers (AFRC) system and is reserved for active-duty and retired military personnel, their families, Department of Defense civilians and other authorised patrons, who stay at rates based on rank and pay grade.

Facilities and setting

The hotel sits on roughly 72 acres of landscaped grounds at Fort DeRussy, one of the last remaining beachfront military reservations in Waikīkī. It has two towers with over 800 rooms, several restaurants and bars, swimming pools, a military exchange store and extensive gardens. Popular events include a weekly luau held on the grounds.

History

The Fort DeRussy site has a long military history, and the Hale Koa was built to provide affordable rest and recreation for service members. A second tower, the Maile Tower, was added in the 1990s. Like other AFRC properties — including Shades of Green in Florida, the Dragon Hill Lodge in Seoul and Edelweiss Lodge and Resort in Germany — the Hale Koa operates without taxpayer funding, sustained by its own revenue.
