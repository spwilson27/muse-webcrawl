Shenyang (沈阳) is the capital and largest city of Liaoning province in northeastern China, and the largest city in Northeast China by urban population. It is a sub-provincial city and a major industrial, commercial, and transport centre of the region. Historically known in English as Mukden (its Manchu name), Shenyang was the early capital of the Qing dynasty before its conquest of Ming China.

History

The area has been settled for thousands of years. In 1625 the Manchu leader Nurhaci moved his capital to Shenyang, and his son Huang Taiji built the Mukden Palace there and proclaimed the Qing dynasty. After the Qing moved their capital to Beijing in 1644, Shenyang remained a secondary capital. The Qing imperial tombs at Shenyang — the Fuling Tomb and the Zhaoling Tomb — together with the Mukden Palace are UNESCO World Heritage Sites as part of the Imperial Palaces and Imperial Tombs listings.

In the early 20th century Shenyang was a focal point of Russo-Japanese rivalry; the Battle of Mukden in 1905 was one of the largest land battles of the Russo-Japanese War. On 18 September 1931, the Mukden Incident — a staged explosion on the Japanese-owned railway near the city — provided the pretext for Japan's invasion of Manchuria. Shenyang was developed as a heavy-industry base after 1949, and during the reform era it became a centre of the "rust belt" revitalisation efforts in the northeast.

Geography and climate

Shenyang lies on the Hun River in the central part of Liaoning, on the Liao River plain. It has a humid continental climate influenced by the Siberian monsoon, with cold, dry winters (January averages around −11 °C) and warm, humid summers (July averages around 25 °C).

Economy and transport

The city's economy is based on heavy industry — including machinery, aerospace (Shenyang Aircraft Corporation), automotive manufacturing, and equipment — alongside a growing services sector. Shenyang Taoxian International Airport serves the city, and the Shenyang Metro, opened in 2010, was the first metro system in Northeast China.
