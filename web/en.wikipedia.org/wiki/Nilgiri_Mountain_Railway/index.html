Nilgiri Mountain Railway

The Nilgiri Mountain Railway (NMR) is a 1,000 mm (3 ft 3⅜ in) metre-gauge railway in Tamil Nadu, India, built by the British in 1908. It runs 46 km from Mettupalayam to Udhagamandalam (Ooty) in the Nilgiri Hills and operates India's only rack railway section, climbing from 326 metres to 2,203 metres in elevation.

Nilgiri Mountain Railway

Location: Tamil Nadu, India

Termini: Mettupalayam – Udhagamandalam (Ooty)

Length: 46 km

Gauge: 1,000 mm metre gauge with Abt rack system

Status: UNESCO World Heritage Site (since 2005)

History

The line was completed in stages: the rack section from Mettupalayam to Coonoor opened in 1899, and the extension to Ooty followed in 1908. The steep gradient of up to 1 in 12.5 on the first section requires the Abt rack and pinion system, and this section is worked by Swiss-built X class steam locomotives.

Route and operations

From Coonoor to Ooty the gradient eases and diesel locomotives haul the trains. The route traverses 250 bridges, 16 tunnels and dramatic forested gorges. In 2005 UNESCO added the railway to the Mountain Railways of India World Heritage listing. Steam-hauled passenger services on the rack section remain a major tourist attraction.
