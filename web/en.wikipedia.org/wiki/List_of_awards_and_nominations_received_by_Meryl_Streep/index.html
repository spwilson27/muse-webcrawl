List of awards and nominations received by Meryl Streep

Meryl Streep is an American actress who has received a record 21 Academy Award nominations, winning three times, along with a record 29 Golden Globe nominations (winning eight competitive awards plus the Cecil B. DeMille Award), three Emmy Awards, two BAFTA Awards, and a Tony nomination. She received the AFI Life Achievement Award in 2004, a Kennedy Center Honor in 2011, and the Presidential Medal of Freedom in 2014.
