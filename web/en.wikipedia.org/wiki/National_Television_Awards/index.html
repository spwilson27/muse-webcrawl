The National Television Awards (NTAs) are a British television awards ceremony, broadcast by ITV, at which the winners are decided entirely by votes from the general public. First held in 1995, the ceremony is one of the most prominent in British television.

Format

Viewers vote in two stages — a longlist and then a shortlist — across categories including serial drama, drama, entertainment, factual, talent show and presenter. The results are announced live at a ceremony held in recent years at The O2 Arena in London.

Hosts

The awards were hosted for their first 14 years by Sir Trevor McDonald, followed by Dermot O'Leary from 2010 to 2019, David Walliams in 2020, and Joel Dommett from 2021 onwards.

Notable records

Presenting duo Ant & Dec won the TV Presenter award for more than twenty consecutive years. Long-running soaps such as Coronation Street and EastEnders have repeatedly contested the serial drama categories, while dramas including Doctor Who, Peaky Blinders and Happy Valley have taken top honours.
