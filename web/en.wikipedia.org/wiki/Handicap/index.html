Handicap

Handicap may refer to several related concepts centred on disadvantage or levelling a competition:

Sport and games

In sport and gaming, a handicap is a system that assigns advantages or disadvantages to competitors of differing ability to equalise the chance of winning — for example the golf handicap, the weight carried in a handicap horse race, or the Go handicap of extra stones given to a weaker player. Handicapping also means predicting race outcomes.

Disability

Historically, "handicap" was used as a term for disability — a usage now widely considered dated or offensive in English, replaced by "disability" or "disabled person". In the World Health Organization's earlier ICIDH framework, handicap denoted the social disadvantage resulting from an impairment or disability.

Etymology

The word comes from the 17th-century trading game "hand in cap", extended first to horse racing (a "handicap race" in which an official assigned weights) and later to any imposed disadvantage.
