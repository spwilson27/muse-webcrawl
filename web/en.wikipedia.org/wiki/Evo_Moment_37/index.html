Evo Moment 37

Evo Moment #37, or the Daigo Parry, refers to a famous incident in competitive gaming that occurred during the Evolution Championship Series (Evo) 2004 tournament in Pomona, California, United States, on 1 August 2004. During a Street Fighter III: 3rd Strike semi-final match, Japanese player Daigo Umehara, playing as Ken, parried all 15 hits of American player Justin Wong's Chun-Li super art — the last of them in mid-air — and counterattacked to win a round he had been on the verge of losing.

Background

Umehara's Ken was down to a pixel of health, meaning any hit, even blocked chip damage, would end the round. When Wong activated Chun-Li's Houyoku-sen super art, Umehara's only survival option was to parry every hit precisely. His successful full parry, capped by an aerial parry and a jump-in combo ending in Ken's Shoryuken, brought the crowd to its feet.

Legacy

The moment is widely regarded as the most iconic in competitive gaming history and is credited with revitalizing interest in the fighting game community. The clip has been watched millions of times; the two players re-enacted the moment on stage at Evo's tenth anniversary. Guinness World Records recognized it as a defining moment in esports.
