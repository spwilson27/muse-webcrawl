Director: Asghar Farhadi
Release: 2011
Awards: Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film

A Separation (Persian: Jodâyi-e Nâder az Simin, "The Separation of Nader from Simin") is a 2011 Iranian drama film written and directed by Asghar Farhadi, starring Leila Hatami and Peyman Moaadi.

Plot

Simin wishes to emigrate; Nader stays to care for his father with Alzheimer's. Their separation leads Nader to hire Razieh, a devout caregiver, whose miscarriage after an altercation triggers accusations, legal conflict and moral ambiguity involving class, religion and truth.

Reception

It won the Golden Bear at Berlin and became the first Iranian film to win the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film, and was widely ranked among the greatest films of the 21st century.
