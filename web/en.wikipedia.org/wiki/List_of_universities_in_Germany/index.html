List of universities in Germany

Germany has over 400 institutions of higher education, including around 110 universities and an extensive sector of universities of applied sciences (Fachhochschulen). Most are public and charge little or no tuition. The oldest is Heidelberg University (1386). This list names the principal research universities.

Notes

Eleven institutions have held "University of Excellence" status under the German Excellence Strategy, including LMU and TU Munich, Heidelberg, RWTH Aachen, Bonn, Tübingen, Hamburg, TU Dresden, Konstanz and the Berlin University Alliance. Germany's largest university by enrolment is the distance-learning FernUniversität in Hagen.
