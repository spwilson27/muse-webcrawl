Thomas Cook Airlines

[Hero image placeholder: a Thomas Cook Airlines Airbus A321 in the sunny heart livery.]

Thomas Cook Airlines Limited was a British charter and scheduled leisure airline headquartered in Manchester, England. It was founded in 2007 by the merger of Thomas Cook Airlines UK and MyTravel Airways, and served leisure destinations worldwide from bases across the United Kingdom.

History

The airline was the UK carrier of the Thomas Cook Group and operated a fleet of Airbus A321, A330 and Boeing 757/767 aircraft to holiday destinations in Europe, the Caribbean, the United States and elsewhere. On 23 September 2019, the airline ceased operations when the Thomas Cook Group entered compulsory liquidation. The collapse stranded around 150,000 British travellers abroad and prompted the UK Civil Aviation Authority's Operation Matterhorn, the largest peacetime repatriation in British history.

Aftermath

The airline's takeoff and landing slots and parts of its operation were subsequently acquired by other carriers, and the Thomas Cook brand was later relaunched as an online travel agency under new ownership (Fosun International), without the airline.
