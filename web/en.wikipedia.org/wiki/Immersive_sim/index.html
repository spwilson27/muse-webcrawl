Immersive sim

An immersive sim (immersive simulation) is a video game genre emphasising player choice and emergent gameplay within a simulated, highly interactive world. Game systems — physics, artificial intelligence, stealth, objects with consistent properties — interact so that players can solve problems in multiple unscripted ways.

History

The genre traces to Ultima Underworld (1992) and the work of Looking Glass Studios, including Thief (1998) and System Shock (1994). Warren Spector's Deus Ex (2000) is a landmark example. Later titles include the Dishonored series, Prey (2017), Hitman and The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild, which borrow parts of the design philosophy.

Design principles

Designers associated with the genre describe goals such as consistent simulation rules, player agency, systems that react to one another, and levels designed as open "sandboxes" rather than linear scripts.
