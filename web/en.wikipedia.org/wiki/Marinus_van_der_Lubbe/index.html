Marinus van der Lubbe

Marinus van der Lubbe (13 January 1909 – 10 January 1934) was a Dutch council communist who was convicted and executed for setting fire to the German Reichstag building on 27 February 1933, an event used by the Nazis to suspend civil liberties.

Born13 Jan 1909, Leiden, Netherlands
Died10 Jan 1934, Leipzig, Germany
Known forReichstag fire trial

Reichstag fire and trial

Van der Lubbe was found inside the burning Reichstag and confessed. At the Leipzig trial he was the sole defendant convicted; the Nazi regime issued the Reichstag Fire Decree and Enabling Act context followed. Historians debate whether he acted alone, though the consensus leans to him as sole arsonist.

Aftermath

He was beheaded in 1934. His conviction was overturned in 2008 under a German law pardoning victims of Nazi injustice.
