Fort Robinson massacre

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Fort Robinson massacre, also known as the Fort Robinson tragedy, occurred on 9 January 1879 at Fort Robinson, Nebraska. A band of Northern Cheyenne, led by Dull Knife (Morning Star), had been captured after fleeing Oklahoma to return to their northern homeland in the Northern Cheyenne Exodus. Held in a barracks at the fort and ordered to return south, the Cheyenne were denied food and firewood when they refused.

The breakout

On the night of 9 January, the Cheyenne broke out using weapons they had hidden, including dismantled guns concealed by the women. In the ensuing pursuit through the snow, soldiers killed many of the escapees; of the roughly 149 Cheyenne held, scores were killed and others recaptured, though a small number escaped and eventually reached sanctuary. The event became one of the most notorious episodes of the Indian Wars and is commemorated at Fort Robinson State Park.
