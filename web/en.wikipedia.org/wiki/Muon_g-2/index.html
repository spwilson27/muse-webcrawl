Muon g-2

Muon g-2 (pronounced "gee minus two") is a particle physics experiment at Fermilab that measures the anomalous magnetic dipole moment of the muon with very high precision.

For a particle, the gyromagnetic ratio g would be exactly 2 in Dirac's theory; quantum effects shift it slightly, so a = (g − 2)/2 is a sensitive probe of virtual particles and possible physics beyond the Standard Model.

Experiment

Polarised muons are stored in a 15-metre-diameter superconducting magnet ring originally built at Brookhaven National Laboratory, where the earlier E821 experiment reported a tantalising discrepancy with theory. Fermilab's E989 experiment confirmed and strengthened the Brookhaven result in results announced in 2021 and 2023, reaching a precision of about 0.20 parts per million. Interpretation depends on Standard Model predictions, particularly hadronic contributions calculated by lattice QCD and data-driven methods.
