Huntington Beach / Los Angeles, California
Channel: 50
Affiliation: PBS
Branding: PBS SoCal

KOCE-TV (channel 50), branded as PBS SoCal, is a PBS member television station licensed to Huntington Beach, California, serving the Los Angeles television market. It is the primary PBS station for Greater Los Angeles and Orange County.

History

The station began broadcasting in 1972, owned by the Coast Community College District, with a strong emphasis on telecourses and educational programming. In 2004 it was sold to the KOCE-TV Foundation. Following the closure of KCET's PBS membership in 2011, KOCE became the principal PBS outlet for the Los Angeles area and rebranded as PBS SoCal; after KCET rejoined PBS in 2019 the two stations came under common management.

Programming

KOCE carries the full PBS schedule together with locally produced programmes about Southern California arts, science and public affairs, and operates several digital subchannels.
