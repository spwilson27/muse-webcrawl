Funeral

A funeral is a ceremony connected with the final disposition of a corpse, such as a burial or cremation, with the attendant observances. Funerary customs comprise the complex of beliefs and practices used by a culture to remember and respect the dead, from interment itself, to various monuments, prayers, and rituals undertaken in their honour. Customs vary widely between cultures and religious groups.

History

Deliberate burial is among the earliest detectable forms of religious practice. Evidence from sites such as Shanidar Cave in Iraq and Qafzeh in Israel suggests that Neanderthals and early modern humans buried their dead with grave goods tens of thousands of years ago. Funerals in the ancient world ranged from the elaborate royal burials of Egypt, with embalming and pyramid tombs, to the cremation pyres described in Greek and Roman literature.

Components

Funerals commonly include a viewing or wake, in which family and friends gather with the body present; a funeral service, religious or secular, with eulogies, music, readings, and prayers; a procession to the place of disposition; and a final committal. In many traditions a meal or reception follows, giving mourners a setting for shared remembrance.

Dispositions

The principal methods of final disposition are burial in the earth, cremation, burial at sea, and newer methods such as alkaline hydrolysis and natural (green) burial. Embalming may be used to preserve the body for viewing, and caskets or shrouds are chosen according to custom, law, and expense.

Religious funerals

Christian funerals typically centre on a funeral Mass or service of thanksgiving and commendation. Jewish funerals emphasise simplicity and prompt burial, followed by the mourning period of shiva. Muslim funerals involve ritual washing of the body, the janaza prayer, and burial — usually within a day of death — without cremation. Hindu funerals generally involve cremation, with rites performed by the eldest son, and Buddhist funerals combine chanting, merit-making, and either cremation or burial depending on region.

State and military funerals

Heads of state, monarchs, and senior military figures may receive state funerals with lying in state, gun carriages, military honours, and national mourning. Military funerals commonly include a flag-draped coffin, a three-volley salute, the sounding of "Last Post" or "Taps", and presentation of the flag to the next of kin.

Modern trends

Recent decades have seen rising cremation rates in many countries, growth in secular and personalised ceremonies, pre-planned and pre-paid funerals, and environmentally oriented "green" funerals. Costs remain a public concern, and funeral poverty — inability to afford a funeral — is tracked by charities and governments in several countries.
