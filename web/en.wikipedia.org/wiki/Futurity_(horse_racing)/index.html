Futurity (horse racing)

A futurity in horse racing is a stakes race in which the owners of the competing horses nominate them — and begin paying entry fees — well before the race is run, often before the horses are even born or while they are foals or yearlings.

Structure

Owners make a series of sustaining payments over months or years to keep a horse eligible. Horses that miss a payment lose eligibility. Because nominations begin so early, futurities reward foresight in breeding and purchasing, and the accumulated fees produce large purses.

Examples

In Thoroughbred racing, the Futurity Stakes name appears at several tracks, including the Belmont Futurity Stakes in New York. In American Quarter Horse racing, major futurities include the All American Futurity at Ruidoso Downs, one of the richest horse races in the world. Harness racing also stages prominent futurities, such as the Kentucky Futurity, a leg of the Trotting Triple Crown.
