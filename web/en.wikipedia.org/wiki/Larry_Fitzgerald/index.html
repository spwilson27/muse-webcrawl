Larry Darnell Fitzgerald Jr. (born August 31, 1983) is an American former professional football wide receiver who played 17 seasons in the National Football League (NFL) for the Arizona Cardinals. He is widely considered one of the greatest wide receivers in NFL history.

Career

Fitzgerald starred at the University of Pittsburgh and was selected third overall by Arizona in the 2004 NFL draft. He ranks second in NFL history in both career receiving yards and receptions, behind only Jerry Rice. An 11-time Pro Bowl selection, he led the Cardinals to Super Bowl XLIII, where he set a single-postseason record for receiving yards. He received the Walter Payton NFL Man of the Year Award in 2016 for his charitable work.
