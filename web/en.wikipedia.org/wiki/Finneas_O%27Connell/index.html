Finneas O'Connell

Finneas Baird O'Connell (born July 30, 1997), known mononymously as Finneas, is an American singer-songwriter, record producer, and actor. He is best known for his collaboration with his younger sister, Billie Eilish, co-writing and producing her music, including the albums When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go? (2019) and Happier Than Ever (2021).

Career

O'Connell has won numerous Grammy Awards, including Producer of the Year, Non-Classical, and shared honours for Record and Song of the Year. Together with Eilish he won Academy Awards for Best Original Song for "No Time to Die" (2021) and "What Was I Made For?" (2023). As a solo artist he released the EP Blood Harmony (2019) and the albums Optimist (2021) and For Cryin' Out Loud! (2024). He has also written or produced for other artists and acted in television series such as Glee and Modern Family.

Early life

Born in Los Angeles to actors Maggie Baird and Patrick O'Connell, Finneas was homeschooled and began writing songs as a teenager, fronting the band The Slightlys before focusing on production work with his sister.
