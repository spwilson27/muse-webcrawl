Purple Mountain (Nanjing)

Purple Mountain (Chinese: 紫金山, Zijinshan) is a mountain in Nanjing, Jiangsu province, China, rising to about 449 metres. Its peaks are often draped in purple and gold clouds at dawn and dusk, giving the mountain its name.

Sites

The mountain and its foothills contain many historic sites, including the Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum of the Hongwu Emperor, the Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum, the Linggu Temple, and the Purple Mountain Observatory, a major Chinese astronomical observatory founded in 1934. The area is a national scenic and forest park.
