Re'im (Hebrew: רֵעִים, lit. "Friends") is a kibbutz in southern Israel. Located in the northwestern Negev desert near the Gaza Strip border, it falls under the jurisdiction of the Eshkol Regional Council. The kibbutz was founded in 1949 by demobilized Palmach soldiers and members of the Israel Boy Scouts movement; its name commemorates fallen comrades ("friends") from the 1948 war.

Geography and economy

Re'im lies at the junction of roads near the Besor stream region, close to an Israel Defense Forces base of the same name (the Re'im base, headquarters of the Gaza Division). The kibbutz economy is based on agriculture, including field crops, citrus, and dairy, as well as a laser-work and printing industry.

2023 attack

On 7 October 2023, during the Hamas-led attack on Israel, the area near Re'im was the site of the Nova music festival massacre, in which hundreds of festival attendees were killed near the kibbutz, and the nearby military base was attacked. The kibbutz itself was evacuated following the attack.
