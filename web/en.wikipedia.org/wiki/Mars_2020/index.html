Mars 2020

Mars 2020 is a NASA Mars rover mission that launched on 30 July 2020 and landed in Jezero Crater on 18 February 2021. Its centrepiece is the Perseverance rover, which seeks signs of ancient life, characterises Mars's geology and climate, and caches samples of rock and regolith for a possible future return to Earth.

Spacecraft

Perseverance is based on the design of the earlier Curiosity rover but carries new instruments, including the Mastcam-Z cameras, the MOXIE oxygen-production experiment, the SuperCam laser instrument and ground-penetrating radar. The mission also deployed Ingenuity, a small helicopter that achieved the first powered, controlled flight on another planet in April 2021.

Objectives

By exploring an ancient lakebed and river delta in Jezero, the mission studies habitable environments of Mars's past and collects sealed sample tubes intended for retrieval by a future sample-return campaign.
