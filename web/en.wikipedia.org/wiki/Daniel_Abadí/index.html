Daniel Abadí

Daniel J. Abadí is a computer scientist specialising in database systems. He is a professor of computer science at the University of Maryland, College Park (formerly at Yale University), known for his work on column-oriented databases and distributed data processing, and as a co-creator of the HadoopDB and Calvin systems.

Research

Abadí's doctoral research at MIT, supervised by Samuel Madden, formed part of the C-Store project, whose ideas underpinned the commercial column-store Vertica. His oft-cited work on column-oriented versus row-oriented storage demonstrated large performance gains for analytical workloads. At Yale he led the development of HadoopDB, combining MapReduce with parallel databases, and of Calvin, a distributed database using deterministic transaction ordering. He also co-authored the influential paper comparing MapReduce with parallel database management systems, and helped formulate the "PACELC" extension to the CAP theorem for reasoning about distributed data consistency trade-offs.
