Red Chillies Entertainment is an Indian film production and distribution company established by actor Shah Rukh Khan and his wife Gauri Khan in 2002. It was founded after the closure of Khan's earlier venture Dreamz Unlimited.

Productions

The studio has produced many successful Hindi films, including Main Hoon Na (2004), Om Shanti Om (2007), Chennai Express (2013), Raees (2017), and Jawan (2023), one of the highest-grossing Indian films of all time.

Subsidiaries

Its visual-effects division, Red Chillies VFX, is one of India's leading VFX studios and pioneered large-scale effects work in Hindi cinema, notably in Ra.One (2011). The company also co-owns the Kolkata Knight Riders cricket franchise in the Indian Premier League.
