Timothée Chalamet

Timothée Chalamet
Born: 27 December 1995, New York City, U.S.
Occupation: Actor
Years active: 2007–present

Timothée Hal Chalamet (born 27 December 1995) is an American and French actor. He began his career in television before gaining international recognition in 2017 for his roles in Call Me by Your Name, for which he received an Academy Award nomination for Best Actor, and Lady Bird.

Career

Chalamet has since starred in films including Beautiful Boy (2018), Little Women (2019), Denis Villeneuve's Dune (2021) and Dune: Part Two (2024) as Paul Atreides, and Wonka (2023) as Willy Wonka. His portrayal of Bob Dylan in A Complete Unknown (2024) earned him further acclaim and award nominations.

Public image

Chalamet is frequently cited as one of the most talented actors of his generation and is known for his distinctive fashion sense, which has made him a prominent figure in contemporary popular culture.
