Ames Laboratory

Ames Laboratory is a United States Department of Energy national laboratory located on the campus of Iowa State University in Ames, Iowa. It specializes in materials science, especially rare-earth metals, condensed matter physics, and chemical and materials research supporting energy technologies.

History

The laboratory grew out of Frank Spedding's wartime work for the Manhattan Project: the Ames Process produced high-purity uranium metal at scale, with Ames supplying large quantities for the Chicago Pile and later reactors. It was formally established as a laboratory in 1947.

Research

Research areas include synthesis and study of novel materials, rare-earth magnetism, superconductivity, catalysis, and computational materials science. The Critical Materials Innovation Hub, led by Ames Laboratory since 2013, addresses supply risks for elements critical to clean energy.
