Alma Har'el

Born1975, Tel Aviv, Israel
OccupationFilm director, music video director
Notable workBombay Beach, Honey Boy

Alma Har'el (born 1975) is an Israeli-American music video and film director. She is best known for her feature film Honey Boy (2019), written by and starring Shia LaBeouf, and for the documentary Bombay Beach (2011), which won the top documentary prize at the Tribeca Film Festival.

Career

Har'el began her career directing music videos, including acclaimed clips for Beirut ("Elephant Gun") and Sigur Rós. Her documentary Bombay Beach, about residents of the Salton Sea community in California, blended documentary and staged musical sequences. She later directed LoveTrue (2016) and the television series Lady in the Lake (2024) for Apple TV+.

Free the Bid

In 2016 she founded Free the Bid, an advocacy initiative pressing advertisers and studios to hire more women directors; it later expanded into the broader Free the Work platform.
