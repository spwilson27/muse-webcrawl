Kodiak, Alaska

Kodiak

State: Alaska

Borough: Kodiak Island Borough

Founded: 1792 (as Russian settlement)

Known for: Fishing port, Kodiak bears, Coast Guard base

Kodiak is the main city and one of seven communities on Kodiak Island in Kodiak Island Borough, Alaska. All commercial transportation between the island's communities and the outside world goes through this city, either via ferry or airline.

History

The area was inhabited for millennia by the Alutiiq people. Grigory Shelikhov established a Russian fur-trading outpost at Three Saints Bay in 1784, and the settlement moved to present-day Kodiak in 1792; it served as the capital of Russian Alaska. The 1964 Good Friday earthquake and resulting tsunami devastated the waterfront and fishing fleet.

Economy

Kodiak is one of the top commercial fishing ports in the United States by value of catch, centred on salmon, pollock, cod, and crab. It also hosts the largest U.S. Coast Guard base on the Pacific coast.
