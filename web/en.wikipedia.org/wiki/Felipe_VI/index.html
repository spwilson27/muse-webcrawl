Felipe VI

Felipe VI (Spanish: [feˈlipe]; born 30 January 1968) is King of Spain. He succeeded to the throne on 19 June 2014 upon the abdication of his father, Juan Carlos I. As Prince of Asturias he had been heir apparent since 1977. His mother is Queen Sofía, and he has two elder sisters, Infanta Elena and Infanta Cristina. His elder daughter, Leonor, Princess of Asturias, is heir presumptive.

Early life and education

Felipe was born in Madrid, the third child and only son of the then Prince Juan Carlos and Princess Sofía. He was educated at Santa María de los Rosales school and at Lakefield College School in Canada, then trained at all three Spanish military academies — the General Military Academy in Zaragoza, the Naval Military Academy at Marín (serving aboard the training ship Juan Sebastián Elcano), and the General Air Academy at San Javier — qualifying as a helicopter pilot. He studied law at the Autonomous University of Madrid and took a Master of Science in Foreign Service at Georgetown University in Washington, D.C.

Felipe competed in sailing at the 1992 Barcelona Olympic Games, where the Spanish soling team finished sixth; he was Spain's flag bearer at the opening ceremony.

Prince of Asturias

As heir, Felipe undertook regular institutional duties, represented Spain abroad, and presided over the Princess of Asturias Awards (then Prince of Asturias Awards) in Oviedo. In 2004 he married the television journalist Letizia Ortiz in Madrid Cathedral. The couple have two daughters, Leonor (born 2005) and Sofía (born 2007).

Reign

On 2 June 2014 Juan Carlos announced his abdication; Felipe was proclaimed king before the Cortes Generales on 19 June. His reign has emphasised transparency in the royal household's finances. Felipe played a constitutional role during the Catalan independence crisis, addressing the nation on 3 October 2017 after the disputed independence referendum. In 2020, amid investigations into his father's financial affairs, he renounced any personal inheritance from Juan Carlos and withdrew his father's allowance. During the COVID-19 pandemic and the 2024 Valencia floods he made prominent visits to affected regions.
