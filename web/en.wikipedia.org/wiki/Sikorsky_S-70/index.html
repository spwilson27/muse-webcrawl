Sikorsky S-70

ManufacturerSikorsky Aircraft
RoleMedium-lift utility helicopter
First flight1974 (as YUH-60)

The Sikorsky S-70 is an American medium-lift helicopter family manufactured by Sikorsky Aircraft. It was developed for the United States Army as the UH-60 Black Hawk, winner of the Utility Tactical Transport Aircraft System competition in 1976.

Variants

The S-70 family includes the UH-60 Black Hawk for the US Army, the SH-60 Seahawk for naval operations, the HH-60 Pave Hawk for combat search and rescue, the MH-60, Coast Guard MH-60 Jayhawk, and the VH-60 used for presidential transport. Civilian and export versions are marketed as the S-70i, and the Firehawk variant is adapted for aerial firefighting.

Design

The S-70 is a twin-engine, four-bladed helicopter designed for transporting troops, medical evacuation, cargo and special operations, with crashworthy design features and the ability to be transported by airlifters such as the C-130.
