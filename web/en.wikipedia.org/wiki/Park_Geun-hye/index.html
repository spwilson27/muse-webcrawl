Park Geun-hye

Park Geun-hye (박근혜)
Born: 2 February 1952, Daegu, South Korea
President of South Korea: 25 February 2013 – 10 March 2017
Party: Saenuri Party (later Liberty Korea Party)
Parents: Park Chung-hee and Yuk Young-soo
Education: Sogang University (electronic engineering)

Park Geun-hye (Korean: 박근혜; born 2 February 1952) is a South Korean politician who served as the 11th president of South Korea from 2013 until her impeachment and removal from office in 2017. She was the first woman elected president of South Korea and the first female head of state in Northeast Asia, as well as the daughter of former president Park Chung-hee.

Early life and career

Park acted as South Korea's de facto first lady after her mother, Yuk Young-soo, was assassinated in 1974; her father was assassinated five years later. She entered politics in 1998, winning a National Assembly seat in Daegu, and rose to lead the conservative Grand National Party (later Saenuri), whose revival under her leadership earned her the nickname "queen of elections". She narrowly defeated Moon Jae-in in the December 2012 presidential election, taking office in February 2013.

Presidency

Her administration pursued "creative economy" policies and a policy of trustpolitik toward North Korea. Her government's slow, widely criticised response to the sinking of the ferry Sewol in April 2014, in which more than 300 people, mostly school students, died, badly damaged her standing.

Impeachment, conviction and pardon

In late 2016, revelations that Park's longtime confidante Choi Soon-sil — who held no official position — had accessed state documents and extorted money from conglomerates triggered mass candlelight protests. The National Assembly impeached Park on 9 December 2016, and the Constitutional Court unanimously upheld her removal on 10 March 2017. She was subsequently convicted of bribery, abuse of power, and coercion, receiving a combined sentence of over 20 years' imprisonment. In December 2021, President Moon Jae-in granted her a special pardon, and she was released from prison.
