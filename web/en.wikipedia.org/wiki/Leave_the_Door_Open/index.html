"Leave the Door Open" is the debut single by the American musical super-duo Silk Sonic, composed of Bruno Mars and Anderson .Paak. It was released on March 5, 2021, as the lead single from their album An Evening with Silk Sonic.

Composition and reception

The song is a lush Philadelphia soul and R&B ballad in the style of 1970s acts such as the Stylistics and the Delfonics, built on piano, glockenspiel, strings and smooth vocal harmonies, with Mars crooning an invitation to a lover. Critics praised its retro craftsmanship and vocal performances. It debuted at number four on the Billboard Hot 100 and reached number one in April 2021, becoming Mars's eighth and .Paak's first chart-topper in the United States; it also topped charts in several other countries.

Accolades

At the 64th Annual Grammy Awards in 2022, "Leave the Door Open" won all four awards for which it was nominated, including Record of the Year and Song of the Year, as well as Best R&B Performance and Best R&B Song. The duo opened the ceremony with a live performance of the song.
