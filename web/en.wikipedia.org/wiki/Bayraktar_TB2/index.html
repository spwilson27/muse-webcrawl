The Bayraktar TB2 is a Turkish medium-altitude long-endurance (MALE) unmanned combat aerial vehicle manufactured by the defence company Baykar. Capable of remotely controlled or semi-autonomous flight, it became one of the most widely exported combat drones in the world and achieved worldwide fame for its use by Ukraine in the opening phase of the Russian invasion of 2022.

Development

Baykar, led technically by Selçuk Bayraktar, developed the TB2 within a Turkish programme for a tactical armed drone; it first flew in August 2014 and entered Turkish service the same year. The aircraft has a distinctive inverted V-tail, a pusher propeller driven by a 100 hp internal combustion engine, and four hardpoints for laser-guided smart micro munitions.

Operational history

Turkey used the TB2 in counter-insurgency operations and in Syria and Libya. Exported to Azerbaijan, it played a prominent role in the 2020 Nagorno-Karabakh war, destroying Armenian armour and air defences. Ukraine, an early export customer, used TB2s to strike Russian convoys, artillery and naval assets in 2022, including around Snake Island; the drone became a symbol of Ukrainian resistance, inspiring a popular folk song. The TB2 has since been exported to more than two dozen countries across Europe, Africa and Asia.

Limitations

The TB2 is slow and vulnerable to modern integrated air defences once those are alert and intact; its greatest successes came against disorganised or suppressed air defences. Its significance lies in offering capable armed drone power at a fraction of the cost of American systems and without equivalent export restrictions.
