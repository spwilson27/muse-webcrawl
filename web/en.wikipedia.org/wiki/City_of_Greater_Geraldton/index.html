City of Greater Geraldton

The City of Greater Geraldton is a local government area in the Mid West region of Western Australia, about 420 kilometres (260 mi) north of Perth. It covers an area of roughly 12,626 square kilometres and administers the city of Geraldton and surrounding districts, including Mullewa. The council's seat is in Geraldton. At the 2021 census the population was around 39,000.

Geraldton, the administrative centre of the City of Greater Geraldton.

History

The City of Greater Geraldton was formed on 1 July 2011 by the merger of the City of Geraldton-Greenough and the Shire of Mullewa. The predecessor City of Geraldton-Greenough had itself been created in 2007 by merging the City of Geraldton and the Shire of Greenough. The amalgamation of 2011 made Greater Geraldton one of the larger regional local governments in Western Australia by area.

Wards and council

The council consists of a popularly elected mayor and twelve councillors elected from wards corresponding broadly to the former local government areas, including wards covering Geraldton's suburbs and the rural districts around Mullewa and Greenough.

Localities

Localities within the city include Geraldton, Beachlands, Beresford, Bluff Point, Drummond Cove, Mount Tarcoola, Rangeway, Spalding, Sunset Beach, Waggrakine, Wandina, Wonthella, Greenough, Walkaway and Mullewa, among others.
