Naval Station Everett

Naval Station Everett is a US Navy shore installation in Everett, Washington, on Port Gardner Bay. Opened in 1994, it was designed as a homeport for a carrier strike group and is one of the Navy's newest and most modern stations.

Homeported units

The station has hosted aircraft carriers including USS Abraham Lincoln and USS Nimitz, as well as destroyers, frigates (later replaced by other ships) and Coast Guard vessels. About 6,000 sailors and civil service personnel are assigned. The campus includes a large pier, support facilities and a separate Navy Support Activity at Smokey Point.
