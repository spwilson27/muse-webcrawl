Taron Egerton

Taron David Egerton (born 10 November 1989) is a Welsh actor. He gained recognition for starring as Gary "Eggsy" Unwin in the action comedy films Kingsman: The Secret Service (2014) and Kingsman: The Golden Circle (2017), and won a Golden Globe Award for Best Actor for his portrayal of Elton John in the musical biopic Rocketman (2019).

Early life

Egerton was born in Birkenhead to a family from Liverpool and Anglesey, and spent much of his childhood in Wales, including Llanfairpwllgwyngyll and Aberystwyth. He speaks Welsh and considers himself Welsh. He trained at the Royal Academy of Dramatic Art, graduating in 2012.

Career

After early television appearances including The Smoke, Egerton's breakthrough came as Eggsy in Matthew Vaughn's Kingsman films. He played Eddie Edwards in Eddie the Eagle (2016), voiced Johnny the gorilla in the animated Sing films, and starred as Robin Hood in the 2018 film of that name. His performance as Elton John in Rocketman (2019) — including singing the songs himself — earned him the Golden Globe for Best Actor in a Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy and BAFTA and Screen Actors Guild nominations.

On stage he appeared in the West End revival of Cock (2022), and on television he received acclaim and a Golden Globe nomination for the true-crime drama Black Bird (2022). He later played Henk Rogers in Tetris (2023) and starred in the thriller Carry-On (2024).
