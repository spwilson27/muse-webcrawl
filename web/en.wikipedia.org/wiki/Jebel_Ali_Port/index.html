Jebel Ali Port

Jebel Ali Port is a deep port located in the Jebel Ali area of Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Operated by DP World, it is the largest man-made harbour in the world and the busiest port in the Middle East, handling a large share of the region's container traffic.

History

Construction of the port began in the late 1970s under the direction of Sheikh Rashid bin Saeed Al Maktoum, and it was inaugurated in 1979. The adjacent Jebel Ali Free Zone, established in 1985, attracted thousands of companies and helped turn the port into a major transshipment and logistics hub.

Facilities

The port has multiple container terminals with deep-water berths able to accommodate the largest container vessels afloat. It is connected by road and logistics corridors to Al Maktoum International Airport and the wider Gulf region.
