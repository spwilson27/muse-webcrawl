Abu Dawud al-Sijistani

Abu Dawud Sulayman ibn al-Ash'ath al-Azdi al-Sijistani (817–889 CE) was a Persian scholar of hadith who compiled Sunan Abi Dawud, one of the six major hadith collections (Kutub al-Sittah) of Sunni Islam.

Born in Sistan, he travelled widely through Iraq, the Hejaz, Syria, Egypt and Khorasan collecting traditions, studying under teachers such as Ahmad ibn Hanbal. His Sunan contains roughly 4,800 hadith selected from a far larger body he examined, arranged by legal topic and focused on traditions relevant to Islamic jurisprudence (fiqh).

Abu Dawud is noted for indicating the strength or weakness of the narrations he included; his letter to the people of Mecca describing the method of his book is a well-known early statement of hadith criticism.
