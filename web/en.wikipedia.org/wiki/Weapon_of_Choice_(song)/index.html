Weapon of Choice (song)

ArtistFatboy Slim featuring Bootsy Collins
AlbumHalfway Between the Gutter and the Stars
Released23 April 2001
GenreBig beat, funk

"Weapon of Choice" is a song by British big beat musician Fatboy Slim from his third studio album, Halfway Between the Gutter and the Stars (2000). It features vocals by American funk musician Bootsy Collins. It was released as a single on 23 April 2001.

The song name derives from the Dune series of books by Frank Herbert, in which the line "Don't be shocked by the tone of my voice" and the phrase about the voice as a weapon are referenced; the lyrics sample and allude to the Bene Gesserit "Voice."

Music video

The music video, directed by Spike Jonze, features actor Christopher Walken dancing and flying around a hotel lobby. It won six awards at the 2001 MTV Video Music Awards, including Breakthrough Video and Best Direction, and won the Grammy Award for Best Short Form Music Video at the 44th Grammy Awards in 2002. The video is frequently ranked among the greatest music videos of all time.

Reception

The single peaked at number 10 on the UK Singles Chart and reached the top 40 in several other countries. Critics praised Collins's funk vocal performance and the track's groove.
