List of Pokémon

The Pokémon franchise, created by Satoshi Tajiri and developed by Game Freak and Nintendo, features fictional creatures called "Pokémon" that inhabit a fictional world and can be captured, trained, and battled by humans called Pokémon Trainers. New species have been introduced with each generation of the core video game series. The first generation (1996) introduced 151 species; subsequent generations added more, and with the ninth generation the total number of known species exceeded 1,000, reaching 1,025 species.

Well-known species

Pikachu, the franchise's mascot, is an Electric-type introduced in Generation I. Other widely recognized Pokémon include Charizard, Mewtwo, Eevee and its evolutions, Snorlax, Gengar, Lucario, Greninja, and the starter Pokémon of each generation. Species are categorized by elemental "types" (such as Fire, Water, Grass, and Electric), and include special classifications such as Legendary Pokémon and Mythical Pokémon.
