Iddhipada

Iddhipāda (Pali; Sanskrit: ṛddhipāda) is a compound term in Buddhism: iddhi means "potency" or "spiritual power", and pāda means "base" or "constituent". The four bases of spiritual power are one of the seven sets of qualities conducive to enlightenment (the bodhipakkhiyādhammā).

The four bases

The four bases are concentration (samādhi) founded upon: chanda — intention, desire or zeal to act; viriya — energy or effort; citta — consciousness or intentness of mind; and vīmaṃsā — investigation or discrimination. Each base pairs the factor with concentrated effort of will (padhāna-saṅkhāra). In the Pali Canon, notably the Iddhipada Saṃyutta, cultivating these bases develops both concentration and the supernormal abilities traditionally associated with the word iddhi.
