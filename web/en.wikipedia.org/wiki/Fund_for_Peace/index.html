The Fund for Peace (FFP) is a US non-profit, non-governmental research and educational organisation based in Washington, D.C. Founded in 1957, it works to prevent violent conflict and promote sustainable security, focusing on conflict assessment, early warning, and the resilience of fragile states.

Fragile States Index

The Fund for Peace is best known for the Fragile States Index (formerly the Failed States Index), published annually since 2005, for many years in partnership with Foreign Policy magazine. The index ranks some 179 countries using twelve social, economic, and political indicators — including demographic pressures, refugees, group grievance, human flight, state legitimacy, public services, human rights, the security apparatus, factionalised elites, and external intervention — scored using its CAST (Conflict Assessment System Tool) framework, which combines content analysis of millions of documents with quantitative data and expert review.

Other work

Beyond the index, FFP conducts conflict assessment and early-warning programmes in countries such as Nigeria, Yemen, and Liberia, and works on business and human-rights issues, including helping develop conflict-sensitive practices for companies operating in fragile environments.
