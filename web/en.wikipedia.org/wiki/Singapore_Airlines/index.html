Singapore Airlines (abbreviation: SIA or SQ) is the flag carrier of Singapore with its hub at Changi Airport. Widely regarded as one of the world's leading airlines, it is known for high standards of service, a young fleet, and the "Singapore Girl" cabin crew icon in sarong kebaya uniform. The airline is majority-owned by the Singapore government's investment arm, Temasek Holdings.

History

Singapore Airlines traces its origins to Malayan Airways, founded in 1947, which became Malaysia–Singapore Airlines (MSA) in 1966. When Singapore and Malaysia split the airline in 1972, the Singapore operation was reconstituted as Singapore Airlines, retaining the Boeing 707s and 737s and the international routes, while the Malaysian side became Malaysian Airline System. SIA grew rapidly through the 1970s and 1980s, pioneering services such as free drinks and checked baggage allowances, and it became a launch customer or early operator for aircraft including the Boeing 747-400 and Airbus A380, which it was the first to fly commercially in October 2007.

Destinations and operations

The airline serves destinations across Asia, Europe, North America, Oceania, the Middle East, and Africa, with a strong presence in Southeast Asia and on the "Kangaroo Route" between Australia and the United Kingdom via Singapore. It operates some of the world's longest non-stop flights, including Singapore–New York services using Airbus A350-900ULR aircraft. Subsidiaries and affiliates have included the regional carrier SilkAir (later merged into SIA), the low-cost carrier Scoot, and SIA Engineering Company.

Fleet

Singapore Airlines operates an all-widebody passenger fleet centred on the Airbus A350-900, A380, Boeing 777-300ER, 787-10, and 777-9 on order, with dedicated freighter operations by Boeing 747-400F aircraft. Its average fleet age is consistently among the youngest of major airlines.

Service and recognition

SIA introduced pioneering in-flight entertainment through its KrisWorld system and has repeatedly been named "World's Best Airline" in the Skytrax World Airline Awards, including in 2023. Its premium Suites class on the A380 features individual cabins with separate bed and chair. The airline's marketing icon, the Singapore Girl, created in 1972 from the Balmain-designed sarong kebaya uniform, is one of the most recognised figures in aviation advertising.

Incidents

Notable incidents include Singapore Airlines Flight 006, a Boeing 747 that crashed on takeoff from Taipei in 2000, and severe turbulence encountered by Flight 321 in May 2024, which caused one fatality.
