iLife

iLife is a software suite for macOS and iOS developed by Apple Inc. It consists of various programs for media creation, organization, editing and publishing. Introduced in January 2003, iLife was preinstalled on all new Macs for many years and marketed around Steve Jobs's "digital hub" strategy, integrating the Mac with digital cameras, camcorders, and the iPod.

Components

At its fullest extent the suite contained:

  • iPhoto — photo organizing and editing (later replaced by Photos)
  • iMovie — consumer video editing
  • iDVD — DVD authoring (discontinued)
  • iWeb — website creation (discontinued)
  • GarageBand — music and podcast creation

Versions were released nearly annually (iLife '04 through iLife '11). From the introduction of the Mac App Store, the components were sold individually, and iPhoto, iMovie, and GarageBand were eventually distributed free with new devices. iDVD and iWeb were dropped as optical media and hosted web publishing declined.
