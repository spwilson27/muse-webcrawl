Dr. Slump (Japanese: Dr. スランプ, Hepburn: Dokutā Suranpu) is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Akira Toriyama. It was serialized in Shueisha's Weekly Shōnen Jump magazine from 1980 to 1984, with the chapters collected into 18 tankōbon volumes. The series follows the humorous adventures of the little robot girl Arale Norimaki, her creator Senbei Norimaki, and the other residents of the bizarre Penguin Village.

Story

Set in Penguin Village, where humans co-exist with anthropomorphic animals and other oddities, the series centres on inventor Senbei Norimaki ("Dr. Slump") and Arale, the super-strong, naive robot girl he builds. Episodes parody science fiction, fairy tales, and Japanese popular culture, with frequent visual gags, puns, and fourth-wall breaks. Characters from Dr. Slump later crossed over into Toriyama's Dragon Ball.

Media

Toei Animation produced two anime television series: Dr. Slump – Arale-chan (1981–1986, 243 episodes) and a 1997–1999 remake, plus numerous films and specials. The manga has over 50 million copies in circulation, won the Shogakukan Manga Award for shōnen in 1981, and launched Toriyama's career before Dragon Ball.
