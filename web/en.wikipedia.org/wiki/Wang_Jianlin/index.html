Wang Jianlin

Wang Jianlin (Chinese: 王健林; born 24 October 1954) is a Chinese billionaire businessman, and the founder of Dalian Wanda Group, China's largest real estate development company, and once one of the world's largest movie theater operators through AMC Theatres and Wanda Cinemas. He served in the People's Liberation Army for 16 years before entering business, and has held roles including deputy to the National People's Congress and membership of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference.

Career

Wang took over a struggling residential development company in Dalian in 1988 and transformed it into Wanda Group, expanding from commercial plazas into hotels, tourism, film, and sports. At his peak in the mid-2010s he was ranked among China's richest people. Beginning in 2017, Wanda sold off many overseas and domestic assets under debt pressure; Wang's fortune and the group's profile declined substantially thereafter.
