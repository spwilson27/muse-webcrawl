Oceanic climate

An oceanic climate, also known as a marine or maritime climate, is the temperate climate sub-type in the Köppen classification Cfb, typical of west coasts at middle latitudes, with mild summers, cool but not cold winters, and precipitation spread through the year.

Oceanic climate
KöppenCfb
RegionsWestern Europe, Pacific Northwest, NZ, SE Australia
FeaturesMild, wet, narrow temperature range

Characteristics

Oceans moderate temperature, so annual ranges are small. Weather is changeable, with frequent cloud, drizzle and westerly winds. Examples: London, Amsterdam (see North Holland), Vancouver, Seattle, Melbourne and most of New Zealand.

Subtypes

Subtropical highland (Cfb/Cwb), subpolar oceanic (Cfc, e.g. Iceland, Faroe Islands) and cold-summer variants grade toward continental or Mediterranean climates inland and equatorward.
