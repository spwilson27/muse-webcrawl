Royal Scottish National Orchestra

The Royal Scottish National Orchestra (RSNO) is a Scottish orchestra based in Glasgow. Founded in 1891 as the Scottish Orchestra, it is one of Scotland's national performing companies.

History

The orchestra became full-time in 1950 as the Scottish National Orchestra and received the "Royal" title in 1991. Principal conductors have included Alexander Gibson — its first Scottish-born music director — Neeme Järvi, Bryden Thomson, Stéphane Denève and Thomas Søndergård.

Activities

The RSNO performs season concerts in Glasgow, Edinburgh, Dundee, Aberdeen and Perth, records extensively, and runs education and community programmes. Its home is the RSNO Centre at Glasgow Royal Concert Hall.
