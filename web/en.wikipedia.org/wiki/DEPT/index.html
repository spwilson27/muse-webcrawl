DEPT

DEPT most commonly refers to a department — an organisational unit of a government, university, company or other institution, typically abbreviated "Dept." or "DEPT". The abbreviation is used worldwide in addresses, directories and official documents for bodies such as a police department, a fire department, or a university academic department.

Other uses

In chemistry, DEPT (Distortionless Enhancement by Polarization Transfer) is a nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) method used, together with 13C NMR spectroscopy, to distinguish between CH3, CH2 and CH groups in an organic molecule. The DEPT experiment transfers polarization from protons to carbon-13 nuclei, and variants such as DEPT-90 and DEPT-135 allow methylene and methine signals to be told apart by their phase.

"DEPT" is also the ticker-style acronym and branding used by various organisations, including the global digital agency Dept, founded in the Netherlands, and government departments in several countries.
