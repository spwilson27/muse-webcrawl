House of Angostura

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Angostura bitters and its distinctive label

The House of Angostura (Angostura Holdings Limited), also known as Angostura Limited, is a Trinidad and Tobago company that produces Angostura bitters, the most famous aromatic bitters brand in the world, as well as a range of rums. It is based in Laventille, Port of Spain, Trinidad.

History

The company was founded around the recipe created in 1824 by Dr. Johann Gottlieb Benjamin Siegert, a German surgeon general in Simón Bolívar's army stationed in the town of Angostura, Venezuela (now Ciudad Bolívar). Intended as a medicinal tonic for the troops' stomach ailments, the bitters became popular with sailors and in cocktails. Siegert's family moved production to Trinidad in 1875, where it remained after his descendants established the company in Port of Spain. The bottle's oversized paper label — the result, according to company tradition, of a sizing mix-up that was never corrected — became its trademark.

Products

Beyond the original aromatic bitters (44.7% alcohol, made from a secret blend of herbs and spices), Angostura produces orange bitters and a range of aged rums including Reserva, 1919, and 1824 expressions. The company holds a Royal Warrant as supplier of bitters to the British monarch and has supplied successive Spanish and Swedish royal households. It is one of Trinidad and Tobago's best-known exporters, and its museum and distillery tours are a local attraction.
