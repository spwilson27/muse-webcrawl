List of best-selling music artists

The following list of best-selling music artists ranks performers by claimed total record sales (albums, singles, and equivalent units) worldwide. Figures combine certified units and claimed sales and should be treated with caution: reliable certification data exists mainly from the 1970s onward in major markets. Only artists with claimed sales of 100 million or more are included.
