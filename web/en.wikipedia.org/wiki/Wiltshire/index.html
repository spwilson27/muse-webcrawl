Wiltshire

Wiltshire is a ceremonial county in South West England, known for its rolling chalk downland, prehistoric monuments including Stonehenge and Avebury, and the cathedral city of Salisbury.

Region: South West England
County town: Trowbridge
Largest town: Swindon
Population: ~720,000

Geography and history

Landlocked, Wiltshire borders Dorset, Somerset, Hampshire, and others. Salisbury Plain hosts a major army training area. Neolithic sites at Stonehenge and Avebury form a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Historic towns include Salisbury, Devizes, Marlborough, and Bradford-on-Avon.

Economy

Agriculture, defence, and towns like Swindon — a railway town and modern commercial centre — shape the economy. See Salisbury and Swindon.
