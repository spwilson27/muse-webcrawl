London Convention
Type: Multilateral treaties (several)
Examples: 1840, 1841, 1864

London Convention or Convention of London may refer to several international agreements signed in London, including the 1840 Convention settling the Egyptian-Ottoman war, the 1841 Straits Convention, and later agreements on warfare, fisheries and the environment.

Notable examples

The 1840 convention, signed by Britain, Austria, Prussia, Russia and the Ottoman Empire, ended the crisis over Muhammad Ali's Egypt. The 1864 convention concerned the cession of the Ionian Islands to Greece.

Other uses

The name also applies to the 1972 Convention on the Prevention of Marine Pollution by Dumping of Wastes, commonly called the London Convention.
