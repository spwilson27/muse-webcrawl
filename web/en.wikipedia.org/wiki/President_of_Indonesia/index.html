The President of the Republic of Indonesia (Indonesian: Presiden Republik Indonesia) is the head of state and head of government of Indonesia. The president leads the executive branch of the Indonesian government and is the commander-in-chief of the Indonesian National Armed Forces. Since 2004, the president and vice president have been directly elected to a five-year term, once renewable, for a total of ten years.

History

The presidency was created on 18 August 1945, the day after Sukarno proclaimed Indonesian independence from Dutch colonial rule. Sukarno served as the first president, initially in a parliamentary system in which much day-to-day power rested with the prime minister. The 1945 Constitution was restored by decree in 1959, giving the presidency much greater authority under Sukarno's "Guided Democracy." After the transition of 1965–66, Suharto held power for more than three decades during the New Order period. Following Suharto's resignation in 1998 amid the Asian financial crisis, Indonesia entered the Reformasi era, with constitutional amendments limiting presidential terms and introducing direct elections.

Powers and duties

Under the amended 1945 Constitution, the president holds executive power, appoints and dismisses ministers, submits bills to the People's Representative Council (DPR), issues government regulations, and serves as commander-in-chief of the armed forces. The president may declare a state of emergency and grants pardons and amnesties with the consideration of the Supreme Court or the DPR. Foreign ambassadors are appointed with the DPR's agreement, and the president receives foreign envoys.

Election

Presidential candidates run as a ticket with a vice-presidential candidate. To be nominated, a ticket must be proposed by a political party or coalition holding at least 20 percent of DPR seats or having won 25 percent of the national popular vote in the previous legislative election. A ticket wins outright in the first round by gaining more than 50 percent of the vote, including at least 20 percent in over half of Indonesia's provinces; otherwise a runoff is held. The first direct presidential election was held in 2004 and won by Susilo Bambang Yudhoyono.

List of presidents

Indonesia has had eight presidents: Sukarno (1945–1967), Suharto (1967–1998), B. J. Habibie (1998–1999), Abdurrahman Wahid (1999–2001), Megawati Sukarnoputri (2001–2004), Susilo Bambang Yudhoyono (2004–2014), Joko Widodo (2014–2024), and Prabowo Subianto, who took office on 20 October 2024 after winning the 2024 election. Megawati is the only woman to have held the office; she is also the daughter of Sukarno.

Residences

The president's official residence is the Merdeka Palace in Jakarta, part of the presidential palace complex facing Monas (the National Monument). Other presidential palaces include the State Palace in Jakarta, the Bogor Palace in West Java, the Tampaksiring Palace in Bali, and the Gedung Agung in Yogyakarta.
