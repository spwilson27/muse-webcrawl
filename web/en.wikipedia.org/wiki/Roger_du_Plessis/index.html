Roger du Plessis

Roger du Plessis (also rendered Roger de Plessis) was a French nobleman and knight of the Order of the Temple (Knights Templar) in the thirteenth century. He belonged to the du Plessis family of Poitou and appears in the records of the Order during the final decades of the Crusader presence in the Levant.

Life

Little is securely documented about his life beyond scattered mentions in charters and chronicles of the period, where knights of his name witness donations and serve in the Templar garrisons of the Holy Land. He should not be confused with the better-documented Plessis family members of later French history, such as Armand de Richelieu, who held the title of duc du Plessis.

Legacy

Like many lesser-known Templar brothers, Roger du Plessis survives mainly through genealogical and regional histories of Poitou and through studies of the personnel of the military orders, which draw on the cartularies of Templar commanderies in western France.
