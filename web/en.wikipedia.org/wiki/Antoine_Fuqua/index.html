Antoine Fuqua

Antoine Fuqua (born May 30, 1965) is an American film director known for his work in action and thriller films. He first gained recognition for directing Training Day (2001), for which Denzel Washington won the Academy Award for Best Actor, and went on to direct films including Tears of the Sun, King Arthur, Shooter, Olympus Has Fallen, The Equalizer trilogy, Southpaw and The Magnificent Seven.

Career

Fuqua began his career directing music videos for artists such as Prince, Toni Braxton and Coolio, and commercials. His feature debut was the action film The Replacement Killers (1998). Training Day established his reputation for gritty urban crime drama. He has frequently collaborated with Denzel Washington, directing him in Training Day, The Equalizer films and The Magnificent Seven. In 2022 he directed the historical action film Emancipation starring Will Smith.
