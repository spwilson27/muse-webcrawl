List of branches of the Queens Public Library

The Queens Public Library (Queens Library) is one of three public library systems serving New York City and provides library service to the borough of Queens through a central library and more than sixty branch locations, making it one of the largest public library systems in the United States by circulation.

Central library

The Central Library in Jamaica opened in 1911 and was expanded over the twentieth century; it houses the Long Island Division's historical collections. The Children's Library Discovery Center is adjacent.

History

The system traces its origins to the Long Island City Public Library in 1896. Andrew Carnegie funded many early branch buildings, several of which — including those in Astoria, Richmond Hill (Lefferts), and Far Rockaway — survive in whole or in part.
