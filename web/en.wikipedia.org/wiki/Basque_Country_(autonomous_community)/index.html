Basque Country (autonomous community)

The Basque Country (Basque: Euskadi [eus̺kadi]; Spanish: País Vasco), officially the Basque Autonomous Community, is an autonomous community in northern Spain. It includes the Basque provinces of Araba (Álava), Bizkaia (Biscay) and Gipuzkoa (Guipúzcoa). The Basque Country or Basque Autonomous Community was granted the status of nationality within Spain, attributed by the Spanish Constitution of 1978. The autonomous community is based on the Statute of Autonomy of the Basque Country, approved in 1979. Its capital is Vitoria-Gasteiz. The largest city is Bilbao.

Geography

The Basque Country borders the autonomous communities of Cantabria to the west, Castile and León to the southwest, La Rioja to the south, and Navarre to the southeast, and France to the northeast, with the Bay of Biscay to the north. The territory is characterised by green, mountainous terrain — the Basque Mountains divide the Atlantic and Mediterranean watersheds — and a wet oceanic climate that contrasts sharply with the drier interior of Spain.

History

The Basque provinces maintained extensive self-governing privileges, the fueros, for centuries, which were curtailed after the Carlist Wars in the 19th century. Basque nationalism emerged in the late 19th century, led by figures such as Sabino Arana. A first autonomy statute was approved in 1936 during the Spanish Civil War, but was abolished under Franco's regime, during which the Basque language was suppressed. Autonomy was restored in 1979. The armed separatist group ETA, founded in 1959, carried out a campaign of violence for decades before declaring a definitive cessation of armed activity in 2011 and dissolving in 2018.

Language and culture

Basque (Euskara) is a language isolate unrelated to any other known language, and is co-official with Spanish. The proportion of Basque speakers has been recovering after decades of decline. Basque culture is noted for its gastronomy, rural sports, the bertsolaritza tradition of improvised sung verse, and festivals such as the Semana Grande.

Economy

The Basque Country is one of Spain's wealthiest regions, with a strong industrial tradition in steel, shipbuilding, machine tools and energy centred on Bilbao, alongside a renowned cooperative movement based in Mondragón. It retains its own tax system under the historic Economic Agreement (Concierto Económico).
