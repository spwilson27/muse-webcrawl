Medicare

Medicare is the name for health insurance programs administered in several countries. This article is a disambiguation overview; the best-known are the programs of the United States, Australia, and Canada.

By country

  • Australia: Medicare is the universal health care system funded by the Medicare levy, providing free or subsidised treatment since 1984.
  • Canada: "Medicare" informally refers to the publicly funded provincial health insurance systems established under the Canada Health Act.
  • United States: See Medicare (United States) — the federal program for people aged 65 and over and some younger people with disabilities, established in 1965.

Reform proposals to expand the U.S. program are discussed at Medicare for All.
