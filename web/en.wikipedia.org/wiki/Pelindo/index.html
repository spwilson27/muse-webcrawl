Pelindo


Full name: PT Pelabuhan Indonesia (Persero)
Type: State-owned enterprise
Industry: Port operations, logistics
Founded: 1 October 2021 (merger); origins 1960s
Headquarters: Jakarta, Indonesia

Pelindo, formally PT Pelabuhan Indonesia (Persero), is Indonesia's state-owned port operator and one of the largest port companies in the world by the number of ports managed. It was formed on 1 October 2021 by the merger of the four regional state port companies Pelindo I, II, III, and IV, as part of the Indonesian government's programme to integrate and modernise the country's port sector.

History

Port administration in Indonesia passed through colonial and early republican port authorities before being organised into state enterprises. From the 1980s–1990s, four regional companies covered the archipelago: Pelindo I (northern Sumatra and surroundings), Pelindo II (including Jakarta's Tanjung Priok, the country's busiest port), Pelindo III (Java eastward and Kalimantan ports including Surabaya), and Pelindo IV (eastern Indonesia, including Makassar). The 2021 merger unified them into a single entity to standardise services, reduce logistics costs, and strengthen Indonesia's competitiveness as a maritime nation.

Operations

Pelindo manages well over one hundred ports across Indonesia, handling container, bulk, liquid, and passenger traffic. After the merger it reorganised into business clusters and subsidiaries covering container terminals, non-container terminals, logistics, and marine and equipment services. Major gateways include Tanjung Priok (Jakarta), Tanjung Perak (Surabaya), Belawan (Medan), and Makassar, along with new facilities such as Makassar New Port. The company plays a central role in the government's sea-toll (tol laut) programme aimed at reducing price disparities between western and eastern Indonesia.
