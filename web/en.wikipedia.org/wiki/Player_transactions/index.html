Player transactions

Player transactions are the roster moves by which professional sports teams acquire, release, or exchange the rights to players. The main types include trades, transfers, free-agent signings, drafts, loans, and waivers.

Types

In North American leagues, a trade exchanges players, draft picks, or cash between teams, generally within rules set by a salary cap and the league's collective bargaining agreement. A player whose contract has expired may sign with any team as a free agent. In most of world football (soccer), by contrast, players move between clubs through transfers, in which the buying club pays a transfer fee to the selling club while the player is under contract, or signs the player on a free transfer after the contract ends; temporary moves are arranged as loans. Other mechanisms include entry drafts, expansion drafts, waivers (in which other teams may claim a released player's contract), and releases or retirements.

Regulation

Transactions are governed by league rules, transfer windows in association football, trade deadlines in North American leagues, and international regulations such as FIFA's rules on the status and transfer of players.
