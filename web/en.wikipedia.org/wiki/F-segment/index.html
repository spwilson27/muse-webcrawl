F-segment

The F-segment is the sixth and largest category in the European car segment classification system, corresponding to full-size luxury cars. F-segment vehicles sit above the E-segment (executive cars) and are typically flagship saloons of luxury marques.

Characteristics

F-segment cars are usually around five metres long or more, offer the highest levels of comfort, technology and performance in a manufacturer's range, and are frequently used as chauffeur-driven vehicles, often in long-wheelbase form.

Examples

  • Mercedes-Benz S-Class
  • BMW 7 Series
  • Audi A8
  • Lexus LS
  • Porsche Panamera
  • Bentley Flying Spur
  • Rolls-Royce Ghost

Market share

The segment is small by volume in Europe but highly profitable. Sales are dominated by the German premium manufacturers, with the Mercedes-Benz S-Class traditionally the best-seller of the class.
