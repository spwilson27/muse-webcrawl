Family Guy

Family Guy is an American animated sitcom created by Seth MacFarlane for the Fox Broadcasting Company. The series premiered on January 31, 1999, following Super Bowl XXXIII, and centers on the Griffins, a dysfunctional family living in the fictional city of Quahog, Rhode Island.

CreatorSeth MacFarlane
NetworkFox
PremieredJanuary 31, 1999
VoicesSeth MacFarlane, Alex Borstein, Seth Green, Mila Kunis, Mike Henry

Characters

The family consists of parents Peter and Lois Griffin, their children Meg, Chris, and Stewie, and their anthropomorphic pet dog, Brian. The show is known for its cutaway gags, surreal humor, and frequent pop-culture parodies.

History

Fox cancelled the series in 2002 after three seasons, but strong DVD sales and reruns on Adult Swim prompted its revival in 2005 — one of the first shows returned to air by such demand. It has since become one of the longest-running American animated series, spawning the spin-off The Cleveland Show and the video game and merchandise lines.
