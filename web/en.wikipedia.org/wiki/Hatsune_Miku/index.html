Hatsune Miku (Japanese: 初音ミク) is a Vocaloid software voicebank developed by Crypton Future Media, originally released in 2007 for Yamaha's Vocaloid 2 singing synthesiser engine. She is personified as a 16-year-old girl with long turquoise twin-tails, and her voice is sampled from voice actress Saki Fujita. Her name combines hatsu (first), ne (sound), and miku (future) — "the first sound of the future".

Popularity

Miku became a cultural phenomenon in Japan and internationally. Because Crypton released her under a permissive character licence, thousands of fans have produced songs, videos, and artwork featuring her, distributed through sites such as Nico Nico Douga and YouTube. Notable producers include ryo (supercell), whose song "Melt" helped propel her fame, and the track "Senbonzakura".

Concerts and appearances

Miku "performs" live concerts as a projected animated figure accompanied by a live band; the annual Magical Mirai and touring Miku Expo events draw large audiences worldwide. She has opened for Lady Gaga on tour, appeared on the Late Show with David Letterman in 2014, and was scheduled to perform at Coachella. She also appears in the Hatsune Miku: Project DIVA rhythm game series.
