The Jeep Grand Cherokee is a range of mid-size SUVs produced by Jeep since 1992. Developed under AMC and launched by Chrysler at the 1992 Detroit auto show — famously by driving through the convention center's glass windows — the Grand Cherokee helped define the premium SUV segment by combining unibody construction, genuine off-road capability, and car-like refinement.

Generations

  • ZJ (1993–1998): original model, with inline-six and V8 engines and Quadra-Trac four-wheel drive.
  • WJ (1999–2004): redesigned with a new 4.7 L PowerTech V8.
  • WK (2005–2010): added independent front suspension, Hemi V8 power, and the high-performance SRT8.
  • WK2 (2011–2021/22): based on the Mercedes-Benz ML platform; included the 707 hp supercharged Trackhawk.
  • WL (2021–present): offered in two-row, three-row Grand Cherokee L, and plug-in hybrid 4xe forms.
