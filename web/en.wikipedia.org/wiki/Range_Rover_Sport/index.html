Range Rover Sport

ManufacturerLand Rover (Jaguar Land Rover)
Production2005–present
ClassMid-size luxury SUV
GenerationsThree (L320, L494, L461)

The Range Rover Sport is a mid-size luxury SUV produced by the British manufacturer Land Rover, part of Jaguar Land Rover. Introduced in 2005, it sits below the flagship Range Rover in the brand's line-up and emphasises on-road performance and a more dynamic driving character while retaining off-road capability.

Generations

The first generation (L320, 2005–2013) was based on the Land Rover Discovery platform. The second generation (L494, 2013–2022) used an all-aluminium monocoque shared with the Range Rover, cutting weight substantially. The third generation (L461) was unveiled in 2022 with a minimalist design, mild-hybrid and plug-in hybrid powertrains, and a high-performance SV model.

Performance models

Range-topping versions have included the supercharged V8 SVR and the later SV, among the fastest SUVs of their time. The Sport has also been offered with diesel, inline-six and V8 engines, and holds records such as the fastest crossing of Saudi Arabia's Empty Quarter desert by a production SUV (2013).
