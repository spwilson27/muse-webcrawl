2010s in fashion

The fashion of the 2010s was shaped by the mass adoption of social media — especially Instagram — and by the global reach of fast fashion retailers. The decade moved from the skinny silhouettes inherited from the 2000s through a 1990s-revival "normcore" phase to the dominance of streetwear and athleisure, while late in the decade sustainability, gender-fluid clothing, and resale platforms became major forces.

Early 2010s (2010–2013)

Early-decade fashion continued 2000s trends: skinny jeans, ballet flats, maxi dresses, and hipster styling, alongside a preppy revival and the soft-grunge and "tumblr" aesthetics circulated on blogs. Colour blocking, peplum tops, and wedge sneakers were prominent womenswear trends, and Lady Gaga's avant-garde outfits (including the 2010 meat dress) drove popular interest in high fashion. Fast-fashion chains such as Zara, H&M, and Forever 21 made catwalk styles available within weeks.

Mid 2010s (2014–2016)

Normcore — deliberately plain, unbranded clothing — was widely discussed, followed by athleisure: leggings, sports bras, and trainers worn as everyday clothing, championed by brands such as Lululemon and by Adidas's and Nike's fashion lines. A strong 1990s revival brought chokers, crop tops, mom jeans, flannel shirts, and platform shoes back into the mainstream. Men's fashion saw jogger trousers, bomber jackets, and longline T-shirts. The rise of fashion influencers and bloggers displaced magazine editors as style arbiters, and brands began designing for social-media visibility.

Late 2010s (2017–2019)

Streetwear merged with luxury after collaborations such as Louis Vuitton × Supreme (2017); "chunky" or "dad" sneakers (e.g. Balenciaga's Triple S), logomania, oversized hoodies, and fanny packs worn cross-body became ubiquitous. Alessandro Michele's maximalist Gucci and Demna Gvasalia's Vetements defined high fashion. Growing criticism of fast fashion's environmental and labour impact fuelled the resale market (Depop, Poshmark, thredUp) and rental services, and by decade's end thrifted and vintage clothing had become a mainstream youth trend. Body positivity and greater size, racial, and gender diversity on runways were widely debated, and modest fashion gained prominence.

Hairstyles and accessories

Notable hairstyles included the men's undercut and man bun, women's ombré and balayage colour, "Instagram makeup" (heavy contouring, matte lips), and by the decade's end the return of curtain bangs and mullets. Accessories included statement necklaces, flower crowns at festivals, smartwatches, and wireless earbuds late in the decade.
