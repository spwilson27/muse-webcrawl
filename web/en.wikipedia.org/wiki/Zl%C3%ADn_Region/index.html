Zlín Region

Country: Czech Republic

Capital: Zlín

Area: about 3,964 km²

The Zlín Region (Czech: Zlínský kraj) is an administrative region of the Czech Republic, located in the east of the historical region of Moravia. It is named after its capital, the industrial city of Zlín.

Geography

The region borders Slovakia to the east and includes the White Carpathians and the Hostýn-Vsetín mountains. It is divided into the districts of Zlín, Kroměříž, Uherské Hradiště and Vsetín.

Economy and sights

Zlín grew around the Baťa shoe company in the early 20th century and retains distinctive functionalist factory and workers' housing architecture. Attractions include the pilgrimage basilica at Velehrad, the castle and gardens at Kroměříž (a UNESCO World Heritage Site), and the Wallachian Open Air Museum in Rožnov pod Radhoštěm.
