DBLP (formerly the Digital Bibliography & Library Project, originally a database and logic programming bibliography) is a computer science bibliography website and open bibliographic database. It provides bibliographic information on major computer science journals and conference proceedings, with curated author pages disambiguating researchers of the same name.

History

DBLP began in 1993 at the University of Trier, Germany, under Michael Ley, initially covering the database and logic programming research community. It grew to cover computer science broadly, indexing millions of publications. Since 2018, DBLP has been operated by the Schloss Dagstuhl – Leibniz Center for Informatics together with the University of Trier.

Features and data

The database indexes conference papers, journal articles, books, theses and other scholarly outputs in computer science. All of its bibliographic data is published as open data (including a large XML dump and an RDF/SPARQL service), making DBLP a standard source for bibliometric studies of computer science, for co-authorship network analysis, and for disambiguated author records that other services reuse.
