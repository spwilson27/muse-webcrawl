AFC Challenge League

The AFC Challenge League is an annual continental club football competition organised by the Asian Football Confederation (AFC). It is the third-tier club competition in Asia, below the AFC Champions League Elite and the AFC Champions League Two, and is contested by clubs from lower-ranked AFC member associations.

History

The competition was launched in the 2024–25 season as part of a major restructuring of Asian club football. It replaced, in part, the role of the former AFC President's Cup, which had served emerging football nations between 2005 and 2014. The inaugural edition featured 18 clubs and a centralised group stage, with the final played as a single match.

Format

Clubs enter based on their associations' rankings and domestic cup or league results. The tournament begins with a group stage hosted in centralised venues, followed by two-legged quarter-finals and semi-finals and a single-match final. The winner may earn a place in the following season's AFC Champions League Two group stage, subject to entry requirements.
