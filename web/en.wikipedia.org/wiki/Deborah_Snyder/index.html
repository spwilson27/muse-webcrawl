Deborah Snyder (née Johnson) is an American film producer. She is the wife and producing partner of director Zack Snyder and has produced most of his films, including 300, Watchmen, Man of Steel, Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice and Zack Snyder's Justice League.

Career

Snyder began in advertising before moving into film production. She met Zack Snyder while working on a commercial and the two later co-founded the production company Cruel and Unusual Films, renamed The Stone Quarry in 2019, through which they developed DC Comics adaptations for Warner Bros. and later projects for Netflix such as Army of the Dead and Rebel Moon.

Notable productions

Her producer and executive producer credits include Sucker Punch, Wonder Woman, Aquaman and The Suicide Squad. She was a prominent advocate for the release of the director's cut of Justice League, which premiered on HBO Max in 2021.
