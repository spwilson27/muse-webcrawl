Seaman (rank)

Seaman is a military rank used in many navies around the world. It is considered a junior enlisted or rating rank and is the equivalent of the NATO rank code OR-3/OR-4 in some navies. In the United States Navy and Coast Guard, Seaman (SN) is the pay grade E-3, above Seaman Apprentice (E-2) and Seaman Recruit (E-1) and below Petty Officer Third Class (E-4).

Usage

In the Royal Navy, the corresponding modern rating is Able Seaman (AB), part of a ladder that historically ran from Landsman and Ordinary Seaman up to Able Seaman. Other navies use equivalent titles such as Matelot (France) or Matrose (Germany).

Merchant service

In merchant navies, "seaman" similarly grades deck crew: an able seaman (AB) is a senior deck rating qualified for all routine deck duties, while an ordinary seaman (OS) is a junior rating still gaining experience.
