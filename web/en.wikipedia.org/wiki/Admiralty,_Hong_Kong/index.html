Admiralty (Chinese: 金鐘; Jyutping: Gam1 zung1; lit. "Golden Bell") is the eastern extension of the central business district of Hong Kong Island, on the coast between Central and Wan Chai. It forms part of the Central and Western District and takes its name from the former Admiralty Dock of the British Royal Navy's HMS Tamar naval base.

Features

Admiralty is home to major office towers, hotels, and shopping centres including Pacific Place and the Lippo Centre, as well as government buildings such as the Central Government Complex at Tamar, the High Court, and Queensway Plaza. Hong Kong Park occupies the former site of the Victoria Barracks.

Transport

Admiralty station is one of the MTR's busiest interchanges, serving the Island line, Tsuen Wan line, South Island line, and East Rail line. The area was a focal point of the 2014 Umbrella Movement protests, when protesters occupied Harcourt Road and surrounding streets, and again saw large gatherings during the 2019 protests.
