Nkrumah

Nkrumah is a Ghanaian surname of Akan origin. It most commonly refers to:

  • Kwame Nkrumah (1909–1972), the first Prime Minister and President of Ghana, a leading figure of Pan-Africanism who led the Gold Coast to independence from Britain in 1957.

Other people

  • Fathia Nkrumah (1932–2007), Egyptian-born First Lady of Ghana, wife of Kwame Nkrumah.
  • Samia Nkrumah (born 1960), Ghanaian politician, daughter of Kwame Nkrumah, chairperson of the Convention People's Party.
  • Sekou Nkrumah (born 1963), Ghanaian politician and activist, son of Kwame Nkrumah.
  • Gamal Nkrumah (born 1959), Ghanaian journalist, eldest son of Kwame Nkrumah.

Places and institutions named after Kwame Nkrumah

  • Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology, Kumasi, Ghana.
  • Kwame Nkrumah Mausoleum and Memorial Park, Accra.
  • Kwame Nkrumah Circle, a major interchange in Accra.
  • Nkrumah University, Kabwe, Zambia.
