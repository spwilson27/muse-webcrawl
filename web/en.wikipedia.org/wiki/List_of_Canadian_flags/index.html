List of Canadian flags

This is a list of flags used in Canada, including the national flag, provincial and territorial flags, flags of the Canadian Armed Forces, viceregal and governmental flags, and notable historical flags.

National flag

The National Flag of Canada, commonly known as the Maple Leaf flag, consists of a red field with a white square at its centre bearing a stylised red maple leaf. It was adopted on 15 February 1965, replacing the Canadian Red Ensign. The earlier Red Ensign, bearing the shield of the Arms of Canada on a red field with the Union Jack in the canton, had been in unofficial use since the 1860s and was authorised for use on land in 1945.

Other flags

The Canadian Armed Forces use distinctive flags for the Royal Canadian Navy (a white ensign with the national flag in the canton and a naval badge), the Canadian Army and the Royal Canadian Air Force. The Governor General's flag is blue with the viceregal crest. Municipal and Indigenous flags, such as the Franco-Ontarian flag and the flag of the Métis Nation, are also widely flown.
