Marbach am Neckar

Marbach am Neckar is a town in the district of Ludwigsburg, Baden-Württemberg, Germany, on the River Neckar northeast of Stuttgart. It is best known as the birthplace of the poet and dramatist Friedrich Schiller, born there in 1759.

Culture

Marbach hosts the Schiller National Museum and the German Literature Archive (Deutsches Literaturarchiv), one of the world's major literary archives, together with the Museum of Modern Literature. The town's population is roughly 16,000. Its preserved old town includes Schiller's birth house on Niklastorstraße.
