The 2010 Summer Youth Olympics, officially the I Summer Youth Olympic Games, were the inaugural edition of the Youth Olympic Games, held in Singapore from 14 to 26 August 2010. The Games brought together about 3,500 athletes aged 14 to 18 from 204 National Olympic Committees, competing in 201 events across 26 sports on the Olympic programme, alongside a Culture and Education Programme designed to promote Olympic values, healthy lifestyles and cultural exchange.

Selection

The Youth Olympic Games were conceived by International Olympic Committee president Jacques Rogge. Singapore won the right to host the first Summer edition in February 2008, defeating Moscow in the final IOC vote by 53 to 44. The city-state organised the Games on a budget of about S$387 million.

Venues and format

Events were staged at 18 venues, many of them existing or temporary facilities, with athletes housed in a Youth Olympic Village at Nanyang Technological University. Several sports used modified formats intended to encourage participation and learning, and mixed-gender and mixed-NOC team events were included — a first for an Olympic event. The opening ceremony was held on the world's largest floating stage at Marina Bay.

Medal table

China topped the medal table with 30 gold medals, ahead of Russia and South Korea, while host Singapore won its first Olympic-level medals, including silvers in swimming and table tennis-related events and bronzes across several sports. Because of the emphasis on participation over national rivalry, the IOC does not publish an official medal table.

Legacy

The Singapore Games established the template for subsequent Youth Olympics, including Nanjing 2014 and Buenos Aires 2018, and introduced innovations — such as 3x3 basketball — that later reached the senior Olympic programme.
