Lotus Elise

ManufacturerLotus Cars
Production1996–2021
LayoutRear mid-engine, rear-wheel drive
ClassSports car / roadster

The Lotus Elise is a two-seat, rear-wheel-drive sports car manufactured by British maker Lotus Cars from 1996 to 2021. Named after Elisa, the granddaughter of Romano Artioli, chairman of Lotus at the time of its launch, the Elise was built around an extruded aluminium chassis with a fibreglass body, keeping weight as low as 725 kg in early versions.

Series

The Series 1 (1996–2001) used Rover K-series engines and established the car's reputation for exceptional handling through lightness rather than power. The Series 2 (2001–2011) brought sharper styling and Toyota engines in later models, while the Series 3 (2011–2021) added variants including the supercharged Elise Sprint and Cup models. The platform also underpinned the Exige, the Opel Speedster/Vauxhall VX220 and, famously, the original Tesla Roadster, whose chassis derived from the Elise.

Legacy

Across 25 years about 35,000 Elises were built. Production ended in 2021 alongside the Exige and Evora as Lotus transitioned to its new generation of cars. The Elise is widely regarded as a modern classic and a benchmark lightweight driver's car.
