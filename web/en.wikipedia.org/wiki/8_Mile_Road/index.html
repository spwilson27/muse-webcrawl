8 Mile Road

8 Mile Road is a major east–west thoroughfare in the Metro Detroit region of Michigan, United States. It forms the boundary between Detroit (in Wayne County) to the south and the suburban communities of Oakland and Macomb counties to the north, and it is designated as part of state highway M-102 along much of its length.

Route

The road follows the baseline of the Michigan survey grid, eight miles north of downtown Detroit's origin point at Campus Martius. It runs for roughly 33 miles (53 km) from the western suburbs to Lake St. Clair in the east.

Cultural significance

Because Detroit to the south is a majority-Black city and the suburbs to the north were historically majority white, 8 Mile became a widely recognized symbol of racial and economic division in the region — a reputation reinforced by segregated housing patterns dating to redlining and a whites-only housing development wall built in 1941. The road was further popularized by the 2002 film 8 Mile, starring Detroit rapper Eminem.
