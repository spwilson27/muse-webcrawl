David Frost

David Frost
Born7 April 1939, Tenterden, Kent
Died31 August 2013
OccupationPresenter and interviewer
[Image: Frost in conversation during the Nixon interviews]

David Frost (1939–2013) was a British television presenter and journalist who rose with the satire boom (That Was the Week That Was, 1962), became a fixture of British political interviewing (Breakfast with Frost), and achieved a historic scoop with his 1977 interviews of disgraced former President Richard Nixon — dramatised in the play and film Frost/Nixon — in which Nixon conceded he had let the American people down.

Career

Frost moved between comedy, entertainment and serious interviewing on both sides of the Atlantic (The David Frost Show in the US), co-founded TV-am, and in later years hosted Through the Keyhole and Al Jazeera's Frost Over the World.
