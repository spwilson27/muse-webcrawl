Wizard (Middle-earth)

In J. R. R. Tolkien's legendarium, the Wizards, known as the Istari, are five Maiar spirits sent to Middle-earth in the Third Age to aid Elves and Men against Sauron. They took the form of old men, were forbidden to dominate by force, and were to guide by counsel and encouragement.

The Five Wizards

The best known are Gandalf the Grey (later the White), Saruman the White, and Radagast the Brown. The other two, the Blue Wizards — named Alatar and Pallando in later writings — travelled into the East and South and do not appear directly in The Hobbit or The Lord of the Rings.

Role

Saruman, head of the White Council and of the Order, fell to pride and sought the One Ring for himself. Gandalf remained faithful, helped organise the resistance to Sauron, died fighting a Balrog in Moria, and returned as Gandalf the White to complete his task before sailing West with the Ring-bearers.
