National Association of State Park Directors

Founded: 1962
Type: Non-profit association
Members: State park directors of all 50 U.S. states
Focus: State park policy and statistics

The National Association of State Park Directors (NASPD) is an American non-profit organisation whose members are the directors of the state park systems of all fifty states.

Purpose

The association promotes the state park movement, shares management expertise among state systems, and advocates for state parks at the national level. State parks collectively protect millions of acres and host hundreds of millions of visitors annually, and the NASPD works to highlight their recreational, conservation and economic contributions.

Activities

The NASPD compiles the Annual Information Exchange, the principal statistical report on state park visitation, acreage, budgets and facilities across the United States. It holds an annual conference for directors and senior staff and presents awards recognising distinguished state park leadership and programmes.
