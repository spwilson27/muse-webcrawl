The European People's Party (EPP) is a European political party with Christian democratic, liberal-conservative and conservative member parties. Founded in 1976, it brings together more than 80 parties and partners from over 40 countries and has been the largest political group in the European Parliament since 1999.

History

The EPP was founded in Luxembourg in 1976 by Christian democratic parties of the European Community. It later expanded to include conservative and centre-right parties from across Europe, including Forza Italia and Spain's People's Party. Its members have included long-serving leaders such as Helmut Kohl and Angela Merkel.

In the EU institutions

EPP politicians have held the presidencies of the European Commission (including Ursula von der Leyen) and the European Parliament, and EPP-affiliated heads of government take part in the party's pre-summit meetings before European Councils.
