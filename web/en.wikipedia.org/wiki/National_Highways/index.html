National Highways

National Highways is a government-owned company in England responsible for operating, maintaining and improving the strategic road network — the motorways and major A-roads of England. It was established in 2015 as Highways England and renamed National Highways in 2021. It is an executive agency sponsored by the Department for Transport.

Network

The strategic road network comprises about 4,300 miles of motorways and trunk roads, carrying roughly a third of all road traffic in England and two-thirds of heavy goods traffic.

Responsibilities

National Highways manages traffic, maintains road surfaces, bridges and tunnels, and delivers major road schemes, including "smart motorway" projects. It employs traffic officers who patrol the network and assist at incidents.
