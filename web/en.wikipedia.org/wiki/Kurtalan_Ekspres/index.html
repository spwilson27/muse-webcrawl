The Kurtalan Ekspresi (Kurtalan Express), officially the Güney Ekspresi (Southern Express), is an overnight inter-city passenger train operated by TCDD Taşımacılık in Turkey. It runs between Ankara and Kurtalan, in Siirt Province in southeastern Turkey, passing through Kayseri, Sivas, Malatya, Elazığ and Diyarbakır.

History and route

The service began in the 1940s as rail lines reached southeastern Anatolia; Kurtalan became the eastern terminus in 1944. For decades the train was one of the principal links between the capital and the southeast, carrying passengers, mail and goods through the rugged terrain of eastern Anatolia, including the Fırat (Euphrates) gorge crossings. The journey takes roughly 24 hours.

Departing Ankara, the train follows the Trans-Anatolian route east via Kırıkkale and Kayseri to Sivas, then Malatya and Elazığ, crossing to Diyarbakır before terminating at Kurtalan. The service offers seated coaches, couchettes (kuşetli) and sleeping cars, plus a dining car.

In popular culture

The train gave its name to Kurtalan Ekspres, the backing band formed in 1972 by Turkish rock pioneer Barış Manço; the group took the name from the train that ran through Manço's home region and continued performing after his death in 1999.
