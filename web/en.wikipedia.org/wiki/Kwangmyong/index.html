Kwangmyong

Kwangmyong (Korean for "bright light" or "bright star") is the national intranet service of North Korea, opened in the early 2000s. It provides email, news, and web-like content to approved users within the country while remaining separate from the global Internet.

Overview

The network hosts thousands of sites maintained by institutions such as the Korea Computer Center and the Grand People's Study House, including encyclopaedias, news services, and e-learning resources. Access is typically through workplace and university computer labs and some home connections via dial-up or local networks. The system uses the .kp domain space internally and a domestic web browser in the Red Star OS environment.
