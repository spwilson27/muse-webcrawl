Scarlets

Full name: Scarlets

Nickname: The Scarlets

Founded: 2003

Location: Llanelli, Wales

Ground: Parc y Scarlets

League: United Rugby Championship

The Scarlets are one of the four professional Welsh regional rugby union teams. Based in Llanelli, Carmarthenshire, they play in the United Rugby Championship and in European competitions organised by European Professional Club Rugby. The region represents the historic counties of Carmarthenshire, Ceredigion and Pembrokeshire in west Wales.

History

The Scarlets were formed in 2003 when the Welsh Rugby Union decided to regionalise the professional game, drawing heavily on the players and traditions of Llanelli RFC, whose famous scarlet jersey gave the new region its name. Llanelli RFC had been one of the most celebrated club sides in Wales, famously beating the touring All Blacks 9–3 at Stradey Park in 1972. The region played its home matches at Stradey Park until moving to the purpose-built Parc y Scarlets in 2008.

The Scarlets won the Celtic League in their inaugural 2003–04 season under coach Gareth Jenkins. Their greatest modern triumph came in the 2016–17 season, when they won the Pro12 title, defeating Munster 46–22 in the final at the Aviva Stadium in Dublin after a semi-final win over Leinster. The following season they reached the semi-finals of the European Rugby Champions Cup.

Stadium

Parc y Scarlets, opened in November 2008, has a capacity of just over 14,800 and lies in the Pemberton area of Llanelli. It replaced Stradey Park, the club's home for nearly 130 years.

Notable players

Scarlets players to have represented the British and Irish Lions include Jonathan Davies, Ken Owens, Liam Williams, Tadhg Beirne and Wyn Jones, while fly-half Stephen Jones was one of the region's most influential early captains.
