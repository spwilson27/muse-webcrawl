Robin (character)

Created byBob Kane, Bill Finger, Jerry Robinson
Alter egosDick Grayson, Jason Todd, Tim Drake, Damian Wayne, Stephanie Brown
PublisherDC Comics

Robin is the alias of several fictional superheroes appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. The character was originally created as the youthful sidekick and partner of Batman.

History

The first Robin, Dick Grayson, debuted in 1940 as a circus acrobat whose parents were murdered, taken in by Bruce Wayne. Grayson later became Nightwing. Jason Todd, the second Robin, was killed by the Joker in "A Death in the Family" (1988) and later revived as Red Hood. Tim Drake, Stephanie Brown, and Damian Wayne — Batman's son — have also held the mantle.

Characterisation

Robin wears a red, green, and yellow costume (later variations darker) and is known as the Boy Wonder. The character softened Batman's stories for younger readers, helped found the Teen Titans, and became a template for superhero sidekicks in popular culture.

In other media

Robin has appeared in the 1960s Batman TV series (Burt Ward), films, Teen Titans and Titans series, and video games.
