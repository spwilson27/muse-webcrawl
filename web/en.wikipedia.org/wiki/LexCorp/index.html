LexCorp is a fictional multinational conglomerate in the DC Comics universe, founded and run by Lex Luthor, the arch-enemy of Superman. Headquartered in the city of Metropolis, it is one of the largest and most powerful corporations in its fictional world, with interests spanning defence contracting, aerospace, energy, media, biotechnology, and real estate.

History in the comics

The modern incarnation of LexCorp was established in John Byrne's 1986 reboot of Superman, The Man of Steel, which recast Luthor from a mad scientist into a ruthless corporate tycoon modelled on contemporary business magnates — an interpretation that has dominated since. Luthor built the company from a small firm in Metropolis's Suicide Slum into a global empire, using it both as the legitimate face of his wealth and as a front for criminal and anti-Superman schemes. At its height LexCorp employed a large share of Metropolis's workforce, and its headquarters, the L-shaped LexCorp Tower, dominated the skyline.

In other media

LexCorp appears throughout Superman adaptations, including the animated series, Smallville (where it is founded by Lionel Luthor), and the film Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice (2016), which depicts it as a technology company. Its public image as a philanthropic employer contrasts with Luthor's hidden agendas, a standard device in the stories.
