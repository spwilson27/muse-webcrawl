Centro Region, Portugal

The Centro Region (Portuguese: Região Centro) is one of the statistical regions of mainland Portugal, corresponding to a NUTS II region of the European Union. It covers much of central Portugal, from the Atlantic coast to the Spanish border, and its largest city and administrative seat is Coimbra.

Geography and economy

The region includes the historic provinces of Beira Litoral, Beira Baixa, and parts of Beira Alta and Estremadura. Its landscape ranges from the beaches of the Costa de Prata and the pine forests of Leiria to the Serra da Estrela, mainland Portugal's highest mountain range. The economy combines tourism, agriculture and wine (notably the Dão and Bairrada regions), manufacturing around Aveiro and Leiria, and the services centred on the University of Coimbra, founded in 1290.

Subdivisions

The region is divided into intermunicipal communities including Região de Coimbra, Região de Aveiro, Região de Leiria, Beiras e Serra da Estrela, Beira Baixa, Médio Tejo, Oeste, and Viseu Dão Lafões.
