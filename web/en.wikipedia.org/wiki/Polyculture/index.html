Polyculture

Polyculture is the practice of growing multiple crop species in the same space, in imitation of natural ecosystems, as opposed to monoculture. It includes intercropping, companion planting, cover crops and integrated livestock systems.

Polyculture

Benefits and challenges

Benefits include pest and disease suppression, better nutrient use, soil health, biodiversity and resilience. Challenges include management complexity and mechanisation difficulties.

Examples

The Three Sisters (maize, beans, squash) in the Americas; rice-fish systems in Asia; agroforestry and permaculture designs. In aquaculture, polyculture and integrated multi-trophic aquaculture raise complementary species together.
