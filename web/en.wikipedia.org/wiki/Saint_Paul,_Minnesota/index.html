Saint Paul (abbreviated St. Paul) is the capital city of the U.S. state of Minnesota and the county seat of Ramsey County. Located on the east bank of the Mississippi River, adjoining Minneapolis, it is the smaller of the "Twin Cities" but the older in character, with a compact, hilly street plan and a large stock of Victorian architecture. The city is home to the Minnesota State Capitol, designed by Cass Gilbert and completed in 1905.

History

The area was inhabited by Dakota peoples; the burial mounds at present-day Indian Mounds Regional Park date back roughly two thousand years. In the 1830s, squatters — including the whiskey trader Pierre "Pig's Eye" Parrant — settled near Fort Snelling; in 1841 Father Lucien Galtier built a log chapel dedicated to St. Paul and urged the settlement take the saint's name. Saint Paul became the capital of the Minnesota Territory in 1849 and of the state in 1858. As the head of practical navigation on the Mississippi (owing to the falls at Minneapolis and the river's bluffs), it boomed as a steamboat landing and rail hub; James J. Hill's Great Northern Railway made it a gateway to the Northwest, and the 1920s–30s saw the city serve as a notorious haven for gangsters under a corrupt police "layover" system.

Geography and neighborhoods

Saint Paul is hillier and greener than Minneapolis, with neighborhoods such as Summit Avenue — among the best-preserved Victorian streets in America, once home to F. Scott Fitzgerald — Cathedral Hill, the Irish West Side (West Side across the river), and Frogtown. The Cathedral of Saint Paul (1915), atop Cathedral Hill, is one of the largest churches in the United States. The city hosts the Minnesota State Fair in Falcon Heights, adjacent to Saint Paul, billed as one of the largest state fairs in the country, and the Saint Paul Winter Carnival, begun in 1886.

Economy and education

State government is the anchor employer, along with health care, education, and companies such as Ecolab, founded in Saint Paul in 1923. Colleges include the University of St. Thomas, Macalester College, Hamline University (Minnesota's oldest, founded 1854), and Metropolitan State University. The city is also the hometown of cartoonist Charles M. Schulz, creator of Peanuts.
