Cyberpunk (role-playing game)

Cyberpunk
Designer: Mike Pondsmith
Publisher: R. Talsorian Games
First edition: 1988 (Cyberpunk 2013)
Editions: 2020, RED; Setting: Night City

Cyberpunk is a dystopian tabletop role-playing game designed by Mike Pondsmith and published by R. Talsorian Games. Set largely in the fictional Night City, California, it defined the look of cyberpunk gaming: megacorporations, cyberware, street mercenaries and the motto "high tech, low life".

Editions

The first edition (1988) was retroactively called Cyberpunk 2013. Cyberpunk 2020 (1990) became the classic version, using the Interlock system with roles such as Solo, Netrunner, Fixer, Rockerboy and Nomad, and rules for cyberpsychosis from excessive augmentation. Cyberpunk Red (2020) bridges the timeline toward the video game Cyberpunk 2077 by CD Projekt Red, which greatly expanded the franchise's audience.

Gameplay

Characters are edgerunners hired for dangerous jobs; combat is lethal, style and reputation matter, and netrunning depicts hacking as a virtual cyberspace.
