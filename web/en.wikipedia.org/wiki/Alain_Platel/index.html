Alain Platel (born 9 April 1959) is a Belgian choreographer and director. Trained as a remedial educationalist working with children with disabilities, he founded the Ghent-based collective les ballets C de la B in 1984, which became one of the most influential contemporary dance companies in Europe.

Work

Platel's productions mix dance, theatre, music and performers of widely varied backgrounds, professional and amateur. Notable works include Bonjour Madame (1993), La tristeza complice (1995), Iets op Bach (1998), Wolf (2003), vsprs (2006), pitié! (2008), Gardenia (2010), C(H)ŒURS (2012) and tauberbach (2014), often created with composers such as Fabrizio Cassol and Steven Prengels. His work is noted for its emotional intensity, collage-like structure and engagement with marginalised lives.

Recognition

He has received numerous European theatre and dance prizes and his productions tour internationally to major festivals and venues.
