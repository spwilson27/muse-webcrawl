Damien Chazelle

Damien Sayre Chazelle (born 19 January 1985) is an American film director and screenwriter known for Whiplash (2014), La La Land (2016), First Man (2018), and Babylon (2022). At 32 he became the youngest winner of the Academy Award for Best Director.


Born: 19 January 1985, Providence, Rhode Island
Alma mater: Harvard University

Early life

Born in Providence to professor Bernard Chazelle and writer Celia Martin, he aspired to be a jazz drummer, inspiring Whiplash. At Harvard he made Guy and Madeline on a Park Bench (2009).

Breakthrough

Whiplash won three Oscars; La La Land won six. Babylon, about early Hollywood, underperformed commercially but gained critical following.

Style

Kinetic editing, jazz scores by Justin Hurwitz, and themes of ambition and sacrifice.
