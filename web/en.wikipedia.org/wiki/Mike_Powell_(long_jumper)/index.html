Mike Powell (long jumper)

Mike Powell
Born: 10 November 1963, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, US
Sport: Track and field (long jump)
Personal best: 8.95 m (world record)

Michael Anthony Powell (born 10 November 1963) is an American former track and field athlete and the holder of the men's long jump world record, 8.95 metres (29 feet 4¼ inches), set at the 1991 World Championships in Tokyo. The mark, which broke Bob Beamon's iconic 23-year-old record, remains unbroken more than three decades later — the longest-standing long jump record in history.

Career

Powell won the silver medal at the 1988 Seoul Olympics behind Carl Lewis, with whom he developed one of the sport's great rivalries. At the Tokyo World Championships on 30 August 1991, in a celebrated duel, Lewis produced the finest series of jumps in history, including a wind-assisted 8.91 m, but Powell responded with a legal 8.95 m in the fifth round to take both the gold medal and the world record.

Powell retained his world title at Stuttgart in 1993, took bronze at Gothenburg in 1995, and added a second Olympic silver at Barcelona in 1992. He retired after failing to qualify for the 1996 Olympic final, later working as a coach, athletics ambassador and broadcaster.
