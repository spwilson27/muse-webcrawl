Storey

A storey (British English; "story" in American English) is any level part of a building with a floor that can be occupied. Buildings are described by their number of storeys, such as "a ten-storey building".

Numbering conventions

Conventions differ worldwide: in the UK the first level above ground is the "first floor" and the ground level is the "ground floor", while in the United States the ground level is the "first floor". Some buildings skip the number 13 (triskaidekaphobia) or, in Chinese-influenced markets, the number 4.

Height

A typical storey is about 3 metres in residential and 3.5–4 metres in commercial construction, including the floor slab and ceiling void. Lofts, mezzanines, and basements may or may not count as storeys under building codes.
