Hickstead (2 March 1996 – 6 November 2011) was a Dutch Warmblood stallion ridden in show jumping by Canadian rider Eric Lamaze. Widely regarded as one of the greatest show jumping horses of all time, Hickstead and Lamaze won individual gold and team silver at the 2008 Beijing Olympics, and individual bronze at the 2010 World Equestrian Games, where Hickstead was named Best Horse after jumping clear rounds with four different riders in the final.

Career

Bred in the Netherlands, Hickstead was purchased for Lamaze in 2004. The pair won major grands prix around the world, including the Spruce Meadows 'Masters' and the grand prix of Aachen. In 2011 they won the $1 million CN International at Spruce Meadows for a second time. Hickstead was known for his exceptional scope, carefulness and competitive temperament despite his relatively small size.

Death and honours

Hickstead collapsed and died of an aortic rupture on 6 November 2011, moments after completing a round at a World Cup event in Verona, Italy; the competition was cancelled in his honour. He was inducted into Canada's Sports Hall of Fame in 2012, and a statue of Hickstead and Lamaze stands at Spruce Meadows.
