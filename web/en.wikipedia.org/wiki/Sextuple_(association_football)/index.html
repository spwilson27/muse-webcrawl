Sextuple (association football)

In association football, a sextuple is the achievement of winning six major trophies in a single calendar year or season: a club's domestic league, domestic cup, league cup (where contested), continental championship, domestic super cup or equivalent, and the FIFA Club World Cup or Intercontinental Cup.

Achievements

The first club to achieve the feat was FC Barcelona in 2009 under Pep Guardiola, winning La Liga, the Copa del Rey, the Supercopa de España, the UEFA Champions League, the UEFA Super Cup, and the FIFA Club World Cup. Bayern Munich repeated the achievement for 2020, completing the set in February 2021 with the delayed Club World Cup final.

Context

The sextuple is considered rarer than the treble because it requires success in every competition entered, including one-off super cup matches and an intercontinental tournament held months after the continental title.
