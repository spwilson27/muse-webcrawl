Moldova

Moldova, officially the Republic of Moldova, is a landlocked country in Eastern Europe between Romania and Ukraine, mostly lying between the Prut and Dniester rivers (historical Bessarabia). It has a population of about 2.5 million (excluding the breakaway region of Transnistria) and its capital is Chișinău.

Moldova is Europe's poorest country by many measures and one of its great wine producers — the Mileștii Mici cellars hold the world's largest wine collection. Romanian-speaking Moldovans debate identity between Romanian and distinct Moldovan nationhood. Transnistria, a Russian-backed separatist strip with Russian troops, broke away in a 1992 war. Since 2020, under President Maia Sandu, Moldova has moved decisively toward EU membership, granted candidate status in 2022 amid the shock of Russia's invasion of neighbouring Ukraine.
