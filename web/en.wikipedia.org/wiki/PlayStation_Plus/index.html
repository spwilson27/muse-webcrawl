PlayStation Plus

DeveloperSony Interactive Entertainment
Launched29 June 2010
PlatformsPlayStation 3, PlayStation Vita, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5
TypeSubscription gaming service

PlayStation Plus is a subscription service offered by Sony Interactive Entertainment for PlayStation consoles. Launched in 2010, it provides members with benefits including access to online multiplayer gaming (required for most paid games on PS4 and PS5), monthly downloadable games, exclusive discounts on the PlayStation Store, and cloud storage for saved games.

Tiers

In 2022 Sony restructured the service into three tiers: Essential, which continues the original benefits; Extra, which adds a downloadable catalogue of PS4 and PS5 games; and Premium (Deluxe in some regions), which additionally offers a catalogue of classic games from earlier PlayStation consoles, game trials, and cloud streaming.

History

The service was announced at E3 2010 and initially offered free monthly games, early access to betas and demos, and automatic updates. It absorbed Sony's former streaming service PlayStation Now into its Premium tier in 2022. Membership has numbered in the tens of millions.
