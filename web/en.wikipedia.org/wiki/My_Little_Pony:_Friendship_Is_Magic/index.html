My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic

My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic is an animated television series produced by Hasbro Studios (later Allspark Animation) and DHX Media, based on Hasbro's My Little Pony toy line. Developed by Lauren Faust, the series premiered on October 10, 2010, on The Hub (later Discovery Family) and concluded on October 12, 2019, after nine seasons and 222 episodes.

Premise

The series is set in Equestria, a kingdom inhabited by ponies and other creatures. Twilight Sparkle, a studious unicorn (later an alicorn princess), is sent by Princess Celestia to the town of Ponyville to study the magic of friendship. There she befriends five ponies — Applejack, Rainbow Dash, Rarity, Fluttershy and Pinkie Pie — who together represent the Elements of Harmony: honesty, loyalty, generosity, kindness, laughter, and magic itself. With the help of her dragon assistant Spike, Twilight sends reports on friendship lessons as the friends face adventures and threats to Equestria.

Production

Hasbro hired Faust, who had worked on The Powerpuff Girls and Foster's Home for Imaginary Friends, to create a show that would appeal to girls and their parents together. The series was animated in Flash by DHX Media in Vancouver, with songs by Daniel Ingram. Faust left after the second season, but the creative team she assembled continued the series, which spawned the spin-off Equestria Girls film franchise and a 2017 theatrical feature film.

Reception and fandom

The series was acclaimed by critics for its writing, characters, music and humour, and it unexpectedly attracted a large adult fandom — the "bronies" — whose conventions, charity work and creative output drew wide media attention. It is regarded as a landmark of 2010s animation and a major influence on later family-oriented cartoons.
