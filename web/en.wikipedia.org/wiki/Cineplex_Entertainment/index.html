Cineplex Inc. (formerly Cineplex Entertainment) is a Canadian entertainment company headquartered in Toronto, Ontario, and the largest motion-picture exhibitor in Canada, operating roughly 170 theatres and some 1,700 screens under brands including Cineplex Cinemas, Cineplex Odeon, SilverCity, Galaxy Cinemas and Scotiabank Theatres. It also runs the Scene loyalty programme (Scene+), the Cineplex Store digital video service, and location-based entertainment venues such as The Rec Room and Playdium.

History

The company traces its origin to 1979, when Garth Drabinsky and Nat Taylor opened the first Cineplex multiplex — an 18-screen theatre in Toronto's Eaton Centre — and built the Cineplex Odeon chain across North America. After passing through the hands of MCA/Universal and the Onex Corporation, the business was reshaped in the 2000s: in 2005 Cineplex Galaxy acquired the Famous Players chain from Viacom, making it Canada's dominant exhibitor.

In December 2019 the British exhibitor Cineworld agreed to acquire Cineplex for about C$2.8 billion; the deal collapsed during the COVID-19 pandemic shutdowns in 2020, and Cineplex later won a court judgment (subsequently upheld on appeal) for damages of about C$1.24 billion against Cineworld.

Operations

Cineplex theatres offer premium formats including UltraAVX, IMAX, Dolby Atmos, 4DX, ScreenX and VIP Cinemas with licensed service. Beyond films, the company broadcasts live events (opera, theatre, sports and esports) and derives growing revenue from food service, amusement gaming and media advertising.
