Product management

Product management is the business process of planning, developing, launching, and managing a product or service. It includes the entire lifecycle of a product, from ideation to marketing, growth, and maturity or decline. A product manager is the person who identified and articulates a customer need or opportunity, and is responsible for the strategy, roadmap, and feature definition of a product.


Product managers own the roadmap and prioritise features.

Role

Product managers sit at the intersection of business, technology, and user experience. They conduct market and customer research, set priorities, write requirements, work with engineers and designers during development, coordinate launches with marketing and sales, and analyse results after release. The role originated in brand management at consumer goods firms such as Procter & Gamble, whose 1931 "brand man" memo is often cited as a founding document, and expanded greatly with the software industry.

Practices

Common practices include maintaining a product roadmap, managing a prioritised backlog, defining key performance indicators, running experiments, and conducting customer interviews and usability tests. In software companies using agile methods, product managers work closely with product owners and engineering teams to decide what gets built in each iteration.
