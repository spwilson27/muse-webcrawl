Pokémon (TV series)

Pokémon
Genre: Adventure, comedy-drama
Country: Japan
Original run: 1 April 1997 – present
Episodes: over 1,200

Pokémon, known in Japan as Pocket Monsters, is a Japanese anime television series produced by OLM and based on the Pokémon video game franchise. The series premiered on TV Tokyo in April 1997 and follows the adventures of Pokémon Trainers as they catch and train creatures called Pokémon and compete in battles and leagues.

Overview

For most of its run, the series followed Ash Ketchum, a ten-year-old from Pallet Town, and his partner Pikachu on a journey to become a Pokémon Master, travelling through regions modelled on the games accompanied by changing companions such as Misty, Brock, May, Dawn, and Serena. After Ash's story concluded in 2023 with his victory in the World Coronation Series, the series continued as Pokémon Horizons with new protagonists Liko and Roy.

Reception and impact

The anime was central to the worldwide "Pokémania" of the late 1990s and has been dubbed into dozens of languages. A 1997 episode, "Dennō Senshi Porygon", caused seizures in hundreds of Japanese children due to rapidly flashing lights, an incident that led to new animation guidelines and the episode's worldwide withdrawal.
