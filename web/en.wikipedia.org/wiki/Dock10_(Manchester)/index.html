Dock10 (stylised dock10) is a television studio complex and media technology company based at MediaCityUK in Salford Quays, Greater Manchester, England. It is one of the largest television studio facilities in the United Kingdom.

Facilities

The complex contains ten studios, including HQ1, one of the largest television studios in Europe at around 12,500 square feet. The studios are equipped for high-definition and 4K production, with extensive post-production, virtual studio and media-management services. A dedicated "virtual studio" capability uses real-time rendering for augmented-reality and virtual sets.

Productions

Dock10 hosts flagship programmes for the BBC, ITV, Channel 4 and other broadcasters, including The Voice UK, Countdown (recorded for Channel 4), Match of the Day and numerous entertainment, sport and children's shows. It became a key part of the BBC's move of departments such as BBC Sport and BBC Children's to Salford in 2011–2012.
