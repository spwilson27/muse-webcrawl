David Tennant (born David John McDonald, 18 April 1971) is a Scottish actor. He is best known for portraying the tenth incarnation of the Doctor in the BBC sci-fi series Doctor Who (2005–2010), a role to which he returned in 2022 as the fourteenth Doctor for the programme's 60th anniversary specials. His other notable screen roles include DI Alec Hardy in the crime drama Broadchurch (2013–2017), Kilgrave in the Marvel series Jessica Jones (2015–2019), and the demon Crowley in Good Omens (2019–present). He is also an accomplished stage actor, particularly in Shakespeare, and a prolific voice performer, notably as Scrooge McDuck in DuckTales (2017–2021).

Early life

Tennant was born in Bathgate, West Lothian, and grew up in Ralston, Renfrewshire, the son of a Church of Scotland minister. A Doctor Who fan from childhood, he decided at age three or four to become an actor. He trained at the Royal Scottish Academy of Music and Drama (now the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland), and adopted the stage name "Tennant" — inspired by Pet Shop Boys singer Neil Tennant — because another David McDonald was already registered with the actors' union Equity.

Career

Theatre

After early television work including Takin' Over the Asylum (1994), Tennant built a stage reputation with the Royal Shakespeare Company, playing roles such as Touchstone in As You Like It and Romeo. His 2008 Hamlet for the RSC in Stratford-upon-Avon was widely acclaimed. Later stage work includes Much Ado About Nothing (2011) opposite Catherine Tate, Don Juan in Soho (2017), Good (2022), and Macbeth (2023–2024) at the Donmar Warehouse and in the West End.

Doctor Who

Tennant took over as the Doctor at the end of the 2005 series, following Christopher Eccleston. His tenure under showrunner Russell T Davies coincided with the show's peak popularity, winning multiple National Television Awards and, in polls of fans, routinely ranking as the most popular Doctor. He departed in the 2010 special "The End of Time", returned for the 50th anniversary episode "The Day of the Doctor" (2013), and returned again in 2022–2023, playing a new-but-familiar incarnation, the Fourteenth Doctor, across three 60th anniversary specials.

Other screen work

Tennant played Barty Crouch Jr. in Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire (2005), and starred in Casanova (2005), Blackpool (2004), Single Father (2010), The Politician's Husband (2013) and Des (2020), in which his portrayal of serial killer Dennis Nilsen won an International Emmy Award for Best Actor. In Broadchurch, his pairing with Olivia Colman became one of British television's most praised partnerships. He also hosts the podcast David Tennant Does a Podcast With....

Personal life

Tennant is married to actress Georgia Moffett, daughter of Fifth Doctor actor Peter Davison; they have five children. He is a supporter of various charities and has served as a patron of the Association for International Cancer Research and other causes.
