Music of Austin, Texas

The music of Austin, Texas, has earned the city its official slogan, "The Live Music Capital of the World," a title adopted in 1991 after research showed Austin had more live music venues per capita than any other American city. Austin's music scene spans country, blues, rock, indie, Tejano, punk and many other genres.

History

Austin's musical reputation grew from honky-tonks and dance halls, the blues clubs of East Austin, and venues such as the Armadillo World Headquarters in the 1970s, which hosted progressive country acts and helped popularise the "cosmic cowboy" scene associated with artists like Willie Nelson. Stevie Ray Vaughan brought Austin blues international attention in the 1980s.

Festivals and venues

The city hosts major events including South by Southwest (SXSW), founded in 1987, the Austin City Limits Music Festival, and the long-running television series Austin City Limits, the longest-running music programme in American television history. Well-known venues include Stubb's, Antone's, the Continental Club and Mohawk.
