Ertuğrul

Born: c. 1191 (traditionally)
Died: c. 1281, Söğüt, Sultanate of Rum
Known for: Father of Osman I, founder of the Ottoman Empire
Father: Suleyman Shah (per later Ottoman tradition)
Children: Osman I, Saru Batu Savcı Bey, Gündüz Bey

Ertuğrul (Ottoman Turkish: ارطغرل; Turkish: Ertuğrul Gazi; often with the title Gazi) was, according to Ottoman tradition, the father of Osman I, the founder of the Ottoman Empire. He is said to have lived in the 13th century and to have ruled the Kayı tribe, a branch of the Oghuz Turks, in the frontier region of north-western Anatolia.

Biography according to tradition

Little is known with certainty about Ertuğrul's life. According to later Ottoman tradition, his father was Suleyman Shah, leader of the Kayı tribe of Oghuz Turks, who fled from western Central Asia to Anatolia to escape the Mongol conquests. After Suleyman Shah drowned in the Euphrates River, the tribe returned to its homeland, while Ertuğrul and his brother Dündar remained in Anatolia with a band of some 400 horsemen.

According to the earliest Ottoman chronicles, Ertuğrul came to the aid of the Seljuk Sultanate of Rûm in a battle against the Byzantines or Mongols, and in reward the Seljuk sultan Ala ad-Din Kayqubad I granted him lands around Karaca Dağ, near present-day Ankara. He later received the town of Söğüt, on the frontier with the Byzantine Empire, which became the nucleus of the future Ottoman state.

Historicity

No contemporary sources mention Ertuğrul; the earliest references date from more than a century after his supposed death. Historians such as Halil İnalcık and others have debated which elements of his biography are historical and which are later dynastic legend constructed to legitimise the Ottoman house. A coin attributed to Osman naming his father as Ertuğrul provides some of the strongest evidence for his existence.

Tomb and legacy

Ertuğrul's tomb in Söğüt is a major site of pilgrimage. An annual festival, the Ertuğrul Gazi Commemoration and Söğüt Festival, is held there each September. Items said to belong to him are preserved in museums in Istanbul.

In popular culture

Ertuğrul is the hero of the Turkish television series Diriliş: Ertuğrul (2014–2019), which dramatises his life and achieved enormous popularity in Turkey and internationally, particularly in Pakistan and the wider Muslim world. He is played in the series by Engin Altan Düzyatan.
