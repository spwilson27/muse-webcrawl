Jaguar Land Rover Automotive PLC is a British multinational automobile manufacturer which produces luxury vehicles and sport utility vehicles under the Jaguar and Land Rover marques. It is a subsidiary of India's Tata Motors, which bought both businesses from Ford in 2008.

History

Jaguar and Land Rover had passed through British Leyland and, in Land Rover's case, BMW, before Ford brought them together in its Premier Automotive Group. Tata's purchase for $2.3 billion in 2008 was followed by substantial investment and strong growth in China and other markets, driven by models such as the Range Rover Evoque, Jaguar F-Type and the all-electric Jaguar I-Pace, which won European and World Car of the Year awards in 2019.

Operations and strategy

The company builds vehicles in the United Kingdom (Solihull, Castle Bromwich, Halewood), Slovakia, Brazil, China and India. Its "Reimagine" strategy announced in 2021 aimed to reinvent Jaguar as an all-electric luxury brand and to electrify Land Rover's Range Rover, Defender and Discovery families, though timelines were later revised.
