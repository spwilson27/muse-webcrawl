Canwest

Canwest Global Communications Corporation, commonly known simply as Canwest, was a major Canadian media conglomerate based in Winnipeg, Manitoba. Founded in 1974 by Izzy Asper, it grew to own the Global Television Network, the National Post newspaper, the Southam newspaper chain, and numerous specialty channels and international assets.

Growth

Starting from a single television station purchased from North Dakota, Asper built Canwest through acquisitions including Western International Communications (2000) and the Hollinger newspaper properties, briefly becoming an international media company with holdings in Australia (Network Ten), New Zealand and elsewhere.

Collapse

Heavy debt taken on for expansion, Asper's death in 2003, and the 2008 advertising downturn pushed Canwest into creditor protection in 2009. Its broadcasting assets were sold to Shaw Media in 2010 (later acquired by Corus Entertainment), and its newspapers to the newly formed Postmedia Network.

Canwest's rise and fall is a prominent case study in Canadian media concentration debates.
