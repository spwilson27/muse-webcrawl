Diplo

Thomas Wesley Pentz (born November 10, 1978), known professionally as Diplo, is an American DJ and record producer. He is a co-founder of the electronic dancehall group Major Lazer, one half of the supergroup LSD (with Sia and Labrinth), one half of Jack Ü (with Skrillex), and one half of Silk City (with Mark Ronson). He founded the record label Mad Decent in 2006 and has produced for artists including Beyoncé, Madonna, Justin Bieber, M.I.A. and Usher.

Career

Pentz grew up in Florida and was exposed to Miami bass music. After moving to Philadelphia he worked as a DJ and schoolteacher, gaining attention with the Hollertronix parties he ran with DJ Lowbudget and with his 2004 debut album Florida. His work with the British-Sri Lankan artist M.I.A., including co-producing the Grammy-nominated "Paper Planes" (2007), brought him international prominence.

In 2009 he formed Major Lazer with the producer Switch, initially as a fictional Jamaican character fronting a dancehall project; the group's later hits included "Lean On" (2015) with DJ Snake and MØ, one of the most streamed songs of its era. As Jack Ü with Skrillex he released the 2015 album that included "Where Are Ü Now" with Justin Bieber. In 2018 he formed the supergroup LSD with Sia and Labrinth, and Silk City with Mark Ronson, whose single "Electricity" (with Dua Lipa) won a Grammy Award.

Mad Decent and other work

Diplo founded the Los Angeles-based label Mad Decent in 2006, which helped popularise Brazilian baile funk and moombahton internationally and released artists such as Dillon Francis and Baauer. He has also released solo material under his own name, including country-influenced work credited to "Thomas Wesley", and is known for an extensive touring schedule across the global festival circuit.
