List of monastic houses in Wales

This is a list of monastic houses in Wales. Welsh monasticism began with early medieval "clas" communities and flowered with Cistercian foundations patronised by Welsh princes and Norman lords alike, before the Dissolution of the 1530s.

Notable houses

  • Tintern Abbey — Cistercian, founded 1131 in the Wye Valley; celebrated by Wordsworth.
  • Strata Florida — Cistercian; cultural and political centre of Welsh Wales, burial place of princes.
  • Valle Crucis Abbey — Cistercian, near Llangollen.
  • Margam Abbey — Cistercian, in Glamorgan.
  • Neath Abbey — Savigniac/Cistercian; once the largest abbey in Wales.
  • St Davids — cathedral and monastic centre of St David, patron saint of Wales.
  • Llanthony Priory — Augustinian, in the Black Mountains.
  • Cymer, Whitland, Aberconwy, Basingwerk — further Cistercian houses.

Later history

After the Dissolution most Welsh houses fell into ruin; in the 20th century new communities were founded, including the Benedictines of Caldey Island and the Cistercians who succeeded them there.
