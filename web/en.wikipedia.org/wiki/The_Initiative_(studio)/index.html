The Initiative (studio)

The Initiative was an American video game development studio based in Santa Monica, California, founded by Microsoft in 2018 as part of Xbox Game Studios. It was led by Darrell Gallagher, formerly of Crystal Dynamics.

History

Announced at E3 2018, the studio recruited veteran developers from studios such as Naughty Dog, Santa Monica Studio, and Rockstar Games. At The Game Awards 2020, The Initiative announced it was developing a new Perfect Dark game, later co-developed with Crystal Dynamics.

Closure

In July 2025, amid widespread layoffs at Microsoft's gaming division, The Initiative was closed and development of Perfect Dark was cancelled.
