Sesame Street


The cast of Sesame Street (placeholder image)

Sesame Street is an American educational children's television series that combines live action, sketch comedy, animation and puppetry. Created by Joan Ganz Cooney and Lloyd Morrisett, it premiered on 10 November 1969 on National Educational Television (later PBS) and is produced by Sesame Workshop (formerly the Children's Television Workshop). It is famous for its Muppet characters created by Jim Henson, including Big Bird, Oscar the Grouch, Bert and Ernie, Cookie Monster, Grover and — from the 1980s onward — Elmo.

Format and educational aims

Set on a fictional urban street, the show was designed from the outset using educational research: formative studies shaped scripts to teach preschool children letters, numbers, and pro-social lessons, with special attention to disadvantaged inner-city children. Its fast-paced magazine format, modelled partly on television advertising, mixed human cast members with Muppets in short segments, songs and celebrity guest appearances. Episodes are produced with a written curriculum and extensive summative research, a model widely credited with demonstrable positive effects on school readiness.

History and influence

One of the longest-running shows in television history, Sesame Street has aired over fifty seasons and won more Emmy Awards than any other children's programme. Since 2016 new episodes have premiered on HBO (later Max) before airing on PBS. Numerous international co-productions — with local characters and curricula — have been made in dozens of countries, making it one of the most widely diffused educational programmes in the world.
