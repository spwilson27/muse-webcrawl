John Cowan (computer programmer)

John Cowan is an American computer programmer and linguist known for his work on the Scheme programming language and on the Lojban constructed language. He was the editor of the Revised5 Report on the Algorithmic Language Scheme (R5RS) and a major contributor to the Scheme standardization process.

Scheme

Cowan served as editor of R5RS, published in 1998, the standard that defined Scheme for over a decade. He has also worked on SRFIs (Scheme Requests for Implementation) and on the R7RS standardization effort, and maintains the Snow package system for Scheme.

Lojban

Cowan is a prominent figure in the Lojban community. He wrote The Complete Lojban Language, the reference grammar of Lojban, and served as president of the Logical Language Group. His work bridges programming language design and the study of constructed languages.
