Overview

Livingstone is a city in Southern Province, Zambia, and a tourist centre for Victoria Falls, which lies about 10 km away on the Zambezi River. It is named after the Scottish explorer and missionary David Livingstone, the first European to see the falls (1855).

History

Livingstone was the capital of Northern Rhodesia from 1907 until 1935, when Lusaka succeeded it. Its Edwardian colonial architecture, the Livingstone Museum and the Mosi-oa-Tunya National Park anchor its heritage tourism. Across the Victoria Falls Bridge (1905) lies the Zimbabwean town of Victoria Falls.
