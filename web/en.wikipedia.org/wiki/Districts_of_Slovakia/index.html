Districts of Slovakia

An okres (in English district) is an administrative unit of Slovakia. Slovakia has 79 districts, grouped within its 8 regions (kraje). The districts are subdivided into municipalities (obce), of which there are nearly 2,900.

Role

Districts are mainly used for statistics and for organizing certain state administrative functions, such as district courts, police, and offices of state administration. Unlike the regions and municipalities, which have elected self-governing bodies, the district level has no self-government; reforms in 2004 and 2013 reorganized state administration away from standalone district offices toward integrated local state administration offices.

The capital, Bratislava, is divided into five districts (Bratislava I–V), and the city of Košice into four (Košice I–IV), reflecting their size and special status. Districts are commonly identified in postal addresses, vehicle registration plates historically, and official statistics coded by the Statistical Office of the Slovak Republic.
