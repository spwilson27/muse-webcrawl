Model car

A die-cast model car in 1:18 scale

A model car, or toy car, is a miniature representation of an automobile. Model cars are produced both as children's toys and as precision collectibles for adults, and may be ready-built (often in die-cast metal) or assembled from kits in plastic, resin or metal.

Scales and types

Common scales include 1:12, 1:18, 1:24, 1:43 and 1:64, with smaller scales favoured for large collections and larger scales allowing opening parts and detailed interiors. Well-known makers include Corgi and Dinky Toys (historically), Matchbox and Hot Wheels in the toy segment, and specialist firms such as Minichamps, Autoart and CMC for collectors' models. Kit manufacturers such as Tamiya, Revell and AMT supply unbuilt plastic kits, historically including promotional models distributed through car dealerships.

Collecting

Model car collecting is an established hobby with price guides, clubs and shows. Value depends on scale, detail, rarity, condition and original packaging. Related hobbies include slot cars and radio-controlled cars, which add working propulsion to the miniature.
