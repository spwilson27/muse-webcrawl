Bremen (Low German: Breem) is a Hanseatic city in northwestern Germany, capital of the two-city state of the Free Hanseatic City of Bremen (with Bremerhaven). A commercial and industrial city on the River Weser, it is Germany's second-largest port by some measures and one of its oldest cities.

History

Founded as a diocese in 787 by Charlemagne, Bremen joined the Hanseatic League, became a free imperial city, and grew rich on maritime trade — coffee, cotton, and emigration to America. Its medieval Town Hall and Roland statue (1404) are a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Culture

Famous for the Brothers Grimm fairy tale "Town Musicians of Bremen" (donkey, dog, cat and rooster), SV Werder Bremen football club, Beck's beer, and the Schnoor old town quarter. See Hanseatic League, Germany.
