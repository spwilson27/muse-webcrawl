Donnie Wayne Johnson (born 15 December 1949) is an American actor, producer and singer. He achieved international stardom as Detective James "Sonny" Crockett in the television series Miami Vice (1984–1989), winning a Golden Globe Award, and later headlined Nash Bridges (1996–2001).

Career

Johnson acted from his teens, with early film roles including A Boy and His Dog (1975). Miami Vice made him a fashion icon — pastel suits, T-shirts and stubble — and he released two pop albums, Heartbeat (1986) and Let It Roll (1989). After the series he starred in films such as Tin Cup (1996) and returned to television with Nash Bridges. A career resurgence brought acclaimed roles in Django Unchained (2012), Cold in July (2014), Knives Out (2019) and the HBO series Watchmen (2019).

Personal life

Johnson's marriages include two to actress Melanie Griffith; their daughter is actress Dakota Johnson. He has five children in total and married Kelley Phleger in 1999.
