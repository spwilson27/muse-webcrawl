Yonko

Series: One Piece
Creator: Eiichiro Oda
Meaning: "Four Emperors" (四皇)
Role: The four most powerful pirate captains of the New World

The Yonko, literally the Four Emperors (Japanese: 四皇), are a group of fictional pirate captains in Eiichiro Oda's manga and anime series One Piece. They are the four most notorious and powerful captains ruling over the second half of the Grand Line, the "New World", and together with the Marines and the Seven Warlords of the Sea formerly formed one of the Three Great Powers maintaining the world's balance.

Role in the story

Each Yonko commands a vast fleet, controls territories and islands, and possesses personal combat power sufficient to resist the World Government's military forces. The Emperors are not a formal organisation and frequently clash with one another; the World Government treats any alliance between two Yonko as a grave emergency, as it believes such a coalition could overwhelm its forces.

Members

The original four Emperors introduced in the series are Whitebeard (Edward Newgate), hailed as the strongest man in the world; Kaido of the Beasts, called the strongest creature; Big Mom (Charlotte Linlin), ruler of Totto Land; and Shanks of the Red-Haired Pirates, the series' youngest Emperor and an early mentor to protagonist Monkey D. Luffy. After Whitebeard's death at the Summit War of Marineford, Blackbeard (Marshall D. Teach) seized his position. Following the events of the Wano Country arc, in which Kaido and Big Mom were defeated, the World Government recognised Luffy and Buggy the Clown as new Emperors alongside Shanks and Blackbeard.
