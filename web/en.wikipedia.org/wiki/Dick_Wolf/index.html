Dick Wolf

Richard Anthony Wolf (born December 20, 1946), known professionally as Dick Wolf, is an American film and television producer and screenwriter. He is best known as the creator and executive producer of the Law & Order franchise, as well as the Chicago and FBI franchises.

Career

After working in advertising — he wrote the Crest slogan "You can't beat Crest for fighting cavities" — Wolf wrote for TV series including Hill Street Blues and Miami Vice. In 1990 he created Law & Order for NBC; its flagship ran 20 seasons initially and was revived in 2022. Spin-offs such as Special Victims Unit became among the longest-running dramas in US television history. His Wolf Entertainment company produces the One Chicago shows (Chicago Fire, Chicago P.D., Chicago Med) and the CBS FBI franchise.

Style

Wolf's shows are known for "ripped from the headlines" plots, procedural structure split between investigation and prosecution, and extensive connected universes with frequent crossovers.
