South West Pacific theatre of World War II

South West Pacific theatre
Date1942 – 1945
LocationAustralia, New Guinea, Dutch East Indies, Philippines, Solomon Islands
ResultAllied victory

The South West Pacific theatre was a major theatre of the Pacific War fought between Allied and Japanese forces from 1942 to 1945 across Australia, New Guinea, the Dutch East Indies, the Philippines and nearby islands. Allied forces in the theatre operated largely under the South West Pacific Area command of General Douglas MacArthur.

Japanese offensives, 1942

After overrunning Malaya, Singapore and the Dutch East Indies in early 1942, Japanese forces landed at Rabaul, Lae and Salamaua, bombed Darwin, and sought to capture Port Moresby — first by sea, turned back at the Battle of the Coral Sea (May 1942), then overland along the Kokoda Track across the Owen Stanley Range. Australian troops, later reinforced by Americans, stopped the advance within sight of Port Moresby and, after the Japanese landing at Milne Bay was defeated, pushed the invaders back over the mountains. The gruelling Buna–Gona and Wau–Salamaua fighting continued into 1943.

Allied counter-offensives, 1943–1944

Under Operation Cartwheel, Allied forces isolated the Japanese stronghold of Rabaul while advancing along New Guinea's coast and through the Solomons. Air power under George Kenney, including the devastating Battle of the Bismarck Sea, underwrote a strategy of amphibious leapfrogging. Landings at Hollandia (April 1944) bypassed the Japanese Eighteenth Army at Wewak, and by late 1944 MacArthur's forces had reached Morotai, the gateway to the Philippines.

Philippines and Borneo, 1944–1945

The return to the Philippines began at Leyte in October 1944, accompanied by the Battle of Leyte Gulf, one of the largest naval battles in history and the first to see organised kamikaze attacks. Luzon was invaded in January 1945 and Manila liberated after a month of destructive fighting. In 1945, Australian forces conducted the Borneo campaign — Tarakan, Brunei Bay and Balikpapan — the last major Allied operations of the war, ending with the Japanese surrender in September 1945.
