People

  • Fernando Arias (Colombian artist, born 1963 in Armenia, Quindío) — a contemporary visual artist whose work in video, photography, installation and print deals with conflict, sexuality, religion and identity in Colombia. He studied in London, represented Colombia at the Venice Biennale (1999, with the project El País de las Delicias), and founded the Más Arte Más Acción foundation, which runs an interdisciplinary base in the rainforest of Chocó on Colombia's Pacific coast.
  • Fernando Arias has also been the name of figures in Latin American public life, sport and academia in various countries, reflecting the commonness of the name in the Spanish-speaking world.

The artist

Arias's work frequently engages the Colombian armed conflict and its social consequences. Projects such as 0566 (a counting project referencing the number of human rights defenders killed) and his video and photographic series on the body, queer identity and Catholic iconography have been exhibited internationally. Más Arte Más Acción (More Art More Action), founded in 2009, invites artists and writers to the Chocó to respond to social and environmental issues.
