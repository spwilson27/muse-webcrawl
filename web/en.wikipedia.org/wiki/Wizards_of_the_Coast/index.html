Wizards of the Coast

Wizards of the Coast LLC (often abbreviated WotC) is an American publisher of games, primarily role-playing and collectible card games. It was founded in 1990 by Peter Adkison and is best known for publishing Magic: The Gathering, the first modern collectible card game, and for owning the Dungeons & Dragons role-playing game. Since 1999 it has been a subsidiary of Hasbro.

History

Wizards of the Coast began in the Seattle area publishing role-playing game supplements such as The Primal Order. In 1993 the company released Magic: The Gathering, designed by Richard Garfield, which became an enormous commercial success and effectively created the collectible card game genre. In 1997 WotC acquired TSR, Inc., the original publisher of Dungeons & Dragons, and in 1998 it acquired the Five Rings Publishing Group. Hasbro purchased Wizards of the Coast in 1999 for approximately $325 million.

Products

The company's flagship products are Magic: The Gathering, which has been published continuously since 1993 and translated into many languages, and Dungeons & Dragons, whose third edition (2000) WotC released under the Open Game License, and whose fifth edition (2014) brought the game to a new mass audience. Wizards also briefly held the English-language licence for the Pokémon Trading Card Game and has published board games and digital titles through associated studios.

Headquarters

Wizards of the Coast is headquartered in Renton, Washington, with additional offices elsewhere in the United States. It operates organised play programmes including Friday Night Magic and the D&D Adventurers League.
