Betty Edwards

[Image placeholder: Betty Edwards portrait]

Betty Edwards (born 1926 in San Francisco) is an American teacher and author, best known for her 1979 book Drawing on the Right Side of the Brain, a widely used text for teaching drawing and creativity. The book has been translated into many languages and has sold millions of copies worldwide.

Edwards earned her doctorate from UCLA and taught art at California State University, Long Beach. Drawing on research into the functional specialization of the cerebral hemispheres, she developed exercises — including drawing an image upside down and working with negative space — intended to shift students from symbolic, verbal modes of seeing toward direct perceptual observation.

Works

Her other books include Drawing on the Artist Within (1986) and Color: A Course in Mastering the Art of Mixing Colors (2004). Revised editions of Drawing on the Right Side of the Brain appeared in 1989, 1999 and 2012.
