Tincture (heraldry)

Tinctures are the colours, metals and furs used in heraldry to depict a coat of arms. The principal metals are Or (gold/yellow) and Argent (silver/white); the principal colours are Gules (red), Azure (blue), Sable (black), Vert (green) and Purpure (purple). Furs include ermine and vair.

Rule of tincture

The fundamental rule of tincture states that metal should not be placed on metal, nor colour on colour, to ensure contrast and legibility at a distance. Exceptions exist, notably the arms of the Kingdom of Jerusalem.

Hatching and stains

In monochrome printing, Petra Sancta's hatching system represents tinctures by patterns of lines and dots. Additional "stains" such as murrey and sanguine appear rarely. Heraldic blazon names the tinctures precisely, allowing an arms to be reproduced from its verbal description.
