Desmond Howard

Desmond Kevin Howard (born May 15, 1970) is an American former professional football player who was a wide receiver and return specialist in the National Football League (NFL) and a college football analyst for ESPN. He won the Heisman Trophy in 1991 while playing for the Michigan Wolverines.

At Michigan, Howard set or tied five NCAA records and twelve school records, and struck the famous "Heisman pose" after a 93-yard punt return touchdown against Ohio State in 1991. Drafted fourth overall by Washington in the 1992 NFL Draft, he later played for the Jacksonville Jaguars, Green Bay Packers, Oakland Raiders, and Detroit Lions. With Green Bay, Howard was named MVP of Super Bowl XXXI after returning a kickoff 99 yards for a touchdown — still the only special teams player to win the award.

Since 2005, Howard has been a regular analyst on ESPN's College GameDay. He was inducted into the College Football Hall of Fame in 2010.
