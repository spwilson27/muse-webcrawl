Snowboarding at the Winter Olympics

Governing bodyFIS
DebutNagano 1998
Events11 (as of 2022)

Snowboarding has been contested at the Winter Olympic Games since the 1998 Games in Nagano, Japan. Its inclusion marked a major shift for the Olympic programme toward youth-oriented action sports.

The Nagano programme included halfpipe and giant slalom. Parallel giant slalom replaced giant slalom in 2002; snowboard cross was added in 2006, slopestyle in 2014, and big air (plus the mixed team snowboard cross) in 2018 and 2022 respectively.

Notable competitors

Shaun White won three halfpipe golds (2006, 2010, 2018); Chloe Kim won halfpipe gold in 2018 and 2022. Ester Ledecká famously won both the snowboarding parallel giant slalom and the alpine skiing super-G at PyeongChang 2018. The United States has historically dominated the medal table, particularly in halfpipe and slopestyle.
