Toni Basil

Antonia Christina Basilotta (born September 22, 1943), better known by her stage name Toni Basil, is an American singer, choreographer, dancer, actress, and filmmaker. She is best known for her 1981 hit single "Mickey", which reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100 in 1982.

Born in Philadelphia, Basil began her career as a dancer and assistant choreographer, appearing in films such as Easy Rider and Five Easy Pieces. She was a member of the pioneering street-dance troupe The Lockers, which helped popularise locking, and she choreographed for artists including David Bowie, Bette Midler, and Talking Heads, for whom she co-directed and choreographed the video for "Once in a Lifetime".

"Mickey" and later work

"Mickey", a cover of the song "Kitty" by Racey, was recorded in 1981 and its video — with Basil in a cheerleader costume — became an early MTV staple; the cheerleading aesthetic it popularised endures in pop culture. Basil continued to choreograph films and stage shows, and was honoured for her contribution to street dance and hip hop culture.
