Rauma Line (Raumabanen)
Country: Norway
Termini: Dombås – Åndalsnes
Length: 114.2 km
Opened: 1924
Operator: SJ Norge (passenger)


A train on the Rauma Line near the Trollveggen.

The Rauma Line (Norwegian: Raumabanen) is a 114.2-kilometre railway line in Norway running between Dombås station on the Dovre Line and the port town of Åndalsnes in Rauma municipality. Opened on 30 November 1924, it is the only railway in Møre og Romsdal county.

Route

The line descends through the Romsdalen valley alongside the Rauma river, passing notable engineering works including the Kylling Bridge and the Verma horseshoe curves. It runs beneath the Trollveggen, northern Europe's tallest vertical rock face, and through dramatic mountain scenery that has made it one of Norway's most scenic rail journeys.

Operations

Passenger services are operated by SJ Norge under contract with the Norwegian Railway Directorate, with connections at Dombås to Oslo–Trondheim trains. The line also carries freight to Åndalsnes. It has featured in films including Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince.
