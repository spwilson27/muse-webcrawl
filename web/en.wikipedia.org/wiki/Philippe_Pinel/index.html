Philippe Pinel

Philippe Pinel (20 April 1745 – 25 October 1826) was a French physician, precursor of psychiatry and incidentally a zoologist. He is famous for instituting more humane psychological treatment of the mentally ill — sometimes described as moral treatment — and for removing chains from patients at the Bicêtre and Salpêtrière hospitals in Paris, an event often seen as symbolic of the birth of modern psychiatry, though the reforms were gradual and collaborative.

Pinel's classification of mental disorders in his Traité médico-philosophique sur l'aliénation mentale (1801) influenced later psychiatric nosology.
