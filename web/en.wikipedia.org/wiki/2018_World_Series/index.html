The 2018 World Series was the championship series of Major League Baseball's 2018 season. The 114th edition of the World Series, it was played between the American League champion Boston Red Sox and the National League champion Los Angeles Dodgers. The Red Sox won the best-of-seven series in five games, securing their ninth championship.

Background

The Red Sox had won 108 games in the regular season under rookie manager Alex Cora, then defeated the New York Yankees and the defending champion Houston Astros in the playoffs. The Dodgers, managed by Dave Roberts, returned to the World Series for the second straight year after beating the Milwaukee Brewers in a seven-game NLCS.

Summary

Boston won the first two games at Fenway Park. Game 3 at Dodger Stadium lasted 18 innings — the longest game in World Series history at 7 hours 20 minutes — before Max Muncy's walk-off home run gave Los Angeles its only win. The Red Sox rallied to win Game 4 and clinched the title with a 5–1 victory in Game 5, behind a strong start from David Price and two home runs by Steve Pearce, who was named World Series MVP.
