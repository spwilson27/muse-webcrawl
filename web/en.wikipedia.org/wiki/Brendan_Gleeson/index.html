Brendan Gleeson

Brendan Gleeson (born 29 March 1955) is an Irish actor. A former schoolteacher, he began acting professionally in his thirties and has become one of Ireland's most acclaimed screen performers, known for roles in Braveheart (1995), In Bruges (2008), The Guard (2011), Calvary (2014), and The Banshees of Inisherin (2022), the last earning him an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actor. He played Alastor "Mad-Eye" Moody in the Harry Potter film series.

Early life and career

Gleeson was born in Dublin and educated at St Joseph's CBS in Fairview and University College Dublin. He worked as a secondary school teacher of Irish and English for about ten years while acting with the Passion Machine theatre company, before turning to acting full-time. Early film roles included The Field (1990) and Braveheart, in which he played Hamish Campbell.

Career highlights

Gleeson's collaborations with writer-director Martin McDonagh — Six Shooter (2004), In Bruges, and The Banshees of Inisherin — and with John Michael McDonagh on The Guard and Calvary are central to his reputation. He won an Emmy Award for playing Winston Churchill in Into the Storm (2009) and later played Donald Trump in the miniseries The Comey Rule (2020). His sons Domhnall and Brian Gleeson are also actors.
