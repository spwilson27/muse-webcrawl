Barney Rubble is a fictional character who appears in the animated television series The Flintstones and its spin-offs. He is the diminutive, blond, good-natured neighbor, best friend, and sidekick of Fred Flintstone, and lives in the prehistoric town of Bedrock with his wife Betty and their adopted son, Bamm-Bamm.

Character

Barney is shorter and more easygoing than Fred, though equally fond of bowling, lodge meetings at the Loyal Order of Water Buffalos, and stone-age gadgets. He typically serves as the loyal accomplice in Fred's schemes, greeting him with his signature cheerful laugh. His occupation varies across episodes—he has been shown working at the Slate Rock and Gravel Company alongside Fred and in various other jobs—an inconsistency often played for comedy. Barney and Betty, who had no child in the original series, adopted the super-strong foundling Bamm-Bamm in the fourth season (1963).

Voice and portrayal

Barney was voiced principally by Mel Blanc, whose characterization drew partly on Art Carney's Ed Norton from The Honeymooners; Daws Butler filled in during Blanc's recovery from a car accident in 1961. Other voices have included Frank Welker and Kevin Michael Richardson. In the 1994 live-action film The Flintstones, Barney was played by Rick Moranis, and by Stephen Baldwin in the 2000 prequel.

In popular culture

Barney appears throughout Flintstones merchandising and spin-offs, including The Pebbles and Bamm-Bamm Show, and the name is referenced by the rapper Barney Rubble characters and puns in later media; the cereal brand Fruity Pebbles features him alongside Fred.
