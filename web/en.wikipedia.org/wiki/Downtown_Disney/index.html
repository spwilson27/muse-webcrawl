Downtown Disney (formally the Downtown Disney District) is an outdoor shopping, dining and entertainment complex at the Disneyland Resort in Anaheim, California. Opened on 12 January 2001 as part of the resort's expansion alongside Disney California Adventure and Disney's Grand Californian Hotel & Spa, it connects the resort hotels and parking structures with the esplanade between the two theme parks. Admission is free, making it a popular destination for visitors without park tickets.

Features

The pedestrian promenade is lined with themed landscaping and buildings housing flagship retail such as the World of Disney store and the LEGO Store, restaurants including Naples Ristorante and Black Tap Craft Burgers & Shakes, and venues such as the Splitsville Luxury Lanes bowling alley. Street performers and live music animate the district in the evenings, and the Disneyland Monorail passes through with a station in the district.

History and changes

Tenants have turned over steadily since opening: long-running originals such as the Rainforest Cafe, ESPN Zone and the AMC theatre closed in a 2018 wave connected with a planned (later cancelled) fourth resort hotel on the district's west end. A multi-year reimagining of the west side began in the 2020s, adding new restaurants (including Din Tai Fung and Paseo/Céntrico), the Parkside Market food hall, and refreshed retail, while the former Florida counterpart — Walt Disney World's Downtown Disney — was expanded and renamed Disney Springs in 2015.
