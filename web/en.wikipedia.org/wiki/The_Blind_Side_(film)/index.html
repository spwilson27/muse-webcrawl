The Blind Side (film)


Theatrical release poster

The Blind Side is a 2009 American biographical sports drama film written and directed by John Lee Hancock. Based on the 2006 book of the same name by Michael Lewis, the film tells the story of Michael Oher, a homeless and traumatized African-American teenager who, with the help of Leigh Anne Tuohy and her family, becomes an All-American football player and first-round NFL draft pick. It stars Sandra Bullock as Leigh Anne Tuohy, Tim McGraw as Sean Tuohy, and Quinton Aaron as Oher.

Plot

Seventeen-year-old Michael Oher, who has drifted through foster homes in Memphis, is admitted to a private Christian school where his size and athleticism catch the attention of the football coach. Taken in by the affluent Tuohy family, he flourishes academically and athletically as an offensive tackle protecting his quarterback's blind side, earning a scholarship to the University of Mississippi despite an NCAA investigation into the Tuohys' motives.

Reception

The film was a major commercial success, grossing over $309 million worldwide against a $29 million budget. Sandra Bullock won the Academy Award for Best Actress for her performance, and the film was nominated for Best Picture. It was also criticised for perpetuating a "white saviour" narrative, a critique later echoed by Oher himself, who disputed elements of his portrayal.

Later controversy

In 2023 Oher filed a legal petition alleging that the Tuohys had never adopted him but had placed him under a conservatorship and profited from the film, charges the family denied; a Tennessee judge ended the conservatorship later that year.
