List of earthquakes in Israel

This is a list of notable earthquakes in Israel and the surrounding Levant region, both historical and modern. The region is crossed by the Dead Sea Transform, a major fault system running from the Red Sea to southern Turkey, which separates the Arabian Plate from the Sinai subplate.

The Dead Sea Transform is the principal source of seismic hazard in Israel and neighbouring countries.

Background

Movement along the Dead Sea Transform has produced destructive earthquakes throughout recorded history, many of them described in biblical and classical sources. Archaeoseismology — the study of damage to ancient buildings and sedimentary disturbances in the Dead Sea — has extended the catalogue back thousands of years.
