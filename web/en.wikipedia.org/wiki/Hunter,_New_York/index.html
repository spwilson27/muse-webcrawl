Hunter, New York

Hunter is a town in Greene County, New York, United States. The population was 2,732 at the 2020 census. The town is in the Catskill Mountains and contains the village of Hunter, the village of Tannersville, and the hamlet of Haines Falls.

History

The town was formed in 1813 from the town of Windham and was earlier known as Greenland. It developed as a summer resort during the 19th century, served by railways that brought tourists to grand hotels in the northern Catskills, including those near Kaaterskill Falls, a celebrated subject of Hudson River School painters.

Geography and recreation

Hunter Mountain, at about 4,040 feet one of the highest peaks in the Catskills, is home to Hunter Mountain ski area, one of the largest ski resorts in the region. The town includes part of Catskill Park and North–South Lake, a popular campground.
