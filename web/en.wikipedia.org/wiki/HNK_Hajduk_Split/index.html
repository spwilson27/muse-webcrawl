HNK Hajduk Split

HNK Hajduk Split (Croatian: Hrvatski nogometni klub Hajduk Split) is a Croatian professional football club based in Split, founded on 13 February 1911. One of the most successful and popular clubs in Croatia, Hajduk won nine Yugoslav league titles and six Croatian league championships, and is renowned for its fanatical supporters' group, Torcida Split, founded in 1950 and considered the oldest organised supporters' group in Europe. The club plays at Stadion Poljud.

History

The club was founded by Split students in Prague, who named it after the hajduks — Balkan outlaws celebrated in folk tradition. Hajduk became a symbol of Croatian and Dalmatian identity, refused to compete under an Italianised name during the wartime occupation, and in 1944 famously escaped to the island of Vis and toured the Mediterranean as the team of the Yugoslav Partisans. In Yugoslav competition it won titles in the 1950s and 1970s "golden generation", and in independent Croatia it won the inaugural league and cup competitions, reaching the Champions League quarter-finals in 1995.

Rivalries

Hajduk's fiercest rivalry is the Eternal Derby with Dinamo Zagreb. The club is majority-owned by the City of Split, with an influential fan association, Naš Hajduk ("Our Hajduk"), holding a significant stake and board representation.
