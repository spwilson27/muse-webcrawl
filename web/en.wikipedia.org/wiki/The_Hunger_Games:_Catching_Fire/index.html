The Hunger Games: Catching Fire

The Hunger Games: Catching Fire is a 2013 American dystopian action film directed by Francis Lawrence from a screenplay by Simon Beaufoy and Michael deBruyn, based on the 2009 novel Catching Fire by Suzanne Collins. The sequel to The Hunger Games (2012) and the second installment in The Hunger Games film series, it stars Jennifer Lawrence as Katniss Everdeen, alongside Josh Hutcherson, Liam Hemsworth, Woody Harrelson, Elizabeth Banks, Lenny Kravitz, Philip Seymour Hoffman, Jeffrey Wright, Sam Claflin, Jena Malone, Stanley Tucci, and Donald Sutherland.

Plot

After winning the 74th Hunger Games, Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark return home to District 12, but President Snow warns Katniss that her defiance has inspired unrest in the districts. For the 75th Games—the third Quarter Quell—Snow announces that the tributes will be reaped from the existing pool of victors. Katniss and Peeta return to the arena, a tropical island designed like a clock with scheduled hazards, and ally with Finnick Odair, Johanna Mason and the inventive Beetee. Unknown to Katniss, the alliance is part of a rebel plot: she is rescued by a District 13 hovercraft, while Peeta is captured by the Capitol. Gale tells her that District 12 has been destroyed.

Reception

The film premiered in London on 11 November 2013 and was released by Lionsgate in the United States on 22 November. It received positive reviews, with praise for Lawrence's performance, the direction, and its faithfulness to the novel, and grossed $865 million worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing film of the franchise and the highest-grossing film of 2013 in North America. It was followed by The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 1 (2014) and Part 2 (2015).
