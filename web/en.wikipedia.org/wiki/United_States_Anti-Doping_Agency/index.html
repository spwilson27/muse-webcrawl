United States Anti-Doping Agency

The United States Anti-Doping Agency (USADA) is the national anti-doping organisation of the United States, recognised by Congress and the US Olympic & Paralympic Committee. A non-profit, non-governmental body founded in 2000, it manages testing, results management, and education for Olympic, Paralympic, Pan American and — historically — UFC athletes.

Major cases

USADA investigated the BALCO scandal and, most famously, in 2012 charged Lance Armstrong with a systematic doping conspiracy, issuing a lifetime ban and a 1,000-page reasoned decision; the UCI stripped Armstrong of his seven Tour de France titles. USADA also sanctioned sprinter Marion Jones-era figures, coach Alberto Salazar (2019), and numerous UFC fighters under its 2015–2023 UFC programme.

Role

USADA administers the World Anti-Doping Code in the US, runs the Athlete Biological Passport and whereabouts programmes, and funds research through Partnership for Clean Competition. Tensions with WADA grew over the Rodchenkov Act (2020) and the 2021 Chinese swimmers case.
