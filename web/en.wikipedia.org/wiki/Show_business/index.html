Show business

Show business, sometimes shortened to show biz or showbiz, is a vernacular term for all aspects of the entertainment industry. From the business side (including managers, agents, producers, and distributors), the term applies to the elements of the performing arts, encompassing film, television, theatre, music, and live performance.

History

The phrase has been in use since the nineteenth century, when travelling theatrical troupes, vaudeville, and music hall flourished. With the rise of cinema in the twentieth century, and later television and streaming media, show business grew into a global industry centred on places such as Hollywood and Broadway.

The industry

Show business involves performers, writers, directors, producers, technicians, and the executives and agents who manage production and distribution. Publications such as Variety and The Hollywood Reporter chronicle the trade.
