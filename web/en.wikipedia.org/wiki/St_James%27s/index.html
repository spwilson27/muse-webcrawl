St James's


Location: City of Westminster, London
Known for: St James's Palace, gentlemen's clubs, luxury retail

St James's is a district of central London in the City of Westminster, lying south of Piccadilly and adjoining Mayfair, Green Park and The Mall. Historically the site of St James's Palace, it has long been associated with the royal court, gentlemen's clubs and high-end shops.

History

The area took its name from a medieval leper hospital dedicated to St James. Henry VIII acquired the site and built St James's Palace, which remained a principal royal residence and still gives the formal name of the British court — the Court of St James's. Aristocratic development followed in the seventeenth century around St James's Square.

Character

Jermyn Street is famous for shirt-makers and tailors, while Pall Mall hosts many of London's oldest gentlemen's clubs. Landmarks include Christie's auction house, Fortnum & Mason and Spencer House.
