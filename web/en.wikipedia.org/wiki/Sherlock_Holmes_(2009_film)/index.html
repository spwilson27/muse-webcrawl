Sherlock Holmes (2009 film)

Directed by: Guy Ritchie
Starring: Robert Downey Jr., Jude Law, Rachel McAdams, Mark Strong
Release: 25 December 2009
Box office: $524 million

Sherlock Holmes is a 2009 period action mystery film directed by Guy Ritchie and based on Arthur Conan Doyle's detective. Robert Downey Jr. stars as Holmes and Jude Law as Dr John Watson, with Rachel McAdams as Irene Adler and Mark Strong as the occultist villain Lord Blackwood.

Plot

London, 1890. Holmes and Watson foil Blackwood's ritual murder and see him hanged, but Blackwood apparently rises from the grave, and Holmes must unravel a conspiracy by a secret society to seize control of Britain using a chemical weapon, staged to look like black magic. The investigation climaxes atop the half-built Tower Bridge. The film ends with the revelation that a hidden adversary — Professor Moriarty — has been pulling strings, setting up the 2011 sequel Sherlock Holmes: A Game of Shadows.

Production and reception

Ritchie and Downey presented a more physical, bohemian Holmes — a bare-knuckle boxer and martial artist who narrates his deductions in slow-motion "Holmes-vision" fight analysis — drawing on Doyle's less-remembered details (Holmes's baritsu and untidiness) rather than the deerstalker clichés. Hans Zimmer's score, built around a deliberately detuned honky-tonk piano, was nominated for an Academy Award; Downey won the Golden Globe for Best Actor in a Comedy or Musical. The film grossed $524 million worldwide against a budget of about $90 million.
