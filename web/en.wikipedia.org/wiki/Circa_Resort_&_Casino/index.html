Circa Resort & Casino is a casino and hotel in downtown Las Vegas, Nevada, on the Fremont Street Experience. It opened on October 28, 2020, as the first ground-up casino resort built downtown in about forty years, and was developed by brothers Derek and Greg Stevens, owners of the D Las Vegas and Golden Gate casinos. The name "Circa" refers to the resort's theme honouring the history of Las Vegas.

Features

The 35-storey tower, the tallest building north of the Las Vegas Strip, contains 777 rooms. The property is distinguished by its sportsbook — billed as the world's largest, with a three-storey, high-definition screen — accompanied by the Vegas Stats & Information Network (VSiN) broadcast studio, and by Stadium Swim, a rooftop pool amphitheatre with six pools facing a giant screen used for sports viewing. Circa is a 21-and-over property throughout, unusual for Las Vegas, and its casino spans two floors with more than a thousand slot machines. The restored neon sign "Vegas Vickie", first erected in 1980, stands in the lobby.

History

The site previously housed the Las Vegas Club casino, the Mermaids casino, and the Girls of Glitter Gulch strip club, which the Stevens brothers acquired and demolished beginning in 2016–2017. The project was announced as "18 Fremont" and named Circa in January 2019; construction proceeded through the COVID-19 pandemic, with the casino floors opening in October 2020 and the hotel tower following at the end of December 2020.
