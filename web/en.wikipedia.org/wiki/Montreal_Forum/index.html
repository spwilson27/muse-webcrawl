The Montreal Forum (French: Forum de Montréal) is a historic building in Montreal, Quebec, Canada, that served as an indoor ice hockey arena from 1924 to 1996. It was the home of the Montreal Canadiens of the National Hockey League for most of that period, during which the team won 22 of its 24 Stanley Cups, and of the Montreal Maroons.

History

The Forum opened on November 29, 1924, built in under six months. It was renovated in 1949 and again in 1968. Stanley Cup–winning celebrations there were accompanied by the famous St. Patrick's Day Riot of 1955, sparked by the suspension of Maurice "Rocket" Richard. The Canadiens played their last game at the Forum on March 11, 1996, defeating the Dallas Stars 4–1, before moving to the Molson Centre (now Bell Centre).

Events and later use

The Forum hosted events at the 1976 Summer Olympics and concerts by artists from the Beatles to Led Zeppelin. After the Canadiens' departure the building was redeveloped as an entertainment complex with cinemas and shops, preserving the centre-ice section and some seating as a display.
