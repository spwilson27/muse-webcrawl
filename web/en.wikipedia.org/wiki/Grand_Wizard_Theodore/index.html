Grand Wizard Theodore

Grand Wizard Theodore, inventor of the scratch technique, at the turntables.

Grand Wizard Theodore (born Theodore Livingston, 5 March 1963) is an American hip hop DJ. He is widely credited as the inventor of the scratch technique of DJing, and is also known for his mastery of needle drops and other turntable tricks.

Early life and the invention of scratching

Livingston grew up in the Bronx, New York, and learned DJing from his older brother Mean Gene, an associate of Grandmaster Flash. According to his own account, he discovered scratching by accident in 1975, at the age of twelve: while playing records loudly in his room, his mother entered to speak to him, and he held a record still with his hand to hear her, noticing that moving the record back and forth under the needle produced a rhythmic sound. He developed the accident into a deliberate technique.

Career

Theodore performed with Grandmaster Flash's circle and appeared as himself in the 1983 film Wild Style, where his scratching is showcased. With his group the Fantastic Five (the L Brothers and later the Fantastic Romantic 5), he recorded tracks such as "Grand Wizard Theodore & The Fantastic 5" appearances and battled the Cold Crush Brothers in a famous 1981 contest. In later decades he has taught DJing, performed internationally, and in 2025 received recognition for his foundational role in hip hop culture, including induction honours among the pioneers of the genre.

Legacy

Scratching became a defining element of hip hop music and turntablism. Theodore is routinely named, alongside Grandmaster Flash and Kool Herc, as one of the art form's founding innovators.
