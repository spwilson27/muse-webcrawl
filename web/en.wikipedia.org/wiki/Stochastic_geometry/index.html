Stochastic geometry

Stochastic geometry is the branch of probability theory that studies random spatial patterns, including point processes, random sets, tessellations, and random shapes in Euclidean space.

Core models

Central objects include Poisson point processes, Voronoi and Delaunay tessellations, Boolean models of random grains, random geometric graphs, and percolation models. Palm theory and moment measures provide the main analytical tools.

Applications

Stochastic geometry is widely used to model wireless networks (base station locations, interference, coverage), materials science, forestry, astronomy, and image analysis.
