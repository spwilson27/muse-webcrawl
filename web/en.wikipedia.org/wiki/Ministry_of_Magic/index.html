Ministry of Magic

The Ministry of Magic is the fictional government of the magical community of Britain in J. K. Rowling's Harry Potter series. It is devoted principally to enforcing the International Statute of Wizarding Secrecy, concealing the existence of magic from the non-magical (Muggle) world, and to regulating magical society, education, creatures, transportation, and law enforcement.

Structure

The Ministry is headed by the Minister for Magic and organised into departments including the Department of Magical Law Enforcement (the largest, incorporating the Auror Office and the Wizengamot administration), the Department of Magical Accidents and Catastrophes, the Department for the Regulation and Control of Magical Creatures, the Department of International Magical Cooperation, the Department of Magical Transportation (overseeing the Floo Network, Portkeys, and broomstick regulation), the Department of Magical Games and Sports, and the Department of Mysteries, whose Unspeakables study arcane subjects in secrecy.

Role in the series

In Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix, under Minister Cornelius Fudge, the Ministry denies Voldemort's return, smears Harry Potter and Albus Dumbledore, and interferes at Hogwarts through Dolores Umbridge. The climactic Battle of the Department of Mysteries forces the Ministry to acknowledge Voldemort's return. Under Rufus Scrimgeour, and later after its fall to the Death Eaters in Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows, the Ministry becomes an instrument of Voldemort's regime, persecuting Muggle-borns through the Muggle-Born Registration Commission, before being reformed under Kingsley Shacklebolt after Voldemort's defeat.
