Krkonoše National Park

Krkonoše National Park (KRNAP)
LocationNorthern Bohemia, Czech Republic
Established1963
Areaapprox. 363 km² (plus buffer zone)
Highest pointSněžka (1,603 m)

Krkonoše National Park (Czech: Krkonošský národní park, KRNAP) is a national park protecting the Czech side of the Krkonoše mountains. Together with Poland's Karkonosze National Park it forms a transboundary UNESCO biosphere reserve.

Conservation

The park protects arctic-alpine tundra ecosystems, endemic plants such as the Sudetic lousewort, and relict species left from the Ice Age. About two-thirds of the area is forested, mostly spruce, much of it recovering from 20th-century air-pollution damage.

Visitors

The park headquarters is in Vrchlabí. Marked trails lead to Sněžka, the source of the Elbe (Labe) river and the Pančava waterfall, the highest in the Czech Republic.
