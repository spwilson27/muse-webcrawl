Clickair

IATA: XG   ICAO: CLI   Callsign: CLICKJET

Founded: 2006

Ceased operations: 15 July 2009 (merged into Vueling)

Hub: Barcelona–El Prat Airport

Headquarters: El Prat de Llobregat, Spain

Clickair was a low-cost airline based in El Prat de Llobregat, near Barcelona, Spain. It operated services to destinations in Europe from its main base at Barcelona–El Prat Airport, using a fleet of Airbus A320 aircraft. Iberia held a substantial minority stake in the airline, and Clickair codeshared many flights with it.

History

The airline was established in 2006 under the working name Catair, derived from Catalonia and "air", but was renamed Clickair before beginning operations — a name intended to evoke the "click" of online booking, the core of its sales model. It began flying on 1 October 2006 with three Airbus A320s on routes from Barcelona.

Clickair grew rapidly, expanding to bases including Madrid, Seville, Valencia, Málaga and Bilbao, and carrying millions of passengers a year. However, intense competition among Spanish low-cost carriers and rising fuel costs placed the airline under financial pressure.

Merger with Vueling

In July 2008 Clickair and rival Spanish low-cost carrier Vueling announced their intention to merge. The merger, cleared by European competition authorities subject to slot divestments, was completed on 15 July 2009. The combined airline continued under the Vueling name, and the Clickair brand and operating certificate disappeared.
