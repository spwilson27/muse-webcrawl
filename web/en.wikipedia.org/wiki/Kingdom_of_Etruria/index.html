Kingdom of Etruria

CapitalFlorence
Existed1801–1807
KingsLouis I; Charles Louis

The Kingdom of Etruria was a short-lived kingdom in Tuscany, Italy, created by Napoleon Bonaparte under the Treaty of Aranjuez (1801) for the Bourbon-Parma branch of the Spanish royal family, in exchange for Parma and Spain's return of Louisiana to France.

History

Its first king was Louis I, succeeded in 1803 by his young son Charles Louis under the regency of his mother Maria Luisa. In 1807 Napoleon dissolved the kingdom and annexed its territory to the French Empire; Tuscany was later given to Elisa Bonaparte as the Grand Duchy of Tuscany.
