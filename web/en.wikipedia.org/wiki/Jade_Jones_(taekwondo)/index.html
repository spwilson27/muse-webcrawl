Jade Jones (taekwondo)

Jade Louise Jones (born 21 March 1993) is a Welsh taekwondo athlete who competes for Great Britain in the 57 kg category. She is a two-time Olympic champion, winning gold at London 2012 and Rio 2016, and won the world title in 2019.

Career

Jones was born in Bodelwyddan, Denbighshire, Wales, and was introduced to taekwondo by her grandfather. She came to prominence by winning gold at the inaugural Youth Olympic Games in Singapore in 2010. At the London 2012 Olympics, aged 19, she defeated China's Hou Yuzhuo in the final to become Britain's first Olympic taekwondo champion, a triumph that made her a national celebrity; her hometown of Flint famously painted a post box gold in her honor.

She retained her Olympic title in Rio de Janeiro in 2016, beating Spain's Eva Calvo in the final, joining the small group of British athletes with back-to-back individual Olympic golds. After several near misses at world level, she won the World Championship in Manchester in 2019. At the Tokyo 2020 Olympics she suffered a shock first-round defeat. She later switched to professional boxing, announcing the move in 2025.

Honors

Jones was appointed MBE in 2013 and OBE in 2020 for services to taekwondo and sport. She is widely regarded as one of Britain's most successful martial artists.
