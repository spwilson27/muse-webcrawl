Bonfire Night

Bonfire Night
Also called: Guy Fawkes Night
Observed: United Kingdom and Commonwealth
Date: 5 November

Bonfire Night, also known as Guy Fawkes Night, is an annual commemoration observed primarily in the United Kingdom on 5 November. Its history stems from the Gunpowder Plot of 5 November 1605, when Guy Fawkes was arrested guarding explosives beneath the House of Lords.

History

The Observance of 5th November Act required thanksgiving for the plot's failure. Bonfires and fireworks became traditional. Effigies of Guy Fawkes — 'guys' — are burned.

Traditions

Firework displays, bonfires, toffee apples, parkin and treacle toffee are traditional. In Lewes, Sussex, celebrations are especially large. Children once asked 'a penny for the guy'.

Modern observance

Large public displays are now common, with safety campaigns. The night is also observed in New Zealand, Canada and parts of the Caribbean.
