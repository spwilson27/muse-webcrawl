Inventiones Mathematicae is one of the premier journals in pure mathematics.

Inventiones Mathematicae is a mathematical journal published monthly by Springer Science+Business Media. The journal was established in 1966 and is regarded as one of the most prestigious mathematics journals in the world, alongside Annals of Mathematics, Acta Mathematica, and Publications Mathématiques de l'IHÉS.

The journal publishes articles of exceptional significance across the whole spectrum of pure mathematics, including algebra, geometry, analysis, number theory, and mathematical physics.

Editors

Managing editors have included Reinhold Remmert, and in more recent years the journal has been overseen by editors such as Jean-Benoît Bost, Janos Kollár, and others. The editorial board comprises leading mathematicians in many fields.

Abstracting and indexing

The journal is abstracted and indexed in Mathematical Reviews, Zentralblatt MATH, and the Science Citation Index. Its impact factor has consistently placed it among the top journals in mathematics.
