Amy Wong is a fictional character in the animated television series Futurama, voiced by Lauren Tom. She is a wealthy engineering student from Mars who works as an intern at Planet Express, the interplanetary delivery company owned by Professor Farnsworth, who keeps her on partly because they share the same blood type.

Character

Amy is the daughter of Leo and Inez Wong, who own the western hemisphere of Mars and run a vast buggalo ranch. Despite her family's enormous wealth, she works at Planet Express while studying engineering at Mars University; in the episode "That Darn Katz!" she earns her doctorate in applied physics. She is generally depicted wearing a pink tracksuit and is portrayed as cheerful, somewhat klutzy and prone to muttering curses in Cantonese when she drops or breaks things.

Her long-running romantic relationship with Kif Kroker, the long-suffering lieutenant of Zapp Brannigan, is a recurring storyline; the two become Fonfon Ru and Fonfon Ru (a form of Amphibiosan marriage), and Kif later bears children after Amy serves as the smizmar whose genetic material is combined with his.
