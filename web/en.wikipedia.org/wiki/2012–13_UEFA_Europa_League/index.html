The 2012–13 UEFA Europa League was the 42nd season of Europe's secondary club football tournament organised by UEFA, and the fourth season since it was renamed from the UEFA Cup to the UEFA Europa League.

Final

The final was held at the Amsterdam Arena on 15 May 2013. Chelsea defeated Benfica 2–1, with Branislav Ivanović heading the winner in stoppage time after Fernando Torres's opener had been equalised by an Óscar Cardozo penalty. Chelsea became the first team to hold the Champions League and Europa League titles simultaneously, having entered the competition after their Champions League group-stage exit.

Knockout phase

Chelsea's run to the final included victories over Sparta Prague, Steaua București, Rubin Kazan and Basel. Benfica, Champions League drop-outs like Chelsea, overcame Bayer Leverkusen, Bordeaux, Newcastle United and Fenerbahçe.

Top goalscorers

Libor Kozák of Lazio was the tournament's top scorer with eight goals.
