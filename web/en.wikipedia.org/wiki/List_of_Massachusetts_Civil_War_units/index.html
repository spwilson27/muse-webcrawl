List of Massachusetts Civil War units

The Commonwealth of Massachusetts played a significant role in the American Civil War (1861–1865). Massachusetts raised numerous regiments of infantry, cavalry, and artillery for the Union Army, as well as coastal defense units. Massachusetts troops were among the first to respond to President Abraham Lincoln's call for volunteers in April 1861, and the 6th Massachusetts was among the first regiments to reach Washington, D.C. In total, Massachusetts furnished roughly 159,000 men to the Union war effort.

Cavalry and artillery

Massachusetts cavalry units included the 1st through 4th Massachusetts Cavalry regiments. Artillery units included the 1st through 16th Massachusetts Batteries of Light Artillery and the 1st through 3rd Regiments of Heavy Artillery, the latter largely serving in the defenses of Washington and Boston Harbor.

Other units

The state also raised independent companies of sharpshooters (the 1st and 2nd Massachusetts Sharpshooters, known as the "Andrews Sharpshooters"), the 5th Massachusetts Colored Cavalry, and numerous militia units called into federal service for 90 days, nine months, or 100 days during emergencies such as the Gettysburg campaign.
