Left Behind is a series of 16 bestselling religious novels by Tim LaHaye and Jerry B. Jenkins, published between 1995 and 2007, dealing with the Christian dispensationalist End Times: the Rapture, the Tribulation, the rise of the Antichrist, and the Second Coming of Christ. The series has sold more than 65 million copies and is one of the best-selling series of all time. It takes its title from the first novel, in which millions of Christians suddenly vanish from the Earth, leaving the rest of humanity "left behind."

Plot

The series opens with the Rapture: aboard a transatlantic flight, airline captain Rayford Steele discovers that dozens of passengers have vanished, leaving their clothes behind. His wife Irene and son have disappeared from home, while he and his daughter Chloe are left behind. Around the world, the charismatic Romanian politician Nicolae Carpathia rises to power as Secretary-General of the United Nations, which he transforms into a one-world government, the Global Community. Rayford, Chloe, journalist Cameron "Buck" Williams and pastor Bruce Barnes form the "Tribulation Force" to oppose Carpathia, who is revealed as the Antichrist, through seven years of Tribulation prophesied in the Book of Revelation, until Christ returns in glory in the twelfth novel, Glorious Appearing (2004). A sequel, Kingdom Come (2007), covers the Millennium, and three prequels describe events before the Rapture.

The thirteen main-sequence books are: Left Behind (1995), Tribulation Force (1996), Nicolae (1997), Soul Harvest (1998), Apollyon (1999), Assassins (1999), The Indwelling (2000), The Mark (2000), Desecration (2001), The Remnant (2002), Armageddon (2003), Glorious Appearing (2004) and Kingdom Come (2007).

Related works and adaptations

The franchise expanded into the 40-book Left Behind: The Kids series for younger readers, graphic novels, audio dramas and video games. The first three novels were adapted into low-budget films by Cloud Ten Pictures starring Kirk Cameron (2000–2005); a 2014 theatrical remake, Left Behind, starring Nicolas Cage, was a critical and commercial failure. A further film, Left Behind: Rise of the Antichrist, followed in 2023.

Reception and criticism

The books were a publishing phenomenon, with several volumes reaching number one on the New York Times bestseller list; their success is credited with transforming the Christian fiction market. They have also drawn criticism: from theologians (including many Christians) who dispute the dispensational, pre-tribulation theology on which they rest, and from reviewers who attacked their prose style and politics. The series is frequently discussed as a cultural expression of American evangelical eschatology.
