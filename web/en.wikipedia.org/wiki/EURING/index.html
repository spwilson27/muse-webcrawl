EURING

EURING, the European Union for Bird Ringing, is the coordinating organisation for European bird ringing (bird banding) schemes, founded in 1963. Its member schemes operate ringing centres across Europe and beyond, and EURING promotes standardised methods so that recoveries of ringed birds can be compared across borders.

Data bank and codes

The EURING Data Bank, established in 1977 and hosted for many years at the Netherlands Institute of Ecology, holds records of ring recoveries reported across Europe, enabling large-scale studies of migration, survival, and dispersal. EURING maintains standard code systems used throughout the schemes, including codes for species, age, sex, condition, and the circumstances of recovery. Its "EURING Exchange Code" formats (notably the 2000 code) define how ringing and recovery data are exchanged between schemes.

Activities

EURING organises conferences and technical meetings for ringers, publishes guidance such as the EURING age and species coding standards, and supports projects like the EURING Swallow Project, which studied the barn swallow across its European breeding range. Ringing under EURING schemes contributes data to conservation science and to atlases of bird migration.
