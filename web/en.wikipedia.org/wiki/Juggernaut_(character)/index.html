Juggernaut (character)

Juggernaut (Cain Marko) is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Stan Lee and artist Jack Kirby, he first appeared in The X-Men #12 (July 1965). He is the stepbrother of Professor Charles Xavier and one of the X-Men's most enduring adversaries, though at times an antihero.

Fictional biography

Cain Marko, the abused son of atomic scientist Kurt Marko, served as a mercenary in Korea alongside his stepbrother Charles Xavier. In a hidden temple he found the Crimson Gem of Cyttorak, whose inscription transformed him into a human juggernaut, the avatar of the god Cyttorak.

Powers and abilities

Empowered by Cyttorak, Juggernaut possesses superhuman strength, durability, and stamina, and once he begins moving in a direction, he is virtually unstoppable — his momentum cannot be halted by physical force. His helmet shields him from telepathic attack.

In other media

The character has appeared in animated X-Men series and films, played by Vinnie Jones in X-Men: The Last Stand (2006) and appearing (voiced by Ryan Reynolds) in Deadpool 2 (2018). The phrase "nothing can stop the Juggernaut" became a popular catchphrase.
