Reedy Creek Improvement District

The Reedy Creek Improvement District (RCID) was a special taxing district of the US state of Florida, created in 1967, that governed the land of the Walt Disney World Resort entirely owned by the Walt Disney Company. It had most of the powers of a county government, including eminent domain, building codes, land-use regulation, and the provision of fire protection, utilities, and road maintenance, and it contained the two cities of Bay Lake and Lake Buena Vista. In 2023, following a political dispute between Disney and Governor Ron DeSantis, the district was restructured and renamed the Central Florida Tourism Oversight District, with a board appointed by the governor.

The district covered some 25,000 acres (39 sq mi) of central Florida. [Image placeholder]

History

Walt Disney sought unprecedented autonomy for his Florida project so he could build the Experimental Prototype Community of Tomorrow (EPCOT) as a functioning city. The Florida legislature, at Disney's request, enacted the Reedy Creek Improvement Act in 1967, creating the district, the Reedy Creek Improvement Authority, and the two municipalities. The district's board was elected by landowners — effectively Disney itself — and it issued tax-exempt bonds to finance infrastructure.

Powers

The district could regulate land use and building codes, operate its own utilities and fire department, maintain roads, manage drainage and flood control, and exercise eminent domain within and, for certain facilities, beyond its borders. Notably, it held authority to build a nuclear power plant, which was never exercised.

2022–2023 dispute and renaming

After Disney publicly opposed Florida's Parental Rights in Education Act in 2022, the state moved first to dissolve the district and then, in February 2023, to place it under state control: Governor DeSantis signed legislation renaming it the Central Florida Tourism Oversight District and replacing the landowner-elected board with gubernatorial appointees, triggering litigation between Disney and the state, largely settled in 2024.
