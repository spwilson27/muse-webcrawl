List of cities in Syria

This is a list of cities in Syria by population. Damascus, the capital, and Aleppo, the country's largest city before the civil war, are among the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world. Population figures have been heavily affected by displacement during the Syrian civil war that began in 2011.

History

Syria's urban network developed along ancient trade routes linking Mesopotamia with the Mediterranean. Cities such as Damascus, Aleppo, Palmyra, and Bosra flourished under Roman, Byzantine, and Islamic rule. The civil war beginning in 2011 caused extensive destruction in Aleppo, Homs, and elsewhere and displaced millions internally and abroad.
