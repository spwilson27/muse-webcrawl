Datasheet

A datasheet, data sheet, or spec sheet is a document that summarises the performance and other characteristics of a product, machine, component, material, or software in sufficient detail that a buyer can understand its role in a system and a design engineer can integrate it. Datasheets are published by manufacturers and are usually distributed as printed documents or PDF files.

[ Hero image: The first page of a typical electronic component datasheet ]

Contents

An electronic component datasheet typically includes: a description and feature list; pin configuration and functions; absolute maximum ratings beyond which the device may be damaged; recommended operating conditions; electrical characteristics (minimum, typical, and maximum values); timing diagrams and characteristic curves; package dimensions and mechanical drawings; ordering information; and application notes and example circuits. For integrated circuits, datasheets may run to hundreds of pages.

Other domains

Chemicals are documented by safety data sheets (SDS), which follow an internationally harmonised 16-section format covering hazards, handling, and first aid. Automotive datasheets publish dimensions, engine performance, and fuel consumption; software products use datasheets to summarise system requirements and supported features.
