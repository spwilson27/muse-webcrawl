Angoulême International Comics Festival

The Angoulême International Comics Festival (French: Festival international de la bande dessinée d'Angoulême) is a major annual comics festival held in Angoulême, France, usually in January. Founded in 1974, it is one of the world's most influential gatherings for comics creators, publishers and readers.

Programme

The festival combines exhibitions, publisher stands, panels, book signings, concerts and professional meetings across venues in the city. It helped establish comics as a serious cultural form in France and draws international guests from Europe, North America and Asia.

Awards

Its prizes include the Fauve d'Or for best album, awards for series, first books, heritage and youth comics, and the Grand Prix de la ville d'Angoulême, a lifetime honour whose recipient traditionally serves as a leading figure of the following year's festival.
