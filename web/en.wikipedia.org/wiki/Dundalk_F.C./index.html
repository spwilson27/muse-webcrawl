Dundalk F.C.

Dundalk Football Club (Irish: Cumann Peile Dhún Dealgan) is a professional association football club based in Dundalk, County Louth, Ireland. Founded in 1903 as the Dundalk G.N.R. works team, the club plays at Oriel Park and competes in the League of Ireland. Dundalk is one of the most successful clubs in Irish football.

History

The club joined the League of Ireland in 1926 as Dundalk G.N.R. and won its first league title in 1932–33. A golden era came under Stephen Kenny in the 2010s: league titles in 2014, 2015, 2018 and 2019, and a famous run to the group stage of the UEFA Europa League in 2016–17, when Dundalk became the first Irish club to win a match in a European group stage.

Honours

Dundalk have won 14 league titles, 12 FAI Cups and numerous League of Ireland Cups. Their traditional rivals are Drogheda United, with whom they contest the Louth derby.
