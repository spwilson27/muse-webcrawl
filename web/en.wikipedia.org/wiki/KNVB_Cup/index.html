KNVB Cup

KNVB Cup
Founded: 1898
Region: Netherlands
Organiser: KNVB
Most successful club: Ajax

The KNVB Cup (Dutch: KNVB Beker) is a knockout cup competition in Dutch football, organised by the Royal Dutch Football Association (KNVB). It is the principal domestic cup in the Netherlands and one of the oldest football cup competitions in the world, first played in 1898.

Format

Clubs from the Eredivisie, Eerste Divisie and lower league and amateur levels enter the competition. Ties are single-legged knockouts, and the final is traditionally held at De Kuip in Rotterdam. The winners qualify for European competition, historically the UEFA Europa League, and contest the Johan Cruyff Shield against the league champions.

History

The cup was modelled on the English FA Cup. Ajax is the most successful club in the competition's history, followed by Feyenoord and PSV Eindhoven. Occasional triumphs by smaller clubs remain celebrated episodes in Dutch football.
