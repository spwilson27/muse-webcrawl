Dansk Melodi Grand Prix

Dansk Melodi Grand Prix is an annual music competition organised by the Danish broadcaster DR since 1957. It determines Denmark's entry for the Eurovision Song Contest. The festival has produced three Eurovision winners: Grethe and Jørgen Ingmann with "Dansevise" in 1963, the Olsen Brothers with "Fly on the Wings of Love" in 2000, and Emmelie de Forest with "Only Teardrops" in 2013.

History

Denmark took part in the second Eurovision Song Contest in 1957, and the national final was held in the same year. The competition has been staged most years since, with an absence between 1967 and 1977 when DR withdrew from the contest. Since the 2000s the show has typically featured two semi-finals or a single grand final with around ten competing songs, decided by a combination of public televote and jury.

Format

Artists and songwriters submit entries to DR, from which a shortlist is chosen. The winning song is performed by the selected artist at the Eurovision Song Contest later the same year. Recent editions have been hosted in arenas such as the Royal Arena in Copenhagen and Jyske Bank Boxen in Herning.

Notable winners

Other well-known winners include Birthe Wilke, Tommy Seebach (three times), Birthe Kjær, Rollo & King, Malene Mortensen, Chanée & N'evergreen, A Friend in London, Soluna Samay, Basim, Anti Social Media, Lighthouse X, Anja Nissen, Leonora, Ben & Tan, Fyr & Flamme, Reddi, Reiley and Saba.
