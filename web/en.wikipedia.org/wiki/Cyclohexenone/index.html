Cyclohexenone

2-Cyclohexenone
Formula: C6H8O
Type: α,β-unsaturated ketone

Cyclohexenone is an organic compound consisting of a six-membered ring with a ketone group and a carbon–carbon double bond. The most common isomer, 2-cyclohexen-1-one, is an α,β-unsaturated ketone widely used as a building block in organic synthesis.

Synthesis and reactivity

2-Cyclohexenone can be prepared by oxidation of cyclohexene or by Birch reduction of anisole followed by hydrolysis. As an enone, it is a good Michael acceptor: nucleophiles add to the β-carbon in conjugate addition, and it serves as an intermediate in the Robinson annulation used to build fused ring systems such as steroids.

Related compounds

The six-membered enone ring appears in numerous natural products and pharmaceuticals. 3-Cyclohexenone, the unconjugated isomer, is less common but also synthetically useful.
