Add-on

An add-on is additional content or functionality provided for a product, typically software or games. In computing, add-ons include browser extensions and plug-ins that extend the features of an application. In video gaming, downloadable content (DLC) and expansion packs serve as add-ons that introduce new levels, characters, or story material.

Software

Browser add-ons such as ad blockers, password managers, and developer tools are distributed through official marketplaces maintained by browser vendors, where they undergo varying degrees of review. Application plug-ins similarly allow third parties to add filters, file format support, and integrations to host programs such as image editors and digital audio workstations.

Other uses

In finance and insurance, "add-on" can refer to optional products bundled with a loan or policy. In retail, add-on sales describe supplementary purchases offered at the point of sale.
