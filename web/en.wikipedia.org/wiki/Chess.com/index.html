Chess.com is an internet chess server and news and social networking website. It is the largest online chess platform in the world, offering free and subscription-based play, puzzles, lessons, computer analysis, and coverage of major chess events.

History

The chess.com domain was originally registered in 1995 for a company selling chess tutoring software. Entrepreneurs Erik Allebest and Jay Severson purchased the domain in 2005 and relaunched it in 2007 as a chess community and playing site. It grew steadily, passing rival Internet Chess Club, and expanded dramatically during the online chess boom of 2020–2021, driven by the pandemic, the popularity of the Netflix series The Queen's Gambit, and chess streaming on Twitch. In 2022–2023 the company acquired the Play Magnus Group, founded by world champion Magnus Carlsen.

Features and events

The site hosts live and correspondence-style games in formats from bullet to daily chess, computer opponents ("bots") with personalities, puzzle modes such as Puzzle Rush, and titled-player events including Titled Tuesday and the Speed Chess Championship. Chess.com also publishes news and maintains an anti-cheating (fair play) programme using statistical detection.
