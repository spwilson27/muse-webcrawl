buildingSMART

buildingSMART, formerly known as the International Alliance for Interoperability (IAI), is an international organisation that develops and maintains open standards for building information modeling (BIM) to improve the exchange of information across the built-asset industry. It is a not-for-profit body with regional chapters worldwide.

Standards

buildingSMART's best-known standard is the Industry Foundation Classes (IFC), an open, neutral data model for describing building and infrastructure data, standardised as ISO 16739. Other standards and services maintained by the organisation include:

  • the BIM Collaboration Format (BCF) for communicating issues in BIM models;
  • the buildingSMART Data Dictionary (bSDD), an online service for definitions and classifications;
  • Information Delivery Specification (IDS) for defining information requirements;
  • model view definitions and the Information Delivery Manual (IDM, ISO 29481).

History

The alliance was founded in 1994 by a group of companies including Autodesk, initially to promote interoperability between construction software applications. It was rebranded as buildingSMART in the 2000s and works closely with ISO and other standards bodies to advance "openBIM".
