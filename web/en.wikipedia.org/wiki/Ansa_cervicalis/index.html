Ansa cervicalis
FromCervical plexus (C1–C3)

The ansa cervicalis is a loop of nerves formed by nerve roots of the cervical plexus (C1–C3) in the neck. It lies embedded in the anterior wall of the carotid sheath and supplies most of the infrahyoid ("strap") muscles.

Structure

The loop has two roots. The superior root is formed by fibres from C1 (travelling briefly with the hypoglossal nerve) and descends in front of the carotid sheath. The inferior root arises from C2 and C3 and descends around the internal jugular vein. The two roots join to form the loop.

Function

Branches from the ansa cervicalis innervate the omohyoid, sternothyroid and sternohyoid muscles, which depress the hyoid bone during swallowing and speech. The thyrohyoid and geniohyoid muscles receive C1 fibres via the hypoglossal nerve rather than through the loop itself.
