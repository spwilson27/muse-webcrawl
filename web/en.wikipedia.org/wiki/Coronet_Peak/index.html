Coronet Peak

Coronet Peak is a commercial skifield in the Otago region of the South Island of New Zealand, about 18 kilometres (11 mi) northeast of Queenstown. At 1,649 metres (5,410 ft), with a skiable area of some 280 hectares, it was the first commercial skifield to open in New Zealand, in 1947.

Coronet Peak

Location: Near Queenstown, Otago, New Zealand

Summit: 1,649 m (5,410 ft)

Skiable area: ≈280 ha

Operator: NZSki

Features

Coronet Peak is known for its extensive snowmaking — among the largest systems in the Southern Hemisphere — its night skiing on Fridays and Saturdays, and its proximity to Queenstown, making it one of the most visited ski areas in the country. It hosts international race training and events in the New Zealand winter season.

Summer use

In summer the mountain offers sightseeing, mountain biking and hiking on the Remarkables range foothills overlooking Lake Wakatipu.
