Johnny Ventura

Juan de Dios Ventura Soriano (8 March 1940 – 28 July 2021), better known as Johnny Ventura, was a Dominican singer, bandleader, merengue composer and politician. Nicknamed "El Caballo Mayor" (The Biggest Horse), he was one of the most popular merengue artists in Dominican history and is credited with modernising merengue in the 1960s by combining it with rock and roll, faster rhythms and energetic stage choreography.

Ventura formed his band, the Combo Show, and recorded dozens of albums with hits such as "Patacón Pisao" and "Capullo y Sorullo". He later entered politics, serving as vice mayor and then mayor of Santo Domingo (1998–2002) and as a deputy in the National Congress, affiliated with the Dominican Revolutionary Party.

He died in Santiago de los Caballeros in 2021. See also Merengue music.
