Neuropsychological assessment

Neuropsychological assessment is the systematic evaluation of cognitive, behavioural and emotional functioning using standardised tests and procedures, in order to infer the integrity of brain function. It is used in the diagnosis and management of brain injury, dementia, stroke, epilepsy and psychiatric and developmental disorders.

Procedure

An assessment usually begins with a clinical interview and review of medical and educational history, followed by a battery of tests measuring domains such as intelligence, memory, language, attention, executive function, visuospatial ability and motor skills. Widely used instruments include the Wechsler Adult Intelligence Scale, the Wechsler Memory Scale, the Mini–Mental State Examination and the Montreal Cognitive Assessment.

Uses

Results help clinicians localise dysfunction, establish baselines, plan rehabilitation and education, assess decision-making capacity, and monitor changes over time or in response to treatment.
