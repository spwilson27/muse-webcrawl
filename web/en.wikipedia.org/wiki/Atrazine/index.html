Atrazine

Atrazine
TypeTriazine herbicide
Chemical formulaC8H14ClN5
Main useWeed control in maize and sorghum

Atrazine is a chlorinated triazine herbicide used to control broadleaf and grassy weeds, principally in maize (corn), sorghum and sugarcane. It works by inhibiting photosynthesis in susceptible plants.

Use and regulation

Atrazine has been one of the most widely used herbicides in the United States since its introduction in 1958, but it is a common contaminant of groundwater and drinking water. The European Union banned it in 2004 because of persistent water contamination, while it remains registered, with restrictions, in the United States and Australia.

Health and environmental effects

Atrazine is an endocrine disruptor in some animal studies, and its effects on amphibians and human health have been extensively studied and debated by regulators and researchers.
