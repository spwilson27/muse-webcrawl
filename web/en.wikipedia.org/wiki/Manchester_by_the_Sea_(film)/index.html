Manchester by the Sea

Manchester by the Sea is a 2016 American drama film written and directed by Kenneth Lonergan, starring Casey Affleck, Michelle Williams, Kyle Chandler and Lucas Hedges. It premiered at the Sundance Film Festival and was among the first films distributed by a streaming service (Amazon Studios) to receive major Academy Award recognition.

Plot

Lee Chandler (Affleck), a withdrawn janitor in Quincy, Massachusetts, learns that his elder brother Joe has died of a heart attack and that Joe's will names him guardian of his teenage nephew Patrick. Reluctantly returning to his home town of Manchester-by-the-Sea, Lee struggles with the practicalities of caring for Patrick while flashbacks reveal why he left: years earlier, his negligence caused a house fire that killed his three children, a trauma that ended his marriage to Randi (Williams) and left him unable to forgive himself. A chance street encounter with Randi becomes the film's emotional climax. In the end Lee arranges for Patrick to be adopted by family friends so the boy can stay in Manchester, admitting, "I can't beat it."

Reception and awards

The film was acclaimed for its writing, direction and performances. At the 89th Academy Awards it won Best Actor (Affleck) and Best Original Screenplay (Lonergan), from six nominations including Best Picture. It also won two BAFTAs and is frequently cited among the finest films of the 2010s.
