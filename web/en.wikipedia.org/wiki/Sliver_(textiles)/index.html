Sliver (textiles)

In textile manufacturing, a sliver (pronounced "sly-ver") is a loose, continuous strand or rope of loosely assembled fibres, produced during the preparation of natural or staple fibres for spinning.

Production

Carding machines comb raw fibre into a thin web, which is condensed into a carded sliver. Subsequent drawing (doubling and drafting several slivers together) evens out thickness and aligns the fibres. The sliver is then drawn into a finer roving before spinning into yarn.

Importance

Sliver quality, measured by evenness and fibre orientation, strongly influences the strength and uniformity of the finished yarn. Container vessels called sliver cans collect and transport slivers between machines in a spinning mill.
