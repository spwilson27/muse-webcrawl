Niko Bellic

Niko Bellic is a fictional character and the playable protagonist of the 2008 video game Grand Theft Auto IV, developed by Rockstar North. He is voiced by Michael Hollick.

Character

Niko is an Eastern European war veteran — implied to be Serbian — who fought in the Yugoslav Wars as a teenager and was betrayed by a member of his unit, an experience that haunts him. Lured to Liberty City by his cousin Roman's exaggerated tales of wealth, and fleeing trouble at home, he arrives to find Roman poor, indebted and running a failing taxi firm. Niko is drawn into the city's underworld while hunting the man who betrayed his squad.

Reception

The character was widely praised as one of gaming's most fully realised protagonists: a criminal anti-hero written with guilt, dry humour and reluctance rather than glamour. Critics cited his story as a darker, more grounded take on the American Dream. Hollick's performance, and a public dispute over his pay (reportedly $100,000 for the role), also drew attention to voice-actor compensation in games.
