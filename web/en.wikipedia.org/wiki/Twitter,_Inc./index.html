Twitter, Inc.

Twitter, Inc. was an American social media company based in San Francisco, California, which operated the microblogging service Twitter from the service's launch in 2006 until the company's dissolution following its acquisition by Elon Musk in 2022.

History

Twitter grew out of the podcasting company Odeo; its short-message format, built around 140-character "tweets", popularised hashtags, retweets and real-time public conversation. The company went public in November 2013. Under CEO Jack Dorsey and later Parag Agrawal it struggled to match the profitability of larger rivals. In October 2022 Elon Musk completed a 4 billion acquisition, took the company private, and in 2023 folded it into X Corp., rebranding the service as X.
