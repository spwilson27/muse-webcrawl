Sukhoi Su-75 Checkmate

RoleStealth multirole fighter (light tactical aircraft)
ManufacturerSukhoi (United Aircraft Corporation)
StatusIn development
Program costReportedly low-cost, export-oriented programme

The Sukhoi Su-75 Checkmate is a single-engine, stealth, fifth-generation light tactical fighter aircraft being developed by Sukhoi and the United Aircraft Corporation of Russia, primarily for export markets. The aircraft was unveiled as a full-scale mock-up at the MAKS air show in July 2021, in a ceremony attended by President Vladimir Putin, and was shown internationally at the Dubai Airshow later that year.

Design

The Su-75 is a single-seat aircraft with a single engine — reported to be the Saturn Izdeliye 30 engine also intended for the Su-57 — a V-tail, a large chin-mounted "divertless"-style intake beneath the fuselage, and internal weapons bays supplemented by side bays for short-range air-to-air missiles. Sukhoi claims a top speed of about Mach 1.8, a combat radius of roughly 1,500 km with internal fuel, a payload of about 7,400 kg, and extensive use of artificial intelligence in the cockpit systems. The design draws on technologies developed for the twin-engine Su-57, and unmanned and two-seat variants have been proposed.

Development and marketing

Rostec heavily promoted the Checkmate as an affordable fifth-generation fighter, quoting a target unit price of US$25–30 million, and marketed it to potential customers in the Middle East, Africa, Latin America, and Asia. A first flight was initially projected for 2023 but slipped repeatedly; Western sanctions following Russia's 2022 invasion of Ukraine, and the absence of a launch export customer, have slowed the programme, though Russian officials have continued to state that a prototype is in production at the Komsomolsk-on-Amur plant.
