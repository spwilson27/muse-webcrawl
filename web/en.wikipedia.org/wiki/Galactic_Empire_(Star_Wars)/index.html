The Galactic Empire is a fictional autocracy featured in the Star Wars franchise. It was first introduced in the 1977 film Star Wars and appears in its two sequels: The Empire Strikes Back (1980) and Return of the Jedi (1983). The Empire is the main antagonist in the original trilogy, opposing the Rebel Alliance's struggle to restore the Galactic Republic.

The Empire was established in 19 BBY by Supreme Chancellor Palpatine — secretly the Sith Lord Darth Sidious — who converted the Republic into an empire under the pretext of security following the Clone Wars, declared himself Emperor for life, and exterminated the Jedi Order through Order 66. The Empire ruled through fear and military might, constructing planet-destroying superweapons including the Death Star and its successor, and maintained order through the Imperial Military and enforcers such as Darth Vader.

Depiction

In the films, the Empire is depicted as a totalitarian regime that dissolved the Imperial Senate, committed acts of mass violence including the destruction of the planet Alderaan, and oppressed non-human species and dissenters. Its reign effectively ended with the deaths of Palpatine and Vader at the Battle of Endor in 4 ABY, after which remnants of the Empire fragmented; some later reorganised into the First Order.

Origins and inspiration

George Lucas drew on historical and contemporary parallels, including Nazi Germany, Imperial Rome and the United States during the Vietnam War era, in designing the Empire's aesthetics and politics; the stormtroopers' name echoes German shock troops, and Imperial officers' uniforms resemble historical military dress.
