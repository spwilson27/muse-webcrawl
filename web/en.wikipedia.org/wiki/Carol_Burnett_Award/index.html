The Carol Burnett Award is an honorary Golden Globe Award presented for outstanding contributions to television, on or off the screen. It is the television counterpart of the Cecil B. DeMille Award for film, and was first presented at the 76th Golden Globe Awards in January 2019. The award is named after its inaugural recipient, the comedian and actress Carol Burnett, a five-time Golden Globe winner for The Carol Burnett Show.
