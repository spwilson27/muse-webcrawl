Mission: Impossible (film series)

Mission: Impossible is a series of American action spy films, based on the 1966 television series created by Bruce Geller. The films follow the missions of the IMF (Impossible Missions Force), led by Ethan Hunt, played by Tom Cruise. The series began in 1996 and is among the highest-grossing film series of all time.

Overview

The first film, directed by Brian De Palma, follows Ethan Hunt as he is framed for the murder of his IMF team during a mission in Prague and sets out to uncover the mole. Later entries expanded the series' emphasis on large-scale practical stunts performed by Cruise himself, including climbing the Burj Khalifa in Ghost Protocol, a HALO jump and helicopter chase in Fallout, and a motorcycle cliff jump in Dead Reckoning.

Recurring elements

The series retains Lalo Schifrin's iconic theme from the television series, the "Your mission, should you choose to accept it" briefing, and self-destructing mission messages. Recurring characters include Luther Stickell (Ving Rhames), Benji Dunn (Simon Pegg), and Ilsa Faust (Rebecca Ferguson).

Reception

The films have grossed over $4 billion worldwide. Fallout is often regarded as the series' critical peak, praised for its direction, stunts, and screenplay.
