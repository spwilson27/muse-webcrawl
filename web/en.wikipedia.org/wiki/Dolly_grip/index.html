Dolly grip

In cinematography, a dolly grip is a dedicated grip (a technician responsible for camera support equipment) who is trained to operate the camera dolly — the wheeled platform that moves the camera smoothly through a shot. The dolly grip works closely with the director of photography and camera operator to execute precisely timed pushes, pulls and tracking moves.

Role

Duties include levelling and laying dolly track, setting marks for the start and end of a move, pushing the dolly at a constant speed while cueing on the action, and coordinating with the focus puller's marks and the boom operator. A skilled dolly grip's moves are a significant element of a film's visual style; elaborate "dolly shots" are rehearsed like choreography.
