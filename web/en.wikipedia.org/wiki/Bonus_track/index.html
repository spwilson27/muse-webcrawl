Bonus track

A bonus track is a piece of music added to a commercial release of an album or single that was not part of the original standard edition. Bonus material may include previously unreleased songs, demo or live recordings, remixes, B-sides or covers.

Purpose

Bonus tracks are used as marketing incentives: to persuade fans to buy a particular edition — for example a Japanese pressing, a deluxe or retailer-exclusive edition, or a reissue — or to reward early purchasers. During the CD era, Japanese releases of Western albums routinely included one or more extra tracks, partly to offset the higher domestic price of Japanese CDs relative to imports.

Hidden tracks

A related phenomenon is the hidden track, a song placed on a disc without being listed, often after a long period of silence following the final listed track, or in the pregap before the first track.
