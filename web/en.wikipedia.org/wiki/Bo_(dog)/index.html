Bo (October 9, 2008 – May 8, 2021) was a neutered male Portuguese Water Dog owned by the family of Barack Obama, the 44th president of the United States. Bo was a gift from Senator Ted Kennedy and his wife, and became known as the "First Dog" during Obama's presidency.

Background

During the 2008 presidential campaign, Obama promised his daughters Malia and Sasha that they could get a dog after the election. Because Malia was allergic, the family sought a hypoallergenic breed, narrowing the choice to the Labradoodle and the Portuguese Water Dog. Bo came from a breeder in Texas and had originally been sold to another family, who returned him; he was then given to the Obamas by the Kennedys, whose own Portuguese Water Dogs were related to him. He was named by Malia and Sasha after family cousins' cat and also in reference to musician Bo Diddley. His pedigree name was Amigo's New Hope.

Life at the White House

Bo was officially introduced on 14 April 2009, attracting international media attention. He made appearances at official events, was featured in children's books and merchandise, and took part in the annual Easter Egg Roll and visits to troops and children's hospitals. In August 2013 the Obamas adopted a second Portuguese Water Dog, Sunny. Bo's official duties were light, but he became one of the most famous presidential pets; the White House released a "baseball card" with his biography and facts, including that his favourite food was tomatoes and that he did not know how to swim at first.

Death

Bo died of cancer on 8 May 2021, aged 12. Michelle Obama announced his death on social media, writing that he had been "a constant, gentle presence in our lives".
