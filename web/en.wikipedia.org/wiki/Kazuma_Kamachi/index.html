Kazuma Kamachi

Placeholder image: light novel cover artwork.

Kazuma Kamachi (鎌池和馬, Kamachi Kazuma) is a Japanese light novel author best known as the creator of A Certain Magical Index and its spin-offs. His works, published under the Dengeki Bunko imprint, have sold tens of millions of copies and spawned manga, anime, film and video game adaptations.

Career

Kamachi debuted in 2004 with the first volume of A Certain Magical Index, which grew into one of the longest-running light novel series, spanning the original series, New Testament, and Genesis Testament continuations. He is noted for an exceptionally prolific output, at times releasing novels at a near-monthly pace, and for supervising spin-offs such as A Certain Scientific Railgun and A Certain Scientific Accelerator.

Other works

Beyond the Index franchise, Kamachi has written series including Heavy Object, The Unexplored Summon://Blood-Sign, and Apocalypse Witch. He has also contributed original stories to manga and anime projects, including Million Arthur material.

Style

Kamachi's writing blends science fiction and fantasy, elaborate worldbuilding mixing science and magic systems, and large ensembles of characters. He keeps a low public profile, and his real name and appearance are not publicly known.
