Lie bracket

In mathematics, the Lie bracket is the bilinear operation that forms the defining structure of a Lie algebra. For elements x and y of a Lie algebra it is written [x, y] and satisfies bilinearity, alternativity ([x, x] = 0), and the Jacobi identity.

Examples

The most familiar example is the commutator of matrices, [A, B] = AB − BA. On smooth vector fields, the Lie bracket measures the failure of their flows to commute and is fundamental in differential geometry and the theory of Lie groups. The cross product in three dimensions is also a Lie bracket.

Related notions

Related operations include the Lie bracket of vector fields, the Poisson bracket of classical mechanics, and graded Lie brackets on differential forms. The term honours the Norwegian mathematician Sophus Lie.
