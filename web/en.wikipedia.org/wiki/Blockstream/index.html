Blockstream is a blockchain technology company founded in 2014 by cryptographer Adam Back and several Bitcoin Core developers. It is one of the main funders of Bitcoin Core development and is best known for creating the Liquid Network, a Bitcoin sidechain for faster settlement between exchanges.

Products and projects

Blockstream operates the Blockstream Satellite network, which broadcasts the Bitcoin blockchain from space so it can be accessed without an internet connection. Its consumer products include the Jade hardware wallet and the Blockstream app, and its Elements platform provides the basis for federated sidechains. The company has raised multiple rounds of venture funding and also runs Bitcoin mining operations.
