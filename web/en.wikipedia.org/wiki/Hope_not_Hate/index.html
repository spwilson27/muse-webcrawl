Hope not Hate

Hope not Hate (stylised HOPE not hate) is a British advocacy group and campaigning organisation founded in 2004 to counter racism and the far right, particularly the British National Party (BNP).

Activities

The organisation grew out of the anti-fascist magazine Searchlight and combined community campaigning, voter-registration drives and research on far-right and extremist movements. Its electoral campaigns against the BNP in the mid-2000s, especially in Barking and Dagenham, were widely credited with helping to defeat the party electorally. Since then it has published research on the international far right, Islamist extremism and online radicalisation, and campaigns on anti-Muslim prejudice and antisemitism.
