Oxford Nanopore Technologies

Oxford Nanopore Technologies plc is a British company that develops and commercialises nanopore sequencing products for the direct, real-time analysis of DNA and RNA. It was founded in 2005 as a spin-out from the University of Oxford, based on research by Hagan Bayley and others.

Technology

Nanopore sequencing passes a strand of DNA or RNA through a protein pore set in a membrane; the characteristic disruption of an ionic current as different bases pass through allows the sequence to be read in real time. Because reads are not limited by synthesis, the platform produces very long reads, and its devices stream data as it is generated.

Products

The company's flagship MinION, launched commercially in 2015, is a portable USB-powered sequencer about the size of a stapler; larger devices include the benchtop GridION and the high-throughput PromethION. The portability of the MinION enabled field sequencing during the Ebola outbreak in West Africa, the Zika epidemic, and aboard the International Space Station. Oxford Nanopore listed on the London Stock Exchange in 2021.
