Meech Lake

Meech Lake (French: Lac Meech) is a lake in the Gatineau Hills of Quebec, Canada, within Gatineau Park, about 20 kilometres northwest of Ottawa. It is best known as the site of Willson House, the federal government conference centre where the Meech Lake Accord was negotiated in 1987.

The lake and park

Meech Lake lies in the southern part of Gatineau Park, the large federal park in the National Capital Region managed by the National Capital Commission. The lake is popular for swimming at its two beaches (Blanchet and O'Brien), canoeing, and hiking on the surrounding trails. Cottages around the lake, some predating the park, have been the subject of long-running disputes between residents and park authorities.

The Meech Lake Accord

On 30 April 1987, Prime Minister Brian Mulroney met the ten provincial premiers at Willson House on the lake to seek Quebec's consent to the Constitution Act, 1982, which Quebec had not signed. The resulting Meech Lake Accord proposed constitutional amendments recognizing Quebec as a "distinct society" within Canada, along with changes to Senate appointments, immigration, and the amending formula. The accord required ratification by all provincial legislatures within three years; it ultimately failed in 1990 when Manitoba and Newfoundland did not ratify it, an event with lasting consequences for Canadian politics and Quebec nationalism.
