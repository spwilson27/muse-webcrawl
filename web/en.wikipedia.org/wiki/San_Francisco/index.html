San Francisco

San Francisco is a city in Northern California, known for its steep hills, Victorian and Edwardian architecture, cable cars, fog, and its position at the tip of a peninsula between the Pacific Ocean and San Francisco Bay.

History

Founded as a Spanish mission and presidio in 1776, the city boomed during the California Gold Rush of 1849. The 1906 earthquake and fire destroyed much of it. In the 20th century it became a centre of the counterculture, the LGBTQ rights movement and, increasingly, the technology industry centred on nearby Silicon Valley.

Landmarks

Landmarks include the Golden Gate Bridge, Alcatraz Island, Fisherman's Wharf, Chinatown — the oldest in North America — and the Painted Ladies row houses.
