Dar es Salaam

Dar es Salaam is the largest city in Tanzania and its former capital (Dodoma became the official capital in 1974, with many government functions moving there since). It is a major port on the Indian Ocean with a population of over 5 million in the city and about 7 million in the metropolitan area, and one of the fastest-growing cities in Africa.

Founded in the 1860s by Sultan Majid bin Said of Zanzibar ('haven of peace'), Dar es Salaam became the capital of German East Africa and then British Tanganyika. Its harbour handles most of Tanzania's trade and transit cargo for landlocked Zambia, Malawi, Burundi and eastern DRC. Landmarks include the Askari Monument, Kariakoo Market, the National Museum with its Olduvai Gorge fossils, and the beaches and islands of Bongoyo and Mbudya.
