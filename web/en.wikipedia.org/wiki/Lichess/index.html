Lichess

Lichess
Type: Internet chess server
Founded: 2010
Founder: Thibault Duplessis
Model: Free and open source, donation-funded

Lichess is a free and open-source Internet chess server run by a non-profit organisation of the same name. It was founded in 2010 by French programmer Thibault Duplessis. The name combines "live/light/libre" with "chess".

Features

Lichess offers online play in standard chess and variants such as Chess960, Crazyhouse, and Antichess; computer analysis powered by the open-source Stockfish engine; puzzles, studies, tournaments including hourly arenas, and broadcasts of major events. Its software is released under free licences, and the service carries no advertising, being funded entirely by donations.

Popularity

The site grew rapidly in the late 2010s and during the 2020 online chess boom, becoming one of the two most-visited chess websites in the world, alongside Chess.com, and the most popular free alternative. Its database of games and open API are widely used by researchers and developers.
