Mikhail Khovanov (born 1972) is a Russian-American mathematician known for work in representation theory, algebraic geometry and knot theory. He is a professor at Columbia University.

Khovanov homology

Around 1999–2000, Khovanov constructed a homology theory for knots and links whose graded Euler characteristic recovers the Jones polynomial. This "categorification" of the Jones polynomial — Khovanov homology — is stronger than the polynomial itself and opened a new field: it was later connected to representation theory, symplectic geometry and physics, and extensions such as Khovanov–Rozansky homology categorify other quantum link invariants. Khovanov has also contributed to categorification of quantum groups and Heisenberg algebras.

Background

Khovanov studied at Moscow State University and received his PhD from Yale University in 1997 under Igor Frenkel, before holding positions at UC Davis and Johns Hopkins.
