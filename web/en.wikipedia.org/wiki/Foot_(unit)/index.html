Foot (unit)

Foot
Unit of: length
Symbol: ft
1 ft = 12 inches = 0.3048 m exactly

The foot (plural feet; symbol ft) is a unit of length in the imperial and United States customary systems of measurement. Since the International Yard and Pound Agreement of 1959, one foot has been defined as exactly 0.3048 metres. A foot is subdivided into 12 inches, and three feet make one yard.

History

The foot as a unit is ancient and was originally based on the length of a human foot, so its precise length varied between cultures and eras. Roman, Greek, Egyptian and medieval European feet all differed somewhat. Standardisation proceeded gradually in Britain and its colonies; the imperial foot was defined in the nineteenth century, and the international foot of 0.3048 m was adopted in 1959 by the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and South Africa.

Usage

The foot remains in common use in the United States for everyday measurements, and internationally in aviation, where altitudes are commonly given in feet. In the United Kingdom it is still widely used informally for human height. The related survey foot, defined slightly differently, was used in United States land surveying until its deprecation at the end of 2022.
