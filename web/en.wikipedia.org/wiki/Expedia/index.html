Expedia

Expedia is an American online travel agency owned by Expedia Group, headquartered in Seattle, Washington. The website and mobile app can be used to book airline tickets, hotel reservations, car rentals, cruise ships, and vacation packages. Expedia.com was launched in 1996 as a division of Microsoft, founded by Rich Barton, and was spun off in an IPO in 1999.

History

Expedia was one of the first major online travel agencies. After passing through ownership by IAC/InterActiveCorp, it was spun off as Expedia, Inc. in 2005 (renamed Expedia Group in 2018). The group grew by acquiring brands including Hotels.com, Hotwire, Orbitz, Travelocity, trivago, Vrbo (formerly HomeAway), and Egencia. The Expedia brand operates localized websites in dozens of countries and offers a loyalty program, One Key (launched 2023), shared with Hotels.com and Vrbo.

Business

Expedia earns revenue from merchant and agency commissions on bookings and from advertising. It is one of the two dominant Western online travel groups, alongside Booking Holdings. The company has faced regulatory scrutiny in several countries over rate-parity clauses and ranking practices.
