Mizo National Front

Flag of the Mizo National Front

The Mizo National Front (MNF) is a regional political party in Mizoram, India. It began as the Mizo National Famine Front, founded by Laldenga in 1959 to protest the Assam government's handling of the Mautam bamboo famine, and became a separatist insurgent organisation that declared Mizo independence in 1966.

History

The MNF launched an armed uprising in March 1966, leading to two decades of insurgency in the Mizo hills. The Mizoram Peace Accord of 1986 ended the conflict: the MNF laid down arms and Mizoram became a full state of India in 1987, with Laldenga as chief minister. The party has since alternated in power with the Indian National Congress and the Zoram People's Movement in the Mizoram Legislative Assembly.
