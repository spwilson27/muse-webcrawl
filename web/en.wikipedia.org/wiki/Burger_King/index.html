Burger King

Burger King Corporation (BK), stylised as Burger King, is an American multinational chain of hamburger fast food restaurants. Headquartered in Miami-Dade County, Florida, the company was founded on 28 July 1953 in Jacksonville, Florida, as Insta-Burger King by Keith J. Kramer and his wife's uncle, Matthew Burns. Its menu has expanded from basic hamburgers, French fries, sodas and shakes to a larger and more diverse set of products; the Whopper sandwich, introduced in 1957, is its signature product.

History

After Insta-Burger King ran into financial difficulties, its Miami franchisees James McLamore and David R. Edgerton bought the company in 1959 and renamed it Burger King. Over the next half century the firm changed hands four times: Pillsbury bought it in 1967; Grand Metropolitan acquired Pillsbury in 1989; and after further ownership by TPG Capital and others, it was bought in 2010 by 3G Capital for $3.26 billion. In 2014 Burger King merged with Canada's Tim Hortons under the new parent Restaurant Brands International, headquartered in Oakville, Ontario.

Products and marketing

Burger King's flame-grilling differentiates it from competitors; the Whopper — a quarter-pound beef patty with mayonnaise, lettuce, tomato, pickles, ketchup and onions — is customisable under the long-running slogan "Have It Your Way" (1974). Other staples include the Big King, BK Chicken Fries and the Impossible Whopper (2019). Marketing campaigns include the creepy "King" mascot (revived 2004), the Subservient Chicken viral campaign and the award-winning "Moldy Whopper" (2020). Burger King is usually the world's second-largest hamburger chain after McDonald's.
