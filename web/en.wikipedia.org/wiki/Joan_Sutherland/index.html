Joan Sutherland

Dame Joan Alston Sutherland (1926–2010) was an Australian dramatic coloratura soprano, celebrated as one of the greatest bel canto singers of the twentieth century. Luciano Pavarotti called her "the voice of the century"; Italian audiences dubbed her La Stupenda.

Career

Born in Sydney, Sutherland studied there and in London, joining the Royal Opera, Covent Garden in 1952. Her breakthrough came in 1959 as Lucia di Lammermoor under Tullio Serafin, followed by triumphs at La Scala, the Metropolitan Opera and elsewhere in Donizetti, Bellini and Handel. She frequently performed with her husband, conductor Richard Bonynge, and with Pavarotti and Marilyn Horne.

Retirement and honours

Sutherland retired from the stage in 1990 after a farewell Die Fledermaus at Covent Garden. She was appointed DBE and was among the first recipients of the Order of Merit. She died in Geneva in 2010.
