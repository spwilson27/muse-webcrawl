Barbenheimer (a portmanteau of Barbie and Oppenheimer) was a cultural phenomenon surrounding the simultaneous theatrical release, on 21 July 2023, of two tonally opposite blockbuster films: Greta Gerwig's pink fantasy comedy Barbie, based on the Mattel doll, and Christopher Nolan's dark biographical thriller Oppenheimer, about J. Robert Oppenheimer and the atomic bomb. The contrast between the films — and the scheduling coincidence after Warner Bros. set Barbie against the date of Nolan's first film with Universal — generated an enormous wave of internet memes, merchandise and same-day double-feature viewing plans.

The phenomenon

Rather than cannibalising each other as conventional counterprogramming analysis might predict, the two films boosted one another: audiences embraced seeing them as a double feature (debating the ideal viewing order), cinemas reported large numbers of same-day ticket purchases for both, and the stars of each film publicly supported the other. The opening weekend was one of the biggest in box-office history, with Barbie grossing $162 million and Oppenheimer $82.5 million domestically; Barbie became the highest-grossing film of 2023 and Warner Bros.' highest-grossing release ever, while Oppenheimer became the highest-grossing biographical film of all time.

Reception and awards

Both films were critical successes and dominated the 2023–24 awards season: at the 96th Academy Awards, Oppenheimer won seven Oscars including Best Picture and Best Director, while Barbie won Best Original Song ("What Was I Made For?") and led in nominations discourse around the perceived snubbing of Gerwig and Margot Robbie. The phenomenon was widely analysed as a revival of event cinema after the pandemic-era downturn and as a case study in meme-driven marketing; in Japan, however, fan-made mash-up imagery combining Barbie with atomic-bomb imagery caused offence near the anniversaries of the Hiroshima and Nagasaki bombings, prompting an apology from Warner Bros.' Japanese arm and criticism of the US studio's social-media engagement with the memes.
