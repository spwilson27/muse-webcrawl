Tamil culture

Tamil culture is the culture of the Tamil people, speakers of the Tamil language, one of the world's longest-surviving classical languages. Tamils are native principally to Tamil Nadu in southern India and to northern and eastern Sri Lanka, with a large global diaspora in Malaysia, Singapore, South Africa, and elsewhere.

Language and literature

Tamil literature extends back more than two thousand years to the Sangam corpus, and includes the ethical classic Tirukkural. Inscriptions, palm-leaf manuscripts, and a continuous literary tradition make Tamil one of the great classical civilisations of Asia.

Arts

Carnatic music and the dance form Bharatanatyam are hallmarks of Tamil performing arts. Temple architecture in the Dravidian style — vast gopurams covered in sculpture, as at Madurai and Thanjavur — and Chola bronze casting are internationally renowned. Tamil cinema ("Kollywood") is a major modern industry.

Religion and festivals

Most Tamils are Hindus, with significant Christian and Muslim minorities and an ancient Jain heritage. Festivals include Pongal, the harvest thanksgiving in January, and temple festivals honouring deities such as Murugan and Meenakshi.

Cuisine

Tamil food is typically served on a banana leaf and centres on rice with sambar, rasam, and vegetable dishes, alongside staples such as idli, dosa, and filter coffee.
