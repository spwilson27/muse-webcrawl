George Fullerton is a Canadian musician, comedian, and actor, best known as one half of the Montreal-based comedy music duo Bowser and Blue, alongside Rick Blue (Richard Elger).

Career

Fullerton, a guitarist and singer, formed Bowser and Blue with Rick Blue in 1978. Performing mainly in Montreal's English-speaking theatre and folk venues, the duo became known for satirical songs about Quebec politics, language laws, and Canadian life, including pieces such as "Working Where the Fern Grows". They released numerous albums and appeared regularly at the Just for Laughs festival and on Canadian television.

Other work

In addition to the duo's stage shows and recordings, Fullerton has worked in Montreal theatre and as a voice and session musician. Bowser and Blue continued performing for more than four decades, becoming fixtures of Montreal's anglophone cultural scene.
