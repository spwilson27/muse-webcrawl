Half Dome

Half Dome
Elevation: 8,839 ft (2,694 m)
Location: Yosemite National Park, California
Rock type: Granodiorite
First ascent: 1875, George G. Anderson

Half Dome is a granite dome at the eastern end of Yosemite Valley in Yosemite National Park, California. It rises more than 4,737 ft (1,444 m) above the valley floor and is one of the most recognised landmarks in the park and in American climbing.

Geology

Half Dome is a granodiorite monolith. Its sheer northwest face was shaped by exfoliation and by glaciers that undercut its base; contrary to popular belief, the other "half" never existed as a complete dome — the formation was never a full dome split in two.

Hiking and climbing

Thousands of hikers reach the summit each year via a 14- to 16-mile round-trip trail, the final 400 feet aided by steel cables installed in 1919; a permit lottery now limits numbers. The northwest face, first climbed in 1957 by Royal Robbins and party, was the first Grade VI climb in the United States and remains a classic big-wall route.
