Santa Clara County, California

Santa Clara County is a county in the US state of California, in the San Francisco Bay Area. Its county seat and largest city is San Jose. It is the heart of Silicon Valley, home to many major technology companies.

Santa Clara County
Seat: San Jose
Largest city: San Jose
Population: ~1.9 million (2020)
Area: ~1,304 sq mi
Region: Silicon Valley / Bay Area

Geography

The county occupies the Santa Clara Valley, bounded by the Santa Cruz Mountains to the west and the Diablo Range to the east, and borders San Francisco Bay to the north. Cities include Palo Alto, Mountain View, Sunnyvale, Cupertino, Santa Clara, Campbell, Los Gatos, Saratoga, Morgan Hill and Gilroy.

Economy

Once known as the "Valley of Heart's Delight" for its orchards, the county transformed after the 1950s into Silicon Valley, headquarters of Apple (Cupertino), Google (Mountain View), and many semiconductor and software firms. Stanford University lies partly in the county at Palo Alto.

History

One of California's original counties (1850), it is named for Mission Santa Clara de Asís, founded in 1777.
