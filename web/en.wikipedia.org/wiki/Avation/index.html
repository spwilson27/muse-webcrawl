Avation

Avation plc is a commercial passenger aircraft leasing company headquartered in Singapore and listed on the London Stock Exchange. The company owns and manages a fleet of turboprop and narrow-body jet aircraft which it leases to airlines around the world.

History

Avation was founded in 2006 by Jeff Chatfield, originally as a leasing business built around ATR turboprops. The company listed in London in 2010. It expanded from its early focus on the ATR 72 into narrow-body jets, ordering Airbus A320 family aircraft, and supplied aircraft to regional and low-cost carriers in Europe, Asia-Pacific and elsewhere. Customers have included airlines such as Virgin Australia Regional Airlines, airBaltic, Mandarin Airlines and Philippine Airlines.

Business

As a lessor, Avation purchases aircraft from manufacturers, finances them, and leases them to operators on multi-year terms, also providing fleet management services. Its portfolio has centred on ATR 72-600 turboprops, of which it has been one of the largest lessor-owners, alongside Airbus A320/A321 and A220-family jets.
