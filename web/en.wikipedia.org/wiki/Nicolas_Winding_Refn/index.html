Nicolas Winding Refn

Nicolas Winding Refn
Born: 29 September 1970, Copenhagen, Denmark
Occupation: Director, screenwriter, producer
Notable works: Pusher trilogy, Bronson, Drive, The Neon Demon

Nicolas Winding Refn (born 1970) is a Danish film director known for highly stylised, violent and neon-lit films.

Career

Refn debuted at 26 with the gritty Copenhagen crime film Pusher (1996) starring Mads Mikkelsen, followed by two sequels. Bronson (2008), with Tom Hardy, and Valhalla Rising (2009) brought international attention. Drive (2011), starring Ryan Gosling, won him Best Director at Cannes and became a cult hit with its synth soundtrack and minimalist style. Later films Only God Forgives (2013) and The Neon Demon (2016) divided critics. For streaming he made the series Too Old to Die Young (2019) and Copenhagen Cowboy (2022).

Style

Refn, who is colour-blind, favours strong contrasting colours, electronic scores (often by Cliff Martinez), long silences, graphic violence and fetishistic imagery. He founded the byNWR streaming platform for restored exploitation films.

Personal life

He is married to actress Liv Corfixen, who documented his work in My Life Directed by Nicolas Winding Refn.
