List of United States military bases

This article contains a list of United States military bases, including major installations of the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Space Force, and Coast Guard located within the United States and, in related lists, overseas. The Department of Defense manages thousands of sites worldwide, ranging from large posts housing tens of thousands of personnel to small reserve facilities.

Army

Major Army posts include Fort Liberty (North Carolina), Fort Cavazos (formerly Fort Hood, Texas), Fort Drum (New York), Fort Carson (Colorado), Fort Stewart (Georgia), and Joint Base Lewis–McChord (Washington). In 2023 several bases named for Confederate officers were redesignated.

Navy

Major naval bases include Naval Station Norfolk (Virginia), the largest naval station in the world; Naval Base San Diego (California); Naval Station Pearl Harbor (Hawaii); and Naval Submarine Base Kings Bay (Georgia).

Air Force and Space Force

Prominent bases include Joint Base San Antonio (Texas), Eglin AFB (Florida), Edwards AFB (California), Wright-Patterson AFB (Ohio), Peterson and Schriever Space Force Bases (Colorado), and Vandenberg Space Force Base (California).

Marine Corps and Coast Guard

The Marine Corps' largest bases are Camp Pendleton (California), Camp Lejeune (North Carolina), and Marine Corps Air Ground Combat Center Twentynine Palms (California). The Coast Guard operates air stations and sectors across the coasts and Great Lakes.

Overseas bases

The United States maintains a large network of overseas installations, notably in Germany (Ramstein Air Base), Japan (Kadena Air Base, Yokota Air Base), South Korea (Camp Humphreys), Italy, the United Kingdom, Guam, and the Middle East.
