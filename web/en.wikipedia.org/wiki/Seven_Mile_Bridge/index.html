Seven Mile Bridge

Seven Mile Bridge
Location: Florida Keys, Florida, USA
Length: 6.79 miles (10.93 km)
Carries: U.S. Route 1 (Overseas Highway)

The Seven Mile Bridge is a famous bridge in the Florida Keys, connecting Knight's Key (Marathon) in the Middle Keys to Little Duck Key in the Lower Keys. It carries the Overseas Highway, the section of U.S. Route 1 through the Keys.

History

The original bridge was built between 1909 and 1912 as part of Henry Flagler's Florida East Coast Railway Key West Extension. After the 1935 Labor Day hurricane destroyed the railway, the bridge was converted for automobile use. The current concrete bridge was constructed between 1978 and 1982.

The old bridge

A section of the old bridge remains open to pedestrians and cyclists as a popular fishing pier and access route to Pigeon Key, and it hosts the annual Seven Mile Bridge Run.
