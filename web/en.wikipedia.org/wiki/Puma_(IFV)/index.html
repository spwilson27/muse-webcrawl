Puma (IFV)


A Puma infantry fighting vehicle of the German Army

The Puma is a German infantry fighting vehicle (IFV) designed to replace the ageing Marder IFVs in service with the German Army. It is built by PSM Projekt System & Management, a joint venture of Krauss-Maffei Wegmann and Rheinmetall. Deliveries to the Bundeswehr began in 2015.

Design

The Puma carries a crew of three plus six infantry in its basic configuration. Its unmanned turret mounts a Rheinmetall MK30-2/ABM 30 mm autocannon capable of firing airburst munitions, and the vehicle can be fitted with Spike-LR anti-tank missiles via the MELLS launcher.

Protection

Protection is modular. In its air-transportable configuration the Puma weighs about 31.5 tonnes; with full add-on armour it reaches about 43 tonnes, making it among the heaviest IFVs in service. The armour protects against medium-calibre cannon fire, mines and IEDs, and the vehicle integrates the MUSS soft-kill active protection system.

Service history

The Puma has had a troubled introduction, with reliability problems widely reported; in December 2022 all 18 Pumas taking part in an exercise broke down, prompting Germany to pause further purchases until reliability improves. The vehicle has since undergone upgrades to the S1 standard.
