Kamal is a town and district (kecamatan) in Bangkalan Regency, East Java, Indonesia, located on the south-western coast of the island of Madura, facing the city of Surabaya across the Madura Strait.

Transport

Kamal was historically the principal ferry port linking Madura with Java: ferries ran from Kamal to Ujung port in Surabaya. After the opening of the Suramadu Bridge in 2009 — the longest bridge in Indonesia, connecting Bangkalan with Surabaya — ferry traffic declined sharply, though the port continues to operate. The town hosts a naval facility and is a gateway for travellers entering Madura from the west.

Education

Kamal is home to the main campus of Trunojoyo University (Universitas Trunojoyo Madura), a public university serving the island.
