List of massacres in Australia

This is a list of massacres in Australia — events in which five or more people were killed. Australia's history includes many massacres of Aboriginal Australians during the frontier wars that followed British colonisation from 1788, as well as massacres of settlers by Aboriginal groups, mass killings by police and settlers, and, in the modern era, mass shootings. Research by the University of Newcastle's Colonial Frontier Massacres project has documented hundreds of frontier massacre sites, mostly of Aboriginal people, between 1788 and 1930.

The 1996 Port Arthur massacre, in which Martin Bryant murdered 35 people, led directly to sweeping national gun law reforms (the National Firearms Agreement) under Prime Minister John Howard.
