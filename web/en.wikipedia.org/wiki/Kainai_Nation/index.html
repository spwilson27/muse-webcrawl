Kainai Nation

Kainai Nation
Also known as: Blood Tribe
Region: Southern Alberta, Canada
Reserve: Blood 148 (largest in Canada)
Confederacy: Blackfoot Confederacy (Siksikaitsitapi)

The Kainai Nation, also known as the Blood Tribe, is a First Nation in southern Alberta, Canada, and one of the four nations of the Blackfoot Confederacy. Its reserve, Blood 148, is the largest First Nations reserve in Canada by area.

History

The Kainai — "Many Chiefs" — were a powerful Plains people whose traditional territory ranged across what is now southern Alberta and northern Montana. They were signatories to Treaty 7 in 1877. The community endured the disruptions of the reserve era, residential schools and restrictions on ceremony, while maintaining its language, societies and governance.

Government and community

The Nation is governed by an elected chief and council based at Stand Off. It operates its own police service, schools, health services and Kainai Studies programmes, and is a member of the Blackfoot Confederacy Tribal Council. The annual Kainai Indian Days are a major powwow and rodeo gathering.
