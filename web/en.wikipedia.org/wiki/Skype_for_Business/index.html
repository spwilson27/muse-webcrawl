Skype for Business

Skype for Business client interface

Skype for Business (formerly Microsoft Lync and Office Communicator) was an enterprise communications platform developed by Microsoft, providing instant messaging, presence information, voice and video calls, and online meetings. It was offered both as on-premises server software and as part of Microsoft 365. Microsoft retired Skype for Business Online on 31 July 2021, directing users to Microsoft Teams.

History

The product line began with Office Communicator and the Live Communications Server/Office Communications Server in the 2000s. It was renamed Lync in 2010, pairing Lync Server 2010 with the Lync client. After Microsoft acquired Skype in 2011, the enterprise product was rebranded Skype for Business with the 2015 release, combining the Lync feature set with a user interface modeled on the consumer Skype client.

Features

Skype for Business integrated with Microsoft Outlook and Exchange, provided presence status drawn from users' calendars, supported federation with other organizations and with consumer Skype users, and could replace traditional PBX phone systems when paired with appropriate licensing and PSTN connectivity. The companion meeting tool allowed scheduled video conferences with screen sharing and whiteboarding.

Discontinuation

With the rise of Microsoft Teams, Microsoft ended Skype for Business Online in 2021; support for the on-premises Skype for Business Server continued on a longer lifecycle through subscription and extended support arrangements. The Mac client and mobile clients were withdrawn in parallel with the cloud retirement.
