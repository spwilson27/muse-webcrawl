Chiba (city)

Chiba (千葉市, Chiba-shi) is the capital city of Chiba Prefecture, Japan, located on Tokyo Bay about 40 kilometres east of central Tokyo. A designated city since 1992, it is a major port, industrial and commuter centre within the Greater Tokyo Area, with a population of roughly 975,000.

History

Chiba grew from a castle town of the Chiba clan, founded in the 12th century by Chiba Tsunetane. It became a town in 1889 and a city on January 1, 1921. Heavily bombed in 1945, it was rebuilt after the war, expanding dramatically through land reclamation on Tokyo Bay and the development of the Keiyō industrial zone, with steel, oil refining and power generation centred on the Port of Chiba — one of Japan's largest ports by cargo tonnage.

Landmarks and transport

The city is known for the Chiba Urban Monorail, at about 15.2 km the longest suspended monorail system in the world, and for Makuhari Messe, one of Japan's largest convention centres, in the waterfront Makuhari new city district. Chiba is home to Chiba University, the Chiba Lotte Marines baseball team, and the start of the Keiyō and Uchibō railway lines along the bay.
