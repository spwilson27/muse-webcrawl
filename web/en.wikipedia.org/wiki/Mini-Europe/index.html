Mini-Europe

Mini-Europe is a miniature park located in Bruparck, at the foot of the Atomium in Brussels, Belgium. Opened in 1989, it displays scale models (1:25) of famous monuments from across the European Union.

Exhibits

The park contains around 350 models representing some 80 cities, including the Eiffel Tower, Big Ben, the Acropolis, the Leaning Tower of Pisa and St Mark's Square, along with moving trains, boats and an erupting Vesuvius. Models are built to fine detail at substantial cost, some exceeding hundreds of thousands of euros.

Context

Mini-Europe presents itself as a celebration of European heritage and integration, and it is one of Brussels's popular tourist attractions, drawing several hundred thousand visitors a year.
