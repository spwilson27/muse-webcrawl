Fifty Shades of Grey is a 2015 American erotic romantic drama film directed by Sam Taylor-Johnson, with a screenplay by Kelly Marcel. Based on the 2011 novel of the same name by E. L. James, it is the first instalment in the Fifty Shades film series. The film stars Dakota Johnson as Anastasia Steele, a college graduate who begins a sadomasochistic relationship with young business magnate Christian Grey, played by Jamie Dornan.

The film premiered at the 65th Berlin International Film Festival on 11 February 2015 and was released theatrically on 13 February 2015 by Universal Pictures and Focus Features. Despite receiving generally negative reviews from critics, it was an immediate box office success, breaking numerous records and earning over $569 million worldwide against a $40 million budget. Two sequels, Fifty Shades Darker and Fifty Shades Freed, were released in 2017 and 2018.

Plot

Anastasia "Ana" Steele, a 21-year-old English literature student at Washington State University Vancouver, agrees to interview wealthy entrepreneur Christian Grey in place of her ill roommate Kate. Christian is intrigued by Ana and later pursues her, revealing an interest in a dominant–submissive relationship governed by a detailed contract. As Ana hesitates over the arrangement, the two begin a relationship that tests her boundaries; the film ends with Ana, overwhelmed by Christian's punishment of her, asking to leave, and departing his apartment in tears.

Production

A film adaptation of James's novel was developed by Universal Pictures and Focus Features, which acquired the rights in March 2012. Sam Taylor-Johnson was announced as director in June 2013. Charlie Hunnam was originally cast as Christian Grey but withdrew in October 2013, after which Jamie Dornan was cast. Principal photography took place in Vancouver, British Columbia, from December 2013 to February 2014.

Music

The film's score was composed by Danny Elfman. The soundtrack album featured contributions from Ellie Goulding ("Love Me like You Do"), The Weeknd ("Earned It"), Sia, Beyoncé, and Annie Lennox. "Love Me like You Do" and "Earned It" both became major international hits, with the latter receiving an Academy Award nomination for Best Original Song.

Reception

The film received generally negative reviews, with criticism directed at its screenplay, pacing, and the leads' chemistry, though Johnson's performance drew some praise. It holds a low approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes. At the 36th Golden Raspberry Awards it "won" five categories, including Worst Picture (tied with Fantastic Four), Worst Actor, and Worst Actress. Commercially, however, it set records for a February opening and for the highest-grossing R-rated film of its year at the time.

Sequels

The sequels Fifty Shades Darker (2017) and Fifty Shades Freed (2018), both directed by James Foley, completed the trilogy, with combined franchise grosses exceeding $1.3 billion worldwide.
