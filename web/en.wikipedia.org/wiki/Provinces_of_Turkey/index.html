Provinces of Turkey

Turkey is divided into 81 provinces (Turkish: il, plural iller). The provinces are the first-level administrative divisions of the country and are administered by a governor (vali) appointed by the central government. Each province is subdivided into districts (ilçe), of which there are 973 in total.

Organisation

Provinces usually share the name of their capital city, the main exception being a few whose capitals bear different names, such as Kocaeli (capital İzmit), Sakarya (capital Adapazarı) and Hatay (capital Antakya). The largest province by population is Istanbul, with over 15 million inhabitants; the least populous is Bayburt in the north-east.

Vehicle licence plates and postal codes are numbered according to an alphabetical ordering of the provinces established in the early Republic, so Ankara is 06, Istanbul 34 and İzmir 35. Thirty provinces are designated metropolitan municipalities (büyükşehir belediyesi), whose municipal government covers the whole provincial territory.

History

The republican province system replaced the Ottoman vilayets. The number of provinces grew over the twentieth century as larger provinces were divided; the most recent province created was Düzce in 1999, split from Bolu after the 1999 earthquake. Provinces are grouped statistically into regions, but these have no administrative function.
