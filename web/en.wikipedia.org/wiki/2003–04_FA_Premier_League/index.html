The 2003–04 FA Premier League was the twelfth season of the Premier League, the top division of English football. It is remembered above all for Arsenal's unbeaten championship season: Arsène Wenger's team won the title without losing a single league match — 26 wins and 12 draws — earning the nickname "The Invincibles," the first side to go a top-flight season undefeated since Preston North End in 1888–89.

Season summary

Arsenal led the title race from the autumn, powered by Thierry Henry, who won the golden boot with 30 goals, alongside Patrick Vieira, Robert Pires and Dennis Bergkamp. Chelsea, transformed by Roman Abramovich's takeover and spending spree, finished second; Manchester United, champions the previous year, finished third after the Rio Ferdinand suspension and the sale of David Beckham unsettled their season. Liverpool took the fourth Champions League place.

The unbeaten run

Arsenal clinched the title with a 2–2 draw at rivals Tottenham Hotspur on 25 April 2004 and completed the season unbeaten with a comeback win over Leicester City. Their unbeaten league sequence eventually reached a record 49 matches before ending at Old Trafford in October 2004. As a one-off mark of the achievement, a gold replica Premier League trophy was commissioned and presented to the club.

Bottom of the table

Leeds United's financial collapse ended in relegation, along with Leicester City and Wolverhampton Wanderers.
