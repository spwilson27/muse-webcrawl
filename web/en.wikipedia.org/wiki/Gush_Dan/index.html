Gush Dan (Hebrew: גּוּשׁ דָּן, "Dan bloc") is the metropolitan area centred on Tel Aviv on Israel's Mediterranean coast, taking its name from the territory of the biblical tribe of Dan. It is Israel's largest metropolitan area and its economic and cultural heartland.

Geography

The conurbation stretches from Netanya in the north to Ashdod in the south and inland to Modi'in and Petah Tikva, encompassing cities such as Ramat Gan, Bnei Brak, Holon, Bat Yam, Rishon LeZion and Herzliya. It contains roughly 44% of Israel's population on a small fraction of its land area.

Economy and transport

Gush Dan hosts the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange, most of Israel's high-tech industry (the "Silicon Wadi"), and the country's main media and universities' satellite campuses. Chronic congestion has spurred construction of the Tel Aviv Light Rail (Red Line opened 2023) and a planned metro system.
