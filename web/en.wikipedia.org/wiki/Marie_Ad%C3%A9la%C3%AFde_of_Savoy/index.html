Marie Adélaïde of Savoy (1685–1712) was the wife of Louis, Duke of Burgundy and Dauphin of France, and the mother of King Louis XV. Her marriage sealed the alliance between France and Savoy in the Treaty of Turin (1696).

Life at Versailles

Betrothed at eleven, Marie Adélaïde arrived at the French court in 1696 and quickly charmed the aging Louis XIV and Madame de Maintenon with her vivacity. She married the Dauphin in 1697. As Dauphine from 1711, she was expected to become queen.

Death

During a measles epidemic in February 1712, Marie Adélaïde fell ill and died at Versailles aged 26. Her husband died of the same illness six days later, and their elder surviving son followed within weeks, leaving the two-year-old future Louis XV as heir.
