Palm (PDA)

Palm was a line of personal digital assistants (PDAs) and later smartphones running Palm OS, produced by Palm, Inc. The original PalmPilot, released in 1996, popularised the handheld organiser with handwriting recognition through the Graffiti input system.

History

Models such as the Palm III, Palm V, m500 series, Tungsten, and Zire lines sold in the millions and defined the PDA category before smartphones absorbed its functions. Palm later produced the Treo smartphone line and, in 2009, the webOS-based Palm Pre.

Legacy

HP acquired Palm in 2010 and discontinued the brand in 2011; the Palm name was briefly revived in 2018 for a small companion phone.
