RP2040

The RP2040 is a microcontroller chip designed by Raspberry Pi. It was introduced in 2021 as the processor of the Raspberry Pi Pico and became widely used in hobbyist, educational and embedded products.

Features

The chip contains dual Arm Cortex-M0+ cores running at up to 133 MHz, 264 KB of SRAM and support for external flash memory. A notable feature is its programmable I/O, or PIO, which uses small state machines to implement custom peripheral interfaces and timing-sensitive protocols in hardware.

Software and use

RP2040 boards can be programmed in C and C++ with the Pico SDK, or in MicroPython and CircuitPython. The combination of low cost, flexible I/O and a large community made it popular for keyboards, retro computing interfaces, robotics and sensor projects.

Its simple digital interfaces can be paired with converters such as an R-2R ladder for audio or signal generation experiments.
