John Elkann

John Philip Jacob Elkann (born 1 April 1976) is an Italian-American industrialist, chairman of Stellantis and of Ferrari, and chief executive of Exor, the Agnelli family's investment company. He is the grandson and chosen heir of longtime Fiat chairman Gianni Agnelli.

Career

Born in New York and educated in Paris and Turin, Elkann studied engineering at the Polytechnic University of Turin and worked at General Electric before joining the family business. He became vice chairman of Fiat in 2004 after the deaths of his grandfather and great-uncle, and chairman of Fiat (later Fiat Chrysler Automobiles) in 2010. He oversaw the merger of FCA with PSA Group that created Stellantis in 2021, becoming its chairman, and also chairs Ferrari and the Giovanni Agnelli Foundation.

Other roles

Through Exor, Elkann controls interests including Juventus football club, The Economist Group, and PartnerRe. He is married to Lavinia Borromeo, a member of the Italian aristocratic family.
