Signify N.V.

Formerly: Philips Lighting
Founded: 2016 (spin-off from Philips)
Headquarters: Eindhoven, Netherlands
Industry: Lighting

Signify N.V. is a Dutch multinational lighting company and the world's largest manufacturer of lighting products. It was spun off from Royal Philips in 2016 as Philips Lighting and renamed Signify in 2018, while continuing to market products under the Philips brand under licence.

Business

Signify sells lamps, luminaires, and connected lighting systems for homes, businesses, and cities. Its consumer connected-lighting brand is Philips Hue; professional brands include Interact for networked lighting, Color Kinetics for architectural lighting, and Wiz. The company has led the industry's transition from conventional lamps to LED and to data-enabled "lighting as a service."

History

The lighting business dates to Philips's first incandescent lamp factories in Eindhoven in 1891. After the 2016 IPO on Euronext Amsterdam, the company acquired several lighting firms, most notably the American company Cooper Lighting Solutions in 2020, strengthening its position in the North American professional market.
