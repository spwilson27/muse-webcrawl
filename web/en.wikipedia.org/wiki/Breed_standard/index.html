Breed standard

A breed standard is a published description of the ideal characteristics of a breed of domesticated animal, used by breeders, judges, and registries to evaluate individual animals and guide breeding programmes. Breed standards exist for dogs, cats, horses, cattle, and many other species.

Contents

A typical standard describes overall appearance, size and proportions, coat type and colour, head and body structure, temperament, and movement. Animals that deviate from the standard in specified ways may be described as having faults, some of which are disqualifying in show competition.

Organisations

Standards are maintained by breed clubs and kennel or registry organisations such as the Fédération Cynologique Internationale, The Kennel Club, and the American Kennel Club. Standards for the same breed may differ between registries.
