Kálmán Darányi de Pusztaszentgyörgy et Tetétlen (22 March 1886 – 1 November 1939) was a Hungarian politician who served as Prime Minister of Hungary from 1936 to 1938, during the regency of Miklós Horthy.

Career

Darányi, a landowner and agricultural expert, served as Minister of Agriculture under Gyula Gömbös and succeeded him as prime minister on Gömbös's death in October 1936. Considered more moderate and conservative than his predecessor, he nevertheless moved Hungary closer to Nazi Germany and Fascist Italy.

Premiership

His government announced the Győr Programme (1938), a major rearmament and economic development plan financed partly by a capital levy. In March 1938 his government drafted the First Jewish Law, restricting Jewish participation in professions and the economy, enacted under his successor Béla Imrédy in May 1938. Darányi resigned in May 1938 amid controversy over his dealings with the radical right, and afterwards served as Speaker of the House of Representatives until his death in 1939.
