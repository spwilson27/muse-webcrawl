Muscogee Nation

The Muscogee Nation, historically known as the Muscogee (Creek) Nation, is a federally recognised Native American tribe based in Oklahoma, with its capital at Okmulgee. It is one of the Five Civilized Tribes and has over 100,000 enrolled citizens, making it among the largest tribes in the United States.

The Muscogee people's ancestral homeland is in the southeastern United States, in present-day Alabama and Georgia. They were forcibly removed to Indian Territory on the Trail of Tears in the 1830s following the Indian Removal Act.

Government

The Nation is governed under a 1979 constitution by a Principal Chief, Second Chief, and a National Council representing tribal towns. The 2020 Supreme Court decision in McGirt v. Oklahoma affirmed that the Muscogee reservation was never disestablished for purposes of federal criminal law. The Nation operates health, education, housing and cultural programmes and enterprises including casinos.
