Bergamot orange

Citrus bergamia, the bergamot orange, is a fragrant citrus fruit the size of an orange, with a yellow or green colour similar to a lime, depending on ripeness. It is a probable hybrid of lemon and bitter orange.

Citrus bergamia
Family: Rutaceae
Genus: Citrus
Origin: Mediterranean, especially Calabria, Italy

Uses

Bergamot essential oil from the rind flavours Earl Grey and Lady Grey teas and is a classic component of Eau de Cologne and perfumery. Production is concentrated in coastal Calabria, Italy. The juice is less sour than lemon but more bitter than grapefruit.

Phytochemistry

The peel contains bergapten, a furanocoumarin that is phototoxic, so cosmetic oil is often sold bergapten-free. Compounds including bergamottin affect drug-metabolising enzymes similarly to grapefruit.
