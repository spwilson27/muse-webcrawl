List of Royal Air Force stations

This is a list of Royal Air Force stations, past and present, in the United Kingdom and overseas. At its Second World War peak the RAF operated hundreds of airfields; today the service is concentrated at a smaller number of main operating bases.

Former stations

Famous former stations include RAF Biggin Hill, RAF Kenley and RAF Tangmere (Battle of Britain), RAF Scampton (home of the Dambusters and later the Red Arrows) and RAF Coltishall. The full article lists several hundred closed airfields.
