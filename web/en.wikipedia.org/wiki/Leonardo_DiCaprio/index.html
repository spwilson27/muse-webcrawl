Leonardo DiCaprio

Leonardo Wilhelm DiCaprio (born 11 November 1974) is an American actor and film producer. Known for his work in biographical and period films, he is the recipient of numerous accolades, including an Academy Award, and is regarded as one of the most prominent actors of his generation.

Born11 November 1974, Los Angeles, California, US

Early life and career

DiCaprio was born in Los Angeles and began his career with television commercials and small roles before gaining attention in What's Eating Gilbert Grape (1993), which earned him his first Academy Award nomination at nineteen.

Stardom

International stardom followed with Romeo + Juliet (1996) and James Cameron's Titanic (1997), then the highest-grossing film of all time. In the 2000s he became a frequent collaborator of Martin Scorsese, starring in Gangs of New York, The Aviator, The Departed, Shutter Island and The Wolf of Wall Street.

Later work and activism

DiCaprio won the Academy Award for Best Actor for playing frontiersman Hugh Glass in The Revenant (2015). Through the Leonardo DiCaprio Foundation, founded in 1998, he is a prominent environmental activist and was designated a United Nations Messenger of Peace for climate issues in 2014.
