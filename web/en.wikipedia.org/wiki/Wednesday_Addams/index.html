Wednesday Addams is a fictional character created by American cartoonist Charles Addams as a member of the Addams Family. She is the daughter of Gomez and Morticia Addams and the sister of Pugsley. Wednesday is depicted as a solemn, deadpan girl with pale skin and black braided pigtails, typically wearing a black dress with a white collar. Her name derives from the nursery rhyme line "Wednesday's child is full of woe."

Character

Wednesday is precociously intelligent, emotionally reserved, and fascinated by the macabre. In the original cartoons she keeps pet spiders and plays with a headless Marie Antoinette doll. Later adaptations added interests such as playing the cello, writing fiction, and investigating mysteries, and gave her a dry, biting wit and a refusal to smile.

Portrayals

Lisa Loring played Wednesday in the 1964–66 television series, establishing the pigtails and the famous "Wednesday dance" shuffle. Christina Ricci's portrayal in the films The Addams Family (1991) and Addams Family Values (1993) made the character an icon of deadpan humour. Jenna Ortega starred as a teenage Wednesday in the Netflix series Wednesday (2022), set at Nevermore Academy; her improvised dance scene to "Goo Goo Muck" became a viral cultural phenomenon, and the series became one of Netflix's most-watched English-language shows.
