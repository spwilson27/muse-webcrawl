Mads Mikkelsen

Mads Dittmann Mikkelsen (born 22 November 1965) is a Danish actor. Originally a gymnast and dancer, he rose to fame in Denmark with roles in the Pusher film trilogy (1996–2005), the TV series Unit One (2000–2004) and the action-comedy The Green Butchers (2003). Internationally he is known for playing the villain Le Chiffre in the James Bond film Casino Royale (2006), Dr Hannibal Lecter in the television series Hannibal (2013–2015), and for acclaimed Danish films including The Hunt (2012) and Another Round (2020).

Career

Mikkelsen made his film debut as Tonny in Nicolas Winding Refn's Pusher (1996). His collaboration with director Thomas Vinterberg brought major honours: for The Hunt (2012), in which he played a teacher falsely accused of child abuse, he won the Best Actor Award at the Cannes Film Festival. Another Round (2020), in which he starred as a teacher experimenting with alcohol, won the Academy Award for Best International Feature Film.

His Hollywood roles include Kaecilius in Doctor Strange (2016), Galen Erso in Rogue One (2016), Gellert Grindelwald in Fantastic Beasts: The Secrets of Dumbledore (2022), and Jürgen Voller in Indiana Jones and the Dial of Destiny (2023). His portrayal of Hannibal Lecter in Bryan Fuller's Hannibal earned widespread critical acclaim and a devoted fan following.

Recognition

Mikkelsen is regarded as one of Denmark's most successful actors internationally. He was appointed a Knight of the Order of the Dannebrog in 2010 and received the European Film Award for Best Actor for Another Round. His brother is the actor Lars Mikkelsen.
