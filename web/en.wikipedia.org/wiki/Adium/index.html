Adium

Adium is a free and open-source instant messaging client for macOS that supports multiple chat networks through the libpurple library. First released in 2001, it became one of the most popular third-party messaging applications on the Mac platform, known for its customisable interface and its duck mascot. It is licensed under the GNU General Public License.

Features

Adium allows a user to connect simultaneously to services such as XMPP (Jabber), IRC (via plug-ins), and historically AIM, MSN/Windows Live Messenger, Yahoo! Messenger, Google Talk, ICQ and others, presenting all contacts in a single list. It supports tabbed chats, file transfer, message encryption via Off-the-Record Messaging (OTR), Growl notifications and extensive theming of contact lists and message styles, distributed as downloadable "Xtras".

History

The application was created by Adam Iser while a student, originally as a simple AIM client. A rewrite, Adium X, followed, and in 2007 it won an Apple Design Award. Development slowed as the underlying proprietary networks shut down or closed to third-party clients during the 2010s; Adium's last major releases date from that period, though it remains functional for open protocols such as XMPP and IRC.
