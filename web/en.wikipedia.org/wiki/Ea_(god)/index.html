Ea (god)

Ea / Enki
Sumerian name: Enki
Domain: Fresh water, wisdom, crafts, magic
Centre: Eridu

Ea, known in Sumerian as Enki, is the ancient Mesopotamian god of water, knowledge (gestú), crafts (gašam), and creation (nudimmud), one of the Anunnaki and god of the underground fresh waters, the Apsu.

Role and myths

Ea was patron of Eridu, among the oldest cities. Clever and benevolent to humanity, he warns the hero Atrahasis (and in other versions Utnapishtim) of the coming flood in the Epic of Gilgamesh tradition, and helps create humankind from clay. In the myth of Inanna and Enki, the me — divine decrees of civilisation — pass to Uruk.

Iconography

He is depicted with flowing water, often with fish, from his shoulders, holding symbols of authority, accompanied by his two-faced vizier Isimud. See also Anu and Mesopotamian religion.
