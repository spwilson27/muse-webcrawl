The Chennai Super Kings (CSK) are a professional Twenty20 cricket franchise based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, competing in the Indian Premier League. Founded in 2008 as one of the league's original eight teams, CSK are among the most successful and valuable franchises in the IPL, renowned for their consistency and loyal fan base, the "Yellow Army".

History

CSK reached the final of the inaugural IPL season in 2008 and won back-to-back titles in 2010 and 2011 under captain MS Dhoni. The franchise was suspended for two seasons (2016–2017) over the 2013 betting scandal but returned in 2018 to win the title immediately. Further championships followed in 2021 and 2023, the latter in a rain-shortened final against Gujarat Titans. CSK have qualified for the playoffs in most seasons they have contested.

Home ground

The team plays at the M. A. Chidambaram Stadium, known as Chepauk, one of India's oldest cricket grounds, noted for its knowledgeable crowds and spin-friendly pitches.

Notable players

Alongside Dhoni, iconic CSK players have included Suresh Raina, Ravindra Jadeja, Dwayne Bravo, Faf du Plessis, and Shane Watson. The franchise is coached for many years by Stephen Fleming and owned by Chennai Super Kings Cricket Limited.
