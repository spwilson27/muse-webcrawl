Birmingham City Football Club is a professional football club based in Birmingham, England. Formed in 1875 as Small Heath Alliance, it was renamed Small Heath in 1888, Birmingham in 1905, and Birmingham City in 1943. The club has played at St Andrew's since 1906 and its nickname is the Blues, after the colour of its kit. Its main rivalry is with Aston Villa, contested in the Second City derby.

History

Small Heath were founder members of the Football Alliance and in 1892 joined the Football League's new Second Division. Birmingham reached the FA Cup final in 1931 and again in 1956, losing on both occasions. In European competition the club were finalists in the Inter-Cities Fairs Cup in 1960 and 1961, losing to Barcelona and Roma. The League Cup triumph of 1963, over Aston Villa, was the club's first major trophy.

Recent years

Under manager Alex McLeish, Birmingham won the 2011 League Cup final, defeating Arsenal 2–1 at Wembley with a late goal from Obafemi Martins, but were relegated from the Premier League the same season. Financial problems and changes of ownership followed, including the 2023 takeover by the American group Knighthead Capital, whose investors include former NFL quarterback Tom Brady.

Records

The club's record goalscorer is Joe Bradford, with 267 goals in the 1920s and 1930s, and Trevor Francis became English football's first £1 million player when transferred to Nottingham Forest in 1979.
