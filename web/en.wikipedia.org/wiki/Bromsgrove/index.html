Country: England

Bromsgrove is a town in Worcestershire, England, about 16 miles (26 km) southwest of Birmingham. It lies at the foot of the Lickey Hills and is the administrative centre of the Bromsgrove District.

History

Bromsgrove was recorded in the Domesday Book and grew as a market town and centre of the nail-making trade. The Bromsgrove Guild of Applied Arts, founded by Walter Gilbert, became famous for decorative metalwork, including the gates of Buckingham Palace. The poet A. E. Housman was born nearby and is commemorated in the town.

Transport and landmarks

The town is served by the M5 and M42 motorways and a railway station on the Cross City Line and the Lickey Incline route. The Avoncroft Museum of Historic Buildings, an open-air museum of rescued buildings, lies just outside the town.
