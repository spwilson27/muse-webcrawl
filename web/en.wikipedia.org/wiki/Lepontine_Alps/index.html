Lepontine Alps

The Lepontine Alps are a mountain range in the north-western part of the Alps, straddling Switzerland and Italy. They lie between the Simplon Pass and the Splügen Pass and include the Gotthard massif, a major European watershed.

CountriesSwitzerland, Italy
Highest pointMonte Leone (3,553 m)
RangeWestern Alps

Geography

The range is bounded by the upper Rhône valley to the north and Lake Maggiore to the south. Important peaks include Monte Leone, Blinnenhorn and Monte Basòdino. The Gotthard Pass, Furka Pass and Nufenen Pass cross the range, and the sources of the Rhône, Rhine, Reuss and Ticino rivers all lie in or near the Gotthard region.

Transport

The Lepontine Alps are pierced by major transalpine tunnels, including the Gotthard railway and road tunnels and the Gotthard Base Tunnel, the world's longest railway tunnel, opened in 2016.
