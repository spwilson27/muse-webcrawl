Booking.com

Booking.com is a Dutch online travel agency for lodging reservations and other travel products, and a subsidiary of Booking Holdings. It is headquartered in Amsterdam and is one of the world's largest travel fare aggregators and travel metasearch engines, offering reservations for accommodation ranging from hotels and hostels to apartments and vacation rentals.

History

Booking.com was formed in 1996 as Bookings.nl, founded by Geert-Jan Bruinsma in Enschede, Netherlands, and became Booking.com after merging with Bookings Online in 2000. In 2005 the company was acquired by the Priceline Group (now Booking Holdings) for approximately US$133 million. The site expanded rapidly to become the largest hotel booking site in the world, operating in dozens of languages and currencies and listing millions of properties.

Business model and criticism

Booking.com primarily operates on an "agency model," collecting commissions from properties after guests complete their stays. The company has faced regulatory scrutiny in Europe and elsewhere over rate-parity clauses, tax matters, and consumer-protection issues, and it was designated a "gatekeeper" under the EU's Digital Markets Act in 2024.
