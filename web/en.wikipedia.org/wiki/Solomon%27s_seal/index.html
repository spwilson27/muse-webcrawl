Solomon's seal


The Seal of Solomon is a legendary signet ring attributed to King Solomon.

The Seal of Solomon is a signet ring attributed in legend to King Solomon, said to have given him power to command spirits, demons, and jinn, and to speak with animals. The seal is commonly depicted as a hexagram (six-pointed star) or pentagram inscribed in a circle.

Traditions

The motif appears in Jewish, Christian, and Islamic traditions. In the Testament of Solomon, a Greek pseudepigraphical text, Solomon uses the ring, given by the archangel Michael, to bind demons to labour on the Temple in Jerusalem. In Islamic tradition, Sulayman's seal is associated with his authority over the jinn and the wind.

Symbolism

The hexagram form overlaps with the Star of David, while the pentagram form features in medieval grimoires such as the Key of Solomon, which prescribed the seal for magical operations. The term "Solomon's seal" is also the common name of plants in the genus Polygonatum, named for marks on their rhizomes.
