Union Public Service Commission

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) is India's premier central recruiting agency, responsible for appointments to and examinations for the All India Services and central services of the Government of India, including the Indian Administrative Service (IAS), Indian Police Service (IPS), and Indian Foreign Service (IFS). It is a constitutional body, established under Part XIV of the Constitution of India, with its headquarters at Dholpur House, New Delhi.

History

The commission traces its origin to the first Public Service Commission set up on 1 October 1926 under the Government of India Act 1919, following the recommendations of the Lee Commission; it became the Federal Public Service Commission in 1937 and assumed its present name with the adoption of the Constitution in 1950. Its chairman and members are appointed by the President of India for six-year terms or until the age of 65.

Examinations

The UPSC conducts numerous examinations, most famously the Civil Services Examination, a three-stage process (preliminary, mains, and interview) entered by hundreds of thousands of candidates each year for a few hundred top posts. Other examinations cover engineering services, combined defence services, the National Defence Academy, and medical services. The commission also advises the President on disciplinary and recruitment matters.
