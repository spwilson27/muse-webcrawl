Hollywood Burbank Airport

Hollywood Burbank Airport (IATA: BUR), formerly known as Bob Hope Airport, is a public airport in Burbank, California, serving the northern Los Angeles area, including Glendale, Pasadena and the San Fernando Valley. It is the closest airport to most of Los Angeles's popular tourist destinations and studios.

Operations

The airport is served mainly by domestic low-cost and major carriers, with Southwest Airlines the largest operator. It is noted for its small, walkable terminal and an adjacent railway station linking it to downtown Los Angeles. In 2017 the airport reverted to the name Hollywood Burbank Airport for branding, while its legal name remains Bob Hope Airport.
