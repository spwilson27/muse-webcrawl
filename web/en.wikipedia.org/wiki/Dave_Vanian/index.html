Dave Vanian

Dave Vanian (born David Lett, 12 October 1956) is an English singer best known as the frontman of the punk and gothic rock band The Damned, one of the first UK punk bands to release a single and album.


Born: 12 October 1956, Hemel Hempstead, England
Occupation: Singer
Associated acts: The Damned

Career

A former gravedigger, Vanian adopted a vampire-like stage persona — pale makeup, dark formal wear — that helped shape gothic fashion. He joined The Damned in 1976; the band released "New Rose" (1976), often cited as the first British punk single, and evolved through Machine Gun Etiquette to the gothic hit album Phantasmagoria (1985) and single "Eloise".

Other work

Vanian also fronted Dave Vanian and the Phantom Chords, releasing rockabilly-tinged material, and remains The Damned's constant vocalist through their later albums including Darkadelic (2023).
