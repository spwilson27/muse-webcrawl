HMMER

HMMER is a free software package for sequence analysis written by Sean Eddy and collaborators. It uses profile hidden Markov models (profile HMMs) to search sequence databases for homologues of a protein or nucleotide sequence, and to build multiple sequence alignments. HMMER powers major biological databases such as Pfam, InterPro and SMART, where profile HMMs represent protein families and domains.

DeveloperSean Eddy and the HMMER team

Programs

The package includes tools such as hmmbuild (build a profile HMM from an alignment), hmmsearch (search a profile against a sequence database), phmmer (single-sequence search, analogous to BLAST), jackhmmer (iterative search, analogous to PSI-BLAST) and nhmmer for nucleotide queries. Its speed derives from the "MSV" filter and vectorised (SIMD) implementations of the profile HMM algorithms.

History

HMMER grew out of Eddy's work in the 1990s. HMMER3, released in 2010, achieved roughly BLAST-comparable speed while retaining the sensitivity of probabilistic modelling, and HMMER web servers at the European Bioinformatics Institute provide searches against UniProt and other databases.
