Retro Studios

Retro Studios, Inc. is an American video game developer and subsidiary of Nintendo based in Austin, Texas. The studio is best known for its work on the Metroid Prime and Donkey Kong Country series.

History

Retro Studios was founded on September 21, 1998, as an alliance between Nintendo and former Iguana Entertainment founder Jeff Spangenberg. Spangenberg left the company in 2000, and Nintendo purchased a majority stake in 2002, buying out his shares. The studio's first project to reach release was Metroid Prime (2002) for the GameCube, which transferred the Metroid series into a first-person adventure format to critical acclaim.

Games

Subsequent releases include Metroid Prime 2: Echoes (2004), Metroid Prime 3: Corruption (2007), and the compilation Metroid Prime: Trilogy (2009). Retro then revived the Donkey Kong Country series with Donkey Kong Country Returns (2010) and Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze (2014), co-developed Mario Kart 7 (2011), and produced Metroid Prime Remastered (2023). In 2019, Nintendo announced that Retro had taken over development of Metroid Prime 4 (later titled Metroid Prime 4: Beyond) after an earlier version by another studio was scrapped.
