Etched glass

Etched glass is glass whose surface has been decorated or frosted by abrasion or by the action of an acid or other corrosive substance. Etching produces a translucent, frosted finish that diffuses light while obscuring vision, and it can range from overall frosting to intricate pictorial designs.

Techniques

Traditional acid etching used hydrofluoric acid applied over stencilled resists. Modern methods include abrasive blasting (sandblasting) through masks, mould-etching, and etching creams based on fluoride compounds. Laser etching can mark patterns within or on the surface of the glass.

Uses

Etched glass is used for decorative windows, doors, mirrors, shower screens, glassware, and signage, both for ornament and for privacy. Victorian pubs and houses in Britain are noted for their elaborate etched window glass.
