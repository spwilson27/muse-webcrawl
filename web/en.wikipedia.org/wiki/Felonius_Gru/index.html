Felonius Gru

Felonius Gru, usually known simply as Gru, is the protagonist of Illumination's Despicable Me franchise, voiced by Steve Carell with a pronounced Eastern European–tinged accent. Introduced as an ambitious supervillain, the character is transformed over the films into a devoted adoptive father and, eventually, an agent fighting other villains.

Character

Gru is tall, bald and broad-shouldered, habitually dressed in a black coat and grey striped scarf, and lives in a fortress-like house served by his army of small yellow henchmen, the Minions, and the elderly scientist Dr. Nefario. In Despicable Me (2010) he plots to steal the Moon to prove himself the world's greatest villain, adopting three orphaned girls — Margo, Edith and Agnes — as part of the scheme, only to find parenthood displacing his ambitions. In the sequels he is recruited by the Anti-Villain League, falls in love with and marries agent Lucy Wilde, and in Despicable Me 4 (2024) goes into witness protection with a baby son, Gru Jr. The prequel films Minions (2015) and Minions: The Rise of Gru (2022) depict his childhood and first steps in villainy.

Reception

Gru became one of the most recognisable animated characters of the 2010s, anchoring a franchise that has grossed several billion dollars and made the Minions a global merchandising phenomenon.
