Marvel Universe

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia


Characters of the Marvel Universe

The Marvel Universe is a fictional shared universe where the stories in most American comic book titles and other media published by Marvel Comics take place. Super-teams such as the Avengers, the X-Men, the Fantastic Four, along with individual superheroes such as Spider-Man, Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, Hulk, Black Panther, Doctor Strange, Wolverine, Daredevil and Deadpool are set in this universe.

The Marvel Universe is strongly based on the real world, with New York City serving as the home of many heroes. It also incorporates science fiction and fantasy elements, including alien races such as the Kree and Skrulls, hidden civilizations such as Atlantis and Wakanda, and cosmic entities. The mainstream continuity of the comics is designated Earth-616 within a wider multiverse of parallel realities.

History

The shared universe grew out of the comics created by Stan Lee, Jack Kirby, Steve Ditko and others during the 1960s, beginning with the Fantastic Four in 1961. Marvel's approach emphasised flawed, human characters and continuing storylines that crossed between titles, building an unusually cohesive fictional world. Over subsequent decades the universe expanded through thousands of characters and storylines, periodic crossover events, and adaptations into film, television and video games.

Adaptations

The Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU), beginning with Iron Man in 2008, adapted the characters and the shared-universe concept for film and became the highest-grossing film franchise of all time. Distinct from the comics' Earth-616, the MCU developed its own continuity while drawing heavily on the comics' characters and storylines.
