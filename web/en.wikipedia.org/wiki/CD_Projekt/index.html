CD Projekt

CD Projekt S.A. is a Polish video game developer, publisher, and distributor based in Warsaw. Founded in 1994 by Marcin Iwiński and Michał Kiciński, it is best known for The Witcher series and Cyberpunk 2077, developed by its CD Projekt Red division, and for the GOG.com digital distribution platform.

History

CD Projekt began by localising and distributing Western games in Poland, including authorised Polish editions of Baldur's Gate. Its studio CD Projekt Red released The Witcher in 2007, adapted from Andrzej Sapkowski's novels; The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt (2015) became one of the most acclaimed and best-selling role-playing games ever. Cyberpunk 2077 (2020) had a troubled launch but recovered through major updates and the Phantom Liberty expansion.
