Gael García Bernal (born 30 November 1978) is a Mexican actor and producer. He is best known for starring in films such as Amores perros (2000), Y tu mamá también (2001), The Motorcycle Diaries (2004), Babel (2006), and Coco (2017), and for his role as conductor Rodrigo de Souza in the Amazon series Mozart in the Jungle (2014–2018), for which he won a Golden Globe Award.

Early life

García Bernal was born in Guadalajara to actress Patricia Bernal and actor and director José Ángel García. He acted in telenovelas as a child and studied at the Central School of Speech and Drama in London, becoming its first Mexican student.

Career

His breakthrough came with Alejandro González Iñárritu's Amores perros, followed by Alfonso Cuarón's Y tu mamá también alongside Diego Luna. He portrayed the young Ernesto "Che" Guevara in Walter Salles's The Motorcycle Diaries, and appeared in Michel Gondry's The Science of Sleep, Pedro Almodóvar's Bad Education, and Babel. He voiced Héctor in Pixar's Coco and starred in the Marvel special Werewolf by Night (2022). With Luna he co-founded the production company Canana Films and the Ambulante documentary touring festival.
