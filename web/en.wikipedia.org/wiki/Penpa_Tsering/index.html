Penpa Tsering

Penpa Tsering (born 1967) is a Tibetan politician in exile who serves as the Sikyong — the political leader — of the Central Tibetan Administration (CTA), the Tibetan government-in-exile based in Dharamshala, India. He was elected in the 2021 Tibetan general election and took office on 27 May 2021, succeeding Lobsang Sangay.

Early life and career

Penpa Tsering was born in the Bylakuppe Tibetan refugee settlement in Karnataka, India, to parents who had fled Tibet after 1959. He studied economics at Madras Christian College. He became involved in Tibetan exile politics and was elected to the Tibetan Parliament-in-Exile, serving as its Speaker from 2008 to 2016. He subsequently served as the Representative of the Dalai Lama to North America, based in Washington, D.C.

Sikyong

In the 2021 election Penpa Tsering defeated Kelsang Dorjee Aukatsang in the final round. As Sikyong he has advocated the Middle Way approach of seeking genuine autonomy for Tibet within the People's Republic of China and has sought to restart dialogue with Beijing, while also raising Tibetan issues with foreign governments and legislatures. He has undertaken official visits to the United States and other countries to build international support for the Tibetan cause.
