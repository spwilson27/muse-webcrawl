Daphne Odjig


Image placeholder: Daphne Odjig

Daphne Odjig, CM, OBC (11 September 1919 – 1 October 2016), born Odjig Kawano, was a Canadian First Nations artist of Odawa-Potawatomi and English heritage. She was a founding member of the Professional Native Indian Artists Inc., popularly known as the "Indian Group of Seven", and a central figure in the emergence of contemporary Indigenous art in Canada.

Life and work

Born on Wikwemikong Unceded Indian Reserve on Manitoulin Island, Ontario, Odjig was largely self-taught, developing a distinctive style blending Anishinaabe pictograph traditions, Woodlands School imagery, and modernist influences. Her subjects included legends, family, and the history and struggles of Indigenous peoples.

Recognition

Odjig received the Order of Canada and the Governor General's Award in Visual and Media Arts. In 2009 the National Gallery of Canada mounted a major retrospective of her work, the first such solo exhibition there for a First Nations artist.
