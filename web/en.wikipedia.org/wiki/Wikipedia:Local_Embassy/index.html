Wikipedia:Local Embassy

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
[Image: A welcome sign translated into many languages, with flags, above a help desk]

The Local Embassy page serves visitors who do not speak English well: it lists multilingual volunteers ('ambassadors') on the English Wikipedia whom users can contact in their own language for help.

Pages like this exist across many language editions (for example the 'Botschaft' and embassy pages on other wikis) and form part of the inter-language help network that connects the hundreds of Wikipedia language communities.

How it works

The page organises contacts by language — a visitor finds their language, follows the link to an ambassador's user or talk page, and asks their question there. It complements the main help pages for editors working across languages.

Related systems

Cross-wiki communication also happens at Meta-Wiki, through interlanguage links, and via the Wikimedia Foundation's community teams.
