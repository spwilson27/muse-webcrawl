Empress Michiko

Michiko (美智子, born 20 October 1934)
Empress consort of Japan: 1989–2019
Spouse: Akihito
Children: Naruhito; Fumihito, Prince Akishino; Sayako Kuroda
Born: Michiko Shōda, Tokyo, Japan

Michiko (美智子, born 20 October 1934) is a member of the Imperial House of Japan. She was Empress of Japan as the wife of Akihito, the 125th Emperor of Japan reigning from 7 January 1989 to 30 April 2019. She was the first commoner to marry into the Japanese imperial family, and her marriage to the then Crown Prince in 1959 was widely celebrated as a symbol of postwar Japan's democratisation.

Early life and marriage

Born Michiko Shōda in Tokyo, she was the daughter of Hidesaburō Shōda, president of the Nisshin Seifun flour milling company. She was educated at Sacred Heart schools and graduated from the University of the Sacred Heart in 1957 with a degree in English literature. She met Crown Prince Akihito on a tennis court in Karuizawa in August 1957 — a romance the press dubbed the "tennis court romance" — and they married on 10 April 1959.

As empress

As crown princess and later empress, Michiko departed from several traditions: she raised her children herself rather than entrusting them entirely to courtiers, and she accompanied Akihito on visits to disaster sites and care facilities that endeared the imperial couple to the public. She is an accomplished pianist and translator of poetry, and has published children's literature, including the picture book Hajimete no Yamanobori ("My First Mountain"). Since Akihito's abdication in 2019 — the first imperial abdication in two centuries — she holds the title of Empress Emerita (Jōkōgō).
