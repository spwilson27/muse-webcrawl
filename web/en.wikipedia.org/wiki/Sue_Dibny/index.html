Sue Dibny

Sue Dibny (née Dearbon) is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. The wife of the superhero Ralph Dibny, the Elongated Man, she was a socialite and frequent supporting character in Justice League stories, known for her detective partnership and marriage with Ralph.

First appearanceThe Flash #119 (March 1961)
Created byJohn Broome, Carmine Infantino
SpouseRalph Dibny (Elongated Man)
PublisherDC Comics

Publication history

Sue Dearbon debuted in The Flash #119 (1961) and married Ralph Dibny shortly afterwards. The couple became fan favourites through the Justice League titles of the 1980s, especially Keith Giffen and J. M. DeMatteis's light-hearted run, and the Formerly Known as the Justice League miniseries.

Identity Crisis and aftermath

In the 2004 miniseries Identity Crisis, Sue was murdered, an event with long-lasting repercussions across the DC Universe. She later returned as a ghost alongside Ralph, the two becoming posthumous detectives in the series 52. A version of Sue Dibny appears in The CW's The Flash television series, played by Natalie Dreyfuss.
