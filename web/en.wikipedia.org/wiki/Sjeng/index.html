Sjeng

Sjeng is a chess engine developed by Gian-Carlo Pascutto of Belgium. It is known for playing chess variants as well as standard chess, including crazyhouse, suicide chess, losers chess and chess960. Sjeng competed in computer chess tournaments and was distributed in both free and commercial (Deep Sjeng) versions.

The engine uses bitboard representations and alpha-beta search with many enhancements. Deep Sjeng achieved strong results in the World Computer Chess Championship in the 2000s, and a version of Sjeng was included with some operating system distributions as a bundled chess program.
