Beyond Our Ken is a BBC radio comedy programme that ran from 1958 to 1964, starring Kenneth Horne. Written by Eric Merriman (with Barry Took in the first two series), it was a sketch show built around Horne's urbane, unflappable straight man and a gallery of comic characters performed by Kenneth Williams, Hugh Paddick, Betty Marsden and Bill Pertwee.

Format and legacy

The show mixed sketches, parodies and running characters, and introduced items such as Horne's mock-documentary features and the hapless Charles and the dim-witted Ambrose. At its peak it attracted audiences of up to 11 million listeners, making it one of the most popular radio comedies of its era despite scandals over occasionally risqué material.

When Beyond Our Ken ended in 1964, Horne and much of the creative team developed its successor, Round the Horne (1965–1968), which carried the format further and became still more famous.
