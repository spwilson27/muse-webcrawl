Idebenone

Idebenone
Class: Benzoquinone; analogue of coenzyme Q10
Status: Approved in EU for LHON (as Raxone)
ATC: N06BX13

Idebenone is a synthetic analogue of coenzyme Q10 (ubiquinone), developed by Takeda in the 1980s. It is a short-chain benzoquinone that can act as an antioxidant and shuttle electrons to complex III of the mitochondrial respiratory chain, bypassing a defective complex I.

Medical use

Idebenone was investigated for Alzheimer's disease, Friedreich's ataxia and other mitochondrial and cognitive disorders with mixed results. Under the trade name Raxone, it was authorised in the European Union in 2015 for the treatment of visual impairment in patients with Leber's hereditary optic neuropathy (LHON). It is also sold in some markets as a nootropic and is used in cosmetic products for its antioxidant properties.
