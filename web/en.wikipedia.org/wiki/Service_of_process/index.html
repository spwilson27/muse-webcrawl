Service of process

Service of process is the legal procedure by which a party to a lawsuit formally delivers legal documents — such as a summons and complaint — to the person or entity being sued, giving that party proper notice of the proceedings and an opportunity to respond.

Methods

Common methods include personal service, in which a process server hands the documents directly to the defendant; substituted service, in which documents are left with another suitable person at the defendant's home or business; service by mail; and, where personal service proves impossible, service by publication. Corporations are often served through a registered agent.

Legal significance

Proper service is a requirement of due process in many legal systems; a court generally cannot exercise jurisdiction over a defendant who has not been validly served. Proof of service is documented in an affidavit or return of service filed with the court.
