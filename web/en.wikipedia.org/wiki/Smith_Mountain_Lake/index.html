Smith Mountain Lake

Smith Mountain Lake is a large reservoir in the Roanoke Region of Virginia, United States, located southeast of the city of Roanoke and southwest of Lynchburg. The lake was created in 1963 by the construction of the Smith Mountain Dam on the Roanoke River.

The lake has a surface area of about 20,600 acres (83 km²) and more than 500 miles (800 km) of shoreline. It is the largest lake contained entirely within the Commonwealth of Virginia. The surrounding area is a popular recreation and retirement destination, known for fishing, boating and waterside homes.

History

Appalachian Power began acquiring land for the project in the late 1950s. The Smith Mountain Dam was completed in 1963, and the lake reached full pond in 1966. The dam generates hydroelectric power and works in tandem with Leesville Lake downstream as a pumped-storage hydroelectric facility: water is pumped back up into Smith Mountain Lake during off-peak hours and released to generate power at peak demand.

Geography

The lake lies primarily in Bedford and Franklin counties, with a small part in Pittsylvania County. It is fed by the Roanoke River and the Blackwater River. Smith Mountain, from which the lake takes its name, rises over its southern shore. Smith Mountain Lake State Park, on the Bedford County shore, offers swimming, camping and hiking.

Recreation and culture

The lake is noted for its striped bass fishery and hosts fishing tournaments. It was a filming location for the 1991 film What About Bob?, in which it stood in for Lake Winnipesaukee. In August 2015, the lake drew national attention when a television news crew was murdered during a live broadcast from Bridgewater Plaza.
