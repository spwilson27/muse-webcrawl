Perfect Dark (upcoming video game)

Perfect Dark
Developer: The Initiative, Crystal Dynamics
Publisher: Xbox Game Studios
Genre: First-person shooter
Platforms: Windows, Xbox Series X/S

Perfect Dark is an upcoming first-person shooter video game reboot of the Perfect Dark series, developed by The Initiative with support from Crystal Dynamics and published by Xbox Game Studios. Players take the role of Joanna Dark, an agent of the Carrington Institute, in a near-future world affected by ecological disasters.

Gameplay

The game is described as a spy thriller blending first-person shooting with traversal and gadget-based gameplay, including parkour-style movement. Trailers have shown Joanna pursuing a target through a futuristic Cairo, using scanning devices and melee combat.

Development

The reboot was announced at The Game Awards in December 2020. The Initiative, a studio founded by Microsoft in 2018, leads development; Crystal Dynamics joined as a co-development partner in 2021. A first gameplay trailer was shown at the Xbox Games Showcase in June 2024. The project has experienced a lengthy development and changes in leadership, and no release date has been announced.
