Rabbi Isaac Elchanan Theological Seminary

The Rabbi Isaac Elchanan Theological Seminary (RIETS), founded in 1896, is the rabbinical seminary of Yeshiva University in the Washington Heights neighbourhood of New York City. It is named after Rabbi Isaac Elchanan Spektor (1817–1896), the renowned chief rabbi of Kovno.

History

RIETS traces its roots to the Rabbinical College of America and the Etz Chaim Yeshiva, an elementary school founded in 1886 on the Lower East Side. In 1896 the rabbinical school was organised, and in 1915 it merged with Yeshiva Etz Chaim under Dr. Bernard Revel, who became the first president of the combined institution that developed into Yeshiva University.

Program

RIETS ordains rabbis (semikhah) for Modern Orthodox Judaism. Students study Talmud, halakha (Jewish law), homiletics and pastoral training, typically while also pursuing undergraduate or graduate studies at Yeshiva University. The seminary has ordained thousands of rabbis serving congregations, schools and communities worldwide. Notable deans and roshei yeshiva have included Rabbi Joseph B. Soloveitchik ("the Rav"), who taught at RIETS for over four decades.
