Gorgopotamos

Gorgopotamos (Greek: Γοργοπόταμος) is a village and a former municipality in Phthiotis, Greece. Since the 2011 local government reform it is part of the municipality Lamia. The village lies beneath Mount Oeta, near the river of the same name, whose name means "rushing river" in Greek.

Gorgopotamos is famous for the railway bridge over its gorge and for Operation Harling, one of the most celebrated sabotage actions of the Second World War. On the night of 25–26 November 1942, a force of British Special Operations Executive commandos together with Greek resistance fighters of EAM-ELAS and EDES blew up the bridge, severing the railway line carrying supplies to Rommel's Afrika Korps in North Africa.

The operation is remembered as a rare moment of cooperation between the rival Greek resistance movements. The reconstructed bridge remains a landmark, and the sabotage is commemorated annually at the site.
