Legendary Pictures

Legendary Pictures, doing business as Legendary Entertainment, is an American film production and mass media company based in Burbank, California, founded by Thomas Tull in 2000. It was the first company of its kind to pair major motion picture production with major Wall Street private equity and hedge fund investors, raising several hundred million dollars to co-finance films with studio partners.

History

Legendary's first co-production deal, with Warner Bros. in 2005, produced hits including Batman Begins, The Dark Knight, 300, Inception and The Hangover. After that deal ended, Legendary moved to Universal Pictures in 2014, then returned to Warner Bros. in 2019. In 2016 China's Dalian Wanda Group acquired a controlling stake for a reported $3.5 billion.

Notable productions

The company is best known for the MonsterVerse franchise (Godzilla, Kong: Skull Island, Godzilla vs. Kong), as well as Pacific Rim, Jurassic World, Warcraft, Pokémon Detective Pikachu, Dune and Enola Holmes. Its television arm, Legendary Television, and its comics imprint extend the business beyond film.
