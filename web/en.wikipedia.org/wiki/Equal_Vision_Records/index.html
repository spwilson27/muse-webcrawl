Equal Vision Records

Equal Vision Records is an independent record label based in Albany, New York. It was founded in the early 1990s by Ray Cappo, vocalist of the hardcore punk bands Youth of Today and Shelter, originally to release music by Shelter and other Krishna-conscious hardcore acts.

History

Under the management of Steve Reddy, who acquired the label in the late 1990s, Equal Vision broadened from its hardcore roots into post-hardcore, emo, and experimental rock. It became known for launching or developing acts such as Coheed and Cambria, Circa Survive, Saves the Day, Bane, Converge (early releases), Armor for Sleep, Chiodos, Pierce the Veil, Sleeping with Sirens, and Waterparks. The label also operated imprints including Mantralogy, for Krishna-related releases, and a partnership with Cave & Canary Goods.

Notable artists

Current and former artists on the roster include Circa Survive, Coheed and Cambria, The Dear Hunter, Eisley, Hail the Sun, I the Mighty, Saves the Day, Weerd Science, and You Blew It!.
