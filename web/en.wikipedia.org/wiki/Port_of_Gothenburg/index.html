Port of Gothenburg

The Port of Gothenburg is the largest port in Scandinavia and one of the largest in Northern Europe, located on both banks of the Göta älv river estuary in Gothenburg, Sweden. It handles a substantial share of Sweden's foreign trade, including the bulk of its container traffic.

Facilities

The port comprises several terminals: container and ro-ro terminals at Skandiahamnen and Älvsborgshamnen, car terminals, passenger ferry terminals serving routes to Denmark and Germany (operated by Stena Line and others), and energy terminals handling crude oil and petroleum products, supplied in part by refineries at nearby Lysekil and Stenungsund. The port has direct rail connections across Sweden and regular container services to major ports worldwide.

History

Gothenburg was founded in 1621 as a fortified trading port, and its harbor developed along the river over the following centuries. The modern outer harbor terminals were built in the 1960s and 1970s as ships outgrew the inner river berths.
