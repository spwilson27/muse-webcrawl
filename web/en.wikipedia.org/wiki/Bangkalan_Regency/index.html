Bangkalan Regency

Bangkalan Regency (Indonesian: Kabupaten Bangkalan) is a regency in East Java province, Indonesia, occupying the western portion of Madura Island. Its capital is the town of Bangkalan. The regency covers roughly 1,260 square kilometres and had a population of about one million in the 2020 census.

Administration

The regency is divided into 18 districts (kecamatan), including Bangkalan, Kamal, Socah, Burneh, Arosbaya, and Tanjung Bumi. The harbour district of Kamal was historically the main ferry port for crossings to Surabaya.

Transport

The Suramadu Bridge, opened in 2009, links the regency to Surabaya and has spurred industrial and housing development near its Madura-side landfall at Labang.

Culture

The population is predominantly Madurese and Muslim. The regency is noted for Madurese batik, bull racing traditions elsewhere on the island, and the tomb of Air Mata Ibu at Arosbaya, a revered pilgrimage site.
