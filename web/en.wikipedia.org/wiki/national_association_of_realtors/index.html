National Association of Realtors

The National Association of Realtors (NAR) is an American trade association composed of residential and commercial real estate brokers, salespeople, property managers, appraisers, and others engaged in the real estate industry. Founded in 1908 as the National Association of Real Estate Exchanges, it is one of the largest trade associations in the United States, with roughly 1.5 million members at its peak.

Activities

NAR owns the trademark "Realtor", which may be used only by members who subscribe to its code of ethics. It operates Realtor.com, publishes widely cited housing statistics such as the existing-home sales report, and is a major political lobbying organization. Members typically must belong to local and state associations in a three-way agreement and participate in a multiple listing service.

Legal challenges

In 2023 a federal jury in Missouri found NAR and major brokerages liable for conspiring to inflate commissions; a nationwide settlement in 2024, exceeding $400 million with practice changes, reshaped how commissions are negotiated in U.S. home sales.
