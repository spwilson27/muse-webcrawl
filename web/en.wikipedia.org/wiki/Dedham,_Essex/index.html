Dedham, Essex

Population: approx. 2,000

District: City of Colchester

County: Essex

Country: England

Dedham is a village in the borough of Colchester in northeast Essex, England, on the River Stour. It lies within the Dedham Vale National Landscape, an area celebrated for its association with the artist John Constable, who was born in nearby East Bergholt and painted the surrounding countryside extensively.

History

Dedham prospered during the medieval period from the cloth trade, and many of its timber-framed and Georgian buildings reflect the wealth of its wool merchants. The parish church of St Mary the Virgin, built largely at the end of the fifteenth century, dominates the village street and was constructed with money from the flourishing cloth industry.

Constable connection

Constable attended the grammar school in Dedham, walking from East Bergholt each day along the route now known as Fen Bridge. The Stour valley around Dedham, including Flatford Mill and Willy Lott's Cottage, forms the heart of "Constable Country".

Amenities

The village has a high street with shops, pubs and tea rooms, an arts and crafts centre housed in a former church, and a popular stretch of river used for rowing boats and walking. Dedham is a frequent destination for visitors exploring the Suffolk–Essex border landscape.
