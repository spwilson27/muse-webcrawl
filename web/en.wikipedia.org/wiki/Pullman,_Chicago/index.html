Pullman, Chicago

Pullman is a neighbourhood and community area on the South Side of Chicago, Illinois. It originated as one of the first planned industrial communities in the United States, built in the 1880s by George Pullman for workers of his Pullman Palace Car Company.

History

The model town provided housing, shops, a church, school and library, with rents deducted from wages. After wage cuts without rent reductions, workers launched the nationwide Pullman Strike of 1894, which was broken by federal intervention. The town was annexed by Chicago in 1889 and the company was later ordered to divest the housing.

In 2015 the district was designated Pullman National Monument, later redesignated a national historical park, the first National Park Service unit in Chicago.
