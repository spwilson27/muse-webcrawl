EE (formerly Everything Everywhere) is a British mobile network operator and internet service provider, and a brand of the BT Group. It was formed in 2010 as a 50:50 joint venture between Deutsche Telekom and France Télécom (now Orange S.A.), merging their respective UK businesses, T-Mobile UK and Orange UK.

History

The merger was announced in 2009 and completed in 2010, creating the largest communications company in the United Kingdom at the time. The two brands initially continued to operate side by side, with customers able to roam between the two networks. In 2012 the company rebranded as EE and launched the United Kingdom's first commercial 4G service on 30 October 2012, ahead of its competitors, using refarmed 1800 MHz spectrum.

In February 2015, BT agreed to acquire EE from Deutsche Telekom and Orange for £12.5 billion; the deal was cleared by the Competition and Markets Authority and completed on 29 January 2016. EE subsequently became BT's flagship consumer brand for mobile and, later, for broadband and converged services.

Services

EE operates 2G, 4G and 5G mobile networks; its 5G service launched in May 2019, the first in the UK. The company also provides home broadband over the Openreach network and hosts several mobile virtual network operators. BT also uses EE as the brand supplying the Emergency Services Network (ESN) radio communications for Great Britain's police, fire and ambulance services.
