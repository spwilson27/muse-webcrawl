Kirsan Ilyumzhinov

Kirsan Nikolayevich Ilyumzhinov (born 5 April 1962) is a Russian businessman and politician who served as president of the Republic of Kalmykia from 1993 to 2010 and as president of FIDE, the international chess federation, from 1995 to 2018.

Born5 April 1962, Elista
Known forPresident of FIDE (1995–2018); head of Kalmykia

Career

A wealthy entrepreneur, Ilyumzhinov won Kalmykia's first presidential election in 1993 and promoted chess heavily in the republic, building Chess City in Elista, which hosted the 1998 Chess Olympiad. As FIDE president he reunified the world championship title in 2006 but was frequently controversial; he claimed to have been abducted by extraterrestrials in 1997. In 2015 he was sanctioned by the United States over alleged dealings involving Syria, and he stepped aside from FIDE in 2018.
