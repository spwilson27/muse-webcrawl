Torreón

Founded: 25 September 1907 (city status)

Torreón is a city in the Mexican state of Coahuila, in the north of the country. Together with Gómez Palacio and Lerdo in neighbouring Durango it forms the La Laguna metropolitan area, one of Mexico's largest urban regions.

History

The city grew up around a railway junction and a watchtower (torreón) built in the nineteenth century to monitor the Nazas River. It was an important cotton-growing and textile centre and was a major battleground during the Mexican Revolution; Pancho Villa's forces captured it several times, notably in 1914.

Economy

Modern Torreón is a centre for metallurgy, dairy production and manufacturing, home to companies such as Grupo Lala and Peñoles' Met-Mex smelting complex.

Landmarks

The Cristo de las Noas, a large statue of Christ on a hill above the city, is a prominent landmark. The city is also home to the football club Santos Laguna.
