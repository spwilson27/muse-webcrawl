Avengers: Infinity War

Avengers: Infinity War is a 2018 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics superhero team the Avengers. Produced by Marvel Studios, it is the sequel to The Avengers (2012) and Avengers: Age of Ultron (2015), and the 19th film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU). Directed by Anthony and Joe Russo, it stars a large ensemble cast led by Robert Downey Jr., Chris Hemsworth, Mark Ruffalo, Chris Evans and Josh Brolin as Thanos.

Plot

The Avengers and their allies, including the Guardians of the Galaxy, Doctor Strange, Spider-Man and Black Panther, attempt to stop the Titan Thanos from collecting the six Infinity Stones, with which he intends to wipe out half of all life in the universe to restore "balance". Despite battles on Earth, Titan and Wakanda, Thanos assembles the Stones, sacrifices his daughter Gamora for the Soul Stone, withstands the heroes' final assault, and snaps his fingers — half of all living beings, including many of the heroes, disintegrate.

Release and reception

Film facts

DirectorsAnthony and Joe Russo
ReleasedApril 2018
Running time149 minutes
BudgetAbout $325–400 million
Box officeOver $2 billion worldwide

Infinity War grossed more than $2 billion worldwide, the first superhero film to do so, and was the highest-grossing film of 2018. It was praised for Brolin's performance, its emotional stakes and its ending, though its large cast drew some criticism. Its sequel, Avengers: Endgame, was released in April 2019.
