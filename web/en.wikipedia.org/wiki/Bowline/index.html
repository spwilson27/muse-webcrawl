Bowline

[Image placeholder: Bowline]

The bowline (pronounced "bow-lin") is an ancient and simple knot used to form a fixed loop at the end of a rope. It has the virtues of being both easy to tie and untie, and is considered one of the most essential knots — sometimes called the "king of knots".

Structure and use

The bowline is tied by forming a small loop in the standing part, passing the working end up through it, around the standing line, and back down — remembered by the mnemonic of a rabbit coming out of its hole, around the tree, and back down. It is widely used in sailing, climbing, rescue and camping, though it can work loose when unloaded or in slippery synthetic rope, and variants such as the Yosemite bowline and double bowline add security.
