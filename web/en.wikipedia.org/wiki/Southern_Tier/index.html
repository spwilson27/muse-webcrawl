Southern Tier

The Southern Tier is a geographic subregion of New York State in the United States, comprising the counties along the state's southern border with Pennsylvania west of the Catskills. It is generally considered to include Broome, Chemung, Chenango, Delaware, Schuyler, Steuben, Tioga, and Tompkins counties.

Geography and cities

The region lies in the Allegheny Plateau. Its principal cities are Binghamton, Elmira, Corning, Ithaca, and Hornell. Interstate 86 (the Southern Tier Expressway) and the Susquehanna and Chemung rivers cross the region from east to west.

Economy and culture

Historically a manufacturing region — IBM was founded in Endicott, and Corning Incorporated remains headquartered in Corning — the Southern Tier has shifted towards education, health care, and glass and materials science. Binghamton University and Cornell University (in Ithaca, sometimes included) are major employers. The region borders, and is sometimes grouped with, the Finger Lakes to the north and Pennsylvania's Northern Tier to the south.
