Actavis was a global pharmaceutical company focused primarily on developing, manufacturing and commercialising generic and brand pharmaceuticals. It originated as Watson Pharmaceuticals, founded in 1983 in the United States, and adopted the Actavis name in 2013 after acquiring the Icelandic generics firm Actavis Group in 2012 for about $5.5 billion.

History

Under chief executive Brent Saunders, the company pursued rapid growth through acquisitions, including Forest Laboratories in 2014. In March 2015 Actavis completed the acquisition of Allergan, Inc. — the maker of Botox — for about $66 billion, and in June 2015 the combined company changed its name to Allergan plc, retiring the Actavis name. The Actavis generics business was later sold to Teva Pharmaceutical Industries in 2016, and Allergan itself was acquired by AbbVie in 2020.

Products

The company marketed generic versions of drugs across many therapeutic areas, along with branded products in central nervous system disorders, gastroenterology, women's health and urology.
