Combat helmet
TypePersonal protective equipment
MaterialsSteel, aramid fibres, UHMWPE

A combat helmet is a type of helmet designed to protect the head of a soldier or other combatant during battle. Modern combat helmets protect against shrapnel, shell fragments, and some ballistic threats, and serve as a mounting platform for communications equipment, night-vision devices, and cameras.

History

Helmets were standard equipment in ancient and medieval warfare. After falling out of use with the rise of firearms, they returned in the First World War, when artillery fragments caused most head wounds: France introduced the Adrian helmet in 1915, followed by the British Brodie helmet and the German Stahlhelm.

Modern designs

Second World War steel helmets such as the American M1 gave way in the 1980s to designs made of ballistic fibres, notably the US PASGT helmet made of Kevlar. Later designs such as the Advanced Combat Helmet and the British Virtus helmet reduced weight while adding modular rails and improved blunt-impact padding.
