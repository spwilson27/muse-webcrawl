Education City Stadium (Arabic: استاد المدينة التعليمية) is a football stadium in Education City, Al Rayyan, Qatar. It was built for the 2022 FIFA World Cup and was officially inaugurated on 15 June 2020 in a virtual ceremony during the COVID-19 pandemic, dedicated to frontline healthcare workers.

Design

Nicknamed the "Diamond in the Desert", the stadium's facade features interlocking triangular panels inspired by traditional Islamic architecture and the facets of a diamond, which appear to change colour with the movement of the sun. The stadium is surrounded by the universities and research institutions of Education City, the flagship campus of the Qatar Foundation.

2022 FIFA World Cup

During the World Cup the stadium hosted eight matches, including a quarter-final. Its tournament capacity was 44,667. After the tournament, plans called for the modular upper tier to be removed, reducing capacity to around 25,000, with the removed seats donated to countries lacking sporting infrastructure.

Sustainability

The stadium was the first World Cup venue to receive a five-star rating from the Global Sustainability Assessment System (GSAS) for both design and construction. Cooling technology keeps spectators and players comfortable in Qatar's climate.
