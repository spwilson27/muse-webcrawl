Knowledge base

FieldKnowledge engineering, artificial intelligence
ComponentsFacts, rules, ontologies, inference engine

A knowledge base (KB) is a technology used to store complex structured and unstructured information used by a computer system. The term was originally used in connection with expert systems, where the knowledge base held the domain knowledge on which an inference engine performed reasoning to solve problems.

History

Knowledge bases emerged in the 1970s as the knowledge component of expert systems such as MYCIN and DENDRAL, in contrast to conventional databases which stored simple data. The process of eliciting, formalising and maintaining the knowledge was termed knowledge engineering, and the difficulty of acquiring expert knowledge became known as the knowledge acquisition bottleneck.

Types

Modern knowledge bases range from rule bases and ontologies expressed in formal representation languages, to large-scale knowledge graphs such as Wikidata and the Google Knowledge Graph, which store entities and their relationships as machine-readable triples. In customer support and information technology, "knowledge base" also denotes a self-service repository of articles and documentation.
