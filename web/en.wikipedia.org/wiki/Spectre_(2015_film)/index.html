Spectre (2015 film)

Spectre
Directed by: Sam Mendes
Starring: Daniel Craig, Christoph Waltz, Léa Seydoux
Release date: 2015
Box office: $880 million

Spectre is a 2015 spy film and the twenty-fourth in the James Bond series produced by Eon Productions. Directed by Sam Mendes, it stars Daniel Craig in his fourth outing as James Bond, alongside Christoph Waltz as Ernst Stavro Blofeld and Léa Seydoux as Madeleine Swann.

Plot

A cryptic message from the late M sends Bond on an unsanctioned mission through Mexico City and Rome, where he infiltrates a secret meeting of the criminal organisation Spectre. As M fights political pressure to dissolve the 00 section in favour of global surveillance, Bond uncovers that Blofeld — revealed as his adoptive brother — has orchestrated his past tragedies and plans to seize control of the world's intelligence data.

Production and reception

The film's opening Day of the Dead sequence in Mexico City was widely praised. Its theme song, "Writing's on the Wall" by Sam Smith, won the Academy Award for Best Original Song. Reviews were mixed-to-positive, considering it a step down from Skyfall; it grossed $880 million worldwide, making it one of the highest-grossing Bond films. It was followed by No Time to Die (2021).
