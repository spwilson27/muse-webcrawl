Sugizo (born Yasuhiro Sugihara, 8 July 1969) is a Japanese musician, singer-songwriter, composer, and record producer best known as the lead guitarist and violinist of the rock band Luna Sea and as a guitarist of X Japan. Trained in classical violin from early childhood, Sugizo is recognized for a distinctive playing style that blends heavy rock guitar with electronic music, ambient textures, and avant-garde experimentation, as well as for his activism on environmental and anti-nuclear issues.

Career

Sugizo formed Luna Sea in 1989 with Ryuichi Kawamura, Inoran, J, and Shinya. The band became one of the defining acts of the visual kei movement, selling millions of records in Japan before its indefinite hiatus in 2000, and reuniting in 2010. In 2009 Sugizo officially joined X Japan as lead guitarist, taking over the role of the late guitarist Hide in live performances, and he also joined the British trance act Juno Reactor as a touring guitarist. His solo work began with the experimental electronic album Truth? (1997) and has included film and stage scores, collaborations across genres, and the supergroup S.K.I.N. (2007) with Yoshiki, Gackt, and Miyavi. In 2022 he co-founded the supergroup The Last Rockstars with Yoshiki, Hyde, and Miyavi.

Style and equipment

Classically trained on violin from age three, Sugizo brings a melodic, legato approach to the guitar, favoring sustained tones, heavy use of effects, and electronic processing. He is strongly associated with ESP guitars, which manufacture his signature models. He regularly performs with both guitar and electric violin in the same concert.

Activism

Sugizo is known for environmental and humanitarian work, supporting renewable energy after the 2011 Fukushima disaster, volunteering in disaster relief, and performing at benefit concerts. He has also been outspoken against nuclear power and in favor of peace initiatives.
