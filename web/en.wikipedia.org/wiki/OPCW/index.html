The Organisation for the Prohibition of Chemical Weapons (OPCW) is an intergovernmental organisation and the implementing body for the Chemical Weapons Convention (CWC), which entered into force on 29 April 1997. Headquartered in The Hague, Netherlands, the OPCW, with 193 member states, oversees the global endeavour to permanently and verifiably eliminate chemical weapons: it verifies the destruction of declared stockpiles and production facilities, inspects chemical industry sites to deter diversion, and investigates allegations of use.

Mandate and work

Under the Convention, member states declare any chemical weapons stockpiles, production facilities, and relevant industrial activities. OPCW inspectors verify declarations through routine and challenge inspections and continuous monitoring. By July 2023 the organisation had verified the destruction of all 72,304 metric tonnes of declared chemical weapons stockpiles worldwide, including those of Russia and the United States. Its laboratory in Rijswijk and a network of designated laboratories analyse samples taken during inspections and investigations.

Investigations and Syria

The OPCW received the Nobel Peace Prize in 2013 "for its extensive efforts to eliminate chemical weapons", in the midst of its mission to remove Syria's declared arsenal following the 2013 Ghouta attacks. Its Fact-Finding Mission has confirmed repeated use of chlorine and sarin in the Syrian civil war, and the Investigation and Identification Team has attributed specific attacks to Syrian government forces and to the Islamic State. The OPCW's work on Syria, Salisbury (2018), and the poisoning of Alexei Navalny (2020) has drawn it into geopolitical controversy, with Russia and Syria disputing its findings and mandate.

Structure

The Conference of the States Parties is the organisation's plenary body; a 41-member Executive Council supervises the Technical Secretariat, headed by the Director-General (since 2018, Fernando Arias of Spain). The OPCW is not a United Nations agency but cooperates with the UN under a relationship agreement, and it hosts an annual Conference of States Parties in The Hague.
