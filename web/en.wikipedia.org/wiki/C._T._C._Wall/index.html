C. T. C. Wall

Charles Terence Clegg "Terry" Wall (born 14 December 1936) is a British mathematician, professor emeritus at the University of Liverpool, known for his work in algebraic topology, surgery theory and singularity theory. He was elected a Fellow of the Royal Society in 1969.

Career and work

Educated at Marlborough College and Trinity College, Cambridge, Wall completed his doctorate under Christopher Zeeman. His early work was in cobordism theory; his thesis and papers on the cobordism ring helped found modern bordism theory. In the 1960s he developed surgery theory, notably in his book Surgery on Compact Manifolds (1970), which systematised the classification of manifolds of dimension five and above through the surgery exact sequence and the Wall finiteness obstruction and surgery obstruction groups (L-groups) bear his name.

Honours

Wall received the Junior Berwick Prize (1965), the Senior Whitehead Prize and the Pólya Prize of the London Mathematical Society, and the Sylvester Medal of the Royal Society (1988). He served as president of the London Mathematical Society from 1978 to 1980.
