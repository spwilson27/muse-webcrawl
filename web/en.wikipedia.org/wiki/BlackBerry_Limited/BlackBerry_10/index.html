BlackBerry 10

BlackBerry 10 (BB10) is a discontinued mobile operating system developed by BlackBerry Limited for its BlackBerry smartphones. Based on the QNX microkernel, it was released on 30 January 2013 alongside the BlackBerry Z10 and Q10, and was the company's attempt to rebuild its platform for the touchscreen era.

Features

BlackBerry 10's interface was built around gestures and the BlackBerry Hub, a unified inbox combining email, messages and social accounts, reachable by a single swipe. Running apps appeared as "Active Frames" on the home screen, and the system included a runtime that could run many Android applications — later versions shipped with the Amazon Appstore preloaded. Devices included the Z10, Q10, Q5, Z30, Passport and Classic.

Decline

The platform never built a competitive app ecosystem, and BlackBerry moved to Android for its later handsets (beginning with the Priv in 2015). The final BB10 version was 10.3.3, and the supporting services were discontinued on 4 January 2022, ending BlackBerry Limited's era as a handset platform company.
