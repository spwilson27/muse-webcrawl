List of British flat horse races

This is a list of notable flat horse races held annually in Great Britain, grouped by the race's status in the Pattern and Listed system. British flat racing is centred on meetings such as Royal Ascot, Glorious Goodwood, the Epsom Derby Festival and York's Ebor Festival.

The five British Classics—the 2000 Guineas, 1000 Guineas, Derby, Oaks and St Leger—form the core of the three-year-old programme, with the Derby, first run in 1780, the most famous of all.

Group 2 and Group 3 races

Below the Group 1 tier sit numerous Group 2 events, such as the Dante Stakes at York (a leading Derby trial), the Hardwicke Stakes and Queen's Vase at Royal Ascot, the Goodwood Cup for stayers, and the Gimcrack Stakes for two-year-olds, followed by a large programme of Group 3 and Listed races run at courses across the country from spring through autumn.
