Shahrekord University

Shahrekord University (Persian: دانشگاه شهرکرد) is a public university in Shahrekord, the capital of Chaharmahal and Bakhtiari Province in southwestern Iran. It is the principal comprehensive university of the province and offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes across the sciences, engineering, humanities and agriculture.

History

The institution began in 1978 as a higher education centre focused on animal science and natural resources, reflecting the agricultural and pastoral character of the region. It grew steadily after the Iranian Revolution, gaining independent university status in 1991 as the University of Shahrekord, and has since expanded into a multi-faculty comprehensive university.

Organisation

The university comprises faculties including Basic Sciences, Mathematical Sciences, Engineering and New Technologies, Humanities and Arts, Letters, Agriculture, Natural Resources and Earth Sciences, and Veterinary Medicine. It enrolls several thousand students and maintains research centres devoted to topics such as biotechnology, nanotechnology and the ecology of the Zagros region.

Campus

The main campus lies at Rahmatieh in Shahrekord, at an elevation of over 2,000 metres — Shahrekord is among Iran's highest provincial capitals and is known as the "roof of Iran". The university also supervises affiliated faculties in other towns of the province, including veterinary and agricultural units in nearby centres.
