Robert Earle "Bobby" Clarke (born August 13, 1949) is a Canadian former professional ice hockey centre who played his entire 15-year National Hockey League career with the Philadelphia Flyers, captaining the "Broad Street Bullies" to Stanley Cup championships in 1974 and 1975.

Career

Drafted 17th overall in 1969, Clarke won the Hart Memorial Trophy as league MVP three times (1973, 1975, 1976) despite playing with type 1 diabetes, a condition that had caused several teams to pass on him. He recorded 1,210 points in 1,144 games, was the first player from an expansion team to reach 1,000 points, and played a famously fierce role in Canada's 1972 Summit Series victory over the Soviet Union.

Post-playing

After retiring, Clarke served multiple stints as the Flyers' general manager and in other front-office roles. He was inducted into the Hockey Hall of Fame in 1987, and his number 16 was retired by the Flyers.
