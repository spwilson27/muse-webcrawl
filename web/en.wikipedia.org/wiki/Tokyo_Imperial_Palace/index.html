Tokyo Imperial Palace

The Tokyo Imperial Palace (皇居, Kōkyo) is the primary residence of the Emperor of Japan. Located in Chiyoda, Tokyo, on the former site of Edo Castle, its grounds cover about 1.15 square kilometres including parks, moats and stone walls.

The palace was built after the Meiji Restoration moved the imperial residence from Kyoto to Tokyo in 1869. The current palace buildings date largely from the 1960s after wartime destruction. The East Gardens are open to the public, while the inner grounds open on 2 January and the Emperor's birthday.
