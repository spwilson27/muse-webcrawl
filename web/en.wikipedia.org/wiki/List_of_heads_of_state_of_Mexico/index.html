List of heads of state of Mexico

The head of state of Mexico is the President of the United Mexican States, who is also head of government. Since independence in 1821, Mexico has also twice been an empire (under Agustín de Iturbide and Maximilian I) and has been governed during periods of instability by juntas, regencies, and numerous interim and de facto rulers. This list summarises the principal heads of state.
