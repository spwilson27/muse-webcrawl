Petroleum, or crude oil, is a naturally occurring liquid mixture of hydrocarbons found in geological formations, refined into fuels (petrol, diesel, kerosene) and petrochemicals such as plastics. Since the first modern wells of 1859 it has become the world's most important energy commodity.

Geopolitics and climate

Oil wealth built industries and states, provoked the 1970s oil shocks, and shapes foreign policy from the Persian Gulf to Karachi's port trade. As a fossil fuel it is a leading source of emissions; peak-demand forecasts and the electric-vehicle transition cloud its long-term future.
