List of sports rivalries

A sports rivalry is intense competition between athletic teams or athletes. Rivalries frequently arise from geographic proximity (derbies), historical encounters, repeated championship meetings, or cultural and political differences. This article lists notable rivalries across sports.

Association football

  • El Clásico — FC Barcelona vs. Real Madrid (Spain)
  • Old Firm derby — Celtic vs. Rangers (Scotland)
  • Superclásico — Boca Juniors vs. River Plate (Argentina)
  • North West derby — Liverpool vs. Manchester United (England)
  • Derby della Madonnina — AC Milan vs. Inter Milan (Italy)
  • Revierderby — Borussia Dortmund vs. Schalke 04 (Germany)
  • Le Classique — Paris Saint-Germain vs. Olympique de Marseille (France)
  • India–Pakistan cricket rivalry is mirrored in football by the Kolkata derby (Mohun Bagan vs. East Bengal)

American football

  • Chicago Bears vs. Green Bay Packers — the NFL's most-played rivalry
  • Michigan vs. Ohio State ("The Game") — college football
  • Alabama vs. Auburn ("Iron Bowl")
  • Army vs. Navy

Baseball

  • New York Yankees vs. Boston Red Sox
  • Chicago Cubs vs. St. Louis Cardinals
  • Los Angeles Dodgers vs. San Francisco Giants

Basketball

  • Boston Celtics vs. Los Angeles Lakers
  • Duke vs. North Carolina (college)

Ice hockey

  • Montreal Canadiens vs. Toronto Maple Leafs
  • Canada vs. United States (international)

Cricket

  • The Ashes — England vs. Australia
  • India vs. Pakistan

Tennis and individual sports

  • Federer–Nadal rivalry
  • Ali–Frazier (boxing)
  • Senna–Prost (Formula One)
