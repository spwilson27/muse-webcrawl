Yanis Varoufakis

Yanis Varoufakis
Born: 24 March 1961, Palaio Faliro, Athens, Greece
Occupation: Economist, politician, author
Office: Minister of Finance of Greece (January–July 2015)
Party: MeRA25 (co-founder)

Yanis Varoufakis (born 24 March 1961) is a Greek economist and politician who served as Greece's Minister of Finance in 2015, becoming internationally prominent during the Greek government-debt crisis. A professor of economics who has taught in Britain, Australia, and Greece, he is known for work in game theory and political economy, including the book The Global Minotaur.

Finance minister

After Syriza won the January 2015 election, Prime Minister Alexis Tsipras appointed Varoufakis finance minister. Varoufakis led Greece's confrontational negotiations with the Eurogroup and the "troika" of creditors over debt restructuring, arguing Greece's debt was unsustainable — a standoff that drew worldwide attention. He resigned on 6 July 2015, the day after Greek voters rejected creditor terms in a referendum, saying the prime minister preferred his departure to facilitate a deal. His memoir of the episode, Adults in the Room (2017), was later adapted into a film by Costa-Gavras.

Later career

In 2016 Varoufakis co-founded the Democracy in Europe Movement 2025 (DiEM25), and in 2018 the Greek party MeRA25, for which he returned to parliament in 2019 (the party lost its seats in 2023). He also worked as an economist-in-residence at Valve Corporation, studying virtual economies, and is a prolific author and commentator. His later books include Technofeudalism: What Killed Capitalism (2023).
