Real estate development

TypeBusiness process
StagesSite selection, entitlement, financing, construction, sale or lease
ParticipantsDevelopers, architects, contractors, lenders, investors

Real estate development, or property development, is a business process encompassing activities that range from the renovation and re-lease of existing buildings to the purchase of raw land and the sale of developed land or parcels to others.

Process

Developers coordinate architects, engineers, contractors, lenders, and public agencies through market analysis, site acquisition, zoning and entitlement approvals, financing, construction, and marketing. Development is inherently risky because projects take years and are exposed to market cycles, interest rates, and regulatory change.

Types

Projects are categorized by use — residential, commercial, industrial, retail, hospitality, and mixed-use — and by strategy, from ground-up construction to adaptive reuse of historic structures.

Finance

Developers typically combine equity with construction loans and later permanent financing, measuring returns with metrics such as yield on cost and internal rate of return. Large-scale urban projects may involve public–private partnerships and tax incentives.
