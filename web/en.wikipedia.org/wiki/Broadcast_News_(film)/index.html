Broadcast News (film)

Broadcast News is a 1987 American romantic comedy-drama film written, produced and directed by James L. Brooks. It stars Holly Hunter, Albert Brooks and William Hurt as journalists working at the Washington, D.C. bureau of a television network news operation, and explores the tension between journalistic substance and entertainment values in American television news.

Plot

Jane Craig, a brilliant and workaholic network news producer, is torn between two colleagues: Aaron Altman, a talented but un-telegenic reporter who is her best friend and secretly loves her, and Tom Grunick, a handsome, charming anchorman who has risen despite limited journalistic ability. When layoffs hit the bureau and Tom stages an emotional moment in a story, Jane must decide what she values more — integrity or success.

Reception

The film was a critical success and received seven Academy Award nominations, including Best Picture, Best Actress for Hunter, Best Actor for Hurt and Best Supporting Actor for Albert Brooks. In 2018 it was selected for preservation in the National Film Registry of the Library of Congress as culturally, historically and aesthetically significant. It is frequently cited by journalists as one of the most accurate depictions of television news production.
