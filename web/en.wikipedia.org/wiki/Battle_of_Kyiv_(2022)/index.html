Battle of Kyiv (2022)

Battle of Kyiv
Date24 February – 2 April 2022
LocationKyiv and Kyiv Oblast, Ukraine
BelligerentsUkraine vs Russia
ResultUkrainian victory; Russian withdrawal

The Battle of Kyiv was a major battle of the Russian invasion of Ukraine in early 2022, in which Ukrainian forces repelled a Russian offensive aimed at capturing the capital and forcing a change of government.

The offensive

On 24 February 2022 Russian airborne troops seized Hostomel Airport in an attempted airbridge, but Ukrainian counterattacks prevented its use for landing reinforcements. Russian armoured columns advanced from Belarus and the northeast, forming a long convoy northwest of the city, while fighting raged in suburbs including Bucha, Irpin and Brovary.

Outcome

Logistical failures, fierce resistance and flooding of the Irpin River stalled the assault. In late March Russia announced a withdrawal from the Kyiv axis, and by 2 April Ukrainian forces had regained the whole of Kyiv Oblast. Evidence of mass killings of civilians in Bucha drew international condemnation.

Significance

The failure to take Kyiv was a strategic defeat for Russia, preserving Ukraine's government and shifting the war's focus to the east and south.
