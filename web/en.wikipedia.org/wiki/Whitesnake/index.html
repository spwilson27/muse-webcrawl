Whitesnake are an English hard rock band formed in London in 1978 by singer David Coverdale, following his departure from Deep Purple. Originally a blues-rock band drawing on Coverdale's soulful vocals and the twin guitars of Bernie Marsden and Micky Moody — with ex-Deep Purple members Jon Lord and Ian Paice in early line-ups — Whitesnake became one of the biggest hard rock acts in the world in the mid-1980s after reinventing themselves in a sleeker, glam-metal image for the American market.

History

The band's early albums — Trouble (1978), Lovehunter (1979), Ready an' Willing (1980, featuring the UK hit "Fool for Your Loving"), Come an' Get It (1981) and Saints & Sinners (1982) — established them across Europe and Japan. After Coverdale rebuilt the band around guitarist John Sykes, Slide It In (1984) broke them in the United States. The eponymous Whitesnake album (1987) was a phenomenon, selling over eight million copies in the US alone on the strength of the number-one single "Here I Go Again" (a re-recording of a 1982 song), "Is This Love", "Still of the Night" and "Give Me All Your Love", propelled by iconic MTV videos featuring actress Tawny Kitaen.

Slip of the Tongue (1989), recorded with guitar virtuoso Steve Vai, continued their success, but Coverdale put the band on hiatus in 1990. He later worked with Jimmy Page on the Coverdale–Page album (1993) and revived Whitesnake sporadically in the 1990s, then permanently in 2002. Later studio albums include Good to Be Bad (2008), Forevermore (2011), The Purple Album (2015, reworkings of Coverdale's Deep Purple songs) and Flesh & Blood (2019). The band announced a farewell tour in the early 2020s, though European dates in 2022 were cancelled owing to health problems among band members.

Style and legacy

Whitesnake's sound combines blues-rock roots with the polished production and virtuoso guitar work of 1980s hard rock. Coverdale remains the only constant member and one of rock's most recognisable vocalists. The band's best-known songs remain staples of classic rock radio and popular culture.
