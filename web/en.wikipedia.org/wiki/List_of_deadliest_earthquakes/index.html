List of deadliest earthquakes

The following is a list of the deadliest earthquakes in recorded history, ranked by estimated death toll. Earthquake mortality depends not only on magnitude but on population density, building quality, and secondary effects such as tsunamis, landslides, and fires.

Notes

Estimates for historical earthquakes vary widely between sources, and figures for ancient and medieval events are especially uncertain. The 1556 Shaanxi earthquake remains the deadliest natural disaster of its type ever recorded, largely because of the collapse of loess cave dwellings (yaodongs).
