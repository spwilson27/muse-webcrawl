Zugspitze

Elevation: 2,962 m (9,718 ft)

Location: Germany–Austria border, Bavaria/Tyrol

First ascent: 1820

The Zugspitze is the highest mountain in Germany, rising to 2,962 metres in the Wetterstein range of the Northern Limestone Alps, on the border between Bavaria and the Austrian state of Tyrol.

Access

The summit is reached by three cable cars and the rack railway of the Bayerische Zugspitzbahn from Garmisch-Partenkirchen. Austria's Tyrolean Zugspitze Cable Car ascends from Ehrwald. A summit station complex straddles the border, with restaurants, a weather station and Germany's highest post office.

Glaciers

The mountain carries two of Germany's few remaining glaciers, the Schneeferner and the Höllentalferner, both of which have shrunk rapidly in recent decades and are expected to disappear.
