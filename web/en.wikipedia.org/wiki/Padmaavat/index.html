Padmaavat is a 2018 Indian Hindi-language epic period drama film directed by Sanjay Leela Bhansali, based on the epic poem Padmavat (1540) by Malik Muhammad Jayasi. Deepika Padukone stars as Rani Padmavati, the legendary queen of Mewar; Shahid Kapoor plays her husband Maharawal Ratan Singh; and Ranveer Singh plays the Delhi sultan Alauddin Khalji, whose obsession with the queen leads him to besiege Chittor.

Plot

Khalji, having heard of Padmavati's beauty, attacks the fortress of Chittorgarh to capture her. When defeat becomes inevitable, the queen and the women of the fort perform jauhar — self-immolation — rather than submit, while the Rajput men ride out to a final battle.

Controversy and reception

The film provoked violent protests from Rajput groups alleging historical distortion, delaying its release and prompting cuts and a title change from Padmavati to Padmaavat. On release it was a major commercial success and won several awards, including National Film Awards for music.
