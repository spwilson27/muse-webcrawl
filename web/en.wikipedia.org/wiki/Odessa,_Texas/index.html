Odessa, Texas

Odessa is a city in and the county seat of Ector County, Texas, United States. It is located primarily in Ector County, although a portion of the city extends into Midland County. Odessa's population was 114,428 at the 2020 census, making it the 28th most-populous city in Texas. Together with Midland, it forms the Midland–Odessa combined statistical area.

Odessa is known for its petroleum industry and for high school football, notably the Permian Panthers, whose story was chronicled in the book and film Friday Night Lights. The city was founded in 1881 as a water stop and cattle-shipping point on the Texas and Pacific Railway.

Economy

The economy is driven by the Permian Basin oil and gas industry, with related manufacturing, healthcare and education sectors, including the University of Texas Permian Basin and Odessa College.
