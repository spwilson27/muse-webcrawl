SIAL Paris (Salon International de l'Alimentation, "International Food Exhibition") is one of the world's largest trade fairs for the food industry, held every two years in Paris, France. Founded in 1964 during the first International Food Week, it brings together producers, retailers, distributors, and food-service professionals from around the world.

Scale and content

Recent editions at the Paris-Nord Villepinte exhibition centre have drawn on the order of 7,000–7,500 exhibitors from more than 100 countries and 150,000–300,000 professional visitors. Exhibits span some twenty sectors, including meat, dairy, beverages, frozen foods, confectionery, organic products, and food processing equipment, with numerous national pavilions. The SIAL Innovation awards highlight new products, and conference programmes address topics such as sustainability, alternative proteins, and consumer trends.

SIAL network

The Paris show anchors a global network of sister events organised by the Comexposium group under the SIAL brand, including editions in Shanghai, Shenzhen, Toronto/Montreal, Abu Dhabi, Jakarta, and New Delhi. Together with Germany's Anuga, SIAL Paris is regarded as one of the two leading global meeting points of the food and beverage trade.
