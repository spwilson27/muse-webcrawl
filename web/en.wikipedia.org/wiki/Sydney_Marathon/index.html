Sydney Marathon

The Sydney Marathon is a marathon race held annually in Sydney, Australia, as the centrepiece of the Sydney Running Festival each September. It traces its origin to the marathon of the 2000 Sydney Olympics; a legacy event was first run in 2001 to commemorate the Games. The race is presented by TCS as the TCS Sydney Marathon.

Course

The course is a point-to-point scenic route: runners start in North Sydney, cross the Sydney Harbour Bridge — closed to traffic for the event — pass through The Rocks and the central business district, Centennial Park and Moore Park, and finish at the Sydney Opera House forecourt. Alongside the full marathon, the festival includes a 10 km race, a family fun run and a mini marathon.

World Marathon Major

After a candidacy period, the Sydney Marathon was announced in November 2024 as the seventh Abbott World Marathon Major, joining Tokyo, Boston, London, Berlin, Chicago and New York City from 2025 — the first Major in the Southern Hemisphere. Participation has grown to tens of thousands of runners, with course records in recent years around 2:06–2:07 for men and 2:19–2:21 for women.
