Trevor Hastie

Trevor John Hastie (born 27 June 1953) is a South African statistician and professor at Stanford University, known for his contributions to statistical learning and data mining.

Career

Hastie studied at the University of Cape Town and earned his PhD in statistics from Stanford in 1984. After working at AT&T Bell Laboratories, he joined the Stanford faculty, where he holds a chair in statistics and biomedical data science.

Work

With Robert Tibshirani, Hastie developed generalized additive models, the subject of their 1990 book. He is co-author, with Robert Tibshirani and Jerome Friedman, of The Elements of Statistical Learning (2001, 2nd ed. 2009), one of the most influential textbooks in machine learning, and of An Introduction to Statistical Learning (2013), widely used with its free online course. His research includes work on principal curves, the lasso, model assessment, and statistical computing in R and S.
