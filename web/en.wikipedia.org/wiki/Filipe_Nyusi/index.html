Filipe Nyusi

Filipe Jacinto Nyusi (born 9 February 1959) is a Mozambican politician who served as the fourth President of Mozambique from 2015 to 2025, and as leader of the ruling FRELIMO party. He was previously Minister of Defence (2008–2014).

Early life and career

Nyusi was born in Mueda, Cabo Delgado, to parents involved in the liberation struggle. He studied mechanical engineering in Czechoslovakia and management in Manchester, worked for the Mozambique Ports and Railways company, and rose in FRELIMO, joining its Central Committee in 2012.

Presidency

Elected in 2014 and re-elected in 2019 amid opposition fraud claims, Nyusi signed the 2019 Maputo peace accord with RENAMO leader Ossufo Momade. His tenure faced the Cabo Delgado Islamist insurgency from 2017, aided later by Rwandan and SADC forces, the hidden-debt scandal inherited from his predecessor, Cyclone Idai (2019), and major liquefied natural gas projects in the Rovuma Basin. He was succeeded by Daniel Chapo in January 2025 after disputed 2024 elections and widespread protests.
