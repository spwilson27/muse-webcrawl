Castle Hill (Budapest)

Castle Hill (Hungarian: Várhegy) is a limestone hill in Buda, Budapest, Hungary, rising about 170 metres above the Danube. It is crowned by Buda Castle and forms the historic Castle District, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1987.

Landmarks

Major sights include the Royal Palace, Matthias Church, Fisherman's Bastion, the medieval old town's baroque houses, and the Castle Hill cave system used as a hospital in the Second World War. The hill is reached by the 1870 funicular from Clark Ádám Square at the Chain Bridge.

History

First fortified by Béla IV after the Mongol invasion of 1241, the district was royal seat, Ottoman stronghold, and Habsburg palace quarter, and was heavily damaged in the 1945 Siege of Budapest before restoration. See Budapest.
