Antonio Joseph Mendez (November 15, 1940 – January 19, 2019) was an American technical operations officer for the Central Intelligence Agency (CIA), who specialized in support of clandestine and covert operations. He is best known for engineering the rescue of six American diplomats from Tehran during the Iran hostage crisis.

The Canadian Caper

In January 1980, Mendez traveled to Iran posing as an Irish film producer and exfiltrated six US diplomats who had taken refuge with Canadian officials. The group posed as a Canadian film crew scouting locations for a fake science-fiction film called Argo, complete with a fabricated studio and trade-press publicity in Hollywood.

Later life and recognition

Mendez retired from the CIA in 1990 with the Intelligence Star and was named one of the agency's 50 "Trailblazers." His memoir Argo: How the CIA and Hollywood Pulled Off the Most Audacious Rescue in History (2012) was adapted into the Academy Award-winning film Argo, in which he was portrayed by Ben Affleck. He also worked as an artist and author on espionage tradecraft.
