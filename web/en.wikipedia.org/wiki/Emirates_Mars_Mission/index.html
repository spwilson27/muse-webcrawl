Emirates Mars Mission

The Emirates Mars Mission (Arabic: مشروع الإمارات لاستكشاف المريخ), also known as the Hope Probe (مسبار الأمل, Misbar Al-Amal), is a space exploration mission by the United Arab Emirates to Mars. The Hope probe was launched on 19 July 2020 and successfully entered orbit around Mars on 9 February 2021, making the UAE the first Arab nation—and only the fifth space agency overall at the time—to reach Mars.

Objectives

The mission is designed to study the Martian atmosphere and climate, producing the first complete picture of the planet's weather throughout the Martian day and year. Its instruments—the Emirates Exploration Imager (EXI), the Emirates Mars Infrared Spectrometer (EMIRS), and the Emirates Mars Ultraviolet Spectrometer (EMUS)—measure dust, water vapour, temperature, and the escape of hydrogen and oxygen from the upper atmosphere.

Development

The probe was developed by the Mohammed bin Rashid Space Centre in collaboration with the Laboratory for Atmospheric and Space Physics at the University of Colorado Boulder, Arizona State University, and the University of California, Berkeley. Rather than buying a finished spacecraft, Emirati engineers worked alongside their partners with the stated goal of building domestic space-science capability. Science data from the mission have been shared openly with the international community.
