Solar water disinfection


SODIS — a low-cost household method of disinfecting water using sunlight and PET bottles.

Solar water disinfection, commonly abbreviated SODIS, is a simple and low-cost method of disinfecting drinking water using sunlight. Contaminated water is filled into transparent plastic (PET) bottles and exposed to full sunlight for six hours or more.

Mechanism

Ultraviolet-A radiation from the sun damages the DNA of bacteria, viruses, and parasites, while the heating of the water in the bottle contributes a pasteurising effect. The combination inactivates most pathogens that cause diarrhoeal disease.

Application

The method was developed and promoted from the 1980s by the Swiss Federal Institute of Aquatic Science and Technology (Eawag). It is used by millions of people in developing countries where safe water supplies are unavailable. SODIS does not remove chemical contamination and requires relatively clear water and sunny weather, but it needs no fuel, chemicals, or spare parts.
