The Meadowlands Sports Complex is a sports and entertainment complex located in East Rutherford, in Bergen County, New Jersey, United States, situated about 8 miles (13 km) west of Midtown Manhattan. It is owned and operated by the New Jersey Sports and Exposition Authority (NJSEA).

The complex opened in 1976 with the Meadowlands Racetrack and Giants Stadium. It is best known today as the home of MetLife Stadium, shared by the New York Giants and New York Jets of the NFL, and the Meadowlands Racetrack, a major harness racing venue that hosts the Hambletonian Stakes.

Facilities

MetLife Stadium, opened in 2010, replaced Giants Stadium and seats about 82,500 spectators, making it one of the largest stadiums in the NFL. It hosted Super Bowl XLVIII in February 2014, the first Super Bowl played in an open-air stadium in a cold-weather city, and is scheduled to host matches of the 2026 FIFA World Cup, including the final.

Meadowlands Racetrack opened in 1976 and hosts harness racing, including the Hambletonian, and thoroughbred racing on a turf course. The Meadowlands Arena (formerly Brendan Byrne Arena, Continental Airlines Arena, and Izod Center) opened in 1981 and was home to the New Jersey Devils of the NHL and the New Jersey Nets of the NBA before those teams moved to Newark and Brooklyn respectively; the arena closed in 2015.

The American Dream retail and entertainment complex, long delayed, partly opened beginning in 2019 on the site, featuring an indoor ski slope, water park, and theme park.

History

The complex was developed in the New Jersey Meadowlands marshlands in the 1970s, after the New York Giants agreed to move from New York City to New Jersey. Giants Stadium opened on 10 October 1976. Over subsequent decades the complex hosted the Cosmos of the NASL, the Generals of the USFL, numerous concerts, WrestleMania events, and Pope John Paul II in 1995.
