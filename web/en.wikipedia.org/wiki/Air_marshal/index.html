Air marshal

Air marshal may refer to a senior air force rank, or to a law enforcement officer who provides security aboard commercial flights.

Rank

Air marshal is a senior commissioned rank in the Royal Air Force and many Commonwealth air forces, equivalent to NATO OF-8 and senior to air vice-marshal and junior to air chief marshal. Variants include air marshal, Marshal of the Air Force, and corresponding titles in other languages. The rank originated in the RAF in the early 20th century and is used in Australia, India, Pakistan, Bangladesh and other air forces.

Aviation security

A sky marshal (also called an air marshal, flight marshal, or in the United States a Federal Air Marshal) is a covert law enforcement or counter-terrorist agent on board a commercial aircraft to counter aircraft hijackings. In the United States, Federal Air Marshals are employed by the Federal Air Marshal Service, part of the Transportation Security Administration, a programme greatly expanded after the September 11 attacks. Other countries, including Israel, India, Germany and Australia, operate similar programmes.
