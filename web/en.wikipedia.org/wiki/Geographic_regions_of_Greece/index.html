Geographic regions of Greece

The traditional geographic regions of Greece

The geographic regions of Greece (Greek: γεωγραφικά διαμερίσματα, "geographic departments") are the country's nine traditional subdivisions. They are primarily geographical and historical units rather than administrative ones, although some correspond roughly to modern administrative regions. They are still widely used in everyday discourse and in statistics.

Relation to administrative regions

Since the Kallikratis reform of 2011, Greece is administered through thirteen regions (peripheries), which subdivide the traditional geographic regions — Macedonia, for example, is split into Western, Central and part of Eastern Macedonia and Thrace. The nine traditional regions remain the framework used by the National Statistical Service of Greece for many publications.
