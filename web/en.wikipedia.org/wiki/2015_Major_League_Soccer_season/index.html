The 2015 Major League Soccer season was the 20th season of Major League Soccer, the top professional soccer league in the United States and Canada. The league expanded to 20 clubs with the addition of New York City FC and Orlando City SC, while Chivas USA folded after 2014.

Season highlights

The season brought an influx of European stars: Kaká joined Orlando, David Villa and later Andrea Pirlo and Frank Lampard joined New York City, Steven Gerrard signed for the LA Galaxy and Didier Drogba for the Montreal Impact. Toronto FC's Sebastian Giovinco produced one of the finest individual seasons in league history, winning the Golden Boot (jointly, on assists tiebreaker), the MVP award and Newcomer of the Year.

Playoffs and MLS Cup

The New York Red Bulls won the Supporters' Shield for the best regular-season record. In the playoffs, the Portland Timbers reached MLS Cup 2015 against Columbus Crew SC. On 6 December 2015 in Columbus, Portland won 2–1 — Diego Valeri scoring after just 27 seconds, the fastest goal in MLS Cup history, after a goalkeeping error — to claim the club's first championship.

Attendance

Average attendance exceeded 21,500 per match, a league record at the time, led by Seattle Sounders FC and expansion Orlando City.
