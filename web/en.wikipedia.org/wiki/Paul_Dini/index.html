Paul Dini

Paul McClaran Dini (born August 7, 1957) is an American writer, animator, and comic book artist. He is best known as a producer and writer for several Warner Bros./DC Comics animated series, including Batman: The Animated Series.

BornAugust 7, 1957, New York City, U.S.
OccupationWriter, producer, comic book creator
Notable workBatman: The Animated Series; Harley Quinn (co-creator)

Career

Dini began in animation at Filmation in the early 1980s, working on series such as He-Man and the Masters of the Universe. At Warner Bros. Animation he became a central creative figure on Tiny Toon Adventures and then Batman: The Animated Series (1992–1995), for which he wrote acclaimed episodes including "Heart of Ice".

Harley Quinn

With artist Bruce Timm, Dini co-created the character Harley Quinn, who debuted in the 1992 episode "Joker's Favor" and went on to become one of DC's most popular characters. Their graphic novel Mad Love (1994) won an Eisner Award.

Later work

Dini has written extensively for DC Comics, including Detective Comics and the Gotham City Sirens series, and authored the autobiographical graphic novel Dark Night: A True Batman Story (2016).
