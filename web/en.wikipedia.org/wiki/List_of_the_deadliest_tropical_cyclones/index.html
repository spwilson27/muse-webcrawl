List of the deadliest tropical cyclones

The following is a list of the deadliest tropical cyclones in recorded history. The deadliest tropical cyclone on record is the 1970 Bhola cyclone, which killed an estimated 300,000 to 500,000 people in East Pakistan (now Bangladesh). Most of the deadliest storms struck the low-lying Ganges Delta of the northern Bay of Bengal, where storm surges can inundate vast, densely populated areas; nearly all of the twenty deadliest occurred in the North Indian Ocean basin.

Deadliest in other basins

The deadliest Atlantic hurricane was the Great Hurricane of 1780 in the Caribbean, which killed an estimated 22,000–27,501 people. The deadliest Pacific typhoon after Nina was Typhoon Haiyan (2013) in the Philippines, with about 6,300 confirmed deaths — illustrating how warning systems, shelters, and evacuation have greatly reduced mortality in recent decades. In Bangladesh, cyclone shelters and early-warning programmes cut the death toll of comparable storms from hundreds of thousands in 1970 to thousands or fewer by the 2000s (e.g., Cyclone Sidr, 2007, ~3,400 deaths).
