Destiny (video game)

Destiny is an online-only multiplayer first-person shooter video game developed by Bungie and previously published by Activision. It was released worldwide on 9 September 2014 for the PlayStation 3, PlayStation 4, Xbox 360 and Xbox One consoles. Set in a "mythic science fiction" world, the game follows players in the role of Guardians — protectors of Earth's last safe city — as they wield a power called Light to defend the city against hostile alien races.

Gameplay

Destiny combines first-person shooting with role-playing and MMO elements in a persistent, shared-world environment. Players choose one of three character classes — Titan, Hunter or Warlock — and one of three species, and undertake player-versus-environment missions across destinations including Earth, the Moon, Venus and Mars, either solo or in three-player "fireteams". Six-player cooperative raids, such as the Vault of Glass and King's Fall, form the game's endgame challenges, while player-versus-player combat takes place in the Crucible. Progress is driven by acquiring increasingly powerful weapons and armour.

Expansions and reception

The game received two small expansions, The Dark Below (2014) and House of Wolves (2015), followed by the major expansion The Taken King (2015), widely credited with substantially improving the game, and Rise of Iron (2016). Reviews at launch praised the gunplay, visuals and art direction but criticised the thin story and repetitive structure; the game nevertheless sold strongly, with Activision reporting over $500 million shipped to stores on its first day. A sequel, Destiny 2, was released in September 2017, and the original game's servers remain online, though it no longer receives updates.

Setting

Centuries before the game, humanity experienced a Golden Age after encountering the Traveler, a vast spherical entity that terraformed planets and extended human lifespans. A catastrophic Collapse ended the Golden Age, leaving the survivors gathered under the Traveler's protection in the Last City, threatened by the Fallen, Hive, Vex and Cabal.
