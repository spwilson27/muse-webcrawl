Uccle

Uccle (French) or Ukkel (Dutch) is one of the 19 municipalities of the Brussels-Capital Region, Belgium. Located in the southern part of the region, it is one of the largest and most affluent residential municipalities of Brussels.

Geography

Uccle covers about 22.9 square kilometres and borders the Sonian Forest to the southeast. It includes neighbourhoods such as Churchill, Fort Jaco, Vivier d'Oie and Stalle. The Wolvendael Park and the Dieweg Cemetery are notable green spaces.

History

The area was settled in the Middle Ages; the church of Saint Peter dates in part to the 13th century. In the 19th and 20th centuries Uccle developed into a leafy residential suburb of Brussels, attracting artists and writers.

Institutions

Uccle hosts the Royal Observatory of Belgium, founded in its present location in 1890, and the Royal Meteorological Institute. It is home to numerous embassies and the European School of Brussels I.
