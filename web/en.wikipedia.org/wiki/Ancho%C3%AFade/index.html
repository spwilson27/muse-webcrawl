Anchoïade

TypeDip / sauce
RegionProvence, France
Main ingredientsAnchovies, olive oil, garlic

Anchoïade is a Provençal sauce or dip made from anchovies pounded or blended with olive oil and garlic, sometimes with vinegar, capers or herbs. It is a staple of the cuisine of Provence in the south of France.

Preparation and serving

Salted anchovies are rinsed, then worked to a paste with garlic and a stream of olive oil until the mixture emulsifies to a creamy consistency. Anchoïade is traditionally served as a spread on toast or as a dip for raw vegetables such as celery, fennel, radishes and peppers, and it may accompany grilled meats or boiled eggs. It is closely related to other Mediterranean anchovy preparations, such as the Piedmontese bagna càuda, which is served warm.
