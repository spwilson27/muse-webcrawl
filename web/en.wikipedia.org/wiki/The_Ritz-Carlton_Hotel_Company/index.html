The Ritz-Carlton Hotel Company, LLC is an American multinational company that operates the Ritz-Carlton luxury hotel chain. It is a subsidiary of Marriott International. The brand traces its heritage to the Ritz hotels of César Ritz and the Ritz-Carlton Boston, opened in 1927.

History

The modern company was formed in 1983 when William B. Johnson acquired the Ritz-Carlton name and the Boston hotel and began expanding the brand. Marriott acquired a majority stake in 1995 and full ownership by 1998. The company operates around a hundred luxury hotels and resorts in some 30 countries and territories, together with branded residences and the Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection.

Service culture

Ritz-Carlton is famous for its service philosophy, summarised in its motto "Ladies and Gentlemen serving Ladies and Gentlemen" and its Gold Standards; it is the only hotel company to have twice won the Malcolm Baldrige National Quality Award (1992 and 1999).
