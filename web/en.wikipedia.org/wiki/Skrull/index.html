Skrull

The Skrulls are a fictional extraterrestrial race appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Stan Lee and artist Jack Kirby, they first appeared in Fantastic Four #2 (1962). The Skrulls are green-skinned humanoids with pointed ears and ridged chins, renowned for their innate shapeshifting ability, which they use for infiltration and espionage.

Fictional background

The Skrulls originate from the planet Skrullos in the Andromeda Galaxy. Long ago, the Celestials experimented on their ancestors, producing three branches: the Prime Skrulls, the Deviant Skrulls — who possessed shapeshifting and ultimately prevailed — and the Eternal Skrulls. The Deviant branch built a vast star-spanning Skrull Empire, one of the oldest interstellar civilisations in the Marvel Universe, and came into prolonged conflict with the Kree Empire, most famously in the Kree–Skrull War storyline.

Role in stories

Skrulls are classic antagonists of the Fantastic Four, the Avengers, and other heroes, typically attempting to conquer Earth by replacing key individuals. In the 2008 crossover storyline Secret Invasion, it was revealed that Skrull agents had infiltrated Earth for years, replacing several superheroes. Other storylines, including their portrayal in the Marvel Cinematic Universe films such as Captain Marvel (2019), have depicted groups of Skrull refugees in a more sympathetic light. Notable Skrulls include the Super-Skrull, who wields the combined powers of the Fantastic Four, and the princess-turned-hero Veranke and Hulkling.

Powers and abilities

All Deviant Skrulls can reshape their bodies at will, mimicking the appearance of other creatures and objects of roughly similar mass. Advanced training and later genetic engineering produced "War-Skrulls" capable of replicating superhuman powers as well as appearance.
