Meliton Varlamovich Kantaria (Georgian: მელიტონ ქანთარია; October 5, 1920 – December 27, 1993) was a Georgian Soviet sergeant of the Red Army credited with hoisting the Victory Banner over the Reichstag building in Berlin on 30 April 1945, together with Mikhail Yegorov, during the Battle of Berlin. The act was commemorated in the celebrated photograph by Yevgeny Khaldei showing the flag over the Reichstag.

Biography

Born into a peasant family in Jvari in western Georgia, Kantaria worked on a collective farm before joining the Red Army in 1938. He served as a scout with the 756th Rifle Regiment of the 150th Rifle Division, 3rd Shock Army. According to the official account, on the evening of 30 April 1945 Kantaria and Yegorov, accompanied by other soldiers, placed the divisional banner — the flag that became known as the Victory Banner — on the roof of the Reichstag, above the main entrance and later on the dome.

In 1946 Kantaria and Yegorov were awarded the title Hero of the Soviet Union. After the war Kantaria returned to Georgia, lived in Sukhumi and Ochamchire, worked in trade administration, and served as a deputy of the Supreme Soviet of the Abkhaz ASSR. During the war in Abkhazia in 1992–93 he was forced to leave his home; he died in Moscow in December 1993 and was buried in Jvari.

Legacy

The Victory Banner raised by Kantaria and Yegorov is preserved at the Central Museum of the Armed Forces in Moscow and a copy of it is carried in Russia's Victory Day parades on Red Square.
