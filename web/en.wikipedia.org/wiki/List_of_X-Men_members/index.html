List of X-Men members

The X-Men are a team of mutant superheroes appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Founded by Professor Charles Xavier, the team first appeared in The X-Men #1 (September 1963), created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby. Over six decades the roster has grown to include dozens of mutants across multiple squads and affiliated teams.
