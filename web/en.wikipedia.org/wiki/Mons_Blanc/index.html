Mons Blanc is a mountain in the Montes Alpes range on the Moon. It is located in the western part of the range, near the northeastern rim of Mare Imbrium, at selenographic coordinates 45.0° N, 1.0° E. It is the tallest mountain in the Montes Alpes, rising about 3.6 kilometres above the surrounding surface.

The name, approved by the International Astronomical Union in 1935, is derived from Mont Blanc, the highest mountain in the Alps on Earth, after which the lunar range was also named. Mons Blanc's diameter is approximately 25 kilometres. Near it lie the promontory Promontorium Agassiz and the smaller Mons Piton to the southwest.
