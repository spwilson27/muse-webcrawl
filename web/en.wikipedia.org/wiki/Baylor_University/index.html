Baylor University

Motto: Pro Ecclesia, Pro Texana ("For the Church, For Texas")
Established: 1845
Type: Private research university
Location: Waco, Texas, United States
Religious affiliation: Baptist General Convention of Texas

Baylor University is a private Baptist Christian research university in Waco, Texas, United States. Chartered in 1845 by the Republic of Texas through the efforts of Baptist pioneers, it is the oldest continuously operating university in Texas and one of the first educational institutions west of the Mississippi River in the United States. It is classified among "R1: Doctoral Universities — Very high research activity" and enrolls around 20,000 students.

History

Baylor was founded in Independence, Texas, and named for Judge R. E. B. Baylor, a Texas Supreme Court justice and one of its founders. The university moved to Waco in 1886, merging with Waco University. Its law school is the oldest in Texas. Baylor became a research-intensive university with a strategic plan ("Illuminate") that led to R1 status in 2021.

Academics

The university is organised into twelve academic units, including the College of Arts & Sciences, the Hankamer School of Business, the School of Law, the Louise Herrington School of Nursing, and the George W. Truett Theological Seminary. It offers more than 120 undergraduate and over 100 graduate programmes.

Athletics

Baylor's athletic teams, the Bears (and Lady Bears historically), compete in the Big 12 Conference. The men's basketball team won the NCAA national championship in 2021, and the women's team won national titles in 2005, 2012, and 2019. Home football games are played at McLane Stadium, opened in 2014 on the banks of the Brazos River.

Campus and traditions

The 1,000-acre campus lies along the Brazos River in Waco. Traditions include the Baylor Line, in which freshmen run onto the field at football games, and Dr Pepper Hour, reflecting Waco's status as the birthplace of the soft drink. The university's mascots are live American black bears housed in an on-campus habitat.
