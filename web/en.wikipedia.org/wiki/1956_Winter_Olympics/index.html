The 1956 Winter Olympics, officially the VII Olympic Winter Games, were held in Cortina d'Ampezzo, Italy, from 26 January to 5 February 1956. They were the first Winter Games broadcast widely on television and marked the Olympic debut of the Soviet Union, which immediately topped the medal table.

Highlights

Austrian skier Toni Sailer won all three men's alpine gold medals, one of the Games' defining performances. The Soviet Union won the ice hockey tournament, ending Canadian dominance. Figure skating was held outdoors for the last time, and the Olympic oath was taken for the first time by a woman, Italian skier Giuliana Minuzzo.

Context

Cortina had been awarded the cancelled 1944 Games. Organisation suffered from funding and infrastructure problems, but the Games were judged a success and established the Dolomite resort's winter-sports reputation.
