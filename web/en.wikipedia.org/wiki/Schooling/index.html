Schooling

Schooling may refer to:

  • Education, particularly attendance at a school — the process of formal instruction of pupils in primary and secondary education.
  • Schooling (fish) — the collective behaviour of fish swimming together in a coordinated group; a school is a group of fish of the same species swimming in the same direction in a synchronised manner.
  • Home schooling — education of children at home, usually by parents, rather than in a public or private school; see Homeschooling.

Fish behaviour

A school of fish moving as one.

In fish biology, a shoal is any group of fish that remains together for social reasons, while a school is a shoal in which the fish swim in the same direction in a coordinated manner. Schooling can confuse predators, improve hydrodynamic efficiency and increase the chance of finding food or mates.
