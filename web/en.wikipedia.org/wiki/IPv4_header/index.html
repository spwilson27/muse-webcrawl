IPv4 header

The IPv4 header is the header of an Internet Protocol version 4 (IPv4) packet, containing addressing and control information that enables the packet to be delivered across a network. The header is typically 20 octets long, but may be longer when options are present.

Fields

The header fields include: Version (4 bits), Internet Header Length (IHL, 4 bits), Type of Service (8 bits), Total Length (16 bits), Identification (16 bits), Flags (3 bits), Fragment Offset (13 bits), Time to Live (8 bits), Protocol (8 bits), Header Checksum (16 bits), the 32-bit Source Address and 32-bit Destination Address, followed by optional Options and Padding.

Fragmentation

The Identification, Flags, and Fragment Offset fields support fragmentation and reassembly of packets that exceed the maximum transmission unit of a network link. The Don't Fragment (DF) flag prohibits fragmentation, while the More Fragments (MF) flag indicates further fragments follow.
