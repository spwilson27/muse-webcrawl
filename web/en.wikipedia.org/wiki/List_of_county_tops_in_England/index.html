List of county tops in England

This is a list of county tops in England — the highest point of each historic and ceremonial county. Visiting every county top is a popular hill-walking challenge, sometimes called "county topping". The highest county top, and the highest ground in England, is Scafell Pike in Cumbria at 978 metres.

The lowest county tops include Beacon Hill in Norfolk (103 m) and the low-lying tops of Lincolnshire and Cambridgeshire. Historic county tops sometimes differ from ceremonial ones where boundaries changed, notably in Lancashire and the former Cumberland and Westmorland.
