Deadlift

Deadlift
TypeCompound weight training
EquipmentBarbell, trap bar
MusclesGlutes, hamstrings, back, traps

The deadlift is a strength exercise in which a loaded barbell is lifted from the floor to standing, hips and knees locked. It is one of three powerlifting lifts, with squat and bench press.

Technique

The lifter grips the bar outside the legs, keeps a neutral spine, and drives through the floor extending hips and knees. The sumo variant uses a wide stance with hands inside the legs.

Records

Deadlifts feature in strongman; Eddie Hall lifted 500 kg in 2016, later exceeded by Hafthor Julius Bjornsson. Variations include Romanian, stiff-leg, trap-bar and deficit deadlifts.
