Assurance
Senses: insurance (esp. life); auditing service; theological certainty; quality management

Assurance may refer to several distinct concepts. In British usage, "assurance" traditionally describes insurance against events that are certain to happen eventually, such as death — hence "life assurance" — as distinct from insurance against contingent risks. In accounting, assurance services are independent professional evaluations that improve the quality of information for decision makers, most commonly audits and reviews of financial statements.

In auditing

Auditing standards distinguish reasonable assurance — the high level obtained in a full audit — from the limited assurance of a review engagement. The concept underpins the wording of auditors' reports.

In theology

In Christian theology, assurance denotes the believer's confidence of salvation, a topic debated particularly in Reformed and Wesleyan traditions.

Other uses

"Assurance" also appears in quality assurance, information assurance, and as the name of ships, companies and works of art.
