Hockey Club Lokomotiv (Russian: ХК Локомотив, English: Locomotive HC), commonly known as Lokomotiv Yaroslavl, is a professional ice hockey team based in Yaroslavl, Russia. It is a member of the Tarasov Division in the Kontinental Hockey League (KHL).

History

The team was founded in 1949 as the team of the local railway depot. It played under several names, including Torpedo Yaroslavl, before taking the Lokomotiv name in 2000. The club won the Russian championship in 1997, 2002 and 2003, the latter two under coach Vladimír Vůjtek, becoming the first club to repeat as Russian league champions in the post-Soviet era.

2011 plane crash

On 7 September 2011, a Yakovlev Yak-42 aircraft carrying the team to Minsk for its opening game of the season crashed shortly after takeoff from Yaroslavl's Tunoshna Airport, killing 44 of the 45 people on board, including the entire playing roster, coaching staff and prospects. The disaster devastated the club and the wider hockey world. Lokomotiv withdrew from the KHL for the 2011–12 season while rebuilding, fielding a team in the second-tier VHL instead, and returned to the KHL in 2012–13. The club's rebuilt roster was composed largely of young players and free-agent signings, and the team has remained a perennial playoff contender since its return.
