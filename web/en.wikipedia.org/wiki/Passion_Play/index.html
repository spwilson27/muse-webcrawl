Passion Play

A Passion Play is a dramatic presentation depicting the Passion of Jesus Christ: his trial, suffering, crucifixion, and often resurrection. Passion plays developed in medieval Europe out of liturgical drama and mystery plays and are traditionally performed during Lent or Holy Week.

History

From the Middle Ages, towns and religious communities staged elaborate cycles. After periods of suppression in the Reformation and Enlightenment, village traditions survived in some places, most famously at Oberammergau in Bavaria.

Oberammergau

The Oberammergau Passion Play has been performed roughly every ten years since 1634, when villagers vowed to stage it if spared from plague. It involves over a thousand local performers and draws an international audience. Other notable productions include those at Erl, Sordevolo, and various open-air theatres in the United States.
