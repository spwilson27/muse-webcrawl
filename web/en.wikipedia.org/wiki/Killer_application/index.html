Killer application

In marketing and technology, a killer application (often shortened to killer app) is any computer program or software that is so necessary or desirable that it proves the core value of some larger technology, such as a computer platform, video game console or operating system.

Examples

The spreadsheet VisiCalc is credited with driving sales of the Apple II in the late 1970s, and Lotus 1-2-3 played the same role for the IBM PC. Electronic mail, the World Wide Web browser, and later applications such as messaging and social media have each been described as killer apps for the internet and smartphones.
