Product data management

Product data management (PDM) is the business function and software discipline concerned with managing and publishing product data — including CAD models, drawings, bills of materials (BOMs), specifications and change records — throughout engineering and manufacturing. PDM systems act as a central vault with version control, check-in/check-out, access permissions, workflow and release management.

Functions

Core PDM functions include secure data storage and retrieval, revision and version control, configuration management of product variants, engineering change management (change requests, orders and notices), parts classification and reuse, and integration with CAD tools and enterprise resource planning (ERP) and manufacturing systems. By ensuring engineers work from a single authoritative version of each document, PDM reduces errors and rework.

Relation to PLM

PDM emerged in the 1980s alongside CAD adoption, with early systems managing drawing files on engineering workstations. It is now generally regarded as the foundation of the broader discipline of product lifecycle management (PLM), which extends data management across the whole product life — from concept and design through manufacturing, service and disposal — and includes processes as well as documents. Major PDM/PLM platforms include Siemens Teamcenter, PTC Windchill, Dassault ENOVIA and Autodesk Vault.
