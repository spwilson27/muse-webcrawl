[Image: The Globe and Mail Centre on King Street East, Toronto]

The Globe and Mail is a Canadian newspaper printed in several cities, based in Toronto, founded in 1844 by George Brown as The Globe. It is Canada's national newspaper of record, owned by the Thomson family's Woodbridge company, with Report on Business — published since the 1960s — as its influential business brand.

Character

The Globe covers federal politics, business and investigations for a national subscriber audience; its reporters have won many National Newspaper Awards, and its motto tradition ('The Globe and Mail' name dates to the 1936 merger with the Mail and Empire) anchors Canadian media history.
