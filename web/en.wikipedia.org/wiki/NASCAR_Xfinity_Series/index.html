NASCAR Xfinity Series

The NASCAR Xfinity Series is the second-highest level of professional stock car racing sanctioned by NASCAR in the United States, below the NASCAR Cup Series. It serves as a proving ground for drivers, crew and teams aspiring to the top level.

The series began in 1982 as the Budweiser Late Model Sportsman Series and has carried several sponsor names, including Busch Series (1984–2007) and Nationwide Series (2008–2014), before Xfinity, Comcast's brand, became title sponsor in 2015.

Format

The season features about 33 races, mostly alongside Cup events, ending in playoffs that crown a champion. Cars are slightly less powerful and use different bodies than Cup cars.
