Skyrail Rainforest Cableway

LocationCairns to Kuranda, Queensland, Australia
Opened31 August 1995
Length7.5 km (4.7 mi)
TerminalsSmithfield · Kuranda
Mid-stationsRed Peak · Barron Falls

The Skyrail Rainforest Cableway is a 7.5-kilometre (4.7 mi) scenic cableway running above the Barron Gorge National Park, in the Wet Tropics of Queensland World Heritage Area, north of Cairns, Queensland, Australia. It operates from the Smithfield terminal in Cairns to the Kuranda terminal, and opened on 31 August 1995.

Route

The cableway takes about 1.5 hours one way, or 2.5 hours return including time at the mid-stations. Two mid-stations, Red Peak and Barron Falls, allow passengers to disembark and explore the rainforest floor on boardwalks, and to view the Barron Falls and Barron Gorge. At Red Peak, complimentary ranger-guided tours depart regularly. The cableway carries six-person gondolas, including a small number of "Diamond View" gondolas with glass floors.

Construction and environment

Construction was carried out with minimal environmental impact: tower sites were cleared by hand, and tower components were delivered by helicopter and assembled without access roads. At the time of its opening it was the longest gondola cableway in the world.

Awards

Skyrail has won numerous tourism and environmental awards and is frequently combined with the Kuranda Scenic Railway for the return journey between Cairns and Kuranda.
