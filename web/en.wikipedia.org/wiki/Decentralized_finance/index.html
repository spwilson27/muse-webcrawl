Decentralized finance

Decentralized finance (DeFi) is a form of finance based on blockchain smart contracts - most commonly on Ethereum - that provides lending, exchange, derivatives and yield services without traditional intermediaries such as banks or brokerages.

How it works

Protocols such as Uniswap (automated market-maker exchanges), Aave and Compound (lending pools) and MakerDAO (the DAI stablecoin) hold user assets in transparent, composable smart contracts accessed via crypto wallets.

Risks and criticism

DeFi boomed in 2020 ("DeFi summer") but has suffered major hacks, exploits, rug pulls and collapses, regulatory uncertainty, and high volatility. Critics question its decentralisation in practice and its real-world utility.
