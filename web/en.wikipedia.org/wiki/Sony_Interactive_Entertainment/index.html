Sony Interactive Entertainment

Sony Interactive Entertainment LLC (SIE) is an American video game and digital entertainment company of Japanese conglomerate Sony Group Corporation. It was founded in Tokyo, Japan, on November 16, 1993, as Sony Computer Entertainment (SCE), to handle Sony's venture into video game development for the PlayStation systems. Since the successful launch of the original PlayStation console in 1994, the company has developed the home console line, controllers, handhelds, and other hardware.

History

Sony Computer Entertainment was founded in 1993 by Ken Kutaragi, who had been inspired to create a gaming console after watching his daughter play Nintendo's Famicom. The PlayStation launched in Japan in December 1994 and worldwide in 1995, becoming the first video game console to ship over 100 million units. Subsequent consoles include the PlayStation 2 (2000), the best-selling home console of all time, the PlayStation 3 (2006), PlayStation 4 (2013), and PlayStation 5 (2020). Handheld systems include the PlayStation Portable and PlayStation Vita.

Reorganization

On April 1, 2016, Sony Computer Entertainment and Sony Network Entertainment International merged to form Sony Interactive Entertainment, headquartered in San Mateo, California. The company operates the PlayStation Network online service and manages first-party development through PlayStation Studios, whose teams include Naughty Dog, Santa Monica Studio, Insomniac Games, Guerrilla Games, and Sucker Punch Productions. In 2022, SIE acquired Bungie, the studio behind Destiny.

Software

SIE publishes major franchises including God of War, The Last of Us, Uncharted, Spider-Man, Gran Turismo, Horizon, and Ghost of Tsushima.
