Earthwork (engineering)

Earthworks in engineering are operations involving the processing of large quantities of soil and unformed rock, moved, placed and compacted to create embankments, cuttings and level formations for construction.

Operations

Typical works include excavation (cut), transport, filling, compaction and grading. Cut material is reused as fill where suitable, balanced using mass-haul diagrams to minimise haul distance and cost. Structures include embankments for roads and railways, earth dams, levees, canals and building platforms.

Machinery

Heavy plant includes bulldozers, excavators, scrapers, graders, dump trucks and compactors (rollers). Volumes are calculated from cross-sections, historically by the average end area method and now by digital terrain models.

Considerations

Design must address soil properties, drainage, slope stability, settlement and environmental impact. See also Earthworks (archaeology and general).
