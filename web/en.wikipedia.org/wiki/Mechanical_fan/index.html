Mechanical fan

A mechanical fan is a powered machine used to create a flow of air. A fan consists of a rotating arrangement of vanes or blades, generally called a blower, which act on the air. The rotating assembly of blades and hub is known as an impeller, a rotor, or a runner.

Types

Common types include ceiling fans, table fans, pedestal fans, axial-flow fans, centrifugal fans (blowers), and cross-flow fans. Fans are used for ventilation, cooling, drying, and air conditioning.

History

Hand fans have been used since antiquity; the punkah was used in India around 500 BCE. Mechanically driven fans appeared in the 19th century, and electrically powered fans became widespread in the early 20th century.
