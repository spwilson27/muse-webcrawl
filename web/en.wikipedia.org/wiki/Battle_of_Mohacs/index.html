Battle of Mohacs

Battle of Mohacs is the unaccented English rendering of the Battle of Mohács (Hungarian: mohácsi csata), fought on 29 August 1526 near Mohács in southern Hungary, in which the army of the Ottoman Empire under Sultan Suleiman the Magnificent annihilated the army of the Kingdom of Hungary. King Louis II died fleeing the field, and the medieval Hungarian kingdom collapsed, leading to its partition between the Ottomans, the Habsburgs and the principality of Transylvania for the following century and a half.

Summary

The Hungarian army of some 25,000–30,000 men, including heavy cavalry and a large contingent of infantry, advanced against an Ottoman force roughly twice its size supported by Janissaries and about 160 cannon. The Hungarian plan depended on a rapid charge before the Ottomans deployed; instead, the cavalry attack broke against prepared Ottoman guns and musket fire, and the army was destroyed in about two hours with perhaps 14,000–20,000 killed.
