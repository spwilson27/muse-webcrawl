France Télécom

Founded1 January 1988 (as an operator), France
FateRenamed Orange S.A. in 2013
IndustryTelecommunications

France Télécom S.A. was the main telecommunications company in France, the third largest in Europe and one of the largest in the world. Created in 1988 as the operator spun off from the French PTT administration, it was progressively privatised from 1997.

History

France Télécom ran pioneering services such as the Minitel videotex network and acquired the British mobile operator Orange in 2000, adopting its brand for many of its services. A wave of employee suicides in 2008–2009 sparked a national scandal over management methods and led to the departure of chief executive Didier Lombard.

Renaming

On 1 July 2013 the group completed its rebranding and took the name Orange, under which it continues to operate as one of Europe's leading telecom operators.
