Dad's Army is a British television sitcom about the Home Guard during the Second World War. It was written by Jimmy Perry and David Croft and originally broadcast on BBC1 from 31 July 1968 to 13 November 1977. It ran for nine series and 80 episodes in total, plus radio adaptations and a feature film.

Premise

The series is set in the fictional south coast town of Walmington-on-Sea and follows a platoon of Home Guard volunteers composed mainly of men too old or in reserved occupations for regular military service. The platoon is led by the pompous bank manager Captain Mainwaring (Arthur Lowe), assisted by his mild-mannered chief clerk Sergeant Wilson (John Le Mesurier), and includes the excitable Lance Corporal Jones (Clive Dunn), the spiv Private Walker, the elderly Private Godfrey and the naive Private Pike, whose mother calls him a "stupid boy".

Reception and legacy

Dad's Army regularly attracted audiences of over 18 million and remains one of Britain's most beloved comedies. Its catchphrases — "Don't panic!", "They don't like it up 'em!" and "We're doomed!" — entered popular culture. A new feature film adaptation was released in 2016.
