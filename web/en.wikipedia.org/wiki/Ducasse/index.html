Ducasse

Ducasse is a French surname. It is most widely associated with the chef Alain Ducasse. This article lists notable people and other uses of the name.

People

  • Alain Ducasse (born 1956) — French-born Monégasque chef known for operating numerous restaurants worldwide and for being among the first chefs to hold three Michelin stars at restaurants in three different cities, including Le Louis XV in Monaco and Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester in London.
  • Isidore Ducasse (1846–1870) — French poet better known by his pen name, the Comte de Lautréamont, author of Les Chants de Maldoror.
  • Jean-Baptiste Ducasse (1646–1715) — French naval officer and colonial administrator, governor of Saint-Domingue.
  • Pierre Ducasse (born 1987) — French footballer, a midfielder.

Other uses

  • Ducasse Paris — the restaurant and hospitality group founded by Alain Ducasse.
  • The name also occurs in gastronomy through establishments such as Alain Ducasse au Plaza Athénée in Paris.
