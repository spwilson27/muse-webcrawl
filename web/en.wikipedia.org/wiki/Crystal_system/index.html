Crystal system

In crystallography, a crystal system is a set of point groups in which the point groups themselves and their corresponding space groups are assigned to a lattice system. A crystal is classified by its lattice and atomic arrangement.

The seven systems

  • Triclinic — no symmetry constraints, a≠b≠c, angles all unequal and not 90°
  • Monoclinic — one twofold axis
  • Orthorhombic — three unequal axes at 90°
  • Tetragonal — a=b≠c, all angles 90°
  • Trigonal (rhombohedral)
  • Hexagonal — a=b≠c, γ=120°
  • Cubic (isometric) — a=b=c, all angles 90°, highest symmetry

Classification

There are 7 crystal systems, 14 Bravais lattices, 32 crystallographic point groups and 230 space groups. The systems are ordered by symmetry, from triclinic (lowest) to cubic (highest).
