Austrian Armed Forces

Austrian Armed Forces (Bundesheer)
Founded: 15 May 1955
Headquarters: Vienna
Branches: Land Forces, Air Force, Special Forces
Commander-in-chief: President of Austria
Conscription: 6 months (males)

The Austrian Armed Forces (German: Bundesheer) are the combined military forces of the Republic of Austria. They were re-established in 1955 following the Austrian State Treaty and the country's declaration of permanent neutrality, which shapes Austria's defensive doctrine.

Organisation

The Bundesheer is administered by the Federal Ministry of Defence. Its principal components are the Land Forces (Landstreitkräfte), the Air Force (Luftstreitkräfte), which operates Eurofighter Typhoon interceptors, and special forces (Jagdkommando). Austria retains conscription: men are liable for six months of military service (or nine months of alternative civilian service), and the total force including reservists numbers in the tens of thousands.

Missions

The constitution assigns the armed forces the defence of Austria's neutrality, assistance after natural disasters, and participation in international peacekeeping, to which Austria has contributed since 1960, including in the Middle East, Bosnia and Kosovo.
