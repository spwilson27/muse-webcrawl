The James Bond film series is a British series of spy films based on the fictional character of MI6 agent James Bond, code name 007, created by Ian Fleming. Beginning with Dr. No in 1962, the films produced by Eon Productions form one of the longest-running and highest-grossing film franchises in history, with 25 films released over six decades.

History

Producers Albert R. "Cubby" Broccoli and Harry Saltzman acquired the film rights to most of Fleming's novels and founded Eon Productions. Dr. No (1962), starring Sean Connery, established the series' template: exotic locations, elaborate action, gadgets, and the famous gun-barrel opening sequence. From Russia with Love (1963) and Goldfinger (1964) consolidated the formula, and by Thunderball (1965) Bond had become a global cultural phenomenon.

Signature elements

The series is known for its recurring features: the "Bond, James Bond" introduction, the vodka martini "shaken, not stirred", the gadget-equipped cars supplied by Q Branch, pre-title action sequences, lavish title sequences with theme songs performed by major recording artists, and villains with elaborate schemes. John Barry's music, beginning with the "James Bond Theme" arranged for Dr. No, defined the series' sound.

The Craig era and beyond

Casino Royale (2006) rebooted the series with Daniel Craig in a grittier, more character-driven style. Skyfall (2012), directed by Sam Mendes, became the first Bond film to gross over $1 billion. Craig's tenure concluded with No Time to Die (2021). In 2022 Amazon acquired MGM, Eon's distribution partner, and in 2025 Amazon MGM Studios took creative control of the franchise, with producers Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson stepping back.
