Tia-Clair Toomey

Tia-Clair Toomey (born 22 July 1993) is an Australian weightlifter and CrossFit Games athlete. She is widely regarded as the most successful athlete in the history of the CrossFit Games, having won the women's individual title seven times between 2017 and 2024, earning the title of "Fittest Woman on Earth".

Career

Toomey grew up in Queensland, Australia, and competed in track and field before taking up weightlifting and CrossFit in her early twenties. She made her CrossFit Games debut in 2015, finishing second and being named Rookie of the Year, and was again runner-up in 2016. In 2016 she also represented Australia in weightlifting at the Rio Olympic Games in the 58 kg class.

She won her first CrossFit Games title in 2017 by the narrowest of margins, then defended the title in 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021 and 2022, surpassing the previous record for consecutive and total wins. After taking the 2023 season off following the birth of her child, she returned in 2024 to win a seventh championship. Toomey has also competed in bobsleigh, winning events on the North American Cup circuit while attempting to qualify for the Winter Olympics, and has won gold at the Commonwealth Games in weightlifting in 2018.

Personal life

Toomey is coached by her husband, Shane Orr, who has also coached other elite CrossFit athletes. The couple have lived and trained in several locations including Cookeville, Tennessee, and Australia.
