Annabel Jones is a British television producer, best known as the executive producer, alongside creator Charlie Brooker, of the dystopian anthology series Black Mirror.

Career

Jones began her career in television production and worked with Brooker through the production company Zeppotron and later House of Tomorrow, part of Endemol Shine. Together they developed Black Mirror for Channel 4 in 2011; the series moved to Netflix in 2016 and became a global success, winning multiple Emmy Awards, including for the episodes "San Junipero" and "USS Callister" and the interactive film Bandersnatch.

Broke & Bones

In 2020 Jones and Brooker left Endemol Shine and founded the production company Broke & Bones, which signed an investment deal with Netflix. Under the new company they continued to produce Black Mirror and other projects.
