Mars Express is a space exploration mission by the European Space Agency to explore the planet Mars — the agency's first planetary mission. It consists of the Mars Express Orbiter and the Beagle 2 lander, which failed after landing.

Mission

Launched from Baikonur aboard a Soyuz rocket, the orbiter entered Mars orbit on 25 December 2003. The small Beagle 2 lander was released but never contacted Earth; it was located in images in 2015, having landed but failed to deploy fully.

Science

Instruments include the HRSC high-resolution stereo camera, the OMEGA and PFS spectrometers, the ASPERA plasma analyser, and the MARSIS radar, which probed subsurface deposits and reported evidence consistent with buried liquid water beneath the south polar layered deposits. Mars Express has also served as a communications relay and produced detailed maps of minerals, ice and atmospheric methane.
