Lemmings is a 1991 puzzle-platform video game developed by DMA Design and published by Psygnosis for the Amiga. A large number of ports followed, and the game is regarded as one of the most influential and successful titles of its era, selling millions of copies and spawning a long-running franchise.

Gameplay

A procession of small green-haired, blue-robed lemmings walks mindlessly forward, turning only at walls and falling to their deaths off edges. The player must assign a limited number of skills — climber, floater, bomber, blocker, builder, basher, miner and digger — to individual lemmings to guide a required percentage of the horde to the level's exit before time runs out. Levels are grouped into Fun, Tricky, Taxing and Mayhem difficulty ratings.

Development and legacy

The game grew out of an animation experiment by DMA programmer Mike Dailly, with Russell Kay and Dave Jones shaping the design. Its blend of indirect control and clockwork puzzles proved enormously popular; sequels such as Lemmings 2: The Tribes and Oh No! More Lemmings followed, and the series' rights passed to Sony with the acquisition of Psygnosis. DMA Design later became Rockstar North, creator of Grand Theft Auto.
