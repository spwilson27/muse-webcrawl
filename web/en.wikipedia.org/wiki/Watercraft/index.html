Watercraft

A watercraft or water vessel is any vehicle designed for travel across or through water, including boats, ships, hovercraft, and submarines. Watercraft have been used for transport, trade, warfare, fishing and recreation since prehistory.

Terminology

Usage varies by tradition: a ship is generally a large seagoing vessel, while a boat is smaller — though submarines are called "boats" by their crews, and legal definitions differ by jurisdiction for registration, safety equipment and right-of-way rules. Vessels are typically classified by function (cargo, passenger, fishing, naval, pleasure) and by hull and propulsion type.

History

The earliest known watercraft were dugout canoes dating back some 8,000–10,000 years. Sailing ships enabled oceanic trade and exploration, steamships industrialised water transport in the nineteenth century, and modern fleets include container ships over 400 metres long, nuclear submarines, and autonomous surface vessels.
