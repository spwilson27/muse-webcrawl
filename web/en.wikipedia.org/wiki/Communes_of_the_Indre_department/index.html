Communes of the Indre department

The following is a list of the 241 communes of the Indre department of France, in the Centre-Val de Loire region. The departmental capital (prefecture) is Châteauroux. The communes cooperate in intercommunalities including the Communauté d'agglomération Châteauroux Métropole and several communautés de communes.
