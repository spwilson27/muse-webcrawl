L3Harris Technologies

L3Harris Technologies, Inc. is an American technology company, defense contractor and information technology services provider that produces products for command and control, communications, intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance systems, avionics, and space and naval systems. It is headquartered in Melbourne, Florida.

History

The company was formed by the merger of L3 Technologies and Harris Corporation, announced in October 2018 and completed on 29 June 2019, creating one of the largest defense contractors in the United States. Both predecessor companies grew through long chains of acquisitions; Harris traced its roots to the Harris Automatic Press Company of the 1890s and became a major supplier of communications equipment.

Operations

L3Harris is a major supplier of tactical radios, night-vision devices, intelligence systems and satellite payloads to the U.S. Department of Defense and allied militaries. It acquired Aerojet Rocketdyne in 2023, adding rocket propulsion capabilities. The company employs tens of thousands of people worldwide.
