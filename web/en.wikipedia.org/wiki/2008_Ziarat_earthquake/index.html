2008 Ziarat earthquake

Date29 October 2008
LocationZiarat, Balochistan, Pakistan
Magnitude6.4 M
Deaths~215

The 2008 Ziarat earthquake struck the Ziarat district of Balochistan, Pakistan, on 29 October 2008 with a magnitude of 6.4. A strong foreshock preceded the main shock by several hours.

Impact

The earthquake struck before dawn, collapsing mud-brick homes in mountain villages near Ziarat and in neighbouring Harnai and Pishin districts. Around 215 people were killed and some 300 injured; landslides buried roads and compounded damage. Tens of thousands were left homeless in cold mountain weather.

Response

Pakistani authorities and international agencies distributed tents, blankets, and food, and the army conducted rescue operations. Rebuilding of the region's vulnerable mud housing remained a long-term challenge.
