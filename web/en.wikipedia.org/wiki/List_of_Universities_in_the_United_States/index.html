List of universities in the United States

The US has several thousand degree-granting postsecondary institutions.

This article provides lists of universities in the United States, organised by state. American higher education includes public state university systems, private non-profit institutions, liberal arts colleges, and for-profit institutions, accredited regionally and nationally.

Notable institutions

The eight Ivy League universities are Brown, Columbia, Cornell, Dartmouth, Harvard (founded 1636, the oldest in the US), Princeton, the University of Pennsylvania, and Yale. Other highly ranked research universities include Stanford, MIT, Caltech, the University of Chicago, Johns Hopkins, and Duke. Large public systems include the University of California, State University of New York (SUNY), and University of Texas systems.

By state (examples)

  • California: Stanford, Caltech, UC Berkeley, UCLA, USC, UC San Diego
  • Massachusetts: Harvard, MIT, Boston University, Tufts, Northeastern
  • New York: Columbia, NYU, Cornell, Rochester, Syracuse
  • Texas: UT Austin, Texas A&M, Rice, Baylor
  • Illinois: University of Chicago, Northwestern, UIUC
  • Pennsylvania: UPenn, Carnegie Mellon, Penn State
