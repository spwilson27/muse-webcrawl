LG Household & Health Care

LG Household & Health Care Ltd. (Korean: LG생활건강) is a South Korean consumer goods company, a subsidiary of the LG conglomerate. Founded in 1947 as Lak-Hui Chemical Industrial Corporation — the origin of the Lucky name — it produced South Korea's first facial cream and grew into a major manufacturer of cosmetics, household products, and beverages.

Business divisions

The company's operations are organized into three main divisions. The Beauty division markets prestige and mass-market cosmetics, most famously The History of Whoo luxury skincare line, alongside brands such as O Hui, su:m37°, Belif, and VDL. The Household & Daily division sells laundry detergents, fabric care, oral care (Perioe toothpaste), hair care, and sanitary products. The Refreshment division, built around what was Coca-Cola Korea's bottling business, sells soft drinks and bottled water.

History

The modern company took its current shape in 2001, when LG Chem's consumer products operations were spun off as LG Household & Health Care. It expanded aggressively in the 2010s through acquisitions in cosmetics, including The Face Shop (2010), and grew rapidly on the back of Chinese demand for Korean beauty products, though its shares fell sharply when China's market slowed after the THAAD dispute of 2017.

Headquarters

LG Household & Health Care is headquartered in the LG Gwanghwamun Building in Jongno-gu, Seoul, and is listed on the Korea Exchange.
