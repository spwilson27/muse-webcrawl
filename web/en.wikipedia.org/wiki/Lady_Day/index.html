Lady Day


Billie Holiday, "Lady Day"

Lady Day is the nickname of the American jazz singer Billie Holiday (1915–1959). The name was bestowed on her by the tenor saxophonist Lester Young, her close friend and musical partner, who was himself nicknamed "Pres" (or "Prez") by Holiday.

Lady Day is also the title of Holiday's autobiography, published in 1956 (written with William Dufty) — more fully titled Lady Sings the Blues — and of the 1972 film adaptation starring Diana Ross, as well as of numerous posthumous album compilations of Holiday's recordings. This article concerns the singer; for full details of her life and career, see Billie Holiday.
