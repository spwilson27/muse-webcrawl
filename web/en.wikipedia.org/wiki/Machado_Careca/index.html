Machado Careca

Antônio Machado, better known as Machado Careca (born October 21, 1973), is a Brazilian former mixed martial artist who competed in the heavyweight division. He fought for organisations including UFC, PRIDE Fighting Championships and Pancrase during a career that began in the mid-1990s, in the era of few weight classes and minimal rules.

Career

Trained in Brazilian jiu-jitsu, Machado was among the many grapplers from Brazil who entered vale tudo and early MMA competition in the 1990s, a period when the sport was dominated by style-versus-style matchups.
