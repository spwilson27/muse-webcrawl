Port of Fujairah

Port of Fujairah
Location: Fujairah, United Arab Emirates
Opened: 1982
Type: Deep-water seaport

The Port of Fujairah is a deep-water seaport in the Emirate of Fujairah in the United Arab Emirates, on the Gulf of Oman just south of the Strait of Hormuz — the only Emirati port on the country's eastern coast, outside the Persian Gulf.

Commencing operations in 1982, the port has grown into one of the world's largest ship bunkering hubs and a major centre for oil storage and trading. Its anchorage hosts large numbers of tankers and bulk carriers. Since 2012 the Abu Dhabi Crude Oil Pipeline has delivered crude from Habshan to Fujairah's export terminal, allowing exports that bypass the Strait of Hormuz.

Facilities

The port operates general cargo and container terminals run under concession, bulk-handling berths, and several very large crude carrier (VLCC) jetties, surrounded by an extensive tank farm complex that is among the largest oil storage sites in the Middle East.
