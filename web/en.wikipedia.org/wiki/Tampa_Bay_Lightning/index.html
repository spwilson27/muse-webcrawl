Tampa Bay Lightning

The Tampa Bay Lightning (colloquially the Bolts) are a professional ice hockey team based in Tampa, Florida. They compete in the National Hockey League (NHL) as a member of the Atlantic Division of the Eastern Conference, and play their home games at Benchmark International Arena (formerly Amalie Arena).

History

The franchise was founded in 1992, with Phil Esposito central to winning the expansion bid. After early struggles, the Lightning won their first Stanley Cup in 2004, defeating the Calgary Flames in seven games under coach John Tortorella, with stars such as Martin St. Louis, Vincent Lecavalier, and Brad Richards.

Recent era

Under owner Jeff Vinik and captains including Steven Stamkos, Tampa Bay became a model franchise, winning back-to-back Stanley Cups in 2020 and 2021 and reaching the Final again in 2022. Key players of the era include Nikita Kucherov, Victor Hedman, Brayden Point, and goaltender Andrei Vasilevskiy, coached by Jon Cooper.

Identity

The team's colours are blue, black, and white, and their logo features a stylised lightning bolt within a circle.
