Mental health

Mental health encompasses emotional, psychological, and social well-being

Mental health encompasses emotional, psychological, and social well-being, influencing cognition, perception, and behavior. According to the World Health Organization (WHO), it is a "state of well-being in which the individual realizes his or her abilities, can cope with the normal stresses of life, can work productively and fruitfully, and can contribute to his or her community." It likewise determines how an individual handles stress, interpersonal relationships, and decision-making.

Mental disorders

Mental health conditions include disorders such as depression, anxiety disorders, schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, eating disorders, and addictive behaviors. They arise from a complex interplay of biological factors (genes, brain chemistry), life experiences (trauma, abuse), and family history. Mental disorders are common: WHO estimates that roughly one in eight people worldwide lives with a mental disorder. Many conditions can be treated with psychotherapy, medication, or a combination, although access to care remains limited in many countries and stigma continues to deter help-seeking.

History

Treatment of mental illness historically ranged from religious interpretation and confinement in asylums to moral treatment reforms in the nineteenth century and the development of psychiatry, psychoanalysis, and psychopharmacology in the twentieth century. From the mid-twentieth century, deinstitutionalization shifted care toward community-based services in many countries, with mixed results. The modern mental health field also emphasizes prevention, resilience, and the social determinants of health.

Promotion and protection

Mental health promotion involves strategies at individual, community, and societal levels: supportive relationships, healthy work environments, anti-bullying and suicide-prevention programs, and policies addressing poverty and discrimination. World Mental Health Day is observed on 10 October each year to raise awareness and mobilize support.
