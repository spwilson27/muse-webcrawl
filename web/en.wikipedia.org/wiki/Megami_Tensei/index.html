Megami Tensei is a Japanese media franchise created by Atlus, centered on a series of role-playing video games. The first game, Digital Devil Story: Megami Tensei (1987), was based on Aya Nishitani's novel of the same name. The franchise is known for its dark themes, post-apocalyptic settings, demon negotiation and fusion mechanics, and philosophical conflicts between law, chaos, and neutrality.

Common elements

Across most entries, players recruit demons — drawn from world mythologies and religions — by negotiating with them in battle, and combine demons through fusion to create stronger allies. Games frequently feature branching narratives in which the player's choices align them with factions representing order, freedom, or a middle path.

Sub-series

  • Shin Megami Tensei — the mainline series, beginning in 1992, including Shin Megami Tensei III: Nocturne and Shin Megami Tensei V.
  • Persona — a spin-off begun in 1996 that mixes dungeon crawling with school life and social simulation; Persona 5 (2016) brought the franchise worldwide mainstream success.
  • Devil Summoner — including the action spin-offs featuring Raidou Kuzunoha.
  • Devil Survivor — tactical role-playing entries.
  • Tokyo Mirage Sessions ♯FE — a crossover with Nintendo's Fire Emblem series.

Reception and influence

Although historically less prominent in the West than Final Fantasy or Dragon Quest, Megami Tensei is regarded as one of the foundational Japanese RPG franchises. Its monster-collecting mechanics preceded and influenced later phenomenons, and the Persona sub-series has grown into one of Atlus's flagship properties.
