Oldest people

This article lists the oldest people — individuals whose ages have been verified by an international body such as the Gerontology Research Group or Guinness World Records. Only ages supported by reliable documentation are included.

Oldest person ever

The oldest verified person in history is Jeanne Calment of France (1875–1997), who lived 122 years and 164 days. The oldest verified man is Jiroemon Kimura of Japan (1897–2013), who lived 116 years and 54 days.

Supercentenarians

People who reach the age of 110 are termed supercentenarians. Verified supercentenarians number in the thousands, with women greatly outnumbering men. Claims of greater ages — such as various alleged 130-year-old individuals — lack sufficient documentation and are not accepted by researchers.
