Midway Geyser Basin

The Midway Geyser Basin is a hydrothermal area in Yellowstone National Park, Wyoming, United States, lying between the Upper and Lower Geyser Basins along the Firehole River. Though small in area, it contains some of the park's largest and most famous hot springs.

Features

Its centrepiece is the Grand Prismatic Spring, the largest hot spring in the United States and the third largest in the world, about 110 m in diameter, known for the vivid rings of colour produced by pigmented microbial mats at different temperatures. Other major features include Excelsior Geyser Crater, which discharges huge volumes of near-boiling water into the Firehole River, and the Opal, Turquoise, and Indigo pools.

Access

A boardwalk loop crosses the basin, and the Fairy Falls trailhead nearby leads to an overlook giving the classic aerial-style view of Grand Prismatic Spring.
