Expo 2015 was a World Expo hosted by Milan, Italy, from 1 May to 31 October 2015. Milan had previously hosted the 1906 World's Fair. The theme was "Feeding the Planet, Energy for Life," encompassing technology, innovation, culture, and traditions concerning food and sustainability.

Participants and pavilions

Around 145 countries and several international organisations and companies participated. National pavilions were organised in thematic clusters (such as rice, cocoa, coffee, and spices), and Italy's pavilion, Palazzo Italia, stood beside the Tree of Life, a 37-metre wooden structure that became the Expo's symbol.

Events and legacy

The Expo drew more than 22 million visitors. It hosted the signing of the Milan Charter, a commitment on food rights and sustainability. The site northwest of Milan has since been redeveloped as MIND (Milano Innovation District), housing research, a hospital, and university facilities.

Controversies

Preparations were marked by construction delays, cost overruns, corruption investigations leading to arrests in 2014, and protests by the No Expo movement on opening day.
