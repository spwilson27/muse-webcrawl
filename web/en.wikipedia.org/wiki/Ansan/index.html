Ansan

Country: South Korea

Province: Gyeonggi Province

Districts: Sangnok-gu, Danwon-gu

Population: approx. 650,000–700,000

Ansan (Korean: 안산) is a city in Gyeonggi Province, South Korea, on the coast of the Yellow Sea southwest of Seoul. It is part of the Seoul Capital Area and is connected to the capital by Seoul Subway Line 4. The city is administered in two districts, Sangnok-gu and Danwon-gu.

Geography

Ansan occupies a coastal plain facing the Yellow Sea and includes a number of islands, among them Daebudo, a popular recreational destination linked to the mainland by bridge and causeway. Extensive tidal flats fringe the coast. The city was developed from the 1970s as a planned industrial city, hosting the Banwol and Sihwa industrial complexes.

Demographics

Ansan has one of the highest proportions of foreign residents of any South Korean city, reflecting the migrant workforce of its industrial estates. The Wongok-dong area of Danwon-gu is a well-known multicultural neighbourhood with businesses and restaurants serving communities from China, Vietnam, Indonesia, and many other countries.

Education and culture

The city is home to Hanyang University's ERICA Campus, Seoul Institute of the Arts, and Ansan University. Cultural institutions include the Gyeonggi Museum of Modern Art. In 2014 Ansan was deeply affected by the Sewol ferry disaster, in which hundreds of students from the city's Danwon High School died; a memorial altar in the city drew mourners from across the country.

Transport

Besides Subway Line 4 and the Suin–Bundang Line, Ansan is served by expressways linking it to Seoul and Incheon, and by the Seohae Line commuter railway, which opened in 2018.
