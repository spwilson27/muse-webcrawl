Syracuse Orange football

First season: 1889

Stadium: JMA Wireless Dome

Conference: ACC

National titles: 1 (1959)

Colors: Orange

The Syracuse Orange football program is the intercollegiate American football team representing Syracuse University in Syracuse, New York. The team competes in the NCAA Division I Football Bowl Subdivision as a member of the Atlantic Coast Conference (ACC), which it joined in 2013 after leaving the Big East.

History

Syracuse's greatest era came under coach Ben Schwartzwalder (1949–1973). Behind running back Ernie Davis — the first African American to win the Heisman Trophy, in 1961 — and before him Jim Brown, widely considered among the greatest players in the sport's history, the Orangemen won the 1959 national championship by defeating Texas in the Cotton Bowl. Syracuse has a notable tradition at the running back position, with the number 44 worn by Brown, Davis and Floyd Little becoming so iconic that the university retired it.

Home stadium

Since 1980 the team has played in the domed stadium on campus, opened as the Carrier Dome and now known as the JMA Wireless Dome, the largest domed stadium on a college campus in the United States.
