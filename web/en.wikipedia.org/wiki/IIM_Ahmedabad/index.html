The Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad (IIMA) is a public business school and Institute of National Importance located in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. Founded in 1961, it was the second Indian Institute of Management established, and is widely regarded as one of India's leading management schools.

History

IIMA was founded with the active support of the Government of India, the Government of Gujarat, Harvard Business School, and local industrialists including Vikram Sarabhai and Kasturbhai Lalbhai. It pioneered the case method of teaching in India.

Campus

The heritage campus, designed by American architect Louis Kahn with B. V. Doshi and Anant Raje, is celebrated for its exposed brick architecture, geometric openings and the Louis Kahn Plaza. A new campus, featuring a distinctive walkway, was added across the road in the 2000s.

Programmes

The flagship programme is the two-year Post Graduate Programme in Management (MBA). The institute also offers executive education, a doctoral programme, and specialist master's programmes.
