Family

In human society, a family is a group of people related either by kinship — birth or recognised descent — or by affinity, such as marriage or cohabitation.

Forms

Family forms vary across cultures and history, from nuclear families of parents and children to extended families spanning several generations, single-parent families and chosen families.

Functions

Families are a primary setting for raising children, economic cooperation, care of the elderly and socialisation into a culture's norms.
