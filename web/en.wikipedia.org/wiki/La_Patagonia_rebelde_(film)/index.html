La Patagonia rebelde (film)

La Patagonia rebelde
Directed by: Héctor Olivera
Written by: Osvaldo Bayer, Fernando Ayala, Héctor Olivera
Release date: 1974
Country: Argentina
Language: Spanish

La Patagonia rebelde (Rebellion in Patagonia) is a 1974 Argentine drama film directed by Héctor Olivera, based on the non-fiction book Los vengadores de la Patagonia trágica by Osvaldo Bayer. It dramatizes the rural workers' strikes of 1920–1922 in Santa Cruz Province, Patagonia, and their brutal suppression by the Argentine Army under Lieutenant Colonel Héctor Benigno Varela.

Plot

Sheep shearers and rural labourers, many of them immigrants influenced by anarchist ideas, strike for better pay and conditions. After an initial settlement collapses, the army intervenes; hundreds of strikers are executed, one of the darkest episodes in Argentine labour history.

Reception and censorship

The film won the Silver Bear at the 1974 Berlin International Film Festival. It was banned in Argentina after the 1976 coup and not shown again until the return of democracy in 1983. Screenwriter Osvaldo Bayer was forced into exile.
