Crystal Pite

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

A scene from one of Pite's large-scale ensemble works for Kidd Pivot.

Crystal Pite (born December 15, 1970) is a Canadian choreographer and dancer, recognized internationally as one of the most significant dance-makers of her generation. She is the founder and artistic director of the Vancouver-based company Kidd Pivot and an associate artist of Sadler's Wells in London. Her work is known for its inventive movement language, theatricality, and large-scale ensemble writing, and frequently explores themes of conflict, loss, and human connection.

Early life and career

Pite was born in Terrace, British Columbia, and raised in Victoria, where she began dancing at age four. She studied at the Banff Centre and the School of Toronto Dance Theatre. In 1988 she joined Ballet BC, and in 1990 choreographed her first professional work, Between the Bliss and Me. In 1995 she moved to Germany to join William Forsythe's Ballett Frankfurt, where she danced for five years and was strongly influenced by Forsythe's improvisational methods.

Kidd Pivot

Returning to Canada, Pite founded Kidd Pivot in 2001 in Vancouver. The company's works, including Uncollected Work (2003), Double Story (2004, with Richard Siegal), Lost Action: Trace (2006), and Dark Matters (2009), toured internationally and established her reputation. The Tempest Replica (2011) won acclaim for its use of white-clad "replica" dancers doubling the protagonists. Betroffenheit (2015), created with playwright and actor Jonathon Young of Electric Company Theatre, addressed trauma and addiction and won the Olivier Award for Best New Dance Production; the follow-up collaboration Revisor (2019) was likewise widely praised.

Commissions and honours

Pite has created works for many of the world's leading companies, including Nederlands Dans Theater, The Royal Ballet, Paris Opera Ballet, Ballett Frankfurt, the National Ballet of Canada, and Cedar Lake Contemporary Ballet. Emergence (2009), made for the National Ballet of Canada, won four Dora Mavor Moore Awards. Her work for The Royal Ballet, Flight Pattern (2017), a response to the refugee crisis, won the Olivier Award for Best New Dance Production and was later expanded into the full-evening Light of Passage (2022).

Pite became an associate choreographer of Nederlands Dans Theater in 2013 and an associate artist of Sadler's Wells in 2017. She is a Member of the Order of Canada.
