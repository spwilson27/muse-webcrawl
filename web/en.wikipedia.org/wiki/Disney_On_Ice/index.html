Disney On Ice, originally known as Walt Disney's World on Ice, is a series of touring ice shows produced by Feld Entertainment's Ice Follies and Holiday on Ice division under license from The Walt Disney Company. The shows feature figure skaters portraying Disney characters in routines derived from Disney films, with the skaters performing in character costumes and without their faces usually visible beneath full character outfits.

History

Walt Disney's World on Ice premiered in 1981, after Feld Entertainment (then Irvin & Kenneth Feld Productions) reached an agreement with Disney following its purchase of the Ice Follies and Holiday on Ice in 1979. The franchise was renamed Disney On Ice in 1998. At any given time, multiple distinct productions tour simultaneously in different regions of the world, making it one of the longest-running and most widely travelled live family entertainment franchises.

Productions

Each production is built around a selection of Disney stories and characters. Long-running and notable productions have included 100 Years of Magic (later Celebrate Memories), Princess Classics, Let's Celebrate!, Treasure Trove, Worlds of Fantasy, Rockin' Ever After, Frozen (adapted from the 2013 film), Frozen & Encanto, Find Your Hero, Magic in the Stars, and Into the Magic. Mickey Mouse, Minnie Mouse, Donald Duck, and Goofy typically serve as hosts linking the segments.
