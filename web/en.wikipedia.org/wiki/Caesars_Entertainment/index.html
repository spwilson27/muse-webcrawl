Caesars Entertainment, Inc. is an American hotel and casino entertainment company. It is the largest casino-entertainment company in the United States, operating more than 50 properties. The modern company was formed in July 2020 when Eldorado Resorts acquired the former Caesars Entertainment Corporation for about $17.3 billion and adopted the Caesars name.

History

The origins of the business lie with William F. Harrah, who opened a bingo parlour in Reno in 1937 and built the Harrah's casino chain. Separately, Caesars Palace opened on the Las Vegas Strip in 1966 and became one of the world's most famous casino resorts. Harrah's Entertainment acquired Caesars Entertainment in 2005, and the combined company was taken private in 2008 by Apollo Global Management and TPG Capital in a leveraged buyout. Its main operating unit filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in 2015, emerging in 2017. Eldorado Resorts, itself grown from a single Reno casino, agreed to buy Caesars in 2019, completing the merger in 2020.

Operations

Caesars operates casinos and resorts across the United States and Canada, including Caesars Palace, Harrah's, Horseshoe, Bally's, Flamingo and Planet Hollywood in Las Vegas, and runs the World Series of Poker. Its loyalty programme, Caesars Rewards, is one of the largest in the industry. The company also offers online casino gaming and sports betting through Caesars Sportsbook and its acquisition of William Hill's US business (the non-US assets were later sold).
