Chicago Outfit

The Chicago Outfit, also known as the Outfit, the Chicago Mob or the Chicago crime family, is an Italian-American organised crime syndicate based in Chicago, Illinois, dating to the 1910s.

History

The Outfit rose under Johnny Torrio and Al Capone during Prohibition, dominating bootlegging and other rackets, and remained a major Mafia family through the later 20th century under bosses including Sam Giancana, with influence extending to Las Vegas casinos.
