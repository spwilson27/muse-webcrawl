Franz Ferdinand

Franz Ferdinand may refer to several people and things, most prominently Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria, whose assassination in 1914 precipitated the First World War.

People

  • Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria (1863–1914), heir presumptive to the Austro-Hungarian throne, assassinated in Sarajevo on 28 June 1914.
  • Franz Ferdinand I of Austria (1793–1875), Emperor of Austria from 1835 to 1848.
  • Franz Ferdinand, Duke of Hohenberg (1927–1977), eldest son of Maximilian, Duke of Hohenberg, and grandson of the Archduke.

Other uses

  • Franz Ferdinand, a Scottish rock band formed in Glasgow in 2002, named after the Archduke; known for songs such as "Take Me Out".
  • Franz Ferdinand, a fictional character in various works.

Assassination

The Archduke and his wife, Sophie, Duchess of Hohenberg, were shot dead in Sarajevo by Gavrilo Princip, a Bosnian Serb member of Young Bosnia. The assassination set off a chain of diplomatic crises and mobilizations among the European great powers that culminated in the outbreak of World War I in July–August 1914.
