Permit (disambiguation)

Permit may refer to:

  • Permit (license), an official document granting authorization to do something, such as a building permit, work permit, or driving permit (learner's permit).
  • Permit (fish) (Trachinotus falcatus), a game fish of the western Atlantic Ocean, prized by saltwater fly and light-tackle anglers.
  • Permit, Indiana, an unincorporated community in the United States.
  • Exit permit, a document required by some governments for citizens or residents leaving the country.
  • Residence permit, a document allowing a foreign national to reside in a country.
