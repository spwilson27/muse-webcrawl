Minister of Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship

The Minister of Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship (French: ministre de l'Immigration, des Réfugiés et de la Citoyenneté) is a minister of the Crown in the Canadian Cabinet who administers Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC), the federal department responsible for immigration, refugee protection and citizenship in Canada.

Responsibilities

The minister oversees the immigration program under the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act, including the selection of economic and family-class immigrants, the refugee protection system shared with the Immigration and Refugee Board, citizenship grants and ceremonies, and the issuance of Canadian passports in cooperation with other departments. The portfolio also involves setting annual immigration levels, which in recent years have targeted roughly 400,000 or more new permanent residents annually, and negotiating immigration agreements with the provinces, including the Canada–Quebec Accord.

History

The modern portfolio dates from 1994, when the Department of Citizenship and Immigration was created; before that, immigration responsibilities had at various times rested with departments of manpower and immigration, employment and immigration, and the interior. The department adopted the name Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada in 2015, and the ministerial title was changed accordingly.

Notable ministers

Recent holders of the office have included Jason Kenney, Chris Alexander, John McCallum — who oversaw the resettlement of Syrian refugees in 2015–16 — Ahmed Hussen, Marco Mendicino, Sean Fraser and Marc Miller.
