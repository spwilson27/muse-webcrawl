Sienna Rose Diana Miller (born 28 December 1981) is an English-American actress. Born in New York City and raised in London, she began her career as a model before moving into acting. Miller first came to prominence with roles in the films Layer Cake and Alfie (both 2004), and became one of the emblematic figures of the mid-2000s "boho-chic" fashion moment. Her later career, increasingly centred on theatre and independent film, brought critical acclaim for performances in Factory Girl (2006), The Edge of Love (2008), Foxcatcher (2014), American Sniper (2014) and The Lost City of Z (2016).

Early life

Miller was born in New York to an American father, a former banker and art dealer, and an English mother who had been a model and worked with David Bailey. The family moved to London when she was a child. She was educated at Heathfield School in Ascot and studied briefly at the Lee Strasberg Theatre and Film Institute in New York.

Career

Breakthrough

After modelling for Coca-Cola, Italian Vogue and Prada, Miller won film roles in South Kensington (2001) and the short-lived television series Keen Eddie (2003). In 2004 she played the glamorous Tammy in Matthew Vaughn's Layer Cake and Nikki in the remake of Alfie, opposite Jude Law—her co-star and, off screen, her fiancé. She starred as Edie Sedgwick in Factory Girl (2006), in the fantasy film Stardust (2007), and as the Baroness in the blockbuster G.I. Joe: The Rise of Cobra (2009).

Theatre and later work

Miller's stage work includes As You Like It in the West End (2005), After Miss Julie (2009) and Flare Path (2011); she played Sally Bowles in the Broadway revival of Cabaret in 2015 and Maggie in Cat on a Hot Tin Roof at the Young Vic in 2017. Screen roles in Foxcatcher, American Sniper (as Taya Kyle), Burnt (2015), The Lost City of Z and the miniseries The Loudest Voice (2019) consolidated her reputation as a serious actress; she led the Netflix legal thriller Anatomy of a Scandal (2022).

Personal life and public image

Miller was a prominent target of tabloid intrusion; she gave evidence to the Leveson Inquiry into press ethics in 2011 and accepted substantial damages from News Group Newspapers over phone hacking. She has a daughter with the actor Tom Sturridge and a second daughter, born in 2023, with the actor Oli Green. Her 2000s wardrobe—floaty dresses, waistcoats and oversized belts—is credited with popularising the boho-chic look, and she co-founded the fashion label Twenty8Twelve with her sister Savannah in 2007.
