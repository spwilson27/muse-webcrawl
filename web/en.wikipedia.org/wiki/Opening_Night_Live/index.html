Opening Night Live (often abbreviated as ONL) is an annual livestreamed video game showcase held at the start of Gamescom, the video game trade fair in Cologne, Germany. The show was created and is hosted and produced by Canadian journalist and presenter Geoff Keighley, who also produces The Game Awards and the Summer Game Fest showcase.

First held in 2019, Opening Night Live opens the Gamescom trade fair with a two-hour programme of premiere trailers, gameplay demonstrations, announcements of new games, and appearances by developers. The event is staged before a live audience at the Koelnmesse exhibition centre and streamed worldwide.

Format

The showcase typically runs for around two hours and features dozens of trailers, alternating between world premieres of new titles and updates on previously announced games, often with release dates. A shorter pre-show, hosted by Kyle Bosman, precedes the main event and contains additional announcements. Keighley frequently conducts short on-stage interviews with developers between trailer blocks.

History

The first Opening Night Live took place on 19 August 2019. During the COVID-19 pandemic the 2020 and 2021 editions were held as digital events without a live audience, before returning to a physical stage in Cologne in 2022. The show has since become a fixture of the August games calendar, serving as the European counterpart to Keighley's Summer Game Fest in June and The Game Awards in December.
