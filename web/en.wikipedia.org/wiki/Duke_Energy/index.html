Duke Energy

Duke Energy Corporation is an American electric power and natural gas holding company headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina. It is one of the largest electric power holding companies in the United States, supplying energy to millions of customers across the Southeast and Midwest, with regulated utilities in North Carolina, South Carolina, Florida, Indiana, Ohio and Kentucky.

History

The company traces its origins to the Catawba Power Company founded in 1900 by Dr. Walker Gill Wylie, and to the Southern Power Company organized in 1905 with backing from James B. Duke, the tobacco and hydroelectric magnate for whom it was later renamed. Duke Power became Duke Energy in 1997. Major mergers followed: with Cinergy in 2006, with Progress Energy in 2012—which briefly made it the largest utility in the United States—and the acquisition of Piedmont Natural Gas in 2016.

Operations

Duke Energy owns a large generation fleet including the Oconee, McGuire and Catawba nuclear stations, coal and natural gas plants, and growing solar and storage capacity. The company committed to ambitious carbon-reduction targets as it retires coal units. It was also at the center of the 2014 Dan River coal ash spill, after which it agreed to extensive ash-basin excavation and cleanup in North Carolina.
