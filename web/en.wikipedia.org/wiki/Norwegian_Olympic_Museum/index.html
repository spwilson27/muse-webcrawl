Norwegian Olympic Museum

The Norwegian Olympic Museum (Norwegian: Norges Olympiske Museum) is a sports museum located in Lillehammer, Norway, at the Maihaugen open-air museum complex. It is the only museum in northern Europe covering the entire history of the Olympic Games, from the ancient Games to the present day.

History

The museum was officially opened by King Harald V and Queen Sonja on 27 November 1997. Its collections grew out of the 1994 Winter Olympics held in Lillehammer and the need to preserve the legacy of those Games, as well as of the 1952 Winter Olympics in Oslo.

Exhibitions

Exhibitions trace the revival of the Olympics under Pierre de Coubertin, present Norwegian Olympic heroes, and showcase medals, torches, equipment and film from both Summer and Winter Games. Interactive displays and the dramatic story of the 1994 Games form the core visitor experience. The museum was significantly renovated and reopened in 2016 in connection with the Winter Youth Olympic Games in Lillehammer.
