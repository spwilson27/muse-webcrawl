ViuTV

ViuTV is a Cantonese-language free-to-air television channel in Hong Kong, operated by HK Television Entertainment Company Limited (HKTVE), a subsidiary of PCCW. It launched on 6 April 2016 on digital channel 99, following the grant of a domestic free television programme service licence after the controversial non-renewal of Asia Television's licence. A sister channel, ViuTVsix, broadcasting mainly in English on channel 96, launched in March 2017.

History

PCCW's HKTVE was awarded a 12-year free television licence in principle in October 2013 and formally in April 2015. ViuTV began broadcasting in 2016 with a positioning aimed at younger viewers, emphasising reality shows and imported and local drama, in contrast to the long-dominant TVB. The channel made its name with "fact-tainment" reality formats such as King Maker (全民造星), the idol audition series launched in 2018 that created the boy bands Mirror and Error — acts credited with reviving Cantopop and the local entertainment industry in the early 2020s.

Programming

ViuTV's schedule mixes local dramas, variety and reality programmes, acquired Japanese and Korean series, news and current affairs from the Now News unit of the PCCW group, and children's and sports programming. Its drama Ossan's Love (大叔的愛), a 2021 remake of the Japanese series, became a major cultural phenomenon. The channel also broadcasts English Premier League football highlights and other sports content linked to the group's Now TV platform.

Reception

Although its average ratings remain below TVB's flagship Jade channel, ViuTV has won a loyal younger audience and numerous awards, and its advertising and entertainment ecosystem — including the management of Mirror under Music Nation — turned it into a significant force in Hong Kong popular culture.
