Robert James "Bobby" Ewing is a fictional character in the American television prime-time soap opera Dallas and its 2012 revival, portrayed by Patrick Duffy. The younger, more principled son of oil baron Jock Ewing, Bobby serves as the moral counterweight to his scheming elder brother J. R. Ewing, though his business and romantic life generate many of the series' storylines.

Character and storylines

The series begins with Bobby's marriage to Pamela Barnes, a member of the Ewings' rival family, which sets the show's central conflict in motion. Bobby works at Ewing Oil and repeatedly clashes with J.R. over the company's direction and ethics; unlike J.R., Bobby insists on running the business honestly. Their battles for control of Ewing Oil, including the famous contest willed by Jock, structure much of the series.

The dream season

At the end of the 1984–85 season, Bobby was killed on screen, run down by his deranged sister-in-law Katherine Wentworth, and Duffy left the show. Ratings fell, and in the celebrated (and controversial) resolution opening the 1986–87 season, Bobby appeared alive in the shower: the entire intervening season was revealed to have been Pamela's dream. The "dream season" remains one of the most famous plot devices in television history.

Revival

Duffy reprised the role in the TNT revival of Dallas (2012–2014), in which Bobby is the patriarch of Southfork, owner of the ranch, and married to Ann Ryland, while mentoring his adopted son Christopher against the next generation of Ewing feuding.
