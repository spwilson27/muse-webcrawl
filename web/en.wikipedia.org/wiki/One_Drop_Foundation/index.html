One Drop Foundation

The One Drop Foundation (French: Fondation One Drop) is a Canadian non-profit organisation dedicated to providing access to safe water, sanitation, and hygiene in vulnerable communities around the world. It was founded in 2007 in Montreal by Guy Laliberté, the founder of Cirque du Soleil.

Work

One Drop works with local partners in Latin America, Africa, and Asia, and with Indigenous communities in Canada, combining water-access infrastructure with social art and behaviour-change programmes to encourage lasting adoption of healthy practices. Its "Social Art for Behaviour Change" approach uses theatre, murals, and other participatory arts in its projects.

Fundraising

The foundation is known for high-profile fundraising events, including the "One Night for One Drop" benefit performances created with Cirque du Soleil in Las Vegas and the Big One for One Drop poker tournaments held in association with the World Series of Poker, which have raised millions of dollars for water projects.
