Clubs (rhythmic gymnastics)

Clubs are one of the five apparatus used in rhythmic gymnastics, alongside the hoop, ball, ribbon, and (formerly) the rope.

Description

The clubs are used in pairs of equal length, between 40 and 50 cm, made of wood or synthetic material. Each club consists of three parts: the body, the neck, and the head (knob). A gymnast uses the pair to perform mills, small circles, throws, asymmetric movements, taps, and rolls, requiring ambidexterity and hand–eye coordination. Club routines are scored under the FIG Code of Points.
