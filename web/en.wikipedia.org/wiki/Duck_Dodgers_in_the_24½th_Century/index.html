Duck Dodgers in the 24½th Century is a 1953 Warner Bros. Merrie Melodies cartoon short directed by Chuck Jones. The cartoon stars Daffy Duck as the space hero Duck Dodgers, Porky Pig as his Eager Young Space Cadet, and Marvin the Martian in only his second appearance.

Plot

Duck Dodgers is assigned to find the rare element Illudium Phosdex, "the shaving cream atom", located on the mysterious Planet X. Following a comic trail of alphabet planets, Dodgers claims Planet X for Earth just as Marvin the Martian claims it for Mars. Their escalating duel of cartoonish weapons ends with Dodgers blowing up the planet, leaving the two standing on a floating chunk of rock. Dodgers declares victory for Earth — what is left of it — as the Cadet delivers the closing line to the audience.

Legacy

The short is a parody of Buck Rogers and other space serials, with designs credited to Maurice Noble. It is frequently ranked among the greatest cartoons ever made, was voted one of the top animated films in the 1994 book The 50 Greatest Cartoons, and inspired the 2003 animated television series Duck Dodgers. George Lucas reportedly asked for it to be shown before screenings of Star Wars.
