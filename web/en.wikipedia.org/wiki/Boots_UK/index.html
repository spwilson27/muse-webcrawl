Boots UK Limited, formerly Boots the Chemists Limited, trading as Boots, is a British health and beauty retailer and pharmacy chain in the United Kingdom. It is one of the largest retailers in the UK by revenue, operating around 1,800–2,000 stores ranging from local community pharmacies to large health and beauty stores.

History

The company was founded in 1849 by John Boot, a farm worker's son, who opened a herbalist shop in Goose Gate, Nottingham. His son Jesse Boot transformed it into a national multiple retailer, pioneering self-service counters and own-brand products such as No7 cosmetics (launched 1935) and Soltan sun care. Boots merged with Alliance UniChem in 2006 to form Alliance Boots, which was acquired by Walgreens in stages (2012–2014) to create Walgreens Boots Alliance. The Boots brand remains a presence on most British high streets and supplies many NHS pharmacy services, including dispensing, vaccinations and the Pharmacy First scheme in England.

Products and services

Beyond dispensing, Boots sells own-brand ranges (No7, Botanics, Soap & Glory is stocked as a third-party brand), optical and hearing care services, and operates Boots Opticians as a sister business. Its Advantage Card loyalty scheme, launched in 1997, is among the UK's largest.
