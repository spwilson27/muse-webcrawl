Barbara Eden

Barbara Eden (born Barbara Jean Morehead; August 23, 1931) is an American actress and singer best known for her starring role as the genie Jeannie in the sitcom I Dream of Jeannie (1965–1970). Born in Tucson, Arizona, and raised in San Francisco, she began her career with television appearances in the 1950s and a contract with 20th Century Fox, appearing in films including Flaming Star (1960) with Elvis Presley and Voyage to the Bottom of the Sea (1961).

I Dream of Jeannie

Producer Sidney Sheldon cast Eden as the 2,000-year-old genie rescued by astronaut Tony Nelson (Larry Hagman) in the NBC series, which ran five seasons. Her pink chiffon harem costume became iconic, and network censors famously forbade showing her navel. Eden reprised the role in two television reunion films and continued to work in television movies, the sitcom Harper Valley PTA (adapted from the song and 1978 film in which she also starred), and stage productions including a national tour of Gentlemen Prefer Blondes.

Later life

Eden published a memoir, Jeannie Out of the Bottle, in 2011, and received a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame in 1988. She has continued to make public appearances celebrating the enduring popularity of I Dream of Jeannie in syndication.
