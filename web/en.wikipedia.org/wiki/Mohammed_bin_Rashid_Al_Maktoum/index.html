Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum

Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum (born 15 July 1949) is the ruler of Dubai and the vice president, prime minister and minister of defence of the United Arab Emirates. He succeeded his brother Maktoum bin Rashid Al Maktoum as ruler of Dubai on 4 January 2006, following the latter's death.

Early life

Sheikh Mohammed was born in Al Shindagha, Dubai, the third son of Sheikh Rashid bin Saeed Al Maktoum. He was educated privately in Dubai and later at the Mons Officer Cadet School at Aldershot in England. On his return he became head of Dubai's police and public security, and in 1971, at the formation of the UAE, he became the federation's first minister of defence.

Ruler of Dubai

Long the driving force behind Dubai's development even before formally succeeding, Mohammed oversaw the creation of Emirates airline, the Jebel Ali port expansion, the Palm Islands, the Burj Khalifa, and the growth of Dubai into a global hub for trade, tourism and finance. He founded the Godolphin thoroughbred horse-racing stable and the Dubai World Cup. As prime minister of the UAE from 2006, he led federal government reform programmes. In 2021, a British court found on the balance of probabilities that he had ordered the abduction of two of his daughters, a judgement he disputes.

Personal life

A keen horseman and poet, Mohammed has authored books including My Vision (2006), outlining his development philosophy. His senior wife was Sheikha Hind bint Maktoum bin Juma Al Maktoum; his junior wife Princess Haya bint Hussein of Jordan left Dubai in 2019, leading to a high-profile custody case in London.
