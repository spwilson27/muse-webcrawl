India Gate

The India Gate is a war memorial in New Delhi, India, dedicated to 70,000 soldiers of the British Indian Army who died in the First World War and the Third Anglo-Afghan War.

Design

Designed by Sir Edwin Lutyens in the triumphal arch tradition, the 42-metre sandstone arch bears the names of more than 13,500 servicemen. A shallow bowl on top was intended for a burning flame.

Amar Jawan Jyoti and later history

After independence, the Amar Jawan Jyoti eternal flame was added in 1972 beneath the arch for soldiers killed in the Indo-Pakistani War of 1971; in 2022 it was merged with the flame at the nearby National War Memorial. The surrounding Kartavya Path (formerly Rajpath) hosts the Republic Day parade.
