List of famous trees

Individual trees have been famous for their great age, enormous size, historical associations, or religious significance. This list includes notable individual trees from around the world, both living and dead.

Famous dead or destroyed trees

TreeNotes
PrometheusBristlecone pine in Nevada cut down in 1964 for research; its ~4,900 rings revealed it to be one of the oldest trees ever recorded
Wawona TreeGiant sequoia tunnel tree in Yosemite; fell in 1969 under snow load
Pioneer Cabin TreeGiant sequoia tunnel tree in Calaveras Big Trees State Park; toppled by a storm in January 2017
Sycamore Gap tree ("Robin Hood Tree")Sycamore beside Hadrian's Wall, England; felled by vandals in September 2023
Anne Frank treeHorse chestnut in Amsterdam visible from the Secret Annexe; collapsed in a storm in 2010
Newton's apple treeAt Woolsthorpe Manor; the original fell in a storm c. 1820, but grafted descendants grow worldwide; said to have inspired the theory of gravity
Royal OakThe oak at Boscobel House in which Charles II hid after the Battle of Worcester (1651); the present tree is a descendant
Liberty TreeElm in Boston, meeting place of the Sons of Liberty; cut down by Loyalists in 1775
Gernika oak (Gernikako Arbola)Symbol of Basque liberties in Spain; successive trees planted on the same site since the Middle Ages
Senator treeBald cypress in Florida, ~3,500 years old; destroyed by fire in 2012
