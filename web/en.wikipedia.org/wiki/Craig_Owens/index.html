Craig Owens

Craig Owens
Born: Craigery Owens, 26 August 1984
Origin: Davison, Michigan, United States
Occupation: Singer, songwriter
Known for: Chiodos, Destroy Rebuild Until God Shows

Craig Owens (born Craigery Owens, 26 August 1984) is an American musician best known as the lead vocalist of the post-hardcore band Chiodos and of the supergroup Destroy Rebuild Until God Shows (D.R.U.G.S.). Owens is recognised for his wide vocal range, shifting between high melodic singing and screaming, and became a prominent figure in the 2000s American post-hardcore and emo scene.

Career

Chiodos

Owens joined the Michigan band that became Chiodos around 2001. The group rose to prominence with the albums All's Well That Ends Well (2005) and Bone Palace Ballet (2007), the latter debuting high on the Billboard 200. Owens parted ways with Chiodos in 2009, returned in 2012, and fronted the band for the album Devil (2014) and subsequent touring before the group again became inactive. A further Chiodos reunion with Owens was announced for festival performances in the mid-2020s.

Other projects

During his time away from Chiodos, Owens released the solo EP Με την αγάπη (With Love) (2009) and formed Destroy Rebuild Until God Shows, whose self-titled debut appeared in 2011; the project returned with the album Destroy Rebuild in 2022. He also fronted the short-lived group Isles & Glaciers, worked as a producer and featured vocalist for other artists, and performed under the electronic-leaning alias badXchannels.

Personal life

Owens has spoken publicly about struggles with addiction and mental health during his career, experiences that informed his songwriting and his later advocacy for openness about those issues within the music scene.
