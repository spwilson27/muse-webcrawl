July Cup

The July Cup is a Group 1 flat horse race in Great Britain open to horses aged three years or older. It is run on the July Course at Newmarket over a distance of 6 furlongs (1,207 metres), and it is scheduled to take place each year in July.

History

The event was established in 1876 and is Britain's most prestigious sprint race of the summer, forming part of Newmarket's July Festival. It regularly attracts leading sprinters from Britain, Ireland, France and further afield, and winners include many champion sprinters. The race is the middle leg of the British leg of the Global Sprint Challenge in some years.
