Stephen Colbert

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Stephen Tyrone Colbert (born May 13, 1964) is an American comedian, writer, producer, political commentator, actor, and television host. He is best known for hosting the satirical Comedy Central program The Colbert Report (2005–2014) and the CBS talk program The Late Show with Stephen Colbert (2015–2026).

Early life and education

Colbert was born in Washington, D.C., the youngest of eleven children, and raised in Charleston, South Carolina. In 1974, his father and two of his brothers were killed in the crash of Eastern Air Lines Flight 212. He attended Northwestern University, where he studied performance and joined the improvisational troupe that led him to Chicago's Second City, where he met Amy Sedaris and Paul Dinello, collaborators on the sketch show Exit 57 and the comedy Strangers with Candy.

The Daily Show and The Colbert Report

Colbert joined The Daily Show in 1997 and became one of its most popular correspondents under host Jon Stewart, developing a pompous, ill-informed conservative pundit persona. In 2005 he spun the character off into The Colbert Report, a parody of personality-driven cable news commentary shows. The program coined the word "truthiness," named Merriam-Webster's Word of the Year in 2006. Colbert's in-character performance at the 2006 White House Correspondents' Dinner, delivered directly before President George W. Bush, became a landmark of American political satire. The show won multiple Emmy and Peabody Awards.

The Late Show

Colbert succeeded David Letterman as host of CBS's Late Show franchise in September 2015, appearing as himself rather than in character. After a slow start in the ratings, the program sharpened its political focus and rose to become the most-watched American late-night talk show for several consecutive seasons. In July 2025, CBS announced the show would end in May 2026, with the network retiring the Late Show franchise entirely.

Other work

Colbert co-authored the best-selling books America (The Book) and I Am America (And So Can You!), co-created the animated series Our Cartoon President, and performed the voice of Phil Ken Sebben and Reducto on Harvey Birdman, Attorney at Law. A devoted fan of J. R. R. Tolkien, he has made a cameo in The Hobbit film trilogy. He is a practicing Roman Catholic and lives in New Jersey with his wife, Evelyn McGee-Colbert; they have three children.
