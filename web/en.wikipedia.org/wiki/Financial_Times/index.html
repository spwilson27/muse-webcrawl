The Financial Times (FT) is a British daily newspaper founded in 1888, printed on its distinctive salmon-pink paper since 1893, covering global business, finance and politics. Owned by Japan's Nikkei since 2015, it is one of the world's most successful quality-news subscription businesses.

History

Founded as the London Financial Guide and renamed within weeks, the FT merged with its rival the Financial News in 1945. It launched an international print network and, in the 2010s, pivoted decisively to paid digital subscriptions, passing a million paying readers.

Content

Signature elements include the Lex column (since 1945), FT Alphaville's markets blogging, prominent economics and politics columnists, and the FT Weekend. Its journalism has won wide recognition, including for investigations into Wirecard.
