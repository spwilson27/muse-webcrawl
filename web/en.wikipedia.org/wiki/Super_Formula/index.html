Super Formula

Super Formula, formerly known as Formula Nippon, is a Japanese open-wheel single-seater motor racing series and the country's premier formula racing championship. Races are held at circuits such as Suzuka, Fuji Speedway, and Motegi.

History

Top-level formula racing in Japan dates to the All-Japan Formula 2000 championship of the 1970s, which evolved through Formula Two and Formula 3000 specifications before becoming Formula Nippon in 1996. The series was rebranded as Super Formula in 2013.

Cars

Recent seasons have used a Dallara-built chassis powered by turbocharged two-litre engines supplied by Toyota and Honda. The cars are among the fastest in the world outside Formula One, and the championship has served as a stepping stone for drivers including future F1 competitors.
