Salem's Lot (disambiguation)

'Salem's Lot or Salem's Lot may refer to:

  • Salem's Lot (1979 miniseries), the television miniseries adaptation directed by Tobe Hooper
  • Salem's Lot (2004 miniseries), a later television adaptation starring Rob Lowe
  • Salem's Lot (2024 film), the feature film adaptation released on Max
  • Jerusalem's Lot, the fictional town itself, also the setting of King's 1978 short story "Jerusalem's Lot"
