MBDA is a European multinational developer and manufacturer of missiles. It was formed in December 2001 by the merger of the missile businesses of EADS (now Airbus), BAE Systems and Finmeccanica (now Leonardo): EADS Euromissile, Matra BAe Dynamics and Alenia Marconi Systems. It is the only European group capable of designing and producing missiles across the full spectrum of operational requirements.

Organisation

The company operates through national subsidiaries in France, the United Kingdom, Italy, Germany and Spain, with a subsidiary in the United States. Its shareholding reflects its parentage: Airbus and BAE Systems each hold 37.5 per cent and Leonardo 25 per cent.

Products

MBDA's portfolio includes the Meteor beyond-visual-range air-to-air missile, the Storm Shadow / SCALP cruise missile, the Aster family of naval air-defence missiles, the Exocet anti-ship missile family, the Mistral short-range air-defence system, the CAMM family of air-defence missiles, the Brimstone precision strike weapon, and the ASRAAM short-range air-to-air missile. It is also part of the consortium structure associated with the Franco-Italian SAMP/T ground-based air defence system.

Programmes

The company participates in major European collaborative programmes, including the Future Cruise/Anti-Ship Weapon (FC/ASW) intended to succeed Storm Shadow and Exocet for France and the United Kingdom, and studies for future air combat systems. Its order book has grown strongly with increased European defence spending.
