Figoni et Falaschi

IndustryCoachbuilding
Founded1935 (partnership)
FoundersGiuseppe Figoni, Ovidio Falaschi
HeadquartersBoulogne-Billancourt, France

Figoni et Falaschi was a French coachbuilding firm renowned for the extravagant, aerodynamic bodies it created on luxury automobile chassis in the 1930s. The partnership between the Italian-born designer Giuseppe Figoni and the businessman Ovidio Falaschi produced some of the most celebrated cars of the Art Deco era.

Designs

Working chiefly on Delahaye, Delage, Talbot-Lago, and Bugatti chassis, Figoni developed a flamboyant style of flowing, teardrop-shaped coachwork — the famous goutte d'eau (teardrop) coupés — with enclosed fenders, sweeping curves, and chrome accents. The Talbot-Lago T150C SS "Teardrop" coupé of 1937–38 is widely regarded as the firm's masterpiece and one of the most beautiful automobiles ever built.

Later years

The luxury coachbuilding market collapsed after the Second World War, and production car bodies became standardized. Falaschi retired in the late 1940s; Figoni continued on a smaller scale into the 1950s. Surviving Figoni et Falaschi cars are now among the most valuable collector automobiles in the world, regularly winning major concours d'elegance awards.
