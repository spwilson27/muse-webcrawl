Kolkata Knight Riders

League: Indian Premier League

Founded: 2008

Titles: 2012, 2014, 2024

The Kolkata Knight Riders (KKR) are a professional Twenty20 franchise cricket team representing the city of Kolkata in the Indian Premier League (IPL). The franchise is owned by Bollywood actor Shah Rukh Khan, actress Juhi Chawla, and her spouse Jay Mehta.

History

KKR were one of the eight founding IPL teams in 2008, with Sourav Ganguly as icon player and first captain. Under Gautam Gambhir, the Knight Riders won the IPL in 2012 and 2014, and won a third championship in 2024 under Shreyas Iyer. The team's home ground is Eden Gardens, one of the largest cricket stadiums in the world.

Brand

The team's colours are purple and gold, and its motto is Korbo, Lorbo, Jeetbo Re ("we will perform, fight, and win"). The Knight Riders franchise group also operates sister teams in other T20 leagues.
