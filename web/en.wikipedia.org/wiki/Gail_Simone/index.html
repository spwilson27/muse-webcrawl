Gail Simone

Gail Simone (born July 29, 1974) is an American writer best known for her work in comics on DC Comics titles including Birds of Prey, Secret Six, Wonder Woman, and Batgirl. She also coined the term "women in refrigerators" as a critic of the treatment of female characters in comics.

Simone is one of the most prominent writers of superhero comics.

Career

Simone first came to attention in 1999 with the website "Women in Refrigerators", listing female comic characters who had been killed or injured as plot devices in stories centred on male heroes — named for a Green Lantern story in which Kyle Rayner's girlfriend was murdered and stuffed into a refrigerator. Her humour column "You'll All Be Sorry!" followed, leading to professional work at Bongo Comics on The Simpsons comics.

At DC, her runs on Birds of Prey and the villain-centred Secret Six were critically acclaimed. From 2008 she wrote Wonder Woman, becoming the character's longest-serving female writer. In 2011 she launched the New 52 Batgirl, restoring Barbara Gordon to the role; her temporary removal from the title in 2012, and swift reinstatement after fan outcry, made industry news. She has also written Red Sonja for Dynamite, Tomb Raider, and creator-owned work, and written for animation and video games.
