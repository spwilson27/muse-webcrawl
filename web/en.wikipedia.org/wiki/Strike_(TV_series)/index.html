Strike (also known as C.B. Strike in the United States) is a British crime drama television series based on the Cormoran Strike detective novels by J. K. Rowling, written under the pseudonym Robert Galbraith. Tom Burke stars as the private detective Cormoran Strike, a war veteran who lost a leg in Afghanistan, and Holliday Grainger as his assistant and later partner Robin Ellacott.

Series overview

Each series adapts one novel:

  • The Cuckoo's Calling (2017) — the death of supermodel Lula Landry.
  • The Silkworm (2017) — the disappearance of novelist Owen Quine.
  • Career of Evil (2018) — a severed leg is delivered to Strike's office.
  • Lethal White (2020) — a troubled young man's account of a childhood crime.
  • Troubled Blood (2022) — a decades-old cold case of a missing doctor.
  • The Ink Black Heart (2024) — the murder of an online cartoon creator.

Production

The series is produced by Brontë Film and Television, Rowling's production company, with Rowling as an executive producer. It premiered on BBC One on 27 August 2017 and airs in North America on HBO (originally Cinemax). Filming takes place largely in London, around Denmark Street, where Strike's office is located in the novels.

Reception

The adaptations have been praised for the chemistry between Burke and Grainger and for faithful, atmospheric renderings of the books, and have performed strongly in ratings for BBC One.
