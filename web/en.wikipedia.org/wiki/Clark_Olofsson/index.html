Clark Olofsson

Clark Olofsson, one of Sweden's most notorious criminals, pictured during the 1970s.

Clark Oderth Olofsson (born 1 February 1947) is a Swedish criminal. He has spent more than half of his life in correctional institutions in Sweden and is regarded as one of the country's most notorious criminals. He has been convicted of attempted murder, assault, robbery, and dealing narcotics, among other offences.

Olofsson is chiefly remembered for his involvement in the Norrmalmstorg robbery of August 1973 in Stockholm, in which his accomplice Jan-Erik Olsson held bank employees hostage for six days and demanded that Olofsson be brought from prison to the bank. The hostages' apparent sympathy for their captors during the siege gave rise to the term Stockholm syndrome.

Early life

Olofsson was born in Trollhättan, Sweden, into a family marked by alcohol abuse; he and his sisters were placed in foster care. By his teens he had accumulated convictions for burglary and car theft, and he escaped from institutions repeatedly. In 1966 he was among the burglars who broke into the summer residence of Prime Minister Tage Erlander, an incident that drew national attention.

Norrmalmstorg robbery

On 23 August 1973, convicted robber Jan-Erik Olsson attempted to rob the Kreditbanken branch at Norrmalmstorg in Stockholm and took four employees hostage. Among his demands was that Olofsson, then serving a sentence, be freed and brought to the bank. The government agreed, and Olofsson spent the six-day siege inside the bank's vault with Olsson and the hostages before police ended the standoff with tear gas on 28 August. Olofsson was returned to prison; he maintained that he had acted to protect the hostages, and a court later acquitted him of charges arising from the robbery itself, though he still had to serve the remainder of his earlier sentence.

Later life

Olofsson continued to be convicted of crimes in the following decades, including armed robbery and drug offences in Denmark and Sweden; in 2009 he was sentenced in Denmark for smuggling amphetamines. He repeatedly featured in Swedish media, cultivated a celebrity outlaw image, and his life was dramatised in the 2022 Netflix series Clark, starring Bill Skarsgård.
