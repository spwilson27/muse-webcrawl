Vessel traffic service

A vessel traffic service (VTS) is a marine traffic monitoring system established by harbour or port authorities, similar to air traffic control for aircraft.

Functions

VTS provides information services, traffic organisation, and navigational assistance using radar, AIS, VHF radio, and CCTV to improve safety and efficiency in busy waterways.

Regulation

The International Maritime Organization's SOLAS Convention requires governments to arrange VTS where traffic volume or risk justifies it.
