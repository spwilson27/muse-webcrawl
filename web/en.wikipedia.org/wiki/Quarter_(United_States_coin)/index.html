Quarter (United States coin)

The quarter, short for quarter dollar, is a United States coin worth 25 cents, one quarter of a dollar. It has been produced since 1796 and is one of the most commonly circulating US coins.

Design

The obverse has featured George Washington since 1932, when his portrait was introduced for the bicentennial of his birth. From 1999 the reverse carried the 50 State Quarters series, followed by the America the Beautiful quarters (2010–2021) and the American Women quarters programme. The standard coin is a clad composition of copper and nickel, 24.26 mm in diameter.
