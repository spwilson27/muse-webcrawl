Civic Center, San Francisco

The Civic Center in San Francisco, California, is an area located a few blocks north of the intersection of Market Street and Van Ness Avenue that contains many of the city's largest government and cultural institutions. It has two large plazas (Civic Center Plaza and United Nations Plaza) and a number of buildings in classical architectural style.

Buildings

Landmarks include San Francisco City Hall with its great dome, the War Memorial Opera House, Davies Symphony Hall, the Asian Art Museum, the San Francisco Public Library's Main Library, and the Bill Graham Civic Auditorium. The complex is designated a National Historic Landmark District.

History

The present Civic Center was built after the 1906 earthquake and fire, following plans associated with the City Beautiful movement and architect John Galen Howard. United Nations Plaza commemorates the signing of the UN Charter in the War Memorial building in 1945.
