List of volcanoes in Mexico

Mexico's volcanoes belong mainly to the Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt, an arc stretching some 1,000 km across central Mexico from the Pacific coast to the Gulf of Mexico, formed by the subduction of the Cocos and Rivera plates. The country has more than 40 recognized volcanoes, including some of the world's most famous peaks.

Monitoring

Volcano monitoring in Mexico is led by the National Center for Disaster Prevention (CENAPRED), particularly for Popocatépetl, which uses a Volcanic Alert Level "traffic light" system.
