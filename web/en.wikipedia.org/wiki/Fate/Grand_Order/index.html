Fate/Grand Order (Japanese: フェイト/グランドオーダー) is a free-to-play Japanese mobile role-playing game developed by Delightworks (whose games division became Lasengle in 2022) and published by Aniplex, a subsidiary of Sony Music Entertainment Japan. Part of Type-Moon's Fate franchise, it was released in Japan in 2015 and in North America in 2017, and became one of the highest-grossing mobile games in the world, with lifetime revenue estimated in the billions of US dollars.

Gameplay and story

The player takes the role of a "Master" of the Chaldea Security Organization, which — after an incident leaves humanity facing extinction — travels through time to "Singularities" to restore history (the eponymous Grand Orders), later confronting the "Lostbelts", alternate histories that must be pruned. Masters summon and command Servants, heroic spirits drawn from history, myth and legend — figures such as King Arthur (depicted as a woman, Artoria Pendragon), Gilgamesh, Jeanne d'Arc and Leonardo da Vinci — in turn-based card-based battles. New Servants are obtained through a gacha system using in-game currency (Saint Quartz).

Reception and adaptations

The game was praised for its extensive, novel-length writing — lead writer Kinoko Nasu's scenarios run to millions of characters — and criticised for low gacha rates. It has spawned anime adaptations (including the First Order special, and series covering the Camelot, Babylonia and Solomon story arcs), films, stage plays, manga, an arcade game and extensive merchandising, and is credited with reviving and vastly expanding the Fate franchise's popularity in the 2010s.
