Fate/stay night is a Japanese visual novel developed by Type-Moon and released for Windows on 30 January 2004. Written by Kinoko Nasu with art by Takashi Takeuchi, it became Type-Moon's commercial breakthrough and the foundation of the vast Fate franchise. An all-ages version, Fate/stay night Réalta Nua, was released for PlayStation 2 in 2007 and later ported to other platforms.

Story

The story follows Shirou Emiya, a teenage apprentice magus in Fuyuki City, who is drawn into the Fifth Holy Grail War: a secret battle royale in which seven Masters summon seven Servants — heroic spirits from myth and history, divided into classes such as Saber, Archer, and Lancer — to fight for the Holy Grail, a wish-granting artefact. Shirou summons Saber, the knight Artoria Pendragon. The narrative branches into three routes: Fate, Unlimited Blade Works, and Heaven's Feel, each re-examining Shirou's ideal of becoming a "hero of justice" through a different heroine and a darker unfolding of the war.

Adaptations and legacy

The visual novel was adapted into a 2006 anime television series by Studio Deen, a 2010 film of Unlimited Blade Works, and — most acclaimed — Ufotable's television adaptations of Unlimited Blade Works (2014–15) and the Heaven's Feel film trilogy (2017–2020). The prequel light novel and anime Fate/Zero, the mobile game Fate/Grand Order, and numerous spin-offs in every medium have made Fate one of the most commercially successful media franchises in Japan.
