Donkey (Shrek)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Donkey is a fictional character in the Shrek franchise of animated films by DreamWorks Animation, voiced by Eddie Murphy. A wisecracking, hyper-talkative talking donkey, he is the loyal sidekick and best friend of the ogre Shrek, whom he accompanies — usually uninvited — on his adventures.

Role in the films

In Shrek (2001), Donkey escapes being sold by his owner and attaches himself to Shrek, helping him rescue Princess Fiona; along the way he falls in love with the fire-breathing Dragon guarding her castle. In later films he and Dragon marry and produce a litter of hybrid babies, the "dronkeys". His cheerful persistence, pop-song interludes, and improvised comic banter — including his enthusiasm for parfaits and his promise "I'm making waffles!" — made him one of the series' most popular characters.

Reception

Murphy's performance was widely praised and Donkey became a breakout character of the franchise, appearing in all the sequels, television specials, a stage musical adaptation (where the role was originated by Daniel Breaker), and extensive merchandise.
