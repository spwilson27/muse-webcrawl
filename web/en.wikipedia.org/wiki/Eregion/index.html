Eregion

Eregion (Sindarin for "Land of Holly"), or Hollin in Westron, is a fictional region in J. R. R. Tolkien's Middle-earth, lying west of the Misty Mountains between the rivers Glanduin and Sirannon. It was the realm of the Elven-smiths of the Noldor in the Second Age, who founded the city of Ost-in-Edhil near the western gates of the Dwarf-kingdom of Khazad-dûm.

In Tolkien's legendarium

Eregion was settled around S.A. 750 by Noldorin Elves led by Galadriel and Celeborn, and later ruled by Celebrimbor, grandson of Fëanor and the greatest of Elven craftsmen. The Gwaith-i-Mírdain, the brotherhood of jewelsmiths, traded with the Dwarves of Khazad-dûm and produced works of surpassing skill. Deceived by Sauron in the guise of Annatar, "Lord of Gifts", the smiths forged the Rings of Power; Celebrimbor alone made the Three Rings of the Elves, untouched by Sauron, while Sauron forged the One Ring in Mordor. When Sauron's deception was revealed, he invaded Eriador in S.A. 1695; Eregion was devastated, Ost-in-Edhil destroyed, and Celebrimbor captured, tortured and killed. Survivors, led by Elrond, founded the refuge of Rivendell.

In The Lord of the Rings

By the time of the War of the Ring, Eregion is long deserted. The Fellowship passes through Hollin on its way south from Rivendell, and notices that the holly trees for which the land was named still mark its old borders; from its hills they spot the crebain — spying crows — that force them to attempt the mountain pass of Caradhras.
