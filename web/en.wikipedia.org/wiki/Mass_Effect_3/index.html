Mass Effect 3

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia


Cover art for Mass Effect 3

Mass Effect 3 is a 2012 action role-playing video game developed by BioWare and published by Electronic Arts. The third major game in the Mass Effect series and the final installment of the original trilogy, it was released for Microsoft Windows, PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360 in March 2012, and later for Wii U. The game is set in the 22nd century, as the galaxy-spanning war against the Reapers, a race of ancient machine intelligences, reaches Earth and the wider galaxy.

Gameplay

Players control Commander Shepard, whose appearance, abilities and decisions can be imported from saved games of the earlier titles, shaping the story accordingly. Gameplay combines third-person shooter combat with role-playing elements: dialogue choices, squad commands, weapon and armour customisation, and exploration aboard the starship Normandy. A player's choices across the trilogy affect alliances and the "war assets" that determine the outcome of the final battle. The game also introduced a cooperative multiplayer mode, Galaxy at War.

Reception

Mass Effect 3 received critical acclaim for its gameplay, story and the way choices from previous games carried over, and it sold over 800,000 copies in its first month. However, its original ending was widely criticised by players as failing to reflect their choices; in response, BioWare released the free "Extended Cut" downloadable content in June 2012, expanding the ending sequences. The controversy became a widely discussed episode in consumer-developer relations in gaming.
