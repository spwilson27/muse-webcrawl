NCAA Division III Football Championship

The NCAA Division III Football Championship determines the national champion of NCAA Division III American college football. The championship game, known as the Stagg Bowl after coach Amos Alonzo Stagg, has been held since 1973.

Division III schools do not award athletic scholarships, and the tournament is a single-elimination playoff of 32 teams selected from conference champions and at-large bids. Mount Union and Wisconsin–Whitewater have been among the most successful programs.

History

The Stagg Bowl was long hosted in Salem, Virginia, and has since rotated among venues including Canton, Ohio, and Annapolis, Maryland.
