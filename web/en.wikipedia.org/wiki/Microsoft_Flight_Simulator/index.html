Microsoft Flight Simulator

Microsoft Flight Simulator is a series of amateur flight simulator programs for Microsoft Windows operating systems, and earlier for MS-DOS and the Classic Mac OS. It was an early product in Microsoft's application portfolio and differed significantly from Microsoft's other software, which was largely business-oriented.

History

The series began with Bruce Artwick's FS1 Flight Simulator, released by his company Sublogic in 1979–1980 for the Apple II. Microsoft licensed the simulator and released its own version in 1982. Successive editions added better scenery, weather, air traffic control, and aircraft. The 2006 release, Flight Simulator X, was the last for several years after Microsoft closed ACES Game Studio in 2009.

In 2020 Microsoft released a new Microsoft Flight Simulator, developed by Asobo Studio, which streamed detailed world scenery generated from Bing Maps data and satellite imagery. A further edition, Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024, followed. The franchise is among the longest-running and best-known home flight simulation series.

Gameplay

Players pilot a wide range of general aviation and commercial aircraft from detailed cockpits, using realistic flight models. Editions include lessons, missions, challenges, and free flight anywhere in the world, with dynamic weather and day–night cycles. A large third-party add-on community produces aircraft, scenery, and utilities.
