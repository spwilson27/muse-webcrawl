La liberazione di Ruggiero

La liberazione di Ruggiero
Composer: Francesca Caccini
Librettist: Ferdinando Saracinelli
Premiere: 3 February 1625, Florence

La liberazione di Ruggiero dall'isola d'Alcina is a comic opera in four scenes by Francesca Caccini to a libretto by Ferdinando Saracinelli, drawn from Ariosto's Orlando furioso. First performed in Florence in 1625 for the visit of Prince Władysław of Poland, it is generally regarded as the earliest surviving opera composed by a woman.

Synopsis

The knight Ruggiero is held in luxurious captivity by the sorceress Alcina on her enchanted island. The good sorceress Melissa arrives to free him, showing him the misery beneath Alcina's illusions; Alcina's spells are broken, her monsters and enchanted prisoners are released, and Ruggiero departs to fulfil his destiny.

Music

The work mixes recitative, arias, choruses, dances, and instrumental ritornellos in the Florentine style of the early Baroque. A balletto of horses concluded the original production. The score was printed in 1625, helping secure Caccini's fame.
