Nvidia Corporation is an American multinational technology company headquartered in Santa Clara, California. Founded in 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky and Curtis Priem, it pioneered the graphics processing unit (GPU), launching the GeForce line and the term "GPU" itself with the GeForce 256 in 1999. Since the deep-learning boom of the 2010s, Nvidia's data-center GPUs and its CUDA software platform have become the dominant hardware for training artificial intelligence models, making the company — through products sold on nvidia.com and by server makers — one of the most valuable companies in the world and the first chip company to reach a $3 trillion market capitalisation in 2024.

History

Nvidia's early graphics chips were aimed at video games, competing with 3dfx and ATI. Its RIVA and GeForce products made it the leading supplier of PC graphics cards, and its GPUs were chosen for the original Xbox and PlayStation 3. The 2006 release of CUDA let developers use GPUs for general-purpose computing, which proved decisive when researchers found GPUs ideal for training deep neural networks — the 2012 AlexNet breakthrough was trained on Nvidia cards.

Data-center sales exploded after 2020 and especially after the release of ChatGPT in late 2022 created enormous demand for AI accelerators such as the A100 and H100. Nvidia's attempted $40 billion acquisition of the chip designer Arm was abandoned in 2022 after regulatory opposition. Successive architectures — Ampere, Hopper and Blackwell — have kept the company at the centre of the AI infrastructure build-out by cloud providers including Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon and Meta.

Products

Nvidia sells GeForce graphics cards for gaming PCs, professional visualisation products (formerly Quadro, now RTX), the Jetson platform for embedded and robotics computing, DRIVE for vehicles, networking hardware from its 2020 acquisition of Mellanox, and complete DGX and rack-scale systems for AI data centers. Its annual GTC developer conference is a major event in the AI industry.

Corporate affairs

Co-founder Jensen Huang has been president and CEO since the company's founding, one of the longest tenures in the industry, and is known for presenting keynotes in a black leather jacket. Nvidia's headquarters are the Voyager and Endeavor buildings in Santa Clara.
