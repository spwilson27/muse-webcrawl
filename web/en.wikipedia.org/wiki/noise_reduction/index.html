Noise reduction

Noise reduction is the process of removing noise from a signal. Noise reduction techniques exist for signals recorded in all domains — audio, images, and video — and generally trade off noise suppression against distortion of the desired signal.

Audio

In audio, analogue systems such as Dolby noise reduction used companding to mask tape hiss; modern digital methods include spectral subtraction, Wiener filtering, and machine-learning-based speech enhancement used in telephony and hearing aids. Active noise control generates an anti-phase signal to cancel ambient sound.

Images

Image denoising algorithms include median and Gaussian filtering, anisotropic diffusion, non-local means, BM3D, and deep-learning denoisers. They are essential in low-light photography, medical imaging, and astronomy, where aggressive reduction can blur fine detail.
