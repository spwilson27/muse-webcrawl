The Best FIFA Football Awards

The Best FIFA Football Awards is an annual football award ceremony presented by FIFA, the sport's world governing body, recognising the best players and coaches of the year. The ceremony was first held on 9 January 2017 in Zurich, Switzerland, replacing the FIFA Ballon d'Or gala after FIFA ended its partnership with France Football, publisher of the Ballon d'Or, in 2016.

Awards

The headline prizes are The Best FIFA Men's Player and The Best FIFA Women's Player. Other awards include The Best FIFA Men's and Women's Goalkeeper, The Best FIFA Men's and Women's Coach, the FIFA Puskás Award for the most aesthetically significant goal of the year, the FIFA Fair Play Award, the FIFA Fan Award, and the FIFA FIFPRO Men's and Women's World 11 teams. A later addition, the Marta Award, honours the best goal in women's football.

Voting

Winners of the player and coach awards are decided by a vote split among four groups, each weighted equally: national team captains, national team coaches, selected media representatives, and fans voting online. Shortlists are compiled with input from FIFA-appointed expert panels.

History and winners

The awards continue a lineage running from the FIFA World Player of the Year (1991–2009) through the merged FIFA Ballon d'Or (2010–2015). Cristiano Ronaldo won the first two editions of The Best FIFA Men's Player (2016 and 2017); subsequent winners have included Luka Modrić, Lionel Messi (a record three times), Robert Lewandowski (twice), and Vinícius Júnior. In the women's award, winners have included Carli Lloyd, Lieke Martens, Megan Rapinoe, Lucy Bronze, Alexia Putellas (twice), and Aitana Bonmatí. The ceremony has been staged in Zurich, London, Milan, and Doha, and in some years held virtually.
