List of monarchs of the Netherlands

This is a list of monarchs of the Netherlands since the establishment of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in 1815. Before that, the Dutch Republic was governed by stadtholders of the House of Orange-Nassau, who were not monarchs. All Dutch sovereigns since 1815 have belonged to the House of Orange-Nassau, descendants of William the Silent.

Stadtholders

During the Dutch Republic (1581–1795), the princes of Orange served as stadtholders — chief executives of most provinces — including William the Silent, Maurice of Nassau, Frederick Henry, William II, William III (also King of England), William IV and William V.
