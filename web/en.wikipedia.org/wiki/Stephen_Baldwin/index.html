Stephen Baldwin

Stephen Andrew Baldwin (born May 12, 1966) is an American actor and evangelical Christian activist. The youngest of the four Baldwin brothers, he emerged in the late 1980s and 1990s with roles in films such as Born on the Fourth of July (1989), Threesome (1994), The Usual Suspects (1995), and Bio-Dome (1996). After a born-again religious conversion in the early 2000s, he redirected much of his work toward Christian ministry, faith-based media, and political activism.

Early life

Baldwin was born in Massapequa, Long Island, New York, the youngest son of Carol Newcomb, founder of a breast cancer support centre, and Alexander Rae Baldwin Jr., a public school teacher and football coach. He is the younger brother of actors Alec, Daniel, and William Baldwin. Raised Catholic, he attended Alfred G. Berner High School and briefly studied at the American Academy of Dramatic Arts before turning to television work.

Career

Baldwin made his television debut in the series The Young Riders (1989–1992) and appeared in Oliver Stone's Born on the Fourth of July (1989). His best-known film role is the wisecracking criminal Michael McManus in Bryan Singer's The Usual Suspects (1995), which won the Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay. He co-starred with Pauly Shore in the comedy Bio-Dome (1996) and played Barney Rubble in The Flintstones in Viva Rock Vegas (2000). In later years he appeared in reality television, including Celebrity Big Brother in the United Kingdom (2010) and The Celebrity Apprentice, and increasingly in low-budget and faith-based films.

Personal life and activism

Baldwin married Brazilian graphic designer Kennya Deodato in 1990; their daughters are Alaia and Hailey Baldwin, the model who married Justin Bieber in 2018. Following the September 11 attacks, Baldwin became a born-again Christian and founded or supported several evangelical outreach organisations, including skateboarding and extreme-sports ministries aimed at young people, and Breakthrough Ministry. He filed for bankruptcy in 2009 and pleaded guilty in 2013 to failing to file New York State income taxes for three years, agreeing to pay restitution. A vocal conservative, he was an early and prominent supporter of Donald Trump's presidential campaigns.
