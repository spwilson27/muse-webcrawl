Wild Turkey (bourbon)

Wild Turkey is a brand of Kentucky straight bourbon whiskey distilled in Lawrenceburg, Kentucky, by the Campari Group. The brand is associated with master distillers Jimmy Russell — among the longest-serving in the industry — and his son Eddie Russell.

History

The Austin, Nichols company began selecting bourbon in the 1940s; the Wild Turkey name, legend says, came from a turkey-hunting trip on which an executive shared his cask-strength whiskey. Campari acquired the brand and distillery in 2009 and built a new distillery in 2011.

Products

Expressions include Wild Turkey 81 and 101 (the high-proof flagship, long bottled at 101 proof / 50.5% ABV), Rare Breed barrel proof, Kentucky Spirit single barrel, Longbranch (developed with Matthew McConaughey) and the Master's Keep series, plus Wild Turkey rye whiskeys. The mash bill is relatively high in rye, and the whiskey is distilled at low proof and aged in heavily charred new oak for a bold flavour.
