Komisyon sa Wikang Filipino

The commission charged with developing and preserving Filipino and the other Philippine languages

The Komisyon sa Wikang Filipino (KWF; English: Commission on the Filipino Language) is the official government agency of the Philippines tasked with developing, preserving, and promoting Filipino, the national language, and the other indigenous languages of the country. It is headquartered in Malacañang, Manila, and operates under the Office of the President.

History

The commission's lineage goes back to the National Language Institute (Surian ng Wikang Pambansa), created in 1936 under the Commonwealth, which selected Tagalog as the basis of the national language. The 1987 Constitution directed Congress to establish a commission on the Filipino language, and Republic Act No. 7104, signed by President Corazon Aquino in 1991, created the present KWF, replacing the Surian and widening its remit to all Philippine languages.

Mandate and work

The commission conducts and promotes research on Filipino and other Philippine languages; undertakes linguistic, orthographic, and grammatical standardisation, including official spelling guides such as the Ortograpiyang Pambansa (2013); publishes dictionaries and reference works; documents endangered languages; and formulates policies and programmes for language use in education, government, and media. It leads the annual celebration of Buwan ng Wikang Pambansa (National Language Month) each August and runs translation services for government documents. The commission is governed by a chairperson and commissioners representing the major ethnolinguistic groups and academic disciplines.

Controversies

The KWF has periodically drawn criticism over orthographic rulings and terminology — most prominently a 2014 memorandum discouraging the use of "Filipinas" as the country's name in Filipino, which was challenged by writers' groups and academics before being withdrawn. Debates over purism, loanwords, and the relative status of regional languages remain central to its public profile.
