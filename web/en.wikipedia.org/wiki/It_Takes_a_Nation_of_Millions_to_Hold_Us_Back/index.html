It Takes a Nation of Millions to Hold Us Back

It Takes a Nation of Millions to Hold Us Back is the second studio album by American hip hop group Public Enemy, released on June 28, 1988, by Def Jam Recordings and Columbia Records. Produced by the Bomb Squad, it is widely regarded as one of the greatest and most influential albums of all time.

Background and production

Conceived by frontman Chuck D as hip hop's equivalent of Marvin Gaye's What's Going On, the album was built from dense layers of samples, sirens, and noise, sequenced to play with a relentless, continuous momentum. Chuck D's politically charged lyrics addressed Black empowerment, media criticism, and social injustice.

Reception and legacy

The album peaked at number 42 on the Billboard 200 and topped the Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums chart. It includes the landmark singles "Don't Believe the Hype", "Bring the Noise", and "Rebel Without a Pause". It was ranked third in Rolling Stone's 2020 list of the 500 greatest albums of all time, and was added to the National Recording Registry in 2024.
