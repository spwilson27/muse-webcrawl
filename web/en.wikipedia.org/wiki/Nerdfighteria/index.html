Nerdfighteria

Nerdfighteria is the online community of fans of the Vlogbrothers, the YouTube channel of brothers John Green and Hank Green. The community originated in 2007 with the brothers' Brotherhood 2.0 project, during which they communicated exclusively by video blogs for a year.

Culture

Members call themselves Nerdfighters — "made of awesome" — and rally around slogans such as "Don't Forget To Be Awesome" (DFTBA). Community symbols include the hand sign popularised by the community and the mascot-like giraffe imagery used in early videos.

Activism

Nerdfighteria is known for charitable activity, notably the annual Project for Awesome (P4A), a 48-hour livestream fundraiser founded in 2007 that has raised millions of dollars for charities, and support for organisations such as Kiva microlending and Partners In Health. The Foundation to Decrease World Suck channels community fundraising.
