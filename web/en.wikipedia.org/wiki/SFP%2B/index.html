SFP+

Full nameEnhanced small form-factor pluggable
StandardSFF-8431
Data rateUp to ~10 Gbit/s (16 Gbit/s Fibre Channel)

SFP+ (enhanced small form-factor pluggable) is a hot-pluggable optical or electrical transceiver module used in data communications equipment such as switches, routers and network interface cards. Standardised in 2006 (SFF-8431), it is a smaller, higher-speed successor to the SFP module.

Characteristics

SFP+ modules support data rates up to 10 Gbit/s for 10 Gigabit Ethernet and 8/16 Gbit/s Fibre Channel. By moving clock and data recovery circuitry onto the host board, SFP+ modules are smaller and lower-power than the earlier XFP format. Media options include direct-attach copper cables (SFP+ DAC), active optical cables, and fibre modules in SR, LR and ER reaches.

Successors

Higher-speed derivatives include SFP28 (25 Gbit/s) and SFP56, which retain the same physical form factor with faster signalling.
