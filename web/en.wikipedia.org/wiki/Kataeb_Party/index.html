Kataeb Party

The Kataeb Party (Arabic: حزب الكتائب اللبنانية, "Party of the Lebanese Phalanges"), commonly known as the Phalange, is a Lebanese political party founded in 1936 by Pierre Gemayel. Historically associated mainly with Lebanon's Maronite Christian community, it advocates Lebanese nationalism, sovereignty and a free-market economy.

History

Gemayel founded the Kataeb as a youth movement in 1936 after visiting Nazi Germany for the Berlin Olympics, modelling its organisation on European phalangist movements. The party supported Lebanese independence from France and played a major role in the politics of the republic after 1943. During the Lebanese Civil War (1975–1990), its militia, the Lebanese Forces, was one of the principal armed factions; party leader Bashir Gemayel was elected president in 1982 but assassinated before taking office. His brother Amine Gemayel served as president from 1982 to 1988.

Modern era

After the civil war the party declined under Syrian tutelage but revived following the 2005 Cedar Revolution, in which Pierre Gemayel's son Pierre Amine Gemayel, a minister, was assassinated in 2006. Since 2015 the party has been led by Samy Gemayel, holding a small bloc in parliament and campaigning against corruption and Hezbollah's arms.
