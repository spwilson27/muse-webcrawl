South Florida

South Florida is the southernmost region of the U.S. state of Florida, centred on the Miami metropolitan area of Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties, and often extended to include Monroe County (the Florida Keys), the Treasure Coast, and the southwestern Gulf counties around Fort Myers and Naples. It is one of the most populous and fastest-growing regions in the United States, home to more than nine million people in its broadest definition.

Geography and climate

The region is low-lying and flat, bounded by the Atlantic Ocean to the east, the Everglades to the west, and Lake Okeechobee to the north. Its tropical monsoon and savanna climates bring hot, humid, rainy summers and mild dry winters. The area is highly vulnerable to hurricanes, flooding, and sea-level rise.

Culture and economy

South Florida is a major centre of international trade, tourism, finance, and Latin American and Caribbean culture, with large Cuban, Haitian, Colombian, and other immigrant communities; Spanish is widely spoken alongside English. Its economy is anchored by PortMiami, Miami International Airport, beaches and resorts, and a large retiree population.
