Alain Ducasse

Alain Ducasse (born 13 September 1956) is a French chef and restaurateur, one of the most decorated chefs in the world, whose restaurants have collectively held more Michelin stars than those of almost any other chef. He was born in Orthez in southwestern France and trained in Bordeaux and on the Côte d'Azur.

Ducasse became the first chef to hold three Michelin stars in three different cities simultaneously, through restaurants including Alain Ducasse au Plaza Athénée in Paris, Le Louis XV in Monte Carlo and Alain Ducasse at The Dorchester in London. His cuisine emphasises vegetables, sustainability and naturalness; he became a naturalised citizen of Monaco in 2008.

Beyond fine dining, his group operates restaurants, inns, culinary schools and chocolate and coffee manufactures in several countries.
