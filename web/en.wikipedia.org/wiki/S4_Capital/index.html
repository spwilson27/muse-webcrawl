S4 Capital plc is a British digital advertising and marketing services company founded in 2018 by Sir Martin Sorrell, shortly after his resignation from WPP, the advertising group he had built over more than three decades. S4 Capital is listed on the London Stock Exchange.

History

Sorrell established S4 Capital in May 2018 by reversing his investment vehicle Derriston Capital into the listed company. In July 2018 the company acquired MediaMonks, a Dutch digital production company, for a reported €300 million, beating WPP in the bidding. In December 2018 it merged with MightyHive, a programmatic advertising business, expanding into media buying and data services.

The group grew through a rapid series of acquisitions, building a unitary operating brand around MediaMonks, combining content production, data and digital media services. Its clients have included major technology and consumer brands. In 2022–2023 the company faced a sharp decline in market value amid weaker technology-sector spending, an accounting delay in 2022 that temporarily suspended its shares, and repeated profit warnings, prompting cost reductions.
