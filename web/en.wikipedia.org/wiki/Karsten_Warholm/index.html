Karsten Warholm (born 28 February 1996) is a Norwegian sprinter and hurdler who competes in the 400 metres hurdles. He is the Olympic champion from Tokyo 2020 and a multiple world champion, and he holds the world record in the event.

Career

Warholm first excelled as a combined-events athlete before specialising in the hurdles. He won the world title in London in 2017 and retained it in Doha in 2019 and Budapest in 2023.

World record

On 1 July 2021 in Oslo he broke Kevin Young's long-standing world record with 46.70 seconds. A month later, in the Tokyo Olympic final, he lowered it to a remarkable 45.94 seconds in a race widely regarded as one of the greatest in athletics history, ahead of Rai Benjamin.
