Hackensack is a city in and the county seat of Bergen County, in the U.S. state of New Jersey. Located in the New York metropolitan area along the Hackensack River, the city lies about 12 miles west of New York City. As of the 2020 census, its population was 46,030.

History

The area was inhabited by the Lenni Lenape people — the name Hackensack derives from the Lenape Achinigeu-hach, referring to the river. Dutch and English settlers arrived in the 17th century, and the settlement served as a village during the American Revolutionary War; British troops raided it in 1780 and burned the courthouse. Hackensack was long governed as part of New Barbadoes Township before incorporating as a city in 1921.

Geography and transport

The city is served by NJ Transit rail stations on the Pascack Valley Line and by the New Jersey Turnpike and Interstate 80 nearby. Hackensack University Medical Center is among the largest employers.
