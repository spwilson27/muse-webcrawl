Progressive Christianity

Progressive Christianity is a movement within Christianity that emphasizes social justice, inclusivity, and the questioning of traditional doctrines through critical interpretation of the Bible. Progressive Christians generally stress compassion, the teachings of Jesus on care for the poor and marginalized, environmental stewardship, and openness to people of other faiths and identities, including LGBTQ people.

Beliefs and practices

The movement draws on liberal theology, historical-critical biblical scholarship, and liberation theology. Many progressive Christians take a non-literal approach to scripture, regard doubt and questioning as part of faith, and focus on ethical living over doctrinal conformity. Organizations such as ProgressiveChristianity.org have articulated points describing the movement, emphasizing Jesus's way, inclusivity, and care for the earth.

Relationship to liberal Christianity

Progressive Christianity overlaps with liberal Christianity but developed partly as a response to both conservative evangelicalism and perceived limitations of older mainline liberalism, placing strong emphasis on practice, community action, and interfaith engagement.
