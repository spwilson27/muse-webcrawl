"Who Shot Mr. Burns?" is a two-part episode of the American animated television series The Simpsons. Part One is the twenty-fifth and final episode of the sixth season (1995) and Part Two is the premiere of the seventh season. Conceived by showrunner David Mirkin as a publicity event and modelled on the famous "Who shot J.R.?" storyline of Dallas, the episodes form a mystery in which the tyrannical nuclear plant owner Montgomery Burns is shot, leaving the whole town of Springfield under suspicion.

Plot

In Part One, oil is discovered beneath Springfield Elementary School, but Mr. Burns steals the oil well, blocks out the sun with a giant sun-blocking disk to force increased electricity use, and enrages nearly every resident of Springfield. At a town meeting Burns is later found shot and collapsed on the town hall sundial, and the episode ends on a cliffhanger. Over the summer of 1995 the network ran a contest, "Who Shot Mr. Burns?", inviting viewers to solve the mystery; a companion special, Springfield's Most Wanted, was hosted by John Walsh.

In Part Two, Chief Wiggum investigates, eliminating suspects one by one through dreams and interrogations. The shooter is finally revealed to be Maggie Simpson: at the scene, Burns fell onto the sundial and, struggling with Maggie over a lollipop, his gun fell into her hands and discharged accidentally.

Reception

The episodes are frequently cited among the series' best, praised for their parody of mystery conventions and for a solution that was deducible from clues but guessed by very few. The contest winner who correctly guessed Maggie could not be used because the entrant had not entered properly, so a random winner was drawn.
