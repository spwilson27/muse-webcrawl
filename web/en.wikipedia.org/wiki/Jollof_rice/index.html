Jollof rice
RegionWest Africa
Main ingredientsRice, tomatoes, peppers, onion, spices

Jollof rice, also called benachin, is a rice dish of West African origin cooked in a blended tomato, pepper and onion sauce with spices. It is especially popular in Nigeria, Ghana, Senegal, Cameroon and Sierra Leone.

Origins

It derives from thieboudienne of Senegal's Wolof people, from whose name 'Jollof' comes. Colonial-era trade in rice and tomatoes spread regional variants.

Variations and culture

Nigerian party jollof, often cooked over firewood for a smoky flavour, and Ghanaian jollof, often using basmati and more tomato, fuel a good-natured rivalry celebrated on World Jollof Day, 22 August. It is served with plantain, chicken, beef or fish.
