Svans

The Svans are an ethnic subgroup of the Georgians living mostly in Svaneti, a region in northwestern Georgia. They speak the Svan language, an unwritten member of the Kartvelian language family distinct from Georgian, and have preserved a distinctive mountain culture marked by medieval defensive tower houses.

Geography

Svaneti lies high in the Greater Caucasus, in the valleys of the Enguri and Tskhenistsqali rivers. Villages such as Mestia and Ushguli are famous for their stone family towers, built between the 9th and 12th centuries, and Ushguli is one of the highest continuously inhabited settlements in Europe.

Culture

Svan culture blends Orthodox Christianity with older folk customs, distinctive polyphonic singing, goldsmithing and icon painting. Historically the Svans were renowned as fierce warriors and guardians of Georgian church treasures hidden in their mountain churches during invasions.
