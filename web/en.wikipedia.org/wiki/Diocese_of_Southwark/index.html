Diocese of Southwark

The Diocese of Southwark is a Church of England diocese covering south London and east Surrey. Created in 1905, largely from the Diocese of Rochester with parts of Winchester, it serves some 2.8 million people. Its cathedral is Southwark Cathedral, the collegiate church of St Saviour and St Mary Overie, standing by London Bridge at the south bank of the Thames.

The Bishop of Southwark is assisted by area (suffragan) bishops of Croydon, Kingston, and Woolwich, corresponding to the diocese's three episcopal areas. The diocese includes the boroughs south of the river from Lambeth and Wandsworth to Bexley and Bromley, together with the Surrey districts of Tandridge and part of Reigate and Banstead.

Note that the Anglican diocese is distinct from the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Southwark, founded in 1850, whose cathedral is St George's, Southwark. The Anglican diocese has been noted for a liberal Catholic tradition and for pioneering work in urban ministry and community organising.
