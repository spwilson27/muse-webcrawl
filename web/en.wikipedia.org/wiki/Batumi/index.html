Batumi

Batumi
Country: Georgia
Region: Adjara
Population: approx. 170,000
Port city on the Black Sea

Batumi is a port city on the Black Sea coast of Georgia and the capital of the Autonomous Republic of Adjara. It is Georgia's second-largest city, a major port and a popular seaside resort known for its subtropical climate and modern skyline.

History

Founded as the Greek colony Bathys, the city passed through Roman, Byzantine, Ottoman and Russian hands. Under Russian rule it became a free port handling Caspian oil exports, and it joined Georgia after the First World War settlements.

Economy and tourism

Batumi's port, oil terminal, casinos, botanical garden and boulevard draw visitors, especially from neighbouring countries.

Geography

The city lies on a bay near the Turkish border, backed by the Lesser Caucasus foothills, with one of the wettest climates in the region.
