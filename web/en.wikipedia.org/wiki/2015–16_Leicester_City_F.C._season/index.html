The 2015–16 season was Leicester City Football Club's 111th season in the English football league system and their 48th (non-consecutive) season in the top tier. The club won the Premier League title in one of the greatest shocks in sporting history, having been rated 5000–1 outsiders by bookmakers at the start of the season.

Season summary

Under new manager Claudio Ranieri, Leicester combined rapid counter-attacking football with remarkable consistency. Striker Jamie Vardy scored in a record eleven consecutive Premier League matches, while Riyad Mahrez was named PFA Players' Player of the Year and N'Golo Kanté anchored the midfield.

Title confirmed

Leicester were confirmed as champions on 2 May 2016 when nearest challengers Tottenham Hotspur drew 2–2 at Chelsea, having led 2–0. The club lifted the trophy after a 3–1 home win over Everton, finishing ten points clear of second-placed Arsenal and losing only three league matches all season.

Squad statistics

Vardy finished with 24 league goals, Mahrez contributed 17 goals and 11 assists, and captain Wes Morgan played every minute of the league campaign.
