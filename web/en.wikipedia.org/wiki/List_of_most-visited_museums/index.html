This is a list of the most-visited museums in the world, ranked by annual visitor numbers. The Louvre in Paris has consistently ranked first, attracting around eight to nine million visitors per year before the COVID-19 pandemic, largely drawn by Leonardo da Vinci's Mona Lisa. Visitor figures are compiled from museum reports, the TEA/AECOM Theme Index and Museum Index, and the annual survey published by The Art Newspaper.
