Battle of the Eastern Solomons
Date: 24–25 August 1942
Location: Solomon Islands
Part of: Pacific War

The Battle of the Eastern Solomons was a naval battle fought on 24 and 25 August 1942 between the United States Navy and the Imperial Japanese Navy during the Guadalcanal campaign. It was the second carrier battle of the Pacific War.

Background

Japan sent a convoy to reinforce Guadalcanal, covered by the carriers Shōkaku, Zuikaku and the light carrier Ryūjō. U.S. carriers Enterprise, Saratoga and Wasp operated nearby.

The battle

American aircraft sank Ryūjō, while Japanese aircraft heavily damaged Enterprise. The Japanese reinforcement convoy turned back after attacks by aircraft from Henderson Field.

Aftermath

The battle was a tactical and strategic success for the United States, though Japanese forces later landed troops by night destroyer runs.
