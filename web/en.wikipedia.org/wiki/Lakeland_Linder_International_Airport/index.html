Lakeland Linder International Airport

Lakeland Linder International Airport (IATA: LAL, ICAO: KLAL) is a public airport in Lakeland, Florida, United States. It is best known as the home of the annual Sun 'n Fun Aerospace Expo, one of the largest aviation events in the United States.

History

The field originated as a municipal airport in the 1930s and served during the Second World War as Drane Field, an auxiliary training base. It was renamed for local businessman and philanthropist Barney Barnett's associate, (the airport honours the Linder family, industrial machinery magnates who supported its development).

Operations

The airport has two runways and supports general aviation, flight training, aircraft maintenance firms, and cargo operations. Regularly scheduled commercial passenger service has operated intermittently, with Avelo Airlines beginning service in 2024.
