Neuralink

Neuralink Corp. is an American neurotechnology company that develops implantable brain–computer interfaces (BCIs). It was founded by Elon Musk and a team of scientists and engineers in 2016 and is headquartered in Fremont, California. Neuralink's stated long-term aim is to achieve a symbiosis between human cognition and artificial intelligence; its first targeted applications are medical, helping people with paralysis control digital devices.

Technology

Neuralink's device, called the Link, is a coin-sized implant placed in an opening in the skull. It connects to the cortex through many flexible electrode threads — in the current device, 64 threads carrying 1,024 electrodes — inserted by a purpose-built surgical robot designed to avoid blood vessels. Signals are processed on-chip and transmitted wirelessly, and the implant charges inductively. The company has also developed a "Telepathy" product name for the first system and has announced work on "Blindsight" for restoring vision.

Animal studies and criticism

The company demonstrated its technology in pigs (2020) and in a macaque playing the game Pong via the implant (2021). Its animal testing drew criticism from animal-welfare groups and scrutiny from US regulators over record-keeping and animal deaths; Neuralink has denied negligence and says animals were treated humanely.

Human trials

In May 2023 the US Food and Drug Administration authorised Neuralink to begin human clinical trials under the PRIME study for people with quadriplegia. The first participant, Noland Arbaugh, received an implant in January 2024 at the Barrow Neurological Institute and demonstrated cursor control and computer gaming by thought; the company reported that some electrode threads retracted, reducing performance, and said the issue was mitigated in the second participant, implanted later in 2024, and subsequent participants in the US, Canada, and the UK.

Reception

Neuroscientists note that the individual components build on decades of academic BCI research, while the integration, scale, and commercialisation are novel; experts also caution that Musk's long-term claims about merging with AI are speculative.
