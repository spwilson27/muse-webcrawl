Casual and family dining

Major casual-dining groups include Applebee's, Chili's, Olive Garden, Red Lobster (US), The Cheesecake Factory, Denny's and IHOP (US diners), Wetherspoons (UK pubs), and casual chains such as Nando's (South African peri-peri chicken), Din Tai Fung (Taiwanese dumplings) and Haidilao (Chinese hot pot).
