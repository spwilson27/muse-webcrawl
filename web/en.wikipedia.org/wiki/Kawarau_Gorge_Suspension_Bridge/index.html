Kawarau Gorge Suspension Bridge

Kawarau Gorge Suspension Bridge
Location: Otago, New Zealand
Crosses: Kawarau River
Completed: 1880
Height: 43 m above the river

The Kawarau Gorge Suspension Bridge is a suspension bridge over the Kawarau River in the Gibbston Valley near Queenstown, New Zealand. Completed in 1880 and designed by engineer Harry Higginson, it is famous as the site of the world's first commercial bungy jumping operation.

History

The bridge was built to provide access to the Central Otago goldfields and carried road traffic until a new highway bridge replaced it in 1963. It is now a scheduled historic structure, used only by pedestrians.

Bungy jumping

In 1988 A. J. Hackett and Henry van Asch launched their first commercial bungy site from the bridge, with jumpers leaping 43 metres toward the river below. The Kawarau Bridge Bungy remains a major tourist attraction.
