Kristen Anderson-Lopez (born March 21, 1972) is an American songwriter. She is known for co-writing the songs for the Walt Disney Animation Studios films Frozen (2013) and Frozen II (2019) and Pixar's Coco (2017) with her husband Robert Lopez. Anderson-Lopez won the Academy Award for Best Original Song for "Let It Go" from Frozen and for "Remember Me" from Coco, and is one of a small number of artists to have won Emmy, Grammy, Oscar and Tony Awards (EGOT).

Early life and education

Anderson-Lopez was born in New York City and raised in Croton-on-Hudson, New York, and later in North Carolina. She graduated from Williams College with a double major in drama and psychology. She later studied at the BMI Lehman Engel Musical Theatre Workshop in New York, where she met Robert Lopez.

Career

Theatre and early work

With Lopez, she co-created the musical Finding Nemo – The Musical for Disney's Animal Kingdom, and wrote songs for the 2011 film Winnie the Pooh. The couple also wrote the musical Up Here (2015), premiered at La Jolla Playhouse.

Frozen and Coco

Anderson-Lopez and Lopez wrote the nine songs for Frozen, including "Let It Go", which won the Academy Award and the Grammy Award for Best Song Written for Visual Media. For Pixar's Coco they wrote "Remember Me", which won a second Academy Award. They returned for Frozen II, contributing "Into the Unknown" and "Show Yourself", and co-wrote songs for the Marvel series WandaVision (earning an Emmy for "Agatha All Along") and its spin-off Agatha All Along. The stage adaptation of Frozen opened on Broadway in 2018, with additional songs by the Lopezes.

Personal life

She married Robert Lopez in 2003; they have two daughters, both of whom performed small voice roles in the Frozen films. Her sister, Kate Anderson, is also a songwriter, known for Olaf's Frozen Adventure.
