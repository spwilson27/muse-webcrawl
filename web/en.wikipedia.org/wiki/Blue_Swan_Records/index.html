Blue Swan Records is an American record label founded in 2013 by Will Swan, guitarist of the post-hardcore band Dance Gavin Dance. The label became closely associated with the "Swancore" scene, a community of progressive post-hardcore and math-rock bands influenced by Swan's playing style.

History

Among the label's earliest signings were Hail the Sun, Stolas and Icarus the Owl. Blue Swan developed a reputation for releasing technically intricate guitar-driven music and for annual showcase tours featuring its roster. In 2019 the label entered a partnership with Rise Records, expanding its distribution and resources.

Roster

Artists associated with the label have included Dance Gavin Dance, Hail the Sun, Royal Coda, Wolf & Bear, Eidola, Andrés, and Tilian. The label remains a focal point of the Sacramento-centred progressive post-hardcore scene.
