Captain Sensible (born Raymond Ian Burns, 24 April 1954) is an English singer, songwriter and guitarist. He co-founded the punk rock band the Damned, playing bass and later guitar, and enjoyed solo success in the 1980s with a UK number-one single, "Happy Talk" (1982), as well as hits with "Wot" and "Glad It's All Over." Recognisable by his red beret and sunglasses, he is noted for melodic, witty songwriting and virtuoso guitar playing within punk.

Early life

Burns was born in Balham, London, and grew up in Croydon. Before music, he worked as a toilet cleaner at Croydon College — where the Damned's precursor circle formed — and played in covers and progressive-influenced bands, including with future Damned drummer Rat Scabies in the group Johnny Moped's circle. His stage name, an ironic title (he was famously the band's most chaotic member and its habitual fancy-dress wearer), came after he drunkenly introduced himself on stage as "Captain Sensible."

The Damned

The Damned formed in 1976 with Dave Vanian (vocals), Brian James (guitar), Captain Sensible (bass) and Rat Scabies (drums). The band released the first British punk single, "New Rose" (October 1976), and the first British punk album, Damned Damned Damned (1977), and was the first UK punk band to tour the United States. After the chaotic second album Music for Pleasure (1977) and a brief split, the band reformed with Sensible switching to guitar and keyboards, becoming a principal songwriter alongside Vanian.

With albums such as Machine Gun Etiquette (1979), The Black Album (1980) — featuring his 18-minute composition "Curtain Call" — Strawberries (1982) and the goth-leaning Phantasmagoria (1985), the Damned moved beyond punk into psychedelic and gothic rock. Sensible left in the mid-1980s to pursue his solo career, rejoined in the 1990s, and remains the band's guitarist; the classic line-up's later work includes the albums Evil Spirits (2018) and Darkadelic (2023).

Solo career

Signed as a solo artist to A&M Records, Sensible released the albums Women and Captains First (1982) and The Power of Love (1983). His cover of "Happy Talk," from the musical South Pacific, reached number one in the UK in July 1982, assisted by backing vocals from the girl group Dolly Mixture. The follow-ups "Wot" (a European hit built on a studio accident of a bassline and his toddler son's exclamations) and "Glad It's All Over" (1984, an anti-Falklands War song) also charted. He formed the side project The Sensible Grey Cells and continued releasing independent solo records, including Revolution Now (1989) and Meathead (1995).

Politics

Sensible has long been politically outspoken, supporting CND and animal rights; in 2006 he founded the short-lived Blah! Party, a satirical protest party intended to channel protest votes, partly funded by the makers of a snack brand. "Glad It's All Over" remains one of British pop's notable anti-war singles.

Personal style and legacy

Known for nurse's uniforms, tutus and his trademark red beret on stage, Captain Sensible is nonetheless regarded by fellow musicians as one of punk's most accomplished guitarists, blending speed with melody and a deep knowledge of 1960s psychedelia. He lives in Brighton.
