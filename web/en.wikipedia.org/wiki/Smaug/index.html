Smaug is a dragon and the main antagonist in J. R. R. Tolkien's novel The Hobbit (1937). A fearsome winged drake with reddish-gold scales, he descended on the Dwarf-kingdom of Erebor and the neighbouring town of Dale in T.A. 2770, driving out King Thrór's people and seizing their treasure, on which he slept for nearly two centuries. Critics regard Smaug as one of the most influential dragons in modern fantasy.

Role in The Hobbit

In 2941 the wizard Gandalf and Thorin Oakenshield's company of thirteen Dwarves recruit the hobbit Bilbo Baggins as their "burglar" to infiltrate Smaug's lair. Bilbo twice enters the treasure hall, stealing a cup and, on the second visit, conversing with the dragon in riddles while wearing the One Ring to remain invisible. During their talk Bilbo flatters Smaug into displaying his jewel-encrusted underbelly — and spots a bare patch over his left breast. Enraged by the theft and by Bilbo's riddling hints of Lake-town involvement, Smaug attacks Esgaroth (Lake-town), burning it, but is killed by Bard the Bowman, who shoots a black arrow into the weak spot, having been told of it by a thrush that overheard Bilbo's report.

Characteristics

Smaug is intelligent, articulate and vain — "a most specially greedy, strong and wicked worm." Tolkien drew on Old English literature: Smaug's hoard-guarding and the cup theft echo Beowulf, and his riddling conversation recalls Fáfnir in the Völsunga saga. Tolkien explained the name as the past tense of the Old Norse/Germanic verb smjúga ("to squeeze through a hole"), a philological joke on Bilbo's infiltration. His illustrations of "Conversation with Smaug" show the dragon atop a mound of gold and jewels.

Portrayals

Smaug was voiced by Richard Boone in the 1977 Rankin/Bass animated film and performed (voice and motion capture) by Benedict Cumberbatch in Peter Jackson's Hobbit trilogy (2012–2014), appearing chiefly in The Desolation of Smaug (2013). A real-life namesake exists in taxonomy: the giant girdled lizard Smaug giganteus was named for him in 2011, and the South American marsupial frog Gastrotheca smaug likewise honours the dragon.
