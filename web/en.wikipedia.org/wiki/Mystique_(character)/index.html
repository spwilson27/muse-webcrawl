Mystique (character)

Mystique is a character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by artist David Cockrum and writer Chris Claremont, she first appeared in Ms. Marvel #16 (1978). Born Raven Darkhölme, she is a mutant shapeshifter with blue skin, red hair, and yellow eyes in her natural form.

Typically portrayed as an adversary of the X-Men and a member of the Brotherhood of Mutants, Mystique is the mother of Nightcrawler and adoptive mother of Rogue. Her slowed aging makes her over a century old.

In other media

She was played by Rebecca Romijn in the early X-Men films and by Jennifer Lawrence in the prequel films beginning with X-Men: First Class (2011).
