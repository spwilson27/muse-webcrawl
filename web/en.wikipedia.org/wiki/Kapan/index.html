Kapan

Kapan (Armenian: Կապան) is a town in south-eastern Armenia, serving as the administrative centre of Syunik Province and of the Kapan Municipality. It is the largest city in the province and one of the most important mining centres of Armenia. The town lies in the valley of the Voghji River, surrounded by the Zangezur Mountains, close to the border with Iran.

History

The name Kapan derives from the Armenian verb kapel, meaning "to lock" or "to enclose", reflecting the town's setting among interlocking mountain ridges. The area was part of the historic province of Syunik within Greater Armenia. Copper mining in the region dates back centuries, and the town grew significantly during the Soviet period as the centre of the Kapan mining and metallurgical combine, which processed copper and molybdenum ores from the nearby Kajaran deposit — one of the largest copper-molybdenum deposits in the world.

During the Nagorno-Karabakh conflict of the early 1990s, Kapan and the surrounding villages came under shelling from across the Azerbaijani border, and the town's economy suffered from the closure of transport links. The town's airport closed during that period.

Geography and climate

Kapan is situated in a deep valley at the confluence of the Voghji and Vachagan rivers. The climate is relatively mild for the region, with warm summers and moderately cold winters. Mount Khustup, a prominent peak sacred to locals, rises near the town and is associated with the 18th-century Armenian liberation hero David Bek, whose fortress of Halidzor is located nearby.

Economy

Mining remains the backbone of the local economy. The Kajaran copper-molybdenum combine, operated by the Zangezur Copper Molybdenum Combine, is one of Armenia's largest industrial enterprises and exporters. Agriculture, food processing and services also contribute to employment.

Culture and landmarks

Landmarks include the Wings of Tatev aerial tramway in the wider region, the medieval Tatev Monastery accessible from the town, the Baghaberd fortress, and the statue of David Bek in the town centre. Kapan has a regional museum and several churches, including the church of Saint Mesrop Mashtots.
