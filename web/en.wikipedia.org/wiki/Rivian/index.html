Rivian Automotive, Inc. is an American electric vehicle manufacturer and automotive technology company founded in 2009 by RJ Scaringe. Headquartered in Irvine, California, Rivian develops electric adventure vehicles built on a flexible "skateboard" platform, as well as electric delivery vans for Amazon.

History

The company operated in relative secrecy for years before unveiling its R1T pickup truck and R1S sport utility vehicle at the LA Auto Show in November 2018. Rivian attracted major investment from Amazon and Ford, and Amazon ordered 100,000 electric delivery vans. Rivian acquired a former Mitsubishi factory in Normal, Illinois, as its main manufacturing plant.

Rivian's initial public offering in November 2021 was one of the largest of the year, briefly valuing the company at over $100 billion despite minimal revenue. Production of the R1T began in late 2021; the R1S and delivery vans followed.

Products

The R1T and R1S are premium off-road-capable electric vehicles noted for features such as a gear tunnel, tank turn demonstrations, and quad-motor all-wheel drive. The smaller, more affordable R2 was unveiled in March 2024 alongside the surprise R3 and R3X crossovers.

Partnerships

In June 2024 Rivian and Volkswagen Group announced a joint venture, worth up to about $5.8 billion, for Volkswagen to use Rivian's zonal electrical architecture and software in future vehicles. Amazon remains a major shareholder and customer; Ford reduced its stake after 2022.
