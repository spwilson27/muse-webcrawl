A Star Is Born is a 2018 American musical romantic drama film produced and directed by Bradley Cooper in his directorial debut, from a screenplay by Cooper, Eric Roth and Will Fetters. The fourth filmed version of the story, it stars Cooper as Jackson Maine, a seasoned, alcoholic country-rock musician who discovers and falls in love with a young singer, Ally, played by Lady Gaga. As Ally's career eclipses his own, Jackson's addictions and tinnitus pull him toward self-destruction.

Plot

After a concert, Jackson Maine wanders into a drag bar where he sees waitress and singer-songwriter Ally performing "La Vie en rose". Impressed, he invites her to his next show, where he brings her on stage to sing her song "Shallow". The performance goes viral; Ally joins Jackson's tour, signs with a record label, and wins a Grammy, shifting toward pop stardom while Jackson's drinking worsens — he publicly humiliates her at the Grammys and later, after a stay in rehab, hangs himself in their garage rather than drag her down. Ally closes the film singing his final composition, "I'll Never Love Again", introducing herself as Ally Maine.

Production

The remake was in development for years — at one point Clint Eastwood was attached to direct with Beyoncé starring — before Cooper took the project over in 2015, casting Gaga after seeing her perform at a charity event. Cooper trained vocally and instrumentally to play Jackson; the concert scenes were filmed live at real festivals including Coachella, Stagecoach and Glastonbury, with audiences asked to keep the plot secret. All music was recorded live on set at Gaga's insistence.

Soundtrack and reception

The soundtrack, credited to Gaga and Cooper with contributions from Lukas Nelson, Jason Isbell, Mark Ronson and Diane Warren, topped charts worldwide. "Shallow" won the Academy Award for Best Original Song, as well as Grammy and Golden Globe awards. The film received eight Academy Award nominations including Best Picture, Best Actor, Best Actress, and Best Supporting Actor for Sam Elliott, and grossed over $436 million worldwide, becoming one of the most acclaimed directorial debuts of its decade.
