Patriots Point Naval & Maritime Museum, commonly known as Patriots Point, is a naval museum located in Mount Pleasant, South Carolina, at the mouth of the Cooper River on Charleston Harbor. Opened in 1975, the museum is centred on the aircraft carrier USS Yorktown (CV-10) and also includes the destroyer USS Laffey (DD-724) and the submarine USS Clamagore (SS-343). It is one of the largest naval museums in the United States and a major tourist attraction in the Charleston area.

History

The Patriots Point Development Authority was created by the South Carolina General Assembly in 1973 to develop a museum site on Charleston Harbor. The first ship to arrive was USS Yorktown, an Essex-class aircraft carrier launched in 1943 and named for the carrier lost at the Battle of Midway. Towed from Bayonne, New Jersey, the ship was dedicated on 13 October 1975, the 200th birthday of the United States Navy. The destroyer Laffey, famous for surviving intense kamikaze attacks off Okinawa in April 1945 and nicknamed "The Ship That Would Not Die," joined the museum in 1981, and the GUPPY III submarine Clamagore arrived in 1983.

Exhibits

Visitors can tour the flight deck, hangar deck and island of Yorktown, which hosts the Medal of Honor Museum and a collection of historic military aircraft, including Second World War carrier aircraft and Cold War jets. Aboard Laffey are exhibits on destroyer operations and antisubmarine warfare, while Clamagore preserves the cramped conditions of a diesel-electric submarine. Ashore, the museum complex includes the Cold War Submarine Memorial, a three-acre Vietnam Experience exhibit with helicopters, a river patrol boat and a firebase diorama, and memorials to merchant mariners and submariners lost at sea.

Education and events

Patriots Point operates an extensive overnight camping program aboard Yorktown for school and scout groups, STEM education programs, and hosts ceremonies, reunions and special events throughout the year. The museum attracts several hundred thousand visitors annually and is a prominent landmark visible from Charleston across the harbor.

Ships

  • USS Laffey (DD-724) — Allen M. Sumner-class destroyer (1944)
