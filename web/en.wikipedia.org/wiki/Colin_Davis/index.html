Colin Davis

Sir Colin Rex Davis (25 September 1927 – 14 April 2013) was an English conductor, chiefly associated with the London Symphony Orchestra, the Royal Opera House, Covent Garden, and the Bavarian Radio Symphony Orchestra. He was particularly renowned for his interpretations of Mozart, Berlioz, Sibelius and Stravinsky.

Career

Originally a clarinettist, Davis rose to prominence deputising for Otto Klemperer and Thomas Beecham in the early 1960s. He was chief conductor of the BBC Symphony Orchestra (1967–71), musical director of the Royal Opera House (1971–86), chief conductor of the Bavarian Radio Symphony Orchestra (1983–92), and principal conductor of the London Symphony Orchestra from 1995 to 2006, completing a celebrated cycle of Sibelius symphonies and championing Berlioz throughout his career.

Honours

Davis was knighted in 1980 and appointed a Member of the Order of the Companions of Honour in 2001. His extensive discography won numerous Grammy Awards, including for his live LSO recordings of Berlioz's Les Troyens.
