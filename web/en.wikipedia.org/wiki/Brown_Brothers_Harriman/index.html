Brown Brothers Harriman & Co. (BBH) is an American multinational private investment bank and financial services company headquartered in New York City. Organised as a partnership rather than a corporation, it is one of the oldest and largest private banks in the United States.

History

The firm traces its origins to 1818, when Irish linen merchant Alexander Brown founded Alex. Brown & Sons in Baltimore. Brown Brothers & Co. was established in Philadelphia and New York by his sons. In 1931, Brown Brothers merged with Harriman Brothers & Co., the investment firm of W. Averell Harriman, to form the present company.

Partners of the firm have included notable public figures such as Prescott Bush. BBH avoided much of the risk-taking of larger banks and survived the 2008 financial crisis without government assistance.

Business

The firm operates three principal businesses: private banking for individuals and families, investment management, and investor services, including global custody for institutional investors.
