Copa América Centenario

The Copa América Centenario was an international men's association football tournament held in the United States from 3 to 26 June 2016. It was a celebratory edition of the Copa América, marking one hundred years since the founding of CONMEBOL and the first Copa América in 1916, and the first to be hosted outside South America.

Tournament summary

Sixteen teams competed: all ten CONMEBOL members and six CONCACAF nations. The tournament set attendance records for the competition in the United States. Chile defeated Argentina in the final at MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford, winning 4–2 on penalties after a goalless draw — a repeat of the 2015 final. After the defeat, Lionel Messi briefly announced his retirement from international football. Eduardo Vargas of Chile finished as top scorer, while Chile's Alexis Sánchez was named best player.

Background

The special edition was organised jointly by CONMEBOL and CONCACAF and was included in the FIFA International Match Calendar. It was regarded as a separate celebratory tournament rather than the 45th numbered edition of the Copa América.
