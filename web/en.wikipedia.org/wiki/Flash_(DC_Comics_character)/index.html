Flash (DC Comics character)

The Flash is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Gardner Fox and artist Harry Lampert, the original Flash, Jay Garrick, debuted in Flash Comics #1 (1940). Several characters have used the name, most famously Barry Allen and Wally West, all defined by superhuman speed granted through the Speed Force.

The Flash
Publisher: DC Comics
First appearance: Flash Comics #1 (1940)
Creators: Gardner Fox, Harry Lampert
Alter egos: Jay Garrick, Barry Allen, Wally West, Bart Allen
Abilities: Super speed, time travel, phasing

History

Jay Garrick, a college student who inhaled heavy water vapours, became the Golden Age Flash. Barry Allen, a police forensic scientist struck by lightning amid chemicals, revived the hero in Showcase #4 (1956), launching the Silver Age of comics. His sidekick Wally West became Kid Flash and later succeeded him after Barry’s death in Crisis on Infinite Earths (1985); Barry returned in 2008.

Powers

Flashes tap the Speed Force, an extradimensional energy allowing them to run faster than light, vibrate through objects, travel in time, heal rapidly and think at superhuman speed. Their rogues’ gallery includes Reverse-Flash, Captain Cold, Gorilla Grodd and Zoom.

In other media

The Flash has starred in television series in 1990 and 2014 (Grant Gustin), animated shows as a Justice League member, and the 2023 feature film The Flash with Ezra Miller.
