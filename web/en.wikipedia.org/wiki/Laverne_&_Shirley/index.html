Laverne & Shirley

Laverne & Shirley is an American television sitcom that aired on ABC from January 27, 1976, to May 10, 1983, for eight seasons and 178 episodes. A spin-off of Happy Days, it starred Penny Marshall as Laverne DeFazio and Cindy Williams as Shirley Feeney, two single working-class friends who room together in Milwaukee in the late 1950s and early 1960s.

Premise

Laverne and Shirley work as bottle-cappers at the fictional Shotz Brewery in Milwaukee. The series follows their dating misadventures, their ambitions beyond factory work, and their neighbours: the greaser duo Lenny and Squiggy, Laverne's father Frank, and landlady Edna Babish. The opening credits, with the characters skipping down the street chanting a Yiddish-American hopscotch rhyme ("Schlemiel! Schlimazel! Hasenpfeffer Incorporated!"), became iconic. In the sixth season the characters moved to Burbank, California.

Reception and legacy

The show was an immediate ratings hit, becoming the most-watched American television programme in the 1977–78 and 1978–79 seasons, and was nominated for two Golden Globe Awards. It remains a landmark of 1970s television comedy, notable for centring on two independent blue-collar women.
