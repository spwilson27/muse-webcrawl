Alberta clipper

An Alberta clipper is a fast-moving low-pressure system that originates in the Canadian province of Alberta (or nearby) and tracks southeastward across the Canadian Prairies and the northern United States, typically during winter.

Clippers generally carry limited moisture and produce light snow, but they are followed by sharp outbreaks of Arctic air, strong winds and blowing snow, and can trigger lake-effect snow downwind of the Great Lakes. Several clippers in succession can keep a region locked in a wintry pattern.

The storms take their name from the clipper ships of the nineteenth century, noted for their speed, and the same term is applied to similar systems from other provinces, such as Saskatchewan screamers and Manitoba maulers.
