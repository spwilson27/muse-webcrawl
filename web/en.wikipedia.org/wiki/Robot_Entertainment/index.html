Robot Entertainment

Robot Entertainment is an American video game developer and publisher based in Plano, Texas. It was founded in February 2009 by former staff of Ensemble Studios, including co-founder Tony Goodman, after Microsoft closed that studio. Many of its early employees had worked on the Age of Empires series.

History

Robot's first project continued support for Age of Empires III, and it subsequently developed Age of Empires Online with Gas Powered Games. The studio found success with its own intellectual property, the Orcs Must Die! series of action tower-defence games, beginning in 2011, followed by Orcs Must Die! 2 (2012), the free-to-play Orcs Must Die! Unchained, Hero Academy, and Orcs Must Die! 3 (2020), initially a Stadia exclusive. In 2025 the studio released Orcs Must Die! Deathtrap.
