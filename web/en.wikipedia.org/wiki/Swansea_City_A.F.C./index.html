Swansea City A.F.C.

Full name: Swansea City Association Football Club

Nickname: The Swans, Jacks

Founded: 1912 (as Swansea Town)

Ground: Swansea.com Stadium

League: EFL Championship

Swansea City Association Football Club is a Welsh professional football club based in Swansea, Wales. Founded in 1912 as Swansea Town, the club joined the English Football League in 1921 and changed its name to Swansea City in 1969 after Swansea gained city status. The club plays its home matches at the Swansea.com Stadium, having moved from the Vetch Field in 2005.

History

Swansea first reached the top flight under John Toshack, rising from the Fourth Division to finish sixth in the First Division in 1982. After a rapid decline that nearly took the club out of the league in 2003, Swansea rebuilt and won promotion to the Premier League in 2011 under Brendan Rodgers, becoming the first Welsh club to play in the competition. Under Michael Laudrup, the club won the League Cup in 2013, defeating Bradford City 5–0 in the final, and competed in the UEFA Europa League. Swansea were relegated from the Premier League in 2018.

Rivalries and identity

The club's principal rivalry is with Cardiff City, contested in the South Wales derby. Supporters are known as the Jack Army. A supporters' trust held a significant ownership stake in the club during its rise through the divisions, a model widely praised in British football.

Honours

  • League Cup winners: 2012–13
  • Football League Trophy winners: 1993–94, 2005–06
  • Welsh Cup winners: 10 times
