List of active Royal Navy ships

The List of active Royal Navy ships lists the commissioned vessels of the United Kingdom's Royal Navy, together with the ships of the Royal Fleet Auxiliary that support them. The modern fleet is centred on two Queen Elizabeth-class aircraft carriers, a ballistic-missile submarine deterrent, guided-missile destroyers and frigates, and a worldwide network of patrol vessels.

Fleet overview

As of the mid-2020s the Royal Navy operates roughly 70 commissioned ships and submarines, supported by about a dozen Royal Fleet Auxiliary vessels including Tide-class tankers and the aviation training ship RFA Argus. Future construction includes the Dreadnought-class deterrent submarines, Type 26 and Type 31 frigates, and Fleet Solid Support ships.
