Walt Disney Studios (Burbank)

The Walt Disney Studios is an American film studio complex located at 500 South Buena Vista Street in Burbank, California. It has served as the corporate headquarters of The Walt Disney Company media conglomerate since its construction in 1940 and is the only major film studio in Hollywood not to offer regular public tours to the general public.

History

Funded by the success of Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937), Walt Disney purchased the Burbank site and built a modern studio designed for the animation process. The lot includes the Animation Building (where Roy E. Disney's office, with its Sorcerer Mickey hat feature, sits), sound stages, the Team Disney building designed by Michael Graves, and the Frank G. Wells Building.

Facilities

The studio houses Walt Disney Pictures, Walt Disney Animation Studios, and numerous corporate offices. Many classic Disney films were produced on the lot, and its stages have hosted television productions as well.
