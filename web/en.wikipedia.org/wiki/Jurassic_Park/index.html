Jurassic Park

Jurassic Park is a 1993 American science fiction action film directed by Steven Spielberg, based on Michael Crichton's 1990 novel. It stars Sam Neill, Laura Dern, Jeff Goldblum and Richard Attenborough as John Hammond, whose company InGen has cloned dinosaurs from DNA in fossilised mosquitoes and built a theme park on the fictional Isla Nublar.

Plot and production

When sabotage by disgruntled programmer Dennis Nedry disables the park's power, the dinosaurs escape and the preview tour turns into a struggle for survival. The film pioneered photorealistic computer-generated imagery by Industrial Light & Magic combined with Stan Winston's animatronics. It grossed over $1 billion worldwide including reissues, won three Academy Awards, and spawned one of the highest-grossing media franchises, including the sequels The Lost World (1997) and the Jurassic World series.
