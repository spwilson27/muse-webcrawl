Great Mosque of Córdoba (disambiguation)

Great Mosque of Córdoba may refer to:

  • Great Mosque of Córdoba (Mezquita-Catedral de Córdoba), the famous mosque-cathedral in Córdoba, Andalusia, Spain, begun in 784 under Abd al-Rahman I.
  • Mosque–Cathedral of Córdoba, an alternative name for the same building.
  • Córdoba Cathedral, Argentina, the cathedral of Córdoba, Argentina, sometimes confused with the Spanish monument.
