Roboto is a neo-grotesque sans-serif typeface family developed by Google as the system font for its mobile operating system Android, and released in 2011 for Android 4.0 "Ice Cream Sandwich". It was designed by Christian Robertson, an interface designer who previously worked at Google.

Design

Roboto has a dual nature: it has a mechanical skeleton and the forms are largely geometric, while the letterforms feature friendly and open curves. Google describes the font as "modern, approachable, and emotional". The entire font family was officially made available for free download on January 12, 2012, and a redesigned version was released in 2014 for Android 5.0 "Lollipop", with rounder letterforms and expanded weights.

Usage

Roboto is the default font on Android and many Google services. In 2014, Matias Duarte announced that Roboto would become the standard typeface across Google's platforms as part of the Material Design language. Variants include Roboto Condensed, Roboto Mono (a monospaced version), and Roboto Slab (a slab serif version). Since 2013, Roboto has also been the default font in many of Google's web products, including Google Maps and Google Play.
