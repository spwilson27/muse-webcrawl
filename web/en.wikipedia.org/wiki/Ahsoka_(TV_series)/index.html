Star Wars: Ahsoka, or simply Ahsoka, is an American space fantasy television series created by Dave Filoni for the streaming service Disney+. It is part of the Star Wars franchise and a spin-off from the series The Mandalorian, following the character Ahsoka Tano, the former Padawan of Anakin Skywalker, in the years after the fall of the Empire, alongside other characters from the animated series Star Wars Rebels.

Premise

After the fall of the Galactic Empire, former Jedi Knight Ahsoka Tano investigates an emerging threat to the vulnerable New Republic: the search for the missing Grand Admiral Thrawn, whose return as "heir to the Empire" could restart the war. Ahsoka reunites with her former apprentice Sabine Wren and General Hera Syndulla; their trail leads to a star map sought by Thrawn's allies Morgan Elsbeth and the mercenaries Baylan Skoll and Shin Hati, and ultimately to another galaxy, where Thrawn and Ezra Bridger have been exiled since the events of Rebels.

Cast and production

Rosario Dawson stars as Ahsoka Tano, reprising the role from The Mandalorian and The Book of Boba Fett. She is joined by Natasha Liu Bordizzo as Sabine Wren, Mary Elizabeth Winstead as Hera Syndulla, Eman Esfandi as Ezra Bridger, Lars Mikkelsen reprising Grand Admiral Thrawn from animation, Ray Stevenson as Baylan Skoll, Ivanna Sakhno as Shin Hati, Diana Lee Inosanto as Morgan Elsbeth, and David Tennant voicing the droid Huyang; Hayden Christensen appears as Anakin Skywalker. The series was announced in December 2020, filmed in Los Angeles using StageCraft virtual production technology, and premiered on August 22, 2023, with its eight-episode first season concluding on October 3, 2023.

Reception and future

The season received generally positive reviews, with praise for Dawson's performance, Stevenson's Skoll, and its treatment of the Rebels characters, alongside criticism of pacing and accessibility to viewers new to the animated series. Lucasfilm reported strong streaming viewership for the premiere. A second season was confirmed in development in January 2024, alongside Filoni's planned theatrical film tying together the interlinked series.
