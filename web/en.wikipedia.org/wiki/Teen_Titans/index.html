Teen Titans

The Teen Titans are a superhero team appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Bob Haney and artist Bruno Premiani, the team first appeared in The Brave and the Bold #54 (1964) as a "junior Justice League" of teenage sidekicks, including Robin, Kid Flash and Aqualad.

The New Teen Titans

The team's most acclaimed era began with The New Teen Titans (1980) by writer Marv Wolfman and artist George Pérez. The revamped roster combined founding members with new characters — Cyborg, Starfire, Raven and Beast Boy (Changeling) — and featured mature, character-driven stories. The storyline "The Judas Contract" and the villain Deathstroke (Slade Wilson), introduced in issue #2, became landmarks of the series, which rivalled Marvel's X-Men in popularity during the 1980s.

Members

Core members across incarnations include Robin/Nightwing (Dick Grayson), Wonder Girl (Donna Troy), Kid Flash (Wally West), Speedy/Arsenal (Roy Harper), Beast Boy, Cyborg, Raven and Starfire.

In other media

The team headlined the animated series Teen Titans (2003–2006) and its comedic successor Teen Titans Go! (2013–present), as well as the live-action streaming series Titans (2018–2023).
