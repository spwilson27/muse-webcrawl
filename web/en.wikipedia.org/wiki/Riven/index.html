Riven (marketed as Riven: The Sequel to Myst) is a puzzle adventure game developed by Cyan and published in 1997 for Microsoft Windows and Mac OS. Like its predecessor Myst (1993), it presents a richly detailed world through pre-rendered imagery, with the player exploring by clicking between nodes and solving environmental puzzles.

Story

The player, the Stranger, is sent by Atrus to the dying Age of Riven — five islands splitting apart — to rescue Atrus's wife Catherine from his tyrannical father Gehn, who rules Riven as a god and seeks the power of the D'ni linking books. The game's puzzles are woven into the world's invented culture, language, and base-5 numbering system.

Reception and legacy

Riven was critically acclaimed for its artistic design and intricate puzzles, selling several million copies, though some reviewers found its difficulty steep. A real-time 3D remake, Riven: New Discoveries from Cyan, was released by Cyan in June 2024.
