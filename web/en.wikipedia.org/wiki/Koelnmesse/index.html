Koelnmesse

Koelnmesse GmbH is an international trade fair and exhibition company based in Cologne, Germany. Founded in 1924, it organises around 80 trade fairs, exhibitions and events each year in Cologne and in markets abroad, and is one of the largest trade fair organisers in the world. Its exhibition centre in the Deutz district, across the Rhine from Cologne Cathedral, offers roughly 284,000 m² of hall space plus extensive outdoor grounds.

History

The company's first exhibition halls were built in 1924 on the Deutz fairgrounds under Cologne's mayor Konrad Adenauer; the distinctive Messeturm (fair tower) and the Rheinhallen date from 1928. Destroyed in the Second World War, the grounds were rebuilt and expanded repeatedly, including the addition of new northern halls in the 2000s. The Rheinhallen were later leased to the broadcaster RTL.

Major fairs

Koelnmesse hosts some of the world's leading sector fairs, including gamescom (video games, the world's largest gaming event by attendance), photokina (imaging, held until its suspension in 2020), Anuga (food), imm cologne (furniture and interiors), IDS (International Dental Show), dmexco (digital marketing) and Art Cologne, one of the oldest art fairs in the world. The company also organises trade fairs abroad, particularly in Asia and Latin America.
