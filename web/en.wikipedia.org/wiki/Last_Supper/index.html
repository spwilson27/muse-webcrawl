Last Supper

The Last Supper is the final meal that, in the Gospel accounts, Jesus shared with his apostles in Jerusalem before his crucifixion. It is commemorated by Christians, especially on Maundy Thursday, and is the scriptural basis for the Eucharist.

Date1st century AD, Jerusalem (tradition)
ParticipantsJesus and the Twelve Apostles
CommemoratedMaundy Thursday; Eucharist

Accounts

The Synoptic Gospels describe a Passover meal at which Jesus blessed bread and wine, identifying them with his body and blood and commanding remembrance; John's Gospel instead recounts the washing of the disciples' feet and the farewell discourse, and places the meal before Passover. During the meal Jesus predicted his betrayal by Judas Iscariot and Peter's denials.

In art

The Last Supper is one of the most depicted subjects in Western art. Leonardo da Vinci's mural (1495–1498) in the refectory of Santa Maria delle Grazie, Milan, showing the moment Jesus announces the betrayal, is the most famous version; earlier and later treatments include works by Tintoretto, and Salvador Dalí's The Sacrament of the Last Supper.
