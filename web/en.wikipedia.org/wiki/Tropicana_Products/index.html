Tropicana Products, Inc. is an American multinational fruit-based beverage company founded in 1947 by Anthony T. Rossi in Bradenton, Florida. It is best known for its orange juice, sold under the Tropicana brand, including the flagship "Pure Premium" not-from-concentrate line.

History

Rossi, an Italian immigrant, began by delivering fresh fruit gift boxes and developed a process for pasteurising and shipping 100% pure orange juice. Tropicana pioneered flash pasteurisation, refrigerated rail transport of juice (including its dedicated "Juice Train" to the New York market), and the aseptic carton. The brand's mascot, Tropic-Ana, an orange-topped island girl, appeared from 1951 to 1985.

Ownership

Beatrice Foods acquired Tropicana in 1978; Seagram bought it in 1988, and PepsiCo acquired it in 1998. In 2021 PepsiCo agreed to sell Tropicana and related juice brands to the private-equity firm PAI Partners, forming the Tropicana Brands Group joint venture, completed in 2022, with PepsiCo retaining a minority stake.
