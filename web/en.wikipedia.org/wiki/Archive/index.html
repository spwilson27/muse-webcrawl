Archive

An archive is an accumulation of historical records or materials — primary sources created during the activities of individuals and organisations — preserved for their long-term value. The term also denotes the institution or location holding them.

Types

Archives include national archives (e.g. the UK National Archives, US NARA), local record offices, university and business archives, film and sound archives, and digital archives such as the Internet Archive. Archivists appraise, arrange, describe and preserve records.

Principles

Core principles include provenance and original order. Finding aids and catalogues enable access for historians, genealogists doing family history, and the public.

Digital preservation

Digitisation improves access but raises challenges of format obsolescence, authenticity, and scale. See library and heraldry sources held in archives.
