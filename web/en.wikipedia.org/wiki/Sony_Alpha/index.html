Sony Alpha

Sony Alpha is the English-language rendering of Sony α, Sony's brand of digital interchangeable-lens cameras. Introduced in 2006 following Sony's acquisition of Konica Minolta's camera business, the Alpha system began with A-mount digital SLRs and later shifted decisively to E-mount mirrorless cameras, which helped establish mirrorless systems as the professional standard in the 2010s.

Overview

The Alpha line encompasses APS-C and full-frame bodies, including the α6000 series for enthusiasts, the α7 series of full-frame cameras, the α9 series optimised for speed and sports, and the flagship α1. Sony pairs the bodies with a large native lens catalogue and sensors that are also supplied to other manufacturers.

Technology

Alpha cameras are known for fast hybrid autofocus with eye detection, in-body image stabilisation on many models, high-resolution sensors, and strong video features such as 4K and 8K recording on flagship bodies. These capabilities attracted many professional photographers and videographers away from traditional DSLR systems.
