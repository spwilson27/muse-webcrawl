Williamina Fleming

Williamina Paton Stevens Fleming (15 May 1857 – 21 May 1911) was a Scottish astronomer who helped develop a common system for classifying stars and discovered the Horsehead Nebula.

Born: 1857, Dundee, Scotland
Died: 1911, Boston
Known for: Stellar classification, Horsehead Nebula

Career

Emigrating to Boston and working as a maid for Harvard Observatory director Edward Pickering, Fleming was hired as one of the Harvard Computers. She devised the Pickering–Fleming spectral classification, catalogued over 10,000 stars for the Draper Catalogue, discovered 59 nebulae, white dwarfs, and variable stars, and in 1888 noted the Horsehead Nebula on a photographic plate.
