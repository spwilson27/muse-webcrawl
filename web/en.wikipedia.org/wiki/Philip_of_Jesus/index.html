Philip of Jesus

Philip of Jesus (Spanish: Felipe de Jesús; 1572 – 5 February 1597) was a Novohispanic Franciscan missionary and martyr. He is the patron saint of Mexico City and one of the Twenty-six Martyrs of Japan.

Born in Mexico City, Philip joined the Franciscans and travelled to the Philippines. On a voyage back to Mexico his ship was driven to Japan, where the missionaries aboard were arrested under orders of Toyotomi Hideyoshi and executed by crucifixion at Nagasaki. He was canonised by Pope Pius IX in 1862.
