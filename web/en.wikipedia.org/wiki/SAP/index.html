SAP SE is a German multinational software company based in Walldorf, Baden-Württemberg, and one of the world's largest providers of enterprise application software. Founded in 1972 by five former IBM engineers, SAP is best known for its enterprise resource planning (ERP) software, used by organisations worldwide to manage business operations and customer relations.

History

SAP was founded as Systemanalyse und Programmentwicklung ("System Analysis and Program Development") by Dietmar Hopp, Hasso Plattner, Hans-Werner Hector, Klaus Tschira and Claus Wellenreuther. Its early products, SAP R/2 for mainframes and SAP R/3 for client–server architectures, established the company as a leader in standardised business software. The company's name later derived from the German Systeme, Anwendungen und Produkte in der Datenverarbeitung ("Systems, Applications and Products in Data Processing").

In the 2010s SAP moved towards in-memory computing with its SAP HANA database platform and launched SAP S/4HANA, its next-generation ERP suite. The company has expanded through acquisitions including Business Objects, Sybase, SuccessFactors, Ariba, Concur and Qualtrics, and has increasingly shifted its business to cloud subscriptions.

Products

SAP's portfolio covers ERP, supply chain management, procurement, human capital management, customer experience and business analytics software, serving hundreds of thousands of customers in more than 180 countries.
