FIS Ski Jumping World Cup

The FIS Ski Jumping World Cup is the world's highest level of ski jumping competition, organised annually by the International Ski and Snowboard Federation (FIS) since the 1979–80 season. A women's World Cup was added in the 2011–12 season.

Ski jumping is scored on distance and style.

Competition

Events are held on normal, large, and ski flying hills at venues across Europe, North America, and Asia. Points are awarded for each competition (100 points to the winner down to 1 point for 30th place), and the skier with the most points at the end of the season wins the World Cup overall title and the crystal globe. The season traditionally includes the prestigious Four Hills Tournament around New Year and, in some years, the Raw Air and Planica7 series.

History

Notable champions include Matti Nykänen and Adam Małysz, each with four overall titles, and Sara Takanashi, the most successful woman in the competition's history. Nations such as Austria, Norway, Finland, Poland, Germany, and Japan have produced many of the sport's leading jumpers.
