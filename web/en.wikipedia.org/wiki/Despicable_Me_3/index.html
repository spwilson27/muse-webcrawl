Despicable Me 3 is a 2017 American animated comedy film produced by Illumination and distributed by Universal Pictures. It is the sequel to Despicable Me 2 (2013) and the third instalment in the Despicable Me franchise.

Plot

After Gru and Lucy are dismissed from the Anti-Villain League for failing to capture Balthazar Bratt, a former child star turned diamond-obsessed supervillain, Gru learns he has a long-lost twin brother, Dru, who invites the family to his mansion in Freedonia. Dru wants to follow in the family's villainous tradition, and the brothers team up to recover the Dumont Diamond from Bratt.

Reception

The film received mixed reviews from critics but grossed over $1 billion worldwide, making it the fourth-highest-grossing film of 2017 and, at the time, the second-highest-grossing animated film of the year. A sequel, Despicable Me 4, was released in 2024.
