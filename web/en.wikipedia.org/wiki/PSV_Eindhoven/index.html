Philips Sport Vereniging (English: Philips Sports Association), abbreviated as PSV and internationally known as PSV Eindhoven, is a Dutch professional football club based in Eindhoven, North Brabant. It plays in the Eredivisie, the top tier of Dutch football. It is one of the country's "big three" clubs, alongside Ajax and Feyenoord.

History

The club was founded on 31 August 1913 by employees of the Philips electronics company as a sports association for its workers, and retains close ties with the company. PSV has won the Eredivisie championship more than twenty times and the KNVB Cup on numerous occasions. Its greatest successes came in Europe: PSV won the UEFA Cup in 1978 and the European Cup in 1988 under coach Guus Hiddink, completing a treble that year with the league and cup. Famous players associated with the club include Ruud Gullit, Romário, Ronaldo, and Ruud van Nistelrooy.

Stadium

PSV plays its home matches at the Philips Stadion, located in the Strijp district of Eindhoven, close to the city centre, with a capacity of about 36,500.

Rivalries

PSV's principal rivals are Ajax (in matches known as De Topper) and Feyenoord.
