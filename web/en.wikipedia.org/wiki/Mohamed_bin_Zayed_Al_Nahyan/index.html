Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan (born 11 March 1961), commonly known by his initials MBZ, is the third president of the United Arab Emirates and the ruler of Abu Dhabi. He is the third son of Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan, the founder and first president of the UAE.

Early life and military career

Mohamed bin Zayed was born in Al Ain in 1961. He was educated in Abu Dhabi and Al Ain and later attended the Royal Military Academy Sandhurst in the United Kingdom, graduating in 1979. He went on to serve as a pilot in the UAE Air Force and held senior military posts, becoming Chief of Staff of the UAE Armed Forces in 1993 and Deputy Supreme Commander in 2005.

Ruler of Abu Dhabi and President

In November 2004, following the death of his father, his elder brother Khalifa became ruler of Abu Dhabi and Mohamed became Crown Prince. Over the following years he became the country's de facto leader as Khalifa's health declined. On 13 May 2022 Khalifa died, and Mohamed succeeded him as ruler of Abu Dhabi; the next day the Federal Supreme Council elected him President of the United Arab Emirates.

Policies

Mohamed bin Zayed has overseen economic diversification efforts in Abu Dhabi, the expansion of the UAE's armed forces, and an assertive regional foreign policy. He played a central role in the Abraham Accords of 2020, which normalised relations between the UAE and Israel.
