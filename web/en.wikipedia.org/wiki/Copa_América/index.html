The Copa América (Spanish for "America Cup") is the main men's football tournament contested among national teams from South America. Organized by CONMEBOL since 1916, it is the oldest existing continental football competition, first held in Argentina to celebrate the centenary of that country's independence. Until 1975 it was known as the South American Championship (Campeonato Sudamericano). The ten CONMEBOL members compete, with guest teams from other confederations — notably Mexico, the United States, Costa Rica, Jamaica and Japan — often invited to complete the field.

History

Uruguay won the first tournament in 1916 and dominated the early editions. The competition was held irregularly, sometimes annually and sometimes with gaps of several years, and extra "extraordinary" editions were staged. Since 2007 it has settled into a four-year cycle aligned with the UEFA European Championship; the special centenary edition, Copa América Centenario, was held in the United States in 2016 and won by Chile. The 2024 edition, also in the United States with 16 teams (including six CONCACAF guests), was won by Argentina, who beat Colombia 1–0 in the final in Miami Gardens.

Results and records

Argentina is the most successful team with 16 titles (most recently 2021 and 2024), followed by Uruguay with 15 and Brazil with 9; Chile, Paraguay and Peru have won twice each, and Colombia and Bolivia once each. Ecuador and Venezuela are the only CONMEBOL members never to have won it. Argentina's Norberto "Tucho" Méndez and Brazil's Zizinho share the record for most goals in the tournament's history with 17 each. Lionel Messi holds the records for most appearances and most assists in the competition.

Format

The modern format divides teams into groups, with the best advancing to knockout quarter-finals, semi-finals and a final; uniquely among major tournaments, tied knockout matches (other than the final) may go directly to penalties without extra time in some editions. The champion is traditionally invited to intercontinental competitions such as the Finalissima against the European champions.
