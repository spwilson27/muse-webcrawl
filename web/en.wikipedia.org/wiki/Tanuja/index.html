Tanuja

Born: Tanuja Samarth, 23 September 1943, Bombay, British India

Occupation: Actress

Years active: 1950–present

Spouse: Shomu Mukherjee (m. 1973; died 2008)

Children: Kajol, Tanishaa Mukerji

Tanuja Samarth, known mononymously as Tanuja (born 23 September 1943), is an Indian actress who works predominantly in Hindi films, with notable appearances in Marathi and Bengali cinema. She is a member of the Mukherjee–Samarth film family, the daughter of actress Shobhna Samarth and the younger sister of actress Nutan.

Career

Tanuja began as a child artist in Hamari Beti (1950), which launched her sister Nutan. Her adult debut came with Chhabili (1960) and Memdidi (1961). She rose to popularity in the 1960s with films such as Baharen Phir Bhi Aayengi (1966), Jewel Thief (1967), and Jeene Ki Raah (1969). Her performance in the Bengali film Deya Neya (1963), opposite Uttam Kumar, was widely praised.

In the 1970s she moved increasingly to strong supporting roles in films such as Haathi Mere Saathi (1971), Anubhav (1971), and Mere Jeevan Saathi (1972), continuing to work steadily in later decades in character parts and on television.

Personal life

Tanuja married filmmaker Shomu Mukherjee, youngest son of producer Sashadhar Mukherjee, in 1973. Their daughters Kajol and Tanishaa Mukerji both became actresses; Kajol is one of Hindi cinema's most acclaimed stars.

Awards

Tanuja won the Filmfare Award for Best Supporting Actress for Paisa Ya Pyar (1969), and received the Filmfare Lifetime Achievement Award in 2014. She was also honoured for her work in Marathi cinema.
