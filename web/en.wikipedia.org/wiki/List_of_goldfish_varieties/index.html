List of goldfish varieties

The goldfish (Carassius auratus) has been bred in China for over a thousand years and in Japan and the West for centuries, producing a wide range of goldfish varieties. Varieties are commonly divided into single-tailed ("common" types) and double-tailed "fancy" goldfish, which have egg-shaped bodies, paired anal and caudal fins, and features such as head growths (wen), telescope or upturned eyes, and absent dorsal fins.

Single-tailed varieties

VarietyCharacteristics
Common goldfishStandard streamlined body and single tail; orange, red, white and other colours; hardy pond fish.
CometLong, deeply forked single tail; developed in the United States in the 1880s.
ShubunkinCalico (nacreous) colouration with single tail; London, Bristol and American forms.
Tamasaba (Sabao)Japanese fancy type with a round body but a single, flowing tail.
WakinJapanese variety with a double tail but a long, common-type body.

Fancy (double-tailed) varieties

VarietyCharacteristics
FantailEgg-shaped body, high dorsal fin, double tail; the basic fancy form.
RyukinProminent shoulder hump, short deep body; from Japan via the Ryukyu Islands.
VeiltailLong, flowing double tail and tall dorsal fin.
OrandaHood-like head growth (wen) covering the head; the redcap form is white with a red wen.
LionheadExtensive raspberry-like wen and no dorsal fin; developed in China.
Ranchu"King of goldfish" in Japan; like a lionhead but with a more arched back and tucked tail.
TelescopeProtruding eyes on stalks; black form called the black moor.
CelestialEyes turned permanently upward; no dorsal fin.
Bubble eyeFluid-filled sacs beneath upturned eyes; no dorsal fin.
PearlscaleDomed scales with pearl-like deposits; crown pearlscale has a wen.
PompomFleshy outgrowths (nasal bouquets) between the nostrils.
Butterfly tailDouble tail spread horizontally, best viewed from above.
Curled-gillOutward-curling gill covers (a fault in most standards).
Egg-fishDorsal-less fancy type without wen, an ancestor of lionhead types.
TosakinJapanese variety whose undivided double tail fans out horizontally with curled edges.
JikinJapanese variety with an X-shaped ("peacock") tail and red-and-white patterning.

Colour and scale types

  • Scale types: metallic, nacreous (calico/mottled), matt.
  • Colours: red, orange, yellow, white, black, blue, chocolate, panda (black-and-white), kirin and red-and-white (kohaku-style) patterns.
