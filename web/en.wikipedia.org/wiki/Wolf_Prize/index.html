Wolf Prize

The Wolf Prize is an international award granted in Israel since 1978 by the Wolf Foundation, founded by Ricardo Wolf. It is awarded in agriculture, chemistry, mathematics, medicine, physics, and the arts, and is regarded as one of the most prestigious honours in its fields, often seen as a precursor to the Nobel Prize.

History

Ricardo Wolf (1887–1981), a German-born inventor, diplomat and philanthropist who lived in Cuba and later Israel, established the foundation in 1975. Prizes are presented by the President of Israel at the Knesset in Jerusalem.

Laureates

Many Wolf laureates have later won Nobel Prizes, particularly in physics, chemistry and medicine. Each prize includes a diploma and a monetary award. The arts prize rotates annually among architecture, music, painting and sculpture.
