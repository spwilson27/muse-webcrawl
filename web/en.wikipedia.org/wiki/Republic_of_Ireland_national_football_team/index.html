The Republic of Ireland national football team represents the Republic of Ireland in men's international football and is governed by the Football Association of Ireland. The team made its debut at the 1924 Summer Olympics as the Irish Free State. Home matches are played at the Aviva Stadium in Dublin.

History

Ireland qualified for its first major tournament at UEFA Euro 1988 under manager Jack Charlton, famously beating England 1–0 in Stuttgart. The team reached the quarter-finals of the 1990 FIFA World Cup in Italy, its best World Cup performance, and also qualified for the 1994 and 2002 World Cups, reaching the round of 16 on both occasions. Ireland later qualified for Euro 2012 and Euro 2016, reaching the last 16 in 2016 before losing to hosts France.

Notable players

Record goalscorer Robbie Keane, Roy Keane, Paul McGrath, Liam Brady, and Shay Given are among the team's most prominent players.
