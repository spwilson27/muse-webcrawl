Innovate UK

Innovate UK is the United Kingdom's innovation agency. Established in 2004 and operating under its current name since 2014 (previously the Technology Strategy Board), it is a non-departmental public body that provides funding and support to science and technology developments judged to have commercial potential. Since April 2018 it has formed part of UK Research and Innovation (UKRI), the body that brings together the UK's research councils and innovation funding.

Activities

Innovate UK runs funding competitions through which companies, usually in collaboration with universities or other firms, can win grants for research, development, and feasibility studies. It manages the Catapult network — centres such as the High Value Manufacturing Catapult and the Cell and Gene Therapy Catapult that provide facilities and expertise to bridge research and commercialization. Through Innovate UK Edge (formerly the Enterprise Europe Network), it offers growth and internationalization support to small and medium-sized enterprises.

Programs

Notable initiatives include the Industrial Strategy Challenge Fund, launched in 2017 to direct investment toward areas such as artificial intelligence, clean growth, healthy ageing, and the future of mobility; the Smart Grants programme for disruptive commercial ideas; and the Knowledge Transfer Partnerships, which place graduates in companies to carry out innovation projects supervised by academics.
