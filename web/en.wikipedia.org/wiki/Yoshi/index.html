Yoshi is a fictional character in Nintendo's Mario video game franchise, a friendly dinosaur-like creature who appears as one of Mario's closest allies and the star of his own series of platform and puzzle games. Yoshi debuted in Super Mario World (1990) for the Super Nintendo Entertainment System, where he served as a rideable mount for Mario and Luigi.

Characteristics

Yoshis are bipedal creatures with large noses, long sticky tongues, and a saddle-like shell on their backs. They hatch from distinctive spotted eggs and come in many colours, green being the most common. Yoshi can swallow enemies with his tongue and convert them into eggs, which he can throw as projectiles; he can also perform a "flutter jump" to gain extra height and pound the ground. His vocalisations consist largely of his own name and cheerful noises, provided by composer Kazumi Totaka using pitch-shifted samples.

Appearances

After his ensemble role in Super Mario World and its sequel, Yoshi headlined Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island (1995), a prequel in which a clan of Yoshis carries the infant Mario to safety. His own series continued with Yoshi's Story (1997), Yoshi's Woolly World (2015) and Yoshi's Crafted World (2019), alongside puzzle games such as Yoshi's Cookie and Tetris Attack. Yoshi is a regular playable character in the Mario Kart, Mario Party, Mario sports and Super Smash Bros. series.

Creation and reception

Shigeru Miyamoto had wanted Mario to ride a dinosaur since the original Super Mario Bros., but technical limits of the NES delayed the idea until the SNES. Designer Shigefumi Hino developed the character from a sketch by Miyamoto. Yoshi has become one of Nintendo's most recognisable mascots; Yoshi's Island is widely regarded as one of the greatest platformers ever made, and the character's name entered Japanese popular culture broadly enough to generate occasional confusion with real people and places of similar name.
