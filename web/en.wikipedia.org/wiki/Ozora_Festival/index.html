Ozora Festival

GenrePsychedelic trance, electronic music
DatesLate July / early August
LocationOzora, near Dádpuszta, Fejér County, Hungary
Years active2004–2019, 2022–present
AttendanceTens of thousands

The Ozora Festival, commonly known simply as Ozora, is an annual psychedelic trance festival held in Ozora, near the village of Dádpuszta in Fejér County, Hungary. It is one of the largest psychedelic trance festivals in the world, drawing visitors from dozens of countries.

The festival site at Dádpuszta first hosted the Solipse solar eclipse festival in 1999. The first Ozora Festival was held in 2004, and it has grown into a week-long event combining music stages (including the Main Stage, the Dome, and the Pumpui stage), art installations, workshops, healing areas, a market, and talks and performances in its cultural village areas.

Description

Ozora is known for its elaborate stage design and decorations, natural setting in a valley of fields, maize and sunflower crops, and its emphasis on psychedelic art and culture. Beyond the music programme, the festival hosts activities such as yoga, meditation, circus arts, lectures, and film screenings.

The festival was cancelled in 2020 and 2021 because of the COVID-19 pandemic, returning in 2022.
