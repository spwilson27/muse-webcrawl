Santa Anita Handicap

Santa Anita Handicap
Location: Santa Anita Park, Arcadia, California
Inaugurated: 1935
Distance: 1¼ miles (10 furlongs), dirt
Grade: I

The Santa Anita Handicap, often called the "Big 'Cap", is an American Thoroughbred horse race held annually at Santa Anita Park in Arcadia, California. A Grade I event for horses four years old and up, it is run over 1¼ miles on dirt and was for many years one of the richest races in North America.

History

The race was inaugurated in 1935 with a then-record purse of $100,000, promoted as the "Hundred-Grander". Its first famous winner was Seabiscuit, who, after losing by a nose in 1937 and 1938, finally won in 1940 in what proved to be his final race. Other celebrated winners include Round Table, Ack Ack, Affirmed, Spectacular Bid, John Henry (twice), Alysheba, Best Pal, Tiznow, Lava Man (twice), Game On Dude (three times), Accelerate, and Flightline in 2023.

Records

Game On Dude is the only three-time winner (2011, 2013, 2014). The race traditionally anchors Santa Anita's winter-spring meeting and closes the Strub Series for older horses.
