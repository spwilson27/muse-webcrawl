Dave McKean

David McKean (born 29 December 1963) is an English artist, photographer, filmmaker and musician. His work incorporates drawing, painting, photography, collage, found objects, digital art and sculpture. He is best known for his long collaboration with writer Neil Gaiman, including illustrating the graphic novel Violent Cases and providing the covers and design for the entire run of The Sandman comic book series.

Career

With Gaiman, McKean also created Black Orchid, Signal to Noise, Mr. Punch, the picture books The Day I Swapped My Dad for Two Goldfish, The Wolves in the Walls and Crazy Hair, and the novel Coraline's illustrated edition. His solo graphic novel Cages (1990–1996) is considered a landmark of the form. In film he directed MirrorMask (2005), written by Gaiman, as well as N[eon] and Luna (2014). McKean has designed album covers for artists including Tori Amos, Alice Cooper, Counting Crows and Bill Bruford, and book covers for Jonathan Carroll and Stephen King, among many others.

Style

McKean's mixed-media style blends expressive line work with manipulated photography and typography, influencing a generation of comics cover artists and illustrators.
