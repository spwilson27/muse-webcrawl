Zaire

Zaire, officially the Republic of Zaire, was the name of the Democratic Republic of the Congo from 1971 to 1997 under the authoritarian regime of Mobutu Sese Seko.

History

Mobutu, in power since 1965, renamed the country as part of his Authenticité campaign, Africanising names and dress. Zaire was a one-party state under the Popular Movement of the Revolution, marked by a personality cult, corruption (kleptocracy) and Cold War support from the West.

Fall

After the Rwandan genocide (1994) spilled into eastern Zaire, a rebellion led by Laurent-Désiré Kabila with Rwandan and Ugandan backing advanced across the country. Mobutu fled in May 1997, Kabila took Kinshasa, and the name Democratic Republic of the Congo was restored. See also Patrice Lumumba.
