NBC News is the news division of the American broadcaster NBC, with roots in network radio news of the 1920s–30s. Its flagship programmes include NBC Nightly News, the Today show, the Sunday interview programme Meet the Press — the longest-running show in American television history — and the newsmagazine Dateline.

History

NBC's television news grew up with the medium: the Camel Newsreel Theatre, anchors Chet Huntley and David Brinkley, and later Tom Brokaw defined eras of the Nightly News. Andrea Mitchell, Lester Holt and many others have fronted its modern coverage.

Digital and cable

NBC News operates nbcnews.com, the streaming channel NBC News Now, and historically supplied journalism to MSNBC and CNBC. Its investigative unit and its string of foreign bureaus support both broadcast and digital output.
