Tannersville, New York

Tannersville
Type: Village
Location: Greene County, New York, United States
Elevation: approx. 579 m (1,900 ft)

Tannersville is a village in Greene County, New York, United States, located within the town of Hunter in the northern Catskill Mountains. It sits along New York State Route 23A at a high valley elevation, which made it a popular summer resort destination in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries.

History

The village takes its name from the tanneries that once processed hemlock bark from the surrounding forests for the leather industry — an industry that largely vanished as the hemlock stands were depleted. With the arrival of the railroad, Tannersville reoriented toward tourism, hosting grand hotels and boarding houses catering to visitors from New York City escaping the summer heat. It was incorporated as a village in 1895.

Attractions

Today Tannersville's Main Street is known for its brightly painted storefronts, an initiative begun in the early 2000s with support from the Hunter Foundation. The village serves as a gateway to nearby hiking in the Catskill Park, including trails to Kaaterskill Falls and the escarpment of the eastern Catskills, and to the Hunter Mountain ski area in the adjacent town.

Geography

Tannersville lies in the Schoharie Creek valley. According to general references, the village covers a small area at the junction of Route 23A and Route 214, surrounded by forested mountain terrain within the Catskill Park's blue line.
