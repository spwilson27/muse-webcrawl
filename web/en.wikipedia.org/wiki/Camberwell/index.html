Camberwell

Camberwell is a district of South London, England, in the London Borough of Southwark, about 2.7 miles southeast of Charing Cross. It was a village until suburban development in the 19th century and remains a busy local centre around Camberwell Green.

History

Camberwell appears in the Domesday Book as Cambrewelle. The parish church of St Giles dates in part to the 12th century. The arrival of trams and railways in the Victorian era transformed it into a residential suburb with notable architecture, including the Salvation Army's William Booth College.

Culture

The area is home to Camberwell College of Arts and the South London Gallery. The rare Camberwell beauty butterfly takes its English name from specimens found locally in the 18th century. Camberwell is known for its diverse community and busy junction at Camberwell Green.
