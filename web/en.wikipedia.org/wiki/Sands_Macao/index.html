Sands Macao
Location: Sé, Macau, China
Opening date: May 18, 2004
Owner: Sands China / Las Vegas Sands
Type: Casino hotel

Sands Macao (Chinese: 金沙娛樂場) is a casino hotel located in Sé, Macau, China. Owned and operated by Sands China, a subsidiary of the American company Las Vegas Sands, it opened on May 18, 2004, becoming the first American-owned casino to operate in Macau after the end of the territory's gaming monopoly.

History

Following the liberalisation of Macau's casino industry in 2002, Las Vegas Sands, chaired by Sheldon Adelson, developed Sands Macao on reclaimed land near the Macau Ferry Terminal. The property reportedly recouped its construction cost within its first year of operation, thanks to intense demand from gamblers arriving from mainland China and Hong Kong. Its success helped prompt the company's much larger developments on the Cotai Strip, including The Venetian Macao.

Facilities

The complex combines a large gaming floor of table games and slot machines with a luxury hotel tower of suites, restaurants, entertainment venues and the Paiza Club for high-stakes players. Its distinctive gold-toned glass facade became a landmark on the Macau Peninsula waterfront.
