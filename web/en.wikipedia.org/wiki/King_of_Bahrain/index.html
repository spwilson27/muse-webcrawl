King of Bahrain

Incumbent: Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa
Style: His Majesty
Heir apparent: Salman bin Hamad Al Khalifa

The King of Bahrain is the monarch and head of state of the Kingdom of Bahrain. The current king is Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa, who succeeded his father, Isa bin Salman Al Khalifa, in 1999.

History

Bahrain has been ruled by the Al Khalifa family since 1783, when the family, part of the Bani Utbah federation, took control of the islands. The rulers used the title Hakim (ruler) until 1971, when Bahrain became independent of Britain and the ruler adopted the title of Emir. In 2002, following the adoption of a new constitution that declared Bahrain a kingdom, the title was elevated to King.

Role and powers

Under the 2002 constitution, the King is head of state, appoints the Prime Minister and ministers, is commander-in-chief of the Bahrain Defence Force, and shares legislative authority with the National Assembly, which consists of the elected Council of Representatives and the appointed Shura Council. The King can issue decrees with the force of law and ratifies all legislation.

List of rulers

Rulers of Bahrain include Ahmed ibn Muhammad ibn Khalifa (1783–1795), Isa bin Salman Al Khalifa (1961–1999, Hakim then Emir), and Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa (from 1999; Emir until 2002, then King).
