Big Merino

LocationGoulburn, New South Wales, Australia
Opened1985
Dimensions15.2 m high, 18 m long

The Big Merino is a large concrete statue of a merino ram in Goulburn, New South Wales, Australia, and one of the country's best-known "big things". It celebrates the region's fine-wool industry.

Description

Standing about 15 metres high, the ram was modelled on a real stud ram named Rambo. The structure contains a gift shop at ground level and a wool exhibition, and visitors can climb inside to look out through the ram's eyes. In 2007 the Big Merino was moved about 800 metres to a new site closer to the Hume Highway interchange.
