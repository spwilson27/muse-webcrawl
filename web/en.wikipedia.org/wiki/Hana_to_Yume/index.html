Hana to Yume

Hana to Yume (花とゆめ, "Flowers and Dreams"), also known as HanaYume, is a Japanese shōjo manga magazine published by Hakusensha. The magazine is published twice a month on the 5th and 20th. It was founded in 1974 and is one of the longest-running and best-known shōjo magazines in Japan.

History

Hana to Yume was launched in 1974 as a monthly magazine and later moved to a semi-monthly schedule. It is aimed primarily at teenage girls and young women, printing long-running romantic and fantasy series in black-and-white with colour opening pages.

Notable series

Many famous shōjo series have been serialised in the magazine, including Glass Mask by Suzue Miuchi, Please Save My Earth by Saki Hiwatari, Fruits Basket by Natsuki Takaya — one of the best-selling shōjo manga of all time — Skip Beat! by Yoshiki Nakamura, Vampire Knight by Matsuri Hino, Yona of the Dawn by Mizuho Kusanagi, and Ourah High School Host Club by Bisco Hatori.

Related magazines

Hakusensha also publishes the sister magazine Bessatsu Hana to Yume and the related shōjo magazine LaLa.
