Oman

Oman, officially the Sultanate of Oman, is a country on the southeastern coast of the Arabian Peninsula, bordered by the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Yemen, facing the Arabian Sea and the Gulf of Oman, and commanding the southern side of the Strait of Hormuz through its Musandam exclave. Its capital is Muscat and its population about 5 million.

Oman is an absolute monarchy; Sultan Qaboos ruled from 1970 until his death in 2020, modernising the once-isolated country with oil wealth while maintaining a famously neutral foreign policy that has made Oman a regional mediator. Historically a maritime empire spanning to Zanzibar, Oman traded in frankincense from Dhofar for millennia. Its landscape runs from Muscat's whitewashed corniche to the fjord-like khors of Musandam and the monsoon-green hills of Salalah.
