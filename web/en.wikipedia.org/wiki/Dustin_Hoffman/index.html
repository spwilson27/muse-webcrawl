Dustin Hoffman

Dustin Lee Hoffman (born August 8, 1937) is an American actor and filmmaker. Known for his versatile portrayals of antiheroes and emotionally vulnerable characters, he is the recipient of numerous accolades, including two Academy Awards, four BAFTA Awards, five Golden Globe Awards, and two Primetime Emmy Awards.

Career

Hoffman gained fame for his breakthrough role as Benjamin Braddock in The Graduate (1967) and went on to star in landmark films including Midnight Cowboy (1969), Little Big Man (1970), Straw Dogs (1971), Lenny (1974), All the President's Men (1976), Marathon Man (1976), Kramer vs. Kramer (1979), Tootsie (1982), Rain Man (1988), Hook (1991) and Wag the Dog (1997). He won the Academy Award for Best Actor for Kramer vs. Kramer and Rain Man.

Later work

In the 2000s Hoffman appeared in Finding Neverland, Meet the Fockers and as the voice of Master Shifu in the Kung Fu Panda films. He made his directorial debut with Quartet (2012). In 2017 several women accused Hoffman of sexual misconduct in the 1970s and 1980s, allegations for which he apologised in part; his career output subsequently diminished.
