Ottis Elwood Toole (March 5, 1947 – September 15, 1996) was an American serial killer and arsonist who was convicted of six murders. A drifter who travelled across the United States, Toole was the sometime accomplice and lover of Henry Lee Lucas; together the two men made sweeping confessions to hundreds of murders, most of which investigators later concluded were fabricated.

Crimes and convictions

Raised in poverty in Jacksonville, Florida, Toole set fires from a young age and spent much of his life in and out of institutions and prisons. He was convicted in the 1980s of several murders in Florida, including the arson-murder of George Sonnenberg and the murders of Ada Johnson and others, receiving death sentences that were overturned on appeal and replaced with life terms. His total confirmed victim count remains disputed because of his habitual false confessions.

Adam Walsh case

Toole repeatedly confessed to—and recanted—the 1981 abduction and murder of six-year-old Adam Walsh, son of America's Most Wanted host John Walsh, who was taken from a shopping mall in Hollywood, Florida. In December 2008, Hollywood police announced that they had concluded Toole was the killer and closed the case, though he was never tried for the crime.

Death

Toole died in Florida State Prison on September 15, 1996, of cirrhosis of the liver, while serving multiple life sentences.
