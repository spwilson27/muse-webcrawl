Natural History Museum of Los Angeles County

The Natural History Museum of Los Angeles County (NHMLAC) is the largest natural and historical museum in the western United States, located in Exposition Park, Los Angeles. Opened in 1913, it holds more than 35 million specimens and artefacts spanning 4.5 billion years.

Collections and sites

Highlights include extensive dinosaur halls, gems and minerals, Native American and Pacific cultures, and the La Brea Tar Pits research. The museum family includes the La Brea Tar Pits and Museum in Hancock Park and the William S. Hart Museum in Newhall. Its Beaux-Arts building with a marble rotunda is listed on the National Register of Historic Places.
