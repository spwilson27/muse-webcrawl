Two and a Half Men

Two and a Half Men is an American television sitcom created by Chuck Lorre and Lee Aronsohn that aired on CBS from September 22, 2003, to February 19, 2015, running for twelve seasons and 262 episodes. It was for much of its run the most-watched comedy on American television.

Premise

The series centres on the Harper brothers: Charlie Harper, a wealthy, hedonistic jingle writer living in a Malibu beach house, and his uptight chiropractor brother Alan, who moves in with his young son Jake after a divorce. Much of the comedy derives from Charlie's womanising, Alan's misfortunes, Jake's naivety, and the brothers' domineering mother Evelyn and sardonic housekeeper Berta.

Cast changes

Charlie Sheen starred as Charlie Harper for eight seasons. Following Sheen's dismissal in 2011 amid a public dispute with the producers, his character was killed off and Ashton Kutcher joined as billionaire Walden Schmidt, who buys the house and lets Alan remain. Angus T. Jones, who played Jake, left as a regular after season ten; Amber Tamblyn joined as Charlie's long-lost daughter Jenny in the final seasons.

Reception

The show won numerous awards, including Emmy Awards for Jon Cryer and guest star Kathy Bates, and was a ratings anchor for CBS's Monday night lineup. It remains widely syndicated worldwide.
