Coffeemaker

[Image placeholder: An electric drip coffeemaker]

A coffeemaker, coffee machine, or coffee brewer is a cooking appliance used to brew coffee. While there are many different types of coffeemakers, the two most common brewing principles use gravity or pressure to move hot water through coffee grounds.

Types

  • Drip brew machines heat water and let it drip through a filter basket of ground coffee into a carafe.
  • Percolators cycle boiling water repeatedly through the grounds.
  • Espresso machines force near-boiling water under pressure through finely ground, compacted coffee.
  • Single-serve brewers (e.g. Keurig, Nespresso) use pre-packaged pods or capsules.
  • French press and moka pot are manual devices often classed with coffeemakers.

History

The first automatic drip coffeemaker, the Wigomat, was patented in Germany in 1954. Mr. Coffee, launched in 1972, popularised the drip machine in American homes, while pod-based systems transformed the market from the 1990s onward.
