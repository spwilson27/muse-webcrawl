Dune (2021 film)

Dune (titled on-screen as Dune: Part One) is a 2021 American epic science fiction film directed by Denis Villeneuve, who co-wrote the screenplay with Jon Spaihts and Eric Roth. Produced by Legendary Pictures and distributed by Warner Bros. Pictures, it is the first of a two-part adaptation of Frank Herbert's 1965 novel Dune, covering roughly the first half of the book.

Plot

In the far future, Duke Leto Atreides accepts control of the desert planet Arrakis, the only source of "spice", a substance vital to interstellar travel. When House Atreides is betrayed and destroyed by the Harkonnens with the secret backing of the Emperor, Leto's son Paul and his mother Jessica escape into the desert and take refuge with the native Fremen, setting Paul on the path toward his destiny.

Cast

The ensemble cast includes Timothée Chalamet as Paul Atreides, Rebecca Ferguson as Lady Jessica, Oscar Isaac as Duke Leto, Josh Brolin as Gurney Halleck, Stellan Skarsgård as Baron Vladimir Harkonnen, Dave Bautista as Glossu Rabban, Zendaya as Chani, Javier Bardem as Stilgar, and Jason Momoa as Duncan Idaho.

Production and release

Principal photography took place in 2019 in locations including Jordan, Abu Dhabi, Norway, and Hungary, with Hans Zimmer composing the score. The film premiered at the Venice International Film Festival in September 2021 and was released theatrically and on HBO Max in the United States in October 2021. It grossed over $400 million worldwide.

Reception and awards

Dune received praise for its direction, visual effects, score, and scope. At the 94th Academy Awards it won six Oscars from ten nominations, including Best Cinematography, Best Production Design, Best Original Score, and Best Visual Effects. Its sequel, Dune: Part Two, was released in 2024.
