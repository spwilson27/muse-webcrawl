Thomas Tull

Thomas Tull (born 1970) is an American billionaire businessman and film producer. He is the founder of Legendary Entertainment, the production company behind films such as The Dark Knight trilogy, Inception, Jurassic World, and the MonsterVerse series, and he later founded the investment firm Tulco.

Early life and career

Tull grew up in Endwell, New York, and attended Hamilton College, where he played football. He began his career in finance and private equity, working in tax and entertainment-related investment before raising capital to enter film financing.

Legendary Entertainment

Tull founded Legendary Pictures in 2000 and raised substantial private-equity backing, striking a co-financing and distribution partnership with Warner Bros. in 2005. Under his leadership, Legendary developed a reputation for fan-oriented, large-scale genre films, beginning with Batman Begins (2005) and including 300, Watchmen, The Hangover series, Pacific Rim, and Godzilla (2014). In 2016, China's Wanda Group acquired Legendary for about $3.5 billion; Tull departed the company in early 2017.

Later ventures

After Legendary, Tull founded Tulco, a holding company applying data science and artificial intelligence to traditional industries. He is a part-owner of the Pittsburgh Steelers, an investor in esports and other ventures, and a noted philanthropist and collector of sports and film memorabilia.
