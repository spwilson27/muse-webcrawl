Wanda Group

Wanda Group (万达集团), officially Dalian Wanda Group Co., Ltd., is a Chinese multinational conglomerate headquartered in Beijing, with major interests in commercial real estate, shopping malls, hotels, culture and entertainment, and finance. Founded in 1988 in Dalian by Wang Jianlin, it grew into one of China's largest private companies and at its peak was among the world's largest cinema operators and real estate developers.

History

Wanda began as a residential property developer in Dalian, Liaoning province. In the 2000s it expanded nationwide with its "Wanda Plaza" mixed-use complexes combining retail, offices, hotels, and entertainment. The group's cultural arm grew rapidly in the 2010s: it acquired the American theatre chain AMC Theatres in 2012 for about $2.6 billion, purchased the Hollywood studio Legendary Entertainment in 2016 for about $3.5 billion, and invested heavily in film studios, theme parks, and sports properties.

Retrenchment

Following regulatory scrutiny of overseas acquisitions and rising debt, Wanda began a major asset sell-off from 2017 onward, divesting hotel and theme-park portfolios to buyers such as Sunac and R&F Properties, reducing its AMC stake, and later selling its remaining stake in Legendary Entertainment. The group has since focused on its asset-light commercial management business, listing its mall-management arm while restructuring its property debts.

Operations

Wanda's core businesses include Wanda Commercial Management, which operates several hundred Wanda Plaza complexes across China; Wanda Film; hotels; and cultural tourism projects. Wang Jianlin remains the group's chairman.
