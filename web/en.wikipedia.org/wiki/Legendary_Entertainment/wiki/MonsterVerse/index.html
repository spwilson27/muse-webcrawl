MonsterVerse

The MonsterVerse is an American multimedia franchise and shared fictional universe centred on a series of giant monster films featuring Godzilla and King Kong. The franchise is produced by Legendary Pictures in partnership with Warner Bros. Pictures, under licence from Toho Co., Ltd., which owns Godzilla. It is organized around the fictional cryptozoological agency Monarch, which studies the giant creatures known as Titans.

Films

  • Godzilla (2014), directed by Gareth Edwards, reintroducing Godzilla and the MUTOs.
  • Kong: Skull Island (2017), a 1970s-set origin story for King Kong.
  • Godzilla: King of the Monsters (2019), introducing Rodan, Mothra, and King Ghidorah.
  • Godzilla vs. Kong (2021), uniting the two title monsters.
  • Godzilla x Kong: The New Empire (2024), continuing the team-up storyline.

Television

The animated series Skull Island premiered on Netflix in 2023, and the live-action series Monarch: Legacy of Monsters premiered on Apple TV+ in 2023, following Monarch across two generations and starring Kurt Russell and Wyatt Russell as the same character at different ages.

Reception

The franchise's films have grossed well over $2 billion at the worldwide box office in aggregate, generally receiving praise for spectacle and visual effects, with more mixed notices for human storylines. The MonsterVerse has also expanded into comics, novels, and video games.
