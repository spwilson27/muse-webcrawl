List of American Civil War generals (Union)

The following is a partial list of generals who served in the Union Army during the American Civil War (1861–1865), holding the grades of brigadier general, major general, lieutenant general, or general, whether in the Regular Army or the Volunteers. In total, more than 580 men held Union general officer rank.
