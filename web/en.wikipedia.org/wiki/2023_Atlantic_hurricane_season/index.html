The 2023 Atlantic hurricane season was the period in 2023 during which tropical and subtropical cyclones formed in the North Atlantic Ocean. The season officially ran from 1 June to 30 November and produced 20 named storms, the fourth most on record for the basin, despite the presence of a strong El Niño that normally suppresses Atlantic activity; record-warm sea surface temperatures offset the usual effects.

Notable storms

Hurricane Idalia made landfall in Florida's Big Bend region as a Category 3 hurricane in August, causing major flooding and damage. Hurricane Lee rapidly intensified into a Category 5 hurricane in September, becoming one of the fastest-intensifying Atlantic storms on record, before affecting Bermuda and Atlantic Canada. Hurricane Franklin struck the Dominican Republic, and Tropical Storm Ophelia made landfall in North Carolina.

Seasonal forecasts

Pre-season forecasts were complicated by the opposing influences of El Niño and anomalously warm Atlantic waters; agencies including NOAA raised their outlooks mid-season as activity exceeded expectations.

Retired names

The name Idalia was retired after the season owing to the damage caused in Florida, and replaced with Idrissa for the 2029 season.
