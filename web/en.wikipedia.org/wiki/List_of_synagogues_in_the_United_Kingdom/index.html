List of synagogues in the United Kingdom

This is a list of synagogues in the United Kingdom. Jews were readmitted to England under Oliver Cromwell in 1656, and synagogue building resumed soon afterwards; the oldest surviving synagogue in continuous use is Bevis Marks in the City of London, opened in 1701. Large communities developed in London, Manchester, Leeds, Liverpool, Birmingham and Glasgow during the 19th and early 20th centuries.

Organisation

Most Orthodox synagogues belong to the United Synagogue (founded 1870) or to independent and strictly Orthodox (Haredi) congregations concentrated in Stamford Hill, Golders Green and Salford. Progressive Judaism is represented by Reform and Liberal movements, and there are also Masorti, Sephardi and Spanish & Portuguese congregations.
