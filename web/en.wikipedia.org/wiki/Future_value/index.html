Future value

Future value is the value of an asset at a specific date. It measures the nominal future sum of money that a given sum of money is "worth" at a specified time in the future assuming a certain interest rate, or more generally, rate of return. The future value calculation assumes that a sum of money grows at a constant rate of return over time.

Formulas

For a single payment with annual compounding, the future value is:

FV = PV × (1 + r)n

where FV is future value, PV is present value, r is the interest rate per period, and n is the number of periods. With compounding m times per year:

FV = PV × (1 + r/m)m·n

The future value of an annuity (a series of equal payments) is:

FV = PMT × [(1 + r)n − 1] / r

Example

For example, $1,000 invested at 5% annual interest compounded annually for 10 years has a future value of $1,000 × 1.0510 ≈ $1,628.89.

Use

Future value is a central concept in finance, used in investment appraisal, retirement planning, loan amortisation, and valuation. It is the counterpart of present value: both express the time value of money.
