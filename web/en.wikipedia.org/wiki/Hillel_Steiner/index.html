Hillel Steiner (born 1942) is a British political philosopher and Emeritus Professor of Political Philosophy at the University of Manchester. He is a leading figure in the analytic study of rights and a prominent defender of left-libertarianism, combining strong individual rights of self-ownership with an egalitarian claim to natural resources.

Work

His book An Essay on Rights (1994), which won the Political Studies Association's Mackenzie Prize, develops a purely "compossibility"-based theory of rights as mutually consistent sets of duties. Steiner has also written extensively on exploitation, justice in genetics ("silver spoons and golden genes"), and the concept of freedom as the possession of physical resources.

Selected publications

An Essay on Rights (1994); papers in Philosophy & Public Affairs, Ethics and the Journal of Political Philosophy.
