The High Tatras in the Prešov Region

The Prešov Region (Slovak: Prešovský kraj) is one of the eight administrative regions of Slovakia, located in the north-eastern part of the country. Its capital and largest city is Prešov. With an area of about 8,974 km² it is the second-largest Slovak region, and it includes much of the High Tatra mountains and the country's highest peak, Gerlachovský štít.

Geography and districts

The region is divided into 13 districts, including Poprad, Humenné, Bardejov and Stará Ľubovňa. It borders Poland to the north and Ukraine to the east, and contains substantial Rusyn and Ukrainian minorities alongside the Slovak majority.
