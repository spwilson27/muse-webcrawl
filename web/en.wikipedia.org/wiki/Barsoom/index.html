Barsoom

Created byEdgar Rice Burroughs
First appearanceA Princess of Mars (serialised 1912)
GenrePlanetary romance / science fantasy
IdentityThe planet Mars as imagined in the series

Barsoom is a fictional representation of the planet Mars created by American writer Edgar Rice Burroughs. The word "Barsoom" is the native Martian name for Mars in the series. The first Barsoom tale, A Princess of Mars, was serialised in 1912 and published in book form in 1917; ten further novels followed, with the last published story appearing in 1948.

The world

Burroughs' Mars is a dying world: a once ocean-covered planet whose seas have receded, leaving moss-covered dead sea bottoms crossed by canals and dotted with the ruined cities of a vanished civilisation. Its thin atmosphere is maintained by an "atmosphere plant". Barsoom is inhabited by several intelligent races: the red Martians, who live in great city-states such as Helium and Zodanga; the fifteen-foot-tall, four-armed green Martians (Tharks and Warhoons); the white apes; and secluded peoples including the black Martians, yellow Martians, and the white-skinned Therns and Lotharians.

John Carter

The central hero is John Carter, a Confederate veteran of the American Civil War who is mysteriously transported to Mars. Because of the planet's lower gravity, Carter possesses greatly enhanced strength and leaping ability. He weds the princess Dejah Thoris of Helium and becomes Warlord of Mars, fighting alongside the green Martian Tars Tarkas and others through the series.

Influence

The Barsoom books helped define the planetary romance genre and influenced generations of science fiction writers and filmmakers; Burroughs' imagined Mars inspired scientists including Carl Sagan, who kept a map of Barsoom in his office corridor. The stories were adapted to film in Disney's John Carter (2012) and have been extensively imitated and referenced in comics and games.
