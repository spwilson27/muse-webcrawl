Palmdale, California

Palmdale is a city in northern Los Angeles County, California, in the Antelope Valley of the Mojave Desert. With a population of about 169,450 at the 2020 census, it is one of the fastest-growing cities in California and, together with neighbouring Lancaster, forms the principal urban centre of the Antelope Valley.

History

The first settlement, called Palmenthal, was founded in 1886 by German and Swiss immigrants; when drought drove them out, a new community arose near the railroad and took the name Palmdale, after the Joshua trees — mistaken by travellers for palms — that dotted the valley. Palmdale was incorporated in 1962, becoming the Antelope Valley's first incorporated city.

Aerospace

Palmdale is a major centre of the American aerospace industry. United States Air Force Plant 42 hosts facilities of Lockheed Martin's Skunk Works, Northrop Grumman, and Boeing; the B-2 Spirit bomber and F-35 components are associated with the site, and nearby Edwards Air Force Base anchors the region's flight-test heritage. The city styles itself the "Aerospace Capital of America".

Geography

Palmdale lies at an elevation of roughly 810 metres on the high desert floor, south of the San Andreas Fault, which runs along the northern edge of the San Gabriel Mountains visible from the city.
