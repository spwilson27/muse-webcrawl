Basmala

The Basmala (Arabic: بسملة), or Bismillah, is the phrase bi-smi llāhi r-raḥmāni r-raḥīm — "In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful" — recited by Muslims before many actions and at the head of most chapters of the Quran.

Basmala
Arabic: بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
Translation: In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful

Use

The basmala prefaces all but two surahs of the Quran (notably absent from Surah At-Tawba). It is used in prayer, before eating, in Islamic calligraphy, and at the start of documents and speeches in many Muslim cultures.
