Arm Limited (Arm Ltd.) is a British semiconductor and software design company based in Cambridge, England. It designs the Arm architecture for computer processors and licenses its intellectual property to hundreds of companies, whose chips power the vast majority of the world's smartphones and vast numbers of embedded and, increasingly, server and laptop devices.

History

Arm was founded in 1990 as Advanced RISC Machines, a joint venture of Acorn Computers, Apple, and VLSI Technology, growing out of the ARM processor designed at Acorn for the BBC Micro and Archimedes lines. Apple's Newton and later the iPhone popularized Arm-based silicon. SoftBank acquired Arm for about £24 billion in 2016; a proposed sale to Nvidia collapsed in 2022, and Arm listed on the Nasdaq in September 2023, with SoftBank retaining a large majority stake.

Business model

Arm does not manufacture chips; it licenses processor designs (such as the Cortex and Neoverse families) and the instruction set architecture itself, earning license fees and per-chip royalties. Its energy-efficient RISC designs dominate mobile computing and have expanded into cloud data centers and personal computers.
