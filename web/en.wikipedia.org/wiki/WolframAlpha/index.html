WolframAlpha

WolframAlpha
Type: Answer engine
Launched: May 15, 2009
Owner: Wolfram Research

WolframAlpha is an answer engine developed by Wolfram Research. It is an online service that answers factual queries directly by computing the answer from externally sourced, curated data, rather than providing a list of documents or web pages that might contain the answer.

Overview

WolframAlpha was released on May 18, 2009, based on Stephen Wolfram's earlier flagship product Mathematica, a computational platform and programming language. It interprets free-form natural language input and applies built-in algorithms and models to a large collection of curated datasets covering mathematics, science, geography, history, finance, nutrition and many other domains.

Technology

The engine is written in the Wolfram Language and runs on a large cluster of servers. In addition to a web interface, WolframAlpha offers an API and powers query answering features in other products; at various times its results have been used by search engines and voice assistants.
