The 2nd SS Panzer Division Das Reich was an armoured division of Nazi Germany's Waffen-SS during the Second World War. One of the original SS divisions, it fought in the invasions of France and the Soviet Union, at Kursk, and in Normandy and the Ardennes. The division was responsible for notorious war crimes in France in June 1944, including the Tulle and Oradour-sur-Glane massacres.

History

Formed in 1939 as the SS-Verfügungsdivision, it was renamed Das Reich in 1940. It served in the Balkans and in Operation Barbarossa, reaching the outskirts of Moscow, and later took part in the recapture of Kharkov and the Battle of Kursk in 1943 as an SS panzergrenadier, then panzer, division.

France 1944

Refitting in southern France, the division was ordered north after the Normandy landings. Harassed by the Resistance, elements of the division hanged 99 hostages at Tulle on 9 June 1944 and, the next day, murdered 643 civilians at Oradour-sur-Glane, whose ruins were preserved as a memorial. The division then fought in Normandy, the Falaise pocket, and the Battle of the Bulge before surrendering to American forces in Austria in May 1945.
