Ethiopian People's Revolutionary Democratic Front

The Ethiopian People's Revolutionary Democratic Front (EPRDF) was a coalition of ethnic-based political parties that ruled Ethiopia from 1991, when it overthrew the Derg regime at the end of the Ethiopian Civil War, until 2019, when it was dissolved into the Prosperity Party.

History

The front was built around the Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF) with allied movements among the Amhara, Oromo and southern peoples. Its forces entered Addis Ababa in May 1991, ending the Derg whose Red Terror had devastated the country. The EPRDF established an ethnic federal system under the 1995 constitution and oversaw rapid economic growth alongside political repression.

Dissolution

Following protests, Abiy Ahmed became leader in 2018 and merged most member parties into the Prosperity Party in 2019; the TPLF refused to join, a rift that preceded the Tigray War of 2020–22.
