Jeethu Joseph

Jeethu Joseph (born 10 November 1972) is an Indian film director and screenwriter who works mainly in Malayalam cinema.

Career

Joseph made his directorial debut with Detective (2007). He became widely known for the thriller Memories (2013) and especially Drishyam (2013), starring Mohanlal, which became one of the highest-grossing Malayalam films of its time and was remade in several Indian languages.

Later work

He directed the sequel Drishyam 2 (2021), 12th Man (2022), and other thrillers, establishing a reputation for tightly plotted suspense films. See also Malayalam cinema.
