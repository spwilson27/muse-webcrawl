Illinois Confederation

The Illinois Confederation, also referred to as the Illiniwek or Illini, was a group of twelve to thirteen Algonquian-speaking tribes in the upper Mississippi River valley of North America at the time of European contact in the 17th and 18th centuries.

Member tribes

Member peoples included the Peoria, Kaskaskia, Cahokia, Tamaroa and Michigamea, along with the Moingwena, Maroa, Tapouaro and others. They inhabited territory in present-day Illinois, Missouri, Iowa and Arkansas.

History

The confederation was severely weakened by war with the Iroquois during the Beaver Wars, by disease, and later by conflicts with other tribes and American settlers. Today, descendants are principally citizens of the Peoria Tribe of Indians of Oklahoma.
