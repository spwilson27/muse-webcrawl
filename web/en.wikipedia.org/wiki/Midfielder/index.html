Midfielder
Sport: Association football
Position area: Between defence and attack
Roles: Central, defensive, attacking, wide

A midfielder is an outfield position in association football situated in the middle of the pitch, between the defenders and the forwards. Midfielders link defence and attack, typically covering more distance during a match than players in other positions.

Roles

Central midfielders operate in the middle of the field, distributing the ball and controlling the tempo. Defensive midfielders (holding midfielders) shield the defence, break up opposition attacks and recycle possession. Attacking midfielders play ahead of the central line, creating chances in the final third — the classic "number 10" playmaker. Wide midfielders and wingers patrol the flanks, providing width and crosses. The versatile box-to-box midfielder contributes in both penalty areas.

Tactical importance

Control of midfield is widely considered decisive in modern football; formations such as 4-3-3 and 4-2-3-1 are built around the balance of midfield roles.
