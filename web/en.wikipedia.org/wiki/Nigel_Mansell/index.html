Nigel Ernest James Mansell (born 8 August 1953) is a British former racing driver who won both the Formula One World Championship (1992) and the CART Indy Car World Series (1993), the only driver to hold both titles simultaneously. Nicknamed "Il Leone" (the Lion) by Ferrari fans, he was famed for his aggressive, never-give-up driving style.

Formula One career

Mansell debuted with Lotus in 1980, then won his first races with Williams in 1985. After three runner-up finishes in the championship (1986, 1987, 1991) — including the famous 1986 title-deciding tyre blowout in Adelaide — he dominated the 1992 season in the Williams FW14B, winning nine races. He also drove for Ferrari (1989–1990), becoming the last driver hand-picked by Enzo Ferrari.

IndyCar and later career

Switching to Newman/Haas in CART, Mansell won the 1993 championship as a rookie. He briefly returned to Formula One with Williams and McLaren before retiring, and later raced in touring car and Grand Prix Masters events. He was inducted into the International Motorsports Hall of Fame in 2005.
