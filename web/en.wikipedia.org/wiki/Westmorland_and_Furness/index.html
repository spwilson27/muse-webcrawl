Westmorland and Furness

Westmorland and Furness is a unitary authority area in Cumbria, North West England, created on 1 April 2023 as part of local government reorganisation that abolished Cumbria County Council and its six district councils. It is governed by Westmorland and Furness Council, based principally in Kendal.

Geography

The authority covers the former districts of Eden, South Lakeland and Barrow-in-Furness — combining the historic county of Westmorland, the Furness peninsula (historically in Lancashire) and the Eden Valley. Its population is about 227,000. The area includes much of the Lake District National Park, including Windermere, England's largest lake, the town of Barrow-in-Furness, a major shipbuilding centre where BAE Systems builds Royal Navy submarines, and market towns such as Kendal, Penrith, Ulverston and Kirkby Stephen.

Governance

The council was first elected in May 2022 and assumed full powers on vesting day in April 2023, replacing the two-tier structure in the eastern and southern parts of Cumbria; the county's north and west form the separate Cumberland unitary authority. The council is responsible for services including education, social care, transport, planning and waste.
