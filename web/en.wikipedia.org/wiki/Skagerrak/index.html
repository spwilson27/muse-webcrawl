Skagerrak

The Skagerrak is a strait running between the Jutland peninsula of Denmark, the east coast of Norway and the west coast of Sweden, connecting the North Sea and the Kattegat sea area, which leads to the Baltic Sea.

The Skagerrak is about 240 km long and between 80 and 140 km wide. It is a busy shipping route and an important fishing ground. During the First World War it was the site of the Battle of Jutland (1916), known in German as the Skagerrakschlacht, the largest naval battle of the war.

Geography

The Norwegian Trench runs along the Norwegian coast, reaching depths of over 700 metres. Ports on the strait include Kristiansand and Arendal in Norway, Skagen in Denmark, and Gothenburg (via the Kattegat approaches) in Sweden.
