The Gibraltar Parliament is the legislature of the British overseas territory of Gibraltar. Between 1969 and 2006 it was known as the Gibraltar House of Assembly; the 2006 Constitution Order renamed it the Parliament and expanded its powers. It is unicameral.

Composition

The Parliament consists of the Speaker, appointed by the Parliament on the nomination of the Chief Minister, and 17 elected members. Elections are held at least every four years under a system of limited voting in which each elector has ten votes to distribute among candidates, typically voting for a party slate. The political parties include the Gibraltar Socialist Labour Party (GSLP), the Gibraltar Social Democrats (GSD), and the Liberal Party of Gibraltar, the latter two frequently allied in elections.

History

The building on John Mackintosh Square was constructed in 1817 as the Exchange and Commercial Library. The first House of Assembly was elected in 1969 under the constitution of that year, when Spain closed the border with Gibraltar. The 2006 constitution gave Gibraltar a modern constitutional relationship with the United Kingdom, and the first elections under the name "Parliament" followed in 2007.
