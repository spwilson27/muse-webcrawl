Decepticon

The Decepticons are a fictional faction of sentient robots in the Transformers franchise. They are typically the antagonists, opposed to the heroic Autobots.

Led by the tyrannical Megatron, the Decepticons seek to conquer their home world Cybertron and plunder the resources of Earth and other planets. Their ranks include the treacherous Starscream, the loyal Soundwave and the massive Devastator.

Media

First introduced in the 1984 toy line and animated series, the Decepticons have appeared across comics, films and video games ever since.
