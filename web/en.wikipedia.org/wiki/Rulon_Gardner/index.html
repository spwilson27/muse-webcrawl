Rulon Ellis Gardner (born August 16, 1971) is an American retired Greco-Roman wrestler. He competed at the 2000 and 2004 Summer Olympics and won the gold medal in 2000, defeating Russia's Aleksandr Karelin, a victory widely known as the "Miracle on the Mat". Karelin had been undefeated in 13 years of international competition and had not given up a point in six years before the match.

Early life

Gardner was raised on a dairy farm in Afton, Wyoming, one of nine children. He wrestled at Star Valley High School and later at Ricks College and the University of Nebraska, where he finished fourth at the 1993 NCAA championships.

Olympic career

At the Sydney 2000 Olympics, Gardner defeated Karelin 1–0 in the gold-medal match, ending one of the most dominant streaks in sport. He won the world championship in 2001. At the 2004 Athens Olympics he won the bronze medal, leaving his shoes on the mat to signal his retirement.

Later life

Gardner survived several brushes with death, including a snowmobile accident in 2002 that cost him a toe to frostbite, and a light plane crash into Lake Powell in 2007. He later appeared on the reality show The Biggest Loser and worked as a motivational speaker.
