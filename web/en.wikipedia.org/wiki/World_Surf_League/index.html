World Surf League

World Surf League (WSL)
Sport: Surfing
Founded: 1976 (as IPS); relaunched as WSL in 2015
Headquarters: Santa Monica, California, United States

The World Surf League (WSL) is the governing body for professional surfers, organizing the annual Championship Tour and other international circuits of surfing events. It administers the world ranking system and crowns the men's and women's world champions each season.

History

Professional surfing world tours were first organized in 1976 by the International Professional Surfers (IPS) under Fred Hemmings and Randy Rarick. In 1983 the Association of Surfing Professionals (ASP) took over the tour. ZoSea acquired the ASP in 2013, and in 2015 the organization relaunched under the World Surf League name, centralizing media rights and expanding live streaming of competitions. In 2019 the WSL implemented equal prize money for men and women across all its events, a landmark in professional sport.

Tours and events

The WSL runs the elite Championship Tour (CT), contested at famous breaks such as Pipeline (Hawaii), Bells Beach (Australia), Jeffreys Bay (South Africa), and Teahupo'o (Tahiti), along with the Challenger Series, Qualifying Series, World Junior Championships, and Big Wave and Longboard tours. Since 2021 the Championship Tour season has concluded with the WSL Finals, a single-day event in which the top five men and women compete for the world title.
