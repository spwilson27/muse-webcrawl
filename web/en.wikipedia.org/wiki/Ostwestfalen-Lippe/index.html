Ostwestfalen-Lippe

Ostwestfalen-Lippe (OWL) is a region in the north-east of the German state of North Rhine-Westphalia, roughly corresponding to the administrative district (Regierungsbezirk) of Detmold. It combines the eastern part of Westphalia with the former principality and state of Lippe.

The region's major city is Bielefeld; other significant towns include Paderborn, Gütersloh, Minden, Detmold and Herford. With about two million inhabitants, OWL is known for a strong Mittelstand economy, with internationally known companies in food production, mechanical engineering, furniture, and household appliances, as well as for universities in Bielefeld and Paderborn.

Geographically the region is shaped by the Teutoburg Forest and the Weser river. Historical sights include the Hermannsdenkmal monument near Detmold and the cathedral and Kaiserpfalz remains in Paderborn.
