Athina Onassis

Born:29 January 1985, Neuilly-sur-Seine, France
Occupation:Equestrian, heiress
Parents:Thierry Roussel and Christina Onassis
Grandfather:Aristotle Onassis

Athina Hélène Onassis (born 29 January 1985) is a French-Greek heiress and equestrian, the only surviving descendant of Greek shipping magnate Aristotle Onassis, and the only child of Aristotle's daughter Christina Onassis and her fourth husband, French businessman Thierry Roussel.

Inheritance

After her mother's death in 1988, Athina was raised in Switzerland by her father. On her 18th birthday in 2003 she took control of an inheritance estimated at about US$2.7 billion, including the island of Skorpios, which she later sold. She remains one of the wealthiest Greek nationals.

Equestrian career

Onassis is an accomplished show jumper, competing in the Global Champions Tour and serving as patroness of the Longines Athina Onassis Horse Show held in Saint-Tropez. She married Brazilian Olympic show jumper Álvaro de Miranda Neto in 2005; the couple separated in 2016 and divorced in 2017.
