The Fugitive is a 1993 American action thriller film directed by Andrew Davis and based on the 1960s television series of the same name. It stars Harrison Ford as Dr. Richard Kimble and Tommy Lee Jones as Deputy U.S. Marshal Samuel Gerard.

Plot

Chicago surgeon Richard Kimble returns home to find his wife Helen murdered by a one-armed man. Lacking evidence of the intruder and being the beneficiary of Helen's life insurance, Kimble is convicted of her murder and sentenced to death. En route to prison, other inmates attempt an escape, causing a spectacular bus and train crash from which Kimble escapes. He sets out to find the real killer — Frederick Sykes, a former police officer with a prosthetic arm — while Gerard and his team of marshals pursue him relentlessly. Kimble eventually uncovers a conspiracy involving his friend Dr. Charles Nichols and a pharmaceutical company that faked trial data for a new drug.

Reception

The film was a critical and commercial success, grossing nearly $369 million worldwide. It was nominated for seven Academy Awards, including Best Picture, and Jones won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor. Its train-wreck sequence and Kimble's dam jump became iconic action scenes. A spin-off, U.S. Marshals (1998), again featured Jones as Gerard.
