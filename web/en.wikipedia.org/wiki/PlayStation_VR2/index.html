PlayStation VR2 (officially abbreviated as PS VR2) is a virtual reality headset for the PlayStation 5, developed by Sony Interactive Entertainment and released on 22 February 2023. It is the successor to the 2016 PlayStation VR for the PlayStation 4, offering higher-resolution displays, inside-out tracking, eye tracking, and new Sense controllers, while connecting to the console with a single USB-C cable.

Hardware

The headset uses a pair of OLED panels with a combined resolution of 4000×2040 (2000×2040 per eye), a refresh rate of 90 or 120 Hz, HDR support, and an approximately 110-degree field of view, with Fresnel lenses and a dial to adjust lens separation. Four cameras built into the headset track the player's head and controllers (inside-out tracking), eliminating the external camera the original PS VR required. Infrared cameras track the wearer's eyes, enabling foveated rendering — rendering in full detail only where the player is looking — and eye-based input. The headset itself has a vibration motor for haptic feedback, while the orb-shaped Sense controllers combine the adaptive triggers and haptics of the DualSense with finger-touch detection.

Software

Launch titles included Horizon Call of the Mountain, an enhanced version of Gran Turismo 7, and Resident Evil Village in VR mode; later releases included Resident Evil 4 VR and Beat Saber. The PS VR2 is not natively backward compatible with PS VR games, which drew criticism, though some developers released upgraded versions. In August 2024 Sony released a PC adapter allowing the headset to be used with SteamVR games on Windows.

Reception and sales

Reviewers praised the display quality, eye tracking, and the simplicity of the single-cable setup, but criticised the high price — more than the PS5 console itself — and the modest game library. Sales fell short of Sony's early expectations; reports in 2024 indicated production had been paused to clear inventory, and Sony reduced support as the wider VR market contracted, though the PC adapter extended the hardware's usefulness.
