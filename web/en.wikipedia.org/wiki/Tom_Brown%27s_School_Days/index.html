Tom Brown's School Days

Tom Brown's School DaysAuthor: Thomas Hughes
Published: 1857
Genre: School novel

Tom Brown's School Days is a novel by Thomas Hughes, published in 1857. Set at Rugby School under the headmastership of Thomas Arnold, it follows the school career of Tom Brown and is considered the founding work of the school story genre.

Plot

Tom Brown, a lively boy from the Vale of White Horse, arrives at Rugby and is befriended by Harry "Scud" East. He suffers at the hands of the bully Flashman, a character based partly on real schoolboys of Hughes's acquaintance, but matures under Arnold's moral influence and befriends the frail, pious George Arthur.

Influence

The novel idealised the Victorian public school ideals of muscular Christianity, fair play and character formation. It influenced generations of school fiction, from later British school stories to Japanese works, and its characters have been adapted repeatedly for film and television.

Sequel

Hughes continued Tom's story at Oxford in Tom Brown at Oxford (1861).
