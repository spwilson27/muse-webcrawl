Trefoil
Type: Wikipedia article (recreated from memory)

A trefoil is a graphic form composed of three overlapping rings or lobes. The word is used in architecture (Gothic tracery), heraldry and knot theory, and — most recognisably today — for the radiation trefoil, the international warning symbol for ionising radiation: a black (or magenta) three-bladed propeller shape on a yellow background.

The radiation symbol was designed in 1946 at the University of California radiation laboratory in Berkeley; its blades represent activity radiating from a central atom. It appears on sources, containers, rooms and equipment where radioactive material or radiation-generating devices are present, standardised by ISO 361.

Other uses

In Gothic architecture a trefoil is a three-lobed shape in window tracery; in heraldry it is a stylised three-lobed leaf. The Girl Guides / Girl Scouts world trefoil is a membership emblem.
