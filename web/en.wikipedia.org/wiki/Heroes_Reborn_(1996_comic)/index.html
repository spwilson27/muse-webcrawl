Heroes Reborn (1996 comic)

"Heroes Reborn" was a 1996–1997 crossover storyline published by Marvel Comics in which the Avengers, the Fantastic Four, Iron Man and Captain America were reimagined and relaunched in a parallel pocket universe. Following the "Onslaught" storyline, in which the heroes appeared to sacrifice their lives, Franklin Richards was revealed to have preserved them in a pocket dimension where their origins were retold for a new generation.

Production

In a controversial move, Marvel outsourced the four titles to the studios of former Marvel artists Jim Lee (WildStorm Productions, taking Fantastic Four and Iron Man) and Rob Liefeld (Extreme Studios, taking The Avengers and Captain America). Liefeld's studio was removed from the project after six issues amid disappointing sales and reception, and Lee's studio assumed all four books.

Story and conclusion

The relaunched series retold the characters' origins with updated elements while omitting much prior continuity. After thirteen issues each, the event concluded with "Heroes Return" (1997–1998), in which the heroes were restored to the mainstream Marvel Universe with their memories and histories intact. The experiment drew mixed reviews: it raised sales temporarily and remains a notorious episode of 1990s comics history, studied for its business as much as its creative merits.
