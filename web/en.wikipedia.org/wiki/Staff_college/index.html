Staff college

A staff college is a military educational institution that trains selected officers for service on the staff of higher headquarters and for higher command. Staff colleges teach the planning, administrative, logistical and operational skills needed to translate a commander's intent into orders, and they form a key step in the professional education of career officers in most armed forces.

Purpose and curriculum

Staff college courses, typically lasting from several months to a year or more, are aimed at mid-career officers, usually majors and lieutenant colonels (or naval equivalents). The curriculum usually covers:

  • the planning process and the production of operational orders and appreciations;
  • logistics, personnel and intelligence staff work;
  • joint and combined operations with other services and allied nations;
  • military history and the study of past campaigns;
  • leadership, management and, increasingly, the political and media dimensions of operations.

Teaching is commonly by syndicate discussion, map exercises, war games and command post exercises rather than by lecture alone. Successful completion — the award of the "psc" (passed staff college) post-nominal in British-style systems — is often a prerequisite for promotion to senior rank.

History

The prototype of the modern staff college was the Prussian Kriegsakademie, founded in Berlin in 1810 in the reform era after Prussia's defeat by Napoleon; its graduates staffed the Prussian and later German General Staff, whose professional competence became famous — and feared — throughout the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries. Other nations followed: the British Staff College at Camberley was founded in 1858, the French École supérieure de guerre in 1876, and the United States established its Command and General Staff College at Fort Leavenworth in 1881 (as the School of Application for Infantry and Cavalry).

Examples

  • Joint Services Command and Staff College (United Kingdom)
  • United States Army Command and General Staff College, Fort Leavenworth
  • Naval War College and Air War College (United States)
  • Defence Services Staff College, Wellington (India)
  • Canadian Forces College, Toronto
  • Australian Command and Staff College

Many staff colleges admit international students, and attendance abroad is a recognised instrument of defence diplomacy.
