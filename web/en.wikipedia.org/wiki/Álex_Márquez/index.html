Álex Márquez Alentà (born 23 April 1996) is a Spanish Grand Prix motorcycle road racer. He is the 2014 Moto3 World Champion and the 2019 Moto2 World Champion, and has competed in the MotoGP World Championship since 2020. He is the younger brother of multiple MotoGP world champion Marc Márquez; the two became the first brothers to win Grand Prix world titles in the same season in 2014.

Career

Born in Cervera, Catalonia, Márquez began racing in Spanish national championships and made his Grand Prix debut in the Moto3 class in 2012, joining the Estrella Galicia 0,0 team full-time in 2013, when he took his first victory at Motegi. In 2014 he won the Moto3 world championship in a season-long duel with Jack Miller, decided at the final round in Valencia. He moved up to Moto2 in 2015 with the Marc VDS team; after several seasons of development and injuries he won the 2019 Moto2 World Championship with five victories.

In 2020 Márquez stepped up to MotoGP with the Repsol Honda Team — briefly becoming his brother's team-mate — scoring two podium finishes in his rookie season, at Le Mans and Aragón. He then spent two seasons with LCR Honda before moving to Gresini Racing on a Ducati for 2023, with which he won sprint races and returned to the podium, before achieving a first full Grand Prix victory in the premier class at Jerez in 2025, becoming, with Marc, the first pair of brothers each to win in MotoGP.

Racing record highlights

  • 2012: CEV Buckler Moto3 champion before his Grand Prix debut.
  • 2014: Moto3 World Champion (3 wins).
  • 2019: Moto2 World Champion (5 wins).
  • 2020: Two MotoGP podiums as a rookie.
  • 2025: First MotoGP Grand Prix victory (Spanish GP, Jerez).
