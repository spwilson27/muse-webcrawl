Ikedaya incident

The Ikedaya incident (池田屋事件, Ikedaya jiken) was an armed encounter on 5 June 1864 between the Shinsengumi, the Tokugawa shogunate's special police force, and anti-shogunate rōnin from Chōshū and Tosa at the Ikedaya Inn in Kyoto, Japan.

Background

The Chōshū activists were meeting at the inn to plot against the shogunate, reportedly planning to set fire to Kyoto and seize the Imperial Palace. Shinsengumi intelligence uncovered the meeting, and a small force led by Kondō Isami raided the inn at night.

Outcome

In the ensuing sword fight, several rōnin were killed and others captured; the plot was foiled. The incident made the Shinsengumi famous and is a staple episode of Bakumatsu-era drama and fiction.
