Inspire Brands

Type: Private
Industry: Restaurants
Founded: 2018
Headquarters: Atlanta, Georgia, U.S.
Parent: Roark Capital

Inspire Brands, Inc. is an American holding company and owner of several restaurant chains. It was formed in 2018 when Arby's Restaurant Group acquired Buffalo Wild Wings and Rusty Taco, creating a multi-brand platform backed by the private equity firm Roark Capital.

Brands

The company's portfolio grew rapidly: it acquired Sonic Drive-In in 2018, Jimmy John's in 2019, and in 2020 completed the purchase of Dunkin' Brands, adding Dunkin' and Baskin-Robbins in a deal valued at about $11.3 billion. Its other chains include Arby's, Buffalo Wild Wings, and Rusty Taco.

Scale

With tens of thousands of restaurants in dozens of countries and system sales in the tens of billions of dollars, Inspire Brands is among the largest restaurant companies in the United States.
