Domino's

Domino's Pizza, Inc., commonly known as Domino's, is an American multinational pizza restaurant chain founded in 1960. Headquartered in Ann Arbor, Michigan, it is one of the world's largest pizza companies, with more than 20,000 stores in over 90 markets, most of them franchised.

History

Brothers Tom and James Monaghan bought a small Michigan pizzeria called DomiNick's in 1960 for $900; Tom soon became sole owner, renamed the business Domino's in 1965, and built the brand around fast delivery, famously promising delivery in 30 minutes or less. Franchising began the same year, and the chain expanded internationally from the 1980s. Tom Monaghan sold his controlling stake to Bain Capital in 1998, and the company went public in 2004.

Business and technology

Domino's pioneered online and mobile ordering, a pizza-tracking interface, and delivery-focused store design, and it earns much of its revenue as a franchisor and through its dough and supply-chain centres serving franchisees. Its logo — a domino tile with three dots — originally represented the chain's first three stores.
