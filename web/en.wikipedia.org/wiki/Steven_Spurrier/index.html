Steven Spurrier

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Steven Spurrier (5 October 1941 – 9 March 2021) was a British wine merchant, writer, and educator who founded the Académie du Vin in Paris. He is best remembered for organizing the 1976 blind tasting known as the Judgment of Paris, at which Californian wines outscored prestigious French wines, a result that shocked the wine world and transformed the international wine industry.

Early life and Paris career

Born in Cambridge and educated at Rugby School and the London School of Economics, Spurrier trained in the wine trade in London, Bordeaux, and Port-producing Portugal before moving to Paris in 1970. There he purchased a small wine shop, Les Caves de la Madeleine, and in 1973 founded the Académie du Vin, the first private wine school in France, which taught many future wine professionals.

The Judgment of Paris

On 24 May 1976, to mark the American Bicentennial, Spurrier and Patricia Gallagher staged a blind tasting for leading French wine judges, comparing top Bordeaux and white Burgundies with recent California Cabernet Sauvignons and Chardonnays that Spurrier had collected on a trip to California. The Californian wines — Chateau Montelena Chardonnay 1973 and Stag's Leap Wine Cellars Cabernet Sauvignon 1973 — took first place in both categories. A report by Time journalist George M. Taber, the only reporter present, publicized the result worldwide; the event was dramatized in the 2008 film Bottle Shock, in which Alan Rickman played Spurrier. Although Spurrier expected the French wines to win and reportedly felt the tasting damaged his standing in Paris, re-tastings over later decades repeatedly confirmed the quality of the Californian entries.

Later life

Spurrier returned to England in the late 1980s, becoming a consultant and long-time columnist for Decanter magazine and a chairman of the Decanter World Wine Awards. His memoir, Wine — A Way of Life, appeared in 2018. With his wife Bella he planted the Bride Valley vineyard in Dorset, producing English sparkling wine. He died in 2021 at the age of 79.
