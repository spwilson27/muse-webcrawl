Academy Award for Best Picture

The Academy Award for Best Picture is the top prize at the Academy Awards, presented annually by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences since 1929, when Wings won the award for Outstanding Picture. It is given to the film's producers.


Presented by: AMPAS
First awarded: 1929 (Wings)
Nominees: 10 (since 2011, fixed from 2021)

History

The category's name and size have changed repeatedly. The nominee field expanded from five to ten in 2009 after acclaimed popular films were snubbed, and became a fixed ten from the 94th ceremony. Voting uses a preferential (instant-runoff) ballot. Landmark winners include Casablanca, The Godfather, Schindler's List, The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (which won all 11 of its nominations), Parasite (2019, first non-English-language winner), Everything Everywhere All at Once (2022), Oppenheimer (2023) and Anora (2024).

Records

Three films — Ben-Hur, Titanic and The Return of the King — have won 11 Oscars. The 2017 ceremony famously announced La La Land in error before correcting to Moonlight.
