Dream

Dream
Occurs mainly inREM sleep
ContentImages, sounds, emotions, narrative
[Image: A surreal dreamscape painting — a melting landscape with floating objects]

A dream is a succession of images, ideas, emotions and sensations that usually occurs involuntarily during REM sleep. Dreams can be mundane or bizarre, joyful or nightmarish; most are forgotten unless the sleeper wakes during or just after them.

Interpretations

Ancient cultures read dreams as omens or divine messages; Freud's Interpretation of Dreams (1900) made them the 'royal road to the unconscious', while modern neuroscience links dreaming to memory consolidation, emotion regulation and threat simulation. Lucid dreaming — knowing one dreams — is studied with eye-signal experiments.
