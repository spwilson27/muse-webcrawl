Pub game

A pub game is a game traditionally played inside or outside a pub (public house). Pub games are a long-standing part of British pub culture and include tabletop, throwing, and dexterity games, many of them centuries old and governed by local or league rules.

Traditional games

Well-known pub games include darts, skittles (and its regional variants), shove ha'penny, bar billiards, pool, dominoes, cribbage and other card games, quoits, aunt sally (in Oxfordshire), toad in the hole, ring the bull, and the pub quiz — a trivia competition that became widespread from the 1970s onward. Many are played in organized leagues between pubs.

History

Games have been part of tavern life since at least the medieval period. Legislation periodically restricted gaming in alehouses, particularly gambling, but games of skill flourished. The Campaign for Real Ale (CAMRA) has supported traditional pub games as part of pub heritage, and organizations record regional variants that might otherwise be lost as pubs close.

Modern games

Contemporary pubs may also host quiz machines, pool tables, table football, and board games, while some traditional games survive mainly as curiosities or in a handful of specialist venues.
