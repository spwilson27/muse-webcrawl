Notions (sewing)

In sewing and needlework, notions are small objects or accessories applied to a finished article, as distinct from the main fabric. The term covers the tools and trimmings kept in a sewing kit or sold in the notions department of a shop.

Types

Notions include fasteners such as buttons, zippers, snaps, hooks and eyes, and hook-and-loop tape; threads and elastic; pins, needles, and pin cushions; bias tape, piping, braid, ribbons, and other trims; interfacing and stabilisers; sewing chalks and markers; thimbles, needle threaders, seam rippers, and measuring tapes. Haberdashery is the closest British equivalent term for the trade in such goods, though in North American usage haberdashery more often concerns men's clothing accessories.

Usage

Patterns for garments typically list the notions required in addition to fabric and yardage. Notions are selected to match the weight and purpose of the project, and their quality strongly affects the durability of the finished piece. Vintage and decorative notions, such as antique buttons, are also collected.
