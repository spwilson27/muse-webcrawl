Dulux

Dulux is an internationally available brand of paint and woodcare products, owned since 2010 by the Dutch multinational AkzoNobel. The brand was first manufactured in the United Kingdom by Imperial Chemical Industries (ICI) from the early 1930s and became the market-leading decorative paint brand in Britain and many Commonwealth countries.

History

The name Dulux, a contraction of "DuPont" and "luxury", dates to 1931, when DuPont and ICI developed the alkyd-based paint together. Dulux is famed for its advertising mascot, the Dulux Dog — an Old English Sheepdog first used in 1961 — which became so strongly associated with the brand that "Dulux dog" is used colloquially for the breed.

Products

The range includes emulsion and gloss paints, exterior masonry paint, and woodcare brands such as Cuprinol and Polycell (sold under the Dulux umbrella in several markets). Dulux operates colour consultancy services and publishes annual colour trend forecasts.
