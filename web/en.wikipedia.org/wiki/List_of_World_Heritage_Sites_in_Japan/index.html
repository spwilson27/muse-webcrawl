List of World Heritage Sites in Japan

Japan has 26 properties inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List: 21 cultural sites and 5 natural sites. The first inscriptions, in 1993, were Himeji Castle, the Buddhist monuments of Horyu-ji, Shirakami-Sanchi and Yakushima.
