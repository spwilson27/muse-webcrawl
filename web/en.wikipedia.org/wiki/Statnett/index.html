Statnett owns and operates Norway's main electricity transmission grid.

Statnett SF is the system operator and owner of the central electricity transmission grid of Norway. It is a state enterprise owned by the Norwegian government and is responsible for ensuring that there is an instantaneous balance between electricity production and consumption throughout the Norwegian power system.

Operations

Statnett owns and operates roughly 11,000 kilometres of high-voltage lines and cables (at 420, 300 and 132 kV) and around 150 substations. It also owns stakes in Norway's international interconnectors, including the Skagerrak link to Denmark, NorNed to the Netherlands, NordLink to Germany, and the North Sea Link to the United Kingdom, the world's longest subsea power cable when it opened in 2021.

History

Statnett was established in 1992 when the state power utility Statkraftverkene was split into Statkraft (generation) and Statnett (transmission), following Norway's energy market deregulation. As Norway's transmission system operator, Statnett is a member of ENTSO-E, the European association of transmission system operators, and works with the Nordic power exchange Nord Pool.
