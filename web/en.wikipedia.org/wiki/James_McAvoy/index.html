James McAvoy (born 21 April 1979) is a Scottish actor. He gained recognition for playing Robbie Turner in Atonement (2007), and is widely known for portraying Charles Xavier in the X-Men film series (2011–2019) and for his multi-role performance in M. Night Shyamalan's Split (2016) and Glass (2019).

Early life and career

McAvoy was born in Glasgow and raised in Drumchapel. His acting debut came at age 15 in The Near Room (1995). After training at the Royal Scottish Academy of Music and Drama, he appeared in the miniseries Band of Brothers (2001) and Children of Dune (2003), and won attention as the car thief Steve McBride in the Channel 4 series Shameless (2004–2005), where he met his future wife Anne-Marie Duff.

Film work

He played Mr Tumnus in The Chronicles of Narnia: The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe (2005), Idi Amin's physician in The Last King of Scotland (2006), and the lead in the action film Wanted (2008). His portrayal of the young Professor X in X-Men: First Class (2011) and its sequels made him a franchise star. He has balanced blockbusters with stage work in London, winning acclaim for Macbeth (2013), The Ruling Class (2015), and Cyrano de Bergerac (2019–2022), and played Lord Asriel in the BBC/HBO series His Dark Materials (2019–2022). In 2024 he starred in the horror remake Speak No Evil.
