The 2012–13 Premier League was the 21st season of the Premier League, the English professional league for association football clubs, since its establishment in 1992. Manchester United won their 13th Premier League title and their 20th English football title overall.

Summary

United, under Sir Alex Ferguson in what proved to be his final season as manager, sealed the championship with four games to spare, defeating Aston Villa 3–0 on 22 April 2013 with a hat-trick from Robin van Persie, whose goals after signing from Arsenal were decisive. Defending champions Manchester City finished second, eleven points behind.

Retirement of Sir Alex Ferguson

On 8 May 2013, Ferguson announced his retirement after 26 years in charge of Manchester United, ending the most successful managerial reign in English football history. David Moyes was appointed as his successor.

League table

Chelsea and Arsenal took the remaining Champions League places in third and fourth. Wigan Athletic, despite winning the FA Cup, were relegated along with Reading and Queens Park Rangers.
