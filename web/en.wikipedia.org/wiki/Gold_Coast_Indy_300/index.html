The Gold Coast Indy 300 was an annual open-wheel motor race held on the streets of Surfers Paradise on the Gold Coast in Queensland, Australia. First run in 1991 as part of the CART IndyCar World Series, it became one of the most popular stops on the North American open-wheel calendar, drawing large crowds to the temporary beachside street circuit.

History

The inaugural event in March 1991 was won by John Andretti. The race quickly established itself as a fixture of the CART series, notable for being one of the few events held outside North America. The street circuit ran along the Surfers Paradise esplanade and surrounding streets, combining fast straights with tight chicanes beside the Pacific Ocean beaches.

Following the reunification of American open-wheel racing in 2008, the event was run as a non-championship round of the IndyCar Series in October 2008, won by Australian Ryan Briscoe. From 2009 the race weekend was reconfigured around V8 Supercars as the Gold Coast 600, ending the Indy era.

Notable winners

Winners of the event included Nigel Mansell (1993), Emerson Fittipaldi, Michael Andretti, Paul Tracy, and Sébastien Bourdais, who won twice in 2005 and 2007. The 1994 race is remembered for a dramatic finish in heavy rain.
