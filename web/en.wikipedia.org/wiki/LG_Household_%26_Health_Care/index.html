LG Household & Health Care Ltd. (Korean: LG생활건강) is a South Korean consumer goods and cosmetics company, part of the LG Group. Founded in 1947 as Lucky Chemical, it produced Korea's first cosmetic cream and went on to become one of the country's largest makers of household products, beverages and beauty brands.

History

The company began in 1947 when Koo In-hwoi founded Lucky Chemical in Busan, launching the "Lucky Cream" cosmetic. It expanded into toothpaste, detergents and soaps, and was renamed LG Chemical's household division before being spun off as a separate company, LG Household & Health Care, in 2001.

Business areas

The company's operations are divided into three main areas: household and personal care products (including detergents, toothpaste and shampoo), cosmetics (including the luxury brands The History of Whoo, SU:M37 and O Hui, and the mass brand The Face Shop, acquired in 2010), and beverages (following the acquisition of Coca-Cola Beverage Company Korea in 2007). It has expanded internationally, notably acquiring the North American business of Avon Products in 2019.

Brands

Well-known brands include Perioe toothpaste, Elastine shampoo, Whoo and O Hui skincare, and The Face Shop retail chain.
