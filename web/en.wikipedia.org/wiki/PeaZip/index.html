PeaZip

PeaZip is a free and open-source file archiver and file manager, developed by Giorgio Tani. It is written mainly in Free Pascal and runs on Windows, Linux and macOS.

DeveloperGiorgio Tani
Initial release2006
LicenseGNU LGPL
TypeFile archiver

Features

PeaZip supports its own PEA archive format and works with more than 200 archive extensions, including 7z, ZIP, TAR, RAR (extraction) and Zstandard. It provides strong encryption (AES-256), two-factor authentication, secure deletion, duplicate-file detection and file hashing, and can convert between archive formats.

Reception

The program is often cited as a free alternative to commercial archivers such as WinZip and WinRAR.
