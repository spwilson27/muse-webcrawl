Bevo is the live mascot of the athletic programs of the University of Texas at Austin. Bevo is a Texas Longhorn steer, and his name is the origin of the university's "Longhorns" nickname. He appears at Texas Longhorns football games and other university events, attended by the Silver Spurs, an honorary men's service organization that serves as his handlers.

History

The first Bevo was presented to the university in 1916, purchased with funds raised by alumni; he was introduced at the Thanksgiving Day game against Texas A&M. The origin of the name "Bevo" is debated, but it is most often linked to a popular near-beer drink of the Prohibition era and to "beeve", slang for cattle. According to a famous campus legend, Texas A&M students branded the steer with "13–0", the score of a Texas loss, and the brand was altered to read "BEVO". Fifteen steers have held the title; Bevo XV took over in 2016. Bevo XIV became a beloved fixture during the 2000s, while Bevo XV made national headlines at the 2019 Sugar Bowl, when he charged toward the Georgia Bulldogs' mascot, Uga, before the game.

Care and appearances

Between appearances Bevo is kept at a private ranch near Austin. He is known for an unusually docile temperament, selected in part so that he can stand calmly amid stadium crowds, cannon fire from the Texas Cowboys' cannon, and the university band.
