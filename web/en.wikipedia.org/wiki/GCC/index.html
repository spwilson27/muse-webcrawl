GCC

GCC may refer to several things. Most commonly it stands for the GNU Compiler Collection, the free software compiler system produced by the GNU Project, or the Gulf Cooperation Council, the political and economic union of six Arab states of the Persian Gulf.

Computing

The GNU Compiler Collection (GCC), originally the GNU C Compiler when first released by Richard Stallman in 1987, supports front ends for C, C++, Objective-C, Fortran, Ada, Go and other languages, and targets a wide range of processor architectures. It is the default compiler for most Linux distributions and a key component of the GNU toolchain.

Organisations

The Gulf Cooperation Council (Cooperation Council for the Arab States of the Gulf), founded in 1981, comprises Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Bahrain and Oman. Other uses of the abbreviation include the German Cross in Gold in some contexts, various colleges, and the Global Climate Coalition.
