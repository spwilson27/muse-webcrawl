Royal Dance Academy

The Royal Academy of Dance (RAD) — sometimes referred to as the Royal Dance Academy — is an international dance education and examination board based in London, specialising in classical ballet.

History

It was founded in 1920 as the Association of Operatic Dancing of Great Britain by leading dancers including Adeline Genée and Phyllis Bedells, and received a royal charter in 1935. Adeline Genée was its first president; Margot Fonteyn later served as president.

Work

The RAD sets graded and vocational ballet syllabi examined worldwide, trains teachers, and runs the Genée International Ballet Competition. Its headquarters are in Battersea, London.
