Joint Entrance Examination

The Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) is an engineering entrance assessment conducted for admission to various engineering colleges in India. It is constituted by two different examinations: JEE Main and JEE Advanced. The exams are held for admission into the Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs), National Institutes of Technology (NITs), and other centrally funded technical institutions.

History

The IITs began conducting a joint entrance examination in 1960. Over the decades the system evolved, and in 2013 the older AIEEE and IIT-JEE formats were replaced by the two-stage JEE Main and JEE Advanced structure. Candidates must first qualify in JEE Main to be eligible to appear for JEE Advanced.

Examination structure

JEE Main consists of papers in physics, chemistry, and mathematics, and is also used for admission to architecture and planning courses. JEE Advanced, administered by one of the IITs on a rotating basis, is widely regarded as one of the most competitive entrance examinations in the world, with only a small fraction of candidates gaining admission.
