Karun-3 Dam

The Karun-3 Dam is a large hydroelectric dam on the Karun River in Khuzestan Province, Iran, located about 28 km east of the city of Izeh. It is one of a series of major dams built on the Karun, Iran's most voluminous river.

Design

The dam is a double-curvature concrete arch dam about 205 m high and 462 m long at the crest, impounding a reservoir of nearly 3 billion cubic metres. Its underground powerhouse contains eight Francis turbine units with a total installed capacity of about 2,280 MW, generating roughly 4,172 GWh of electricity per year. Construction began in the 1990s and the first units came online in 2004–2005.

Purpose

In addition to power generation, the dam regulates the Karun's flow for flood control and irrigation supply downstream. Its construction required the relocation of the old Shiraz–Isfahan road, with a replacement route built including one of Iran's highest bridges across the reservoir.
