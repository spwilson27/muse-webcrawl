Watcher (comics)

The Watchers are a fictional species appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. The best-known Watcher is Uatu, who observes Earth and its superheroes from the Moon, sworn never to interfere — though he frequently does.

Watchers
PublisherMarvel Comics
First appearanceFantastic Four #13 (1963)
Created byStan Lee and Jack Kirby

Concept

The Watchers are among the oldest beings in the universe, possessing vast cosmic knowledge and power. After a disastrous attempt to share technology with a younger species, they vowed only to observe and record events across the cosmos. Uatu, assigned to watch Earth, has repeatedly broken his oath to aid the Fantastic Four and other heroes at moments of cosmic crisis, beginning with the first coming of Galactus.

In other media

Uatu, voiced by Jeffrey Wright, serves as narrator of the animated series What If...? (2021–2024) in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.
