Canale 5

Canale 5 (Italian for "Channel 5") is an Italian free-to-air television channel operated by Mediaset, the media company founded by Silvio Berlusconi. Launched on 30 September 1980, it was the first national private television network in Italy and remains the flagship channel of the Mediaset group and one of the most watched channels in the country. Its programming is generalist, centred on entertainment shows, Italian and international drama series, films, news, and reality television.

History

Canale 5 grew out of the Milanese cable station Telemilano 58, which Berlusconi transformed in 1980 into a syndication network of local stations broadcasting the same schedule — a model used to circumvent the RAI state monopoly on national broadcasting. Within a few years Canale 5 became a genuinely national network and the anchor of Berlusconi's Fininvest/MediaForEurope empire. The channel's news programme, TG5, launched in 1992, and the satirical news show Striscia la notizia became long-running fixtures.

Programming

Signature programmes have included the reality show Grande Fratello (Big Brother), the talent shows Amici di Maria De Filippi and Italia's Got Talent, the talk show Maurizio Costanzo Show, and popular soap operas and telenovelas. The channel broadcasts in high definition as Canale 5 HD and is available on digital terrestrial television, satellite, and streaming via Mediaset Infinity.
