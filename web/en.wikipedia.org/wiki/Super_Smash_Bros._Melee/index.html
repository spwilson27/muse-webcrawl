Super Smash Bros. Melee is a 2001 crossover fighting video game developed by HAL Laboratory and published by Nintendo for the GameCube. The second entry in the Super Smash Bros. series, it features characters from Nintendo franchises including Mario, The Legend of Zelda, Pokémon, Star Fox and Metroid, who fight to knock each other off stages using percentage-based damage.

Gameplay

Melee expanded the original's roster to 26 characters and added new modes including Adventure, All-Star, and an extensive trophy collection system, alongside events and the Home-Run Contest. Its faster, more technical movement — including techniques such as wavedashing and L-cancelling — gave it exceptional depth at high levels of play.

Reception and legacy

The game received critical acclaim and sold over seven million copies, making it the best-selling GameCube title. Unusually for a game of its era, Melee developed a large competitive community that sustained major tournaments for more than two decades, and it is frequently cited among the greatest video games of all time. The 2013 documentary series The Smash Brothers chronicled its competitive scene.
