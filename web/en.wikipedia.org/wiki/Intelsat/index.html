Intelsat

Intelsat S.A.
Founded: August 20, 1964
Headquarters: McLean, Virginia, U.S. (administrative); Luxembourg (legal)
Industry: Satellite communications

Intelsat S.A. is a multinational satellite services provider originally created in 1964 as the International Telecommunications Satellite Organization (ITSO), an intergovernmental consortium that owned and managed a constellation of communications satellites providing international telecommunications services.

Intelsat's first satellite, Intelsat I ("Early Bird"), launched in 1965, was the first commercial communications satellite in geostationary orbit. The organization was privatized in 2001. Today Intelsat operates one of the world's largest commercial fleets of geostationary satellites, providing video distribution, broadband, and mobility services.

In 2024, SES announced an agreement to acquire Intelsat.
