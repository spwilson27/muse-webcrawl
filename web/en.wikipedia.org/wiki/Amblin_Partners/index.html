Amblin Partners, LLC is an American entertainment production company that develops and produces film, television, and other content. It was formed on December 16, 2015, as a joint venture between Steven Spielberg, Jeff Skoll of Participant, Anil Ambani of Reliance Entertainment, and Darren Throop of Entertainment One. The company operates through three production labels: Amblin Entertainment for family and genre films, DreamWorks Pictures for adult dramas, and Amblin Television.

History

Amblin Partners was created to replace the live-action DreamWorks studio arrangement after DreamWorks' distribution deal with Walt Disney Pictures expired. The new venture signed a five-year distribution, marketing, and loan agreement with Universal Pictures in December 2015, under which its films would be distributed and marketed by Universal. The arrangement was renewed in 2020.

Investment in the company came from Reliance Entertainment, Entertainment One (later acquired by Hasbro, and subsequently Lionsgate), Participant, and Alibaba Pictures, which took a minority stake in 2016 and agreed to co-produce and distribute films in China.

Film library

Films released under the Amblin Partners banners include The BFG (2016), The Girl on the Train (2016), Thank You for Your Service (2017), The Post (2017), Ready Player One (2018), Green Book (2018, with Participant), 1917 (2019), West Side Story (2021), The Fabelmans (2022), and The Color Purple (2023).

Divisions

  • Amblin Entertainment — family, adventure, and genre films
  • Amblin Television — television production, successor in part to DreamWorks Television
  • DreamWorks Pictures — adult-oriented dramas
