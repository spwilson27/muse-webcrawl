Servas International

Servas International is a non-profit organisation and hospitality exchange network founded in 1949 by Bob Luitweiler and others in Denmark. Its members offer free accommodation to travelling members in their homes, with the aim of fostering cross-cultural understanding, tolerance and world peace. The name Servas is the Esperanto word for "we serve".

History

Servas began as the "Peacebuilders" movement in the aftermath of the Second World War, inspired by work camps and reconciliation efforts in Europe. Bob Luitweiler, an American conscientious objector who had worked with refugees, helped found the network of "open doors" through which travellers could stay with local hosts. Servas International was formally constituted as an international federation in 1973 and holds consultative status with the United Nations Economic and Social Council.

Operation

Members are typically screened through an interview with a local coordinator before joining. Travellers traditionally carry a "letter of introduction" valid for one year and stay with hosts for a standard period of two nights (longer by agreement), sharing meals and daily life rather than simply receiving a bed. Hosts are listed in country host lists; since the 2010s these have increasingly been delivered through Servas's online platform, ServasOnline. National groups exist in more than 100 countries, with membership in the tens of thousands.
