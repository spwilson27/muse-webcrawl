Juliette Gordon Low

Juliette Gordon Low (née Gordon; October 31, 1860 – January 17, 1927) was the American founder of Girl Scouts of the USA. Inspired by the Scouting movement in England, she formed the first American Girl Guide troop in Savannah, Georgia, on March 12, 1912.

Life

Born in Savannah to a prominent family, "Daisy" Gordon married William Mackay Low in 1886 and spent much time in England, where she met Robert Baden-Powell. After her husband's death she devoted her fortune and energy to the girls' movement, personally recruiting leaders and lobbying for a charter. The organisation was renamed Girl Scouts in 1913 and grew nationwide. Low died of breast cancer in 1927; in 2012 she was posthumously awarded the Presidential Medal of Freedom.
