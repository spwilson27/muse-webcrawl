Grand Veymont

[Image: Grand Veymont]

The Grand Veymont is a mountain in the Vercors Massif in the French Prealps, in the department of Isère, Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes. Rising to 2,341 metres (7,680 ft), it is the highest point of the Vercors.

Geography

The summit lies on the eastern rim of the Vercors plateau, overlooking the commune of Gresse-en-Vercors. It forms part of the Vercors Regional Natural Park and the Réserve naturelle nationale des hauts plateaux du Vercors. Its long ridge is a landmark visible from Grenoble.

Access

The normal route from Gresse-en-Vercors passes the Pas de la Ville. The area is noted for ibex and other alpine wildlife, and for winter ski touring.
