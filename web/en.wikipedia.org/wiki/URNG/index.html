The Guatemalan National Revolutionary Unity (Spanish: Unidad Revolucionaria Nacional Guatemalteca, URNG-MAÍZ or simply URNG) is a Guatemalan political party, formerly the umbrella organisation of the four main Guatemalan guerrilla groups that fought the military government during the Guatemalan Civil War.

Formation and civil war

The URNG was formed on 7 February 1982 in Havana as the joint command of the Guerrilla Army of the Poor (EGP), the Revolutionary Organization of Armed People (ORPA), the Rebel Armed Forces (FAR), and the National Directing Core of the Guatemalan Party of Labour (PGT). It sought to coordinate the insurgency against Guatemala's military regimes during the 36-year internal armed conflict, in which an estimated 200,000 people—most of them indigenous Maya civilians killed by state forces, according to the UN-sponsored Historical Clarification Commission—lost their lives.

Peace accords and party politics

On 29 December 1996 the URNG signed the Agreement on a Firm and Lasting Peace with the government of Álvaro Arzú, ending the civil war. The URNG demobilised its combatants and registered as a legal political party in 1998. Its best-known candidate, Nobel Peace laureate Rigoberta Menchú, ran for president under allied banners in 2007 and 2011. The party has held seats in the Congress of Guatemala and in 2019 entered the alliance that became known as URNG–Winaq. The "MAÍZ" suffix in its registered name refers to the Movimiento Amplio de Izquierda (Broad Movement of the Left) with which it merged.
