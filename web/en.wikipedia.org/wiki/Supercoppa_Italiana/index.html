The Supercoppa Italiana (English: Italian Super Cup) is an annual super cup tournament in Italian football. Founded in 1988, it traditionally pitted the winners of Serie A against the winners of the Coppa Italia. Since the 2023 edition, the competition has been expanded to a four-team format including the Serie A runners-up and the Coppa Italia runners-up, staged as a mini-tournament usually held in Saudi Arabia.

The Supercoppa Italiana trophy

History

The first edition was held in 1988, when AC Milan defeated Sampdoria. For most of its history the match was a single game played at the start of the season, sometimes hosted abroad in countries including the United States, Libya, China, Qatar and Saudi Arabia as part of the league's international promotion.

Format

Under the four-team format adopted in 2023, two semi-finals are played, with the winners meeting in a final. Matches that are level after 90 minutes go directly to a penalty shoot-out. The tournament is typically staged in January.

Records

Juventus are the most successful club in the competition's history, followed by Inter Milan and AC Milan. The all-time top scorers include Paulo Dybala and Lautaro Martínez.
