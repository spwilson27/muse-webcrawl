LB Châteauroux

La Berrichonne de Châteauroux, commonly referred to as LB Châteauroux or simply Châteauroux, is a French association football club based in Châteauroux, Indre. The club was founded in 1916 and plays its home matches at the Stade Gaston-Petit, which has a capacity of about 17,000.

History

Châteauroux spent most of its history in the French second division. The club's sole season in the top flight came in 1997–98, when it played in Division 1 after winning promotion, only to be relegated immediately. Its most famous achievement came in 2004, when Châteauroux reached the final of the Coupe de France, losing 1–0 to Paris Saint-Germain. Because PSG had already qualified for the Champions League, Châteauroux took France's UEFA Cup place and made its only appearance in European competition in the 2004–05 season, losing in the first round to Club Brugge.

Later years

The club was relegated from Ligue 2 to the Championnat National in 2015, won promotion back in 2017 as National champions, and dropped again to the third tier in 2021. Châteauroux is known for its youth academy, which developed players such as Florent Malouda. The club's nickname is La Berri, after the historic province of Berry.
