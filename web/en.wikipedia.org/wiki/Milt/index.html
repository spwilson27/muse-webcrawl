Milt

Milt is the seminal fluid of fish, molluscs and other aquatic animals, released over eggs during spawning to fertilise them. The term also refers to the sperm sacs themselves, contained in the male reproductive glands.

As food

In Japanese cuisine, milt of cod, anglerfish and pufferfish, called shirako ("white children"), is a delicacy eaten steamed or fried. In Sicilian cooking, tuna milt (lattume) is used as a pasta topping, and herring milt is consumed in several northern European traditions. Milt is rich in protein and, in some species, in the protein protamine.
