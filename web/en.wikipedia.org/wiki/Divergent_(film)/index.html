Divergent (film)

Divergent is a 2014 American dystopian science fiction action film directed by Neil Burger, based on the 2011 novel of the same name by Veronica Roth. It is the first instalment in The Divergent Series. The film stars Shailene Woodley as Beatrice "Tris" Prior and Theo James as Tobias "Four" Eaton, with Kate Winslet, Ashley Judd, Jai Courtney, Ray Stevenson, and Miles Teller in supporting roles.

Plot

In a future, post-apocalyptic Chicago, society is divided into five factions based on personality virtues: Abnegation (the selfless), Amity (the peaceful), Candor (the honest), Dauntless (the brave), and Erudite (the intelligent). At sixteen, Beatrice Prior takes an aptitude test that reveals she is "Divergent" — suited to multiple factions and therefore dangerous to the regime. She chooses Dauntless, undergoes brutal initiation training under instructor Four, and becomes entangled in an Erudite plot, led by Jeanine Matthews, to overthrow the Abnegation-led government.

Production and reception

Summit Entertainment acquired the rights before the novel's publication; filming took place in Chicago in 2013. The film received mixed reviews praising its performances and entertainment value while drawing comparisons to The Hunger Games, and it grossed about $289 million worldwide against an $85 million budget.

Sequels

It was followed by The Divergent Series: Insurgent (2015) and The Divergent Series: Allegiant (2016); a planned fourth film was cancelled after Allegiant underperformed.
