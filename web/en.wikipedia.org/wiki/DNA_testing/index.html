DNA testing

DNA testing, also known as genetic testing, is the use of DNA profiles to determine ancestry, biological relationships, or predisposition to disease. It includes forensic identification, paternity testing, medical diagnostics, and consumer genealogical testing.

Types

Genealogical tests examine autosomal DNA, Y-DNA (paternal line) and mitochondrial DNA (maternal line) to estimate ethnicity and find relatives. Companies include 23andMe and AncestryDNA. Medical tests screen for conditions such as BRCA mutations and carrier status.

Forensics

DNA profiling, developed by Alec Jeffreys in 1984, revolutionised criminal investigation. Investigative genetic genealogy identified the Golden State Killer in 2018.

Issues

Concerns include privacy, data breaches, unexpected parentage findings, and limits of ethnicity estimates. See family history.
