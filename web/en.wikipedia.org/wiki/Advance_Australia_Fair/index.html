Advance Australia Fair

"Advance Australia Fair" is the national anthem of Australia. Written by Scottish-born composer Peter Dodds McCormick, it was first performed in Sydney in 1878 and proclaimed the national anthem in 1984.

History

The song competed for decades with "God Save the King/Queen", "Waltzing Matilda" and "Song of Australia". A 1977 plebiscite chose it as the national song, and it became the official anthem on 19 April 1984. Five original verses were reduced to two in the official version.

Lyric change

On 1 January 2021 the second line was amended from "For we are young and free" to "For we are one and free", recognising the long history of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples.

Lyrics (first verse)

Australians all let us rejoice, for we are one and free; we've golden soil and wealth for toil, our home is girt by sea...
