Jakarta

[Image: Jakarta skyline and the National Monument]
Jakarta skyline and the National Monument

Jakarta, officially the Special Capital Region of Jakarta, is the capital and largest city of Indonesia, with about 10.5 million residents in the city and over 32 million in the metropolitan area (Jabodetabek) — one of the world's largest urban agglomerations. It lies on the northwest coast of Java on Jakarta Bay.

Founded as Jayakarta in 1527 and renamed Batavia by the Dutch in 1619, the city was the capital of the Dutch East Indies; Sukarno proclaimed Indonesian independence there in 1945. Jakarta is the seat of ASEAN's secretariat. The city faces severe congestion, flooding and subsidence — parts of north Jakarta are sinking by up to 25 centimetres a year — which prompted Indonesia's decision to build a new capital, Nusantara, in Borneo; Nusantara became the official capital in law in 2024, though Jakarta remains the seat of government during the transition.
