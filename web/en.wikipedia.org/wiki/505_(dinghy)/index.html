505 (dinghy)

DesignerJohn Westell
Year1954
Crew2 (one on trapeze)
Hull length5.05 m

The International 505 is a two-person, one-design racing dinghy, 5.05 metres long, from which it takes its name. Designed by John Westell in 1954, it is one of the most popular high-performance trapeze dinghies in the world.

Design

The 505 features a powerful sail plan with a large spinnaker, a single trapeze for the crew, and extensive controls allowing the rig to be adjusted while racing. Hulls may be built of wood, foam-sandwich fibreglass, or epoxy composites under strict class measurement rules.

Racing

The class holds annual world championships that attract over a hundred boats, and it has been a proving ground for many elite sailors. Its blend of speed, tactical racing, and development within one-design limits keeps it competitive decades after its introduction.
