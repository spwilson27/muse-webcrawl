Grace and Frankie is an American comedy-drama television series created by Marta Kauffman and Howard J. Morris for Netflix. It stars Jane Fonda and Lily Tomlin as Grace Hanson and Frankie Bergstein, two women in their seventies whose husbands — law partners Robert (Martin Sheen) and Sol (Sam Waterston) — announce that they have been in love with each other for twenty years and intend to marry.

Premise

Thrown together by the upheaval, the uptight, martini-drinking Grace and the free-spirited, eccentric Frankie move into a shared beach house in La Jolla, California, and build an unlikely friendship and, eventually, a business together — including a line of products for older women. The series follows their families through divorce, new romances, ageing, illness, and career reinventions.

Production and reception

The series premiered on May 8, 2015. It ran for seven seasons and 94 episodes, concluding on April 29, 2022, making it one of Netflix's longest-running original series at the time. It received praise for its performances and its frank treatment of ageing, sexuality, and friendship among older adults, earning Emmy and Screen Actors Guild Award nominations for Tomlin and Fonda.
