Wendy's is an American international fast food restaurant chain founded by Dave Thomas in Columbus, Ohio, in 1969. It is one of the world's largest hamburger fast-food chains, with thousands of restaurants, most of them franchised.

History

Thomas named the chain after his daughter Melinda Lou "Wendy" Thomas, whose likeness inspired the logo. Wendy's distinguished itself with square, fresh-never-frozen beef patties, the Frosty dessert and made-to-order service. Famous campaigns include the 1984 "Where's the beef?" advertisements. In 2008 Wendy's merged with Triarc, owner of Arby's, forming Wendy's/Arby's Group; the company was later renamed The Wendy's Company.

Menu

The menu centres on hamburgers such as the Dave's Single and the Baconator, chicken sandwiches, salads, baked potatoes, chilli and the Frosty. See also Dave Thomas.
