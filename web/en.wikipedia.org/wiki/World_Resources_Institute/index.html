World Resources Institute

The World Resources Institute (WRI) is a global non-profit research organisation that seeks to protect the Earth's environment and improve people's lives. Founded in 1982 and headquartered in Washington, D.C., WRI works in more than 50 countries on climate change, energy, forests, water, food, cities and the ocean.

Activities

WRI produces data platforms such as Global Forest Watch, Aqueduct (water risk), Climate Watch and Resource Watch, and co-develops the Greenhouse Gas Protocol corporate accounting standards. Andrew Steer served as president from 2012 to 2021, succeeded by Ani Dasgupta.
