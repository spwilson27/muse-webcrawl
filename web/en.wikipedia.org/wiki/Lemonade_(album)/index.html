Lemonade is the sixth studio album by American singer Beyoncé, released on April 23, 2016, by Parkwood Entertainment and Columbia Records. It was accompanied by a 65-minute film of the same name, which premiered on HBO the same day. The album was released as a surprise, initially as an exclusive streaming release on Tidal.

Background and themes

Described by Beyoncé as a "visual album", Lemonade explores themes of infidelity, betrayal, anger, forgiveness and redemption within a marriage, widely interpreted as a response to her husband Jay-Z's alleged infidelity, most famously in the lyric "Becky with the good hair" from "Sorry". The work is structured around eleven chapters inspired by the stages of grief: Intuition, Denial, Anger, Apathy, Emptiness, Accountability, Reformation, Forgiveness, Resurrection, Hope and Redemption. It also celebrates Black womanhood, Southern Black culture and the singer's Louisiana heritage, drawing its title from her grandmother Agnéz Deréon's saying about making lemonade out of lemons.

Music and collaborations

The album spans many genres, including R&B, pop, hip hop, reggae, blues, rock, country and gospel. It features guest appearances by Jack White ("Don't Hurt Yourself"), the Weeknd ("6 Inch"), James Blake ("Forward") and Kendrick Lamar ("Freedom"). The country track "Daddy Lessons" was controversially rejected by the Country Music Association's committee for the 2017 Grammy Awards.

Reception and legacy

Lemonade was met with widespread critical acclaim and is frequently ranked among the greatest albums of all time. It won two Grammy Awards, including Best Urban Contemporary Album, and a Peabody Award for its film. The album debuted at number one on the US Billboard 200, and every track charted on the Billboard Hot 100. Its lead single "Formation", performed at Super Bowl 50, became an anthem associated with the Black Lives Matter movement.
