The Great Hall of the People (Chinese: 人民大会堂) is a large state building located at the western edge of Tiananmen Square in Xicheng District, Beijing. It is the meeting place of the National People's Congress (NPC), China's legislature, and is used for legislative and ceremonial activities of the Chinese Communist Party and the People's Republic of China, including state banquets and receptions for foreign heads of state.

History

The hall was one of the "Ten Great Buildings" constructed in just ten months to celebrate the tenth anniversary of the founding of the People's Republic of China, opening in September 1959. Tens of thousands of volunteer workers took part in its construction. The design, selected through a competition overseen by Premier Zhou Enlai, combines neoclassical monumental scale with Chinese decorative elements.

Interior

The building contains more than 300 meeting halls, offices, and reception rooms, including named halls for each province, region, and municipality of China, decorated with regional art. Its largest spaces are the Great Auditorium, seating about 10,000, and the Banqueting Hall, which can seat about 5,000 diners and famously hosted the banquet for US President Richard Nixon during his 1972 visit to China. The building appears on the reverse of the 100-yuan note of the fifth series of the renminbi.

Use

Besides sessions of the NPC and meetings of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference during the annual "Two Sessions", the hall hosts Communist Party congresses, diplomatic summits, concerts, and state funerals of senior leaders. When not in political use, parts are open to tourists.
