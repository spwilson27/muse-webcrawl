The 2003 World Series was the championship series of Major League Baseball's 2003 season, played between the National League champion Florida Marlins and the American League champion New York Yankees. The wild-card Marlins defeated the heavily favoured Yankees four games to two, winning their second championship in franchise history.

Background

The Marlins had started the season poorly and replaced manager Jeff Torborg with 72-year-old Jack McKeon in May, after which they produced one of the best records in baseball. They reached the Series after a famous National League Championship Series comeback against the Chicago Cubs. The Yankees, meanwhile, had beaten the Boston Red Sox in a gruelling seven-game ALCS decided by Aaron Boone's walk-off home run.

The series

Florida's young pitching staff, led by Josh Beckett, Dontrelle Willis and Brad Penny, subdued the Yankee line-up. Beckett was outstanding, and in Game 6 at Yankee Stadium he pitched a complete-game five-hit shutout, ending the series by tagging out Jorge Posada on a slow roller for the final out. Beckett was named the Series MVP. The Marlins' win is remembered as one of the great World Series upsets.
