Xabi Alonso

Xabier Alonso Olano (born 25 November 1981) is a Spanish professional football manager and former player who played as a central midfielder. Regarded as one of the finest deep-lying playmakers of his generation, he was noted for his passing range, composure and long-distance shooting.

Playing career

Born in Tolosa in the Basque Country, Alonso came through the youth system of Real Sociedad, the club where his father Periko Alonso had played. He broke into the first team under John Toshack and was made captain at a young age; in 2002–03 Sociedad unexpectedly finished second in La Liga, qualifying for the Champions League.

In 2004 he joined Liverpool, becoming a key part of Rafael Benítez's side. In his first season he won the UEFA Champions League, scoring the equalising goal in the famous 2005 final in Istanbul against AC Milan. He also won the FA Cup in 2006, scoring a memorable goal from his own half against Newcastle United the following season.

Alonso moved to Real Madrid in 2009 for around £30 million. There he formed a celebrated midfield, winning La Liga in 2011–12 and a second Champions League in 2014 ("La Décima"). In 2014 he joined Bayern Munich under Pep Guardiola, winning three Bundesliga titles before retiring in 2017.

With Spain he won UEFA Euro 2008, the 2010 FIFA World Cup and UEFA Euro 2012, earning 114 caps before retiring from international football in 2014.

Managerial career

After managing Real Sociedad B, Alonso was appointed head coach of Bayer Leverkusen in October 2022. In 2023–24 he led Leverkusen to their first-ever Bundesliga title, completing an undefeated domestic season — the first in Bundesliga history — and also won the DFB-Pokal, ending Bayern Munich's eleven-year title streak. In 2025 he left Leverkusen to become head coach of Real Madrid.
