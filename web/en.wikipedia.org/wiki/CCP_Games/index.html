CCP Games

Industry: Video games
Founded: 1997
Headquarters: Reykjavík, Iceland
Key people: Hilmar Veigar Pétursson (CEO)
Products: EVE Online

CCP Games (Crowd Control Productions) is an Icelandic video game developer based in Reykjavík, Iceland, best known for developing and publishing the space-based massively multiplayer online game EVE Online. The company was founded in 1997 by Reynir Harðarson, Ívar Kristjánsson, and Thorolfur Beck.

History

CCP's first product was the board game Hættuspil (Danger Game), a social deduction game. The company used revenue from the board game to begin development of EVE Online, which launched in May 2003. EVE Online became notable for its single-shard universe, player-driven economy, and large-scale space battles.

In 2006, CCP merged with White Wolf Publishing, the American role-playing game publisher. CCP later developed the first-person shooter DUST 514 (2013), set in the EVE universe, and the virtual-reality game EVE: Valkyrie. In 2018, CCP was acquired by South Korean publisher Pearl Abyss, the developer of Black Desert Online, for approximately US$425 million. The company maintains studios in London and Shanghai in addition to its Reykjavík headquarters.
