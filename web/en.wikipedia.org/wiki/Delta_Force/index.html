Delta Force, formally the 1st Special Forces Operational Detachment-Delta (1st SFOD-D), is a US Army special missions unit under Joint Special Operations Command, specialising in counter-terrorism, hostage rescue, direct action and reconnaissance.

History

Founded by Colonel Charles Beckwith in 1977, modelled on the British SAS, Delta's first major mission was the failed 1980 Iran hostage rescue, Operation Eagle Claw, which spurred creation of USSOCOM. It later served in Grenada, Panama, the Gulf War, Somalia (the 1993 Battle of Mogadishu), Afghanistan and Iraq, including operations against ISIS leadership.

Selection

Recruits, mostly from Ranger and Special Forces backgrounds, endure a gruelling selection and operator training course at Fort Bragg (Fort Liberty), North Carolina; the unit's existence was long not officially acknowledged.
