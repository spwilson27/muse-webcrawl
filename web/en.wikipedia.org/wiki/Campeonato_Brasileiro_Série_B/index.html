Campeonato Brasileiro Série B

The Campeonato Brasileiro Série B, commonly known as Série B, is the second tier of the Brazilian football league system. It is contested by 20 clubs, with the top four promoted to Série A and the bottom four relegated to Série C.

Founded: 1971
Country: Brazil
Confederation: CONMEBOL
Teams: 20
Promotion to: Série A

History

The competition was first held in 1971 and has been played in various formats. Since 2006 it has used a double round-robin league format, with each team playing the others home and away over 38 rounds.

Format

Twenty teams play 38 matches. The four highest finishers earn promotion to the top-flight Série A for the following season, while the four lowest are relegated to Série C.

Champions

Successful clubs in Série B history include Coritiba, Goiás, Palmeiras, Vasco da Gama and Botafogo, several of which won the second division after relegation from the top flight.
