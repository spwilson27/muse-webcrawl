List of busiest airports by cargo traffic

Notes

Memphis is the home of the FedEx Express World Hub, Louisville hosts UPS Worldport, and Leipzig/Halle hosts DHL's main European hub; Cologne Bonn is a major UPS/FedEx base and Luxembourg is home to Cargolux. Anchorage's high ranking reflects its role as a technical stop for trans-Pacific freighter flights, whose cargo is counted though it does not clear customs there.
