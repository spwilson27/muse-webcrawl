Milk (2008 film)

Directed by: Gus Van Sant
Written by: Dustin Lance Black
Starring: Sean Penn, Emile Hirsch, Josh Brolin, Diego Luna, James Franco
Release date: 2008
Country: United States
Language: English

Milk is a 2008 American biographical film based on the life of gay rights activist and politician Harvey Milk, who was the first openly gay person to be elected to public office in California, as a member of the San Francisco Board of Supervisors. Directed by Gus Van Sant from a screenplay by Dustin Lance Black, the film stars Sean Penn as Milk.

Plot

The film follows Milk from his arrival in San Francisco in the early 1970s, where he opens a camera shop on Castro Street, through his growing political activism, his repeated campaigns for office, his election in 1977, and his fights against statewide anti-gay initiatives, including the Briggs Initiative (Proposition 6). It culminates in his assassination in November 1978 by former supervisor Dan White, played by Josh Brolin, who also murdered Mayor George Moscone.

Reception and awards

Milk was released to critical acclaim. At the 81st Academy Awards it received eight nominations and won two: Best Actor for Sean Penn and Best Original Screenplay for Dustin Lance Black. Critics praised Penn's performance and the film's recreation of 1970s San Francisco, where much of it was shot on location, including at Milk's former Castro Camera shop.
