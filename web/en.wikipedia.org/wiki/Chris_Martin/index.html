Chris Martin

Christopher Anthony John Martin (born 2 March 1977) is an English singer, songwriter, musician and producer. He is best known as the lead vocalist, pianist, rhythm guitarist and co-founder of the rock band Coldplay. Born in Exeter, Devon, Martin went to University College London, where he formed Coldplay with Jonny Buckland, Guy Berryman and Will Champion.

Early life

Martin was born in Whitestone, Exeter, the eldest of five children. He was educated at Exeter Cathedral School and Sherborne School in Dorset, where he formed his first band, The Rocking Honkies. At University College London he studied Ancient World Studies and met his future bandmates.

Career with Coldplay

Coldplay achieved worldwide fame with their debut album Parachutes (2000) and the single "Yellow". Subsequent albums — A Rush of Blood to the Head (2002), X&Y (2005), Viva la Vida or Death and All His Friends (2008), Ghost Stories (2014), A Head Full of Dreams (2015), Everyday Life (2019), Music of the Spheres (2021) and Moon Music (2024) — made them one of the best-selling music acts of all time, with over 100 million albums sold. Martin is the band's principal songwriter, known for hits including "Clocks", "Viva la Vida", "Paradise" and "A Sky Full of Stars".

Other work and activism

Martin has written songs for other artists, collaborated with Jay-Z, Kanye West, Rihanna, BTS and many others, and is a patron of charities including Oxfam — for which he has campaigned on fair trade — and Global Citizen.
