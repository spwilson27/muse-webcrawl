List of longest-reigning monarchs

This is a list of the longest-reigning monarchs of sovereign states whose reigns can be verified by reliable sources. The longest verified reign belongs to Louis XIV of France, who ruled for 72 years and 110 days.

Louis XIV, the "Sun King", reigned for over 72 years.

Longest-reigning current monarchs

Following the death of Elizabeth II in 2022, the longest-reigning current monarchs include Hassanal Bolkiah of Brunei (reigning since 1967), Carl XVI Gustaf of Sweden (since 1973) and Margrethe II of Denmark until her abdication in 2024 (since 1972).
