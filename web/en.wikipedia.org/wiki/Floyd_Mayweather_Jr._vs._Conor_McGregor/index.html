Floyd Mayweather Jr. vs. Conor McGregor, billed as The Money Fight and The Biggest Fight in Combat Sports History, was a professional boxing match between undefeated eleven-time five-division champion Floyd Mayweather Jr. and two-division mixed martial arts champion and UFC lightweight champion Conor McGregor. It took place at T-Mobile Arena in Paradise, Nevada on 26 August 2017, at a catchweight of 154 lbs (69.9 kg), over 12 rounds.

The fight

McGregor, making his professional boxing debut, started aggressively and took the early rounds on many unofficial cards with unorthodox movement and counters. As the fight progressed, McGregor fatigued and Mayweather — fighting more aggressively than usual — took control in the middle and late rounds. In the tenth round, with McGregor pinned against the ropes and unable to defend himself effectively, referee Robert Byrd stopped the contest, giving Mayweather a technical knockout victory and a 50–0 professional record. The official scorecards at the stoppage had Mayweather ahead on all three.

Commercial performance

The bout generated about 4.3 million pay-per-view buys in the United States, the second-highest total in history behind Mayweather's 2015 fight with Manny Pacquiao, with total revenue estimated in excess of $600 million. Mayweather was guaranteed $100 million and McGregor $30 million before pay-per-view shares; both ultimately earned considerably more. The event drew criticism from boxing purists as a spectacle but was widely credited with demonstrating McGregor's competitiveness for a debutant.
