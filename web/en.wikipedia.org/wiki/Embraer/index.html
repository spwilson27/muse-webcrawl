Embraer

Embraer S.A. is a Brazilian multinational aerospace corporation that designs and manufactures commercial, military, executive, and agricultural aircraft, and provides aeronautical services. Founded in 1969 by the Brazilian government, it is headquartered in São José dos Campos, São Paulo.

History

Embraer (Empresa Brasileira de Aeronáutica) was created as a state-owned company and produced early successes such as the EMB 110 Bandeirante turboprop and the EMB 312 Tucano trainer. Privatized in 1994, the company expanded into the regional jet market with the ERJ family and later the E-Jet family, becoming one of the largest manufacturers of civil aircraft of up to 150 seats.

Products

Commercial lines include the ERJ 135/140/145 family, the E-Jets (E170–E195) and the second-generation E-Jets E2 (E175-E2, E190-E2, E195-E2). Military products include the A-29 Super Tucano light attack aircraft and the C-390 Millennium transport. The executive aviation division produces the Phenom, Legacy, Praetor, and Lineage business jets.
