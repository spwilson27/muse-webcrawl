Kingspan Stadium, historically known as Ravenhill Stadium, is a rugby union stadium in Belfast, Northern Ireland, and the home ground of Ulster Rugby. Opened in 1923, it is owned by the Irish Rugby Football Union.

History

Ravenhill hosted its first international in 1924 and was for decades the venue for Ireland home matches played in Belfast, as well as Ulster Schools' Cup finals. A major redevelopment completed in 2014 raised capacity to just over 18,000, with new stands at both ends and along the Memorial side. In June 2014 the naming rights were sold to the Kingspan Group, an Irish building materials company, in a ten-year deal, and the ground was renamed the Kingspan Stadium. The stadium hosted matches during the 2017 Women's Rugby World Cup and regularly stages United Rugby Championship and European fixtures.
