SeaWorld is a chain of marine mammal parks, oceanariums and animal theme parks in the United States and the United Arab Emirates, operated by United Parks & Resorts (formerly SeaWorld Parks & Entertainment). The first park opened in San Diego, California, in 1964; parks in Orlando, Florida, and San Antonio, Texas, followed, and SeaWorld Abu Dhabi opened in 2023.

A SeaWorld park.

Attractions and animals

The parks combine aquariums and marine animal exhibits — dolphins, sea lions, sharks, penguins, rays and beluga whales — with roller coasters and rides. Historically the signature attraction was the orca (killer whale) show, beginning with the whale Shamu, whose name became a stage name for successive performing orcas.

Controversy

SeaWorld has long been criticised by animal-welfare groups for keeping cetaceans in captivity. Scrutiny intensified after the 2010 death of trainer Dawn Brancheau, killed by the orca Tilikum, and the 2013 documentary film Blackfish. Attendance and share price fell; in 2016 the company announced it would end orca breeding and phase out theatrical orca shows, replacing them with "Orca Encounter" educational presentations. SeaWorld also operates a large marine animal rescue and rehabilitation programme.
