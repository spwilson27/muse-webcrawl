Needham Research Institute

The Needham Research Institute (NRI) is a research centre in Cambridge, England, devoted to the study of the history of science, technology and medicine in East Asia. It grew from the work of Joseph Needham (1900–1995), author of the monumental series Science and Civilisation in China.

Library and work

The institute's building on Robinson College grounds, opened in 1991, houses the Needham Library, one of the finest collections on Chinese science outside China. The NRI supports scholars, publishes the Science and Civilisation in China series with Cambridge University Press, and hosts research on China, Japan and Korea's scientific heritage.
