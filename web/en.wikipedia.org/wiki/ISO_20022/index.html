ISO 20022 is an ISO standard for electronic data interchange between financial institutions. It describes a metadata repository containing descriptions of messages and business processes, from which XML (and other syntax) message schemas are generated. It covers payments, securities, trade services, cards and foreign exchange.

Overview

ISO 20022 uses a modelling methodology based on UML: business processes and data are modelled independently of the message syntax, and messages (such as pacs.008 for a customer credit transfer or pain.001 for payment initiation) are generated from the model. This allows much richer, structured data than legacy formats — for example full structured remittance information and party names and addresses — improving straight-through processing, screening and reconciliation.

Migration

Major payment systems have migrated to ISO 20022, including TARGET2/T2 in the euro area, CHAPS in the UK, Fedwire in the United States and many real-time payment schemes such as SEPA Instant. SWIFT began cross-border payment migration (the CBPR+ programme) in March 2023, with a coexistence period with legacy MT messages scheduled to end in November 2025.
