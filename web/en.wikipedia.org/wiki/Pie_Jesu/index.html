Pie Jesu

Pie Jesu ("Merciful Jesus") is a text from the final couplet of the hymn Dies irae and is often included as a movement in musical settings of the Requiem Mass. The full text is:

Pie Jesu Domine, dona eis requiem (sempiternam).
Merciful Lord Jesus, grant them rest (everlasting).

In Requiem settings

Composers including Gabriel Fauré, Maurice Duruflé, John Rutter, and Karl Jenkins have set the Pie Jesu as a separate movement, often for solo soprano or treble voice. Andrew Lloyd Webber's Pie Jesu from his Requiem (1985), written in memory of his father, became especially popular as a standalone piece, frequently sung by boy trebles and sopranos such as Sarah Brightman.

Character

In contrast to the wrathful imagery of the Dies irae sequence to which it belongs, the Pie Jesu is a gentle prayer for mercy and rest, and its settings are typically lyrical, quiet, and consoling.
