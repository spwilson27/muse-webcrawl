Prix Renaudot

The Prix Renaudot (French: Prix Théophraste-Renaudot) is a French literary award created in 1926 by ten art critics awaiting the announcement of the winner of the Prix Goncourt. It is generally announced on the same day, at the same place (the restaurant Drouant in Paris), and is considered one of the most prestigious literary prizes in France, second only to the Goncourt itself. The prize is named after Théophraste Renaudot (1586–1653), founder of France's first newspaper, La Gazette.

History

In 1926, while waiting for the Goncourt jury's deliberations, ten journalists and critics decided to create their own prize. The first winner was Armand Salacrou for Le Patron, although in that first year the laureate was not announced. Since 2003 the jury has also awarded a prize for essays (Prix Renaudot de l'essai), and there are related prizes for pocket editions and for lycée students (Prix Renaudot des lycéens).

Notable winners

  • 1929 — Louis-Ferdinand Céline, Voyage au bout de la nuit (Journey to the End of the Night) — the Renaudot went to Marcel Aymé's La Table aux crevés that year; Céline's novel instead won the Renaudot jury's favour in retrospect after famously losing the Goncourt.
  • 1947 — Jean Cayrol, Je vivrai l'amour des autres
  • 1961 — Roger Peyrefitte, Les Ambassades
  • 1970 — Bertrand Poirot-Delpech, La Folle de Camargue
  • 1987 — René-Jean Clot, L'Enfant halluciné
  • 1995 — Patrick Besson, Les Braban
  • 2001 — Ahmadou Kourouma, Allah n'est pas obligé
  • 2007 — Daniel Pennac, Chagrin d'école
  • 2011 — Emmanuel Carrère, Limonov
  • 2015 — Delphine de Vigan, D'après une histoire vraie
  • 2019 — Sylvain Tesson, La Panthère des neiges
  • 2021 — Amélie Nothomb, Premier sang

Relationship with the Prix Goncourt

The Renaudot jury includes some critics who follow the same autumn literary season as the Goncourt. Tradition holds that the Renaudot can serve as a "consolation" prize — a book that narrowly misses the Goncourt may win the Renaudot — though the juries are independent and the outcomes vary.
