BMW i4

BMW i4 (G26)
Manufacturer: BMW
Production: 2021–present
Class: Compact executive liftback
Powertrain: Battery electric, single or dual motor
Range: up to approx. 600 km (WLTP, variant dependent)

The BMW i4 is a battery electric compact executive car manufactured by BMW since 2021. Sold as a five-door liftback Gran Coupé sharing its body with the 4 Series Gran Coupé, it was BMW's first electric model in the D-segment.

Variants

Launch variants were the rear-wheel-drive eDrive40 (340 hp) and the dual-motor M50 (544 hp), the first electric model from BMW M GmbH. Later additions include the eDrive35 and xDrive40, and the high-performance M60. Batteries of roughly 70–84 kWh usable capacity provide DC fast charging up to about 200 kW.

Reception

The i4 was praised for performance, refinement and build quality, and became one of BMW's best-selling EVs, competing with the Tesla Model 3 and Polestar 2.
