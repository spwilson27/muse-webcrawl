Largest buildings

This article lists the largest buildings in the world by usable floor area and by volume. The largest building by floor area is generally considered to be the New Century Global Center in Chengdu, China, while the largest by volume is the Boeing Everett Factory in Washington State, United States, originally built to assemble the Boeing 747.

Largest by volume

The Boeing Everett Factory encloses about 13.3 million cubic metres and covers nearly 40 hectares of floor space under a single roof, making it the largest building by volume. Other very large-volume buildings include the Vehicle Assembly Building at NASA's Kennedy Space Center in Florida (about 3.66 million m³), the Jean-Luc Lagardère plant in Toulouse used for the Airbus A380, and Boeing's factory in Charleston, South Carolina. The Aerium hangar in Germany, built for airships and now housing the Tropical Islands resort, is among the largest free-standing halls.

Categories

Rankings depend heavily on definitions: whether one counts a single building or a connected complex, whether parking and plant areas are included, and whether the measurement is gross external area or usable internal floor space. Airport terminals dominate by floor area because of their vast single-level concourses, while factories and assembly halls dominate by volume because of their great internal heights.
