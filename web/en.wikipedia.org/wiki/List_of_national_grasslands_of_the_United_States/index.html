List of national grasslands of the United States

National grasslands are protected areas of prairie land managed by the United States Forest Service under Title III of the Bankhead–Jones Farm Tenant Act of 1937. There are 20 national grasslands, totalling about 3.8 million acres (1.5 million hectares), located mainly in the Great Plains states. They are managed for multiple uses including grazing, recreation, wildlife habitat and mineral production, in the same manner as national forests.

History

Most national grasslands were assembled from submarginal farmland purchased by the federal government after the Dust Bowl of the 1930s, when drought and poor farming practices devastated the plains. The Land Utilization Program bought worn-out farms and reseeded them with native grasses; administration passed to the Forest Service in 1960.

Little Missouri National Grassland, at just over one million acres, is the largest; McClellan Creek in Texas is the smallest.
