Work Song (song)

ComposerNat Adderley
Released1960
GenreJazz, hard bop

"Work Song" is a jazz standard written by cornetist Nat Adderley, first recorded by the Cannonball Adderley Quintet on the 1960 album Them Dirty Blues. Built on a blues form with a driving, work-chant feel, it became one of Nat Adderley's best-known compositions and a signature tune for his brother Julian "Cannonball" Adderley's band.

Versions

Lyrics were later added by Oscar Brown Jr., and vocal versions were recorded by singers including Nina Simone and Sammy Davis Jr. The tune has been widely covered by jazz musicians and remains a staple jam-session blues, noted for its call-and-response riff and sly evocation of a chain-gang chant.
