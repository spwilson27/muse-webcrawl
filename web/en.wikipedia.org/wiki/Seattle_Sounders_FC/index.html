Seattle Sounders FC is an American professional soccer club based in Seattle, Washington. The Sounders compete in Major League Soccer (MLS) as a member of the Western Conference. The team was established in 2007 and began play in 2009 as an MLS expansion team. The club plays its home matches at Lumen Field, which it shares with the Seattle Seahawks of the NFL.

The Sounders inherited their name from earlier Seattle soccer teams, including the North American Soccer League side of 1974–1983 and the USL First Division team that played from 1994 to 2008. The club is owned by a group led by Adrian Hanauer, with minority stakes held by figures including the estate of Paul Allen, actor Drew Carey, and rapper Macklemore. Drew Carey introduced the tradition of a marching band, the Sound Wave, and a fan vote on the general manager position every four years.

History

Seattle was awarded an MLS expansion franchise on November 13, 2007. The team set league attendance records from its first season, regularly drawing crowds above 40,000 and helping drive the growth of MLS attendance league-wide. Under inaugural head coach Sigi Schmid, the Sounders won the U.S. Open Cup in each of their first three seasons (2009, 2010, 2011) and added a fourth in 2014, the same year they won the Supporters' Shield for the best regular-season record.

After Schmid's departure in 2016, assistant coach Brian Schmetzer took over and led the club to its first MLS Cup championship that December, defeating Toronto FC on penalties. The Sounders won a second MLS Cup in 2019, again over Toronto, before a record home crowd of 69,274. In 2022 the club became the first MLS team to win the modern CONCACAF Champions League, defeating Mexico's Pumas UNAM in the final and qualifying for the FIFA Club World Cup. The Sounders also won the Leagues Cup in 2025.

Supporters

The club's principal supporters' groups include the Emerald City Supporters, Gorilla FC, North End Faithful, and Eastside Supporters. The Sounders contest the Cascadia Cup with regional rivals the Portland Timbers and Vancouver Whitecaps FC, one of the most intense rivalries in North American soccer, and the Heritage Cup with the San Jose Earthquakes.

Notable players

Notable players in club history include Fredy Montero, Osvaldo Alonso, Clint Dempsey, Obafemi Martins, Nicolás Lodeiro, Raúl Ruidíaz, Jordan Morris, and goalkeeper Stefan Frei, whose save in the 2016 MLS Cup final is regarded as one of the greatest in league history.
