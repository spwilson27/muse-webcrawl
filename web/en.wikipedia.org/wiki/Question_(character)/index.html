Question (character)

The Question
First appearance: Blue Beetle #1 (June 1967)
Created by: Steve Ditko
Alter ego: Charles Victor Szasz (Vic Sage); Renee Montoya
Publisher: Charlton Comics; later DC Comics

The Question is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books, originally published by Charlton Comics and later acquired by DC Comics. Created by artist and writer Steve Ditko, the character first appeared in Blue Beetle #1 (June 1967). The Question's secret identity is Vic Sage, an investigative journalist in Hub City who uses a mask made of the artificial skin Pseudoderm to make his face appear completely blank and featureless.

Publication history

Ditko created the Question as an outlet for his Objectivist philosophy, in the tradition of his earlier character Mr. A, presenting crime stories with a stark black-and-white moral outlook. After Charlton's line folded, DC acquired the character and integrated him into its universe following Crisis on Infinite Earths.

A critically acclaimed solo series by Dennis O'Neil and Denys Cowan (1987–1990) recast Sage as a student of Zen philosophy, softening the character's earlier absolutism. In the 2006–2007 series 52, a dying Sage trains Gotham detective Renee Montoya as his successor, and she became the second Question.

Powers and personality

The Question has no superpowers. He is a skilled martial artist, detective, and journalist, aided by the Pseudoderm mask and a gas that temporarily changes the colour of his hair and clothes. Later incarnations, particularly the animated version in Justice League Unlimited, emphasise his obsession with conspiracy theories, making him an eccentric but uncannily perceptive investigator.

Influence

Alan Moore originally intended to use the Charlton characters in Watchmen; when DC objected, he created Rorschach as an analogue of the Question, inheriting the character's uncompromising worldview.
