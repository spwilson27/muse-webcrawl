Canvas (learning management system)

Canvas is a web-based learning management system developed by Instructure. Launched in 2011, it is used by schools, universities and businesses to manage course content, assignments, grading and communication.

Developer: Instructure
Released: 2011
Type: Learning management system

Features

Canvas offers modules, quizzes, discussions, a gradebook, SpeedGrader annotation tools, and integrations with third-party apps via LTI standards, plus mobile apps for students and teachers.

Adoption

It became one of the most widely used LMS platforms in North American higher education during the 2010s.
