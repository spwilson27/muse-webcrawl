Yang Hyun-suk (Korean: 양현석; born January 9, 1970) is a South Korean music executive, record producer, and former singer and dancer. He rose to fame as a member of the pioneering group Seo Taiji and Boys during the 1990s and went on to found YG Entertainment, one of the largest entertainment companies in South Korea, home to acts such as Big Bang, 2NE1, Winner, iKon, Blackpink, Treasure, and Babymonster.

Career

Seo Taiji and Boys

Yang debuted in 1992 as the dancer and rapper of Seo Taiji and Boys, whose fusion of hip-hop, new jack swing, and social commentary transformed Korean popular music and is widely credited with launching modern K-pop. The group disbanded in 1996 at the height of its popularity.

YG Entertainment

In 1996 Yang founded Hyun Planning, later YG Entertainment. The label developed a hip-hop-oriented identity with artists such as Jinusean, 1TYM, Se7en, and Gummy before achieving global success with Big Bang (debuted 2006), 2NE1 (2009), and Psy, whose "Gangnam Style" became a worldwide phenomenon in 2012. Blackpink, launched in 2016, became one of the best-selling girl groups of all time. Yang also served as a judge on the SBS audition program K-pop Star and produced survival shows including Mix Nine and YG Treasure Box.

Resignation and return

In June 2019, amid investigations connected to the Burning Sun scandal and allegations involving YG artists, Yang resigned from all positions at YG Entertainment. He was later convicted of gambling overseas and fined, and was tried on other charges. In December 2022 YG announced his return as executive producer; he subsequently led the launch of the girl group Babymonster in 2023–2024 and announced plans for the comebacks and tours of Blackpink, Treasure, and a reunited 2NE1.

Personal life

Yang is married to Lee Eun-ju, a former member of the YG girl group Swi.T, with whom he has two children.
