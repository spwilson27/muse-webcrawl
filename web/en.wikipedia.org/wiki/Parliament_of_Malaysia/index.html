Parliament of Malaysia

TypeBicameral
HousesDewan Negara (Senate); Dewan Rakyat (House of Representatives)
Seats70 senators; 222 members of the Dewan Rakyat
LocationKuala Lumpur, Malaysia

The Parliament of Malaysia is the national legislature of Malaysia, modelled on the Westminster system. It consists of the Yang di-Pertuan Agong (King) and two houses: the Dewan Negara (Senate) with 70 members, most appointed, and the directly elected Dewan Rakyat (House of Representatives) with 222 members.

Functions

Parliament enacts federal law, approves taxation and expenditure, and scrutinises the executive. Money bills originate in the Dewan Rakyat; the Dewan Negara can delay but not ultimately block most legislation.

History

The first federal legislature met after independence in 1959, and the Malaysian Parliament House in Kuala Lumpur, overlooking the Lake Gardens, was completed in 1963. Parliament was suspended during the 1969–1971 emergency period, when the National Operations Council ruled by decree.
