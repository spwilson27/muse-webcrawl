Kessel Run

The Kessel Run is a fictional hyperspace smuggling route in the Star Wars universe, used by smugglers to transport the drug spice from the mines of the planet Kessel, evading Imperial patrols.

"In less than twelve parsecs"

In Star Wars (1977), Han Solo boasts that his ship, the Millennium Falcon, "made the Kessel Run in less than twelve parsecs". Because a parsec is a unit of distance rather than time, the line long drew comment. Later works explained it as a distance feat: the standard route is about 18 parsecs, arcing around the Maw black-hole cluster, and a daring pilot can shorten the trip by flying closer to the cluster. The film Solo: A Star Wars Story (2018) depicts the run on screen.

In popular culture

The phrase has entered general usage as a byword for an implausible boast, and the route is a staple of Star Wars expanded media.
