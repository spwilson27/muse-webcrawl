MIPI Alliance

The MIPI Alliance is a global business alliance that develops technical specifications for the mobile ecosystem, including interfaces for cameras, displays, sensors, batteries and radio components in smartphones and other devices.

MIPI Alliance
Founded: 2003
Type: Industry consortium
Focus: Mobile interface specifications
Key specs: CSI-2, DSI, M-PHY, I3C

Specifications

Well-known MIPI specifications include CSI-2 (camera serial interface), DSI (display serial interface), the M-PHY and D-PHY physical layers, and I3C, a successor to I2C for sensor connectivity. These standards are used in nearly every modern smartphone.

Membership

The Alliance has hundreds of member companies across the semiconductor, device-manufacturing and software industries. Specifications are developed in working groups and licensed to members.
