Old Fashioned

The old fashioned is a cocktail made by muddling sugar with bitters and water, adding whiskey (typically rye or bourbon) or, less commonly, brandy, and garnishing with an orange slice or zest and a cocktail cherry. It is traditionally served with ice in an old fashioned glass (also known as a rocks glass), which predated the cocktail.

Developed during the 19th century, the drink gave its name to the glass in which it was originally served. It is widely regarded as one of the archetypal cocktails, representing an early style of mixed drink that combined spirits, bitters, water, and sugar.
