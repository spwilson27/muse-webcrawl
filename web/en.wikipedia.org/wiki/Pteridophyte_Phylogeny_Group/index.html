Pteridophyte Phylogeny Group

The Pteridophyte Phylogeny Group (PPG) is an informal international group of systematic botanists who collaborate to establish a consensus classification of lycophytes and ferns (pteridophytes) reflecting current phylogenetic knowledge.

PPG I

In 2016 the group published PPG I in the Journal of Systematics and Evolution, a community-derived classification recognising two classes, 11 orders, 48 families and 319 genera, modelled on the approach of the Angiosperm Phylogeny Group classifications for flowering plants.
