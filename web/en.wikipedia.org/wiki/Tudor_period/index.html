Tudor period

The Tudor period in England and Wales lasted from 1485 to 1603, spanning the reigns of the Tudor monarchs from Henry VII to Elizabeth I, between the medieval period and the Stuart era.

Overview

Beginning with Henry VII's victory at Bosworth Field ending the Wars of the Roses, the period saw the English Reformation under Henry VIII, the dissolution of the monasteries, war and reconciliation with Scotland, the reigns of Mary I and the Elizabethan era — a flowering of literature (Shakespeare, Marlowe), exploration and the defeat of the Spanish Armada in 1588.

Society

Population grew, enclosures and price inflation transformed rural life, and central government and Parliament strengthened. See House of Tudor and Elizabethan era.
