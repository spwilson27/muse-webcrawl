Hope Summers (character)


Hope Summers, the mutant messiah

Hope Summers is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Mike Carey and artist Chris Bachalo, she first appeared in X-Men #205 (2007) during the "Messiah Complex" storyline. Hope is the first mutant born after the events of "House of M," in which the Scarlet Witch depowered most of the world's mutants, and she is regarded by many as a "mutant messiah."

Fictional biography

Hope was born in Cooperstown, Alaska, shortly after the Decimation. Her birth was detected by Cerebro, and both the X-Men and their enemies raced to find her. She was rescued as an infant by the time-travelling mutant Cable, who raised her in the future as his adopted daughter, teaching her survival skills while fleeing the traitorous Bishop, who believed Hope would one day cause a catastrophe. Returning to the present as a teenager, Hope helped reignite the X-gene and later became a member of the Five on Krakoa, the group responsible for mutant resurrection.

Powers and abilities

Hope's mutant power is power mimicry: she can copy the powers of nearby mutants at their maximum potential without the physical strain the original users experience. Her abilities are connected to the Phoenix Force, and during the "Avengers vs. X-Men" storyline she was a prospective host for it. She is also a trained soldier and tactician thanks to her upbringing with Cable.

In other media

Hope Summers is referenced in the film Deadpool 2 (2018), and appears in animated series and video games including X-Men: Battle of the Atom and Marvel Puzzle Quest.
