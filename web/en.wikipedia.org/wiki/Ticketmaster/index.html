Ticketmaster Entertainment, LLC is an American ticket sales and distribution company. Founded in 1976, it sells and distributes tickets for concerts, sporting events, theatre and other live entertainment on behalf of venues, promoters and teams. It is the largest ticketing company in the world, operating in dozens of countries, and since 2010 has been part of Live Nation Entertainment.

History

Ticketmaster was founded at Arizona State University in 1976 as a seller of computerized ticketing systems, initially licensing ticketing software to venues before moving into ticket sales itself. Under CEO Fred Rosen in the 1980s it shifted to selling tickets directly under exclusive contracts with venues, a model that made it dominant in North America. In 2010, amid controversy, Ticketmaster merged with concert promoter Live Nation to form Live Nation Entertainment, approved by regulators subject to a consent decree in the United States.

Criticism and legal issues

The company has long been criticised over service fees, exclusive venue contracts and its position in primary and secondary ticketing. In 2022, the collapse of its systems during the presale for Taylor Swift's Eras Tour drew intense public and political scrutiny, prompting a US Senate hearing. In 2024 the US Department of Justice and state attorneys general filed an antitrust suit seeking to break up Live Nation and Ticketmaster, alleging monopolisation of live entertainment markets — a case the company has contested.
