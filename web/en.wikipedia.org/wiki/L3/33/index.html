The L3/33, officially the Carro Veloce CV-33, was an Italian tankette of the interwar period, built by Ansaldo from 1933 and based on the British Carden Loyd tankette concept. It weighed about 3.1 tonnes and carried a two-man crew protected by up to 14 mm of armour.

Service

Armed initially with a single 6.5 mm machine gun (later two 8 mm guns in the L3/35 variant), the L3 served in the Second Italo-Ethiopian War, the Spanish Civil War, and the early campaigns of the Second World War in North Africa, Greece, and the Soviet Union. Its thin armour and light armament made it vulnerable to anti-tank weapons and even heavy machine guns, but it was widely exported and remained in Italian service throughout the war.
