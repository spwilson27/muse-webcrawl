Durangaldea (Spanish: Duranguesado) is a comarca in the province of Biscay in the Basque Country, northern Spain. It occupies the southeastern part of the province, centred on the town of Durango and the upper valley of the Ibaizabal River, and borders Gipuzkoa and Álava as well as the province of Burgos.

Geography

The comarca is mountainous, lying at the edge of the Urkiola Natural Park, whose limestone peaks such as Anboto — associated in Basque mythology with the goddess Mari — dominate the skyline. The Ibaizabal flows westward through the valley toward Bilbao.

Municipalities

Besides Durango, the comarca includes Abadiño, Amorebieta-Etxano, Atxondo, Berriz, Elorrio, Ermua, Garai, Iurreta, Izurtza, Mallabia, Mañaria, Otxandio and Zaldibar, among others. Historically the area formed the Merindad de Durango, a lordship with its own institutions that was incorporated into the Lordship of Biscay in the 13th century.

Economy and culture

Traditionally agricultural, the valley industrialised with metalworking, machine tools and paper; Elorrio and Durango retain notable old quarters with palaces and churches. The Basque-language cultural fair of Durango (Durangoko Azoka), held each December, is one of the most important events for Basque books and music.
