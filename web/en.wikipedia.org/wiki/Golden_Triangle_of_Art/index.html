Golden Triangle of Art

The Golden Triangle of Art (Spanish: Triángulo de Oro) is an area in Madrid, Spain, formed by three major art museums close together along the Paseo del Prado: the Prado Museum, the Museo Reina Sofía, and the Museo Thyssen-Bornemisza.

Golden Triangle of Art
Location: Paseo del Prado, Madrid, Spain
Museums: Prado, Reina Sofía, Thyssen-Bornemisza

The museums

The Prado (1819) holds Spanish masters such as Velázquez, Goya and El Greco. The Reina Sofía focuses on modern art and houses Picasso's Guernica. The Thyssen-Bornemisza (opened 1992) fills gaps with international art from the Renaissance to Pop Art, from a private collection assembled by Baron Hans Heinrich Thyssen-Bornemisza.

Significance

Together the three collections offer an unusually complete survey of Western art within walking distance. The surrounding Paseo del Prado and Retiro Park landscape was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2021. Compare the Getty Center in Los Angeles.
