Our Lady of Fátima

LocationFátima, Portugal
Date13 May – 13 October 1917
WitnessesLúcia dos Santos, Francisco and Jacinta Marto
TypeMarian apparition
Approval1930, Diocese of Leiria

Our Lady of Fátima is a Catholic title of the Virgin Mary based on reported apparitions to three shepherd children at Fátima, Portugal, in 1917. Lúcia dos Santos and her cousins Francisco and Jacinta Marto said a radiant lady appeared to them on the 13th of each month from May to October.

The apparitions

The children reported that the Lady asked for prayer, penance, and devotion to her Immaculate Heart, and entrusted them with prophetic messages known as the Three Secrets of Fátima. The final apparition on 13 October 1917 was accompanied by the "Miracle of the Sun", in which tens of thousands of onlookers reported the sun seeming to dance and plunge in the sky.

Devotion

The local bishop declared the apparitions worthy of belief in 1930. The Sanctuary of Fátima, with its basilica and the Chapel of the Apparitions, became one of the world's foremost Marian pilgrimage centers, visited by several popes. Francisco and Jacinta, who died young in the influenza pandemic, were canonized in 2017, the centenary of the apparitions.
