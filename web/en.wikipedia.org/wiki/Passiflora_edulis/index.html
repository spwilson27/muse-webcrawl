Passiflora edulis

Passiflora edulis is a vine species of passion flower native to southern Brazil, Paraguay, and northern Argentina. It is the species most widely cultivated for passion fruit.

Forms

Two main forms are recognised: the purple passion fruit (P. edulis f. edulis), adapted to cooler highland climates, and the yellow passion fruit (P. edulis f. flavicarpa), adapted to tropical lowlands and widely used as a rootstock and for juice production.

Cultivation

The vine is grown commercially in tropical and subtropical regions worldwide, including Brazil, Colombia, Kenya, Australia, and Hawaii. Fruit is eaten fresh and processed into juice, concentrates, and flavourings. The plant requires well-drained soil, support for climbing, and in many regions hand or insect pollination.
