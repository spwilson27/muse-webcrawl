Gotham City

Gotham City is a fictional city in DC Comics, best known as the home of Batman. Depicted as a dark, crime-ridden metropolis modelled on New York City, its name derives from a nickname for New York popularised by Washington Irving in 1807.

Gotham City
Universe: DC Comics
First named: Batman #48 (1940)
Home of: Batman, Commissioner Gordon
Features: Arkham Asylum, Wayne Tower

History and depiction

Writer Bill Finger named the city "Gotham" (he found it in a phone book as Gotham Jewelers). Its geography has varied, usually placed in New Jersey opposite Metropolis. Storylines emphasise corruption, organised crime and villains such as the Joker, Penguin and Two-Face, policed from Arkham Asylum and Blackgate Penitentiary.

In other media

Gotham has been portrayed in Burton, Nolan and Reeves Batman films and the TV series Gotham (2014–2019), each giving it a distinctive architectural character blending Art Deco and Gothic styles.
