Kusanagi no Tsurugi

Kusanagi no Tsurugi is a legendary Japanese sword and one of the three Imperial Regalia of Japan. In mythology, the storm god Susanoo discovered the sword in the tail of the eight-headed serpent Yamata no Orochi and presented it to the sun goddess Amaterasu.

Legend

According to the Kojiki and Nihon Shoki, Susanoo slew Orochi and found the sword within its fourth tail, naming it Ame-no-Murakumo-no-Tsurugi ("Sword of the Gathering Clouds of Heaven"). It later came into the hands of the hero Yamato Takeru, who used its power to cut grass to escape a fire, giving it the name Kusanagi ("grass-mower").

Status

The sword is said to be kept at Atsuta Shrine in Nagoya, though it is never publicly displayed. Along with the Yata no Kagami mirror and the Yasakani no Magatama jewel, it symbolises the legitimacy of the Japanese imperial line.
