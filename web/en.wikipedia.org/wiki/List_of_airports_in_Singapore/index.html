List of airports in Singapore

This is a list of airports in Singapore. The city-state is served principally by Changi Airport, one of the world's busiest and most awarded international hubs, and by Seletar Airport for general aviation and business flights. Several air bases are operated by the Republic of Singapore Air Force.

Military air bases

The Republic of Singapore Air Force operates Paya Lebar Air Base, Tengah Air Base, Changi Air Base, and Sembawang Air Base (helicopters). Civilian operations were consolidated at Changi when it opened in 1981, replacing Paya Lebar as the international airport.

Former airports

Historical airfields include Kallang Airport (opened 1937, Singapore's first civil airport), the former RAF stations at Kallang, Tengah, and Seletar, and the old Paya Lebar Airport, which handled international traffic from 1955 to 1981.
