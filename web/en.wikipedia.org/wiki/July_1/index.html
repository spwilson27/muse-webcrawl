July 1 is the 182nd day of the year (183rd in leap years) in the Gregorian calendar; 183 days remain until the end of the year. It is the first day of the second half of the year and, in many countries, the start of the new fiscal or academic year.

Events

  • 1867 – The British North America Act takes effect, uniting the colonies of Canada, New Brunswick and Nova Scotia into the Dominion of Canada, celebrated as Canada Day.
  • 1863 – The Battle of Gettysburg begins during the American Civil War; slavery is abolished in the Dutch colonies of Suriname and the Netherlands Antilles (Keti Koti).
  • 1916 – The first day of the Battle of the Somme, the bloodiest day in British military history.
  • 1960 – Ghana becomes a republic and Somalia gains independence.
  • 1962 – Rwanda and Burundi gain independence from Belgium.
  • 1991 – The Warsaw Pact is officially dissolved in Prague.
  • 1997 – The United Kingdom hands sovereignty of Hong Kong to China.
  • 2004 – The Cassini–Huygens spacecraft enters orbit around Saturn.
  • 2013 – Croatia becomes the 28th member of the European Union.

Births

  • 1646 – Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz, German philosopher and mathematician.
  • 1804 – George Sand, French novelist.
  • 1961 – Diana, Princess of Wales.
  • 1967 – Pamela Anderson, Canadian-American actress.

Deaths

  • 1974 – Juan Perón, President of Argentina.
  • 1991 – Michael Landon, American actor.
  • 2004 – Marlon Brando, American actor.

Holidays and observances

  • Canada Day (Canada)
  • Hong Kong Special Administrative Region Establishment Day
  • Keti Koti (Suriname and the Netherlands) – emancipation day
  • Republic Day in Ghana; Independence Day in Somalia, Rwanda and Burundi
  • International Tartan Day in Australia and New Zealand
