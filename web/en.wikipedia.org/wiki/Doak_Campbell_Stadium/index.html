Doak Campbell Stadium

Doak S. Campbell Stadium, popularly known as "Doak", is a football stadium on the campus of Florida State University in Tallahassee, Florida. It is the home field of the Florida State Seminoles football team and, with a capacity of over 79,000, one of the largest stadiums in the Atlantic Coast Conference.

History

The stadium opened on 7 October 1950 with a capacity of 15,000 and was named for Doak S. Campbell, the university's first president after it became Florida State University. Repeated expansions wrapped the stadium in a brick facade that also houses university classrooms and offices, making it one of the largest continuous brick structures in the United States. The playing surface is named Bobby Bowden Field, honouring the Seminoles' long-time head coach.
