Jasper National Park

ProvinceAlberta
SizeLargest park in the Canadian Rockies
NoteMajor wildfire, July 2024
[Image: Spirit Island on Maligne Lake]
Spirit Island on Maligne Lake

Jasper National Park is the largest national park in the Canadian Rockies, in Alberta, Canada — 11,000 km² of mountains, glaciers and valleys around the town of Jasper, and a designated dark-sky preserve.

Overview

Jasper is Banff's wilder northern sibling: the Columbia Icefield spills its glaciers (the Athabasca among them) beside the Icefields Parkway, Maligne Lake hosts the photographed Spirit Island, and elk and bears wander the townsite. In July 2024 a catastrophic wildfire — the park's largest in a century — destroyed roughly a third of the town of Jasper's structures and burned vast forest, a landmark of Canada's intensifying fire era; rebuilding continues. The park protects caribou herds, the Tonquin Valley and some of the darkest skies in the inhabited world.
