Lanthanum

SymbolLa
Atomic number57
GroupLanthanide
AppearanceSilvery white

Lanthanum is a chemical element with the symbol La and atomic number 57. It is a soft, ductile, silvery-white metal that tarnishes slowly when exposed to air. Lanthanum is the first element in the lanthanide series and gives the group its name; its name derives from the Greek lanthanein, "to lie hidden", because it was concealed in cerium minerals for many years.

History and uses

Lanthanum was discovered in 1839 by Swedish chemist Carl Gustaf Mosander. Applications include nickel–metal hydride batteries for hybrid vehicles, camera and telescope lenses (lanthanum oxide improves optical quality), carbon arc lights, lighter flints as a component of misch metal, and catalysts in petroleum refining.
