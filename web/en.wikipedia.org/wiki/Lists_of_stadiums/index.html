Lists of stadiums

A major stadium during an evening event

This article indexes lists of stadiums around the world, organized by capacity, sport, and country. The largest stadium in the world by capacity is the Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad, India (132,000, for cricket), followed by the Rungrado 1st of May Stadium in Pyongyang, North Korea, and Michigan Stadium in the United States.

Largest stadiums

Among the largest are: Narendra Modi Stadium (132,000), Rungrado 1st of May Stadium (~114,000), Michigan Stadium (107,601), Beaver Stadium (106,572), Ohio Stadium (102,780), Kyle Field (102,733), Wembley Stadium (90,000), Camp Nou (~99,000 before redevelopment), and the Melbourne Cricket Ground (100,024).
