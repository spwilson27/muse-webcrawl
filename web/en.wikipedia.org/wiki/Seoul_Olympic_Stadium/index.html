Seoul Olympic Stadium

Seoul Olympic Stadium
Location: Jamsil, Songpa-gu, Seoul
Capacity: about 69,950
Opened: 1984

The Seoul Olympic Stadium (also known as Jamsil Olympic Stadium) is a multi-purpose stadium in Seoul, South Korea. Built for the 1988 Summer Olympics, it hosted the opening and closing ceremonies as well as the athletics and equestrian events of those Games. It also hosted events of the 1986 Asian Games.

Design

Designed by architect Kim Swoo-geun, the stadium's profile was inspired by the curves of a Joseon dynasty porcelain vase. Construction began in 1977 and the stadium was completed in 1984 as the centrepiece of the Jamsil Sports Complex.

Events

Beyond the Olympics, the stadium has hosted major concerts, football matches of the South Korea national team, and large cultural events. It remains one of the most recognisable sports venues in South Korea.
