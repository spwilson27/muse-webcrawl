Beta Orionis (β Orionis, abbreviated Beta Ori or β Ori), traditionally called Rigel, is a blue supergiant star in the constellation of Orion. It marks the left foot (or knee) of the hunter and is normally the brightest star in the constellation and one of the brightest stars in the night sky, with an apparent magnitude of about 0.13.

Characteristics

Rigel is a blue supergiant of spectral type B8 Ia, estimated to be roughly 860 light-years from the Sun and tens of thousands of times more luminous. It is a multiple star system: the primary is orbited by a close binary companion, and additional fainter stars are associated with the system. Rigel is slightly variable and is expected to end its life in a supernova explosion.

Name

The name Rigel derives from the Arabic Rijl Jauzah al Yusra, "the left leg of Jauzah" (Orion). It was approved as a proper name for Beta Orionis by the International Astronomical Union.
