Cuba Mark Gooding Jr. (born January 2, 1968) is an American actor. He first gained attention for his breakout role as Tre Styles in John Singleton's Boyz n the Hood (1991), and won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor for his portrayal of football player Rod Tidwell in Jerry Maguire (1996), a performance famous for the line "Show me the money!"

Early life

Gooding was born in the Bronx, New York City, the son of singer Cuba Gooding Sr., lead vocalist of the soul group The Main Ingredient, and Shirley Sullivan. The family moved to Los Angeles when he was a child, and he attended several high schools there before turning to acting.

Career

After early guest roles on television series such as Hill Street Blues and MacGyver, Gooding's film career took off with Boyz n the Hood. He followed it with roles in A Few Good Men (1992), Outbreak (1995) and the Oscar-winning turn in Jerry Maguire. Later films include As Good as It Gets (1997), Men of Honor (2000), Pearl Harbor (2001), Radio (2003), American Gangster (2007) and Lee Daniels' The Butler (2013).

On television, he portrayed O. J. Simpson in the acclaimed 2016 limited series The People v. O. J. Simpson: American Crime Story, earning an Emmy Award nomination.
