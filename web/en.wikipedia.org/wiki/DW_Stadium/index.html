DW Stadium

The DW Stadium is a multi-use stadium in Wigan, Greater Manchester, England. It is the home ground of Wigan Athletic football club and the Wigan Warriors rugby league club. Its current capacity is about 25,133.

History

The stadium was built by Alfred McAlpine and opened in August 1999, replacing Wigan Athletic's Springfield Park and the Warriors' Central Park. It was originally named the JJB Stadium after the sports retail company of Wigan owner Dave Whelan. In 2009 the naming rights were transferred to Whelan's new company DW Sports Fitness, and the stadium became the DW Stadium.

The ground's record attendance is 25,133, set for a Premier League match against Manchester United in May 2008. The stadium has also hosted rugby league internationals and was a venue for the 2013 Rugby League World Cup.

Structure

The stadium consists of four single-tiered stands: the North and South stands behind the goals, and the East and West stands along the sides. The pitch is natural grass shared by both clubs, which has periodically drawn criticism for its condition in winter.
