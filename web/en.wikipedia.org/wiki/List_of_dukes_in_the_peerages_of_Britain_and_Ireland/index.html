List of dukes in the peerages of Britain and Ireland

Dukes in the British peerages
Highest rank of the peerage below royalty. There are currently 24 non-royal dukedoms extant.

This is a list of the 24 present and extant dukes in the peerages of the Kingdom of England, Kingdom of Scotland, Kingdom of Great Britain, Kingdom of Ireland and the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Ireland. It does not include dukes in other countries' peerages, nor does it include royal dukes (dukes who are members of the royal family).

A duke is the highest-ranking title in the British peerage system, ranking below the monarch but above marquesses. Dukedoms were first created in England in 1337, when Edward III made his eldest son, Edward the Black Prince, Duke of Cornwall.

Order of precedence

Dukes take precedence by the date of creation of their dukedom within each peerage, with the peerages themselves ordered England, Scotland, Great Britain, Ireland and the United Kingdom. The senior non-royal dukedom is the Dukedom of Norfolk, created in 1483 in the Peerage of England and held by the Howard family.
