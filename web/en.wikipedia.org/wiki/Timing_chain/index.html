Timing chain

A timing chain is a roller chain inside an internal combustion engine that synchronises the rotation of the crankshaft and the camshaft(s) so that the engine's valves open and close at the correct times during each cylinder's intake and exhaust strokes.

Function

The chain links the crankshaft sprocket to the camshaft sprocket, typically at a 2:1 ratio. Tensioners and guides keep the chain taut; chains are lubricated by engine oil. In interference engines, chain failure or stretching can allow pistons to strike valves, causing severe damage.

Comparison with belts

Timing chains are generally more durable than rubber timing belts and often last the life of the engine, but are noisier and costlier to manufacture; stretched chains remain a known failure mode on some modern engines.
