List of longest suspension bridge spans

The longest suspension bridge spans in the world are ranked by the length of the main span (the distance between towers). Since March 2022 the record has been held by the 1915 Çanakkale Bridge across the Dardanelles in Turkey, with a main span of 2,023 metres — the first suspension bridge to exceed two kilometres.

Historical progression

The record has been held successively by the Brooklyn Bridge (486 m, 1883), the George Washington Bridge (1,067 m, 1931), the Golden Gate Bridge (1,280 m, 1937), the Verrazzano-Narrows Bridge (1,298 m, 1964), the Humber Bridge (1,410 m, 1981), the Great Belt Bridge (1,624 m, 1998), the Akashi Kaikyō Bridge (1,991 m, 1998), and the 1915 Çanakkale Bridge (2,023 m, 2022).
