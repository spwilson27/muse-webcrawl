After-school activity

After-school activities, also known as after-school programs or out-of-school-time activities, are organised programs that children and adolescents participate in outside the regular school day, typically in the hours between school dismissal and parents' return from work.

Types

Activities include sports teams, music and arts instruction, homework clubs, scouting, academic enrichment and tutoring, language classes, and in some countries intensive private study such as cram schools and hagwons. Programs may be run by schools, municipalities, charities, or private providers, and may be free, subsidised, or fee-charging.

Research

Studies associate high-quality after-school programs with improved homework completion, social skills, and school engagement, and with reduced unsupervised time during the afternoon hours when juvenile offending peaks. Access is uneven, however, and critics note cost barriers and the risk of overscheduling children.
