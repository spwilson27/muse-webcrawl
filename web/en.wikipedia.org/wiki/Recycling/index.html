Recycling

Recycling
DefinitionConverting waste materials into new products
HierarchyReduce, reuse, recycle
[Image: Colour-coded recycling bins filled with sorted cans, bottles and paper]

Recycling is the process of converting waste materials into new materials and objects — paper, glass (in the wider crawl), plastic, aluminium, steel and e-waste are the main streams. It conserves resources and energy (recycled aluminium saves ~95% of production energy) and cuts landfill and extraction impacts.

Limits

Recycling sits below prevention and reuse in the waste hierarchy and faces real limits: plastics degrade when recycled and markets fluctuate. Design for disassembly, deposits and producer-responsibility laws improve systems; contaminated loads still doom much collected material.
