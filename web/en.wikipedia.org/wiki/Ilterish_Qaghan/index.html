Ilterish Qaghan

Ilterish Qaghan (Old Turkic: 𐰃𐰠𐱅𐰼𐰾 𐰴𐰍𐰣; Chinese: 頡跌利施可汗; personal name Kutlug, also rendered Qutlugh) was the founder of the Second Turkic Khaganate, ruling from 682 until his death in 691.

Rise

A descendant of the Ashina clan, Kutlug rebelled against Tang dynasty domination with a small band of followers — the inscriptions say seventeen men — and rapidly gathered support, taking the title Ilterish ("he who gathers the people/states"). He campaigned against the Tang and neighbouring peoples, restoring an independent Turkic empire on the Mongolian steppe.

Legacy

Ilterish's campaigns, advised by the statesman Tonyukuk, laid the foundations consolidated by his brother Qapaghan Qaghan and his sons Bilge Qaghan and Kul Tigin, commemorated in the Orkhon inscriptions.
