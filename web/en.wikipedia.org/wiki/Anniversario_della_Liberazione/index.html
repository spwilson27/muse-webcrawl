Anniversario della Liberazione

The Anniversario della Liberazione, or Liberation Day, is a national holiday in Italy observed on 25 April. It commemorates the end of Nazi occupation and Fascist rule during the Second World War and honours the Italian resistance movement.

History

On 25 April 1945 the National Liberation Committee for Northern Italy proclaimed a general insurrection in the cities still occupied in northern Italy. Milan and Turin were liberated by partisans around this date, and Benito Mussolini was captured and executed days later. The holiday was made permanent in 1949.

Observance

The President of Italy lays a wreath at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier in Rome, and ceremonies, marches and concerts are held across the country. The song "Bella ciao", associated with the partisans, is widely sung. The day remains politically significant because it is tied to the anti-fascist foundations of the Italian Republic.
