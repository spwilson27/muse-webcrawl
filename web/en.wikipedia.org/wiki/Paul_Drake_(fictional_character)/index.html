Paul Drake (fictional character)

Paul Drake is a fictional private detective created by author Erle Stanley Gardner. He is the head of the Drake Detective Agency and the chief investigator for defence attorney Perry Mason, appearing in Gardner's novels, the 1957–1966 CBS television series, and later adaptations.

Character

Drake does the legwork Mason's cases require — tracing witnesses, checking alibis, and shadowing suspects — and reports in terse, wisecracking style. Mason relies on his discretion, since Drake sometimes works at the edge of what the law allows on a client's behalf.

Portrayals

In the classic television series Drake was played by William Hopper, whose tall, silver-haired version is the best known. In the HBO revival series beginning in 2020, the character was reimagined and played by Chris Chalk.
