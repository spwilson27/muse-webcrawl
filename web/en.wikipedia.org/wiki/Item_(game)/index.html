Item (game)

In video games and tabletop games, an item is an object within the game world that a player character can collect, carry, use or trade. Items are a fundamental element of game mechanics, especially in role-playing games and adventure games.

Types

Common categories include weapons and armour (equipment), consumables such as potions and food that are used up on activation, quest items required to advance the story, crafting materials, currency, and collectible or cosmetic items. Many games manage items through an inventory system with limits on weight or slots.

Design

Items serve as rewards, tools for solving problems, and drivers of in-game economies. Randomised item rewards ("loot") and rarity tiers are central to many role-playing and action games.
