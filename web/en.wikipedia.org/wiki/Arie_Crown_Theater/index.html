The Arie Crown Theater is a theatre at McCormick Place, the convention centre on the Near South Side of Chicago, Illinois. Named for Lithuanian-born lawyer and philanthropist Arie Crown, it opened in 1960 and seats 4,188, making it one of the largest theatres in Chicago.

History and use

The theatre survived the 1967 fire that destroyed much of the original McCormick Place. It hosts touring Broadway musicals, concerts, opera, comedy, and large corporate and political events, and has been a regular venue for productions presented by Broadway In Chicago.
