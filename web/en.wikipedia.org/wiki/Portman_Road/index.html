Portman Road

Portman Road
Location: Ipswich, Suffolk, England
Owner: Ipswich Borough Council
Capacity: approx. 30,000
Opened: 1884

Portman Road is a football stadium in Ipswich, Suffolk, England, which has been the home ground of Ipswich Town F.C. since 1884. The stadium has also hosted many England youth international matches and one senior England friendly, against Croatia in 2003. It has staged several other sporting events, including athletics meetings and international hockey matches, and music concerts.

The ground's four stands are the Sir Alf Ramsey Stand, the Cobbold Stand, the Sir Bobby Robson Stand, and the West Stand, honouring two of the club's most celebrated managers, Alf Ramsey and Bobby Robson, both of whom also managed England. The record attendance at Portman Road is 38,010, set for an FA Cup tie against Leeds United in 1975.

History

Ipswich played at Broomhill Park and Brook's Hall before moving to Portman Road in 1884. Substantial redevelopment around the club's First Division title in 1962 and again in the early 2000s expanded and modernised the ground, including the rebuilding of both end stands.
