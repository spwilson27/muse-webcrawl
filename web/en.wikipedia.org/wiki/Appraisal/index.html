Appraisal

An appraisal is an unbiased estimate of the value of something, most commonly real estate, personal property, antiques, collectibles, or a business. In the real estate context, an appraisal is typically performed by a licensed or certified appraiser and is often required by lenders before approving a mortgage.

Field: Valuation / Finance
Practitioner: Appraiser
Common subjects: Real estate, antiques, businesses, jewellery

Approaches to value

Appraisers commonly use three approaches: the sales comparison (market) approach, which compares the subject to recently sold similar properties; the cost approach, which estimates replacement cost minus depreciation plus land value; and the income approach, which capitalizes the income the property can generate.

Regulation

In the United States, real estate appraisers are licensed by states and follow the Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice (USPAP). Appraisals are also used for taxation, insurance, estate settlement, and divorce proceedings.
