The Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) is the national governing body for cricket in India. Founded in 1928, it is the richest cricket board in the world and one of the most influential bodies in the international game.

Role and competitions

The BCCI organises domestic competitions including the Ranji Trophy, oversees the India men's and women's national teams, and founded the Indian Premier League (IPL) in 2008, whose media rights have sold for billions of dollars. Its member associations are state-level cricket bodies.

Governance

The board is a registered society, independent of the Indian government. Following match-fixing and conflict-of-interest scandals surrounding the IPL, the Supreme Court of India appointed a committee of administrators in 2017 to implement reforms recommended by the Lodha Committee; elections under a revised constitution were held in 2019.
