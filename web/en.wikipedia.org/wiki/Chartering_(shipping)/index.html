Chartering (shipping)

Chartering is an activity within the shipping industry in which a shipowner hires out the use of their vessel to a charterer. The contract for the hire is called a charterparty.

Types of charter

There are three main types: a voyage charter, in which the vessel is hired for a single voyage between specified ports; a time charter, in which the vessel is hired for a period of time, with the charterer directing its commercial employment while the owner retains nautical management; and a bareboat (or demise) charter, in which the charterer takes full possession and control of the vessel, providing crew and assuming operational responsibility.

Chartering is arranged through shipbrokers, and freight rates are reported by exchanges such as the Baltic Exchange in London. Standard charterparty forms are published by organisations such as BIMCO.
