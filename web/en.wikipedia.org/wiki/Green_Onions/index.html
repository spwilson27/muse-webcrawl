Green Onions

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

"Green Onions" is an instrumental composition by Booker T. & the M.G.'s, released as a single on Stax Records in 1962 and as the title track of the group's debut album. Written by Booker T. Jones, Steve Cropper, Lewie Steinberg, and Al Jackson Jr., it reached No. 1 on the US R&B chart and No. 3 on the Billboard Hot 100.

The track grew out of a jam session at Stax's Memphis studio while the band waited for singer Billy Lee Riley. Built on a twelve-bar blues riff played on Jones's Hammond M3 organ, with Cropper's stinging Telecaster guitar, it became one of the most popular instrumental songs of the rock era.

Legacy

"Green Onions" has been covered and sampled extensively, used in films such as American Graffiti and The Blues Brothers (in which Cropper and bassist Donald "Duck" Dunn appeared), and was inducted into the Grammy Hall of Fame. In 1999 it was honoured as one of the greatest instrumental recordings of all time in various polls, and it was selected for preservation in the US National Recording Registry.
