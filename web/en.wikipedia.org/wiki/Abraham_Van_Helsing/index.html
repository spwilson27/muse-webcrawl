Professor Abraham Van Helsing is a fictional character from the 1897 gothic horror novel Dracula by Bram Stoker. A Dutch doctor with a wide range of interests and accomplishments, he is a polymath — holding degrees in medicine, philosophy and letters — and is the mentor of Dr. John Seward, one of the novel's narrators. Van Helsing is summoned from Amsterdam to examine Lucy Westenra, and recognises that she is the victim of a vampire.

Role in the novel

Van Helsing leads the group of protagonists — Seward, Jonathan Harker, Mina Harker, Arthur Holmwood and Quincey Morris — in their hunt for Count Dracula. His knowledge of folklore and obscure diseases, combined with modern science, makes him the Count's principal adversary. He organises the destruction of the vampirised Lucy, protects Mina, and leads the pursuit of Dracula back to Transylvania.

In popular culture

The character has been portrayed in numerous adaptations, including by Edward Van Sloan in the 1931 film Dracula, Peter Cushing in Hammer's Dracula (1958) and its sequels, Laurence Olivier in Dracula (1979), and Anthony Hopkins in Bram Stoker's Dracula (1992). The 2004 film Van Helsing reimagined him as the monster hunter Gabriel Van Helsing, played by Hugh Jackman. The character is the archetype of the vampire hunter in popular fiction.
