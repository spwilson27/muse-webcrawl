Drangey

Drangey (Icelandic pronunciation: [ˈtrauŋkˌeiː]) is an uninhabited island in the Skagafjörður fjord in northern Iceland. It is the remnant of an ancient volcano, mostly made of volcanic palagonite tuff, forming a massive rock fortress with sheer cliffs rising about 180 metres above the sea.

Geography and wildlife

The island is roughly 20 hectares on its flat top. Its cliffs are home to large colonies of seabirds, including puffins, guillemots, razorbills, fulmars and kittiwakes, and it has long been used for egg collecting and bird hunting, accessed by climbing and by ropes from above.

In saga and folklore

Drangey is famous as the refuge of the outlaw Grettir Ásmundarson, hero of Grettis saga. Grettir spent the last three years of his outlawry on the island with his brother Illugi and a servant, before being killed there in 1031. A single ladder provides access from the shore to the top.
