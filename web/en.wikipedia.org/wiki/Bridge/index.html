Bridge

Bridge
FunctionStructure carrying a route over an obstacle
TypesBeam, arch, suspension, cable-stayed, cantilever
[Image: A long suspension bridge spanning a misty strait at sunrise]

A bridge is a structure built to span a physical obstacle — a river, valley or road — without blocking the way beneath. Bridges are among civilisation's signature works, from Roman stone arches to the great steel suspension spans of the industrial age.

Engineering

Designs balance compression and tension: arches push outward, suspension cables hang in catenaries, cable-stayed decks fan from towers. The longest spans now approach two kilometres (Türkiye's 1915 Çanakkale Bridge). Failures — Tacoma Narrows (1940) — taught aerodynamic lessons; bridges like the Golden Gate became city icons.
