Snow sculpture

A snow sculpture is a temporary artwork carved from a block of compacted snow. Unlike a snowman, which is assembled from rolled balls, a snow sculpture is typically cut and shaped from a single large mass of snow using saws, chisels, and other tools.

Technique

Sculptors begin with a form filled with snow that is stomped and allowed to sinter into a solid block. After the form is removed, the block is carved from large shapes down to fine detail. Because snow melts and sublimates, sculptures are ephemeral, lasting days or weeks depending on temperature.

Events

Major snow sculpture competitions are held at winter festivals worldwide, including the Harbin International Ice and Snow Festival in China, the Sapporo Snow Festival in Japan, and the International Snow Sculpture Championships in Breckenridge, Colorado.
