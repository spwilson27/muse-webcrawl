Rambo is an American media franchise centred on a series of action films featuring John J. Rambo, a former United States Army Special Forces soldier played by Sylvester Stallone. The character originated in David Morrell's 1972 novel First Blood, which was adapted into the film First Blood (1982).

Films

  • First Blood (1982) — Rambo, a Vietnam veteran drifter, clashes with a small-town sheriff, triggering a manhunt in the mountains of Washington state.
  • Rambo: First Blood Part II (1985) — Rambo returns to Vietnam on a mission to photograph POW camps and ends up rescuing prisoners.
  • Rambo III (1988) — Rambo travels to Afghanistan to rescue his former commander, Colonel Trautman.
  • Rambo (2008) — Rambo leads mercenaries into war-torn Burma to rescue captured missionaries.
  • Rambo: Last Blood (2019) — Rambo confronts a Mexican cartel after his adopted niece is kidnapped.

Character

John Rambo is depicted as a decorated but traumatised veteran suffering from post-traumatic stress disorder. The first film treated him sympathetically as a victim of circumstance and institutional neglect, while later entries shifted toward larger-scale action spectacle. The name "Rambo" entered popular culture as a byword for a lone, heavily armed fighter.

Other media

The franchise has included an animated series, Rambo: The Force of Freedom (1986), novelisations, comic books, and video games, including the notoriously poorly received Rambo: The Video Game (2014).

Reception and legacy

The five films have grossed over $800 million worldwide. The series influenced 1980s action cinema and remains a touchstone of the genre; the original First Blood is often regarded as the most critically respected entry.
