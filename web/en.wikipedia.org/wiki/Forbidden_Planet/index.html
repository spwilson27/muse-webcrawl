Forbidden Planet

Directed byFred M. Wilcox
StarringWalter Pidgeon, Anne Francis, Leslie Nielsen
Released1956
StudioMetro-Goldwyn-Mayer

Forbidden Planet is an American science fiction film released by MGM in 1956, directed by Fred M. Wilcox. Loosely based on Shakespeare's The Tempest, it stars Walter Pidgeon as Dr. Morbius, Anne Francis as his daughter Altaira, and Leslie Nielsen as Commander Adams.

Plot

In the twenty-third century, the starship C-57D travels to the planet Altair IV to learn the fate of an expedition sent twenty years earlier. The only survivors are Morbius and his daughter, who live with the remarkably capable Robby the Robot and the technology of the extinct Krell civilization. Morbius warns the crew away; a powerful invisible monster — ultimately revealed to be a manifestation of Morbius's own subconscious, unleashed by Krell machinery that materializes thought — attacks the ship.

Significance

The film was a landmark: the first science fiction film set entirely on another planet in deep space with a faster-than-light starship, the first to feature an entirely electronic musical score (by Bebe and Louis Barron), and the debut of Robby the Robot, one of cinema's most famous robots. Its visual effects were nominated for an Academy Award. It is frequently cited as an influence on later science fiction, including Star Trek.
