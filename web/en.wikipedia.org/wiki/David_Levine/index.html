David Levine

David Levine (December 20, 1926 – December 29, 2009) was an American artist and illustrator best known for his caricatures in The New York Review of Books. Over a career of more than four decades he produced some 3,800 pen-and-ink caricatures of writers, artists, and political figures.

Life and work

Born in Brooklyn, New York, Levine studied painting at the Tyler School of Art in Philadelphia. His first drawings for the New York Review appeared in 1963, and his sharp, cross-hatched caricatures — including his famous depiction of President Lyndon B. Johnson showing a Vietnam-shaped scar — became a signature of the magazine. He was also an accomplished watercolor painter of Coney Island and New York life.

Later years

Macular degeneration forced Levine to retire in 2007. He died in New York City in 2009.
