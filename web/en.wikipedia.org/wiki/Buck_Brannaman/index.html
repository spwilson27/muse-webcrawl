Buck Brannaman

Dan "Buck" Brannaman (born January 29, 1962) is an American horse trainer and a leading practitioner of natural horsemanship. He travels for most of the year giving clinics in which he teaches handlers to work with horses using calm, consistent communication rather than force.

Background

Brannaman and his brother performed trick roping as children, but their home life was marked by their father's violence; after being placed in foster care, he found stability with the Shirleys, a ranching family in Montana. He was influenced by trainers Ray Hunt and Tom Dorrance, central figures in the natural horsemanship movement.

Work and media

Brannaman was a technical adviser and stunt rider on Robert Redford's film The Horse Whisperer (1998), in which the fictional trainer was partly modelled on him. His life and methods are the subject of Cindy Meehl's award-winning 2011 documentary Buck. He has written books including The Faraway Horses, a memoir, and is known for starting young horses and helping owners understand behaviour problems as communication problems.
