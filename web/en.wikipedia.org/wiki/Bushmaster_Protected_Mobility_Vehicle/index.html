Bushmaster Protected Mobility Vehicle

The Bushmaster Protected Mobility Vehicle is an Australian-built four-wheel drive armoured vehicle, designed and manufactured by Thales Australia (formerly ADI Limited) in Bendigo, Victoria. It is an infantry mobility vehicle with a V-shaped monocoque hull designed to deflect land mine and improvised explosive device blasts away from the occupants.

Design and service

The Bushmaster can carry up to ten troops including the driver, protected by armour against small arms fire and shell fragments, and it can operate for several days without resupply. It entered service with the Australian Army in 2005 and was deployed extensively in Iraq and Afghanistan, where its mine protection was credited with saving many lives. Variants include troop carrier, command, ambulance, assault pioneer and mortar versions.

Operators

Beyond Australia, the type has been operated by the Netherlands, the United Kingdom, Japan, New Zealand, Ukraine and others; hundreds were supplied to Ukraine following the 2022 Russian invasion.
