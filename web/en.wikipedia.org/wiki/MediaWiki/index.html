MediaWiki is a free and open-source wiki software package written in PHP, originally for use on Wikipedia. It is now used by all Wikimedia Foundation projects and by thousands of other public and private wikis, including Fandom, wikiHow and the websites of many corporations and governments.

History

MediaWiki was developed by Magnus Manske to replace the Perl-based UseModWiki on Wikipedia; its "Phase III" rewrite was deployed on 20 July 2002 (Manske Day), with major contributions by Lee Daniel Crocker and later developers including Tim Starling and Brion Vibber. It was named MediaWiki in 2003. Features developed for Wikipedia's scale — revision histories, watchlists, categories, templates, namespaces, a parser for wikitext, and an extension architecture — made it the most widely deployed wiki engine. A visual editor (VisualEditor) was added in 2013, and Parsoid provides bidirectional conversion between wikitext and HTML.

Features

MediaWiki stores content in a relational database (MySQL/MariaDB, PostgreSQL or SQLite), supports extensive caching and internationalisation in hundreds of languages, access controls and user rights, file uploads with versioning, and thousands of extensions, including Semantic MediaWiki for structured data and Citoid/Cite for references. Its interface is skinnable; the Vector skin long served as Wikipedia's default.
