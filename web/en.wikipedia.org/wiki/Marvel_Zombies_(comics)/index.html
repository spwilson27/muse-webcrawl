Marvel Zombies (comics)

Marvel Zombies is a comic book metaseries published by Marvel Comics and set in an alternate universe (designated Earth-2149) in which the world's superheroes have been transformed into flesh-eating zombies by an alien virus. The concept first appeared in Ultimate Fantastic Four in 2005 before spinning off into its own limited series.

Publication history

The original five-issue Marvel Zombies (2005–2006) was written by Robert Kirkman, creator of The Walking Dead, with art by Sean Phillips and covers by Arthur Suydam that reimagined classic Marvel covers in zombified form. It was followed by several sequels and spin-offs, including Marvel Zombies 2, Marvel Zombies 3 and 4 (which shifted toward a horror-action tone under writer Fred Van Lente), Marvel Zombies 5, and crossovers involving Ash Williams of the Evil Dead franchise.

Story

Retaining their intelligence and powers, the zombified heroes devour Earth's population, then — after the arrival of the Silver Surfer and Galactus, whom they also consume — gain cosmic power and take their hunger to other worlds.
