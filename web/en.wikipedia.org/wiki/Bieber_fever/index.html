Bieber fever

Bieber fever is the popular culture phenomenon of intense fan devotion surrounding Canadian singer Justin Bieber, whose fans are known as "Beliebers". The term, a pun on "fever", came into wide use around 2009–2010 as Bieber rose to fame from YouTube videos to international pop stardom.

Characteristics

Bieber fever is marked by highly enthusiastic, predominantly young female fans, screaming at concerts, large social-media followings, and intense media coverage of Bieber's music, appearance, and personal life. It has frequently been compared to Beatlemania of the 1960s, in which fans displayed similar frenzied behaviour toward the Beatles.

Media and commentary

Commentators have discussed Bieber fever as an example of celebrity-driven fandom amplified by the internet and social networking. Psychologists and media scholars have occasionally examined the phenomenon's effects on fan identity and consumer culture, while popular coverage has sometimes used the term humorously or pejoratively.
