WPP plc is a British multinational communications, advertising, public relations, technology, and commerce holding company headquartered in London, England. It is one of the world's largest advertising and marketing groups, operating in more than 100 countries through agency networks including Ogilvy, VML, GroupM, AKQA, and Hogarth, and research businesses such as Kantar (majority sold in 2019).

History

The company originated in 1971 as Wire and Plastic Products, a maker of wire shopping baskets. In 1985 Martin Sorrell, a former finance director of Saatchi & Saatchi, took a controlling stake and used the listed company as a vehicle for an acquisition spree in marketing services. Renamed WPP Group, it acquired the American agency J. Walter Thompson in 1987, then Ogilvy & Mather (1989) in a landmark hostile takeover, followed over the decades by Young & Rubicam (2000), Grey Global (2005), TNS, AKQA, and numerous digital and data businesses. By the 2010s WPP had become the world's largest advertising group by revenue.

Sorrell resigned as chief executive in 2018 after 33 years, following an internal investigation; he was succeeded by Mark Read. Under Read, WPP consolidated hundreds of agency brands into fewer, larger networks — merging Wunderman Thompson and VMLY&R into VML (2023) — and undertook a major cost-cutting and simplification programme amid pressure on traditional advertising from technology platforms and in-housing.

Operations

WPP's businesses span creative advertising, media investment management through GroupM, public relations and public affairs (including BCW and Hill+Knowlton), brand consulting, design, production (Hogarth), and commerce and technology services. Its clients include many of the world's largest consumer brands.
