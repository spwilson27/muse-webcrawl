Golden Joystick Awards

The Golden Joystick Awards, also known as the People's Gaming Awards, is a video game award ceremony; it awards the best video games of the year, as voted for originally by the British general public, but is now a global online ceremony. As of 2023, the ceremony was in its 41st year, making it the second-oldest video game award ceremony after the Arcade Awards.

History

The awards were first held in 1983 in Berkeley Square, London, hosted by DJ Dave Lee Travis, and were organised by the magazine Computer and Video Games. The first winner of Game of the Year was Jetpac. After a hiatus in the late 1990s, the ceremony was revived and is now run by Future plc's GamesRadar+ brand, with voting conducted online and attracting millions of votes.

Categories

The flagship category is Ultimate Game of the Year. Other long-running categories include Best Storytelling, Best Visual Design, Studio of the Year, Most Wanted Game, and a Lifetime Achievement award. Winners have included The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild, God of War, Resident Evil Village and Baldur's Gate 3.
