L5 Society

Founded1975
FateMerged 1987 into the National Space Society

The L5 Society was an American space-advocacy organisation founded in 1975 to promote the space-colonisation ideas of physicist Gerard K. O'Neill, particularly large rotating habitats at the Earth–Moon L5 Lagrange point. Its motto was "We want to live in space."

Activities

The society took its name from the L5 libration point, where O'Neill proposed stationing cylindrical colonies ("Island Three"). It campaigned politically, notably lobbying against ratification of the Moon Treaty in 1980, which it argued would hinder space settlement. In 1987 the L5 Society merged with the National Space Institute to form the National Space Society.
