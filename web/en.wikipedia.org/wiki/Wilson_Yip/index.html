Wilson Yip Wai-shun (born 1963/1964) is a Hong Kong film director, producer, screenwriter and actor. He is best known internationally as the director of the Ip Man series of martial arts films starring Donnie Yen, which dramatize the life of the Wing Chun grandmaster Ip Man, the teacher of Bruce Lee. Yip's frequent collaboration with Yen, which also includes SPL: Sha Po Lang (2005), Flash Point (2007) and Dragon Tiger Gate (2006), helped define the modern Hong Kong martial arts action film.

Career

Yip began working in the Hong Kong film industry in the 1980s, taking on a variety of jobs before directing his first feature, 01:00 A.M. (1995), a horror anthology segment film. Through the late 1990s and early 2000s he directed a string of genre films including the horror films Bio-Zombie (1998) and The Mummy, Aged 19 (2002), the gangster drama Bullets Over Summer (1999), the romance Juliet in Love (2000)—which earned critical praise and award nominations—and the action film 2002 (2001).

His breakthrough came with SPL: Sha Po Lang (2005, released in the West as Kill Zone), a gritty police action film featuring Donnie Yen and Sammo Hung. Ip Man (2008) was a major commercial and critical success, winning Best Film at the Hong Kong Film Awards and spawning sequels directed by Yip: Ip Man 2 (2010), Ip Man 3 (2015) and Ip Man 4: The Finale (2019). The series grossed hundreds of millions of dollars worldwide and revived global interest in Wing Chun.
