In geology, a rock is a naturally occurring solid mass or aggregate of one or more minerals. Rocks are classified into three groups by formation: igneous (cooled magma or lava, e.g. granite, basalt), sedimentary (compressed deposits, e.g. sandstone, limestone) and metamorphic (transformed by heat and pressure, e.g. marble, slate).

The rock cycle

Plate tectonics (Plate tectonics in the wider crawl) and surface processes endlessly recycle rocks — the foundation of geology and of mining for copper, iron and fuels. Fossils occur almost exclusively in sedimentary rocks, recording life for the dinosaur pages.
