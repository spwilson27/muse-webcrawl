List of United States counties by population

The List of United States counties by population ranks the 3,000-plus counties and county-equivalents of the United States by resident population, using figures from the decennial census and the Census Bureau's annual estimates. The distribution is extremely uneven: a handful of urban counties hold millions of residents, while the least populous county, Loving County, Texas, has fewer than one hundred.
