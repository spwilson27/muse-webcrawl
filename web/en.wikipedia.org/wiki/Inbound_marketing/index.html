Inbound marketing

Inbound marketing is a business methodology and marketing strategy based on attracting customers by creating valuable content and experiences tailored to them, rather than interrupting audiences with advertising. The term was popularised in the mid-2000s by the software company HubSpot and its founders, including Brian Halligan and Dharmesh Shah.

Methodology

Inbound marketing typically proceeds through stages of attracting visitors (with blogs, search engine optimisation, social media and other content), converting them into leads (with landing pages, forms and calls to action), closing them as customers (with email marketing and customer relationship management tools), and delighting them so they become promoters. It is often contrasted with outbound marketing such as cold calls, purchased email lists and interruptive advertising.

Criticism

Critics note that inbound techniques can be slow to produce results, that content production is resource-intensive, and that in practice most firms combine inbound and outbound methods.
