BBC Alba

CountryUnited Kingdom (Scotland)
LanguageScottish Gaelic
Launched19 September 2008
OwnersBBC and MG Alba
HeadquartersStornoway and Glasgow

BBC Alba is a Scottish Gaelic-language free-to-air television channel jointly run by the BBC and MG Alba. Its name uses Alba, the Gaelic word for Scotland. The channel launched on 19 September 2008 and was the first channel to be delivered under a BBC licence by a partnership, as well as the first multi-genre channel to come entirely from Scotland.

Programming

The channel broadcasts up to seven hours a day of new Gaelic programming, including news (An Là), current affairs (Eòrpa), drama, documentaries, sport, music, and children's programmes, most of it subtitled in English. Its drama series Bannan (2014–2022) was the first Gaelic drama series on Scottish television. Sport coverage has included Scottish football, rugby, and shinty, while music programmes draw on events such as the Belladrum festival and the Royal National Mòd.

Distribution and reception

BBC Alba was initially available only by satellite and cable, which excluded many viewers in the Gaelic-speaking heartlands; it was added to Freeview in Scotland in June 2011. Although the number of fluent Gaelic speakers is small, the channel regularly reaches several times that audience over a week, attracting learners and non-speakers who use the English subtitles.
