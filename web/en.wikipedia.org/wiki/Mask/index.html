A mask is an object normally worn on the face, typically for protection, disguise, performance, or entertainment, and often for ceremonial purposes. Masks have been used since antiquity in theatre, ritual, and medicine, and in modern times for occupational safety, sport, and disease prevention.

History and ritual use

Masked figures appear in the art of many cultures, from Egyptian funerary masks such as Tutankhamun's to Japanese Noh theatre and West African masquerade traditions. Masks often embody spirits, ancestors, or archetypal characters and transform the wearer's identity.

Functional masks

Beyond ceremony, masks serve practical ends: respirators and surgical masks protect against airborne particles and pathogens, gas masks filter toxic gases, and sports such as fencing, ice hockey, and baseball use protective face masks.
