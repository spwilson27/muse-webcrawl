Leahy

Leahy is an Irish surname, derived from the Gaelic Ó Laochdha, meaning "descendant of the heroic one." It is also the name given to several people and places.

People

  • Patrick Leahy (born 1940), American politician who served as a United States senator from Vermont from 1975 to 2023, the longest-serving member of his party at his retirement.
  • William D. Leahy (1875–1959), United States Navy admiral and the first officer to hold the five-star rank of fleet admiral.
  • Frank Leahy (1908–1973), American college football coach, notably at the University of Notre Dame.

Other uses

The Leahy-Smith America Invents Act of 2011, a major reform of United States patent law, bears Senator Patrick Leahy's name. The surname also appears in the names of schools, streets, and institutions throughout the United States and Ireland.
