Harpo Productions
Founded: 1986, Chicago
Founder: Oprah Winfrey
Parent: Harpo, Inc.
Productions: The Oprah Winfrey Show, OWN programming, films

Harpo Productions (or Harpo Studios) is an American multimedia production company founded by Oprah Winfrey — 'Harpo' is 'Oprah' spelled backwards, also evoking a Marx Brothers character. It produced The Oprah Winfrey Show (1986–2011), one of the most successful talk shows in television history.

Divisions and output

Harpo's film arm produced adaptations including Beloved and The Color Purple musical film involvement, and its television arm supplied programming for the Oprah Winfrey Network (OWN, launched 2011, co-owned with Discovery/Warner Bros. Discovery). Harpo also produced Dr. Phil, Rachael Ray and The Dr. Oz Show in their early runs.

Later work

Harpo produced Winfrey's interviews and specials for Apple TV+ and other platforms, including the 2021 interview with Prince Harry and Meghan, Duchess of Sussex (co-produced), and continues as Winfrey's production vehicle.
