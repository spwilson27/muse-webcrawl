Paolo Rossi (23 September 1956 – 9 December 2020) was an Italian professional footballer who played as a forward. He led Italy to victory at the 1982 FIFA World Cup, scoring six goals to win the Golden Boot as top scorer and the Golden Ball as best player — and, the same year, the Ballon d'Or.

Career

Rossi emerged at Vicenza, finishing as Serie A top scorer in 1977–78. Returning to Juventus after a loan at Perugia, he was implicated in the 1980 Totonero betting scandal and banned for three years (reduced to two on appeal), a sanction he always maintained was unjust. He returned just in time for the 1982 World Cup in Spain.

1982 World Cup

After a scoreless group stage, Rossi scored a famous hat-trick in Italy's 3–2 defeat of favourites Brazil, two goals in the semi-final against Poland, and the opening goal in the 3–1 final win over West Germany. With Juventus he later won two Serie A titles, the 1984 Cup Winners' Cup, and the 1985 European Cup. After retirement he worked as a television pundit. His death in December 2020 prompted national mourning in Italy.
