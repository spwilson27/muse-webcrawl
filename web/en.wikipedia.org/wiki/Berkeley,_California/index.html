Berkeley is a city on the eastern shore of San Francisco Bay in northern Alameda County, California. It is home to the University of California, Berkeley, the oldest campus of the University of California system.

History

The area was part of Rancho San Antonio in Mexican California. The city grew around the university, founded in 1868, and was named after Anglo-Irish philosopher George Berkeley. Berkeley became a byword for political activism during the Free Speech Movement of 1964 and the anti-Vietnam War protests.

Education and economy

UC Berkeley and the Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory anchor a science-driven economy; several chemical elements, including berkelium, were named for the city. The Gourmet Ghetto on Shattuck Avenue, centred on Alice Waters's Chez Panisse (opened 1971), helped launch California cuisine.
