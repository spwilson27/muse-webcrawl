Duck Dodgers in the 24½th Century is a 1953 Warner Bros. Merrie Melodies cartoon directed by Chuck Jones. A science-fiction parody starring Daffy Duck as the heroic Duck Dodgers and Porky Pig as his loyal "Eager Young Space Cadet", it pits Dodgers against Marvin the Martian in a contest for Planet X, the last known source of the rare element Illudium Q-36.

Plot

Summoned by Dr. I.Q. Hi, Dodgers sets out to claim Planet X — which he locates by following planets A, B, C and so on in alphabetical order. Marvin the Martian arrives simultaneously to claim the planet for Mars, and the two battle with increasingly absurd weapons, including an "Ultimatum Dispatcher" and disintegrating pistols. The contest ends with Planet X blown to fragments, Dodgers clinging triumphantly to the last chunk.

Legacy

The cartoon is frequently ranked among the greatest animated shorts ever made, noted for its stylish design, Michael Maltese's script and Mel Blanc's voice performances. It inspired later sequels, the character's appearances in Tiny Toon Adventures and an animated television series, Duck Dodgers (2003–2005).
