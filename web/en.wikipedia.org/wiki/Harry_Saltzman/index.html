Harry Saltzman

Harry Saltzman (October 27, 1915 – September 28, 1994) was a Canadian theatre and film producer best known for co-founding, with Albert R. "Cubby" Broccoli, the production company Eon Productions, which produced the James Bond film series.

Career

Born in Sherbrooke, Quebec, Saltzman served in the Royal Canadian Air Force during the Second World War and worked in entertainment in France and Britain. In the late 1950s he produced acclaimed British films such as Look Back in Anger (1959) and Saturday Night and Sunday Morning (1960) through Woodfall Film Productions.

James Bond

Saltzman optioned Ian Fleming's Bond novels and partnered with Broccoli to make Dr. No (1962). He co-produced the Bond films through The Man with the Golden Gun (1974), and also produced the Harry Palmer spy films starring Michael Caine. Financial difficulties forced him to sell his stake in the Bond franchise in 1975.
