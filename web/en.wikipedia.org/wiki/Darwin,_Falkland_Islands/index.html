Darwin, Falkland Islands

Darwin is a settlement in East Falkland, Falkland Islands, situated on Choiseul Sound on the east side of the isthmus that joins Lafonia to the northern part of the island. It lies a few kilometres north of Goose Green. It is named after Charles Darwin, who visited the islands in 1833–34 aboard HMS Beagle.

History

Darwin was founded in 1859 as a sheep-farming station of the Falkland Islands Company and became a centre for sheep shearing and the tallow trade; a tallow factory operated there in the 1870s. The settlement also features the remains of a corral built by gauchos from mainland South America who worked the cattle herds of Lafonia.

Falklands War

During the Falklands War of 1982, Argentine forces occupied the Darwin–Goose Green area, and the Battle of Goose Green (28–29 May 1982), fought nearby, was the first major land battle of the war. An Argentine military cemetery, the largest in the islands, is located near Darwin and is maintained by the Commonwealth War Graves Commission.
