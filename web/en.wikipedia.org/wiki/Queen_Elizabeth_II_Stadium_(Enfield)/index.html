Queen Elizabeth II Stadium (Enfield)

LocationEnfield, London, England
Opened1953 (stadium); 2011 (refurbished)
Capacity~2,500
TenantsEnfield Town F.C.; Enfield and Haringey Athletic Club

The Queen Elizabeth II Stadium is a multi-purpose sports stadium in Enfield, London, England. The stadium's main building, with its distinctive Art Deco-influenced pavilion, was completed in 1953 and is Grade II listed. It should not be confused with the Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park stadium in Stratford.

History

Built as an athletics venue, the stadium fell into disrepair in the late 20th century. Enfield Town Football Club, a supporter-owned club founded in 2001 as the first fan-owned club in the country, campaigned for its restoration and took a long lease. After a major refurbishment, Enfield Town played its first match there in November 2011.

Facilities

The ground has a capacity of around 2,500, with a seated main stand in the listed pavilion, a running track surrounding the pitch (unusual among English football grounds), and facilities shared with Enfield and Haringey Athletic Club. It is regarded as a model of community sports-facility regeneration.
