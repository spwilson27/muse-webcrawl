The Dark Knight Rises

The Dark Knight Rises is a 2012 superhero film directed by Christopher Nolan, who co-wrote the screenplay with his brother Jonathan Nolan. It is the third and final instalment of Nolan's The Dark Knight trilogy, following Batman Begins (2005) and The Dark Knight (2008). Christian Bale returns as Bruce Wayne / Batman, who, eight years after the events of the previous film, is forced out of exile to save Gotham City from the terrorist Bane. The film grossed over $1.08 billion worldwide.

Plot

Eight years after Batman took the blame for Harvey Dent's crimes, organised crime in Gotham has been suppressed by the Dent Act, and the reclusive Bruce Wayne has retired. The mercenary Bane arrives in Gotham, bankrupts Wayne through a stock-exchange raid, defeats Batman in single combat — breaking his back — and imprisons him in a remote pit prison, the Pit, while seizing Gotham with a stolen fusion reactor converted into a neutron bomb and posing as a populist liberator. Wayne escapes by making the legendary climb from the Pit, returns, and, with Selina Kyle, Commissioner Gordon, and Officer John Blake, frees the city: Batman flies the bomb out over the bay, apparently sacrificing himself. In the closing scenes, Alfred sees Wayne alive in Florence with Kyle, Blake discovers the Batcave, and a repaired Bat-Signal shines over Gotham.

Production

Nolan, initially reluctant to make a third film, drew on A Tale of Two Cities and the Batman arcs Knightfall, The Dark Knight Returns, and No Man's Land. Filming took place in India, Pittsburgh, New York, Los Angeles, and the UK, with extensive practical effects and IMAX photography; the flying vehicle "the Bat" and the mid-air prologue hijack were staged for real. Hans Zimmer's score introduced the "Rise" chant built from fan-submitted voices.

Release and reception

The film premiered in New York on 16 July 2012. Its release was overshadowed by the mass shooting at a midnight screening in Aurora, Colorado, on 20 July, in which 12 people were killed. Critics gave generally positive reviews, praising the scale, performances, and conclusion of the trilogy, though some found the plot overstuffed; it appeared on many year-end top-ten lists and was, at the time, the third-highest-grossing film of all time in initial release behind Avatar and Titanic among 2012 releases. It won numerous awards and is frequently ranked among the greatest superhero films.
