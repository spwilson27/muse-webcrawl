Boiler

A boiler is a closed vessel in which fluid (generally water) is heated. The fluid does not necessarily boil; the heated or vaporised fluid exits the boiler for use in various processes or heating applications, including water heating, central heating, boiler-based power generation, cooking, and sanitation. Boilers are a core component of steam engines, power stations, district heating systems and most industrial process plants.

Types

Fire-tube boilers

In a fire-tube boiler, hot gases from a fire pass through one or more tubes running through a sealed container of water. The heat of the gases is transferred through the walls of the tubes by thermal conduction, heating the water and ultimately creating steam. The classic locomotive boiler and the Lancashire boiler are of this type.

Water-tube boilers

In a water-tube boiler, water circulates in tubes heated externally by the fire. Fuel is burned inside the furnace, creating hot gas which heats the water in the steam-generating tubes. Water-tube boilers can operate at much higher pressures and are standard in power stations and large ships. The Babcock & Wilcox design is a well-known example.

Materials and construction

The pressure vessel of a boiler is usually made of steel; historically wrought iron was used. Copper or brass may be used in small model boilers. Cast iron may be used for heating boilers in domestic applications, but such boilers are often made in sections joined together.

Safety

Boiler explosions were a frequent cause of death in the 19th century, and codes such as the ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code were developed to regulate design, inspection and operation. Every steam boiler is fitted with a safety valve that vents steam if pressure exceeds a safe limit, and with water-level controls to prevent overheating of dry surfaces.

Applications

Boilers drive steam turbines for electricity generation, propel ships, heat buildings, and supply process steam for industries from brewing to petrochemicals. Domestic boilers, usually fired by natural gas or oil, supply hot water and central heating.
