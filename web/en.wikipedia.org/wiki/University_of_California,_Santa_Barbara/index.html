The University of California, Santa Barbara (UCSB) is a public land-grant research university in Santa Barbara County, California, and one of the ten campuses of the University of California system. Its campus occupies a site on the Pacific coast near the community of Isla Vista.

Academics

UCSB is organized into three undergraduate colleges and two graduate schools, and is especially noted for physics and materials research through the Kavli Institute for Theoretical Physics; faculty and alumni have won multiple Nobel Prizes, including for the blue LED. The university enrols roughly 26,000 students.

Campus life

The university owns beaches and a lagoon on its grounds, and most undergraduates live in adjacent Isla Vista. The Gauchos compete in NCAA Division I, with a celebrated men's soccer program that won the 2006 national championship.
