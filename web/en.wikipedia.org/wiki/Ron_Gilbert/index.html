Ron Gilbert

Ron Gilbert
Born: 1 January 1964, La Grande, Oregon
Occupation: Video game designer, programmer
Known for: SCUMM, Monkey Island, Maniac Mansion

Ron Gilbert (born January 1, 1964) is an American video game designer, programmer, and producer, best known for his work on the classic LucasArts adventure games Maniac Mansion (1987) and The Secret of Monkey Island (1990).

Career

At Lucasfilm Games (later LucasArts), Gilbert co-created the SCUMM scripting engine with Aric Wilmunder, which powered a decade of point-and-click adventure games. He designed or co-designed Zak McKracken and the Alien Mindbenders, Monkey Island 2: LeChuck's Revenge, and supervised Day of the Tentacle. In 1992 he co-founded Humongous Entertainment, which made children's games using SCUMM, including the Putt-Putt and Freddi Fish series.

Gilbert later worked with Hothead Games on DeathSpank and with Double Fine on The Cave (2013). In 2017 he released Thimbleweed Park, a crowdfunded retro adventure, with Gary Winnick, and in 2022 Return to Monkey Island, developed with his studio Terrible Toybox in collaboration with Lucasfilm Games.
