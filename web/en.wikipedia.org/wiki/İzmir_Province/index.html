İzmir Province

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

İzmir Province (Turkish: İzmir ili) is a province in western Turkey, on the Aegean coast. Its capital is the city of İzmir, Turkey's third most populous city and its principal Aegean port. The province covers an area of about 11,900 km2 and has a population of roughly 4.5 million. It is divided into 30 districts, including popular coastal resorts such as Çeşme, Foça, and Seferihisar, and inland towns such as Bergama and Selçuk.

Geography and economy

The province features a Mediterranean climate, fertile river valleys (the Gediz, Küçük Menderes, and Bakırçay), and mountain ranges rising inland. Its economy combines the port and industry of İzmir with agriculture—figs, olives, grapes, and cotton—and tourism along the Aegean shore. The Port of İzmir is one of Turkey's largest container ports.

Archaeological sites

İzmir Province contains some of the most important ancient sites in Turkey, including Ephesus near Selçuk, Pergamon at Bergama, and ancient Smyrna in the modern city of İzmir. Both Ephesus and Pergamon are UNESCO World Heritage Sites.
