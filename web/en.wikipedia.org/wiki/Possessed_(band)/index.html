Possessed (band)

Background information
Origin:San Francisco Bay Area, California, U.S.
Genres:Death metal, thrash metal
Years active:1982–1993, 2007–present
Labels:Combat, Nuclear Blast
Key member:Jeff Becerra

Possessed is an American death metal band, originally formed in 1982 in the San Francisco Bay Area. The band is widely credited with pioneering the death metal genre; their 1984 demo was titled "Death Metal", and their debut album, Seven Churches (1985), is considered one of the first and most influential death metal records, bridging thrash metal and the more extreme style that followed.

History

Bassist and vocalist Jeff Becerra joined the band in 1983 and became its frontman. Seven Churches, recorded while guitarist Larry LaLonde was still a teenager, featured fast, heavily distorted riffing and Becerra's guttural vocals. The follow-up, Beyond the Gates (1986), and the EP The Eyes of Horror (1987, produced by Joe Satriani, LaLonde's former guitar teacher) moved toward a more technical sound. LaLonde later found fame with Primus. In 1989 Becerra was shot during a robbery and paralyzed from the chest down; the band dissolved in 1993.

Reformation and later work

Becerra reformed Possessed in 2007 and performs from a wheelchair. After decades of touring line-ups, the band released Revelations of Oblivion in 2019, their first studio album in over thirty years, on Nuclear Blast.
