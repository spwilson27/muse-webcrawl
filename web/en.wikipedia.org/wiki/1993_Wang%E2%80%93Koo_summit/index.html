1993 Wang–Koo summit

Dates27–29 April 1993
LocationSingapore
ParticipantsWang Daohan (PRC), Koo Chen-fu (ROC)

The 1993 Wang–Koo summit was the first formal meeting between representatives of mainland China (the People's Republic of China) and Taiwan (the Republic of China) since the Chinese Civil War. It took place in Singapore from 27 to 29 April 1993.

Participants and agenda

Wang Daohan headed the mainland's Association for Relations Across the Taiwan Straits, while Koo Chen-fu led Taiwan's Straits Exchange Foundation. These semi-official bodies allowed both sides to negotiate without formal diplomatic recognition. The talks focused on practical issues such as document authentication, registered mail, and fisheries disputes.

Outcomes

The two sides signed four agreements, including arrangements on verifying documents and handling lost registered mail. A second summit was held in Shanghai in 1998, though cross-strait dialogue was later suspended amid political tensions.
