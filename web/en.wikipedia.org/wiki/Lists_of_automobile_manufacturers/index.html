Lists of automobile manufacturers

This article indexes lists of automobile manufacturers, organized by country of origin and by other criteria. Thousands of companies have manufactured automobiles since the 1880s; today production is dominated by a small number of multinational groups such as Toyota, Volkswagen Group, Hyundai–Kia, General Motors, Ford, Stellantis, BYD, and Tesla.

By country

  • United States: Ford, General Motors (Chevrolet, GMC, Cadillac, Buick), Tesla, Rivian, Lucid; defunct: Studebaker, Packard, AMC, Pontiac, Oldsmobile, Saturn, Hummer (revived as GMC sub-brand).
  • Germany: Volkswagen, Audi, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Porsche, Opel.
  • Japan: Toyota (Lexus), Honda (Acura), Nissan (Infiniti), Mazda, Subaru, Mitsubishi, Suzuki.
  • China: BYD, Geely, SAIC, Chery, Great Wall, NIO, XPeng, Li Auto.
  • South Korea: Hyundai, Kia, Genesis.
  • France: Renault, Peugeot, Citroën, DS, Alpine, Bugatti.
  • Italy: Fiat, Alfa Romeo, Maserati, Ferrari, Lamborghini, Lancia.
  • United Kingdom: Jaguar Land Rover, Mini, Rolls-Royce, Bentley, Aston Martin, McLaren, Lotus.
  • India: Tata Motors, Mahindra, Maruti Suzuki.
  • Sweden: Volvo Cars, Koenigsegg; defunct: Saab.

By production

The largest manufacturer groups by annual vehicle production (recent years, approximate): Toyota Group (~10–11 million), Volkswagen Group (~8–9 million), Hyundai Motor Group (~7 million), General Motors (~6 million), and Stellantis (~6 million). China is the world's largest national producer, exceeding 30 million vehicles per year, and BYD became the world's largest maker of new-energy vehicles.
