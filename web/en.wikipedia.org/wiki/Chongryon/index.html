Chongryon

Chongryon, formally the General Association of Korean Residents in Japan, is an organisation representing Zainichi Koreans affiliated with North Korea. Founded in 1955, it has served as a de facto North Korean presence in Japan, which has no formal diplomatic relations with Pyongyang.

Activities

Chongryon has run a network of Korean schools, banks, businesses and cultural bodies in Japan and historically channelled remittances to North Korea. Its membership and assets declined sharply from the late 20th century amid financial scandals, falling membership and members switching allegiance to the South Korea-aligned Mindan.

Status

Japanese authorities monitor the organisation, and its headquarters in Tokyo have been the subject of prolonged legal and financial disputes.
