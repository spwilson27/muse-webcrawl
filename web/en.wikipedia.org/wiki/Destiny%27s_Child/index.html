Destiny's Child
Origin: Houston, Texas, U.S.
Years active: 1990–2006
Members (final): Beyoncé Knowles, Kelly Rowland, Michelle Williams

Destiny's Child was an American girl group whose final line-up comprised Beyoncé Knowles, Kelly Rowland and Michelle Williams. Formed in Houston, Texas, in 1990 as Girl's Tyme, the group rose to fame in the late 1990s with the line-up of Knowles, Rowland, LeToya Luckett and LaTavia Roberson.

Career

Their breakthrough came with the album The Writing's on the Wall (1999), featuring the number-one singles "Bills, Bills, Bills" and "Say My Name". After line-up changes, the trio released Survivor (2001), including the hits "Independent Women", "Survivor" and "Bootylicious", and the final studio album Destiny Fulfilled (2004). The group disbanded in 2006 following a farewell tour, having sold more than 60 million records worldwide, and reunited briefly for Beyoncé's 2018 Coachella performance.
