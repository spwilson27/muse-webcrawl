India national cricket team

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The India men's national cricket team represents India in international cricket and is governed by the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI). One of the most successful and widely followed teams in the sport, India played its first Test match in 1932, won the Cricket World Cup in 1983 and 2011, the T20 World Cup in 2007 and 2024, and the ICC Champions Trophy in 2002 (shared) and 2013.

History

Cricket was introduced to India by the British, and an Indian team first toured England in 1911. India became a Test nation in 1932 under captain C. K. Nayudu. The 1983 World Cup win under Kapil Dev transformed the sport's popularity in India; the 2011 triumph under M. S. Dhoni, on home soil, is another landmark.

Modern era

India has regularly been ranked among the top teams in all formats, producing players such as Sachin Tendulkar — the highest run-scorer in international cricket — Virat Kohli and Rohit Sharma. The Indian Premier League (founded 2008) made the BCCI the financial centre of world cricket.
