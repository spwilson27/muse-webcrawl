Taṇhā

Taṇhā is a Pali word in Buddhism meaning "thirst", "craving", or "desire". It is identified as the cause of suffering (dukkha) in the Four Noble Truths and as a link in the chain of dependent origination.

Types

Buddhist texts describe three kinds of craving: craving for sensual pleasures, craving for existence, and craving for non-existence. Abandoning taṇhā through the Noble Eightfold Path leads to liberation.
