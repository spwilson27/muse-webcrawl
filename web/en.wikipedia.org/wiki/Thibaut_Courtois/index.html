Thibaut Courtois

Thibaut Nicolas Marc Courtois (born 11 May 1992) is a Belgian professional footballer who plays as a goalkeeper for La Liga club Real Madrid and the Belgium national team. Regarded as one of the best goalkeepers in the world, he is known for his height (2.00 m), reflexes, command of the penalty area and shot-stopping.

Club career

Courtois began his career at Genk, helping them win the Belgian Pro League title in 2010–11, before joining Chelsea in 2011. He was immediately loaned to Atlético Madrid for three seasons, where he won La Liga, the Copa del Rey, the Europa League and reached the 2014 UEFA Champions League final, winning the Zamora Trophy twice. Returning to Chelsea, he won two Premier League titles and the Premier League Golden Glove. In 2018 he transferred to Real Madrid, where he has won multiple La Liga titles and was named Player of the Match in the 2022 Champions League final, making a record nine saves in the final as Madrid beat Liverpool.

International career

Courtois debuted for Belgium in 2011 and was part of the squad that finished third at the 2018 FIFA World Cup, where he won the Golden Glove as the tournament's best goalkeeper.
