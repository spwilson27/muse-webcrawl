Red Bull Racing

Image placeholder: a Red Bull Racing Grand Prix car.

Red Bull Racing, currently competing as Oracle Red Bull Racing, is a Formula One racing team owned by the Austrian beverage company Red Bull GmbH. Based in Milton Keynes, England, the team was formed in 2005 after Red Bull purchased the Jaguar Racing team, and has since become one of the most successful teams in the sport.

History

Red Bull Racing scored its first victory at the 2009 Chinese Grand Prix with Sebastian Vettel. From 2010 to 2013 the team won four consecutive Constructors' Championships, with Vettel taking four consecutive Drivers' Championships in the Adrian Newey-designed cars of that era. Following the hybrid-era dominance of Mercedes, Red Bull returned to the top with Max Verstappen, who won the Drivers' Championship in 2021 and subsequent seasons, while the team regained the Constructors' title in 2022.

Organisation

The team is led by team principal Christian Horner, with technical development long guided by chief technical officer Adrian Newey and motorsport adviser Helmut Marko overseeing the Red Bull junior driver programme, which has supplied much of the team's driving talent. The sister team, Racing Bulls (formerly Toro Rosso and AlphaTauri), serves as a proving ground for young drivers.
