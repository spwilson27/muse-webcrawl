Gülen movement

The Gülen movement (Turkish: Gülen cemaati), also called Hizmet ("Service"), is an Islam-inspired transnational social and religious movement led by Turkish Islamic scholar Fethullah Gülen, who lived in self-exile in the United States from 1999 until his death in 2024. The movement ran a network of schools, universities, media outlets, businesses and dialogue organizations in Turkey and over 100 other countries.

Once allied with Turkey's governing AKP, the movement fell out with President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan after 2013 corruption investigations. The Turkish government blamed it for the attempted coup of 15 July 2016, designated it a terrorist organization ("FETÖ"), and carried out extensive purges and closures. Members and observers dispute the movement's degree of involvement.
