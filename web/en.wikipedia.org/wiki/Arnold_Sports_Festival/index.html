Arnold Sports Festival

The Arnold Sports Festival, known as the Arnold Classic, is an annual multi-sport event held in Columbus, Ohio, since 1989. Founded by bodybuilder and former California governor Arnold Schwarzenegger and promoter Jim Lorimer, it centres on the Arnold Classic professional bodybuilding competition.

Events

The festival has grown into one of the largest multi-sport events in the world, hosting competitions in strongman (the Arnold Strongman Classic), powerlifting, martial arts, gymnastics, armwrestling, fitness and physique, alongside a large sports and fitness expo.

International editions

Sister festivals have been held in Europe, South America, Africa and Australia. Winners of the Arnold Classic bodybuilding title include Ronnie Coleman, Jay Cutler, Dexter Jackson and Flex Wheeler.
