Southbank, Victoria

Southbank is an inner-city suburb of Melbourne, Victoria, Australia, lying on the southern bank of the Yarra River directly opposite the Melbourne central business district. Once an industrial and warehouse district, it has been transformed since the 1990s into one of Australia's most densely populated residential and entertainment precincts.

Landmarks

Southbank contains the Melbourne Arts Precinct, including the Arts Centre Melbourne, the National Gallery of Victoria's international collection and the Melbourne Recital Centre, as well as the Crown Casino and Entertainment Complex, opened in 1997. Its skyline includes Eureka Tower and Australia 108, among the tallest residential buildings in the Southern Hemisphere.

Development

The Southgate development and the Southbank Boulevard promenade converted former industrial land into restaurants, offices and apartments, linked to the city centre by several pedestrian and road bridges. The suburb's population grew rapidly with high-rise construction in the 2000s and 2010s, raising debate about density, amenity and overshadowing of the river.
