The Jazz Singer (1980 film)

The Jazz Singer is a 1980 American musical drama film starring Neil Diamond, Laurence Olivier and Lucie Arnaz. It is a remake of the 1927 landmark film of the same name, itself adapted from Samson Raphaelson's play.

DirectorRichard Fleischer
StarringNeil Diamond, Laurence Olivier, Lucie Arnaz
MusicNeil Diamond
Release date19 December 1980

Plot

Yussel Rabinovitch, a cantor in his father's New York synagogue, secretly writes and performs pop songs as "Jess Robin". Torn between family tradition and his musical ambitions — and his marriage and a new love — he eventually reconciles with his father after performing Kol Nidre.

Soundtrack and reception

The soundtrack, featuring "Love on the Rocks", "Hello Again" and "America", was a major hit, becoming Diamond's best-selling album. The film itself was panned by critics and became a fixture of worst-film lists, though it performed moderately at the box office and has a cult following.
