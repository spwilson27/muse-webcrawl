List of pizza chains

This article lists notable pizza restaurant chains by country and size. The global pizza restaurant industry is dominated by a few large American-founded chains, alongside strong regional chains in many countries.

United States chains

Other major US chains include Marco's Pizza, Hungry Howie's, Papa Murphy's (take-and-bake), Jet's Pizza, Round Table Pizza, Chuck E. Cheese, Blaze Pizza, MOD Pizza and Mountain Mike's Pizza. Gourmet and regional favourites include Lou Malnati's and Giordano's (Chicago), and Pizzeria Uno.
