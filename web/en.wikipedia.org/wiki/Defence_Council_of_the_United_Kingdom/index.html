Defence Council of the United Kingdom

The Defence Council is the senior official body of the British Ministry of Defence, legally responsible for the conduct of defence business, chaired by the Secretary of State for Defence.

Composition

It includes ministers, the Chief of the Defence Staff, service chiefs, the Permanent Under Secretary and other senior officials and specialists.

History

It was created in 1964 when the modern Ministry of Defence was formed, succeeding the separate service councils in many functions.

Role

Service boards act as committees of the Defence Council for the administration of the armed forces.
