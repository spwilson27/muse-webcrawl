London Fields (novel)

London Fields (novel)
Author: Martin Amis
Published: 1989

London Fields is a 1989 novel by the British writer Martin Amis. Set in London in 1999, it is a blackly comic murder mystery narrated by the terminally ill American writer Samson Young.

Plot

Femme fatale Nicola Six foresees her own murder on her 35th birthday and manipulates the men around her, including petty criminal Keith Talent and banker Guy Clinch, as the city drifts toward an unnamed crisis.

Reception

The novel is considered one of Amis's major works, noted for its virtuoso prose, dart-playing antihero Keith Talent, and apocalyptic portrait of millennial London. A film adaptation appeared in 2018.
