Prophetstown State Park is a state park in Tippecanoe County, Indiana, near Battle Ground and West Lafayette, at the confluence of the Tippecanoe and Wabash rivers. Established in 2004 as Indiana's newest state park, it preserves restored tallgrass prairie and the site of Prophetstown, the Native American village founded in 1808 by the Shawnee prophet Tenskwatawa and his brother Tecumseh.

History and features

Prophetstown became the centre of Tecumseh's confederacy until its destruction by United States forces under William Henry Harrison after the Battle of Tippecanoe in November 1811. The park interprets this history with a recreated Native American village and, in partnership with a living-history organisation, a 1920s farmstead demonstrating early twentieth-century agriculture. Trails, an aquatic centre, campgrounds and prairie restoration plantings occupy the remainder of the site.
