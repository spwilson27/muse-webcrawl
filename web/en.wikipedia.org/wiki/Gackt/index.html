Gackt Camui (神威 楽斗, Kamui Gakuto), known mononymously as Gackt, is a Japanese singer-songwriter, musician, and actor. He rose to fame as the vocalist of the visual kei rock band Malice Mizer (1995–1999) before launching a highly successful solo career in 1999.

Career

Gackt joined Malice Mizer in 1995, fronting the band during its most commercially successful period, including the album Merveilles (1998). After leaving the band in 1999 he began a solo career with the EP Mizérable and the album Mars (2000). His solo albums — including Rebirth (2001), Moon (2002), Crescent (2003), Love Letter (2005), and Diabolos (2005) — have sold millions of copies in Japan, and his record for consecutive top-ten singles made him one of the country's best-selling male solo artists.

Outside music, Gackt became a fixture of Japanese television variety shows, winning fame for his long undefeated streak on the New Year celebrity ranking game of Geinōjin Kakuzuke Check. As an actor he played Uesugi Kenshin in the NHK taiga drama Fūrin Kazan (2007), starred in the film Moon Child (2003) alongside Hyde, appeared in Bunraku (2010), and voiced characters in anime and video games, including Genesis Rhapsodos in the Final Fantasy VII compilation.

Health hiatus

In September 2021 Gackt announced an indefinite hiatus from entertainment activities, citing a nervous-system disorder affecting his voice (dysphonia) and a serious physical condition. He gradually resumed activities from late 2022.
