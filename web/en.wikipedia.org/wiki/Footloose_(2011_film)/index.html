Footloose is a 2011 American musical drama film directed by Craig Brewer. It is a remake of the 1984 film of the same name. The film stars Kenny Wormald as Ren McCormack, Julianne Hough as Ariel Moore, Dennis Quaid as Reverend Shaw Moore and Andie MacDowell as Vi Moore.

Plot

After his mother's death, Boston teenager Ren McCormack moves to the small town of Bomont to live with his aunt and uncle. He discovers that the town, led by Reverend Shaw Moore, has banned loud music and public dancing following a car crash that killed five teenagers, including the Reverend's son. Ren befriends Willard and falls for the Reverend's rebellious daughter Ariel. Determined to restore the senior prom, Ren challenges the ordinance before the town council, quoting scripture in support, and ultimately wins the town — and the Reverend — over, culminating in a celebratory dance.

Production

The remake was developed at Paramount Pictures for several years; Zac Efron and Chace Crawford were attached to the lead role at different times before dancer Kenny Wormald was cast. Filming took place in Georgia in late 2010. The soundtrack updates songs from the original — including Kenny Loggins' "Footloose", re-recorded by Blake Shelton, and "Holding Out for a Hero" — alongside new material.

Reception

The film received mixed-to-positive reviews, with praise for its energy and fidelity to the original, and grossed about $63 million worldwide against a $24 million budget.
