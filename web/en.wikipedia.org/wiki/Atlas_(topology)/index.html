Atlas (topology)

In mathematics, particularly topology, an atlas is a collection of charts that describes a topological space, especially a manifold. Each chart maps an open subset of the space homeomorphically onto an open subset of Euclidean space.

Definitions

An atlas on a topological space M is a collection of pairs (U, φ), called charts, such that the domains U cover M and each φ is a homeomorphism onto its image. Where charts overlap, the compositions of one chart with the inverse of another are called transition maps; requiring these to be smooth defines a smooth atlas and hence a smooth manifold.

Examples

The surface of a sphere cannot be covered by a single chart onto the plane, but stereographic projection from the north and south poles gives a simple two-chart atlas. The term borrows from cartography, where an atlas is a book of maps covering the Earth.
