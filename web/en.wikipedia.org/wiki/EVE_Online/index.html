Eve Online

Eve Online (stylised EVE Online) is a space-based, persistent-world massively multiplayer online role-playing game (MMORPG) developed and published by CCP Games. Released in May 2003, it is set in a galaxy of over 7,800 star systems known as New Eden, where players pilot customizable spaceships and pursue activities including mining, manufacturing, trading, exploration, and combat (both PvE and PvP).

Gameplay and world

Uniquely among major MMOs, nearly all Eve players share a single persistent server universe ("Tranquility"), so events have permanent, galaxy-wide consequences. The player-driven economy is almost entirely supplied by player production, and the game's "sandbox" design tolerates scamming, espionage, and corporate warfare. Some fleet battles have involved thousands of participants and losses equivalent to tens of thousands of US dollars in in-game assets, such as the Battle of B-R5RB in 2014.

Development

CCP Games, based in Reykjavík, Iceland, has released numerous expansions, moving to a free-to-play "Alpha clone" model in 2016. Eve Online is noted in academic literature for its economy, which CCP has studied with professional economists, and its museum exhibits include a permanent place in the Museum of Modern Art's video game collection.
