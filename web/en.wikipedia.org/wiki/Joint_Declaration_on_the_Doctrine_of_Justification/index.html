Joint Declaration on the Doctrine of Justification

The Joint Declaration on the Doctrine of Justification (JDDJ) is a document created by representatives of the Roman Catholic Church and the Lutheran World Federation, signed in Augsburg, Germany, on 31 October 1999 — the anniversary of the start of the Reformation.

The declaration states that the churches share "a common understanding of our justification by God's grace through faith in Christ", resolving much of the central doctrinal dispute of the 16th-century Reformation over whether humans are saved by faith alone or by faith and works, and declaring that the mutual condemnations of that era do not apply to the partner's present teaching. The World Methodist Council affirmed the declaration in 2006, the World Communion of Reformed Churches in 2017, and the Anglican Communion welcomed it the same year.
