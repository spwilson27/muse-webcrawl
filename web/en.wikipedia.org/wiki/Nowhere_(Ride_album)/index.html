Nowhere (Ride album)

Nowhere
Studio album by Ride
Released: 15 October 1990
Recorded: 1990, Blackwing Studios, London
Genre: Shoegaze, alternative rock
Label: Creation
Producer: Ride

Nowhere is the debut studio album by the English rock band Ride, released on 15 October 1990 by Creation Records. It is widely regarded as one of the defining albums of the shoegaze genre, combining walls of distorted guitar with melodic songwriting and the dual vocals of Mark Gardener and Andy Bell.

Background and recording

Ride formed in Oxford in 1988 and quickly attracted attention with a series of EPs on Creation Records in early 1990. The band recorded Nowhere at Blackwing Studios in London. The album's sound layers heavily effected guitars over the propulsive drumming of Loz Colbert and Steve Queralt's bass, while its songs draw on the Byrds, the Velvet Underground and My Bloody Valentine.

The closing track, "Vapour Trail", became one of the band's best-known songs and was ranked among the top tracks of 1990 in year-end polls.

Reception and legacy

Nowhere reached number 11 on the UK Albums Chart. It has frequently appeared on lists of the greatest shoegaze albums, and in 2013 NME ranked it among the 500 greatest albums of all time. The album was reissued in expanded editions, including a 25th-anniversary edition in 2015 accompanied by live performances of the album in full.
