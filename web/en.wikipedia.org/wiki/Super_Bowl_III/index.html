Super Bowl III

Joe Namath guaranteed — and delivered — a Jets victory

Super Bowl III was the third AFL–NFL Championship Game in professional American football, and the first to officially bear the trademark name "Super Bowl". It was played on 12 January 1969 at the Orange Bowl in Miami, Florida. The American Football League (AFL) champion New York Jets defeated the National Football League (NFL) champion Baltimore Colts 16–7, in what is regarded as one of the greatest upsets in sports history and the game that legitimised the AFL before the merger.

The guarantee

The Colts, coached by Don Shula, were 13–1 and widely considered one of the greatest teams ever; oddsmakers made them 18-point favourites. At a Miami Touchdown Club dinner three days before the game, Jets quarterback Joe Namath, exasperated by heckling, declared: "We're going to win the game. I guarantee it."

The game

The Jets' defence intercepted Colts quarterbacks Earl Morrall and Johnny Unitas four times, while fullback Matt Snell rushed for 121 yards and the game's first touchdown. Jim Turner added three field goals. Baltimore scored only late in the fourth quarter. Namath completed 17 of 28 passes for 206 yards and was named MVP despite not throwing a touchdown pass — his audacious guarantee and poised play-calling against the blitz defined the game.

Legacy

The Jets' victory proved the AFL could beat the NFL's best, silencing critics of the upstart league a year before the AFL–NFL merger took full effect. Super Bowl III remains the archetype of the championship upset, and Namath's guarantee is among the most famous pronouncements in American sport.
