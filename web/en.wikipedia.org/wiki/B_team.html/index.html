B team

A B team is a secondary team fielded by a sports organisation, composed of players not selected for the first team, typically younger or developing athletes, reserve players returning from injury, or fringe squad members.

In association football

In several European countries, major clubs maintain B teams that compete in the regular league pyramid rather than a separate reserve league. In Spain, examples include FC Barcelona Atlètic and Real Madrid Castilla, which play in the third tier and cannot be promoted to the same division as their parent club; in Germany, second teams (e.g. Borussia Dortmund II) play no higher than the 3. Liga. In England, by contrast, reserve teams traditionally play in competitions such as the Premier League 2.

National B teams

Some countries have fielded national B teams in football and other sports, allowing selectors to assess emerging players in international fixtures below full senior level.

Other sports

B teams also exist in rugby, basketball and ice hockey (where farm teams in leagues such as the AHL serve a similar development function).
