Axwell Λ Ingrosso

Axwell Λ Ingrosso (stylised with the Greek letter lambda) is a Swedish DJ duo consisting of Axwell and Sebastian Ingrosso, two of the three members of the Swedish House Mafia. The duo formed in 2014 after Swedish House Mafia's split and performed together until the group's surprise reunion at Ultra Music Festival in 2018.

Career

Axwell and Ingrosso debuted as a duo at the Governors Ball Music Festival in New York in June 2014. Their first releases included "Something New" (2014) and "On My Way". The single "Sun Is Shining" (2015) reached number one in Sweden and was used in an H&M campaign. In 2017 they released "More Than You Know", which became their biggest hit, topping charts across Europe and reaching the top ten in many countries, followed by "Dreamer".
