Canadian (disambiguation)

Canadian may refer to something of, from, or related to Canada, the country occupying the northern part of North America, or to a person from Canada (a Canadian). The term has a number of more specific uses:

People and language

  • Canadians – people identified with the country of Canada.
  • Canadian English – the variety of English spoken in Canada.
  • Canadian French – the varieties of French spoken in Canada.

Transportation

  • The Canadian – a transcontinental passenger train operated by Via Rail between Toronto and Vancouver, introduced in 1955 by Canadian Pacific.
  • Canadian Pacific Railway and Canadian National Railway – Canada's two historic Class I freight railways.

Places

  • Canadian, Oklahoma – a town in Pittsburg County, Oklahoma, United States.
  • Canadian, Texas – a city in Hemphill County, Texas, United States.
  • Canadian River – the longest tributary of the Arkansas River, flowing through New Mexico, Texas, and Oklahoma.
  • Canadian County, Oklahoma – a county in the U.S. state of Oklahoma.

Other uses

  • Canadian whisky – a type of whisky produced in Canada.
  • Canadian bacon – a form of back bacon popular in the United States.
  • Canadian (horse) – a breed of horse from Canada.
  • Le Canadien – a historic French-language newspaper in Lower Canada.
