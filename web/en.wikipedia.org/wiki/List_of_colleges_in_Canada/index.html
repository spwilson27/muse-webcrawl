List of colleges in Canada

In Canada, the word college usually refers to a post-secondary institution offering applied and technical programs, diplomas, and certificates — distinct from a university, which is degree-granting and research-focused, although many Canadian colleges now also offer applied bachelor's degrees. In Quebec, the term refers to the CEGEP system of pre-university and technical colleges that form a mandatory step between secondary school and university. This list covers notable public colleges by province and territory.

Ontario

Ontario has 24 publicly funded Colleges of Applied Arts and Technology, created beginning in 1965:

  • Algonquin College (Ottawa)
  • Seneca College (Toronto) — one of the largest in Canada
  • Humber College (Toronto)
  • George Brown College (Toronto)
  • Centennial College (Toronto)
  • Sheridan College (Oakville/Brampton) — noted for animation
  • Fanshawe College (London)
  • Mohawk College (Hamilton)
  • Conestoga College (Kitchener-Waterloo)
  • Durham College (Oshawa)
  • St. Lawrence College, Confederation College, Cambrian College, Canadore College, Niagara College, and others

Quebec

Quebec's 48 public CEGEPs (Collèges d'enseignement général et professionnel) include:

  • Dawson College, Vanier College, and John Abbott College (English-language)
  • Cégep du Vieux Montréal, Cégep de Sainte-Foy, Collège de Maisonneuve, and Cégep Limoilou (French-language)

Western and Atlantic Canada

  • British Columbia: BCIT (British Columbia Institute of Technology), Langara College, Douglas College, Vancouver Community College, Okanagan College, Camosun College
  • Alberta: NAIT and SAIT (polytechnics), Bow Valley College, NorQuest College, Red Deer Polytechnic
  • Manitoba: Red River College Polytechnic, Assiniboine Community College
  • Saskatchewan: Saskatchewan Polytechnic
  • Atlantic: Nova Scotia Community College (NSCC), Holland College (PEI), College of the North Atlantic (Newfoundland and Labrador), New Brunswick Community College (NBCC)
  • North: Yukon University, Aurora College (NWT), Nunavut Arctic College
