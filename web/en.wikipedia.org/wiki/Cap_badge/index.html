Cap badge

A cap badge, also called a head badge or hat badge, is a badge worn on military or other uniform headgear to indicate the wearer's regiment, corps or organisation.

Type: Military insignia
Worn on: Caps, berets and other headdress

History

Cap badges became standard in the British Army in the late 19th century and are used by armed forces worldwide, often incorporating regimental symbols, mottoes and battle honours.

Materials

Badges are typically made of metal, though cloth and plastic versions exist, and they are attached by pins, sliders or lugs.
