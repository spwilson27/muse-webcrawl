The Hand (comics)

The Hand is a fictional order of evil mystical ninja assassins appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer-artist Frank Miller, the organisation first appeared in Daredevil #174 (September 1981) and serves as a recurring antagonist of Daredevil, Elektra, Wolverine and other heroes.

Fictional history

The Hand's origins lie in 1588 Japan, when a secret society of samurai grew corrupted by worship of the demon known as the Beast, becoming a cult devoted to power and assassination. Its ninja can be resurrected after death, dissolving into dust when slain, and its members — traditionally clad in red — practise occult ninjutsu of extraordinary lethality. In Miller's Daredevil run, the Hand battles Daredevil and its rival order, the Chaste, led by Stick, who trained both Daredevil and the assassin Elektra; Elektra, killed and resurrected by the Hand, became one of its most prominent figures.

In other media

The Hand is a major antagonist in Marvel Television's Netflix series, appearing in Daredevil and Iron Fist and as the central villain of the crossover miniseries The Defenders (2017), where its five founding leaders — including Madame Gao and Alexandra Reid, played by Sigourney Weaver — scheme to harvest the substance of dragon bones beneath New York City.
