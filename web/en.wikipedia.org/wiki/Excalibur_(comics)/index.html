Excalibur (comics)

Excalibur is a comic book series published by Marvel Comics, featuring a British superhero team of the same name. Created by writer Chris Claremont and artist Alan Davis, the team first appeared in Excalibur Special Edition #1 (1988) before launching into an ongoing series the same year. The founding members — Captain Britain (Brian Braddock), Meggan, and three former X-Men, Nightcrawler, Kitty Pryde (Shadowcat), and Rachel Summers (Phoenix) — were brought together in Britain after believing the X-Men had died.

Publication history

The Claremont–Davis run was characterised by whimsical, swashbuckling adventures, including the "Cross-Time Caper," in which the team's train journeyed through a series of alternate Earths. Alan Davis later returned as writer and artist, bringing the series closer to the Captain Britain mythology developed in Marvel UK titles and introducing elements such as the dragon Lockheed's companions and the dragon of the story arc involving Kylun and Cerise.

The original series ran for 125 issues until 1998. The title was later revived in several forms: a 2001 miniseries, a 2004 series centred on Professor X and Magneto on Genosha, and a 2019 relaunch written by Tini Howard as part of the X-Men line's "Dawn of X" era, with a roster including Betsy Braddock as Captain Britain, Gambit, Rogue, Jubilee, Rictor, and Apocalypse.

Fictional team biography

Based initially in a lighthouse on the Cornish coast, Excalibur defended Britain and the multiverse, guarding the nexus of realities associated with the Captain Britain Corps. Members over the years have included Colossus, Pete Wisdom, Wolfsbane, and Doctor Faiza Hussain, who wielded the sword Excalibur itself.
