The Exterminating Angel (opera)

The Exterminating Angel is an English-language opera in three acts by the British composer Thomas Adès, with a libretto by Tom Cairns in collaboration with the composer. It is based on Luis Buñuel's 1962 surrealist film El ángel exterminador. The opera was commissioned by the Salzburg Festival, the Royal Opera House, Covent Garden, the Metropolitan Opera, and the Royal Danish Opera.

Premiere and productions

The opera premiered at the Salzburg Festival on 28 July 2016, conducted by the composer and directed by Cairns. It was subsequently staged at Covent Garden and at the Metropolitan Opera in 2017. The work calls for a large ensemble cast of fifteen principal singers and an orchestra including ondes Martenot.

Synopsis

Guests assemble for a dinner party at the mansion of Edmundo and Lucía de Nobile after an evening at the opera. As the night wears on, the guests find themselves inexplicably unable to leave the music room, though no physical barrier exists. Over days of confinement, politeness collapses into squalor, hysteria, and violence; some die, and a sacrificial victim is demanded. At last, guided by the singer Leticia, the guests exactly re-enact the positions and words of their first evening — and are able to walk out, only for the ending to hint that the entrapment begins again on a larger scale.
