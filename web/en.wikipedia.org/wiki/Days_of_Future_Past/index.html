Days of Future Past

"Days of Future Past" is a storyline in the Marvel Comics series The Uncanny X-Men, published in issues #141–142 in 1981. Written by Chris Claremont and illustrated by John Byrne, it is regarded as one of the most influential X-Men stories and introduced the dystopian future timeline that has recurred throughout X-Men media.

The cover of Uncanny X-Men #141, parodied and homaged many times since.

Plot

The story alternates between 1981 and the year 2013, in which most mutants of North America have been killed or imprisoned in internment camps by giant robotic Sentinels, following the Brotherhood of Evil Mutants' assassination of Senator Robert Kelly. The adult consciousness of Kitty Pryde is projected back in time into her younger self to warn the X-Men and prevent the assassination, thereby averting the dark future. The team succeeds, but the narrative leaves open whether the future has truly been changed.

Legacy and adaptations

The storyline established alternate-timeline storytelling as a staple of the X-Men franchise, with later continuations depicting the "Days of Future Past" timeline (designated Earth-811) and characters from it, such as Rachel Summers and Bishop. It was adapted in the 1990s X-Men animated series and loosely formed the basis of the 2014 film X-Men: Days of Future Past, in which Wolverine's consciousness is sent back to 1973 to prevent the creation of the Sentinel programme.
