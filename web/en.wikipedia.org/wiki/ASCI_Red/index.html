ASCI Red

ASCI Red
Also known as: Intel Teraflops
Location: Sandia National Laboratories
Performance: First to exceed 1 TFLOPS

ASCI Red, also known as Intel ASCI Option Red or the Intel Teraflops, was a supercomputer built by Intel and Sandia National Laboratories for the U.S. Department of Energy's Accelerated Strategic Computing Initiative. In 1997 it became the first computer to exceed one trillion floating-point operations per second on the LINPACK benchmark.

Design

The system used thousands of Intel Pentium Pro and later Pentium II Xeon processors in a distributed-memory MIMD architecture, eventually reaching 1.8 TFLOPS after upgrades. It was used for nuclear weapons stockpile stewardship simulations.
