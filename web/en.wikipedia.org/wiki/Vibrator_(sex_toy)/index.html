Vibrator (sex toy)

TypeSex toy
MechanismSmall electric motor with offset weight
Early modelHitachi Magic Wand (1968)

A vibrator is a sex toy that produces vibration, typically through a small electric motor with an eccentric (unbalanced) weight on its shaft, and is used for sexual stimulation and pleasure, alone or with a partner. Vibrators come in many forms, including external "wand" and "bullet" designs and insertable designs shaped for vaginal or anal use, made from materials such as silicone, glass and ABS plastic.

History

Electromechanical vibrators were among the earliest home electrical appliances, marketed from the 1880s onward as massage and medical devices. The popular historical claim that Victorian physicians used them to treat "hysteria" has been influential but is disputed by later historians. The Hitachi Magic Wand, launched in 1968 as a massager, became widely known as a vibrator, and from the 1970s feminist sex shops such as Good Vibrations promoted vibrators openly as sex toys.

Use and regulation

Surveys in several countries indicate that vibrator use is common among both women and men. Vibrators are also sometimes recommended in sexual medicine, for example to help with anorgasmia. Their sale has at times been restricted by obscenity and anti-sex-toy laws in parts of the United States and elsewhere; several such bans were struck down or repealed in the 2000s.
