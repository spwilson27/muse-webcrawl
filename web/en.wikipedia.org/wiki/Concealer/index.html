A concealer or color corrector is a type of cosmetic that is used to mask imperfections on the skin. These imperfections can include dark circles under the eyes, blemishes, and hyperpigmentation. Concealer is similar to, and can be used in conjunction with, foundation, a lighter and more uniform coverage cosmetic. Concealer is generally more heavily pigmented and thicker in consistency than foundation, and is applied to specific areas rather than over the whole face.

Use

Concealer is applied in small amounts to individual spots, under the eyes, or around the nose, then blended at the edges with a brush, sponge, or fingertip. It may be worn alone, under foundation, or over it, and is often set with powder to prevent creasing and increase wear. Colour-correcting concealers use complementary colours to neutralise discolouration: green tones counteract redness, peach and orange tones counteract blue-purple under-eye shadows, and lavender can counteract sallowness.

Formulations

Liquid concealers, often with doe-foot applicators, suit larger areas and dry or mature skin; cream concealers in pots give high coverage; stick concealers are convenient for touch-ups. Modern formulations commonly include silicones for smoothing, humectants, and pigments such as iron oxides and titanium dioxide.

Concealers are used by people of all genders, in everyday makeup, stage and screen makeup, and photography.
