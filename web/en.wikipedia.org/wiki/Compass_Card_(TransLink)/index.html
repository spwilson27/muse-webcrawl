Compass Card (TransLink)

The Compass Card is a reloadable stored-value smart card used for electronic payment of public transit fares in Metro Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. It is operated by TransLink and was rolled out across the region in 2015, replacing paper fares and monthly passes.

Use

Passengers tap the card on readers when boarding buses and entering and leaving SkyTrain and West Coast Express stations. The card supports stored value, monthly passes and concession fares, and can be registered online for balance protection. Contactless credit and debit cards and mobile wallets are also accepted on the same system.
