List of retired Atlantic hurricane names

The World Meteorological Organization retires the name of a tropical cyclone when a storm is so deadly or damaging that reusing the name would be insensitive or confusing. Retired names are replaced on the six-year rotating Atlantic lists by another name of the same initial letter. More than 90 Atlantic names have been retired since the practice began in 1954.


Names of the most destructive Atlantic hurricanes are permanently retired.
