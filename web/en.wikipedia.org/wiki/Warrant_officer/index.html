Warrant officer

A warrant officer (WO) is an officer in a military organisation who is designated an officer by a warrant, as distinguished from a commissioned officer who is designated by a commission, and a non-commissioned officer who is designated by an enlisted contract. Warrant officers typically sit somewhere between commissioned officers and enlisted personnel in the hierarchy.

United States

In the US armed forces, warrant officers are technical specialists — helicopter pilots, intelligence technicians, special forces operators and maintenance experts — appointed by warrant in the grade of warrant officer one (WO1) and commissioned as chief warrant officers (CW2–CW5) as they advance. They form a small, distinct corps prized for deep technical expertise.

British and Commonwealth usage

In the British Army and Royal Navy, warrant officer is instead the most senior non-commissioned rank (WO1 and WO2), held by figures such as the Regimental Sergeant Major, who are appointed by warrant but remain part of the other-ranks structure rather than the commissioned officer corps.
