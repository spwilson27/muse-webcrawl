Rail grinding

Rail grinding is a track maintenance process in which the running surface of railway rails is ground by a train carrying rotating grinding stones or milling tools. It restores the rail profile, removes corrugation and surface defects, and extends rail life.

Purpose

Regular grinding controls rolling contact fatigue defects such as head checks, removes corrugated wear that causes noise and vibration, restores the designed transverse profile for good wheel–rail contact, and removes decarburised and damaged metal. Grinders work at relatively slow speeds, often at night, and can throw significant sparks, so fire precautions are taken in dry conditions.
