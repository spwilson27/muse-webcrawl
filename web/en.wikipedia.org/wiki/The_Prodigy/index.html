The Prodigy are an English electronic dance music band formed in Braintree, Essex, in 1990 by producer and songwriter Liam Howlett. Alongside the Chemical Brothers and Fatboy Slim, they are credited with pioneering the big beat genre and bringing rave culture into the mainstream in the 1990s. The band has sold over 25 million records worldwide.

History

The Prodigy emerged from the UK rave scene with early singles such as "Charly" (1991) and the debut album Experience (1992). Music for the Jilted Generation (1994) responded to the UK government's Criminal Justice Bill and expanded their sound. Their third album, The Fat of the Land (1997), topped charts in many countries and included "Firestarter", "Breathe" and the controversial "Smack My Bitch Up", with frontman Keith Flint's spiked-hair image making the band a defining act of the decade.

Later albums include Always Outnumbered, Never Outgunned (2004), Invaders Must Die (2009), The Day Is My Enemy (2015) and No Tourists (2018), their seventh consecutive UK number-one studio album. Flint died in March 2019; Howlett and Maxim continued the band, returning to live performance in 2022.
