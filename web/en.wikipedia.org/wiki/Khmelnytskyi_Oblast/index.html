Khmelnytskyi Oblast (Ukrainian: Хмельницька область), also called Khmelnychchyna, is an oblast (province) in western Ukraine. Its administrative centre is the city of Khmelnytskyi. The oblast has a population of roughly 1.2 million and covers an area of about 20,600 square kilometres in the historical regions of Podolia and southern Volhynia.

History

The oblast was created on 22 September 1937 as Kamianets-Podilskyi Oblast, out of the former Vinnytsia Oblast of the Ukrainian SSR. In 1941 the administrative centre was moved to Proskuriv, and in 1954 both the city and the oblast were renamed in honour of the Cossack hetman Bohdan Khmelnytsky, on the 300th anniversary of the Pereiaslav Agreement.

Geography

The oblast lies on the Podolian Upland and is crossed by the Southern Bug and by tributaries of the Dniester and Prypiat. Its south-western border follows the Dniester river. The region is largely agricultural, known for fertile black earth (chernozem), and also hosts the Khmelnytskyi Nuclear Power Plant near Netishyn.

Main towns and sights

  • Khmelnytskyi — the oblast capital, formerly Proskuriv
  • Kamianets-Podilskyi — a historic fortress city on the Smotrych river canyon, one of the most visited sights in Ukraine
  • Shepetivka, Starokostiantyniv, Netishyn and Slavuta
  • The Bakota flooded village and the Podilski Tovtry National Nature Park
