National Film Development Corporation of India

Founded: 1975
Headquarters: Mumbai, India
Type: Government enterprise
Parent: Ministry of Information and Broadcasting

The National Film Development Corporation of India (NFDC) is a government agency under India's Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, established in 1975 to promote Indian cinema, particularly independent and art-house filmmaking.

Activities

The NFDC finances, produces and distributes films in various Indian languages, supporting directors whose work might not attract commercial backing. It organises the Film Bazaar, a co-production market held alongside the International Film Festival of India in Goa, and operates Cinemas of India distribution and streaming initiatives.

Notable productions

NFDC-backed films include classics of the parallel cinema movement such as Jaane Bhi Do Yaaro, Mirch Masala, Salaam Bombay! (co-production) and works by Satyajit Ray, Mrinal Sen, Shyam Benegal and Adoor Gopalakrishnan. In 2022 the government merged several film bodies, including the Films Division and Directorate of Film Festivals, into the NFDC.
