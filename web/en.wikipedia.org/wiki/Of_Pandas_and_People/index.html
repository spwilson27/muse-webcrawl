Of Pandas and People

Of Pandas and People: The Central Question of Biological Origins is a controversial 1989 school book advocating intelligent design (ID). The book was written by Percival Davis and Dean H. Kenyon and published by the Foundation for Thought and Ethics (FTE) of Richardson, Texas.

The book became notable in the case of Kitzmiller v. Dover Area School District (2005), where drafts showing the word "creationism" replaced by "intelligent design"—including the transitional form "cdesign proponentsists"—were presented as evidence that intelligent design was a relabelling of creationism, leading the court to rule that teaching ID in public school science classes violated the Establishment Clause.
