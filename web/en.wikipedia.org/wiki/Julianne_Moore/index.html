Julianne Moore

Julianne Moore (born Julie Anne Smith; December 3, 1960) is an American actress. Prolific in film since the early 1990s, she is known for portrayals of emotionally troubled women in both independent films and blockbusters, and has received an Academy Award, a BAFTA, two Golden Globes and two Emmy Awards.

Career

Moore began on television, winning an Emmy for the soap opera As the World Turns. Film breakthroughs included Short Cuts (1993), Boogie Nights (1997), The End of the Affair (1999), Far from Heaven and The Hours (both 2002). She won the Academy Award for Best Actress for playing a woman with early-onset Alzheimer's disease in Still Alice (2014). Other notable roles include Magnolia, Children of Men, A Single Man, The Kids Are All Right and May December. She is also the author of the children's book series Freckleface Strawberry.
