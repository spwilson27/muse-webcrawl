Juan Sebastián Verón (born 1975) is an Argentine former professional footballer who played as a midfielder. Nicknamed La Brujita ("The Little Witch"), a reference to his father Juan Ramón Verón, he is regarded as one of the finest playmakers of his generation.

Club career

Verón began his career at Estudiantes de La Plata, the club of his father, before a brief spell at Boca Juniors and a move to Italy with Sampdoria. He won the UEFA Cup with Parma in 1999 and a Serie A title with Lazio in 2000. In 2001 he became the most expensive transfer in English football when he joined Manchester United, and he later played for Chelsea and Inter Milan.

Return to Estudiantes

In 2006 Verón returned to Estudiantes, captaining the club to the 2009 Copa Libertadores title. He was named South American Footballer of the Year in 2008 and 2009. After retiring, he became president of Estudiantes, and briefly returned to the pitch for the club in 2017.

International career

Verón earned 73 caps for Argentina and represented his country at the 1998, 2002 and 2010 FIFA World Cups.
