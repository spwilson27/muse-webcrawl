Business plan

A business plan is a formal written document containing the goals of a business, the methods for attaining those goals, and the time frame for the achievement of the goals. It also describes the nature of the business, background information on the organisation, the organisation's financial projections, and the strategies it intends to implement to achieve the stated targets. In its entirety, this document serves as a road map (a plan) that provides direction to the business.

A written business plan used to guide a venture and raise finance.

Purpose

Business plans may be written for internal use, to guide management, or for external audiences such as investors, lenders, and prospective partners. Externally focused plans typically aim to raise capital or secure credit, and therefore emphasise financial projections and the experience of the management team. An entrepreneur seeking venture capital will usually be required to present a business plan.

Typical structure

Although formats vary, a conventional business plan includes:

  • Executive summary — a concise overview of the business and its aims.
  • Company description — legal structure, history, and ownership.
  • Products and services — what the business sells and its competitive advantages.
  • Market analysis — the target market, industry conditions, and competitors.
  • Marketing and sales strategy — how customers will be reached and won.
  • Operations and management — organisational structure and key personnel.
  • Financial plan — forecasts of revenue, costs, cash flow, and funding requirements.

Criticism

The value of formal business planning is debated. Research and practitioner experience suggest that extensive planning does not reliably predict success, particularly for novel ventures facing high uncertainty; approaches such as the lean startup and effectuation favour experimentation and shorter planning documents, such as the one-page Business Model Canvas. Nevertheless, plans remain standard requirements for bank lending and many grant programmes.
