Jochebed

Jochebed (Hebrew: יוֹכֶבֶד, Yōkheved) was, according to the Book of Exodus, a Levite woman and the mother of Miriam, Aaron and Moses. She was the wife of Amram and the aunt of her own husband, being the daughter of Levi and sister of Amram's father Kohath.

Jochebed entrusting the infant Moses to the Nile.

Biblical account

Exodus 2 recounts that, when Pharaoh ordered the death of Hebrew male infants, Jochebed hid her newborn son for three months and then placed him in a waterproofed basket among the reeds of the Nile. The baby was found by Pharaoh's daughter, and — through his sister Miriam's intervention — Jochebed was hired, unknowingly to the princess, as his wet nurse, so Moses was raised by his own mother before being taken to the palace. Jochebed's name is given in Exodus 6:20 and Numbers 26:59, which place her among the children of Levi born in Egypt.

In tradition

Jewish tradition praises Jochebed's courage and faith; the New Testament's Hebrews 11:23 commends Moses' parents for hiding him "because they saw he was no ordinary child". Rabbinic sources identify her with Shiphrah, one of the midwives who defied Pharaoh, though this is debated. In Islam she is revered as the mother of Musa (Moses), though the Qur'an does not name her.
