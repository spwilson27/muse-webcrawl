St Patrick's College, Maynooth is the national seminary for Ireland and a pontifical university, located in the village of Maynooth, County Kildare. Founded in 1795, it was for much of its history the largest seminary in the world.

History

The college was established by an Act of the Irish Parliament in 1795, partly so that Irish Catholic priests could be educated at home rather than in revolutionary France. The campus is noted for its Gothic Revival architecture, much of it designed by Augustus Welby Pugin, including the landmark College Chapel.

Maynooth University

In 1997, the university functions of the college were separated to form Maynooth University, which shares the campus. St Patrick's continues to train seminarians and grants pontifical degrees in theology and related fields.
