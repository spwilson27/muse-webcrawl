List of S&P 400 companies

The List of S&P 400 companies enumerates the constituents of the S&P MidCap 400 index, maintained by S&P Dow Jones Indices. The index covers roughly 7% of the United States equity market and is composed of mid-sized companies, generally with market capitalizations in the range of several billion dollars. Together with the S&P 500 and the S&P SmallCap 600, it forms the S&P Composite 1500.

Selection criteria

To be eligible, a company must be a U.S. company with an adequately liquid float-adjusted market capitalization, positive earnings under generally accepted accounting principles for the most recent quarter and the sum of the trailing four quarters, and sufficient public float and trading volume. Index membership is reviewed and rebalanced quarterly.

Characteristics

Mid-cap stocks are often described as a balance between the growth potential of small caps and the stability of large caps, and the S&P MidCap 400 has historically outperformed the S&P 500 over long horizons with moderately higher volatility. The list changes regularly as companies grow into the S&P 500 or shrink into the SmallCap 600.
