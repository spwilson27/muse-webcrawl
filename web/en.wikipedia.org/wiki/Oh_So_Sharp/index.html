Oh So Sharp (1982–2001) was an Irish-bred, British-trained Thoroughbred racehorse best known for winning the English Fillies' Triple Crown in 1985 — the 1000 Guineas, the Epsom Oaks and the St Leger. Trained by Henry Cecil at Newmarket and owned by Sheikh Mohammed, she was one of the outstanding fillies of her generation. She is a racehorse, and not connected with Stephen Foster's song "Oh! Susanna".

Racing career

Unbeaten in three starts as a two-year-old in 1984, including the Fillies' Mile at Ascot, Oh So Sharp returned in 1985 to win the Nell Gwyn Stakes and then the 1000 Guineas at Newmarket under Steve Cauthen. At Epsom she won the Oaks in record time. After a narrow defeat by Petoski in the King George VI and Queen Elizabeth Stakes at Ascot — her first loss — she was beaten again in the Benson & Hedges Gold Cup at York, but completed the Triple Crown by winning the St Leger at Doncaster, the first filly to achieve the feat since Meld in 1955. She was retired at the end of the season with seven wins from nine starts.

Breeding and legacy

As a broodmare at Dalham Hall Stud, Oh So Sharp produced the Prix Saint-Alary winner Rosefinch and the useful Shaima among her foals. She died in 2001. The Oh So Sharp Stakes, a Group 3 race for two-year-old fillies at Newmarket, is run in her memory.
