List of Legendary Pictures films

Legendary Pictures (Legendary Entertainment) is an American film production company founded in 2000 by Thomas Tull. It is known for large-scale genre films, including Christopher Nolan's Batman films (co-productions), the MonsterVerse franchise, Dune and Pokémon Detective Pikachu.
