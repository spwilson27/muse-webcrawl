Richard of Clare

Richard of Clare may refer to several members of the Anglo-Norman Clare family, who held the earldoms of Hertford, Gloucester and Pembroke in the 11th–14th centuries:

  • Richard fitz Gilbert (c. 1030–c. 1090), also called Richard de Bienfaite or Richard of Tonbridge, ancestor of the family and companion of William the Conqueror.
  • Richard fitz Gilbert de Clare (died 1136), created Earl of Hertford, killed in an ambush in Wales.
  • Richard de Clare, 2nd Earl of Pembroke (1130–1176), known as "Strongbow", who led the Norman invasion of Ireland.
  • Richard de Clare, 3rd Earl of Hertford (c. 1153–1217), one of the twenty-five barons who enforced Magna Carta.
  • Richard de Clare, 6th Earl of Gloucester (1222–1262), powerful magnate during the reign of Henry III.
