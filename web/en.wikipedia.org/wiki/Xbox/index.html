Xbox is a video gaming brand owned by Microsoft, created with the launch of the original Xbox console in 2001. The brand encompasses a series of home video game consoles, the Xbox network online service (formerly Xbox Live), game development studios under Xbox Game Studios, the Xbox Game Pass subscription service, and cloud streaming. Xbox consoles compete principally with Sony's PlayStation and Nintendo's consoles, and the brand is one of the pillars of Microsoft Gaming, which grew further with the acquisitions of ZeniMax Media (2021) and Activision Blizzard (2023).

Consoles

  • Xbox (2001): Microsoft's entry into the console market, notable for a built-in hard drive and the launch of Halo: Combat Evolved.
  • Xbox 360 (2005): a major commercial success that popularised online console multiplayer, achievements and downloadable content through Xbox Live, despite the costly "Red Ring of Death" hardware failures.
  • Xbox One (2013): launched amid controversy over digital rights restrictions (quickly reversed) and the bundled Kinect; later revised as the One S and the powerful One X (2017).
  • Xbox Series X and Series S (2020): the current generation, emphasising fast SSD loading, backward compatibility and subscription services.

Services

The Xbox network (Xbox Live, launched 2002) pioneered standardised online play, friends lists and digital storefronts on consoles. Xbox Game Pass (2017) offers a rotating library of downloadable games, including all first-party titles on release day, and is considered central to Microsoft's gaming strategy; Xbox Cloud Gaming (xCloud) streams games to phones, PCs and browsers. The Xbox app and ecosystem extend across Windows PCs, mobile devices and smart TVs.
