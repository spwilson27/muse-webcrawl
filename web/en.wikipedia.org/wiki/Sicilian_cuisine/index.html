Sicilian cuisine
RegionSicily, Italy

Sicilian cuisine is the cooking style of Sicily, Italy, shaped by Greek, Arab, Norman, Spanish and mainland Italian influences over two millennia. It is known for seafood, citrus, aubergines, tomatoes, olives, capers and rich desserts.

History

Arab rule in the 9th–11th centuries introduced rice, citrus, sugar cane, saffron and ice-cream techniques. Spanish rule brought New World tomatoes and peppers.

Dishes

Signature foods include arancini, caponata, pasta alla Norma, pasta con le sarde, cannoli, cassata, granita and brioche con gelato. Street food in Palermo includes panelle and sfincione. See Italian cuisine and Trieste for other Italian regional traditions.
