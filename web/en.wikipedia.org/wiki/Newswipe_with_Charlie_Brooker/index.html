Newswipe with Charlie Brooker

Newswipe with Charlie Brooker is a British television programme broadcast on BBC Four in 2009 and 2010, written and presented by Charlie Brooker. A companion piece to his Screenwipe series, it applies the same satirical, media-literate format to television news and current affairs coverage.

Format

Across two series of six episodes, Brooker dissected the conventions of rolling news: the theatrical graphics, pseudo-urgency, vox pops, expert punditry, and the packaging of events such as elections, swine flu, and celebrity stories. Segments by contributors including Adam Curtis examined the history and language of news reporting. One 2010 segment, "How to Report the News", became widely circulated online as a parody blueprint of a standard news package.

Reception and legacy

The show was critically praised for its media criticism and won a Royal Television Society award. It was followed by Brooker's How TV Ruined Your Life (2011) and the annual Wipe specials; Brooker later created the anthology drama Black Mirror.
