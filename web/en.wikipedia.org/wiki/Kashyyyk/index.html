Kashyyyk is a fictional planet in the Star Wars universe and the homeworld of the Wookiees, including Chewbacca. It is depicted as a lush jungle world covered by immense wroshyr trees, whose canopies rise hundreds of metres and stratify the planet's ecology into distinct layers from canopy to shadowy forest floor.

Depiction

Kashyyyk first appeared in the 1978 Star Wars Holiday Special and featured prominently in Star Wars: Episode III – Revenge of the Sith (2005), where a Clone Wars battle is fought on its beaches and in its lagoons, with Wookiee forces under Tarfful and Chewbacca aided by Jedi Master Yoda. The planet also appears in video games including Star Wars Battlefront and Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order, which depicts the Empire enslaving Wookiees to harvest sap for military compounds.

Characteristics

Wookiee cities such as Kachirho are built within and atop the giant trees. In Star Wars lore the planet's ecosystems include dangerous predators on the lower levels, and the Wookiees' physical strength and climbing abilities are presented as adaptations to this arboreal environment. Under the Galactic Empire, Kashyyyk was blockaded and its population enslaved; after the Empire's fall it regained its freedom.
