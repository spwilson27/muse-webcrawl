InterContinental

InterContinental Hotels & Resorts
Founded: 4 April 1946 (by Juan Trippe / Pan Am)
Parent: IHG Hotels & Resorts
Segment: Luxury hotels

InterContinental Hotels & Resorts is a British-American luxury hotel brand, the flagship luxury marque of IHG Hotels & Resorts. Founded in 1946 by Pan American World Airways president Juan Trippe to house airline crews and passengers in Latin America, it became one of the first international hotel chains.

History

The first hotel opened in Belém, Brazil, in 1949. The brand expanded along Pan Am's routes through the Americas, Europe, the Middle East and Asia. Pan Am sold the chain in 1981; after ownership by Grand Metropolitan and Bass plc, it became part of InterContinental Hotels Group (now IHG) when the hotels business was separated in 2003.

Properties

InterContinental operates more than 200 hotels in some 70 countries, typically large city-centre, resort and landmark properties such as the InterContinental Paris Le Grand, the Mark Hopkins in San Francisco, and hotels in Hong Kong, London and Sydney. Its loyalty programme is IHG One Rewards, with the InterContinental Ambassador paid tier.
