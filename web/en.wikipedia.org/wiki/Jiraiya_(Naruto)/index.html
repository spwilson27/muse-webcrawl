Jiraiya (自来也) is a fictional character in the Naruto manga and anime series created by Masashi Kishimoto. Introduced as the eccentric mentor of the title character Naruto Uzumaki, Jiraiya is a former student of the Third Hokage and one of the three legendary Sannin of Konohagakure, alongside Tsunade and Orochimaru.

Conception and design

Kishimoto based the character on Jiraiya, the hero of the Japanese folktale Jiraiya Gōketsu Monogatari, a ninja who can turn into a toad. In the series Jiraiya is a master of toad-summoning techniques and sage mode, wears a forehead protector bearing the kanji for "oil" (油), and is depicted with long spiky white hair. Kishimoto has said the mentor–student relationship between Jiraiya and Naruto was central to the story's themes.

Personality and role

Jiraiya is presented as a flamboyant, self-deprecating pervert—author of the in-universe romance novels Make-Out Paradise—who masks a formidable intelligence network and deep loyalty to his village. He trained the Fourth Hokage, Minato Namikaze, and later Naruto, teaching him the Rasengan. Guided by a prophecy from the Great Toad Sage that he would train a revolutionary, Jiraiya also mentored Nagato and the Ame orphans.

Story

After investigating the Akatsuki leader Pain in Amegakure, Jiraiya is killed in battle by the Six Paths of Pain, but manages to send a coded message identifying Pain's secret back to Konoha—an act widely regarded as one of the series' most emotional arcs. His legacy drives Naruto's growth and his decision to confront Pain with dialogue rather than vengeance.

Reception

Jiraiya consistently ranks among the most popular characters in Naruto popularity polls, and his death is frequently cited by critics as one of the manga's defining moments.
