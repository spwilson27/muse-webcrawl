Flow Country

The Flow Country is a vast area of rolling peatland and wetland in Caithness and Sutherland in the far north of Scotland. It is one of the largest expanses of blanket bog in the world.

Ecology

The peat bogs, formed over thousands of years, store large quantities of carbon and support rare breeding birds including greenshank, dunlin, and golden plover. Parts are protected as the Forsinard Flows nature reserve and within designated sites.

History and recognition

In the 1980s large areas were drained and planted with non-native conifers under tax incentives, provoking a major conservation controversy; much planting has since been removed and bog restoration undertaken. In 2024 the Flow Country was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, the first peatland site so recognized.
