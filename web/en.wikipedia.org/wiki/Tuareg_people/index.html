Tuareg people

The Tuareg people are a large Berber ethnic group inhabiting the Sahara in a vast area stretching from far southwestern Libya to southern Algeria, Niger, Mali and Burkina Faso. Traditionally nomadic pastoralists, they are known for men's indigo veils, earning them the name blue people.

Society and culture

Tuareg society is traditionally matrilineal in many respects, with women holding high status; their language is written in the ancient Tifinagh script. Historically they controlled trans-Saharan caravan trade in salt and gold.

Modern history

Colonial borders split their homeland; Tuareg rebellions in Mali and Niger since the 1960s, including the 2012 conflict in northern Mali, have sought autonomy. See Sahara and Berbers.
