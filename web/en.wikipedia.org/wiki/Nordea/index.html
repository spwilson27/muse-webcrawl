Nordea

Nordea Bank Abp, commonly referred to as Nordea, is a European financial services group operating in Northern Europe, headquartered in Helsinki, Finland. It is the largest financial group in the Nordic region, with around 10 million customers, and is one of the few European banks designated a global systemically important bank by the Financial Stability Board.

History

Nordea was formed between 1997 and 2000 through the merger of Finland's Merita Bank, Sweden's Nordbanken, Denmark's Unibank and Norway's Christiania Bank og Kreditkasse — themselves the products of numerous earlier mergers among hundreds of local banks, some dating to the 1820s. The name "Nordea" combines "Nordic" and "ideas".

In 2017–2018 the group moved its legal domicile from Stockholm to Helsinki, within the European banking union, in part in response to Swedish proposals for higher bank resolution fees; the redomiciliation was effected by a cross-border merger in October 2018. Nordea has since focused on its four Nordic home markets, divesting earlier operations in Russia, the Baltic states and Poland.

Operations

The group serves personal and business customers, large corporates and institutions, and asset-management clients across Finland, Sweden, Norway and Denmark. It employs roughly 29,000 people and has total assets well over €500 billion. Nordea shares are listed on the Helsinki, Stockholm and Copenhagen stock exchanges. The bank has been the subject of money-laundering investigations and fines connected to the wider Nordic banking scandals of the late 2010s, after which it substantially expanded compliance functions.
