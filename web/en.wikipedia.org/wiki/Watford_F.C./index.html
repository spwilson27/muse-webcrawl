Watford Football Club is a professional football club based in Watford, Hertfordshire, England. The club competes in the EFL Championship, the second tier of English football.

Founded in 1881 as Watford Rovers, the club entered the Football League in 1920. Watford's most successful period came under manager Graham Taylor in the late 1970s and 1980s, when the club rose from the Fourth Division to finish second in the First Division in 1982–83 and reached the FA Cup final in 1984, losing to Everton. The club's colours are yellow, red and black, and its nickname, the Hornets, derives from those colours and the hornet on its crest.

History

After decades in the lower divisions, Elton John became chairman in 1976 and appointed Graham Taylor as manager the following year. Three promotions in five seasons took Watford into the top flight, where they finished runners-up to Liverpool and qualified for the UEFA Cup. The club reached a second FA Cup final in 2019, losing heavily to Manchester City. Watford has since alternated between the Premier League and the Championship.
