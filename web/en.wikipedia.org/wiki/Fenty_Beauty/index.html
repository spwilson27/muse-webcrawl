Fenty Beauty (stylised as FEИTY BEAUTY) is a cosmetics brand launched in September 2017 by the singer Rihanna, in partnership with LVMH's beauty incubator Kendo. It is widely credited with reshaping industry standards for shade inclusivity.

Launch and the "Fenty effect"

The brand debuted with a foundation range in 40 shades — later expanded to 50 — spanning very light to very deep skin tones that many competitors had neglected. The launch generated over 0 million in earned media value in its first month and prompted rival brands to widen their own shade ranges, a phenomenon dubbed the "Fenty effect". The Pro Filt'R foundation and the Gloss Bomb lip gloss became bestsellers, and the brand reported roughly 70 million in revenue in its first full year.

Expansion

Fenty Beauty sells in more than 30 countries through Sephora and other retailers. Rihanna followed it with the skincare line Fenty Skin in 2020 and Fenty Hair in 2024. The brand's success contributed substantially to Rihanna's status as a billionaire, with the bulk of her fortune attributed to her stake in Fenty Beauty rather than her music career.
