Insects are hexapod invertebrates of the class Insecta — the largest group of animals, with about a million described species and millions more suspected. Their body has three parts (head, thorax, abdomen), six legs, compound eyes and usually wings; most undergo metamorphosis.

Roles

Insects pollinate crops (bees), recycle waste, feed countless animals, and produce honey and silk, but also spread disease (mosquitoes) and eat crops (locusts). The bee queued in Wave 1 belongs here. Insect declines reported in recent decades worry ecologists because so much of the forest and farm web depends on them, including the pollination of flowers and coffee.
