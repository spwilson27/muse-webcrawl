South Mumbai

South Mumbai (SoBo)
City: Mumbai, Maharashtra, India
Comprises: Colaba, Fort, Malabar Hill, Worli and other southern districts
Known for: Historic core, business district, prime real estate

South Mumbai, popularly called SoBo, is the southernmost precinct of Mumbai, India, roughly corresponding to Mumbai City district. It is the historic and administrative heart of the metropolis and among the most expensive real-estate markets in India.

Areas and landmarks

South Mumbai includes Colaba, Fort, Churchgate, Marine Drive, Malabar Hill, Cumballa Hill, Tardeo, Worli, and Mahalaxmi. Landmarks include the Gateway of India, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus, the Bombay High Court, the University of Mumbai, Bandra–Worli Sea Link's southern end, and the Hanging Gardens. Nariman Point and Ballard Estate form major business districts.

History

The area developed on the original seven islands of Bombay, joined by reclamation under British rule. It housed the port, the cotton trade, and the colonial administration, and its Victorian Gothic and Art Deco building ensembles are inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage list.
