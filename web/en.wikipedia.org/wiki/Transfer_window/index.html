Transfer window

A transfer window is the period during the year in which association football clubs can transfer players from other clubs into their playing staff. Such a transfer is completed by registering the player into the new club through FIFA's registration system. Outside a transfer window, players generally cannot be registered, with limited exceptions such as free agents in some leagues.

Structure

Most European leagues operate two windows per season: a long pre-season (summer) window, typically lasting up to 12 weeks, and a shorter mid-season (winter) window in January, usually lasting up to four weeks. The exact dates are set by each national association within FIFA limits, and the closing day is widely known as "deadline day", accompanied by intense media coverage.

History

The current system was introduced for the 2002–03 season across European football, following negotiations between FIFA and the European Commission over the compatibility of the transfer system with EU competition law and the Bosman ruling.
