Kingsley Amis

Sir Kingsley William Amis (16 April 1922 - 22 October 1995) was an English novelist, poet, critic and teacher. He is best known for the comic novel Lucky Jim (1954), which launched his career and associated him with the Angry Young Men.

Amis wrote more than twenty novels, including That Uncertain Feeling, The Old Devils (winner of the Booker Prize in 1986), science fiction, a James Bond novel Colonel Sun as Robert Markham, and studies of drinking and language such as On Drink. He taught at Swansea and Cambridge. His politics moved from left to conservative. He was the father of novelist Martin Amis.
