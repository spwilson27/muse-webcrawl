MG 08

The Maschinengewehr 08, or MG 08, was the German Army's standard machine gun in the First World War. It was an adaptation of Hiram Maxim's 1884 Maxim gun design, manufactured by the Deutsche Waffen und Munitionsfabriken (DWM).

MG 08
Type: Heavy machine gun
Origin: German Empire
Designer: Based on Hiram Maxim's design
Cartridge: 7.92×57mm Mauser
In service: 1908–1945

Design

The MG 08 is a water-cooled, recoil-operated weapon fed from fabric or metal belts, typically mounted on a heavy sled mount (Schlittenlafette). A lighter variant, the MG 08/15, was introduced to give infantry units more mobile automatic fire.

Service

Deployed in enormous numbers during the First World War, the MG 08 was central to the defensive firepower of entrenched positions. It remained in limited German and foreign service through the Second World War.
