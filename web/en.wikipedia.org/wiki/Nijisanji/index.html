Nijisanji (Japanese: にじさんじ) is a virtual YouTuber (VTuber) agency owned by the Japanese company Anycolor Inc. (formerly Ichikara Inc.), launched in 2018 and home to a large roster of "Liver" streamers in Japan and overseas.

Nijisanji

Nijisanji (stylised NIJISANJI) is a Japanese virtual YouTuber agency operated by Anycolor Inc. Founded in early 2018, it was one of the first agencies built around livestreaming virtual performers, using the company's iPhone face-tracking application to let performers animate 2D avatars in real time.

History

Nijisanji's first generation of eight "official Livers" debuted in February 2018, led in popularity by Tsukino Mito, who became a breakout star of the early VTuber boom. The roster expanded rapidly to well over a hundred Japanese talents, alongside affiliated projects including VirtuaReal in China (with Bilibili) and Nijisanji EN, the English-language branch launched in 2021, whose groups LazuLight, OBSYDIA, Ethyria, Luxiem, Noctyx and Iluna attracted a large international audience. Anycolor listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange in 2022.

Activities

Nijisanji talents stream games, music, talk shows and events on YouTube and other platforms, release songs through agency labels, stage arena concerts, and sell merchandise. The agency is known for its relatively permissive, improvisation-friendly style compared with idol-model rivals such as Hololive. It has also faced public controversies, notably the 2024 termination of Nijisanji EN member Selen Tatsuki, which prompted discussion across the VTuber industry about talent management and contract terms.
