Skip Beat! (Japanese: スキップ・ビート!, Sukippu Bīto!) is a Japanese shōjo manga by Yoshiki Nakamura, serialized in Hakusensha's Hana to Yume magazine since 2002. It is one of the longest-running series in the magazine, with the collected volumes numbering in the dozens.

Plot

Kyoko Mogami, a sixteen-year-old girl, devotes herself to supporting her childhood friend and first love, Sho Fuwa, working multiple jobs in Tokyo so he can pursue his career as a pop idol. When she overhears Sho admit that he only keeps her around as a maid, her innocence shatters and she vows revenge: she will enter show business herself and surpass him. Joining the LME talent agency, Kyoko is placed in the unconventional "Love Me" section, where she takes on odd jobs within the industry while discovering a genuine talent — and passion — for acting. She crosses paths with Ren Tsuruga, Japan's top actor, who initially disapproves of her revenge motive but gradually recognizes her ability.

Media

The manga has been adapted into a 25-episode anime television series produced by Hal Film Maker, aired from 2008 to 2009, and a Taiwanese television drama, Extravagant Challenge (2011–2012), starring Ivy Chen, Choi Siwon, and Lee Donghae. A drama CD series has also been released. The manga is licensed in English by Viz Media under its Shojo Beat imprint.

Reception

The series is praised for its slow-burn character development, its detailed depiction of the acting craft, and Kyoko's transformation from a naive girl into a driven professional. It remains one of Hakusensha's best-known long-running shōjo titles.
