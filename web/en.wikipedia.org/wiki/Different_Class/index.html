Different Class

Image: Different Class album cover

Different Class is the fifth studio album by English rock band Pulp, released on 30 October 1995 by Island Records. Recorded with producer Chris Thomas, it is the band's most successful album, reaching number one in the United Kingdom and winning the 1996 Mercury Prize. It is regarded as one of the defining albums of the Britpop era.

Background and themes

Frontman Jarvis Cocker's lyrics chronicle working-class life, class resentment, sex, and voyeurism in 1990s Britain. The album's best-known song, "Common People", was inspired by a Greek art student Cocker met at Central Saint Martins who said she wanted to "live like common people".

Reception

The singles "Common People" and "Disco 2000" reached numbers two and seven respectively on the UK Singles Chart. The album was certified four times platinum in the UK and appears on many critics' lists of the greatest albums of all time. The sleeve, featuring life-size cardboard cutouts of the band members, was designed so fans could substitute their own photograph.
