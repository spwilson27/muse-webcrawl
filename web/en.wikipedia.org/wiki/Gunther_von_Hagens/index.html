Gunther von Hagens

Gunther von Hagens (born 10 January 1945 as Gunther Gerhard Liebchen) is a German anatomist and inventor of plastination, a technique for preserving biological tissue specimens by replacing water and lipids with polymers. In 1977, while working at the University of Heidelberg, he developed the process, and in 1993 he founded the Institute for Plastination.

His Body Worlds exhibitions of plastinated human bodies, first shown in Japan in 1995, have toured worldwide and been seen by tens of millions of visitors, while also attracting ethical controversy and criticism from religious groups and some anatomists. Von Hagens is frequently seen in public wearing his trademark black fedora. In 2011 he revealed that he suffers from Parkinson's disease.
