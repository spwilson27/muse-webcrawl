Grocery store

A grocery store (or grocer's shop) is a retail store that primarily sells food and household provisions, including fresh produce, meat, dairy, bakery goods and packaged foods.

History

Early grocers sold dry bulk goods; self-service was pioneered by Piggly Wiggly in Memphis in 1916, leading to supermarkets. Modern formats range from convenience stores to hypermarkets and discount chains.

Operations

Grocers manage perishable supply chains, private-label brands, loyalty programmes and increasingly online ordering, pickup and delivery. Independent grocers, cooperatives and ethnic markets remain important in many communities.
