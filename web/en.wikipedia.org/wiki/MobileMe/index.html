MobileMe

SuccessoriCloud

MobileMe was a subscription-based collection of online services and software offered by Apple Inc., providing push email, contacts and calendar synchronisation, online storage (iDisk), photo galleries and the Find My iPhone service across Mac, Windows, iPhone and iPod Touch. Accounts used an @me.com email address (earlier .Mac accounts used @mac.com).

History

MobileMe succeeded Apple's earlier iTools (2000) and .Mac (2002) services. It launched in July 2008 alongside the iPhone 3G, but the rollout was marred by outages, sync failures and lost mail, prompting Apple to apologise and extend subscriptions; Steve Jobs later famously described it internally as a failure that damaged Apple's reputation. MobileMe was replaced by the free iCloud in October 2011, and the service shut down on 30 June 2012.

Features

Services included synchronised mail, contacts and calendars via push, 20 GB of iDisk storage, web albums, Back to My Mac remote access, and web applications at me.com mimicking the desktop Mail, Contacts and Calendar apps.
