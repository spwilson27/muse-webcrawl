List of rivers of Africa

This is a list of the principal rivers of Africa, organised by the ocean or sea into which they drain. Africa's drainage is dominated by a handful of great river systems, among them the Nile — generally regarded as the longest river in the world — and the Congo, which carries the second-largest discharge of any river on Earth.

Draining into the Mediterranean Sea

  • Nile — about 6,650 km; formed by the White Nile and Blue Nile, meeting at Khartoum; flows through eleven African countries to a delta in Egypt
  • Chelif River (Algeria)
  • Medjerda (Algeria, Tunisia)
  • Moulouya (Morocco)

Draining into the Atlantic Ocean

  • Congo River — about 4,700 km; second only to the Amazon in discharge; major tributaries include the Ubangi, Kasai, Sangha and Lualaba
  • Niger River — about 4,180 km; arcs from Guinea through Mali and Niger to Nigeria, ending in the Niger Delta
  • Senegal River
  • Gambia River
  • Volta (Ghana; largely impounded as Lake Volta)
  • Orange River (South Africa, Namibia, Botswana)
  • Cunene (Kunene)
  • Cross River
  • Ogooué (Gabon)

Draining into the Indian Ocean

  • Zambezi — about 2,574 km; passes over Victoria Falls; tributaries include the Kafue and Luangwa
  • Limpopo
  • Rovuma (Ruvuma)
  • Rufiji (Tanzania)
  • Juba and Shebelle (Horn of Africa)
  • Save River
  • Tana (Kenya)

Endorheic (inland) basins

  • Chari and Logone — drain into Lake Chad
  • Okavango — ends in the Okavango Delta of Botswana, never reaching the sea
  • Awash — ends in Lake Abe on the Ethiopia–Djibouti border
