MusicPrince and the Revolution

Purple Rain is a 1984 American rock musical drama film directed by Albert Magnoli and starring Prince in his film debut as "The Kid", a young Minneapolis musician fronting the band The Revolution, struggling with a rival band led by Morris Day, a troubled home life and a new romance with singer Apollonia.

Music and reception

The accompanying soundtrack album by Prince and the Revolution — including "When Doves Cry", "Let's Go Crazy" and the title track — topped the charts and won an Academy Award for Best Original Song Score. Made on a modest budget, the film was a major box-office hit and is credited with establishing Prince as a superstar; it was selected for the US National Film Registry in 2019.
