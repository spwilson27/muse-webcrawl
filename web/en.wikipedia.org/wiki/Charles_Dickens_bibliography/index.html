Charles Dickens bibliography

Charles Dickens (1812–1870) was an English novelist and social critic who wrote across four decades. His bibliography comprises fifteen novels (one unfinished), numerous novellas — most famously A Christmas Carol — hundreds of short stories and essays, journalism, edited weekly magazines, plays, poetry, and voluminous correspondence. Many of his works first appeared in serial form, in weekly or monthly parts, a mode of publication he did much to popularise.

Short fiction and sketches

His earliest published fiction was collected as Sketches by Boz (1836), written under the pen name "Boz". Later collections include The Mudfog Papers, Reprinted Pieces, and The Uncommercial Traveller, much of it first printed in the journals he edited, Household Words (1850–59) and All the Year Round (1859–70).

Non-fiction

  • American Notes (1842) — travel account of North America
  • Pictures from Italy (1846)
  • A Child's History of England (1851–53)
  • The Life of Our Lord (written 1846–49; published 1934)

Letters

Dickens was a prolific correspondent. The standard scholarly edition, the Pilgrim Edition of his letters (Oxford), runs to twelve volumes and collects more than 14,000 letters.
