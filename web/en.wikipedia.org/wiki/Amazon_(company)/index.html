Amazon.com, Inc. is an American multinational technology company focused on e-commerce, cloud computing (Amazon Web Services), online advertising, digital streaming and artificial intelligence. Founded by Jeff Bezos in 1994 as an online bookstore, it became the largest online retailer in many markets and one of the world's most valuable companies.

Amazon's businesses include the Prime subscription, Kindle e-readers, Alexa/Echo devices, Prime Video, the Whole Foods grocery chain, and AWS, which provides much of its profit. It has been criticised over warehouse working conditions, market power, tax and its effects on retail.
