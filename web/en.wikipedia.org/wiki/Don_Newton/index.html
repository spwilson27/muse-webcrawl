Don Newton (4 November 1952 – 1984) was an American comic book artist whose richly painted, atmospheric style graced DC Comics' Batman titles in the late 1970s and early 1980s. A devoted fan-turned-professional, he is remembered as one of the definitive Batman artists of his era despite a career cut short by his early death.

Career

Newton participated in comics fandom and contributed to Charlton Comics, drawing The Phantom in the mid-1970s. At DC he drew Captain Marvel in Shazam! and then became a regular artist on Batman and Detective Comics, often inked by Dan Adkins and Alfredo Alcala. His tenure included the introduction of characters and moody Gothic renderings of Gotham City praised by readers and fellow artists. He also worked on the New Teen Titans and drew for Marvel's magazines.

Death and legacy

Newton died of a heart attack in 1984, aged 33, shortly after being diagnosed with throat cancer. His Batman work has been repeatedly collected and is frequently cited by later artists as an influence.
