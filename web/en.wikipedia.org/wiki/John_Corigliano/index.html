John Paul Corigliano Jr. (born February 16, 1938) is an American composer of orchestral, chamber, opera, and film music. One of the most widely performed living classical composers, he has won the Pulitzer Prize, the Grawemeyer Award, five Grammy Awards, and an Academy Award, and is known for a distinctive style that fuses elements from various musical traditions, both historical and contemporary.

Early life and education

Corigliano was born in New York City into a musical family: his father, John Corigliano Sr., was concertmaster of the New York Philharmonic for 23 years, and his mother, Rose Buzen, was an accomplished pianist. He studied composition at Columbia University and at the Manhattan School of Music, with teachers including Otto Luening, Vittorio Giannini, and Paul Creston. Before establishing himself as a composer, he worked in music production, programming concerts for Leonard Bernstein's Young People's Concerts.

Career and major works

Corigliano's breakthrough came with his Clarinet Concerto (1977), written for Stanley Drucker and the New York Philharmonic. His Symphony No. 1 (1988–89), a response to the AIDS epidemic inspired in part by the NAMES Project AIDS Memorial Quilt, won the Grawemeyer Award. His opera The Ghosts of Versailles (1991), commissioned by the Metropolitan Opera, became a major success and revived the "grand opera" tradition. In 2001 he won the Pulitzer Prize for Music for his Symphony No. 2 for string orchestra. More recent works include the song-cycle opera The Lord of Cries (2021).

Film music

Corigliano scored the Ken Russell film Altered States (1980), earning an Academy Award nomination, and Hugh Hudson's Revolution (1985). His score for François Girard's The Red Violin (1998), featuring a virtuoso chaconne for solo violin, won the Academy Award for Best Original Score.

Teaching and honors

Corigliano has taught at Lehman College of the City University of New York and at the Juilliard School. He is a member of the American Academy of Arts and Letters. His husband, Mark Adamo, is himself a composer and librettist.
