The president of Mexico (Spanish: Presidente de México), officially the President of the United Mexican States (Presidente de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos), is the head of state and head of government of Mexico. Under the Constitution of 1917, the president is the chief of the executive branch of the federal government and is the commander in chief of the Mexican Armed Forces.

The president is elected by direct popular vote for a single six-year term (the sexenio); re-election is constitutionally prohibited — "No re-election" (Sufragio efectivo, no reelección) was a rallying cry of the Mexican Revolution. Since 2024 the presidential term begins on 1 October of the election year (previously 1 December).

History

The first president, Guadalupe Victoria, took office in 1824 after the fall of Agustín de Iturbide's brief empire. The 19th century saw chronic instability, with the presidency changing hands dozens of times; Antonio López de Santa Anna held it on numerous occasions, and Benito Juárez led the liberal government through the Reform War and the French intervention. The dictatorship of Porfirio Díaz (1876–1911) ended with the Revolution, and the 1917 Constitution institutionalised the strong, single-term presidency. The Institutional Revolutionary Party (PRI) held the presidency continuously from 1929 to 2000, an era of dominant-party rule sometimes called the "perfect dictatorship". Vicente Fox of the PAN won the 2000 election, ending 71 years of PRI rule; power has since alternated among parties.

Current office holder

Claudia Sheinbaum, a climate scientist and former head of government of Mexico City, won the June 2024 general election as the candidate of the Morena-led coalition and took office on 1 October 2024 as Mexico's first woman president.

Powers

The president appoints and removes cabinet secretaries, commands the armed forces, conducts foreign policy, promulgates and executes laws, may veto legislation, and presents the federal budget. Inauguration day is a national holiday, and the president delivers an annual report (informe de gobierno) to Congress.
