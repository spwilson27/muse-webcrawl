Alex Paterson (born 15 October 1959) is an English electronic musician, DJ and co-founder of the ambient house group The Orb. Born in Battersea, London, he worked as a roadie for the post-punk band Killing Joke in the early 1980s and later as an A&R man, before DJing chill-out sets at Paul Oakenfold's Land of Oz nights at the London club Heaven.

The Orb

Paterson formed The Orb with Jimmy Cauty in 1988. After Cauty's departure, Paterson continued the project with a rotating cast of collaborators, most notably Thomas Fehlmann. The Orb became known for drifting, sample-heavy ambient house, epitomised by the 1989 single "A Huge Ever Growing Pulsating Brain That Rules from the Centre of the Ultraworld" and the albums The Orb's Adventures Beyond the Ultraworld (1991) and U.F.Orb (1992), which reached number one in the UK.

Later work

Paterson has continued to record and tour with The Orb for over three decades, collaborating with artists including David Gilmour and Lee "Scratch" Perry, and remains a prominent figure in ambient and dub-influenced electronic music.
