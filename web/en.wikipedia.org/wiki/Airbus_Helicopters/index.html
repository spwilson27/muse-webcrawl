Airbus Helicopters SAS, formerly Eurocopter Group, is the helicopter manufacturing division of Airbus. Formed in 1992 from the merger of the helicopter divisions of Aérospatiale of France and Deutsche Aerospace of Germany, it is the world's largest helicopter manufacturer by revenue, with headquarters at Marignane, France, near Marseille Provence Airport.

Products

Civil and military models include the H120, H125 Écureuil, H130, H135, H145, H155, H160, H175, H215 and H225 Super Puma families, the NH90 (through NHIndustries), the Tiger attack helicopter, and the VSR700 unmanned system. The company adopted the Airbus Helicopters name on 1 January 2014 as part of the rebranding of its parent group.

Facilities

Major sites are at Marignane and Paris-Le Bourget in France, Donauwörth and Kassel in Germany, Albacete in Spain, and Grand Prairie, Texas and Columbus, Mississippi in the United States.
