Dallas (TV series)

Genre: Prime time soap opera
Created by: David Jacobs
Starring: Larry Hagman, Patrick Duffy, Linda Gray, Barbara Bel Geddes
Network: CBS
Original run: 1978–1991; 357 episodes

Dallas is an American prime time television soap opera that aired on CBS from April 2, 1978, to May 3, 1991. The series revolves around the wealthy, feuding Ewing family of Dallas, Texas, whose fortunes rest on oil and cattle ranching, centring on the scheming oil baron J. R. Ewing, played by Larry Hagman, one of television's most famous villains.

"Who shot J.R.?"

The 1980 cliffhanger in which J.R. was shot became a global cultural phenomenon; the episode revealing the shooter, "Who Done It", was one of the most-watched broadcasts in American television history at the time. The show's later "dream season" — in which an entire season was retroactively explained as a character's dream after Patrick Duffy's return — remains a famous and controversial plot device.

Legacy

Dallas spawned the successful spin-off Knots Landing and was revived on TNT from 2012 to 2014 with original cast members alongside a new generation of Ewings. Southfork Ranch, the exterior location near Parker, Texas, remains a tourist attraction.
