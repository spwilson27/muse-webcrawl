Sébastien Loeb (born 26 February 1974) is a French professional rally, racing and rallycross driver. He is the most successful driver in the history of the World Rally Championship (WRC), winning the world title a record nine consecutive times from 2004 to 2012, all with Citroën and long-time co-driver Daniel Elena. He holds the records for most WRC event wins (80), most podium finishes, and most points scored.

Early career

Loeb was originally a gymnast, winning regional titles, before turning to rallying in his early twenties. Working as an electrician, he progressed through French national series and won the French Citroën Saxo Kit Car Cup in 1999. He won the inaugural Junior World Rally Championship in 2001 with a perfect record, and made his mark with Citroën by nearly winning the 2001 San Remo Rally on a guest outing in a World Rally Car.

WRC dominance

Loeb became a full-time Citroën driver in 2002 and won his first world rally in Germany that year. After narrowly losing the 2003 title to Petter Solberg, he won nine straight championships from 2004 to 2012, first in the Xsara WRC, then the C4 WRC and DS3 WRC. Remarkably, he won the 2006 title despite missing the final rounds with a broken shoulder from a mountain-biking accident. His single-season record of ten wins came in 2005, and he was renowned for his speed on asphalt, winning every Monte Carlo Rally from 2004 onward for several years.

Later career

After semi-retiring from full-time WRC competition at the end of 2012, Loeb won events on part-time returns with Citroën and Hyundai, most recently the 2022 Monte Carlo Rally at age 47 — making him the oldest winner of a WRC event. He switched to circuit racing with Citroën in the World Touring Car Championship (2014–2015), finished on the podium at the Dakar Rally several times, set a record time at the Pikes Peak International Hill Climb in 2013 (8:13.878), and competed in the World Rally-Raid Championship and Extreme E. He also holds the record for most consecutive wins at a single WRC event, nine at Rally Deutschland.

Legacy

Loeb is widely regarded as the greatest rally driver of all time. He was made a knight of the Legion of Honour in France, and his nine consecutive world titles across a major FIA championship remain unmatched in rallying.
