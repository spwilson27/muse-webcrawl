Television in Greece

Television in Greece began experimentally in the 1960s, with regular broadcasts starting in 1966 under the National Radio Foundation (EIR). The public broadcaster ERT long dominated; private channels such as Mega Channel and ANT1 launched in 1989, ending the state monopoly.

History

Television was introduced under the military junta, and early programming included the armed forces channel YENED. After democratisation, ERT was reorganised; colour broadcasts (SECAM) arrived around 1979. The 1989 liberalisation produced a crowded private market. In June 2013 the government abruptly shut down ERT, sparking protests; it was replaced by NERIT and ERT itself was relaunched in 2015.

Channels and viewing

Major national channels include ERT1, ERT2, ANT1, Mega, Alpha, Star and Skai. Greece completed digital switchover in the 2010s, with Digea operating the private multiplexes.
