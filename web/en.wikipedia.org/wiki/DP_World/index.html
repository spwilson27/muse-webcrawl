DP World

DP World is an Emirati multinational logistics company based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. It specialises in cargo logistics, port terminal operations, maritime services and free trade zones. Formed in 2005 by the merger of Dubai Ports Authority and Dubai Ports International, DP World handles 70 million containers that are brought in by around 70,000 vessels annually. This equates to roughly 10% of global container traffic taken by their 82 marine and inland terminals in more than 40 countries.

The company is majority-owned by Dubai World, the investment company of the Government of Dubai, and operates flagship terminals at Jebel Ali Port in Dubai, the largest port in the Middle East.

History

Dubai Ports International was founded in 1999 to develop ports abroad, beginning with projects in Jeddah and Djibouti. In 2005 it merged with the Dubai Ports Authority to form DP World. In 2006 DP World acquired the British P&O ports and ferries group for £3.9 billion; the resulting review of P&O's American port operations by the U.S. Committee on Foreign Investment led DP World to sell those U.S. terminals in 2007.

Operations

DP World runs container terminals across Europe (including London Gateway in the UK, Antwerp and Rotterdam), the Americas, Africa, Asia and Australia, and has expanded into end-to-end logistics, freight forwarding (through acquisitions such as Imperial Logistics and Syncreon), economic zones, and maritime services (through Unifeeder and P&O Ferries).

Sponsorship

The company is the title sponsor of golf's DP World Tour (formerly the European Tour) and has sponsored cricket and sailing events.

Criticism

DP World has faced criticism and legal disputes concerning labour practices and port concessions in several countries, including a long-running dispute with Djibouti over the Doraleh container terminal, which the government seized in 2018.
