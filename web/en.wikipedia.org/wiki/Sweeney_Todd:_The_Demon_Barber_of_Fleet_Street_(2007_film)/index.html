Sweeney Todd: The Demon Barber of Fleet Street is a 2007 musical slasher film directed by Tim Burton from a screenplay by John Logan. It is an adaptation of Stephen Sondheim and Hugh Wheeler's 1979 Tony Award–winning stage musical of the same name, itself based on the Victorian penny dreadful tale of Sweeney Todd. The film stars Johnny Depp as the title character, a barber who murders his customers so that his accomplice, Mrs. Lovett (Helena Bonham Carter), can bake their remains into meat pies.

Plot

Benjamin Barker, a skilled London barber, was falsely imprisoned and transported to Australia by the corrupt Judge Turpin, who lusted after Barker's wife Lucy. Fifteen years later Barker escapes and returns to London under the name Sweeney Todd, aided by the young sailor Anthony Hope. He resumes his trade in Fleet Street above the failing pie shop of Mrs. Lovett, who tells him Lucy poisoned herself and that his daughter Johanna is now Turpin's ward. Vowing revenge, Todd slits the throat of the blackmailing rival barber Pirelli and, when Turpin escapes his chair, descends into indiscriminate murder, with Lovett disposing of the bodies in her pies — to great commercial success. Anthony plans to rescue Johanna, whom Turpin has confined to an asylum after she refuses his advances. In the climax Todd kills Turpin and a beggar woman, only to discover the beggar was Lucy, alive but deranged; learning that Lovett concealed this, he throws her into her own oven, then cradles Lucy's body as Toby, Lovett's young assistant, kills him with his own razor.

Production and reception

Burton, a longtime admirer of the musical, directed Depp and Bonham Carter in their own singing performances despite their lack of formal vocal training. The film's muted, desaturated gothic visual design contrasts with vivid red blood in its stylised violence. It premiered in December 2007 and grossed over $153 million worldwide. Critics praised its visuals, performances and fidelity to Sondheim's score, though some stage purists noted cuts to the score. The film won the Golden Globe Award for Best Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy, Depp won the Golden Globe for Best Actor and received an Academy Award nomination, and the film won the Academy Award for Best Art Direction.
