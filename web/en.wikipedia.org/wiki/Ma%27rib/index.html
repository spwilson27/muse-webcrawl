Ma'rib

Ma'rib (Arabic: مأرب) is a city in Yemen and the capital of Ma'rib Governorate. It was the capital of the ancient kingdom of Saba, famous for the Ma'rib Dam, one of the great engineering works of the ancient world.

Ancient history

Ancient Ma'rib prospered on the incense trade between southern Arabia and the Mediterranean. The great dam, parts of which date to the 8th century BCE, irrigated extensive farmland around the oasis; its final breach in the 6th century CE is remembered in the Qur'an as the flood of the dam.

Modern city

The modern town lies a few kilometres from the ancient ruins. Since 2015, during the Yemeni civil war, Ma'rib has been a stronghold of the internationally recognised government and has absorbed large numbers of internally displaced people, while nearby oil and gas facilities make it strategically important.
