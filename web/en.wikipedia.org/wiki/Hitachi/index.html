Hitachi, Ltd. is a Japanese multinational conglomerate founded in 1910 by Namihei Odaira as an electrical repair shop within a copper mine. It grew into one of Japan's largest industrial groups, known for products ranging from trains and power systems to IT services.

Business areas

Modern Hitachi focuses on its "Social Innovation Business," combining operational technology with information technology and its Lumada digital platform. Businesses include energy systems, railway systems (including trains built in the UK and Italy), construction machinery (spun off as Hitachi Construction Machinery), and digital services through its subsidiary GlobalLogic.

Restructuring

After posting one of the largest losses in Japanese corporate history in 2009, Hitachi restructured, selling or listing many subsidiaries such as Hitachi Metals, Hitachi Chemical, and its stake in Hitachi Kokusai, while acquiring ABB's power grids business in 2020.

Name

The company takes its name from the city of Hitachi in Ibaraki Prefecture, where it was founded. See also Keiretsu.
