Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam

The Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam (VU Amsterdam) is a public research university in Amsterdam, Netherlands, founded in 1880 by a group led by the statesman and theologian Abraham Kuyper. Its name means "Free University", referring to its founding independence from both church and state.

History

Kuyper, a leader of the neo-Calvinist movement and later Prime Minister of the Netherlands, founded the university with just five professors and a handful of students. It began in rented rooms in the Scottish Mission Church before moving to its grand building on the Keizersgracht. Since the 1960s the university has been located on a compact campus in the Buitenveldert district of Amsterdam-Zuid, near the Zuidas business district.

Organisation

VU Amsterdam comprises faculties including the School of Business and Economics, the Faculty of Science, the Faculty of Humanities, and the Amsterdam UMC medical faculty (shared with the University of Amsterdam). It enrols roughly 30,000 students and is a member of the Aurora university network.
