Opera North

Opera North is an English opera company based in Leeds, West Yorkshire. It was founded in 1978 as a northern offshoot of English National Opera, becoming independent in 1981. Its home is the Leeds Grand Theatre, where it performs its main season, and it also tours to theatres across northern England and beyond.

The company is known for a wide repertoire ranging from standard operas by Mozart, Verdi, Puccini and Wagner to contemporary works and neglected pieces. It has commissioned new operas and staged British premieres, and maintains an orchestra, the Orchestra of Opera North, which also gives concerts in its own right.

Opera North also runs extensive education and community programmes, including youth ensembles and projects in schools. Notable music directors have included David Lloyd-Jones, its founding music director, Richard Farnes and Garry Walker.
