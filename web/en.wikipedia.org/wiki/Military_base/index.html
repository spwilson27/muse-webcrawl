Military base

A military base is a facility directly owned and operated by or for a military or one of its branches that shelters military equipment and personnel, and facilitates training and operations.

Functions

Bases vary from small outposts to large installations resembling towns, with barracks, mess facilities, headquarters, depots, ranges, hospitals, and housing for service families. Naval bases support fleets, air bases host squadrons and runways, and training bases provide instruction and exercises. Bases also serve logistical roles: storing munitions and supplies, maintaining vehicles and aircraft, and projecting force overseas.

Overseas bases

Many states maintain bases abroad under status-of-forces agreements with host nations. Such bases can be politically contentious, raising questions of sovereignty, cost, and local impact, while host governments may value the security guarantee and economic benefits they bring.
