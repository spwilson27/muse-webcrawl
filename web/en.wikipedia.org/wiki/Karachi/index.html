Karachi is the largest city in Pakistan and the capital of Sindh province, a sprawling port metropolis on the Arabian Sea coast. It was Pakistan's first capital after independence in 1947 and remains the country's financial and industrial centre, home to its main stock exchange, central bank and busiest seaport.

History and character

A small fishing and trading town under British rule, Karachi grew with its harbour and railway links, and swelled after Partition with refugees from India. Today it is a megacity of well over ten million people (estimates vary), overwhelmingly Urdu-speaking but highly diverse, known for areas such as Saddar, Clifton and the mausoleum of Pakistan's founder, Muhammad Ali Jinnah.
