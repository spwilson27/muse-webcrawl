Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf

Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI; "Pakistan Movement for Justice") is a political party in Pakistan founded in 1996 by former cricketer Imran Khan. It positions itself as a centrist, populist, and anti-corruption party advocating justice, welfare, and an Islamic democratic state.

History

After years as a minor party, PTI became the second-largest party by votes in the 2013 general election and formed the government in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. It won the 2018 general election, and Imran Khan served as prime minister from 2018 until his removal by a no-confidence vote in April 2022.

Recent years

Following Khan's arrest and the events of 2023, the party faced a crackdown and the loss of its "bat" election symbol; PTI-backed independent candidates won the largest number of National Assembly seats in the 2024 election.
