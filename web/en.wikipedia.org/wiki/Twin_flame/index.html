Twin flame

A twin flame is a concept in New Age spirituality describing an intense soul connection between two people, believed to be the two halves of a single soul that was split and incarnated in separate bodies. Proponents distinguish twin flames from soulmates: whereas a person may have many soulmates, they have only one twin flame, and the relationship is said to serve as a catalyst for spiritual growth and self-discovery.

Beliefs

Popular accounts describe stages including recognition, intense attraction, separation (often framed as a "runner and chaser" dynamic) and eventual reunion. The concept draws loosely on the myth in Plato's Symposium of humans originally having four arms and four legs before being split by Zeus. Critics and psychologists caution that the belief can be used to rationalise obsessive or abusive relationships, and the concept gained renewed attention through documentaries about the Twin Flames Universe group in 2023.
