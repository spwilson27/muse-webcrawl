Drain-waste-vent system

In modern plumbing, a drain-waste-vent system (DWV) is the part of a plumbing system that carries sewage and greywater away from a building and vents sewer gases to the outside, while maintaining proper atmospheric pressure in the pipes so that traps do not siphon dry.

Components

The drain and waste pipes carry used water and waste by gravity to a sewer or septic system. Every fixture is protected by a trap — a U-shaped bend that retains water as a seal against sewer gas. Vent pipes connect the drainage system to the outdoors, usually through the roof, equalising pressure and allowing gases to escape.

Regulation

DWV systems are governed by plumbing codes such as the International Plumbing Code and the Uniform Plumbing Code, which specify pipe sizes, slopes, vent distances, and trap requirements.
