The World Athletics Continental Tour is an annual series of independent track and field meetings organised by World Athletics. Introduced in 2020, the tour sits one level below the Diamond League in the global one-day meeting circuit and provides athletes with opportunities to earn world ranking points and prize money outside the Diamond League programme.

Background

The Continental Tour replaced the former IAAF World Challenge series as part of a restructuring of the international athletics calendar announced in 2019. World Athletics sought to create a clearer pathway of competitions, with meetings graded according to the quality of their fields, facilities, prize money and broadcast coverage.

Structure

Meetings on the tour are divided into tiers:

  • Gold – the highest level of Continental Tour meetings, featuring strong international fields and significant prize money.
  • Silver – meetings with a mix of international and regional athletes.
  • Bronze – meetings aimed primarily at regional and developing athletes.
  • Challenger – entry-level meets within the World Athletics ranking system.

Results achieved at Continental Tour events count towards the World Athletics Rankings, which determine qualification for major championships such as the World Athletics Championships and the Olympic Games.

Notable meetings

Well-known Gold-level meetings on the tour have included the Kip Keino Classic in Nairobi, the Paavo Nurmi Games in Turku, the Gyulai István Memorial in Hungary, the Meeting Madrid, and events in Tokyo, Kingston and Gaborone. The tour spans all six continental areas of World Athletics.
