List of federal agencies in the United States

Federal agencies of the United States government are organised within the executive departments, as independent agencies, and within the legislative and judicial branches. The executive branch alone employs over two million civilian workers.

Independent agencies

Major independent agencies include the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), the National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA), the Central Intelligence Agency (CIA), the Federal Reserve System, the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), the Federal Communications Commission (FCC), the Federal Trade Commission (FTC), the National Labor Relations Board, the Social Security Administration, the U.S. Postal Service, and the Small Business Administration.
