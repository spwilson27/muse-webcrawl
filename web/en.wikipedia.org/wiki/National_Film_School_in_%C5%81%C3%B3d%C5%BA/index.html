National Film School in Łódź

Founded: 1948
Location: Łódź, Poland
Type: Public film school
Full name: Polish National Film, Television and Theatre School

The National Film School in Łódź, formally the Leon Schiller Polish National Film, Television and Theatre School, is one of the world's most renowned film schools, located in Łódź, Poland.

History

Founded in 1948, the school became the training ground of the Polish Film School movement of the 1950s and 1960s. Its graduates include Andrzej Wajda, Roman Polanski, Jerzy Skolimowski, Krzysztof Kieślowski and Krzysztof Zanussi, as well as cinematographers such as Sławomir Idziak and Paweł Edelman.

Programmes

The school offers degrees in directing, cinematography, acting, production, screenwriting, photography and animation. Students make numerous short films during their studies, and student works regularly win prizes at international festivals.
