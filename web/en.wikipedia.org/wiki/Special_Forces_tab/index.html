Special Forces tab

The Special Forces tab is a service school tab of the United States Army awarded to soldiers who complete the Special Forces Qualification Course (SFQC), the formal training program for the United States Army Special Forces (the "Green Berets"). It is worn above the unit patch on the left shoulder of the Army uniform, and denotes qualification rather than assignment: a soldier who has earned the tab remains "Special Forces qualified" for the remainder of his or her career.

The Special Forces tab, introduced in 1983.

Description

The tab is an embroidered arch-shaped device 3¼ inches wide, with a teal-blue background and yellow border, bearing the words "SPECIAL FORCES" in yellow letters. The teal blue and yellow derive from the colours of the Special Forces branch insignia. A subdued version in olive drab and black is worn on field uniforms, and metal replicas exist for dress uniforms.

History

The tab was approved by the Chief of Staff of the Army on 27 June 1983 and first awarded in 1984 to graduates of the qualification course. Before that, Special Forces soldiers were identified principally by the green beret and the full-colour shoulder sleeve insignia worn with the airborne tab of their unit. In 1987, when Special Forces became a separate basic branch of the Army, the tab's significance as a permanent qualification badge was reinforced.

Award criteria

The tab is awarded on successful completion of the SFQC at Fort Liberty (formerly Fort Bragg), North Carolina, run by the John F. Kennedy Special Warfare Center and School. Retroactive awards were made to personnel who had served honorably with Special Forces units in earlier conflicts, including certain veterans of Vietnam-era detachments and their predecessors.

Related tabs

The Special Forces tab is one of a small family of permanent "tabs" in the US Army, alongside the Ranger tab, Sapper tab, and President's Hundred tab, plus the Jungle tab awarded by US Army South. Unlike the Ranger tab, which is also a school qualification, the Special Forces tab additionally denotes membership of a career branch specialty.
