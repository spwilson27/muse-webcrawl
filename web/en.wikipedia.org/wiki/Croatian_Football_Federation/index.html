Croatian Football Federation

The Croatian Football Federation (Croatian: Hrvatski nogometni savez, HNS) is the governing body of football in Croatia, headquartered in Zagreb. It organises the Croatian national football teams and the country's domestic competitions, including the top-flight league and the Croatian Cup.

History

The federation traces its origins to 1912 and was a member of FIFA in various forms during the Yugoslav era; it was readmitted to FIFA in 1992 following Croatia's independence, and joined UEFA in 1993. Under its administration the national team finished third at the 1998 World Cup, runners-up in 2018, and third again in 2022.

Presidents

Notable presidents include Vlatko Marković (1998–2012) and former striker Davor Šuker (2012–2021), succeeded by Marijan Kustić.
