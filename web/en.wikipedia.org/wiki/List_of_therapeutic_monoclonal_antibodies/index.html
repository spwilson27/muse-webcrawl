Monoclonal antibodies (mAbs) are laboratory-produced antibodies engineered to bind to specific targets such as cancer cells, immune molecules, or pathogens. Over a hundred have been approved as medicines. A selection of notable therapeutic monoclonal antibodies is listed below.

Nomenclature

Antibody names are built from a prefix, a substem indicating the target or disease class, a substem indicating the species of origin (e.g. -zu- humanised, -u- fully human), and the suffix -mab.
