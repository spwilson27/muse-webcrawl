Peggy Hill

Margaret J. "Peggy" Hill (née Platter) is a fictional character in the animated television series King of the Hill, voiced by Kathy Bates. Peggy is the wife of protagonist Hank Hill and mother of Bobby Hill, and lives in the fictional town of Arlen, Texas.

Character

Peggy is a substitute teacher — specialising, to her great pride, in Spanish, though her grasp of the language is comically poor ("Peggy Hill, Substitute Teacher of the Year" three times, in her telling). She writes a column of household tips ("The Peggy Hill Home Hints") for the local newspaper, the Arlen Bystander, plays Boggle competitively at a high level, and is a former state-championship softball pitcher. Her family originates from Montana, and she has unusually large feet (size 16½), a recurring source of plotlines and pride alike.

Personality

Peggy is characterised by her enormous, frequently unjustified self-confidence: she overestimates her intelligence, her Spanish, and her abilities, and is often overambitious and susceptible to flattery and scams. At the same time she is resourceful, determined, and fiercely supportive of her family, and several storylines show her succeeding through sheer persistence. Her exclamation of triumph is "Ho yeah!"

Reception

Kathy Bates won an Annie Award for voicing Peggy. The character is often cited among the most distinctive in adult animation for the gap between her self-image and reality, played for both comedy and occasional pathos.
