Indiana House of Representatives

Type: Lower house of the Indiana General Assembly

Seats: 100

Term length: 2 years

Meeting place: Indiana Statehouse, Indianapolis

The Indiana House of Representatives is the lower house of the Indiana General Assembly, the state legislature of the U.S. state of Indiana. Together with the Indiana Senate, it makes laws for the state.

Composition

The House has 100 members, each elected from a single-member district of roughly equal population for a two-year term. Members must be at least 21 years old, citizens of the United States, and residents of their district.

Leadership

The House is led by the Speaker of the House, elected by the membership at the start of each session. The Speaker appoints committee chairs and controls the flow of legislation.

Sessions

The General Assembly meets annually at the Indiana Statehouse in Indianapolis, with longer "long sessions" in odd-numbered years devoted to the biennial budget.
