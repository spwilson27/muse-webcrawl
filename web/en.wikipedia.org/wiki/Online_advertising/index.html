Online advertising, also known as online marketing, internet advertising, or digital advertising, is a form of marketing that uses the Internet to deliver promotional messages to consumers. It includes display advertising (banner ads), search engine advertising, social media advertising, video ads, and email marketing.

History

The first clickable banner ad was sold in 1993, and the first widely recognised banner, for AT&T on HotWired.com in 1994, achieved a click-through rate of 44%. Pay-per-click search advertising, pioneered by GoTo.com and then Google's AdWords (2000), transformed the industry by charging advertisers only when users clicked.

Methods and targeting

Modern online advertising relies on data-driven targeting — contextual, demographic, behavioural, and location-based — and on real-time programmatic auctions in which impressions are bought and sold in milliseconds. Common compensation models are cost per mille (CPM), cost per click (CPC), and cost per action (CPA).

Issues

The industry faces criticism over privacy and tracking (leading to regulations such as the GDPR and moves to phase out third-party cookies), ad fraud, viewability, and the use of ad blockers by a substantial minority of users.
