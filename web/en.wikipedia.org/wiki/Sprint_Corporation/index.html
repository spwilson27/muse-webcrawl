Sprint Corporation

HeadquartersOverland Park, Kansas
IndustryTelecommunications

Sprint Corporation was an American telecommunications company that provided wireless and internet services. Before its merger with T-Mobile US in 2020, it was the fourth-largest mobile network operator in the United States, serving tens of millions of customers.

History

Sprint's roots lie in the Brown Telephone Company, founded in Abilene, Kansas, in 1899. The name Sprint is often said to derive from "Switched Private Network Telecommunications". The company pioneered long-distance competition against AT&T, famously advertising its pin-drop-quiet fibre network, and merged with Nextel in 2005 — a combination widely judged unsuccessful. Japan's SoftBank acquired a majority stake in 2013.

Merger with T-Mobile

After regulatory approval, Sprint merged with T-Mobile US on 1 April 2020, and the Sprint brand was retired that August.
