Wikipedia block in Turkey

Access to Wikipedia was blocked in Turkey from April 2017 to January 2020.

The block of Wikipedia in Turkey was a restriction on access to the online encyclopedia imposed by the Turkish authorities that lasted from 29 April 2017 until 15 January 2020. The block applied to all language editions of Wikipedia and was one of the longest nationwide blocks of the site imposed by any country.

Background

The block was imposed after Wikipedia refused to remove content from articles that the Turkish government regarded as suggesting cooperation between Turkey and terrorist groups, notably material concerning the Syrian civil war and state-sponsored terrorism. Turkish officials demanded the removal of the articles; when the Wikimedia Foundation declined, access was cut off under Law No. 5651, Turkey's internet regulation law.

Implementation

On 29 April 2017 the Information and Communication Technologies Authority (BTK) blocked access, citing a provisional administrative measure. Courts in Ankara subsequently upheld the restriction. Users in Turkey could only reach the site via VPNs or mirrors, and traffic from Turkey to Wikipedia fell dramatically.

Legal challenges and lifting

The Wikimedia Foundation pursued legal remedies in Turkey and applied to the European Court of Human Rights in 2019, arguing the block violated freedom of expression. In December 2019 the Constitutional Court of Turkey ruled the block unconstitutional. Access was restored on 15 January 2020, shortly before Wikipedia's 19th birthday.

Reactions

The block drew international criticism from free-expression advocates and was widely covered in the press. Campaigns such as "We Miss Turkey" highlighted the absence of Wikipedia for Turkish students, researchers and the general public during the nearly three-year restriction.
