List of government agencies in DC Comics

The fictional DC Universe includes numerous government agencies, most of them concerned with monitoring, controlling or exploiting metahumans. Several were created as part of the interconnected espionage landscape developed across DC Comics titles since the 1980s.

Agencies

AgencyPurpose / notes
A.R.G.U.S.Advanced Research Group Uniting Super-Humans; federal agency supporting superheroes, led at times by Steve Trevor
CheckmateUN-chartered covert operations agency organised with chess-piece ranks; associated with Maxwell Lord and Sasha Bordeaux
D.E.O.Department of Extranormal Operations; monitors extraterrestrial and metahuman threats; led by Mr. Bones
S.H.A.D.E.Super Human Advanced Defense Executive; agency employing Frankenstein and the Creature Commandos
Task Force XCovert programme fielding the Suicide Squad under Amanda Waller
Project CadmusGenetics research facility responsible for cloning, including Superboy (Kon-El)
StormwatchUN-backed superhuman response team
Bureau of NormalcyAgency dedicated to eliminating "abnormal" beings; antagonist in Doom Patrol
Institute of Metahuman StudiesResearch body studying metahumans
Suicide Squad programme (Task Force X)Expendable black-ops unit of imprisoned supervillains

Amanda Waller is the most prominent government official in this landscape, having directed Task Force X and served as Secretary of Metahuman Affairs. In DC films and television, A.R.G.U.S. and Task Force X feature centrally in the Suicide Squad adaptations and Peacemaker.
