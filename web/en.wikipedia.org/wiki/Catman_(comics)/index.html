Catman (Thomas Reese Blake) is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Bill Finger and artist Jim Mooney, he first appeared in Detective Comics #311 (1963) as a villain modelled on Batman, a big-game hunter who turned to crime out of boredom and modelled his costume and equipment on both Batman and big cats.

Fictional biography

Thomas Blake was a world-famous trapper and hunter of big cats who, having exhausted the thrill of the hunt, became a burglar wearing a cat costume made — he believed — from a magical African cloth that granted him nine lives. Initially a third-rate Batman adversary, the character declined into obscurity and comic relief, portrayed in the 1990s as overweight and washed-up.

Reinvention

Writer Gail Simone reinvented Catman in the 2005 miniseries Villains United and the ongoing series Secret Six. Restored to fitness after living among lions in Africa, Blake became a morally ambiguous antihero: a superb tracker and fighter, loyal to his mercenary teammates, and one of DC's few openly bisexual major characters. The portrayal was widely praised and made Catman a fan-favourite character.

Other versions

A heroic Cat-Man also exists in the separate history of Charlton/DC's earlier characters, and Thomas Blake has appeared in animated adaptations and as a member of the Secret Six in various continuities following DC's relaunches.
