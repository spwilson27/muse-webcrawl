Coat of arms of Sweden

The coat of arms of Sweden exists in two versions: the greater coat of arms, used by the monarch and the government, and the lesser coat of arms, used more generally by state authorities. Both are based on the three crowns (tre kronor) and the arms of the House of Bjälbo.

Coat of arms of Sweden
Blazon (lesser)Azure, three crowns or
Adopted1448 (three crowns, seals); law 1908/1982

Description

The lesser arms show three golden crowns on a blue field. The greater arms quarter the three crowns with the Bjälbo lion, add an inescutcheon with the arms of Vasa and Bernadotte, and are surrounded by the collar of the Order of the Seraphim and supported by two lions.
