Arunachal Pradesh is a state in Northeast India, situated in the eastern Himalayas. It borders the Indian states of Assam and Nagaland to the south, and shares international borders with Bhutan in the west, Myanmar in the east, and China (Tibet) in the north. Its name means "land of the dawn-lit mountains" in Sanskrit. The state capital is Itanagar.

History

The region was administered by British India as the North-East Frontier Agency (NEFA) after independence, became the Union Territory of Arunachal Pradesh in 1972, and achieved full statehood in 1987. Much of the state is claimed by China, which refers to it as part of "South Tibet"; the disputed boundary (the McMahon Line) was a central cause of the Sino-Indian War of 1962, during which Chinese forces briefly occupied large areas before withdrawing.

Geography and climate

Arunachal Pradesh is India's largest north-eastern state and one of its most mountainous and biodiverse. Elevations range from the Brahmaputra plains in the south to Himalayan peaks such as Kangto (over 7,000 m). Major rivers — the Siang (the Brahmaputra's upper course), Kameng, Subansiri, Lohit and Tirap — cut deep valleys through the hills. Much of the state remains forested, and it is exceptionally rich in orchids and wildlife, with protected areas such as Namdapha National Park.

People and culture

The state is home to more than two dozen major tribal groups — including the Monpa, Nyishi, Apatani, Galo, Adi and Mishmi — speaking languages of the Tibeto-Burman family. Religious practice ranges from Tibetan Buddhism in the west (the great Tawang Monastery is a major centre) through indigenous faiths such as Donyi-Polo to Hinduism and Christianity. The population density, around 17 persons per km², is among the lowest in India.

Economy

Agriculture, especially shifting (jhum) cultivation and terrace farming of rice, maize, millet and oilseeds, employs most of the population. Handloom weaving, cane and bamboo crafts, horticulture, hydropower potential and a growing tourism sector are also important.
