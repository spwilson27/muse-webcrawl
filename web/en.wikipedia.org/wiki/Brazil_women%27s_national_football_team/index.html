Brazil women's national football team

The Brazil women's national football team represents Brazil in women's international association football and is governed by the Brazilian Football Confederation (CBF). Brazil is historically one of the strongest women's teams in South America, competing in every edition of the FIFA Women's World Cup since the inaugural 1991 tournament and winning the Copa América Feminina multiple times.

History

Women's football was formally banned in Brazil from 1941 until 1979, which stunted the early development of the game. After the ban was lifted, the first official national team was formed in the mid-1980s. Brazil reached the final of the 2007 FIFA Women's World Cup, losing to Germany, and has won silver medals at the Olympic Games, including at Paris 2024, where the team again reached the gold-medal match.

Players

The most famous Brazilian women's player is Marta, widely regarded as one of the greatest footballers of all time and the all-time top scorer in World Cup history across both men's and women's tournaments. Other notable players include Formiga, Cristiane, and Debinha.

Honours

Brazil has won the Copa América Feminina a record number of times and regularly contests the final of major tournaments, though a first World Cup title has remained elusive.
