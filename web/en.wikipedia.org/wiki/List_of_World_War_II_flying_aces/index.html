List of World War II flying aces

A flying ace is a military aviator credited with shooting down five or more enemy aircraft during aerial combat. The Second World War produced thousands of aces on all sides. The highest-scoring aces in history were German Luftwaffe pilots on the Eastern Front, where Erich Hartmann accumulated 352 aerial victories — a record unlikely ever to be approached. Confirmation standards differed between air forces, and historians note that claims, especially in large formations, were often overstated.
