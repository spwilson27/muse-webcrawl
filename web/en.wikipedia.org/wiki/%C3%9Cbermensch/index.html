Übermensch

The Übermensch (German for "Overman") is a concept in the philosophy of Friedrich Nietzsche. In his 1883 book Thus Spoke Zarathustra, Nietzsche has his character Zarathustra posit the Übermensch as a goal for humanity to set for itself: the human being who has overcome himself, creates his own values, and affirms life.

Interpretation

The Übermensch is often contrasted with the "last man" (letzter Mensch), who seeks only comfort and security. Scholars debate whether the concept is a biological, cultural, or existential ideal; Nietzsche himself presented it as the meaning of the earth, in opposition to otherworldly religious ideals.

Influence

The idea influenced literature, philosophy and popular culture, and was later distorted by Nazi propaganda despite Nietzsche's opposition to antisemitism and nationalism.
