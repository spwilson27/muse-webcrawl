Northern Lights Cathedral

The Northern Lights Cathedral – Alta Church (Norwegian: Nordlyskatedralen – Alta kirke) is a parish church of the Church of Norway in Alta Municipality, Troms og Finnmark county, Norway. Consecrated in 2013, it is an architectural landmark whose spiralling form and titanium cladding are inspired by the aurora borealis, the northern lights visible in the region's winter skies.

Design

The Danish firm Schmidt Hammer Lassen won the 2001 architectural competition with a design whose rising spiral tower, 47 metres high, evokes the swirling curtains of the aurora. The exterior is clad in titanium sheets that reflect the changing Arctic light; the interior is of pale concrete and wood, lit by narrow openings. The Danish artist Peter Brandes contributed the church art, including a gilded bronze figure of Christ.

Role

The cathedral serves as the main church of Alta parish and a major venue for concerts and cultural events, as well as a prime tourist attraction in a town that markets itself as the “town of the northern lights”. It replaced the old Alta Church (1858) as the parish's principal building.
