SFP+

SFP+
Full name: Enhanced small form-factor pluggable
Standard: SFF-8431 / SFF-8432
Data rate: up to 10 Gbit/s (16 Gbit/s for Fibre Channel)

SFP+ (enhanced small form-factor pluggable) is a compact, hot-pluggable network interface transceiver module used for both telecommunication and data communications applications. It is an enhanced version of the older SFP module, supporting data rates up to 10 gigabits per second, and became the dominant 10-gigabit form factor for Ethernet and Fibre Channel equipment.

Characteristics

An SFP+ module is slightly smaller than the competing XFP module because it moves clock and data recovery functions onto the host board. Modules are available for multi-mode and single-mode optical fibre at various wavelengths and reaches, as well as passive and active direct-attach copper (DAC) twinaxial cables for short in-rack links, and 10GBASE-T copper variants. SFP+ cages and connectors are specified by the SFF-8431 and SFF-8432 multi-source agreements.

Applications

SFP+ ports are ubiquitous on enterprise switches, routers, servers and storage arrays, carrying 10 Gigabit Ethernet, 8/16G Fibre Channel, SONET OC-192 and related protocols. Later generations include SFP28 (25G) and SFP56 (50G), which retain the same physical form factor.
