Olympique de Marseille, commonly OM, is a French professional football club based in Marseille, founded in 1899. It plays at the Stade Vélodrome and is one of France's most popular and successful clubs.

History

Marseille won the inaugural Ligue 1 in 1937 and became, in 1993, the first and still only French club to win the UEFA Champions League, beating AC Milan 1–0 in Munich. That era ended in a match-fixing scandal under president Bernard Tapie, leading to relegation and loss of the 1993 league title.

Honours and rivalry

OM have won multiple Ligue 1 titles and Coupes de France. Their rivalry with Paris Saint-Germain is known as Le Classique. Motto: Droit au but ('Straight to the goal').
