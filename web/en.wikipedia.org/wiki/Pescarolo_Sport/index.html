Pescarolo Sport

Pescarolo Sport was a French motorsport team founded in 2000 by racing driver Henri Pescarolo, a four-time winner of the 24 Hours of Le Mans. Based near Le Mans, the team competed in endurance racing, fielding prototypes in the Le Mans Series and at the 24 Hours of Le Mans.

History

The team began by running Courage prototypes before developing its own Pescarolo P01 chassis with Judd V10 power. Pescarolo cars won Le Mans Series championships in 2005 and 2006 and were regular front-runners against factory Audi and Peugeot entries despite a much smaller budget. Financial difficulties forced the team into liquidation in 2010; Pescarolo later returned briefly with new backers before withdrawing again.

Legacy

The Pescarolo name also appeared in the Gran Turismo video game series, whose virtual Pescarolo prototypes introduced the team to a wide audience.
