Riek Machar

Born: 26 November 1952, Leer, Anglo-Egyptian Sudan
Office: First Vice President of South Sudan
Party: SPLM-IO
Spouse: Angelina Teny

Riek Machar Teny Dhurgon (born 26 November 1952) is a South Sudanese politician who has served as First Vice President of South Sudan, most recently under the 2018 revitalised peace agreement. An ethnic Nuer, he holds a PhD in mechanical engineering from the University of Bradford.

SPLA career

Machar joined the Sudan People's Liberation Army (SPLA) during the Second Sudanese Civil War. In 1991 he split from John Garang to form the SPLA-Nasir faction, a rupture followed by the Bor massacre; he later signed the 1997 Khartoum Peace Agreement with the Sudanese government before rejoining the SPLM in 2002.

South Sudan

Machar was vice president of South Sudan from independence in 2011 until President Salva Kiir dismissed him in July 2013. Ethnic violence that December escalated into civil war between forces loyal to Kiir and Machar's SPLM-IO. A peace deal returned Machar to Juba in 2016, but fighting forced him to flee; under the 2018 agreement he again became First Vice President in February 2020.
