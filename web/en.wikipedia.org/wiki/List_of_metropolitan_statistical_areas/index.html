List of metropolitan statistical areas

A metropolitan statistical area (MSA) is a geographical region in the United States defined by the Office of Management and Budget, centred on an urban core of at least 50,000 people with strong economic ties to surrounding counties. There are more than 380 MSAs.

Definitions

MSAs differ from combined statistical areas (CSAs), which group adjacent MSAs and micropolitan areas, and from micropolitan statistical areas, whose cores have 10,000–50,000 people. Delineations are updated after each census.
