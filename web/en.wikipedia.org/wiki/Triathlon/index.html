Triathlon

A triathlon is an endurance multisport race consisting of swimming, cycling, and running over various distances, completed in sequence without a break; the periods between disciplines are called transitions.

Distances

  • Sprint – 750 m swim, 20 km bike, 5 km run
  • Olympic (standard) – 1.5 km swim, 40 km bike, 10 km run
  • Half (70.3) – 1.9 km swim, 90 km bike, 21.1 km run
  • Ironman (full) – 3.86 km swim, 180.25 km bike, 42.2 km (marathon) run

History

The modern triathlon began in San Diego, California, in 1974; the first Ironman was held in Hawaii in 1978. Triathlon became an Olympic sport at the Sydney 2000 Games. The sport is governed internationally by World Triathlon. See Ironman Triathlon.
