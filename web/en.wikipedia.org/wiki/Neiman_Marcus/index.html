Neiman Marcus

Neiman Marcus is an American luxury department store chain founded in 1907 in Dallas, Texas, by Herbert Marcus, his sister Carrie Marcus Neiman, and her husband Abraham Lincoln Neiman. Long headquartered in Dallas, the chain became famous for its high-end fashion, personal service, and extravagant "Fantasy Gifts" featured in its annual Christmas Book, first published in 1926.

History

The founders famously chose retailing over an alternative investment — an early soft-drink venture that became Coca-Cola. The downtown Dallas flagship, opened in 1914 after a fire destroyed the original store, remains the chain's best-known location. Neiman Marcus expanded nationally from the 1950s onward and for decades was grouped with Bergdorf Goodman under the Neiman Marcus Group.

Recent years

After a series of leveraged buyouts, the company filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in May 2020 amid the COVID-19 pandemic, emerging later that year. In 2024, Saks Global, the parent of Saks Fifth Avenue, announced its acquisition of the Neiman Marcus Group in a deal valued at about $2.65 billion.
