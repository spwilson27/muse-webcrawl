Tandag

Tandag is a city in the Philippines and the capital of the province of Surigao del Sur, in the Caraga region of northeastern Mindanao. It lies on the coast facing the Philippine Sea.

History

The area was inhabited by Manobo and Mamanwa peoples before Spanish colonization; the name is said to derive from local words for the settlement's early inhabitants or its fishing grounds. Tandag became a municipality under Spanish rule and developed as a trading point for abacá and copra. It became the provincial capital when Surigao del Sur was created in 1960, and was converted into a component city in 2007 — a status briefly lost and restored in the Philippine "cityhood" litigation — with final confirmation thereafter.

Economy

Fishing, agriculture (rice, coconut, and banana), and commerce are the city's main activities. Tandag Airport provides air links within Mindanao.
