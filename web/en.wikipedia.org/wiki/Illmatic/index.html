Illmatic is the debut studio album by American rapper Nas, released on April 19, 1994, by Columbia Records. Recorded when Nas was around twenty years old, the album depicts life in the Queensbridge housing projects of New York City through densely layered, multisyllabic lyrics over sparse, jazz-influenced production by DJ Premier, Large Professor, Pete Rock, Q-Tip, and L.E.S.

Background

Nas gained attention with his verse on Main Source's "Live at the Barbeque" (1991) and his debut single "Halftime" (1992). Signed to Columbia, he assembled a celebrated team of producers unusual for a debut. Despite modest initial sales, the album received a rare five-microphone rating from The Source magazine.

Legacy

Illmatic is widely regarded as one of the greatest hip hop albums of all time, acclaimed for Nas's vivid street reportage and internal rhyme schemes and for its cohesive, jazz-sampling production. It was certified platinum in the United States, selected for preservation in the National Recording Registry by the Library of Congress in 2020, and its twentieth anniversary was marked by the expanded reissue Illmatic XX (2014).
