A marzban (Middle Persian: marzpān; Persian: مرزبان) was a class of margraves — wardens of the marches — and military governors of border provinces in the Sasanian Empire of Iran. The office combined civil administration with command of frontier defences against nomadic and foreign invasion.

Role and hierarchy

Marzbans ranked among the highest officials of the empire, below the king of kings and the grand vizier. They collected taxes, administered justice, and maintained garrisons in their provinces, particularly along the volatile frontiers with the Roman and later Byzantine world and Central Asian peoples.

After the Sasanians

The title survived the Arab conquest of Persia and was used by local dynasties such as the Bavandids, and was even applied in the Caucasus and in translated form to frontier lords of neighbouring states.
