Commerzbank AG is a German bank headquartered in Frankfurt am Main. Founded in Hamburg in 1870 as the Commerz- und Disconto-Bank, it is one of Germany's largest banks, serving private customers and the Mittelstand of small and medium-sized companies, as well as corporate and institutional clients.

History

The bank expanded across Germany in the early twentieth century and merged with other regional banks in 1929. After the Second World War it was split in West Germany and re-formed in 1958. During the 2008 financial crisis Commerzbank acquired Dresdner Bank and received state aid, leaving the German government a major shareholder for years afterwards.

Headquarters

Its Frankfurt headquarters, the Commerzbank Tower, completed in 1997 to a design by Norman Foster, was Europe's tallest building at the time and is notable for its internal sky gardens.
