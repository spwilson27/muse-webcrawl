Captain Atom is a superhero appearing in American comic books, created by writer Joe Gill and artist Steve Ditko for Charlton Comics in Space Adventures #33 (March 1960). After DC Comics acquired the Charlton characters, Captain Atom was relaunched in 1987 as a Justice League member, in the incarnation of Nathaniel Adam, a US Air Force officer framed for a crime and subjected to a nuclear experiment that blasted him forward in time and bonded alien metal to his body.

Powers and abilities

Captain Atom draws power from the "quantum field": he can absorb and project enormous amounts of energy, fly, survive in space, and manipulate matter at the atomic level. His metallic shell (dilustel) contains his energy; a breach can cause a catastrophic release. Time travel occurs involuntarily when he absorbs excessive energy.

Legacy

The character served as the model for Doctor Manhattan in Alan Moore's Watchmen (1986–87), after DC asked Moore not to use the Charlton heroes directly. Later storylines recast Captain Atom as the villain Monarch in an alternate future, and he has appeared in animated series including Justice League Unlimited and Young Justice.
