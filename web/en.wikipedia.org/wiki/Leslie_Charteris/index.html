Leslie Charteris

Leslie Charteris (born Leslie Charles Bowyer-Yin; 12 May 1907 – 15 April 1993) was a British-Chinese author best known as the creator of Simon Templar, the gentleman adventurer known as "The Saint," hero of a long-running series of books and of film and television adaptations.

Born12 May 1907, Singapore

Early life

Charteris was born in Singapore to a Chinese father and an English mother. Educated in England, he left Cambridge before graduating to pursue writing, supporting himself with a variety of jobs while his early thrillers were published.

The Saint

Simon Templar first appeared in Meet the Tiger (1928) and became the central figure of dozens of novels and short-story collections over five decades. The character reached a mass audience through the 1960s television series The Saint, starring Roger Moore, and later adaptations including a 1997 film with Val Kilmer.

Later life

From the 1960s Charteris increasingly supervised Saint books written by collaborators. He spent later years between the United States, France and England, and died in Windsor in 1993.
