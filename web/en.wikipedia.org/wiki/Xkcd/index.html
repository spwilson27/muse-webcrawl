xkcd

xkcd is a webcomic of romance, sarcasm, math, and language created by American author Randall Munroe. The comic's tagline describes it as such. Set in stick figures, it frequently features science, technology, mathematics, and computer science humour.

xkcd
Author: Randall Munroe
Launched: September 2005
Genre: Humour, science, geek culture
Schedule: Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays

History

Munroe, a former NASA roboticist, began posting doodles from his school notebooks in 2005. The name "xkcd" is a deliberately unpronounceable four-letter string chosen as a screen name. The comic grew to become one of the most popular webcomics, known for hover-text (alt-text) jokes on each strip.

Style and themes

Strips range from single-panel gags to elaborate charts and maps, including the famous "Click and Drag" (2012), an enormous explorable world, and detailed infographics such as the Money chart and Saturn V blueprint. Recurring characters include Black Hat, White Hat, Cueball, and Megan.

Related works

Munroe's books include xkcd: Volume 0, What If? (2014), Thing Explainer (2015), How To (2019), and What If? 2 (2022).
