First aid

First aid
DefinitionImmediate assistance given to a person suffering illness or injury
PrioritiesAirway, breathing, circulation; call emergency services; stop bleeding
[Image: A first-aid kit open showing bandages, antiseptic and gloves]

First aid is the first and immediate assistance given to any person with a minor or serious illness or injury (wider crawl), with care provided to preserve life, prevent the condition from worsening, or promote recovery — CPR, the recovery position, choking relief and bleeding control.

Systems

First aid bridges the gap until professionals arrive; survival from cardiac arrest doubles or triples with bystander CPR, and public defibrillators (AEDs) extend the chain. Training by organisations like the Red Cross and St John Ambulance is a civic staple, like the Bandage in every kit.
