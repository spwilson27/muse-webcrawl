Krum (Bulgarian: Крум), called "the Fearsome," was khan of the First Bulgarian Empire from about 803 to 814, who greatly expanded its territory at Byzantine expense.

Wars with Byzantium

Krum destroyed the Avar Khaganate's remnants, taking eastern Hungary and Transylvania. When Emperor Nikephoros I invaded and sacked Pliska in 811, Krum ambushed and annihilated the Byzantine army at the Varbitsa Pass (Battle of Pliska), killing Nikephoros — famously having his skull lined with silver as a drinking cup. He then defeated Michael I and besieged Constantinople in 813–814, dying suddenly in April 814 while preparing a renewed assault.

Laws

Tradition credits Krum with Bulgaria's first written laws, severe penalties intended to curb theft, drunkenness and false accusation, and with ordering vineyards uprooted to fight drunkenness.
