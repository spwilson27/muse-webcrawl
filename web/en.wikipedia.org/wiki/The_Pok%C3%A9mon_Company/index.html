The Pokémon Company

The Pokémon Company (株式会社ポケモン, Kabushiki gaisha Pokemon) is a Japanese company responsible for brand management, production, publishing and licensing of the Pokémon franchise. It was founded in 1998 as a joint venture between Nintendo, Game Freak and Creatures.

Founded23 April 1998
HeadquartersRoppongi, Minato, Tokyo, Japan
OwnersNintendo, Game Freak, Creatures
ProductsPokémon video games, trading cards, anime, merchandise

Operations

The company oversees the Pokémon video game series (developed chiefly by Game Freak), the Pokémon Trading Card Game, the long-running anime television series and films, and an extensive merchandising empire including Pokémon Center retail stores. International operations are handled by The Pokémon Company International, based in Bellevue, Washington, and London.

Scale

Pokémon is the highest-grossing media franchise of all time, with lifetime revenue estimated well over $100 billion, making the company central to one of the entertainment industry's most valuable properties.
