Master Levels for Doom II is an official expansion pack for the first-person shooter Doom II, released by id Software in December 1995. It consists of 21 levels (20 regular levels plus one secret level) designed by members of the Doom modding community under contract with id Software, after the company recognised the quality of fan-made WAD files circulating online.

Content

The contributing designers included John Anderson (Dr. Sleep), Jim Flynn, Sverre André Kvernmo, Christen Klie, and Tim Willits. The levels are not arranged as a linear campaign in the original release; players select them individually using the included launcher. The package also shipped with Maximum Doom, a collection of over 3,000 fan-made levels downloaded from the internet, included as a bonus with no quality control — a fact noted in contemporary reviews.

Legacy

The Master Levels have been bundled with later re-releases of Doom II, including versions for modern consoles and the 2019 Unity-based ports, where they are presented as a single episode. The expansion is notable as an early example of a game developer commercially commissioning work from its own modding community.
