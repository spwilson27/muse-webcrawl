List of largest optical telescopes

This list covers the largest optical reflecting telescopes in the world, ranked by the diameter of their primary mirror or combined aperture. Segmented mirrors, which combine many smaller mirror segments into a single surface, dominate the largest instruments.

Under construction

The next generation of "extremely large telescopes" includes the Extremely Large Telescope (ELT) in Chile with a 39.3 m segmented primary mirror, the Thirty Meter Telescope (30 m), and the Giant Magellan Telescope (24.5 m effective aperture). The ELT is expected to see first light in the late 2020s and would dwarf all current instruments.

Historical milestones

For roughly four decades the 5.1 m Hale Telescope (1949) and the 6 m BTA-6 (1975) defined the upper limit of monolithic mirrors, until active optics and segmented designs in the 1990s pushed apertures beyond 8 m.
