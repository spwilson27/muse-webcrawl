Odia language

The Odia script, distinctive for its rounded letterforms.

Odia (ଓଡ଼ିଆ), formerly rendered Oriya, is an Indo-Aryan language spoken mainly in the Indian state of Odisha, where it is an official language. It is also one of the 22 scheduled languages of India and was designated a classical language of India in 2014.

Speakers and script

Odia has more than 35 million native speakers. It is written in the Odia script, a Brahmic script whose rounded shapes are traditionally attributed to writing on palm leaves, which would tear under straight lines.

History

Odia descends through Middle Indo-Aryan Prakrits; inscriptions in recognisable Odia date to the 10th century CE. Its literature, including the medieval works of Sarala Das, is among the oldest of the modern Indo-Aryan languages.
