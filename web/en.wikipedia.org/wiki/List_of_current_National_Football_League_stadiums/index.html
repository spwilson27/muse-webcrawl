List of current National Football League stadiums

The National Football League (NFL) has 32 teams, but only 30 stadiums, because the New York Giants and New York Jets share MetLife Stadium, and the Los Angeles Rams and Los Angeles Chargers share SoFi Stadium. NFL stadiums range in capacity from roughly 61,500 (Soldier Field, Chicago) to over 82,000 (MetLife Stadium), with AT&T Stadium in Arlington able to expand beyond 100,000 for special events using standing room.

Characteristics

Most current NFL stadiums opened after 1995 during a stadium-building boom financed partly with public funds. Recent venues such as SoFi Stadium (2020), Allegiant Stadium (2020) and U.S. Bank Stadium (2016) feature fixed or translucent roofs and cost well over US$1 billion. Lambeau Field (1957) is the oldest stadium in the league still in use, and Soldier Field (1924, extensively rebuilt in 2003) occupies the oldest site.
