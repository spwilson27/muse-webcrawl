Dakota Mayi Johnson (born 4 October 1989) is an American actress and producer. The daughter of actors Don Johnson and Melanie Griffith, she made her film debut at age ten in Crazy in Alabama (1999) and was named Miss Golden Globe 2006, the first second-generation honouree in the award's history.

Career

After supporting roles in The Social Network (2010), 21 Jump Street (2012) and Need for Speed (2014), and the lead in the sitcom Ben and Kate (2012–2013), Johnson achieved international fame as Anastasia Steele in the Fifty Shades film series (2015–2018), which grossed over $1.3 billion worldwide despite mixed-to-negative reviews.

She went on to earn critical acclaim in Luca Guadagnino's A Bigger Splash (2015) and Suspiria (2018), and appeared in Black Mass (2015), How to Be Single (2016), Bad Times at the El Royale (2018), The Peanut Butter Falcon (2019) and The Lost Daughter (2021). In 2019 she co-founded the production company TeaTime Pictures. She starred in Cha Cha Real Smooth (2022) and played the title role in the superhero film Madame Web (2024).

Personal life

Johnson was born in Austin, Texas, and raised largely in Colorado and California. She is the granddaughter of actress Tippi Hedren and the stepdaughter of Antonio Banderas. She has been in a relationship with Coldplay frontman Chris Martin since 2017. Johnson has spoken publicly about living with depression since her teenage years and is an advocate for women's rights organisations.
