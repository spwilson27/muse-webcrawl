StarTalk (podcast)

Host: Neil deGrasse Tyson
Genre: Science, comedy, talk
Debut: 1 June 2009
Network: StarTalk Radio Network; iHeartRadio

StarTalk is a podcast and radio programme hosted by astrophysicist Neil deGrasse Tyson. It mixes science, popular culture and comedy, billing itself as a show where "science meets pop culture," and features Tyson interviewing celebrity guests, scientists and comedians about astronomy, physics, and technology.

Format

Episodes typically pair Tyson with a comedian co-host — a chair occupied on rotation by figures such as Chuck Nice, Eugene Mirman, Leighann Lord and others — and a guest or subject expert. A recurring segment, "Cosmic Queries," answers questions submitted by listeners. Regulars have included Bill Nye as a frequent guest and commentator. The show began as a radio programme in 2009 before becoming one of the most-downloaded science podcasts on iTunes.

Related programmes

StarTalk expanded into a television talk show on the National Geographic channel (2015–2020), interviewing guests such as Richard Dawkins, Buzz Aldrin and former President Jimmy Carter, and into spin-off podcasts including StarTalk Sports Edition and StarTalk Playing with Science, applying the same format to athletics with co-hosts Gary O'Reilly and Chuck Nice. A related mainstream science series hosted by Tyson, Cosmos: Possible Worlds, drew on the StarTalk production team.
