Marie Antoinette

Marie Antoinette (1755–1793) was queen of France as wife of Louis XVI. An Austrian archduchess married into the French court, she became a symbol — fairly or not — of royal extravagance during the French Revolution.

Death

After the abolition of the monarchy and the king's execution, she was tried by the Revolutionary Tribunal and executed by guillotine on 16 October 1793. The famous 'let them eat cake' line attributed to her is not believed to have been hers.
