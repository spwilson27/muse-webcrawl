Greenwich Peninsula

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Greenwich Peninsula is an area of South East London, England, within the Royal Borough of Greenwich. The peninsula is bounded on three sides by a loop in the Thames, between the Isle of Dogs to the west and Silvertown to the east.

History

Drained marshland known as Greenwich Marshes, the site was industrialised in the 19th century with gasworks, chemical plants, and shipbuilding; the South Metropolitan Gas Works was once the largest in Europe. A major gas explosion in 1917 at the nearby Silvertown works damaged the area. Deindustrialisation left the peninsula heavily contaminated and largely derelict by the 1980s.

Redevelopment

English Partnerships acquired the site in 1997 and undertook vast decontamination. The Millennium Dome (now The O2) was built for the year 2000 celebrations and later converted into a successful entertainment arena. The Jubilee line station at North Greenwich opened in 1999. Since the 2010s a masterplan has added thousands of new homes, the Ravensbourne university building, a design district, and the Greenwich Peninsula Ecology Park around the remaining wetlands.
