IC 1101

IC 1101 is a supergiant elliptical galaxy at the centre of the galaxy cluster Abell 2029, about 1.04 billion light-years from Earth in the constellation Serpens. Classified as a cD galaxy, it is one of the largest known galaxies, with an extended diffuse halo of stars and an estimated population of up to 100 trillion stars.

Size

The galaxy's effective radius is exceptionally large, and its faint stellar halo extends to several hundred thousand light-years; some measurements put the overall diameter near 400,000 light-years, several times that of the Milky Way. The galaxy likely grew by accreting many smaller galaxies that fell into the cluster centre.

Central black hole

IC 1101 hosts an ultramassive black hole, estimated at tens of billions of solar masses, among the largest known, powering a radio source in the cluster core. The cluster's hot intracluster gas glows strongly in X-rays.
