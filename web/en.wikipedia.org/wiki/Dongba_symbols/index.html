Dongba symbols

Dongba symbols are a system of pictographic glyphs used by the Dongba priests of the Nakhi people of southwestern China to record the Bon religion's rituals and myths.

The script

With roughly a thousand glyphs, Dongba is often described as the only pictographic writing system still in use. Priests recite texts from memory aided by the pictures, which depict people, animals, objects and actions.

Manuscripts

Thousands of Dongba manuscripts survive, many held in museums; the tradition is inscribed in UNESCO's Memory of the World register. A related syllabic script, Geba, is used alongside.
