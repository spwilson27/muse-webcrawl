Bunge Global

Bunge Global S.A. is a global agribusiness and food company. Founded in 1818 in Amsterdam by Johann Bunge, the company is one of the world's largest oilseed processors and a major trader of grains and other agricultural commodities. It is commonly counted among the "ABCD" group of companies — with ADM, Cargill and Louis Dreyfus — that dominate world agricultural trade.

Operations

Bunge buys, stores, transports, processes and sells oilseeds (especially soybeans), grains, wheat, corn and sugar, producing edible oils, shortenings, mayonnaise, bakery products, plant-based ingredients and animal feed. The company operates grain elevators, port terminals and crushing plants across the Americas, Europe and Asia, and is a leading supplier of bottled vegetable oils.

History

After its founding in Amsterdam, the firm expanded into Argentina (1884), Brazil, and the United States, where it became a central handler of grain exports. Its principal executive offices moved to White Plains, New York, and later to Chesterfield, Missouri. In 2023 the company redomiciled to Switzerland, becoming Bunge Global S.A. In 2025 Bunge completed a merger with Viterra, creating one of the largest agribusiness companies in the world.
