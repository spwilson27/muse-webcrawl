Overview

The Secret Life of the American Teenager is an American teen drama television series created by Brenda Hampton that aired on ABC Family from July 1, 2008, to June 3, 2013. The series centers on Amy Juergens (Shailene Woodley), a fifteen-year-old who becomes pregnant after a one-night encounter at band camp with the school's notorious Ricky Underwood (Daren Kagasoff).

Across five seasons and 121 episodes, the show follows Amy, her family, and her friends at the fictional Ulysses S. Grant High School as they deal with pregnancy, marriage, adoption, relationships, and loss. Molly Ringwald plays Amy's mother, Anne Juergens.

Reception

The premiere drew record ratings for ABC Family at the time, and the series became one of the network's signature programs. Reviews were mixed, with criticism of its dialogue and after-school-special tone, but it found a loyal young audience and launched Woodley's career.
