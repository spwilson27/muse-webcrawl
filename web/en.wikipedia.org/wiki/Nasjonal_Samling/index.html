Nasjonal Samling

Founded: 1933
Dissolved: 1945
Leader: Vidkun Quisling
Ideology: Fascism, Norwegian nationalism
Country: Norway

Nasjonal Samling (Norwegian for "National Gathering" or "National Unity") was a Norwegian far-right political party active from 1933 to 1945. Led by Vidkun Quisling, it collaborated with Nazi Germany during the occupation of Norway in the Second World War.

History

Quisling, a former defence minister, founded the party in May 1933. It never won significant support in free elections, polling only a few percent of the vote. After Germany invaded Norway in April 1940, Quisling attempted a coup and was eventually installed as head of a collaborationist government in 1942, though real power rested with the German Reichskommissariat under Josef Terboven.

Post-war fate

The party was banned after liberation in 1945. Quisling was tried and executed for treason in October 1945, and his surname became a byword for a traitorous collaborator. Party membership files were used extensively in Norway's post-war legal purge.
