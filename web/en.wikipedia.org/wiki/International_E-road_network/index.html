International E-road network

The International E-road network is a numbering system for roads in Europe managed by the United Nations Economic Commission for Europe (UNECE). The network is governed by the European Agreement on Main International Traffic Arteries (AGR), concluded in 1975. E-roads are marked with green signs bearing a white "E" followed by a number, such as E4 or E40.

Numbering

Even-numbered routes generally run west–east and odd-numbered routes north–south. Reference roads have two-digit numbers (three digits for extensions); the network extends eastward into Central Asia, where it links with the Asian Highway network. Major west–east reference roads increase in number from north to south (E10, E20 … E90), and north–south routes from west to east (E05, E15 … E95).

Extent

The network comprises over 50,000 km of roads. Signage and adoption vary by country: some countries (such as Sweden and Belgium) rely heavily on E-road numbers, while others, including the United Kingdom, do not sign them at all.
