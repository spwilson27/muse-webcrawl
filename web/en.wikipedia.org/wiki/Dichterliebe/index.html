Dichterliebe

Dichterliebe ("A Poet's Love"), Op. 48, is the best-known song cycle by Robert Schumann. Composed in 1840 — Schumann's "year of song" — it sets sixteen poems from Heinrich Heine's Lyrisches Intermezzo, tracing a poet's journey from the joy of new love through loss and bitterness to a final, ironic burial of love itself.

The songs

The cycle opens with "Im wunderschönen Monat Mai" and includes such celebrated songs as "Ich grolle nicht", "Und wüssten's die Blumen", "Am leuchtenden Sommermorgen" and the concluding "Die alten, bösen Lieder". Schumann's piano parts, with their extended preludes and postludes, are integral to the cycle's psychological depth, often seeming to comment on or contradict the singer's words in the spirit of Heine's irony.

Reception

Dichterliebe is regarded as one of the supreme achievements of the German Lied and is a staple of the recital repertoire for voice and piano.
