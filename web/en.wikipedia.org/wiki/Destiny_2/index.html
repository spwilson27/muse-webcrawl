Destiny 2 is a free-to-play online multiplayer first-person shooter developed by Bungie, released in 2017 as the sequel to Destiny. Players assume the role of a Guardian defending humanity's Last City from alien races and the Darkness.

Expansions

Major expansions include Forsaken (2018), Shadowkeep (2019), Beyond Light (2020), The Witch Queen (2022), Lightfall (2023), and The Final Shape (2024), which concluded the Light and Darkness saga. The game went free-to-play under the "New Light" model in 2019.
