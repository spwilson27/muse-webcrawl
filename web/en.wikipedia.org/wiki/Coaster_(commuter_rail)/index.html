Coaster (commuter rail)

Coaster (stylised COASTER) is a commuter rail service in San Diego County, California, running between Oceanside and downtown San Diego along the Pacific coast. The service is operated by the North County Transit District (NCTD).

Route

Trains run on the Surf Line, serving Oceanside, Carlsbad Village, Poinsettia, Encinitas, Solana Beach, Sorrento Valley, Old Town and Santa Fe Depot in San Diego, sharing track with Amtrak's Pacific Surfliner and freight services.
