Doepfer

Doepfer Musikelektronik GmbH is a German manufacturer of audio hardware, mostly modular synthesizers, based in Gräfelfing near Munich. The company was founded in 1992 by Dieter Döpfer. Its best-known product is the A-100 modular system, introduced in 1995, whose physical format and power specifications became the widely adopted Eurorack standard used by hundreds of other manufacturers.

A Doepfer A-100 system — the origin of the Eurorack format.

History

Dieter Döpfer began designing electronic instruments in the late 1970s, producing a voice modular system and MIDI controllers such as the MAQ16/3 sequencer and the Regelwerk before launching the A-100. The system's affordability and openness — Doepfer published the mechanical and electrical specifications — helped ignite the modern modular synthesizer revival in the 2000s and 2010s.

Products

Besides A-100 modules (oscillators, filters, sequencers, and numerous utility and experimental modules, such as the A-196 phase-locked loop and the A-106-1 Xtreme filter), Doepfer makes MIDI keyboards, controllers, and DIY kits.
