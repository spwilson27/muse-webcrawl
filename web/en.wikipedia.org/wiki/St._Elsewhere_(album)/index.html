St. Elsewhere is the debut album by American duo Gnarls Barkley, formed by singer CeeLo Green and producer Danger Mouse. It was released on 24 April 2006 in the United Kingdom and on 9 May 2006 in the United States. The album is named after the 1980s television medical drama St. Elsewhere.

Background and release

The album's lead single, "Crazy", became an international sensation, reaching number one in several countries; in the United Kingdom it was the first single ever to top the chart on download sales alone. The album blends hip hop, soul, funk, and psychedelic influences, and its lyrics explore themes of mental health, fame, and mortality.

Reception

St. Elsewhere received widespread critical acclaim and won the Grammy Award for Best Alternative Music Album in 2007; "Crazy" won Best Urban/Alternative Performance. The album was certified platinum in several countries and has been ranked among the best albums of the 2000s by publications including Rolling Stone and Pitchfork.
