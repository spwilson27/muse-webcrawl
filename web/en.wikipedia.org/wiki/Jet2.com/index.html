Jet2.com Limited (styled as Jet2) is a British low-cost leisure airline and the third-largest scheduled airline in the United Kingdom, behind easyJet and British Airways. Its headquarters are at Leeds Bradford Airport, with additional bases across the UK. The airline is closely integrated with the package holiday operator Jet2holidays, together forming Jet2 plc.

History

The airline's origins lie in Channel Express, founded in 1983, which flew freight and later passenger services from Bournemouth and Leeds. In 2002 the company rebranded its passenger operations as Jet2.com, launching low-cost flights from Leeds Bradford to European destinations in February 2003. It expanded steadily across northern England, the Midlands and Scotland, retired its Boeing 757 fleet in favour of Boeing 737-800s, and placed large orders for Airbus A321neo aircraft.

Operations

Jet2.com operates mainly leisure routes to Mediterranean, Canary Islands and European city destinations from bases including Leeds Bradford, Manchester, Birmingham, London Stansted, Newcastle, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Bristol, Belfast and others. Jet2holidays became the UK's largest tour operator in 2023, overtaking TUI.
