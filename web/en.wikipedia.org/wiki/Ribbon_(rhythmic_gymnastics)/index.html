Ribbon (rhythmic gymnastics)

The ribbon is one of the apparatus used in rhythmic gymnastics, alongside the hoop, ball, clubs, and rope. It consists of a long strip of satin or similar fabric attached to a stick, which the gymnast keeps in constant flowing motion.

Specifications

Under International Gymnastics Federation (FIG) rules, the ribbon for senior gymnasts is 6 metres long (5 metres for juniors) and 4 to 6 centimetres wide, attached by a swivel to a stick of wood, bamboo, plastic, or fibreglass about 50–60 cm long. The total weight is at least 35 grams.

Elements

Routines feature spirals, snakes, circles, figures of eight, throws and catches of the stick, and "boomerang" throws in which the gymnast pulls the ribbon back by its end. The ribbon must never touch the floor stationary or become knotted, and the pattern it traces in the air is judged for fluidity and amplitude.
