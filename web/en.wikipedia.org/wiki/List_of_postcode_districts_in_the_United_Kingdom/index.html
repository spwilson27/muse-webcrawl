List of postcode districts in the United Kingdom

The United Kingdom is divided into 124 postcode areas, subdivided into roughly 2,900 postcode districts — the outward code before the space in a UK postcode (e.g. "SW1" in SW1A 1AA). Districts are listed within separate articles for each postcode area.

Structure

A postcode area is identified by one or two letters referring to a city or region (E for east London, EH for Edinburgh, BT for all of Northern Ireland). Districts are numbered, not always consecutively or geographically, and some areas have alphanumeric districts such as London's EC1–EC4 and WC1–WC2.

Examples of areas

  • London — E, EC, N, NW, SE, SW, W, WC
  • Birmingham — B (over 70 districts)
  • Glasgow — G; Edinburgh — EH; Cardiff — CF; Belfast — BT
  • Manchester — M; Leeds — LS; Sheffield — S; Liverpool — L
  • Non-geographic and special codes include GIR (Girobank) and SAN TA1 for Father Christmas letters.

History

Postal districts began in London in 1857; the modern national postcode system was rolled out from 1959 (Norwich trial) and completed in 1974. Royal Mail maintains the Postcode Address File.
