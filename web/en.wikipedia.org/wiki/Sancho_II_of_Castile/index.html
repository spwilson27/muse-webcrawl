Sancho II (c. 1036/1038 – 7 October 1072), called the Strong (el Fuerte), was King of Castile from 1065 and, briefly in 1072, of León and Galicia as well. He was the eldest son of Ferdinand I of León, whose death divided his realms among his children.

Reign and wars

Determined to reassemble his father's inheritance, Sancho first fought his brother García of Galicia, seizing Galicia in 1071 with the help of his alférez Rodrigo Díaz de Vivar (El Cid), and then turned on his brother Alfonso VI of León, defeating and exiling him in 1072. His sister Urraca held the city of Zamora against him.

Death

While besieging Zamora, Sancho was assassinated by the nobleman Vellido Dolfos, who struck him down outside the walls in October 1072. Alfonso returned from exile and succeeded to all three crowns. Sancho's dramatic reign and death became a rich subject of Spanish epic and ballad tradition.
