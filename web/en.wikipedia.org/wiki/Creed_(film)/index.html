Creed (film)

Directed by: Ryan Coogler
Starring: Michael B. Jordan, Sylvester Stallone, Tessa Thompson
Release date: November 25, 2015
Budget: $35–40 million
Box office: $173.6 million

Creed is a 2015 American sports drama film directed by Ryan Coogler, who co-wrote the screenplay with Aaron Covington. It is both a spin-off of and sequel to the Rocky film series, and stars Michael B. Jordan as Adonis Creed, the son of boxer Apollo Creed, with Sylvester Stallone reprising his role as Rocky Balboa. Tessa Thompson and Phylicia Rashad also star.

Plot

Adonis Johnson, the son born from an extramarital affair of the late former heavyweight champion Apollo Creed, is raised by Creed's widow, Mary Anne. Though he builds a successful career in finance, Adonis quits his job to pursue boxing full-time. He travels to Philadelphia to persuade his father's old rival and friend, Rocky Balboa, to train him. Rocky, now running a restaurant and initially reluctant, agrees. Adonis, fighting under the name "Donnie Creed", rises through the ranks while hiding his parentage; when his identity is revealed, he accepts a title fight against British light-heavyweight champion Ricky Conlan. Rocky is diagnosed with non-Hodgkin's lymphoma and undergoes treatment as he corners Adonis, who loses a split decision but gains the respect of the crowd and his opponent.

Reception

Creed premiered in Los Angeles on November 19, 2015, and was released in the United States on November 25, the 40th anniversary of the opening scene of the original Rocky. The film was praised as a return to form for the franchise. Stallone won the Golden Globe Award for Best Supporting Actor and was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor. Sequels Creed II (2018) and Creed III (2023) followed.
