Joe Flacco

Joseph Vincent Flacco (born January 16, 1985) is an American football quarterback. He spent most of his career with the Baltimore Ravens, whom he led to victory in Super Bowl XLVII, winning the game's Most Valuable Player award.

Career

Flacco played college football at Pittsburgh and Delaware and was drafted by Baltimore in the first round (18th overall) of the 2008 NFL Draft. He became the Ravens' starter as a rookie and guided the team to the playoffs in each of his first five seasons. In the 2012 postseason he threw 11 touchdowns without an interception, culminating in the Super Bowl win over the San Francisco 49ers.

Later career

Flacco was traded to the Denver Broncos in 2019 and later played for the New York Jets, Philadelphia Eagles, Cleveland Browns — earning NFL Comeback Player of the Year honours in 2023 — and the Indianapolis Colts, valued late in his career as a veteran starter and backup.
