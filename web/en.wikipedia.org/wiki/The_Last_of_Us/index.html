The Last of Us

The Last of Us is a 2013 action-adventure game developed by Naughty Dog and published by Sony Computer Entertainment for the PlayStation 3. Players control Joel, a smuggler tasked with escorting a teenage girl, Ellie, across a post-apocalyptic United States devastated by a mutated Cordyceps fungus that turns its hosts into aggressive creatures.

Plot

Twenty years after the outbreak kills his daughter Sarah and collapses civilization, Joel is hired in Boston to take 14-year-old Ellie — who is immune to the infection — to the Fireflies, a militia group hoping to make a cure. Their cross-country journey through Pittsburgh, Wyoming, and Colorado forges a father-daughter bond. Reaching the Fireflies' hospital in Salt Lake City, Joel learns a cure requires surgery that will kill Ellie; he storms the hospital, kills the surgeons, and escapes, lying to Ellie that the Fireflies had stopped searching for a cure.

Reception and legacy

The game was acclaimed for its narrative, characterization, and performances by Troy Baker and Ashley Johnson, and won numerous Game of the Year awards; it is regarded as one of the greatest video games ever made. It sold over 17 million copies across its original and remastered versions. A sequel, The Last of Us Part II, was released in 2020, and a television adaptation starring Pedro Pascal and Bella Ramsey premiered on HBO in 2023.
