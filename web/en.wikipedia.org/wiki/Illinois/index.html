Illinois is a state in the Midwestern United States. It borders Lake Michigan to the northeast, Indiana to the east, Kentucky to the south, Missouri to the west, and Iowa and Wisconsin to the northwest. Its capital is Springfield, in the center of the state, while its largest city — and the third-most populous in the United States — is Chicago, on the southwestern shore of Lake Michigan. With about 12.8 million residents, Illinois is the sixth-most populous U.S. state and the most populous in the Midwest.

History

The region was home to Mississippian culture cities such as Cahokia, the largest pre-Columbian settlement north of Mexico. French explorers and traders arrived in the 17th century, and the area formed part of New France, then passed to Britain and, after the American Revolutionary War, to the United States as part of the Northwest Territory. Illinois became the 21st state on December 3, 1818. Abraham Lincoln, who lived in Springfield and served in the state legislature, gives Illinois its nickname, "Land of Lincoln". The state remained in the Union during the Civil War and contributed more than 250,000 soldiers.

Geography

Most of Illinois is flat prairie shaped by Ice Age glaciation, part of the Central Lowland. The highest natural point is Charles Mound (1,235 ft / 376 m) in the unglaciated Driftless Area of the northwest. Major rivers include the Mississippi, Ohio, Wabash, and Illinois rivers. The state is conventionally divided into Northern Illinois, dominated by the Chicago metropolitan area; Central Illinois, largely agricultural; and Southern Illinois ("Little Egypt"), hillier and more forested, including the Shawnee National Forest.

Economy

Illinois has one of the most diverse state economies in the country. Chicago is a global financial center, home to the Chicago Mercantile Exchange and a major transportation hub anchored by O'Hare International Airport and an unrivaled rail network. Downstate, Illinois is a leading producer of corn and soybeans, and manufacturing, food processing, machinery, and energy production are significant statewide.
