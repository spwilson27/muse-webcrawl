Predictive policing


A crime-mapping heat map

Predictive policing is the use of analytical techniques — including statistical models, machine learning and geographic information systems — to identify likely targets, places and times for police intervention and to prevent crime or solve past crimes.

Methods

Approaches range from hotspot mapping of historical incident data to risk terrain modelling and person-based forecasting tools such as PredPol (now Geolitica) and Chicago's Strategic Subject List.

Criticism

Critics argue that training models on historical arrest data can entrench racial and socioeconomic bias, creating feedback loops in which already over-policed neighbourhoods receive still more patrols. Several US cities have restricted or abandoned person-based predictive tools, and the EU AI Act classifies certain predictive policing systems as unacceptable or high-risk.
