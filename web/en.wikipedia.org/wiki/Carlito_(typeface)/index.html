Carlito is a humanist sans-serif typeface designed by Łukasz Dziedzic of the Warsaw-based foundry tyPoland. It was commissioned by Google as a metric-compatible substitute for Microsoft's Calibri, the default font of Microsoft Office from 2007 onward, and is distributed under the SIL Open Font License.

Design and use

Because Carlito shares Calibri's character widths and general proportions, documents formatted in Calibri can be rendered in Carlito without reflowing text — an important property for cross-platform document exchange. It is a core part of Google's ChromeOS font set, alongside Caladea (the metric match for Cambria). Carlito ships with many Linux distributions and with the LibreOffice suite, where it serves as the fallback when Calibri is unavailable.

Styles

The family includes regular, bold, italic and bold italic styles. Its letterforms follow Calibri's soft, rounded humanist model closely, with open apertures and a moderately large x-height suited to on-screen reading at small sizes.
