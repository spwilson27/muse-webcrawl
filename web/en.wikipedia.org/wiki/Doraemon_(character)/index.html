Doraemon (character)

Doraemon (Japanese: ドラえもん) is a fictional character in the Japanese manga and anime series of the same name created by Fujiko Fujio. He is a male robotic cat from the 22nd century who travels back in time to aid a preteen boy named Nobita Nobi. An "official" birthdate for the character was proffered as 3 September 2112, the day a robot cat was built at a factory. Doraemon's appearance is that of a blue, earless cat-like robot with a round body, a bell on his collar, and a four-dimensional pocket on his abdomen from which he produces futuristic gadgets.

Characteristics

Doraemon is sent back in time by Sewashi Nobi, Nobita's great-great-grandson, to improve the circumstances of his ancestor so that his descendants may enjoy a better future. Doraemon's ears were eaten by robotic mice, which left him with a lasting fear of mice, and his original yellow paint faded to blue after he cried over the loss. He is fond of dorayaki, a Japanese confection, and his gadgets — including the Anywhere Door, the Take-copter, and the Time Machine — drive most of the series' plots.

Reception

Doraemon is considered a cultural icon in Japan and across Asia. In 2008, Japan's Foreign Ministry appointed the character as the country's first "anime ambassador". The character appears on merchandise, in films, and in educational materials throughout the region.
