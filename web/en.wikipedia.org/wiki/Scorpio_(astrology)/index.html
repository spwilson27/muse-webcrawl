Scorpio (♏︎; Greek: Σκορπιός, Skorpiós, Latin for "scorpion") is the eighth astrological sign in the zodiac. Under the tropical zodiac, the Sun transits this sign on average from October 23 to November 22. It is a fixed water sign, grouped with Cancer and Pisces, and is associated with the constellation Scorpius.

Background

Scorpio is one of the oldest recognised constellations and signs. In Greek mythology, the scorpion was sent by Gaia (or Artemis) to kill the hunter Orion, which explains why the constellations Scorpius and Orion are on opposite sides of the sky. In Western astrology, people born under the sign are commonly described in popular astrology as intense, determined, secretive and passionate, though such characterisations have no scientific basis.

Associations

Scorpio is traditionally ruled by Mars and, in modern astrology, co-ruled by Pluto. Its symbol is the scorpion, and its glyph ♏ is said to depict the scorpion's legs and stinging tail. The sign is associated with themes of death and rebirth, transformation, shared resources, sexuality and the occult in astrological literature. Colours associated with Scorpio include deep red, maroon and black; its birthstones are commonly given as topaz and citrine (for November births).

Compatibility

In popular astrology, Scorpio is generally considered compatible with the other water signs (Cancer and Pisces) and with the earth signs (Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn), though such claims are not empirically supported.
