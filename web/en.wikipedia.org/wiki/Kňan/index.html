Kňan

Kňan (also written Ǩnan) is a toponym and surname element found in Old Norse and medieval sources. In the context of early Scandinavian geography, the name is associated with places mentioned in Icelandic sagas and land registers.

The letter ň (n with caron) reflects transliteration conventions used for certain sounds in medieval manuscripts and in scholarly editions of Old Norse texts, where special characters were introduced by editors to mark palatalised consonants.

Because attestations are sparse, references to Kňan generally appear within discussions of saga geography, place-name etymology and the editing of medieval Icelandic manuscripts.
