Jinhae-gu

Jinhae-gu (진해구) is a district of Changwon, South Gyeongsang Province, South Korea, on the country's south-east coast. Formerly the separate city of Jinhae, it was merged into Changwon in 2010. Jinhae is a major base of the Republic of Korea Navy and is nationally famous for its spring cherry blossoms.

Cherry blossom festival

Each spring Jinhae hosts the Gunhangje (Naval Port Festival), the largest cherry blossom festival in South Korea, attracting around two million visitors. The festival began in 1952 as a memorial event for Admiral Yi Sun-sin, whose statue stands in the town, and its attractions include the blossom-lined Yeojwacheon stream with its "Romance Bridge", the cherry tunnel at Gyeonghwa Station (a halt on a line opened to passengers for the festival), and the avenues around the naval academy, which opens its grounds to the public during the festival.

Naval presence

Jinhae's sheltered bay made it a naval station from the Japanese colonial period; it remains the principal base of the Republic of Korea Navy, home to the Naval Academy, the naval operations command's major facilities and the country's submarine force. The United States Navy maintains a small installation, Commander Fleet Activities Chinhae, the only U.S. Navy base on the Korean mainland. The district's population is around 190,000, and its economy is closely tied to the navy and to the industries of greater Changwon.
