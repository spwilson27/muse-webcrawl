AppearanceColourless liquid
Principal useProduction of poly(methyl methacrylate) (acrylic)

Methyl methacrylate (MMA) is an organic compound with the formula CH₂=C(CH₃)COOCH₃. This colourless liquid, the methyl ester of methacrylic acid, is a monomer produced on a large scale for the production of poly(methyl methacrylate) (PMMA), a transparent thermoplastic sold under names such as Plexiglas and Perspex.

Production

The dominant industrial route is the acetone cyanohydrin process, in which acetone and hydrogen cyanide form acetone cyanohydrin, which is treated with sulfuric acid and then methanol to give MMA. Alternative routes based on ethylene, isobutylene, or methyl propionate have been commercialized to avoid the cyanide and sulfate byproducts of the classic process.

Uses

Most MMA is polymerized to PMMA sheet and moulding resin used in glazing, signage, lighting, and automotive parts. It is also copolymerized into paints, adhesives, and coatings, and polymerizes in situ as bone cement in orthopedic surgery and as dental materials.

Safety

MMA is a volatile, flammable liquid with a sharp fruity odour. It is an irritant to skin, eyes, and respiratory tract and can cause contact sensitization; occupational exposure limits are set in many jurisdictions.
