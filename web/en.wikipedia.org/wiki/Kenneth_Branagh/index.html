Kenneth Branagh

Branagh in 2015

Sir Kenneth Charles Branagh (born 10 December 1960) is a British actor and filmmaker. Born in Belfast and raised in England, he trained at the Royal Academy of Dramatic Art and first won acclaim with the Royal Shakespeare Company before founding the Renaissance Theatre Company. He is widely credited with bringing Shakespeare to a broad film audience through his adaptations.

Shakespeare films

Branagh's directorial debut, Henry V (1989), earned him Academy Award nominations for Best Actor and Best Director. He followed it with Much Ado About Nothing (1993), Othello (1995, as Iago), an unabridged four-hour Hamlet (1996), Love's Labour's Lost (2000) and As You Like It (2006).

Other work

As a director, Branagh has made the thriller Dead Again (1991), Mary Shelley's Frankenstein (1994), the Marvel film Thor (2011), Disney's live-action Cinderella (2015), and three Hercule Poirot films — Murder on the Orient Express (2017), Death on the Nile (2022) and A Haunting in Venice (2023) — in which he also starred. His semi-autobiographical film Belfast (2021) won the Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay. On television he played the title role in the English-language Wallander series (2008–2016).

Honours

Branagh was knighted in 2012 for services to drama and to the community in Northern Ireland. He has been nominated for eight Academy Awards across acting, directing, writing and producing categories — one of the few people nominated in five different Oscar categories.
