The Spectacular Now

AuthorTim Tharp
Published2008
GenreYoung adult novel
Film adaptation2013

The Spectacular Now is a 2008 young adult novel by American author Tim Tharp. It tells the story of Sutter Keely, a charismatic, hard-partying high school senior in Oklahoma who drifts through life on charm and a spiked soft drink, and whose worldview is challenged when he wakes up one morning on the lawn of Aimee Finecky, a shy classmate he barely knows.

Plot

Sutter narrates the novel in the first person. After being dumped by his girlfriend Cassidy, he meets Aimee and, initially intending to "save" her, draws her into his social world. As the two fall in love, Sutter's fear of becoming his absent, alcoholic father collides with his philosophy of living entirely in "the spectacular now". The novel ends on an ambiguous note: after pushing Aimee toward a future in Houston, Sutter must decide whether his way of life is freedom or flight.

Reception

The novel was a 2008 National Book Award finalist in Young People's Literature and was praised for its authentic voice and unflinching depiction of adolescent alcoholism. Critics noted its debt to The Catcher in the Rye while crediting Tharp with a distinctly contemporary setting.

Film adaptation

A film adaptation directed by James Ponsoldt premiered at the 2013 Sundance Film Festival, starring Miles Teller as Sutter and Shailene Woodley as Aimee, from a screenplay by Scott Neustadter and Michael H. Weber. The film won a Special Jury Prize at Sundance and was widely praised, though it softens some of the novel's darker ambiguity.
