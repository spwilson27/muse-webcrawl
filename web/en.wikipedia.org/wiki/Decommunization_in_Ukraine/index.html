Decommunization in Ukraine

Decommunization in Ukraine is the process of removing symbols and legacies of the communist era. It began after Ukraine's independence in 1991 and accelerated dramatically during and after the 2013–14 Euromaidan protests, when crowds toppled Lenin statues across the country in the so-called "Leninopad".

In 2015 Ukraine enacted decommunization laws condemning the communist and Nazi regimes, banning their symbols, and requiring the renaming of settlements and streets bearing Soviet names. Nearly 1,000 settlements were renamed, including the city of Dnipropetrovsk, which became Dnipro. The process intensified further after Russia's full-scale invasion in 2022, expanding into broader "derussification".
