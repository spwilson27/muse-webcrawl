Goose Green

Coordinates: 51°49′44″S 58°58′08″W
Country: Falkland Islands
Island: East Falkland
Population: about 40

Goose Green is a hamlet in Lafonia on East Falkland in the Falkland Islands. It lies on Choiseul Sound, on the east side of the island's central isthmus, 2 miles (3.2 km) south-southwest of Darwin. With a population of about 40, it is the third-largest settlement of the Falklands, after Stanley and Mount Pleasant.

The local area is called "Camp" — from the Spanish word campo, meaning countryside. Goose Green farm extends over some 170,000 acres (690 km²) and is one of the largest sheep farms in the islands, with around 170,000 sheep. The settlement has a large sheep-shearing shed and a shop.

History

Goose Green was established as a tallow factory in 1875. During the early twentieth century many of the local farms were owned by the Falkland Islands Company, and Goose Green grew as a centre for sheep farming in Lafonia.

Falklands War

Goose Green is best known as the site of the first major land battle of the Falklands War in 1982. Argentine forces occupied the settlement and held more than one hundred islanders confined in the community hall for nearly a month. On 28–29 May 1982, the 2nd Battalion, the Parachute Regiment (2 Para), under the command of Lieutenant Colonel H. Jones, attacked and defeated a larger Argentine garrison in the Battle of Goose Green.

Lt Col Jones was killed leading an attack on an Argentine trench and was posthumously awarded the Victoria Cross. Eighteen British and approximately fifty Argentine servicemen were killed in the battle. After the Argentine surrender, the imprisoned islanders were freed. The settlement hall still bears the marks of the occupation, and there are memorials to the fallen of both sides nearby.
