Brahma Sutras

The Brahma Sutras (Sanskrit: ब्रह्म सूत्र), also known as the Vedanta Sutras (वेदान्त सूत्र), is a Sanskrit text attributed to the sage Badarayana. The text systematizes and summarizes the philosophical and spiritual ideas in the Upanishads. It is one of the foundational texts of the Vedānta school of Hindu philosophy, along with the principal Upanishads and the Bhagavad Gita — together the Prasthanatrayi.

Structure

The Brahma Sutras consist of 555 aphorisms (sutras) arranged in four chapters (adhyaya), each subdivided into four sections (pada). The sutras are terse and open to interpretation, and each major Vedanta sub-school has produced its own commentary (bhashya).

Commentaries

Famous commentaries include those of Adi Shankara (Advaita), Ramanuja (Vishishtadvaita), Madhvacharya (Dvaita), and Vallabha (Shuddhadvaita). The differences among these commentaries are a principal reason for the divergences among Vedanta sub-traditions.
