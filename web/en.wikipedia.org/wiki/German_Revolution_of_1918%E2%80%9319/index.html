German Revolution of 1918–19

Date29 October 1918 – August 1919
LocationGerman Empire
ResultAbolition of the monarchy; Weimar Republic established

The German Revolution of 1918–19 was the civil conflict that ended the German Empire and established a parliamentary republic. Beginning with the Kiel mutiny of sailors in late October 1918, workers' and soldiers' councils spread across Germany.

Course

On 9 November 1918 the republic was proclaimed in Berlin and Kaiser Wilhelm II abdicated. The majority Social Democrats under Friedrich Ebert formed a provisional government, while the Spartacus League under Karl Liebknecht and Rosa Luxemburg sought a socialist council republic. Their January 1919 uprising was crushed by Freikorps units; Liebknecht and Luxemburg were murdered.

Outcome

Elections in January 1919 produced a National Assembly that met in Weimar and adopted a democratic constitution in August 1919, founding the Weimar Republic.
