The Equalizer (film)

The Equalizer is a 2014 American vigilante action thriller film directed by Antoine Fuqua, loosely based on the 1980s television series of the same name. It stars Denzel Washington as Robert McCall, a retired US Marine and former DIA officer who uses his skills to protect a young woman from Russian gangsters.

Plot

McCall lives quietly in Boston, working at a home-improvement store. When Teri, a teenage prostitute he has befriended, is beaten by her Russian mafia handlers, McCall kills the gangsters, provoking retaliation from a brutal syndicate enforcer. McCall methodically destroys the local Russian operation and travels to Moscow to eliminate its leader.

Sequels

The film was a commercial success and spawned two sequels, The Equalizer 2 (2018) and The Equalizer 3 (2023), both also directed by Fuqua and starring Washington.
