Proceedings

In academia and librarianship, proceedings are the published collection of papers presented at an academic conference, symposium or workshop. Conference proceedings are a major channel of scholarly communication, particularly in computer science and engineering, where leading conferences publish peer-reviewed proceedings that are cited like journal articles.

Publishing

Proceedings may be published as a book or volume by the organising society, a university press, or a commercial publisher such as Springer (Lecture Notes in Computer Science), the ACM, or IEEE. Papers may appear before the event (pre-proceedings), at the event, or afterwards, sometimes in expanded journal versions. Proceedings are typically identified by an ISBN or ISSN and indexed in bibliographic databases; their citation style usually includes the conference name, location, date and page range.

Other meanings

The word also denotes the official record of the meetings of a learned society or legislature, and in law, the course of a judicial action ("legal proceedings") and the record of steps taken in a case.
