Archer Daniels Midland

Archer-Daniels-Midland Company, commonly known as ADM, is an American multinational food processing and commodities trading corporation headquartered in Chicago, Illinois. It is one of the world's largest agricultural processors and food ingredient providers, part of the "ABCD" group of major grain trading companies.

Founded: 1902
Headquarters: Chicago, Illinois, U.S.
Industry: Food processing, commodities
Products: Vegetable oils, corn sweeteners, flour, animal feed, biofuels

Operations

ADM processes oilseeds, corn, wheat, and other agricultural commodities into products used in food, beverages, animal feed, industrial applications, and biofuels such as ethanol and biodiesel. It operates hundreds of processing plants and procurement facilities worldwide and runs a major transportation network of trucks, railcars, barges, and vessels.

History

The company traces its roots to the Daniels Linseed Company founded in 1902; it took its present name in 1923 after acquiring Midland Linseed Products. In the 1990s ADM was involved in a major lysine price-fixing case that resulted in large fines and criminal convictions of several executives.
