Bob Kersee is an American track and field coach widely regarded as one of the most successful in the history of the sport. He coached his wife, Jackie Joyner-Kersee, considered by many the greatest female athlete of all time, to six Olympic medals in the heptathlon and long jump.

Career

Kersee coached at UCLA before building a roster of elite athletes that has included sprinter and hurdler Gail Devers, Olympic 400 m champion Valerie Brisco-Hooks, 100 m hurdles champion Dawn Harper-Nelson, Sydney McLaughlin-Levrone, Athing Mu, and Allyson Felix, the most decorated American track athlete in Olympic history. His athletes have won dozens of Olympic and World Championship medals.

Coaching style

Known for meticulous periodised training and attention to sprint mechanics, Kersee has remained a central figure in United States sprinting and hurdling for more than four decades.
