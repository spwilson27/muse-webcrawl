Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS and Super Smash Bros. for Wii U are a pair of 2014 crossover fighting games developed by Bandai Namco Games and Sora Ltd. and published by Nintendo. Collectively the fourth instalment of the Super Smash Bros. series, the twin versions share a roster of 58 characters (including downloadable content) but differ in stages and modes: the 3DS game features stages from handheld titles and the Smash Run mode, while the Wii U game offers stages from console titles, eight-player battles, and the Smash Tour board game mode.

Development

Series creator Masahiro Sakurai returned as director, with Namco Bandai handling most development. Newcomers included Mega Man, Pac-Man, the Wii Fit Trainer, Rosalina & Luma, Greninja and the Mii Fighters. Post-launch DLC brought back Mewtwo, Lucas and Roy and added Ryu, Cloud Strife, Corrin and Bayonetta.

Reception

Both versions were acclaimed; the Wii U version is frequently ranked among the platform's best games, while the 3DS version sold over nine million copies despite hardware constraints. Combined sales exceeded 14 million. The games were a major focus of competitive play until succeeded by Super Smash Bros. Ultimate in 2018.
