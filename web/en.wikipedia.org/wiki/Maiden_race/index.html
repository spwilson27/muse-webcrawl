Maiden race

A maiden race is a horse, dog or other racing event for runners that have never won a race. Races can be for juveniles (two-year-olds) or for older animals. The winner is said to "break its maiden".

Horse racing

In thoroughbred horse racing, maiden races are a common class of event, divided into maiden claiming races (where each horse may be claimed, i.e. bought, for a set price) and the more prestigious maiden special weight races. Horses may compete in many maiden races before winning; a horse that remains a maiden after numerous starts is considered of limited racing ability, though some later become successful at higher levels. Winning horses graduate to allowance or claiming company.
