Rivers of Ireland

Longest riverRiver Shannon, 360.5 km
Largest basinShannon, 16,865 km²

The rivers of Ireland drain an island whose central lowland plain is ringed by coastal mountains. Because of the island's topography, most rivers are relatively short. The longest river in Ireland is the River Shannon, at 360.5 kilometres (224 mi), which also has the largest drainage basin of any river in Ireland or Britain.

The Three Sisters

The Barrow, Nore and Suir are known as the Three Sisters; the Nore and Suir join the Barrow before it enters the sea at Waterford Harbour. The Shannon flows through a chain of large lakes — Lough Allen, Lough Ree and Lough Derg — and is managed for hydroelectric power at Ardnacrusha.
