Prague offensive

Date6–11 May 1945
LocationPrague, Protectorate of Bohemia and Moravia
ResultSoviet/Czechoslovak victory

Red Army troops entering Prague, May 1945

The Prague offensive (Russian: Пражская наступательная операция) was the last major military operation of World War II in Europe, fought from 6 to 11 May 1945. Forces of the Soviet 1st, 2nd and 4th Ukrainian Fronts, assisted by the Prague uprising and Czechoslovak and Romanian units, liberated Prague and destroyed the German Army Group Centre.

Course of the operation

Launched after the fall of Berlin, the offensive converged on Prague from the north, east and south. The Czech resistance had risen in the city on 5 May, and Soviet spearheads entered Prague on 9 May. The last German resistance east of the city ended by 11–12 May, several days after Germany's formal surrender.

Significance

The operation ended organised German resistance in Czechoslovakia and was the final large-scale offensive of the European war.
