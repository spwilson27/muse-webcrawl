Gulbenkian Orchestra

The Gulbenkian Orchestra (Portuguese: Orquestra Gulbenkian) is a Portuguese orchestra based in Lisbon, at the headquarters of the Calouste Gulbenkian Foundation. Founded in 1962 as a chamber orchestra of twelve musicians, it expanded over the decades into a full symphony orchestra of around sixty players.

The orchestra performs its main concert season in the Grand Auditorium of the Gulbenkian Foundation and tours internationally. It has worked with many distinguished conductors and soloists and has made numerous recordings. The Swiss conductor Lawrence Foster was its music director and principal conductor from 2002 to 2013; Lorenzo Viotti held the post from 2018 to 2021, followed by Hannu Lintu from 2023.

Alongside the orchestra, the Foundation also maintains the Gulbenkian Choir, founded in 1964.
