Aubrey Drake Graham (born October 24, 1986), known mononymously as Drake, is a Canadian rapper, singer and actor. Regarded as one of the most influential figures in popular music, he is credited with popularising melodic singing in hip hop and with bringing Toronto's music scene to international prominence. His signature blend of rapping and crooning, confessional lyrics and atmospheric production made him one of the best-selling music artists of all time.

Early life and acting career

Graham was born in Toronto to an African-American father, Dennis Graham, a drummer from Memphis, and a Canadian Jewish mother, Sandra, who worked as an educator and florist. As a teenager he gained fame playing Jimmy Brooks, a basketball player who becomes paraplegic after a school shooting, on the teen drama series Degrassi: The Next Generation (2001–2008).

Music career

Drake self-released the mixtapes Room for Improvement (2006) and Comeback Season (2007) before the breakthrough mixtape So Far Gone (2009), whose singles "Best I Ever Had" and "Successful" led to a contract with Lil Wayne's Young Money Entertainment. His debut studio album Thank Me Later (2010) debuted at number one in the United States. Take Care (2011) won the Grammy Award for Best Rap Album, and Nothing Was the Same (2013) consolidated his commercial dominance. In 2015 he released the mixtape If You're Reading This It's Too Late and a collaborative mixtape with Future, What a Time to Be Alive.

Views (2016) spent 13 weeks atop the Billboard 200 and contained the global hit "One Dance", the first song to reach one billion streams on Spotify. The playlist project More Life (2017) and the double album Scorpion (2018) followed; Scorpion spawned the number-one singles "God's Plan", "Nice for What" and "In My Feelings". Later releases include Certified Lover Boy (2021), Honestly, Nevermind (2022), Her Loss (2022, with 21 Savage) and For All the Dogs (2023). In 2024 his widely publicised feud with Kendrick Lamar produced a series of diss tracks and further chart records.

Business and legacy

Drake co-founded the record label and lifestyle brand OVO (October's Very Own) and its annual OVO Fest in Toronto. He has served as a global ambassador for the Toronto Raptors of the NBA. Among the best-selling digital singles artists in history, he holds numerous Billboard records, including the most charted songs of any artist on the Hot 100 and the most simultaneous top-ten singles.
