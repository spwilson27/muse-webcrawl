The BioLogos Foundation is a Christian advocacy group that supports the view that God created the world using the process of evolution — a position it calls evolutionary creation, broadly known as theistic evolution. BioLogos argues that science and evangelical Christian faith are compatible and that the scientific consensus on evolution and the age of the Earth should be accepted rather than opposed.

History

BioLogos was founded in 2007 by geneticist Francis Collins, director of the Human Genome Project, following the success of his book The Language of God (2006). It began as a website answering questions about science and faith, growing into a foundation with staff, conferences, educational resources (including the INTEGRATE curriculum for Christian schools), and a popular podcast, The Language of God.

Positions and reception

BioLogos affirms core Christian doctrines, including the resurrection and miracles, while rejecting a literal reading of Genesis as science and arguing that Adam and Eve can be understood in several models consistent with population genetics. It has drawn support from figures such as pastor Tim Keller and N. T. Wright, and criticism from young-earth creationist organisations such as Answers in Genesis and from advocates of intelligent design.
