Cossacks (video game series)

Cossacks is a series of real-time strategy video games developed by the Ukrainian studio GSC Game World. The series is set in Europe between the 17th and 18th centuries and is noted for its very large-scale battles, in which thousands of soldiers can appear on screen at once.

Games

The series began with Cossacks: European Wars (2000), followed by the expansions The Art of War and Back to War. Cossacks II: Napoleonic Wars appeared in 2005, shifting the focus to the Napoleonic era, and Cossacks 3, a remake of the original game, was released in 2016.

Gameplay

Players gather resources such as food, wood, stone, gold, iron and coal, construct towns, and raise armies of infantry, cavalry and artillery. The games emphasise formations, morale and terrain, and historical campaigns retell conflicts such as the English Civil War and the wars of Louis XIV.
