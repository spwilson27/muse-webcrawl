Todd Howard

Todd Howard
Born: 1970
Occupation: Video game designer
Employer: Bethesda Game Studios

Todd Andrew Howard (born 1970) is an American video game designer, director, and producer. He serves as director and executive producer at Bethesda Game Studios, where he has overseen the development of the Fallout and The Elder Scrolls series.

Career

Howard joined Bethesda Softworks in 1994. His early work included producing and designing The Terminator: Future Shock and work on The Elder Scrolls II: Daggerfall. He was project leader on The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind (2002), game director of The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion (2006) and The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim (2011), and director of Fallout 3 (2008), Fallout 4 (2015), and the space role-playing game Starfield (2023).

Recognition

Howard is one of few developers to have directed multiple games named Game of the Year by major outlets, and Skyrim became one of the best-selling video games of all time. He was inducted into the Academy of Interactive Arts and Sciences Hall of Fame in 2017. His frequent appearances at Bethesda presentations have made him a prominent public face of the studio.
