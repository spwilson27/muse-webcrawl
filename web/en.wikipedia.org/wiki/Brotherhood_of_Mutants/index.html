The Brotherhood of Mutants (originally known as the Brotherhood of Evil Mutants) is a fictional group of superpowered mutants appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. The group is devoted to mutant superiority over normal humans and is the principal adversary of the X-Men. Its members have included some of Marvel's most prominent mutant characters.

Publication history

The Brotherhood was created by writer Stan Lee and artist/co-writer Jack Kirby and first appeared in The X-Men #4 (March 1964). The original team was assembled by Magneto and consisted of Mastermind, Quicksilver, the Scarlet Witch and Toad. Quicksilver and the Scarlet Witch soon left to join the Avengers, and many later incarnations of the Brotherhood have formed under leaders including Mystique, Blob and Toad himself.

Ideology and role

Whereas Charles Xavier's X-Men seek peaceful coexistence between mutants and humans, the Brotherhood traditionally holds that mutants — Homo superior — should dominate or at least defend themselves by any means necessary. In later storylines, incarnations of the group have ranged from terrorist cells to freedom fighters, reflecting shifting portrayals of Magneto himself. In the Krakoan era of X-Men comics, many former Brotherhood members became citizens of the mutant nation of Krakoa.

In other media

The Brotherhood appears in the X-Men animated series and in the 20th Century Fox X-Men film series, where it is led by Magneto (played by Ian McKellen and, in prequel films, Michael Fassbender) and includes Mystique.
