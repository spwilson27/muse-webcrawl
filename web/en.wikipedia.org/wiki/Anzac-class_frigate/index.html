Anzac-class frigate

The Anzac class (also identified as the ANZAC class and the MEKO 200 ANZ type) is a ship class of ten frigates: eight operated by the Royal Australian Navy (RAN) and two by the Royal New Zealand Navy (RNZN). Based on Blohm + Voss's MEKO 200 modular design, the ships were built in Australia by Tenix Defence at Williamstown, Victoria, under the ANZAC ship project, a joint Australian–New Zealand procurement that began with an agreement in 1989.

Design and construction

The Anzac class was selected to replace the RAN's River-class destroyer escorts and to give New Zealand a modern frigate force. The modular MEKO construction allowed sections of the hull to be fabricated at multiple sites and assembled at Williamstown. The ships entered service between 1996 (HMAS Anzac) and 2006 (HMAS Perth). New Zealand's ships are HMNZS Te Kaha and HMNZS Te Mana.

Upgrades

The class was designed "fitted for but not with" several weapons, allowing later growth. Australian ships received Harpoon anti-ship missiles, Evolved Sea Sparrow missiles in vertical launchers, and the Anti-Ship Missile Defence (ASMD) upgrade, which fitted CEA phased-array radars in a distinctive mast structure and modernised combat systems. A further mid-life upgrade (AMCAP) replaced the long-range air-search radar. New Zealand's frigates underwent a Frigate Systems Upgrade in Canada, installing new combat management systems, radars, and Sea Ceptor surface-to-air missiles in place of Sea Sparrow.

Service

Anzac-class frigates have formed the backbone of both navies' surface fleets. Australian ships served repeatedly in the Persian Gulf and Middle East, in East Timor, and in border-protection and Pacific deployments; HMAS Anzac fired in support of coalition forces during the 2003 Iraq War, the first RAN warship to fire in anger in decades. New Zealand ships have deployed to the Gulf, counter-piracy operations, and Pacific assistance missions, including disaster relief.

Ships of the class

  • RAN: Anzac, Arunta, Warramunga, Stuart, Parramatta, Ballarat, Toowoomba, Perth
  • RNZN: Te Kaha, Te Mana

The class is planned to be progressively replaced in Australian service by the Hunter-class frigates, with Anzac-class ships retiring from the 2020s onward.
