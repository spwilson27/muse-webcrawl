Charleroi is a city and municipality in the province of Hainaut, Wallonia, Belgium. It is the largest city in Wallonia by metropolitan population and a historic centre of coal mining, steel and glass production in the Sambre valley.

History

The modern city began as a Spanish fortress founded in 1666 and named after King Charles II of Spain. In the 19th century Charleroi grew rapidly with the Industrial Revolution, becoming a core of Belgium's industrial sillon. The belfry of its town hall is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Transport and culture

Brussels South Charleroi Airport, Belgium's second-busiest airport, serves the city. Charleroi is home to the Musée de la Photographie, one of Europe's largest photography museums.
