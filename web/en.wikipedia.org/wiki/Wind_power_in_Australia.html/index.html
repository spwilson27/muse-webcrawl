Wind power in Australia

Wind power in Australia is a significant and growing source of renewable electricity. By the early 2020s wind farms supplied roughly a tenth or more of Australia's total electricity generation, with the strongest resources in South Australia, Victoria, New South Wales, Western Australia and Tasmania.

History

Australia's first commercial wind farm, at Salmon Beach near Esperance in Western Australia, began operating in 1987. Growth accelerated in the 2000s under the Renewable Energy Target scheme. South Australia became a world leader in wind penetration, at times meeting more than its entire demand from wind generation.

Major wind farms

Large projects include Macarthur (420 MW) in Victoria, Stockyard Hill, Snowtown in South Australia, and the Coopers Gap wind farm in Queensland. Offshore wind was enabled by legislation in 2021, with the Gippsland coast off Victoria declared the first offshore wind zone.
