The Departed

The Departed is a 2006 American epic crime thriller film directed by Martin Scorsese and written by William Monahan. It is a remake of the 2002 Hong Kong film Infernal Affairs and is also loosely based on the real-life Boston Winter Hill Gang. The film stars Leonardo DiCaprio, Matt Damon, Jack Nicholson, and Mark Wahlberg.

Plot

In South Boston, Irish mob boss Frank Costello (Nicholson) plants Colin Sullivan (Damon) as a mole inside the Massachusetts State Police, while the police assign Billy Costigan (DiCaprio) to infiltrate Costello's crew. Over the years both men become deeply embedded in their opposing organisations, and each side grows aware that it harbours a traitor; the two moles are set searching for each other — and for themselves. The hunt ends in a cascade of violence in which nearly all the principal characters are killed.

Reception and awards

The film was a critical and commercial success. At the 79th Academy Awards it won four Oscars: Best Picture, Best Director (Scorsese's first, after seven previous nominations), Best Adapted Screenplay, and Best Film Editing. It is regularly ranked among Scorsese's finest films and among the best films of the 2000s.
