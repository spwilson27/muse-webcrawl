Great Dome (MIT)

The Great Dome is the large domed structure crowning Building 10 of the Massachusetts Institute of Technology in Cambridge, Massachusetts, overlooking Killian Court and the Charles River. Designed by architect William Welles Bosworth as the centerpiece of MIT's Cambridge campus (dedicated in 1916), the dome is modeled on the Pantheon in Rome and on McKim, Mead & White's Low Memorial Library at Columbia.

Barker Library and hacks

Beneath the dome sits the Barker Engineering Library, whose reading room lies beneath a large oculus. The dome is a favored target of MIT student hacks: pranksters have placed a campus police car, a model of the Apollo Lunar Module, an upside-down lounge and giant items such as a huge slide rule atop it, and in 2019 students lit the dome as the Avengers logo following the death of Marvel creator Stan Lee. The smaller "Small Dome" of the adjacent MacLaurin Buildings mirrors its form.
