Small Axe is a British anthology film series created and directed by Steve McQueen. Consisting of five films, it premiered on BBC One in the United Kingdom in November 2020 and was released on Amazon Prime Video in the United States. Set between the late 1960s and the mid-1980s, each film tells a distinct story about London's West Indian community and their experiences of racism, policing, education, and joy. The title is taken from a Jamaican proverb popularised by Bob Marley's song "Small Axe": "If you are the big tree, we are the small axe".

Films

Mangrove recounts the trial of the Mangrove Nine, Black activists tried at the Old Bailey in 1971 after campaigning against police harassment of the Mangrove restaurant in Notting Hill; it starred Letitia Wright and Shaun Parkes. Lovers Rock, the only fictional story, follows a reggae house party in 1980 and was widely acclaimed for its depiction of Black joy and music. Red, White and Blue dramatises the story of Leroy Logan (played by John Boyega), a forensic scientist who joined the Metropolitan Police to reform it from within. Alex Wheatle follows the novelist's youth through the 1981 Brixton uprising. Education depicts the scandal of Black children being shunted into schools for the "educationally subnormal".

Reception

The series received near-universal critical acclaim. Lovers Rock was named the best film of 2020 by the British magazine Sight and Sound, and the anthology won multiple awards, including recognition at the BAFTAs and the Los Angeles Film Critics Association, which awarded it Best Picture as a complete work. The films were praised for recovering neglected chapters of Black British history with historical rigour and emotional warmth.
