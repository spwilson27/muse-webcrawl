Au Bon Pain

Au Bon Pain is an American chain of bakery cafés founded in Boston, Massachusetts, in 1981 by Louis Kane and Louis Rapuano. Its name is French for "at the good bread" (or "to the good bread").

History

The chain grew from a concept inspired by French bakeries, initially demonstrating bread-making in its stores, and expanded across the United States, particularly in the Northeast and in locations such as hospitals, universities, airports and office buildings. It was for a time a sister brand of Panera Bread under the Panera company, which sold Au Bon Pain in 2017; the brand was subsequently reacquired and many locations were converted or closed.

Menu

Au Bon Pain serves baked goods such as croissants and muffins, along with soups, salads, sandwiches and coffee.
