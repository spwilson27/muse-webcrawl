Shelby American

Shelby American, Inc. is an American automotive manufacturer and former racing team founded by racing driver Carroll Shelby in 1962. The company is best known for producing the Shelby Cobra sports car and high-performance versions of the Ford Mustang.

History

Shelby, who had won the 24 Hours of Le Mans in 1959, combined the British AC Ace chassis with Ford V8 engines to create the Cobra in 1962. Shelby American went on to build the Daytona Coupe and, in partnership with Ford, campaigned the Ford GT40 to victories at Le Mans in 1966 and 1967. From 1965 it produced the Shelby GT350 and GT500 Mustangs.

In later decades the company produced performance packages and complete vehicles, including continued-production Cobras and, under Shelby American's modern operations in Las Vegas, Nevada, modified Mustangs such as the Super Snake and Shelby GT.
