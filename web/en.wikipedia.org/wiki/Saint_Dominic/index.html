Saint Dominic

Saint Dominic (Spanish: Santo Domingo; born Domingo Félix de Guzmán, 8 August 1170 – 6 August 1221) was a Castilian Catholic priest and founder of the Dominican Order. He is the patron saint of astronomers and of the Dominican Republic. Dominic is celebrated for his itinerant preaching in southern France against the Cathar heresy and for establishing an order of friars devoted to study and preaching.

Life

Dominic was born at Caleruega in Castile and educated at the schools of Palencia, where he studied arts and theology. He joined the cathedral chapter of Osma as a canon regular. In 1203–1204 he accompanied his bishop, Diego de Acebo, on a diplomatic mission to Denmark, and while passing through southern France he encountered the Cathars of Languedoc. Convinced that the heresy could only be met by preachers whose poverty matched that of the Cathar perfecti, he and Diego adopted an apostolic life of itinerant preaching, conducted public disputations, and, in 1206–1207, founded a convent for converted women at Prouille — the seed of the Dominican nuns.

Foundation of the Order

During the Albigensian Crusade Dominic continued preaching by persuasion rather than arms. In 1215, with the support of Bishop Fulk of Toulouse, he gathered a small community of preachers in Toulouse, and on 22 December 1216 Pope Honorius III confirmed the Order of Preachers (Ordo Praedicatorum). Dominic deliberately sent his small band out in 1217 to found priories at the great university centres of Paris, Bologna, Madrid, and Oxford, making study central to the order's life. He presided over the first general chapters at Bologna in 1220 and 1221 and died there on 6 August 1221.

Legacy

Dominic was canonized by Pope Gregory IX in 1234. His order became one of the principal intellectual forces of the medieval Church, producing figures such as Thomas Aquinas and Albertus Magnus. Tradition credits Dominic with a vision of the Virgin Mary that spread devotion to the Rosary, though the rosary's association with him is largely legendary. His emblem in art includes a star on his forehead and a dog carrying a flaming torch — a pun on Domini canes, "dogs of the Lord".
