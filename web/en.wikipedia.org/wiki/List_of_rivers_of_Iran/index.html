List of rivers of Iran

Iran is a largely arid country, and many of its rivers are seasonal or drain into inland salt lakes and desert basins rather than the sea. The only navigable river is the Karun in the south-west. River systems drain toward five outlets: the Caspian Sea, the Persian Gulf and Gulf of Oman, Lake Urmia, and the central desert basins.

Draining into the Persian Gulf and Gulf of Oman

  • Karun — 950 km; Iran's longest and largest river, joining the Shatt al-Arab at Khorramshahr; major dams for hydroelectric power
  • Karkheh — 755 km; ends in the Hawizeh Marshes on the Iraq border
  • Dez — tributary of the Karun, site of the Dez Dam
  • Jarahi, Zohreh, Mand, Mehran, Minab, Halil
  • Arvand Rud (Shatt al-Arab) — border river formed by the Tigris and Euphrates

Draining into the Caspian Sea

  • Sefid-Rud — 670 km; the largest river flowing into the Caspian from Iran, formed by the Qezel Ozan and Shah Rud
  • Haraz, Tajan, Gorgan, Atrek (Atrak), Chalus, Sardab
  • Aras — forms much of Iran's northern border with Azerbaijan and Armenia

Inland basins

  • Zayandeh Rud — 400 km; waters Isfahan before ending in the Gavkhouni salt marsh; notorious in recent decades for running dry
  • Qom River, Kashkan, Sirvan (Diyala)
  • Helmand (Hirmand) — rises in Afghanistan and ends in the Hamun lakes of Sistan
  • Harirud (Tedzhen) — along the Afghan and Turkmen borders
  • Rivers of the Lake Urmia basin: Zarrineh Rud, Simineh Rud, Talkheh Rud — feeding the shrinking salt lake
  • Kor River — ends in Lake Bakhtegan in Fars
