Mission: Impossible (1966 TV series)

Mission: Impossible
Created by: Bruce Geller
Original run: 1966–1973
Network: CBS

Mission: Impossible is an American espionage television series that aired on CBS from September 1966 to March 1973. It was revived in 1988 on ABC and later inspired a long-running film series starring Tom Cruise.

Premise

The series follows the Impossible Missions Force (IMF), a team of secret government agents led first by Daniel Briggs (Steven Hill) and then Jim Phelps (Peter Graves). Each episode opens with the team leader receiving instructions via a recorded message that ends with the famous phrase: "This message will self-destruct in five seconds."

Music

Lalo Schifrin's theme, written in an unusual 5/4 time signature, became one of the most recognisable television themes of all time.
