Rockstar North Limited is a British video game developer based in Edinburgh, Scotland. The company was founded as DMA Design in Dundee in 1984 by David Jones, and is best known as the lead developer of the Grand Theft Auto series and the puzzle-platform game Lemmings.

Rockstar North's headquarters in Edinburgh.

History

DMA Design achieved early success with the Lemmings series (1991) and released the first Grand Theft Auto in 1997, a top-down open-world action game. After a series of acquisitions, DMA became part of Take-Two Interactive's Rockstar Games label in 1999 and was renamed Rockstar North in 2002. Its Grand Theft Auto III (2001) redefined the series in 3D and helped establish the modern open-world genre; subsequent titles, including Grand Theft Auto V (2013) and contributions to Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018), rank among the most successful entertainment products ever released.
