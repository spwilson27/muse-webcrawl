Largest earthquakes by magnitude

The largest earthquakes by magnitude are all megathrust earthquakes occurring at subduction zones. The largest instrumentally recorded earthquake was the 1960 Valdivia earthquake in Chile, with a moment magnitude of 9.5.

Other giant events include the 1964 Alaska earthquake (M 9.2), the 2004 Indian Ocean earthquake off Sumatra (M 9.1–9.3), the 2011 Tōhoku earthquake in Japan (M 9.0–9.1) and the 1952 Kamchatka earthquake (M 9.0). All generated destructive tsunamis.

Estimates for pre-instrumental earthquakes, such as the 1700 Cascadia earthquake (about M 8.7–9.2) and the 1755 Lisbon earthquake, rely on historical, geological and tsunami evidence and are less certain.
