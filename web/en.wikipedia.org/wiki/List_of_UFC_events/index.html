List of UFC events

The Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC) is the largest mixed martial arts promotion in the world. Since its first event, UFC 1 in November 1993 in Denver, Colorado, the promotion has staged more than 700 events, including numbered pay-per-view events, UFC Fight Night cards, and events from The Ultimate Fighter and other series.
