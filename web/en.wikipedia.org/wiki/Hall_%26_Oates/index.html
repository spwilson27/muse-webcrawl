Hall & Oates
Origin: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, US
Years active: 1970–2024
Members: Daryl Hall, John Oates
Genres: Pop rock, blue-eyed soul, soft rock

Hall & Oates, formally Daryl Hall & John Oates, was an American pop rock duo formed in Philadelphia in 1970 by singer Daryl Hall and guitarist John Oates. Blending rock, soul, and R&B — a style they called "rock and soul" — they became the best-selling music duo of all time.

Career

After early success with "Sara Smile" (1976) and "Rich Girl" (1977), the duo reached its commercial peak in the early 1980s with a string of hits including "Kiss on My List", "Private Eyes", "I Can't Go for That (No Can Do)", "Maneater", and "Out of Touch" — six US number-one singles in total, and 29 Top 40 singles between 1974 and 1991.

Legacy and split

Hall & Oates were inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2014. In 2023 Hall filed a lawsuit and restraining order against Oates over a proposed sale of his share of their joint venture; in 2024 Hall confirmed the duo would never perform together again.
