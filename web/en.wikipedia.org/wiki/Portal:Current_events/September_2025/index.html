Portal:Current events/September 2025

This page collects the current-events entries posted on Wikipedia during September 2025, organised by day, with armed conflicts and ongoing events, politics and elections, disasters, science, sport, and deaths of notable people.

How this archive works

Entries summarised the day's major stories in a sentence or two, linking to full Wikipedia articles rather than external news sites, and were drawn from reporting by agencies and outlets such as Reuters, AP, BBC News and Al Jazeera.
