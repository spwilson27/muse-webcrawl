Springer Nature

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
[Image: Photograph of a row of Nature journal covers on a display stand]

Springer Nature is a German–British academic publishing company formed in 2015 by the merger of Springer Science+Business Media with Holtzbrinck Publishing Group's Nature Publishing Group, Palgrave Macmillan and Macmillan Education. It publishes many of science's best-known journals, including Nature — founded in 1869 — and the Scientific American magazine.

Publishing

The group's portfolio spans journals, books and databases across science, technology, medicine, humanities and social sciences, including the Springer imprint (founded in Berlin in 1842), BioMed Central, and the Nature Portfolio journals such as Nature Medicine and Nature Communications.

Open access

Springer Nature is one of the largest publishers of open-access research and has signed numerous “transformative agreements” with institutions and consortia linking subscription and open-access publishing payments.
