Crysis (video game series)

DeveloperCrytek
GenreFirst-person shooter
First releaseCrysis (2007)

Crysis is a series of first-person shooter video games developed by the German studio Crytek. The games centre on soldiers equipped with a technologically advanced "Nanosuit" that grants enhanced strength, speed, armour and cloaking abilities, fighting North Korean forces and the alien Ceph.

Games

The original Crysis (2007) was acclaimed for its graphics and became a benchmark for PC hardware, spawning the phrase "Can it run Crysis?" It was followed by the standalone expansion Crysis Warhead (2008), Crysis 2 (2011), set in New York City, and Crysis 3 (2013). A remastered trilogy was released in 2021, and a fourth main entry, Crysis 4, was announced in 2022.

Gameplay

The series is known for open-ended levels that let players switch Nanosuit modes on the fly to favour stealth, direct assault or mobility, and for highly destructible environments powered by the CryEngine.
