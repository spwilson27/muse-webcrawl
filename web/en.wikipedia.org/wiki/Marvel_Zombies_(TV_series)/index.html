Marvel Zombies (TV series)

Marvel Zombies is an American animated television miniseries created by Bryan Andrews and Zeb Wells for the streaming service Disney+, based on the Marvel Comics series of the same name. It is a spin-off of the animated series What If...? and part of the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU).

Premise

The series continues the storyline introduced in the What If...? episode "What If... Zombies?!", following a new generation of heroes as they battle an ever-spreading zombie plague across a ravaged world.

Production

The series was developed as a four-episode event with a TV-MA rating, featuring a number of returning MCU voice actors.
