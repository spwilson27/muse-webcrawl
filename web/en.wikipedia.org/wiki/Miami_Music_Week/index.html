Miami Music Week

Miami Music Week is an annual week-long series of electronic dance music events held in Miami, Florida, each March. It brings together hundreds of parties, pool sessions, label showcases and club nights across Miami Beach and downtown Miami, and culminates in the Ultra Music Festival. Hundreds of thousands of visitors attend from around the world.

History

The week grew out of the Winter Music Conference (founded in 1985), for decades the dance music industry's main networking and showcase event in South Beach. As Ultra Music Festival (founded 1999) expanded, the surrounding days filled with competing and complementary events; from around 2011 the whole period was branded "Miami Music Week" as an umbrella for the hundreds of shows staged that week. The Winter Music Conference has since been incorporated into the same week.

Events

Events range from hotel pool parties on Collins Avenue and daytime boat parties to warehouse raves in Wynwood and all-night club sessions at venues such as Space Miami. Record labels and artist collectives host branded showcases — historically including parties by labels from the worlds of house, techno, trance, drum and bass and EDM — and the week serves as a launchpad where DJs debut tracks aimed at the summer festival season.

Impact

Miami Music Week is a major contributor to the city's tourism economy and one of the most important dates in the global electronic music calendar, alongside the Amsterdam Dance Event and the Ibiza summer season.
