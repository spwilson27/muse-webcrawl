2014 Summer Youth Olympics

The 2014 Summer Youth Olympics (Chinese: 2014年夏季青年奥林匹克运动会), officially the II Summer Youth Olympic Games, were held in Nanjing, China, from 16 to 28 August 2014. Around 3,600 athletes aged 15 to 18 from 203 nations competed in 222 events across 28 sports.

II Summer Youth Olympic Games

Host city: Nanjing, China
Motto: Share the Games, Share our Dreams
Nations: 203
Athletes: ~3,600
Events: 222 in 28 sports
Dates: 16–28 August 2014

Bidding

Nanjing was elected host city at the 122nd IOC Session in Vancouver in February 2010, defeating the Polish city of Poznań by 47 votes to 42. The city had previously hosted the 2013 Asian Youth Games as a test event.

Sports programme

The programme featured 28 sports, including debut appearances for golf and rugby sevens ahead of their return to the senior Olympic programme at Rio 2016. Several events used mixed-nationality and mixed-gender teams, a hallmark of the Youth Olympic format. The Games also included cultural and educational activities for the young athletes, and showcase events in sport climbing, roller sports, skateboarding and wushu.

Medal table

Hosts China topped the medal table with 38 gold medals, followed by Russia and the United States. Mixed-NOC teams also won medals, which are credited separately.
