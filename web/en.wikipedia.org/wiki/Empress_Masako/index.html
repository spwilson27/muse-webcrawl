Empress Masako

Masako (雅子, born Masako Owada (小和田 雅子, Owada Masako); 9 December 1963) is the Empress of Japan as the wife of Emperor Naruhito. Educated at Harvard and Oxford, she was a career diplomat in the Japanese Foreign Ministry before her marriage to the then Crown Prince in 1993. She became empress consort on 1 May 2019, when Naruhito ascended the Chrysanthemum Throne following the abdication of Emperor Akihito.

Early life and career

Masako Owada was born in Toranomon, Tokyo, the eldest daughter of Hisashi Owada, a senior diplomat who later served as a judge and president of the International Court of Justice. Raised partly in Moscow and New York, she graduated from Harvard University in 1985 with a degree in economics and studied international relations at Balliol College, Oxford. In 1987 she joined the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, one of very few women in her intake, and worked on economic relations with the United States during the trade negotiations of the period.

Marriage and life as crown princess

She married Crown Prince Naruhito on 9 June 1993 after a courtship that attracted intense public interest, having initially been reluctant to give up her diplomatic career. The couple's daughter, Princess Aiko, was born in 2001. From 2004 Masako largely withdrew from public duties after being diagnosed with an adjustment disorder, attributed in part to the pressures of imperial life and the expectation that she bear a male heir; her gradual return to engagements was widely reported, and her condition improved over the following years.

As empress

As empress, Masako has accompanied Naruhito on state occasions and visits, including the state visit to the United Kingdom in 2024. Drawing on her diplomatic background, she is known for her interest in international affairs and in issues such as water resources and the welfare of children.
