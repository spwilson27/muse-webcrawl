Sultan of Oman

IncumbentHaitham bin Tariq (since 11 January 2020)
StyleHis Majesty
ResidenceAl Alam Palace, Muscat
Formation749 (Imamate); Al Bu Said dynasty from 1744
Heir apparentTheyazin bin Haitham (Crown Prince)

The Sultan of Oman is the monarch and head of state of the Sultanate of Oman, and the country's absolute ruler — combining the roles of head of state, head of government, supreme commander of the armed forces, and minister of defence, foreign affairs, and finance. The Sultan is the paramount decision-maker of the state; Oman is one of the world's few remaining absolute monarchies. The ruling house is the Al Bu Said dynasty, founded by Ahmad bin Said al-Busaidi in 1744.

History

Oman's monarchical tradition traces through the Ibadi Imamate, whose elected imams ruled the interior from the 8th century, to the coastal sultanates that later commanded a maritime empire stretching from Zanzibar and the East African coast to Gwadar in Baluchistan. Sayyid Said bin Sultan (r. 1806–1856) made Oman a major Indian Ocean power, moving his court to Zanzibar; on his death the empire was divided between his sons into the Sultanates of Zanzibar and Muscat and Oman. The modern state took shape under Sultan Said bin Taimur and was transformed after 1970 when his son Qaboos bin Said deposed him in a palace coup and launched a sweeping programme of modernization funded by oil revenues, ending the Dhofar Rebellion and building modern infrastructure, schools, and hospitals.

Succession

Under Oman's Basic Law of 1996, the Sultan is chosen from among the male descendants of Sayyid Turki bin Said; the ruling family council was to select a successor within three days of the throne falling vacant, failing which a sealed letter left by the Sultan naming his choice would be opened. When Sultan Qaboos — the Arab world's longest-serving ruler — died childless on 10 January 2020, the family council chose to open the letter, which named his cousin Haitham bin Tariq, who acceded the following day. In January 2021 Sultan Haitham amended the Basic Law to establish a formal line of succession by primogeniture and created the office of Crown Prince, held by his eldest son Theyazin bin Haitham.

Powers and role

The Sultan issues laws by royal decree, appoints the Council of Ministers and judges, commands the armed forces, and directs foreign policy — Oman being noted for a neutral, mediating diplomacy in regional disputes. The bicameral Council of Oman (the appointed State Council and the elected Consultative Council, or Majlis al-Shura) advises but cannot override the Sultan.
