Kansas Speedway is a 1.5-mile (2.4 km) tri-oval race track in Kansas City, Kansas. Built in 2001 as part of the Village West development near the interchange of Interstates 70 and 435, it hosts two NASCAR Cup Series race weekends a year and has also held IndyCar, sports car and ARCA events. The Hollywood Casino at Kansas Speedway overlooks the second turn.

History

The track opened in June 2001 with NASCAR's secondary series and held its first Cup Series race, the Protection One 400, that autumn, won by Jeff Gordon. A major 2012 reconfiguration rebanked the turns with progressive banking of 17–20 degrees and added an infield road course. Since 2011 its autumn Cup race has been named the Hollywood Casino 400, and its spring race the AdventHealth 400.
