Skaro is a fictional planet in the British science fiction television series Doctor Who. Created by writer Terry Nation, it is the home planet of the Daleks and of their creator Davros, and the setting of the Doctor's first encounter with the Daleks in the 1963–64 serial The Daleks.

Description

Skaro is depicted as a largely desolate world of petrified forests, irradiated deserts, and swamps inhabited by mutated creatures — the legacy of the Thousand Year War between its two humanoid peoples, the Kaleds and the Thals. Beneath a scarred surface lies the Kaled city, a great domed bunker in which the scientist Davros engineered the Dalek mutants. The planet's Dalek city, first seen in The Daleks, draws power from static electricity conducted through its metal floors.

History within the series

Skaro's history is revisited across the series: "Genesis of the Daleks" (1975) shows the end of the Kaled–Thal war and Davros's betrayal by his own creations; "Destiny of the Daleks" and "Remembrance of the Daleks" return to the planet, the latter ending (in the Seventh Doctor's account) with Skaro's sun going supernova. Yet Skaro reappears in later stories — apparently restored or duplicated — including "Asylum of the Daleks" (2012) and "The Magician's Apprentice" (2015), in which the Twelfth Doctor finds Davros dying among the planet's ruins and witnesses its rebuilding into a new Dalek capital.

Significance

Skaro's ruined landscapes visualised post-nuclear anxieties of the early 1960s, and the planet remains the symbolic centre of the Dalek mythos — the place where, in the Doctor's phrase, the creatures learned to hate.
