List of indoor arenas

The List of indoor arenas catalogues enclosed sports and entertainment venues around the world and in the United States. Indoor arenas host ice hockey, basketball, indoor soccer, boxing, and concerts, and range from small university field houses to mega-arenas seating more than 50,000.

Organisation

The full list is divided by country and, for the United States, by league and state. Many venues are multipurpose, reconfiguring between ice, court, and concert layouts. Arena design evolved dramatically over the twentieth century with the advent of cantilevered roofs, luxury suites, and HD scoreboards.
