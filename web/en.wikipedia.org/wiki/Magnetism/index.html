Magnetism is a class of physical phenomena mediated by magnetic fields, generated by electric currents and the magnetic moments of elementary particles. Lodestones (natural magnets) were known in antiquity; compasses using Earth's field enabled the age of exploration from ports such as Byzantium's successors.

Unification

In the 19th century, Ørsted, Ampère, Faraday and Maxwell unified magnetism with electricity into electromagnetism — the foundation of motors, generators and the electric machines of industry, and of instruments from compasses to MRI scanners. Earth's field also shields the surface from solar wind studied by Parker Solar Probe.
