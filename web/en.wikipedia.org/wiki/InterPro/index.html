InterPro

InterPro is a database of protein families, protein domains, and functional sites in which identifiable features found in known proteins can be applied to new protein sequences in order to functionally characterise them. It is maintained at the European Bioinformatics Institute (EBI) and integrates member databases including Pfam, SMART, PROSITE, PANTHER, CATH-Gene3D, SUPERFAMILY, and others.

Method

Member databases contribute predictive models — sequence signatures such as hidden Markov models, profiles, and fingerprints — that diagnose particular families or domains. InterPro groups these signatures into curated entries with descriptive abstracts and Gene Ontology terms, and its InterProScan software lets users scan novel sequences against the combined signature library.

Use

InterPro is widely used in genome annotation pipelines, and its classifications feed into resources such as UniProt. The database is released in periodic versions and is freely accessible through a web interface and programmatic APIs.
