Audi Sport GmbH, formerly known as quattro GmbH, is the high-performance subsidiary of German automobile manufacturer Audi AG, itself part of the Volkswagen Group. Based in Neckarsulm, it develops and produces Audi's RS models and the R8 sports car, as well as running Audi's customer racing programmes. The separate Audi Sport racing department, based in Neuburg an der Donau, handles factory motorsport such as the brand's former Le Mans and Formula E entries and its Dakar Rally programme.

An RS model produced by Audi Sport GmbH in Neckarsulm.

History

The company was founded in 1983 as quattro GmbH, initially offering accessories and lifestyle products under the quattro name before moving into vehicle individualisation through the Audi exclusive programme. Its first dedicated performance car was the Audi S6 plus of 1996, followed by the RS 4 Avant (1999), developed in the spirit of the earlier RS 2 Avant built with Porsche. In November 2016 quattro GmbH was renamed Audi Sport GmbH to align the road-car business with the Audi Sport brand used in racing.

Products

Audi Sport GmbH is responsible for the RS range — including RS 3, RS 4 Avant, RS 5, RS 6 Avant, RS 7 Sportback, RS Q8 and TT RS — and for the mid-engined Audi R8. RS models typically feature quattro all-wheel drive, uprated turbocharged engines, enlarged brakes, adaptive suspension and wider bodywork compared with the standard S models.

Motorsport

Audi Sport customer racing, administered from Neckarsulm, supplies the R8 LMS GT3 and related GT2, GT4 and TCR cars to private teams worldwide. The factory Audi Sport Team has won the 24 Hours of Le Mans 13 times, multiple DTM titles, and competed in Formula E and the World Rally-Raid Championship with the RS Q e-tron.
