Eric Liddell

Eric Henry Liddell (16 January 1902 – 21 February 1945) was a Scottish sprinter, rugby union international, and Christian missionary, Olympic champion in the 400 metres at Paris 1924.

Eric Henry Liddell (16 January 1902 – 21 February 1945) was a Scottish sprinter, rugby union player, and Christian missionary. Born in China to Scottish missionary parents, he attended Eltham College in London and the University of Edinburgh, where his speed made him famous. He was known as the "Flying Scotsman" and became one of Britain's best-loved athletes.

The 1924 Olympics

Liddell's fame rests on the Paris Olympics of 1924. A devout Christian, he refused to run in heats of the 100 metres — his strongest event — because they were held on a Sunday. Instead he concentrated on the 400 metres, an event for which he was not favoured. In the final he ran from the front with his distinctive style, head back and mouth open, and won gold in a world-record 47.6 seconds; he also took bronze in the 200 metres. His story, and that of his rival Harold Abrahams, was told in the Academy Award-winning film Chariots of Fire (1981), in which Ian Charleson played Liddell.

Rugby

Liddell also played rugby union for Scotland, winning seven caps as a winger in 1922–23, scoring four tries. He retired from international sport at the height of his powers.

Missionary work and death

In 1925 Liddell returned to China as a missionary teacher with the London Missionary Society, working at the Anglo-Chinese College in Tientsin (Tianjin). He married Florence Mackenzie, a Canadian missionary's daughter, in 1934. As the Second Sino-Japanese War and then the Pacific War engulfed China, he undertook dangerous relief work in rural Hebei. In 1943 he was interned by the Japanese at the Weihsien Internment Camp, where he became a beloved teacher and organiser of camp life. He died there of a brain tumour in February 1945, five months before liberation. In 1991 a memorial headstone was erected at his grave, inscribed with words from Isaiah: "They shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run and not be weary."
