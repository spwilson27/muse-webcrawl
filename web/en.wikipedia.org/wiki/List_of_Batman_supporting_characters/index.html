List of Batman supporting characters

The Batman family of characters, also known as the "Bat-Family", is a group of fictional characters appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. In addition to Batman himself, the family includes allies, members of the Gotham City Police Department, and other associates who aid Batman's crusade in Gotham City.

Other associates

  • Leslie Thompkins — a doctor and pacifist who comforted the young Bruce Wayne after his parents' murder and runs a clinic in Crime Alley.
  • Vicki Vale — a photojournalist and occasional love interest of Bruce Wayne.
  • Julia Pennyworth — Alfred's daughter, a former intelligence operative.
  • Tamara Fox — daughter of Lucius Fox.
