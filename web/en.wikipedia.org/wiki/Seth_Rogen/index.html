Seth Aaron Rogen (born April 15, 1982) is a Canadian actor, comedian and filmmaker. Known for his work in comedy films, he began his career performing stand-up in Vancouver as a teenager and gained early recognition in the television series Freaks and Geeks (1999–2000) and Undeclared (2001–2002).

Career

Rogen broke through as a writer and supporting actor on The 40-Year-Old Virgin (2005) and as the lead of Knocked Up (2007). He co-wrote and starred in Superbad (2007) and Pineapple Express (2008) with his longtime collaborator Evan Goldberg. The pair went on to write and direct This Is the End (2013) and The Interview (2014). With Goldberg, Rogen co-founded the production company Point Grey Pictures, which has produced films and series including The Disaster Artist (2017), Good Boys (2019), The Boys and Invincible.

Other work

Rogen has voiced characters in animated films including Kung Fu Panda, Monsters vs. Aliens and the 2023 Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Mutant Mayhem, which he also produced. He portrayed Steve Wozniak in Steve Jobs (2015), earning critical praise, and starred opposite Charlize Theron in Long Shot (2019). He is also known for pottery, a cannabis company (Houseplant), and advocacy following his mother's experience with Alzheimer's disease.
