Bobby Robson

Sir Bobby Robson
Born: 18 February 1933, Sacriston, England
Died: 31 July 2009, County Durham
Position: Inside forward
Teams managed: Ipswich Town, England, PSV, Sporting CP, Porto, Barcelona, Newcastle United

Sir Robert William Robson (18 February 1933 – 31 July 2009) was an English football manager and player. His playing career, as an inside forward, included Fulham and West Bromwich Albion. As a manager he won trophies in England, the Netherlands, Portugal and Spain, and led England to the semi-finals of the 1990 World Cup.

Managerial career

Robson built Ipswich Town into a leading club, winning the FA Cup in 1978 and the UEFA Cup in 1981. He managed England from 1982 to 1990, then won league titles with PSV Eindhoven and Porto, the Copa del Rey and European Cup Winners' Cup with Barcelona, and restored his hometown club Newcastle United to the Champions League.

Legacy

Knighted in 2002, Robson founded the Sir Bobby Robson Foundation cancer charity in 2008. Statues stand at Ipswich and Newcastle.
