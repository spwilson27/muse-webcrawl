List of postcode areas in the United Kingdom

UK postcodes
Postcode areas124
Introduced1959 (Norwich) – 1974 (nationwide)
OperatorRoyal Mail

The United Kingdom postcode system, operated by Royal Mail, divides the country into 124 postcode areas, each identified by one or two letters at the start of the outward code — usually an abbreviation of the main town or city (for example, EH for Edinburgh, G for Glasgow) or, in London, a compass point (E, EC, N, NW, SE, SW, W, WC). Each area is subdivided into postcode districts, sectors and units.
