The Old Town Hall (Czech: Staroměstská radnice) in Prague, the capital of the Czech Republic, is one of the city's most visited monuments. It stands on the Old Town Square in the historic centre of the city and is best known for the Prague astronomical clock (the Orloj) mounted on its southern wall.

History

The hall was founded in 1338, when the councillors of the Old Town bought a large patrician house from the Volflin family; the town hall thus established was the first of its kind in Bohemia. Over the following centuries the complex grew by the incorporation of neighbouring houses, and a tall Gothic tower was added in the 1360s. The famous astronomical clock was installed in 1410 by the clockmaker Mikuláš of Kadaň and the astronomer Jan Šindel, making it one of the oldest astronomical clocks in the world still in operation.

The building served as the seat of the Old Town administration until 1784, when the Prague towns were unified and the administration moved to the New Town Hall. In May 1945, during the Prague uprising at the end of the Second World War, the hall was heavily damaged by German shelling and fire; the neo-Gothic eastern wing was destroyed and never rebuilt, and the clock's wooden apostles were burned, though the mechanism itself survived and was restored after the war.

The astronomical clock

The clock's astronomical dial shows the positions of the Sun and Moon and various astronomical details, while the hourly show of the Walk of the Apostles, a calendar dial, and Gothic sculptures draw crowds to the square below. According to legend, the clockmaker Hanuš was blinded on the order of the councillors so that he could not repeat his work elsewhere — a story not supported by historical records, which credit Mikuláš of Kadaň with the original mechanism.

The building today

Today the Old Town Hall is used for exhibitions, ceremonies and weddings, and visitors can ascend the tower for views over the Old Town. Its historic interiors include the council chamber and the Chapel of the Virgin Mary, decorated with frescoes and the coats of arms of the Old Town guilds.
