California vowel shift

The California vowel shift (CVS) is a systematic change in the pronunciation of vowels in the English spoken in California, documented by sociolinguists beginning in the late 20th century. It is part of the broader Western United States vowel pattern.

Features

Key features include the cot–caught merger, fronting of back vowels such as /u/ (so "dude" sounds more fronted), and lowering and retraction of front vowels, so that "kit" approaches "ket" and "dress" approaches "drass" in impressionistic terms. The shift is often associated popularly with "Valley girl" and surfer speech.

Research

Studies by Penelope Eckert and others in the San Francisco Bay Area showed the shift advancing among younger speakers across ethnic and social groups, though its realization varies regionally between Northern and Southern California.
