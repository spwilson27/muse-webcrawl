Laverne & Shirley is an American television sitcom that aired on ABC from 1976 to 1983. A spin-off of Happy Days, it stars Penny Marshall as Laverne DeFazio and Cindy Williams as Shirley Feeney, two single roommates and friends who work as bottle-cappers at the fictional Shotz Brewery in late-1950s Milwaukee.

Premise

The series follows the comic misadventures of Laverne, the tough, wisecracking daughter of a pizza parlor owner, and Shirley, the sweeter and more optimistic of the pair. Their friends included neighbors Lenny and Squiggy, Shirley's boyfriend Carmine "The Big Ragoo" Ragusa, and Laverne's father Frank. The show's opening sequence, in which the pair skip down a street chanting a hopscotch rhyme, became iconic.

Reception and changes

The show was the most-watched American television program during the 1977–78 and 1978–79 seasons. For the sixth season the setting moved from Milwaukee to Burbank, California. Cindy Williams left during the final season, and the series ended in 1983 with Penny Marshall continuing as Laverne.

Legacy

The famous "schlemiel, schlimazel" chant and the theme song "Making Our Dreams Come True" remain widely recognized pieces of 1970s American pop culture.
