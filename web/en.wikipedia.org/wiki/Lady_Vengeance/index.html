Lady Vengeance

Lady Vengeance (Korean: 친절한 금자씨, Chinjeolhan Geumja-ssi, lit. "Kind-hearted Ms. Geum-ja") is a 2005 South Korean neo-noir psychological thriller film directed by Park Chan-wook. It is the third and final installment in Park's Vengeance Trilogy, following Sympathy for Mr. Vengeance (2002) and Oldboy (2003). The film stars Lee Young-ae as Geum-ja, a woman released from prison after serving thirteen years for a murder she did not commit.

Plot

Lee Geum-ja is released from prison after serving time for the kidnapping and murder of a young boy, a crime of which she was falsely convicted. During her imprisonment she earned a reputation for kindness, but in secret she has been planning her revenge against Mr. Baek, the schoolteacher who committed the crime and forced her to confess by threatening her infant daughter. Upon release, she enlists the help of former inmates she befriended in prison to carry out her meticulously prepared plan.

Cast

  • Lee Young-ae as Lee Geum-ja
  • Choi Min-sik as Mr. Baek
  • Kim Si-hoo as Detective Choi
  • Nam Il-woo as Detective Choi's partner

Reception

The film premiered in competition at the 62nd Venice International Film Festival, where it won the Cinema of the Future and Young Lion awards. It was praised for its visual style, including a distinctive "fade to black and white" version shown in some releases, and for Lee Young-ae's performance.
