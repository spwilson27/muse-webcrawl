FIBA Hall of Fame

Established1991 (inductees from 2007)
LocationAlcobendas, Madrid, Spain
AuthorityFIBA

The FIBA Hall of Fame honours players, coaches, referees and other contributors who have been instrumental in the development of international basketball. It is administered by FIBA, the sport's world governing body, and is housed at the Pedro Ferrándiz Foundation in Alcobendas, near Madrid, Spain.

History

A hall of fame was proposed in 1991 by Spanish basketball promoter Pedro Ferrándiz, after whom the foundation is named. The first induction ceremony took place in 2007, marking FIBA's 75th anniversary, when figures including Bill Russell, Dražen Petrović and Nikos Galis were honoured.

Selection

Inductees are chosen in the categories of players, coaches, referees, contributors and teams. Players must have been retired from international competition for a set period before becoming eligible. Induction ceremonies are usually held during major FIBA events such as the Basketball World Cup.
