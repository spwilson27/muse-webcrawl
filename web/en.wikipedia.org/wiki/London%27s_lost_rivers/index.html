London's lost rivers
Location: London, England
Examples: Fleet, Tyburn, Effra

The lost rivers of London are the many tributaries of the Thames and Lea that were culverted, covered over or built upon as London grew, including the Fleet, Tyburn, Westbourne, Effra and Walbrook.

History

Once open streams used for water, mills and transport, they became polluted sewers in the growing city and were progressively buried during the 18th and 19th centuries, many incorporated into Bazalgette's sewer system.

Legacy

Their courses survive in street names and topography, and some, like stretches of the Wandle and Ravensbourne, still flow in the open in outer London.
