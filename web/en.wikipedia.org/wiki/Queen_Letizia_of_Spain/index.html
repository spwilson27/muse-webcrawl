Queen Letizia of Spain (born Letizia Ortiz Rocasolano, 15 September 1972) is the queen consort of Spain as the wife of King Felipe VI. Before her marriage she was a journalist and news anchor, working for the newspaper ABC, the EFE news agency, and the television channels Televisión Española (TVE) and CNN+. She became queen consort on 19 June 2014, upon the abdication of her father-in-law, King Juan Carlos I.

Early life and education

Letizia was born in Oviedo, Asturias, the eldest daughter of journalist Jesús Ortiz and nurse Paloma Rocasolano. She studied journalism at the Complutense University of Madrid, earning a bachelor's degree and later a master's degree in audiovisual journalism, and pursued doctoral coursework in Mexico, where she also wrote for the newspaper Siglo 21.

Journalism career

Returning to Spain, Ortiz worked at ABC and the EFE agency before joining CNN+ and then TVE. She presented the weekend edition of the Telediario, Spain's most-watched newscast, and reported from abroad, including coverage of the September 11 attacks, the Iraq War and the 2002 Prestige oil spill in Galicia. She received the Larra Award for the most promising journalist under 30.

Marriage and family

Her engagement to Felipe, Prince of Asturias, was announced on 1 November 2003, surprising a press that had not known of the relationship. They married on 22 May 2004 at the Almudena Cathedral in Madrid. The couple have two daughters: Leonor, born in 2005, heir presumptive to the throne, and Sofía, born in 2007. Letizia had previously been briefly married to the writer Alonso Guerrero Pérez, a civil marriage that ended in divorce.

Queen consort

As queen, Letizia undertakes solo engagements and specialises in social causes, serving as honorary president of the Spanish Association Against Cancer and as a Special Ambassador for Nutrition for the UN Food and Agriculture Organization. She is noted in the Spanish and international press for her work on rare diseases, education, and culture, and for a distinctive professional style.
