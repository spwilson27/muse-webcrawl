Olodumare

Olodumare (Yoruba: Olódùmarè), also known as Olorun (Ọlọ́run, "Owner of Heaven") or Olofin, is the supreme God in the Yoruba religion. Olodumare is the Supreme Creator and the source of all existence, energy, and life in Yoruba cosmology. The name Olodumare is commonly translated as "the Almighty" or "the one who has all authority to do whatever he wishes".

In Yoruba belief, Olodumare is omnipotent and responsible for the creation of the universe and everything in it. Unlike the orishas, the deities who act as intermediaries between humanity and the divine, Olodumare is distant from everyday earthly affairs and is not directly worshipped through shrines or priests; there are no temples dedicated specifically to Olodumare, and devotees approach the orishas instead.

Attributes

Olodumare is considered to be without gender and is the ultimate source of àṣẹ (ase), the divine life force that animates the universe. According to Yoruba creation accounts, Olodumare entrusted the orisha Obatala (and in some versions Oduduwa) with creating the earth and humanity, providing the materials and the life force for the task.

In the diaspora

The concept of Olodumare carried into Afro-Caribbean and Afro-Brazilian religions derived from Yoruba tradition, such as Santería (Regla de Ocha), Candomblé, and Trinidad Orisha, where Olorun/Olodumare remains the remote supreme creator above the orishas.
