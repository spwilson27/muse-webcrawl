Big Boy Restaurants

Big Boy is an American restaurant chain that began in 1936 with Bob's Pantry in Glendale, California, founded by Bob Wian. It is known for its mascot — a chubby boy in red-and-white checkered overalls holding a giant double-decker hamburger — and for the Big Boy double-decker burger.

Big Boy

Regions: Franchise names included Bob's, Elias Brothers, Frisch's, Shoney's (former)

History

Wian created the double-decker hamburger in 1937. The chain expanded through franchising under regional names. Marriott Corporation owned Big Boy from 1967 to 1987. Frisch's Big Boy in Ohio, Kentucky and Indiana and Michigan's Elias Brothers restaurants long operated under the brand; the remaining Big Boy restaurants are concentrated in Michigan, Ohio and California, with a Japanese presence dating to the 1970s.

In culture

The mascot statues are iconic Americana, referenced in films such as Austin Powers; comic books given to children featured the Adventures of the Big Boy.
