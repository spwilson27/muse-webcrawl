Kazuma Kaneko

Kazuma Kaneko (金子 一馬)
Occupation: video game artist, designer
Known for: character and demon designs for Megami Tensei and Persona
Employer: Atlus (1988–2023); COLOPL from 2023

Kazuma Kaneko is a Japanese video game artist best known for his work at Atlus, where he created the distinctive character and demon designs that define the Megami Tensei series and contributed to the early Persona games. Often nicknamed the "Demon Artist", he joined Atlus in 1988 after a period working in animation.

Work

Kaneko's illustrations draw on world mythology, religion and occultism, giving the demons of Megami Tensei a striking, fashion-influenced look. He served as character designer and art director on titles including Shin Megami Tensei (1992), Shin Megami Tensei III: Nocturne (2003), on which he also contributed to the scenario, the Devil Summoner series, and the first two Persona games. His demon designs have been reused across the franchise for decades.

Later career

After a long period in a more supervisory role, Kaneko left Atlus in 2023 after 35 years and joined the mobile-game company COLOPL, where he was announced as working on a new project.
