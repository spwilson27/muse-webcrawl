Oricon Albums Chart

Albums sold in Japan are tracked by the Oricon Albums Chart.

The Oricon Albums Chart is the Japanese music industry standard albums popularity chart issued by Oricon. Chart rankings are based on physical album sales gathered from a panel of retailers across Japan; results are compiled every Tuesday and published as a weekly chart. Oricon began publishing an LP chart in the 1970s, and the modern albums chart dates from the late 1980s.

Methodology

For most of its history the chart counted only physical formats — vinyl, cassette, CD and later other media. As the market changed, Oricon added a separate Digital Albums Chart and, in December 2018, a Combined Albums Chart that incorporates downloads and streaming equivalents. The standard weekly chart publishes a top 300, with the top positions receiving the most media attention.

Best-selling albums

The best-selling album of all time in Japan according to Oricon is First Love (1999) by Hikaru Utada, which has sold over seven million copies. Other long-running best sellers include works by B'z, Mr. Children, Ayumi Hamasaki, Southern All Stars and Glay. Oricon's data are routinely cited in Japanese media as the authoritative measure of record sales.
