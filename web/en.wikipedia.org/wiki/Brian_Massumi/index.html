Brian Massumi

Brian Massumi (born 1956) is a Canadian philosopher and theorist known for his work in philosophy, media theory, and political theory, particularly his influential writings on affect. He is known as the English translator of Gilles Deleuze and Félix Guattari's A Thousand Plateaus and of Jean-François Lyotard's The Postmodern Condition.

Work

His book Parables for the Virtual: Movement, Affect, Sensation (2002) is a key text in affect theory. Later works, including Ontopower (2015), examine preemption, threat, and the politics of the post-9/11 security state. Massumi taught at the Université de Montréal and has collaborated with artist Erin Manning through the SenseLab.
