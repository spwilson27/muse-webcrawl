La Solidaridad

La Solidaridad was a newspaper published in Barcelona and later Madrid, Spain, from 1889 to 1895. It was the organ of the Propaganda Movement, the association of Filipino reformists (ilustrados) who campaigned for reforms in the Spanish Philippines.

History

The paper was founded by Graciano López Jaena, its first editor, and later edited by Marcelo H. del Pilar. José Rizal was among its leading contributors, writing essays under the pen name Laong Laan. Published fortnightly in Spanish, the paper demanded representation of the Philippines in the Spanish Cortes, freedom of the press, secularization of parishes, and equality of Filipinos and Spaniards before the law.

End

Financial difficulties and factional disputes forced the paper to cease publication on 15 November 1895. Its campaign, though unsuccessful in its own terms, helped awaken Filipino national consciousness and preceded the Philippine Revolution of 1896.
