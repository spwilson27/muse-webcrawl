Doom II

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Doom II, also known as Doom II: Hell on Earth, is a first-person shooter game developed and published by id Software in 1994. The sequel to Doom (1993), it was sold in retail stores rather than distributed as shareware, and became one of the best-selling games of its era.

Gameplay

The player, a space marine returning to Earth, fights through 32 levels after demons invade the planet. Doom II uses the same engine as its predecessor, so its innovations are in content: larger, more open levels; new monsters including the Chaingunner, Hell Knight, Mancubus, Revenant, Arachnotron, Pain Elemental, and the final Icon of Sin; and one new weapon, the double-barrelled Super Shotgun. Levels flow continuously rather than in separate episodes, and designers included John Romero, Sandy Petersen, and American McGee.

Reception and legacy

Doom II won major game-of-the-year awards, was praised for its level design and multiplayer deathmatch over networks and modem, and has been ported to dozens of platforms. Its commercial success and modding community — level editors and total conversions flourished around it — helped define the first-person shooter genre.
