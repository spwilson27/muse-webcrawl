"You'll Never Walk Alone" is a show tune from the 1945 Rodgers and Hammerstein musical Carousel, written by Richard Rodgers (music) and Oscar Hammerstein II (lyrics). Sung in the show to comfort a grieving character, it became a standard and, from the 1960s, one of the most famous football anthems in the world.

Football anthem

In 1963 the Merseybeat group Gerry and the Pacemakers took the song to number one in the UK. Supporters of Liverpool F.C. adopted it as their anthem, sung before matches at Anfield with scarves held aloft; the title appears on the club crest and the Shankly Gates. Celtic, Borussia Dortmund, and many other clubs also adopted it. After the 1989 Hillsborough disaster the song became closely associated with remembrance and solidarity.

Other notable versions

Recordings include versions by Frank Sinatra, Elvis Presley, Barbra Streisand, and Johnny Cash. In 2020 a charity version by Captain Tom Moore, Michael Ball and the NHS Voices of Care choir reached number one in the UK during the COVID-19 pandemic.
