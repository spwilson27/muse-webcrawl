The Joker is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by Bill Finger, Bob Kane, and Jerry Robinson, the character first appeared in the debut issue of the comic book Batman on 25 April 1940. He is the archenemy of the superhero Batman and one of the most iconic and recognisable villains in popular culture.

Characterisation

The Joker is presented as a criminal mastermind with a warped, sadistic sense of humour. Depicted with chalk-white skin, green hair, red lips stretched into a permanent grin, and a purple suit, he commits crimes themed around jokes and clowning, using weaponised props such as razor-tipped playing cards, acid-squirting flowers, and lethal laughing gas. His origin is deliberately uncertain — in Alan Moore's The Killing Joke (1988) he claims to prefer his past "multiple choice" — though the most retold version has a failed comedian falling into a vat of chemicals while fleeing Batman as the hoodlum Red Hood.

During the character's first appearances he was a mass murderer played straight; censorship under the Comics Code softened him into a prankster in the 1950s–60s, before writers of the 1970s restored his homicidal edge. He has killed major characters, including Robin (Jason Todd) and Sarah Essen, and paralysed Barbara Gordon.

In other media

The Joker has been portrayed by Cesar Romero in the 1960s television series, Jack Nicholson in Batman (1989) opposite Michael Keaton, and Mark Hamill (voice) in Batman: The Animated Series and related works. Heath Ledger's performance in The Dark Knight (2008) won a posthumous Academy Award, and Joaquin Phoenix won the Academy Award for Best Actor for the title role of Joker (2019), in which he is failed comedian Arthur Fleck. Other portrayals include Jared Leto and Barry Keoghan.
