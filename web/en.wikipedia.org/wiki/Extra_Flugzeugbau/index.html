Extra Flugzeugbau is a German manufacturer of aerobatic aircraft, based at Dinslaken/Hünxe in North Rhine-Westphalia. The company was founded in 1980 by Walter Extra, a competition aerobatic pilot who began by building aircraft of his own design after campaigning a Pitts Special. Extra aircraft are renowned for their composite construction and extremely strong airframes, stressed for load factors of ±10 g or more.

Aircraft

The company rose to prominence with the Extra 300, a mid-wing two-seat monoplane that first flew in 1988 and became one of the most successful competition aerobatic aircraft ever built, winning numerous national and world championship titles. The single-seat Extra 300S and the later Extra 330SC refined the design for unlimited-class competition, while the Extra 230 and the two-seat Extra 200 served as smaller, lower-powered stablemates. Extra also produces the Extra EA-400 and EA-500 high-performance touring aircraft with pressurised cabins, and the Extra NG, an updated all-composite aerobatic model introduced in 2019.

Extra aircraft have been flown by many of the world's leading aerobatic pilots and are regular fixtures of airshows and the former Red Bull Air Race, whose standard aircraft in later seasons was based on competing designs of the same class.
