Sarah Burton

Sarah Jane Burton OBE (born 1974) is an English fashion designer. She was the creative director of the fashion house Alexander McQueen from 2010 to 2023, succeeding the house's founder Lee Alexander McQueen after his death, and designed the wedding dress of Catherine Middleton for her marriage to Prince William in 2011. In 2024 she was appointed creative director of Givenchy.

Early life and education

Burton was born in Macclesfield, Cheshire, one of five children. She studied print fashion at Central Saint Martins in London, where a tutor recommended her for an internship at Alexander McQueen. She joined the company full-time after graduating in 1997 and became head of womenswear in 2000.

Career

After McQueen's death in February 2010, Burton was named creative director of the brand. Her collections married the house's dramatic tailoring and craftsmanship with a softer, more romantic sensibility. The 2011 royal wedding dress — ivory satin gazar with hand-cut lace appliqué — brought her global recognition and is widely credited with renewing interest in British lace-making.

She was appointed an Officer of the Order of the British Empire (OBE) in 2012 for services to the fashion industry. Burton left Alexander McQueen in 2023 after 26 years with the house, and in September 2024 was announced as creative director of Givenchy, presenting her first collection for the house in 2025.
