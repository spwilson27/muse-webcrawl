Narita International Airport

Narita International Airport
IATA: NRT · ICAO: RJAA
Location: Narita, Chiba, Japan
Opened: 20 May 1978
Hub for: All Nippon Airways, Japan Airlines, Jetstar Japan, Peach, Spring Japan
Passengers: approx. 40 million (2019)

Narita International Airport (Japanese: 成田国際空港), also known as Tokyo Narita Airport, is the primary international airport of the Greater Tokyo Area, located approximately 60 kilometres east of central Tokyo in Narita, Chiba Prefecture. It handles the majority of Tokyo's international passenger traffic and is Japan's busiest airport by international passengers and international cargo.

History

The airport was built on farmland in Chiba after fierce protests over land expropriation; the Sanrizuka struggle by local farmers and activists delayed construction and led to violent clashes, and a protester's farm remained enclosed within the airport grounds for decades. Narita opened in 1978 with a single runway; a second runway opened in 2002. For years an operating curfew (23:00–06:00) and short second runway limited capacity, gradually relaxed in the 2010s.

Facilities and access

The airport has three terminals: Terminal 1 (opened 1978) serves mainly SkyTeam and Star Alliance carriers, Terminal 2 serves oneworld airlines, and the low-cost Terminal 3 opened in 2015. Rail access to central Tokyo is provided by the Narita Express and Keisei Skyliner services, the latter reaching Nippori in about 36 minutes. Since Haneda Airport's expansion of international flights after 2010, Narita has increasingly focused on long-haul, connecting and low-cost traffic.
