Mission: Impossible (1966 TV series)

Mission: Impossible is an American espionage television series created by Bruce Geller, aired on CBS from September 17, 1966, to March 30, 1973, for 171 episodes over seven seasons. It follows the Impossible Missions Force (IMF), a team of secret agents given covert assignments introduced by recorded messages that self-destruct.

Format and cast

Each episode opens with the famous tape scene — "Your mission, should you choose to accept it..." — and Lalo Schifrin's theme in 5/4 time. Steven Hill played team leader Dan Briggs in season one, succeeded by Peter Graves as Jim Phelps. Regulars included Barbara Bain as Cinnamon Carter, Martin Landau as Rollin Hand, Greg Morris as Barney Collier, and Peter Lupus as Willy Armitage; Leonard Nimoy later joined as Paris.

Legacy

The series won Emmy Awards and spawned a 1988 revival and the long-running film franchise starring Tom Cruise beginning in 1996.
