National Defense Reserve Fleet

The National Defense Reserve Fleet (NDRF) is a fleet of reserve ships maintained by the United States Maritime Administration (MARAD) for use in times of war or national emergency. Established shortly after World War II, the fleet once numbered more than 2,000 vessels at its peak in 1950.

Fleet sites

Reserve fleets have been anchored at sites including Suisun Bay in California, the James River in Virginia, and Beaumont, Texas. Ships are moored in groups, preserved in a laid-up ("mothballed") condition so that some can be reactivated on short notice.

Ready Reserve Force

A subset of ships, the Ready Reserve Force (RRF), is maintained in a higher state of readiness, able to be activated within five to ten days to support military sealift operations. As older vessels age out, many NDRF ships are scrapped or sunk as artificial reefs.
