Hideo Kojima

Born: 24 August 1963, Tokyo, Japan
Occupation: Video game designer, director, producer, writer
Known for: Metal Gear, Death Stranding

Hideo Kojima (Japanese: 小島 秀夫, born 24 August 1963) is a Japanese video game designer, writer, director and producer. He is regarded as an auteur of video games, noted for cinematic storytelling and for helping to establish the stealth genre. He joined Konami in 1986 and created the Metal Gear series, beginning with Metal Gear (1987) for the MSX2 and culminating in the internationally acclaimed Metal Gear Solid (1998) and its sequels.

Kojima Productions

After a widely reported split from Konami in 2015, Kojima re-founded his studio Kojima Productions as an independent company with support from Sony Interactive Entertainment. Its first game, Death Stranding (2019), starred Norman Reedus and Mads Mikkelsen, won multiple awards, and was followed by a sequel, Death Stranding 2: On the Beach (2025). Kojima has also announced the horror project OD with Xbox Game Studios and Jordan Peele.

Style and influence

A passionate cinephile, Kojima fills his games with film references and lengthy cutscenes, and has pursued games as an artistic medium. He holds a Guinness World Record as the most-followed video game director on social media and was honoured with the BAFTA Fellowship in 2020.
