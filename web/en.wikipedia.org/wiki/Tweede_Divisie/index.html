Tweede Divisie

The Tweede Divisie (Dutch pronunciation: [ˈtʋeːdə diˈvizi]; English: Second Division) is the third tier of football in the Netherlands, sitting below the Eredivisie and the Eerste Divisie. It was founded in 1956, abolished in 1971, and re-established for the 2016–17 season as a semi-professional league of 18 clubs.

History

In its original incarnation the Tweede Divisie was fully professional and at times split into two groups. After abolition in 1971 the Netherlands had no third professional tier until the league's 2016 revival as part of a restructuring that created promotion and relegation between the professional and amateur pyramids, though promotion from the Tweede Divisie to the Eerste Divisie was initially restricted and has repeatedly been debated.
