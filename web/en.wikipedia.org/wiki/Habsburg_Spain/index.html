Habsburg Spain

Period: 1516–1700
Monarchs: Charles I/V, Philip II, Philip III, Philip IV, Charles II
Capital: Madrid (from 1561)
Ended: Death of Charles II, War of the Spanish Succession

Habsburg Spain refers to Spain under the House of Habsburg, from Charles I's accession in 1516 to Charles II's death in 1700. At its height the Spanish Empire spanned Europe, the Americas, Asia and Africa, and Spain was Europe's leading power.

Charles I and Philip II

Charles I of Spain, also Holy Roman Emperor Charles V, inherited vast territories. His son Philip II ruled from Madrid and the Escorial, annexed Portugal in 1580, fought the Dutch Revolt, and sent the Spanish Armada against England in 1588.

Golden Age

The period saw the Spanish Golden Age in arts and letters — Cervantes, Velázquez, Lope de Vega — financed in part by American silver, which also fuelled inflation and repeated royal bankruptcies.

Decline

Under Philip III and Philip IV Spain fought the Thirty Years' War, lost Dutch independence formally in 1648, and faced revolts in Catalonia and Portugal. The inbred, childless Charles II's death triggered the War of the Spanish Succession and Bourbon rule.
