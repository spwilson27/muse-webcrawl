Moriteru Ueshiba

Moriteru Ueshiba (植芝 守央, Ueshiba Moriteru, born April 2, 1951) is a Japanese master of aikido. He is the grandson of Morihei Ueshiba, founder of aikido, and son of Kisshomaru Ueshiba. Since 1999 he has been Doshu — the hereditary head — of the Aikikai, the main aikido organisation, following the death of his father.

Biography

Ueshiba was born in Tokyo and began training in aikido as a child under his grandfather and father at the Aikikai Hombu Dojo. He graduated from Meiji Gakuin University with a degree in economics. In 1996 he became dojocho (head) of the Hombu Dojo, and on January 4, 1999, upon Kisshomaru's death, he assumed the title of Doshu.

As Doshu, Moriteru Ueshiba travels worldwide giving seminars, oversees the international recognition of aikido organisations through the International Aikido Federation, and represents aikido at events such as the World Games and SportAccord combat games demonstrations. His son, Mitsuteru Ueshiba, is expected to succeed him as Doshu.

Works

He is the author of instructional books including Best Aikido: The Fundamentals (2002, with Kisshomaru Ueshiba), The Aikido Master Course: Best Aikido 2 (2003), and Progressive Aikido: The Essential Elements (2005).
