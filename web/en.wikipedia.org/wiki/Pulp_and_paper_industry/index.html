Pulp and paper industry


A modern pulp and paper mill.

The pulp and paper industry comprises companies that use wood, recycled fibers, or other plant material as raw material and produce pulp, paper, paperboard, and other cellulose-based products. It is one of the largest industrial sectors in the world, dominated by North American, northern European, and East Asian producers, with production increasingly shifting to South America and Southeast Asia.

Manufacturing process

Manufacturing begins with raw material preparation: logs are debarked and chipped, or recycled paper is sorted and deinked. Pulping then separates cellulose fibers from lignin and other components. Mechanical pulping grinds or refines wood, producing high-yield pulp used for newsprint and magazines; chemical pulping—chiefly the kraft process—cooks chips in a solution of sodium hydroxide and sodium sulfide to dissolve lignin, yielding strong fibers for printing papers, packaging, and tissue. The pulp may be bleached, then formed into a sheet on a paper machine, pressed, dried, and finished.

Products

Major product categories include newsprint; printing and writing papers; containerboard and cartonboard for packaging; tissue and sanitary products; and specialty papers. Packaging has become the largest and fastest-growing segment as demand for graphic papers declines in many markets due to digital media.

Environmental issues

The industry is capital- and energy-intensive and historically a significant source of water pollution, including chlorinated compounds from bleaching; modern elemental-chlorine-free and totally chlorine-free processes have greatly reduced such emissions. Forestry practices, carbon emissions, and water use remain central concerns, alongside the industry's large-scale use of recycled fiber and biomass energy from pulping liquors.

Major companies

Large producers include International Paper, WestRock, Smurfit Kappa, UPM, Stora Enso, Oji Paper, and Suzano. The American Forest & Paper Association and the Confederation of European Paper Industries are principal trade bodies.
