Roberto Martínez

Roberto Martínez Montoliu (born 13 July 1973) is a Spanish professional football manager and former player who is the manager of the Portugal national team. As a midfielder he began his career in Spain with Real Zaragoza before spending a decade in British football with Wigan Athletic, Motherwell, Walsall, Swansea City and Chester City.

Managerial career

Martínez began managing at Swansea City in 2007, winning the League One title and establishing a possession-based style that shaped the club's subsequent rise. In 2009 he moved to Wigan Athletic, keeping the club in the Premier League on modest resources and, in 2013, leading them to a famous FA Cup triumph over Manchester City — the first major trophy in the club's history — although Wigan were relegated days later.

He then managed Everton from 2013 to 2016, finishing fifth in his first season. In 2016 he became head coach of Belgium, guiding their "golden generation" to third place at the 2018 FIFA World Cup, the country's best ever finish, and to the top of the FIFA World Rankings. After the 2022 World Cup he took charge of Portugal, winning the UEFA Nations League in 2025.
