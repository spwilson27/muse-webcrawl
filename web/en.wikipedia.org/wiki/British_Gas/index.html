British Gas (trading as British Gas in England, Scotland and Wales, and Scottish Gas in Scotland) is an energy and home services provider in the United Kingdom, and a subsidiary of Centrica. It is the largest energy supplier in the UK, serving millions of residential and business customers with gas and electricity, and employing thousands of engineers who install and maintain heating systems.

History

The brand descends from the Gas Light and Coke Company, founded in 1812, the world's first public utility company. The British gas industry was nationalised in 1949 into regional area boards, which were consolidated in 1973 into the British Gas Corporation. The state-owned corporation was privatised in 1986 under the slogan "Tell Sid," in one of the Thatcher government's flagship share offers.

In 1997 British Gas plc demerged: Centrica retained the British Gas supply and services businesses, while the exploration, production and transportation operations became BG Group. Transco, the national gas pipeline business, later became part of National Grid.

Services

Besides supplying gas and electricity, British Gas offers home services such as boiler installation and repair, central heating cover under the HomeCare brand, plumbing and electrical services, smart meters and smart home products. The company has been a major installer of smart meters in the national rollout and markets energy-efficiency and low-carbon heating products, including heat pumps.

Criticism

British Gas, like the other large UK suppliers (the former "Big Six"), has been criticised over prices, profits during the energy crisis of 2021–2023, and its use of prepayment meters. In 2023 it suspended forced prepayment meter installations after an investigation into agents breaking into the homes of vulnerable customers.
