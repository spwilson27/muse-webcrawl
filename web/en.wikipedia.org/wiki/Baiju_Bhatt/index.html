Baiju Prafulkumar Bhatt (born 1984/1985) is an American entrepreneur who co-founded the financial services company Robinhood along with Vladimir Tenev. He served as co-CEO of Robinhood alongside Tenev until November 2020, after which he became the company's chief creative officer before departing in 2024.

Early life and education

Bhatt is the son of Indian immigrants from Gujarat and grew up in Poquoson, Virginia. He studied physics at Stanford University, where he met Tenev, and later earned a master's degree in mathematics there.

Career

After earlier ventures in high-frequency trading software, Bhatt and Tenev founded Robinhood in 2013, launching a commission-free stock-trading app aimed at younger investors. The company went public in 2021. In 2024 Bhatt left Robinhood to focus on Aetherflux, an energy startup working on transmitting solar power from space to Earth using lasers.
