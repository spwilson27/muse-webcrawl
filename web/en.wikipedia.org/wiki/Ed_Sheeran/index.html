Edward Christopher Sheeran (born 17 February 1991) is an English singer-songwriter. Born in Halifax, West Yorkshire, and raised in Framlingham, Suffolk, he began writing songs around the age of eleven and independently released several EPs before signing with Asylum Records in 2011.

Career

His debut album, + (pronounced "plus"), was released in 2011 and topped charts in several countries. Subsequent albums x (2014), ÷ (2017), = (2021), - (2023) and Autumn Variations (2023) were major commercial successes. His single "Shape of You" became one of the best-selling digital singles of all time.

Musical style

Sheeran is known for performing solo with a loop pedal and acoustic guitar, and for blending pop, folk and hip-hop influences. He has won four Grammy Awards and multiple Brit Awards, and was appointed MBE in 2017 for services to music and charity.
