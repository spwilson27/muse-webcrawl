The New York Times Best Seller list

Published by: The New York Times Book Review
First published: 12 October 1931
Frequency: Weekly
Categories: Fiction, nonfiction, children's, and more

The New York Times Best Seller list is a series of weekly rankings of books by sales in the United States, published in The New York Times Book Review since 1931. It is widely regarded as the most influential bestseller list in American publishing; books that reach it are marketed as "New York Times bestsellers," a designation that itself drives further sales.

Methodology

The lists are compiled from sales reported by a confidential panel of thousands of bookstores, online retailers, and other outlets across the country. The Times applies statistical weighting and editorial judgment to the figures, and it does not disclose its full methodology — a secrecy that has periodically drawn criticism and legal challenges from authors who disputed their rankings.

Categories

Originally a single list for New York City sales, the ranking expanded nationally during the Second World War and has since multiplied into numerous categories: hardcover and paperback fiction and nonfiction, children's books, young adult, graphic books, business, and others. Appearance symbols mark titles whose sales include bulk purchases.
