The Missouri Valley Football Conference (MVFC) is a collegiate athletic conference affiliated with the NCAA's Division I Football Championship Subdivision (FCS). Member schools are located in the Midwestern United States. The conference competes only in football; its members play most other sports in separate conferences, including the Missouri Valley Conference, with which the MVFC shares a name and headquarters but not an administration.

History

Football in the league began in 1985, when the Gateway Collegiate Athletic Conference — previously a women's sports league — added football after absorbing several teams. In 1992, when the Gateway's women's sports merged into the Missouri Valley Conference, the football side became the Gateway Football Conference. It adopted the Missouri Valley Football Conference name in 2008 to align more closely with the MVC.

Members and success

Members have included North Dakota State, South Dakota State, Northern Iowa, Illinois State, Indiana State, Missouri State, Southern Illinois, South Dakota, Western Illinois, Youngstown State, North Dakota, and Murray State. The conference is regarded as the strongest in FCS football: North Dakota State won nine FCS national championships in eleven seasons between 2011 and 2021, and South Dakota State won back-to-back titles in 2022 and 2023.
