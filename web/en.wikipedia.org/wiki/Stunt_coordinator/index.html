Stunt coordinator

A stunt coordinator choreographing a fight sequence with performers

A stunt coordinator is the member of a film or television crew responsible for designing, casting, and supervising the performance of stunts. Usually a veteran stunt performer, the coordinator works with the director to realise the action described in the script while keeping it acceptably safe, and arranges an entire team of stunt performers and riggers as needed.

Duties

The coordinator reads the script and breaks down every action sequence, budgeting and planning each gag — falls, fights, vehicle work, fire burns and wire work. They hire and cast stunt doubles matched to the principal actors, choreograph and rehearse sequences, specify safety equipment such as air bags, decelerators and padding, and liaise with special-effects, armoury and picture-vehicle departments. On set, the coordinator (or an assistant) directs the stunt unit during takes and has authority to halt an unsafe action.

Recognition

Stunt coordinators supervise some of cinema's most celebrated sequences, yet the Academy Awards long lacked a category for stunts; after decades of campaigning, the Academy announced a new award for stunt design, to be presented for films from 2027 onward. Organisations such as the Taurus World Stunt Awards and industry guilds honour coordinators' work, and the role increasingly overlaps with that of the second-unit action director.
