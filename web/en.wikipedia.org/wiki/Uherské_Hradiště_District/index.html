Uherské Hradiště District (Czech: Okres Uherské Hradiště) is a district in the Zlín Region of the Czech Republic. Its capital is the town of Uherské Hradiště. With an area of about 991 km² and some 142,000 inhabitants, it is the most populous district of the region.

Location of Uherské Hradiště District in the Czech Republic.

Geography

The district lies in southeastern Moravia. Its western and central parts are formed by the Lower Morava Valley along the Morava River, while the east rises into the White Carpathians (Bílé Karpaty), a protected landscape area and UNESCO biosphere reserve shared with Slovakia. The district borders Slovakia to the southeast.

Administrative division

The district is divided into the administrative districts of municipalities with extended powers of Uherské Hradiště and Uherský Brod. Its 78 municipalities include seven towns: Uherské Hradiště, Uherský Brod, Staré Město, Kunovice, Bojkovice, Hluk, and Uherský Ostroh.

Economy and sights

The district is known for firearms manufacture (Česká zbrojovka in Uherský Brod), aircraft production in Kunovice, and wine growing in the Slovácko region. Notable sights include Buchlov Castle, the Great Moravian archaeological sites at Staré Město and Modrá, and the pilgrimage basilica at Velehrad, one of the most important pilgrimage sites in the country, associated with Saints Cyril and Methodius.
