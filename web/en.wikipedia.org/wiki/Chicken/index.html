The chicken is a domesticated bird descended mainly from the red junglefowl of Southeast Asia, and the most numerous bird on Earth — tens of billions alive at any time. Chickens are raised worldwide for eggs and meat, central to cuisines and farming from smallholder coops to industrial barns.

Issues

Intensive production raises welfare and disease concerns — avian influenza outbreaks periodically threaten flocks and, rarely, people, a surveillance topic beside the pandemic experience. Backyard keeping surges in popularity periodically; the chicken's egg is a staple ingredient from Bread to cheese-topped dishes.
