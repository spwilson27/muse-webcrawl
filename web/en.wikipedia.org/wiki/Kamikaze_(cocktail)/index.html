Kamikaze (cocktail)

TypeCocktail
Primary alcoholVodka
ServedStraight up, chilled, in a cocktail glass or as a shot
IngredientsVodka, triple sec, lime juice (equal parts)

The Kamikaze is a cocktail made of equal parts vodka, triple sec and lime juice. It is shaken with ice and strained, and is served either in a cocktail glass garnished with a lime wedge or divided into shot glasses. According to the International Bartenders Association, it is prepared with 30 ml each of vodka, triple sec and lime juice.

History

The drink is commonly dated to the mid-1970s in the United States and is often attributed to bartender Liam Neeson (of Boston, not the actor) or to the disco-era bar scene; its name borrows the Japanese word kamikaze, "divine wind", also applied to the suicide pilots of the Second World War. The Kamikaze is essentially a vodka-based variant of the Margarita pattern — spirit, orange liqueur and citrus — and is closely related to the later Cosmopolitan.

Variations

Common variants substitute blue curaçao for the triple sec, add cranberry, or present the drink as a layered shooter. A "SoCo Kamikaze" uses Southern Comfort in place of vodka.
