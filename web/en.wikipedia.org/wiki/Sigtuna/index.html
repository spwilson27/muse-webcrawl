Sigtuna is a locality in Sigtuna Municipality, Stockholm County, Sweden, situated on the shore of Lake Mälaren. It is considered Sweden's oldest surviving town, founded around 980.

History

Sigtuna was founded during the late Viking Age, traditionally attributed to King Eric the Victorious or Olof Skötkonung. Sweden's first coins were minted there around the year 1000. The town was an early centre of Christianity in Sweden, with several stone churches and a Dominican convent. After pirates burned Sigtuna in 1187, its importance declined and nearby Stockholm eventually surpassed it.

Today

Modern Sigtuna is a small town known for its well-preserved main street, ruins of medieval churches, runestones, and boarding schools. Stockholm Arlanda Airport lies within the municipality.
