Subaru BRZ

The Subaru BRZ is a lightweight front-engine, rear-wheel-drive sports coupé manufactured by Subaru. It was jointly developed with Toyota, which markets its version as the Toyota 86 (originally also sold as the Scion FR-S in North America). The name BRZ stands for "Boxer engine, Rear-wheel drive, Zenith". The car is built at Subaru's Gunma plant in Japan.

First generation (2012–2020)

The first-generation BRZ uses a naturally aspirated 2.0-litre Subaru flat-four engine (Toyota 4U-GSE / Subaru FA20) with Toyota's D-4S direct and port injection, producing about 200 PS (197 hp). Emphasis was placed on a very low centre of gravity, near 50:50 weight distribution, and handling balance rather than outright power. A six-speed manual or six-speed automatic transmission was offered.

Second generation (2021–present)

The second-generation BRZ, launched for 2022 alongside the Toyota GR86, adopted a larger 2.4-litre FA24 flat-four producing about 228 hp, a stiffer chassis, and updated styling, while retaining the lightweight rear-drive formula and a standard limited-slip differential.

Reception

The BRZ and its Toyota siblings have been widely praised by motoring journalists for their steering feel and chassis balance and have won numerous "car of the year" awards. They are popular in amateur motorsport, drifting, and one-make racing series.
