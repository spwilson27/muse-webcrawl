Het Laatste Nieuws

Het Laatste Nieuws (Dutch for "The Latest News") is a Dutch-language newspaper published in Belgium, based in Antwerp. Founded in 1888, it is the most widely read newspaper in Flanders and, by circulation, one of the largest in the country, and is published by DPG Media.

History

The paper was founded on 7 June 1888 in Brussels by Julius Hoste Sr. as a liberal, Flemish-minded popular daily. It gained a mass readership through accessible language, extensive sport coverage and serialised stories. After the Second World War it expanded under the Hoste family's De Persgroep publishing house, which grew into DPG Media, now one of the largest media groups in the Benelux.

Profile

Het Laatste Nieuws appears in tabloid format with numerous regional editions and is known for its extensive sports section and its website HLN.be, consistently the most-visited news site in Belgium. Its editorial line is popular and broadly liberal, with an emphasis on human-interest reporting alongside politics and crime.

Circulation

Paid circulation has historically hovered around 300,000 copies daily, making it the best-selling Flemish newspaper, ahead of Het Nieuwsblad and De Standaard.
