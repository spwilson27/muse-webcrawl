Stockholm City Hall

Location: Kungsholmen, Stockholm
Architect: Ragnar Östberg
Completed: 1923
Height: 106 m tower

Stockholm City Hall (Swedish: Stockholms stadshus) is the seat of Stockholm Municipality, standing on the eastern tip of Kungsholmen island. Designed by Ragnar Östberg in the National Romantic style and inaugurated in 1923, it is one of Sweden's most famous buildings.

Architecture

Built from some eight million dark red bricks, the hall is crowned by the Three Crowns at the top of its 106-metre tower. The Golden Hall is decorated with over 18 million gold mosaic tiles by Einar Forseth, centred on the Queen of Lake Mälaren.

Nobel Prize banquet

The Blue Hall hosts the annual Nobel Prize banquet on 10 December, attended by the Swedish royal family and laureates, despite its name its brick walls were left unpainted.
