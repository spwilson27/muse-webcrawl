Genesis of the Daleks

Genesis of the Daleks is the fourth serial of the twelfth season of the British science fiction television series Doctor Who. Written by Terry Nation and directed by David Maloney, it was broadcast on BBC1 in six weekly parts from 8 March to 12 April 1975.

Genesis of the Daleks
Doctor Who serial, Season 12
Doctor: Fourth (Tom Baker)
Companions: Sarah Jane Smith, Harry Sullivan
Writer: Terry Nation

Plot

The Time Lords send the Doctor to Skaro at the time of the Daleks' creation, with a mission to avert their genesis or alter their development. On the war-ravaged planet, the scientist Davros is creating the Daleks as survival machines for the mutated Kaled race in its thousand-year war with the Thals. The Doctor is captured, witnesses Davros's ruthlessness, and faces a moral dilemma — most famously, holding two wires whose touching would destroy the Dalek embryos, he asks: "Do I have the right?" He ultimately delays, rather than prevents, the Daleks' emergence.

Reception and legacy

The serial introduced Davros and is consistently ranked among the greatest Doctor Who stories in fan polls. Its dark tone, moral themes and parallels between Davros and fascism are widely praised, though its violence prompted criticism from campaigner Mary Whitehouse. It established the origin story retold and revisited throughout later Doctor Who.
