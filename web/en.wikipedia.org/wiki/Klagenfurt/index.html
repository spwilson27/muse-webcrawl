Klagenfurt

Klagenfurt am Wörthersee is the capital of the Austrian state of Carinthia, with a population of around 100,000. It lies on the Glan River near the eastern shore of Lake Wörth (Wörthersee), close to the Slovenian border.

History and sights

Founded in the 12th century and rebuilt after a fire in 1514 with Italian-influenced Renaissance planning, Klagenfurt's symbols include the Lindwurm, a dragon fountain on Neuer Platz commemorating the legendary dragon said to have haunted the marshes. The city is home to the Alpen-Adria-Universität Klagenfurt and the Minimundus miniature park, and is a gateway to the resorts of the Wörthersee.
