Donald J. Cram

Donald James Cram (1919–2001) was an American chemist who shared the 1987 Nobel Prize in Chemistry with Jean-Marie Lehn and Charles J. Pedersen for work on host–guest chemistry and molecules with structure-specific interactions of high selectivity.

Career

Born in Vermont, Cram studied at Rollins College and the University of Nebraska, earned his PhD at Harvard, and spent his career at UCLA, where he became a celebrated teacher.

Research

Cram's rule describes stereochemical outcomes in nucleophilic additions to carbonyl compounds. His work on spherands and preorganisation helped found supramolecular chemistry.

Works

He co-authored the textbook Organic Chemistry with George Hammond and wrote the memoir From Design to Discovery.
