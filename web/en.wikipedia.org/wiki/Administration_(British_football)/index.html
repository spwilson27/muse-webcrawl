Administration (British football)

In British football, administration is a form of corporate insolvency proceedings in which an insolvent club is run by an appointed administrator to rescue it as a going concern or achieve a better result for creditors.

Consequences

Entering administration typically triggers a points deduction — 12 points in the English Football League and 15 points in the Scottish Premiership under current rules — which can cause relegation. A company voluntary arrangement (CVA) is usually required to exit administration, with football creditors (players, other clubs) historically paid in full under the football creditors rule.

Notable cases

High-profile administrations include Leeds United (2007), Portsmouth (2010), Rangers (2012, followed by liquidation), Bolton Wanderers and Bury (2019), and Derby County (2021).
