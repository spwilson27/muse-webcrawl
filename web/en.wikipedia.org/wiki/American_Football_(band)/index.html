American Football (band)

American Football is an American emo band from Urbana, Illinois, originally active from 1997 to 2000. Known for their intricate guitar work, unusual time signatures, and wistful trumpet lines, the band released one EP and one self-titled studio album during their initial run, both of which later became highly influential in the emo and math rock genres.

History

The band was formed by Mike Kinsella (vocals, guitar), Steve Holmes (guitar), and Steve Lamos (drums, trumpet), students at the University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign. Their 1999 album American Football, recorded in a matter of days, sold modestly on release, and the members drifted to other projects; Kinsella went on to front Owen and play in Cap'n Jazz, Owls, and Joan of Arc.

Reunion and legacy

Over the following decade the album's reputation grew enormously through word of mouth and the internet; its cover — a white suburban house in Urbana at dusk — became an icon of the genre, and the house itself a pilgrimage site for fans. The band reunited in 2014 for sold-out shows and released a second self-titled album in 2016 (with bassist Nate Kinsella) and a third in 2019, featuring guest vocalists including Hayley Williams and Elizabeth Powell.
