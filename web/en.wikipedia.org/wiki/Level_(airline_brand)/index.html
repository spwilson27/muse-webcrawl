Level (airline brand)

Level, styled as LEVEL, is an airline brand under which airlines owned by the International Airlines Group (IAG) operate low-cost long-haul flights. The brand was announced on 15 March 2017 and operations began on 1 June 2017 with flights from Barcelona to Los Angeles, Oakland, Punta Cana and Buenos Aires.

History

IAG created Level in response to competition from low-cost long-haul carriers. Initial services were operated by Iberia using Airbus A330-200 aircraft. A French operation based at Paris Orly, Level France, was launched in 2018, and a short-haul Austrian operation, Level Europe, was created from the assets of Niki. Level Europe ceased operations in 2020 during the COVID-19 pandemic, and Level France also closed in 2020. The long-haul Barcelona operation continued under Iberia.

Destinations and fleet

Level has served destinations in the Americas including Boston, New York, San Francisco, Los Angeles, Miami, Buenos Aires, Santiago and Cancún, using Airbus A330-200 aircraft in a two-class configuration.
