Sorbonne University

Sorbonne Université
Established: 1 January 2018 (merger of Paris-Sorbonne and Pierre and Marie Curie universities)
Type: Public research university
Location: Paris, France
Faculties: Arts and Humanities; Science and Engineering; Medicine

Sorbonne University (French: Sorbonne Université) is a public research university in Paris, France, created on 1 January 2018 by the merger of Paris-Sorbonne University (Paris IV) and Pierre and Marie Curie University (Paris VI). It is one of the successors of the historic University of Paris and regularly ranks among the world's leading universities, particularly in mathematics and the sciences.

Organisation

The university is organised into three faculties: Arts and Humanities; Science and Engineering; and Medicine (centred on the Pitié-Salpêtrière and Saint-Antoine hospitals). It has around 55,000 students and several thousand academic staff, with campuses including the historic Sorbonne building in the Latin Quarter and the Jussieu campus.

History

The merger reunited the humanities tradition of Paris-Sorbonne with the scientific strength of UPMC, which itself traced to the science faculty of the old University of Paris and counted numerous Nobel laureates among its faculty. Together with partners such as the Muséum national d'histoire naturelle, Sorbonne University forms a major multidisciplinary research pole.
