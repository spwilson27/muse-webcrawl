The Berkshires

The Berkshires are a highland geologic and cultural region in western Massachusetts and northwestern Connecticut, part of the Appalachian mountain system. Known for rural scenery, fall foliage, and cultural institutions, the region is a popular tourist and second-home destination.

Geography

Peaks include Mount Greylock (3,491 ft), the highest point in Massachusetts. Principal towns include Pittsfield, Great Barrington, Lenox, Stockbridge, and Williamstown.

Culture

The Berkshires host Tanglewood, summer home of the Boston Symphony Orchestra; Jacob's Pillow dance festival; MASS MoCA in North Adams; the Norman Rockwell Museum; and Williams College. Writers such as Herman Melville and Edith Wharton lived in the region.
