St George's Chapel, Windsor Castle

St George's Chapel at Windsor Castle in England is a castle chapel built in the late-medieval Perpendicular Gothic style. It is a Royal Peculiar, and the Chapel of the Order of the Garter. Many royal ceremonies and burials have taken place there.

St George's Chapel
Location: Windsor Castle, Berkshire
Founded: 1475 (present building)
Style: Perpendicular Gothic
Status: Royal Peculiar; Chapel of the Order of the Garter

History

Edward III founded the college of St George in 1348. Edward IV began the present chapel in 1475; Henry VII and Henry VIII continued it, with the magnificent stone fan vault completed in the early 16th century. The quire stalls display the crests of Knights of the Garter.

Royal burials and weddings

Ten monarchs are buried in the chapel, including Henry VIII, Jane Seymour, Charles I, George V, George VI and Elizabeth II (in the attached King George VI Memorial Chapel). Royal weddings held there include those of Prince Harry and Meghan Markle (2018) and Prince Edward and Sophie Rhys-Jones (1999).

Music and college

The College of St George comprises the Dean and Canons, a choir school (St George's School), and Military Knights. The choir sings at regular services.
