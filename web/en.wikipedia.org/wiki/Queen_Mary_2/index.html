Queen Mary 2

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

[Image: RMS Queen Mary 2, black-hulled ocean liner, sailing past the Statue of Liberty]
Queen Mary 2 arriving in New York.

RMS Queen Mary 2 (QM2) is a British transatlantic ocean liner operated by Cunard. Launched by Queen Elizabeth II in 2004, she was for a time the largest passenger ship in the world (149,215 GT, 345 m long) and remains the only ship designed and built as a true ocean liner for regular Atlantic crossings between Southampton and New York, with a strengthened hull and 30-knot service speed far beyond cruise-ship norms.

Design

Built by Chantiers de l\'Atlantique in Saint-Nazaire, QM2 uses diesel-electric and gas-turbine power driving four Rolls-Royce Mermaid pods. Her deep-draught, ice-strengthened hull and high freeboard suit North Atlantic winter crossings. Interiors recall golden-age liners: the Britannia Restaurant spans two decks, and she carries the first planetarium at sea.

Service

QM2 replaced the beloved Queen Elizabeth 2 (1969–2008) as Cunard flagship. Beyond scheduled crossings she makes an annual world cruise and cruises from Southampton, New York and elsewhere. Famous moments include a 2004 rendezvous with QE2 and carrying the first copies of a Harry Potter book to America in a locked trunk (publicity voyage).

Place among giants

Cruise ships soon outgrew her in tonnage (Oasis class from 2009), but QM2 remains unique: no other large passenger ship is engineered for sustained high-speed ocean crossings. A major \'remastering\' refit in 2016 added cabins and refreshed interiors.
