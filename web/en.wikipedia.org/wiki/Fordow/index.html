Fordow

Fordow is a village in Qom Province, Iran, near the city of Qom. It is best known internationally as the site of the Fordow Fuel Enrichment Plant, an underground uranium enrichment facility built inside a mountain near the village.

The enrichment plant

The existence of the Fordow plant, constructed in secrecy inside tunnels near a military base, was revealed in September 2009 by the United States, United Kingdom, and France, after Western intelligence had monitored its construction. Iran then acknowledged the site to the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA), saying it was built deep underground for protection against attack.

Under the 2015 Joint Comprehensive Plan of Action (JCPOA), Iran agreed to convert Fordow into a research center and to cease enrichment there for fifteen years. After the United States withdrew from the agreement in 2018, Iran resumed enrichment at Fordow in 2019, including production of uranium enriched to 60 percent purity, drawing IAEA criticism. The site was struck during the June 2025 conflict between Israel, the United States, and Iran, with the United States using bunker-penetrating bombs against it.
