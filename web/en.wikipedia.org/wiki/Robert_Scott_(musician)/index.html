Robert Scott (musician)

BornTaumarunui, New Zealand
GenresIndie rock, Dunedin sound
InstrumentsBass guitar, vocals
BandsThe Clean, The Bats, The Magick Heads

Robert Scott is a New Zealand musician, one of the central figures of the Dunedin sound. He is best known as the bassist and vocalist of The Bats, whom he founded in 1982, and as a longtime member of The Clean, the seminal Flying Nun band formed by David and Hamish Kilgour.

Career

Scott joined The Clean in their classic line-up for the albums and EPs that defined the Flying Nun label's jangling guitar sound, including the Boodle Boodle Boodle EP (1981). With The Bats he has released a long sequence of albums beginning with Daddy's Highway (1987), distinguished by his melodic basslines and understated songwriting. He has also recorded with The Magick Heads, worked as a solo artist, and is a visual artist and former primary-school teacher. The Bats' songs such as "North by North" remain emblematic of New Zealand indie rock.
