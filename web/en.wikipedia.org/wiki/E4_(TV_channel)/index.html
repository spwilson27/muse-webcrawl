E4 (TV channel)

Country: United Kingdom

Launched: 18 January 2001

Owner: Channel Four Television Corporation

Sister channels: Channel 4, Film4, More4, 4seven

E4 is a British free-to-air public broadcast television channel owned and operated by Channel Four Television Corporation. Launched on 18 January 2001, the "E" stands for entertainment, and the channel is primarily aimed at the 16–34 age demographic.

History

E4 began as a pay-television companion to Channel 4, carrying first-run and preview episodes of popular programmes. In May 2005 it became free-to-air on digital terrestrial television, a move timed with its coverage of Big Brother, whose live streaming and spin-off shows were a mainstay of the early schedule. A one-hour timeshift channel, E4 +1, and the high-definition simulcast E4 HD followed.

Programming

The channel built its identity around imported American comedy and drama — including Friends, The Big Bang Theory, Brooklyn Nine-Nine and The Goldbergs — alongside British commissions. Original programmes closely associated with E4 include the comedy-drama Skins, the sitcom The Inbetweeners, the superhero comedy-drama Misfits, Made in Chelsea and reality formats such as Married at First Sight UK. From 2019, E4 also became the free-to-air home of the network's coverage built around late-night and youth-skewing entertainment strands.

Branding

E4 is noted for its irreverent presentation and its animated channelidents, frequently refreshed through design competitions and collaborations with artists. Its purple colour scheme and playful continuity announcements are long-running features of the brand.
