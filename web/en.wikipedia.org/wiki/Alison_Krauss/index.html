Alison Maria Krauss (born 23 July 1971) is an American bluegrass-country singer and fiddler. She signed with Rounder Records as a teenager and has released albums both solo and with her band Union Station, becoming one of the most awarded singers in Grammy history.

Career

Krauss released her debut album at 16 and won her first Grammy in 1991. Her work on the soundtrack of the film O Brother, Where Art Thou? (2000) brought bluegrass to a mass audience. Her collaboration with Robert Plant, Raising Sand (2007), won the Grammy for Album of the Year, and the pair reunited for Raise the Roof (2021). Her soprano voice and fiddle playing are noted for their clarity and precision.
