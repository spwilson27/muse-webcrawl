Gloating

Gloating is the act of rejoicing or expressing triumph, especially over another person's misfortune, failure, or defeat. It overlaps with the German concept of schadenfreude — pleasure derived from another's trouble — but gloating typically implies the open display of that pleasure rather than merely feeling it.

Psychology

Psychologists link gloating to social comparison, rivalry, and self-esteem: the downfall of a competitor can raise one's relative status. Studies suggest that envy toward a rival increases the likelihood of gloating at their misfortune, and that gloating expressions can damage relationships and reputations, which is why many cultures treat it as poor sportsmanship.

In culture and sport

Gloating is discouraged in sporting codes and etiquette, where excessive celebration at an opponent's expense may be penalised or condemned by commentators. Biblical and literary traditions likewise warn against rejoicing at the fall of an enemy.
