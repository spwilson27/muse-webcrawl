Indian River Lagoon

The Indian River Lagoon is a group of three lagoons — Mosquito Lagoon, Banana River and the Indian River — on the Atlantic coast of Florida, stretching about 156 miles (251 km) from Ponce de Leon Inlet near New Smyrna Beach south to Jupiter Inlet. It is one of the most biologically diverse estuaries in North America, home to more than 4,000 species of plants and animals, including manatees, dolphins and numerous fish and bird species.

Ecology and threats

The lagoon system supports seagrass beds, mangroves and salt marshes and a major commercial and recreational fishery. In recent decades it has suffered severe environmental degradation from nutrient pollution, stormwater runoff, algal blooms and large-scale seagrass die-offs, which contributed to a record manatee mortality event in 2021–2022 when hundreds of manatees starved. Restoration efforts include muck removal, septic-to-sewer conversion and oyster reef restoration.
