List of Six Feet Under episodes

Six Feet Under is an American drama television series created by Alan Ball for HBO. It premiered on June 3, 2001, and concluded on August 21, 2005, spanning 63 episodes over five seasons. The series centers on the Fisher family, who run a funeral home in Los Angeles, and nearly every episode opens with a death that sets its theme.

Fisher & Sons (later Fisher & Diaz) Funeral Home, the show's central setting.

Reception

The series finale, "Everyone's Waiting", written and directed by Alan Ball, is frequently cited among the greatest television finales of all time, in particular for its final six-minute sequence set to Sia's "Breathe Me".
