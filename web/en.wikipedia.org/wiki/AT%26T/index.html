AT&T Inc. is an American multinational telecommunications holding company headquartered at Whitacre Tower in downtown Dallas, Texas. It is one of the largest telecommunications companies in the world and the largest wireless carrier in the United States.

History

AT&T descends from the Bell System founded by Alexander Graham Bell; the original American Telephone and Telegraph Company held a near-monopoly on US telephone service until an antitrust settlement broke it up in 1984 into the "Baby Bells". Southwestern Bell, one of those regionals, later acquired the AT&T name and much of the old system, re-forming the modern company.

Business

The company provides mobile and fixed broadband, fibre internet, and business communications services. It expanded into media by acquiring DirecTV (2015) and Time Warner (2018), then spun off WarnerMedia in 2022 to refocus on connectivity.
