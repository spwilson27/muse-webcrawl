The University of Melbourne is a public research university located in Melbourne, Victoria, Australia. Founded in 1853, it is Australia's second-oldest university and the oldest in Victoria. It is a member of the Group of Eight and is consistently ranked among the world's leading universities.

Structure

The university's main campus at Parkville, just north of Melbourne's city centre, is home to faculties of arts, science, medicine, law, engineering, and business, and to a system of residential colleges modelled in part on Oxford and Cambridge. Around 50,000 students are enrolled.

Melbourne Model

In 2008 the university introduced the "Melbourne Model," offering broad undergraduate degrees followed by specialized graduate professional programs, a structure distinctive within Australia. Alumni include four prime ministers of Australia and several Nobel laureates.
