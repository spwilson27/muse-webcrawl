2021 Peruvian general election

The 2021 Peruvian general election was held on 11 April 2021, with a presidential run-off on 6 June 2021. In the second round, Pedro Castillo of the left-wing Free Peru party narrowly defeated Keiko Fujimori of the right-wing Popular Force, winning 50.13% of the vote in one of the closest elections in Peru's history.

Background

Peru entered the election after years of political turmoil: four presidents had held office since 2016, and the COVID-19 pandemic had hit the country with one of the world's highest per-capita death rates. Eighteen candidates contested the first round. Castillo, a rural schoolteacher and union leader from Cajamarca who had led a teachers' strike in 2017, surged late on support from poor and rural voters. Fujimori, daughter of former president Alberto Fujimori, was making her third run for the presidency while facing corruption charges.

After the run-off, Fujimori alleged fraud without providing decisive evidence, and her legal challenges delayed the official proclamation for six weeks. International observers found no significant irregularities. Castillo was proclaimed president-elect on 19 July and inaugurated on 28 July 2021, Peru's bicentennial. His turbulent presidency ended in December 2022 when he was removed and arrested after attempting to dissolve Congress.
