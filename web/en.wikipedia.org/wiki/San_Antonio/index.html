San Antonio is a city in the U.S. state of Texas and the seat of Bexar County. With a population of roughly 1.4 million, it is the second-most populous city in Texas and one of the largest in the United States. The city is famous for the Alamo, its Spanish colonial missions, and the River Walk, and it has a large military presence centred on Joint Base San Antonio.

History

San Antonio was founded in 1718, when a Spanish expedition established Mission San Antonio de Valero — later known as the Alamo — and a presidio at a Coahuiltecan settlement near the San Antonio River. Canary Islander settlers arrived in 1731, forming the first chartered civil settlement in Spanish Texas. After Mexican independence the city became part of Mexico, and in 1836 it was the site of the Battle of the Alamo during the Texas Revolution, where a small Texan garrison was overwhelmed by General Santa Anna's army. Texas annexation by the United States followed in 1845. San Antonio grew as a military and ranching centre; the arrival of the railroad in 1877 spurred rapid growth, and the city was Texas's largest for much of its early history.

Geography and culture

San Antonio lies on the Balcones Escarpment between the Texas Hill Country and the South Texas plains; its climate is humid subtropical with hot summers. Five Spanish colonial missions, including the Alamo, constitute a UNESCO World Heritage Site (inscribed 2015). The River Walk (Paseo del Río), a network of walkways along the San Antonio River below street level, is the city's signature attraction. San Antonio's population is majority Hispanic or Latino, and its culture blends Mexican, Tejano, German and Anglo influences, celebrated at the annual Fiesta San Antonio. The San Antonio Spurs of the NBA are the city's major professional sports team.

Economy

The economy rests on the military (Joint Base San Antonio, including Fort Sam Houston and Lackland and Randolph Air Force Bases), healthcare and bioscience, financial services (USAA is headquartered there), tourism and a growing cybersecurity sector.
