Gartner, Inc. is an American technological research and consulting firm based in Stamford, Connecticut. Founded in 1979 by Gideon Gartner, the company provides research, analysis and advisory services on information technology to executives and IT leaders at corporations, government agencies and investors.

Business

Gartner's research is sold largely by subscription. Its analysts publish reports evaluating technology markets and vendors; well-known products include the Magic Quadrant, a graphical positioning of vendors in a market by "completeness of vision" and "ability to execute", and the Hype Cycle, a model of the maturity and adoption of emerging technologies. The company also runs conferences such as its IT Symposium/Xpo, and provides consulting and contract optimisation services.

History

Gartner was taken public in 1986, acquired by Saatchi & Saatchi in 1988, and bought back by management with investment from Bain Capital in 1990; it listed again on the New York Stock Exchange in 1993. It expanded by acquiring firms including Meta Group (2005) and Burton Group (2010), and acquired CEB Inc. in 2017 for about $2.6 billion, extending its coverage beyond IT into other corporate functions.
