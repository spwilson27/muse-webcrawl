Cloud 9 (Siargao)

LocationGeneral Luna, Siargao, Philippines
TypeReef break surf spot

Cloud 9 is a famous surfing break on the island of Siargao in the Philippines, near the town of General Luna. It is renowned for its thick, hollow, barreling right-hand waves breaking over a shallow coral reef.

The break

The wave is powerful and breaks best with offshore winds and mid-to-high tide; at low tide the reef is dangerously shallow. A wooden boardwalk and viewing tower extend over the reef, becoming an emblem of the spot.

Events and fame

Cloud 9 hosts international surfing competitions, including events sanctioned by the World Surf League, and has helped make Siargao one of the best-known surf destinations in Asia. The island's surf season peaks roughly from September to November.
