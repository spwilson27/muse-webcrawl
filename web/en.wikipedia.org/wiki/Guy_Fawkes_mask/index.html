Guy Fawkes mask

The Guy Fawkes mask is a stylised depiction of Guy Fawkes, created by illustrator David Lloyd for the graphic novel V for Vendetta (1982–1989) and popularised by its 2005 film adaptation. The white mask features an exaggerated smile, rosy cheeks, a thin moustache turned up at both ends and a pointed beard.

From 2008 the mask was adopted by the hacktivist group Anonymous during protests against the Church of Scientology, and it subsequently became a worldwide symbol of protest movements, including the Occupy movement of 2011. Sales of the mask have at times made it one of the best-selling masks on Amazon.
