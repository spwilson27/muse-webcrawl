List of top international women's football goal scorers

This is a list of the highest-scoring players in women's international association football, ranked by goals scored for their national teams. The all-time leader is Canada's Christine Sinclair, who surpassed the previous record of Abby Wambach of the United States in January 2020 and scored 190 international goals before retiring in 2023 — the most by any player, male or female, in international football.

Notes

Statistics depend on the recognition of matches by national federations and FIFA, and totals for active players change with each international window. Sinclair's mark of 190 stands as the world record for international goals in both the women's and men's games.
