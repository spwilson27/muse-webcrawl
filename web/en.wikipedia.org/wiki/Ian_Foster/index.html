Ian Douglas Foster (born 1 May 1965) is a New Zealand rugby union coach and former player. He was head coach of the New Zealand national team, the All Blacks, from 2020 to 2023, leading them to the final of the 2023 Rugby World Cup in France, where they lost 12–11 to South Africa.

Foster during his tenure as All Blacks head coach.

Playing career

A first five-eighth from Putāruru, Foster played a record 148 matches for Waikato in provincial rugby, forming a noted halves combination with scrum-half Kevin Greene. He never won an All Blacks cap, touring competition for the position being intense in his era, but he did represent New Zealand's second-tier sides and was a respected provincial captain.

Coaching career

Foster coached Waikato and then the Chiefs in Super Rugby from 2004 to 2011, taking them to the 2009 final. In 2012 he became assistant coach of the All Blacks under Steve Hansen, serving through two World Cup cycles including the 2015 triumph. Appointed head coach in December 2019, he won the Rugby Championship several times but also oversaw rare home defeats, including a first-ever home loss to Argentina and a series defeat to Ireland in 2022, which brought heavy public criticism. Retained after a review, he led the team to a strong 2023 World Cup campaign before the narrow final loss. He was succeeded by Scott Robertson and subsequently took a coaching role in Japan with Toyota Verblitz.
