International Data Corporation (IDC) is an American market intelligence and advisory firm specialising in information technology, telecommunications and consumer technology markets. Founded in 1964 by Patrick McGovern in Newtonville, Massachusetts, it is a wholly owned subsidiary of the International Data Group (IDG), itself owned by Blackstone since 2021.

Activities

IDC employs over a thousand analysts in more than 100 countries. It is best known for its "Tracker" products, which estimate vendor market shares for devices such as smartphones, PCs, tablets and servers, and for forecast and advisory services sold by subscription. Its quarterly smartphone and PC shipment figures are widely cited in the technology press.
