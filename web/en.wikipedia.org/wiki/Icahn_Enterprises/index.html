Icahn Enterprises L.P.
Type: Public (NASDAQ: IEP)
Industry: Conglomerate holding company
Founded: 1987
Key person: Carl Icahn

Icahn Enterprises L.P. is an American publicly traded master limited partnership and conglomerate holding company controlled by investor Carl Icahn. Founded in 1987 as American Real Estate Partners, it holds interests across sectors including investment management, energy (such as CVR Energy), automotive parts, food packaging, metals, real estate and home fashion.

History

Icahn built the vehicle over decades as the listed flagship of his activist investing strategy, taking large positions in companies and pressing for changes such as spin-offs, buybacks and board representation. In 2023 the firm was the subject of a critical report by short-seller Hindenburg Research, which alleged an inflated valuation and unsustainable distributions; the company's unit price fell sharply and it cut its quarterly distribution.
