Sunn O)))

Sunn O))) (pronounced simply "Sunn") is an American drone metal band formed in Seattle, Washington, in 1998 by Stephen O'Malley and Greg Anderson. The band is known for its extremely slow, heavily amplified, low-frequency sound, and for live performances featuring the members in hooded robes, dense fog and overwhelming volume.

History

O'Malley and Anderson named the band after the logo of Sunn amplifiers, with the parenthetical waves representing sound emanating from the name. The band emerged from the members' earlier projects, including Burning Witch and Goatsnake, and took inspiration from the drone doom of Earth. Their debut album, ØØ Void (2000), established the template of downtuned guitars, minimal rhythm and extended durations.

Subsequent records — Flight of the Behemoth (2002), White1 and White2 (2003–2004), Black One (2005) and Monoliths & Dimensions (2009) — expanded the palette with guest vocalists, orchestration, choirs and jazz instrumentation. In 2014 the band collaborated with Scott Walker on Soused, and with Ulver on Terrestrials. Later albums include Kannon (2015), Life Metal (2019), produced by Steve Albini, and its companion Pyroclasts (2019).

Style and live performances

Sunn O)))'s music dispenses with conventional song structure in favour of sustained tones, feedback and glacial chord changes, played at volumes intended to be felt physically. Concerts are ritualistic events: the band performs in monk-like robes (Grimmrobes) amid artificial fog, with walls of Sunn and Ampeg amplifiers, and vocalist Attila Csihar of Mayhem has frequently joined them live and on record.
