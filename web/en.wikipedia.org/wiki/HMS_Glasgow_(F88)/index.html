HMS Glasgow (F88)

HMS Glasgow (pennant number F88) was a Batch 2 Type 22 frigate of the Royal Navy, launched in 1979 and commissioned in 1981. The sixth ship of the Royal Navy to bear the name, she served for nearly three decades on patrol, anti-submarine and maritime security duties before being decommissioned in 2005 and scrapped in 2009.

Launched14 April 1979
CommissionedMay 1981

Service

Built by Swan Hunter on the Tyne, Glasgow was designed primarily for anti-submarine warfare against Soviet submarines in the North Atlantic, carrying Sea Wolf point-defence missiles, Exocet anti-ship missiles, torpedo tubes and a Lynx helicopter. She deployed widely, including to the South Atlantic after the Falklands War, the Gulf, the Caribbean on counter-narcotics patrol, and the Adriatic during the Balkan conflicts. In 1999 she supported operations connected with the East Timor crisis as part of an international deployment.

Decommissioning

Under the 2004 defence review, Glasgow was paid off early, decommissioning on 1 February 2005. She was towed to Turkey and broken up in 2009.
