TenneT

TenneT is a transmission system operator (TSO) for electricity in the Netherlands and a large part of Germany. Wholly owned by the Dutch state, it operates the high-voltage grid of over 25,000 kilometres of lines and cables, connecting offshore wind farms in the North Sea and balancing supply and demand across its control area of some 43 million end-users.

TypeState-owned TSO
HeadquartersArnhem, Netherlands
OwnerKingdom of the Netherlands

Operations

TenneT was formed from the Dutch grid operator and expanded into Germany in 2010 by acquiring the E.ON high-voltage network (transpower). It is a major builder of offshore grid connections and interconnectors, including links to the United Kingdom (BritNed), Norway (NordLink, shared) and Denmark (COBRAcable).
