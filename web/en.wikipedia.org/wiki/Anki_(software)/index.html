Anki is a free and open-source flashcard program using spaced repetition — a technique from cognitive science for fast and long-lasting memorisation. Its name comes from the Japanese word anki (暗記), meaning "memorisation". Anki schedules reviews of cards just before the user is predicted to forget them, based on the SuperMemo SM-2 algorithm in its classic versions, with an FSRS scheduler added in later releases.

Features

Cards are stored in decks and built from notes with fields; card templates support HTML, CSS, images, audio, video, and LaTeX. Anki supports cloze deletions, image occlusion, typed answers, and a large ecosystem of add-ons and shared decks. Collections synchronise through the free AnkiWeb service. The official iOS app (AnkiMobile) is paid, funding development, while the desktop and Android (AnkiDroid) versions are free.

Use

Anki is especially popular with language learners and medical students, where very large decks — vocabulary lists, anatomy, pharmacology — are maintained over years. Studies of spaced-repetition software generally support its effectiveness for retention compared with massed study.
