IEC 60906-1

International standard for domestic AC power plugs and sockets

The IEC 60906-1 plug as used in Brazil

IEC 60906-1 is an international standard for 230 V, 16 A domestic AC power plugs and sockets, published by the International Electrotechnical Commission. It was intended as a common, modern standard for countries adopting a new plug system, with a compact three-pin design in which the pins are arranged in a shallow triangular pattern and partly insulated for safety.

Adoption

Despite the IEC's intention that it become a widely used standard, IEC 60906-1 has been adopted nationally only by Brazil, where it forms the basis of the NBR 14136 standard introduced in the 2000s (in 10 A and 20 A variants), and historically influenced the South African SANS 164-2 plug, which is dimensionally similar though not identical. Most countries retained their established national plug types.

Design

The plug resembles the Europlug in compactness but adds an earth pin, and its socket is designed to accept the two-pin Europlug as well in some national implementations. Features such as shutters and insulated pin sleeves address common safety concerns with older designs.
