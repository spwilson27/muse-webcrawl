A season is a division of the year marked by changes in weather, ecology and daylight, caused by Earth's axis being tilted relative to its orbit — not by distance from the Sun. As Earth orbits, each hemisphere leans toward the Sun in turn, receiving more direct light and longer days.

Variations

Tropical regions have wet and dry seasons instead; monsoons dominate South and East Asia's climate. Seasons structure agriculture (Rice, Wheat, Corn sowing and harvest), animal migration and human festivals worldwide.
