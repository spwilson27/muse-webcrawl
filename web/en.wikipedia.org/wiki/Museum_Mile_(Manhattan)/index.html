Museum Mile (Manhattan)

Museum Mile is the name for a section of Fifth Avenue in Manhattan, New York City, running roughly from 82nd to 110th Streets along the eastern edge of Central Park on the Upper East Side. The stretch contains one of the densest concentrations of major museums in the world.

Institutions

Museums along or immediately adjacent to Museum Mile include:

  • The Metropolitan Museum of Art (82nd Street)
  • The Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum (88th Street)
  • Neue Galerie New York (86th Street)
  • Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum (91st Street)
  • The Jewish Museum (92nd Street)
  • Museum of the City of New York (103rd Street)
  • El Museo del Barrio (105th Street)
  • The Africa Center (110th Street)

The Frick Collection, just south of the official stretch at 70th Street, is often associated with the group as well.

Museum Mile Festival

Since 1978, the institutions have jointly hosted the Museum Mile Festival, an annual evening event in June during which Fifth Avenue is closed to traffic and the museums open free to the public, drawing large crowds.
