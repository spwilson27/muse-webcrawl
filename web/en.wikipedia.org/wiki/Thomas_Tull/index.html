Thomas Tull

Thomas Tull
Born: 28 June 1970, Endwell, New York, US
Occupation: Businessman, film producer
Known for: Founder of Legendary Entertainment

Thomas Tull (born June 28, 1970) is an American billionaire businessman and film producer. He is the founder of Legendary Entertainment, the film production company behind such successful franchises as Christopher Nolan's The Dark Knight trilogy (as a co-financier), The Hangover, Inception, Godzilla, and Jurassic World.

Early life and career

Tull grew up in Endwell, New York, the son of a dental hygienist single mother. He attended Hamilton College, initially planning a legal career, but instead entered business, building and selling ventures in the laundromat and auto-repair sectors, and later working in private equity and tax-planning finance, where he became involved in film financing.

Legendary Entertainment

Tull founded Legendary Pictures in 2000 (later Legendary Entertainment), raising substantial private-equity capital to co-finance films with major studios — first Warner Bros. and later Universal Pictures and others. The company distinguished itself with a data-driven "moneyball" approach to green-lighting genre films with strong fan followings, especially comic-book, science-fiction, and monster properties; it launched the MonsterVerse with Godzilla (2014). In 2016 China's Dalian Wanda Group acquired Legendary for about $3.5 billion; Tull left the company in 2017.

Other ventures

After Legendary, Tull founded Tull Investment Group, investing in technology and life-sciences companies including Pinterest, Magic Leap, and Oculus. He is a part-owner of the Pittsburgh Steelers, a board member and benefactor of institutions such as MIT and Carnegie Mellon, and a noted collector of sports memorabilia and movie props.
