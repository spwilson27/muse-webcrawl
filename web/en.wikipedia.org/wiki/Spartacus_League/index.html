Spartacus League

The Spartacus League (German: Spartakusbund) was a Marxist revolutionary movement organised in Germany during World War I, founded by Rosa Luxemburg, Karl Liebknecht, Clara Zetkin and others, named after Spartacus.

History

The group originated in opposition to the SPD's support for war credits in 1914. As the 'Internationale' group and then the Spartacus League (from 1916), it published illegal anti-war letters signed 'Spartacus'. Luxemburg and Liebknecht were imprisoned for much of the war.

Revolution and uprising

Released during the November 1918 revolution, the Spartacists founded the Communist Party of Germany at the turn of 1919. In January 1919 the Spartacist uprising in Berlin was crushed by government forces and Freikorps; Luxemburg and Liebknecht were murdered on 15 January 1919.

Legacy

The League's martyred leaders became icons of the German left, and its critique of both reformism and Bolshevik centralism remains influential.
