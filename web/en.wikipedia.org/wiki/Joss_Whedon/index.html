Joss Whedon

Joss Whedon
Born: Joseph Hill Whedon, June 23, 1964, New York City, U.S.
Occupation: Screenwriter, director, producer
Known for: Buffy the Vampire Slayer; Firefly; The Avengers

Joseph Hill Whedon (born June 23, 1964) is an American screenwriter, director, and producer. He is the creator of the television series Buffy the Vampire Slayer (1997–2003), Angel (1999–2004), Firefly (2002), Dollhouse (2009–2010), and Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. (2013–2020), and the writer-director of the Marvel films The Avengers (2012) and Avengers: Age of Ultron (2015).

Early life and family

Whedon was born in New York City into a television-writing family: his grandfather John Whedon was a sitcom writer in the 1950s and his father, Tom Whedon, wrote for shows including The Dick Cavett Show and The Golden Girls. He studied film at Wesleyan University and began his career as a staff writer on the sitcom Roseanne, moving into film script doctoring on movies such as Speed and Toy Story — the latter earning him an Academy Award nomination for Best Original Screenplay.

Career highlights

The television series Buffy the Vampire Slayer, redeveloped from his 1992 film, became a major critical and cultural success and a landmark of genre television, noted for its wit, serialised storytelling, and feminist themes; its spin-off Angel followed. The space western Firefly was cancelled after one season but developed an enduring cult following, leading to the film continuation Serenity (2005), which Whedon wrote and directed. His web musical Dr. Horrible's Sing-Along Blog (2008), made during a writers' strike, won an Emmy Award.

At Marvel Studios, The Avengers grossed over $1.5 billion and helped define the modern superhero ensemble film; he also served as a creative consultant across the studio's "Phase Two" films and co-created Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.. He later took over Justice League (2017) for extensive reshoots.

Controversies

From 2020 onward, several actors and collaborators publicly accused Whedon of abusive and unprofessional behaviour on productions including Justice League, Buffy, and Angel. The allegations, which Whedon partly disputed while acknowledging some failings, severely damaged his reputation, and he departed the HBO series The Nevers, which he had created, in 2020.
