Attack on Titan

Attack on Titan (Japanese: 進撃の巨人, Shingeki no Kyojin, lit. "The Advancing Giant") is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Hajime Isayama. It is set in a world where humanity lives inside cities surrounded by enormous walls that protect them from gigantic man-eating humanoids referred to as Titans; the story follows Eren Yeager, who vows to exterminate the Titans after they bring about the destruction of his hometown and the death of his mother.

Author: Hajime Isayama

Publisher: Kodansha

Magazine: Bessatsu Shōnen Magazine

Original run: September 9, 2009 – April 9, 2021

Volumes: 34

Genres: Action, dark fantasy, post-apocalyptic

Plot

A century before the story begins, humanity retreated behind three concentric walls—Maria, Rose, and Sina—after the appearance of the Titans. When the Colossal Titan breaches Wall Maria, the district of Shiganshina falls, and Eren Yeager enlists in the military, joining the Survey Corps alongside his friends Mikasa Ackerman and Armin Arlert. Eren discovers he can transform into a Titan himself, and the struggle gradually uncovers the true history of the walls, the Titans, and the world beyond them.

Media

The manga was serialized in Kodansha's Bessatsu Shōnen Magazine from 2009 to 2021 and collected in 34 volumes. An anime television series, produced first by Wit Studio (seasons 1–3) and then MAPPA (the final season), aired from 2013 to 2023 and greatly expanded the franchise's international audience. The series has also spawned light novels, spin-off manga, video games, and a two-part Japanese live-action film.

Reception

Attack on Titan became one of the best-selling manga series of all time, with over 140 million copies in circulation. It won the Kodansha Manga Award and other honours, and has been praised for its storytelling, themes, and action, while prompting wide discussion of its political themes and its concluding arc.
