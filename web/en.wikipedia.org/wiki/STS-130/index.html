STS-130

STS-130 (ISS assembly flight 20A) was a NASA Space Shuttle mission to the International Space Station (ISS). Space Shuttle Endeavour launched on 8 February 2010 and landed on 21 February 2010, delivering the Tranquility module (Node 3) and the Cupola observatory module to the station.

Crew

The mission was commanded by George Zamka, with Terry Virts as pilot. Mission specialists were Nicholas Patrick, Robert Behnken, Stephen Robinson, and Kathryn Hire.

Mission highlights

During three spacewalks, astronauts installed the Tranquility node — built by the European Space Agency and the Italian Space Agency — on the port side of the Unity module, and attached the seven-windowed Cupola, which provides panoramic views for monitoring robotics operations and Earth observation. With the delivery of Tranquility, the US Orbital Segment of the ISS was essentially complete; the flight brought the station to about 90 per cent of its final mass. The mission also transferred water recycling and oxygen generation systems into the new module.
