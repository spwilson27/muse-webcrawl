Perfect Dark Zero
Developer: Rare
Publisher: Microsoft Game Studios
Release: November 2005
Genre: First-person shooter
Platform: Xbox 360

Perfect Dark Zero is a first-person shooter video game developed by Rare and published by Microsoft Game Studios. It was released as a launch title for the Xbox 360 in 2005. The game is a prequel to the Nintendo 64 game Perfect Dark (2000), following agent Joanna Dark earlier in her career as she works with her father Jack as bounty hunters before joining the Carrington Institute.

Gameplay

The campaign can be played solo or cooperatively, locally or online. Multiplayer supports up to 32 players online, with modes including deathmatch variants and the DarkOps objective-based modes, alongside bots. New mechanics included a cover system, dodge roll and an extensive arsenal of futuristic weapons with secondary fire functions.

Reception

The game received generally favourable reviews, with praise for its multiplayer, co-operative campaign and large maps, though it was widely felt not to match the impact of its predecessor. It sold over a million copies and was later included in the Rare Replay compilation with enhancements.
