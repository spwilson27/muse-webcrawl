Organ transplantation

Organ transplantation
DefinitionMoving an organ or tissue from one body to another
MilestonesFirst successful kidney transplant 1954; heart 1967
[Image: A surgical team performing an operation under bright theatre lights]

Organ transplantation is a medical procedure in which an organ is removed from one body and placed in another (or moved within the same body). Landmarks include the first successful kidney transplant (1954, between identical twins) and Christiaan Barnard's first human heart transplant (1967).

Challenges

Immunosuppression lets recipients keep grafts but invites infection — a balance managed for life. Organ shortage drives waiting lists, allocation ethics, xenotransplantation experiments (pig hearts and kidneys, 2020s) and lab-grown organ research. Transfusion is transplantation's liquid elder.
