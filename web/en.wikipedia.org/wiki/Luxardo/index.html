Luxardo

Girolamo Luxardo S.p.A.
Type: Private
Industry: Alcoholic beverages
Founded: 1821, Zara (Zadar)
Headquarters: Torreglia, Italy
Known for: Maraschino liqueur

Luxardo is an Italian liqueur company founded in 1821 by Girolamo Luxardo in Zara (now Zadar, Croatia), then part of the Austrian Empire. The company is best known for its Maraschino, a clear, slightly bittersweet liqueur distilled from sour marasca cherries, including the pits, which give it a subtle almond-like note.

History

Girolamo Luxardo, a Genoese consul in Zara, developed the liqueur from a domestic recipe and built a distillery whose product became famous across Europe. After the Second World War, Zara passed to Yugoslavia and the Luxardo family fled to Italy, where surviving members rebuilt the firm at Torreglia, near Padua, planting marasca cherry orchards in the Euganean Hills.

Products

Besides Maraschino Originale, Luxardo produces cherry brandy ("Sangue Morlacco"), amaretto, sambuca, limoncello, grappa, and preserved maraschino cherries used as cocktail garnishes. Its maraschino is an ingredient in classic cocktails such as the Aviation, the Hemingway Special, and the Last Word.

Today

The company remains family-owned, run by descendants of the founder, and exports its products worldwide.
