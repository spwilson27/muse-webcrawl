Servant of the People (TV series)

Servant of the People (Ukrainian: Слуга народу, Sluha narodu) is a Ukrainian political satire comedy television series created by and starring Volodymyr Zelenskyy, who plays Vasily Petrovych Holoborodko, a humble history teacher who unexpectedly becomes President of Ukraine. The series was produced by Kvartal 95 and aired on the 1+1 channel from 2015 to 2019.

Plot

Holoborodko, a divorced history teacher living with his parents, is secretly filmed by a student delivering an expletive-laden rant against corruption in Ukraine. The video goes viral, and a crowdfunding campaign registers him as a presidential candidate. To everyone's shock, he wins. As president, he refuses the trappings of office — cycling to work and living modestly — while battling oligarchs, corrupt officials, and the entrenched machinery of government.

Real-life parallels

The show's popularity became the foundation of Zelenskyy's real political career. In 2018, a political party was registered under the same name, Servant of the People, and on 31 December 2018 Zelenskyy announced his candidacy for president. He won the 2019 Ukrainian presidential election with roughly 73% of the vote in the second round, and his party won a parliamentary majority later that year.

Distribution and reception

A feature film, Servant of the People 2, was released in 2016. After Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022 thrust Zelenskyy onto the world stage, international demand for the series surged; it was licensed to broadcasters in many countries and returned to Netflix in several markets.
