Teleost

Teleostei
Kingdom: Animalia
Phylum: Chordata
Class: Actinopterygii
Infraclass: Teleostei
Species: ~30,000+

Teleosts (Teleostei) are by far the largest infraclass of ray-finned fishes, comprising about 96% of all living fish species — more than 30,000 species including salmon, cod, eels, flatfish, seahorses and tuna.

Characteristics

Teleosts have a movable maxilla and premaxilla, a homocercal tail fin, and highly protrusible jaws in many groups. Their diversification in the Mesozoic and Cenozoic made them dominant in oceans and fresh waters.

Evolution and classification

Teleosts originated in the Triassic. Major groups include Elopomorpha (eels), Osteoglossomorpha, Clupeomorpha (herrings), Ostariophysi (carps, catfish) and the vast Percomorpha (perch-like fishes).
