Marco Simoncelli

Marco Simoncelli (20 January 1987 – 23 October 2011) was an Italian Grand Prix motorcycle road racer. He won the 250cc World Championship in 2008 with Gilera and competed in the MotoGP class with the San Carlo Honda Gresini team.

Born in Cattolica and raised in Coriano in Emilia-Romagna, Simoncelli began racing minibikes as a child and won the European 125cc Championship in 2002 before debuting in the 125cc world championship. He moved to the 250cc class in 2006, winning his first Grand Prix in 2008 and taking that year's title. In 2010 he stepped up to MotoGP, earning a reputation for an aggressive, crowd-pleasing riding style and distinctive tall frame and voluminous hair. He achieved his first MotoGP pole positions and two podium finishes in 2011, including second place at Phillip Island a week before his death.

Death and legacy

On 23 October 2011, during the second lap of the Malaysian Grand Prix at the Sepang International Circuit, Simoncelli lost control of his motorcycle, which veered across the track into the path of Colin Edwards and Valentino Rossi. He suffered fatal injuries and died that afternoon, aged 24. The race was cancelled.

The Misano World Circuit was renamed Misano World Circuit Marco Simoncelli in his honor, and his racing number 58 was retired from Grand Prix racing. The Simoncelli Foundation, established by his family, supports charitable projects, and his father Paolo later founded the SIC58 Squadra Corse junior racing team.
