List of first overall NFL draft picks

The first overall pick in the NFL draft is awarded to the team with the worst record in the preceding season. The first draft was held in 1936, when the Philadelphia Eagles selected Heisman Trophy winner Jay Berwanger, who never played in the NFL. Quarterbacks have been the most common selection in the modern era.

Notes
