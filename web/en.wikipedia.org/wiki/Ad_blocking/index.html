Ad blocking

Ad blocking is the removal or alteration of advertising content in a web page, typically by a browser extension, using filtering rules that match known advertising scripts, images and network requests. Popular tools have included Adblock Plus, uBlock Origin and built-in browser blockers.

Publishers dependent on advertising revenue have responded with paywalls, ad-block detection walls and 'acceptable ads' programmes, and the practice has raised debates about user privacy, tracking, page performance and the economics of free online content.

Blocking also extends beyond browsers: network-level tools such as Pi-hole filter advertising domains for entire networks, and mobile operating systems and browsers increasingly restrict third-party tracking by default.
