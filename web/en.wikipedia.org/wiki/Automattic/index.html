Automattic Inc. is an American global distributed company founded in August 2005 by Matt Mullenweg, the co-creator of the open-source WordPress content management system. Its best-known product is WordPress.com, a hosted blogging and website platform. The company's name is a play on "automatic" and its founder's first name, "Matt".

History and products

Automattic was founded to commercialise services around WordPress without controlling the open-source project, which is stewarded by the WordPress Foundation. Its early products included the Akismet anti-spam service, the Gravatar avatar service and the bbPress forum software. Later acquisitions and launches added the Jetpack plug-in suite, the WooCommerce e-commerce platform (acquired with WooThemes in 2015), the VaultPress and WPScan services, and the note-taking app Simplenote.

In 2019 Automattic acquired the social blogging platform Tumblr from Verizon for a reported nominal sum, and in 2021 it acquired the journaling app Day One and the podcast app Pocket Casts. A 2019 funding round valued the company at about $3 billion; a 2021 round raised its valuation to about $7.5 billion.

Distributed work

Automattic is noted as a pioneer of fully distributed work: it closed its San Francisco office in 2017 after concluding that few staff used it, and its roughly 2,000 employees work from dozens of countries, communicating through internal blogs (called "P2s") and chat rather than email. The company publishes an "Automattic Creed" emphasising open source, transparency and distributed autonomy. From October 2024, a dispute between Mullenweg/Automattic and the hosting company WP Engine over trademarks and contributions to WordPress drew wide attention and litigation.
