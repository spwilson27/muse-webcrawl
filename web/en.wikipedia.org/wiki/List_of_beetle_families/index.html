List of beetle families

Beetles (order Coleoptera) are the largest order of insects, with roughly 400,000 described species grouped into about 180 families across four suborders: Adephaga, Polyphaga, Myxophaga and Archostemata. The following is a selection of the most prominent families.

Suborders Myxophaga and Archostemata

  • Myxophaga — minute aquatic beetles: Hydroscaphidae (skiff beetles), Lepiceridae, Sphaeriusidae, Torridincolidae.
  • Archostemata — a small, relict group: Crowsoniellidae, Cupedidae (reticulated beetles), Jurodidae, Micromalthidae (telephone-pole beetle), Ommatidae.
