Tung Chung line

The Tung Chung line is one of the ten lines of the MTR rapid transit system in Hong Kong. It runs from Hong Kong station in Central to Tung Chung on Lantau Island, serving the new town of Tung Chung and providing an interchange with the Airport Express, which shares its tracks for part of the route.

History

The line was built as part of the Airport Core Programme supporting the new Hong Kong International Airport at Chek Lap Kok. It opened on 22 June 1998, a few weeks before the airport itself, crossing the Tsing Ma Bridge — one of the world's longest suspension bridges carrying both road and rail traffic. Sunny Bay station opened in 2005 to serve Hong Kong Disneyland, connecting with the Disneyland Resort line.

Route and stations

From Hong Kong station, the line calls at Kowloon, Olympic, Nam Cheong, Lai King, Tsing Yi, Sunny Bay, and Tung Chung. It shares infrastructure with the Airport Express between Hong Kong and Tsing Yi. A proposed extension, the Tung Chung line extension to Tung Chung West and Tung Chung East stations, is under construction to serve new housing areas.
