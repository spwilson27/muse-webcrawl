Sonny John Moore (born 15 January 1988), known professionally as Skrillex, is an American DJ, record producer and singer-songwriter. He rose to fame as the frontman of the post-hardcore band From First to Last before reinventing himself as a solo electronic producer whose aggressive dubstep sound — often labelled "brostep" — helped drive the worldwide EDM boom of the early 2010s. He has won nine Grammy Awards, among the most of any electronic artist.

Early life and From First to Last

Moore was born in Highland Park, Los Angeles, and raised partly in San Francisco before returning to Los Angeles, where he attended a private arts academy and was home-schooled after bullying. At 16 he contacted the band From First to Last, intending to play guitar, but was recruited as lead vocalist. He sang on the albums Dear Diary, My Teen Angst Has a Body Count (2004) and Heroine (2006) before leaving in 2007 amid vocal problems and a desire to pursue solo work.

Solo breakthrough

Moore began producing electronic music in Los Angeles clubs and released the My Name Is Skrillex EP as a free download in 2010. Signed to deadmau5's Mau5trap label, he released Scary Monsters and Nice Sprites (2010), whose title track and "Bangarang" (from the 2011 EP of the same name) became global hits. His remix of Benny Benassi's "Cinema" and collaborations such as "Make It Bun Dem" with Damian Marley broadened his reach.

Labels, duos and later work

In 2011 Moore founded the label and creative collective Owsla, which launched artists including Porter Robinson, Zedd and Marshmello. He formed the duo Dog Blood with Boys Noize and, with Diplo, Jack Ü, whose 2015 album featured the Justin Bieber hit "Where Are Ü Now." His debut studio album Recess topped dance charts in 2014. He produced for Bieber (Purpose), Lady Gaga, Beyoncé and others, and in 2023 released back-to-back albums Quest for Fire and Don't Get Too Close, followed by the 2025 album abbreviated as F*ck U Skrillex…, marking a return to heavier dubstep.

Style and influence

Skrillex's productions are characterised by chopped vocal samples, abrasive FM-synthesis bass "growls," and abrupt drops. Though polarising among dubstep purists, he is credited with bringing the genre from UK clubs to American mainstream audiences and reshaping the sound of pop production in the 2010s.
