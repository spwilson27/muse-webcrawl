Detectorists is a British comedy-drama television series written and directed by Mackenzie Crook, who stars alongside Toby Jones as Andy Stone and Lance Stater, two metal-detecting enthusiasts in the fictional Essex town of Danebury.

Reception

The series, which ran for three series from 2014 to 2017 plus Christmas specials, was praised for its gentle humour and won the BAFTA for Best Scripted Comedy in 2018.
