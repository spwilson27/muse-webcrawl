Gangan Comics

Gangan (ガンガン, Gangan) is a manga imprint owned by Square Enix Holdings. It originated as a manga imprint of Enix before the company merged with Square in 2003. The imprint publishes manga in several magazines aimed at different reader demographics, including Monthly Shōnen Gangan, Monthly GFantasy, Monthly Big Gangan, and online through Gangan Online and Manga UP!.

Magazines

Monthly Shōnen Gangan

Monthly Shōnen Gangan (月刊少年ガンガン) was launched by Enix in 1991. It is a monthly shōnen manga magazine that has serialized such popular series as Fullmetal Alchemist by Hiromu Arakawa, Soul Eater by Atsushi Ohkubo, and The World God Only Knows's contemporary competitors in the fantasy-adventure genre. It is known for fantasy manga with strong action elements.

Monthly GFantasy

Monthly GFantasy (月刊Gファンタジー), launched in 1993, publishes fantasy manga aimed at a mixed audience and has serialized series such as Black Butler, Pandora Hearts, and Durarara!!.

Other magazines

  • Monthly Big Gangan — a monthly seinen magazine launched in 2010.
  • Young Gangan — a biweekly seinen magazine launched in 2004.
  • Gangan Online — a free web magazine launched in 2008.
  • Manga UP! — a manga app launched in 2017.
