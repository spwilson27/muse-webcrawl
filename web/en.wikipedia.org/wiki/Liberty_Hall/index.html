Dublin

Liberty Hall (Irish: Halla na Saoirse) in Dublin, Ireland, is the headquarters of the Services, Industrial, Professional and Technical Union (SIPTU). The present building, completed in 1965 and rising 59.4 metres (195 ft), was one of Ireland's first high-rise buildings. The original Liberty Hall on the site was a centre of the Irish Citizen Army and trade-union movement; the socialist leaders James Connolly and Jim Larkin were based there, and the proclamation banners of the 1916 Easter Rising period — including "We serve neither King nor Kaiser, but Ireland" — were displayed on it. Proclamations of the Irish Republic were printed in its basement.

United States

  • Liberty Hall (Frankfort, Kentucky) — a historic house museum, built 1796–1800, home of Senator John Brown.
  • Liberty Hall (Crawfordville, Georgia) — the plantation home of Alexander H. Stephens, vice president of the Confederacy.
  • Liberty Hall Academy — the predecessor institution of Washington and Lee University in Lexington, Virginia.
  • Liberty Hall, Kean University in Union, New Jersey — a mansion museum dating to 1772.
  • Liberty Hall (Lamoni, Iowa) — associated with the early history of the Reorganized Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints.

Other uses

  • Liberty Hall, Kingston, Jamaica — the headquarters of Marcus Garvey's Universal Negro Improvement Association in Jamaica, now a museum.
  • Liberty Hall (film) — a 2014 play/film production.
  • Liberty Hall is also the name of theatres and music venues in several cities, including Lawrence, Kansas, and Tyler, Texas.
