Planet Express, Inc. is a fictional interplanetary delivery company in the animated television series Futurama, created by Matt Groening. Founded and owned by Professor Hubert J. Farnsworth, the company operates from a hangar and office building in New New York in the year 3000 and serves as the workplace of the show's main characters and the vehicle for their adventures.

Premise

Farnsworth runs Planet Express largely as a means of funding his research and inventions; its finances are perpetually precarious and it competes with the ruthless conglomerate MomCorp. Deliveries take the crew to planets and situations across the galaxy, typically with catastrophic complications. The company's delivery ship — painted bright green with the firm's red logo — was designed by Farnsworth and features prominently in the series; its engines (in an oft-quoted joke) work by moving the universe around the ship rather than the ship through the universe.

Main crew

The crew comprises Captain Turanga Leela, the ship's competent one-eyed pilot; Philip J. Fry, a 20th-century pizza delivery boy cryogenically frozen for a thousand years and employed as delivery boy; Bender, an alcoholic, kleptomaniac bending robot; intern Amy Wong; bureaucrat Hermes Conrad; and staff physician Dr. Zoidberg, a lobster-like alien. Supporting employees include accountant LaBarbara Conrad (off-site) and janitor Scruffy.

Reception

Planet Express's ship and logo are among the most recognisable visual motifs of Futurama. The fictional company anchors the series' workplace-comedy structure, framing episodic plots around delivery missions.
