Hailey Bieber

Hailey Rhode Bieber (née Baldwin; born November 22, 1996) is an American model, socialite, and businesswoman. She has been featured in advertising campaigns for brands such as Guess, Ralph Lauren, and Tommy Hilfiger, and in 2022 founded the skincare brand Rhode. She is married to Canadian singer Justin Bieber.

Hailey Bieber, model and founder of Rhode.

Early life

Bieber was born in Tucson, Arizona, to actor Stephen Baldwin, the youngest of the Baldwin brothers, and Kennya Deodato Baldwin, a Brazilian graphic designer of Portuguese and Italian descent. She was named after Halley's Comet. As a child she trained in ballet before an injury redirected her to modelling; she was homeschooled as a teenager.

Career

Signed to Ford Models, Bieber made her runway debut for Topshop in 2014 and went on to appear in campaigns and editorials for Vogue, Elle, and other magazines, and to walk for major fashion houses. She co-hosted the TBS show Drop the Mic beginning in 2017. In June 2022 she launched Rhode, a skincare line built around "glazed" skin aesthetics, which became a commercially successful celebrity beauty brand; in 2025 it was announced that e.l.f. Beauty would acquire Rhode in a deal valued at up to $1 billion.

Personal life

She married Justin Bieber in a civil ceremony in New York in September 2018, followed by a wedding celebration in South Carolina in 2019. Their son, Jack Blues Bieber, was born in August 2024. Bieber has spoken publicly about health issues including a patent foramen ovale procedure after a mini-stroke in 2022 and an ovarian cyst.
