Anna Magnani

Anna Magnani (7 March 1908 – 26 September 1973) was an Italian actress, regarded as one of the greatest of Italian cinema and a central figure of neorealism. She won the Academy Award for Best Actress for the English-language film The Rose Tattoo (1955), the first Italian actress to win that award.

Born7 March 1908, Rome, Italy
Died26 September 1973, Rome
Notable filmsRome, Open City; Bellissima; Mamma Roma; The Rose Tattoo

Career

Born in Rome and raised in poverty, Magnani sang in nightclubs and acted in theatre before film. Her searing performance as Pina in Roberto Rossellini's Rome, Open City (1945) made her an international symbol of wartime Italy. She starred in Luchino Visconti's Bellissima (1951) and Pier Paolo Pasolini's Mamma Roma (1962).

Later work and legacy

Tennessee Williams wrote The Rose Tattoo for her; its film version earned her the Oscar. She appeared in Jean Renoir's The Golden Coach and Fellini's Roma. Volcanic, earthy and unglamorous, she embodied passionate working-class womanhood and influenced generations of Italian actresses.
