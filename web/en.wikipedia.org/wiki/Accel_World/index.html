Accel World (Japanese: アクセル・ワールド, Akuseru Wārudo) is a Japanese light novel series written by Reki Kawahara and illustrated by HiMA. The series began publication in February 2009 under ASCII Media Works' Dengeki Bunko imprint. It has been adapted into manga series, a 24-episode anime television series by Sunrise (2012), the film Accel World: Infinite Burst (2016), and several video games.

Plot

In 2046, people use Neuro-Linkers to interface with virtual networks. Haruyuki Arita, a bullied middle-school student, is introduced by the school's vice-president, Kuroyukihime, to "Brain Burst", a secret augmented-reality fighting program that accelerates users' thought processes a thousandfold. Players duel to earn points and defend their ability to accelerate, and Kuroyukihime seeks Haruyuki's help to reach Level 10 and meet the program's creator.

Reception

The series won the 15th Dengeki Novel Prize (Grand Prize) in 2008 under its manuscript title. Kawahara is also the author of Sword Art Online, and the two series share a loosely connected future setting. The light novels have sold millions of copies in Japan and are licensed in English by Yen Press.
