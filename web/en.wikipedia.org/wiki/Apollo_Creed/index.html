Apollo Creed

Apollo Creed is a fictional character from the Rocky film series, played by Carl Weathers. A flamboyant, undefeated world heavyweight boxing champion, Creed serves first as Rocky Balboa's opponent, then as his trainer and close friend, and his legacy drives the spin-off Creed films centred on his son, Adonis Creed.

Story

In Rocky (1976), Creed, seeking a publicity-rich title defence for the American Bicentennial, gives unknown club fighter Rocky Balboa a title shot; Rocky goes the distance and Creed keeps the belt by split decision. He loses the rematch in Rocky II (1979). Retired, he trains Rocky in Rocky III (1982) and comes out of retirement for an exhibition against Soviet champion Ivan Drago in Rocky IV (1985), during which Drago kills him in the ring.

Character

Creed was modelled partly on Muhammad Ali — talkative, theatrical and supremely confident, entering fights in Uncle Sam costume — and his name combines the god Apollo with a surname suggesting belief and pride. Weathers's performance made Creed one of the series' most popular characters; the character's son, played by Michael B. Jordan, headlines Creed (2015), Creed II (2018) and Creed III (2023).
