Peacock is an American over-the-top video streaming service owned and operated by Peacock TV LLC, a subsidiary of NBCUniversal, itself owned by Comcast. Named after NBC's longtime peacock logo, the service launched on 15 July 2020, following an early preview for Comcast customers in April of that year.

Content

Peacock's library combines current and classic NBC television series (including The Office, Parks and Recreation, and Saturday Night Live), films from Universal Pictures and its sister studios, Bravo and other NBCUniversal cable programming, and original series such as Poker Face, Bel-Air, and Dr. Death. It has become a significant sports platform, streaming Premier League football, WWE events, Sunday Night Football, Big Ten athletics, and — beginning with Paris 2024 — serving as the US streaming home of the Olympic Games.

Business model

Peacock originally offered a free advertising-supported tier alongside paid Premium plans; the free tier was closed to new subscribers in 2023 and later discontinued for existing users. The service passed roughly 40 million paid subscribers in the mid-2020s while NBCUniversal worked to narrow its streaming losses.
