Table d'hôte

Table d'hôte (French: "the host's table") is a menu in which a multi-course meal with only a few choices at each course is offered at a fixed total price, in contrast to à la carte dining where items are priced separately.

Usage

The format, sometimes called a prix fixe menu, is common in traditional French restaurants and at communal host tables in inns, where guests eat what the host serves.
