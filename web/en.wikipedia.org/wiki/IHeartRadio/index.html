IHeartRadio

Streaming radio and podcast platform by iHeartMedia

The iHeartRadio logo

iHeartRadio is an American freemium broadcast, podcast and streaming radio platform owned by iHeartMedia, launched in 2008. It aggregates live streams of hundreds of US radio stations, custom artist stations, playlists and, after iHeartMedia's growth in podcasting, one of the largest podcast catalogues in the United States.

History

The service began as the website iheartmusic.com and a mobile app streaming the stations of Clear Channel (the former name of iHeartMedia). It expanded into custom stations to compete with Pandora, launched paid tiers (iHeartRadio Plus and All Access) in 2016–17, and became the namesake of the iHeartRadio Music Awards, the iHeartRadio Music Festival in Las Vegas, and the iHeartCountry and ALTer EGO events.

Features

Free accounts can listen to live radio and curated stations; subscription tiers add on-demand playback, offline listening and unlimited skips. The service is available on mobile, web, connected cars, smart speakers and game consoles, primarily in the US, Canada, Mexico, Australia and New Zealand (some under licence).
