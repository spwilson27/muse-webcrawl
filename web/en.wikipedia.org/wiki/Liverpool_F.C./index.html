Liverpool Football Club is a professional football club based in Liverpool, England, competing in the Premier League. Founded in 1892 after a dispute between Everton and the owner of Anfield, Liverpool became England's most successful club in European competition and one of the most famous in the world.

History

Bill Shankly, manager from 1959, rebuilt Liverpool into a powerhouse and a social institution ("football is not a matter of life and death — it's much more important than that", as he joked). His successors Bob Paisley and Joe Fagan made Liverpool Europe's dominant team in the 1970s–80s, winning four European Cups in eight seasons (1977, 1978, 1981, 1984). The club's history is marked by tragedy: at Heysel in 1985, 39 Juventus fans died when a wall collapsed amid crowd trouble before the European Cup final, leading to a ban on English clubs in Europe; at Hillsborough in 1989, 97 Liverpool fans were unlawfully killed in a crush — a disaster finally judged, after decades of campaigning by families, to have been caused by police failures, not the fans. Rafael Benítez's team won the 2005 Champions League in Istanbul, coming back from 0–3 down against AC Milan. Under Jürgen Klopp (2015–24), Liverpool won the Champions League in 2019 and ended a 30-year wait for the league title in 2020; his successor Arne Slot won the Premier League again in 2024–25, equalling Manchester United's record of 20 titles.
