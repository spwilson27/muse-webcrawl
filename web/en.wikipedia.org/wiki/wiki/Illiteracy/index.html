Illiteracy

Image: Illiteracy

Illiteracy is the inability to read or write. The opposite, literacy, is usually defined as the ability to read and write at a level sufficient for communication and understanding ideas in a society.

Global rates

According to UNESCO estimates, the global adult literacy rate is around 86–87 percent, with illiteracy concentrated in parts of sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia, and higher rates among women than men. Functional illiteracy describes reading and writing skills insufficient to manage everyday tasks despite basic schooling.

Efforts

International campaigns, compulsory education and adult literacy programmes have driven long-term declines in illiteracy since the 19th century.
