Style guide

Image: Style guide

A style guide is a set of standards for the writing, formatting, and design of documents. It is often called a style manual or manual of style when published as a book.

Purpose

Style guides promote consistency in spelling, punctuation, capitalisation, citation and typography within an organisation or publication. Well-known examples include The Chicago Manual of Style, the Associated Press Stylebook used in journalism, and the APA Publication Manual used in academic social sciences.

House style

Publishers and companies maintain house styles that may adopt a general manual and add their own rules for branding, terminology and inclusivity.
