Legal writing

Image: Legal writing

Legal writing involves the analysis of fact patterns and presentation of arguments in legal memoranda and briefs. It is a type of technical writing used by lawyers, judges and others in law to express legal analysis and rights and duties.

Categories

Legal writing is commonly divided into predictive writing (memoranda analysing likely outcomes), persuasive writing (briefs and motions advocating a position), and legal drafting (contracts, statutes and other instruments that create legal rules and obligations).

Style

Modern legal-writing instruction emphasises clarity and the plain-English movement has pushed against traditional legalese, while citation formats such as the Bluebook govern references to authority.
