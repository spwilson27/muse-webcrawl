Accessibility

Image: Accessibility

Accessibility is the design of products, devices, services, vehicles, or environments so as to be usable by people with disabilities. The concept aims to enable access for people with a range of abilities, including those with physical, sensory, or cognitive impairments.

Areas

Accessibility covers built environments (ramps, lifts, accessible toilets), transport, information and communications technology (including web accessibility under guidelines such as WCAG), and education. Many countries have legislation, such as the Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990, mandating accessible design.

Assistive technology

Assistive technologies — screen readers, captions, hearing loops, mobility aids — work together with accessible design to remove barriers to participation.
