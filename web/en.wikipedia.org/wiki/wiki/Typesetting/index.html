Typesetting

Image: Typesetting

Typesetting is the composition of text by means of arranging physical type (in traditional printing) or digital glyphs. Stored text is composed in a language, with typographic considerations such as typeface, point size, line length, leading and kerning.

History

For centuries typesetting was done by hand with movable type. Mechanical hot-metal systems such as the Linotype (1884) automated composition, followed by phototypesetting and, from the 1980s, desktop publishing software.

Digital typesetting

Modern systems include TeX/LaTeX for technical documents and professional page-layout applications; typography rules guide legibility and aesthetics in both print and screen media.
