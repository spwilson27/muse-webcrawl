Curriculum

Image: Curriculum

In education, a curriculum is broadly defined as the totality of student experiences that occur in the educational process. The term often refers specifically to a planned sequence of instruction, or to a view of the student's experiences in terms of the educator's or school's instructional goals.

Types

Curricula may be explicit (the subjects taught), hidden (unintended lessons of schooling), or null (what is not taught). Many jurisdictions set a national curriculum prescribing content and standards for schools.

Development

Curriculum development involves selecting content, sequencing it, choosing teaching methods and assessment, and evaluating outcomes; influential models include Ralph Tyler's objectives model of 1949.
