OpenStax

Image: OpenStax

OpenStax is a nonprofit educational technology initiative based at Rice University. Since 2012, it publishes peer-reviewed, openly licensed textbooks that are free online in web and PDF formats and available in print at low cost.

History

OpenStax grew out of the Connexions project founded by Richard Baraniuk. Its first titles, published in 2012, covered physics and sociology; the catalogue has since expanded to dozens of titles in science, mathematics, business, humanities and social sciences used widely in United States higher education.

Funding

The initiative is supported by philanthropic foundations including the Gates Foundation, and aims to improve educational access and relieve textbook costs for students.
