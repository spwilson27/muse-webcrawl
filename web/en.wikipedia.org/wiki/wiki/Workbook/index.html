Workbook

A workbook is a textbook of problems, exercises and practice material that students complete and write in, typically used alongside a main textbook in schools. Workbooks are common in subjects such as mathematics, languages and science, where repeated practice helps reinforce concepts introduced in lessons.

Unlike a conventional textbook, which is kept for reference and often reused by later classes, a workbook is a consumable: it is designed to be written in by a single student, with blank spaces, tables and question boxes provided for answers. Many workbooks are perforated or spiral-bound so pages can be removed and handed in.

Digital workbooks

With the growth of educational technology, digital and interactive workbooks have become widespread. Spreadsheet software also uses the term: in Microsoft Excel and similar programs, a "workbook" is a file containing one or more worksheets.
