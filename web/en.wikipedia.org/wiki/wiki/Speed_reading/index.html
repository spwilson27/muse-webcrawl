Speed reading

Image: Speed reading

Speed reading is any of several techniques claiming to improve one's ability to read quickly. Methods include skimming, scanning, meta-guiding the eyes, and eliminating subvocalisation, with the goal of reading faster than the typical 200–400 words per minute.

History

Speed reading was popularised in the late 1950s by Evelyn Wood's Reading Dynamics courses. Later programmes and apps have claimed extremely high reading speeds.

Research

Scientific reviews find that very high speeds come with substantial comprehension losses, and that skilled reading involves processes that place practical limits on speed; skimming is considered a distinct, useful strategy rather than full reading.
