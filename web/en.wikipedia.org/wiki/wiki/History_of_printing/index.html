History of printing

Image: History of printing

The history of printing starts as early as 3000 BC, when the proto-Elamite and Sumerian civilizations used cylinder seals to certify documents. Printing developed through woodblock printing in China, movable type, and the European printing press.

Woodblock and movable type

Woodblock printing was in use in Tang-dynasty China by the 7th century; the oldest surviving printed book is the Diamond Sutra of 868 AD. In the 1040s Bi Sheng invented movable type made of ceramic, and metal movable type was later developed in Korea.

The printing press

Around 1440 Johannes Gutenberg developed a movable-type printing press in Mainz, Germany, which dramatically lowered the cost of books and helped spread literacy, science and the Reformation across Europe.
