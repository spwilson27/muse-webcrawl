Phonics

Image: Phonics

Phonics is a method for teaching reading and writing of an alphabetic language by developing learners' phonemic awareness — the ability to hear, identify and manipulate phonemes — and teaching the correspondence between sounds (phonemes) and the spelling patterns (graphemes) that represent them.

Approaches

Synthetic phonics teaches letter sounds and blending from the start, while analytic phonics infers sounds from whole words. Phonics is one part of reading instruction alongside phonemic awareness, fluency, vocabulary and comprehension.

Evidence and debate

Research reviews such as the US National Reading Panel (2000) found systematic phonics instruction effective for beginning readers; its prominence within balanced-literacy debates continues.
