Prose

Image: Prose

Prose is a form of written or spoken language that follows the natural flow of speech, uses ordinary grammatical structures, and is organised into sentences and paragraphs, as opposed to verse, which traditionally uses metrical rhythm.

Characteristics

Prose is the most common form of writing, used in novels, essays, journalism, letters and most non-fiction. Its structures reflect everyday communication rather than the rhythmic or rhyming schemes of poetry.

History

Although poetry is older, prose developed with the growth of literacy and was refined in Greek, Latin and later vernacular literature; the rise of printing cemented prose as the dominant written form.
