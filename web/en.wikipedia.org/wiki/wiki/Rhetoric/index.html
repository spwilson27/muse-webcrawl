Rhetoric

Image: Rhetoric

Rhetoric is the art of persuasion. It is one of the three ancient arts of discourse (trivium) along with grammar and logic, and is the study of how language, whether written or spoken, can be used to inform, persuade, or motivate audiences.

Classical rhetoric

Aristotle defined rhetoric as "the faculty of observing in any given case the available means of persuasion", identifying the appeals of ethos (credibility), pathos (emotion) and logos (reason). Classical training organised the art into five canons: invention, arrangement, style, memory and delivery.

Modern use

Today rhetoric is studied across composition, communication, political science and literary studies, covering everything from speeches to visual and digital media.
