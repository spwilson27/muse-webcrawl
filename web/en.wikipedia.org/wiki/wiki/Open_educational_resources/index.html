Open educational resources

Image: Open educational resources

Open educational resources (OER) are teaching, learning and research materials in any medium that reside in the public domain or have been released under an open license permitting no-cost access, use, adaptation and redistribution.

Definition and licensing

The term was coined at a UNESCO forum in 2002. OER commonly use Creative Commons licences and include textbooks, course materials, modules, videos, tests and software.

Movement

The OER movement aims to reduce cost barriers to education; notable projects include OpenStax textbooks, MIT OpenCourseWare and Khan Academy.
