List of countries by literacy rate

Image: List of countries by literacy rate

This is a list of countries by literacy rate. The figures are mostly collected by the UNESCO Institute for Statistics and generally refer to the percentage of the population aged 15 and over who can read and write.

Overview

Many high-income countries report literacy rates of 99 percent, a figure often treated as a nominal maximum because of differing measurement methods. The lowest rates, in some cases below 40 percent, are found in parts of West Africa and Afghanistan. Youth literacy rates (ages 15–24) are higher than adult rates in most countries, reflecting expanded schooling.

Methodology

Some countries measure literacy through household surveys or censuses with self-reporting, while others use educational attainment as a proxy; figures are therefore not perfectly comparable.
