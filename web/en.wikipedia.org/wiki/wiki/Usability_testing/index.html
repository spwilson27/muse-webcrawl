Usability testing

Image: Usability testing

Usability testing is a technique used in user-centered interaction design to evaluate a product by testing it on users. Participants attempt representative tasks while observers record where they succeed, struggle or make errors.

Methods

Common approaches include moderated think-aloud sessions, remote unmoderated testing, and A/B testing of design variants. Jakob Nielsen's research popularised the idea that testing with about five users uncovers most usability problems in iterative design.

Goals

The aim is to identify usability problems, collect qualitative and quantitative data such as task success and time-on-task, and determine participants' satisfaction with the product.
