History of writing

Image: History of writing

The history of writing traces the development of writing systems and how their use transformed societies. Writing emerged independently in several regions, beginning with proto-writing systems using ideographic and mnemonic symbols.

Origins

The earliest true writing — the cuneiform script of Mesopotamia and Egyptian hieroglyphs — appeared around 3400–3100 BC, developed from accounting tokens. Writing was later invented independently in China (oracle bone script, c. 1200 BC inscriptions with earlier precursors) and in Mesoamerica.

Alphabets

Alphabetic systems descend largely from Proto-Sinaitic script via Phoenician, giving rise to Greek, Latin, Cyrillic, Hebrew and Arabic scripts among others.
