Basic English

Image: Basic English

Basic English is a controlled language based on standard English, created by the British linguist and philosopher Charles Kay Ogden in the 1920s and described in his book Basic English: A General Introduction with Rules and Grammar (1930).

Vocabulary

The system uses a core vocabulary of 850 words, chosen to express the ideas of ordinary English through a small set of nouns, adjectives and 18 "operators" (verbs). Ogden intended it as an international auxiliary language and as a teaching tool for learners of English.

Influence

Basic English influenced later controlled languages and simplified English projects, and provided the wordlist basis for Voice of America's Special English.
