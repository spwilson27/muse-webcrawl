List of meteor showers

A meteor shower occurs when Earth passes through a stream of debris left by a comet or asteroid, producing many meteors radiating from a single point (the radiant) in the sky. This list gives the principal annual showers.

Observing

Showers are best seen after midnight under dark skies, with rates quoted as the zenithal hourly rate (ZHR). The Geminids and Perseids are generally the most reliable displays of the year.
