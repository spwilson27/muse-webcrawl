Creepypasta

Creepypastas are horror-related legends that have been shared and copied around the Internet. The term is a blend of "creepy" and "copypasta", text copied and pasted repeatedly across forums. User-generated paranormal stories are distributed in order to scare readers, and tales often range from ghost encounters and haunted objects to lost episodes of television shows, cursed video games, and mysterious disappearances.

History

Creepypasta emerged on imageboards and forums such as 4chan's /x/ (paranormal) board in the late 2000s, evolving from chain-letter-style horror and urban legends circulated by email and Usenet. Dedicated communities later formed on Reddit (notably r/nosleep, founded in 2010, where stories are treated as true), the Creepypasta Wiki, and YouTube narration channels.

Notable examples

The most famous creepypasta is Slender Man, a tall, faceless figure created by Eric Knudsen on the Something Awful forums in 2009, which inspired games, a film, and — controversially — real-world violence including the 2014 Waukesha stabbing. Other well-known examples include Jeff the Killer, Ben Drowned (a haunted Majora's Mask cartridge story), the Russian Sleep Experiment, and the SCP Foundation collaborative fiction project.
