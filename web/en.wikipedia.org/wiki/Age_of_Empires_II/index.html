Age of Empires II

Age of Empires II: The Age of Kings is a real-time strategy video game developed by Ensemble Studios and published by Microsoft in 1999. Set in the Middle Ages, it has players gather resources, build towns and armies, and advance through four ages — Dark, Feudal, Castle, and Imperial — while playing one of thirteen civilisations, each with unique units and technologies.

Gameplay and campaigns

The single-player campaigns follow historical figures including William Wallace, Joan of Arc, Saladin, Genghis Khan, and Frederick Barbarossa. An expansion, The Conquerors (2000), added civilisations such as the Aztecs and Maya. The game is remembered for its balanced multiplayer and its long-lived competitive community.

Re-releases

A high-definition edition appeared on Steam in 2013 with new expansions, and a Definitive Edition followed in 2019 with remastered graphics, additional civilisations and campaigns, and continuing updates — unusually sustained support for a game of its era.
