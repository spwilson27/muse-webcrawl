Gang Starr
Origin: Brooklyn, New York City, United States
Genre: East Coast hip hop, jazz rap
Years active: 1986–2006, 2019
Members: Guru (MC), DJ Premier (producer/DJ)

Gang Starr was an American hip hop duo formed in New York City, consisting of rapper Guru and producer DJ Premier. They are widely considered one of the most influential groups in hip hop history, known for combining jazz samples with hard-edged beats and socially conscious lyrics.


Gang Starr in performance

History

The original Gang Starr was founded in Boston in 1986; after the line-up dissolved, Guru (Keith Elam) teamed with Houston-born producer DJ Premier (Christopher Martin) in New York. Their albums Step in the Arena (1991), Daily Operation (1992), Hard to Earn (1994) and Moment of Truth (1998) are regarded as classics of East Coast hip hop.

Legacy

Guru died in 2010. In 2019 DJ Premier released the posthumous Gang Starr album One of the Best Yet, built around previously unreleased Guru vocals, featuring the single "Family and Loyalty" with J. Cole.
