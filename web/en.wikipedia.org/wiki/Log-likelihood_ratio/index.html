Log-likelihood ratio
Field: Statistics
Related: Likelihood ratio test, Bayes factor

The log-likelihood ratio is the logarithm of the ratio of the likelihoods of data under two competing hypotheses or models. Using the log transforms a product of probabilities into a sum and is central to hypothesis testing and model comparison.

('Definition', 'For hypotheses H1 and H0, the statistic is log( L(H1) / L(H0) ). Positive values favour H1 and negative values favour H0, with magnitude indicating strength of evidence.')

('Applications', "Log-likelihood ratios underlie the Neyman-Pearson lemma, likelihood-ratio tests, logistic regression, information theory, and sequential analysis such as Wald's sequential probability ratio test.")
