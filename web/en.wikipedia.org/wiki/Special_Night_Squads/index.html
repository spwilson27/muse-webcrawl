Special Night Squads

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Special Night Squads (SNS) were a joint British and Jewish counter-insurgency military unit raised in Mandatory Palestine in 1938 during the 1936–1939 Arab revolt. They were organised by the British officer Orde Wingate, a committed supporter of Zionism, and consisted of British soldiers together with volunteers from the Jewish Settlement Police and the Haganah.

Operations

The squads were based mainly in the Jezreel Valley and Galilee, guarding the Iraq Petroleum Company oil pipeline and Jewish settlements. Wingate trained his men in night fighting and offensive ambush tactics, taking the fight to Arab rebel bands rather than waiting to be attacked. Their methods were effective but controversial, and the squads were accused of brutality against Arab villagers.

Legacy

The SNS were disbanded in 1939 after Wingate was transferred out of Palestine. Nevertheless, the squads are regarded as a formative experience for many future commanders of the Israel Defense Forces, including Moshe Dayan and Yigal Allon, and as an early example of modern counter-insurgency night operations.
