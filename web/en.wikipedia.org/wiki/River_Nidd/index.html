River Nidd

The River Nidd is a tributary of the River Ouse in North Yorkshire, England. It rises in Nidderdale on the slopes of Great Whernside and flows about 95 km (59 mi) south-east, past Pateley Bridge, Ripley, and Knaresborough, to join the Ouse at Nun Monkton.

Course

The upper valley, Nidderdale, is an Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty containing the reservoirs of Angram, Scar House, and Gouthwaite, built to supply Bradford. The river disappears underground in places along its limestone reaches and passes the caves of Stump Cross Caverns and How Stean Gorge. At Knaresborough it flows beneath the famous railway viaduct and past Mother Shipton's Cave and the Petrifying Well.
