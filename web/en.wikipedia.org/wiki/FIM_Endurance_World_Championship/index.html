The FIM Endurance World Championship (EWC) is the premier worldwide endurance motorcycle racing series, sanctioned by the Fédération Internationale de Motocyclisme (FIM). Teams of two or three riders share a single production-based motorcycle over long-distance races lasting 8 or 24 hours, competing for manufacturers' and teams' world titles.

History

Endurance racing for motorcycles dates to events such as France's Bol d'Or, first run in 1922. A European endurance cup was organized from 1960 as the Coupe d'Endurance, and the series received full world championship status in 1980. After a period in the 1990s when the championship was contested over a single race, a multi-round series was re-established; since 2016 the championship has been promoted by Eurosport Events (later Warner Bros. Discovery Sports) under a long-term agreement with the FIM, and seasons have spanned calendar years (beginning in autumn) before returning to a calendar-year format.

Races

The championship's classic events include:

  • 24 Heures Motos at Le Mans, France
  • Bol d'Or (24 hours) at Circuit Paul Ricard, France
  • Suzuka 8 Hours in Japan — the series' marquee event, famous for factory entries and guest MotoGP and World Superbike riders
  • 8 Hours of Spa Motos at Spa-Francorchamps, Belgium, reviving the former Spa 24-hour tradition
  • Past rounds have included the 8 Hours of Sepang, the 12 Hours of Estoril, and races at Oschersleben and the Slovakiaring.

Classes and format

The top class, Formula EWC, allows extensive modification within a silhouette resembling production superbikes; the Superstock class restricts changes to near-showroom specification and competes for the FIM Endurance World Cup. Races run continuously through the night, with pit stops for fuel, tires, and rider changes. Points are also awarded for positions at intermediate marks (after 8 and 16 hours) in 24-hour races, and qualifying results grant bonus points.

Notable teams

Suzuki's endurance racing team (SERT, now Yoshimura SERT Motul) is the most successful in championship history, with numerous world titles. Other leading squads have included YART (Yamaha Austria Racing Team), F.C.C. TSR Honda France, BMW Motorrad World Endurance Team, and ERC Endurance Ducati. The championship is regarded as a test of reliability, logistics, and teamwork as much as outright speed.
