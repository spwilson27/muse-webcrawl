William, Prince of Wales (William Arthur Philip Louis; born 21 June 1982) is the heir apparent to the British throne. He is the elder son of King Charles III and Diana, Princess of Wales.

Born at St Mary's Hospital, London, William was educated at Wetherby School, Ludgrove School and Eton College. He studied geography at the University of St Andrews, graduating in 2005. During a gap year he spent time in Chile and Belize. After university he trained at Sandhurst and served in the British Army, the Royal Navy and the Royal Air Force, qualifying as a search and rescue helicopter pilot. He later worked as an air ambulance pilot for East Anglian Air Ambulance from 2015 to 2017.

William married Catherine Middleton at Westminster Abbey on 29 April 2011. The couple, formerly the Duke and Duchess of Cambridge, became Prince and Princess of Wales upon his father's accession in September 2022. They have three children: Prince George, Princess Charlotte and Prince Louis.

Early life and education

William was born during the reign of his grandmother, Queen Elizabeth II, and was second in the line of succession from birth. His mother Diana died in a car crash in Paris in 1997, when he was fifteen. At St Andrews he met Catherine Middleton, with whom he shared a flat as students.

Military career

After graduating from the Royal Military Academy Sandhurst in 2006, William was commissioned into the Blues and Royals. He subsequently undertook attachments with the Royal Navy and RAF, completing pilot training and serving with RAF Search and Rescue Force at RAF Valley on Anglesey, co-piloting Sea King helicopters on rescue missions until 2013.

Marriage and family

The wedding of Prince William and Catherine Middleton in 2011 was watched by a global television audience estimated in the hundreds of millions. As Duke and Duchess of Cornwall and of Cambridge, and since 2022 as Prince and Princess of Wales, the couple carry out official engagements on behalf of the King.

Public life and charity

William's charitable focus includes mental health — notably the Heads Together campaign launched with Catherine and Prince Harry in 2016 — homelessness, and the environment. In 2020 he founded the Earthshot Prize, a £50 million initiative awarding grants for environmental solutions over a decade.

Titles and styles

As the eldest son of the sovereign he is Duke of Cornwall and Duke of Rothesay; he was created Duke of Cambridge on his marriage and Prince of Wales on 9 September 2022, the day after his father's accession.
