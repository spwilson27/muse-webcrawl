California

The California coast at Big Sur with Highway 1

California is a state in the western United States, on the Pacific coast. With about 39 million residents it is the most populous US state, and at 423,970 square kilometres the third-largest by area. Its capital is Sacramento; its largest city is Los Angeles. California borders Oregon, Nevada, Arizona and the Mexican state of Baja California.

California's geography is famously varied: the Sierra Nevada (with Mount Whitney, the highest point in the contiguous US), the fertile Central Valley, the Mojave Desert, Death Valley (the lowest point in North America), coastal redwood forests with the tallest trees on Earth, and the San Andreas Fault. If it were a country, California's economy — about 4 trillion dollars — would rank among the world's five largest, driven by Silicon Valley technology, Hollywood entertainment, agriculture and trade. Spanish and Mexican rule preceded US statehood in 1850, hastened by the Gold Rush of 1849.
