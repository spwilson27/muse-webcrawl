Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation

Founded: 2007
Location: Seattle, Washington, U.S.
Affiliation: University of Washington
Director: Christopher Murray

The Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation (IHME) is a research institute working in the area of global health statistics and impact evaluation at the University of Washington in Seattle. It was launched in 2007 with funding from the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation and the state of Washington, under the direction of Christopher Murray.

Global Burden of Disease

IHME is best known for coordinating the Global Burden of Disease (GBD) study, the largest systematic effort to quantify health loss from hundreds of diseases, injuries, and risk factors across countries and over time. Its estimates of mortality, life expectancy, and disability-adjusted life years are widely cited by governments and international agencies.

Other work

The institute produces health financing analyses, forecasts of population and health trends, and — during the COVID-19 pandemic — widely followed models of infections, hospitalizations, and deaths. Its results are published in journals such as The Lancet and through open data-visualization tools.

Criticism

Some of IHME's methods and estimates have been debated by other health agencies, particularly where its modelled figures differ from World Health Organization statistics.
