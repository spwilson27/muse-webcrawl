Anakin Skywalker is a fictional character in the Star Wars franchise created by George Lucas. A Jedi Knight believed to be the "Chosen One" destined to bring balance to the Force, he falls to the dark side and becomes the Sith Lord Darth Vader; his story forms the central tragedy of the saga's prequel trilogy and his redemption is the climax of the original trilogy.

Story

Born a slave on Tatooine, Anakin is discovered by Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn, who frees him and brings him before the Jedi Council. Trained by Obi-Wan Kenobi, Anakin becomes a hero of the Clone Wars but secretly marries Senator Padmé Amidala. Manipulated by Chancellor Palpatine, who is secretly the Sith Lord Darth Sidious, Anakin turns to the dark side hoping to save Padmé from death, and is christened Darth Vader. After a duel with Kenobi on Mustafar leaves him maimed, he is rebuilt in black life-support armour. Decades later, confronted by his son Luke, Vader turns on the Emperor and destroys him, dying redeemed as Anakin Skywalker.

Portrayal

Jake Lloyd played the young Anakin in The Phantom Menace (1999), and Hayden Christensen played him in Attack of the Clones (2002), Revenge of the Sith (2005), the Obi-Wan Kenobi series and Ahsoka. Matt Lanter voices the character in the animated series The Clone Wars.
