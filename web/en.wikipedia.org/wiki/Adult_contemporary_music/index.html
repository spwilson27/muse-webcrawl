Adult contemporary music

Adult contemporary music (AC) is a form of radio-played popular music, ranging from 1960s easy listening and soft rock to contemporary ballads and up-tempo pop, usually aimed at adults aged 25–54.

Formats

Sub-formats include mainstream AC, soft AC, hot AC (more current and energetic, targeting younger adults), and urban and rhythmic AC variants. Billboard's Adult Contemporary chart, launched in 1961, tracks airplay.

Characteristics

AC programming emphasises melodic, inoffensive, familiar songs — ballads and mid-tempo hits by artists such as Elton John, Celine Dion, Billy Joel, Adele and Ed Sheeran — avoiding hard rock, hip-hop extremes and teen-oriented pop, though boundaries shift over time.
