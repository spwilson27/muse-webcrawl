Hypocrisy

Hypocrisy is the practice of engaging in the same behavior or activity for which one criticizes another or the practice of claiming to have moral standards or beliefs to which one's own behavior does not conform. In moral psychology, it is the failure to follow one's own expressed moral rules and principles.

Etymology

The word hypocrisy comes from the Greek ὑπόκρισις (hypokrisis), which means "jealous", "play-acting", "acting out", "coward" or "dissembling". The word hypocrite is from the Greek word ὑποκριτής (hypokritēs), meaning an actor or stage player.

Psychology

Hypocrisy has been studied in social psychology, where it can serve purposes such as signalling virtue, maintaining group membership, or cognitive dissonance reduction. Moral hypocrisy is the motivation to appear moral while avoiding the cost of actually being moral.
