Hamburger Sport-Verein e.V., commonly known as Hamburger SV, HSV or Hamburg, is a German sports club based in Hamburg, whose football team is one of the most famous in Germany. Founded in 1887 (through predecessor club SC Germania), it is among the country's oldest football clubs.

History

HSV won three German championships in the pre-Bundesliga era and three Bundesliga-era titles (1979, 1982, 1983). The peak came under coach Ernst Happel, when Hamburg won the European Cup in 1983, defeating Juventus in the final, having won the European Cup Winners' Cup in 1977. Star players of the era included Kevin Keegan, Felix Magath and Manfred Kaltz.

The "Dinosaur"

HSV was the only club to have played every Bundesliga season from the league's founding in 1963 — earning the nickname "the Dinosaur" and a stadium clock counting its top-flight tenure — until its first-ever relegation in 2018. The club's city rivals are FC St. Pauli, with whom it contests the Hamburg derby.
