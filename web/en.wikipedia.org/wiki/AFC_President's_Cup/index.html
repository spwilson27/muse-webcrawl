AFC President's Cup

AFC President's Cup
Founded: 2005
Abolished: 2014
Region: Asia (AFC)
Most successful: Regar-TadAZ (3 titles)

The AFC President's Cup was an annual continental club football competition between domestic champion clubs from football nations categorised as "emerging" by the Asian Football Confederation. It was held from 2005 until 2014.

History

The competition formed the third tier of Asian club football below the AFC Champions League and AFC Cup. Regar-TadAZ of Tajikistan won the first edition and a record three titles. After the 2014 edition the tournament was discontinued as its member associations were integrated into the AFC Cup qualifying structure.
