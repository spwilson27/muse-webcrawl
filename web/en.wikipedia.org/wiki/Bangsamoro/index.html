Bangsamoro

The Bangsamoro, officially the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM), is an autonomous region in the southern Philippines. The name combines the Malay word bangsa ("nation") with "Moro", the collective term for the Muslim peoples of Mindanao, the Sulu Archipelago and Palawan. The region's capital and largest city is Cotabato City.

History

Muslim sultanates such as Sulu and Maguindanao resisted Spanish and later American colonisation for centuries, and a Moro separatist insurgency began in the late 1960s, led first by the Moro National Liberation Front and then by the Moro Islamic Liberation Front (MILF). The Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (ARMM) was created in 1989 but was widely judged a failure. After the 2014 Comprehensive Agreement on the Bangsamoro between the Philippine government and the MILF, the Bangsamoro Organic Law was enacted in 2018 and ratified by plebiscite in January and February 2019, replacing the ARMM with the BARMM. The Bangsamoro Transition Authority, led by MILF figures, governs the region during a transition period ahead of regular parliamentary elections.

Geography and people

The region comprises the provinces of Basilan, Lanao del Sur, Maguindanao del Norte, Maguindanao del Sur, Sulu and Tawi-Tawi, together with Cotabato City and several municipalities of Cotabato province that voted to join. Its population is majority Muslim, belonging to groups including the Maguindanaon, Maranao, Tausug, Yakan and Sama, alongside Christian settlers and indigenous Lumad peoples.
