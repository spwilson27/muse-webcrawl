Sunn O))) (pronounced "sun") is an American drone metal band formed in Seattle in 1998 by Stephen O'Malley and Greg Anderson. The band takes its name and logo from the Sunn amplifier company, and its music is built from extremely slow, massively amplified, heavily distorted guitar drones played at volumes intended to be felt physically as much as heard. The two founders, performing in hooded robes amid stage fog before walls of amplifiers, form the core of a rotating cast of collaborators.

Music and performances

Sunn O)))'s compositions typically consist of extended, tectonic chord progressions with minimal conventional rhythm or melody, exploring resonance, feedback and the acoustic properties of performance spaces. Their concerts are renowned for extreme volume and a ritualistic atmosphere. A frequently repeated (and probably apocryphal) story claims their low frequencies caused physical effects on audiences; the band has generally encouraged the mythology while insisting the experience is about immersion in sound.

Collaborations and discography

The band has released numerous studio albums, including ØØ Void (2000), Black One (2005), Monoliths & Dimensions (2009), which incorporated choirs, orchestras and the vocals of Attila Csihar of Mayhem, Kannon (2015), and the Steve Albini-recorded Life Metal and Pyroclasts (both 2019). Collaborations include Altar (2006) with Japanese band Boris, Terrestrials (2014) with Ulver, and Soused (2014) with singer Scott Walker. Anderson also runs the Southern Lord label, central to the development of doom and drone metal.
