Knoxville, Tennessee

Knoxville

State: Tennessee

County: Knox

Founded: 1791

Named for: Henry Knox

Knoxville is a city in and the county seat of Knox County in the U.S. state of Tennessee. It is the third-most populous city in Tennessee after Nashville and Memphis and the principal city of the Knoxville metropolitan area.

History

Settled in 1786 and founded in 1791, Knoxville was the first capital of Tennessee. It prospered as a river and railroad town and hosted the 1982 World's Fair, for which the Sunsphere tower was built. The city is home to the flagship campus of the University of Tennessee, founded in 1794.

Geography and economy

Knoxville lies in the Tennessee River valley near the Great Smoky Mountains. Major employers include the Tennessee Valley Authority, headquartered downtown, the University of Tennessee, and nearby Oak Ridge National Laboratory.
