Roark Capital Group

Roark Capital Group is an American private equity firm based in Atlanta, Georgia. Founded in 2001 by Neal Aronson, the firm specialises in investments in franchised and multi-unit consumer businesses, particularly restaurant chains, as well as health, wellness and business services. The firm is named after Howard Roark, the protagonist of Ayn Rand's novel The Fountainhead.

Investments

Roark's best-known holding was Inspire Brands, the restaurant company it assembled from Arby's (acquired 2011) and expanded with the purchases of Buffalo Wild Wings, Sonic Drive-In, Jimmy John's, Dunkin' Brands and Baskin-Robbins, creating one of the largest restaurant companies in the world. Other current or former portfolio companies include:

  • Focus Brands (Auntie Anne's, Cinnabon, Jamba, Moe's Southwest Grill, Schlotzsky's, Carvel)
  • Carl's Jr. and Hardee's (CKE Restaurants)
  • Wingstop
  • Massage Envy
  • Orangetheory Fitness
  • Anytime Fitness (Self Esteem Brands)
  • Driven Brands
  • US Foods / Chefs' Store interests and ServiceMaster Brands
  • Subway — in 2023 Roark agreed to acquire the sandwich chain in one of the largest restaurant buyouts on record, completed in 2024.

Strategy

Roark focuses on franchise business models, in which local operators own and run outlets under a brand owner's system, providing the firm with royalty-based, capital-light economics. The firm has raised a succession of multi-billion-dollar funds from institutional investors and has managed tens of billions of dollars in assets.
