Early decision

Early decision (ED) or early acceptance is a common policy used in college admissions in the United States for admitting undergraduate students to baccalaureate universities and colleges. As the name implies, early decision allows students to apply early — typically in November — and receive a decision well in advance of the usual notification date, usually by mid-December.

Binding commitment

Unlike early action, which is non-binding, early decision is a binding plan: a student who is accepted under early decision must withdraw all other applications and enroll at that institution, provided the financial aid offer is adequate. Students typically sign an early decision agreement, also endorsed by a parent and school counselor, committing to attend if admitted.

Because of the binding commitment, applicants are advised to apply early decision only to a first-choice institution where they are certain they wish to enroll, and to be mindful that they cannot compare financial aid offers from multiple schools.

Early Decision II

Many colleges also offer a second round, Early Decision II, with application deadlines typically in January and decisions by February. It carries the same binding commitment but allows students more time in the autumn to prepare applications or improve their record.

Admission rates and debate

Early decision applicants are often admitted at higher rates than regular decision applicants, a fact that has generated debate. Supporters argue that the pool is self-selecting and that colleges benefit from enrolling students with demonstrated enthusiasm. Critics contend that the practice favors wealthier applicants who can commit without comparing aid packages, disadvantages students who need more time, and pressures applicants to choose a college before they are ready.

Release from commitment

Students may generally be released from an early decision commitment if the institution's financial aid package is insufficient to make attendance affordable. Colleges may also share lists of admitted early decision students with peer institutions.
