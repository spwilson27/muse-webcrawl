A. C. Gilbert Company
Founded: 1909
Founder: Alfred Carlton Gilbert
Headquarters: New Haven, Connecticut, US
Products: Erector Set, American Flyer trains, chemistry sets

The A. C. Gilbert Company was an American toy company based in New Haven, Connecticut, founded in 1909 by Alfred Carlton Gilbert, an athlete, magician and inventor. The company was best known for its Erector Set construction toys, American Flyer electric trains, and educational chemistry and science sets.

History

Gilbert began by selling magic sets under the Mysto Manufacturing name. In 1913 he introduced the Erector Set, inspired by the steel girders of railway construction, which became one of the most popular construction toys in America. The company later acquired the American Flyer line of model trains in 1938.

Products and legacy

Beyond toys, Gilbert marketed chemistry sets, microscopes and even an atomic energy laboratory kit in 1950. During the Second World War the company produced equipment for the military. The firm declined after Gilbert's death in 1961 and went out of business in 1967, but the Erector brand survived under Meccano.
