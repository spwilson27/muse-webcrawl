Bright Star Catalogue

The Bright Star Catalogue, also known as the Yale Catalogue of Bright Stars, is a star catalogue listing all stars of apparent magnitude 6.5 or brighter — essentially all stars visible to the naked eye. Its fifth revised edition (1991) contains 9,110 entries.

Contents

Each entry gives the star's HR (Harvard Revised) number, names and designations, position, magnitude, spectral type, and proper motion. HR numbers remain a common way of referring to bright stars. The catalogue is maintained by Yale University Observatory and has appeared in successive editions since 1930.
