Yasushi Akimoto

Yasushi Akimoto (Japanese: 秋元 康; born 2 May 1958) is a Japanese record producer, lyricist, television writer, and professor. He is best known as the creator and producer of the AKB48 idol franchise, one of the highest-selling musical acts in Japan, and of its sister groups in Japan and across Asia.

Career

Akimoto began his career as a television scriptwriter in the 1970s and became a sought-after lyricist in the 1980s, writing hits for artists including Misora Hibari, whose "Kawa no Nagare no Yō ni" (1989) became one of Japan's most beloved songs. In 1985 he created the idol group Onyanko Club. In 2005 he launched AKB48, based in a dedicated theatre in Akihabara, Tokyo, around the concept of "idols you can meet".

The 48 and 46 franchises

The AKB48 model — large rotating membership, handshake events, and fan-voted "general elections" — expanded to sister groups such as SKE48, NMB48, HKT48, and NGT48 in Japan and JKT48, BNK48, and others overseas, as well as the rival "46" series Nogizaka46, Keyakizaka46 (now Sakurazaka46), and Hinatazaka46. Akimoto has written the lyrics for thousands of songs, and his groups' singles routinely topped the Japanese charts through the 2010s.
