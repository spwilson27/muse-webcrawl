Orochimaru (大蛇丸) is a fictional character from Naruto, a manga series created by Masashi Kishimoto. In the anime and manga, Orochimaru is a former ninja from the village of Konohagakure who was known as one of the three legendary Sannin. Title of "Legendary Sannin" was given to him and his teammates, Tsunade and Jiraiya, in recognition of their prowess.

Obsessed with learning every technique in existence and achieving immortality so as to have the time to do so, Orochimaru conducted forbidden experiments on human subjects and founded his own village, Otogakure (the Village Hidden in the Sound). He sought to possess the body of Sasuke Uchiha to gain the Sharingan, and he could transfer his soul into new host bodies, shedding his skin like the snakes with which he is associated. His design and name derive from the character Orochimaru of the Japanese folk tale Jiraiya Gōketsu Monogatari, a magician who commands a giant serpent.

Story

Orochimaru served as a principal antagonist through much of the series: he murdered the Third Hokage, his former teacher, during an invasion of Konoha, and manipulated Sasuke into defecting to him. Sasuke eventually turned on and defeated him, but Orochimaru survived within Kabuto Yakushi and later within a curse mark, and was revived by Sasuke during the Fourth Great Ninja War to reincarnate the previous Hokage. After the war he was permitted to live under surveillance, continuing his research; in the sequel series Boruto he is the "parent" of the synthetic human Mitsuki. Kishimoto created Orochimaru as one of the series' most evil and powerful figures, deliberately making his appearance and mannerisms unsettling so that readers would recognize him instantly as a villain.
