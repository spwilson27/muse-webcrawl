Broadwell (microarchitecture)

[Image placeholder: Broadwell (microarchitecture)]

Broadwell is the fifth generation of Intel Core processors, released from late 2014 as the 14-nanometre "die shrink" (tick) of the Haswell microarchitecture in Intel's tick–tock model. Broadwell parts served mobile, desktop and server markets under the Core i3/i5/i7, Core M and Xeon brands.

Features

Produced on the 14 nm FinFET process, Broadwell improved energy efficiency and integrated graphics, introducing the Iris Pro 6200 graphics with optional 128 MB of embedded eDRAM on some desktop and mobile SKUs. Desktop offerings were limited, most notably the Core i7-5775C and i5-5675C. Broadwell was succeeded by Skylake, and server variants (Broadwell-EP/EX) extended the platform in Xeon E5 and E7 lines.
