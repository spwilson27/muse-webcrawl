Ländler

The Ländler is a folk dance in 3/4 time that was popular in Austria, southern Germany, German Switzerland, and Slovenia at the end of the 18th century. It is a partner dance featuring hopping, stamping, and turning steps.

History and influence

Rooted in Alpine peasant dancing, the Ländler moved into urban ballrooms and influenced the development of the waltz, which largely displaced it. Composers including Mozart, Haydn, Beethoven, Schubert, Bruckner, and Mahler wrote Ländler or movements in Ländler style; Schubert's numerous Ländler sets for piano are especially notable.
