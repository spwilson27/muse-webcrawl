Mortgage

A mortgage is a loan in which real property is used as collateral. The borrower gives the lender a security interest in the property, which the lender releases when the loan is repaid. Mortgage loans are commonly used to purchase homes.

Types

Common types include fixed-rate and adjustable-rate (variable-rate) mortgages, as well as interest-only and repayment (amortising) loans. Typical terms run from 15 to 30 years.

Legal structure

In some jurisdictions the borrower grants a mortgage deed to the lender; in others the arrangement takes the form of a lien. Failure to repay can lead to foreclosure, in which the lender forces a sale of the property.
