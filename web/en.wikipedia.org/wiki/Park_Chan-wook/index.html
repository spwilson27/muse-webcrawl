Park Chan-wook

Park Chan-wook (born 23 August 1963) is a South Korean filmmaker regarded as one of the most prominent directors of modern Korean cinema. He is best known for his "Vengeance Trilogy" — Sympathy for Mr. Vengeance (2002), Oldboy (2003) and Lady Vengeance (2005) — films noted for their striking visuals, dark humour and themes of revenge.

Career

Park studied philosophy at Sogang University before entering film. His breakthrough came with Joint Security Area (2000), then a record-breaking success in Korea. Oldboy won the Grand Prix at the 2004 Cannes Film Festival and became an international cult classic. Later works include the vampire film Thirst (2009), his English-language debut Stoker (2013), the erotic thriller The Handmaiden (2016), the television series The Little Drummer Girl (2018), and Decision to Leave (2022), for which he won Best Director at Cannes.

Style

Park's films feature elaborate production design, precisely choreographed camera movement and meticulous framing, often juxtaposing extreme violence with beauty and wit.
