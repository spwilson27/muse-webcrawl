San Francisco Ballet is a ballet company, founded in 1933 as the San Francisco Opera Ballet under the leadership of ballet master Adolph Bolm. The company is currently based in the War Memorial Opera House, San Francisco, and is the first professional ballet company in the United States. It is among the world's leading dance companies, presenting more than 100 performances annually, with a repertoire that spans both classical and contemporary ballet. Along with the American Ballet Theatre and the New York City Ballet, San Francisco Ballet has been described as part of the "triumvirate of great classical companies defining the American style on the world stage today."

History

The company traces its origins to 1933, when it was formed as an adjunct to the San Francisco Opera. Gaetano Merola, the opera's founder, engaged Adolph Bolm as ballet master, and the San Francisco Ballet School was established the same year, making it the oldest ballet school in the United States. Under brothers Willam, Harold, and Lew Christensen, the company grew in prominence. Lew Christensen directed the company from 1952 to 1984 and choreographed the first full-length American production of The Nutcracker in 1944, a tradition the company continues.

In 1985, Icelandic dancer Helgi Tómasson became artistic director, a tenure that lasted 37 years and transformed the company into an internationally acclaimed institution. He established the company's reputation for new work, commissioning choreographers including Mark Morris, Christopher Wheeldon, and Alexei Ratmansky. In 2023, Spanish dancer Tamara Rojo succeeded Tómasson as artistic director.

Repertoire

The company's repertoire includes full-length story ballets such as Swan Lake, Giselle, Romeo and Juliet, Cinderella, and Don Quixote, alongside neoclassical works by George Balanchine and contemporary creations. San Francisco Ballet gave the American premieres of several major works and is noted for its annual Nutcracker, set in San Francisco during the 1915 Panama–Pacific International Exposition.

School

The San Francisco Ballet School, founded in 1933, enrolls students from around the world and provides much of the company's dancer pipeline. Its trainees perform in company productions, and the school operates a scholarship program named for Helgi Tómasson.
