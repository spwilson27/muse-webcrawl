Oranjestad, Aruba

Oranjestad
Country: Aruba (Kingdom of the Netherlands)
Founded: 1796
Population: about 28,000
Role: Capital and largest city of Aruba

Oranjestad (Dutch for "Orange City") is the capital and largest city of Aruba, a constituent country of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in the southern Caribbean. Founded in 1796 around Fort Zoutman, the city lies on the island's leeward southern coast and is named after the Dutch royal House of Orange.

Geography and landmarks

The city is noted for its colourful Dutch colonial architecture, its harbour serving cruise ships, and landmarks such as Fort Zoutman — Aruba's oldest building — the Willem III Tower, and the National Archaeological Museum. Queen Beatrix International Airport lies just south-east of the city.

Economy

Tourism dominates the economy of Oranjestad, with duty-free shopping along L.G. Smith Boulevard, resort hotels nearby, and a tramway linking the cruise terminal to the city centre.
