Overview

Serekunda is the largest urban centre in the Gambia, forming part of the Kanifing Municipal area near the Atlantic coast, west of the capital Banjul. Together with neighbouring Bakau, Fajara and the Atlantic resorts it forms the core of the country's tourism and commercial zone.

Features

Serekunda Market is one of the country's main markets. The town grew rapidly in the late 20th century as the Gambia urbanised; its name honours Sayerr Jobe, a 19th-century founder figure (Sayerre Jobe). The Albert Market is in Banjul; Serekunda's market is known for fabric, produce and crafts.
