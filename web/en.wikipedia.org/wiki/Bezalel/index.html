Bezalel

In the Hebrew Bible, Bezalel (Hebrew: בְּצַלְאֵל, "in the shadow of God") was the chief artisan of the Tabernacle and was in charge of building the Ark of the Covenant, assisted by Aholiab.

Biblical account

According to Exodus 31 and 35–36, Moses announced that God had filled Bezalel, of the Tribe of Judah, son of Uri son of Hur, with the Spirit of God — wisdom, understanding and knowledge in all kinds of craftsmanship in gold, silver, bronze, stone and wood. He oversaw construction of the Tabernacle, its furnishings, the priestly garments and the anointing oil.

Legacy

Bezalel is celebrated in Jewish tradition as the archetypal artist. Jerusalem's Bezalel Academy of Arts and Design, founded in 1906, bears his name, as does the Bezalel school style of early Zionist art.
