"Crisis on Infinite Earths" is the sixth annual Arrowverse crossover event, featuring episodes of the television series Supergirl, Batwoman, The Flash, Arrow, and Legends of Tomorrow on The CW. The event, adapted from the 1985–86 DC Comics limited series of the same name, aired in five parts between December 2019 and January 2020.

Plot

The crossover follows the heroes of the Arrowverse as they unite against the Anti-Monitor, a cosmic being destroying the multiverse with a wave of antimatter. Guided by the Monitor, heroes from multiple Earths gather, several worlds are destroyed, and a group of "Paragons" is identified to carry on the fight. In the concluding episodes, the surviving heroes battle at the dawn of time, the multiverse is reborn as a single "Earth-Prime", and Oliver Queen — the Green Arrow — dies having sacrificed himself to restart the universe.

Production and cameos

The event was noted for its scale and for numerous guest appearances linking the Arrowverse to other adaptations of DC Comics, including Burt Ward from the 1960s Batman series, Tom Welling and Erica Durance from Smallville, Kevin Conroy as an older Bruce Wayne, and a brief appearance by Ezra Miller as the film version of the Flash alongside Grant Gustin's television Flash.

Reception

The crossover was generally well received by critics, who praised its ambition and emotional payoffs, particularly the conclusion of Oliver Queen's story in the final season of Arrow.
