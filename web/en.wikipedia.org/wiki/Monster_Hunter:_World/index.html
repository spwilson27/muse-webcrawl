Monster Hunter: World is an action role-playing game developed and published by Capcom. It is the fifth mainline installment in the Monster Hunter series and was released worldwide for PlayStation 4 and Xbox One in January 2018, with a Windows version following in August 2018. In the game, the player takes the role of a Hunter, tasked with hunting down and killing or trapping monsters that roam in one of several environmental spaces. If successful, the player is rewarded through loot consisting of parts from the monster and other elements that are used to craft weapons and armor, amongst other equipment.

The game's core loop has the player crafting appropriate gear to be able to hunt down more difficult monsters, which in turn provide parts that lead to more powerful gear. Players may hunt alone or in a group of up to four players via the game's online multiplayer.

Gameplay

Monster Hunter: World is an action role-playing game played from a third-person perspective. Similar to previous games in the series, the player takes the role of a player-created character who travels to the "New World", an unpopulated land mass filled with monsters, to join the Research Commission that studies the land from their central command base of Astera. The Research Commission tasks the Hunter to hunt down and either kill or capture large monsters that roam outside Astera to both protect the Commission and to study the monsters there.

The character does not have any intrinsic attributes, but instead these are determined by what gear the character is equipped with. This includes a weapon, selected from the series' fourteen archetypes (such as long sword, bow, or hammer), which then further defines the types of combat moves and abilities the player can use, and pieces of armor, which can lead to beneficial or detrimental combat effects if matched properly. While some basic gear can be purchased or crafted at the start of the game, the most effective gear is built from materials obtained by defeating monsters, a core gameplay loop of the series.

Unlike previous games, in which the player was limited to individual hunting zones separated by loading screens, World features seamless, living ecosystems: large maps that monsters and other creatures traverse freely, with day-night cycles and weather. Areas include the Ancient Forest, the Wildspire Waste, the Coral Highlands, the Rotten Vale, and the Elder's Recess.

Plot

The player character joins the Fifth Fleet, a wave of hunters dispatched to the New World to study the Elder Crossing, a phenomenon in which elder dragons migrate across the sea once every ten years. After their ship is attacked by the colossal elder dragon Zorah Magdaros, the Hunter and their handler partner investigate the migration, ultimately confronting threats including Nergigante and the newly discovered elder dragon Xeno'jiiva.

Development

Monster Hunter: World was developed by Capcom's main studio in Osaka under series producer Ryozo Tsujimoto and director Yuya Tokuda. The team aimed to design a game that would appeal to Western audiences — with larger, seamless environments and full voice acting — while retaining the series' core hunting loop. The game was announced at Sony's E3 2017 press conference.

Expansion and reception

A major expansion, Monster Hunter World: Iceborne, was released in September 2019 for consoles and January 2020 for Windows, adding the Hoarfrost Reach region and Master Rank hunts. World received critical acclaim for its gameplay, world design, and accessibility to newcomers. It became Capcom's best-selling game of all time, with shipments exceeding 20 million copies including Iceborne editions, by the mid-2020s.
