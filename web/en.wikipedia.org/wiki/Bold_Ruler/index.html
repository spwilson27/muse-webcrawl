Bold Ruler (April 6, 1954 – July 12, 1971) was an American Thoroughbred racehorse who was the 1957 American Horse of the Year and, more importantly, one of the most influential sires of the 20th century. He led the North American sire list eight times and sired the great Triple Crown winner Secretariat.

Racing career

Bred and owned by Gladys Mill's Wheatley Stable and trained by "Sunny Jim" Fitzsimmons, Bold Ruler was a fast but fragile colt who nevertheless won 23 of his 33 starts. His major victories included the Futurity Stakes at two, the Preakness Stakes, the Flamingo Stakes, the Wood Memorial, and the Vosburgh and Suburban handicaps at four. He was named American Horse of the Year for 1957 and was later inducted into the National Museum of Racing and Hall of Fame.

Stud record

Retired to Claiborne Farm, Bold Ruler became North America's leading sire a record eight times (1963–1969 and 1973). His offspring included champions such as Boldnesian, Successor, and Bold Lad, and he appears in the pedigree of numerous classic winners. His son Bold Reasoning sired the 1977 Triple Crown winner Seattle Slew, while his most famous son, Secretariat, won the 1973 Triple Crown. Bold Ruler died of cancer in 1971 and is buried at Claiborne Farm.
