GÉANT

GÉANT is the pan-European data network for the research and education community. Together with its partner national research and education networks (NRENs), it interconnects tens of thousands of institutions and tens of millions of users across Europe, and links to research networks on other continents.

GÉANT
Type: Research & education network
Members: European NRENs
Headquarters: Amsterdam / Cambridge

History

GÉANT grew out of earlier European networking projects such as TEN-34 and TEN-155. The first GÉANT network launched in 2000–2001, with successive generations (GÉANT2, GÉANT3, GÉANT4) raising backbone speeds to hundreds of gigabits per second.

Services

Beyond connectivity, GÉANT provides services such as eduroam (co-developed with the community), eduGAIN identity federation, perfSONAR monitoring, and trust and identity services for research collaborations, including support for data-intensive science such as the Large Hadron Collider and SKA.
