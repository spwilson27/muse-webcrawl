Turanga Leela is a fictional character from the animated television series Futurama. Voiced by Katey Sagal, she is the captain of the Planet Express Ship and one of the show's central characters. Leela grew up believing she was an alien cyclops abandoned by her parents; she later learns she is in fact a mutant, the least mutated mutant ever born, given up by her sewer-dwelling parents so she could have a better life on the surface.

Character

Leela is pragmatic, disciplined, and highly competent — the de facto leader who keeps the irresponsible crew alive. Her single large eye gives her poor depth perception, a trait played for comedy but which she compensates for with martial-arts skill. She is an animal lover and environmentalist, and adopts the small alien pet Nibbler. Her on-again, off-again relationship with Philip J. Fry forms one of the series' main emotional arcs; the two eventually marry in the timeline depicted in the episode "Meanwhile".

Background

Leela's parents, Morris and Munda Turanga, are mutants living in the sewers of New New York. They left their infant daughter at an orphanarium with a note written in an "alien" script (later revealed to be a mutant language) so that she would be mistaken for an alien and accepted on the surface. The truth is revealed in the episode "Leela's Homeworld" (2002).
