Albert Anastasia (born Umberto Anastasio; 26 September 1902 – 25 October 1957) was an Italian-American mobster, hitman and crime boss. One of the founders of the modern American Mafia, he was a co-founder and later boss of Murder, Inc., the enforcement arm of the National Crime Syndicate, and eventually rose to lead the Mangano crime family, which became the modern Gambino crime family. He was notorious for his extraordinary violence and was nicknamed the "Lord High Executioner" and the "Mad Hatter".

Early life

Anastasia was born in Tropea, Calabria, Italy, and immigrated to the United States in 1917, working on the Brooklyn waterfront as a longshoreman. In 1921 he was convicted of murdering a fellow longshoreman and sentenced to death at Sing Sing, but won a retrial and was freed in 1922 after key witnesses disappeared.

Murder, Inc. and rise to power

During Prohibition, Anastasia aligned himself with Lucky Luciano and Frank Costello, and helped Luciano eliminate rivals Joe Masseria and Salvatore Maranzano in 1931. As a reward he became underboss of the Mangano family and, alongside Louis "Lepke" Buchalter, ran Murder, Inc., which is estimated to have carried out hundreds of contract killings during the 1930s.

In 1951, after boss Vincent Mangano disappeared and his brother Philip was found murdered, Anastasia seized control of the family with the Commission's approval.

Assassination

On the morning of 25 October 1957, Anastasia was shot dead while seated in a barber's chair at the Park Sheraton Hotel in Manhattan, in one of the most famous mob hits of the era. The killing is generally attributed to rivals within his own family, particularly Carlo Gambino, acting with Vito Genovese, who coveted Anastasia's power. The gunmen were never identified.

Legacy

Anastasia's murder marked a turning point in Mafia history, occurring just weeks before the disastrous Apalachin meeting exposed the American Mafia to national scrutiny. His family passed to Gambino, under whose leadership it became the most powerful crime family in the United States.
