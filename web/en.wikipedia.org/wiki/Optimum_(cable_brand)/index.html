Optimum is an American telecommunications brand owned by Altice USA, providing cable television, high-speed internet, telephone and mobile services. The brand originated with Cablevision, the cable operator founded by Charles Dolan that served the New York metropolitan area, which launched the Optimum name for its bundled digital cable, internet and voice "triple play" services. Altice acquired Cablevision in 2016 for about $17.7 billion and Suddenlink in 2015; in 2022 Altice USA rebranded its Suddenlink footprint as Optimum, unifying both operations under a single name.

Services

Optimum provides broadband over hybrid fibre-coaxial networks, with an ongoing buildout of fibre-to-the-home marketed as Optimum Fiber, offering symmetrical gigabit and multi-gigabit tiers. Its television service combines traditional cable packages with streaming apps and the Optimum Stream device, and Optimum Voice provides digital home phone service. Optimum Mobile, launched in 2019, operates as a mobile virtual network operator, latterly on T-Mobile's network.

Coverage and criticism

The service operates in 21 states, with its densest footprint in the New York tri-state area and former Suddenlink territories across the south-central United States. Like other large cable operators, the company has faced recurring criticism over pricing, fees and customer service rankings, and it has invested in network upgrades and service improvement programmes in response.
