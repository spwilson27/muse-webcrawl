Earth-Two

Earth-Two (also Earth-2) is a fictional parallel universe in the DC Comics multiverse, home to the Golden Age versions of DC's superheroes. It was established in the early 1960s as a way to reconcile the original 1930s–1950s incarnations of characters such as Superman, Batman and Wonder Woman with their modernised Silver Age counterparts on Earth-One.

Earth-Two is the home of the Justice Society of America and DC's Golden Age heroes.

History

The concept began with "Flash of Two Worlds!" (The Flash #123, 1961), in which Barry Allen met the Golden Age Flash, Jay Garrick, on a parallel Earth. Justice League of America #21–22 (1963), "Crisis on Earth-One!" and "Crisis on Earth-Two!", formally named Earth-Two and reunited the Justice League with the Justice Society of America, beginning an annual tradition of crossovers. On Earth-Two, heroes debuted during the Second World War era, aged in something close to real time, married, and in some cases had children who became heroes themselves, such as the Huntress (Helena Wayne) and Power Girl.

Crisis and later versions

Earth-Two was destroyed in Crisis on Infinite Earths (1985–86), which merged the surviving universes into a single continuity; its heroes were retconned or folded into the new history. Variations of Earth-2 later returned: a version appeared in the post-Infinite Crisis 52-Earth multiverse, and a reimagined Earth 2—with younger, rebooted versions of the Justice Society—was launched as part of The New 52 in 2012 in the series Earth 2 by James Robinson.
