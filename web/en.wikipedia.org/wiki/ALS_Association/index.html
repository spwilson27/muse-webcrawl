The ALS Association is an American nonprofit organisation dedicated to combating amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS), also known as Lou Gehrig's disease. Founded in 1985, it funds research, operates a network of certified clinics and chapters providing services to people living with ALS, and advocates for public policy supporting patients and research funding.

Ice Bucket Challenge

The association became globally prominent in the summer of 2014 through the Ice Bucket Challenge, a viral social-media campaign in which participants dumped buckets of ice water over themselves and donated to ALS research. The campaign raised more than $115 million for the association in a matter of weeks and over $220 million worldwide, funding research that contributed to discoveries such as the NEK1 gene's association with ALS.

Activities

The association is the largest private funder of ALS research, supporting hundreds of scientists worldwide. Its local chapters organise the Walk to Defeat ALS and provide equipment loans, support groups and respite care.
