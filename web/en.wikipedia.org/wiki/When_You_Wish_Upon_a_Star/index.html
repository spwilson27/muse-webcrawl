"When You Wish Upon a Star" is a song written by Leigh Harline (music) and Ned Washington (lyrics) for Walt Disney's animated film Pinocchio (1940). It is sung by Cliff Edwards in the character of Jiminy Cricket over the opening credits and reprised at the film's close. The song won the Academy Award for Best Original Song — the first Disney song to win an Oscar — and has become the signature theme of The Walt Disney Company.

History and Disney theme

The recording was inducted into the Grammy Hall of Fame in 2002, and the American Film Institute ranked the song seventh in its 2004 list of the 100 greatest songs in American film. A rendition plays as the Walt Disney Pictures production logo appears, accompanying the arc of light over the Disney castle, and it is used in the fireworks and parades of Disney theme parks. In Scandinavia the song, drawn from Disney's television Christmas special, is a traditional part of Christmas Eve celebrations.

Cover versions

Its wide melodic leaps (an octave in the opening line) have made it a jazz standard: notable recordings include versions by Dave Brubeck, Bill Evans, Louis Armstrong, and Keith Jarrett, and it has been covered in many genres. In Japan the song is associated with the Star Festival (Tanabata). The chorus's promise — "when you wish upon a star, your dreams come true" — has passed into general culture.
