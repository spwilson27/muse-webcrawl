Brazilian Air Force

The Brazilian Air Force (Portuguese: Força Aérea Brasileira, FAB) is the air branch of the Brazilian Armed Forces and one of the three national uniformed services. Founded on 20 January 1941 by merging the Army and Navy aviation arms under a new Ministry of Aeronautics, it is the largest air force in South America, with tens of thousands of personnel and several hundred aircraft. Its motto is Asas que protegem o país — "Wings that protect the country."

History

Military aviation in Brazil began with the Army's aviation school at Campo dos Afonsos in 1919 and a naval aviation arm dating to 1916. The creation of the FAB in 1941 under President Getúlio Vargas unified these services. During the Second World War, the FAB's 1st Fighter Group (1º Grupo de Aviação de Caça) fought in the Italian campaign of 1944–45 flying P-47 Thunderbolts with the U.S. 350th Fighter Group, while FAB patrol aircraft hunted Axis submarines in the South Atlantic. The fighter group's motto, Senta a Púa!, remains famous in Brazil.

Modern force

The FAB is responsible for the air defence of Brazilian airspace through the integrated air defence and air traffic control system (SISCEAB), assisted by the AEW&C aircraft such as the Embraer E-99. Its fighter fleet long centred on the Northrop F-5EM and the AMX attack aircraft built with Italy; in 2014 Brazil selected the Saab Gripen E/F (designated F-39), with the first aircraft delivered in the 2020s and partial licence production by Embraer. The force also operates the Embraer C-390 Millennium transport, developed in Brazil, and an extensive fleet supporting Amazon surveillance through the SIVAM/SIPAM programmes.

Organisation and culture

The FAB administers the Alcântara Launch Center for Brazil's space programme and runs the Smoke Squadron (Esquadrilha da Fumaça), its aerobatic demonstration team flying Embraer Super Tucanos. Santos-Dumont, the Brazilian aviation pioneer, is honoured as the patron of Brazilian aviation, and 23 October — the date of his 14-bis flight in 1906 — is Aviator's Day in Brazil.
