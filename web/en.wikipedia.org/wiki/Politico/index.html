Politico

Politico
Founded2007
FoundersJohn Harris; Jim VandeHei
OwnerAxel Springer (from 2021)
[Image: Politico's newsroom in Arlington, Virginia, across the river from Washington]

Politico is an American political journalism organisation founded in 2007 by former Washington Post journalists John Harris and Jim VandeHei, with backing from Robert Allbritton. Built for the web, it pioneered the fast, insider style of digital political coverage and the must-read morning newsletter, Playbook.

Model

Politico covers politics and policy for a professional Washington audience; its subscription service Politico Pro sells policy reporting by vertical. A European edition, Politico Europe, launched in Brussels in 2015.

Ownership

Allbritton sold Politico to Germany's Axel Springer in 2021, in one of the largest US media deals of the period, while its founders had earlier left to start Axios.
