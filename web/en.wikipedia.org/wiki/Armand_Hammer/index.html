Armand Hammer

Armand Hammer, oil magnate and art collector

Armand Hammer (May 21, 1898 – December 10, 1990) was an American business manager and owner, best known for his oil company Occidental Petroleum, which he led from 1957 until his death. He was also famous for his decades of business dealings with the Soviet Union, his art collection, and his philanthropy.

Early life

Hammer was born in New York City to Russian Jewish immigrant parents. His father, Julius Hammer, was a physician and a founder of the Communist Labor Party of America. Armand trained as a doctor at Columbia but entered business early, travelling to the Soviet Union in 1921, ostensibly for medical relief work, and meeting Lenin. He obtained lucrative concessions in the young USSR, trading in asbestos, furs, wheat and pencils, and built the first of several fortunes.

Occidental Petroleum

After ventures in whisky distilling, cattle and art dealing, Hammer in 1956–57 acquired control of Occidental Petroleum, then a small, nearly dormant California oil company. Under his chairmanship it struck major oilfields, notably in Libya in the 1960s and later in the North Sea, becoming one of the largest oil companies in the United States. Hammer remained chairman and chief executive into his nineties, a tenure sometimes criticised for its autocratic style and Occidental's appetite for diversifications such as chemicals and meatpacking.

Diplomacy and philanthropy

Hammer maintained close relations with successive Soviet leaders from Lenin to Gorbachev and promoted himself as an unofficial intermediary between the superpowers. He funded cancer research, endowed cultural institutions, and assembled a major collection of European and American art, now housed principally at the Hammer Museum in Los Angeles, which he founded and which opened shortly before his death.

Legacy

Hammer's reputation remained contested: admirers praised his energy, deal-making and bridge-building, while biographers documented his father's communist ties, murky early Soviet dealings and aggressive self-promotion. His great-grandson is the actor Armie Hammer.
