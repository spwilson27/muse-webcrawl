Ironside (TV series)

Ironside

Genre: Police procedural

Starring: Raymond Burr

Original network: NBC

Original release: 1967–1975

No. of seasons: 8

Ironside is an American television crime drama that aired on NBC from September 14, 1967, to January 16, 1975. The series stars Raymond Burr as Robert T. Ironside, a San Francisco police detective who, after being shot and paralyzed from the waist down, continues to solve crimes from a wheelchair as a consultant to the police department.

Premise

Chief of Detectives Robert Ironside is shot by a sniper while on vacation and left paraplegic. Refusing to retire, he persuades the police commissioner to let him work as a special consultant, assisted by Detective Sergeant Ed Brown, Officer Eve Whitfield, and ex-convict Mark Sanger, who serves as his aide and driver.

Cast

  • Raymond Burr as Chief Robert T. Ironside
  • Don Galloway as Sgt. Ed Brown
  • Barbara Anderson as Officer Eve Whitfield
  • Don Mitchell as Mark Sanger
  • Elizabeth Baur as Officer Fran Belding

Legacy

The series ran for 199 episodes and was notable for its theme music by Quincy Jones, later famously sampled in the Kill Bill films. A short-lived remake aired on NBC in 2013 starring Blair Underwood.
