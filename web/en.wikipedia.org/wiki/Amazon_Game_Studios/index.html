Amazon Games, formerly known as Amazon Game Studios, is an American video game developer and publisher owned by Amazon. The division was founded on August 7, 2012, initially to develop casual games for mobile platforms, and later expanded into large-budget PC and console titles through internal studios in Seattle, San Diego, Orange County, and Montreal.

History

Amazon announced its game studio in 2012 with the social game Living Classics for Facebook. In 2014 the company acquired developer Double Helix Games, which became its Orange County studio. Amazon hired notable designers including Kim Swift and Clint Hocking, and built games on its own Lumberyard engine, derived from CryEngine.

Several early projects, including the hero shooter Crucible (2020), were cancelled or withdrawn shortly after launch. The studio found major commercial success publishing the Korean MMORPG Lost Ark in western markets in 2022, and with its internally developed MMORPG New World (2021). The division was rebranded from Amazon Game Studios to Amazon Games in 2020 alongside the launch of the cloud gaming service Amazon Luna.
