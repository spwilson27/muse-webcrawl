Singles' Day

Date: 11 November
Origin: Nanjing University, China, 1990s
Also known as: Double 11 (双十一)

Singles' Day (光棍节, Guānggùn Jié) is a Chinese festival held on 11 November celebrating being single, the date chosen because the numeral 1 resembles a bare stick and four 1s suggest a group of singles. It originated among Nanjing University students in the 1990s.

Shopping festival

In 2009 Alibaba's Tmall turned the day into an online shopping festival with deep discounts. "Double 11" grew into the world's largest retail event, with sales surpassing those of Black Friday and Cyber Monday combined, driven by livestream commerce and presales.

Spread

The event has spread across Southeast Asia and other markets via platforms such as Lazada and Shopee, and its scale has drawn regulatory scrutiny in China over discount practices and consumer protection.
