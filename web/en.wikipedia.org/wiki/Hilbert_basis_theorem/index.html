Hilbert basis theorem

The Hilbert basis theorem states that every ideal in the ring of polynomials in finitely many variables over a Noetherian ring is finitely generated. In particular, every ideal in a polynomial ring over a field has a finite basis.

History

David Hilbert proved the theorem in 1888–1890, in work that transformed invariant theory. Paul Gordan, the leading invariant theorist of the time, reportedly remarked "This is not mathematics; this is theology," because Hilbert's proof was non-constructive.

Applications

The theorem underlies algebraic geometry: it implies that every algebraic variety is defined by finitely many polynomial equations, and it is a foundation for the theory of Gröbner bases.
