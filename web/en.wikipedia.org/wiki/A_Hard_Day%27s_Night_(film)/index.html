A Hard Day's Night is a 1964 British musical comedy film directed by Richard Lester, starring the English rock band the Beatles — John Lennon, Paul McCartney, George Harrison and Ringo Starr — during the height of Beatlemania. It was written by Alun Owen and originally released by United Artists.

Plot

The film portrays 36 hours in the lives of the group as they prepare for a television performance in London, evading screaming fans, management, and the press, with comic detours involving Paul's mischievous grandfather. It features the songs "A Hard Day's Night", "Can't Buy Me Love", "I Should Have Known Better", and "And I Love Her".

Legacy

A Hard Day's Night was both a financial and critical success and is credited with pioneering the modern music video through its innovative editing and musical sequences. It is regularly ranked among the greatest British films and the greatest films of all time.
