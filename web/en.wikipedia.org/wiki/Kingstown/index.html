Kingstown

Kingstown is the capital, chief port and main commercial centre of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines in the Caribbean. Founded by the French in 1722, it became the capital under British rule. Its population is about 12,500 in the city proper, with a larger urban area.

Kingstown is known as the 'City of Arches' for its stone architecture, and features the Botanical Gardens founded in 1765, among the oldest in the Western Hemisphere, Fort Charlotte, and St George's Anglican Cathedral. The harbour exports bananas, arrowroot and agricultural produce. The city was affected by the 2021 eruption of La Soufrière.
