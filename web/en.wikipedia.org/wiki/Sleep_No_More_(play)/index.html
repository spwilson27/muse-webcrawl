Sleep No More (play)

Sleep No More is an immersive theatre production created by the British company Punchdrunk, loosely based on William Shakespeare's Macbeth with inspiration from the 1960s Paisley witch trials and film noir. Audience members, wearing white masks, roam freely through a large multi-room set and choose which performers and scenes to follow.

The show premiered in London in 2003, was revived in Boston in 2009, and opened in New York in 2011 at the McKittrick Hotel, a fictional hotel environment in Chelsea, where it ran for over a decade. A Shanghai production opened in 2016. The production is regarded as a landmark of immersive and site-specific theatre.

Format

Scenes loop and repeat through the evening; individual audience members may be pulled aside by performers for one-on-one encounters. No dialogue-focused seating exists — the story is told through movement, dance and environment.
