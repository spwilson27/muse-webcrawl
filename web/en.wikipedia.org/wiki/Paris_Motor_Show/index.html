Paris Motor Show

Exhibition halls at the Paris Motor Show (Mondial de l'Automobile).

The Paris Motor Show (French: Mondial de l'Automobile) is a biennial auto show held in Paris, France. It is one of the most important auto shows in the world, frequently the venue for debuts of new production cars and concept cars. The show takes place at the Paris Expo Porte de Versailles exhibition centre.

History

The show was inaugurated in 1898 by the industry pioneer Albert de Dion as the Salon de l'Automobile, initially held in the Tuileries Gardens — making it one of the oldest motor shows in the world. It was held annually until 1976, when it became a biennial event alternating with the Frankfurt Motor Show (IAA). Landmark debuts at Paris have included numerous iconic French models from Renault, Peugeot and Citroën, alongside international premieres.

Recent editions

The 2018 edition attracted over one million visitors, making it the most visited auto show in the world that year. The 2020 edition was cancelled due to the COVID-19 pandemic. The show returned in 2022 in a reduced format with a stronger focus on electric vehicles and new mobility, and again in 2024 under the Mondial de l'Auto banner.
