Harry's House is the third studio album by English singer and songwriter Harry Styles, released on 20 May 2022 by Columbia and Erskine Records. Written and recorded largely with producers Kid Harpoon and Tyler Johnson during 2020–2021, it is a pop, pop-funk and synth-pop record named, in part, after Joni Mitchell's 1975 song "Harry's House/Centerpiece"; Mitchell publicly approved of the title.

Singles and commercial performance

The lead single, "As It Was", was released on 1 April 2022 and became one of the biggest hits of the year, spending 15 non-consecutive weeks atop the Billboard Hot 100 and topping charts worldwide. Further singles included "Late Night Talking", "Music for a Sushi Restaurant" and "Satellite". The album debuted at number one in numerous countries including the United Kingdom and the United States, where it set a then-record for vinyl sales week for an album in the US.

Critical reception and awards

Harry's House received generally favourable reviews, praised for its warmth and experimentation. At the 65th Annual Grammy Awards in 2023 it won Album of the Year and Best Pop Vocal Album, and it won British Album of the Year at the Brit Awards 2023, where Styles won all four categories in which he was nominated. The album was supported by the Love On Tour concert tour.
