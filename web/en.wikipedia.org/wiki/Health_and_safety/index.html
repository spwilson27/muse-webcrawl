Health and safety

Health and safety refers to the body of laws, standards, and practices concerned with protecting the safety, health, and welfare of people at work and others affected by work activities. Occupational safety and health (OSH) aims to prevent work-related injuries, illnesses, and deaths, and to promote well-being in the workplace.

Scope

OSH covers hazard identification and risk assessment, control of chemical, biological, physical, and ergonomic hazards, machine safety, fire safety, personal protective equipment, training, and health surveillance. Psychosocial risks such as stress and bullying have become increasingly prominent.

Regulation

Many countries regulate workplace safety through legislation such as the UK's Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 and the US Occupational Safety and Health Act 1970, enforced by agencies like the Health and Safety Executive and OSHA. Internationally, the International Labour Organization sets conventions and promotes standards. Management systems such as ISO 45001 structure organisational OSH practice.
