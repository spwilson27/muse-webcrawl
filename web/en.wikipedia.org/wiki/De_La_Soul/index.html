De La Soul is an American hip hop group formed in 1988 in the village of East Massapequa in Amityville, Long Island, New York. The group — Posdnuos (Kelvin Mercer), Trugoy the Dove (David Jolicoeur), and Maseo (Vincent Mason) — is best known for its eclectic sampling, playful lyrics, and contributions to the development of alternative hip hop and jazz rap.

History

Their debut album, 3 Feet High and Rising (1989), produced by Prince Paul, was a critical and commercial breakthrough, containing hits such as "Me Myself and I" and ushering in the "D.A.I.S.Y. Age" (Da Inner Sound Y'all) of Afrocentric positivity. The follow-up, De La Soul Is Dead (1991), deliberately challenged their hippie image. Later albums include Buhloone Mindstate (1993), Stakes Is High (1996), and the Art Official Intelligence series.

The group also famously collaborated with Gorillaz on the hit single "Feel Good Inc." (2005), which won a Grammy Award. After years of being unavailable on streaming services due to sample-clearance and contract disputes with their former label Tommy Boy Records, De La Soul's classic catalog finally arrived on streaming platforms in March 2023. Trugoy the Dove died in February 2023, shortly before the re-release.
