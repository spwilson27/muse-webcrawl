Cruzeiro Esporte Clube is a Brazilian professional football club based in Belo Horizonte, Minas Gerais. Founded in 1921 by Italian immigrants as Società Sportiva Palestra Itália, the club was renamed Cruzeiro in 1942 when Brazil entered the Second World War and the government banned references to Axis nations.

Cruzeiro is one of Brazil's most successful clubs. It has won the Campeonato Brasileiro Série A four times (1966, 2003, 2013, 2014), the Copa do Brasil a record six times, and the Copa Libertadores twice (1976 and 1997). In 2003 it won the domestic treble of state championship, league, and cup — the only Brazilian club to do so.

History

The club plays at the Mineirão stadium and contests the Clássico Mineiro derby with Atlético Mineiro. Notable players include Tostão, Ronaldo (who began his career there), Dirceu Lopes, and Dida. After a financial crisis, Cruzeiro was relegated for the first time in 2019; in December 2021 Ronaldo acquired a controlling stake in the club through the SAF model, and Cruzeiro returned to the top flight as Série B champions in 2022. In 2024 Ronaldo sold his stake to businessman Pedro Lourenço.
