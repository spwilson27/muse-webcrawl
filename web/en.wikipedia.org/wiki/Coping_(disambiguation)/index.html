Coping (disambiguation)

Coping may refer to:

  • Coping (psychology) — conscious efforts to manage stress, solve problems, and regulate emotion
  • Coping (architecture) — the capping or covering on top of a wall, often of stone, brick, or metal, that protects the wall from water
  • Coping (joinery) — a woodworking technique in which the end of a moulding is cut to fit the profile of an adjoining piece
  • Coping saw — a type of bow saw used to cut intricate shapes and curves in wood
  • Pool coping — the stones or material at the edge of a swimming pool
  • Coping mechanism — see coping in psychology
