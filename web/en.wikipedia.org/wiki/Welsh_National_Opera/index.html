Welsh National Opera

Welsh National Opera (WNO) (Welsh: Opera Cenedlaethol Cymru) is an opera company based in Cardiff, Wales. Founded in 1946, it is one of the leading opera companies in the United Kingdom and is resident at the Wales Millennium Centre in Cardiff Bay. The company tours extensively throughout Wales and England and performs internationally.

History

The company's roots go back to amateur choral groups in South Wales. Welsh National Opera was formally established in 1946, giving its first performances at the Prince of Wales Theatre, Cardiff. Idloes Owen, a singing teacher and conductor, is regarded as its founder. The company grew steadily and received Arts Council funding, becoming fully professional in the late 1960s and early 1970s. In 2004 it moved into its purpose-built home at the Wales Millennium Centre.

Repertoire and productions

WNO performs a wide repertoire from Mozart, Verdi and Puccini to contemporary works, and has championed new commissions and works by Welsh composers. Its productions are noted for strong ensemble work and an acclaimed chorus. Notable music directors have included Sir Richard Armstrong, Carlo Rizzi, Lothar Koenigs and Tomáš Hanus.

Touring and outreach

As a touring company, WNO regularly visits venues including Bristol, Birmingham, Liverpool, Milton Keynes, Plymouth, Southampton and Oxford, alongside performances in Cardiff. Its award-winning community and outreach programme engages thousands of participants across Wales.
