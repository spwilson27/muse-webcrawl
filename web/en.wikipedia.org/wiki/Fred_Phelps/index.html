Fred Phelps

Fred Waldron Phelps Sr. (November 13, 1929 – March 19, 2014) was an American pastor and disbarred lawyer who headed the Westboro Baptist Church of Topeka, Kansas.

Westboro Baptist Church

Phelps and his followers, largely members of his extended family, became notorious for picketing funerals — including those of American soldiers and victims of tragedies — with signs bearing slogans such as "God Hates Fags", arguing that the deaths were divine punishment for tolerance of homosexuality. Their protests led to state and federal restrictions on funeral picketing and the 2011 U.S. Supreme Court decision Snyder v. Phelps, which protected the picketing as free speech.

Life

Before his ministry, Phelps was a civil rights attorney in Kansas but was disbarred in 1979. He died in Topeka in 2014.
