The National Naval Aviation Museum, formerly the National Museum of Naval Aviation, is a military and aerospace museum located at Naval Air Station Pensacola, Florida. It is one of the largest aviation museums in the world and the largest devoted to naval aviation.

Exhibits

The museum displays more than 150 restored aircraft representing the United States Navy, Marine Corps and Coast Guard, spread across some 350,000 square feet of exhibit space and 37 acres of grounds. Notable aircraft include early Curtiss biplanes, Second World War fighters, the Blue Angels' jets, and aircraft recovered from Lake Michigan and the Pacific.

History

The museum was established in 1962 under the direction of Captain Magruder H. Tuttle and opened in a small wooden building in 1963. Successive expansions produced the current complex, supported by the Naval Aviation Museum Foundation.

Programmes

The museum hosts the National Flight Academy, an immersive aviation-themed learning programme for students, along with simulators, IMAX films and the annual Naval Aviation Symposium.
