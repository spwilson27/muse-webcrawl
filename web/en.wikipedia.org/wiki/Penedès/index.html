Penedès

Penedès is a natural and historical region of Catalonia, Spain, lying between the pre-coastal mountain range and the Mediterranean Sea, southwest of Barcelona. It is divided between the comarques of Alt Penedès, Baix Penedès, and Garraf, with Vilafranca del Penedès as its traditional capital.

The region is one of Spain's most important wine-producing areas and is best known as the home of cava, the sparkling wine produced mainly around the town of Sant Sadurní d'Anoia, as well as still wines protected under the Penedès Denominació d'Origen (DO), one of the oldest in Spain. The landscape is dominated by vineyards, and wine has been produced there since Phoenician and Roman times.

Besides viticulture, the Penedès is known for its castellers (human tower) traditions, its Festa Major celebrations, and prehistoric and Roman archaeological remains.
