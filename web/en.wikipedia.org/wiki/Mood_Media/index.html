Mood Media Corporation is an international in-store provider of media and marketing services, best known as the successor to Muzak, the company that popularised background music in retail and workplace environments. Mood Media provides music, digital signage, scent marketing, interactive mobile marketing, and audio-visual systems to retailers, hotels, restaurants, and other businesses.

History

The company's roots lie in Muzak Holdings, founded in 1934. Mood Media grew through a series of acquisitions in the 2000s and 2010s, combining businesses in in-store media across North America and Europe. In 2011, Mood Media acquired Muzak for a reported US$345 million, retiring the Muzak brand in 2013 in favour of the Mood name. In 2020, the company was acquired by Vector Capital.

Services

Mood Media designs customer experiences for physical business locations, supplying curated music playlists, messaging, digital signage content, drive-thru systems, and scent diffusion intended to reinforce brand identity and influence customer behaviour.
