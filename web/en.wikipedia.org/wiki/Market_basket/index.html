Market basket

A market basket is a fixed selection of goods and services whose total cost is tracked over time to measure changes in the cost of living and inflation. The best-known example is the basket underlying the consumer price index (CPI) calculated by statistical agencies such as the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics.

Composition

The basket is designed to represent typical household spending and includes categories such as food and beverages, housing, apparel, transportation, medical care, recreation, education and other goods and services. The relative weights of items are derived from consumer expenditure surveys and are updated periodically.

Market basket analysis

In retail and data mining, "market basket analysis" also refers to the study of items purchased together, using association rules to find products frequently bought in combination — a technique used for store layout, promotions and recommendation systems.
