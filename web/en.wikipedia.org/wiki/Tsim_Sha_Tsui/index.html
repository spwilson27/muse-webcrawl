Tsim Sha Tsui

Tsim Sha Tsui (often abbreviated TST) is an urban area in southern Kowloon, Hong Kong, at the tip of the Kowloon Peninsula facing Victoria Harbour. It is one of the city's principal commercial, shopping, and tourist districts, administered as part of the Yau Tsim Mong District.

Landmarks

The district is known for the Tsim Sha Tsui Promenade and Avenue of Stars along the waterfront, the Clock Tower of the former Kowloon Station, the Hong Kong Cultural Centre, the Hong Kong Museum of Art, the Hong Kong Space Museum, and the historic Peninsula Hotel. Nathan Road, lined with shops and the Chungking Mansions, runs through the area.

Transport

Tsim Sha Tsui is served by the MTR (Tsim Sha Tsui and East Tsim Sha Tsui stations), numerous bus routes, and the Star Ferry, which crosses Victoria Harbour to Central and Wan Chai on Hong Kong Island. The name is sometimes romanised from the Cantonese for "pointy sand spit", referring to the original shape of the shoreline before reclamation.
