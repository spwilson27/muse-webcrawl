Academic dress of the University of Oxford

The academic dress of the University of Oxford is the gowns, hoods and caps prescribed for members of the university and worn at formal occasions such as matriculation, graduation ceremonies, examinations (in the form of subfusc) and formal dinners. Oxford retains one of the most elaborate and consistently worn systems of academic dress in the world.

Subfusc

Subfusc is the formal clothing worn under the gown: a dark suit, skirt and white shirt or blouse, a white bow tie, black tie or black ribbon, black socks and shoes, and a mortarboard or soft cap. It is required at matriculation, degree ceremonies and university examinations.

Gowns

Undergraduates wear the commoners' gown, a short black sleeveless gown, while scholars and exhibitioners wear a longer gown with sleeves. Graduates wear gowns corresponding to their degrees: bachelors' gowns with long closed sleeves, masters' gowns, and doctors' dress gowns of scarlet for higher doctorates, with undress gowns of black.

Hoods

Hoods are distinguished by shape and lining colour: for example, the BA hood is black with white fur, the MA hood black silk lined with crimson, and research degree hoods use faculty colours such as dark blue for DPhil. The system grew from medieval clerical dress and is regulated by the university's decree on academic dress.
