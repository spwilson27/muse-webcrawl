Army of the Potomac

The Army of the Potomac was the principal Union army in the Eastern Theater of the American Civil War. Created in 1861, it fought most of the war's famous eastern battles against Robert E. Lee's Confederate Army of Northern Virginia.

Commanders

Its commanders included Irvin McDowell, George B. McClellan, Ambrose Burnside, Joseph Hooker and George Meade, who remained in command from Gettysburg to Appomattox while Ulysses S. Grant, as general-in-chief, directed its operations from 1864.

Major battles

The army fought at Antietam, Fredericksburg, Chancellorsville, Gettysburg, and in the Overland and Petersburg campaigns of 1864–65, accepting Lee's surrender at Appomattox Court House on 9 April 1865. It was disbanded in June 1865.
