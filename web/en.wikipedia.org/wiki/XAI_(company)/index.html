xAI is an American artificial intelligence company founded by Elon Musk in 2023, with the stated goal of understanding the true nature of the universe. The company developed the Grok series of large language models and the Grok chatbot, integrated with the social media platform X (formerly Twitter), which Musk also owns.

History

xAI was announced in July 2023 and recruited researchers from organisations including DeepMind, OpenAI, Google and Microsoft. Grok-1 was released in November 2023 and its weights were later open-sourced under the Apache 2.0 licence; subsequent versions added multimodal capabilities and reasoning. The company raised billions of dollars in funding rounds in 2024 and built the "Colossus" supercomputer cluster in Memphis, Tennessee, one of the largest AI training clusters in the world.

Products

Grok is a conversational assistant offered through X and standalone apps, positioned as a less censored alternative to rival chatbots, with real-time access to posts on X. xAI also offers an API for developers.
