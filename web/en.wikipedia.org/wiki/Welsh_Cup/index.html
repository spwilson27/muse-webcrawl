The Welsh Cup (Welsh: Cwpan Cymru) is a knock-out football competition for teams in Wales, organised by the Football Association of Wales. First held in the 1877–78 season, it is one of the oldest national cup competitions in world football.

History

The inaugural final was won by Wrexham, who remain the competition's most successful club. For much of its history the cup was open to Welsh clubs playing in the English league system, including Cardiff City, Swansea City and Wrexham; Cardiff's triumph in the English FA Cup in 1927 makes them unique among Welsh winners. English border clubs also competed and occasionally won in the early decades.

Modern era

Since the creation of the League of Wales in 1992, eligibility has centred on clubs in the Welsh football league system, and the winners qualify for European competition. The final is typically played at a neutral venue in Wales.
