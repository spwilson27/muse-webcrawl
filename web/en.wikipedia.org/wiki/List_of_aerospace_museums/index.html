List of aerospace museums

This is a list of aerospace museums and museums featuring significant aerospace exhibits around the world. Aerospace museums preserve aircraft, spacecraft, rockets, and related artifacts, and range from national collections to small volunteer-run sites.

Large aerospace museums display dozens of aircraft under one roof.
