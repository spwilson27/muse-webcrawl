Nico Rosberg

Nico Erik Rosberg (born 27 June 1985) is a German–Finnish former racing driver who competed in Formula One and won the 2016 Formula One World Championship driving for Mercedes. The son of 1982 World Champion Keke Rosberg, he raced professionally from 2006 until his abrupt retirement days after securing the title.

Career

Rosberg progressed through karting and junior single-seater categories, winning the inaugural GP2 Series championship in 2005 with ART Grand Prix, which earned him a Formula One seat with Williams for 2006. In his debut race in Bahrain he scored the fastest lap, becoming one of the youngest drivers to do so. He spent four seasons with Williams, taking podium finishes but no wins.

In 2010 Rosberg joined the new Mercedes works team alongside Michael Schumacher, whom he generally outperformed over their three seasons together. Rosberg scored his first victory at the 2012 Chinese Grand Prix, the first win for a Mercedes factory car since 1955.

From 2013 he was partnered by Lewis Hamilton, a former karting rival. The pair dominated Formula One during the turbo-hybrid era; Hamilton took the 2014 and 2015 titles after intense battles, and Rosberg finally won the championship in 2016, sealing it with a second-place finish in Abu Dhabi. Five days later he announced his retirement from racing, saying he had achieved his ambition.

After racing

Since retiring, Rosberg has worked as a television pundit, invested in sustainability and mobility start-ups, and founded an environmental festival. He speaks several languages and lives in Monaco.
