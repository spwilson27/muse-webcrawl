Wildlife

Wildlife refers to undomesticated animal species, and in modern usage commonly to all organisms that grow and live wild in an area without being introduced by humans — including plants, fungi, and other organisms. Wildlife can be found in all ecosystems, from deserts and rainforests to urban areas.

Human interaction

Humans have depended on wildlife for food, clothing, and materials since prehistory, and wildlife underpins activities such as hunting, fishing, tourism, and wildlife watching, worth billions of dollars annually. At the same time, human activity has driven steep declines: global assessments estimate that monitored wildlife populations have fallen by an average of more than two-thirds since 1970, driven by habitat destruction, overexploitation, pollution, invasive species, and climate change.

Conservation

Wildlife conservation works through protected areas, species recovery programmes, and international agreements such as CITES, which regulates trade in endangered species. The legal definition of wildlife in many jurisdictions covers all wild animals, whether or not they are game species.
