Hairspray (2007 film)

Hairspray is a 2007 American musical romantic comedy film directed and choreographed by Adam Shankman. It is an adaptation of the 2002 Broadway musical of the same name, which was itself based on John Waters' 1988 comedy film. The film stars newcomer Nikki Blonsky as Tracy Turnblad, alongside John Travolta, Michelle Pfeiffer, Christopher Walken, Zac Efron, Queen Latifah, and James Marsden.

Plot

In 1962 Baltimore, plus-sized teenager Tracy Turnblad dreams of dancing on The Corny Collins Show, a local television dance programme. When she wins a place on the show, she becomes an overnight celebrity and uses her fame to campaign for racial integration of the programme, which segregates Black dancers to a single monthly "Negro Day". With the help of her friends and family — including her mother Edna, played by Travolta — Tracy challenges station manager Velma Von Tussle and her daughter Amber in the Miss Teenage Hairspray pageant.

Music and reception

The score by Marc Shaiman and Scott Wittman adapts most of the stage songs and adds new numbers, including "Ladies' Choice" and "Come So Far (Got So Far to Go)". The film was a critical and commercial success, grossing over $200 million worldwide, and was nominated for the Golden Globe Award for Best Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy; Travolta was also nominated for his performance as Edna Turnblad.
