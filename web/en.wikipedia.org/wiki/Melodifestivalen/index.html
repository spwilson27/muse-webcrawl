Melodifestivalen

Melodifestivalen (Swedish pronunciation: [mɛlʊˈdiːfɛstɪˌvɑːlɛn]; literally "the Melody Festival") is an annual song competition organised by Swedish public broadcasters SVT and SR. It determines Sweden's representative for the Eurovision Song Contest and has been staged nearly every year since 1959. It is one of Sweden's most popular television programmes and one of the largest national selection shows for Eurovision in the world.

Format

Since 2002 the contest has toured Swedish cities in a series of heats (deltävlingar) held over several weeks, with a second-chance round and a grand final usually staged in Stockholm at venues such as Friends Arena. Winners are decided by a combination of international jury groups and Swedish public televoting and app voting. Songs from the contest regularly dominate Swedish charts for months.

Notable winners

Melodifestivalen winners who went on to win Eurovision include ABBA with "Waterloo" (1974), Herreys (1984), Carola (1991), Charlotte Nilsson (1999), Loreen with "Euphoria" (2012) and again with "Tattoo" (2023), and Måns Zelmerlöw with "Heroes" (2015), making Sweden one of the most successful Eurovision countries.
