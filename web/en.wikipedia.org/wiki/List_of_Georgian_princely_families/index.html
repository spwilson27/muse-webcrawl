List of Georgian princely families

Georgian nobility
Country: Georgia
Upper class: Tavadi (princes)
Lower nobility: Aznavuri

This is a list of Georgian princely families — the noble houses of Georgia ranked as tavadi, the princely class of the Georgian feudal hierarchy, below the royal Bagrationi dynasty and above the untitled nobility (aznavuri) and the gentry.

Status and recognition

Under the kingdoms of Kartli-Kakheti and Imereti, the leading princely houses held great estates, hereditary offices and seats at court. Following the Russian annexation of Georgia in the 19th century, many families were confirmed in the dignity of princes (knyaz) of the Russian Empire, though recognition varied and disputes about the status of particular houses were common.

Principal houses

Well-known princely families of Georgia include the Amilakhvari, Andronikashvili, Baratashvili, Chavchavadze, Chichua, Dadiani (princes of Mingrelia), Dadeshkeliani (princes of Svaneti), Eristavi, Gurieli (princes of Guria), Jambakurian-Orbeliani, Machabeli, Mukhrani (a branch of the Bagrationi), Orbeliani, Palavandishvili, Shervashidze (princes of Abkhazia), Tsitsishvili and Vachnadze, among many others. Several of these dynasties — notably the Dadiani, Gurieli and Shervashidze — ruled their principalities in a semi-sovereign capacity for centuries.
