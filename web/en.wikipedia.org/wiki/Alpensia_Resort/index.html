Alpensia Resort (Korean: 알펜시아 리조트) is a ski resort and tourism complex in Daegwallyeong-myeon, Pyeongchang County, Gangwon Province, South Korea. Its name combines "Alps", "Asia", and "fantasia". It served as the main mountain cluster of the 2018 Winter Olympics.

Facilities

The resort includes ski slopes for beginners and intermediates, the Alpensia Ski Jumping Stadium, the Alpensia Biathlon Centre, a cross-country centre, a sliding centre for bobsleigh, luge and skeleton, and the Yongpyong/Alpensia venues used for technical alpine skiing events. It also contains hotels, condominiums, a convention centre, and a golf course.

2018 Winter Olympics

During the Pyeongchang 2018 Games, Alpensia hosted ski jumping, Nordic combined, biathlon, cross-country skiing, and sliding sports, and contained the main Olympic Village and the International Broadcast Centre area.
