The Associated Board of the Royal Schools of Music (ABRSM) is a British examination board and registered charity that provides graded music examinations and assessments worldwide. It was founded in 1889 by the Royal Academy of Music and the Royal College of Music, later joined by the Royal Northern College of Music and the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland.

Examinations

ABRSM offers graded exams from an introductory level through Grade 8 in practical musicianship — performance on instruments and singing — and in music theory, as well as diplomas beyond Grade 8. Practical exams traditionally assess set pieces, scales and arpeggios, sight-reading and aural tests, marked out of 150 with pass, merit and distinction bands. More than 600,000 candidates take ABRSM exams each year in over 90 countries, making it one of the world's largest music examination bodies. In recent years it has added digital and remotely assessed performance grades.

Publishing and charitable work

The Board publishes sheet music, recordings and theory workbooks through ABRSM Publishing, and its charitable activities support music education through scholarships, bursaries and instrument schemes. Its patron is the British monarch.
