Snow Leopard award

The Snow Leopard award (Russian: Снежный барс) was an official Soviet mountaineering award granted to climbers who ascended all five peaks of 7,000 metres and above in the former Soviet Union – in the Pamir and Tian Shan ranges of Tajikistan, Kyrgyzstan and Kazakhstan.

Snow Leopard award
Established1967, USSR
Peaks5 peaks over 7,000 m

The five peaks

  • Ismoil Somoni Peak (formerly Communism/Stalin Peak), 7,495 m, Tajikistan
  • Jengish Chokusu (Pobeda Peak), 7,439 m, Kyrgyzstan–China
  • Ibn Sina Peak (Lenin Peak), 7,134 m, Kyrgyzstan–Tajikistan
  • Korjenevskaya Peak, 7,105 m, Tajikistan
  • Khan Tengri, 7,010 m, Kyrgyzstan–Kazakhstan–China

History

Originally the list included Khan Tengri at its then-surveyed lower height, replacing it in some versions; the award required summiting all five (in any order). About 600+ climbers earned the title during the Soviet era, and the challenge remains a prestigious informal goal for high-altitude mountaineers today, overseen post-Soviet by national federations.
