Vital record


A historical birth certificate

Vital records are civil records of life events maintained by government authorities, including birth certificates, marriage licences and certificates, divorce records, and death certificates. In some jurisdictions, vital records may also include records of civil unions or domestic partnerships.

Purpose

Vital registration systems provide legal proof of identity, citizenship, family relationships and death, and they supply the raw data for vital statistics used in public health planning and demography.

Administration

Registration is usually a function of local or regional government: in the United States it is organised by states and territories, while in England and Wales the General Register Office oversees registration districts. Copies of certificates are issued to entitled applicants and are routinely required for passports, probate and genealogy.

History

Parish registers of baptisms, marriages and burials long preceded state systems; England mandated parish registers in 1538, and civil registration began in 1837. Many countries still have incomplete registration, and international agencies promote civil registration and vital statistics (CRVS) strengthening worldwide.
