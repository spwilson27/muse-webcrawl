Stockholm Stock Exchange

The Stockholm Stock Exchange (Swedish: Stockholmsbörsen), operating as Nasdaq Stockholm since its acquisition by OMX and later Nasdaq, is the primary securities exchange of Sweden.

History

Securities trading in Stockholm dates to the 18th century; the exchange was formally founded in 1863. In 1993 it became the first in the world to allow remote members, and in 1998 it merged with the derivatives exchange OM. OMX AB was formed in 2003 and acquired by Nasdaq in 2008.

Market

Nasdaq Stockholm lists many of Sweden's largest companies including Ericsson, H&M, Volvo, Atlas Copco, and AstraZeneca, with the OMX Stockholm 30 (OMXS30) as its main blue-chip index. Sweden has an unusually high rate of household equity participation and many listings for its size.
