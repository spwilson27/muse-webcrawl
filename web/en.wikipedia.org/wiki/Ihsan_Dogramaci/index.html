İhsan Doğramacı

İhsan Doğramacı (3 April 1915 – 25 February 2010) was a Turkish paediatrician, entrepreneur, philanthropist and founder of higher education institutions. He founded Bilkent University, Turkey's first private, non-profit university, and Hacettepe University.

Career

Born in Erbil, then part of the Ottoman Empire, Doğramacı studied medicine in Istanbul and trained in paediatrics in the United States, including at Harvard. Returning to Turkey, he built Hacettepe into a leading medical centre and served as rector of Ankara University. He chaired the Turkish Council of Higher Education (YÖK) from 1981 to 1992.

Philanthropy

He established foundations supporting children's health and education, and worked with the World Health Organization and UNICEF. Bilkent University, founded in 1984, grew from his vision of a research university modelled on international standards.
