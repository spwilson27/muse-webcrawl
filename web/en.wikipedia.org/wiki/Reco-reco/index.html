Reco-reco

The reco-reco is a Brazilian scraper musical instrument, similar to the güiro. Traditionally made from a gourd or a hollow bamboo or wooden cylinder with transverse ridges, it is played by rubbing a stick along the ridges to produce a rasping sound.

Construction

Modern reco-recos are often made of metal, with ridges rolled into the body and sometimes containing spring wires inside that resonate sympathetically, giving a sharper, louder sound suited to amplified ensembles.

Use

The reco-reco is a standard member of the samba bateria (percussion section) and is also used in other Brazilian genres such as pagode and choro.
