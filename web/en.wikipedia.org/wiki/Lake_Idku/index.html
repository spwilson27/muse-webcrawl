Lake Idku

Lake Idku
Location: Beheira Governorate, Nile Delta, Egypt
Coordinates: 31°15′N 30°14′E
Type: Coastal lagoon (brackish)
Surface area: approx. 85–126 km² (variable)
Outflow: Mediterranean Sea via the El-Maadia channel

Lake Idku (Arabic: بحيرة إدكو) is a brackish coastal lagoon on the northern edge of the Nile Delta in Egypt, west of the Rosetta branch of the Nile and near the town of Idku (Edku). It is one of the four large northern delta lakes, along with Lake Burullus, Lake Manzala and Lake Mariout.

Geography and ecology

The lake is connected to the Mediterranean Sea through a narrow channel at El-Maadia, through which seawater and fish fry enter. Its southern and eastern shores receive large volumes of agricultural drainage water, making the lake fresher and more eutrophic over time. Extensive reed beds of Phragmites and other emergent vegetation cover much of the shallows. The lake supports an important local fishery — tilapia, mullet and catfish — and is a wintering and stopover habitat for migratory waterbirds.

Threats

Like the other delta lagoons, Lake Idku suffers from pollution from agricultural runoff and industrial and municipal wastewater, encroachment and land reclamation, and declining area as margins are converted to fish farms and farmland. Its surface area has shrunk significantly from historical extents reported in the 19th and early 20th centuries.
