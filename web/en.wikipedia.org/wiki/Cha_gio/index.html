Chả giò

Chả giò, also known as nem rán in northern Vietnam, is a popular Vietnamese fried spring roll. It consists of minced filling wrapped in rice paper (bánh tráng) and deep-fried until crisp and golden.

Chả giò / Nem rán
Origin: Vietnam
Type: Fried spring roll
Main ingredients: Rice paper, minced pork, mushrooms, glass noodles, vegetables
Served with: Nước chấm, herbs, lettuce

Preparation

The filling typically combines ground pork (sometimes crab or shrimp), wood-ear mushrooms, glass noodles, carrot, jícama or taro, and seasoning. Rolls are wrapped tightly in moistened rice paper and fried, traditionally sometimes twice for extra crispness.

Serving

Chả giò are served hot with fresh herbs and lettuce for wrapping, and nước chấm dipping sauce. They appear as appetisers, in festive meals, and as a component of noodle dishes. They resemble but differ from fresh gỏi cuốn (unfried summer rolls) and from Chinese spring rolls, which use wheat wrappers.
