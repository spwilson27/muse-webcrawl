European route E40

European route E40 is the longest European route, extending over 8,500 kilometres (5,300 mi) from Calais in France eastwards through Belgium, Germany, Poland, Ukraine, Russia, and Central Asia to Ridder in Kazakhstan, near the Chinese border.

Route

From Calais the route runs through Bruges and Ghent to Brussels, then via Liège and Aachen into Germany, passing Cologne, Dresden, and Wrocław and Kraków in Poland. It crosses Ukraine through Lviv and Kyiv, enters Russia, and continues across Kazakhstan and through Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, and Turkmenistan on its eastern sections, making it a principal road link between Western Europe and Central Asia.

Significance

The E40 corridor overlaps historic east–west trade routes and is used heavily by freight traffic. Sections in Western Europe are full motorways, while road standards vary widely further east.
