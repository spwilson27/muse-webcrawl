Chaos;Head (stylized as ChäoS;HEAd) is a visual novel video game developed by 5pb. and Nitroplus and released in 2008. It is the first entry in the Science Adventure series, later continued by Steins;Gate, Robotics;Notes, and Chaos;Child. An enhanced version, Chaos;Head Noah, added new routes and content.

Gameplay

The game is largely a linear visual novel, but features a "delusional trigger" system: at certain points the player can choose to make the protagonist experience positive (often escapist or romantic) or negative (paranoid or horrific) delusions, or remain in reality. These choices alter scenes and steer the story toward different endings.

Plot

The story follows Takumi Nishijo, a reclusive hikikomori high-school student in Shibuya who spends his days playing online games and chatting online. A series of brutal murders known as the "New Generation Madness" killings grips the city, and after an online acquaintance sends him a disturbing image of a murder scene before it occurs, Takumi becomes entangled in the mystery, increasingly unable to distinguish reality from delusion.

Adaptations

The game was adapted into a manga, a 12-episode anime television series by Madhouse in 2008, and stage productions. Its sequel, Chaos;Child, was released in 2014.
