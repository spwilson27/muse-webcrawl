Cycling, also called bicycling or biking, is the use of bicycles for transport, recreation, exercise or sport. In cities like Amsterdam and Copenhagen it is a primary transport mode; as sport it crowns the Tour de France, three weeks of racing watched worldwide.

Urban cycling

Cycle lanes, bike-share schemes and e-bikes revived urban cycling as climate policy — a bicycle emits a fraction of a car's footprint per kilometre. Safety (helmets, separated infrastructure) and end-of-ride facilities decide whether cycling mainstreams.
