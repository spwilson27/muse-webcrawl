Jason Todd

Jason Peter Todd is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. He debuted as the second character to assume the role of Robin, Batman's sidekick, before being killed by the Joker and later resurrected as the violent vigilante the Red Hood.

Publication history

Created by Gerry Conway and Don Newton, Todd replaced Dick Grayson as Robin in 1983. In the 1988 storyline A Death in the Family, readers voted by telephone for Todd's death at the hands of the Joker — one of the most famous events in comics publishing.

Red Hood

Todd returned in 2005's Under the Hood storyline, resurrected via a Lazarus Pit and operating as the Red Hood, an antihero willing to kill criminals, in tension with Batman's moral code. He has since appeared in animation, video games and the Titans television series.
