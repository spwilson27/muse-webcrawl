LIFO

LIFO is an acronym that may refer to several concepts, most notably last in, first out, a principle used in computing and accounting.

In computing

In computer science, LIFO describes the order in which elements are removed from a stack data structure: the last element inserted is the first one removed. This contrasts with FIFO (first in, first out), used by queues.

In accounting

In accounting, last in, first out is an inventory valuation method in which the most recently produced or purchased items are recorded as sold first. LIFO is permitted under United States generally accepted accounting principles but is prohibited under International Financial Reporting Standards.
