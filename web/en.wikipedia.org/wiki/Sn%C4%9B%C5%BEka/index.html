Sněžka

Sněžka (Polish: Śnieżka; German: Schneekoppe) is a mountain on the border between the Czech Republic and Poland, in the Krkonoše (Giant Mountains). At 1,603 metres, it is the highest point in the Czech Republic and in the Sudetes range.

Elevation: 1,603 m (5,259 ft)
Range: Krkonoše / Giant Mountains
Countries: Czech Republic, Poland

Summit

The summit, which enjoys some 100 days of fog a year and harsh weather, holds the 17th-century chapel of St Lawrence, a Polish meteorological station and restaurant built in a distinctive disc-like form in the 1970s, and a Czech post office. Chairlifts from Pec pod Sněžkou and Karpacz serve the mountain.

History

The mountain was first climbed in recorded history in 1456. Its name means "snowy" in Czech and Polish, reflecting its long snow cover. The summit area lies within national parks on both sides of the border.
