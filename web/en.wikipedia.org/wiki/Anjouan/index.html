Anjouan

Anjouan (Comorian: Ndzwani) is an island in the Comoros archipelago in the Indian Ocean and an autonomous island within the Union of the Comoros. Its capital is Mutsamudu. The island is volcanic, mountainous and densely populated, with an economy based on agriculture, fishing and remittances.

Geography

Anjouan is the second-largest island of the archipelago after Grande Comore. Mount Ntringui rises to about 1,595 metres. Steep slopes, heavy rainfall and deforestation contribute to soil erosion and pressure on freshwater supplies. The climate is tropical, with a hot rainy season and a cooler dry season.

History

The island was ruled by sultans before becoming a French protectorate in the nineteenth century and part of the French Comoros colony. It joined the independent Comoros in 1975. In 1997 Anjouan declared secession, seeking return to French rule or independence; it was reintegrated after political agreements and a 2008 African Union-backed intervention removed the renegade leader Mohamed Bacar.
