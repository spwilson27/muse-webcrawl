List of metropolitans and patriarchs of Moscow

The heads of the Russian Orthodox Church in Moscow have borne the titles of Metropolitan of Moscow (from the fifteenth century, after the see moved from Kiev and Vladimir), Patriarch of Moscow and all Rus' (1589–1721 and again from 1917), and — during the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries — were subordinated to the Most Holy Synod. The patriarchate was abolished by Peter the Great in 1721 and restored by the Local Council of 1917–18.
