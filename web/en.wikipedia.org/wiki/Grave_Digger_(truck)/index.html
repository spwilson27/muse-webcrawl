Grave Digger is a monster truck racing team in the Monster Jam series, founded by driver Dennis Anderson in 1982 in Kill Devil Hills, North Carolina. Recognisable by its black-and-green graveyard-themed paint scheme with skull and crossbones imagery, it is one of the most famous and popular monster trucks of all time.

History

Anderson built the original Grave Digger from a red 1952 Ford F-100 panel truck, initially as a mud bogger; legend holds the name came from trash talk — "I'll take this old junk and dig you a grave with it" — before a race. The truck evolved through purpose-built racing chassis, and Anderson's flamboyant driving style, frequent crashes and freestyles made Grave Digger a fan favourite, credited with popularising the freestyle event format.

Team

Multiple Grave Digger trucks now tour simultaneously with Monster Jam, driven by members of the Anderson family — including Dennis Anderson's sons Adam and Ryan — and other drivers. The team has won numerous Monster Jam World Finals championships in racing and freestyle.
