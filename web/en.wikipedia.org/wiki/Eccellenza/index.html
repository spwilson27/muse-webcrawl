Eccellenza

Eccellenza (Italian for "Excellence") is the fifth level of the Italian football league system and the highest level of regional amateur football in Italy. It sits below Serie D and above the Promozione. The league is administered by the Lega Nazionale Dilettanti (LND) through its regional committees.

Format

The Eccellenza is divided into 28 divisions, organised geographically by region. Some larger regions, such as Lombardy, Piedmont, Tuscany, Lazio, Campania, Apulia and Sicily, have two or three groups, while smaller regions have a single group. Each division typically contains 16 to 18 teams.

At the end of each season, the winner of each division is promoted directly to Serie D. Additional promotion places are contested through play-offs: the winners of the regional play-offs advance to a national play-off phase, from which further teams earn promotion to Serie D. The lowest-placed teams in each division are relegated to the Promozione, and some relegation places are decided by play-outs between teams finishing just above the automatic relegation zone.

History

The championship was established in 1991 as part of a reorganisation of Italian amateur football, replacing the former Promozione as the top regional division and pushing that league down to the sixth tier. Since its creation it has served as the main bridge between fully amateur regional football and the national semi-professional level of Serie D.

Structure

Because it is run by regional committees of the LND, details such as the exact number of teams, play-off formats and relegation rules vary slightly from region to region. Many clubs in Eccellenza are historic local sides, and the league regularly features derby matches between neighbouring towns. Winners of each regional division also typically compete in the regional phase of the Coppa Italia Dilettanti.
