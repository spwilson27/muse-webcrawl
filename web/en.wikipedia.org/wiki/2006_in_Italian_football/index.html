2006 in Italian football

The year 2006 in Italian football was one of the most dramatic in the sport's history in Italy, combining the country's greatest triumph of the modern era with its biggest corruption scandal.

Calciopoli

In May 2006, published transcripts of telephone conversations revealed that Juventus general manager Luciano Moggi and others had influenced the appointment of referees. The ensuing Calciopoli scandal led to Juventus being stripped of the 2004–05 and 2005–06 Serie A titles and relegated to Serie B; AC Milan, Fiorentina, Lazio, and Reggina received points penalties. The 2005–06 championship was reassigned to Inter Milan.

World Cup triumph

Amid the scandal, the Italy national team, coached by Marcello Lippi, won the 2006 FIFA World Cup in Germany. Italy defeated France in the final in Berlin on 9 July, winning 5–3 on penalties after a 1–1 draw, in a match famous for Zinedine Zidane's headbutt on Marco Materazzi. It was Italy's fourth World Cup title. Captain Fabio Cannavaro later won the Ballon d'Or for his performances.

Domestic competitions

Inter Milan won the Coppa Italia, defeating Roma in the final, in a season whose league standings were ultimately rewritten by the Calciopoli verdicts.
