The following is a list of most expensive association football transfers, which details the highest transfer fees ever paid for players, as well as transfers which set new world transfer records. The first confirmed transfer for a fee of over £100 was that of Jack Southworth to Everton in the early 1890s. The current record is the €222 million paid by Paris Saint-Germain to Barcelona for Neymar in August 2017, when the French club activated the Brazilian's buyout clause — more than double the previous record.
