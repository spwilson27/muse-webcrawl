Mountains of the Philippines
HighestMount Apo, 2,954 m
Major islandsLuzon, Mindanao, Visayas

The following is a list of mountains in the Philippines, an archipelago of volcanic and tectonic origin. The country's highest peak is Mount Apo in Mindanao, a potentially active stratovolcano rising to 2,954 metres above sea level. Other major summits include Mount Dulang-dulang and Mount Kitanglad in the Kitanglad range, Mount Pulag in the Cordillera of Luzon, and the active volcanoes Mayon and Mount Pinatubo.
