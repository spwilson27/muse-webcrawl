Treta Yuga

Treta Yuga (Sanskrit: त्रेतायुग) is the second of the four yugas (world ages) in Hindu cosmology, following Satya Yuga and preceding Dvapara Yuga and Kali Yuga. Its name derives from treta, "a collection of three things", in reference to dharma standing on three of its four legs in this age.

Duration and characteristics

Tradition assigns Treta Yuga a duration of 1,296,000 human years. Virtue is said to decline compared with the perfectly righteous Satya Yuga: sacrifices and rituals become necessary, and human lifespan and stature diminish.

In scripture

Treta Yuga is the age of Rama, the seventh avatar of Vishnu, whose story is told in the Ramayana. Other avatars associated with this age include Vamana and Parashurama. See yuga and Hindu cosmology.
