President's Hundred Tab

The President's Hundred Tab is a marksmanship tab worn by members of the United States Armed Forces to recognise the top one hundred competitors at the annual President's Match, a prestigious rifle and pistol competition held during the National Matches at Camp Perry, Ohio.

History

The President's Match was established in 1878, and participation was opened to civilians as well as military shooters. The top-scoring one hundred competitors — the "President's Hundred" — receive a certificate from the President of the United States; military members are authorised to wear the tab on their uniforms.

Design and wear

The tab is an arc-shaped patch worn on the upper sleeve. It exists in full-colour dress and subdued versions. On Army uniforms it is among the few tabs authorised for permanent wear as an individual award for marksmanship excellence.
