Devon Aoki

Devon Edwenna Aoki (born August 10, 1982) is an American model and actress. Born in New York City and raised in California and London, she is the daughter of Japanese businessman Rocky Aoki, founder of the Benihana restaurant chain, and was discovered at a concert as a teenager.

Aoki became one of the most prominent models of the late 1990s and 2000s, walking for houses including Chanel, Versace, and Balenciaga, and succeeding Naomi Campbell as the face of Versace. Despite being shorter than the industry standard at 5 ft 5 in, she is regarded as an icon of the era's fashion scene.

As an actress, she appeared in films including 2 Fast 2 Furious (2003), D.E.B.S. (2004), Sin City (2005) as Miho, DOA: Dead or Alive (2006), and Mutant Chronicles (2008). Her half-brother is the DJ and producer Steve Aoki.
