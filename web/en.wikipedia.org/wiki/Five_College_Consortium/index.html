Five College Consortium

Formation1965
HeadquartersAmherst, Massachusetts
MembersAmherst College, Hampshire College, Mount Holyoke College, Smith College, University of Massachusetts Amherst

The Five College Consortium (formally Five Colleges, Incorporated) is an association of five institutions of higher education in the Pioneer Valley of western Massachusetts: Amherst College, Hampshire College, Mount Holyoke College, Smith College, and the University of Massachusetts Amherst. It is one of the oldest and most extensive higher-education consortia in the United States.

Cooperation

Students at any member institution may cross-register for courses at the others without additional tuition, and the consortium coordinates shared academic programs, including certificates in areas such as African studies and Buddhist studies, joint departments, shared library resources and an inter-campus bus system (operated with the Pioneer Valley Transit Authority). The five campuses together enrol roughly 40,000 students.

History

Cooperation began in 1914 with joint agricultural extension work and deepened through the mid-twentieth century; the members were instrumental in founding Hampshire College, which admitted its first students in 1970. Five Colleges, Inc. was chartered in 1965.
