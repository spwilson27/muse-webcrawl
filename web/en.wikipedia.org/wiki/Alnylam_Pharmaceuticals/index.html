Alnylam Pharmaceuticals, Inc. is an American biopharmaceutical company founded in 2002 and headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts. The company focuses on developing therapeutics based on RNA interference (RNAi), a naturally occurring cellular mechanism for silencing the expression of specific genes. Alnylam is widely regarded as the leading company in the RNAi therapeutics field.

History

Alnylam was founded in 2002 by a group of scientists and venture investors to translate the recently discovered RNAi mechanism — recognized by the 2006 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine awarded to Andrew Fire and Craig Mello — into a new class of medicines. The company takes its name from Alnilam, the central star in the belt of the constellation Orion. John Maraganore served as founding chief executive until 2021, when Yvonne Greenstreet succeeded him.

For many years the central challenge facing the field was delivering RNA molecules to target tissues. Alnylam's development of lipid nanoparticle delivery and later of GalNAc-conjugate technology, which targets the liver, enabled clinical success.

Products

In August 2018, the company's first product, patisiran (Onpattro), was approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration for the treatment of polyneuropathy of hereditary transthyretin-mediated amyloidosis, becoming the first RNAi therapeutic ever approved. Subsequent approvals included givosiran (Givlaari) for acute hepatic porphyria, lumasiran (Oxlumo) for primary hyperoxaluria type 1, inclisiran (Leqvio, partnered with Novartis) for cholesterol lowering, and vutrisiran (Amvuttra) for transthyretin amyloidosis.
