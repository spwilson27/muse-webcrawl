Predation

Predation is a biological interaction in which a predator kills and eats another organism, its prey. It is a powerful driver of Evolution, population dynamics and behaviour.

Dynamics and adaptations

Predator and prey populations can cycle (the Lotka–Volterra equations model the classic lynx–hare oscillations). The arms race produces speed, armour, toxins (Venom), Camouflage, Mimicry, herding and alarm calls. Predators include carnivores, insectivorous plants and filter-feeding giants; grazing and parasitism are related strategies (see Parasite).

Ecological role

Apex predators exert top-down control (trophic cascades): removing keystone predators transforms whole ecosystems, while their restoration — as with Yellowstone wolves — can re-green landscapes.
