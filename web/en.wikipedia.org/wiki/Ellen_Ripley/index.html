Lieutenant First Class Ellen Louise Ripley is a fictional character and the protagonist of the Alien film franchise. Portrayed by Sigourney Weaver, she appears in Alien (1979), Aliens (1986), Alien 3 (1992) and Alien Resurrection (1997). Ripley is widely regarded as one of the most significant female protagonists in cinema and a landmark character in science fiction and horror.

Appearances

In Alien, Ripley is the warrant officer of the commercial towing vehicle Nostromo. When a parasitic creature — brought aboard after a distress call on LV-426 — kills the crew one by one, Ripley's refusal to break quarantine and her resourcefulness leave her the sole survivor, escaping in the shuttle Narcissus with the ship's cat, Jones. Aliens (directed by James Cameron) finds her after 57 years in hypersleep, returning to LV-426 with a squad of colonial marines and forming a maternal bond with the orphan Newt. In Alien 3, directed by David Fincher in his feature debut, Ripley sacrifices herself to destroy the Alien Queen gestating inside her. Alien Resurrection revives her as "Ripley 8", a human–alien hybrid clone.

Reception

Weaver was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Actress for Aliens — a rare honour for a science-fiction performance. The American Film Institute ranked Ripley the eighth-greatest film hero in American cinema. Originally written as a male character, Ripley's gender was changed during casting, helping make her a feminist icon of the action genre.
