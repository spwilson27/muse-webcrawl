Andrei Dmitriyevich Linde (Russian: Андрей Дмитриевич Линде; born 2 March 1948) is a Russian-American theoretical physicist and the Harald Trap Friis Professor of Physics at Stanford University. Linde is one of the main authors of the inflationary universe theory, as well as the theory of eternal inflation and inflationary multiverse.

Work

Working at the Lebedev Physical Institute in Moscow, Linde proposed in 1982 a modification of Alan Guth's original inflation model known as "new inflation", and in 1983 introduced chaotic inflation, in which inflation can begin under a wide range of initial conditions without fine-tuning. He showed that in many models inflation never ends globally: quantum fluctuations continually spawn new inflating regions, a process called eternal inflation, implying a multiverse of pocket universes with potentially different physical properties.

Linde moved to Stanford in 1990. His honours include the Dirac Medal, the Gruber Cosmology Prize, and a share of the inaugural Breakthrough Prize in Fundamental Physics. He is married to physicist Renata Kallosh.
