Raymond A. Spruance

Raymond Ames Spruance (3 July 1886 – 13 December 1969) was a United States Navy admiral during World War II. He commanded U.S. naval forces at the Battle of Midway (1942) and the Battle of the Philippine Sea (1944), two of the Pacific War's decisive carrier battles.

Career

A Naval Academy graduate of 1906, Spruance was noted for calm, analytical command. He later led the Fifth Fleet in the Central Pacific drive, including Iwo Jima and Okinawa, served as president of the Naval War College, and as U.S. Ambassador to the Philippines (1952–1955). The Spruance-class destroyers were named in his honour.
