Donkey Kong Country is a 1994 platform video game developed by Rare and published by Nintendo for the Super Nintendo Entertainment System. In it, Donkey Kong, with his nephew Diddy Kong, journeys across Donkey Kong Island to reclaim his stolen banana hoard from the crocodilian King K. Rool and his Kremling army.

Gameplay and technology

The game is a side-scrolling platformer with two playable characters the player can swap between, animal buddies such as Rambi the rhino, barrel cannons, mine-cart stages and bonus rooms. Its visuals were revolutionary: characters and environments were modelled in 3D on Silicon Graphics workstations and pre-rendered into sprites, giving the 16-bit SNES a pseudo-3D look that stunned players. David Wise's atmospheric soundtrack, especially "Aquatic Ambience", is widely acclaimed.

Reception and legacy

Donkey Kong Country sold over nine million copies, becoming one of the best-selling SNES games, and revived the Donkey Kong franchise. It spawned two SNES sequels, Diddy's Kong Quest (1995) and Dixie Kong's Double Trouble! (1996), Game Boy adaptations, and later revivals Donkey Kong Country Returns (2010) and Tropical Freeze (2014) by Retro Studios.
