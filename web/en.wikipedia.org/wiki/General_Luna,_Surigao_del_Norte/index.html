General Luna
Municipality, Surigao del Norte, Philippines
Island: Siargao
Barangays: 19

General Luna, officially the Municipality of General Luna, is a municipality in the province of Surigao del Norte, Philippines, located on the southeastern coast of Siargao Island. It is named after General Antonio Luna, a general of the Philippine Revolutionary Army. Formerly known by its old name Cabuntog, the town is now internationally known as the surfing gateway of Siargao.

Surfing and tourism

General Luna is home to Cloud 9, a powerful right-hand reef break over shallow coral that hosts an annual international surfing cup competition each autumn, drawing professional surfers from around the world. The break's viewing tower and boardwalk, originally built for competitions, became an icon of Philippine surf tourism. The town serves as the main base for visitors, with hostels, resorts, restaurants, and boat tours departing for nearby islands such as Naked Island, Daku Island, and Guyam Island.

Geography and economy

The municipality comprises 19 barangays and faces the Philippine Sea, giving it exposure to Pacific swells. Fishing and copra farming were traditionally the main livelihoods; tourism has since become a major employer. In December 2021, Super Typhoon Odette (Rai) made landfall on Siargao and devastated General Luna, destroying the Cloud 9 tower and much of the town's infrastructure, which has since been substantially rebuilt.

Access is via Sayak Airport in neighbouring Del Carmen, with vans connecting to General Luna about 45 minutes away.
