Gustav de Vries
Born: 22 January 1866
Died: 16 December 1934
Nationality: Dutch
Known for: Korteweg–de Vries equation

Gustav de Vries (22 January 1866 – 16 December 1934) was a Dutch mathematician best known for his work with Diederik Korteweg on the Korteweg–de Vries (KdV) equation, describing shallow water waves and solitons.

Life and work

De Vries studied at the University of Amsterdam under Korteweg. His 1894 doctoral thesis presented the equation now known as the KdV equation, modelling waves on shallow water surfaces, including the solitary wave first observed by John Scott Russell.

Legacy

The KdV equation became a central model in nonlinear wave theory, soliton mathematics, and mathematical physics. De Vries later worked as a schoolteacher in Haarlem and wrote textbooks, publishing less research than his thesis promised.
