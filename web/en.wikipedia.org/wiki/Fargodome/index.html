Fargodome

The Fargodome is an indoor athletics stadium in Fargo, North Dakota, on the campus of North Dakota State University. Opened in 1992, it is a city-owned multipurpose arena best known as the home of the NDSU Bison football team, whose sustained success in the NCAA Division I Football Championship Subdivision has made the dome one of the loudest and most intimidating venues in college football at its level.

Location:Fargo, North Dakota
Opened:1992
Owner:City of Fargo
Capacity:~18,700 (football); up to ~25,000 (concerts)
Tenant:North Dakota State Bison football

History and features

The stadium was financed partly by a half-cent city sales tax approved by voters in 1989 and built on land leased from NDSU. Despite its name, it is not an air-supported or geodesic dome but a conventional structure with a large clear-span roof of metal decking supported by trusses. Its brick exterior and location adjoining the campus helped it win architectural praise.

Events

Beyond Bison football, the Fargodome hosts concerts, trade shows, rodeos, monster truck events, high school tournaments and the annual North Dakota winter sports events. It has repeatedly served as a venue for community flood-fighting operations, including sandbagging efforts during the Red River floods. Crowd noise for big games has been measured at levels comparable to much larger stadiums, contributing to NDSU's formidable home record.
