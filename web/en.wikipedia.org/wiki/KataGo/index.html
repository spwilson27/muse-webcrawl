KataGo
Developer: David Wu
Initial release: 2019
License: MIT (code), open weights
Written in: C++, CUDA

KataGo is a free and open-source computer Go program, released in 2019 by researcher David Wu. Based on the AlphaGo Zero paradigm with many efficiency improvements, it is among the strongest publicly available Go engines and is widely used for game analysis.

Features

KataGo's neural network outputs not only a policy and value, but also predictions of the final score, ownership of board points and other auxiliary targets that speed up training. It supports variable board sizes and komi, and can analyse positions under both Japanese and Chinese rules.

Training

Its networks are trained by a distributed volunteer effort: users contribute self-play games generated on their own hardware, from which progressively stronger networks are produced and published openly.

References: Wu (2019), "Accelerating Self-Play Learning in Go"; KataGo project documentation.
