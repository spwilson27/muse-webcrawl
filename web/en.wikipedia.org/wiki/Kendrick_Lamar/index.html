Kendrick Lamar

Kendrick Lamar Duckworth
Born: 17 June 1987, Compton, California
Genres: Hip hop
Occupation: Rapper, songwriter
Awards: Pulitzer Prize for Music (2018)

Kendrick Lamar Duckworth (born 17 June 1987) is an American rapper and songwriter from Compton, California, widely regarded as one of the most influential artists of his generation.

Career

After mixtapes under the name K.Dot and signing with Top Dawg Entertainment, Lamar released Section.80 (2011) and his major-label debut good kid, m.A.A.d city (2012). To Pimp a Butterfly (2015) fused hip hop with jazz and funk to wide acclaim. DAMN. (2017) won the Pulitzer Prize for Music — the first popular-music work so honoured. Later albums include Mr. Morale & the Big Steppers (2022) and GNX (2024).

Artistry and impact

Lamar's writing addresses race, faith, family and inner-city life with dense wordplay and shifting voices. He has won numerous Grammy Awards and headlined the Super Bowl LIX halftime show in 2025.
