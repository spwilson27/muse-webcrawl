Salon des Indépendants

The Salon des Indépendants is an annual art exhibition held in Paris by the Société des Artistes Indépendants, founded in 1884. True to its founding principle, it accepts works by any artist without a selection jury or prizes.

History

The society was founded by artists including Paul Signac, Georges Seurat, Odilon Redon and Albert Dubois-Pillet, in reaction to the conservative jury system of the official Salon. The first exhibition opened on 1 December 1884.

Significance

The Salon des Indépendants became a key venue for Post-Impressionism, Fauvism and Cubism. Memorable moments include the 1905 room shared with the Fauves and the scandal of Marcel Duchamp's Nude Descending a Staircase at the 1912 show. It continues to be held in Paris today.
