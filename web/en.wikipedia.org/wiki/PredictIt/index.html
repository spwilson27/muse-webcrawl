PredictIt

PredictIt is an online prediction market based in New Zealand that offers exchange-traded markets on the outcome of political and financial events. It is operated by the Victoria University of Wellington with support from Aristotle, Inc., and launched in 2014 under a no-action letter from the US Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) that permitted it to operate as a not-for-profit research and education project.

How it works

Traders buy shares in event outcomes — for example, which candidate will win an election — at prices between $0.01 and $0.99, which can be interpreted as the market's implied probability of the event. Winning shares pay out $1. Under the terms of its regulatory relief, participation was limited (for example, caps on the number of traders per contract and on individual investment) in order to keep the market small and experimental in character. PredictIt's prices are widely cited by journalists during US election campaigns alongside polling data.

Regulatory history

In August 2022 the CFTC withdrew the no-action letter, directing PredictIt to wind down markets; the operator sued, and litigation over the platform's future continued through the mid-2020s. The dispute became a prominent case in the broader debate over the regulation of event contracts in the United States, alongside the growth of rivals such as Kalshi. See also prediction market.
