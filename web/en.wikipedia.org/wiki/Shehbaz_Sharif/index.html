Shehbaz Sharif

Mian Muhammad Shehbaz Sharif (born 23 September 1951) is a Pakistani politician who has served as the 24th prime minister of Pakistan since March 2024, having previously held the office from April 2022 to August 2023. He is the president of the Pakistan Muslim League (N) (PML-N) and the younger brother of three-time prime minister Nawaz Sharif.

Political career

A member of the Sharif family of Lahore industrialists, Shehbaz Sharif entered politics in the 1980s and was elected to the Punjab Assembly. He served three terms as chief minister of Punjab (1997–1999, 2008–2013 and 2013–2018), becoming the province's longest-serving chief minister. His tenure was marked by rapid infrastructure development — including the Lahore Metrobus, motorways and power projects — and a hands-on administrative style that earned him the nickname "Shahbaz Speed". His second term was overshadowed by the 2014 Model Town incident, and after the 1999 coup he spent years in exile in Saudi Arabia.

Prime minister

In April 2022 Sharif became prime minister after Imran Khan was removed in a no-confidence vote, heading a broad coalition government that steered Pakistan through devastating floods and an acute economic crisis, securing an IMF programme before dissolving the National Assembly in August 2023. Following the February 2024 general election, he returned as prime minister of a PML-N-led coalition.
