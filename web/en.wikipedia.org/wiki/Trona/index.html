Trona

Trona is an evaporite mineral, a hydrated sodium carbonate-bicarbonate with the chemical formula Na2CO3·NaHCO3·2H2O. It forms in arid lake beds and playa deposits where sodium-rich brines evaporate, and it is the principal natural source of soda ash (sodium carbonate) in the United States. The world's largest known deposit lies in the Green River Formation of Wyoming, with other significant deposits in Turkey and at Lake Magadi in Kenya.

CategoryCarbonate mineral (evaporite)
FormulaNa2CO3·NaHCO3·2H2O
Crystal systemMonoclinic
ColourColourless, white, grey, yellowish

Uses

Mined trona is calcined to produce soda ash, a bulk industrial chemical used in glassmaking, detergents, paper production and water treatment. Wyoming's trona district supplies the great majority of United States soda ash output, much of it for export.
