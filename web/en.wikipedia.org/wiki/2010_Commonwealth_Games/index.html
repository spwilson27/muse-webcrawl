The 2010 Commonwealth Games, officially the XIX Commonwealth Games, were held in Delhi, India, from 3 to 14 October 2010. They were the largest multi-sport event staged in India, surpassing the Asian Games of 1951 and 1982, and the first Commonwealth Games hosted by the country.

A total of 71 Commonwealth teams and more than 6,000 athletes competed in 272 events across 17 sports. Preparations were marred by delays, corruption allegations, cost overruns, and concerns about conditions at the athletes' village in the weeks before the Games, though the event itself was widely judged a success.

Medal table

Australia topped the medal table with 74 gold medals, while host nation India achieved its best-ever performance, finishing second with 38 golds — including a strong showing in shooting, wrestling, boxing, and weightlifting — ahead of England in third.
