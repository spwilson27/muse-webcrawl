Red Hots

Red Hots is a brand of small, hard, cinnamon-flavored candies made by the Ferrara Candy Company. The red-colored candies are flavored with cinnamon and deliver a strong, spicy-sweet taste. They were originally created by the Ferrara Pan Candy Company of Forest Park, Illinois.

Production

Red Hots are made by the cold panning process, in which sugar cores are rotated in pans while syrup, flavoring (including cinnamon oil), and red coloring are gradually added, building up the hard shell layer by layer. The same technique is used for other Ferrara products such as Lemonheads and Boston Baked Beans.

Uses

Beyond eating as candy, Red Hots are used decoratively on baked goods and in recipes — traditionally dissolved into applesauce, spiced cider, and the pickling liquid for cinnamon pickles, and sometimes to flavor homemade liqueurs and jams.

Other uses of the name

"Red hot" is also a general term for a frankfurter or hot dog in parts of the United States, and red-hot candies gave their name to the idiom for something intensely hot or popular.
