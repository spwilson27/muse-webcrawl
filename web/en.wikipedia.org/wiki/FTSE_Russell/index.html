Type: Subsidiary
Industry: Financial services
Parent: London Stock Exchange Group
Headquarters: London, United Kingdom
Products: Stock market indices, analytics

FTSE Russell is a subsidiary of the London Stock Exchange Group (LSEG) that produces, maintains, licenses, and markets stock market indices. Its best-known products include the UK FTSE 100 Index and the US Russell 2000 Index of small-cap companies.

History

The FTSE brand originated in 1984 with the FTSE 100, created by the Financial Times and the London Stock Exchange ("FTSE" stands for Footsie, from Financial Times Stock Exchange). The Russell Indexes business, founded by Frank Russell Company in the United States, was acquired by the London Stock Exchange Group in 2014 for US$2.7 billion, and the combined index business was rebranded FTSE Russell in 2015.

Indices

FTSE Russell calculates thousands of indices covering equities, fixed income, real estate, and sustainable investment across more than 80 countries, including the FTSE All-World, FTSE 250, Russell 1000, Russell 2000, and Russell 3000. The annual reconstitution of the Russell US indexes each June is a major market event.

Business

Index data is licensed to issuers of exchange-traded funds and other investment products benchmarked to the indices, as well as to exchanges, asset managers, and financial media.
