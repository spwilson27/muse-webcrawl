The 94th Fighter Squadron is a unit of the United States Air Force, assigned to the 1st Operations Group at Joint Base Langley–Eustis, Virginia. It is one of the oldest fighter squadrons in the Air Force, famous as the First World War unit of ace Eddie Rickenbacker, and today flies the F-22 Raptor.

First World War

Organised in 1917 as the 94th Aero Squadron, the unit deployed to France and became the most successful American pursuit squadron of the war, credited with 64⅔ victories. Its "Hat in the Ring" insignia — Uncle Sam's hat inside a ring — was painted on its SPAD XIII fighters. Captain Eddie Rickenbacker, its commanding officer, became America's leading ace with 26 victories.

Later service

Between the wars the squadron flew pursuit aircraft from Selfridge Field, Michigan. In the Second World War it operated the P-38 Lightning in North Africa and Italy with the Twelfth and Fifteenth Air Forces. During the Cold War it served as an interceptor squadron, and from 1971 it flew the F-15 Eagle for over three decades.

Modern era

In 2005 the 94th became the second operational squadron to convert to the F-22 Raptor, flying air-dominance missions from Langley. It has deployed in support of operations in the Middle East and regularly participates in exercises worldwide.
