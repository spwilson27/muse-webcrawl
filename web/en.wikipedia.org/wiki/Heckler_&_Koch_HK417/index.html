Heckler & Koch HK417

The Heckler & Koch HK417 is a gas-operated battle rifle chambered for the 7.62×51mm NATO cartridge, designed and manufactured by the German company Heckler & Koch. It is the larger-calibre counterpart of the 5.56mm HK416 and uses a short-stroke piston system derived from the G36/AR-15 lineage.

Design

The HK417 feeds from 10- or 20-round box magazines, features a free-floating barrel with accessory rails, and is offered with barrel lengths of 12, 16.5 and 20 inches. The 20-inch accurised variant, the G28, serves as a designated marksman rifle with the German Bundeswehr and other forces.

Users

The rifle has been adopted by numerous military and law-enforcement units, including special forces of Germany, Norway, France, the United Kingdom and others. The United States Army's M110A1 compact semi-automatic sniper system is based on the G28/HK417 platform.
