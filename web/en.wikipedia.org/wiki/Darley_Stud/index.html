Darley Stud

Darley is the thoroughbred breeding arm of Godolphin, the global racing and breeding operation founded by Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, ruler of Dubai. Darley stands stallions at stud farms in the United Kingdom, Ireland, the United States, Australia, and Japan, and is one of the largest thoroughbred breeding operations in the world.

History and farms

The operation takes its name from the Darley Arabian, one of the three foundation sires of the thoroughbred breed, imported to England in the early 18th century. Its principal farms include Dalham Hall Stud at Newmarket, England — purchased by Sheikh Mohammed in 1981 — which stands stallions such as Dubawi, one of the world's most valuable sires. In Ireland, Darley operates Kildangan Stud in County Kildare; in the United States, Jonabell Farm in Kentucky; and in Australia, Kelvinside in New South Wales and Northwood Park in Victoria.

Notable stallions

Darley stallions have included Dubawi, sire of numerous Group 1 winners; Shamardal; Street Cry, sire of Zenyatta and Winx; Medaglia d'Oro; and Too Darn Hot. The racing arm, Godolphin, races in royal blue silks and has won many of the world's premier races, including the Dubai World Cup and the Epsom Derby.
