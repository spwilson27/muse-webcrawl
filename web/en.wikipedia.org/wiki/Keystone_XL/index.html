Keystone XL (sometimes abbreviated KXL, with "XL" standing for "export limited") was a proposed expansion of the Keystone Pipeline System, intended to transport crude oil from the oil sands of Alberta, Canada, to refineries on the Gulf Coast of the United States.

History

The project was proposed by TransCanada Corporation (later TC Energy) in 2008 as a fourth phase of the Keystone system. It became a major political controversy in both Canada and the United States, drawing opposition from environmental groups, Indigenous communities, and landowners along the route.

Cancellation

President Barack Obama rejected the required presidential permit in 2015; President Donald Trump revived the project in 2017. On 20 January 2021, President Joe Biden revoked the permit on his first day in office, and TC Energy formally terminated the project in June 2021.
