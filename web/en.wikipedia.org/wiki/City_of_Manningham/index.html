City of Manningham

The City of Manningham is a local government area in Victoria, Australia, in the north-eastern suburbs of Melbourne. It covers an area of about 114 square kilometres and had a population of roughly 125,000 at the 2021 census. The council offices are at the Manningham civic centre in Doncaster. The city takes its name from Manningham, a suburb of Bradford in England.

Bushland along the Yarra River in Manningham's north-east.

History

The area was originally part of the Shire of Bulleen and later the City of Doncaster & Templestowe. The City of Manningham was created on 15 December 1994 as part of the Kennett government's local government amalgamations, combining most of Doncaster & Templestowe with a portion of the Shire of Lillydale (including Warrandyte).

Suburbs and townships

Suburbs within Manningham include Bulleen, Doncaster, Doncaster East, Donvale, Nunawading (part), Park Orchards, Ringwood North (part), Templestowe, Templestowe Lower, Warrandyte, Warrandyte South and Wonga Park. The city's eastern portion along the Yarra River retains extensive bushland and a semi-rural character, in contrast to its denser western suburbs.

Governance

Since the 2020 general election, Manningham has been divided into three wards, each electing three councillors. The city is notable for its large Chinese-Australian and Greek-Australian communities. Major landmarks include Westfield Doncaster, one of Melbourne's largest shopping centres, and the Heidelberg School-associated art sites around Warrandyte.
