Tel Aviv Light Rail

LocaleTel Aviv metropolitan area (Gush Dan), Israel
Transit typeLight rail / light metro
LinesRed Line (operating); Green and Purple lines under construction
Began operation18 August 2023 (Red Line)

The Tel Aviv Light Rail (also known as the Dankal) is a mass transit system for the Tel Aviv metropolitan area in Israel. Its first line, the Red Line, opened on 18 August 2023 after decades of planning and repeated delays. The system is being built by the government-owned NTA Metropolitan Mass Transit System.

Red Line

The Red Line runs for 24 km from Petah Tikva through Bnei Brak, Ramat Gan and Tel Aviv to Bat Yam, with 34 stations; the central section in Tel Aviv runs underground with 10 subway stations. It is operated by the Tevel company using CRRC-built low-floor trainsets.

Future lines

The Green Line (39 km, linking Holon and Rishon LeZion with Tel Aviv and Herzliya) and the Purple Line (27 km, serving eastern suburbs including Kiryat Ono and Yehud) are under construction. A full metro system (the Tel Aviv Metro, lines M1–M3) is planned for later decades to complement the light rail network.

History

Plans for rapid transit in Tel Aviv date to the 1960s and a subway was approved in the 1970s but never built. The Red Line was originally tendered as a build-operate-transfer concession in 2006, nationalised in 2010, and its opening was postponed several times before services began in 2023.
