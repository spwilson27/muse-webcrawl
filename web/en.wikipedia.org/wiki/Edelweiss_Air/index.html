Edelweiss Air AG is a Swiss leisure airline headquartered at Zurich Airport. It is a sister company of Swiss International Air Lines and a member of the Lufthansa Group, operating scheduled and charter flights to holiday destinations in Europe and intercontinental leisure destinations in the Americas, Africa, Asia and the Indian Ocean.

History

The airline was founded on 19 October 1995 in Bassersdorf, Switzerland, taking its name from the edelweiss, the Swiss alpine flower that also appears on its aircraft tails. Early operations used McDonnell Douglas MD-83 aircraft on charter services. Edelweiss rebuilt its fleet around Airbus types, introducing the A320 for short- and medium-haul routes and the A330, and later the A340-300, for long-haul services.

In 2008 the airline became part of the Lufthansa Group through its acquisition by Swiss International Air Lines, while retaining its own brand and leisure-focused network. From the mid-2010s it expanded substantially in long-haul leisure flying from Zurich — destinations have included destinations in the Caribbean, Costa Rica, Mexico, the Maldives, Mauritius, Sri Lanka, Vietnam, South Africa and North America — and modernised its fleet, announcing the introduction of the Airbus A350 to replace its A340s.

Fleet

Edelweiss operates an all-Airbus fleet. Short- and medium-haul services use the Airbus A320 family, while long-haul routes have been flown by Airbus A330-300 and A340-300 aircraft, with A350-900s ordered as their successors. Aircraft are configured in a leisure-oriented layout with business and economy cabins.

Destinations

The network centres on Zurich and seasonal holiday markets: the Mediterranean and Canary Islands in summer, and long-haul sun destinations in winter, including several routes not served by its sister airline Swiss.
