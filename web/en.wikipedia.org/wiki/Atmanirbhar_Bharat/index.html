Atmanirbhar Bharat

Atmanirbhar Bharat (Hindi: आत्मनिर्भर भारत, lit. 'self-reliant India') is a policy initiative and slogan used by the Prime Minister of India, Narendra Modi, and the Government of India in relation to the country's economic development plans. The term was first prominently used in May 2020, when the Prime Minister announced the Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan (Self-reliant India Mission) as part of the response to the COVID-19 pandemic.

Announcement

On 12 May 2020, the Prime Minister announced a special economic package of ₹20 lakh crore (equivalent to about 10% of India's GDP) under the mission. The initiative is described by the government as resting on five pillars: economy, infrastructure, systems (technology-driven), vibrant demography, and demand. The slogan "Vocal for Local" was promoted alongside it.

Policy measures

Measures associated with the initiative include production-linked incentive (PLI) schemes for manufacturing sectors such as electronics, pharmaceuticals and automobiles; reforms in agriculture marketing and labour law; support for micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs); increased emphasis on domestic defence production; and restrictions or quality controls on some imports. The government emphasizes that self-reliance does not mean isolation or a return to protectionism, but a greater integration of India into global supply chains on stronger terms.

Reception

Supporters argue the initiative can reduce dependence on imports (particularly from China, a point of salience after the 2020 border tensions), boost manufacturing and create jobs. Critics have questioned aspects of implementation, argued that some measures are repackaged earlier schemes, and debated the balance between self-reliance and openness to trade and foreign investment.
