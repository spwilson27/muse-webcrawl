Christmas calendar

A Christmas calendar (Swedish: julkalender, Danish: julekalender, Norwegian: julekalender, Finnish: joulukalenteri) is a Nordic tradition of daily radio or television programmes broadcast during Advent, typically from 1 to 24 December, one episode per day leading to Christmas Eve.

Tradition

Sweden's Sveriges Radio began a children's Christmas calendar in 1957, and Sveriges Television followed in 1960; Denmark, Norway, Finland, and Iceland developed similar institutions. The programmes — often accompanied by a physical paper calendar with daily doors — are major cultural events for children, and many series are remembered nostalgically by generations.

Related customs

The concept relates to the Advent calendar, originating in 19th-century Germany, but the Nordic broadcast calendar is a distinctive media tradition.
