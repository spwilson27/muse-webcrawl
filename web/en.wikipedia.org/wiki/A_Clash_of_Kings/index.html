A Clash of Kings

A Clash of Kings is the second novel in George R. R. Martin's epic fantasy series A Song of Ice and Fire, published in 1998 in the UK and 1999 in the US. It won the Locus Award for Best Fantasy Novel.


Author: George R. R. Martin
Published: 1998
Series: A Song of Ice and Fire #2
Preceded by: A Game of Thrones
Followed by: A Storm of Swords

Plot

As a red comet crosses the sky, the War of the Five Kings engulfs Westeros: Joffrey Baratheon holds the Iron Throne, Robb Stark is King in the North, and brothers Stannis and Renly Baratheon each claim the crown, while Balon Greyjoy declares himself King of the Iron Islands. Stannis, aided by the red priestess Melisandre, kills Renly by shadow magic and is crushed at the Battle of the Blackwater by a Lannister–Tyrell alliance and Tyrion's wildfire. In the north, Theon Greyjoy seizes Winterfell; beyond the Wall, Jon Snow joins a Night's Watch ranging. In Essos, Daenerys Targaryen travels through Qarth with her young dragons.

Adaptation

The novel forms the basis of the second season of HBO's Game of Thrones (2012).
