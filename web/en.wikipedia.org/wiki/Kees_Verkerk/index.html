Kees Verkerk

Cornelis Arie Verkerk
Born: 28 October 1942, Maasdam, Netherlands
Sport: Speed skating
Olympic medals: 1 gold, 3 silver

Cornelis Arie "Kees" Verkerk (born 28 October 1942) is a Dutch former speed skater. He was one of the dominant allround skaters of the late 1960s and early 1970s, renowned for his rivalry with fellow Dutchman Ard Schenk.

Career

Verkerk won the World Allround Championships in 1966 and 1967 and the European Allround Championships in 1967. At the 1968 Winter Olympics in Grenoble he won gold in the 1500 metres and silver in the 5000 metres. At the 1972 Sapporo Olympics he added silver medals in the 10000 metres.

Later life

After turning professional in 1972, Verkerk later settled in Norway, where he ran a campsite near Kristiansand. He was named Dutch Sportsman of the Year in 1966 and 1967.
