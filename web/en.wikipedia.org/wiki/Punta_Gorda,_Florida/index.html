Punta Gorda is a city in and the county seat of Charlotte County, Florida, United States. It lies on the southern shore of Charlotte Harbor where the Peace River meets the harbour, on Florida's Gulf Coast.

History

The town was founded in the 1880s with the arrival of the railroad and was incorporated in 1887; its name is Spanish for "fat point", describing the land jutting into the harbour. Punta Gorda suffered severe damage from Hurricane Charley in 2004 and was again struck by Hurricane Ian in 2022.

Today

Known for its historic district, waterfront parks, canals with Gulf access and retirement community, the city is a centre for boating and fishing.
