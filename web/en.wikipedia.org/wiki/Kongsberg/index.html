Kongsberg

Country: Norway

County: Buskerud

Founded: 1624

Known for: Silver mining, technology industry

Kongsberg is a town and municipality in Buskerud county, Norway, located on the river Numedalslågen at the entrance of the Numedal valley. It is a historic mining town and now a centre of high-technology industry.

History

Kongsberg was founded in 1624 by King Christian IV after silver was discovered in the surrounding hills. The Kongsberg Silver Mines grew into Norway's largest mining operation, employing thousands, and a royal mint was established in the town in 1686 — the mint still produces all Norwegian coins. The town's Baroque church, completed in 1761, is one of the largest in Norway.

Economy

Modern Kongsberg hosts the headquarters of Kongsberg Gruppen and firms in defence, maritime, automotive, and subsea technology. The annual Kongsberg Jazz Festival, held since 1964, is a major cultural event.
