NSYNC (stylized as *NSYNC) is an American boy band formed in Orlando, Florida, in 1995 by Chris Kirkpatrick. The group's members are Kirkpatrick, Justin Timberlake, JC Chasez, Joey Fatone, and Lance Bass. They became one of the best-selling boy bands of all time, selling over 70 million records worldwide.

History

Formed under manager Lou Pearlman, the group first found success in Europe before breaking through in the United States with their self-titled debut album (1998). Their third album, No Strings Attached (2000), sold 2.4 million copies in its first week — a record at the time — and produced the hit "Bye Bye Bye". Celebrity (2001) included the singles "Pop" and "Girlfriend".

Hiatus and reunion

The group went on hiatus in 2002 and never formally released another album; Timberlake launched a highly successful solo career. NSYNC reunited briefly for the 2013 MTV Video Music Awards and received a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame in 2018. In 2023 they recorded "Better Place" for the film Trolls Band Together, their first new song in over two decades.

Legacy

Alongside Backstreet Boys, NSYNC defined the late-1990s teen pop boom. Their choreographed performances, harmonies, and merchandising influence made them a cultural phenomenon of the era.
