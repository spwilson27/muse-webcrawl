Digestif

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Digestif
TypeAlcoholic drink served after a meal
CounterpartApéritif (served before a meal)
Common typesAmaro, grappa, brandy, aged whisky, herbal liqueurs, fortified wines
Origin of termFrench digestif, "digestive"

A digestif (French pronunciation: [diʒɛstif]) is an alcoholic beverage served after a meal, traditionally in the belief that it aids digestion. The custom is the mirror image of the apéritif, served before a meal to stimulate the appetite. Digestifs are commonly taken neat, in small quantities, and are frequently more strongly flavoured, bitter or aged than drinks served earlier in a meal.

Types

The classic digestifs are the bitter Italian amari, such as Fernet-Branca, Averna and Montenegro, macerated from herbs, roots and spices whose bitter compounds were historically associated with digestive remedies. Grape-based spirits are widespread: French Cognac and Armagnac, Italian grappa (and its gentler cousin acquavite), and Spanish brandy. Aged whiskies, dark rums and añejo tequilas serve the same role, as do sweet liqueurs such as Chartreuse (made by Carthusian monks from 130 herbs), Bénédictine, Drambuie, limoncello and German herbal liqueurs like Jägermeister and the gentler Underberg bitters. In Central and Eastern Europe fruit brandies such as pálinka, rakia and slivovica are traditional, while in Scandinavia akvavit — flavoured with caraway or dill — accompanies heavy meals. Fortified wines (port, Madeira, sherry) and dessert wines can also close a meal.

Physiology

Alcohol does stimulate gastric acid secretion in modest doses and the bitter principles in amari may promote bile flow, which plausibly explains the subjective sense of relief after rich food; however, larger quantities of alcohol slow gastric emptying, and there is little clinical evidence that digestifs materially improve digestion. Their persistence is as much ritual and gastronomic as medicinal: a small, intense drink marking the end of the meal and the beginning of conversation.
