David Heyman

David Jonathan Heyman (born 26 July 1961) is a British film producer, best known as the producer of all eight films in the Harry Potter film series. He founded the production company Heyday Films in 1996, after acquiring the film rights to J. K. Rowling's novels.

Career

Heyman began his career in the United States, working as an assistant to David Puttnam and later as an executive at Warner Bros. and United Artists. Returning to London, he produced films such as Ravenous (1999) before the success of Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone (2001) made Heyday one of the most prominent British production companies.

Other productions

His other credits include I Am Legend (2007), The Boy in the Striped Pyjamas (2008), Gravity (2013), Paddington (2014) and its sequel, the Fantastic Beasts series, Once Upon a Time in Hollywood (2019), Marriage Story (2019), Wonka (2023), and Barbie (2023).
