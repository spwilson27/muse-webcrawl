Thomas Carlyle Ford (born August 27, 1961) is an American fashion designer and filmmaker. He launched his eponymous luxury brand in 2005, having previously served as creative director at Gucci and Yves Saint Laurent, where he is credited with transforming Gucci from a near-bankrupt house into a multi-billion-dollar brand during the 1990s.

Career at Gucci

Ford joined Gucci in 1990 and became creative director in 1994. His sleek, glamorous, overtly sensual designs — and provocative advertising campaigns — revived the house's fortunes, increasing sales dramatically over the decade. When Gucci acquired Yves Saint Laurent in 1999, Ford also designed its ready-to-wear line. He left the Gucci Group in 2004 following a dispute with management.

Own label and film

In 2005 Ford launched the Tom Ford brand, beginning with eyewear and beauty before opening his first flagship menswear store in New York in 2007; womenswear followed in 2010. The Estée Lauder Companies acquired the Tom Ford brand in a deal announced in 2022 valued at about $2.8 billion, with Ford stepping down as creative director in 2023.

Ford has also directed two acclaimed films: A Single Man (2009), for which Colin Firth received an Academy Award nomination, and Nocturnal Animals (2016), which won the Grand Jury Prize at Venice.
