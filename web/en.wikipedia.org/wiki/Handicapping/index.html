Handicapping

Handicapping, in sport and games, is the practice of assigning advantage through scoring compensation or other advantage given to different contestants to equalize their chances of winning. The word also applies to the various methods by which the advantage is calculated.

In horse racing, a handicap race assigns different weights for the horses to carry based on their past performance, theoretically giving each an equal chance. In golf, a player's handicap is a numerical measure of their potential ability, used to enable players of varying abilities to compete against one another.

Methods

Handicapping systems may be based on previous results, ratings maintained by a governing body, or adjustments within a single event. Betting markets also use the term for the practice of predicting outcomes, and bookmakers set handicaps such as point spreads to balance wagering.
