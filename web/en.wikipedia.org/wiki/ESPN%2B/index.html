ESPN+

ESPN+ (pronounced "ESPN Plus") is an American over-the-top subscription video streaming service owned by ESPN, a division of the Walt Disney Company. Launched on 12 April 2018, it offers live sports, original programming, documentaries, and an on-demand library as a complement to ESPN's cable networks.

Content

ESPN+ carries thousands of live events not shown on ESPN's television channels, including UFC events and pay-per-views (for which it is the exclusive US distributor), NHL and MLB games, PGA Tour golf, Top Rank boxing, international and domestic soccer, college sports, tennis, and cricket. It also streams the 30 for 30 documentary library, original series, and studio shows. Core NFL Monday Night Football and NBA national games long remained primarily on linear television, though select events are simulcast or exclusive to the service.

Role in the streaming market

ESPN+ was Disney's first direct-to-consumer sports service, built on technology from BAMTech, and it is frequently sold in a bundle with Disney+ and Hulu. It reached roughly 25 million subscribers in the mid-2020s. In 2025 ESPN launched a broader direct-to-consumer flagship service under the ESPN name, into which ESPN+ content was folded for subscribers.
