General Archive of the Indies

The General Archive of the Indies (Spanish: Archivo General de Indias), housed in the former merchants' exchange (Casa Lonja) of Seville, Spain, is the principal archive of documents relating to the Spanish Empire in the Americas and the Philippines. Created in 1785 by order of King Charles III, it centralised records previously scattered among the archives of Simancas, Cádiz and Seville. Its holdings — some 43,000 files and around 80 million pages — are an irreplaceable source for the history of the Spanish colonial world.

The Casa Lonja de Mercaderes (1584–1598) houses the archive.

History

The archive was the brainchild of Minister of the Indies José de Gálvez and historian Juan Bautista Muñoz, who wanted the documentation of Spain's overseas empire gathered in one place, in part to support a projected official history of the New World. Documents began arriving in 1785, including records of the Council of the Indies, the Casa de Contratación (House of Trade), consulates, and colonial ministries.

Collections

The archive preserves documents from the voyages of Columbus — including his correspondence and the records surrounding his 1493 letters — through to the end of Spanish rule in Cuba and the Philippines in the late 19th century. Sections include Patronato (royal patronage), Government records for each audiencia (Santo Domingo, Mexico, Lima, and others), Justice, Finance, and the huge Contratación series documenting ships, passengers and trade between Spain and America. Researchers use the archive to study colonial administration, indigenous peoples, trade, cartography and genealogy; extensive digitisation has made millions of pages available through Spain's PARES portal.

The building

The Casa Lonja, designed by Juan de Herrera in Herrerian Renaissance style and completed in 1598, was built for Seville's merchant guild. Its austere stone facades and central courtyard adjoin the cathedral. Together with the cathedral and the Alcázar, it was inscribed on UNESCO's World Heritage List in 1987.
