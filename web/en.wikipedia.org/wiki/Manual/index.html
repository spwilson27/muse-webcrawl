Manual

Manual may refer to several things:

Uses

Instruction manual — an owner's manual or user guide: a book or document of instructions supplied with a product, machine, system or piece of software, explaining how to install, operate and maintain it safely and correctly. Instruction manuals range from single sheets to comprehensive reference works; in computing, "man pages" provide documentation for Unix commands.

Manual (music) — on an organ or harpsichord, one of the keyboards played by the hands, as distinct from the pedalboard played by the feet. Large pipe organs may have four or five manuals.

Manual labour and control — work done by hand rather than by machines or animals; "manual" also describes control exercised directly by an operator, as opposed to automatic operation, and a manual transmission (gearbox) in vehicles.

Other

Manual is also a given name and the name of various places and publications. Above are the principal meanings of the term; see related entries for specialised senses in medicine (manual therapy), law, and the military (drill manual).
