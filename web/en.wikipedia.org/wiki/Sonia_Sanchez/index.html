Sonia Sanchez

Sonia Sanchez (born Wilsonia Benita Driver; September 9, 1934) is an American poet, writer, and professor. She was a leading figure in the Black Arts Movement and has authored dozens of books of poetry, plays, children's books and essays.

Life

Born in Birmingham, Alabama, Sanchez lost her mother in infancy and was raised partly by her grandmother in Harlem. She graduated from Hunter College in 1955 and studied poetry at New York University. She taught at several universities, notably Temple University, where she held the Laura Carnell Chair.

Career

Her first book, Homecoming (1969), and We a BaddDDD People (1970) used Black vernacular, jazz rhythms and innovative typography. She was active in CORE and the civil rights movement and helped establish Black studies programmes. She was Philadelphia's first Poet Laureate (2012–2013).

Awards

Honours include the American Book Award, the Langston Hughes Poetry Award, the Frost Medal (2021), and the Ruth Lilly Poetry Prize (2021).
