Deutsche Welle (DW) is Germany's state-funded international broadcaster, founded in 1953, providing television, radio and online news in some 30 languages from Bonn and Berlin. Modelled partly on the BBC World Service, it is financed from the federal budget while statutorily independent in its journalism.

Services

DW's flagship English TV channel and language services (notably in Africa, Asia, Eastern Europe and Latin America) reach audiences where press freedom is limited; its 'Learning German' offerings and the DW Akademie media-development work are distinctive. The Russian service was forced to close its Moscow bureau after Russia designated DW in the 2022 wartime measures.
