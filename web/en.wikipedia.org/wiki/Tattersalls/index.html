Tattersalls (formerly spelled Tattersall's) is the main auctioneer of racehorses in the United Kingdom and Ireland. Founded in 1766 by Richard Tattersall, it is the oldest bloodstock auctioneers in the world and among the oldest in any field. The company is based at Park Paddocks in Newmarket, Suffolk.

History

Richard Tattersall, a former stud groom to the Duke of Kingston, set up premises at Hyde Park Corner in London in 1766, selling horses and hounds and providing rooms where members of the Jockey Club settled bets. The business became the recognised centre of sporting transactions in Georgian London; "Tattersall's" subscription rooms were where odds were made and wagers recorded. In 1865 the firm moved to Knightsbridge, and the sales operation gradually shifted to Newmarket, the centre of British flat racing. The Hyde Park Corner site is commemorated by the Tattersall Tavern and street names nearby.

Sales

Tattersalls conducts a series of major sales at Park Paddocks, including the October Yearling Sale—Europe's leading yearling auction, whose Book 1 regularly produces some of the world's highest-priced yearlings—as well as the December Mare Sale, the Craven and Guineas Breeze-Up sales, and sales of horses in training. The company also operates Tattersalls Ireland, at Fairyhouse near Dublin, and conducts sales in France through a partnership. Record prices at Tattersalls have exceeded 5 million guineas for a single lot.
