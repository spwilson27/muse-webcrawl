Lake Revelstoke

Lake Revelstoke is a reservoir on the Columbia River in British Columbia, Canada, created by the Revelstoke Dam, which was completed in 1984.

Description

The lake extends roughly 130 km northward from the dam near the city of Revelstoke toward Mica Dam, following the flooded valley of the Columbia River between the Selkirk and Monashee mountains. The Big Bend Highway section was partly inundated by the reservoir.

Hydroelectricity

Revelstoke Dam's powerhouse is one of the largest in British Columbia, with an installed capacity of over 2,400 megawatts.
