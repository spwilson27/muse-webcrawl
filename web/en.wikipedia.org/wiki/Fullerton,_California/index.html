Fullerton is a city in northern Orange County, California, United States. As of the 2020 census, the city had a population of 143,617. It was founded in 1887 by George and Edward Amerige and named after George Fullerton, president of the Pacific Land and Improvement Company, who secured the land for the Atchison, Topeka and Santa Fe Railway. Fullerton is known for its historic downtown, its association with the citrus industry, and as the home of California State University, Fullerton.

History

The area was originally inhabited by the Tongva (Gabrieleño) people. In the Spanish and Mexican eras it formed part of Rancho San Juan Cajón de Santa Ana and Rancho La Habra. The Amerige brothers purchased land in 1886 and, after negotiating a rail stop with the Santa Fe Railway — using George Fullerton as an intermediary — founded the town in 1887. Fullerton grew as an agricultural centre, becoming a major producer of Valencia oranges and walnuts, and later of avocados. Oil was discovered in the surrounding hills, adding petroleum to the local economy.

Geography

Fullerton lies about 25 miles (40 km) southeast of downtown Los Angeles and borders Anaheim to the south, La Habra and Brea to the north, and Placentia to the east. The terrain ranges from flat in the south to the Coyote Hills in the north. The city has a Mediterranean climate with warm, dry summers and mild winters.

Education

Fullerton is home to California State University, Fullerton (CSUF), one of the largest campuses in the California State University system, and to Fullerton College, founded in 1913 and among the oldest community colleges in California. Hope International University is also located in the city.

Culture

Leo Fender founded the Fender Musical Instruments company in Fullerton, and the city is often called the birthplace of the solid-body electric guitar. The Muckenthaler Cultural Center, the Fullerton Museum Center, and a large collection of historic buildings — including the 1920s Fox Theatre — anchor the downtown arts scene. Fullerton Arboretum, on the CSUF campus, is a 26-acre botanical garden.

Transportation

The Fullerton Transportation Center serves Amtrak's Pacific Surfliner and Southwest Chief and Metrolink commuter trains. Interstate 5 and State Routes 57 and 91 pass through or near the city.
