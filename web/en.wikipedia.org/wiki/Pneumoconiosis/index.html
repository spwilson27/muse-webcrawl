Pneumoconiosis

Pneumoconiosis is the general term for a class of interstitial lung diseases caused by inhalation of dust. The dust is often encountered in the workplace; hence pneumoconiosis is considered an occupational lung disease.

Types

Depending on the type of dust, pneumoconioses include asbestosis (from asbestos), silicosis (from silica dust), and coal workers' pneumoconiosis, also known as black lung disease (from coal dust). Other forms include siderosis from iron, byssinosis from cotton dust, and berylliosis from beryllium.

Signs and symptoms

Symptoms include chronic cough, shortness of breath, and progressively worsening lung function as fibrosis develops. There is no cure; prevention focuses on limiting dust exposure through ventilation, respirators and workplace regulation, and management is supportive.
