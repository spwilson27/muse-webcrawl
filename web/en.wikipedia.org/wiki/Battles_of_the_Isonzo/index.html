Battles of the Isonzo
Date: June 1915 – October 1917
Location: Isonzo River, Austria-Hungary/Italy
Part of: First World War

The Battles of the Isonzo were a series of twelve battles between Italy and Austria-Hungary along the Isonzo River on the Italian Front of the First World War, fought between June 1915 and October 1917.

Course

Italian commander Luigi Cadorna launched eleven offensives against Austro-Hungarian positions in difficult mountainous and karst terrain, gaining little ground at enormous cost in lives.

Twelfth battle

The twelfth battle, known as Caporetto, saw a German-Austro-Hungarian offensive in October 1917 break the Italian line and drive Italy back to the Piave River.

Legacy

The Isonzo battles consumed hundreds of thousands of soldiers and remain central to Italian and Slovenian memory of the war; the river is now the Soča in Slovenia.
