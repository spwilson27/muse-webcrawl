Solaris Bus & Coach

A Solaris Urbino city bus

Solaris Bus & Coach S.A. is a Polish manufacturer of buses, trolleybuses, and trams based in Bolechowo near Poznań. Founded in 1996 by Krzysztof Olszewski, the company grew from a small local producer into one of Europe's largest bus manufacturers and a market leader in electric buses. Since 2018 it has been owned by the Spanish rolling-stock group CAF (Construcciones y Auxiliar de Ferrocarriles).

History

Olszewski, who had worked at Neoplan, founded the company (originally Neoplan Polska) in the 1990s, producing licensed low-floor city buses. The Solaris brand debuted in 1999 with the Urbino family of city buses; the company's green dachshund mascot became widely recognized. Production expanded to Bolechowo's large plant in 1996–2000s, and exports spread across Europe. In 2018 the Olszewski family sold the company to CAF for roughly €300 million, with the Polish PFR fund briefly holding a minority stake.

Products

The flagship Solaris Urbino city bus has gone through several generations (the fourth launched in 2014) in lengths from about 9 to 24 metres, including hybrid, CNG, hydrogen fuel-cell (Urbino 12 hydrogen), and battery-electric versions; the Urbino 12 electric won the Bus of the Year 2017 title. The Trollino trolleybus range and the Tramino tram range round out the portfolio, and Solaris supplies vehicles to operators in hundreds of European cities.
