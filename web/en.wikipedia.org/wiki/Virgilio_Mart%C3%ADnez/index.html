Virgilio Martínez Véliz (born 31 August 1977) is a Peruvian chef and restaurateur. He is the founder and head chef of Central in Lima, which was ranked No. 1 on The World's 50 Best Restaurants list in 2023 — the first South American restaurant to hold the top position.

Career

Martínez trained at Le Cordon Bleu in Ottawa and London and worked in restaurants in New York, Madrid and Bangkok before returning to Peru. After serving as executive chef at Astrid & Gastón in Lima and Madrid, he opened Central in 2008 in Lima's Miraflores district; the restaurant later moved to the Barranco district, where it shares a site with Kjolle, run by his wife Pía León, and with the research organisation Mater Iniciativa.

Philosophy

Central's tasting menus are organised around Peru's ecosystems and altitudes: each course is based on ingredients found at a specific elevation, from below sea level on the Pacific coast to high Andean plateaus above 4,000 metres. Through Mater Iniciativa, founded with his sister Malena Martínez, he documents and researches indigenous ingredients and works with local communities across Peru.

Martínez also founded Mil, a restaurant and research centre near the Moray archaeological site in the Sacred Valley of the Incas, and has written the books Central (2016) and The Latin America Cookbook (2021). He appeared in the Netflix documentary series Chef's Table.
