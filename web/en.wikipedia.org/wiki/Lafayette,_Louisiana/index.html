Lafayette, Louisiana

Lafayette (French: [lafajɛt]) is the fourth-most populous city in the U.S. state of Louisiana and the parish seat of Lafayette Parish, with a population of about 121,000 at the 2020 census; its consolidated city-parish government, created in 1996, covers some 244,000 residents. Located along the Vermilion River in the southwestern part of the state, in the heart of Acadiana, Lafayette is widely regarded as the centre of Cajun and Louisiana Creole culture.

History

The area was originally inhabited by Attakapa and other Native American peoples, and later settled by Acadian exiles expelled from Nova Scotia after 1755. Jean Mouton founded the settlement of Vermilionville in 1821; it grew into a farming and trading centre on the bayou country crossroads, earning Lafayette the nickname "Hub City". The town was renamed for the Marquis de Lafayette in 1884. The 20th-century discovery of oil and gas in south Louisiana transformed it into a service centre for the Gulf of Mexico energy industry, with rapid suburban growth; the 2014–16 oil downturn hit the local economy hard, and recent decades have seen diversification into healthcare, technology and a municipal fibre-optic utility (LUS Fiber).

Culture

Lafayette is celebrated for Cajun and Creole food (gumbo, jambalaya, boudin, crawfish), zydeco and Cajun music, and one of the largest free festivals in the United States, Festival International de Louisiane, held each April, alongside Festivals Acadiens et Créoles in October. Heritage sites include Vermilionville, a living-history Acadian village, and the Acadian Village museum. The city is home to the University of Louisiana at Lafayette, the largest campus of the University of Louisiana System by enrolment.

Geography and climate

The city lies on the West Gulf Coastal Plain at about 11 m (36 ft) elevation; the Vermilion River runs through it. Its humid subtropical climate brings long, hot, humid summers and mild winters, and it is exposed to hurricanes from the Gulf, most recently major damage from Hurricane Delta in 2020.
