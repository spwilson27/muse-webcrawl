BMW M5

BMW M5
Manufacturer: BMW M
Production: 1984–present
Class: Executive sports saloon
Layout: Front-engine, rear- or all-wheel drive
Related: BMW 5 Series

The BMW M5 is a high-performance variant of the BMW 5 Series executive car developed by BMW's motorsport division, BMW M GmbH. Introduced in 1984 with the hand-built E28 generation using the M88 straight-six from the M1, it is widely credited with creating the super-saloon formula: supercar performance in a discreet four-door body.

Generations

Generations include the E28 (1984–1988), E34 (1988–1995), E39 (1998–2003, V8), E60 (2005–2010, V10), F10 (2011–2016, twin-turbo V8), F90 (2017–2023, all-wheel drive) and the G90 from 2024, which uses a plug-in hybrid V8 powertrain and was offered for the first time as a Touring estate in many markets.

Performance

Output has risen from 286 PS in the E28 to over 700 hp in the hybrid G90. See BMW 5 Series and BMW M.
