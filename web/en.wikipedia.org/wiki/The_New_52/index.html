The New 52

Publisher: DC Comics
Launched: September 2011
Ended: 2016 (superseded by DC Rebirth)
Preceded by: Flashpoint

The New 52 was a 2011 revamp and relaunch by DC Comics of its entire line of ongoing monthly superhero books. Following the conclusion of the Flashpoint crossover storyline, DC cancelled all its existing titles and debuted 52 new series in September 2011, with renumbered first issues for flagship titles such as Action Comics and Detective Comics.

Overview

The relaunch established a streamlined continuity in which most heroes were younger and their histories compressed into roughly five years. Superman, Batman, Wonder Woman, the Flash, Green Lantern, and Aquaman anchored the line, while characters from the WildStorm and Vertigo imprints were folded into the main universe. Launch titles included Justice League by Geoff Johns and Jim Lee, which set sales records.

Reception and legacy

The New 52 initially boosted sales across the industry and attracted new readers, but creative turnover, editorial disputes, and controversial changes to characters drew criticism over time. In 2016 DC launched DC Rebirth, which restored many pre-2011 elements while retaining the New 52's accessibility goals. Elements of the era persist in modern DC continuity.
