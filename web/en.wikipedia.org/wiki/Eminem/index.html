Eminem

Eminem
Born: Marshall Bruce Mathers III, 17 October 1972, St. Joseph, Missouri, U.S.
Also known as: Slim Shady, M&M
Occupations: Rapper, songwriter, record producer, actor
Years active: 1988–present

Marshall Bruce Mathers III (born October 17, 1972), known professionally as Eminem, is an American rapper, songwriter, and record producer. He is credited with popularising hip hop in Middle America and is regarded as one of the greatest rappers of all time. His global success and acclaimed works are credited with breaking racial barriers for the acceptance of white rappers in popular music. He is also known for the transgressive alter ego Slim Shady and for transgressive work during the late 1990s and early 2000s that made him highly controversial.

Life and career

Mathers was born in St. Joseph, Missouri, and raised largely in Detroit, Michigan, in unstable circumstances, moving frequently and living in public housing. He dropped out of high school and worked odd jobs while pursuing rap, performing in local clubs and battle competitions and releasing the independent debut album Infinite (1996) to little success. After placing second in the 1997 Rap Olympics in Los Angeles, he came to the attention of producer Dr. Dre, who signed him to Aftermath Entertainment.

His major-label debut, The Slim Shady LP (1999), won the Grammy Award for Best Rap Album. The Marshall Mathers LP (2000) became the fastest-selling rap album in history at the time, driven by singles such as "Stan" and "The Real Slim Shady", and The Eminem Show (2002) was the best-selling album worldwide that year. He starred in the semi-autobiographical film 8 Mile (2002), whose song "Lose Yourself" won the Academy Award for Best Original Song.

After Encore (2004) and a hiatus marked by prescription-drug addiction, he returned with Relapse (2009) and Recovery (2010), the latter the best-selling album worldwide in its year, featuring the hits "Not Afraid" and "Love the Way You Lie". Subsequent albums include The Marshall Mathers LP 2 (2013), Revival (2017), Kamikaze (2018), Music to Be Murdered By (2020), and The Death of Slim Shady (Coup de Grâce) (2024). He also founded Shady Records, which launched artists such as 50 Cent and D12.

Artistry and legacy

Eminem is noted for his technical skill — intricate rhyme schemes, multisyllabic rhyming, rapid-fire delivery, and narrative storytelling — as well as for shifting between personas and tones, from comic shock humour to confessional material about poverty, family, addiction, and fame. He has sold more than 220 million records worldwide, making him one of the best-selling music artists of all time, and was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2022. Among his many awards are 15 Grammy Awards, an Academy Award, and numerous American Music and Billboard Music Awards.
