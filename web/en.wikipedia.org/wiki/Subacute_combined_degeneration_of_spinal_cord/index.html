Subacute combined degeneration of spinal cord

Subacute combined degeneration of spinal cord, also known as Lichtheim's disease or Putnam-Dana syndrome, is a degeneration of the posterior and lateral columns of the spinal cord as a result of vitamin B12 deficiency, most commonly associated with pernicious anemia. It is usually associated with, and may precede, megaloblastic anemia, and can also occur in copper deficiency.

Signs and symptoms

The condition develops gradually and progresses relatively uniformly. Damage to the posterior columns causes loss of vibration sense and proprioception, leading to sensory ataxia and a positive Romberg's sign, while damage to the lateral corticospinal tracts causes spasticity, weakness, and hyperreflexia. Patients commonly report symmetrical tingling, numbness, and weakness beginning in the hands and feet, with pins-and-needles sensations, unsteadiness of gait, and, in some cases, cognitive changes, visual disturbance (optic atrophy), and bladder or bowel dysfunction.

Causes

The principal cause is deficiency of vitamin B12 (cobalamin), required for the maintenance of myelin. Causes of deficiency include pernicious anemia (autoimmune destruction of gastric parietal cells or intrinsic factor), malabsorption after gastric or ileal surgery, strict vegan diets without supplementation, and prolonged use of certain medications. Nitrous oxide abuse can inactivate B12 and produce an identical syndrome. Copper deficiency, often after bariatric surgery or excessive zinc intake, is an important mimic.

Diagnosis and treatment

Diagnosis rests on the clinical picture together with low serum B12 (or elevated methylmalonic acid and homocysteine levels), macrocytic anemia, and MRI findings showing T2 hyperintensity in the posterior columns of the cervical and thoracic cord, sometimes described as an "inverted V" sign. Treatment is prompt replacement of vitamin B12, typically by intramuscular injection, which halts progression; established neurological damage may recover only partially, especially if treatment is delayed.
