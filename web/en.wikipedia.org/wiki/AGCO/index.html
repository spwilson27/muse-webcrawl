AGCO Corporation

Founded: 1990

Headquarters: Duluth, Georgia, United States

Industry: Agricultural machinery

Brands: Massey Ferguson, Fendt, Valtra, Challenger, Gleaner

Products: Tractors, combine harvesters, hay tools, sprayers, grain storage and protein production systems

AGCO Corporation is an American agricultural machinery manufacturer headquartered in Duluth, Georgia. Founded in 1990, it is one of the world's largest makers of farm equipment, selling products in more than 140 countries through a network of several thousand dealers. Its name derives from the Allis-Gleaner Corporation.

History

AGCO grew out of the management buyout of Deutz-Allis in 1990, built around the Gleaner combine harvester business and the Allis-Chalmers tractor heritage. Under chairman Robert J. Ratliff, the company expanded through a rapid series of acquisitions: it purchased Massey Ferguson in 1994, the German premium tractor maker Fendt in 1997, Challenger (from Caterpillar) in 2002, and Finland's Valtra in 2004, assembling a portfolio of established regional brands.

Products and business

AGCO's core brands are Massey Ferguson (global volume brand), Fendt (premium, technology-led), Valtra (Nordic), and Challenger (track tractors and application equipment, primarily North America), along with Gleaner combines, Fella and other forage brands, and grain handling and protein production systems sold under GSI. In 2024 AGCO sold a majority stake in its grain and protein business and formed a precision-agriculture joint venture with Trimble, reflecting a strategy focused on tractors, combines, and smart-farming technology.
