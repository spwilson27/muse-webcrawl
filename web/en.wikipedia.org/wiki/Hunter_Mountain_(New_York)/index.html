Hunter Mountain (New York)

Hunter Mountain is a peak in the Catskill Mountains of southeastern New York, in the town of Hunter, Greene County. At 4,040 feet (1,231 m), it is the second-highest peak in the Catskills, after Slide Mountain. Its summit is occupied by a fire tower and the Hunter Mountain ski area operates on its northern slopes.

Geography

Hunter Mountain rises between the Schoharie Creek valley to the west and the Stony Clove Notch to the east. A long summit ridge connects it to neighbouring peaks; the Devil's Path hiking trail traverses the eastern portion of the Catskill range near the mountain. The summit fire tower, at 4,040 feet the highest fire tower in New York State, was built in 1917 and is listed on the National Register of Historic Places; the surrounding forest is part of the Catskill Park Forest Preserve.

Ski area

The Hunter Mountain ski resort opened in 1959 under the Slutzky brothers, local construction contractors who developed the trails. It is one of the closest major ski areas to New York City and is known for extensive snowmaking. The resort was acquired by Peak Resorts in 2015 and subsequently became part of Vail Resorts in 2019. Expansion onto the mountain's west side (Hunter North and Hunter West) added new terrain in the 2010s.
