List of monarchies

This is a list of monarchies: sovereign states and subnational entities in which a monarch serves as head of state. A monarchy is a form of government in which a person, the monarch, reigns as head of state, usually for life or until abdication, and typically inherits the position. As of the 21st century there are 43 sovereign states with a monarch as head of state, of which several share the same person as monarch.

Types of monarchy

Monarchies are commonly classified as absolute, in which the monarch exercises sovereign power, or constitutional, in which the monarch's powers are limited by a constitution and an elected parliament typically governs. Some monarchies are hereditary, others elective, as in Vatican City, Cambodia, and Malaysia. Subnational monarchies also exist, such as the emirates of the United Arab Emirates and traditional kingdoms within republics.
