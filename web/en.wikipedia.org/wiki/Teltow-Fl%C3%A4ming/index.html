Teltow-Fläming

Teltow-Fläming is a district (Landkreis) in the southwestern part of Brandenburg, Germany, bordering Berlin to the north. Its capital is Luckenwalde. The district was created in 1993 by merging the former districts of Luckenwalde, Jüterbog and Zossen. It takes its name from the Teltow plateau and the Fläming hills.

Geography

The north lies within Berlin's commuter belt and includes towns such as Ludwigsfelde, Teltow-adjacent suburbs, Blankenfelde-Mahlow and Rangsdorf; Berlin Brandenburg Airport lies just outside its northeastern edge. The rural south, around Jüterbog and Dahme, is part of the Fläming heath and is crossed by the Flaeming-Skate inline-skating circuit.
