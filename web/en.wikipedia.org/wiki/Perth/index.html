Perth

Perth is the capital and largest city of Western Australia, with about 2.2 million residents — most of the state's population. It lies on the Swan River (Derbarl Yerrigan) on the Indian Ocean coast and is one of the world's most isolated major cities: Adelaide, the nearest city of comparable size, is 2,100 kilometres away.

Perth was founded in 1829 as the Swan River Colony. Its modern prosperity rests on Western Australia's mining boom (iron ore, gold, LNG). The city is noted for sunshine (more than any other Australian capital), Kings Park — one of the world's largest inner-city parks — Cottesloe and Scarborough beaches, and the port of Fremantle. Rottnest Island offshore is home to the quokka. Perth's sprawl makes it one of the longest cities in the world.
