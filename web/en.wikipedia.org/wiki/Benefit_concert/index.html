Benefit concert

A benefit concert or charity concert is a musical performance held to raise funds or awareness for a charitable cause, disaster relief, medical aid, or a social or political campaign.

History

Notable examples include George Harrison's Concert for Bangladesh (1971), Live Aid (1985) for Ethiopian famine relief, and many telethons and disaster-relief concerts that followed.

Organisation

Artists often perform without fees, venues and promoters may donate services, and proceeds are distributed by organising charities. Modern benefits also use livestreaming and text donations.
