List of airports in Vietnam

This is a list of airports in Vietnam, grouped by type and sorted by location. Vietnam has more than twenty civil airports with scheduled service, operated under the oversight of the Civil Aviation Authority of Vietnam, with most managed by the Airports Corporation of Vietnam (ACV). The busiest are Tan Son Nhat (Ho Chi Minh City), Noi Bai (Hanoi), and Da Nang.

Tan Son Nhat International Airport in Ho Chi Minh City is Vietnam's busiest.

Future

Long Thanh International Airport, under construction in Dong Nai Province, is planned to become the main gateway for Ho Chi Minh City, with an initial capacity of about 25 million passengers per year.
