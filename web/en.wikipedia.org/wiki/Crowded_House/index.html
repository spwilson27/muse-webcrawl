Crowded House

OriginMelbourne, Victoria, Australia
GenresRock, pop rock, alternative rock
Years active1985–1996, 2006–present
Key membersNeil Finn, Nick Seymour, Mark Hart, Matt Sherrod

Crowded House are a rock band formed in Melbourne, Australia, in 1985 by New Zealand singer-songwriter Neil Finn after the breakup of his previous band Split Enz. They achieved international success with singles including "Don't Dream It's Over" (1986) and "Weather with You" (1992).

History

The band's self-titled debut album (1986) produced the hit "Don't Dream It's Over", which reached number two in the United States. Subsequent albums Temple of Low Men (1988), Woodface (1991) — which featured Finn's brother Tim Finn — and Together Alone (1993) consolidated their reputation, particularly in the United Kingdom, Australia and New Zealand.

Crowded House disbanded in 1996 after a farewell concert on the steps of the Sydney Opera House attended by an estimated 100,000 or more people. Drummer Paul Hester died in 2005. The band reformed in 2006 with drummer Matt Sherrod and released further albums including Time on Earth (2007), Intriguer (2010), Dreamers Are Waiting (2021) and Gravity Stairs (2024).
