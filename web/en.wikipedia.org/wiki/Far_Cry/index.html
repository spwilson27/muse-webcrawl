Far Cry

Genre: First-person shooter
Developers: Crytek (2004); Ubisoft Montreal and others
Publisher: Ubisoft
First release: Far Cry (23 March 2004)

Far Cry is a franchise of first-person shooter games published by Ubisoft. The series is known for its open world environments, typically exotic or frontier settings, in which the player confronts a powerful antagonist who has seized control of the region. Storylines between the main entries are largely independent, linked mainly by recurring themes, gameplay systems, and the occasional returning character.

History

The original Far Cry (2004) was developed by the German studio Crytek and showcased the CryEngine's large outdoor environments and advanced artificial intelligence; it followed ex-special forces operative Jack Carver on a fictional Micronesian archipelago. Ubisoft acquired the rights to the series and the engine, and subsequent development moved in-house. Far Cry 2 (2008), set in a war-torn African country, introduced a dynamic fire-propagation system and a malaria mechanic. Far Cry 3 (2012), set on the tropical Rook Islands, was a major critical and commercial success and established the series' modern formula of open-world exploration, outpost liberation, hunting and crafting, and a charismatic villain — in this case the pirate Vaas Montenegro.

Main series

Later entries include Far Cry 4 (2014, the Himalayan country of Kyrat), Far Cry Primal (2016, a Stone Age setting), Far Cry 5 (2018, a doomsday cult in Montana), Far Cry New Dawn (2019), and Far Cry 6 (2021, the fictional Caribbean island of Yara, ruled by dictator Antón Castillo, played by Giancarlo Esposito). The franchise has sold tens of millions of copies worldwide.

Gameplay

Players explore a large map on foot and in vehicles, complete story and side missions, liberate enemy outposts, hunt wildlife for crafting materials, and climb towers or otherwise reconnoitre to reveal the map — a structure that strongly influenced later Ubisoft open-world design.
