Kennedy half dollar

The Kennedy half dollar is a fifty-cent coin of the United States first minted in 1964. It was issued as a memorial to President John F. Kennedy following his assassination in November 1963, and features his portrait on the obverse.

Design and composition

The obverse portrait was adapted from Kennedy's presidential medal by Gilroy Roberts, and the reverse, by Frank Gasparro, shows the presidential seal. Coins of 1964 were 90% silver; from 1965 to 1970 the composition was 40% silver-clad, and since 1971 the coin has been copper-nickel clad.

Usage

The half dollar saw hoarding from its first year and circulates only rarely, though it continues to be struck for collectors and in limited quantities for circulation.
