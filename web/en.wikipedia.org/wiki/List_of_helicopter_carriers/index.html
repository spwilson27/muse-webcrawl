List of helicopter carriers

A helicopter carrier is a type of aircraft carrier whose primary purpose is to operate helicopters. Helicopter carriers have been used for anti-submarine warfare and amphibious warfare. This article lists helicopter carriers by country and class.

Helicopter carriers operate rotary-wing aircraft for ASW and amphibious assault.
