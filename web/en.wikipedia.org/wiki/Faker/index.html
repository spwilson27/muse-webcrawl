Lee Sang-hyeok (Korean: 이상혁; born May 7, 1996), better known as Faker, is a South Korean professional League of Legends player for T1. He is widely regarded as the greatest League of Legends player of all time, and his longevity at the top of professional play has made him the most famous figure in esports. Originally a solo queue prodigy under the name GoJeonPa, he was recruited by SK Telecom T1 in 2013, winning the World Championship in his rookie year.

Career

Faker debuted professionally in April 2013 with a solo kill against Kang "Ambition" Chan-yong in his first game, immediately establishing his reputation. With SK Telecom T1 K he won the 2013 World Championship in Los Angeles, earning tournament MVP recognition. After a disappointing 2014 season in which the team failed to qualify for Worlds, the rebuilt roster returned to win back-to-back World Championships in 2015 and 2016 — the latter in a five-game final against Samsung Galaxy widely considered among the best series in the game's history — along with two Mid-Season Invitational titles in 2016 and 2017.

Renowned for an exceptionally deep champion pool and calm, precise teamfighting, Faker became famous for signature plays such as his 2013 Zed duel against Ryu "Ryu" Sang-wook, and for champions including LeBlanc, Ryze, Orianna, Azir, and Ahri — the last commemorated with a Hall of Legends skin in 2024, when Faker became the first inductee into Riot Games' Hall of Legends.

Later career

After several lean years and roster changes around the rebranded T1, Faker captained a young roster to the 2022 World final (a narrow loss to DRX) and then to consecutive World Championship titles in 2023, in Seoul, and 2024, in London, defeating Bilibili Gaming in a five-game final — giving him a record five world titles. He also won a gold medal with South Korea in the League of Legends demonstration event at the 2018 Asian Games and the esports title at the 2022 Asian Games, the latter earning military service exemption. In 2020 he became a part-owner of T1. Faker reached 1,000 career LCK games and record numbers of kills and wins in Korea's league, and his popularity has drawn sponsorships and mainstream recognition in South Korea rare for an esports player.

Playing style and legacy

Commentators praise Faker's mechanics, game sense, and adaptability across a decade of patches and metas; his practice discipline and reserved public persona earned him the nickname "the Unkillable Demon King." He is the only player to win five World Championships and is generally treated as the sport's defining figure, comparable in stature to the greatest athletes in traditional sports.
