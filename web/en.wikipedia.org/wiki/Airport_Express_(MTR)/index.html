The Airport Express (Chinese: 機場快綫) is one of the ten railway lines of the MTR system in Hong Kong. It links the Hong Kong International Airport and the AsiaWorld-Expo exhibition centre with Hong Kong station in Central, in the heart of the city. It is the only rail link to the airport and shares much of its track with the Tung Chung line.

The line is shown in teal on MTR route maps. The journey between Hong Kong station and the airport takes about 24 minutes, making it one of the fastest airport rail links in the world when it opened in 1998 alongside the new airport at Chek Lap Kok.

History

The Airport Express was built as part of the Airport Core Programme, the group of ten infrastructure projects supporting the new Hong Kong International Airport. Construction began in 1994; the route crosses the harbour, follows the north shore of Lantau Island across the Tsing Ma and Kap Shui Mun bridges, and was substantially complete by 1997. Services began on 6 July 1998, the same day the airport opened. An extension to AsiaWorld-Expo station opened on 20 December 2005.

Stations

  • Hong Kong — Central business district terminus, with in-town check-in facilities.
  • Kowloon — serving West Kowloon and the Elements/ICC development.
  • Tsing Yi — interchange with the Tung Chung line.
  • Airport — serving Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 of Hong Kong International Airport.
  • AsiaWorld-Expo — serving the exhibition and concert venue of the same name.

Features

The Airport Express pioneered in-town check-in: passengers at Hong Kong and Kowloon stations could check in baggage for many airlines before travelling to the airport, a service widely imitated since. Trains are dedicated Adtranz-CAF units with luggage racks, wide seats and flight information displays. The line runs at up to 135 km/h and for much of its length shares tracks with Tung Chung line trains, diverging at the airport branch near Tung Chung.
