Overview

The Omar Ali Saifuddien Mosque is a mosque in Bandar Seri Begawan, the capital of Brunei, considered one of the most beautiful in Southeast Asia. Completed in 1958 and named after the 28th Sultan of Brunei, Omar Ali Saifuddien III (the present Sultan's father), it is built in Mughal and Italian styles in marble, with a golden main dome, and stands in an artificial lagoon on the Brunei River near Kampong Ayer.

Features

Its minaret (about 52 m) is the tallest structure in central Bandar Seri Begawan — heights nearby have historically been restricted so as not to exceed it. A replica 16th-century royal barge appears to float beside the mosque, used for Quran-reading ceremonies. The mosque symbolises Brunei's Malay Islamic Monarchy ideology and appears on Bruneian currency.
