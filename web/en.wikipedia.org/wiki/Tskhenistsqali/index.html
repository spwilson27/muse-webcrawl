Tskhenistsqali

The Tskhenistsqali (Georgian: ცხენისწყალი, "horse river") is a river in western Georgia and one of the principal right-bank tributaries of the Rioni. It rises on the southern slopes of the Greater Caucasus in the Lentekhi area of Lower Svaneti and flows southwestward through the historical provinces of Lechkhumi and Samegrelo before joining the Rioni. The river is about 160–180 kilometres long.

Course

From its mountain headwaters the Tskhenistsqali passes the towns of Lentekhi and Tsageri, cuts gorges through limestone country, and emerges onto the Colchis lowland, where its waters are used for irrigation. The river's name — "horse's water" — is a common Georgian hydronym also borne by other streams in the country.
