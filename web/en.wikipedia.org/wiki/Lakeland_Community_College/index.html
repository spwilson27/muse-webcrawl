Lakeland Community College

Lakeland Community College is a public community college in Kirtland, Ohio, United States. Founded in 1967, it was the first public community college in Ohio created by a vote of residents. It serves Lake County and surrounding areas of northeastern Ohio.

History

The college held its first classes in 1967 in borrowed facilities in Painesville before moving to its permanent campus in Kirtland, at the intersection of Interstate 90 and State Route 306.

Academics

Lakeland offers more than 140 associate degree and certificate programs, including applied business, engineering technologies, health technologies, and arts and sciences transfer pathways to four-year universities. Through its Holden University Center, students can complete bachelor's and graduate degrees from partner universities on Lakeland's campus.

Campus and athletics

The campus includes the Lakeland Civic Theatre and the Mooreland Mansion conference centre. The college's athletic teams, the Lakers, compete in the Ohio Community College Athletic Conference in sports such as basketball, soccer, baseball, softball, and volleyball.
