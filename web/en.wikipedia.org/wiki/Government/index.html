Government

Government
DefinitionSystem or group governing an organised community/state
FormsDemocracy, monarchy, republic, dictatorship, etc.
[Image: A parliament chamber in session, rows of representatives debating]

A government is the system by which a state or community is controlled: the institutions that make and enforce laws, provide public services, defend territory and conduct foreign policy. Governments are classed as democracies, monarchies, republics, oligarchies, dictatorships and more.

Functions

Modern governments tax, spend, regulate and run infrastructure from roads to satellite licensing; their scope is the core dispute of politics between capitalist and communist traditions and their critics. The word derives from the Greek for steering a ship — the metaphor still shapes words like 'governor'.
