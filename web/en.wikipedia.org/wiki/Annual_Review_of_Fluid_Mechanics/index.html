Annual Review of Fluid Mechanics

The Annual Review of Fluid Mechanics is a peer-reviewed scientific journal published by Annual Reviews, covering research in fluid mechanics. It was first published in 1969 and is among the most highly cited review journals in its field.

Scope

The journal publishes invited review articles surveying significant developments across fluid dynamics, including turbulence, aerodynamics, geophysical flows, multiphase flow, microfluidics, biological flows and computational fluid dynamics. Articles are written by leading specialists and are intended to be accessible to researchers entering an area.

Editorial history

The founding editor was William R. Sears; later editors included M. Van Dyke, John L. Lumley, Stephen H. Davis, and Parviz Moin. The journal consistently ranks near the top of its category by impact factor among journals in mechanics and fluids.
