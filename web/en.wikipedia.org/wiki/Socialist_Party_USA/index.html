Socialist Party USA


Founded: 1973
Headquarters: New York City, New York
Ideology: Democratic socialism, multi-tendency socialism
Political position: Left-wing
Colors: Red

The Socialist Party USA, officially the Socialist Party of the United States of America (SPUSA), is a socialist political party in the United States. It was established in 1973 as a successor to the Socialist Party of America, which had split a year earlier, resulting in another organization called Social Democrats, USA.

The party is officially committed to democratic socialism and is a multi-tendency organization. Along with its predecessor, the Socialist Party USA has received varying degrees of support when its candidates have competed against those from the Republican and Democratic parties. The SPUSA advocates for complete independence from the Democratic Party.

History

In 1972, the Socialist Party of America changed its name to Social Democrats, USA by a vote of 73 to 34 at its December convention. In 1973, members of the former Debs Caucus of the Socialist Party of America formed the Union for Democratic Socialism, which later that year became the Socialist Party USA. The party claims the legacy of early socialist leaders such as Eugene V. Debs and Norman Thomas.

Ideology

The Socialist Party USA describes itself as a democratic socialist, multi-tendency party. It supports social ownership of the means of production, workplace democracy, a democratically planned economy, universal healthcare, and the abolition of capitalism. The party's statement of principles emphasizes radical democracy, non-violence, and solidarity with oppressed peoples.

Presidential tickets

The party has run presidential candidates since 1976. Its tickets have included David McReynolds, Willa Kenoyer, Ron Daniels, Mary Cal Hollis, Eric Chester, Walt Brown, Brian Moore, Stewart Alexander, Alex Mendoza, Angela Nicole Walker, and Howie Hawkins (in 2020, jointly with the Green Party in some states). Candidates typically appear on a small number of state ballots.
