Vladislav Ardzinba

Vladislav Grigori-ipa Ardzinba (1945–2010) was an Abkhaz historian and politician who became the first President of the Republic of Abkhazia, serving from 1994 to 2005, and led the Abkhaz side in the 1992–1993 war with Georgia.

Career

A scholar of Hittitology who worked at the Institute of Oriental Studies in Moscow, Ardzinba returned to Abkhazia in 1988 and entered politics as tensions rose in the late Soviet period, becoming chairman of Abkhazia's Supreme Soviet in 1990. During the war he directed the Abkhaz war effort from Gudauta. After victory, he was elected president in 1994 and re-elected in 1999, presiding over a de facto independent but internationally unrecognised state under economic hardship.

Later life

Ill health led him to step down in 2005, succeeded by Sergei Bagapsh. Ardzinba died in Moscow in 2010 and is venerated in Abkhazia as a founding father.
