Jim Beam is an American brand of bourbon whiskey produced in Clermont, Kentucky, by Suntory Global Spirits. It is one of the best-selling brands of bourbon in the world. Since 1795 (with interruptions during Prohibition), seven generations of the Beam family have been involved in the whiskey business that bears the family name.

History

The brand traces its origins to Jacob Beam, who began selling barrels of corn whiskey around 1795 in Kentucky; his son David Beam and grandson David M. Beam expanded the business in the 19th century. James B. Beam (1864–1947), known as "Jim Beam," rebuilt the company after Prohibition ended in 1933, and the whiskey was renamed in his honor in 1943. The company passed through corporate owners including Fortune Brands and Beam Inc. before being acquired by Japan's Suntory Holdings in 2014.

Products

The flagship product is Jim Beam "white label," a Kentucky straight bourbon aged at least four years. The range also includes Jim Beam Black, Devil's Cut (which incorporates whiskey extracted from barrel wood), Rye, flavored variants, and the "Small Batch Bourbon Collection," which includes Booker's, Baker's, Basil Hayden's, and Knob Creek.
