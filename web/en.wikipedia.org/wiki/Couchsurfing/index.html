Couchsurfing

Couchsurfing International, founded 2004; headquarters San Francisco, California.

Couchsurfing is a hospitality exchange service and social networking website that allows members to request free accommodation ("surf") in other members' homes, offer their own homes to travellers ("host"), or meet for social activities. The concept derives from the practice of sleeping on spare couches while travelling cheaply.

History

The idea was conceived by American programmer Casey Fenton in 1999 after a trip to Iceland, where he emailed students at the University of Iceland seeking a place to stay and received many offers. Couchsurfing International was founded in 2004 by Fenton, Daniel Hoffer, Sebastian Le Tuan, and Leonardo Bassani da Silveira, building on the model of earlier hospitality networks such as Servas International and Hospitality Club.

The organization operated as a non-profit for its first years, relying on member donations and verification fees. In 2011 it converted to a for-profit benefit corporation and raised venture capital, a change that drew criticism from some members. In May 2020, during the COVID-19 pandemic, the platform introduced a mandatory subscription fee in many countries, prompting further controversy and migration of some users to alternative networks such as BeWelcome and Trustroots.

How it works

Members create profiles describing themselves and any accommodation they can offer. Surfers send personalized requests to prospective hosts; payment for lodging is prohibited, though guests often cook a meal, bring a gift, or otherwise reciprocate. After a stay, both parties may leave public references, which form the platform's trust and reputation system. Local communities also organize meetups, hikes, and language exchanges.

Criticism and safety

The service has faced criticism over safety incidents, the commercialization of a formerly volunteer-run gift economy, and changes to data and privacy policies. Supporters argue that the reference system and community norms make stays generally safe and culturally enriching.
