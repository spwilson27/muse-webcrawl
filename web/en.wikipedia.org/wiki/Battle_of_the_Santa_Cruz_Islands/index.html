The Battle of the Santa Cruz Islands, fought during 25–27 October 1942, was the fourth carrier battle of the Pacific War and was fought as part of the Guadalcanal campaign. It was the last major naval engagement of that campaign. Japanese carrier forces sought to support an army offensive to retake Henderson Field on Guadalcanal, while the US Navy attempted to intercept them.

The battle

On the morning of 26 October, scout aircraft from both sides found the opposing carriers almost simultaneously. In the ensuing exchange of strikes, the US carrier Hornet was fatally damaged by bombs and torpedoes and was later scuttled, and the carrier Enterprise was damaged; the destroyer Porter was also lost. On the Japanese side the fleet carrier Shōkaku and the light carrier Zuihō were heavily damaged and put out of action for months.

Aftermath

Although Japan won the tactical exchange, its losses in veteran carrier aircrew were severe and irreplaceable, and the damaged carriers could not support further offensives. The Japanese ground offensive on Guadalcanal failed, and the United States retained control of Henderson Field, securing the strategic initiative in the campaign.
