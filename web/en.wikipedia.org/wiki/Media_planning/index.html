Media planning

Media planning is the process of strategising and purchasing advertising placements in media — such as television, digital, radio, print and outdoor — in order to deliver a marketing message to a target audience at the right time, place and frequency for a given budget.

Process

Media planners analyse the target market, set objectives such as reach and frequency, select channels and vehicles, negotiate and buy space or impressions, and evaluate performance. Key measures include reach (the proportion of the audience exposed), frequency (average number of exposures), gross rating points and cost per thousand impressions (CPM).

Digital era

Programmatic buying, social media platforms and data-driven targeting have shifted planning from broad demographic buys toward audience-based planning, real-time optimisation and cross-device measurement, while traditional planning still guides major brand campaigns.
