Cover Corporation


Cover Corp., operator of Hololive Production

Cover Corporation (Japanese: カバー株式会社, Kabā Kabushiki-gaisha) is a Japanese technology and entertainment company founded in 2016 by Motoaki "Yagoo" Tanigo. The company is best known for operating Hololive Production, a virtual YouTuber (VTuber) talent agency whose performers stream using animated avatars driven by motion-capture technology.

History

Cover initially developed virtual and augmented reality software before pivoting to VTuber entertainment in 2017, debuting its first talent, Tokino Sora, in September of that year. The Hololive roster expanded rapidly from 2018, adding successive "generations" of Japanese talents, followed by branches in Indonesia (2020) and English-speaking markets (2020), as well as the male Holostars branch. Cover listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange Growth Market in March 2023.

Business

Cover's revenue derives from livestreaming and Super Chat donations, merchandise, music, concerts and other live events, and licensing of its characters. The company has staged large-scale concerts at venues such as the Tokyo Garden Theater and has collaborated with professional baseball teams, game publishers, and anime productions.
