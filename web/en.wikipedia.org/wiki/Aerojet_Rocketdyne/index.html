Industry: Aerospace and defence

Products: Rocket engines, propulsion systems
Parent: L3Harris Technologies (from 2023)

Aerojet Rocketdyne is an American manufacturer of rocket, hypersonic and electric propulsion systems for space, defence and commercial applications.

History

The company was formed in 2013 when GenCorp combined Aerojet with Pratt & Whitney Rocketdyne. Its product lines include the RS-25 engines that power NASA's Space Launch System, the RL10 upper-stage engine, and a range of satellite and missile propulsion systems. Rocketdyne's earlier engines powered the Saturn V and the Space Shuttle. In 2023 the company was acquired by L3Harris Technologies.

Products

Major products include liquid rocket engines, solid rocket motors, monopropellant and bipropellant thrusters, and electric propulsion systems for spacecraft.
