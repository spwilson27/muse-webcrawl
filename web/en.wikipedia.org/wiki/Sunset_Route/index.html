Sunset Route

The Sunset Route is a major railroad line in the southern United States, historically operated by the Southern Pacific Railroad and today part of the Union Pacific Railroad network. It runs from New Orleans, Louisiana, westward across Texas, New Mexico, and Arizona to Los Angeles, California.

History

The route was completed in the early 1880s when Southern Pacific's lines met near the Pecos River in Texas, giving the United States its second transcontinental railroad connection. It formed the western portion of the famous Sunset Limited passenger train route, which Amtrak continues to operate between New Orleans and Los Angeles.

Operations

The line is a heavily used freight corridor, carrying intermodal containers between the ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach and points east. Much of the route through Arizona and New Mexico has been double-tracked to increase capacity.
