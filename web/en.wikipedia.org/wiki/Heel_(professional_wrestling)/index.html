Heel (professional wrestling)

In professional wrestling, a heel (also known as a rudo in lucha libre) is a wrestler who portrays a villain, "bad guy" or rulebreaker, acting as an antagonist to the face (heroic) wrestlers. Heels are portrayed as behaving in an immoral manner — cheating, insulting the audience, attacking opponents unfairly — in order to provoke anger and jeers ("heat") from the crowd.

Role and techniques

Heels traditionally cheat to win, use foreign objects, exploit referees' blind spots and berate fans in interviews. Famous heels include Ric Flair, Roddy Piper and Ted DiBiase. A wrestler switching from face to heel is called a "heel turn". In some eras and promotions, popular heels have attracted cheers, blurring the traditional alignment.
