Colonna family

The Colonna family is an Italian noble family, forming part of the papal nobility. It was powerful in medieval and Renaissance Rome, supplying one pope (Martin V) and many other church and political leaders, and was a long-standing rival of the Orsini family.

History

The family traced its origins to counts of Tusculum and took its name from the town of Colonna near Rome. Cardinal Giovanni Colonna was a leading figure of the early 13th century. Sciarra Colonna famously participated in the assault on Pope Boniface VIII at Anagni in 1303. Oddone Colonna's election as Pope Martin V in 1417 ended the Western Schism. Marcantonio Colonna commanded the papal fleet at the Battle of Lepanto (1571), and the poet Vittoria Colonna was a friend of Michelangelo.

Palazzo Colonna

The family's Roman palace, Palazzo Colonna, built over centuries on the slopes of the Quirinal, houses a celebrated gallery of paintings and remains partly occupied by the family.
