CBC Radio One is the English-language news and current affairs radio network of the Canadian Broadcasting Corporation. It is a commercial-free public service consisting of a national feed with regional and local programming produced in more than 40 communities across Canada, and is also distributed internationally via satellite and online streaming.

Programming

Radio One's schedule combines national flagship programmes — including The Current, As It Happens, Ideas, Q, Cross Country Checkup and the morning and drive-time local shows in each market — with hourly national newscasts and regional news. Overnight, the network broadcasts a selection of programmes from international public broadcasters. Its French-language counterpart is Ici Radio-Canada Première, and CBC Music (formerly Radio Two) is its sister music network.

Distribution

The network is carried on FM in most major cities and on a mixture of AM and FM transmitters and low-power repeaters in smaller communities, reaching the vast majority of Canada's population. Radio One is consistently the most-listened-to public radio service in the country and a central institution of Canadian public broadcasting.
