Blackmagic Design Pty Ltd is an Australian manufacturer of digital cinema and broadcast video equipment and software, founded in 2001 by Grant Petty and based in Port Melbourne. The company is known for aggressively lowering the price of professional film and television tools, from capture cards and production switchers to cinema cameras and colour-grading software.

History

Blackmagic's first product, the DeckLink capture card (2002), brought uncompressed video editing to standard computers at a fraction of previous prices. The company expanded into converters, routers, monitors, and live production switchers (the ATEM line). In 2009 it acquired DaVinci Systems and its colour-grading software, relaunching it as DaVinci Resolve — later expanded into a full editing, visual-effects, and audio suite offered in free and Studio versions. In 2012 it entered camera manufacture with the Blackmagic Cinema Camera, followed by the popular Pocket Cinema Camera series and the URSA line of professional digital film cameras. It acquired Fairlight (audio) in 2016 and Eyeon Software (Fusion compositing) in 2014, integrating both into Resolve.

Products

Major product lines include DaVinci Resolve and Fusion software; URSA Mini Pro and Pocket Cinema Camera 4K/6K cameras; ATEM Mini and Constellation live production switchers; HyperDeck recorders; UltraStudio and DeckLink capture and playback devices; and the Blackmagic Cloud collaboration services. Blackmagic cameras record in the company's Blackmagic RAW format, and its products are widely used in independent film, streaming, and broadcast production.
