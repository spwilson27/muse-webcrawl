The American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics (AIAA) is the world's largest aerospace professional society, representing engineers, scientists and students in aeronautics and astronautics. Its motto is "Shaping the future of aerospace". AIAA publishes technical journals and books, organizes conferences, develops engineering standards, and advocates for the aerospace profession; its members work across fields such as aerodynamics, structures, propulsion, guidance and space systems.

History

AIAA was formed in 1963 by the merger of two older organizations: the Institute of the Aerospace Sciences (IAS, founded 1932 as the Institute of the Aeronautical Sciences by aviation pioneers including Theodore von Kármán) and the American Rocket Society (ARS, founded 1930 as the American Interplanetary Society, whose members included Robert H. Goddard's circle). The merged institute consolidated the professions of aeronautics and astronautics at the height of the Space Race.

Activities

AIAA publishes Aerospace America magazine and peer-reviewed journals including the AIAA Journal, the Journal of Aircraft, the Journal of Spacecraft and Rockets and the Journal of Guidance, Control, and Dynamics. Its events include AIAA SciTech Forum, the largest aerospace research and development conference in the world, and the AVIATION and ASCEND conferences. Technical committees and standards cover subjects descending from the foundational work of pioneers such as George Cayley and Otto Lilienthal. The AIAA Foundation supports student design competitions — notably Design/Build/Fly — scholarships and STEM outreach. The society maintains an extensive history programme, including the Historic Aerospace Sites designation honouring locations such as Kitty Hawk and the major wind-tunnel and launch complexes.

Membership

Membership grades range from student member through member and associate fellow to fellow and honorary fellow. The society's highest honours include the Daniel Guggenheim Medal (awarded jointly with other societies) and the Reed Aeronautics Award.
