Vocal score


A page from a vocal score

A vocal score or piano–vocal score is a music score that presents the vocal parts of an opera, musical, oratorio or similar work on their own staves, together with a reduction of the orchestral accompaniment arranged for piano. Such scores allow singers and rehearsal pianists to learn and rehearse a work without a full orchestra.

Contents

Unlike a full score, which shows every instrumental line, a vocal score compresses the orchestral texture into two piano staves while printing all sung lines, lyrics, stage directions and cues for important instrumental entries.

Use

Vocal scores are used in auditions, rehearsals, choral preparation and amateur performance. Historically they were also the principal way operatic music reached domestic music-making in the nineteenth century, when arrangements for piano were a major part of publishers' catalogues.

Related forms

A choral score prints only the choral parts with the piano reduction, while a short score is a composer's own condensed draft of a work in progress.
