The 2022 Commonwealth Games, officially the XXII Commonwealth Games and commonly known as Birmingham 2022, was an international multi-sport event for members of the Commonwealth held in Birmingham, England, from 28 July to 8 August 2022. Birmingham stepped in as host after Durban, South Africa, was stripped of the Games in 2017.

Events

The programme featured 20 sports, including athletics at the redeveloped Alexander Stadium, aquatics at the new Sandwell Aquatics Centre, and the debut of women's T20 cricket. For the first time at a major multi-sport event, there were more medal events for women than for men. Esports were held as a pilot event.

Medal table

Australia topped the medal table with 67 golds and 179 medals overall, followed by host nation England with 57 golds, and Canada in third. India finished fourth, boosted by success in wrestling, weightlifting and badminton.

Ceremonies

The opening ceremony at Alexander Stadium featured the mechanical "Raging Bull" from the city's industrial heritage, which afterwards went on public display. The Games were formally opened by Charles, Prince of Wales, reading a message from Queen Elizabeth II.
