Google Talk

[Image: Google Talk]

Google Talk was an instant messaging service developed by Google that provided both text and voice communication. Launched on 24 August 2005, it used the open XMPP protocol and was integrated into Gmail as a chat sidebar.

History

Google Talk supported voice calls through a Jingle-based protocol and interoperability with other XMPP services. A web-based version was embedded in Gmail, and clients were available for Windows and other platforms. In 2013 Google began migrating users to Google Hangouts, and the original Google Talk service for most platforms was discontinued; the Windows application was withdrawn in February 2015 and XMPP federation was wound down.

Features

The service offered one-to-one text chat, voice calling, voicemail, and file transfer, and chat histories were stored in users' Gmail accounts. It became the basis for later Google communication products.
