Pierce Manufacturing is an American manufacturer of custom fire rescue apparatus based in Appleton, Wisconsin. A wholly owned subsidiary of the Oshkosh Corporation, Pierce is one of the largest fire-apparatus builders in North America.

History

The company was founded in 1913 by Humphrey Pierce and his son Dudley as the Pierce Auto Body Works, initially building wooden truck bodies. It shifted to fire apparatus in the following decades and grew into a leading supplier of pumpers, aerials, tankers, and rescue vehicles. Oshkosh acquired Pierce in 1996.

Products

Pierce builds vehicles on its own custom chassis (such as the Dash, Enforcer, and Velocity) as well as commercial chassis, including pumper trucks, aerial ladders and platforms, water tenders, wildland units, and command vehicles, sold to municipal and airport fire departments.
