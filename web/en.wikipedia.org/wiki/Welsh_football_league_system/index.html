Welsh football league system

The Welsh football league system is a series of interconnected association football leagues in Wales administered by the Football Association of Wales (FAW) and regional associations. It provides promotion and relegation between divisions so that, in principle, any club can rise to the top flight.

Structure

The top division is the Cymru Premier (founded in 1992 as the League of Wales), with 12 clubs. Below it, the second tier is split geographically into the Cymru North and Cymru South. The third tier consists of the Ardal Leagues, and the fourth tier of area leagues run by the regional football associations (such as the Gwent, Mid Wales, North East Wales, North Wales Coast, South Wales, and West Wales associations). A restructuring implemented from the 2020–21 season standardized tiers 3 and 4.

Welsh clubs in England

Several of Wales's largest clubs—Cardiff City, Swansea City, Wrexham, and Newport County—play in the English football league system rather than the Welsh system, while retaining historic ties to Welsh cup competitions. The New Saints, based in Oswestry, England, play in the Cymru Premier. Welsh clubs in the English pyramid compete for European qualification through English competitions, whereas Cymru Premier clubs qualify for UEFA competitions through Wales.
