Porsche Macan


Porsche Macan (first generation)

The Porsche Macan is a compact luxury crossover SUV produced by German manufacturer Porsche since 2014. It is built in Leipzig, Germany, and shares its platform with the Audi Q5. The name Macan is derived from the Indonesian word for tiger. Positioned below the larger Cayenne, the Macan quickly became Porsche's best-selling model worldwide.

First generation (Type 95B; 2014)

The Macan was unveiled at the 2013 Los Angeles Auto Show and went on sale in 2014 with turbocharged petrol and diesel V6 engines and a standard seven-speed PDK dual-clutch transmission and all-wheel drive. Later variants included a 2.0-litre four-cylinder base model, the sporty Macan GTS, and the high-performance Macan Turbo (later Turbo GT and GTS trims after a 2019 facelift; a second facelift followed in 2021). Diesel versions were discontinued in 2018.

Electric Macan

An all-electric Macan, built on the Premium Platform Electric (PPE) architecture shared with Audi, was introduced in 2024 and is sold alongside the petrol model in many markets. It offers single- and dual-motor variants, an 800-volt electrical architecture for fast charging, and represents Porsche's shift toward electrification of its volume models.
