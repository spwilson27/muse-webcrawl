Atlantic City, sometimes abbreviated as AC, is a Jersey Shore seaside resort city in Atlantic County, in the state of New Jersey, United States. As of the 2020 United States census, the city's population was 38,497. The city is located on Absecon Island and is famed for its casinos, skyline, gambling, boardwalk and beaches.

History

Founded in 1854 as a health resort reached by rail from Philadelphia, Atlantic City built the first boardwalk in the United States in 1870. It boomed during Prohibition as a hub of nightlife, inspired the street names of the board game Monopoly, and hosted the Miss America pageant for most of its history.

Gambling era

Voters approved casino gambling in a 1976 referendum, and Resorts International opened the first legal casino in the eastern United States in 1978. Casino revenue peaked in 2006, then declined amid competition from neighbouring states; several casinos closed in 2014. A state takeover of city finances followed in 2016, and newer properties such as Hard Rock and Ocean Casino Resort opened in 2018.
