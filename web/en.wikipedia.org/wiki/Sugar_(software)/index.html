Sugar (software)

DeveloperSugar Labs
Written inPython
Operating systemLinux (Fedora-based)
TypeDesktop environment / graphical user interface
LicenseGNU GPL

Sugar is a free and open-source desktop environment designed specifically for children, originally developed as the user interface of the One Laptop per Child (OLPC) XO laptop. Since 2008, development has been coordinated by Sugar Labs, an independent non-profit spun off from OLPC. Sugar departs radically from the conventional desktop metaphor of files, folders, and windows, and is instead organised around "Activities", collaboration, and a journal that automatically records everything a child does.

Design

Sugar's interface was designed by the firm Pentagram working with OLPC, under the guidance of principles drawn from constructionist learning theory associated with Seymour Papert and Alan Kay. The home view shows a central figure representing the user surrounded by a ring of activities. There are no files or applications in the traditional sense: children launch full-screen activities, and work is saved automatically to the Journal, a chronological log that functions as the system's filing cabinet. A "neighborhood view" shows other XO users and shared activities as icons, encouraging ad hoc collaboration over the wireless mesh network.

Activities

Sugar ships with activities for writing (based on AbiWord), browsing (a Gecko-based browser), reading e-books, drawing (Paint), music composition (TamTam), programming (Pippy, Turtle Art, and Etoys), photography and video (Record), and chat. Many activities support real-time collaborative editing, in which several children can work on the same document or canvas simultaneously over the network.

Distribution

Although developed for the XO, Sugar has been ported to ordinary PCs as "Sugar on a Stick", a bootable USB image based on Fedora, and packaged for other Linux distributions. It has been deployed in large national programmes, notably in Uruguay's Plan Ceibal and in Peru, as well as in smaller school deployments worldwide.
