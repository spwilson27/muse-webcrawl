Jewish Liberation

Jewish Liberation is an online journal of Jewish left-wing and anti-Zionist writing, founded in 2023 in the aftermath of the October 7 attacks and the subsequent Gaza war.

Background and content

The journal publishes essays, interviews, and cultural criticism arguing for the liberation of Palestinians and Jews alike, and situates itself in a tradition reaching back to the Jewish Labor Bund, which advocated diasporic cultural autonomy ("doikayt", or "hereness") rather than Jewish nationalism. Contributors have included academics and activists critical of Zionism and of mainstream Jewish communal institutions.

Reception

The publication has been cited in coverage of the growing Jewish anti-Zionist movement in the United States and has drawn both support from left-wing Jewish organizations and criticism from those who regard its politics as outside the communal mainstream.
