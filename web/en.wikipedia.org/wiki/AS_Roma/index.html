AS Roma

Associazione Sportiva Roma (AS Roma) is a professional football club based in Rome, Italy. Founded in 1927 by a merger of three clubs, Roma have played in the top tier of Italian football for all but one season of their history. They have won Serie A three times (1941–42, 1982–83 and 2000–01), the Coppa Italia nine times, and the UEFA Europa Conference League in 2022. Roma share the Stadio Olimpico with city rivals Lazio, against whom they contest the Derby della Capitale.

History

Roma were formed in 1927 when Italo Foschi engineered the merger of Roman FC, SS Alba-Audace and Fortitudo-Pro Roma to create a club able to challenge the dominant northern teams. Roma won their first scudetto in 1942 under Alfréd Schaffer, a second in 1983 under Nils Liedholm with stars such as Bruno Conti and Paulo Roberto Falcão, and a third in 2001 under Fabio Capello, inspired by captain Francesco Totti, who spent his entire career at the club and is its record scorer. In Europe, Roma won the Inter-Cities Fairs Cup in 1961 and reached the European Cup final in 1984, losing on penalties to Liverpool. Under José Mourinho, Roma won the inaugural UEFA Europa Conference League in 2022.

Colours and crest

The club's colours are imperial purple-red with golden yellow trim, from which comes the nickname Giallorossi. The crest features the Capitoline Wolf suckling Romulus and Remus, symbols of the city of Rome.
