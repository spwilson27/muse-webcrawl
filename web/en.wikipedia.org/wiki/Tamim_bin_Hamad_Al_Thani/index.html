Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani

Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani (born 3 June 1980) is the Emir of Qatar, having acceded on 25 June 2013 when his father, Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani, abdicated. He is the fourth son of his father and was appointed heir apparent in 2003.

Early life and education

Tamim was educated at Sherborne School in Dorset and Harrow School, and graduated from the Royal Military Academy Sandhurst in 1998. He was commissioned in the Qatar Armed Forces and took on roles in sport administration, chairing the Qatar Olympic Committee and the organising efforts behind the 2006 Asian Games in Doha.

Reign

As emir he has presided over Qatar's hosting of the 2022 FIFA World Cup, continued investment through the Qatar Investment Authority, and the diplomatic crisis of 2017–2021, when Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Bahrain and Egypt blockaded Qatar; the dispute ended with the Al-Ula declaration in January 2021. Qatar under Tamim also mediated in regional conflicts and expanded liquefied natural gas production from the North Field.

Personal life

Tamim has married three times and has a number of children. He is a keen sportsman and a member of the International Olympic Committee.
