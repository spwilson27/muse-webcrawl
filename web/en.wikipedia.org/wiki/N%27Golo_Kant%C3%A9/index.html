N'Golo Kanté (born 29 March 1991) is a French professional footballer who plays as a defensive midfielder. Widely regarded as one of the best midfielders of his generation, he is renowned for his stamina, ball-winning ability, tactical intelligence, and humble temperament.

Club career

Kanté began his professional career at Boulogne and then Caen, whom he helped earn promotion to Ligue 1. In 2015 he moved to Leicester City, where he was the engine of the team that won the 2015–16 Premier League in one of the greatest upsets in sporting history. He then joined Chelsea in 2016, winning the Premier League again — becoming one of the few players to win consecutive titles with different clubs — along with the FA Cup, the UEFA Europa League, and the 2020–21 UEFA Champions League, in which he was named Player of the Match in both semi-final legs and the final. In 2023 he moved to Saudi Arabian club Al-Ittihad.

International career

Kanté debuted for France in 2016 and was a key member of the team that won the 2018 FIFA World Cup in Russia. He was named French Player of the Year in 2017.
