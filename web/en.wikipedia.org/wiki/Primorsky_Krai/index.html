Primorsky Krai
Country: Russia
Federal district: Far Eastern
Administrative centre: Vladivostok
Area: ~164,700 km²
Population: ~1.9 million

Primorsky Krai (Russian: Примо́рский край), informally known as Primorye, is a federal subject of Russia located in the far southeast of the country, bordering China and North Korea and facing the Sea of Japan. Its administrative centre and largest city is Vladivostok.

Geography

The krai covers about 164,700 square kilometres. Much of its interior is covered by the Sikhote-Alin mountain range and the Manchurian mixed forests, habitat of the Amur tiger and Amur leopard. The largest lake is Lake Khanka, shared with China.

History

The region was ceded by Qing China to the Russian Empire under the Treaty of Aigun (1858) and the Convention of Peking (1860). Vladivostok was founded in 1860 and became the principal Russian Pacific port and home of the Pacific Fleet. Primorsky Krai was formally established in 1938.

Economy

Shipping, fishing, shipbuilding, and trade through the ports of Vladivostok and Nakhodka anchor the economy. The krai is the eastern terminus of the Trans-Siberian Railway and hosted the 2012 APEC summit on Russky Island.
