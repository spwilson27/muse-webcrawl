Children's Laureate

CountryUnited Kingdom
First awarded1999 (Quentin Blake)
TermTwo years
Administered byBookTrust

The Children's Laureate is a position awarded in the United Kingdom to a distinguished writer or illustrator of children's books to celebrate achievement and promote children's reading.

History

The post was inspired by the Poet Laureate and established in 1999 with the support of the former Poet Laureate Ted Hughes and children's author Michael Morpurgo. The first holder was illustrator Quentin Blake; subsequent laureates have included Anne Fine, Michael Morpurgo, Jacqueline Wilson, Michael Rosen, Julia Donaldson, Malorie Blackman, Chris Riddell and Joseph Coelho.

Role

The laureate serves a two-year term, undertakes a personal project promoting reading or libraries, and receives a bursary and a medal. Similar positions exist in other countries, such as the U.S. National Ambassador for Young People's Literature.
