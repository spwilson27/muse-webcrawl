Form W-2

Form W-2
Official name: Wage and Tax Statement
Issued by: Internal Revenue Service
Filed by: Employers

Form W-2, the Wage and Tax Statement, is an Internal Revenue Service form used in the United States. Employers must complete and send a W-2 to each employee and to the Social Security Administration each year, reporting the employee's annual wages and the taxes withheld from their pay.

Contents

Key boxes report wages, tips and other compensation (Box 1), federal income tax withheld (Box 2), Social Security wages and tax withheld (Boxes 3–4), Medicare wages and tax withheld (Boxes 5–6), and state and local wage and tax information. Other boxes report items such as dependent care benefits, retirement plan participation and nonqualified plan income.

Filing

Employers must furnish W-2s to employees by 31 January following the tax year. Employees use the information when preparing their Form 1040 income tax return. Independent contractors instead receive Form 1099-NEC. A transmittal form, W-3, accompanies paper copies sent to the Social Security Administration.
