The Addams Family is a 1991 American supernatural black comedy film based on the characters from the cartoon created by Charles Addams and the 1964 television series produced by David Levy. Directed by Barry Sonnenfeld in his directorial debut, the film stars Anjelica Huston as Morticia Addams, Raul Julia as Gomez Addams, Christopher Lloyd as Uncle Fester, and Christina Ricci as Wednesday Addams.

Plot

Gomez Addams has been estranged for 25 years from his brother Fester, who disappeared after a dispute. Gomez's corrupt lawyer, Tully Alford, schemes with loan shark Abigail Craven to steal the Addams fortune: Craven's son Gordon, who bears a striking resemblance to Fester, poses as the long-lost brother to infiltrate the family and find their hidden vault. Gordon, accepted warmly by the family, gradually grows attached to them and ultimately turns against his mother; after Craven is defeated, it is revealed that Gordon truly is Fester, who had lost his memory after being found in the Bermuda Triangle.

Reception

The film was a commercial success, grossing over $191 million worldwide against a $30 million budget, despite mixed reviews from critics, who praised the cast and production design. It was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Costume Design. A sequel, Addams Family Values, followed in 1993.
