Battle of Vittorio Veneto

Battle of Vittorio Veneto
Date: 24 October – 4 November 1918
Location: Northern Italy
Part of: First World War

The Battle of Vittorio Veneto was fought from 24 October to 4 November 1918 between Italy and Austria-Hungary on the Italian Front of the First World War. The Italian victory led to the Armistice of Villa Giusti and the dissolution of the Austro-Hungarian army in Italy.

Background

A year after the disaster of Caporetto, Italy, reinforced by British, French and American units, attacked as Austria-Hungary was collapsing politically.

The battle

Italian forces crossed the Piave, broke through at Vittorio Veneto and advanced deep into enemy territory, taking hundreds of thousands of prisoners.

Aftermath

The armistice took effect on 4 November 1918, a week before the Western Front armistice.
