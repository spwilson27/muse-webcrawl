Gemayel family

The Gemayel family is a prominent Lebanese Maronite Christian political family, based in Bikfaya in the Metn District. Members of the family founded and have long led the Kataeb Party (Phalange).

Notable members

  • Pierre Gemayel (1905–1984) — founder of the Kataeb Party in 1936 and a leading figure in Lebanese politics for decades.
  • Bachir Gemayel (1947–1982) — son of Pierre; commander of the Lebanese Forces during the civil war; elected President of Lebanon in 1982 and assassinated before taking office.
  • Amine Gemayel (born 1942) — son of Pierre; President of Lebanon from 1982 to 1988.
  • Pierre Amine Gemayel (1972–2006) — son of Amine; minister of industry, assassinated in 2006.
  • Samy Gemayel (born 1980) — son of Amine; leader of the Kataeb Party since 2015.
  • Nadim Gemayel (born 1982) — son of Bachir; member of parliament.

History

The family's political influence dates to Pierre Gemayel's founding of the Phalange movement, modelled partly on European youth movements of the 1930s. The Gemayels played central roles in the Lebanese Civil War (1975–1990) and in the politics of the post-war republic.
