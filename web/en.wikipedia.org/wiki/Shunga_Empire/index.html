Shunga Empire
Periodc. 185 – c. 73 BCE
CapitalPataliputra; Vidisha
FounderPushyamitra Shunga
Last rulerDevabhuti

The Shunga Empire (also Śuṅga) was an ancient Indian dynasty of the Magadha region that controlled parts of northern and central India from around 185 to 73 BCE. The dynasty was established by Pushyamitra Shunga, a commander-in-chief who assassinated the last Mauryan emperor, Brihadratha.

History

Pushyamitra is remembered in later tradition as a patron of Brahmanism who performed Vedic horse sacrifices. His successors contended with the Indo-Greek kingdoms to the northwest. The last Shunga ruler, Devabhuti, was overthrown by his minister Vasudeva Kanva, who founded the Kanva dynasty.

Art and culture

The Shunga period saw important developments in Indian art, including the stone railings and gateways of the Bharhut and Sanchi stupas, and early terracotta sculpture.
