Hidden Valley Ranch

Hidden Valley is an American brand of salad dressing and seasoning mix, best known as the original creator of ranch dressing. The brand is owned by the Clorox Company and has been the best-selling ranch dressing in the United States for decades.

History

Ranch dressing was created in the early 1950s by plumber-turned-cowboy Steve Henson, who developed the buttermilk-and-herb dressing while working as a contractor in Alaska. In 1954 Henson and his wife, Gayle, bought a guest ranch near Santa Barbara, California, and named it Hidden Valley Ranch. The dressing, served to guests, grew so popular that the Hensons began selling dry seasoning packets by mail order, and in 1972 a shelf-stable bottled version was introduced for supermarkets.

The Hensons sold the Hidden Valley Ranch company to Clorox in 1973 for a reported $8 million. Clorox reformulated the dressing for national production and expanded the line into dip mixes, seasoning packets, and a wide range of flavoured dressings. Ranch became the most popular salad dressing flavour in the United States in the early 1990s, overtaking Italian, and Hidden Valley has remained the category's leading brand, extending into products such as ranch-flavoured snacks, sauces, and a plant-based variant.

Products

The core line includes Original Ranch bottled dressing, dry salad dressing and dip mixes, and varieties such as Buttermilk Ranch, Spicy Ranch, and Buffalo Ranch. The brand has also produced limited-edition and novelty products, licensed its flavour to other food manufacturers, and operates an online merchandising shop — at one point selling a ranch-dressing fountain and holiday-themed products that drew wide media attention.
