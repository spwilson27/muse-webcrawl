U.S. Space Command (USSPACECOM)
Established1985; re-established 29 August 2019
TypeUnified combatant command
HeadquartersPeterson Space Force Base, Colorado

United States Space Command (USSPACECOM) is a unified combatant command of the U.S. Department of Defense responsible for military operations in, from, and to space. It employs forces from all armed services to conduct operations including satellite communications, missile warning, space surveillance, and space control.

Space Command was originally established in 1985 and merged into U.S. Strategic Command in 2002. It was re-established as a separate combatant command on 29 August 2019, shortly before the creation of the U.S. Space Force as an independent service in December 2019.

Distinction from Space Force

While the Space Force organises, trains, and equips space forces, Space Command is the warfighting headquarters that employs them operationally. Its components include the Combined Force Space Component Command and the Joint Task Force-Space Defense, and it works closely with allied partners through initiatives such as the multinational Operation Olympic Defender.
