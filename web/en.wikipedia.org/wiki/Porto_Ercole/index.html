Porto Ercole

Porto Ercole
Country: Italy
Region: Tuscany
Comune: Monte Argentario

Porto Ercole is a town on the Monte Argentario promontory in southern Tuscany, Italy, and a frazione of the comune of Monte Argentario. A picturesque harbour town and resort, it is overlooked by Spanish fortresses built when the area formed part of the State of the Presidi in the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries.

The painter Caravaggio died in Porto Ercole in 1610 while travelling back to Rome, in circumstances that remain debated. The town's fortresses — Forte Filippo, Forte Stella and La Rocca — guarded the harbour and now frame its skyline.

Porto Ercole is also known as a yachting centre and as a summer retreat; the last king of Italy, Umberto II, kept a villa nearby, and the Dutch royal family long had a holiday residence in the area.
