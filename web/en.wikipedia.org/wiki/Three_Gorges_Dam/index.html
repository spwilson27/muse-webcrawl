Three Gorges Dam
Type: Wikipedia article (recreated from memory)

The Three Gorges Dam is a hydroelectric gravity dam that spans the Yangtze River near Yichang, Hubei, China. By installed capacity (22,500 MW) it is the world's largest power station. Completed structurally in 2006 and fully operational by 2012, its reservoir stretches some 600 km upstream toward Chongqing.

The dam was built for power generation, flood control (the Yangtze's floods killed hundreds of thousands in the 20th century) and navigation (a five-stage ship lock and a ship lift). It displaced well over a million people, inundated towns, farmland and archaeological sites, and altered the river's ecology and sediment flow — costs that make it one of the most debated infrastructure projects in the world, advocated by the Chinese state since Sun Yat-sen first proposed it in 1919.
