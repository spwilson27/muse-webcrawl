A selfie is a self-portrait photograph, typically taken with a smartphone or webcam and often shared on social media. Selfies are usually informal, taken at arm's length or via a mirror or selfie stick.

History

Self-portraiture is old — Robert Cornelius took a daguerreotype self-portrait in 1839 — but the modern selfie followed front-facing phone cameras (popularized by the iPhone 4, 2010) and social platforms such as Instagram. The word "selfie", attested in an Australian forum in 2002, was Oxford Dictionaries' Word of the Year in 2013. Ellen DeGeneres's group selfie at the 2014 Oscars became one of the most retweeted images of its time. The famous 2011 "monkey selfie" by a crested macaque sparked a copyright dispute.

Culture and criticism

Selfies function in self-expression, tourism, marketing, and politics, while critics link heavy selfie culture to narcissism, body-image pressure, and risky behaviour — deaths while taking selfies in dangerous locations have been documented. Museums and venues have alternately banned selfie sticks and embraced "selfie" installations.
