Washing machine

Washing machine
FunctionAppliance that washes laundry automatically
PrincipleRotating drum with water and detergent
[Image: A front-loading washing machine mid-cycle, clothes tumbling in suds]

A washing machine is a home appliance used to wash laundry, agitating clothes in water and detergent in a rotating drum and spinning them nearly dry. Electric models spread through the 20th century, mechanising one of the most labour-intensive household chores.

Social effects

Historians credit washing machines (with the refrigerator and other appliances) as quiet economic revolutions, freeing hours — mostly women's — for education and employment. Environmental concerns include microfibres shed from synthetic clothes (plastics in miniature) reaching rivers and seas.
