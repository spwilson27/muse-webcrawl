Naivedya

Naivedya (Sanskrit: नैवेद्य) is a Sanskrit word meaning an offering of food to a deity in Hindu worship. The food is presented to the god during puja and, once blessed, is distributed to devotees as prasada.

Practice

Offerings are typically sattvic — vegetarian food prepared without onion or garlic — and may include fruit, sweets, milk, cooked rice and specially prepared dishes. In temples, elaborate daily offerings follow prescribed ritual schedules; in homes, a portion of cooked food is offered before meals.

Significance

The offering expresses devotion and gratitude, and the act of sharing the blessed food symbolises the deity's grace. Famous temple naivedya include the Mahaprasada of the Jagannath Temple at Puri.
