Malaysia Airlines (Malay: Penerbangan Malaysia) is the flag carrier of Malaysia and one of Asia's oldest airlines, headquartered at Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA), its main hub. Operating scheduled services to destinations across Asia, Australia, Europe, and the Middle East, the airline traces its origins to Malayan Airways, founded in 1947. It is a member of the Oneworld alliance and is wholly owned by the Malaysian sovereign wealth fund Khazanah Nasional through the Malaysia Aviation Group.

History

The airline began as Malayan Airways Limited in 1947, flying between Singapore and Kuala Lumpur. With Malayan independence and the formation of Malaysia, it became Malaysian Airways and then, after Singapore's separation, Malaysia-Singapore Airlines (MSA). In 1972 MSA split into Singapore Airlines and Malaysian Airline System — restructured as Malaysia Airlines — which expanded through the jet age into a major intercontinental carrier, winning repeated awards for cabin service. Severe losses after the 1997 Asian financial crisis and again in the 2010s forced successive restructurings.

2014 disasters and restructuring

In 2014 the airline suffered two catastrophic losses within five months. On 8 March 2014, Flight MH370, a Boeing 777 en route from Kuala Lumpur to Beijing with 239 people aboard, disappeared over the southern Indian Ocean; despite the largest search in aviation history the main wreckage was never found. On 17 July 2014, Flight MH17, another Boeing 777 flying from Amsterdam to Kuala Lumpur, was shot down over eastern Ukraine by a surface-to-air missile, killing all 298 aboard; Dutch-led investigators attributed the attack to a Buk missile from a Russian-backed separatist area, and a Dutch court convicted three men in absentia in 2022. The disasters, falling on an already loss-making airline, led Khazanah to take it private, cut roughly a third of its workforce, drop many long-haul routes, and relaunch it as Malaysia Airlines Berhad in 2015.

Operations

The fleet centres on Airbus A330s and A350s for long-haul routes and Boeing 737s for regional services, with A330neo aircraft ordered to renew the widebody fleet. Subsidiaries include Firefly and MASwings for regional and rural services. The airline's frequent-flyer programme is Enrich, and its service concept emphasises "Malaysian Hospitality".
