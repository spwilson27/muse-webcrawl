Engineering Council

The Engineering Council is the UK regulatory body for the engineering profession, established by Royal Charter in 1981 following the Finniston Report. It maintains the national register of professional engineers and technicians.

Registration

Through licensed professional engineering institutions (descendants of the tradition of engineers like William Jessop), it awards the titles Chartered Engineer (CEng), Incorporated Engineer (IEng), Engineering Technician (EngTech) and ICT Technician (ICTTech), setting standards in its UK Standard for Professional Engineering Competence (UK-SPEC).
