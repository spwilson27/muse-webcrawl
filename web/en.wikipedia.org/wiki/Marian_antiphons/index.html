Marian antiphons

The Marian antiphons are a group of four Latin hymns to the Virgin Mary sung in the Roman Catholic Church, particularly at the end of Compline (Night Prayer) in the Liturgy of the Hours. Each is assigned to a season of the liturgical year.

The four antiphons

The four are: Alma Redemptoris Mater (from Advent until Candlemas), Ave Regina Caelorum (from Candlemas through Holy Week), Regina Caeli (during Eastertide, replacing the Angelus in that season) and Salve Regina (from after Pentecost until Advent). They have been set to music by countless composers, from Gregorian chant melodies to polyphonic settings by Palestrina, Lassus and many others.
