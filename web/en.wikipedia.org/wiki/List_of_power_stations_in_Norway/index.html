Main sourceHydroelectricity (~90% of generation)

Largest operatorStatkraft

Norway generates almost all of its electricity from renewable sources, with hydroelectricity providing around 90 per cent of production — the highest share of any major country. This is a list of the main power stations in Norway, dominated by large hydroelectric plants operated by the state-owned Statkraft and regional utilities, together with growing onshore wind farms.

Wind power

Onshore wind capacity grew rapidly in the 2010s; the Fosen Vind project in Trøndelag — including the Storheia and Hitra 2 wind farms — is among the largest onshore wind developments in Europe, totalling about 1,057 MW.
