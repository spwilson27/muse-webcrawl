Aviation weather

Products: METAR, TAF, SIGMET, AIRMET
Hazards: Icing, turbulence, thunderstorms, low visibility

Aviation weather is the study and reporting of weather conditions as they affect flight operations. Pilots and dispatchers rely on standardised observations and forecasts — including METAR surface reports and TAF aerodrome forecasts — to plan safe flights.

Hazards

Significant hazards include airframe and carburettor icing, clear-air and convective turbulence, thunderstorms and microbursts, fog and other visibility restrictions, strong winds and wind shear, and volcanic ash.

Services

National meteorological services and aviation authorities issue warnings such as SIGMETs for severe phenomena and AIRMETs for moderate conditions. Flight categories (VFR, MVFR, IFR, LIFR) summarise ceiling and visibility for operational decisions.
