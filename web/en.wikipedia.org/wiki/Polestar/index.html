Polestar

A Polestar electric performance car.

Polestar Automotive Holding UK PLC, known simply as Polestar, is a Swedish automotive brand specialising in electric performance cars. The company was founded in 1996 as Flash Engineering, later became Polestar Racing, and was acquired by Volvo Cars in 2015. In 2017, Polestar was relaunched as a standalone electric vehicle brand jointly owned by Volvo Cars and its parent company, the Chinese conglomerate Geely.

History

Polestar began as a racing team competing in the Scandinavian Touring Car Championship with Volvo cars. After Volvo acquired the company, Polestar became its official performance division, producing tuned "Polestar Engineered" versions of Volvo models. In June 2017, Volvo announced that Polestar would become a separate brand focused on high-performance electric vehicles.

Models

  • Polestar 1 (2019–2021): a hybrid grand tourer coupe, limited production.
  • Polestar 2 (2020–present): a battery-electric fastback, the brand's first mass-produced all-electric car.
  • Polestar 3 (2024–present): a mid-size luxury electric SUV.
  • Polestar 4 (2024–present): a coupe-style electric SUV without a rear window.
  • Polestar 5 and Polestar 6: announced grand tourer and roadster models.

Public listing

In June 2022, Polestar listed on the Nasdaq stock exchange through a merger with a special-purpose acquisition company (SPAC), Gores Guggenheim. The company emphasises minimalist Scandinavian design, sustainability reporting for its vehicles, and a direct-to-consumer sales model in many markets.

Motorsport

The racing heritage continues through Cyan Racing (formerly Polestar Racing), which competes in touring car championships.
