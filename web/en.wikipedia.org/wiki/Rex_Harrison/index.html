Sir Reginald Carey "Rex" Harrison (5 March 1908 – 2 June 1990) was an English actor of stage and screen. One of the great leading men of twentieth-century British theatre, he is best remembered for his portrayal of Professor Henry Higgins in the stage musical My Fair Lady and its 1964 film adaptation, a performance that defined his career and won him the Academy Award for Best Actor.

Early life and career

Harrison was born in Huyton, Lancashire, and educated at Liverpool College. He made his stage debut in 1924 and his film debut in The Great Game (1930). After serving in the Royal Air Force during the Second World War, he achieved major screen success with Blithe Spirit (1945), Anna and the King of Siam (1946), The Ghost and Mrs. Muir (1947) and The Foxes of Harrow (1947).

My Fair Lady and later career

In 1956 Harrison created the role of Higgins on Broadway opposite Julie Andrews in Lerner and Loewe's My Fair Lady, adapted from Shaw's Pygmalion. His half-spoken, rhythmically delivered singing style—sometimes called "talk-singing"—became iconic. He won the Tony Award for Best Actor in a Musical and repeated the role in London and in George Cukor's 1964 film, winning the Academy Award for Best Actor.

Other major roles included Julius Caesar in the epic Cleopatra (1963), for which he received an Oscar nomination, and the title role in Doctor Dolittle (1967). On stage he won a second Tony for Anne of the Thousand Days (1949) and appeared triumphantly in revivals of The Circle, Heartbreak House and a 1983 production of My Fair Lady. He was knighted in 1989 and published his autobiography, Rex (1974), and a memoir of his theatre years, A Damned Serious Business (1990).

Personal life

Harrison was married six times, including to actress Lilli Palmer and to Kay Kendall, whose death from leukaemia in 1959 deeply affected him. He also had a well-publicised relationship with actress Carole Landis, who died by suicide in 1948. Harrison died of pancreatic cancer in New York in 1990, shortly after appearing on stage in The Circle.
