Canadian National Exhibition

The Canadian National Exhibition (CNE), affectionately known as The Ex, is an annual fair held at Exhibition Place in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. Running for eighteen days ending on Labour Day, it is Canada's largest annual fair and one of the largest in North America, drawing around 1.5 million visitors in a typical year.

History

Founded in 1879 as the Toronto Industrial Exhibition, the fair was renamed the Canadian National Exhibition in 1912. Its grounds along the Lake Ontario shore developed iconic structures including the Princes' Gates (1927) and the Food Building. The fair was suspended during the COVID-19 pandemic, cancelling the 2020 and 2021 editions for the first time since the Second World War era.

Attractions

The CNE combines an agricultural fair with a large midway of rides and games, concerts at the Bandshell, air shows by the Canadian International Air Show, dog and horse shows, casino, and its famous food offerings — from Tiny Tom Donuts to inventive novelty foods in the Food Building. The Warriors' Day Parade, held since 1921, honours Canadian veterans.
