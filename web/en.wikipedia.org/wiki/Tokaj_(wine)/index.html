Tokaj (wine)

Tokaj is the name of wines from the Tokaj wine region in Hungary, also called Tokaji. The region is famous for sweet wines made from grapes affected by noble rot (Botrytis cinerea), particularly Tokaji Aszú, one of the world's oldest botrytised wines.

Principal grapes include Furmint, Hárslevelű and Yellow Muscat. Aszú wines were graded by puttonyos according to sweetness. Tokaj's vineyard classification in the 18th century was among the earliest, and the region is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
