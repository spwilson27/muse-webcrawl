List of renamed cities in India

Many cities in India have been renamed since independence in 1947. Renamings have generally sought to restore names used before British rule, replace anglicised spellings with forms closer to local pronunciation, or honour historical and cultural figures. The process requires approval from the central government, in particular the Ministry of Home Affairs, after a proposal from the state government.

Background

During British rule, many place names were recorded in anglicised forms that diverged from local usage. After independence, state governments proposed restorations of earlier names. A second wave of renaming in the 1990s and 2000s aligned official English spellings with names in regional languages, as with Bombay–Mumbai, Madras–Chennai and Calcutta–Kolkata. More recent renamings have often invoked pre-colonial or religious history, such as Allahabad becoming Prayagraj, the ancient name of the confluence of the Ganga and Yamuna rivers.
