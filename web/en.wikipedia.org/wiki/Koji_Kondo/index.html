Koji Kondo
Born: 13 August 1961, Nagoya, Japan
Occupation: Composer, sound designer
Employer: Nintendo (since 1984)

Koji Kondo is a Japanese composer and senior sound director at Nintendo. He is best known for composing the music of the Super Mario and The Legend of Zelda series, including some of the most recognisable themes in video game history.

Career

Kondo joined Nintendo in 1984, its first hire specialising in music. He scored Super Mario Bros. (1985), whose main theme became iconic, and The Legend of Zelda (1986). For The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (1998) he integrated the ocarina into gameplay. In later years he has supervised and mentored Nintendo's sound teams while continuing to compose.

Style

His work is noted for catchy, economical melodies composed within severe hardware limits, and for adaptive musical ideas in 3D games.
