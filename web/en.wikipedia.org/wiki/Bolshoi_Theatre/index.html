Bolshoi Theatre

Bolshoi Theatre
Location: Theatre Square, Moscow, Russia
Opened: 1825 (current building)
Capacity: ~1,740
Companies: Bolshoi Ballet, Bolshoi Opera

The Bolshoi Theatre (Russian: Большой театр, "Grand Theatre") is a historic opera and ballet theatre in Moscow, Russia, and home to the Bolshoi Ballet, one of the world's leading ballet companies.

History

The company was founded in 1776. The present neoclassical building, designed by Joseph Bové, opened in 1825 on Theatre Square; after a fire it was rebuilt by Alberto Cavos, reopening in 1856. Tchaikovsky's Swan Lake premiered there in 1877.

Renovation

The main building underwent a major restoration from 2005 to 2011, restoring its imperial-era interiors and acoustics. See also Mariinsky Theatre and Moscow.
