DJ Yella

Antoine Carraby (born December 11, 1961), better known by his stage name DJ Yella, is an American DJ, rapper, record producer, and film director from Compton, California. He was a member of the pioneering gangsta rap group N.W.A, alongside Dr. Dre, Eazy-E, Ice Cube, MC Ren, and Arabian Prince.

Career

Yella began his career as a DJ in Los Angeles clubs and was a member of the electro group World Class Wreckin' Cru with Dr. Dre. The two went on to co-produce much of N.W.A's output, including the landmark album Straight Outta Compton (1988), as well as Eazy-E's solo work and releases on Ruthless Records by artists such as Bone Thugs-n-Harmony. Yella was one of only two members, with MC Ren, to remain with N.W.A until the group's breakup in 1991.

After Eazy-E's death in 1995, Yella released the solo album One Mo Nigga ta Go (1996), dedicated to his friend. He later directed adult films before returning to music. In 2015 he published the memoir Straight Outta Compton: My Untold Story and was portrayed by Neil Brown Jr. in the biographical film Straight Outta Compton. N.W.A was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2016.
