The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes

The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes is a dystopian novel by American author Suzanne Collins, a prequel to her Hunger Games trilogy. Published in May 2020, it is set 64 years before the events of the original series, during the 10th Hunger Games.

Plot

Eighteen-year-old Coriolanus Snow, heir to a once-great Capitol family now fallen on hard times, is assigned as mentor to Lucy Gray Baird, the charismatic songstress tribute from District 12. As Snow schemes to keep her alive in a primitive, brutal early version of the Games — and to secure his own future — their fates intertwine, setting him on the path to becoming the tyrannical president of the trilogy.

Film adaptation

A film adaptation directed by Francis Lawrence, starring Tom Blyth as Snow and Rachel Zegler as Lucy Gray, was released in November 2023 and grossed over $340 million worldwide.
