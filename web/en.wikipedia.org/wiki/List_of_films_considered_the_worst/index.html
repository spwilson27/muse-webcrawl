List of films considered the worst

The films listed below have been cited by a variety of notable critics and publications as among the worst films ever made. Examples of such sources include Metacritic, Roger Ebert's lists of most-hated films, the Golden Raspberry Awards ("Razzies"), Leonard Maltin's Movie Guide, and the cult appreciation of "so bad it's good" cinema. Many of these films are also box-office bombs or have been adopted as midnight-movie and riffing staples (notably through Mystery Science Theater 3000).

Notable examples by era

FilmYearWhy cited

The Conqueror1956John Wayne miscast as Genghis Khan; infamously filmed near nuclear test sites.
Eegah1962Amateurish caveman-meets-teens horror.
Manos: The Hands of Fate1966Made by a fertiliser salesman; notorious after its MST3K episode.
Santa Claus Conquers the Martians1964Cheap children's sci-fi with Pia Zadora.
Heaven's Gate1980Runaway budget; its failure reshaped Hollywood financing.
Inchon1981Expensive, critically derided Korean War epic.
Howard the Duck1986Big-budget Marvel adaptation and box-office bomb.
Mac and Me1988Widely seen as an E.T. copycat and product placement vehicle.
Troll 21990Contains no trolls; famous "they're eating her!" scene; subject of the documentary Best Worst Movie.

Street Fighter, Super Mario Bros.1994 / 1993Early video-game adaptations cited for incoherence.

The Room2003Tommy Wiseau's melodrama; the definitive "best worst movie", documented in The Disaster Artist.

Cats2019Musical adaptation infamous for its digital effects; major box-office bomb.
