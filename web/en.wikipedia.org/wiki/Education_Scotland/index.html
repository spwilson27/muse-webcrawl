Education Scotland

Formation1 July 2011
TypeExecutive agency of the Scottish Government
PurposeEducation improvement, inspection and support
PredecessorsHM Inspectorate of Education; Learning and Teaching Scotland

Education Scotland (Scottish Gaelic: Foghlam Alba) is an executive agency of the Scottish Government responsible for supporting quality and improvement in Scottish education. It was formed on 1 July 2011 by the merger of HM Inspectorate of Education (HMIE) and Learning and Teaching Scotland (LTS), together with elements of other bodies.

Functions

The agency combines inspection of schools and other educational establishments with national support for curriculum development and professional learning. It supports the implementation of Scotland's Curriculum for Excellence, provides the Glow Schools digital learning platform, and publishes guidance and resources for teachers. Its inspectors — His Majesty's Inspectors of Education — evaluate the quality of education in schools, colleges, early learning settings and community learning.

Reform

Following criticism of the agency's dual role as both inspector and improvement supporter, the Scottish Government announced in 2021 that the inspection function would be separated into a new independent body, His Majesty's Inspectorate of Education in Scotland, and that the Scottish Qualifications Authority would be replaced. Legislative reform of the education agencies continued through the Education (Scotland) Bill process in the mid-2020s.
