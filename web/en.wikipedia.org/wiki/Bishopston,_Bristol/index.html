Bishopston, Bristol

Bishopston is a suburb of the city of Bristol in south-west England. It lies to the north of the city centre, centred on the Gloucester Road (A38), and is known for its largely Victorian terraced housing and its long high street of independent shops, cafes and pubs, often claimed to be one of the longest independent high streets in the United Kingdom.

History

Bishopston takes its name from the Bishop of Bristol, whose estate once included the area: the name is recorded from the 19th century as "Bishop's town". The area was developed mainly in the later Victorian period as a residential suburb, with much of its housing built between the 1880s and the early 20th century.

Community

The Gloucester Road forms the commercial heart of Bishopston, with a high proportion of independent traders. The Memorial Stadium, home of Bristol Rovers F.C. from 1996 until 2024, stands at the northern end of the district in neighbouring Horfield. Bishopston is part of the Bishopston and Ashley Down electoral ward of Bristol City Council.
