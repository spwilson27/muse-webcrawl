Mohamed Salah (born 15 June 1992) is an Egyptian professional footballer who plays as a right winger or forward for Premier League club Liverpool and captains the Egypt national team. Widely regarded as one of the best players of his generation and one of the greatest African footballers of all time, he is known for his dribbling, finishing and record-breaking goalscoring with Liverpool. In Egypt he is a national icon — often called "the Egyptian King" by Liverpool supporters.

Club career

Salah began his career with Cairo club Al Mokawloon (Arab Contractors), making his Egyptian Premier League debut in 2010. Swiss club FC Basel signed him in 2012; he won two Swiss Super League titles and impressed in European competition, prompting Chelsea to sign him in January 2014 for about £11 million. Playing time in London was limited, and after loan spells at Fiorentina and then Roma — made permanent in 2016 — he flourished in Serie A, scoring 29 league goals in two seasons.

Liverpool signed Salah in June 2017 for a then club-record fee of about £36.9 million. His debut season was extraordinary: 32 Premier League goals set the record for a 38-game season, and 44 in all competitions; he won the PFA Players' Player of the Year and the first of his Premier League Golden Boots. With Liverpool he has won the UEFA Champions League (2019), the Premier League (2019–20, ending a 30-year wait, and again in 2024–25), the FA Cup, League Cups, the UEFA Super Cup and the FIFA Club World Cup. He became the fastest Liverpool player to 100 and then 200 club goals, and ranks among the Premier League's all-time top scorers, repeatedly breaking records for goal involvements.

International career

Salah earned youth honours with Egypt before his senior debut in 2011. His late penalty against Congo in October 2017 sent Egypt to the 2018 FIFA World Cup, their first since 1990. He was African Footballer of the Year in 2017 and 2018, and led Egypt to the Africa Cup of Nations finals in 2017 and 2021 (played 2022), losing both.

Playing style and influence

A left-footed forward operating mainly from the right, Salah cuts inside onto his stronger foot to shoot, combining explosive pace, close dribbling and clinical finishing with increasingly creative playmaking. Beyond football, charitable donations in his home village of Nagrig, his openness about his Muslim faith, and studies suggesting a fall in hate crime and Islamophobic social-media posts in Liverpool after his arrival have made him a notable cultural figure.
