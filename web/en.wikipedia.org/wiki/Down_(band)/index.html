Down, the New Orleans sludge-metal supergroup.

Down is an American heavy metal supergroup formed in New Orleans, Louisiana, in 1991. The band's current lineup includes vocalist Phil Anselmo (Pantera and Superjoint Ritual), guitarist Pepper Keenan (Corrosion of Conformity), guitarist Kirk Windstein (Crowbar), bassist Pat Bruders (Goatwhore), and drummer Jimmy Bower (Eyehategod). Since its formation, Down has released five studio albums and one EP.

Down was conceived as a side project by friends from the New Orleans heavy music scene who shared a love of Black Sabbath, Saint Vitus, and Lynyrd Skynyrd, blending doom and sludge metal with Southern rock influences. A three-song demo circulated by word of mouth and tape trading built strong underground anticipation for the band's debut.

History

NOLA and Down II (1995–2002)

The debut album NOLA was released in 1995 and is widely regarded as a landmark of sludge metal, featuring the songs "Stone the Crow", "Temptation's Wings", and "Bury Me in Smoke". After a long interval in which the members returned to their main bands, the follow-up Down II: A Bustle in Your Hedgerow appeared in 2002.

Down III and EPs (2007–present)

Down III: Over the Under (2007) addressed the aftermath of Hurricane Katrina, the murder of Anselmo's former Pantera bandmate Dimebag Darrell, and other personal losses, and debuted in the US top 30. The band then issued the two-part Down IV as EPs: Part I – The Purple EP (2012) and Part II (2014). Down has toured sporadically amid its members' other commitments, remaining a revered live act in the metal underground.

Musical style

Down's sound combines the downtuned, blues-drenched riffing of Black Sabbath with the heaviness of the Louisiana sludge scene and Southern rock swing, delivered with Anselmo's varied vocals, which range from melodic singing to hardcore-influenced screams. Critics commonly cite the band as a defining act of "Southern metal".
