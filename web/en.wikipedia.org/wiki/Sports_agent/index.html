Sports agent

A sports agent is a legal representative for professional sports figures such as athletes and coaches. Agents procure and negotiate employment and endorsement contracts for their clients, typically earning a commission of around 3–10 percent depending on the league and the type of contract, with playing-contract fees often capped by players' associations.

Roles

Beyond contract negotiation, agents commonly manage public relations, financial referrals, career planning, and post-career advice, and they recruit young athletes as future clients. Large agencies — including Creative Artists Agency (CAA), Wasserman, Excel Sports Management, and Klutch Sports — represent many of the world's highest-paid athletes, while prominent individual agents such as Scott Boras in baseball and Jorge Mendes in football have become influential figures in their sports.

Regulation

Agents are regulated by players' unions, which certify agents permitted to negotiate standard player contracts (for example the NFLPA, NBPA, and MLBPA in the United States), and by law: many U.S. states and the federal Sports Agent Responsibility and Trust Act (SPARTA, 2004) govern the recruitment of student athletes, and world football's governing bodies have repeatedly revised agent licensing rules. Ethical controversies have included conflicts of interest, inducements to college athletes, and agents' influence over transfers.

In popular culture

The profession is familiar from the 1996 film Jerry Maguire, whose title character was inspired in part by real agent Leigh Steinberg, and from television series about athlete representation.
