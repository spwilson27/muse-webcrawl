Queen Máxima of the Netherlands

Tenure: 30 April 2013 – present
Born: Máxima Zorreguieta, 17 May 1971, Buenos Aires, Argentina
Spouse: Willem-Alexander of the Netherlands (m. 2002)
Issue: Catharina-Amalia, Princess of Orange; Princess Alexia; Princess Ariane
House: Orange-Nassau (by marriage)
Religion: Roman Catholic

Queen Máxima (born Máxima Zorreguieta on 17 May 1971) is the wife of King Willem-Alexander of the Netherlands and the queen consort of the Netherlands since her husband's accession on 30 April 2013, following the abdication of Queen Beatrix. Argentine by birth, she worked in finance before marrying into the Dutch royal family, and she is the first queen consort of the Netherlands born outside Europe and the first from Latin America.

Early life and career

Máxima Zorreguieta was born in Buenos Aires, the daughter of Jorge Zorreguieta, who served as Secretary of Agriculture under the Argentine military government of Jorge Rafael Videla, and María del Carmen Cerruti Carricart. She studied economics at the Pontifical Catholic University of Argentina, graduating in 1995. She worked in sales and finance in Buenos Aires and New York, including for Boston Securities, HSBC James Capel, and Deutsche Bank. Her father's role in the Videla regime prompted an official Dutch investigation before her engagement; he was barred from attending the wedding, and her mother also stayed away out of solidarity.

Marriage and family

She met Willem-Alexander, then Prince of Orange, in Seville in 1999. Their engagement was announced on 30 March 2001, and she was granted Dutch citizenship. They married in Amsterdam on 2 February 2002 in civil and religious ceremonies. The couple have three daughters: Catharina-Amalia (born 2003), Alexia (born 2005), and Ariane (born 2007). She addressed the Dutch public in Dutch at her engagement, and learning the language and culture quickly made her one of the most popular members of the royal family.

Work and interests

As Princess and later Queen, Máxima has focused on the integration of immigrants, financial inclusion, and mental health. She serves as the United Nations Secretary-General's Special Advocate for Inclusive Finance for Development (UNSGSA), a role she has held since 2009, promoting access to financial services worldwide. She is honorary chair of the Dutch Money Wise Platform and has been involved with the G20 Global Partnership for Financial Inclusion. In the Netherlands she is also patron of the Oranje Fonds and an advocate for music education for children through the programme "Meer Muziek in de Klas".

Titles and honours

Upon her marriage she became Princess of the Netherlands and Princess of Orange-Nassau. On Willem-Alexander's accession she became queen consort, styled Her Majesty Queen Máxima. She holds numerous Dutch and foreign orders, including Knight Grand Cross of the Order of the Netherlands Lion.
