Sir Samuel Alexander Mendes (born 1 August 1965) is an English film and stage director, producer and screenwriter. He rose to prominence as artistic director of the Donmar Warehouse in London, where his productions of Cabaret and The Blue Room achieved international success. His feature film debut, American Beauty (1999), won the Academy Award for Best Picture, and Mendes won the Academy Award for Best Director.

Career

Mendes's films include Road to Perdition (2002), Jarhead (2005), Revolutionary Road (2008) and Away We Go (2009). He directed the James Bond films Skyfall (2012) — the franchise's highest-grossing entry at the time — and Spectre (2015). His First World War film 1917 (2019), designed to appear as a single continuous shot, won him the Golden Globe and BAFTA awards for Best Director and received ten Academy Award nominations. He followed it with Empire of Light (2022).

In theatre, Mendes directed the musical Charlie and the Chocolate Factory (2013), Jez Butterworth's The Ferryman (2017), which won the Tony Award for Best Play after transferring to Broadway, and The Lehman Trilogy, which also won the Tony for Best Play and Best Director. In 2019 he founded the Neal Street production company ventures and was knighted in the 2020 New Year Honours for services to drama.

Personal life

Mendes was born in Reading to Valerie Mendes, an author of children's books, and Jameson Peter Mendes, a university lecturer of Portuguese descent. He was educated at Magdalen College School, Oxford, and Peterhouse, Cambridge, where he read English. He was married to actress Kate Winslet from 2003 to 2011, and in 2017 married classical trumpeter Alison Balsom.
