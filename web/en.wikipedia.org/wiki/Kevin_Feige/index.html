Kevin Feige

Kevin Feige (born June 2, 1973) is an American film and television producer. He has been the president of Marvel Studios since 2007 and the primary producer and creator of the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) franchise. The films he has produced have a combined worldwide box office gross of over US$31 billion, making him the highest-grossing producer of all time, with Avengers: Endgame (2019) becoming the highest-grossing film at the time of its release.

Early life

Feige was born in Boston, Massachusetts, and raised in Westfield, New Jersey. He attended the USC School of Cinematic Arts, applying repeatedly before being admitted; his early jobs included work as an assistant to producer Lauren Shuler Donner on films such as You've Got Mail.

Career

Donner made Feige an associate producer on X-Men (2000) because of his extensive knowledge of the Marvel comics. He joined Marvel Studios and rose to president of production in 2007, overseeing the independently financed launch of the MCU with Iron Man (2008). In 2019 he became chief creative officer of Marvel Entertainment, overseeing television and publishing as well. He also produces Star Wars projects for Lucasfilm.
