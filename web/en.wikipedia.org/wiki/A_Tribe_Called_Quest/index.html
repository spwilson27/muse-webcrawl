A Tribe Called Quest
Origin: Queens, New York City
Years active: 1985–1998, 2004–2017
Members: Q-Tip, Phife Dawg, Ali Shaheed Muhammad, Jarobi White
Genre: Alternative hip hop, jazz rap

A Tribe Called Quest was an American hip hop group formed in Queens, New York City, in 1985, originally composed of Q-Tip, Phife Dawg, Ali Shaheed Muhammad and Jarobi White. Pioneers of alternative hip hop, they blended jazz samples with Afrocentric, socially conscious lyrics as part of the Native Tongues collective.

History

Their albums The Low End Theory (1991) and Midnight Marauders (1993) are considered hip hop classics, featuring tracks like "Can I Kick It?", "Scenario" and "Electric Relaxation". After disbanding in 1998, they reunited and released a final album, We Got It from Here... Thank You 4 Your Service, in 2016, months after Phife Dawg's death.

Legacy

The group was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2024.
