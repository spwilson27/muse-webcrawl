Phase II Pan Groove

OriginWoodbrook, Port of Spain, Trinidad and Tobago
GenresSteelband, calypso, soca
Years active1972–present
Musical directorLen "Boogsie" Sharpe

Phase II Pan Groove is a steelband based in Woodbrook, Port of Spain, Trinidad and Tobago. Founded in 1972, it is one of the most successful and influential steel orchestras in the history of the Panorama competition, closely associated with its long-time arranger and musical director Len "Boogsie" Sharpe, widely regarded as one of the greatest arrangers in steelband history.

History

The band emerged in the early 1970s from the Woodbrook area, taking its name from the idea of a "second phase" in the development of the steelpan movement. Under Boogsie Sharpe's arranging, Phase II distinguished itself by performing original compositions written specifically for steelband, rather than arrangements of popular calypsos — a practice that was unusual at the time and initially controversial.

Panorama success

Phase II won its first Panorama title in 1987 with Sharpe's composition "This Feeling Nice", and went on to win multiple national Panorama championships, including a famous run of victories in the 2000s and 2010s with pieces such as "Musical Wine", "More Love", and "Happiness". The band's partnership with Sharpe produced some of the most celebrated arrangements in the competition's history, and Phase II is regularly among the leading large bands at the annual Carnival competition.

Legacy

The band is credited with elevating the artistic ambitions of Panorama arranging and with championing the composer-arranger model in steelband music. Its yard in Woodbrook remains a center of pan culture during the Carnival season.
