A lighting technician is a member of the production crew in theatre, film, television and live events responsible for rigging, focusing, operating and maintaining lighting equipment. Technicians implement the creative intent of the lighting designer (in theatre) or the director of photography and gaffer (in film).

Duties

Work includes hanging and cabling lanterns or fixtures according to a lighting plot, rigging truss and power distribution, focusing and colouring units, programming and operating lighting consoles, and running followspots. On film sets, electricians ("sparks") in the lighting department set lamps under the gaffer and best boy electric. Technicians must work safely at height and with high electrical loads.

Training and organisation

Many technicians enter through stage crew work, technical theatre courses or apprenticeships. In North America theatrical electricians are often represented by IATSE; in film, union lighting departments are organised under a gaffer-led hierarchy. Master electricians supervise the crew and the electrical safety of the rig.
