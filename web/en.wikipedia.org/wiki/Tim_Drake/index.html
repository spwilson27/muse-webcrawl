Timothy Jackson "Tim" Drake is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, commonly in association with the superhero Batman. Created by Marv Wolfman and Pat Broderick, he first appeared in Batman #436 (August 1989) and assumed the identity of Robin, Batman's crime-fighting partner, in the 1990 storyline "A Lonely Place of Dying." He was the third character to take up the Robin mantle, succeeding Jason Todd, and later operated as Red Robin.

Publication history

Tim Drake was introduced as a young teenager who had deduced Batman's and the original Robin Dick Grayson's secret identities after witnessing the Graysons' deaths as a small child. Following Jason Todd's death in "A Death in the Family," Tim argued that Batman needed a Robin to keep him from becoming reckless, and was eventually accepted into the role. He headlined the first ongoing Robin solo series in 1993, which ran for 183 issues, and was a founding member of the superhero team Young Justice before joining the Teen Titans. In 2009, after Bruce Wayne's apparent death, Tim took the name Red Robin while Damian Wayne became Robin.

Fictional character biography

Unlike his predecessors, Tim came from a wealthy family with living parents; his mother was killed and his father paralyzed by the Obeah Man early in his career, and his father Jack Drake was later murdered by Captain Boomerang in Identity Crisis, after which Bruce Wayne adopted him. Tim is characterized as the most cerebral of the Robins — a detective and strategist rivalling Batman himself — and is an expert with the bo staff. He has been a leader of the Teen Titans and a core member of the "Bat-Family." In stories published from 2021 onward, Tim came out as bisexual after accepting a date with former schoolmate Bernard Dowd, a development widely covered in mainstream media.

In other media

Tim Drake has appeared in animated series including The New Batman Adventures (where his characterization was partly merged with Jason Todd's), Young Justice, and in video games such as the Batman: Arkham series and Gotham Knights (2022), in which he is a playable protagonist. He was portrayed by Jay Lycurgo in the live-action series Titans.
