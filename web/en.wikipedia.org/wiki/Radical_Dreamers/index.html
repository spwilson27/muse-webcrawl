Radical Dreamers

Radical Dreamers is a 1996 text-based visual novel adventure video game developed and published by Square (now Square Enix) for the Satellaview, a satellite modem peripheral for Nintendo's Super Famicom console in Japan. The game is part of the Chrono series and was created as a side story to the 1995 title Chrono Trigger, resolving loose ends from that game's plot.

Developer: Square
Director / writer: Masato Kato
Composer: Yasunori Mitsuda
Platform: Satellaview (Super Famicom)
Release: 1996 (Japan); 2022 worldwide as part of Chrono Cross: The Radical Dreamers Edition

Gameplay and story

Presented almost entirely through text with static backgrounds and minimal animation, the game follows Serge, a young adventurer and the narrator; Kid, a teenage thief; and Magil, a shadowy masked magician, who together infiltrate Viper Manor to steal the Frozen Flame. The scenario was written by series creator Masato Kato and features music by Yasunori Mitsuda. Depending on the player's choices, the story branches into several endings.

Legacy

Because the Satellaview service was discontinued, Radical Dreamers became obscure and was never officially released outside Japan for many years; an unofficial English fan translation circulated from 2003. Its characters and concepts were reworked into Chrono Cross (1999). In 2022 the game received its first official international release, newly translated, as a bonus within the remaster Chrono Cross: The Radical Dreamers Edition.
