Ninja Hattori-kun (Japanese: 忍者ハットリくん, "Ninja Hattori") is a Japanese manga series created by Motoo Abiko of the duo Fujiko Fujio, best known as the creator of Doraemon's partner works. It follows Kanzō Hattori, a young ninja from Iga who comes to Tokyo and befriends a boy named Kenichi Mitsuba. The manga was serialized beginning in 1964 and is considered one of the classic Japanese children's comedies.

Plot

Kanzō Hattori, an 11-year-old boy ninja, arrives in the city to complete his training and stays with the Mitsuba family. He helps his friend Kenichi — a lazy, not particularly capable schoolboy — out of everyday scrapes using ninja techniques such as running on water, wall-climbing, and throwing shuriken. He is accompanied by his younger brother Shinzo and his ninja dog Shishimaru. His chief rival is Kemumaki, a ninja of the rival Koga clan, who tries to outwit Hattori together with his ninja cat Kagechiyo.

Adaptations

The series was adapted into a live-action television drama in 1966–1968, an anime series produced by Shin-Ei Animation that ran from 1981 to 1987, several anime films, and a 2004 live-action film, Nin × Nin: Ninja Hattori-kun, the Movie. In 2012 a new animated series, co-produced with Reliance MediaWorks, premiered in India, where the character enjoys enduring popularity; dubbed versions have aired across Asia and in many other countries.
