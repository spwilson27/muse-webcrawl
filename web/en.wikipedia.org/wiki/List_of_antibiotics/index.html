List of antibiotics

Antibiotics are medicines used to treat bacterial infections. They are grouped into classes by chemical structure and mechanism of action. This list gives representative members of the main classes.

History

The first widely used antibiotics were the sulfonamides in the 1930s, followed by penicillin, discovered by Alexander Fleming in 1928 and developed for clinical use in the early 1940s. Streptomycin, the first effective treatment for tuberculosis, followed in 1943. Rising antimicrobial resistance is a major global health concern.
