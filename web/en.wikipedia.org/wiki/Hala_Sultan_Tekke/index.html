Hala Sultan Tekke

Hala Sultan Tekke is a mosque and tekke complex on the western shore of Larnaca Salt Lake in Cyprus, near the village of Dromolaxia. It is one of the holiest sites in Islam, traditionally ranking after Mecca, Medina and Jerusalem in importance for many Cypriot Muslims.

History

The complex is built around the tomb of Umm Haram bint Milhan, a companion and foster-aunt of the Prophet Muhammad, who according to tradition died during the first Arab raids on Cyprus in 647–649 AD, falling from a mule. The present mosque was largely built in the 18th century under Ottoman rule, with minaret and tekke buildings added later.

Complex

The site includes the mosque with its dome and minaret, the türbe (tomb) covered in green cloth, gardens, a fountain, and the surrounding salt lake, which is also famous for wintering flamingos. Excavations nearby revealed the Late Bronze Age city at Dromolaxia-Vizatzia.
