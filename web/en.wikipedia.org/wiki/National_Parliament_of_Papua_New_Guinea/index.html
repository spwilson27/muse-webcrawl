National Parliament of Papua New Guinea
Type: Unicameral legislature
Seats: 118
Meeting place: Parliament House, Waigani, Port Moresby
Electoral system: Limited preferential voting

The National Parliament of Papua New Guinea is the unicameral legislature of Papua New Guinea. It is based at Parliament House in Waigani, a suburb of the capital Port Moresby, in a distinctive building opened in 1984 whose design draws on the traditional haus tambaran (spirit house) architecture of the Sepik region.

Composition

The Parliament has 118 members, expanded from 111 ahead of the 2022 elections with the creation of new open electorates. Members are elected from single-member electorates — provincial electorates and open (district) electorates — using limited preferential voting, in which voters rank three candidates. Parliamentary terms are five years.

Functions

The Parliament elects the Prime Minister after each general election and can remove the government through votes of no confidence, which have been a frequent feature of Papua New Guinean politics. The head of state, the monarch of Papua New Guinea, is represented by the Governor-General, who formally opens Parliament and assents to legislation.
