Meiko (software)

Meiko
TypeComputer-aided design / plant design software
DeveloperMeiko (CAD/engineering software)

Meiko is software associated with computer-aided design used in engineering and plant construction contexts. Plant design packages of this kind support three-dimensional modelling of industrial facilities, piping layout, and the generation of construction drawings and material lists from a shared model.

Such software belongs to the generation of CAD systems that moved industrial design from 2D drafting to integrated 3D plant models, allowing clash detection between equipment, piping, and structures before construction. Modern plant design environments also integrate process and instrumentation data with the geometric model.
