The Korps Commandotroepen (KCT) is the special forces unit of the Royal Netherlands Army. The corps traces its origins to No. 2 (Dutch) Troop of No. 10 (Inter-Allied) Commando, formed in the United Kingdom on 22 March 1942, and to the Korps Insulinde in the Dutch East Indies. It is headquartered at the Engelbrecht van Nassau Barracks in Roosendaal and is famous for its green beret.

Role and tasks

The KCT is tasked with special reconnaissance, direct action, and military assistance, and supports Dutch crisis-management operations worldwide. Operators typically serve in small teams and are trained in parachuting (including high-altitude insertion), diving, mountain warfare, and operations in arctic and jungle environments. The unit has deployed to Bosnia, Kosovo, Iraq, Afghanistan (including under Task Force Viper and the International Security Assistance Force), and Mali.

Selection and training

Candidates undergo a demanding selection and an elementary commando course (ECO) culminating in a final week of severe testing; those who pass receive the green beret and then complete an extended advanced commando course lasting roughly a year, followed by specialist qualifications. The corps' motto is Nunc aut Nunquam ("Now or Never").
