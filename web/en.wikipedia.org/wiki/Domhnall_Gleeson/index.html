Domhnall Gleeson

Domhnall Gleeson (born 12 May 1983) is an Irish actor and writer. He is the son of actor Brendan Gleeson. He first gained attention for his role as Bill Weasley in the Harry Potter film series (2010–2011).

Domhnall Gleeson
Born: 12 May 1983, Dublin, Ireland
Occupation: Actor, writer
Years active: 2001–present
Father: Brendan Gleeson

Career

Gleeson starred in the romantic drama About Time (2013), the science fiction film Ex Machina (2014), the western The Revenant (2015), and played General Hux in the Star Wars sequel trilogy (2015–2019). In 2015 he appeared in four Academy Award-nominated films: Ex Machina, Brooklyn, The Revenant and Star Wars: The Force Awakens.

On television he starred in the Black Mirror episode "Be Right Back" (2013), the HBO series Run (2020), and The Patient (2022). He received a Golden Globe nomination for his role as Sam Fortner in The Patient. He also co-created and starred in Frank of Ireland (2021) with his brother Brian Gleeson.
