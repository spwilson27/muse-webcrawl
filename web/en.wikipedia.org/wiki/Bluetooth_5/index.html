Bluetooth 5

The Bluetooth wordmark

Bluetooth 5 is the fifth major version of Bluetooth technology, announced by the Bluetooth Special Interest Group (SIG) on 16 June 2016 and formally adopted in December 2016. Its new features are mainly focused on the low-energy mode, Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), and on emerging Internet of Things applications.

Features

Compared with Bluetooth 4.2, Bluetooth 5 provides:

  • Twice the speed — a 2 Mbit/s physical layer for BLE, reducing transmission time and energy per bit.
  • Four times the range — a coded physical layer using forward error correction, trading data rate for link budget and reach.
  • Eight times the broadcasting capacity — advertising extensions that enlarge the payload of beacon packets from 31 bytes to up to 255 bytes, enabling richer beacon and location services.
  • Improved coexistence with other wireless technologies such as LTE.

Revisions

Bluetooth 5.1 (2019) added direction finding using angle of arrival and angle of departure, enabling centimetre-level positioning. Bluetooth 5.2 (2020) introduced LE Audio with the LC3 codec and Auracast broadcast audio. Bluetooth 5.3 and 5.4 added periodic advertising enhancements and encrypted advertising data.

Adoption

Support appeared in flagship smartphones from 2017 onward, including the Samsung Galaxy S8 and Apple's iPhone 8 and iPhone X, and rapidly became standard in laptops, wearables, and IoT devices.
