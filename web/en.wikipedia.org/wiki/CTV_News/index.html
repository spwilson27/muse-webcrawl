CTV News

CTV News is the news division of the CTV Television Network in Canada, owned by Bell Media. It produces national and local newscasts for CTV stations across the country, the 24-hour channels CTV News Channel and CP24, and digital news services.

National programming

The flagship national broadcast is CTV National News, anchored for decades by Lloyd Robertson until 2011, then by Lisa LaFlamme until 2022, and subsequently by Omar Sachedina. Other national programs have included the morning show Your Morning, the political program Question Period, and the long-running investigative series W5, which aired from 1966 until 2024.

Local news

CTV-owned stations produce local newscasts in most major Canadian markets, generally at noon, 5 p.m., 6 p.m., and 11:30 p.m. CTV News is one of the two principal national television news organizations in Canada, alongside CBC News, and its newscasts frequently lead national ratings.
