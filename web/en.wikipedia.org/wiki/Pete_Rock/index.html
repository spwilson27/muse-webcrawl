Peter Phillips (born June 21, 1970), better known by his stage name Pete Rock, is an American record producer, DJ and rapper. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest hip hop producers of all time, celebrated for his soulful, jazz-inflected sampling style, heavy drums and signature horn arrangements. He rose to prominence as one half of the duo Pete Rock & CL Smooth.

Career

Raised in Mount Vernon, New York, Pete Rock began as a DJ and made his name in the early 1990s. With rapper CL Smooth he released the EP All Souled Out (1991) and the albums Mecca and the Soul Brother (1992) and The Main Ingredient (1994). The single "They Reminisce Over You (T.R.O.Y.)", written in memory of Trouble T-Roy of Heavy D & the Boyz, became one of hip hop's most revered songs.

Production work

After the duo split, Pete Rock produced for artists including Nas ("The World Is Yours"), Run-DMC, Public Enemy, A Tribe Called Quest and Rakim, and released solo and instrumental albums including Soul Survivor (1998) and the PeteStrumentals series. His chopped-soul production style influenced a generation of producers, including J Dilla and Kanye West.
