Women's empowerment

Women's empowerment (or female empowerment) may be defined in several ways, including accepting women's viewpoints, making an effort to seek them and raising the status of women through education, awareness, literacy, and training. Women's empowerment equips and allows women to make life-determining decisions through the different societal problems.

Dimensions

Empowerment is commonly discussed across several dimensions: economic empowerment (access to employment, credit, land and markets), political empowerment (voting, representation and leadership), social and educational empowerment, and legal empowerment through rights and protection from discrimination and violence.

Policy and measurement

Gender equality and women's empowerment form Sustainable Development Goal 5 of the United Nations. Measurement tools include the Global Gender Gap Index and indices of women's economic participation. Development programmes such as microfinance, girls' education initiatives and quotas for political representation are frequently framed as empowerment interventions, though scholars debate whether they achieve structural change.
