Cat Scratch Fever is the third studio album by American rock musician Ted Nugent. It was released on May 13, 1977, by Epic Records. The album is Nugent's most successful, going multi-platinum and featuring the hit title track, which remains one of his signature songs. Vocalist Derek St. Holmes returned for this album after departing during the sessions of the previous record, Free-for-All.

Reception

Cat Scratch Fever peaked at No. 17 on the Billboard 200 chart and was certified triple platinum by the RIAA. The title track reached No. 30 on the Billboard Hot 100 and is widely regarded as a hard rock staple; its riff is one of the most recognizable in 1970s rock. The album solidified Nugent's status as a major arena-rock act in the United States in the late 1970s.
