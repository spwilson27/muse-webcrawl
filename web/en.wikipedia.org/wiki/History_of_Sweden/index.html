History of Sweden

The history of Sweden begins with post-glacial settlement and the Viking Age (c. 800–1050), when Swedes — the Rus' among them — traded and raided across eastern Europe. A unified Christian kingdom emerged in the Middle Ages.

Great power era

Under Gustavus Adolphus and the Vasa kings, Sweden became a Baltic great power in the 17th century, intervening in the Thirty Years' War. Defeat in the Great Northern War (1700–1721) ended its empire. Finland was lost to Russia in 1809; Norway was in union with Sweden 1814–1905.

Modern Sweden

Nineteenth-century industrialisation, emigration, and democratisation led to the 20th-century welfare state. Sweden remained neutral in both world wars and joined the EU in 1995 and NATO in 2024.
