Big Bear Lake

Big Bear Lake is a reservoir in the San Bernardino Mountains of San Bernardino County, California, United States, and also the name of the city on its southern shore. The lake is approximately 7 miles (11 km) long with a shoreline of about 22 miles, lying at an elevation of about 6,750 feet (2,060 m).

History

The lake was formed by the construction of the Big Bear Dam, originally built in 1884 as a single-arch rock dam for irrigation and enlarged in 1912. The valley was named for its once-large population of grizzly bears, and the area became a popular resort destination in the early twentieth century.

Recreation

Big Bear Lake is a year-round resort area. In summer it offers boating, fishing for rainbow trout and bass, and hiking; nearby ski resorts Snow Summit and Bear Mountain provide winter sports. The lake community hosts the Big Bear Lake International Film Festival and attracts visitors from the greater Los Angeles area, about 100 miles to the west.
