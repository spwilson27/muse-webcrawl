Finding Nemo

Finding Nemo is a 2003 American computer-animated comedy-drama adventure film produced by Pixar Animation Studios for Walt Disney Pictures. Directed by Andrew Stanton with co-direction by Lee Unkrich, the film stars the voices of Albert Brooks, Ellen DeGeneres, Alexander Gould, and Willem Dafoe.

Plot

The film tells the story of an overprotective clownfish named Marlin who, along with a forgetful regal blue tang named Dory, searches for his missing son Nemo, who has been captured by a diver and placed in a fish tank in a dentist's office in Sydney.

Reception

Finding Nemo won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature and was the highest-grossing animated film at the time, earning over $940 million worldwide. A sequel, Finding Dory, was released in 2016.
