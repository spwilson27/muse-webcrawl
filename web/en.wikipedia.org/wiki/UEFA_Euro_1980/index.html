The 1980 UEFA European Football Championship, commonly referred to as UEFA Euro 1980, was the sixth UEFA European Championship, held in Italy between 11 and 22 June 1980. It was the first championship to feature eight teams in the final tournament, and the first in which the host nation qualified automatically.

Tournament summary

The eight teams were divided into two groups of four. The group winners advanced directly to the final, while the runners-up contested a third-place play-off. West Germany topped Group 1 ahead of Czechoslovakia, while Belgium surprisingly won Group 2 ahead of hosts Italy. In the final at the Stadio Olimpico in Rome, West Germany defeated Belgium 2–1, with Horst Hrubesch scoring both German goals, including a late header. Czechoslovakia beat Italy on penalties in the third-place match.

Venues

Matches were played in Rome, Milan, Naples, and Turin. The tournament is often remembered for low attendances at some matches and incidents of hooliganism, but it marked the beginning of the modern expanded format of the European Championship.
