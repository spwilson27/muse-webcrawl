André Lotterer

André Lotterer (born 19 November 1981) is a German professional racing driver. He is best known for his success in endurance racing, including three overall victories at the 24 Hours of Le Mans with Audi, and for winning the Super Formula championship in Japan and the Super GT title.

Racing career

Lotterer began his career in karting before moving into single-seaters in the German Formula BMW and Formula 3 series. He spent much of his career based in Japan, where he competed in Formula Nippon (later Super Formula) and Super GT, winning the 2011 Formula Nippon championship and Super GT titles in 2006 and 2009.

As a factory Audi driver in the FIA World Endurance Championship, Lotterer won the 24 Hours of Le Mans in 2011, 2012 and 2014 alongside Marcel Fässler and Benoît Tréluyer, and the three shared the 2012 World Endurance Drivers' Championship. He later raced for Porsche in Formula E and in the World Endurance Championship, and made a single Formula One appearance for Caterham at the 2014 Belgian Grand Prix.
