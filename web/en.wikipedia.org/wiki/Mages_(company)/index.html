Mages (company)

Mages. Inc. (stylised as MAGES.) is a Japanese video game and music company, and a subsidiary of Colopl. It is best known for the Science Adventure series of visual novels, including Chaos;Head, Steins;Gate, Robotics;Notes and Chaos;Child, many of which have been adapted into anime, manga and stage productions.

History

The company originated as 5pb. Inc., founded in 2005 by Chiyomaru Shikura, initially as a music and sound production company. Its games division developed the Science Adventure series beginning with Chaos;Head in 2008; Steins;Gate (2009) became a major critical and commercial success. In 2011 5pb. merged with the agency AG-ONE to form Mages. Inc. In 2019 the company was acquired by the mobile game developer Colopl.

Works

Beyond visual novels, Mages. produces music — including releases by the fictional idol group Zwei and artist Ayane — and develops licensed games such as adaptations of The Quintessential Quintuplets and Re:Zero. The Science Adventure series shares a loosely unified setting and recurring themes of science, conspiracy and time travel.
