The Tokyo Game Show (東京ゲームショウ, Tōkyō Gēmu Shō), commonly known as TGS, is a video game trade fair and convention held annually in September at the Makuhari Messe convention center in Chiba, Japan. It is presented by the Computer Entertainment Supplier's Association (CESA) and Nikkei Business Publications, and is one of the largest video game exhibitions in Asia, alongside gamescom in Europe.

History

The first Tokyo Game Show was held in 1996. From 1996 to 2002, the show was held twice a year: once in the spring and once in the autumn (in Tokyo and later Makuhari). From 2002 onward the show has been held once a year, in autumn. The first two days of the event are reserved for industry and press visitors, with the final two days open to the general public.

Attendance has grown substantially over the years, regularly exceeding 200,000 visitors, with a record of around 298,000 attendees in 2018. The 2020 and 2021 editions were held online due to the COVID-19 pandemic, before returning as a physical event in 2022.

Exhibitions

The show features booths from major Japanese and international publishers — historically including Sony Interactive Entertainment, Bandai Namco, Square Enix, Sega, Capcom, Konami and Microsoft — alongside areas for indie games, mobile gaming, esports, virtual reality, and game schools. New consoles and flagship titles have frequently been announced or demonstrated at the event.
