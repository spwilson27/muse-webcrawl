Transavia Airlines C.V., trading as Transavia and legally incorporated as Transavia Airlines C.V., is a Dutch low-cost airline and a wholly owned subsidiary of the Air France–KLM group. Its main base is Amsterdam Airport Schiphol, with other bases at Rotterdam The Hague Airport and Eindhoven Airport. Transavia was the second-largest airline in the Netherlands after KLM, carrying millions of passengers annually to leisure destinations around the Mediterranean and Europe.

History

Transavia was established on 14 October 1965 under the name Transavia Limburg N.V. and began operations on 17 November 1966 with a flight between Amsterdam and Naples. It initially operated charter flights for tour operators, and from the 1990s expanded into scheduled services. KLM acquired a controlling interest in 1991 and full ownership in 2003, and Transavia became part of the Air France–KLM group after the 2004 merger. A French sister carrier, Transavia France, began operations in 2007 from Paris-Orly.

Destinations

Transavia operates scheduled and charter services to more than 100 destinations in Europe, North Africa and the Middle East, with a strong focus on leisure destinations in Spain, Portugal, Greece, Italy, France and Turkey, as well as winter sports and city destinations.

Fleet

The fleet has long consisted mainly of Boeing 737 aircraft, principally the 737-700 and 737-800. In the 2020s Transavia began transitioning to the Airbus A320neo family under the Air France–KLM group's fleet renewal order, with the first A321neo entering service in late 2023.
