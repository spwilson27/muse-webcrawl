List of awards and nominations received by Jennifer Lawrence

American actress Jennifer Lawrence (born 1990) has received numerous accolades, including an Academy Award, a British Academy Film Award, three Golden Globe Awards, and a Screen Actors Guild Award. She broke out with Winter's Bone (2010), for which she received her first Oscar nomination aged 20, and won the Academy Award for Best Actress for Silver Linings Playbook (2012), becoming the second-youngest winner in the category.

Other notable accolades

  • Venice Film Festival — Marcello Mastroianni Award for Best Young Actress for The Burning Plain (2008).
  • Critics' Choice Awards — multiple wins, including Best Actress in a Comedy and Best Actress in an Action Movie (for The Hunger Games).
  • Independent Spirit Awards — Best Female Lead for Silver Linings Playbook.
  • MTV Movie Awards — several wins for the Hunger Games films, including Best Female Performance and Best Hero.
  • People's Choice Awards and Teen Choice Awards — multiple wins across drama and action categories.
  • Named to Time's 100 most influential people list (2013) and for a period the world's highest-paid actress in Forbes rankings (2015–2016).
