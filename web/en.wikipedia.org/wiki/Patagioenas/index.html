Patagioenas

Patagioenas is a genus of New World pigeons in the family Columbidae. Its species were formerly placed in the genus Columba, but genetic and morphological studies led to their separation into Patagioenas, a reinstatement of a name proposed by Reichenbach in 1853.

Species

The genus contains around seventeen species distributed across North, Central, and South America. They include the band-tailed pigeon (Patagioenas fasciata), the white-crowned pigeon (P. leucocephala), the scaly-naped pigeon (P. squamosa), the picazuro pigeon (P. picazuro), and the red-billed pigeon (P. flavirostris).

These are generally large, robust pigeons of forests, woodlands, and agricultural land, feeding mainly on fruit, seeds, and grain.
