Crown Sydney, also known as One Barangaroo, is a skyscraper in Barangaroo, New South Wales, Australia. At 271.3 metres (890 feet), it is the tallest building in Sydney and the fourth tallest in Australia. The tower comprises a hotel, residential apartments, and a casino operated by Crown Resorts.

Development

The project originated in 2012 when Crown's James Packer proposed a hotel and high-roller casino resort on the Barangaroo waterfront, a former container terminal being redeveloped on the western edge of Sydney's central business district. The design by WilkinsonEyre features a twisting, sculptural form inspired by three petals. Construction began in 2016 and the tower topped out in 2020.

Opening and licensing controversy

The hotel and restaurants opened on 28 December 2020, but the casino could not open because the NSW Independent Liquor & Gaming Authority inquiry led by Patricia Bergin found Crown unsuitable to hold a casino licence, following revelations about money laundering and links to junket operators. After corporate reforms and a change of ownership — Crown was acquired by Blackstone in 2022 — the casino was granted a conditional licence and began gaming operations in August 2022.

Facilities

The resort includes the Crown Towers hotel, luxury residences, several restaurants and bars, a spa, and members-only gaming salons. Its VIP-focused casino has no poker machines, in accordance with its licence arrangements.
