Bethesda Game Studios (BGS) is an American video game developer and a studio of Bethesda Softworks based in Rockville, Maryland. The company was established in 2001 as the development unit of Bethesda Softworks, and is best known for its open-world role-playing games in The Elder Scrolls and Fallout series.

History

Bethesda Game Studios was spun out of Bethesda Softworks in 2001, beginning with the team that developed The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind (2002). Under game director Todd Howard, the studio produced Oblivion (2006), Fallout 3 (2008), Skyrim (2011) — one of the best-selling video games of all time — Fallout 4 (2015), the multiplayer title Fallout 76 (2018), and the science-fiction game Starfield (2023), its first new franchise in over two decades. The studio expanded with additional offices in Montreal (2015), Austin, and Dallas.

The studio's games are built on its proprietary Creation Engine and are noted for their large explorable worlds, simulation detail, and strong support for user modification ("modding"). Following Microsoft's acquisition of ZeniMax Media in 2021, Bethesda Game Studios became part of Xbox Game Studios.
