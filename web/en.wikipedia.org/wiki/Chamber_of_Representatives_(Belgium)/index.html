The Chamber of Representatives (Dutch: Kamer van volksvertegenwoordigers; French: Chambre des représentants) is one of the two chambers of the bicameral Federal Parliament of Belgium, the other being the Senate. It holds the bulk of legislative power at the federal level.

Composition

The Chamber has 150 members elected by proportional representation in 11 electoral districts for five-year terms. Members are divided into Dutch-language and French-language groups, a division relevant for certain special-majority laws.

Powers

The Chamber alone exercises political control over the federal government, including votes of confidence and no confidence, and holds primary authority over budgetary matters and naturalization. Since the sixth state reform, the Senate no longer shares most ordinary legislative powers.
