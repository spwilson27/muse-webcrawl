The Parent Trap (1998 film)

The Parent Trap is a 1998 American family comedy film directed by Nancy Meyers in her directorial debut. A remake of the 1961 Disney film, it stars Lindsay Lohan in her feature debut as identical twins, with Dennis Quaid and Natasha Richardson as their parents.

DirectorNancy Meyers
StarringLindsay Lohan, Dennis Quaid, Natasha Richardson
Release date29 July 1998
Box officeAbout $92 million

Plot

Hallie Parker and Annie James, twin sisters separated at birth, meet at a summer camp in Maine. They switch lives — Hallie going to London, Annie to a Napa Valley vineyard — and scheme to reunite their parents before their father marries the scheming Meredith Blake.

Reception

The film was a commercial success and received positive reviews, with Lohan's dual performance widely praised. It has since become a beloved family classic and a frequent point of nostalgia for its generation of viewers.
