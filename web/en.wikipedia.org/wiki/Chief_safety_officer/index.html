Chief safety officer

AbbreviationCSO
DomainOccupational safety, product safety, public safety

A chief safety officer (CSO) is a senior executive responsible for an organization's safety policies and performance, covering areas such as occupational health and safety, product safety, process safety, or passenger and public safety depending on the industry.

Responsibilities

The chief safety officer develops safety management systems, oversees incident investigation and reporting, ensures compliance with safety regulations, and promotes a safety culture. The title is common in railways, aviation, energy, mining and large technology firms, where CSOs may oversee trust-and-safety functions.

Related roles

The abbreviation CSO is shared with the chief security officer and chief strategy officer, so organizations often spell out the title in full.
