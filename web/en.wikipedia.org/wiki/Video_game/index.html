Video game

Video game
DefinitionElectronic game involving interaction with a user interface or device
IndustryLarger than film and music combined (revenue)
[Image: A player holding a controller before a glowing game screen in a dark room]

A video game is an electronic game that involves interaction with a user interface or input device to generate visual feedback. From Pong (1972) and Space Invaders through consoles, PCs and mobile phones, video games became the largest entertainment industry by revenue.

Culture

Games span genres from puzzles to vast open worlds; esports fill arenas, streaming platforms broadcast play, and game engines power film and simulation. Chess migrated online; game studies examine narrative, addiction and learning (see Learning). Modding and indie development democratised creation alongside giant studios.
