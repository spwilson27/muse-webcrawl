Manufacturer: Blue Origin

Propellant: LOX / liquefied natural gas (methane)

The BE-4 (Blue Engine 4) is a methane-fuelled rocket engine developed by Blue Origin. It uses an oxygen-rich staged combustion cycle burning liquid oxygen and liquefied natural gas, chosen in part for reusability and reduced coking compared with kerosene.

Applications

Seven BE-4 engines power the first stage of Blue Origin's New Glenn heavy-lift launch vehicle. Two BE-4s power the first stage of United Launch Alliance's Vulcan Centaur, which first flew in January 2024, replacing the Russian RD-180 used on Atlas V.

Development

Development began in the early 2010s; full-scale hot-fire testing began in 2017. Production engines are built at Blue Origin's factory in Huntsville, Alabama, and tested in West Texas and at Marshall Space Flight Center.
