Formula Kite
Type: One-design kiteboarding class
Equipment: Hydrofoil board, ram-air kite
Governing body: International Kiteboarding Association
Olympic debut: Paris 2024

Formula Kite is a one-design class of kiteboarding racing in which competitors ride hydrofoil boards powered by foil (ram-air) kites. The class is administered by the International Kiteboarding Association under World Sailing and was selected as an Olympic sailing event for the Paris 2024 Games.

Equipment and racing

Sailors use registered production hydrofoil boards and kites in a small range of sizes chosen according to wind strength. The hydrofoil lifts the board clear of the water, greatly reducing drag and allowing speeds well above 30 knots. Races are short-course fleet races sailed around marks, with series decided by low-point scoring and medal races.

Olympic inclusion

Kiteboarding had earlier appeared at the 2018 Youth Olympic Games in a twin-tip racing format. Formula Kite made its full Olympic debut at Paris 2024, with events for men and women held off Marseille; the first champions were Valentin Bontus of Austria and Ellie Aldridge of Great Britain.
