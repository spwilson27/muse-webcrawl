French National Centre for Scientific Research

The French National Centre for Scientific Research (French: Centre national de la recherche scientifique, CNRS) is the largest French public scientific research organisation and one of the largest fundamental science agencies in the world. It is an institution under the authority of the French Ministry of Higher Education, Research and Innovation.

Organisation

Founded in 1939, the CNRS employs tens of thousands of staff — researchers, engineers and technicians — organised into ten national institutes covering fields from mathematics and physics to the humanities and social sciences. Most of its research units are joint units operated together with French universities. The CNRS ranks among the world's leading research institutions by publication output.
