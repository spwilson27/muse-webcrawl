Seven Sisters, Sussex

The Seven Sisters are a series of chalk sea cliffs on the English Channel coast of East Sussex, in south-east England. They form part of the South Downs and run between the towns of Seaford and Eastbourne, between Cuckmere Haven in the west and Birling Gap in the east. The cliffs lie within the Seven Sisters Country Park and the South Downs National Park.

The cliffs

Despite the name, the cliff line is made up of eight distinct peaks and seven intervening dips: Haven Brow, Short Brow, Rough Brow, Brass Point, Flagstaff Point, Bailey's Hill, Michel Dean and Went Hill Brow. The chalk cliffs reach heights of up to about 77 metres (253 ft) at Beachy Head just to the east. Because the soft chalk is continually eroded by the sea, the cliffs remain a brilliant white, unlike the more famous White Cliffs of Dover, which have become partially vegetated.

In film and television

The Seven Sisters are frequently used as a stand-in for the Dover cliffs in film and television, appearing in productions such as Atonement (2007) and the BBC series Luther. The coastguard cottages at Cuckmere Haven, overlooking the cliffs, are among the most photographed views in Sussex.

Access

The South Downs Way long-distance footpath follows the cliff tops, and the country park visitor centre is at Exceat, near Seaford. The cliffs are a Site of Special Scientific Interest noted for their chalk grassland flora and fauna.
