Harold Scott MacDonald Coxeter

H. S. M. Coxeter
Born: 9 February 1907, London, England
Died: 31 March 2003, Toronto, Canada
Fields: Geometry
Known for: Coxeter groups, regular polytopes

Harold Scott MacDonald "Donald" Coxeter (9 February 1907 – 31 March 2003) was a British-Canadian geometer, regarded as one of the greatest geometers of the 20th century.

Work

Coxeter worked chiefly on regular polytopes and higher-dimensional geometry. His book Regular Polytopes (1948) remains a classic, and the symmetry groups known as Coxeter groups, described by Coxeter diagrams, are named after him. He also collaborated with M. C. Escher, whose tessellation work drew on Coxeter's mathematics.

Life

Educated at Cambridge, Coxeter moved to the University of Toronto in 1936, where he remained for the rest of his career. He wrote twelve books and was a Fellow of the Royal Society.
