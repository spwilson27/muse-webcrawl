Bern

Bern's medieval old town occupies a steep-sided peninsula in a loop of the Aare.

Bern is the de facto capital of Switzerland and the capital of the canton of Bern. The fifth-most populous Swiss city, with around 134,000 inhabitants in the city and some 420,000 in its agglomeration, it is the seat of the Swiss federal government: the Federal Assembly and Federal Council meet in the Federal Palace (Bundeshaus). Switzerland has no capital in law; Bern was chosen as the federal city in 1848.

History

According to legend, Duke Berchtold V of Zähringen founded the city in 1191 and named it after the first animal he hunted in the surrounding woods — a bear (Bär), which remains Bern's heraldic animal, with live bears kept in the city's bear pits since the 16th century (now the spacious Bear Park on the Aare). Bern became a free imperial city and in 1353 joined the Swiss Confederacy, growing into its largest city-state north of the Alps and acquiring a broad subject territory. Much of the sandstone old town was rebuilt after a great fire in 1405.

The old town

Bern's Altstadt, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1983, is one of the best-preserved medieval city centres in Europe, with some six kilometres of arcaded walkways (Lauben), Renaissance fountains topped by painted figures, the great Gothic Bern Minster with Switzerland's tallest church tower, and the Zytglogge, the 13th-century clock tower whose astronomical clock and mechanical figures draw crowds each hour. Albert Einstein lived in Bern while working at the patent office in 1905, his annus mirabilis, when he published the papers founding special relativity; his flat on Kramgasse is now the Einsteinhaus museum. The Federal Palace, completed in 1902, dominates the southern edge of the old town.

Culture and transport

Bern is home to the University of Bern, the Kunstmuseum and the Zentrum Paul Klee, and to the Universal Postal Union's headquarters, reflecting its role as a modest but significant administrative capital. The Aare, whose turquoise waters loop around the old town, is famously used in summer by swimmers who float downriver through the city.
