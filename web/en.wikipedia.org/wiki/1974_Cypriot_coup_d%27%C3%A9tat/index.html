1974 Cypriot coup d'état

Date15 July 1974
LocationCyprus
ResultPresident Makarios III deposed; Turkish invasion followed

The 1974 Cypriot coup d'état was a military coup staged by the Cypriot National Guard and the Greek military junta of 1967–1974. It deposed the Cypriot President, Archbishop Makarios III, and installed Nikos Sampson as president.

Events

On 15 July 1974, National Guard units, led by Greek officers and aided by the EOKA-B paramilitary group, attacked the presidential palace. Makarios narrowly escaped and fled abroad. The coup aimed at enosis — union of Cyprus with Greece.

Aftermath

Five days later, on 20 July 1974, Turkey invaded Cyprus, citing its rights as a guarantor power. Turkish forces occupied the northern part of the island, displacing some 200,000 Greek Cypriots. The coup collapsed within days, and the Greek junta fell, but Cyprus remains divided to this day.
