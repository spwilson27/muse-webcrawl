Perseverance (rover)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Perseverance descending on the sky crane, February 2021.

Perseverance is a NASA Mars rover launched on 30 July 2020 and landed in Jezero Crater on 18 February 2021 as the centrepiece of the Mars 2020 mission. Based on the Curiosity design, it carries improved wheels, cameras and instruments to search for signs of ancient microbial life in a crater that once held a lake and river delta.

Perseverance is the first leg of a planned Mars sample return: it seals drilled rock cores in titanium tubes for potential pickup by later missions. Its MOXIE experiment produced oxygen from the carbon-dioxide atmosphere, and its companion, the Ingenuity helicopter, made the first powered flights on another planet — 72 flights between 2021 and 2024, far beyond its five-flight technology demonstration. The rover's landing was filmed in high definition by cameras on the spacecraft and descent stage.
