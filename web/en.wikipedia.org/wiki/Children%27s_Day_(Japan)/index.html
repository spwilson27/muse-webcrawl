Children's Day (Japan)

Official nameKodomo no Hi (こどもの日)
Observed byJapan
DateMay 5
SymbolsKoinobori windsocks, kashiwa mochi, kabuto helmets

Children's Day (子供の日, Kodomo no Hi) is a Japanese national holiday that takes place annually on May 5 and is the final day of Golden Week. It is a day set aside to respect children's personalities and celebrate their happiness.

History

The day was originally the festival of Tango no Sekku, one of the five seasonal festivals, and was associated with boys, complementing the Girls' Day (Hinamatsuri) on March 3. It was designated a national holiday by the Japanese government in 1948.

Traditions

Families fly koinobori — carp-shaped windsocks symbolizing strength and perseverance — and display samurai dolls and helmets (kabuto). Traditional foods include kashiwa mochi, rice cakes wrapped in oak leaves, and chimaki.
