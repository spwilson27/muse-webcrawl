Johnny Stompanato

Born: John Stompanato Jr., October 10, 1925, Woodstock, Illinois, U.S.
Died: April 4, 1958 (aged 32), Beverly Hills, California, U.S.
Cause of death: Stab wound
Known for: Bodyguard and enforcer for Mickey Cohen; boyfriend of Lana Turner

John Stompanato Jr. (October 10, 1925 – April 4, 1958) was an American United States Marine, bodyguard, and enforcer for gangster Mickey Cohen. He was also in a relationship with actress Lana Turner, and was stabbed to death by Turner's daughter, Cheryl Crane, during a domestic dispute in 1958—a killing that became one of the most notorious Hollywood scandals of the era.

Early life and career

Stompanato was born in Woodstock, Illinois, to Italian immigrant parents. He served in the U.S. Marine Corps during World War II, seeing action in the Pacific. After the war he settled in Los Angeles, where his good looks and underworld connections brought him into the orbit of mobster Mickey Cohen, for whom he worked as a bodyguard and enforcer. He also operated a gift shop in Westwood as a front.

Relationship with Lana Turner and death

In 1957 Stompanato began dating film star Lana Turner. The relationship was reportedly abusive and tumultuous. On the evening of April 4, 1958, during a violent argument at Turner's Beverly Hills home, Turner’s 14-year-old daughter Cheryl Crane stabbed Stompanato with a kitchen knife; he died at the scene. A coroner's inquest ruled the killing justifiable homicide, concluding that Crane had acted to protect her mother. The case generated enormous publicity and later inspired novels, films, and songs.
