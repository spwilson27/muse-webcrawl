Energy policy of the European Union

The energy policy of the European Union focuses on energy security, sustainability, and the integration of national energy markets into a single internal market. The EU is one of the world's largest energy importers, historically dependent on imports of oil and natural gas, including from Russia.

Policy framework

The Treaty of Lisbon gave energy a legal basis in the treaties, with aims including ensuring the functioning of the energy market, security of supply, energy efficiency, and interconnection of networks. The European Green Deal and the "Fit for 55" package set targets for reducing greenhouse gas emissions, expanding renewable energy, and improving efficiency. Following Russia's invasion of Ukraine in 2022, the REPowerEU plan sought to end dependence on Russian fossil fuels and accelerate the clean energy transition.
