Founded: 1982
Founder: David Brower
Headquarters: Berkeley, California, United States
Type: Nonprofit environmental organisation

Earth Island Institute is a non-profit environmental organisation founded in 1982 by the veteran conservationist David Brower in Berkeley, California. It acts as an incubator and fiscal sponsor for grassroots environmental projects, providing administrative, legal and fundraising support so that activists can focus on campaigning.

Earth Island Institute is based at the David Brower Center in Berkeley, California.

History

David Brower, the first executive director of the Sierra Club and founder of Friends of the Earth, established Earth Island Institute after leaving earlier organisations, intending it to nurture new environmental initiatives rather than run a single centralised campaign. Its network model now sponsors dozens of projects at any given time.

Projects and campaigns

Well-known projects have included the International Marine Mammal Project, which campaigned for "dolphin-safe" tuna and helped establish labelling standards, and efforts on plastic pollution, fossil-fuel resistance and food sustainability. Earth Island publishes the quarterly Earth Island Journal, an environmental magazine combining investigative reporting and commentary.

Brower Youth Awards

Since 2000 the institute has presented the Brower Youth Awards, recognising young environmental leaders in North America, named in honour of its founder.
