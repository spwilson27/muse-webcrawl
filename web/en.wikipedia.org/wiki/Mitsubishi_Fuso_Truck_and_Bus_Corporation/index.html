Mitsubishi Fuso Truck and Bus Corporation

The Mitsubishi Fuso Truck and Bus Corporation (MFTBC) is a Japanese manufacturer of trucks, buses, and industrial engines headquartered in Kawasaki, Kanagawa. The Fuso name was first used in 1932 for a bus built at Mitsubishi's Kobe shipyard. The company became part of Daimler Truck (formerly Daimler AG), which holds the majority stake, with Mitsubishi companies holding the remainder.

Products

Best-known models include the light-duty Canter, the medium-duty Fighter, the heavy-duty Super Great, the Rosa minibus, and the Aero Bus series. The eCanter, launched in 2017, was among the first mass-produced all-electric light trucks.

History

The truck and bus operations originated in Mitsubishi Motors and were spun off as a separate corporation in 2003, after which DaimlerChrysler acquired control.
