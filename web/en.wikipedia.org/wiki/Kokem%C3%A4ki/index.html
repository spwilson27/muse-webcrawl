Kokemäki

Kokemäki (Swedish: Kumo) is a town and municipality in the Satakunta region of Finland, situated on the Kokemäenjoki river. It has a population of roughly 7,000 and is one of the oldest settlement areas in Finland, with archaeological evidence of continuous habitation reaching back to the Stone Age.

History

Kokemäki was an important prehistoric and medieval trading centre at the head of navigation on the Kokemäenjoki, Finland's fifth-longest river. Nearby Teljä was an Iron Age trading place mentioned in sagas. The parish is among the oldest in Finland, and the town received its municipal status in the nineteenth century.

Geography and economy

The municipality covers an agricultural landscape along the river valley, with industry, services and food production among its main employers. The Kokemäenjoki is noted for its salmon and whitefish fishing.
