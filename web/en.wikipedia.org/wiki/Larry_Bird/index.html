Larry Joe Bird (born December 7, 1956) is an American former professional basketball player, coach, and executive, widely regarded as one of the greatest basketball players of all time. Nicknamed "Larry Legend" and "the Hick from French Lick", he played his entire 13-season NBA career for the Boston Celtics.

Career

Drafted sixth overall in 1978 after starring at Indiana State, where he led the Sycamores to the 1979 NCAA final against Magic Johnson's Michigan State, Bird won three NBA championships with Boston (1981, 1984, 1986) and was named NBA Most Valuable Player three consecutive times (1984–1986). He was a 12-time All-Star and won a gold medal with the 1992 "Dream Team". After retiring, he coached the Indiana Pacers and later served as their president of basketball operations.
