Xi'an

Xi'an
Country: China
Province: Shaanxi
Population: approx. 13 million (municipality)
Known for: Terracotta Army; ancient capital

Xi'an is the capital of Shaanxi Province in northwestern China and one of the oldest cities in the country. Known historically as Chang'an, it served as the capital under several major dynasties, including the Western Han and Tang, and marked the eastern terminus of the Silk Road.

History

The Zhou, Qin, Han and Tang dynasties all ruled from the Xi'an area; Tang Chang'an was among the largest and most cosmopolitan cities in the world. In 1974 farmers discovered the Terracotta Army guarding the mausoleum of Qin Shi Huang near the city, now a UNESCO World Heritage Site, as are the Silk Roads corridors listed in 2014.

Sights

Landmarks include the Ming city walls, the Bell and Drum Towers, the Giant Wild Goose Pagoda and the Muslim Quarter. Today Xi'an is a major centre for education, aerospace, software and tourism. See also Terracotta Army.
