BMW Dixi

BMW Dixi
ManufacturerBMW (Fahrzeugfabrik Eisenach)
Production1928–1932
ClassSmall car

The BMW Dixi was the first car produced by BMW under that name, built from 1928 to 1932. When BMW bought the Fahrzeugfabrik Eisenach in 1928 it acquired production of the Dixi, a small car based on a licence-built version of the British Austin 7. The Dixi 3/15, later sold as the BMW 3/15, marked BMW's entry into automobile manufacturing.

Background

Eisenach had built cars under the Dixi brand since 1904. The Dixi DA-1 of 1927 was an Austin 7 built under licence. After the BMW takeover the car gained BMW badging, and an improved version, the 3/15 DA-2, appeared in 1929 with a wider body and four-wheel brakes. A sporting variant, the 3/15 DA-3 Wartburg, was BMW's first sports car and won its class in rallies.

Production

Around 25,000 examples of the 3/15 series were built before production ended in 1932, when BMW introduced its own design, the 3/20.
