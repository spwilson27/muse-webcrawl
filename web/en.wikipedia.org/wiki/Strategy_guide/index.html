Strategy guide

Strategy guides are instruction books that contain hints or complete solutions to specific video games. The line between strategy guides and walkthroughs is somewhat blurred, with the former often containing or being written in the style of the latter. Strategy guides are often published in print, both in book form and as articles in video game magazines. In cases of exceptionally popular game titles, guides may be sold through more mainstream publication channels, such as bookstores or even newsstands. Some publishers also sell E-Book versions on their websites.

Strategy guides marketed by individual game publishers such as Nintendo and Prima Games are usually created by their respective companies or licensed publishing partners. Guides for some major franchises have been published by Prima Games and Piggyback Interactive.

Contents

Typically, the contents of a strategy guide include:

  • Detailed gameplay information, for example maneuvers that are not detailed in the manual.
  • Complete maps of the game, which show the placement of all items (hidden and secret items included).
  • Detailed instructions of specific locations, including how to defeat a boss, or obtain a particular secret.
  • Puzzle solutions.
  • Details of enemies, including techniques for defeating individual enemies (especially bosses), the segment for minor enemies, and combat strategies.
  • Collectible items list and locations.
  • Cheats and game editing, although this is less common in official guides and often relegated to secret codes sections.
  • Walkthroughs to help players complete an entire game.

History

While printed strategy guides were once a major segment of the gaming publishing market, the rise of the Internet displaced many printed guides with freely available online walkthroughs and FAQs hosted on sites such as GameFAQs and IGN. This shift weakened sales of printed strategy guides through the 2000s and 2010s, though guides for major releases continue to be produced, often as collector's editions.
