British Rail Class 777

ManufacturerStadler Rail (Stadler METRO platform)
Built2018–2021
Number built53 units (777/0 and battery-equipped 777/1)
OperatorMerseyrail
OwnerLiverpool City Region Combined Authority
Configuration4 cars, articulated; 750 V DC third rail; some units fitted with batteries

The British Rail Class 777 is a class of electric multiple unit trains built by Stadler Rail for the Merseyrail network in the Liverpool City Region, England. Ordered in December 2016 as part of the "Metro" train family, the 53 units replaced the Class 507 and Class 508 trains dating from the late 1970s, and are notable for being owned by the Liverpool City Region Combined Authority rather than a rolling stock leasing company.

Design

The four-car articulated trains feature walk-through interiors, wide doors with retractable steps that align with platform edges for level boarding, air conditioning, free Wi-Fi, charging points and space for bicycles and wheelchairs. They operate from the 750 V DC third rail used by Merseyrail. Seven units (subclass 777/1) are fitted with battery traction equipment (classified as IPEMU — independently powered electric multiple units), enabling them to run beyond the electrified network; battery operation was used to extend services to the new Headbolt Lane station in Kirkby, which opened in October 2023.

Entry into service

Testing and driver training began in 2020, though the programme was delayed by the COVID-19 pandemic, flooding damage to a stored unit, and industrial disputes. The first units entered passenger service in January 2023, and the fleet progressively took over Merseyrail services during 2023–2024 as the Class 507/508 fleet was withdrawn.
