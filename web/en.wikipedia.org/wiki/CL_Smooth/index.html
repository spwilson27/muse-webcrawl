CL Smooth

Birth name: Corey Brent Penn Sr.
Born: 8 October 1968, New Rochelle, New York, United States
Genres: Hip hop, East Coast hip hop, jazz rap
Years active: 1989–present

Corey Brent Penn Sr. (born October 8, 1968), better known by his stage name CL Smooth, is an American rapper. He is best known as the vocal half of the hip hop duo Pete Rock & CL Smooth, whose 1992 single "They Reminisce Over You (T.R.O.Y.)" is widely regarded as one of the greatest hip hop songs of all time.

Career

Penn grew up in Mount Vernon, New York, and formed a duo with his friend, producer and DJ Pete Rock. Their debut EP All Souled Out (1991) was followed by the albums Mecca and the Soul Brother (1992) and The Main Ingredient (1994), both critically acclaimed for Rock's jazz- and soul-infused production and CL Smooth's reflective, melodic flow. The duo split in 1995 amid creative tensions, reuniting periodically for tours and occasional recordings.

As a solo artist, CL Smooth released the albums American Me (2006) and The Outsider (2007), and has made guest appearances on tracks by artists including AZ and others. He has continued to perform with Pete Rock on reunion tours.
