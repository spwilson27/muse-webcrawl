The Eurovision Song Contest 2014 was the 59th edition of the annual Eurovision Song Contest. It took place in Copenhagen, Denmark, following Emmelie de Forest's victory in 2013 with "Only Teardrops". The contest was held at B&W Hallerne, a former shipyard hall on Refshaleøen converted for the event under the slogan "#JoinUs".

Result

Austria won with Conchita Wurst, the drag persona of Thomas Neuwirth, performing the dramatic ballad "Rise Like a Phoenix", scoring 290 points. It was Austria's first victory since 1966 and became one of the contest's most widely reported results, celebrated as a statement of tolerance and widely discussed in European media. The Netherlands finished second with the Common Linnets' "Calm After the Storm", and Sweden third with Sanna Nielsen's "Undo".

Participation

Thirty-seven countries competed. Poland returned after a two-year absence, while several countries including Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus and Serbia withdrew, mostly citing financial constraints. Montenegro and San Marino reached the final for the first time.
