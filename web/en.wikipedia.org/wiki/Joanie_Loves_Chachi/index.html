Joanie Loves Chachi

Joanie Loves Chachi is an American sitcom television series and a spin-off of Happy Days that aired on ABC from March 23, 1982, to May 24, 1983. It stars Scott Baio as Chachi Arcola and Erin Moran as Joanie Cunningham, who move to Chicago to pursue a music career with their band.

Joanie and Chachi performing with their band.

Premise

Joanie Cunningham and her boyfriend Chachi Arcola — Fonzie's younger cousin, introduced on Happy Days — relocate from Milwaukee to Chicago, where they form a band and try to break into the music business, renting rooms from Chachi's stepfather Louisa and mother (played by Al Molinaro and Ellen Travolta, reprising their Happy Days roles as Al Delvecchio and Louisa Delvecchio).

Broadcast history

The series debuted strongly, benefiting from the popularity of its parent show, ranking in the top of the ratings in its first short season. ABC moved it to a new time slot for the second season, and ratings fell sharply; it was cancelled after 17 episodes. Baio and Moran then returned to Happy Days for its final season, in which Joanie and Chachi were married.

Legacy

Despite its brief run, Joanie Loves Chachi is often cited in discussions of spin-offs, and it famously achieved high ratings in South Korea decades later — popularly attributed to "Chachi" sounding similar to a Korean word, though television historians regard this as apocryphal.
