Viswanathan "Vishy" Anand (born 11 December 1969) is an Indian chess grandmaster and a former five-time World Chess Champion. He became the first grandmaster from India in 1988, and is one of the few players to have surpassed an Elo rating of 2800, a feat he first achieved in 2006.

Anand defeated Alexei Shirov in a six-game match to win the 2000 FIDE World Chess Championship, a title he held until 2002. He became the undisputed world champion in 2007, and defended his title against Vladimir Kramnik in 2008, Veselin Topalov in 2010, and Boris Gelfand in 2012. In 2013, he lost the title to Magnus Carlsen, and he lost a rematch to Carlsen in 2014.

Career

Anand was known in his early career for his exceptional speed of play, earning the nickname "Lightning Kid", and he won the World Junior Chess Championship in 1987. He has won the World Rapid Chess Championship twice and numerous elite tournaments including Wijk aan Zee, Linares and Dortmund. He is also a six-time winner of the Chess Oscar.

Honours

Anand has received many of India's highest civilian and sporting honours, including the Arjuna Award, the Padma Shri, the Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna (India's highest sporting honour, of which he was the first recipient), the Padma Bhushan and the Padma Vibhushan. He is widely credited with popularising chess in India, inspiring the country's subsequent generation of grandmasters.
