Muhurta

A muhurta (Sanskrit: मुहूर्त) is a unit of time and a concept in Hindu astrology and calendrical tradition denoting an auspicious moment chosen for beginning an important undertaking, such as a wedding, journey, business venture, or religious ceremony.

The time unit

In classical Indian time measurement, a day is divided into 30 muhurtas, each lasting 48 minutes. The first muhurta of the day begins at sunrise. The period roughly an hour and a half before sunrise, the brahma muhurta, is considered especially favourable for meditation, study, and spiritual practice.

Electional astrology

In the branch of Jyotisha called muhurta or electional astrology, a practitioner examines the almanac (panchanga) — considering the lunar day (tithi), weekday, nakshatra, yoga, and karana — to select a moment free of inauspicious influences for the activity in question.

Use

Consulting a muhurta remains common in India for marriages, housewarmings (griha pravesh), naming ceremonies, and the launch of new enterprises.
