List of busiest ports by cargo tonnage

This is a list of the world's busiest seaports ranked by total cargo tonnage handled annually, measured in millions of tonnes. Chinese ports dominate the upper ranks; the Port of Ningbo-Zhoushan has been the world's busiest port by tonnage since the late 2000s, largely on the strength of iron ore, coal, and crude oil volumes.
