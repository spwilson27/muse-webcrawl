Bari Bari Densetsu

Bari Bari Densetsu (バリバリ伝説, "Vroom Vroom Legend") is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Shuichi Shigeno, the creator of Initial D. It was serialized in Kodansha's Weekly Shōnen Magazine from 1983 to 1991, and its chapters were collected in 38 tankōbon volumes.

Plot

The series follows Gun Koma and Hideyoshi Ichihara, two high-school motorcycle street racers whose rivalry and friendship carry them from illegal mountain-pass races into professional road racing. The manga is noted for its detailed depiction of motorcycles and racing technique, and it is partly based on the real-life experiences of the 500cc Grand Prix rider Takazumi Katayama's era of Japanese racing.

Adaptations

The manga was adapted into an original video animation (OVA) produced by Studio Pierrot, released in 1986, and into video games. Shigeno's later work Initial D applied a similar formula to car racing.

Reception

By the end of its run, the manga had sold over 22 million copies, making it one of the best-known motorcycle manga of its era.
