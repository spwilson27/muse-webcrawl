Musakhan

Musakhan
OriginPalestine
Main ingredientsChicken, sumac, onions, taboon bread, olive oil

Musakhan (Arabic: مسخّن) is a Palestinian Arab cuisine dish composed of roasted chicken baked with onions, sumac, allspice, saffron, and fried pine nuts, served over taboon bread. It is often considered the national dish of Palestine.

Preparation

Large quantities of onions are caramelised in olive oil with sumac, then spread over flat taboon bread. Roasted chicken pieces are placed on top, and the dish is garnished with pine nuts and additional sumac.

Cultural significance

Musakhan is traditionally made in autumn during the olive oil pressing season, celebrating the new harvest. In 2010, Palestinians in Burin baked a record musakhan weighing over a tonne, recognised as the largest ever made.
