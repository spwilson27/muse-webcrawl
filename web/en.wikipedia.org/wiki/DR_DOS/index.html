DR DOS

DR DOS
Developer: Digital Research (later Novell, Caldera)
First released: 1988
Predecessor: DOS Plus / CP/M lineage

DR DOS was an operating system of the DOS family developed by Digital Research as a compatible, enhanced alternative to Microsoft's MS-DOS. Version 5.0 (1990) and 6.0 (1991) were notable retail successes.

Features

DR DOS offered features ahead of contemporary MS-DOS, including memory management that loaded the kernel into high memory, disk compression (in later versions), a graphical file manager (ViewMAX), multitasking precursors and bundled utilities such as DiskOpt and a powerful batch language.

Decline

Competition with MS-DOS — including the famous episode in which a beta of Windows 3.1 displayed a spurious error message with DR DOS — eroded its market. Digital Research was sold to Novell, then the product passed to Caldera, which sued Microsoft and settled in 2000. DR DOS survives in embedded and open-source descendant forms.
