British Comedy Awards

British Comedy Awards
First awarded: 1990
Last awarded: 2014
Country: United Kingdom

The British Comedy Awards were an annual awards ceremony recognising excellence in British comedy in television, radio and film. First held in 1990, the awards were presented by Michael Parkinson and later hosted for many years by Jonathan Ross, and were broadcast on ITV and later Channel 4.

History

The awards honoured categories including Best Comedy Actor and Actress, Best Sitcom, Best Comedy Entertainment Programme and the King or Queen of Comedy, voted by the public. Lifetime achievement and writers' awards recognised figures such as Victoria Wood and Barry Cryer. The ceremony became known for unpredictable live moments, including Julian Clary's 1993 appearance. The final ceremony took place in 2014.
