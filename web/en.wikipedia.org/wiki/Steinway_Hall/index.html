Steinway Hall

Steinway Hall is the name given to several concert halls and showrooms associated with the piano manufacturer Steinway & Sons. The most famous is in New York City.

New York

The first Steinway Hall opened in 1866 on East 14th Street in Manhattan and, with over 2,000 seats, was one of the city's principal concert venues; it served as the home of the New York Philharmonic until Carnegie Hall opened in 1891. The present Steinway Hall, at 1133 Avenue of the Americas (Sixth Avenue) since 2016, continues the tradition; its predecessor at 109 West 57th Street (opened 1925, at the base of Steinway Tower's site) was a New York City landmark housing showrooms and a recital space.

London

Steinway Hall in London, on Marylebone Lane, has been the company's UK showroom and recital venue since the 19th century, hosting generations of pianists.
