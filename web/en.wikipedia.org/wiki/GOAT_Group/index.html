GOAT Group, Inc. is an American holding company that operates platforms for buying and selling sneakers, apparel and accessories. Founded in 2015 by Eddy Lu and Daishin Sugano, the company's primary platform is GOAT, a marketplace originally focused on authenticating high-demand sneakers for resale. The name derives from the sports acronym "GOAT" ("Greatest Of All Time").

History

Lu and Sugano launched GOAT in 2015 after Lu bought a pair of counterfeit sneakers online; the platform's model requires sellers to ship items to GOAT for authentication before delivery to buyers. In 2018 GOAT merged with consignment retailer Flight Club, known for its flagship sneaker stores in New York, Los Angeles and Miami, in a deal valuing the combined business at about $250 million. Successive funding rounds raised the company's valuation to over $1 billion in 2019 and about $3.7 billion in 2021.

Operations

GOAT Group operates the GOAT marketplace, the Flight Club retail and consignment business, and alias, a selling app launched in 2020. The company has expanded from sneakers into apparel, accessories and luxury goods, with fulfilment and authentication facilities in the United States and internationally, and a large global user base concentrated among younger consumers.
