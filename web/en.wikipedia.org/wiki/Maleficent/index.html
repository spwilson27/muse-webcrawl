Maleficent, the Mistress of All Evil.

Maleficent is a fictional character who first appears in Walt Disney Productions' animated film Sleeping Beauty (1959). Styled the "Mistress of All Evil", she is a powerful dark fairy who, after not being invited to the christening of Princess Aurora, curses the infant to prick her finger on a spinning wheel's spindle and die before sunset on her sixteenth birthday.

Origins

The character derives from the wicked fairy in Charles Perrault's and the Brothers Grimm's versions of "Sleeping Beauty", and her dragon form partly from the tale's prince's battle with an ogress. Animator Marc Davis designed her distinctive horned silhouette and green-flamed magic; Eleanor Audley provided her voice, having also voiced Lady Tremaine in Cinderella (1950).

Other appearances

Maleficent is a central antagonist in the Kingdom Hearts video-game series and appears in the Descendants franchise as the mother of Mal. Angelina Jolie portrayed a sympathetic reimagining of the character in the live-action films Maleficent (2014) and Maleficent: Mistress of Evil (2019), which retell the story from her perspective: a wronged fairy whose curse is ultimately undone by her own maternal love for Aurora.

Reception

Maleficent is consistently ranked among the greatest Disney villains and one of the most iconic animated antagonists in film history.
