Marksman

A marksman is a person skilled in precision shooting. In the military, marksmen are soldiers trained to shoot accurately at longer ranges than regular infantry, while a designated marksman (DM) is a soldier occupying a role within an infantry squad, providing accurate fire at ranges beyond those of the standard rifleman but short of those expected of a dedicated sniper.

Designated marksman

The designated marksman concept differs from that of the sniper: the DM operates as an integral member of a squad, uses a specially accurised rifle — often a modified service rifle with a magnifying optic — and engages targets in support of the unit's fire and movement. Snipers, by contrast, usually operate independently or in pairs, with extensive training in fieldcraft, concealment and reconnaissance.

Terminology

The term is also used in civilian marksmanship sports, where grades such as marksman, sharpshooter and expert denote levels of qualification, and historically in archery and other projectile skills. Female shooters are sometimes called markswomen. Armies have long valued such specialists; embarked marines historically served as sharpshooters in the tops of fighting ships.

Equipment

Designated marksman rifles include the Mk 12 SPR, the SVD Dragunov, the Heckler & Koch HK417 and the M110 SASS, generally chambered for full-power rifle cartridges and fitted with variable-power telescopic sights.
