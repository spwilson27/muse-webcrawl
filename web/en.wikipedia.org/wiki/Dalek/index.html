The Daleks are a fictional extraterrestrial race of mutants in the BBC science fiction series Doctor Who. Created by writer Terry Nation (and designed by Raymond Cusick), they first appeared in 1963 and became the series' most iconic villains.

Description

Daleks are Kaled mutants integrated into tank-like mechanical casings armed with a gunstick and a manipulator arm resembling a plunger. Driven by hatred of all non-Dalek life, they seek universal conquest and extermination, chanting "Exterminate!" in a harsh electronic voice. Their creator in-universe is the scientist Davros of the planet Skaro.

History in the series

Daleks sparked "Dalekmania" in 1960s Britain, spawning films with Peter Cushing, toys and comics. They returned repeatedly to fight successive Doctors, in stories such as Genesis of the Daleks (1975), and featured prominently in the revived series from 2005, including the Time War mythology.

Cultural impact

The word "Dalek" entered the Oxford English Dictionary, and the design — which famously could not climb stairs, until later stories gave them levitation — is among the most recognisable in science fiction.
