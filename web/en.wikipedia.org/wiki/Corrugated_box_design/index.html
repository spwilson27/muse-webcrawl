Corrugated box design

Corrugated box design is the process of matching the design factors for corrugated fibreboard boxes with the functional physical, processing and end-use requirements. Packaging engineers work to meet the performance requirements of a box while controlling total costs throughout the system.

Overview

Material: Corrugated fibreboard

Key tests: Edge crush test, box compression test, burst test

Factors

Designers consider the product's weight, fragility and dimensions, stacking and storage conditions, transport hazards such as vibration and impact, and humidity, which can halve the compression strength of board. The McKee formula estimates box compression strength from the edge crush test value, board thickness and box perimeter.

Styles and manufacture

International box styles are codified in the FEFCO code; the most common is the regular slotted container (0201). Boxes are converted from corrugated board by printing, slotting, die-cutting, folding and gluing, and may be tested against distribution test standards such as those of ISTA and ASTM.
