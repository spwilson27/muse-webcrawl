Steel Magnolias

Steel Magnolias is a 1987 stage play by American playwright Robert Harling, inspired by the death of his sister from complications of type 1 diabetes. Set in a beauty salon in the fictional town of Chinquapin, Louisiana, the play follows a close-knit group of Southern women as they support one another through marriage, illness, birth and loss.

Film adaptation

The play was adapted into a successful 1989 film directed by Herbert Ross, with an ensemble cast including Sally Field, Dolly Parton, Shirley MacLaine, Daryl Hannah, Olympia Dukakis and Julia Roberts, whose performance as Shelby earned her an Academy Award nomination. A television remake with an African-American cast, starring Queen Latifah, aired on Lifetime in 2012.

Themes

The title evokes women who are both delicate as magnolia blossoms and strong as steel. The work is noted for its witty dialogue and its blend of humour and tragedy in depicting female friendship and resilience.
