Rafael Alberti

Rafael Alberti Merello (16 December 1902 – 28 October 1999) was a Spanish poet, a member of the Generation of '27. He is considered one of the greatest literary figures of the so-called Silver Age of Spanish Literature, and he won numerous prizes and awards, including the Premio Cervantes in 1983. He was also a political figure and a member of the Communist Party of Spain.

Born in El Puerto de Santa María, Cádiz, Alberti initially pursued painting before turning to poetry. His first mature book, Marinero en tierra ("Sailor on Land", 1925), won the Premio Nacional de Poesía. Later collections include Cal y canto (1929) and the surrealist Sobre los ángeles ("Concerning the Angels", 1929).

Exile and return

Alberti supported the Republic during the Spanish Civil War and went into exile after Franco's victory in 1939, living first in Argentina and later in Rome for nearly four decades. He returned to Spain in 1977, after Franco's death, and was elected deputy for Cádiz in the first democratic elections. He died in 1999 in his home town.
