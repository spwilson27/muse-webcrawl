Khorog (Tajik: Хоруғ) is the capital of the Gorno-Badakhshan Autonomous Region in eastern Tajikistan. It lies at the confluence of the Gunt and Panj rivers in the Pamir Mountains, close to the border with Afghanistan.

Geography

At an elevation of about 2,200 metres, Khorog is one of the highest towns in Central Asia. It is a junction on the Pamir Highway (M41) and is known for its botanical garden, one of the highest in the world, and for the University of Central Asia campus.
