Naughty Dog, LLC is an American video game developer based in Santa Monica, California, and a subsidiary of PlayStation Studios. Founded by Andy Gavin and Jason Rubin in 1984 as JAM Software, the studio adopted the name Naughty Dog in 1989. It is best known for creating the Crash Bandicoot, Jak and Daxter, Uncharted, and The Last of Us franchises.

History

Gavin and Rubin began developing games as teenagers, releasing titles such as Ski Crazed and Dream Zone. Their breakthrough came with Crash Bandicoot (1996) for the original PlayStation, developed in an informal partnership with Sony Computer Entertainment, which acquired the studio outright in 2001. On the PlayStation 2 the studio created the Jak and Daxter series of platform games.

Later work

For the PlayStation 3, Naughty Dog developed the Uncharted action-adventure series, beginning in 2007, notable for its cinematic presentation. The Last of Us (2013), a post-apocalyptic action-adventure game, received widespread critical acclaim and was adapted into an HBO television series. Its sequel, The Last of Us Part II (2020), won numerous Game of the Year awards. Long-time creative director Neil Druckmann became studio head in 2023.
