Young V&A, formerly the V&A Museum of Childhood, is a branch of the Victoria and Albert Museum in Bethnal Green, east London, devoted to childhood, play, and design for children. It holds one of the world's major collections of toys, games, dolls' houses, children's clothing and nursery artefacts.

History

The building opened in 1872 as the Bethnal Green Museum, reusing the iron structure of an early South Kensington exhibition building — earning it the nickname of an "iron cathedral". Through the 20th century its focus shifted to childhood under curator Roy Strong's influence, and it was renamed the Museum of Childhood.

Transformation

After a major redevelopment co-designed with children, the museum reopened on 1 July 2023 as Young V&A, reorganised around themes of Play, Imagine and Design, with hands-on galleries aimed at visitors from infancy to early teens and free admission.

Collection highlights

Highlights include historic dolls' houses (some dating to the 17th century), rocking horses, board games, construction toys, and objects made by children themselves.
