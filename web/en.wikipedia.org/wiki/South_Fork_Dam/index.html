South Fork Dam

South Fork Dam
Location: Cambria County, Pennsylvania, USA
Impounds: South Fork of the Little Conemaugh River
Reservoir: Lake Conemaugh
Failed: May 31, 1889

The South Fork Dam was an earthen dam forming Lake Conemaugh on the South Fork of the Little Conemaugh River in southwestern Pennsylvania, about 14 miles (23 km) upstream of Johnstown. Originally built by the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania between 1838 and 1853 as a reservoir for the Pennsylvania Main Line canal system, the dam was later abandoned, sold, and rebuilt by the private South Fork Fishing and Hunting Club. Its catastrophic failure on May 31, 1889, caused the Johnstown Flood.

Construction and modifications

The original dam stood about 72 feet (22 m) high and impounded a reservoir of some 20 million tons of water. After the canal system became obsolete, the dam passed through private hands. The South Fork Fishing and Hunting Club — whose members included Pittsburgh industrialists such as Andrew Carnegie and Henry Clay Frick — purchased the property in 1879 and repaired the dam, but lowered the crest by several feet to widen the top for a road, removed the discharge pipes (selling them for scrap), and installed fish screens that trapped debris against the spillway.

Failure

After exceptionally heavy rain on May 30–31, 1889, Lake Conemaugh rose to the lowered crest. Efforts to clear the spillway and raise the dam failed, and at 3:10 p.m. the dam overtopped and collapsed. The released water rushed down the valley, destroying several towns and striking Johnstown, where more than 2,200 people were killed — one of the deadliest disasters in United States history. Club members contributed to relief, but lawsuits against the club failed, and no member was held legally responsible.

Legacy

The site is preserved as part of the Johnstown Flood National Memorial, administered by the National Park Service. Engineering analyses of the failure have emphasized the reduced spillway capacity, the lowered crest, and the absence of a functioning low-level outlet.
