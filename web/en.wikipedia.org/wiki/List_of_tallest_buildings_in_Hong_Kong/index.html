Hong Kong has more skyscrapers than any other city in the world. This list of tallest buildings in Hong Kong ranks completed buildings by height; the city has over 550 high-rise buildings above 150 metres (490 ft). The tallest is the International Commerce Centre in West Kowloon, completed in 2010.

History

Hong Kong's high-rise development accelerated after the Second World War, driven by limited flat land between the harbour and steep hillsides. Landmark towers of the 1980s and 1990s — the HSBC Main Building and I. M. Pei's Bank of China Tower — made the skyline famous, and the relocation of the airport from Kai Tak in 1998 removed height limits over Kowloon, enabling the ICC and the Union Square development.
