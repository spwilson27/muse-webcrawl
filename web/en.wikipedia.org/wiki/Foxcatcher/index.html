Foxcatcher

Foxcatcher is a 2014 American biographical drama film directed by Bennett Miller and written by E. Max Frye and Dan Futterman. It stars Steve Carell, Channing Tatum and Mark Ruffalo, and dramatises the story of multimillionaire John du Pont's sponsorship of Olympic wrestlers Mark and Dave Schultz, and his murder of Dave Schultz in 1996.

Plot

Olympic gold-medal wrestler Mark Schultz, living in the shadow of his brother Dave, is invited by wealthy heir John du Pont to join Team Foxcatcher at du Pont's Pennsylvania estate and train for the 1988 Seoul Olympics. Isolated and increasingly dependent on du Pont, Mark's performance and mental state deteriorate; Dave is later brought in as coach. The relationship between the three men ends in tragedy when du Pont shoots and kills Dave Schultz.

Production

Miller began developing the film in 2007. Carell's performance as du Pont, aided by prosthetic makeup, marked a dramatic departure from his comedic roles. The film premiered at the Cannes Film Festival in May 2014, where Miller won the Best Director award.

Reception

Foxcatcher received widespread critical acclaim for its direction and the performances of Carell and Ruffalo. It was nominated for five Academy Awards, including Best Director, Best Actor (Carell) and Best Supporting Actor (Ruffalo).
