New World Order conspiracy theory

The New World Order (NWO) is a conspiracy theory which hypothesizes a secretly emerging totalitarian world government. The common theme is that a secretive power elite with a globalist agenda is conspiring to eventually rule the world through an authoritarian one-world government that would replace sovereign nation-states.

History of the term

The phrase "new world order" has been used by statesmen including Woodrow Wilson, Winston Churchill, and George H. W. Bush—who used it in a 1990 speech about the post-Cold War order—to describe periods of dramatic change in international politics. In conspiracy literature, the term acquired sinister meanings through writers such as Pat Robertson, whose 1991 bestseller The New World Order linked it to Freemasonry, the Illuminati, and international finance.

Themes and alleged conspirators

Variant theories implicate groups such as the Bilderberg Group, the Trilateral Commission, the Council on Foreign Relations, and the United Nations, and interpret symbols like the Eye of Providence and the Latin motto Novus ordo seclorum on the Great Seal of the United States as evidence. Academics classify the belief as a form of conspiracism and note its overlap with antisemitic tropes in some far-right literature, as well as its influence on militia movements and popular culture.
