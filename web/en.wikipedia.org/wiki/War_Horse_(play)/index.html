War Horse (play)

War Horse is a play based on the book of the same name by writer Michael Morpurgo, adapted for the stage by Nick Stafford. Originally staged at the Royal National Theatre, it premiered on 9 October 2007 in the Olivier Theatre, London. The production is famed for its life-size horse puppets created by South Africa's Handspring Puppet Company.

Productions

The play tells the story of Albert and his beloved horse Joey, requisitioned for service in the First World War. The National Theatre production transferred to the West End's New London Theatre in 2009 and ran until 2016. A Broadway production at Lincoln Center opened in 2011 and won five Tony Awards, including Best Play, followed by tours and productions worldwide.
