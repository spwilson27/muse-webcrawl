European Union Aviation Safety Agency

The European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA) is an agency of the European Union with responsibility for civil aviation safety. Established in 2002 and based in Cologne, Germany, it carries out certification, regulation, and standardization, and also investigates and monitors safety performance.

Responsibilities

EASA certifies aircraft types such as Airbus models, approves design and maintenance organizations, develops common safety rules for EU member states plus Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, and Switzerland, and works with counterparts including the U.S. Federal Aviation Administration.

History

EASA succeeded the Joint Aviation Authorities. Its remit expanded in 2008 and 2018 to operations, licensing, air traffic management, and drones.
