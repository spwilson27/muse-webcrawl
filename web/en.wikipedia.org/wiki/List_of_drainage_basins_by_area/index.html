List of drainage basins by area

This article lists the world's largest drainage basins (river basins and catchment areas) ranked by surface area. A drainage basin is the area of land from which all surface water flows to a single river, lake or ocean.

The Amazon basin is by far the largest, covering roughly 7 million square kilometres of South America — about 40 per cent of the continent. The largest endorheic (internally drained) basins include those of the Caspian Sea, Lake Chad and the Great Artesian Basin of Australia.
