Tyneside

Tyneside is a built-up area and metropolitan borough conurbation in North East England, centred on the banks of the River Tyne. It encompasses Newcastle upon Tyne on the north bank and Gateshead on the south, together with the boroughs of North Tyneside and South Tyneside extending to the North Sea coast at Tynemouth and South Shields.

History and culture

Tyneside grew rich in the nineteenth century on coal mining, shipbuilding and heavy engineering; yards such as Swan Hunter launched famous ships including the Mauretania. The area's residents are known as Geordies, also the name of the distinctive local dialect. Landmarks include the Tyne Bridge, the Sage Gateshead concert hall and the BALTIC Centre for Contemporary Art. The conurbation is served by the Tyne and Wear Metro, opened in 1980.
