Baltimore Gas and Electric Company (BGE) is a subsidiary of Exelon Corporation and Maryland's largest gas and electric utility, serving more than 1.3 million electric customers and around 700,000 natural gas customers in central Maryland, including the city of Baltimore and much of the surrounding metropolitan area.

History

Founded in 1816 as the Gas Light Company of Baltimore, BGE is the oldest gas utility in the United States; Baltimore was the first American city to light its streets with gas. The company merged its gas and electric operations around the turn of the 20th century. It later became part of Constellation Energy Group, and in 2012 Constellation merged with Chicago-based Exelon, making BGE an Exelon operating company.

Operations

BGE delivers electricity and gas through networks it owns and maintains, while generation is supplied by the competitive market and affiliates. It has drawn scrutiny over rate increases and infrastructure spending, including its multi-year grid-reliability plans before the Maryland Public Service Commission.
