Daigo Umehara

Daigo Umehara (born 19 May 1981), known by his gamertag Daigo or "The Beast", is a Japanese professional fighting game player. He specialises in Capcom's Street Fighter series, playing primarily Ryu and Guile, and is widely regarded as one of the greatest fighting game players of all time.

Umehara gained international fame for "Evo Moment 37", his full parry comeback against Justin Wong at Evo 2004 in Street Fighter III: 3rd Strike. He won back-to-back Evo titles in Street Fighter IV in 2009 and 2010, and held Guinness World Records as the most successful player in major Street Fighter tournaments.

In 2010 he became one of the first fighting game players to receive a major sponsorship, with Mad Catz, and he has written books on competition and mindset.
