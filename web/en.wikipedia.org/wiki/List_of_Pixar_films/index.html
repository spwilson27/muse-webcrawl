List of Pixar films

Pixar Animation Studios is an American computer animation studio based in Emeryville, California, known for its feature films created with RenderMan software. Its first feature, Toy Story (1995), was the first fully computer-animated feature film. The studio's films have won numerous Academy Awards and grossed billions of dollars worldwide.

Luxo Jr., the hopping lamp, is Pixar's mascot

Reception

Pixar films are consistently among the highest-rated animated films; Toy Story 3, Inside Out and Coco each won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature, and Up and Toy Story 3 were nominated for Best Picture.
