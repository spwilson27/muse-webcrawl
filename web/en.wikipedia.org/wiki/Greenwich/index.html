Overview

Greenwich is a district of south-east London, on the south bank of the River Thames, within the Royal Borough of Greenwich. It is known throughout the world for its maritime history, for the Royal Observatory, and for giving its name to the prime meridian and Greenwich Mean Time.

The town grew up around a Tudor royal palace, of which the Queen's House by Inigo Jones survives, beside Wren's Royal Naval College and the tea clipper Cutty Sark. Maritime Greenwich is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and the park's hill carries the Observatory and the meridian line.
