Drishyam is a 2015 Indian Hindi-language crime thriller film directed by Nishikant Kamat. A remake of Jeethu Joseph's 2013 Malayalam film of the same name, it stars Ajay Devgn as Vijay Salgaonkar, Tabu as Inspector General Meera Deshmukh, and Shriya Saran as Vijay's wife Nandini.

Plot

Vijay Salgaonkar, a semi-literate cable-TV operator in Goa who is obsessed with films, lives contentedly with his wife and two daughters. When the teenage son of the state police chief goes missing after attempting to blackmail Vijay's elder daughter, an accidental killing in the family's home sets Vijay against the full machinery of the police. Drawing on plots absorbed from countless crime movies, he constructs an elaborate, meticulously rehearsed alibi for his family, fabricating a trip to Panaji with bills, tickets and coached witnesses. As the investigation — led personally by the boy's mother, IG Meera Deshmukh — closes in, Vijay's cover story survives every test, and the film ends with the secret of the body's hiding place remaining his alone.

Reception

The film was a commercial success and received positive reviews, with particular praise for the performances of Devgn and Tabu and for the screenplay's construction. A sequel, Drishyam 2, was released in 2022. The story has also been remade in several other languages, including Kannada, Telugu, Tamil and Sinhala, and inspired the Chinese film Sheep Without a Shepherd.
