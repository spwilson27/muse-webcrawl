The Audi S7 is the performance version of the Audi A7 five-door liftback, produced by Audi Sport (formerly quattro GmbH). It sits below the RS 7 in the range and uses quattro all-wheel drive as standard.

First generation (2012–2018)

The first S7 was launched in 2012 alongside the S6 and S8, sharing their 4.0-litre twin-turbocharged V8 petrol engine. In the S7 it produced 420 PS (later 450 PS after a facelift), coupled to a seven-speed S tronic dual-clutch transmission and a sport differential option. Cylinder-on-demand technology allowed the engine to run on four cylinders under light load.

Second generation (2019–present)

The second-generation S7 is powered by a 2.9-litre twin-turbo V6 petrol engine with a 48-volt mild-hybrid system, producing around 450 PS in most markets; European models adopted a 3.0-litre TDI diesel V6 with an electric compressor. The car features a coupe-like liftback silhouette, frameless doors and adaptive air suspension.
