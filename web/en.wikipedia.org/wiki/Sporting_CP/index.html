Sporting Clube de Portugal, commonly known as Sporting CP or simply Sporting, is a Portuguese sports club based in Lisbon. Founded in 1906, it is one of the "Big Three" clubs of Portuguese football, alongside Benfica and FC Porto, and has never been relegated from the Primeira Liga.

History

Sporting won its first league title in 1941 and enjoyed a golden era in the 1940s and 1950s behind the famous "Five Violins" forward line. The club won the European Cup Winners' Cup in 1964, its only European trophy, and reached the UEFA Cup final in 2005, losing at its own stadium to CSKA Moscow.

Academy

The club's Alcochete academy is renowned as one of the finest in world football, producing Ballon d'Or winners Luís Figo and Cristiano Ronaldo, along with many other internationals.

Other sports

Sporting is a multi-sport club with successful teams in futsal, handball, basketball, athletics, and many other disciplines, and its athletes have won numerous Olympic medals for Portugal.
