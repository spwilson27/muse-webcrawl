Brazil

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

[Image: Christ the Redeemer statue with outstretched arms above Rio de Janeiro, Sugarloaf Mountain and the bay behind]

Brazil, officially the Federative Republic of Brazil, is the largest country in South America and the fifth-largest in the world by area and (approximately) population — over 200 million people. Its capital is Brasília and its largest city is São Paulo. Brazil is the world's most biodiverse country, containing most of the Amazon rainforest, and Portuguese is its official language — the legacy of Portuguese colonisation.

Contents1. Society and economy

Society and economy

Brazil is famed for football (its national team has won a record five World Cups), carnival, samba and bossa nova. Its economy, Latin America's largest, spans agriculture (coffee, soy, beef), mining, oil and industry, alongside major challenges of inequality and deforestation.
