Luka Modrić

Born: 9 September 1985, Zadar, SR Croatia, Yugoslavia
Height: 1.72 m (5 ft 8 in)
Position: Midfielder
National team: Croatia (captain)

Luka Modrić (born 9 September 1985) is a Croatian professional footballer who plays as a central midfielder and captains the Croatia national team. Widely regarded as one of the greatest midfielders of all time and the greatest Croatian player ever, he won the Ballon d'Or in 2018, breaking the decade-long Messi–Ronaldo duopoly, after leading Croatia to the World Cup final.

Club career

Modrić began at Dinamo Zagreb, with formative loans at Zrinjski Mostar — where he was named Bosnian Premier League Player of the Year as a teenager — and Inter Zaprešić, before becoming Dinamo's key player and winning three league titles. In 2008 he moved to Tottenham Hotspur, developing into one of the Premier League's finest playmakers. In 2012 he joined Real Madrid, where over thirteen seasons he formed a legendary midfield with Toni Kroos and Casemiro, winning numerous La Liga titles and six UEFA Champions League trophies among many honours, and becoming the club's most decorated player. In 2025, at the end of his Madrid career, he joined AC Milan.

International career

Modrić debuted for Croatia in 2006 and became captain in 2016. He inspired Croatia's run to the 2018 FIFA World Cup final, winning the tournament's Golden Ball, and to third place at the 2022 World Cup, as well as the 2023 Nations League final. He is Croatia's most-capped player.

Style of play

A creative deep-lying playmaker, Modrić is renowned for his passing range, outside-of-the-boot (trivela) technique, press resistance, dribbling in tight spaces, work rate and tactical intelligence, dictating the tempo of matches well into his late thirties.
