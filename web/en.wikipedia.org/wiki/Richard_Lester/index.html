Richard Lester

Born: 19 January 1932, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, US
Occupation: Film director
Years active: 1960–1991

Richard Lester Liebman (born 19 January 1932), commonly known as Richard Lester, is an American film director based in the United Kingdom. He gained prominence in British cinema for his work with the Beatles, directing A Hard Day's Night (1964) and Help! (1965), whose fast cutting, visual comedy, and irreverent style influenced music videos and pop filmmaking.

Career

Lester began in American television before moving to Britain, where his short The Running Jumping & Standing Still Film (1959) with Spike Milligan and Peter Sellers led to features such as The Knack ...and How to Get It (1965), which won the Palme d'Or at Cannes. His later films include The Three Musketeers (1973) and its sequel, Robin and Marian (1976), Superman II (1980), and Superman III (1983). Following the death of his friend Roy Kinnear during the filming of The Return of the Musketeers (1989), Lester effectively retired from directing.
