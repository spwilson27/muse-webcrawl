Capo (disambiguation)

Capo (Italian for "head" or "chief") may refer to several things.

Capo may refer to:

Music

  • Capo, a device clamped to the neck of a string instrument to raise the pitch of the strings
  • Da capo, a musical term meaning "from the head", instructing the performer to repeat from the beginning

Organised crime

  • Caporegime or capo, a rank in the Mafia — a "captain" who commands a crew of soldiers and reports to the boss or underboss
  • Capo (concentration camp), a prisoner functionary in Nazi concentration camps, more commonly spelled kapo

Places

  • Capo, a headland or cape in Italian placenames, e.g. Capo d'Orlando in Sicily
  • Capo di Ponte, a commune in Lombardy, Italy

Other uses

  • Capo, a leader of football ultras in some supporter cultures, who directs chanting from a stand
  • Capo (surname), a Spanish and Italian surname
