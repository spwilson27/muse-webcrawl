Morbius

Morbius, the Living Vampire, otherwise known simply as Morbius, is a fictional character created by writer Roy Thomas and designed by artist Gil Kane, appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. The character first appeared in The Amazing Spider-Man #101 (October 1971), an issue notable as the first of the series not written by co-creator Stan Lee.

Dr. Michael Morbius, a Nobel Prize–winning biochemist, attempted to cure himself of a rare blood disease using an experimental treatment involving vampire bat DNA and electroshock therapy. The experiment instead transformed him into a pseudo-vampire: he gained superhuman strength, speed, flight and a thirst for blood, along with pale skin and fangs, but without most supernatural weaknesses of true vampires.

Initially introduced as a villain for Spider-Man, Morbius evolved into a brooding antihero, headlining his own horror-tinged series such as Vampire Tales and Morbius: The Living Vampire, and teaming with the Midnight Sons. The character's debut was permitted after the Comics Code Authority loosened its ban on vampires and other supernatural characters in early 1971.

A live-action film, Morbius, starring Jared Leto as the title character, was released in 2022 as part of Sony's Spider-Man Universe. It was a critical and commercial disappointment on release, though it later gained a cult following through internet memes.
