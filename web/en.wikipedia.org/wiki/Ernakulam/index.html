Ernakulam is the mainland, central business district portion of the city of Kochi in Kerala, India, and also the name of the surrounding district. Located on the eastern shore of Lake Vembanad opposite Fort Kochi and Mattancherry, it is Kerala's commercial capital and a major transport and IT hub.

History

The name derives from Erayanakulam, "pond of Lord Shiva", after the Ernakulathappan temple. Ernakulam grew under the Kingdom of Cochin and became the capital of the princely state; after Indian independence it merged with Kochi's twin cities into the Kochi corporation (1967).

Economy and landmarks

Marine Drive, a waterfront promenade, the High Court of Kerala, and the commercial strip of MG Road define the district. InfoPark and Kakkanad host IT campuses, and the Kochi Metro (opened 2017) serves the area. Ernakulam Junction (South) and Ernakulam Town (North) are major railway stations connecting Kerala to the rest of India.
