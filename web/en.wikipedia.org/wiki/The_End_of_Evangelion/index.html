The End of Evangelion

The End of Evangelion (Japanese: 新世紀エヴァンゲリオン劇場版 THE END OF EVANGELION) is a 1997 Japanese animated psychological science fiction film written by Hideaki Anno and directed by Anno and Kazuya Tsurumaki. It was produced by Gainax and Production I.G as an alternate ending to the television series Neon Genesis Evangelion.

Background

The final two episodes of the TV series, constrained by budget and scheduling problems, took place largely inside the characters' minds and left many viewers dissatisfied; Anno received hate mail and death threats, some of which appear briefly in the film. The End of Evangelion retells the conclusion externally, in parallel with the series' internal ending, and is divided into two episodes: "Air" (episode 25′) and "Magokoro wo, Kimi ni / My Purest Heart for You" (episode 26′).

Plot

The secret organisation SEELE, having lost patience with Gendo Ikari's own agenda, sends the Japan Strategic Self Defense Force to seize NERV headquarters by force. Asuka, recovering from her catatonia inside Eva-02, repels the attackers but is overwhelmed and killed by the mass-produced Evangelions. Shinji, summoned to pilot Eva-01, triggers the apocalyptic Third Impact as the Human Instrumentality Project unfolds; ultimately he rejects Instrumentality, choosing the pain of individual existence, and awakens on a devastated shoreline beside Asuka.

Reception

The film won the Animage Anime Grand Prix and is frequently ranked among the greatest animated films ever made, praised for its animation, direction and thematic ambition, though its violent and surreal imagery has also been controversial. It grossed over ¥1.45 billion in Japan.
