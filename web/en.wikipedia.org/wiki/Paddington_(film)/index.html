Paddington is a 2014 live-action/animated family comedy film directed and co-written by Paul King. It is based on the Paddington Bear stories created by Michael Bond and produced by David Heyman for StudioCanal. The film stars Ben Whishaw as the voice of Paddington, with Hugh Bonneville, Sally Hawkins, Julie Walters, Jim Broadbent, Peter Capaldi and Nicole Kidman.

Paddington tells the story of a young anthropomorphic bear from "Darkest Peru" who, after his home is destroyed by an earthquake, travels to London in search of a new home. Taken in by the kindly Brown family, he must evade a sinister taxidermist who wants to add him to her collection at the Natural History Museum.

Plot

A British explorer named Montgomery Clyde travels to Peru, where he befriends two bears, later named Lucy and Pastuzo. The bears learn English from him and develop a love of marmalade. Years later, an earthquake destroys the bears' home and kills Pastuzo. Lucy, now too old to travel, sends her young nephew Paddington to London with a tag reading "Please look after this bear."

Paddington arrives at Paddington station, where the Brown family reluctantly agree to let him stay the night. In his search for the explorer who might give him a permanent home, Paddington draws the attention of Millicent Clyde, a museum taxidermist. After a series of mishaps and Millicent's attempts at capture, Paddington realises that the home he has been searching for is with the Browns, who defend him from Millicent and adopt him as a member of the family.

Production

The film was announced in 2007 with David Heyman producing. Paddington was created using CGI by the visual effects company Framestore, with Guillermo Navarro originally announced as director before Paul King took over. Colin Firth was originally cast as Paddington's voice but withdrew, feeling his voice did not suit the character; Ben Whishaw replaced him.

Release and reception

Paddington premiered in London on 23 November 2014 and was released in the United Kingdom on 28 November 2014. It received positive reviews from critics, who praised the humour, charm and visual effects, and grossed $268 million worldwide against a budget of approximately $65 million. The film was followed by a sequel, Paddington 2, in 2017, and a third film, Paddington in Peru, in 2024.

The film was nominated for two BAFTA Awards: Outstanding British Film, and Best Adapted Screenplay.
