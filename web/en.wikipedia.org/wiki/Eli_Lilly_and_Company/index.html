Eli Lilly and Company

Type: Public company
Industry: Pharmaceuticals
Founded: May 10, 1876
Founder: Eli Lilly
Headquarters: Indianapolis, Indiana, U.S.

Eli Lilly and Company, doing business as Lilly, is an American pharmaceutical company headquartered in Indianapolis, Indiana, with offices in 18 countries. Its products are sold in approximately 125 countries. The company was founded in 1876 by Eli Lilly, a pharmaceutical chemist and Union Army veteran of the American Civil War.

History

Colonel Eli Lilly founded the company in 1876 in Indianapolis. It was one of the first pharmaceutical firms to mass-produce medicines and to employ a dedicated research staff. In the 1920s Lilly collaborated with researchers at the University of Toronto to commercialize insulin, marketed as Iletin, the first mass-produced insulin product. Lilly later became a major producer of penicillin and other antibiotics.

Products

Lilly's notable products have included the antidepressant Prozac (fluoxetine), the antipsychotic Zyprexa (olanzapine), the diabetes treatments Humalog and Trulicity, and, more recently, the GLP-1 receptor agonist tirzepatide, sold as Mounjaro for diabetes and Zepbound for obesity. Tirzepatide's commercial success drove Lilly to become the most valuable pharmaceutical company in the world by market capitalization in the mid-2020s.

Research and development

The company invests heavily in research and development, with major research facilities in Indianapolis, San Diego, and other locations, focusing on diabetes, oncology, neuroscience, and immunology.
