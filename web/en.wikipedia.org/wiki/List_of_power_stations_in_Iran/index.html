List of power stations in Iran

Iran has one of the largest electricity generation capacities in the Middle East, exceeding 80 gigawatts of installed capacity. Most generation comes from natural gas-fired steam and combined-cycle plants, supplemented by large hydroelectric dams on the Karun River, a nuclear reactor at Bushehr, and growing wind and solar facilities.

Iran's electricity grid is managed by Tavanir, the state power generation and transmission company. The country exports electricity to neighbours including Iraq, Afghanistan and Pakistan, though summer demand peaks have periodically strained supply, leading to blackouts and restrictions on energy-intensive industries such as cryptocurrency mining.
