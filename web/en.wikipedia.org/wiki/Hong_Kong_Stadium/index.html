Hong Kong Stadium

LocationSo Kon Po, Hong Kong Island
Capacity40,000
Opened1953; rebuilt 1994

Hong Kong Stadium is the main sports venue of Hong Kong, located in So Kon Po on Hong Kong Island. Rebuilt in 1994 on the site of the former Government Stadium, it holds 40,000 spectators.

Events

It is the home of the Hong Kong Sevens rugby tournament, one of the world's premier rugby sevens events, held there since 1982, as well as Hong Kong football internationals and the Lunar New Year Cup. In 1996 it hosted the first Bledisloe Cup match played on neutral ground.

Future

With the opening of Kai Tak Sports Park, the stadium's role is expected to reduce, with redevelopment plans discussed for downsizing.
