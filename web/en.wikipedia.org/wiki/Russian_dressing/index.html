Russian dressing

Russian dressing is a piquant American salad dressing consisting of mayonnaise and ketchup-style chili sauce blended with flavourings such as horseradish, pimentos, chives, and spices. Despite its name, it was created in the United States and has no particular connection to Russian cuisine.

History

The dressing is generally credited to James E. Colburn of Nashua, New Hampshire, who developed a shelf-stable version in the 1910s and sold it to retailers and hotels; earlier mayonnaise-based dressings with caviar and other luxury ingredients may account for the "Russian" name. Russian dressing is a classic component of the Reuben sandwich and is closely related to Thousand Island dressing, which differs chiefly in the addition of finely chopped pickles and other relish ingredients.
