Migration is a 2023 American animated adventure comedy film produced by Illumination and distributed by Universal Pictures. Directed by Benjamin Renner and written by Mike White, it features the voices of Kumail Nanjiani, Elizabeth Banks, Keegan-Michael Key, Awkwafina, Carol Kane, David Mitchell and Danny DeVito.

Plot

The Mallard family lives a safe but sheltered life on a pond in a New England forest. The overprotective father, Mack, is content to keep his family close to home forever, while his wife Pam longs for adventure. When a flock of migrating ducks stops at their pond with tales of exotic destinations, Pam persuades Mack to take the family — son Dax, daughter Gwen, and grouchy Uncle Dan — on a journey south to Jamaica. Along the way they brave a New York City storm, escape a heron couple, navigate a fog-bound farm, and are captured by a chef determined to serve duck à l'orange, before finally reaching Jamaica, where Mack learns to embrace the unknown.

Production and release

The film was announced in February 2022, with French animator Benjamin Renner (director of Ernest & Celestine) chosen to direct and The White Lotus creator Mike White writing the screenplay. It premiered at the VIEW Conference in Turin in October 2023 and was released in the United States on December 22, 2023, preceded in theatres by the short film Mooned, featuring Vector from Despicable Me.

Reception

Migration grossed approximately $300 million worldwide against a budget of about $70 million. It received generally favourable reviews, with critics praising its hand-crafted visual style, humour and heartfelt themes about stepping outside one's comfort zone, though some found the story familiar.
