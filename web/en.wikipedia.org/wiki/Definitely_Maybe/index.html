Definitely Maybe is the debut studio album by the English rock band Oasis, released on 29 August 1994 by Creation Records. Written by lead guitarist and chief songwriter Noel Gallagher, it became the fastest-selling debut album in British history at the time and is regarded as a landmark of the Britpop movement and one of the greatest albums of all time.

Recording and release

The album was recorded after earlier sessions with producer Dave Batchelor were scrapped; the band re-recorded at Monnow Valley Studio in Wales and Sawmills Studio in Cornwall, with Owen Morris completing the production and famously loud mastering. It entered the UK Albums Chart at number one. The singles included "Supersonic", "Shakermaker", "Live Forever" — regularly voted among the greatest British songs ever — and "Cigarettes & Alcohol".

Legacy

Definitely Maybe has sold over 15 million copies worldwide. Its success launched Oasis's rapid rise, culminating in the following year's (What's the Story) Morning Glory? and the record-breaking Knebworth concerts of 1996. The album consistently ranks near the top of critics' lists of the greatest British albums, and its cover — the band lounging in guitarist Paul "Bonehead" Arthurs's house in Didsbury, Manchester, amid football and film memorabilia — is iconic.
