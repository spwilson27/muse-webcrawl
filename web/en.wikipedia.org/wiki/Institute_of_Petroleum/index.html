Institute of Petroleum
Founded: 1913 (as Institution of Petroleum Technologists)
Successor: Energy Institute (2003 merger)
Headquarters: London, United Kingdom

The Institute of Petroleum was a British professional organisation for those working in the oil and gas industry. Founded in 1913 as the Institution of Petroleum Technologists, it later became the Institute of Petroleum and served as a learned society and professional body, publishing technical standards and journals and promoting education in petroleum science and engineering.

History

The institution was established as the petroleum industry expanded rapidly in the early twentieth century. It developed standard methods for testing petroleum products — many IP test methods remain in use worldwide — and organised conferences and local branches.

Merger

In 2003 the Institute of Petroleum merged with the Institute of Energy to form the Energy Institute, a chartered professional body for the whole energy sector. The Institute's library and test-method work continued under the new organisation.

Publications

It published the Journal of the Institute of Petroleum and numerous codes of practice for the safe handling of petroleum products.
