Battle of Vitoria
Date: 21 June 1813
Location: Vitoria, Spain
Part of: Peninsular War

The Battle of Vitoria was fought on 21 June 1813 during the Peninsular War. An allied army under the Duke of Wellington decisively defeated the French army of King Joseph Bonaparte near Vitoria in northern Spain.

Background

Wellington's 1813 offensive outflanked the French, forcing Joseph to concentrate near Vitoria with a vast baggage train carrying the accumulated plunder of occupied Spain.

The battle

Allied columns converged on the French position, breaking its centre and cutting the road to France. The French fled, abandoning artillery, baggage and Joseph's treasury, much of it looted by pursuing troops.

Legacy

The victory effectively ended French rule in Spain. Beethoven commemorated it in his composition Wellington's Victory.
