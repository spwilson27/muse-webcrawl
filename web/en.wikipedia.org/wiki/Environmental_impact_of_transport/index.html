Environmental impact of transport

The environmental impact of transport is significant because transport is a major user of energy and burns most of the world's petroleum. Transportation accounts for a substantial share of global greenhouse gas emissions, principally carbon dioxide from road vehicles, aviation, and shipping, and also produces air pollutants such as nitrogen oxides and particulate matter.

Effects and mitigation

Beyond emissions, transport infrastructure fragments habitats and generates noise pollution. Mitigation strategies include shifting travel to public transport, cycling, and walking; improving vehicle efficiency; electrification; and using sustainable fuels. Policies range from fuel economy standards to congestion pricing and low-emission zones in cities.
