List of World Heritage Sites in Europe

UNESCO has designated more than 500 World Heritage Sites in Europe (including transcontinental states such as Russia and Turkey counted regionally by UNESCO as Europe and North America), the largest number of any region. Italy and China compete for the most sites of any country worldwide, with Italy's total exceeding 55. Because of the number of sites, UNESCO articles divide Europe into Eastern, Northern, Southern and Western Europe; this page presents a selection of the most notable sites by country.
