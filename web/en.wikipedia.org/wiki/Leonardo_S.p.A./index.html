Leonardo S.p.A.

Leonardo S.p.A., formerly Finmeccanica, is an Italian multinational company specialising in aerospace, defence, and security, headquartered in Rome. It is one of the world's largest defence contractors and is partially owned by the Italian government, which holds about 30 percent of its shares through the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

History

Finmeccanica was founded in 1948 as the mechanical engineering arm of the state holding company IRI. Over the decades it gathered many of Italy's leading engineering firms, including Agusta (helicopters), Alenia (aeronautics), Selex (electronics), Oto Melara (land and naval armaments), and Ansaldo. After a period of international expansion and restructuring in the 2000s, the group's operating companies were folded into a single company in 2016, and in 2017 it took the name Leonardo, after Leonardo da Vinci.

Business

Leonardo's activities cover helicopters (including the AW139 and AW101), military and civil aircraft (it is a partner in the Eurofighter Typhoon and produces fuselage sections for the Boeing 787), electronics and radar, cyber security, space systems through joint ventures such as Telespazio and Thales Alenia Space, and land and naval weapons. It employs roughly 50,000 people, with major operations in Italy, the United Kingdom — where Leonardo UK is a major defence supplier — the United States (through Leonardo DRS), and Poland. Annual revenues are on the order of €15–17 billion.
