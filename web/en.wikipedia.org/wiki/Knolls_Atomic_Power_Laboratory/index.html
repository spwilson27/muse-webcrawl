The Knolls Atomic Power Laboratory (KAPL) is an American research and development facility dedicated to the support of the United States Naval Nuclear Propulsion Program. Established in 1946 under contract with General Electric, it is sponsored jointly by the Department of Energy's National Nuclear Security Administration and the United States Navy.

Sites and work

KAPL operates two sites in upstate New York: the Knolls site in Niskayuna and the Kenneth A. Kesselring site in West Milton, where land-based prototype reactor plants are used to test designs and train naval reactor operators. Along with its sister laboratory, Bettis Atomic Power Laboratory near Pittsburgh, KAPL designs, builds and supports the nuclear reactors that power the U.S. Navy's submarines and aircraft carriers, and trains the sailors who operate them. Reactor plants developed under the program powered vessels from the first nuclear submarine, USS Nautilus, onward.

History

The laboratory's contract was operated by General Electric from its founding until 1993, then by Lockheed Martin (through Martin Marietta and KAPL, Inc.), and from 2018 by Fluor Marine Propulsion. Early work at the site included the S1G and S3G prototype plants at West Milton and contributions to early naval reactor designs under the direction of Admiral Hyman G. Rickover's Naval Reactors office.
