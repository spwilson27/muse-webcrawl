Locomotive Software

Locomotive Software
Industry: Software
Founded: 1983, United Kingdom
Key products: LocoScript, Mallard BASIC, Amstrad CPC firmware

Locomotive Software was a British software company founded in 1983, best known for its close work with Amstrad. It wrote the firmware and BASIC interpreter for the Amstrad CPC range of home computers and developed LocoScript, the bundled word processor for the Amstrad PCW.

Products

Mallard BASIC, Locomotive's implementation of BASIC for CP/M, shipped with the PCW and other machines. LocoScript (1985) became one of the best-known word processors of the 1980s in Britain, later sold in enhanced versions for CP/M and for IBM PC compatibles. The company also produced LocoSpell, LocoFile and other add-ons.

Legacy

Locomotive's work underpinned Amstrad's success in the UK home and small-business computer market. Its founders and staff were closely associated with the distinctive, highly integrated design philosophy of the CPC and PCW systems.
