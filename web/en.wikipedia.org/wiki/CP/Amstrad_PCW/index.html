Amstrad PCW

Amstrad PCW
Manufacturer: Amstrad
Released: 1985 (PCW8256)
CPU: Zilog Z80
Operating system: CP/M Plus
Bundled software: LocoScript

The Amstrad PCW series is a range of personal computers produced by Amstrad from 1985 into the 1990s, sold as dedicated word processors. The name PCW stands for Personal Computer Wordprocessor. The first model, the PCW8256, combined a Z80-based computer, a green-screen monitor, a printer and the LocoScript word processing software in a single low-cost package.

Design and market

Amstrad, led by Alan Sugar, targeted small businesses and home writers who found general-purpose computers intimidating or expensive. The all-in-one system — computer, monitor, disk drive and printer — undercut rivals dramatically and sold in the millions, dominating the UK word processor market.

Software

Although marketed as a word processor launching straight into LocoScript, the machines ran CP/M Plus and could use a large library of CP/M business software. Later models, including the PcW10 and PcW16, continued the line into the mid-1990s.
