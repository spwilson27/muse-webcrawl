Simon Pegg

Born: Simon John Beckingham, 14 February 1970, Brockworth, Gloucestershire, England
Occupation: Actor, comedian, screenwriter, producer
Years active: 1995–present

Simon Pegg (born Simon John Beckingham, 14 February 1970) is an English actor, comedian, and screenwriter. He came to prominence as co-creator of the Channel 4 sitcom Spaced (1999–2001), which he co-wrote with Jessica Hynes and which was directed by Edgar Wright.

Career

Pegg achieved international fame with the "Three Flavours Cornetto" trilogy directed by Wright and co-written with him: the zombie comedy Shaun of the Dead (2004), the action comedy Hot Fuzz (2007), and the science-fiction comedy The World's End (2013), all co-starring his frequent collaborator Nick Frost.

He is also known for playing Montgomery "Scotty" Scott in the Star Trek reboot film series (2009–2016) — he co-wrote Star Trek Beyond (2016) — and Benji Dunn in the Mission: Impossible film series (2006–present). His voice roles include Buck in the Ice Age franchise and appearances in The Chronicles of Narnia: The Voyage of the Dawn Treader (2010). Other films include Paul (2011), co-written with Frost, The Adventures of Tintin (2011), and Ready Player One (2018).
