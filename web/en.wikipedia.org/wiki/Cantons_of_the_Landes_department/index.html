Cantons of the Landes department

The following is a list of the 15 cantons of the Landes department, in southwestern France, following the French canton reorganisation which came into effect in March 2015. Cantons in France are electoral districts used to elect members of departmental councils; they do not correspond to administrative structures in their own right.

Background

Before the 2015 reform the department had 30 cantons. The reform, applied across France, halved the number of cantons while making each elect a man and a woman jointly to the departmental council, with populations balanced to within about 20 percent of the departmental average. Landes is divided into two arrondissements, Dax and Mont-de-Marsan.
