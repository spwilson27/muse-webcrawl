Ivar Ballangrud

Ivar Ballangrud
Born: 7 March 1904, Lunner, Norway
Died: 1 June 1969, Trondheim, Norway
Sport: Speed skating

Ivar Ballangrud (born Ivar Eriksen; 7 March 1904 – 1 June 1969) was a Norwegian speed skater, a four-time Olympic champion in the sport. He was one of the most successful speed skaters of the interwar period.

Career

Ballangrud won Olympic gold medals in the 5000 metres at Chamonix in 1928 (then as part of a dominant Norwegian team performance), and at the 1936 Winter Olympics in Garmisch-Partenkirchen he won three gold medals — in the 500, 5000, and 10,000 metres — plus a silver in the 1500 metres. He also won four World Allround Championships (1926, 1932, 1936, and 1938) and four European Championships.

World records

He set several world records during his career, including in the 5000 metres and 10,000 metres, and was known for his smooth technique and endurance over the long distances.

Later life

After retiring from competition, Ballangrud ran a sports shop in Trondheim. He died in 1969.
