Reformist Movement

Image placeholder: the MR is a francophone Belgian liberal party.

The Reformist Movement (French: Mouvement Réformateur, MR) is a liberal political party in Belgium, active in the French-speaking Community and in Brussels. It was formed in 2002 from the merger and reorganisation of francophone liberal parties, principally the Liberal Reformist Party (PRL), together with the French-speaking Democratic Front (FDF, which later left) and the Citizens' Movement for Change (MCC).

Ideology and programme

The MR advocates economic liberalism, lower taxation, labour-market reform and pro-European policies, combined with socially liberal positions on many cultural questions. It traditionally draws support from business owners, professionals and parts of the middle class in Wallonia and Brussels.

History

Francophone liberalism in Belgium dates to the Liberal Party founded in 1846. The PRL became a dominant party of government in the 1980s and 1990s. As the MR it has served in numerous federal and regional governments; its leader Charles Michel became Prime Minister of Belgium in 2014 and later President of the European Council. The party is affiliated at European level with the Renew Europe group and the Alliance of Liberals and Democrats for Europe.
