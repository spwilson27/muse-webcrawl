Domestic rabbit
SpeciesOryctolagus cuniculus domesticus
Recognising bodiesARBA (US), British Rabbit Council
Recognised breeds49 (ARBA) / 50+

As of the 21st century there are over 300 breeds of the domestic rabbit (Oryctolagus cuniculus domesticus) around the world, selectively bred for size, coat, colour and temperament from the European rabbit. This list of rabbit breeds includes those recognised by the American Rabbit Breeders Association (ARBA) and the British Rabbit Council, ranging from the tiny Netherland Dwarf to the giant Flemish Giant.
