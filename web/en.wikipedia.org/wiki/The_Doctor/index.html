The Doctor

The Doctor is the protagonist of the BBC science fiction series Doctor Who, broadcast since 1963. A Time Lord from the planet Gallifrey, the Doctor travels through time and space in the TARDIS, usually with companions, helping others and opposing threats.

SpeciesTime Lord
Home planetGallifrey
VehicleTARDIS
First appearanceAn Unearthly Child (1963)

Character

When mortally injured, the Doctor regenerates into a new incarnation with a different appearance and personality, a device that has allowed many actors to play the role, beginning with William Hartnell and including Tom Baker, David Tennant, Matt Smith, Peter Capaldi, Jodie Whittaker and Ncuti Gatwa. Signature items include the sonic screwdriver and psychic paper.

History

The character's origins have been repeatedly expanded: the Doctor fled Gallifrey, was involved in the Time War against the Daleks, and later revelations (the 'Timeless Child') reframed the Doctor's past. Adversaries include Daleks, Cybermen, the Master and Weeping Angels. See Tardis (disambiguation).
