Oradell, New Jersey

Oradell is a borough in Bergen County, New Jersey, United States. As of the 2020 census, its population was about 8,200. It is a suburban community of New York City, located roughly 15 miles north-west of Manhattan, and has repeatedly ranked among the most desirable small towns in the state.

History

The area was settled by Dutch farmers in the colonial era. The borough was formed in 1894 as Delford, from portions of Hackensack, Harrington, and Palisades townships, and its name was changed to Oradell in 1920. The Oradell Reservoir, created by damming the Hackensack River in 1923, borders the borough and is a major regional drinking-water source, near Lake Tappan upstream.

Transportation and education

Oradell is served by NJ Transit's Pascack Valley Line at the Oradell station, with trains to Hoboken and connections to New York Penn Station. Students attend River Dell Regional High School, shared with neighbouring River Edge, and the borough runs its own elementary school district.
