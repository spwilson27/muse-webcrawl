Expo 2012 was an International Recognised Exhibition held in the coastal city of Yeosu, South Korea, from 12 May to 12 August 2012. Recognised by the Bureau International des Expositions (BIE), it was the second international exposition hosted by South Korea after Expo '93 in Daejeon. Its theme, "The Living Ocean and Coast", focused attention on the conservation of marine ecosystems, sustainable development of coastal communities and the future of ocean science and technology.

Site and attractions

The expo site occupied reclaimed waterfront land around the former Yeosu harbour, centred on the "Big-O", a large circular water feature used for multimedia fountain shows combining water jets, flame, laser and projection. Landmark structures included the Sky Tower, converted from a disused cement silo and offering panoramic views, and an aquarium that was among the largest in South Korea. The Korea Pavilion and theme pavilion explored the expo's ocean-focused message, while more than one hundred national pavilions presented their countries' maritime cultures.

Participation and visitors

A total of 104 countries and 10 international organisations participated, and the expo drew roughly 8.2 million visits over its 93 days. Yeosu was selected over competing bids from Tangier, Morocco, and Wrocław, Poland, at the BIE general assembly in November 2007.

Legacy

The expo spurred substantial infrastructure investment in the Yeosu region, including upgraded rail and road links. Parts of the site were later redeveloped for tourism and marine leisure use, though, as with many expositions, the long-term reuse of specialised pavilions proved challenging.
