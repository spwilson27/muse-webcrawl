Ohio General Assembly

The Ohio General Assembly is the state legislature of the U.S. state of Ohio. It is a bicameral body composed of the Ohio Senate, with 33 members, and the Ohio House of Representatives, with 99 members. The General Assembly convenes at the Ohio Statehouse in Columbus.

History

The first General Assembly met in Chillicothe, then the state capital, in 1803 following Ohio's admission to the Union. The current constitution, adopted in 1851, established much of the legislature's present structure, including two-year terms for representatives and four-year staggered terms for senators.

Composition

Members of the House serve two-year terms, while senators serve four-year terms. Term limits, enacted by voters in 1992, restrict legislators to eight consecutive years in either chamber. The governor may call special sessions, and the General Assembly may override a gubernatorial veto with a three-fifths vote of both houses.
