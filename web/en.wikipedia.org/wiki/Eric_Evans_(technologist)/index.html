Eric Evans (technologist)

Eric Evans
Occupation: Software engineer, consultant, author
Known for: Domain-driven design
Notable work: Domain-Driven Design: Tackling Complexity in the Heart of Software (2003)

Eric Evans is a software engineer, consultant and author known for founding the field of domain-driven design (DDD), an approach to software design that focuses on modeling software to match the underlying business domain. His 2003 book, Domain-Driven Design: Tackling Complexity in the Heart of Software, is regarded as a foundational text in the discipline.

Career

Evans has worked on large object-oriented systems across several industries, including shipping, finance and retail. He founded Domain Language, a consultancy dedicated to helping teams apply domain-driven design. He has been a frequent speaker at software conferences and remains a prominent figure in the DDD community.

Domain-driven design

Evans articulated the concept of the ubiquitous language, a shared vocabulary used by both developers and domain experts, and patterns such as entities, value objects, aggregates, repositories and bounded contexts. He later followed up with a shorter companion volume, Domain-Driven Design Reference (2015), summarizing the definitions and patterns of the approach.
