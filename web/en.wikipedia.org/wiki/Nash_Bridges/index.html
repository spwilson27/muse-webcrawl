Nash Bridges

Nash Bridges is an American police procedural television series created by Hunter S. Thompson and Don Johnson, starring Johnson as the title character, an inspector (later captain) with the San Francisco Police Department's elite Special Investigations Unit. It aired on CBS from March 29, 1996, to May 4, 2001, for six seasons and 122 episodes. A television film revival, also titled Nash Bridges, aired on USA Network in 2021.

Premise and characters

Nash Bridges is a charismatic, wisecracking inspector who solves crimes across San Francisco while driving a distinctive yellow 1971 Plymouth Barracuda convertible — in fact several cars were used in production, modified to resemble the rare hemi 'Cuda convertible. His partner was Joe Dominguez, played by Cheech Marin, a retired inspector lured back to work who later ran a bar and a private-eye sideline with Nash. The supporting cast included Jodi Lyn O'Keefe as Nash's daughter Cassidy, Jeff Perry as the eccentric Inspector Harvey Leek, and James Gammon as Nash's father Nick, whose dementia provided a recurring dramatic storyline.

Production

The series was filmed in San Francisco and produced by Don Johnson's company in association with Paramount Television. Hunter S. Thompson, a friend of Johnson's, contributed to the original concept and appeared in a guest role. The show was noted for elaborate car chases and action sequences and for its cost — among the most expensive network dramas of its time per episode — which contributed to CBS's decision to end it despite respectable ratings.

Revival

In November 2021 USA Network aired a two-hour Nash Bridges television film with Johnson and Marin reprising their roles, Nash now running the Special Investigations Unit in a changed department, intended as a backdoor pilot for a possible series.
