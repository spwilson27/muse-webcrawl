Ethan Zuckerman

Ethan Zuckerman (born January 4, 1973) is an American media scholar, blogger, and Internet activist. He was the director of the MIT Center for Civic Media from 2011 to 2020 and is a principal research scientist at the MIT Media Lab. He co-founded the international blogging network Global Voices and the news site Media Cloud.

Work

Zuckerman is the author of Rewire: Digital Cosmopolitans in the Age of Connection (2013) and Mistrust: Why Losing Faith in Institutions Provides the Tools to Transform Them (2021). In the 1990s, while working at Tripod.com, he helped create the pop-up advertisement, for which he later publicly apologized. Since 2020 he has been at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, where he directs the Initiative for Digital Public Infrastructure.
