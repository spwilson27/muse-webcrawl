The President of Austria (German: Bundespräsident der Republik Österreich) is the head of state of the Republic of Austria. Though theoretically entrusted with considerable executive power by the Federal Constitutional Law, in practice the president is largely a ceremonial and representative figure. The president is directly elected by universal suffrage for a term of six years and may be re-elected once consecutively. The incumbent is Alexander Van der Bellen, a former leader of the Green Party, first elected in 2016 and re-elected in 2022.

Role and powers

The president formally appoints the federal chancellor and, on the chancellor's advice, the ministers of the federal government; accepts their resignations or can dismiss the government; signs federal legislation into law; serves as commander-in-chief of the Austrian Armed Forces; accredits ambassadors; grants pardons; and can dissolve the National Council under defined circumstances. By constitutional convention, these powers are exercised on the proposal or advice of the government, and every presidential act generally requires a ministerial countersignature. Only the appointment of the chancellor and a few reserve powers are exercised with genuine personal discretion.

Election

Presidential elections have been by direct popular vote since 1951 (the constitution had provided for direct election since 1929, but the first presidents were chosen by parliament). Candidates must be at least 35 years old. If no candidate wins an absolute majority in the first round, a run-off is held between the two leading candidates. The 2016 election was notable: the Constitutional Court annulled the first run-off result because of irregularities in counting postal ballots, and the re-run in December 2016 returned Van der Bellen as winner over Norbert Hofer.

History

The office traces to the republic proclaimed in 1918. Karl Seitz served as president of the Constituent National Assembly and effectively first head of state; Michael Hainisch became the first Federal President under the 1920 constitution. Wilhelm Miklas was president at the time of the 1938 Anschluss. After 1945, Karl Renner became provisional head of government and then the first president of the Second Republic. Subsequent presidents include Theodor Körner, Adolf Schärf, Franz Jonas, Rudolf Kirchschläger, Kurt Waldheim, Thomas Klestil and Heinz Fischer.
