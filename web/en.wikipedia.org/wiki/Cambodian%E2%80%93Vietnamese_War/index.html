Cambodian–Vietnamese War
Date1975–1989 (invasion December 1978)
ResultKhmer Rouge removed from power; Vietnamese occupation of Cambodia until 1989

The Cambodian–Vietnamese War was an armed conflict between Democratic Kampuchea, ruled by the Khmer Rouge, and the Socialist Republic of Vietnam. Border clashes escalated into a full-scale Vietnamese invasion in December 1978.

Invasion

Vietnam launched its offensive on 25 December 1978 after repeated Khmer Rouge attacks on Vietnamese border villages, including the Ba Chúc massacre. Vietnamese forces captured Phnom Penh on 7 January 1979 and installed the People's Republic of Kampuchea under Heng Samrin.

Occupation and aftermath

The Khmer Rouge retreated to the Thai border and waged guerrilla war with Chinese, Thai, and Western support, while Vietnam faced international sanctions. Vietnam withdrew its forces in 1989, and the 1991 Paris Peace Accords led to UN-supervised elections in 1993.
