Teen Titans Go! To the Movies is a 2018 American animated superhero comedy film based on the television series Teen Titans Go!, itself based on the DC Comics team the Teen Titans. The film was directed by Aaron Horvath and Peter Rida Michail and written by Horvath and series co-developer Michael Jelenic. It was released by Warner Bros. Pictures on July 27, 2018.

Plot

The Teen Titans — Robin, Starfire, Cyborg, Raven, and Beast Boy — grow frustrated that every other superhero seems to have a feature film while they are dismissed as goofy side characters. Robin becomes determined to get the team a movie of their own, and the group travels to Hollywood to convince director Jade Wilson (Kristen Bell) to make one. Believing they need an arch-nemesis to be taken seriously, they target Slade (Will Arnett), who is plotting to steal the Doomsday Device crystal and use the film industry itself to brainwash the world's superheroes. The film is packed with cameos, musical numbers, and satirical jokes about superhero cinema, including a running parody of the Justice League and Marvel films, and a mid-credits tease featuring the 2003 Teen Titans.

Reception

The film received generally positive reviews from critics, who praised its humour, self-awareness, and animation, and it grossed over $52 million worldwide against a budget of $10 million. A sequel crossover film, Teen Titans Go! vs. Teen Titans, followed in 2019.
