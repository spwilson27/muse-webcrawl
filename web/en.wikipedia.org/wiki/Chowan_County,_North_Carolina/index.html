Chowan County, North Carolina

Chowan County is a county in the U.S. state of North Carolina, located on Albemarle Sound in the state's northeastern coastal plain. Its county seat is Edenton, and its population is roughly 14,000.

History

One of North Carolina's oldest counties, Chowan was formed in 1670 and named for the Chowanoke Indigenous people. Edenton, incorporated in 1722, served briefly as the colonial capital and was the site of the 1774 Edenton Tea Party, an early political protest by American women.

Geography

The county is bounded by the Chowan River to the west and Albemarle Sound to the south; agriculture and fishing remain important to its economy.
