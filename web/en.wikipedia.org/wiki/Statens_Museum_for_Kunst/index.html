Statens Museum for Kunst

Statens Museum for Kunst (SMK), also known as the National Gallery of Denmark, is the country's largest art museum, located in Copenhagen. Its collections comprise about 260,000 works spanning 700 years of European and Danish art.

Collections

The museum holds Danish Golden Age painting, works by European masters such as Mantegna, Titian, Rubens, and Rembrandt, and one of the world's largest collections of Matisse works. The Royal Collection of Graphic Art contains over 240,000 prints and drawings.

Building

The main building, designed by Vilhelm Dahlerup and Georg Møller, opened in 1896; a modern extension by Anna Maria Indrio, added in 1998, is connected by a glass-roofed "sculpture street". Admission to the permanent collection is free.
