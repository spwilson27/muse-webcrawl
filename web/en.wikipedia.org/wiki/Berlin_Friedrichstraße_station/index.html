Berlin Friedrichstraße station
Location: Mitte, Berlin
Opened: 1882

Berlin Friedrichstraße is a major railway station in central Berlin, Germany, on Friedrichstraße in Mitte, serving S-Bahn, U-Bahn, regional and long-distance trains and trams.

History

Opened in 1882 as the starting point of the Berlin Stadtbahn, the station became a famous border crossing between East and West Berlin during division, with its Tränenpalast ('Palace of Tears') departure hall for travellers leaving East Berlin.

Today

After reunification the station was rebuilt and is a busy interchange beneath Friedrichstraße's shopping district, near Unter den Linden and Museum Island.
