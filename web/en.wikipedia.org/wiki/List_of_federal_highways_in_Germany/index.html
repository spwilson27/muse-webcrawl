List of federal highways in Germany

Federal highways in Germany, known as Bundesstraßen (singular Bundesstraße), form the trunk road network below the Autobahn system. They are numbered B 1 through B 999 and are marked with black numbers on a yellow rectangular sign. The network totals roughly 38,000 km.

History

The numbering system derives from the Reichsstraßen established in 1932. After reunification in 1990, federal highways were extended throughout the new eastern states. Many urban sections have been bypassed or downgraded where parallel motorways exist.
