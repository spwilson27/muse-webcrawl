Xbox Game Studios (previously known as Microsoft Game Studios and Microsoft Studios) is an American video game publisher and development division of Microsoft, responsible for the creation and publication of games for the Xbox consoles, Windows PCs and other platforms. Founded in 2000 ahead of the launch of the original Xbox, it coordinates a large family of in-house development studios.

History

Microsoft entered the console business with the Xbox in 2001, and its publishing arm grew around flagship acquisitions and internal teams such as Bungie (creators of Halo) and Ensemble Studios (Age of Empires). The division was renamed Microsoft Studios in 2011 and Xbox Game Studios in 2019.

Under Xbox head Phil Spencer and later studios chief Matt Booty, Microsoft embarked on a major expansion, acquiring Mojang (2014, creators of Minecraft), Obsidian Entertainment, Ninja Theory, Playground Games and Double Fine (2018–2019), and ZeniMax Media/Bethesda Softworks (2021). In 2023 Microsoft completed its acquisition of Activision Blizzard for about $68.7 billion — the largest deal in video game history — though Activision Blizzard operates as a sibling division within Microsoft Gaming rather than inside Xbox Game Studios itself.

Studios and franchises

Studios under the Xbox Game Studios banner have included 343 Industries (Halo), The Coalition (Gears of War), Turn 10 Studios (Forza Motorsport), Playground Games (Forza Horizon, Fable), Rare (Sea of Thieves), Obsidian (The Outer Worlds, Avowed), inXile, Ninja Theory (Hellblade), Compulsion Games, Undead Labs, Double Fine (Psychonauts) and World's Edge (Age of Empires). Since 2019 most first-party titles launch day one on the Xbox Game Pass subscription service.
