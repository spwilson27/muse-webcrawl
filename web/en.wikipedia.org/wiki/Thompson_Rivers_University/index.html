Thompson Rivers University (TRU) is a public teaching and research university in Kamloops, British Columbia, Canada. It offers undergraduate and graduate degrees, vocational training, and one of Canada's largest distance and online learning programs through TRU Open Learning.

History

TRU began in 1970 as Cariboo College. It became the University College of the Cariboo in 1989, gaining degree-granting powers, and in 2005 was established as a full university under its present name. Its law school, the first new law school opened in Canada in more than three decades, welcomed its first class in 2011.

Academics and campus

The university's main campus sits on McGill Road in Kamloops, with a satellite campus in Williams Lake. Faculties include Arts, Science, Business and Economics, Education and Social Work, Nursing, Law, and Adventure, Culinary Arts and Tourism. TRU is a member of the Research Universities' Council of British Columbia.
