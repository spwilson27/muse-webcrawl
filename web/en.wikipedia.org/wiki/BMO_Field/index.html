BMO Field is a soccer and Canadian football stadium located at Exhibition Place in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. It is the home of Toronto FC of Major League Soccer and the Toronto Argonauts of the Canadian Football League, and it has served as a venue of the Canada national soccer teams. Naming rights are held by the Bank of Montreal (BMO).

History

The stadium opened on 28 April 2007 on the site of the former Exhibition Stadium, built to host matches of the 2007 FIFA U-20 World Cup and to serve as the home of Toronto FC in its inaugural MLS season. It originally had artificial turf; a natural grass surface was installed in 2010.

A major expansion in 2015–2016 added an upper deck to the east stand, a canopy roof over most seats, and increased capacity to about 30,000, allowing the Argonauts to move in and enabling the stadium to host the 2016 Grey Cup and the 2016 MLS Cup. BMO Field is scheduled to be one of the Canadian venues for the 2026 FIFA World Cup, when it will temporarily be known as Toronto Stadium.

Major events

In addition to club matches, the stadium has hosted the 2007 FIFA U-20 World Cup final, MLS Cups, international soccer friendlies, rugby internationals and matches of the 2015 CONCACAF Gold Cup.
