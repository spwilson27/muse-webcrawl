Founded: 1985 (as Cable Educational Network)

Defunct: 8 April 2022 (merged)
Successor: Warner Bros. Discovery
Brands: Discovery Channel, Animal Planet, TLC, HGTV, Food Network

Discovery, Inc. was an American multinational mass media factual television company based in New York City. Established in 1985, it operated factual and lifestyle television brands worldwide.

History

The company began with the Discovery Channel, launched 17 June 1985 by John Hendricks. It grew through launches and acquisitions including Animal Planet, TLC, and the 2018 purchase of Scripps Networks Interactive, adding HGTV and Food Network. It launched the streaming service discovery+ in 2021.

Merger

In 2021 AT&T announced combining WarnerMedia with Discovery. The deal closed on 8 April 2022, forming Warner Bros. Discovery, led by Discovery chief David Zaslav, and Discovery, Inc. ceased to exist as a separate company.
