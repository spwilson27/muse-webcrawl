River Cherwell

The River Cherwell is a tributary of the River Thames in central England. It rises near Hellidon in Northamptonshire and flows generally south for about 64 km (40 mi) through Banbury and Oxford to join the Thames just south of Oxford.

Course and history

The river passes the market town of Banbury and the villages of the Cherwell valley, running alongside the Oxford Canal for much of its course. The canal crosses and shares the river's channel in places. In Oxford the Cherwell flows past the University Parks and the meadows of Christ Church, and is famous for punting.

Name

The name is pronounced both "CHUR-wel" and "CHUR-chil"-like variants locally; the river gives its name to the Cherwell local government district and to the Oxford student newspaper Cherwell.
