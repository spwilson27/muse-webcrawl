DisneyToon Studios was an American animation studio, a division of Walt Disney Animation Studios, responsible for producing direct-to-video and occasional theatrical sequels and spin-offs of Disney animated films. Founded in 1990 as Disney MovieToons — beginning with DuckTales the Movie: Treasure of the Lost Lamp — it was based in Glendale, California, with satellite studios in Paris, Tokyo and Sydney (the latter inherited as Walt Disney Animation Australia).

Productions

The studio's first sequel, The Return of Jafar (1994), demonstrated the commercial potential of direct-to-video animation, and dozens of follow-ups followed, including The Lion King II: Simba's Pride, Lady and the Tramp II and Cinderella III: A Twist in Time. After John Lasseter became chief creative officer in 2006, planned sequels were curtailed as part of a quality initiative, and the studio pivoted to original franchise work: the computer-animated Tinker Bell film series (2008–2015), set in Pixie Hollow, and the Planes films (2013–2014), spin-offs of Pixar's Cars.

Closure

Following disappointing returns and a corporate refocus on theatrical features, Disney announced the studio's closure in June 2018, ending development of a planned third Planes-universe film and laying off its remaining staff. DisneyToon had produced 47 films over its life, and its output remains a case study in the economics of the home-video sequel era.
