Historic Crew Stadium

Historic Crew Stadium, previously known as Columbus Crew Stadium and Mapfre Stadium, is a soccer-specific stadium in Columbus, Ohio, United States. Opened on May 15, 1999, as Columbus Crew Stadium, it was the first soccer-specific stadium built by a Major League Soccer team, and was the home of the Columbus Crew until 2021.

History

The stadium was constructed for about $28.5 million on the grounds of the Ohio Expo Center. It hosted the 2003 FIFA Women's World Cup and numerous United States national team matches, famously including several 2–0 World Cup qualifying wins over Mexico, giving rise to the "Dos a Cero" tradition. Following the Crew's move to Lower.com Field in 2021, the stadium was renamed Historic Crew Stadium and now serves as the training ground of the Crew and home of Columbus Crew 2.

Facilities

The venue seats about 20,000 spectators and has also hosted concerts, lacrosse, and other sporting events.
