National Museum of American History

The National Museum of American History (NMAH) is a Smithsonian Institution museum on the National Mall in Washington, D.C., dedicated to collecting and preserving the heritage of the United States in social, political, cultural, scientific and military history. It opened in 1964 as the Museum of History and Technology and took its present name in 1980.

Collections

The museum's best-known artefact is the original Star-Spangled Banner, the flag that inspired the US national anthem, displayed in a dedicated climate-controlled gallery. Other highlights include the First Ladies' inaugural gowns, Abraham Lincoln's top hat, Thomas Edison's light bulbs, and Judy Garland's ruby slippers from The Wizard of Oz.
