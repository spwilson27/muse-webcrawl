Holstein Friesians (often shortened to Holsteins in North America, or Friesians elsewhere) are a breed of dairy cattle originating from the Dutch province of Friesland and the adjoining German region of Schleswig-Holstein. They are the world's highest-producing dairy animals and the dominant breed in industrial dairy farming, known for their distinctive black-and-white markings and very large milk yields.

Characteristics

Holsteins are large cattle: a mature cow typically weighs around 680 kg (1,500 lb) and stands about 145–150 cm at the shoulder. The coat is characteristically piebald black and white; a recessive gene occasionally produces red-and-white animals. The breed has been intensively selected for milk production for more than a century, and modern cows routinely produce over 10,000 kg of milk per lactation, with elite animals far exceeding that — though Holstein milk tends to have a lower butterfat and protein percentage than that of breeds such as the Jersey.

History

The breed descends from black-and-white cattle kept by Frisian and Batavian peoples in the Rhine delta region for roughly two thousand years. Dutch and German farmers developed distinct but related populations — the Dutch Friesian and the German Holstein — which were exported widely from the mid-19th century. The first Holsteins were imported into the United States in the 1850s, and breed societies in North America and Europe eventually merged the two strains into a single breed. Holstein genetics, largely through artificial insemination and, more recently, genomic selection, now dominate the global dairy herd, raising concerns about narrowing genetic diversity and the breed's relatively poor fertility, longevity and calving ease compared with traditional breeds.

Use

Holsteins predominate in intensive, high-input dairy systems in North America, Europe and elsewhere. Male calves are commonly raised for veal or beef, and crossbreeding with beef breeds or with other dairy breeds (such as Jersey or Scandinavian Reds) is increasingly practised to improve fertility and health.
