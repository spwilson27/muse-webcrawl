1989 Loma Prieta earthquake

Date17 October 1989
Magnitude6.9 Mw
EpicentreNear Loma Prieta peak, Santa Cruz Mountains, California
Casualties63 killed, more than 3,700 injured (as commonly reported)

The 1989 Loma Prieta earthquake struck California's San Francisco Bay Area on 17 October 1989, shortly before the start of Game 3 of the World Series between the Oakland Athletics and the San Francisco Giants — leading to its nickname the World Series earthquake.

Damage

The quake caused severe damage in San Francisco and Oakland. A section of the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge's upper deck collapsed, and the double-deck Cypress Street Viaduct on Interstate 880 in Oakland collapsed, accounting for many of the deaths. In San Francisco's Marina District, built on filled land, buildings collapsed and fires broke out. In Santa Cruz, close to the epicentre, the Pacific Garden Mall was heavily damaged.

Aftermath

The World Series was postponed for ten days. The disaster led to strengthened building and highway standards in California and long-running retrofit programmes for bridges.
