The BBC Scotland television channel is a Scottish free-to-air channel owned by the BBC, launched on 24 February 2019 to replace the BBC Two Scotland opt-out service. It was the centrepiece of a substantial increase in BBC investment in Scottish production announced in 2017, and is based at BBC Pacific Quay in Glasgow.

Schedule

The channel broadcasts from midday, simulcasting BBC Two programmes in the afternoon, before presenting its own Scottish schedule from 7pm to midnight. Its flagship launch programme was the hour-long nightly news programme The Nine, presenting Scottish, UK, and international news from a Scottish perspective; The Seven provided a shorter weekend bulletin. Original commissions have included comedy and drama such as Scot Squad and Guilt, the soap River City (which moved from BBC One Scotland), documentaries, arts, and sport.

Reception

The channel was created partly to address long-standing arguments that Scotland was underserved by network production spending relative to its licence-fee contribution. Viewing figures for its original programming have been modest compared with BBC One Scotland, and the channel has periodically faced scrutiny over the cost and reach of programmes such as The Nine, which the BBC announced in 2024 would be replaced by an extended edition of Reporting Scotland in a new evening slot.
