John Eleuthère du Pont

John Eleuthère du Pont (1943–2010) was an American heir to the DuPont chemical fortune, a convicted murderer, and a wrestling patron who founded the Team Foxcatcher training facility at his Foxcatcher Farm estate in Pennsylvania.

Life

Du Pont, great-great-grandson of DuPont founder Éleuthère Irénée du Pont, held a doctorate in natural science and published on ornithology and conchology. An eccentric sports enthusiast, he built an Olympic-standard wrestling facility on his estate, sponsored the national team and supported athletes including Olympic champion Dave Schultz.

Murder and death

On 26 January 1996 du Pont shot and killed Schultz at the estate. After a two-day standoff he was arrested, and in 1997 convicted of third-degree murder but found mentally ill, receiving a sentence of 13 to 30 years. He died in prison in 2010. His story was dramatised in the 2014 film Foxcatcher.
