Mühlviertel

The Mühlviertel is the northern of the four traditional quarters of the Austrian state of Upper Austria, lying north of the Danube and taking its name from the Mühl rivers (Große Mühl, Kleine Mühl, Steinerne Mühl).

Its landscape of the Bohemian Massif consists of rolling granite and gneiss hills, forests, and farmland. Major towns include Freistadt, Rohrbach-Berg, and Perg. Historically the region was known for linen weaving, hop growing, and granite quarrying.

Geography

It borders Lower Austria to the east, the Czech Republic to the north, and Bavaria to the northwest.
