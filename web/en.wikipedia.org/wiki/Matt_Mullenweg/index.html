Matthew Charles Mullenweg (born January 11, 1984) is an American entrepreneur and web developer known for co-developing the free and open-source WordPress content management system and for founding Automattic, the company behind WordPress.com, WooCommerce, Tumblr, and other services.

Early life

Mullenweg was born in Houston, Texas, and attended the High School for the Performing and Visual Arts, where he studied jazz saxophone. He later studied political science at the University of Houston before dropping out in 2004 to work at CNET Networks.

WordPress

In January 2003, Mullenweg and Mike Little forked the discontinued b2/cafelog blogging software to create WordPress, whose first version was released in May 2003. WordPress grew to become the most widely used content management system in the world, powering a large share of all websites. Mullenweg remains a lead developer of the WordPress project and is closely involved in its community events, including the WordCamp conferences.

Automattic

Mullenweg founded Automattic in 2005. The company operates WordPress.com and has acquired or built services including Akismet, Gravatar, WooCommerce (acquired 2015), and Tumblr (acquired 2019). Automattic is notable for its fully distributed workforce, with employees working remotely from dozens of countries. Mullenweg also runs the investment firm Audrey Capital, which has invested in numerous startups.

Other work

Mullenweg is a proponent of open-source software and distributed work, and frequently writes on his blog, ma.tt. In 2024 he became involved in a public dispute with WP Engine, a major WordPress hosting company, over trademark use and contributions to the WordPress ecosystem.
