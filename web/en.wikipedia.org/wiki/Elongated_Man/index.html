The Elongated Man (Ralph Dibny) is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer John Broome and artist Carmine Infantino, he first appeared in The Flash #112 (May 1960) as a supporting character. A cheerful detective with stretching powers, he is notable as one of the first superheroes to reveal his secret identity to the public and to marry.

Fictional character biography

Fascinated as a youth by contortionists, Ralph Dibny discovered that celebrated "India rubber men" drank a soda derived from the rare "gingo" fruit. He isolated the extract and drank it, gaining the ability to stretch and reshape his body to extraordinary lengths. Rather than keep a secret identity, Dibny became a celebrity hero, travelling the world solving mysteries with his wife Sue Dibny, audiences invited to follow their adventures in his backup features in Detective Comics. His nose twitches whenever he smells a mystery.

Dibny was a member of several incarnations of the Justice League. In the 2004 storyline Identity Crisis, Sue Dibny was murdered, an event that profoundly affected Ralph; he was killed in the 2006–07 series 52, after which the Dibnys continued as ghost detectives. The character has appeared in animation and was portrayed by Hartley Sawyer in the television series The Flash.

Powers and abilities

The Elongated Man can stretch, flatten and contort his body to lengths of hundreds of metres while retaining full control, and his rubbery form resists bullets and impacts. He is also a genuinely talented detective and chemist, and his sense of smell sharpens when a mystery is near.
