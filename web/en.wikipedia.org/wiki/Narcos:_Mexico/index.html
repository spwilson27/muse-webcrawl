Narcos: Mexico
Genre: Crime drama, biographical
Created by: Carlo Bernard, Chris Brancato, Doug Miro
Starring: Michael Peña, Diego Luna
Country: United States
Original release: 2018–2021
Seasons: 3; Episodes: 30

Narcos: Mexico is an American crime drama television series created and produced by Chris Brancato, Carlo Bernard, and Doug Miro. It premiered on Netflix on November 16, 2018. Originally intended as a fourth season of the series Narcos, which focused on Colombia, it was ultimately developed as a companion series set in Mexico.

Plot

The series explores the origins of Mexico's modern drug war, beginning in the 1980s when the Guadalajara Cartel unified independent marijuana and cocaine smugglers into an organized criminal empire. The first two seasons follow Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) agent Kiki Camarena (Michael Peña) and cartel leader Miguel Ángel Félix Gallardo (Diego Luna), whose conflict culminates in Camarena's kidnapping and murder in 1985 and its political fallout. The third season follows journalist Andrea Núñez and examines the fragmented cartels that emerged in the 1990s after Félix Gallardo's arrest, including the rise of figures such as Amado Carrillo Fuentes and the Arellano Félix brothers.

Cast

The series stars Michael Peña as Kiki Camarena and Diego Luna as Félix Gallardo. Other cast members include Tenoch Huerta as Rafael Caro Quintero, Alyssa Diaz as Mika Camarena, Joaquín Cosío as Ernesto "Don Neto" Fonseca Carrillo, José María Yazpik as Amado Carrillo Fuentes, Scoot McNairy as the narrator Walt Breslin, and Luis Gerardo Méndez in the third season. The series is narrated in English voice-over while much of the dialogue is in Spanish with subtitles.

Production

Filming took place in Mexico. In September 2017, a location scout for the production, Carlos Muñoz Portal, was found murdered in a rural area near Temascalapa, State of Mexico, an event that drew international attention to the dangers of filming in regions affected by cartel violence.

Reception

Narcos: Mexico received generally positive reviews, with praise for its performances, particularly Luna's portrayal of Félix Gallardo. The third and final season premiered on November 5, 2021.
