Nuclear fission product

Nuclear fission products are the atomic fragments left after a large atomic nucleus undergoes nuclear fission.

Formation

Fission of uranium-235 or plutonium-239 typically splits the nucleus asymmetrically into two lighter nuclei with masses around 95 and 140, plus neutrons. Products include caesium-137, strontium-90, iodine-131 and technetium-99, many highly radioactive, decaying by beta emission through chains to stable isotopes.

Significance

Fission products generate decay heat after reactor shutdown, poison neutron flux (notably xenon-135), and constitute high-level radioactive waste requiring long-term management.
