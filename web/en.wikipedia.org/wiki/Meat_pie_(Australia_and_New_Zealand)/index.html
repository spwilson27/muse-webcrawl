Meat pie (Australia and New Zealand)

The meat pie is a hand-sized pie containing minced or diced meat and gravy, sometimes with onion, mushrooms or cheese. In Australia and New Zealand it is regarded as an iconic food, a staple of bakeries, service stations and sporting venues.

Composition

A standard pie has a shortcrust or flaky pastry base and a puff pastry lid, filled with minced beef in a thick gravy. Australian food standards require meat pies to contain at least 25 percent meat flesh. Variants include steak, curry, chicken, and bacon-and-egg pies; in New Zealand, mince and cheese is especially popular.

Culture

Pies are sold heated in paper bags and commonly eaten with tomato sauce. They are closely associated with Australian rules football matches and workplace "smoko" breaks, and commercial brands such as Four'N Twenty and Mrs Mac's are household names.
