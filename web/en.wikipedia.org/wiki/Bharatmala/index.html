Bharatmala

Launched2017
AgencyMinistry of Road Transport and Highways; NHAI
ScopeNational highway development across India

Bharatmala Pariyojana is a centrally sponsored and funded road and highways project of the Government of India, launched in 2017. The programme aims to optimise the efficiency of freight and passenger movement across the country by developing economic corridors, inter-corridor and feeder routes, border and international connectivity roads, coastal and port connectivity roads, and greenfield expressways.

Phase I

Phase I of Bharatmala envisaged the construction of about 34,800 km of national highways (including projects remaining under the earlier National Highways Development Project) with an approved outlay of about ₹5.35 lakh crore. Key components include around 9,000 km of economic corridors linking major production and consumption centres, expressways such as the Delhi–Mumbai Expressway, and roads improving access to border areas and ports.

The programme is implemented mainly by the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) and the National Highways and Infrastructure Development Corporation. Phase I timelines have been extended and costs revised upward as land acquisition and project execution proceeded.
