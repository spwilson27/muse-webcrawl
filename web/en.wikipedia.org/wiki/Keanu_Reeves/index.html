Keanu Reeves

Reeves in 2019

Keanu Charles Reeves (born 2 September 1964) is a Canadian actor. Born in Beirut and raised in Toronto, he began acting in theatre productions and television before making his feature film debut in Youngblood (1986). He gained fame for the comedy Bill & Ted's Excellent Adventure (1989) and its sequels, and established himself as an action star with Point Break (1991) and Speed (1994).

Career

Reeves achieved international stardom playing Neo in The Matrix (1999) and its sequels, a landmark of science-fiction cinema. His other notable roles include the exorcist John Constantine in Constantine (2005) and the hitman John Wick in the John Wick franchise beginning in 2014, which revitalised his career and spawned multiple sequels. He voiced Duke Caboom in Toy Story 4 (2019) and portrayed Johnny Silverhand in the video game Cyberpunk 2077 (2020).

Beyond acting, Reeves co-founded the production company Company Films, helped launch the motorcycle manufacturer ARCH Motorcycle, and co-created the comic book series BRZRKR. He has played bass guitar in the bands Dogstar and Becky.

Public image

Reeves is widely noted for his low-key, generous public persona; stories of his charitable giving and unpretentious behaviour have made him a popular cultural figure, frequently described in the press as one of the most likeable actors in Hollywood.

Early life

Reeves was born in Beirut, Lebanon, to an English mother and an American father of Chinese-Hawaiian descent. After his parents divorced, he moved with his mother to Sydney, then New York City, and finally Toronto, where he was raised. He attended several high schools and pursued ice hockey — he was a successful goalie — before turning fully to acting.
