AirPods Pro are wireless Bluetooth in-ear headphones designed by Apple, initially released on October 30, 2019. They are Apple's mid-range wireless headphones, sold alongside the base AirPods and the over-ear AirPods Max. Unlike the standard AirPods, they use silicone ear tips for an in-ear fit and feature active noise cancellation and transparency mode.

First generation

The first-generation AirPods Pro use the Apple H1 chip, support "Hey Siri", and include Adaptive EQ, sweat and water resistance (IPX4), and a wireless charging case. Squeezing the force sensor on the stem switches between noise cancellation and transparency, which uses outward-facing microphones to let in ambient sound.

Second generation

The second-generation model, released in September 2022, uses the H2 chip, which Apple claims provides up to twice the noise cancellation, along with improved sound, touch controls for volume on the stem, longer battery life (up to 6 hours per charge and 30 hours with the case), and a MagSafe charging case with a speaker for Find My alerts, a lanyard loop, and an Apple Watch charger compatibility. A revision in September 2023 replaced the case's Lightning port with USB-C and added dust resistance. Software updates later added features such as Adaptive Audio, Conversation Awareness, and a clinically oriented hearing-aid function.
