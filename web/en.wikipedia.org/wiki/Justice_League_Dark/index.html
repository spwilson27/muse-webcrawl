The Justice League Dark (JLD) is a fictional superhero team appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Introduced as part of the New 52 relaunch in 2011, the team consists of DC's supernatural and occult characters, and handles threats of a magical nature that the main Justice League is ill-equipped to confront.

Publication history

The series was announced in June 2011 and launched under writer Peter Milligan and artist Mikel Janín, drawing on characters long associated with DC's Vertigo imprint. Its founding storyline brought the team together to battle the Enchantress after she defeated the Justice League. Subsequent creative teams included Jeff Lemire and James Tynion IV, under whom the team was relaunched in 2018 with Wonder Woman as leader.

Fictional team biography

The original line-up united the sorceress Madame Xanadu, the occult detective John Constantine, the magician Zatanna, the ghost Deadman, and Shade, the Changing Man; later members included Swamp Thing, Frankenstein, Black Orchid, Doctor Fate, the Phantom Stranger and Man-Bat. Their headquarters is the House of Mystery. Major storylines include the quest for the Books of Magic, the "Trinity War" and "Forever Evil: Blight" crossovers, and the Witching Hour conflict with Hecate.

In other media

The team starred in the animated film Justice League Dark (2017) and its sequel Justice League Dark: Apokolips War (2020), and appeared in Constantine: City of Demons and other DC animated projects.
