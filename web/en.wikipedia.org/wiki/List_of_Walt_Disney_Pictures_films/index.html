List of Walt Disney Pictures films

This is a list of films produced by Walt Disney Pictures, the flagship film studio of the Walt Disney Company, founded as the Disney Brothers Cartoon Studio in 1923. The list spans animated features beginning with Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937), live-action films, and releases produced with studios such as Pixar.

Walt Disney Pictures
Founded1923 (as Disney Brothers Cartoon Studio)
First featureSnow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937)
ParentThe Walt Disney Company

1930s–1950s

Early animated features include Pinocchio and Fantasia (1940), Dumbo (1941), Bambi (1942), Cinderella (1950), Alice in Wonderland (1951), Peter Pan (1953), Lady and the Tramp (1955), and Sleeping Beauty (1959). Live-action production grew with Treasure Island (1950), 20,000 Leagues Under the Sea (1954), and Mary Poppins (1964).

Disney Renaissance and beyond

The period from 1989 to 1999 — the Disney Renaissance — produced The Little Mermaid, Beauty and the Beast, Aladdin, The Lion King, and Tarzan. Pixar's films, beginning with Toy Story (1995), were distributed by Disney before Pixar's acquisition in 2006. Later successes include Frozen (2013) and its sequel, Zootopia (2016), Moana (2016), and a series of live-action remakes such as Beauty and the Beast (2017) and The Lion King (2019).
