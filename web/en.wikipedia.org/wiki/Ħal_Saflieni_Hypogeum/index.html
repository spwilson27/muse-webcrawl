Ħal Saflieni Hypogeum

The Ħal Saflieni Hypogeum is a Neolithic subterranean structure in Paola, Malta, dating from the Saflieni phase (3300–3000 BC) of Maltese prehistory. It is the only known prehistoric underground temple in the world and has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1980.

Structure

The hypogeum is carved into globigerina limestone on three levels, with halls and chambers imitating megalithic temple architecture — including carved ceilings replicating corbelled roofs. The middle level contains the famous "Holy of Holies" and an oracle chamber with remarkable acoustic properties, where a niche amplifies a deep male voice through the complex.

Discoveries

Discovered accidentally in 1902 during house construction, the site yielded the remains of some 7,000 individuals and artefacts including the celebrated "Sleeping Lady" figurine. Visitor numbers are strictly limited to protect the fragile microclimate.
