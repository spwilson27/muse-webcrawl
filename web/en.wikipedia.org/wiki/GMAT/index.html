GMAT

The Graduate Management Admission Test (GMAT) is a computer adaptive test and assessment for measuring analytical, quantitative, verbal, and data literacy skills in written English for use in admission to a graduate management program, such as a Master of Business Administration (MBA) program.

The GMAT does not measure business knowledge or skill, nor does it measure intelligence. It is administered by the Graduate Management Admission Council (GMAC).

Format

The current version, the GMAT Focus Edition (introduced in 2023, and renamed simply the "GMAT" in 2024), has three 45-minute sections: Quantitative Reasoning (21 questions), Verbal Reasoning (23 questions), and Data Insights (20 questions). Total scores range from 205 to 805. The previous edition included Analytical Writing Assessment and Integrated Reasoning and was scored 200–800.

Use

More than 7,000 programs at over 2,300 graduate business schools worldwide accept the GMAT. Scores are valid for five years. The test is offered at Pearson VUE test centres and online, and costs about US$275 at test centres (varying by country).
