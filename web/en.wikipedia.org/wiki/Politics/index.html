Politics

Politics is the set of activities associated with making decisions in groups, or other forms of power relations among individuals, such as the distribution of resources or status. The academic study of politics is political science.

Governments and states

Politics operates at many levels, from local councils to national governments and international organisations. Political systems include democracies, monarchies, authoritarian and totalitarian regimes, distinguished by how power is obtained and exercised.

Ideologies

Major political ideologies include liberalism, conservatism, socialism, nationalism and environmentalism, differing over the role of the state, markets, tradition and equality.
