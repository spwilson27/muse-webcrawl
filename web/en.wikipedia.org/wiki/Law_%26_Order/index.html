Law & Order

GenrePolice procedural, legal drama
Created byDick Wolf
Original networkNBC
Original run1990–2010; revived 2022

Law & Order is an American police procedural and legal drama television series created by Dick Wolf and first broadcast on NBC in 1990. Set in New York City, each episode is typically divided between two halves: police detectives investigate a crime and arrest a suspect, after which prosecutors from the District Attorney's office take the case to trial.

Format and legacy

The series is known for its distinctive two-part structure, its opening narration, and its signature "dun-dun" scene-transition sound. Stories were frequently drawn from contemporary headlines. The original run of twenty seasons tied it among the longest-running primetime dramas in American television; the series was revived in 2022.

Franchise

Law & Order launched one of the most successful franchises in television history, including Law & Order: Special Victims Unit, Criminal Intent, and several other spin-offs, as well as international adaptations such as Law & Order: UK.
