En (typography)

An en is a typographic unit, half of an em. By definition, it is equivalent to half of the body height of the typeface (e.g., in 16-point type it is 8 points). The en gets its name from being supposedly equal in width to the capital letter "N" in the typeface in which it is used, just as the em is supposedly the width of the capital "M".

Usage

The en is most familiar as the width of the en dash (–), which is used principally to denote ranges (such as "pages 112–118") and, with spaces around it, in place of an em dash in some British styles. In typesetting, fixed spaces of en width (the en space, U+2002) are also available. The nut, a related term in letterpress printing, refers to an en quadrat — a piece of type metal an en wide used for spacing.
