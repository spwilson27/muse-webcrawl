Kingdom of Württemberg

The Kingdom of Württemberg was a German state in southwestern Germany from 1805 to 1918, with its capital at Stuttgart. It grew out of the Duchy of Württemberg, raised to an electorate in 1803 and to a kingdom by Napoleon in 1805, and joined the German Empire in 1871 while retaining its own king, army contingent and posts.

History

Frederick I, the first king, doubled the state's territory through mediatisation. His son William I granted a constitution in 1819. Württemberg industrialised around Stuttgart, Esslingen and Ulm, and after 1871 kept a measure of autonomy within the Empire. The monarchy ended in November 1918 when William II abdicated, and the kingdom became the Free People's State of Württemberg in the Weimar Republic.
