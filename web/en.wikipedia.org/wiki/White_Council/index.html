The White Council is a fictional council in J. R. R. Tolkien's legendarium of Middle-earth, appearing principally in The Lord of the Rings and The Silmarillion. It was a council of the Wise — the Wizards (Istari) and the chief Eldar of Middle-earth — formed in the Third Age to contend against the return and growing power of the Dark Lord Sauron.

History

The White Council was formally constituted around T.A. 2463, after the end of the Watchful Peace, when Sauron (in the guise of the Necromancer) began to re-establish himself at Dol Guldur in southern Mirkwood. Its members included the Wizards Saruman the White and Gandalf the Grey, together with Elven lords and ladies such as Galadriel, Elrond, and Círdan the Shipwright, as well as other chief Elves and possibly Radagast the Brown.

Galadriel wished Gandalf to lead the Council, but Gandalf refused, saying he would have no ties or allegiances save to those who sent him. Saruman was instead chosen as head of the Council, on account of his deep knowledge of Sauron's works and devices from the earlier ages.

The assault on Dol Guldur

In T.A. 2851 Gandalf, having entered Dol Guldur and confirmed that the Necromancer was Sauron returned, urged the Council to act. Saruman, however, dissuaded them, hoping that the One Ring would reveal itself if Sauron was left in peace, and seeking it for himself. Only in T.A. 2941 did Saruman at last agree, and the Council drove Sauron from Dol Guldur — an event that coincided with the Quest of Erebor recounted in The Hobbit. Sauron had anticipated the attack and withdrew to Mordor, where he soon openly declared himself.

The last meeting

The White Council met for the last time in T.A. 2953. At that meeting Saruman falsely assured the Council that the One Ring had been swept down the Anduin to the Sea and was lost forever. Thereafter he fortified Isengard and pursued his own designs, while Gandalf, suspicious, set a watch on the Shire and the heirs of Isildur. The Council never met again; its work was effectively superseded by the Fellowship of the Ring and the War of the Ring.

Members

Named members of the White Council across Tolkien's writings include Saruman (head), Gandalf, Galadriel, Elrond, and Círdan. Other members are described generally as "other lords of the Eldar" and the remaining Istari.
