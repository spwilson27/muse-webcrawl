Virgin Money

Virgin Money is a financial services brand whose UK banking arm, Virgin Money UK plc, operated as a challenger bank offering current accounts, savings, mortgages, credit cards, and insurance. It was part of Richard Branson's Virgin Group brand family.

History

The business began as Virgin Direct in 1995, a joint venture selling personal equity plans and later index-tracking funds. In 2010 Virgin Money acquired the small regional bank Church House Trust, gaining a banking licence, and in January 2012 it completed the purchase of the nationalised Northern Rock plc for about £747 million, acquiring its branch network and mortgage book. The bank floated on the London Stock Exchange in 2014.

In 2018 CYBG plc, owner of Clydesdale Bank and Yorkshire Bank, acquired Virgin Money for £1.7 billion and adopted the Virgin Money name for the combined group, retiring the Clydesdale and Yorkshire brands. In 2024 Nationwide Building Society agreed to acquire Virgin Money UK for about £2.9 billion, the largest UK bank takeover since the financial crisis, with the Virgin Money brand to be phased out over time.

Other Virgin Money businesses

Related brands operated internationally: Virgin Money Australia (sold to Bank of Queensland) and Virgin Money South Africa, among others.
