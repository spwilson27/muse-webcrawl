Galileo is a global distribution system (GDS) owned by Travelport, used by travel agencies and online booking services to search, price, and book airline seats, hotel rooms, rental cars, rail journeys, and other travel products.

History

The system traces its origins to United Airlines' Apollo reservation system (created in 1971) and to the European Galileo consortium founded in 1987 by a group of European and North American airlines, including British Airways, Swissair, KLM, and Alitalia. The European and North American systems merged in the 1990s as Galileo International, which later became part of Travelport alongside the Worldspan and Apollo brands.

Function

Galileo connects travel suppliers' inventories with tens of thousands of travel retailers, processing fare searches and ticket issuance in real time; travel agents traditionally interacted with it through cryptic command formats, now supplemented by graphical and API interfaces.
