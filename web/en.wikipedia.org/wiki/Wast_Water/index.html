Wast Water

Wast Water or Wastwater is a lake in the Wasdale valley of the Lake District National Park in Cumbria, England. At about 79 metres deep it is the deepest lake in England.

Setting

The lake lies beneath steep scree slopes — the famous Wastwater Screes — and at the head of the valley stand some of England's highest fells, including Scafell Pike. The view westward across the lake toward Great Gable forms the model for the Lake District National Park's logo. The lake is owned by the National Trust, and its cold, nutrient-poor waters support arctic char; water is abstracted by the nuclear industry at Sellafield under licence.

Folklore

Local legend associates the lake with a drowned "water witch" and with the underwater "gnome garden" left by divers. The lake has also figured in several celebrated crime and mystery stories, and bodies recovered from its depths have earned it a macabre reputation.
