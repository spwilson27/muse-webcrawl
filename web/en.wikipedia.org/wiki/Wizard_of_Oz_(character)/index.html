Wizard of Oz (character)

Wizard of Oz
Full name: Oscar Zoroaster Phadrig Isaac Norman Henkle Emmannuel Ambroise Diggs
First appearance: The Wonderful Wizard of Oz (1900)
Created by: L. Frank Baum

The Wizard of Oz is a fictional character in the Land of Oz created by American author L. Frank Baum. He is depicted as an elderly man who has been the Ruler of the Emerald City for as long as anyone can remember. He is named Oscar Diggs, a balloonist who was carried by the wind from Omaha to the Land of Oz and, after defeating the Wicked Witch of the West, engaged in enough humbug to be taken for a powerful sorcerer.

Character

The Wizard appears in the first and most famous Oz book, The Wonderful Wizard of Oz (1900). Dorothy Gale and her friends the Scarecrow, Tin Woodman, and Cowardly Lion travel to the Emerald City to ask the Wizard for his help, only to discover that he is not a great wizard at all, but a "humbug" — an ordinary man who uses stage tricks, ventriloquism and a balloon to maintain his mystique.

Later books

In Baum's later books the Wizard returns to Oz, becomes a genuine magician under the tutelage of Glinda, and is granted permission to perform magic. He serves as an inventor and adviser to Princess Ozma.

In other media

The character was played by Frank Morgan in the 1939 MGM film The Wizard of Oz, by James Franco in Oz the Great and Powerful (2013), and by Jeff Goldblum in the film adaptation of Wicked (2024).
