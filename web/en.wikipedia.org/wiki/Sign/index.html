A sign is an object, quality, event or entity whose presence or occurrence indicates the probable presence or occurrence of something else. In semiotics, the sign is the basic unit of meaning-making: Peirce's triad divides signs into icons (resembling their object, like a portrait), indices (causally connected, like smoke for fire or a medical symptom) and symbols (connected by convention, like words).

The word also covers traffic and commercial signs, medical signs (objective findings, versus subjective symptoms), mathematical signs (+, −, √) and zodiac signs. In medicine since Hippocrates, reading signs has been a core diagnostic art; in everyday usage 'sign' and 'symbol' blur, but theorists reserve 'symbol' for conventional, often layered meanings.
