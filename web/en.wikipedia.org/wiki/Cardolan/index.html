Cardolan

Cardolan is a fictional realm in J. R. R. Tolkien's legendarium. It was one of the three kingdoms into which Arnor, the northern kingdom of the Dúnedain in Middle-earth, was divided in the 10th century of the Third Age, alongside Arthedain and Rhudaur.

Cardolan
Parent realm: Arnor
Founded: T.A. 861
Ended: T.A. 1636 (great plague)
Location: Southern Eriador

History

Cardolan occupied the lands between the rivers Baranduin (Brandywine) and Gwathló, including the Barrow-downs and the Old Forest's eastern marches. It disputed the Weather Hills and the North-South Road with Arthedain and Rhudaur, and suffered in the wars with Angmar. Invasions in T.A. 1409 destroyed much of the kingdom; the Great Plague of 1636 killed the royal line and most of the population, after which Cardolan effectively ceased to exist, though a remnant of Dúnedain held the Barrow-downs for a time.

Legacy

The burial mounds of Cardolan's princes became the haunted Barrow-downs encountered in The Fellowship of the Ring.
