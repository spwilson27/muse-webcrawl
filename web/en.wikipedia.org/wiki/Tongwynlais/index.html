Tongwynlais

TongwynlaisCountry: Wales
Principal area: Cardiff
Population: around 1,900

Tongwynlais is a village and community in the north of Cardiff, Wales, lying at the foot of the Taff Gorge near the River Taff. It is best known for Castell Coch, the fairytale-style castle that overlooks the village.

History

The village grew around ironworking and quarrying along the Taff, served by the Glamorganshire Canal and later railways. The name derives from Welsh elements meaning roughly "the meadow of the church" or similar, and the parish church is dedicated to St Michael.

Castell Coch

Castell Coch ("Red Castle") was rebuilt in the 1870s by architect William Burges for the 3rd Marquess of Bute on the ruins of a medieval fortification. Its conical towers make it one of the most recognisable landmarks in South Wales.

Transport

Tongwynlais sits close to junction 32 of the M4 motorway, and the Taff Trail cycle route passes through the village.
