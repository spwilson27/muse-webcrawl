B.G. (rapper)

B.G.
Birth name: Christopher Noel Dorsey
Born: September 3, 1980, New Orleans, Louisiana, U.S.
Genres: Hip hop, Southern hip hop
Labels: Cash Money, Universal

Christopher Noel Dorsey (born September 3, 1980), better known by his stage name B.G. (short for Baby Gangsta), is an American rapper from New Orleans, Louisiana. He was a prominent member of Cash Money Records and the Hot Boys alongside Lil Wayne, Juvenile and Turk.

Career

Dorsey began recording as a teenager, signing to Cash Money in the early 1990s. His albums Chopper City (1996), It's All on U, Vol. 1 and Vol. 2 established him regionally before Chopper City in the Ghetto (1999) reached national success, featuring the single "Bling Bling" — a phrase widely credited with popularising the term for flashy jewellery.

As a member of the Hot Boys, he released Get It How U Live! (1997) and Guerrilla Warfare (1999). After leaving Cash Money in 2001 following disputes over finances, he founded Chopper City Records and released independent albums including Livin' Legend (2003) and The Heart of tha Streetz volumes.

Legal issues and later life

In 2012 Dorsey was sentenced to 14 years in federal prison on gun and witness-tampering charges. He was released in 2023 and resumed recording and performing, reuniting publicly with former Cash Money collaborators.

Discography

Studio albums include True Story (1995), Chopper City, Chopper City in the Ghetto, Checkmate (2000), Livin' Legend, Life After Cash Money (2004) and Too Hood 2 Be Hollywood (2009).
