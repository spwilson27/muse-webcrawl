The Chevrolet Corvette (C8) is the eighth generation of the Corvette sports car, manufactured by Chevrolet since 2020. It is the first Corvette with a rear mid-engine layout — a radical departure from the front-engine configuration used since 1953 — and the first since 1982 offered without a manual transmission, using an 8-speed dual-clutch automatic instead.

Models

The launch Stingray is powered by the 6.2-litre LT2 V8 producing 495 hp with the performance exhaust, reaching 0–60 mph in under 3 seconds with the Z51 package. The Z06 (2023) introduced the 5.5-litre LT6 flat-plane-crank V8 producing 670 hp, the most powerful naturally aspirated production V8. The E-Ray (2024) added an electric front axle, making it the first hybrid and first all-wheel-drive Corvette. The twin-turbocharged ZR1 (2025) produces 1,064 hp, and the hybrid ZR1X exceeds 1,250 hp.

Reception

The C8 was widely praised for bringing supercar performance and layout to a comparatively accessible price, winning North American Car of the Year for 2020. It is built at Bowling Green Assembly in Kentucky and was the first Corvette offered in right-hand drive for export markets.
