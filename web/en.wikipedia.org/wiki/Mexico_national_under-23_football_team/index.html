The Mexico national under-23 football team represents Mexico in international football competitions for players aged 23 and under, most prominently the football tournament of the Olympic Games and the CONCACAF Men's Olympic Qualifying Championship. It is controlled by the Mexican Football Federation.

History

The under-23 side came to prominence after the Olympic football tournament adopted an under-23 format, with a small number of over-age players permitted. Mexico's greatest triumph at this level came at the London 2012 Olympics, where the team defeated Brazil 2–1 in the final at Wembley Stadium to win the gold medal — Mexico's first Olympic football title and one of the most celebrated results in the country's sporting history. Oribe Peralta scored both goals in the final.

Mexico also won the bronze medal at the Tokyo 2020 Olympics (held in 2021), defeating hosts Japan 3–1 in the third-place match. The team has regularly won the CONCACAF Olympic qualifying tournament, earning further titles in cycles leading to the 1996, 2004, 2012, 2016 and 2020 Games.

Competitive record

In addition to its Olympic success, the under-23 team has competed in the Pan American Games football tournament, winning gold medals on several occasions, and in invitational events such as the Toulon Tournament (now the Tournoi Maurice Revello), which Mexico won in 2012.
