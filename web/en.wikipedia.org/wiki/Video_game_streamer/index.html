Video game streamer

A video game streamer is a type of online streamer who broadcasts themselves playing video games live, typically with commentary and audience interaction via chat. Streaming grew with platforms such as Justin.tv's gaming spin-off Twitch (2011), YouTube Gaming and Facebook Gaming.

Content

Streamers specialise in genres or formats: competitive play, speedrunning, Let's Play walkthroughs, variety streaming and esports commentary. Top personalities such as Ninja, Pokimane and xQc drew audiences rivalling television, signing exclusive platform deals worth tens of millions of dollars.

Revenue

Income comes from subscriptions, donations, advertising, sponsorships and merchandise. Most streamers earn little; a small top tier earns the majority of platform revenue.

Culture

Streaming reshaped game marketing — a hit stream can revive an older title — and created communities with their own emotes, memes and charity events such as Games Done Quick.
