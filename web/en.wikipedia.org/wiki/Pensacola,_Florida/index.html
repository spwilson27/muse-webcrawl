Pensacola, Florida

Pensacola is a city in and the county seat of Escambia County, Florida, United States, located on the northwestern coast of the Florida Panhandle on Pensacola Bay, an inlet of the Gulf of Mexico. Its population was about 54,000 at the 2020 census.

Pensacola is nicknamed "The City of Five Flags" because five flags have flown over it: those of Spain, France, Britain, the Confederacy, and the United States. A Spanish settlement was first attempted there in 1559 by Tristán de Luna, making it one of the earliest European settlement sites in the continental United States, though it was soon abandoned; the town was permanently re-established in 1698.

The city is home to Naval Air Station Pensacola, the primary training base for naval aviation and the home of the Blue Angels flight demonstration squadron. Tourism, healthcare, and the military are major parts of the local economy, and the area is known for its white-sand beaches.
