List of rivers by age

This is a list of the oldest rivers in the world, ranked by their geological age. The age of a river is generally taken to be the age of the drainage system or of the valley through which it flows, and is estimated from the geological record of the surrounding terrain. Several of the world's oldest rivers predate the mountain ranges they now cut through; such rivers are called antecedent streams, because they maintained their course as the land rose around them.
