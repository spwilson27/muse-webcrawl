Chicago Med is an American medical drama television series created by Dick Wolf and Matt Olmstead, and is the third installment of Wolf Entertainment's Chicago franchise. The series premiered on NBC on 17 November 2015. It follows the emergency department (ED) doctors and nurses of the fictional Gaffney Chicago Medical Center as they tackle unique cases inspired by occurrences in the city of Chicago.

Premise

The show centres on the ED attending physicians, residents, nurses and psychiatrists of Gaffney, whose hospital regularly receives the victims of the mass-casualty events that also feature in the sibling series. Storylines alternate between the patients of the week, the staff's professional rivalries and romances, and the ethics of practising medicine under Chicago's pressures. Dr. Will Halstead (Nick Gehlfuss), Dr. Natalie Manning (Torrey DeVitto), Dr. Daniel Charles (Oliver Platt) and Maggie Lockwood (Marlyne Barrett) are among its long-running characters.

Production

Filmed on location in Chicago, Chicago Med spun off from Chicago Fire, where its pilot aired as a backdoor episode. The series has been renewed through multiple seasons and has crossed over repeatedly with Chicago Fire and Chicago P.D., as well as with the now-cancelled Chicago Justice. The show's medical cases are developed with the advice of working physicians to maintain technical plausibility.
