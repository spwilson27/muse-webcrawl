Pisces (♓) is the twelfth and final astrological sign in the zodiac. It is a negative, mutable sign. It spans 330° to 360° of celestial longitude. Under the tropical zodiac, the Sun transits this area between February 19 and March 20. In sidereal astrology, the Sun transits the constellation of Pisces around March 14 to April 14.

According to some astrologers, Pisces is the sign of Pisces because it is associated with the god Poseidon, deity of the sea. The sign is also said to be ruled by Neptune and Jupiter, and its symbol is two fish swimming in opposite directions, yet bound by a cord — said to represent the tension between the soul, the personality, and the material and spiritual worlds.

Background

The constellation Pisces was known to the Babylonians as Šinunutu, "the swallow", associated with the goddess Inanna. The Greeks adopted the constellation and its associated myth, in which Aphrodite and Eros leapt into a river to escape the monster Typhon and were transformed into fish, or carried to safety by two fish — the origin of the two-fish symbol.

Characteristics

In astrology, Pisces is a water sign, along with Cancer and Scorpio. People born under the sign are traditionally described as compassionate, intuitive, artistic, and gentle, though also prone to escapism and melancholy. Such characterizations are not supported by scientific evidence; astrology has been shown to have no validity as a description of personality.

Compatibility

Pisces is said to be most compatible with the fellow water signs Cancer and Scorpio, and with the earth signs Taurus and Capricorn.
