Dragon Ball Z

Dragon Ball Z (Japanese: ドラゴンボールZ) is a Japanese anime television series produced by Toei Animation, adapting the later chapters of Akira Toriyama's Dragon Ball manga. It aired in Japan from 1989 to 1996 for 291 episodes.

Story

Five years after Dragon Ball, adult Goku learns he is a Saiyan, a warrior race. With his son Gohan and allies including Vegeta, Piccolo and Krillin, he defends Earth through the Saiyan, Frieza, Cell and Majin Buu sagas, achieving transformations such as Super Saiyan that became genre-defining.

Reception and legacy

Syndication and the Funimation dub on Toonami in the late 1990s made DBZ a gateway anime worldwide, hugely influential on shōnen action series. A digitally remastered, filler-reduced recut, Dragon Ball Z Kai (2009–2015), was followed as sequel by Dragon Ball Super.
