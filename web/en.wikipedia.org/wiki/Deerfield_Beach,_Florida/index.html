Deerfield Beach, Florida

Deerfield Beach is a city in Broward County, Florida, United States, just south of the Palm Beach County line. It is part of the Miami–Fort Lauderdale–West Palm Beach metropolitan area. The city's population was 86,859 at the 2020 census. Deerfield Beach is known for its beaches, its International Fishing Pier, and Sullivan Park on the Intracoastal Waterway.

History

The area was first settled in the 1890s as a farming community along the Hillsboro River, growing beans and other vegetables for northern markets. The settlement took the name Deerfield from the herds of deer that grazed along the riverbanks. The town was incorporated in 1925, and in 1939 its name was changed to Deerfield Beach to emphasize its coastal attractions. Agriculture, particularly green beans, remained important into the mid-20th century, after which tourism, retirement communities, and suburban development transformed the city. Century Village, a large retirement community built in the 1970s, significantly increased the population.

Geography

Deerfield Beach is located in northeastern Broward County, bordered by Boca Raton to the north, Pompano Beach and Lighthouse Point to the south, and the Atlantic Ocean to the east. The Intracoastal Waterway passes through the eastern part of the city, separating the barrier-island beach area — including the cove area around the International Fishing Pier — from the mainland. The city has a tropical climate, with hot, wet summers and mild, drier winters.

Demographics and economy

The 2020 census recorded 86,859 residents. The city has significant Brazilian and Haitian communities. Its economy is based largely on tourism, retail, health care, and services for its large retiree population. Major attractions include the beach and pier, Quiet Waters Park — which offers water skiing, mountain bike trails, and a water park — and the South Florida Railway Museum at the historic Seaboard Air Line station.

Transportation

Interstate 95 and Florida's Turnpike run through or near the city, and the Deerfield Beach station is served by Amtrak and Tri-Rail commuter trains.
