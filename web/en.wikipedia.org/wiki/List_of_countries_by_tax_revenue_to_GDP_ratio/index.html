List of countries by tax revenue to GDP ratio

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This article lists countries by their tax revenue as a percentage of gross domestic product (GDP), including social security contributions where reported. Figures are principally drawn from the OECD Revenue Statistics for member countries and from IMF and World Bank data elsewhere. High ratios are characteristic of European welfare states; the lowest ratios are found in resource-rich states funded by oil rents and in countries with large informal economies.

Interpretation

The tax-to-GDP ratio is a rough indicator of the scale of government in the economy and of a state's fiscal capacity. Ratios tend to rise with income levels (an empirical regularity known as Wagner's law). Comparisons are complicated by differing treatment of social contributions, subnational taxes, resource revenues and tax expenditures.
