Lucerne

CountrySwitzerland
LakeLake Lucerne
LandmarkChapel Bridge
[Image: The Chapel Bridge and old town of Lucerne]
The Chapel Bridge and old town of Lucerne

Lucerne (Luzern) is a city in central Switzerland on the western end of Lake Lucerne, where the river Reuss leaves the lake. Its covered wooden bridges and old town beneath Mount Pilatus make it one of Switzerland's most visited cities.

Sights

The Chapel Bridge (Kapellbrücke, 1333) — Europe's oldest covered wooden bridge, its interior painted with 17th-century panels, largely rebuilt after a 1993 fire — and the octagonal Water Tower are the city's emblems; the Spreuer Bridge preserves a Dance of Death cycle. The Lion Monument, carved into a cliff face, commemorates the Swiss Guards killed in the French Revolution and was called by Mark Twain the saddest stone in the world. The Rosengart collection, the KKL concert hall by Jean Nouvel and the Swiss Transport Museum complete a dense cultural offer between lake steamers and mountain railways.
