List of Antarctic expeditions

This is a list of notable expeditions to Antarctica, from the first sightings of the continent in 1820 through the Heroic Age of Antarctic Exploration and into the modern era of permanent research stations.

19th century

YearsExpeditionLeader / vesselNotes
1820Russian expeditionFabian Gottlieb von Bellingshausen, Vostok and MirnyFirst sighting of the Antarctic mainland (27 January 1820)
1820American sealing voyageNathaniel Palmer, HeroSighted the Antarctic Peninsula
1837–1840French expeditionJules Dumont d'Urville, AstrolabeDiscovered Adélie Land; named for his wife
1838–1842United States Exploring ExpeditionCharles WilkesCharted Wilkes Land
1839–1843British expeditionJames Clark Ross, HMS Erebus and HMS TerrorDiscovered the Ross Sea, Ross Ice Shelf, and Mount Erebus
1897–1899Belgian Antarctic ExpeditionAdrien de Gerlache, BelgicaFirst expedition to winter in Antarctica
1898–1900Southern Cross ExpeditionCarsten BorchgrevinkFirst wintering on the Antarctic mainland (Cape Adare)

YearsExpeditionLeaderNotes
1901–1904Discovery ExpeditionRobert Falcon ScottFirst exploration of the interior
1901–1903Gauss Expedition (First German Antarctic Expedition)Erich von DrygalskiDiscovered Kaiser Wilhelm II Land
1902–1904Scottish National Antarctic ExpeditionWilliam Speirs BruceEstablished Orcadas station
1907–1909Nimrod ExpeditionErnest ShackletonCame within 97 miles of the South Pole; first ascent of Mount Erebus
1910–1912Norwegian expeditionRoald Amundsen, FramFirst to reach the South Pole, 14 December 1911
1910–1913Terra Nova ExpeditionRobert Falcon ScottReached the Pole 17 January 1912; Scott's party perished on the return
1911–1914Australasian Antarctic ExpeditionDouglas MawsonFamous lone survival trek
1914–1917Imperial Trans-Antarctic ExpeditionErnest Shackleton, EnduranceShip crushed by ice; crew rescued after Shackleton's open-boat journey
1921–1922Shackleton–Rowett ExpeditionErnest ShackletonShackleton died at South Georgia; marks the end of the Heroic Age
