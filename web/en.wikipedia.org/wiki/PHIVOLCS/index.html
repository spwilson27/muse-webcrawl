Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology

The Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology (PHIVOLCS) is a Philippine government agency under the Department of Science and Technology responsible for monitoring volcanoes, earthquakes, and tsunamis, and for issuing warnings to reduce disaster losses.

History

Its origins lie in earlier seismological and volcanological commissions; it took its present name in 1984. It gained prominence for its work around the 1991 eruption of Mount Pinatubo, one of the largest eruptions of the 20th century, and the 2020 Taal eruption.

Work

PHIVOLCS operates seismic and volcano monitoring networks, maintains alert level systems for active volcanoes such as Mayon, Taal, Kanlaon, and Bulusan, developed the PHIVOLCS Earthquake Intensity Scale (PEIS), and provides hazard maps and public education.
