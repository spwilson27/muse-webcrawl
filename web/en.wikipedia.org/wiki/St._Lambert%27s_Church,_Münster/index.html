St. Lambert's Church, Münster

St. Lambert's Church (St. Lamberti) is a Roman Catholic parish church in Münster, North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany, built in the late Gothic style between about 1375 and 1525. It stands on the Prinzipalmarkt, the city's historic market square, and its tower is a landmark of Münster.

The Anabaptist cages

The church is famous for three iron cages hanging from its tower. In 1536, after the fall of the Anabaptist Münster rebellion, the corpses of its leaders — Jan van Leiden, Bernhard Krechting, and Bernhard Knipperdolling — were displayed in the cages as a warning. The original cages still hang on the tower today. The church also maintains one of Germany's last tower watchmen traditions, with a horn blown nightly from the tower.
