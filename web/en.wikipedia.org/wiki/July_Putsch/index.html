July Putsch

The July Putsch was a failed coup d'état attempt by Austrian Nazis on 25 July 1934 against the Austrofascist government of Engelbert Dollfuss. SS men disguised as soldiers and police seized the Federal Chancellery in Vienna, where Dollfuss was shot and left to die, and occupied the RAVAG radio station, broadcasting a false report of his resignation.

Outcome

The coup failed because most of the army and police remained loyal and Mussolini mobilised Italian troops on the Brenner Pass, deterring German intervention. The conspirators surrendered; several were executed and the Austrian Nazi Party was driven further underground. Kurt Schuschnigg succeeded Dollfuss as chancellor. Hitler publicly disavowed the putsch, but it foreshadowed the Anschluss of 1938.
