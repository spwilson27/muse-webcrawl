Northrop Grumman B-21 Raider

The Northrop Grumman B-21 Raider is an American strategic bomber under development for the United States Air Force (USAF) by Northrop Grumman. A long-range, stealth, heavy strategic bomber capable of delivering both conventional and thermonuclear weapons, it is intended to replace the B-1 Lancer and B-2 Spirit and eventually form the backbone of the US bomber fleet alongside the B-52 Stratofortress.

Development

The B-21 was developed under the Long Range Strike Bomber (LRS-B) programme. Northrop Grumman won the contract in October 2015, and the aircraft was named "Raider" in honour of the Doolittle Raiders of the Second World War. The first B-21 was publicly unveiled at a rollout ceremony at Air Force Plant 42 in Palmdale, California, on 2 December 2022, and the type made its first flight on 10 November 2023.

Design

The B-21 is a flying-wing design resembling a smaller, evolved B-2, incorporating advances in low-observable materials and maintainability, an open-systems architecture for rapid upgrades, and the ability to operate optionally uncrewed. Details of its size, engines and performance remain classified; it is believed to be somewhat smaller than the B-2 with a single weapons bay. The Air Force plans to acquire at least 100 aircraft, and the type is expected to enter service in the mid-to-late 2020s, with Ellsworth Air Force Base, South Dakota, as its first main operating base.
