The Arena District is a mixed-use entertainment and business district in downtown Columbus, Ohio, in the United States. Developed around Nationwide Arena, home of the Columbus Blue Jackets of the National Hockey League, the district transformed a formerly industrial area northwest of the city center into one of Columbus's principal nightlife and office centers.

History

The district occupies land that included the site of the old Ohio Penitentiary, which closed in 1984 and was demolished in the late 1990s. Following voter approval of arena funding arrangements, Nationwide Arena opened in 2000, anchoring a wave of private development that included offices, apartments, restaurants, bars, and event venues.

Venues and attractions

In addition to Nationwide Arena, the district includes Huntington Park, the home stadium of the Columbus Clippers minor-league baseball team, which opened in 2009, as well as concert venues such as KEMBA Live!, the North Market area nearby, and numerous hotels and residential buildings. The district is known for crowds before and after sporting events and concerts.
