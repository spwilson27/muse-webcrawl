Avengers: Infinity War

Avengers: Infinity War is a 2018 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics superhero team the Avengers. Produced by Marvel Studios and distributed by Walt Disney Studios Motion Pictures, it is the sequel to The Avengers (2012) and Avengers: Age of Ultron (2015), and the 19th film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU). Directed by Anthony and Joe Russo and written by Christopher Markus and Stephen McFeely, the film features an ensemble cast including Robert Downey Jr., Chris Hemsworth, Mark Ruffalo, Chris Evans, Scarlett Johansson, Benedict Cumberbatch and Josh Brolin as Thanos.

Plot

The Avengers and their allies must risk everything to stop the intergalactic warlord Thanos from collecting the six Infinity Stones, with which he intends to snap his fingers and wipe out half of all life in the universe. Despite battles on Earth and in space, and the sacrifice of Gamora and Vision, Thanos completes his gauntlet and succeeds, leaving the surviving heroes in defeat.

Release and reception

The film premiered in Los Angeles on 23 April 2018 and was released in the United States on 27 April, shot entirely with IMAX digital cameras. It grossed over $2 billion worldwide, becoming the first superhero film to do so and the highest-grossing film of 2018. Its ending, in which the villain wins, was widely discussed as an audacious cliffhanger; the sequel, Avengers: Endgame, followed in 2019.
