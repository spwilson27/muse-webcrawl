Annapurna Interactive, LLC is an American video game publisher founded in 2016 as the gaming division of Annapurna Pictures. The company built a reputation for publishing artistically ambitious independent games, many of which received major critical acclaim and industry awards.

History

Annapurna Interactive was announced on 1 December 2016, with a team drawn from the games industry and from Annapurna Pictures. Its first titles included What Remains of Edith Finch and Gorogoa. Subsequent successes included Outer Wilds, Sayonara Wild Hearts, Telling Lies, The Artful Escape, Neon White, Stray and Cocoon. In September 2024, following a dispute over spinning the division out from the parent company, the publisher's president and much of its staff resigned en masse; the company subsequently rebuilt its team and continued publishing.
