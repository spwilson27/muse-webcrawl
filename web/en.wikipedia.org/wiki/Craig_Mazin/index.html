Craig Mazin

Craig Mazin (born April 8, 1971) is an American writer, director, and producer. He is best known as the creator, writer, and executive producer of the HBO historical drama miniseries Chernobyl (2019), for which he won two Primetime Emmy Awards, and as co-creator of the HBO post-apocalyptic drama series The Last of Us (2023–present), adapted from the video game of the same name.

Career

Mazin, a Princeton University graduate, began his career in comedy, writing films including Senseless (1998), Scary Movie 3 and 4, Superhero Movie, and The Hangover Part II and III. He co-hosted the long-running screenwriting podcast Scriptnotes with John August. His turn to drama with Chernobyl, a five-part dramatisation of the 1986 nuclear disaster, was a major critical success, winning the Emmy for Outstanding Limited Series and for writing.

With Neil Druckmann he adapted The Last of Us for HBO, earning further Emmy nominations; he has also been attached to screenplays for a Borderlands film (uncredited on release) and a Pirates of the Caribbean reboot.
