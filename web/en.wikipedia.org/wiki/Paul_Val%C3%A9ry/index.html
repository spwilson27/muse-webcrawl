Paul Valéry

Ambroise Paul Toussaint Jules Valéry (30 October 1871 – 20 July 1945) was a French poet, essayist and philosopher, one of the leading literary figures of early 20th-century France.

Born30 October 1871, Sète, France
Died20 July 1945, Paris, France
OccupationPoet, essayist, philosopher

Poetry

After a youthful crisis in 1892 Valéry largely abandoned poetry for two decades, devoting himself to the study of mind and method in his voluminous notebooks (the Cahiers). He returned with La Jeune Parque (1917) and the collection Charmes (1922), containing "Le Cimetière marin" ("The Graveyard by the Sea"), widely considered one of the greatest French poems of the century.

Prose and public life

His prose character Monsieur Teste embodies his fascination with pure intellect. Valéry was elected to the Académie française in 1925 and became a prominent public intellectual between the wars. He died in Paris in July 1945 and received a state funeral.
