TikTok is a short-form video hosting service owned by the Chinese company ByteDance. Launched internationally after ByteDance's earlier app Douyin for the Chinese market, it merged with Musical.ly in 2018 and became one of the most downloaded apps in the world.

Its 'For You' recommendation feed, editing tools, sounds and duets shaped short-video culture and prompted rivals including Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. TikTok has faced sustained political and regulatory scrutiny in the United States, India (which banned it in 2020) and elsewhere over data security and its Chinese ownership.

Features

Vertical videos from seconds to several minutes, live streaming, effects and filters, a large licensed music/sound library, and e-commerce through TikTok Shop in some markets.
