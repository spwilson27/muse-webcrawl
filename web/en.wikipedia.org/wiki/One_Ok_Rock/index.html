One Ok Rock
Origin: Tokyo, Japan
Genres: Alternative rock, post-hardcore, pop punk
Years active: 2005–present
Members: Taka, Toru, Ryota, Tomoya

One Ok Rock (stylized as ONE OK ROCK) is a Japanese rock band formed in Tokyo in 2005. The band consists of vocalist Takahiro "Taka" Moriuchi, guitarist and leader Toru Yamashita, bassist Ryota Kohama, and drummer Tomoya Kanki. The name derives from "one o'clock", the time at which the band originally rehearsed.

Career

The band made its major debut in 2007 and rose to prominence in Japan with albums such as Niche Syndrome (2010) and Zankyo Reference (2011). Their 2013 album Jinsei×Boku= included songs tied to the Rurouni Kenshin films, broadening their audience. Known for mixing Japanese and English lyrics and for energetic live shows in stadiums and arenas, they have toured extensively overseas.

International activity

From the mid-2010s, One Ok Rock signed with Fueled by Ramen in the United States and released increasingly English-language albums, including 35xxxv (2015), Ambitions (2017), Eye of the Storm (2019), and Luxury Disease (2022), collaborating with artists such as Avril Lavigne, 5 Seconds of Summer, and Ed Sheeran.
