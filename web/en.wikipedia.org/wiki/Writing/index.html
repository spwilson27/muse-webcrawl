Writing is the recording of language by means of visible marks, and one of humanity's defining inventions. It emerged independently in Mesopotamia (cuneiform) and Egypt (hieroglyphs) around 3400–3200 BC, and later in China and Mesoamerica, initially for accounting and administration.

Development

Writing systems range from logographic (characters for words, as in Chinese) through syllabaries to alphabets derived ultimately from Phoenician script. Writing transformed law, literature, science and bureaucracy — making publishing and this encyclopedia possible — and its decipherment (as with hieroglyphs by Egyptologists) unlocked lost civilisations.
