Merkava

The Merkava (Hebrew: מרכבה, "chariot") is a series of main battle tanks used by the Israel Defense Forces and the backbone of the IDF's Armored Corps. Development began in 1970 under Major General Israel Tal, with crew survivability as the paramount design priority; the first Merkava Mark I entered service in 1979.

Design

Unusually, the Merkava mounts its engine at the front, providing additional protection to the crew compartment behind it. The rear of the hull can carry infantry, wounded soldiers, or extra ammunition in place of some ammunition storage. Later marks added modular composite armour, improved fire control, and — on the Mark IV — the Trophy active protection system against anti-tank missiles.
