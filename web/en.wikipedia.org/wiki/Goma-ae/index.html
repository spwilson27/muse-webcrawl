Goma-ae

Goma-ae
CourseSide dish
Place of originJapan
Main ingredientsVegetables, sesame seeds, sugar, soy sauce

Goma-ae (胡麻和え), sometimes spelled gomaae or gomae, is a Japanese side dish of vegetables dressed with a sweet ground-sesame sauce. The name combines goma (sesame seeds) and ae (to dress or mix), referring to a family of dressed salads in Japanese home cooking and shōjin ryōri (Buddhist vegetarian cuisine).

Preparation

The most common version uses spinach (hōrensō no goma-ae), which is blanched, squeezed dry and cut into short lengths. Green beans (ingen), broccoli, carrots and other vegetables are also used. The dressing is made by toasting sesame seeds, grinding them in a suribachi (grinding bowl), and mixing with sugar, soy sauce and sometimes dashi or miso. The vegetables are tossed with the dressing just before serving.

Variations

Related aemono dressings include shira-ae, based on mashed tofu, and sumiso-ae, based on vinegar and miso. Goma-ae is a standard component of Japanese set meals (teishoku) and bento boxes, and has become popular internationally as a simple, nutritious accompaniment.
