List of motorways in the United Kingdom

Motorways in the United Kingdom form a network of high-capacity, access-controlled roads. The first section — the Preston Bypass, now part of the M6 — opened in 1958, and the first full-length motorway, the M1, opened in 1959. The network totals roughly 3,700 km (2,300 miles). Motorways in Great Britain are numbered in zonal systems radiating from London and Edinburgh; Northern Ireland has its own M-series.

Notes

Many motorways include sections upgraded from A-roads and designated A(M), such as the A1(M) between London and Newcastle. Smart motorway schemes, which convert the hard shoulder to a running lane, have been applied to parts of the M1, M6, M25 and others.
