Warragamba Dam

Warragamba Dam is a heritage-listed concrete gravity dam across the Warragamba River in New South Wales, Australia. It impounds Lake Burragorang, the principal water supply reservoir for Sydney.

Height142 m
ReservoirLake Burragorang, capacity about 2,031 GL

History

A dam at the site was proposed from the 1840s, and drought during the Second World War made the project urgent. Construction began in 1948, creating the township of Warragamba for workers, and the dam was officially opened in 1960. It supplies about 80 percent of Sydney's water. The dam wall was strengthened in the late 1980s and early 2000s, and proposals to raise the wall for flood mitigation downstream have been debated since the 2010s.

Operation

Water is piped about 27 kilometres to Prospect Reservoir for distribution. A small hydroelectric station at the dam generates power when water is released for supply.
