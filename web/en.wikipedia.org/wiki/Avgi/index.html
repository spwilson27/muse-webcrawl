Avgi

Avgi (Greek: Η Αυγή, "The Dawn") is a Greek daily newspaper affiliated with the Coalition of the Radical Left (SYRIZA). It was founded in 1952 as the newspaper of the United Democratic Left (EDA) and became closely associated with the Greek left, including during the years when communist publications were restricted.

History

The paper continued publication after the fall of the Greek military junta in 1974 as the organ of the Eurocommunist KKE Interior tendency and later of the coalitions that succeeded it, including Synaspismos and then SYRIZA. Published in Athens, it carried political reporting, analysis and cultural coverage from a left-wing perspective, and for much of its history served as the semi-official voice of its sponsoring party.

Closure

Like much of the Greek press, Avgi suffered from declining circulation and advertising revenue after the Greek debt crisis. In June 2024, SYRIZA's leadership announced the suspension of the daily print edition, citing financial unsustainability, a decision that provoked protest from staff and debate within the party; the newspaper continued in digital form and with a Sunday edition for a period thereafter.
