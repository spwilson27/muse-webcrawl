Australian Bicentenary
Date: 26 January 1988
Location: Australia
Also known as: Bicentennial celebrations
Commemorates: Arrival of the First Fleet, 1788

The Australian Bicentenary was the national commemoration in 1988 of the 200th anniversary of the arrival of the First Fleet at Sydney Cove on 26 January 1788, marking the beginning of European settlement in Australia.

Celebrations

Events throughout 1988 included a re-enactment voyage of the First Fleet from Portsmouth to Sydney, a tall ships fleet in Sydney Harbour on Australia Day, World Expo 88 in Brisbane, the opening of the new Parliament House in Canberra, and the Bicentennial Australian Bicentennial road and cultural programmes administered by the Australian Bicentennial Authority. A commemorative medallion and special coinage were issued.

Protests

The celebrations were met by large Indigenous protests, most notably a march of some 40,000 people in Sydney on Australia Day under the themes of land rights and "Invasion Day", asserting 200 years of dispossession and survival. The contrast between celebration and protest became a defining feature of the anniversary.
