Biblioteca Marciana

LocationPiazza San Marco, Venice, Italy
ArchitectJacopo Sansovino
Established1468 (bequest); building begun 1537

The Biblioteca Nazionale Marciana is a public library in Venice, Italy, housed in a celebrated Renaissance building on the Piazzetta di San Marco. It holds one of the world's great collections of classical texts and Greek manuscripts.

History

The library grew from Cardinal Bessarion's donation of his Greek and Latin manuscripts to the Republic of Venice in 1468. Jacopo Sansovino designed its permanent home, begun in 1537 and completed by Vincenzo Scamozzi; its reading room is decorated with paintings by Titian, Tintoretto, Veronese and other Venetian masters.

Collections

Holdings include the will of Marco Polo, early printed books, the Grimani Breviary and thousands of Greek codices that made Venice a centre of Renaissance humanism and classical scholarship.
