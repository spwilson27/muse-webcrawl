Syriza (Greek: ΣΥΡΙΖΑ), formally the Coalition of the Radical Left – Progressive Alliance, is a left-wing political party in Greece. Its name is an acronym of the Greek words for "Coalition of the Radical Left". Originally formed in 2004 as an electoral alliance of leftist parties and movements, it registered as a unified party in 2012. Under the leadership of Alexis Tsipras, Syriza rose from the margins to become Greece's governing party during the Greek government-debt crisis.

History

The coalition brought together Synaspismos (Coalition of the Left, of Movements and Ecology) and a range of smaller socialist, Trotskyist, Maoist and eco-socialist groups. In the 2009 election Syriza won 4.6% of the vote, but the austerity measures imposed after Greece's 2010 bailout transformed its fortunes: in the two 2012 elections it surged to become the main opposition, winning 26.9% in June 2012.

In government (2015–2019)

Syriza won the January 2015 election on an anti-austerity platform and formed a coalition government with the Independent Greeks; Tsipras became prime minister. After months of confrontation with Greece's creditors, the government held a referendum in July 2015 in which voters rejected the creditors' bailout terms — yet days later Tsipras accepted a third bailout programme (the Third Memorandum) involving further austerity. The party's Left Platform split to form Popular Unity. Syriza nevertheless won the September 2015 snap election and remained in government until July 2019, when it lost to New Democracy under Kyriakos Mitsotakis and Tsipras resigned as prime minister.

Later developments

In opposition, Syriza adopted the expanded name "Syriza – Progressive Alliance". It lost further ground in the 2023 elections, after which Tsipras resigned as leader. Stefanos Kasselakis won the ensuing leadership election in September 2023, but internal conflict led to his ouster in 2024, when Sokratis Famellos became leader. Despite these upheavals, Syriza remains one of the principal parties of the Greek left.

Ideology

Syriza is generally characterised as a democratic socialist and left-wing populist party. It has advocated rolling back austerity, renegotiating Greece's debt, expanding the welfare state, raising the minimum wage, and ecological transition, while remaining committed to Greek membership of the eurozone and the European Union.
