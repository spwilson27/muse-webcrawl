Canton Fair

The Canton Fair, officially the China Import and Export Fair, is a major trade fair held in Guangzhou (Canton), China, in spring and autumn each year since 1957. It is the oldest and one of the largest trade fairs in China.

First held: 1957
Location: Guangzhou, China
Frequency: Twice yearly

Organisation

The fair is held in three phases covering different product categories, from electronics and machinery to consumer goods and textiles, attracting hundreds of thousands of overseas buyers.
