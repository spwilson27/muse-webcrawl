John Bertrand (sailor)

John Joseph Bertrand (born 25 March 1946) is an Australian sailor who skippered Australia II to victory in the 1983 America's Cup, ending the New York Yacht Club's 132-year winning streak — the longest in international sport.

Career

Bertrand, born in Melbourne, won a bronze medal in the Finn class at the 1976 Montreal Olympics and competed in America's Cup campaigns in 1970 and 1974 before commanding Alan Bond's syndicate. In September 1983 at Newport, Rhode Island, Australia II — famous for its secret winged keel designed by Ben Lexcen — defeated Dennis Conner's Liberty 4–3 after trailing 1–3.

Later life

Bertrand has been a businessman, sports administrator and chairman of Swimming Australia, and was chairman of the Sport Australia Hall of Fame. He was appointed an Officer of the Order of Australia and wrote the memoir Born to Win.
