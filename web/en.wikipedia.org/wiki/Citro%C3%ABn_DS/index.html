Citroën DS

ManufacturerCitroën
Production1955–1975
ClassExecutive car

The Citroën DS is a front mid-engined, front-wheel-drive executive car manufactured and marketed by Citroën from 1955 to 1975, in fastback/sedan, wagon/estate, and convertible body configurations, across three series of one generation.

Design

Designed by Italian sculptor and industrial designer Flaminio Bertoni and French aeronautic engineer André Lefèbvre, the DS pioneered hydropneumatic self-levelling suspension, and also featured disc brakes, semi-automatic transmission, and power steering in production cars. Its aerodynamic body made it strikingly futuristic for 1955.

Legacy

In the 1999 Car of the Century competition, the DS ranked third. It placed fifth on Automobile Magazine's 2005 list of the "100 Coolest Cars", and was named the most beautiful car of all time by Classic & Sports Car magazine.
