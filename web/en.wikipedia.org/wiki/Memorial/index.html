Memorial

A memorial is an object or structure which serves as a focus for the memory or commemoration of something, usually an influential, deceased person or a historical, tragic event.

Forms

Popular forms include landmark objects or works of art such as sculptures, statues or fountains and parks, as well as plaques, museums, halls, and digital memorials. Larger memorials may be entire buildings or sites, like the Lincoln Memorial.

Purpose

Memorials serve public mourning, education, and national identity. They can be contested when interpretations of history change. See War memorial, Statue and Triumphal arch.

Types

  • War memorials and cenotaphs
  • Memorial gardens, days and services
  • Online / digital memorials
