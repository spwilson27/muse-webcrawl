Viking Link is a high-voltage direct current (HVDC) submarine power cable between the United Kingdom and Denmark, running beneath the North Sea from Bicker Fen in Lincolnshire, England, to Revling in southern Jutland. When it entered service at the end of 2023 it was the world's longest land and subsea interconnector, at roughly 765 kilometres.

Project

The link was developed jointly by National Grid Ventures (UK) and Energinet (Denmark). Its 1,400 MW capacity allows the two countries to trade electricity, helping Britain import power — including Danish wind energy — and giving Denmark an export route for surplus renewable generation. Siemens Energy supplied the converter stations at each end.

Operation

Commercial operation began on 29 December 2023. The interconnector is credited with improving energy security and price stability in both markets, and it forms part of a growing network of cables linking Great Britain to continental Europe and Scandinavia.
