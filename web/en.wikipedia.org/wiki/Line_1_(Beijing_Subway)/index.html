Line 1 (Beijing Subway)

Line 1 of the Beijing Subway is the oldest and one of the busiest lines in the network. It runs east–west beneath Chang'an Avenue, from Pingguoyuan in Shijingshan District in the west to Universal Resort in Tongzhou District in the east, through Tiananmen Square. The line is coloured red.

Status: Operational
Opened: 1 October 1969 (initial section)
Stations: 38
Length: ~50 km

History

Construction began in 1965, and the first section between Beijing Railway Station and Pingguoyuan opened on 1 October 1969, initially restricted to official use; it opened fully to the public in 1971. Extensions eastward reached Sihuidong in 1999. In 2021 the line was operationally merged with the Batong line, enabling through services from Gucheng to Universal Resort station, which serves the Universal Beijing Resort.

Route

Key stations include Pingguoyuan, Bajiao Amusement Park, Yuquanlu, Wukesong, Gongzhufen, Fuxingmen, Xidan, Tiananmen West, Tiananmen East, Wangfujing, Dongdan, Jianguomen, Guomao, Dawanglu, Sihui, and continuing on the Batong line through Tongzhou to Universal Resort.

Rolling stock

The line uses six-car Type B trains. Line 1 is notable for being one of the few Beijing lines with platform screen doors retrofitted in older stations, completed in the late 2010s.
