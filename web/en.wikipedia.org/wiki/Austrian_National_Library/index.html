Austrian National Library
Location: Hofburg, Vienna, Austria
Established: 1368 (as Imperial Court Library)
Collection: Over 12 million items
German name: Österreichische Nationalbibliothek

The Austrian National Library (German: Österreichische Nationalbibliothek) is the largest library in Austria, with more than 12 million items in its collections. It is located in the Neue Burg wing of the Hofburg Palace in Vienna and grew out of the Imperial Court Library of the Habsburgs.

History and collections

The library traces its origins to the book collection of Duke Albert III in the 14th century. Its centrepiece is the Baroque State Hall (Prunksaal), built in the 1720s under Emperor Charles VI, housing hundreds of thousands of volumes. Holdings include major papyrus, map, globe, music, portrait and photograph collections, the Austrian National Library's planned languages collection with the world's largest Esperanto library, and extensive digital services such as the ANNO newspaper archive. The library holds legal deposit for Austrian publications.
