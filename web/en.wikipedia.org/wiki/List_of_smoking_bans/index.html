List of smoking bans

Smoking bans are public policies — including criminal laws and occupational safety rules — that prohibit tobacco smoking in workplaces and public spaces. Since the early 2000s many countries have introduced comprehensive national bans, encouraged by the WHO Framework Convention on Tobacco Control (2003). Ireland became the first country to implement a nationwide workplace smoking ban, including pubs and restaurants, on 29 March 2004; New Zealand followed later the same year, and Italy in 2005.

Bans vary widely: some countries prohibit smoking in all enclosed public spaces, others allow designated smoking rooms, and enforcement ranges from fines for smokers to penalties on proprietors. Studies generally associate comprehensive bans with rapid reductions in second-hand smoke exposure and hospital admissions for heart attacks.
