Battle of Yamazaki

Battle of Yamazaki
Date: 2 July 1582
Location: Yamazaki, Yamashiro Province
Result: Toyotomi victory; death of Akechi Mitsuhide

The Battle of Yamazaki (Yamazaki no tatakai) was fought in Yamashiro Province, Japan, on 2 July 1582 between Toyotomi Hideyoshi and Akechi Mitsuhide, who had assassinated Oda Nobunaga at the Honnō-ji Incident eleven days earlier.

Background

After Nobunaga's death, Hideyoshi concluded a swift truce with the Mōri clan at Takamatsu and force-marched his army roughly 200 km in days — the famous Chūgoku Ōgaeshi — to confront Mitsuhide before he could consolidate support.

Battle

Hideyoshi's larger force (about 20,000–40,000 against Mitsuhide's roughly 10,000–16,000) seized the strategic heights of Mount Tennō and the Yodo River marshes constrained Mitsuhide's deployment. After intense fighting around Enmyōji, Mitsuhide's army broke; he fled toward Ogurisu and was killed by bandits or peasants.

Aftermath

Victory made Hideyoshi the dominant successor to Nobunaga and paved his path to unifying Japan, culminating in his appointment as kampaku in 1585.
