Lahore Qalandars (Urdu: لاہور قلندرز) is a Pakistani professional Twenty20 cricket franchise that competes in the Pakistan Super League (PSL). The team represents the city of Lahore, the capital of Punjab province, and plays its home matches at Gaddafi Stadium. The franchise is owned by Fawad Rana of the Qatar Lubricants Company (QALCO).

The Qalandars were one of the five founding franchises of the PSL when the league was launched in 2016. The team's name derives from the Qalandar, a Sufi saint — most notably Lal Shahbaz Qalandar — and the whirling dervish imagery associated with Sufi mysticism; the team's logo features a dervish performing the Sema.

History

The franchise was sold for US$25.1 million for a ten-year period, making it the second most expensive team in the inaugural PSL. Lahore drafted West Indies star Chris Gayle with its first pick in the inaugural season, but the team struggled badly in the league's early years, finishing last in each of the first four seasons (2016–2019) and becoming known for its passionate but long-suffering fanbase.

The appointment of a core built around young Pakistani talent — including fast bowler Shaheen Shah Afridi, who became captain, and power-hitter Fakhar Zaman — gradually turned the franchise around. Lahore reached the final for the first time in 2020, losing to Karachi Kings, and won its maiden title in 2022, defeating Multan Sultans in the final at Gaddafi Stadium. The Qalandars successfully defended the title in 2023, beating Multan Sultans again in a final decided by a single run, becoming the first PSL franchise to win back-to-back championships.

Player development

The franchise is widely credited with establishing one of the most extensive talent-hunt and player development programmes in Pakistan, holding open trials across the country and overseas. The programme unearthed pace bowler Haris Rauf, who had played tape-ball cricket before being spotted at Qalandars trials, and the franchise later established a high-performance centre and development tours abroad.

Seasons

Lahore has generally been a crowd favourite despite its early failures; its anthem and the personality of owner Fawad Rana made it one of the league's most commercially prominent teams. The turnaround from 2020 onward, built largely on homegrown and development-programme players, is frequently cited as a model for franchise building in the PSL.
