Solvay (company)


Industry: Chemicals
Founded: 1863
Founder: Ernest Solvay
Headquarters: Brussels, Belgium

Solvay is a Belgian multinational chemical company headquartered in Brussels. Founded in 1863 by Ernest Solvay to exploit his ammonia-soda process for making soda ash, it grew into one of Europe's largest chemical groups.

History

The Solvay process for producing sodium carbonate revolutionised the alkali industry, displacing the older Leblanc process. The company expanded internationally, and Ernest Solvay also used his fortune to fund the famous Solvay Conferences in physics and chemistry, begun in 1911. In 2011 Solvay acquired the French chemical company Rhodia, expanding into specialty chemicals and polymers.

Restructuring

In December 2023 the group split into two independent listed companies: Solvay, retaining the essential chemicals businesses such as soda ash and peroxides, and Syensqo, holding the specialty materials and solutions businesses.
