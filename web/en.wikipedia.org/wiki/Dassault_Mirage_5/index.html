Dassault Mirage 5

The Dassault Mirage 5 is a French supersonic attack aircraft developed by Dassault Aviation in the 1960s as a clear-weather, ground-attack derivative of the Mirage III fighter. It first flew on 19 May 1967. By deleting the radar and other avionics of the Mirage III, Dassault created a cheaper aircraft with greater fuel and weapons capacity, optimised for low-level strike in good visibility. The Mirage 5 and its close derivatives (including the Israeli Nesher and Kfir lineage) were widely exported and saw extensive combat service.

Development

The Mirage 5 originated in an Israeli Air Force request for a simplified, day-clear-weather strike version of the Mirage III, with the radar removed to make room for more fuel and additional stores pylons. The French government embargoed the 50 aircraft ordered by Israel (which were taken into French service as the Mirage 5F) after the 1967 Six-Day War; Israel nevertheless obtained the design and produced it as the IAI Nesher, the ancestor of the Kfir.

Design

The tailless delta-wing Mirage 5 is powered by a single SNECMA Atar 9C turbojet, giving speeds around Mach 2. It carries two 30 mm DEFA cannon and up to 4,000 kg of external stores on seven hardpoints. The longer nose (on most variants) housed extra avionics or fuel, distinguishing it visually from the Mirage III.

Operational history

Mirage 5s were operated by France, Belgium (assembled locally by SABCA), and numerous export customers including Pakistan, Egypt, Libya, Colombia, Peru, Venezuela, Chile, Argentina and Zaire. Belgian Mirage 5s served until 1993; Pakistan's large fleet — upgraded under the ROSE programme — has remained in service into the 2020s. The type saw combat in the Yom Kippur War (as the Nesher), the Iran–Iraq War era regional conflicts, the Falklands War (Argentine Daggers, a Nesher derivative), and various African and Latin American operations.

Specifications (Mirage 5F)

Crew: 1. Length: 15.55 m; wingspan: 8.22 m. Maximum speed: Mach 2.2 at altitude. Combat range: about 1,250 km. Armament: 2 × 30 mm DEFA cannon; up to 4,000 kg of bombs, rockets and missiles on seven hardpoints.
