Onegai My Melody (おねがいマイメロディ, Onegai Mai Merodi, lit. "Please My Melody") is a Japanese anime series produced by Studio Comet, based on the Sanrio character My Melody. It aired on TV Osaka and TV Tokyo from 2005, and was followed by several sequels, including Onegai My Melody: KuruKuru Shuffle!, Sukkiri♪, and Kirara★.

Plot

In the magical kingdom of Mari Land, Kuromi escapes from prison with the Melody Key, a magical artefact that can grant wishes but also unleash nightmares, and flees to the human world. The king sends My Melody to Earth with the Melody Baton to stop her. My Melody befriends a schoolgirl, Uta Yumeno, and together they must collect one hundred pink notes by undoing the dream magic Kuromi and her sidekick Baku inflict on people, before Kuromi gathers one hundred black notes to resurrect the Spirit of Dark Power.

The series is notable for giving Kuromi — previously a minor character — a starring antagonistic role that made her one of Sanrio's most popular characters.
