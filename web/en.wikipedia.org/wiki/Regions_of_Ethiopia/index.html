Regions of Ethiopia

Ethiopia is a federation subdivided into ethnically based regional states (Amharic: kililoch) and chartered cities (Amharic: astedader akababiwoch). This system of administrative regions replaced the provinces of Ethiopia in 1992 under the Transitional Government of Ethiopia, and was formalised when the 1995 Constitution of Ethiopia came into force.

In addition there are two chartered cities, the capital Addis Ababa and Dire Dawa. New regions have been created by referendum in recent years, including Sidama (2020) and the reorganisations of the former Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region into Sidama, South West Ethiopia, South Ethiopia, and Central Ethiopia.
