Morning dress

Morning dress, also known as formal day dress, is the formal Western dress code for daytime events, consisting principally of a morning coat, waistcoat, and formal (striped) trousers for men, and an appropriate gown for women. It is worn to social functions such as weddings, memorial services, and races, typically events held before 6 p.m. The name originates from the practice of gentlemen riding in the morning.

Morning dress: morning coat, waistcoat, striped trousers and top hat

History

Morning dress first appeared towards the end of the 19th century as a less formal alternative to the frock coat, evolving from the practice of wearing a cutaway coat for horseback riding. By the early 20th century it had become the standard formal dress for daytime, and after the Second World War it was retained for the most formal daytime occasions, even as everyday formal dress declined.

Composition

For men, the outfit consists of a black or dark grey single-breasted morning coat with tails, cut away at the front; a waistcoat, traditionally buff, grey or black; striped or checked formal trousers in shades of grey; a white or light-coloured shirt with a turndown or detachable wing collar; a tie or cravat; and polished black shoes. A grey or black top hat is the traditional headwear, and gloves and a boutonnière may complete the ensemble. A less formal variant, the morning suit, matches coat and trousers in the same grey cloth and is popular at summer weddings and race meetings.

Occasions

Morning dress is specified on invitations for formal weddings, royal and state occasions, formal garden parties, and prestigious race meetings such as Royal Ascot, where it is required in the Royal Enclosure. In the United Kingdom, members of the royal family and officers on ceremonial duties wear it frequently; in the United States it is sometimes called "cutaway" dress and appears at formal daytime weddings and inaugurations.

Women's morning dress

For women, formal day dress generally consists of a smart dress or suit, often with a jacket, accompanied by a hat or fascinator and matching accessories. Length and formality vary with the occasion and local custom.
