BIMCO is the world's largest international shipping association, representing shipowners, operators, managers, brokers and agents. Founded in 1905 as the Baltic and International Maritime Conference, it is best known for developing the standard-form contracts and clauses used throughout the shipping industry, and for representing its members at the International Maritime Organization (IMO) and other regulatory bodies.

Standard contracts

BIMCO's documentary committees draft and maintain hundreds of widely adopted standard contracts, including the GENCON and BALTIME charter parties, the SHIPMAN ship-management agreement, the BIMCO Bill of Lading, and sale-and-purchase and shipbuilding forms. Its clauses address practical problems as they arise: piracy and war risks, bunker fuel quality, sanctions compliance, and the shipping industry's decarbonisation under IMO and EU rules, including emissions-trading and fuel-standard clauses in the 2020s. Contracts are distributed through the SmartCon editing platform.

Role and services

BIMCO holds consultative status at the IMO and observer status at other UN bodies, lobbies on safety, security and environmental regulation, and provides members with market analysis, freight-market data, port and holiday information, training and advice on contractual disputes. Its membership accounts for around 62 per cent of world merchant-fleet tonnage. The association is headquartered in Bagsværd, outside Copenhagen, with offices in Athens, Houston, Shanghai and Singapore.
