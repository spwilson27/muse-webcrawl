Associated British Foods

Associated British Foods plc (ABF) is a British multinational food processing and retailing company headquartered in London, England. It is a constituent of the FTSE 100 Index. Its divisions produce sugar, baker's yeast, ingredients, and grocery brands, and it owns the Primark clothing retail chain, which accounts for a large share of its profits.

History

The company was founded in 1935 by Canadian businessman W. Garfield Weston as a British offshoot of the Weston food empire, originally operating bakeries and food investments. It adopted the name Associated British Foods in 1960. The Weston family retains a controlling stake through Wittington Investments. ABF acquired British Sugar in 1991 and launched and expanded Primark (founded in Ireland in 1969 as Penneys), which grew into one of Europe's largest clothing retailers by sales volume.

Operations

ABF operates through five segments: Sugar; Agriculture; Retail (Primark); Grocery — including Twinings teas, Ovaltine, Jordans cereals, and Kingsmill bread through its Allied Bakeries business; and Ingredients, including the yeast and bakery ingredients producer AB Mauri. It is one of the world's largest producers of sugar and baker's yeast.
