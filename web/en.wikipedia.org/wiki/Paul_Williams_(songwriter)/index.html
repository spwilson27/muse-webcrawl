Paul Williams (songwriter)

Paul Hamilton Williams Jr. (born 19 September 1940) is an American composer, singer, songwriter, and actor. He wrote many popular songs of the 1970s, including "We've Only Just Begun" and "Rainy Days and Mondays" for the Carpenters, "Evergreen" from A Star Is Born, and "Rainbow Connection" from The Muppet Movie.

Career

Williams began as a struggling actor and staff songwriter before hits recorded by Three Dog Night, Helen Reddy, and the Carpenters made him one of the decade's defining songwriters. He won an Academy Award for "Evergreen" (with Barbra Streisand) and has been inducted into the Songwriters Hall of Fame. He acted in films including Phantom of the Paradise (1974), which he also scored, and was a frequent television guest.

Later work

After years of addiction, Williams became sober and active in recovery advocacy. He served as president and chairman of ASCAP, and collaborated with Daft Punk on their album Random Access Memories (2013), co-writing and singing on "Touch".
