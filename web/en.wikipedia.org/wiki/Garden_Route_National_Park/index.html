Garden Route National Park

LocationWestern Cape and Eastern Cape, South Africa
Nearest cityGeorge
Area1,570 km² (610 sq mi)
Established6 March 2009
Governing bodySouth African National Parks (SANParks)

The Garden Route National Park is a national park in the Garden Route region of South Africa, spanning the Western Cape and Eastern Cape provinces. It was established on 6 March 2009 by amalgamating the existing Tsitsikamma and Wilderness national parks, the Knysna National Lake Area, and various tracts of state forest land into a single protected area of about 1,570 square kilometres, including roughly 605 km² of indigenous forest.

Sections of the park

The park is not a single contiguous block but a mosaic of protected areas stretching some 200 kilometres along the coast between Mossel Bay and Storms River:

  • Tsitsikamma section — a marine and coastal reserve famous for the Storms River Mouth suspension bridge, rugged coastline, and the multi-day Otter Trail, one of South Africa's most popular hiking routes.
  • Wilderness section — a system of lakes, rivers, and estuaries (including Eilandvlei, Langvlei, and Rondevlei) set between the Outeniqua Mountains and the Indian Ocean, renowned for birdwatching and canoeing on the Touw River.
  • Knysna section — protecting the Knysna estuary and the great indigenous forests around Knysna, home to giant Outeniqua yellowwood trees and the elusive Knysna elephants.

Ecology

The park protects some of the largest remaining tracts of Afromontane forest in South Africa, as well as fynbos vegetation, wetlands, and a marine protected area extending 5.5 km out to sea at Tsitsikamma — one of the oldest marine protected areas in the world, proclaimed in 1964. Wildlife includes bushbuck, blue duiker, leopard, baboons, the Knysna turaco (loerie), and rich estuarine and marine life.

Tourism

Rest camps at Storms River Mouth, Nature's Valley, and Wilderness offer accommodation, and activities include hiking, kayaking, canopy tours, and whale watching in season. The park forms the natural centrepiece of the Garden Route, one of South Africa's most travelled tourist routes.
