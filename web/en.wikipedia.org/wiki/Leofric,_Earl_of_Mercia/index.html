Leofric, Earl of Mercia

Leofric (died 31 August or 30 September 1057) was Earl of Mercia from about 1032 until his death. He was one of the most powerful magnates in eleventh-century England and the husband of Lady Godiva, famed in later legend for her naked ride through Coventry.

Died1057, Bromley, Staffordshire

Career

Leofric was the son of Leofwine, ealdorman of the Hwicce. He rose under King Cnut and retained his earldom through the reigns of Cnut's sons and of Edward the Confessor, acting as a counterweight to the growing power of Earl Godwin of Wessex. In 1051 he helped Edward resist Godwin's armed defiance.

Religious patronage

Together with Godiva, Leofric founded and endowed religious houses, notably the Benedictine monastery at Coventry in 1043, as well as houses at Leominster, Stow and elsewhere. He was buried at Coventry.

Family

His son Ælfgar succeeded him as earl; his grandchildren included Edwin, Earl of Mercia, and Morcar, Earl of Northumbria, who resisted the Norman Conquest.
