HVAC

HVAC: Heating, ventilation and air conditioning
Functions: Thermal comfort, indoor air quality
Key equipment: Furnaces, boilers, heat pumps, chillers, air handlers, ducts

HVAC (heating, ventilation, and air conditioning) is the technology of indoor environmental comfort, providing acceptable thermal conditions and indoor air quality in homes, offices, hospitals, vehicles and industrial buildings.

Heating

Heating systems include furnaces burning fuel, electric resistance, boilers distributing hot water or steam, and heat pumps that move heat efficiently and can also cool.

Ventilation

Ventilation exchanges indoor and outdoor air, dilutes pollutants and controls moisture. It may be natural or mechanical, with energy recovery ventilators reclaiming heat from exhaust air.

Air conditioning

Air conditioners use a vapour-compression refrigeration cycle — compressor, condenser, expansion valve and evaporator — to remove heat and humidity. Efficiency is rated by measures such as SEER and EER, and refrigerants are shifting to lower global-warming-potential fluids.

Controls and sustainability

Thermostats, building automation and zoning improve efficiency. HVAC is a major share of building energy use, so design standards such as ASHRAE guidelines, insulation and heat pumps are central to decarbonisation.
