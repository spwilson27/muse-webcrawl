Myakka River

LocationFlorida, United States
LengthApprox. 72 miles (116 km)
MouthCharlotte Harbor

The Myakka River is a river in southwestern Florida. It rises in Manatee County near the Hardee County line and flows southwest through Manatee, Sarasota and Charlotte counties before emptying into Charlotte Harbor. A 34-mile portion of the river was designated a Florida Wild and Scenic River by the state legislature in 1985.

Course and ecology

The river passes through Myakka River State Park, one of Florida's oldest and largest state parks, where it widens into Upper and Lower Myakka Lakes. Its basin includes flatwoods, prairies, hammocks and wetlands that support abundant wildlife, including alligators, wading birds and numerous fish species. Tidal influence increases near the mouth, where mangrove estuaries line the lower river.
