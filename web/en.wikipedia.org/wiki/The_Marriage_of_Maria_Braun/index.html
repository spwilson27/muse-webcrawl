The Marriage of Maria Braun

Directed by: Rainer Werner Fassbinder
Starring: Hanna Schygulla
Released: 1979 (West Germany)
Language: German

The Marriage of Maria Braun (German: Die Ehe der Maria Braun) is a 1979 West German drama film directed by Rainer Werner Fassbinder. It is the first part of his unofficial BRD Trilogy, examining West Germany's postwar "economic miracle" through the life of its heroine, played by Hanna Schygulla.

Plot

During an Allied bombing raid, Maria marries the soldier Hermann Braun, who is promptly sent to the Eastern Front and later reported missing, then imprisoned after returning and taking the blame for a killing Maria committed. Through the years of reconstruction Maria rises from bar hostess to the powerful right hand of a textile magnate, amassing wealth in single-minded devotion to the marriage she believes awaits her — until a devastating revelation about the bargain Hermann struck without her knowledge.

Reception

The film was Fassbinder's greatest commercial and international success. Schygulla won the Silver Bear for Best Actress at the 1979 Berlin International Film Festival, and the film is consistently ranked among the masterpieces of New German Cinema.
