Film Finance Corporation Australia

The Film Finance Corporation Australia (FFC) was the Australian Government's principal agency for funding film and television production from 1988 to 2008. It invested in a wide range of Australian feature films, television drama, and documentaries.

History

The FFC was established after the winding back of the 10BA tax concession scheme, to provide direct government investment in screen production. Over two decades it supported internationally successful films such as Strictly Ballroom, The Adventures of Priscilla, Queen of the Desert, and Shine.

Successor

In 2008 the FFC was merged with the Australian Film Commission and Film Australia to form Screen Australia.
