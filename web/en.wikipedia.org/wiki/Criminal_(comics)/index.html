Criminal (comics)

Criminal is an American crime comic book series created by writer Ed Brubaker and artist Sean Phillips. First published by Marvel Comics' Icon imprint in 2006 and later by Image Comics, it is noted for its noir storytelling about thieves, heists, and the underworld.

Overview

The series consists of interlinked story arcs, each focusing on different characters in a shared criminal underworld — including pickpocket Leo Patterson, the heist-planner and getaway-driver characters around him, and corrupt police. Arcs include "Coward", "Lawless", "The Dead and the Dying", "Bad Night", "The Sinners", and "The Last of the Innocent".

Reception

Criminal won the Eisner Award for Best New Series in 2007, and Brubaker won Best Writer for his work on it. The series is regarded as a landmark of modern crime comics, and an Amazon television adaptation has been developed.
