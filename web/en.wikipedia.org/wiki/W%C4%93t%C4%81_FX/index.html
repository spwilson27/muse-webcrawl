Wētā FX

Wētā FX, formerly known as Weta Digital, is a New Zealand digital visual effects and computer animation company based in Miramar, Wellington. It was founded in 1993 to produce the effects for Heavenly Creatures, and grew to fame through Peter Jackson's The Lord of the Rings trilogy.

History and work

Weta's breakthrough came with the Lord of the Rings films (2001–2003), for which it developed the MASSIVE crowd-simulation software and created the digital character Gollum. The company went on to produce the effects for King Kong, James Cameron's Avatar films — pioneering performance capture and virtual production — and the modern Planet of the Apes series. In 2021, game engine company Unity acquired Weta's tools, technology and engineering division for US$1.625 billion, and the effects company rebranded from Weta Digital to Wētā FX.
