Hilsa

The hilsa (Tenualosa ilisha), also known as ilish, is a species of fish in the herring family (Clupeidae) found in the northern Indian Ocean and the rivers that drain into it. It is the national fish of Bangladesh and is highly prized as a food fish in Bengal, Odisha, and Assam.

Distribution and life cycle

Hilsa are anadromous: they live in the sea and swim up rivers such as the Ganges, Brahmaputra, and Irrawaddy to spawn in fresh water. Bangladesh produces the majority of the world catch, and the Padma River (lower Ganges) fishery is especially famous for the flavour of its fish.

Cuisine

Hilsa is celebrated in Bengali cuisine, prepared in dishes such as shorshe ilish (hilsa in mustard sauce), ilish bhaja (fried hilsa), and ilish paturi (fish steamed in banana leaf). The fish is notoriously bony, with many fine intermuscular bones.
