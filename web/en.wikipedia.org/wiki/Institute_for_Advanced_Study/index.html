Institute for Advanced Study

The Institute for Advanced Study (IAS) is an independent centre for theoretical research in Princeton, New Jersey, founded in 1930, known for hosting scholars free from teaching duties.

Institute for Advanced Study
Founded1930
FoundersLouis Bamberger, Caroline Bamberger Fuld; first director Abraham Flexner
LocationPrinceton, New Jersey, US
SchoolsMathematics, Natural Sciences, Historical Studies, Social Science

History

Early faculty included Albert Einstein, Kurt Gödel, John von Neumann and J. Robert Oppenheimer (director 1947–1966). The IAS has no students or tuition; a small permanent faculty hosts visiting Members chosen for exceptional promise.

Influence

It became the model for similar institutes worldwide and a landmark of curiosity-driven basic research.
