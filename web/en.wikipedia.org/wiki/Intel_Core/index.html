Intel Core

Intel Core
Launched: 2006
Manufacturer: Intel
Product line: Desktop, mobile, and workstation CPUs
Successor branding: Core Ultra (2023– )

Intel Core is a line of multi-core (with one exception) consumer-oriented microprocessors designed and manufactured by Intel for desktops, laptops, and embedded systems. Introduced in 2006 with the Core and Core 2 series derived from the Pentium M/Core microarchitecture, it succeeded the Pentium 4 and eventually replaced the Pentium brand at the high end.

Product tiers

From 2009 the line was organized into the Core i3 (entry), i5 (mainstream), i7 (high-end), and later i9 (enthusiast, from 2017) tiers. Successive generations—Nehalem, Sandy Bridge, Ivy Bridge, Haswell, Skylake and its many refreshes, and the Alder Lake and Raptor Lake hybrid designs—combined performance and efficiency cores in later models.

Rebranding

In 2023 Intel introduced new branding for its premium consumer chips: the "i" was dropped (Core 3/5/7/9), and a new flagship tier, Core Ultra, was launched with the Meteor Lake generation, featuring a chiplet design and a neural processing unit (NPU) for AI workloads.

Generations

  • 1st–4th gen: Nehalem through Haswell (2008–2013)
  • 5th–10th gen: Broadwell through Comet Lake (2014–2020)
  • 11th–14th gen: Rocket Lake, Alder Lake, Raptor Lake (2021–2023)
  • Core Ultra Series 1 and 2: Meteor Lake, Lunar Lake, Arrow Lake (2023– )
