Music of Pakistan

The music of Pakistan includes diverse elements ranging from music from parts of South Asia as well as Central Asian, Middle Eastern, and modern Western popular music influences. With these multiple influences, a distinctive Pakistani musical style has emerged.

A qawwali ensemble performing at a Sufi shrine.

Qawwali

Qawwali is the devotional music of Sufism, performed at shrines and urs festivals to induce spiritual ecstasy. Sung in Urdu, Punjabi, and Persian, it features a lead singer, chorus with hand-clapping, harmonium, and tabla, building from slow verses to ecstatic climaxes. Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan brought qawwali to world audiences, collaborating with Peter Gabriel and achieving global fame before his death in 1997. The Sabri Brothers and Aziz Mian are other celebrated exponents; in the modern era, the genre reached new audiences through Coke Studio performances.

Classical and semi-classical music

Pakistan shares the Hindustani classical tradition of raga and tala, with instruments such as the sitar, tabla, sarangi, and harmonium. The ghazal — a poetic song of love and loss — was popularised by singers such as Mehdi Hassan, the "King of Ghazal", and Iqbal Bano. Ustad Bade Fateh Ali Khan was a leading khyal vocalist.

Folk music

Regional folk traditions include Punjabi music with the dhol drum and energetic bhangra; Sindhi music centred on Sufi poetry of Shah Abdul Latif Bhittai and instruments such as the ektara and alghoza; Pashto music; and Balochi music. Sufi folk singers such as Abida Parveen, the "Queen of Sufi music", and Allan Fakir are national figures.

Pop and rock

Modern Pakistani pop began with Ahmed Rushdi in the 1960s and took off in the 1980s–90s with Nazia Hassan ("Disco Deewane"), the band Vital Signs ("Dil Dil Pakistan"), Junoon — pioneers of "Sufi rock" — and Strings. The television series Coke Studio Pakistan (from 2008) became a cultural phenomenon, reviving folk and classical songs for young audiences; its rendition of "Tajdar-e-Haram" by Atif Aslam and the viral hit "Pasoori" (2022) reached worldwide audiences.
