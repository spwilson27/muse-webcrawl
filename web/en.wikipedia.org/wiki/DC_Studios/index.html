DC Studios

DC Studios (originally known as DC Films) is an American film, television, and animation studio that is a division of Warner Bros. Entertainment, part of Warner Bros. Discovery. It is responsible for the production of live-action and animated films and series based on characters from DC Comics.

History

The DC Films banner was formalized in 2016 following the mixed reception of Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice, with Geoff Johns and Jon Berg appointed to oversee the division. After further restructuring, Walter Hamada served as president of DC Films from 2018 to 2022.

In October 2022, following the merger that created Warner Bros. Discovery, filmmaker James Gunn and producer Peter Safran were named co-chairmen and co-CEOs of the renamed DC Studios, reporting directly to Warner Bros. Discovery CEO David Zaslav. Gunn handles the creative side of the studio while Safran handles the business and production side.

DC Universe and Elseworlds

Gunn and Safran planned a new shared continuity, the DC Universe (DCU), beginning with the animated series Creature Commandos (2024) and the film Superman (2025) as part of "Chapter One: Gods and Monsters". Projects outside the main continuity, such as Matt Reeves's The Batman films and Todd Phillips's Joker, are branded under the DC Elseworlds label.
