Finding Dory

Finding Dory is a 2016 American animated adventure film produced by Pixar Animation Studios and released by Walt Disney Pictures. Directed by Andrew Stanton and co-directed by Angus MacLane, it is the sequel to Finding Nemo (2003). Ellen DeGeneres returns as the voice of Dory, a blue tang with short-term memory loss, joined by Albert Brooks as Marlin and Hayden Rolence as Nemo.

Plot

A year after reuniting with Marlin and Nemo, Dory begins to remember fragments of her past and sets out across the ocean to find her parents, Jenny and Charlie. Her journey leads to a marine life institute in California, where she befriends Hank, a cantankerous octopus, Destiny, a nearsighted whale shark, and Bailey, a beluga whale. With their help, Dory finds her parents and learns to embrace her way of seeing the world.

Reception

The film was a major commercial success, grossing over $1 billion worldwide, and received praise for its animation, humour, and emotional story, including its sensitive portrayal of memory loss and disability. It won the People's Choice Award for Favorite Family Movie, among other accolades.
