Golden Globe Cecil B. DeMille Award

The Cecil B. DeMille Award is an honorary Golden Globe Award bestowed by the Hollywood Foreign Press Association (HFPA, now the Golden Globe Foundation) for "outstanding contributions to the world of entertainment". The award was first presented at the 9th Golden Globe Awards in February 1952 and is named in honour of its first recipient, the director Cecil B. DeMille.

Selection

Recipients are chosen by the HFPA board of directors from among actors, directors, writers, and producers who have made a significant mark on the film industry. Honourees have included Walt Disney, John Wayne, Alfred Hitchcock, Sidney Poitier (the first African-American recipient), Barbra Streisand, Steven Spielberg, Meryl Streep, Tom Hanks, Oprah Winfrey, and Jane Fonda.

Notable facts

  • The youngest recipient was Judy Garland, aged 39 in 1962; the oldest was Samuel Goldwyn, aged 90 in 1973.
  • Audrey Hepburn is among the few honourees recognised primarily for acting work spanning both Hollywood and international cinema.
  • No award was presented at the 2022 ceremony, which was not televised.
