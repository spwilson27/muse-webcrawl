Galina Viktorovna Timchenko (born 8 May 1962) is a Russian journalist and co-founder of the independent news outlet Meduza, based in Riga, Latvia.

Career

Timchenko worked at the newspaper Kommersant and from 2004 to 2014 was editor-in-chief of Lenta.ru, which she built into one of Russia's most-read online news sources. Her removal by the site's owner in March 2014, amid pressure over coverage of Ukraine, prompted most of the editorial team to resign in protest.

Meduza

Later in 2014 Timchenko and former Lenta.ru colleagues launched Meduza from Latvia, publishing in Russian (and later English) free of direct Russian state control. She has served as its chief executive. In 2021 Russia designated Meduza a "foreign agent", and in 2023 an "undesirable organisation", forcing it to rely on reader donations from abroad.
