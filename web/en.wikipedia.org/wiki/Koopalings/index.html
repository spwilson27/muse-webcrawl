Koopalings

The Koopalings are a group of seven fictional characters in Nintendo's Mario franchise. They serve as leaders of the Koopa Troop under Bowser and are fought as boss characters in many of the main-series platform games. They debuted in Super Mario Bros. 3 (1988), where each had stolen a magic wand from a king of the Mushroom World and transformed him into an animal.

Their English names were given by Nintendo of America's localization team and mostly honour musicians and entertainers — Ludwig van Beethoven, Lemmy Kilmister, Roy Orbison, Iggy Pop, Wendy O. Williams, Morton Downey Jr., and Larry King.

Role in the series

The Koopalings were initially presented in the games' manuals and the animated series The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3 as Bowser's children; later Nintendo continuity (from New Super Mario Bros. Wii, 2009, onwards, and stated by Shigeru Miyamoto in 2012) treats Bowser Jr. as Bowser's only child and the Koopalings as his high-ranking minions instead. After a long absence in the 3D era, they returned as the boss cast of the New Super Mario Bros. games, appeared as playable racers in Mario Kart 8, and feature in spin-offs such as Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga and Paper Mario: Color Splash.
