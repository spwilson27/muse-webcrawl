Universal Music Publishing Group (UMPG) is the music publishing division of Universal Music Group and one of the largest music publishers in the world. It administers a catalogue of millions of copyrights, licensing songs for recordings, film, television, advertising, and live performance, and collects royalties on behalf of songwriters.

Operations

UMPG represents a wide roster of songwriters and catalogues spanning pop, rock, hip-hop, country, classical, and film and television music. It maintains offices in dozens of countries and is consistently ranked among the top music publishers by market share.

History

The company grew through acquisitions including the publishing arms of PolyGram and BMG Music Publishing, the latter acquired in 2007, which made Universal the world's largest music publisher at the time.
