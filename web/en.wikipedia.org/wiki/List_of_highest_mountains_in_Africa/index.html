List of highest mountains in Africa

This article lists the highest mountains in Africa. The highest peak on the continent is Mount Kilimanjaro in Tanzania, a dormant stratovolcano whose Uhuru Peak rises 5,895 metres above sea level.

Mount Kilimanjaro, Africa's highest mountain at 5,895 m.
