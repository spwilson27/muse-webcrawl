Filipino martial arts

Filipino martial arts (FMA) refer to the ancient and newer fighting methods devised in the Philippines. The most widely known systems are arnis, eskrima (escrima), and kali, which emphasize weapons-based fighting with sticks, knives, and bladed weapons, together with empty-hand techniques.

Characteristics

Training typically begins with weapons, on the principle that weapon movements translate to empty-hand skills. Common drills include sinawali (weaving stick patterns) and practice with rattan sticks, the balisong knife, and the bolo.

History

The arts developed over centuries of warfare and were influenced by Spanish fencing during the colonial period, when practice was at times driven underground. Filipino martial arts have since spread worldwide and appear in military training and film choreography.
