Barts Health NHS Trust


St Bartholomew's Hospital ("Barts")

Barts Health NHS Trust is an NHS trust based in London, England, formed on 1 April 2012 by the merger of Barts and The London NHS Trust, Whipps Cross University Hospital NHS Trust and Newham University Hospital NHS Trust. It is one of the largest NHS trusts in the country, employing around 17,000 staff and serving a population of some 2.5 million people across east London.

Hospitals

The trust runs five hospitals: St Bartholomew's Hospital ("Barts") in the City of London, founded in 1123 and the oldest hospital in Britain still on its original site; The Royal London Hospital in Whitechapel, a major trauma centre and home of the London Air Ambulance helipad; Mile End Hospital; Whipps Cross University Hospital in Leytonstone; and Newham University Hospital in Plaistow. The Barts Heart Centre and the trust's cancer services are among the largest specialist centres of their kind in Europe.

History and oversight

The trust inherited substantial Private Finance Initiative debts from the rebuilding of the Royal London and Barts sites. It was placed under regulatory scrutiny by the Care Quality Commission in the mid-2010s, with Whipps Cross rated inadequate in 2015, and has since undergone improvement programmes; its medical school partner is Queen Mary University of London's Faculty of Medicine and Dentistry, historically centred on the medical colleges of Barts and the London.
