Axel Christofer Hedfors (born 18 December 1977), better known by his stage name Axwell, is a Swedish DJ, record producer, remixer and owner of Axtone Records. He is a member of the Swedish House Mafia along with Sebastian Ingrosso and Steve Angello, and of the duo Axwell Λ Ingrosso. He is a two-time DJ Awards winner and was ranked highly in DJ Magazine's Top 100 DJs poll during the group's rise.

Career

Axwell broke through as a solo artist with singles such as "Feel the Vibe" (2004) and the worldwide club hit "I Found U" (2007), and as a remixer for artists including Madonna and Pharrell Williams. His collaboration with Bob Sinclar, "What a Wonderful World" (2008), preceded the formation of Swedish House Mafia, whose singles "One (Your Name)", "Save the World", "Don't You Worry Child" and "Greyhound" became era-defining big-room and progressive house anthems. After the group's farewell tour in 2013, he released music with Sebastian Ingrosso as Axwell Λ Ingrosso ("Sun Is Shining", "More Than You Know"), and the Swedish House Mafia reunited at Ultra Music Festival in 2018, later releasing the album Paradise Again (2022).

Axtone Records

Founded by Axwell in 2005, Axtone Records has released music by himself and artists including Hard Rock Sofa, Adrian Lux and Thomas Gold.
