2021 United Nations Climate Change Conference

The 2021 United Nations Climate Change Conference, more commonly known as COP26, was the 26th UN Climate Change conference, held at the SEC Centre in Glasgow, Scotland, from 31 October to 13 November 2021. Convened under the UNFCCC and presided over by Alok Sharma of the host United Kingdom (in partnership with Italy), it concluded with the adoption of the Glasgow Climate Pact.

Negotiations and outcomes

COP26 was the first major test of the Paris Agreement's ratchet mechanism, with countries submitting updated nationally determined contributions (NDCs). The Glasgow Climate Pact, for the first time in a COP decision, explicitly mentioned fossil fuels — calling for a "phase-down of unabated coal power" and phase-out of inefficient fossil fuel subsidies; the coal language was softened from "phase-out" in the final hours at the insistence of India and China. Parties agreed to revisit and strengthen 2030 targets in 2022 and completed the Paris rulebook, including Article 6 rules on carbon markets.

Other announcements

During the summit, more than 100 countries pledged to halt and reverse deforestation by 2030, over 100 joined the Global Methane Pledge to cut methane emissions 30% by 2030, and a group of countries and manufacturers committed to 100% zero-emission new car sales by 2040 (2035 in leading markets). Climate finance remained contentious: the long-promised $100 billion per year for developing countries had not been delivered on schedule. UN analyses estimated the updated pledges still put the world on track for roughly 2.4 °C of warming.
