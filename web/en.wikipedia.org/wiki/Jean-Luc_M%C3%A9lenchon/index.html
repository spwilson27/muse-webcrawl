Jean-Luc Mélenchon

Jean-Luc Mélenchon (born 19 August 1951) is a French politician who has been a leading figure of the radical left. He founded the movement La France Insoumise (France Unbowed) in 2016 and has stood in three presidential elections.

Born19 August 1951, Tangier, Morocco
PartyLa France Insoumise
OfficesMinister delegate for Vocational Education (2000–2002); MEP; Deputy

Career

Formerly a member of the Socialist Party and a senator, Mélenchon left in 2008 to co-found the Left Party. Running for president in 2012, 2017, and 2022, he won roughly one fifth of the vote in the latter two campaigns, narrowly missing the run-off in 2022.

Positions

His program, "L'Avenir en commun," advocates a Sixth Republic, ecological planning, withdrawal from or renegotiation of EU treaties, and raising the minimum wage.
