Agence France-Presse

TypeNews agency (statutory corporation)

Agence France-Presse (AFP) is a French international news agency founded in 1944, successor to the Havas agency founded in 1835 — the world's oldest news agency lineage. Headquartered in Paris with bureaus in some 150 countries, it files in six languages and is one of the three great global wires alongside the Associated Press and Reuters.

Status and services

AFP operates under a 1957 statute that guarantees its independence while giving it a public-service mission and state representation on its board; French state subscriptions are a major revenue source. It is a leader in photo and video news and runs one of the world's biggest fact-checking networks.

History

Havas pioneered the telegraph-era agency model in the 1830s; after the Second World War the liberated agency was refounded as AFP under Resistance figures, growing into a global multimedia wire.
