Integrated bridge system

An integrated bridge system (IBS) is a combination of interconnected equipment on a ship's bridge that allows centralised access to navigational, propulsion and safety information, increasing the efficiency of watchkeeping and ship handling.

Components

A typical system links radar, electronic chart display and information systems (ECDIS), the automatic identification system (AIS), gyrocompass and GPS receivers, autopilot and conning displays through a shared network, with data presented at multifunction workstations.

Regulation

Performance standards for integrated bridge systems are set by the International Maritime Organization, notably resolution MSC.64(67), and are implemented through the SOLAS Convention. Requirements cover alarm management, redundancy and failure behaviour so that a single fault cannot disable the whole bridge.
