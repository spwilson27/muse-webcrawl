List of best-selling video games

This is a list of the best-selling video games of all time, ranked by copies sold worldwide. The best-selling game is Minecraft (2011), which surpassed 300 million copies in 2023. Tetris, in its many versions, has sold an estimated 520 million copies across all platforms, though figures aggregate numerous distinct releases.

Minecraft, Grand Theft Auto V, and Wii Sports are among the biggest sellers ever.
