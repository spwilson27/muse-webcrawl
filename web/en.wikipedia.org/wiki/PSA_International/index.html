PSA International

PSA International Pte Ltd
Formerly: Port of Singapore Authority
Founded: 1972 (authority); corporatised 1997
Headquarters: Singapore
Owner: Temasek Holdings

PSA International Pte Ltd is one of the world's largest port operators. It grew out of the Port of Singapore Authority, founded in 1964 and reconstituted in 1972, and was corporatised in 1997 as PSA Corporation, taking its present name in 2003. It is wholly owned by Temasek Holdings, Singapore's sovereign investment company.

Operations

PSA operates dozens of deepsea, rail and inland terminals across Asia, Europe, the Middle East and the Americas, handling tens of millions of TEUs of containers annually. Its flagship is the Port of Singapore, for many years the world's busiest container transshipment hub, which PSA is consolidating into the new Tuas Mega Port.

Business

Beyond container terminals, PSA invests in port logistics, supply chain solutions and digital platforms for cargo flows, working with shipping lines, cargo owners and governments.
