Wehani rice

TypeWhole-grain aromatic rice
Developed byLundberg Family Farms, California
ParentageBasmati-derived cultivar
CharacteristicsReddish-brown bran, nutty aroma, chewy texture

Wehani rice is a variety of aromatic brown rice developed in California by Lundberg Family Farms of Richvale. It was bred from a basmati-derived seed line in the late 20th century and is named after the Lundberg brothers — Wendell, Eldon, Homer, and Albert — taking letters from their names (W-E-H-A-N).

Characteristics

Wehani is a whole-grain rice: its bran layer is a distinctive reddish-brown (russet) colour that is retained after milling because the rice is sold unpolished. When cooked it gives off a strong aroma reminiscent of hot buttered peanuts or popcorn and has a slightly chewy texture, similar in character to brown basmati. Like other whole-grain rices it requires a longer cooking time than white rice and retains more fibre, vitamins, and minerals.

Production

The rice is grown in the Sacramento Valley of California, where Lundberg Family Farms has grown rice since 1937. It is marketed as a specialty and gourmet rice, sold both on its own and in whole-grain blends such as wild rice mixtures.
