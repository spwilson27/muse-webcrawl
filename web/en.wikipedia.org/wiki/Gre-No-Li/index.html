Gre-No-Li

Gunnar Gren, Gunnar Nordahl and Nils Liedholm — the Gre-No-Li trio.

Gre-No-Li is a contraction of the surnames of three Swedish footballers: Gunnar Gren, Gunnar Nordahl and Nils Liedholm. The trio formed a celebrated forward line for the Italian club A.C. Milan and the Sweden national team during the late 1940s and 1950s, and the term is a popular moniker in both Swedish and Italian football culture.

The trio

Nordahl, a powerful centre-forward from IFK Norrköping, joined Milan in 1949 and became Serie A's all-time top scorer with 225 goals, winning the Capocannoniere title five times. Gren, an elegant inside forward, and Liedholm, a deep-lying playmaker famed for his passing, followed from Norrköping and elsewhere in Sweden; together the three helped Milan win the Scudetto in 1950–51, the club's first league title in 44 years, and the Latin Cup in 1951.

International careers

All three were members of the Sweden team that won the gold medal at the 1948 London Olympics, defeating Yugoslavia in the final. Professional contracts abroad initially made them ineligible for the national side under Sweden's amateur rules, but the ban was lifted in time for Liedholm — as captain — and Gren to help Sweden reach the final of the 1958 World Cup on home soil, where they lost to Brazil. Liedholm scored the opening goal of that final and later became one of Italy's most respected coaches, winning Serie A with Milan and Roma.
