Laser designator

A laser designator is a laser light source which is used to designate a target. Laser designators provide targeting for laser-guided bombs, missiles, or precision artillery munitions, such as the Paveway series of bombs, Hellfire missiles, and copperhead shells.

Operation

The designator projects a laser beam, usually in the near-infrared so it is invisible to the naked eye, onto the target in a coded pulse pattern. The seeker head of the weapon detects the reflected laser energy within its field of view and steers toward the spot. Designators may be mounted on aircraft, ground vehicles, or carried by infantry and special forces teams.
