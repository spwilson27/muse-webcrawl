List of tallest trees

The world's tallest trees are coast redwoods (Sequoia sempervirens) of Northern California. The tallest known living tree is Hyperion, discovered in Redwood National Park in 2006 and measured at 115.92 metres (380.3 ft). Its exact location is kept secret to protect it from damage, and visiting it was made a punishable offence in 2022.

Other giant species include the giant sequoia (Sequoiadendron giganteum) — the largest by volume but somewhat shorter — the Douglas-fir, the mountain ash (Eucalyptus regnans) of Tasmania and Victoria, and the yellow meranti (Shorea faguetiana) of Borneo, one of which, named Menara, was measured at 100.8 metres in 2019 and is among the tallest known tropical trees.

Limits to tree height

Biologists estimate a practical maximum of roughly 122–130 metres: beyond this height, gravity and the friction of the xylem make it impossible for a tree to draw water to its crown, and leaves near the top show drought stress even in well-watered soils.
