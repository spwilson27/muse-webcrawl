Éowyn

Éowyn is a fictional character in J. R. R. Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings. She is a noblewoman of Rohan, the sister of Éomer and niece of King Théoden. Describing herself as a shieldmaiden, she disguises herself as a man, "Dernhelm", to ride to war at the Battle of the Pelennor Fields.

Role in the story

At the Pelennor Fields, Éowyn confronts the Witch-king of Angmar, who boasts that no living man may hinder him. Revealing herself as a woman, she slays him with the help of the hobbit Meriadoc Brandybuck, fulfilling Glorfindel's prophecy. She later renounces war, becomes a healer, and marries Faramir, Steward of Gondor.

Reception

Éowyn is regarded by critics as one of Tolkien's most complex female characters; her portrayal by Miranda Otto in Peter Jackson's film trilogy was widely praised.
