Netta Barzilai

Netta Barzilai (Hebrew: נטע ברזילי; born 22 January 1993), also known mononymously as Netta, is an Israeli singer. After winning the fifth season of HaKokhav HaBa, she earned the right to represent Israel in the Eurovision Song Contest 2018, which she won with the song "Toy", giving Israel its fourth Eurovision victory.

Early life

Barzilai was born in Hod HaSharon, Israel, and spent four years of her childhood in Nigeria, where her father worked on construction projects. She studied electronic music at the Rimon School of Music, sang in clubs and at weddings, and performed with the Israeli Navy Band during her military service. Her live act developed around a vocal looper, which became her trademark instrument.

Eurovision 2018

In February 2018 Barzilai won HaKokhav HaBa, the Israeli selection show, and was chosen to represent her country. Her entry, "Toy", written by Doron Medalie and Stav Beger, featured her looped vocals, clucking sounds, and lyrics addressing female empowerment in the spirit of the #MeToo movement. At the final in Lisbon on 12 May 2018, the song won with 529 points, combining a third-place jury result with first place in the televote.

Later career

Following Eurovision, Netta released singles including "Bassa Sababa" (2019) and "Ricki Lake" (2020), performed at Eurovision 2019 in Tel Aviv, and appeared as a guest performer in the Netflix film Eurovision Song Contest: The Story of Fire Saga. She has remained a prominent Israeli pop artist known for exuberant stagecraft and body-positive messaging.
