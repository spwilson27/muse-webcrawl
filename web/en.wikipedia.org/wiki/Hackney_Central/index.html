Hackney Central

Hackney Central is a neighbourhood and an electoral ward in the London Borough of Hackney, in east London, England. It forms the historic core of the borough, centred on Mare Street and the area around Hackney Town Hall, and is served by Hackney Central station on the London Overground.

Geography and character

The district lies roughly five miles north-east of Charing Cross, between Dalston to the west, Homerton to the east, and London Fields to the south. It contains a mix of Georgian and Victorian terraces, post-war estates, and the commercial strip of Mare Street and the Narrow Way. Landmarks include St John-at-Hackney church, Hackney Empire theatre, and the gardens around the town hall square.

Transport

Hackney Central station, opened in its present form in 1980 on the site of earlier stations, provides Overground services on the North London line between Stratford and Richmond/Clapham Junction, with a pedestrian link to Hackney Downs station for services to Liverpool Street. Numerous bus routes serve Mare Street.
