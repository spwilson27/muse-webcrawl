Fontvieille, Monaco

CountryMonaco
Areaabout 0.33 km² (33 ha)
Populationabout 3,600

Fontvieille is the southernmost ward (quartier) of the Principality of Monaco. It was developed under Prince Rainier III beginning in the 1970s on land reclaimed from the Mediterranean Sea, making it the newest part of the principality.

Development

The district was built on roughly 22 hectares (later expanded) of reclaimed land at the foot of the Rock of Monaco. It contains residential towers, a shopping centre, the Stade Louis II — home of AS Monaco football club and venue for the UEFA Super Cup for many years — a heliport, and a marina, Port de Fontvieille, accommodating several hundred boats.

Sights

Landmarks include the Princess Grace Rose Garden, created in memory of Grace Kelly; the Monaco Top Cars Collection (the car museum of the Prince's collection); a naval museum; and a sculpture trail. Fontvieille is also home to several light-industry and technology companies, unusual for Monaco's largely service-based economy.
