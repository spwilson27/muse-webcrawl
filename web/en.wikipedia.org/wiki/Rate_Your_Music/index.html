Rate Your Music (often abbreviated RYM) is an online music database and social networking community where users rate, review, and catalog music releases. Founded by Hossein Sharifi in Seattle on December 24, 2000, the site allows members to build collections, maintain listening histories, and contribute to a crowd-sourced metadata database of albums, singles, films, and other media.

Features

Users rate releases on a scale of 0.5 to 5 stars. Aggregate ratings generate charts of the top-rated albums of all time and of each year, which have become influential in online music culture. Members can write reviews, compile custom lists and charts, and discuss music on community forums. A linked film database, Glitchwave for video games, and the album-of-the-year aggregator Sonemic were developed as sister projects.

Reception

Rate Your Music is frequently cited as one of the most influential community-driven music databases on the internet. Its all-time charts, which favor critically acclaimed and underground releases, are widely referenced by listeners and publications.
