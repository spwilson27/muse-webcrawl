The Oregon Ducks are the intercollegiate athletic teams of the University of Oregon in Eugene, Oregon. The university fields 18 varsity teams, most notably in American football, men's and women's basketball, and track and field. Oregon joined the Big Ten Conference in 2024 after a century in the Pac-12 and its predecessors.

Football

The football team plays at Autzen Stadium (capacity about 54,000) and has won multiple conference championships, appearing in national championship games in the 2010 and 2014 seasons. Quarterback Marcus Mariota won the school's first Heisman Trophy in 2014. The programme is famous — and sometimes notorious — for its vast array of Nike uniform combinations, reflecting the university's close relationship with Nike co-founder Phil Knight, an Oregon alumnus. The annual rivalry game with Oregon State is known as the Civil War (now the Platypus Trophy game).

Track and field and other sports

Eugene's Hayward Field has made Oregon a centre of American track and field — "TrackTown USA" — producing legends such as Steve Prefontaine and hosting the 2022 World Athletics Championships. The men's basketball team won the first NCAA tournament in 1939. The Duck mascot is modelled on Donald Duck under a long-standing arrangement with Disney.
