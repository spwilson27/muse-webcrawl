Mini 4WD

Mini 4WD (ミニ四駆, Mini Yonku) is a line of 1/32 scale plastic model cars produced by the Japanese company Tamiya since 1982. Unlike conventional model cars, Mini 4WD cars are powered by an electric motor driving all four wheels through a fixed gear train, run on two AA batteries, and have no steering: they are guided around special three-lane plastic racing tracks by guide rollers mounted on the body and chassis. The cars are built from snap-together kits and can be extensively modified with tuning parts such as motors, gears, rollers, brakes, and mass dampers.

History

Tamiya released the first Mini 4WD kits in 1982, adapting its 1/32 scale static model bodies to a simple four-wheel-drive chassis. The line became a major phenomenon in Japan in the late 1980s and again in the mid-1990s, boosted by manga and anime series such as Dash! Yonkuro and Bakusō Kyōdai Let's & Go!!. Sales boomed across Asia and in Italy, and organized racing — from shop-level events to the official Mini 4WD Japan Cup — became a central part of the hobby. After a quieter period in the 2000s, the line enjoyed a renewed boom in the 2010s with adult hobbyists returning to the hobby.

Design and racing

A Mini 4WD car consists of a polycarbonate or ABS body mounted on a chassis housing a small Mabuchi-type motor, a propeller shaft or direct gear train driving both axles, and terminals for two AA batteries. Because the cars cannot steer, tracks are banked and walled, and setup focuses on speed, stability through corners and jumps, and controlled braking on slope sections. Racers tune gear ratios (from about 3.5:1 to 5:1), tire diameter and grip, roller configuration, and aerodynamic downforce. Cars leaving the track ("course out") are disqualified, so competitive tuning balances outright speed against stability.

Product lines

Series have included the original Type chassis cars, Super Mini 4WD, Fully Cowled Mini 4WD, Aero Mini 4WD, Mighty Mini 4WD, Mini 4WD Pro (with a midship-mounted motor), and the Mini 4WD REV series. Famous models include the Magnum Saber, Sonic Saber, and Avante Jr.
