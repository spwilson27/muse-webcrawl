Cherry picking (disambiguation)

Harvesting cherries by hand (image placeholder)

Cherry picking may refer to:

  • Cherry picking, the act of harvesting cherries, by hand or machine.
  • Cherry picking (fallacy), the fallacy of selecting only evidence that supports one's position while ignoring contradictory evidence; also known as suppressed evidence or incomplete evidence.
  • Cherry picking, in software version control, the act of applying selected commits (changesets) from one branch to another, as with the git cherry-pick command.
  • Cherry picking, in ice hockey, the practice of a player loitering near the opponent's goal awaiting a long pass, away from the main play.
  • Cherry picking, in business and finance, selecting only the most profitable customers, cases, or assets, leaving the rest to competitors or the public sector.
  • Cherry picking, in basketball slang, staying back on defence to receive a fast-break pass for an easy basket.
