Cartoon Saloon is an Irish animation studio based in Kilkenny, founded in 1999 by Tomm Moore, Nora Twomey and Paul Young. Known for its distinctive hand-drawn style drawing on Irish art and folklore, the studio's feature films have received multiple Academy Award nominations.

Feature films

The studio's debut feature, The Secret of Kells (2009), co-directed by Moore and Twomey, told the story of the creation of the Book of Kells and was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature. It was followed by Song of the Sea (2014), based on the selkie legend; The Breadwinner (2017), directed by Twomey and set in Taliban-era Kabul, executive produced by Angelina Jolie; and Wolfwalkers (2020), the concluding part of Moore's informal "Irish folklore trilogy". Each film was Oscar-nominated, establishing the small studio as a major force in independent animation alongside larger American competitors.

Other work

Cartoon Saloon also produces television series, including Puffin Rock, the preschool show narrated by Chris O'Dowd, and has contributed segments to anthology projects such as Star Wars: Visions. The studio champions traditional 2D hand-drawn techniques combined with digital tools.
