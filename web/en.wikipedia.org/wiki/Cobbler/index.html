Cobbler

The term cobbler has several meanings. Its most common sense is a craftsman who repairs shoes; shoemakers who make new footwear are traditionally distinguished from cobblers, who mend them, though the words are now often used interchangeably.

Other meanings

A cobbler is also a baked fruit dessert with a biscuit or batter topping, popular in the United States and Britain, and a type of shaker cocktail made with wine or spirits, sugar and fruit. In Australia, "cobber" is slang for a friend, a distinct but similar-sounding word.

History of the trade

Shoe repair was a widespread village trade for centuries; modern cobblers additionally repair luggage, replace zips and cut keys. The trade has declined with the spread of cheap, glued footwear but persists for quality shoe restoration.
