List of metropolitan areas in India

India is a highly urbanising country with more than 50 urban agglomerations of over one million people. The metropolitan areas of India, as defined by the Census of India (2011) and extrapolated estimates, are headed by Mumbai and Delhi, two of the largest urban agglomerations in the world. Under the 74th Constitutional Amendment, a "metropolitan area" in India is an area with a population of one million or more comprising one or more districts.
