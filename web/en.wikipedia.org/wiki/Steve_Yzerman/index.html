Steve Yzerman

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Stephen Gregory Yzerman (born May 9, 1965) is a Canadian former professional ice hockey player and current executive. He spent his entire 22-season National Hockey League (NHL) playing career with the Detroit Red Wings, serving as team captain for two decades — the longest tenure of any captain in major North American professional sports — and leading the club to three Stanley Cup championships. He later served as general manager of the Tampa Bay Lightning, building the core of a Stanley Cup-winning team, before returning to Detroit as general manager in 2019.

Playing career

Yzerman was drafted fourth overall by Detroit in 1983 and named captain in 1986 at age 21. Initially a prolific scorer — he recorded six consecutive seasons of 100 or more points, peaking at 155 points in 1988–89 — he remade himself under coach Scotty Bowman into an elite two-way centre, winning the Frank J. Selke Trophy as best defensive forward in 2000. He captained the Red Wings to Stanley Cups in 1997, 1998, and 2002, winning the Conn Smythe Trophy as playoff most valuable player in 1998. His double-overtime goal against St. Louis in the 1996 playoffs is among the most famous in franchise history.

Yzerman retired in 2006 ranked among the NHL's all-time leaders with 692 goals, 1,063 assists, and 1,755 points. He won an Olympic gold medal with Canada at the 2002 Salt Lake City Games — Canada's first in 50 years — and was inducted into the Hockey Hall of Fame in 2009.

Executive career

After four years in Detroit's front office, Yzerman became general manager of the Tampa Bay Lightning in 2010. His drafting and trades built a perennial contender that reached the 2015 Stanley Cup Final and won the Presidents' Trophy in 2019; the team won the Stanley Cup the year after his departure with a roster he had largely assembled, and again in 2021. As executive director of Canada's men's Olympic teams, he built the gold-medal squads of 2010 and 2014. In April 2019 he returned to the Red Wings as general manager, embarking on a long-term rebuild of the franchise.
