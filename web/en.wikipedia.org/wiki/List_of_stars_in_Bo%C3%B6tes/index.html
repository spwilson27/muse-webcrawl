List of stars in Boötes

This is a list of notable stars in the constellation Boötes, the herdsman, sorted by decreasing brightness. Boötes contains Arcturus, the brightest star in the northern celestial hemisphere and the fourth-brightest star in the night sky.

Stars with exoplanets

Several Boötes stars are known to host planets, including Tau Boötis, whose hot Jupiter was among the first exoplanets discovered around a Sun-like star, and the giant star HD 132406 and others catalogued by exoplanet surveys.
