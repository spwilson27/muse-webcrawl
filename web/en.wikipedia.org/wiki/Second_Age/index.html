Second Age

The Second Age is a period of 3,441 years in the fictional history of J. R. R. Tolkien's Middle-earth, spanning from the defeat of Morgoth at the end of the First Age to the defeat of Sauron by the Last Alliance of Elves and Men. It is the age of the rise and fall of the island kingdom of Númenor, the forging of the Rings of Power, and the founding of the exiled realms of Arnor and Gondor. The events of the Second Age form the background of The Lord of the Rings and are recounted in its Appendices, in The Silmarillion (chiefly the Akallabêth and "Of the Rings of Power and the Third Age"), in Unfinished Tales, and in the television series The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power.

Outline of events

The age begins after the War of Wrath destroys Beleriand and Morgoth is cast into the Void. The Valar raise the star-shaped island of Númenor in the Great Sea as a reward for the Edain, the Men who fought beside the Elves, granting them lifespans several times those of other Men. The Elves who remain in Middle-earth found the realm of Eregion, whose smiths, led by Celebrimbor, are befriended by Sauron in the fair guise of Annatar, "Lord of Gifts". Guided by him they forge the Rings of Power; Sauron then forges the One Ring in Mount Doom (c. S.A. 1600) to dominate the others. War follows: Eregion is destroyed and Celebrimbor killed, but Númenórean fleets drive Sauron back.

Over the centuries Númenor grows proud and resentful of the Ban of the Valar, which forbids its mariners to sail west toward Valinor. Its kings become colonisers and then tyrants in Middle-earth. The last king, Ar-Pharazôn, sails to Middle-earth and takes Sauron prisoner — a humiliation Sauron turns to advantage, corrupting the court and instigating worship of Morgoth. Persuaded that immortality can be seized by force, Ar-Pharazôn launches a great armada against Valinor in S.A. 3319. Eru Ilúvatar intervenes: Númenor is sunk beneath the sea in the Downfall, the world is made round, and the Undying Lands are removed from mortal reach.

The end of the age

The Faithful Númenóreans, led by Elendil and his sons Isildur and Anárion, escape the Downfall and found the kingdoms of Arnor in the north and Gondor in the south. Sauron, whose spirit survives, returns to Mordor and attacks Gondor. The Last Alliance of Elves and Men, led by Elendil and the Elven-king Gil-galad, besieges Barad-dûr for seven years; in the final battle on the slopes of Mount Doom (S.A. 3441) Sauron is overthrown, Isildur cuts the One Ring from his hand, and the Second Age ends.

Sources and adaptation

Tolkien wrote far less about the Second Age than about the First or Third, and much of its narrative exists as annals and summaries. The 2022 television series The Rings of Power dramatises the age, compressing its 3,441 years into a shorter span for narrative purposes.
