William Morris Endeavor

Formerly: William Morris Agency; WME-IMG; Endeavor Talent Agency
Founded: 2009 (merger); William Morris Agency 1898
Headquarters: Beverly Hills, California
Industry: Talent and entertainment agency

William Morris Endeavor, generally known as WME, is an American talent agency representing artists in film, television, music, theatre, and other fields, and a division of Endeavor Group Holdings. It was formed in 2009 by the merger of the William Morris Agency — founded in 1898 and the oldest talent agency in Hollywood — with the Endeavor Talent Agency.

History

The William Morris Agency traced its origins to 1898, when William Morris began representing vaudeville acts in New York; it grew into a dominant force in film, television, and music. Endeavor, founded in 1995 by Ari Emanuel and others, rose quickly as an aggressive challenger. Their 2009 merger created WME, with Emanuel and Patrick Whitesell as co-CEOs.

Expansion

In 2014 WME, backed by Silver Lake Partners, acquired the sports and media agency IMG for about $2.4 billion, forming WME-IMG. The parent company, renamed Endeavor, expanded into sports properties including the UFC and later went public in 2021. Following Endeavor's take-private transaction with Silver Lake in 2025, WME continued as its core representation business.

Operations

WME represents many of the entertainment industry's leading actors, directors, writers, and musicians, and packages film and television projects by combining talent across departments.
