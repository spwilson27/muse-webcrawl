Colleges and Institutes Canada

Colleges and Institutes Canada (CICan; French: Collèges et instituts Canada) is the national association representing publicly funded colleges, institutes, cégeps and polytechnics in Canada. Based in Ottawa, it advocates on behalf of its members — more than 130 institutions with campuses serving over 3,000 communities across the country — and provides services in areas such as applied research, international partnerships and Indigenous education.

History

The organisation was founded in 1972 as the Association of Canadian Community Colleges (ACCC) and adopted the name Colleges and Institutes Canada in 2014 to reflect the evolving scope of Canada's college system and its members' growing applied-research and degree-granting activities.

Activities

CICan represents members' interests to the federal government, organises national conferences and awards, and runs international development projects that connect Canadian colleges with partners abroad, particularly in support of technical and vocational education and training. It also publishes research and labour-market analysis on skills needs and promotes college-based applied research conducted with business and community partners.

Membership

Members include the country's major college systems, such as provincial college networks in Ontario, Quebec's cégeps, and polytechnic institutions in the West and Atlantic Canada, as well as northern and Indigenous institutes.
