Nicki Minaj

Born Onika Tanya Maraj, December 8, 1982, Saint James, Port of Spain, Trinidad and Tobago

Occupation Rapper, singer, songwriter, actress

Years active 2004–present

Onika Tanya Maraj-Petty (born December 8, 1982), known professionally as Nicki Minaj, is a Trinidadian-born rapper, singer, and songwriter based in the United States. Often referred to as the "Queen of Rap," she is regarded as one of the most influential female rappers of all time, known for her animated, rapid-fire rapping style, musical versatility, and use of alter egos and accents.

Early life

Minaj was born in Port of Spain, Trinidad and Tobago, and raised in Queens, New York City. She studied acting at Fiorello H. LaGuardia High School of Music & Art and Performing Arts before turning to music, releasing the mixtapes Playtime Is Over (2007), Sucka Free (2008), and Beam Me Up Scotty (2009), the last of which brought her wider attention. She signed with Lil Wayne's Young Money Entertainment in 2009.

Career

Her debut studio album, Pink Friday (2010), reached number one on the Billboard 200 and included the hit single "Super Bass." Follow-up albums Pink Friday: Roman Reloaded (2012), The Pinkprint (2014), Queen (2018), and Pink Friday 2 (2023) cemented her commercial success; Pink Friday 2 made her the first female rapper with multiple number-one albums in different decades. Her many hit collaborations include "Bang Bang" and "Anaconda," the latter's video breaking viewing records on release. In 2022, "Super Freaky Girl" made her the first solo female rapper to debut at number one on the Billboard Hot 100 in the 21st century.

Minaj's accolades include numerous American Music Awards, BET Awards, and MTV Video Music Awards, including the Michael Jackson Video Vanguard Award in 2022. Her fan base is known as the "Barbz."
