Gustavo Santaolalla

Gustavo Alfredo Santaolalla (born 19 August 1951) is an Argentine musician, composer and record producer. He won two consecutive Academy Awards for Best Original Score, for Brokeback Mountain (2005) and Babel (2006), and is widely credited as a pioneer of rock en español through his production work with artists such as Café Tacuba, Molotov, Julieta Venegas and Juanes.

Beginning his career with the Argentine band Arco Iris in the late 1960s, Santaolalla emigrated to Los Angeles in 1978. His film work includes Amores perros, 21 Grams, The Motorcycle Diaries and Biutiful; he also composed the acclaimed scores for the video games The Last of Us (2013) and its sequel, and the television adaptation. His playing of the ronroco, a Bolivian lute, is a signature of his sound.
