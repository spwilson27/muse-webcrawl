Schizoanalysis

Schizoanalysis maps flows of desire rather than interpreting symptoms

Schizoanalysis is a concept created by philosopher Gilles Deleuze and psychoanalyst Félix Guattari, developed in their joint works Capitalism and Schizophrenia — Anti-Oedipus (1972) and A Thousand Plateaus (1980). Conceived as a critique and alternative to Freudian and Lacanian psychoanalysis, schizoanalysis analyses social and psychic life in terms of flows of desire and their coding, rather than in terms of representation, symbolism, and the Oedipal family triangle.

Core concepts

  • Desiring-production — desire is not lack but a productive process: "desiring machines" couple flows of energy, bodies, and codes.
  • Body without organs — the unproductive, unorganised surface on which desiring machines operate and which records their production.
  • Codes — society and the psyche encode flows of desire (e.g. the incest prohibition, wage labour); schizophrenia is posited as the process in which codes break down.
  • The schizo as process — not merely a clinical condition but a limit and potential of capitalism itself, which both decodes flows and reterritorialises them.

Critique of psychoanalysis

Deleuze and Guattari argue that psychoanalysis reduces the multiplicity of desire to the familial theatre of "daddy-mommy-me", subordinating social and political investments of desire to the Oedipus complex. Schizoanalysis instead asks how desire is invested in the social field — how it can desire its own repression, as in fascism — and aims to free flows from restrictive codes. Guattari applied related ideas in his clinical work at the La Borde clinic and in institutional psychotherapy.

Influence

Schizoanalysis has influenced political theory, literary studies, media theory, and some therapeutic practice, though it remains controversial within clinical psychology and psychiatry. Its concepts — desiring machines, deterritorialisation, the body without organs — became central vocabulary in post-structuralist and postmodern thought.
