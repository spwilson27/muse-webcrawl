FedEx Express

FedEx Express is a major American cargo airline based in Memphis, Tennessee, United States. It is the largest cargo airline in the world in terms of freight tons flown and operates the world's largest fleet of widebody aircraft.

History

Founded by Frederick W. Smith, Federal Express began operations in 1973 with a fleet of Dassault Falcon 20 aircraft flying from Memphis. The hub-and-spoke system Smith described in a Yale term paper became the model for overnight express delivery. The company rebranded as FedEx Express as part of the parent company's 2000 reorganization.

Fleet

FedEx Express operates Boeing 767-300F, 777F, MD-11F (being retired), Airbus A300-600RF, and ATR and Cessna feeder aircraft via contractors. It was the launch customer for several freighter types and pioneered head-up displays and enhanced vision systems for all-weather operations.

Operations

The Memphis World Hub (SuperHub) sorts millions of packages nightly. Major regional hubs include Indianapolis (second largest), Oakland, Newark, Fort Worth Alliance, Cologne, Paris Charles de Gaulle, and Guangzhou Baiyun. FedEx acquired TNT Express in 2016, expanding its European network.
