Overview

Bayterek is a monument and observation tower in Astana, the capital of Kazakhstan, and the city's defining symbol. Completed in 2002, its design embodies the Kazakh folk tale of the Bayterek, the tree of life, in whose branches the mythical bird Samruk laid a golden egg containing the secrets of human happiness — the egg is the tower's 22-metre golden sphere.

Features

The tower is 105 metres tall overall; the structure's 97-metre height symbolises 1997, when the capital moved from Almaty to Astana. Its observation deck holds a gilded handprint of the first president, Nursultan Nazarbayev, in which visitors place their hands for luck. Bayterek faces the Ak Orda presidential palace across Nurzhol Boulevard and appears on Kazakh banknotes.
