Princess Aiko, Princess Toshi (敬宮愛子内親王, Toshi-no-miya Aiko Naishinnō, born 1 December 2001) is a member of the Imperial House of Japan. She is the only child of Emperor Naruhito and Empress Masako.

Her personal name, Aiko, is written with the kanji characters for "love" (愛) and "child" (子) and means "a person who loves others". Her imperial title, Princess Toshi (敬宮, Toshi-no-miya), means "a person who respects others".

Education

Princess Aiko began her education at Gakushuin Kindergarten in April 2006. She graduated from Gakushuin Girls' Junior High School in March 2017 and entered Gakushuin Girls' Senior High School. In April 2020, she enrolled at Gakushuin University, where she studied Japanese language and literature in the Faculty of Letters. She graduated from the university in March 2024, with a graduation thesis on the medieval waka poet Princess Shikishi.

Public life

After coming of age in December 2021, Princess Aiko began taking part in official duties, including New Year ceremonies at the Imperial Palace and state events. Following her graduation, she began working at the Japanese Red Cross Society in April 2024, where she is involved in volunteer training and disaster relief coordination, while continuing to perform imperial duties.

Succession debate

Aiko's birth sparked debate in Japan about amending the Imperial Household Law, which restricts succession to the Chrysanthemum Throne to males only. Public opinion polls showed strong support for allowing a reigning empress. However, the debate subsided after the birth of her cousin Prince Hisahito in 2006, the first male born to the imperial family in over forty years.
