Nazi book burnings

The Nazi book burnings were a campaign conducted by the German Student Union in 1933 to ceremonially burn books deemed "un-German" — works by Jewish, communist, pacifist and other authors. On 10 May 1933, some 25,000 volumes were burned in Berlin's Opernplatz, with similar burnings in university towns across Germany.

Context

Targeted authors included Bertolt Brecht, Thomas Mann, Erich Maria Remarque, Albert Einstein and Sigmund Freud. Propaganda minister Joseph Goebbels spoke at the Berlin event. The burnings foreshadowed wider Nazi censorship and persecution; a memorial at Bebelplatz, an empty underground library by Micha Ullman, commemorates them.
