Márta Sebestyén

Born19 August 1957, Budapest, Hungary
GenresHungarian folk music, world music
OccupationSinger

Márta Sebestyén (born 19 August 1957) is a Hungarian folk singer renowned for her interpretations of traditional Hungarian folk songs, including music from Transylvania and other regions of the historical Hungarian-speaking world. She was a central figure of the táncház (dance house) folk revival movement.

Career

Sebestyén sang with the group Muzsikás, whose recordings brought Hungarian village music to international audiences, and collaborated with many other ensembles. Her singing was heard by a worldwide audience in the film The English Patient (1996), which featured her performance of the Hungarian folk song "Szerelem, szerelem", and she also contributed to the soundtrack of the anime film Only Yesterday. In 2010 she was named a UNESCO Artist for Peace.
