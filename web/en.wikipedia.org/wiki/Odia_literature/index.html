Odia literature

Odia literature is the literature written in the Odia language, spoken mainly in the Indian state of Odisha. The earliest literature in Odia can be traced to the Charyapadas composed in the 7th to 9th centuries. The modern Odia language formed during the medieval period, with major works by poets such as Sarala Dasa, author of the Odia Mahabharata in the 15th century.

History

Medieval Odia literature flourished with devotional poetry, epics and the Panchasakha poets including Balarama Dasa and Jagannatha Dasa. The modern period began in the 19th century with Fakir Mohan Senapati, considered the father of modern Odia prose fiction, and poets such as Radhanath Ray and Madhusudan Rao. In the 20th century, writers like Gopinath Mohanty won national honours including the Jnanpith Award.
