Cyclone (Coney Island)

Coney Island Cyclone
Location: Luna Park, Coney Island, Brooklyn
Opened: June 26, 1927
Height: 85 ft (26 m)
Designer: Vernon Keenan

The Coney Island Cyclone is a wooden roller coaster at Luna Park in Coney Island, Brooklyn, New York City. It opened on June 26, 1927, and is one of the oldest wooden coasters still operating in the United States.

History

Designed by Vernon Keenan and built by Harry C. Baker, the Cyclone cost about 75,000 to construct. It survived the decline of Coney Island's amusement parks and threats of demolition; in 1988 it was designated a New York City landmark, and in 1991 it was listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

Ride experience

The coaster stands 85 feet tall with a first drop of about 59 degrees, reaching speeds near 60 mph over 2,640 feet of track with twelve drops and twenty-seven elevation changes. It is celebrated by enthusiasts for its intense airtime and is often called one of the greatest wooden roller coasters ever built.
