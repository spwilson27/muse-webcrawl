Aviation

Aviation
DefinitionOperation and development of aircraft
MilestonesWright brothers (1903), jet airliners (1950s)
[Image: Air traffic arcs over a globe in a stylised route map]

Aviation is the design, development, production, operation and use of aircraft. From the Wright brothers' airplane it grew into a global system of airlines, airports, air-traffic control and regulators (ICAO, FAA, EASA) carrying billions of passengers a year.

Challenges

Aviation is hard to decarbonise — sustainable fuels and new designs are its frontiers — and safety systems made it statistically the safest way to travel. Drones and urban air mobility extend aviation's reach, regulated alongside the satellites that navigate the fleet.
