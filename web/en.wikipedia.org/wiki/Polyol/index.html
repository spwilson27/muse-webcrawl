Polyol

A polyol is an organic compound containing multiple hydroxyl groups (–OH). The term covers both simple molecules such as glycerol and ethylene glycol and polymeric polyols used in industry.

Types and uses

Sugar alcohols — sorbitol, mannitol, xylitol, and erythritol — are polyols used as sweeteners in food. In polymer chemistry, polyether and polyester polyols are reacted with isocyanates to make polyurethanes, including flexible and rigid foams, coatings, and elastomers. Polyols are also used in alkyd resins and as humectants in cosmetics and pharmaceuticals.

Properties

Industrial polyols are characterised by their hydroxyl number, molecular weight, and functionality (the number of hydroxyl groups per molecule), which determine the properties of the resulting polymer.
