Zastava Automobiles

Founded: 1953, Kragujevac, Yugoslavia (now Serbia)

Defunct: 2008

Industry: Automotive

Zastava Automobiles was a Serbian (formerly Yugoslav) car manufacturer based in Kragujevac, best known for the Yugo, a small hatchback exported to the United States in the 1980s.

History

The Zastava works began as a 19th-century arms factory; automobile production under licence from Fiat started in 1954. Zastava produced Fiat-derived models such as the 750 ("Fića"), the 101, and later its own designs including the Yugo 45 and the Florida. The Yugo was sold in the US from 1985 at a very low price, gaining notoriety for poor reliability.

Decline

Production was disrupted by sanctions and the 1999 NATO bombing of the Kragujevac plant. Fiat took a majority stake in a successor venture in 2008, ending the Zastava marque; the site now builds Fiat vehicles as FCA Serbia.
