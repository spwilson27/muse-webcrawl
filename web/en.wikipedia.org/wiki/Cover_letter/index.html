Cover letter

A cover letter, also called a covering letter, application letter or letter of motivation, is a letter of introduction attached to or accompanying another document, most commonly a résumé or curriculum vitae submitted when applying for a job. Its purpose is to introduce the applicant, explain their interest in the position, and highlight the experience and skills most relevant to the employer.

TypeBusiness correspondence

Structure

A conventional cover letter contains the applicant's and employer's contact details, a salutation, an opening paragraph stating the position sought, one or two paragraphs connecting the applicant's qualifications to the job requirements, and a closing paragraph requesting an interview and thanking the reader. Career advisers generally recommend tailoring each letter to the specific employer rather than sending a generic text, and keeping it to a single page.

Other uses

The term also applies to letters accompanying manuscripts submitted to academic journals, grant applications, or documents sent to publishers, briefly explaining the work's significance and suitability.
