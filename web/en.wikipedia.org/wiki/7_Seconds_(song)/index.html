"7 Seconds" is a song performed by Senegalese singer Youssou N'Dour and Swedish singer Neneh Cherry, released in June 1994 as a single from N'Dour's album The Guide (Wommat). The song is sung in three languages — Wolof, French, and English — and became a worldwide hit, reaching number one in several countries including France, Finland, Iceland, and Switzerland, and number three in the United Kingdom.

Composition and themes

N'Dour sings in Wolof and French while Cherry sings in English. The title refers to the first seven seconds of a child's life, a time of innocence before the child becomes aware of the world's problems and violence; the lyrics address racism and prejudice. The song's evocative chorus and cross-cultural collaboration made it one of the most memorable world-music crossover hits of the 1990s. It won the MTV Europe Music Award for Best Song in 1994.

Reception and legacy

"7 Seconds" spent 16 weeks at number one in France and sold millions of copies worldwide. It has been covered and adapted several times, and N'Dour and Cherry performed it together again at events including Live 8 in 2005. Critics have praised the duet as a landmark collaboration between African and Western pop artists.
