A-1 Pictures Inc. is a Japanese animation studio founded in 2005 by Mikihiro Iwata, a former producer at Sunrise. It is a subsidiary of Aniplex, Sony's anime production company, and is headquartered in Suginami, Tokyo.

History and works

The studio was established to animate Aniplex productions and quickly became one of the most prolific television anime studios. Its breakthrough successes include Sword Art Online (2012), which became a global franchise, the music drama Your Lie in April (2014), The Seven Deadly Sins (first seasons), Kaguya-sama: Love Is War, Lycoris Recoil (2022) and Solo Leveling (2024). Film credits include the Persona 3 film series and work on theatrical features. In 2018 its Kōenji studio was spun off as CloverWorks, which became a sister studio under Aniplex.

Reputation

A-1 Pictures is known for polished, commercially successful adaptations and for its unusual practice of crediting the studio itself prominently as author. It has also faced industry scrutiny over heavy production schedules and reliance on freelance staff, common across the anime industry.
