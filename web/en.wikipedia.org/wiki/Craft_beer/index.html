Craft beer

Image: A flight of craft beers in tasting glasses (placeholder)

Craft beer is beer produced by a craft brewery — a brewery that is small, independent and traditional. In the United States, the Brewers Association defines an American craft brewer as one producing fewer than 6 million barrels a year, with less than 25 percent ownership by a non-craft alcohol company, and brewing most of its volume in traditional or innovative styles. Craft breweries include microbreweries, nanobreweries, brewpubs and regional craft breweries.

History

The modern craft beer movement began in the 1960s and 1970s as a reaction against the dominance of pale mass-market lager. In Britain, the Campaign for Real Ale (CAMRA, founded 1971) championed cask-conditioned ale; in the United States, Fritz Maytag's revival of Anchor Brewing in San Francisco from 1965 and Jack McAuliffe's New Albion Brewing Company (1976) pioneered the microbrewery model. Legalisation of homebrewing in the US in 1978 fed a generation of founders, including Ken Grossman of Sierra Nevada Brewing (founded 1979) and Jim Koch of Boston Beer Company (1984).

Growth and styles

The number of US craft breweries grew from a handful in the late 1970s to more than 9,000 by the 2020s, and craft beer has taken a significant share of the overall beer market by value. Craft brewers revived and popularised styles such as India pale ale (especially American and hazy/New England IPA), barrel-aged stout, sour and wild ales, and saisons, using flavourful ingredients and local experimentation. Similar movements spread internationally, to countries including Canada, Australia, Italy, Japan and the Scandinavian nations.

Issues

The definition of "craft" is debated, particularly when successful craft breweries are acquired by large brewing groups, or when large brewers market "crafty" brands of their own. Craft brewers emphasise independence, flavour and community connection; the Brewers Association introduced an "independent craft brewer seal" in 2017 to distinguish qualifying brands on packaging.
