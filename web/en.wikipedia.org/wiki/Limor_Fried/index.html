Limor Fried is an American electrical engineer and open-source hardware pioneer, founder of the electronics company Adafruit Industries. Known online by the handle Ladyada, she is recognised for expanding public access to electronics education and for championing open-source hardware licensing.

Career

Fried studied electrical engineering and computer science at MIT, earning bachelor's and master's degrees. While a student she began selling electronics kits online; in 2005 she founded Adafruit, named for Ada Lovelace, initially from her dorm room. The company grew into a major manufacturer and educator in New York City, shipping kits and components such as the Circuit Playground and Feather boards and publishing extensive free tutorials.

Recognition

Fried was the first female engineer featured on the cover of Wired magazine (2011) and received the White House's "Champion of Change" recognition and Entrepreneur magazine's Entrepreneur of the Year award in 2012. She has served as a prominent voice for open-source hardware and women in engineering.
