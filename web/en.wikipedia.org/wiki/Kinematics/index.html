Kinematics

Kinematics is a subfield of physics and mathematics, and a branch of classical mechanics, that describes the motion of points, bodies and systems of bodies without considering the forces that cause the motion. It is often called the "geometry of motion".

Concepts

Kinematics studies trajectories in terms of position, velocity and acceleration. For motion in a straight line with constant acceleration, the kinematic equations relate displacement, initial and final velocity, acceleration and time. Rotational kinematics describes angular position, angular velocity and angular acceleration. Relative motion and reference frames are central to the analysis, and the subject underlies dynamics, robotics and astronomy.
