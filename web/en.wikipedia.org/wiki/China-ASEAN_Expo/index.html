China-ASEAN Expo

The China-ASEAN Expo (CAEXPO) is an annual international trade and investment exhibition held in Nanning, Guangxi, China. It is co-sponsored by the governments of China and the ten member states of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN), with the ASEAN Secretariat as a co-organiser. The expo was proposed by Premier Wen Jiabao in 2003 and first held in 2004, alongside the China-ASEAN Business and Investment Summit.

Purpose

The expo serves as a platform to promote trade, investment and economic cooperation within the China–ASEAN Free Trade Area, which came into full effect in 2010 and covers some 1.9 billion people. Each year features a designated "Country of Honour" from among the ASEAN members and includes commodity exhibitions, investment promotion forums and cultural exchanges. It has since been extended to support cooperation under the Belt and Road Initiative and the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership (RCEP).

Venue

The event is hosted at the Nanning International Convention and Exhibition Center, whose design incorporates the hibiscus, the city flower of Nanning. Guangxi's proximity to Southeast Asia — it borders Vietnam — underpins Nanning's role as China's gateway to ASEAN.
