SpaceX Starship

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Starship's integrated flight test from Starbase.

Starship is a fully reusable super heavy-lift launch vehicle under development by SpaceX. The system consists of the Super Heavy booster, powered by 33 Raptor engines burning liquid methane and oxygen, and the Starship spacecraft itself, with six Raptors. At about 120 metres tall it is the largest and most powerful rocket ever flown.

Starship made its first integrated flight test in April 2023 from Starbase in Boca Chica, Texas; the vehicle was destroyed after stage separation failed. Subsequent tests achieved stage separation, orbital-velocity flight, a booster catch by the launch tower's 'chopstick' arms in October 2024, and ship landings in the Indian Ocean. NASA has selected a lunar variant of Starship as the human landing system for the Artemis program's return to the Moon, and SpaceX intends the vehicle for cargo and crew missions to Mars.
