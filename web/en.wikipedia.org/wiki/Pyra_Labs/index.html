Pyra Labs

Pyra Labs was a San Francisco-based company founded in 1999 by Evan Williams and Meg Hourihan. It created Blogger, one of the first dedicated web applications for creating and maintaining weblogs, which helped popularize blogging as a mainstream online activity.

History

Pyra Labs originally set out to build web-based project management software. Its blogging tool, launched in August 1999, began as a side feature but rapidly became the company's focus. Blogger allowed users to publish to their own websites via FTP, and later offered hosted Blogspot accounts. The service grew rapidly during the dot-com era despite financial difficulties; in 2001, after the company's funds ran low, its staff worked without pay for a period to keep the service running.

Acquisition

In February 2003, Pyra Labs was acquired by Google, in a deal that brought Blogger and its team into the company. Evan Williams left Google in 2004 and later co-founded Twitter and Medium.
