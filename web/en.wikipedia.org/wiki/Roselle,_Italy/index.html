Roselle, Italy

Location: Grosseto, Tuscany, Italy
Region: Maremma
Type: Ancient city (Etruscan, then Roman)
Site notes: Archaeological park

Roselle (Italian: Roselle), known in antiquity as Rusellae, was an ancient city of Etruria. Its ruins lie near the modern village of Roselle in the municipality of Grosseto in Tuscany, Italy.

History

Rusellae was one of the cities of the Etruscan league and flourished in the 7th–6th centuries BC. It was conquered by Rome in 294 BC. The city retained importance into the early Middle Ages as a bishopric, but declined because of the silting of the nearby lagoon and malaria, and its population eventually transferred to Grosseto.

Archaeology

The site preserves impressive stretches of its cyclopean city walls, an amphitheatre, the forum, and remains of Etruscan and Roman houses. Excavations have made Roselle an important archaeological park of the Maremma.
