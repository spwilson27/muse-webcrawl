Sweet Caroline

"Sweet Caroline" is a song written and performed by American singer Neil Diamond, released in May 1969 as a single. It reached number 4 on the Billboard Hot 100 and has become one of Diamond's signature songs and a popular singalong at sporting events worldwide.

Inspiration

Diamond long declined to name the song's inspiration, later revealing that a photograph of Caroline Kennedy as a young girl had inspired the title, though he wrote the song about his then wife Marcia, with the name adjusted to fit the melody.

Sporting anthem

The song became a fixture at Boston Red Sox games at Fenway Park, played in the middle of the eighth inning, and has been adopted by fans of many other teams, including the England national football team, whose supporters made it an unofficial anthem during Euro 2020.
