ISO 3166-2:MZ

ISO 3166-2:MZ is the entry for Mozambique in ISO 3166-2, part of the ISO 3166 standard published by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO), which defines codes for the names of the principal subdivisions of all countries coded in ISO 3166-1. Currently for Mozambique, ISO 3166-2 codes are defined for 1 city and 10 provinces.

Background

Mozambique's country code in ISO 3166-1 is MZ (alpha-2), with alpha-3 MOZ and numeric 508. Each subdivision code consists of the country code, a hyphen, and one to three letters. Maputo city (MZ-MPM) is distinct from surrounding Maputo Province (MZ-L). The codes were first published with ISO 3166-2 in 1998 and have been maintained through periodic newsletters and updates of the standard.
