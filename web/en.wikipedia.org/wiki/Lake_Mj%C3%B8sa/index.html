Lake Mjøsa

Mjøsa is the largest lake in Norway and one of the deepest in Europe. It lies in the counties of Innlandet and Akershus, stretching about 117 km from Lillehammer in the north to Minnesund in the south.

Geography

The lake covers about 365 square kilometres and reaches a maximum depth of around 449 metres. Its main inflow is the Gudbrandsdalslågen, and it drains via the Vorma River into the Glomma. The towns of Hamar, Gjøvik, and Lillehammer stand on its shores, and the island of Helgøya is its largest island.

Transport

The paddle steamer Skibladner, launched in 1856, still carries passengers on the lake in summer and is the world's oldest paddle steamer in scheduled service.
