David Leslie Schultz (6 June 1959 – 26 January 1996) was an American freestyle wrestler. He won the Olympic gold medal in the 74 kg class at the 1984 Los Angeles Games and the world championship in 1983 in Kyiv, along with four World Cup titles, making him one of the most decorated American wrestlers of his era.

Career

Schultz wrestled collegiately at Oklahoma State and the University of Oklahoma, winning the 1982 NCAA title. Internationally he won seven world and Olympic medals in total, a record for an American at the time, and was known for learning Russian to study Soviet technique and for coaching and mentoring younger wrestlers, including his younger brother Mark Schultz, also an Olympic champion in 1984.

Death and legacy

In the 1990s Schultz coached the "Team Foxcatcher" squad financed by millionaire John du Pont at Foxcatcher Farm in Pennsylvania. On 26 January 1996 du Pont shot and killed Schultz outside his home on the estate; du Pont, who had schizophrenia, was convicted of third-degree murder and died in prison in 2010. The story was dramatised in the 2014 film Foxcatcher, in which Schultz was played by Mark Ruffalo.
