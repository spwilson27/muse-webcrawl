Hurlburt Field

Hurlburt Field (HRT)
Location: Mary Esther, Florida, U.S.
Type: U.S. Air Force base
Host: 1st Special Operations Wing
Higher command: Air Force Special Operations Command (headquarters)

Hurlburt Field is a United States Air Force installation located in Okaloosa County, Florida, immediately west of the town of Mary Esther and adjacent to Eglin Air Force Base. It is the headquarters of Air Force Special Operations Command (AFSOC) and home of the 1st Special Operations Wing.

History

The field was originally Eglin Air Force Base Auxiliary Field No. 9, laid out during the Second World War, and is named for First Lieutenant Donald Wilson Hurlburt, a pilot killed in a crash at Eglin in 1943. In the early 1960s it became the centre for the Air Force's renewed special-operations and counterinsurgency mission — the Air Commandos — training crews for operations in Southeast Asia. It was designated a separate Air Force base in the 1970s and grew with the creation of the 23rd Air Force and then AFSOC in 1990.

Units and aircraft

The 1st Special Operations Wing flies aircraft including the AC-130J Ghostrider gunship, MC-130J Commando II, CV-22 Osprey, and U-28A Draco. Hurlburt also hosts the Air Force Special Operations Air Warfare Center and training units that prepare air commandos for worldwide deployment. An air park near the main gate displays historic special-operations aircraft, including early AC-47 and AC-130 gunships.
