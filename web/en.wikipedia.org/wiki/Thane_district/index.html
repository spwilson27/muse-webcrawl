Thane district

Thane district (Marathi: ठाणे जिल्हा) is a district in the Konkan division of Maharashtra state in western India. At the 2011 census it was the most populated district in India, with a population of about 11 million; in 2014 the district was bifurcated to create the new Palghar district. The district headquarters is the city of Thane.

Geography

The district lies immediately northeast of Mumbai, bounded by the Arabian Sea to the west and the Sahyadri (Western Ghats) hills to the east. It includes several large and rapidly growing cities of the Mumbai Metropolitan Region, including Thane, Kalyan-Dombivli, Mira-Bhayandar, Bhiwandi, Ulhasnagar, and Badlapur. The Ulhas and Vaitarna river systems drain the district.

Economy and transport

Historically noted for agriculture and fishing, the district has become heavily urbanised and industrialised, with manufacturing, logistics, and information-technology employment. It is served by the Central and Western lines of the Mumbai Suburban Railway and by national highways linking Mumbai with Nashik and Ahmedabad.
