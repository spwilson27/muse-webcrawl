Workington Man

Workington Man is a political term used in British politics from 2019 for a stereotypical swing voter: typically a working-class, Leave-voting man in a northern English town such as Workington, Cumbria, whom the Conservative Party sought to win from Labour.

Origin: Onward think tank report, 2019
Archetype: Northern working-class swing voter
Related: Red wall

Origin and use

The think tank Onward coined the persona ahead of the 2019 general election, describing a voter over 45, interested in rugby league and security, sceptical of metropolitan liberalism. The Conservatives won Workington in 2019 as part of their red wall gains; Labour regained the wider area in 2024. The term is compared with earlier archetypes like Essex man and Worcester woman.
