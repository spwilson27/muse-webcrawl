Dracaena

Dracaena is a genus of about 200 species of trees and succulent shrubs in the family Asparagaceae, subfamily Nolinoideae. The genus is native mostly to the tropics of Africa, with a few species extending into southern Asia and one into tropical Central America. Following molecular studies, the former genus Sansevieria (snake plants) is now included within Dracaena.

Many species are popular houseplants, grown for their attractive foliage and tolerance of low light and irregular watering. Notable species include Dracaena fragrans (the corn plant), Dracaena marginata (the dragon tree), and Dracaena draco, the Canary Islands dragon tree, whose red resin is known as dragon's blood.

Description

Species of Dracaena have secondary thickening meristems in their trunks, allowing some to grow into trees. Leaves are typically long and strap-shaped, often variegated with stripes of green, cream, or red.
