County Wicklow

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

County Wicklow (Irish: Contae Chill Mhantáin) is a county in Ireland, in the province of Leinster, bordering Dublin to the north and the Irish Sea to the east. The county town is Wicklow, and the largest town is Bray; the population was about 156,000 in 2022. It was the last of Ireland's traditional counties to be created, in 1606.

Geography

Known as the "Garden of Ireland", the county is dominated by the Wicklow Mountains, the largest continuous upland area in Ireland, much of it protected as Wicklow Mountains National Park. The highest point is Lugnaquilla at 925 metres. The monastic settlement of Glendalough, founded by St Kevin in the 6th century, the Powerscourt Estate and waterfall, and the Sally Gap and Wicklow Gap passes are among its best-known attractions. Ardmore Studios at Bray made the county a centre of Irish film production.
