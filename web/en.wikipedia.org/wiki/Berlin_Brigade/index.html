Berlin Brigade

The Berlin Brigade was a United States Army brigade-sized garrison based in West Berlin during the Cold War. Together with British and French forces, it represented the Western Allies' occupation rights in the divided city.

Berlin Brigade
Active: 1961–1994
Country: United States
Branch: U.S. Army
Garrison: Clay Headquarters Compound, West Berlin

History

The brigade was formed in 1961 during the Berlin Crisis that produced the Berlin Wall. Its infantry battalions rotated from the 502nd, 503rd/509th and other regiments over the years. U.S. troops had access to West Berlin via the Helmstedt–Berlin autobahn and rail corridors under four-power agreements. After German reunification, the brigade was inactivated in 1994 as Allied forces withdrew.
