Upright Citizens Brigade

Formation1990 (troupe); 1996 (New York theatre)
FoundersMatt Besser, Amy Poehler, Ian Roberts, Matt Walsh
TypeImprovisational and sketch comedy troupe and theatre
LocationsNew York City; Los Angeles

The Upright Citizens Brigade (UCB) is an American improvisational and sketch comedy troupe and theatre organisation. The troupe originated in Chicago in the early 1990s, where its members — Matt Besser, Amy Poehler, Ian Roberts, and Matt Walsh — trained at the ImprovOlympic under Charna Halpern and Del Close, and relocated to New York City in 1996, bringing long-form improvisation, particularly the "Harold" format, to a wide audience.

Theatre and training centre

The UCB Theatre became a major comedy institution, offering inexpensive performances and a structured training programme in improvisation and sketch writing. Its theatres in New York (Chelsea, then Hell's Kitchen and the East Village) and Los Angeles trained generations of performers. In 2020 the New York theatres closed amid financial difficulties and the COVID-19 pandemic; the organisation was subsequently acquired by a group including comedian Mike McHale, and a New York theatre reopened in 2024. The Los Angeles venue on Franklin Avenue continued operating.

Television

The troupe starred in the sketch comedy series Upright Citizens Brigade on Comedy Central (1998–2000), structured around a shadowy organisation controlling society from an underground lair. Members and alumni went on to prominent careers in television and film, including work on Saturday Night Live, Parks and Recreation, Veep, 30 Rock, and Broad City.
