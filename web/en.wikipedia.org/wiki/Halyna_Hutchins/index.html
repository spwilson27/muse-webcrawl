Halyna Hutchins (née Androsovych; 10 April 1979 – 21 October 2021) was a Ukrainian cinematographer. She worked on more than 30 feature-length films, short films, and television series. Her career was cut short when she was fatally shot on the set of the film Rust during production in New Mexico.

Early life and education

Hutchins was born in Horodets, Zhytomyr Oblast, in what was then the Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic. She grew up on a Soviet military base in the Arctic Circle. She studied international journalism at Kyiv National University, then worked as an investigative journalist with British documentary productions in Eastern Europe before moving to Los Angeles. She earned a master's degree from the AFI Conservatory in 2015.

Career

Hutchins worked her way up from production assistant and grip to director of photography. Her credits include the films Archenemy (2020), Blindfire (2020), and Darlin' (2019), which premiered at SXSW. In 2019 the magazine American Cinematographer named her one of its "Rising Stars of Cinematography".

Death

On 21 October 2021, during filming of the Western Rust at the Bonanza Creek Ranch in Santa Fe County, New Mexico, actor Alec Baldwin discharged a single-action revolver he had been told was "cold" (unloaded); the gun contained a live round. Hutchins was struck in the chest and director Joel Souza was wounded. Hutchins was airlifted to the University of New Mexico Hospital in Albuquerque, where she was pronounced dead. The incident prompted a broad reckoning over firearm safety on film sets, extensive litigation, and criminal proceedings against the production's armourer and others.

Legacy

The AFI Conservatory established a scholarship fund for women cinematographers in Hutchins' memory. The film Rust was completed and dedicated to her.
