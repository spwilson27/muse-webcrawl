Battle of Ansi

The Battle of Ansi was a major engagement of the first Goguryeo–Tang War, fought in 645 CE around the fortress of Ansi (Korean: 안시성, Ansi-seong) in the Liaodong region of present-day Liaoning, China. Emperor Taizong of the Tang dynasty personally led a large expedition against the Korean kingdom of Goguryeo. After defeating a Goguryeo relief army at Mount Jupil, Tang forces besieged Ansi for some sixty days but failed to take it, and withdrew as winter approached. The defence of Ansi is one of the most celebrated episodes in Korean military history, and the fortress's unnamed commander — later tradition identifies him as Yang Manchun — became a national hero.

Background

Emperor Taizong, smarting from Goguryeo's defiance and the coup of 642 in which the military strongman Yeon Gaesomun seized power, invaded in 645 with a large land army and a supporting fleet. Tang forces captured a string of fortresses including Liaodong and Baegam; the fall of Gaemo and the fortress of Yodong brought them before Ansi, a key stronghold guarding the route into the Goguryeo interior.

Battle of Mount Jupil

A Goguryeo relief army of about 150,000 men, drawn from Goguryeo and its Mohe allies, advanced to raise the siege. Taizong, leaving a small force to mask Ansi, met the relief army at Mount Jupil (Zhupilsan). In a bold flanking manoeuvre, Tang cavalry under the emperor's direction struck the Goguryeo army from the rear while infantry engaged from the front, routing it with heavy losses. The victory left Ansi isolated, but the fortress itself held.

Siege

For about two months the Tang assaulted Ansi with siege engines, rams and a huge earthen ramp built to overlook the walls. The defenders repeatedly counter-attacked; in one famous episode they sortied and seized the partially built ramp, holding it against Tang assaults. Korean tradition credits the garrison commander with wounding Taizong himself with an arrow — a story repeated in later literature but not in the earliest Chinese records. With supplies running low, autumn rains turning the ground to mud, and winter approaching, Taizong ordered a withdrawal in September/October 645.

Legacy

The failed siege ended Tang ambitions in Taizong's lifetime; although the Tang and Silla eventually destroyed Goguryeo in 668, the defence of Ansi entered Korean folklore and modern popular culture, including the 2018 South Korean film The Great Battle, which dramatises the siege with Yang Manchun as its hero.
