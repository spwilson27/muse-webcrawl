Let's Play

A Let's Play (LP) is a type of video (or, historically, a screenshot-based forum thread) in which a person plays a video game while providing commentary. Let's Plays differ from walkthroughs or strategy guides in emphasizing the player's subjective experience, humor and reactions rather than instructing the viewer how to complete the game.

History

The format originated around 2006–2007 on the Something Awful forums, where the term "Let's Play" was coined and early screenshot-and-text playthroughs appeared; one of the earliest video examples covered The Oregon Trail. The format migrated to YouTube, where creators such as PewDiePie built enormous audiences during the early 2010s, helping make gaming one of the platform's dominant content categories.

Format and culture

Let's Plays range from blind first-time playthroughs to highly edited compilations, and from solo commentary to groups. They overlap with video game live streaming on platforms such as Twitch. The genre has influenced game marketing, with publishers courting creators for coverage, and has raised copyright questions about the use of game footage, addressed by many publishers through permissive content policies.

Impact

Let's Play creators became major online celebrities, and the format contributed to the rise of influencer culture and the popularity of certain games, particularly horror and sandbox titles whose sales surged after viral videos.
