Mandukhai Khatun

Mandukhai Khatun (c. 1449 – 1510), also known as Mandukhai Sechen Khatun, was an empress consort and later regent of the Northern Yuan dynasty in Mongolia. She is remembered for restoring Borjigin rule and for raising and marrying Dayan Khan.

Life

Widowed when her husband Manduul Khan died in 1479, Mandukhai refused proposals from warlords seeking the throne and instead sought out Batumöngke, a young direct descendant of Genghis Khan, proclaiming him Dayan Khan. She served as regent, led campaigns against the Oirats to reunify the Mongols, and later married Dayan Khan when he came of age.

Legacy

Mandukhai is celebrated in Mongolian tradition as a wise and heroic queen who preserved the khanate during a period of fragmentation. Her life has been retold in chronicles, novels, and film.
