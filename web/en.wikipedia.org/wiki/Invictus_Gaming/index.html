Invictus Gaming

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Invictus Gaming at a League of Legends tournament

Invictus Gaming (iG) is a Chinese multi-game esports organisation founded in 2011 by Wang Sicong, who purchased the team formerly known as CCM. The organisation is best known for its successes in Dota 2 and League of Legends.

Achievements

Invictus Gaming's Dota 2 team won The International 2012 in Seattle, defeating Natus Vincere in the final and winning one million US dollars. The organisation's League of Legends team won the 2018 World Championship in South Korea, defeating Fnatic 3–0 in the final—the first world championship title for a team from China's LPL.

Divisions

In addition to Dota 2 and League of Legends, iG has fielded rosters in games including StarCraft II, Counter-Strike: Global Offensive, Overwatch and Hearthstone, and is based in Shanghai.
