Avalanche Software is an American video game developer based in Salt Lake City, Utah. Founded in 1995 by four programmers, the studio is best known for the toys-to-life Disney Infinity series developed for Disney Interactive Studios and, after its acquisition by Warner Bros. Games, the open-world action role-playing game Hogwarts Legacy (2023), one of the best-selling video games of all time.

History

Avalanche Software was founded in October 1995. In its early years it developed titles for publishers including Midway and Acclaim, producing games such as Mortal Kombat Mythologies: Sub-Zero (1997), Rampage 2: Universal Tour, and 25 to Life. In 2005 the studio was acquired by Buena Vista Games, the games arm of Disney, and it became part of Disney Interactive Studios.

Under Disney, Avalanche developed licensed games including Chicken Little, Meet the Robinsons, Bolt, and the Toy Story 3 tie-in game, which was widely praised. In 2013 the studio launched Disney Infinity, a toys-to-life platform combining physical figurines with sandbox gameplay; two sequels followed in 2014 and 2015. Disney cancelled the series and closed the studio in May 2016 as part of its exit from console game publishing.

In January 2017, Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment acquired and reopened Avalanche Software, retaining much of its staff. The studio developed Cars 3: Driven to Win (2017) and then Hogwarts Legacy, released in February 2023. Set in the Wizarding World of Harry Potter, the game sold more than 22 million copies in its first year and became the best-selling game of 2023 worldwide.
