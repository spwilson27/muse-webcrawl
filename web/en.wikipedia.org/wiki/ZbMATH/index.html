zbMATH, now zbMATH Open, is an international abstracting and reviewing service for research in pure and applied mathematics. It indexes articles, books, and conference proceedings in mathematics and related fields, providing bibliographic data, abstracts, and expert reviews, classified by the Mathematics Subject Classification (MSC).

History

The service began in 1931 as the journal Zentralblatt für Mathematik und ihre Grenzgebiete, founded by Otto Neugebauer and published by Springer — the first abstracting journal devoted to mathematics. After the Second World War the editorial office was re-established in Berlin, and the Zentralblatt became a joint project involving academies and mathematical societies in East and West. It later moved online as the MATH database and was renamed zbMATH, standing for "Zentralblatt MATH."

Open access

On 1 January 2021 zbMATH became fully open access under the name zbMATH Open, maintained by FIZ Karlsruhe – Leibniz Institute for Information Infrastructure in cooperation with the European Mathematical Society and the Heidelberg Academy of Sciences and Humanities. The database contains several million records covering mathematical literature from the 19th century to the present, and it is, alongside the American Mathematical Reviews (MathSciNet), one of the two principal reviewing services in mathematics.
