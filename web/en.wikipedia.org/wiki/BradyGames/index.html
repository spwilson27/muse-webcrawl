BradyGames was an American publisher of video game strategy guides. Founded in 1993 in Indianapolis as part of the Brady division of Macmillan/Simon & Schuster publishing, it became one of the two dominant North American strategy-guide publishers, the other being Prima Games, and published its guides under the banner of "Take Your Game Further".

History and output

BradyGames produced official guides, licensed by game publishers, alongside many unofficial titles. Its catalogue covered major franchises including Final Fantasy, Kingdom Hearts, Grand Theft Auto, World of Warcraft, Diablo, The Legend of Zelda and Pokémon-era blockbusters; typical guides ran to several hundred glossy pages with maps, walkthroughs, item lists, artwork and developer interviews. Several million-selling guides were issued, and limited "Signature Series" and hardcover collector's editions catered to fans.

The imprint passed through several owners — Pearson's Dorling Kindersley held it in the 2000s — and moved into digital products, releasing eGuides and apps as print sales declined with the rise of free online walkthroughs, wikis and video guides.

Closure

In June 2015, BradyGames' parent announced that the imprint would be merged into Prima Games, ending the BradyGames name after more than two decades and hundreds of titles. Prima itself was closed in 2019 (and subsequently revived online), marking the end of the large-scale print strategy-guide industry the two firms had dominated.
