Europass

Europass is a European Union initiative to increase the transparency and comparability of qualifications and competences across Europe, helping citizens present their skills clearly when applying for jobs or training in another country. It was established by a Decision of the European Parliament and the Council in 2004 and is coordinated by national Europass centres.

Components

The original Europass consisted of five documents: the Europass CV, a standardised curriculum vitae format; the Language Passport, based on the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages; Europass Mobility, recording periods of learning abroad; the Diploma Supplement, attached to higher-education degrees; and the Certificate Supplement, describing vocational qualifications.

The new Europass platform

In July 2020 the EU launched a redesigned Europass as a free digital platform managed by the European Commission. Registered users create an online profile describing their education, work experience, language and digital skills, and can generate CVs and cover letters in multiple formats and languages, store documents in a personal library, and receive information on jobs and courses across the EU through links with EURES. The platform is available in 29 languages. While widely used — the Europass CV became one of the most recognisable CV formats in Europe — the format has also been criticised by recruiters as rigid and over-standardised.
