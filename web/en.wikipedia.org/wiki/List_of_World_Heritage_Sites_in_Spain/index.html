List of World Heritage Sites in Spain

The List of World Heritage Sites in Spain enumerates the properties inscribed by UNESCO in the Kingdom of Spain. With around fifty sites, Spain ranks among the countries with the most inscriptions in the world, spanning prehistoric cave art, Roman engineering, Islamic palaces, Gothic cathedrals, and the modernist works of Antoni Gaudí.

Overview

The first Spanish sites were inscribed in 1984. The list includes transnational entries such as the Pyrenees–Mont Perdu (shared with France) and the Mediterranean rock art of the Iberian Peninsula, which alone encompasses hundreds of individual shelters.
