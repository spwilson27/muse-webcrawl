Tomahawk (missile)

The Tomahawk is a long-range, all-weather, jet-powered, subsonic cruise missile used by the United States Navy, Royal Navy and Royal Australian Navy. Manufactured by RTX (Raytheon), it can be launched from ships and submarines to strike land targets with high precision.

Introduced in the 1980s and first used in combat in the 1991 Gulf War, Tomahawk variants have ranges exceeding 1,000 miles, fly at low altitude, and use GPS, inertial navigation, TERCOM and DSMAC guidance. Block V is the latest variant.
