Voice of the customer

Voice of the customer (VOC) is a term used in business and information technology to describe the in-depth process of capturing a customer's expectations, preferences and aversions. In Six Sigma and quality function deployment (QFD), VOC data provide the foundation for defining critical-to-quality characteristics of products and services.

Methodology

VOC is typically gathered through interviews, focus groups, surveys, ethnographic observation, warranty and complaint data, and analysis of customer feedback channels. The verbatim comments are organized into a hierarchy of needs — for example with affinity diagrams or the Kano model — and translated into measurable product requirements in the "house of quality" matrix of QFD.

Applications

Organizations use VOC programs in product design, service improvement, and customer experience management, increasingly combining structured surveys such as Net Promoter Score with unstructured text analytics of reviews, social media, and contact-centre recordings.
