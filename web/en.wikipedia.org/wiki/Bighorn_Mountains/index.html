Bighorn Mountains

LocationWyoming and Montana, United States
Highest peakCloud Peak (4,013 m)
LengthAbout 320 km

The Bighorn Mountains are a mountain range in northern Wyoming and southern Montana, a spur of the Rocky Mountains running roughly 320 km north from Wyoming's interior toward the Little Bighorn River.

Geography

The highest summit is Cloud Peak at 4,013 metres. Much of the range lies within the Bighorn National Forest, with the Cloud Peak Wilderness protecting the rugged core. Deep canyons, including Bighorn Canyon, cut the flanks.

History

The mountains are named for the bighorn sheep and were the homeland and hunting grounds of the Crow and Shoshone peoples. The Medicine Wheel, an ancient stone circle on Medicine Mountain, remains a sacred site. Today the range is used for recreation, grazing and timber.
