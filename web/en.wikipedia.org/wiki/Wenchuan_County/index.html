Wenchuan County

Wenchuan County (汶川县)
Province: Sichuan
Prefecture: Ngawa (Aba)
County seat: Weizhou
Population: around 80,000–100,000

Wenchuan County (Chinese: 汶川县; pinyin: Wènchuān Xiàn) is a county in the Ngawa Tibetan and Qiang Autonomous Prefecture of north-western Sichuan province, China. The county seat is Weizhou. It lies along the Min River in mountainous terrain on the eastern edge of the Tibetan Plateau and is home to significant Qiang and Tibetan populations.

2008 earthquake

Wenchuan was the epicentre of the Great Sichuan earthquake of 12 May 2008, a magnitude 7.9 (Chinese scale 8.0) event on the Longmenshan fault that killed nearly 70,000 people across the region and devastated towns in the county, including Yingxiu, which sat almost directly above the rupture. Landslides dammed rivers and destroyed roads, complicating rescue operations. The county became the focus of a massive national relief and reconstruction effort, with rebuilt towns, schools and an earthquake memorial museum at Yingxiu.

Attractions

The county includes part of the Wolong National Nature Reserve, famous for giant panda conservation, and Qiang villages with distinctive stone watchtowers. It is a gateway on the road from Chengdu to Jiuzhaigou and Huanglong.
