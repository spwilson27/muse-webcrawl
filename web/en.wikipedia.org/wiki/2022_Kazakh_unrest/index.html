2022 Kazakh unrest

Date: 2–11 January 2022

Location: Kazakhstan, especially Almaty and Zhanaozen

Outcome: Protests suppressed; CSTO intervention; government reshuffle

The 2022 Kazakh unrest began on 2 January 2022 after a sharp rise in liquefied gas prices in the oil town of Zhanaozen, spreading nationwide into protests over inequality, corruption and the influence of former president Nursultan Nazarbayev.

Escalation

In Almaty, protesters stormed government buildings and the airport, and armed clashes followed. President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev — see President of Kazakhstan — declared a state of emergency, dismissed the government, and issued a 'shoot to kill without warning' order.

CSTO intervention and toll

At Tokayev's request, the Russia-led Collective Security Treaty Organization deployed troops for the first time, guarding infrastructure before withdrawing by mid-January. Official figures reported at least 238 people killed and thousands detained, with widespread reports of torture in custody.

Aftermath

Tokayev sidelined Nazarbayev's family and allies, stripped his titles, and held a 2022 constitutional referendum reducing presidential terms, while critics questioned how far reforms went.
