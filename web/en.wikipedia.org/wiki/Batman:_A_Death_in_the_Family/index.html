"A Death in the Family" is a four-issue Batman comic book storyline published by DC Comics in Batman #426–429 (1988–89), written by Jim Starlin and drawn by Jim Aparo. It is famous for the death of Jason Todd, the second Robin, at the hands of the Joker — an outcome decided by readers in a telephone vote.

Plot and the vote

In the story, Jason Todd travels to the Middle East searching for his mother and is betrayed to the Joker, who beats him with a crowbar and leaves him in a warehouse explosion. DC famously invited readers to call a 1-900 number to vote on whether Todd would live or die; by a margin of just 72 votes (5,343 to 5,271), readers chose death. Batman finds his partner's body in the rubble, in one of the most reproduced images in comics.

Legacy

The storyline shaped Batman's darker modern characterisation and the character of Jason Todd, who was resurrected in 2005 as the Red Hood in "Under the Hood". An interactive animated film adaptation, Batman: Death in the Family, was released in 2020.
