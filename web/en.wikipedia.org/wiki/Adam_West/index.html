Adam West (born William West Anderson; September 19, 1928 – June 9, 2017) was an American actor. He portrayed Batman in the 1960s ABC series Batman and its 1966 theatrical feature film, delivering the role with a straight-faced earnestness amid the show's camp style that made him a pop-culture icon. Decades later he won a new audience voicing Mayor Adam West, a fictionalised, eccentric version of himself, on the animated series Family Guy.

Early life

West was born in Walla Walla, Washington, the son of a farmer and an opera singer and concert pianist. He served in the United States Army, working as an announcer for the American Forces Network, and after college worked in radio and television in Hawaii, where he hosted a local children's show, before moving to Hollywood.

Career

After supporting roles in films and guest spots on television westerns and detective shows, West was cast as Bruce Wayne / Batman in 1966. The series was a ratings phenomenon but lasted only three seasons; typecasting then limited his leading roles for years, and he worked steadily in voice acting, B-movies, and personal appearances. His career revived with animation: he voiced Batman again in several productions, played recurring characters on shows such as The Fairly OddParents, and from 2000 until his death voiced Quahog's deranged Mayor Adam West on Family Guy. He reprised Batman in the animated films Batman: Return of the Caped Crusaders (2016) and Batman vs. Two-Face (2017).

Death and legacy

West died in Los Angeles on June 9, 2017, following a short battle with leukemia, aged 88. Los Angeles projected the Bat-Signal onto City Hall in his honour. His deadpan Batman remains a defining interpretation of the character, celebrated for its warmth and gentle self-parody.
