Mars sample-return mission

A Mars sample-return mission is a proposed spacecraft mission to collect rock and dust samples from Mars and return them to Earth for detailed laboratory analysis. Such analysis could answer questions about Mars's geological history and whether it ever harboured life, using instruments too large or sensitive to fly to Mars.

Concepts

Plans have generally involved several elements: a rover to collect and cache samples (a role fulfilled from 2021 by NASA's Perseverance), a lander carrying a small rocket — a Mars ascent vehicle — to launch the samples into orbit, and an orbiter to capture them and carry them back to Earth.

Status

The joint NASA–ESA campaign was extensively studied, but cost growth and schedule delays led to restructuring in the mid-2020s, with alternative architectures under review. China has also announced a sample-return mission, Tianwen-3, targeted for launch around 2028.
