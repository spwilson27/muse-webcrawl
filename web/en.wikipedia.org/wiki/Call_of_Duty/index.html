Call of Duty

Call of Duty is a first-person shooter video game franchise published by Activision. Starting in 2003, it first focused on games set in World War II. Over time, the series has seen games set in the midst of the Cold War, futuristic worlds, and outer space. The games were first developed by Infinity Ward, then also by Treyarch and Sledgehammer Games.

GenreFirst-person shooter

History

The first Call of Duty (2003) and its expansion United Offensive were set in World War II and drew heavily on the success of the Medal of Honor series. Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare (2007) moved the series to a contemporary setting and was a landmark success, establishing the formula of cinematic campaigns and highly popular online multiplayer. The Black Ops sub-series by Treyarch explored the Cold War and near-future settings, while the free-to-play battle royale title Call of Duty: Warzone launched in 2020.

Sales and impact

By the early 2020s the series had sold more than 400 million copies, making it one of the best-selling video game franchises of all time and generating over $30 billion in revenue. It has been credited with popularizing the modern military shooter and shaping online console multiplayer.
