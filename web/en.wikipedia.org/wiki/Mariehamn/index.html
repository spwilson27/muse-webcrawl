Mariehamn

Mariehamn (Finnish: Maarianhamina) is the capital of Åland, an autonomous and demilitarised Swedish-speaking region of Finland. Founded in 1861 and named after Maria Alexandrovna, consort of Tsar Alexander II, it is Åland's only town and seat of its government and parliament.

Economy and sights

About 11,000 people live in Mariehamn, roughly 40 percent of Åland's population. Shipping and tourism dominate the economy; the port hosts ferries to Sweden and mainland Finland and the museum ship Pommern, a four-masted barque, beside the Åland Maritime Museum. The town is known for its linden-lined avenues.
