Orange UK

Orange UK
Industry: Telecommunications
Launched: 28 April 1994
Fate: Merged into EE (2010–2015)

Orange UK was a mobile network operator and internet service provider in the United Kingdom, launched on 28 April 1994. Its striking minimal branding, the slogan "The future's bright, the future's Orange", and innovative tariffs quickly made it one of Britain's best-known mobile brands.

History

The network was originally owned by Hutchison Whampoa and floated on the stock market in 1996. After a takeover battle, it was acquired by France Télécom in 2000 and became the international Orange brand's flagship. Orange UK was an early leader in per-second billing and bundled minutes, and later offered home broadband services.

Merger into EE

In 2010 Orange UK merged with T-Mobile UK to form a joint venture, EE, which launched Britain's first 4G network in 2012. The Orange brand was gradually retired from the UK market, with remaining customers migrated to EE plans by 2015.
