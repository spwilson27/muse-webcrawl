Eastgate Systems

Eastgate Systems is a software and publishing company based in Watertown, Massachusetts, founded in 1982 by Mark Bernstein. It is a pioneer of hypertext publishing and is best known as the publisher of pioneering works of hypertext fiction and as the developer of the Storyspace and Tinderbox software systems.

Hypertext publishing

In the late 1980s and 1990s Eastgate published many of the foundational works of electronic literature on floppy disk and CD-ROM, including Michael Joyce's afternoon, a story (1990), often considered the first hypertext novel; Stuart Moulthrop's Victory Garden; and Shelley Jackson's Patchwork Girl (1995). The company also published hypertext nonfiction and poetry, and for many years maintained the Hypertext Reading Room, an influential resource for the field.

Software

Storyspace, developed by Jay David Bolter, Michael Joyce and John B. Smith in the 1980s and distributed by Eastgate, was one of the first hypertext writing environments and the tool used to create much early hypertext literature. Tinderbox, released in 2002, is a personal content assistant for macOS used for note-taking, mapping ideas and information management; its companion tool Storyspace 3 and the hypertext narrative tool Twine trace intellectual lineage to Eastgate's work, as Twine's creator was influenced by the Storyspace tradition.
