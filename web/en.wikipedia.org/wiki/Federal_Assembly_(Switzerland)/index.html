Federal Assembly (Switzerland)

The Federal Assembly (German: Bundesversammlung) is the federal bicameral legislature of Switzerland. It comprises the National Council (200 members) and the Council of States (46 members), and meets in the Federal Palace in Bern.

Composition

The National Council represents the people, with seats apportioned among cantons by population; the Council of States represents the cantons, with two members per canton and one per half-canton. Members serve four-year terms.

Powers

The Assembly elects the seven members of the Federal Council (the federal executive), the Federal Chancellor, and judges of the Federal Supreme Court. Sitting jointly as the United Federal Assembly, it elects the executive and decides conflicts of jurisdiction. It is, subject to the rights of the people and cantons, the supreme authority of the Confederation.
