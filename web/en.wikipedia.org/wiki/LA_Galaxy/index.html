The LA Galaxy are an American professional soccer club based in the Los Angeles metropolitan area, competing in Major League Soccer (MLS) as a member of the Western Conference. One of the league's ten charter members, the Galaxy are the most successful club in MLS history by MLS Cup titles.

History

The Galaxy played their early seasons at the Rose Bowl before moving to their soccer-specific stadium in Carson in 2003. The signing of David Beckham in 2007 transformed the club's global profile and MLS's designated-player economics. Under coach Bruce Arena, the team won MLS Cups in 2011, 2012 and 2014 with stars Landon Donovan and Robbie Keane. The club won a record sixth MLS Cup in 2024.

Rivalries

The Galaxy's main rivals are Los Angeles FC in the derby known as El Tráfico (begun 2018), and historically the San Jose Earthquakes in the California Clásico.
