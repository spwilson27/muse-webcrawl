Suits is an American legal drama television series created by Aaron Korsh. It premiered on USA Network on 23 June 2011 and ran for nine seasons, concluding on 25 September 2019. The series is set at a fictional New York City corporate law firm and follows Mike Ross (Patrick J. Adams), a college dropout with an eidetic memory who never attended law school, as he talks his way into a job as an associate working for Harvey Specter (Gabriel Macht), one of the city's best closers. Together they handle cases while concealing Mike's lack of qualifications.

Premise

The firm — successively named Pearson Hardman, Pearson, Pearson Darby, Pearson Specter, Pearson Specter Litt and finally Zane Specter Litt Wheeler Williams — only hires Harvard Law graduates. Mike's secret, known to a small circle, is a constant source of jeopardy throughout the series. Major characters include managing partner Jessica Pearson (Gina Torres), eccentric partner Louis Litt (Rick Hoffman), paralegal and later lawyer Rachel Zane (Meghan Markle), and Harvey's formidable executive assistant Donna Paulsen (Sarah Rafferty).

Cast and characters

Gabriel Macht stars as Harvey Specter and Patrick J. Adams as Mike Ross. Adams left the series after season seven, as did Markle; Gina Torres departed after season six and headlined the spin-off Pearson (2019). Katherine Heigl, Dulé Hill and Amanda Schull joined the main cast for the final two seasons.

Reception and legacy

Suits was a ratings success for USA Network and received praise for its dialogue and the chemistry of its leads. After being added to streaming services in 2023, it experienced an enormous resurgence in popularity, becoming one of the most-streamed acquired series in the United States and prompting the development of a new spin-off, Suits LA, which premiered in 2025.
