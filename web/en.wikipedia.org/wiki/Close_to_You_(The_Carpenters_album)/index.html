Close to You is the second studio album by American duo the Carpenters, released on August 19, 1970, by A&M Records. The album produced two of the duo's signature hits — "(They Long to Be) Close to You" and "We've Only Just Begun" — and established Karen and Richard Carpenter as leading soft-pop artists of the 1970s.

Background

The title track, a Burt Bacharach and Hal David song previously recorded by Richard Chamberlain and Dionne Warwick, was arranged by Richard Carpenter with Karen's close-miked contralto at its center; released as a single it spent four weeks at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 in the summer of 1970 and won the Grammy for Best Contemporary Performance by a Duo, Group or Chorus, while the Carpenters won Best New Artist. "We've Only Just Begun", written by Paul Williams and Roger Nichols from a bank commercial jingle, reached No. 2 and became a wedding standard.

Track listing

The album includes "We've Only Just Begun", "Love Is Surrender", "Maybe It's You", "Reason to Believe", "(They Long to Be) Close to You", "Help", "Mr. Guder", "I Kept on Loving You", "Another Song", and "Crescent Noon", mixing originals with covers of the Beatles and Tim Hardin. It peaked at No. 2 on the Billboard 200 and was certified double platinum in the United States.
