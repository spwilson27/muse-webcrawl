Reflux (disambiguation)

Image placeholder: a reflux condenser, one sense of the term.

Reflux may refer to several distinct concepts, most of them involving a backward or return flow of a fluid.

Chemistry and engineering

  • Reflux, a laboratory and industrial technique in which a solvent is boiled and its vapour condensed and returned to the vessel, allowing reactions to be heated for long periods without loss of solvent.
  • Reflux in distillation, where part of the condensed overhead liquid is returned to the column to improve separation.

Medicine

  • Gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD), the backflow of stomach contents into the oesophagus, commonly called acid reflux.
  • Vesicoureteral reflux, the abnormal backward flow of urine from the bladder toward the kidneys.
  • Venous reflux, incompetence of venous valves causing backward blood flow in the legs.
