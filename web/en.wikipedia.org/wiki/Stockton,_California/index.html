Stockton, California

Stockton is a city in and the county seat of San Joaquin County, California, United States. Located on the San Joaquin River in the northern San Joaquin Valley, it is the most populous city in the county, with a population of roughly 320,000 at the 2020 census. Stockton was founded during the California Gold Rush and is home to the Port of Stockton, the farthest inland deep-water port on the West Coast of the United States.

History

The area was inhabited for millennia by Yokuts-speaking peoples. The modern city was founded by Captain Charles Maria Weber in 1849 on the Rancho Campo de los Franceses land grant, and was named after Commodore Robert F. Stockton. As the gateway to the southern gold mines, Stockton boomed as a supply and river port; it was incorporated in 1850 and became one of California's earliest cities. In the later nineteenth century it became a centre of agriculture and manufacturing, notably of farm machinery and the Holt (later Caterpillar) tractor.

Economy and the bankruptcy

Stockton's economy rests on agriculture, logistics and warehousing, telecommunications, and its port, through which bulk commodities such as cement, steel, and grain move. The city gained national attention in 2012 when it filed for Chapter 9 bankruptcy — then the largest municipal bankruptcy in U.S. history — after the housing-market collapse devastated revenues that had supported generous pension commitments and downtown redevelopment. Stockton exited bankruptcy in 2015. Under Mayor Michael Tubbs the city also ran one of the first modern guaranteed-income experiments in the United States, the Stockton Economic Empowerment Demonstration (SEED), beginning in 2019.

Geography and demographics

Stockton sits amid the canals and waterways of the Sacramento–San Joaquin River Delta, with hundreds of miles of navigable channels nearby. The climate is Mediterranean, with hot dry summers and mild, foggy winters. The city is one of the most ethnically diverse in the United States, with large Latino, Filipino (centred on the historic Little Manila district), African American, Hmong, Cambodian, and Sikh communities. It is home to the University of the Pacific, California's oldest chartered university, founded in 1851.
