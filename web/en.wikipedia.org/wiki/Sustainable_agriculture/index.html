Sustainable agriculture
Practices: crop rotation, cover crops, agroforestry, integrated pest management

Sustainable agriculture is farming in sustainable ways meeting society's present food needs without compromising the ability of future generations to meet theirs, based on ecosystem services, soil health and social equity.

Practices

Key methods include crop rotation and diversity, cover cropping, reduced tillage, compost and manure use, integrated pest management, agroforestry, polyculture, managed grazing, and efficient irrigation such as drip systems. Related approaches include organic farming, permaculture, regenerative agriculture and agroecology.

Challenges

Industrial agriculture can deplete soil, pollute water with fertiliser runoff, emit greenhouse gases and reduce biodiversity. Sustainable methods aim to build soil organic matter, sequester carbon and cut inputs, though debates continue over yield gaps and land use.
