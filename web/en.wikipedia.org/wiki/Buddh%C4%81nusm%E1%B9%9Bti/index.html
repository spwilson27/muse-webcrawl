Buddhānusmṛti

Sanskritबुद्धानुस्मृति (buddhānusmṛti)
PaliBuddhānussati
Meaning"Recollection of the Buddha"
TypeBuddhist meditation practice
Related practicesNembutsu (nianfo); Pure Land Buddhism

Buddhānusmṛti (Sanskrit; Pali: buddhānussati), literally "recollection of the Buddha", is a foundational Buddhist meditation practice in which the practitioner recollected the qualities of the Buddha, traditionally by reciting a formula such as the Pali Itipi so passage, which praises the Buddha as worthy, fully awakened, perfect in knowledge and conduct, and teacher of gods and humans.

In early Buddhism

Buddhānussati is the first of the six (or ten) recollections (anussati) in Theravāda Buddhism, alongside recollection of the Dhamma, the Saṅgha, virtue, generosity and the devas. It is said to gladden the mind, dispel fear and lead to access concentration, and the Buddha recommended it even as a layperson's practice for a favourable rebirth.

In Mahāyāna

In Mahāyāna traditions the practice expanded into visualisation of the Buddha and recitation of his name, becoming central to Pure Land Buddhism, where recitation of Amitābha's name (Chinese nianfo, Japanese nembutsu) is the principal path to rebirth in the Pure Land.
