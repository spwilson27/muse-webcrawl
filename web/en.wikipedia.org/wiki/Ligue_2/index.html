Ligue 2 (French pronunciation: [liɡ dø]), also known as Ligue 2 BKT for sponsorship reasons, is a French professional football league. The league serves as the second division of French football and is part of the Ligue de Football Professionnel (LFP), the other being Ligue 1, the country's top football division. Contested by 18 clubs, it operates on a system of promotion and relegation with both Ligue 1 and the third division Championnat National.

History

The second division was created in 1933, one year after the creation of the first division, under the name Division 2. For its first season it comprised 23 clubs drawn partly from teams relegated from Division 1 and partly from newly professional sides. The league was renamed Ligue 2 in 2002 when the French league system was rebranded. For much of its history the division fluctuated between 18 and 20 clubs and between single-group and two-group formats; from the 2024–25 season it returned to 18 clubs.

Format

Each club plays the others twice, home and away, over a season running from August to May. At the end of the season the top two clubs are promoted to Ligue 1, while a play-off involving the next three clubs and a Ligue 1 side decides a further promotion place. The bottom clubs are relegated to the Championnat National, with a play-off for the side finishing just above the automatic relegation places. Le Havre and Metz are among the most successful clubs in the division's history.
