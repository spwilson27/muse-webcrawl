Super Bowl XXXVIII was an American football game between the American Football Conference champion New England Patriots and the National Football Conference champion Carolina Panthers to decide the champion of the National Football League for the 2003 season. The Patriots defeated the Panthers by the score of 32–29 at Reliant Stadium in Houston, Texas, on February 1, 2004.

The game

After a scoreless first 27 minutes — the longest scoreless start in Super Bowl history — the teams exchanged two touchdowns each before halftime. The fourth quarter was a shootout: the teams combined for 37 points, a Super Bowl record for a single quarter, with quarterbacks Tom Brady and Jake Delhomme trading the lead. Delhomme's 85-yard touchdown pass to Muhsin Muhammad was the longest play from scrimmage in Super Bowl history at the time. With the game tied 29–29, Brady drove the Patriots 37 yards in the final minute, and Adam Vinatieri kicked a 41-yard field goal with four seconds remaining — his second Super Bowl-winning kick in three years.

Halftime show controversy

The game's halftime show, headlined by Janet Jackson and Justin Timberlake, ended with a brief exposure of Jackson's breast during their duet — an incident attributed to a "wardrobe malfunction" that triggered a major public controversy, a record FCC fine against CBS (later voided), tighter broadcast indecency enforcement, and a lasting shift in how halftime shows were produced and delayed. The incident is also credited with spurring the creation of YouTube, whose founders cited frustration at being unable to find the clip online.
