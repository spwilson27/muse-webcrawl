Scottish independence (Scottish Gaelic: Neo-eisimeileachd na h-Alba; Scots: Scots unthirldom) is the political movement for Scotland to become a sovereign state, independent from the United Kingdom. Scotland was an independent kingdom until the Acts of Union 1707 united it with England to form Great Britain. The modern independence movement grew through the 20th century and is principally represented by the Scottish National Party (SNP).

Background

Scotland retained its own legal system, established church and education system after 1707. Calls for "home rule" were debated from the late 19th century. A referendum in 1979 narrowly favoured a devolved assembly but failed a turnout threshold; in 1997 a referendum passed decisively, and the Scottish Parliament was established in 1999 with devolved powers.

2014 referendum

Following the SNP's majority win in the 2011 Scottish election, the Edinburgh Agreement (2012) between the UK and Scottish governments enabled a legal referendum. On 18 September 2014 voters were asked "Should Scotland be an independent country?" The result was 55.3% No and 44.7% Yes on a turnout of 84.6%, the highest in UK electoral history. Key campaign issues included currency, EU membership, North Sea oil revenues and public spending.

Since 2014

The independence debate continued after the 2016 Brexit referendum, in which Scotland voted 62% to remain in the EU, prompting the SNP to seek a second independence referendum. The UK government refused consent, and in November 2022 the UK Supreme Court ruled that the Scottish Parliament cannot legislate for an independence referendum without Westminster's authority. Support for independence in polls has generally hovered around 45–50% since 2014.

Arguments

Supporters argue independence would give Scotland full control of its economy, resources and foreign policy and allow it to rejoin the EU. Opponents argue Scotland benefits from the UK's economic scale, shared currency, pensions and defence, and that independence would create border and trade frictions with Scotland's largest market.
