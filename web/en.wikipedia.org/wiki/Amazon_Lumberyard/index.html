Amazon Lumberyard

Amazon Lumberyard was a freeware cross-platform game engine developed by Amazon and based on technology licensed from CryEngine. Announced in 2016, it was integrated with Amazon Web Services and Twitch and was aimed at developers building connected, cloud-backed games.

Features

Lumberyard provided a visual editor, asset pipeline, animation and physics tools, networking, and the component entity system inherited in part from Crytek's technology. It supported development for personal computers and consoles of its period, while encouraging use of AWS services such as GameLift for multiplayer hosting.

Transition to open source

In 2021 Amazon contributed a reworked version of the engine to the Linux Foundation as Open 3D Engine (O3DE), under an open-source licence. Lumberyard itself was effectively superseded by O3DE, though its name remains associated with Amazon's first major attempt to enter game-engine development.
