Sareyn (Persian: سرعین; also romanised as Sar‘eyn, and known as Sarain) is a city in northwestern Iran, in Ardabil Province, and the capital of Sareyn County. It lies at the foot of the Sabalan mountains.

Hot springs

Sareyn is famous throughout Iran for its numerous mineral hot springs, which are believed to have therapeutic properties for rheumatic and joint complaints. The springs, warmed by the region's volcanic geology associated with Mount Sabalan, draw large numbers of domestic tourists each summer. Several modern spa complexes and hotels serve visitors.

Geography and climate

The city sits at an elevation of roughly 1,650 metres above sea level, giving it cool summers that contrast with the heat of Iran's lowlands, and cold, snowy winters. Nearby attractions include the Alvares ski resort on the slopes of Sabalan.

Economy

Tourism centred on the hot springs dominates the local economy, supplemented by agriculture and animal husbandry in the surrounding villages.
