Walt Disney Television Animation

Walt Disney Television Animation (DTVA) is an American animation production company, the television animation division of Disney Branded Television, a unit of the Walt Disney Company. Founded in 1984, it produces animated series primarily for Disney Channel, Disney Junior, Disney XD and Disney+.

History

The studio was established in 1984 under president Gary Krisel as the Walt Disney Pictures Television Animation Group. Its first series, The Wuzzles and Disney's Adventures of the Gummi Bears, premiered in 1985. The studio found major success with the syndicated afternoon block The Disney Afternoon (1990), featuring DuckTales, Chip 'n Dale: Rescue Rangers, TaleSpin and Darkwing Duck. Later hits include Recess, Kim Possible, Phineas and Ferb, Gravity Falls, Star vs. the Forces of Evil, DuckTales (2017), Amphibia and The Owl House.

Production

The studio is based in Glendale, California. Many series have historically been animated by overseas studios in Japan, South Korea, the Philippines and elsewhere, with creative supervision from California. DTVA series have won numerous Emmy and Annie Awards and are a major source of original programming for Disney's television and streaming platforms.
