The University of Wisconsin–Superior (UW–Superior or UWS) is a public liberal arts university in Superior, Wisconsin, United States. Founded in 1893 as a teacher training school, it is part of the University of Wisconsin System and enrolls roughly 2,500 students.

History

The school opened in 1893 as Superior Normal School to train teachers for northern Wisconsin. It became Superior State Teachers College, then Wisconsin State University–Superior, and joined the University of Wisconsin System in 1971. In 2007 it was designated Wisconsin's public liberal arts college.

Academics

UW–Superior offers more than 40 undergraduate majors and several graduate programs, with notable strengths in transportation and logistics management, aquatic biology, and education. The university is also home to the Lake Superior Research Institute and the Transportation and Logistics Research Center.

Campus

The main campus occupies about 125 acres in Superior, near the western tip of Lake Superior. Old Main, built in 1915, is the oldest building on campus. The university also operates the Lake Superior National Estuarine Research Reserve.
