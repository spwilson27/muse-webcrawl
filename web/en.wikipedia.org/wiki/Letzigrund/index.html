Letzigrund

The Letzigrund is a stadium in Zurich, Switzerland, and the home ground of the football clubs FC Zürich and Grasshopper Club Zürich. It is also the venue of Weltklasse Zürich, one of the world's premier annual track and field meetings, part of the Diamond League. The current stadium, opened in 2007 on the site of the original 1925 ground, has a capacity of roughly 26,000 for football.

History

The original Letzigrund opened in 1925 and was built and owned for many years by FC Zürich. It hosted matches during the 1954 FIFA World Cup. The old stadium was demolished in 2006 and replaced by a new arena built by the City of Zurich, designed with a low, partially sunken bowl and a distinctive ring of floodlight masts and ramps. The new Letzigrund opened in August 2007 and was one of the Swiss venues for UEFA Euro 2008, hosting three group matches.

Events

Weltklasse Zürich has been held at the Letzigrund since 1928 and is famous for the number of world records set on its track; the meeting traditionally closes the Diamond League season. The stadium hosted the 2014 European Athletics Championships. Concerts and other sporting events, including rugby and American football, are also staged there. Because both Zurich football clubs share the ground while a dedicated football stadium project has faced repeated delays, the Letzigrund remains the city's principal sports venue.
