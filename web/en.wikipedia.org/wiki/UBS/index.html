UBS

UBS Group AG is a Swiss multinational investment bank and financial services company founded in 1862, with co-headquarters in Zurich and Basel. It is the largest Swiss banking institution and one of the largest private banks in the world, known above all for its global wealth management business. The name UBS derives from the Union Bank of Switzerland, one of its predecessor firms, though it is no longer officially an acronym.

History

UBS in its modern form was created in 1998 by the merger of the Union Bank of Switzerland and the Swiss Bank Corporation, then the two largest banks in Switzerland. During the 2007–2008 financial crisis the bank suffered massive losses on U.S. subprime mortgage securities and was rescued with support from the Swiss government and the Swiss National Bank; it also became embroiled in a U.S. tax evasion investigation, paying a $780 million settlement in 2009 and disclosing thousands of client accounts.

Under subsequent leadership the bank refocused on wealth management and reduced the size of its investment bank. Sergio Ermotti served as CEO from 2011 to 2020 and returned to the role in 2023.

Credit Suisse takeover

In March 2023, after a collapse in confidence in Credit Suisse, Switzerland's second-largest bank, Swiss authorities brokered an emergency takeover of Credit Suisse by UBS for about CHF 3 billion. The acquisition, completed in June 2023, created a banking giant with over $5 trillion in invested assets and made UBS the sole remaining Swiss global systemically important bank.

Business

UBS operates through Global Wealth Management, Personal & Corporate Banking (in Switzerland), Asset Management, and the Investment Bank. It manages the largest pool of private wealth in the world and maintains offices in more than 50 countries, with major presences in the Americas, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. Client confidentiality rooted in Swiss banking law remains a hallmark of its private banking franchise, though automatic exchange of tax information has narrowed the scope of bank secrecy.
