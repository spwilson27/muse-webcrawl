Innsbruck

Country: Austria
State: Tyrol
Population: approx. 130,000
Elevation: 574 m

Innsbruck is the capital of the Austrian federal state of Tyrol and the fifth-largest city in Austria. It lies in the Inn valley at the junction with the Wipp valley, which leads to the Brenner Pass, surrounded by high Alpine peaks. Its name means "bridge over the Inn".

History

Innsbruck became a city in the 13th century and an important trading post on the route between Italy and Germany. Under Emperor Maximilian I it flourished as an imperial residence; the Golden Roof (Goldenes Dachl), a balcony decorated with gilded copper tiles, remains its most famous landmark.

Sport

The city hosted the Winter Olympic Games in 1964 and 1976, and the first Winter Youth Olympic Games in 2012. Skiing, ski jumping at Bergisel, and other winter sports are central to its identity, and it is a popular base for hiking and mountaineering in the Nordkette and surrounding ranges.

Education

The University of Innsbruck, founded in 1669, is one of Austria's major universities, with strengths in physics and mountain research.
