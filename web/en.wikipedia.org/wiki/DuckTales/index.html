DuckTales

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Scrooge McDuck and his grandnephews, the centre of the DuckTales franchise

DuckTales is an American animated television series produced by Walt Disney Television Animation. The original series aired in syndication from September 18, 1987, to November 28, 1990, comprising 100 episodes across four seasons. It follows the globe-trotting adventures of Scrooge McDuck, the richest duck in the world, together with his grandnephews Huey, Dewey and Louie and a rotating cast including pilot Launchpad McQuack, inventor Gyro Gearloose, and the housekeeper Mrs. Beakley and her granddaughter Webby.

The series was based largely on Carl Barks's Uncle Scrooge comic books, adapting several of his classic treasure-hunting stories, though Barks's central character Donald Duck was largely replaced by Launchpad so the nephews could live with Scrooge. Recurring villains included the Beagle Boys, Magica De Spell, and the rival billionaire Flintheart Glomgold. Its theme song, written by Mark Mueller, became one of the most recognisable of the era.

Legacy and reboot

DuckTales helped anchor The Disney Afternoon programming block and spun off video games — notably Capcom's 1989 NES platformer — and the theatrical film DuckTales the Movie: Treasure of the Lost Lamp (1990). A reboot series premiered on Disney XD in 2017, running for three seasons (2017–2021) and giving greater roles to Webby, Mrs. Beakley and Donald Duck; it concluded with the series finale "The Last Adventure!" in March 2021.
