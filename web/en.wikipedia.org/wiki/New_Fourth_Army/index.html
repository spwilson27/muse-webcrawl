New Fourth Army

The New Fourth Army (simplified Chinese: 新四军; traditional Chinese: 新四軍) was a unit of the National Revolutionary Army of the Republic of China, nominally under Nationalist command but effectively controlled by the Chinese Communist Party. It was established in 1937–1938 during the Second United Front against Japan, from Communist guerrilla forces left behind in southeastern China after the Long March.

History

Headquartered initially at Hankou and later active in the lower Yangtze region south of the river, the New Fourth Army was commanded by Ye Ting, with Xiang Ying as deputy and Chen Yi a senior commander. It grew from about 10,000 men in 1938 to roughly 90,000 by 1940, conducting guerrilla operations against Japanese forces in Jiangsu, Anhui, and neighboring provinces.

In January 1941, Nationalist forces ambushed a New Fourth Army column in southern Anhui—the New Fourth Army Incident—which shattered the United Front. The Communists rebuilt the army under Chen Yi and Liu Shaoqi, and it expanded into a major force in central China by 1945. Its units were later reorganized into the People's Liberation Army.
