Haar measure

In mathematical analysis, the Haar measure assigns an "invariant volume" to subsets of locally compact topological groups, allowing an integral to be defined over the group. Introduced by Alfréd Haar in 1933, it is unique up to a positive constant factor.

Properties

A left Haar measure is invariant under left multiplication by group elements. On the real numbers it coincides with Lebesgue measure; on general groups it underlies harmonic analysis and representation theory. Groups for which left and right Haar measures coincide are called unimodular.
