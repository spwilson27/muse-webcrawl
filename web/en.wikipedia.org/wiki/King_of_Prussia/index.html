King of Prussia

King of Prussia is a census-designated place in Upper Merion Township, Montgomery County, Pennsylvania, United States. It takes its name from the King of Prussia Inn, an 18th-century tavern named in honour of Frederick the Great of Prussia.

Economy and landmarks

The community is a major edge city of Philadelphia, centred on the King of Prussia mall, one of the largest shopping malls in the United States. It adjoins Valley Forge National Historical Park, site of the Continental Army's 1777–78 winter encampment.
