Suez Canal University

Established: 1976
Type: Public university
Location: Ismailia, Egypt
Motto: "Knowledge, Faith, and Work" (approx.)

Suez Canal University is an Egyptian public university serving the Suez Canal region, with its main campus in Ismailia and facilities in the governorates of Suez, Port Said, and North Sinai. It began teaching in 1977, having been established by presidential decree in 1976.

History and development

The university was founded to provide higher education to the canal cities and the Sinai, which had been heavily affected by the wars of 1967–1973, and to support the region's reconstruction and development. Initially comprising a small number of faculties in Ismailia, it expanded to include faculties of medicine, engineering, science, agriculture, commerce, education, veterinary medicine, pharmacy, nursing, dentistry, computing and informatics, and others across its campuses. Branch campuses in Suez and Port Said later became independent universities (Suez University and Port Said University, both separated around 2010–2012), as did the Arish branch, which became Arish University.

Academics

Suez Canal University is known for community-oriented and problem-based medical education; its Faculty of Medicine, founded in the late 1970s, was among the first in Egypt to adopt an integrated, community-based curriculum, in cooperation with international networks of health-professions education. The university operates teaching hospitals in Ismailia, research centres addressing canal-region agriculture, fisheries, and environmental questions, and postgraduate institutes including an Institute of Biotechnology and an African studies institute.
