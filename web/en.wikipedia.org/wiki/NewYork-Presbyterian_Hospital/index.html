NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital (abbreviated as NYP) is a nonprofit academic medical center in New York City. It was formed in 1998 through the merger of The New York Hospital and The Presbyterian Hospital, and it is affiliated with two Ivy League medical schools: Weill Cornell Medicine and the Columbia University Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons. It is consistently ranked among the top hospitals in the United States.

History

The hospital's two parent institutions were among the oldest in the United States. The New York Hospital, founded in 1771 by a royal charter from King George III, was the second hospital established in New York City. The Presbyterian Hospital was founded in 1868 by philanthropist James Lenox. On January 1, 1998, the two institutions merged to form NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital, creating one of the largest academic medical centers in the country.

Campuses

NewYork-Presbyterian/Weill Cornell Medical Center

Located on the Upper East Side of Manhattan, this campus includes the David H. Koch Center, which opened in 2018, and the Alexandra Cohen Hospital for Women and Newborns, which opened in 2020.

NewYork-Presbyterian/Columbia University Irving Medical Center

Located in Washington Heights in northern Manhattan, this campus is home to the Milstein Hospital Building and the Morgan Stanley Children's Hospital, the only dedicated children's hospital in Manhattan.

Regional hospitals

The health system also includes regional campuses such as NewYork-Presbyterian Westchester, Lower Manhattan Hospital, Brooklyn Methodist Hospital, Queens, Hudson Valley Hospital, Lawrence Hospital, and the Allen Hospital.

Rankings and recognition

NewYork-Presbyterian is regularly named to the U.S. News & World Report Best Hospitals Honor Roll and is nationally ranked in numerous adult and pediatric specialties, including cardiology and heart surgery, neurology and neurosurgery, psychiatry, and rheumatology.
