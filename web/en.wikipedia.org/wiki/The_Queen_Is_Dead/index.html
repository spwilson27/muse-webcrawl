The Queen Is Dead

Artist: The Smiths
Released: 16 June 1986
Label: Rough Trade

The Queen Is Dead is the third studio album by the English rock band The Smiths, released in June 1986 on Rough Trade Records. It is widely regarded as the band's finest work and one of the greatest albums of all time, regularly placing near the top of critics' lists.

Background and recording

Written by Morrissey and Johnny Marr and produced by them with engineer Stephen Street, the album was recorded in 1985 while the band was in dispute with its label. Its cover features actor Alain Delon from the 1964 film L'Insoumis.

Reception and legacy

The album peaked at number two in the UK. Its blend of literate, wry lyricism and jangling guitar pop — epitomized by "There Is a Light That Never Goes Out" — profoundly influenced British indie music. In 2013 NME named it the greatest album of all time.
