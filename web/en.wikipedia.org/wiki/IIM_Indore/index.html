IIM Indore

Indian Institute of Management Indore

The IIM Indore campus

The Indian Institute of Management Indore (IIM Indore or IIM-I) is a public business school in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India. Established in 1996, it is one of the twenty Indian Institutes of Management and is designated an institute of national importance. Its 193-acre hilltop campus sits atop Prabandh Shikhar, overlooking the city.

Programmes

IIM Indore offers the two-year Post Graduate Programme in Management (its flagship MBA-equivalent), a five-year Integrated Programme in Management (IPM) — the first of its kind among IIMs, admitting students directly after school — executive and doctoral programmes, and certificate courses. Admission to the flagship programme is through the Common Admission Test (CAT).

Accreditations and rankings

The institute holds the "triple crown" of EQUIS, AACSB and AMBA accreditations, and regularly places among the top business schools in India's NIRF management rankings.
