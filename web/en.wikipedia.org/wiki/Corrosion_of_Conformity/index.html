Corrosion of Conformity

Corrosion of Conformity (also known as COC) is an American heavy metal band from Raleigh, North Carolina, formed in 1982. The band has undergone multiple line-up changes, with guitarist Woody Weatherman as the sole constant member until recently. They started as a hardcore punk band, later moving to a slower, groove-laden metal sound incorporating elements of sludge and stoner rock.

History

Founded by Woody Weatherman, bassist Mike Dean and drummer Reed Mullin, COC released the hardcore albums Eye for an Eye (1984) and Animosity (1985), which made them pioneers of the crossover thrash style. After a period of transition, the band added vocalist/guitarist Pepper Keenan and achieved commercial success with Blind (1991), Deliverance (1994) — featuring the hit "Albatross" and the acoustic instrumental "Without Wings" — and Wiseblood (1996), whose track "Drowning in a Daydream" was Grammy-nominated. Later albums include America's Volume Dealer (2000), In the Arms of God (2005) and No Cross No Crown (2018). Founding drummer Reed Mullin died in 2020.
