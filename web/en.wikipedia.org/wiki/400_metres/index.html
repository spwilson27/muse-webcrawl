400 metres

World record (men)Wayde van Niekerk, 43.03 s (2016)
World record (women)Marita Koch, 47.60 s (1985)

The 400 metres, or 400-metre dash, is a sprinting event in track and field competitions. It has been featured in the athletics programme at the Summer Olympic Games since 1896 for men and since 1964 for women. On a standard outdoor running track, it is exactly one lap, run in lanes with a staggered start.

Race demands

The 400 metres is the longest event classified as a sprint and demands a rare combination of raw speed and speed endurance; athletes must distribute effort carefully to resist the effects of lactic acid build-up in the final straight.

Records and notable athletes

Wayde van Niekerk of South Africa set the men's world record of 43.03 seconds at the 2016 Rio Olympics, running from lane eight. The women's record of 47.60 seconds by East Germany's Marita Koch, set in 1985, is one of the oldest records in the sport. Michael Johnson, Marie-José Pérec, and Shaunae Miller-Uibo are among the event's most celebrated champions.
