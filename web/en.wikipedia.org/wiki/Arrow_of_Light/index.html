Arrow of Light

The Arrow of Light is the highest rank and award in Cub Scouting, the programme of the Boy Scouts of America (Scouting America) for younger children. It is the only Cub Scout award that may continue to be worn on the youth and adult uniforms of the older Scouts BSA programme, and earning it marks a Cub Scout's preparation to cross over into a troop.


The Arrow of Light badge

Requirements

The award is earned by fifth-grade Cub Scouts in the Arrow of Light programme. Candidates must be active in their den, complete required adventures covering outdoors skills, citizenship, first aid, fitness and duty to God, build patrol spirit, and prepare for troop membership, including visiting a troop meeting and an outdoor troop activity. Requirements were restructured in programme updates, including the 2024 revision of Cub Scouting.

Symbolism

The emblem depicts a golden arrow beneath a rising sun with seven rays. The arrow points the way forward, and the seven rays are traditionally associated with the seven virtues the Scout is to follow. The badge is a gold-coloured device worn on the left pocket flap.

Crossover

Completion of the Arrow of Light is usually marked at a pack's blue and gold banquet or a special crossover ceremony, in which the youth symbolically leaves the pack — often crossing a bridge — and is received by members of their new troop.
