JAB Holding Company

JAB Holding Company S.à r.l. is a Luxembourg-based investment firm that manages the assets of Germany's Reimann family and invests heavily in consumer goods, particularly coffee, beverages, and fast-casual restaurants. Its portfolio has included Keurig Dr Pepper, Jacobs Douwe Egberts, Peet's Coffee, Panera Bread, and Krispy Kreme.

History

The family's wealth originates in the Ludwigshafen chemical company Joh. A. Benckiser, founded in 1823. In the late 20th century the family divested its detergents and cosmetics interests (which became part of Reckitt Benckiser) and reoriented through JAB toward branded consumer businesses.

Investments

From 2012 JAB undertook a rapid series of acquisitions in coffee, buying Peet's, Caribou Coffee, and Einstein Noah, merging Keurig Green Mountain with Dr Pepper Snapple Group in 2018, and listing JDE Peet's in Amsterdam in 2020. JAB also built stakes in restaurant chains and pet care. In 2019 the family acknowledged the Nazi-era history of the company and family members, and announced a charitable foundation.
