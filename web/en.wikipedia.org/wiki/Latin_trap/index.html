Latin trap

Latin trap is a subgenre of trap music performed mainly in Spanish, drawing on Southern hip hop beats — heavy 808 bass, rapid hi-hats, and dark minor-key melodies — combined with the urban culture of reggaeton. It emerged in Puerto Rico in the mid-2010s and became one of the dominant sounds in Spanish-language popular music.

History

Early Latin trap circulated on platforms such as SoundCloud around 2015, with artists including Bryant Myers, Anuel AA, and Almighty developing a raw style with explicit street themes that contrasted with commercial reggaeton. The genre broke into the mainstream in 2017–2018: Anuel AA's debut album Real Hasta la Muerte and Bad Bunny's rise — crowned by Un Verano Sin Ti (2022) — carried trap rhythms to global audiences, while the bilingual hit "I Like It" (2018) showed its crossover reach.

Themes and influence

Latin trap lyrics often address street life, loyalty, wealth, and relationships, and the genre was criticised for violent and explicit content, prompting debates in Puerto Rico. Musically it fed back into reggaeton, Latin pop, and Spanish-language rap in Spain and Latin America, and helped establish streaming-era stars who alternate between trap, reggaeton, and other styles.
