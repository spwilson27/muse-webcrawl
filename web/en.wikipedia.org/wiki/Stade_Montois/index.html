Stade Montois

Stade Montois is a French multisports club based in Mont-de-Marsan in the Landes department of southwestern France. Founded in 1908, it is best known for its rugby union team, which has competed in the French Top 14 and, in recent seasons, in Pro D2. The club's colours are yellow and black.

Rugby history

Stade Montois won its only French championship title in 1963, defeating US Dax in an all-Landes final. The club also won the Challenge Yves du Manoir three times (1960, 1961 and 1962) and was championship runner-up in 1949, 1953 and 1959. Legendary players of the post-war era included the Boniface brothers, André and Guy, after whom the club's stadium is named.

In the professional era the club has moved between the Top 14 and Pro D2, winning promotion play-offs on several occasions, including at the end of the 2011–12 and 2021–22 Pro D2 seasons.

Other sections

As a multisports club, Stade Montois has historically fielded sections in football (Stade Montois football), basketball, cycling, judo and other sports.
