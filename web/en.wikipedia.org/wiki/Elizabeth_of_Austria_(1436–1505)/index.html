Elizabeth of Austria (German: Elisabeth von Habsburg; Polish: Elżbieta Rakuszanka; c. 1436 – 30 August 1505) was Queen of Poland and Grand Duchess of Lithuania as the wife of King Casimir IV Jagiellon. She was the daughter of Albert II of Germany, King of the Romans, Hungary and Bohemia, and Elizabeth of Luxembourg, and thus an heiress of the Sigismund line of the Luxembourg dynasty.

Marriage and queenship

Elizabeth married Casimir IV in Kraków on 10 February 1454. The marriage strengthened Casimir's position against the Teutonic Order on the eve of the Thirteen Years' War and brought her claims to the Hungarian and Bohemian crowns into the Jagiellonian family. She was an influential consort, involved in dynastic diplomacy and the education of her children.

Issue

Elizabeth and Casimir had thirteen children, an exceptional number of whom reached royal thrones, earning her the nickname "mother of the Jagiellonians" (matka Jagiellonów):

  • Vladislaus II (1456–1516), King of Bohemia and Hungary
  • Hedwig (1457–1502), Duchess of Bavaria
  • Saint Casimir (1458–1484), patron saint of Lithuania
  • John I Albert (1459–1501), King of Poland
  • Alexander (1461–1506), King of Poland and Grand Duke of Lithuania
  • Sophia (1464–1512), Margravine of Brandenburg-Ansbach
  • Sigismund I the Old (1467–1548), King of Poland
  • Frederick (1468–1503), Cardinal and Archbishop of Gniezno
  • Anna (1476–1503), Duchess of Pomerania
  • Barbara (1478–1534), Duchess of Saxony
  • Elizabeth (1482–1517), Duchess of Legnica

Later life and death

Widowed in 1492, Elizabeth remained an influential figure at the Polish court through the reigns of her sons John Albert, Alexander and Sigismund. She died in Kraków on 30 August 1505 and was buried in Wawel Cathedral beside her husband.
