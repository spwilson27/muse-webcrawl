Willard Galen Garfield Weston OC CVO OOnt (29 October 1940 – 12 April 2021) was a British-Canadian billionaire businessman and philanthropist. He was the chairman of George Weston Limited and the executive chairman of Loblaw Companies Limited, Canada's largest food retailer. Through the family's holding company he also controlled luxury retail interests including Selfridges, Brown Thomas, Holt Renfrew, and Fortnum & Mason.

Early life and education

Weston was born in Marlow, Buckinghamshire, England, the youngest of nine children of Garfield Weston, the Canadian bakery magnate who built an international food empire, and Reta Lila Howard. He was educated at Upper Canada College in Toronto and at the University of Western Ontario, where he studied business.

Career

Weston began his career working in the family's Irish businesses in the 1960s, where he developed the supermarket chain that became Quinnsworth. In 1972 he moved to Canada to take over Loblaw Companies, which was then close to bankruptcy. Over the following decades he revived the company, introducing the No Name and President's Choice private-label brands, the latter developed with chef Dave Nichol, which became enormously successful.

He became chairman of George Weston Limited in 1974 following his father's death. Under his leadership the group expanded into luxury department stores, acquiring Holt Renfrew in Canada (1986), Selfridges in London (2003), and other European retailers. By the time of his death, the Weston family was among the wealthiest in Canada and the United Kingdom.

Philanthropy and honours

Weston and his wife Hilary, a former Lieutenant Governor of Ontario, were major philanthropists through the Weston Family Foundation (formerly the W. Garfield Weston Foundation), supporting education, land conservation, and research in Canada's North. He was appointed an Officer of the Order of Canada and a Commander of the Royal Victorian Order.

Weston died at his home in Toronto on 12 April 2021, at the age of 80. His son, Galen G. Weston, succeeded him as chairman of George Weston Limited and Loblaw.
