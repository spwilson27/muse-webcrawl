The Conjuring Universe

Created by: James Wan
First film: The Conjuring (2013)
Distributed by: Warner Bros. Pictures / New Line Cinema
Total box office: over $2 billion

The Conjuring Universe is an American horror media franchise and shared universe centred on films produced by New Line Cinema, Atomic Monster, and the Safran Company. The series is loosely based on the case files of real-life paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren, portrayed by Patrick Wilson and Vera Farmiga. With worldwide grosses exceeding $2 billion, it is the highest-grossing horror franchise in film history.

Films

  • The Conjuring (2013) — the Perron family haunting in Rhode Island.
  • Annabelle (2014) — spin-off about the possessed doll.
  • The Conjuring 2 (2016) — the Enfield poltergeist in London.
  • Annabelle: Creation (2017) — origin of the doll.
  • The Nun (2018) — the demon Valak in 1950s Romania.
  • The Curse of La Llorona (2019) — loosely connected entry.
  • Annabelle Comes Home (2019) — the Warrens' occult museum.
  • The Conjuring: The Devil Made Me Do It (2021) — the Arne Johnson trial case.
  • The Nun II (2023) — Valak returns in France.
  • The Conjuring: Last Rites (2025) — concluding main-series chapter about the Smurl haunting.

Development

James Wan directed the first two Conjuring films and produced the series through Atomic Monster with Peter Safran. The franchise expanded the haunted-object "cold open" cases of the main films into standalone spin-offs. Critics have generally praised the main-series entries while giving the spin-offs more mixed assessments, but the films' modest budgets have made them consistently profitable.
