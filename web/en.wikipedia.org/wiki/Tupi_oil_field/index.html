Tupi oil field

The Tupi oil field, renamed the Lula oil field in 2010, is a large deepwater oil and gas field in the Santos Basin, about 250 kilometres off the coast of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. It was the first major discovery in Brazil's pre-salt province.

Discovery

The field was discovered in 2006 by a consortium led by Petrobras (with BG Group and Galp Energia) in block BM-S-11. The reservoirs lie beneath roughly 2,000 metres of water and a thick layer of salt some 2,000 metres thick, at total depths of around 7,000 metres. Initial estimates placed recoverable volumes at 5–8 billion barrels of oil equivalent, making Tupi one of the largest oil discoveries of its decade.

Production

Pilot production began in 2009, and the field has since been developed with multiple floating production storage and offloading (FPSO) vessels. Lula/Tupi became Brazil's largest producing field and anchored the development of the wider pre-salt play, which transformed Brazil into one of the world's top oil producers.
