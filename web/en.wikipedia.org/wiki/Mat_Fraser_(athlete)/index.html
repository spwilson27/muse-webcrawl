Mathew Fraser (born 25 January 1990) is a retired Canadian-American professional CrossFit athlete. He won the men's individual title at the CrossFit Games five consecutive times, from 2016 to 2020, earning the title "Fittest Man on Earth" and becoming the most dominant male competitor in the sport's history.

Early life and weightlifting

Fraser was born in Kingston, Ontario, Canada, to Canadian Olympic figure skaters Don Fraser and Candace Jones, and was raised in Colchester, Vermont. He began competitive Olympic weightlifting as a teenager and won junior national titles, but a serious back injury — a fractured L5 vertebra, later surgically repaired — ended his weightlifting ambitions around 2009. After retiring from weightlifting and completing a degree, he was introduced to CrossFit.

CrossFit career

Fraser made his CrossFit Games debut in 2014, finishing second, and was again runner-up in 2015 before beginning his championship run. His victories in 2017 and 2020 were by record margins — in 2020 he won by 545 points, the largest in Games history. Fraser announced his retirement from competition in February 2021. After retiring he co-founded the training company HWPO (Hard Work Pays Off), authored a book of the same name, and has coached other athletes, including Mal O'Brien and Jayson Hopper.
