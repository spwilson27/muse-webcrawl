Smart motorway

A smart motorway is a section of motorway in the United Kingdom that uses active traffic management techniques — variable speed limits, overhead gantry signals, and in many cases conversion of the hard shoulder to a running lane — to increase capacity and smooth traffic flow. Schemes are operated by National Highways in England.

Three main types exist: controlled motorways (variable speed limits, hard shoulder retained), dynamic hard shoulder running, and all-lane running, in which the hard shoulder is permanently converted with emergency refuge areas at intervals. Smart motorways have been controversial over the safety of stopped vehicles in live lanes; in 2023 the UK government cancelled plans for new smart motorways while committing to safety upgrades on existing ones.

Technology

Systems use CCTV, radar-based stopped-vehicle detection and MIDAS traffic sensors to set speeds and close lanes. See also motorway.
