Biathlon World Championships

[Image placeholder: Biathlon World Championships shooting range]

The Biathlon World Championships are the top international biathlon events, organized by the International Biathlon Union (IBU). The first championships were held in 1958 in Saalfelden, Austria, with the men's 20 km individual and team events; women's events were added in 1984, and the championships have been held jointly since 1989.

In Winter Olympic years, championship races are generally not held for Olympic events, though non-Olympic disciplines may still be contested. The modern program includes sprint, pursuit, individual, mass start, relay, mixed relay and single mixed relay competitions.

Notable athletes

The most successful biathletes at the World Championships include Ole Einar Bjørndalen of Norway, with 20 gold medals, and more recently Johannes Thingnes Bø. Leading host venues include Ruhpolding, Antholz-Anterselva, Östersund, Pokljuka and Oberhof.
