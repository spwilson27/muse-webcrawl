Mount Scenery

Mount Scenery is a dormant volcano on the island of Saba in the Caribbean Netherlands. At 870 metres (2,854 ft) above sea level, its summit is the highest point in the Kingdom of the Netherlands. The mountain dominates the small island and is covered in lush cloud forest.

Description

Mount Scenery is a stratovolcano with a potentially active history; its last eruption is thought to have occurred around 1640. A hiking trail of some 1,064 steps climbs from the village of Windwardside to the summit, passing through rainforest and elfin cloud forest near the top. Mount Scenery National Park protects the mountain and surrounding areas, noted for their biodiversity and hiking. Since the dissolution of the Netherlands Antilles in 2010 and Saba's becoming a Dutch special municipality, the summit has stood as the Netherlands' highest point, exceeding the Vaalserberg on the mainland.
