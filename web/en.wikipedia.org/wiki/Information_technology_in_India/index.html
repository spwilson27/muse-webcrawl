Information technology in India

The information technology industry in India is one of the country's largest economic sectors, encompassing IT services, software products, engineering services and business process management. India is the world's leading sourcing destination for IT services, accounting for a majority share of the global outsourcing market.

Scale

The industry generates revenues in the region of a quarter of a trillion US dollars annually and employs more than five million people directly, with technology exports forming a major share of India's service exports. Industry association NASSCOM represents the sector.

Major firms and hubs

Leading Indian IT companies include Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), Infosys, Wipro, HCL Technologies and Tech Mahindra, alongside the Indian development centres of global technology firms. Bengaluru is the best-known hub — often called India's Silicon Valley — with other major centres in Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, the National Capital Region (Gurugram and Noida), Kolkata and Mumbai.

History

The industry grew from software services firms founded in the late 1960s–1980s (TCS in 1968, Infosys in 1981), expanded rapidly after the 1991 economic liberalisation, and boomed around the Y2K problem at the end of the 1990s, which showcased Indian programming capacity to Western clients. Favourable factors included a large English-speaking engineering workforce, time-zone advantages and supportive policies such as Software Technology Parks.
