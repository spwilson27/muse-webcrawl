Kinetic energy

In physics, the kinetic energy of an object is the energy it possesses due to its motion. For a non-rotating object of mass m travelling at speed v, it is given by Ek = ½mv².

Description

Kinetic energy is a scalar quantity measured in joules in the SI system. It can be transferred between objects and transformed into other forms of energy; the work–energy principle states that the work done on an object equals its change in kinetic energy.

Relativistic kinetic energy

At speeds approaching the speed of light, the classical formula breaks down and kinetic energy is instead described by special relativity, increasing without limit as speed approaches c.
