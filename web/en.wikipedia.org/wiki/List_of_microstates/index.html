List of microstates

Microstate
DefinitionSovereign state with a very small population or land area
SmallestVatican City (0.49 km²)
Least populousVatican City

A microstate or ministate is a sovereign state having a very small population or very small land area, usually both. However, the meanings of "state" and "very small" are not well-defined in international law. Examples of microstates include Andorra, Liechtenstein, the Marshall Islands, Monaco, Nauru and Vatican City.
