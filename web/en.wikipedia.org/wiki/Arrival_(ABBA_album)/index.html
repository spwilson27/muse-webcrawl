Arrival (ABBA album)

Arrival is the fourth studio album by the Swedish pop group ABBA. It was originally released in Sweden on 11 October 1976 by Polar Records.

Content

The album contains some of ABBA's biggest hits: "Dancing Queen", "Money, Money, Money", "Knowing Me, Knowing You" and "Fernando" (on many editions). "Dancing Queen" became ABBA's only US Billboard Hot 100 number one.

Reception and legacy

Arrival was one of the best-selling albums of 1977 in the UK and cemented ABBA's global stardom at the height of Abbamania in Australia and Europe. It has been reissued many times, including deluxe editions.

Track listing

Side one: "When I Kissed the Teacher", "Dancing Queen", "My Love, My Life", "Dum Dum Diddle", "Knowing Me, Knowing You". Side two: "Money, Money, Money", "That's Me", "Why Did It Have to Be Me?", "Tiger", "Arrival".
