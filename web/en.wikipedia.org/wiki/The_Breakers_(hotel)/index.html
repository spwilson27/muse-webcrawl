The Breakers (hotel)

The Breakers is a luxury oceanfront hotel in Palm Beach, Florida, United States. Originally built by Henry Flagler as the Palm Beach Inn in 1896, the resort was renamed The Breakers in 1901 and rebuilt after fires in 1903 and 1925; the current Italian Renaissance Revival building opened in 1926.

Location1 South County Road, Palm Beach, Florida
Opened1896 (original); 29 December 1926 (current)
OwnerFlagler System (Kenan family)
Rooms538
Grounds140 acres (57 ha)

History

Flagler, the developer of Florida's east coast railway, built the hotel to accommodate wealthy travellers to Palm Beach. The present structure, designed by the New York firm Schultze and Weaver, drew inspiration from the Villa Medici in Rome. It is listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

Amenities

The resort includes two golf courses (including Florida's oldest 18-hole course), a beach club, pools, tennis courts, a spa and multiple restaurants, and remains owned by descendants of Flagler's family.
