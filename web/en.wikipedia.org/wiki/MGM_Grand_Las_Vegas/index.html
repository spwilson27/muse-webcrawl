The MGM Grand Las Vegas is a hotel and casino located on the Las Vegas Strip in Paradise, Nevada. When it opened on December 18, 1993, it was the largest hotel in the world, and with 6,852 rooms it remains one of the largest hotels and the largest single hotel in the United States. The property is owned and operated by MGM Resorts International.

History

The resort was developed by Kirk Kerkorian's MGM Grand Inc. on the site of the former Marina Hotel. Its original theme drew on MGM's film heritage and The Wizard of Oz, with an Emerald City attraction and a giant lion entrance that was later replaced by the current bronze lion statue, one of the largest bronze sculptures in the United States. A major remodeling completed in the 2010s removed the Oz theme in favor of a contemporary design.

Attractions

The complex includes a large casino floor, the MGM Grand Garden Arena — a major venue for boxing, MMA and concerts — residencies such as Cirque du Soleil's Kà, nightclubs including Hakkasan, numerous restaurants, a convention center, the Grand Pool complex and the adjacent Signature at MGM Grand towers. The property is connected to the Las Vegas Monorail.
