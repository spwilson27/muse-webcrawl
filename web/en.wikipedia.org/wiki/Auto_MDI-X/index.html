Auto MDI-X

Auto MDI-X
Full name: Automatic Medium-Dependent Interface Crossover
Context: Ethernet over twisted pair
Function: Automatic detection and correction of TX/RX pair orientation

Auto MDI-X (automatic medium-dependent interface crossover) is a technology for Ethernet interfaces that automatically detects the required cable connection type and configures the connection appropriately. It removes the need to choose between straight-through and crossover cables when connecting devices.

Background

Traditional Ethernet ports come in two types: MDI (used by end devices such as computers) and MDI-X (used by hubs and switches), with the transmit and receive pairs swapped between them. Auto MDI-X, developed by Hewlett-Packard engineers and implemented in the 1000BASE-T standard, lets a port sense which pair a link partner is transmitting on and assign its own receiver accordingly. As a result, any combination of ports can be connected with a standard straight-through cable, and gigabit Ethernet devices almost universally include the feature.
