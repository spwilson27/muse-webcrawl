List of school shootings in the United States

This article documents notable school shootings in the United States. The full list, maintained by year, records hundreds of incidents on school property from the colonial era to the present, including attacks on elementary and secondary schools, colleges and universities. The deadliest such attack was the 2007 Virginia Tech shooting, in which 32 people were killed.

The frequency of school shootings is a central subject of the American gun-politics debate, with policy responses ranging from campus security and threat-assessment programmes to firearm legislation. Definitions differ among trackers (such as the K-12 School Shooting Database and the Gun Violence Archive), so counts vary by source and by whether incidents without injuries are included.
