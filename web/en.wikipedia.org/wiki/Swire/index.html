Swire

Swire Group
Type: Private / Public (Swire Pacific)
Founded: 1816
Founder: John Swire
Headquarters: Hong Kong / London
Industry: Conglomerate — aviation, property, shipping, beverages

Swire is a highly diversified global conglomerate founded in Liverpool, England, by John Swire in 1816. The group's principal holding company is John Swire & Sons Limited in London, while its principal operating arm in Asia is Swire Pacific Limited, listed in Hong Kong. In Chinese, Swire is known as Taikoo (太古), meaning "great and ancient".

History

John Swire began as a modest Liverpool textile merchant. His son John Samuel Swire expanded the business into overseas trade, opening offices in Shanghai in 1866 and Hong Kong in 1870, initially trading tea and silk and operating shipping on the Yangtze. The China Navigation Company was founded in 1872. The group built the Taikoo Sugar Refinery and the Taikoo Dockyard in Hong Kong, becoming one of the great British "hong" trading houses of the China coast.

Businesses

Today Swire's activities span:

  • Aviation — controlling shareholder of Cathay Pacific and interests in aircraft engineering (HAECO)
  • Property — Swire Properties, developer of Taikoo Place, Pacific Place and Taikoo Hui
  • Beverages — one of the largest Coca-Cola bottlers in the world, covering much of China and parts of the United States
  • Marine services and shipping — Swire Shipping and offshore support services
  • Trading and industrial — including cold storage and food businesses
