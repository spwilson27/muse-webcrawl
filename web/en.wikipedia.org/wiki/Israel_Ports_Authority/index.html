Israel Ports Authority

The Israel Ports Authority (Hebrew: רשות הנמלים) was a governmental body that operated Israel's Mediterranean and Red Sea seaports from 1961 until 2005, when it was replaced under a reform programme by the Israel Ports Development & Assets Company and separate operating companies.

Founded1961
Dissolved2005
HeadquartersTel Aviv, Israel
PortsHaifa, Ashdod, Eilat

History

The authority was established by the Ports Authority Law of 1961, taking over the port of Haifa and the then-new port of Ashdod, which opened in 1965, as well as the Red Sea port of Eilat.

Reform

A 2004 law transformed the authority into a landlord model: infrastructure and assets passed to a state-owned development company, while Haifa, Ashdod, and Eilat each became separate operating companies, a structure intended to introduce competition and private investment.
