Aruba Networks

Founded: 2002, Sunnyvale, California
Founder: Keerti Melkote
Parent: Hewlett Packard Enterprise
Products: Wireless LAN, network access control

Aruba is now the networking brand of HPE

Aruba Networks, now generally marketed as HPE Aruba Networking, is an American wireless networking company founded in Sunnyvale, California in 2002 by Keerti Melkote and Pankaj Manglik. It built its business on wireless LAN mobility controllers, access points and network security software for enterprise campuses.

History

Aruba went public in 2007 and became the second-largest vendor in enterprise WLAN. In March 2015 Hewlett-Packard announced it would acquire Aruba for about $3 billion; after the HP split, Aruba became part of Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE), where it forms the core of the Intelligent Edge business. Its product lines include ArubaOS, the ClearPass access-control platform, Aruba Central cloud management, and SD-WAN technology acquired with Silver Peak in 2020. In 2023 HPE rebranded the unit as HPE Aruba Networking.
