Cocktail onion

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

A cocktail onion garnishing a Gibson cocktail.

A cocktail onion is a small pickled pearl onion used as a cocktail garnish, typically about 1 inch (2.5 cm) or less in diameter. Cocktail onions are usually pickled in a brine of vinegar with turmeric and other spices, giving them a mild, sweet-sour flavour distinct from that of raw onions.

Use

The cocktail onion is best known as the garnish that distinguishes the Gibson cocktail from the Martini: a Gibson is a gin (or vodka) martini garnished with one or more cocktail onions instead of olives or a lemon twist. The onions' slight savoury tang complements the drink, and the garnish is usually served skewered on a cocktail pick.

Pearl onions used for cocktail onions are grown for their small size; varieties include the white "Crystal Wax" type. Commercially, cocktail onions are sold in jars of brine and are also used in some cooking and in drinks such as certain Bloody Mary variations.
