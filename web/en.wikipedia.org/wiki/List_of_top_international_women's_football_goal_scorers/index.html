This is a list of top international women's football goal scorers, ranking the women footballers who have scored the most goals in official senior international matches. Canada's Christine Sinclair is the all-time leading scorer in international football — men's or women's — with 190 goals, having passed the previous record of 184 held by the United States' Abby Wambach in January 2020.
