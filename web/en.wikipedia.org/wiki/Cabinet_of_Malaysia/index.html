Cabinet of Malaysia

Native nameJemaah Menteri
HeadPrime Minister of Malaysia
Appointed byYang di-Pertuan Agong
SeatPutrajaya

The Cabinet of Malaysia (Malay: Jemaah Menteri) is the executive branch of the Government of Malaysia. Led by the Prime Minister, it comprises ministers who head the various ministries and are collectively responsible to Parliament.

Members of the Cabinet are appointed by the Yang di-Pertuan Agong on the advice of the Prime Minister, and must be members of either house of Parliament.

Structure

The Cabinet includes the Prime Minister, one or more Deputy Prime Ministers, ministers, and deputy ministers (who attend only when relevant). Ministries cover areas such as finance, home affairs, defence, education, health, and foreign affairs.

Meetings

The Cabinet typically meets weekly, on Wednesdays, at the Perdana Putra building in Putrajaya. Decisions are made collectively, and ministers are bound by collective responsibility.

History

The first Malayan Cabinet was formed after the 1955 federal elections under Tunku Abdul Rahman, and the institution developed through independence in 1957 and the formation of Malaysia in 1963. Subsequent cabinets have been reshaped at each change of government, notably in 2018 and 2022.
