List of color palettes

A color palette is a set of colors used together in a design, interface, or hardware system. Early computers displayed limited palettes imposed by graphics hardware; artists and pixel artists also adopt fixed palettes for stylistic consistency. This article lists notable hardware, system, and artistic palettes.

Hardware palettes defined the look of early computer and game graphics.
