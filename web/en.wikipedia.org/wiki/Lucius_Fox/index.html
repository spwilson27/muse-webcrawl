Lucius Fox is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Len Wein and artist John Calnan, he first appeared in Batman #307 (January 1979). Fox serves as the chief executive officer and president of Wayne Enterprises and is one of Bruce Wayne's closest allies, supplying him with much of the technology used in his war on crime in Gotham City.

Fictional character biography

Lucius Fox is recruited by Bruce Wayne to run Wayne Enterprises and rescue the company from near-bankruptcy, turning it into a hugely successful multinational corporation. A brilliant businessman and inventor, Fox manages both the business and philanthropic arms of Wayne's empire while discreetly developing prototype technologies — vehicles, body armour, gadgets and surveillance equipment — that Batman adapts for his own use. In most continuities Fox is aware, or strongly suspects, that Wayne is Batman, though the two rarely discuss it openly.

Fox has faced numerous attempts by corporate raiders and criminals to seize control of Wayne Enterprises, and has at times been targeted by villains including Ra's al Ghul and the Ventriloquist. He is the father of several children who have entered the Batman mythos in their own right, including his son Luke Fox, who becomes the vigilante Batwing, and his daughter Tamara Fox.

In other media

The character was portrayed by Morgan Freeman in Christopher Nolan's Dark Knight film trilogy (2005–2012), where he serves as Wayne Enterprises' applied sciences division head and later CEO, acting as Batman's armorer. Fox also appears in animated series including Batman: The Animated Series (voiced by Brock Peters), The Batman, and in the television series Gotham (portrayed by Chris Chalk), as well as in numerous video games in the Batman: Arkham series.
