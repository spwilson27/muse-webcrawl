West Anglia Main Line

The West Anglia Main Line is one of the two main lines that form the Greater Anglia network in eastern England. It runs from London Liverpool Street to Cambridge, with branches to Hertford East, Stansted Airport, Chingford and Enfield Town. The line serves the Lea Valley and communities in Hertfordshire, Essex and Cambridgeshire.

Route

From Liverpool Street the line passes through Tottenham Hale, where it connects with the Victoria line, then follows the Lea Valley through Cheshunt and Broxbourne. At Broxbourne the Hertford East branch diverges, while the main line continues through Bishop's Stortford and Audley End to Cambridge. The Stansted Airport branch, opened in 1991, diverges near Stansted Mountfitchet.

Services

Services are operated by Greater Anglia, including Stansted Express trains to Stansted Airport and stopping and semi-fast services to Cambridge. CrossCountry trains use the line between Stansted Airport and Cambridge. The Lea Valley lines section between Liverpool Street and Cheshunt, Chingford and Enfield Town transferred to London Overground in 2015.
