Hainan Airlines

Hainan Airlines is an airline headquartered in Haikou, Hainan, China. It is one of China's largest airlines and operates scheduled domestic and international services from hubs including Haikou Meilan International Airport and Beijing Capital International Airport.

History

The airline was founded in 1989 as Hainan Province Airlines and began operations in 1993. It became China's first joint-stock air transport company and grew under the HNA Group, expanding internationally in the 2000s and 2010s with Boeing 787 Dreamliners and Airbus A330s. Following HNA Group's bankruptcy reorganisation in 2021, control passed to Liaoning Fangda Group.

Destinations and fleet

Hainan Airlines serves destinations across China, Asia, Europe, North America and Oceania. Its fleet has included Boeing 737, 767, 787 and Airbus A330 and A350 aircraft. The airline has held a Skytrax five-star rating.

Affiliates

Related carriers have included Hong Kong Airlines, Tianjin Airlines, Capital Airlines and other HNA-affiliated airlines.
