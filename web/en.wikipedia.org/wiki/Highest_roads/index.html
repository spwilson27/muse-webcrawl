Highest roads

This is a list of the highest paved roads in the world, ranked by the elevation of their highest point above sea level. Many of the world's highest motorable passes are found in the Himalayas and the Andes.

Highest passes

Among the highest motorable mountain passes are Umling La in Ladakh, India, at around 5,800 metres (19,000 ft), and Khardung La, also in Ladakh, at about 5,359 metres (17,582 ft). In the Andes, passes in Peru, Bolivia, and Chile exceed 4,800 metres. Europe's highest paved pass is the Col de l'Iseran in France at 2,764 metres (9,068 ft), while in North America the Trail Ridge Road in Colorado and the road up Mount Evans (Mount Blue Sky) reach over 3,700 metres.

Criteria

Lists of highest roads vary depending on whether unpaved roads, dead-end summit roads, and roads restricted to military or official use are included. Elevations quoted for some passes have also historically been exaggerated in local signage.
