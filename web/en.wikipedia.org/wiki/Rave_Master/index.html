Rave Master, titled Rave (レイヴ) in Japan, is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Hiro Mashima. It was serialised in Kodansha's Weekly Shōnen Magazine from July 1999 to July 2005, with its chapters collected in 35 tankōbon volumes. An anime television series adaptation by Studio Deen aired from October 2001 to September 2002.

Plot

Fifty years before the story begins, the world was threatened by the power of the Dark Bring, stones of darkness, until the first Rave Master, Shiba Roses, used the holy Rave stones to destroy them in an explosion called the Overdrive, which scattered the Rave stones across the world. Sixteen-year-old Haru Glory, a boy from Garage Island, is chosen as the second Rave Master. Armed with the Ten Commandments sword and guided by the strange creature Plue, Haru journeys with amnesiac girl Elie and former thief Musica to gather the Rave stones and defeat the criminal organisation Demon Card, led by King and later by his son Lucia Raregroove.

Characters

  • Haru Glory — the second Rave Master, wielder of the Ten Commandments sword.
  • Elie — a girl with no memory who holds the secret of Etherion, a powerful magic.
  • Plue — a small white creature, guardian of the Rave, with a carrot-shaped nose.
  • Hamrio Musica — a silver-claimer who can manipulate silver.
  • Lucia Raregroove — the main antagonist and master of the Dark Bring.

Media and reception

The manga circulated widely in Japan and was published in English by Tokyopop and later Kodansha USA. Mashima went on to create Fairy Tail, in which Plue and other Rave Master concepts make cameo appearances; the crossover manga Heros later united characters from both series and Edens Zero. By 2021 the manga had millions of copies in circulation.
