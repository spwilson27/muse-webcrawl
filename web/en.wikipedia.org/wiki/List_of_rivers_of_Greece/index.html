List of rivers of Greece

Greece has a large number of mostly short rivers, reflecting its mountainous terrain and deeply indented coastline. No river in Greece is navigable for large vessels. Many rivers are seasonal, running low or dry in summer. Greece's rivers flow into the Aegean Sea, the Ionian Sea, or form part of transboundary systems shared with neighbouring countries.

Rivers in Greek mythology

Several Greek rivers were personified as river gods in ancient mythology, including the Achelous, the Alpheus — said to pursue the nymph Arethusa beneath the sea to Sicily — and the underworld rivers Styx, Acheron, Lethe, Phlegethon and Cocytus. The name of the river god Asopus recurs in several regions of Greece.
