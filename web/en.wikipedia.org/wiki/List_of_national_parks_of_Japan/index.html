List of national parks of Japan

National parks of Japan (国立公園, Kokuritsu Kōen) are places of scenic beauty designated by the Minister of the Environment under the Natural Parks Act. There are 34 national parks, covering about 5.8% of Japan's land area (over 21,000 km² including marine areas). Unlike in some countries, most land within Japanese national parks is privately or municipally owned and inhabited; the parks are zoned for conservation and recreation. The first three parks — Setonaikai, Unzen and Kirishima — were designated in 1934, following the National Parks Act of 1931. The system also includes 57 quasi-national parks and numerous prefectural natural parks.
