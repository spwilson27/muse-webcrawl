Cessna Citation Mustang

Cessna Citation Mustang
Manufacturer: Cessna
First flight: 23 April 2005
Produced: 2006–2017; 479 built
Crew: 1–2; Passengers: 4–5
Engines: 2 × Pratt & Whitney Canada PW615F turbofans

The Cessna Citation Mustang (Model 510) is a very light jet built by Cessna in the United States. It was one of the first aircraft in its class to receive full type certification (in 2006) and helped define the entry-level business jet market aimed at owner-pilots.

Design

The Mustang seats four passengers in the cabin plus up to two pilots, cruises at about 340 knots, and has a range of roughly 1,150 nautical miles (2,130 km). It is powered by two Pratt & Whitney Canada PW615F turbofans mounted at the rear fuselage and uses the Garmin G1000 glass cockpit. Unusually for its class, it has a small externally accessible baggage compartment and an optional lavatory in place of a seat.

Production

Production ran at Cessna's Independence, Kansas plant from 2006 until 2017, when the Mustang was discontinued after 479 aircraft as buyers moved to the larger Citation M2. Many Mustangs serve in air-taxi and fractional or club fleets in Europe and North America.
