Rocksteady Studios

Rocksteady Studios Limited is a British video game developer based in London. Founded on 13 December 2004 by Jamie Walker and Sefton Hill, the studio is best known for developing the Batman: Arkham series. It is owned by Warner Bros. Games.

History

Rocksteady's first game was the first-person shooter Urban Chaos: Riot Response (2006). Its breakthrough came with Batman: Arkham Asylum (2009), praised for its "FreeFlow" combat and stealth gameplay, which won numerous Game of the Year awards. Sequels Batman: Arkham City (2011) and Batman: Arkham Knight (2015) were also critical and commercial successes, and the studio developed the virtual-reality title Batman: Arkham VR (2016).

Later projects

The studio's next major release was Suicide Squad: Kill the Justice League (2024), a cooperative action shooter set in the Arkham universe, which received mixed reviews and underperformed commercially. Founders Hill and Walker departed in 2022 to establish a new studio, Hundred Star Games.
