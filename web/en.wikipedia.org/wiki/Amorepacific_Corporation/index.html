Amorepacific Corporation (Korean: 아모레퍼시픽) is a South Korean beauty and cosmetics chaebol, operating more than 30 beauty, personal care and health brands. Founded in 1945, it is one of the world's largest cosmetics companies and a leading force in the global popularity of Korean beauty ("K-beauty").

History

The company's origins date to the 1930s, when Yun Dok-jeong sold camellia hair oil at a market stall in Gaeseong. Her son, Suh Sung-whan, founded the company in 1945 under the name Taepyeongyang (Pacific Chemical Industries). The firm launched Korea's first branded cosmetic product and, in the 1990s and 2000s, expanded aggressively into China, the rest of Asia, Europe and North America. Its global headquarters in Yongsan, Seoul — a distinctive cube-shaped building designed by David Chipperfield — opened in 2017.

Brands and innovations

Amorepacific's portfolio spans luxury and mass-market tiers. Sulwhasoo is a premium herbal brand; Laneige is known for its "Water Bank" skincare; Innisfree markets natural ingredients from Jeju Island; Etude targets younger consumers. The company is credited with popularising the cushion compact foundation in 2008 with its IOPE Air Cushion, a format widely copied across the global beauty industry. It also operates the green-tea brand and museum complex O'Sulloc on Jeju Island.
