Christine Jane Baranski (born May 2, 1952) is an American actress. Equally at home on stage, in film and on television, she is celebrated for her precise comic timing and portrayals of polished, formidable women. Over a career spanning five decades she has won an Emmy Award, two Tony Awards and multiple Screen Actors Guild Awards.

Early life and education

Baranski was born in Buffalo, New York, to Polish-American parents; her grandparents were actors in the Polish theatre. She studied at the Juilliard School in New York City, graduating in 1974.

Career

Baranski built her reputation on Broadway, winning Tony Awards for The Real Thing (1984) and Rumors (1989), and starring in musicals including Mame. Television brought her wider fame as the witty, martini-loving Maryann Thorpe in the sitcom Cybill (1995–1998), for which she won an Emmy. Film roles include The Birdcage (1996), Chicago (2002), Mamma Mia! (2008) and its sequel, and Into the Woods (2014). From 2009 she played the elegant litigator Diane Lockhart in The Good Wife, reprising the role as lead of the spin-off The Good Fight (2017–2022). She also appears in The Gilded Age as Agnes van Rhijn.

Personal life

She was married to actor Matthew Cowles from 1983 until his death in 2014; they had two daughters.
