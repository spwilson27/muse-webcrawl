List of current monarchs

There are presently 43 sovereign states in the world with a monarch as head of state. Thirteen of these are in Asia, twelve in Europe, nine in North America (the Caribbean and Central America, including the Commonwealth realms), six in Oceania, and three in Africa. This list gives the reigning sovereigns of independent states; it does not include subnational monarchs such as the emirs of the United Arab Emirates' constituent emirates in their federal capacity, or traditional rulers.

The longest-reigning current sovereign is Sultan Hassanal Bolkiah of Brunei, who has reigned since 1967. The Commonwealth realms share a single monarch, Charles III, who is separately king of each realm. Malaysia's Yang di-Pertuan Agong and the Pope are elective monarchs; Andorra is unique in having two co-princes, one of whom is the President of France.
