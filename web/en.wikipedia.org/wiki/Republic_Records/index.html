Republic Records is an American record label owned by Universal Music Group (UMG). Based in New York City, it was founded by brothers Avery Lipman and Monte Lipman as an independent label in 1995, and was acquired by UMG in 2000. Republic has been one of the most commercially successful labels of the streaming era, repeatedly named Billboard's top label of the year.

History

The Lipman brothers founded Republic after working at other labels; an early success came with the Bloodhound Gang's single "Fire Water Burn". After UMG acquired the label, it was at times folded into Universal Motown Republic Group and operated as Universal Republic before reverting to the Republic Records name in 2012. Under Monte Lipman as chairman and CEO, the label developed a reputation for aggressive marketing and close integration with streaming services.

Republic has served as the releasing label or marketing partner for imprints including Cash Money Records (whose roster included Drake, Nicki Minaj, and Lil Wayne through much of the 2010s), Taylor Swift's releases beginning with Lover (2019), and numerous film soundtracks.

Artists

Artists on the Republic roster have included Ariana Grande, Taylor Swift, Post Malone, The Weeknd (via XO/Republic), Drake, Nicki Minaj, Lorde, Florence + the Machine, James Blake, John Legend, Morgan Wallen (via Big Loud/Republic), and Pearl Jam. The label's releases dominated streaming charts through the late 2010s and 2020s.

Subsidiaries and partnerships

Republic has worked with numerous partner labels, including Island Records in certain configurations, American Recordings, Brushfire Records, Casablanca Records, Lava Records, and XO. In 2022 Monte Lipman and Avery Lipman were given expanded roles overseeing a group of UMG's East Coast labels.
