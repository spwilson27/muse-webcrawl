Robert Capa

Robert Capa
Born22 October 1913, Budapest
Died25 May 1954, Thái Bình, Indochina
OccupationWar photographer; Magnum co-founder
[Image: Capa with his camera in Spain, 1937]

Robert Capa (1913–1954), born Endre Friedmann in Budapest, was a Hungarian-American war photographer and photojournalist, famed for The Falling Soldier image of the Spanish Civil War and his D-Day photographs at Omaha Beach. A co-founder of the Magnum Photos cooperative (1947), he was killed by a landmine in Indochina in 1954.

Work and debates

Capa's maxim — 'If your pictures aren't good enough, you're not close enough' — defined combat photography. The Falling Soldier's authenticity has been debated for decades; Magnum and most exhibitions continue to present it as genuine. Only a fraction of his D-Day film survived a London darkroom accident, adding to its legend.
