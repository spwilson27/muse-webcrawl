The Miami Hurricanes football team represents the University of Miami in college football at the NCAA Division I Football Bowl Subdivision. The Hurricanes compete in the Atlantic Coast Conference and play their home games at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens, Florida.

History

Miami fielded its first team in 1926 but rose to national prominence in the 1980s under Howard Schnellenberger, winning the program's first national championship in 1983. Jimmy Johnson and Dennis Erickson added titles in 1987, 1989, and 1991, and the 2001 Hurricanes under Larry Coker are widely regarded as one of the greatest college teams ever assembled, sending an extraordinary number of players to the NFL.

Culture and rivalries

The program's swaggering style in the 1980s and 1990s reshaped college football culture. Miami's principal rivals include Florida State, Florida, and Notre Dame, and its turnover chain tradition, introduced in 2017, became a widely copied sideline celebration.
