The governor of Texas is the head of government of the U.S. state of Texas. Texas was admitted to the Union in 1845 and has had 48 governors. Two Texas governors, George W. Bush and (by birth, Lyndon B. Johnson was a senator, not governor) — only Bush went directly from the governorship to the presidency.
