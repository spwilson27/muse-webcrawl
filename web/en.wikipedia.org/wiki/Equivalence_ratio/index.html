Equivalence ratio

SymbolΦ (phi)
FieldCombustion engineering

The equivalence ratio Φ is defined as the ratio of the fuel mass to oxidizer mass divided by the same ratio at stoichiometric conditions. It is a key parameter in combustion used to describe whether a fuel–oxidizer mixture is fuel-rich, fuel-lean, or stoichiometric.

Definition

Φ = (fuel/oxidizer)actual / (fuel/oxidizer)stoichiometric. A value of Φ = 1 indicates a stoichiometric mixture; Φ < 1 is fuel-lean (excess oxidizer), and Φ > 1 is fuel-rich (excess fuel). The inverse of the equivalence ratio is sometimes called the excess air ratio, lambda (λ).

Applications

Equivalence ratio governs flame temperature, pollutant formation, and engine performance. Gas turbine and internal combustion engines are typically run lean, while some industrial burners operate near stoichiometric conditions for maximum temperature.
