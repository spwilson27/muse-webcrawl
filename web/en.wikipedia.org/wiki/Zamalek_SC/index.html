Zamalek Sporting Club (Arabic: نادي الزمالك للألعاب الرياضية), commonly known as Zamalek, is an Egyptian sports club based in Giza, Greater Cairo. Founded in 1911, it is one of the most successful football clubs in Egypt and Africa, and shares an intense rivalry with Al Ahly, contested in the Cairo derby.

History

The club was founded on 5 January 1911 as Qasr El Nile Club, initially intended to be open to Egyptians and foreigners alike at a time when other clubs restricted membership. It took its present name from the Zamalek district of Cairo in 1952. Zamalek was the first Egyptian team to win the Egypt Cup (1922) and has won numerous league championships.

Honours

Zamalek has won the Egyptian Premier League more than a dozen times, the Egypt Cup over 25 times, and the CAF Champions League five times (1984, 1986, 1993, 1996, and 2002), as well as the CAF Confederation Cup. The club also fields successful teams in handball, basketball, and volleyball, making it one of the most decorated multi-sport clubs in Africa.
