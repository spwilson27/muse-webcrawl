Fastest animals

This is a list of the fastest animals in the world, grouped by type of locomotion. Speeds are maximum recorded or reliably estimated values.

Fastest land animal

The cheetah is the fastest land animal, capable of reaching 60–70 mph (97–113 km/h) in short bursts covering up to about 500 m, with exceptional acceleration.

Fastest bird

The peregrine falcon is the fastest member of the animal kingdom, reaching over 200 mph (320 km/h) during its hunting dive, the stoop. In level flight, the white-throated needletail and gyrfalcon are among the fastest.

Fastest fish

The Indo-Pacific sailfish is generally considered the fastest fish, with leaps recorded at about 68 mph (110 km/h), though black marlin have also been reported at comparable speeds.
