Lars Vogt

Lars Vogt (8 September 1970 – 5 September 2022) was a German classical pianist and conductor. He was music director of the Orchestre de chambre de Paris and of the Royal Northern Sinfonia, and a prominent chamber musician and recording artist.

Career

Vogt was born in Düren, Germany, and rose to prominence after winning second prize at the 1990 Leeds International Piano Competition. He performed with many of the world's leading orchestras, including the Berlin Philharmonic, and was known for his interpretations of Mozart, Beethoven, Brahms and Schumann. He founded the chamber music festival Spannungen in Heimbach, Germany, in 1998.

Conducting and death

Vogt became principal conductor and later music director of the Royal Northern Sinfonia in Gateshead in 2015, and music director of the Orchestre de chambre de Paris in 2020. He was diagnosed with cancer in 2021 and died in September 2022, three days before his 52nd birthday.
