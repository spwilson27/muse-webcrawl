Seal of Solomon

The Seal of Solomon is the signet ring attributed in legend to King Solomon of Israel. In medieval Jewish, Islamic and Christian occult traditions, the ring bore a symbol — commonly a pentagram or hexagram engraved with the name of God — that gave Solomon power over demons (jinn) and the ability to speak with animals.

Traditions and depiction

The tale is developed in the pseudepigraphal Testament of Solomon and in Islamic stories of Sulayman. In Arabic contexts the seal (khatam Sulayman) frequently takes the form of the hexagram, while Western grimoires often used a pentagram. The hexagram form of the Seal of Solomon is closely related to the Star of David and appears on the modern flag of Israel, as well as in the heraldry and coinage of several other states and cities.
