Hard Rock Stadium is a multi-purpose stadium located in Miami Gardens, Florida, United States. It is the home stadium of the Miami Dolphins of the National Football League and the Miami Hurricanes college football team.

History

The stadium opened in 1987 as Joe Robbie Stadium, named for the Dolphins' owner. It has carried several corporate names over the years, including Pro Player Stadium, Dolphin Stadium, Land Shark Stadium, and Sun Life Stadium, before Hard Rock Cafe acquired the naming rights in 2016.

Events

The stadium has hosted multiple Super Bowls, the Orange Bowl, WrestleMania XXVIII, and international football matches. A major renovation completed in 2016 added a canopy roof over the seating areas. The surrounding complex also hosts the Miami Open tennis tournament and the Miami International Autodrome, which stages the Miami Grand Prix in Formula One.
