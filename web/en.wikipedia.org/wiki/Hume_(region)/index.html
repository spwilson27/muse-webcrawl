Hume (region)

The Hume region is one of the nine regions of Victoria, Australia. Located in the northeastern part of the state, the region covers an area of about 40,677 square kilometres and had a population of about 300,000 at the 2021 census. It includes the major centres of Wodonga, Wangaratta, Shepparton and Benalla.

Geography

The region extends from the Murray River in the north to the Victorian Alps in the southeast, encompassing parts of the Goulburn Valley and the Ovens and King valleys. It borders New South Wales along the Murray River.

Economy

Agriculture, food processing, manufacturing and tourism are the principal industries. The region is known for fruit growing and dairy farming in the Goulburn Valley, and for alpine tourism around Mount Buller, Mount Hotham and Falls Creek.
