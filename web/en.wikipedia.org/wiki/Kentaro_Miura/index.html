Kentaro Miura

Kentaro Miura (Japanese: 三浦 建太郎, July 11, 1966 – May 6, 2021) was a Japanese manga artist, best known for his acclaimed dark fantasy series Berserk, which began serialization in 1989. The series is among the best-selling manga of all time, with over 60 million copies in circulation, and is noted for its highly detailed artwork, brutal medieval setting, and themes of fate, trauma, and perseverance.

Miura began drawing manga as a child and published his first work in a school magazine at age ten. He was influenced by artists such as Go Nagai and Buronson and Tetsuo Hara, and worked as an assistant to George Morikawa on Hajime no Ippo.

Death and legacy

Miura died of acute aortic dissection on May 6, 2021, at the age of 54. His death prompted tributes worldwide. Berserk resumed serialization in June 2022 under the supervision of his friend Kouji Mori, based on Miura's drafts and recollections.
