K League 2

The K League 2 (Korean: K리그2) is the second division of the South Korean professional football league system, below K League 1. It was founded in 2013 as the K League Challenge, when the K League split into two divisions, and was renamed K League 2 in 2018.

Clubs compete for promotion to K League 1, with the champions promoted automatically and further places decided through play-offs; there is no relegation below K League 2, though expansion clubs have joined from the semi-professional ranks under licensing rules.

Teams in the division have included Gimcheon Sangmu (the military team), Suwon FC, Busan IPark and Jeonnam Dragons.
