Timeline of United States history


Milestones in American history

This timeline of United States history summarizes major political, social, and military events from the first European colonization of North America through the founding of the United States and its growth into a world power.

Selected events

YearEvent
1607Jamestown founded, the first permanent English settlement in America.
1776Declaration of Independence adopted on 4 July.
1788The United States Constitution is ratified.
1803Louisiana Purchase doubles the territory of the United States.
1861–1865American Civil War; the Union is preserved and slavery abolished.
1869First transcontinental railroad completed.
1920Nineteenth Amendment grants women the right to vote.
1929Stock market crash begins the Great Depression.
1941–1945The United States fights in the Second World War.
1964Civil Rights Act outlaws major forms of discrimination.
1969Apollo 11 lands the first humans on the Moon.
2001September 11 attacks lead to the War on Terror.
