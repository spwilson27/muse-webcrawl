University of Texas System

The University of Texas System (UT System) is a public university system in Texas, headquartered in Austin. Established in 1881 with the University of Texas at Austin, it now includes eight academic universities and five health institutions, enrolling more than 240,000 students.

Governance and endowment

A nine-member Board of Regents, appointed by the governor, oversees the system. The UT System shares the Permanent University Fund, a sovereign wealth fund built largely on West Texas oil lands, with the Texas A&M University System; its endowment is among the largest in American higher education.

Institutions

Members include UT Austin, UT Dallas, UT Southwestern Medical Center, MD Anderson Cancer Center and UTHealth Houston.
