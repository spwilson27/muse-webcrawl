Medellín Cartel

The Medellín Cartel was a powerful Colombian drug cartel based in the city of Medellín. Founded and led by Pablo Escobar together with the Ochoa brothers and others, it dominated the cocaine trade in the late 1970s and 1980s, at its height supplying an estimated majority of the cocaine smuggled into the United States.

Operations

The organisation trafficked cocaine from Colombia through the Caribbean and Mexico into the US, generating billions of dollars. It used bribery ("plata") and extreme violence ("plomo") against officials, judges, journalists and rivals, including assassinations and bombings such as the destruction of Avianca Flight 203 in 1989.

Decline

A campaign by Colombian security forces, aided by the United States and by the rival Cali Cartel and the paramilitary group Los Pepes, dismantled the organisation. Escobar was killed by police in Medellín on 2 December 1993, effectively ending the cartel.
