University of Maryland, College Park

The University of Maryland, College Park (UMD) is a public land-grant research university in College Park, Maryland, and the flagship institution of the University System of Maryland. Founded in 1856 as the Maryland Agricultural College, it enrolls over 40,000 students.

Academics

UMD comprises more than a dozen colleges and schools, with noted programs in computer science, engineering, business, public policy and journalism, and close ties to federal agencies and laboratories near Washington, D.C.

Campus and athletics

The campus centers on McKeldin Mall. Its teams, the Maryland Terrapins, compete in the Big Ten Conference.
