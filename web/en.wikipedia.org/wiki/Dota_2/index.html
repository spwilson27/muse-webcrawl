Dota 2

Dota 2 is a multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) video game developed and published by Valve. Released in 2013, it is the sequel to Defense of the Ancients (DotA), a community-made mod for Warcraft III.

Gameplay

Two teams of five players occupy opposite bases (Radiant and Dire) on a square map. Each player controls a hero — over 120 are available, all free — with unique abilities, gaining gold and experience by killing creeps, heroes and neutral monsters. The objective is to destroy the opposing team's Ancient, a large structure in their base, defended by towers and waves of creeps along three lanes.

Esports

Dota 2 is famed for The International, inaugurated in 2011 with a then-record $1.6 million prize pool; crowdfunded editions later exceeded $40 million (2021), among the largest in esports. The game is free-to-play with revenue from cosmetics and the battle pass, and remains one of the most-played titles on Steam.
