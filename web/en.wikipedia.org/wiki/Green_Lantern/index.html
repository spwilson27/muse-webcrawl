Green Lantern

Green Lantern is the name of several superheroes appearing in DC Comics. Each wields a power ring that creates hard-light constructs fuelled by willpower, as a member of the intergalactic Green Lantern Corps policing 3,600 sectors of the universe for the Guardians of the Universe on Oa.

First appearanceAll-American Comics #16 (1940)
Created byBill Finger, Martin Nodell (Alan Scott); John Broome, Gil Kane (Hal Jordan)
Notable bearersAlan Scott, Hal Jordan, Guy Gardner, John Stewart, Kyle Rayner, Jessica Cruz
PowersPower ring constructs, flight, force fields

History

Golden Age Green Lantern Alan Scott (1940) had a magic ring. The Silver Age reboot (Showcase #22, 1959) introduced test pilot Hal Jordan, given a ring by dying alien Abin Sur. John Stewart became one of DC's first Black superheroes (1971); Kyle Rayner carried the 1990s; the Corps expanded in modern events.

In other media

John Stewart starred in the animated Justice League (2001–04); a 2011 film starred Ryan Reynolds as Hal Jordan. The oath begins: "In brightest day, in blackest night, no evil shall escape my sight…" See DC Comics.
