Marine Drive is a 3.6-kilometre-long promenade and six-lane concrete road along the Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose Road in South Mumbai, India. Built on reclaimed land along Back Bay, it sweeps in a C-shape from Nariman Point to Chowpatty Beach at the foot of Malabar Hill.

The Queen's Necklace

At night, the curve of streetlights along the bay resembles a string of pearls, earning Marine Drive the nickname "the Queen's Necklace". The promenade is a favourite spot for walking and watching the sunset over the Arabian Sea.

Art Deco buildings

The road is lined with Art Deco residential buildings erected in the 1930s and 1940s by wealthy Parsis and other Bombay merchants. In 2018 these buildings, together with the Victorian Gothic ensembles around the Oval Maidan, were inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Landmarks

Landmarks along the drive include the Wankhede Stadium, Nariman Point business district, the Taraporewala Aquarium and Girgaon Chowpatty, famous for its food stalls and Ganesh Chaturthi immersions.
