Manatee County, Florida

Manatee County is a county in the U.S. state of Florida, on the Gulf of Mexico coast south of Tampa Bay. Its county seat is Bradenton, its largest city. The county was created in 1855 and named for the Florida manatee, the sea cow found in its waters.

Geography

The county borders Tampa Bay and the Gulf of Mexico to the west, Hillsborough County to the north, and Sarasota County to the south. The Manatee River flows through Bradenton into Tampa Bay. Barrier islands including Anna Maria Island lie along its coast.

Population and economy

The population was about 400,000 at the 2020 census. The economy centres on tourism, agriculture — historically tomatoes and citrus — Port Manatee, and services for a large retiree population. Communities include Bradenton, Palmetto, Lakewood Ranch, Parrish, and Anna Maria Island towns.
