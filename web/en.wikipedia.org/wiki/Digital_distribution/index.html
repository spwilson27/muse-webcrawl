Digital distribution

Digital distribution, also referred to as content delivery or online distribution, is the delivery of media content such as audio, video, software and video games over the internet without the use of physical media such as paper, optical discs or videocassettes. The term is generally used to describe distribution over an online delivery medium, such as downloads or streaming.

History and impact

Digital distribution grew with broadband internet in the 2000s. Services such as iTunes (2003), Steam (2003) and Netflix streaming (2007) transformed the music, games and film industries, reducing manufacturing and retail costs while raising new issues concerning digital rights management, ownership versus licensing, and preservation of content when services close.
