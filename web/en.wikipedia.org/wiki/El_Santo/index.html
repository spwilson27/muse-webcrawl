El Santo

Rodolfo Guzmán Huerta (September 23, 1917 – February 5, 1984), better known as El Santo ("The Saint"), was a Mexican luchador (professional wrestler), film actor, and folk hero. He is one of the most famous and iconic figures in lucha libre, renowned for his silver mask, which he famously never removed in public.

Career

El Santo debuted in the 1930s and rose to fame in the 1940s and 1950s. He starred in more than 50 films, beginning in the late 1950s, in which the character battled monsters, vampires, and spies; these became cult classics across Latin America. A comic book series bearing his likeness ran for decades.

Legacy

El Santo was buried wearing his silver mask, and his son continued the legacy as El Hijo del Santo. He remains a symbol of justice and popular culture in Mexico.
