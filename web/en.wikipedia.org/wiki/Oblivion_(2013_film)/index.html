Oblivion is a 2013 American post-apocalyptic science fiction film directed by Joseph Kosinski and starring Tom Cruise. It is based on Kosinski's unpublished graphic novel of the same name. The film is set in 2077, decades after a war with alien invaders, the "Scavs", shattered the Moon and devastated Earth.

Plot

Jack Harper is a technician stationed on the ruined Earth to repair drones and protect giant fusion energy stations that harvest seawater for humanity's new home on Titan. Living in a sky tower with his communications partner Victoria, Jack has two weeks left before joining the rest of humanity — but the crash of a pre-war spacecraft carrying a survivor, Julia, and encounters with the resistance leader Malcolm Beech force him to question his memory-wiped past and the true nature of his mission. He ultimately learns that the "Tet" space station commanding him is the alien intelligence itself and sacrifices himself to destroy it.

Reception

The film grossed about $286 million worldwide and received mixed reviews, with praise for its visuals, production design and score, and criticism of its derivative story.
