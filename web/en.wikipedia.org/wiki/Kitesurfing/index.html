Kitesurfing

Kitesurfing or kiteboarding is a wind-powered water sport in which the rider on a board is pulled across water by a large controllable kite. Modern inflatable kites developed by the Legaignoux brothers and others in the 1980s-1990s made relaunching and safety practical, and the sport grew rapidly in the 2000s.

Riders use a harness, bar and lines to steer the kite, performing freestyle jumps, wave riding, racing and big-air tricks. Kitesurfing debuted as an Olympic discipline at Paris 2024 in Formula Kite. Safety systems allow depowering and quick release, though lessons are strongly recommended.
