Castlevania, known in Japan as Akumajō Dracula, is a gothic horror action-adventure video game series created by Konami. Debuting in 1986, the series follows the Belmont family of vampire hunters in their recurring battles against Count Dracula, who resurrects roughly once a century, and his castle, which changes form in each appearance.

Gameplay and history

The early games were linear action-platformers in which the hero wields the Vampire Killer whip through Dracula's castle. Castlevania: Symphony of the Night (1997), directed under Koji Igarashi's influence, redefined the series with non-linear exploration and RPG elements borrowed from Metroid, giving rise to the "Metroidvania" genre label. Major entries include Castlevania III: Dracula's Curse (1989), Super Castlevania IV (1991), Rondo of Blood (1993), Aria of Sorrow (2003) and the 3D reboot Lords of Shadow (2010).

Other media

The series inspired the acclaimed Netflix animated series Castlevania (2017–2021) and its sequel Castlevania: Nocturne (2023). With tens of millions of copies sold, Castlevania is one of Konami's flagship franchises and one of the most influential in video game history.
