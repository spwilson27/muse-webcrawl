The Staatsoper Unter den Linden (Berlin State Opera) is a historic opera house on the Unter den Linden boulevard in Berlin, Germany. It is home to the Berlin State Opera company and the Staatskapelle Berlin orchestra.

History

Commissioned by Frederick the Great of Prussia, the opera house was built between 1741 and 1743 to designs by Georg Wenzeslaus von Knobelsdorff, making it one of the oldest opera houses in Germany. It was destroyed by fire in 1843 and rebuilt, and was again heavily damaged during the Second World War before being reconstructed in the 1950s.

Modern era

The house underwent an extensive renovation completed in 2017, raising the ceiling to improve acoustics. Notable music directors have included Daniel Barenboim, who led the company for three decades.
