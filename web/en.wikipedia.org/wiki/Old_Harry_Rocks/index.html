Old Harry Rocks

Old Harry Rocks at Handfast Point, Dorset.

Old Harry Rocks are three chalk formations, including a stack and a stump, located at Handfast Point on the Isle of Purbeck in Dorset, southern England. They mark the eastern end of the Jurassic Coast, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Geology

The rocks formed from chalk that was once continuous with The Needles on the Isle of Wight; erosion by the sea cut through the headland, and continues to shape the stacks. One stack, Old Harry's Wife, collapsed into the sea in 1509's latter centuries of erosion, and the largest stack's arch fell in 1774.

Name

"Old Harry" is a nickname traditionally associated with the Devil, though a local legend connects the name to a notorious Poole pirate, Harry Paye.
