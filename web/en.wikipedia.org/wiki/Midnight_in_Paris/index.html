Midnight in Paris

Midnight in Paris is a 2011 fantasy comedy-drama film written and directed by Woody Allen. Set in Paris, it follows screenwriter Gil Pender (Owen Wilson), who on nightly walks at midnight is transported back to the 1920s.

Plot

Holidaying with his fiancée Inez and her parents, Gil wanders Paris at night and is picked up by a car that takes him to the Paris of F. Scott Fitzgerald, Ernest Hemingway, Gertrude Stein, Pablo Picasso and Salvador Dalí. There he falls for Adriana, who herself dreams of the Belle Époque, teaching him that nostalgia for an imagined golden age is itself an illusion.

Reception

The film premiered at Cannes in 2011, became Allen's highest-grossing film, and won the Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay.
