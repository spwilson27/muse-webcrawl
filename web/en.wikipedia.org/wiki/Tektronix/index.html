Tektronix, Inc., historically widely known as Tek, is an American company best known for manufacturing test and measurement devices such as oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, and video and mobile test protocol equipment. It is headquartered in Beaverton, Oregon, in the area known as the "Silicon Forest".

History

The company traces its roots to the electronics revolution that immediately followed World War II. It was founded in 1946 by Howard Vollum and Melvin Jack Murdock. Its first product, the Model 511 oscilloscope, established Tektronix's reputation for high-quality, high-bandwidth instruments. Through the 1960s–1980s Tektronix oscilloscopes became standard equipment in laboratories worldwide, and the company also produced computer terminals and workstations.

Later years

Tektronix was acquired by Danaher Corporation in 2007 and became part of Fortive when that company split off in 2016. In later years it operated as an operating company within Fortive's test and measurement segment, with a renewed focus on digital oscilloscopes, spectrum analyzers, and software-defined instrumentation.
