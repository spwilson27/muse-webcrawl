Project Management Professional

Project Management Professional (PMP) is a globally recognized professional certification for project managers offered by the Project Management Institute (PMI). First offered in 1984, it is widely considered the leading certification in project management, with well over one million holders worldwide.

Requirements

Candidates must meet education and experience requirements—typically a secondary degree with 60 months of project leadership experience, or a four-year degree with 36 months—plus 35 hours of project management training or a CAPM certification. They must then pass a 180-question examination covering people, process, and business environment domains, including agile and hybrid approaches.

Maintenance

Certification holders must earn 60 professional development units (PDUs) every three years to maintain the credential. The exam is based on the PMP Examination Content Outline rather than being solely tied to PMI's PMBOK Guide.
