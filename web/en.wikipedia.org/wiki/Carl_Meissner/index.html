Carl Meissner

Carl Daniel Friedrich Meissner (1 November 1800 – 2 May 1874) was a Swiss botanist. Born in Bern, he studied medicine and natural history at the University of Göttingen and became professor of botany at the University of Basel, where he taught for more than three decades.

Career

Meissner worked on the plant families Proteaceae, Polygonaceae, Lauraceae and Thymelaeaceae, and contributed treatments of Australian flora based on the collections of early colonial botanists. His major works include Plantarum vascularium genera (1836–1843) and contributions to de Candolle's Prodromus Systematis Naturalis Regni Vegetabilis and to Martius's Flora Brasiliensis.

Legacy

The standard author abbreviation Meisn. is used to indicate him as the author when citing a botanical name. The genus Meissneria was named in his honour.
