Jeff Bridges

Born: 4 December 1949, Los Angeles, California
Occupation: Actor, musician, producer
Years active: 1951–present

Jeffrey Leon Bridges (born 4 December 1949) is an American actor and musician. He comes from a prominent acting family — the son of Lloyd Bridges and brother of Beau Bridges — and is known for his leading roles in films across seven decades, including one of cinema's most enduring cult characters, "The Dude" in The Big Lebowski (1998). He won the Academy Award for Best Actor for Crazy Heart (2009).

Career

Bridges first appeared on screen as an infant and worked in television as a child alongside his father in Sea Hunt. His breakthrough came with Peter Bogdanovich's The Last Picture Show (1971), earning his first Oscar nomination. His notable films include Thunderbolt and Lightfoot (1974), King Kong (1976), Tron (1982), Starman (1984), The Fabulous Baker Boys (1989), The Fisher King (1991), Fearless (1993), The Big Lebowski (1998), The Contender (2000), Iron Man (2008), Crazy Heart (2009), the Coen brothers' True Grit (2010), and Hell or High Water (2016), receiving eight Academy Award nominations in total.

Music and other work

Bridges has released country music albums, including Jeff Bridges (2011), recorded with T Bone Burnett after Crazy Heart. He is also a photographer, painter, narrator, and a long-time campaigner against childhood hunger through the No Kid Hungry campaign.
