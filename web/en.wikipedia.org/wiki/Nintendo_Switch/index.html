The Nintendo Switch is a video game console developed by Nintendo and released worldwide on March 3, 2017. It is a hybrid console: it can be used as a home console connected to a television via a dock, as a handheld device with an integrated screen, or in tabletop mode. Its detachable controllers, the Joy-Con, can be used attached to the console, held separately in each hand, or combined in a grip.

Hardware

The console uses a custom Nvidia Tegra processor. The original model has a 6.2-inch capacitive touchscreen with 1280×720 resolution; games are distributed on proprietary game cards and via the Nintendo eShop. A revised model with improved battery life appeared in 2019, alongside the handheld-only Nintendo Switch Lite (2019) and the Nintendo Switch – OLED Model (2021) with a 7-inch OLED screen.

Software and services

Launch titles included The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild. The system's best sellers include Mario Kart 8 Deluxe, Animal Crossing: New Horizons, Super Smash Bros. Ultimate and Pokémon Sword and Shield. The Nintendo Switch Online subscription service provides online play and access to libraries of classic games. With over 140 million units sold, the Switch became Nintendo's best-selling home console and one of the best-selling consoles of all time.

Successor

Nintendo announced the successor console, the Nintendo Switch 2, in January 2025, with release in June 2025; it retains the hybrid design and backward compatibility with most Switch games.
