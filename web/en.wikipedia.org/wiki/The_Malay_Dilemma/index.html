The Malay Dilemma

The Malay Dilemma is a book written by Mahathir Mohamad, later Prime Minister of Malaysia, and published in 1970. Written after the 13 May 1969 racial riots in Kuala Lumpur and Mahathir's expulsion from the ruling UMNO party, the book analyses the economic and social position of the Malay community.

Argument

Mahathir argued that Malays were marginalized in their own country because of heredity and environment, colonial-era economic structures, and their own attitudes, and he advocateded affirmative action and "constructive protection" to help Malays compete economically with the Chinese Malaysian community. He also called for greater Malay political assertiveness.

Reception and influence

The book was initially banned in Malaysia, but its ideas strongly influenced the New Economic Policy introduced in 1971, with its system of ethnic preferences (bumiputera policies). The ban was lifted in 1981 when Mahathir became Prime Minister, and the book remains central to debates about Malaysian ethnic policy.
