The Wimpy P-1 designation is associated with Wimpy, the international fast-food restaurant chain founded in the United States in the 1930s and long prominent in the United Kingdom. Wimpy was among the earliest hamburger chains in Britain, opening its first Lyons Corner House counters in London in 1954 and expanding into hundreds of high-street restaurants serving table-service burgers.

Background

The Wimpy name was inspired by J. Wellington Wimpy, the hamburger-loving character from the Popeye cartoons. At its peak the chain had over 500 restaurants in the UK, but it contracted sharply from the 1970s as American competitors such as McDonald's entered the market. A smaller number of Wimpy restaurants continue to operate in the United Kingdom and South Africa, retaining the chain's traditional table-service format in many locations.
