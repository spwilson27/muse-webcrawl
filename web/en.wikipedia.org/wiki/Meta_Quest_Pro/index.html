The Meta Quest Pro is a mixed reality (MR) headset developed by Reality Labs, a division of Meta Platforms. Announced on 11 October 2022 at Meta Connect and released on 25 October 2022, it was positioned as a premium device for professionals and early adopters, introducing colour passthrough mixed reality, eye and face tracking, and pancake lenses to Meta's Quest line.

Meta Quest Pro headset and controllers

Hardware

The Quest Pro uses a Qualcomm Snapdragon XR2+ Gen 1 processor with 12 GB of RAM. Its optics use pancake lenses and quantum-dot LCD panels with local dimming, giving a slimmer visor than the Quest 2; the battery is built into the curved rear of the head strap to balance the device's 722 g weight. Ten sensors support inside-out tracking, high-resolution colour passthrough for mixed reality, and inward-facing cameras for eye tracking and facial-expression tracking used to animate avatars. Its Touch Pro controllers are self-tracking, each containing its own camera sensors and Snapdragon 662 processor, and can be used with other Quest headsets.

Reception and discontinuation

Reviews praised the displays, controllers, and build quality but criticised the high price, short battery life of roughly two hours, and the limited professional software available at launch. Meta cut the price to $999.99 in March 2023. In 2024 Meta ended production and sold through remaining stock, as the cheaper Quest 3 offered comparable mixed-reality features; Meta shifted its high-end strategy toward licensing its Horizon OS to third-party manufacturers.
