John Joseph Travolta (born 18 February 1954) is an American actor and singer. He became a cultural icon in the 1970s with the sitcom Welcome Back, Kotter (1975–1979) and the films Saturday Night Fever (1977) and Grease (1978), and enjoyed a major career resurgence with Pulp Fiction (1994).

Career

Travolta, the youngest of six children in a show-business family in New Jersey, dropped out of school at 16 to pursue acting in New York. His role as Vinnie Barbarino in Welcome Back, Kotter made him a teen idol, and his performance as Tony Manero in Saturday Night Fever earned an Academy Award nomination for Best Actor. Grease, opposite Olivia Newton-John, became one of the highest-grossing musicals of its era. After a slump in the 1980s, Quentin Tarantino cast him as hitman Vincent Vega in Pulp Fiction, earning a second Oscar nomination and restoring his leading-man status.

Subsequent successes included Get Shorty (1995), for which he won a Golden Globe, Face/Off (1997), Primary Colors (1998), the musical Hairspray (2007), and the role of Robert Shapiro in The People v. O. J. Simpson: American Crime Story (2016), which earned an Emmy nomination as actor and a win as producer.

Personal life

Travolta married actress Kelly Preston in 1991; she died in 2020. Their eldest son, Jett, died in 2009 at age 16. Travolta is a long-time member of the Church of Scientology and a licensed private pilot who owns several aircraft; his Florida home in Jumbolair Aviation Estates was designed with runways for his planes.
