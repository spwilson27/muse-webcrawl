The Librarians is an American fantasy-adventure television series developed by John Rogers, a spin-off of The Librarian film series starring Noah Wyle. It aired on TNT from December 7, 2014, to February 7, 2018, running for four seasons and 42 episodes.

Premise

The series centers on a new team of Librarians recruited by the ancient Library—a repository of magical artifacts—assisted by Guardian Eve Baird (Rebecca Romijn) and caretaker Jenkins (John Larroquette). The Librarians-in-training are Cassandra Cillian (Lindy Booth), a synesthete mathematician; Ezekiel Jones (John Harlan Kim), a master thief; and Jacob Stone (Christian Kane), an art and history expert. Together with Flynn Carsen (Noah Wyle), the original Librarian, they protect the world from magical threats, recover artifacts, and battle enemies such as the Serpent Brotherhood, Moriarty, and the Department of Statistical Anomalies.

Production

The series was produced by Electric Entertainment, Dean Devlin's production company, which also produced the original films. Bob Newhart and Jane Curtin reprised their film roles in guest appearances. TNT cancelled the series in March 2018 after four seasons. A sequel series, The Librarians: The Next Chapter, premiered in 2025.

Reception

The series received generally favorable reviews for its whimsical tone and ensemble chemistry, and was a solid ratings performer for TNT. It won a Saturn Award nomination and developed a devoted fan following.
