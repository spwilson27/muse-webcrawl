Willis Carrier

Willis Haviland Carrier (November 26, 1876 – October 7, 1950) was an American engineer, best known for inventing modern air conditioning.

Born: 1876, Angola, New York
Died: 1950, New York City
Known for: Modern air conditioning, Carrier Corporation

Invention

Working for Buffalo Forge in 1902, Carrier designed a system to control humidity for a Brooklyn printing plant — recognised as the first modern air conditioner. His 1906 apparatus for treating air and his rational psychrometric formulae (1911) founded air-conditioning engineering.

Company and legacy

In 1915 he co-founded Carrier Engineering Corporation. Air conditioning transformed manufacturing, cinemas, offices, and the growth of the American Sun Belt. See Air conditioning and Psychrometrics.
