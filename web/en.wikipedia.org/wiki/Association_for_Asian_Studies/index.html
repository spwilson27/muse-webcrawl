Association for Asian Studies (AAS)
Founded: 1941 (as Far Eastern Association)
Headquarters: Ann Arbor, Michigan
Publication: Journal of Asian Studies
Membership: ~6,500

The Association for Asian Studies (AAS) is a scholarly, non-profit professional association open to all persons interested in Asia and the study of Asia. Founded in 1941 as the Far Eastern Association, it took its present name in 1957 as its scope widened beyond the Far East to all of Asia.

Publications and meetings

The AAS publishes the Journal of Asian Studies, the field's flagship quarterly, along with the monograph series and the bibliographic resource Bibliography of Asian Studies. Its annual conference is among the largest gatherings of Asian studies scholars in the world.

Organisation

The association is governed by a board and area councils for China and Inner Asia, Japan, Korea, South Asia and Southeast Asia, reflecting the regional organisation of Asian studies as a field.
