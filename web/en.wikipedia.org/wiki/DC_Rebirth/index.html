DC Rebirth is a 2016 relaunch by DC Comics of its superhero line, intended to restore elements of the DC Universe that had been lost in the 2011 New 52 reboot — in particular legacy, long-term relationships and a sense of hope — while retaining much of the New 52's continuity. It began with the 80-page one-shot DC Universe: Rebirth #1, written by Geoff Johns.

Premise and storyline

The Rebirth special is narrated by Wally West, lost in the Speed Force, who reveals that ten years of history were stolen from the universe after Flashpoint — not by any DC villain, but, it is implied, by Doctor Manhattan of Watchmen. The integration of the Watchmen characters into the DC Universe was developed in the crossover "The Button" (2017) and the twelve-issue sequel Doomsday Clock (2017–2019) by Johns and Gary Frank.

Publishing changes

Under Rebirth, core titles such as Action Comics and Detective Comics returned to their original numbering, many series shipped twice monthly at a uniform $2.99 price, and creative teams re-emphasised classic characterisations: Superman was restored as married to Lois Lane with a son, Jon Kent; the original Wally West returned; and legacy heroes and the Justice Society were gradually reintroduced.

Reception and aftermath

Rebirth was a critical and commercial success, with the launch special reprinted repeatedly and DC's market share recovering strongly through 2016. The Rebirth branding was phased out in late 2017, with storylines continuing into subsequent initiatives including Dark Nights: Metal, the "New Justice" era, Infinite Frontier (2021) and Dawn of DC (2023).
