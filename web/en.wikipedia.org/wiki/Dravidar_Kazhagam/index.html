The Dravidar Kazhagam (DK; "Dravidian Federation") is a social and political movement in Tamil Nadu, India, founded by Periyar E. V. Ramasamy. It is the parent organisation of the modern Dravidian movement: not itself an electoral party, it campaigns for the abolition of caste, rationalism and atheism, women's rights, and the self-respect of the Dravidian peoples of South India.

Origins

The DK descends from the Justice Party (founded 1916), which represented non-Brahmin interests in the Madras Presidency. Periyar, who had launched the Self-Respect Movement in 1925, took over the Justice Party in 1938 and in 1944 renamed it the Dravidar Kazhagam, converting it from an electoral party into a revolutionary social movement demanding, at first, a separate Dravidian nation (Dravida Nadu). Its members adopted black shirts, and its propaganda — through journals such as Viduthalai — attacked caste, superstition and priestcraft.

Split and later history

In 1949 Periyar's marriage to Maniammai, decades his junior, and his decision to name her his successor provoked his lieutenant C. N. Annadurai to break away and found the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (DMK), which chose the electoral path the DK eschewed. The DK thereafter functioned as the ideological fountainhead of Dravidian politics: its campaigns included agitations against Hindi imposition, temple-entry and priesthood-for-all-castes campaigns, and rationalist propaganda tours. After Periyar's death in 1973, Maniammai led the organisation, followed by K. Veeramani, under whom it continues from its headquarters at Periyar Thidal in Chennai, running educational institutions and campaigning on social-justice issues while supporting Dravidian parties electorally.

Ideology

The DK's programme combines militant rationalism and atheism — symbolised by its black flag — with anti-caste activism, advocacy of self-respect marriages conducted without priests, Tamil pride, and opposition to Hindi imposition and to what it sees as North Indian and Brahminical dominance of Indian public life.
