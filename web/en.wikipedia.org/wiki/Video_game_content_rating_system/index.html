Video game content rating system

A video game content rating system assigns age and content ratings to video games to inform consumers, especially parents.

Systems

Major systems include ESRB in North America, PEGI in Europe, CERO in Japan, ACB in Australia, and USK in Germany. Ratings consider violence, language, sexual content, gambling, and drugs.

History

Systems emerged in the 1990s after controversies over games such as Mortal Kombat and Night Trap, leading to industry self-regulation.
