Elections in Hong Kong

Elections in Hong Kong take place for the Legislative Council (LegCo), the Chief Executive, District Councils, and village representatives. Hong Kong, a Special Administrative Region of China, has never had universal suffrage for all offices; the Chief Executive is chosen by an Election Committee.

Legislative Council

Following the 2021 electoral overhaul under the principle of "patriots administering Hong Kong", the 90-member LegCo comprises 40 members returned by the Election Committee, 30 by functional constituencies, and 20 by geographical constituencies through direct elections.

History

Limited elections began under British colonial rule in the nineteenth century, expanding in the 1980s and 1990s. The post-2019 reforms and the 2020 national security law significantly reshaped the political landscape and opposition participation.
