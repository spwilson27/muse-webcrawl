Fargo (TV series)

Fargo is an American black comedy-crime drama anthology television series created and principally written by Noah Hawley. It premiered on FX on April 15, 2014, and is inspired by the 1996 Coen brothers film of the same name, which serves as a shared-universe predecessor.

Premise

Each season is set in a different era and location — largely in Minnesota, North Dakota, South Dakota, Missouri and Kansas — with a new cast and a new tale of murder, deceit and "Minnesota nice" colliding with violence. Connections between seasons and to the film emerge through characters, locations such as Lou Solverson's family, and recurring motifs.

Seasons

Season 1 (2014), set in 2006 in Bemidji, Minnesota, stars Billy Bob Thornton as drifter Lorne Malvo and Martin Freeman as insurance salesman Lester Nygaard, with Allison Tolman as deputy Molly Solverson. Season 2 (2015), set in 1979, stars Kirsten Dunst, Patrick Wilson and Jesse Plemons. Season 3 (2017), set in 2010, stars Ewan McGregor in dual roles. Season 4 (2020), set in 1950 Kansas City, stars Chris Rock and Jason Schwartzman as rival crime bosses. Season 5 (2023–24), set in 2019, stars Juno Temple, Jon Hamm and Jennifer Jason Leigh.

Cast and characters

The anthology format allows acclaimed film actors to lead each installment. Recurring themes include polite Midwestern manners masking brutality, chance encounters, and moral consequences spiralling from a single dishonest act.

Reception

The series has won Primetime Emmy Awards including Outstanding Miniseries for its first season, along with Golden Globe Awards, and is consistently ranked among the best television series of the 2010s for its writing, direction, cinematography and performances.
