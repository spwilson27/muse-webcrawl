Dhaka Metro Rail

The Dhaka Metro Rail is a mass rapid transit system serving Dhaka, the capital city of Bangladesh. It is operated by the Dhaka Mass Transit Company Limited (DMTCL). The first line, MRT Line 6, opened on 28 December 2022, making it the first metro rail system in the country.

Network

The planned network comprises several lines under the MRT (Mass Rapid Transit) designation. Line 6 runs on an elevated viaduct from Uttara in the north to Motijheel, the commercial centre of the city, and is being extended further south. Additional lines, including Line 1 and Line 5, are under construction or planned, combining elevated and underground sections.

History

The project was developed with financial and technical assistance from the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA). Construction of Line 6 began in 2016. The system was introduced to relieve the severe traffic congestion for which Dhaka is notorious.

Rolling stock

Trains for Line 6 were built by Kawasaki Heavy Industries of Japan and run on standard-gauge elevated track, powered by overhead lines.
