Vale of Glamorgan

The Vale of Glamorgan (Welsh: Bro Morgannwg) is a county borough in Wales, bordering Cardiff to the east, Rhondda Cynon Taf to the north, Bridgend to the west, and the Bristol Channel to the south. With a population of about 132,000, its largest town and administrative centre is Barry.

Geography

The Vale is largely rural, a low plateau of fertile farmland falling to the dramatic Jurassic cliffs of the Glamorgan Heritage Coast. Other towns include Penarth, Dinas Powys, Llantwit Major, and Cowbridge, an affluent market town. The resort of Barry Island and the villages of the western Vale are popular with visitors from Cardiff and the South Wales Valleys.

History and economy

The area formed part of the historic county of Glamorgan. It contains important archaeological sites including the Tinkinswood burial chamber and St Lythans cromlech, and the remains of Roman villas. Cardiff Airport, Wales's main international airport, is located at Rhoose in the Vale. St Athan hosts a major RAF and defence site. The Vale is regarded as one of the most affluent areas of Wales, with many residents commuting to Cardiff.
