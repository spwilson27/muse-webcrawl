Chung Ling Soo

A theatrical poster for Chung Ling Soo

Chung Ling Soo was the stage name of the American magician William Ellsworth Robinson (April 2, 1861 – March 24, 1918). Robinson performed in the persona of a Chinese conjurer, wearing elaborate robes and speaking through an interpreter, and became one of the most famous magicians of the vaudeville era.

Career

Robinson began performing magic as a teenager and worked for magicians such as Harry Kellar and Alexander Herrmann before launching his own act. Around 1900 he adopted the Chung Ling Soo character — the name adapted from that of a genuine Chinese performer, Ching Ling Foo — and toured Europe to great success. His act included producing bowls of water and fish, firing ribbons and doves from empty containers, and other large-scale illusions presented in an "Oriental" style.

Death

Chung Ling Soo died performing the famous bullet catch trick at the Wood Green Empire theatre in London on March 23, 1918. The trick used a specially prepared rifle in which the marked bullet was secretly retained and a blank charge fired; years of wear had allowed a real bullet to enter the barrel. He was shot in the chest and died the following day, famously breaking character to say in English, "Oh my God. Something's happened. Lower the curtain." His true identity as an American became widely known only after his death.
