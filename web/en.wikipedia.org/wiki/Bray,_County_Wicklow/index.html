Bray, County Wicklow

Bray
Irish: Bré
County: Wicklow, Ireland
Population: about 33,000
Bray seafront and Bray Head

Bray (Irish: Bré) is a coastal town in north County Wicklow, Ireland, situated about 20 km south of Dublin on the Irish Sea. It is the largest town in the county, with a population of roughly 33,000, and forms part of the Dublin metropolitan area, connected to the capital by the DART suburban railway.

History and character

Bray developed in the nineteenth century as a seaside resort for Dubliners after the arrival of the railway in 1854, and its mile-long Victorian promenade remains its focal point. To the south, the hill of Bray Head rises 241 metres above the town; a cliff walk runs from Bray around the head to neighbouring Greystones. The town is associated with Ardmore Studios, long a centre of Irish film production, and with the author James Joyce, who lived briefly at Martello Terrace. The River Dargle flows through the town to the sea at Bray Harbour.
