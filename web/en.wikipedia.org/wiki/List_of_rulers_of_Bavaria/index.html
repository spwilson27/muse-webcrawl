List of rulers of Bavaria

Bavaria was ruled successively by dukes (from the 6th century), electors (from 1623) and kings (1806–1918). From 1180, when Emperor Frederick Barbarossa granted the duchy to Otto of Wittelsbach, the House of Wittelsbach ruled Bavaria almost without interruption for 738 years, until the revolution of November 1918 deposed King Ludwig III.
