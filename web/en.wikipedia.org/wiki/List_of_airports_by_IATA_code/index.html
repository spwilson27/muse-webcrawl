Codes beginning with I, J, O and Q, and certain letters such as X, Y and Z, also exist in large numbers (e.g. YVR Vancouver, YYZ Toronto, ZRH Zurich). Cities with multiple airports may also have a metropolitan area code, such as NYC, LON and PAR.
