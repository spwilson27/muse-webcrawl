Kansas City metropolitan area

The Kansas City metropolitan area is a bi-state metropolitan area anchored by Kansas City, Missouri, and straddling the Missouri–Kansas state line at the confluence of the Kansas and Missouri rivers. It comprises fifteen counties in Missouri and Kansas and had a population of about 2.2 million at the 2020 census, making it the second-largest metropolitan area in Missouri and the largest in Kansas.

Principal cities

The largest city is Kansas City, Missouri (population about 510,000), followed by Overland Park, Kansas; Kansas City, Kansas; Olathe; Independence, Missouri; and Lee's Summit. Other major suburbs include Shawnee, Lenexa, Leawood, Liberty and Blue Springs.

Economy

The region is a major transportation and logistics hub owing to its central location and rail and river traffic, and has significant employment in telecommunications, animal health and life sciences (the "Animal Health Corridor"), finance, and engineering. It is home to companies such as Cerner (now Oracle Health), H&R Block, Hallmark Cards and Garmin. Professional sports teams include the Kansas City Chiefs (NFL), Kansas City Royals (MLB) and Sporting Kansas City (MLS).
