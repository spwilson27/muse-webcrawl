Jerry Brown

Edmund Gerald Brown Jr. (born April 7, 1938) is an American politician who served as the 34th and 39th governor of California, an unprecedented four terms spanning 1975–1983 and 2011–2019.

Career

The son of Governor Pat Brown, Jerry Brown served as California secretary of state and later mayor of Oakland (1999–2007) and state attorney general (2007–2011). He also ran for the Democratic presidential nomination in 1976, 1980, and 1992.

Governorships

His first governorship was known for fiscal restraint and environmental policy; his second for managing California's budget after the Great Recession and for ambitious climate policies. He is the longest-serving governor in California history. See also Governor of California.
