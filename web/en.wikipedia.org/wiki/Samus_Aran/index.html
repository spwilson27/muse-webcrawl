Samus Aran

First appearance: Metroid (1986)

Created by: Makoto Kano; designed by Hiroji Kiyotake

Species: Human infused with Chozo and Metroid DNA

Occupation: Bounty hunter; former Galactic Federation soldier

Samus Aran (Japanese: サムス・アラン) is the protagonist of the Metroid science fiction action-adventure game series by Nintendo. An ex-soldier of the Galactic Federation, she works as a bounty hunter wearing the Power Suit, a powered exoskeleton built by the Chozo that grants her superhuman abilities, including the Arm Cannon and the ability to morph into a compact ball.

Character biography

Samus was born on the mining colony K-2L. When Space Pirates led by Ridley destroyed the colony and killed her parents, the bird-like Chozo rescued the young Samus and raised her on planet Zebes, infusing her with Chozo DNA. After serving in the Federation army, she struck out on her own, undertaking missions against the Space Pirates and the parasitic Metroids. In Metroid Fusion, she was infused with Metroid DNA to survive an X parasite infection.

Reception

The original 1986 Metroid famously concealed Samus's gender until the ending, making her one of the earliest prominent female protagonists in video games. She is regarded as one of gaming's most iconic characters and also appears as a fighter in the Super Smash Bros. series.
