Poultry

Chickens are by far the most numerous poultry species.

Poultry are domesticated birds kept by humans for their eggs, meat, feathers or as pets. The category chiefly comprises chickens, ducks, turkeys, geese and guinea fowl, and may also include pigeons (squabs), quail, pheasants, ostriches and emus. Chickens descend mainly from the red junglefowl of Southeast Asia, domesticated several thousand years ago; ducks from the mallard and Muscovy, turkeys from the wild turkey of North America, and geese from the greylag and swan goose.

Production

Poultry meat is the most widely consumed meat in the world by volume, with global production exceeding 140 million tonnes annually, and eggs are produced in quantities of over 80 million tonnes. Production systems range from backyard flocks and free-range farming to large-scale intensive operations: broiler chickens are raised to slaughter weight in as little as five to seven weeks, while laying hens are kept in battery cages, enriched colonies, barns or free-range systems depending on local regulation and market. Major producing countries include the United States, China, Brazil and the European Union states.

Health and welfare

Intensive poultry farming raises concerns about animal welfare, antibiotic use and disease. Poultry are susceptible to avian influenza, some strains of which (such as H5N1) can infect humans and have caused large culls; other important diseases include Newcastle disease, infectious bursal disease and salmonella contamination of eggs and meat. Selective breeding for rapid growth in broilers has been associated with leg disorders and metabolic disease, prompting welfare standards and slower-growing breeds in some markets.

Terminology and culture

Young chickens are chicks; a male is a cockerel or rooster and a female a hen. Poultry feature in cuisines worldwide — roast chicken, Peking duck and turkey at Thanksgiving and Christmas are familiar examples — and in idioms and folklore. Keeping hens in gardens and allotments has become popular in many countries.
