Neste

Neste Oyj is an oil refining and marketing company located in Espoo, Finland. It produces, refines and markets oil products and engineering services, as well as licensing production technologies. Neste has operations in 14 countries and is the world's largest producer of renewable diesel and sustainable aviation fuel refined from waste and residue raw materials.

History

Neste was founded in 1948 as Finland's state oil company, charged with securing the country's oil supply. Its name comes from the Finnish word neste, meaning "liquid". The first refinery at Naantali began operating in 1957, followed by the much larger Porvoo refinery in the 1960s. In the 1990s Neste merged with the power company Imatran Voima to form Fortum, but the oil business was spun off again in 2005 and listed as Neste Oil; the company reverted to the name Neste in 2015.

Renewable products

Neste pioneered hydrotreated vegetable oil (HVO) renewable diesel, marketed as Neste MY Renewable Diesel, produced using its proprietary NEXBTL technology. Its renewable refineries are located in Porvoo, Rotterdam, and Singapore, with a joint-venture plant in Martinez, California. The company also produces sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) and renewable feedstocks for the polymers and chemicals industry.

Oil products

The traditional business refines crude oil at Porvoo into gasoline, diesel, jet fuel, marine fuels, bitumen, solvents, and lubricant base oils. Neste also operates a network of service stations in Finland and the Baltic states, though it has divested station networks elsewhere.

Sustainability debate

Neste's use of palm oil as a feedstock has drawn criticism from environmental groups concerned about deforestation; the company says it uses only certified, traceable palm oil and increasingly relies on wastes and residues such as used cooking oil and animal fat tallow.
