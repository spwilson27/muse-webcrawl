List of traditional star names

This is a list of traditional star names — proper names historically used for individual stars, many of them derived from Arabic, Greek and Latin through medieval astronomy. In 2016 the International Astronomical Union (IAU) established a Working Group on Star Names to standardise proper names for stars.

History

Most traditional names were transmitted through medieval Arabic astronomical works, which themselves preserved and renamed stars catalogued by Ptolemy in the Almagest. Some names, such as those of the stars of the Plough (Dubhe, Merak, Phecda, Megrez, Alioth, Mizar and Alkaid), reflect this heritage.
