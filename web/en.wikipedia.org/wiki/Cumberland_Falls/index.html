Cumberland Falls

Cumberland Falls, sometimes called the Little Niagara, the Niagara of the South, or the Great Falls, is a waterfall on the Cumberland River in southeastern Kentucky, United States.

Cumberland Falls

Location: McCreary / Whitley counties, Kentucky
Height: 68 ft (21 m)
Width: 125 ft (38 m)

Description

The falls are 68 feet high and 125 feet wide, with an average flow of about 3,600 cubic feet per second. They lie within Cumberland Falls State Resort Park and the Daniel Boone National Forest.

Moonbow

Cumberland Falls is famous for its moonbow — a lunar rainbow produced by the mist on clear nights around a full moon — one of the few places in the Western Hemisphere where moonbows occur regularly.

History

The area was sacred to Native American peoples for thousands of years. Efforts to develop hydroelectric power in the 1920s were defeated, and the falls were preserved as a state park.
