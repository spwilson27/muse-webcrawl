More4

More4 is a British free-to-air television channel owned by Channel Four Television Corporation. The channel launched on 10 October 2005 as a sister channel to Channel 4, originally aimed at an older, middle-class audience with documentaries, arts, drama and current affairs programming.

History

More4 launched with a schedule built around repeats and spin-offs of Channel 4 programmes, documentaries such as The Last Word, and acquired American drama including The West Wing and ER. Its news programme, More4 News, ran from 2005 until 2009.

In 2012 the channel was repositioned with a greater emphasis on lifestyle programming — property, cooking and gardening shows such as Grand Designs, Location, Location, Location and Come Dine with Me repeats — under the slogan "the home of more". More4 subsequently became a home for foreign-language drama under the Walter Presents strand and for long-running formats.

Availability

More4 is available on Freeview, Freesat, Sky, Virgin Media and Channel 4's streaming service. A one-hour timeshift channel, More4 +1, is also broadcast. Its programming reaches Ireland through cable and satellite carriage.
