Friendship Games

The Friendship Games (Russian: Дружба-84, Druzhba-84) were a multi-sport event held in the summer of 1984 in the Soviet Union and other Eastern Bloc countries, organized as an alternative to the 1984 Summer Olympics in Los Angeles, which the Soviet Union and its allies boycotted.

Background

The Soviet-led boycott of the Los Angeles Games was announced in May 1984, officially citing safety concerns and alleged anti-Soviet sentiment, though it was widely seen as retaliation for the American-led boycott of the 1980 Moscow Olympics. The Eastern Bloc nations then staged their own competitions under the motto "Friendship-84".

Events

Competitions were spread across venues in the Soviet Union, Poland, East Germany, Czechoslovakia, Hungary, Bulgaria, Cuba, Mongolia and North Korea between July and September 1984. In several sports, such as athletics, swimming and weightlifting, the marks set exceeded those recorded at Los Angeles, although in team sports the fields were incomplete.

Legacy

The Friendship Games were not repeated; the Eastern Bloc returned to Olympic competition at Seoul in 1988. The event remains a notable episode in the politics of Cold War sport.
