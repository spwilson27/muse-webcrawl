The Young Turks

The Young Turks (TYT) is an American progressive news commentary and media network founded by Cenk Uygur in 2002. Named after the early-20th-century Ottoman reform movement, whose name became a generic term for young insurgents challenging an establishment, it began as a radio show before becoming one of the first and largest news shows on YouTube.

History

Uygur, a former lawyer and broadcaster, launched TYT on satellite radio in 2002; the show moved to video online in 2005 and grew with YouTube, becoming the platform's most-subscribed news channel, with billions of views. Ana Kasparian joined as a producer and became the show's longtime co-host. TYT briefly aired on Current TV (2011–2013). The network expanded into a multi-channel operation with entertainment, sports and investigative verticals, operated a membership model, and in 2017 raised venture funding to expand; ventures such as TYT Investigates produced documentary reporting. The network's shows covered the 2016 and subsequent U.S. elections extensively, sometimes controversially, and TYT anchors founded the Justice Democrats political action committee in 2017.

Format

The flagship show mixes news segments, interviews and outspoken progressive commentary, and is distributed live and on demand through YouTube and its own platform. Uygur and Kasparian's candid, unscripted style helped define the genre of online political commentary.
