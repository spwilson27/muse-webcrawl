Integrated care

Integrated care is an approach to health and social care that seeks to coordinate services around the needs of the individual, joining up primary, community, hospital and social care so that patients experience continuous rather than fragmented care.

Models

Integration can be organisational (merging providers), functional (shared records and back-office systems), clinical (shared care pathways and multidisciplinary teams) or normative (shared values and culture). In England, integrated care systems were placed on a statutory footing by the Health and Care Act 2022.

Evidence

Integrated care programmes aim to improve outcomes for people with chronic conditions and frailty, reduce avoidable hospital admissions and improve patient experience, though evaluations show mixed results depending on design and context.
