Tonatiuh

Tonatiuh is the Aztec sun deity, associated in Aztec mythology with the Fifth Sun, the current era. Depicted at the centre of the Aztec calendar stone with a protruding tongue representing a sacrificial knife, Tonatiuh was believed to require nourishment through human sacrifice to continue moving across the sky.

He was a god of warriors and the sun's daily journey and nightly struggle. The tonatiuhichan was the paradise reserved for warriors who died in battle.
