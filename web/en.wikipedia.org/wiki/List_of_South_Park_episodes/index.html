List of South Park episodes

South Park is an American animated sitcom created by Trey Parker and Matt Stone for Comedy Central. It premiered on August 13, 1997, with "Cartman Gets an Anal Probe" and has run for more than 25 seasons and over 320 episodes, along with several television specials and a feature film, South Park: Bigger, Longer & Uncut (1999). The series follows Stan Marsh, Kyle Broflovski, Eric Cartman, and Kenny McCormick in the fictional Colorado town of South Park.

The show is famous for being produced in under a week per episode, enabling topical satire.

Notable episodes

  • "Scott Tenorman Must Die" (2001) — Cartman's infamous revenge story.
  • "Make Love, Not Warcraft" (2006) — Emmy-winning episode made with Blizzard's cooperation.
  • "Imaginationland" trilogy (2007) — Emmy-winning three-parter.
  • "Trapped in the Closet" (2005) — satire of Scientology; associated with Isaac Hayes's departure.
  • "200" and "201" (2010) — anniversary episodes heavily censored over depictions of Muhammad.
  • "The Pandemic Special" (2020) — first of several Paramount+ specials.

Production

Since the show's early seasons, episodes have famously been written, animated, and aired within about six days, a process documented in 6 Days to Air (2011).
