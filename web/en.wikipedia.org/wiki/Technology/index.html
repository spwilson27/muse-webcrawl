Technology

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Technology is the application of conceptual knowledge to achieve practical goals, especially in a reproducible way. The word, from Greek techne (art, craft) and logos, can refer to tangible tools such as machinery and utensils, or intangible systems such as Software and methods. Technology plays a critical role in Science, engineering, medicine and everyday life, and its history stretches from stone tools to the internet.

Contents1. Themes
2. Philosophy and society

Themes

Major areas include information technology, biotechnology, energy, transport, materials and manufacturing. New technologies bring benefits and risks: printing, electricity and antibiotics transformed societies, while automation displaces some kinds of work and digital technology raises questions of privacy and attention. See also {W('History_of_technology')}.

Philosophy and society

Thinkers debate whether technology is neutral or value-laden, autonomous or socially shaped. Terms such as 'appropriate technology' and movements for sustainability argue that technological choices should serve human and environmental goals.
