Doreen Valiente
Born: 4 January 1922, Mitcham, Surrey
Died: 1 September 1999, Brighton
Occupation: Author, Wiccan high priestess
Known for: Rewriting Gardnerian Book of Shadows material; 'Charge of the Goddess'

Doreen Edith Dominy Valiente (1922–1999) was an English author and poet, often called the 'mother of modern witchcraft' for her central role in early Wicca.

Involvement with Gardner

Initiated in 1953 by Gerald Gardner into the Bricket Wood coven, Valiente became his high priestess and helped revise his Book of Shadows—removing much material borrowed from Aleister Crowley and composing beloved texts including the prose version of the Charge of the Goddess.

Later work

After a split with Gardner in 1957, she worked with Robert Cochrane's tradition and became a public advocate and historian of witchcraft through books such as Where Witchcraft Lives (1962), An ABC of Witchcraft (1973), and The Rebirth of Witchcraft (1989). Her magical artefacts are preserved by the Doreen Valiente Foundation.
