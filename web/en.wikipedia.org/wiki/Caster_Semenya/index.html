Caster Semenya

Mokgadi Caster Semenya OIB (born 7 January 1991) is a South African middle-distance runner and winner of two Olympic gold medals and three World Championships in the women's 800 metres. She first won gold at the 2009 World Championships with a personal best of 1:55.45, and went on to win Olympic titles at London 2012 and Rio 2016.

Career

Born in Ga-Masehlong, Limpopo, Semenya rose rapidly in 2009, winning the African Junior Championships and then the World title in Berlin. She defended her Olympic title in Rio and won a third world title in London in 2017, also taking bronze in the 1500 metres.

Eligibility controversy

Semenya has a difference of sex development (DSD): she has XY chromosomes and naturally elevated testosterone due to 5α-reductase deficiency. Following her 2009 victory she was subjected to sex verification testing, prompting worldwide debate. In 2018 World Athletics introduced regulations requiring athletes with certain DSDs to reduce testosterone to compete in events from 400 m to the mile; the Court of Arbitration for Sport upheld the rules in 2019, and Semenya's legal challenges in Swiss courts and at the European Court of Human Rights followed, the latter ruling in 2023 that she had been discriminated against and denied a fair hearing. Barred from her main events, she turned to longer distances and the 200 metres.

Personal life

Semenya married Violet Raseboya in 2017; they have children. She has studied sports science and worked as a coach.
