Genderqueer

ClassificationGender identity
Related termNon-binary
Flag coloursLavender, white, green

Genderqueer is an umbrella term for gender identities that are not solely male or female — identities that are outside the gender binary. The term emerged in queer and activist circles in the 1990s and is often used interchangeably with non-binary, though some people distinguish between the two, with genderqueer carrying a connotation of deliberately queering or transgressing gender norms.

Identity and expression

Genderqueer people may identify as having an identity that is a blend of masculine and feminine, as neither, as moving between genders (genderfluid), or as a third or otherwise distinct gender. Gender identity under this umbrella is distinct from sexual orientation. Many genderqueer people use gender-neutral pronouns such as singular they, though pronoun use varies individually.

History

The word appeared in print in the mid-1990s, notably in the work of writer and activist Riki Wilchins, and gained wider use through online communities in the 2000s. The genderqueer pride flag — lavender, white and dark chartreuse green stripes — was created by Marilyn Roxie in 2011. During the 2010s, non-binary became the more common umbrella term in surveys and official contexts, while genderqueer remains in use as a personal identity label.
