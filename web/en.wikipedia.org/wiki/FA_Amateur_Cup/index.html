The FA Amateur Cup was an English football competition for amateur clubs, organised by the Football Association from 1893 until 1974. It was created after the legalisation of professionalism, when leading amateur clubs demanded a national cup of their own.

History

The first winners, in 1894, were Old Carthusians. Finals were held at various grounds before settling at Wembley Stadium, where they drew large crowds; the 1949 final between Bromley and Romford attracted around 90,000 spectators. Famous winners included Bishop Auckland, Wycombe Wanderers, Pegasus and Enfield.

Abolition

The competition ended when the FA abolished the official distinction between amateur and professional players in 1974. Its role was effectively succeeded by the FA Trophy for semi-professional clubs and the FA Vase for lower-level amateur sides.
