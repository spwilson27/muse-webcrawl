Newman's Own is an American food company founded in 1982 by actor Paul Newman and author A. E. Hotchner. The company produces salad dressings, pasta sauces, frozen pizza, lemonade, snacks, and other foods, each package bearing Newman's image in humorous costume. From the start, Newman pledged that all after-tax profits would go to charity; the company and its foundation have donated more than $600 million to charitable causes.

History

The venture began with Newman's homemade salad dressing, which he and Hotchner bottled as Christmas gifts in 1980; encouraged by the response, they launched it commercially in 1982, famously spending their own money on the first production run. The dressing was an immediate success, and the line expanded to pasta sauce, salsa, lemonade, popcorn, frozen pizza, cookies, and pet food. Newman's original arrangement — "all profits to charity" — was unusual and at times contested by tax authorities, but it became the brand's core identity, summarized by its joking motto about "shameless exploitation in pursuit of the common good."

Charitable work

Profits flow through Newman's Own Foundation, which supports causes including nutrition for underserved children, programs for veterans, and the SeriousFun Children's Network of camps for children with serious illnesses, founded by Newman in 1988 with the Hole in the Wall Gang Camp in Connecticut. After Newman's death in 2008, governance disputes between the foundation and his family over control of the brand were eventually settled, and in 2022 the company was converted so that it is wholly owned by the foundation, formalizing the give-away model.
