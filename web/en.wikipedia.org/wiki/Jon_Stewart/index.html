Jon Stewart

Jon Stewart (born Jonathan Stuart Leibowitz, November 28, 1962) is an American comedian, writer, producer, director, political commentator, actor, and television host. He is best known as the host of The Daily Show, the satirical news programme on Comedy Central, from 1999 to 2015, a tenure during which the show became one of the most influential voices in American political comedy and media criticism.

Early life

Stewart was born in New York City and raised in Lawrenceville, New Jersey. His father was a physics professor and his mother an educational consultant. He attended the College of William & Mary in Virginia, graduating in 1984 with a degree in psychology, and played on the university's soccer team. Before breaking into comedy he worked a variety of jobs, including as a bartender, puppeteer, and busboy.

Career

Stewart began performing stand-up comedy in New York in the late 1980s. He hosted Short Attention Span Theater on Comedy Central and The Jon Stewart Show on MTV (1993–1995), one of the network's first talk shows. After a period of acting roles — including in the films Half Baked (1998) and Big Daddy (1999) — he succeeded Craig Kilborn as host of The Daily Show in 1999.

Under Stewart, The Daily Show sharpened its focus on politics and the news media itself, covering elections with its "Indecision" specials and winning numerous Emmy and Peabody Awards. Stewart's 2004 appearance on CNN's Crossfire, in which he criticised the programme's hosts for partisan "hackery", became a widely discussed moment in American media culture. He co-authored the bestselling books America (The Book) (2004) and Earth (The Book) (2010).

Later work and advocacy

Stewart stepped down from The Daily Show in 2015 and directed the film Irresistible (2020). He hosted The Problem with Jon Stewart on Apple TV+ (2021–2023) and returned to The Daily Show in 2024 as a part-time host. Away from comedy he has been a prominent advocate for veterans and for first responders, campaigning successfully for the permanent reauthorisation of the 9/11 Victim Compensation Fund and for legislation supporting veterans exposed to toxic burn pits.
