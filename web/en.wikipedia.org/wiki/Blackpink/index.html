Blackpink (Korean: 블랙핑크; stylised in all caps or as BLΛƆKPIИK) is a South Korean girl group formed by YG Entertainment. The group consists of four members: Jisoo, Jennie, Rosé, and Lisa. They debuted in August 2016 with the single album Square One, whose tracks "Whistle" and "Boombayah" became number-one hits in South Korea and on Billboard's World Digital Songs chart.

Career

Blackpink rose rapidly to international prominence with songs including "Ddu-Du Ddu-Du" (2018), whose video became the most-viewed by a K-pop group on YouTube, and "Kill This Love" (2019). Their first full-length album, The Album (2020), was the first by a Korean girl group to sell over a million copies, and their second album, Born Pink (2022), topped the Billboard 200 — the first album by a Korean girl group to do so. In 2019 they became the first K-pop girl group to perform at Coachella, and in 2023 the first Asian act to headline the festival.

Records and influence

Blackpink are among the most-followed music acts on YouTube and were the most-subscribed group on the platform. Their Born Pink World Tour became the highest-grossing concert tour by a girl group. The members are also global ambassadors for luxury fashion houses, and in 2022 Time named Blackpink its Entertainer of the Year. In late 2023 the members renewed their group contract with YG Entertainment while establishing individual labels for their solo activities.
