Khnumhotep II

Title: Nomarch of the Oryx nome
Period: 12th Dynasty, reigns of Amenemhat II & Senusret II
Tomb: Beni Hasan, BH3

Khnumhotep II was an ancient Egyptian official of the 12th Dynasty who served as nomarch (governor) of the Oryx nome in Middle Egypt under Amenemhat II and Senusret II. He is best known for his richly decorated rock tomb at Beni Hasan (tomb BH3).

Tomb

The tomb chapel features celebrated paintings, including a biographical inscription of 222 columns recounting his career and family, and the famous scene of a caravan of 37 Asiatics (Aamu) bringing eye-paint to Egypt — often discussed in connection with early Levantine migration into Egypt and compared with biblical narratives of Semitic groups entering the Nile valley.

Family

Khnumhotep came from a powerful family of nomarchs at Menat Khufu; his grandfather Khnumhotep I was appointed by Amenemhat I. His wife was Khety, and his son Nakht also held office.
