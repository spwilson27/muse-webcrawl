Banzuke

A banzuke (Japanese: 番付), officially banzuke-hyō (番付表), is the ranking document published before each professional sumo tournament (honbasho) in Japan. It lists every wrestler in the ranking hierarchy — from the top-division yokozuna down to the newest recruits in jonokuchi — together with their stable, and is issued by the Japan Sumo Association about two weeks before a tournament begins.

Format and production

The banzuke is printed on a single large sheet of washi paper in a distinctive hand-drawn calligraphic style produced by specialist gyōji (referees) trained in sumō-ji, a fat, rounded script designed so that no white space remains, for good luck. Wrestlers are divided into East (right side) and West (left side) columns. In the top division the ranking runs yokozuna, ōzeki, sekiwake, komusubi and then the numbered maegashira ranks. The full banzuke covers all six professional divisions, though the lower divisions are printed in progressively smaller type.

Ranking decisions

Rankings are determined by a committee of shimpan (judges) after each tournament, based on wrestlers' win–loss records: a majority of wins (kachi-koshi) earns promotion and a majority of losses (make-koshi) demotion, with the size of the move reflecting the score. The banzuke also functions as the official programme and a popular souvenir at tournaments.
