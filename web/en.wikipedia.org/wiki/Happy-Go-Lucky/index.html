Happy-Go-Lucky

Happy-Go-Lucky is a 2008 British comedy-drama film written and directed by Mike Leigh. The film stars Sally Hawkins as Poppy, an irrepressibly cheerful primary-school teacher in North London, alongside Eddie Marsan as Scott, her volatile driving instructor.

Plot

Poppy Cross is a thirty-year-old teacher who approaches life with unfailing optimism. When her bicycle is stolen, she takes driving lessons instead, bringing her into weekly contact with Scott, whose rigid, paranoid temperament clashes sharply with hers. The film follows Poppy's work, flatshare with her friend Zoe, flamenco classes, and encounters that test — but never break — her sunny outlook.

Production and reception

As with Leigh's other films, the script was developed through months of improvisation and rehearsal with the cast. The film premiered at the Berlin International Film Festival in 2008, where Hawkins won the Silver Bear for Best Actress. She went on to win the Golden Globe Award for Best Actress in a Comedy or Musical. Critics praised Hawkins's performance and debated whether Poppy's optimism was inspiring or infuriating — a response Leigh said he intended.
