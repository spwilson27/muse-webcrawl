China Petroleum & Chemical Corporation, commonly known as Sinopec (Chinese: 中国石化; pinyin: Zhōngguó Shíhuà), is a Chinese oil and gas enterprise based in Beijing. It is listed in Hong Kong and also in Shanghai, and is one of the largest integrated energy and chemical companies in the world by revenue.

History

Sinopec was created in its modern form in 1998, when the Chinese government restructured the state petroleum industry along regional lines. China Petrochemical Corporation (the Sinopec Group) took over most refining and distribution assets in southern and eastern China, while PetroChina, the upstream arm of CNPC, took most oil and gas production in the north and west. In 2000 the operating assets were injected into the listed subsidiary, China Petroleum & Chemical Corporation, which completed one of the largest initial public offerings of that period.

Operations

Sinopec's businesses include oil and gas exploration and production, petroleum refining, petrochemical production and marketing, and the sale of petroleum products through one of the largest fuel retail networks in the world, with tens of thousands of service stations in China. It operates major refineries including Zhenhai and Maoming, and large ethylene and aromatics complexes. Its fuel retail and convenience-store business operates under brands such as Easy Joy.
