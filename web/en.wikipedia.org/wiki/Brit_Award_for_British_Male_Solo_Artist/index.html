Brit Award for British Male Solo Artist

The Brit Award for British Male Solo Artist was an award given by the British Phonographic Industry (BPI), an organisation representing record companies and artists in the United Kingdom, at the annual Brit Awards ceremony. It recognised the best British male solo artist of the previous year.

History

The category dated from the first Brit Awards in 1977, when it was won by Cliff Richard. Notable repeat winners included Paul Weller, Robbie Williams — the most successful winner, with four awards — Ed Sheeran, David Bowie and Stormzy. The last recipient was Harry Styles in 2021.

Ahead of the 2022 ceremony, the Brit Awards abolished the separate male and female solo categories, replacing them with a single gender-neutral award for British Artist of the Year, a move prompted by debate about how gendered categories treated non-binary artists such as Sam Smith.

Selected winners

  • 1977: Cliff Richard
  • 1983: Paul McCartney
  • 1991: Elton John
  • 1999, 2001, 2002, 2005: Robbie Williams
  • 2014: David Bowie
  • 2015, 2018: Ed Sheeran (2018) and Stormzy (2019, 2020)
  • 2021: J Hus
  • 2022 onwards: replaced by Artist of the Year

(Harry Styles won the combined Artist of the Year award in 2023, sweeping all four categories for which he was nominated.)
