The Prosperity Party (Amharic: ብልጽግና ፓርቲ, Biltsiginna Parti; Oromo: Paartii Badhaadhiinaa) is a political party in Ethiopia that was established on 1 December 2019 as a successor to the Ethiopian People's Revolutionary Democratic Front (EPRDF) by incumbent Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed.

The unification of the EPRDF and several other regional parties into a single national party was controversial among Ethiopian politicians, particularly the Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF), which refused to join and was removed from federal government as a result.

History

Shortly after taking office in April 2018, Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed announced sweeping political and economic reforms, including plans to merge the ethnically based coalition parties of the EPRDF into a single multi-ethnic national party. On 21 November 2019, the EPRDF's executive committee approved the merger, and the Prosperity Party was formally launched days later. Eight parties merged into the new party, including the Oromo Democratic Party, the Amhara Democratic Party and the Southern Ethiopian People's Democratic Movement, as well as several parties from the so-called developing regions.

The TPLF, which had dominated the EPRDF for three decades, voted against the merger and refused to join the new party, a decision that deepened the rift between the federal government and the Tigray regional government and contributed to the outbreak of the Tigray War in November 2020.

Ideology

The Prosperity Party describes itself as a multi-ethnic, pan-Ethiopian party committed to federalism, democracy and a market-oriented economy. Its founding documents emphasise national unity, citizenship-based politics and moving beyond the ethnic-party model of the EPRDF era, although critics argue its structure retains strong regional branches organised along ethnic lines.

Elections

The party won a large majority in the 2021 Ethiopian general election, the first held after its formation, securing the overwhelming share of seats in the House of Peoples' Representatives and confirming Abiy Ahmed as prime minister.
