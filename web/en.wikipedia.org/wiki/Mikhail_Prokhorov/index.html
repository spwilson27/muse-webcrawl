Mikhail Prokhorov

Mikhail Dmitrievitch Prokhorov (born 3 May 1965) is a Russian-Israeli businessman and former politician. After the fall of the Soviet Union he built a fortune in banking and metals, becoming chairman of Norilsk Nickel and head of the Onexim Group investment fund.

Business career

Prokhorov graduated from the Moscow Finance Institute and worked at the International Bank for Economic Co-operation. In 1993 he and Vladimir Potanin acquired Norilsk Nickel, the world's largest producer of nickel and palladium, in the loans-for-shares era; he served as its chairman and CEO until 2007. He then founded Onexim Group, with interests in metals, mining (including Polyus Gold), energy and finance. Notably, he sold most of his Russian assets before the 2008 financial crisis, preserving much of his fortune.

Sport

In 2010 Prokhorov purchased a controlling stake in the NBA's New Jersey Nets — becoming the league's first non-North American owner — and oversaw the team's move to Brooklyn as the Brooklyn Nets in 2012. He sold his remaining stake in the team and the Barclays Center in 2019.

Politics

Prokhorov entered politics, leading the Right Cause party briefly in 2011, and ran as an independent in the 2012 Russian presidential election, finishing third with about 8 per cent of the vote. He later founded the Civic Platform party before leaving active politics.
