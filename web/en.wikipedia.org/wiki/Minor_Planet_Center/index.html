The Minor Planet Center (MPC) is the official body for observing and reporting asteroids and comets under the auspices of the International Astronomical Union (IAU). Founded in 1947, it operates at the Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory in Cambridge, Massachusetts, as part of the Center for Astrophysics | Harvard & Smithsonian.

Functions

The MPC is responsible for the collection, computation, checking and dissemination of astrometric measurements and orbits for minor planets, comets and outer irregular natural satellites of the major planets. It assigns provisional designations and, once an orbit is secure, permanent numbers to minor planets, and it works with the Central Bureau for Astronomical Telegrams, which announces discoveries through the Minor Planet Electronic Circulars and other announcements.

History

The center was established at the University of Cincinnati in 1947 under director Paul Herget, and moved to the Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory in 1978, where it was directed for many years by Brian G. Marsden, followed by Timothy B. Spahr and later Matthew J. Holman.

Databases

The MPC maintains publicly available databases of minor-planet orbits and observations, including the Minor Planet Orbit Database, ephemeris services, and catalogues of near-Earth objects useful for planetary defense and impact-risk assessment.
