Surface metrology

Surface metrology is the science of measuring the small-scale features of surfaces — their texture, roughness, waviness, and form. It is important in manufacturing engineering, tribology, and precision engineering, because surface topography strongly affects friction, wear, sealing, lubrication, appearance, and the performance of optical and electronic components.

Measurement techniques

Traditional measurement uses a stylus profilometer, which drags a fine diamond tip across the surface and records vertical displacement along a line. Modern areal (three-dimensional) techniques include optical methods such as coherence scanning interferometry, confocal microscopy, and focus variation, as well as atomic force microscopy for the finest scales.

Parameters

Surface texture is quantified by standard parameters defined in documents such as ISO 25178 for areal texture and ISO 4287 for profiles. Familiar profile parameters include the arithmetic average roughness Ra and the root-mean-square roughness Rq; their areal counterparts are written Sa and Sq.
