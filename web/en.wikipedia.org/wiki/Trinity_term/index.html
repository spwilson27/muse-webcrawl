Trinity term

Trinity term is the third and final term of the academic year at several British and Irish universities, named after Trinity Sunday, which falls within it. It is the summer term, generally running from late April to late June or early July.

Oxford and Cambridge

At the University of Oxford, the academic year consists of Michaelmas, Hilary and Trinity terms, each with eight weeks of full term; Trinity is the term of final examinations for most undergraduates, of May morning celebrations, and of Eights Week rowing. The University of Cambridge uses Lent and Easter terms for its second and third terms instead, but Trinity College Dublin follows the Oxford pattern of Michaelmas, Hilary and Trinity, with Trinity term ending around the famous Trinity Ball season and exam period.

Legal year

"Trinity term" is also one of the four sittings of the courts of England and Wales, alongside Hilary, Easter and Michaelmas terms — a usage inherited from the historic legal year that once governed the Westminster courts.
