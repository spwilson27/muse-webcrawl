Je suis Charlie

Je suis Charlie (French for "I am Charlie") is a slogan and logo created by French art director Joachim Roncin and adopted by supporters of freedom of speech and freedom of the press after the 7 January 2015 shooting at the Paris offices of the satirical newspaper Charlie Hebdo, in which twelve people were killed.

Origin and spread

Roncin posted the phrase on Twitter shortly after the attack. It quickly became a viral hashtag and appeared on placards at rallies around the world. On 11 January 2015, millions marched in Paris and other French cities in the Republican marches, with world leaders joining in Paris.

Usage and debate

The slogan was widely adopted by newspapers, websites, and public figures as an expression of solidarity, though some critics and counter-slogans such as "Je ne suis pas Charlie" questioned its implications regarding the paper's provocative content.
