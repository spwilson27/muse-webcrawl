Makoto Shinkai (新海 誠, Shinkai Makoto; born February 9, 1973) is a Japanese animator, filmmaker, and manga artist. His romantic dramas — distinguished by photorealistic skies, trains, and stories of distance and longing — culminated in Your Name (2016), one of the highest-grossing anime films of all time.

Career

Shinkai worked at the game company Nihon Falcom, making opening animations, before creating the short Voices of a Distant Star (2002) almost entirely alone on his home computer. Features followed: The Place Promised in Our Early Days (2004), the three-part 5 Centimeters per Second (2007), Children Who Chase Lost Voices (2011), and The Garden of Words (2013), each refining his themes of separation and his painterly rendering of light, weather, and urban landscapes.

Breakthrough

Your Name, about two teenagers who swap bodies across time, became a cultural phenomenon in Japan and grossed over US$380 million worldwide, briefly making it the highest-grossing anime film ever. Its successors, Weathering with You (2019) and Suzume (2022), weave romance together with the memory of the 2011 Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami.

Style

Shinkai's work is defined by meticulous background art, lens effects and radiant clouds, asynchronous communication (letters, texts, voicemail) as an emotional motif, and the influence of novelist Haruki Murakami. He is frequently described as a successor to Hayao Miyazaki, a comparison he has called an overestimation.
