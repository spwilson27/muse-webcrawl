Ministry of Defense (Argentina)

Formed1949
HeadquartersLibertador Building, Buenos Aires
JurisdictionGovernment of Argentina

The Ministry of Defense (Spanish: Ministerio de Defensa) of Argentina is the government ministry responsible for national defence and for the administration of the country's armed forces. It is headquartered in the Libertador Building in Buenos Aires, which also houses the headquarters of the Argentine Army.

History

The ministry was created in 1949, consolidating earlier secretariats for war, the navy and aeronautics under a single defence ministry. Its powers were substantially reshaped after the return of democracy in 1983, when new defence (1988) and internal security (1992) laws separated external defence from internal security and subordinated the armed forces firmly to civilian authority.

Organisation

The minister is a civilian appointed by the President. The ministry oversees the Joint General Staff of the Armed Forces and, through it, the Argentine Army, the Navy and the Air Force. It is also responsible for defence industries and agencies such as Fabricaciones Militares, the naval shipyards and the state aircraft factory FAdeA.
