The Duke of Edinburgh's Award (DofE) is a youth awards programme founded in the United Kingdom in 1956 by Prince Philip, Duke of Edinburgh, together with Kurt Hahn, founder of Gordonstoun School, and Lord Hunt. It has since expanded to 144 countries.

Programme

Participants aged 14 to 24 work through three progressive levels — Bronze, Silver and Gold — completing activities in four sections: volunteering, physical activity, skills, and an expedition. The Gold Award adds a residential section. The programme aims to develop confidence, resilience and teamwork.

Gold Award presentations traditionally take place at royal palaces, including Buckingham Palace and St James's Palace. Millions of young people have taken part worldwide since the scheme began.
