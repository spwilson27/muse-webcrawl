The Los Angeles Times is a daily newspaper in Los Angeles, founded in 1881, the largest metropolitan daily in the western United States and for much of the 20th century one of the country's most powerful regional papers. Under Otis Chandler from 1960 it shed a parochial image and won national standing; it has won dozens of Pulitzer Prizes.

History

The Times grew with Los Angeles under the Otis and Chandler families, whose conservative paper promoted the city's growth. The Times Mirror era ended with sale to the Tribune Company in 2000; after further changes the biotech billionaire Patrick Soon-Shiong bought the paper in 2018.

Coverage

The paper is California's paper of record: state government, water and wildfires, immigration, Hollywood and the entertainment industry, and Los Angeles city life. Like other metros it cut staff heavily in the digital era and refocused on subscriptions.
