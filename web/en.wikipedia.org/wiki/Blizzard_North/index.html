Blizzard North was an American video game developer based in San Mateo, California. Founded in 1993 as Condor by David Brevik, Erich Schaefer and Max Schaefer, the studio was acquired by Blizzard Entertainment's parent company in 1996 and renamed Blizzard North. It is best known for creating the Diablo series of action role-playing games.

History

As Condor, the studio developed console titles including Justice League Task Force for the Sega Genesis. Its pitch for a turn-based, claymation-styled role-playing game called Diablo was taken up by Blizzard, which acquired the company during development. Diablo was released at the end of 1996 in a real-time form and became a major success, helping to popularise the action RPG genre and Blizzard's Battle.net online service. The sequel, Diablo II (2000), and its expansion Lord of Destruction (2001) were even more successful.

Closure

In 2003 the Schaefer brothers, Brevik and other senior staff resigned amid disagreements with parent company Vivendi, going on to found Flagship Studios and Runic Games. Work at Blizzard North continued on a version of Diablo III that was later scrapped. Blizzard closed the studio in 2005, consolidating development at its Irvine headquarters.
