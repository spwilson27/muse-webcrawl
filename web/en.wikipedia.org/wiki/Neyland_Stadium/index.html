Neyland Stadium is an American football stadium in Knoxville, Tennessee, on the campus of the University of Tennessee. It is the home of the Tennessee Volunteers football team and, with a capacity of 101,915, is one of the largest stadiums in the United States and the world.

History

The stadium opened in 1921 as Shields–Watkins Field with a capacity of about 3,200. It was renamed in 1962 for Robert Neyland, the legendary Volunteers coach and athletic director who led the campaign to build and repeatedly expand it. The field sits beside the Tennessee River, allowing fans to arrive by boat — the "Vol Navy".

Traditions

The Volunteers run onto the field through the Power T formed by the Pride of the Southland Band, and the end zones are painted in the school's famous orange-and-white checkerboard pattern.
