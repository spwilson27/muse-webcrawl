National Health Mission

Launched: 2013
Country: India
Agency: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare
Components: National Rural Health Mission, National Urban Health Mission

The National Health Mission (NHM) is an initiative of the Government of India under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, launched in 2013 by merging the National Rural Health Mission (2005) and the National Urban Health Mission.

Objectives

The mission aims to provide accessible, affordable and quality health care to India's population, with emphasis on maternal and child health, immunisation, communicable and non-communicable disease control, and strengthening public health infrastructure from village health centres to district hospitals.

Programmes

NHM supports over a million Accredited Social Health Activist (ASHA) community health workers, funds facility upgrades, mobile medical units and free drugs and diagnostics initiatives in the states. It encompasses programmes such as Janani Suraksha Yojana for institutional deliveries and Rashtriya Bal Swasthya Karyakram for child health screening. Funding is shared between the central government and the states.
