Waymo

Waymo LLC
Industry: Autonomous vehicles
Founded: 2009 (as Google Self-Driving Car Project)
Parent: Alphabet Inc.
Headquarters: Mountain View, California

Waymo LLC is an American autonomous driving technology company, a subsidiary of Alphabet Inc. It began in 2009 as Google's self-driving car project, led initially by Sebastian Thrun, and was spun out under the Waymo name in 2016. Waymo operates Waymo One, a commercial robotaxi service.

History

The project's early milestones included the first fully driverless ride on public roads, given to a blind passenger in Austin, Texas, in 2015. In 2018 Waymo launched Waymo One in the Phoenix, Arizona area, the first commercial self-driving service, initially with safety operators and later fully driverless. The company developed its own vehicle platforms, including the pod-like "Firefly" prototype, retrofitted Chrysler Pacifica minivans and Jaguar I-Pace electric cars, and later purpose-built Zeekr vehicles.

Service and expansion

Waymo One has since expanded to San Francisco, Los Angeles, Austin and other cities, offering paid rides through its app to the general public. The company has driven tens of millions of rider-only miles and publishes safety data comparing its crash rates with human benchmarks. Waymo also explored autonomous trucking under the Waymo Via brand before refocusing on ride-hailing.

Technology

The Waymo Driver combines lidar, cameras, radar and detailed prior maps with machine-learned perception and planning, trained partly in large-scale simulation.
