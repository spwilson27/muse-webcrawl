The Landmark (Hong Kong)

The Landmark is a luxury shopping complex and office development in Central, Hong Kong, owned by Hongkong Land. It comprises the retail podium Landmark Atrium and the office towers Gloucester Tower, Edinburgh Tower, and York House, together with the Landmark Oriental hotel (now The Landmark Mandarin Oriental).

History

The complex was developed in phases beginning in the 1970s on the site of earlier buildings in Hong Kong's prime central business district. Landmark Atrium became famous for pioneering luxury-brand flagship shopping in the city, hosting boutiques of brands such as Louis Vuitton, Gucci, and Harvey Nichols, and restaurants in its multi-level atrium around a central fountain court.

Description

The Landmark is connected by pedestrian walkways to other Hongkong Land properties in Central, including Prince's Building and Alexandra House. Major redevelopment and expansion plans announced in the 2020s aimed to enlarge its luxury retail footprint.
