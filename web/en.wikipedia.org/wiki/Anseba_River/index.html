Anseba River

The Anseba River is a river in Eritrea in the Horn of Africa. It rises in the highlands near Asmara and flows generally north-westward through the Anseba Region, passing near Keren before joining the Barka River system.

Course and setting

The river drains a rugged landscape of highland plateaus, escarpments and dry lowlands. Flow is strongly seasonal, reflecting summer rains in the Eritrean highlands and the semi-arid climate of much of the basin. Settlements and agriculture along its banks depend on runoff, wells, small dams and spate irrigation.

Region

The Anseba Region takes its name from the river, and Keren, one of Eritrea's largest towns, is closely associated with the valley. The river and its tributaries are important locally for livestock watering, cultivation and transport routes through difficult terrain.
