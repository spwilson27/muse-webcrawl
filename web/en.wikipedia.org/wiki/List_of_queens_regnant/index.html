List of queens regnant

A queen regnant is a female monarch who reigns in her own right, as opposed to a queen consort (wife of a king). This list collects notable ruling queens across history.

Ancient and medieval

  • Hatshepsut — pharaoh of Egypt, r. c. 1479–1458 BC.
  • Tomyris — Massagetae queen who defeated Cyrus the Great.
  • Zenobia — queen of Palmyra, 3rd century AD.
  • Theodora — Byzantine empress regnant (with co-rulers), and Irene of Athens, sole empress 797–802.
  • Matilda — disputed ruler of England, 1141.
  • Margaret I — ruler of Denmark, Norway and Sweden (Kalmar Union).
  • Isabella I of Castile — co-ruler of Spain with Ferdinand.
  • Seondeok and Jindeok — queens of Silla (Korea).
  • Suiko — first reigning empress of Japan, 593–628.

Early modern to modern

  • Elizabeth I of England (1558–1603); Mary I; Anne; Victoria (1837–1901); Elizabeth II (1952–2022), Britain's longest-reigning monarch.
  • Mary, Queen of Scots; Christina of Sweden; Catherine the Great of Russia (1762–1796).
  • Maria Theresa of Austria; Raziya Sultan of Delhi; Liliʻuokalani, last queen of Hawaii.
  • Wilhelmina, Juliana and Beatrix of the Netherlands; Margrethe II of Denmark (1972–2024).

Reigning today

As of the mid-2020s there are no reigning queens regnant in Europe following Margrethe II's abdication; ruling queens remain in a few monarchies elsewhere, and many states retain queens as consorts.
