Diana Lorena Taurasi (born 11 June 1982) is an American former professional basketball player, widely regarded as one of the greatest women's basketball players of all time. She spent her entire WNBA career with the Phoenix Mercury after being drafted first overall in 2004.

Career

Taurasi led the UConn Huskies to three consecutive NCAA championships (2002–2004). With Phoenix she won three WNBA championships (2007, 2009, 2014) and became the WNBA's all-time leading scorer in 2017, the first player to reach 10,000 career points. With Team USA she won six Olympic gold medals, a record for a basketball player.

Retirement

Taurasi announced her retirement in February 2025, ending a 20-season WNBA career.
