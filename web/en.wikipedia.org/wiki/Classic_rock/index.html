Classic rock

Classic rock is a radio format which developed from the album-oriented rock (AOR) format in the early 1980s. In the United States, the classic rock format features rock music ranging generally from the mid-1960s to the early 1990s, primarily focusing on commercially successful blues rock and hard rock popularized in the 1970s AOR format.

History

The format originated in the early 1980s as programmers sought to retain aging baby-boomer audiences. Stations such as WMET in Chicago and KRBE-associated consultants popularized the approach. By the late 1980s and 1990s classic rock had become one of the most popular radio formats in North America.

Characteristics

Playlists typically center on artists such as Led Zeppelin, Pink Floyd, The Rolling Stones, The Who, Eagles, Fleetwood Mac, Aerosmith, and Van Halen. Although originally centered on older rock, the format has gradually expanded to include music from the 1980s and 1990s.

Criticism

Critics have noted the format's narrow, repetitive playlists and its relative underrepresentation of women and minority artists.
