The George H. W. Bush Presidential Library and Museum is the presidential library of George H. W. Bush, the 41st president of the United States (1989–1993). Located on a 90-acre site on the west campus of Texas A&M University in College Station, Texas, it was dedicated on 6 November 1997 and is administered by the National Archives and Records Administration.

Holdings and exhibits

The library holds more than 44 million pages of official and personal papers, 2 million photographs, and tens of thousands of artefacts from Bush's career as a naval aviator, congressman, ambassador, director of Central Intelligence, vice president and president. Museum exhibits include a replica of the Oval Office, a section of the Berlin Wall, displays on the Gulf War, and a TBM Avenger torpedo bomber of the type Bush flew in the Second World War.

The complex also includes the Bush School of Government and Public Service at Texas A&M and the gravesites of George H. W. Bush and Barbara Bush, who were buried on the grounds in 2018.
