Talia al Ghul

Talia al Ghul (Arabic: تاليا الغول) is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, commonly in association with Batman. Created by writer Dennis O'Neil and artist Bob Brown, she first appeared in Detective Comics #411 (May 1971). She is the daughter of the supervillain Ra's al Ghul, a member of the League of Assassins, the on-and-off lover of Batman, and the mother of their son Damian Wayne (Robin).

Publication history

Talia debuted in "Into the Den of the Death-Dealers!" in 1971, rescuing Batman, and was soon revealed as the daughter of Ra's al Ghul, who hoped to make Batman his heir. In the 1987 graphic novel Son of the Demon, Talia and Bruce Wayne had a child; the storyline was reintroduced in 2006 by Grant Morrison, revealing Damian Wayne, who became the fifth Robin.

Characterization

Talia is torn between loyalty to her father's crusade and her love for Batman. Highly intelligent and trained in many martial arts, she has at times run her father's organization and founded the criminal empire Leviathan, opposing Batman directly in Batman Incorporated.

In other media

Marion Cotillard portrayed Talia in The Dark Knight Rises (2012). The character has also appeared in animated series including Batman: The Animated Series and various DC animated films, and in the Arkham video games.
