Vuelta a España

The Vuelta a España (Spanish for "Tour of Spain") is an annual multi-stage bicycle race primarily held in Spain. Along with the Tour de France and the Giro d'Italia, it is one of cycling's three Grand Tours. First held in 1935, it has been organised since 2010 by Unipublic, a subsidiary of ASO.

History

The race was founded by the newspaper Informaciones, inspired by the success of the Tour de France and Giro d'Italia. It was interrupted by the Spanish Civil War and the Second World War, and since 1955 has been held annually. In 1995 it moved from spring to late August–September in the racing calendar.

The race

The Vuelta typically covers about 3,300 km over 21 stages, including steep mountain finishes such as the Alto de l'Angliru and Lagos de Covadonga. The general classification leader wears a red jersey, introduced in 2010. Roberto Heras holds the record with four overall victories; Primož Roglič has also won the race four times.
