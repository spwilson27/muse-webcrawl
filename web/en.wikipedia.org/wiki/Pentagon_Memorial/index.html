Pentagon Memorial

LocationThe Pentagon, Arlington County, Virginia
Coordinates38.8703° N, 77.0598° W
EstablishedSeptember 11, 2008
DesignerJulie Beckman and Keith Kaseman (KBAS)

The National 9/11 Pentagon Memorial is a memorial in Arlington County, Virginia, on the southwest side of the Pentagon, commemorating the 184 people who died when American Airlines Flight 77 was deliberately crashed into the Pentagon during the September 11 attacks in 2001 — 59 aboard the aircraft and 125 in the building.

Design

The winning design, by Julie Beckman and Keith Kaseman of the Philadelphia studio KBAS, was selected from more than 1,100 entries in an international competition judged by a panel including victims' family members. The two-acre site is organised as an Age Wall and 184 cantilevered Memorial Units — stainless-steel benches, each engraved with a victim's name and suspended over a shallow, illuminated reflecting pool.

The benches are arranged along age lines running parallel to the flight path, ordered by the victims' ages, from Dana Falkenberg, aged three, to John D. Yamnicky Sr., aged 71. Benches for victims who were in the Pentagon face the building; benches for those aboard Flight 77 face outward toward the sky along the plane's approach, so that a visitor reading a name on a Pentagon victim's bench looks toward the sky, and vice versa. A grove of maple trees provides shade over the gravel field.

History

The memorial was dedicated and opened to the public on September 11, 2008, the seventh anniversary of the attacks, at a ceremony addressed by President George W. Bush. It is open to the public 24 hours a day, seven days a week, and is administered by the Department of Defense. It was the first of the three major 9/11 crash-site memorials to be completed.
