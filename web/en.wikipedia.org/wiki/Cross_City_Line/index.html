Cross City Line


Image placeholder: Cross City Line

The Cross City Line is a suburban railway line in the West Midlands of England, running from Lichfield Trent Valley in the north, through Birmingham New Street, to Redditch and Bromsgrove in the south. It is one of the most heavily used suburban rail routes in the region.

History

The line was created in the 1970s by linking existing routes through the rebuilt Birmingham New Street, giving through services between the northern and southern suburbs. The southern section to Redditch was singled in the 1980s and later partly redoubled. Electric services began in 1993. The extension of electric services from Longbridge to Bromsgrove opened in 2018.

Services

Trains are operated by West Midlands Railway, with frequent services — typically up to six trains per hour on the core section — calling at stations including Sutton Coldfield, Four Oaks, Erdington, Aston, University, Selly Oak, and King's Norton.
