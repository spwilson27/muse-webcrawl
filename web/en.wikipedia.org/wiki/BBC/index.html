BBC

The British Broadcasting Corporation (BBC) is a British public service broadcaster, established by royal charter in 1927 (founded as the British Broadcasting Company in 1922), funded principally by a television licence fee, and the world's oldest and largest national broadcaster of its kind.

Services

The BBC operates television channels, national and local radio, the BBC News operation, BBC iPlayer and BBC Sounds, and the BBC World Service in dozens of languages, alongside a major online presence.

Reputation and debates

The BBC is widely regarded for international news reach, and periodically debated at home over its funding, impartiality and governance.
