DC Universe Animated Original Movies

The DC Universe Animated Original Movies (DCUAOM) are a series of American direct-to-video superhero animated films based on DC Comics characters and stories, produced by Warner Bros. Animation and DC Entertainment and distributed by Warner Bros. Home Entertainment. The films are generally aimed at an adult audience, often carrying PG-13 or R ratings, in contrast to DC's television animation.

History

The line began in 2007 with Superman: Doomsday and was overseen for many years by executive producer Bruce Timm. Early releases largely adapted famous comic-book storylines, including Justice League: The New Frontier (2008), Batman: Under the Red Hood (2010), Batman: The Dark Knight Returns (2012–2013), adapting Frank Miller's graphic novel, and Justice League: The Flashpoint Paradox (2013).

Shared continuities

From Justice League: War (2014) through Justice League Dark: Apokolips War (2020), most films formed a shared continuity known as the DC Animated Movie Universe, loosely based on DC's New 52 reboot. A subsequent continuity, informally called the "Tomorrowverse", began with Superman: Man of Tomorrow (2020) and concluded with the Justice League: Crisis on Infinite Earths trilogy (2024). Standalone adaptations, such as Batman: The Killing Joke (2016) and Batman and Harley Quinn (2017), were released alongside the shared-universe entries.

Reception

The films have generally been commercially successful for the home-video market and are frequently praised for faithful adaptations of acclaimed storylines and strong voice casts, including Kevin Conroy as Batman and Mark Hamill as the Joker.
