Hainaut (French: Hainaut; Dutch: Henegouwen) is a province of Wallonia and Belgium. Its capital is Mons and its largest city is Charleroi. It borders France to the south and west, and the Belgian provinces of West Flanders, East Flanders, Flemish Brabant, Walloon Brabant and Namur.

History

The province corresponds roughly to the medieval County of Hainaut. In the 19th century it was a centre of coal mining and heavy industry along the Sambre and Haine valleys — the sillon industriel — which drove Belgium's industrial revolution before declining in the later 20th century.

Subdivisions

Hainaut is divided into seven arrondissements — Ath, Charleroi, La Louvière, Mons, Soignies, Thuin and Tournai-Mouscron — comprising 69 municipalities.
