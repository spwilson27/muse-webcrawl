Amanda Michelle Seyfried (born December 3, 1985) is an American actress and singer. Born and raised in Allentown, Pennsylvania, she began modelling at age eleven and acting at fifteen, appearing in the soap operas As the World Turns and All My Children. Her film breakthrough came as Karen Smith in the teen comedy Mean Girls (2004).

Career

Seyfried gained wider recognition starring as Sophie Sheridan in the jukebox musical Mamma Mia! (2008) and its sequel Mamma Mia! Here We Go Again (2018), showcasing her singing. Her other film roles include Jennifer's Body (2009), Dear John (2010), Les Misérables (2012) as Cosette, Lovelace (2013), A Million Ways to Die in the West (2014), First Reformed (2017) and The Art of Racing in the Rain (2019). On television she played Sarah Henrickson in HBO's Big Love (2006–2011).

For portraying actress Marion Davies in David Fincher's Mank (2020), Seyfried received widespread acclaim and a nomination for the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress. She won the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Limited Series for playing Elizabeth Holmes in the Hulu miniseries The Dropout (2022).

Personal life

Seyfried married actor Thomas Sadoski in 2017; they have two children and live on a farm in the Catskill Mountains of New York. She has spoken publicly about living with anxiety and obsessive-compulsive disorder, and serves on the board of the INARA charity for injured children in conflict zones. She founded the toy company Make/Believe.
