Pedro Almodovar

Pedro Almodovar.

Pedro Almodovar is an unaccented rendering of the name of Pedro Almodóvar (born 25 September 1949), the Spanish film director, screenwriter, and producer.

Almodóvar is one of the most internationally successful Spanish filmmakers. Emerging from the Madrid countercultural scene of the Movida Madrileña after the death of Franco, he founded the production company El Deseo with his brother Agustín in 1985. His films are noted for melodrama, bold colours, complex female characters, and treatment of desire, identity, and memory.

His feature films include Pepi, Luci, Bom (1980), Women on the Verge of a Nervous Breakdown (1988), All About My Mother (1999), which won the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film, Talk to Her (2002), which won the Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay, Volver (2006), The Skin I Live In (2011), and the autobiographical Pain and Glory (2019). His first English-language feature, The Room Next Door (2024), won the Golden Lion at the Venice Film Festival.
