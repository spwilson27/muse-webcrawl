Andor, marketed in its second season as Andor: A Star Wars Story, is an American science-fiction television series created by Tony Gilroy for Disney+. A prequel to the film Rogue One (2016), it follows the thief-turned-spy Cassian Andor (Diego Luna) during the five years before that film, charting his transformation into a committed Rebel operative and the birth of the Rebel Alliance against the Galactic Empire.

Premise and structure

The first season (2022) tells its story in multi-episode arcs: a recruitment mission, a daring payroll heist on Aldhani, imprisonment in an Imperial factory-prison on Narkina 5, and the uprising on Ferrix. The second season (2025) unfolds in four three-episode arcs, each jumping forward a year, to the eve of Rogue One. Parallel storylines follow Senator Mon Mothma's covert financing of the rebellion, spymaster Luthen Rael's clandestine network, and Imperial Security Bureau officers Dedra Meero and Syril Karn.

Reception

Andor was widely acclaimed as one of the finest entries in the Star Wars franchise, praised for its writing, performances, grounded tone, production design favouring real locations and sets, and its mature treatment of fascism, surveillance and resistance. It received numerous award nominations, including for Outstanding Drama Series at the Primetime Emmy Awards.
