HomeAway, Inc. was an American vacation rental marketplace company headquartered in Austin, Texas. Founded in 2005, it operated a network of websites on which property owners and managers listed holiday homes for short-term rental, most prominently Vrbo (Vacation Rentals by Owner), which it acquired in 2006, as well as regional brands such as OwnersDirect in the United Kingdom, Abritel in France and Fewo-direkt in Germany.

History

The company was founded in 2005 by Brian Sharples and Carl Shepherd, initially as WVR Group, with funding from venture capital firms. Through a rapid series of acquisitions it consolidated the fragmented vacation-rental listings market, buying VRBO.com in 2006 and HomeAway.com, and expanding internationally with purchases in Europe, Australia, Brazil and elsewhere. HomeAway went public on the NASDAQ in 2011. In November 2015, Expedia, Inc. announced it would acquire HomeAway for approximately $3.9 billion in cash and stock; the deal closed in December 2015.

Rebranding to Vrbo

Following the acquisition, Expedia shifted the business from a subscription-listing model toward online booking with service fees. In 2019 Expedia announced that Vrbo would become the group's primary vacation-rental brand, and the HomeAway brand and websites were progressively retired and merged into Vrbo in the early 2020s. The unified Vrbo platform competes with Airbnb and Booking.com in the short-term rental market.
