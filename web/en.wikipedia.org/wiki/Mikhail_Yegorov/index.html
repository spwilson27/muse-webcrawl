Mikhail Yegorov

Mikhail Yegorov
Born: 5 May 1923, Rudnya, Soviet Union
Died: 20 June 1975
Known for: Raising the Victory Banner over the Reichstag (1945)

Mikhail Alekseyevich Yegorov (Russian: Михаил Алексеевич Егоров; 5 May 1923 – 20 June 1975) was a Soviet sergeant of the Red Army who, together with Meliton Kantaria, raised the Soviet Victory Banner over the Reichstag building in Berlin on 30 April 1945, during the Battle of Berlin in the final days of the Second World War in Europe.

The banner raising

Serving in the 150th Rifle Division of the 3rd Shock Army, Yegorov and the Georgian sergeant Kantaria planted the banner — assault flag No. 5 — on the roof of the Reichstag, an event staged again for the famous photograph by Yevgeny Khaldei. Both men were named Heroes of the Soviet Union in 1946.

Later life

After the war Yegorov worked at a dairy plant in his native Rudnya, Smolensk Oblast. He died in a car accident in 1975 and is commemorated in his hometown.
