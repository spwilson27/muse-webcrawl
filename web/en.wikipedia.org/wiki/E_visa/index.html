E visa

E visa
CountryUnited States
TypeNon-immigrant visa
CategoriesE-1, E-2, E-3
PurposeTrade and investment

An E visa is a category of non-immigrant visa issued by the United States to nationals of countries with which the United States maintains treaties of commerce and navigation. The E visa program allows foreign nationals to enter the United States to carry on substantial trade, or to develop and direct the operations of an enterprise in which they have invested a substantial amount of capital.

Categories

There are three main subcategories of E visa:

  • E-1 (Treaty Trader) — for individuals or employees of companies engaged in substantial trade in goods, services, or technology between the United States and the treaty country.
  • E-2 (Treaty Investor) — for individuals who have invested, or are actively in the process of investing, a substantial amount of capital in a bona fide enterprise in the United States.
  • E-3 — a special category created in 2005 for Australian nationals coming to the United States to work in a specialty occupation, similar in structure to the H-1B visa but reserved exclusively for Australians.

Requirements

For E-1 and E-2 classification, the applicant must be a national of a treaty country, and the trading or investing enterprise must have the nationality of the treaty country, meaning at least 50 percent of the business must be owned by nationals of that country. There is no statutory minimum investment amount for the E-2 visa, but the investment must be "substantial" in relation to the total cost of the business, must be irrevocably committed, and must be sufficient to ensure the investor's financial commitment to the successful operation of the enterprise.

Duration and renewal

E visas are typically issued for periods of up to five years, depending on the reciprocity schedule for the treaty country, and may be renewed indefinitely in increments, so long as the holder continues to meet the requirements. Holders are generally admitted for an initial period of stay of up to two years per entry. Spouses and unmarried children under 21 may accompany the principal holder in E dependent status; spouses may apply for employment authorization.
