Tigray People's Liberation Front

The Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF) is an ethnic nationalist political party and former ruling party in Ethiopia, originating as a guerrilla movement in the Tigray region. Founded on 18 February 1975, it led the Ethiopian People's Revolutionary Democratic Front (EPRDF) coalition that governed Ethiopia from 1991 until 2018.

History

The TPLF began as a small insurgent group fighting the Derg military regime during the Ethiopian Civil War. Growing into a large, disciplined army, it formed the EPRDF with allied movements and captured Addis Ababa in May 1991. Under Meles Zenawi, prime minister from 1995 until his death in 2012, the TPLF-dominated government pursued developmental-state policies alongside restrictions on political opposition.

Loss of power and war

After Abiy Ahmed became prime minister in 2018, the TPLF lost its dominant position and retreated to Tigray. Relations with the federal government collapsed, culminating in the Tigray War of 2020–2022 between the TPLF and federal forces allied with Eritrea. A cessation of hostilities agreement was signed in Pretoria in November 2022, after which the TPLF's terrorist designation was lifted and an interim regional administration was established.
