Google Assistant is a virtual assistant software application developed by Google that is primarily available on home automation and mobile devices. Based on artificial intelligence, unlike the company's previous virtual assistant, Google Now, the Google Assistant can engage in two-way conversations.

History

The Assistant debuted in May 2016 as part of Google's messaging app Allo and its voice-activated speaker Google Home. After a period of exclusivity on the Pixel and Pixel XL phones, it was deployed on other Android devices in February 2017, including third-party smartphones and Android Wear. It was released as a standalone app on iOS in May 2017.

Features

Users interact with the Assistant primarily via natural voice, though keyboard input is also supported. The Assistant can search the Internet, schedule events and alarms, adjust hardware settings on the user's device, and show information from the user's Google account. Google also announced that the Assistant would be able to identify objects and gather visual information through the device's camera, support buying products and sending money, and identify songs.

Reception

Reviewers praised the Assistant's conversational ability and contextual understanding. In 2023, Google announced that the Assistant would be enhanced with generative artificial intelligence features based on its large language models, and later began transitioning users toward Gemini.
