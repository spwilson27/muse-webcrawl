Kyuss

OriginPalm Desert, California, USA
Years active1987–1995
GenresStoner rock, desert rock, heavy metal
Key membersJosh Homme, John Garcia, Brant Bjork, Nick Oliveri, Scott Reeder

Kyuss was an American rock band formed in Palm Desert, California in 1987. Widely regarded as pioneers of stoner rock (also called desert rock), the band developed a heavy, down-tuned sound at open-air "generator parties" in the California desert, plugging amplifiers into petrol generators far from any venue.

History

The band began as Katzenjammer, then Sons of Kyuss — taking the name Kyuss from a creature in the Dungeons & Dragons Fiend Folio — before shortening it to Kyuss. Their albums Wretch (1991) and Blues for the Red Sun (1992) established their reputation; the latter, produced by Chris Goss, is frequently cited as a stoner rock landmark. Welcome to Sky Valley (1994) and ...And the Circus Leaves Town (1995) followed, with bass duties passing from Nick Oliveri to Scott Reeder. Internal tensions led to the band's split in 1995.

Legacy

Guitarist Josh Homme and bassist Nick Oliveri went on to form Queens of the Stone Age; singer John Garcia and drummer Brant Bjork pursued projects including Hermano, Unida and Vista Chino. In 2010 Garcia, Bjork and Oliveri toured as "Kyuss Lives!", leading to a trademark lawsuit from Homme and Reeder; the project continued under the name Vista Chino. Kyuss's low, fuzz-laden riffing remains a template for stoner and desert rock worldwide.
