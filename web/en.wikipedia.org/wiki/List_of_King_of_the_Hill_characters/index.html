List of King of the Hill characters

King of the Hill is an American animated sitcom created by Mike Judge and Greg Daniels that originally aired on Fox from 1997 to 2010, set in the fictional town of Arlen, Texas. The series centers on the Hill family and their neighbors on Rainey Street. A revival season premiered on Hulu in 2025 with the characters aged up. This article lists the main and recurring characters of the series.

The Hill family

Hank Hill (voiced by Mike Judge) is the protagonist: a propane and propane-accessories salesman at Strickland Propane, proud Texan, and devoted father. Peggy Hill (Kathy Najimy) is his wife, a substitute Spanish teacher and former Boggle champion. Bobby Hill (Pamela Adlon) is their son, an aspiring prop comic. Luanne Platter (Brittany Murphy) is Peggy's niece, who lives with the Hills for much of the series.

Neighbors and friends

Hank's alley companions are Dale Gribble (Johnny Hardwick), a chain-smoking exterminator and conspiracy theorist unaware his son Joseph was fathered by John Redcorn; Bill Dauterive (Stephen Root), a divorced, lonely Army barber; and Jeff Boomhauer (Mike Judge), the fast-talking ladies' man. Other recurring figures include Dale's wife Nancy, Kahn and Minh Souphanousinphone and their daughter Connie, Hank's father Cotton Hill, Buck Strickland, and John Redcorn.
