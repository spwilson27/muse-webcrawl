The 1936 Green Bay Packers season was the franchise's 16th season in the National Football League. Coached by Curly Lambeau, the Packers finished 10–1–1, won the Western Division, and captured the NFL championship.

Season summary

Led by quarterback Arnie Herber and end Don Hutson, the league's premier deep threat, Green Bay's passing attack was the most potent in professional football. Clarke Hinkle and Hank Gremminger anchored a strong supporting cast, and rookie Hank Groves and veteran Johnny "Blood" McNally contributed to the offence.

Championship game

In the 1936 NFL Championship Game, held at the Polo Grounds in New York because Boston's owner moved the game in a dispute over ticket sales, the Packers defeated the Boston Redskins 21–6. It was Green Bay's fourth NFL championship.
