The Sherman Brothers were an American songwriting duo who specialised in musical films: the brothers Robert B. Sherman (December 19, 1925 – March 6, 2012) and Richard M. Sherman (June 12, 1928 – May 25, 2024). As Walt Disney's staff songwriters from 1960 to 1973, they wrote more motion-picture musical songs than any other songwriting team in film history, including the scores of Mary Poppins, The Jungle Book, The Aristocats and Bedknobs and Broomsticks, and the theme-park song "It's a Small World (After All)", often described as the most publicly performed song ever written.

Career

Sons of the Russian-born Tin Pan Alley songwriter Al Sherman, the brothers began writing together after Robert was challenged by Richard to a songwriting contest. Early successes included pop hits for Annette Funicello such as "Tall Paul" (1958). Hired by Walt Disney in 1960, their first assignment, "The Strummin' Song" for The Horsemasters, led to a decade of Disney classics: The Parent Trap (1961), Summer Magic (1963), The Sword in the Stone (1963) and the Sherman-written score for Mary Poppins (1964), which won Academy Awards for Best Original Score and Best Original Song ("Chim Chim Cher-ee"), and also contained "Feed the Birds", reportedly Walt Disney's favourite song.

After Disney's death and their departure from the studio in the early 1970s, the brothers wrote Chitty Chitty Bang Bang (1968, for Albert R. Broccoli) and stage and film musicals including Snoopy Come Home, Charlotte's Web (1973), The Slipper and the Rose (1976) and the stage musical Over Here! (1974). Their autobiography, Walt's Time: From Before to Beyond, appeared in 1998; the documentary The Boys: The Sherman Brothers' Story (2009), made by their sons, explored their long personal estrangement. Richard Sherman was portrayed alongside his brother in Saving Mr. Banks (2013).

Selected works

  • "It's a Small World (After All)" (1964)
  • Mary Poppins — "A Spoonful of Sugar", "Supercalifragilisticexpialidocious", "Chim Chim Cher-ee", "Feed the Birds"
  • The Jungle Book — "The Bare Necessities" (by Terry Gilkyson), "I Wan'na Be Like You", "Trust in Me"
  • Chitty Chitty Bang Bang — title song, "Hushabye Mountain"
  • Winnie the Pooh shorts — "Winnie the Pooh", "The Wonderful Thing About Tiggers"

Honours

The brothers received a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame, were inducted into the Songwriters Hall of Fame in 2005, and in 2008 were awarded the National Medal of Arts by President George W. Bush.
