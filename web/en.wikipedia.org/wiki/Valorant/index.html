Valorant is a free-to-play first-person tactical hero shooter developed and published by Riot Games. It was first released for Windows in June 2020 following a closed beta, and later released for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S in 2024. The game combines precise gunplay inspired by Counter-Strike with character-based abilities.

Gameplay

In the main mode, two teams of five players compete over multiple rounds. One team, the attackers, attempts to plant a device called the Spike at a designated site; the defenders try to prevent the plant or defuse the Spike. Alternatively, a team can win a round by eliminating the opposing team. The first team to win 13 rounds wins the match. Players buy weapons, shields and abilities at the start of each round with credits earned from their performance.

Each player selects an agent, a character with unique abilities organised into four roles: duelists, initiators, controllers and sentinels. Twenty-eight or more agents have been released since launch, alongside a rotating pool of maps such as Bind, Haven, Ascent, Lotus and Sunset.

Development and release

Valorant was developed under the codename Project A and announced in October 2019. Riot emphasised competitive integrity, building a custom 128-tick server infrastructure and the always-on Vanguard anti-cheat system, which drew both praise for its effectiveness and criticism for its kernel-level operation. The closed beta, access to which was granted through Twitch stream drops, set viewership records. The game launched on 2 June 2020.

Esports

Riot organises the Valorant Champions Tour (VCT), a global circuit of regional leagues culminating in Masters events and the Champions world championship. The game has become one of the most-watched esports titles, and its Game Changers programme promotes competition for women and marginalised genders.
