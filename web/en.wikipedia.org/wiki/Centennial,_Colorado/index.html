Centennial is a home rule municipality in Arapahoe County, Colorado, United States, forming part of the Denver metropolitan area. It is one of the largest new cities incorporated in the United States in recent decades, having voted to incorporate in 2000 and formally come into existence in 2001; its name reflects Colorado's nickname, the "Centennial State," admitted to the Union in 1876.

Geography and economy

Centennial lies south of Denver and includes the Centennial Airport, one of the busiest general aviation airports in the country. The Denver Tech Center corridor along Interstate 25 hosts many corporate offices. The city contracts many services, including public works and law enforcement through the Arapahoe County Sheriff's Office, and is known for relatively low municipal taxes.
