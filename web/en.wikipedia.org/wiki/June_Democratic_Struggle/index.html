June Democratic Struggle

The June Democratic Struggle, also known as the June Democracy Movement, was a nationwide pro-democracy protest movement in South Korea in June 1987. Sparked by the torture death of student activist Park Jong-cheol and the regime's decision to retain indirect presidential elections, millions of citizens joined protests across the country.

Outcome

On 29 June 1987, ruling-party candidate Roh Tae-woo issued the June 29 Declaration accepting direct presidential elections and democratic reforms. A new constitution was adopted, and Roh won the December 1987 election, South Korea's first free direct presidential vote, beginning the Sixth Republic.
