Red Bull GmbH is an Austrian multinational private conglomerate company known for its range of energy drinks of the same name. It is also known for its sponsorship and ownership of a wide range of sporting teams and events, including Formula One teams Red Bull Racing and RB, and football clubs such as RB Leipzig and the New York Red Bulls.

History

The company was founded in 1984 by Dietrich Mateschitz, who adapted the Thai energy drink Krating Daeng created by Chaleo Yoovidhya. Red Bull was launched in Austria in 1987 and expanded worldwide, creating the modern energy-drink market in the West.

Sports and media

Red Bull is deeply involved in extreme sports, music, and media through Red Bull Media House, and owns or sponsors hundreds of athletes and events such as the Red Bull Air Race and Red Bull Cliff Diving World Series.
