Griffith Park

Griffith Park is a large municipal park in the Los Feliz neighbourhood of Los Angeles, California, covering about 4,310 acres (1,744 ha) of rugged hills at the eastern end of the Santa Monica Mountains. It is one of the largest urban parks in North America and home to the Griffith Observatory and a famous view of the Hollywood Sign.

History

Welsh immigrant Griffith J. Griffith donated 3,015 acres to the city in 1896. The park includes the Los Angeles Zoo, Autry Museum, Greek Theatre amphitheatre, merry-go-round, and extensive hiking and equestrian trails around Mount Hollywood.

Wildlife and fire

The park supports coyotes, deer and, famously from 2012 to 2022, the mountain lion P-22. Brush fires, notably in 2007, have burned hundreds of acres. The Hollywood Sign, erected 1923 on Mount Lee, sits within the park area. See Los Angeles and Griffith Observatory.
