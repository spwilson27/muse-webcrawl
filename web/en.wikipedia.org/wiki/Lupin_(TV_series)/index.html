Lupin is a French mystery thriller streaming television series created by George Kay and François Uzan for Netflix. It stars Omar Sy as Assane Diop, a professional thief inspired by the adventures of Maurice Leblanc's fictional gentleman burglar Arsène Lupin. The first part premiered on 8 January 2021 and became one of Netflix's most-watched non-English-language series, viewed by an estimated 70 million households in its first month.

Premise

Assane Diop, the son of a Senegalese immigrant chauffeur, seeks revenge against the wealthy Pellegrini family, whose patriarch framed his father for the theft of a diamond necklace — a crime for which his father was imprisoned and died by suicide. Using the Lupin stories his father gave him as a child as a playbook, Assane plans and executes audacious thefts and cons while evading police detective Youssef Guedira, one of the few who recognises the Lupin pattern.

Production and reception

The series was filmed in Paris and other French locations, including the Louvre, the Musée Nissim de Camondo, and Étretat in Normandy — Leblanc's own town. Part 2 followed in June 2021 and Part 3 in October 2023, in which Assane, in hiding, returns to Paris for his family's sake. Critics praised Sy's charismatic performance and the show's stylish heists. A fourth part has been confirmed.
