49er (dinghy)

DesignerJulian Bethwaite
Year1995
Crew2 (both on trapeze)
Hull length4.99 m

The 49er is a two-handed skiff-type high-performance sailing dinghy. Designed by Julian Bethwaite, it was selected as the Olympic class for the 2000 Sydney Games and has remained in the Olympic programme since. The women's counterpart, the 49erFX, uses a smaller rig.

Design

Both crew members sail from trapeze wires while standing on extendable wings, maximizing righting moment. The boat carries a large asymmetric spinnaker flown from a retractable bowsprit and is notoriously demanding to sail in strong winds, rewarding athletic, acrobatic crews.

Competition

The class is sailed worldwide at world championships and the Olympic Games. Its spectacular speed and frequent capsizes made it a popular spectator class in Olympic sailing.
