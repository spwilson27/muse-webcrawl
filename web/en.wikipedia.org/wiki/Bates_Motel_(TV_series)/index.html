Bates Motel (TV series)

Bates Motel is an American psychological horror drama television series that aired on A&E from 18 March 2013 to 24 April 2017, running for five seasons and 50 episodes. Developed by Carlton Cuse, Kerry Ehrin and Anthony Cipriano, it is a contemporary prequel to Alfred Hitchcock's 1960 film Psycho (itself based on Robert Bloch's 1959 novel), depicting the adolescence of Norman Bates and his intense relationship with his mother, Norma, before the events of the film.

The motel and hilltop house, rebuilt as a set in Aldergrove, British Columbia.

Premise and cast

After the death of her husband, Norma Bates (Vera Farmiga) buys a motel in the fictional coastal town of White Pine Bay, Oregon, and moves there with her teenage son Norman (Freddie Highmore). As Norman's mental state deteriorates and his blackouts and violent episodes increase, Norma goes to ever greater lengths to protect him. The series also stars Max Thieriot as Norman's half-brother Dylan Massett, Olivia Cooke as Emma Decody, Nicola Peltz as Bradley Martin, and Nestor Carbonell as Sheriff Alex Romero. Rihanna guest-starred in the final season as Marion Crane, the role made famous by Janet Leigh in Psycho.

Development and reception

Although set in the present day, the series deliberately blends contemporary technology with a timeless, 1950s-inflected look. The final season overlaps with and reworks the plot of Psycho. The series was praised for the performances of Farmiga and Highmore; Farmiga received an Emmy nomination for the first season, and both actors won or were nominated for multiple critics' awards. The show was filmed in British Columbia, Canada, where the motel and house exterior were constructed as a practical set.
