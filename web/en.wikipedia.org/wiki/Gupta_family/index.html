Gupta family

The Gupta family is a wealthy Indian-born South African business family whose close relationship with former South African president Jacob Zuma became the focus of allegations of state capture and corruption. The three brothers — Ajay, Atul and Rajesh Gupta — moved from Uttar Pradesh, India, to South Africa in 1993 and built a business empire spanning mining, media, technology and engineering through companies such as Sahara Computers, Oakbay Investments, Tegeta, The New Age newspaper and ANN7 television.

The family came to national prominence in 2013 after a chartered aircraft carrying wedding guests landed at Waterkloof Air Force Base without proper authorisation — the "Guptagate" scandal. Subsequent investigations, including the Zondo Commission into state capture, examined the family's influence over cabinet appointments and state-owned enterprises. The brothers left South Africa for Dubai in 2018 as investigations intensified; the article describes events in general terms as reported widely in the press.
