Bryan Christopher Williams (born February 15, 1969), better known by his stage names Birdman and Baby, is an American rapper and record executive. With his older brother Ronald "Slim" Williams, he co-founded the record label Cash Money Records in 1991, which signed and launched the careers of Lil Wayne, Drake, Nicki Minaj, Juvenile and the Hot Boys, becoming one of the most successful independent hip hop labels.

Career

Birdman began his recording career as one half of the duo the B.G.'z and performed with the group Big Tymers alongside producer Mannie Fresh, achieving success with singles such as "Still Fly" (2002). His solo debut album Birdman was released in 2002, followed by Fast Money (2005), 5 * Stunna (2007) — which included the hit "Pop Bottles" featuring Lil Wayne — and Priceless (2009). He recorded the collaborative album Like Father, Like Son (2006) with Lil Wayne, spawning the single "Stuntin' Like My Daddy", and Rich Gang (2013) with Young Thug and Rich Homie Quan, including the hit "Lifestyle" (2014).

Business ventures and disputes

Beyond music, Birdman has pursued ventures in oil and gas, clothing and spirits. His tenure has also been marked by public business and legal disputes with former Cash Money artists, notably a widely publicised falling-out with Lil Wayne over unpaid royalties, which was settled in 2018. Birdman is noted for his flamboyant "stunting" lifestyle lyrics and for his role in building Cash Money into a major-label powerhouse through its distribution deal with Universal.
