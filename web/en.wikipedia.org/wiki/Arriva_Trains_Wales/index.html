Arriva Trains Wales

An Arriva Trains Wales Class 175 at Cardiff Central

Arriva Trains Wales (Welsh: Trenau Arriva Cymru) was a British train operating company owned by Arriva UK Trains that operated the Wales & Borders franchise from 7 December 2003 until 13 October 2018, when services transferred to KeolisAmey Wales (later Transport for Wales Rail).

Services

The company operated most local and regional rail services within Wales, including the Valley Lines around Cardiff, the North Wales Coast Line, the Cambrian Line, and the Heart of Wales line, plus cross-border services to Manchester, Birmingham, and the West Country. Its principal hub was Cardiff Central.

Fleet

Its fleet included Class 142, 143, 150, 153, 158 and 175 diesel multiple units, locomotive-hauled Mark 3 coaching stock on the "Gerald" premier service, and Class 121 "bubble car" units in its final years.
