Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse is a 2018 American animated superhero film based on the Marvel Comics character Miles Morales. Produced by Columbia Pictures and Sony Pictures Animation in association with Marvel Entertainment, it was distributed by Sony Pictures Releasing. The film was directed by Bob Persichetti, Peter Ramsey, and Rodney Rothman from a screenplay by Phil Lord and Rothman. It stars Shameik Moore as the voice of Miles Morales, alongside Jake Johnson, Hailee Steinfeld, Mahershala Ali, Brian Tyree Henry, Lily Tomlin, Luna Lauren Vélez, John Mulaney, Kimiko Glenn, Nicolas Cage, and Liev Schreiber.

Set in a shared multiverse called the "Spider-Verse", the film follows teenager Miles Morales of Brooklyn, who is bitten by a radioactive spider and witnesses the death of his universe's Peter Parker at the hands of the crime lord Kingpin. Kingpin has constructed a particle accelerator—the Super-Collider—hoping to access parallel universes to bring back his deceased wife and son. The activation of the collider pulls several Spider-people from other dimensions into Miles's world: an older, jaded Peter B. Parker; Gwen Stacy / Spider-Woman; the black-and-white Spider-Man Noir; the anime-inspired Peni Parker; and the cartoonish Spider-Ham. Miles must learn to control his new powers and work with the other heroes to return them home and stop Kingpin before reality collapses.

Development and style

Development began after the email leak of 2014 revealed Sony's plans for an animated Spider-Man film by Phil Lord and Christopher Miller. The film's distinctive visual style blends computer animation with traditional hand-drawn comic book techniques, including printed halftone dots, thought captions, and onomatopoeia, making the finished film resemble a living comic book. Animators worked on ones (12 frames per second) rather than the usual twos for many sequences to heighten the graphic effect. The production employed up to 140 animators, the largest crew ever used by Sony Pictures Animation for a feature film.

Reception

The film premiered at the Regency Village Theater in Los Angeles on December 1, 2018, and was released in the United States on December 14. It grossed $384.3 million worldwide against a $90 million budget and received widespread critical acclaim for its animation, story, voice acting, humor, and emotional depth. It won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature at the 91st Academy Awards, as well as the Golden Globe and BAFTA awards in the same category, making it the first non-Disney/Pixar film to win the Oscar in that category since Rango (2011).

Sequels

A sequel, Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse, was released in June 2023 to further acclaim and greater commercial success. A third film, Spider-Man: Beyond the Spider-Verse, is in production.
