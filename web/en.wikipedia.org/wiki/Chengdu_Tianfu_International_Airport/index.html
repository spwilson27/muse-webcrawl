Chengdu Tianfu International Airport
IATA: TFU · ICAO: ZUTF
Location: Jianyang, Chengdu, Sichuan, China
Opened: 27 June 2021

Chengdu Tianfu International Airport (IATA: TFU, ICAO: ZUTF) is a major international airport serving Chengdu, the capital of Sichuan province in China. Located in Jianyang, about 50 kilometres southeast of central Chengdu, it opened on 27 June 2021 and made Chengdu the third Chinese city, after Beijing and Shanghai, to have two international airports, alongside Chengdu Shuangliu International Airport.

Facilities

The airport's first phase includes two terminals and three runways, with a design intended to handle tens of millions of passengers annually. It serves as a hub for airlines including Air China and Sichuan Airlines.

Transport

The airport is connected to the city by motorways and by Chengdu Metro Line 18, with high-speed rail links as well.
