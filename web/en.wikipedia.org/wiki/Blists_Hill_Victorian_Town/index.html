Blists Hill Victorian Town is an open-air museum built on a former industrial site in the Ironbridge Gorge, near Madeley in Telford, Shropshire, England. It recreates the sights, sounds and smells of a Victorian industrial town in the years around 1900, and is one of the ten Ironbridge Gorge Museums.

The site

The 52-acre site was once the location of brick and tile works, blast furnaces, and coal, iron and fireclay mines, served by the Shropshire Canal. Surviving features include the canal, inclined plane remains, and brick kilns. The museum opened in 1973 and has grown by relocating historic buildings from around the region and constructing replicas.

The town

Costumed staff operate working shops and trades, including a chemist, grocer, ironmonger, sweet shop, bakery, candle maker, printer, and a branch of Lloyds Bank where visitors can exchange modern money for replica Victorian tokens. Other attractions include a working steam winder at a mineshaft, fairground rides, a school, a doctor's surgery, cottages, and the New Inn public house serving period-style refreshments.

Significance

The Ironbridge Gorge is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, recognised as a cradle of the Industrial Revolution. Blists Hill interprets the social history of that industrial landscape for a large visiting public and school groups.
