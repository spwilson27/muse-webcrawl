Pierre Bergé, French businessman and patron of the arts.

Pierre Bergé (14 November 1930 – 8 September 2017) was a French industrialist, art collector and patron of the arts. He is best known as the business partner and lifelong companion of the couturier Yves Saint Laurent, with whom he co-founded the house of Yves Saint Laurent in 1961.

Early life

Bergé was born on the Île d'Oléron, off the Atlantic coast of France. As a young man he moved to Paris, where on his first day in the capital he witnessed the poet Paul Éluard being applauded in the street — an omen he later recalled fondly. He became involved in leftist politics, co-founding the magazine La Patrie Mondiale and defending the civil rights of Algerians during the Algerian War.

Yves Saint Laurent

Bergé met Saint Laurent in 1958. When Saint Laurent was dismissed from Christian Dior in 1960, the two men decided to found their own house, backed by the American businessman J. Mack Robinson. Bergé ran the business side of the enterprise for decades, describing himself as having "the head of an accountant." He served as chief executive of Yves Saint Laurent until 2002, when the house was sold to the Gucci Group.

Cultural activities

Bergé directed the Théâtre de l'Athénée in Paris, presided over the Opéra Bastille, and was a major supporter of French cinema, theatre and the visual arts. He co-founded the magazine Globe and the gay magazine Têtu, and was a prominent supporter of the association Act Up-Paris and of AIDS research funding.

Art collection and the 2009 sale

With Saint Laurent, Bergé assembled one of the great private art collections of the twentieth century, filling their Paris apartment and the Château Gabriel in Normandy with works by Picasso, Matisse, Mondrian, Brâncuși and Goya. After Saint Laurent's death in 2008, the collection was auctioned at Christie's in Paris in February 2009 — the celebrated "sale of the century" — raising some €375 million, with proceeds going to medical research and the Pierre Bergé – Yves Saint Laurent Foundation.

Death

Bergé died on 8 September 2017 at Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, aged 86, after suffering from myopathy.
