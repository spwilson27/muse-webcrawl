Image gradient

An image gradient is a directional change in the intensity or colour in an image. The gradient of an image is one of the fundamental building blocks of image processing: the gradient at each pixel gives the direction of the steepest increase in intensity and the rate of that increase.

Mathematics

For an image I(x, y), the gradient is the vector of partial derivatives (∂I/∂x, ∂I/∂y). In digital images these derivatives are approximated by convolution with finite-difference kernels such as the Sobel, Prewitt or Roberts operators.

Applications

Gradients are used in edge detection, feature detection, texture analysis and gradient-domain image editing techniques such as Poisson blending. The gradient magnitude is large at edges and near zero in flat regions.
