Languages of Afghanistan

Afghanistan is a multilingual country in which some thirty languages are spoken. The official languages are Dari and Pashto. Dari is the variety of Persian spoken in Afghanistan and serves as the country's principal lingua franca, understood by the great majority of the population; it is the first language of the Tajiks, Hazaras, Aimaks and many others. Pashto, an Eastern Iranian language, is the native tongue of the Pashtuns, the largest ethnic group, concentrated in the south and east.

Major languages

Under the constitution of 2004, Dari and Pashto are the official languages of the state, and other languages—including Uzbek, Turkmen, Balochi, Pashayi, Nuristani languages and Pamiri languages—are recognized as a "third official language" in the areas where they are spoken by a majority. Estimated proportions of first-language speakers vary widely between sources: Dari is commonly placed around half the population, Pashto around a third to two-fifths, with Uzbek the third-largest language at roughly ten percent.

Other languages

The north of the country is home to speakers of Turkic languages, principally Uzbek and Turkmen. Balochi is spoken in the southwest, and the mountainous northeast (Badakhshan) hosts a remarkable diversity of Pamiri (Eastern Iranian) languages, including Shughni, Wakhi and Ishkashimi. The isolated valleys of Nuristan preserve their own Nuristani language family, including Kamkata-vari, Waigali and Ashkun. Smaller communities speak Pashayi, Gujari, Brahui (a Dravidian outlier) and Kyrgyz, the latter among the Afghan Kyrgyz of the Wakhan Corridor.

Script and education

Dari, Pashto and most of the country's languages are written in forms of the Perso-Arabic script, while Uzbek in Afghanistan has also used Arabic-based orthography. Bilingualism is widespread, and Dari functions as the main language of government, media and inter-ethnic communication, with Pashto prominent in the south and east. English is widely taught in schools and is important in higher education and international work.
