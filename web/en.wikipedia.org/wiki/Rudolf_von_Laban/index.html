Rudolf von Laban

Rudolf von Laban (15 December 1879 – 1 July 1958), also known as Rudolf Laban, was an Austro-Hungarian, German and British dance artist, choreographer and dance theorist. He is considered a pioneer of modern dance in Europe and the founder of a system of movement analysis and notation.

Life and work

Laban was born in Pressburg (today Bratislava) in the Kingdom of Hungary. He studied architecture in Paris before turning to movement and dance. In Germany he established schools of expressive dance (Ausdruckstanz) and developed large-scale "movement choirs" involving amateur dancers.

After falling out of favour with the Nazi regime in the 1930s, he emigrated to England in 1938, where he worked on movement in industry and education and co-founded the Art of Movement Studio in Manchester.

Legacy

Laban's system of notation, published in 1928 as Schrifttanz, developed into Labanotation, one of the most widely used systems for recording dance. His framework of movement analysis — describing body, effort, shape and space — underlies Laban Movement Analysis, used in dance, theatre, therapy and ergonomics. The institution now known as Trinity Laban Conservatoire of Music and Dance in London preserves his name.
