Michele Ferrero

Michele Ferrero (26 April 1925 – 14 February 2015) was an Italian billionaire businessman, the owner of the Ferrero SpA chocolate manufacturer, Europe's second largest confectionery company.

Career

Ferrero joined the family business founded by his father Pietro Ferrero in Alba, Piedmont. He oversaw the creation and worldwide expansion of brands including Nutella, Ferrero Rocher, Kinder and Tic Tac. Nutella was launched in 1964 based on an earlier hazelnut spread.

Wealth and legacy

At his death he was widely reported as the richest person in Italy. Management passed to his son Giovanni Ferrero. The company remained family-owned and grew into one of the largest chocolate and confectionery groups in the world.
