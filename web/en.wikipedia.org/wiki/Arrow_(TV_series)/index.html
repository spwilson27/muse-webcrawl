Arrow is an American superhero television series developed by Greg Berlanti, Marc Guggenheim and Andrew Kreisberg, based on the DC Comics character Green Arrow. It premiered on The CW on 10 October 2012 and ran for eight seasons, concluding on 28 January 2020. The series launched the shared Arrowverse franchise.


Stephen Amell as Oliver Queen / Green Arrow

Premise

Billionaire playboy Oliver Queen (Stephen Amell) returns to Starling City after five years shipwrecked on the island of Lian Yu, secretly operating as a hooded vigilante armed with a bow. Across the seasons he assembles a team including John Diggle and Felicity Smoak, confronts enemies such as Malcolm Merlyn, Slade Wilson and Ra's al Ghul, and gradually transforms from a kill-list vigilante into the hero Green Arrow.

Production and reception

Filmed in Vancouver, the series was praised for its grounded tone and stunt choreography, and it revived DC Comics properties on television. Its success led to spin-offs including The Flash, Supergirl, Legends of Tomorrow and Batwoman, united in annual crossover events culminating in "Crisis on Infinite Earths" (2019–20), during which Oliver Queen dies and is succeeded by the multiverse's new order.
