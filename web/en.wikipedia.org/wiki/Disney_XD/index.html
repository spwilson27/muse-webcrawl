Disney XD is an American pay television channel owned by the Disney Entertainment unit of the Walt Disney Company. Aimed primarily at older children and pre-teens, the channel broadcasts original animated and live-action series, acquired programmes, and films, with an emphasis on action, comedy, and gaming-related content.

History

Disney XD launched on February 13, 2009, replacing Toon Disney in the United States (and the Jetix brand internationally). The name "XD" was chosen because it resembles a smiley face and was intended to convey an extreme, boy-targeted brand, though the channel's programming soon broadened. The channel expanded internationally over the following years, though many international versions were closed in the late 2010s and early 2020s as Disney shifted focus to the Disney+ streaming service.

Programming

Original series produced for Disney XD have included the animated series Phineas and Ferb (shared with Disney Channel), Kick Buttowski: Suburban Daredevil, Motorcity, Tron: Uprising, Randy Cunningham: 9th Grade Ninja, Wander Over Yonder, Gravity Falls (later seasons), Penn Zero: Part-Time Hero, Star vs. the Forces of Evil (early seasons), Milo Murphy's Law, DuckTales (2017), and Big Hero 6: The Series, as well as Marvel animated series such as Ultimate Spider-Man, Avengers Assemble, and Guardians of the Galaxy. Live-action originals included Zeke and Luther, I'm in the Band, Pair of Kings, Lab Rats, Kickin' It, and Mighty Med.

Programming blocks have included a Marvel Universe block, anime broadcasts such as Pokémon and Beyblade Burst, and the D|XP block of gaming and eSports-oriented programming launched in 2017.
