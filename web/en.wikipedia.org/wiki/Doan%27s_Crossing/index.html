Doan's Crossing

Doan's Crossing was a historic ford and trading post site on the Red River in Wilbarger County, Texas, United States, near the present-day community of Doans. Established in 1878 by the Doan family, it became a principal crossing point for cattle drives on the Western Trail to Dodge City, Kansas.

History

Corwin F. Doan and his uncle Jonathan Doan settled at the crossing in 1878 and built a store and adobe house serving drovers moving Texas cattle north. At its peak in the early 1880s, tens of thousands of cattle crossed the river at Doan's each season, and the settlement included a hotel, saloon, blacksmith, school, and post office.

Decline

The arrival of railroads in the region in the mid-1880s ended the great trail drives, and the settlement dwindled to a small community. The annual Doan's May Day Picnic, held since 1884, commemorates the crossing and is among the oldest continuous celebrations in Texas. A granite monument marks the site.
