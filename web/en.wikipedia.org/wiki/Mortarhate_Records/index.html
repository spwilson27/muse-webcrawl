Mortarhate Records is a British independent record label associated with the anarcho-punk movement. It was set up in the early 1980s by members of the anarcho-punk band Conflict, following the model of Crass Records, to release the band's own recordings and those of like-minded groups on their own terms, outside the commercial music industry.

History

Conflict founded Mortarhate after early releases with Corpus Christi Records, using the label to put out their own singles and albums — including Increase the Pressure (1984) and the live and studio material that made them one of the most prominent bands of the anarcho-punk scene — together with records by other bands from the same milieu, among them Icons of Filth and Exit-Stance. Like other DIY punk labels, Mortarhate kept prices low, printed political statements on its sleeves, and distributed through independent networks and the Cartel distribution system.

Legacy

The label's catalogue documents the second wave of British anarcho-punk in the mid-1980s. Conflict continued to reactivate the Mortarhate name for later releases and reissues, and its records have remained in circulation among collectors of punk and hardcore.
