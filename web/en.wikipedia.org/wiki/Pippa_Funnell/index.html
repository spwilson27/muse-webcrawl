Pippa Funnell

Philippa Rachel "Pippa" Funnell MBE (née Nolan, born 7 October 1968) is an equestrian sportswoman who competes in eventing for Britain. She is best known for winning the Rolex Grand Slam of Eventing in 2003, the first — and for many years only — rider to achieve the feat by winning Kentucky, Badminton, and Burghley in a single year.

Career

Funnell won gold at the European Young Rider Championships and went on to represent Great Britain at three Olympic Games, winning team silver at Sydney 2000 and Athens 2004 and team bronze at Rio 2016. Her Grand Slam year of 2003 was achieved with two horses, Supreme Rock (Badminton) and Primmore's Pride (Kentucky and Burghley). She has also won multiple European Championship medals, including individual gold in 1999 and 2001.

Personal life and other work

Funnell is married to show jumper William Funnell. She has written an autobiography and the popular "Tilly's Pony Tails" series of children's books. She was appointed MBE for services to equestrian sport.
