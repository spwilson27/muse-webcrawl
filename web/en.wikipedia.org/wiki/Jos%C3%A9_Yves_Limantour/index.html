José Yves Limantour Marquet (1854–1935) was a Mexican financier and statesman who served as Secretary of Finance under President Porfirio Díaz from 1893 until the fall of the regime in 1911. Of French descent, he was the leading figure of the científicos, the technocratic advisers of the Porfiriato.

Financial reforms

Limantour balanced the federal budget for the first time in Mexico's history, renegotiated the foreign debt, consolidated railway concessions, and in 1905 placed Mexico on the gold standard. His fiscal conservatism stabilized public finances but also limited social spending. He resigned in March 1911 as the Mexican Revolution began, and lived in exile in France.
