Reuters

Reuters is a news agency owned by Thomson Reuters, founded in London in 1851 by Paul Julius Reuter, originally famed for transmitting stock-market prices by telegraph — and earlier, famously, by carrier pigeon across a gap in the telegraph network.

Agency today

Reuters employs thousands of journalists worldwide and supplies text, photographs and video to broadcasters, newspapers and websites, competing with the Associated Press and Agence France-Presse. Its editorial principles emphasise accuracy, speed and freedom from bias, overseen by a Trust Principles framework dating to 1941.
