List of Disney Channel original films

Disney Channel has broadcast a large catalogue of made-for-television films, branded since the late 1990s as Disney Channel Original Movies (DCOMs). The earliest Disney Channel "Premiere Films" date to the channel's launch in 1983; the DCOM brand began with Under Wraps in 1997 and the seasonal tradition of Halloweentown (1998). Several DCOMs became major franchises, most notably High School Musical (2006), whose sequel premiered to 17.2 million viewers, a record for basic cable at the time.

Selected films

  • 1997: Under Wraps.
  • 1998: You Lucky Dog, Brink!, Halloweentown.
  • 1999: Zenon: Girl of the 21st Century, Smart House, Johnny Tsunami, The Thirteenth Year.
  • 2000: Quints, Alley Cats Strike, The Color of Friendship.
  • 2001: The Luck of the Irish, Motocrossed, Halloweentown II: Kalabar's Revenge.
  • 2002: Cadet Kelly, Get a Clue, Double Teamed.
  • 2003: The Cheetah Girls, The Even Stevens Movie, Eddie's Million Dollar Cook-Off.
  • 2005: Now You See It..., Kim Possible Movie: So the Drama, The Proud Family Movie.
  • 2006: High School Musical, The Cheetah Girls 2, Read It and Weep.
  • 2007: Jump In!, High School Musical 2, Twitches Too.
  • 2008: Camp Rock, The Cheetah Girls: One World, Minutemen.
  • 2009: Dadnapped, Princess Protection Program, Wizards of Waverly Place: The Movie.
  • 2011: Lemonade Mouth, Phineas and Ferb the Movie: Across the 2nd Dimension, Good Luck Charlie, It's Christmas!
  • 2013: Teen Beach Movie.
  • 2015: Descendants, Teen Beach 2, Bad Hair Day.
  • 2017: Descendants 2, Tangled: Before Ever After.
  • 2018: Zombies, Freaky Friday.
  • 2019: Kim Possible, Descendants 3.
  • 2022: Zombies 3 (Disney+ premiere).

The full catalogue contains well over one hundred films, continuing with later releases on Disney Channel and Disney+.
