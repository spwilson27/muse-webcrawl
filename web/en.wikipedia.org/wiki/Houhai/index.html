Houhai

Houhai (simplified Chinese: 后海; traditional Chinese: 後海; pinyin: Hòuhǎi, "Rear Lake") is a lake in central Beijing, China, and the largest of the three lakes — along with Qianhai ("Front Lake") and Xihai ("West Lake") — that make up Shichahai. The area around Houhai is famous for its historic hutong lanes, courtyard residences, bars and restaurants.

Geography and history

Houhai lies in Xicheng District, northwest of the Forbidden City. The lake was part of the old canal system that carried grain barges into the Yuan dynasty capital, and its shores were lined with temples, princely mansions and markets. Notable nearby sites include the former residences of Soong Ching-ling and Guo Moruo, and the Drum and Bell Towers to the north.

Since the early 2000s the hutongs around Houhai, especially along Yandai Xiejie and the Silver Ingot Bridge (Yinding Bridge) which divides Houhai from Qianhai, have become a popular nightlife and tourist district, while rickshaw tours explore the surviving courtyard neighbourhoods.
