Sanji (Japanese: サンジ), born Vinsmoke Sanji (ヴィンスモーク・サンジ) and commonly called "Black Leg" Sanji, is a fictional character in the One Piece franchise created by Eiichiro Oda. He is the cook of the Straw Hat Pirates, one of the crew of protagonist Monkey D. Luffy, and the fifth member to join. He first appeared in the manga in 1998 during the Baratie arc.

Aboard the crew's ship Sanji prepares meals and serves as one of the crew's three strongest fighters alongside Luffy and Roronoa Zoro. His dream is to find the All Blue, a legendary sea where fish from all four oceans gather — a chef's paradise.

Conception and creation

Oda designed Sanji as a blond, sharp-dressed cook whose spiral eyebrow curls in the opposite direction on each side (only one is normally visible because his hair covers an eye). His fighting style, based almost entirely on kicks, reflects his belief that a cook's hands must be reserved for cooking and never dirtied in battle — though he will use knives against edible opponents. Sanji was originally to be named "Naruto", but the name was changed because the manga Naruto was about to begin serialisation.

Character

Sanji is chivalrous to a fault and refuses to harm or even insult women, frequently falling into exaggerated romantic swooning. He has a fierce rivalry with Zoro, with whom he constantly bickers, though the two trust each other completely in battle. As a cook he wastes nothing and will feed anyone who is hungry, friend or enemy alike — a value he learned from his mentor "Red Leg" Zeff, who once sacrificed his own leg and endured starvation alongside the young Sanji after a shipwreck.

Story

Sanji was born the third son of the Vinsmoke family, rulers of the Germa Kingdom, whose children were genetically enhanced. Judged a failure for retaining his emotions, he was imprisoned and disowned, escaping to the East Blue, where he became an apprentice and later sous-chef at the floating restaurant Baratie under Zeff. After helping defend the Baratie from the pirate Don Krieg, Sanji joined the Straw Hats on Luffy's invitation, seeking the All Blue.

Subsequent arcs reveal his past: during the Whole Cake Island arc, his family forces him into a political marriage with Charlotte Pudding, daughter of the Emperor Big Mom; Sanji's kindness to Pudding and the support of his crew — and his elder sister Reiju — free him from the arrangement, and he formally renounces the Vinsmoke name for a time while still accepting his lineage's technology, including a raid suit. In the Wano Country arc he struggles with the awakening of his dormant genetic modifications, fearing the loss of his humanity, before accepting them as part of himself.

Abilities

Sanji fights with the "Black Leg Style", powerful kicks taught by Zeff, later augmented by techniques learned during two years of training on Kamabakka Kingdom, including Sky Walk (running on air) and Blue Walk underwater. His signature technique Diable Jambe heats his leg by friction to ignite it; his later Ifrit Jambe raises the temperature further with Armament Haki. He is also a master chef and a capable strategist, having twice saved the crew under the alias "Mr. Prince".

Reception

Sanji has consistently placed among the most popular characters in One Piece popularity polls worldwide. Critics have praised the Whole Cake Island arc for deepening his character beyond his comic romantic persona.
