Human sexuality


Illustration of human sexual development and diversity.

Human sexuality is the way people experience and express themselves sexually. This involves biological, psychological, physical, erotic, emotional, social, or spiritual feelings and behaviors. Because it is a broad term, which has varied with historical contexts over time, it lacks a precise definition.

Biological and psychological aspects

The biological and physical aspects of sexuality largely concern the human reproductive functions, including the human sexual response cycle, and the basic biological drive that exists in all animal species. A person's sexual orientation refers to their pattern of sexual interest in the opposite or same gender, which can be categorized as heterosexuality, homosexuality, bisexuality, or asexuality. Psychological aspects include the ways sexuality is experienced and expressed in thoughts, fantasies, desires, and relationships.

Social and cultural aspects

Social aspects deal with the effects of human society on one's sexuality, while spirituality concerns an individual's spiritual connection with others. Culture and religion have strongly shaped norms and laws relating to sexuality throughout history, and attitudes continue to vary widely across societies.

Health and education

Sexual health involves the prevention and treatment of sexually transmitted infections, contraception, and reproductive health. Comprehensive sex education is considered by health organizations an important tool for reducing risks such as unintended pregnancy and STIs.
