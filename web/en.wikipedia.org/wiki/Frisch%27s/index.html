Frisch's

Frisch's Restaurants, Inc. is an American regional restaurant chain headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, best known for its Frisch's Big Boy restaurants, which operated under the Big Boy franchise system in Ohio, Kentucky and Indiana.

History

The company traces its origins to 1905, when Samuel Frisch opened a café in Cincinnati. His son David Frisch took over the business and in the 1930s–40s began developing drive-in restaurants. In 1947 Frisch became one of the first franchisees of the Big Boy system, acquiring exclusive rights for parts of the Midwest and building the chain around the double-decker Big Boy hamburger, tartar-sauce-topped sandwiches and a mascot statue of a chubby boy in checked overalls.

Operations

At its peak the chain numbered well over 200 restaurants. It faced mounting competition and financial difficulties in the 2010s and 2020s, with ownership passing to private equity firms and many locations closing; a wave of evictions and closures in 2024 sharply reduced the chain's footprint in the Cincinnati area.
