Minimal polynomial (linear algebra)

In linear algebra, the minimal polynomial of a square matrix A is the monic polynomial of least degree that annihilates it — that is, the unique monic polynomial μA of smallest degree such that μA(A) = 0. A similar definition applies to a linear operator on a finite-dimensional vector space.

Properties

The minimal polynomial divides every polynomial that annihilates A, in particular the characteristic polynomial (by the Cayley–Hamilton theorem). A scalar λ is an eigenvalue of A if and only if it is a root of the minimal polynomial. The matrix is diagonalisable exactly when its minimal polynomial splits into distinct linear factors.
