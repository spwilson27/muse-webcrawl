Determination of the day of the week

The determination of the day of the week for any date may be performed with a variety of algorithms, including Zeller's congruence, Gauss's algorithm, and John Conway's Doomsday algorithm.

Doomsday algorithm

Conway's method rests on the observation that certain dates always fall on the same weekday within a year — the "doomsdays" — such as 4/4, 6/6, 8/8, 10/10, 12/12, and the last day of February. From the century anchor and the year's doomsday, any date's weekday can be computed by counting offsets.

Zeller's congruence

Zeller's congruence is a formula published by Christian Zeller in 1882 that computes the weekday from the day, month, and year arithmetically.
