Filtration

Filtration is a physical separation process that separates solid matter and fluid from a mixture using a filter medium that has a complex structure through which only the fluid can pass. Solid particles that cannot pass through the filter medium are described as oversize and the fluid that passes through is called the filtrate.

Methods

Filtration can be driven by gravity, vacuum, pressure, or centrifugal force. Common techniques include hot filtration, cold filtration, and membrane filtration such as microfiltration, ultrafiltration, and reverse osmosis.

Applications

Filtration is used in water treatment, air conditioning and HVAC systems, chemical manufacturing, pharmaceuticals, and laboratory analysis, as well as in biological contexts such as the kidney's glomerular filtration.
