Satyaki, also known as Yuyudhana, is a warrior in the Hindu epic Mahabharata. A member of the Vrishni clan of the Yadavas and a devoted follower of Krishna, he was a disciple of Arjuna and fought on the side of the Pandavas in the Kurukshetra War, unlike most of the Narayani Sena, which Krishna gave to the Kauravas.

Role in the war

Satyaki served as one of the seven commanders (akshauhini leaders) of the Pandava army. On the fourteenth day he defended Arjuna during his quest to kill Jayadratha, duelling with Bhurishravas, whom Arjuna killed while Bhurishravas was preparing to strike the exhausted Satyaki — an episode that drew criticism for its circumstances. He also defeated the Kaurava warrior Alambusha and fought fiercely against Drona's forces. Earlier, Satyaki had acted as Krishna's envoy and was present at peace negotiations in Hastinapura.

Death

Thirty-six years after the war, Satyaki was killed during the fratricidal destruction of the Yadava clans at Prabhasa, when a drunken brawl among the Vrishnis escalated; he was slain by Kritavarma's party after Satyaki killed Kritavarma's companions — an event described in the Mausala Parva.
