Primus is a fictional character in the Transformers franchise. He is the benevolent god of the Transformers, the creator of the planet Cybertron — which is, in most continuities, his own transformed body — and the eternal opposite of the chaos-bringer Unicron. Primus is the source of the Creation Matrix (or Matrix of Leadership) and, through it, of all Transformer life.

Fictional biography

Primus's origin was developed by writer Simon Furman in the Marvel Comics series. According to the mythos, at the dawn of time the extra-dimensional being known as the One created two entities: Unicron, embodying chaos and destruction, and Primus, embodying order and life. The two battled, and Primus, unable to win by force, lured Unicron's essence into a metallic asteroid and trapped his own within another. Unicron reshaped his prison into the planet-eating world that bears his name, while Primus's asteroid became Cybertron, and Primus populated it by creating the Thirteen original Primes and, through them, the Transformer race, who would one day stand against Unicron. In the 2000s "Unicron Trilogy" cartoons and subsequent media, Primus appears more directly, transforming Cybertron itself into a colossal robot form to battle Unicron, and in the Aligned continuity (including Transformers: Prime) the planet's core is Primus's spark chamber, slowly poisoned by Dark Energon.

Character

Primus is usually a remote, background presence — a god who speaks rarely, often through proxies such as Vector Prime or the oracle of the Matrix — reflecting his role as a creation myth rather than an active combatant. His name derives from the Latin primus, "first". In toy lines, a planet-sized Primus figure was released in the Transformers: Cybertron series (2005–06), transforming between Cybertron and robot modes.
