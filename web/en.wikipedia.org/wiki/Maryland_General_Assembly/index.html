Maryland General Assembly
Type: Bicameral
Houses: Senate; House of Delegates
Seats: 188 (47 senators, 141 delegates)
Meeting place: Maryland State House, Annapolis

The Maryland General Assembly is the state legislature of Maryland. It is a bicameral body consisting of a 47-member Senate and a 141-member House of Delegates. The General Assembly meets at the Maryland State House in Annapolis.

History

Maryland's legislative tradition dates to colonial assemblies in the 17th century. The modern structure was established under the Constitution of Maryland of 1867.

Composition and sessions

Members are elected every four years. Regular sessions last 90 days, beginning in January. The legislature enacts the state budget, laws, and proposes constitutional amendments. Special sessions may be called by the governor.

Leadership

The Senate is led by the President of the Senate and the House by the Speaker of the House of Delegates.
