Molson Coors Beverage Company is a Canadian-American multinational drink and brewing company headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, with main offices also in Montreal and in Golden, Colorado. It was formed in 2005 through the merger of the American Coors Brewing Company and the Canadian Molson Brewery, two of the oldest brewing families in North America.

History

Molson was founded in Montreal in 1786 by John Molson, making it one of Canada's oldest companies. Coors was founded in Golden, Colorado, in 1873 by German immigrant Adolph Coors. The two companies merged on February 9, 2005, creating the Molson Coors Brewing Company, then the world's fifth-largest brewer by volume.

In 2016, following Anheuser-Busch InBev's acquisition of SABMiller, Molson Coors acquired SABMiller's full stake in the MillerCoors joint venture for approximately US$12 billion, gaining full ownership of the Miller brand portfolio, including Miller Lite, Miller High Life, and Miller Genuine Draft, as well as the rights to brands such as Peroni and Pilsner Urquell in the United States. The company renamed itself the Molson Coors Beverage Company in 2020 to reflect its expansion beyond beer into other beverages.

Brands

The company's core portfolio includes Coors Light, Coors Banquet, Molson Canadian, Carling, Miller Lite, Miller High Life, Blue Moon Belgian White, Leinenkugel's, and Vizzy Hard Seltzer. It has also entered energy drinks and non-alcoholic beverages through partnerships and acquisitions.

Operations

Molson Coors operates large breweries in Golden, Colorado (the largest single-site brewery in the world), Milwaukee, Wisconsin, Montreal, and elsewhere in North America and Europe. Its shares trade on the New York Stock Exchange and Toronto Stock Exchange under the symbol TAP.
