Urdd Gobaith Cymru


The Urdd logo: red, green and white

Urdd Gobaith Cymru (the Welsh League of Hope) is a national youth organisation in Wales, founded in 1922 by Sir Ifan ab Owen Edwards to give Welsh-speaking children and young people opportunities to live their lives through the medium of Welsh. With tens of thousands of members aged roughly 8 to 25, it is one of the largest youth movements in Europe.

Activities

The Urdd is best known for the Urdd National Eisteddfod (Eisteddfod Genedlaethol yr Urdd), one of Europe's largest youth arts festivals, held annually during the May half-term, alternating between north and south Wales. It also organises regional and national sporting competitions through Urdd Chwaraeon, runs residential centres at Llangrannog, Glan-llyn and Cardiff, publishes Welsh-language magazines such as Cip, and stages the annual Bryn Terfel Scholarship for young performers.

Symbolism

The organisation's triangular logo comes in red (courage), green (the land of Wales) and white (purity/goodwill). Its peace and goodwill message, broadcast by Welsh schoolchildren every May since 1922, was the world's first such annual message from children and is now translated into many languages.
