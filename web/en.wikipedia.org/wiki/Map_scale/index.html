Map scale

The scale of a map is the ratio of a distance on the map to the corresponding distance on the ground. It is commonly expressed as a representative fraction such as 1:50,000, as a verbal scale, or graphically as a scale bar.

Large and small scale

A large-scale map, such as 1:25,000, shows a small area in great detail; a small-scale map, such as 1:10,000,000, shows a large area with little detail. Because the Earth is curved, scale varies slightly across a map projection, a distortion quantified by Tissot's indicatrix.
