Fiber Bragg grating

A fiber Bragg grating (FBG) is a type of distributed Bragg reflector constructed in a short segment of optical fiber that reflects particular wavelengths of light and transmits all others. This is achieved by creating a periodic variation in the refractive index of the fiber core.

Principle

Light traveling in the fiber is reflected when its wavelength satisfies the Bragg condition, twice the product of the effective refractive index and the grating period. Changes in strain or temperature shift the reflected wavelength, making FBGs useful as sensors.

Applications

Fiber Bragg gratings are used in telecommunications as filters and multiplexers, and in fiber-optic sensing for measuring strain, temperature, and pressure in structures such as bridges, aircraft, and pipelines.
