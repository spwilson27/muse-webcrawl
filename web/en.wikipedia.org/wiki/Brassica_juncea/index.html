Brassica juncea

Brassica juncea
Common names: Brown mustard, Indian mustard, leaf mustard
Family: Brassicaceae
Hybrid: B. rapa × B. nigra

Brassica juncea, commonly known as brown mustard, Indian mustard or leaf mustard, is a species of mustard plant. It is an allotetraploid hybrid of Brassica rapa and Brassica nigra.

Uses

Grown as a leaf vegetable (mustard greens, Chinese gai choy), as an oilseed and spice (brown mustard seed, central to Indian cooking and Dijon-style mustards), and as a green manure. It is used in phytoremediation because it accumulates heavy metals from contaminated soils.

Cultivation

Major producers include India, Canada, Nepal, Ukraine and Myanmar. Varieties range from mild salad leaves to pungent curled greens. See Mustard plant, Brassica.
