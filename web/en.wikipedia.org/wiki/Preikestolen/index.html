Preikestolen

Preikestolen or Prekestolen ("Pulpit Rock") is a steep cliff rising 604 metres above the Lysefjorden in Rogaland county, Norway. Its nearly flat top, about 25 by 25 metres, is one of Norway's most famous natural tourist attractions.

Geology and access

The cliff was formed by frost weathering during the last ice age. A marked hiking trail of about 3.8 kilometres from the Preikestolen Fjellstue leads to the summit plateau; the hike takes roughly two hours each way and draws hundreds of thousands of visitors annually.
