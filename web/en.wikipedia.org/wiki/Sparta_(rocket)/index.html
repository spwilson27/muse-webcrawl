Sparta (rocket)

Sparta was an Australian three-stage rocket used to launch Australia's first satellite, WRESAT, on 29 November 1967 from the Woomera Test Range in South Australia.

Design

Sparta was built from surplus American Redstone missile components: a modified Redstone first stage, an Antares-2 second stage derived from the Scout programme, and a small solid-fuel third stage. Ten vehicles were supplied under a joint programme between Australia, the United States, and the United Kingdom to study re-entry physics in support of the Blue Streak and warhead research.

Flights

Nine suborbital test flights were flown from 1966; the tenth launch placed the 45 kg WRESAT (Weapons Research Establishment Satellite) into orbit, making Australia the third country to launch a satellite from its own territory. WRESAT carried scientific instruments and remained in orbit for 642 days.
