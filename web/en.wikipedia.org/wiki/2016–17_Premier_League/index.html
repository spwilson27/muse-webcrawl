The 2016–17 Premier League was the 25th season of the Premier League, the top tier of English football. Chelsea won their fifth Premier League title under new manager Antonio Conte.

Summary

After a mixed start, Conte's switch to a 3–4–3 formation in the autumn sparked a run of thirteen consecutive league victories, and Chelsea led the table from November onwards. They secured the title with two matches remaining by winning 1–0 at West Bromwich Albion, and finished with a then-record 30 wins in a 38-game season.

Other positions

Tottenham Hotspur finished second with 86 points, the highest total for a runner-up without winning the title at the time. Manchester City and Liverpool claimed the remaining Champions League places, while defending champions Leicester City finished twelfth. Arsenal's fifth place ended their run of nineteen consecutive top-four finishes under Arsène Wenger.

Awards

N'Golo Kanté, signed by Chelsea from Leicester, won both the PFA Players' Player of the Year and FWA Footballer of the Year awards. Harry Kane won the Golden Boot for the second straight season with 29 goals.
