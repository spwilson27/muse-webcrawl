Home invasion

A home invasion is an illegal and usually forceful entry into an occupied dwelling with the intent to commit a violent crime against the occupants, such as robbery, assault or murder. It differs from burglary, which usually targets unoccupied premises and involves theft only.

Legal treatment

Several jurisdictions, notably in the United States and Australia, treat home invasion as a distinct, aggravated offence carrying heavier penalties than burglary, reflecting the danger to occupants. Laws on self-defence in the home, including castle doctrine, are closely debated in this context.

Prevention

Police guidance emphasises secured doors and windows, lighting, alarms and neighbourhood awareness.
