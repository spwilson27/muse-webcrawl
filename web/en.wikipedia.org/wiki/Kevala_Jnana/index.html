Kevala Jnana

Kevala jnana (Sanskrit: केवल ज्ञान) or Kevalin is the supreme, absolute knowledge — omniscience — in Jainism. It is attained by a soul that has completely destroyed all its karmas, including the knowledge-obscuring (jnanavarniya) karmas. A being who has attained kevala jnana is called a kevalin, and such beings, while still embodied, are revered as arihants; the liberated, disembodied ones are siddhas.

Nature of omniscience

According to Jain doctrine, the soul is inherently omniscient; ignorance results from karmic matter obscuring its natural capacity. When the four destructive (ghati) karmas are annihilated, the soul directly and simultaneously knows all substances in all their modes, past, present and future. Unlike ordinary knowledge, which is mediated by the senses and mind, kevala jnana is immediate and absolute.

Path to attainment

Kevala jnana arises at the thirteenth stage of spiritual development (gunasthana), after the destruction of deluding karma and the attainment of perfect conduct. The tirthankaras, such as Mahavira, are the most celebrated kevalins; after attaining omniscience they preach the path of liberation before finally shedding the remaining non-destructive karmas at death.
