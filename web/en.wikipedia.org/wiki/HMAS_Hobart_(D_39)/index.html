Launched9 January 1964

HMAS Hobart (D 39) was the lead ship of the Perth-class guided missile destroyers operated by the Royal Australian Navy. Built in the United States to a modified Charles F. Adams-class design, she was commissioned in 1965 and served for nearly 35 years, including deployments during the Vietnam War.

Service history

Hobart completed three deployments to Vietnam between 1967 and 1970, providing naval gunfire support and operating with United States Navy forces. On 17 June 1968, during her second deployment, she was attacked in error by United States Air Force jets; two Australian sailors were killed and the ship was damaged, later returning to service after repairs. The incident remains one of the most serious friendly-fire episodes of the war involving Australian forces.

Later career and fate

The destroyer underwent modernisation in the 1970s and continued in fleet service through the 1980s and 1990s. She was decommissioned on 12 May 2000. After negotiations over her disposal, the ship was scuttled off Yankalilla, South Australia, on 5 November 2002 to serve as an artificial reef and dive site, where she remains a popular wreck dive.
