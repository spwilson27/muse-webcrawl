Spawn is a 1997 American superhero film based on the Image Comics character of the same name created by Todd McFarlane. Directed by Mark A.Z. Dippé, it stars Michael Jai White as Al Simmons / Spawn, John Leguizamo as the Violator, and Martin Sheen as Jason Wynn.

Plot

Assassin Al Simmons is murdered by his employer, Jason Wynn, and makes a pact with the demon Malebolgia to return to Earth and see his wife again. Reborn as a "hellspawn" with supernatural powers and a living symbiotic costume, Simmons must resist Hell's plan to use him to lead its armies, while confronting Wynn and the demonic Violator, disguised as a grotesque clown.

Reception

The film grossed over $87 million worldwide against a budget of about $40 million but was panned by critics, who criticized its story and visual effects despite some praise for its dark tone. It is notable as one of the first superhero films with an African-American actor in the title role. A reboot has been in development for many years.
