McMurdo Station is an American Antarctic research station on the southern tip of Ross Island, on the shore of McMurdo Sound. It is the largest community in Antarctica, capable of supporting up to 1,258 residents, and serves as the logistics hub for the United States Antarctic Program, operated by the National Science Foundation.

History

The station was built in 1955–56 during Operation Deep Freeze in preparation for the International Geophysical Year (1957–58), near the site of Robert Falcon Scott's 1902 Discovery expedition hut at Hut Point. It was named for McMurdo Sound, itself named after Archibald McMurdo of HMS Terror. From 1962 to 1972 the station was powered in part by the PM-3A "Nukey Poo" nuclear reactor, later removed. A long-running modernization program, the Antarctic Infrastructure Modernization for Science (AIMS) project, began construction in 2019.

Facilities and transport

McMurdo has roughly 85 buildings, including dormitories, the Albert P. Crary Science and Engineering Laboratory, a fire station, power plant, warehouses, and the Chapel of the Snows. It has a harbour, the seasonal sea-ice port serviced by icebreaking fuel tankers and cargo ships, and three airfields: Williams Field (skiway), Phoenix Airfield, and the seasonal Ice Runway. The station connects by an overland traverse route, the South Pole Traverse, to Amundsen–Scott South Pole Station, and a 3 km road links it with New Zealand's Scott Base.

Science

Research supported from McMurdo spans glaciology, geophysics, climate science, biology in the McMurdo Dry Valleys and under the sea ice, astrophysics via long-duration balloon launches, and studies of Mount Erebus, the active volcano looming over Ross Island.

Life at the station

Residents include scientists and support staff—cooks, mechanics, tradespeople, and administrators. The town-like station has a coffee house, retail store, post office, and social clubs; the weekly newspaper The Antarctic Sun covers program news. Winter crews endure months of darkness, and the station famously hosts the New Year's "IceStock" music festival.
