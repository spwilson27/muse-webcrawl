Determiner

A determiner, also called a determinative, is a word, phrase, or affix that occurs together with a noun or noun phrase and generally serves to express the reference of that noun or noun phrase in context.

Types

English determiners include articles (the, a, an), demonstratives (this, those), possessives (my, their), quantifiers (some, many, few), and numerals. Determiners typically mark definiteness, proximity, quantity, or possession.

Analysis

In modern analyses influenced by the DP hypothesis, determiners are treated as the heads of determiner phrases, with the noun phrase as their complement.
