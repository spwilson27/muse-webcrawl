Ford Performance Vehicles

Ford Performance Vehicles (FPV)
Industry: Automotive
Founded: 2002
Defunct: 2014
Headquarters: Campbellfield, Victoria, Australia
Parent: Prodrive / Ford Australia joint venture

Ford Performance Vehicles (FPV) was the performance vehicle division of Ford Australia, established in 2002 as a joint venture between Ford and the British engineering firm Prodrive. Based in Campbellfield, Melbourne, FPV produced high-performance versions of the Ford Falcon and Territory, competing directly with Holden Special Vehicles.

History

FPV replaced Tickford Vehicle Engineering as Ford Australia's performance arm. Its first models, based on the BA Falcon, were launched in 2003 and included the GT, GT-P and Pursuit ute. Later ranges added the turbocharged six-cylinder F6 models, widely praised for their pace, and limited editions such as the GT-F of 2014, the most powerful Falcon GT built.

Closure

In 2012 Ford bought out Prodrive's share and folded FPV operations back into Ford Australia. The FPV brand was retired in 2014, ahead of the end of Falcon production in 2016.
