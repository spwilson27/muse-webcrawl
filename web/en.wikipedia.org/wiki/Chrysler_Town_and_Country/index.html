Chrysler Town and Country

The Chrysler Town and Country is a nameplate used by Chrysler for several vehicles over eight decades: first for a luxury wood-bodied station wagon (1941–1988, with interruptions) and then for the company's flagship minivan (1990–2016).

Woodie wagon

The original Town & Country, introduced in 1941, was famous for its "woodie" construction, with body panels of white ash framing and mahogany veneer. Pre-war models included a distinctive barrel-back sedan; after the war the line included a convertible and hardtop coupe prized by collectors today.

Minivan

For 1990 the name moved to a luxury version of Chrysler's pioneering minivans, positioned above the Dodge Caravan and Plymouth Voyager, with leather trim and later features such as Stow 'n Go seating. The model was replaced by the Chrysler Pacifica minivan for 2017.
