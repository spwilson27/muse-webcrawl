The Copa América is the main men's football tournament contested among national teams from South America. Organised by CONMEBOL, it is the oldest still-running continental football competition, first held in 1916 in Argentina as the South American Championship, and one of the most widely watched sporting events in the world.

Format

All ten CONMEBOL member associations compete; since 1993 guest teams from other confederations — most often Mexico and the United States — have been invited to complete the field. The tournament is usually held every four years, alternating group-stage and knockout rounds, with the final crowning the champions of South America.

History and records

Argentina is the most successful team with sixteen titles, including 2021 and 2024; Uruguay has fifteen and Brazil nine. For most of the twentieth century the tournament was called the Campeonato Sudamericano. A special centenary edition, the Copa América Centenario, was held in the United States in 2016 and won by Chile.
