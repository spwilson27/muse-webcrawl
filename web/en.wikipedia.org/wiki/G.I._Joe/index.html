G.I. Joe

G.I. Joe is an American media franchise and a line of action figures owned and produced by the toy company Hasbro. The initial product offering represented four of the branches of the U.S. armed forces with the Action Soldier (U.S. Army), Action Sailor (U.S. Navy), Action Pilot (Air Force) and Action Marine (Marine Corps). The name is derived from the usage of "G.I." as a generic term for U.S. soldiers.

The development of G.I. Joe led to the coining of the term "action figure". The line, created by Larry Hama and Stan Weston, debuted in 1964 as 12-inch figures with realistic uniforms and accessories.

A Real American Hero

In 1982, the line was relaunched in a 3¾-inch scale complete with vehicles, playsets, and a complex background story involving the ongoing struggle between the G.I. Joe Team and the evil Cobra organisation which seeks to take over the world. This relaunch pioneered a multi-media approach with a Marvel comic book series written by Larry Hama and an animated television series (1983–1986) famous for its public service announcements ending with "...and knowing is half the battle."

Films and legacy

The franchise has expanded into live-action films — G.I. Joe: The Rise of Cobra (2009), G.I. Joe: Retaliation (2013), and Snake Eyes (2021) — as well as video games, novels, and later toy lines. Iconic characters include Duke, Snake Eyes, Scarlett, Cobra Commander, Destro, and Storm Shadow.
