OpenSolaris

OpenSolaris is a discontinued open-source computer operating system based on Solaris, developed by Sun Microsystems. It was released in 2005 under the Common Development and Distribution License (CDDL), a modified version of the Mozilla Public License. OpenSolaris introduced or popularised technologies such as the ZFS file system, DTrace dynamic tracing framework, Solaris Containers (zones), and the Image Packaging System.

History

Sun announced the OpenSolaris project in 2004 and published the bulk of the Solaris source code on June 14, 2005. The project community, governed by the OpenSolaris Governing Board, developed the ON (OS/Net) consolidation that formed the basis both of Sun's own releases and of community distributions. The first official binary distribution, OpenSolaris 2008.05, appeared in May 2008, followed by 2008.11 and 2009.06 releases, with the GNOME desktop and the IPS package manager.

End of the project and forks

After Oracle Corporation acquired Sun in 2010, it ended public development of OpenSolaris and returned to closed development of Solaris 11. An internal Oracle memo in August 2010 confirmed the project's discontinuation. The community forked the code as illumos, and the OpenIndiana distribution continues the OpenSolaris lineage, alongside other illumos distributions such as SmartOS and OmniOS.

Distributions

OpenSolaris-based distributions included Sun's official distribution, Nexenta OS (combining the OpenSolaris kernel with GNU userland), BeleniX, SchilliX, and MilaX. The source release of ZFS and DTrace under the CDDL also enabled ports and adoption of those technologies in other systems.
