Cormoran Strike

Cormoran Blue Strike is a fictional character and the protagonist of the Cormoran Strike detective novel series, written by J. K. Rowling under the pen name Robert Galbraith. The series began with The Cuckoo's Calling (2013) and follows Strike, a private investigator in London, and his partner Robin Ellacott.

Character

Strike is the illegitimate son of a 1970s rock star, Jonny Rokeby, and a model and "super groupie", Leda Strike, who named him for the Cornish giant Cormoran. After Oxford he joined the Royal Military Police's Special Investigation Branch, serving in Afghanistan, where he lost the lower part of his right leg in an IED attack. In the novels he runs a struggling detective agency from Denmark Street, employing Robin Ellacott, who progresses from temporary secretary to business partner.

Novels

The series comprises The Cuckoo's Calling (2013), The Silkworm (2014), Career of Evil (2015), Lethal White (2018), Troubled Blood (2020), The Ink Black Heart (2022), The Running Grave (2023) and The Hallmarked Man (2025). The books have been adapted by the BBC and HBO as the television series Strike.
