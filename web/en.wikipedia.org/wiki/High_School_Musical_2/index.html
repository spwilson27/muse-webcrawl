High School Musical 2 is a 2007 American musical television film, the sequel to High School Musical. The East High Wildcats spend their summer working at the Lava Springs country club owned by Sharpay Evans's family; Sharpay schemes to separate Troy Bolton from Gabriella Montez by offering Troy college-basketball connections.

Reception

Its premiere drew about 17.2 million viewers in the United States, a record for cable television at the time. Songs including "What Time Is It", "You Are the Music in Me" and "Bet On It" charted, and the soundtrack was a major commercial success.
