Cranford (TV series)

Cranford is a British television drama series produced by the BBC and WGBH Boston for BBC One. First broadcast in 2007, it is an adaptation of three novellas by Elizabeth Gaskell: Cranford (1853), My Lady Ludlow (1859) and Mr Harrison's Confessions (1851). The series was created by Sue Birtwistle and Susie Conklin and written principally by Heidi Thomas.

Plot

Set in the fictional Cheshire market town of Cranford in 1842–43, the series depicts a community dominated by genteel, mostly unmarried or widowed women whose quiet routines of visiting, gossip and rigid social etiquette are disrupted by change: the arrival of the young London doctor Frank Harrison, the threat of the new railway, and the shifting fortunes of the town's residents. Central characters include the kindly Miss Matty Jenkyns, played by Judi Dench, her formidable sister Deborah, and the aristocratic Lady Ludlow, played by Francesca Annis.

Production and reception

The first series of five episodes aired from November to December 2007 and was filmed largely in Lacock, Wiltshire. It was a critical and popular success, winning awards including BAFTAs for its design and make-up, and Judi Dench received Emmy and Golden Globe nominations. A two-part Christmas special, Return to Cranford, followed in December 2009, incorporating later Gaskell material.
