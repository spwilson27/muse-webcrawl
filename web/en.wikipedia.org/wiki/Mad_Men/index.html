Mad Men

Mad Men is an American period drama television series created by Matthew Weiner that aired on AMC from July 19, 2007, to May 17, 2015, spanning seven seasons and 92 episodes. Set primarily in 1960s Manhattan, it follows the staff of the fictional Sterling Cooper advertising agency, centred on creative director Don Draper, played by Jon Hamm.

Overview

The title refers to the "Madison Avenue ad men" of the era. The series portrays the social mores, gender politics, drinking and smoking culture of 1960s America while tracking great historical changes, from the Kennedy assassination to the Apollo 11 landing.

Reception

Mad Men won 16 Emmy Awards, including four consecutive wins for Outstanding Drama Series, and is consistently ranked among the greatest television series of all time, praised for its writing, acting and meticulous period detail.
