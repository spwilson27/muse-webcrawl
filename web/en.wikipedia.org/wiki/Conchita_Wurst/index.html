Conchita Wurst is the stage persona of Austrian singer, drag queen and model Thomas Neuwirth (born 6 November 1988). Neuwirth achieved international fame when Wurst won the Eurovision Song Contest 2014, held in Copenhagen, Denmark, representing Austria with the song "Rise Like a Phoenix".

Early life and career

Neuwirth was born in Gmunden, Upper Austria, and grew up in Bad Mitterndorf. He first came to public attention on the Austrian talent show Starmania in 2006–07, and was briefly a member of the boy band Jetzt Anders!. The Conchita character — a bearded diva in elegant gowns — debuted in 2011 on the ORF show Die große Chance. Neuwirth has explained that the beard is a statement about tolerance and acceptance: an attempt to show that "you can achieve anything, no matter who you are or how you look."

Eurovision 2014

Selected internally by ORF, Wurst performed the Bond-style ballad "Rise Like a Phoenix" in Copenhagen. The entry won with 290 points — Austria's first Eurovision victory since 1966 — and became an international media event, celebrated by many as a symbol of diversity and condemned by some conservative and religious figures. In her acceptance, Wurst declared the win was for "everyone who believes in a future of peace and freedom… we are unity and we are unstoppable."

Later work

Wurst's debut album Conchita appeared in 2015, followed by the album From Vienna with Love (2018) with the Vienna Symphony. Neuwirth has appeared in fashion shows for Jean Paul Gaultier, addressed the European Parliament, and performed at the United Nations in Vienna. In 2019 he also presented a second, more masculine electronic persona, WURST.
