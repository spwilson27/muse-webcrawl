Gheorghe Zamfir

Gheorghe Zamfir (Romanian pronunciation: [ˈɡe̯orɡe zamˈfir]; born 6 April 1941) is a Romanian nai (pan flute) musician, composer and conductor. Known worldwide as the "Master of the Pan Flute", he is credited with popularising the Romanian panpipe internationally, expanding its range and technique, and bringing it into classical, popular and film music. Zamfir has sold over 120 million albums worldwide and recorded the pan flute solos on several famous film soundtracks.

Early life and education

Zamfir was born in Găești, Dâmbovița County. Originally intending to become an accordionist, he took up the nai at 14 under the influence of the pan flutist Fănică Luca, whom he heard on the radio. He studied at the Ciprian Porumbescu Conservatory in Bucharest (now the National University of Music), initially for piano and conducting, and later earned a doctorate. He was discovered internationally through the efforts of the Swiss musicologist Marcel Cellier, who championed Romanian folk music in the West.

Career

Zamfir revolutionised the nai, traditionally a 20-pipe instrument, building versions with 22, 25, 28 and 30 pipes and refining its tuning so that a full range of ornaments and chromatic notes became possible. He toured the world with Romanian folk ensembles such as Taraf and later his own groups, and performed as soloist with major orchestras. His 1976 recording of "Doina de Jale" reached the UK charts after being used as a theme by the BBC. With James Last he recorded "The Lonely Shepherd" (1977), his most famous piece, later revived in Quentin Tarantino's film Kill Bill: Volume 1 (2003). Zamfir's pan flute is prominent in the soundtracks of Picnic at Hanging Rock (1975) and Ennio Morricone's score for Once Upon a Time in America (1984), as well as The Karate Kid films.

Later life

Zamfir has also composed concertos, masses and film scores, written poetry and an autobiography, and taught the nai in Romania. In his later career he performed widely with orchestras and choirs, and remained a national cultural figure in Romania.
