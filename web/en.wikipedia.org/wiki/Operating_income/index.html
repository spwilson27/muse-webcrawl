Operating income

Operating income, also referred to as operating profit or earnings before interest and taxes (EBIT), is the measure of a company's profit from its core business operations. It is generated from the company's core operations and excludes income from investments and effects of taxes and interest.

Calculation

Operating income is calculated as:

Operating income = Gross profit − Operating expenses − Depreciation − Amortization

where gross profit equals revenue minus the cost of goods sold (COGS). Operating expenses include selling, general and administrative expenses (SG&A), research and development, and other day-to-day costs of running the business.

Use

Operating income is an important measure of a company's profitability from operations because it strips out financing and tax effects, allowing analysts to compare the underlying performance of businesses. It appears on the income statement between gross profit and net income.
