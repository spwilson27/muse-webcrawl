American Gods is an American fantasy drama television series based on Neil Gaiman's 2001 novel of the same name, developed by Bryan Fuller and Michael Green for the premium cable network Starz. It premiered on 30 April 2017 and ran for three seasons until 2021.

Premise

Recently released ex-convict Shadow Moon (Ricky Whittle) meets the mysterious Mr. Wednesday (Ian McShane), who offers him a job as a bodyguard. Shadow is drawn into a hidden world where the old gods brought to America by immigrants — figures such as Odin, Czernobog and Bilquis — are fading as belief wanes, while new gods of media, technology and globalisation rise to power, setting the stage for a coming war between them.

Production

Fuller and Green served as showrunners for the first season but departed before the second amid reported budget and creative disputes; the series went through several further showrunner changes. The ambitious, visually stylised first season was critically acclaimed, though later seasons received a more mixed response, and Starz cancelled the series after its third season in March 2021.

Reception

The series was praised for its striking visuals, production design and performances, particularly those of McShane and Emily Browning as Laura Moon. It received Emmy Award nominations for its title design and visual effects.
