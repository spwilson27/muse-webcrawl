Wolfram Mathematica

Wolfram Mathematica is a software system with built-in libraries for technical computing, developed by Wolfram Research and first released in 1988. It integrates symbolic and numeric computation, visualisation, and the Wolfram Language in a notebook interface.

Features

Mathematica covers calculus, algebra, differential equations, statistics, image and signal processing, machine learning, and curated real-world data. Its symbolic engine and arbitrary-precision arithmetic made it a standard tool in mathematics, physics and engineering.

History

Conceived by Stephen Wolfram, Mathematica has been released in many major versions and now also powers WolframAlpha. The notebook document format (.nb) mixes code, results, graphics and formatted text.
