Flow visualization

Flow visualization or flow visualisation in fluid dynamics is used to make the flow patterns visible in order to get qualitative or quantitative information about them.

Methods

Classical techniques include introducing dye, smoke, or hydrogen bubbles into a flow; tufts and surface oil-flow patterns on models; and schlieren and shadowgraph imaging for compressible flows, famously used by Ludwig Prandtl and others.

Computational methods

In computational fluid dynamics, flow fields are visualized with streamlines, particle tracing, contour and vector plots, and techniques such as line integral convolution.
