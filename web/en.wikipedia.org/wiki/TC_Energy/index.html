TC Energy Corporation is a major North American energy company headquartered in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. Founded in 1951 as TransCanada PipeLines, it develops and operates energy infrastructure across Canada, the United States, and Mexico, including a natural gas pipeline network of more than 90,000 kilometres and power generation assets.

History and projects

The company's original transcontinental pipeline carried Alberta natural gas across Canada in the 1950s. TC Energy (renamed from TransCanada in 2019) is known for the Keystone Pipeline system carrying oil sands crude to U.S. refineries; its proposed Keystone XL extension became a major political controversy in the United States, and its presidential permit was revoked in 2021, after which the project was cancelled. Other significant projects include the Coastal GasLink pipeline in British Columbia, supplying the LNG Canada terminal.

Operations

TC Energy's businesses span natural gas pipelines, liquids pipelines, and power and energy solutions, including stakes in the Bruce Power nuclear generating station in Ontario. In 2024 the company spun off its crude oil pipelines business as South Bow Corporation.
