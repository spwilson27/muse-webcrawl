RELX

RELX plc is a British multinational information and analytics company headquartered in London, England. It operates in four market segments: scientific, technical and medical; risk solutions; legal; and exhibitions. It was formed in 1993 by the merger of Reed International and Elsevier, and was known as Reed Elsevier until 2015.

The company's brands include LexisNexis, Elsevier, and Reed Exhibitions (RX). RELX is listed on the London Stock Exchange, Euronext Amsterdam and the New York Stock Exchange, and is a constituent of the FTSE 100 Index. Its scientific publishing arm, Elsevier, publishes around a quarter of the world's scientific journal articles, including The Lancet and Cell.
