Sao Paulo

Sao Paulo is the unaccented ASCII spelling of São Paulo, the most populous city in Brazil and the capital of the state of São Paulo. The name, in Portuguese orthography, carries a tilde over the "a" (São); the plain form "Sao Paulo" is frequently used in English-language and international contexts where diacritics are omitted, in domain names, and in older texts.

About the city

São Paulo is the largest city in the Americas by city proper, with a population of about 11.5 million (2022 census) and more than 22 million in its metropolitan area. Founded by Jesuit priests in 1554, it grew explosively with the coffee economy and industrialization in the 19th and 20th centuries. It is Brazil's principal financial and cultural centre, home to the São Paulo Stock Exchange (B3), Paulista Avenue, Ibirapuera Park, and the São Paulo Museum of Art (MASP).
