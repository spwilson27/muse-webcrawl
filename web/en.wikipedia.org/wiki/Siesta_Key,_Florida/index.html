Siesta Key, Florida

StateFlorida
CountySarasota
TypeBarrier island, census-designated place

Siesta Key is a barrier island off the southwestern coast of Florida, in Sarasota County, and a census-designated place. It is connected to the mainland city of Sarasota by two bridges.

Beaches

Siesta Key is best known for its beaches, whose sand is composed almost entirely of quartz, giving it a bright white colour and keeping it cool underfoot. Siesta Beach has repeatedly been ranked among the top beaches in the United States. Other beaches include Crescent Beach and Turtle Beach.

Geography

The island is about eight miles long. Siesta Key Village, near the northern end, is the main commercial and dining district. The island has a year-round population of several thousand, with many seasonal residents and visitors.
