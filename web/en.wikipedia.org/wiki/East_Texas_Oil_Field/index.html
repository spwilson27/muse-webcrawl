East Texas Oil Field

The East Texas Oil Field is a large oil and gas field in east Texas, discovered on October 5, 1930 by Columbus Marion 'Dad' Joiner. It is the second-largest oil field in the United States outside Alaska and first in total production, having produced over 5.4 billion barrels.

East Texas Oil Field
Location: Gregg, Rusk, Upshur, Smith counties, Texas
Discovered: 5 October 1930
Production: Over 5.4 billion barrels

Discovery

Wildcatter Dad Joiner drilled the Daisy Bradford No. 3 near Kilgore after years of failure, triggering a boom that collapsed oil prices and prompted martial law by Governor Ross Sterling in 1931 and proration by the Railroad Commission.

Geology and later history

The field is a stratigraphic trap in the Upper Cretaceous Woodbine sandstone, about 42 miles long and 5-9 miles wide. Waterflooding revived production, and it remains productive today.
