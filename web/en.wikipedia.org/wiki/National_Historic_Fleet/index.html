National Historic Fleet

The National Historic Fleet is a list of historic ships and boats in the United Kingdom, maintained as part of the National Register of Historic Vessels by National Historic Ships UK. Vessels on the list are of pre-eminent national or regional significance in maritime history, architecture or technology.

Criteria

To qualify, vessels must be at least 50 years old, have demonstrable UK associations, be based in the UK, and retain a substantial proportion of original fabric. The fleet includes famous ships such as HMS Victory, the Cutty Sark, SS Great Britain and HMS Belfast, alongside smaller working craft, lifeboats and yachts.

Purpose

Registration raises awareness of significant vessels and helps guide conservation priorities, though it confers no statutory protection of itself.
