Forge FC, also known as Forge Football Club, is a Canadian professional soccer club based in Hamilton, Ontario, that competes in the Canadian Premier League, the top tier of Canadian soccer. The club plays its home matches at Tim Hortons Field and was one of the league's founding members, beginning play in 2019.

History

Forge was unveiled in 2018 as one of the founding clubs of the Canadian Premier League, owned by Hamilton Sports Group, the group behind the Hamilton Tiger-Cats of the Canadian Football League. Head coach Bobby Smyrniotis, a co-founder of the Sigma FC academy, built a squad with a strong core of Sigma graduates.

The club won the inaugural Canadian Premier League championship in 2019, defeating Cavalry FC in the two-legged Finals. Forge went on to establish itself as the league's early dynasty, winning further titles in 2020, 2022 and 2023, and making regular appearances in continental competition, including the CONCACAF League and the CONCACAF Champions Cup.

Colours and identity

The club's colours are spark orange, platinum steel grey and waterfall blue, reflecting Hamilton's steel industry and the waterfalls of the Niagara Escarpment. The crest features an "H" formed with a forge hammer motif, honouring the city's industrial heritage.

Supporters

The club's main supporters' group is the Barton St. Battalion, named for the street adjacent to the stadium's north stand.
