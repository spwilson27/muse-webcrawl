Gender variance

Gender variance, or gender nonconformity, is behaviour or gender expression that does not conform to the masculine or feminine norms of a given society and culture. Gender-variant people may identify as transgender, non-binary, genderqueer, or cisgender with a nonconforming expression; gender variance concerns expression and role rather than necessarily identity or sexual orientation.

Contexts

Many cultures have recognised gender-variant roles, such as hijra in South Asia, fa'afafine in Samoa, and Two-Spirit people in some Indigenous North American communities. Clinical literature historically used terms such as "gender identity disorder", replaced in DSM-5 by "gender dysphoria", which refers to distress that may accompany incongruence rather than variance itself.
