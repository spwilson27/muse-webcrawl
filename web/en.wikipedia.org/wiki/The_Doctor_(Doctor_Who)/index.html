The Doctor (Doctor Who)

First appearance: An Unearthly Child (1963)
Species: Time Lord (Gallifreyan)
Home planet: Gallifrey
Vehicle: The TARDIS

The Doctor is the title character in the long-running BBC science fiction television programme Doctor Who. A renegade Time Lord from the planet Gallifrey, the Doctor travels through time and space in a TARDIS, a ship disguised as a British police box, exploring the universe and intervening against injustice, usually accompanied by companions. When mortally wounded, the Doctor can regenerate into a new body with a different appearance and personality, a device that has allowed numerous actors to play the role since 1963.

Incarnations

The numbered incarnations include the First Doctor (William Hartnell), Second (Patrick Troughton), Third (Jon Pertwee), Fourth (Tom Baker), Fifth (Peter Davison), Sixth (Colin Baker), Seventh (Sylvester McCoy), Eighth (Paul McGann), Ninth (Christopher Eccleston), Tenth (David Tennant), Eleventh (Matt Smith), Twelfth (Peter Capaldi), Thirteenth (Jodie Whittaker, the first woman in the role), Fourteenth (Tennant again), and Fifteenth (Ncuti Gatwa). Additional incarnations, such as the War Doctor (John Hurt) and the Fugitive Doctor (Jo Martin), have been revealed in the programme's evolving mythology, which in 2020 introduced the "Timeless Child" origin, redefining the Doctor's beginnings.

Character

The Doctor is defined by curiosity, wit, pacifism, and a fierce moral opposition to tyranny, wielding a sonic screwdriver rather than a weapon. The character's real name is a closely guarded secret — hence the question "Doctor who?" — while "the Doctor" is a chosen name, described as a promise to be kind, never cruel or cowardly, and never to give up.

Cultural impact

The Doctor is one of the most recognizable characters in British popular culture, and the regeneration concept is credited with sustaining the programme for more than six decades. The character has appeared in films, spin-offs such as Torchwood and The Sarah Jane Adventures, books, comics, and audio dramas.
