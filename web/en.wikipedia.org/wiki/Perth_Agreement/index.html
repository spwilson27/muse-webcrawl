The Perth Agreement is an agreement reached on 28 October 2011 by the prime ministers of the sixteen Commonwealth realms at the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting in Perth, Australia, to reform the rules of succession to their shared Crown.

Provisions

The realms agreed to: replace male-preference primogeniture with absolute primogeniture for persons born after 28 October 2011; end the disqualification of a person who marries a Roman Catholic; and repeal the Royal Marriages Act 1772, requiring instead that only the first six persons in line to the throne obtain the sovereign's consent to marry.

Implementation

Because the Crown is shared, each realm had to legislate for itself, coordinated so the changes took effect simultaneously. In the United Kingdom the changes were enacted by the Succession to the Crown Act 2013; other realms passed their own statutes (Australia's required legislation in every state as well as the Commonwealth). The reforms came into force on 26 March 2015. The first visible effect was that Princess Charlotte, born in 2015, retained her place ahead of her younger brother Prince Louis, born in 2018 — the first princess not to be displaced by a younger brother.
