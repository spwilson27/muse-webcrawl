Battle of Clontarf

The Battle of Clontarf took place on 23 April 1014 at Clontarf, near Dublin, Ireland. It pitted the forces of High King Brian Boru against a Norse-Irish alliance led by the King of Leinster and the King of Dublin. Brian's forces were victorious, but Brian himself was killed.

Battle of Clontarf
Date: 23 April 1014 (Good Friday)
Location: Clontarf, Dublin
Result: Irish victory; Brian Boru killed

Significance

The battle is traditionally seen as breaking Viking power in Ireland, though modern historians view it as a struggle for the high kingship rather than a simple Irish-versus-Viking war. It is described in the Cogadh Gáedhel re Gallaibh and Icelandic sagas.
