Nissan 370Z

The Nissan 370Z (model code Z34; Fairlady Z in Japan) is a sports car produced by Nissan from 2009 to 2020, the successor to the 350Z. It kept the front-mid-engine, rear-drive layout on a shortened wheelbase with wider tracks and extensive use of aluminium body panels.

Specifications

Power came from the 3.7-litre VQ37VHR V6 with VVEL variable valve lift, producing about 332 hp (350 hp in the Nismo version), paired with a six-speed manual — notable for its SynchroRev Match automatic rev-matching — or a seven-speed automatic. A roadster was offered until 2019 in most markets.

History

Unveiled at the 2008 Los Angeles Auto Show, the 370Z enjoyed an unusually long production run with only minor updates, celebrated for old-school analogue character as rivals automated. Production ended in 2020; it was replaced by the Nissan Z (RZ34) in 2022.
