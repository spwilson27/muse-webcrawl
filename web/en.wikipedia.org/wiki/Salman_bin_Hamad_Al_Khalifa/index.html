Salman bin Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa (born 21 October 1969) is the Crown Prince and Prime Minister of Bahrain. The eldest son of King Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa, he became Crown Prince in 1999 and was appointed prime minister on 11 November 2020, succeeding his great-uncle Khalifa bin Salman Al Khalifa, who had held the premiership since 1970.

Education and early career

Salman was educated in Bahrain and at the American University in Washington, D.C., graduating with a bachelor's degree in political science in 1992, and earned a master's degree in history and philosophy of science from the University of Cambridge. He chaired Bahrain's Economic Development Board from 2002, driving the "Bahrain Vision 2030" economic diversification programme, and served as deputy supreme commander of the Bahrain Defence Force.

Premiership

As prime minister, Salman has led the government's fiscal balance programme, subsidy reforms and the national COVID-19 response, for which Bahrain's vaccination and testing programme drew international notice. He has also overseen continued normalisation-era diplomacy following Bahrain's signing of the Abraham Accords with Israel in 2020, and regular strategic dialogue with the United States, whose Fifth Fleet is headquartered in Bahrain.

Personal life

He was married to Sheikha Hala bint Duaij Al Khalifa (died 2018), with whom he had four children. The Crown Prince's International Scholarship Programme, founded in 1999, sends Bahraini students to leading universities abroad.
