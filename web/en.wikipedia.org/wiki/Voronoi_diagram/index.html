Voronoi diagram

A Voronoi diagram partitions a plane into regions around seed points, where each region consists of points closer to its seed than to any other.

Properties

Named after Georgy Voronoy, it is dual to the Delaunay triangulation. Cells are convex polygons in the Euclidean case.

Applications

Uses include nearest-neighbour analysis, telecommunications coverage, ecology, crystallography, and computer graphics.
