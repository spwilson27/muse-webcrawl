Sir Roger George Moore (14 October 1927 – 23 May 2017) was an English actor. He was the third actor to portray Ian Fleming's fictional secret agent James Bond in the Eon Productions film series, playing the character in seven feature films between 1973 and 1985 — the most of any Bond actor in the Eon series. Before Bond, he achieved fame as Simon Templar in the television series The Saint (1962–1969).

Career

Moore served in the Royal Army Service Corps during the Second World War and trained at RADA. After contracts with MGM and Warner Bros. in the 1950s, he found success on television in Ivanhoe, The Alaskans, Maverick, and The Saint, followed by The Persuaders! opposite Tony Curtis. His Bond debut, Live and Let Die (1973), was followed by The Man with the Golden Gun, The Spy Who Loved Me, Moonraker, For Your Eyes Only, Octopussy, and A View to a Kill (1985). His interpretation brought a lighter, more humorous tone to the role.

Humanitarian work and honours

Moore became a UNICEF Goodwill Ambassador in 1991 and was a prominent campaigner for children's rights and against animal cruelty. He was knighted in 2003 for services to charity. He died in Switzerland in 2017 following a short battle with cancer.
