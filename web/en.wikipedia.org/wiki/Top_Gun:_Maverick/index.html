Top Gun: Maverick is a 2022 American action drama film directed by Joseph Kosinski. It is the sequel to Top Gun (1986) and stars Tom Cruise reprising his role as naval aviator Pete "Maverick" Mitchell. The film also stars Miles Teller, Jennifer Connelly, Jon Hamm, Glen Powell, Monica Barbaro, Lewis Pullman, Ed Harris and Val Kilmer, returning as Tom "Iceman" Kazansky.

Plot

More than thirty years after his graduation from TOPGUN, Maverick — still a captain, deliberately evading promotion — is ordered to train a squadron of young graduates for a near-impossible mission: destroying an unsanctioned uranium enrichment plant in hostile territory before it becomes operational. Among the trainees is Lt. Bradley "Rooster" Bradshaw, the son of Maverick's late best friend "Goose", who blames Maverick for pulling his academy application years earlier. Maverick must confront his past, reconcile with Rooster, and ultimately fly the mission himself when the trainees falter, proving the value of human pilots in the age of drones.

Production and reception

Filmed with IMAX-certified cameras mounted inside real F/A-18 Super Hornets, with actors enduring g-force training and operating their own cameras, the film was praised for its practical aerial sequences. After pandemic delays it premiered at CinemaCon in April 2022 and became the highest-grossing film of 2022's early summer and of Cruise's career, earning $1.496 billion worldwide. It was nominated for six Academy Awards including Best Picture, winning Best Sound; the Lady Gaga theme "Hold My Hand" headlined the soundtrack. Critics widely judged it superior to the original.
