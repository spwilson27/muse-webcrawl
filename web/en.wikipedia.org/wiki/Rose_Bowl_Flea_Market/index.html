Rose Bowl Flea Market

Location: Rose Bowl Stadium, Pasadena, California

Founded: 1968

Held: Second Sunday of each month

Vendors: approx. 2,500

The Rose Bowl Flea Market is a large monthly swap meet held around the Rose Bowl Stadium in Pasadena, California. Founded in 1968 by promoter R.G. Canning, it takes place on the second Sunday of every month, rain or shine, and is widely regarded as one of the largest and most famous flea markets in the United States.

Description

The market spreads around the perimeter of the stadium and into adjacent parking areas, hosting roughly 2,500 vendors and drawing around 20,000 shoppers per event. Goods on offer range from antiques and vintage clothing to mid-century furniture, collectibles, architectural salvage, plants, and new merchandise. A premium early-morning admission period attracts professional dealers and interior designers, while general admission runs later into the morning.

Significance

Often described as a "shopping place of the stars," the market is a fixture of Los Angeles vintage culture and a sourcing destination for stylists, set decorators, and collectors. It has been featured in numerous television programmes and travel guides, and it is credited with helping to popularise the large-scale curated flea market format in the United States.
