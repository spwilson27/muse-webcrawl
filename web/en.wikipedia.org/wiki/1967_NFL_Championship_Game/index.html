The 1967 NFL Championship Game, remembered as the Ice Bowl, was played on 31 December 1967 at Lambeau Field in Green Bay, Wisconsin, between the Green Bay Packers and the Dallas Cowboys. Played in temperatures around −13 °F (−25 °C), it is regarded as one of the greatest games in NFL history.

Game summary

Green Bay led 14–0 before Dallas rallied to lead 17–14 in the fourth quarter. In the final minute Bart Starr drove the Packers to the goal line and, with 16 seconds left, scored the winning touchdown on a quarterback sneak behind Jerry Kramer's block on Jethro Pugh. Green Bay won 21–17.

Aftermath

The Packers went on to defeat the Oakland Raiders in Super Bowl II, completing Vince Lombardi's third consecutive championship season.
