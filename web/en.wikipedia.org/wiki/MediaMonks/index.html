MediaMonks is a digital-first marketing and advertising services company and the operating brand of S4 Capital, the group founded by Sir Martin Sorrell. Founded in the Netherlands in 2001 as Media.Monks, it grew from a digital production house in Hilversum into a global network producing websites, campaigns, films, games, and experiential and virtual-production content for major brands.

History

MediaMonks gained a reputation for high-end digital production, winning numerous industry awards including Cannes Lions and FWA honours. In July 2018, Sorrell's S4 Capital acquired MediaMonks in its first major deal, reported at around €300 million, outbidding WPP, Sorrell's former company. MediaMonks subsequently merged with and absorbed other S4 acquisitions, including MightyHive, and in 2021 the combined business unified under the MediaMonks name. In 2024 the brand was shortened to Monks.

Services

The company provides creative content production, media planning and buying, data and digital media services, technology services, and virtual production, employing several thousand people across more than 30 countries.
