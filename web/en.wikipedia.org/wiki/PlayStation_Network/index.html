PlayStation Network

Developer: Sony Interactive Entertainment

Launched: November 2006

The PlayStation Network (PSN) is a digital media and online multiplayer service operated by Sony Interactive Entertainment. Launched in November 2006 alongside the PlayStation 3, it provides online gaming, the PlayStation Store for digital purchases and downloads, streaming and social features, and the subscription services PlayStation Plus and PlayStation Now (later merged into tiered PlayStation Plus plans in 2022).

Services

A free PSN account gives access to the store, friends lists, trophies, and messaging; most online multiplayer on PlayStation 4 and PlayStation 5 requires a paid PlayStation Plus subscription, whose tiers (Essential, Extra, and Premium) add monthly games, a downloadable game catalogue, and cloud streaming. The service is available on PlayStation consoles, PC, and mobile apps, and has well over 100 million monthly active users.

2011 outage

In April 2011, an external intrusion compromised the personal data of about 77 million accounts, and Sony shut the network down for 23 days in one of the largest data breaches then known. The incident led to regulatory scrutiny, lawsuits, and a "Welcome Back" compensation programme.
