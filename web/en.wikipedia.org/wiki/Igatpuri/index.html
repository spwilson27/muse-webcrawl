Igatpuri

Igatpuri is a town and municipal council in Nashik District of Maharashtra state in western India. It lies in the Sahyadri (Western Ghats) mountain range, about 130 kilometres northeast of Mumbai, at an elevation of roughly 600 metres above sea level. The town is surrounded by some of the highest peaks of the Sahyadri range and is known for its scenic valleys, monsoon greenery, and heavy rainfall.

Geography and climate

Igatpuri is situated on National Highway 160 (formerly NH-3), the Mumbai–Agra highway, and on the Mumbai–Nashik section of the Central Railway. The Kasara Ghat section of the railway, one of the steepest gradients in India, begins nearby. The area receives very heavy monsoon rainfall, and numerous dams and reservoirs, including the Vaitarna Dam, are located in the vicinity.

Attractions

The town is known for the Dhamma Giri meditation centre, one of the largest Vipassana meditation centres in the world, founded by S. N. Goenka, which draws students from around the globe. Nearby attractions include Bhatsa River Valley, Arthur Lake, Kalsubai Peak (the highest peak in Maharashtra), and the historic Tringalwadi Fort.

Demographics

As of the 2011 Census of India, Igatpuri had a population of about 31,000. Marathi is the predominant language spoken in the town.
