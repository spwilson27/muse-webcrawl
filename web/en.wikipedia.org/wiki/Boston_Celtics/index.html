The Boston Celtics are an American professional basketball team based in Boston, competing in the NBA as a member of the Eastern Conference Atlantic Division. Founded in 1946, they are one of the league's most successful franchises.

History

Under coach Red Auerbach and centre Bill Russell, the Celtics won 11 championships in 13 seasons (1957, 1959–1966, 1968, 1969), including eight straight. Larry Bird led titles in 1981, 1984 and 1986. The 'Big Three' of Paul Pierce, Kevin Garnett and Ray Allen won in 2008, and Jayson Tatum and Jaylen Brown led the 2024 title, the franchise's record 18th.

Rivalries

The Celtics' rivalry with the Los Angeles Lakers is the NBA's most famous.
