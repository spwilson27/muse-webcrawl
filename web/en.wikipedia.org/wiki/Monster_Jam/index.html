Monster Jam

Monster Jam is a live motorsport event tour operated by Feld Entertainment. Events feature monster trucks — custom-built vehicles roughly 12 feet tall weighing about 12,000 pounds, with five-foot tires and supercharged engines producing around 1,500 horsepower — competing in racing, skills, donut, and freestyle competitions in stadiums and arenas around the world.

The tour is famous for trucks such as Grave Digger, one of the most iconic monster trucks in the world, as well as Max-D, El Toro Loco, Monster Mutt, and Zombie. Points are awarded across events in several stadium and arena series, culminating in the Monster Jam World Finals, held annually since 2000. Events are judged partly by the audience, who score freestyle runs via a mobile voting system. Drivers, increasingly including prominent women drivers, are trained at "Monster Jam University". The brand extends into toys (notably Hot Wheels and later Spin Master die-cast), video games, and licensed merchandise.
