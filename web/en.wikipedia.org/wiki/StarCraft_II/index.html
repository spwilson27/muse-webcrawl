StarCraft II

Developer: Blizzard Entertainment
Initial release: Wings of Liberty, 27 July 2010
Expansions: Heart of the Swarm (2013); Legacy of the Void (2015)
Genre: Real-time strategy
Mode(s): Single-player, multiplayer

StarCraft II is a military science fiction real-time strategy video game developed and published by Blizzard Entertainment. It is the sequel to the 1998 game StarCraft and was released in three instalments: Wings of Liberty (2010), centred on the Terrans; Heart of the Swarm (2013), centred on the Zerg; and Legacy of the Void (2015), centred on the Protoss.

Gameplay

Players control one of three asymmetric factions — the human Terrans, the insectoid Zerg, and the psionic Protoss — harvesting minerals and vespene gas to build bases, raise armies and destroy opponents. Each campaign delivers about 25–30 single-player missions with branching choices and upgrade systems. Multiplayer features ranked ladders, co-operative missions, and an "Arcade" of user-made games built with the included editor.

Story

Set four years after StarCraft: Brood War, the campaigns follow rebel commander Jim Raynor against the Terran Dominion of Arcturus Mengsk; Sarah Kerrigan, the deposed Queen of Blades, in her campaign of vengeance; and the Protoss hierarch Artanis uniting his people against the fallen Xel'Naga Amon, concluding with Kerrigan's ascension and the defeat of Amon in the epilogue.

Esports and legacy

StarCraft II became one of the defining esports titles of the early 2010s, with major leagues including the GSL in South Korea, the WCS circuit and IEM events, and legendary players such as Lim "BoxeR" Yo-hwan's successors, Lee "Life" Seung-hyun and Kim "sOs" Yu-jin. The base game became free-to-play in 2017. Although Blizzard ended major content development in 2020, the game remains a reference point for competitive RTS design and for deep-learning research — DeepMind's AlphaStar agent reached grandmaster level on the ladder in 2019.
