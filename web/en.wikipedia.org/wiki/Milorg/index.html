Milorg

Milorg (an abbreviation of militær organisasjon, "military organisation") was the main Norwegian resistance movement during the Second World War.

Activities

Milorg was founded in 1941 and grew to some 40,000 members by liberation in 1945. It was recognised by the Norwegian government-in-exile in London and worked under the Norwegian High Command. Its activities included intelligence gathering, maintaining a standby military force, assisting escapes to Sweden and Britain, and in the final phase, protecting industrial facilities from German demolition during the retreat.
