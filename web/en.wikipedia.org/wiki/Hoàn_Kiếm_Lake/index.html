Hoàn Kiếm Lake

Hoàn Kiếm Lake (Vietnamese: Hồ Hoàn Kiếm, "Lake of the Returned Sword") is a freshwater lake in the historical centre of Hanoi, Vietnam. It is one of the city's major scenic spots and the focal point of its public life.

Legend

According to legend, Emperor Lê Lợi, who led the Vietnamese resistance against Ming Chinese occupation in the 15th century, received a magical sword, Heaven's Will, from the Dragon King. After victory, while boating on the lake, a golden turtle god (Kim Quy) surfaced and asked him to return the sword to its owner — giving the lake its name. The Turtle Tower (Tháp Rùa) on a small island in the lake commemorates the event.

Features

The lake covers about 12 hectares. Near its northern shore stands the Jade Island (Đảo Ngọc), home to the Temple of the Jade Mountain (Đền Ngọc Sơn), reached by the scarlet-painted Huc Bridge ("Bridge of the Rising Sun"). The lake was famous for its giant softshell turtles, the Hoàn Kiếm turtle; the last known individual died in 2016.
