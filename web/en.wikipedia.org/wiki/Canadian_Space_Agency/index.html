Canadian Space Agency

The Canadian Space Agency (CSA; French: Agence spatiale canadienne) is the national space agency of Canada, established in 1989. Its headquarters are at the John H. Chapman Space Centre in Longueuil, Quebec.

Established: 1 March 1989
Headquarters: Longueuil, Quebec
Jurisdiction: Government of Canada

Programs

Canada is known for the Canadarm and Canadarm2 robotic arms used on the Space Shuttle and International Space Station, the RADARSAT Earth-observation satellites, and Canadian astronauts including Chris Hadfield.

Cooperation

The CSA cooperates closely with NASA and the European Space Agency, contributing technology to the ISS and the Lunar Gateway program.
