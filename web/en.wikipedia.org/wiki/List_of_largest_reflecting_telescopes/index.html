List of largest optical reflecting telescopes

Largest reflectors
The Gran Telescopio Canarias (10.4 m) is the largest single-aperture optical telescope in operation.

This is a list of the largest optical reflecting telescopes, ranked by the diameter of the primary mirror. Modern giant telescopes use segmented mirrors or multiple mirrors on a common mount. The list includes operational telescopes and historically significant instruments.

Under construction

The Extremely Large Telescope (ELT) in Chile, with a 39.3 m segmented primary, is expected to become the world's largest optical telescope when completed. The Thirty Meter Telescope (planned for Mauna Kea or La Palma) and the Giant Magellan Telescope (24.5 m equivalent) in Chile are also in development.
