Team records

The 1972 Miami Dolphins completed the only perfect season in NFL history, finishing 17–0 including Super Bowl VII. The 2007 New England Patriots went 16–0 in the regular season but lost Super Bowl XLII to the New York Giants. The Chicago Bears of 1985 finished 18–1 and are widely considered among the greatest teams ever. The Green Bay Packers have won the most NFL championships overall with 13, including four Super Bowls.
