Apostolic Fathers

The Apostolic Fathers were Christian theologians who lived in the 1st and 2nd centuries AD, traditionally said to have personally known some of the Twelve Apostles. Their writings are early and foundational, though not part of the New Testament canon.

Works

  • First and Second Clement
  • Letters of Ignatius of Antioch
  • Polycarp to the Philippians and Martyrdom of Polycarp
  • Didache
  • Epistle of Barnabas
  • Shepherd of Hermas
  • Epistle to Diognetus
  • Fragments of Papias and Quadratus

Significance

They illuminate church order, martyrdom, and doctrine in the post-apostolic age. See Apostolic Tradition.
