Ajax (armoured vehicle)

An Ajax reconnaissance vehicle on trials

The Ajax, formerly known as the Scout SV (Specialist Vehicle), is a family of tracked armoured fighting vehicles developed by General Dynamics UK for the British Army. Based on the ASCOD 2 platform, Ajax is intended to provide a modern armoured reconnaissance capability, replacing roles performed by the ageing Combat Vehicle Reconnaissance (Tracked) family. The programme covers 589 vehicles in six variants, with the turreted Ajax reconnaissance variant at its centre.

Variants

The family comprises the turreted Ajax reconnaissance and strike vehicle; Ares, an armoured personnel carrier; Athena, a command and control vehicle; Apollo, an equipment support (repair) vehicle; Atlas, an equipment support (recovery) vehicle; and Argus, an engineer reconnaissance vehicle. All share a common base platform powered by an MTU diesel engine.

Programme history

The programme, valued at about £5.5 billion, was awarded to General Dynamics in 2014, but suffered long delays. Trials were halted in 2021 after crews reported excessive noise and vibration causing injuries, prompting a major review and design fixes. Deliveries of deployable vehicles resumed in the mid-2020s, and the type was declared to have reached initial operating capability with the Household Cavalry Regiment in 2025.
