Newsletter

A newsletter is a printed or electronic report containing news concerning the activities of a business, organisation, or community, sent regularly to its members, customers, employees, or subscribers.

History

Early newsletters were handwritten news sheets circulated among merchants in early modern Europe, carrying commercial and political intelligence. Printed newsletters proliferated in the 17th century, and in the 20th century organisations adopted mimeographed and offset-printed newsletters for internal and external communication.

Email newsletters

With the internet, email newsletters became a major publishing and marketing channel. Services such as Mailchimp, Substack, and Buttondown allow independent writers to distribute and monetise subscription newsletters, a model that revived personal and niche journalism in the 2010s and 2020s.

Uses

Newsletters serve marketing, internal communications, investor relations, community updates, and journalism. Effective newsletters typically have a consistent schedule, a defined audience, and a mix of news, features, and calls to action.
