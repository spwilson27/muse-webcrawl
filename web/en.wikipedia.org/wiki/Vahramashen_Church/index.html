The Vahramashen Church (Armenian: Վահրամաշեն եկեղեցի), also known as the Church of St. Astvatsatsin of Amberd, is an 11th-century Armenian church located on the slopes of Mount Aragats in Aragatsotn Province, Armenia, beside the fortress of Amberd at an elevation of about 2,300 metres.

History

The church was built in 1026 by Prince Vahram Pahlavouni, commander of Armenian forces at Ani, who also developed the Amberd fortress complex. It formed part of the stronghold that guarded the approaches to the Bagratid capital of Ani. After the fortress was damaged by Seljuk and later Mongol invasions, the site was gradually abandoned, though the church survived substantially intact.

Architecture

Vahramashen is a domed cruciform church typical of its period: a central dome on a tall circular drum rises over a cross-in-square plan, built of reddish tuff stone. The exterior is relatively austere, with a sculpted portal and inscriptions recording its foundation. The church stands a short distance below the fortress walls and the remains of a small bathhouse and cisterns associated with the Amberd complex.
