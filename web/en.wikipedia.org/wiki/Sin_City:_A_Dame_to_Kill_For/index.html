Sin City: A Dame to Kill For

Sin City: A Dame to Kill For (also known as Frank Miller's Sin City: A Dame to Kill For) is a 2014 American neo-noir crime anthology film directed by Robert Rodriguez and Frank Miller, and a sequel to Sin City (2005). It is based on Miller's graphic novel series, chiefly the second book, A Dame to Kill For.

Plot

The film interweaves several stories set in Basin City: gambler Johnny takes on the corrupt Senator Roark at poker; Dwight McCarthy is drawn back by his former lover Ava Lord; and Nancy Callahan seeks revenge for the death of John Hartigan.

Cast and reception

The ensemble cast includes Mickey Rourke, Jessica Alba, Josh Brolin, Joseph Gordon-Levitt, Eva Green and Bruce Willis. Shot largely on green screen to reproduce the high-contrast black-and-white look of the comics, the film received mixed reviews and performed below its predecessor at the box office.
