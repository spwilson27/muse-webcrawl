The Scottish Professional Football League (SPFL) is the national men's association football league system in Scotland. It was formed in June 2013 following a merger between the Scottish Premier League (SPL) and the Scottish Football League (SFL), bringing all 42 senior professional clubs in Scotland under a single governing body for the first time.

History

Scottish league football was historically organised by the Scottish Football League, founded in 1890. In 1998 the top clubs broke away to form the Scottish Premier League, modelled on England's Premier League, in order to retain a greater share of commercial revenue. Discussions about reunifying the leagues ran for many years, and in 2013 the SPL and SFL clubs voted to merge, creating the SPFL with a four-division structure and a play-off system linking the Premiership to the Championship and, from 2015, a pyramid play-off with the Highland and Lowland Leagues.

Structure

The league comprises four divisions of 12, 10, 10 and 10 clubs respectively: the Scottish Premiership, Scottish Championship, Scottish League One and Scottish League Two. The Premiership operates a split after 33 matches, dividing into a top six and bottom six for the final five fixtures. Promotion and relegation operate between all divisions, with play-offs at several levels.

Cup competitions

The SPFL organises the Scottish League Cup and the Scottish Challenge Cup (for lower-division clubs and invited guests). The Scottish Cup is administered separately by the Scottish Football Association.

Sponsorship

The league has been sponsored by Ladbrokes, and later by cinch, whose name adorned all four divisions from 2021. A subsequent deal with William Hill took effect in 2024.
