Advance Publications, Inc. is a privately held American media company owned by the descendants of Samuel Irving Newhouse Sr. Headquartered on Staten Island, New York, it operates newspapers, magazines, and digital properties, and holds significant stakes in entertainment and technology companies.

History

Samuel Newhouse began building the company in 1922 with the purchase of a controlling interest in the Staten Island Advance, acquiring additional newspapers across the United States over the following decades. In 1959 he purchased Condé Nast, publisher of Vogue, The New Yorker, and Vanity Fair, for about $5 million. On his death in 1979, control passed to his sons, S.I. Newhouse Jr. and Donald Newhouse.

Holdings

Advance's holdings include the magazine group Condé Nast; Advance Local, which operates regional news sites such as NJ.com, Cleveland.com, and AL.com alongside papers including The Star-Ledger and The Plain Dealer; and American City Business Journals. The company has held large stakes in cable operator Charter Communications and in Warner Bros. Discovery, and is a major shareholder of Reddit, holding roughly a third of the company when it went public in 2024.

Newhouse family

The Newhouse family, one of the wealthiest media families in the United States, also endowed the S.I. Newhouse School of Public Communications at Syracuse University. The family is known for its private management style and for collecting modern art.
