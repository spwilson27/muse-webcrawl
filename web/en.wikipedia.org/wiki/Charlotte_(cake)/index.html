Charlotte (cake)

Charlotte
TypeDessert (molded cake or icebox cake)
Place of originUnited Kingdom / France
Main ingredientsLadyfingers or sponge cake; custard, mousse, or fruit purée
VariationsCharlotte russe; charlotte aux pommes; chocolate charlotte

A charlotte is a type of dessert made by lining a mold with ladyfingers, sponge cake, or bread and filling it with custard, Bavarian cream, mousse, whipped cream, or fruit. Charlottes may be served warm (baked, typically with fruit) or cold (set with gelatin). The name is often associated with Queen Charlotte, wife of George III of the United Kingdom, a patron of apple growers, though its exact origin is uncertain.

A charlotte russe with ladyfinger lining

History

The charlotte appears in English cookery by the late 18th century, usually as a molded pudding of bread and stewed apples — the apple charlotte — baked and served hot. In France, the form was elaborated by Marie-Antoine Carême in the early 19th century. Carême created the charlotte à la parisienne, lined with ladyfingers and filled with Bavarian cream; after the Bourbon Restoration he renamed it charlotte russe, possibly in honor of Tsar Alexander I, in whose service Carême briefly worked.

Types

  • Apple charlotte — a warm pudding of buttered bread encasing spiced cooked apples, often served with custard.
  • Charlotte russe — a cold dessert: a mold lined with ladyfingers, filled with Bavarian cream (crème bavaroise) flavored with vanilla, coffee, or chocolate, and chilled until set. In New York bakeries, "charlotte russe" also became a street treat of sponge cake topped with whipped cream, chocolate, and a cherry, sold in a paper cup.
  • Chocolate charlotte — lined with ladyfingers or sponge and filled with chocolate mousse.
  • Fruit charlottes — filled with fruit mousse or purée, such as strawberry or pear, set with gelatin.

Preparation

Cold charlottes depend on a tight lining of ladyfingers against the mold so the soft filling holds its shape when unmolded; the finished cake is often tied with a ribbon and garnished with fruit. Warm charlottes are baked in the mold and turned out onto a plate. Related molded desserts include the summer pudding and the Italian zuccotto.
