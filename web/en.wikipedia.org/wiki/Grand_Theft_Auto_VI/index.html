Grand Theft Auto VI

Grand Theft Auto VI is an upcoming action-adventure game developed and published by Rockstar Games. It is the eighth main entry in the Grand Theft Auto series, following Grand Theft Auto V (2013), and the sixteenth entry overall.

Setting and development

The game is set in the fictional state of Leonida, based on Florida, including a return to Vice City. The story follows criminal partners Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval. Development was confirmed by Rockstar in February 2022; in September 2022, unfinished development footage was leaked online in one of the industry's largest leaks. The first trailer was released in December 2023, becoming one of the most-viewed video game trailers ever, and a second trailer followed in May 2025.

Release

Grand Theft Auto VI is scheduled for release for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S. Originally targeted for 2025, the release was delayed to 26 May 2026. Anticipation for the game has been widely reported across the entertainment industry, and it won awards for Most Anticipated Game ahead of release.
