The Kominsky Method is an American comedy-drama streaming television series created by Chuck Lorre for Netflix. It stars Michael Douglas as Sandy Kominsky, an ageing actor who, after a brief career, now works as an acting coach in Hollywood, and Alan Arkin as his long-time agent and friend Norman Newlander. The series explores ageing, loss, friendship and family with a mixture of humour and melancholy.

Premise

Sandy Kominsky runs an acting studio in Los Angeles with his daughter Mindy (Sarah Baker). Alongside the everyday indignities of growing older — prostate trouble, money worries, bereavement — he navigates his friendship with Norman, who grieves for his wife Eileen and struggles with his troubled daughter Phoebe. In the third and final season, following Arkin's departure, Kathleen Turner joined as Sandy's ex-wife Roz.

Reception

The series received critical acclaim. Douglas won the Golden Globe Award for Best Actor in a Television Series – Musical or Comedy, and the show won the Golden Globe for Best Television Series – Musical or Comedy for its first season. It was also nominated for several Primetime Emmy Awards, including for Douglas and Arkin.
