Waviness

Waviness is a measure of surface texture in engineering and manufacturing: it is the component of a surface profile with wavelength longer than that of roughness but shorter than the overall form of the part. Surface texture is conventionally divided into three bands — form, waviness and roughness — separated by filtering.

Causes

Waviness typically arises from machine or workpiece deflections, vibration, chatter during machining, runout, or thermal distortion during heat treatment. Because its spacing is wider than roughness asperities, it can affect such functional properties as bearing contact, sealing, coating adhesion and appearance.

Measurement

Waviness is measured with profilometers and coordinate measuring machines, and is specified on engineering drawings under standards such as ASME B46.1 and ISO 4287. Common parameters include Wa (arithmetic average waviness height) and Wt (total waviness height), evaluated after applying a cutoff filter that separates waviness from roughness.
