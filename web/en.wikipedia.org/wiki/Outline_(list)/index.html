Outline (list)

An outline, also called a hierarchical outline, is a list arranged to show hierarchical relationships and is a type of tree structure. It is used to present the main points or topics of a given subject, forming a skeleton or summary.

Outlines use indentation, numbering systems such as Roman numerals, letters, and decimal notation to show levels. They are widely used in writing, education, and on Wikipedia, where outline articles provide structured overviews of broad topics such as geography, history, and science.
