Philomena (film)


Philomena is a 2013 drama film directed by Stephen Frears, starring Judi Dench and Steve Coogan, based on Martin Sixsmith's book The Lost Child of Philomena Lee (2009).

Philomena is a 2013 British drama film directed by Stephen Frears, based on the 2009 nonfiction book The Lost Child of Philomena Lee by journalist Martin Sixsmith. It stars Judi Dench as Philomena Lee and Steve Coogan (who also co-wrote the screenplay with Jeff Pope) as Sixsmith.

Plot

Fifty years after being compelled in 1950s Ireland — as an unmarried mother confined to a Magdalene laundry at Sean Ross Abbey in Roscrea — to give up her young son Anthony for adoption by an American couple, Philomena Lee enlists the help of disgraced journalist Martin Sixsmith to find him. Their search leads to Washington, D.C., where they discover that Anthony, renamed Michael Hess, had become a senior lawyer in the Reagan and George H. W. Bush administrations, and had died of AIDS in 1995 — having himself searched for his mother and asked to be buried in the abbey grounds in Ireland.

Reception

The film premiered at the 70th Venice International Film Festival in 2013, where it won the Golden Osella for best screenplay. It was nominated for four Academy Awards, including Best Picture, Best Actress for Dench, and Best Adapted Screenplay, and won the BAFTA for Best Adapted Screenplay. The film drew attention to the practices of Ireland's Magdalene laundries and forced adoptions, and prompted the real Philomena Lee to campaign for adoption rights legislation.
