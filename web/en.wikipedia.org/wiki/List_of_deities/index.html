List of deities

A deity or god is a supernatural being considered divine or sacred. This list compiles deities organised by the culture, religion, or pantheon to which they belong. Deities have been worshipped in polytheistic, monotheistic, henotheistic and animistic traditions across the world, and many continue to be central figures in living religions.
