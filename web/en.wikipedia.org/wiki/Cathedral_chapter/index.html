Cathedral chapter

A cathedral chapter (Latin: capitulum) is the body of clergy, called canons, attached to a cathedral church. In the Roman Catholic, Anglican, and some Lutheran traditions, the chapter advises the bishop, maintains the cathedral's worship, and historically elected the bishop.

Organisation

The chapter is typically headed by a dean, with officers such as the precentor, chancellor, treasurer, and archdeacons. Canons originally lived a common life under a rule — hence 'chapter', from the daily reading of a chapter of the rule in the chapter house.

Functions

During a vacancy of the see (sede vacante), a Catholic cathedral chapter elects a vicar capitular to administer the diocese. In the Church of England, the chapter, reconstituted under modern cathedrals measures, shares governance of the cathedral with the bishop.
