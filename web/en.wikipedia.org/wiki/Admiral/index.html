Admiral

Admiral is one of the highest ranks in many navies of the world. It derives, via medieval Latin and Old French, from Arabic amīr al-baḥr, "commander of the sea".

Grades

Navies commonly distinguish rear admiral, vice admiral, admiral, and in some countries admiral of the fleet / fleet admiral as the senior grade, corresponding to OF-7 through OF-10 in NATO rank codes. In the United States, admiral is a four-star rank (O-10).

Insignia

Admirals traditionally wear broad sleeve stripes (one wide band plus thinner bands by grade) or shoulder boards with stars, and fly a personal flag when in command.
