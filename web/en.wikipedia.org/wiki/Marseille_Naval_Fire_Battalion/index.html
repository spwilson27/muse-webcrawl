Marseille Naval Fire Battalion

The Marseille Naval Fire Battalion (French: Bataillon de marins-pompiers de Marseille, BMPM) is a French Navy unit responsible for firefighting, rescue, and emergency medical services in the city of Marseille, the autonomous port of Marseille, Marseille Provence Airport, and other sites. It is, with the Paris Fire Brigade, one of only two military fire services in France.

History

The battalion was created by royal decree in 1838 after a series of serious fires in the port, and modelled on the naval firefighting organisation at Toulon. Its sailors-firefighters (marins-pompiers) fought major fires during the Second World War and in the post-war port, and the unit has since expanded into emergency medicine, specialist rescue, and protection of naval and industrial sites.

Organisation

The BMPM numbers roughly 2,400 personnel, military and civilian, operating from stations across Marseille. Its area of responsibility is the second-largest of any French fire service, covering about 640 km² of land and sea. The battalion responds to tens of thousands of incidents each year, the majority being emergency medical calls.
