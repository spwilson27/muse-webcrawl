Spy × Family

Spy × Family (stylised as SPY×FAMILY) is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Tatsuya Endo. It has been serialised on Shueisha's Shōnen Jump+ platform since March 2019 and follows a spy who must build a fake family for a mission, unaware that his adopted daughter is a telepath and his pretend wife is an assassin.

Plot

Master spy Twilight, operating in the divided nation of Ostania, takes the identity of psychiatrist Loid Forger and assembles a family to enrol a child at an elite school. He adopts Anya, a young girl who can read minds, and enters a marriage of convenience with Yor Briar, a civil servant who secretly works as the assassin known as the Thorn Princess. Only Anya knows everyone's secrets.

Reception

The series became a major commercial success, with tens of millions of copies in circulation, and was adapted into an anime television series by Wit Studio and CloverWorks beginning in 2022, followed by the film Code: White (2023).
