The Liverpool City Region is a combined authority region of England centred on the city of Liverpool, in the north-west of the country. It comprises the six local authority areas of the former county of Merseyside — Liverpool, Knowsley, St Helens, Sefton and Wirral — together with the Borough of Halton in Cheshire. Its population is around 1.6 million.

Governance

The Liverpool City Region Combined Authority was established on 1 April 2014, one of the first combined authorities in England, to coordinate economic development, transport and regeneration across the six boroughs. Under a devolution deal with the UK government agreed in 2015, the region gained powers over transport, skills, housing and planning, and in May 2017 elected its first Metro Mayor, Steve Rotheram, who has been re-elected since. The combined authority is the transport authority for the region, overseeing the Merseyrail network and Merseytravel, and it brought the region's bus network back under public control through franchising reforms in the 2020s.

Geography and economy

The city region straddles the River Mersey estuary: Liverpool, Sefton, Knowsley and St Helens lie on the river's east (historic Lancashire) side, while the Wirral Peninsula and Halton lie to the west and south. Historically one of the world's great ports, the region's economy today centres on the Port of Liverpool (including the Liverpool2 deep-water container terminal, opened 2016), the visitor and cultural economy, the life sciences and knowledge quarter in Liverpool, advanced manufacturing (including the Jaguar Land Rover plant at Halewood), and the maritime and logistics sectors.

Transport

Merseyrail, a self-contained urban rail network electrified since the early 20th century, serves the conurbation; mainline services from Liverpool Lime Street connect to London, Manchester and other major cities. The Mersey is crossed by the Mersey Tunnels between Liverpool and Birkenhead/Wallasey, and by the Silver Jubilee Bridge and Mersey Gateway Bridge at Runcorn and Widnes. Liverpool John Lennon Airport serves the region from Speke.
