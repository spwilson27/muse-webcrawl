Isten

Isten is the Hungarian word for God. In Hungarian folk religion and mythology, Isten is regarded as the supreme deity, creator of the world, whose name appears in everyday expressions, blessings, and the opening of the Hungarian national anthem, "Isten, áldd meg a magyart" ("O Lord, bless the Hungarian").

Etymology

The origin of the word is uncertain; it may be an ancient Ugric inheritance or an early loanword. Some scholars connect it with the name of the ancient Mesopotamian goddess Ishtar via Iranian intermediaries, though this remains disputed.

In Hungarian mythology

Pre-Christian Hungarian religion is poorly documented, but folk tradition preserves Isten as a benevolent sky god and world creator in the dualistic creation tale in which Isten creates the world with the help of Ördög (the Devil), who dives for earth from the primordial waters. Following the Christianisation of Hungary under Saint Stephen, Isten simply became the name of the Christian God in Hungarian.
