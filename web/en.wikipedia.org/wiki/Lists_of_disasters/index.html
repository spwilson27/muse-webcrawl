Deadliest events overall

The deadliest single disasters in recorded history are dominated by floods, earthquakes, and famines. The 1931 China floods may have killed between one and four million people; the 1556 Shaanxi earthquake killed an estimated 830,000; the 1970 Bhola cyclone killed up to 500,000; the 2004 Indian Ocean earthquake and tsunami killed approximately 228,000 people across fourteen countries; and the 2010 Haiti earthquake killed an estimated 100,000–316,000.
