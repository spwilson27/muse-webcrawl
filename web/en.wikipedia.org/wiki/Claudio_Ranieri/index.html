Claudio Ranieri

Claudio Ranieri (born 20 October 1951) is an Italian football manager and former player. He is best known for leading Leicester City to the Premier League title in 2015–16, regarded as one of the greatest shocks in sporting history.

Career

A defender with Roma, Catanzaro and Catania, Ranieri began coaching in the amateur game and rose through Cagliari and Napoli to Fiorentina, where he won the Coppa Italia in 1996. He then managed Valencia (winning the Copa del Rey and the UEFA Intertoto Cup), Atlético Madrid, and Chelsea (2000–2004), where his habit of rotating his team earned him the nickname "the Tinkerman". Later spells included Juventus, Roma, Inter, Monaco, the Greece national team and Nantes.

Leicester City

Appointed by Leicester in July 2015, Ranieri inherited a team tipped for relegation. With astute tactics built on counter-attacking, and stars such as Jamie Vardy, Riyad Mahrez and N'Golo Kanté, Leicester — 5,000-to-1 outsiders at the season's start — won the 2015–16 Premier League with two games to spare. Ranieri was named FIFA's men's coach of the year. He was dismissed the following February with Leicester struggling, a decision that shocked football, and later managed Fulham, Roma again, Sampdoria, Watford and Cagliari, whom he led to promotion and kept in Serie A before announcing his retirement from club management in 2024; he subsequently returned to Roma in a third spell as head coach.
