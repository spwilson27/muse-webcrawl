Wayne Manor is a fictional mansion appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. It is the home of Bruce Wayne, owner of Wayne Enterprises and secretly the superhero Batman, and is maintained by his butler, Alfred Pennyworth.

Description

The manor stands on extensive grounds on the outskirts of Gotham City, above the cave system that houses the Batcave, Batman's headquarters. Secret entrances — famously including a passage behind a grandfather clock in Bruce Wayne's study — connect the house to the cave below. Depictions of the manor's architecture vary between artists and adaptations, but it is consistently portrayed as a vast, stately residence reflecting the Wayne family's wealth and old-money status.

In other media

Various real stately homes have stood in for Wayne Manor in film and television, including Knebworth House in the 1989 film Batman, Mentmore Towers in Batman Begins, and Wollaton Hall in The Dark Knight Rises. In several storylines the manor has been destroyed and rebuilt, and Bruce Wayne has at times lived instead in a penthouse in Gotham or left the manor to serve as a school or orphanage.
