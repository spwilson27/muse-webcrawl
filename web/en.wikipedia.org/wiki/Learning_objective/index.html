Learning objective

A learning objective (also called a learning outcome) is a statement describing what a learner is expected to know, understand, or be able to do after completing a course, lesson, or other instructional activity. Objectives guide the design of teaching, materials, and assessment.

Formulation

Well-formed objectives are typically specific and measurable, framed in terms of observable learner behaviour. Methods such as the ABCD model (audience, behaviour, condition, degree) and frameworks like Bloom's taxonomy — which classifies objectives from recall of facts up to analysis, evaluation, and creation — help instructors write objectives at appropriate levels of demand. Learning objectives differ from broader educational goals in being narrower and more directly assessable.

Use in education

Objectives underpin approaches such as outcomes-based education, competency-based training, and backward design, in which teachers first specify desired outcomes, then design assessments, and only then plan instruction.
