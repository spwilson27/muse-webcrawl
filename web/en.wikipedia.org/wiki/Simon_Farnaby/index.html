Simon Farnaby (born 2 April 1973) is an English actor, comedian, and writer. He is a member of the Them There troupe best known for the CBBC sketch show Horrible Histories (2009–2013), the Sky comedy Yonderland (2013–2016), and the BBC sitcom Ghosts (2019–2023), which he co-created and in which he played the disgraced politician Julian Fawcett MP.

Farnaby, co-creator and star of the BBC sitcom Ghosts.

Career

Farnaby was born in Darlington and studied drama at university before working in theatre and comedy, including with the Cambridge Footlights generation of performers. Early television work included appearances in The Mighty Boosh — as the Harbour Master and other roles — and in Jam & Jerusalem and The Midnight Beast.

With the ensemble from Horrible Histories, he co-created the fantasy comedy Yonderland and the feature film Bill (2015), a comic reimagining of Shakespeare's "lost years." The same team created Ghosts, in which Farnaby's character, a trouserless Tory MP who died in a scandal, became a fan favourite; the series ran for five series and a Christmas special, concluding in 2023.

Film work

Farnaby co-wrote, with Paul King, the acclaimed film Paddington 2 (2017), in which he also played Barry the security guard, and he appeared in Mindhorn (2016), Christopher Robin (2018), and The Phantom of the Open (2021). He again collaborated with King as co-writer of Wonka (2023), playing zoo nightwatchman Basil.
