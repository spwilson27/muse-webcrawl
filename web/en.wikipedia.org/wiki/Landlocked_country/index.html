Landlocked country

A landlocked country is a country that does not have territory connected to an ocean or sea, or whose coastline lies solely on endorheic basins. There are currently 44 landlocked countries, 16 of them in Africa — the highest number of any continent. Kazakhstan is the world's largest landlocked country by area, and Ethiopia the most populous.

Double landlocked countries

Two countries, Liechtenstein and Uzbekistan, are "doubly landlocked": they are surrounded entirely by other landlocked countries, so a citizen would have to cross at least two borders to reach a coastline.

Significance

Being landlocked often puts a country at an economic disadvantage, as it lacks direct access to maritime trade and must depend on transit agreements with neighbours. The United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea grants landlocked states rights of access to and from the sea.
