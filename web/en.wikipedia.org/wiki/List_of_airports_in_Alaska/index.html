List of airports in Alaska

This is a list of airports in Alaska (a U.S. state), grouped by type and sorted by location. It contains all public-use and military airports in the state. Some private-use and former airports may be included where notable. Alaska has more airports per capita than any other U.S. state, because much of the state is not connected to the road system and communities depend on aviation for passengers, mail, and freight.
