Michigan Wolverines football

The Michigan Wolverines football team represents the University of Michigan in college football at the NCAA Division I Football Bowl Subdivision level. Michigan has the most all-time wins in college football history and has competed since 1879. The Wolverines play their home games at Michigan Stadium in Ann Arbor and are members of the Big Ten Conference. The programme claims 12 national championships, has won 45 conference titles, and produced three Heisman Trophy winners — Tom Harmon, Desmond Howard and Charles Woodson.

History

Michigan rose to national prominence under Fielding H. Yost, whose "Point-a-Minute" teams of 1901–1905 lost only one game and won the first Rose Bowl, 49–0 over Stanford in 1902. Later golden eras came under Fritz Crisler, who introduced the famous winged helmet in 1938; under Bo Schembechler (1969–1989), whose teams won 13 Big Ten titles and whose rivalry with Woody Hayes at Ohio State defined the "Ten Year War"; and under Lloyd Carr, who won the 1997 national championship. After leaner years under Rich Rodriguez and Brady Hoke, Jim Harbaugh (2015–2023) rebuilt the programme, winning three straight Big Ten titles and the national championship for the 2023 season with a 15–0 record capped by victory over Washington in the College Football Playoff final. Harbaugh then left for the NFL, succeeded by Sherrone Moore.

Rivalries

The season-ending game against Ohio State — simply "The Game" — is among the most famous rivalries in American sport. Michigan also contests the Paul Bunyan Trophy with Michigan State, the Little Brown Jug with Minnesota (the oldest trophy game in the FBS, dating to 1903), and a storied intersectional rivalry with Notre Dame.

Traditions

The Wolverines' winged helmet design, the banner the players touch while running onto the field ("Go Blue — M Club Supports You"), the fight song "The Victors" (hailed by John Philip Sousa as the greatest college fight song), and the marching band's high-stepping style are among college football's most recognisable traditions.
