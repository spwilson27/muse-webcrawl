VAL (automated subway)

VAL (Véhicule Automatique Léger, "automatic light vehicle") is a type of driverless (automated), rubber-tyred people mover technology, first developed in France by Matra and now marketed by Siemens Mobility.

History

The first VAL line opened in Lille in 1983 as the Lille Metro, the world's first fully automated metro line of a large city. The technology uses rubber tyres running on concrete guideways, with guidance by horizontal wheels against side guide bars, and fully automatic operation controlled from a central post.

Systems

VAL systems operate in Lille, Toulouse and Rennes (France), Turin (Italy), Taipei (Taiwan, the Wenhu line, using the wider VAL 256 variant), and at several airports including Paris Orly (Orlyval), Chicago O'Hare and others. Vehicle types include the VAL 206, VAL 208 and VAL 256.
