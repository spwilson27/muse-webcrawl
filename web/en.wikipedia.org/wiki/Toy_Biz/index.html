Toy Biz, Inc. was an American toy company best known for producing action figures based on Marvel Comics characters. Founded in 1987 as the American arm of the Canadian company Charan Industries, it became a subsidiary of Marvel Entertainment and played a central role in Marvel's survival and revival during the 1990s.

History

Toy Biz began by licensing properties such as DC Comics characters before securing the Marvel license, which became its mainstay. Its X-Men and Spider-Man action figure lines of the early 1990s, tied to popular animated series, were major commercial successes. In 1993 Marvel acquired a large stake in the company, and toy executive Avi Arad became a key figure in both companies.

When Marvel filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in 1996 amid the collapse of the comic book speculation market, a bitter boardroom battle followed between Carl Icahn and Ronald Perelman. Toy Biz, led by Isaac Perlmutter and Avi Arad, effectively merged with Marvel in 1998 to form Marvel Enterprises; Perlmutter later became Marvel's CEO.

Products

Toy Biz produced lines for X-Men, Spider-Man, the Fantastic Four, the Hulk, Daredevil and The Lord of the Rings film trilogy. Its 6-inch Marvel Legends line, launched in 2002, was acclaimed by collectors for its articulation and sculpting and set the standard for collector-oriented action figures.

End of the brand

In 2006 Marvel licensed its toy rights to Hasbro, effective 2007. Toy Biz was renamed Marvel Toys, but the line was soon discontinued and the Toy Biz name passed into history, though its Marvel Legends format continued under Hasbro.
