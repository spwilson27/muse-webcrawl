Climate Change Committee

Formed2008
JurisdictionUnited Kingdom
TypeNon-departmental public body

The Climate Change Committee (CCC) is an independent statutory body in the United Kingdom, established under the Climate Change Act 2008. It advises the UK government on setting carbon budgets and reports on progress towards emissions targets.

Role

The Committee recommends the level of five-yearly carbon budgets, assesses progress in reducing greenhouse gas emissions, and, through its Adaptation Committee, advises on preparing for the impacts of climate change. Its reports are laid before Parliament.

Carbon budgets

The CCC recommended the UK's successive carbon budgets and advised on the legislated target of net zero emissions by 2050, adopted in 2019, making the UK the first major economy to set such a target in law.
