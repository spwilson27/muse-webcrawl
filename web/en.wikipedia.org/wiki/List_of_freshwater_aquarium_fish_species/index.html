List of freshwater aquarium fish species

Freshwater aquarium fish
Thousands of species are kept in home aquaria; a few dozen dominate the trade.

This is a list of freshwater fish species commonly kept in aquaria, drawn from families such as the characins, cyprinids, cichlids, catfishes and livebearers. Species are generally selected for hardiness, colour, temperament and compatibility in community tanks.
