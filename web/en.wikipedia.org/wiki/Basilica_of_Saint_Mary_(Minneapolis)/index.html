The Basilica of Saint Mary is a Roman Catholic minor basilica located on Hennepin Avenue in downtown Minneapolis, Minnesota, United States. It was the first basilica established in the United States. The basilica serves as a co-cathedral of the Archdiocese of Saint Paul and Minneapolis, together with the Cathedral of Saint Paul.

The Basilica of Saint Mary from Hennepin Avenue.

History

The parish was founded in 1866 as the Church of the Immaculate Conception. Archbishop John Ireland commissioned French-American architect Emmanuel Louis Masqueray — who also designed the Cathedral of Saint Paul — to build a grand new church. Construction began in 1907, and the first Mass was celebrated in the unfinished structure in 1914; the ornate interior, with its marble altar and 84-foot-high (26 m) granite dome, took decades to complete. In 1926 Pope Pius XI raised the church to the rank of minor basilica, the first in the United States.

Community life

The basilica is known for its social outreach, its music programme, and the annual Basilica Block Party, a two-day music festival held since 1995 whose proceeds fund the basilica's restoration and charitable works. The building was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 1975.
