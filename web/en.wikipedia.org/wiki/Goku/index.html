Son Goku is a fictional character and the main protagonist of the Dragon Ball manga series created by Akira Toriyama. Loosely inspired by Sun Wukong, the Monkey King of the Chinese novel Journey to the West, Goku is a Saiyan raised on Earth who defends the planet against a succession of increasingly powerful villains while continually seeking stronger opponents and new levels of power.

Character

Goku is introduced as a cheerful, naive boy with a monkey tail and extraordinary strength, trained in martial arts by his adoptive grandfather Gohan and later by Master Roshi, Kami and King Kai. As an adult he learns that he is a member of the Saiyan race, sent to Earth as an infant. He marries Chi-Chi and has two sons, Gohan and Goten. His signature techniques include the Kamehameha energy wave and transformations such as Super Saiyan, first achieved in the battle against Frieza, and later forms including Ultra Instinct in Dragon Ball Super.

Appearances

Goku is the central character of the Dragon Ball manga (1984–1995) and its anime adaptations Dragon Ball, Dragon Ball Z, Dragon Ball GT and Dragon Ball Super, as well as numerous films, video games and other media. In Japanese media he is voiced by Masako Nozawa; in English dubs he is principally voiced by Sean Schemmel as an adult.

Cultural impact

Goku is one of the most recognisable characters in anime and manga worldwide. He has been cited as an influence by numerous later manga artists and his rivalry with Vegeta and battles with Frieza and Jiren are regarded as landmarks of the shōnen genre. In 2015, 9 May was officially registered in Japan as "Goku Day", from the Japanese reading of the numbers 5 (go) and 9 (ku).
