Cuttack
State: Odisha, India
Known for: Silver filigree (tarakasi)
Rivers: Mahanadi, Kathajodi

Cuttack is a city in the eastern Indian state of Odisha and the administrative headquarters of Cuttack district. One of the oldest cities in India, it was the capital of Odisha for centuries before Bhubaneswar, and today forms a twin-city metropolitan area with it.

History

Cuttack's name derives from the Sanskrit word Kataka, meaning a military cantonment or fort, referring to the Barabati Fort around which the city grew. The fort was built in the 14th century by the Ganga dynasty. The city later came under Maratha and then British rule.

Culture and economy

Cuttack is famed for its silver filigree work (tarakasi), horn and brass crafts, and textiles. It hosts the historic Bali Jatra festival commemorating Odisha's maritime trade with Southeast Asia. The city is also the birthplace of Subhas Chandra Bose and is home to the Odisha High Court and Ravenshaw University.
