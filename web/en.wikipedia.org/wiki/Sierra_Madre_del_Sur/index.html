Sierra Madre del Sur

LocationSouthern Mexico
StatesGuerrero, Oaxaca, Michoacán
LengthAbout 1,000 km

The Sierra Madre del Sur is a mountain range in southern Mexico, extending for roughly 1,000 kilometres from the state of Michoacán through Guerrero and Oaxaca along the Pacific coast. It lies between the Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt to the north and the Isthmus of Tehuantepec to the east.

Geography

The range consists of rugged, deeply dissected terrain with elevations commonly between 2,000 and 3,000 metres. Many rivers flow southward from the range to the Pacific Ocean, while north-flowing streams drain toward the Balsas River.

Ecology

The Sierra Madre del Sur pine–oak forests ecoregion covers the higher elevations and is noted for high biodiversity and endemism, while dry forests occupy the lower slopes and basins such as the Balsas dry forests. The region is one of Mexico's most important centres of plant diversity.
