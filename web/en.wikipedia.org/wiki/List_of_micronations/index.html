List of micronations

Micronations are political entities whose representatives claim to constitute independent nations but which lack recognition by world governments. They are distinct from microstates, which are recognised sovereign states of very small size. Micronations are typically founded for reasons ranging from political protest and social experimentation to art, entertainment and, occasionally, fraud.

Characteristics

Many micronations issue passports, stamps, coins and titles, hold elections and maintain elaborate constitutions, flags and national anthems. Scholars treat them as a form of political expression and model-state building; international law does not recognise them because they generally lack effective government over a defined territory and population, or because the territory is already claimed by a recognised state.
