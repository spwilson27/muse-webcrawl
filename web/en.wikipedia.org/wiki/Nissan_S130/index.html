Nissan S130

The Nissan S130 is the second generation of the Z-car, sold from 1978 to 1983 as the Datsun 280ZX (Nissan Fairlady Z in Japan). Where the original S30 was a pure sports car, the S130 shifted toward grand touring comfort, with softer suspension, more sound insulation and luxury options.

Specifications

It used the 2.8-litre L28 inline-six (about 135–145 hp), and from 1981 a turbocharged L28ET version producing about 180 hp — then among the fastest Japanese cars sold in America. A T-top roof was offered, and the 1982–83 models introduced features unusual for the era, including a voice warning system.

Reception

The 280ZX was a strong seller and was named Motor Trend Import Car of the Year in 1979, though enthusiasts debated its softer character. It was replaced by the Z31 300ZX in 1983.
