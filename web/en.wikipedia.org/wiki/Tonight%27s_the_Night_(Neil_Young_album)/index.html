Tonight's the Night (Neil Young album)

Tonight's the NightArtist: Neil Young
Released: 20 June 1975
Recorded: 1973
Genre: Rock, country rock

Tonight's the Night is the sixth studio album by Canadian-American musician Neil Young, released in 1975. Recorded mostly in 1973, it is a raw, grief-stricken record made in the wake of the drug-overdose deaths of Crazy Horse guitarist Danny Whitten and roadie Bruce Berry.

Background

Young recorded the album live in the studio with members of Crazy Horse and Nils Lofgren, deliberately retaining rough performances and between-song patter. The record company delayed its release for nearly two years, during which Young released On the Beach instead.

Content

Songs such as the title track, "Tired Eyes" and "Albuquerque" confront addiction and loss directly. The album closes with "Come On Baby Let's Go Downtown", a live recording featuring Danny Whitten.

Reception

Though initially a modest seller, Tonight's the Night is now regarded as one of Young's greatest albums and a landmark of 1970s rock, frequently appearing on critics' lists of the best albums of all time.
