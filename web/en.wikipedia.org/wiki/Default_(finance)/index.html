Default (finance)

In finance, default is failure to meet the legal obligations of a loan - failing to make scheduled principal or interest payments or breaching covenants. A debtor who defaults may face enforcement, restructuring or bankruptcy.

Types

Technical default breaches a covenant without a missed payment; sovereign default is a government failing on its debt, as with Argentina and Greece in recent decades. Corporate defaults are tracked by credit rating agencies, and default risk is priced into bond yields and credit default swaps.

Consequences

Default damages credit ratings, raises borrowing costs, and may trigger cross-default clauses accelerating other debts. Secured creditors may seize collateral.
