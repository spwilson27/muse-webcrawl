Die Hard (franchise)

Die Hard is an American action film franchise centred on John McClane, a New York City police detective played by Bruce Willis, who repeatedly finds himself the lone obstacle to elaborate criminal schemes. The original Die Hard (1988), directed by John McTiernan, is widely regarded as one of the greatest action films ever made.

Films

The series comprises Die Hard (1988), Die Hard 2 (1990), Die Hard with a Vengeance (1995), Live Free or Die Hard (2007) and A Good Day to Die Hard (2013). The first film, adapted from Roderick Thorp's novel Nothing Lasts Forever, pits McClane against Hans Gruber's thieves in a Los Angeles skyscraper at Christmas, and helped redefine the action genre around an ordinary, vulnerable hero.

Legacy

The franchise spawned video games, comics and the catchphrase "Yippee-ki-yay", and its formula inspired a generation of "Die Hard on a…" imitators.
