UnitedHealth Group Incorporated is an American multinational health insurance and health services company headquartered in Minnetonka, Minnesota. It is one of the largest healthcare companies in the world by revenue and the largest health insurer in the United States.

The company operates through two main platforms: UnitedHealthcare, which provides health insurance coverage to tens of millions of people, and Optum, which delivers health services, pharmacy benefit management, data analytics, and care delivery through a large network of clinics and physicians.

History

UnitedHealth Group traces its origins to the founding of Charter Med in 1974 and United HealthCare Corporation in 1977 by Richard Burke. It grew through numerous acquisitions, including the formation of Optum and purchases of pharmacy benefit managers and care providers. The company has also faced public scrutiny over claim denials and billing practices, and in December 2024 its insurance division CEO, Brian Thompson, was killed in New York City.
