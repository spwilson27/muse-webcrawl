Lorenzo Jerald Patterson (born June 16, 1969), better known by his stage name MC Ren, is an American rapper from Compton, California. He is a founding member of the influential gangsta rap group N.W.A, alongside Eazy-E, Ice Cube, Dr. Dre and DJ Yella, and the founder of his own label, Villain.

Career

Ren began rapping as a teenager and joined Eazy-E's Ruthless Records in the late 1980s. He was a principal songwriter for N.W.A, contributing heavily to the group's landmark albums Straight Outta Compton (1988) and Niggaz4Life (1991), and to Eazy-E's solo debut. Known for his aggressive delivery and politically charged lyrics, he was sometimes called "The Villain" or "The Ruthless Villain".

Solo work

After N.W.A disbanded in 1991, Ren released the EP Kizz My Black Azz (1992), which went platinum, followed by solo albums including Shock of the Hour (1993), The Villain in Black (1996) and Ruthless for Life (1998). In 1993 he converted to Islam. He was portrayed by Aldis Hodge in the 2015 biographical film Straight Outta Compton, and he was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame as a member of N.W.A in 2016.
