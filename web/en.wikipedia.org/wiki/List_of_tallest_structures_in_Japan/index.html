List of tallest structures in Japan

The tallest structure in Japan is the Tokyo Skytree, a broadcasting and observation tower in Sumida, Tokyo, completed in 2012. At 634 metres (2,080 ft), it was the tallest tower in the world at completion and the second-tallest structure of any kind, after Dubai's Burj Khalifa. The height was chosen so that the digits 6-3-4 can be read "Mu-sa-shi," after the historic Musashi Province.

Japan's tallest building is the Azabudai Hills Mori JP Tower in Tokyo, completed in 2023 at 325.2 metres, which surpassed Osaka's Abeno Harukas. Because of earthquake risk, Japanese high-rise construction uses advanced base isolation and damping systems, and building heights were limited by law to 31 metres until the 1960s.
