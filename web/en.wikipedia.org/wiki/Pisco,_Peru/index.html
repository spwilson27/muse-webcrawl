Pisco is a city and port on the Pacific coast of south-central Peru, in the Ica Region, about 235 kilometres (146 mi) south of Lima. It is the capital of Pisco Province. The city gives its name to pisco, the grape brandy that is Peru's national spirit, which was historically shipped abroad from this port.

History

The area was inhabited in pre-Columbian times by the Paracas culture, famed for its textiles and funerary bundles, and later formed part of the Inca Empire. The Spanish town was founded in 1640 near the site of an Indigenous settlement whose name, Pisco, derives from the Quechua word pishku, meaning "bird", a reference to the abundant seabirds of the nearby bay and islands.

During the colonial period Pisco became an important port for the export of wine and aguardiente produced in the vineyards of the Ica valley. The grape spirit shipped from the port became known simply as "pisco". The city was attacked by pirates and rebuilt several times, and it played a role in the Peruvian War of Independence: in September 1820 the liberating expedition of General José de San Martín landed at nearby Paracas Bay.

2007 earthquake

On 15 August 2007 a magnitude 8.0 earthquake struck off the coast near Pisco, devastating the city. A large majority of its buildings were damaged or destroyed, and hundreds of residents were killed, many when the roof of the San Clemente church collapsed during a funeral mass. Reconstruction proceeded over the following years.

Geography and economy

Pisco lies in the coastal desert, irrigated by the Pisco River. Its economy rests on fishing and fishmeal, cotton and agricultural processing, tourism, and the port. Nearby attractions include the Paracas National Reserve and the Ballestas Islands, known for sea lions, Humboldt penguins and guano birds; guano from the Chincha Islands offshore was a major export in the 19th century.

Transport

The city is served by the Pan-American Highway and by Capitán FAP Renán Elías Olivera Airport, used mainly for flights over the Nazca Lines and general aviation.
