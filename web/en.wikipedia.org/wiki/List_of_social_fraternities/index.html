List of social fraternities

Social fraternities are social (as distinct from professional, service or honorary) Greek-letter organisations for undergraduate students, principally in North America. Most belong to the North American Interfraternity Conference (NIC). The first modern social fraternity, Kappa Alpha Society, was founded in 1825 at Union College; together with Sigma Phi (1827) and Delta Phi (1827) it forms the Union Triad.
