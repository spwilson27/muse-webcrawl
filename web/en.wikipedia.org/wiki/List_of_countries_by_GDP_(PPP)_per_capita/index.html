List of countries by GDP (PPP) per capita

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This article lists countries and dependencies by their gross domestic product (GDP) at purchasing power parity (PPP) per capita — the value of all final goods and services produced within a country in a given year, divided by the average population for the same year and adjusted for cost-of-living differences. Figures are given in international dollars (Int$), principally from the International Monetary Fund (IMF) World Economic Outlook.

Methodology and caveats

Purchasing power parity adjusts for differences in price levels between countries, so PPP figures are often considered more meaningful than nominal GDP for comparing living standards. However, per-capita figures are statistical averages and say nothing about income distribution. Ireland's and Luxembourg's figures are inflated by multinational profit-shifting and cross-border workers respectively; Ireland's statistics office publishes modified GNI (GNI*) as an alternative measure. Estimates for some countries are based on older data and should be treated with caution.
