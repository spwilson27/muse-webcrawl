Chasseurs alpins

CountryFrance
BranchFrench Army
RoleMountain infantry
NicknameLes Diables Bleus ("The Blue Devils")

The Chasseurs alpins are the elite mountain infantry of the French Army, trained for mountain and arctic warfare. They were created in 1888 to counter Italian Alpini units along France's alpine frontier.

History

Chasseurs alpins battalions distinguished themselves in both world wars, earning the nickname "Blue Devils" from their German opponents in the First World War. In the Second World War they fought in Norway, France and Italy.

Today

The modern mountain troops are grouped principally in the 27th Mountain Infantry Brigade, headquartered at Varces, and are recognizable by their wide dark-blue beret, the tarte.
