Guadalcanal

Guadalcanal is the principal island in Guadalcanal Province of the Solomon Islands in the southwestern Pacific Ocean. It is the largest island in the country, covering about 5,300 square kilometres, and is home to Honiara, the national capital, located on its northern coast.

The island is mountainous and covered in tropical rainforest, with Mount Austen and the weather coast among its notable features. Its indigenous peoples are Melanesian, speaking numerous local languages alongside Solomon Islands Pijin.

Guadalcanal became world-famous during the Second World War as the site of the Guadalcanal campaign (August 1942 – February 1943), the first major Allied offensive against the Empire of Japan. Fierce fighting on land, at sea and in the air around Henderson Field ended in an Allied victory and marked a turning point in the Pacific War.
