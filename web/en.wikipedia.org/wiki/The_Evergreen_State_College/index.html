The Evergreen State College

The Evergreen State College is a public liberal arts college in Olympia, Washington. Founded in 1967 and opened to students in 1971, it was created as an experimental alternative to traditional higher education, offering interdisciplinary study and narrative student evaluations in place of conventional letter grades and majors.

Academics

Evergreen's curriculum is organised around interdisciplinary "programs," often team-taught by faculty from several disciplines, in place of separate courses and departments. Students design their own areas of emphasis and receive detailed written evaluations from faculty rather than grades, supplemented by an academic transcript summarising their work. The college offers bachelor's degrees (B.A. and B.S.) and master's programmes including the Master of Environmental Studies and the Master in Teaching.

Campus and identity

The campus occupies about 1,000 acres of forest on the Eld Inlet shore of Puget Sound, with its own organic farm and beach. Its sports teams are the Geoducks, named for the large clam native to the region, and its unofficial motto — Omnia Extares, a joking pseudo-Latin phrase meaning "let it all hang out" — reflects its countercultural reputation. Notable alumni include cartoonist Matt Groening, musician Courtney Love, and rapper Macklemore. The college drew national attention in 2017 for campus protests and disputes over its "Day of Absence" tradition.
