Founded2016/2017 (from TCDD restructuring)

TCDD Taşımacılık A.Ş. is the state-owned railway operating company of Turkey. It was created in the restructuring of Turkish State Railways (TCDD) under the 2013 railway liberalisation law: TCDD retained ownership of the infrastructure, while train operations — passenger and freight — were transferred to the new subsidiary, which began operations on 1 January 2017.

Services

TCDD Taşımacılık operates Turkey's high-speed YHT services (Ankara–Istanbul, Ankara–Konya, Ankara–Sivas and related lines), conventional intercity trains such as the Eastern Express (Doğu Ekspresi) to Kars, regional trains, and suburban services including Istanbul's Marmaray (jointly significant) and Ankara's Başkentray. It also carries the great majority of rail freight in Turkey, including block trains to border crossings with Iran, Georgia and Europe.

Liberalisation

The restructuring was designed to open the Turkish rail market to private operators, who may run their own trains over the TCDD network by paying track-access charges. TCDD Taşımacılık remains by far the dominant operator and continues to receive public-service support for loss-making passenger routes.
