Harvard Business School (HBS) is the graduate business school of Harvard University, located on a 40-acre campus in Allston, Massachusetts, across the Charles River from Harvard Square in Cambridge. Founded in 1908 as the first graduate school of business administration in the United States, it offers a full-time MBA program, doctoral programs (including the DBA and PhD), and numerous executive education programs. It owns Harvard Business Publishing, which publishes business books, leadership articles, online management tools, and the monthly Harvard Business Review magazine.

History

The school was founded in 1908 under the leadership of Harvard president Charles William Eliot, initially operating as part of the Faculty of Arts and Sciences. Its first dean was the historian Edwin Francis Gay. In 1927 the school moved to its purpose-built Georgian-style campus in Allston, funded in part by a gift from banker George F. Baker, for whom Baker Library is named. Women were first admitted to the regular MBA program in 1963. The school became famous worldwide for its case method of teaching, in which students analyse real business situations drawn from written cases.

Case method

HBS is the originator and best-known practitioner of the case method: rather than lectures, classes revolve around discussion of detailed case studies of actual companies facing actual decisions. Students are expected to prepare extensively, and class participation forms a large share of the grade. The school's case collection, sold through Harvard Business Publishing, is used by business schools around the world.

Programs and rankings

The MBA program enrols roughly 930 students per class year, divided into sections of about 90. HBS consistently ranks among the top business schools in the world in publications such as U.S. News & World Report, The Economist, and the Financial Times. Its doctoral programs are run jointly with the Graduate School of Arts and Sciences. The campus includes Baker Library/Bloomberg Center, Aldrich Hall, and the Chao Center for executive education.

Notable alumni

Graduates include former U.S. president George W. Bush, former New York City mayor Michael Bloomberg, investor and former Treasury Secretary Henry Paulson, former Facebook COO Sheryl Sandberg, and JPMorgan Chase CEO Jamie Dimon.
