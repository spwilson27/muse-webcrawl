The Divergent Series is a trilogy of young adult dystopian novels by American author Veronica Roth: Divergent (2011), Insurgent (2012), and Allegiant (2013). A companion volume, Four: A Divergent Collection (2014), retells scenes and events from the perspective of the character Tobias "Four" Eaton.

Setting and plot

The series is set in a future Chicago where society is divided into five factions based on personality traits: Abnegation (the selfless), Amity (the peaceful), Candor (the honest), Dauntless (the brave), and Erudite (the intelligent). At sixteen, citizens choose their faction for life. The protagonist, Beatrice "Tris" Prior, discovers she is "Divergent" — fitting into multiple factions — a trait considered dangerous by the ruling authorities. As conflict between factions escalates into civil war, later books reveal that Chicago is an experiment run by an outside Bureau of Genetic Welfare concerned with genetically "damaged" and "pure" populations.

Adaptations

The novels were adapted by Summit Entertainment into a film series starring Shailene Woodley as Tris and Theo James as Four: Divergent (2014), The Divergent Series: Insurgent (2015), and The Divergent Series: Allegiant (2016). A planned fourth film, Ascendant, was cancelled after the third film underperformed, and a proposed television continuation did not proceed.
