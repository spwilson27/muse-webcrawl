The Dark Knight is a 2008 superhero film directed by Christopher Nolan from a screenplay he co-wrote with his brother Jonathan. Based on the DC Comics character Batman, it is the second installment of Nolan's Dark Knight trilogy and the sequel to Batman Begins (2005). Christian Bale stars as Bruce Wayne / Batman, who, with Lieutenant James Gordon (Gary Oldman) and district attorney Harvey Dent (Aaron Eckhart), moves to dismantle organized crime in Gotham City — only to face the Joker (Heath Ledger), an anarchic criminal who plunges the city into chaos.

Plot

Batman, Gordon, and Dent form an alliance against the mob, aided by a fraudster, Lau, extradited from Hong Kong. The Joker, a scarred agent of chaos hired by the mob, assassinates officials, forces Batman into impossible choices, and corrupts Dent after the death of Rachel Dawes, transforming him into the vengeful Two-Face. In the climax, Gotham's citizens and convicts refuse the Joker's social experiment with two rigged ferries, Batman defeats him, and then takes the blame for Dent's killings so that Dent's image as Gotham's "White Knight" can survive.

Production and release

Nolan cited the crime films of Michael Mann and the Joker's earliest comic appearances as influences; Ledger's immersive preparation for the role became legendary. Sequences were shot with IMAX cameras, a first for a major feature. Ledger died in January 2008, months before release; he posthumously won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor. The film grossed over $1 billion in re-releases and received eight Oscar nominations, also winning Best Sound Editing.

Legacy

The Dark Knight is widely regarded as one of the greatest films of the 2000s and among the finest superhero films ever made. Its exclusion from the Best Picture field in 2008 is widely seen as prompting the Academy to expand that category. In 2020 it was selected for preservation in the US National Film Registry. Its crime-saga tone is often contrasted with the Gothic adaptations of earlier decades, and it stands apart from quieter dramas like The Fighter as a blockbuster landmark.
