Lebanon

Lebanon, officially the Republic of Lebanon, is a country in the Levant region of West Asia, bordered by Syria, Israel and the Mediterranean Sea. It has a population of about 5.5 million (plus very large numbers of Palestinian and Syrian refugees) and its capital is Beirut.

Lebanon is home to some of the world's oldest cities — Byblos, Tyre, Sidon, Baalbek — Phoenician ports that gave the alphabet to the world. Its confessional political system divides power among Maronite Christians, Sunni and Shia Muslims and Druze. A civil war (1975-1990) shattered 'the Switzerland of the Middle East'; reconstruction under Rafic Hariri (assassinated 2005) followed. Hezbollah, the Iran-backed Shia party-militia, is a state within the state; war with Israel in 2006 and again in 2023-2024 devastated the south and Beirut's suburbs. The 2020 Beirut port explosion and financial collapse — among the worst in modern history — left the state near-failed.
