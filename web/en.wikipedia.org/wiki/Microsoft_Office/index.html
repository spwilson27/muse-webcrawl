Microsoft Office, or simply Office, is a family of client software, server software, and services developed by Microsoft. It was first announced by Bill Gates on 1 August 1988 and first released on 19 November 1990 as a bundle of applications: Microsoft Word, Microsoft Excel, and Microsoft PowerPoint.

Applications

The core desktop applications are Word (word processing), Excel (spreadsheets), PowerPoint (presentations), and Outlook (email and personal information management). Other components include Access (database), Publisher (desktop publishing), OneNote (note-taking), Teams (collaboration), and the server products SharePoint and Exchange. Office file formats — .doc/.docx, .xls/.xlsx, .ppt/.pptx — became de facto industry standards; the XML-based Office Open XML formats were standardized as ISO/IEC 29500.

History

Office evolved from separate applications into an integrated suite with a common interface, adopting the ribbon interface in Office 2007. Versions were released for Windows and Mac, and later as web applications (Office on the web) and mobile apps. In 2011 Microsoft launched Office 365, a subscription service that gradually replaced perpetual licences as the primary offering; in 2020 the consumer and small-business plans were rebranded as Microsoft 365, and the Office brand has since been progressively retired in favour of Microsoft 365, though "Office" releases such as Office 2021 and Office 2024 continued.

Market position

Microsoft Office has long been the dominant office productivity suite worldwide, used by over a billion people, with competition from Google Workspace, LibreOffice, and Apple's iWork.
