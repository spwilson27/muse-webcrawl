This is a list of sponsored stadiums: sports venues whose names have been sold to corporate sponsors under naming rights agreements. The practice became common in North American professional sports in the 1970s and 1980s and later spread to association football in Europe and to venues worldwide. Some deals are among the largest sponsorship contracts in sport.

Other regions

In Australia and New Zealand, sponsored names include Accor Stadium and Marvel Stadium. In Japan, naming rights are widely used for municipal ballparks, and in South Africa venues such as the DHL Newlands and Emirates Airline Park have carried sponsor names. Critics note that fans often continue to use traditional names regardless of sponsorship.
