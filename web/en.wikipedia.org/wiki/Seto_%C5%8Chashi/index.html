Seto Ōhashi

Seto Ōhashi (Great Seto Bridge)
Location: Seto Inland Sea, Japan
Length: 13.1 km
Opened: 1988

The Seto Ōhashi (瀬戸大橋), or Great Seto Bridge, is a series of double-deck bridges connecting Okayama and Kagawa prefectures in Japan across the Seto Inland Sea. Completed in 1988 after a decade of construction, it carries the Honshū–Shikoku highway and a JR railway line.

Structure

The crossing hops across five small islands and combines several bridge types — suspension, cable-stayed, and truss bridges — over a total length of about 13.1 kilometres. The upper deck carries a four-lane expressway, while the lower deck carries the JR Seto-Ōhashi Line, with provision originally made for Shinkansen tracks.

Significance

It was the first of the three Honshū–Shikoku bridge routes to open and dramatically reduced travel time between Honshū and Shikoku, previously dependent on ferries.
