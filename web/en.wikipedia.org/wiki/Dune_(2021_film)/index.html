Dune (2021 film)

Dune (on-screen: Dune: Part One)
Director: Denis Villeneuve
Based on: Dune by Frank Herbert
Starring: Timothée Chalamet, Rebecca Ferguson, Oscar Isaac, Zendaya
Released: 2021 · Runtime: 155 minutes
Box office: approx. $434 million

Dune (titled on-screen Dune: Part One) is a 2021 American epic science fiction film directed by Denis Villeneuve, who co-wrote the screenplay with Jon Spaihts and Eric Roth. It is the first of a two-part adaptation of Frank Herbert's 1965 novel, covering roughly the first half of the book.

Plot

In the year 10191, Duke Leto Atreides accepts control of the desert planet Arrakis, sole source of the spice melange, from the Padishah Emperor — a trap that brings the Atreides into conflict with the brutal House Harkonnen. After a devastating betrayal, Leto's son Paul and his mother Jessica, a Bene Gesserit acolyte, escape into the deep desert among the Fremen, where Paul's prescient visions begin to unfold.

Production and reception

Filmed in Jordan, Abu Dhabi, Hungary and Norway with production design by Patrice Vermette and cinematography by Greig Fraser, and scored by Hans Zimmer, the film premiered at Venice in September 2021 and was released simultaneously in cinemas and on HBO Max. It grossed about $434 million and won six Academy Awards, including Best Cinematography, Best Production Design and Best Original Score. Its sequel, Dune: Part Two, followed in 2024.
