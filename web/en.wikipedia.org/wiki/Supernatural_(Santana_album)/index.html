Supernatural is the eighteenth studio album by American rock band Santana, released on 15 June 1999 by Arista Records. Conceived by label head Clive Davis as a comeback record, the album pairs Carlos Santana with a series of guest artists, including Rob Thomas, Eric Clapton, Lauryn Hill, Dave Matthews, Maná, Eagle-Eye Cherry and Wyclef Jean.

Cover art of Supernatural (1999)

Commercial performance

The album was a major commercial success and a career revival for Santana. It reached number one on the US Billboard 200, sold more than 30 million copies worldwide, and became one of the best-selling albums of all time. The lead single "Smooth", featuring Rob Thomas of Matchbox Twenty, topped the Billboard Hot 100 for 12 weeks, while "Maria Maria", featuring the Product G&B, also reached number one.

Grammy Awards

At the 42nd Annual Grammy Awards in 2000, Supernatural won nine awards, including Album of the Year, equalling the record then held by Michael Jackson's Thriller. "Smooth" won Record of the Year and Song of the Year.
