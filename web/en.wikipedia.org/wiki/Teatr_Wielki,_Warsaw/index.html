The Grand Theatre (Polish: Teatr Wielki) in Warsaw is one of the largest theatres in Europe and the home of the Polish National Opera and the Polish National Ballet. It stands on Theatre Square (Plac Teatralny) in central Warsaw.

History

Designed by Antonio Corazzi, the neoclassical building opened in 1833 with a performance of Rossini's The Barber of Seville. The theatre was largely destroyed during the Second World War — only the façade survived — and was rebuilt between 1949 and 1965, reopening with greatly expanded stage facilities.

Today

The main auditorium seats about 1,800 people. The building also houses the National Museum's theatre collection and smaller performance spaces, and it remains the centrepiece of Polish operatic life.
