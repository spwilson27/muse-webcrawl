Karumba

Karumba
State: Queensland
Country: Australia
Location: Gulf of Carpentaria
Known for: Barramundi and prawn fishing

Karumba is a town and coastal locality in the Shire of Carpentaria, Queensland, Australia. Situated where the Norman River meets the Gulf of Carpentaria, it is one of the few places on the Gulf coast accessible by sealed road.

Economy

Karumba is a centre for the Gulf's barramundi and prawn fisheries and a popular destination for recreational fishing and birdwatching. The town also services the Century zinc mine through its port facilities.

Geography and climate

The town has a tropical savanna climate with a pronounced wet season. It is noted for spectacular sunsets over the Gulf and, in the warmer months, for the Morning Glory cloud phenomenon that rolls in from the Gulf.
