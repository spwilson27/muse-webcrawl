21st Century Fox

21st Century Fox was an American multinational mass media corporation based in Midtown Manhattan, New York City. It was formed on June 28, 2013, as the legal successor to News Corporation, following a spin-off of the media conglomerate's publishing assets. The new company took over the entertainment and media operations of the old News Corporation, including the Fox Entertainment Group, STAR TV, and 20th Century Fox.

History

The split of News Corporation into 21st Century Fox (entertainment) and a new News Corp (publishing) was announced in 2012 and completed in 2013, with Rupert Murdoch remaining chairman of both companies. The company's holdings included the Fox Broadcasting Company, Fox News Channel, FX, National Geographic Partners, and a 39% stake in the British satellite broadcaster Sky plc.

In December 2017, The Walt Disney Company announced it would acquire most of 21st Century Fox's entertainment assets in a deal valued at approximately $71.3 billion. The acquisition closed on March 20, 2019. Following the sale, the remaining assets — the Fox Broadcasting Company, Fox News, Fox Sports 1 and 2, and the Fox studio lot — were spun off into a new company, Fox Corporation.

Assets

Major assets before the Disney acquisition included 20th Century Fox film studio, Fox Searchlight Pictures, Blue Sky Studios, Fox Television Stations, FX Networks, Fox Sports, and stakes in Hulu and Tata Sky.
