The Venetian Macao (Chinese: 澳門威尼斯人度假村酒店) is a luxury hotel and casino resort in Macau owned by the American Las Vegas Sands company. It is modelled on its sister casino resort The Venetian Las Vegas, and like that property features Venice-themed architecture, canals with gondolas, and replicas of Venetian landmarks such as St Mark's Campanile and the Rialto Bridge.

Scale and attractions

Opened on August 28, 2007, the resort contains 10,500,000 square feet (980,000 m²) of floor space, making it one of the largest buildings in the world by floor area and the largest single-structure hotel building in Asia. Its casino, with some 3,400 slot machines and 800 gaming tables, was the largest in the world at opening. The Cotai Arena, seating 15,000, hosts sporting events and concerts, and the resort includes extensive convention space, over 100,000 square metres of retail at the Shoppes at Venetian, and dozens of restaurants.

The Venetian Macao was the anchor development of the Cotai Strip, the reclaimed-land district between the islands of Taipa and Coloane that has made Macau the world's largest gambling market by revenue.
