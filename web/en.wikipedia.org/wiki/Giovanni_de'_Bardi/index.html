Giovanni Maria de' Bardi, Count of Vernio (1534–1612) was an Italian literary critic, writer, composer, and soldier. His Florence salon hosted the Florentine Camerata, the circle of musicians and scholars whose experiments with monody and ancient Greek drama led directly to the invention of opera.

Intermedi and early opera

Bardi organised the famous intermedi of 1589 for the wedding of Ferdinando I de' Medici, and members of his circle — Jacopo Peri, Giulio Caccini, and Ottavio Rinuccini — created Dafne and Euridice, among the first operas.

Calcio

In 1580 Bardi wrote the Discorso sopra il giuoco del calcio fiorentino, the earliest codified rules of Calcio Fiorentino, the Florentine form of early football still played today.
