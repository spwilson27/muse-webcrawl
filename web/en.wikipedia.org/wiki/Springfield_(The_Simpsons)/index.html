Springfield (The Simpsons)

Springfield is the fictional town in the American animated sitcom The Simpsons, home to the Simpson family and a vast cast of characters. The town's location within the United States is deliberately vague and contradictory, a running gag of the series: it lies near a desert, a glacier, an ocean port, mountains, and a gorge — whatever the plot requires. Creator Matt Groening named it after the generic American town name Springfield, common to many states, and later noted he had been partly inspired by Springfield, Oregon, near his childhood home of Portland, though he insisted the town represents Anytown, USA.

Description

Springfield features the Simpsons' home at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Springfield Elementary School, Moe's Tavern, the Kwik-E-Mart, Springfield Nuclear Power Plant (owned by Mr. Burns), Krusty Burger, and Springfield Town Hall. Its society satirizes American small-town life: corrupt Mayor "Diamond Joe" Quimby, incompetent police chief Clancy Wiggum, and the eternally optimistic Reverend Lovejoy. Geography is elastic — the Springfield Gorge, Mount Springfield, and the desert of the neighbouring town of Shelbyville appear as needed.

The location gag and 2007 contest

The writers guard the town's state jealously; episodes give contradictory clues (a state once implied to be "Northern Kentucky"). For The Simpsons Movie (2007), USA Today ran a contest in which real Springfields competed to host the premiere; Springfield, Vermont won. Springfield also appears in the Universal theme parks' Springfield: Home of the Simpsons lands in Florida and Hollywood.
