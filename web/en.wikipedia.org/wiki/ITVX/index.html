ITVX

ITVX is a British ad-supported video-on-demand streaming service operated by ITV plc. Launched on 8 December 2022, it replaced the ITV Hub as the broadcaster's main streaming platform, offering catch-up television, box sets, live streaming of ITV channels, and a large library of films and acquired programmes.

Service

ITVX is available free with advertising, with an optional Premium tier that removes advertisements and includes access to additional content, including BritBox content in the UK and downloads for offline viewing. The service premiered a number of original and acquired series with full box sets available before or alongside their broadcast on ITV's linear channels, a strategy ITV called "streaming first".

Content

At launch ITV promised around 9,000 hours of content, far more than the ITV Hub. Programming includes ITV dramas and entertainment shows, US acquisitions, anime (following a deal with a specialist distributor), films, and FAST (free ad-supported streaming television) channels dedicated to single series or genres.

Availability

ITVX is available via web browsers and apps on smart TVs, streaming sticks, games consoles and mobile devices in the United Kingdom. The service reported strong growth in streaming hours and monthly active users following its launch.
