Lincoln Futura

Manufacturer: Lincoln (Ford Motor Company)
Designer: Bill Schmidt; built by Ghia, Turin
Year: 1955 concept car
Engine: 368 cu in (6.0 L) Y-block V8

The Lincoln Futura is a concept car designed by the Lincoln division of Ford Motor Company. It was built by Ghia in Turin, Italy, at a cost of $250,000, and hand-built in 1954–55. Styled under Lincoln-Mercury chief stylist Bill Schmidt, the Futura featured a double-bubble Plexiglas canopy, huge hooded headlights, prominent tailfins, and pearl-white (later red) paint. It toured the auto show circuit in 1955 and appeared in the 1959 film It Started with a Kiss.

Conversion into the Batmobile

In 1966 customizer George Barris was commissioned to build the Batmobile for the Batman television series on a tight deadline. Barris converted the Futura he had acquired from Ford for a nominal sum, adding the bat nose, arch, gadgets, and black paint with red pinstriping. The Futura-based Batmobile debuted with the series in January 1966 and became one of the most famous television cars. The original car sold at auction in 2013 for $4.62 million.
