Bnei Atarot (Hebrew: בְּנֵי עֲטָרוֹת, "Sons of Atarot") is a moshav in central Israel, located near Yehud and Ben Gurion International Airport and falling under the jurisdiction of Hevel Modi'in Regional Council.

History

The moshav was founded in 1948 by former residents of Atarot, a settlement north of Jerusalem that was evacuated and destroyed during the 1948 Arab–Israeli War. The new settlement was built on the lands of the depopulated Palestinian village of Rantiya, near the site of the former German Templer colony of Wilhelma, whose residents had been deported during the Second World War.

Several of the Templer-era buildings survive in the moshav. Owing to its proximity to the airport, Bnei Atarot has been affected by noise and expansion plans, and some land was expropriated for airport development.
