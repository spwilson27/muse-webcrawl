I Dream of Jeannie

I Dream of Jeannie is an American fantasy sitcom television series, created by Sidney Sheldon, starring Barbara Eden as a sultry 2,000-year-old genie and Larry Hagman as an astronaut named Anthony "Tony" Nelson, her master. The show was produced by Screen Gems and aired on NBC for five seasons, from September 18, 1965, to May 26, 1970, comprising 139 episodes.

Premise

Captain (later Major) Tony Nelson, an Air Force officer and NASA astronaut, crash-lands on a deserted island in the South Pacific after his space capsule goes off course. On the beach he finds a strange ornate bottle. When he rubs it and removes the stopper, purple smoke pours out and coalesces into a beautiful blonde genie named Jeannie, who grants his rescue and decides to follow him home to Cocoa Beach, Florida.

Jeannie, who speaks archaic English at first and grants wishes by blinking with her arms crossed, constantly complicates Tony's life. He tries to keep her existence secret, particularly from the base psychiatrist, Colonel Alfred Bellows, who repeatedly witnesses Jeannie's magic but can never quite prove what he saw. Over the series, Tony and Jeannie fall in love and are married in the fifth season.

Cast

Barbara Eden played Jeannie, and Larry Hagman played Tony Nelson. Bill Daily played Roger Healey, Tony's best friend and fellow astronaut, who eventually learns Jeannie's secret. Hayden Rorke played Colonel Dr. Alfred Bellows, the skeptical base psychiatrist.

Legacy

Though it received moderate ratings during its original run, the series became a long-running success in syndication and remains a staple of classic television. Its first season was filmed in black and white, with the remaining four seasons in color.
