Checklist

A checklist is a type of job aid used in repetitive tasks to reduce failure by compensating for potential limits of human memory and attention. It helps to ensure consistency and completeness in carrying out a task. A basic example is the "to do list". A more advanced checklist would be a schedule, which lays out tasks to be done according to time of day or other factors, or a pre-flight checklist.

Applications

Checklists are widely used in aviation, where pre-flight checklists help ensure critical steps are not forgotten; in medicine, where surgical safety checklists have been shown to reduce complications and mortality; and in industry, software deployment, and many other fields.

Design

Effective checklists are simple, precise, and arranged in the order tasks are performed. They are often divided into "do-confirm" lists, where tasks are done from memory and then verified, and "read-do" lists, where items are read aloud and then carried out.
