Agamas (Jainism)

The Agamas are the canonical scriptures of Jainism, held by the Śvetāmbara tradition to preserve the teachings of Mahavira as compiled by his disciples (ganadharas). They are written in Ardhamagadhi Prakrit.

Contents

The canon is traditionally organised into groups including the twelve Angas (limbs), among them the Acharanga Sutra on monastic conduct and the Sutrakritanga; the twelve Upangas; the Prakirnaka texts; the Cheda Sutras on monastic discipline; and the Mula Sutras, including the Uttaradhyayana Sutra.

Transmission and sectarian views

According to tradition the canon was preserved orally for centuries and finally written down at a council at Vallabhi in the fifth century CE under Devarddhigani. The Digambara tradition holds that the original Angas were lost, and does not accept the Śvetāmbara canon as authentic, relying instead on later works such as the Shatkhandagama.
