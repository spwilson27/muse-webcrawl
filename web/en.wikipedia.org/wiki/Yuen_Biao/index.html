Yuen Biao (元彪)
Born: Hsiao Ling-chun, 26 July 1957, Nanjing, China
Occupation: Actor, martial artist, stuntman, action choreographer
Years active: 1962–present

Yuen Biao (born Hsiao Ling-chun; 26 July 1957) is a Hong Kong actor, martial artist, and stunt choreographer. He specialises in acrobatics and Chinese martial arts and has worked on over 80 films as actor, stuntman, and action choreographer. Along with Peking Opera School "brothers" Jackie Chan and Sammo Hung, he was a member of the Seven Little Fortunes and is regarded as one of the most acrobatic Hong Kong action stars.

Career

Trained from childhood at the China Drama Academy under master Yu Jim-yuen, Yuen Biao performed acrobatics and stunt work from a young age, doubling for Bruce Lee in Fist of Fury and Enter the Dragon. He gained leading roles in films such as Knockabout (1979) and The Prodigal Son (1981), and starred alongside Chan and Hung in the celebrated trio films Project A (1983), Wheels on Meals (1984), and Dragons Forever (1988).

Renowned for his aerial somersaults and backflips, Yuen Biao also headlined On the Run (1988) and later collaborated extensively as an action choreographer. His performance in The Prodigal Son is frequently cited among the finest in martial arts cinema.
