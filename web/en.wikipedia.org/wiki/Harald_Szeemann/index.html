Harald Szeemann

Harald Szeemann
Born: 11 June 1933, Bern, Switzerland
Died: 18 February 2005, Tegna, Switzerland
Occupation: Curator, art historian
Known for: 'When Attitudes Become Form' (1969), documenta 5

Harald Szeemann (1933–2005) was a Swiss curator who pioneered the role of the independent exhibition-maker, shaping contemporary art through thematic exhibitions.

Career

Director of the Kunsthalle Bern from 1961, Szeemann staged the landmark 1969 exhibition Live in Your Head: When Attitudes Become Form, showcasing conceptual art, land art and Arte Povera. The controversy led to his resignation and founding of his 'Agency for Spiritual Guest Work'.

Major exhibitions

He directed documenta 5 (1972), curated the Venice Biennale's Aperto and later the whole Biennale (1999, 2001), and organised exhibitions on subjects from utopias to 'total works of art' (Der Hang zum Gesamtkunstwerk).

Legacy

Szeemann's vast archive and library were acquired by the Getty Research Institute. He is credited with creating the model of the auteur curator.
