Hip fracture

A hip fracture is a break that occurs in the upper part of the femur (thigh bone). Symptoms may include pain around the hip, particularly with movement, and shortening of the affected leg.

Causes

Most hip fractures in older adults result from a fall onto weakened, osteoporotic bone; in younger people they usually follow high-energy trauma such as car crashes. Risk rises with age, osteoporosis, and certain medications.

Treatment and prognosis

Treatment generally involves surgery — internal fixation, hemiarthroplasty, or total hip replacement — followed by rehabilitation. Hip fractures carry substantial mortality and loss of independence in the elderly, making prevention via bone health and fall reduction important.
