CORINE Land Cover

CORINE Land Cover (CLC)
Operator: European Environment Agency / Copernicus
Coverage: 39 European countries
Editions: 1990, 2000, 2006, 2012, 2018
Classes: 44 land cover classes

CORINE Land Cover (CLC) is a European programme and dataset providing a standardised inventory of land cover across Europe. It is produced by the European Environment Agency as part of the Copernicus Land Monitoring Service, based mainly on interpretation of satellite imagery.

Methodology

The dataset classifies land into 44 classes in three levels — from artificial surfaces (urban fabric, industry, transport) through agricultural areas and forests to wetlands and water bodies. The minimum mapping unit is 25 hectares for the status layers, with change layers mapped at 5 hectares.

Uses

Successive editions (1990, 2000, 2006, 2012, 2018) allow consistent tracking of land-use change — urban sprawl, deforestation, agricultural abandonment — and are used in environmental reporting, spatial planning and research across Europe.
