Alexis Alejandro Sánchez Sánchez (born 19 December 1988), known as Alexis Sánchez, is a Chilean professional footballer who plays as a forward. Nicknamed El Niño Maravilla ("The Wonder Child"), he is Chile's all-time top goalscorer and most-capped player.

Club career

Sánchez began his career at Cobreloa before joining Udinese, with loan spells at Colo-Colo and River Plate. He moved to Barcelona in 2011, winning La Liga and the Copa del Rey, then to Arsenal in 2014, where he won two FA Cups and was named PFA Fans' Player of the Year. He subsequently played for Manchester United and Inter Milan, winning Serie A with Inter, and later for Marseille before returning to Inter and then Udinese.

International career

Sánchez was a central figure in Chile's golden generation, winning the Copa América in 2015 and the Copa América Centenario in 2016, in both cases defeating Argentina in finals decided on penalties.
