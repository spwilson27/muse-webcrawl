The Cheltenham Gold Cup is a Grade 1 National Hunt steeplechase held annually at Cheltenham Racecourse in Gloucestershire, England, as the centrepiece of the Cheltenham Festival in March. It is the most prestigious race of the British and Irish jump-racing season and one of the most valuable, with prize money exceeding £600,000.

The race

The race is run over a distance of about 3 miles 2½ furlongs on the New Course at Cheltenham, with 22 fences to be jumped. It is a weight-for-age race open to horses aged five and over, and its undulating, stamina-sapping track — ending with the famous Cheltenham hill — makes it a supreme test of a staying chaser.

History and notable winners

First run as a jumps race in 1924, the Gold Cup has been won by some of the greatest steeplechasers in history. Golden Miller won a record five consecutive runnings (1932–1936); Arkle won three times in the 1960s; Best Mate (2002–2004) was the first horse since Arkle to win three in a row. Other celebrated winners include Dawn Run — still the only horse to win both the Champion Hurdle and the Gold Cup — Desert Orchid, Kauto Star (the first horse to regain the title, winning in 2007 and 2009), and Al Boum Photo and Galopin Des Champs in recent years.
