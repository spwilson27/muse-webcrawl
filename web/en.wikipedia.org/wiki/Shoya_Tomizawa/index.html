Shoya Tomizawa (富沢 祥也, December 10, 1990 – September 5, 2010) was a Japanese motorcycle racer. He won the inaugural race of the Moto2 World Championship, the 2010 Qatar Grand Prix, riding for the Technomag-CIP team.

Career

Tomizawa began racing in the All Japan Road Race Championship, winning the 125cc title in 2006 and finishing runner-up in the 250cc class in 2008. He made his full-time Grand Prix debut in the 250cc class in 2009 and moved to the new Moto2 class in 2010, winning the season opener at Losail and taking pole position at Jerez.

Death

On September 5, 2010, during the San Marino Grand Prix at Misano, Tomizawa crashed at the Curvone turn and was struck by the following motorcycles of Scott Redding and Alex de Angelis. He died from his injuries later that day. His number 48 was retired from Moto2 in his honour.
