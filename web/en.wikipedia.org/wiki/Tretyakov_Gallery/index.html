Tretyakov Gallery

The State Tretyakov Gallery is an art gallery in Moscow, Russia, and the foremost depository of Russian fine art in the world. It was founded by the merchant and patron Pavel Tretyakov, who began collecting Russian art in the 1850s and donated his collection to the city of Moscow in 1892.

Collection

The gallery's holdings range from medieval icons — including works by Andrei Rublev such as The Trinity — through the great 19th-century realists such as Ilya Repin, Vasily Surikov, and Ivan Shishkin, to Russian avant-garde art, which is displayed in the New Tretyakov building on Krymsky Val.

Buildings

The historic gallery in Lavrushinsky Lane, near the Kremlin, has a distinctive facade designed by the artist Viktor Vasnetsov. The collection numbers well over 100,000 works. See Russian art.
