Copypasta

Copypasta is a block of text that gets copied and pasted across the Internet by individuals through online forums and social networking websites. The term derives from "copy and paste" (pronounced like "pasta"). Copypastas are similar to spam as they are often used to annoy other users and disrupt online discourse; unlike spam, they are generally humorous or absurd texts shared deliberately by users rather than automated advertising.

History

The word "copypasta" is believed to have first appeared on the imageboard 4chan around 2006, and it was added to Urban Dictionary in the same era. Early famous copypastas include the "Navy Seal" rant, in which a poster comically exaggerates his military credentials, and various chain-letter-style stories. On Twitch and other livestreaming platforms, chat copypastas are repeated en masse by viewers as emotes and running jokes.

Creepypasta

A related term, creepypasta, refers to horror-related legends or images that have been copied and pasted around the Internet, such as the Slender Man stories. Other variants include "spamypasta" for spam-like copypasta.
