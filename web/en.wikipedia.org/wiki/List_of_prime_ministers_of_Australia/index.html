List of prime ministers of Australia

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The prime minister of Australia is the head of government of the Commonwealth of Australia. The office has been held by 31 people since federation in 1901. The longest-serving prime minister is Robert Menzies, who served two terms totalling over 18 years; the shortest-serving is Frank Forde, who held office for seven days in 1945. Four prime ministers — Hughes, Menzies, Whitlam and Gillard among them — changed or led parties in unusual circumstances, and several, including Alfred Deakin and Kevin Rudd, served non-consecutive terms.

Records

  • Longest total service: Robert Menzies — 18 years, 5 months, 12 days.
  • Shortest service: Frank Forde — 7 days (caretaker after Curtin's death).
  • Between 2007 and 2022 no prime minister served a full term between elections without being replaced by their own party — a period of chronic leadership instability dubbed the "coup decade".
  • Two prime ministers died in office: Joseph Lyons (1939) and John Curtin (1945); Harold Holt disappeared in office (1967).
