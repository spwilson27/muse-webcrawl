The Type 45 destroyer, also known as the Daring class, is a class of six guided-missile destroyers built for the United Kingdom's Royal Navy. The class is primarily designed for anti-aircraft and anti-missile warfare and is built around the PAAMS (Sea Viper) air-defence system, which combines the SAMPSON multi-function radar and the Aster 30 surface-to-air missiles launched from a Sylver vertical launching system.

The ships — Daring, Dauntless, Diamond, Dragon, Defender and Duncan — replaced the Type 42 destroyers that had served since the 1970s. They use integrated electric propulsion with two Rolls-Royce WR-21 gas turbines and diesel generators; early reliability problems in warm waters led to a Power Improvement Programme fitting additional diesel generators. Type 45s have deployed worldwide, including air-defence tasking in the Gulf and Red Sea, where HMS Diamond shot down drones and a missile in 2023–24.
