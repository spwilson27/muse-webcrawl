Israel Aerospace Industries (IAI) is Israel's largest aerospace and defence manufacturer and a wholly government-owned corporation. Founded in 1953 as Bedek Aviation by Shimon Peres and American engineer Al Schwimmer, it produces military and civil aircraft, unmanned aerial vehicles, satellites, missiles, radar and naval systems, and is Israel's largest industrial exporter of defence equipment.

History

Founded to overhaul Israeli Air Force aircraft, IAI moved into licensed and indigenous production, building the Arava transport, the modified Fouga Magister (Tzukit) trainer, the Nesher and Kfir fighters, and later the cancelled Lavi programme. Its ELTA subsidiary developed the EL/M-2075 Phalcon airborne early warning system. IAI's unmanned aircraft — the Heron, Heron TP (Eitan) and Searcher families — have given Israel a leading export position in UAVs. In space, IAI built the Ofek series of reconnaissance satellites launched by its Shavit rocket, and the Beresheet lunar lander (with SpaceIL) of 2019. Other products include the Arrow anti-ballistic-missile system (with Boeing), the Barak 8 naval air-defence missile (with India), loitering munitions such as the Harop, and passenger-to-freighter aircraft conversions.

Structure and business

IAI is organised into groups covering military aircraft, commercial aviation, missiles and space (MLM), ELTA systems, and engineering. It sells widely abroad, including to India, its largest customer in recent decades, and to customers in Europe, Asia and the Americas.
