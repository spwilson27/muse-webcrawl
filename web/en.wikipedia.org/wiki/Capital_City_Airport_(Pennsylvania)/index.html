Capital City Airport (Pennsylvania)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Capital City Airport (IATA: HAR, ICAO: KCXY, FAA LID: CXY) is a public-use general aviation airport in Fairview Township, York County, Pennsylvania, United States, about five kilometres southwest of Harrisburg, the state capital. It lies on the west shore of the Susquehanna River, directly across from the city.

The airport covers roughly 320 acres and has two runways, the longer about 5,000 feet. It is owned by the Susquehanna Area Regional Airport Authority (SARAA), which also operates Harrisburg International Airport. Capital City serves as the reliever and general aviation airport for the Harrisburg area, hosting corporate and private aircraft, flight training, and the Pennsylvania State Police aviation unit; it was Harrisburg's commercial airport before airline service moved to Harrisburg International (the former Olmsted Air Force Base) in the late 1960s.

History

Capital City Airport opened in 1930 and was an important early airmail and passenger stop; capital-city dignitaries and visiting US presidents passed through it. During the Second World War it supported military traffic associated with nearby installations. Its short runway and constrained site prompted the transfer of scheduled airline operations to Middletown, after which it settled into its general aviation role.
