List of Academy Award for Best Picture winners and nominees

The Academy Award for Best Picture is one of the Academy Awards (Oscars) presented annually by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences since the awards debuted in 1929. It is generally considered the most prestigious of the Oscars: the final award of the night, given to the producers of the year's best motion picture. From 2009 the category has had ten nominees (with a fixed ten since the 94th ceremony).

Records

Ben-Hur (1959), Titanic (1997) and The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (2003) share the record of eleven Oscars won. Parasite (2019) was the first non-English-language film to win Best Picture; The Artist (2011) was the first mostly-silent winner since the inaugural ceremony. At the 89th ceremony (2016) the award was mistakenly announced for La La Land before the actual winner, Moonlight, was confirmed — the "Envelope-gate" incident.
