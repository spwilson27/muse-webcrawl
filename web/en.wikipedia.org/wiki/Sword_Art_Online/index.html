Sword Art Online (Japanese: ソードアート・オンライン) is a Japanese light novel series written by Reki Kawahara and illustrated by abec. It follows Kazuto "Kirito" Kirigaya and Asuna Yuuki as they play through virtual reality massively multiplayer online role-playing games (VRMMORPGs) in the near future. Begun as a web novel in 2002 and published in print from 2009, the series has sold over 30 million copies worldwide and spawned manga, anime, films, video games and a live-action project.

Premise

In 2022, the VRMMORPG Sword Art Online is launched for the NerveGear, a headset that immerses users' senses and is controlled by their brains. On launch day its creator, Akihiko Kayaba, traps the 10,000 logged-in players inside the game: logging out or dying in-game will trigger the NerveGear to destroy the player's brain, and freedom requires clearing all 100 floors of the floating castle Aincrad. Kirito, a former beta tester, becomes a solo "beater" and eventually leads the assault team alongside Asuna, vice-commander of the Knights of the Blood guild; the two fall in love and, two years in, Kirito defeats Kayaba and frees the survivors. Later arcs (Fairy Dance, Phantom Bullet, Alicization, and others) explore successor VR worlds — ALfheim Online, Gun Gale Online, and the military Soul Translator project Underworld — as well as augmented reality in the film Ordinal Scale.

Reception and influence

The 2012 anime adaptation by A-1 Pictures was a major international hit and helped popularise the "trapped in a video game" and broader isekai boom of the 2010s. The franchise has been praised for its action, music (by Yuki Kajiura), and Asuna's prominence, and criticised for pacing and later plot turns. It has generated numerous console and mobile games, extensive merchandising, and real-world collaborations in Japan.
