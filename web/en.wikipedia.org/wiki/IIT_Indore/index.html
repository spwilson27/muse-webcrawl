IIT Indore

Indian Institute of Technology Indore

IIT Indore's permanent campus at Simrol

The Indian Institute of Technology Indore (IIT Indore or IIT-I) is a public technical university in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India. Founded in 2009 as one of the eight new Indian Institutes of Technology established by the Ministry of Education, it is an institute of national importance.

History and campus

The institute initially operated from temporary premises under the mentorship of IIT Bombay, before moving to its permanent 501-acre campus at Simrol, about 25 km from Indore city. It has grown rapidly in research output, frequently ranking among the strongest of the newer IITs in international university rankings.

Academics

IIT Indore offers BTech programmes in branches including computer science, electrical, mechanical, civil and metallurgical engineering, as well as MTech, MSc and PhD programmes. Admission to undergraduate programmes is through JEE Advanced. The institute was notable for early emphasis on interdisciplinary research, including centres in astronomy and space science.
