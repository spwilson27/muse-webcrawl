Gunpla

Gunpla (Japanese: ガンプラ) is the popular name for plastic model kits depicting the vehicles and characters of the Gundam franchise, manufactured by Bandai since 1980. The name is a portmanteau of "Gundam plastic model". Gunpla kits are sold in several grades distinguished by scale, complexity and price.

A completed Gunpla kit

Grades

The principal grades include High Grade (HG, usually 1/144 scale), Real Grade (RG, 1/144 with advanced inner frames), Master Grade (MG, 1/100 with full inner skeletons), Perfect Grade (PG, 1/60 with extreme detail) and Super Deformed (SD) kits of stylised proportions. Entry Grade kits, introduced in 2020, are designed for beginners with simple snap-fit assembly and no tools required.

Popularity

Gunpla is credited with reviving the Gundam franchise after the original television series and with transforming the Japanese plastic model market. Cumulative shipments passed 500 million units by 2019, and Bandai produced its 500 millionth kit around the franchise's 40th anniversary. The hobby supports an active customising culture of painting, weathering and scratch-building, as well as the Gunpla Builders World Cup competition. The anime series Gundam Build Fighters (2013) dramatised the hobby itself.
