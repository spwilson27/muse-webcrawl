Delphic Oracle

Oracle of Delphi
LocationDelphi, Mount Parnassus, Greece
DeityApollo
PriestessThe Pythia

The Delphic Oracle was the priestess called the Pythia at the Temple of Apollo at Delphi, the most prestigious oracle of the ancient Greek world, consulted on colonies, wars and laws for over a thousand years.

Consultation

Seated on a tripod over a chasm in the adyton, the Pythia delivered often ambiguous utterances, interpreted by priests into verse. Famous responses include the "wooden walls" oracle before Salamis and the prophecy to Croesus that crossing a river would destroy a great empire - his own.

Decline

Influence waned under Rome; the oracle was suppressed with pagan temples under Theodosius I in the late 4th century CE. Modern theories link the Pythia's trance to ethylene or other gases rising through fault lines at the site.
