Ezhou Huahu International Airport (IATA: EHU, ICAO: ZHEC) is an airport in Ezhou, Hubei province, China. It is China's first dedicated cargo hub airport, developed with SF Express (SF Airlines) as its anchor operator, whose transshipment hub is at the airport. The airport also handles some passenger flights.

History

The airport was planned from 2016 and construction began in 2019. It opened on 11 July 2022, when SF Airlines began cargo operations; passenger services by airlines including SF-partnered and domestic carriers followed. It has two 3,600-metre runways and a large automated sorting facility designed to handle millions of tonnes of freight annually, rivalling Memphis International Airport as a model for a freight-centric "airport city". The facility serves the Wuhan metropolitan area alongside Wuhan Tianhe International Airport, which focuses on passenger traffic.
