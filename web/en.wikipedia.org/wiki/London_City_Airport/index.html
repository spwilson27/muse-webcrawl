London City Airport (IATA: LCY, ICAO: EGLC) is an international airport in the Royal Docks area of the London Borough of Newham, about 11 km (6.9 mi) east of the City of London. It was developed in the 1980s by the engineering company Mowlem on the site of the King George V Dock, and opened in 1987.

Operations

The airport has a single short runway of 1,508 metres, requiring a steep 5.5-degree approach, and only aircraft types certified for steep approaches and short-field performance may operate there, such as the Embraer E-Jets and the Airbus A220. Its proximity to Canary Wharf and the City makes it popular with business travellers; British Airways' CityFlyer is the largest operator, and the airport is noted for its quick check-in and arrival times.

The airport is connected to central London by the Docklands Light Railway station of the same name.
