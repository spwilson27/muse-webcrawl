Eiji Aonuma

Eiji Aonuma (Japanese: 青沼 英二, born 16 March 1963) is a Japanese video game designer, director, and producer at Nintendo. He is best known as the producer of The Legend of Zelda series, having directed games including Majora's Mask (2000), The Wind Waker (2002), and Twilight Princess (2006), and produced Breath of the Wild (2017) and its sequel Tears of the Kingdom (2023).

Career

Aonuma joined Nintendo in 1988 after graduating from the Tokyo University of the Arts, where he studied design and worked with automated puppets. His early work included design on Mario Open Golf and directing Marvelous: Another Treasure Island (1996). Mentored by Shigeru Miyamoto, he joined the Zelda team as a designer on Ocarina of Time (1998), for which he designed several dungeons, and then directed its follow-up, Majora's Mask, developed in about a year using the earlier game's engine and assets.

Work on Zelda

As producer of the series from the mid-2000s onward, Aonuma sought repeatedly to rethink the series' conventions, an ambition realised in Breath of the Wild, whose open-world design was a major critical and commercial success, winning numerous Game of the Year awards. In 2019 he became deputy general manager of Nintendo's Entertainment Planning & Development division while continuing to oversee the Zelda series. In 2023 he produced Tears of the Kingdom and served as a producer on The Super Mario Bros. Movie.
