John Wayne Airport (IATA: SNA) is the sole commercial airport in Orange County, California, United States. It is located near the cities of Irvine, Newport Beach, and Costa Mesa, and serves the Orange County area and the broader Los Angeles metropolitan region.

History

The airport began as a privately owned airfield in the 1920s and came under county ownership in 1939. It was renamed in 1979 in honour of the actor John Wayne, who lived in Newport Beach. A bronze statue of Wayne stands in the terminal.

Operations

Because it is surrounded by densely populated areas, the airport operates under some of the strictest noise-abatement rules in the United States, requiring steep departure climbs. Its main runway, at about 5,700 feet, is one of the shortest of any major American airport. Annual passenger traffic is capped by a settlement agreement with the surrounding communities.
