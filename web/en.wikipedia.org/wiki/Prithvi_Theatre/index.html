Prithvi Theatre

Location: Juhu, Mumbai, India
Opened: 5 November 1978
Founded by: Shashi Kapoor and Jennifer Kapoor
Capacity: about 200
Named after: Prithviraj Kapoor

Prithvi Theatre is one of Mumbai's best-known theatres, located in the Juhu suburb. It belongs to the Shri Prithviraj Kapoor Memorial Trust & Research Foundation, set up by actor Shashi Kapoor and his wife Jennifer Kapoor to realise the dream of Shashi's father, Prithviraj Kapoor, the legendary stage and film actor whose travelling company, Prithvi Theatres, performed across India from 1944 to 1960.

History

The theatre opened on 5 November 1978 and quickly became the centre of Mumbai's Hindi and English theatre scene, staging plays on most days of the year at deliberately affordable ticket prices. Jennifer Kapoor ran the theatre until her death in 1984; Shashi Kapoor's daughter Sanjna Kapoor led it for over two decades, building its festivals and outreach, before Kunal Kapoor took over its management.

Venue and activities

The intimate, fan-shaped auditorium seats roughly 200 people and is noted for its clear acoustics and steep rake; no seat is far from the stage. The complex includes the Prithvi Café, a celebrated meeting place for artists, and Prithvi House for rehearsals and workshops. The annual Prithvi Theatre Festival, children's theatre programmes (such as "Summertime"), workshops and play readings support new writing in Hindi, English, Marathi and Gujarati theatre.
