Mon Mothma is a fictional character in the Star Wars franchise. A senator from the planet Chandrila, she is depicted as a leader of the Rebel Alliance against the Galactic Empire and later Chancellor of the New Republic. The character first appeared in Return of the Jedi (1983), played by Caroline Blakiston, and Genevieve O'Reilly later portrayed her in Revenge of the Sith (deleted scenes, 2005), Rogue One (2016), and the television series Andor (2022–2025) and Ahsoka.

Character biography

Within the Star Wars narrative, Mon Mothma served in the Galactic Senate during the Clone Wars, opposing Chancellor Palpatine's accumulation of power. As the Republic became the Empire, she covertly organised resistance cells and, in 2 BBY in the in-universe chronology, publicly denounced the Emperor and resigned her seat to lead the newly proclaimed Alliance to Restore the Republic.

After the Empire's defeat at Endor, she became the first Chancellor of the New Republic and pursued demilitarisation and the restoration of the Senate.

Portrayal and reception

In Andor, O'Reilly's expanded portrayal of Mothma's political and family struggles on Coruscant received critical acclaim, deepening a character previously known mainly for the briefing-room scene in Return of the Jedi, in which she delivers the line about the many Bothans who died to obtain the Death Star plans.
