The Infinity Gauntlet is an American comic book storyline published by Marvel Comics, comprising a six-issue limited series written by Jim Starlin with art by George Pérez and Ron Lim, published in 1991, along with tie-in issues. It follows the villain Thanos, who has assembled the six Infinity Gems into a gauntlet granting him omnipotence.

Plot

Seeking to win the affection of Mistress Death, Thanos uses the Gauntlet to erase half of all living beings in the universe with a snap of his fingers. Led by the resurrected Adam Warlock, Earth's surviving heroes and cosmic beings confront Thanos; the gauntlet passes through several hands, including that of Thanos's granddaughter Nebula, before Warlock claims it and undoes the catastrophe. The story was followed by the sequels The Infinity War (1992) and The Infinity Crusade (1993).

Adaptations

The storyline provided the basis for the Marvel Cinematic Universe films Avengers: Infinity War (2018) and Avengers: Endgame (2019), in which Thanos collects the six Infinity Stones.
