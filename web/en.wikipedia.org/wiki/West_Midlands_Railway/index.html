West Midlands Railway (WMR) is a train operating company in the United Kingdom, owned by West Midlands Trains, a consortium of Abellio, JR East and Mitsui & Co. It operates local and regional services in the West Midlands region of England under the West Midlands franchise, which began on 10 December 2017, taking over from London Midland.

Services

West Midlands Railway operates the suburban and regional services around Birmingham, including the Cross City Line between Lichfield and Redditch/Bromsgrove, the Snow Hill lines to Stratford-upon-Avon, Worcester and Kidderminster, services to Coventry, Wolverhampton, Shrewsbury, Telford, Hereford, Nuneaton and Stoke-on-Trent, and the branch lines to Leamington Spa via Kenilworth and to Bedford via the Marston Vale line (the latter operated under the sister brand London Northwestern Railway from Bletchley). Its sister brand London Northwestern Railway, run by the same company, operates the longer-distance services between Birmingham, Liverpool and London Euston.

Rolling stock

The fleet includes Class 150, 153, 170 and 172 diesel multiple units and Class 323 electric multiple units, the latter long associated with the Cross City Line. New Class 196 diesel trains built by CAF and Class 730 electric trains built by Alstom (originally Bombardier) were ordered to replace older stock and expand capacity.

Branding

Unlike most franchises, the West Midlands franchise is specified jointly by the Department for Transport and West Midlands Rail Executive, a partnership of local authorities. West Midlands Railway trains carry an orange and purple livery devised locally, reflecting this devolved arrangement.
