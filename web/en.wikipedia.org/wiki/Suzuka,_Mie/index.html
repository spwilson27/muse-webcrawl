Suzuka, Mie

Suzuka (鈴鹿市, Suzuka-shi) is a city in Mie Prefecture, Japan. It is internationally known as the home of the Suzuka Circuit, a motorsport race track that hosts the Formula One Japanese Grand Prix.

Geography

Suzuka lies in north-eastern Mie Prefecture on the coast of Ise Bay, backed by the Suzuka Mountains along the border with Shiga Prefecture. The Suzuka River flows through the city.

Economy

The city is a major manufacturing centre. Honda operates a large plant in Suzuka, established in 1960, and the city has a long tradition of traditional crafts including Ise-katagami paper stencils and Suzuka sumi ink.

Suzuka Circuit

Opened in 1962 as a Honda test track, the Suzuka International Racing Course is famous for its figure-eight layout and challenging corners such as the 130R and the Degner curves. It has hosted the Japanese Grand Prix since 1987, with some interruptions.
