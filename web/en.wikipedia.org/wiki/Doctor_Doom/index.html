Doctor Doom

Doctor Doom (Dr. Victor von Doom) is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer-editor Stan Lee and artist/co-plotter Jack Kirby, the character first appeared in The Fantastic Four #5 (July 1962). He is the archenemy of the Fantastic Four and is widely regarded as one of the greatest comic book villains of all time.

Fictional character biography

Victor von Doom was born to a Romani healer and a witch in the fictional European nation of Latveria. A scientific prodigy, he won a scholarship to Empire State University in New York, where he met Reed Richards. Blaming Richards for an experiment that scarred his face, Doom forged a suit of powered armour and an iron mask, seized control of Latveria, and ruled it as an absolute monarch. Combining sorcery learned from Tibetan monks with his own technology, Doom has repeatedly sought world domination and godhood, and has clashed with nearly every major Marvel hero.

Portrayals

Doom has been portrayed in film by Julian McMahon in the 2005 and 2007 Fantastic Four films and by Toby Kebbell in the 2015 film. Robert Downey Jr. was announced in 2024 to play Victor von Doom in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.
