Resolute Bay is an Arctic waterway in Nunavut, Canada, on the southern shore of Cornwallis Island, forming part of the Northwest Passage. The Inuit hamlet of Resolute (Inuktitut: Qausuittuq, "place with no dawn") sits on its shore and is one of Canada's northernmost civilian communities.

History

The bay is named after HMS Resolute, one of the ships sent in search of the lost Franklin expedition, which wintered in the area in the 1850s. During the Cold War an airfield and joint military-civilian station were established, and in 1953–55 Inuit families were relocated to Resolute from northern Quebec and Pond Inlet in the controversial High Arctic relocation.

Today

Resolute is a hub for Arctic research, polar logistics, and expeditions to the North Pole, served by Resolute Bay Airport. The Canadian Armed Forces maintain an Arctic training centre there.
