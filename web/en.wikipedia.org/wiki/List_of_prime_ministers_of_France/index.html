List of prime ministers of France

The Prime Minister of France is the head of government of the French Republic, appointed by the President and responsible to the National Assembly. The office in its modern form dates from the Fifth Republic (1958), whose first prime minister was Michel Debré. Under the Third and Fourth Republics the equivalent post was styled President of the Council of Ministers (Président du Conseil des ministres), and the office is generally traced to 1815, when the Duke of Richelieu formed a government under the restored monarchy.

Periods when president and prime minister come from opposing parties are known as "cohabitation" (1986–1988, 1993–1995, 1997–2002). The prime minister resides at the Hôtel Matignon in Paris. The youngest appointee of the Fifth Republic was Gabriel Attal, named at 34, while Michel Barnier, appointed at 73, was the oldest.
