MAVEN

MAVEN (Mars Atmosphere and Volatile Evolution) is a NASA Mars orbiter mission launched in 2013 to study the upper atmosphere of Mars and determine how the planet lost most of its early atmosphere — and with it, the warm climate that once allowed liquid water to flow on its surface. MAVEN is the second mission of NASA's Mars Scout programme and is led by principal investigator Bruce Jakosky of the University of Colorado Boulder.

Objectives

MAVEN was designed to measure the composition and structure of Mars's upper atmosphere and ionosphere, characterise how the solar wind and solar storms strip atmospheric gases to space, and quantify the present-day escape rate and its variation with solar conditions. These measurements allow scientists to extrapolate backwards and reconstruct the history of the Martian climate and the planet's loss of water.

Spacecraft and instruments

The spacecraft carries eight instruments: the Solar Wind Electron Analyzer (SWEA), Solar Wind Ion Analyzer (SWIA), SupraThermal And Thermal Ion Composition (STATIC), Solar Energetic Particle detector (SEP), Langmuir Probe and Waves (LPW), a magnetometer (MAG), the Imaging Ultraviolet Spectrograph (IUVS), and the Neutral Gas and Ion Mass Spectrometer (NGIMS). Periodically, MAVEN executes "deep-dip" campaigns, lowering its periapsis to about 125 km to sample the upper atmosphere directly. Its distinctive gull-wing solar arrays span over 11 metres.

Findings

MAVEN results confirmed that sputtering and pickup by the solar wind have removed a large fraction of the early Martian atmosphere to space, with escape rates increasing sharply during solar storms. The mission observed a diffuse global aurora in December 2014 and discrete auroras mapped by IUVS, discovered a layer of metal ions in the ionosphere deposited by comet Siding Spring's close pass in October 2014, and found a twisted, comet-like magnetotail resulting from the interaction of the solar wind with Mars's induced magnetosphere. In addition to its science, MAVEN serves as a communications relay for NASA's Mars surface missions.
