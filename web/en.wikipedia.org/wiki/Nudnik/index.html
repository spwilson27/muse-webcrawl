Nudnik

Nudnik is an animated cartoon character created by American animator Gene Deitch, and the name of the series of theatrical shorts featuring him, produced in the mid-1960s. The word "nudnik" comes from Yiddish, meaning a persistently annoying or pestering person, a bore or a nag.

The series

The Nudnik shorts were produced by Rembrandt Films and directed by Gene Deitch, who had developed the character while working in Prague. The title character is a hapless, well-meaning little man whose simplest activities spiral into comic disaster: everything he touches goes wrong, and the world seems determined to frustrate him. The films are largely pantomime, relying on visual gags and timing rather than dialogue.

The first short, Here's Nudnik (1965), was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Animated Short Film. Deitch later revived the character in the 1990s in a series of short cartoons and comic panels published on the web.
