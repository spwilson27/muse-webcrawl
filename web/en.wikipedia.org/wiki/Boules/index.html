Boules

Boules is a collective name for ball games in which heavy balls are thrown or rolled as close as possible to a smaller target ball. The family includes the French game pétanque, Provençal jeu provençal, Italian bocce, and British crown green and flat green bowls. The name derives from the French word for "ball".

Pétanque

In pétanque, players throw hollow metal boules toward a small wooden jack (cochonnet) while standing with both feet inside a drawn circle. The game originated in La Ciotat, Provence, in 1907, when a player suffering from rheumatism devised a shorter version of jeu provençal that did not require a running throw. It is played recreationally across France and competitively worldwide under an international federation.

Bocce

Bocce is played with larger balls on a long, groomed court, with an underhand throw or roll and the option of knocking opponents' balls away. It descends from games played in Roman times and is especially popular in Italy and in the Italian diaspora.

Technique and rules

Common tactics include pointing, placing a ball near the jack, and shooting, striking an opponent's ball to displace it. Points are scored by the team whose balls lie closest to the jack once all balls are played.
