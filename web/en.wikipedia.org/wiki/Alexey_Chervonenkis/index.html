Alexey Yakovlevich Chervonenkis (7 September 1938 – 22 September 2014) was a Russian mathematician and one of the founders of statistical learning theory. Together with Vladimir Vapnik he developed the Vapnik–Chervonenkis (VC) theory, a foundational framework in machine learning.

Work

VC theory provides conditions for the uniform convergence of empirical frequencies to probabilities and introduced the concept of VC dimension, a measure of the capacity of a set of functions that underpins the analysis of learning algorithms, including support vector machines. Chervonenkis spent much of his career at the Institute of Control Sciences in Moscow and later also worked in London. He died in 2014 after becoming lost while walking in Losiny Ostrov National Park near Moscow.
