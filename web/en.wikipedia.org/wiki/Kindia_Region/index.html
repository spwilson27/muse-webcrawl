Kindia Region

CountryGuinea
CapitalKindia
PrefecturesCoyah, Dubréka, Forécariah, Kindia, Télimélé

Kindia Region (French: Région de Kindia) is one of the eight regions of Guinea, in the west of the country adjoining the capital region of Conakry. Its capital is the city of Kindia.

Geography

The region spans coastal plains and the foothills of the Fouta Djallon highlands. It is an important agricultural area, producing fruit including bananas and pineapples, and contains part of Guinea's bauxite-mining belt.
