Medroxyprogesterone acetate

Medroxyprogesterone acetate (MPA) is a synthetic progestin medication used in hormone therapy, contraception and the treatment of gynaecological disorders. It is sold under brand names including Provera (oral) and Depo-Provera (a long-acting injection).

Uses

Orally, MPA is used in menopausal hormone therapy to protect the endometrium, and to treat amenorrhoea, abnormal uterine bleeding and endometriosis. As Depo-Provera, a 150 mg intramuscular injection every three months, it is a widely used contraceptive. High-dose MPA has also been used in hormone therapy for some cancers.

Side effects

Common effects include menstrual changes, weight gain and headache. Long-term injectable use is associated with reversible loss of bone mineral density, which prompted regulatory warnings. MPA is a derivative of progesterone with some glucocorticoid and androgenic activity.
