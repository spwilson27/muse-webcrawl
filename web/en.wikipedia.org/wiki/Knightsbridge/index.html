Knightsbridge is a residential and retail district in central London, on the south side of Hyde Park, divided between the City of Westminster and the Royal Borough of Kensington and Chelsea. It is one of the most affluent districts in the world and is identified in the London Plan as one of the capital's two international retail centres, alongside the West End.

History

The name derives from a bridge over the River Westbourne where, according to legend, two knights fought a duel. Long a village on the road west from London, the area was notorious in the 18th century for highwaymen before being developed in the 19th century into a fashionable neighbourhood of stucco terraces and garden squares such as those around Montpelier Square and Ennismore Gardens.

Shopping and landmarks

Knightsbridge is dominated by the department stores Harrods in Brompton Road — with its famous food halls and green terracotta facade — and Harvey Nichols on the corner of Sloane Street, the heart of London's luxury fashion retailing. Nearby landmarks include One Hyde Park, the ultra-prime residential development; the Mandarin Oriental Hyde Park hotel; the Bulgarian, French, and other embassies in the district; and the barracks of the Household Cavalry at Hyde Park Barracks, overlooked by its distinctive residential tower. Property prices in the area are among the highest in the world, and the district is home to many of London's wealthiest residents.
