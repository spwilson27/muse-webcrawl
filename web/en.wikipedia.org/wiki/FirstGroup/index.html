FirstGroup

FirstGroup plc is a British multi-national transport group, based in Aberdeen, Scotland. The company operates transport services in the United Kingdom and Ireland. It was founded in 1986 as the Grampian Regional Transport company and expanded through bus deregulation and rail privatisation to become, for a period, the largest transport operator in the UK, and also a major operator of school buses and transit services in North America before divesting those businesses in 2021–2022.

History

FirstGroup began in 1986 as the municipal bus operator of the Grampian region, bought out by its management in 1989. Renamed FirstBus and then FirstGroup, it grew by acquiring privatised bus companies across Britain and entered rail in 1996, later holding franchises including First Great Western, First ScotRail, First TransPennine Express and First Capital Connect. In 2007 it bought the US company Laidlaw, gaining Greyhound Lines and First Student school buses; these North American operations were sold to EQT Infrastructure in 2021, and Greyhound was resold to FlixBus the same year, refocusing the group on the UK and Ireland.

Operations

First Bus is one of the "big five" UK bus operators, running around a fifth of local bus services in Great Britain. First Rail operates or has operated train companies including Great Western Railway, South Western Railway, TransPennine Express, Avanti West Coast (in partnership with Trenitalia) and the open-access operators Hull Trains and Lumo.
