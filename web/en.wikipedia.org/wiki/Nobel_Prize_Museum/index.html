Nobel Prize Museum

The Nobel Prize Museum (Swedish: Nobelprismuseet) is a museum in Stockholm, Sweden, devoted to the Nobel Prize, its founder Alfred Nobel, and the prize laureates. It opened in 2001, the centenary year of the Nobel Prizes.

Location and exhibits

The museum occupies the former stock exchange building (Börshuset) on Stortorget square in Gamla stan, Stockholm's old town — the same building that houses the Swedish Academy and the Nobel Library. Its exhibitions present the lives and work of laureates through artifacts, films and interactive displays, and it displays objects donated by laureates alongside a replica of Nobel's will.

The museum hosts temporary exhibitions, educational programmes for schools, lectures and events connected with each year's Nobel Prize announcements and award ceremonies on 10 December.
