The 2003 California gubernatorial recall election was a special election held on 7 October 2003 to decide whether Governor Gray Davis should be recalled from office, and if so, who would replace him. It was the first gubernatorial recall in California's history to reach the ballot, and only the second in United States history, after North Dakota in 1921.

Voters answered two questions: whether Davis should be recalled, and which candidate should succeed him if the recall passed. A recall campaign funded in part by Congressman Darrell Issa gathered the necessary signatures amid public anger over a state budget deficit, an energy crisis, and a tripling of the vehicle registration fee.

Result

The recall passed with 55.4 percent in favour, and actor Arnold Schwarzenegger won the replacement vote with 48.6 percent, ahead of Lieutenant Governor Cruz Bustamante. Schwarzenegger was sworn in as the 38th Governor of California on 17 November 2003 and served until 2011.
