Eastern Wu

Wu (222-280), commonly known as Dong Wu (Eastern Wu) or Sun Wu, was a dynastic state of China and one of the three major states that competed for supremacy during the Three Kingdoms period (220-280). It was founded by Sun Quan, who declared himself emperor in 229.

Eastern Wu
Capital: Jianye (Nanjing)
Founded: 229 (imperial claim)
Founder: Sun Quan
Fell: 280, conquered by Jin

History

Sun Quan ruled the southeast from the lower Yangtze, allied with Shu Han against Cao Wei after the Battle of Red Cliffs (208). Wu's navy dominated the Yangtze and its fleets reached Liaodong and possibly Taiwan (Yizhou).

Fall

After Sun Quan's death in 252, succession struggles weakened Wu. The Western Jin dynasty conquered it in 280 under Emperor Wu, reuniting China, in a campaign memorialised by Wang Jun's naval advance down the Yangtze.
