Districts of Turkey

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia


The provinces of Turkey are divided into districts

The 81 provinces of Turkey (il) are divided into a total of 973 districts (Turkish: ilce; pronounced [iltse]). In the Ottoman period and in the early years of the Turkish Republic, the corresponding unit was the kaza.

Administration

Each district is administered by a district governor (kaymakam), appointed by the Ministry of the Interior, who oversees the local branches of central government agencies. Districts also contain municipalities headed by elected mayors (belediye baskani) responsible for local services. One district in each province serves as the capital district, sharing its name with the province.

Under the metropolitan municipality system, expanded by Law No. 6360 in 2012, provinces with a population above 750,000 became metropolitan municipalities whose boundaries coincide with the provincial boundary, and their districts function as second-tier municipalities.
