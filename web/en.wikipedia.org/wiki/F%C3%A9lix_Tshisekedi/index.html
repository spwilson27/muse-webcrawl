Félix Tshisekedi

Félix Antoine Tshisekedi Tshilombo (born 13 June 1963) is a Congolese politician who has served as the President of the Democratic Republic of the Congo since 24 January 2019. He is the son of Étienne Tshisekedi, a long-time opposition leader and founder of the Union for Democracy and Social Progress (UDPS).

Presidency

Tshisekedi was declared the winner of the December 2018 general election, succeeding Joseph Kabila in the country's first nominally peaceful transfer of power, though the result was disputed by rival candidate Martin Fayulu and questioned by observers. He was re-elected in the December 2023 election. His presidency has been dominated by insecurity in the eastern provinces, particularly the conflict involving the M23 rebel group, and by efforts to renegotiate mining contracts.
