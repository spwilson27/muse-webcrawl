Tony Award for Best Actress in a Musical

The Tony Award for Best Performance by a Leading Actress in a Musical is an annual award presented by the American Theatre Wing and The Broadway League honouring the best leading female performance in a Broadway musical. It was first presented in 1948, when Grace Hartman won for Angel in the Wings; the acting categories were divided by genre (play vs. musical) from that year.

Notable winners

Mary Martin won the award three times (South Pacific, Peter Pan, The Sound of Music). Other celebrated recipients include Ethel Merman (Call Me Madam), Angela Lansbury (four wins in the category, including Mame and Sweeney Todd), Julie Andrews's contemporary rivals, Chita Rivera, Bernadette Peters, Audra McDonald, Sutton Foster and Patti LuPone. Angela Lansbury holds the record for most wins in this category.

Eligibility

Performances must be in a production officially opened on Broadway during the eligibility season, with the leading/featured distinction determined by billing and the Tony Administration Committee. Winners are chosen by a body of theatre professionals and journalists voting in secret ballot administered by an accounting firm.
