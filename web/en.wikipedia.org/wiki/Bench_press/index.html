Bench press

The bench press is a compound upper-body strength training exercise in which the trainee presses a weight upwards from a supine position, lying on a weight bench. It is one of the three lifts in powerlifting, alongside the squat and deadlift.

Movement

The lifter lies on a flat bench, grasps a barbell with a grip wider than shoulder width, unracks it, lowers it to the chest, pauses (in competition), and presses it up until the arms are extended. Primary muscles are the pectoralis major, anterior deltoids, and triceps brachii.

Variations

  • Incline and decline bench press
  • Close-grip bench press (triceps emphasis)
  • Dumbbell bench press and floor press
  • Equipped bench press using a bench shirt

Safety

A spotter or safety arms are recommended for heavy attempts; failure under a loaded bar can cause serious injury.

Records

Equipped and raw world records exceed 300 kg, with equipped lifts surpassing 600 kg in specialised federations.
