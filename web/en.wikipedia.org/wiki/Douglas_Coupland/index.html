Douglas Coupland OC OBC (born 30 December 1961) is a Canadian novelist, designer and visual artist. His first novel, Generation X: Tales for an Accelerated Culture (1991), popularised the term "Generation X" and the word "McJob", and his work is noted for its engagement with mass media, popular culture and technology.


Douglas Coupland, author of Generation X.

Fiction

Coupland's novels include Shampoo Planet (1992), Microserfs (1995), set among Microsoft employees, Girlfriend in a Coma (1998), Hey Nostradamus! (2003), jPod (2006) and Generation A (2009). His short non-fiction includes Life After God and a biography of Marshall McLuhan (2009).

Art and design

Trained at Emily Carr College of Art and Design in Vancouver, Coupland has exhibited widely, with a major retrospective at the Vancouver Art Gallery in 2014. His public artworks include the Digital Orca in Vancouver. He was made an Officer of the Order of Canada in 2013.
