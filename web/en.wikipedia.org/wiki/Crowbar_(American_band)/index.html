Crowbar is an American sludge metal band from New Orleans, Louisiana, formed in 1990. The band is fronted by Kirk Windstein, its sole constant member, and is considered, along with bands such as Eyehategod, Acid Bath, and Down, a pioneering act of the New Orleans sludge metal scene — a style blending the slow tempos and heavy riffing of doom metal with the aggression of hardcore punk.

Crowbar's slow, down-tuned, massively heavy sound — sometimes tagged "sludge doom core" — and Windstein's themes of endurance and self-reliance have influenced generations of heavy bands. The band gained wider exposure in the 1990s through rotation on MTV's Headbangers Ball and Beavis and Butt-Head (the video for "Existence Is Punishment"), and through Windstein's parallel membership in the supergroup Down with Phil Anselmo.

History

The band began in 1989 as The Slugs, adopting the name Crowbar in 1990. Their debut, Obedience Thru Suffering (1991), was followed by the self-titled Crowbar (1993), produced by Phil Anselmo, which included the enduring song "Planets Collide" in live sets for decades and tracks such as "All I Had (I Gave)." Subsequent albums — Time Heals Nothing (1995), Broken Glass (1996), Odd Fellows Rest (1998), and Equilibrium (2000) — consolidated their reputation, though commercial success remained modest and the band repeatedly weathered record-label and lineup turbulence.

Windstein folded Crowbar in the mid-2000s to concentrate on Down and on Kingdom of Sorrow (with Jamey Jasta), then relaunched the band. A second productive era followed with Sever the Wicked Hand (2011), Symmetry in Black (2014), The Serpent Only Lies (2016), and Zero and Below (2022), accompanied by extensive touring that finally matched the band's influence with durable career success.

Musical style and influence

Crowbar's sound rests on extremely down-tuned guitars, slow-to-mid tempos, crushing production, and Windstein's hoarse but melodic vocals. Influences include Black Sabbath, Saint Vitus, the Melvins, Carnivore, and hardcore punk. Metallica, among many bands, have cited Crowbar as an influence, and Kirk Windstein is widely regarded as one of the defining riff writers of sludge metal. Their lyrics frequently treat perseverance, addiction, and recovery through a direct, aphoristic style: "Planets collide" and "I can't turn away" among quoted lines.

Members

Kirk Windstein — lead vocals, rhythm guitar (all albums). The lineup has rotated frequently; notable long-serving members include drummer Tommy Buckley (since 2005), bassist Todd "Sexy T" Strange (an early member who returned for a period in the 2010s), guitarist Matt Brunson, and earlier members Jimmy Bower (drums, also of Eyehategod and Down), Kevin Noonan, Craig Nunenmacher, and Sammy Duet (later of Acid Bath and Goatwhore).
