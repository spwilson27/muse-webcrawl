Mister Fantastic

Mister Fantastic (Reed Richards) is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby, he first appeared in The Fantastic Four #1 (November 1961) as the leader of the Fantastic Four.

Fictional biography

Reed Richards is a scientific genius who gained the ability to stretch and reshape his body after exposure to cosmic rays during a space flight with Susan Storm, Johnny Storm, and Ben Grimm. He is married to Susan Storm, the Invisible Woman, and is father of Franklin and Valeria Richards.

Powers and abilities

Richards possesses extreme elasticity and malleability, and is regarded as one of the most intelligent characters in the Marvel Universe, a master of engineering, physics, and numerous other sciences.
