A municipality (Georgian: მუნიციპალიტეტი, munitsipaliteti) is a local self-government unit in Georgia. Under the Local Self-Government Code adopted in 2014, the country is divided into municipalities plus five self-governing cities: Tbilisi, Kutaisi, Batumi, Rustavi and Poti. Each municipality is governed by an elected council (sakrebulo) and a mayor.
