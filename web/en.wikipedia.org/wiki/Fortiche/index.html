Fortiche

Fortiche Production is a French animation studio based in Paris, with additional studios in Montpellier and Las Palmas, Spain. Founded in 2009 by Pascal Charrue, Arnaud Delord and Jérôme Combe, the studio is best known for producing the Netflix animated series Arcane (2021–2024), set in Riot Games' League of Legends universe, which won widespread critical acclaim and multiple Emmy and Annie Awards for its distinctive painterly 2D/3D hybrid visual style.

History

Fortiche built its reputation through music videos and video game cinematics, including work for Gorillaz and the League of Legends music video "Get Jinxed" (2013) and the K/DA "POP/STARS" video (2018), as well as the Ekko cinematic "Seconds". Its long collaboration with Riot Games led to Arcane, co-created by Christian Linke and Alex Yee, which premiered on Netflix in November 2021. In 2022 Riot Games announced a significant equity investment in Fortiche, deepening the partnership. Following Arcane's second and final season in November 2024, Fortiche announced its first original feature film project, Penelope of Sparta, a science-fiction retelling of the Odyssey myth.

Style

Fortiche's visual signature mixes 3D character animation and camera work with hand-painted 2D backgrounds, textures and effects such as smoke and explosions, producing a painterly, graphic-novel look. The studio's approach eschews motion capture, relying on keyframed character animation informed by hand-drawn reference.
