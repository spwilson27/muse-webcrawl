Johannes Burgers

Johannes Martinus Burgers (1895–1981) was a Dutch physicist known for his work in fluid mechanics, including the Burgers equation, a fundamental model equation exhibiting convection, diffusion and shock formation.

Career

Burgers studied at Leiden under Paul Ehrenfest, receiving his doctorate in 1918. He became professor at Delft University of Technology, where he helped build Dutch aeronautical research, and in 1955 moved to the University of Maryland, where he worked at the Institute for Fluid Dynamics and Applied Mathematics.

Contributions

Beyond the Burgers equation, he contributed to dislocation theory in crystals (the Burgers vector describes a dislocation's magnitude and direction), magnetohydrodynamics and aerodynamics. His brother Wilhelm Gerard Burgers was also a noted physicist.
