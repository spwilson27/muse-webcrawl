International Bobsleigh and Skeleton Federation

The International Bobsleigh and Skeleton Federation (IBSF) is the international federation for bobsleigh and skeleton. Founded in 1923 in Paris as the Fédération Internationale de Bobsleigh et de Tobogganing (FIBT), it adopted its current name in 2015. Its headquarters are in Lausanne, Switzerland.

Role

The IBSF governs the sports of bobsleigh and skeleton worldwide, sets competition and equipment rules, sanctions tracks, and organizes the World Championships, the World Cup series, and continental and junior championships. It works with the International Olympic Committee on the Olympic bobsleigh and skeleton events.

History

Bobsleigh entered the Winter Olympics at the first Games in Chamonix in 1924. Skeleton, governed by the FIBT from 1926, appeared in 1928 and 1948 before returning permanently in 2002. The federation's member associations cover more than 60 nations.

Competitions

Flagship events include the IBSF World Championships, held annually except in Olympic years, and the Bobsleigh World Cup and Skeleton World Cup, series of races at tracks in Europe, North America, and Asia.
