The Arri Alexa is a digital motion picture camera system developed by the German manufacturer Arri, first introduced in 2010. Renowned for its film-like image, wide dynamic range and colour science, the Alexa became the dominant camera system in high-end film and television production during the 2010s, used on numerous Academy Award-winning productions.


An Alexa camera rigged for production

History

The original Alexa, shipping in mid-2010, recorded in HD and 2K to SxS cards in ProRes or uncompressed ARRIRAW formats. The range expanded with the Alexa Plus, Studio and M models, then the compact Alexa Mini (2015), which became ubiquitous on gimbals and drones. In 2018 Arri introduced the Alexa LF, with a large-format sensor slightly larger than full frame, followed by the Mini LF (2019). The Alexa 35 (2022) returned to Super 35 with a new sensor offering about 17 stops of dynamic range.

Image characteristics

The Alexa's ALEV sensor family uses dual-gain readout to combine two exposures per frame, yielding highlight roll-off and skin-tone rendering widely praised by cinematographers. The system records in the wide-gamut ARRI colour spaces and supports on-set look management.

Use in production

Alexa cameras shot films including Skyfall (2012), Gravity (2013), The Revenant (2015) and 1917 (2019), and a majority of prestige television drama of the period. Successive surveys of top-grossing films showed the Alexa family as the most-used camera system in professional production.
