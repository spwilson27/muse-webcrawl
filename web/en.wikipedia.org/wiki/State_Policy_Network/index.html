State Policy Network

The State Policy Network (SPN) is an American nonprofit organisation that serves as an umbrella network for state-level free-market think tanks in the United States. Founded in 1992, it is headquartered in Arlington, Virginia.

Membership and activities

SPN has more than 60 member think tanks operating in all 50 states, plus affiliate organisations. It provides member groups with training, grants, communications support, and coordination on policy areas including taxation, education choice, labour regulation, and energy. The network's members typically advocate lower taxes, limited government, and free enterprise, and many are aligned with conservative and libertarian causes.

Funding and criticism

SPN and its members receive funding from foundations, corporations, and individual donors. Critics describe the network as a vehicle for coordinated, donor-driven advocacy; supporters describe it as federalism in action.
