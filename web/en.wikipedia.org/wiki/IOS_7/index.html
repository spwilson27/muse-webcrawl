iOS 7

iOS 7 is the seventh major release of Apple's iOS mobile operating system, unveiled at the company's Worldwide Developers Conference on 10 June 2013 and released on 18 September 2013. It succeeded iOS 6 and was itself succeeded by iOS 8 in 2014. iOS 7 is remembered chiefly for its comprehensive visual redesign, led by Jony Ive, which replaced the skeuomorphic look of earlier versions with a flat, brightly coloured interface.

New features

Beyond the redesign, iOS 7 introduced Control Center, a swipe-up panel of quick settings; a redesigned Notification Center and multitasking view; AirDrop for sharing files between nearby Apple devices; iTunes Radio; the Camera app's new filters and photo organisation in Moments; and automatic app updates. Activation Lock tied a lost or stolen device to the owner's Apple ID, deterring theft. Adoption was exceptionally fast: Apple reported iOS 7 on the majority of devices within days of release.

Reception

Reviews praised the redesign's modernity and many functional additions, while criticising some icon designs, parallax effects that some users found nauseating, and early stability. The design language of iOS 7 — translucency, thin typography and flat iconography — defined Apple's software appearance for the following decade.
