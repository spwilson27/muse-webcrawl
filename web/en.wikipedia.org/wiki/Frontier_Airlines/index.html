Frontier Airlines

A Frontier Airbus with the airline's signature wildlife tail livery.

Frontier Airlines is a major American ultra-low-cost airline headquartered in Denver, Colorado. Founded in 1994 — reusing the name of an earlier Denver carrier that operated from 1950 to 1986 — it operates scheduled services across the United States and to international destinations in Mexico, the Caribbean and Central America. It is known for very low base fares with fees for most extras, and for the distinctive photographs of North American wildlife painted on its aircraft tails.

History

Frontier began flying in July 1994, filling part of the gap left at Denver by the collapse of the original Frontier and by Continental's hub withdrawal. It went through Chapter 11 bankruptcy in 2008 and was acquired by Republic Airways Holdings in 2009, then sold in 2013 to Indigo Partners, the private equity firm associated with ultra-low-cost carriers. Under Indigo, Frontier converted fully to the ultra-low-cost model, unbundling fares and expanding aggressively to leisure markets including Orlando, Las Vegas, Phoenix and Tampa. A proposed merger with Spirit Airlines was announced in 2022 but terminated later that year after Spirit's shareholders rejected it.

Fleet and model

The airline operates an all-Airbus A320 family fleet — A320s, A321s and fuel-efficient A320neos/A321neos — among the youngest and most fuel-efficient fleets in the United States, with high-density seating. Its "Low Fares Done Right" branding emphasises à la carte pricing: passengers pay separately for seat selection, carry-on and checked bags and refreshments, though bundled fare options are also sold.
