Drive-By Truckers

Origin Athens, Georgia, U.S.
Genres Southern rock, alternative country
Years active 1996–present
Key members Patterson Hood, Mike Cooley

Drive-By Truckers are an American Southern rock/alternative country band based in Athens, Georgia, formed in 1996 by Patterson Hood (son of Muscle Shoals bassist David Hood) and Mike Cooley. The band is noted for literate, story-driven songwriting examining the history, myths and contradictions of the American South — what Hood calls "the duality of the Southern thing".

Career

Their breakthrough was the double album Southern Rock Opera (2001), which interweaves the story of Lynyrd Skynyrd with Hood's own Alabama upbringing. Subsequent albums including Decoration Day (2003), The Dirty South (2004), Brighter Than Creation's Dark (2008) and American Band (2016) earned sustained critical acclaim. Singer-guitarist Jason Isbell was a member from 2001 to 2007 before leaving for a successful solo career; bassist Shonna Tucker and pedal-steel player John Neff are among the notable alumni. The long-running line-up of Hood, Cooley, Brad Morgan, Jay Gonzalez and Matt Patton continues to tour and record.
