List of Capsicum cultivars

This is a list of Capsicum cultivars: cultivated varieties of chili peppers and sweet peppers in the genus Capsicum. The cultivated peppers belong mainly to five domesticated species: Capsicum annuum, C. baccatum, C. chinense, C. frutescens, and C. pubescens. Cultivars vary enormously in fruit size, shape, color, flavor, and pungency, the latter measured in Scoville heat units (SHU).

Organization of the list

The full list is organized by species, with cultivars grouped and described by origin, fruit characteristics, and culinary use. Sweet cultivars such as bell peppers and banana peppers appear alongside hot types within C. annuum, the most widely grown species.
