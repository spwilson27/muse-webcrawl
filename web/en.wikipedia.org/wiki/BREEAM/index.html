BREEAM

BREEAM (Building Research Establishment Environmental Assessment Method) is a sustainability assessment method for buildings, masterplanning projects and infrastructure. First launched in 1990 by the Building Research Establishment (BRE) in the United Kingdom, it is the world's longest established method of assessing, rating and certifying the environmental performance of buildings, and has been used in more than 80 countries.

Assessment

Licensed assessors score a project against credits in categories such as management, health and wellbeing, energy, transport, water, materials, waste, land use and ecology, and pollution. Scores are weighted and combined into a percentage, which maps to a rating from Pass (≥30%) through Good, Very Good and Excellent to Outstanding (≥85%). Minimum standards apply in key categories at the higher ratings.

Schemes and influence

BREEAM schemes cover new construction, refurbishment and fit-out, buildings in use, communities and civil engineering infrastructure. Adapted national versions operate in countries including the Netherlands, Norway, Spain, Sweden and Germany, and a USA scheme was launched in 2016. BREEAM predates and influenced other green building certification systems such as LEED and Green Star.
