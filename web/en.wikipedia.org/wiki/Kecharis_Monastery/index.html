Kecharis Monastery

Kecharis Monastery is a medieval Armenian monastic complex in the ski resort town of Tsaghkadzor, in Kotayk Province, Armenia. Founded in the 11th century and largely built in the 12th and 13th centuries, it was a major centre of religion and learning.

Churches

The complex comprises four churches, including Saint Gregory the Illuminator (1003), Saint Nshan, Katoghike, and the small Chapel of the Holy Resurrection, along with two gavits and an ancient cemetery with carved khachkars.

History

Kecharis flourished under the Pahlavuni princes in the 12th and 13th centuries before being damaged in invasions and earthquakes. It was restored in the late 20th and early 21st centuries and functions again as a monastery.
