Whats Opera Doc

What's Opera, Doc? is a 1957 American animated cartoon short in the Merrie Melodies series, directed by Chuck Jones and written by Michael Maltese. It stars Bugs Bunny and Elmer Fudd in a parody of the operas of Richard Wagner, most notably Der Ring des Nibelungen. It is widely regarded as one of the greatest cartoons ever made.

Plot

Elmer Fudd, cast as the heroic Siegfried, pursues Bugs Bunny through a Wagnerian landscape accompanied by music adapted from Die Walküre, Siegfried, Götterdämmerung, Tannhäuser and Der fliegende Holländer. Bugs appears in drag as the Valkyrie Brünnhilde, and the two perform the famous duet "Return My Love" to the tune of the "Ride of the Valkyries". Elmer finally "slays" Brünnhilde with his spear, only for her to die in his arms in a parody of operatic death scenes — before Bugs breaks character and delivers the punchline: "Well, what did you expect in an opera? A happy ending?"

Production and legacy

The short's elaborate, stylised backgrounds by Maurice Noble and the full orchestral score, arranged by Milt Franklyn, were unusually ambitious for a seven-minute cartoon. The production took far longer than a typical short. In 1992, What's Opera, Doc? was the first cartoon short selected for preservation in the United States National Film Registry, and it regularly tops polls of the greatest animated cartoons of all time.
