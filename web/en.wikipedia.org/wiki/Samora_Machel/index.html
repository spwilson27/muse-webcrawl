Overview

Samora Moisés Machel (1933–1986) was a Mozambican military commander and revolutionary who led the independence movement FRELIMO after the assassination of Eduardo Mondlane, becoming the first President of independent Mozambique in 1975. A former nurse, he built FRELIMO's army during the war against Portugal (1964–74) and, after the Carnation Revolution in Lisbon, took power in a one-party Marxist-Leninist state.

Presidency and death

Machel's Mozambique faced economic collapse as Portuguese settlers fled, and a devastating civil war with RENAMO, an insurgency created by Rhodesian intelligence and later backed by apartheid South Africa. His 1984 Nkomati Accord with Pretoria failed to end the war. On 19 October 1986 his Soviet-crewed Tupolev jet crashed at Mbuzini inside South Africa, killing him and 34 others (Graça Machel survived — she later married Nelson Mandela); apartheid-era South African involvement has long been alleged, and investigations (the Margo Commission, and later TRC-era inquiries) never produced a universally accepted verdict.
