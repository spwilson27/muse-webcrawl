3rd SS Panzer Division Totenkopf

The 3rd SS Panzer Division "Totenkopf" was one of 38 divisions of the Waffen-SS of Nazi Germany during World War II. Formed in 1939 largely from personnel of the SS-Totenkopfverbände concentration camp guard units, it fought primarily on the Eastern Front.

History

Commanded initially by Theodor Eicke, the division took part in the invasion of France (1940), where its troops committed the Le Paradis massacre, and in Operation Barbarossa (1941). It fought in the Demyansk Pocket, at Kharkov, at Kursk, and in the retreats through Romania, Poland and Hungary, surrendering to American forces in Austria in May 1945.

War crimes

The division was declared a criminal organization at Nuremberg as part of the SS; its members were implicated in massacres of prisoners and civilians and in the administration of the concentration camp system from which its founding cadre was drawn.
