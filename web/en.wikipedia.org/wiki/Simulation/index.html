Simulation

A simulation is an imitative representation of the operation of a process or system over time. Simulations are used in many contexts, including modelling of natural and human systems for scientific study, engineering design and safety testing, training and education, and entertainment such as video games.

Types

Simulations may be physical (such as a wind tunnel or flight simulator), computer-based, or a combination. Computer simulations rely on mathematical models and range from discrete-event simulation and Monte Carlo methods to continuous system dynamics. Live simulation involves real people operating real equipment in simulated conditions, as in military exercises.

Applications

Flight and driving simulators train operators safely; medical simulations train clinicians; climate models, traffic models and business simulations support planning and research. Key concerns in any simulation are the validity of the model and the fidelity with which it represents the real system.
