Match Group, Inc. is an American internet and technology company headquartered in Dallas, Texas, that operates the world's largest portfolio of online dating services. Its brands include Tinder, Match.com, Hinge, OkCupid, Plenty of Fish, Meetic, and others, offered in more than 40 languages in over 190 countries.

History

Match Group originated as the dating division of IAC/InterActiveCorp. Match.com, one of the earliest online dating sites, was launched in 1995 and acquired by IAC in 1999. Over the following two decades IAC acquired numerous competitors, including OkCupid (2011) and Plenty of Fish (2015, for $575 million). The division acquired a majority stake in Tinder in 2017 and full ownership of Hinge in 2019.

In 2015 the dating businesses were grouped under the Match Group name and taken public on the Nasdaq under the ticker MTCH, with IAC retaining control. On June 30, 2020, IAC completed a full separation, distributing its Match Group shares to IAC shareholders and making Match Group a fully independent public company.

Business model

Match Group's services are generally free to download and use, with revenue generated through premium subscriptions and à la carte features such as boosts and super likes. Tinder and Hinge are the company's largest growth drivers, while Match.com and Meetic serve older demographics in North America and Europe respectively.

Criticism and legal issues

The company has faced antitrust scrutiny — it sued Bumble in 2018 over patent and trademark claims after Bumble rejected an acquisition offer, and the companies later settled. Match Group has also been a prominent litigant against Google's app-store billing policies. Regulators have examined the safety and marketing practices of its platforms, including an FTC suit (later settled) concerning Match.com's handling of fraudulent accounts.
