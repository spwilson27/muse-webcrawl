Playhouse Disney

NetworkDisney Channel
Launched8 May 1997 (as a block); 2001 (as a channel in some regions)
Closed14 February 2011 (rebranded as Disney Junior in the US)
Target audiencePreschool children (ages 2–7)

Playhouse Disney was a brand of programming blocks and television channels owned by the Walt Disney Company aimed at preschool-age children. It began in 1997 as a morning block on the Disney Channel in the United States and expanded into standalone channels in many countries.

Programming

Programmes shown under the brand included Bear in the Big Blue House, Rolie Polie Olie, Stanley, Mickey Mouse Clubhouse, My Friends Tigger & Pooh, Little Einsteins, Handy Manny, and Special Agent Oso. The shows emphasised simple social, educational, and problem-solving themes for very young children.

Rebranding

In 2010 Disney announced the block and channels would be relaunched as Disney Junior; the change took place in the United States in February 2011 and rolled out internationally over the following years. Several regions, such as parts of Asia, kept the Playhouse Disney name somewhat longer before switching.
