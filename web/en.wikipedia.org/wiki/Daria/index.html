Daria

Daria is an American adult animated sitcom created by Glenn Eichler and Susie Lewis Lynn for MTV. It aired from March 3, 1997, to January 21, 2002, running for five seasons of 13 episodes each, plus two television films.

Premise

The series is a spin-off of Beavis and Butt-Head, in which the title character, Daria Morgendorffer, appeared as a recurring foil. Set in the fictional suburban town of Lawndale, the show follows the intelligent, cynical and deadpan Daria as she navigates high school, satirising popular culture, social classes and the absurdities of suburban life. Her best friend is the artistic Jane Lane; her family includes her workaholic mother Helen, her eccentric father Jake, and her image-obsessed younger sister Quinn.

Reception and legacy

Daria was praised for its sharp writing, feminist perspective and portrayal of a smart teenage heroine, and it has retained a strong cult following. The intro theme, "You're Standing on My Neck", was performed by Splendora. A revival spin-off centred on the character Jodie was announced by MTV in the 2020s.
