Triumph of the Will

Triumph of the Will (German: Triumph des Willens) is a 1935 German Nazi propaganda film directed, produced, edited and co-written by Leni Riefenstahl, documenting the 1934 Nazi Party Congress in Nuremberg.

Content and technique

The film shows speeches by Adolf Hitler and other Nazi leaders intercut with massed troops, crowds and aerial photography. Its innovative camera movement, editing and use of music made it a landmark in film technique, while its purpose was to portray Hitler as saviour of a united Germany.

Legacy

Widely regarded as one of the most effective — and disturbing — propaganda films ever made, it remains restricted in Germany and studied in film history. See Leni Riefenstahl.
