Doğu Ekspresi

The Doğu Ekspresi (English: Eastern Express) is an overnight intercity sleeper train operated by TCDD Taşımacılık, the passenger division of the Turkish State Railways, between Ankara and Kars. The route is 1,310 kilometres (810 mi) long and the journey takes about 24 to 26 hours, passing through Kayseri, Sivas, Erzincan, and Erzurum before terminating at Kars near the Armenian border.

Tourism

In the late 2010s the journey became famous on social media for its scenic winter landscapes through eastern Anatolia, and demand outstripped the capacity of the regular service. In response, the Turkish Ministry of Transport launched the separate Touristic Eastern Express (Turistik Doğu Ekspresi) in May 2019, with longer sightseeing stops at intermediate stations and sleeping cars only, running alongside the classic service.
