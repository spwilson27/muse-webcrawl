Book of Enoch

Book of Enoch (1 Enoch)
Author (attributed): Enoch
Date: c. 300–200 BC (earliest sections)
Language: Ge'ez (complete); Aramaic fragments

The Book of Enoch is an ancient Jewish apocalyptic text ascribed to Enoch, great-grandfather of Noah. It describes the fall of the Watchers — angels who fathered the Nephilim giants — Enoch's journeys through heaven and hell, and coming judgement.

Contents

Its five parts are the Book of the Watchers, Book of Parables (Similitudes), Astronomical Book, Book of Dream Visions, and Epistle of Enoch. Aramaic fragments were found among the Dead Sea Scrolls.

Canon status

It is canonical only in the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church and Eritrean Orthodox Church; elsewhere it is non-canonical but historically influential, quoted in the New Testament Epistle of Jude. Complete text survives only in Ge'ez. See Enoch, Apocrypha, Nephilim.
