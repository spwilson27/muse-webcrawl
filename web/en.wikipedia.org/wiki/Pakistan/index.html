Pakistan

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
[Image: The Pakistan Monument in Islamabad at dusk, petal-shaped arches above the city, and northern mountains beyond]

Pakistan, officially the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, is a country in South Asia. It is the fifth-most populous country in the world, with over 240 million people, and borders India to the east, Afghanistan to the west, Iran to the southwest and China to the northeast, with a coastline on the Arabian Sea.

Pakistan emerged as an independent state in August 1947, when British India was partitioned and Pakistan became a homeland for the subcontinent's Muslims under the leadership of Muhammad Ali Jinnah, its founder and first governor-general. Its capital is Islamabad; its largest city is Karachi.

Geography

The Indus River system waters the country's agricultural heartland of Punjab and Sindh. The north contains the Karakoram, including K2, the world's second-highest peak. Pakistan sits at the junction of the Indian tectonic plate with the Eurasian plate, giving it seismic risk alongside its mountains.

History and politics

The region was home to the ancient Indus Valley Civilisation (Mohenjo-daro, Harappa). Since independence Pakistan has alternated between civilian and military rule, fought wars with India (especially over Kashmir), and become a declared nuclear-weapons state in 1998.
