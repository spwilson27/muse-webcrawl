Ape Inc.

Ape Inc. (株式会社エイプ, Kabushiki-gaisha Eipu) was a Japanese video game development company founded in 1989. It is best known for creating the Mother series of role-playing games (released in the West as EarthBound Beginnings and EarthBound) in collaboration with Nintendo. The company was established with involvement from Shigesato Itoi, the copywriter and designer who created the series, and former Nintendo president Hiroshi Yamauchi supported its formation.

History

Ape Inc. was founded when Shigesato Itoi pitched his concept for a contemporary-setting role-playing game to Nintendo. Rather than developing the game entirely in-house, Nintendo set up Ape as an external company staffed partly with young developers, with Itoi directing and writing. Mother was released for the Famicom in July 1989 and became a hit in Japan, praised for its modern setting, emotional story, and Itoi's distinctive writing.

The sequel, Mother 2 (1994) for the Super Famicom — released in North America as EarthBound (1995) — had a long and troubled development, during which programmer Satoru Iwata of HAL Laboratory famously rebuilt the game's code. Though a commercial disappointment in the West at the time, EarthBound later became a cult classic and one of the most celebrated RPGs of its era.

Products and publishing

Besides the games themselves, Ape Inc. produced official guidebooks and other publications, and members of its staff were involved with early work connected to the Pokémon franchise. In 1995, Tsunekazu Ishihara and other Ape staff involved in Pokémon founded Creatures Inc., which took over Pokémon-related development and production roles, while Ape itself faded from active game development. The third series entry, Mother 3 (2006) for the Game Boy Advance, was developed instead by Brownie Brown (later 1-Up Studio) with HAL and Nintendo.

Legacy

Ape Inc. is remembered chiefly through the enduring reputation of the Mother series: its characters, especially Ness, became fixtures of Nintendo's Super Smash Bros. games, and EarthBound influenced a generation of independent RPGs. Nintendo re-released EarthBound Beginnings (the previously unreleased English version of Mother) in 2015.
