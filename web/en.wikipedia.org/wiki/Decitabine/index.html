Decitabine

Decitabine (trade name Dacogen) is a chemotherapy drug used to treat myelodysplastic syndromes (MDS) and acute myeloid leukaemia (AML).

It is a hypomethylating agent: a cytidine analogue that incorporates into DNA and inhibits DNA methyltransferases, reactivating silenced genes in cancer cells. It is administered intravenously; an oral combination with cedazuridine (Inqovi) was later approved. Common side effects include myelosuppression, nausea and fatigue.
