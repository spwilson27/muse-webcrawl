Michael Jung (born 31 July 1982) is a German equestrian who competes in eventing. Widely regarded as one of the greatest event riders in history, he has won individual Olympic gold medals at London 2012, Rio 2016, and Paris 2024, and is the only rider to have held Olympic, World, and European individual titles simultaneously.

Career

Jung rose to prominence with the horse La Biosthetique-Sam FBW ("Sam"), with whom he won the 2010 World Championship and both Olympic golds at London 2012. In 2015–16, riding FischerRocana FST, he won the Rolex Grand Slam of Eventing by winning Kentucky, Badminton, and Burghley consecutively. At Paris 2024 he won individual gold on Chipmunk FRH.
