List of largest power stations

Largest power station
Three Gorges Dam, China — 22,500 MW, the world's largest by installed capacity.

This page lists the largest power stations in the world by current installed electrical generation capacity. The Three Gorges Dam in China, a hydroelectric facility, is the largest power station overall at 22,500 MW; the largest non-hydroelectric station is the Kashiwazaki-Kariwa nuclear plant in Japan.

Records by type

  • Solar: Bhadla Solar Park, India (~2,245 MW).
  • Wind (offshore): Hornsea projects, UK (Hornsea 2, 1,386 MW).
  • Geothermal: The Geysers, United States (~1,500 MW complex).
  • Tidal: Sihwa Lake Tidal Power Station, South Korea (254 MW).
  • Gas: Burullus / Egypt megaproject plants, among the largest at ~4,800 MW each.
References: International Energy Agency; Global Energy Monitor; operator disclosures.
