The 2016 Atlantic hurricane season was the first above-average Atlantic season since 2012, producing 15 named storms, seven hurricanes and four major hurricanes. It was notable for Hurricane Matthew, the strongest and most destructive storm of the season and the first Category 5 Atlantic hurricane since 2007, as well as Hurricane Hermine, the first hurricane to make landfall in Florida in eleven years. The season officially ran from 1 June to 30 November 2016, although Hurricane Alex formed unusually in mid-January.

Seasonal summary

Activity began early with Alex, which became a rare January hurricane in the far eastern Atlantic and struck the Azores as a tropical storm. Bonnie and Colin affected the US Southeast in late spring. Hermine made landfall in Florida's Big Bend region on 2 September as a Category 1 hurricane, ending the state's record hurricane landfall drought dating back to Wilma in 2005. The season peaked in early October with Matthew, which devastated western Haiti before tracking along the coasts of Florida, Georgia and the Carolinas, causing catastrophic flooding. Nicole reached Category 4 strength east of Bermuda, and Otto crossed into the Pacific basin in late November after striking Nicaragua and Costa Rica — the latest-forming major hurricane activity of the season.

Hurricane Matthew

Hurricane Matthew reached Category 5 intensity south of Jamaica with winds of 165 mph, the strongest Atlantic storm since Felix in 2007. Its landfall near Les Anglais, Haiti, as a Category 4 hurricane on 4 October killed hundreds — Haitian authorities reported 546 deaths, with some estimates considerably higher — and destroyed much of the Tiburon Peninsula. In the United States, Matthew caused severe coastal and riverine flooding, particularly in North and South Carolina, and was blamed for around 50 deaths. The name Matthew was retired after the season and replaced with Harold.

Retired names

The World Meteorological Organization retired the names Matthew and Otto following the season, owing to the deaths and damage they caused.
