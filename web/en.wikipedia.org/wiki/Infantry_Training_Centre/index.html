Infantry Training Centre

Infantry Training Centre (ITC)
Location: Catterick Garrison, North Yorkshire
Branch: British Army
Role: Infantry initial training

The Infantry Training Centre (ITC) is a unit of the British Army, headquartered at Catterick Garrison in North Yorkshire, that administers the training of the Army's infantry soldiers, except for the junior soldiers of the Household Division and the Parachute Regiment.

Recruits pass out after completing the Combat Infantryman's Course.

Combat Infantryman's Course

The ITC delivers the Combat Infantryman's Course (CIC), the Phase 2 training course for infantry recruits. The standard course for line infantry lasts around 26 weeks, combining weapons training, fieldcraft, physical training, live-firing exercises and a final gruelling test exercise, culminating in a pass-out parade. Regiments with additional requirements, such as the Foot Guards, run longer versions of the course incorporating ceremonial drill.

Organisation

The centre comprises several infantry training battalions, through which recruits are streamed by regiment, together with support and training companies. Junior entry soldiers who begin training at the Army Foundation College, Harrogate, proceed to Catterick for the shorter junior CIC.
