The OMX Helsinki 25 (OMXH25) is a stock market index for the Helsinki Stock Exchange (Nasdaq Helsinki). It is a market capitalisation-weighted index consisting of the 25 most actively traded stocks listed in Helsinki, and is the principal benchmark index for the Finnish equity market.

Composition

The index composition is reviewed twice a year. Major constituents have traditionally included Nokia, the forest industry companies UPM and Stora Enso, the bank Nordea, the energy company Fortum, the elevator manufacturer KONE, and the engineering groups Wärtsilä and Neste. The weight of any single company is capped to limit concentration. A broader companion measure, the OMX Helsinki All-Share Index, covers all shares listed on the exchange.
