Timeline of European Union history


The flag of Europe

This timeline of European Union history lists the principal events in the formation and development of the European Union, from the first post-war steps toward integration to the present day.

Key events

YearEvent
1950Schuman Declaration proposes a European Coal and Steel Community.
1951Treaty of Paris signed by six founding states: Belgium, France, Italy, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, and West Germany.
1957Treaties of Rome create the European Economic Community (EEC) and Euratom.
1973First enlargement: Denmark, Ireland, and the United Kingdom join.
1979First direct elections to the European Parliament.
1986Single European Act signed, setting the goal of a single market by 1992.
1992Maastricht Treaty signed; it establishes the European Union and paves the way for the euro.
1999The euro is introduced for accounting purposes; coins and banknotes follow in 2002.
2004Largest single enlargement: ten new member states, mostly from Central and Eastern Europe.
2007Lisbon Treaty signed, reforming the EU's institutions; Romania and Bulgaria join.
2013Croatia becomes the 28th member state.
2016The United Kingdom votes in a referendum to leave the EU.
2020The United Kingdom withdraws ("Brexit"), leaving 27 member states.
