K Foundation

The K Foundation was an art foundation created in 1993 by Bill Drummond and Jimmy Cauty, formerly of the pop group The KLF. Operating in the fields of art and advertising, it is best known for its activities in 1994, funded from the duo's music earnings.

The Foundation staged the K Foundation Award, an alternative art prize awarded on the same night as the 1993 Turner Prize, offering £40,000 to the "worst artist" — won by Rachel Whiteread, who also won the Turner Prize that year. On 23 August 1994, on the island of Jura in Scotland, Drummond and Cauty burned one million pounds sterling in cash, an act filmed as Watch the K Foundation Burn a Million Quid.

The pair signed a contract on a car agreeing to a moratorium on K Foundation activities, and the project was wound up, though its legacy in discussions of art, money and situationist pranks endures.
