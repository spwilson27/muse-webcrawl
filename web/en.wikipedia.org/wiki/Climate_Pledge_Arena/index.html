Climate Pledge Arena
Location: Seattle, Washington
Opened: April 21, 1962 (original); October 19, 2021 (rebuilt)
Capacity: 18,100 (basketball); 17,151 (ice hockey)
Tenants: Seattle Kraken (NHL), Seattle Storm (WNBA)

Climate Pledge Arena is a multi-purpose indoor arena in Seattle, Washington, United States, located north of Downtown Seattle in the 74-acre (30 ha) entertainment complex known as Seattle Center, the site of the 1962 World's Fair, for which it was originally developed.

History

The arena opened on April 21, 1962, as the Washington State Coliseum for the Century 21 Exposition. It later became the Seattle Center Coliseum and, from 1995 to 2008, KeyArena, during which time it was home to the Seattle SuperSonics of the NBA until the team's relocation to Oklahoma City in 2008.

In 2018, construction began on a $1.15 billion redevelopment that preserved the landmark roof while completely rebuilding the interior. Amazon purchased the naming rights in 2020 and named the venue after The Climate Pledge, its initiative to reach net-zero carbon emissions by 2040. The arena reopened in October 2021 as the home of the Seattle Kraken of the NHL and the Seattle Storm of the WNBA.

Sustainability features

Climate Pledge Arena is designed to be the first net zero carbon certified arena in the world, powered entirely by renewable electricity, with features including an all-electric building, reclaimed rainwater for the ice surface, and a "rain to rink" system. It aims for zero waste and uses locally sourced food.
