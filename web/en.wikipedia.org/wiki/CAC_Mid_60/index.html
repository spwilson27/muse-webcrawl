CAC Mid 60

The CAC Mid 60 is a free-float market capitalisation-weighted stock market index calculated and published by Euronext. It represents the 60 largest companies listed on Euronext Paris ranked below the 40 companies of the CAC 40 index — that is, positions 41 to 100 by market capitalisation and liquidity — and serves as the benchmark for French mid-cap equities.

Composition

The index was created in 2005 as part of the reorganization of the French index family, replacing the former Second Marché index. Together with the CAC 40 and the CAC Small, it forms the CAC All-Tradable; the CAC Mid 60 combined with the CAC 40 makes up the SBF 120. Constituents are reviewed quarterly by an independent steering committee.

Investing

The index is tracked by exchange-traded funds and serves as the underlying for derivative products. Well-known constituents over the years have included mid-sized French companies from sectors such as aerospace, engineering, retail and healthcare that fall just below the CAC 40 threshold.
