Yelena Belova is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. A spy and assassin trained in the Red Room, she is the second modern character to use the name Black Widow, after Natasha Romanova.

Fictional biography

Belova was created by writer Devin Grayson and artist J. G. Jones, debuting in Inhumans #5 (1999) and receiving her own Black Widow miniseries the same year. Raised and trained from childhood in the Soviet/Russian Red Room programme, she was dispatched to challenge and later replace Romanova, whom she alternately fought and allied with. Over subsequent storylines she was manipulated by Hydra, briefly transformed into a Super-Adaptoid, and later operated as the White Widow.

In other media

Florence Pugh portrays Yelena Belova in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, debuting in Black Widow (2021) and returning in the Disney+ series Hawkeye (2021), the film Thunderbolts* (2025), and further Avengers productions, where she is depicted as Romanova's adoptive sister.
