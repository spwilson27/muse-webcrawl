The European Vegetarian Union (EVU) is an umbrella organisation of vegetarian and vegan societies across Europe, founded in 1988 in Hilversum, the Netherlands, with roots in international vegetarian congress cooperation. It represents vegetarian interests at European institutions and coordinates campaigns on food policy, labelling and public health.

V-Label

The EVU created and owns the V-Label, a standardised green "V" symbol licensing vegetarian and vegan products; national partner organisations audit and certify foods, cosmetics and services in their territories. The label appears on tens of thousands of products, and since 2024 the V-Label organisation has also operated beyond Europe under a separate international structure.

Activities

The EVU advocates for legal definitions of "vegan" and "vegetarian" in EU food law, supports initiatives such as the European Citizens' Initiative "End the Slaughter Age" and plant-based options in public canteens, and publishes position papers on the environmental and animal-welfare impacts of animal agriculture.
