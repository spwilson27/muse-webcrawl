List of Roman auxiliary regiments

This is a list of Roman auxiliary regiments — the non-citizen units of the Imperial Roman army known as auxilia. By the mid-2nd century AD there were roughly 380 auxiliary regiments: cavalry alae, infantry cohortes, and mixed cohortes equitatae, some of them 1,000 strong (miliaria). Regiments were initially raised from particular peoples whose names they retained, though over time they recruited locally.
