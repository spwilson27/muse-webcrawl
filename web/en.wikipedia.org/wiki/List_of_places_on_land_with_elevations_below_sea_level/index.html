List of places on land with elevations below sea level

This is a list of places on land with elevations below sea level. Such depressions occur in arid rift valleys, endorheic basins and areas of isostatic or tectonic subsidence. The lowest exposed land on Earth is the shore of the Dead Sea, more than 430 metres below sea level and falling roughly a metre per year as the lake shrinks.
