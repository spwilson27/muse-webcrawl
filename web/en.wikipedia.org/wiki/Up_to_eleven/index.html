Up to eleven

"Up to eleven", also phrased "these go to eleven", is an idiom from popular culture, coined in the 1984 film This Is Spinal Tap, used ironically to mean going to an extreme or maximum, exceeding the usual limits. The phrase entered the Oxford English Dictionary in 2016.

Origin

In a famous scene from This Is Spinal Tap, guitarist Nigel Tufnel proudly shows the interviewer his guitar amplifier, whose volume knobs are all marked up to eleven instead of the usual ten. When asked why the numbers don't simply end at ten, Tufnel replies: "These go to eleven."

Usage in culture

The phrase "turn it up to eleven" is widely used to suggest intensifying something to its utmost. It has been applied to music volume, spicy food, effort in sports, and countless metaphors in journalism and advertising. The fictional detail that Nigel Tufnel's amplifier went to eleven has been used as a real-world Easter egg: some audio and software products have included volume controls that genuinely go to eleven as a homage.

Legacy

The expression is one of the most quoted lines from the film and a standard piece of pop-cultural vocabulary. The amplifier in the scene is preserved as part of the film's legacy, and the phrase is frequently cited in lists of memorable movie quotations.
