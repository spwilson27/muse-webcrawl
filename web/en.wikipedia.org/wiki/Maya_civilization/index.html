The Maya civilisation was a Mesoamerican civilisation noted for the only fully developed writing system of the pre-Columbian Americas, its art and architecture, and sophisticated mathematics and astronomy — including the concept of zero and precise calendars.

Collapse and survival

Classic-period Maya cities of the southern lowlands were largely abandoned by about AD 900 (causes debated: drought, warfare, overpopulation), while northern and highland cities continued into the Spanish conquest. Millions of Maya people today speak Mayan languages and maintain traditions; great sites such as Tikal and Chichen Itza draw visitors and scholars of Egyptology's American counterparts.
