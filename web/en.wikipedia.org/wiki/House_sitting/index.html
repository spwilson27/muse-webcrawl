House sitting

House sitting is the practice whereby a person leaving their home for a period of time entrusts it to one or more "house sitters", who by agreement live in and look after the property, usually in exchange for free accommodation rather than payment. Duties commonly include caring for pets, maintaining the garden, collecting mail and keeping the home secure.

Practice

House sitting is common in countries such as Australia, New Zealand, the United Kingdom, Canada and the United States, where it serves homeowners who travel for long periods and travellers seeking low-cost lodging. Since the 1990s, online platforms have matched homeowners with sitters, and some individuals house sit full time, moving from assignment to assignment.

Arrangements vary: sitters may be expected to pay utilities, and written agreements often set out responsibilities, insurance and what happens if plans change. Pet care is the most frequent requirement, and experienced sitters with references are in particular demand.
