The Johns Hopkins Blue Jays men's lacrosse team represents Johns Hopkins University in National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) Division I college lacrosse. Founded in 1883, it is one of the oldest and most successful programmes in the sport.

History

Johns Hopkins dominated intercollegiate lacrosse in the pre-NCAA era, winning numerous national championships. Since the NCAA tournament began in 1971, the Blue Jays have won nine NCAA titles, most recently in 2007, and have appeared in many championship games. The team played as an independent for most of its history before joining the Big Ten Conference in 2015.

Rivalries

The Blue Jays' greatest rivalry is with the Maryland Terrapins, a series dating to 1895 and widely considered the premier rivalry in college lacrosse. Other traditional rivals include Virginia, Syracuse, and Princeton.
