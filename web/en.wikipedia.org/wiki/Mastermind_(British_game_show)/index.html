Mastermind is a British television quiz show created by Bill Wright and first broadcast by the BBC in 1972. Contestants sit in a black chair under a spotlight and answer questions on a specialist subject, then on general knowledge, against the clock, under the catchphrase-laden interrogation of the question master.

Format

Each contender has two minutes per round; passes are permitted but time-wasting earns the famous rebuke 'I've started so I'll finish.' Long hosted by Magnus Magnusson (1972–1997), the programme was later presented by John Humphrys and then Clive Myrie.

Cultural impact

The show's austere set, spotlight, and black chair became iconic in British culture, spawning international versions and frequent parodies. Its specialist-subject format tests deep knowledge ranging from history to popular music.
