Kuala Lumpur

Kuala Lumpur, officially the Federal Territory of Kuala Lumpur, is the capital and most populous city of Malaysia, with about 2 million residents in the city and 8.8 million in the Greater Kuala Lumpur (Klang Valley) region. Its name means 'muddy confluence' — of the Klang and Gombak rivers, where tin miners founded it in 1857.

Kuala Lumpur's skyline is defined by the Petronas Twin Towers (452 metres, the world's tallest twin towers), the KL Tower and, since 2023, Merdeka 118, the world's second-tallest building at 679 metres. The city blends Malay, Chinese and Indian cultures — visible in its mosques, temples, food and festivals — and hosts the Malaysian Parliament, the King's palace (Istana Negara) and the historic Merdeka Square where independence was proclaimed in 1957.
