Lasse Artturi Virén (born 22 July 1949) is a Finnish former long-distance runner, winner of four Olympic gold medals. He is best known for winning both the 5000 metres and the 10,000 metres at the 1972 Munich Olympics and repeating the double at the 1976 Montreal Olympics, a feat matched by very few athletes in history.

Career

Virén came to prominence at the 1972 Munich Games, where in the 10,000 m final he fell after colliding with another runner, got up, and still won the race in a world record time of 27:38.4. Days later he won the 5000 m. At Montreal in 1976 he defended both titles and also placed fifth in the marathon, run less than a day after his 5000 m victory. He competed again at the 1980 Moscow Olympics but did not medal.

Later life

After retiring from competition, Virén entered politics and served as a member of the Finnish Parliament. He remained an iconic figure in Finnish sport, associated with the tradition of the "Flying Finns."
