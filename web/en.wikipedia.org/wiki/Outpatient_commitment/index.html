Outpatient commitment

Outpatient commitment—also called assisted outpatient treatment (AOT) or community treatment orders (CTO)—refers to a civil court procedure in which a court orders an individual diagnosed with a severe mental illness to adhere to an outpatient treatment plan, including medication, while living in the community.

Laws exist in most U.S. states, including New York's Kendra's Law and California's Laura's Law. Supporters argue it reduces hospitalization and violence; critics raise civil-liberties and effectiveness concerns.
