Arsène Lupin III, usually called Lupin III, is a fictional character created by Japanese manga artist Monkey Punch. He is the grandson of Maurice Leblanc's gentleman thief Arsène Lupin and the protagonist of the Lupin the Third franchise, one of the most influential manga and anime series.

Character

Lupin is the world's most wanted thief, cheerfully announcing his heists in advance with calling cards. He is aided by sharpshooter Daisuke Jigen, swordsman Goemon Ishikawa XIII, and the enigmatic Fujiko Mine, and is relentlessly pursued by Inspector Koichi Zenigata of Interpol. Despite his criminal career, Lupin typically steals from villains and follows his own code of honour.

Franchise

The manga began in 1967, followed by multiple anime television series from 1971 onward, films including Hayao Miyazaki's directorial debut The Castle of Cagliostro (1979), television specials, video games and live-action adaptations. Lupin usually wears a brightly coloured jacket whose colour changes between series.
