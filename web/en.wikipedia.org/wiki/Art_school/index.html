Art school

An art school is an educational institution with a primary focus on the visual arts, including fine art – especially illustration, painting, photography, sculpture, and graphic design.

Forms

Art schools can offer elementary, secondary, post-secondary or undergraduate, or graduate degrees, or deliver short-term continuing education. They may be independent institutions, such as the Royal College of Art or Rhode Island School of Design (RISD), or faculties within universities.

History

The academy model, from Renaissance workshops and the French Académie, emphasised life drawing and classical training. The Bauhaus (1919–1933) revolutionised art and design education, merging craft and fine art. Modern curricula add digital media, installation, and critical theory, often centred on studio practice and the crit.

Admissions

Admission typically requires a portfolio. Degrees include BFA and MFA.
