Israel

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

[Image: The Jerusalem skyline with the golden Dome of the Rock on the Temple Mount / Haram al-Sharif]

Israel, officially the State of Israel, is a country in the Levant region of West Asia, bordered by Lebanon, Syria, Jordan, Egypt and the Mediterranean Sea. Its capital and largest city — claimed and administered — is Jerusalem, whose status is internationally disputed; its financial centre is Tel Aviv. Established in 1948 amid war with neighbouring Arab states, Israel has about 10 million citizens, roughly three-quarters Jewish and about a fifth Arab, and is a high-tech economy ('Start-up Nation'). The Israeli–Palestinian conflict, including the war in Gaza since October 2023, remains a central international issue.
