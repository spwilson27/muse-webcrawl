Overview

The Real is an American syndicated talk show created by SallyAnn Salsano. It premiered in limited markets in the summer of 2013 and began national syndication in September 2014. The panel-format show featured a group of women co-hosts discussing lifestyle topics, celebrity interviews, and personal stories, with the popular segment Girl Chat as its centerpiece.

The original panel consisted of Tamar Braxton, Tamera Mowry-Housley, Adrienne Bailon, Jeannie Mai, and Loni Love. Braxton departed in 2016; Amanda Seales joined briefly in 2020, and Garcelle Beauvais joined later that year. Mowry-Housley left in 2020.

History and reception

The show was noted for its diverse panel and candid discussions of relationships, family, and culture, and it won a Daytime Emmy Award for Outstanding Entertainment Talk Show Hosts in 2018. The Real ran for eight seasons before ending in June 2022 after its cancellation was announced earlier that year.
