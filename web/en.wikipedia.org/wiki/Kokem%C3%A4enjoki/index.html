Kokemäenjoki

Country: Finland

Length: about 121 km

Source: Lake Liekovesi, Sastamala

Mouth: Gulf of Bothnia at Pori

The Kokemäenjoki ("Kokemäki River", Swedish: Kumo älv) is a river in southwestern Finland. It is among the most significant rivers in Finland in terms of its drainage basin and is known for its rich delta, one of the largest inland river deltas in the Nordic countries.

Course

The river begins at Lake Liekovesi in Sastamala and flows westward about 121 km through the Satakunta region, passing Kokemäki and Huittinen, before emptying into the Gulf of Bothnia at Pori. Its estuary and delta at Pori are important bird habitats and protected wetland areas.

Use

Several hydroelectric power stations harness the river, and its broad valley has been settled since prehistoric times.
