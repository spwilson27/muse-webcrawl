Tourism in Singapore

Tourism in SingaporeVisitors: about 19 million (2019)
Major attractions: Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay, Sentosa, Orchard Road

Tourism in Singapore is a major industry and contributor to the economy of the city-state, attracting roughly nineteen million international visitors in peak years — more than three times the country's population.

Attractions

Singapore markets itself through iconic developments such as Marina Bay Sands, the Gardens by the Bay with its Supertrees, the resort island of Sentosa, and the Singapore Zoo and Night Safari. Heritage districts including Chinatown, Little India and Kampong Glam draw cultural tourists.

Events and shopping

The Singapore Grand Prix night race, the Great Singapore Sale and major conventions at Suntec and Marina Bay contribute to arrivals. Orchard Road is the country's principal shopping district.

Industry bodies

The Singapore Tourism Board promotes the destination abroad under campaigns such as "Passion Made Possible", while Changi Airport — consistently ranked among the world's best — serves as the main gateway.
