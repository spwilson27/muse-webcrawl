Soulslike

A Soulslike (or Souls-like) is a subgenre of action role-playing games known for high difficulty, environmental storytelling, and mechanics inspired by FromSoftware's Souls series, beginning with Demon's Souls (2009) and Dark Souls (2011).

Soulslike
GenreAction RPG subgenre
OriginatorFromSoftware; Hidetaka Miyazaki
Key titlesDark Souls, Bloodborne, Elden Ring, Nioh, Lies of P

Common features

Stamina-based combat, checkpoints (bonfires) that respawn enemies, currency lost on death and recoverable at the death site, large interconnected worlds with shortcuts, cryptic item-based lore, and asynchronous multiplayer messages. Boss fights are a central challenge.

History

The genre's conventions spread widely after Dark Souls' critical and commercial success. Elden Ring (2022) brought the formula to an open world and mass audience. The label itself is debated, with some developers resisting it as reductive.
