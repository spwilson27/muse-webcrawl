Recuerdos de la Alhambra

Recuerdos de la Alhambra (Memories of the Alhambra) is a classical guitar piece composed in Málaga by Spanish composer and guitarist Francisco Tárrega. It is one of the best-known works in the guitar repertoire and a benchmark of the tremolo technique.

Music

The piece is a study in tremolo, in which a single melody line is played in the bass by the thumb while the rapidly repeated upper notes are produced by the fingers, creating the illusion of a sustained melody. Its title recalls the Alhambra palace in Granada, and the work captures the impression of the fountains of the palace.

Legacy

Recuerdos de la Alhambra has been arranged for many instruments and is a standard test of a classical guitarist's tremolo. It has appeared in numerous films and recordings, including performances by Andrés Segovia and John Williams.
