Cacophony Society

Formed1986
OriginSan Francisco, California
TypeCulture jamming / prank network
PredecessorSuicide Club

The Cacophony Society is an American network of culture jammers and pranksters, founded in San Francisco in 1986 by former members of the Suicide Club. It organised surreal urban adventures, public pranks, and costumed events, and inspired chapters in other cities.

Its motto might be summarised by its own phrase: 'You may already be a member.'

Origins

The Suicide Club, founded in 1977 by Gary Warne and others, staged infiltration games, bridge climbs, and mock rituals. After its dissolution, several members founded the Cacophony Society to continue the tradition of participatory absurdity, announced through a newsletter called Rough Draft.

Activities

Signature events included the SantaCon pub crawl (begun 1994 as 'Santarchy'), the Brides of March, urban games, billboard alterations, and explorations of off-limits places. Cacophony culture fed directly into Burning Man: John Law and other Cacophonists were central to the event's early Black Rock Desert years.

Influence

The Society is often credited as a precursor to flash mobs and modern prank networks such as Improv Everywhere. Fight Club author Chuck Palahniuk, a Portland chapter participant, drew on Cacophony for elements of Project Mayhem.
