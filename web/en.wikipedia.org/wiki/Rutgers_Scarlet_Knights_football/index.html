The Rutgers Scarlet Knights football team represents Rutgers University–New Brunswick in American college football. The Scarlet Knights compete in the NCAA Division I Football Bowl Subdivision as members of the Big Ten Conference, which they joined in 2014. Rutgers is celebrated as "the Birthplace of College Football": on 6 November 1869, Rutgers hosted Princeton in New Brunswick in the first intercollegiate football game ever played, winning 6–4 in a match closer to soccer than the modern game.

History

After that pioneering era, Rutgers football spent most of the twentieth century as a small-program independent, fielding notable teams such as the undefeated 1976 squad (11–0) under Frank Burns, which finished ranked 17th without a bowl invitation. The program joined the Big East Conference in 1991 and endured years of losing seasons before Greg Schiano, hired in 2001, rebuilt it from the ground up.

The breakthrough came in 2005 with the school's first bowl in 27 years, and in 2006 the Scarlet Knights went 11–2, beat #3 Louisville in a celebrated Thursday-night game at a rocking home stadium, reached as high as #7 in the rankings, and won the Texas Bowl over Kansas State — the program's first bowl victory. Star players of the era included running back Ray Rice, receiver Kenny Britt, and the McCourty twins. Rutgers won five straight bowl games between 2006 and 2011.

Big Ten era

Rutgers, along with Maryland, joined the Big Ten in 2014, a move driven by the New York metropolitan media market; the early Big Ten years were difficult, with coaching changes from Kyle Flood to Chris Ash. Greg Schiano returned as head coach in 2020 and gradually rebuilt the program's competitiveness, returning the Scarlet Knights to bowl eligibility.
