World of A Song of Ice and Fire

The world of A Song of Ice and Fire is the fictional setting of George R. R. Martin's epic fantasy novels and their television adaptations, centred on the continents of Westeros and Essos, with seasons that can last for years.

Creator: George R. R. Martin
Continents: Westeros, Essos, Sothoryos, Ulthos

Geography

Westeros, roughly Britain-sized in the author's conception but a continent, holds the Seven Kingdoms ruled from King's Landing. Across the Narrow Sea, Essos stretches to the Free Cities, Slaver's Bay, and the Dothraki Sea. North of the Wall lies the haunted far north of the Others.

History and cultures

Its deep history — First Men, Andals, Aegon's Conquest, dragons of House Targaryen — is chronicled in The World of Ice & Fire (2014) and Fire & Blood. See A Song of Ice and Fire, Westeros, Game of Thrones.
