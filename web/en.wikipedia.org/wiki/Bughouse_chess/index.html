Bughouse chess

Bughouse chess
Players: 4 (two teams of two)
Boards: 2
Also known as: Exchange chess, tandem chess, transfer chess

Bughouse chess (also known as exchange chess, tandem chess, or transfer chess) is a popular chess variant played on two chessboards by four players in teams of two. Each team member sits opposite the opponent's partner, one playing White and the other Black.

Rules

Normal chess rules apply, with one key addition: pieces captured by a player are passed to their partner, who may place them on their own board as a move ("dropping") instead of making a normal move. Pawns may not be dropped on the first or eighth ranks. A team wins when one of its members checkmates an opponent or an opponent's flag falls on time; games are usually played at very fast time controls, often three to five minutes per player.

Strategy

Because dropped pieces can deliver sudden checkmate attacks, defence of the king and communication between partners are vital. Players may stall or sacrifice material to obtain a specific piece for their partner, and the value of pieces differs markedly from standard chess — pawns are relatively stronger because they can be dropped to block attacks and promote.

Online play

Bughouse is widely played on internet chess servers, and the variant has an enthusiastic following particularly in North America and Europe.
