Space Operations Command (SpOC) is the United States Space Force's space operations, space domain awareness, space battle management, satellite communications, missile warning, navigational warfare, intelligence, and cyber operations field command. It is headquartered at Peterson Space Force Base in Colorado Springs, Colorado.

History

Space Operations Command was established on 20 December 2019, the same day the United States Space Force was signed into law, through the redesignation of Air Force Space Command. The command was initially redesignated as Headquarters, Space Operations Command, with the operational units of the former Fourteenth Air Force forming its nucleus. On 21 October 2020, Space Operations Command was formally activated as the Space Force's first field command, absorbing the forces previously organized under the Fourteenth Air Force.

Structure

The command is organized into a number of Space Deltas, each responsible for a specific mission area. These include Space Delta 2 (space domain awareness), Space Delta 3 (space electromagnetic warfare), Space Delta 4 (missile warning), Space Delta 5 (command and control), Space Delta 6 (cyberspace operations), Space Delta 7 (intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance), Space Delta 8 (satellite communication and navigation warfare), and Space Delta 9 (orbital warfare).

Mission

Space Operations Command generates, presents, and sustains combat-ready intelligence, cyber, space, and combat support forces. Its personnel operate the majority of the United States' military satellite constellations, including those providing missile warning, global positioning, and satellite communications to joint forces around the world.
