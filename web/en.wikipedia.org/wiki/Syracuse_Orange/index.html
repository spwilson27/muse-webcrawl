Syracuse Orange

University: Syracuse University

Conference: Atlantic Coast Conference (ACC)

NCAA: Division I FBS

Location: Syracuse, New York

Mascot: Otto the Orange

Colors: Orange and blue

The Syracuse Orange are the athletic teams that represent Syracuse University in Syracuse, New York. The university is a member of the Atlantic Coast Conference (ACC) in NCAA Division I, which it joined in 2013 after being a founding member of the Big East Conference. The school's mascot is Otto the Orange, and orange has been the official color since 1890.

History

Syracuse fielded its first football team in 1889. For most of their history the teams were known as the Orangemen and Orangewomen; in 2004 the university standardized the nickname as the gender-neutral "Orange". Syracuse was a founding member of the Big East in 1979 and moved to the ACC on July 1, 2013.

Football

The Syracuse Orange football team won the national championship in 1959, defeating Texas in the Cotton Bowl. The program produced legendary running backs wearing the number 44, including Jim Brown, Ernie Davis — the first African American to win the Heisman Trophy, in 1961 — and Floyd Little. The team plays at the JMA Wireless Dome (formerly the Carrier Dome), the largest domed stadium on a college campus in the United States.

Men's basketball

The men's basketball program is one of the most successful in NCAA history. Under coach Jim Boeheim, who led the team from 1976 to 2023, Syracuse won the NCAA national championship in 2003, led by freshman Carmelo Anthony, defeating Kansas in the final. The Orange have made multiple Final Four appearances, including 1975, 1987, 1996, 2003, 2013, and 2016.

Other sports

Syracuse men's lacrosse is among the most decorated programs in the sport, with ten NCAA championships. The women's lacrosse, field hockey, ice hockey, cross country, and rowing programs have also won conference and national honors; the men's cross country team won the NCAA title in 2015.
