Subfusc

Subfusc is the formal dress worn under an academic gown by candidates at examinations and formal ceremonies of the University of Oxford. The word, from the Latin sub fuscus ("darkish"), denotes dark, sober clothing prescribed by university regulations.

Requirements

Traditional subfusc for men comprises a dark suit and socks, black shoes, a plain white shirt and collar, and a white bow tie; for women, a white blouse, black tie or ribbon, dark skirt or trousers, black stockings and shoes. Following a student referendum in 2015, the university made the clothing requirements gender-neutral. Candidates also wear the appropriate academic gown and, historically, carried a mortarboard.

Usage

Subfusc is required for matriculation, degree ceremonies, examinations and certain formal university occasions such as Encaenia. Similar dress codes, though less codified, exist at other ancient British universities.
