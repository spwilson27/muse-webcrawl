The United States Marine Corps (USMC), also referred to as the United States Marines, is the maritime land force service branch of the United States Department of Defense responsible for conducting expeditionary and amphibious operations through combined arms, implementing its own infantry, artillery, aerial, and special operations forces. The Corps is one of the eight uniformed services of the United States and one of the two armed services within the Department of the Navy, alongside the United States Navy.

History

The Marine Corps was founded at Tun Tavern in Philadelphia on 10 November 1775 by a resolution of the Second Continental Congress, which raised two battalions of Continental Marines under Captain Samuel Nicholas. Marines landed at New Providence in the Bahamas in 1776, fought in the American Revolutionary War, and famously stormed the Barbary pirate stronghold at Derna, Tripoli, in 1805 — an action recalled in the line "to the shores of Tripoli" in the Marines' Hymn.

The Corps distinguished itself in the Mexican–American War (1847), the American Civil War, and in the "Banana Wars" of the early 20th century. In the First World War, Marines earned the nickname "Devil Dogs" (Teufel Hunden) at the Battle of Belleau Wood (1918). In the Second World War the Corps executed the island-hopping campaign across the Pacific, including Guadalcanal, Tarawa, Iwo Jima and Okinawa. Marines held the Pusan Perimeter and landed at Inchon in the Korean War, fought at Khe Sanh and Huế in Vietnam, and served extensively in Iraq and Afghanistan.

Mission and structure

By law, the Marine Corps provides forces for the seizure or defense of advanced naval bases and conducts land operations essential to a naval campaign. It is organized into Marine Expeditionary Forces, divisions, air wings and logistics groups, and provides Marine detachments aboard naval vessels and security forces for U.S. embassies. Recruits train at Parris Island, South Carolina, and San Diego, California.

Culture

The Corps' emblem is the Eagle, Globe, and Anchor. Its motto is Semper Fidelis, adopted in 1883. Marines observe the Marine Corps Birthday on 10 November each year, and the flag raising at Iwo Jima in 1945 remains one of its most iconic images.
