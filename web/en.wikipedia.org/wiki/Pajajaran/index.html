Pajajaran

Pajajaran was the capital of the Sunda Kingdom, a Hindu kingdom in western Java, generally located near present-day Bogor in Indonesia. The name is also used for the kingdom itself in later Sundanese tradition.

History

The Sunda Kingdom, with its capital at Pakuan Pajajaran, flourished from the 7th to the 16th century and was known for pepper trade through ports such as Sunda Kelapa and Banten. Prabu Siliwangi is its most famous semi-legendary king. In 1527 the capital's port fell to Demak and Cirebon forces, and in 1579 Pajajaran was finally destroyed by the Sultanate of Banten, ending the kingdom.

Legacy

Pajajaran remains a powerful symbol of Sundanese identity; Universitas Padjadjaran in Bandung takes its name from the kingdom.
