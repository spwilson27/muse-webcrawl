Avengers Campus

Locations: Disney California Adventure; Walt Disney Studios Park (Paris); Hong Kong Disneyland
Opened: 2021 (California)
Theme: Marvel Cinematic Universe

Avengers Campus is a Marvel-themed land at Disney theme parks, first opened at Disney California Adventure in Anaheim, California, in June 2021, and at Walt Disney Studios Park in Paris in 2022. The land is themed as a campus established by the Avengers to recruit and train new heroes.

Attractions

Anchor attractions include Web Slingers: A Spider-Man Adventure, an interactive dark ride, and Guardians of the Galaxy – Mission: Breakout! (converted from the Twilight Zone Tower of Terror in California). Paris features Avengers Assemble: Flight Force and Spider-Man W.E.B. Adventure. The land includes the Pym Test Kitchen restaurant and live character encounters.
