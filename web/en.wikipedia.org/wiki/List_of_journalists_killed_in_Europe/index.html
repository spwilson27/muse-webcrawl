List of journalists killed in Europe

Journalists in Europe are generally considered to work in one of the safer regions of the world, but a number have been murdered, assassinated or killed while reporting, often in connection with investigations into organised crime, corruption and terrorism. Organisations such as the Committee to Protect Journalists and Reporters Without Borders document these cases.

Notable cases by country

France

On 7 January 2015, gunmen attacked the Paris offices of the satirical magazine Charlie Hebdo, killing 12 people including editor Stéphane Charbonnier and cartoonists Cabu, Wolinski, Tignous and Honoré. The attack was one of the deadliest on journalists in Western Europe.

Ireland

Veronica Guerin, an investigative reporter for the Sunday Independent who wrote about Dublin's drug trade, was shot dead in her car on 26 June 1996. Her murder led to the creation of the Criminal Assets Bureau. Lyra McKee was shot while observing rioting in Derry in April 2019 during an attack attributed to dissident republicans.

Malta

Daphne Caruana Galizia, Malta's best-known investigative journalist and author of the Running Commentary blog, was killed by a car bomb on 16 October 2017 near her home in Bidnija. She had been reporting on corruption linked to the Panama Papers.

Netherlands

Crime reporter Peter R. de Vries was shot in Amsterdam on 6 July 2021 and died of his wounds nine days later. He had acted as an adviser to a key witness in a major organised-crime trial.

Russia and Ukraine

Anna Politkovskaya of Novaya Gazeta, known for her reporting on Chechnya, was murdered in Moscow on 7 October 2006. Paul Klebnikov, editor of Forbes Russia, was shot in 2004. In Ukraine, Georgiy Gongadze, founder of Ukrainska Pravda, was abducted and murdered in 2000, and Pavel Sheremet was killed by a car bomb in Kyiv in 2016. Since Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, numerous Ukrainian and foreign journalists and media workers have been killed covering the war.

Slovakia

Ján Kuciak, an investigative reporter for Aktuality.sk, and his fiancée Martina Kušnírová were shot dead in February 2018. Kuciak had been investigating tax fraud and links between organised crime and political figures; the murders triggered mass protests and the resignation of the government.

Greece

Crime journalist Giorgos Karaivaz was shot dead outside his home in Athens in April 2021. Socratis Giolias was murdered in 2010.

United Kingdom

Jill Dando, a BBC presenter, was shot on her doorstep in London in 1999; the case remains unsolved after a conviction was quashed. Martin O'Hagan of the Sunday World was shot by loyalist paramilitaries in Northern Ireland in 2001.

Turkey

Hrant Dink, editor of the Armenian-Turkish newspaper Agos, was assassinated in Istanbul in 2007. Uğur Mumcu was killed by a car bomb in Ankara in 1993.
