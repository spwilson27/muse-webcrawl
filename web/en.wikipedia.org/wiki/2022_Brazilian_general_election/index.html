The 2022 Brazilian general election was held on 2 October 2022, with a presidential runoff on 30 October. Former president Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva of the Workers' Party narrowly defeated the incumbent, Jair Bolsonaro of the Liberal Party, by 50.9 to 49.1 per cent — the closest presidential result in Brazil's history and the first time a Brazilian president seeking re-election had lost. The election, conducted amid intense polarisation and unfounded claims about the electronic voting system, returned Lula to the presidency twelve years after he left office.

Background

Bolsonaro, elected in 2018 on a far-right, anti-corruption platform, governed through the COVID-19 pandemic — in which Brazil suffered one of the world's highest death tolls — and an economic downturn. Lula, president from 2003 to 2010, had been imprisoned in 2018 on corruption convictions that barred him from that year's election; the Supreme Federal Court annulled the convictions in 2021 on grounds of judicial bias, restoring his political rights. He built a broad coalition, choosing centrist former rival Geraldo Alckmin as his running mate.

Campaign and voting

The campaign was dominated by the economy, hunger and poverty, the environment and Amazon deforestation, and Bolsonaro's repeated attacks on the integrity of Brazil's electronic voting machines. Polls consistently led by Lula nonetheless underestimated Bolsonaro in the first round: Lula won 48.4 per cent to Bolsonaro's 43.2 per cent. In the runoff, Lula prevailed by about 2.1 million votes out of more than 118 million cast. Voting is compulsory in Brazil, and turnout exceeded 79 per cent.

Aftermath

Bolsonaro did not explicitly concede but authorised the transition; many of his supporters protested, blocking highways and demanding military intervention. Lula was inaugurated on 1 January 2023. On 8 January 2023, thousands of Bolsonaro supporters stormed the National Congress, the presidential palace and the Supreme Court in Brasília, an attack widely compared to the U.S. Capitol riot of 2021; hundreds were arrested, and subsequent investigations reached into Bolsonaro's circle.
