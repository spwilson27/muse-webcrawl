Poll tax (Great Britain)

The Community Charge, commonly known as the poll tax, was a system of local taxation introduced by the government of Margaret Thatcher in Scotland in 1989 and in England and Wales in 1990, replacing domestic rates with a flat-rate charge on every adult resident.

Opposition

The tax was deeply unpopular because it charged the same amount regardless of income. A mass non-payment campaign developed, and on 31 March 1990 a large demonstration in London escalated into the Poll Tax Riots in Trafalgar Square. Opposition to the tax contributed to Thatcher's fall in November 1990.

Abolition

Her successor, John Major, replaced the Community Charge with the Council Tax, a property-based levy, from 1993.
