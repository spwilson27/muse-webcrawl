Hummer

Hummer is a brand of pickups and sport utility vehicles (SUVs) that was first marketed in 1992 when AM General began selling a civilian version of its M998 Humvee military vehicle. In 1998, General Motors (GM) purchased the brand name from AM General and marketed three models: the original Hummer H1, based on the military Humvee, and the H2 and H3 models, based on smaller GM platforms.

History

The civilian Humvee gained fame during the Gulf War and was championed by Arnold Schwarzenegger, who lobbied for a civilian version. GM discontinued the brand in 2010 following its bankruptcy, amid criticism of the vehicles' poor fuel economy. In 2020, GM revived the name as the GMC Hummer EV, an all-electric pickup truck and SUV, with production beginning in late 2021.

Models

Models included the H1 (1992–2006), H2 (2002–2009), H3 (2005–2010), and the GMC Hummer EV (2022–present), which features a removable roof, four-wheel steering ("Crab Walk"), and outputs of up to about 1,000 horsepower in its most powerful configuration.
