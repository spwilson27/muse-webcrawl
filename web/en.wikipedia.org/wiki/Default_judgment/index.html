Default judgment

A default judgment is a binding judgment in favour of one party based on the failure of the other party to take action, such as failing to respond to a summons or appear in court.

Procedure

In civil cases a claimant may seek default judgment when the defendant does not file a defence within the prescribed time.

Setting aside

Courts may set aside a default judgment if the defendant shows a good reason for the default and a meritorious defence.

Enforcement

Once entered, a default judgment is enforceable like any other judgment, though amounts may require assessment.
