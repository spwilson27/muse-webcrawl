Mercadona

Mercadona is a Spanish supermarket chain, the largest in Spain, with more than 1,600 stores across Spain and a growing presence in Portugal. Founded in 1977 in Tavernes Blanques, Valencia, as a small butcher's shop within a family business, it remains family-owned and is chaired by Juan Roig, who has led the company since 1981.

Business model

Mercadona is known for its "always low prices" strategy (in Spanish, Siempre Precios Bajos), avoiding promotions and special offers in favour of stable low prices. It places strong emphasis on its private-label brands — including Hacendado (food), Deliplus (personal care), Bosque Verde (household cleaning), and Compy (pet food) — which account for a large share of sales. The company works closely with integrated suppliers ("interproveedores") located near its logistics centres to control quality and reduce costs.

Scale and employment

With revenues exceeding €30 billion annually and a Spanish grocery market share of roughly a quarter, Mercadona is one of Spain's largest private employers, with a workforce of around 100,000. Its permanent-contract employment model and comparatively high wages are frequently studied in business schools. The company began international expansion with stores in Portugal in 2019 and has invested heavily in online sales and automated logistics blocks.
