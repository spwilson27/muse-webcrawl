Comparative army enlisted ranks of Europe

This article presents a comparison of the enlisted ranks (other ranks) of the armies of European countries, aligned with the NATO rank classification system (OR-1 through OR-9).

NATO rank codes

NATO's STANAG 2116 defines "other rank" grades from OR-1 (most junior, e.g. private) to OR-9 (most senior non-commissioned grades, e.g. warrant officer class 1 or command sergeant major equivalents).

Examples

In the British Army the sequence runs Private (OR-1/2), Lance Corporal (OR-3), Corporal (OR-4), Sergeant (OR-6), Staff Sergeant (OR-7), and Warrant Officer Class 2 and Class 1 (OR-8 and OR-9). Other nations' taxonomies, such as the French grades of soldat, caporal, sergent, and adjudant, are mapped to the same framework for interoperability.

Purpose

Comparative tables of this kind support joint operations, protocol, and mutual recognition of seniority among allied forces.
