FIFA Puskás Award

The FIFA Puskás Award is an award established on 20 October 2009 by FIFA at the behest of then-president Sepp Blatter, to be awarded to the player (male or female) judged to have scored the most aesthetically significant, or "most beautiful", goal of the calendar year. The award is named in honour of Ferenc Puskás, the legendary Hungarian and Real Madrid striker of the 1950s and early 1960s.

Criteria and voting

According to FIFA, the goal should be aesthetically beautiful, scored without the result of luck or an opponent's mistake, and in support of fair play. From 2009 to 2018 the winner was chosen solely by fan votes on FIFA's website; from 2019 a combination of fan voting and a panel of FIFA legends decides the winner from a shortlist, usually of ten or eleven goals. Since 2024, women's goals are recognized with the separate FIFA Marta Award, named after the Brazilian forward Marta.

Winners

The first winner was Cristiano Ronaldo, for his long-range strike for Manchester United against Porto in the 2008–09 UEFA Champions League. Other winners include Hamit Altıntop (2010), Neymar (2011), Miroslav Stoch (2012), Zlatan Ibrahimović (2013), James Rodríguez (2014), Wendell Lira (2015), Mohd Faiz Subri (2016), Olivier Giroud (2017), Mohamed Salah (2018), Dániel Zsóri (2019), Son Heung-min (2020), Érik Lamela (2021), Marcin Oleksy (2022) — the first amputee footballer to win — Guilherme Madruga (2023), and Alejandro Garnacho (2024), for his overhead kick for Manchester United against Everton.
