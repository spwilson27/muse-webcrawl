Solomon Hykes

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Solomon Hykes is a French-American software engineer and entrepreneur best known as the founder of Docker, Inc. and the creator of Docker, the containerization platform that reshaped how software is packaged and deployed. He later co-founded Dagger, a programmable CI/CD engine.

Career

Hykes was born in France and moved to the United States in his twenties. In 2008 he founded dotCloud, a platform-as-a-service company in San Francisco, with Kamel Founadi and Sébastien Pahl. While building the platform, the company developed an internal tool for managing Linux containers. In March 2013 Hykes demonstrated the tool, named Docker, at the PyCon conference in Santa Clara; the five-minute lightning talk introduced the Docker container format and the idea of "build, ship, and run" any application anywhere.

Docker was released as open source and grew explosively, becoming the standard interface for OS-level containers. dotCloud rebranded as Docker, Inc., and Hykes served as chief technology officer and the public face of the project, presenting the technology at conferences worldwide. Under his tenure the company launched Docker Hub, Docker Compose, and Docker Swarm, and helped found the Open Container Initiative in 2015 to standardize container formats and runtimes.

Hykes stepped down from day-to-day roles at Docker in 2018 and left the company he founded. In 2019 he co-founded Dagger, a developer tool for building CI/CD pipelines as code using containers, which launched publicly in 2022.
