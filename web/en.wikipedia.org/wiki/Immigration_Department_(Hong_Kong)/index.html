Immigration Department (Hong Kong)

Immigration Department
入境事務處
Formed: 1961
Jurisdiction: Hong Kong SAR
Headquarters: Immigration Tower, Wan Chai

The Immigration Department (Chinese: 入境事務處) of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region is responsible for immigration control, the issue of travel documents and identity cards, nationality matters, and the registration of births, deaths and marriages in Hong Kong.

History

Immigration control was originally a police responsibility; a separate Immigration Department was established in 1961. The department took over registration of persons from the police in the following decades and introduced the Hong Kong identity card system, later including smart identity cards.

Duties

The department staffs border control points at the airport, harbour crossings and land boundary with mainland China, issues HKSAR passports and visas, and handles right-of-abode and nationality applications under the Basic Law and immigration ordinances.
