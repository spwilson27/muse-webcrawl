Western Union

The Western Union Company is an American multinational financial services company headquartered in Denver, Colorado. Founded in 1851 as a telegram company in Rochester, New York, it built the first transcontinental telegraph line in 1861 and for more than a century dominated American telegraphy. Today it is best known for its international money-transfer services, operating a network of hundreds of thousands of agent locations in more than 200 countries and territories.

Telegraph era

The company was renamed the Western Union Telegraph Company in 1856, reflecting its ambition to unite the telegraph lines of the western United States. In 1861 it completed the first transcontinental telegraph line, an achievement that led to the immediate closure of the Pony Express. Western Union introduced the first stock ticker in 1866, standard time in 1884, and the singing telegram in 1933.

In 1879 the company settled its patent wars with the Bell Telephone Company, withdrawing from telephony in exchange for Bell's withdrawal from telegraphy. Western Union introduced money transfers by telegraph in 1871, a service that would eventually become its core business.

Decline of the telegram

Telegraph traffic declined through the 20th century as telephone, fax and later email spread. The company diversified into telex, satellite communications (launching Westar 1, the first American domestic communications satellite, in 1974) and financial services. After financial difficulties in the 1980s and a bankruptcy restructuring in the early 1990s, its telegram-era assets were sold off; the money-transfer business was acquired by First Data in 1995 and spun off as an independent public company in 2006.

End of telegrams

Western Union sent its last telegram on 27 January 2006, ending a service that had lasted 155 years.

Modern business

Today the company focuses on consumer-to-consumer money transfers, cross-border remittances, business payments and money orders, increasingly delivered through digital channels alongside its agent network.
