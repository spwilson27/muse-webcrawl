List of diplomatic missions of Israel

This is a list of the diplomatic missions of the State of Israel, which maintains embassies and consulates in the large majority of the world's countries. Israel's diplomatic network expanded significantly following the peace treaties with Egypt (1979) and Jordan (1994), and again after the Abraham Accords of 2020 normalised relations with the United Arab Emirates, Bahrain, Morocco, and Sudan. Israel also maintains a permanent mission to the United Nations in New York and Geneva, and missions to the European Union and other international organisations.

Selected missions

Americas

  • United States — Embassy in Washington, D.C.; consulates-general in New York, Los Angeles, Houston, Chicago, Boston, Miami, Atlanta, and San Francisco
  • Canada — Embassy in Ottawa; consulates in Toronto and Montreal
  • Mexico — Embassy in Mexico City
  • Brazil — Embassy in Brasília; consulate in São Paulo
  • Argentina — Embassy in Buenos Aires
  • Chile, Colombia, Peru, Uruguay, Panama, Guatemala, and others — embassies

Europe

  • United Kingdom — Embassy in London
  • France — Embassy in Paris
  • Germany — Embassy in Berlin; consulate in Munich
  • Russia — Embassy in Moscow
  • Italy — Embassy in Rome; Ukraine — Kyiv; Poland — Warsaw; Spain — Madrid; Netherlands — The Hague; and embassies in most other European capitals

Middle East and Africa

  • Egypt — Embassy in Cairo (since 1980)
  • Jordan — Embassy in Amman (since 1994)
  • United Arab Emirates — Embassy in Abu Dhabi and consulate in Dubai (since 2021)
  • Bahrain — Embassy in Manama; Morocco — liaison office/embassy in Rabat
  • Embassies in Ethiopia, Kenya, Nigeria, Ghana, Senegal, South Africa, Rwanda, Angola, and others

Asia-Pacific

  • China — Embassy in Beijing; consulates in Shanghai, Guangzhou, Chengdu, and Hong Kong
  • India — Embassy in New Delhi; consulates in Mumbai and Bengaluru
  • Japan — Tokyo; South Korea — Seoul; Australia — Canberra; Vietnam — Hanoi; Thailand — Bangkok; Singapore; Philippines — Manila; and others
