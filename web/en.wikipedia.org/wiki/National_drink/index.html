National drink

A national drink is a distinct beverage that is strongly associated with a particular country, and can be part of its national identity and self-image. Such drinks may be officially designated, or simply widely recognised as emblematic. They include both alcoholic beverages, such as whisky in Scotland or vodka in Russia and Poland, and non-alcoholic ones, such as tea in China or coffee in several producing nations.
