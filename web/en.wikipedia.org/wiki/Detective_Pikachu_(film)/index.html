Detective Pikachu (film)

Pokémon Detective Pikachu is a 2019 fantasy mystery film directed by Rob Letterman. Based on the Pokémon franchise and loosely on the 2016 video game Detective Pikachu, it is the first live-action Pokémon film and was produced by Legendary Pictures.

The film stars Ryan Reynolds as the voice and facial motion capture of Pikachu, with Justice Smith as Tim Goodman, a young man who teams up with the talking Pikachu to investigate the disappearance of his father, a police detective, in Ryme City — a metropolis where humans and Pokémon live side by side. Kathryn Newton and Ken Watanabe also star.

Released in May 2019, the film grossed over $449 million worldwide, becoming one of the highest-grossing video game film adaptations at the time, and received generally positive reviews for its visuals and Reynolds's performance.
