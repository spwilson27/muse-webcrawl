Crystal City, Texas

Crystal City is a city in and the county seat of Zavala County, Texas, United States. The population was about 6,300 at the 2020 census. The city is known as the "Spinach Capital of the World" and gained national significance during the Chicano movement of the late 1960s and 1970s as the birthplace of the La Raza Unida Party.

History

Crystal City was founded in the early 20th century as a planned agricultural community in the Winter Garden region of South Texas, growing and canning vegetables, particularly spinach. A statue of the cartoon character Popeye was erected in 1937 in honour of the spinach industry. During the Second World War the city hosted one of the largest internment camps for enemy aliens and their families, the Crystal City Internment Camp, which held Japanese, German and Italian families.

Chicano movement

In 1963, Mexican-American cannery workers and residents mounted a successful electoral challenge to the city's Anglo political establishment ("Los Cinco Candidatos"). In 1969, Mexican-American high school students staged a major walkout protesting discrimination, an event that drew national attention and led to the formation of La Raza Unida Party under leaders including José Ángel Gutiérrez, making Crystal City a landmark in the Chicano civil rights movement.

Geography and demographics

The city lies along U.S. Highway 83 in semi-arid South Texas, about 50 miles from the Mexican border. The population is overwhelmingly Hispanic or Latino, and the economy remains centred on agriculture and food processing.
