National anthem of Israel

Hatikvah (Hebrew: הַתִּקְוָה, "The Hope") is the national anthem of Israel. Its lyrics are adapted from a poem by Naftali Herz Imber, a Jewish poet from Złoczów (now Zolochiv, Ukraine), then part of the Kingdom of Galicia and Lodomeria in Austria-Hungary. Imber wrote the first version in 1877 while in Iași, Romania.

History

The theme reflects the 2,000-year-old hope of the Jewish people for a free and sovereign Jewish land in the Land of Israel. The anthem was unofficially adopted at the First Zionist Congress in 1897 and formally adopted in 2004, with shortened and modified lyrics referencing the hope of Zion and Jerusalem.

The melody is based on a Moldavian folk song, "Cucuruz cu frunza-n sus", popularised in Italy as "La Mantovana". It was arranged by Samuel Cohen.

Lyrics

The current anthem consists of the first stanza and refrain of Imber's poem "Tikvatenu": "As long as in the heart, within, / A Jewish soul still yearns, / And onward, towards the ends of the east, / An eye still looks toward Zion..."
