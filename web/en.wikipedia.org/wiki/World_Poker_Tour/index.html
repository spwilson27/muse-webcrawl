The World Poker Tour (WPT) is a series of internationally televised poker tournaments, most of them played in the no-limit Texas hold 'em variant. It premiered in the United States in 2003 and is credited — along with televised coverage of the World Series of Poker — with igniting the poker boom of the 2000s.

History

The tour was created by television producer Steven Lipscomb, who founded World Poker Tour Enterprises in 2002. Its first televised season, broadcast on the Travel Channel beginning in March 2003, introduced the use of hole-card cameras — adapted from a European format — allowing viewers to see players' hidden cards and transforming poker into compelling television. Commentators Mike Sexton and Vince Van Patten fronted the broadcasts for many years.

Format

WPT Main Tour events typically carry buy-ins in the range of several thousand dollars, with final tables filmed for television. The season traditionally culminates in the WPT World Championship. The tour later expanded internationally and added lower buy-in circuits such as WPT Prime and WPTDeepStacks.

Player of the Year

Each season the tour awards a Player of the Year title based on a points system across events. Notable champions over the years have included Gus Hansen, Daniel Negreanu, Carlos Mortensen, and Darren Elias, who holds the record for most WPT Main Tour titles.

Ownership

World Poker Tour Enterprises went public in 2004 and has changed hands several times, being acquired by PartyGaming in 2009, Ourgame in 2015, and Element Partners in 2021, after which the company again became publicly traded.
