Ferae

Ferae is a mirorder of placental mammals whose members include the orders Carnivora and Pholidota (pangolins), as well as the extinct orders Creodonta and Cimolesta. Molecular phylogenetic studies have repeatedly supported the grouping of carnivorans and pangolins as sister taxa.

Classification

The name Ferae was introduced by Linnaeus for a grouping of carnivorous mammals, but its modern phylogenetic usage dates to studies in the late 20th century. Within Ferae, the clade Carnivoramorpha unites Carnivora with the extinct viverravids and miacids, while pangolins are grouped with the extinct palaeanodonts in some classifications. Ferae is placed within the superorder Laurasiatheria, and its sister group is generally considered to be the ungulates (clade Fereuungulata).
