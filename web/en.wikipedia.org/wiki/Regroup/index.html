Regroup

Regroup (from re-, "again", and group) is a verb meaning to reorganise into a group or groups after a period of dispersal, disorder, or setback. It is used in military, sporting, business, and general contexts to describe the act of reassembling personnel or elements in order to resume coordinated action.

Military usage

In military usage, to regroup is for units that have become scattered — for example during an advance, a retreat, or after an engagement — to reform at a designated rally point, reorganise their order of battle, redistribute ammunition and supplies, and prepare for further operations. Regrouping differs from retreating in that its purpose is the restoration of cohesion rather than withdrawal from the field.

Other usage

The word is also used figuratively: a sports team may regroup at half-time, a company may regroup after a failed product launch, and a political party may regroup after an electoral defeat, in each case meaning to pause, reorganise, and plan afresh.
