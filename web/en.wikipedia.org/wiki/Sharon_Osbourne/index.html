Sharon Osbourne

Sharon Osbourne
Born: 9 October 1952, London, England
Occupation: Television personality, music manager, author
Spouse: Ozzy Osbourne (m. 1982; died 2025)

Sharon Rachel Osbourne (née Levy, later Arden) is an English television personality, entertainment manager and author. Daughter of music manager Don Arden, she managed her husband Ozzy Osbourne's solo career after his departure from Black Sabbath, founding the Ozzfest touring festival.

Television career

She rose to mainstream fame on MTV's reality show The Osbournes (2002–2005), and later served as a judge on The X Factor UK and America's Got Talent, and as a co-host of CBS's The Talk (2010–2021), leaving after an on-air dispute. She has written autobiographies including Extreme (2005).
