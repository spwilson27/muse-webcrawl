Groundhog Day (film)

Directed byHarold Ramis
StarringBill Murray, Andie MacDowell
Release dateFebruary 12, 1993

Groundhog Day is a 1993 American fantasy comedy film directed by Harold Ramis, written by Ramis and Danny Rubin, and starring Bill Murray and Andie MacDowell. It follows Phil Connors, a cynical television weatherman who finds himself reliving the same day — February 2, Groundhog Day, in Punxsutawney, Pennsylvania — in an endless time loop.

Plot

Sent to cover the annual groundhog ceremony, Phil becomes trapped repeating the day. After indulging in hedonism and despair, he gradually uses the loop to improve himself and help the townspeople, eventually winning the affection of his producer, Rita, and breaking the cycle.

Reception and legacy

The film was a modest box-office success on release but has grown enormously in stature; it is frequently ranked among the greatest comedy films of all time and was selected for preservation in the United States National Film Registry in 2006. Its title has entered the language as a term for a monotonously repeating situation. A stage musical adaptation premiered in 2016.
