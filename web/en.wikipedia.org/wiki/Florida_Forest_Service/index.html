Florida Forest Service

The Florida Forest Service is a division of the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services responsible for managing state forests, preventing and fighting wildfires, and assisting private forest landowners.

Responsibilities

The service protects millions of acres of forestland, conducts prescribed burns, issues burn authorisations, and responds to wildfires with firefighters and equipment including bulldozers and aircraft coordination.

It manages Florida's state forest system of more than 30 forests covering over a million acres, offering recreation such as hiking, camping, and horseback riding.

History

Florida's organised forestry dates to the 1920s with the creation of a state board of forestry; the modern service emphasises both wildfire suppression and the ecological use of prescribed fire.
