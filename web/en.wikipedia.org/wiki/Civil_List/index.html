Civil list

A civil list is a list of individuals to whom money is paid by the government, typically for service to the state or as honorary pensions. In the United Kingdom, the Civil List was the grant of revenues by Parliament to the Sovereign to meet the official expenditure of the Royal Household.

United Kingdom

From 1760, when George III surrendered the income of the Crown Estate, Parliament voted a fixed Civil List payment. The arrangement was replaced in 2012, under the Sovereign Grant Act 2011, by the Sovereign Grant, calculated as a percentage of Crown Estate profits.

Other countries

Similar arrangements exist or existed in other monarchies, where the civil list covers the monarch's official expenses and the costs of the royal household, distinct from the monarch's private income.
