Take My Hand, Precious Lord

GenreGospel hymn
Written1932
WriterThomas A. Dorsey
MelodyAdapted from "Maitland" (George N. Allen, 1844)

"Take My Hand, Precious Lord" (often rendered "Precious Lord, Take My Hand") is a gospel song written by Thomas A. Dorsey in 1932. Dorsey, the "father of gospel music," wrote the words in the depths of grief: his wife Nettie Harper had died in childbirth in August 1932, and their infant son died the following day. The melody is the hymn tune "Maitland" by George N. Allen, also used for "Must Jesus Bear the Cross Alone."

History

Dorsey first performed the song at the Ebenezer Baptist Church in Chicago, where Theodore Frye sang it; it spread quickly through Black churches and gospel publishing networks. It became a signature song of Mahalia Jackson, who performed it around the world and sang it at the request of Martin Luther King Jr. on many occasions. Jackson sang it at King's funeral in 1968; Aretha Franklin also performed it at Jackson's own funeral in 1972.

Recordings and influence

The hymn has been recorded by Elvis Presley (1957, on his Christmas album), Mahalia Jackson, Aretha Franklin, Nina Simone, Clara Ward, B.B. King and many others, and appears in numerous hymnals. It was Martin Luther King Jr.'s favourite song, frequently requested during civil rights movement mass meetings. In 2013, Beyoncé performed it at the Grammy Awards in connection with the film Selma. The song is considered one of the defining works of the gospel tradition and a landmark in American sacred music.
