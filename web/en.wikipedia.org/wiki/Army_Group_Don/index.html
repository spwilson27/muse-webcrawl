Army Group Don (German: Heeresgruppe Don) was a German army group formation on the Eastern Front of World War II, created on 21 November 1942 in response to the Soviet encirclement of the German 6th Army at Stalingrad. It was commanded by Field Marshal Erich von Manstein.

Formation and operations

The army group was formed from the headquarters of the 11th Army and assembled the remnants of Army Group B's southern wing, including the 6th Army (encircled), Fourth Panzer Army, and Romanian armies. Manstein's mission was to restore the front along the Don and Chir rivers and relieve Stalingrad.

Operation Winter Storm

In December 1942 the army group launched Operation Winter Storm, a relief attack by LVII Panzer Corps from Kotelnikovo toward the pocket. The advance stalled roughly 48 km short of Stalingrad, and the Soviet Operation Little Saturn against the Italian 8th Army forced a retreat; the Tatsinskaya raid also destroyed much of the Luftwaffe's airlift capacity. The 6th Army surrendered at the end of January 1943.

Disbandment

On 12 February 1943 Army Group Don merged with the remnants of Army Group B to form the new Army Group South, again under Manstein, which then conducted the counteroffensive that recaptured Kharkov in March 1943.
