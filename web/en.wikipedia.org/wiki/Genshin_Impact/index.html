Genshin Impact is an action role-playing game developed by Chinese studio miHoYo (published outside mainland China by its subsidiary Cognosphere under the HoYoverse brand). Released on 28 September 2020, the game features an anime-style open world, an action-based combat system built around elemental reactions, and gacha monetization through which players obtain new characters and weapons.

Gameplay

Players control the Traveler, a dimension-hopping adventurer searching the world of Teyvat for their lost twin. They assemble parties of four characters, switching between them in real time to combine the game's seven elements — Anemo, Geo, Electro, Dendro, Hydro, Pyro and Cryo — whose interactions (such as Vaporize, Melt and Bloom) multiply damage. The open world supports climbing, swimming and gliding almost without restriction, and the story advances through regions modelled on real cultures: Mondstadt (Germany), Liyue (China), Inazuma (Japan), Sumeru (South Asia/Middle East), Fontaine (France), and Natlan (Latin America), released through regular six-week updates.

Reception and commercial success

Genshin Impact was a global phenomenon on release, grossing over $1 billion on mobile within six months and more than $3 billion in its first year, making it one of the highest-grossing video games ever. It won Best Mobile Game at The Game Awards 2021 and the inaugural Players' Voice category in 2022. Critics praised its world, music and combat while criticising its gacha mechanics and endgame depth. It is credited with mainstreaming Chinese-developed games worldwide and influencing subsequent open-world titles.
