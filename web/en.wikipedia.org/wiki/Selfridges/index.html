Selfridges

Selfridges is a British chain of high-end department stores. Its flagship store on Oxford Street in London, opened in 1909, is the second-largest department store in the United Kingdom (after Harrods) and one of the most famous shops in the world. The business was founded by the American retailer Harry Gordon Selfridge.

History

Harry Gordon Selfridge, a former partner at Marshall Field's in Chicago, invested some £400,000 to build a new store at the then-unfashionable western end of Oxford Street. Selfridges opened on 15 March 1909 and revolutionised British retailing: goods were displayed openly for browsing rather than kept behind counters, departments such as a beauty hall were placed at the front of the store, and the shop offered restaurants, a roof garden, reading and resting rooms, and spectacular window displays. Selfridge popularised the maxim "The customer is always right" in Britain, and the store became a social destination — Louis Blériot's Channel-crossing monoplane was exhibited there in 1909, drawing thousands. John Logie Baird gave early demonstrations of television in the store in the 1920s.

After Selfridge's fall from the company in 1939 amid debts, the chain passed through several owners, including Lewis's and the Sears group, before the Canadian Weston family acquired it in 2003 and revived it as a luxury experiential retailer. In 2021–2022 the Selfridges Group was sold to a joint venture between Thailand's Central Group and Austria's Signa Holding; Signa's subsequent collapse led to restructurings, with Central Group taking majority control.

Stores and reputation

Besides Oxford Street, Selfridges has stores in Manchester (Exchange Square and the Trafford Centre) and Birmingham's Bullring, whose futuristic aluminium-disc façade (2003) became a landmark. The London store has repeatedly been named the world's best department store at industry awards, and is noted for creative window displays and concept spaces such as the Corner Shop. The founder's story was dramatised in the television series Mr Selfridge (2013–2016).
