Capital London is an Independent Local Radio station owned and operated by Global Media & Entertainment, broadcasting to London on 95.8 FM. It forms part of the Capital radio network and is one of the United Kingdom's most listened-to commercial radio stations.

History

The station began broadcasting on 16 October 1973 as Capital Radio, one of Britain's first two legal commercial radio stations, alongside LBC. Initially a general entertainment service, it evolved into a contemporary hit radio format, becoming synonymous with pop music radio in the capital. Its flagship breakfast show has been hosted by a succession of high-profile presenters, including Kenny Everett, Chris Tarrant, Johnny Vaughan, and Roman Kemp.

Programming

Capital London broadcasts chart music and entertainment programming, with local news, travel, and breakfast output for London alongside networked shows shared with other Capital stations. The brand's large events include the Summertime Ball at Wembley Stadium and the Jingle Bell Ball at The O2 Arena.
