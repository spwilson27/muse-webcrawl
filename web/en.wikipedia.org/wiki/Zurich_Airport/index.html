Zurich Airport (IATA: ZRH, ICAO: LSZH) is the largest international airport in Switzerland and the principal hub of Swiss International Air Lines. It is located near Kloten, about 13 kilometres (8 mi) north of central Zurich, within the canton of Zurich, and serves as one of Europe's major connecting hubs.

History

The airport opened in 1948, replacing the Dübendorf airfield as Zurich's commercial airport; the first runways and terminal followed in the late 1940s and early 1950s. It expanded through successive construction phases, including the finger docks of the 1970s and 1980s, the midfield Dock E (2003), and the landside Circle complex (2020). The airport was formerly known as Zurich-Kloten Airport.

Facilities

Zurich Airport has three passenger concourses — the Airside Center with gates A, B and D, and the midfield Dock E — connected by the Skymetro underground people mover. Three runways (2,500 m, 3,300 m and 3,700 m) handle roughly 275,000 movements annually in peak years. THE CIRCLE, opened in 2020 adjacent to the terminals, combines hotels, offices, medical facilities and retail.

Ground transport

Zürich Flughafen railway station beneath the terminal provides frequent trains to Zürich Hauptbahnhof (about 10 minutes) and across Switzerland; trams and regional buses also serve the airport.
