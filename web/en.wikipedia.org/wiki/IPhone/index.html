The iPhone is a line of smartphones developed and marketed by Apple Inc. since 2007. Running Apple's iOS operating system, the first-generation iPhone was announced by Steve Jobs on January 9, 2007, and released that June; he introduced it as an "iPod, a phone, and an Internet communicator" in one device. Its large multi-touch screen, lack of a physical keyboard and desktop-class web browser redefined the smartphone market, and the App Store, launched in 2008, created the modern mobile-app economy. The iPhone is Apple's best-selling product and a major driver of its revenue; current models are sold through the Apple online store.

History

The original iPhone combined a 3.5-inch capacitive touchscreen with a mobile version of Mac OS X. The iPhone 3G (2008) added 3G and the App Store; the iPhone 4 (2010) introduced the Retina display and a glass-and-steel design; the iPhone 5s (2013) added Touch ID fingerprint recognition; the iPhone X (2017) removed the home button in favour of Face ID and an edge-to-edge OLED display. Later generations added 5G (iPhone 12, 2020), the Dynamic Island (iPhone 14 Pro, 2022), USB-C (iPhone 15, 2023, following EU regulation) and Apple Intelligence features. Annual line-ups typically include standard and larger "Plus"/"Max" sizes and higher-end "Pro" models in titanium.

Software

iPhones run iOS, updated annually with free major releases. Software distribution is centred on Apple's App Store, a policy that has drawn antitrust scrutiny in the United States and the European Union, where the Digital Markets Act forced Apple to allow alternative app marketplaces in the EU from 2024.

Reception and impact

The iPhone is credited with popularising the smartphone and mobile internet, displacing dedicated cameras, MP3 players and satellite navigation devices for many users. It is manufactured mainly in China and, in growing volumes, India, by contractors including Foxconn. More than 2.3 billion iPhones had been sold by the mid-2020s, and the installed base of active iPhones exceeds one billion.
