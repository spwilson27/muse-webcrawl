The Best FIFA Men's Player is an association football award presented annually by FIFA to the best men's player in the world, as part of The Best FIFA Football Awards. It was created in 2016 after FIFA ended its partnership with France Football over the Ballon d'Or, reviving a separate FIFA world player honour.

Selection

Winners are chosen by a vote split equally between national team captains, national team coaches, media representatives, and fans voting online, each selecting their top three players.

Winners

The inaugural winner in 2016 was Cristiano Ronaldo, who also won in 2017. Subsequent winners include Luka Modrić (2018), Lionel Messi (2019, 2022, 2023), Robert Lewandowski (2020, 2021), and Vinícius Júnior (2024). Messi is the record holder with three wins in the award's current form.
