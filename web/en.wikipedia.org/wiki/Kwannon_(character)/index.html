Kwannon is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, commonly in association with the X-Men. A Japanese mutant assassin employed by the crime lord Lord Nyoirin and linked to the ninja clan the Hand, Kwannon is best known for the long-running storyline in which her mind was merged and swapped with that of the British telepath Betsy Braddock (Psylocke) — a convoluted arc in which the two women occupied each other's bodies for decades of publication history.

Publication history

Kwannon debuted in X-Men vol. 2 #17 (1993), created by writer Fabian Nicieza and artist Andy Kubert. In the retconned account, the assassin Kwannon was fatally injured and, through the machinations of the villain Spiral and the Hand, her consciousness was combined with Betsy Braddock's, placing Braddock's mind in Kwannon's body while the persona calling herself "Revanche" carried Kwannon's memories in Braddock's original body. Revanche later died of the Legacy Virus. For many years the character in Kwannon's body was simply presented as Psylocke.

As a distinct character

In 2018–2019 storylines, Betsy Braddock was restored to her original body as Captain Britain, and Kwannon — revived in her own body — reclaimed her name and identity. She became a regular in the Krakoan-era X-Men titles, serving on the Hellions team under Mister Sinister and later with the Marauders, exploring her past, her relationship with Greycrow, and her reconciliation with Betsy Braddock. Kwannon retains low-level telepathic and empathic powers, the ability to form psychic blades and a psychic katana, and elite hand-to-hand and sword-fighting skills honed as an elite assassin — once rated among the Hand's finest killers, second only to its grandmaster.
