Naval Base Ventura County

Naval Base Ventura County (NBVC) is a United States Navy base located in Ventura County, California. Formed in 2000, it is a consolidation of three installations: Naval Air Station Point Mugu, Naval Construction Battalion Center Port Hueneme, and Naval Air Station San Nicolas Island. The base covers roughly 4,700 acres and supports a workforce of naval personnel, civilians, and contractors.

History

The Point Mugu site was developed during World War II as an anti-aircraft training center and became a naval air station in 1946, later serving as a major missile test range facility. Port Hueneme, commissioned in 1942, became the home of the Naval Construction Battalions, better known as the Seabees. The command consolidation of October 2000 unified the three sites under a single commanding officer while preserving their distinct missions.

Missions and tenants

NBVC hosts more than 80 tenant commands, including naval air warfare test squadrons, the Sea Range missile-testing complex, fleet logistics centers, and the homeport of the Pacific Seabees. The San Nicolas Island site supports instrumentation and range operations in the Sea Test Range west of Los Angeles.
