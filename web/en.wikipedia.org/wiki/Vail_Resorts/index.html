Vail Resorts


Type: Public (NYSE: MTN)
Headquarters: Broomfield, Colorado, United States
Industry: Hospitality, ski resorts

Vail Resorts, Inc. is an American mountain resort company headquartered in Broomfield, Colorado. It is the largest ski resort operator in the world, running more than 40 resorts in the United States, Canada, Australia and Switzerland, including Vail, Breckenridge, Park City, Whistler Blackcomb and Stowe.

History

The company grew from Vail Mountain, founded in 1962. Through a long series of acquisitions — including Heavenly, Keystone, Crested Butte, and the 2016 purchase of Whistler Blackcomb and 2019 purchase of Peak Resorts — it consolidated much of the North American ski industry.

Epic Pass

In 2008 the company launched the Epic Pass, a season pass granting access to all of its resorts at a price far below the traditional single-resort season pass. The product reshaped the ski industry's economics toward subscription-style advance sales and prompted rival operators to form competing pass alliances such as the Ikon Pass.
