Suspension (punishment)

Suspension is a form of punishment that people in authority impose for violating rules. It involves temporarily removing a person from their position, privilege or activity, in contexts such as schools, workplaces, professional bodies and sports.

In schools

School suspension excludes a student from school for a set number of days, either in-school (the student remains on campus in a supervised setting) or out-of-school. It is used for serious misconduct and sits between detention and expulsion in severity. Critics note that out-of-school suspensions are associated with poorer academic outcomes and are applied disproportionately to some groups of students, prompting some districts to adopt restorative practices instead.

In employment and professions

Employers may suspend an employee, with or without pay, pending investigation of alleged misconduct. Professional licences — for example in medicine, law or sport — may also be suspended by regulatory bodies, temporarily barring the holder from practising. In sport, suspension bars an athlete or official from a specified number of matches or a period of time.
