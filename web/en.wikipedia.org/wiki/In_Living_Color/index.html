In Living Color

In Living Color
Created by: Keenen Ivory Wayans
Original network: Fox
Original run: 1990 – 1994
Seasons: 5

In Living Color is an American sketch comedy television series created by Keenen Ivory Wayans that aired on Fox from April 1990 to May 1994. The show, whose title playfully referenced NBC's slogan "in living color", featured a predominantly Black cast and sharp, hip-hop-influenced comedy at a time when such voices were rare on network television.

Cast and characters

The ensemble included Keenen Ivory Wayans, Damon Wayans, Kim Wayans, Shawn and Marlon Wayans, Jim Carrey, David Alan Grier, Tommy Davidson, T'Keyah Crystal Keymáh and Kelly Coffield, with the Fly Girls dance troupe — among them Jennifer Lopez and choreographer Rosie Perez. Recurring characters included Homey the Clown, Fire Marshal Bill, Wanda, and the reviewers Blaine and Antoine of "Men on Film".

Impact

The series launched numerous comedy careers, won an Emmy for Outstanding Variety Series, and famously prompted the creation of the Super Bowl halftime show format after its live 1992 counter-programming special drew millions of viewers away from the game.
