Inessive case

The inessive case is a locative grammatical case meaning "inside" or "in", generally indicating location within something. It belongs to the system of local cases found in languages such as Finnish, Estonian and Hungarian, where it is distinguished from the elative (out of) and illative (into).

Usage

In Finnish, the inessive is formed with the suffix -ssa/-ssä: talossa, "in the house", from talo, "house". It is one of the six locative cases in Finnish, arranged into internal and external series. In Hungarian the corresponding suffix is -ban/-ben. Estonian forms its inessive with -s, often with stem changes. Besides literal location, the case can have extended meanings, such as expressing time spans in Finnish (for example, "in an hour").
