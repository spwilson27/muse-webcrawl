University of Galway

The University of Galway (Irish: Ollscoil na Gaillimhe) is a public research university in Galway, Ireland. Founded in 1845 as Queen's College Galway, it later became University College Galway and then the National University of Ireland Galway, before adopting its current name in 2022.

History and campus

The university opened in 1849 with its landmark Quadrangle building. Its campus extends along the River Corrib and enrolls around 19,000 students.

Academics

It has colleges of arts, science, business, law, medicine and engineering, with research strengths in biomedical science, marine science, data analytics and Irish studies. It is a bilingual university with a strong commitment to the Irish language.
