Great Mazinger (グレートマジンガー) is a Japanese super robot manga and anime series created by Go Nagai. It is the sequel to Mazinger Z and aired on Fuji TV from September 1974 to September 1975 for 56 episodes. The story introduces a new hero, Tetsuya Tsurugi, who pilots the more powerful Great Mazinger against the Mikene Empire.

Plot

After the defeat of Dr. Hell, a new enemy, the Mikene Empire — an ancient underground civilisation — launches an invasion of the surface led by the Great General of Darkness. Tetsuya, trained from childhood for this battle, pilots Great Mazinger from the Fortress of Science, aided by Jun Honoo in the female robot Venus A. In the finale, Koji Kabuto returns in the repaired Mazinger Z to fight alongside Great Mazinger.

The robot

Great Mazinger is built from Super Alloy Z and stands 25 metres tall. Its signature weapons include the Thunder Break, the Great Typhoon, the Drill Pressure Punch and the Mazinger Blade. It docks with the flying Brain Condor.
