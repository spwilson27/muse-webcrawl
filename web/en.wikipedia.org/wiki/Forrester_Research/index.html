Forrester Research

Forrester Research, Inc. is an American business-to-business research and advisory firm headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts. Founded in 1983 by George F. Colony, the company provides advice on information technology, marketing, and customer experience to business and technology leaders worldwide.

History

Forrester was founded in July 1983 by George F. Colony in Cambridge, Massachusetts. The firm grew out of Colony's interest in the impact of personal computing on large organizations. It went public on the NASDAQ stock exchange in 1996 under the ticker symbol FORR. During the 1990s technology boom, Forrester became known for reports on the internet economy, including coined concepts such as "Techno-Graphics," a demographic model of consumers' technology attitudes.

In the early 2000s the company expanded through acquisitions, including Fletcher Research in the United Kingdom, Giga Information Group, and JupiterResearch (founded by Josh Bernoff, among others). It later acquired SiriusDecisions in 2019, a research firm focused on business-to-business sales, marketing, and product management.

Business model

Forrester sells subscriptions to its research, along with consulting services, events, and advisory programs. Its analysts publish reports evaluating markets and vendors; its best-known evaluation frameworks include the Forrester Wave, a graphical ranking of vendors in a specific market, and the Customer Experience Index (CX Index). Forrester's "Age of the Customer" thesis argues that empowered customers are reshaping competitive strategy.
