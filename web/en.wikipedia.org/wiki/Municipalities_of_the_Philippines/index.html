A municipality (Tagalog: bayan) in the Philippines is a local government unit (LGU) that forms part of the country's hierarchy of provinces, cities, municipalities, and barangays. Municipalities are governed by an elected mayor, vice mayor, and a municipal council (Sangguniang Bayan).

Classification

Municipalities are classified by income into classes, and are further divided into component municipalities and independent component municipalities. A municipality may petition to become a city if it meets population and income requirements under the Local Government Code of 1991.

Government

The municipal mayor serves as chief executive, while the Sangguniang Bayan acts as the legislative body, enacting ordinances and approving budgets. The smallest administrative unit within a municipality is the barangay.

List

There are over 1,400 municipalities in the Philippines. Notable large municipalities include San Jose del Monte (now a city) and Rodriguez in Rizal province.
