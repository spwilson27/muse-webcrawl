Larry Hagman

Larry Martin Hagman (September 21, 1931 – November 23, 2012) was an American actor best known for two of television's most successful roles: the amiable astronaut Major Anthony Nelson in the sitcom I Dream of Jeannie (1965–1970) and the ruthless oil baron J. R. Ewing in the prime-time soap opera Dallas (1978–1991), a role he reprised in the 2012 revival.

Life and career

Hagman was born in Fort Worth, Texas, the son of actress Mary Martin and attorney Benjamin Hagman. After brief work in theatre and service in the U.S. Air Force, he built a career in New York theatre and daytime television before I Dream of Jeannie made him a star opposite Barbara Eden. Dallas made him a global phenomenon: the 1980 "Who shot J. R.?" cliffhanger drew one of the largest television audiences in history, and Hagman became one of the highest-paid actors on television. His films included Fail-Safe (1964), The Eagle Has Landed (1976), Superman (1978), Nixon (1995) and Primary Colors (1998).

Hagman, a longtime heavy drinker, underwent a liver transplant in 1995 and became an outspoken advocate for organ donation and anti-smoking causes. He died in Dallas in 2012 of complications from leukaemia, during production of the second season of the revived Dallas.
