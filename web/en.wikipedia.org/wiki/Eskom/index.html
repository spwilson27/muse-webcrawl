Eskom Holdings SOC Ltd

Founded: 1 March 1923 (as the Electricity Supply Commission)
Headquarters: Megawatt Park, Sandton, South Africa
Owner: Government of South Africa
Industry: Electric utility

Supplies: about 95% of South Africa's electricity; around 40% of Africa's

Eskom Holdings SOC Ltd is a South African electricity public utility, the largest producer of electricity in Africa. Established in 1923 as the Electricity Supply Commission (ESCOM), it is a state-owned company wholly owned by the South African government. Eskom generates, transmits, and distributes electricity to industrial, mining, commercial, agricultural, and residential customers, and operates the national grid.

History

Eskom was founded under the Electricity Act of 1922 and began operating on 1 March 1923, taking over and building power stations to supply the railways, mines, and growing cities. Through the twentieth century it built a fleet dominated by large coal-fired stations in the Mpumalanga coalfields, the Koeberg nuclear power station near Cape Town (commissioned 1984–85, Africa's only commercial nuclear plant), hydroelectric and pumped-storage schemes, and gas turbines. For decades it supplied some of the cheapest electricity in the world.

Power crisis and load shedding

From 2007–08 onward, generation capacity failed to keep up with demand and an ageing fleet suffered declining reliability. Eskom began implementing load shedding — scheduled rolling blackouts by "stages" — to protect the grid from total collapse. The crisis deepened through the 2010s and peaked in severity in 2022–2023, when outages occurred on most days of the year. Two giant new coal stations, Medupi and Kusile, were completed years late and far over budget amid technical defects. Eskom's debt reached roughly R400 billion, and the company was repeatedly implicated in "state capture" corruption under the Zuma administration, investigated by the Zondo Commission.

Under new leadership and an unbundling programme, Eskom has been restructured into generation, transmission, and distribution subsidiaries; the National Transmission Company of South Africa began operating in 2024. Improved plant performance and rapid growth of rooftop solar produced an extended period without load shedding from 2024, though the system's long-term finances and generation mix remain contested.

Operations

Eskom's installed capacity is about 40–45 GW, over 80% of it coal-fired. Major stations include Kendal, Kusile, Medupi, Majuba, Tutuka, and Lethabo. Koeberg provides about 1,860 MW of nuclear capacity. Eskom also imports power, notably from the Cahora Bassa hydro scheme in Mozambique, and exports to neighbouring members of the Southern African Power Pool.
