List of Disney villains

This is a list of Disney villains, the principal antagonists of Walt Disney Animation Studios feature films and related media. The "Disney Villains" franchise markets a core line-up that includes Maleficent, the Evil Queen, Cruella de Vil, Ursula, the Queen of Hearts, Captain Hook, Hades, Scar, Jafar and the Wicked Stepmother.
