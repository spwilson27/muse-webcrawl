Unfinished obelisk

The unfinished obelisk is the largest known ancient Egyptian obelisk, lying as left in the stone quarries of Aswan, Egypt. Commissioned during the New Kingdom — probably under Hatshepsut or Thutmose III, around the 18th Dynasty — it was abandoned when cracks appeared in the granite.

LocationAswan, Egypt
Length~41.75 m
Estimated weight~1,090 tonnes
MaterialRed granite

Description

Had it been completed and erected, the obelisk would have measured about 41.75 metres and weighed over a thousand tonnes, surpassing the Lateran Obelisk. Workers carved it directly from bedrock on three sides using dolerite pounding balls; the underside was never detached.

Significance

The quarry provides rare evidence of ancient Egyptian stone-working techniques. The site is part of the UNESCO World Heritage listing for the monuments of Nubia from Abu Simbel to Philae (1979).
