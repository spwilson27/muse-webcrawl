The Loft is a 2014 American-Belgian erotic thriller film directed by Erik Van Looy. It is a remake of the 2008 Dutch-language Belgian film Loft, which Van Looy also directed. The film stars Karl Urban, James Marsden, Wentworth Miller, Eric Stonestreet, and Matthias Schoenaerts—who reprised his role from the original—alongside Isabel Lucas, Rachael Taylor, and Rhona Mitra.

Plot

Five married friends share ownership of a luxury penthouse loft where they can discreetly meet their lovers and mistresses. The arrangement collapses when the body of a murdered woman is discovered in the apartment, handcuffed to the bed. Since only the five men hold keys, each suspects the others, and through flashbacks and interrogation scenes the group's secrets, betrayals, and manipulations are progressively exposed, culminating in the revelation of who arranged the killing and why.

Production

The remake was filmed in 2011 in New Orleans and Brussels but its release was repeatedly delayed as it changed distributors, eventually opening in the United States in January 2015 through Open Road Films with a limited release. The original Belgian film had been a significant commercial hit in Flanders and was followed by a Dutch remake in 2010.

Reception

The American remake received generally negative reviews from critics, who criticised its convoluted plotting and treatment of its characters, and it grossed about $11 million worldwide. The production is nonetheless notable as one of the more prominent examples of a European director remaking his own film for the American market.
