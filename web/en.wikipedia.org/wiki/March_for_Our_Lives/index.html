March for Our Lives

Date: 24 March 2018 (main march)
Location: Washington, D.C., and worldwide
Cause: Gun control advocacy after the Parkland school shooting
Organisers: Survivors of Marjory Stoneman Douglas High School

March for Our Lives was a student-led demonstration in support of gun control legislation held on 24 March 2018, centred on Washington, D.C., with over 880 sibling events across the United States and around the world. It was organised by survivors of the shooting at Marjory Stoneman Douglas High School in Parkland, Florida, in which 17 people were killed on 14 February 2018.

The march

Hundreds of thousands of demonstrators filled Pennsylvania Avenue; organisers estimated 800,000 in Washington alone, making it one of the largest youth-led protests in American history. Speakers were almost exclusively students and young survivors of gun violence, including Emma González, whose minutes-long silence on stage — the length of the Parkland attack — became an iconic moment.

Organisation and aftermath

The student activists formed the March for Our Lives organisation, campaigned for voter registration through the "Road to Change" tour, and advocateded measures such as universal background checks and an assault weapons ban. A second major march took place on 11 June 2022 following the Uvalde and Buffalo shootings. The movement is credited with helping shift youth voter engagement and state-level gun legislation debates.
