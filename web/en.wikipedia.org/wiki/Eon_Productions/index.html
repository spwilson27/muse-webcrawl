Eon Productions

Eon Productions Ltd, British film production company, founded 1961

Eon Productions Limited is a British film production company that primarily produces the James Bond film series. Formed in 1961 by producers Albert R. "Cubby" Broccoli and Harry Saltzman, the company is based at Pinewood Studios in Buckinghamshire, England. The name "Eon" is sometimes said to be an acronym for "Everything or Nothing", though this origin is disputed.

James Bond films

Eon's first production was Dr. No (1962), starring Sean Connery as Ian Fleming's secret agent James Bond. The series went on to become one of the longest-running and most commercially successful franchises in cinema history, with 25 official films released through No Time to Die (2021). After Saltzman sold his stake to United Artists in 1975, Broccoli assumed sole control; following his death in 1996, production passed to his daughter Barbara Broccoli and stepson Michael G. Wilson. In 2025, Amazon MGM Studios took creative control of the franchise, with Broccoli and Wilson remaining co-owners of the property.

Other productions

Beyond Bond, Eon produced the film Chitty Chitty Bang Bang (1968) — adapted from a story by Ian Fleming and produced by Broccoli — and later non-Bond features such as Film Stars Don't Die in Liverpool (2017) and The Rhythm Section (2020), as well as stage and musical productions through affiliated companies.
