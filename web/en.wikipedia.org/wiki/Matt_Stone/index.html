Matt Stone

Matthew Richard Stone (born May 26, 1971) is an American actor, animator, writer, producer, and composer. He is best known for co-creating the animated television series South Park (1997–present) with his creative partner Trey Parker.

Early life

Stone was born in Houston, Texas, and grew up in the Denver, Colorado area, in Littleton. He attended Heritage High School and studied film and mathematics at the University of Colorado Boulder, where he met Parker. Their student short films led to the animated shorts The Spirit of Christmas (1992 and 1995), which became the basis for South Park.

Career

Stone voices several South Park characters, including Kyle Broflovski, Kenny McCormick, and Butters Stotch. With Parker, he co-wrote and starred in the films Orgazmo (1997), Baseketball (1998), South Park: Bigger, Longer & Uncut (1999), and Team America: World Police (2004). Stone is also a co-creator of the hit Broadway musical The Book of Mormon (2011), which won nine Tony Awards including Best Musical. Along with Parker, he has won multiple Primetime Emmy Awards for South Park.

Personal life

Stone married Angela Howard in 2008; they have two children. He and Parker founded the production company Important Studios.
