The World Weightlifting Championships is an annual competition organised by the International Weightlifting Federation (IWF) to determine world champions in Olympic weightlifting. The first championship was held in London in 1891; a separate women's championship began in 1987, and men's and women's events have been held together since 1991.

Format

Athletes compete in bodyweight categories in two lifts — the snatch and the clean and jerk — with medals awarded for each lift and for the combined total. Category boundaries have been revised many times, most recently in 2018, when ten categories per sex were introduced and world records were reset. In Olympic years the championships were historically not held or were combined with the Olympic tournament (as in 1964, 1968, 1972, 1976, 1980 and 1984, when the Games doubled as world championships).

History

Early championships were dominated by European lifters; the Soviet Union became the leading nation after the Second World War, and China has been the most successful country in recent decades, particularly in the women's events. The championships also serve as a qualification event for the Olympic Games.
