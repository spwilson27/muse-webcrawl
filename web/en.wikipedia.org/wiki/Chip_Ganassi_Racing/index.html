Chip Ganassi Racing (CGR) is an American auto racing organization owned by former driver Chip Ganassi. Founded in 1990, the team has competed in IndyCar, CART/Champ Car, NASCAR, sports car racing, and rallycross, and is one of the most successful teams in North American motorsport. Its cars have won multiple Indianapolis 500s, IndyCar championships, Daytona 500 and Brickyard 400 victories in NASCAR, and overall wins at the 24 Hours of Daytona and the 24 Hours of Le Mans class honors.

History

Founding and CART success

Chip Ganassi, a former Indy car driver whose career was cut short by injury, became a team owner in the 1980s and formed his own CART operation for the 1990 season. The team rose to prominence with Target sponsorship in the mid-1990s: Jimmy Vasser won the 1996 CART championship, and Álex Zanardi won back-to-back titles in 1997 and 1998 with a series of dramatic victories. Juan Pablo Montoya won the 1999 championship and the 2000 Indianapolis 500 with the team.

IndyCar era

Ganassi moved full-time to the Indy Racing League (now the IndyCar Series) in 2003. Scott Dixon won the championship in his first season with the team and went on to become its defining driver, adding titles in 2008, 2013, 2015, 2018, and 2020. Dario Franchitti won three consecutive championships (2009–2011) and two Indianapolis 500s (2010, 2012) with Ganassi. Álex Palou added championships in 2021, 2023, and 2024, and Marcus Ericsson won the 2022 Indianapolis 500 for the team.

NASCAR

Ganassi entered NASCAR in 2001 and later partnered with Felix Sabates. The team won the 2010 Daytona 500 and Brickyard 400 with Jamie McMurray and fielded cars for drivers including Kyle Larson. In 2021 Ganassi sold its NASCAR operation to Trackhouse Racing and exited stock car racing.

Sports cars

In Grand-Am and IMSA competition, Ganassi's Daytona Prototype program with drivers such as Scott Pruett and Memo Rojas won numerous championships and a record string of Rolex 24 at Daytona overall victories. The team later ran Ford GTs in IMSA and the FIA World Endurance Championship, winning its class at Le Mans in 2016, and subsequently fielded Cadillac prototypes in IMSA's GTP class and the WEC Hypercar category.

Championships and major wins

  • Multiple CART/IndyCar drivers' championships (Vasser, Zanardi, Montoya, Dixon, Franchitti, Palou)
  • Indianapolis 500 victories: 2000, 2008, 2010, 2012, 2022
  • Daytona 500 (2010) and Brickyard 400 (2010)
  • Numerous Rolex 24 at Daytona overall wins
  • Le Mans GTE Pro class win (2016)
