Calorie

Calorie
DefinitionUnit of energy; food energy measured in kilocalories (kcal)

[Image: A food label zoomed in on the energy (kcal) line]

The calorie is a unit of energy. In nutrition, the 'calorie' on labels is the kilocalorie (kcal) — the energy to raise a kilogram of water by 1 °C. Food energy comes from carbohydrates and protein (~4 kcal/g), alcohol (7) and fat (9).

Use and limits

Energy balance underlies weight change (Obesity), though hormones, satiety and food quality complicate 'calories in, calories out'. Labels, menu counts and fitness trackers make the calorie the public's default energy currency — imperfect, but serviceable.
