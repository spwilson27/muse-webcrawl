Vladivideos

The Vladivideos are thousands of secretly recorded videotapes made by Vladimiro Montesinos, head of Peru's National Intelligence Service, showing him bribing politicians, judges, military officers, businessmen and media owners during the government of President Alberto Fujimori.

Scandal

Montesinos recorded the meetings in his office as insurance. In September 2000 one tape, showing the bribery of opposition congressman Alberto Kouri to defect to Fujimori's party, was leaked and broadcast. The scandal destroyed the Fujimori government: Montesinos fled, Fujimori announced new elections and then resigned by fax from Japan in November 2000. Roughly 2,700 tapes were eventually recovered.

Aftermath

The videos became a potent symbol of corruption in Peru and key evidence in trials that convicted Montesinos and, in 2009, Fujimori himself. Transcripts and tapes were progressively released to the public.
