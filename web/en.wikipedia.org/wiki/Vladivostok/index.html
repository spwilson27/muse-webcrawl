Vladivostok

Vladivostok is a city and seaport in the Russian Far East, the administrative centre of Primorsky Krai, located on the Golden Horn Bay on the Sea of Japan, near Russia's borders with China and North Korea. It has a population of about 600,000.

Founded in 1860 as a military outpost — its name means 'Lord of the East' — Vladivostok is the home port of the Russian Pacific Fleet and the terminus of the Trans-Siberian Railway, 9,289 kilometres from Moscow. The Russky Bridge (2012), among the world's longest cable-stayed bridges, links it to Russky Island, where Far Eastern Federal University hosted the 2012 APEC summit. The city was closed to foreigners in the Soviet era and remains a strategic stronghold; car imports from Japan shape its streets (most cars are right-hand drive).
