Cristo Rei (Almada)

LocationAlmada, Portugal
Height110 m (incl. pedestal)
Inaugurated17 May 1959
SculptorFrancisco Franco de Sousa

The Sanctuary of Christ the King (Cristo Rei) is a Catholic monument and shrine dedicated to the Sacred Heart of Jesus Christ, overlooking the Tagus River in Almada, Portugal, opposite Lisbon. Its arms-open figure was inspired by Christ the Redeemer in Rio de Janeiro.

History

Cardinal Manuel Gonçalves Cerejeira conceived the monument after visiting Rio in 1934. The Portuguese episcopate vowed in 1940 to build it if Portugal were spared from the Second World War; construction began in 1949 and the statue was inaugurated in 1959.

Description

The statue, designed by sculptor Francisco Franco de Sousa, stands on a trapezoidal pedestal 75 metres high on a cliff 133 metres above the Tagus; the figure of Christ itself is 28 metres tall. An elevator and stairs in the pedestal lead to a viewing platform beneath the statue.
