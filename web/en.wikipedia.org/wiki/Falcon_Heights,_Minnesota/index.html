Falcon Heights, Minnesota

Falcon Heights is a suburb of St. Paul and a city in Ramsey County, Minnesota, United States. The population was about 5,300 at the 2020 census. It is home to the Minnesota State Fairgrounds and the St. Paul campus of the University of Minnesota.

History and features

Falcon Heights was incorporated in 1949 and took its name from a planned 1920s development. The Minnesota State Fair, held for twelve days ending on Labor Day each year, occupies a 322-acre fairgrounds in the city and is one of the largest state fairs in the United States, drawing around two million visitors. The University of Minnesota's St. Paul campus, with its colleges of agricultural and veterinary sciences, also lies partly within the city, as does the Gibbs Museum of Pioneer and Dakotah Life, based on a pioneer farmstead.

Philando Castile shooting

On 6 July 2016, Philando Castile, a 32-year-old African American man, was fatally shot by a St. Anthony police officer during a traffic stop on Larpenteur Avenue in Falcon Heights. His girlfriend livestreamed the aftermath on Facebook, and the incident drew nationwide protests and attention to police use of force. The officer was charged with second-degree manslaughter and acquitted in June 2017.

Geography

The small city, about 2.3 square miles, borders St. Paul to the south, Roseville to the north and east, and Minneapolis (via its Como neighbourhood boundary area) and Lauderdale to the west. Snelling Avenue (State Highway 51) and Larpenteur Avenue are its principal roads.
