Dickinson is an American comedy-drama television series created by Alena Smith for Apple TV+, starring Hailee Steinfeld as the poet Emily Dickinson. A deliberately anachronistic coming-of-age retelling of Dickinson's youth in 1850s Amherst, Massachusetts, the series blends period settings with modern dialogue, music, and sensibilities, and ran for three seasons from 2019 to 2021 as one of the launch titles of Apple TV+.

Premise

The series follows teenage Emily Dickinson, an aspiring writer chafing against the expectations of her prominent family — her stern father Edward, anxious mother Emily Norcross, brother Austin, and sister Lavinia — and against the conventions of a society that discourages female ambition. Central storylines include her intense bond with her best friend and later sister-in-law Sue Gilbert, her flirtations with literary fame, and surreal encounters with a personified Death, played by Wiz Khalifa. Each episode is organised around one of Dickinson's poems, which appear handwritten on screen.

Reception

Dickinson was praised for Steinfeld's performance, its playful tone, and its treatment of the poet's interior life and sexuality, and it won a Peabody Award in 2020. Critics noted its loose relationship to biography — the historical Dickinson's most productive years came later — while crediting the show with bringing her poetry to a young audience. The third and final season, set during the American Civil War, aired in late 2021.
