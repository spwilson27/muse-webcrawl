The Chiemgau is a region in southeastern Bavaria, Germany, between Munich and Salzburg, named after the Chiemsee, the largest lake in Bavaria, which is sometimes called the "Bavarian Sea."

Geography

The region stretches from the foothills of the Chiemgau Alps in the south to the Inn river valley in the north. On the lake's Herrenchiemsee island stands a palace built by King Ludwig II modeled on Versailles; the Frauenchiemsee island hosts a Benedictine convent.

Impact structure

The Chiemgau is also known for a proposed Holocene meteorite impact field around the Tüttensee, a hypothesis that remains debated among geologists.
