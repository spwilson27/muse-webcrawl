Sandia (disambiguation)

Sandia (Spanish for "watermelon") may refer to several places, institutions, and other topics, especially in the American Southwest and in Spanish-speaking regions.

Places

  • Sandia, Texas — an unincorporated community and census-designated place in Jim Wells County, Texas, United States.
  • Sandia, Peru — a town in the Puno Region of Peru, and the capital of Sandia Province.
  • Sandia Province — a province of the Puno Region in Peru.
  • Pueblo of Sandia — a federally recognized tribe of Native American Pueblo people in New Mexico, and their reservation north of Albuquerque.
  • Sandia Mountains — a mountain range in New Mexico, immediately east of Albuquerque, known for the Sandia Peak Tramway.
  • Sandia Heights, New Mexico — a census-designated place in Bernalillo County, New Mexico.

Institutions and organizations

  • Sandia National Laboratories — a United States Department of Energy national laboratory in Albuquerque, New Mexico, operated for nuclear weapons research, energy, and national security science.
  • Sandia Resort & Casino — a casino and resort operated by the Pueblo of Sandia near Albuquerque.

Other uses

  • Sandia Base — a former United States military installation in Albuquerque, now part of Kirtland Air Force Base.
  • Sandia — Spanish word for watermelon (Citrullus lanatus); the Sandia Mountains were named for the watermelon-red colour of their sunset glow (alpenglow).
