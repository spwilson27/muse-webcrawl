Nissin Foods

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia


Cup Noodles, Nissin's best-known product

Nissin Foods Co., Ltd. (日清食品株式会社) is a Japanese food company that specialises in the production and sale of convenience food and instant noodles. It was founded in Osaka in 1948 by Momofuku Ando, the inventor of instant ramen, and is credited with creating both instant noodles and cup noodles, products that transformed eating habits around the world.

History

Ando founded the company as a small family business in Ikeda, Osaka. After years of experimentation in a backyard shed, he launched Chicken Ramen in 1958 — the world's first instant noodle product — using a flash-frying method ("instant oil heat drying") that dehydrated cooked, seasoned noodles for long shelf life. In 1971 Nissin introduced Cup Noodles (Cup Noodle in Japan), packaging the noodles in a waterproof foam cup that could serve as its own cooking vessel, an idea Ando developed after observing American buyers crumbling ramen into cups in the United States.

Products

  • Chicken Ramen (1958)
  • Cup Noodles (1971), sold in dozens of flavours worldwide
  • Top Ramen (sold in the United States)
  • Nissin Demae Iccho, UFO yakisoba, and Donbei udon and soba lines
  • Space Ram and other novelty products developed for astronauts

Legacy

Nissin operates factories and subsidiaries across Asia, Europe, and the Americas, and is listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange. The Cup Noodles Museums in Yokohama and Ikeda commemorate Ando's inventions; global demand for instant noodles now exceeds 100 billion servings a year, a market Nissin created.
