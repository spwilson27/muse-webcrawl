Neverwhere

Neverwhere is an urban fantasy television series and novel by Neil Gaiman. It was first broadcast as a six-part BBC television series in 1996, co-created with Lenny Henry; Gaiman's novelisation was published the same year and has since been adapted for stage and radio.

Plot

Richard Mayhew, an ordinary young Londoner, stops to help an injured girl named Door and is drawn into "London Below", a hidden world beneath the city inhabited by people who have fallen through the cracks of society. London Below is built from literal interpretations of London place names — an earl holds court at Earl's Court, and there is a real angel named Islington. Pursued by the assassins Mr. Croup and Mr. Vandemar, Richard journeys with Door, the Marquis de Carabas and the warrior Hunter to uncover the murder of Door's family.

Reception

The novel became one of Gaiman's best-known works, and he later wrote related stories set in the same world, including the novella How the Marquis Got His Coat Back.
