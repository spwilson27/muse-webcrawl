The Arirang Mass Games, or Arirang Festival, is a mass gymnastics and artistic performance held in the Rungrado May Day Stadium in Pyongyang, North Korea. The Games, named after the Korean folk song "Arirang", are renowned as the largest choreographed spectacle in the world.

Performance

Up to 100,000 participants take part, including gymnasts, dancers, acrobats, and tens of thousands of schoolchildren in the stands who create giant mosaics by flipping coloured cards in precise synchrony. The narrative celebrates North Korean history, the Korean Workers' Party, and the country's leadership.

History

The Games were first staged in 2002 and ran in most years until 2013, resuming in 2018. Guinness World Records recognised the event as the largest gymnastic display.
