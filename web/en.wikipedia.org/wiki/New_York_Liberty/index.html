The New York Liberty are an American professional basketball team based in New York City, playing in the Women's National Basketball Association (WNBA) as part of the league's Eastern Conference. One of the league's eight original franchises, the Liberty won their first WNBA championship in 2024.

History

The Liberty were one of the WNBA's founding teams in 1997 and reached the WNBA Finals four times in the league's first six years (1997, 1999, 2000, 2002) without winning a title, led by players such as Teresa Weatherspoon and Rebecca Lobo.

Championship era

After years of rebuilding, the Liberty assembled a 'superteam' in 2023 with Breanna Stewart, Jonquel Jones and Sabrina Ionescu, reaching the Finals that year. In 2024 they defeated the Minnesota Lynx to win the franchise's first championship and the first major professional basketball title for New York City since 1973.

Venue

Originally based at Madison Square Garden, the team moved to the Prudential Center and Westchester County Center during renovations and ownership changes before settling at Barclays Center in Brooklyn in 2020. Their mascot is Ellie the Elephant.
