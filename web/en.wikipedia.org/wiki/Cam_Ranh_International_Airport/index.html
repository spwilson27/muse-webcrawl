Cam Ranh International Airport

The international terminal at Cam Ranh.

Cam Ranh International Airport (IATA: CXR, ICAO: VVCR) is an airport serving Nha Trang and the province of Khánh Hòa in south-central Vietnam. It is located at Cam Ranh, about 30 km south of Nha Trang, on the site of the former United States military air base at Cam Ranh Bay.

History

The airfield was built by the United States military during the Vietnam War as part of the vast Cam Ranh Bay logistics complex. After 1975 it was used by the Vietnamese People's Air Force and, between 1979 and 2002, by Soviet and later Russian forces under an agreement concerning the Cam Ranh Bay facilities. The airport opened to civil traffic in 2004, replacing the old Nha Trang Airport within the city. An international terminal opened in 2009, and a second, larger international terminal (Terminal 2) together with a second runway were inaugurated in 2018.

Traffic

Cam Ranh became one of Vietnam's busiest airports on the strength of beach tourism to Nha Trang, handling over 8 million passengers in 2019, including large numbers of charter and scheduled flights from Russia, China and South Korea. Domestic services connect it with Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang.
