De facto

De facto (Latin: "in fact") describes practices that exist in reality though not officially recognised by law, contrasted with de jure ("by law").

Usage

A de facto government exercises power without legal legitimacy; a de facto standard dominates through widespread use rather than formal standardisation, e.g. the QWERTY keyboard.

Examples

  • A de facto capital hosts government without official status.
  • Segregation persisted de facto after de jure segregation ended.
  • English is the de facto language of international aviation.
