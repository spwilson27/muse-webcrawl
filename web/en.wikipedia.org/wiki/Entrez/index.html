Entrez

The Entrez Global Query Cross-Database Search System is a federated search engine, or web portal, that allows users to search many discrete health sciences databases at the National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI) website. The name Entrez (a greeting meaning "come in" in French) was chosen to reflect the spirit of welcoming the public to search the NCBI's repository of scientific data.

Databases searched

Entrez provides a single point of access to a wide range of databases, including:

  • PubMed and PubMed Central for biomedical literature
  • Nucleotide and Protein sequence databases
  • Gene, Genome, and taxonomy databases
  • Structure (molecular modelling), SNP, and expression databases such as GEO
  • Bookshelf, for online books and reports

Features

Entrez supports Boolean queries, field-restricted searching, and automatic term mapping against controlled vocabularies such as MeSH. Records in different databases are cross-linked — for example, a journal article in PubMed may link to the sequences it reports and to the gene records describing them. Programmatic access is provided through the Entrez Programming Utilities (E-utilities), a set of server-side programs that allow automated querying and retrieval from scripts and other software.
