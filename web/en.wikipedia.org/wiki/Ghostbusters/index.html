Ghostbusters is a 1984 American supernatural comedy film directed by Ivan Reitman and written by Dan Aykroyd and Harold Ramis. It stars Bill Murray, Aykroyd and Ramis as Peter Venkman, Ray Stantz and Egon Spengler, three parapsychologists who lose their university funding and start a ghost-removal business in New York City, equipped with proton packs and a converted ambulance, the Ecto-1. Sigourney Weaver and Rick Moranis co-star.

Plot

As paranormal activity surges across the city, the Ghostbusters become celebrities, but a EPA official shuts down their containment unit, releasing hundreds of captured ghosts. The ancient god Gozer threatens to enter the world through an apartment building designed by its cult; after a battle involving a giant Stay Puft Marshmallow Man, the team "crosses the streams" of their proton packs to close the portal and save the city.

Reception and legacy

Made for about $30 million, the film grossed over $295 million worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing comedy of its era. Its theme song by Ray Parker Jr. reached number one. It spawned a franchise including the sequel Ghostbusters II (1989), animated series, video games, a 2016 reboot and the sequels Ghostbusters: Afterlife (2021) and Ghostbusters: Frozen Empire (2024). In 2015 it was selected for preservation in the US National Film Registry.
