List of Flash enemies

The various incarnations of the Flash — Jay Garrick, Barry Allen, Wally West and Bart Allen — have faced a distinctive rogues gallery. The villains of Central City famously organised themselves as "the Rogues", a loose brotherhood with an informal code: no killing women or children, and no unnecessary violence. The Flash's deadliest individual foe is generally considered to be Eobard Thawne, the Reverse-Flash.
