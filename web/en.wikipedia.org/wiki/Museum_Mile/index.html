Museum Mile

This article is about the stretch of Fifth Avenue in New York City. For the area in London, see Museum Mile, London.

Museum Mile is the name for a section of Fifth Avenue in the Upper East Side of Manhattan, New York City, running roughly from 82nd to 105th Streets, which contains one of the densest concentrations of museums in the world. The name has also been applied to a similar museum district in London and elsewhere.

Institutions

The New York Museum Mile's institutions include, from south to north: the Metropolitan Museum of Art (82nd Street), the Goethe-Institut, the Neue Galerie New York, the Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum (88th–89th Streets), the National Academy Museum, the Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, the Jewish Museum, the Museum of the City of New York (103rd Street) and El Museo del Barrio (105th Street). The Frick Collection, at 70th Street, is sometimes counted as an honorary member.

Museum Mile Festival

Since 1978 the mile has hosted the annual Museum Mile Festival, usually on the second Tuesday evening in June, when participating museums open free of charge and Fifth Avenue is closed to traffic for a street fair attracting tens of thousands of visitors.
