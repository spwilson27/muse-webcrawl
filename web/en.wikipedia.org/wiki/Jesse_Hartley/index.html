Jesse Hartley

Jesse Hartley (21 December 1780 – 24 August 1860) was a British civil engineer and Superintendent of the Concerns of the Dock Estate of Liverpool, England, from 1824 until his death.

Work in Liverpool

Appointed at the age of 43, Hartley designed and built much of Liverpool's dock system, including the Albert Dock (opened 1846), whose cast-iron columns, warehouses, and hydraulic systems were pioneering. He also built the Stanley Dock, Collingwood Dock, and many of the granite walls and lock systems of the port.

Legacy

Hartley's robust masonry construction and innovations in fireproof dock warehouses made Liverpool's docks among the most advanced in the world, and the Albert Dock remains a celebrated part of the city's waterfront. See also Royal Albert Dock.
