List of Formula One World Drivers' Champions

The Formula One World Drivers' Championship (WDC) is awarded by the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA) to the most successful Formula One driver over the course of a season, determined by a points system based on Grand Prix results. The championship has been awarded since the inaugural season in 1950.

Michael Schumacher and Lewis Hamilton hold the record for most championships, with seven each. Juan Manuel Fangio won five, while Alain Prost and Sebastian Vettel won four apiece.

Records

The youngest world champion is Sebastian Vettel, who won the 2010 title aged 23 years and 134 days. The oldest is Juan Manuel Fangio, who won the 1957 championship aged 46. Schumacher holds the record for the most consecutive championships, with five from 2000 to 2004 with Ferrari. By nationality, drivers from the United Kingdom have won the most titles.
