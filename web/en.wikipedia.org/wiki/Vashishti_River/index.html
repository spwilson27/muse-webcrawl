Vashishti River

The Vashishti River is one of the largest rivers of the Konkan coast of Maharashtra, India. It rises in the Western Ghats near the Koyna region and flows westward about 130 kilometres through Ratnagiri district before meeting the Arabian Sea near Dabhol.

Course

The river is formed by tributaries descending the Sahyadri escarpment. Its principal tributary is the Jagbudi River. The town of Chiplun lies on its banks, and downstream the channel widens into a tidal estuary navigable to Dabhol, an historic port town.

Development

The Koyna Hydroelectric Project diverts water connected with the upper catchment, and the river supports irrigation, fishing, and local transport in its lower reaches. The estuary and creeks around Dabhol are known for mangrove vegetation and migratory birds, and flooding of the monsoon-swollen river periodically affects Chiplun, notably in severe floods such as those of 2005 and 2021.
