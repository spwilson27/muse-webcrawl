Mycetophilidae

The Mycetophilidae are a family of small flies, forming the bulk of those known as fungus gnats. About 3,000 described species are placed in 150 genera, but the true number is thought to be vastly higher.

These generally small, fragile insects, with characteristically humpbacked bodies and long legs, are usually found in damp habitats where their host fungi grow. Larvae typically feed on fungi, decaying plant material, or plant roots, while adults are short-lived and feed, if at all, on nectar.

Ecology

Larvae of some genera, such as Arachnocampa in other families, are bioluminescent. Mycetophilid larvae help decompose wood and leaf litter and are food for many predators.
