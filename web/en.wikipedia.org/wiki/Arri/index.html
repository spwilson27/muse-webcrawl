Arri is a German manufacturer of motion picture film equipment. Founded in Munich in 1917 as Arnold & Richter Cine Technik, the company's name combines the first two letters of its founders' surnames, August Arnold and Robert Richter.

History

The young Arnold and Richter began by building film projectors and lighting equipment and produced their first camera, the Kinarri 35, in 1924. The Arriflex 35, introduced in 1937, was the first production camera with a reflex viewing system using a spinning mirrored shutter, a design that dominated professional cinematography for decades. Subsequent film cameras included the Arriflex 16, 535, 435 and the lightweight Arricam series, made with Moviecam.

Digital era

In 2010 Arri launched the Alexa digital camera system, which became the industry standard for feature films, television drama and commercials, valued for its image quality, dynamic range and reliability. The Alexa line evolved through models including the Alexa XT, SXT, LF (large format), Mini LF and Alexa 35, complemented by the affordable Amira. Arri also makes the SkyPanel LED lighting range, lenses (including Signature Primes), camera stabilisers and the Orbiter luminaire, and offers rental services through Arri Rental.

Recognition

Arri has received numerous Scientific and Technical Awards from the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences — more than any other company — for its camera, lighting and digital technology.
