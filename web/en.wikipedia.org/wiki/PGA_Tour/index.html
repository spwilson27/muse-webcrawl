The PGA Tour is the principal organiser of professional golf tournaments in the United States and North America, and the world's richest golf circuit. It runs the main weekly tour, the developmental Korn Ferry Tour and the over-50 PGA Tour Champions, and organises the Presidents Cup. Its season builds to the FedEx Cup Playoffs each August; its flagship event, The Players Championship at TPC Sawgrass, is often called the unofficial fifth major. Membership is earned through qualifying and the Korn Ferry Tour, and the tour's all-time wins list is headed by Sam Snead and Tiger Woods (82 wins each), ahead of Jack Nicklaus (73).

History and the LIV era

The touring professionals split from the PGA of America in 1968 to run their own circuit, which grew with television through the Palmer, Nicklaus and Woods eras. In 2022 the Saudi-backed LIV Golf league lured away leading players with enormous contracts, splitting the men's professional game; a framework agreement between the PGA Tour and the Saudi Public Investment Fund was announced in June 2023, but a final settlement proved elusive and negotiations continued amid a strategic investment in the tour by American sports owners. The tour's counterpart in Europe is the DP World Tour, with which it maintains a strategic alliance.
