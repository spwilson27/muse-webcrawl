Super Mario Maker is a 2015 creation-and-platform video game developed and published by Nintendo for the Wii U, released in September 2015 to mark the 30th anniversary of Super Mario Bros. It allows players to design their own side-scrolling Mario courses using assets from several games in the series, play them, and — in the Wii U version — share them online for others to attempt.

Gameplay

Courses are built on the Wii U GamePad's touchscreen using visual styles based on Super Mario Bros., Super Mario Bros. 3, Super Mario World and New Super Mario Bros. U, across themes such as ground, underground, underwater, ghost house, airship and castle. Players place blocks, pipes, enemies and power-ups, can combine elements — stacking enemies, enlarging them with Super Mushrooms, or giving them wings — and must complete their own course from start to finish before uploading it, a requirement that guarantees every shared level is beatable. Online modes included the 100 Mario Challenge, in which players tackle randomly selected user courses with a limited stock of lives.

Reception and legacy

The game was widely praised for its intuitive tools and the creativity of its community, selling over four million copies on Wii U. A Nintendo 3DS port followed in 2016 without online course sharing. A sequel, Super Mario Maker 2, was released for Nintendo Switch in 2019, adding slopes, new themes and a full story mode. The Wii U version's online services were discontinued in April 2024, ending access to its shared courses.
