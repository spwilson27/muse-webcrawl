The Docklands Light Railway (DLR) is an automated light metro system serving the redeveloped Docklands area of London and Greenwich. It opened in 1987 to support regeneration of the former docklands, including the Canary Wharf financial district.

History

The original network ran from Stratford and Tower Gateway to Island Gardens on the Isle of Dogs, largely reusing former railway alignments and elevated viaducts. Extensions followed to Bank (1991), Beckton (1994), Lewisham (1999), London City Airport (2005), Woolwich Arsenal (2009) and Stratford International (2011). The line was extended and re-routed as Canary Wharf grew, with Heron Quays and Canary Wharf stations among the busiest.

Operation

Trains are fully automated and driverless, controlled from a control centre at Poplar, though each train carries a Passenger Service Agent who can operate it manually. The system uses two- and three-car articulated vehicles, standard gauge track and third-rail electrification at 750 V DC. It is part of Transport for London and integrates with the Oyster and contactless payment systems.
