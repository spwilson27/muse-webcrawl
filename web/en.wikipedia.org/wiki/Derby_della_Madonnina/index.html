The Derby della Madonnina, also known as the Derby di Milano (Milan Derby), is a football match between the two prominent Milanese clubs AC Milan and Inter Milan. It is called Derby della Madonnina in honour of one of the city's main sights, the statue of the Virgin Mary (the Madonnina) atop the Duomo di Milano.

Unlike many derbies rooted in geography, religion, or class, the Milan derby is a rivalry between two clubs that share the same stadium, the San Siro, and split from a single ancestor: Inter was founded in 1908 by dissident members of Milan who insisted on allowing foreign players.

History

The clubs first met on 18 October 1908 in Chiasso, Switzerland, in a friendly won 2–1 by Milan. For much of the early 20th century Milan was associated with the working class (nicknamed casciavit, "screwdrivers") and Inter with the bourgeoisie (baùscia, "braggers"), though these class identities have since blurred. The rivalry intensified in the 1960s, when Helenio Herrera's Inter and Nereo Rocco's Milan both conquered Europe, and again in the late 1980s and 2000s.

Notable matches

The clubs met in the 2002–03 UEFA Champions League semi-finals; Milan advanced on away goals after a 1–1 aggregate draw — with both "away" legs played in the same stadium — and went on to win the trophy. They met again in the 2004–05 quarter-finals, whose second leg was abandoned after flares thrown by Inter fans struck Milan goalkeeper Dida. In the 2022–23 Champions League semi-finals Inter won 3–0 on aggregate. A famous league encounter in 2001 ended 6–0 to Milan; in 2009 Inter won 4–0.

Supporters and culture

Milan's Curva Sud and Inter's Curva Nord stage elaborate choreographed displays (coreografie) that are a celebrated feature of the fixture. The derby is regularly listed among the world's great football rivalries.

Statistics

The clubs have met well over 240 times in official competition. Inter has generally held the edge in total wins and in Serie A meetings; both clubs have won 19 league titles each as of the mid-2020s — Inter's 20th scudetto, clinched in a derby win in April 2024, put them ahead — and Milan leads in European Cups, with seven to Inter's three.
