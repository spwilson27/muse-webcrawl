YouTube Gaming

YouTube Gaming was a video gaming-oriented service and app by YouTube, launched on 26 August 2015 as a competitor to Amazon's Twitch. The standalone app and website provided a curated front-end for gaming content on YouTube, including live streams and video on demand, with a darker interface and dedicated pages for more than 25,000 games. The standalone app was retired on 30 May 2019, and the gaming destination was folded back into the main YouTube site.

History

YouTube had attempted to acquire Twitch in 2014 before Amazon purchased it for about US$970 million, and the launch of YouTube Gaming the following year was widely seen as a response. The service introduced features aimed at streamers, including 60 frames-per-second streams, DVR mode for broadcasts, and channel memberships (originally "sponsorships"). YouTube also signed prominent creators to exclusive deals and streamed esports events such as the Overwatch League and Call of Duty League beginning in 2020.

Shutdown

In September 2018 YouTube announced it would retire the standalone Gaming app in favour of a gaming hub on the main site, noting that most users watched gaming content on YouTube itself rather than in the app. The app was removed from stores in early 2019 and shut down entirely on 30 May 2019. Gaming remains one of the largest content categories on YouTube, and many of the app's features, such as game pages and live chat, continue in integrated form.
