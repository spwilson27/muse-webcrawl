Gemini 6A

Gemini 6A (officially Gemini VI-A) was a 1965 crewed United States spaceflight in NASA's Gemini program. It achieved the first crewed rendezvous in space, meeting its sister craft Gemini 7 in Earth orbit.

CrewWally Schirra, Thomas P. Stafford
Launch15 December 1965
Landing16 December 1965
Orbits16

Mission

The original mission, planned for October 1965, was cancelled after its Agena target vehicle failed during launch. The rescheduled flight rendezvoused with the long-duration Gemini 7 (Frank Borman and Jim Lovell), coming within about 30 cm of the other spacecraft and station-keeping for several orbits. Commanded by Wally Schirra with pilot Thomas Stafford, Gemini 6A splashed down in the Atlantic on 16 December 1965.
