Miracle of Lanciano

The reliquary of the miracle, Church of San Francesco, Lanciano

The Miracle of Lanciano is a Eucharistic miracle said to have occurred in the eighth century in Lanciano, Italy. According to tradition, a Basilian monk who doubted the real presence of Christ in the Eucharist found, during Mass, that the consecrated host had changed into flesh and the wine into blood.

Relics

The relics — a piece of flesh and coagulated blood — are preserved in the Church of San Francesco in Lanciano. Scientific examinations carried out in 1970–71 by Professor Odoardo Linoli concluded that the flesh is human cardiac tissue and the blood is of type AB, findings frequently cited by Catholic commentators, though the study's methods and interpretation have been questioned by sceptics.
