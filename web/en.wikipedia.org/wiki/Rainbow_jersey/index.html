Rainbow jersey

The rainbow jersey of the UCI world champion.

The rainbow jersey is the distinctive cycling jersey worn by the reigning world champion in each cycling discipline governed by the Union Cycliste Internationale (UCI). It is a white jersey bearing five horizontal coloured bands — blue, red, black, yellow, and green — around the chest, representing the colours of the Olympic rings.

Rules and traditions

The champion is entitled, and in road racing effectively required, to wear the jersey in races of the discipline, category, and format in which the title was won, for the year following the victory, until the next world championships. Former champions may wear rainbow piping on the collar and cuffs of their regular jerseys for the rest of their careers. The jersey is awarded across road, track, mountain bike, cyclo-cross, BMX, and para-cycling disciplines.

"Curse of the rainbow jersey"

Folklore speaks of a "curse" in which champions suffer misfortune or poor results in their year in the jersey; statistically, champions do tend to win less the following season, partly because they become heavily marked riders and face heightened expectations. Notable wearers include Eddy Merckx, and in the modern era riders such as Peter Sagan, who won three consecutive road race titles (2015–2017). See also UCI World Championships and cycling jersey.
