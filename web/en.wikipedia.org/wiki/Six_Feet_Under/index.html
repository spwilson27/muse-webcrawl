Six Feet Under

Six Feet Under is an American drama television series created by Alan Ball that aired on HBO from June 3, 2001 to August 21, 2005, running for five seasons and 63 episodes. It is regarded as one of the greatest television series of all time, and its series finale is frequently cited among the best ever made.

Premise

The series centres on the Fisher family, who run a funeral home in Los Angeles: widowed Ruth Fisher and her children Nate, David and Claire, along with funeral director Federico Diaz. Each episode traditionally opens with a death, whose circumstances set the episode's themes in motion as the family confronts mortality and their own lives.

Cast and acclaim

The ensemble includes Peter Krause, Michael C. Hall, Frances Conroy, Lauren Ambrose, Freddy Rodriguez, Mathew St. Patrick, Jeremy Sisto and Rachel Griffiths. The show won numerous Emmy and Golden Globe awards, praised for its writing, acting and honest treatment of death and grief.

The title is also shared by an unrelated Swedish death metal band and other works; this article concerns the HBO series.
