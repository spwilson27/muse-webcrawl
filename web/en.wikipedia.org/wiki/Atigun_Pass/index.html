Atigun Pass

Atigun Pass
Elevation: 4,739 ft (1,444 m)
Range: Brooks Range
Location: North Slope Borough, Alaska, US
Route: Dalton Highway

Atigun Pass (elevation 4,739 feet, 1,444 m) is a high mountain pass across the Brooks Range in the U.S. state of Alaska. It is the highest year-round pass in Alaska and the highest point on the Dalton Highway, the road that runs from Fairbanks to Prudhoe Bay on the Arctic Ocean coast.

The pass is the only road crossing of the Brooks Range and carries both the Dalton Highway and, alongside it, the Trans-Alaska Pipeline, which transports crude oil from the Prudhoe Bay Oil Field to the port of Valdez. It marks the continental divide: rivers north of the pass flow to the Arctic Ocean, while those to the south drain into the Yukon River system.

Conditions

Atigun Pass is notorious among the truckers who haul freight to the North Slope oil fields for its steep grades (up to 12 percent), avalanches, whiteouts, and extreme winter cold. The pass and the drivers who cross it were featured in the television series Ice Road Truckers. At the summit, temperatures can fall far below −40 °F (−40 °C) in winter, and the road over the pass is kept open year-round by state maintenance crews.

Geography

The pass lies in the Endicott Mountains of the central Brooks Range, in the Arctic National Wildlife Refuge's vicinity to the east and the Gates of the Arctic National Park to the west. The area is tundra above the tree line, home to Dall sheep, grizzly bears, and caribou.
