The Nutcracker

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
[Image: The Land of Sweets: the Sugar Plum Fairy and her Cavalier in a glittering set of candy columns and falling snow]

The Nutcracker (Russian: Shchelkunchik) is an 1892 two-act classical ballet by Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky, set on Christmas Eve at the foot of a giant Christmas tree in a German home. The ballet, with music widely regarded as some of the finest ever written for the stage, is now the most performed ballet in the world, especially at Christmas.

Adapted from E. T. A. Hoffmann's story via Dumas, and choreographed by Marius Petipa and Lev Ivanov, it follows Clara (Marie) and her nutcracker-turned-prince through a battle with the Mouse King to the Land of Sweets, ruled by the Sugar Plum Fairy — whose dance introduced the celesta to orchestral music. Tchaikovsky extracted an orchestral suite that became a concert favourite.
