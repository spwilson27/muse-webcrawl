Sexual Offences Act 2003

Sexual Offences Act 2003
Parliament of the United Kingdom
Royal assent: 20 November 2003
Commenced: 1 May 2004

The Sexual Offences Act 2003 is an Act of the Parliament of the United Kingdom that comprehensively reformed the law of sexual offences in England and Wales, replacing the Sexual Offences Act 1956.

Provisions

The Act redefined rape — extending it to oral penetration — and created offences of assault by penetration, sexual assault, and causing sexual activity without consent. It set out a statutory definition of consent and evidential and conclusive presumptions about it, created specific offences protecting children and vulnerable people, addressed grooming, trafficking and voyeurism, and reformed the notification requirements for offenders (the "sex offenders register").
