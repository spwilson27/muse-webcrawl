Wolf & Bear
Origin: Sacramento, California, U.S.
Genres: Post-hardcore, math rock, progressive rock
Years active: 2014–present

Wolf & Bear is an American post-hardcore band from Sacramento, California, formed in 2014. The band is associated with the "swancore" scene surrounding Blue Swan Records and is known for combining melodic clean vocals, harsh screams, and intricate, technically demanding guitar work.

History

The band released early singles and EPs beginning in 2014 before issuing their debut full-length album Everything Is Going Grey in 2017. They followed it with EP II and extensive touring alongside other bands in the progressive post-hardcore scene. Their second studio album, Bloodletter, was released in 2023.

Style

Wolf & Bear's sound mixes post-hardcore with elements of math rock, funk and progressive rock, characterised by tapping-heavy dual guitar lines, shifting time signatures and the contrast between the band's clean vocalist and screamer.
