Ancien Régime

The Ancien Régime (French for "old regime") was the political and social system of the Kingdom of France from roughly the late Middle Ages (c. 1500) until the French Revolution of 1789. The term, popularized by Alexis de Tocqueville in The Old Regime and the Revolution (1856), denotes the order swept away by the Revolution: absolute monarchy, a society of legally distinct orders, and a patchwork of privileges and customary laws.

Versailles, the court of Louis XIV and symbol of the Ancien Régime

Society of orders

French society was formally divided into three Estates: the First Estate (clergy), the Second Estate (nobility), and the Third Estate (everyone else, from wealthy bourgeois to peasants — some 97 percent of the population). The privileged orders enjoyed exemptions from the principal direct tax, the taille, while the Third Estate bore most of the fiscal burden, along with feudal dues, tithes, and the corvée. Venality of office — the sale of state positions, often conferring nobility — blurred and complicated the hierarchy.

Government

The king ruled in principle by divine right, and from Louis XIV onward the monarchy pursued administrative centralization through intendants sent into the provinces. Yet France remained a mosaic of provinces with different laws (customary law in the north, Roman-influenced written law in the south), internal customs barriers, and powerful intermediary bodies such as the parlements, which could obstruct royal reform.

Crisis and collapse

By the 1780s the regime faced fiscal bankruptcy driven by war debts, including support for the American Revolution, and repeated reform attempts by ministers such as Turgot, Necker, and Calonne failed against privileged resistance. In 1789 Louis XVI summoned the Estates General for the first time since 1614; the Third Estate's defiance, the Tennis Court Oath, and the storming of the Bastille set the Revolution in motion. On the night of 4 August 1789 the National Assembly abolished feudal privilege, formally ending the Ancien Régime.
