The first season of the South Korean dystopian survival thriller television series Squid Game was released worldwide on Netflix on September 17, 2021. Created, written and directed by Hwang Dong-hyuk, the season consists of nine episodes.

Premise

Seong Gi-hun (Lee Jung-jae), a divorced, debt-ridden chauffeur, is invited to play a series of children's games for a chance at a prize of ₩45.6 billion. He joins 455 other financially desperate players at a secret island facility, where masked guards in pink jumpsuits oversee the games — and eliminated players are killed. Among the players are Gi-hun's childhood friend Cho Sang-woo, North Korean defector Kang Sae-byeok, elderly Oh Il-nam, and gangster Jang Deok-su. Police officer Hwang Jun-ho infiltrates the facility disguised as a guard, searching for his missing brother.

Episodes and games

The contests adapt Korean children's games: "Red Light, Green Light"; the dalgona (honeycomb candy) challenge; a nighttime riot and tug of war; marbles; the glass stepping-stone bridge; and a final squid game between Gi-hun and Sang-woo. The season ends with Gi-hun winning the prize but traumatised, learning that Il-nam was the game's creator, and deciding not to board a plane to visit his daughter.

Reception

The season became Netflix's most-watched series launch at the time, reaching number one in more than 90 countries. It won multiple awards, including the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama Series for Lee Jung-jae — the first for a non-English-language performance — and Outstanding Directing for Hwang. Commentary focused on its critique of capitalism, debt and inequality.
