Tony Scott

Anthony David Leighton Scott (21 June 1944 – 19 August 2012) was an English film director and producer. He was known for his highly stylised action films, including Top Gun (1986), Beverly Hills Cop II (1987), Days of Thunder (1990), The Last Boy Scout (1991), True Romance (1993), Crimson Tide (1995), Enemy of the State (1998), Man on Fire (2004), Déjà Vu (2006), and Unstoppable (2010).

Scott was the younger brother of director Ridley Scott; both began in advertising through their company Ridley Scott Associates before moving into features. Tony Scott's debut feature was the stylish vampire film The Hunger (1983), and Top Gun made him one of Hollywood's most successful action directors.

Style and death

His films are marked by saturated colour, frenetic editing, kinetic camera movement, and atmospheric smoke and backlight, a look that influenced a generation of action and music-video directors. He frequently collaborated with actors Denzel Washington and Tom Cruise. Scott died on 19 August 2012 in Los Angeles.
