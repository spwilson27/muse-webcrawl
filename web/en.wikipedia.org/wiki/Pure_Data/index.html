Pure Data (Pd) is a visual programming language developed by Miller Puckette in the 1990s for creating interactive computer music and multimedia works. It is free and open-source software, released under a BSD license, and maintained by Puckette together with a worldwide community of contributors. Pd is closely related to Puckette's earlier Max/MSP system, developed at IRCAM, but was rebuilt as an open environment that anyone could extend.

The patching model

Programs in Pd are called patches. The user places boxes — objects that perform operations such as generating oscillators (osc~), arithmetic, filtering or reading files — on a canvas and connects them with patch cords. Two kinds of data flow through the cords: messages (control events, numbers and symbols) and audio signals, computed in blocks (by default 64 samples). Graphical interface elements such as sliders, number boxes and toggles can be placed in patches, and patches themselves can be reused as abstractions or subpatches. Pd's "data structures" feature allows visualisation and editing of graphical scores and other structured data.

Extensions and variants

The base distribution ("vanilla" Pd) is deliberately lean; libraries of externals add synthesis methods, video processing (via the GEM environment), physical modelling, sensor interfaces and networking. Notable variants and derivatives include Pd-extended (a long-lived bundled distribution), Purr Data, and Pd forks for embedded hardware. Through libpd, the Pd engine can be embedded in other applications, games and mobile apps, and tools such as Camomile and Heavy compile patches into audio plug-ins.

Uses

Pure Data is widely used in electronic and electroacoustic music, sound design, interactive installations, live coding, digital instrument building and education. Because it runs on inexpensive hardware such as the Raspberry Pi, it is popular for standalone sound installations and DIY instruments. Its community maintains extensive tutorial material, and the annual Pd Convention has been held in cities around the world.
