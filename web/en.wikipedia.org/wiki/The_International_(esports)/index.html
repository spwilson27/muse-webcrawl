The International is an annual esports world championship tournament for the video game Dota 2, organised by the game's developer, Valve. First held in 2011 at Gamescom in Cologne to promote the then-unreleased game, the tournament became famous for its prize pools, which from 2013 were crowdfunded through in-game purchases and grew to be the largest in esports history.

History

The inaugural tournament in 2011 offered a then-unprecedented $1.6 million prize pool and was won by the Ukrainian team Natus Vincere. The event moved to Seattle, where it was held at KeyArena for several years, and has since been staged in Vancouver, Shanghai, Stockholm (held in Bucharest in 2021), Singapore, and other cities. The prize pool peaked at over $40 million for The International 10 in 2021, won by Team Spirit.

Format

Teams qualify through regional leagues and the Dota Pro Circuit (and, after its discontinuation, through regional qualifiers and direct invites). The main event features a group stage followed by a double-elimination playoff bracket. The winning team is awarded the Aegis of Champions, a large shield-shaped trophy on which the names of champions are engraved.
