Queen & Country

Queen & Country is an espionage comic book series written by Greg Rucka and published by Oni Press from 2001 to 2007. It follows Tara Chace, an operative in the Special Operations Section ("the Minders") of Britain's Secret Intelligence Service (MI6).

Overview

The series is known for its grounded, procedural depiction of intelligence work, emphasising bureaucracy, inter-agency rivalry, and the psychological cost of operations rather than glamour. Artists on the series included Steve Rolston, Brian Hurtt, Christine Norrie, and Leandro Fernández. It ran for 32 issues, collected in several trade paperbacks and definitive editions.

Related works

Rucka also wrote three prose novels featuring Tara Chace: A Gentleman's Game (2004), Private Wars (2005), and The Last Run (2010). The series won the Eisner Award for Best New Series in 2002. A later comics revival, Queen & Country: Declassified, presented stories by other writers.
