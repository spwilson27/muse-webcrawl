W Hotels is a luxury hotel chain owned by Marriott International that is marketed toward a younger clientele. Founded in 1998 by Starwood Hotels and Resorts, the brand debuted with the W New York in a converted building on Lexington Avenue in Manhattan, and became known for a design-led, nightlife-inflected style with dark lobbies, electronic music and "Whatever/Whenever" service.

W properties emphasise contemporary design and a social, lounge-like atmosphere.

History

The brand expanded rapidly through the 2000s to cities including Los Angeles, Chicago, Barcelona, Bali, Dubai and Hong Kong, and pioneered the "hotel as lifestyle brand" model later adopted by competitors. Starwood, including the W brand, was acquired by Marriott International in 2016 in a deal creating the world's largest hotel company.

Properties and concept

W hotels typically feature a "Living Room" in place of a traditional lobby, a destination bar, a Bliss or Away spa, and FIT gym facilities. The brand operates several dozen properties across North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, the Middle East and Latin America.
