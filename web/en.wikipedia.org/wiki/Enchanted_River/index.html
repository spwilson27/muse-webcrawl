Enchanted River

The Enchanted River, also known as the Hinatuan Enchanted River, is a deep spring river on the island of Mindanao in the Philippines. It flows into the Philippine Sea at Barangay Talisay, Hinatuan, Surigao del Sur. The river earned its name from its clear, seemingly bottomless blue waters and the local legends surrounding it.

Description

The river emerges from an underground spring system; its exact source has not been fully explored despite several diving expeditions into its cave system, some of which ended in fatalities. The water displays striking shades of blue and green, and the site is surrounded by lush forest. Management of the area was taken over by the local government, and swimming in the main pool was restricted to protect the ecosystem, with designated swimming areas downstream.
