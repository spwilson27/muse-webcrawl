Astrological aspect
Major aspects: Conjunction (0°), Sextile (60°), Square (90°), Trine (120°), Opposition (180°)
Allowance: Orb

In astrology, an aspect is an angle that planets make to each other in the horoscope, measured by the ecliptic longitude difference between them. Aspects are held to indicate timing of events and to describe the harmony or tension between the planetary influences involved; the five major or "Ptolemaic" aspects date from Hellenistic astrology.

Major aspects

A conjunction (0°) blends two planets' significations; an opposition (180°) is associated with polarity and tension; a trine (120°) is considered harmonious; a square (90°) challenging; and a sextile (60°) moderately favourable. Minor aspects such as the quincunx (150°) and semisextile (30°) were developed later.

Orbs

An aspect need not be exact: the permitted deviation is called the orb, varying by planet and aspect, with wider orbs allowed for the Sun and Moon.
