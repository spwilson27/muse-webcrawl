Quarter rest

A quarter rest (British: crotchet rest) is a symbol in musical notation indicating a silence lasting for the duration of one quarter note (crotchet) — one beat in 4/4 time. It is one of the standard rests of Western notation.

Appearance

The modern quarter rest is a distinctive squiggle, sometimes described as a stylized "Z" combined with a "C", drawn vertically in the middle of the staff. Before about 1850, printed music often used a mirrored eighth rest instead. The Unicode character is U+1D13D (𝄽), in the Musical Symbols block.

Use

Like other rests, the quarter rest may be dotted to extend its length by half. A whole measure of silence in most time signatures is conventionally written with a whole rest rather than a series of quarter rests.
