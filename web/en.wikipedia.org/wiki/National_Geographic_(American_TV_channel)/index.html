National Geographic (formerly the National Geographic Channel, sometimes abbreviated Nat Geo) is an American pay television network and flagship channel of National Geographic Partners. The channel broadcasts non-fiction programming related to nature, science, culture, history and adventure.

History

The channel launched in the United States on January 1, 2001, as a joint venture between the National Geographic Society and Fox Cable Networks. International versions had debuted earlier in Europe and Australia in the late 1990s. In 2015 the media properties were reorganized under National Geographic Partners, and after Disney's acquisition of 21st Century Fox in 2019, Disney became the majority owner.

Programming

Well-known series on the channel include documentaries and reality formats such as Life Below Zero, Wicked Tuna, Air Crash Investigation (broadcast internationally), and scripted anthology series like Genius. The brand's yellow border rectangle logo echoes the cover design of National Geographic magazine.
