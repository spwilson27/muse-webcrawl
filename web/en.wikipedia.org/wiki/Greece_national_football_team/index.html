The Greece national football team represents Greece in men's international football and is controlled by the Hellenic Football Federation, the governing body for football in Greece. Greece is one of only ten national teams to have won the UEFA European Championship.

Euro 2004 triumph

Greece's greatest achievement came at UEFA Euro 2004 in Portugal, where Otto Rehhagel's defensively disciplined side, huge underdogs, defeated hosts Portugal twice — in the opening match and again 1–0 in the final, with Angelos Charisteas scoring the winner — as well as France and the Czech Republic in the knockout rounds. Captain Theodoros Zagorakis was named Player of the Tournament. The victory is regarded as one of the greatest upsets in football history.

History

Greece first competed at a World Cup in 1994 and returned in 2010 and 2014, reaching the round of 16 in 2014 for the first time before losing to Costa Rica on penalties. The team also won the 1990s revival under coach Alketas Panagoulias and has produced players such as Giorgos Karagounis, Angelos Charisteas, and Kostas Mitroglou.
