Drainage basins of South Africa

South Africa's rivers are organised into drainage basins grouped by the Department of Water and Sanitation into primary drainage regions lettered A to X, covering systems flowing to the Atlantic, the Indian Ocean and inland.

Major systems

The Orange River basin (region D) is by far the largest, draining much of the interior to the Atlantic. The Limpopo (A) flows northeast to Mozambique, while the Tugela, Umgeni and other KwaZulu-Natal rivers run short, steep courses to the Indian Ocean.

Management

Water management areas and catchment agencies administer transfers between basins, notably the Lesotho Highlands Water Project feeding the Vaal system supplying Gauteng.
