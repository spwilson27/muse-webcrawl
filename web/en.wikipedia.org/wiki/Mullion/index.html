Mullion

A mullion is a vertical element that forms a division between units of a window, door, or screen, or is used decoratively. In windows, mullions separate individual panes of glass in a multi-light window and provide structural support, especially in large Gothic church windows where stone mullions carry tracery.

Historically, stone mullions were characteristic of Gothic and Tudor architecture, while in modern construction mullions of aluminium, steel, wood or uPVC divide curtain walls and glazed façades into sections. The horizontal counterpart of a mullion is the transom. The term is also applied to the vertical divisions between panels in panelling and furniture.
