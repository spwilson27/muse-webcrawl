Inland Empire

The Inland Empire is a metropolitan area and region in Southern California, centered on San Bernardino and Riverside counties. Sometimes considered part of the Greater Los Angeles area, it is a major hub of population growth, logistics, warehousing, and transportation.

Geography

The region lies inland from the coastal Los Angeles Basin, separated from it by hills and mountain passes, and stretches eastward toward the San Bernardino Mountains and the deserts beyond. Principal cities include San Bernardino, Riverside, Ontario, Rancho Cucamonga, Fontana, and Moreno Valley.

Economy

Once known for citrus groves and agriculture, the Inland Empire's economy is now dominated by logistics and distribution, driven by its highway and rail junctions, proximity to the ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach, and available warehouse land. Healthcare, education, and manufacturing are also major employers.

Population

Relatively affordable housing compared with coastal counties has made the region one of California's fastest-growing, with a population exceeding four million.
