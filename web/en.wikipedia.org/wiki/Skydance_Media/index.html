Skydance Media

Skydance Media, LLC is an American production company based in Santa Monica, California, founded by David Ellison in 2006. The company produces films, television series, animation, interactive and new media content, frequently co-financing large-scale franchises with major studios, most notably Paramount Pictures.

History

David Ellison, son of Oracle co-founder Larry Ellison and an aerobatic pilot, founded the company in 2006 — the name references his love of flying. After raising capital in 2009–2010, Skydance entered a slate co-financing partnership with Paramount. Its breakthrough came with Mission: Impossible – Ghost Protocol (2011), followed by productions including Jack Reacher, Star Trek Into Darkness, World War Z, Terminator Genisys, Mission: Impossible – Fallout, Gemini Man, Top Gun: Maverick (2022), and Air (2023).

The company expanded into television (Skydance Television, producer of series such as Grace and Frankie, Altered Carbon, Jack Ryan, Reacher and Foundation), animation (Skydance Animation, founded in 2017 and led from 2019 by John Lasseter, producer of Luck and Spellbound), interactive media and sports (Skydance Sports).

Merger with Paramount Global

In July 2024, Skydance and its partners, including RedBird Capital, reached a definitive agreement to merge with and acquire control of Paramount Global through National Amusements, in a deal valuing the combined enterprise at roughly $28 billion, with David Ellison becoming chairman and CEO of the combined "New Paramount". After a protracted regulatory review, the merger was completed in August 2025, forming Paramount Skydance Corporation, and Skydance Media ceased to exist as a separate company.
