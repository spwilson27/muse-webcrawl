George Perry Floyd Jr. (October 14, 1973 – May 25, 2020) was an African-American man who was murdered by Minneapolis police officer Derek Chauvin during an arrest in Minneapolis, Minnesota. Chauvin knelt on Floyd's neck for more than nine minutes while Floyd, handcuffed and lying face down, repeatedly said "I can't breathe." Video of the killing, recorded by bystander Darnella Frazier, sparked the largest protest movement in United States history and demonstrations against police brutality and racism in cities around the world.

Life

Floyd was born in Fayetteville, North Carolina, and raised in Houston's Third Ward, in the Cuney Homes public housing project. A standout football and basketball player at Yates High School, he played college basketball at South Florida Community College. In Houston's hip-hop scene he was known as "Big Floyd," appearing on mixtapes of DJ Screw's Screwed Up Click. He later worked jobs including truck driving and nightclub security. Floyd had several arrests in Texas, including a 2007 aggravated robbery for which he served about four years in prison. He moved to Minneapolis around 2014, worked as a truck driver and as a bouncer, and was a mentor in his church community. He lost his restaurant security job in the COVID-19 pandemic shortly before his death and was a father of five.

Death and aftermath

On May 25, 2020, police responded to a report that Floyd had used a counterfeit $20 bill at a Cup Foods grocery. During the arrest at 38th Street and Chicago Avenue, Chauvin pinned Floyd's neck with his knee for 9 minutes 29 seconds while officers J. Alexander Kueng and Thomas Lane restrained him and Tou Thao kept bystanders back. Floyd became unresponsive and was pronounced dead at a hospital. The Hennepin County medical examiner ruled the death a homicide; an independent autopsy commissioned by the family concurred.

The protests that followed spread to thousands of localities; estimates suggest 15–26 million participants in the United States alone, most peaceful, though some cities saw rioting, arson, and clashes, including the burning of Minneapolis's Third Precinct station. Chauvin was convicted in April 2021 of second-degree unintentional murder, third-degree murder, and second-degree manslaughter, and sentenced to 22.5 years in state prison; he and the other three officers also received federal civil-rights convictions. The intersection where Floyd died became George Floyd Square, a memorial site, and his name became a rallying cry for police-reform legislation, including the George Floyd Justice in Policing Act, which passed the House but stalled in the Senate.
