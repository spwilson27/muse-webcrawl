Portland Timbers

The Portland Timbers are an American professional soccer club based in Portland, Oregon, competing in Major League Soccer (MLS) as a member of the Western Conference. The club plays its home matches at Providence Park. The Timbers joined MLS in 2011 as an expansion team, continuing a name used by earlier Portland clubs in the NASL and USL.

History

Soccer in Portland under the Timbers name dates to 1975, when a North American Soccer League expansion side drew enthusiastic crowds and reached Soccer Bowl '75. After the NASL club folded, the name was revived in the USL First Division from 2001 to 2010. In 2009, owner Merritt Paulson was awarded an MLS expansion franchise, and the Timbers began MLS play in 2011 in a renovated Providence Park.

Under coach Caleb Porter, the Timbers won MLS Cup 2015, defeating the Columbus Crew 2–1 in the final — the club's first league championship and the first for a Cascadia club. In 2020, under Giovanni Savarese, Portland won the MLS is Back Tournament in Orlando. Star players of the MLS era include Diego Valeri, the club's emblematic Argentine playmaker, Darlington Nagbe, Diego Chará and Sebastián Blanco.

Culture

The Timbers are famous for their supporters' group, the Timbers Army, and for traditions such as mascot Timber Joey slicing a slab from a log with a chainsaw after each goal. The club's fierce rivalry with the Seattle Sounders, contested as part of the Cascadia Cup, dates to 1975 and is regarded as one of the great rivalries in American soccer.
