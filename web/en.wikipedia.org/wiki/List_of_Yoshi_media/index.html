List of Yoshi media

Yoshi is a fictional dinosaur from Nintendo's Mario franchise who debuted in Super Mario World (1990). Yoshi has starred in his own series of platform and puzzle games, mostly developed by Nintendo and Good-Feel. This list covers video games in which Yoshi is the principal star or title character.
