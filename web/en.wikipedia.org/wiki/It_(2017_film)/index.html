It (2017 film)

It (titled onscreen as It Chapter One) is a 2017 American supernatural horror film directed by Andy Muschietti and written by Chase Palmer, Cary Fukunaga, and Gary Dauberman. It is the first of a two-part adaptation of the 1986 novel by Stephen King, following seven young outcasts in Derry, Maine, who are terrorised by a shape-shifting entity that takes the form of Pennywise the Dancing Clown, played by Bill Skarsgård.

Plot

In October 1988, twelve-year-old Bill Denbrough's younger brother Georgie is killed by a creature lurking in a storm drain. The following summer Bill and his friends — the self-styled "Losers' Club" — each encounter the entity, which feeds on children's fear every 27 years. The group descends into the sewers beneath Derry to confront It, wounding the creature and forcing it into hibernation.

Reception

The film premiered in September 2017 and grossed over $700 million worldwide against a production budget of about $35 million, becoming the highest-grossing horror film of all time at that point. Critics praised Skarsgård's performance, the direction, and the young cast. A sequel, It Chapter Two, was released in 2019.
