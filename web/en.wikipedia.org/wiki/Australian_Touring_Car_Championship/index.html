Australian Touring Car Championship

Australian Touring Car Championship
Category: Touring cars
Country: Australia
First season: 1960
Successor: Supercars Championship

The Australian Touring Car Championship (ATCC) was a motor racing title for touring cars contested in Australia from 1960. Its winners were recognised as national touring car champions, and from 1999 the championship evolved into what became the V8 Supercars (now Supercars) Championship, with the ATCC title continuing to be awarded to its winner.

History

The first championship, in 1960, was a single race at Gnoo Blas won by David McKay in a Jaguar Mk 1. The series expanded through the Improved Production, Series Production, Group C (1973–1984) and Group A (1985–1992) eras, dominated at various times by drivers such as Ian Geoghegan, Allan Moffat, Peter Brock, Dick Johnson and Jim Richards. From 1993 the series adopted the local Group 3A formula for Ford Falcon and Holden Commodore V8s, which developed into V8 Supercars. Mark Skaife, Craig Lowndes and Jamie Whincup are among the most successful champions of the modern era.
