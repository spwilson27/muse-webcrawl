BRP Inc.

BRP Inc. (Bombardier Recreational Products; TSX: DOO) is a Canadian manufacturer of snowmobiles, personal watercraft, all-terrain vehicles, motorcycles and boats, headquartered in Valcourt, Quebec. Its brands include Ski-Doo and Lynx snowmobiles, Sea-Doo watercraft and boats, Can-Am on- and off-road vehicles, Alumacraft, Manitou, Quintrex and Rotax marine propulsion systems and engines. The company traces its origins to Joseph-Armand Bombardier's invention of the modern snowmobile; it was spun off from Bombardier Inc. in 2003 and taken public in 2013.

History

Joseph-Armand Bombardier patented an early tracked vehicle in 1937, founded his company in 1942, and launched the Ski-Doo in 1959, effectively creating the sport of snowmobiling. Within Bombardier Inc. the recreational products division expanded into Sea-Doo personal watercraft (1968, relaunched 1988), Can-Am motorcycles and ATVs, and Austrian engine-maker Rotax. In 2003 a group of investors led by Bain Capital, the Bombardier-Beaudoin family and the Caisse de dépôt bought the division, creating BRP as an independent company. BRP listed on the Toronto Stock Exchange in 2013 and grew through acquisitions in marine products.

Products

BRP's Ski-Doo and Lynx lines lead the world snowmobile market; Sea-Doo pioneered the modern sit-down personal watercraft and the Switch pontoon boat; Can-Am produces ATVs, side-by-side vehicles, the three-wheeled Ryker and Spyder roadsters, and announced a return to electric two-wheel motorcycles. Rotax engines power many BRP vehicles as well as aircraft, karts and other manufacturers' machines.
