The Federal National Mortgage Association (FNMA), commonly known as Fannie Mae, is a United States government-sponsored enterprise (GSE) and, since 1968, a publicly traded company. Founded during the Great Depression as part of Franklin D. Roosevelt's New Deal, it provides liquidity to the US mortgage market by purchasing home loans from lenders, which allows lenders to make more loans.

How it works

Fannie Mae does not lend directly to home buyers. It buys mortgages from banks and other originators, bundles them into mortgage-backed securities (MBS) that it guarantees for timely payment of principal and interest, and sells those securities to investors. This secondary-market system helps standardise mortgages — notably the 30-year fixed-rate mortgage — and keep capital flowing into housing. Fannie Mae, together with its sibling GSE Freddie Mac, backs a large share of US residential mortgages.

History

Fannie Mae was created in 1938 to buy FHA-insured mortgages and stimulate housing construction. In 1968 it was converted into a shareholder-owned corporation, and its government portfolio was split off into Ginnie Mae. In 1970 it was authorised to buy conventional (non-government-insured) loans. During the 2000s housing boom, Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac were criticised for lowering underwriting standards, taking on risky loans and for accounting irregularities, including a major earnings restatement at Fannie Mae in 2004.

2008 conservatorship

As the subprime mortgage crisis unfolded, losses on mortgage guarantees overwhelmed the companies' capital. In September 2008 the Federal Housing Finance Agency placed Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac into conservatorship, and the US Treasury provided financial support under the Housing and Economic Recovery Act, ultimately injecting about $187 billion into the two enterprises combined. Subsequent profits were swept to the Treasury under a 2012 amendment; the conservatorship has continued ever since, with periodic debate in Washington about reforming or releasing the enterprises.
