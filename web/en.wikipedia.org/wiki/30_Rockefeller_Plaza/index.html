30 Rockefeller Plaza, officially the Comcast Building and formerly known as the RCA Building and the GE Building, is the centrepiece skyscraper of Rockefeller Center in Midtown Manhattan, New York City. Completed in 1933, the 70-storey Art Deco tower is 259 metres (850 feet) tall.

History

The building opened in 1933 as the RCA Building, headquarters of the Radio Corporation of America and its broadcasting subsidiary NBC, whose studios have occupied the building ever since. It became the GE Building in 1988 when General Electric, RCA's parent, moved its headquarters there, and was renamed the Comcast Building in 2015 after Comcast took a major lease, with its name placed atop the tower.

Features

The tower is home to NBC Studios, including Studio 8H, long-time home of Saturday Night Live. Its 67th, 69th and 70th floors house the Top of the Rock observation deck, reopened in 2005 after a long closure. The sitcom 30 Rock (2006–2013), set in the building, took its name from the address.
