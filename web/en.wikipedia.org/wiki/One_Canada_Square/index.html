One Canada Square

One Canada Square
Location: Canary Wharf, London
Height: 235 m (771 ft); Floors: 50
Completed: 1991
Architect: César Pelli (Adamson Associates)

One Canada Square is a skyscraper in Canary Wharf, London. It was the tallest building in the United Kingdom from its completion in 1991 until it was surpassed by the Shard in 2010. It remains one of the tallest structures in London and the emblem of the Canary Wharf financial district.

Design and construction

Designed by César Pelli with Adamson Associates, the 50-storey tower is clad in stainless steel and rises 235 metres (771 feet) above ground, topped by a distinctive pyramid roof with an aircraft warning beacon. Construction began in 1988 and was completed in 1990, with the building opening in August 1991.

History

The tower was developed by Olympia & York, the Reichmann family's property company, whose collapse into administration in 1992 followed the building's completion amid a property crash; occupancy was initially low before the district matured into a major financial centre. The building has principally been used as office space, with a lobby open to the public.
