Selhurst is an area in south London, England, within the London Borough of Croydon. It lies north-east of Croydon town centre and south of South Norwood. The area is principally residential and is best known as the location of Selhurst Park, the home ground of Crystal Palace Football Club.

Transport

Selhurst railway station is served by Southern trains to London Victoria and London Bridge, and the Selhurst train maintenance depot nearby is a significant local employer. Selhurst Road (part of the A213) is the area's main thoroughfare.

Education and notable features

The BRIT School, a performing-arts and technology school whose alumni include the singer Adele, is located in Selhurst. The area also contains Selhurst Park stadium, which has been Crystal Palace's home since 1924, and a number of local parks and churches serving the community.
