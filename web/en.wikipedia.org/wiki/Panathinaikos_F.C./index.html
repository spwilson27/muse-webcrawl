Panathinaikos Football Club (Greek: ΠΑΕ Παναθηναϊκός) is a Greek professional football club based in Athens. Founded in 1908 by Giorgos Kalafatis as the football section of the Panathinaikos multi-sport club, it is one of the most successful and widely supported clubs in Greece, famous for its green colours and shamrock (trifolium) emblem. Its traditional home is the Apostolos Nikolaidis Stadium on Leoforos Avenue.

History

Panathinaikos has won 20 Greek league titles and numerous Greek Cups, and is the only Greek club to have reached a European Cup final, doing so in 1971 under manager Ferenc Puskás, where it lost to Ajax at Wembley. The club also reached the semi-finals of the European Cup in 1985 and of the UEFA Champions League in 1996. Players such as Mimis Domazos, Dimitris Saravakos and Krzysztof Warzycha became club icons. The club's fierce rivalry with Olympiacos of Piraeus is known as the "derby of the eternal enemies".

Supporters

The club's organised supporters, Gate 13, were founded in 1966 and are among the oldest fan groups in Greece. Panathinaikos draws support from across Athens and Greece and fields teams in many other sports under the Panathinaikos A.O. umbrella, including basketball, where the club is one of the most successful in Europe.
