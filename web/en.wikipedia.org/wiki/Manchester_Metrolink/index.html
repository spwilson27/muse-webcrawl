Manchester Metrolink

Manchester Metrolink is a tram/light-rail system in Greater Manchester, England. Opened in 1992, it has grown through successive expansion phases into the largest light-rail network in the United Kingdom, with seven lines, 99 stops and about 64 miles (103 km) of track, carrying roughly 45 million passengers a year. The system is owned by Transport for Greater Manchester (TfGM) and operated under concession, currently by KeolisAmey Metrolink.

History

Phase 1 converted the heavy-rail lines to Bury and Altrincham to tram operation and linked them by street running through the city centre. Phase 2 extended the system to Eccles via Salford Quays (1999–2000). Phase 3, funded through a large government grant after a proposed congestion-charge scheme was rejected by referendum in 2008, added lines to Oldham and Rochdale, Chorlton and East Didsbury, Ashton-under-Lyne and Manchester Airport (opened 2014). A second city-centre crossing opened in 2017, and the Trafford Park line opened in 2020.

Network

Lines serve Altrincham, Ashton-under-Lyne, Bury, East Didsbury, Eccles/MediaCityUK, Manchester Airport, Rochdale and the Trafford Centre, all passing through the city-centre "Castlefield–Piccadilly" core. Trams run on a mix of converted railway alignments and on-street sections, at frequencies of every 6–12 minutes on most routes.
