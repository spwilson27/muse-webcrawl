Starwood Hotels and Resorts Worldwide, Inc. was one of the largest companies that owned, operated, franchised, and managed hotels, resorts, spas, residences, and vacation ownership properties. It was acquired by Marriott International in 2016, creating the world's largest hotel company at the time.

History

Starwood Hotels originated in 1969 as a real estate investment trust. In the 1990s, under chief executive Barry Sternlicht, the company began acquiring hotel assets and brands, most notably ITT Sheraton in 1998, which brought the Sheraton brand and The Luxury Collection into the portfolio. The Westin brand was also acquired during this period.

Starwood was known for brand innovation in the lodging industry. It launched W Hotels in 1998, a lifestyle brand aimed at younger business travellers, and later the select-service brands Aloft and Element. Its loyalty programme, Starwood Preferred Guest (SPG), was widely regarded as one of the most generous in the industry and was a significant factor in its customer base.

Acquisition by Marriott

In November 2015 Marriott International announced an agreement to acquire Starwood in a cash-and-stock deal. After a bidding contest with China's Anbang Insurance Group, Marriott completed the acquisition on 23 September 2016 for approximately $13.6 billion. The Starwood brands continued to operate within Marriott's portfolio, and SPG was eventually merged into Marriott's loyalty programme, Marriott Bonvoy.
