Luxury train

A luxury train is a premium passenger rail service that offers a high level of comfort, fine dining and scenic journeys, often aimed at tourists rather than point-to-point transport. Luxury trains typically feature sleeping cars, lounge and observation cars, and gourmet restaurant cars, and travel slowly through notable landscapes.

Famous luxury trains

The best-known historical example is the Orient Express, which ran between Paris and Istanbul from 1883. Modern and heritage luxury trains include the Venice Simplon-Orient-Express in Europe, the Maharajas' Express and Palace on Wheels in India, the Blue Train and Rovos Rail in South Africa, The Ghan in Australia, the Rocky Mountaineer in Canada, and the Shiki-shima and Seven Stars in Kyushu in Japan.

Characteristics

Luxury trains emphasise the journey itself: private cabins with en-suite facilities, panoramic windows, observation cars, and multi-course meals prepared on board. Fares are typically priced per journey and include accommodation, meals and excursions.
