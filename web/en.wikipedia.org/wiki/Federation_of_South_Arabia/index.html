Federation of South Arabia

The Federation of South Arabia was an organisation of British protectorates in South Arabia, existing from 1962 to 1967 in what is now Yemen.

History

It was formed on 4 April 1962 from the Federation of Arab Emirates of the South, and the colony of Aden joined on 18 January 1963. The federation comprised Aden and a group of sheikhdoms, sultanates and states of the Western Aden Protectorate. It faced insurgency during the Aden Emergency, led by the National Liberation Front (NLF) and FLOSY.

Dissolution

Britain withdrew in November 1967, and the federation was abolished on 30 November 1967, when the People's Republic of South Yemen (later the People's Democratic Republic of Yemen) was proclaimed under the NLF.
