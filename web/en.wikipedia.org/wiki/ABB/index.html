ABB

ABB Ltd
Swiss multinational engineering company
Headquarters: Zurich, Switzerland
Formed 1988 by merger of ASEA and Brown, Boveri & Cie.

ABB is a Swiss multinational company specialising in electrification, automation, robotics and motion. Headquartered in Zurich, it supplies utilities, industry, transport and infrastructure customers with equipment ranging from switchgear, motors and drives to industrial robots and control systems. Its shares are listed in Switzerland and Sweden, reflecting its origins in two European electrical engineering firms.

Formation

ABB was created in 1988 by the merger of Sweden’s ASEA and Switzerland’s Brown, Boveri & Cie (BBC). Both predecessor companies dated to the nineteenth century and had helped pioneer alternating-current transmission, turbines, locomotives and heavy electrical machinery. The merger was one of the largest cross-border industrial combinations of its time and created a group with operations around the world.

Business

The company later reorganised around customer-facing business areas: Electrification, Motion, Process Automation and Robotics & Discrete Automation. It sold its Power Grids division to Hitachi in 2020, forming Hitachi Energy, and has continued to adjust its portfolio toward digital services, energy efficiency and industrial software. ABB robots are widely used in automotive and electronics factories, and its drives and motors are important in reducing industrial energy use.

Sponsorship and profile

ABB became title partner of Formula E, the electric single-seater racing championship, linking its public image to electric mobility. The company employs on the order of one hundred thousand people and is frequently cited among the leading suppliers in factory automation and power technology, competing with firms such as Siemens, Schneider Electric and Rockwell Automation.
