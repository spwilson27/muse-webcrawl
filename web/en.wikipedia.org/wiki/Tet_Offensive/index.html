Tet Offensive
Date: 30 Jan – 28 Sep 1968
Location: South Vietnam
Part of: Vietnam War
Result: US/South Vietnamese tactical victory; North Vietnamese strategic/political impact

The Tet Offensive was a major escalation of the Vietnam War, launched on 30 January 1968 by North Vietnam and the Viet Cong against South Vietnam, the United States and allies, with surprise attacks on over 100 cities during the Tết holiday ceasefire.

Course

Attackers briefly penetrated the US Embassy in Saigon; the Battle of Huế lasted a month, and Khe Sanh was besieged. Militarily the communists suffered heavy losses and failed to spark a general uprising.

Impact

Shocking American television audiences who had been told the war was being won, Tet eroded US public support, led President Johnson to halt escalation and not seek re-election, and shifted US policy toward Vietnamization and negotiation.
