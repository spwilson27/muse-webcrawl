Wayne Enterprises, also known as WayneCorp, is a fictional company appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. It is the corporate empire owned by Bruce Wayne, the alter ego of Batman, and one of the largest fictional conglomerates in the DC Universe. Headquartered in Gotham City, the company provides the fortune that finances Batman's crime-fighting activities, as well as much of his advanced equipment, which is frequently developed through the company's research divisions.

History in fiction

The Wayne family's business interests are depicted as dating back to the 17th century, when the merchant ancestors of the Waynes traded in furs and goods; the company's modern industrial form was built in the 19th century by Solomon Wayne and expanded under later generations. After the murder of Thomas and Martha Wayne, the company was run by executives until Bruce Wayne came of age and assumed control. In most modern continuities, Lucius Fox serves as chief executive and designs much of the technology Batman adapts for his own use.

Divisions

Wayne Enterprises is typically portrayed as a diversified multinational with subsidiaries in aerospace and defence (Wayne Aerospace), biotechnology (Wayne Biotech), chemicals, shipping, entertainment, and electronics (WayneTech). The charitable Wayne Foundation funds social programmes in Gotham City. Storylines have frequently involved hostile takeovers, corruption within the board, and villains such as Lex Luthor or the Court of Owls attempting to seize or undermine the company.

In other media

The company appears throughout Batman film and television adaptations, including the Christopher Nolan film trilogy, in which Lucius Fox (played by Morgan Freeman) runs the Applied Sciences division that supplies Batman's arsenal, and in the television series Gotham and Batwoman.
