Wally West

Wallace Rudolph "Wally" West is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by John Broome and Carmine Infantino, he debuted in The Flash #110 (1959) as Kid Flash, the teen sidekick of Barry Allen, and later succeeded his mentor as the third Flash, a role he held for over two decades.

Publication history

Wally gained his powers as a boy when the same lightning-and-chemicals accident that created Barry Allen's Flash was repeated in front of him. As Kid Flash he was a founding member of the Teen Titans. After Barry Allen died in Crisis on Infinite Earths (1985), Wally took up the mantle of the Flash, headlining the relaunched Flash series from 1987. Writers such as Mark Waid and Geoff Johns developed his mastery of the Speed Force, his marriage to Linda Park, and stories including "The Return of Barry Allen" and "Blitz".

Powers and other versions

Like other Flashes, Wally possesses superhuman speed channelled through the Speed Force, enabling time travel, phasing, accelerated healing and the "infinite mass punch". Following DC's 2011 New 52 reboot, a younger, separate Wally West was introduced, and the original returned in DC Rebirth (2016). Versions of Wally have appeared in animated series including Justice League and Young Justice, and in the Arrowverse television shows.
