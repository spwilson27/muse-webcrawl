Victoria Davey Spelling (born May 16, 1973) is an American actress and author. She first gained recognition for starring as Donna Martin on the television series Beverly Hills, 90210 from 1990 to 2000, a role she reprised in the sequels 90210 (2008) and BH90210 (2019).

Early life

Spelling was born in Los Angeles, California, the daughter of television producer Aaron Spelling and Carol Jean "Candy" Spelling. She has a younger brother, Randy Spelling. She attended Beverly Hills High School and graduated from Harvard-Westlake School.

Career

Spelling began acting as a child with small roles in series produced by her father, including The Love Boat, T. J. Hooker, Hotel, Fantasy Island, and Saved by the Bell. Her breakthrough came with Beverly Hills, 90210, produced by her father's company. She later starred in television films and the sitcom So NoTORIous (2006), and appeared with her husband Dean McDermott in reality series such as Tori & Dean: Inn Love. She is also the author of several books, including the memoir sTori Telling (2008).

Personal life

Spelling married actor Dean McDermott in 2006; they have five children. The couple separated in 2023, and McDermott filed for divorce in 2024.
