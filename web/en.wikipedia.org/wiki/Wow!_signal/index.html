Wow! signal

The Wow! signal was a strong narrowband radio signal detected on August 15, 1977, by Ohio State University's Big Ear radio telescope in the search for extraterrestrial intelligence. Astronomer Jerry R. Ehman wrote "Wow!" beside the printout sequence 6EQUJ5.

Detected: August 15, 1977
Telescope: Big Ear, Ohio State University
Duration: 72 seconds
Frequency: ~1420 MHz (hydrogen line)

Characteristics

The signal bore expected hallmarks of an artificial interstellar transmission near the hydrogen line, rose and fell over 72 seconds as the telescope's beam swept past, and was never detected again despite many follow-up searches.

Explanations

Proposed sources include natural astrophysical processes, cometary hydrogen (a disputed 2017 hypothesis), and an interstellar signal; none is conclusively established. See SETI.
