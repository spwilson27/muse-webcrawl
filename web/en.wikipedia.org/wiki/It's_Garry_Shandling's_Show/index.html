It's Garry Shandling's Show

Created by: Garry Shandling, Alan Zweibel
Starring: Garry Shandling, Molly Cheek, Michael Tucci
Network: Showtime (1986–1990); Fox (reruns/new episodes syndication)
Episodes: 72

It's Garry Shandling's Show is an American sitcom that ran on Showtime from 1986 to 1990, created by comedian Garry Shandling and Alan Zweibel. It is remembered as one of the earliest and most inventive cable sitcoms, built around Shandling's awareness that he was a character in a television show.

Premise and style

Shandling played a fictionalised version of himself, a neurotic comedian living in Sherman Oaks, California. He frequently broke the fourth wall, addressing the studio audience, fast-forwarding through scenes, and inviting viewers into the set. The theme song, "This Is the Theme to Garry's Show", was itself a joke about theme songs.

Legacy

The series anticipated the self-referential comedy of later shows and preceded Shandling's acclaimed HBO series The Larry Sanders Show (1992–1998).
