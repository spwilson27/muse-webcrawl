List of heads of state of Afghanistan

This is a list of heads of state of Afghanistan, from the founding of the modern Afghan state under Ahmad Shah Durrani in 1747 to the present. Afghanistan has been, at various times, an emirate, a kingdom, a republic, a socialist republic, an Islamic state and an Islamic emirate. The current head of state is the Supreme Leader of the Islamic Emirate of Afghanistan, Hibatullah Akhundzada, whose government (restored in 2021) remains unrecognised by most countries.
