Ramsar Convention

The Ramsar Convention, formally the Convention on Wetlands of International Importance, is an international treaty for the conservation and sustainable use of wetlands. It was signed in Ramsar, Iran, on 2 February 1971 and came into force in 1975.

List of sites

Contracting parties designate at least one Wetland of International Importance (Ramsar site). Over 2,400 sites covering more than 2.5 million square kilometres have been listed by more than 170 parties. World Wetlands Day is celebrated on 2 February. The convention promotes wise use of all wetlands through national policies, protected areas, and international cooperation.
