Walgreens

Walgreen Company, doing business as Walgreens, is an American company that operates one of the largest pharmacy store chains in the United States. Founded in Chicago, Illinois, in 1901 by Charles Rudolph Walgreen, the company grew from a single drugstore into a nationwide retailer of prescription and over-the-counter medicines, health and beauty products, photo services and general merchandise.

History

Charles R. Walgreen bought the Chicago drugstore where he had worked in 1901. The chain expanded during Prohibition in the 1920s, when prescription whisky sales and the popularity of Walgreen's malted milkshakes — popularised by employee Ivar Coulson — fuelled rapid growth. Walgreens became publicly traded in 1927 and continued expanding through the twentieth century, pioneering self-service stores, drive-through pharmacies and photo labs. In 2014 Walgreens combined with the European group Alliance Boots to form Walgreens Boots Alliance, with Walgreens operating as its American retail pharmacy division, headquartered in Deerfield, Illinois. The company operates thousands of stores across the United States and Puerto Rico.
