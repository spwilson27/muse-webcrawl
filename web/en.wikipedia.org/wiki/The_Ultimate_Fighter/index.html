The Ultimate Fighter is an American reality television series and mixed martial arts (MMA) competition produced by the Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC). The show features professional MMA fighters living together in a house in Las Vegas, Nevada, training and competing against each other in a tournament for a six-figure UFC contract.

Format

Each season, fighters in one or two weight classes are divided into two teams coached by established UFC fighters. They compete in a single-elimination tournament, with the coaches typically fighting each other at the season's end. Winners of the tournament are awarded a UFC contract, traditionally described as "six-figure".

History

The series premiered on Spike TV on January 17, 2005. Its first-season live finale—headlined by Forrest Griffin vs. Stephan Bonnar, a bout later inducted into the UFC Hall of Fame—is widely credited with saving the UFC and launching the mainstream popularity of MMA in the United States. Notable winners and participants include Forrest Griffin, Rashad Evans, Michael Bisping, Nate Diaz, Tony Ferguson, Kamaru Usman, and Julianna Peña. Versions and parallel seasons have run for decades, including international editions such as The Ultimate Fighter: Brazil and The Ultimate Fighter: Latin America.
