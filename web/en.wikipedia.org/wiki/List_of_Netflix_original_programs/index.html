List of Netflix original programs

Netflix is an American streaming service. This is a list of television programs that Netflix has produced, co-produced or distributed exclusively on its platform, branded as "Netflix Originals". Netflix's first original series was the political drama House of Cards, released in February 2013, and it has since become one of the largest producers of original television in the world, commissioning drama, comedy, documentary, reality, animation and non-English-language programming in dozens of countries.
