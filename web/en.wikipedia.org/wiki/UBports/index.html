UBports

UBports is a community project and non-profit foundation that continues the development of Ubuntu Touch, the mobile operating system originally created by Canonical Ltd. After Canonical discontinued Ubuntu Touch in April 2017, the UBports community took over the project, maintaining the operating system for a range of smartphones and tablets as a free and open-source, privacy-focused mobile platform.

History

The UBports project was founded by Norwegian developer Marius Gripsgård in 2015 to port Ubuntu Touch to devices not officially supported by Canonical. When Canonical ended the project in April 2017 as part of its decision to drop the Unity desktop and convergence strategy, UBports became the primary maintainer of Ubuntu Touch. The UBports Foundation was later established in Berlin to provide a legal and financial home for the project.

Ubuntu Touch development

Under UBports, Ubuntu Touch development continued with over-the-air (OTA) updates, a move to newer Ubuntu base releases, and support for devices including the Fairphone, OnePlus, Google Nexus and Pixel ranges, and the PinePhone. The community maintains the Lomiri user interface, the OpenStore app catalogue, and an installer tool that simplifies flashing devices.
