Home improvement

Home improvement, also called home renovation or remodelling, is the process of renovating, repairing or making additions to a home. Projects range from routine maintenance and redecoration to major structural work such as extensions, loft conversions and complete kitchen or bathroom refits. Home improvement is carried out both by professional contractors and by homeowners themselves (DIY), and supports a large retail and trades industry.

Types of project

  • Maintenance and repair — roofing, plumbing, heating and electrical work needed to keep a home sound.
  • Comfort and efficiency — insulation, double glazing, heat pumps and other energy-saving upgrades.
  • Interior remodelling — kitchens, bathrooms, flooring and decoration.
  • Additions — extensions, conservatories, garage and loft conversions, and extra rooms.
  • Exterior and garden work — patios, decking, fencing, driveways and landscaping.
  • Accessibility and safety — ramps, stairlifts, alarms and adaptations for ageing in place.

Industry and regulation

Home improvement is a major sector of the construction industry; large chains such as Home Depot and Lowe's in the United States and B&Q in the United Kingdom supply materials to professionals and DIY customers alike. Depending on the jurisdiction, structural, electrical and gas work may require permits and licensed contractors, and projects are subject to building codes and planning rules. Television programmes and online video have popularised renovation culture, though surveys regularly find that many projects exceed their budgets and schedules, and not all improvements recoup their cost in added property value.
