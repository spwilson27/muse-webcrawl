Subway (restaurant)

Type: Private

Industry: Fast food restaurants

Founded: August 28, 1965; Bridgeport, Connecticut, U.S.

Founders: Fred DeLuca, Peter Buck

Headquarters: Shelton, Connecticut, U.S.

Products: Submarine sandwiches, salads, wraps, pizza (some locations)

Subway is an American multinational fast food restaurant franchise that specializes in submarine sandwiches (subs), wraps, salads and drinks. It was founded by Fred DeLuca and financed by Peter Buck in 1965 as Pete's Super Submarines in Bridgeport, Connecticut. After several name changes, it was renamed Subway in 1972, and a franchise operation began in 1974 with a second restaurant in Wallingford, Connecticut.

History

Seventeen-year-old Fred DeLuca borrowed $1,000 from family friend Peter Buck to open a submarine sandwich shop, intending to fund his medical education. The business grew through franchising, and by the early 2010s Subway had become one of the largest restaurant chains in the world by number of locations, at one point surpassing McDonald's. The company was sold to Roark Capital in 2023.

Products and marketing

Subway's core product is the submarine sandwich, assembled to order in front of customers from a choice of breads, fillings, cheeses, vegetables, and sauces. The chain became known for promotions such as the "$5 Footlong" and for marketing its sandwiches as a fresher, healthier alternative, prominently featuring spokesman Jared Fogle from 2000 until 2015, when his contract was terminated following criminal investigations.
