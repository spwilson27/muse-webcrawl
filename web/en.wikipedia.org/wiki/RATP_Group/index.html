The RATP Group (French: Groupe RATP) is a French state-owned public transport operator and maintainer headquartered in Paris. Its original and core business is the operation of the Paris Métro, the RER suburban express network (jointly with SNCF), the Paris tramway network and the bus network of Paris and its inner suburbs, under contract with the regional transport authority Île-de-France Mobilités.

The group has expanded internationally through subsidiaries such as RATP Dev, which operates and maintains urban and intercity networks in more than a dozen countries, including tramways, buses and metro systems in Europe, North America, Africa and the Middle East. Engineering services are provided by subsidiaries including Systra, in which RATP holds a stake alongside SNCF.

History

The Régie autonome des transports parisiens (RATP) was created on 1 January 1949, bringing together the assets of the Compagnie du chemin de fer métropolitain de Paris (CMP) and the Société des transports en commun de la région parisienne (STCRP), which had been nationalised. It took over operation of the Métro and surface transport across Paris.

During the post-war decades RATP oversaw the modernisation of the Métro, the conversion of some lines to rubber-tyred operation, and, from 1969, the development of the RER regional express network. In 2000, changes in French law allowed RATP to compete for transport contracts outside its historic Île-de-France monopoly area, leading to the creation of its development subsidiary RATP Dev in 2002.

Operations

Île-de-France

In its home region, the group operates 16 Métro lines, parts of RER lines A and B, 14 tramway lines and a bus network of several hundred routes, carrying several billion passengers a year before the COVID-19 pandemic. It also manages the region's transport infrastructure, including stations, tunnels and depots.

International

Through RATP Dev, the group operates networks including the Cairo Metro lines, tramways in cities such as Casablanca and Florence, bus networks in London (through its former London United operations) and the United States, and it has been involved in the operation of the Riyadh metro in Saudi Arabia.
