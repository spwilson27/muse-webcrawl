The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power

Genre: Fantasy drama
Based on: The works of J. R. R. Tolkien
Starring: Morfydd Clark, Robert Aramayo, Charlie Vickers
Network: Amazon Prime Video
Premiered: 1 September 2022

The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power is an American fantasy television series developed by J. D. Payne and Patrick McKay for Amazon Prime Video. Based on J. R. R. Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings and its appendices, it is set in the Second Age of Middle-earth, thousands of years before the events of The Hobbit and The Lord of the Rings.

Premise

The series dramatizes the major events of the Second Age: the forging of the Rings of Power, the rise of the Dark Lord Sauron, the fall of the island kingdom of Númenor, and the last alliance between Elves and Men. Storylines follow a young Galadriel hunting the remnants of evil, Elrond and the Dwarves of Khazad-dûm, the Harfoots' encounter with a mysterious Stranger, and the Southlands' descent into darkness.

Production

Amazon acquired the television rights in 2017 for a reported $250 million, committing to multiple seasons and making the series the most expensive ever produced, with the first season alone costing an estimated $465 million. The first season was filmed in New Zealand; production moved to the United Kingdom for the second season, which premiered in 2024.

Reception

The series drew enormous viewership — Amazon reported 25 million viewers for its premiere day — and reviews praised its scale, cinematography, and score by Bear McCreary, while criticism focused on pacing and departures from Tolkien's timeline.
