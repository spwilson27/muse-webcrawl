Alonso Ancira Elizondo (born 1955) is a Mexican businessman who served as chairman of the board of Altos Hornos de México (AHMSA), one of Mexico's largest steel producers, based in Monclova, Coahuila.

Career

Ancira built his fortune in mining and steel. Under his leadership AHMSA was privatized in the early 1990s and became a major producer; he also controlled Minera del Norte. He was known for his prominence in Mexican business circles and his close ties to political figures.

Legal troubles

In 2019 Ancira was arrested in Spain in connection with Mexico's investigation of the Agronitrogenados fertilizer plant sale, in which state oil company Pemex allegedly overpaid for an AHMSA plant. He was extradited to Mexico in 2021 and reached a reparations agreement with Pemex of about US16 million. AHMSA later halted operations amid financial collapse.
