Dale Earnhardt Jr.

Born: Ralph Dale Earnhardt Jr., October 10, 1974, Kannapolis, North Carolina
Occupation: Racing driver, team owner, broadcaster
Parent: Dale Earnhardt

Ralph Dale Earnhardt Jr. (born October 10, 1974) is an American stock car racing driver, team owner and broadcaster. The son of seven-time NASCAR champion Dale Earnhardt, he is a two-time winner of the Daytona 500 (2004 and 2014) and was voted NASCAR's Most Popular Driver 15 consecutive times from 2003 to 2017.

Career

Earnhardt Jr. won consecutive Busch Series championships in 1998 and 1999 before moving full-time to the Cup Series with Dale Earnhardt, Inc. in 2000. After his father's death in the 2001 Daytona 500, he became the sport's most prominent figure. He joined Hendrick Motorsports in 2008, scoring wins including his second Daytona 500 in 2014, and retired from full-time Cup racing at the end of 2017 with 26 career victories.

Later work

He co-owns JR Motorsports, a successful Xfinity Series team, and has worked as a race analyst for NBC Sports. He continues to run occasional late model and Xfinity races, and hosts The Dale Jr. Download podcast. He was inducted into the NASCAR Hall of Fame in 2022.
