List of cities and towns in Georgia (country)

Georgia, a country in the Caucasus, has 55 officially designated cities (qalaqi) and a number of smaller towns (daba). The capital, Tbilisi, is by far the largest city, with over a million inhabitants — roughly a third of the national population. Population figures below are approximate, based on national statistics estimates.

Status note

Georgia's official statistics do not include reliable recent counts for cities in Abkhazia (e.g., Sukhumi, Gagra) and South Ossetia (Tskhinvali), territories outside the central government's control. Sokhumi/Sukhumi was historically the second city of the country.
