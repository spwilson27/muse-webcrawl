Galileo (horse)

Galileo
Foaled: 30 March 1998, Ireland
Died: 10 July 2021 (aged 23)
Sire: Sadler's Wells; Dam: Urban Sea
Trainer: Aidan O'Brien
Major wins: Epsom Derby, Irish Derby, King George VI and Queen Elizabeth Stakes (2001)

Galileo (30 March 1998 – 10 July 2021) was an Irish Thoroughbred racehorse and sire. In a racing career which lasted from October 2000 to October 2001, he won six of his eight races, including the Derby, Irish Derby Stakes, and King George VI and Queen Elizabeth Stakes in 2001. As a breeding stallion he became one of the most influential sires of the modern era, standing at Coolmore Stud in Ireland, where his stud fee was privately negotiated and reported to be among the highest in the world.

Background

Galileo was a bay horse bred in Ireland by David Tsui and the Orpendale syndicate. He was sired by Sadler's Wells, fourteen-time champion sire, out of Urban Sea, winner of the Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe and one of the most outstanding broodmares of her generation. He was trained at Ballydoyle by Aidan O'Brien for the Coolmore partnership of Sue Magnier and Michael Tabor.

Racing career

Unbeaten in his first six starts, Galileo won the Derby by three and a half lengths under Mick Kinane, then defeated older horses in the King George VI and Queen Elizabeth Stakes at Ascot, out-duelling Fantastic Light. Fantastic Light reversed the form narrowly in the Irish Champion Stakes, and Galileo ended his career finishing unplaced in the Breeders' Cup Classic at Belmont Park, his only race on dirt.

Stud career

Galileo was champion sire in Great Britain and Ireland twelve times. His progeny include the undefeated Frankel, widely regarded as one of the greatest racehorses of all time, as well as New Approach, Teofilo, Australia, Minding, Highland Reel, and Waldgeist. He became a notable sire of sires and broodmare sire. Galileo was euthanised on 10 July 2021 owing to a chronic, debilitating foot injury.
