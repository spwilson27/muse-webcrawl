In modern criminal law, a crime is an unlawful act punishable by a state or other authority. Crimes range from theft and assault to murder, fraud and offences against the state; what counts as crime varies by era and jurisdiction — behaviours once criminal (blasphemy, homosexuality) are now rights in many places.

Crime trends

Recorded crime fell dramatically in most rich countries from the 1990s (the 'crime decline'), for debated reasons, though fraud and cybercrime rose steeply online. Criminology studies causes from poverty and opportunity to self-control, informing policing (Police) and prevention.
