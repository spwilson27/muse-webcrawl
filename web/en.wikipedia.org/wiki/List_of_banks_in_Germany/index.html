List of banks in Germany

Germany has the largest banking sector in the European Union, organized into a distinctive "three-pillar" structure: private commercial banks, public-sector banks (Sparkassen and Landesbanken), and cooperative banks (Volksbanken and Raiffeisenbanken). Oversight is exercised by the Deutsche Bundesbank and the Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (BaFin), alongside the European Central Bank's Single Supervisory Mechanism for significant institutions.

Structure

The Sparkassen (savings banks) number more than 350 locally oriented public institutions coordinated by the Deutscher Sparkassen- und Giroverband (DSGV), while the cooperative sector comprises several hundred Volksbanken and Raiffeisenbanken under the BVR. Alongside these operate private banks, branches of foreign banks, building societies (Bausparkassen), and Germany's central bank, the Deutsche Bundesbank.
