Xinyang Maojian tea

TypeGreen tea
OriginXinyang, Henan, China
Other namesYu Maofeng

Xinyang Maojian (信阳毛尖) is a green tea produced in Xinyang, Henan province, China. It is regarded as one of China's ten most famous teas. The name "Maojian" refers to the tea's appearance: small, slender leaves covered in fine downy hairs ("mao") with pointed tips ("jian").

Production

The tea is grown in the mountainous districts around Xinyang, including the areas near Dongjiazhai and the Gushi and Shangcheng counties, where misty hillsides and a mild climate favour tea cultivation. Leaves are hand-picked in spring, typically one bud with one or two tender leaves, then pan-fired and rolled by hand in a process that shapes the characteristic tight, thin strips.

Characteristics

Properly brewed, Xinyang Maojian yields a pale jade-green liquor with a fresh, chestnut-like aroma and a slightly sweet, brisk flavour. It won a gold medal at the 1915 Panama-Pacific International Exposition in San Francisco and remains a flagship product of Henan's tea industry.
