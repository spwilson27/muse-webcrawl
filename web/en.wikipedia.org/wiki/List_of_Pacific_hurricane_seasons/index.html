List of Pacific hurricane seasons

The List of Pacific hurricane seasons covers the annual cycle of tropical cyclone formation in the eastern and central Pacific Ocean. The eastern Pacific season officially runs from May 15 to November 30, and the central Pacific season from June 1 to November 30. The basin is the second-most active in the world after the western North Pacific.

Basin overview

The National Hurricane Center in Miami is responsible for the eastern Pacific, while the Central Pacific Hurricane Center in Honolulu covers the area west of 140°W. On average the eastern Pacific produces about 15 named storms a year, of which roughly eight become hurricanes and four major hurricanes.

Records

Hurricane Patricia (2015), with maximum sustained winds of 215 mph (345 km/h) and a minimum pressure of 872 mbar, is the most intense Pacific hurricane on record. The 1994 Hurricane John, lasting 31 days and travelling some 13,180 km, was the longest-lasting and farthest-travelling tropical cyclone ever observed.
