Balcones Fault

The Balcones Fault or Balcones Fault Zone is a geological fault system in the U.S. state of Texas that runs roughly from the southwest part of the state near Del Rio, following Interstate 35 northeast through San Antonio and Austin, and toward Dallas. It marks the boundary between the Edwards Plateau (Texas Hill Country) to the west and the Gulf Coastal Plain to the east.

Geology

The fault zone consists of many small features, including normal faults with displacements of up to several hundred metres, formed tens of millions of years ago, likely in the Ouachita orogeny and subsequent extension. Movement on the Balcones system helped shape the region's topography: the escarpment and spring lines (such as those feeding Barton Springs and the San Marcos Springs) occur along the fault zone where it intersects the Edwards Aquifer.

Significance

The fault delineates the eastern edge of the Hill Country and strongly influences groundwater flow, soils, and vegetation. Texas's major I-35 corridor cities sit along or near the fault zone. Though faults in the zone have generated minor historical earthquakes, the system is considered largely inactive.
