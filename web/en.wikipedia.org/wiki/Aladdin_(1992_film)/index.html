Aladdin (1992 film)

Aladdin
Directed by: Ron Clements, John Musker
Produced by: Walt Disney Feature Animation
Starring: Scott Weinger, Robin Williams, Linda Larkin, Jonathan Freeman
Music: Alan Menken; lyrics by Howard Ashman and Tim Rice
Release date: 25 November 1992
Box office: $504 million

Aladdin is a 1992 American animated musical fantasy comedy film produced by Walt Disney Feature Animation and released by Walt Disney Pictures. Based on the Arabic folktale of the same name from One Thousand and One Nights, it is the 31st Disney animated feature film and the fourth of the Disney Renaissance.

Plot

In the fictional city of Agrabah, the evil vizier Jafar seeks a magic lamp hidden in the Cave of Wonders. The street rat Aladdin, chosen as the "diamond in the rough", retrieves the lamp and befriends the Genie within, who grants him three wishes. Wishing to become a prince to woo Princess Jasmine, Aladdin must ultimately defeat Jafar, who seizes the lamp and wishes himself sultan and sorcerer. Aladdin tricks Jafar into wishing to become a genie, trapping him in a lamp, and uses his final wish to free the Genie.

Reception and legacy

The film was the highest-grossing of 1992, earning over $504 million worldwide. Its songs by Alan Menken, Howard Ashman and Tim Rice include "A Whole New World", which won the Academy Award for Best Original Song, and "Friend Like Me". Robin Williams's performance as the Genie was widely acclaimed and changed the role of celebrity voice acting in animation. A live-action remake directed by Guy Ritchie was released in 2019.
