Don-2N radar

LocationPushkino, Moscow Oblast, Russia
TypePhased-array early-warning / fire-control radar
NATO namePill Box

The Don-2N radar (Russian: Дон-2Н; NATO reporting name: Pill Box) is a large battle-management phased-array radar near Pushkino, Moscow Oblast, Russia. It is the central radar of the A-135 anti-ballistic missile system defending Moscow.

Description

The installation is a truncated pyramid about 33 m tall with faces roughly 130 m long, each bearing circular phased-array antennas for tracking and missile guidance. The radar provides target tracking and midcourse guidance for the A-135's interceptor missiles and can track objects over ranges of thousands of kilometres. Construction began in 1978 and the system entered service in the 1990s; the radar has been progressively modernised in connection with the successor A-235 system.
