The 10th Mountain Division (Light Infantry) is a light infantry division of the United States Army based at Fort Drum, New York. Originally constituted during the Second World War as a unit trained for fighting in mountainous and arctic conditions, it is the only division of the U.S. Army to receive specialised training for mountain warfare. Since its reactivation in 1985 it has been among the most frequently deployed divisions in the Army.

History

World War II

The division was activated on 15 July 1943 at Camp Hale, Colorado, drawing heavily on skiers and outdoorsmen recruited with the help of the National Ski Patrol. After training at Camp Hale and Camp Swift, Texas, the division deployed to Italy in early 1945. In February 1945 its soldiers scaled the cliffs of Riva Ridge at night to surprise German positions, then took Mount Belvedere in the North Apennine campaign. In the spring offensive the division advanced into the Po Valley and reached Lake Garda by the end of the war in Europe. The division was inactivated in November 1945.

Cold War and reactivation

The division was briefly reactivated as a training division from 1948 to 1958. On 13 February 1985 it was reactivated at Fort Drum, New York, as the 10th Mountain Division (Light Infantry), organised as a rapidly deployable light infantry force.

Post-Cold War operations

Elements of the division deployed to Florida after Hurricane Andrew (1992), to Somalia in Operation Restore Hope (1992–93), to Haiti in Operation Uphold Democracy (1994), and to the Balkans for peacekeeping in Bosnia and Kosovo. After the September 11 attacks, the division deployed repeatedly to Afghanistan — among the first conventional forces to arrive in late 2001 — and to Iraq. Its headquarters and brigade combat teams have rotated through both theatres many times, making it one of the most deployed divisions in the U.S. Army.

Organisation

The division comprises three infantry brigade combat teams (1st, 2nd and 3rd Brigade Combat Teams), the 10th Combat Aviation Brigade, the 10th Mountain Division Sustainment Brigade, and a division artillery headquarters, all based principally at Fort Drum, with the 3rd Brigade Combat Team at Fort Polk, Louisiana.
