The Beijing–Guangzhou high-speed railway (Jingguang HSR) is a high-speed rail line in China connecting Beijing West railway station with Guangzhou South railway station. At 2,298 kilometres, it was the world's longest high-speed railway line when it fully opened on 26 December 2012, cutting the journey between the two cities from more than 20 hours to about 8 hours.

Route

The line passes through the provincial capitals Shijiazhuang, Zhengzhou, Wuhan, and Changsha, crossing the Yellow River and the Yangtze River. It forms the central spine of China's "four vertical" high-speed rail grid and connects at Zhengzhou with the east–west Xuzhou–Lanzhou corridor. Through services continue south from Guangzhou to Shenzhen and Hong Kong via the Guangzhou–Shenzhen–Hong Kong Express Rail Link, opened in 2018.

Construction

The Wuhan–Guangzhou section opened first, in December 2009, followed by the Zhengzhou–Wuhan section in September 2012 and the Beijing–Zhengzhou section completing the line in December 2012. Trains operate at up to 310 km/h in regular service. See also high-speed rail in China.
