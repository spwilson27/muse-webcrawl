The horse is a large domesticated odd-toed ungulate, one of the animals that most changed human history. Domesticated on the Eurasian steppe several thousand years ago, horses provided transport, farm power, cavalry and communication — the mount of couriers, conquerors from Alexander to the Mongols, and of Byzantium's armies.

Modern roles

Engines replaced working horses in the 20th century — the engine pages tell that story — but horses remain central to sport (racing, show jumping, dressage), leisure riding, therapy and some police and ceremonial units. Wild relatives and feral herds, and the ethics of racing, are continuing debates.
