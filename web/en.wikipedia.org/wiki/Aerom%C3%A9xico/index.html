Aeroméxico is the flag carrier of Mexico, based in Mexico City. It operates scheduled services to more than 90 destinations in Mexico, North and South America, Europe and Asia, and is a founding member of the SkyTeam airline alliance.

History

The airline was founded in 1934 as Aeronaves de México, beginning with a flight between Mexico City and Acapulco. It was nationalized in the mid-20th century, adopting the Aeroméxico name in 1972, and was privatized in the 2000s. Its regional subsidiary Aeroméxico Connect operates feeder services.

Fleet

Aeroméxico operates a fleet of Boeing 737 aircraft, including the 737 MAX, and Boeing 787 Dreamliners for long-haul routes.
