Lost (2004 TV series)

Original run: 22 September 2004 – 23 May 2010 (ABC)

Lost is an American science fiction drama television series created by Jeffrey Lieber, J. J. Abrams and Damon Lindelof that aired on ABC from September 22, 2004, to May 23, 2010, over six seasons and 121 episodes. The series follows the survivors of Oceanic Airlines Flight 815, which crashes on a mysterious tropical island in the South Pacific; each episode typically features a storyline on the island intercut with flashbacks (and later flash-forwards and "flash-sideways") centred on a particular character.

Story and themes

The survivors — including surgeon Jack Shephard, fugitive Kate Austen, con man Sawyer, former Iraqi soldier Sayid Jarrah, lottery winner Hurley and paraplegic-turned-believer John Locke — encounter polar bears, a "Smoke Monster", a hidden hatch, the DHARMA Initiative and the island's earlier inhabitants, the "Others". The series blends science fiction and supernatural elements with themes of faith versus science, destiny and redemption, and became famous for its mythology, cliff-hangers and online fan theorising.

Reception and legacy

Lost won the Emmy Award for Outstanding Drama Series in 2005 and numerous other honours; its two-part pilot was among the most expensive ever made. The series finale, "The End", drew 13.5 million U.S. viewers and remains widely debated. Consistently ranked among the greatest television series of all time, Lost transformed serialised network drama and early internet fan culture.
