Born again

Born again, or to experience the new birth, is a phrase originating in the New Testament — notably John 3, where Jesus tells Nicodemus one must be "born again" (or "born from above") to see the kingdom of God — referring to spiritual rebirth through faith and the Holy Spirit.

Interpretations

Traditions differ: evangelical Protestantism emphasises a personal conversion experience and decision for Christ; Catholic, Orthodox and many mainline churches link new birth closely to baptism. Anabaptists and Pentecostals have their own emphases.

Cultural use

In modern usage, especially in the United States, "born-again Christian" often identifies evangelical Christians. The phrase is also used metaphorically for a profound personal transformation in other contexts.
