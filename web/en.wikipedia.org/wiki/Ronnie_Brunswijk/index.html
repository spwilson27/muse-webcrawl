Ronnie Brunswijk

Born: 7 March 1961, Moengotapoe, Suriname
Office: Vice President of Suriname (2020–2025)

Ronnie Brunswijk (born 7 March 1961) is a Surinamese politician and businessman, and a former rebel leader. He served as Vice President of Suriname from 2020 to 2025 under President Chan Santokhi.

Jungle Commando and civil war

Brunswijk, a Ndyuka Maroon, was once a bodyguard of military ruler Dési Bouterse. In 1986 he founded the Jungle Commando rebel group and fought the Surinamese army in the Surinamese Interior War (1986–1992), a conflict that devastated Maroon communities in the east of the country. A peace accord ended the war in 1992.

Political career

Brunswijk founded the General Liberation and Development Party (ABOP) and became a kingmaker in Surinamese politics, representing the Maroon interior. In 1999 he was convicted in absentia in the Netherlands for drug trafficking, a conviction he disputes. Following the 2020 elections his party joined a coalition with Santokhi's VHP, and Brunswijk became vice president — and, briefly, acting president, at that time the first Maroon to hold the offices. He is also known as a football club owner (Inter Moengotapoe) and famously played in a CONCACAF League match at the age of 60 in 2021.
