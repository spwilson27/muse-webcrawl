Copeton Dam

Copeton Dam is a major clay-core rock-fill embankment dam with a concrete chute spillway across the Gwydir River near Inverell in the New England region of New South Wales, Australia. Completed in 1976, the dam's purpose includes irrigation, hydroelectric power, water supply and environmental flows.

Features

The dam wall is about 113 metres high and 1,484 metres long, creating Lake Copeton with a capacity of roughly 1,364 gigalitres — more than twice the volume of Sydney Harbour. A 21-megawatt hydroelectric power station operates at the dam. Lake Copeton is a popular recreation destination for fishing, camping and water sports, and the surrounding area forms Copeton Waters State Park.
