National Council for Scientific and Technological Development

CNPq
Portuguese: Conselho Nacional de Desenvolvimento Científico e Tecnológico
Type: Government agency
Founded: 1951
Headquarters: Brasília, Brazil

The National Council for Scientific and Technological Development (Portuguese: Conselho Nacional de Desenvolvimento Científico e Tecnológico, CNPq) is a Brazilian federal agency linked to the Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation. It is one of the main funders of scientific and technological research in Brazil.

History

CNPq was founded in 1951, initially as the National Research Council, at a time when Brazil sought to develop its own scientific capacity, including in nuclear research. Physicist César Lattes was one of the prominent figures associated with its creation.

Activities

The agency awards research grants, fellowships and scholarships at undergraduate, graduate and postdoctoral levels, and funds research projects across all areas of knowledge. CNPq also maintains the Lattes Platform, a widely used national database of curricula of Brazilian researchers and research groups, named after César Lattes.
