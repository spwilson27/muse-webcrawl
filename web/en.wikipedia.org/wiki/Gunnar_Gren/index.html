Johan Gunnar Gren (31 October 1920 – 10 November 1991) was a Swedish professional footballer who played as an inside forward. He is remembered as one of Sweden's greatest players and as part of the famous "Gre-No-Li" trio at AC Milan, alongside his Swedish countrymen Gunnar Nordahl and Nils Liedholm.

Gren with the Swedish national team

Career

Gren began his career in Gothenburg with Gårda BK and IFK Göteborg, with whom he won the Allsvenskan. In 1949 he moved to Italy to join AC Milan, where the Gre-No-Li forward line helped the club win the Scudetto in 1951. He later played for Fiorentina and Genoa before returning to Sweden with Örgryte IS and GAIS.

With the Swedish national team, Gren won the gold medal at the 1948 Summer Olympics in London and was part of the side that finished as runners-up at the 1958 FIFA World Cup on home soil, when Sweden lost the final to Brazil. Renowned for his technique, passing and tactical intelligence — nicknamed "Il Professore" in Italy — he was awarded the Guldbollen as Sweden's best footballer and remained a beloved figure in Gothenburg, where a statue of him stands outside the Gamla Ullevi stadium.
