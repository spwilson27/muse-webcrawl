The National Republican Greek League (Greek: Εθνικός Δημοκρατικός Ελληνικός Σύνδεσμος, Ethnikos Dimokratikos Ellinikos Syndesmos, EDES) was one of the major resistance groups during the Axis occupation of Greece in the Second World War, second in size only to the communist-led EAM-ELAS.

Foundation

EDES was founded on 9 September 1941 by the Venizelist former army officer Napoleon Zervas, together with Komninos Pyromaglou. Its founding proclamation called for the abolition of the monarchy, the establishment of a republic, and the expulsion of the occupying forces.

Wartime activity

Supported by the British Special Operations Executive, EDES guerrillas operated mainly in Epirus in northwestern Greece. In November 1942 EDES jointly with ELAS and British commandos blew up the Gorgopotamos railway viaduct, one of the most celebrated sabotage operations of the Greek resistance.

Conflict and dissolution

Relations with ELAS collapsed into open fighting in 1943–44, encouraged by British policy favouring EDES as a counterweight. After liberation, EDES was largely dissolved, its remnants absorbed into the National Guard, and Zervas entered postwar politics as a minister and party leader.
