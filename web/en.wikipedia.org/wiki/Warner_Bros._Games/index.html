Warner Bros. Games (formerly Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment) is an American video game publisher based in Burbank, California, and part of Warner Bros. Discovery. Founded in 2004, it publishes games based on Warner Bros. film and television properties as well as original franchises, through a network of in-house studios.

Studios and franchises

The division's studios include Rocksteady Studios (the Batman: Arkham series), NetherRealm Studios (Mortal Kombat and Injustice), TT Games (the Lego game series), Avalanche Software (Hogwarts Legacy, 2023 — one of the best-selling games of its year), Monolith Productions (the Middle-earth series, known for its Nemesis system), and WB Games Montréal. Earlier successes included the F.E.A.R. and Scribblenauts franchises.

History

Warner Bros. expanded aggressively into games from the late 2000s, acquiring TT Games (2007), a majority stake in Rocksteady (2010), and the assets of Midway Games (2009), from which NetherRealm emerged. The label was renamed from Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment to Warner Bros. Games in 2020. After Discovery's acquisition of WarnerMedia in 2022, the games division was retained within Warner Bros. Discovery. In 2025 the company closed Monolith Productions, Player First Games, and WB Games San Diego as part of a restructuring to focus on its largest franchises.
