Live 8

Live 8
Date2 July 2005 (additional concerts 6 July 2005)
LocationsLondon, Philadelphia, Paris, Berlin, Rome, Toronto (Barrie), Johannesburg, Moscow, Chiba, Cornwall (Eden Project)
OrganisersBob Geldof and Midge Ure
CampaignMake Poverty History

Live 8 was a series of benefit concerts held on 2 July 2005 in the G8 states and South Africa, timed to coincide with the 31st G8 summit at Gleneagles, Scotland, and with the 20th anniversary of Live Aid. Organised by Bob Geldof and Midge Ure as the climax of the Make Poverty History campaign, the concerts were not fundraisers but pressure events: their aim was to persuade G8 leaders to increase aid to Africa, cancel debt, and pursue fairer trade. An estimated 1,000 musicians performed, and the shows were broadcast to a potential audience of billions.

The concerts

The flagship concert in London's Hyde Park before about 200,000 people was opened by Paul McCartney with U2 ("Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band"), and featured Coldplay, Elton John, Madonna, Robbie Williams, The Who, R.E.M., Mariah Carey and Pink Floyd — who performed together with Roger Waters for the first time in 24 years. The Philadelphia concert, on the Benjamin Franklin Parkway, featured Stevie Wonder, Destiny's Child, Bon Jovi, Dave Matthews Band and Linkin Park. Parallel concerts took place in Paris, Berlin, Rome, Barrie (Canada), Johannesburg, Moscow and Chiba (Japan), with an "Africa Calling" event at the Eden Project in Cornwall curated by Peter Gabriel.

Impact and criticism

At Gleneagles the G8 leaders agreed to double aid to Africa by 2010 and to cancel the debts of the poorest countries — commitments Geldof and Bono hailed as significant, though campaigners later criticised how far short the aid increases fell. Live 8 drew criticism for the scarcity of African performers at the main concerts and for its heroic narrative of Western charity; Geldof countered that the purpose was political pressure, not a festival bill. A further concert in Edinburgh on 6 July, "The Final Push", accompanied the Long Walk to Justice marches as the summit opened.
