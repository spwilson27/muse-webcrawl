Toyosu

Country: Japan
City: Tokyo
Ward: Kōtō
Known for: Toyosu Market

Toyosu (豊洲) is a neighbourhood in Kōtō Ward, Tokyo, Japan, built largely on reclaimed land in Tokyo Bay. Originally developed for industry — the area hosted shipyards, power facilities, and a manufactured-gas plant — it has been redeveloped since the 2000s into a district of high-rise condominiums, offices, shopping centres such as Urban Dock LaLaport Toyosu, and the campus of Shibaura Institute of Technology.

Toyosu Market

Toyosu is best known as the site of the Toyosu Market, the wholesale food market that replaced the historic Tsukiji Market. The move was delayed by the discovery of soil and groundwater contamination (including benzene) beneath the site, a legacy of the former gas plant, requiring extensive remediation and additional safety works. The new market finally opened on 11 October 2018, featuring closed, temperature-controlled facilities and the famous tuna auctions viewed from observation decks.

Transport

The district is served by Toyosu Station on the Tokyo Metro Yurakucho Line and the Yurikamome line, linking it to Shimbashi, Ginza, and Odaiba.
