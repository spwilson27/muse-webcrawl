List of longest arch bridge spans

This is a list of the longest arch bridge spans in the world. China dominates the modern list, holding most of the top positions for both steel and concrete arch bridges. The longest arch span since 2020 has been the Pingnan Third Bridge in Guangxi, China, at 575 metres.
