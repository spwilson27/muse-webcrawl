Charles Xavier

Professor Charles Francis Xavier, also known as Professor X, is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Stan Lee and artist Jack Kirby, he first appeared in The X-Men #1 (September 1963). Xavier is a powerful mutant telepath and the founder of the X-Men, a team of mutant superheroes trained at his School for Gifted Youngsters in Westchester County, New York.

Publication history

Lee and Kirby introduced Xavier as the wheelchair-using mentor of the original X-Men — Cyclops, Marvel Girl, Beast, Angel, and Iceman. Over the decades writers expanded his biography: his meeting and falling-out with Magneto, his romance with Moira MacTaggert and the alien Lilandra, his stepbrother the Juggernaut, and his dark psychic counterpart Onslaught. In recent storylines Xavier became a central figure of the mutant nation of Krakoa.

Fictional biography

Born to wealthy parents, Charles Xavier manifested telepathic abilities in youth. After earning doctorates and travelling the world, he resolved to protect a world that feared mutants and to teach young mutants to use their powers for good. From his family's mansion he operates Cerebro, a machine that amplifies his telepathy to locate mutants worldwide. His dream of peaceful coexistence contrasts with Magneto's militant separatism, giving the X-Men stories their central ideological tension.

In other media

Patrick Stewart portrayed Xavier in the X-Men film series (2000–2017), and James McAvoy played a younger version in the prequel films beginning with X-Men: First Class (2011). The character has appeared in numerous animated series, including X-Men: The Animated Series and X-Men '97.
