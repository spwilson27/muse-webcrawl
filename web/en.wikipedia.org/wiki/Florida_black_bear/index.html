Florida black bear

The Florida black bear (Ursus americanus floridanus) is a subspecies of the American black bear that is historically found in Florida and parts of neighbouring Georgia, Alabama, and Mississippi.

Description

Adults typically weigh 250 to 400 pounds for males, with a distinctive patch of blonde or white fur on the chest in some individuals, and a long, straight snout.

They are omnivores, feeding largely on plant material such as sabal palm berries and acorns, plus insects and occasional animal matter.

Conservation

Hunting and habitat loss reduced the population to a few hundred by the 1970s; protection led to recovery, and the subspecies was removed from Florida's threatened list in 2012.

Human–bear conflict over food and garbage remains a major management issue.
