CR Flamengo

Clube de Regatas do Flamengo (Brazilian Portuguese: [ˈklubi dʒi ʁeˈɡataz du flaˈmẽɡu]), more commonly referred to as simply Flamengo, is a Brazilian multi-sports club based in Rio de Janeiro, in the neighbourhood of Gávea, best known for its professional football team. Founded in 1895 as a rowing club — the football department dates to 1911 — Flamengo is widely regarded as Brazil's most popular club, with an estimated fan base of over 40 million supporters.

History

Flamengo was founded on 15 November 1895 by a group of rowers in the Flamengo neighbourhood of Rio de Janeiro. The football team was formed in 1911 by dissatisfied Fluminense players and won its first Rio state championship in 1914. The club's golden era came in the late 1970s and early 1980s under stars such as Zico, winning the Copa Libertadores and the Intercontinental Cup in 1981 (beating Liverpool 3–0 in Tokyo), plus three Brazilian championships in 1980, 1982 and 1983.

After leaner decades, a financial restructuring from 2013 transformed the club's finances. Under coach Jorge Jesus in 2019, Flamengo won both the Brasileirão and the Copa Libertadores — the latter in a dramatic late comeback against River Plate in Lima — and added a third Libertadores title in 2022.

Rivalries

Flamengo's fiercest rivalry is the Fla-Flu derby with Fluminense; it also contests major clashes with Vasco da Gama (the Clássico dos Milhões) and Botafogo. The club plays home matches at the Maracanã stadium.

Honours

  • Copa Libertadores: 3 titles (1981, 2019, 2022 — a fourth was won in 2025)

  • Copa do Brasil: 5 titles
