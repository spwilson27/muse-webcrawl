Army and Air Force Exchange Service

The Army and Air Force Exchange Service (AAFES), commonly called the Exchange, is a United States Department of Defense agency that provides retail stores and services on Army and Air Force installations around the world. Its stores sell tax-free goods to authorised patrons, principally active-duty service members, reservists, retirees and their families.

Mission

AAFES states its mission as providing quality merchandise and services at competitively low prices and generating earnings that support Morale, Welfare and Recreation programs for soldiers and airmen. It is one of the oldest and largest of the military exchange systems, alongside the Navy Exchange and Marine Corps Exchange.

Facilities

Exchange facilities range from large main stores (comparable to department stores) to troop stores, shoppettes, express convenience outlets, fast-food concessions, barber shops and cinemas, including stores in deployed and contingency locations.
