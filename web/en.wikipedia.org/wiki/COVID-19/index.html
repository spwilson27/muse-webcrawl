COVID-19

COVID-19 (coronavirus disease 2019) is a contagious disease caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus. Symptoms range from none to fever, cough, loss of taste or smell, breathlessness and pneumonia; severe disease is more likely in older people and those with conditions such as diabetes, obesity and heart disease. Long COVID — persistent symptoms after the acute illness — affects a minority of patients.

The disease spreads mainly through respiratory particles. Diagnosis is by PCR or antigen tests. Treatment includes supportive care, oxygen, dexamethasone for severe cases, and antivirals such as nirmatrelvir/ritonavir (Paxlovid); vaccination greatly reduces the risk of severe illness and death. First identified in Wuhan in December 2019, COVID-19 became the defining global health event of the early 2020s.
