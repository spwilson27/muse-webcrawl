Michael Leslie Jones (born 27 December 1944) is an English guitarist, songwriter and record producer, best known as the founder and leader of the rock band Foreigner. He is the band's only continuous member since its formation in 1976 and has written or co-written most of its hit songs, including "Feels Like the First Time", "Cold as Ice", "Hot Blooded", "Double Vision", "Urgent", "Juke Box Hero", "Waiting for a Girl Like You" and "I Want to Know What Love Is".

Early life and career

Jones was born in Portsmouth, Hampshire. He began playing guitar as a teenager and worked as a session musician in France during the 1960s, where he played with artists including Johnny Hallyday and Sylvie Vartan. In the early 1970s he formed the band Wonderwheel with drummer Gary Wright, and when Wright left to form Spooky Tooth, Jones joined him, appearing on Spooky Tooth's later albums.

After moving to New York, Jones worked as an A&R man for Warner Bros. and played in the Leslie West Band before deciding to form his own group.

Foreigner

In 1976 Jones assembled Foreigner from a mix of British and American musicians: singer Lou Gramm, drummer Dennis Elliott, keyboardist Al Greenwood, bassist Ed Gagliardi and multi-instrumentalist Ian McDonald. The band's self-titled debut album (1977) was a major success, and Foreigner went on to become one of the best-selling rock acts of all time, with worldwide sales exceeding 80 million records.

Jones produced or co-produced all of Foreigner's studio albums. Beyond the band, he has worked as a producer for other artists, most notably co-producing Billy Joel's album Storm Front (1989), and he played guitar on Van Halen's 5150 sessions as an outside contributor during its production.

Personal life and legacy

Jones has battled health problems in later years, which reduced his touring schedule, though he continued to appear at selected Foreigner performances. In 2013 he and Lou Gramm were inducted into the Songwriters Hall of Fame. Foreigner was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2024.
