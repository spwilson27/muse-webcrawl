Jiuzhaigou

Jiuzhaigou ("Nine Settlement Valley") is a nature reserve and national park in the Min Mountains of northern Sichuan, China, famous for its multi-coloured travertine lakes, waterfalls and snow-capped peaks. It was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1992.

Geography

The reserve covers about 720 square kilometres at elevations between roughly 2,000 and 4,800 metres. Its Y-shaped valleys — Rize, Zechawa and Shuzheng — contain over a hundred lakes noted for vivid blue, green and turquoise water, including Five Flower Lake, Mirror Lake and Long Lake, and waterfalls such as Nuorilang and Pearl Shoal Falls.

People and ecology

The valley is named for nine Tibetan villages within it. Its forests are home to giant pandas and Sichuan golden snub-nosed monkeys. The park is one of China's most popular tourist destinations; a major earthquake in August 2017 damaged some features and led to a phased reopening.
