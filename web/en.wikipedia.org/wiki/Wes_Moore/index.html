Westley Watende Omari Moore (born 1978) is an American politician, author and former nonprofit executive who has served as the 63rd governor of Maryland since January 2023, the first African American governor in the state's history.

Early life and career

Raised in Baltimore and the Bronx, Moore attended Valley Forge Military Academy, Johns Hopkins University, and Oxford as a Rhodes Scholar. He served as a U.S. Army officer in Afghanistan with the 82nd Airborne Division, then worked in finance and as chief executive of the Robin Hood Foundation, a large anti-poverty nonprofit.

Author

His bestselling book The Other Wes Moore (2010) contrasts his own life with that of another Baltimore man of the same name serving a life sentence for murder, exploring how environment and support shape outcomes.

Governor of Maryland

Moore won the Democratic nomination and the general election in 2022. His administration has focused on poverty reduction, public service, education and infrastructure, and he led the state's response to the 2024 collapse of the Francis Scott Key Bridge.
