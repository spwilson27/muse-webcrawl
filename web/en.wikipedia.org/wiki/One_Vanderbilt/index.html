One Vanderbilt

One Vanderbilt is a 93-story supertall skyscraper at the corner of 42nd Street and Vanderbilt Avenue in Midtown Manhattan, New York City. Designed by Kohn Pedersen Fox and developed by SL Green Realty, it opened in September 2020. At 1,401 feet (427 metres) including its spire, it is among the tallest buildings in New York City.

Design and construction

The tower stands immediately west of Grand Central Terminal and incorporates direct connections to the terminal and the subway, along with a public transit hall and a pedestrianised section of Vanderbilt Avenue, delivered as part of the rezoning of Midtown East in exchange for additional floor area. Its terra-cotta and glass facade echoes the materials of Grand Central. The building contains about 1.7 million square feet of office space.

Summit One Vanderbilt

The top floors house Summit One Vanderbilt, an observation experience opened in October 2021, featuring mirrored immersive rooms designed by Kenzo Digital and glass sky boxes ("Levitation") projecting from the facade more than 1,000 feet above Madison Avenue.
