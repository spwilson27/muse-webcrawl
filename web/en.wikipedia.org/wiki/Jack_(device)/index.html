Jack (device)

A jack is a mechanical device used to lift heavy loads or apply great forces. A mechanical jack employs a screw thread or hydraulic cylinder for power; common types include scissor jacks fitted to cars for changing tyres, bottle and floor jacks used in workshops, and the large jacks used to raise buildings or support structures during construction.

Types

The scissor jack uses a lead screw driving a folding diamond-shaped linkage. Hydraulic jacks, including the bottle jack and the trolley (floor) jack, use Pascal's principle to multiply force through an incompressible fluid. Rack-and-pinion and screw jacks are used for railway maintenance, stage machinery and house lifting.

Safety

Because a jack supports a load at a small contact area and may fail or slip, safety standards require that persons never work beneath a vehicle supported only by a jack; jack stands are used to hold the load once raised.
