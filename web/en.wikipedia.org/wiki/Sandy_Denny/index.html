Sandy Denny

Alexandra Elene MacLean "Sandy" Denny (6 January 1947 – 21 April 1978) was an English singer-songwriter, lead singer of the British folk rock band Fairport Convention. She has been described as "the pre-eminent British folk rock singer".

Sandy Denny
Born: 6 January 1947, London
Died: 21 April 1978, London (aged 31)
Genres: Folk, folk rock
Associated acts: Fairport Convention, Fotheringay, Strawbs

Career

Denny joined Fairport Convention in 1968, singing on What We Did on Our Holidays, Unhalfbricking and Liege & Lief (1969), the latter a landmark of British folk rock. Her song "Who Knows Where the Time Goes?" became a standard, covered by Judy Collins and many others. She is the only guest vocalist on a Led Zeppelin album, duetting with Robert Plant on "The Battle of Evermore" (Led Zeppelin IV, 1971).

Solo work

After forming Fotheringay, she recorded solo albums including The North Star Grassman and the Ravens (1971), Sandy (1972) and Like an Old Fashioned Waltz (1974).

Death and legacy

Denny died in 1978 from injuries following a fall, aged 31. Her voice and songwriting continue to be widely admired; she was twice voted best female singer by Melody Maker.
