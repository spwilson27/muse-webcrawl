Brewster McCloud

Brewster McCloud

Directed by: Robert Altman

Starring: Bud Cort, Sally Kellerman, Michael Murphy, Shelley Duvall

Released: 1970

Brewster McCloud is a 1970 American black comedy film directed by Robert Altman. It stars Bud Cort as a young loner living in the fallout shelter of the Houston Astrodome who constructs a pair of wings and dreams of flying, while a series of stranglings of bigoted victims occurs around him.

Cast and production

The film co-stars Sally Kellerman as a mysterious guardian figure and marked the film debut of Shelley Duvall. Margaret Hamilton, of The Wizard of Oz, also appears. The film's framing device features lectures on birds by "The Lecturer", played by René Auberjonois.

Reception

Initially a commercial disappointment, the film has since developed a cult following as one of Altman's eccentric early works.
