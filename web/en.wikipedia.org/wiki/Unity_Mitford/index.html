Unity Mitford

Unity Valkyrie Freeman-Mitford (8 August 1914 – 28 May 1948) was a British socialite, one of the Mitford sisters, who became known for her devotion to Nazism and her personal acquaintance with Adolf Hitler.

Early life

Unity Mitford was born in London and raised at the family homes in Oxfordshire, the fourth daughter of David Freeman-Mitford, 2nd Baron Redesdale. She was, family lore held, conceived in the town of Unity in Ontario, Canada, and her middle name, Valkyrie, reflected her father's interest in Germanic mythology.

In Germany

Mitford moved to Munich in 1934, having become an admirer of Nazism and of Hitler, and contrived to meet him in February 1935 at the Osteria Bavaria restaurant. She became part of Hitler's social circle, attended Nuremberg rallies, and made virulently antisemitic speeches and writings, including an open letter to Der Stürmer in 1935. Her sister Diana married the British fascist leader Oswald Mosley, with Hitler as a wedding guest.

Suicide attempt and death

On 3 September 1939, the day Britain declared war on Germany, Mitford shot herself in the head in Munich's English Garden. She survived but was left permanently impaired by the bullet lodged in her brain. Hitler arranged her medical care and repatriation. She died in 1948 in Oban, Scotland, of meningitis caused by swelling around the bullet.
