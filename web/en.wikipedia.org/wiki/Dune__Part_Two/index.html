Dune: Part Two is a 2024 American epic science fiction film directed and co-produced by Denis Villeneuve, who co-wrote the screenplay with Jon Spaihts. It is the sequel to Dune (2021) and the second of a two-part adaptation of Frank Herbert's 1965 novel Dune, covering the second half of the book.

Plot

Paul Atreides unites with Chani and the Fremen of Arrakis, learning their desert ways and riding the giant sandworms, while seeking revenge against House Harkonnen and the Emperor for the destruction of his family. As his prescient visions grow, Paul faces a choice between the love of his life and the fate of the known universe, as a holy war in his name looms.

Reception

The film premiered in London in February 2024 and was released in the United States on 1 March 2024. It grossed over $714 million worldwide and received widespread critical acclaim for its direction, cinematography, visual effects and performances. At the 97th Academy Awards it won Best Sound and Best Visual Effects from eight nominations. A third film, based on Dune Messiah, is in production.
