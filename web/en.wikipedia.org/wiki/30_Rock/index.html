30 Rock

30 Rock is an American satirical sitcom created by Tina Fey, airing on NBC from 11 October 2006 to 31 January 2013, with 138 episodes over seven seasons. It is loosely based on Fey's experiences as head writer of Saturday Night Live.


Creator: Tina Fey
Starring: Tina Fey, Alec Baldwin, Tracy Morgan, Jane Krakowski, Jack McBrayer
Network: NBC
Seasons: 7; Episodes: 138

Premise

Liz Lemon (Fey), head writer of sketch show TGS with Tracy Jordan, juggles her chaotic cast — volatile star Tracy Jordan and vain Jenna Maroney — her mentor and boss, network executive Jack Donaghy (Alec Baldwin), and her own stalled personal life, at 30 Rockefeller Plaza.

Reception

Despite modest ratings, the fast-paced, surreal series was a critical favourite, winning 16 Primetime Emmy Awards including three consecutive Outstanding Comedy Series awards, plus Golden Globes and a Peabody Award.
