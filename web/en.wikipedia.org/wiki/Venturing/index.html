Venturing

Venturing is a core program of Scouting America (formerly the Boy Scouts of America) for young men and women ages 14 to 20 (or 13 and having completed the eighth grade). Introduced in 1998 as the successor to earlier senior and Exploring programs for older youth, Venturing emphasises high adventure, leadership, personal growth, and service. Its local units are called crews.

Program

Venturing crews choose a specialty or programme focus — such as outdoor high adventure, arts and hobbies, sports, or youth ministry — and plan their own activities under adult advisers. The programme's methods include leadership, group activities, adult association, recognition, and teaching others. The Venturing motto is "Lead the Adventure". The distinctive uniform is a forest-green shirt.

Advancement and awards

Venturing has its own advancement system, separate from Scouts BSA. Beginning in 2014–2015, the programme was reorganised around four awards — Venturing, Discovery, Pathfinder, and Summit — replacing the earlier Bronze, Gold, and Silver awards. The Summit Award is the highest in Venturing, comparable to Eagle Scout in Scouts BSA. Specialty awards include the Ranger Award for outdoor skills, the Quest Award for sports, and the TRUST Award for religious life.

History

Venturing traces its origins to senior Scouting programmes of the 1930s such as Sea Scouts and Explorer Scouts. The Exploring programme, which had become largely career-oriented, was split in 1998: career-based posts became Learning for Life Exploring, while the traditional outdoor-oriented programme became Venturing.
