Escuela Nacional Preparatoria

Escuela Nacional Preparatoria

Motto: Amor, Orden y Progreso ("Love, Order and Progress")

Founded: February 3, 1868

Type: Public preparatory school system

Parent institution: National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM)

Location: Mexico City, Mexico

The Escuela Nacional Preparatoria (ENP; National Preparatory School) is a Mexican public educational institution forming part of the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM). It provides the bachillerato (upper secondary) level of education and serves as a feeder system for the university. It is one of the oldest and most prestigious preparatory schools in Mexico.

History

The ENP was founded under President Benito Juárez by decree of December 2, 1867, as part of the educational reforms following the Reform War and the French intervention. Its first director was the physician and positivist philosopher Gabino Barreda, who organised the curriculum on the principles of Auguste Comte's positivism, reflected in the school's motto, "Love, Order and Progress." Classes began on February 3, 1868, in the former Jesuit Colegio de San Ildefonso in the historic centre of Mexico City.

The historic San Ildefonso building later became famous as the birthplace of Mexican muralism: in the 1920s, artists including Diego Rivera, José Clemente Orozco, and David Alfaro Siqueiros painted murals on its walls under the patronage of education minister José Vasconcelos. The building is now the Museo Colegio de San Ildefonso.

Campuses

The ENP today comprises nine campuses (planteles) across Mexico City, each named for a distinguished figure in Mexican education and culture, including Gabino Barreda (Plantel 1), Erasmo Castellanos Quinto (Plantel 2), Justo Sierra (Plantel 3), Vidal Castañeda y Nájera (Plantel 4), José Vasconcelos (Plantel 5), Antonio Caso (Plantel 6), Ezequiel A. Chávez (Plantel 7), Miguel E. Schulz (Plantel 8), and Pedro de Alba (Plantel 9). Graduates of the ENP enjoy a pase reglamentado — preferential admission to UNAM undergraduate programmes.
