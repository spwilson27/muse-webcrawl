80 Plus

80 Plus (trademarked 80 PLUS) is a voluntary certification program launched in 2004 to promote efficient energy use in computer power supply units (PSUs). Certification requires a power supply to be at least 80% energy efficient at 20%, 50%, and 100% of its rated load, with a true power factor of 0.9 or greater.

Tiers

Higher tiers were added above the base certification — Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, and Titanium — requiring progressively higher efficiency, peaking at 94% at 50% load for Titanium units, which also adds a 10% load efficiency requirement. The program is administered by CLEAResult, and certified products are published in a public database.

Significance

More efficient power supplies waste less electricity as heat, reducing cooling needs and operating costs, particularly in data centers where thousands of units run continuously. The 80 Plus label became a standard marketing feature for consumer and server PSUs.
