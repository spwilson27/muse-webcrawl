The Ice Follies, formally the Shipstads and Johnson Ice Follies, was a touring ice-skating revue founded in 1936 by brothers Eddie and Roy Shipstad and their friend Oscar Johnson. It was one of the first and most successful travelling ice shows, pioneering the lavish production numbers, chorus lines of precision skaters, comedy acts, and elaborate costumes that defined the genre.

History

The company's first performance took place in Tulsa, Oklahoma, in November 1936. The Ice Follies toured arenas across North America annually for six decades, serving as a showcase for champion skaters and a major employer of professional skaters. In 1949 it introduced "Disney characters on ice" segments by licence, and the Follies company became the vehicle for early performances by future Olympic stars.

In 1979 the Ice Follies was acquired by Irvin and Kenneth Feld, who in 1981 launched Walt Disney's World on Ice; the Follies continued alongside it before the two productions were merged in 1997 under the Disney on Ice name.
