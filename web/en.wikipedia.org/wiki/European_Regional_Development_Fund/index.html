European Regional Development Fund (ERDF)
Founded1975
TypeEU structural fund
PurposeRegional development and cohesion

The European Regional Development Fund (ERDF) is one of the European Structural and Investment Funds of the European Union. Created in 1975, it aims to strengthen economic and social cohesion by correcting imbalances between regions.

Activities

The ERDF finances investment in infrastructure, innovation and research, digital connectivity, support for small and medium-sized enterprises, and the transition to a low-carbon economy. Funding is concentrated on less-developed regions, with programmes managed jointly by the European Commission and national or regional authorities.

Budget

Together with the Cohesion Fund and the European Social Fund, the ERDF forms part of the EU's cohesion policy, which accounts for roughly a third of the EU budget; the ERDF allocation for 2021–2027 is over €200 billion.
