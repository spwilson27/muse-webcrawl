Middle Eastern studies

Middle Eastern studies is a field of study concerning the history, culture, politics, economy and geography of the modern Middle East and North Africa, drawing on disciplines including history, political science, anthropology, sociology and languages such as Arabic, Persian, Turkish and Hebrew.

History

The field grew out of earlier Oriental studies and expanded rapidly in Western universities after the Second World War. Edward Said's Orientalism (1978) prompted extensive debate about Western representations of the region and reshaped approaches within the field.

Institutions

Research is organised through university centres and associations such as the Middle East Studies Association (MESA), founded in 1966, and journals devoted to regional history, politics and culture.
