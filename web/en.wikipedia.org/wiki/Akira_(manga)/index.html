Akira (manga)

Akira (アキラ) is a Japanese cyberpunk manga series written and illustrated by Katsuhiro Otomo. It was serialized in Young Magazine from 1982 to 1990 and collected in six volumes comprising more than 2,000 pages. Set in a post-apocalyptic Neo-Tokyo in 2019, decades after a mysterious explosion destroyed the city, the story follows biker gang leader Shōtarō Kaneda and his friend Tetsuo Shima, who develops uncontrollable psychic powers after a motorcycle accident involving a child esper linked to the secret government project "Akira".

Reception and influence

Akira is widely regarded as one of the greatest manga ever created and a landmark of the cyberpunk genre. It was among the first manga published in its entirety in English (by Marvel's Epic Comics imprint, in a colourised edition), helping to introduce manga to Western audiences. Otomo's own 1988 anime film adaptation, though condensing the plot and ending differently from the still-unfinished manga, became a foundational work of anime worldwide. The series influenced generations of creators in comics, animation, and science-fiction film, and its imagery — Kaneda's red motorcycle, the expanding psychic mass of Tetsuo — remains iconic. In 1984 the manga received the Kodansha Manga Award for the general category.
