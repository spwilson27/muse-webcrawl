Iraq

Iraq, officially the Republic of Iraq, is a country in West Asia, bordered by Turkey, Iran, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Jordan and Syria. Its capital and largest city is Baghdad, and its population is about 45 million.

Iraq covers much of ancient Mesopotamia, the 'cradle of civilisation' between the Tigris and Euphrates, where writing, law codes and some of the world's first cities emerged at Sumer, Akkad, Babylon and Assyria. Modern Iraq was created from Ottoman provinces after the First World War and became independent from Britain in 1932. Saddam Hussein's Ba'athist regime fought a devastating war with Iran (1980-1988) and invaded Kuwait in 1990, triggering the Gulf War. A US-led invasion toppled Saddam in 2003, unleashing years of insurgency, sectarian war and the rise of ISIS, which seized Mosul in 2014 and was defeated territorially by 2017. Iraq is one of the world's largest oil producers.
