Silko

Silko may refer to several topics; most prominently it is the surname of Leslie Marmon Silko (born 1948), a Native American writer of Laguna Pueblo, Mexican and European ancestry, and a key figure in the Native American Renaissance. Her works include the novel Ceremony (1977), Storyteller (1981), Almanac of the Dead (1991) and Gardens in the Dunes (1999). She received a MacArthur "genius" grant in 1981.

The name may also refer to places, companies and other persons sharing the surname; see the main article on Leslie Marmon Silko.
