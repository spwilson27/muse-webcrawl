Sebastian Kurz (born 27 August 1986) is an Austrian former politician who served as Chancellor of Austria from December 2017 to May 2019 and again from January 2020 to October 2021. As leader of the Austrian People's Party (ÖVP) from 2017, he became, at 31, the youngest head of government in the world at his first appointment.

Early career

Kurz grew up in Vienna's Meidling district, joined the ÖVP youth organization as a teenager, and studied law at the University of Vienna without completing the degree. He became Secretary of State for Integration in 2011 and, in December 2013, Austria's youngest-ever Foreign Minister at age 27. During the 2015 European migrant crisis he advocateded closing the Balkan route and took restrictive positions on immigration and integration.

Chancellorship

Taking over the ÖVP in May 2017 and rebranding it with the color turquoise, Kurz led the party to first place in the October 2017 election and formed a coalition with the right-wing Freedom Party (FPÖ). His government cut taxes and tightened asylum rules. The coalition collapsed in May 2019 after the "Ibiza affair" video scandal involving FPÖ leader Heinz-Christian Strache; a no-confidence vote removed Kurz, making him the first Austrian chancellor so removed.

After the ÖVP won the September 2019 snap election, Kurz returned as Chancellor in January 2020 at the head of a coalition with the Greens. His second government managed Austria's response to the COVID-19 pandemic. In October 2021 he resigned amid a corruption investigation into alleged misuse of public funds for favorable media coverage, and he left politics entirely in December 2021, moving into international business consulting. In February 2024 a Vienna court convicted him of making false statements to a parliamentary inquiry; he received a suspended sentence and appealed.
