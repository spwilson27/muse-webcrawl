Sun Yee On

Sun Yee On (Chinese: 新義安, "New Righteousness and Peace") is one of the largest triad societies in Hong Kong and China, and among the most powerful Chinese organised-crime groups worldwide, with membership estimates in the tens of thousands.

History

Sun Yee On was founded in Hong Kong in 1919 by Heung Chin, a native of Teochew (Chaozhou), and grew out of labour and fraternal associations. After Heung Chin was deported to Taiwan, leadership passed within the Heung family, and the group expanded into legitimate businesses including film production — notably Win's Entertainment and China Star Entertainment, founded by Charles Heung and associated with his brother Jimmy Heung.

Activities

Like other major triads such as the 14K and Wo Shing Wo, Sun Yee On has been linked by police and researchers to drug trafficking, extortion, illegal gambling, loan-sharking and money laundering, alongside extensive investments in entertainment and property. It is regarded as among the most structured and business-oriented of the Hong Kong triads.
