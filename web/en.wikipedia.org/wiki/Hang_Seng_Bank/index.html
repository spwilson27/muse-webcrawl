Hang Seng Bank

Hang Seng Bank Limited
Founded: 1933
Headquarters: Central, Hong Kong
Parent: HSBC (majority shareholder)
Listed: Hong Kong Stock Exchange (SEHK: 11)

Hang Seng Bank Limited is a Hong Kong–based banking and financial services company with headquarters in Central, Hong Kong. It is one of Hong Kong's leading listed companies by market capitalisation and is a member of the HSBC Group, which holds a majority stake following its acquisition in 1965 during a bank run on Hang Seng.

History

The bank was founded in 1933 as Hang Sang Ngan Ho, a money-changing shop in Shanghai Street, Kowloon, by partners including Lam Bing Yim and Ho Tim. Its name, "Hang Seng" (恒生), means "ever-growing". The bank survived the Second World War and grew rapidly in the postwar decades.

Hang Seng Index

The bank's name is attached to the Hang Seng Index, the principal stock market index of Hong Kong, which was launched in 1969 by Hang Seng's research head Stanley Kwan and is now maintained by Hang Seng Indexes Company, a subsidiary.
