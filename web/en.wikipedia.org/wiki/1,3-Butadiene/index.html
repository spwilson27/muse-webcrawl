1,3-Butadiene is an organic compound with the formula (CH₂=CH)₂. It is a colourless, flammable gas that is important industrially as a monomer in the production of synthetic rubber.

Production

Butadiene is produced mainly as a co-product of steam cracking of hydrocarbons for ethylene, from which it is separated by extractive distillation. It can also be made by catalytic dehydrogenation of butane or butenes, a route historically important during the Second World War rubber programmes.

Uses

Most butadiene is polymerised: styrene-butadiene rubber (SBR) for tyres, polybutadiene rubber, and ABS resin are major products. It is also a classic diene in Diels–Alder reactions and a feedstock for adiponitrile and chloroprene.

Health

1,3-Butadiene is classified as a known human carcinogen; occupational exposure is regulated, and it is monitored as an air pollutant from vehicle exhaust and industrial emissions.
