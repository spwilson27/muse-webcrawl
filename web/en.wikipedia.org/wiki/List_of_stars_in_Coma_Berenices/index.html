This is the list of notable stars in the constellation Coma Berenices, sorted by decreasing brightness. Coma Berenices is a small, faint northern constellation whose stars were historically part of Leo; it contains the North Galactic Pole and the rich Coma Cluster of galaxies.

Stars with exoplanets

HD 114762 in Coma Berenices hosted one of the earliest substellar companions reported (1989), long debated as either a brown dwarf or massive planet. 11 Comae Berenices b is a confirmed substellar companion of about 19 Jupiter masses.
