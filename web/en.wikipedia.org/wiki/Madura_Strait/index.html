Madura Strait

The Madura Strait is a stretch of sea separating the islands of Java and Madura in Indonesia. Roughly 150 kilometres (93 mi) long, it connects the Java Sea to the north with the Bali Strait and the Indian Ocean approaches, and its shores include Surabaya, Indonesia's second-largest city and principal eastern port.

Crossing

Since 10 June 2009 the strait has been spanned by the Suramadu Bridge, at 5.4 km Indonesia's longest bridge, linking Surabaya on Java with Bangkalan on Madura. Before its opening, ferries from Ujung (Surabaya) to Kamal (Madura) carried the traffic.

Geography

The strait contains small islands including Kambang and Giliraja, and the shallow waters near Surabaya are heavily used by shipping entering Tanjung Perak harbour.
