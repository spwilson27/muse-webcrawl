Felipe Gonzalez

Felipe Gonzalez may refer to several people; this unaccented spelling is most often used in English-language databases for the Spanish prime minister whose name is properly written Felipe González.

Felipe González (born 1942) was Prime Minister of Spain from 1982 to 1996. For the full article, see Felipe González.
