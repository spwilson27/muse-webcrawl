Booster Gold

Booster Gold is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by Dan Jurgens, he first appeared in Booster Gold #1 (February 1986). His real name is Michael Jon Carter, a disgraced former college football star from 25th-century Gotham City who stole futuristic technology — a Legion of Super-Heroes flight ring, a force-field belt and a power suit — from a museum, along with the robot Skeets, and travelled back to the 20th century to become a celebrity superhero.

Initially portrayed as a glory-seeking showman who pursued corporate sponsorships, Booster Gold gradually developed into a genuine hero, serving as a long-standing member of the Justice League International alongside his best friend Blue Beetle (Ted Kord). Later series recast him as a secret guardian of the timestream, deliberately maintaining his buffoonish public image while quietly preventing damage to history.
