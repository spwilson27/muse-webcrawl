Cherry picker

A cherry picker being used for overhead work (image placeholder)

A cherry picker, also known as an aerial work platform, boom lift, or (in the United States) a bucket truck, is a type of elevating work platform that consists of a bucket or platform at the end of a hydraulic lifting system. It allows workers to reach elevated positions safely, originally for picking fruit in orchards, hence the name.

Description

The platform is attached to an articulated or telescopic boom mounted on a truck, van, trailer, or self-propelled chassis. Controls both in the bucket and at the base allow the operator to position the platform with precision. Working heights commonly range from about 10 to over 50 metres.

Uses

Cherry pickers are used by utility workers maintaining overhead power and telephone lines, tree surgeons, window cleaners, film and television crews (for lighting and camera positions), firefighters (in the form of aerial ladder platforms), warehouse operators, and construction and maintenance workers. Their use is regulated by safety standards requiring harnesses, training, and inspection, because falls and contact with overhead power lines are significant hazards.
