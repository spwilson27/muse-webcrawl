Llangollen (Welsh: [ɬaŋˈɡɔɬɛn]) is a town and community in Denbighshire, north-east Wales, situated on the River Dee and at the edge of the Berwyn mountains. It takes its name from St Collen, a 6th/7th-century saint who founded a church there.

Attractions and history

The town is known for the Llangollen International Musical Eisteddfod, held annually since 1947, for the ruins of Castell Dinas Brân above the town, Valle Crucis Abbey nearby, Plas Newydd — home of the Ladies of Llangollen, Lady Eleanor Butler and Sarah Ponsonby — and the Pontcysyllte Aqueduct and Canal UNESCO World Heritage Site, which includes the Llangollen Canal. The Llangollen Railway is a preserved heritage line running toward Corwen.

Geography

Llangollen lies on the A5 London–Holyhead road engineered by Thomas Telford. The Horseshoe Falls on the Dee feed the canal.
