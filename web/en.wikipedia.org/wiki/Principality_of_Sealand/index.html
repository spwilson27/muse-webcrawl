Principality of Sealand

Motto: E Mare Libertas ("From the Sea, Freedom")

Structure: Unitary constitutional monarchy (claimed)

Established: 2 September 1967

Area claimed: HM Fort Roughs, North Sea

Currency: Sealand dollar (pegged to USD)

The Principality of Sealand is an unrecognised micronation that claims HM Fort Roughs, an offshore platform in the North Sea about 12 kilometres (7.5 miles) off the coast of Suffolk, England, as its territory. Roughs Tower is a Maunsell Sea Fort built during the Second World War by the British.

History

In 1967, former British Army major Paddy Roy Bates occupied the fort, which he had taken over from pirate radio broadcasters, and declared it the independent Principality of Sealand, naming himself Prince Roy and his wife Joan Princess. At the time, the fort lay outside British territorial waters. In 1968 a British court ruled it had no jurisdiction over the platform, an outcome Bates regarded as de facto recognition.

In 1978, German and Dutch mercenaries briefly seized the fort and captured Bates's son Michael, but the Bates family retook it by force; the German government negotiated the prisoners' release, which Bates again cited as recognition. A fire in 2006 caused serious damage. After Roy Bates's death in 2012, Michael Bates succeeded him as head of the family. Sealand issues its own stamps, coins, and novelty passports, and sells noble titles, which fund its upkeep. It has never been recognised by any sovereign state, and its claims are generally not taken seriously by governments, though it remains the world's best-known micronation.
