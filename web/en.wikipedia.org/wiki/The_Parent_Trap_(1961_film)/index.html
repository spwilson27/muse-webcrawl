The Parent Trap (1961 film)

The Parent Trap is a 1961 American family comedy film produced by Walt Disney and directed by David Swift. It stars Hayley Mills in a dual role as twin sisters who scheme to reunite their divorced parents, played by Maureen O'Hara and Brian Keith. It is based on Erich Kästner's 1949 German novel Lisa and Lottie.

DirectorDavid Swift

Plot

Twins Susan and Sharon, separated in infancy when their parents divorced, meet by chance at a summer camp. Realising they are sisters, they swap places and plot to bring Mitch and Maggie back together, foiling Mitch's fiancée along the way.

Legacy

The film was a critical and commercial success, earning two Academy Award nominations. It spawned television sequels in the 1980s and a well-known 1998 remake starring Lindsay Lohan.
