The 2024 Copa América was the 48th edition of the Copa América, South America's premier men's football tournament, held in the United States from 20 June to 14 July 2024. It featured all ten CONMEBOL nations plus six invited CONCACAF teams. Argentina defeated Colombia 1–0 after extra time in the final to win a record 16th title.

Tournament

Matches were played in 14 stadiums across the United States, with the final at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens, Florida. Defending champions Argentina won all three group games. Uruguay, Colombia and Venezuela were among the group winners, while the hosts, the United States, were eliminated in the group stage.

Final

The final was delayed by more than an hour because of crowd disorder outside the stadium. After a goalless 90 minutes in which Lionel Messi left the game injured, Lautaro Martínez scored the winning goal in the 112th minute. Martínez finished as top scorer with five goals; Colombia's James Rodríguez was named the tournament's best player.
