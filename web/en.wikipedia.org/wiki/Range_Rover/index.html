Range Rover
Manufacturer: Land Rover (Jaguar Land Rover)
Production: 1970–present
Class: Luxury 4x4 / full-size luxury SUV
Assembly: Solihull, England

The Range Rover is a 4x4 luxury sport utility vehicle produced by Land Rover, a marque of Jaguar Land Rover. Launched in 1970, the Range Rover was one of the first vehicles to combine serious off-road capability with a comfortable, well-appointed interior, and is widely credited with creating the luxury SUV segment.

First generation (1970–1996)

The original Range Rover "Classic" was introduced in June 1970 with a 3.5-litre Rover V8 petrol engine, a steel ladder-frame chassis, coil-spring suspension and permanent four-wheel drive. Initially available only as a two-door, a four-door version followed in 1981. An example was exhibited at the Louvre in Paris as an "exemplary work of industrial design".

Later generations

The second generation (P38A, 1994–2002) added greater luxury and air suspension. The third generation (L322, 2002–2012) adopted monocoque construction. The fourth generation (L405, 2012–2022) used an all-aluminium body, and the fifth generation (L460) was launched in 2021 with petrol, diesel, plug-in hybrid and (planned) fully electric variants.

Sub-brands

The Range Rover name now heads a family of models including the Range Rover Sport, Range Rover Evoque and Range Rover Velar. The flagship Range Rover remains a status symbol and is used by heads of state, including members of the British royal family.
