Zoom (software)

Zoom

Developer: Zoom Video Communications

Released: 2011

Type: Video-conferencing and communications platform

Zoom is a cloud-based communications platform developed by Zoom Video Communications, providing video meetings, voice calling, webinars, chat and related workplace tools. It was founded by Eric Yuan, a former Cisco Webex engineer, and launched in 2011.

Growth

The software became a household name during the COVID-19 pandemic, when daily meeting participants rose to hundreds of millions in 2020 as workplaces, schools and families moved online; "Zoom" became a generic term for video calls.

Privacy and security

Rapid growth brought scrutiny of the platform's encryption claims and of "Zoombombing" disruptions, leading to a security overhaul, a 90-day feature freeze in 2020, and settlements with regulators.
