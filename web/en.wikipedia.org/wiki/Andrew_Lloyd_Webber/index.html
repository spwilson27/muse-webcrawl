Andrew Lloyd Webber

Andrew Lloyd Webber, Baron Lloyd-Webber (born 22 March 1948) is an English composer of musical theatre. Several of his musicals ran for more than a decade in London's West End and on Broadway. He has composed 21 musicals, film scores and a requiem mass, and is an EGOT winner.

Born22 March 1948, London, England
OccupationComposer, theatre producer
Notable worksCats, The Phantom of the Opera, Evita, Jesus Christ Superstar

Career

With lyricist Tim Rice he wrote Joseph and the Amazing Technicolor Dreamcoat (1968), Jesus Christ Superstar (1970) and Evita (1976). Solo successes followed: Cats (1981), Starlight Express, The Phantom of the Opera (1986) — Broadway's longest-running show until 2023 — Sunset Boulevard, School of Rock and Bad Cinderella.

Business and honours

His Really Useful Group owns London theatres. Knighted in 1992 and made a life peer in 1997, he sat in the House of Lords. He founded the Andrew Lloyd Webber Foundation supporting arts education.
