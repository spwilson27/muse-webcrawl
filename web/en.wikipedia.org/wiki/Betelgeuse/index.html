Betelgeuse

Betelgeuse is a red supergiant star in the constellation of Orion, marking the hunter's right shoulder, and usually the tenth-brightest star in the night sky. It is a semiregular variable star whose brightness variations are noticeable to the naked eye, and one of the largest stars known: its photosphere would engulf the orbits of Mercury through Mars and approach Jupiter's if placed at the Sun's position (radius estimates vary with measurement method).

Betelgeuse lies a few hundred light-years away (distance estimates have ranged around 400–650 ly) and is near the end of its life: it will explode as a Type II supernova within roughly the next 100,000 years, briefly shining as bright as the full Moon but posing no danger to Earth. In late 2019–early 2020 it dimmed dramatically — the 'Great Dimming' — exciting supernova speculation before being attributed to a dust cloud and surface cooling ejected by the star itself.
