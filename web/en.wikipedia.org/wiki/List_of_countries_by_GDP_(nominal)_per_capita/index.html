List of countries by GDP (nominal) per capita

Gross domestic product (GDP) per capita is the value of all final goods and services produced within a country in a given year divided by the average population for the same year. This article lists countries by nominal GDP per capita (in current US dollars), based primarily on International Monetary Fund estimates. The following figures are approximate, for a recent year (circa 2024).

Notes

Small financial centres and tax havens (Luxembourg, Ireland, Bermuda, the Cayman Islands, Monaco, Liechtenstein) show figures inflated by cross-border workers and multinational profit-shifting; Ireland's statistical office publishes modified GNI (GNI*) because GDP is so distorted by multinational activity. Oil-rich states such as Qatar, Kuwait, the UAE and Brunei also rank highly. Nominal per-capita figures fluctuate strongly with exchange rates; PPP-adjusted rankings differ substantially.
