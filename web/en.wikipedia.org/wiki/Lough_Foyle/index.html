Lough Foyle, sometimes Loch Foyle (from Irish Loch Feabhail), is the estuary of the River Foyle on the north coast of Ireland. It lies between County Londonderry in Northern Ireland (United Kingdom) and County Donegal in the Republic of Ireland. The city of Derry stands at the south-western end of the lough, where the Foyle narrows, and the lough opens to the Atlantic between Magilligan Point on the western shore and Greencastle on the Inishowen shore.

Geography and nature

The lough is a shallow sea inlet fringed by extensive intertidal mudflats and salt marshes which are internationally important for wildfowl and waders. Lough Foyle is designated a Ramsar site and a Special Protection Area; tens of thousands of wintering birds, including whooper swans, light-bellied brent geese, bar-tailed godwits and wigeon, use its shores. The eastern shore between Limavady and the sea is backed by the long dune system and strand at Magilligan, which includes a nature reserve and a Martello tower.

Practical navigation is governed by the port of Londonderry, which maintains the shipping channel into Derry. A car ferry links Greencastle with Magilligan Point across the mouth of the lough, and the Foyle is crossed downstream in Derry by the Craigavon Bridge and the Peace Bridge, and further south at Lifford.

Jurisdictional dispute

The status of Lough Foyle is a long-standing, low-level territorial dispute between the United Kingdom and Ireland. The whole of the lough was historically part of County Londonderry; after partition, Ireland claimed that the boundary ran along the eastern (County Londonderry) shore, while the United Kingdom claimed the median line — a disagreement never formally resolved, and managed in practice by pragmatic cooperation. The Foyle Fisheries Commission, a cross-border body established in 1952 and now part of the Loughs Agency under the Good Friday Agreement structures, manages fishing and aquaculture in the Foyle system for both jurisdictions, and similar practical arrangements cover navigation and oyster fisheries.
