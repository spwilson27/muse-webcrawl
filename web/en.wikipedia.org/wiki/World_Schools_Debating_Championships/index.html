World Schools Debating Championships

The World Schools Debating Championships (WSDC) is an annual international debating tournament for high-school students, held each year in a different country and conducted in English.

Format

National teams of three to five students debate in the World Schools style: three substantive speeches per side plus a reply speech, with prepared and impromptu motions. Teams compete in preliminary rounds before knockout stages leading to a grand final.

History

The tournament began in 1988 with six countries. It has grown to draw more than 60 nations; Australia, England, Canada, Singapore, South Africa and others have won titles. Hosts have included Doha, Cape Town, Bangkok, Zagreb, Singapore and — in online editions during the pandemic — a virtual format.
