The United States Army Combat Capabilities Development Command, or DEVCOM, is a major subordinate command of the United States Army Futures Command, responsible for the Army's research, development and engineering enterprise. Headquartered at Aberdeen Proving Ground, Maryland, DEVCOM employs thousands of scientists, engineers and technicians who research and develop technologies and capabilities for soldiers, spanning weapons, vehicles, aviation, soldier systems, networks and chemical-biological defence.

History

The command was established in 2003 as the Research, Development and Engineering Command (RDECOM), bringing together the Army's research laboratories and engineering centres under one headquarters. When Army Futures Command was created in 2018 to lead modernisation, RDECOM was transferred to it and in February 2019 redesignated the Combat Capabilities Development Command, aligning the science and technology workforce with the Army's modernisation priorities and cross-functional teams.

Organisation

DEVCOM includes the Army Research Laboratory (ARL), the Army's corporate research laboratory, together with specialised centres including the Armaments Center (Picatinny Arsenal), Aviation & Missile Center (Redstone Arsenal), Ground Vehicle Systems Center (Warren, Michigan), Soldier Center (Natick, Massachusetts), C5ISR Center (Aberdeen Proving Ground), Chemical Biological Center (Edgewood), and the Analysis Center, which provides systems analysis across the portfolio.
