David Holmgren

David Holmgren (born 1955) is an Australian environmental designer, author, and co-originator, with Bill Mollison, of the permaculture concept and movement.


Born: 1955, Fremantle, Western Australia
Occupation: Environmental designer, author
Known for: Co-creating permaculture

Work

Holmgren met Mollison in Tasmania in the 1970s; their collaboration produced Permaculture One (1978). While Mollison popularised the movement globally, Holmgren developed its design practice at his Melliodora property in Hepburn Springs, Victoria, a long-running demonstration site.

Ideas and books

His Permaculture: Principles and Pathways Beyond Sustainability (2002) codified twelve design principles. Later works, including Future Scenarios and RetroSuburbia (2018), apply permaculture to energy descent, climate adaptation, and suburban retrofit.
