Emergency vehicle

An emergency vehicle is a vehicle used by emergency services to respond to an incident. It is usually operated by designated agencies, often part of a government, including police, fire, and emergency medical services.

Equipment

Emergency vehicles are typically fitted with visual warnings such as flashing lights (light bars in red, blue, or amber) and audible warnings such as sirens and horns, together with distinctive livery and reflective markings. Motorists in most jurisdictions are legally required to yield the right of way to emergency vehicles operating their warning devices.

Types

Examples include police cars, fire engines, ambulances, rescue vehicles, and specialist units such as bomb disposal and hazardous materials vehicles.
