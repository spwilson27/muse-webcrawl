Coconut cream
TypePlant milk / cooking ingredient
Main ingredientsCoconut flesh, water
Fat contentTypically 19–22%

Coconut cream is a thick, rich liquid extracted from grated mature coconut flesh. It is similar to coconut milk but contains less water and a higher proportion of fat, giving it a consistency closer to that of dairy cream.

Production

Coconut cream is produced by steeping grated coconut flesh in hot water and squeezing it through cheesecloth. The fat-rich portion rises to the top and is skimmed off, or the first, least-diluted pressing is collected. Chilling canned coconut milk and skimming the solidified top layer yields a similar product at home.

Culinary uses

Coconut cream is used in curries, soups, desserts and drinks throughout Southeast Asian, South Asian, Pacific and Caribbean cuisines. A sweetened, stabilised variant called "cream of coconut" is used in cocktails such as the piña colada.
