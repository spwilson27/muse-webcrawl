Double A-side

A double A-side is a single in which both songs are designated as A-sides, meaning that both are promoted as the featured songs rather than one being the subordinate B-side. The practice originated in the 1960s, when artists and record labels wished to give equal prominence to two tracks on a 7-inch single. Famous examples include the Beatles' "Day Tripper" / "We Can Work It Out" (1965) and "Strawberry Fields Forever" / "Penny Lane" (1967), as well as Queen's "We Are the Champions" / "We Will Rock You".

Chart treatment

Because both songs on a double A-side are promoted equally, chart compilers historically treated the single as one entry, though radio stations might favour one track. In the digital download and streaming era, the concept persists when two lead tracks are released simultaneously from an album campaign.
