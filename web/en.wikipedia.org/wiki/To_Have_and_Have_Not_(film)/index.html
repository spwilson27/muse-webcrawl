To Have and Have Not (film)

To Have and Have Not
Directed by: Howard Hawks
Starring: Humphrey Bogart, Lauren Bacall, Walter Brennan
Released: 1944
Studio: Warner Bros.

To Have and Have Not is a 1944 American romantic war adventure film directed by Howard Hawks, loosely based on Ernest Hemingway's 1937 novel of the same name. It marked the screen debut of Lauren Bacall, then 19, opposite Humphrey Bogart.

Plot

In Vichy-controlled Martinique in 1940, American fishing-boat captain Harry 'Steve' Morgan (Bogart) reluctantly helps smuggle French Resistance members, while romancing the pickpocket singer Marie 'Slim' Browning (Bacall). Walter Brennan plays Harry's alcoholic mate Eddie.

Production

The screenplay was by Jules Furthman and William Faulkner — famously, Hemingway and Faulkner, two Nobel laureates, both worked on the story, though Faulkner reshaped it far from the novel. Bacall's line 'You know how to whistle, don't you, Steve? You just put your lips together and blow' became iconic. Bogart and Bacall fell in love during filming and married in 1945.

Reception

The film was a commercial success and launched Bacall as a star, establishing the Bogart–Bacall pairing continued in The Big Sleep and Key Largo. See also Howard Hawks.
