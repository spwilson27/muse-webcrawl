Anser (genus)

Anser is a genus of geese in the waterfowl family Anatidae. It includes the "grey geese" and the white-fronted geese of the Northern Hemisphere. The genus name is the Latin word for "goose".

Species

Species in the genus include the greylag goose (Anser anser), the ancestor of most domestic geese; the pink-footed goose; the greater and lesser white-fronted geese; the taiga and tundra bean geese; the swan goose; and the bar-headed goose, famed for migrating over the Himalayas.

Distribution and behaviour

Anser geese breed in northern Eurasia and North America and migrate south to winter in temperate regions. They graze on grasses and agricultural crops and typically form lifelong pair bonds, migrating in family groups.
