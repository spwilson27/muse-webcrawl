The North Dakota State Bison football program represents North Dakota State University in college football at the NCAA Division I Football Championship Subdivision (FCS) level and competes in the Missouri Valley Football Conference. The team plays its home games at the Fargodome in Fargo, North Dakota.

History

The program was founded in 1894. It won five national championships in NCAA Division II (1965, 1968, 1969 poll titles and playoff titles in 1983, 1985, 1986, 1988, 1990 — eight titles counting polls across eras) before reclassifying to Division I FCS in 2004.

FCS dynasty

Under head coach Craig Bohl (2003–2013) and his successor Chris Klieman (2014–2018), the Bison won an unprecedented nine FCS national championships in eleven seasons (2011, 2012, 2013, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2021), dominating the subdivision with a power running game and stout defense. Matt Entz succeeded Klieman in 2019 and won the 2019 and 2021 titles; Tim Polasek became head coach in 2024. Notable alumni include NFL quarterbacks Carson Wentz and Trey Lance.

The Bison are also known for upset wins over FBS opponents, including Minnesota, Kansas State, and Iowa, and for hosting ESPN's College GameDay in downtown Fargo.
