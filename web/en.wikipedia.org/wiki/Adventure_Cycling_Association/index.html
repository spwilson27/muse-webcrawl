Adventure Cycling Association

Image placeholder: touring cyclists on an Adventure Cycling route

The Adventure Cycling Association is a non-profit membership organisation based in Missoula, Montana, United States, dedicated to bicycle travel. It is the largest cycling membership organisation in North America, and it develops, maps and promotes long-distance bicycle touring routes across the United States and Canada.

Founded: 1973 (as Bikecentennial)
Headquarters: Missoula, Montana
Type: Non-profit organisation

History

The organisation was founded as Bikecentennial to organise a mass bicycle ride across the United States for the 1976 US Bicentennial. In the summer of 1976 more than 4,000 cyclists rode all or part of the new TransAmerica Trail, the first cross-country bicycle route in the country. The organisation was later renamed the Adventure Cycling Association.

Routes and maps

Adventure Cycling's route network exceeds 50,000 miles (80,000 km), including the TransAmerica Trail, the Great Divide Mountain Bike Route along the Continental Divide, the Southern Tier, the Northern Tier, and routes in the Great Lakes and along the Atlantic and Pacific coasts. Its waterproof printed maps and digital route data note services, campsites, elevation profiles and bike-shop locations for touring cyclists. The association also works with the American Association of State Highway and Transportation Officials to designate the US Bicycle Route System.

Programmes

The association publishes Adventure Cyclist magazine, runs guided tours and educational courses, and advocates for cycling-friendly infrastructure and Amtrak bicycle access.
