Admiral Sir Henry Harwood Harwood, KCB, OBE (19 January 1888 – 9 June 1950) was a Royal Navy officer who won fame for defeating the German pocket battleship Admiral Graf Spee at the Battle of the River Plate in December 1939.

River Plate

As Commodore commanding the South America Division, Harwood anticipated that the raider Graf Spee would head for the shipping lanes off the River Plate estuary. On 13 December 1939 his cruisers HMS Exeter, HMS Ajax and HMNZS Achilles engaged the German ship, damaging her sufficiently that she took refuge in Montevideo and was scuttled by her captain days later. Harwood was promoted to rear admiral and knighted for the victory.

Later career

Harwood served in the Mediterranean and succeeded Sir Andrew Cunningham as Commander-in-Chief, Mediterranean Fleet, in 1942. Ill health forced his retirement in 1945.
