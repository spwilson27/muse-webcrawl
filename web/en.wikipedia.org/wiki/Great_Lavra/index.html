Great Lavra

The Monastery of Great Lavra (Greek: Μονὴ Μεγίστης Λαύρας) is the oldest and largest monastery on Mount Athos in Greece, founded in 963 AD by Saint Athanasius the Athonite with support from Emperor Nikephoros II Phokas. It ranks first in the hierarchy of the twenty Athonite monasteries.

Founded963 AD

History

Great Lavra introduced cenobitic (communal) monasticism to Athos. Its katholikon contains frescoes by Theophanes the Cretan (1535). Its library holds thousands of manuscripts, imperial chrysobulls, and the monastery treasury preserves Byzantine icons, vestments and relics.

Today

The monastery remains an active community within the autonomous monastic state of Mount Athos, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Its dependencies include sketes and the island hermitages of the Athonite desert.
