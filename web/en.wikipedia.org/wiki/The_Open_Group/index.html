The Open Group

Type: Industry consortium
Founded: 1996
Headquarters: Reading, Berkshire, United Kingdom

The Open Group is a global consortium of technology organizations and enterprises that develops vendor-neutral technology standards and certifications. It was formed in 1996 through the merger of the Open Software Foundation (OSF) and X/Open Company, inheriting stewardship of the Single UNIX Specification and the UNIX trademark.

History

The Open Group emerged from the consolidation of two earlier standards bodies. X/Open, founded in 1984, had published portability guides for Unix systems, while the Open Software Foundation, founded in 1988, developed technologies such as Motif and the Distributed Computing Environment. Their merger in 1996 created a single organization for open systems standards.

Standards and frameworks

The consortium is known for the TOGAF (The Open Group Architecture Framework) standard for enterprise architecture, the ArchiMate modeling language, the Open Agile Architecture standard, and the FACE (Future Airborne Capability Environment) consortium. It also maintains the Single UNIX Specification, against which operating systems may be certified as UNIX products.

Certification

The Open Group runs professional certification programs including TOGAF certification, Open CA (Certified Architect), IT4IT, and product certifications for UNIX conformance.
