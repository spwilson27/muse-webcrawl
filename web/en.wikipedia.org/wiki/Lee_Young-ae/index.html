Lee Young-ae (Korean: 이영애; born 31 January 1971) is a South Korean actress. She is best known internationally for starring in the historical television drama Dae Jang Geum (2003), known in English as Jewel in the Palace, and for her role as the vengeful single mother Lee Geum-ja in Park Chan-wook's film Lady Vengeance (2005).

Career

Lee debuted as a model in 1991 and began acting in television dramas in 1993. She rose to prominence in South Korea through a series of popular dramas and films, including Joint Security Area (2000), one of the highest-grossing Korean films of its time.

Her portrayal of Seo Jang-geum, a royal cook and physician in Joseon-era Korea, in the MBC drama Dae Jang Geum (2003–2004) made her one of the leading figures of the Korean Wave (Hallyu). The drama achieved huge ratings across Asia and the Middle East, and Lee received the Daesang (Grand Prize) at the MBC Drama Awards. In 2005 she starred in Lady Vengeance, the final film of Park Chan-wook's Vengeance Trilogy, winning Best Actress at the Blue Dragon Film Awards.

Later work and public life

After marrying in 2009 and giving birth to twins, Lee took a long break from acting before returning in the 2017 drama Saimdang, Memoir of Colors, in which she played the Joseon artist Shin Saimdang. She later appeared in the film Bring Me Home (2019) and the television series Inspector Koo (2021). Lee is also known for philanthropic work and has served as a goodwill ambassador, and she holds a doctorate-level academic background in theatre and film studies.
