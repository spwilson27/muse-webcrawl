The Baylor Bears are the intercollegiate athletic teams representing Baylor University in Waco, Texas. Baylor competes in NCAA Division I as a member of the Big 12 Conference, of which it is a founding and the only private-school member. The women's teams were long known as the Lady Bears, a name phased out in 2021–22 so that all teams are simply the Bears.

History

Baylor fielded its first football team in 1899 and was a charter member of the Southwest Conference in 1915 before helping found the Big 12 in 1996. The school's colours are Baylor green and gold, and two live North American black bears live in a habitat on campus as mascots.

Basketball

Baylor basketball is the programme's flagship. The women's team under Kim Mulkey won NCAA championships in 2005, 2012 and 2019, completing an undefeated 40–0 season in 2012 behind Brittney Griner. The men's team won the 2021 NCAA championship under Scott Drew, defeating Gonzaga in the final — a remarkable culmination of a rebuild that began in 2003 after a major scandal. In 2010 Robert Griffin III won football's Heisman Trophy, Baylor's first.

Other sports and facilities

Baylor has won NCAA team titles in men's tennis (2004) and women's equestrian and acrobatics and tumbling. Football has been played at McLane Stadium, on the banks of the Brazos River, since 2014. Baylor's chief rivals include TCU, in the series known as "The Revivalry", and Texas A&M, formerly the "Battle of the Brazos".
