At bat
SportBaseball / Softball
AbbreviationAB

In baseball, an at bat (AB) is a batter's turn batting against a pitcher. An at bat is counted for statistical purposes whenever a batter reaches first base safely on a hit or an error, is called out, or reaches on a fielder's choice. Plate appearances that end in a base on balls, hit by pitch, sacrifice bunt or sacrifice fly, or catcher's interference are not counted as at bats.

Uses

At bats are the denominator in the calculation of batting average and, together with other events, feature in slugging percentage. The phrase "at bat" is also used colloquially for any plate appearance, though the official scoring rules distinguish the two.

Records

Pete Rose holds the Major League Baseball record for career at bats with 14,053.
