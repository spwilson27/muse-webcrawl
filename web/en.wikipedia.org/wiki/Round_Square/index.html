Round Square

Round Square is an international network of schools, founded in 1966, that share an educational approach based on the philosophy of Kurt Hahn, the German educator who founded Salem, Gordonstoun and Outward Bound. The network takes its name from the round square building at Gordonstoun in Scotland, where the first conference was held.

Philosophy

Round Square schools commit to character education organised around six "IDEALS": Internationalism, Democracy, Environmentalism, Adventure, Leadership and Service. Students are encouraged to take part in exchanges, service projects, conferences and expeditions. The King Constantine Medal recognises exceptional service by members of the community.

Membership

The network comprises around 250 schools in some 50 countries on six continents, including Gordonstoun, Salem, Athenian School, and schools across Asia, Africa, the Americas and Australasia. King Constantine II of Greece was its long-serving patron and chair until his death in 2023.
