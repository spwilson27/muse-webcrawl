Thomas Adès CBE (born 1 March 1971) is a British composer, pianist, and conductor. He came to prominence while still in his twenties with the chamber opera Powder Her Face (1995) and the orchestral work Asyla (1997), and is regarded as one of the leading composers of his generation.

Works

His operas include The Tempest (2004), after Shakespeare, premiered at the Royal Opera House, and The Exterminating Angel (2016), after Buñuel's film, premiered at the Salzburg Festival and staged at the Metropolitan Opera. Major orchestral works include the violin concerto Concentric Paths (2005), Tevot (2007), Polaris (2010), and a piano concerto written for Kirill Gerstein (2018). The ballet Dante, choreographed by Wayne McGregor, was premiered in 2021.

Career

Adès studied at King's College, Cambridge, and was Britten Professor of Composition at the Royal Academy of Music. He has served as artistic director of the Aldeburgh Festival and has appeared as pianist and conductor with leading orchestras worldwide. He was appointed CBE in 2018.
