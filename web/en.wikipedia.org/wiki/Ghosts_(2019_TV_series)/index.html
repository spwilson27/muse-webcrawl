Ghosts is a British sitcom first broadcast on BBC One in April 2019. Created by and starring the ensemble behind Horrible Histories — Mathew Baynton, Simon Farnaby, Martha Howe-Douglas, Jim Howick, Laurence Rickard and Ben Willbond — it follows Alison and Mike Cooper, a young couple who inherit a crumbling country mansion, Button House, and discover it is haunted by a squabbling group of ghosts from across the centuries who died on the property.

Premise

After a fall leaves Alison able to see and hear the dead, she must mediate between her husband and the house's permanent residents: a caveman, a Tudor nobleman, a Stuart poet, a Georgian lady, a Regency scoutmaster, a Victorian lady, an Edwardian captain, a 1980s politician and a 1990s victim of a party accident, among others. The series ran for five series and several Christmas specials, ending in 2023 to critical acclaim.

Adaptations

A successful American adaptation, Ghosts, premiered on CBS in 2021, with further international versions following.
