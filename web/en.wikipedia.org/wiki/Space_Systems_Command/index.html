Space Systems Command

Space Systems Command
Founded: 2021 (as SSC)
Country: United States
Branch: United States Space Force
Headquarters: Los Angeles Air Force Base, California
Role: Space systems development and acquisition

Space Systems Command (SSC) is a United States Space Force field command headquartered at Los Angeles Air Force Base in El Segundo, California. It is responsible for developing, acquiring, equipping, fielding, and sustaining lethal and resilient space capabilities in support of the joint warfighter.

History

Space Systems Command traces its lineage to the Space and Missile Systems Center (SMC), which for decades served as the Air Force's center for military space acquisition. On 13 August 2021, SMC was redesignated as Space Systems Command, becoming one of three field commands of the newly established United States Space Force, alongside Space Operations Command and Space Training and Readiness Command.

Organization

SSC oversees a broad portfolio of space programs, including military satellite communications, missile warning and tracking, space domain awareness, positioning, navigation and timing (the GPS constellation), launch acquisition, and space-based environmental monitoring. It manages tens of billions of dollars in annual acquisition and sustainment activity across a workforce of military personnel, civilians, and contractors.

Mission

The command's mission centers on delivering capability to the warfighter at speed, fostering innovation with commercial industry, and ensuring the resilience of U.S. space systems against growing threats in orbit.
