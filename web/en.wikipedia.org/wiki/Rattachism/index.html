Rattachism


Rattachism advocated the reunion of Wallonia with France.

Rattachism (French: Rattachisme) is a political ideology within the Walloon Movement of Belgium advocating the "reattachment" (rattachement) of the French-speaking region of Wallonia — and sometimes Brussels and the six Flemish municipalities with language facilities around Brussels — to the French Republic, rather than the creation of an independent Walloon state.

Background

The idea traces back to the annexation of the Austrian Netherlands by revolutionary France in 1795 and to French-speaking Belgian sentiment during and after the Belgian Revolution of 1830, when some notables favoured union with France. Rattachism re-emerged in the 20th century as part of the broader debate over Belgium's federal future.

Modern movement

The main rattachist party is the Rassemblement Wallonie France (RWF), founded in 1999. Opinion polls have periodically shown a minority of Walloons — rising in times of Belgian political crisis — favouring union with France if Belgium were to split. Rattachists argue that Wallonia shares language, culture, and economic ties with France and would integrate into its system of regions and departments.
