Mi Teleférico ("My Cable Car") is an aerial cable car urban transit system serving the Bolivian cities of La Paz and El Alto. Opened in 2014, it is the largest urban cable car network in the world, and was built to relieve severe traffic congestion on the steep roads linking El Alto, perched on the Altiplano at about 4,150 metres, with La Paz in the canyon below.

Network

The first phase comprised three lines — Red, Yellow and Green — opened during 2014. A second phase added further lines (Blue, Orange, White, Sky Blue, Purple, Brown, Gold and Silver), bringing the network to ten lines and more than 30 kilometres, with over 1,300 gondola cabins. The system was constructed by the Austrian company Doppelmayr. Lines are identified by colour in Spanish and connect with the cities' bus systems.

Impact

Mi Teleférico carries tens of thousands of passengers daily and cut the El Alto–La Paz commute dramatically. It has been widely cited as a model for cable-propelled transit in mountainous and congested cities, and has also become a tourist attraction for its panoramic views of La Paz and the Cordillera Real.
