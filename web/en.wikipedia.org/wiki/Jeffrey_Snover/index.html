Jeffrey Snover

Jeffrey Snover is an American software engineer known as the inventor of Windows PowerShell, Microsoft's task automation and configuration management framework. He spent much of his career as a Microsoft Technical Fellow and served as chief architect for Windows Server and Azure infrastructure.

PowerShell

Snover authored the influential "Monad Manifesto" in 2002, setting out a vision for an object-based command-line shell for Windows. The project shipped as PowerShell in 2006 and later became open source and cross-platform as PowerShell Core.

Career

Before Microsoft, Snover worked at Apollo Computer, DEC, and NetView, contributing to systems management technologies. He is known for advocating automation and for popularizing the phrase "to ship is to choose." After leaving Microsoft, he joined Google.
