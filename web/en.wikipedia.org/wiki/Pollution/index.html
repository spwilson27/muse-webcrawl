Pollution

Pollution
DefinitionIntroduction of harmful materials or energy into the environment
FormsAir, water, soil, plastic, noise, light
[Image: Smokestacks and a haze over an industrial city at dusk]

Pollution is the introduction of substances or energy (heat, noise, light) into the environment that cause adverse change. Air pollution from coal, oil and vehicles kills millions yearly; plastic pollution reaches the deepest oceans and the blood of animals; nutrient runoff creates dead zones.

Control

Regulation (clean-air acts, treaties on ozone-destroying chemicals and persistent pollutants), technology (filters, catalytic converters, wastewater treatment) and recycling have cut many pollutants in richer countries, while global problems — CO2, plastics — need the collective action described in Sustainability.
