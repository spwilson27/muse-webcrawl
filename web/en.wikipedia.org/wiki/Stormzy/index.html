Stormzy

Origin: Croydon, London, England
Genres: Grime, British hip hop, gospel
Occupation: Rapper, singer, songwriter

Michael Ebenezer Kwadjo Omari Owuo Jr. (born 26 July 1993), known professionally as Stormzy, is a British rapper, singer, and songwriter. He is credited with popularising grime music to a mainstream audience and is considered one of the most influential British artists of his generation.

Career

Born in Croydon, London, to a Ghanaian mother, Stormzy gained attention through his Wicked Skengman freestyle series and the 2014 EP Dreamers Disease. His freestyle "Shut Up" went viral in 2015 and reached the UK top ten after a campaign for the Christmas number one. His debut album, Gang Signs & Prayer (2017), was the first grime album to reach number one on the UK Albums Chart and won British Album of the Year at the 2018 Brit Awards, where he also won British Male Solo Artist.

His second album, Heavy Is the Head (2019), also topped the chart and produced his first UK number-one singles, "Vossi Bop" and "Own It" (with Ed Sheeran and Burna Boy). In June 2019 he became the first Black British solo artist to headline the Glastonbury Festival. His third album, This Is What I Mean (2022), debuted at number one as well and took a more soul- and gospel-inflected direction.

Activism and other work

Stormzy founded the #Merky label and imprint, launched the Stormzy Scholarship for Black students at the University of Cambridge, and funded the Merky Foundation charity. He has been outspoken on racism and inequality in Britain, notably criticising the government response to the Grenfell Tower fire.
