Hay rake

A hay rake is an agricultural implement used to collect cut hay or straw into windrows ready for baling or loading, and to turn the crop so it dries evenly. Raking is an intermediate step in haymaking between mowing (or tedding) and baling.

Types

Early rakes were hand tools with wooden teeth, followed by horse-drawn dump rakes and side-delivery rakes. Modern tractor-drawn or mounted designs include wheel rakes (star-wheel rakes), rotary rakes with rotating tine arms, and belt rakes. Rotary rakes produce fluffy, well-aerated windrows and are gentler on legume leaves than wheel rakes.

Operation

Rakes are typically used when the crop has partly dried in the swath. By gathering forage into windrows, the rake allows a baler or forage harvester to pick it up efficiently, and turning the swath exposes damp material to sun and air, reducing mould risk.
