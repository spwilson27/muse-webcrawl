Telling Lies

Telling Lies is a 2019 interactive film video game developed by Sam Barlow and Half Mermaid, published by Annapurna Interactive. The player searches a database of stolen video clips—video calls recorded from a laptop—to piece together a story involving an undercover FBI agent and the people around him. It is a spiritual successor to Barlow's Her Story (2015).

DeveloperHalf Mermaid
DesignerSam Barlow
PublisherAnnapurna Interactive
Released23 August 2019

Gameplay

Players type search terms to retrieve clips of up to five minutes, watching one side of video conversations and inferring the other. The narrative, spanning about two years, follows undercover agent David Smith as he infiltrates an eco-activist group, and the lives of three women connected to him. The cast includes Logan Marshall-Green, Alexandra Shipp, Kerry Bishé and Angela Sarafyan.

Reception

The game was praised for its acting and non-linear storytelling, and won several awards for its narrative and performances.
