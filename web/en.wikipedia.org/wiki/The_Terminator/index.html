The Terminator is a 1984 American science fiction action film directed by James Cameron. It stars Arnold Schwarzenegger as the Terminator, a cybernetic assassin sent back in time from 2029 to 1984 to kill Sarah Connor (Linda Hamilton), whose unborn son will one day lead humanity in a war against the machines of Skynet. Michael Biehn plays Kyle Reese, the soldier sent back to protect her.

Reception and legacy

Made on a modest budget, the film was a surprise critical and commercial success, grossing $78 million worldwide and launching Cameron's career and a major media franchise. Schwarzenegger's line "I'll be back" became iconic. The film was selected for preservation in the United States National Film Registry in 2008. Its sequel, Terminator 2: Judgment Day (1991), was an even greater success.
