United States Medical Licensing Examination

USMLE
Sponsors: FSMB and NBME
Steps: Step 1, Step 2 CK, Step 3

The United States Medical Licensing Examination (USMLE) is a three-step examination for medical licensure in the United States. It is sponsored by the Federation of State Medical Boards (FSMB) and the National Board of Medical Examiners (NBME). Physicians must pass the examination sequence to obtain a license to practice medicine in the United States.

Steps

Step 1 assesses foundational science concepts, focusing on the biomedical sciences; since January 2022 it has been reported as pass/fail. Step 2 Clinical Knowledge (CK) assesses clinical sciences and patient care under supervision. Step 3, taken during residency, assesses the application of medical knowledge in the unsupervised practice of medicine, emphasizing patient management in ambulatory settings.

History

The USMLE replaced the separate NBME Part examinations and the FSMB's FLEX examination in the 1990s, creating a single uniform examination pathway. The former Step 2 Clinical Skills examination, which used standardized patients, was discontinued in 2021.

International graduates

Graduates of medical schools outside the United States and Canada must be certified by the Educational Commission for Foreign Medical Graduates (ECFMG), which includes passing USMLE steps, in order to enter accredited residency programs.
