Trial of George Zimmerman

State of Florida v. George Zimmerman was a criminal trial held in Seminole County, Florida, in 2013. George Zimmerman, a neighbourhood watch volunteer, was charged with second-degree murder in the fatal shooting of 17-year-old Trayvon Martin on 26 February 2012 in Sanford, Florida.

Background

Zimmerman maintained he shot Martin in self-defence during a physical altercation. The initial decision not to charge him sparked nationwide protests and debate about self-defence laws, racial profiling, and Florida's "stand your ground" law; Zimmerman waived a stand-your-ground hearing and relied on ordinary self-defence at trial.

Trial and verdict

The trial began on 10 June 2013 before Judge Debra Nelson, with an all-female six-person jury. On 13 July 2013 the jury acquitted Zimmerman of both second-degree murder and the lesser charge of manslaughter. The verdict prompted further protests and discussion across the United States.
