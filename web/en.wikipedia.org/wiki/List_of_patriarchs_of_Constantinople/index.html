List of patriarchs of Constantinople

The Ecumenical Patriarch of Constantinople is the spiritual leader of the Eastern Orthodox Church, recognised as primus inter pares (first among equals) among Orthodox bishops. Tradition traces the see to St Andrew the Apostle; its bishops rose in importance after Constantine made the city the imperial capital in 330, and the title of patriarch was formally recognised at the Council of Chalcedon (451). This list summarises the early bishops and a selection of notable patriarchs; the full succession runs to more than 270 holders.


The Patriarchal Cathedral of St George, Istanbul, seat of the patriarchate since 1601.
