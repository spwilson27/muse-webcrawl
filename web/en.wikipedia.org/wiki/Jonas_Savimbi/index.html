Jonas Savimbi

Jonas Malheiro Savimbi (3 August 1934 – 22 February 2002) was an Angolan revolutionary and politician who founded and led UNITA (National Union for the Total Independence of Angola). After fighting Portuguese colonial rule, UNITA fought the governing MPLA in the long Angolan Civil War (1975–2002).

Backed at various times by the United States, South Africa and China, Savimbi rejected the results of the 1992 elections and returned to war. He was killed in combat with government troops in Moxico Province in February 2002; his death led within weeks to a ceasefire ending the 27-year civil war.
