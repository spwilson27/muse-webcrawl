Monica Anna Maria Bellucci (born 30 September 1964) is an Italian actress and model. She began her career as a fashion model, modelling for Dolce & Gabbana, Dior and Elite Model Management, before transitioning to Italian films in the early 1990s and then to French and Hollywood productions. She is widely regarded as one of the most prominent Italian actresses of her generation and a sex symbol of international cinema.

Career

Born in Città di Castello, Umbria, Bellucci began modelling at 13 and, while studying law at the University of Perugia, modelled to pay her tuition before abandoning her studies for a full-time career in Milan, Paris and New York. Her early film roles included Bram Stoker's Dracula (1992). She gained critical acclaim in France for The Apartment (1996), which earned her a César Award nomination, and married her co-star Vincent Cassel.

Her title role in Giuseppe Tornatore's Malèna (2000) brought her international attention. She subsequently starred in Brotherhood of the Wolf (2001), the controversial Irréversible (2002), the Matrix sequels as Persephone (2003), Mel Gibson's The Passion of the Christ (2004) as Mary Magdalene, The Brothers Grimm (2005), and Shoot 'Em Up (2007). In 2015 she played Lucia Sciarra in the James Bond film Spectre, becoming, at 50, the oldest actress to play a leading Bond role. Her later work includes On the Milky Way (2016), the third season of Twin Peaks (2017), and stage work in the one-woman show Maria Callas: Letters and Memoirs.

Recognition

Bellucci has received numerous honours, including the Globo d'oro and Nastro d'Argento awards in Italy, and was appointed a Knight of the French Legion of Honour in 2016. She speaks Italian, French, English and some Spanish, and has performed roles in several languages, including Aramaic in The Passion of the Christ.
