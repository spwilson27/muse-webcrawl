The Bōsō Peninsula is a peninsula in Chiba Prefecture on Honshu, Japan. It separates Tokyo Bay to the west from the Pacific Ocean to the east and forms the eastern edge of the Tokyo metropolitan area's bay coastline.

Geography

Much of the peninsula is occupied by the hilly Bōsō Hill Range, with Mount Atago — also the site of a Japan Air Self-Defense Force radar station — as its highest point. The southern part lies within Minami Bōsō Quasi-National Park, and the mild climate supports flower farming and tourism.

Transport

The Tokyo Bay Aqua-Line bridge-tunnel crosses the bay from Kawasaki to Kisarazu on the peninsula's western shore, greatly shortening road travel to the Tokyo area.
