Ian Smith

Ian Douglas Smith (8 April 1919 – 20 November 2007) was a Rhodesian politician, farmer and fighter pilot who served as Prime Minister of Rhodesia (later Zimbabwe) from 1964 to 1979. He led the white-minority government that unilaterally declared independence from Britain in 1965.

Early life and war service

Born to British immigrants, Smith studied at Rhodes University and served in the Royal Air Force in the Second World War, surviving a crash in Egypt that left lasting facial injuries, and later being shot down over Italy and fighting with partisans.

Premiership and UDI

Smith became prime minister in 1964 demanding independence without a commitment to majority rule. On 11 November 1965 his government issued the Unilateral Declaration of Independence (UDI). Britain and the UN imposed sanctions; no country recognised Rhodesia.

Bush War and settlement

The Rhodesian Bush War against ZANU and ZAPU guerrillas intensified in the 1970s. Smith conceded majority rule in principle, signed the Internal Settlement in 1978, and after the Lancaster House Agreement elections in 1980 brought Robert Mugabe to power in independent Zimbabwe. Smith remained an opposition MP until 1987.
