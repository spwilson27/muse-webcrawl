Kenilworth Road

The ground from the Kenilworth Stand

Kenilworth Road is a football stadium in Luton, Bedfordshire, England, and the home ground of Luton Town Football Club since 1905. Known for its compact, old-fashioned character — including an away entrance passing through a terrace of houses on Oak Road — it has a capacity of about 12,000 following improvements made for the club's promotion to the Premier League in 2023.

History

Luton Town moved to Kenilworth Road in 1905 after leaving Dunstable Road. The ground developed piecemeal over the 20th century: the Main Stand dates from the 1920s, floodlights were installed in 1953, and the stadium became all-seater in 1991 during the club's spell in the First Division. Between 1986 and 1991 the ground had an artificial pitch, and Luton controversially banned away supporters following crowd trouble in 1985.

The record attendance is 30,069, for an FA Cup sixth-round replay against Blackpool in 1959.

Stands

The ground's stands are the Main Stand, the Kenilworth Stand, the David Preece Stand, the Oak Road End (for away supporters, entered beneath a row of houses) and the Bobbers Stand, whose executive boxes were converted back to seating in 2023 to increase capacity for Premier League football.

Future

Luton Town have long planned to leave Kenilworth Road for a new stadium at Power Court in the town centre; the club obtained planning permission for the development, and the ground's days as a league venue are numbered despite its renewed top-flight use.
