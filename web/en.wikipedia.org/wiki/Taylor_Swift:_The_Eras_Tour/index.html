Taylor Swift: The Eras Tour is a 2023 American concert film produced by singer-songwriter Taylor Swift and directed by Sam Wrench. It documents the Los Angeles shows of the Eras Tour, Swift's sixth concert tour, recorded over three performances at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California, in August 2023.

Production and release

Swift bypassed the major studios and negotiated distribution directly with AMC Theatres, an unprecedented arrangement in which she and her family financed the production — reportedly for between $10 and $20 million — and retained a majority share of box-office receipts. The film premiered on 11 October 2023 at The Grove in Los Angeles and opened worldwide on 13 October, with theatres encouraging audience singing, dancing and friendship-bracelet trading in the auditorium.

Reception

The film grossed more than $260 million worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing concert film of all time. It was praised for capturing the scale of the production and Swift's stamina across a set of more than three hours. An extended version, The Eras Tour (Taylor's Version), adding songs including "Cardigan" and additional acoustic performances, premiered on Disney+ in March 2024.

Impact

Alongside the tour itself, the film was credited with measurable effects on local economies and cinema attendance during a period disrupted by Hollywood strikes, and its direct-distribution model was widely discussed as a template for future event releases.
