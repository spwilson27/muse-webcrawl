Veracruz

Veracruz, formally Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave, is one of the 31 states of Mexico, located along the Gulf of Mexico. Its capital is Xalapa and its largest city is the port of Veracruz.

Geography

The state stretches along the Gulf coast, with coastal plains rising to the Sierra Madre Oriental and Pico de Orizaba, the highest peak in Mexico.

History

Hernán Cortés founded the port city in 1519, the first Spanish settlement in mainland Mexico. Veracruz was a key port for trade and invasions, including US occupations in 1847 and 1914.

Economy and culture

Petroleum, agriculture, and port trade dominate. Veracruz is known for jarocho music, son jarocho, and its carnival.
