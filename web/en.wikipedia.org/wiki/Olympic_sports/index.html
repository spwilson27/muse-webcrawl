Olympic sports

Olympic pictograms representing the programme of sports.

Olympic sports are sports contested in the Summer and Winter Olympic Games. The Olympic programme consists of core sports governed by international federations recognized by the International Olympic Committee (IOC), together with additional sports proposed by host organizing committees under the Olympic Agenda 2020 reforms.

Summer programme

The Summer Games programme has grown from 9 sports in Athens 1896 to 32 sports at Paris 2024, including athletics, aquatics (swimming, diving, water polo, artistic swimming, marathon swimming), cycling, gymnastics, rowing, canoeing, judo, wrestling, boxing, fencing, shooting, archery, football, basketball, volleyball, handball, hockey, tennis, table tennis, badminton, golf, rugby sevens, triathlon, taekwondo, weightlifting, equestrianism, modern pentathlon, sailing, skateboarding, sport climbing, surfing, and breaking (the last making its debut in Paris). Host-added sports for Los Angeles 2028 include baseball/softball, cricket, flag football, lacrosse, and squash.

Winter programme

The Winter Games, first held at Chamonix in 1924, comprise 15 disciplines in 7–8 sports: ice hockey, figure skating, speed skating, short-track speed skating, curling, alpine skiing, cross-country skiing, ski jumping, Nordic combined, freestyle skiing, snowboarding, biathlon, bobsleigh, skeleton, and luge. Ski mountaineering joins the programme at Milano Cortina 2026.

Recognition and changes

For a sport to be added it must be widely practised and its federation must adopt the World Anti-Doping Code and the Olympic Charter; since Agenda 2020, the IOC caps athlete numbers (about 10,500 for the Summer Games) and lets hosts propose new sports for their edition only. Sports have also been removed — baseball and softball were dropped after 2008 before returning at Tokyo 2020 — and demonstration sports, once a regular feature, were discontinued after 1992. Esports has been formally embraced with the creation of the Olympic Esports Games, announced for 2027 in Saudi Arabia.
