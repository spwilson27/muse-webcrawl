Kim Philby

Harold Adrian Russell "Kim" Philby (1 January 1912 – 11 May 1988) was a British intelligence officer and a double agent for the Soviet Union. A member of the Cambridge Five spy ring, he rose to a senior position in MI6 while secretly passing information to the KGB.

Career

Recruited by Soviet intelligence while at Cambridge in the 1930s, Philby joined the Secret Intelligence Service during the Second World War and later headed its anti-Soviet section — a post from which he betrayed numerous Western operations and agents. Suspicion fell on him after the 1951 defection of fellow spies Guy Burgess and Donald Maclean, but he was publicly cleared in 1955. In 1963, confronted in Beirut, he defected to Moscow, where he lived until his death.
