Jacques Bermon Webster II (born April 30, 1991), known professionally as Travis Scott (stylised as Travis $cott), is an American rapper, singer, songwriter, and record producer. His musical style blends hip hop with lo-fi and ambient influences, heavy with Auto-Tuned vocal processing and psychedelic production. Throughout his career Scott has achieved five number-one hits on the Billboard Hot 100 and has had four number-one albums on the Billboard 200.

Career

Born and raised in Houston, Scott formed a duo called the Graduates as a teenager before signing with Epic Records and T.I.'s Grand Hustle imprint in 2012, and with Kanye West's GOOD Music as a producer. His mixtapes Owl Pharaoh (2013) and Days Before Rodeo (2014) built his reputation. His debut studio album Rodeo (2015) was led by the single "Antidote"; Birds in the Trap Sing McKnight (2016) became his first number-one album.

His third album, Astroworld (2018) — named after Houston's defunct Six Flags AstroWorld theme park — was a critical and commercial triumph, containing the number-one single "Sicko Mode" featuring Drake. In 2019 his compilation JackBoys topped the charts, and his 2020 single "The Scotts" (with Kid Cudi) and "Franchise" also reached number one. His fourth album, Utopia (2023), debuted at number one in several countries. Scott founded the label Cactus Jack Records in 2017 and became known for lucrative brand collaborations, massive festival-scale tours, and a virtual concert in the game Fortnite in 2020 watched by millions.

Astroworld Festival crowd crush

On November 5, 2021, a crowd crush during Scott's headlining set at his Astroworld Festival in Houston killed ten people and injured hundreds. The event led to criminal investigations (a grand jury declined to indict in 2023), extensive civil litigation — largely settled — investigations into festival safety, and lasting controversy over Scott's history of encouraging rowdy crowd behaviour, for which he had previously faced charges.

Personal life

Scott has two children with media personality Kylie Jenner, with whom he was in a widely publicised relationship. He is known for his Houston roots and advocacy for the city, including the Cactus Jack Foundation's community projects.
