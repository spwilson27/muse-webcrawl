DESTINY+

DESTINY+
Operator: JAXA
Mission type: Asteroid flyby / dust science
Target: 3200 Phaethon (and 2005 UD)
Launch vehicle: Epsilon S (planned)

DESTINY+ (Demonstration and Experiment of Space Technology for INterplanetary voYage with Phaethon fLyby and dUst Science) is a Japanese space mission led by JAXA to fly by the active asteroid 3200 Phaethon, the source of the Geminid meteor shower.

Objectives

The spacecraft will image Phaethon's surface and analyse interplanetary and interstellar dust with an onboard dust analyser, investigating how dust — a potential carrier of organic material — is supplied to Earth. A flyby of asteroid 2005 UD is also planned on an extended trajectory.

Spacecraft

DESTINY+ is a small, low-cost technology demonstrator using solar electric propulsion (ion engines) and lightweight solar array panels, launched from Uchinoura. Repeated delays to its launch vehicle have pushed the launch toward the late 2020s.
