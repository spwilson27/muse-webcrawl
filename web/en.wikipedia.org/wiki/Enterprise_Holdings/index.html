Enterprise Holdings, Inc.
Founded: 1957 (as Executive Leasing Company)
Founder: Jack C. Taylor
Headquarters: Clayton, Missouri, US
Industry: Car rental
Brands: Enterprise Rent-A-Car, National Car Rental, Alamo Rent a Car

Enterprise Holdings, Inc. is an American car rental holding company headquartered in Clayton, Missouri. It operates the Enterprise Rent-A-Car, National Car Rental and Alamo Rent a Car brands and is the largest car rental company in North America by fleet size, locations and revenue. The company remains privately owned by the Taylor family.

History

Jack C. Taylor founded the business in St. Louis in 1957 as Executive Leasing Company, leasing cars with a small fleet; he named the company after the aircraft carrier USS Enterprise, on which he had served as a naval aviator in the Second World War. Renamed Enterprise in 1969, it distinguished itself by focusing on the local "home city" market — rentals for people whose own cars were being repaired — rather than airports, and by pioneering the practice of picking customers up, advertised by the slogan "We'll pick you up."

Acquisitions and growth

In 2007 Enterprise acquired Vanguard Automotive Group, owner of the National and Alamo brands, giving it a major airport presence to complement its neighbourhood network. The combined operation runs well over a million vehicles and thousands of locations across North America, Europe and beyond, and has expanded into car sharing (Enterprise CarShare), vanpooling, truck rental and fleet management. Leadership passed from Jack Taylor to his son Andrew C. Taylor and then to the first non-family CEO, Pamela Nicholson, and subsequently Chrissy Taylor.
