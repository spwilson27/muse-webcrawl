Joshua Nkomo

Born19 June 1917, Semokwe, Matabeleland
Died1 July 1999, Harare
PartyZAPU, later ZANU–PF

Joshua Mqabuko Nyongolo Nkomo (1917–1999) was a Zimbabwean revolutionary and politician, founder of the Zimbabwe African People's Union (ZAPU), popularly called "Father Zimbabwe" (Umdala Wethu).

Career

A trade unionist and ANC (Southern Rhodesia) leader, Nkomo founded successive nationalist movements banned by the Rhodesian government. ZAPU's armed wing ZIPRA, Soviet-backed, fought in the Rhodesian Bush War alongside ZANU's ZANLA, with whom relations were often tense.

Post-independence

After Mugabe's 1980 election victory, Nkomo served in cabinet, was dismissed amid arms-cache allegations, and his Matabeleland base suffered the Gukurahundi massacres (1983–87). The 1987 Unity Accord merged ZAPU into ZANU–PF; Nkomo became vice-president until his death.
