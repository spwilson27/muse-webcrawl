FIRST Tech Challenge

OrganiserFIRST
Founded2005 (as FIRST Vex Challenge)
ParticipantsStudents in grades 7–12

The FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) is a robotics competition for students in grades 7–12, run by the non-profit FIRST. Teams of up to 15 students design, build and programme robots from reusable kits to compete in alliances in a floor game that changes each season.

Format

Robots are built on an 18-inch cube chassis using standard motors, servos and structural parts, controlled by Android-based hardware and programmed in Java or block-based languages. Matches combine an autonomous period with a driver-controlled period, and alliance partners are drawn from competing teams.

History

The programme began in 2005 as the FIRST Vex Challenge and was rebranded as the FIRST Tech Challenge in 2008, adopting its own Tetrix and later goBILDA-based kit of parts. It sits between FIRST LEGO League and the FIRST Robotics Competition in the FIRST progression of programmes and reaches tens of thousands of students internationally.
