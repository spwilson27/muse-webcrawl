Gurkha Contingent

Country: Singapore

Parent: Singapore Police Force

Formed: 9 April 1949

Personnel: Recruited from Nepal

The Gurkha Contingent is a line unit of the Singapore Police Force made up of Gurkhas recruited from Nepal. Formed on 9 April 1949, shortly after the British Gurkha units were divided between the British and Indian armies, it was raised to provide a neutral, disciplined force for internal security duties.

Role

The contingent is based at Mount Vernon Camp, where its officers live with their families; recruits serve terms in Singapore before retiring to Nepal. Its duties include guarding key installations and the residences of senior officials, supporting the police during public disorder, and providing security at major events. Its members carry the kukri as part of their identity and drill tradition.
