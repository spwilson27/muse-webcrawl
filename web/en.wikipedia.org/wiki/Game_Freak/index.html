Game Freak

Game Freak Inc. is a Japanese video game developer based in Tokyo, best known as the primary developer of the Pokémon role-playing games published by Nintendo and The Pokémon Company. The company employs around 200 people.

History

Game Freak began in 1982 as a self-published arcade-game fanzine written by Satoshi Tajiri and illustrated by Ken Sugimori. Tajiri incorporated Game Freak as a game studio on 26 April 1989, with early titles including the platformer Mendel Palace (1989) for the Famicom, the puzzler Yoshi (1991) and the platformer Pulseman (1994) for the Mega Drive.

Pokémon

Tajiri's idea of exchanging information — and collecting creatures — over the Game Boy Link Cable became Pocket Monsters Red and Green, released in Japan in 1996 after a six-year development that nearly bankrupted the company. As Pokémon, the series became one of the highest-grossing media franchises in history, and Game Freak has developed every mainline entry, from Red and Green through Pokémon Black and White, X and Y, Sun and Moon, Sword and Shield, and Scarlet and Violet (2022), as well as the open-world spin-off Pokémon Legends: Arceus (2022) and its follow-up Legends: Z-A.

Other work

Game Freak periodically develops non-Pokémon games, including Drill Dozer (2005), Tembo the Badass Elephant (2015) and Little Town Hero (2019), under its "Gear Project" initiative encouraging staff to pitch original ideas. In 2020 the company moved its headquarters to Kanda Square in Chiyoda, Tokyo, in the same building as Nintendo's Tokyo office.
