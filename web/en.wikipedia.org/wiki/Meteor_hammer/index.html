Meteor hammer

The meteor hammer (Chinese: 流星錘; pinyin: liúxīng chuí), often simply called the meteor, is a Chinese weapon consisting of one or two metal weights attached to the ends of a long rope or chain. It belongs to the family of flexible weapons in Chinese martial arts, related to the rope dart.

Description

A single-headed meteor hammer has one weight, traditionally a rounded or melon-shaped metal head, on a rope several metres long, with a small flag or streamer near the head to help the user track its movement. The double-headed version, sometimes called the double meteors, has a weight at each end. The weapon is swung in circular patterns to build momentum, then released to strike, wrap around, or entangle an opponent or their weapon.

Use

Techniques involve swinging the meteor around the body, neck, and limbs, changing its direction suddenly, and shooting the head out at a target while retaining the rope. Because the trajectory of the head can be altered mid-swing, the weapon is difficult to predict, but it demands considerable skill to avoid striking oneself. It is practised today mainly in wushu demonstrations and traditional Shaolin-derived schools.
