Reichskommissariat Ukraine

The Reichskommissariat Ukraine (RKU; Ukrainian: Райхскомісаріат Україна) was the civilian occupation regime of German-occupied Ukraine during the Second World War. It was established by Nazi Germany in September 1941 following the invasion of the Soviet Union in Operation Barbarossa, and encompassed much of the Ukrainian SSR, excluding several districts at its western and eastern fringes.

Administration

The Reichskommissar was Erich Koch, notorious for the brutality of his rule, and the capital was Rivne (Rowno). The RKU was administered as a colonial territory to be exploited for food and raw materials for the German war economy under the Hunger Plan. Its existence coincided with the Holocaust in Ukraine, in which well over a million Ukrainian Jews were murdered, and with mass deportations of Ukrainians as forced labourers (Ostarbeiter) to Germany.

End

The territory was progressively liberated by the Red Army during 1943–1944, and the RKU ceased to function by mid-1944.
