Attention management

Attention management
DomainProductivity, organisational behaviour
Related conceptsAttention economy, time management, information overload
Key thinkersHerbert Simon, Linda Stone, Cal Newport

Attention management refers to the practice of controlling distractions and consciously directing one's focus in order to be as productive and effective as possible. It applies models from neuroscience and cognitive psychology to individual work habits and to the design of work environments and information systems, and it has gained importance with the growth of the attention economy, in which digital platforms compete for users' limited attention.

Background

The economist Herbert Simon observed in 1971 that "a wealth of information creates a poverty of attention," framing attention as a scarce resource. Because human cognitive capacity for sustained focus is limited, and because task-switching and interruptions impose measurable costs on performance, managing what receives attention — rather than merely managing time — has become a focus of productivity literature.

Approaches

Common techniques include batching email and notifications into scheduled windows, single-tasking instead of multitasking, using time-blocking, structuring the physical environment to remove distractions, and deliberate breaks to restore directed-attention capacity ("attention restoration"). Writers such as Cal Newport (Deep Work, 2016) advocate scheduled periods of uninterrupted concentration on cognitively demanding tasks. In organisations, attention management also covers meeting hygiene, notification policies, and the allocation of collective focus to strategic priorities.

Criticism and research

Research supports costs from media multitasking and interruption, though effect sizes and causal claims remain debated; attention is also influenced by sleep, health, and motivation. Critics note that individual attention management cannot fully offset systems designed to capture attention, motivating parallel discussions of regulation and platform design.
