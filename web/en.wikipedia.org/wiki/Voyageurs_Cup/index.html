Voyageurs Cup

The Voyageurs Cup is the domestic trophy for professional soccer in Canada, awarded annually to the winner of the Canadian Championship. From 2002 to 2007 it was awarded by The Voyageurs, Canada's national-team supporters group, to the Canadian team in the USL First Division with the best record in league matches against other Canadian sides.

History

The Voyageurs created the cup in 2002, raising funds from supporters to buy the trophy. The Montreal Impact won the first six editions. In 2008 the Canadian Soccer Association launched the Canadian Championship as the official national cup competition, and the Voyageurs Cup became its trophy, with the winner also qualifying for the CONCACAF Champions Cup/Champions League (now the CONCACAF Champions Cup).

Format and winners

The modern Canadian Championship includes Canada's Major League Soccer clubs (Toronto FC, CF Montréal and Vancouver Whitecaps FC), Canadian Premier League clubs, and league champions from provincial League1 competitions. Toronto FC is the most successful club in the competition's history, with eight titles; the Montreal Impact/CF Montréal and Vancouver Whitecaps follow. The trophy is named in honour of the voyageurs, the French-Canadian fur-trade canoe men, and of the supporters group that founded it.
