Sylvester Stallone

Sylvester Gardenzio Stallone (born July 6, 1946) is an American actor and filmmaker. After his beginnings as a struggling actor for a number of years upon arriving in New York City in 1969, he achieved his greatest critical and commercial success as an actor and screenwriter with the boxing drama Rocky (1976), and went on to create two of cinema's most famous franchises: Rocky Balboa and John Rambo.

Career

Stallone wrote Rocky in days and insisted on starring in it; the film won the Academy Award for Best Picture and made him a star. He expanded his action-hero status with the Rambo series beginning with First Blood (1982), and later led the ensemble Expendables films. His return as an ageing Rocky in Creed (2015) earned him a Golden Globe and an Academy Award nomination.
