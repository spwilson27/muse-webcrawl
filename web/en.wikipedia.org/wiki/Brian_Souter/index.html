Sir Brian Souter (born 5 May 1954) is a Scottish billionaire businessman. With his sister, Dame Ann Gloag, he founded the transport company Stagecoach Group in 1980. He also founded Highland Global Transport, the private investment vehicle Souter Holdings Ltd and the bus manufacturer Alexander Dennis.

Career

Souter, a trained accountant and former bus conductor, started Stagecoach with two second-hand buses running express services from Dundee to London, exploiting bus deregulation under the Transport Act 1980. The company grew through acquisitions of privatised bus operators and rail franchises, including South West Trains, to become one of Britain's largest transport groups. Souter served as chief executive and later chairman of Stagecoach; the group returned to private ownership in 2022.

Public life

Souter is known for his evangelical Christian faith and his charitable work through the Souter Charitable Trust. In 2000 he funded the privately run "Keep the Clause" campaign against the repeal of Section 28 in Scotland. He was a prominent financial supporter of the Scottish National Party, and was knighted in 2011 for services to transport and the voluntary sector.
