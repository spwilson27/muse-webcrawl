Library

Library
DefinitionCollection of books and other media, and the institution holding it
Famous examplesLibrary of Alexandria, British Library, Library of Congress
[Image: Long reading room of a great library, with rows of desks and towering book stacks]

A library is a collection of books, manuscripts, periodicals, recordings and digital media organised for use, and the institution or space that houses it. Libraries range from the legendary ancient Library of Alexandria to national libraries, university libraries and public lending libraries.

Role

Libraries preserve the written record — the product of writing and publishing — provide education and research access, and increasingly lend e-books (see E-book), offer internet access and community services. Librarianship manages cataloguing, preservation and lending; the digital age has transformed storage and discovery without removing the library's civic role.
