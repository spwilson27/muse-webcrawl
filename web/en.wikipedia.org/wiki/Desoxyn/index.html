Desoxyn

Desoxyn is a brand name for methamphetamine hydrochloride tablets, a central nervous system stimulant legally prescribed in the United States in limited circumstances. It is classified as a Schedule II controlled substance.

The drug is approved by the FDA for the treatment of attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) in children six years and older — typically only when other treatments have failed — and for the short-term treatment of exogenous obesity. Because of its high potential for abuse and dependence, prescribing is rare compared with other stimulants such as Adderall or methylphenidate products.

Desoxyn was formerly produced by Abbott Laboratories and later marketed by Ovation Pharmaceuticals and Recordati Rare Diseases. Its existence as a legal pharmaceutical contrasts with illicit methamphetamine, which is chemically the same substance but produced and used outside medical supervision.
