Narvik

Narvik is a town and municipality in Nordland county, Norway, located on the shores of the Ofotfjorden above the Arctic Circle. It is a major ice-free port for the export of Swedish iron ore.

History

Narvik grew up after 1902 as the Atlantic terminus of the Ofoten Line railway from the Kiruna and Gällivare iron mines in Sweden. In April–June 1940 it was the scene of the Battles of Narvik, major naval and land engagements of the Second World War between German forces and Allied (British, French, Polish and Norwegian) troops.

Geography and economy

Thanks to the North Atlantic Current, Narvik's harbour remains ice-free year-round despite its latitude of 68°N. Ore shipping, rail operations and tourism — including skiing at Narvikfjellet — underpin the economy.
