The New Jewel Movement (NJM) was a Marxist–Leninist political party in Grenada that seized power in a near-bloodless coup on 13 March 1979, establishing the People's Revolutionary Government (PRG). The movement's rule ended in 1983 after internal conflict and a United States invasion.

Origins

The NJM was formed in 1973 from the merger of groups including the Joint Endeavour for Welfare, Education and Liberation (JEWEL). It opposed the authoritarian government of Prime Minister Eric Gairy, whose regime was accused of corruption and repression by the 'Mongoose Gang' paramilitary force.

People's Revolutionary Government

On 13 March 1979, while Gairy was abroad, NJM forces seized power. Maurice Bishop became prime minister of the PRG, which suspended the constitution and governed by decree while introducing literacy campaigns, free healthcare, new schools and an international airport built with Cuban assistance.

Downfall

In October 1983 a faction led by deputy prime minister Bernard Coard placed Bishop under house arrest; Bishop was freed by supporters but then captured and executed by elements of the army. Six days later, on 25 October 1983, the United States and Caribbean allies invaded Grenada in Operation Urgent Fury, ending the revolutionary government.
