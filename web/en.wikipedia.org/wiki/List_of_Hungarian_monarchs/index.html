List of Hungarian monarchs

This is a list of Hungarian monarchs, from the grand princes of the Hungarians through the kings of Hungary to the Habsburg emperors who held the Hungarian crown until 1918. The Kingdom of Hungary was founded in 1000 (or 1001) with the coronation of Stephen I, later canonized as St Stephen.

Grand Princes (before 1000)

The Árpád dynasty's grand princes included Árpád (c. 895–907), and later Géza (972–997), who began the Christianization of Hungary, and his son Stephen.
