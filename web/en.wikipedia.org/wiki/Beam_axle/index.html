Beam axle

Beam axle
Type: Vehicle suspension axle
Also called: Rigid axle, solid axle
Used in: Trucks, SUVs, trailers

A beam axle, also called a rigid axle or solid axle, is a vehicle suspension design in which the wheels on each side are connected by a single rigid beam, so that movement of one wheel affects the other.

Description

The beam may be live, containing a differential and driveshafts that power the wheels, or dead, simply supporting them. Leaf springs or coil springs with locating links attach it to the chassis.

Advantages and disadvantages

Beam axles are strong, simple and maintain constant wheel alignment under load, but they are heavy and can give poorer ride and handling than independent suspension.

Applications

They remain common on heavy trucks, off-road vehicles such as the Jeep Wrangler, and as rear axles on many pickups and vans.
