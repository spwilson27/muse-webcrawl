AH1

AH1 is an enzyme — an NAD(P)H dehydrogenase (quinone) — encoded in humans by the gene of the same family of flavoproteins. It is also the name used in a number of catalogues for related oxidoreductases that participate in the reduction of quinones and the detoxification of reactive compounds.

Function

AH1 catalyses two-electron reductions of quinone substrates using NADH or NADPH as electron donor, a reaction that avoids the formation of semiquinone radicals and helps protect cells from oxidative stress. Enzymes of this class are studied for their roles in drug metabolism and in the activation or detoxification of antitumour quinones.
