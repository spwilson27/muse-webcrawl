Bootstrap paradox

The bootstrap paradox, also known as an ontological paradox, is a paradox of time travel in which an object, piece of information, or person sent back in time becomes trapped in an infinite causal loop with no discernible origin.

Description

For example, a time traveller copies a famous book from the future and publishes it in the past; the published copy becomes the source of the future copy, so the text has no original author. The name refers to 'pulling oneself up by one's bootstraps'.

In fiction

Robert A. Heinlein's stories 'By His Bootstraps' and '—All You Zombies—' popularized the idea. Examples appear in Doctor Who (the episode 'Blink' and the term's modern popularization), Predestination, Dark, and Terminator storylines.

Relation to physics

Closed timelike curves in general relativity permit causal loops; Novikov's self-consistency principle proposes only self-consistent loops can occur.
