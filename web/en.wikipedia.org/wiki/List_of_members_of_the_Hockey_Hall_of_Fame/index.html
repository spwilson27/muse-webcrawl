List of members of the Hockey Hall of Fame

The Hockey Hall of Fame, located in Toronto, Ontario, honours players, builders and on-ice officials who have made outstanding contributions to ice hockey. Members are inducted in three categories: players, builders and referees/linesmen. This article lists notable members of the Hockey Hall of Fame.

The Hockey Hall of Fame at Brookfield Place, Toronto.
