List of rivers of Vermont

This is a list of rivers in the U.S. state of Vermont. Vermont's rivers drain into three major basins: Lake Champlain to the west, the Connecticut River along the eastern border, and (for a small area in the south) the Hudson River in New York.

History

Vermont's rivers powered the mills of the state's early industry and remain important for hydroelectric generation, recreation and trout fishing. Catastrophic flooding struck during the Great Vermont Flood of 1927 and again with Hurricane Irene in 2011, when the West River and Otter Creek valleys suffered extensive damage.
