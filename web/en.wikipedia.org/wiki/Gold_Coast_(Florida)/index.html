Gold Coast (Florida)

The Gold Coast is a region of the U.S. state of Florida, comprising the southeastern portion of the state along the Atlantic coast. It includes the Miami metropolitan area and the counties of Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach, stretching from around Miami north through Fort Lauderdale and West Palm Beach to Palm Beach and beyond.

The name reflects the region's history of affluence, glamour and lavish development, recalling the gold-coloured wealth of its resort cities and, some say, treasure recovered from wrecked Spanish galleons along the coast. It is one of several "coasts" of Florida, lying south of the Treasure Coast and the Space Coast.

The Gold Coast is known for its beaches, boating, Art Deco and Mediterranean Revival architecture in places such as Palm Beach and Miami Beach, and for a large retiree and international population.
