Herculis

Herculis is an annual track and field athletics meeting held at the Stade Louis II in Fontvieille, Monaco. It is one of the stops of the Diamond League, the top tier of one-day athletics meetings, and is organised by the Monegasque Athletics Federation under the patronage of Prince Albert II.

History

The meeting traces its origins to 1987. It joined the IAAF Golden League in 1998 and became a founding member of the Diamond League in 2010. The meeting's name recalls Hercules (Heracles), the legendary founder of Monaco's port district.

Notable performances

The fast Monaco track is famous for middle-distance records. In 2020 Joshua Cheptegei broke the 5000 metres world record there with 12:35.36, while Faith Kipyegon set a 1500 metres world record of 3:49.11 at the meeting in 2023. The meeting is regularly voted among the best in the Diamond League on World Athletics' scoring system.

Venue

The Stade Louis II, opened in 1985, seats about 18,500 spectators and is also the home ground of AS Monaco football club. Athletics events are held there each July.
