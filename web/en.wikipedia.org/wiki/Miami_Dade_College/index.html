Miami Dade College (MDC) is a public college in Miami, Florida, United States. Founded in 1959 as Dade County Junior College, it is among the largest institutions of higher education in the United States by enrolment and is noted for the diversity of its student body, enrolling students from more than 160 countries.

History and campuses

The college opened in 1960 and grew alongside Miami-Dade County. It was renamed Miami-Dade Community College and, after being authorised to grant bachelor's degrees, Miami Dade College. Its campuses include Wolfson (downtown), Kendall, North, Homestead, Hialeah, West, Medical and Padrón. The Freedom Tower, a National Historic Landmark in downtown Miami, is owned by the college.

Academics and culture

MDC offers associate and bachelor's degrees, certificates and workforce training across hundreds of programmes, including nursing, engineering technology, education and film. It hosts the Miami Book Fair International, founded in 1984, and the Miami Film Festival, two of the city's signature cultural events, as well as the Museum of Art and Design (MOAD) at the Freedom Tower.
