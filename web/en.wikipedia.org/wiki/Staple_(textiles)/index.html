Staple (textiles)

In textiles, staple refers to the length of individual fibres. Natural fibres such as cotton and wool occur in short lengths and are called staple fibres, in contrast to filament fibres such as silk or synthetic yarns that run in continuous lengths.

Staple length

Staple length is a key quality measure: longer cotton staples (extra-long staple, up to about 50 mm) produce finer, stronger yarns, while short staples yield coarser fabrics. Wool staple length determines whether fibres are processed on the woollen or worsted system.

Staple fibres

Synthetic fibres are often cut into staple lengths to mimic natural fibres and to be blended and spun on conventional equipment.
