Adobe Photoshop Lightroom

DeveloperAdobe Inc.
Initial releaseFebruary 19, 2007
TypePhoto management and editing software
PlatformWindows, macOS, iOS, Android, web

Adobe Photoshop Lightroom, often shortened to Lightroom, is a photo management and photo editing application developed by Adobe, primarily aimed at photographers for managing large volumes of digital images and performing post-production work. Unlike Adobe Photoshop, Lightroom is a non-destructive editor: edits are stored as instructions applied to the image rather than altering the original file.

Overview

Lightroom organizes work into modules: Library (importing, cataloguing, keywording, and rating images), Develop (raw conversion and adjustments such as exposure, white balance, tone curves, lens corrections, and local adjustments), and output modules for slideshows, printing, and web galleries. It supports most camera raw formats as well as JPEG, TIFF, and DNG.

Versions

The first public beta was released in 2006, and version 1.0 followed in February 2007. In 2017 Adobe split the product line: Lightroom Classic retained the folder-based desktop workflow, while a new cloud-based service, simply called Lightroom (formerly Lightroom CC), stores images in Adobe's cloud and runs across desktop, mobile, and web. The software is offered by subscription through Adobe Creative Cloud.
