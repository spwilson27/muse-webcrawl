Peter John Sallis (February 1, 1921 – June 2, 2017) was an English actor, known for his long-running role as Norman Clegg in the BBC sitcom Last of the Summer Wine and as the voice of Wallace in the Wallace and Gromit films.

Career

Sallis served in the Royal Air Force during the Second World War and trained as an actor afterwards, building a substantial stage and screen career. In 1973 he joined the cast of Last of the Summer Wine as the mild-mannered Clegg, a role he played for the programme's entire 37-year run until 2010 — he was the only actor to appear in all 295 episodes. His many screen credits include The Wind in the Willows, episodes of Doctor Who and The Avengers, and films such as Taste the Blood of Dracula.

Wallace

In 1985 Sallis was cast by animator Nick Park as the voice of the absent-minded, cheese-loving inventor Wallace, beginning with the Oscar-winning short A Grand Day Out (1989). He voiced the character in The Wrong Trousers (1993), A Close Shave (1995), the feature film The Curse of the Were-Rabbit (2005) and A Matter of Loaf and Death (2008), before retiring from the role due to age; the part subsequently passed to Ben Whitehead. Sallis died aged 96 in 2017.
