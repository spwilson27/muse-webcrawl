Enfield Town railway station

Location: Enfield, London Borough of Enfield
Zone: Travelcard Zone 5
Opened: 1 March 1849
Operator: London Overground
Platforms: 3

Enfield Town railway station is the terminus of a branch line in Enfield Town, in the London Borough of Enfield, north London. It is served by London Overground services on the Lea Valley lines, running to London Liverpool Street via Seven Sisters.

History

The station opened on 1 March 1849 as Enfield, the terminus of the Eastern Counties Railway's branch from Angel Road; it was renamed Enfield Town in 1886 to distinguish it from Enfield Lock on the main Lea Valley line. The line was part of the Great Eastern Railway and later the London and North Eastern Railway. In 2015 the service transferred from Abellio Greater Anglia to London Overground as part of its Lea Valley takeover, with new Class 710 trains introduced on the route.

Services

Trains generally run every 30 minutes to Liverpool Street, calling at Bush Hill Park, Edmonton Green, Silver Street, White Hart Lane, Seven Sisters (for the Victoria line), and Hackney Downs among other stops. The station has three platforms, a modest period building on Southbury Road, and a small station car park and bus interchange nearby.
