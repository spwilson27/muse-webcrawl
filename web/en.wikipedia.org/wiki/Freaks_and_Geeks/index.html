Freaks and Geeks

Freaks and Geeks is an American teen comedy-drama television series created by Paul Feig and executive-produced by Judd Apatow. It aired on NBC during the 1999–2000 television season. Set at a suburban Detroit high school in 1980–81, the show follows gifted student Lindsay Weir, who befriends a group of "freaks", and her younger brother Sam, who navigates freshman year with his "geek" friends.

Cast and production

The ensemble cast included Linda Cardellini, John Francis Daley, James Franco, Samm Levine, Seth Rogen, Jason Segel, Martin Starr, and Busy Philipps. Despite critical acclaim for its realistic portrayal of adolescence, the series struggled in the ratings, was frequently pre-empted, and was cancelled after 12 of its 18 produced episodes had aired.

Legacy

Freaks and Geeks has since been widely praised as one of the greatest television series of all time, appearing on numerous critics' lists, and it launched the careers of much of its cast and creative team. The remaining episodes aired on Fox Family Channel, and the complete series has been released on DVD and streaming services.
