Alexis Tsipras (Greek: Αλέξης Τσίπρας; born 28 July 1974) is a Greek politician who served as Prime Minister of Greece from 2015 to 2019. He was the leader of the left-wing Syriza party from 2008 until 2023.

Rise to power

A civil engineer by training, Tsipras became active in politics through the youth wing of the Communist Party and later Synaspismos. Under his leadership Syriza grew from a small coalition into Greece's main opposition party during the debt crisis, campaigning against austerity imposed under the country's bailout programmes.

Premiership

After winning the January 2015 election, Tsipras formed a government with the nationalist ANEL party. His government held a referendum in July 2015 in which voters rejected creditors' bailout terms, but facing the imminent collapse of the banking system he accepted a third bailout with further austerity, and won re-election that September. His government's other major achievement was the 2018 Prespa Agreement with North Macedonia, ending the long-running Macedonia naming dispute. Syriza lost the 2019 election to New Democracy, and Tsipras resigned as party leader after poor results in 2023.
