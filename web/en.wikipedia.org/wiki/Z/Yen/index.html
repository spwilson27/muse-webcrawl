Z/Yen is a commercial think tank and consultancy based in the City of London. Founded in 1994, the firm works in financial services, technology and policy research, combining consultancy with an in-house research programme. Its best-known publication is the Global Financial Centres Index (GFCI), a widely cited ranking of the competitiveness of financial centres around the world.

Activities

Z/Yen was founded by Professor Michael Mainelli and colleagues after leaving a major accountancy and defence consultancy. The firm has undertaken projects in risk assessment, strategy, technology evaluation and market reform for clients in finance and government. Its research arm, Long Finance, was established to explore long-term thinking in finance and society, and has produced research on topics including the future of financial centres, sustainable finance and distributed ledger technology.

Indices

The Global Financial Centres Index, first published in 2007 and updated twice yearly, combines statistical data with survey responses from financial professionals to rank centres such as New York, London, Hong Kong and Singapore. Z/Yen also publishes the Global Green Finance Index, launched in 2018, which assesses the quality and depth of green finance offerings in centres worldwide. These indices are produced in association with partners including the China Development Institute in Shenzhen.
