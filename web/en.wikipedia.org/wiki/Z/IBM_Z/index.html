The IBM Z brand covers IBM's modern mainframe servers, the successors to the zSeries line. Successive generations — z13 (2015), z14 (2017), z15 (2019), z16 (2022), and z17 (2025) — have added pervasive encryption, cloud integration with IBM Cloud and Red Hat OpenShift, on-chip AI acceleration, and confidential-computing features.

Design priorities

IBM Z design targets "seven nines"-class availability goals at the system level, the industry's highest Common Criteria security certifications for partitioning, and support for thousands of virtual servers on one footprint. The systems remain fully compatible with applications written for the System/360 era in many cases.

Market role

Mainframes of the IBM Z line are concentrated in banking, insurance, government, retail, and airlines, where very high transaction throughput and data integrity justify their cost; IBM estimates that most of the world's top banks run IBM Z.
