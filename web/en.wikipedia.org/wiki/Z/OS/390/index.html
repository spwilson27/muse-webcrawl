z/OS/390

z/OS/390

Developer: IBM

Family: IBM mainframe operating systems

Platform: IBM System/390 and zSeries

z/OS/390 refers to the release of IBM's flagship mainframe operating system z/OS as it ran on System/390 hardware, and is also the name under which the OS/390 product line was marketed before the z/OS name was adopted in 2000.

History

OS/390 was introduced by IBM in 1995 as a consolidation of the MVS product line and its associated program products into a single packaged offering for the System/390 architecture. When IBM announced the 64-bit zSeries servers in 2000, OS/390 was renamed z/OS, and the final releases of OS/390 continued to be supported on 31-bit System/390 machines.

Architecture

The operating system provides virtual storage management, job entry subsystems (JES2 and JES3), the UNIX System Services environment, and support for large transaction-processing workloads via CICS and IMS. Workloads are managed by the Workload Manager (WLM), which allocates system resources according to service policies defined by the installation.
