Leesville Lake is a reservoir in Virginia used for hydroelectric power generation in conjunction with Smith Mountain Lake. It is located southeast of Roanoke, and created by Leesville Dam on the Roanoke River. The lake has a surface area of about 3,270 acres and roughly 110 miles of shoreline, lying within Pittsylvania, Bedford and Campbell counties.

Pumped storage

Leesville Lake serves as the lower reservoir in a pumped-storage scheme with Smith Mountain Lake. Water is pumped from Leesville up into Smith Mountain Lake during periods of low electricity demand and released back through turbines at times of peak demand, helping the operator, Appalachian Power, balance the regional grid.

Recreation

The lake is used for boating, fishing and swimming. Anglers target largemouth bass, striped bass, catfish and crappie. Development around Leesville Lake is more limited than at the larger Smith Mountain Lake upstream, giving it a quieter character.
