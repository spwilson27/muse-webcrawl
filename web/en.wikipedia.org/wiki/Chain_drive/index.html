Chain drive

A chain drive is a way of transmitting mechanical power from one place to another using a chain engaging sprockets. It is most familiar as the drive system of bicycles and motorcycles, and is widely used in industrial machinery, agricultural equipment and conveyors.

Design

The most common form is the roller chain, consisting of pins, bushings, rollers and side plates. The chain meshes with toothed sprockets, providing a positive drive with no slip and allowing precise speed ratios.

Advantages and disadvantages

Chain drives are efficient, compact, and can transmit high torque, but require lubrication, alignment and periodic tension adjustment, and are noisier than belt drives.
