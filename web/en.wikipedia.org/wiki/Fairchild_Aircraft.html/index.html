Fairchild Aircraft

Fairchild Aircraft was an American aircraft and aerospace manufacturing company based at various times in Farmingdale, New York, and Hagerstown, Maryland. Founded by Sherman Fairchild in 1925 as Fairchild Aviation, it became one of the pioneering firms of American aviation.

History

Fairchild produced a wide range of aircraft, including the FC-2 and Model 71 cabin monoplanes, the PT-19 Cornell trainer of the Second World War, the C-82 Packet and C-119 Flying Boxcar transports, and the A-10 Thunderbolt II attack aircraft, built by Fairchild Republic. In later decades the company's remnants passed through Fairchild Dornier, which collapsed in 2002, and M7 Aerospace, acquired by Elbit Systems in 2010.
