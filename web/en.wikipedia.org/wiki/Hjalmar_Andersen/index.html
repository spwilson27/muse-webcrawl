Hjalmar Johan "Hjallis" Andersen (12 March 1923 – 27 March 2013) was a Norwegian speed skater who dominated the sport in the early 1950s and became one of Norway's most beloved sports figures.

Career

Andersen won the World Allround Championships and the European Championships three consecutive years (1950–1952). At the 1952 Winter Olympics in Oslo he won three gold medals — in the 1,500, 5,000, and 10,000 metres — the latter two by the largest margins in Olympic history. He set several world records, including being the first skater to break 16 minutes for the 10,000 metres.

Later life

After a brief comeback for the 1956 Olympics, Andersen worked in shipping and ran a business in Trondheim. He remained a national celebrity in Norway, publishing memoirs and appearing frequently in the media until his death in 2013. See also Speed skating.
