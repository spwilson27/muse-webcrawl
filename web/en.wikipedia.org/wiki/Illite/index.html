Illite

Illite is a group of closely related non-expanding clay minerals. It is a secondary mineral precipitate and a phyllosilicate (sheet silicate) similar in structure to muscovite, with more silicon, magnesium, iron and water and less tetrahedral aluminium and interlayer potassium.

Occurrence

Illite occurs widely in soils, sediments and sedimentary rocks such as shales, formed by the weathering of feldspars and micas or by alteration during burial diagenesis. The name was coined in 1937 from samples in Illinois.

Uses in geology

Because illite forms and orders progressively with burial temperature, its crystallinity (the Kübler index) is used as an indicator of metamorphic grade, and potassium–argon dating of illite can date fault movements and diagenetic events.
