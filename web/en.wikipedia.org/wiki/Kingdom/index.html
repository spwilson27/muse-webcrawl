Kingdom (political)

Kingdom (political)
DefinitionState or realm ruled by a king or queen
ExamplesUnited Kingdom, Saudi Arabia, Thailand, historical kingdoms worldwide
[Image: A medieval king enthroned in a great hall, courtiers attending (painting style)]

A kingdom is a realm or state ruled by a king or queen — the classic unit of the medieval and early-modern world (the Middle Ages in the wider crawl), from England and France to the kingdoms of Africa and Southeast Asia.

Today

Surviving kingdoms are mostly constitutional monarchies or the few absolute ones; 'kingdom' also names biological classification's broadest ranks — the animal and plant kingdoms of the Dog, Flower pages' taxonomy.
