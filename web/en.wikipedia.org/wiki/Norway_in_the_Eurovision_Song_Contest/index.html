Norway in the Eurovision Song Contest

Norway has participated in the Eurovision Song Contest since its debut in 1960, organised by the broadcaster NRK through the national selection Melodi Grand Prix. Norway has won the contest three times, but also holds the record for the most last-place finishes, including several with zero points ("nul points").

Victories

Norway's wins came in 1985 with Bobbysocks and "La det swinge", in 1995 with Secret Garden's largely instrumental "Nocturne", and in 2009 with Alexander Rybak's "Fairytale", which won with a then-record 387 points. Norway hosted the contest in Bergen (1986) and Oslo (1996 and 2010).

Record

Norwegian results have been famously uneven: the country finished last eleven times, more than any other, with zero points in 1963, 1978, 1981 and 1997. Successful recent entries include Margaret Berger (2013), KEiiNO's "Spirit in the Sky" (2019), which won the public vote, and Subwoolfer (2022). Notable earlier Norwegian songs include "I evighet" (1996, second place) and Jahn Teigen's appearances, a beloved figure despite his 1978 zero-point result.
