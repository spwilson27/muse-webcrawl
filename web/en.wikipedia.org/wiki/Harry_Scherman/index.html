Harry Scherman

Harry Scherman (February 1, 1887 – November 12, 1969) was an American publisher and economist who co-founded the Book of the Month Club in 1926, revolutionising the marketing and distribution of books in the United States.

Book of the Month Club

Scherman, who had worked in advertising, founded the club with Robert K. Haas and others. Subscribers received a monthly selection chosen by a panel of judges, with the option to decline or choose alternates. The model brought serious literature to a mass readership and at its peak had well over a million members.

Writing

Scherman wrote popular works on economics, including The Promises Men Live By (1938), a widely read account of economic life, and worked to explain economic ideas to general audiences.
