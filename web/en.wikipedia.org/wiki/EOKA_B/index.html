EOKA B

EOKA B
Active: 1971–1974
Leader: George Grivas
Goal: Enosis (union with Greece)

EOKA B was a Greek Cypriot paramilitary organisation active from 1971 to 1974. It was founded by General George Grivas, leader of the original EOKA, to pursue enosis — the union of Cyprus with Greece — and to undermine President Makarios III, who had accepted an independent Cypriot state.

Activities

EOKA B carried out bombings, assassinations and attacks on government targets and on Greek Cypriot opponents, and clashed with the pro-Makarios paramilitary Tactical Police Reserve. Funded and directed in part by the Greek military junta, it was declared illegal by the Cypriot government in April 1974.

1974 coup and aftermath

On 15 July 1974 the Cypriot National Guard and EOKA B overthrew Makarios in a coup backed by Athens. Five days later Turkey invaded Cyprus, citing the coup, leading to the island's de facto partition. EOKA B dissolved amid the collapse of the junta; some members were later prosecuted for crimes committed during the period.
