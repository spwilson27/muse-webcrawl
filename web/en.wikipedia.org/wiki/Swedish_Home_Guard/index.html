Swedish Home Guard

Swedish Home Guard
Swedish: Hemvärnet
Founded: 29 May 1940
Country: Sweden
Branch: Swedish Armed Forces
Role: Territorial defence, support to society

The Swedish Home Guard (Swedish: Hemvärnet) is a military reserve force within the Swedish Armed Forces. It consists of volunteer personnel who serve part-time and forms a substantial share of Sweden's territorial defence capability. Its motto is "Överallt och alltid" — "Everywhere and always".

History

The Home Guard was established on 29 May 1940, during the Second World War, when Sweden sought to strengthen its defences while remaining neutral. In its early years it grew rapidly, at its height numbering over 100,000 members. After the end of the Cold War the organisation shrank considerably, but a renewed focus on national defence following Russia's aggression against Ukraine led to a surge in applications and an expansion of the force.

Organisation

The Home Guard is organised into battalions across Sweden's military regions. Its core consists of the National Security Forces (Nationella skyddsstyrkorna), rapidly deployable light infantry units, alongside support functions such as transport, medical services, CBRN defence, military bands and dog units. Members sign a service agreement and train for a number of days each year, keeping their equipment at home or in local depots.

Tasks

In wartime the Home Guard guards and protects important infrastructure and supports the regular armed forces. In peacetime it regularly assists civil society during crises, such as forest fires, floods, storms and searches for missing persons.
