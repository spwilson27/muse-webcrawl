Emmett Shear (born 1983) is an American technology entrepreneur and investor. He is the co-founder of the live-streaming platform Twitch, which he led as chief executive officer from its launch until 2023, and briefly served as interim CEO of OpenAI in November 2023.

Early life and education

Shear grew up in Seattle and studied computer science at Yale University, graduating in 2005, where he met his future co-founder Justin Kan.

Career

Through the startup accelerator Y Combinator, Shear and Kan first built the calendar application Kiko and then Justin.tv, a lifecasting site launched in 2007. Its gaming section was spun out in 2011 as Twitch, with Shear as CEO. Twitch grew into the dominant platform for video game live streaming and was acquired by Amazon in 2014 for approximately $970 million. Shear stepped down as CEO in March 2023, after 16 years at the company, remaining an advisor.

OpenAI interim CEO

On 19 November 2023, following the OpenAI board's abrupt removal of Sam Altman, Shear was appointed interim CEO of OpenAI. His tenure lasted only a few days: after negotiations and an overwhelming staff letter in support of Altman, Altman was reinstated on 22 November and Shear stepped aside.

Other work

Shear has been a part-time partner at Y Combinator and an angel investor, and has written publicly about artificial intelligence alignment and child development.
