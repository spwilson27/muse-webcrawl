The Chevrolet Corvette (C6) is the sixth generation of the Corvette sports car, produced by Chevrolet for the 2005 to 2013 model years. It was the first Corvette since 1962 with exposed headlamps, and it was shorter overall than its predecessor while having a longer wheelbase.

Models

The base model used the 6.0-litre LS2 V8 producing 400 hp, replaced in 2008 by the 6.2-litre LS3 with 430 hp. The track-focused Z06 (2006) introduced the 7.0-litre LS7 V8 with 505 hp, an aluminium frame, and carbon-fibre body panels. In 2009 the supercharged ZR1 arrived with the 6.2-litre LS9 producing 638 hp, at the time the most powerful production car ever built by General Motors. A Grand Sport model, combining the LS3 with Z06-style bodywork and chassis components, was added for 2010, and a 427 Convertible Collector Edition marked the final year.

Motorsport

The C6.R racing version, campaigned by Corvette Racing, won its class at the 24 Hours of Le Mans and dominated the American Le Mans Series GT1 category.
