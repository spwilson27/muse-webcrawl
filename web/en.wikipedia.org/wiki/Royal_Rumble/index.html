Royal Rumble

The Royal Rumble is a professional wrestling event produced annually since 1988 by WWE. It is named after the Royal Rumble match, a modified battle royal in which participants enter at timed intervals rather than all beginning in the ring at the same time. The event is one of WWE's "Big Five" pay-per-views, alongside WrestleMania, SummerSlam, Survivor Series and Money in the Bank, and traditionally takes place in January, beginning the "Road to WrestleMania".

The Royal Rumble match

In the standard Royal Rumble match, 30 wrestlers draw entry numbers and enter the ring at intervals of about 90 seconds to two minutes. A wrestler is eliminated when thrown over the top rope with both feet touching the floor. The last competitor remaining wins. Since 1993, the prize has been a world championship match at that year's WrestleMania; winners traditionally celebrate by pointing at the WrestleMania sign above the arena.

The match concept was created by WWE executive Pat Patterson. The first event took place on 24 January 1988, as a television special on the USA Network, and became a pay-per-view the following year. "Hacksaw" Jim Duggan won the inaugural 20-man match. Record holders include Stone Cold Steve Austin, the only three-time winner (1997, 1998, 2001).

Women's Royal Rumble

In 2018 WWE introduced a women's Royal Rumble match with the same 30-participant format, with the winner earning a women's championship match at WrestleMania. Asuka won the first women's match. Both matches now headline the event each year.
