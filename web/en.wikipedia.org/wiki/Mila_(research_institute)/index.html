Mila (research institute)

Mila (French: Institut québécois d'intelligence artificielle, "Quebec Artificial Intelligence Institute") is a non-profit artificial intelligence research institute in Montreal, Quebec, Canada. Founded by Professor Yoshua Bengio of the Université de Montréal, Mila grew out of the Montreal Institute for Learning Algorithms (also MILA), established in 1993.

Overview

Mila brings together one of the world's largest concentrations of academic deep-learning researchers — hundreds of graduate students, postdoctoral fellows and professors affiliated mainly with the Université de Montréal and McGill University. Its core research spans deep learning theory, reinforcement learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and AI for science, including climate, health and drug discovery.

The institute is one of three national AI institutes created under the Pan-Canadian AI Strategy (2017), alongside Amii in Edmonton and the Vector Institute in Toronto. Since 2019 it has been based in the Mile-Ex district of Montreal, in a hub shared with industrial AI labs such as those of Google, Microsoft, Meta, IBM and Samsung, and it maintains numerous partnerships with industry while keeping an academic, open-publication ethos.

AI for humanity

Mila has promoted the responsible development of AI, hosting work on AI ethics and governance and contributing to the Montreal Declaration for Responsible Development of AI. Bengio's public advocacy on AI safety is closely associated with the institute.
