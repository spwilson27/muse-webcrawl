List of films based on Marvel Comics

Marvel Comics films
Films based on Marvel properties have grossed over $30 billion worldwide, led by the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

Marvel Comics is a comic book publishing company founded in 1939. Its characters have been adapted into numerous films, beginning with the serial Captain America in 1944. The modern era of Marvel filmmaking began with Blade (1998) and X-Men (2000), and was transformed by the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU), launched with Iron Man in 2008.

Major franchises

  • Marvel Cinematic Universe — produced by Marvel Studios since 2008; the highest-grossing film franchise of all time.
  • X-Men — produced by 20th Century Fox from 2000 to 2020, including the Wolverine and Deadpool sub-series.
  • Spider-Man — Sam Raimi trilogy (2002–2007), Marc Webb's Amazing Spider-Man films, and the MCU-integrated Tom Holland films with Sony.
  • Fantastic Four — adaptations in 1994 (unreleased), 2005–2007, 2015, and the MCU's 2025 entry.
  • Blade, Ghost Rider, Daredevil, Punisher — earlier licensed productions from New Line, Columbia and Lionsgate.
  • Animated films — including Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018), winner of the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature, and its sequels.

In 2009, The Walt Disney Company acquired Marvel Entertainment for approximately $4 billion, consolidating most film rights under Marvel Studios, although Sony retains the Spider-Man film rights.
