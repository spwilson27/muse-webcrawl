Sharif family

The Sharif family is a prominent Pakistani political and business family based in Lahore, Punjab. The family leads the Pakistan Muslim League (N) (PML-N) and has produced three prime ministers of Pakistan: Nawaz Sharif, who served three non-consecutive terms, and his younger brother Shehbaz Sharif, who became prime minister in 2022 and again in 2024.

Origins and business

The family's patriarch, Muhammad Sharif (died 2004), migrated from Amritsar to Lahore at Partition and founded the Ittefaq Group, a steel and industrial conglomerate that grew into one of Pakistan's largest business houses. The family's ancestral village is Jati Umra in Punjab, India, and its Lahore residence is also named Jati Umra.

Political career

Nawaz Sharif entered politics under General Zia-ul-Haq, becoming Chief Minister of Punjab in 1985 and Prime Minister in 1990. He was removed in 1993, returned in 1997, was overthrown by General Pervez Musharraf in 1999 and exiled, then won a third term in 2013 before his disqualification by the Supreme Court in the Panama Papers case in 2017. Shehbaz Sharif served multiple long terms as Chief Minister of Punjab and became party president. The next generation includes Maryam Nawaz, Nawaz's daughter, who became Chief Minister of Punjab in 2024 — the first woman to hold that office — and Hamza Shehbaz, briefly Chief Minister in 2022.

Controversies

The family has faced repeated corruption allegations and accountability cases, which it describes as politically motivated. Nawaz Sharif was convicted in the Avenfield and Al-Azizia references in 2018; his convictions were later overturned on appeal in 2023, clearing his return to electoral politics.
