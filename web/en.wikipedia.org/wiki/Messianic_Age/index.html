Messianic Age

The Messianic Age is, in Abrahamic religions, the future period of time on Earth in which the messiah will reign and bring universal peace and brotherhood, without any evil. Many religions believe that such an age will come, including Judaism, Christianity, Islam, and others.

In Judaism

Jewish eschatology holds that the Messiah (Mashiach), a descendant of King David, will gather the exiles of Israel, rebuild the Temple in Jerusalem, and inaugurate an era of peace in which the knowledge of God fills the earth. Rabbinic literature distinguishes the days of the Messiah from the World to Come (Olam Ha-Ba) that follows.

In Christianity

Christian views differ. Many Christians associate the Messianic Age with the Second Coming of Christ and the millennial reign described in the Book of Revelation, though amillennial interpretations read the kingdom spiritually in the present age. The age is characterised by the defeat of evil and the establishment of God's kingdom.

In Islam

In Islam, the Mahdi is expected to appear before the end of time and, together with Isa (Jesus), establish justice on earth after it has been filled with oppression.
