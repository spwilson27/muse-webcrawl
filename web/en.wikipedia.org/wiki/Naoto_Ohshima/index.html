Naoto Ohshima

Naoto Ohshima (大島 直人, Ōshima Naoto, born 26 February 1964) is a Japanese video game designer best known as the original character designer of Sonic the Hedgehog and Doctor Eggman, and as director of games including Nights into Dreams and Burning Rangers.

Career

Ohshima joined Sega in 1987 and worked on character designs for the Master System Phantasy Star and other titles. In 1990, as part of the team that became Sonic Team, his design for a speedy hedgehog character was chosen as Sega's new mascot, and he served as art director of Sonic the Hedgehog (1991), drawing the character, his nemesis Eggman, and much of the game's visual identity. He went on to direct Sonic CD (1993) and the Saturn titles Nights into Dreams (1996) and Burning Rangers (1998).

In 1999 Ohshima left Sega to co-found Artoon, which developed games such as Blinx: The Time Sweeper and Yoshi's Island DS. After Artoon's absorption into AQ Interactive, he later founded the studio Arzest in 2010. He returned to the Sonic series as character designer for Sonic Superstars (2023), developed by Arzest.
