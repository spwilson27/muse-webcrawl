List of digital camera brands

This is a list of digital camera brands. The market for standalone digital cameras peaked around 2010 and has since contracted sharply as smartphones absorbed the compact-camera segment; the surviving industry concentrates on interchangeable-lens cameras, premium compacts, instant cameras and specialist systems. The largest manufacturers by share in recent years have been Canon, Sony and Nikon, followed by Fujifilm and Panasonic.

Defunct or exited brands

  • Samsung — exited the dedicated camera market around 2017.
  • Minolta / Konica Minolta — camera business sold to Sony in 2006.
  • Contax, Yashica — Kyocera ended camera production in 2005.
  • Casio — withdrew from compact cameras in 2018.
  • Olympus (as a camera brand) — rebranded OM System in 2021.
