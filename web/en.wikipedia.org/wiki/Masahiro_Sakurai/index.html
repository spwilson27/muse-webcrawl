Masahiro Sakurai
Born: August 3, 1970, Tokyo, Japan
Occupation: Video game designer, director
Known for: Kirby, Super Smash Bros.

Masahiro Sakurai (born August 3, 1970) is a Japanese video game designer and director, best known as the creator of the Kirby and Super Smash Bros. series. He began his career at HAL Laboratory, where he created Kirby at age 19 for Kirby's Dream Land (1992).

Career

Sakurai directed Super Smash Bros. (1999) and its sequels. He left HAL in 2003 and founded Sora Ltd. in 2005, developing Meteos, Kid Icarus: Uprising, and later Smash Bros. titles with Bandai Namco and Nintendo, culminating in Super Smash Bros. Ultimate (2018).

Design philosophy and media

Sakurai wrote a long-running column in Weekly Famitsu and in 2022 launched the YouTube channel "Masahiro Sakurai on Creating Games," sharing game-design lessons. He is known for a focus on accessibility for beginners alongside depth for experts.
