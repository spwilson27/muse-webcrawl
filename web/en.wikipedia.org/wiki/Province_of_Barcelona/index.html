Province of Barcelona

Country: Spain
Autonomous community: Catalonia
Capital: Barcelona
Area: 7,726 km²
Population: about 5.6 million

The Province of Barcelona (Catalan: Província de Barcelona) is a province of eastern Spain, in the southern part of the autonomous community of Catalonia. It is Spain's second most populous province and contains the city of Barcelona, the Catalan capital and one of Europe's major metropolitan areas.

Geography

The province borders Tarragona to the southwest, Lleida to the northwest, Girona to the northeast, and the Mediterranean Sea to the southeast. Its terrain ranges from the coastal plain of the Barcelonès to the Pre-Coastal Range and the foothills of the Pyrenees, including the Montseny massif.

Government

The provincial council, the Diputació de Barcelona, supports the province's 311 municipalities. Major cities besides the capital include L'Hospitalet de Llobregat, Badalona, Terrassa, Sabadell, Mataró, and Manresa.
