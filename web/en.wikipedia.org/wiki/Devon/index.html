Devon
Country: England
Region: South West
County town: Exeter
Population: ~1.2 million
Related: Cornwall, Dartmoor, Exmoor

Devon is a ceremonial county in South West England, bordered by Cornwall to the west, Somerset and Dorset to the east, the English Channel to the south and the Bristol Channel to the north. Its county town is Exeter.

Geography

Devon contains two national parks, Dartmoor and part of Exmoor, and five Areas of Outstanding Natural Beauty. It is the only English county with two separate coastlines. Major towns include Plymouth, Torbay, Barnstaple and Paignton.

History

The name derives from the Dumnonii, a British Celtic tribe. Devon gave its name to the Devonian geological period, first described from its rocks. Plymouth was a departure point for the Mayflower in 1620 and a major naval base.

Economy and culture

Tourism, agriculture — famed for cream teas with clotted cream — and the military and marine sectors are important. The county is known for its narrow lanes, thatched villages and seaside resorts.
