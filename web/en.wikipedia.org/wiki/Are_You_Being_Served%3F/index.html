Are You Being Served? is a British television sitcom created and written by Jeremy Lloyd and David Croft. Broadcast on BBC One from 1972 to 1985, it is set in the ladies' and gentlemen's clothing departments of Grace Brothers, a fictional London department store.

Characters

The ensemble included Mrs Betty Slocombe (Mollie Sugden), the sales assistant famous for references to her cat; Mr Humphries (John Inman), the camp menswear assistant whose catchphrase was "I'm free!"; Captain Peacock (Frank Thornton), the floorwalker; and the young Mr Lucas (Trevor Bannister).

Legacy

The series ran for ten series and a 1977 feature film, and produced a sequel, Grace & Favour (1992–1993). It became one of Britain's most exported comedies and is noted for its double entendres and running gags.
