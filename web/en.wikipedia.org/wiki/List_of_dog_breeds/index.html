List of dog breeds

This list of dog breeds includes both extant and extinct breeds, varieties, and types. The Fédération Cynologique Internationale (FCI) recognises over 350 breeds, and national kennel clubs recognise many more varieties. Breeds are commonly grouped by function: sporting, herding, working, terrier, toy, hound, and non-sporting.
