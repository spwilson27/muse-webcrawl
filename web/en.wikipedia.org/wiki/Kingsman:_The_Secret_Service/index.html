Kingsman: The Secret Service

Kingsman: The Secret Service is a 2014 action spy film directed by Matthew Vaughn, based on the comic book The Secret Service by Mark Millar and Dave Gibbons. It stars Colin Firth as Harry Hart, Taron Egerton as Gary 'Eggsy' Unwin, Samuel L. Jackson as villain Richmond Valentine, and Michael Caine and Mark Strong.

A secret British spy organisation operating from a Savile Row tailor recruits streetwise Eggsy. Valentine plans to use free SIM cards to trigger a neurologically induced mass culling to combat climate change. The film was a commercial success, grossing over $414 million, praised for its stylish action including the church fight scene, and spawned the Kingsman franchise including Kingsman: The Golden Circle and The King's Man.
