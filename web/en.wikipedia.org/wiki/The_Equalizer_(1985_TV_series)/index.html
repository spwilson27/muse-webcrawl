The Equalizer (1985 TV series)

The Equalizer is an American spy thriller television series created by Michael Sloan and Richard Lindheim, broadcast on CBS from 1985 to 1989. It stars Edward Woodward as Robert McCall, a retired intelligence agent who offers his services, free of charge, to ordinary people in trouble through newspaper advertisements.

Premise

McCall, a former operative of an unnamed agency referred to as "the Company", places ads reading: "Got a problem? Odds against you? Call the Equalizer. 212-555-4200." He helps clients facing criminals, corrupt officials and spies, assisted by former colleagues including Mickey Kostmayer. The series is remembered for its gritty New York atmosphere and Stewart Copeland's electronic score.

Legacy

The series earned Woodward a Golden Globe Award. It was adapted into a film series starring Denzel Washington beginning in 2014, and a reimagined television series starring Queen Latifah premiered in 2021.
