West Bromwich Albion Football Club, commonly known as West Brom or the Baggies, is a professional football club based in West Bromwich, West Midlands, England. Founded in 1878 by workers at George Salter's Spring Works, it was one of the twelve founding members of the Football League in 1888.

History

Albion won the FA Cup in 1888 and 1892 before moving to The Hawthorns in 1900, a ground notable as the highest above sea level in English professional football. The club won its only league championship in 1919–20 and further FA Cups in 1931, 1954 — the famous "Team of the Century" final against Preston — and 1968, plus the League Cup in 1966. The late-1970s side under Ron Atkinson, featuring Laurie Cunningham, Cyrille Regis and Brendon Batson, was admired for its attacking football and broke racial barriers in the English game.

Recent years

Since 2002 Albion have alternated frequently between the Premier League and the EFL Championship, becoming something of a "yo-yo club". Their principal rivals are Wolverhampton Wanderers (the Black Country derby) and Aston Villa.
