List of stations in London fare zone 1

Zone 1 is the central zone of Transport for London's fare zone system, covering central London. It contains most of the city's principal railway termini, the City of London, the West End and many major tourist attractions. This article lists the London Underground, National Rail and Docklands Light Railway stations located within Zone 1.

Overview

The zone system was introduced in the early 1980s to simplify bus and Underground ticketing, and travel within Zone 1 attracts the highest fares. The Circle line runs largely around the boundary of the zone. Zone 1 includes around 60 Underground stations plus the main line termini.
