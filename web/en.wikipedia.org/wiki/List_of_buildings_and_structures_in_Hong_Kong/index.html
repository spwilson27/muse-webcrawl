List of buildings and structures in Hong Kong

Hong Kong has more skyscrapers than any other city in the world — over 550 buildings taller than 150 metres. This list catalogs notable buildings and structures in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, including its tallest towers, government and cultural buildings, bridges, and historic structures.
