Cheyenne Mountain

Cheyenne Mountain is a mountain summit at the southwest side of Colorado Springs, Colorado, United States. It is famous for the Cheyenne Mountain Complex, a United States Space Force installation bored into the mountain during the Cold War to house NORAD's command centre, designed to survive a nuclear attack.

The complex

Excavated in the 1960s, the underground complex consists of buildings mounted on springs inside granite caverns, protected by massive blast doors. While NORAD's operational centre later moved to Peterson Space Force Base, the mountain complex remains in use as an alternate command site.

Other features

The mountain also hosts the Cheyenne Mountain Zoo, the Broadmoor's Cloud Camp, and the Will Rogers Shrine of the Sun.
