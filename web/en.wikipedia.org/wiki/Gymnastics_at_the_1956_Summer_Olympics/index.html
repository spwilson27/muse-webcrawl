Gymnastics at the 1956 Summer Olympics

Gymnastics at the Games of the XVI Olympiad
VenueWest Melbourne Stadium, Melbourne
Dates3–7 December 1956
Competitors128 from 21 nations
Competition at the West Melbourne Stadium, December 1956

At the 1956 Summer Olympics in Melbourne, Australia, fifteen events in artistic gymnastics were contested — eight for men and seven for women — at the West Melbourne Stadium from 3 to 7 December 1956. The competition was dominated by the Soviet Union, whose gymnasts won the majority of the gold medals, with Japan emerging as a rising power in the men's events.

Format

Each nation could enter a team of up to eight gymnasts for the men's events and seven for the women's. The all-around and team scores were computed from compulsory and optional exercises on each apparatus. The women's programme included the portable apparatus team exercise, a rhythmic group event that was discontinued after these Games.

Medal table

The Soviet Union topped the medal table with eleven gold medals. Hungary, led by the 35-year-old Ágnes Keleti — who won four golds despite an injury to her hand — placed second. Melbourne 1956 marked the Olympic debut of Larisa Latynina, who would go on to become the most decorated female Olympian of the twentieth century.
