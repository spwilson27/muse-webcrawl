Water signs

The water triplicity: Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces.

In astrology, water signs are the three signs of the zodiac assigned to the element of water: Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces. The zodiac's twelve signs are grouped into four triplicities of three signs each, according to the classical elements — fire, earth, air and water. Water and earth signs are traditionally classed as feminine or negative (receptive), in contrast to the masculine fire and air signs.

Characteristics

Astrologers describe the water element as governing emotion, intuition, memory and the unconscious. In traditional astrological interpretation, people with strong water placements in their natal charts are characterized as sensitive, empathetic, imaginative and protective, but also prone to moodiness and withdrawal. Each sign expresses the element through a different modality: Cancer is the cardinal water sign, associated with home, family and care; Scorpio is the fixed water sign, associated with intensity, transformation and secrecy; Pisces is the mutable water sign, associated with dreams, compassion and dissolution of boundaries. Their traditional planetary rulers are the Moon (Cancer), Mars and, in modern astrology, Pluto (Scorpio), and Jupiter and, in modern astrology, Neptune (Pisces).

Background

The grouping derives from Hellenistic astrology, in which the triplicities were systematized by authors such as Ptolemy in the Tetrabiblos. Water signs were linked to the cold and wet qualities of humoral theory and to the phlegmatic temperament. Astrological attributions are a feature of cultural and historical study; astrology itself is regarded by the scientific community as a pseudoscience.
