Stepanakert

Stepanakert (Armenian: Ստեփանակերտ) is a city in the Nagorno-Karabakh region of Azerbaijan. Known in Azerbaijani as Khankendi, it served as the capital of the Nagorno-Karabakh Autonomous Oblast during the Soviet period and later as the capital of the self-proclaimed Republic of Artsakh.

History

The settlement was renamed in 1923 after the Armenian Bolshevik Stepan Shaumian and made the capital of the newly created autonomous oblast. The city was heavily involved in the First Nagorno-Karabakh War (1988–1994), after which it functioned as the seat of the unrecognised Artsakh republic.

2023 exodus

Following Azerbaijan's military offensive of September 2023 and the dissolution of Artsakh's institutions, nearly the entire ethnic Armenian population fled to Armenia within days — one of the swiftest mass displacements in the region's modern history. The city then came under full Azerbaijani administration.
