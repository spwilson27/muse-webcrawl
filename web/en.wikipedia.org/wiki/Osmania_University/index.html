Osmania University

Osmania University is a public state university located in Hyderabad, Telangana, India. Founded in 1918 by a royal firman of Mir Osman Ali Khan, the seventh and last Nizam of Hyderabad, it is named after its founder and is one of the oldest modern universities in India.

Osmania was the first Indian university to use Urdu as a medium of instruction, with English as a compulsory subject. Its campus, covering roughly 1,300 acres, is noted for its grand Indo-Saracenic architecture, especially the Arts College building, one of Hyderabad's heritage landmarks.

The university offers undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes across faculties including arts, sciences, commerce, law, engineering, technology, medicine and management, through constituent and affiliated colleges. Its alumni include former Prime Minister P. V. Narasimha Rao.
