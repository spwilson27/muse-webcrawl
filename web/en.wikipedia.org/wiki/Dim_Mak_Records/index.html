Dim Mak Records

Dim Mak Records is an American independent record label, events company and lifestyle brand founded in Los Angeles in 1996 by DJ and producer Steve Aoki. The label's name derives from the Chinese martial-arts "death touch" (dim mak), popularised by Bruce Lee films.

History

Dim Mak began in Aoki's college apartment releasing hardcore punk and indie rock 7-inches by local bands. In the 2000s it became a tastemaker indie label, releasing early records by acts such as Bloc Party (including the single "Banquet"), The Kills, Battles and MSTRKRFT. From the late 2000s the label pivoted toward electronic dance music, releasing work by artists including The Bloody Beetroots, Dada Life, Zedd, Steve Aoki himself and many others, and staging festival stages and international tours.

Activities

Beyond records, Dim Mak operates publishing, a clothing line and event brands, and remains one of the best-known independent EDM labels. In 2014 it launched the sub-label New Noise to give away copyright-free releases for online creators.
