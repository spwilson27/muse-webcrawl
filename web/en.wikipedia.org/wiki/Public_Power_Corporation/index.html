The Public Power Corporation (Greek: Δημόσια Επιχείρηση Ηλεκτρισμού, DEI; traded as PPC) is the largest electric power company in Greece. Founded in 1950, it historically held a near-monopoly on electricity generation and supply in the country.

Operations

PPC generates electricity from lignite, natural gas, hydroelectric plants and a growing portfolio of renewables, and supplies millions of customers. Its lignite mines in Western Macedonia and Megalopolis long powered Greek generation, though the company has been phasing out lignite under Greece's decarbonisation plans.

Liberalisation

Following EU energy market liberalisation, PPC's market share has gradually declined as independent suppliers entered, and the Greek state reduced its shareholding, though it remains the dominant incumbent.
