The Royal Regiment of Scotland (SCOTS) is the senior and only Scottish infantry regiment of the British Army. It was formed on 28 March 2006 by the amalgamation of Scotland's six remaining infantry regiments: the Royal Scots, the King's Own Scottish Borderers, the Royal Highland Fusiliers, the Black Watch, the Highlanders and the Argyll and Sutherland Highlanders.

History

The amalgamation, part of the 2004 defence reforms, was controversial in Scotland; the historic names survive as battalion subtitles. The Royal Scots and King's Own Scottish Borderers merged fully in August 2006 to form the Royal Scots Borderers after a campaign to save the Royal Scots' separate identity.

Battalions

The regular battalions are numbered 1 to 5 SCOTS (with the Balaklava Company as a ceremonial increment), and the 6th and 7th Battalions are Army Reserve. Soldiers wear the Tam o' Shanter with the regiment's cap badge, and each battalion retains distinctive tartan and hackle traditions from its antecedents.
