Mika Pauli Häkkinen (born 28 September 1968) is a Finnish former racing driver who won the Formula One World Drivers' Championship in 1998 and 1999 with McLaren. Known as "The Flying Finn", he was one of Michael Schumacher's principal rivals at the turn of the century.

Career

Häkkinen debuted with Lotus in 1991 and joined McLaren in 1993, famously out-qualifying Ayrton Senna in his first race for the team. A near-fatal crash in practice for the 1995 Australian Grand Prix left him in a coma; he recovered to win his first Grand Prix at Jerez in 1997. With the Adrian Newey-designed McLarens of 1998 and 1999 he took back-to-back titles, winning 20 races in all before retiring at the end of 2001. He later raced in the DTM and became a driver manager and pundit.

Style

Häkkinen was noted for his calm demeanour, late-braking overtaking, and famously monosyllabic press conferences.
