The Producers Guild of America Awards (often called the PGA Awards) are annual accolades presented by the Producers Guild of America in recognition of outstanding achievements by producers in film and television. The awards were originally established in 1990 as the Golden Laurel Awards.

The top film honour is the Darryl F. Zanuck Award for Outstanding Producer of Theatrical Motion Pictures. Because the PGA's membership and voting methods resemble those of the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences, the Zanuck Award winner has frequently gone on to win the Academy Award for Best Picture, making the PGA Awards one of the most closely watched precursors in the awards season. The Guild was also an early adopter of the preferential ballot for its top category.

Categories

Major categories include the Darryl F. Zanuck Award (theatrical film), the Award for Outstanding Producer of Animated Theatrical Motion Pictures, the Norman Felton Award (episodic television drama), the Danny Thomas Award (episodic television comedy), the David L. Wolper Award (limited series), and awards for documentary, non-fiction, live entertainment, and streamed/televised films. Honorary awards include the Milestone Award, the Norman Lear Achievement Award in Television, and the David O. Selznick Achievement Award in Theatrical Motion Pictures.
