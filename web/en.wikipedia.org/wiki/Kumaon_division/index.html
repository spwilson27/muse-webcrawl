Kumaon is one of the two administrative divisions of the Indian state of Uttarakhand, the other being Garhwal. It comprises six Himalayan and foothill districts in the eastern half of the state, bordering Tibet and Nepal.

Geography

The region is dominated by the Kumaon Himalayas, including Nanda Devi, India's second-highest peak. Hill stations such as Nainital, Almora and Ranikhet overlook deep river valleys including the Kali (Sharda) river along the Nepal border.

People and history

The Kumaoni people speak the Kumaoni language. The region was ruled by the Katyuri and Chand dynasties before coming under Gorkha and then British rule, when it joined the North-Western Provinces after 1816. Jim Corbett National Park, India's first national park, lies in its southern foothills.
