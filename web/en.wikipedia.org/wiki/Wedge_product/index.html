Wedge product

In mathematics, the wedge product (or exterior product) is the multiplication operation of the exterior algebra. For vectors u and v, the wedge product u ∧ v is a bivector representing the oriented parallelogram spanned by u and v; it is antisymmetric, so v ∧ u = −u ∧ v and u ∧ u = 0.

Properties

The wedge product is bilinear and associative, and satisfies the graded-commutativity rule α ∧ β = (−1)pq β ∧ α for a p-form α and q-form β. In three dimensions, the magnitude of u ∧ v equals |u||v| sin θ, the area of the spanned parallelogram, paralleling the cross product, which can be recovered from the wedge product via the Hodge star operator.

Applications

Wedge products underlie the theory of differential forms, determinants (the wedge of n one-forms dual to a basis gives the volume form), and modern formulations of electromagnetism and integration on manifolds.
