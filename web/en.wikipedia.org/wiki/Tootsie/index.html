Tootsie is a 1982 American romantic comedy film directed by Sydney Pollack and starring Dustin Hoffman as Michael Dorsey, a perfectionist actor so difficult to work with that no one will hire him. Desperate for work, he disguises himself as a woman, "Dorothy Michaels", and lands a leading role on a television soap opera — becoming a star while falling in love with his co-star, played by Jessica Lange, who does not know his secret.

Reception and legacy

The film was a major commercial and critical success and received ten nominations at the 55th Academy Awards, with Jessica Lange winning Best Supporting Actress. It also featured early screen appearances by Bill Murray and Geena Davis. In 1998 the film was selected for preservation in the United States National Film Registry, and the American Film Institute ranked it among the greatest American comedies. A stage musical adaptation opened on Broadway in 2019.
