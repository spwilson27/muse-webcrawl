Boat

A boat is a watercraft of a large range of types and sizes, but generally smaller than a ship. Boats have been used for transport, fishing, warfare, sport and leisure since prehistoric times; the oldest recovered boat, the Pesse canoe, dates to around 8000 BC.

Types

Boats are categorised by propulsion (oars, sails, engines), hull design (displacement, planing, catamaran), and purpose. Common types include dinghies, canoes, kayaks, rowing boats, narrowboats, lifeboats, fishing boats and yachts.

Propulsion

Human power, wind and internal combustion or electric motors are the main means of propulsion. Buoyancy, explained by Archimedes' principle, keeps a boat afloat. See also Sailing and Watercraft.
