Half-Life (series)

Half-Life
Genre: First-person shooter
Developer: Valve
First release: Half-Life (1998)
Latest release: Half-Life: Alyx (2020)
Protagonists: Gordon Freeman, Alyx Vance

Half-Life is a series of first-person shooter video games developed and published by Valve. The games combine shooting, puzzles and storytelling told entirely from the player's perspective, without cutscenes. The series follows theoretical physicist Gordon Freeman and, in its most recent entry, resistance fighter Alyx Vance, in their struggle first against creatures from another dimension and then against the alien Combine empire that conquers Earth.

Games

The original Half-Life (1998) was Valve's debut title. Set at the Black Mesa Research Facility in New Mexico, it opens with an experiment that triggers a "resonance cascade", tearing open portals to the border world Xen. It was acclaimed for its seamless narrative and artificial intelligence, and produced expansions by Gearbox Software — Opposing Force (1999) and Blue Shift (2001) — as well as the co-operative mod lineage that led to Counter-Strike and Team Fortress.

Half-Life 2 (2004) moved the action to the dystopian City 17, ruled by the Combine through the puppet administrator Dr Wallace Breen. Its episodic sequels, Episode One (2006) and Episode Two (2007), continued the story but ended on a cliffhanger; a planned third episode never appeared, and the fate of the series became a long-running topic of speculation and humour among fans.

In 2020 Valve released Half-Life: Alyx, a virtual-reality game set between the first two games and the first new mainline entry in thirteen years. It was widely praised as a landmark VR title and ended with a scene that retconned the close of Episode Two, reopening the series' story.

Setting and characters

Recurring elements include the crowbar-wielding Freeman, the enigmatic "G-Man" who hires and observes him, the Vortigaunts, the resistance scientist Eli Vance and his daughter Alyx, and the lambda symbol of the resistance. The games never explain events directly; the story is assembled from environments, dialogue and background detail, an approach influential across the medium.

Legacy

The series has sold tens of millions of copies and is repeatedly listed among the greatest video game franchises. Its engines — GoldSrc and Source — powered numerous Valve and third-party games, and its modding community produced Counter-Strike, Day of Defeat and Garry's Mod. The extended absence of a numbered sequel turned the phrase "Half-Life 3" into a byword for eagerly awaited, long-delayed works.
