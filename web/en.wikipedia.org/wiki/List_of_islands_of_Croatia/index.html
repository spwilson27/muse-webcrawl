List of islands of Croatia

The Adriatic Sea coast of Croatia includes more than a thousand islands, islets and reefs. According to the Hydrographic Institute of the Republic of Croatia, there are 1,246 islands, islets, rocks and reefs, of which 79 are classified as islands. This article lists the largest islands of Croatia.

The Kornati archipelago off the Dalmatian coast.
