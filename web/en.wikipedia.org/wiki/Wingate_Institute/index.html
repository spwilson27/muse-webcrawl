Wingate Institute

The Wingate Institute (Hebrew: מכון וינגייט), officially the Orde Wingate Institute for Physical Education and Sports, is a sports training facility located south of Netanya, Israel. It serves as the national institute for physical education and sport in Israel and is the training base for many of the country's Olympic athletes and national teams.

History

The institute was founded in 1957 and named in honour of Major General Orde Wingate, a British officer who trained Jewish special forces units in Mandatory Palestine during the 1930s. The institute was established with the aim of promoting physical education, training coaches and instructors, and developing elite sport in the young state of Israel.

Facilities and activities

The campus includes stadiums, gymnasiums, swimming pools, sports medicine and sports science facilities, and a boarding school for gifted young athletes. It houses the Israel Olympic Committee's training infrastructure, national sports federations, and the Zinman College of Physical Education and Sport Sciences, which offers academic degrees in physical education.
