Na Píobairí Uilleann

Na Píobairí Uilleann (Irish for "The Uilleann Pipers") is a non-profit organisation founded in 1968 in Dublin, Ireland, to promote the uilleann pipes, the characteristic national bagpipe of Ireland, and their music.

Activities

The organisation runs classes, publishes recordings and tutor materials, maintains an archive of piping recordings and documents, and supports pipe makers. It played a central role in the revival of the instrument in the twentieth century.

Recognition

Uilleann piping was inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2017, a campaign in which Na Píobairí Uilleann was closely involved.
