"Papa's Got a Brand New Bag" is a song written and recorded by James Brown. Released as a two-part single in 1965, it was Brown's first song to reach the top ten of the Billboard Hot 100, peaking at No. 8, and it won Brown his first Grammy Award, for Best Rhythm & Blues Recording. Critics and historians regard it as one of the founding recordings of funk music.

Recording and style

Recorded in about an hour at the end of a session, the song was originally much longer and was cut into two parts for single release. Its innovation is rhythmic: the band's emphasis shifts decisively onto the first beat of the bar — "on the one" — with Jimmy Nolen's choked, syncopated guitar, the horn section's stabbing riffs and Maceo Parker's saxophone solo riding over a stripped-down groove. The lyric describes an older man newly confident on the dance floor, doing dances like the Jerk and the Mashed Potato.

Legacy

Rolling Stone ranked it among the 500 greatest songs of all time, and it is preserved in the Grammy Hall of Fame. Its groove laid the template Brown himself developed in "I Got You (I Feel Good)", "Cold Sweat" and the funk of the late 1960s, influencing everyone from Sly Stone to Prince and hip-hop producers who sampled it.
