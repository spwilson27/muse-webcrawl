The Munsters is an American sitcom about a family of friendly monsters who live at 1313 Mockingbird Lane in the fictional suburb of Mockingbird Heights. It aired on CBS from 1964 to 1966, running concurrently with ABC's similar The Addams Family.

Characters

  • Herman Munster (Fred Gwynne) — the Frankenstein's-monster-like, childlike head of the family, employed at a funeral parlour.
  • Lily Munster (Yvonne De Carlo) — his vampire wife, originally from Transylvania.
  • Grandpa (Al Lewis) — Lily's father, Count Dracula, an eccentric inventor and mad scientist.
  • Eddie Munster (Butch Patrick) — their werewolf-like young son.
  • Marilyn Munster (Beverley Owen, then Pat Priest) — Lily's niece, an attractive, "normal" blonde whom the family consider the unfortunate one.

Premise and legacy

The comedy arises from the family's gentle bewilderment at the ordinary American world around them, which they regard as strange. The series was cancelled after two seasons when ratings fell, partly attributed to competition from the colour series Batman, but it became a lasting success in syndication, spawning the feature film Munster, Go Home! (1966), a 1988–1991 revival (The Munsters Today), the unbroadcast 2012 pilot Mockingbird Lane, and Rob Zombie's 2022 film The Munsters.
