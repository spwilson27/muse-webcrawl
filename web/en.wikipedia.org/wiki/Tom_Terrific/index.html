Tom Terrific is an animated cartoon character created by Gene Deitch under the Terrytoons studio (then owned by CBS). The black-and-white five-minute episodes debuted on the children's television program Captain Kangaroo in 1957, where they were shown in instalments during the week.

Character

Tom Terrific is a young boy who lives in a treehouse and can transform himself into anything he wishes, thanks in part to his magical funnel-shaped hat, which he calls his "thinking funnel". His loyal companion is Mighty Manfred the Wonder Dog, a lazy, sarcastic hound who is often reluctant to join Tom's adventures. Tom's recurring nemesis is the villain Crabby Appleton, whose motto is that he is "rotten to the core".

Production and legacy

The cartoons were produced in a deliberately simple, limited-animation style with minimal backgrounds, which suited both the small budgets of early television animation and the show's fairy-tale tone. Tom's voice, and the voices of most other characters, were performed by Lionel Wilson. Tom Terrific was later ranked among the greatest cartoon characters of all time in magazine polls, remembered for its wit and gentle storytelling.
