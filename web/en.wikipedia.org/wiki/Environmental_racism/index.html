Environmental racism

Environmental racism is the disproportionate exposure of racial and ethnic minority communities to environmental hazards — including toxic waste sites, landfills, incinerators, polluting industry and contaminated water — together with their exclusion from environmental decision-making. The term emerged in the United States in the 1980s and is a central concept of the environmental justice movement.

Origins

The movement is commonly dated to the 1982 protests in Warren County, North Carolina, where a predominantly Black community resisted the siting of a landfill for PCB-contaminated soil; hundreds were arrested. The term itself is credited to civil rights leader Benjamin Chavis. A landmark 1987 report by the United Church of Christ, Toxic Wastes and Race in the United States, found race to be the strongest predictor of the location of commercial hazardous waste facilities — stronger than income.

Evidence and mechanisms

Studies in many countries document that minority and low-income neighbourhoods host more polluting facilities, have higher air pollution exposure, and suffer higher rates of associated illness such as asthma. Mechanisms include discriminatory land-use and zoning decisions, the legacy of segregation and redlining, cheaper land prices, and the weaker political power of affected communities to resist unwanted development — a pattern sometimes summarised as following the "path of least resistance". Globally, analogous patterns appear in the siting of waste exports and extractive industries affecting Indigenous peoples.

Policy responses

In 1994, U.S. President Bill Clinton signed Executive Order 12898 directing federal agencies to address environmental justice in minority and low-income populations. Environmental justice considerations have since been incorporated into permitting, cleanup prioritisation (e.g. Superfund), and screening tools. Critics debate how much of the disparity is attributable to racism per se versus market dynamics after facilities are sited, but the disparate outcomes themselves are well documented.
