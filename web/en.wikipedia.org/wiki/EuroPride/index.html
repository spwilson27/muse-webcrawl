EuroPride

EuroPride is a pan-European international LGBT event, hosted by a different European city each year. The host is chosen by the European Pride Organisers Association (EPOA), which also owns the EuroPride title and licenses it to the local organising team.

History

The first EuroPride was held in London in 1992, attracting an estimated 100,000 participants. Subsequent editions have taken place in cities including Paris, Amsterdam, Berlin, Stockholm, Madrid, Vienna, Copenhagen and Belgrade. Madrid's 2007 edition drew crowds estimated in the millions and is considered one of the largest Pride events held in Europe.

Organisation

Cities bid for the title several years in advance at the EPOA annual general meeting. The event typically runs for one to two weeks and combines a political human-rights conference, cultural programming and a closing parade. In years when WorldPride is held in Europe, that event also carries the EuroPride title.

Notable editions

EuroPride 2022 in Belgrade was the first held in Southeast Europe; despite an announced ban by Serbian authorities, the march went ahead under heavy police protection. EuroPride 2023 was hosted by Valletta, Malta, and the 2025 edition was awarded to Lisbon.
