A Coruña
Country: Spain
Region: Galicia
Population: ~244,000
Known for: Tower of Hercules

A Coruña (Galician: A Coruña; Spanish: La Coruña) is a city and municipality in Galicia, northwestern Spain, and capital of the province of the same name. A major Atlantic port, it is known for the Tower of Hercules, an ancient Roman lighthouse still in use.

History

Settlement dates to pre-Roman times; the Romans built the lighthouse in the 1st-2nd century AD. The Armada sailed from here in 1588, and Sir Francis Drake attacked in 1589. The Battle of Corunna was fought in 1809 during the Peninsular War.

Geography

The city occupies a peninsula on the Golfo Ártabro with beaches such as Riazor and Orzán, a historic old town, and glazed gallery houses called galerías.
