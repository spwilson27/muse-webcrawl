King Power

IndustryDuty-free retail
Founded1989
HeadquartersBangkok, Thailand
Known forOwner of Leicester City F.C.

King Power International Group is a Thai duty-free retail conglomerate founded by Vichai Srivaddhanaprabha in 1989. It operates duty-free shops at major Thai airports including Suvarnabhumi Airport in Bangkok.

Sport

The group bought English football club Leicester City in 2010; the club won the Premier League in 2016. Leicester's stadium is named the King Power Stadium. Vichai died in a helicopter crash outside the stadium in 2018, and leadership passed to his son Aiyawatt Srivaddhanaprabha.
