20th Century Animation, Inc. is an American animation studio and the animation division of 20th Century Studios, a subsidiary of Walt Disney Studios. It is best known as the former parent studio of Blue Sky Studios and for franchises such as Ice Age and Rio.

History

The division began in 1994 as Fox Family Films before becoming Fox Animation Studios, which operated a Phoenix, Arizona studio that produced Anastasia (1997) and Titan A.E. (2000). After the Phoenix studio closed in 2000, the label (renamed 20th Century Fox Animation) focused on overseeing Blue Sky Studios in Connecticut, which it had acquired with the purchase of VIFX.

Following Disney's acquisition of 21st Century Fox in 2019, the studio was renamed 20th Century Animation. Disney closed Blue Sky Studios in 2021, ending the Ice Age franchise's original run, though spin-off productions continued under the 20th Century Animation banner via other units.

Notable productions

  • Ice Age series (2002–2016)
