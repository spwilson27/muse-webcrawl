Catskill High Peaks

The Catskill High Peaks are the mountains in New York's Catskill Mountains with elevations above 3,500 feet (1,067 m). There are 35 such peaks traditionally recognised, all within the Catskill Park. The highest is Slide Mountain at 4,180 feet (1,274 m).

List and geography

Other major peaks include Hunter Mountain (4,040 ft), Black Dome (3,980 ft), Blackhead, Thomas Cole, Kaaterskill High Peak, West Kill, Cornell, Wittenberg, Plateau, Sugarloaf, and Balsam. Most lie in Greene and Ulster counties, with ranges arranged along escarpments above the Hudson Valley.

Hiking

Climbing all 35 peaks is the requirement of the Catskill Mountain 3500 Club. Many summits are trailless or reached by unmarked herd paths; several have canisters maintained by the club for sign-in.
