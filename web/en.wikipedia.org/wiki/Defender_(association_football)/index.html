Defender (association football)

In association football, a defender is an outfield player whose primary role is to stop opposing attacks, protect the goal and support play from the back.

Positions

Common roles include centre-back, full-back, wing-back and sweeper. Defenders are usually numbered 2 to 6 in traditional systems.

Skills

Defenders need tackling, marking, aerial ability, positioning and increasingly passing and ball progression in modern systems.

Formations

Teams typically field three, four or five defenders depending on formation and tactics.
