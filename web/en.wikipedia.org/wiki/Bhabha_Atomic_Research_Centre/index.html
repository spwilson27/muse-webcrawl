The Bhabha Atomic Research Centre (BARC) is India's premier nuclear research facility, headquartered in Trombay, Mumbai, Maharashtra. Established in 1954 by the physicist Homi J. Bhabha as the Atomic Energy Establishment, Trombay, it was renamed in Bhabha's honour after his death in 1966. BARC is a multidisciplinary research centre with extensive infrastructure for advanced research in nuclear science, engineering, and related areas.

Facilities and research

BARC operates several research reactors, including APSARA (1956), the first nuclear reactor in Asia outside the Soviet Union; CIRUS; DHRUVA; and the pool-type reactor APSARA-U. The centre conducts research across nuclear power, fuel reprocessing, waste management, radioisotopes for medicine, agriculture and industry, radiation technology, and basic sciences including physics, chemistry, and materials science. It played a central role in India's nuclear programme, including the development of the devices tested in 1974 and 1998.

Institutions

BARC's Training School, founded in 1957, supplies scientists and engineers to the Department of Atomic Energy, and the centre is the nucleus of a family of research institutions around India, many now under the Homi Bhabha National Institute.
