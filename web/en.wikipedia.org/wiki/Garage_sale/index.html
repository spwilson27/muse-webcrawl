Garage sale

A garage sale (also known as a yard sale, tag sale, rummage sale, or moving sale) is an informal event for the sale of used goods by private individuals, in which sellers are not required to obtain business licenses or collect sales tax (though in some jurisdictions permits may be required). Typically the goods in a garage sale are unwanted items from the household selling them, displayed in a garage, driveway, or front yard.

Description

Garage sales are advertised through signs posted in the neighbourhood, local newspapers, and increasingly through websites and social media. Prices are usually low and negotiable; haggling is customary. Items commonly sold include clothing, furniture, toys, books, tools, kitchenware, and household appliances.

Garage sales usually occur on weekends during warmer months. Some communities organise neighbourhood-wide or town-wide sale days, and multi-family sales are common. "Early birds" who arrive before the posted start time are a familiar feature of sale culture.

Regional names

  • Yard sale — common in much of the United States.
  • Tag sale — used in parts of New England and the New York area, referring to price tags on items.
  • Rummage sale — often used for sales organised by churches or charities, where donated goods are sold.
  • Car boot sale — a related British format in which sellers gather in a field or car park and sell from their car boots.
  • Moving sale — held when a household is relocating and sells a large share of its possessions.
