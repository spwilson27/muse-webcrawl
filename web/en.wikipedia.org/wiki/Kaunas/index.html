Overview

Kaunas is the second-largest city in Lithuania and a major industrial, academic and cultural centre, standing at the confluence of the Nemunas and Neris rivers.

History

Kaunas was the temporary capital of interwar Lithuania (1919–1940) while Vilnius was held by Poland, and its interwar modernist architecture was inscribed on the UNESCO list in 2023. The Ninth Fort is a memorial to victims of Nazi massacres, including Jews of the Kaunas Ghetto; the Japanese diplomat Chiune Sugihara issued life-saving transit visas from Kaunas in 1940. Kaunas was a European Capital of Culture in 2022. Its basketball club, Žalgiris, is a national institution.
