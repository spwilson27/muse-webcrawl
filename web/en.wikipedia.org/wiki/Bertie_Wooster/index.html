Bertram Wilberforce "Bertie" Wooster is a fictional character in the comedic Jeeves stories of P. G. Wodehouse. A wealthy, good-natured but dim-witted young English gentleman of the interwar era, Bertie is the narrator of most of the stories, in which he is repeatedly rescued from scrapes — usually unwanted engagements and the schemes of his formidable aunts — by his supremely capable valet, Jeeves.

Character

Bertie was educated at Eton and Magdalen College, Oxford, where he won a prize for Scripture knowledge. He is a member of the Drones Club and lives largely in London and at the country houses of friends and relations. Though not unintelligent in his narration — his prose is fluent, allusive, and witty — he is hopelessly impractical, and his well-meaning attempts to help friends invariably entangle him in complications.

The stories

Bertie appears in ten novels and numerous short stories, from "Extricating Young Gussie" (1915) to Aunts Aren't Gentlemen (1974). Recurring figures include his Aunt Dahlia, proprietor of Milady's Boudoir; his terrifying Aunt Agatha; and friends such as Gussie Fink-Nottle and Bingo Little. In the ITV series Jeeves and Wooster (1990–1993), Bertie was played by Hugh Laurie.
