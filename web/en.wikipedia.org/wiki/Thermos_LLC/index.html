Thermos LLC

Thermos LLC
Founded: 1904
Products: Vacuum flasks, insulated food and drink containers
Owner: Taiyo Nippon Sanso Corporation

Thermos LLC is an American manufacturer of insulated food and beverage containers and other consumer products. The company is a subsidiary of Taiyo Nippon Sanso Corporation of Japan. Its name became so closely associated with vacuum flasks that "thermos" is widely used as a generic term for such containers in many countries.

History

The vacuum flask was invented by the Scottish scientist Sir James Dewar in 1892. In 1904, German glassblower Reinhold Burger and Albert Aschenbrenner founded Thermos GmbH to commercialise the invention, choosing the name "Thermos" (from the Greek therme, heat) in a naming contest. The American Thermos Bottle Company was established in 1907, and the brand grew rapidly as the flasks became standard equipment for workers, travellers, and explorers.

Genericised trademark

In a landmark 1963 United States court decision, "thermos" was declared a genericised trademark in the US, meaning competitors could use the word generically under certain conditions, although Thermos retains trademark rights in many other jurisdictions.

Products

The company's products include vacuum-insulated bottles and flasks, lunch boxes, food jars, coolers, and children's drinkware sold under the Thermos brand.
