Monkey D. Luffy (Japanese: モンキー・D・ルフィ) is the protagonist of the manga series One Piece, created by Eiichiro Oda. Luffy made his debut in the first chapter of One Piece, published in Weekly Shōnen Jump on July 22, 1997. He is the founder and captain of the Straw Hat Pirates and dreams of becoming the Pirate King by finding the legendary treasure known as the "One Piece".

Creation and conception

Oda designed Luffy as a cheerful, carefree young man whose body gained the properties of rubber after he ate a Devil Fruit. His signature straw hat was given to him by the pirate "Red-Haired" Shanks, who inspired Luffy to become a pirate. Luffy's character was designed to embody simplicity and a straightforward desire for freedom and adventure.

Appearances

In One Piece

Luffy sets out from his home in the East Blue sea to gather a crew and cross the Grand Line in search of the One Piece. Along the way he recruits swordsman Roronoa Zoro, navigator Nami, marksman Usopp, cook Sanji, doctor Tony Tony Chopper, archaeologist Nico Robin, shipwright Franky, musician Brook, and helmsman Jinbe. His victories over powerful pirates and World Government forces earn him an ever-increasing bounty, and after the events at Wano Country he is recognised as one of the Four Emperors of the sea.

It is later revealed that Luffy's Devil Fruit, believed to be the Paramecia-type Gomu Gomu no Mi, is in fact the Mythical Zoan-type Hito Hito no Mi, Model: Nika, which allows him to awaken the form known as "Gear Fifth".

In other media

Luffy appears in the One Piece anime adaptation, films, video games, and the 2023 Netflix live-action series, in which he is portrayed by Iñaki Godoy.

Reception

Luffy is consistently ranked among the most popular manga and anime characters of all time, frequently placing first in One Piece popularity polls. Critics have praised his cheerful personality and the emotional weight of his determination to protect his friends.
