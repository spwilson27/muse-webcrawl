Janet Gaynor

Janet Gaynor (born Laura Augusta Gainor; 6 October 1906 – 14 September 1984) was an American film actress and artist, the first winner of the Academy Award for Best Actress and one of the biggest box-office stars of late silent and early sound cinema.

Career

Gaynor won the first Best Actress Oscar for three roles — 7th Heaven, Street Angel, and F. W. Murnau's Sunrise — a unique combined award for the 1927/28 season. Frequently paired with Charles Farrell, she remained a top star into the 1930s, earning another Oscar nomination for A Star Is Born (1937).

Later life

She retired from film in 1939, married costume designer Adrian, and later pursued a successful career as a painter. She died in 1984 from complications following injuries sustained in a 1982 taxi accident.
