Senegambia Confederation

Years1982–1989
MembersSenegal, The Gambia
Capital seatsDakar / Banjul

Senegambia was a loose confederation between Senegal and The Gambia, in effect from 1 February 1982 to 30 September 1989, intended as a step towards integration between the Francophone Senegal and Anglophone Gambia, which is almost entirely surrounded by Senegal.

Formation

The pact followed Senegalese intervention to foil a 1981 coup attempt against Gambian President Dawda Kairaba Jawara. It created a confederal president (Senegal's Abdou Diouf) and vice-president (Jawara), a union parliament and coordination on security, economy and foreign policy, while each state retained sovereignty.

Dissolution

Progress stalled over customs, rotation of offices and Gambian fears of domination; Senegal withdrew troops and the confederation was dissolved in 1989.
