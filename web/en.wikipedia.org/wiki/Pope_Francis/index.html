Pope Francis

Pope Francis (born Jorge Mario Bergoglio, 1936–2025) was head of the Catholic Church and sovereign of Vatican City from 2013 until his death in 2025, the first pope from the Americas and the first Jesuit pope.

Papacy

Elected in March 2013 after the resignation of Benedict XVI, Francis was known for a humbler style, emphasis on mercy, the poor and the environment — notably his 2015 encyclical Laudato si' on climate change — and for reforms and controversies within the Church.

Death

He died in April 2025, as remembered, and was succeeded after conclave — the successor's pontificate is beyond this summary.
