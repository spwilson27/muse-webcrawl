Virgin America Inc. was a low-cost airline headquartered in Burlingame, California, in the San Francisco Bay Area. Primarily a point-to-point service with operations concentrated in a handful of focus cities, particularly in the West Coast of the United States, the airline flew mainly to larger metropolitan areas along the West Coast, East Coast, Midwest, and Mexico. It was founded in 2004 as a U.S.-based airline of the British Virgin Group, though the group held only a minority stake to comply with U.S. foreign-ownership limits on airlines. The airline won numerous "best domestic airline" awards from travel publications for its service, which featured mood lighting, seatback entertainment (Red), and leather seats in all cabins.

History

Virgin America was established in 2004, with plans to launch a U.S. low-fare airline bearing the Virgin brand, led by former Delta executive Fred Reid. Certification by the U.S. Department of Transportation was contested by rival carriers and labor groups over foreign control; after restructuring its ownership and management, the airline received final approval and began service on August 8, 2007, with flights from New York JFK and Los Angeles to its San Francisco base.

The airline grew through the 2010s, adding destinations such as Boston, Chicago O'Hare, Dallas/Fort Worth (later Dallas Love Field), Seattle, Denver, and leisure destinations in Mexico and Hawaii. Its loyalty program was called Elevate. Virgin America went public in November 2014 (NASDAQ: VA) and reported its first full-year profits shortly thereafter.

Acquisition by Alaska Airlines

In April 2016, Alaska Air Group announced it would acquire Virgin America for approximately $2.6 billion, outbidding JetBlue. The acquisition closed on December 14, 2016. Alaska initially maintained the Virgin America brand while studying customer preferences, but in 2017 announced that the Virgin America brand would be retired, with operations combined under a single operating certificate. The last Virgin America flight operated on April 24, 2018, after which the fleet, crews, and routes were integrated into Alaska Airlines, and the Airbus fleet was gradually retained, reconfigured, or retired.

Onboard product

Virgin America was noted for its distinctive cabins: purple-pink mood lighting, black leather seats, seatback touch-screen entertainment with live satellite television, movies, games, music, and food ordering, as well as in-seat power and (later) Wi-Fi. The airline offered a First Class cabin with recliner seats and complimentary meals, a premium-economy "Main Cabin Select," and a standard Main Cabin.
