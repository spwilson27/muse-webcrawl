List of aqueducts in the Roman Empire

The Romans built aqueducts throughout their Republic and Empire to bring water from distant springs and rivers into cities, supplying public baths, latrines, fountains and private households. Most aqueduct channels ran underground; the famous arched bridges were only a small part of each system.
