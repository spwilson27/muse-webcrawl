Castelo Branco District

Castelo Branco District lies in east-central Portugal, bordering Spain.

The District of Castelo Branco (Portuguese: Distrito de Castelo Branco) is a district in the Centro Region of Portugal. Its capital is the city of Castelo Branco. The district borders Spain to the east, the districts of Guarda to the north, Coimbra and Leiria to the west, and Portalegre and Santarém to the south.

Geography and municipalities

The district covers about 6,600 square kilometres of interior territory crossed by the Tagus River and backed by mountain ranges including the Serra da Estrela foothills and Serra da Gardunha. It comprises 11 municipalities, among them Castelo Branco, Covilhã, Fundão, Belmonte, Idanha-a-Nova, and Penamacor. Covilhã, at the foot of the Serra da Estrela, is a university city historically associated with wool textiles.

Population and economy

The district is one of Portugal’s least densely populated, with under 200,000 inhabitants and long-standing trends of ageing and rural depopulation. Agriculture, forestry, food processing, and tourism—including the historic villages network around Monsanto and Idanha—are important to the local economy.
