BBC iPlayer is a video on demand service from the BBC, launched on 25 December 2007. It allows UK viewers to stream BBC television programmes after broadcast and, increasingly, to watch box sets and live channels, on computers, televisions, tablets and phones.

History

iPlayer grew out of earlier BBC experiments with downloadable radio and the integrated Media Player. Its launch followed approval by the BBC Trust after a public value test. Programmes were initially available for seven days after broadcast; the window was later extended to 30 days and then, for many series, to a year or more.

Features

The service offers catch-up TV, live streaming of BBC channels, downloads for offline viewing, subtitles and audio description. A television licence is required to use it. An international version operated in some countries between 2011 and 2015.
