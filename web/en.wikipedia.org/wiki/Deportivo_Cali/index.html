Deportivo Cali

Asociación Deportivo Cali, commonly known as Deportivo Cali, is a Colombian professional football club based in Cali, Valle del Cauca. Founded in 1912, it is one of Colombia's oldest and most successful clubs, playing in green and white and nicknamed Los Azucareros ("the Sugar Makers") for the region's sugar industry, and El Glorioso.

History

The club was founded on 23 November 1912 by students returning from England, initially as Cali Football Club. Deportivo Cali has won the Colombian league championship (Categoría Primera A) ten times, most recently the 2021 Finalización tournament, along with the Copa Colombia (2010) and the Superliga (2014). Internationally, the club was runner-up in the Copa Libertadores in 1978 and 1999 — the 1978 side, coached by Carlos Bilardo, was the first Colombian team to reach the final — and in the Copa Merconorte in 1998.

Stadium

Since 2010 the club has played at the Estadio Deportivo Cali in Palmira, one of the largest stadiums in Colombia, having previously shared the Estadio Pascual Guerrero with its fierce city rival América de Cali, against whom it contests the Clásico Vallecaucano. The club is also noted for its youth academy, which has produced many Colombian internationals, and for its member-owned (asociación) structure.
