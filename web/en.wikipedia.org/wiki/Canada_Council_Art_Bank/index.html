Canada Council Art Bank

The Canada Council Art Bank is a programme of the Canada Council for the Arts that purchases works by contemporary Canadian artists and rents them to government departments, public institutions and private-sector clients. Established in 1972, it holds one of the largest collections of contemporary Canadian art, with more than 17,000 paintings, sculptures, prints, photographs and mixed-media works.

History

The Art Bank was created to support living Canadian artists by buying their work and to bring contemporary art into public spaces. Its collection includes works by many of Canada's most prominent artists, acquired through juried purchase programmes over five decades.

Operations

Works are rented for a fee, typically for display in offices and public buildings, generating revenue that helps fund new acquisitions and operating costs. The Art Bank is based in Ottawa and also lends works for exhibitions and provides art-consulting services to clients across Canada.
