Major Arcana

The Major Arcana are the named or numbered suit of cards in a cartomantic tarot pack — the twenty-two trump cards of the standard 78-card deck. The name was devised by occultists and is rarely used by tarot game players. In games they are permanent trumps that outrank all cards of the plain suits.

The cards

The standard Major Arcana run from 0 (The Fool) to XXI (The World): The Fool, The Magician, The High Priestess, The Empress, The Emperor, The Hierophant, The Lovers, The Chariot, Strength, The Hermit, Wheel of Fortune, Justice, The Hanged Man, Death, Temperance, The Devil, The Tower, The Star, The Moon, The Sun, Judgement and The World. (In some decks Justice and Strength swap numbers VIII and XI.)

Use in divination

Since the eighteenth century, occultists from Court de Gébelin and Etteilla onward have used the Major Arcana in cartomancy, attaching elaborate symbolic and esoteric interpretations to each card; they form the most symbolically rich part of modern tarot reading.
