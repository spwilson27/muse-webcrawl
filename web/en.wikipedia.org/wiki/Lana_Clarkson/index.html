Lana Jean Clarkson (April 5, 1962 – February 3, 2003) was an American actress and fashion model. She became known for her roles in sword-and-sorcery films of the 1980s, most notably as the title character in Barbarian Queen (1985), produced by Roger Corman. She also appeared in films including Deathstalker (1983), Wizards of the Lost Kingdom (1985), Barbarian Queen II: The Empress Strikes Back (1990), and made guest appearances on television series such as Three's Company, The A-Team, Knight Rider and Silk Stalkings.

Career

Born in Long Beach and raised in Cloverdale, California, Clarkson began modelling as a teenager and moved into acting in the early 1980s. Her statuesque presence made her a cult figure in low-budget genre cinema, and she remained a popular guest at fan conventions. By the early 2000s she was working as a hostess at the House of Blues in West Hollywood while continuing to pursue acting work and performing stand-up comedy.

Death

On February 3, 2003, Clarkson was found dead of a gunshot wound at the Alhambra mansion of record producer Phil Spector, whom she had met that night at the House of Blues. Spector was tried for murder; his first trial in 2007 ended in a hung jury, and at a retrial in 2009 he was convicted of second-degree murder and sentenced to 19 years to life in prison. He died in prison in 2021.
