Chandravanshi

Chandravanshi (Sanskrit: चन्द्रवंशी, "descendant of the Moon") refers to the Lunar dynasty, one of the two great lineages of warriors and kings in Hindu tradition, the other being the Suryavanshi or Solar dynasty. The dynasty is said to descend from Chandra, the moon god, through his son Budha and the king Pururavas.

Mythology

According to the Puranas and epics, the Chandravanshi line includes Yayati, whose descendants founded branches such as the Yadavas, to which Krishna belongs, and the line of Puru, from which the Kuru kings of the Mahabharata descend, including the Pandavas and Kauravas.

Historical claims

Numerous historical royal houses and communities in India have claimed Chandravanshi descent, and the term is also used today by certain caste groups, particularly among Rajput and other communities in northern India.
