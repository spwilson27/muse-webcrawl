News Corp (officially News Corporation) is an American mass-media and publishing company created in 2013 as the publishing spin-off of Rupert Murdoch's former News Corporation. Headquartered in New York, it owns The Wall Street Journal and Dow Jones, the New York Post, The Times and The Sun in Britain (via News UK), major Australian papers, HarperCollins publishers and real-estate and book interests.

The 2013 split

After the phone-hacking scandal and investor pressure, the old News Corporation split in 2013: entertainment assets went to 21st Century Fox, publishing and newspapers to the new News Corp, with Murdoch chairing both.

Holdings

Dow Jones (Journal, Barron's, MarketWatch), News UK (Times, Sunday Times, Sun), News Corp Australia (The Australian, Daily Telegraph of Sydney and others), the New York Post, HarperCollins, and stakes in real-estate listings (REA Group, Move/Realtor.com).

Leadership

Robert Thomson has been chief executive since 2013; Rupert Murdoch became chairman emeritus in 2023 as Lachlan Murdoch took the chair. A proposed re-merger with Fox Corporation was explored in 2022-23 and abandoned.
