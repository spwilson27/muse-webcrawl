List of Florida hurricanes

This is a list of Atlantic hurricanes that have struck Florida, the U.S. state most frequently hit by tropical cyclones. Since records began in 1851, hundreds of tropical storms and hurricanes have affected Florida, more than any other state. The list is organized by era and covers storms that made landfall in Florida or produced significant effects there.

Climatology

Florida's long, exposed peninsula between the Atlantic Ocean and the Gulf of Mexico makes it vulnerable from both basins. The peak of the season is from mid-August to mid-October. Long "hurricane droughts" in major-hurricane landfalls, such as 2006–2016, are interspersed with destructive clusters such as the 2004–05 seasons.
