Las Vegas Sands

Type: Public
Industry: Hospitality, gaming
Founded: 1988
Founder: Sheldon Adelson
Headquarters: Las Vegas, Nevada, United States

Las Vegas Sands Corp. is an American casino and resort company based in Las Vegas, Nevada. It is best known for developing integrated resorts combining casinos, hotels, convention space, retail and entertainment.

History

The company traces its origins to the Sands Hotel and Casino purchased by Sheldon Adelson and partners in 1988. The old Sands was demolished and replaced by The Venetian, opened in 1999, an all-suite resort themed on Venice with canals and gondolas. The Palazzo opened adjacent in 2007.

In Asia, the company developed major properties in Macau through Sands China, including Sands Macao (2004), The Venetian Macao (2007) on the Cotai Strip, The Parisian Macao and The Londoner Macao. In Singapore it built Marina Bay Sands, opened in 2010, recognizable by its three towers topped by a boat-shaped SkyPark.

Sale of Las Vegas properties

After Adelson's death in 2021, the company sold its Las Vegas assets — The Venetian, Palazzo and the Sands Expo — to Apollo Global Management and Vici Properties in a deal completed in 2022, focusing thereafter on its Asian resorts.
