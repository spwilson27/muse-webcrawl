English Triple Crown race winners

The English Triple Crown is a series of three classic horse races for three-year-old thoroughbreds: the 2000 Guineas Stakes at Newmarket (1 mile), the Epsom Derby (1 mile 4 furlongs) and the St Leger Stakes at Doncaster (1 mile 6½ furlongs). A colt or filly who wins all three in a single season is said to have won the Triple Crown. Only fifteen horses have achieved the feat since West Australian in 1853, and none since Nijinsky in 1970 — the longest drought in the series' history.

Fillies' Triple Crown

The fillies' equivalent substitutes the 1000 Guineas and the Epsom Oaks for the Derby. Nine fillies have won it, most recently Oh So Sharp in 1985. Earlier winners include Formosa (1868), Hannah (1871), Apology (1874), La Fleche (1892), Sceptre (1902 — who also won the 2000 Guineas), Pretty Polly (1904), Sun Chariot (1942) and Meld (1955).

Near misses

Several great horses won two legs and were beaten in the third, among them Sea the Stars, who in 2009 won the 2000 Guineas and Derby but was not entered for the St Leger, and Camelot (2012), who finished second in the St Leger after winning the first two legs. The modern tendency to specialise by distance and to target international races has made attempts on the Crown increasingly rare.
