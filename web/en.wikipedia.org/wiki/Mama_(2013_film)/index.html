Mama is a 2013 supernatural horror film directed and co-written by Andy Muschietti, based on his 2008 Argentine short film Mamá. The film stars Jessica Chastain and Nikolaj Coster-Waldau, and was presented by Guillermo del Toro, who served as an executive producer.

Plot

After a financial crash, Jeffrey Desange kills his business partners and his wife before fleeing with his young daughters, Victoria and Lilly. The family crashes in the woods, and in an abandoned cabin Jeffrey is killed by a shadowy figure — a ghost the girls come to call "Mama", who raises them in the wild for five years.

When the girls are found, feral and traumatised, they are placed in the care of their uncle Lucas, Jeffrey's twin brother, and his girlfriend Annabel, a rock musician. Mama follows the girls to their new home, becoming violently possessive as Annabel grows closer to them. Research reveals Mama to be Edith Brennan, a 19th-century asylum patient whose baby was taken from her and who died searching for the child. In the climax, Annabel returns the remains of Mama's actual baby, and the ghost departs with Lilly, who chooses to remain with her.

Reception

The film was a commercial success, grossing over $146 million worldwide against a budget of about $15 million. Critics praised Chastain's performance and the film's atmosphere and emotional core, while some faulted its reliance on jump scares and its ending.
