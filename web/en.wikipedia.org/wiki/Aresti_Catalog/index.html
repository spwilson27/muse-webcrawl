The Aresti Catalog is the internationally adopted system of notation for aerobatic maneuvers used in aerobatic competition and judging. Devised by Spanish aviator José Luis Aresti Aguirre and first published in 1964, the catalog assigns standardized pictorial symbols and numeric difficulty coefficients (K-factors) to aerobatic figures, allowing competition sequences to be drawn, communicated, and scored uniformly worldwide.

System

Each maneuver family — lines, loops, rolls, spins, hammerheads, and combinations — is represented by a diagrammatic symbol drawn as seen by an observer, showing the flight path, rotations, and direction. Every figure receives a catalog number and a K-factor reflecting its difficulty; the total K of a sequence contributes to the competitor's score. The catalog is maintained under the authority of the Commission Internationale de Voltige Aérienne (CIVA) of the Fédération Aéronautique Internationale.

Use

Pilots submit sequences drawn in Aresti notation on standardized forms (Forms A, B, and C) used by judges during competition flights. Software tools allow competitors to design and print sequences from the catalog's figure library.
