Club Internacional de Fútbol Miami, known as Inter Miami CF or simply Inter Miami, is an American professional soccer club based in the Miami metropolitan area. The club competes in Major League Soccer (MLS) as a member of the Eastern Conference. Founded through an expansion bid led by former England captain David Beckham, the club began play in 2020.

History

Beckham's 2007 contract with the LA Galaxy included an option to buy an MLS expansion team at a discount, which he exercised in 2014. After years of stadium-site struggles in Miami, MLS officially awarded the team in January 2018. Inter Miami played its first match in March 2020 and initially hosted games in Fort Lauderdale while pursuing a permanent stadium at Miami Freedom Park. The club's colours are pink and black, and its crest features two great white herons.

The Messi era

In July 2023 Inter Miami signed Lionel Messi, widely regarded as the greatest player of all time, in a deal that transformed the club's global profile and MLS's economics. Messi was joined by former Barcelona teammates Sergio Busquets, Jordi Alba and Luis Suárez. Within weeks of his arrival, Miami won the 2023 Leagues Cup — the first trophy in club history — and in 2024 it won the Supporters' Shield with a league-record points total. Former Argentina midfielder Javier Mascherano became head coach ahead of the 2025 season, when the club also competed in the expanded FIFA Club World Cup.
