Richard Dawson

Richard Dawson
Born: Colin Lionel Emm, 20 November 1932, Gosport, England
Died: 2 June 2012, Los Angeles, California
Occupations: Actor, comedian, game show host
Known for: Hogan's Heroes; Family Feud

Richard Dawson (born Colin Lionel Emm; 20 November 1932 – 2 June 2012) was an English-American actor, comedian and game show host. He played Corporal Peter Newkirk in the sitcom Hogan's Heroes (1965–71) and became a television institution as the original host of the game show Family Feud.

Early life and career

Born in Gosport, Hampshire, Dawson ran away at 14 to join the merchant navy, later boxed and worked as a waiter before entering stand-up comedy in London clubs. Moving to Los Angeles in the early 1960s, he took small television roles before landing Newkirk on Hogan's Heroes. In the 1970s he was a regular panellist on Match Game, known for his sharp wit.

Family Feud

Dawson hosted Family Feud from its debut in 1976 to 1985, winning a Daytime Emmy Award in 1978. His trademark habit of kissing female contestants — he estimated some 20,000 kisses — and his catchphrase delivery made the show a ratings phenomenon. He returned briefly to host a revived season in 1994–95.

Film and later life

He played the villainous game show host Damon Killian in the 1987 film The Running Man opposite Arnold Schwarzenegger. Dawson largely retired from public life afterwards, and died in Los Angeles in 2012 of complications from oesophageal cancer, aged 79.
