Deutsche Bahn AG (DB) is the national railway company of Germany, headquartered at the Bahntower in Berlin. It was formed in 1994 by the merger of the Deutsche Bundesbahn of West Germany and the Deutsche Reichsbahn of East Germany.

Operations

DB is one of the world's largest transport and logistics companies, operating long-distance services under DB Fernverkehr (including ICE high-speed trains), regional services under DB Regio, freight through DB Cargo, and infrastructure through DB InfraGO.
