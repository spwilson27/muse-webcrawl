Mark Ruffalo

Mark Alan Ruffalo (born November 22, 1967) is an American actor and producer. Known for roles in dramas and as Bruce Banner / the Hulk in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, he has received multiple Academy Award nominations and won Primetime Emmy and Golden Globe awards.

Career

Born in Kenosha, Wisconsin, Ruffalo trained at the Stella Adler Conservatory and co-founded the Orpheus Theatre Company in Los Angeles. His breakthrough came in Kenneth Lonergan’s You Can Count on Me (2000). He earned Academy Award nominations for The Kids Are All Right (2010), Foxcatcher (2014), Spotlight (2015), and Poor Things (2023). From The Avengers (2012) onward he has played the Hulk in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

Activism

Ruffalo is a prominent environmental activist, co-founding The Solutions Project to promote renewable energy and campaigning against hydraulic fracturing in New York. He won an Emmy for his dual performance in the HBO miniseries I Know This Much Is True (2020).
