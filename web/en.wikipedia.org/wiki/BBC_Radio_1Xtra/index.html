BBC Radio 1Xtra is a British digital radio station owned and operated by the BBC, launched on 16 August 2002 as a sister station to BBC Radio 1. It specialises in Black music and urban genres — grime, hip hop, R&B, dancehall, drum and bass, UK garage and afrobeats — with a remit to serve young audiences, particularly 15–24-year-olds, and to reflect Black British culture.

Programming and role

1Xtra shares news and some production resources with Radio 1 and broadcasts from Broadcasting House, London. The station has been an important platform for UK rap and grime, championing artists before mainstream breakthrough; presenters have included Trevor Nelson, DJ Target, Mistajam, Yasmin Evans and Jeremiah Asiamah. Its "1Xtra Live" events and the annual "Hot for [year]" list promote new talent. During the 2010 BBC digital-radio reviews 1Xtra survived proposed cuts that closed the Asian Network (a closure later reversed).
