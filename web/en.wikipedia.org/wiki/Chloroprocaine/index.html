Chloroprocaine

Chloroprocaine is an ester local anaesthetic drug given by injection during surgical procedures and labour. It is notable for its very rapid onset and short duration of action.

Pharmacology

Like other local anaesthetics, chloroprocaine blocks voltage-gated sodium channels in nerve membranes. It is rapidly hydrolysed by plasma cholinesterase, giving it the shortest half-life of the ester anaesthetics and a favourable toxicity profile at clinical doses.

Uses

Chloroprocaine is used for spinal and epidural anaesthesia, peripheral nerve blocks and infiltration, particularly in ambulatory surgery and obstetrics where a brief block is desired.
