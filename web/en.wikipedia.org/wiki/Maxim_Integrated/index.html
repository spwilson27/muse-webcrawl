Maxim Integrated
Founded: 1983
Headquarters: San Jose, California
Industry: Semiconductors (analog and mixed-signal)
Fate: Acquired by Analog Devices (2021)

Maxim Integrated was an American, publicly traded company that designed, manufactured and sold analog and mixed-signal integrated circuits. Founded in 1983 by Jack Gifford and colleagues, it was headquartered in San Jose, California, and produced thousands of products used in automotive, industrial, communications, consumer and computing applications.

History

Maxim grew through internal development and acquisitions, including the purchase of Dallas Semiconductor in 2001, which added products such as 1-Wire devices and the DS1307 real-time clock. In August 2021, Analog Devices completed its acquisition of Maxim in an all-stock transaction valued at roughly $21 billion, one of the largest semiconductor deals of its time, and the Maxim name was folded into ADI.

Products

Its portfolio included data converters, power management ICs, interface products, microcontrollers, sensors and security/authentication devices.
