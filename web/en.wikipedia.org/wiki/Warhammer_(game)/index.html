Warhammer (game)

Warhammer is a fictional universe and media franchise created by Games Workshop, originating with the tabletop miniature wargame Warhammer Fantasy Battle (1983). The name is most widely associated today with the science-fiction setting Warhammer 40,000.

Settings

The fantasy setting, known as the Warhammer World, depicts a grim world of humans, elves, dwarfs, orcs and the corrupting forces of Chaos. In 2015 Games Workshop ended the original fantasy game with the "End Times" event and replaced it with Warhammer Age of Sigmar, set in the Mortal Realms; the old setting returned in 2024 as Warhammer: The Old World. The science-fiction setting Warhammer 40,000 (1987) portrays a dystopian far future in which the Imperium of Man wars against aliens and Chaos across the galaxy.

Other media

The franchise includes role-playing games, novels published by Black Library, board games, and numerous video games such as the Total War: Warhammer trilogy and Warhammer: Vermintide.
