Fee simple

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

In English law, a fee simple or fee simple absolute is an estate in land, a form of freehold ownership. A "fee" is a vested estate in land held in return for feudal service, and "simple" means the estate is not subject to conditions beyond inheritance by general heirs. A fee simple is the most complete form of ownership known to the common law: it is the largest estate a person can hold, potentially lasting forever, freely alienable during the owner's life, and inheritable by the owner's heirs.

Characteristics

The owner in fee simple ("fee simple absolute in possession") may use, lease, mortgage, sell, or bequeath the land, subject only to public regulation such as taxation, zoning, eminent domain, and police power. At common law, the traditional words of conveyance were "to A and his heirs"; modern statutes generally presume a fee simple is transferred unless a lesser estate is stated. If the owner dies intestate without heirs, the land escheats to the state.

Origins

The fee simple developed within the feudal doctrine of tenure: technically, in England, all land is held of the Crown, and what owners hold is an estate in the land. The statute Quia Emptores (1290) forbade subinfeudation and allowed free alienation, helping transform the fee into a practically absolute ownership. In most Commonwealth jurisdictions the fee simple remains the standard freehold estate; in England and Wales, unregistered freehold land is usually held as fee simple absolute in possession, and in Canada, Australia, and the United States fee simple is the predominant form of land ownership.

Related estates

A fee simple may be made defeasible: a fee simple determinable ends automatically on a stated event; a fee simple subject to condition subsequent can be terminated by the grantor's re-entry; and a fee simple subject to executory limitation passes to a third party. These contrast with the fee tail, which restricts inheritance to lineal descendants, and the life estate, which lasts only for a lifetime.
