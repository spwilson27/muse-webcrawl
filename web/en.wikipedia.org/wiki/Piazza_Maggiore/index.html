Piazza Maggiore

Piazza Maggiore is a central square in Bologna, Italy, laid out in the 13th century as the civic and religious heart of the city. It is surrounded by some of Bologna's most important medieval and Renaissance buildings.

Buildings

The square is bounded by the Basilica of San Petronio, one of the largest churches in the world, whose unfinished facade dominates the south side; the Palazzo d'Accursio (Palazzo Comunale), the historic town hall, to the west; the Palazzo del Podestà and Palazzo Re Enzo to the north; and the Palazzo dei Banchi to the east. Nearby stands the 16th-century Fountain of Neptune by Giambologna in the adjoining Piazza del Nettuno.

History and use

The piazza took shape from 1200, when the commune began acquiring houses to create a market and civic space. It remains the setting for public gatherings, celebrations, and the summer open-air cinema of the Cineteca di Bologna's Sotto le stelle del cinema festival.
