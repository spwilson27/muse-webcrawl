Dragon Quest (video game)

Dragon Quest, released as Dragon Warrior in North America until 2005, is a role-playing video game developed by Chunsoft and published by Enix for the Nintendo Entertainment System. It was released in Japan in May 1986 and in North America in August 1989. It is the first game in the Dragon Quest series.

The game was designed by Yuji Horii, with artwork by Akira Toriyama and music by Koichi Sugiyama. The player controls a lone hero, a descendant of the legendary Erdrick (Loto in Japanese), tasked with rescuing Princess Gwaelin and defeating the Dragonlord who has stolen the Ball of Light from the kingdom of Alefgard.

Gameplay

Dragon Quest is a single-character role-playing game. The player explores an overworld, visits towns to buy weapons, armour and items, and battles monsters in random encounters using a menu-driven, turn-based combat system with commands such as Fight, Spell, Run and Item. Experience points and gold are earned from victories, allowing the hero to gain levels and grow stronger.

Development and legacy

Horii was inspired by Wizardry, Ultima and his own work on the adventure game Portopia Serial Murder Case. Together with Toriyama's character designs and Sugiyama's orchestral-style score, the game established many conventions of the Japanese role-playing game genre and spawned one of the best-selling video game franchises in Japan.
