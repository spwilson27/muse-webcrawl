Teddy Thompson (born 19 February 1976) is an English folk and rock musician, the son of folk-rock artists Richard Thompson and Linda Thompson. Raised partly in a Sufi commune in Sussex, he began performing in his father's band as a teenager before moving to Los Angeles and later New York to pursue a solo career distinguished by a clear tenor voice and wry, melancholic songwriting.

Career

Thompson's self-titled debut album appeared in 2000. Separate Ways (2005) followed, and his breakthrough came with Upfront & Down Low (2007), an album of classic country covers recorded in Nashville. A Piece of What You Need (2008) reached the UK albums chart, and Bella (2011) continued his series of polished pop-folk records. In 2014 he reunited with his mother and sister Kami for Family, an album credited to Thompson (the family band), written largely by him and produced by him. He has also produced records for others and collaborated frequently with Rufus and Martha Wainwright; his duet with Martha Wainwright appeared on the Brokeback Mountain soundtrack project, and he contributed to the Leonard Cohen tribute I'm Your Man.

Style

Critics frequently note the tension in his work between bright, tuneful melodies and lyrics about loneliness, family and failed romance, as well as the influence of the Everly Brothers, Hank Williams and 1960s pop.
