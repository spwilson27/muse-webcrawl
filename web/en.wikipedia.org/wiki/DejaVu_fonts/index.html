DejaVu fonts

The DejaVu fonts are a family of free and open-source typefaces designed for broad coverage of the Unicode character repertoire. Based on the Bitstream Vera fonts, the DejaVu project expanded the original designs with thousands of additional glyphs while maintaining their public-domain-style licence terms.

History

The project was started by Štěpán Roh in 2004, building on Bitstream Vera Sans and Vera Serif, which Bitstream had released in 2003 under a permissive licence. Community contributors steadily added coverage for scripts and symbols absent from the originals, including additional Latin, Greek, Cyrillic, Armenian, Georgian, Hebrew, Arabic and many symbol blocks.

Typefaces

The family includes DejaVu Sans (in Book, Bold, Oblique and Condensed variants), DejaVu Serif, and DejaVu Sans Mono. The fonts became the default typefaces on many Linux distributions and are widely used as a fallback font because of their extensive Unicode coverage.

Licence

The fonts are distributed under the Bitstream Vera licence, which permits use, copying, modification and redistribution, with the condition that the fonts not be sold by themselves.
