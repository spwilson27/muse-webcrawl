Jennifer Hudson

Born: 12 September 1981, Chicago, Illinois
Occupation: Singer, actress, talk show host
Years active: 2004–present

Jennifer Kate Hudson (born 12 September 1981) is an American singer, actress, and talk show host. She rose to fame in 2004 as a finalist on the third season of American Idol, and achieved stardom with her film debut as Effie White in Dreamgirls (2006), which won her the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress. She is the youngest woman and second African-American woman to achieve EGOT status, having won Emmy, Grammy, Oscar, and Tony awards.

Career

After her seventh-place finish on American Idol — widely considered one of the show's most shocking eliminations — Hudson was cast in Dreamgirls over hundreds of other candidates. Her performance of "And I Am Telling You I'm Not Going" became her signature. Her self-titled debut album (2008) won the Grammy Award for Best R&B Album. Subsequent acting roles include The Secret Life of Bees, Winnie Mandela, Sing, and the Aretha Franklin biopic Respect (2021), in which she was hand-picked by Franklin for the role. On Broadway she appeared in The Color Purple (2015) and won a Tony Award as a producer of A Strange Loop (2022), completing her EGOT. Since 2022 she has hosted the syndicated talk show The Jennifer Hudson Show.

Personal life

In October 2008, Hudson's mother, brother, and seven-year-old nephew were murdered in Chicago; the perpetrator, a former brother-in-law, was convicted in 2012. Hudson subsequently co-founded the Julian D. King Gift Foundation in her nephew's memory to support children in the Chicago area.
