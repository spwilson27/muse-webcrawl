Graphics tablet

A graphics tablet (also digitizing tablet, drawing tablet or pen tablet) is a computer input device that enables a user to hand-draw images, animations and graphics with a special pen-like stylus, similar to the way one draws with a pencil and paper. The device consists of a flat surface on which the user may draw, and the drawing appears on a connected computer screen — or, in pen display models, on the tablet's own built-in display.

Technology

Most modern tablets detect the stylus through electromagnetic resonance or an active pen, providing pressure sensitivity (often thousands of levels), tilt recognition and high positional accuracy without requiring the pen to touch-bear a battery. Capacitive and resistive technologies are also used in some devices.

Uses

Graphics tablets are widely used in digital illustration, photo retouching, animation, 3D sculpting, handwriting input for East Asian characters, signature capture, and computer-aided design. Major manufacturers include Wacom, XP-Pen and Huion.
