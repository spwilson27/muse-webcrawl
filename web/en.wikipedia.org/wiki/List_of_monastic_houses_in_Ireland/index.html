This is a list of monastic houses in Ireland. Ireland's monastic tradition began in the early Christian period, when foundations such as those associated with St Patrick, St Brigid and St Columba became centres of learning and missionary activity. From the 12th century the continental orders — Cistercians, Augustinians, Benedictines, Franciscans and Dominicans — established hundreds of houses across the island, most of which were suppressed during the Dissolution of the Monasteries under Henry VIII between 1536 and 1541.
