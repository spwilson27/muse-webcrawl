Planet

Planet
Definition (IAU 2006)Orbits a star, rounded by gravity, has cleared its neighbourhood
In the Solar SystemEight: Mercury to Neptune
[Image: The eight planets of the Solar System in a row, to relative size]

A planet is an astronomical body orbiting a star or stellar remnant, massive enough to be rounded by its own gravity but not to fuse hydrogen, and — by the IAU's 2006 definition — having cleared its orbital neighbourhood, the clause that demoted Pluto to dwarf planet.

Exoplanets

Since the 1990s astronomers have confirmed thousands of exoplanets — hot Jupiters, super-Earths, ocean worlds — making planets common and sharpening the search for habitable ones, targeted by missions like Europa Clipper and telescopes like Webb. Formation from protoplanetary discs is the standard model.
