Autonomous robot

Examples: Roomba, Mars rovers, self-driving cars
Key abilities: Perception, localisation, planning, control

An autonomous robot is a robot that performs behaviours or tasks with a high degree of autonomy, without external control for extended periods. Autonomous robots sense their environment, make decisions and act to achieve goals, and are used where human presence is impractical, dangerous or uneconomical.

Capabilities

Essential capabilities include environmental perception through sensors such as cameras, lidar and sonar; self-localisation and mapping (SLAM); path planning and obstacle avoidance; and the ability to operate for long periods, including self-charging.

Applications

Examples include domestic robots such as robotic vacuum cleaners, warehouse and delivery robots, agricultural robots, planetary rovers such as NASA's Curiosity and Perseverance, autonomous underwater vehicles, and self-driving cars.
