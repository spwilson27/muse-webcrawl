European Accessibility Act

European Accessibility Act
Directive(EU) 2019/882
Adopted17 April 2019
Applies from28 June 2025
PurposeCommon accessibility requirements for products and services

The European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882) is a directive of the European Union that sets common accessibility requirements for a range of products and services across member states, aiming to improve the functioning of the internal market for accessible products and services and to remove barriers created by divergent national rules.

Scope

The Act covers consumer computer hardware and operating systems, ATMs and other self-service terminals, smartphones, television equipment with digital interactive services, e-books and e-readers, e-commerce services, banking services, and elements of passenger transport services, including websites, mobile applications and ticketing machines. Requirements relate to, among other things, perceivability, operability and compatibility with assistive technologies.

Implementation

Member states were required to transpose the directive into national law by 28 June 2022, with the substantive obligations applying from 28 June 2025. Microenterprises providing services are exempt, and transitional periods apply to some products already in use. The Act complements the earlier Web Accessibility Directive covering public sector websites and apps.
