Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood is a Japanese anime television series produced by the studio Bones and adapted from Hiromu Arakawa's manga Fullmetal Alchemist. A second adaptation after the 2003 anime series, Brotherhood follows the manga's storyline closely to its conclusion.

Plot

Brothers Edward and Alphonse Elric, alchemists in the fictional country of Amestris, search for the Philosopher's Stone to restore their bodies after a failed attempt to resurrect their mother through human transmutation cost Edward an arm and a leg and Alphonse his entire body. Their quest uncovers a vast conspiracy involving the homunculi, the military government and the true nature of the Stone.

Reception

The series aired 64 episodes from April 2009 to July 2010. It has been widely acclaimed by critics and audiences and is frequently ranked among the greatest anime series of all time, praised for its faithful plotting, character development and themes of sacrifice and equivalent exchange.
