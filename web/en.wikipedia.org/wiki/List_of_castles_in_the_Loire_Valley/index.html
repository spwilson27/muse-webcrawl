List of castles in the Loire Valley

The châteaux of the Loire Valley are hundreds of castles, manor houses, and fortified residences along the River Loire and its tributaries in central France, built or rebuilt from the tenth century onward and especially during the French Renaissance, when the valley was the seat of royal power. The central part of the valley is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

History

Counts of Anjou and Blois built the earliest fortresses against Norman and rival incursions. In the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries, French kings from Charles VIII to Henry IV made the valley their court's home, and Italian Renaissance ideas reshaped its architecture. Many châteaux were later slighted in the Revolution but survive today as museums and hotels.
