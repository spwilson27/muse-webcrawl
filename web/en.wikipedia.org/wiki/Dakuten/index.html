Dakuten

The dakuten (濁点, "voicing mark"), colloquially ten-ten (点々, "dots"), is a diacritic used in the Japanese kana syllabaries to indicate that the consonant of a syllable should be pronounced voiced. It takes the form of two short strokes at the upper right of the character, as in か → が (ka → ga).

The related handakuten (半濁点, "half-voicing mark"), a small circle, changes h-row kana to p sounds: は → ぱ. Combining characters and stand-alone forms of the marks exist in Unicode and in Japanese typesetting conventions.
