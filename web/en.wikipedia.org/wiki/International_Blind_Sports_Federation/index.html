International Blind Sports Federation

The International Blind Sports Federation (IBSA) is a non-profit organisation founded in 1981 in Paris. It is the international sports federation for athletes who are blind or partially sighted, and a full member of the International Paralympic Committee.

Sports

IBSA governs three Paralympic sports: goalball, Paralympic judo, and blind football (football 5-a-side). It also oversees other sports for visually impaired athletes, including showdown, ninepin bowling, powerlifting, and torball.

Classification

IBSA is responsible for the visual impairment classification system (B1, B2, B3 categories) used across sports for blind athletes, based on visual acuity and visual field.
