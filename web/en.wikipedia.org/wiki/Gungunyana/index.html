Gungunyana (also spelled Gungunhana, c. 1850 – 1906) was the last emperor of the Gaza Empire, a Nguni kingdom in present-day southern Mozambique. His defeat by the Portuguese in 1895 completed the colonial occupation of Mozambique.

Reign

Gungunyana succeeded his father Muzila in 1884 after a succession struggle. From his capital at Mandhlakazi he manoeuvred between Portuguese and British interests, signing treaties with both while resisting effective occupation. In 1890–91 he rebuffed Portuguese demands and moved his capital to Chaimite.

Defeat and exile

In 1895 Portuguese forces under António Enes and Mouzinho de Albuquerque invaded Gaza. After the battle of Magul, Gungunyana was captured at Chaimite on 28 December 1895. He was paraded in Lisbon and exiled to the Azores, where he was baptised a Catholic and died at Angra do Heroísmo in 1906. His remains were repatriated to Mozambique in 1985.
