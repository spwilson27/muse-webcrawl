Madanapalle is a city in Annamayya district of the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh. It is a municipality and the headquarters of Madanapalle revenue division and mandal. Situated at the tri-junction of Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu influences, it is one of the larger towns of the Rayalaseema region and is known for its salubrious climate, silk and tomato trade, and its association with the philosopher Jiddu Krishnamurti.

Geography and climate

Madanapalle lies at an elevation of roughly 695 metres on the Deccan plateau, which gives it a milder climate than the surrounding lowlands — it has long been called the "Ooty of Andhra Pradesh"-like hill retreat of the region, with the Horsley Hills (named after W. D. Horsley, a British collector) lying nearby. Temperatures are moderate year-round, and the area experiences both southwest and northeast monsoons.

History and notable connections

The area passed through the hands of the Vijayanagara Empire, the Mughals, the Nizam of Hyderabad, and the British Madras Presidency. In 1915, the Theosophical Society's Besant Theosophical College operated here, and Madanapalle is the birthplace of Jiddu Krishnamurti (born 11 May 1895), whose discovery by the Theosophists began here. Rabindranath Tagore visited Madanapalle in 1919 and, staying at Besant Theosophical College, translated his Bengali song "Jana Gana Mana" into English as "The Morning Song of India" with Margaret Cousins setting its western notation — the song later became the national anthem of India. Madanapalle's tomato market is among the largest in Asia, and the town is a centre of the regional silk industry.

Transport and economy

The town is connected by National Highway 71 and state highways to Chittoor, Tirupati, and Bengaluru, and has a railway station on the Pakala–Dharmavaram line. Agriculture (tomatoes, mangoes, groundnuts, tamarind), sericulture, and education anchor the economy; the town hosts several colleges and the district's institutions following the 2022 reorganisation that placed it in Annamayya district.
