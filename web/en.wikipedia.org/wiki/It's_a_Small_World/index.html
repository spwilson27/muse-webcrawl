"It's a Small World"

"It's a Small World" (styled as "it's a small world") is an Old Mill boat ride located in the Fantasyland area at various Disney theme parks worldwide. It is widely recognized for its signature song of the same name, written by the Sherman Brothers, Richard M. Sherman and Robert B. Sherman, which plays continuously throughout the ride in a looping, multi-language arrangement.

History

The attraction was originally created for the 1964–1965 New York World's Fair, where it was presented by Pepsi-Cola as a tribute to UNICEF and the world's children. Walt Disney was asked to design the pavilion only about a year before the fair opened. The ride opened at Disneyland in California on May 28, 1966, and versions have since been installed at Magic Kingdom (1971), Tokyo Disneyland (1983), Disneyland Paris (1992), and Hong Kong Disneyland (2008).

The song

The theme song was written by Richard M. and Robert B. Sherman in the wake of the Cuban Missile Crisis, with a message of peace and global unity. It is estimated to be the most-performed and most-publicly-played song of all time, given that it plays nearly continuously in all five park versions. The song is performed in multiple languages, representing each region of the ride.

Ride experience

Guests board boats that sail through a series of dioramas representing the continents, populated by nearly 300 Audio-Animatronics dolls in national costumes designed by Alice Davis, singing the theme song. Mary Blair's distinctive, childlike art style defines the visual identity, with Rolly Crump's toy-like architectural flourishes and Marc Davis's character designs. The finale room depicts all the dolls of the world gathered together.

Cultural impact

The ride's repetitive, cheerful song has become a pop-cultural touchstone, frequently parodied in television and film as an example of an inescapable earworm. Despite its reputation for repetitiveness, the attraction remains one of the most popular family rides in the Disney parks, celebrated for its message of international harmony.
