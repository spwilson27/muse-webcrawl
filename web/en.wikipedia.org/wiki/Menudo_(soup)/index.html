Menudo (soup)

Menudo
OriginMexico
TypeSoup
Main ingredientsBeef tripe, hominy, chili peppers, lime, onions, oregano
VariationsMenudo rojo (red), menudo blanco (white)

Menudo is a traditional Mexican soup made with cow's stomach (tripe) in a broth seasoned with red chili peppers (menudo rojo) or a clear broth (menudo blanco), typically including hominy, lime, onions and oregano.

Preparation and culture

The tripe is simmered for hours until tender; calf's foot is sometimes added for its gelatin. Because of its long cooking time, menudo is often prepared in large batches for gatherings, and in restaurants it is traditionally a weekend dish, especially on Sunday mornings. In Mexican and Mexican-American folklore it is widely reputed to be a cure for hangovers. Regional variants exist across Mexico, including versions with garbanzo beans or without hominy in parts of the north.

Similar dishes

Related tripe soups occur throughout Latin America and Spain, such as Ecuadorian guatita and Spanish callos.
