Toni Bou

Antoni Bou i Mena (born 17 October 1986), known as Toni Bou, is a Spanish professional motorcycle trials rider. Riding for Repsol Honda, he is widely regarded as the greatest trials rider in history.

Bou has won an unprecedented run of consecutive FIM Trial World Championship titles outdoors and X-Trial titles indoors beginning in 2007, accumulating more than 30 world championships in total, surpassing the records of Dougie Lampkin and Jordi Tarrés.
