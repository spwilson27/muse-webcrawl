Lee Sang-hyeok (Korean: 이상혁; born 7 May 1996), better known as Faker, is a South Korean professional League of Legends player for T1. Widely regarded as the greatest League of Legends player of all time, he has played as the team's mid laner since his debut in 2013 and is the first player to win the World Championship five times, as well as the first to win consecutive titles twice.

Career

Faker joined SK Telecom T1 in 2013 and immediately won the World Championship in his rookie season. He won further world titles in 2015 and 2016 — becoming nicknamed the "Unkillable Demon King" — and, after a long title drought, led the rebranded T1 to victory again at Worlds 2023 in South Korea and Worlds 2024 in London, the latter earning him the Finals MVP award. Domestically he has won numerous LCK titles and holds league records including the first player to reach 500, 600 and 700 wins and thousands of kills in the LCK. He also won two Mid-Season Invitational titles (2016, 2017) and a gold medal with South Korea at the 2022 Asian Games, held in 2023.

Playing style and legacy

Faker is known for mechanical precision, an exceptionally deep champion pool and calm decision-making in high-pressure games; his 2013 outplay against Ryu's Zed as Zed is one of the most famous plays in esports history. He is a part-owner of T1 and the first inductee into Riot Games' Hall of Legends in 2024. In South Korea he is a mainstream celebrity, appearing in national advertising and being cited as a cultural ambassador for esports.
