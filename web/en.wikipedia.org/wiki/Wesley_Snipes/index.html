Wesley Snipes

Wesley Trent Snipes (born July 31, 1962) is an American actor and martial artist. He starred in numerous action films in the 1990s and is best known for playing the Marvel Comics character Blade in the Blade film trilogy (1998–2004), a role that helped pave the way for modern superhero cinema.

Career

Snipes was discovered by an agent while performing at a competition and made his film debut in Wildcats (1986). He appeared in Michael Jackson's "Bad" music video and gained prominence with Major League (1989), Spike Lee's Mo' Better Blues (1990) and Jungle Fever (1991), New Jack City (1991), White Men Can't Jump (1992), Passenger 57 (1992), Demolition Man (1993), U.S. Marshals (1998), and The Expendables 3 (2014). He later appeared in Dolemite Is My Name (2019) and Coming 2 America (2021), and reprised Blade in Deadpool & Wolverine (2024).

Martial arts

Snipes began training in martial arts as a child and holds black belts in Shotokan karate and hapkido, and has trained in kung fu, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, and capoeira, performing many of his own stunts.

Tax case

In 2008 Snipes was convicted of three misdemeanour counts of failing to file federal income tax returns and sentenced to three years in prison, which he served from 2010 to 2013. He had been acquitted of felony tax fraud and conspiracy charges.
