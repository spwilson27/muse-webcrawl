Enrico Letta

Enrico Letta (born 20 August 1966) is an Italian politician who served as Prime Minister of Italy from April 2013 to February 2014, leading a grand coalition government. He later served as secretary of the Democratic Party from 2021 to 2023.

Early life and career

Born in Pisa, Letta studied international law, entered politics through Christian-democratic youth movements, became one of Italy's youngest ministers, and served as an MEP and deputy.

Premiership

Appointed after the inconclusive 2013 election, his government included centre-left, centre and centre-right parties. It faced the eurozone debt crisis aftermath and internal tensions; he resigned after Matteo Renzi challenged his leadership within the Democratic Party.

Later career

He became dean of the Paris School of International Affairs at Sciences Po, returned to lead the Democratic Party in 2021, led it into the 2022 election, and subsequently worked on European single-market reporting for EU institutions.
