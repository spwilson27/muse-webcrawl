Binali Yıldırım (born 20 December 1955) is a Turkish politician who served as the last Prime Minister of Turkey from 2016 to 2018, when the office was abolished following the transition to an executive presidency. A close ally of Recep Tayyip Erdoğan, he was a co-founder of the Justice and Development Party (AKP).

Early life and career

Yıldırım studied naval architecture and ocean engineering at Istanbul Technical University. He worked in the shipbuilding industry and served as general manager of the Istanbul Fast Ferries Company (İDO) from 1994 to 2000, during Erdoğan's tenure as Mayor of Istanbul.

Political career

Elected to parliament in 2002, Yıldırım served for over a decade as Minister of Transport, Maritime and Communication, overseeing major infrastructure projects including high-speed rail lines, highway expansion, and the growth of Turkish Airlines. He became AKP leader and Prime Minister in May 2016 after the resignation of Ahmet Davutoğlu. Following the 2018 constitutional changes he became the first Speaker of the Grand National Assembly under the new system, and later ran as the AKP candidate for Mayor of Istanbul in 2019.
