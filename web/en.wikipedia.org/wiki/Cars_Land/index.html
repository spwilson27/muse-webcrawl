Cars Land

Cars Land recreates Radiator Springs from Pixar’s Cars films.

Cars Land is a themed land at Disney California Adventure in Anaheim, California. Opened on June 15, 2012, as the centerpiece of the park’s major expansion, it recreates Radiator Springs, the fictional Route 66 town from Pixar’s Cars (2006). The land covers about 12 acres and is noted for its detailed rockwork representing the Cadillac Range.

Attractions

The signature attraction is Radiator Springs Racers, a dark ride and slot-car racing attraction in which guests tour Radiator Springs before a head-to-head race through Ornament Valley. Other attractions include Mater’s Junkyard Jamboree, a spinning tractor ride, and Luigi’s Rollickin’ Roadsters, a trackless dancing-car ride that replaced Luigi’s Flying Tires in 2016.

Dining and shops

Restaurants and snack locations include Flo’s V8 Cafe, the Cozy Cone Motel cone stands, and Fillmore’s Taste-In. Shops include Ramone’s House of Body Art and Sarge’s Surplus Hut. The land’s main street reproduces buildings from the film, including Radiator Springs Curios and the Cozy Cone Motel.

Design and reception

Imagineers developed the land with Pixar creative input, aiming for an immersive environment at full architectural scale. Cars Land received praise for its atmosphere and for Radiator Springs Racers, and it significantly increased attendance at Disney California Adventure.
