Hollywood Boulevard

Hollywood Boulevard is a major east–west street in Los Angeles, California, running through Hollywood, East Hollywood, Little Armenia, Thai Town and Los Feliz. Its western stretch is world-famous as a centre of the entertainment industry.

Landmarks

The boulevard hosts the Hollywood Walk of Fame, with more than 2,700 stars embedded in the sidewalks honouring figures in film, television, music, radio and theatre. Other landmarks include the TCL Chinese Theatre, the Dolby Theatre (home of the Academy Awards), the El Capitan Theatre, the Hollywood Roosevelt Hotel and the Hollywood and Highland complex.

History

Originally called Prospect Avenue, the street was renamed in 1910 when Hollywood was annexed by Los Angeles. From the 1920s it became the glamorous heart of the film business, lined with theatres and studios, a status it retains today as a major tourist destination.
