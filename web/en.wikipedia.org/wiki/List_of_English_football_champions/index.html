List of English football champions

The English football champions are the winners of the top-level league in English men's football, which was the Football League First Division from 1888 until 1992 and the Premier League since 1992–93. Twenty-four clubs have been champions in total.

Single-title winners include Sheffield United (1897–98), West Bromwich Albion (1919–20), Ipswich Town (1961–62), Nottingham Forest (1977–78) and Leicester City (2015–16), whose 5,000–1 triumph is regarded as one of sport's greatest upsets.

Records

Preston North End won the first championship in 1888–89 without losing a match. Liverpool's 2020 title ended a 30-year wait, while Manchester United's 20th title in 2013 took them past Liverpool's record haul; Liverpool drew level again in 2025. Manchester City became the first club to win four consecutive English titles, from 2021 to 2024.
