Official name: Atlanta–Sandy Springs–Roswell MSA
State: Georgia
Principal city: Atlanta
Population (MSA): approx. 6.3 million (2023)

The Atlanta metropolitan area, designated by the United States Office of Management and Budget as the Atlanta–Sandy Springs–Roswell, GA Metropolitan Statistical Area, is the most populous metropolitan statistical area in the U.S. state of Georgia and the sixth-largest in the United States. Its population was over 6 million at the 2020 census and it has continued to grow since.

Geography

The metropolitan area comprises 29 counties in north Georgia, sprawling across the rolling hills of the Piedmont plateau south of the Blue Ridge Mountains. The city of Atlanta itself, with a population of about 500,000, occupies only a fraction of the region; the majority of residents live in suburban communities such as Sandy Springs, Roswell, Marietta, Alpharetta, and Johns Creek. The region is noted for its heavy tree canopy, sometimes called a "city in a forest".

Economy

Metro Atlanta is a major business centre of the southeastern United States and the corporate headquarters of companies including The Coca-Cola Company, Delta Air Lines, UPS, Home Depot, and CNN's parent Warner Bros. Discovery operations. Hartsfield–Jackson Atlanta International Airport, long ranked among the world's busiest airports, anchors the region's role as a transportation and logistics hub. The area is also a significant centre for film and television production, earning Atlanta the nickname "Hollywood of the South".

Transportation

The region is served by a network of interstate highways, including I-75, I-85, and the I-285 Perimeter, and by MARTA, a heavy-rail and bus system serving the city of Atlanta and Fulton, DeKalb, and Clayton counties. Traffic congestion and suburban sprawl are recurring regional issues.
