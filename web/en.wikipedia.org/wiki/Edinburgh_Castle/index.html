Edinburgh Castle

Edinburgh Castle is a historic castle in Edinburgh, Scotland. It stands on Castle Rock, which has been occupied by humans since at least the Iron Age, and has been a royal castle since the reign of David I in the 12th century.

Edinburgh Castle
Location: Edinburgh, Scotland
Site: Castle Rock
Visitors: ~2 million/year
Contains: St Margaret's Chapel, Honours of Scotland

History

The castle was a royal residence until the Union of the Crowns in 1603, involved in many conflicts including the Wars of Scottish Independence and the Lang Siege (1571–73). St Margaret's Chapel, dating from the early 12th century, is Edinburgh's oldest surviving building.

Features

It houses the Honours of Scotland (crown jewels), the Stone of Destiny (returned 1996, moved to Perth Museum in 2024 for display rotation), the One O'Clock Gun, the Scottish National War Memorial, and Mons Meg, a 15th-century bombard. The Royal Edinburgh Military Tattoo is held on its esplanade.

Today

Managed by Historic Environment Scotland, it is Scotland's most popular paid tourist attraction.
