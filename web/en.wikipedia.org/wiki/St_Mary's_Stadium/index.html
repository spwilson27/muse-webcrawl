St Mary's Stadium

St Mary's Stadium Location: Britannia Road, Southampton, England
Opened: August 2001
Capacity: 32,384
Tenants: Southampton F.C.
Surface: Grass (hybrid)

St Mary's Stadium is a football stadium in Southampton, Hampshire, England, and the home ground of Southampton F.C. since 2001, when the club moved from its historic former home, The Dell. With a capacity of about 32,400, it is the largest football stadium in south-east England outside London.

St Mary's Stadium, home of Southampton F.C.

History

The stadium was built on the site of a former gasworks near the River Itchen. The club had sought to move from the cramped Dell for decades, and earlier plans for a stadium at Stoneham foundered; St Mary's — named for the St Mary's area and the club's origins as St Mary's Y.M.A. — finally opened with a friendly against Espanyol in August 2001.

Features and events

The ground is a complete bowl with four distinct stands: the Itchen, Kingsland, Northam and Chapel stands. It has hosted England international matches, European football, and concerts, and is named in full as the St Mary's Stadium.
