Rishi Kapoor

Rishi Raj Kapoor (4 September 1952 – 30 April 2020) was an Indian actor, film director, and producer who worked in Hindi films. A member of the Kapoor family, he was the second son of actor-director Raj Kapoor and grandson of Prithviraj Kapoor. He received the National Film Award for Best Child Artist for his debut role in his father's film Mera Naam Joker (1970).

Career

Kapoor's first lead role, in Bobby (1973), made him a teen idol and one of Hindi cinema's leading romantic heroes; he went on to star in more than 90 romantic films, including Khel Khel Mein, Kabhi Kabhie, Amar Akbar Anthony, Karz, Prem Rog, Chandni and Henna. In the 2000s he successfully transitioned to character roles, earning acclaim for Do Dooni Chaar (2010), Agneepath (2012), Kapoor & Sons (2016) and Mulk (2018). He directed the film Aa Ab Laut Chalen (1999).

Personal life and death

He married actress Neetu Singh in 1980; their son Ranbir Kapoor is also a leading actor. Kapoor died in Mumbai in 2020 after a two-year battle with leukaemia.
