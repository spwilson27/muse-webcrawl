List of art museums

The List of art museums is an international directory of institutions devoted to the collection and exhibition of visual art. The world's great encyclopedic museums — the Louvre, the Metropolitan Museum of Art, the British Museum, and the State Hermitage — hold millions of objects, while thousands of smaller museums focus on particular periods, regions, or artists.

Oldest museums

The Capitoline Museums in Rome (public from 1734), the Ashmolean in Oxford (1683), and the Kunstmuseum Basel (1661, the oldest public art collection) are among the earliest institutions of their kind. The Louvre opened as a museum in 1793 in the former royal palace.
