Beavis and Butt-Head

Beavis and Butt-Head, created by Mike Judge.

Beavis and Butt-Head is an American adult animated sitcom created by Mike Judge. The series follows two teenage slackers, Beavis and Butt-Head, who live in the fictional town of Highland, Texas, attend high school sporadically, work at the fast-food restaurant Burger World, and spend most of their time watching television and commenting on music videos.

History

The characters first appeared in Judge's 1992 short film Frog Baseball, shown on MTV's Liquid Television. The series proper premiered on MTV in 1993 and became one of the network's defining programmes of the decade, running until 1997. A feature film, Beavis and Butt-Head Do America, was released in 1996. The series was revived on MTV in 2011, again in 2022 on Paramount+ with the film Beavis and Butt-Head Do the Universe, and later on Comedy Central.

Cultural impact

The show was both celebrated as a shrewd satire of American youth and media culture and condemned for its crude humour and perceived influence on children. Judge voiced both title characters himself; their distinctive chuckles ("huh huh" and "heh heh"), catchphrases and obsession with heavy metal became widely recognised pieces of 1990s popular culture.
