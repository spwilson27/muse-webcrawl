European Golden Shoe

The European Golden Shoe, also known as the European Golden Boot, is an award presented each season to the player with the most points in league matches in the top division of a European national league. Originally awarded by L'Équipe from 1968, it is now presented by European Sports Media.

First awarded1968 (Eusébio)
Most winsLionel Messi (6)
Record seasonMessi, 50 goals, 2011–12

Scoring

Since 1996–97 points are weighted: goals in the top five UEFA-ranked leagues count double, goals in mid-ranked leagues count 1.5, and goals elsewhere count once. Lionel Messi holds the record with six awards and 50 league goals in 2011–12 for Barcelona; Cristiano Ronaldo won four times. Other multiple winners include Gerd Müller, Eusébio, Robert Lewandowski and Mohamed Salah contenders in recent seasons.
