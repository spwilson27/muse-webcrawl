Hamad International Airport (IATA: DOH, ICAO: OTHH) is the sole international airport of Qatar, located in the capital, Doha. It opened on 30 April 2014, replacing the former Doha International Airport as the hub of Qatar Airways and the country's principal gateway.

Facilities

Built partly on reclaimed land in Doha Bay at a cost of roughly US$16 billion, the airport's passenger terminal covers about 600,000 square metres. It is noted for its distinctive design elements, including the giant "Lamp Bear" sculpture by Urs Fischer in the main hall, extensive retail and lounge space, and an indoor tropical garden called The Orchard, added in an expansion opened in November 2022 ahead of the FIFA World Cup. The expansion raised capacity to around 58 million passengers a year.

Recognition

Hamad International has repeatedly ranked at or near the top of the Skytrax World Airport Awards, being named World's Best Airport in 2021, 2022 and 2024. Its two parallel runways include one of about 4,850 metres, among the longest in the world.
