Horse (zodiac)

Horse
The Horse, seventh sign of the Chinese zodiac
Chinese馬 (simplified 马), pinyin: Mǎ
Position7th of 12
Element (cyclic)Fire (fixed), plus rotating Wu Xing element by year
Hours11 a.m. – 1 p.m.

The Horse (馬) is the seventh of the 12-year cycle of animals which appear in the Chinese zodiac related to the Chinese calendar. The Year of the Horse is associated with energy, independence, and warmth in traditional Chinese astrology.

Traits

In popular astrology, people born in the Year of the Horse are said to be cheerful, quick-witted, energetic, and independent, but also impatient and hot-tempered. The Horse is said to be most compatible with the Tiger and the Dog, and least compatible with the Rat, its opposite sign in the cycle.

Fire Horse superstition

In Japan, the year of the Fire Horse (hinoe-uma), which occurs every 60 years, carries a superstition that girls born that year will have fierce temperaments and bring misfortune to their husbands. The belief measurably depressed the birth rate in Japan in 1966, when many couples avoided having children or the birth registration was manipulated; a marked dip in births that year is visible in Japanese demographic statistics.

In other cultures

The Horse occupies the corresponding place in the zodiac traditions derived from the Chinese system, including the Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, and Tibetan calendars, and in the Mongolian calendar, with local variations.
