Women's National Basketball Association (WNBA)
Founded: April 22, 1996
First season: 1997
Teams: 12 (expanding)
Country: United States

The Women's National Basketball Association (WNBA) is a women's professional basketball league based in the United States. It is composed of 12 teams, all of which are owned by NBA team owners or independent owners, and it is the counterpart to the National Basketball Association (NBA). The league was founded on April 22, 1996, as the women's counterpart to the NBA, and league play started in 1997.

History

The first WNBA game was played between the New York Liberty and the Los Angeles Sparks in 1997. The Houston Comets won the first four championships, led by Cynthia Cooper, Sheryl Swoopes and Tina Thompson. Other dynasties followed, including the Minnesota Lynx and the Seattle Storm.

Format

The regular season runs from May to September, followed by playoffs culminating in the WNBA Finals. The league also holds an All-Star Game and the mid-season Commissioner's Cup tournament. Prominent players have included Diana Taurasi, the league's all-time leading scorer, Sue Bird, Candace Parker, A'ja Wilson, Breanna Stewart and Caitlin Clark, whose 2024 arrival coincided with a sharp rise in attendance and television audiences.
