Shopify Inc. is a Canadian multinational e-commerce company headquartered in Ottawa, Ontario. It develops the Shopify platform, a subscription-based software service that allows merchants to set up online stores and sell products, manage inventory, process payments, and handle marketing and shipping.

History

Shopify was founded in 2006 by Tobias Lütke, Daniel Weinand, and Scott Lake after they attempted to open an online store for snowboarding equipment and found existing e-commerce software inadequate. The company went public in 2015 on the New York Stock Exchange and Toronto Stock Exchange. By the 2020s, millions of merchants in more than 175 countries used the platform.

Products and services

Core offerings include the Shopify store builder, Shopify Payments, Shop Pay, Shopify Markets for cross-border sales, and an app store of third-party extensions. Shopify Plus targets larger enterprises.
