The Salesman

The Salesman (Persian: فروشنده, Forushande) is a 2016 Iranian-French drama film written and directed by Asghar Farhadi. It stars Taraneh Alidoosti and Shahab Hosseini, who won the Best Actor award at the 2016 Cannes Film Festival, where Farhadi also won Best Screenplay.

Plot

Emad and Rana, a young couple in Tehran, are forced to leave their collapsing apartment and move into a flat once occupied by a prostitute. While performing in an amateur production of Arthur Miller's Death of a Salesman, Rana is assaulted at home by an intruder who mistook her for the previous tenant. Emad becomes obsessed with tracking down the attacker, and the couple's relationship unravels as his search for revenge collides with questions of dignity, forgiveness, and honour.

Reception

The film won the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film at the 89th Academy Awards, Farhadi's second Oscar after A Separation (2011). Farhadi did not attend the ceremony in protest at the United States travel ban affecting Iranian citizens.
