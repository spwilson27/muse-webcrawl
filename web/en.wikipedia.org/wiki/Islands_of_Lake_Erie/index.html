Lake Erie Islands

The Lake Erie Islands are a chain of archipelagic islands in Lake Erie. Most of them lie in the US state of Ohio, with a few — most notably Pelee Island, the largest of the group — belonging to the Canadian province of Ontario. The islands are popular tourist destinations in summer, known for recreation, wineries, and historic sites, and are served by ferries and small airports.

Principal islands

  • Pelee Island (Ontario) — the largest Lake Erie island and the southernmost populated place in Canada, noted for vineyards and bird migration.
  • South Bass Island (Ohio) — home to the village of Put-in-Bay and Perry's Victory and International Peace Memorial, commemorating the Battle of Lake Erie (1813).
  • Middle Bass Island (Ohio) — quieter island once known for the Lonz Winery.
  • North Bass Island (Ohio) — largely a state park, with vineyards and few residents.
  • Kelleys Island (Ohio) — known for the Glacial Grooves, some of the largest accessible glacial striations in the world.
  • Johnson's Island (Ohio) — site of a Confederate Civil War prison camp and cemetery.
  • West Sister Island (Ohio) — a national wildlife refuge and major heron rookery.
  • Rattlesnake Island (Ohio) — privately owned island near Put-in-Bay.

History

The islands were frequented by Native American peoples before European settlement. During the War of 1812, Commodore Oliver Hazard Perry's victory in the Battle of Lake Erie (September 10, 1813), fought near the islands, secured the lake for the United States. In the 19th century the islands developed industries of quarrying, fishing and grape-growing; Kelleys Island and the Bass Islands became centres of wine production. During the American Civil War, Johnson's Island held a prisoner-of-war camp for Confederate officers.

Geology and nature

The islands are composed largely of limestone and dolomite, shaped by glaciation. Kelleys Island's Glacial Grooves preserve deep furrows scoured by the ice sheet. Located on major migratory flyways, the islands (especially Pelee and Point Pelee nearby) are renowned birdwatching sites in spring and autumn.

Tourism and transport

Ferries connect the Ohio islands with Sandusky, Port Clinton, Catawba Island and Marblehead, and Pelee Island with Kingsville and Leamington in Ontario and Sandusky. Summer tourism centres on Put-in-Bay's restaurants, bars, caves (including Crystal Cave and Perry's Cave) and the 352-foot Perry's Victory column.
