Johnny Lee Bench (born December 7, 1947) is an American former professional baseball catcher who played his entire Major League Baseball career, from 1967 to 1983, with the Cincinnati Reds. Widely considered the greatest catcher in baseball history, he was the backbone of the Reds' "Big Red Machine" teams that won consecutive World Series in 1975 and 1976.

Career

Bench burst into the majors as National League Rookie of the Year in 1968, revolutionising the catching position with one-handed catching, a powerful throwing arm and middle-of-the-order hitting. He won the National League Most Valuable Player Award in 1970 and 1972, led the league in home runs twice and in runs batted in three times, and won ten consecutive Gold Glove Awards. He hit 389 career home runs, a record for catchers at his retirement.

Legacy

Bench was elected to the Baseball Hall of Fame in 1989 on the first ballot. He was named to the Major League Baseball All-Century Team in 1999, and the Johnny Bench Award is presented annually to the top collegiate catcher in the United States.
