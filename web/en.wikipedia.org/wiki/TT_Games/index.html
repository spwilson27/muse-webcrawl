TT Games Limited is a British video game developer and publisher, a subsidiary of Warner Bros. Games, best known for the family-oriented Lego video games. It was formed in 2005 by the merger of developer Traveller's Tales with publisher Giant Interactive, and was acquired by Warner Bros. in 2007.

History

Traveller's Tales, founded in 1989 by Jon Burton, had built its reputation on technically ambitious titles such as Leander, Sonic 3D and movie tie-ins. Its 2005 game Lego Star Wars: The Video Game, published by Giant Interactive, was a surprise critical and commercial success, and the two companies merged the same year to form TT Games. Warner Bros. acquired the group in November 2007. Founder Jon Burton left the company in 2019.

Lego games

The studio's Lego titles combine slapstick, wordless storytelling with cooperative drop-in/drop-out play and puzzle-solving based on building and smashing Lego objects. The series has covered Star Wars (including the compilation Lego Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga, 2022), Batman, Indiana Jones, Harry Potter, Marvel Super Heroes, The Lord of the Rings, Jurassic World and The Lego Movie, selling well over 200 million copies in total. TT Games also developed the toys-to-life game Lego Dimensions (2015–2017).

Studios

The group is centred on Traveller's Tales in Knutsford, Cheshire, with sister studios including TT Fusion (Wilmslow) and TT Odyssey (Brighton), and has also developed non-Lego titles such as The Chronicles of Narnia games under its earlier structure.
