List of people executed in Texas

Texas has executed more people than any other U.S. state since the death penalty was reinstated in 1976 — well over 580 executions, roughly a third of the national total. Executions are carried out by lethal injection at the Huntsville Unit. Before 1923, executions were carried out by hanging at the county level; from 1923 the state used the electric chair at Huntsville, electrocuting 361 prisoners, beginning with Charles Reynolds in 1924 and ending with Joseph Johnson in 1964.

Modern era

During the governorship of George W. Bush (1995–2000), Texas executed 152 people. Under Governor Rick Perry, the state carried out a record 279 executions. Texas death row is housed at the Polunsky Unit in West Livingston, and condemned women at the Mountain View Unit in Gatesville.
