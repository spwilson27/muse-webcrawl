Automotive industry

The automotive industry comprises the companies and activities involved in the design, development, manufacturing, marketing and selling of motor vehicles. It is one of the world's largest industries by revenue, producing on the order of 80–95 million vehicles per year in recent decades.

The term auto industry is sometimes used more narrowly for the car-building sector covered on this page; see Automotive industry for the full article, including its history from the early manufacturers through mass production, the major producing countries (led by China, the United States and Japan), the largest manufacturers such as Toyota, Volkswagen Group and Hyundai-Kia, and the industry's transition toward electric vehicles.
