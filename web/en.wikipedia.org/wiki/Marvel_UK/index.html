Marvel UK

Marvel UK was a British publishing imprint of Marvel Comics, active mainly from 1972 through the 1990s. It reprinted American Marvel material in weekly black-and-white anthology comics for the British market and also created original characters and stories, most famously Captain Britain.

History

Marvel UK launched in 1972 with The Mighty World of Marvel, followed by Spider-Man Comics Weekly and The Avengers. British comics were traditionally weekly anthologies, so US stories were split into instalments and supplemented by British editorial features. In 1976 Marvel UK introduced Captain Britain, created by Chris Claremont and Herb Trimpe, in his own weekly comic.

Original material

Under editor Dez Skinn from 1978, the line expanded with titles such as Hulk Comic, Doctor Who Weekly (later Doctor Who Magazine), and Star Wars Weekly. The 1980s saw acclaimed original work: Alan Moore and Alan Davis on Captain Britain, and the creation of Death's Head, a robot bounty hunter from the Transformers comic who gained his own series. In the early 1990s Marvel UK launched an aggressive line of US-format titles (the "Genesis" line, including Knights of Pendragon, Warheads, Digitek, and Motormouth), which collapsed in the mid-1990s market downturn.

Legacy

Marvel UK's operations were largely wound down by the late 1990s, with Panini Comics taking over the UK licence for reprint titles. Its characters, including Captain Britain, remain part of the Marvel Universe, and its magazines laid the groundwork for the later Marvel Magazine Group titles.
