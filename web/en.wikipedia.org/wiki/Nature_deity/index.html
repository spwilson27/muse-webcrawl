Nature deity

A nature deity is a god or goddess in mythology associated with nature, natural phenomena, the earth, fertility, or particular places such as mountains, rivers and forests. Examples include Gaia and Demeter in Greek religion, Ceres in Roman religion, Pachamama in Andean tradition, Prithvi in Hinduism, and numerous kami in Shinto.

Themes

Nature deities often personify the sky, sun, moon, sea, storms, harvests and seasons, and may be worshipped to ensure fertility, rain and protection from natural disasters. Scholars relate them to animism and early agricultural religion.
