Magna International

Magna International Inc. is a Canadian parts manufacturer for automakers. It is one of the largest companies in Canada and was recognized by Forbes in the Global 2000. The company is the largest automobile parts manufacturer in North America by sales of original equipment parts, and one of the world's largest automotive suppliers.

Overview

Magna is headquartered in Aurora, Ontario. It was founded in 1957 by Frank Stronach as Multimatic Investments. Magna designs and manufactures automotive systems, assemblies, modules and components, and through its Magna Steyr subsidiary in Austria assembles complete vehicles under contract for manufacturers including Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Toyota and Jaguar Land Rover. The company employs well over 150,000 people across several hundred manufacturing facilities in dozens of countries.
