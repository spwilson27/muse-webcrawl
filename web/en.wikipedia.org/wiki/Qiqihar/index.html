Qiqihar (Chinese: 齐齐哈尔; Manchu: Cicigar) is the second-largest city in Heilongjiang Province in north-eastern China and a prefecture-level city on the Nen River. Founded as a fortified outpost in the 17th century, it served for a time as the capital of Heilongjiang.

Geography and economy

The city lies on flat, fertile plains and is a centre for heavy industry, including machinery and railway equipment. The surrounding wetlands of the Zhalong Nature Reserve are famous as a breeding habitat for the endangered red-crowned crane, symbol of the city.

History

Qiqihar was a garrison town under the Qing dynasty and grew with the arrival of the Chinese Eastern Railway. In 1931 it saw early fighting during the Japanese invasion of Manchuria, including resistance at the Nenjiang River Bridge.
