Juventus Football Club (from Latin iuventus, 'youth'), known colloquially as Juve, is an Italian professional football club based in Turin, Piedmont, that competes in Serie A, the top flight of Italian football. Founded in 1897 by a group of Torinese students, the club has worn its famous black-and-white striped kit since 1903 and has been owned, with brief interruptions, by the Agnelli family since 1923.

History

Juventus won its first league championship in 1905 and became a dominant force in the 1930s with five consecutive titles. Under coach Giovanni Trapattoni in the 1970s and 1980s, the club won numerous domestic titles and its first European Cup in 1985, a triumph overshadowed by the Heysel Stadium disaster before the final. Marcello Lippi's side won the UEFA Champions League in 1996.

In 2006 the club was relegated to Serie B for the first time in its history as a consequence of the Calciopoli scandal and stripped of two league titles. Returning immediately to Serie A, Juventus rebuilt and won nine consecutive league titles from 2011–12 to 2019–20 under Antonio Conte, Massimiliano Allegri, and Maurizio Sarri, moving in 2011 to the Juventus Stadium, the first club-owned modern stadium in Italy.

Honours

Juventus is Italy's most successful club by official trophies, with a record 36 official league titles (excluding the two revoked in 2006), a record number of Coppa Italia trophies, two European Cup/UEFA Champions League titles (1985, 1996), three UEFA Cups, and two Intercontinental Cups. It was the first club to win all three major UEFA club competitions.

Supporters and rivalries

Juventus has the largest fan base in Italy and one of the largest worldwide. Its principal rivalries are the Derby della Mole with Torino and the Derby d'Italia with Inter Milan.
