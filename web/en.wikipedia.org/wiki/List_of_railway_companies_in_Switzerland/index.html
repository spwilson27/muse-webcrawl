List of railway companies in Switzerland

This is a list of railway companies in Switzerland. Switzerland has one of the densest railway networks in the world, operated by the federal railway and a large number of private and cantonal companies, many of them narrow-gauge mountain railways.

Background

Although the Swiss Federal Railways dominate passenger traffic, dozens of so-called "private railways" — often majority-owned by cantons and municipalities — operate regional, suburban and mountain lines. Many cooperate in tariff alliances and in the national timetable. The network is almost entirely electrified, and Switzerland records the highest per-capita rail usage in Europe.
