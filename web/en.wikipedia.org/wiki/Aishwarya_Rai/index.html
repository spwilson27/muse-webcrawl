Aishwarya Rai Bachchan (born 1 November 1973) is an Indian actress who is primarily known for her work in Hindi and Tamil films. She won the Miss World 1994 pageant and established herself as one of the most popular and influential celebrities in India, receiving numerous accolades including two Filmfare Awards and the Padma Shri in 2009.

Career

After early modelling work, Rai made her acting debut in Mani Ratnam's Tamil film Iruvar (1997) and her Hindi debut in Aur Pyaar Ho Gaya the same year. Commercial and critical successes included Jeans (1998), Hum Dil De Chuke Sanam (1999), Taal (1999), Mohabbatein (2000), Devdas (2002), Chokher Bali (2003), Raincoat (2004), Dhoom 2 (2006), Guru (2007), Jodhaa Akbar (2008) and Ponniyin Selvan (2022–2023). She also acted in English-language films such as Bride and Prejudice (2004) and The Pink Panther 2 (2009).

Public image

Rai is a regular attendee of the Cannes Film Festival, a long-time brand ambassador for L'Oréal Paris, and has been a goodwill ambassador for UNAIDS. She is married to actor Abhishek Bachchan, with whom she has a daughter.
