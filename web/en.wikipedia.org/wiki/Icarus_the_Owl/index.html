Icarus the Owl

OriginPortland, Oregon, United States
GenresProgressive rock, post-hardcore, math rock
Years active2009–present
LabelsBlue Swan Records

Icarus the Owl is an American progressive rock band from Portland, Oregon, formed in 2009. The band is known for its technical, guitar-driven sound combining elements of post-hardcore, math rock and progressive rock, and is associated with the "swancore" scene centred on Blue Swan Records.

History

The band released its debut album, The Spotless Mind, in 2012, followed by the self-titled Icarus the Owl in 2014. Subsequent releases include Pilot Waves (2015) and Rearm Circuits (2017). The group has toured with other progressive post-hardcore acts and built a following for its intricate arrangements and melodic vocals.

Musical style

Icarus the Owl's music features complex time signatures, tapping guitar lines, and dynamic shifts between aggressive and melodic passages. Vocalist and guitarist Joey Rubenstein's singing is a defining element of the group's sound.

Discography

Studio albums include The Spotless Mind (2012), Icarus the Owl (2014), Pilot Waves (2015) and Rearm Circuits (2017).
