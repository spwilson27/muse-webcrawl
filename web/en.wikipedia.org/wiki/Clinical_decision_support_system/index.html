Clinical decision support system

FieldHealth informatics
PurposeAssist clinical decision-making

A clinical decision support system (CDSS) is a health information technology system designed to assist clinicians and other health professionals with decision-making tasks, such as diagnosis, prescribing and preventive care reminders.

Types

Knowledge-based systems apply rules derived from medical evidence to patient data, while non-knowledge-based systems use machine learning to find patterns in clinical data. Common functions include drug–drug interaction alerts, diagnostic suggestions, order sets and reminders for screening or vaccination.

Benefits and risks

CDSS tools can reduce errors and improve adherence to guidelines, but poorly designed alerts cause "alert fatigue", and systems can introduce new kinds of errors if not carefully implemented and integrated with electronic health records.
