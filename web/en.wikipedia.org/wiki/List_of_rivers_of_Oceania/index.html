List of rivers of Oceania

This is a list of rivers in Oceania, covering Australia, New Zealand, Papua New Guinea and the Pacific islands. The region's longest rivers are found in Australia (the Murray–Darling system) and in the great lowland swamps of New Guinea (the Sepik and Fly rivers).

Australia

  • Murray — 2,508 km; Darling — 1,545 km; Murrumbidgee — 1,485 km
  • Cooper Creek, Flinders, Fitzroy (Queensland), Burdekin, Ord, Fitzroy (WA), Swan, Derwent, Franklin, Gordon
  • See the full list: List of rivers of Australia

New Zealand

  • Waikato — 425 km; the longest river in New Zealand, flowing from Lake Taupo to the Tasman Sea
  • Clutha / Mata-Au — 322 km; the largest by discharge
  • Whanganui — 290 km; granted legal personhood in 2017
  • Taieri, Waimakariri, Rangitata, Rakaia, Waitaki, Buller, Grey, Clarence, Manawatu, Mohaka, Waiau

Papua New Guinea

  • Sepik — 1,126 km; one of the world's great wetland river systems
  • Fly River — 1,060 km; enormous discharge for its length, through a vast tidal estuary
  • Mamberamo — formed by the Tariku and Taritatu in Indonesian New Guinea
  • Ramu, Purari, Kikori, Digul, Markham, Strickland

Pacific islands

  • Rewa River (Fiji) — the widest river in Fiji, on Viti Levu; also the Sigatoka and Navua
  • Most smaller Pacific islands have only short perennial streams; examples include the rivers of Samoa, Vanuatu and the Solomon Islands
