Grammarly

[Image: Grammarly]

Grammarly is a Ukraine-founded cloud-based typing assistant that reviews spelling, grammar, punctuation, clarity, engagement, and delivery mistakes in English texts. It also detects plagiarism and suggests replacement words. The service was released in July 2009 by Grammarly Inc., which is headquartered in San Francisco, California.

History

Grammarly was founded by Max Lytvyn, Alex Shevchenko, and Dmytro Lider. Early versions were sold to universities to help students with writing and academic integrity. In 2017 the company raised $110 million in its first funding round, and a 2019 round valued it at more than $1 billion; a 2021 round raised its valuation to about $13 billion.

Products

Grammarly works through a browser extension, desktop and mobile applications, and integrations with word processors and email clients. The company has added generative AI features — marketed as GrammarlyGO in 2023 — to help draft and rewrite text.
