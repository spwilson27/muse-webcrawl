Oricon Singles Chart

Physical CD singles remain central to the Oricon Singles Chart.

The Oricon Singles Chart is the Japanese music industry standard singles popularity chart issued by Oricon. It ranks the best-selling physical singles in Japan, compiled from daily and weekly sales data reported by a network of retailers. The chart was first published on 4 January 1968, making it one of the longest-running national record charts in the world.

Methodology

Rankings are based on physical sales of CD singles (and historically vinyl and cassette). Oricon later introduced separate digital singles and streaming charts, and a Combined Singles Chart from December 2018 that merges physical sales, downloads and streaming. Weekly charts are released on Tuesdays.

Records

The best-selling single in Oricon history is "Oyoge! Taiyaki-kun" (1975), followed by Chage and Aska's "Say Yes" and SMAP's "Sekai ni Hitotsu Dake no Hana". Idol groups such as AKB48 and its sister groups have dominated the chart in the 2010s, frequently occupying all of the top annual positions with million-selling singles. Pink Lady, Southern All Stars, B'z and Mr. Children are among the acts with the most number-one singles.
