Bidzina Ivanishvili (Georgian: ბიძინა ივანიშვილი; born 18 February 1956) is a Georgian politician and billionaire businessman. He served as Prime Minister of Georgia from 25 October 2012 to 20 November 2013. Founder of the Georgian Dream coalition, he has remained widely regarded as the most powerful figure in Georgian politics after leaving formal office.

Business career

Born in the village of Chorvila, Ivanishvili studied engineering and economics in Tbilisi and Moscow. During the late Soviet and early post-Soviet period he built a business empire in Russia spanning banking (Rossiyskiy Kredit), metals, and real estate, amassing a fortune estimated by Forbes at several billion dollars, a large share of Georgia's GDP. He later sold much of his Russian assets and invested in Georgia, including large-scale philanthropy and infrastructure, through his Cartu Foundation.

Political career

Ivanishvili entered politics in 2011, founding Georgian Dream, which defeated Mikheil Saakashvili's United National Movement in the October 2012 parliamentary election. As prime minister he cohabited with President Saakashvili for a year before resigning in November 2013, saying his mission was complete. He later returned as party chairman (2018–2021) and became honorary chairman in 2023. Critics, including Western governments and Georgian opposition figures, accuse him of exercising informal, oligarchic control over state institutions and steering Georgia away from Euro-Atlantic integration — accusations he and his party deny; in 2024 he was sanctioned by the United States for undermining Georgia's democratic and Euro-Atlantic future.
