The Hot Boys, New Orleans rap group

The Hot Boys (often styled Hot Boy$) were an American hip hop group from New Orleans, Louisiana, formed in 1997 by Cash Money Records. The group consisted of Juvenile, Lil Wayne, B.G., and Turk. They were among the most commercially successful acts of Southern hip hop in the late 1990s and helped popularise the "bling bling" era of rap.

Career

The group released its debut album, Get It How U Live!, in 1997, selling strongly in the South as an independent release. Their major-label breakthrough came with Guerrilla Warfare (1999), which reached number 5 on the Billboard 200 and was certified platinum; it included the singles "We on Fire" and "I Need a Hot Girl". A third album, Let 'Em Burn, compiled largely from previously recorded material, was released in 2003 after the group's dissolution.

The Hot Boys broke up in the early 2000s as members left Cash Money over financial disputes, though Lil Wayne remained and became the label's flagship artist. The four members have reunited on stage several times since, including festival performances in the 2010s and 2020s, and members have periodically discussed a reunion album.
