Cars (film)

Directed byJohn Lasseter
Produced byPixar Animation Studios
Release date2006

Cars is a 2006 American computer-animated comedy film produced by Pixar Animation Studios and released by Walt Disney Pictures. Directed by John Lasseter, it is set in a world populated entirely by anthropomorphic vehicles.

Plot

Lightning McQueen, a hotshot rookie race car, is stranded in the run-down town of Radiator Springs on Route 66 while travelling to the Piston Cup championship in California. There he befriends the tow truck Mater, the Porsche Sally and the retired racer Doc Hudson, and learns that there is more to life than fame and winning.

Reception and legacy

The film was a major box-office success, grossing over 62 million worldwide, and spawned the sequels Cars 2 (2011) and Cars 3 (2017), the spin-off Planes films, and the Cars Land area at Disney California Adventure.
