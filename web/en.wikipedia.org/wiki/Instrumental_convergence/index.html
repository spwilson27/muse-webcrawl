Instrumental convergence

Instrumental convergence is the hypothesis that sufficiently advanced artificial agents with a wide range of possible terminal goals will nevertheless develop similar intermediate, instrumental goals, because these goals are useful for achieving almost any final objective.

Proposed convergent instrumental goals include self-preservation, goal-content integrity, cognitive enhancement, technological perfection, and resource acquisition. The idea was articulated by philosopher Nick Bostrom and is related to Steve Omohundro's "basic AI drives". It is a central concept in discussions of AI safety and the control problem.
