Butter chicken

Butter chicken, traditionally known as murgh makhani, is an Indian curry of chicken in a spiced tomato and butter (makhan) sauce. It originated in Delhi in the 1950s at the Moti Mahal restaurant, where leftover tandoori chicken was simmered in a rich tomato gravy with butter and cream. Its mild, creamy flavour made it one of the most popular Indian dishes worldwide.

Butter chicken
OriginDelhi, India (Moti Mahal, 1950s)
Main ingredientsChicken, tomato, butter, cream, spices
ServedHot, with naan or rice

Preparation

Chicken is marinated in yogurt and spices, roasted in a tandoor or pan, then simmered in a sauce of tomatoes, butter, cream, ginger, and garam masala, often finished with kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves). The related British dish chicken tikka masala is frequently compared with it.

Popularity

Butter chicken is a staple of Indian restaurants globally and of ready meals in Britain, North America, and Australia. In 2023–2024, the families of its claimed inventors, Kundan Lal Gujral and Kundan Lal Jaggi, litigated in Delhi over the dish's authorship.
