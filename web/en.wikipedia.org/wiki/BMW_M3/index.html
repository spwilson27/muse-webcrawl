BMW M3

BMW M3
ManufacturerBMW M GmbH
Production1986–present
ClassCompact executive sports car

The BMW M3 is a high-performance version of the BMW 3 Series, developed by BMW's in-house motorsport division, BMW M GmbH. It was introduced in 1986 as a homologation special for touring car racing and has been produced for every generation of the 3 Series since.

Generations

The original E30 M3 (1986–1991) used a 2.3-litre four-cylinder engine and became the most successful touring car of its era. Successive generations — E36, E46, E90/E92/E93 (the only V8 M3) and F80 (the first turbocharged M3) — followed. The current G80 generation, launched in 2021, uses the twin-turbocharged S58 straight-six and is also offered for the first time as an estate (M3 Touring).

Motorsport

The E30 M3 won numerous touring car championships including the DTM, the European Touring Car Championship and the World Touring Car Championship, as well as the 24 Hours Nürburgring and Spa endurance races.
