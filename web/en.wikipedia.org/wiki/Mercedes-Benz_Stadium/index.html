Mercedes-Benz Stadium

Mercedes-Benz Stadium
Location: Atlanta, Georgia, United States
Capacity: 71,000 (expandable)
Opened: August 26, 2017

Mercedes-Benz Stadium is a multi-purpose stadium located in Atlanta, Georgia, United States. Opened in August 2017 as a replacement for the Georgia Dome, it is the home stadium of the Atlanta Falcons of the National Football League (NFL) and Atlanta United FC of Major League Soccer (MLS).

Design

Designed by the architecture firm HOK, the stadium is noted for its retractable roof composed of eight translucent triangular panels that open and close like a camera aperture, inspired by the oculus of the Roman Pantheon. A 360-degree halo video board rings the roof opening. The stadium also features a large glass wall overlooking the Atlanta skyline and a 41-foot (13 m) steel falcon sculpture, among the largest freestanding bird sculptures in the world.

Events

The stadium hosted Super Bowl LIII in February 2019, in which the New England Patriots defeated the Los Angeles Rams. It regularly hosts the SEC Championship Game, the Peach Bowl, and the College Football Playoff National Championship (2018). Atlanta United won the MLS Cup there in 2018 before a then-record crowd. The stadium is scheduled to host matches of the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

Sustainability and concessions

Mercedes-Benz Stadium was the first professional sports stadium in North America to achieve LEED Platinum certification, featuring extensive solar panels, rainwater capture, and energy-efficient systems. It also drew attention for its "fan first" concession pricing, offering unusually low prices for food and beverages.
