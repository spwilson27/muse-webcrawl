Robert Clark Seger (born 6 May 1945) is an American retired singer, songwriter and musician. As a locally successful Detroit-area artist, he performed and recorded as Bob Seger and the Last Heard and the Bob Seger System in the 1960s. By the early 1970s he had broken through nationally with the Silver Bullet Band, with a decade of successful albums and hit singles built on a raspy voice and songs about working-class life, love and the road.

Career

Seger's mainstream breakthrough came with Beautiful Loser (1975), the live album Live Bullet (1976) and Night Moves (1976), whose title track became his signature song. Stranger in Town (1978) yielded "Still the Same" and "Old Time Rock and Roll", and Against the Wind (1980) reached number one on the Billboard 200 and won a Grammy. His 1987 single "Shakedown", from Beverly Hills Cop II, was a number-one hit.

Legacy

Seger was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2004 and the Songwriters Hall of Fame in 2012. His farewell tour concluded in 2019. He has sold more than 75 million records worldwide.
