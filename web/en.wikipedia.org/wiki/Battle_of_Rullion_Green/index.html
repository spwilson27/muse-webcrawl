Battle of Rullion Green

The Battle of Rullion Green was fought on 28 November 1666 in the Pentland Hills near Penicuik, Scotland, and ended the Pentland Rising of the Covenanters. A government army under General Thomas Dalyell defeated a force of around 900 Covenanter rebels. The rising had begun in Galloway in protest against the religious settlement imposed after the Restoration of Charles II.

The rising

The rising began at Dalry in Galloway when Covenanters rescued an elderly man from soldiers abusing him, and disarmed troops there. Gathering supporters, the rebels marched toward Edinburgh expecting wider support, but cold, desertion, and the closure of the city against them reduced their numbers to under a thousand.

The battle

Dalyell's force caught the rebels at Rullion Green in the late afternoon. The Covenanter foot repelled several cavalry attacks before being broken toward dusk; around 50 were killed and over 100 taken prisoner.

Aftermath

Prisoners were tortured with the boot to reveal information and more than thirty were hanged in Edinburgh and elsewhere; others were transported to Barbados. The defeat was followed by harsher persecution of Presbyterian dissent, remembered as part of the "Killing Time" of the 1680s.
