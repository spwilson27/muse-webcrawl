Super Bowl halftime show

The Super Bowl halftime show is a musical performance held during the halftime interval of the Super Bowl, the championship game of the National Football League (NFL). What began as modest entertainment featuring marching bands evolved into one of the most-watched musical events in the world, headlined by major recording artists performing elaborately staged medleys of their hits.

History

The earliest Super Bowl halftimes featured university marching bands, drill teams and acts such as Carol Channing and the novelty group Up with People. In the early 1990s, facing counterprogramming from rival networks — most famously Fox's In Living Color special during Super Bowl XXVI — the NFL booked major pop acts. New Kids on the Block appeared in 1991, and Michael Jackson's performance at Super Bowl XXVII in 1993 is widely credited with transforming the show into a marquee spectacle, dramatically boosting halftime ratings.

Notable performances

Headliners have included Diana Ross, Aerosmith, U2 (whose 2002 show paid tribute to the victims of the September 11 attacks), Paul McCartney, the Rolling Stones, Prince (whose rain-soaked 2007 performance is often rated the greatest halftime show), Bruce Springsteen, Madonna, Beyoncé, Bruno Mars, Coldplay, Lady Gaga, Shakira and Jennifer Lopez, Dr. Dre and a roster of hip hop artists (2022), Rihanna (2023), Usher (2024) and Kendrick Lamar (2025).

The 2004 show produced by MTV ended with the notorious "wardrobe malfunction" involving Janet Jackson and Justin Timberlake, which led to a large fine by the Federal Communications Commission, tighter broadcast censorship, and several years in which the NFL booked veteran rock acts rather than pop performers.

Economics and viewership

Headline performers are not paid a performance fee beyond union scale, but the exposure — routinely reaching more than 100 million viewers — drives large increases in music sales and streaming. Since 2013 the show has carried corporate sponsorship, first Pepsi and, from 2023, Apple Music.
