Savar

Savar (Bengali: সাভার) is an upazila (sub-district) of Dhaka District in Bangladesh, lying northwest of the capital Dhaka, of which it forms part of the greater metropolitan sprawl. It is best known internationally as the site of the National Martyrs' Memorial (Jatiyo Sriti Shoudho) and, tragically, of the Rana Plaza factory collapse of 2013.

Landmarks

The National Martyrs' Memorial, designed by architect Syed Mainul Hossain and completed in 1982, commemorates those killed in the Bangladesh Liberation War of 1971; its soaring folded-concrete planes rise from landscaped grounds with mass graves. Jahangirnagar University, a fully residential public university founded in 1970, has its campus in Savar, as do the Atomic Energy Research Establishment and the Centre for the Rehabilitation of the Paralysed.

Rana Plaza collapse

On 24 April 2013 the eight-storey Rana Plaza building in Savar, housing several garment factories, collapsed, killing 1,134 people and injuring around 2,500 — the deadliest structural failure in the garment industry's history. The disaster prompted global scrutiny of factory safety in Bangladesh's clothing sector and led to the Accord on Fire and Building Safety and major, if incomplete, reforms.

Economy

Savar hosts part of the Dhaka Export Processing Zone, tanneries (relocated from Hazaribagh), garment factories, dairy farming and agriculture serving the capital's markets.
