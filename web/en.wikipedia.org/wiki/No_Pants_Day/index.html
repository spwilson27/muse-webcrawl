No Pants Day

No Pants Day is an annual event held on the first Friday of May in which participants are encouraged to go about their day in public without wearing trousers (pants), typically wearing ordinary underwear and otherwise normal clothing, and to act as though nothing is unusual. It is distinct from the No Pants Subway Ride, a separate January event organised by Improv Everywhere in New York City since 2002.

Origins

No Pants Day originated in the mid-1980s at the University of Texas at Austin, associated with the campus humour society the Knights of the Occasional Table, and was promoted as a light-hearted end-of-term prank. The event later spread to other cities and countries through the internet.

Observance

The stated ethos is harmless public silliness: participants wear no trousers but keep the rest of their outfit ordinary, avoid offensive underwear, and do not draw attention to the stunt themselves, leaving bystanders to react. Organised gatherings have been held in cities across the United States, Canada, Australia and Europe.
