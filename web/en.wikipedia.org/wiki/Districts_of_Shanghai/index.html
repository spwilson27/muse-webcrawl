Districts of Shanghai

The municipality of Shanghai, a direct-controlled municipality of China, is divided into 16 districts. Having become the last county-level unit to be converted, Chongming County became Chongming District in 2016, completing the transition of Shanghai's subdivisions to districts.

Overview

The seven central districts form the historic urban core (Puxi and the Huangpu riverfront). Pudong New Area, established in 1990 on the east bank of the Huangpu River, is by far the largest district by population and contains the Lujiazui financial district. Chongming District covers Chongming Island and neighbouring islands at the mouth of the Yangtze.

Administration

Each district is governed by a district people's government and is further divided into subdistricts, towns, and townships. Shanghai's districts are county-level divisions despite the municipality itself being at province level.
