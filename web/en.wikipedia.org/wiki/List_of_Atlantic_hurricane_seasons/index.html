List of Atlantic hurricane seasons

This is a list of Atlantic hurricane seasons, covering tropical cyclone seasons in the Atlantic Ocean basin. The Atlantic hurricane season officially runs from June 1 to November 30 each year, when most tropical cyclones form in the North Atlantic. Records of Atlantic seasons date back to 1851, the start of the HURDAT database, and the list summarizes each season's activity, including the number of tropical storms, hurricanes, and major hurricanes, along with accumulated cyclone energy (ACE).

Overview

Seasonal activity varies greatly with climate patterns such as El Niño–Southern Oscillation and the Atlantic Multidecadal Oscillation. The most active season on record by named storms was 2020, with 30 named storms, while the hyperactive 2005 season produced 28 storms including hurricanes Katrina, Rita, and Wilma. Some of the costliest and deadliest seasons, including 2005, 2017, and 2022, are noted for major landfalling hurricanes in the United States and the Caribbean.
