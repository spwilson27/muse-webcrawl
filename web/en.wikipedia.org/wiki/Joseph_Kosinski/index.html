Joseph Kosinski (born May 3, 1974) is an American film director known for his use of computer graphics and his background in architecture. His films include Tron: Legacy (2010), Oblivion (2013), Only the Brave (2017) and Top Gun: Maverick (2022), the highest-grossing film of his career.

Early life and commercials

Kosinski grew up in Marshalltown, Iowa, studied mechanical engineering at Stanford and architecture at Columbia University's Graduate School of Architecture, Planning and Preservation. After teaching 3D modelling, he moved into commercials, directing CGI-driven spots for video games such as Gears of War ("Mad World") and Halo 3 ("Starry Night"), which attracted Hollywood attention.

Film career

His feature debut, Disney's Tron: Legacy (2010), grossed over $400 million worldwide and was noted for its digital de-aging of Jeff Bridges. He followed it with the Tom Cruise science-fiction film Oblivion (2013), based on his own graphic novel concept, and the firefighter drama Only the Brave (2017), about the Granite Mountain Hotshots.

Top Gun: Maverick (2022), his reunion with Cruise, used cameras mounted inside real F/A-18 cockpits, earned widespread acclaim and grossed nearly $1.5 billion, receiving six Academy Award nominations including Best Picture. Kosinski went on to direct the Formula One film F1 (2025) with Brad Pitt.
