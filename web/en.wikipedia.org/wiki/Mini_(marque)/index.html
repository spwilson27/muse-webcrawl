Mini (marque)

Mini (stylised as MINI) is a British automotive marque owned by BMW since 1994. Under BMW the nameplates of the classic Mini were revived as a premium small-car brand, with production centred at Plant Oxford in Cowley, England.

Modern range

The new MINI Hatch was launched in 2001, followed by the Convertible, Clubman estate, Countryman crossover and Paceman, together with high-performance John Cooper Works versions. Recent generations have added electric models, beginning with the Mini Electric (Cooper SE) in 2020, and the larger Aceman crossover.

History

BMW acquired the Mini name as part of its 1994 purchase of the Rover Group, retaining it after selling Rover in 2000. The marque's styling deliberately echoes Alec Issigonis's 1959 original.
