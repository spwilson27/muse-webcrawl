List of Marvel Cinematic Universe films

The Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) is an American media franchise and shared universe centered on a series of superhero films produced by Marvel Studios, based on characters from Marvel Comics. The films are grouped into "Phases" and "Sagas": the Infinity Saga (Phases One–Three) and the Multiverse Saga (Phases Four–Six).
