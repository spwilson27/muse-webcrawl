The Japan Rail Pass (ジャパンレールパス, Japan Rēru Pasu) is a rail pass sold by the Japan Railways Group (JR Group) exclusively to overseas visitors. It offers unlimited travel on most JR trains across Japan for a fixed period at a flat price, and is widely regarded as one of the most economical ways for tourists to travel around the country.

Eligibility and use

The pass is available to people visiting Japan from abroad under the entry status of "Temporary Visitor". Historically it was sold mainly as an exchange order through overseas travel agents, to be exchanged for the pass on arrival in Japan; since 2020 it can also be bought through the official JR website and at some stations, and seat reservations can be made online and at ticket machines. In March 2023 the long-running exchange-order sales through overseas agents ended in favour of the official channels, and substantial price increases took effect in October 2023.

Coverage

The pass covers JR lines nationwide, including most Shinkansen (bullet train) services — the fastest Nozomi and Mizuho trains became usable with payment of a supplementary fare after the 2023 revision — along with JR limited express, express, rapid and local trains, JR highway and local buses, and the JR ferry to Miyajima. It does not cover non-JR private railways or subways. Holders may reserve seats on most services at no extra charge.

Regional passes

Alongside the nationwide pass, the individual JR companies sell regional passes (such as the JR East Pass and JR Kansai Pass) covering smaller areas for shorter periods; some of these are also available to foreign residents of Japan, unlike the national pass.
