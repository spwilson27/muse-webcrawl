Pratt & Whitney Canada PT6

The Pratt & Whitney Canada PT6 is a turboprop aircraft engine produced by Pratt & Whitney Canada. First run in 1960 and entering service in 1964, it is one of the most popular turboprop engines in history, used in more than 100 aircraft types.

Design

The PT6 is a free-turbine engine with a reverse-flow gas path: air enters at the rear, is compressed by axial and centrifugal stages, and exhausts near the front, simplifying installation. Power ratings range from roughly 500 to over 2,000 shaft horsepower across variants such as the PT6A and the PT6C turboshaft for helicopters.

Applications

Well-known applications include the Beechcraft King Air, Cessna Caravan, Pilatus PC-12, de Havilland Canada Twin Otter and Air Tractor agricultural aircraft.
