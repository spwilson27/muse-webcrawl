Production I.G

Production I.G, Inc. is a Japanese animation studio founded on December 15, 1987, by producer Mitsuhisa Ishikawa and character designer Takayuki Goto as I.G Tatsunoko, a branch of Tatsunoko Production. The letters "I" and "G" derive from the initials of Ishikawa and Goto. The studio is headquartered in Musashino, Tokyo, and is a subsidiary of the holding company IG Port.


Production I.G is known for technically ambitious anime features.

Works

Production I.G is internationally known for Mamoru Oshii's Ghost in the Shell (1995) and its television sequels Stand Alone Complex, as well as Patlabor, Blood: The Last Vampire, Jin-Roh: The Wolf Brigade, Attack on Titan (produced by its sister studio Wit Studio in its early seasons' wider group context), Psycho-Pass, Haikyu!!, and Kuroko's Basketball. The studio also contributed the anime sequence to Quentin Tarantino's Kill Bill: Volume 1 and collaborated on The Animatrix.

Style and significance

The studio was an early adopter of digital compositing and computer graphics in anime, blending traditional cel-style animation with CGI. Its works are frequently cited for their detailed backgrounds, cinematic direction, and philosophical themes, and Ghost in the Shell in particular influenced science fiction cinema worldwide.
