The Fast Show, known as Brilliant in the United States, is a British sketch comedy television series created by Paul Whitehouse and Charlie Higson, which aired on BBC Two from 1994 to 1997, with specials in 2000 and later online revivals. Taking its title from its rapid-fire format, the show revolutionized British sketch comedy with short, punchy sketches built around recurring characters and catchphrases.

Format and characters

The programme dispensed with long setups, cutting sketches off at the punchline — sometimes within seconds. Memorable recurring characters included Ted and Ralph (the inhibited aristocrat and his taciturn estate worker), the "Suits You" tailors Ken and Kenneth, Swiss Toni, the car salesman who compared everything to making love to a beautiful woman, Jazz Club presenter Louis Balfour ("Nice!"), Rowley Birkin QC with his barely intelligible reminiscences ("I'm afraid I was very, very drunk"), the Channel 9 news team from a fictional Mediterranean country ("Scorchio!"), and the indecisive Arthur Atkinson. Catchphrases from the show — "Suits you, sir!", "Scorchio!", "Nice!", "Brilliant!", and "This week, I have been mostly eating..." — entered everyday British speech.

Cast and influence

The ensemble included Whitehouse, Higson, Simon Day, Arabella Weir, John Thomson, Mark Williams, Caroline Aherne, and Rhys Thomas. Johnny Depp, a notable fan, appeared in a 2000 special. The series won multiple awards, including BAFTAs for comedy, and its format influenced a generation of sketch shows. A stage tour and online-only series for Foster's lager followed in 2011.
