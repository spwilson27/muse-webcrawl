Julia Fiona Roberts (born 1967) is an American actress. One of the most bankable stars of the 1990s and 2000s, she is known for leading roles in films of many genres and received an Academy Award and three Golden Globe Awards.

Career

Roberts gained notice with Mystic Pizza (1988) and an Academy Award nomination for Steel Magnolias (1989). She became a global star with the romantic comedy Pretty Woman (1990), then one of the highest-grossing films of its kind. Through the 1990s she headlined successes including My Best Friend's Wedding, Notting Hill and Runaway Bride.

Erin Brockovich and later work

Her portrayal of the title character in Erin Brockovich (2000) won her the Academy Award for Best Actress, and she became the first actress paid $20 million for a film. Her later credits include Ocean's Eleven, Eat Pray Love, August: Osage County, Wonder and Ticket to Paradise.

Personal life

Roberts is married to cinematographer Daniel Moder, with whom she has three children. She is involved in humanitarian work, including with UNICEF.
