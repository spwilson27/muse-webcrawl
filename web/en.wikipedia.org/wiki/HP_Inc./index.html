HP Inc.

HP Inc. is an American multinational information technology company headquartered in Palo Alto, California. It was formed on 1 November 2015 when the original Hewlett-Packard Company split: HP Inc. retained the personal computer and printer businesses, while Hewlett Packard Enterprise took enterprise products and services.

Products

HP is one of the world's largest manufacturers of personal computers and printers, selling lines including Spectre, Envy, Pavilion, EliteBook and LaserJet. It adopted the historic "HP" branding and the New York Stock Exchange ticker HPQ.
