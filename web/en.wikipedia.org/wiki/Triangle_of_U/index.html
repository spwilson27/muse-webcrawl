Triangle of U

The Triangle of U is a theory in plant genetics describing the evolutionary relationships among the six species of the genus Brassica — the cabbages, mustards and rapes. Proposed by Korean-Japanese botanist U Nagaharu in 1935, it shows how three ancestral diploid species combined to form three allotetraploid species.

The model

The diploids are Brassica rapa (AA, turnip), B. nigra (BB, black mustard) and B. oleracea (CC, cabbage). Their pairwise hybrids doubled to give B. juncea (AABB, Indian mustard), B. napus (AACC, rapeseed/canola) and B. carinata (BBCC, Ethiopian mustard).

Significance

The model has been confirmed by cytogenetics and DNA analysis and guides Brassica breeding. See Brassica and Allopolyploidy.
