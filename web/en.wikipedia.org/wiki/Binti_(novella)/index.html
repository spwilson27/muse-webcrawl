Binti (novella)

Binti is a science fiction novella written by Nnedi Okorafor, published in 2015 by Tor.com. It is the first in the Binti trilogy, followed by Binti: Home (2017) and Binti: The Night Masquerade (2018).

Binti
Author: Nnedi Okorafor
Publisher: Tor.com
Published: 22 September 2015
Genre: Science fiction
Awards: Hugo Award for Best Novella (2016), Nebula Award for Best Novella (2015)

Plot

Binti Ebele, a gifted teenage Himba mathematician from Namibia, is the first of her people accepted to Oomza University, a prestigious interstellar university. Leaving Earth secretly, her ship is attacked by the jellyfish-like Meduse, who kill everyone else aboard. Binti survives through an ancient device, an edan, and her mathematical ability, and negotiates peace, transforming herself in the process.

Themes and reception

The novella explores identity, belonging, cultural negotiation and transformation, blending African culture with space opera. It won both the Hugo and Nebula awards for best novella and helped popularise Africanfuturism. A television adaptation has been in development.
