Honiton

Honiton is a market town and civil parish in East Devon, England, on the River Otter, known historically for lace making. It lies on the A30 and A35 roads and has a station on the West of England line to Exeter and London Waterloo.

Lace

Honiton lace, a fine bobbin lace introduced by Flemish immigrants in the 16th century, became famous when Queen Victoria's wedding dress (1840) used Honiton lace, reviving the trade. The town's Allhallows Museum displays notable examples.

Other features

Honiton was a Saxon settlement and later a coaching stop; its wide High Street reflects rebuilding after fires. A traditional hot pennies ceremony, dating to charter fairs, is still held each July.

References: Devon county histories; Allhallows Museum.
