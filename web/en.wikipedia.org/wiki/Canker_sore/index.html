Canker sore

A canker sore, or aphthous ulcer, is a small, painful ulcer that forms on the soft tissues inside the mouth. Canker sores are not contagious and usually heal within one to two weeks without scarring.

Medical name: Aphthous stomatitis
Symptoms: Painful round oral ulcer
Duration: 7-14 days

Causes

The exact cause is unclear; triggers can include minor trauma, stress, acidic foods and nutritional deficiencies. Recurrent cases are called recurrent aphthous stomatitis.

Treatment

Most sores need no treatment. Pain can be eased with protective pastes, topical treatments and avoiding irritating foods.
