Shanghai

Shanghai is a direct-controlled municipality and the most populous urban area in China, with an urban population above 24 million (approximate). Located on the southern estuary of the Yangtze River on the East China Sea coast, it is a global centre for finance, business, trade, technology and shipping — its port is the world's busiest container port.

Shanghai rose as a treaty port after 1842, developing the Bund's colonial waterfront and international concessions; its skyline icon is now Pudong's Lujiazui towers (Oriental Pearl Tower, Shanghai Tower — China's tallest building). The municipality hosted Expo 2010 and remains a symbol of China's economic opening since the 1990s.
