1982–83 European Cup

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The 1982–83 European Cup was the 28th season of the European Cup, UEFA's premier club football tournament for the champion clubs of its member associations. It was won for the first time by Hamburger SV of West Germany, ending a six-year run of English winners.

Final

The final was held at the Olympic Stadium in Athens, Greece, on 25 May 1983. Hamburg, coached by Ernst Happel, faced Michel Platini's Juventus, a side containing most of Italy's 1982 World Cup-winning team, and were widely considered underdogs. Felix Magath scored the game's only goal in the 9th minute, a powerful shot from distance, and goalkeeper Uli Stein and the Hamburg defence withstood heavy pressure to secure a 1–0 victory.

Route to the final

Hamburg eliminated Dinamo Berlin, Olympiacos, Dynamo Kyiv, and Real Sociedad on the way to Athens. Juventus, champions of Italy, beat Hvidovre, Standard Liège, holders Aston Villa, and Widzew Łódź. The English champions' defeat of Aston Villa's defence of the trophy in the quarter-finals marked the end of England's dominance of the competition, which had produced every winner from 1977 to 1982.

Top scorers

Paolo Rossi of Juventus finished as the tournament's leading scorer, adding to his Golden Boot performance at the previous summer's World Cup.
