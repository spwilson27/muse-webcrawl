McMaster University is a public research university in Hamilton, Ontario, Canada. Founded in 1887 in Toronto under Baptist auspices with a bequest from Senator William McMaster, it moved to Hamilton in 1930. It is a member of the U15 group of Canadian research-intensive universities.

Academics

The university is organised into six faculties: Engineering, Health Sciences, Humanities, Science, Social Sciences and the DeGroote School of Business. Its Michael G. DeGroote School of Medicine is known for pioneering problem-based learning, a curriculum approach now used by medical schools worldwide.

Research

McMaster hosts the McMaster Nuclear Reactor, one of the largest university research reactors in the Commonwealth, and is a major producer of medical isotopes. The Bertrand Russell Archives are also held by the university library.
