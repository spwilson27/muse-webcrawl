Daredevil (Marvel Comics character)

Daredevil is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer-editor Stan Lee and artist Bill Everett, with an unspecified amount of input from Jack Kirby, the character first appeared in Daredevil #1 (April 1964). Daredevil is the alias of Matthew Michael Murdock, a blind lawyer from Hell's Kitchen, New York City, whose remaining senses were heightened to superhuman levels by a childhood accident involving radioactive material.

Publication history

Daredevil debuted in 1964, one of the wave of new heroes Marvel introduced in the Silver Age. The character's early adventures were written by Stan Lee, but the title found its defining voice in the late 1970s and early 1980s under writer-artist Frank Miller, whose run introduced Elektra, expanded the role of the Kingpin, and retold Daredevil's origin in The Man Without Fear. Later acclaimed runs include those by Brian Michael Bendis and Alex Maleev, Ed Brubaker, Mark Waid, and Chip Zdarsky.

Fictional character biography

Matt Murdock was raised in Hell's Kitchen by his father, boxer "Battlin' Jack" Murdock. As a boy, Matt pushed an elderly man out of the path of an oncoming truck; radioactive isotopes spilled from the vehicle, blinding him but heightening his other senses and granting him a "radar sense." After his father was murdered by mobsters for refusing to throw a fight, Murdock trained under the blind master Stick and became a lawyer by day and the vigilante Daredevil by night. His archenemies include the crime lord Wilson Fisk, the Kingpin, and the assassin Bullseye. His great love, Elektra Natchios, was killed by Bullseye in one of the most famous stories of the Miller era.

Powers and abilities

Although blind, Daredevil's four remaining senses function with superhuman accuracy, and his radar sense gives him a 360-degree awareness of his surroundings. He is a master of boxing, ninjutsu, and other martial arts, and wields a billy club that can split into nunchaku or extend a cable.

In other media

Daredevil was portrayed by Ben Affleck in the 2003 film Daredevil, and by Charlie Cox in the Marvel Television series Daredevil (2015–2018) and subsequent Marvel Cinematic Universe appearances, including Daredevil: Born Again.
