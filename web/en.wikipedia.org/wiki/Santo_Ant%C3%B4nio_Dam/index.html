Santo Antônio Dam

Santo Antônio Dam
Location: Madeira River, Rondônia, Brazil
Installed capacity: about 3,568 MW
Completed: 2016

The Santo Antônio Dam is a hydroelectric dam on the Madeira River near Porto Velho in Rondônia, Brazil. It is a run-of-the-river plant using bulb turbines suited to the river's high flow and low head.

Construction

Built by the Santo Antônio Energia consortium, construction began in 2008 and the plant was completed in 2016, with its final generating units taking installed capacity to roughly 3,568 MW, among Brazil's largest hydroelectric stations. Together with the nearby Jirau Dam it forms the Madeira River hydroelectric complex.

Impacts and controversy

The project drew criticism over flooding of rainforest, effects on migratory catfish and fisheries, sedimentation, and impacts on riverside and indigenous communities. A serious flood in 2014 on the Madeira worsened flooding upstream, fuelling disputes about the dams' role. Its reservoir is comparatively small for its output because of the run-of-river design.
