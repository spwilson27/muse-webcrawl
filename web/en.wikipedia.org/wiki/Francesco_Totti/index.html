Francesco Totti

Francesco Totti (born 27 September 1976) is an Italian former professional footballer who played as an attacking midfielder and forward, spending his entire club career with AS Roma. Regarded as one of the greatest Italian players, he captained Roma for a record span and is the club's all-time top scorer and appearance holder.

Club career

Totti debuted for Roma in 1993 aged 16 and went on to make 786 appearances, scoring 307 goals. He won Serie A in 2000–01, two Coppa Italia titles and two Supercoppa Italiana titles. Playing largely as a trequartista and later a false nine, he became Serie A's second-highest scorer of all time with 250 league goals. He retired in 2017 after 25 seasons with one club.

International career

Totti won 58 caps for Italy, scoring 9 goals. He starred at Euro 2000, where Italy reached the final, and was part of the squad that won the 2006 FIFA World Cup, contributing a decisive penalty against Australia and numerous assists.

Legacy

Nicknamed "Il Capitano" and "Er Pupone", Totti is a symbol of loyalty in modern football; his number 10 shirt was retired by Roma in his honour.
