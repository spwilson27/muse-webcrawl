DNA Data Bank of Japan

The DNA Data Bank of Japan (DDBJ) is a biological database that collects DNA sequences. It is located at the National Institute of Genetics (NIG) in Mishima, Shizuoka, Japan. It is also a member of the International Nucleotide Sequence Database Collaboration (INSDC) along with the European Bioinformatics Institute (EBI, EMBL-Bank) and the National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI, GenBank).

DDBJ began as a database in 1987 at NIG and remains the sole nucleotide sequence data bank in Asia. It exchanges data daily with its INSDC partners, so the three databases hold essentially the same collection of sequence records, each with its own accession numbering conventions.

Services

DDBJ provides sequence submission systems including DDBJ Web Submission and the Mass Submission System, the Sequence Read Archive (DRA) for high-throughput sequencing data, BioProject and BioSample databases, and analysis tools such as BLAST and ClustalW. It also operates the DDBJ Omics Archive and the Japanese Genotype-phenotype Archive (JGA) for personally identifiable genomic data under controlled access.
