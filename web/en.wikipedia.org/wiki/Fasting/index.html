Fasting
DefinitionWilling abstinence from some or all food and drink for a period
ContextsReligious (Ramadan, Lent, Yom Kippur), medical, intermittent fasting diets
[Image: An empty plate with a fork on a table beside a glass of water at dusk]

Fasting is the willing abstention from some or all food and drink for a set period. It is an ancient religious practice — Ramadan in Islam, Lent in Christianity, Yom Kippur in Judaism, fasts in Buddhist and Hindu traditions — and a modern health fashion in intermittent forms (16:8, 5:2).

Physiology

After glycogen stores deplete, the body shifts to fat metabolism and ketone production; research probes effects on insulin sensitivity, autophagy and longevity, with evidence still young. Fasting before surgery and blood tests is routine medicine; eating disorders are its dangerous shadow.
