BT Group

BT Group plc (trading as BT and formerly British Telecom) is a British multinational telecommunications holding company headquartered in London, England. It has operations in around 180 countries and is the largest provider of fixed-line, broadband and mobile services in the United Kingdom, and also provides subscription television and IT services.

History

BT's origins lie in the Electric Telegraph Company, founded in 1846, and the state telephone network operated by the General Post Office. British Telecommunications was established as a statutory corporation in 1981, separated from the Post Office, and was privatised by the Thatcher government in 1984 in one of the largest share flotations of the era.

Operations

The group includes Openreach, which operates the UK's access network on behalf of competing providers; the consumer brands BT, EE and Plusnet; BT Business; and BT Wholesale. The company operates the iconic BT Tower in London and is a constituent of the FTSE 100 Index.
