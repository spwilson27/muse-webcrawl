Champaner-Pavagadh Archaeological Park

The Champaner-Pavagadh Archaeological Park is a UNESCO World Heritage Site in Panchmahal district, Gujarat, India. It preserves the largely unexcavated city of Champaner, founded by Vanraj Chavda in the 8th century and made capital of the Gujarat Sultanate by Mahmud Begada in 1484.

Features

The park includes the hill fortress of Pavagadh with the Kalika Mata Temple, mosques such as the Jama Masjid and Kevada Masjid, palaces, tombs, stepwells and fortifications spanning Hindu, Jain and Islamic architecture from the 8th to 16th centuries.
