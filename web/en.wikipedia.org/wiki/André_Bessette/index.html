André Bessette

André Bessette, CSC (August 9, 1845 – January 6, 1937), commonly known as Brother André (French: Frère André), was a lay brother of the Congregation of Holy Cross and a significant figure of the Catholic Church in Quebec. Credited with thousands of reported miraculous healings, he founded Saint Joseph's Oratory of Mount Royal in Montreal, Canada's largest church. He was canonized by Pope Benedict XVI on October 17, 2010, becoming the first Canadian-born male saint.

Early life

Alfred Bessette was born in Mont-Saint-Grégoire, Canada East (now Quebec), into a working-class French-Canadian family. Orphaned by age twelve and plagued by chronic ill health and a weak stomach, he worked at various jobs in Quebec and in textile mills in New England. In 1870 he entered the novitiate of the Congregation of Holy Cross in Montreal, taking the name André. Because of his poor health and limited literacy, he was initially nearly dismissed, but was accepted after the intervention of Archbishop Ignace Bourget.

Ministry

Brother André was assigned as doorkeeper (porter) at Collège Notre-Dame in Montreal, a humble post he held for some forty years. He developed a reputation for healing the sick who visited him, which he attributed entirely to prayer to Saint Joseph and to anointing with oil from a lamp burning before a statue of the saint. As his fame spread, thousands of pilgrims came to see him, and discarded crutches and canes accumulated at the college.

Saint Joseph's Oratory

Brother André dreamed of building a shrine to Saint Joseph on Mount Royal. In 1904 he began with a small chapel, funded by donations, on land across from the college. The project grew over decades into Saint Joseph's Oratory of Mount Royal, one of the world's great pilgrimage churches. Brother André died in 1937, before its completion; an estimated one million people viewed his body during the week of mourning. The Oratory, completed in 1967, receives some two million visitors annually, and its dome is the highest point in Montreal.

Canonization

Brother André was beatified by Pope John Paul II on May 23, 1982, and canonized by Pope Benedict XVI on October 17, 2010, after the recognition of a miraculous healing. His feast day is January 6.
