N95 respirator

An N95 respirator is a disposable filtering facepiece respirator that meets the U.S. NIOSH N95 classification, filtering at least 95% of airborne particles with a mass median diameter of 0.3 micrometres, and is not resistant to oil.

N95s are widely used in healthcare and industry to protect against particulates including dusts, and infectious aerosols. Fit is essential; unlike loose surgical masks, a properly fitted N95 seals to the face. They became prominent worldwide during the COVID-19 pandemic.

History

The modern N95 design derives from work by Peter Tsai in the 1990s on electrostatically charged melt-blown fabric.
