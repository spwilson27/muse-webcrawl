Seongnam
Country: South Korea
Region: Gyeonggi Province
Population: about 920,000

Seongnam is a city in Gyeonggi Province, South Korea, immediately southeast of Seoul. It is one of the largest cities in the province and a major residential and industrial centre in the Seoul Capital Area. The city is famous for Bundang, a planned new town built in the 1990s to relieve housing pressure in Seoul, and for Pangyo Techno Valley, a hub of South Korean technology companies.

History

Seongnam developed rapidly from the late 1960s, when the government relocated residents displaced by redevelopment projects in Seoul to the area, then known as Gwangju-daedanji. It was incorporated as a city in 1973. The Bundang district was developed as one of the first-generation new towns in the early 1990s, followed by Pangyo in the 2000s.

Districts

The city is divided into three districts (gu): Sujeong-gu, Jungwon-gu, and Bundang-gu. Bundang is known for its affluent residential areas, the large Bundang Central Park, and the headquarters of companies such as Naver and Kakao in the Pangyo area.

Transport

Seongnam is served by several subway lines including the Bundang Line, Shinbundang Line, and Gyeonggang Line, connecting it to Seoul and other parts of Gyeonggi Province.
