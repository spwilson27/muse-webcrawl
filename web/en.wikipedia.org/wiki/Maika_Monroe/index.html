Maika Monroe

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Maika Monroe (born Dillon Monroe Buckley; 29 May 1993) is an American actress. She became known as a modern "scream queen" through her leading roles in the horror films The Guest (2014) and It Follows (2014), and later starred in the science fiction sequel Independence Day: Resurgence (2016), the thriller Watcher (2022) and the horror hit Longlegs (2024).

Early life

Monroe was born in Santa Barbara, California. Before acting full-time she pursued professional kiteboarding, training in the Dominican Republic, before giving it up after an injury and in light of her acting commitments.

Career

After early roles in At Any Price (2012), Labor Day (2013) and The Bling Ring (2013), Monroe's breakthrough came in 2014 with The Guest and David Robert Mitchell's It Follows, in which her performance as Jay Height was widely praised. Subsequent work has included The 5th Wave (2016), Tau (2018), Greta (2018), Honey Boy (2019), Villains (2019), The Stranger (2020) and Significant Other (2022). Her turn as an FBI agent in Osgood Perkins's Longlegs (2024) was a critical and commercial success.
