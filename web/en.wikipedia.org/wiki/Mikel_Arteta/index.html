Mikel Arteta Amatriain (born 26 March 1982) is a Spanish professional football manager and former player who is the manager of Arsenal. A midfielder, he came through Barcelona's La Masia academy and played for Paris Saint-Germain, Rangers, Real Sociedad, Everton, and Arsenal, captaining the latter two clubs. He won the FA Cup twice with Arsenal as a player.

Playing career

Born in San Sebastián in the Basque Country, Arteta joined Barcelona's youth academy alongside future stars such as Xavi and Andrés Iniesta, but could not break into the first team and was loaned to Paris Saint-Germain, where he won the 2001 UEFA Intertoto Cup. He then joined Rangers, winning a domestic treble in 2002–03, before a brief return to his hometown club Real Sociedad.

Arteta found sustained success in England with Everton, whom he joined in 2005 and where he was twice named the club's Player of the Season. In 2011 he moved to Arsenal, becoming club captain in 2014 and lifting the FA Cup in 2014 and 2015 before retiring in 2016. Despite youth international honours with Spain, he never won a senior cap.

Managerial career

On retirement, Arteta joined Pep Guardiola's coaching staff at Manchester City, winning two Premier League titles as assistant coach. In December 2019 he returned to Arsenal as head coach (later manager), winning the FA Cup in his first season, in 2020. After rebuilding the squad around young players, he led Arsenal to consecutive Premier League title challenges, finishing second in 2022–23 and 2023–24 behind Manchester City, and established the club as regulars in the UEFA Champions League again.

Honours

As a player

  • Paris Saint-Germain: UEFA Intertoto Cup (2001)
  • Rangers: Scottish Premier League, Scottish Cup, Scottish League Cup (2002–03)
  • Arsenal: FA Cup (2013–14, 2014–15), FA Community Shield (2014, 2015)

As a manager

  • Arsenal: FA Cup (2019–20), FA Community Shield (2020, 2023)
