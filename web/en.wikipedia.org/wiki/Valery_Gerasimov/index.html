Valery Gerasimov

Valery Vasilyevich Gerasimov (born 8 September 1955) is a Russian army general serving as the Chief of the General Staff of the Russian Armed Forces and first deputy Minister of Defence. He has also been commander of the Russian forces in Ukraine since January 2023.

Career

Gerasimov was born in Kazan and graduated from the Kazan Higher Tank Command School in 1977, later attending the Malinovsky Military Armored Forces Academy and the General Staff Academy. He served in the Northern Group of Forces in Poland, the Far Eastern Military District, and commanded the 58th Army in the North Caucasus during the Second Chechen War. He commanded the Leningrad and Moscow Military Districts and became Deputy Chief of the General Staff in 2010.

Chief of the General Staff

Gerasimov was appointed Chief of the General Staff by President Vladimir Putin in November 2012, succeeding Nikolay Makarov. He is associated with the informal Western term "Gerasimov doctrine". In January 2023 he replaced Sergey Surovikin as overall commander of the joint group of forces in the Russian invasion of Ukraine.
