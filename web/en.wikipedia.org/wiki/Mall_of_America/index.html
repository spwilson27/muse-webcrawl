The Mall of America (MOA) is a large shopping mall located in Bloomington, Minnesota, United States. Lying south of the Twin Cities metropolitan area, it opened on August 11, 1992, on the site of the former Metropolitan Stadium, where the Minnesota Vikings and Minnesota Twins had played before moving to the Hubert H. Humphrey Metrodome. The mall is owned and managed by the Triple Five Group.

Upon opening, the Mall of America was the largest shopping mall in the United States in terms of total floor area, and it remains one of the largest malls in the world. It receives roughly 40 million visitors annually, making it one of the most visited tourist destinations in the United States.

Attractions

At the center of the mall is Nickelodeon Universe, a 7-acre (2.8 ha) indoor theme park with roller coasters and other rides, originally opened as Camp Snoopy. Other attractions include the Sea Life Minnesota Aquarium, a miniature golf course, a comedy club, and a large LEGO store. The mall has also hosted concerts, book signings, and other special events.

History

The concept was developed by the Ghermezian brothers' Triple Five Group, which had previously built the West Edmonton Mall in Alberta, Canada. Groundbreaking took place in 1989. A major expansion, sometimes called Phase II, added a Radisson Blu hotel (opened 2013), a JW Marriott hotel (2015), additional retail space, and an office tower.

Transit

The mall is a major transit hub for the Minneapolis–Saint Paul area. The Metro Blue Line light rail connects it to downtown Minneapolis and Minneapolis–Saint Paul International Airport, which lies just north of the mall.
