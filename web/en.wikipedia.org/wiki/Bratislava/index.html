Bratislava

Bratislava is the capital and largest city of Slovakia, with about 475,000 residents in the city and 660,000 in the metropolitan area. It is located in southwestern Slovakia, on both banks of the Danube and at the foot of the Little Carpathians, bordering Austria and Hungary — the only national capital in the world that borders two other countries.

Bratislava, historically Pressburg/Pozsony, was the capital of the Kingdom of Hungary from 1536 to 1783, when Budapest was unsafe under Ottoman threat, and the coronation city of Hungarian kings and queens (Maria Theresa among them) in St Martin's Cathedral. Its compact Old Town lies beneath Bratislava Castle. Since Slovak independence in 1993 and EU accession, the city has boomed as part of the Vienna-Bratislava twin-city region, just 60 kilometres apart.
