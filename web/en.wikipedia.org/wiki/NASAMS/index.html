NASAMS

Type: Surface-to-air missile system
Origin: Norway / United States
Manufacturer: Kongsberg Defence & Aerospace / Raytheon
Missiles: AIM-120 AMRAAM, AIM-9X, IRIS-T SLS
In service: 1994–present

NASAMS (Norwegian Advanced Surface-to-Air Missile System, also National Advanced Surface-to-Air Missile System) is a distributed and networked short- to medium-range ground-based air defence system developed by Kongsberg Defence & Aerospace and Raytheon. It was the first surface-based application of the AIM-120 AMRAAM air-to-air missile.

Design

A NASAMS battery consists of a fire distribution centre, AN/MPQ-64 Sentinel radar, electro-optical sensors and several launchers, all linked by a real-time network so that components can be widely dispersed. The system can fire the AMRAAM, the extended-range AMRAAM-ER, the AIM-9X Sidewinder and the IRIS-T SLS, giving it flexibility against aircraft, drones and cruise missiles.

Service

NASAMS entered service with the Royal Norwegian Air Force in the 1990s. It has been exported to several countries including the United States — where it helps protect Washington, D.C. — Spain, the Netherlands, Finland, Hungary, Lithuania and others. From 2022, NASAMS batteries supplied by the United States and Canada entered combat use in Ukraine against Russian air and missile attacks. The improved NASAMS 3 adds a mobile launcher and updated command systems.
