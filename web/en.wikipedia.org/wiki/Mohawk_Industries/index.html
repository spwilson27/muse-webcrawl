Mohawk Industries

Mohawk Industries, Inc. is an American flooring manufacturer headquartered in Calhoun, Georgia. It is the world's largest flooring company, producing carpet, rugs, ceramic tile, laminate, wood, stone and luxury vinyl flooring for residential and commercial markets. The company is listed on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol MHK and is a component of the S&P 500.

History

The company traces its origins to 1878, when the Shuttleworth brothers installed looms in Amsterdam, New York, under the Mohawk name. The modern Mohawk Industries was formed from textile operations in the southeastern United States and grew through a series of acquisitions beginning in the late 1980s, under the leadership of David Kolb and later Jeffrey Lorberbaum.

Acquisitions and growth

Mohawk expanded from carpet into hard-surface flooring by acquiring Dal-Tile (2002), one of the largest ceramic tile makers in North America; Unilin (2005), the Belgian maker of Quick-Step laminate flooring; and the Pergo brand (2013). It also added the Marazzi Group of Italy and operations in Australia, Brazil, Europe and elsewhere, building a global manufacturing and distribution footprint.

Operations

The company reports segments including Global Ceramic, Flooring North America and Flooring Rest of the World, with annual revenues of roughly US$11 billion in recent years. Its brands include Mohawk, Karastan, Ralph Lauren, Daltile, American Olean, Marazzi, Quick-Step, Pergo and IVC.
