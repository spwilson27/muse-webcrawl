Stoner 63

Type: Modular weapon system
Designer: Eugene Stoner
Manufacturer: Cadillac Gage
Cartridge: 5.56×45mm

The Stoner 63, also known as the XM207, is a modular 5.56×45mm weapon system designed by Eugene Stoner in the early 1960s. From a common receiver, it could be configured as a rifle, carbine, belt-fed light machine gun, or fixed vehicle weapon.

Service

Though never adopted at scale by the U.S. Army, the Stoner 63 was famously used by U.S. Navy SEALs in Vietnam, who prized the light machine gun version's firepower. Production by Cadillac Gage ran from 1963 to 1971, with roughly 3,500 units made.

Legacy

Its modularity anticipated later modular rifle concepts, and derivatives such as the RobArm M96 and Knights Armament LAMG/Stoner 96 draw on its design.
