Jurassic Park (franchise)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

[Image: Jurassic Park franchise logo]

Jurassic Park, later also promoted as Jurassic World, is an American science fiction media franchise centred on a disastrous attempt to create a theme park of cloned dinosaurs. It began with Michael Crichton's 1990 novel Jurassic Park and Steven Spielberg's blockbuster 1993 film adaptation, and has grown into one of the highest-grossing media franchises of all time.

Films

The film series comprises Jurassic Park (1993), The Lost World: Jurassic Park (1997), Jurassic Park III (2001), Jurassic World (2015), Jurassic World: Fallen Kingdom (2018), Jurassic World Dominion (2022), and Jurassic World Rebirth (2025). Together the films have grossed over $6 billion worldwide.

Other media

The franchise includes video games, theme park attractions at Universal Studios parks, toys, comics, and the animated series Jurassic World Camp Cretaceous and Jurassic World: Chaos Theory.
