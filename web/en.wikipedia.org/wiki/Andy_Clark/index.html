Andy Clark, FBA (born 1957) is a British philosopher and cognitive scientist whose work explores the relationship between mind, body and technology. He is Professor of Cognitive Philosophy at the University of Sussex, having previously held posts at the University of Edinburgh and Washington University in St. Louis.

Work

With David Chalmers, Clark co-authored the influential 1998 paper "The Extended Mind", which argues that external objects such as notebooks can form part of the machinery of an individual's cognitive processes, provided they play the right functional role. His books include Being There: Putting Brain, Body, and World Together Again (1997), Natural-Born Cyborgs (2003) and Surfing Uncertainty: Prediction, Action, and the Embodied Mind (2016).

In later work Clark has developed an account of the brain as a prediction machine, drawing on the Bayesian brain hypothesis and predictive processing: perception and action are explained as the minimisation of prediction error through hierarchical inference. His 2023 book The Experience Machine: How Our Minds Predict and Shape Reality presents this view for a general audience.
