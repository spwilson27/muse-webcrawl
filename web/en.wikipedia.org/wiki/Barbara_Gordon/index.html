Barbara Gordon is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Gardner Fox and artist Carmine Infantino, she debuted in Detective Comics #359 (January 1967) as Batgirl. She is the daughter of Gotham City police commissioner James Gordon and one of Batman's closest allies.

Fictional biography

Barbara, a librarian with a doctorate in library science and an eidetic memory, fashioned her own Batgirl costume and began fighting crime independently before Batman formally accepted her as a partner. In the 1988 graphic novel Batman: The Killing Joke, the Joker shot her through the spine, leaving her paraplegic. Rather than ending her heroic career, Barbara reinvented herself as Oracle, an information broker and master hacker who coordinated heroes worldwide and founded the Birds of Prey team.

New 52 and beyond

In DC's 2011 New 52 relaunch, experimental surgery restored Barbara's mobility and she resumed the Batgirl identity, a development debated by readers who valued Oracle's representation of disability. Later storylines have allowed her to operate in both roles. The character has appeared in numerous animated series, films, and video games, voiced most famously by Tara Strong and played in live action by Yvonne Craig in the 1960s Batman television series.
