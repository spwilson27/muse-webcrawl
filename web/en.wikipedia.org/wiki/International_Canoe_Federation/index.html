International Canoe Federation

The International Canoe Federation (ICF) is the umbrella organization of all national canoe organizations worldwide. It was founded in 1924 in Copenhagen, Denmark, as the Internationale Repräsentantenschaft Kanusport (IRK). Its headquarters are in Lausanne, Switzerland.

Disciplines

The ICF governs ten disciplines, including canoe sprint and canoe slalom (both Olympic sports), wildwater canoeing, canoe marathon, canoe polo, paracanoe, dragon boat, canoe freestyle, ocean racing, and stand-up paddling.

Competitions

The federation organises world championships and World Cup series in its disciplines and sanctions the canoeing events at the Olympic Games. It has over 160 national federation members.
