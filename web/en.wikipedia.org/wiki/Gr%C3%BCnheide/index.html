Grünheide (Mark) is a municipality in the Oder-Spree district of Brandenburg, Germany, located southeast of Berlin. It consists of several villages — Grünheide, Hangelsberg, Kagel, Kienbaum, Mönchwinkel, and Spreeau — set among forests and lakes in the valley of the river Löcknitz. The municipality is best known internationally as the location of Tesla's Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg.

Gigafactory

In November 2019, Tesla announced it would build its first European factory in Grünheide, near the new Berlin Brandenburg Airport. Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg, producing the Model Y and battery cells, was inaugurated in March 2022. The factory's construction and planned expansion have been the subject of local debate over water usage and clearing of pine forest.

Geography and transport

Grünheide lies on the Berlin-Frankfurt (Oder) railway, with a station at Fangschleuse serving the Tesla plant, and near the A10 motorway (Berliner Ring). The surrounding lakes and forests make it a popular excursion destination from Berlin.
