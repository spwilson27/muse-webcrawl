Fine Line (album)

Fine Line is the second studio album by English singer Harry Styles, released on 13 December 2019 by Erskine and Columbia Records. The album includes the singles "Lights Up", "Adore You", "Falling", "Watermelon Sugar", "Golden", and "Treat People with Kindness".

Reception and success

Fine Line debuted atop charts in several countries, including the US Billboard 200. "Watermelon Sugar" became Styles's first number-one single on the Billboard Hot 100, and the album won the Grammy Award for Best Pop Solo Performance for that song; it was also named among Rolling Stone's 500 Greatest Albums of All Time.

Track listing

Standard tracks include "Golden", "Watermelon Sugar", "Adore You", "Lights Up", "Cherry", "Falling", "To Be So Lonely", "She", "Sunflower, Vol. 6", "Canyon Moon", "Treat People with Kindness", and the title track "Fine Line".
