Eyewitness Books (known as Eyewitness Guides in the United Kingdom) is a series of educational non-fiction books for children published by Dorling Kindersley (DK). The series is known for its distinctive visual design: full-bleed spreads of colour photographs of museum specimens and models arranged on white backgrounds, surrounded by short blocks of text and captions that allow readers to dip in at any point.

History and format

The first Eyewitness titles were published in 1988 and quickly became one of DK's most successful children's series, selling tens of millions of copies worldwide and being translated into dozens of languages. More than a hundred titles have appeared, covering natural history, science, history, geography, and technology — from Dinosaur, Ancient Egypt, and Knight to Forensic Science and World War I. Later editions added "Clip-art" CDs and poster material.

The format influenced children's reference publishing generally, encouraging image-led layouts. A related video series, Eyewitness, was produced in the 1990s, narrated by Martin Sheen in its US version.
