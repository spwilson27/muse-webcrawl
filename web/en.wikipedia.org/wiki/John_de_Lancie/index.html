John de Lancie (born 20 March 1948) is an American actor, comedian, director, producer and writer, best known for his role as the omnipotent trickster Q in the Star Trek franchise.

Career

The son of principal oboist John de Lancie of the Philadelphia Orchestra, he trained at Kent State University and Juilliard. He first gained attention as Eugene Bradford on the soap opera Days of Our Lives (1982–86). Cast by Gene Roddenberry, he debuted as Q in the pilot episode of Star Trek: The Next Generation (1987) and returned across Deep Space Nine, Voyager, Lower Decks and Picard, becoming one of the franchise's most popular recurring antagonists.

De Lancie voiced the draconequus Discord in My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic (2011–2019), a role that made him a fixture of the "brony" fandom and the subject of the documentary Bronies: The Extremely Unexpected Adult Fans of My Little Pony. His other roles include Frank Simmons in Stargate SG-1 and Donald Margolis in Breaking Bad. A keen sailor and a prominent secular activist, he has narrated orchestral works, co-written the novel Soldier of Light, and produced audio dramas through his company Alien Voices.
