The Melbourne Football Club, nicknamed the Demons, is a professional Australian rules football club based in Melbourne, Victoria. Founded in 1858, it is the oldest professional football club in the world, and its members famously codified the rules of Australian rules football in 1859. The club plays in the Australian Football League (AFL) and is based at the Melbourne Cricket Ground.

History

Melbourne was a foundation member of the Victorian Football Association in 1877 and of the Victorian Football League (later AFL) in 1897. The club enjoyed its greatest era under coach Norm Smith in the 1950s and 1960s, winning six premierships between 1955 and 1964. After a 57-year premiership drought, Melbourne defeated the Western Bulldogs in the 2021 AFL Grand Final to claim its thirteenth flag.

Club identity

The club's colours are navy blue and red, and its guernsey features a navy body with a red yoke. Its theme song is "It's a Grand Old Flag," sung to the tune of "You're a Grand Old Flag."

Honours

Melbourne has won 13 VFL/AFL premierships: 1900, 1926, 1939, 1940, 1941, 1948, 1955, 1956, 1957, 1959, 1960, 1964 and 2021.
