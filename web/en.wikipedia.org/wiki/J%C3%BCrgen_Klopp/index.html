Jürgen Norbert Klopp (born 16 June 1967) is a German football manager and former player, widely regarded as one of the best managers of his generation. He is best known for his spells at Borussia Dortmund and Liverpool, where his teams played an intense, high-pressing style he associated with Gegenpressing.

Playing and early management

Klopp spent most of his playing career at Mainz 05, making over 300 league appearances, initially as a striker and later as a defender. Immediately after retiring in 2001 he became Mainz's manager, leading the club to its first Bundesliga promotion in 2004.

Dortmund and Liverpool

At Borussia Dortmund from 2008, Klopp won back-to-back Bundesliga titles in 2011 and 2012, the DFB-Pokal, and reached the 2013 UEFA Champions League final. Joining Liverpool in 2015, he won the Champions League in 2019 and the Premier League in 2019–20 — the club's first league title in thirty years — along with the FA Cup and other trophies, before stepping down in 2024. In 2025 he took up a role as head of global soccer for the Red Bull group.
