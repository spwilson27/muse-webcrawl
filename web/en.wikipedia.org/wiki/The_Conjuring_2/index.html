The Conjuring 2

Directed by: James Wan
Starring: Vera Farmiga, Patrick Wilson, Madison Wolfe, Frances O'Connor
Released: June 10, 2016
Box office: $322.8 million

The Conjuring 2 is a 2016 American supernatural horror film directed by James Wan. The sequel to The Conjuring (2013) and the third entry in The Conjuring Universe franchise, it stars Patrick Wilson and Vera Farmiga as paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren, who travel to London to help a family beset by the Enfield poltergeist.

Plot

In 1976, the Warrens investigate the Amityville haunting, during which Lorraine receives a vision of a demonic nun and Ed's death. A year later, in the London borough of Enfield, single mother Peggy Hodgson and her four children experience violent paranormal activity centred on eleven-year-old Janet, who appears to channel the spirit of a deceased former resident. The Warrens discover the haunting is orchestrated by the demon Valak, the "Marquis of Snakes," in the form of the nun Lorraine envisioned. Recalling Valak's name, Lorraine banishes the demon and saves Janet and Ed.

Production and reception

The film dramatizes the real Enfield poltergeist case of 1977–1979, one of Britain's most famous alleged hauntings, which the film acknowledges was disputed by sceptics as a possible hoax. Made on a budget of about $40 million, the film grossed $322.8 million worldwide and received generally positive reviews praising Wan's direction and the leads' performances. The character of Valak proved popular and headlined the spin-off The Nun (2018).
