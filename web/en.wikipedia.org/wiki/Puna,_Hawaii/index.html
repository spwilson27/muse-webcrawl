Puna is one of the nine districts of Hawaii County on the island of Hawaiʻi (the "Big Island") in the U.S. state of Hawaii. Occupying the island's southeastern flank, it covers much of the East Rift Zone of the active volcano Kīlauea and is known for its volcanic landscapes, rainforests, black-sand beaches and comparatively affordable land — much of it lying in high-risk lava zones.

Geography

Puna stretches from the summit region of Kīlauea down to the sea at Cape Kumukahi, the easternmost point of the island. Its communities are largely unincorporated subdivisions carved out of former sugar and ranch land, including Hawaiian Paradise Park, Hawaiian Beaches, Leilani Estates, Nanawale Estates, Kapoho and the town of Pāhoa, the district's commercial centre. The district receives abundant rainfall on its windward slopes, supporting dense ʻōhiʻa forest, tree-fern understory, orchid and anthurium farms, and papaya agriculture.

Volcanism

Puna is one of the most volcanically active inhabited areas in the United States. The village of Kalapana and the famous Kaimū black-sand beach were destroyed by lava flows from the Puʻu ʻŌʻō eruption beginning in the 1980s and 1990s. In May 2018, a major eruption in the Lower East Rift Zone opened dozens of fissures in and near Leilani Estates, destroying more than 700 homes and structures, inundating the Kapoho tide pools and Vacationland subdivisions, and filling Kapoho Bay to create new land. The district is home to the Puna Geothermal Venture plant, which supplies a significant share of the island's electricity and was temporarily shut down by the 2018 eruption.

Attractions

Points of interest include the eastern entrances to Hawaiʻi Volcanoes National Park, the Chain of Craters Road corridor, Isaac Hale Beach Park at Pohoiki (where the 2018 eruption created a new black-sand beach), warm springs and tide pools along the coast, and the painted church of Star of the Sea in Kalapana. Before it was closed by lava in 2018, the Kapoho tide pools were among the district's best-known snorkelling sites.
