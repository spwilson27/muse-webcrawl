Zōri

Image: Zōri

Zōri (草履) are flat Japanese sandals made of rice straw or other plant fibres, cloth, lacquered wood, leather, rubber, or synthetic materials. They are worn with traditional Japanese clothing such as the kimono.

Description

Zōri consist of a flat sole and two straps (hanao) that pass between the big toe and second toe, similar to flip-flops. Formal zōri for women are often lacquered or made with brocade straps, while informal ones may be plain straw (setta are a related leather-soled form).

Use

Zōri are worn with tabi socks on formal occasions. They are not worn indoors; outdoor footwear is removed when entering a Japanese home.
