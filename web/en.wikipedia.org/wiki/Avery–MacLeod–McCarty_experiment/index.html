Avery–MacLeod–McCarty experiment

Published1944
ResearchersOswald Avery, Colin MacLeod, Maclyn McCarty
InstitutionRockefeller Institute

The Avery–MacLeod–McCarty experiment (1944) demonstrated that DNA is the substance that causes bacterial transformation and, by implication, the material of heredity. Oswald Avery, Colin MacLeod and Maclyn McCarty at the Rockefeller Institute in New York built on Griffith's 1928 transformation experiment with Streptococcus pneumoniae.

Experiment

They purified extracts from heat-killed virulent (smooth) bacteria and showed that only DNA — not protein or RNA — transformed live harmless (rough) bacteria into the virulent type; enzymes destroying DNA abolished transformation. The finding contradicted the prevailing belief that protein carried genetic information.

Reception

Initial scepticism, partly over possible protein contamination, faded after the 1952 Hershey–Chase experiment, and the work paved the way for Watson and Crick's 1953 DNA structure.
