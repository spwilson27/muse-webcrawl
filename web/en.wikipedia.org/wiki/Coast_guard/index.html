Coast guard

A coast guard or coastguard is a maritime security organization of a particular country. The term embraces a range of responsibilities in different countries, from an armed naval force with customs duties and safety responsibilities to an organization of volunteers charged only with search and rescue. A coast guard may be a branch of a country's military or a civilian law-enforcement agency.

Roles

Typical coast guard functions include search and rescue at sea; enforcement of maritime law, including fisheries, customs, and immigration controls; maintenance of aids to navigation such as buoys and lighthouses; protection of the marine environment, including oil-spill response; and port and coastal security. In wartime some coast guards assume naval duties, including convoy escort and harbour defence.

By country

United States

The United States Coast Guard, founded in 1790 as the Revenue-Marine, is a branch of the armed forces administered by the Department of Homeland Security. It may be transferred to the Department of the Navy in wartime. It operates cutters, boats, and aircraft from stations around the U.S. coastline and inland waters.

United Kingdom

His Majesty's Coastguard (HM Coastguard) is a civilian service coordinating maritime search and rescue from national coordination centres; actual rescues are often carried out by the volunteer Royal National Lifeboat Institution and by contracted helicopters.

Asia

The China Coast Guard, placed under the People's Armed Police in 2018, is among the world's largest maritime law-enforcement fleets. Japan's and India's coast guards perform similar roles, while some countries, such as Germany, split the functions among several agencies coordinated by a central command.

History

Coast guards originated in the revenue cutters and preventive services established in the late 18th and early 19th centuries to suppress smuggling. The lifesaving and rescue role developed later in the 19th century, and formal coast guard services were consolidated in many countries during the 20th century.
