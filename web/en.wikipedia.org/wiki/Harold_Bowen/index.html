Harold Bowen

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Harold Bowen (6 June 1908 – 22 September 1988) was an English economic historian and Islamic scholar. He was a lecturer and then reader in history at the School of Oriental and African Studies (SOAS), University of London, and is best known for his studies of the Ottoman Empire and eighteenth-century Britain.

Career

Bowen contributed extensively to the first and second editions of The Encyclopaedia of Islam. With C. E. Bosworth he edited and completed H. A. R. Gibb's classic Islamic Society and the West, and he substantially revised Gibb's Mohammedanism: An Historical Survey. His British research included work on the structure of British government and politics in the eighteenth century.

Death of scholarship note

Bowen served as president of the Royal Asiatic Society and of the British Society for Middle Eastern Studies. He died at Banbury, Oxfordshire, in 1988.
