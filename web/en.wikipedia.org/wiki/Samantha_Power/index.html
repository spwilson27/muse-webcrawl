Samantha Power

Samantha Power (born September 21, 1970) is an Irish-American journalist, author, diplomat and government official. She served as US Ambassador to the United Nations (2013–2017) under President Barack Obama and as Administrator of USAID (2021–2025) under President Joe Biden.

Early life and journalism

Power was born in London and raised in Dublin before emigrating to the United States at age nine. She reported from Bosnia during the Bosnian War, an experience that shaped her work on genocide. Her book "A Problem from Hell": America and the Age of Genocide (2002) won the Pulitzer Prize for General Nonfiction.

Government service

After advising Obama's 2008 campaign and serving on the National Security Council, she became UN Ambassador, dealing with the Syrian civil war, the Iran nuclear deal, the Ebola crisis and Russia's annexation of Crimea. As USAID Administrator she led responses to global food insecurity and Russia's invasion of Ukraine. Her memoir The Education of an Idealist appeared in 2019.
