Kamla Persad-Bissessar (born 22 April 1952) is a Trinidadian lawyer and politician who has served as Prime Minister of Trinidad and Tobago since 2025, having previously held the office from 2010 to 2015. She was the country's first female prime minister, attorney general, and leader of the opposition, and leads the United National Congress (UNC).

Career

A former teacher and social worker, Persad-Bissessar studied law in Jamaica and London and was called to the bar in England and Trinidad. Elected MP for Siparia in 1995, she served as Attorney General and Minister of Education before becoming UNC leader in 2010. Her People's Partnership coalition won the 2010 general election, making her prime minister.

Return to office

After a decade as opposition leader, Persad-Bissessar led the UNC to victory in the April 2025 general election and was sworn in for a second term as prime minister.
