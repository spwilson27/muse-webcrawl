University of Lapland

The University of Lapland (Finnish: Lapin yliopisto) is a public university in Rovaniemi, Finland. Founded in 1979, it is the northernmost university in Finland and in the European Union, located just south of the Arctic Circle. It has about 4,000–5,000 students.

Faculties and research

The university has four faculties: Art and Design, Education, Law, and Social Sciences. Its Arctic Centre is an internationally known institute for Arctic research, focusing on climate change, sustainable development, indigenous peoples, and tourism in the circumpolar North. The faculty of law is known for environmental and Arctic law, and the university hosts research on the Sámi and northern regions.

Rovaniemi is marketed as the official hometown of Santa Claus, and the university works closely with the city and with Lapland University of Applied Sciences, with which it forms the Lapland University Consortium (LUC).
