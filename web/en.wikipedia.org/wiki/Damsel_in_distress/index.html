Damsel in distress

The damsel in distress is a stock character and narrative trope in which a young woman is placed in peril by a villain or monster and requires rescue by a male hero. The motif appears from ancient myth — Andromeda chained to a rock, Persephone, and numerous fairy-tale heroines — through medieval romance, Gothic novels, silent-film serials such as The Perils of Pauline, and adventure fiction and video games.

Criticism

Feminist critics argue the trope reduces female characters to passive prizes motivating male action, and modern works frequently subvert or invert it. The related term "distressed damsel" and discussions of characters such as Princess Peach and Princess Zelda are recurring topics in media studies.
