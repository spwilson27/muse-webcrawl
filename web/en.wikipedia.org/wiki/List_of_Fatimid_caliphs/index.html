List of Fatimid caliphs

The Fatimid Caliphate was an Ismaili Shia caliphate founded in Ifriqiya (modern Tunisia) in 909 by Abd Allah al-Mahdi Billah, who claimed descent from Fatima, the daughter of the Prophet Muhammad and wife of Ali. The Fatimids conquered Egypt in 969, founded Cairo as their capital, and at their height ruled much of North Africa, the Levant, and the western Arabian Peninsula. The dynasty lasted until 1171, when Saladin abolished the caliphate and restored Abbasid (Sunni) suzerainty in Egypt.

Succession disputes after the deaths of al-Mustansir (1094) and al-Amir (1130) produced lasting divisions within Ismaili Islam, giving rise to the Nizari, Musta'li, Hafizi, and Tayyibi traditions.
