The England national football team represents England in men's international football. Governed by the Football Association, it played the first international match in history against Scotland in 1872. England won the FIFA World Cup once, on home soil in 1966, defeating West Germany 4–2 in the final.

History

England declined to enter the first three World Cups and struggled after joining in 1950. Under Alf Ramsey, England won the 1966 World Cup at Wembley, with Geoff Hurst scoring a hat-trick in the final. Later decades brought semi-final runs at the 1990 World Cup and Euro 96. Under Gareth Southgate (2016–2024), England reached the 2018 World Cup semi-final and consecutive European Championship finals in 2020 and 2024, losing both. Thomas Tuchel succeeded Southgate in 2025.

Identity

The team badge features three lions, derived from the royal arms of England. The traditional home kit is white shirts, navy shorts and white socks. Wembley Stadium in London is the home ground.

Rivalries

Historic rivalries include Scotland (the oldest rivalry in international football), Germany, and Argentina.

Honours

  • FIFA World Cup: Winners 1966
  • UEFA European Championship: Runners-up 2020, 2024
