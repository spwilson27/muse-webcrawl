Overview

Entebbe is a city in central Uganda, on a peninsula on the northern shore of Lake Victoria, about 35 km southwest of Kampala. It was the seat of the colonial government of the Uganda Protectorate and remains the location of State House, the official residence of the President of Uganda. Entebbe International Airport is Uganda's chief international airport.

The 1976 raid

Entebbe is famous for Operation Entebbe (Operation Thunderbolt), the Israeli hostage-rescue mission of 4 July 1976, when commandos freed most of the hostages of a hijacked Air France flight held at the old terminal. The raid's commander, Yonatan Netanyahu, was the only Israeli soldier killed.

Other features

The Uganda Wildlife Education Centre (Entebbe Zoo) and the Entebbe Botanical Gardens are visitor attractions; the city sits almost exactly on the equator's latitude band north of the lake.
