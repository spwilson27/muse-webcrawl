OpenStax

OpenStax is a non-profit educational initiative based at Rice University in Houston, Texas, that publishes free, peer-reviewed, openly licensed textbooks. Founded in 2012 (as the Connexions/OpenStax College initiative), it aims to improve educational access and learning by making high-quality college textbooks available at no cost online and in low-cost print editions.

Catalogue and impact

OpenStax textbooks cover widely taught subjects including physics (University Physics), biology, chemistry, economics, psychology, statistics, sociology, anatomy and physiology, US history and mathematics from prealgebra through calculus. The books are licensed under Creative Commons licences, are written and reviewed by subject experts, and are reported to save students hundreds of millions of dollars annually, with adoption at thousands of institutions.

Related projects

OpenStax grew out of Connexions, an earlier Rice repository of modular learning content, and has developed homework and tutoring tools such as OpenStax Tutor and the research-focused OpenStax Labs.
