The Benjamin N. Cardozo School of Law is the law school of Yeshiva University in New York City. Founded in 1976, it is named after Benjamin Nathan Cardozo, the celebrated judge of the New York Court of Appeals and Associate Justice of the Supreme Court of the United States. The school is located at 55 Fifth Avenue in Greenwich Village, Manhattan, near Union Square.

History

Cardozo was established as Yeshiva University's law school after decades of discussion, admitting its first class in 1976 — the first students met in temporary premises before the school settled into its Fifth Avenue building, a former union headquarters acquired and converted by the university. Despite its youth, the school rose quickly in national reputation, drawing on a faculty hired from established schools and on its Manhattan location.

Academics

The school offers the Juris Doctor (JD), Master of Laws (LL.M.) degrees including in intellectual property and comparative legal thought, and a Doctor of Juridical Science (JSD). It is particularly known for its programs in intellectual property and information law, dispute resolution, and legal theory. Its clinical programs include the Innocence Project — founded at Cardozo in 1992 by Barry Scheck and Peter Neufeld — which uses DNA evidence to exonerate wrongfully convicted people, and the Bet Tzedek legal services clinic.

Centers and journals

Cardozo hosts a number of research centres, including the FAME Center for fashion, arts, media and entertainment law, the Center for Intellectual Property and Information Law, and the Jacob Burns Center for Ethics in the Practice of Law. Student-edited journals include the Cardozo Law Review, the Cardozo Arts & Entertainment Law Journal (founded 1982, among the first of its kind) and the Cardozo Journal of Conflict Resolution.

Student body and outcomes

The school enrols roughly a thousand students across its programs. Its entering classes are competitive, with median undergraduate grades and LSAT scores in the upper range of American law schools. Alumni practise prominently in New York in intellectual property, media, litigation, real estate and public interest law.
