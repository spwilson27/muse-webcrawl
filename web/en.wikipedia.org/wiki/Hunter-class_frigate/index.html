Hunter-class frigate

The Hunter class is a class of frigates being built for the Royal Australian Navy under the SEA 5000 program. Based on the British Type 26 frigate design by BAE Systems, the ships are primarily intended for anti-submarine warfare, with additional air defence and surface warfare capabilities.

Program

In June 2018 the Australian government announced that BAE Systems' Type 26 design had been selected for the Future Frigate program, with construction by ASC Shipbuilding (later BAE Systems Maritime Australia) at the Osborne Naval Shipyard in South Australia. Steel was cut for the first ship, HMAS Hunter, in 2024. The program was originally planned for nine ships; in 2024 the government announced a restructured fleet plan reducing the class to six ships.

Design

The Hunter class displaces around 8,800 tonnes, is about 150 metres long, and is equipped with the Australian CEAFAR2 phased-array radar, the Aegis combat system with the Australian interface, Mk 41 vertical launch systems, and facilities for an embarked MH-60R helicopter.
