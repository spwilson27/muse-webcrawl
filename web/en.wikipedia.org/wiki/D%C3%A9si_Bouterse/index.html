Dési Bouterse

Desiré Delano "Dési" Bouterse (13 October 1945 – 23 December 2024) was a Surinamese military officer, politician and convicted criminal who served as the 8th President of Suriname from 2010 to 2020. From 1980 to 1987, he was Suriname's de facto leader after conducting a military coup and establishing a period of military rule.

Military career and coup

Bouterse trained at the Royal Military School in the Netherlands and rose through the Surinamese armed forces. On 25 February 1980, he led a group of sixteen sergeants in a coup d'état that overthrew the government of Prime Minister Henck Arron, barely five years after Suriname's independence from the Netherlands. He became chairman of the National Military Council and the effective ruler of the country.

December murders

In December 1982, fifteen prominent opponents of the regime — journalists, lawyers, academics, trade unionists and military officers — were arrested, tortured and murdered at Fort Zeelandia in Paramaribo. The "December murders" caused the Netherlands and the United States to suspend aid and left a lasting shadow over Bouterse's career. Decades later, in 2019, a Surinamese military court convicted Bouterse in absentia of the murders and sentenced him to 20 years in prison; the verdict was upheld on final appeal in December 2023, after which he went into hiding.

Drug trafficking conviction

In 1999, a Dutch court convicted Bouterse in absentia of cocaine trafficking and sentenced him to 11 years in prison. He avoided travel abroad where he risked arrest, although he could not be extradited from Suriname.

Presidency

As leader of the National Democratic Party (NDP), Bouterse was elected president by the National Assembly in 2010 and re-elected in 2015. His presidency saw expanded social welfare spending and strained relations with the Netherlands. He left office in 2020 after the NDP lost the general election, and was succeeded by Chan Santokhi.

Death

Bouterse died on 23 December 2024, at the age of 79, at an undisclosed location in Suriname while a fugitive from his prison sentence.
