The 2034 FIFA World Cup will be the 25th edition of the FIFA World Cup, the quadrennial international men's football championship. It is scheduled to be hosted by Saudi Arabia, which was confirmed as host by the FIFA Congress on 11 December 2024.

Host selection

FIFA restricted bidding for the 2034 tournament to member associations from Asia (AFC) and Oceania (OFC). Saudi Arabia announced its bid in October 2023; a rival Australian-led bid was withdrawn before the deadline, leaving Saudi Arabia as the sole bidder. FIFA's evaluation report rated the bid highly on infrastructure while flagging medium risks on human rights and timing.

Venues

The bid proposes 15 stadiums across five cities: Riyadh, Jeddah, Khobar, Abha and Neom. Several stadiums are to be newly built, including the King Salman International Stadium in Riyadh, planned to host the opening match and final. Because of the desert climate, the tournament is expected to be staged in winter, as the 2022 World Cup in Qatar was, though dates have not been finalised.

Criticism

The award drew criticism from human rights organisations, which raised concerns about migrant workers' rights, freedom of expression, and discrimination, and about the accelerated, uncontested bidding process. Saudi officials stated the tournament is part of the country's Vision 2030 modernisation programme.
