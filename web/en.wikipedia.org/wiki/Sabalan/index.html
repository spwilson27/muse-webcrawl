Sabalan

Sabalan (Persian: سبلان)
Elevation: 4,811 m
Location: Ardabil Province, Iran
Type: Inactive stratovolcano

Sabalan is an inactive stratovolcano in Ardabil Province of northwestern Iran. At 4,811 metres it is the third-highest peak in Iran, after Damavand and Alam-Kuh. The mountain has a permanent crater lake at its summit, formed in a small crater and frozen for most of the year.

Geography and geology

Sabalan is a large volcanic massif with several secondary peaks, alpine meadows and hot springs on its flanks, notably around the towns of Meshgin Shahr and Sarein, which support a local spa tourism industry. The volcano is associated with geothermal activity, and its slopes are used in summer by nomadic Shahsavan pastoralists whose distinctive round yurt-like tents dot the high pastures.

Cultural significance

The mountain holds a place in Zoroastrian tradition and local folklore and is a popular mountaineering destination in Iran. The suspended bridge at Meshgin Shahr, among the longest in the Middle East, offers views toward the peak.
