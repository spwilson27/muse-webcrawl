Nordderby

The Nordderby (Northern derby) is the name given to football matches between Hamburger SV and SV Werder Bremen, the two most successful clubs of northern Germany. It is one of the oldest and most frequently played derbies in German football, with the first meeting taking place in 1927.

History

Hamburg and Bremen, two Hanseatic cities separated by about 100 kilometres, developed a civic rivalry that extended naturally to the football pitch. The derby gained national prominence after the founding of the Bundesliga in 1963, when both clubs were regular members of the top flight. Matches in the 1980s were especially significant, as Hamburg and Bremen were among the dominant teams in West Germany, contesting league titles and European places.

Notable matches

The derby has produced memorable encounters in the Bundesliga, the DFB-Pokal, and the UEFA Cup, including a remarkable run in the spring of 2009 when the clubs met four times within a few weeks across league, cup, and European competition.
