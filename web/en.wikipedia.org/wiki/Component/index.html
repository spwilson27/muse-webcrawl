Component

Component may refer to a constituent part of a system in many fields:

Science and technology

  • Electronic component — a basic discrete device in an electronic system, such as a resistor, capacitor, transistor or integrated circuit.
  • Component (thermodynamics) — a chemically independent constituent of a system, used in Gibbs' phase rule.
  • Component video — an analogue video signal split into two or more component channels (e.g. YPbPr).
  • Component-based software engineering — building software from reusable, independently deployable components; also software component generally.
  • Connected component — in graph theory, a maximal connected subgraph; in topology, a maximal connected subset of a space; in image processing, a connected region of pixels.
  • Component (UML) — a modular element of a system model in the Unified Modeling Language.

Mathematics

  • Vector component — one of the projections of a vector along the axes of a coordinate system.
  • Component (group theory) — a quasisimple subnormal subgroup of a group.
  • Irreducible component — in algebraic geometry, a maximal irreducible subset of an algebraic variety.

Other uses

  • Component (military) — in NATO and US usage, the service branch element of a joint command, e.g. the land component.
  • Component (food) — an ingredient forming part of a dish or ration.
  • Bicycle component — the parts of a bicycle, including the drivetrain "groupset".
