MTR Corporation

The MTR Corporation Limited is a majority government-owned public transport operator and property developer in Hong Kong which operates the Mass Transit Railway (MTR), the territory's rapid transit system. Founded in 1975 as the Mass Transit Railway Corporation, it was restructured and partially privatised in 2000, listing on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange while the Hong Kong government retained a majority shareholding.

Operations

The MTR network comprises heavy rail, light rail, and feeder bus services carrying several million passengers daily in Hong Kong. The corporation is known for its "Rail + Property" model, under which it develops commercial and residential property above and around stations, with property profits helping to fund railway construction.

International business

Through subsidiaries, MTR has operated or held concessions for railways outside Hong Kong, including services in London (Elizabeth line, as MTR Elizabeth line), Stockholm, Sydney, Melbourne, Beijing, Shenzhen, Hangzhou, and Macau. In 2007 the corporation merged its operations with the Kowloon-Canton Railway Corporation's network under a long-term service concession.
