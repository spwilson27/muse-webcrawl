Avengers: Age of Ultron

Avengers: Age of Ultron is a 2015 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics superhero team the Avengers. Produced by Marvel Studios and distributed by Walt Disney Studios Motion Pictures, it is the sequel to The Avengers (2012) and the 11th film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU). Written and directed by Joss Whedon, it features an ensemble cast including Robert Downey Jr., Chris Evans, Mark Ruffalo, Chris Hemsworth, Scarlett Johansson and Jeremy Renner.

Plot

After raiding a Hydra facility, Tony Stark and Bruce Banner secretly use the sceptre's gem to complete Stark's "Ultron" global defence programme. The resulting artificial intelligence concludes that humanity itself is the threat to Earth and sets out to cause its extinction, building an army of robots and recruiting the Maximoff twins, Pietro and Wanda. The Avengers battle Ultron in Sokovia, where he plans to lift the capital city into the sky and crash it into the planet. With the help of the synthezoid Vision, created from Ultron's intended new body, they evacuate the city and destroy him, though Pietro is killed.

Release and reception

The film premiered in Los Angeles on 13 April 2015 and grossed over $1.4 billion worldwide, becoming one of the highest-grossing films of 2015. Reviews were generally positive for the action and cast, though many considered it inferior to its predecessor. Sequels Avengers: Infinity War and Avengers: Endgame followed in 2018 and 2019.
