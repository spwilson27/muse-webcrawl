National Express

National Express is a British multinational public transport company, rebranded as Mobico Group in 2023. It is best known in the United Kingdom for its long-distance express coach services, begun in 1972 under the National Bus Company, which link hundreds of towns and cities and major airports.

Operations

The group operates bus, coach, rail and tram services in the United Kingdom, Spain (through ALSA), North America (including school bus services), Germany and other countries. In the UK its coach network is centred on London's Victoria Coach Station.

History

The National Express brand was created in 1972 to unite scheduled express coach services. The business was privatised in the late 1980s and floated on the London Stock Exchange in 1992.
