Fall of Kabul (2021)

The Fall of Kabul on 15 August 2021 was the capture of Afghanistan's capital by the Taliban during their 2021 offensive, marking the effective end of the War in Afghanistan and the collapse of the Islamic Republic of Afghanistan.

Background

Following the 2020 Doha Agreement and the withdrawal of US and NATO forces ordered in 2021, the Taliban rapidly seized provincial capitals from May 2021 as Afghan security forces collapsed or surrendered.

Events

As Taliban fighters reached Kabul's outskirts, President Ashraf Ghani fled the country. The Taliban entered the city largely unopposed, occupied the presidential palace, and declared the war over. Panic at Hamid Karzai International Airport saw thousands attempt to flee; a massive Western airlift evacuated over 120,000 people by 31 August, marred by a suicide bombing on 26 August that killed some 170 Afghans and 13 US service members.

Aftermath

The Taliban restored the Islamic Emirate of Afghanistan, formed a caretaker government, and faced resistance in Panjshir and an insurgency by Islamic State–Khorasan. The fall prompted international debate over the withdrawal and a humanitarian and economic crisis in Afghanistan.
