St James' Park

St James' Park may refer to several places, most prominently St James's Park, a royal park in the City of Westminster, London, and St James' Park, the football stadium in Newcastle upon Tyne, home of Newcastle United.

Other uses

The name is also borne by St James' Park in Exeter, home ground of Exeter City F.C., by a London Underground station on the Circle and District lines serving the Westminster park (spelled St. James's Park), and by various streets, schools, and public gardens in Britain and the Commonwealth named in honour of Saint James the Greater.
