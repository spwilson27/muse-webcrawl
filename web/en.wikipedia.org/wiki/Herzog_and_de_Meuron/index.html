Herzog and de Meuron is the Basel-based architecture practice of Jacques Herzog (born 1950) and Pierre de Meuron (born 1950), two Swiss architects who have worked together since founding the office in 1978 after studying at ETH Zurich.

Approach

The partners' early work in Switzerland — such as the Ricola storage building and the signal box at Basel station — was notable for experimental treatment of ordinary materials: printed glass, copper, stone gabions and plywood. Their architecture combines a minimal, almost austere formal language with richly tactile surfaces, an approach that shaped a generation of European architects.
