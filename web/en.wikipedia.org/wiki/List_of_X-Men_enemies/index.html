List of X-Men enemies

The X-Men, Marvel Comics' team of mutant superheroes created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby in 1963, have faced a large rogues' gallery of villains, ranging from fellow mutants to cosmic entities. The principal enemies are listed below.
