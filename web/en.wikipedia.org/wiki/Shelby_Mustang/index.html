Shelby Mustang

A 1965–66 Shelby GT350.

The Shelby Mustang is a high-performance variant of the Ford Mustang built by Shelby American from 1965 to 1967, and by the Ford Motor Company from 1968 to 1970. Following the introduction of the fifth generation Ford Mustang in 2005, the Shelby nameplate was revived as a high-performance model developed jointly by Ford's Special Vehicle Team (SVT) and Shelby American.

1965–1966 Shelby GT350

The first Shelby Mustangs, the GT350, were built in 1965 and 1966 at Shelby American's facility in Venice, California, and later at Los Angeles International Airport. They used Ford's 289 cu in (4.7 L) Windsor V8, modified with an aluminum high-rise intake manifold, a Holley four-barrel carburetor, and Tri-Y exhaust headers to produce 306 hp in street form. Racing "R" models were lighter, more powerful, and won the B-Production class in SCCA racing for three consecutive years.

1967–1968 GT350 and GT500

For 1967 the Mustang's restyling allowed a longer, wider Shelby with a fibreglass front end and distinctive tail. The new GT500 used Ford's 428 cu in (7.0 L) Police Interceptor big-block V8, officially rated at 355 hp. A small number of GT500 Super Snakes and PA prototypes were also built. Production moved to Michigan in 1968 under A.O. Smith, and a convertible body style was added.

1969–1970

The 1969 models were extensively restyled with more fibreglass bodywork and were sold as GT350 and GT500; unsold 1969 cars were updated with black hood stripes and new identification numbers and sold as 1970 models. Ford ended the program after 1970.

2005–present revival

The Shelby name returned in 2007 with the Ford Shelby GT500, powered by a supercharged 5.4 L V8 producing 500 hp, then the most powerful production Mustang ever built. Later versions included the 2013 GT500 with a 662 hp 5.8 L engine, the track-focused flat-plane-crank GT350 (2015–2020), and the 2020–2022 GT500 with a 760 hp supercharged 5.2 L V8 and dual-clutch transmission. Shelby American also offers post-title packages such as the Super Snake and Shelby GT.
