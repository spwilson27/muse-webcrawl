Cainiao Network Technology Co., Ltd., commonly known as Cainiao (Chinese: 菜鸟网络; pinyin: Càiniǎo Wǎngluò), is a Chinese logistics company and the logistics arm of Alibaba Group. Founded in 2013, Cainiao provides warehousing, fulfilment, last-mile delivery, and cross-border logistics services for Alibaba's e-commerce platforms such as Taobao and Tmall, as well as for third-party merchants. Its name, cainiao, literally means “rookie” or “newbie” in Chinese.

History

Alibaba announced Cainiao in May 2013 together with a consortium of partners including Yintai Group, Fosun Group and several major Chinese courier companies, with the ambition of building a nationwide smart logistics network able to support deliveries anywhere in China within 24 hours. Alibaba progressively increased its stake, gaining majority control in 2017, and in 2023 announced plans for an initial public offering of Cainiao in Hong Kong; Alibaba withdrew the IPO proposal in March 2024 and offered to buy out minority shareholders instead.

Operations

Cainiao operates a network of warehouses and distribution hubs, parcel collection points known as Cainiao Post stations, and a cross-border logistics network serving AliExpress deliveries to markets in Europe, Latin America, the Middle East and elsewhere, promoting five-day global delivery on selected routes. The company emphasises logistics data technology, including electronic shipping labels, demand forecasting, warehouse automation and route optimisation.
