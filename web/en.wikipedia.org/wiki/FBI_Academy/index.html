FBI Academy

The FBI Academy is the Federal Bureau of Investigation's training and research centre near Quantico, Virginia, on the grounds of Marine Corps Base Quantico. Opened in 1972, the 547-acre facility trains new FBI special agents and intelligence analysts, and also provides training to state, local and international law-enforcement partners.

Training

New agent trainees complete a roughly 20-week Basic Field Training Course covering academics, firearms, defensive tactics, operational skills and legal studies at the Academy, including practical exercises at "Hogan's Alley", a simulated town built with Hollywood set designers where trainees practise investigations and arrests. The Academy is also home to the FBI National Academy, a professional course of study for U.S. and international law-enforcement managers, established in 1935.

Units and facilities

The Academy hosts the FBI Laboratory division's research components, the Behavioral Analysis Units made famous by the former Behavioral Science Unit, and the FBI's library. The facility includes dormitories, a dining hall, firing ranges, a gym and an aquatic centre.

In popular culture

The Academy features prominently in The Silence of the Lambs and the television series Quantico and Mindhunter.
