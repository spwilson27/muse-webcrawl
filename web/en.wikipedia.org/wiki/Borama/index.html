Borama

Borama (Somali: Boorama) is a city in the northwestern Awdal region of Somaliland, near the border with Ethiopia and Djibouti. It is the administrative capital of Awdal and one of the larger cities in Somaliland, situated in a mountainous area at an elevation of roughly 1,400 metres, which gives it a relatively mild climate.

History

Borama lies near the ancient ruins of Amud, associated with the medieval Adal Sultanate. The modern town grew during the British Somaliland protectorate period. In 1993, Borama hosted the Grand Conference of National Reconciliation (the Borama Conference), a landmark clan elders' meeting that established the political framework of the Somaliland government, including its system of government and the role of the House of Elders (Guurti).

Education and transport

The city is home to Amoud University, founded in 1998 as the first post-war university in Somaliland. Borama is served by road links to Hargeisa, Zeila, and the Ethiopian border town of Tog Wajaale, and has a small airport.
