TypeLanguage name (multiple languages)

Mono language may refer to several distinct languages around the world that share the name Mono. Because the name is applied independently in different regions, it most often requires disambiguation by country or language family.

Languages named Mono

Mono language (California): an Uto-Aztecan language spoken by the Mono people of the Sierra Nevada in California, United States, with Western and Eastern (Owens Valley Paiute) varieties.

Mono language (Congo): a Ubangian language of the Banda group spoken in the northwestern Democratic Republic of the Congo.

Mono language (Solomon Islands): an Austronesian language spoken on Mono Island in the Treasury Islands of the Solomon Islands.

Mono language (Cameroon): a Mbum language spoken in northern Cameroon. It may also refer in some older sources to the Alsea language of Oregon (also called Yakona), whose alternate names include Mono.
