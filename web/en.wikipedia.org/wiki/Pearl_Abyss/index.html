Pearl Abyss Corp. (Korean: 펄어비스) is a South Korean video game developer and publisher founded in 2010 by Kim Dae-il, known for the MMORPG Black Desert Online. The company is listed on the KOSDAQ exchange.

History

Kim Dae-il, previously a developer on games such as R.Y.L. and C9, founded Pearl Abyss in 2010 to develop an open-world action MMORPG using a proprietary engine. Black Desert launched in South Korea in 2015 and internationally from 2016, earning praise for its graphics engine, character customisation and action combat, and later appearing on consoles and mobile as Black Desert Mobile.

Acquisitions and later projects

In 2018 Pearl Abyss acquired Icelandic developer CCP Games, the maker of EVE Online, for a reported US$425 million. The company has announced further titles including the open-world action-adventure Crimson Desert, the creature-collecting game DokeV, and the exfiltration shooter Black Clover-era projects under development in its studios. Pearl Abyss operates offices in North America, Europe, Japan, and Taiwan.
