Garfield Sobers

Sir Garfield St Aubrun Sobers (born 28 July 1936) is a Barbadian former cricketer who played for the West Indies and is widely considered cricket's greatest all-rounder.

Born28 July 1936, Bridgetown, Barbados
RoleAll-rounder: left-handed bat, slow left-arm / left-arm fast-medium, slip fielder
TeamsWest Indies, Barbados, Nottinghamshire, South Australia

Career

Debuting at 17 in 1954, Sobers scored 365 not out against Pakistan in 1958, a world Test record that stood for 36 years until broken by Brian Lara. In 1968 he became the first batsman to hit six sixes in one first-class over, for Nottinghamshire against Glamorgan. He captained the West Indies in 39 Tests.

Statistics and honours

In 93 Tests he scored 8,032 runs at 57.78 and took 235 wickets. Knighted in 1975 and named a National Hero of Barbados in 1998, he is honoured by the Sir Garfield Sobers Trophy for the ICC Cricketer of the Year and a statue at Kensington Oval. Don Bradman called him one of the greatest cricketers he saw; later stars such as Viv Richards followed in his tradition.
