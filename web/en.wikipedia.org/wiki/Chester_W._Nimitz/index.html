Chester William Nimitz
Born: 24 February 1885, Fredericksburg, Texas
Died: 20 February 1966
Rank: Fleet Admiral, US Navy

Chester William Nimitz (1885–1966) was a fleet admiral of the United States Navy. He played a major role in the naval history of World War II as Commander in Chief, U.S. Pacific Fleet and Commander in Chief, Pacific Ocean Areas, commanding Allied air, land, and sea forces during the Pacific War.

World War II

Appointed to command the Pacific Fleet days after the attack on Pearl Harbor, Nimitz directed the campaigns that won at Midway, the Solomon Islands, the Philippine Sea, Leyte Gulf, Iwo Jima and Okinawa. He signed the Japanese Instrument of Surrender on behalf of the United States aboard USS Missouri in Tokyo Bay on 2 September 1945.

Later career

Nimitz served as Chief of Naval Operations from 1945 to 1947. The Nimitz-class aircraft carriers are named in his honour.
