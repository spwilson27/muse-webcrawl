FIFA Intercontinental Cup

The FIFA Intercontinental Cup is an annual international association football competition organised by FIFA, contested by the champion clubs of each of the six continental confederations. It was announced in December 2023 and first played in 2024, filling the annual slot left when the FIFA Club World Cup was expanded to a four-yearly 32-team tournament beginning in 2025. Its name revives that of the historic Intercontinental Cup played between European and South American champions from 1960 to 2004.

Format

The champions of Asia, Africa and Oceania meet in early rounds, and the winners progress through a series of branded sub-trophies (the African–Asian–Pacific Cup, the Derby of the Americas and the Challenger Cup), while the UEFA Champions League winners enter directly into the single-match final. In the inaugural edition, held in Qatar in December 2024, Real Madrid defeated Mexico's Pachuca 3–0 in the final at Lusail Stadium.

Background

FIFA regards the competition as the annual successor to the old seven-team Club World Cup; earlier Club World Cup winners are still recognised as annual world champions, a status FIFA extended retroactively to Intercontinental Cup winners in 2017.
