Royal Opera, London

The Royal Opera is an opera company based in London at the Royal Opera House, Covent Garden. Together with the Royal Ballet, it forms the resident companies of the house; since 2024 the umbrella organisation is styled the Royal Ballet and Opera.

History

The company was founded in 1946 as the Covent Garden Opera Company, presenting its first production, Purcell's The Fairy-Queen, in December 1946. It received the "Royal" title in 1968. Music directors have included Karl Rankl, Rafael Kubelík, Georg Solti, Colin Davis, Bernard Haitink and Antonio Pappano.

Work

The Royal Opera performs a repertory of classic and new operas each season, sung in their original languages with surtitles, accompanied by the Orchestra of the Royal Opera House.
