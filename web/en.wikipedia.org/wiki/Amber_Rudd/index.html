Amber Rudd

Amber Augusta Rudd (born 1 August 1963) is a British former politician who served as Home Secretary from 2016 to 2018 and as Secretary of State for Work and Pensions from 2018 to 2019. A member of the Conservative Party, she was Member of Parliament (MP) for Hastings and Rye from 2010 to 2019. She previously served as Secretary of State for Energy and Climate Change under David Cameron.

Early life and career

Rudd was educated at Cheltenham Ladies' College and read history at the University of Edinburgh. She worked in investment banking in London and New York and in venture capital and financial public relations before entering politics. Her brother is the public-relations executive Roland Rudd.

Parliamentary career

Elected for Hastings and Rye in 2010, she rose quickly, becoming Energy Secretary in 2015 and campaigning for Remain in the 2016 EU referendum. Theresa May appointed her Home Secretary in July 2016. She resigned in April 2018 over the Windrush scandal, after admitting she had inadvertently misled Parliament over targets for the removal of illegal immigrants. She returned to the cabinet in November 2018 as Work and Pensions Secretary.

Resignation and later life

In September 2019 Rudd resigned from Boris Johnson's cabinet and surrendered the Conservative whip in protest at the expulsion of 21 rebel MPs and the government's approach to Brexit. She stood down at the 2019 general election and has since worked in business and commentary roles.
