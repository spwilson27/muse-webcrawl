Microsoft OneNote

Microsoft OneNote
Developer: Microsoft
Initial release: 2003
Type: Note-taking software

Microsoft OneNote is a note-taking program for free-form information gathering and multi-user collaboration, developed by Microsoft. It gathers users' notes, drawings, screen clippings and audio commentaries, organising them into notebooks divided into sections and pages.

Features

OneNote files can be placed anywhere on a free-form canvas, typed or handwritten with a stylus, and notes are automatically saved and synchronised through OneDrive. Optical character recognition makes text in images searchable, and the program integrates with the rest of Microsoft Office. Versions exist for Windows, macOS, iOS, Android and the web; the Windows 10 "OneNote for Windows 10" app was later retired in favour of the unified OneNote app.

History

OneNote was announced in 2002 and first released in November 2003 as part of the Office suite, targeting tablet PC users. It became free of charge in 2014.
