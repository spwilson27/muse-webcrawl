Adobe Inc.

Adobe Inc. is an American multinational computer software company headquartered in San Jose, California. Originally incorporated in Delaware and founded in December 1982 by John Warnock and Charles Geschke, the company is best known for its creative and document software, including Photoshop, Illustrator, Acrobat Reader, and the Portable Document Format (PDF), which Geschke and Warnock helped pioneer.

History

Warnock and Geschke left Xerox PARC to develop the PostScript page description language, which became the foundation of desktop publishing. Apple's adoption of PostScript in the LaserWriter printer in 1985 helped launch the desktop publishing revolution. Adobe went public in 1986.

The company launched Photoshop in 1990 (developed by Thomas and John Knoll), Illustrator for vector graphics, and in 1993 introduced PDF and Acrobat. In 2005 Adobe acquired Macromedia, gaining Flash, Dreamweaver, and Fireworks. In 2012–2013 Adobe shifted its creative products to the subscription-based Creative Cloud model, ending perpetual licenses for new versions.

Adobe expanded into digital marketing and analytics through acquisitions including Omniture (2009), Magento and Marketo (2018), and Workfront (2020). Its proposed $20 billion acquisition of Figma, announced in 2022, was abandoned in December 2023 after regulatory opposition in the UK and EU. Adobe has since integrated generative AI (branded Firefly) across its product line.

Products

Adobe's business is organized around three clouds: Creative Cloud (Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, After Effects, InDesign, Lightroom), Document Cloud (Acrobat, PDF services, Adobe Sign), and Experience Cloud (analytics, advertising, commerce and marketing automation tools).
