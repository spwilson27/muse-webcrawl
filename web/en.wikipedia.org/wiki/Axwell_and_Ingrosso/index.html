Axwell & Ingrosso

Axwell & Ingrosso (stylised as Axwell Λ Ingrosso) was a Swedish DJ duo consisting of Axwell and Sebastian Ingrosso, two of the three members of the Swedish House Mafia. Active from 2014 to 2018, the duo released the album More Than You Know and the hit singles "Sun Is Shining", "On My Way", "Something New" and "More Than You Know".

History

Following the break-up of Swedish House Mafia in 2013, Axwell and Ingrosso announced their joint project at the Governors Ball Music Festival in New York in 2014. Their debut single "Something New" appeared later that year, and "Sun Is Shining" (2015) became a number-one hit in Sweden. The single "More Than You Know" (2017) was a major European success and gave its title to their debut album, released in December 2017.

In March 2018, at Ultra Music Festival in Miami, the duo closed the festival with a surprise Swedish House Mafia reunion performance; the project was then placed on hold as Swedish House Mafia reunited for a world tour and new music.
