Guts (Berserk)

Guts (Japanese: ガッツ, Gattsu) is the protagonist of Berserk, the dark fantasy manga series by Kentaro Miura. Known as the "Black Swordsman", he is a wandering mercenary who wields an oversized sword called the Dragonslayer and a prosthetic left forearm concealing a cannon, hunting the demonic Apostles and the God Hand after the catastrophic events of the Eclipse.

Raised by mercenaries after being found as an infant beneath his hanged mother, Guts rose to prominence in the Band of the Hawk under Griffith, whose ambition and betrayal drive the story. Critics have praised Guts as one of manga's most compelling protagonists, and his struggle against fate and trauma is central to Berserk's themes.
