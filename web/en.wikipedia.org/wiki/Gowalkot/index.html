Gowalkot is a small fortified island and village in the Vashishti River estuary near Chiplun, in Ratnagiri district of Maharashtra, India. The island, roughly 2 hectares in area, lies close to the town of Chiplun on the Konkan coast and is known for its small island fort.

The fort

The fortifications on Gowalkot were built in the 17th-18th centuries, during the period when control of the Konkan coast was contested between the Marathas, the Siddis of Janjira, and the British. The island fort was part of the network of coastal defences guarding the trade of the Vashishti river. The remains include bastions, defensive walls, and a temple on the island.

Geography

Gowalkot sits in the tidal reach of the Vashishti, surrounded by mangrove-fringed creeks. At high tide it is fully an island; the surrounding waters are used by local fishing boats. The town of Chiplun, a commercial centre on the Mumbai-Goa highway, lies a short distance inland.
