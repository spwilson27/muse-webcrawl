Boogaloo movement
Origin: United States, online forums
Ideology: Anti-government, pro-gun, accelerationist currents
Known for: Hawaiian shirts, 'big luau' slang

The boogaloo movement is a loosely organized American far-right anti-government extremist movement. Adherents say they are preparing for, or seek to incite, a second American Civil War, which they call the 'boogaloo'.

Origins

The term emerged on 4chan and other forums in the 2010s, derived from the film Breakin' 2: Electric Boogaloo used jokingly for sequels. Variants include 'big igloo' and 'big luau', giving rise to Hawaiian shirts as an identifier.

Beliefs and incidents

Participants range from libertarian gun-rights activists to white supremacists and accelerationists. Some adherents appeared at 2020 protests and lockdown protests; several were charged in violent plots, including the murder of a federal officer in California in 2020.

Response

Social media platforms banned many boogaloo groups in 2020. Researchers and law enforcement monitor the movement as a domestic extremism concern.
