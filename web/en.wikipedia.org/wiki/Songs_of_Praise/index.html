Songs of Praise


Genre: Religious broadcasting
Country: United Kingdom
Network: BBC One
First broadcast: 1 October 1961

Songs of Praise is a British television programme broadcast by the BBC, featuring Christian hymns and worship songs sung by choirs and congregations in churches and cathedrals around the United Kingdom, together with interviews and stories of faith. First broadcast in October 1961, it is one of the longest-running religious television programmes in the world.

Format

Each edition visits a different location, combining congregational singing of well-known hymns with features about local people and causes. Presenters have included Pam Rhodes, Aled Jones, Katherine Jenkins, and Sean Fletcher. Special editions include the annual search for the nation's favourite hymn and the Gospel Choir of the Year.

History

The programme began in 1961 from the Tabernacle Baptist Church in Cardiff and built a large Sunday audience. Over the decades its style has broadened from traditional hymn singing to include contemporary worship music and a wider range of Christian traditions, while remaining a fixture of the BBC's religious output.
