Rogue One

Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (or simply Rogue One) is a 2016 American epic space opera film directed by Gareth Edwards. The screenplay by Chris Weitz and Tony Gilroy is from a story by John Knoll and Gary Whitta. It was produced by Lucasfilm and distributed by Walt Disney Studios Motion Pictures.

Plot

Set immediately before the events of the original Star Wars (1977), the film follows Jyn Erso, the daughter of Imperial weapons engineer Galen Erso, who joins a band of Rebel fighters — including Cassian Andor, Chirrut Îmwe, Baze Malbus, Bodhi Rook, and the reprogrammed Imperial droid K-2SO — on a mission to steal the plans for the Death Star from the planet Scarif.

Cast and production

The film stars Felicity Jones, Diego Luna, Ben Mendelsohn, Donnie Yen, Mads Mikkelsen, Alan Tudyk, Jiang Wen, and Forest Whitaker. Extensive reshoots in 2016, overseen in part by Gilroy, reshaped the final act. Michael Giacchino composed the score.

Reception

Rogue One grossed over $1 billion worldwide and was praised for its performances, action sequences, and darker tone. It received two Academy Award nominations, for Best Sound Mixing and Best Visual Effects. A prequel television series, Andor, premiered on Disney+ in 2022.
