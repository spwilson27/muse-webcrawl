List of national parks of South Africa

South Africa has 19–20 national parks managed by South African National Parks (SANParks), covering more than 37,000 km² — roughly 3–4% of the country's land area. The oldest and most famous is Kruger National Park, proclaimed in its modern form in 1926 out of the earlier Sabi Game Reserve (1898); at nearly 20,000 km² it is one of Africa's largest game reserves and home to the "Big Five". The parks span ecosystems from fynbos and karoo to savanna, desert and coastline.

History

The park system traces to Paul Kruger's proclamation of the Sabi Game Reserve in 1898, expanded under warden James Stevenson-Hamilton. The National Parks Act of 1926 united several reserves and created Kruger National Park, opened to the public in 1927 (three cars visited that year). SANParks today emphasises conservation, community benefit and tourism, managing over 4 million hectares including marine protected areas adjacent to coastal parks.
