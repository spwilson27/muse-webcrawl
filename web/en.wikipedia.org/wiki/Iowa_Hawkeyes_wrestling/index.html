Iowa Hawkeyes wrestling


University: University of Iowa
Conference: Big Ten
Arena: Carver–Hawkeye Arena
Head coach: Tom Brands (since 2006)

The Iowa Hawkeyes wrestling team represents the University of Iowa in NCAA Division I college wrestling and is one of the most successful programmes in the sport's history, with 24 NCAA team championships. The Hawkeyes compete in the Big Ten Conference and host home meets at Carver–Hawkeye Arena in Iowa City, regularly leading the nation in attendance.

History

Under coach Dan Gable (1976–1997), Iowa won 15 NCAA team titles, including nine consecutive championships from 1978 to 1986, and produced numerous individual champions and Olympians; Gable himself had won Olympic gold in 1972 without conceding a point. Jim Zalesky continued the run with three titles (1998–2000). Tom Brands, a former Hawkeye and Olympic champion, became head coach in 2006 and has led Iowa to further team titles, including 2008–2010 and 2021.

Notable wrestlers

Standout Hawkeyes include Ed and Lou Banach, Barry Davis, Mark Ironside, Brent Metcalf, Tony Ramos, and Spencer Lee, a three-time NCAA champion and Hodge Trophy winner. Iowa's intense rivalry with Oklahoma State, Penn State, and Iowa State is a centrepiece of the collegiate wrestling season.
