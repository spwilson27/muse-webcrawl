Guy Stuart Ritchie (born 10 September 1968) is an English film director, producer and screenwriter, known for his fast-cut, stylised crime films and for later big-budget studio pictures including the Sherlock Holmes films and Disney's Aladdin.

Career

Ritchie came to prominence with his debut feature Lock, Stock and Two Smoking Barrels (1998), a comedy-crime film whose success launched the careers of several cast members and helped introduce contemporary British gangster cinema to a wide audience. He followed it with Snatch (2000), in a similar vein. After the poorly received Swept Away (2002) and Revolver (2005), he returned to form with RocknRolla (2008).

His Sherlock Holmes (2009) and its 2011 sequel were major box-office successes starring Robert Downey Jr. and Jude Law. Subsequent films include The Man from U.N.C.L.E. (2015), King Arthur: Legend of the Sword (2017), the live-action Aladdin (2019) — his highest-grossing film — and a return to crime comedy with The Gentlemen (2019), followed by Wrath of Man (2021), Operation Fortune: Ruse de Guerre (2023), The Covenant (2023) and The Ministry of Ungentlemanly Warfare (2024). He also created the television series The Gentlemen (2024) for Netflix.
