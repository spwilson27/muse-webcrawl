Denomination

Denomination may refer to:

Religion

  • Religious denomination, a subgroup within a religion that operates under a common name, tradition, and identity, such as the denominations of Christianity (Catholic, Lutheran, Baptist and so on).
  • Christian denomination, a distinct religious body within Christianity.

Money and measurement

  • Denomination (currency), the face value of a coin, banknote or other unit of currency; the proper description of individual coins or banknotes in a currency system.
  • Denomination, in numismatics, the value assigned to a coin.
  • Redenomination, the process of changing the face value of banknotes and coins in circulation.

Other uses

  • Denomination, a class or category of things sharing a common name.
  • In winemaking, an appellation or controlled designation of origin for wine, such as a French appellation d'origine contrôlée.
