The 2012–13 UEFA Champions League was the 58th season of Europe's premier club football tournament organised by UEFA, and the 21st season since it was renamed from the European Champion Clubs' Cup to the UEFA Champions League.

Final

The final was played at Wembley Stadium in London on 25 May 2013, the first all-German final in the competition's history. Bayern Munich defeated Borussia Dortmund 2–1, with Arjen Robben scoring the winning goal in the 89th minute after Mario Mandžukić's opener had been cancelled out by an İlkay Gündoğan penalty.

Knockout phase

In the semi-finals, Bayern overwhelmed Barcelona 7–0 on aggregate, while Dortmund edged Real Madrid 4–3 on aggregate, highlighted by Robert Lewandowski's four goals in the first leg. Defending champions Chelsea were eliminated in the group stage, the first holders to fail to reach the knockout rounds.

Top goalscorers

Cristiano Ronaldo of Real Madrid finished as top scorer with 12 goals, ahead of Lewandowski and Lionel Messi.
