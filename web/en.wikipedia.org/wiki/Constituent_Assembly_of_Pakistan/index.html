Established: 1947
Dissolved: 1954 (first assembly)

Constituent Assembly of Pakistan

The Constituent Assembly of Pakistan was formed after the partition of British India to frame a constitution for the new Dominion of Pakistan and to serve as its first legislature. It held its first session in Karachi on 10 August 1947, and Muhammad Ali Jinnah was elected its president on 11 August 1947.

Work

The Assembly passed the Objectives Resolution in March 1949, moved by Prime Minister Liaquat Ali Khan, which became a foundational document of Pakistani constitutionalism. Drafting the constitution proved slow, delayed by disputes between East and West Pakistan over representation, language, and the role of Islam.

Dissolution

Before it completed a constitution, Governor-General Ghulam Muhammad dissolved the first Constituent Assembly in October 1954. A second assembly was formed in 1955 and adopted Pakistan's first constitution in 1956. Subsequent constitutions followed in 1962 and, after the secession of Bangladesh, in 1973.
