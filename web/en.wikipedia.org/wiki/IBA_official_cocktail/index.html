IBA official cocktail

An IBA official cocktail is one of the cocktails recognised by the International Bartenders Association (IBA), the professional body founded in 1951 that represents bartenders worldwide. The IBA maintains a standardised list of recipes with specified ingredients, measures, methods, and garnishes, so that a named drink is prepared consistently in bars around the world. The list also serves as the basis for the association's World Cocktail Championships.

Categories

The official list, revised periodically (notably in 2020), organises the drinks into three categories:

  • The Unforgettables — the classics, including the Martini, Manhattan, Old Fashioned, Negroni, Daiquiri, Whiskey Sour, Margarita, and the Aviation.
  • Contemporary Classics — well-established modern drinks such as the Cosmopolitan, Bloody Mary, Mojito, Caipirinha, Espresso Martini, and the Mai Tai.
  • New Era Drinks — more recent creations, including the Penicillin, Paper Plane, Spritz, Dark 'n' Stormy, and Porn Star Martini.

Role

Because the IBA recipes are public and standardised, they are widely used in bartender training and competitions and serve as a reference point in cocktail literature; individual entries on Wikipedia for these drinks generally give the IBA recipe where one exists. The list has grown and changed over time, with drinks added, removed, and moved between categories as tastes evolve.
