History of ITV

The history of ITV, the United Kingdom's first commercial television network, begins with the Television Act 1954, which created the Independent Television Authority to license regional franchises funded by advertising as an alternative to the BBC.

Launch and growth

ITV launched in London on 22 September 1955 with Associated-Rediffusion and ATV; other regions followed through 1962. A network of competing regional companies — Granada, Thames, LWT, Yorkshire, Central and others — produced shared national programming.

Consolidation

The Broadcasting Act 1990 deregulated ownership, leading to mergers that by 2004 unified almost all England and Wales franchises into ITV plc. The network later launched digital channels ITV2, ITV3, ITV4 and the streaming service ITVX.
