Janamejaya

Janamejaya (Sanskrit: जनमेजय) is a legendary king of the Kuru Kingdom and a descendant of the Pandavas, appearing in the Hindu epic Mahabharata and its frame narrative. He is the great-grandson of Arjuna and the king for whom the Mahabharata is recited.

Frame narrative

Janamejaya, son of Parikshit (who died from the bite of the serpent Takshaka), performed a sarpasatra (snake sacrifice) at Hastinapura to avenge his father. During the sacrifice, the sage Vaishampayana narrated the Mahabharata to him, retelling the Kurukshetra War between his ancestors.

Legacy

Janamejaya is remembered as a patron of sacrifice and as the pivotal listener for whom both the Mahabharata and several Puranic recitations are framed in Hindu literature.
