Punggye-ri Nuclear Test Site

Punggye-ri Nuclear Test Site
LocationKilju County, North Hamgyong, North Korea
TestsSix underground tests, 2006–2017
StatusTunnels demolished in 2018; later activity reported

The Punggye-ri Nuclear Test Site was North Korea's only known nuclear test site, located in mountainous terrain in the country's northeast. All six of North Korea's underground nuclear tests, from October 2006 to September 2017, were conducted there in tunnels beneath Mount Mantap.

History

The tests grew in yield over time, culminating in the large September 2017 explosion claimed as a hydrogen bomb. In May 2018, North Korea demolished tunnel entrances before foreign journalists as part of a diplomatic thaw, though no inspectors verified full dismantlement. Satellite imagery in later years indicated restoration activity at the site.
