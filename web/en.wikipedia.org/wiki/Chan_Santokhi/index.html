Chandrikapersad "Chan" Santokhi (born 3 February 1959) is a Surinamese politician and former police officer who served as the ninth president of Suriname from 2020 to 2025. He is the leader of the Progressive Reform Party (VHP).

Career

Santokhi trained as a police officer in the Netherlands and rose to become a senior police official in Suriname. As Minister of Justice and Police from 2005 to 2010, he led investigations into the December murders, earning the nickname "the Sheriff".

Presidency

After the VHP's victory in the 2020 general election, Santokhi was inaugurated as president on 16 July 2020. His administration dealt with a debt crisis, negotiated IMF support, and oversaw final investment decisions for offshore oil development. Following the 2025 elections he was succeeded by Jennifer Geerlings-Simons.
