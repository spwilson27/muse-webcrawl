Reliance Industries Limited is an Indian multinational conglomerate headquartered in Mumbai. Its businesses include energy, petrochemicals, natural gas, retail, telecommunications, mass media, and textiles. Reliance is one of the most profitable companies in India and the largest publicly traded company in the country by market capitalisation.

History

The company traces its origins to Reliance Commercial Corporation, founded by Dhirubhai Ambani in 1957 as a commodity trading business dealing in spices and polyester yarn. Reliance entered textiles in 1966 and expanded into petrochemicals and refining; its Jamnagar refinery in Gujarat became one of the largest refining complexes in the world. After Dhirubhai Ambani's death in 2002, the group was divided between his sons Mukesh and Anil Ambani.

Businesses

Major subsidiaries and divisions include Jio Platforms (telecommunications and digital services, launched commercially in 2016, which rapidly became India's largest telecom operator), Reliance Retail (India's largest retailer), and the oil-to-chemicals segment operating the Jamnagar complex.
