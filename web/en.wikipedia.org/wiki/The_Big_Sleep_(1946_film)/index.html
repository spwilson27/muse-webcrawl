The Big Sleep (1946 film)

The Big Sleep
Director: Howard Hawks
Year: 1946
Starring: Humphrey Bogart, Lauren Bacall
Based on: Raymond Chandler novel (1939); character Philip Marlowe

The Big Sleep is a 1946 American film noir directed by Howard Hawks, from Raymond Chandler's novel. Private detective Philip Marlowe (Humphrey Bogart) is hired by General Sternwood to resolve his daughter's blackmail case, uncovering murder, pornography and gambling in a famously convoluted plot.

Production

Screenwriters included William Faulkner. Famously, neither Hawks nor Chandler could say who killed one victim. Bacall's role was expanded after To Have and Have Not.

Legacy

A quintessential noir, added to the National Film Registry in 1997.
