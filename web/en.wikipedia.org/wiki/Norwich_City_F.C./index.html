Norwich City Football Club is a professional football club based in Norwich, Norfolk, England. Nicknamed the Canaries for the city's historical breeding of the birds and their yellow-and-green kit, the club plays its home matches at Carrow Road.

History

Founded in 1902, Norwich turned professional in 1905 and joined the Football League in 1920. The club won the League Cup in 1962 and 1985. Its finest league campaign came in 1992–93, the first Premier League season, finishing third and qualifying for the UEFA Cup, where it famously defeated Bayern Munich at the Olympic Stadium — the first English side to win there.

Recent decades

Since the 2010s Norwich has become known as a "yo-yo club", repeatedly winning promotion from the EFL Championship (including title-winning campaigns in 2019 and 2021 under Daniel Farke) while struggling to survive in the Premier League. The club's anthem, "On the Ball, City", is regarded as one of the oldest football songs in the world still in use.
