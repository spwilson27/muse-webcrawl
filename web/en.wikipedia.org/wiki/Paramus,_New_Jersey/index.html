Paramus is a borough in Bergen County, New Jersey, United States. Situated about 15 miles northwest of Midtown Manhattan, it is one of the largest retail shopping destinations in the United States, home to several enclosed shopping malls including Westfield Garden State Plaza and Paramus Park, despite having a population of only around 26,000.

History

The name Paramus derives from a Lenape word meaning "land of turkeys" or "fertile land". The borough was incorporated in 1922 from portions of Midland Township. Dutch settlers farmed the area from the seventeenth century.

Retail and blue laws

Paramus generates billions of dollars in annual retail sales. Bergen County's blue laws keep most retail stores closed on Sundays, and Paramus's own ordinances are among the strictest in the nation, restricting even many non-retail activities on Sundays.
