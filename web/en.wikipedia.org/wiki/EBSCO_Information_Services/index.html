EBSCO Information Services
Parent: EBSCO Industries
Headquarters: Ipswich, Massachusetts
Products: EBSCOhost, research databases

EBSCO Information Services, headquartered in Ipswich, Massachusetts, is a major provider of research databases, e-books, journal subscription services and discovery tools for libraries, universities, hospitals and corporations. It is a division of EBSCO Industries, founded in 1944.

Products

Its flagship platform, EBSCOhost, provides access to thousands of full-text and bibliographic databases including Academic Search, Business Source, MEDLINE, CINAHL and SPORTDiscus. The EBSCO Discovery Service indexes library collections through a single search box. The company also publishes proprietary databases, manages journal subscriptions, and supplies e-book collections.

History

EBSCO grew from a magazine subscription agency founded by Elton B. Stephens. It expanded into electronic databases in the 1980s and 1990s and acquired numerous publishers and services. In 2010 it released its discovery service, and it has continued acquiring library technology firms since.
