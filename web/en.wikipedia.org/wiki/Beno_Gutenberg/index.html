Beno Gutenberg
Born: 4 June 1889, Darmstadt
Died: 25 January 1960
Field: Seismology

Beno Gutenberg (4 June 1889 – 25 January 1960) was a German-American seismologist who made fundamental contributions to understanding Earth's interior, including the core-mantle boundary.

Career

Born in Darmstadt, Gutenberg emigrated to the United States in 1930 and worked at the California Institute of Technology with Charles Richter, with whom he developed the Gutenberg-Richter law relating earthquake magnitude and frequency.

Discoveries

He located the boundary between the mantle and core at about 2,900 km depth, studied seismic wave shadow zones, and helped establish global earthquake magnitude scales.
