List of best-selling game consoles

This is a list of video game consoles ranked by the number of units sold or shipped worldwide. It includes home consoles, handheld consoles and hybrid systems. The best-selling console of all time is Sony's PlayStation 2, with more than 160 million units sold, followed by the Nintendo DS handheld family and the Nintendo Switch.
