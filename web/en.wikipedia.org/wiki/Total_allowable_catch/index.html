Total allowable catch

AbbreviationTAC
FieldFisheries management
PurposeCap total fishery removals per period

The total allowable catch (TAC) is a catch limit set for a particular fishery, generally for a year or a fishing season, expressed in tonnes of live-weight fish or in numbers of individuals. It is one of the central output controls used in fisheries management to keep removals within levels advised by scientific stock assessments.

Allocation

A TAC is normally divided into quotas for fleets, companies or individual fishers. In some jurisdictions quotas are transferable between holders, as in individual transferable quota (ITQ) systems. The European Union sets TACs annually for most commercial stocks, shared among member states by fixed allocation keys, and similar systems operate in Iceland, New Zealand, the United States and elsewhere.

Criticism

Setting TACs above scientific advice, discarding of fish over quota, and difficulty of enforcement have all been criticised as causes of continued overfishing despite TAC systems.
