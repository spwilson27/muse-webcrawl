Information system

An information system (IS) is a formal, sociotechnical, organisational system designed to collect, process, store and distribute information. From a sociotechnical perspective, information systems are composed of four components: task, people, structure (or roles) and technology. Computer information systems are the field of study of such systems, their interaction with organisations and society, bridging computer science and business.

Types

Information systems in organisations include transaction processing systems that handle routine operations, management information systems that support reporting and decision making, decision support systems, executive information systems, and enterprise systems such as ERP and CRM platforms that integrate processes across the organisation. Beyond organisations, geographic information systems, health information systems and many other specialised forms exist.

Study and careers

As an academic discipline, information systems research examines how systems are developed, adopted and used, and their effects on individuals, groups and organisations. Development typically follows a systems development life cycle of analysis, design, implementation and maintenance.
