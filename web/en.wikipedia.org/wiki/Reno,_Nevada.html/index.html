Reno, Nevada

Reno is a city in the northwest section of the US state of Nevada, along the Nevada–California border, and the county seat of Washoe County. Known as "The Biggest Little City in the World", it is famous for its casinos and tourism, and is the birthplace of Caesars Entertainment. Its population was about 264,000 at the 2020 census.

Reno lies in the Truckee River valley at the eastern edge of the Sierra Nevada, near Lake Tahoe. Historically a railroad and mining town, it became a centre for gambling and quick divorces in the 20th century, and its economy has since diversified into logistics, technology and outdoor recreation. It is home to the University of Nevada, Reno, founded in 1874.
