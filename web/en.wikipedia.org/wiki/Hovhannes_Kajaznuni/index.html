Hovhannes Kajaznuni

Born1868, Akhaltsikhe
Died1938, Yerevan
OfficeFirst Prime Minister of Armenia (1918–1919)

Hovhannes Kajaznuni (1868–1938) was an Armenian architect and politician who served as the first Prime Minister of the First Republic of Armenia from 1918 to 1919. A member of the Armenian Revolutionary Federation (Dashnaktsutyun), he had previously worked as an architect in Baku and Tbilisi.

Premiership

Kajaznuni formed the republic's first government in the aftermath of the Battle of Sardarabad, facing famine, refugees and war with neighbouring states. His cabinet relocated from Tbilisi to Yerevan in July 1918; he resigned in 1919 and was succeeded by Alexander Khatisian.

Later life

In 1923 Kajaznuni published a self-critical manifesto arguing that the Dashnaktsutyun had exhausted its role, and he later broke with the party. He worked as an architect in Soviet Armenia, was arrested during the Great Purge, and died in prison in Yerevan in 1938.
