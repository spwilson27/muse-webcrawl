Scoot Pte Ltd, operating as Scoot, is a low-cost airline based in Singapore and a wholly owned subsidiary of Singapore Airlines. It launched flights on 4 June 2012 on medium- and long-haul routes from Singapore, principally to Australia, China, and destinations across Asia. Scoot's fleet combines widebody Boeing 787 Dreamliner aircraft for longer routes with Airbus A320 family aircraft for shorter regional services.

History

Singapore Airlines announced the creation of a new low-cost subsidiary on 1 November 2011, and the name "Scoot" and its branding were unveiled shortly afterwards. Its first aircraft, Boeing 777-200s transferred from the parent airline, began service to Sydney in June 2012. In 2014 Scoot began replacing the 777s with Boeing 787 Dreamliners, becoming an all-787 widebody operator. A short-haul sister brand, Scoot's joint venture NokScoot in Thailand, operated from Bangkok until its liquidation in 2020.

In July 2017, Scoot merged with Tigerair, the Singapore Airlines group's short-haul budget carrier, with the combined airline retaining the Scoot name and Tigerair's IATA code TR. The merger made Scoot one of the largest low-cost carriers in the Asia-Pacific region, operating both narrowbody and widebody fleets under a single brand. In 2024 Scoot began taking delivery of Embraer E190-E2 jets, the first Singaporean operator of the type, to serve thinner regional routes.

Destinations and fleet

Scoot serves dozens of destinations across Asia, Australia, the Middle East, and Europe, including Athens and, in recent years, Vienna and other European points flown with the 787. Its fleet has included the Airbus A320, A320neo and A321neo, Boeing 787-8 and 787-9, and Embraer E190-E2. The airline's branding uses a bright yellow livery and a playful tone, positioning the airline between full-service carriers and ultra-low-cost operators.
