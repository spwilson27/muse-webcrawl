Zachary David Alexander Efron (born 18 October 1987) is an American actor. He began acting professionally in the early 2000s with guest roles on television and rose to mainstream prominence as Troy Bolton in Disney Channel's High School Musical trilogy (2006–2008), which made him one of the most recognisable teen idols of his generation. He has since pursued a varied film career spanning musicals, comedies and dramatic roles.

Career

Efron's early credits included recurring roles on Summerland and guest appearances on series such as Firefly and ER. After High School Musical became a cultural phenomenon, he starred in the film adaptation of the Broadway musical Hairspray (2007) and the comedy 17 Again (2009). In the 2010s he headlined romantic dramas such as The Lucky One (2012) and comedies including Neighbors (2014) and its sequel, Mike and Dave Need Wedding Dates (2016), and Baywatch (2017). He returned to musicals with the hit The Greatest Showman (2017).

Efron won critical praise for his portrayal of serial killer Ted Bundy in Extremely Wicked, Shockingly Evil and Vile (2019). He later hosted the Netflix travel documentary series Down to Earth with Zac Efron (2020–2022), which won him a Daytime Emmy Award, and starred in the survival thriller Gold (2022), the wrestling drama The Iron Claw (2023) — widely cited as among his finest performances — and the comedy Ricky Stanicky (2024).

Personal life

Efron grew up in Arroyo Grande, California. He has spoken publicly about struggles with alcohol and substance abuse in the early 2010s and completed rehabilitation in 2013. He has since been associated with fitness, outdoor pursuits and environmental themes in his documentary work.
