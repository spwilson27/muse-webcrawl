Type foundry

A type foundry is a company or workshop that designs and manufactures typefaces and the physical type used in printing. Historically, foundries cast individual pieces of movable type from an alloy of lead, tin and antimony using hand moulds and, later, machines such as the Monotype caster, and sold the sorts to printers.

History

Typefounding began with Johannes Gutenberg's workshop in the fifteenth century and became a specialised trade: famous historical foundries include the Caslon foundry in London, the Haas foundry in Switzerland and American Type Founders in the United States. As phototypesetting and then digital typography replaced metal type, foundries such as Linotype, Monotype and Adobe became distributors of digital fonts; modern independent foundries sell font licences online.
