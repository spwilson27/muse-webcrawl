The Straw Hat Pirates (麦わらの一味, Mugiwara no Ichimi) are the main protagonist crew in Eiichiro Oda's manga and anime series One Piece. Led by the rubber-bodied captain Monkey D. Luffy, they sail the Grand Line in search of the legendary treasure known as the "One Piece", which would make Luffy the Pirate King.

Crew

The crew comprises Luffy (captain), Roronoa Zoro (swordsman), Nami (navigator), Usopp (sniper), Sanji (cook), Tony Tony Chopper (doctor), Nico Robin (archaeologist), Franky (shipwright), Brook (musician), and Jinbe (helmsman). Each carries a bounty set by the World Government; as the story progresses the crew becomes one of the most infamous and powerful pirate crews in the world.

Theme

The Straw Hats embody Oda's recurring themes of freedom, chosen family, and pursuing dreams at any cost. The straw hat itself was passed down from Shanks to Luffy, symbolising inherited will — a central motif of the series.
