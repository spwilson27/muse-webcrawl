Catskill Mountain 3500 Club

The Catskill Mountain 3500 Club, founded in 1962, is a hiking organisation whose members have climbed all 35 Catskill peaks above 3,500 feet. It was modelled on the Adirondack Forty-Sixers and was the first peak-bagging club of its kind in the eastern United States to require winter climbs.

Membership

Aspirants must climb all 35 high peaks, and repeat four of them — Slide, Blackhead, Balsam, and Panther — in winter, to earn regular membership. The club maintains summit canisters on the trailless peaks, publishes the quarterly Catskill Canister, and promotes conservation and trail stewardship in the Catskill Park.

History

Founders including Bill and Elinore Leavitt surveyed the range to establish the peak list. Membership numbers are assigned sequentially; thousands have completed the list.
