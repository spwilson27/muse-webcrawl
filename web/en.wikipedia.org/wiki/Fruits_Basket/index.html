Fruits Basket

Fruits Basket (Japanese: フルーツバスケット), sometimes abbreviated Furuba or Fruba, is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Natsuki Takaya. It was serialised in the semi-monthly Japanese shōjo manga magazine Hana to Yume from 1998 to 2006, and collected in 23 tankōbon volumes.


First volume cover

Plot

After her mother dies in a car accident, high school student Tohru Honda begins living in a tent on land belonging to the Sohma family. Taken in by Shigure Sohma and his cousins Yuki and Kyo, Tohru discovers the family's secret: thirteen members of the Sohma family are possessed by the spirits of the animals of the Chinese zodiac and transform into those animals when embraced by a person of the opposite sex or when their bodies come under stress. Tohru promises to keep their secret and gradually helps the Sohmas confront the curse and their abusive family head, Akito.

Media

The manga was adapted into a 26-episode anime television series in 2001, directed by Akitaro Daichi. A second anime adaptation, covering the entire manga, ran for three seasons from 2019 to 2021. A compilation film, Fruits Basket: Prelude, followed in 2022. The series was one of the best-selling shōjo manga of its era, with over 30 million copies in circulation.

Reception

Fruits Basket won the 2001 Kodansha Manga Award for shōjo manga, and the Tokyopop English edition was one of the best-selling manga series in North America.
