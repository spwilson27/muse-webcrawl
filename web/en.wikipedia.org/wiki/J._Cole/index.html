J. Cole
Born: Jermaine Lamarr Cole, 28 January 1985, Frankfurt, West Germany
Origin: Fayetteville, North Carolina, US
Occupations: Rapper, singer, songwriter, record producer

Jermaine Lamarr Cole (born January 28, 1985), known professionally as J. Cole, is an American rapper and record producer. Born in Frankfurt, Germany, and raised in Fayetteville, North Carolina, he was the first artist signed to Jay-Z's Roc Nation label in 2009.

Career

After a series of acclaimed mixtapes, including The Warm Up (2009) and Friday Night Lights (2010), Cole's debut studio album Cole World: The Sideline Story (2011) debuted at number one on the US Billboard 200. His subsequent albums — Born Sinner (2013), 2014 Forest Hills Drive (2014), 4 Your Eyez Only (2016), KOD (2018), and The Off-Season (2021) — also topped the charts. 2014 Forest Hills Drive was notable for achieving platinum certification without featured artists.

Dreamville

Cole founded the record label Dreamville Records in 2007, which has released the Revenge of the Dreamers compilation series and launched the Dreamville Festival in Raleigh, North Carolina.
