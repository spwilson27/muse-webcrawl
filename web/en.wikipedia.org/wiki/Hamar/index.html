Hamar

Hamar
Country: Norway
County: Innlandet
Founded: 1152 (diocese); town 1849
Population: approx. 29,000

Hamar is a town in Hamar Municipality in Innlandet county, Norway, on the shores of Lake Mjøsa, Norway's largest lake. It is the administrative centre of Innlandet county.

History

Hamar was founded as a bishopric in 1152, and its medieval cathedral — now in ruins under a protective glass structure at the Hedmark Museum's Domkirkeodden site — was a major pilgrimage destination. The town declined after the Reformation and a 1567 fire, and was refounded as a market town in 1849.

Sport and culture

The Vikingskipet ("Viking Ship") arena, built for the 1994 Winter Olympics in nearby Lillehammer, hosted speed-skating events and is one of the world's fastest ice rinks. Hamar is home to the Norwegian Railway Museum and the Hedmark Museum.
