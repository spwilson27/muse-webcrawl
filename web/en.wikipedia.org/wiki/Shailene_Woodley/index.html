Shailene Woodley

Born: Shailene Diann Woodley, November 15, 1991, San Bernardino, California, U.S.
Occupation: Actress, activist
Years active: 1999–present

Shailene Diann Woodley (born November 15, 1991) is an American actress and activist. She first gained recognition as a teenager for playing Amy Juergens in the ABC Family drama series The Secret Life of the American Teenager (2008–2013), and rose to wider prominence with supporting roles in The Descendants (2011) and The Spectacular Now (2013).

Woodley achieved international fame starring as Beatrice "Tris" Prior in the Divergent film series (2014–2016) and as Hazel Grace Lancaster in the romantic drama The Fault in Our Stars (2014). She later received acclaim for her portrayal of a sexual assault survivor in the HBO drama series Big Little Lies (2017–2019), earning Emmy and Golden Globe nominations.

Early life

Woodley was born in San Bernardino, California, and raised in Simi Valley. Her mother is a school counselor and her father a school principal. Diagnosed with scoliosis at age fifteen, she wore a brace for two years. She began modelling at age four and took acting classes as a child.

Career

After early appearances in series such as Crossing Jordan and The O.C., Woodley's film breakthrough came in Alexander Payne's The Descendants, playing the troubled daughter of George Clooney's character. She went on to headline the young-adult adaptations Divergent and The Fault in Our Stars, both major box-office successes in 2014. Later films include Snowden (2016), Adrift (2018), The Mauritanian (2021), Ferrari (2023) and Killer Heat (2024).

Activism

Woodley is a committed environmental activist. In 2016 she was arrested while protesting the Dakota Access Pipeline at Standing Rock; she later pleaded guilty to disorderly conduct. She has campaigned on ocean conservation, plastic pollution and climate policy, and serves on the board of Greenpeace USA.
