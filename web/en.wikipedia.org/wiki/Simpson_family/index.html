The Simpson family is a fictional family featured in the animated television series The Simpsons. The nuclear family consists of parents Homer and Marge and their three children: Bart, Lisa, and Maggie. They live at 742 Evergreen Terrace in the fictional town of Springfield. The family was created by cartoonist Matt Groening, who named the characters after members of his own family.

Creation

Groening conceived the Simpsons in the lobby of James L. Brooks's office in 1986, sketching the family in minutes while waiting for a meeting. He named Homer and Marge after his parents, Lisa and Maggie after his sisters, and substituted "Bart" — an anagram of "brat" — for his own name. The family debuted in shorts on The Tracey Ullman Show on April 19, 1987, beginning with "Good Night", before becoming a half-hour prime-time series in December 1989.

The nuclear family

Homer Simpson

Homer Jay Simpson is the bumbling, donut-loving father who works as a safety inspector at the Springfield Nuclear Power Plant. Voiced by Dan Castellaneta, he is characterised by laziness, gluttony, and a short temper, balanced by genuine devotion to his family.

Marge Simpson

Marjorie Jacqueline "Marge" Simpson (née Bouvier), voiced by Julie Kavner, is the patient, moral centre of the family, recognisable by her tall blue beehive hairstyle. She is a homemaker who has periodically held jobs ranging from police officer to pretzel franchisee.

Bart Simpson

Bartholomew JoJo "Bart" Simpson, voiced by Nancy Cartwright, is the ten-year-old eldest child, a mischievous prankster and underachiever whose catchphrases — "Eat my shorts!", "¡Ay, caramba!", and "Don't have a cow, man" — became cultural phenomena during "Bartmania" in the early 1990s.

Lisa Simpson

Lisa Marie Simpson, voiced by Yeardley Smith, is the precocious, saxophone-playing eight-year-old middle child. Intelligent, vegetarian, Buddhist, and environmentally conscious, she often serves as the show's voice of reason.

Maggie Simpson

Margaret Evelyn Lenny "Maggie" Simpson is the perpetually pacifier-sucking baby of the family, whose first word, "Daddy", was voiced by Elizabeth Taylor. Other guest voices for Maggie have included Jodie Foster and James Earl Jones.

Extended family

Other recurring relatives include Homer's father Abraham "Grampa" Simpson, Marge's mother Patty Bouvier, and Marge's sisters Patty and Selma. The family pets are the dog Santa's Little Helper and the cat Snowball II (and successors). Family-history episodes have introduced ancestors such as Virgil Simpson and various 19th-century relatives.

Cultural impact

The Simpsons are widely regarded as one of the most influential families in television history. In 1998, Time named Bart one of the 100 most influential people of the century, and the family received a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame in 2000.
