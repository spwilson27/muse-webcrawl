Dinis Dias

Dinis Dias was a 15th-century Portuguese explorer and navigator. In 1444, sailing in the service of Prince Henry the Navigator, Dias reached the westernmost point of mainland Africa, the Cap-Vert peninsula in present-day Senegal, and the island of Gorée. His voyage pushed Portuguese exploration past the mouth of the Senegal River, opening the way to the Guinea coast and demonstrating that the lands south of the Sahara were fertile and inhabited, unlike the arid coast further north.
