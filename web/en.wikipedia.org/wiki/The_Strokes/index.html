The Strokes are an American rock band formed in New York City in 1998. The band consists of vocalist Julian Casablancas, guitarists Nick Valensi and Albert Hammond Jr., bassist Nikolai Fraiture and drummer Fabrizio Moretti.

History

Their debut album Is This It (2001) was met with widespread critical acclaim and is frequently ranked among the greatest albums of all time; it spearheaded the garage rock and post-punk revival of the early 2000s. Follow-up albums include Room on Fire (2003), First Impressions of Earth (2006), Angles (2011), Comedown Machine (2013) and The New Abnormal (2020), which won the Grammy Award for Best Rock Album.

Style and influence

The Strokes' lean, interlocking guitar sound and Casablancas's drawled vocals influenced a generation of indie bands including Arctic Monkeys, The Libertines and Franz Ferdinand.
