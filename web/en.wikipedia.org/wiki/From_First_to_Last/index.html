From First to Last is an American post-hardcore band formed in Tampa, Florida, in 1999 by lead guitarist Matt Good. The band is best known for its mid-2000s albums featuring vocalist Sonny Moore, who later achieved worldwide fame as the electronic music producer Skrillex.

History

After releasing the EP Aesthetic (2003) with singer Phillip Reardon, the band signed to Epitaph Records and recruited the fifteen-year-old Sonny Moore as vocalist. Their debut album Dear Diary, My Teen Angst Has a Bodycount (2004) and its follow-up Heroine (2006), produced by Ross Robinson, made them central figures of the emo and post-hardcore scene. Moore left in 2007 to pursue a solo career, and Good took over lead vocals for the albums From First to Last (2008) and Throne to the Wolves (2010).

The band went on hiatus in 2010, reformed in 2013, and in 2017 released the single "Make War" with Moore back on vocals and drums by Travis Barker. They have continued to release new material and perform live since their reunion.
