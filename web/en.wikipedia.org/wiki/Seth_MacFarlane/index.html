Seth MacFarlane

Seth Woodbury MacFarlane (born October 26, 1973) is an American actor, animator, writer, producer, director, comedian, and singer. He is the creator and star of the television series Family Guy (since 1999) and The Orville (2017–2022), and co-creator of American Dad! (since 2005) and The Cleveland Show (2009–2013). He also wrote, directed, and starred in the films Ted (2012), Ted 2 (2015), and A Million Ways to Die in the West (2014).

Early life

MacFarlane was born in Kent, Connecticut, and studied animation at the Rhode Island School of Design (RISD). His senior thesis film, The Life of Larry, attracted the attention of Hanna-Barbera, where he worked as an animator and writer on shows including Johnny Bravo, Dexter's Laboratory, and Cow and Chicken.

Family Guy

Fox executives who had seen The Life of Larry and its sequel commissioned a pilot; Family Guy premiered after Super Bowl XXXIII on January 31, 1999. MacFarlane voices many of the show's characters, including Peter Griffin, Stewie Griffin, Brian Griffin, Glenn Quagmire, and Tom Tucker. The show was cancelled twice and revived in 2005 thanks to strong DVD sales and rerun ratings on Adult Swim, becoming one of the longest-running American animated series.

Other work

MacFarlane hosted the 85th Academy Awards in 2013, and his song "Everybody Needs a Best Friend" from Ted was nominated for an Oscar. A devoted singer of the Great American Songbook, he has released several studio albums of standards in a style influenced by Frank Sinatra, and he executive-produced the documentary series Cosmos: A Spacetime Odyssey (2014). He created and starred in the science-fiction dramedy The Orville. He has won several Primetime Emmy Awards, largely for his voice work and music on Family Guy.
