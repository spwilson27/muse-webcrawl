Operation Dominic

Mushroom cloud from the Dominic Housatonic shot.

Operation Dominic was a series of 31 nuclear test explosions conducted by the United States in the Pacific Ocean in 1962, during the final phase of atmospheric nuclear testing before the Partial Test Ban Treaty.

Tests

The detonations, with a total yield of about 38 megatons, took place mainly over the open ocean near Christmas Island (Kiritimati) and Johnston Island. The series included the only complete operational test of a U.S. ballistic missile with a live warhead, the Frigate Bird Polaris shot, and the high-altitude Starfish Prime detonation, whose electromagnetic pulse was felt in Hawaii.

Context

Dominic was mounted quickly after the Soviet Union ended the 1958–61 testing moratorium, and was the last atmospheric test series conducted by the United States.
