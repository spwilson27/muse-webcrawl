List of beer styles

Beer style is a term used to differentiate and categorise beers by factors such as colour, flavour, strength, ingredients, production method, recipe, history and origin. Most styles derive from the broad division between top-fermenting ales and bottom-fermenting lagers, plus spontaneously fermented beers such as lambic. The following is a selection of recognised styles, broadly following the BJCP and Brewers Association guidelines.

Spontaneous and sour

  • Lambic — spontaneously fermented wheat beer from Belgium's Pajottenland; includes gueuze (blended young and old lambic), kriek (cherries) and framboise (raspberries).
  • Berliner Weisse — low-alcohol sour wheat beer from Berlin, often served with syrup.
  • Gose — sour wheat beer with coriander and salt, from Leipzig.
  • American wild ale and modern kettle sours.
