Lady Grey (tea)

Lady Grey is a trademarked black tea blend produced by Twinings of London. It is a variation on the famous Earl Grey tea, being flavoured with bergamot oil but in smaller quantity, together with added lemon peel, orange peel and cornflower petals, producing a lighter, more delicate citrus character than the original.

History

Lady Grey was created by Twinings in the early 1990s and launched in 1994, initially for the Norwegian and wider northern European market, where consumers found traditional Earl Grey too strongly perfumed. It was introduced in the United Kingdom in 1996. The blend is named after Mary Elizabeth Grey, wife of Charles Grey, 2nd Earl Grey, the 19th-century British prime minister after whom Earl Grey tea is named. Because "Lady Grey" is a registered trademark of Twinings, other producers sell similar blends under names such as "Empress Grey" or "Duchess Grey".

Composition and preparation

The base is black tea scented with bergamot oil, supplemented with dried lemon and orange peel and blue cornflower petals. As with Earl Grey it is brewed with freshly boiled water for a few minutes and is commonly drunk without milk, sometimes with a slice of lemon; it is also sold in iced-tea and ready-to-drink forms in some markets.
