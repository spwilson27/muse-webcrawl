Post Tower

The Post Tower is a 162.5-metre (533 ft) skyscraper in Bonn, Germany, and the headquarters of Deutsche Post DHL Group. Completed in 2002, it is among the tallest buildings in Germany outside Frankfurt am Main and the tallest German building built in the postmodern style of glass-and-steel high-rise architecture of its period.

Design

Designed by German-American architect Helmut Jahn, the tower has an oval floor plan split into two offset halves separated by a glass atrium. Its double-skin glass façade allows natural ventilation for much of the year and contributes to the building's energy efficiency. Sky gardens interrupt the office floors at intervals, and the transparent envelope gives employees views over the Rhine and the Siebengebirge hills.

Construction and use

Construction began in 2000 on the site of former government buildings in the federal district of Bonn. The tower houses around 2,000 staff of Deutsche Post DHL. At night, a computer-controlled lighting scheme by artist Yann Kersalé illuminates the façade in changing colours.
