Susana Alimivna Jamaladinova (born 27 August 1983), known professionally as Jamala, is a Ukrainian singer and songwriter of Crimean Tatar and Armenian descent. She represented Ukraine and won the Eurovision Song Contest 2016 in Stockholm with the song "1944".

Early life

Jamala was born in Osh, Kyrgyzstan, to a Crimean Tatar father and an Armenian mother; her father's family had been deported from Crimea to Central Asia under Stalin in 1944. The family returned to Crimea after Ukrainian independence. Trained as an opera singer at the Kyiv Conservatory, she also performed jazz and won the New Wave competition in Jūrmala in 2009.

Eurovision 2016

Her winning song "1944" concerns the deportation of the Crimean Tatars by the Soviet regime and drew directly on her great-grandmother's experience. The entry was widely read against the backdrop of Russia's 2014 annexation of Crimea, and its victory — decided by combined jury and televote — gave Ukraine its second Eurovision win.

Later career

Jamala has released several albums moving between pop, electronics, and Crimean Tatar folk material, served as a coach on The Voice of Ukraine, and remained a prominent cultural voice during the Russian invasion of Ukraine from 2022, performing charity concerts abroad.
