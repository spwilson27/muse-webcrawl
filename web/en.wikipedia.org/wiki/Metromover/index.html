Metromover
Location: Downtown Miami, Florida
Opened: 17 April 1986
Stations: 21
Fare: Free

The Metromover is a free, electrically powered, fully automated people mover system in downtown Miami, Florida, United States. Operated by Miami-Dade Transit, it connects with the Metrorail rapid transit system and serves the downtown, Brickell, Park West and Arts & Entertainment District neighbourhoods.

System

Metromover runs on elevated concrete guideways in three loops: the Inner Loop (Downtown Loop) and two branch loops, the Omni Loop and the Brickell Loop. The system has 21 stations over roughly 7.1 km (4.4 miles) of track. Rubber-tyred vehicles, built by Adtranz (later Bombardier), run driverlessly; trains arrive every few minutes during service hours, which run from early morning until around midnight.

History

The Metromover opened on 17 April 1986 as one of the earliest downtown people movers in the United States. The Omni and Brickell extensions were completed in 1994, roughly doubling the system's length. Riding has been free of charge since 2002, a policy intended to encourage downtown circulation and transfers to Metrorail; the system carries tens of thousands of passengers on a typical weekday and is a familiar sight threading between the towers of downtown Miami.
