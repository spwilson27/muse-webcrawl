Strength training

Strength training
Also known asResistance training, weight training
EquipmentFree weights, machines, resistance bands, body weight
GoalsMuscular strength, hypertrophy, endurance, bone density

Strength training, also known as resistance training or weight training, is a type of physical exercise specialising in the use of resistance to induce muscular contraction, which builds the strength, anaerobic endurance, and size of skeletal muscles. It is a core component of sports such as bodybuilding, weightlifting, and powerlifting, and of general fitness programmes.

Principles

Effective training follows the principle of progressive overload: gradually increasing the load so muscles adapt and grow stronger. Training variables include sets, repetitions, intensity (often expressed as a percentage of one-repetition maximum), tempo, and rest intervals. Compound movements such as the squat, deadlift, bench press, and overhead press recruit multiple muscle groups, while isolation exercises target individual muscles.

Benefits

Regular resistance training increases muscle mass and strength, strengthens bones and joints, improves metabolic health and insulin sensitivity, and helps counteract age-related muscle loss (sarcopenia). Health guidelines commonly recommend muscle-strengthening activities at least two days per week.
