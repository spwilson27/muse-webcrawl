Shelf life

Shelf life is the length of time that a commodity may be stored without becoming unfit for use, consumption, or sale. In other words, it might refer to whether a commodity is safe to use, or is no longer viable, or has diminished in quality. Shelf life applies to foods, beverages, pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, chemicals and many other perishable goods.

Date labels

Regulations and manufacturers use terms such as "best before", "use by", "sell by" and expiration dates to guide retailers and consumers. A "use by" date generally relates to safety, while "best before" relates to quality. Storage conditions — temperature, humidity, light and packaging — strongly affect actual shelf life.

Determination

Manufacturers estimate shelf life through stability testing and accelerated aging studies, measuring microbial growth, oxidation, moisture change and loss of potency over time.
