List of largest hydroelectric power stations

This article lists the largest hydroelectric power stations in the world by installed generating capacity. Hydroelectricity is the largest source of renewable electricity globally. Only facilities with a capacity of 3,000 MW or more are included in the main table.

Generation records

Although the Three Gorges Dam has the largest installed capacity, Itaipu has historically produced more electricity in some years because of more favourable hydrology; Itaipu set a world annual generation record of about 103 TWh in 2016. Three Gorges surpassed that mark in 2020 with roughly 112 TWh.

Under construction

Large projects under construction or planned include the Grand Inga scheme on the Congo River in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, which could ultimately exceed 40,000 MW, and Ethiopia's Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam on the Blue Nile, with a planned capacity of about 5,150 MW.
