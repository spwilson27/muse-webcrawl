Ufotable

Ufotable, Inc. (stylised ufotable) is a Japanese animation studio founded in October 2000 by Hikaru Kondō and former Telecom Animation Film staff. Based in Suginami, Tokyo, it is renowned for blending 2D character animation with digital compositing and 3D effects.

Works

Early acclaim came with The Garden of Sinners film series (2007–2013). Ufotable's adaptations of Type-Moon works — Fate/Zero (2011), Fate/stay night: Unlimited Blade Works (2014) and the Heaven's Feel film trilogy — cemented its reputation. Its biggest commercial success is Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba (2019– ), whose film Mugen Train (2020) became the highest-grossing Japanese film worldwide at the time.

History

In 2019 the studio was investigated for tax evasion; founder Kondō was convicted in 2021, receiving a suspended sentence, and the company paid substantial back taxes and fines. Ufotable also operates cafés and the Machi Asobi event in Tokushima.
