Mamma Mia! (promoted as Mamma Mia! The Movie) is a 2008 jukebox musical romantic comedy film directed by Phyllida Lloyd and written by Catherine Johnson, based on her book for the 1999 stage musical of the same name. The film is built around the songs of the Swedish pop group ABBA, including the title song. It stars Meryl Streep as Donna Sheridan and Amanda Seyfried as her daughter Sophie.

Plot

On the fictional Greek island of Kalokairi, twenty-year-old Sophie Sheridan is about to marry Sky, but she wants her unknown father to give her away. Reading her mother Donna's diary, she finds three possible fathers — Sam Carmichael, Bill Anderson and Harry Bright — and invites all three to the wedding without telling Donna. The three men arrive, old romances are rekindled, and after a chaotic wedding eve Sophie decides not to marry yet, while Sam proposes to Donna, who accepts. The question of Sophie's paternity is left happily unresolved, with all three men content to share the role.

Production

The film was shot largely on location on the Greek islands of Skopelos and Skiathos, with additional work at Pinewood Studios in England. ABBA members Benny Andersson and Björn Ulvaeus were executive producers and appear in cameos.

Reception

Reviews were mixed, with criticism directed at some of the singing — particularly Pierce Brosnan's — but praise for the film's energy and Streep's performance. It was nevertheless a major commercial success, grossing about $695 million worldwide and becoming the highest-grossing live-action musical film of its time. A sequel, Mamma Mia! Here We Go Again, was released in 2018.

Soundtrack

The soundtrack album, featuring the cast performing eighteen ABBA songs, was a major hit, reaching number one in several countries.
