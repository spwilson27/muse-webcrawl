David Graeber

David Rolfe Graeber (12 February 1961 – 2 September 2020) was an American anthropologist, anarchist activist, and author known for Debt: The First 5,000 Years (2011), Bullshit Jobs (2018), and, posthumously, The Dawn of Everything (2021, with David Wengrow).


Born: 12 February 1961, New York City, US
Died: 2 September 2020 (aged 59), Venice, Italy
Fields: Anthropology, economic history

Career

Graeber did fieldwork in Madagascar, taught at Yale (whose non-renewal of his contract sparked controversy), Goldsmiths, and the London School of Economics. He was active in the alter-globalisation movement and helped shape the language of Occupy Wall Street, including popularising "We are the 99%".

Ideas

Debt argued debt and credit predate coinage and barter myths; Bullshit Jobs argued many modern jobs are experienced as pointless; The Dawn of Everything challenged linear narratives of social evolution, stressing early human political experimentation.
