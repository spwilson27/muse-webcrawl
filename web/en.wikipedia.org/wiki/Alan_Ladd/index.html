Alan Walbridge Ladd (3 September 1913 – 29 January 1964) was an American actor and film producer. He became a major star of film noir and Westerns in the 1940s and 1950s, invariably playing cool, understated leading men.

Career

After small roles in the 1930s, Ladd achieved stardom as the hitman Raven in This Gun for Hire (1942), the first of several films co-starring Veronica Lake, including The Glass Key (1942) and The Blue Dahlia (1946). His most famous role was the title character in the classic Western Shane (1953). Other notable films include The Great Gatsby (1949), Whispering Smith (1948) and The Carpetbaggers (1964). Through Jaguar Productions he produced and starred in films and the radio series Box 13.

Personal life

Ladd was married to his agent Sue Carol. His son Alan Ladd Jr. became a major studio executive and producer. Ladd died in 1964 from an accidental combination of alcohol and sedatives.
