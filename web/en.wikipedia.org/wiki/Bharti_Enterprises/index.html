Bharti Enterprises Limited is an Indian multinational conglomerate headquartered in Gurugram. Founded in 1976 by Sunil Bharti Mittal, the group is best known for its flagship telecommunications company, Bharti Airtel, one of the world's largest mobile network operators. The group's interests have spanned telecom, insurance, real estate, agriculture and food processing, retail, and digital services.

History

Sunil Bharti Mittal founded Bharti in 1976 in Ludhiana, Punjab, initially manufacturing bicycle parts and later crankshafts. In the 1980s the business moved into importing and assembling telecom equipment, famously introducing push-button telephones to India. In 1992, Bharti bid successfully for a cellular license for Delhi, and in 1995 launched mobile services under the Airtel brand through Bharti Cellular (later Bharti Tele-Ventures, now Bharti Airtel). The group's low-cost, outsourced "minutes factory" model revolutionized Indian telephony.

Businesses

  • Telecom — Bharti Airtel operates in India and Africa and is the group's principal business.
  • Financial services — Bharti entered insurance through joint ventures with AXA (life and general insurance); it later exited, with Bharti AXA Life merging into ICICI Prudential Life (completed in 2025) and Bharti AXA General merging into ICICI Lombard in 2021.
  • Food and agriculture — Bharti's FieldFresh Foods operates the Del Monte business in India.
  • Real estate — Bharti Realty develops commercial and telecom infrastructure properties.
  • Retail — Bharti previously ran Easyday stores and a wholesale joint venture with Walmart, Bharti Walmart, which ended in 2013.

Philanthropy

The Bharti Foundation, the group's philanthropic arm, runs the Satya Bharti School Program, providing free quality education to underprivileged children in rural India, and supports initiatives such as the Satya Bharti Abhiyan sanitation program in Punjab.
