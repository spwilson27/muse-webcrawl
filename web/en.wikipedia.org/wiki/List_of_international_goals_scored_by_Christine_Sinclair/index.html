List of international goals scored by Christine Sinclair

Christine Sinclair is a Canadian former professional soccer player who captained the Canada women's national team from 2000 until her international retirement in 2023. With 190 international goals in 331 appearances, she is the all-time leading goalscorer in international football — men's or women's.

Career summary

Sinclair scored at five FIFA Women's World Cups and won an Olympic gold medal at Tokyo 2020 (held in 2021) and bronze medals at London 2012 and Rio 2016. She scored goals against more than 40 different opponents; the United States, Mexico and China were among the teams she scored against most often. Her goals came in World Cups, Olympic Games, CONCACAF championships, qualifiers and friendlies across a 23-year international career — one of the longest in the history of the women's game.

She broke Abby Wambach's long-standing world record of 184 goals on 29 January 2020, scoring twice in an Olympic qualifying match against Saint Kitts and Nevis in Edinburg, Texas. Sinclair retired from international football in December 2023 and from club football with the Portland Thorns in 2024.
