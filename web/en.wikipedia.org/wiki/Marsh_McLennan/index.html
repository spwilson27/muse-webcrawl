Marsh McLennan (stylised as MarshMcLennan), formerly Marsh & McLennan Companies, is an American multinational professional services firm specialising in risk, strategy and people. It is the world's largest insurance broker and risk advisor, with operations in more than 130 countries and tens of thousands of employees. The company is headquartered in New York City and is listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

Businesses

  • Marsh — insurance broking and risk management services for businesses and individuals.
  • Guy Carpenter — reinsurance broking and advisory.
  • Mercer — consulting in health, wealth (investments and retirement) and careers.
  • Oliver Wyman — management consulting.

History

The firm traces its origins to 1871, when Henry W. Marsh co-founded a Chicago insurance agency; Dan Burrows and later Guy Carpenter were among the predecessor firms. Marsh & McLennan was formed in 1905 from the merger of the Marsh and McLennan agencies. Over the twentieth century it grew by acquisition, adding reinsurance broker Guy Carpenter (1923 founding), the consulting firm Mercer (acquired 1959), and management consultancy Oliver Wyman (acquired 2003). Major acquisitions included Johnson & Higgins (1997), Sedgwick Group (1998) and Jardine Lloyd Thompson (2019), the latter among the largest deals in the industry's history.

The company's offices in the World Trade Center were devastated in the September 11 attacks in 2001, in which 295 Marsh & McLennan employees and consultants were killed. In 2004–05, then-New York Attorney General Eliot Spitzer's investigation of bid-rigging and contingent commissions in the insurance industry led to an $850 million settlement by the company and reforms to its practices. The firm rebranded from Marsh & McLennan Companies to Marsh McLennan in 2021.
