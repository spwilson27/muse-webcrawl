List of One Piece characters

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The manga and anime series One Piece, created by Eiichiro Oda, features one of the largest casts in fiction. The story follows Monkey D. Luffy and the Straw Hat Pirates as they search for the legendary treasure, the One Piece, so that Luffy can become the next Pirate King.

Major allies

  • Portgas D. Ace — Luffy's sworn older brother and commander of the Whitebeard Pirates, executed at Marineford after his capture by Blackbeard.
  • Sabo — Luffy and Ace's other sworn brother, chief of staff of the Revolutionary Army, who inherits Ace's Flame-Flame Fruit.
  • Trafalgar D. Water Law — Captain of the Heart Pirates and a "Surgeon of Death", Luffy's ally from Punk Hazard through Wano.
  • Nefertari Vivi — Princess of Alabasta who traveled with the crew and is considered an honorary Straw Hat.
  • Boa Hancock — The "Pirate Empress" of Amazon Lily and former Warlord, in love with Luffy.
  • Silvers Rayleigh — The former first mate of the Roger Pirates, who trains Luffy in Haki during the two-year timeskip.

Antagonists and other figures

  • Gol D. Roger — The late Pirate King, whose execution began the Great Pirate Era.
  • Dracule Mihawk — The world's greatest swordsman and Zoro's goal; a former Warlord.
  • Sir Crocodile — Leader of Baroque Works and the first Warlord Luffy defeated.
  • Donquixote Doflamingo — The "Heavenly Demon" of Dressrosa.
  • Rob Lucci — CP9/CP0 assassin and Luffy's opponent at Enies Lobby.
  • Monkey D. Garp — Luffy's grandfather, a legendary Marine vice admiral.
  • Monkey D. Dragon — Luffy's father, leader of the Revolutionary Army.
  • Sakazuki (Akainu) — Fleet Admiral of the Marines, who killed Ace.
  • Imu — The shadowy sovereign who sits on the Empty Throne of the World Government.
