Overview

Lusail is a planned city in Qatar, about 23 km north of Doha, built largely on reclaimed coastal land as part of Qatar's National Vision 2030. It was developed by Qatari Diar and designed to house around 200,000 residents in themed districts, marinas and island developments such as the Crescent-shaped Lusail Boulevard area.

World Cup

Lusail Stadium — the largest stadium of the 2022 FIFA World Cup (about 89,000 seats) — hosted the final, in which Argentina beat France on penalties. The city's Katara Towers (the crescent-shaped Fairmont/Raffles building) became an icon of the tournament. Post-tournament conversion of the stadium has been debated.
