István Dobó

István Dobó (c. 1502 – 1572) was a Hungarian soldier and landowner, best known as the captain who defended Eger Castle against the Ottoman Empire in 1552. His successful stand against a far larger besieging army made him a national hero of Hungary.

Siege of Eger

In the autumn of 1552, during Sultan Suleiman the Magnificent's campaigns in Hungary, an Ottoman army of tens of thousands marched on Eger. Dobó commanded a garrison of roughly 2,000–2,300 soldiers, joined by townspeople and women of the town. After about five weeks of bombardment and assaults, the Ottomans withdrew before the onset of winter. The victory became legendary in Hungarian culture, celebrated in Géza Gárdonyi's novel Eclipse of the Crescent Moon (Egri csillagok, "Stars of Eger").

Later life

Dobó was rewarded with estates in Transylvania and became voivode of Transylvania (1553–1556). His later years were clouded by suspicion of treason; he was imprisoned for a time by the Habsburg court before being released, and he died in 1572. He is buried at Eger, and Dobó Square in the town bears his statue.
