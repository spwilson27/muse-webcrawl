Josef Müller-Brockmann

Josef Müller-Brockmann
Born1914, Rapperswil, Switzerland
Died1996, Zurich, Switzerland
OccupationGraphic designer, typographer
Known forSwiss Style; Grid Systems

Josef Müller-Brockmann (1914–1996) was a Swiss graphic designer and teacher, the most prominent exponent of the International Typographic Style (Swiss Style). His concert posters for the Tonhalle Zürich, built from grids, sans-serif type (especially Akzidenz-Grotesk and Helvetica), and mathematical composition, became canonical examples of objective graphic design.

Work and influence

He co-founded the journal Neue Grafik in 1958 and the design consultancy Müller-Brockmann & Co. in 1967. His books, including Grid Systems in Graphic Design (1981) and A History of Visual Communication, remain standard references for designers worldwide.
