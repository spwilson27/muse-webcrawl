Jack Brabham

Sir John Arthur Brabham AO, OBE (2 April 1926 – 19 May 2014), known as Jack Brabham, was an Australian racing driver who was Formula One World Champion in 1959, 1960 and 1966.

Career

Brabham, a former RAAF flight mechanic, began racing midget cars in Australia before moving to Britain, where he won two titles with Cooper's rear-engined cars. In 1966 he became the first — and still only — driver to win the Formula One World Championship in a car of his own construction, the Brabham BT19, and his team also won the constructors' title in 1966 and 1967.

Legacy

He was knighted in 1978. The Brabham team he co-founded won 35 races and two constructors' championships.
