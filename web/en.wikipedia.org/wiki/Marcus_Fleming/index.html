Marcus Fleming

Born: 1986/1987
Nationality: British
Occupation: Window fitter; television personality
Known for: Big Brother 2009 (UK series 10)

Marcus Fleming (born 1986/1987) is a British reality television personality best known as a housemate on the tenth series of Big Brother UK in 2009.

Big Brother

Fleming, a window fitter from London, became one of the series' most prominent housemates, noted for his long hair, darkly comic diary room monologues, and his rebellious attitude toward Big Brother's authority, which earned him the nickname "the Wolverine" style cult following among viewers. He was evicted in the final weeks of the series in a double eviction vote against fellow housemate Siavash, finishing just short of the final.

After the show

Following the series, Fleming made media appearances and remained a fan-discussed figure in retrospectives of the Channel 4 era of Big Brother.
