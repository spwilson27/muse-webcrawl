List of international sports federations

International sports federations govern individual sports worldwide, set their rules, and organise world championships. Most Olympic federations are recognised by the International Olympic Committee (IOC) and grouped under the Association of Summer Olympic International Federations (ASOIF) or the Association of International Olympic Winter Sports Federations (AIOWF).

Other major federations

Non-Olympic and recognised federations include the ICC (cricket), FIA (motor sport), FIDE (chess), the World Darts Federation, and the International Surfing Association. GAISF (formerly SportAccord) long served as an umbrella body for both Olympic and non-Olympic federations.
