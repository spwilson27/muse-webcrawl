Anastasia is a 1997 American animated musical drama film produced by Fox Animation Studios and released by 20th Century Fox. Directed by Don Bluth and Gary Goldman, it is a loose adaptation of the legend of Grand Duchess Anastasia Nikolaevna of Russia, which claims that she escaped the execution of her family in 1918. The film stars the voices of Meg Ryan, John Cusack, Kelsey Grammer, Christopher Lloyd, Hank Azaria, Angela Lansbury and Bernadette Peters.

Plot

In 1916 St. Petersburg, the sorcerer Rasputin, having sold his soul for revenge against Tsar Nicholas II, curses the Romanov family. During the ensuing revolution, the young Grand Duchess Anastasia becomes separated from her grandmother, the Dowager Empress Marie, and is presumed dead. Ten years later, the amnesiac orphan "Anya" joins con men Dimitri and Vladimir in their scheme to pass her off as the lost princess and claim a reward in Paris — unaware that she truly is Anastasia. Rasputin, undead and decaying, seeks to complete his curse by killing her, but Anya ultimately destroys his reliquary and chooses a life with Dimitri over joining her grandmother as a princess.

Music

Songs by Stephen Flaherty (music) and Lynn Ahrens (lyrics) include "Once Upon a December", "Journey to the Past", "Learn to Do It", "In the Dark of the Night" and "At the Beginning". "Journey to the Past", performed in the film by Liz Callaway (the singing voice of Anya) and as a pop version by Aaliyah, was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Original Song.

Reception and legacy

Anastasia grossed $139.8 million worldwide against a $50 million budget, becoming the most profitable film from Bluth and Fox Animation Studios. It received generally positive reviews and won several Annie Awards. A stage musical adaptation premiered in 2016 and opened on Broadway in 2017. Since The Walt Disney Company's 2019 acquisition of 21st Century Fox, the film has been available on Disney+.
