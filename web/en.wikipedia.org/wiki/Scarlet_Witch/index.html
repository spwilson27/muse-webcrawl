Scarlet Witch

Wanda Maximoff, the Scarlet Witch

The Scarlet Witch (Wanda Maximoff) is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Stan Lee and artist Jack Kirby, she first appeared in The X-Men #4 (March 1964). Originally depicted as a supervillain alongside her twin brother Pietro (Quicksilver) in Magneto's Brotherhood of Mutants, she soon reformed and became a longstanding member of the Avengers.

Powers and abilities

Wanda's abilities have been variously described as "hex powers" that alter probability, and later as powerful chaos magic and reality-warping. In the storyline House of M (2005), her breakdown led her to reshape reality and utter the phrase "No more mutants", depowering most of the mutant population — widely regarded as her most consequential and infamous act in the comics.

In other media

The character is portrayed by Elizabeth Olsen in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, appearing in films including Avengers: Age of Ultron (2015), Captain America: Civil War (2016), Avengers: Infinity War (2018), Avengers: Endgame (2019), and Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness (2022), and starring in the television miniseries WandaVision (2021), which explored her grief over the death of her partner, the android Vision.
