Women's marathon world record progression

The women's marathon world record progression lists the improvement of the world record in the women's marathon as recognised by World Athletics. Because women were long excluded from official competition, formal ratification began only in the late twentieth century.

Key marks

  • 1967 – Maureen Wilton ran 3:15:22 in Toronto, an early recognised best.
  • 1975 – Jacqueline Hansen became the first woman under 2:40, running 2:38:19.
  • 1985 – Ingrid Kristiansen ran 2:21:06 in London.
  • 2001 – Naoko Takahashi ran 2:19:46 in Berlin, the first sub-2:20.
  • 2003 – Paula Radcliffe ran 2:15:25 in London, a record that stood for 16 years.
  • 2019 – Brigid Kosgei ran 2:14:04 in Chicago.
  • 2023 – Tigst Assefa ran 2:11:53 in Berlin.
  • 2024 – Ruth Chepng'etich ran 2:09:56 in Chicago, the first women's sub-2:10 marathon.

Record categories

Since 2011, World Athletics has distinguished records set in women-only races from those set in mixed-gender races, because pacemaking by male runners can assist performance.
