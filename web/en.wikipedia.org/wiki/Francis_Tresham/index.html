Francis Tresham

Bornc. 1567, Northamptonshire, England
Died23 December 1605, Tower of London
Known forInvolvement in the Gunpowder Plot

Francis Tresham (c. 1567 – 23 December 1605) was a member of the group of English provincial Catholics who planned the failed Gunpowder Plot of 1605, an attempt to assassinate King James I and blow up the House of Lords.

Role in the plot

Tresham, cousin of plotter Robert Catesby, was recruited late into the conspiracy, in October 1605. He is the leading suspect as author of the anonymous warning letter sent to his brother-in-law Lord Monteagle, which prompted the search that discovered Guy Fawkes beneath Parliament.

Death

Arrested on 12 November, Tresham died of natural causes in the Tower of London on 23 December 1605, before he could be tried, although his head was nonetheless displayed at Northampton after his death.
