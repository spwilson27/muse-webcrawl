Anansi Boys is a fantasy novel by English author Neil Gaiman, published in 2005. The book shares a character with Gaiman's earlier novel American Gods — the trickster god Anansi, "Mr. Nancy" — but is a lighter, more comic work. It won the British Fantasy Award and the Locus Award for Best Fantasy Novel.

Plot

Charlie Nancy, an unremarkable Londoner, learns at his father's funeral in Florida that his father was the god Anansi — and that he has a brother, Spider, who inherited the divine powers Charlie lacks. Spider moves into Charlie's life, charms his fiancée and takes over his job, and Charlie's attempts to get rid of him draw the brothers into conflict with older, dangerous gods such as Tiger and Bird Woman. The story turns on the power of stories and songs, Anansi's domain.

Adaptations

The novel was adapted as a BBC Radio drama and as a television series for Amazon Prime Video, developed by Gaiman with Lenny Henry, who had narrated the audiobook.
