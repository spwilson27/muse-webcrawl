Silversmith

A silversmith is a metalworker who crafts objects from silver or other precious metals. Silversmiths must be confident in forming metal, sawing, filing, soldering, and finishing techniques such as hammering, raising, chasing, engraving and polishing.

History

The craft is ancient: silver was worked in the Near East and Mediterranean world for millennia, and in the medieval period silversmiths were organised in guilds, often together with goldsmiths. Silversmiths traditionally made tableware, vessels, cutlery, liturgical objects and jewellery. Famous silversmiths include Paul Revere in colonial America and the Bateman family in Georgian London.

Silverwork is marked in many countries with hallmarks indicating purity and origin; sterling silver is an alloy of 92.5% silver.
