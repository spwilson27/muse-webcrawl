D-segment

ClassificationVehicle size class (European Commission segments)
DescriptionLarge cars / mid-size cars
US equivalentMid-size car
ExamplesVolkswagen Passat, Ford Mondeo, Toyota Camry, BMW 3 Series (as "premium D")

The D-segment is a vehicle classification in the European segment scheme, corresponding to "large cars"—roughly equivalent to the mid-size car category in North America. D-segment cars are typically around 4.6–4.9 metres long and are aimed at families and executive buyers who need more space and refinement than a C-segment (small family) car offers, without the size and price of an E-segment executive car.

Characteristics and market

Classic D-segment models include the Volkswagen Passat, Ford Mondeo, Opel/Vauxhall Insignia, Škoda Superb, Toyota Camry, and Honda Accord, while premium marques market entries such as the BMW 3 Series, Mercedes-Benz C-Class, and Audi A4 in the same size class. Traditionally dominated by saloons (sedans) and estates (wagons), the segment has contracted in Europe since the 2010s as buyers shifted toward crossover SUVs, and several long-running nameplates were withdrawn from the European market. In North America, the equivalent mid-size class—led by the Camry and Accord—remains a significant though declining category.
