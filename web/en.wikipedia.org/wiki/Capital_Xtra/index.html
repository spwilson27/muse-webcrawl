Capital Xtra (stylised Capital XTRA) is a British digital radio station owned and operated by Global. It broadcasts nationally on Digital One DAB, digital television, and online, with FM frequencies in London. The station plays urban contemporary and dance music, including hip hop, grime, R&B, Afrobeat, and electronic dance genres.

History

The station began as Choice FM, launched in 1990 as the first British radio station devoted entirely to Black music, initially broadcasting to South London before gaining a second frequency for North London. Global (then GCap and later Global Radio) acquired the station, and on 7 October 2013 Choice FM was rebranded as Capital Xtra, a sister station to the Capital network, and rolled out nationally on DAB.

Programming

Capital Xtra's schedule has included breakfast and drivetime shows aimed at a young urban audience, specialist evening programming devoted to genres such as Afrobeat and grime, and the long-running late-night programme The Hip Hop Show. The station has also staged large-scale live events, including the Capital Xtra Reloaded and Homegrown Live tours.

Capital Xtra Reloaded

A sister station, Capital Xtra Reloaded, launched in 2019, playing classic hip hop, R&B, and garage anthems from the 1990s and 2000s.
