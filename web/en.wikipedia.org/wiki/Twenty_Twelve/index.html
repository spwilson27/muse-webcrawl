Twenty Twelve

Twenty Twelve is a British television comedy series written and directed by John Morton, broadcast on BBC Four in 2011 and BBC Two in 2012. A mockumentary about the fictional Olympic Deliverance Commission organising the London 2012 Olympic Games, it stars Hugh Bonneville as the hapless head of deliverance Ian Fletcher and Jessica Hynes as the excitable head of brand Siobhan Sharpe, with narration by David Tennant.

Reception and legacy

The series was praised for its satire of bureaucracy and management jargon and won awards including a British Comedy Award. Its characters and style continued in the sequel series W1A (2014–2017), in which Fletcher and Sharpe take jobs at the BBC.
