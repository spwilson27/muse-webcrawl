Grand Theft Auto III

Grand Theft Auto III is a 2001 action-adventure game developed by DMA Design and published by Rockstar Games. It is the third main entry in the Grand Theft Auto series and the first to be presented in a fully three-dimensional open world. It was released for PlayStation 2 in October 2001, with later versions for Windows, Xbox, and mobile platforms.

Gameplay and setting

The game is set in the fictional Liberty City, based on New York City. The player controls Claude, a silent protagonist betrayed by his girlfriend during a bank robbery, who works his way through the city's criminal underworld. Gameplay combines third-person shooting, driving, and open-ended exploration, with missions undertaken for crime families and other characters across three island districts.

Impact

Grand Theft Auto III was a landmark release: it popularized the modern open-world "sandbox" game design and was credited with establishing the template copied by numerous subsequent titles. It received universal acclaim and sold over 14.5 million copies, though its violence drew controversy and legal challenges. It was followed by Vice City (2002) and San Andreas (2004), forming a trilogy sharing the same fictional universe.
