Dee Hock

Dee Ward Hock (March 21, 1929 – July 16, 2022) was an American businessman best known as the founder and first CEO of the Visa card network. In 1968 Hock, then an executive at Bank of America, persuaded BankAmericard licensee banks to form a new company, National BankAmericard Inc., which became Visa, organised on his principles of decentralised, 'chaordic' organisation.

Visa

Hock designed Visa as a non-stock, membership organisation owned by its member banks — a system he described as neither centralised nor decentralised but distributed, enabling competing banks to cooperate on a shared credit card infrastructure. He served as CEO until 1984.

Later work

After leaving Visa, Hock promoted 'chaordic' organisations blending chaos and order, founding the Chaordic Commons and writing Birth of the Chaordic Age (1999) and One from Many (2005). He was inducted into the Business Hall of Fame in 1991.
