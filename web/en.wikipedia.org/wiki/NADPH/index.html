NADPH

Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate, abbreviated NADP+ in its oxidised form and NADPH in its reduced form, is a cofactor used in anabolic reactions, such as the Calvin cycle and lipid and nucleic acid syntheses, which require NADPH as a reducing agent.

In plants, NADPH is produced in the light reactions of photosynthesis by ferredoxin-NADP reductase. In animals, the pentose phosphate pathway is the major source. NADPH also powers the respiratory burst of phagocytes and the cytochrome P450 system.

Function

NADPH provides reducing equivalents for biosynthesis and protection against oxidative stress via glutathione regeneration.
