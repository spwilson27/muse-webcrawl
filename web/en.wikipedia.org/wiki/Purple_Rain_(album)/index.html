Purple Rain (album)


Album cover

Purple Rain is the sixth studio album by the American singer, songwriter, producer, and multi-instrumentalist Prince and his backing band the Revolution. It was released by Warner Bros. Records on June 25, 1984, as the soundtrack to the 1984 film of the same name. It is widely regarded as one of the greatest albums of all time.

Content

The album was more musically dense than Prince's previous efforts, emphasising full-band performances and multiple layers of guitar, keyboards and synthesiser. Four songs were recorded live at First Avenue in Minneapolis in August 1983 and then overdubbed; the nine-minute title track, "I Would Die 4 U" and "Baby I'm a Star" derive from that show.

Reception and sales

Purple Rain was Prince's first album to reach number one on the Billboard 200, spending 24 consecutive weeks there. Singles included "When Doves Cry" and "Let's Go Crazy", both Billboard Hot 100 number ones, and the title track, which reached number two. The album won the Grammy Award for Best Rock Performance by a Duo or Group with Vocal and was certified thirteen times platinum in the United States; worldwide sales exceed 25 million copies.
