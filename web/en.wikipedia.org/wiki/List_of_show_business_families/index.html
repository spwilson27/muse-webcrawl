Many families have produced multiple members who achieved fame in entertainment. This list of show business families collects some of the best-known acting and filmmaking dynasties.

Film and theatre families

  • Barrymore family — Lionel, Ethel and John Barrymore; later Drew Barrymore.
  • Coppola family — Francis Ford Coppola; his daughter Sofia Coppola; nephew Nicolas Cage; brother-in-law etc. Talia Shire is Francis's sister.
  • Huston family — Walter Huston, his son John Huston, and granddaughter Anjelica Huston — three generations of Oscar winners.
  • Fonda family — Henry Fonda; children Jane and Peter Fonda; granddaughter Bridget Fonda.
  • Redgrave family — Michael Redgrave; Vanessa Redgrave; Natasha Richardson and Joely Richardson.
  • Douglas family — Kirk Douglas and his son Michael Douglas.
  • Sheen / Estevez family — Martin Sheen; sons Charlie Sheen and Emilio Estevez.
  • Baldwin family — brothers Alec, Daniel, William and Stephen Baldwin.
  • Arquette family — Rosanna, Patricia and David Arquette.
  • Wayans family — Keenen Ivory, Damon, Marlon and Shawn Wayans.
  • Kapoor family — the "first family" of Indian cinema, from Prithviraj Kapoor through Raj Kapoor to Ranbir Kapoor.
  • Bachchan family — Amitabh Bachchan, Jaya Bachchan and Abhishek Bachchan.
  • Bridges family — Lloyd Bridges and sons Beau and Jeff Bridges.
  • Carradine family — John Carradine and sons David, Keith and Robert.
  • Smith family — Will Smith, Jada Pinkett Smith, and children Jaden and Willow Smith.
  • Skarsgård family — Stellan Skarsgård and sons Alexander, Gustaf and Bill.
  • Gyllenhaal family — Jake and Maggie Gyllenhaal, children of director Stephen Gyllenhaal.
  • Deschanel family — Zooey and Emily Deschanel.
  • Hemsworth family — brothers Chris, Liam and Luke Hemsworth.

Music families

  • Jackson family — the Jackson 5, Michael Jackson and Janet Jackson.
  • Carter family — pioneers of country music; June Carter Cash.
  • Marsalis family — Ellis, Wynton and Branford Marsalis (jazz).
  • Marley family — Bob Marley and his sons, including Ziggy Marley.
