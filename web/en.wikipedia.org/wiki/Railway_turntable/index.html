Railway turntable

A railway turntable, or wheelhouse, is a device for turning railway rolling stock, usually locomotives, so that they can be moved back in the direction from which they came. It consists of a circular pit with a rotating bridge carrying a section of track, often surrounded by the stalls of a roundhouse.

Turntables were essential in the steam era because steam locomotives were generally designed to run chimney-first. Although most modern diesel and electric locomotives can run in either direction, turntables remain in use at depots, heritage railways and some terminals.
