List of elevation extremes by country

This is a list of the highest and lowest points of sovereign countries. The highest point on Earth is the summit of Mount Everest (8,849 m), on the border of Nepal and China, while the lowest land point is the shore of the Dead Sea (−430 m), shared by Israel, Jordan, and Palestine.
