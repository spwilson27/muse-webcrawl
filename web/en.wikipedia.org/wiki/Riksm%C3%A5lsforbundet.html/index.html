Riksmålsforbundet

Riksmålsforbundet (English: The Riksmål Society – the Society for the Preservation of Traditional Standard Norwegian) is a Norwegian language organisation founded in 1907 by the poet Bjørnstjerne Bjørnson. It promotes and preserves Riksmål, the conservative form of the Norwegian written language based on the Danish-influenced writing tradition, as opposed to the officially promoted Samnorsk rapprochement with Landsmål/Nynorsk.

Activities

The society publishes the journal Ordet, maintains the Riksmål dictionary tradition through the Norwegian Academy for Language and Literature, and awards literary prizes, including the Riksmål Society Literature Prize (awarded since 1957) and prizes for children's literature, translation, and media language. It opposed the mid-twentieth-century language reforms that sought a common "Samnorsk" standard, and advocates the teaching of the traditional standard in schools and media.
