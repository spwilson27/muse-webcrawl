Kathleen Kennedy

Kathleen Kennedy (born 5 June 1953) is an American film producer and the president of Lucasfilm since 2012. One of the most successful producers in Hollywood history, her films have collectively grossed over $13 billion worldwide, and she has been nominated for eight Academy Awards.

Career

Born in Berkeley, California, Kennedy began in television and became an assistant to Steven Spielberg, who made her his producer on E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial (1982). In 1981 she co-founded Amblin Entertainment with Spielberg and her husband Frank Marshall, producing films including the Back to the Future trilogy, Who Framed Roger Rabbit, Jurassic Park, Schindler's List and The Sixth Sense. She later formed the Kennedy/Marshall Company.

Lucasfilm

After Disney acquired Lucasfilm in 2012, Kennedy became its president, overseeing the Star Wars sequel trilogy and spin-offs beginning with The Force Awakens (2015), as well as streaming series such as The Mandalorian and the 2023 film Indiana Jones and the Dial of Destiny. She received the Academy's Irving G. Thalberg Memorial Award in 2018, together with Marshall.
