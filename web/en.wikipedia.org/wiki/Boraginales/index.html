Boraginales

Scientific classification
Kingdom: Plantae
Clade: Angiosperms
Clade: Asterids
Order: Boraginales

Boraginales is an order of flowering plants in the asterid clade, comprising the borage family Boraginaceae and a number of related families. In the APG IV system (2016), Boraginales is recognized as a single order within the lamiids, although its internal classification has been debated, with some botanists splitting it into as many as eleven families and others retaining one broadly defined Boraginaceae.

FAMILIES

Depending on the classification, the order includes Boraginaceae (in a broad or narrow sense), Codonaceae, Coldeniaceae, Cordiaceae, Ehretiaceae, Heliotropiaceae, Hoplestigmataceae, Hydrophyllaceae, Lennoaceae, Namaceae, and Wellstediaceae. Members are often herbs or shrubs with hairy foliage and scorpioid cymes (coiled inflorescences that uncurl as flowers open).

Members

Familiar plants in the order include borage, forget-me-nots, comfrey, heliotrope, and phacelia. The order contains roughly 2,700 species distributed worldwide, especially in Mediterranean and arid regions.
