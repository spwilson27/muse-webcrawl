WGN America

WGN America was an American subscription television network owned by Tribune Media and later Nexstar Media Group. It originated in 1978 as the national satellite feed ("superstation") of Chicago's WGN-TV, became a distinct general-entertainment channel in 2014, and was replaced by the news channel NewsNation in 2021.

LaunchedNovember 9, 1978 (as WGN superstation feed)
ClosedMarch 1, 2021 (replaced by NewsNation)
OwnerNexstar Media Group

History

As a superstation, WGN's national feed carried Chicago Cubs and Bulls games, syndicated series, and movies to cable systems nationwide. In the 2010s, under Tribune's ownership, WGN America attempted a pivot to prestige original programming with series such as Salem, Manhattan, Underground and Outsiders. After Nexstar acquired Tribune in 2019, it converted the channel into the 24-hour news network NewsNation, completing the rebrand in 2021.
