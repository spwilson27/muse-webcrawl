Everything Everywhere All at Once is a 2022 American absurdist comedy-drama film written and directed by Daniel Kwan and Daniel Scheinert, known collectively as "Daniels". It stars Michelle Yeoh as Evelyn Quan Wang, a Chinese-American immigrant laundromat owner who, while being audited by the IRS, discovers she must connect with versions of herself across parallel universes to save the multiverse from a powerful adversary.

Plot

Evelyn is drawn into an interdimensional conflict when she learns that her daughter Joy's alternate-universe persona, Jobu Tupaki, threatens all existence. Using "verse-jumping" technology, Evelyn channels the skills and lives of her counterparts — including a movie star, a chef, and a rock — while confronting family estrangement, her strained marriage to Waymond, and her relationship with her father.

Reception and awards

The film premiered at South by Southwest on 11 March 2022 and was released by A24. It received widespread critical acclaim for its originality, direction, performances and emotional core, and became A24's highest-grossing film. At the 95th Academy Awards it won seven Oscars, including Best Picture, Best Director, Best Actress for Yeoh (the first Asian winner of the award), Best Supporting Actor for Ke Huy Quan, and Best Supporting Actress for Jamie Lee Curtis.
