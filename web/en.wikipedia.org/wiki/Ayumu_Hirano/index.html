Ayumu Hirano

Ayumu Hirano (平野 歩夢, born 29 November 1998) is a Japanese Olympic champion snowboarder and Olympic skateboarder. He won the gold medal in the men's halfpipe at the 2022 Winter Olympics in Beijing, after taking silver medals at the 2014 Sochi and 2018 Pyeongchang Games. He also competed in skateboarding at the Tokyo 2020 Summer Olympics, making him one of a small number of athletes to compete in both the Summer and Winter Games.

Career

Hirano was born in Murakami, Niigata, Japan, and began snowboarding at the age of four, encouraged by his father, who ran a surf and snowboard shop. He rose to prominence as a teenager, winning the Burton US Open in 2013 and an X Games silver medal the same year. At the 2014 Sochi Olympics, aged 15, he became the youngest snowboarding medallist in Olympic history at the time with his silver in the halfpipe.

At the 2018 Winter Olympics in Pyeongchang he won silver again, behind Shaun White, landing back-to-back double cork 1440s in a celebrated final. At Beijing 2022 he finally won gold with a run featuring a triple cork 1440 — the first landed in Olympic competition — after a controversially low second-run score sparked widespread criticism of the judging.

Hirano has also pursued professional skateboarding, qualifying for the park event at the Tokyo 2020 Olympics held in 2021, where he placed 14th.
