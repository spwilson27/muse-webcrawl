Kongsberg Defence & Aerospace (KDA) is a business area of the Norwegian technology group Kongsberg Gruppen, supplying defence and aerospace systems to the Norwegian Armed Forces and international customers. Headquartered in Kongsberg, Norway, it is one of the country's principal defence contractors, with operations in numerous countries including the United States, Australia and several European states.

Products

KDA is best known for the NASAMS (Norwegian Advanced Surface to Air Missile System), a medium-range air defence system developed with Raytheon and used by Norway, the United States (including for the defence of Washington, D.C.) and a growing list of export customers, including Ukraine from 2022. Its missiles division produces the Naval Strike Missile (NSM) and its air-launched derivative, the Joint Strike Missile (JSM), designed for the F-35. The Protector family of remote weapon stations is fitted to armoured vehicles of many nations, and the company also supplies command, control and communications systems, aerostructures for the F-35 programme, and space and surveillance electronics.

History

The business descends from the state armaments works at Kongsberg, founded in 1814, and from Kongsberg Våpenfabrikk, which diversified into missiles and air defence from the 1950s, developing the Penguin anti-ship missile. Following restructuring of the state-owned company in the late 1980s and 1990s, the defence activities became part of the partially state-owned Kongsberg Gruppen, and grew substantially through export sales and acquisitions in the 2000s and 2010s.
