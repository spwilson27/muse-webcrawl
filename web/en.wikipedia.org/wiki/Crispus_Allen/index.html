Crispus Allen

Crispus Allen, Gotham City detective and third Spectre

Crispus Allen is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. A police detective in Gotham City, he was created by writer Greg Rucka and artist Shawn Martinbrough and first appeared in Detective Comics #742 (2000). Allen was a central figure in the series Gotham Central, and after his death he became the third human host of the Spectre, the wrathful Spirit of Vengeance.

Fictional biography

Allen transferred to the Gotham City Police Department from Baltimore and was partnered with Renee Montoya in the Major Crimes Unit. A devoted family man and a sceptic by temperament, he distrusted Batman and the city's reliance on vigilantes. During the Infinite Crisis storyline he was murdered by the corrupt detective Jim Corrigan — a namesake, but no relation, of the original Spectre's host. Because his killer initially escaped earthly justice, Allen's spirit was chosen to host the Spectre, God's spirit of vengeance, though he at first resisted the role.

As the Spectre

As the Spectre, Allen was compelled to judge and punish the guilty, a duty that put him at odds with his own sense of justice; his first act of vengeance was against his own son, who had killed Corrigan in revenge. He starred in the miniseries Final Crisis: Revelations, hunting members of the Religion of Crime, and later appeared in connection with the Question, Renee Montoya. Following the DC Universe relaunch in The New 52, a version of Allen returned as a living GCPD detective.

In other media

A character named Crispus Allen appears in the television series Gotham (played by Andrew Stewart-Jones) as a detective and partner of Renee Montoya.
