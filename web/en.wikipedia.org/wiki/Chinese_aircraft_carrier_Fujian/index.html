Chinese aircraft carrier Fujian

Fujian (福建舰; hull number 18) is the third aircraft carrier of the Chinese People's Liberation Army Navy (PLAN) and the first of the Type 003 class. Named after Fujian province, it is China's first carrier designed and built domestically with a catapult launch system — using electromagnetic catapults (EMALS) rather than a ski jump — and was launched on 17 June 2022.

Design

Unlike the ski-jump carriers Liaoning and Shandong, Fujian has a flat deck with three electromagnetic catapults, allowing heavier aircraft such as the KJ-600 airborne early warning aircraft to operate. The ship is conventionally powered and has an estimated displacement of more than 80,000 tonnes, making it the largest warship built by an Asian country since the Second World War.

Trials

Fujian began sea trials in May 2024, testing propulsion, power generation, and later catapult launches of aircraft including the J-35 and J-15T. Chinese state media reported in September 2025 that catapult-assisted takeoffs and arrested landings of several aircraft types had been completed aboard the ship.
