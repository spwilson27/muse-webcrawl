Outdoor education

Outdoor education is organised learning that takes place in the outdoors, typically involving experiential learning, environmental education, adventure activities and personal and social development. Programmes may range from school camps and field trips to expeditions lasting weeks.

Goals commonly include building self-confidence and teamwork, developing leadership, raising environmental awareness, and improving physical health. Activities include hiking, camping, canoeing, ropes courses, orienteering and rock climbing, often combined with reflection and group discussion.

Organisations such as Outward Bound, founded by Kurt Hahn, and the Duke of Edinburgh's Award have been influential in spreading outdoor education worldwide. Research links outdoor learning to improved motivation and wellbeing, though programmes must manage safety risks inherent in outdoor environments.
