Magners

Magners is a brand of Irish cider produced in Clonmel, County Tipperary, by the C&C Group. In the Republic of Ireland the same cider is sold under its original name, Bulmers — the Magners name is used for export markets because the rights to the Bulmers name in the United Kingdom belong to a separate company, H. P. Bulmer of Hereford. Magners is made from the juice of 17 varieties of apple, fermented and matured in oak vats, and is traditionally served over ice.

History

William Magner began commercial cider production in Clonmel in 1935, in partnership with the English cider maker H. P. Bulmer; the Irish company acquired full ownership of its business in 1946 and traded as Bulmers Ltd. For export, the product was rebranded as Magners in 1999 to avoid trademark conflict with the Hereford Bulmers brand, which by then was separately owned.

Marketing and the "over ice" revival

Magners' launch in Glasgow in 2005 and London in 2006, with heavy advertising promoting cider poured over ice in a pint bottle, is widely credited with sparking a major revival of cider sales in the United Kingdom in the mid-2000s. The campaign turned cider from a declining category into a fashionable summer drink, prompting numerous competing "premium bottled cider over ice" products. The brand later extended into pear cider, flavoured variants, and sponsorships including Celtic F.C. and comedy festivals.
