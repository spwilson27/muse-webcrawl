Student society

A student society, student organization, or student club is an organization operating at an educational institution, typically a university, whose membership consists primarily of students and whose activities are organised by and for students. Societies may be based on academic subjects, hobbies, sports, culture, religion, politics, or social causes, and most universities support hundreds of them through their students' unions.

Organisation

Societies are usually run by an elected committee of students — typically a president, secretary, and treasurer — and funded by a mixture of membership subscriptions, university or students' union grants, and sponsorship. Fresher's fairs (activities fairs) at the start of the academic year are the main recruitment opportunity. Many societies affiliate to national umbrella bodies for their activity.

By country

In the United Kingdom, societies are administered and funded through students' unions. In the Netherlands and Flanders, traditional studentenverenigingen (student associations) combine social and organisational roles, distinct from study associations. In Germany, Studentenverbindungen (including fraternities such as Burschenschaften and Corps) are historically significant forms of student society, while in North America fraternities and sororities and thousands of registered student organizations serve similar functions.
