List of dinosaur-bearing rock formations

This is a list of geological formations (and, informally, other stratigraphic units) which are known to produce dinosaur fossils. Dinosaur remains are found on every continent, but a relatively small number of exceptionally productive units — many of them Lagerstätten with articulated skeletons or soft-tissue preservation — account for a large share of known genera.

Major dinosaur-bearing formations

North America

  • Morrison Formation (USA, Late Jurassic) — Allosaurus, Apatosaurus, Stegosaurus, Diplodocus
  • Hell Creek Formation (USA, latest Cretaceous) — Tyrannosaurus, Triceratops, Edmontosaurus
  • Dinosaur Park Formation (Alberta, Canada) — extraordinarily diverse hadrosaurs and ceratopsians
  • Chinle Formation (USA, Late Triassic) — early dinosaurs such as Coelophysis (also from the Ghost Ranch quarries)
  • Cedar Mountain, Kaiparowits, Lance, Judith River, Two Medicine, Fruitland, and Kirtland formations

South America

  • Candeleros and Huincul formations (Argentina) — giant titanosaurs such as Argentinosaurus and the theropod Giganotosaurus
  • Ischigualasto Formation (Argentina, Late Triassic) — among the oldest known dinosaurs, Eoraptor and Herrerasaurus
  • Santana (Romualdo) Formation (Brazil) — pterosaurs and fish, with some dinosaurs

Asia

  • Yixian and Jiufotang formations (Liaoning, China — the Jehol Biota) — feathered dinosaurs such as Sinosauropteryx, Microraptor, and Yutyrannus
  • Djadochta and Barun Goyot formations (Mongolia) — Protoceratops, Velociraptor, and the famous "fighting dinosaurs"
  • Nemegt Formation (Mongolia) — Tarbosaurus, Deinocheirus, giant sauropods
  • Lameta Formation (India) — Rajasaurus and titanosaur nesting sites
  • Shishugou and Wucaiwan formations (Xinjiang, China)

Europe, Africa, and Australia

  • Solnhofen Limestone (Germany, Late Jurassic) — Archaeopteryx
  • Wealden Group (England) — Iguanodon, Baryonyx, Hypsilophodon
  • Tendaguru Formation (Tanzania) — Giraffatitan, Kentrosaurus
  • Kem Kem Group (Morocco) — Spinosaurus, Carcharodontosaurus
  • Bahariya Formation (Egypt) — the original Spinosaurus material
  • Winton Formation (Australia) — Australovenator, Diamantinasaurus
  • Elliot Formation (South Africa, Triassic–Jurassic) — Massospondylus
  • Maevarano Formation (Madagascar) — Majungasaurus
