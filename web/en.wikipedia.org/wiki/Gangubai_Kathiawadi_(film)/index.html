Gangubai Kathiawadi (film)

Gangubai Kathiawadi is a 2022 Indian Hindi-language biographical crime drama film directed by Sanjay Leela Bhansali and produced by Bhansali and Jayantilal Gada. Based on the book Mafia Queens of Mumbai by S. Hussain Zaidi, it tells the story of Gangubai Kothewali, whose life was documented and she rose to power as a brothel madam and political figure in Kamathipura, Mumbai. The film stars Alia Bhatt in the title role, alongside Shantanu Maheshwari, Vijay Raaz, Indira Tiwari, Seema Pahwa, Varun Kapoor and Jim Sarbh, with Ajay Devgn in a special appearance.

The film premiered at the 72nd Berlin International Film Festival on 16 February 2022, and was released in cinemas on 25 February 2022. It received widespread critical acclaim, with particular praise for Bhatt's performance, and emerged as a commercial success at the box office, grossing over ₹200 crore worldwide. At the 69th National Film Awards, the film won five awards, including Best Actress for Bhatt.

Plot

Ganga Jagjeevan Kathiawadi, a young girl from Kathiawar who aspires to become a Bollywood actress, elopes to Mumbai with her boyfriend, who sells her to a brothel in Kamathipura. Forced into prostitution, she gradually asserts herself, adopts the name Gangubai, and rises to become the brothel's madam. She builds an alliance with the local mafia don Rahim Lala, fights for the rights and dignity of sex workers and their children, contests an election, and addresses a gathering at Azad Maidan demanding the legalisation and protection of sex workers.

Reception

The film was praised for its production design, cinematography, and Bhatt's central performance, and featured prominently in year-end lists of the best Hindi films of 2022.
