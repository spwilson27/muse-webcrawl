Cyclohexane

Cyclohexane
Formula: C6H12
Appearance: colourless liquid
Boiling point: 80.7 °C

Cyclohexane is a cycloalkane with the molecular formula C6H12. It is a colourless, flammable liquid with a distinctive detergent-like odour, produced industrially by the hydrogenation of benzene.

Conformation

The six-carbon ring is not planar; it adopts interconverting "chair" conformations that minimise angle and torsional strain. In the chair form, substituents occupy axial or equatorial positions, and ring flipping exchanges them — a foundational concept in conformational analysis of organic chemistry.

Uses

Most cyclohexane is converted to cyclohexanone and cyclohexanol (KA oil), intermediates in the manufacture of adipic acid and caprolactam, which are used to make nylon 6,6 and nylon 6. Smaller amounts serve as a solvent and paint remover.
