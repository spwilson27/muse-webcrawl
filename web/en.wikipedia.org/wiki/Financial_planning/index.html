Financial planning

Financial planning, in personal finance, is the process of setting financial goals and preparing a strategy to achieve them. It typically encompasses budgeting and cash-flow management, saving and investing, retirement planning, tax planning, insurance and risk management, and estate planning.

The planning process

Professional standards, such as those of the CFP Board, describe a multi-step process: understand the client's circumstances, identify and select goals, analyse the client's current course of action and potential alternatives, develop recommendations, implement them, and monitor progress and update the plan as circumstances change. A plan is built on assumptions about inflation, investment returns, longevity, and future earnings, and is revisited periodically.

Professionals

Financial planners may hold credentials such as Certified Financial Planner (CFP), Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA), or Chartered Financial Consultant (ChFC). Advice may be compensated by fees, commissions, or a combination, and advisors may owe a fiduciary duty to act in the client's interest, depending on their role and jurisdiction.
