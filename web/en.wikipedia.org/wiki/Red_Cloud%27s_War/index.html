Red Cloud's War

Date: 1866–1868
Location: Powder River Country, Wyoming/Montana
Result: Treaty of Fort Laramie (1868)

Red Cloud's War was an armed conflict between an alliance of Lakota, Northern Cheyenne, and Northern Arapaho peoples and the United States, fought from 1866 to 1868 over control of the Powder River Country in present-day Wyoming and Montana.

Course

The war was triggered by the construction of U.S. forts along the Bozeman Trail, which crossed prized hunting grounds. Under the leadership of the Oglala Lakota chief Red Cloud, Native warriors besieged the forts; the Fetterman Fight of December 1866, in which 81 U.S. soldiers were killed, was the army's worst defeat on the northern plains to that date.

Outcome

The Treaty of Fort Laramie (1868) conceded the Native demands: the forts were abandoned and burned, and the Powder River Country was recognized as unceded hunting territory — a rare clear victory for Plains peoples, though the peace later collapsed in the Great Sioux War of 1876.
