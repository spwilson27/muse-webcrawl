Myitsone Dam

The Myitsone Dam is a large hydroelectric dam project under construction (and suspended since 2011) on the Irrawaddy River in Kachin State, Myanmar. Located at the confluence of the Mali and N'Mai rivers — the Myitsone — which forms the start of the Irrawaddy, the project was planned as one of the world's largest hydroelectric stations, with a capacity of 6,000 MW.

Controversy and suspension

The project, developed by the Chinese state-owned China Power Investment Corporation with Myanmar partners, drew widespread opposition over environmental damage to the Irrawaddy, displacement of Kachin villagers, seismic risk, and the fact that most of the electricity was contracted for export to China. In September 2011 President Thein Sein announced a suspension of construction for the duration of his term, a landmark decision in Myanmar's reform era; the project has remained stalled and a major issue in Myanmar–China relations.
