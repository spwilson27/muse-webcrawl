Dalton Minimum

The Dalton Minimum was a period of low sunspot activity lasting roughly from 1790 to 1830, named after English meteorologist John Dalton. Like the Maunder Minimum, it coincided with below-average global temperatures.


Period: c. 1790–1830
Named after: John Dalton

Description

Solar cycles 5 and 6 were unusually weak. The period overlapped volcanic cooling, notably the 1815 Tambora eruption causing the Year Without a Summer in 1816.

Climate impact

Some regions saw temperature drops around 1°C during the later Little Ice Age. Tree rings and ice cores are used to reconstruct this activity.
