KDE

KDE is an international free software community that develops free and open-source software, best known for the KDE Plasma desktop environment and a wide range of applications built on the Qt framework. The project was founded in 1996 by Matthias Ettrich, who sought a consistent, user-friendly desktop for Unix systems.

The name originally stood for "K Desktop Environment" (a play on the Common Desktop Environment, CDE). Today "KDE" refers to the community itself, while its products include Plasma (the desktop), KDE Frameworks (libraries), and applications such as the Dolphin file manager, Kate text editor, Okular document viewer and the Krita painting program.

KDE software is developed by a worldwide community of volunteers and is supported by the non-profit KDE e.V. organisation in Germany. Its mascot is Konqi, a green dragon.
