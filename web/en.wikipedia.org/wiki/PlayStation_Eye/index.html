PlayStation Eye

The PlayStation Eye camera for PlayStation 3

The PlayStation Eye is a digital camera device for the PlayStation 3, developed by Sony Computer Entertainment and released in 2007 as the successor to the EyeToy camera for the PlayStation 2. It connects to the console by USB and allows motion-controlled and augmented-reality play, video chat and user-created content.

Features

The camera captures video at 640×480 resolution at 60 frames per second, or 320×240 at 120 frames per second, and has a two-setting zoom lens and a four-microphone array that can localise sound and suppress background noise. It shipped bundled with the augmented-reality title The Eye of Judgment. From 2010 the PlayStation Eye also served as the tracking camera for the PlayStation Move motion controller, following the glowing sphere on the controller to determine its position in space.

Games and software

Titles using the camera include EyePet, SingStar entries, LittleBigPlanet's creation tools and various downloadable games, and the console's system software supported video calling between PS3 users. The PlayStation Eye was succeeded on the PlayStation 4 by the PlayStation Camera.
