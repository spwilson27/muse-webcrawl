Beach volleyball
Players: 2 per team (professional)
Court: Sand, 16 × 8 m
Olympic sport: Since 1996

Beach volleyball is a team sport played by two teams on a sand court divided by a net. The professional game is played two-against-two, and beach volleyball has been an Olympic sport since the Atlanta 1996 Games.

Rules

Teams rally to ground the ball on the opponents' side. Matches are best of three sets, played to 21 points (15 in the third). Unlike indoor volleyball, there are no positional faults, and the block counts as a team contact.

History

The game originated on the beaches of Hawaii and California in the early 20th century and professionalised in California in the 1970s, leading to a world tour governed by the FIVB.

Major competitions

The Olympic tournament, the Beach Volleyball World Championships and the Volleyball World Beach Pro Tour are the sport's premier events.
