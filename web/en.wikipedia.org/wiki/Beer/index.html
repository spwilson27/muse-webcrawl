Beer

Beer
IngredientsWater, malted barley (or other grain), hops, yeast
Oldest evidenceAncient Mesopotamia and Egypt
StylesLager, ale, stout, IPA, and many more
[Image: A frothy glass of golden lager with barley and hops beside it]

Beer is one of the world's oldest and most consumed alcoholic drinks, brewed from cereal grains — most often malted barley — flavoured with hops and fermented by yeast. Brewing accompanied the first farming societies growing wheat and barley.

Brewing

Beer styles divide broadly into ales (warm-fermented) and lagers (cold-fermented); industrialisation added refrigeration, pure yeast cultures and global brands, while a craft movement revived variety. Beer was a wage and a ration in ancient Egypt — known to Egyptologists — and remains culturally central from Munich's Oktoberfest to Geneva's bars.
