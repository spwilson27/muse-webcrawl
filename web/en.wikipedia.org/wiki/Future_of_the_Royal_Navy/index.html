Future of the Royal Navy

The future of the Royal Navy concerns the planned warships, submarines and programmes of the United Kingdom's naval service, including vessels under construction, on order, or proposed in defence reviews such as the Strategic Defence and Security Review and subsequent Integrated Reviews.

Aircraft carriers

The two Queen Elizabeth-class aircraft carriers, HMS Queen Elizabeth and HMS Prince of Wales, form the centrepiece of the future fleet, operating F-35B Lightning II jets.

Escorts

Planned escorts include the Type 26 City-class frigates (anti-submarine warfare), the Type 31 Inspiration-class general-purpose frigates, and the future Type 83 destroyer intended to succeed the Type 45 Daring-class air-defence destroyers.

Submarines

The Astute-class attack submarines are entering service, while the Dreadnought-class ballistic missile submarines will replace the Vanguard class as the United Kingdom's nuclear deterrent. Under AUKUS, the SSN-AUKUS design will succeed the Astute class.

Support and auxiliaries

Fleet Solid Support ships and Multi-Role Support Ships are planned to sustain carrier and amphibious groups, alongside new autonomous mine-hunting and survey systems.
