Sagarmatha National Park

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

[Image: Mount Everest and the Khumbu valley inside Sagarmatha National Park: prayer-flagged villages beneath immense snowy peaks]
Everest region, Sagarmatha National Park.

Sagarmatha National Park is a protected area in the Himalayas of eastern Nepal, established in 1976 and covering about 1,148 km² in the Solukhumbu District. It contains much of Mount Everest and other seven- and eight-thousand-metre peaks, and was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1979. The park is home to the Sherpa people.

Contents1. Nature and tourism

Nature and tourism

The park ranges from deep gorges to the summit of Everest, with pine and rhododendron forests, and wildlife including red panda, musk deer, Himalayan tahr and the occasional snow leopard. Namche Bazaar, the park's gateway town, serves tens of thousands of trekkers and climbers each year on the classic Everest trail.
