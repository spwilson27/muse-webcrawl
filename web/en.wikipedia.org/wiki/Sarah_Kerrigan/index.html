Sarah Louise Kerrigan, the self-styled Queen of Blades, is a fictional character in Blizzard Entertainment's StarCraft franchise. She first appears in the 1998 real-time strategy game StarCraft as a twenty-six-year-old Terran psychic operative, a "Ghost", serving the rebel leader Arcturus Mengsk.

Story

Betrayed by Mengsk and abandoned to the Zerg on the planet Tarsonis, Kerrigan is captured and infested by the Zerg Overmind, which transforms her into a powerful human–Zerg hybrid. As the Queen of Blades she becomes one of the most feared powers in the Koprulu sector, outmaneuvering Terran, Zerg, and Protoss factions alike in the expansion Brood War.

In StarCraft II, Kerrigan is the central figure of the trilogy's plot: she is partially restored to human form in Wings of Liberty, seeks revenge against Mengsk in Heart of the Swarm by reuniting the Zerg swarm, and ultimately ascends to become a xel'naga to defeat the fallen Amon in Legacy of the Void.

Reception

Kerrigan is widely considered one of the most iconic villains and characters in video game history, frequently appearing on lists of the greatest game characters. Her complex portrayal as both victim and tyrant has been praised by critics.
