Pratt & Whitney F119

TypeAfterburning turbofan
ManufacturerPratt & Whitney
Major applicationsLockheed Martin F-22 Raptor

An F119 engine on a test stand

The Pratt & Whitney F119, company designation PW5000, is an afterburning turbofan engine developed for the Lockheed Martin F-22 Raptor stealth fighter. In pairs, the engines give the F-22 supercruise capability — sustained supersonic flight without afterburner.

Design

The F119 produces more than 35,000 lbf (156 kN) of thrust with afterburner. It features a three-stage fan and six-stage compressor driven by single-stage turbines, integrally bladed rotors, and two-dimensional thrust-vectoring nozzles that pitch ±20 degrees to enhance manoeuvrability.

Development

The engine grew out of the Joint Advanced Fighter Engine programme of the 1980s and was selected over the competing General Electric YF120.
