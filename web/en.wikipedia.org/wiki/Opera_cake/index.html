Opera cake

Opera cake (French: gâteau opéra) is a French cake made of layers of almond sponge cake (known as joconde in French) soaked in coffee syrup, layered with ganache and coffee-flavoured French buttercream, and covered in a chocolate glaze.

The cake is traditionally rectangular and finished with a smooth, glossy dark chocolate icing, sometimes with the word "Opéra" written on top or decorated with gold leaf. Popular accounts attribute its creation in the 1950s to the Parisian pâtisserie Dalloyau, where it is said to have been created by Cyriaque Gavillon and named by his wife after the Paris Opera.

Opera cake is regarded as a classic of French pâtisserie and a test of a pastry chef's precision, because its many thin layers must be even and cleanly cut.
