Söğüt

CountryTurkey
ProvinceBilecik
SignificanceFirst capital of the Ottoman Empire

Söğüt is a town and district in Bilecik Province in northwestern Turkey. It is celebrated as the birthplace of the Ottoman Empire: the small beylik founded by Ertuğrul Gazi and his son Osman I in the late 13th century had Söğüt as its first seat.

History

Ertuğrul, leader of the Kayı tribe, was granted lands around Söğüt by the Seljuk Sultanate of Rum. His son Osman I, founder of the Ottoman dynasty, is commemorated by monuments and an annual festival in the town. The tomb of Ertuğrul Gazi in Söğüt remains a site of pilgrimage and ceremony, guarded by soldiers in traditional Alp costume.

Today

The town hosts the Ertuğrul Gazi Museum and hosts annual Ertuğrul Gazi commemoration festivities drawing visitors from across Turkey. Agriculture and marble quarrying are local industries.
