Dame Rosemary Anne Squire DBE (born 1956) is a British theatre producer and businesswoman. With her husband, Sir Howard Panter, she co-founded the Ambassador Theatre Group (ATG) in 1992 and built it into the world's largest live theatre company, operating or programming dozens of venues in the United Kingdom, the United States and continental Europe, including many West End and Broadway theatres.

Career

Squire grew up in Nottingham, where her parents ran a cycle business, and studied at university before beginning her career in theatre administration. She worked at the Wyvern Theatre, Swindon, and in London theatre management and producing before acquiring, with Panter, the Duke of York's Theatre in 1992 — the foundation of ATG. Through acquisitions including the Ambassador Theatres group (from which the company took its name) and, later, major venue portfolios in the UK and theatres on Broadway such as the Lyric Theatre, ATG expanded into ticketing, production and theatre education. In 2016 Squire and Panter stepped back from day-to-day leadership while remaining significant shareholders and producers through their production company.

Recognition

Squire has repeatedly appeared in lists of the most influential figures in British theatre. She was appointed OBE in 2007 and created a Dame Commander of the Order of the British Empire (DBE) in the 2018 New Year Honours for services to theatre. Together with Panter she has also been recognised for philanthropy in the arts and for supporting regional theatre and new writing.
