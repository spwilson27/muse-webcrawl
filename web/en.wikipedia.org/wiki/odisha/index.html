Odisha

Odisha
Capital: Bhubaneswar
Language: Odia
Location: Eastern India

Odisha, formerly known as Orissa, is a state in eastern India, located in the northeastern part of the Indian peninsula. It is bordered by West Bengal and Jharkhand to the north, Chhattisgarh to the west, Andhra Pradesh to the south, and the Bay of Bengal to the east.

History

The region corresponds in part to the ancient kingdom of Kalinga, whose invasion by the Mauryan emperor Ashoka in the 3rd century BCE is said to have prompted his conversion to Buddhism. Odisha was formed as a province in 1936 and renamed from Orissa in 2011.

Culture

Odia is the official language. The state is known for the temples of Puri, Konark, and Bhubaneswar, the Odissi classical dance, and the Ratha Yatra festival.
