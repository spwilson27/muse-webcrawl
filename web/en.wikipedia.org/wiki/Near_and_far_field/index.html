Near and far field

The near field and far field are regions of the electromagnetic field around an object such as a transmitting antenna. In the far field (Fraunhofer region), radiation dominates, field strength falls as 1/r, and the angular pattern is independent of distance. In the near field (Fresnel and reactive regions), non-radiative and evanescent components are significant.

Boundaries

The far field is commonly taken to begin beyond the Fraunhofer distance 2D²/λ, where D is the largest dimension of the radiator and λ the wavelength. The distinction matters in antenna measurement, optics, acoustics and wireless power transfer.
