Leave.EU

Formation2015
FoundersArron Banks, Richard Tice
PurposeCampaign for UK withdrawal from the EU
HeadquartersBristol, England

Leave.EU was a political campaign organisation that supported the withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the European Union in the 2016 referendum. Founded in 2015 by businessman Arron Banks and property developer Richard Tice, it was originally launched as "The Know" before rebranding. Leave.EU campaigned for the official designation from the Electoral Commission but lost out to Vote Leave in April 2016.

Referendum campaign

Working closely with UKIP and its leader Nigel Farage, Leave.EU ran a populist, social-media-heavy campaign emphasising immigration and sovereignty. It claimed a large online supporter base and was credited with energetic grassroots mobilisation, though its messaging — including the controversial "Breaking Point" poster unveiled by Farage — drew criticism. Despite lacking official status, the organisation spent millions of pounds, largely funded by Banks, the largest single donor of the referendum.

Investigations and fines

Leave.EU was investigated by the Electoral Commission over the sources and reporting of its funding and spending. In 2018 the Commission fined the organisation and referred Banks's companies to the National Crime Agency over suspected offences concerning loans used to fund the campaign; the NCA later concluded in 2019 that it had found no evidence of criminal offences in the matters referred to it. Leave.EU and Banks were also fined by the Information Commissioner's Office over unlawful direct marketing connected with Banks's insurance businesses.

After the referendum

The organisation continued campaigning for a complete ("no-deal" style) Brexit and against a second referendum. In 2020 it drew further attention when it was reported that Leave.EU had applied to relocate its registration to Ireland to retain its .eu domain after Brexit; the domain was later suspended by the registry.
