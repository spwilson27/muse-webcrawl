Exor

Exor N.V. is a Dutch-incorporated holding company controlled by the Italian Agnelli family through Giovanni Agnelli B.V. Founded in 1927 by Giovanni Agnelli as Istituto Finanziario Industriale (IFI), it is one of Europe's largest diversified holding companies.

Exor's major investments have included stakes in Ferrari, Stellantis, CNH Industrial, Juventus football club, the Italian media group Gedi, and The Economist Group. The company is chaired by John Elkann, a descendant of the Agnelli family.

Exor redomiciled from Turin, Italy to Amsterdam, Netherlands in 2016, and its shares are listed on Euronext Amsterdam.
