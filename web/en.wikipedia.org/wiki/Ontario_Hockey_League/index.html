Ontario Hockey League

OHL action — the league is a major source of NHL talent.

The Ontario Hockey League (OHL) is one of the three major junior ice hockey leagues that constitute the Canadian Hockey League. It comprises 20 teams based in Ontario, Michigan and Pennsylvania, and is sanctioned for players aged 16 to 21.

History

The league traces its origins to the Ontario Hockey Association junior league and took its present form in 1980. OHL champions compete for the J. Ross Robertson Cup and advance to the Memorial Cup tournament against the champions of the WHL and QMJHL.

Development role

The OHL is one of the world's premier development leagues for the National Hockey League; its alumni include Wayne Gretzky, Bobby Orr and John Tavares.
