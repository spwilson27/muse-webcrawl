List of largest cities

The ranking of the world's largest cities depends on the definition used: the city proper (administrative boundary), the urban area (continuously built-up land), or the metropolitan area (including commuting zones). By metropolitan population, Tokyo is the largest urban agglomeration in the world, with roughly 37–38 million people.

Largest cities proper

By administrative area, Chinese direct-controlled municipalities dominate: Chongqing's municipality covers ~82,400 km² (the size of Austria) although its urban core population is far smaller than its ~32 million registered residents. Among conventionally bounded cities, Shanghai (~24.9 million), Beijing (~21.9 million), Istanbul, Karachi, Shenzhen, Guangzhou and Lagos rank near the top.
