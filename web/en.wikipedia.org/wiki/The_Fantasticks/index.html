The Fantasticks

The Fantasticks is a 1960 musical with music by Harvey Schmidt and book and lyrics by Tom Jones. It tells an allegorical story of two neighbouring fathers who trick their children, Luisa and Matt, into falling in love by pretending to feud. Its original off-Broadway production ran for 42 years, making it the world's longest-running musical.

Productions

The original production opened at the Sullivan Street Playhouse in Greenwich Village on 3 May 1960 and ran for 17,162 performances before closing on 13 January 2002. Jerry Orbach originated the role of El Gallo, the narrator and bandit. The score's best-known song is "Try to Remember".

Synopsis

The fathers build a wall between their houses to make their children desire each other; after a staged abduction makes Matt a hero, the couple discover the deception and part, experiencing the disappointments of the wider world before reuniting in a wiser love.

Revivals

The musical was revived off-Broadway in 2006, running a further eleven years, and has been produced thousands of times internationally. A film version was released in 1995.
