Temple of Set

The Temple of Set is a magico-religious organisation founded in 1975 by Michael A. Aquino and several former members of the Church of Satan, following a schism with Anton LaVey. It is dedicated to the worship and emulation of Set, the ancient Egyptian god of darkness and chaos, whom adherents regard as the true creator of humanity's separate, self-conscious intellect — the "Black Flame."

Beliefs and practices

The Temple's philosophy, called Setianism, centres on Xeper (Egyptian for "I have come into being"), the self-directed process of self-deification and personal evolution. Members progress through six degrees, and pursue "black magic," defined as willed action intended to bring about changes aligned with the magician's will. The organisation recognises specialised orders devoted to particular interests.

History

Aquino claimed a mandate conveyed in The Book of Coming Forth by Night, said to have been dictated by Set in 1975. The Temple incorporated as a non-profit church in California and received federal religious recognition in the United States.
