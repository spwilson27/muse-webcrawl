Wayzata (pronounced wy-ZET-ə) is a city in Hennepin County, Minnesota, United States, situated on the northeastern shore of Lake Minnetonka about 12 miles west of Minneapolis. With a population of roughly 4,400, it is one of the smaller suburbs in the Twin Cities metropolitan area but is a major gateway community to Lake Minnetonka.

History

The name derives from a Dakota word meaning "north shore." Wayzata developed in the mid-nineteenth century as a farming and milling settlement and grew with the arrival of the Great Northern Railway, becoming a popular resort destination in the era of grand lake hotels. In the early twentieth century it became known for the Wayzata Country Club and as a commuter suburb.

Today

Downtown Wayzata along Lake Street retains a village character with shops, restaurants and a public beach and marina on Wayzata Bay. The city is served by Interstate 394 and is known for its highly regarded public school district, whose high school campus is located in nearby Plymouth.
