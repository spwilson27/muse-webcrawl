Christopher Lloyd

Christopher Allen Lloyd (born October 22, 1938) is an American actor known for eccentric, energetic roles: Emmett "Doc" Brown in the Back to the Future trilogy, Reverend Jim Ignatowski in Taxi (winning two Emmys), and Uncle Fester in The Addams Family films.

Christopher Lloyd
Born: October 22, 1938, Stamford, Connecticut
Occupation: Actor
Years active: 1961–present

Career

Lloyd began on stage, debuted on film in One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest (1975), and gained fame on Taxi (1978–1983). Other roles include Star Trek III, Who Framed Roger Rabbit (Judge Doom), Clue, and numerous voice roles. He won a third Emmy for a guest role on Road to Avonlea.
