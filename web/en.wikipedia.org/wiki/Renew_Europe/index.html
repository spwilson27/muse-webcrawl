Renew Europe (abbreviated Renew) is a liberal, pro-European political group in the European Parliament founded for the ninth European Parliament term. It is the successor to the Alliance of Liberals and Democrats for Europe (ALDE) group, which existed from 2004 to 2019. The group was formed after the 2019 European Parliament election, when the French delegation led by Emmanuel Macron's Renaissance party joined with the former ALDE members.

History

In May 2019, discussions between ALDE and Macron's La République En Marche led to the creation of a new group under the name Renew Europe. Stéphane Séjourné became an early leading figure, Dacian Cioloș was elected the group's first president in June 2019, and Stéphane Séjourné took over in 2021. Valérie Hayer became president in January 2024.

Composition

The group brings together MEPs from the Alliance of Liberals and Democrats for Europe Party, the European Democratic Party, and unaffiliated national parties. Following the 2024 European Parliament election, it became the fifth-largest group in the Parliament.
