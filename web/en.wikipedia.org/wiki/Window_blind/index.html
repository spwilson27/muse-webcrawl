Window blind

A window blind is a type of window covering, typically consisting of long horizontal or vertical slats of various types of hard material, including wood, plastic or metal, held together by cords that run through the slats. Blinds control light, view and privacy and may be adjusted by rotating or drawing the slats.

Types

Common types include Venetian blinds (horizontal slats), vertical blinds, roller blinds, Roman blinds, pleated blinds and honeycomb (cellular) shades. Persian blinds of slatted wood were described in the 18th century; Venetian blinds were popularised in the 19th century.

Safety

Looped blind cords have been identified as a strangulation hazard for young children, leading regulators in several countries to require cordless designs or tensioning devices.
