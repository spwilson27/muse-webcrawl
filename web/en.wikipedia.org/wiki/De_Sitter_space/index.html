De Sitter space is the Lorentzian analogue of a sphere: a maximally symmetric spacetime with positive constant curvature, named after Dutch astronomer Willem de Sitter (1917), in Einstein's general relativity.

Definition

De Sitter space in n dimensions is a one-sheeted hyperboloid embedded in (n+1)-dimensional Minkowski space, with isometry group O(1,n).

Cosmology

A universe dominated by a positive cosmological constant approaches de Sitter space asymptotically. Cosmic inflation is approximately de Sitter, and the far future dominated by dark energy will be too.

Horizons

An observer is surrounded by a cosmological event horizon with Gibbons-Hawking temperature and entropy, important for holography and the proposed dS/CFT correspondence, contrasting anti-de Sitter space.
