Lake Otjikoto

Lake Otjikoto is a sinkhole lake near Tsumeb in the Oshikoto Region of Namibia. It is one of only two permanent natural lakes in Namibia and was formed when the roof of an underground cavern in dolomite limestone collapsed.

History

During the First World War, retreating German Schutztruppe forces dumped large quantities of weapons and equipment into the lake in 1915 to keep them from falling into South African hands. Many artefacts, including artillery pieces, were later recovered and are displayed in the Tsumeb Museum; others remain in the lake's depths.

Geology

The lake's visible surface is small — about 51 by 84 metres — but it widens below the surface and reaches depths exceeding 58 metres.
