Hackney carriage

A hackney carriage (or hackney) is a carriage or car for hire. In the United Kingdom the term most commonly denotes a licensed taxi, especially the London black cab, which may be hailed on the street or hired at a rank — unlike private-hire vehicles, which must be pre-booked.

London taxis

London hackney carriages are licensed by Transport for London. Drivers must pass "the Knowledge", a demanding test of London streets introduced in the 19th century. The distinctive vehicle design, with a tight turning circle and high roof, became an icon of the city; modern models include purpose-built electric cabs.
