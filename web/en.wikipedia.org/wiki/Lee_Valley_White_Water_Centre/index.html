Lee Valley White Water Centre

Opened9 December 2010

The Lee Valley White Water Centre is a white-water slalom centre in Waltham Cross, Hertfordshire, England, built to host the canoe slalom events of the London 2012 Olympic Games. It lies within the Lee Valley Park near the River Lea, about 30 km north of central London, and was the only entirely new venue purpose-built for the London Olympics.

Courses

The centre has two artificial courses fed by pumped water. The 300-metre Olympic course drops 5.5 metres and provides fast, technical white water for slalom competition and elite training. A shorter 160-metre legacy course with a 1.6-metre drop serves training, coaching, and public rafting and kayaking. Because the water is pumped around a closed system, the courses can run regardless of river conditions, and the flow can be adjusted for different abilities.

The 2012 Olympics and legacy

The venue opened to the public in April 2011, ahead of the Games, and hosted the Olympic canoe slalom in July–August 2012, where events were won by paddlers including Émilie Fer, Daniele Molmenti, and the British pair Etienne Stott and Tim Baillie in the men's C2. After the Games it became the home of the British Canoeing slalom programme and hosted the 2015 ICF Canoe Slalom World Championships, and it remains a major public white-water rafting and kayaking facility.
