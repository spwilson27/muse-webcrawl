Poprad

Poprad (Hungarian: Poprád) is a city in northern Slovakia at the foot of the High Tatra Mountains, in the Prešov Region. With a population of around 50,000, it is the tenth largest city in Slovakia and a gateway to the High Tatras, the country's most popular mountain resort area.

Geography

Poprad lies in the Poprad Basin (Podtatranská kotlina) on the Poprad River, at an elevation of about 672 metres. The city is a transport hub, with the Poprad–Tatry railway station and the nearby Poprad–Tatry Airport, one of the highest airports in Europe.

History

First mentioned in 1256, the area was settled by German colonists as part of the Spiš (Zips) towns of the Kingdom of Hungary. The modern city was formed in 1947 by merging five former towns: Poprad, Spišská Sobota, Veľká, Stráže pod Tatrami, and Matejovce. Spišská Sobota retains a well-preserved historic centre with a Gothic church.

Economy and sport

Tourism, light industry, and railway manufacturing are important to the local economy. Poprad has twice bid for the Winter Olympic Games, and its ice hockey club, HK Poprad, plays in the Slovak Extraliga.
