Women's National Basketball Association

The Women's National Basketball Association (WNBA) is a women's professional basketball league based in the United States. Founded on April 22, 1996, as the women's counterpart to the National Basketball Association (NBA), the league played its first season in 1997. The WNBA regular season runs from May to September, with the playoffs and Finals concluding in October.

History

The league began with eight teams, many owned by NBA franchises and sharing their arenas. The Houston Comets won the first four championships (1997–2000), led by Cynthia Cooper, Sheryl Swoopes and Tina Thompson. Over time several teams folded or relocated, and ownership diversified to independent groups. The league has featured stars such as Lisa Leslie — who scored the first dunk in WNBA history in 2002 — Diana Taurasi, Sue Bird, Candace Parker, Breanna Stewart, A'ja Wilson and Caitlin Clark, whose arrival in 2024 coincided with sharp growth in attendance and television ratings.

Format

The league currently consists of 12 teams (with expansion teams announced for the mid-2020s), split into Eastern and Western Conferences. Teams play a 40-game regular season; the eight best teams qualify for the playoffs, culminating in the WNBA Finals. The league also holds an in-season tournament, the Commissioner's Cup, and an annual All-Star Game.
