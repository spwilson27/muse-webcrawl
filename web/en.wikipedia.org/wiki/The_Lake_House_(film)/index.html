The Lake House is a 2006 American romantic fantasy drama film directed by Alejandro Agresti and written by David Auburn. A remake of the South Korean film Il Mare (2000), it stars Keanu Reeves and Sandra Bullock, who had previously appeared together in Speed (1994).

Plot

In 2006, Dr. Kate Forster leaves a glass lake house on Lake Michigan in Illinois and moves to Chicago, leaving a note for the next tenant in the house's mailbox. In 2004, architect Alex Wyler moves into the same house and finds the letter—although from his perspective the house has been long vacant. Through the mailbox, which appears to transport letters across exactly two years, the two begin a correspondence and fall in love, despite living in different times. They attempt to meet within each other's timelines, and Kate realises that a man killed in a traffic accident she witnessed in 2006 was Alex. She races to warn him, asking him to wait two years before coming to the lake house. In the rewritten timeline, Alex survives, and the two finally meet at the house.

Production

The glass lake house central to the film was built for the production on Maple Lake in the Cook County Forest Preserves near Willow Springs, Illinois, and was dismantled after filming. The structure, designed by architects Nathan Crowley and others, rested on steel columns in the lake and became one of the production's most remarked-upon features.

Reception

The film opened at number four at the North American box office and went on to gross $114.8 million worldwide. Critical reviews were mixed: reviewers praised the chemistry between Reeves and Bullock and the film's elegiac tone, while some found the time-travel mechanics implausible or confusing. The film's score by Rachel Portman, and its use of the song "This Never Happened Before" by Paul McCartney, were noted positively.
