O Antiphons

The O Antiphons (also known as the Great Advent Antiphons or Great Os) are seven antiphons sung or recited at Vespers (Evening Prayer) in Western Christian traditions during the last seven days of Advent, from 17 to 23 December. Each addresses Jesus by a title drawn from the prophecies of Isaiah and begins with the invocation "O".

The seven antiphons

  • 17 December: O Sapientia — "O Wisdom"
  • 18 December: O Adonai — "O Lord"
  • 19 December: O Radix Jesse — "O Root of Jesse"
  • 20 December: O Clavis David — "O Key of David"
  • 21 December: O Oriens — "O Dayspring" (or "O Rising Sun")
  • 22 December: O Rex Gentium — "O King of the Nations"
  • 23 December: O Emmanuel — "O Emmanuel" (God with us)

Read backwards, the first letters of the Latin titles after the initial "O" (Sapientia, Adonai, Radix, Clavis, Oriens, Rex, Emmanuel) form the acrostic ERO CRAS, meaning "Tomorrow, I will be [there]". The antiphons also provide the basis for the verses of the Advent hymn "O Come, O Come, Emmanuel".
