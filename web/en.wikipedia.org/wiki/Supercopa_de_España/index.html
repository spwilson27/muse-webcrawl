Supercopa de España

The Supercopa de España (Spanish Super Cup) is an annual football competition in Spain, organised by the Royal Spanish Football Federation (RFEF). Since 2020 it has been contested by four teams: the winners and runners-up of La Liga and the Copa del Rey.

History

The competition was founded in 1982 as a two-legged tie between the league champions and the cup winners, modelled on the earlier Copa Eva Duarte played in the 1940s and 1950s. For most of its history it opened the Spanish season in August, home and away. Barcelona became the competition's most successful club, with Real Madrid close behind; the frequent meeting of the two giants in the final turned the Supercopa into a regular Clásico fixture.

Four-team format and Saudi Arabia

In 2019 the RFEF restructured the tournament into a four-team "final four" with single-match semi-finals and final, moved it to January, and struck a multi-year deal to stage it in Saudi Arabia — a decision that drew criticism over the host country's human-rights record and the finances of the arrangement. The first edition in the new format, held in Jeddah in January 2020, was won by Real Madrid, who defeated Atlético Madrid on penalties. Subsequent editions, held in Riyadh, have been won by Athletic Bilbao, Real Madrid and Barcelona, with Barcelona's 2023 and 2025 victories underlining their strong overall record in the competition.
