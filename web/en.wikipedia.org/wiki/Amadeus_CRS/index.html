Amadeus CRS
Developer: Amadeus IT Group
Type: Global distribution system / computer reservation system

The Amadeus Computer Reservation System (Amadeus CRS) is a computer reservation system and global distribution system (GDS) owned by the Amadeus IT Group, headquartered in Madrid, Spain. It is used by airlines, travel agencies, hotels, rail operators, and other travel providers to distribute inventory, check availability, and make bookings.

History

Amadeus was created in 1987 by Air France, Iberia, Lufthansa, and SAS as a European alternative to the American Sabre system. It grew into one of the world's largest GDS platforms, alongside Sabre and Travelport. The system processes hundreds of millions of bookings annually and provides the passenger service systems (notably the Altéa suite) used by many airlines for reservations, inventory, and departure control.

Functionality

The CRS stores schedules, fares, and seat availability, and allows travel sellers to construct passenger name records (PNRs), issue tickets, and manage itinerary changes. Modern versions expose these functions through web services and graphical agency desktop products such as Amadeus Selling Platform.
