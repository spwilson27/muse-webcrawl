Tata Group

Founded: 1868, Mumbai, India

Founder: Jamsetji Tata

Headquarters: Bombay House, Mumbai

Parent: Tata Sons

Industry: Conglomerate (steel, automotive, IT, consumer, hospitality, aviation, and more)

The Tata Group is an Indian multinational conglomerate headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra. Founded in 1868 by Jamsetji Tata, it is India's largest conglomerate, comprising dozens of independently run companies — including several listed majors — operating in over 100 countries. The group's holding company is Tata Sons, the majority of whose equity is held by the philanthropic Tata Trusts.

History

Jamsetji Tata began with a trading company in 1868 and went on to found textile mills, the Taj Mahal Palace Hotel (1903), and to plan the steel, hydroelectric, and research institutions realized by his successors: Tata Steel (1907, at Jamshedpur), the Indian Institute of Science (1909), and Tata Power. Under J. R. D. Tata, chairman from 1938 to 1991, the group expanded into chemicals, automobiles (Tata Motors), tea, and information technology (TCS, founded 1968), and founded Air India in 1932.

Under Ratan Tata (chairman 1991–2012), the group globalized aggressively, acquiring Tetley Tea (2000), the Anglo-Dutch steelmaker Corus (2007), and the British car marques Jaguar and Land Rover (2008), and launching the Tata Nano people's car. Natarajan Chandrasekaran became chairman of Tata Sons in 2017.

Major companies

  • Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) — one of the world's largest IT services firms.
  • Tata Steel — among the top global steel producers.
  • Tata Motors — cars, trucks, and owner of Jaguar Land Rover.
  • Tata Consumer Products — Tata Tea, Tetley, Tata Salt.
  • Indian Hotels Company — the Taj hotel chain.
  • Titan, Trent, Tata Power, Tata Chemicals, Tata Communications, Tata Elxsi, and the Air India group (reacquired by the group in 2022) are among other major affiliates.

Philanthropy

Roughly two-thirds of Tata Sons' shares are held by charitable trusts endowed by members of the Tata family, which fund education, health, and research institutions across India — a structure frequently cited as distinctive of the group's ethos.
