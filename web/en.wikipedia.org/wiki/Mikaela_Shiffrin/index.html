Mikaela Shiffrin

Mikaela Pauline Shiffrin (born March 13, 1995) is an American alpine ski racer and the most successful alpine skier in World Cup history by race wins. A specialist in the technical events of slalom and giant slalom, she has won multiple overall World Cup titles, Olympic gold medals, and World Championship titles, and is widely regarded as one of the greatest ski racers of all time.

Career

Shiffrin grew up in Colorado and New Hampshire and attended Burke Mountain Academy in Vermont. She made her World Cup debut at 15 in March 2011, won her first World Cup race in December 2012, and at 17 became the youngest slalom world champion in U.S. history in 2013. At the 2014 Sochi Olympics she won slalom gold, becoming the youngest Olympic slalom champion ever. She added giant slalom gold and combined silver at Pyeongchang 2018.

She won the overall World Cup title five times (2017, 2018, 2019, 2022, 2023) and a record number of slalom crystal globes. In March 2023 she surpassed Ingemar Stenmark's long-standing record of 86 World Cup victories, and in February 2025 she reached the unprecedented milestone of 100 World Cup wins. Her career was marked by a hiatus in 2020 following the sudden death of her father, Jeff Shiffrin, whose memory she has credited as a continuing motivation.
