Floods
Flooding is among the deadliest and costliest categories of natural disaster worldwide.

A flood is an overflow of water that submerges land that is usually dry. Floods can result from heavy rainfall, storm surge, snowmelt, dam failure or tsunamis. This article lists notable floods in recorded history, including the deadliest flood events, most of which occurred in China along the Yellow River (Huang He).

Flood types

Riverine (fluvial) floods, flash floods, coastal floods and storm surges, glacial lake outburst floods and dam failures are distinguished by hydrologists. Modern flood risk management combines levees, floodplains, forecasting and land-use planning.
