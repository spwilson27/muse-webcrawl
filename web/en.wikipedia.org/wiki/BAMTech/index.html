BAMTech

BAMTech
Formerly: MLB Advanced Media streaming division
Founded: 2015 (spun off)
Successor: Disney Streaming
Parent: The Walt Disney Company
Industry: Streaming technology

BAMTech was an American technology company specialising in video streaming, spun off from MLB Advanced Media, the digital arm of Major League Baseball. Its platform powered services including MLB.tv, HBO Now, WWE Network and later Disney streaming products.

History

MLB Advanced Media built one of the earliest large-scale sports streaming operations in the 2000s. In 2015 the streaming technology business was separated as BAMTech. The Walt Disney Company acquired a minority stake in 2016, increased it to a controlling 75% in 2017 for about $1.58 billion, and later took full control.

Disney Streaming

BAMTech technology became the foundation for ESPN+ (2018) and Disney+ (2019). The unit was reorganised as Disney Streaming, providing the playback, subscription and personalisation infrastructure for Disney's direct-to-consumer services worldwide.
