Landguard Fort

Landguard Fort is a fort at the mouth of the River Orwell near Felixstowe, Suffolk, England, built to guard the approach to Harwich harbour. The site has been fortified since the reign of Henry VIII, and the last opposed seaborne invasion of England, the Dutch raid of 1667, was repelled at an earlier fort on the site.

History

The first fortifications were erected in 1540. A new fort was built in 1625 and fought off a Dutch landing force under Admiral Michiel de Ruyter in 1667. The present pentagonal bastioned fort dates largely from the 18th century, with a new battery added in 1872 and further modifications during both World Wars. The fort was disarmed in 1956 and is now managed as a museum and historic attraction.
