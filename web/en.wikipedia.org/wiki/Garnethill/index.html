Garnethill

Garnethill is a residential district in the centre of Glasgow, Scotland, situated on a steep hill immediately west of the city centre and north of Sauchiehall Street. Developed mainly in the 19th century, it is known for its Victorian tenements and terraces and for a cluster of cultural and religious institutions, including Glasgow School of Art and Scotland's first purpose-built synagogue.

Landmarks

The district contains the Glasgow School of Art's Mackintosh Building (largely destroyed by fires in 2014 and 2018), the Glasgow Film Theatre, the Tenement House museum — a preserved early-20th-century tenement flat — and Garnethill Synagogue, opened in 1879 and the oldest in Scotland. The Glasgow Dental Hospital and parts of Glasgow Caledonian University also lie nearby. The hill offers notable views over the city centre and the Clyde valley.

Community

Garnethill has a long history as one of Glasgow's most multicultural neighbourhoods, home over time to Jewish, Irish, Italian, South Asian and Chinese communities, with mosques, temples and community centres alongside churches. Its residential streets mix long-term residents with a large student population from the nearby art school and universities.
