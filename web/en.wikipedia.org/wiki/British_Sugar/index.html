British Sugar plc is a British manufacturer of sugar and related products and a subsidiary of Associated British Foods (ABF). It is the sole processor of the United Kingdom's sugar beet crop: it buys the entire beet harvest of some 3,000 growers in eastern and central England — around 7.5 million tonnes of beet a year — and converts it into more than one million tonnes of sugar, sold to food and drink manufacturers and to consumers under the Silver Spoon brand.

History

The company was formed in 1936 as the British Sugar Corporation, a state-backed body that amalgamated the country's subsidised beet sugar factories, many of which had been built in the 1920s. It was privatised in 1982, acquired by Berisford International in 1986, and sold to Associated British Foods in 1991 following Berisford's financial difficulties. Consolidation reduced the number of factories over time; closures at sites including York and Allscott (2006–07) left four factories in operation.

Operations

British Sugar's four factories — at Bury St Edmunds (Suffolk), Cantley (Norfolk), Newark (Nottinghamshire) and Wissington (Norfolk) — slice beet during the autumn-to-spring "campaign," extracting and crystallising sugar for bulk and retail customers. Wissington is among the largest sugar factories in the world and has become a showcase for co-products: it generates renewable electricity, produces bioethanol from sugar fermentation residues, recovers soil and stones from beet washing (sold as topsoil and aggregate), supplies carbon dioxide and waste heat to a large tomato-growing glasshouse, and manufactures animal feed products such as molassed beet pulp. The company also produces the pharmaceutical and food ingredient betaine and other speciality products at some sites.

Market position

British Sugar supplies roughly half of the UK sugar market, competing with cane sugar refined by Tate & Lyle Sugars and with imports. The abolition of EU sugar production quotas in 2017 reshaped the market, allowing British Sugar to expand beet contracting and export surplus production.
