Mr Selfridge

Genre: Period drama
Created by: Andrew Davies
Starring: Jeremy Piven
Network: ITV (UK), PBS Masterpiece (US)
Original run: 2013–2016 (4 series, 40 episodes)

Mr Selfridge is a British period drama television series about Harry Gordon Selfridge, the American-born founder of the London department store Selfridges. Created by Andrew Davies and produced by ITV Studios, the series stars Jeremy Piven as Selfridge and ran for four series of ten episodes each, broadcast on ITV in the United Kingdom from 2013 to 2016 and on PBS's Masterpiece in the United States. It is loosely based on Lindy Woodhead's biography Shopping, Seduction & Mr. Selfridge.

Premise

The series begins in 1908–09 with Selfridge's arrival in London and his determination to build a grand department store on the unfashionable western end of Oxford Street, which opened in March 1909. Subsequent series follow the store and its staff through the Edwardian era, the First World War, the Roaring Twenties, and into the Great Depression, tracking Selfridge's flamboyant retail innovations — dramatic window displays, in-store restaurants, cosmetics at the front of the store, and publicity stunts such as displaying Louis Blériot's cross-Channel aeroplane — alongside his gambling, affairs, and eventual financial decline. The ensemble cast includes Frances O'Connor as his wife Rose, Aisling Loftus as shopgirl-turned-businesswoman Agnes Towler, and Katherine Kelly as the scheming Lady Mae Loxley.

Production and reception

The store interior was recreated in a purpose-built set in a former carpet factory in Neasden, London, supplemented by filming at locations including the real Selfridges building. The series was a ratings success for ITV and sold widely internationally; critics generally praised its lavish production design and ensemble performances while noting its melodramatic tone.
