Zurich Airport (IATA: ZRH) is the largest international airport in Switzerland and the principal hub of Swiss International Air Lines. Located in Kloten, about 13 km north of central Zurich, it serves as the country's main gateway for long-haul air travel.

Facilities

The airport has three runways and terminals centred on Airside Center gates A, B/D and the satellite Dock E, reached by the Skymetro automated people mover. The landside Airport Shopping area and the adjacent mixed-use development The Circle opened in 2020. A busy railway station beneath the airport connects to the Swiss Federal Railways network.

Operations

Zurich is known for strict night-flight restrictions and noise-abatement procedures reflecting dense surroundings and disputes over approach paths over southern Germany. It consistently ranks among Europe's best-regarded airports for passenger experience.
