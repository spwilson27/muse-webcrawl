List of holidays by country

This is a summary of public holidays observed in selected countries, listing each country's best-known national and widely observed holidays. Most countries observe New Year's Day (1 January) and their own national or independence day; many also observe religious holidays of their majority faiths.

Counting methodologies differ: some countries "move" holidays falling on weekends to the nearest weekday, while others grant substitute days. Cambodia, India (counting state-level festivals), Sri Lanka, and Nepal are often cited among the countries with the most public holidays, while England and Wales, with eight, have among the fewest in Europe.
