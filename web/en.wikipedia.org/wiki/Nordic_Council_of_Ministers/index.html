Nordic Council of Ministers

The Nordic Council of Ministers is the official intergovernmental body for cooperation among the Nordic countries — Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway and Sweden — together with the autonomous territories of the Faroe Islands, Greenland and Åland. Founded in 1971, it complements the Nordic Council (the inter-parliamentary body founded in 1952) and is headquartered in Copenhagen.

Work

Although named in the singular, the Council of Ministers consists of numerous ministerial councils for policy areas such as culture, environment, education, labour and social affairs. National governments implement its decisions; it has no supranational authority. It funds Nordic institutions and programmes, coordinates policy initiatives, and administers bodies such as the Nordic Culture Fund and NordForsk. The presidency rotates annually among the five countries.
