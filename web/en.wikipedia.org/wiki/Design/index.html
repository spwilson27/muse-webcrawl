Design

A design is the concept of or proposal for an object, process, or system. The word design refers to something that is or has been intentionally created by a thinking agent, and is sometimes used to refer to the inherent nature of something – its design. As a verb, to design refers to the process of developing a design. In some cases, direct construction without explicit prior plan may also be considered design activity.

Overview

Designing requires considering the aesthetic, functional, economic, and sociopolitical dimensions of both the object and the design process. It involves research, thought, modelling, interactive adjustment, and re-design. Major examples of designs include architectural and engineering drawings, circuit diagrams, sewing patterns, and less tangible artefacts such as business process designs.

The person designing is called a designer, a term used for people who work professionally in design areas such as graphic design, user interface design, industrial design, fashion design and interior design.

Philosophies and approaches

Design approaches include user-centred design, participatory (co-)design, sustainable design and universal design. Herbert A. Simon characterised design broadly as devising courses of action aimed at changing existing situations into preferred ones — "the science of the artificial".
