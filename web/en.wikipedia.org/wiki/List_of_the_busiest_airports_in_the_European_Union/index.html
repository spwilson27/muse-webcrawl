List of the busiest airports in the European Union

This is a list of the busiest airports in the European Union by passenger traffic. Since the United Kingdom left the EU, London Heathrow no longer appears in EU rankings; the busiest EU airport is typically Paris Charles de Gaulle or Amsterdam Schiphol, followed by Frankfurt and Madrid–Barajas.
