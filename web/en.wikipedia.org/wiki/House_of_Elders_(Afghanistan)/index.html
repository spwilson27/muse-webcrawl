The House of Elders or Meshrano Jirga (Pashto: مشرانو جرګه‎) was the upper house of the bicameral National Assembly of Afghanistan, alongside the lower House of the People (Wolesi Jirga). It was created under the 2004 Constitution of Afghanistan, and ceased functioning as an elected legislature following the Taliban takeover in August 2021.

Composition

The Meshrano Jirga had 102 members. One-third (34) were elected by district councils for terms of three years, one-third (34) were elected by provincial councils for terms of four years, and one-third (34) were nominated by the President of Afghanistan for terms of five years. Half of the presidential nominees had to be women, and two seats were reserved for nominees from the disabled and impaired community and two for the nomadic Kuchi population.

Role

The House of Elders primarily had an advisory and reviewing role in legislation, although it could initiate bills and its consent was required for certain matters. Its members were intended to bring provincial and local interests into the national legislative process, complementing the directly elected House of the People.
