Pohang

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Pohang (Korean: 포항) is a city in North Gyeongsang Province, South Korea, on the east coast of the Korean Peninsula at the mouth of the Hyeongsan River on Yeongil Bay. With a population of roughly 500,000, it is one of South Korea's principal industrial centres, dominated by the steel industry, and a major port and ferry terminal with services to Ulleungdo island.

History

Pohang was a fishing village until the 1960s, when it was chosen for the construction of an integrated steel mill. POSCO (Pohang Iron and Steel Company), founded in 1968, completed its first phase in 1973 and grew into one of the world's largest steel producers, transforming the city. During the Korean War the area saw the Pohang landing (July 1950) and heavy fighting in the Battle of P'ohang-dong in August 1950 during the defence of the Pusan Perimeter. In November 2017 a magnitude 5.4 earthquake — the second strongest in South Korea's modern record — damaged buildings across the city.

Economy and institutions

Besides POSCO and its supplier industries, Pohang hosts Hyundai Steel facilities, the Pohang Accelerator Laboratory, and POSTECH (Pohang University of Science and Technology), a leading research university founded by POSCO in 1986. The city's K League football club, Pohang Steelers, is one of Asia's most successful teams.
