Fantastic Four

Publisher: Marvel Comics
First appearance: The Fantastic Four #1 (Nov. 1961)
Created by: Stan Lee, Jack Kirby
Members: Mister Fantastic, Invisible Woman, Human Torch, Thing

The Fantastic Four is a superhero team appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Stan Lee and artist Jack Kirby, the team debuted in The Fantastic Four #1 (cover-dated November 1961), the title that launched the Marvel Universe and ushered in a new level of realism and character-driven storytelling in superhero comics.

Premise

The four members acquired their powers after exposure to cosmic rays during an unauthorised test flight of an experimental spacecraft. Scientist Reed Richards became Mister Fantastic, able to stretch his body to great lengths; his fiancée (later wife) Susan Storm became the Invisible Woman, able to render herself invisible and project force fields; her brother Johnny Storm became the Human Torch, able to engulf himself in flame and fly; and pilot Ben Grimm was transformed into the Thing, a hulking creature with a rocky, orange hide and superhuman strength. Unlike other superhero teams of the era, the four quarrelled, held no secret identities, and functioned as a family as much as a unit — a template that became central to Marvel's style.

Publication history

The Lee–Kirby run lasted 102 issues and introduced many of Marvel's most enduring characters and concepts, including Doctor Doom, the Silver Surfer, Galactus, the Black Panther, the Inhumans, and the Skrulls. The Baxter Building in New York serves as the team's headquarters. Later creative teams, notably John Byrne in the 1980s and Jonathan Hickman in the 2000s, produced acclaimed runs. Reed and Sue's children, Franklin and Valeria Richards, play increasingly important roles in modern stories.

In other media

The team has appeared in several animated series and in feature films, including the 2005 film Fantastic Four and its 2007 sequel Fantastic Four: Rise of the Silver Surfer, the 2015 reboot Fantastic Four, and the Marvel Studios film The Fantastic Four: First Steps (2025), which introduced the team to the Marvel Cinematic Universe.
