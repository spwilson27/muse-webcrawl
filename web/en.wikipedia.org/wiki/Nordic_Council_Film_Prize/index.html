Nordic Council Film Prize

The Nordic Council Film Prize is an annual film prize administered by Nordisk Film & TV Fond (the Nordic Film and TV Fund) on behalf of the Nordic Council. It was first awarded in 2002 on the occasion of the Nordic Council's 50th anniversary, and has been awarded annually since 2005. One film from each of the five Nordic countries — Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway and Sweden — is nominated, and the winner receives a prize (currently around DKK 300,000, shared between the director, screenwriter and producer), intended to promote the production of high-quality Nordic films.

Selection

National juries in each country select one nominee; a Nordic jury then chooses the winner, announced during the Nordic Council's autumn session. Eligible films must have had their cinema premiere in the preceding year and be deeply rooted in Nordic culture.
