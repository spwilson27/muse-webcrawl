Science Olympiad is an American team competition in which students compete in events pertaining to various fields of science, including earth science, biology, chemistry, physics, and engineering. Founded in 1984 by Gerard Putz, it has grown to involve thousands of schools and roughly a quarter-million students annually.

Structure

Teams of 15 students compete in Division A (elementary), Division B (middle school), and Division C (high school). A tournament typically features 23 events per division, mixing knowledge tests, laboratory exercises, and pre-built engineering devices (such as towers, vehicles, and flight events). Teams advance from invitational and regional tournaments to state tournaments and finally the National Tournament, held each May at a host university.

Events

Signature events include Anatomy and Physiology, Astronomy, Codebusters, Disease Detectives, Experimental Design, Forensics, Tower Building, and Wright Stuff. Event rules are revised annually, and alumni frequently return as event supervisors and coaches.
