Penn & Teller: Fool Us

Penn & Teller: Fool Us is a magic competition television series in which magicians perform tricks before the duo Penn & Teller. If Penn & Teller cannot figure out how a trick was done, the performer wins a "Fool Us" trophy and the opportunity to open for Penn & Teller at their Las Vegas show.

Format

Each episode features several acts. After each performance, Penn Jillette discusses the method in coded language, using jargon that knowledgeable viewers may decode without exposing the secret to lay audiences. Teller remains characteristically silent on stage. Successful foolers have included Shin Lim, Piff the Magic Dragon, and Shawn Farquhar, the first to fool the duo twice.

History

The show began in the United Kingdom on ITV in 2011, hosted by Jonathan Ross. It was revived in the United States by The CW in 2014, hosted first by Ross and later by Alyson Hannigan, and filmed at the Rio All-Suite Hotel and Casino in Las Vegas, where Penn & Teller perform.
