The Mitre 10 Cup was the sponsor name, from 2016 to 2020, of New Zealand's provincial rugby union competition, officially the National Provincial Championship (NPC). The competition features fourteen provincial unions and has historically been structured into Premiership and Championship divisions with promotion and relegation between them.

History

The National Provincial Championship was founded in 1976. Following a restructuring in 2006, the competition was split into two divisions. Sponsorship naming rights passed through several holders: the Air New Zealand Cup (2006–2009), the ITM Cup (2010–2015), and the Mitre 10 Cup from 2016. In 2021 the competition reverted to the NPC name with Bunnings Warehouse as sponsor.

Format

Each season the fourteen unions play a round-robin schedule, including crossover matches between divisions, followed by semifinals and finals in each division. The Championship winner is promoted to the Premiership and the lowest Premiership side relegated. Matches for the Ranfurly Shield, New Zealand rugby's most famous challenge trophy, are played during the season by the holding union.
