Kaiju

Kaiju (Japanese: 怪獣, "strange beast") is a Japanese genre of films and television featuring giant monsters. The term may also refer to the monsters themselves, the most famous of which is Godzilla. Kaiju films usually feature a giant creature attacking a major city, battling the military or other monsters, and are a form of tokusatsu (special-effects entertainment) traditionally using suit actors in miniature sets.

Origins

The 1954 film Godzilla, directed by Ishirō Honda for Toho, popularised the genre, using the monster as a metaphor for nuclear destruction. Earlier precedents include King Kong (1933) and The Beast from 20,000 Fathoms (1953).

Major franchises

Besides Godzilla and its many co-stars (Mothra, Rodan, King Ghidorah, Mechagodzilla), the genre includes the flying turtle Gamera and television heroes such as Ultraman. In the 21st century the genre went global with films such as Pacific Rim (2013) and Legendary Pictures' MonsterVerse series.
