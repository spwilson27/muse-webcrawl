Clutha District

Clutha District is a local government district in the Otago region of New Zealand's South Island. Its largest town is Balclutha, which serves as the district's administrative centre. The district takes its name from the Clutha River / Mata-Au, New Zealand's largest river by volume.

Geography

The district extends from the South Otago coast inland to the Blue Mountains and the Lammerlaw Range. Other towns include Milton, Kaitangata, Owaka, Lawrence and Tapanui. The Catlins, a rugged coastal area known for native forest, waterfalls and wildlife such as yellow-eyed penguins and sea lions, lies partly within the district.

Economy

Agriculture, particularly sheep and dairy farming, dominates the local economy, along with forestry and food processing.
