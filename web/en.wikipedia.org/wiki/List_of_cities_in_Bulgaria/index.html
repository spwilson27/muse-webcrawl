List of cities in Bulgaria

This is a list of cities and towns in Bulgaria. Bulgaria has 257 towns and cities (as settlements with town status) and more than 5,000 villages. The capital and largest city is Sofia, home to roughly 1.2 million people, followed by Plovdiv and the Black Sea ports of Varna and Burgas.

Other notable towns include the medieval capital Veliko Tarnovo, the old town of Nesebar on the Black Sea coast, the ski resort of Bansko, and Melnik, traditionally cited as Bulgaria's smallest town. Population figures are drawn from National Statistical Institute estimates and have generally declined since 1989 due to emigration and low birth rates.
