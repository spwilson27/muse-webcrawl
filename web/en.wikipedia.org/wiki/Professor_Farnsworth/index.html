Professor Hubert J. Farnsworth is a fictional character in the animated television series Futurama, voiced by Billy West. A senile, amoral and frequently contradictory mad scientist, he is the founder and owner of the Planet Express delivery company in the year 3000 and the employer of the series' main characters. His catchphrase, used to announce both wonderful and terrible developments alike, is "Good news, everyone!"

Character

Farnsworth is depicted as being well over 150 years old, with failing eyesight, hearing and memory. He is a many-times-over great-nephew (and, owing to time travel, also a distant descendant) of protagonist Philip J. Fry. Despite his frailty, he is a brilliant inventor whose creations — including the Planet Express ship's dark-matter engines, the Smell-O-Scope, and various doomsday devices — drive many episodes' plots. He teaches at Mars University and is a member of the Academy of Inventors.

Biography

Born in 2841 in New New York's nerdiest slum, Farnsworth spent decades in a mental institution after a breakdown, worked for MomCorp (and was romantically involved with its owner, Mom), and founded Planet Express to fund his research. His clone, Cubert Farnsworth, was grown as an heir and introduced in the second season.

Reception

Farnsworth is regarded as one of Futurama's most popular characters, with his "Good news, everyone!" line becoming a widely used internet meme. Billy West has cited the voice as a favorite among the many he performs for the show.
