Paradores

Paradores de Turismo de España is a Spanish state-owned chain of luxury hotels, founded in 1928. Paradores are frequently located in historic buildings such as castles, palaces, monasteries, and fortresses, or in places of outstanding natural beauty.

History

The first parador opened in 1928 in the Sierra de Gredos under King Alfonso XIII, with the aim of promoting tourism and preserving Spain's architectural heritage. The network grew through the 20th century and now numbers around 97 establishments across Spain and one in Portugal.

Character

Paradores are known for regional cuisine and for restoring monuments that might otherwise decay; well-known examples include the Parador de Granada within the Alhambra complex and the Hostal de los Reyes Católicos in Santiago de Compostela.
