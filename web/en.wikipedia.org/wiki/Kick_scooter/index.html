Kick scooter

A kick scooter is a human-powered stand-up vehicle with a handlebar, deck and wheels that is propelled by the rider pushing off the ground with one foot. Modern kick scooters are typically made of aluminium, fold for transport, and have two small hard wheels.

History

Early scooters in the early 20th century were homemade from wood and roller-skate wheels. The modern lightweight folding scooter was popularised in the late 1990s, notably by the Razor brand, sparking a worldwide craze among children and commuters. Larger-wheeled models and electric scooters (e-scooters) later expanded the category, with shared e-scooter schemes appearing in cities from 2017.

Use

Kick scooters are used for recreation, short-distance commuting and in freestyle scooter sport, involving tricks performed at skate parks.
