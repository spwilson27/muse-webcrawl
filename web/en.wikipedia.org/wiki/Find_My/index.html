Find My

Find My is an app and service provided by Apple Inc. that enables users to track the locations of their Apple devices—including iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, and AirPods—as well as accessories such as AirTag, and to share locations with other people. It was introduced in 2019, merging the earlier Find My iPhone and Find My Friends apps.

Features

Users can see a device's location on a map, play a sound on it, mark it as lost, display a message on its screen, or remotely erase it. The Find My network, launched in 2021, uses Bluetooth signals from nearby Apple devices to locate offline items such as AirTags, with end-to-end encryption designed so that neither Apple nor third parties can read the location reports.

Privacy and safety

The service has drawn both praise for recovering lost and stolen property and concern over misuse of AirTags for unwanted tracking, prompting Apple to add alerts for unknown trackers travelling with a person and to cooperate on cross-platform unwanted-tracking standards.
