Diatonic button accordion

The diatonic button accordion is a type of button accordion in which the melody-side keyboard is restricted (diatonically) to the notes of a single scale or a few related scales, and in which each button produces a different note on the push and the pull of the bellows (bisonoric). It is known by many regional names, including melodeon in Britain and simply "button box".

Design

Instruments have one to three rows of treble buttons, each row tuned to a different key, and a small set of bass and chord buttons for the left hand. The bisonoric action doubles the notes available from each reed pair but requires characteristic bellows technique.

Regional traditions

The diatonic accordion is central to many folk traditions: Cajun music in Louisiana, Tex-Mex and norteño music, Irish and English folk music, French musette, and the musics of Madagascar, Colombia and the Dominican Republic (merengue típico).
