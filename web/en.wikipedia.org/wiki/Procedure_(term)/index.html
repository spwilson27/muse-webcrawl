Procedure (term)

A procedure is a document or established way of doing something that specifies a series of actions or steps to be performed in a definite order to achieve a particular end. Procedures are used in business, government, medicine, law, science, and computing to ensure consistency, safety, and quality.

Uses of the term

In quality management, a procedure documents how a process is carried out, as formalised in standards such as ISO 9000. In law, procedure denotes the rules governing how cases proceed through courts (civil and criminal procedure). In medicine, a procedure is an intervention performed on a patient, such as a surgical operation. In computing, a procedure is a subroutine — see subroutine.

Related concepts

Procedures sit within a hierarchy of documentation that may include policies (what and why), processes (the overall sequence of activities), procedures (detailed steps), and work instructions. A standard operating procedure (SOP) is a formally established procedure followed routinely.
