List of most popular dog breeds

This article lists dog breeds by popularity, based on registration statistics maintained by national kennel clubs such as the American Kennel Club (AKC), The Kennel Club of the United Kingdom, and the Canadian Kennel Club. Registration figures measure purebred puppies registered in a given year and are the most widely used proxy for breed popularity, though they exclude unregistered dogs and most mixed-breed animals.


The Labrador Retriever held the AKC's top spot for a record 31 consecutive years.

United States

The Labrador Retriever was the most-registered breed with the American Kennel Club from 1991 to 2022, a streak of 31 years. In 2023 the French Bulldog took first place for the first time, reflecting a long rise in the popularity of small companion breeds.

United Kingdom

The Kennel Club's registration statistics have likewise been dominated by the Labrador Retriever, with the Cocker Spaniel, French Bulldog, English Springer Spaniel and Dachshund also consistently near the top. During the COVID-19 pandemic, registrations of companion breeds such as the Dachshund and Pomeranian rose sharply.

Other rankings

Surveys based on pet-insurance data, veterinary visits and household pet-ownership panels sometimes produce different orders, since popular crossbreeds such as the Cockapoo and Labradoodle do not appear in purebred registration tables but are very common pets.
