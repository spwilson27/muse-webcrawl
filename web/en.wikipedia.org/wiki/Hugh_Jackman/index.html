Hugh Jackman

Hugh Michael Jackman (born 12 October 1968) is an Australian actor and singer. He is best known for playing Wolverine in the X-Men film series (2000–2017) and Deadpool & Wolverine (2024), a role that earned him a Guinness World Record for the longest career as a live-action Marvel character.

Early life

Jackman was born in Sydney, New South Wales, to English immigrant parents. He studied communications at the University of Technology Sydney and later acting at the Western Australian Academy of Performing Arts, graduating in 1994. He gained attention in Australia in the television drama Correlli and the stage musical Oklahoma! in London's West End.

Career

Cast as Wolverine in X-Men (2000), Jackman became an international star. His other films include Van Helsing, The Prestige, Australia, Les Misérables (2012), for which he was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Actor, The Greatest Showman (2017), and Logan (2017). On stage he won a Tony Award for The Boy from Oz and has hosted the Tony Awards and the 81st Academy Awards.

Personal life

Jackman was married to actress Deborra-Lee Furness from 1996 until their separation in 2023; they have two adopted children. He is known for his philanthropy, including work with World Vision and fair-trade coffee.
