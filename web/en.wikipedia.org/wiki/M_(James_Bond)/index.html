M (James Bond)

M is a fictional character in Ian Fleming's James Bond book and film series; the character is the head of MI6, the British Secret Intelligence Service, and Bond's superior. The codename is derived from the initial of the first head of the Secret Service Bureau, Mansfield Smith-Cumming, who signed documents "C" — a tradition continued by real SIS chiefs, while Fleming chose "M" for his character.

In the novels and films

In Fleming's novels M is Vice Admiral Sir Miles Messervy, a former Royal Navy officer who runs the service from an office overlooking Regent's Park. In the Eon Productions film series, M has been played by Bernard Lee (1962–1979), Robert Brown (1983–1989), Judi Dench (1995–2012), and Ralph Fiennes (2012–present). Dench's portrayal — the first female M — continued into the Daniel Craig era, and her character was revealed to be named Olivia Mansfield before dying in Skyfall (2012), after which Fiennes's Gareth Mallory succeeded her.

Character

M is typically portrayed as a stern, demanding figure who alternates between exasperation at Bond's methods and complete confidence in his abilities. The relationship between M and Bond — part commanding officer, part surrogate parent — is a recurring emotional thread across the series.
