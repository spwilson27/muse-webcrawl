List of The Hunger Games characters

The following is a list of characters in The Hunger Games novels by Suzanne Collins and their film adaptations. The series is set in Panem, a post-apocalyptic nation ruled from the Capitol, which forces its twelve districts to send tributes to fight to the death in the annual Hunger Games.

Main characters

CharacterDescription
Katniss EverdeenThe protagonist and narrator. A hunter from District 12 who volunteers to take her sister Prim's place in the 74th Hunger Games; she becomes the "Mockingjay", symbol of the rebellion.
Peeta MellarkThe male tribute from District 12, a baker's son who has loved Katniss since childhood; famed for his declaration of love on live television.
Gale HawthorneKatniss's hunting partner and best friend from District 12; becomes a rebel soldier and weapons designer.
Haymitch AbernathyThe only living District 12 victor before Katniss and Peeta; their alcoholic but shrewd mentor.
Primrose "Prim" EverdeenKatniss's gentle younger sister, a healer; her selection at the reaping prompts Katniss to volunteer.
Effie TrinketThe Capitol escort for District 12's tributes, known for her wigs and catchphrase "May the odds be ever in your favour".
CinnaKatniss's stylist, whose designs make her the "Girl on Fire"; secretly supports the rebellion.

Capitol and antagonists

CharacterDescription
President Coriolanus SnowThe tyrannical president of Panem, main antagonist; subject of the prequel The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes.
President Alma CoinPresident of District 13 who leads the rebellion and seeks to replace Snow with her own regime.
Seneca CraneHead Gamemaker for the 74th Hunger Games; executed after allowing two victors.
Plutarch HeavensbeeHis successor as Head Gamemaker; secretly a rebel leader.
Caesar FlickermanThe flamboyant television host who interviews the tributes.
Claudius TemplesmithThe Games announcer and commentator.

Tributes and victors

CharacterDescription
RueThe young District 11 tribute in the 74th Games who allies with Katniss; her death sparks unrest.
ThreshThe strong male tribute from District 11 in the 74th Games.
CatoThe brutal Career tribute from District 2, final antagonist of the 74th Games.
Glimmer, Marvel, Clove, FoxfaceOther notable tributes of the 74th Hunger Games.
Finnick OdairThe charismatic District 4 victor of the 65th Games, a key rebel in Mockingjay.
Johanna MasonThe blunt District 7 victor of the Quarter Quell.
Beetee Latier and WiressDistrict 3 victors whose knowledge of the arena's clock design aids the Quarter Quell escape.
Mags FlanaganThe elderly District 4 victor who volunteers for the Quarter Quell.
Annie CrestaA District 4 victor traumatised by her Games; Finnick's wife.
Enobaria, Brutus, Cashmere, GlossCareer victors from Districts 1 and 2 in the Quarter Quell.

District 13 and others

  • Boggs — Coin's right-hand man who becomes Katniss's trusted guard commander.
  • Cressida — a Capitol film director who defects to make rebel propaganda ("propos").
  • Pollux and Castor — cameramen brothers working with Cressida.
  • Greasy Sae — an elderly District 12 market vendor who cares for Katniss after the war.
  • Madge Undersee — the mayor's daughter who gives Katniss the mockingjay pin.
  • Buttercup — Prim's cat.
