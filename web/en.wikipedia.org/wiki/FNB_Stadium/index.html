FNB Stadium

FNB Stadium
Former names: Soccer City (2010 FIFA World Cup)
Location: Soweto, Johannesburg, South Africa
Opened: 1989; rebuilt 2009
Capacity: 94,736
Tenants: Kaizer Chiefs; South Africa national football team

First National Bank Stadium or simply FNB Stadium, also known as Soccer City and The Calabash, is an association football stadium located in Nasrec, bordering Soweto in Johannesburg, South Africa. It is the largest stadium in Africa by capacity.

History

The original stadium opened in 1989 and was the site of Nelson Mandela's first speech after his release from prison in 1990, and of Chris Hani's funeral in 1993. For the 2010 FIFA World Cup it was extensively rebuilt, its distinctive design inspired by the calabash, a traditional African pot, with a mosaic of fire and earthen colours and a ring of lights resembling fire under the pot.

2010 FIFA World Cup

As Soccer City, it hosted the opening match and the final of the 2010 World Cup, in which Spain defeated the Netherlands 1–0. It was also the venue of Mandela's last public appearance, at the final, and of his memorial service in December 2013.

Today

The stadium is home to Kaizer Chiefs and regularly hosts the Soweto derby, national team matches, cup finals, and major concerts.
