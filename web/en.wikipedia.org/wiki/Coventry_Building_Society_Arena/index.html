Coventry Building Society Arena

Coventry Building Society Arena
Location: Coventry, England
Opened: 2005
Capacity: about 32,600
Tenants: Coventry City F.C.; formerly Wasps RFC

The Coventry Building Society Arena is a stadium and events complex in Coventry, England. Opened in 2005 as the Ricoh Arena, it is the home ground of Coventry City Football Club, who moved there from Highfield Road; the rugby club Wasps also played there from 2014 until entering administration in 2022.

History and uses

The complex includes an exhibition hall, hotel and casino. It hosted football matches at the 2012 Olympic Games (as the City of Coventry Stadium), Rugby League World Cup fixtures, and music concerts. Coventry City bought the arena in 2025 following years of disputes over rent and ownership that had twice forced the club to groundshare elsewhere.
