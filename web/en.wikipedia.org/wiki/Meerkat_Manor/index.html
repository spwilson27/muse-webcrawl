Meerkat Manor

Meerkat Manor is a British television programme produced by Oxford Scientific Films that was first broadcast on Animal Planet International in September 2005. The show follows the lives of several groups of meerkats living in the Kalahari Desert, studied by the Kalahari Meerkat Project, presenting their behaviour in a soap-opera-like narrative style. It ran for four series between 2005 and 2008, with a prequel film, Meerkat Manor: The Story Begins, released in 2008.

Overview

The series centred principally on the Whiskers family, led by the matriarch Flower, and their rivals including the Lazuli, Zappa, Commandos, and Aztecs groups. Individual meerkats were given names and personalities, and episodes dramatised struggles over territory, dominance, pup-rearing, predation, and drought. The UK version was narrated by Bill Nighy; Michelle Fairley narrated series four, and the US version used Sean Astin. The death of Flower in series three attracted substantial viewer response and media coverage.

Production

The programme drew on the long-term Kalahari Meerkat Project, led by Cambridge University researchers including Tim Clutton-Brock, whose habituated study groups allowed close filming. The show combined documentary footage with anthropomorphic storytelling, prompting debate about the balance between accuracy and entertainment in wildlife television. It was among Animal Planet's most successful series and was sold internationally, spawning books, a feature-length prequel film, and revivals in later years, including Meerkat Manor: Rise of the Dynasty (2021).
