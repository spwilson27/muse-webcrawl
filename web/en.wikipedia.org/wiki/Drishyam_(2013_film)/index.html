Drishyam (2013 film)

Drishyam (transl. "Visual") is a 2013 Indian Malayalam-language crime thriller film written and directed by Jeethu Joseph and starring Mohanlal and Meena. It follows Georgekutty, a cable-TV operator whose family becomes the prime suspect when the son of a high-ranking police officer goes missing after an altercation with his daughter.

Plot

Georgekutty, an orphan who dropped out of school in the fourth standard, lives with his wife and two daughters in a remote village in Kerala. When a visiting boy's blackmail attempt leads his daughter to kill him accidentally, Georgekutty — drawing on crime stories absorbed from the films he watches — constructs an elaborate alibi that withstands intense police interrogation.

Reception and remakes

The film was a critical and commercial success, becoming one of the highest-grossing Malayalam films of its time. It was remade in several Indian languages, including a Hindi version in 2015, and internationally, including in Chinese as Sheep Without a Shepherd. A sequel, Drishyam 2, was released in 2021.
