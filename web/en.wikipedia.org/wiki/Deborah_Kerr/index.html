Deborah Kerr CBE (1921-2007) was a Scottish actress, nominated six times for the Academy Award for Best Actress, and famed for playing prim yet passionate characters.

Career

After British films including Black Narcissus (1947), Kerr moved to Hollywood. Her beach embrace with Burt Lancaster in From Here to Eternity (1953) is iconic. Other major roles include The King and I (1956), An Affair to Remember (1957) with Cary Grant, Separate Tables (1958), The Sundowners (1960) and The Innocents (1961).

Honours

Never winning a competitive Oscar, she received an Honorary Academy Award in 1994 for an artist of impeccable grace and beauty. She was appointed CBE in 1998.
