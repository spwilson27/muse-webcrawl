Toltec

The Toltec culture was a pre-Columbian Mesoamerican culture that dominated a state centred in Tula, Hidalgo, Mexico, in the early post-classic period, roughly 900–1168 CE. Later Aztec accounts presented the Toltecs as a highly civilised predecessor culture from the legendary city of Tollan.

Archaeology at Tula shows colossal Atlantean warrior statues, a ballcourt, pyramids and chacmool sculptures. The Toltecs' influence spread through trade and possibly military expansion, though the extent of their empire is debated by scholars.
