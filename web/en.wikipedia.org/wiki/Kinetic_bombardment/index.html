Kinetic bombardment

Kinetic bombardment, or a kinetic orbital strike, is the hypothetical act of attacking a target on Earth from orbit with a non-explosive projectile, whose destructive force comes entirely from its kinetic energy at hypersonic speed. The concept is popularly known by the nickname "rods from God".

Concept

Proposals have typically involved bundles of tungsten rods released from a satellite platform, striking with energy comparable to a small explosive charge. The idea emerged during the Cold War and has been studied periodically, though no operational system has been deployed, owing to cost and treaty considerations under the Outer Space Treaty.
