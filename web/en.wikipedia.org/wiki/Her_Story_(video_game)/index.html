Her Story

Her Story is an interactive film video game written and directed by Sam Barlow and released in 2015. It was published by Half Mermaid and released for Windows, OS X, and iOS in June 2015, with an Android version following in 2016. The game is a full-motion video (FMV) title built around live action footage of a single performer, Viva Seifert, who plays the role of Hannah Smith, a woman interviewed by police about the disappearance of her husband, Simon.

Gameplay

The player explores a fictional police database containing 271 short video clips, each a fragment of one of seven interviews conducted in 1994. The clips are not presented in order; the player searches the database by typing words, and the system returns up to the first five clips containing the searched word in their transcript. Through this nonlinear process, the player pieces together the story and interprets the narrator's reliability.

Plot

The interviews concern the disappearance and death of Simon Smith. As the player accumulates clips, a more complicated narrative emerges involving twins, inheritance, deceit, and a glass-making family business, and the identity of the interviewee herself comes into question. The game deliberately offers no single resolution, leaving the interpretation to the player.

Development

Barlow, known for his work on Silent Hill: Shattered Memories, designed Her Story as an experimental take on the FMV genre, which had declined after its 1990s peak. Filming took place over several days in a police interview room setting in Cornwall, England, with Seifert performing against a fixed camera. Seifert researched real police interviews to ground her performance.

Reception

Her Story was critically acclaimed for its writing, performance, and innovative structure, and won numerous awards, including a prize at the Independent Games Festival and three BAFTA Games Awards. It sold hundreds of thousands of copies and helped revive interest in FMV games. Barlow's follow-up, Telling Lies (2019), expanded on the same format.
