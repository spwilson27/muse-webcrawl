China National Petroleum Corporation

Industry: Oil and gas
Founded: November 5, 1999 (origins 1955)
Headquarters: Dongcheng District, Beijing, China
Owner: Government of China (state-owned)

The China National Petroleum Corporation (CNPC) is a major national oil and gas corporation of China and one of the largest energy groups in the world. Its headquarters are in Dongcheng District, Beijing.

Overview

CNPC is the parent company of the publicly listed PetroChina, created in 1999 as part of a restructuring of CNPC, in which most domestic exploration and production assets were transferred. CNPC operates oil and gas exploration, development, refining, marketing, pipelines, and oilfield services in China and in dozens of countries abroad, including in Central Asia, Africa, the Middle East, and the Americas.

History

The corporation's roots lie in China's Ministry of Petroleum Industry. CNPC was established in 1988 when the ministry was dissolved and its business functions transferred to a state corporation; it was reorganized in 1999 into CNPC and its listed arm PetroChina. By revenue, CNPC consistently ranks among the largest companies in the world.
