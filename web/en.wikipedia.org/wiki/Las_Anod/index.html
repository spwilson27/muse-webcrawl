Las Anod

Las Anod (Somali: Laascaanood) is the capital of the Sool region in northern Somalia. The city has been contested between Somaliland, which administered it from 2007, and local Dhulbahante clan forces aligned with the SSC-Khatumo movement.

Following protests and conflict in late 2022 and 2023, heavy fighting in and around Las Anod displaced many residents; Somaliland forces withdrew to bases outside the city in August 2023, and SSC-Khatumo authorities took control, seeking status as a federal member state of Somalia.

Las Anod is a trading centre on the road between Garowe and Erigavo, in an arid area where livestock herding is the main livelihood.
