Bering Island
Location: Commander Islands, Russia
Area: about 1,660 km2

Bering Island is the largest of the Commander Islands in the Bering Sea, off the Kamchatka Peninsula in the Russian Far East, named after explorer Vitus Bering.

History

Bering's expedition shipwrecked on the island in 1741 during the Great Northern Expedition; Bering died there that winter. Survivors built a vessel from the wreck and returned to Kamchatka.

Nature

The island is treeless tundra with rich marine wildlife. It was home to the extinct Steller's sea cow, discovered by the expedition and hunted to extinction within decades. The main settlement is Nikolskoye.
