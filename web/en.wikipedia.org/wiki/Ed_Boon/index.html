Ed Boon

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Boon, co-creator of Mortal Kombat and creative director at NetherRealm Studios

Edward John Boon (born February 22, 1964) is an American video game programmer and director, best known as the co-creator, with John Tobias, of the Mortal Kombat fighting game franchise. He has worked on the series continuously since its 1992 arcade debut and is the creative director and chief creative officer of NetherRealm Studios in Chicago, a Warner Bros. Games studio.

Career

Boon studied mathematics and computer science at the University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign and joined Williams Electronics (later Midway) as a pinball programmer, contributing to tables such as FunHouse (for which he provided the voice of the ventriloquist dummy Rudy). With Tobias, he led the small team that created Mortal Kombat (1992), notable for its digitised actors, graphic "Fatality" finishing moves, and a secrecy-laden mythology; its violence was a centrepiece of the 1993 US congressional hearings that led to the creation of the ESRB ratings system. Boon also voiced the iconic "Get over here!" and "Come here!" lines of Scorpion in the early games and 1995 film.

Following Midway's bankruptcy, Boon's team became NetherRealm Studios under Warner Bros. in 2010, releasing the rebooted Mortal Kombat (2011), the DC Comics fighting series Injustice: Gods Among Us (2013) and Injustice 2 (2017), Mortal Kombat 11 (2019) and Mortal Kombat 1 (2023). He is known for engaging directly with fans on social media and hinting at roster additions; the characters Noob Saibot (from Boon and Tobias's surnames spelled backwards) and the hidden joke character "Mokap" reflect the series' inside humour.
