Ferrari Fernando Alonso

The Ferrari Fernando Alonso is a special-edition Ferrari 599 GTB Fiorano produced by Ferrari to celebrate 60 years of Ferrari victories in Formula One and named after driver Fernando Alonso. Announced in December 2011, production was limited to 40 cars, all in right-hand drive for the Hong Kong and other markets.

Specification

Based on the 599 HGTE, the Fernando Alonso edition is finished in a unique Rosso Scuderia paint scheme with white accents recalling Alonso's helmet and the Spanish flag colors, with carbon-fiber and Alcantara interior trim bearing Alonso's signature embroidered on the seats. Its 6.0-litre V12 produces about 620 PS (456 kW), driving the rear wheels through a six-speed automated manual F1 transmission.

Background

The edition commemorated the line from Froilán González's 1951 British Grand Prix victory — Ferrari's first in Formula One — to Alonso's wins for Scuderia Ferrari, six decades later.
