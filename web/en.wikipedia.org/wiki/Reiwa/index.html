Reiwa

The characters 令和 (Reiwa), announced on 1 April 2019

The Reiwa era (Japanese: 令和, pronounced [ɾeːwa]) is the current and 232nd era of the official calendar of Japan. It began on 1 May 2019, the day on which Emperor Akihito's eldest son, Naruhito, ascended the throne as the 126th Emperor of Japan. The day before, Emperor Akihito abdicated the Chrysanthemum Throne, marking the end of the Heisei era. The year 2019 corresponds with Heisei 31 until 30 April, and with Reiwa 1 (令和元年, Reiwa gannen, "the first year of Reiwa") from 1 May.

Background

Emperor Akihito's abdication was the first by a Japanese emperor in over two centuries. Because the era name was to be announced before the new emperor's accession for the first time under the modern system, the name was revealed on 1 April 2019 by Chief Cabinet Secretary Yoshihide Suga, who held up a framed calligraphy of the two characters — an image that became iconic in Japan.

Meaning

The characters 令 (rei) and 和 (wa) were taken from a poem in the Man'yōshū, the oldest extant collection of Japanese waka poetry — the first time an era name was derived from a Japanese classical text rather than Chinese literature. The poem describes plum blossoms in early spring. The government gave the name the interpretation "beautiful harmony": rei can mean "beautiful" or "orderly" in this context, and wa means "harmony" or "peace".

Conversion

To convert a Gregorian calendar year from 2019 onwards to the Reiwa year, subtract 2018. Reiwa 1 corresponds to 2019, Reiwa 2 to 2020, and so on.
