List of countries by population density

Population density is the number of inhabitants per square kilometre of total land area. The figures below are based on UN data for a recent year and are approximate. City-states and small island nations dominate the top of the ranking.

Least densely populated

The least densely populated sovereign states are Mongolia (~2 per km²), Namibia (~3), Australia (~3.5), Iceland (~3.7), Libya, Canada, Mauritania, Botswana and Kazakhstan (~7). Greenland, a Danish territory, has about 0.03 people per km² — the lowest of any major land area.
