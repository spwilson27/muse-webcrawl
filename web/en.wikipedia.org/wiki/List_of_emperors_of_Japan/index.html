List of emperors of Japan

The Emperor of Japan is the head of state and the head of the Imperial Family of Japan. The Japanese monarchy is the oldest continuous hereditary monarchy in the world, traditionally founded by the legendary Emperor Jimmu in 660 BC. The list below follows the traditional order of succession; the first several dozen emperors are largely legendary, with reigns becoming historically verifiable around the 6th century AD. Naruhito, the current emperor (reigned from 2019), is traditionally counted as the 126th emperor.
