Ronaldo Luís Nazário de Lima (born 18 September 1976), known simply as Ronaldo, is a Brazilian former professional footballer who played as a striker. Nicknamed O Fenômeno ("The Phenomenon") and R9, he is widely regarded as one of the greatest players of all time. In his prime he combined explosive pace, power (standing 1.83 m), close dribbling with step-overs, and clinical finishing.

Club career

Ronaldo began his career at Cruzeiro before moving to Europe with PSV Eindhoven in 1994. After two prolific seasons in the Netherlands he joined FC Barcelona, scoring 47 goals in 49 games in 1996–97 and winning the FIFA World Player of the Year award. A world-record transfer took him to Inter Milan in 1997, where his career was blighted by serious knee injuries, including two ruptured tendons. He recovered to join Real Madrid in 2002 as one of the Galácticos, winning La Liga in his first season, and later played for AC Milan and, back in Brazil, Corinthians, retiring in 2011.

International career

Ronaldo won the FIFA World Cup with Brazil in 1994 (as an unused squad member) and 2002, when his eight goals — including both in the final against Germany — won him the Golden Boot. He was runner-up in 1998, when he suffered a convulsive fit hours before the final. His 15 goals across three World Cups stood as the all-time tournament record until Miroslav Klose surpassed it in 2014. He scored 62 goals in 98 appearances for Brazil and also won two Copa América titles (1997, 1999).

Honours and legacy

Ronaldo won the Ballon d'Or in 1997 and 2002 and was named FIFA World Player of the Year three times (1996, 1997, 2002). After retiring, he became a businessman and club owner, acquiring a controlling stake in Spanish club Real Valladolid in 2018 and in Cruzeiro in 2021, later selling his stakes. He was inducted into the Brazilian Football Museum Hall of Fame and the Inter Milan Hall of Fame.
