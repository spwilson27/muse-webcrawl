AerCap Holdings N.V. is the world's largest aircraft leasing company, headquartered in Dublin, Ireland, with offices around the world including Shannon, Miami, Singapore, Amsterdam, and Shanghai. The company leases aircraft, engines, and helicopters to airlines and other operators, and provides fleet management and asset trading services.

History

AerCap traces its origins to Guinness Peat Aviation (GPA), the Irish aircraft leasing pioneer founded in 1975. After GPA's financial difficulties in the early 1990s, its leasing platform passed through the hands of GE Capital and the Dutch company debis AirFinance before becoming AerCap in 2005. The company listed on the New York Stock Exchange in 2006.

Major acquisitions

In 2014 AerCap acquired International Lease Finance Corporation (ILFC) from American International Group for about $5 billion, roughly doubling its fleet and making it the industry's largest lessor by fleet value among independent companies. In March 2021 AerCap announced the acquisition of GE Capital Aviation Services (GECAS) from General Electric in a deal valued at more than $30 billion; the transaction closed in November 2021 and created a leasing giant with more than 2,000 owned, managed, or on-order aircraft, together with over 300 helicopters and more than 900 engines.

Operations

AerCap's customers number around 300 airlines in approximately 80 countries. Its portfolio is weighted toward modern, fuel-efficient narrowbody and widebody types from Airbus and Boeing, including the A320neo family, A350, 737 MAX, and 787. The company finances its fleet in the capital markets and is one of the largest issuers of aviation asset-backed securities.
