Jurassic World

Jurassic World is a 2015 American science fiction adventure film directed by Colin Trevorrow and written by Rick Jaffa, Amanda Silver, Derek Connolly, and Trevorrow. It is the fourth instalment of the Jurassic Park franchise and the first of the Jurassic World trilogy. Set twenty-two years after the original film, it depicts a fully operational dinosaur theme park on Isla Nublar that descends into chaos when a genetically engineered hybrid dinosaur, the Indominus rex, escapes.

Plot

Brothers Zach and Gray Mitchell visit their aunt Claire Dearing, operations manager of Jurassic World, a luxury resort on Isla Nublar owned by Simon Masrani's Masrani Global. To counter declining visitor interest the park has created the Indominus rex, a secretive hybrid engineered by Dr. Henry Wu. When the animal appears to climb out of its paddock, former Navy veteran and raptor trainer Owen Grady investigates; the dinosaur, able to camouflage and mask its thermal signature, escapes, killing the containment team. As the park is evacuated, military-minded executive Vic Hoskins seizes control, intending to weaponise Owen's trained Velociraptors. The raptors turn on the soldiers under the hybrid's influence; in the climax Claire releases the veteran Tyrannosaurus rex, which, aided by the surviving raptor Blue, fights the Indominus to the lagoon's edge, where the Mosasaurus drags it underwater.

Production

A fourth Jurassic film languished in development from 2001; Steven Spielberg produced, and Trevorrow was hired in 2013. Filming took place in Hawaii and Louisiana in 2014, combining animatronics by Legacy Effects with extensive CGI by Industrial Light & Magic. Michael Giacchino composed the score, incorporating John Williams's themes.

Reception

The film grossed $1.67 billion worldwide, then the third-highest total ever, and was the top-earning film of 2015. Critics praised its spectacle and set pieces while finding the human characters thin. It was followed by Jurassic World: Fallen Kingdom (2018) and Jurassic World Dominion (2022).
