A Certain Scientific Railgun (Japanese: とある科学の超電磁砲) is a Japanese manga series written by Kazuma Kamachi and illustrated by Motoi Fuyukawa, serialized in Monthly Comic Dengeki Daioh since 2007. It is a spin-off of Kamachi's A Certain Magical Index light novels, retelling events of that series from the perspective of Mikoto Misaka, one of its major characters, and expanding on storylines set in Academy City.

Plot

In the futuristic Academy City, where students are trained as espers with scientifically developed powers, Mikoto Misaka is one of only seven Level 5 espers, nicknamed "Railgun" for her signature attack of electromagnetically firing coins at hypersonic speed. Together with her roommate Kuroko Shirai, a teleporter working for the student disciplinary committee Judgment, and their friends Kazari Uiharu and Ruiko Saten, Mikoto confronts conspiracies including the Level Upper network and the Sisters — thousands of her own clones produced for a secret experiment.

Media

An anime television adaptation by J.C.Staff aired in 2009, followed by Railgun S in 2013, an OVA, and Railgun T in 2020. The series is widely regarded as one of the most popular branches of the Index franchise, with the S season's adaptation of the Sisters arc receiving particular praise. Spin-off manga and video game adaptations have also been produced.
