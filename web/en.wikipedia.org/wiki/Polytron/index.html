Polytron

Polytron Corporation
Industry: Video games
Founded: 2005, Montreal, Canada
Best known for: Fez

Polytron Corporation is a Canadian independent video game developer based in Montreal, founded by game designer Phil Fish and producer (later co-founder) Renaud Bédard. The studio is best known for the puzzle-platform game Fez.

Fez

Fez was announced in 2007 and won the Excellence in Visual Art award at the 2008 Independent Games Festival. The game stars Gomez, a two-dimensional character who discovers that his world is three-dimensional; the player rotates the world in 90-degree steps to navigate its levels. After a long development marked by legal and business difficulties documented in the film Indie Game: The Movie, Fez was released for Xbox Live Arcade in April 2012 and went on to win the IGF's Seumas McNally Grand Prize; it was later ported to many platforms and sold over a million copies.

Later history

A sequel, Fez II, was announced in 2013 but cancelled shortly afterward when Fish announced his departure from the games industry following public disputes. Polytron subsequently collaborated on projects such as the interactive music experience Panoramical (2015) and, with other partners, SuperHyperCube (2016), a virtual reality puzzle game.
