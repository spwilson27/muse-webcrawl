Little Shop of Horrors is a 1986 American horror comedy musical film directed by Frank Oz. It is an adaptation of the 1982 off-Broadway musical of the same name by composer Alan Menken and writer Howard Ashman, itself based on the 1960 film by Roger Corman. The film stars Rick Moranis as Seymour Krelborn, a meek florist who discovers a strange plant that feeds on human blood.

Plot

Seymour Krelborn works at Mushnik's Skid Row flower shop alongside Audrey, the kind but downtrodden woman he secretly loves. During a total eclipse, Seymour finds a mysterious plant, which he names Audrey II. The plant draws crowds and saves the failing shop, but Seymour discovers it survives only on human blood. As Audrey II grows enormous and demanding ("Feed me!"), Seymour is drawn into murder: he feeds it Audrey's sadistic dentist boyfriend Orin Scrivello (Steve Martin) and later Mr. Mushnik himself. In the theatrical ending, Seymour destroys the plant and escapes with Audrey; the original ending, in which Audrey II triumphs and its offspring conquer the world, was reshot after test audiences reacted badly.

Cast

  • Cameos include Jim Belushi, John Candy, Christopher Guest, and Bill Murray as a masochistic dental patient

Production and reception

The film was shot at Pinewood Studios in England, where a full Skid Row street set was built; the giant Audrey II puppets required dozens of puppeteers for the finale. Songs include "Skid Row (Downtown)", "Somewhere That's Green", "Suddenly, Seymour", "Mean Green Mother from Outer Space" (written for the film and nominated for the Academy Award for Best Original Song), and "Dentist!". The film received positive reviews and has become a cult classic. A director's cut restoring the original 23-minute ending was released in 2012 to acclaim.
