Team Meat, the studio behind Super Meat Boy

Team Meat is an American independent video game developer based in Santa Cruz, California. It was founded in 2009 by Edmund McMillen and Tommy Refenes. The studio is best known for Super Meat Boy (2010), a critically acclaimed precision platformer that became one of the emblematic successes of the early-2010s indie game boom.

History

McMillen and Refenes first collaborated on Meat Boy, a Flash game released on Newgrounds in 2008. Its popularity led the pair to develop a full commercial sequel for Xbox Live Arcade and PC. Super Meat Boy, in which a cube of raw meat rescues his girlfriend Bandage Girl from the villain Dr. Fetus, sold over a million copies within its first years and won multiple awards; the duo's development struggles were chronicled in the 2012 documentary Indie Game: The Movie.

After McMillen's departure in the mid-2010s to focus on The Binding of Isaac, Team Meat continued under Refenes. The studio developed Super Meat Boy Forever, an auto-runner sequel released in December 2020, and later projects including Dr. Fetus' Mean Meat Machine (2023), a puzzle spin-off developed with Headup Games. A further sequel, Super Meat Boy 3D, has also been announced.
