List of land borders

This is a list of land borders between sovereign states, with their lengths. The longest international land border is that between Canada and the United States, at 8,891 km including the Alaska segment. China and Russia each border 14 countries, more than any other states.

Total border length by country

China has the longest total land frontier of any country, at roughly 22,000 km, followed by Russia at about 20,000 km. A number of island states, such as Japan, Australia, and Iceland, have no land borders at all.
