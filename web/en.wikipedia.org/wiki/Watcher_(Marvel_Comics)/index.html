Watcher (Marvel Comics)

The Watchers are a fictional species appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby, they first appeared in Fantastic Four #13 (April 1963). The best-known Watcher is Uatu, who is assigned to observe Earth and often narrates stories in the Marvel Universe.

Fictional history

The Watchers are among the oldest beings in the universe. Billions of years ago they attempted to share their knowledge with the younger race of the Prosilicans; the gift of nuclear technology led that race to destroy itself, after which the Watchers swore an oath of strict non-interference, vowing only to watch and record events. Uatu, stationed on Earth's Moon, repeatedly breaks this oath to aid the Fantastic Four and other heroes, most famously warning Earth of the coming of Galactus.

In other media

Uatu, voiced by Jeffrey Wright, serves as narrator of the Disney+ animated series What If...? (2021–2024), which explores alternate timelines of the Marvel Cinematic Universe. A group of Watchers also cameoed, with Stan Lee, in Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2 (2017).
