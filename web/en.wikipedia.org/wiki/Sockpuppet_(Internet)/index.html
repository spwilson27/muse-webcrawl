Sockpuppet (Internet)

A sockpuppet is an online identity used for purposes of deception. The term, a reference to a hand puppet made from a sock, originally denoted a false identity used by a member of an internet community to praise, defend or support their own main account while pretending to be someone else.

Sockpuppet
TypeDeceptive online identity
RelatedAstroturfing, Sybil attack, meatpuppet

Uses

Sockpuppets are used to evade bans, create false consensus, cast extra votes, review one's own products, or conduct astroturfing campaigns. A "meatpuppet" is a real person recruited to act similarly. On Wikipedia, undisclosed multiple accounts used deceptively are banned; legitimate alternate accounts (e.g., for privacy or bots) are allowed within policy. CheckUser tools compare technical data to expose abuse.

History and notable cases

The term dates to Usenet in the 1990s. Notable cases include paid-review scandals, corporate and political astroturfing exposed by journalists, and large state-linked troll operations. Detection relies on behaviour patterns, writing style and technical metadata.
