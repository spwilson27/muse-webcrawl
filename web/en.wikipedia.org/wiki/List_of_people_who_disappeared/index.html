List of people who disappeared

This is a list of people who disappeared mysteriously and whose fate remains unknown, or who were never found. It excludes people later located alive or whose remains were identified. Many of history's most famous disappearances involve aviators, explorers, judges and children.

Before 1900

NameYearNotes
The Princes in the Tower (Edward V and Richard of Shrewsbury)1483Vanished in the Tower of London; presumed murdered.
The Roanoke Colony ("Lost Colony")1590Entire English colony in present-day North Carolina found abandoned.
Lord Percy Percy / the crew of the Mary Celeste1872The brigantine was found drifting in the Atlantic with no one aboard.

1900–1950

NameYearNotes
Ambrose Bierce1914American writer who vanished in revolutionary Mexico.
Percy Fawcett1925British explorer lost in the Amazon searching for the "Lost City of Z".
Joseph Force Crater1930New York judge whose disappearance earned him the nickname "the missingest man in America".
Amelia Earhart and Fred Noonan1937Vanished over the Pacific during an attempted circumnavigation; declared dead 1939.
Antoine de Saint-Exupéry1944French writer-aviator lost on a reconnaissance flight; wreckage found in 2000.
Raoul Wallenberg1945Swedish diplomat arrested by Soviet forces in Budapest; fate long disputed.
Flight 191945Five US Navy torpedo bombers lost in the Bermuda Triangle region.
Glenn Miller1944American bandleader whose aircraft vanished over the English Channel.

1950–present

NameYearNotes
Frank Morris and the Anglin brothers1962Escaped from Alcatraz; never confirmed to have survived or died.
Jim Thompson1967American silk entrepreneur who vanished in Malaysia's Cameron Highlands.
D. B. Cooper1971Hijacker who parachuted from a Boeing 727 with ransom money; never identified or found.
Jimmy Hoffa1975American labour leader; disappeared from a Detroit-area restaurant car park.
Malaysia Airlines Flight 370 passengers and crew2014The aircraft vanished over the Indian Ocean; the main wreckage has never been located.
Lord Lucan (Richard Bingham)1974British peer who vanished after the murder of his children's nanny.
Harold Holt1967Prime Minister of Australia who disappeared while swimming at Cheviot Beach.
Jean Spangler1949American actress who vanished in Los Angeles.
The Sodder children1945Five children unaccounted for after a house fire in West Virginia.
