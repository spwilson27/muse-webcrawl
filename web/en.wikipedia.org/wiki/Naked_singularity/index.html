Naked singularity

In general relativity, a naked singularity is a gravitational singularity that is not hidden within an event horizon, so that light and information could in principle escape from it and be observed by distant observers.

Cosmic censorship

Roger Penrose's cosmic censorship hypothesis proposes that nature forbids naked singularities: singularities formed in realistic gravitational collapse are always clothed by event horizons, preserving the predictability of physics outside them.

Possible examples

Mathematical solutions such as overspinning Kerr black holes or certain collapse models can exhibit naked singularities, but whether they can form from realistic initial conditions remains an open question in theoretical physics.
