Coming Home (1978 film)

Directed by: Hal Ashby
Written by: Waldo Salt, Robert C. Jones
Story by: Nancy Dowd
Starring: Jane Fonda, Jon Voight, Bruce Dern
Release date: February 15, 1978
Running time: 127 minutes

Coming Home is a 1978 American drama film directed by Hal Ashby, from a screenplay by Waldo Salt and Robert C. Jones based on a story by Nancy Dowd. It stars Jane Fonda, Jon Voight, and Bruce Dern, and follows a military wife who volunteers at a veterans' hospital while her husband serves in Vietnam, where she falls in love with a paraplegic veteran.

Plot

In 1968, Sally Hyde's husband Bob, a Marine captain, ships out to Vietnam. Lonely and searching for purpose, Sally volunteers at a veterans' hospital, where she reconnects with Luke Martin, a high-school acquaintance left paraplegic by his wounds. As Luke becomes an outspoken anti-war activist, Sally's worldview transforms, and the two fall in love. When Bob returns home, psychologically damaged, the triangle resolves in tragedy.

Reception and awards

The film was a critical and commercial success and a landmark in Hollywood's engagement with the Vietnam War. At the 51st Academy Awards, it won Best Actor for Jon Voight, Best Actress for Jane Fonda, and Best Original Screenplay, with nominations including Best Picture and Best Director. Voight and Fonda also won acting prizes at the Cannes Film Festival and the Golden Globes.

The soundtrack famously uses songs of the 1960s, including tracks by the Rolling Stones, Buffalo Springfield, and Tim Buckley.
