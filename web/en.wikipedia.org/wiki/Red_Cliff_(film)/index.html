Red Cliff (film)

Red Cliff (Chinese: 赤壁; pinyin: Chìbì) is a 2008–09 Chinese epic war film based on the Battle of Red Cliffs (AD 208–209) and the events at the end of the Han dynasty and immediately prior to the Three Kingdoms period in imperial China. The film was directed by John Woo and stars Tony Leung, Takeshi Kaneshiro, Zhang Fengyi, Chang Chen, Zhao Wei, Hu Jun and Lin Chi-ling.

Release

The film was released in two parts in Asia, with the first part premiering in July 2008 and the second in January 2009. Outside Asia, a single condensed 148-minute version was released in 2009. With a budget of roughly US$80 million, it was the most expensive Asian-financed film at the time, and it became the highest-grossing domestic film in Chinese box-office history on its release.

Plot

In AD 208, the Han-dynasty chancellor Cao Cao seeks permission from Emperor Xian to launch a campaign against the southern warlords Liu Bei and Sun Quan, ostensibly to reunify the empire. The allied forces of Liu Bei and Sun Quan, advised by the strategists Zhuge Liang and Zhou Yu, make their stand at Red Cliffs, where a much smaller force defeats Cao Cao's vast fleet, decisively shaping the division of China into three kingdoms.
