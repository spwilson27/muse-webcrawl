B.P.R.D. is a comic book series published by Dark Horse Comics, spinning off from Mike Mignola's Hellboy. The name refers to the fictional Bureau for Paranormal Research and Defense, an agency that investigates and combats occult threats. After Hellboy left the Bureau in the Conqueror Worm storyline, the B.P.R.D. series followed the remaining agents on their own adventures.

Overview

The Bureau was founded in 1944 by Professor Trevor Bruttenholm to fight hidden Nazi occult operations. Its field team has included the amphibious Abe Sapien, the pyrokinetic Liz Sherman, the homunculus Roger, the ectoplasmic Johann Kraus, Captain Ben Daimio and director Kate Corrigan.

Story cycles

Long-running arcs drawn largely by Guy Davis include Plague of Frogs, in which a fungal cult spreads monstrous frog creatures; Hell on Earth, depicting apocalyptic catastrophe; and The Devil You Know, which concluded the saga begun in Hellboy.
