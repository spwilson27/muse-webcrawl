Dolby Cinema

Dolby Cinema is a premium cinema created by Dolby Laboratories that combines Dolby proprietary technologies such as Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos, as well as other signature entrance and intrinsic design qualities.

Technology

Dolby Cinema uses a Dolby Vision projection system — dual modular 4K laser projectors capable of high dynamic range with a contrast ratio far exceeding standard digital cinema — and Dolby Atmos object-based surround sound with speakers mounted throughout the auditorium including the ceiling. Auditoriums feature curved video walls at the entrance, reserved recliner seating and acoustic treatments.

History

The first Dolby Cinema installations opened in December 2014 in the Netherlands and Spain. In the United States, Dolby partnered with AMC Theatres (AMC Prime at AMC, later Dolby Cinema at AMC). The format competes with IMAX and other premium large formats.
