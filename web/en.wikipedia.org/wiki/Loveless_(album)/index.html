Loveless is the second studio album by the Irish-English rock band My Bloody Valentine, released on 4 November 1991 by Creation Records. It is widely regarded as the definitive album of the shoegaze genre and one of the greatest albums of all time.

Recording

Frontman Kevin Shields spent two years and a reported £250,000 — a sum said to have nearly bankrupted Creation Records — recording the album across numerous studios with many engineers. Its sound was built from layers of distorted and pitch-bent guitar ("glide guitar"), sampled drums, and the half-buried, androgynous vocals of Shields and Bilinda Butcher.

Music

Tracks such as "Only Shallow", "When You Sleep", "Sometimes" and "Soon" dissolve conventional song structures in waves of noise and melody. The album's abstract pink cover art, a blurred photograph of a guitar, mirrors its hazy sonics.

Reception and legacy

Contemporary reviews were favourable but sometimes bewildered; acclaim has grown steadily since. Loveless regularly appears near the top of critics' greatest-album lists and has influenced generations of alternative, dream pop and experimental artists. My Bloody Valentine did not release a follow-up until m b v in 2013.
