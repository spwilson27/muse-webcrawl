Kilkenny GAA

Kilkenny GAA
Irish: Cill Chainnigh
Nickname: The Cats
Colours: Black and amber
Ground: UPMC Nowlan Park, Kilkenny
Dominant sport: Hurling

The Kilkenny County Board of the Gaelic Athletic Association (Kilkenny GAA) is one of the 32 county boards of the GAA in Ireland and is responsible for Gaelic games in County Kilkenny. The county team, nicknamed the Cats, plays in black and amber striped jerseys and is the most successful team in the history of hurling.

Hurling

Kilkenny has won the All-Ireland Senior Hurling Championship a record number of times—over thirty titles, beginning with their first in 1904—and the county's domination reached its peak under manager Brian Cody, who led the team from 1999 to 2022 and won eleven All-Ireland titles, including a four-in-a-row from 2006 to 2009. Kilkenny players such as Eddie Keher, Henry Shefflin—widely regarded as the greatest hurler of all time—and T. J. Reid have become icons of the sport.

Uniquely among Irish counties, Kilkenny does not field a senior Gaelic football team in the All-Ireland championship, hurling being overwhelmingly the dominant code. The county also competes strongly in camogie, winning numerous All-Ireland senior titles.

Grounds

The county's principal ground is Nowlan Park (UPMC Nowlan Park for sponsorship reasons) in Kilkenny city, with a capacity of around 27,000.
