Giuseppe Bertone, known as Nuccio Bertone (4 July 1914 – 26 February 1997), was an Italian automobile designer and entrepreneur. He took over the family coachbuilding business, Carrozzeria Bertone, from his father Giovanni Bertone in 1946 and transformed it into one of the most influential automotive design and manufacturing companies in the world.

Career

Under Nuccio Bertone's leadership, the company produced designs for many of the most celebrated Italian sports cars of the postwar era. He recruited talented young designers including Giorgetto Giugiaro and Marcello Gandini. During Gandini's tenure, Bertone created the Lamborghini Miura, the Lamborghini Countach, and the Lancia Stratos, cars that defined the wedge-shaped supercar aesthetic of the 1960s and 1970s.

Bertone also designed more accessible production cars, including the Fiat X1/9 and various Alfa Romeo, Citroën and Volvo models. Nuccio Bertone was inducted into the Automotive Hall of Fame in 2006.
