Norwegian National Opera and Ballet

The Norwegian National Opera and Ballet (Norwegian: Den Norske Opera & Ballett) is the national opera and ballet company of Norway and the country's largest performing-arts institution. It is based at the Oslo Opera House in the Bjørvika district of Oslo.

History

The company was founded in 1957 as Den Norske Opera, with the celebrated soprano Kirsten Flagstad as its first artistic director, and gave its first performances in 1959 at the Folketeateret. The Norwegian National Ballet was established as part of the institution in the following years, becoming Norway's only professional ballet company. For decades the company performed in cramped premises before moving to its purpose-built home.

Oslo Opera House

The Oslo Opera House, designed by the architectural firm Snøhetta, opened on 12 April 2008. Clad in white Carrara marble and rising from the Oslofjord, the building's sloping roof is open to the public and has become a landmark of the city. Inside are three stages, including the 1,364-seat main auditorium built largely of oak. The institution employs around 600 people, among them opera soloists, a chorus, orchestra, ballet dancers and extensive workshop and technical staff.

Activities

The company performs a repertoire of classical and contemporary opera and ballet, commissions new Norwegian works, and tours domestically and internationally. It also runs education and outreach programmes, and its productions are frequently broadcast and streamed.
