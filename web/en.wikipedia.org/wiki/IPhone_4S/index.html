The iPhone 4S is a smartphone designed and marketed by Apple Inc. It is the fifth generation of the iPhone, succeeding the iPhone 4 and preceding the iPhone 5. Announced on October 4, 2011, it was the last Apple product announced in Steve Jobs's lifetime, the day before his death.

Features

The 4S retained the iPhone 4's glass-and-steel design while introducing the dual-core Apple A5 chip, an 8-megapixel camera with 1080p video recording, a redesigned antenna system supporting both GSM and CDMA, and Siri, Apple's voice-activated intelligent assistant. It shipped with iOS 5.

Reception

The iPhone 4S sold four million units in its first weekend, setting a record for Apple at the time, and its name was jokingly associated with the phrase "4 Steve".
