Empire

Empire
DefinitionPolitical unit of extensive territory ruled by a single supreme authority
ExamplesRoman, Persian, Mongol, British, Ottoman empires
[Image: A map-style image of a vast empire in a single colour spanning continents]

An empire is a political unit made up of several territories and peoples, ruled by a single supreme authority, often an emperor. Empires — Rome, Persia, the Mongols, the Ottomans, Britain — were history's default large-scale states, built by conquest and held by administration and force.

After empires

The 20th century dissolved the great empires into nation-states; 'empire' survives as critique of superpower influence and in the names of institutions (Imperial measurements, the British Empire's honours). Historians debate whether empires delivered order and exchange or extraction and violence — usually both.
