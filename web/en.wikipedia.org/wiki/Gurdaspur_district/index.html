Gurdaspur district

State: Punjab, India

Headquarters: Gurdaspur

Region: Majha

Gurdaspur district is a district in the Majha region of the Indian state of Punjab, in the north of the state along the border with Pakistan and the union territory of Jammu and Kashmir. Its headquarters is the city of Gurdaspur; other towns include Batala, Dera Baba Nanak, Dinanagar and Kalanaur.

Geography and history

The district lies between the Ravi and Beas rivers. In 1947 its award to India under the Radcliffe Line gave India road access to Kashmir. In 2011 the southern tehsils were split off to form Pathankot district. The Kartarpur Corridor, opened in 2019 from Dera Baba Nanak, allows Indian pilgrims visa-free access to the Sikh shrine of Kartarpur just across the Pakistani border.
