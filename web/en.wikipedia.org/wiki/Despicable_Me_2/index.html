Despicable Me 2 is a 2013 American animated comedy film produced by Illumination Entertainment and distributed by Universal Pictures. It is the sequel to Despicable Me (2010) and the second instalment in the Despicable Me franchise.

Plot

Former supervillain Gru, now a devoted father to Margo, Edith and Agnes, is recruited by the Anti-Villain League to help track down the perpetrator who stole a top-secret Arctic laboratory and its mutagen compound PX-41, which can transform living things into indestructible killing machines. Paired with agent Lucy Wilde, Gru suspects Eduardo Perez, the owner of a Mexican restaurant in the Paradise Shopping Mall, of being the supposedly deceased villain El Macho.

Reception

The film received generally positive reviews and grossed over $970 million worldwide, becoming the third-highest-grossing film of 2013. It was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature and for Best Original Song ("Happy" by Pharrell Williams). A sequel, Despicable Me 3, was released in 2017, and the prequel Minions in 2015.
