Sarah Essen is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, primarily in association with Batman. Created by writer Frank Miller, she first appeared in Batman #405 (March 1987), the opening chapter of the "Batman: Year One" storyline.

Sarah Essen, detective and later commissioner of the Gotham City Police Department.

Fictional character biography

Essen is introduced as a detective sergeant in the Gotham City Police Department and the partner of Lieutenant James Gordon during his first year in Gotham. The two develop a close working relationship that briefly becomes an affair, which ends when the corrupt Commissioner Loeb attempts to use it as blackmail against Gordon. Essen subsequently transfers out of Gotham.

Years later, Essen returns to Gotham and is reunited with Gordon following his divorce. The two marry, and Essen rises through the ranks of the department, eventually becoming Commissioner of Police herself. During the "No Man's Land" storyline, in which Gotham is devastated by an earthquake and abandoned by the federal government, Essen is murdered by the Joker, who shoots her while she attempts to protect a group of infants he has taken hostage. Her death is one of the defining tragedies of Gordon's career.

In other media

Sarah Essen appears in the television series Gotham, portrayed by Zabryna Guevara, where she serves as captain of the Gotham City Police Department's homicide squad and a mentor figure to the young James Gordon. A version of the character also appears in animated adaptations of "Batman: Year One".
