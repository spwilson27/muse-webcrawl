William McChord Hurt (March 20, 1950 – March 13, 2022) was an American actor. He studied at the Juilliard School and began his career on stage before his film debut in the science-fiction film Altered States (1980). He became one of the most acclaimed leading men of the 1980s with roles in Body Heat (1981), The Big Chill (1983), and Gorky Park (1983).

Awards and major roles

Hurt won the Academy Award for Best Actor for playing Luis Molina in Kiss of the Spider Woman (1985), a performance that also won him the Best Actor award at Cannes and the BAFTA. He received further Oscar nominations for Children of a Lesser God (1986), Broadcast News (1987), and — in a brief but celebrated supporting turn — A History of Violence (2005). Other notable films include The Accidental Tourist (1988), Dark City (1998), A.I. Artificial Intelligence (2001), and Syriana (2005).

Later career

To television audiences Hurt was known for the legal drama Damages, earning an Emmy nomination, and he played General Thaddeus "Thunderbolt" Ross in several Marvel Cinematic Universe films beginning with The Incredible Hulk (2008). He died in 2022, a week before his 72nd birthday, from complications of prostate cancer.
