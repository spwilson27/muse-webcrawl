The Chief of the Air Staff (CAS) is the professional head of the Royal Air Force and the most senior officer in the RAF. The post was created in 1918 with the founding of the RAF.

History

The first Chief of the Air Staff was Hugh Trenchard, regarded as the father of the Royal Air Force. During the Second World War, Charles Portal held the post and oversaw the RAF's expansion and the strategic bombing campaign.

Role

The CAS is a member of the Chiefs of Staff Committee and the Air Force Board, advises the government on air power, and commands the RAF through Air Command.
