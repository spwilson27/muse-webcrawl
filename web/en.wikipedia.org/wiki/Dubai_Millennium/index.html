Dubai Millennium

Dubai Millennium (1996–2001) was a British-bred Thoroughbred racehorse and sire, owned by Godolphin and trained by Saeed bin Suroor. Regarded as one of the best racehorses of his era, he won nine of his ten races, including the Dubai World Cup in 2000, the Prince of Wales's Stakes and the Prix Jacques le Marois.

Racing career

Born at Dalham Hall Stud, he was originally named Yaazer before being renamed to reflect Sheikh Mohammed's ambitions for the new millennium. Unbeaten at two and three apart from a disappointing run in the Epsom Derby, he dominated in 2000, winning the Dubai World Cup at Nad Al Sheba by six lengths and the Prince of Wales's Stakes at Royal Ascot by eight lengths under Frankie Dettori.

Stud and death

Retired to stud as a highly valuable stallion, he died in April 2001 of grass sickness after covering only a limited number of mares; his best offspring was the Irish Derby winner Dubawi, who became a leading sire.
