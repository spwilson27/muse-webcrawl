Alfred Molina

Alfred Molina (born 24 June 1953) is a British-American actor known for his versatile character roles across film, television, and stage. His best-known performances include the drug dealer Rahad Jackson in Boogie Nights (1997), Diego Rivera in Frida (2002), and the villain Doctor Octopus in Spider-Man 2 (2004), a role he reprised in Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021).

Early life

Molina was born in Paddington, London, to a Spanish father who worked as a waiter and a Italian mother who worked as a cleaner and cook. He trained at the Guildhall School of Music and Drama and began his career with the Royal Shakespeare Company.

Career

His film debut came with a small role in Raiders of the Lost Ark (1981), followed by acclaim for Letter to Brezhnev (1985) and Prick Up Your Ears (1987). Through the 1990s he appeared in Not Without My Daughter, Enchanted April, Maverick, and Paul Thomas Anderson's Boogie Nights and Magnolia. His portrayal of artist Diego Rivera opposite Salma Hayek's Frida Kahlo earned him numerous award nominations, and his Dr. Otto Octavius in Spider-Man 2 is widely regarded as one of the finest villains of the superhero genre.

On stage he has earned Tony Award nominations for Art, Fiddler on the Roof, and Red, and on television he played Ben Weeks in Ryan Murphy's Feud: Bette and Joan (2017).
