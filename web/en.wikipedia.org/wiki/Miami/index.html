Miami

Miami is a coastal city in Florida, the seat of Miami-Dade County, with about 440,000 residents in the city and 6.2 million in the Miami metropolitan area, the largest in the southeastern US. It is nicknamed the 'Magic City' and the 'Capital of Latin America'.

Founded in 1896 — the only major US city founded by a woman, Julia Tuttle — Miami boomed in the 1920s land rush and again after 1959, when Cuban exiles transformed it into a bilingual, Latin American-facing metropolis. Its Art Deco Historic District in Miami Beach, Little Havana's Calle Ocho, the Wynwood Walls and events such as Art Basel Miami Beach define its image. Miami is a hub of international banking, cruise ships ('cruise capital of the world' via PortMiami) and real estate — and among the US cities most exposed to sea-level rise and hurricanes, such as Andrew in 1992.
