Kunta Kinte

Kunta Kinte is a fictional character in Alex Haley's 1976 novel Roots: The Saga of an American Family, portrayed by LeVar Burton (young) and John Amos (adult) in the 1977 television miniseries. Haley claimed Kunta was based on a real Mandinka man captured in the Gambia in the 1760s and enslaved in Virginia.

Story

Kunta, a young Mandinka Muslim, is captured while gathering wood, survives the Middle Passage aboard the Lord Ligonier, and is sold in Annapolis, Maryland in 1767. Given the name 'Toby', he repeatedly resists enslavement; after an escape attempt part of his foot is cut off. His daughter Kizzy passes on the family history — African words, his name, his story — through generations to Haley.

Historical basis and legacy

Historians and genealogists have questioned Haley's claimed documentation, and Haley settled a plagiarism suit over the novel, but Kunta Kinte became an enduring cultural symbol of African-American heritage and the memory of slavery. A memorial stands at the Annapolis dock where the character arrived.
