El Salvador

El Salvador, officially the Republic of El Salvador, is the smallest and most densely populated country in Central America, bordered by Guatemala, Honduras and the Pacific Ocean. It has a population of about 6.3 million and its capital is San Salvador.

El Salvador ('The Saviour') was the heartland of the Pipil people and a Spanish colonial backwater before coffee made its oligarchy ('the fourteen families'). A civil war (1979-1992) between the US-backed government and the FMLN guerrillas killed about 75,000 people and ended with the Chapultepec Accords. Gang violence (MS-13, Barrio 18) later made it one of the world's most violent countries, until President Bukele's crackdown from 2022 collapsed homicide rates. El Salvador became the first country to adopt bitcoin as legal tender (2021) and is famed for surf (Surf City) and pupusas.
