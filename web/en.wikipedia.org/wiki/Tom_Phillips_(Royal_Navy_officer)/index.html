Tom Phillips (Royal Navy officer)

Admiral Sir Tom Spencer Vaughan Phillips (19 February 1888 – 10 December 1941) was a Royal Navy officer who commanded Force Z during the Japanese invasion of Malaya in December 1941.

Phillips sailed from Singapore with the battleship HMS Prince of Wales and battlecruiser HMS Repulse without air cover to intercept Japanese invasion convoys. Both ships were sunk by Japanese land-based torpedo bombers off Kuantan on 10 December 1941; Phillips went down with Prince of Wales. He was one of the highest-ranking Allied officers killed in action in the Second World War.
