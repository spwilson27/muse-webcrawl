Shonda Lynn Rhimes (born January 13, 1970) is an American television producer, screenwriter, and author. She is best known as the showrunner — creator, head writer, and executive producer — of the medical drama Grey's Anatomy (2005–present), its spin-off Private Practice (2007–2013), and the political thriller Scandal (2012–2018), which was credited with helping revitalize ABC's Thursday-night lineup, branded "TGIT" (Thank God It's Thursday). Her production company is called Shondaland.

Early life and career

Rhimes was born in Chicago and raised in University Park, Illinois; her father was a university administrator and her mother a professor. She studied English literature at Dartmouth College, then earned an MFA in screenwriting from the USC School of Cinematic Arts. Early film work included co-writing Introducing Dorothy Dandridge (1999) and writing Crossroads (2002) and The Princess Diaries 2: Royal Engagement (2004).

Television success

Grey's Anatomy premiered in 2005 and became one of the longest-running American primetime medical dramas. Scandal, starring Kerry Washington as fixer Olivia Pope, made Washington the first African-American woman to headline a network drama in nearly 40 years. Other Shondaland series include How to Get Away with Murder (2014–2020), Station 19, and For the People.

Netflix era

In 2017 Rhimes signed a major overall deal with Netflix. Her first series there, the Regency drama Bridgerton (2020), became one of the platform's most-watched English-language series, followed by its prequel Queen Charlotte: A Bridgerton Story (2023) and the limited series Inventing Anna (2022). Her memoir Year of Yes (2015) was a bestseller. Time named her one of the 100 most influential people in the world in 2007 and 2021, and she was appointed a Dame Commander of the Order of the British Empire (DBE) in 2023 for services to media and broadcasting (substantive, as an honorary award was made substantive after she became a British citizen by descent). She was inducted into the Television Academy Hall of Fame in 2017.
