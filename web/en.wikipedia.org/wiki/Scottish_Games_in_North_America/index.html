Scottish Games in North America

Scottish Games (Highland games) in North America are festivals celebrating Scottish and Celtic culture held throughout the United States and Canada. Descended from gatherings organised by Scottish immigrant societies in the 19th century, modern events combine heavy athletics, pipe bands, Highland dancing, clan tents, music, and Scottish food and crafts. Several hundred festivals are held annually, making North America home to the largest Highland games scene outside Scotland.

History

Scottish immigrants founded Caledonian clubs and St. Andrew's societies in the colonial and early republic periods; among the earliest organised games were those in New York and in Nova Scotia in the 19th century. The Grandfather Mountain Highland Games in North Carolina, founded in 1956, grew into one of the largest gatherings, modelled partly on the Braemar Gathering in Scotland. In Canada, long-running events include the Glengarry Highland Games (Maxville, Ontario, since 1948), home to the North American Pipe Band Championships, and games in Antigonish, Nova Scotia, held since 1863.

Events and features

The heavy events typically comprise the caber toss, stone put, Scottish hammer throw, weight for distance, weight for height, and sheaf toss (a distinctively North American addition). Massed pipe bands, solo piping and drumming competitions, and Highland dance championships sanctioned by Scottish governing bodies are standard. Clan and family societies host tents along "Clan Row", and festivals often feature sheepdog demonstrations, harp and fiddle competitions, and ceilidh dancing. Governing and coordinating bodies include Scottish Games Athletics and regional associations that standardise rules and records.

Significance

For the Scottish diaspora — one of the largest ethnic heritages claimed in the United States — the games function as a focal point of identity, tourism, and fundraising for scholarships and cultural preservation. Tartan Day (April 6), officially recognised in the US and Canada, is frequently celebrated in conjunction with games season.
