Rafael Nadal Parera (born 3 June 1986) is a Spanish former professional tennis player. He won 22 Grand Slam men's singles titles, including a record 14 French Open titles — the most by any player at a single major tournament — and is known as the "King of Clay" for his unmatched dominance on that surface. He was ranked world No. 1 for 209 weeks.

Coached by his uncle Toni, the naturally right-handed Nadal plays left-handed on Toni's advice. He won the French Open on his debut in 2005, days after turning 19, and built the sport's greatest rivalry with Roger Federer, culminating in the 2008 Wimbledon final, widely called the greatest match in tennis history, which Nadal won in near-darkness. With Novak Djokovic he contested a record 60 matches. Nadal completed the career Golden Slam by 24, won 92 ATP singles titles and led Spain to five Davis Cups, before injuries forced his retirement after the 2024 Davis Cup in Málaga.
