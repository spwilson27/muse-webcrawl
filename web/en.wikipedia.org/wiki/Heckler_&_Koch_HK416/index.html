Heckler & Koch HK416

HK416
Type: Assault rifle, carbine
Place of origin: Germany
Manufacturer: Heckler & Koch
Cartridge: 5.56×45mm NATO
Action: Gas-operated, short-stroke piston
Feed: STANAG magazines

The Heckler & Koch HK416 is an assault rifle chambered for the 5.56×45mm NATO cartridge. It is designed and manufactured by the German company Heckler & Koch.

History

Although the design is based on the AR-15 class of firearm — specifically the Colt M4 carbine family — it uses a short-stroke gas piston system derived from the HK G36, intended to improve reliability and reduce fouling compared with direct impingement. It was originally developed for United States special operations forces.

Adoption

The HK416 was adopted as the standard rifle of the Norwegian Armed Forces and by the French Armed Forces as the FAMAS replacement (HK416F). A modified variant, the M27 Infantry Automatic Rifle, serves with the United States Marine Corps. The rifle gained public prominence as the weapon used by SEAL Team Six in the operation that killed Osama bin Laden in 2011.

Variants

Variants are offered in barrel lengths from 9 to 20 inches, the semi-automatic MR556/MR223 civilian versions, and the heavier 7.62mm HK417 counterpart.
