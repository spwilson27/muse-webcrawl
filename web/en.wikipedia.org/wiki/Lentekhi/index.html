Lentekhi

Lentekhi is a small town (daba) in western Georgia, the administrative centre of Lentekhi Municipality in the Racha-Lechkhumi and Kvemo Svaneti region. It lies in the Tskhenistsqali river valley on the southern slopes of the Greater Caucasus.

Geography

Lentekhi sits in the historical province of Kvemo Svaneti (Lower Svaneti), surrounded by high mountain ranges. The Tskhenistsqali river flows through the district, and the town is a gateway for travel toward the Svaneti highlands and the Lentekhi–Ushguli routes.

Culture

The area preserves Svan cultural traditions, including medieval defensive towers, churches with frescoes, and the Svan language. Local museums and festivals celebrate the heritage of the mountain communities.
