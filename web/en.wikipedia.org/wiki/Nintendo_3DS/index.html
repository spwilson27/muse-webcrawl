The Nintendo 3DS is a handheld game console developed and manufactured by Nintendo. Announced in March 2010 and released in Japan on 26 February 2011 (and internationally the following month), its signature feature is an autostereoscopic upper display capable of producing a 3D effect without special glasses, with a slider to adjust or disable the depth. A lower touchscreen, an analog Circle Pad, motion and gyro sensors, and dual cameras for 3D photos and augmented reality rounded out the system.

Launch and revisions

The 3DS launched at $249.99 in North America, but slow early sales led Nintendo to cut the price to $169.99 within six months; early adopters were compensated with free downloadable games under the "Ambassador Program". The hardware line grew to include the larger Nintendo 3DS XL (2012), the budget slab-form Nintendo 2DS (2013, no 3D and no hinge), the New Nintendo 3DS models (2014–15) with a more powerful CPU, a C-Stick, and improved "super-stable" 3D, and the New Nintendo 2DS XL (2017). The system is backward compatible with Nintendo DS software.

Features and software

Built-in applications included the Mii Maker, the Nintendo eShop, an internet browser, Netflix and YouTube clients (later discontinued), and AR Games using bundled cards. The wireless StreetPass feature exchanged game data with nearby systems automatically, while SpotPass delivered content via Wi-Fi. Bestselling titles included Mario Kart 7, the Pokémon games of generations VI and VII, Animal Crossing: New Leaf, Super Mario 3D Land, The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D, and Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS. The Nintendo eShop for 3DS closed to new purchases in March 2023, and online play services ended in April 2024. Total sales reached about 75.9 million units and roughly 392 million games.

Legacy

The 3DS sustained Nintendo's dedicated handheld business through the decade and was formally discontinued in September 2020, leaving the Nintendo Switch as the company's single platform. Its stereoscopic experiment remains a distinctive chapter in portable gaming.
