Blazing Saddles

Directed by: Mel Brooks
Starring: Cleavon Little, Gene Wilder, Harvey Korman, Madeline Kahn
Release date: February 7, 1974
Box office: $119.5 million

Blazing Saddles is a 1974 American Western black comedy film directed by Mel Brooks. It stars Cleavon Little as Bart, a Black railroad worker appointed sheriff of the frontier town of Rock Ridge, and Gene Wilder as his deputy, the Waco Kid.

Plot

Corrupt attorney general Hedley Lamarr schemes to drive the residents of Rock Ridge from their land so a railroad can pass through. His plan to discredit the town by appointing a Black sheriff backfires as Bart earns the townspeople's respect and foils the scheme; the climax famously breaks the fourth wall onto the Warner Bros. studio lot.

Legacy

The film was a major commercial success and is routinely ranked among the greatest comedy films ever made. It was selected for preservation in the U.S. National Film Registry in 2006.
