Trotskyism

Trotskyism is the Marxist political theory and organisational practice advocated by Leon Trotsky and his supporters, opposed to Stalinism after their split in the Communist Party of the Soviet Union in the 1920s.

Key ideas

Central tenets include the theory of permanent revolution — that in backward countries bourgeois-democratic tasks can only be completed by a proletarian revolution that grows over into socialism — support for international revolution against Stalin's socialism in one country, and analysis of the USSR as a degenerated workers' state requiring political revolution. Trotsky founded the Fourth International in 1938.

History

Expelled from the USSR in 1929, Trotsky was assassinated in Mexico in 1940. Trotskyist groups have since fragmented into many internationals but remain active in labour and left politics worldwide. See Leon Trotsky and Stalinism.
