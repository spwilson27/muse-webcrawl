The League of Assassins (sometimes known as the League of Shadows) is a fictional group of assassins appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Denny O'Neil and artist Bob Brown, the group first appeared in Strange Adventures #215 (December 1968). The League is led by Ra's al Ghul and is a frequent adversary of Batman.

Fictional history

The League is an ancient, secretive organisation of highly trained killers devoted to Ra's al Ghul's vision of restoring balance to the world, principally by purging humanity, which he views as a plague upon the Earth. Its members are fanatically loyal, trained in martial arts, stealth, and archaic weaponry. The League maintains strongholds around the world, classically including the Himalayan city of 'Eth Alth'eban, and controls the life-restoring Lazarus Pits.

Notable members include Talia al Ghul, Nyssa Raatko, Lady Shiva, David Cain, Bronze Tiger, and Merlyn. Damian Wayne, Batman's son, was raised by the League before defecting to his father's side. Ra's sought Batman as his successor, attempting to recruit him as leader and as husband to Talia.

In other media

The organisation appears as the "League of Shadows" in Christopher Nolan's Dark Knight trilogy, beginning with Batman Begins (2005). It features prominently in the television series Arrow and in animated series and films such as Batman: The Animated Series and Young Justice, as well as in the Batman: Arkham video games.
