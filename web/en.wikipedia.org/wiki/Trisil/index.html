Trisil

Trisil is a fictional crystalline substance in the Star Wars expanded universe. It is described as an amber-coloured crystal material associated with the planet and technologies featured in licensed novels and reference works of the franchise. Within the fiction, trisil is noted for its unusual physical properties and its use in specialised applications by the cultures that mine and refine it.

Created byStar Wars expanded universe authors
GenreSpace opera / science fiction
TypeFictional material

In the expanded universe

Trisil appears in the body of licensed Star Wars fiction produced before the 2014 reorganisation of the franchise's canon, after which such material was rebranded as "Legends". Reference guides catalogued the substance among the many invented minerals, alloys and crystals that populate the galaxy, alongside better-known examples such as cortosis and beskar.
