Shades of Green is an Armed Forces Recreation Center (AFRC) resort located at the Walt Disney World Resort in Lake Buena Vista, Florida. It is one of only five AFRCs worldwide, and the only one located in the continental United States. The resort is owned and operated by the United States Department of Defense and is exclusively for use by members of the United States military, retirees, veterans, and their families, as well as certain Department of Defense civilians.

History

The resort originally opened in December 1973 as a Disney-owned hotel called The Golf Resort, named for its location adjacent to Disney's Palm and Magnolia golf courses. It was later renamed The Disney Inn. In 1994, the U.S. Army leased the property and renamed it Shades of Green; the Department of Defense purchased it outright in 1996. A major expansion completed in 2004 nearly doubled its size.

Facilities

The resort has nearly 600 guest rooms, two swimming pools, restaurants, a fitness center, and direct bus transportation to Walt Disney World theme parks. Room rates are based on the guest's military rank or pay grade.

Eligibility

Eligible guests include active-duty service members, members of the Reserve and National Guard, military retirees, veterans with service-connected disabilities, Medal of Honor recipients, and Department of Defense civilian employees, among others.
