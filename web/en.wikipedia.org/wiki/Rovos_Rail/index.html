Rovos Rail

Founded: 1989
Founder: Rohan Vos
Headquarters: Pretoria, South Africa
Industry: Luxury rail travel

Rovos Rail is a private railway company operating out of Pretoria, South Africa, that runs luxury train journeys through southern Africa. Founded by Rohan Vos in 1989, it markets its main train as "the most luxurious train in the world", the Pride of Africa.

Operations

The company's restored wood-panelled coaches, built and refurbished at its Capital Park depot in Pretoria, carry passengers in period style without televisions or radios in the suites. Journeys include routes between Pretoria and Cape Town, Victoria Falls, and Durban, as well as long expeditions such as Cape Town to Dar es Salaam in Tanzania, and a transcontinental trip from Dar es Salaam to Lobito, Angola, first run in 2019.

Fleet

Rovos Rail maintains a fleet of steam, diesel and electric locomotives and historic coaches, many of them restored from South African Railways stock.
