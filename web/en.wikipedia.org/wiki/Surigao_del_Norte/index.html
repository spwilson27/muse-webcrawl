Surigao del Norte is a province in the Philippines located in the Caraga region of Mindanao. Its capital is Surigao City. The province consists of land on the northeastern tip of Mindanao together with several offshore islands, most famously Siargao, known internationally for the Cloud 9 surf break.

Geography and economy

The province was created in 1960 when the old province of Surigao was divided into Surigao del Norte and Surigao del Sur. Mining, particularly of nickel, is a major industry on the mainland and on islands such as Nonoc, while fishing, agriculture, and tourism — centred on Siargao's surfing and island-hopping — also contribute to the economy.

History

The Surigao Strait offshore was the site of one of the last battleship-versus-battleship actions in naval history during the Battle of Leyte Gulf in October 1944. In December 2021 the province was devastated by Typhoon Rai (Odette), which made landfall on Siargao.
