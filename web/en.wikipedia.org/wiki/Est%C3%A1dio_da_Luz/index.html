The Estádio da Luz, officially the Estádio do Sport Lisboa e Benfica, is a multi-purpose stadium in Lisbon, Portugal, and the home ground of S.L. Benfica. Its name, "Stadium of Light", carries over from the original stadium on the site, opened in 1954 and once one of the largest stadiums in Europe.

History

The present stadium was designed by the architects Populous (then HOK Sport) and built for UEFA Euro 2004, which Portugal hosted; it staged the tournament final, in which Greece beat Portugal 1–0. It replaced the old Estádio da Luz, which was demolished in 2002–03. The stadium later hosted the 2014 UEFA Champions League final and, uniquely, the final stages of the 2019–20 Champions League, played as a single-leg tournament in Lisbon during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Features

The stadium is distinguished by its translucent roof and the red steel arch over one end. An eagle — the club's symbol — is released to circle the pitch before Benfica home matches. With a capacity of roughly 65,000, it is the largest stadium in Portugal.
