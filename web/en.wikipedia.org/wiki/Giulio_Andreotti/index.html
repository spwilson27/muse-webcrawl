Giulio Andreotti (1919–2013) was an Italian statesman of the Christian Democracy party who served as Prime Minister of Italy in seven governments and held ministerial office in nearly every post-war administration until the 1990s. A senator for life from 1991, he was the dominant figure of Italy's First Republic.

Career

A protégé of Alcide De Gasperi, Andreotti entered parliament in 1946 and served as foreign minister (1983–1989) and interior minister, among many posts. His governments of "national solidarity" in the late 1970s relied on the external support of the Italian Communist Party during the Years of Lead.

Trials

After the collapse of Christian Democracy, Andreotti was tried on charges of Mafia association and of ordering the 1979 murder of journalist Mino Pecorelli. He was ultimately acquitted on the murder charge (2003), and the courts found he had committed the offence of association with the Mafia only up to 1980, which was time-barred.
