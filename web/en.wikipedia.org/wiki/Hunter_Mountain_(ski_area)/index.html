Hunter Mountain (ski area)

Hunter Mountain is a ski resort in the town of Hunter, Greene County, New York, in the Catskill Mountains, about two and a half hours north of New York City. It is one of the major ski areas in the northeastern United States and has been operated by Vail Resorts since 2019.

Mountain

The resort's summit is at 3,200 feet (about 980 m) on Hunter Mountain, with a vertical drop of 1,600 feet. It comprises three main areas — Hunter Mountain (the main face), Hunter West, and Hunter North (opened in 2018, the resort's largest expansion) — with around 67 trails and 13 lifts, and 100% snowmaking coverage.

History

The ski area was developed by the Slutzky brothers, local contractors, and opened in 1959–60. It pioneered modern snowmaking in the Catskills. After passing to Peak Resorts in 2015, it was acquired by Vail Resorts in 2019 and added to the Epic Pass network.
