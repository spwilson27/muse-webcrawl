Whitewash (sport)

A whitewash is a sports term, closely related to sweep, describing a series of contests in which one side wins every game or match, leaving the opposing side with no victories at all. The term is especially common in cricket, darts, snooker and rugby.

Usage

In Test cricket, a whitewash occurs when a team wins every match of a series, such as a 5–0 Ashes result. In darts and snooker frames, whitewash describes winning a match without the opponent winning a single leg or frame. The word derives from whitewash paint covering a surface completely, metaphorically leaving the losing side blanked.

Related terms

Clean sweep, bagel (tennis), shutout.
