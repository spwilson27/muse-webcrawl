Mother 3 is a 2006 role-playing video game developed by Brownie Brown and HAL Laboratory and published by Nintendo for the Game Boy Advance. It is the third and final entry in the Mother series, created and written by Shigesato Itoi. Released only in Japan on April 20, 2006, the game follows the boy Lucas and his family and friends on the Nowhere Islands as their pastoral home is invaded and transformed by the Pigmask Army under the mysterious Porky Minch. Though never officially localized, Mother 3 became famous internationally through a celebrated fan translation released in 2008, and it is widely praised for its emotional storytelling.

Gameplay

Like its predecessor EarthBound (1994), Mother 3 is a turn-based role-playing game with a modern-day-inspired setting, whimsical enemies, and a rolling health meter that lets players act before damage fully registers. New to this entry is a rhythm system: pressing a button in time with the background music during attacks lets the player chain up to 16 combo hits. The story unfolds over eight chapters with shifting points of view, following characters including the cowboy Flint, the monkey Salsa, the thief Duster, Princess Kumatora, and the dog Boney, before Lucas becomes the central protagonist.

Plot

The game opens in Tazmily Village, a utopian community on the Nowhere Islands, where twins Lucas and Claus live with their parents, Flint and Hinawa. After Hinawa is killed by a mechanized Drago and Claus disappears seeking revenge, the Pigmask Army gradually modernizes and corrupts the village with money, technology, and "Happy Boxes." Years later, Lucas learns that the islands rest upon a colossal dragon, sealed by seven Needles; whoever pulls the Needles will wake the dragon and remake the world in the image of their heart. Lucas races the Masked Man — ultimately revealed to be a brainwashed Claus — to pull the Needles first. In the final confrontation, Claus regains his memory through his mother's voice and sacrifices himself, and Lucas pulls the last Needle as the world is engulfed, with the ending leaving the fate of the world — implied hopeful — to the player's imagination.

Development

The game had a famously protracted development. It began in 1994 for the Super Famicom, then moved to the Nintendo 64 and its 64DD peripheral under the name EarthBound 64, before being cancelled in 2000. Itoi later revived the project for the Game Boy Advance, reusing much of the planned story in simplified 2D form. The final game launched in 2006 to strong sales in Japan.

Reception and legacy

Mother 3 was acclaimed by critics, including a 35/40 from Famitsu, for its writing, music by Shogo Sakai, and emotional depth, including themes of loss, family, and the costs of modernization. Because Nintendo has never released it outside Japan, dedicated fans at Starmen.net produced a professional-quality English fan translation in October 2008 after years of work, one of the most notable fan translations in gaming history. Lucas appeared as a playable fighter in the Super Smash Bros. series, and persistent fan campaigns have kept hope for an official Western release alive; the game was re-released on the Wii U Virtual Console in Japan in 2015 and on Nintendo Switch Online in Japan in 2024.
