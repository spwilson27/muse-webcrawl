New Journalism (disambiguation)

New Journalism may refer to:

  • New Journalism, the 1960s and 1970s style of news writing that employed literary techniques such as scene-by-scene construction, dialogue and interior point of view, associated with writers including Tom Wolfe, Truman Capote, Hunter S. Thompson, Joan Didion and Gay Talese.
  • New Journalism in the 1880s and 1890s, the populist, campaigning style of British newspaper writing associated with W. T. Stead and the Pall Mall Gazette, emphasizing interviews, headlines and human-interest stories.
  • The New Journalism, Tom Wolfe's 1973 anthology and manifesto of the movement, edited with E. W. Johnson.
  • New journalism (Spanish: nuevo periodismo), Latin American literary journalism and the related foundation work promoting it.

See also: Gonzo journalism, Literary journalism.
