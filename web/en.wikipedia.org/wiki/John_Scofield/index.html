John Scofield (born 26 December 1951) is an American guitarist and composer whose music draws on post-bop, jazz fusion, funk, soul jazz and R&B. Alongside Pat Metheny and Bill Frisell, he is regarded as one of the "big three" jazz guitarists to emerge in the 1970s and 1980s.

Career

Scofield studied at the Berklee College of Music and gained early recognition playing with Gerry Mulligan and Chet Baker, and in the Billy Cobham–George Duke band. From 1982 to 1985 he toured and recorded with Miles Davis, appearing on albums including Star People, Decoy and You're Under Arrest, which brought his slashing, harmonically adventurous style to a wide audience.

As a leader he has recorded dozens of albums, including Still Warm (1986), Blue Matter (1987), Time on My Hands (1990), Hand Jive (1994), A Go Go (1998, with Medeski Martin & Wood), Überjam (2002) and the Grammy-winning Country for Old Men (2016) and Past Present (2015). His 2022 solo album John Scofield, built around looping, also won critical acclaim. Scofield has taught at New York University's Steinhardt School.
