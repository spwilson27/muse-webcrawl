Canadian Pacific Kansas City (CPKC) is a Canadian railway holding company and Class I freight railroad operating a network of roughly 32,000 kilometres (20,000 miles) of track across Canada, the United States, and Mexico. It was formed on 14 April 2023 by the merger of the Canadian Pacific Railway (CP) and Kansas City Southern (KCS), creating the first single-line rail network connecting all three countries of North America.

History

Canadian Pacific announced its agreement to acquire Kansas City Southern in March 2021, valuing the deal at about US$31 billion including assumed debt. After a competing bid from Canadian National was rejected on regulatory and contractual grounds, CP and KCS shareholders approved the combination. The United States Surface Transportation Board approved the merger in March 2023, and the two railways were formally combined on 14 April 2023 under the name Canadian Pacific Kansas City Limited.

Operations

CPKC's network links ports and industrial centres from Vancouver and Montreal through the U.S. Midwest and Gulf Coast to Veracruz and other points in Mexico. The railway moves bulk commodities such as grain, coal, and potash, as well as automotive, energy, and intermodal traffic. Its slogan emphasizes it as the only truly North American railway, with a single network spanning the continent.
