Joseph John Maddon (born February 8, 1954) is an American former professional baseball manager. He managed the Tampa Bay Rays from 2006 to 2014, the Chicago Cubs from 2015 to 2019, and the Los Angeles Angels from 2020 to 2022.

Maddon spent over three decades in the Angels organisation as a minor league player, scout, coach and interim manager before taking over the Rays, whom he led from the worst record in baseball to an American League pennant in 2008. He won the AL Manager of the Year award in 2008 and 2011.

Chicago Cubs

With the Cubs, Maddon won the National League Manager of the Year award in 2015 and in 2016 led the franchise to its first World Series championship in 108 years, defeating Cleveland in seven games. He is known for an unconventional, analytical approach, themed road trips and slogans, and for popularising defensive shifts and flexible line-ups.
