Laurent-Désiré Kabila

Laurent-Désiré Kabila (27 November 1939 – 18 January 2001) was a Congolese politician and revolutionary who served as President of the Democratic Republic of the Congo from 1997 until his assassination in 2001.

Early life

A supporter of Patrice Lumumba, Kabila led a long-running Marxist guerrilla movement in eastern Congo after Lumumba's murder, with brief support from Che Guevara in 1965.

Presidency

As leader of the AFDL alliance backed by Rwanda and Uganda, he overthrew Mobutu and ended Zaire in 1997. His break with his backers triggered the Second Congo War (1998). He was shot by a bodyguard in January 2001 and succeeded by his son Joseph.
