Coke Studio (Pakistani TV program)

Coke Studio
Genre: Music television
Created: 2008
Original producer: Rohail Hyatt
Country: Pakistan
Languages: Urdu, Punjabi, Sindhi, Pashto, Balochi and others

Coke Studio (Urdu: کوک اسٹوڈیو) is a Pakistani television programme and international music franchise featuring live studio-recorded music performances by established and emerging artists. It is the longest-running annual television music show in Pakistan, airing since 2008, and is renowned for fusing traditional forms — Sufi poetry, qawwali, ghazal, and regional folk — with contemporary rock, pop, hip hop, and electronic music backed by a house band.

History and format

The show was created by Rohail Hyatt, formerly of the band Vital Signs, together with his wife Umber Hyatt, and is sponsored by Coca-Cola Pakistan. Hyatt produced the first six seasons, shaping the format of intimate studio sessions in which songs are performed in a single live take with a resident ensemble of session musicians. Later seasons were produced by the band Strings (seasons 7–10), then by Hyatt's successors including Zulfiqar "Xulfi" Khan and, for season 14, a widely praised return to adventurous collaborations.

Notable performances

The programme has produced some of the most viewed music videos in South Asia, including "Tajdar-e-Haram" by Atif Aslam, "Afreen Afreen" by Rahat Fateh Ali Khan and Momina Mustehsan, and "Pasoori" by Ali Sethi and Shae Gill (season 14, 2022), which became a global streaming hit. Performances have featured legends such as Abida Parveen, Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan's repertoire, and folk artists from Sindh, Balochistan, and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, often bringing regional languages to national and international audiences.

Impact

Coke Studio is credited with reviving interest in Pakistani folk and classical traditions among younger listeners and with projecting a softer cultural image of Pakistan abroad. Its format has been exported to India (Coke Studio @ MTV) and other countries. Critics have debated the role of corporate sponsorship in shaping the repertoire, but the show's influence on the region's popular music is widely acknowledged.
