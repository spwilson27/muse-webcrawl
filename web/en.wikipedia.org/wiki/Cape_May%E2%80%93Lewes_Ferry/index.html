Cape May–Lewes Ferry

The Cape May–Lewes Ferry is a ferry system in the United States that travels across the Delaware Bay, connecting North Cape May, New Jersey, with Lewes, Delaware. The crossing of about 17 miles (27 km) takes roughly 85 minutes and forms part of U.S. Route 9.

Cape May–Lewes Ferry
Route: Cape May, NJ – Lewes, DE
Distance: 17 mi (27 km)
Began: 1 July 1964
Operator: Delaware River and Bay Authority

History

Service began on 1 July 1964, using vessels acquired from the Chesapeake Bay ferry system after the Chesapeake Bay Bridge–Tunnel opened. The ferry carries vehicles and foot passengers, and is popular with travellers between the New Jersey shore and the Delaware beaches and points south.

Fleet and terminals

The fleet has included vessels such as MV Delaware, MV New Jersey, MV Cape Henlopen, MV Twin Capes, and MV Cape May. Terminals at both ends offer food, shops, and shuttle connections. The crossing passes the Harbor of Refuge Light and Delaware Breakwater East End Light.
