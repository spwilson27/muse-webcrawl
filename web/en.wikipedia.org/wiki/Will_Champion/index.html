William Champion (born 31 July 1978) is an English musician and songwriter, best known as the drummer and backing vocalist of the rock band Coldplay. A multi-instrumentalist, he also plays guitar and piano and occasionally sings lead vocals in concert.

Early life

Champion was born and raised in Southampton, Hampshire, where his father Timothy was a professor of archaeology at the University of Southampton. He learned guitar at a young age and later took up piano and other instruments. He studied anthropology at University College London, where he met Chris Martin, Jonny Buckland and Guy Berryman.

Coldplay

Champion joined the band in 1997–98 despite having little prior experience as a drummer, quickly adapting to the role as the group that became Coldplay formed. The band has described him as its internal arbiter and "pedant" on musical quality. He co-writes songs with the rest of the group, and the band splits songwriting royalties equally among its four members. His lead-vocal performances include an acoustic rendition of "Death Will Never Conquer" and the live staple "A Message".

Personal life

Champion studied anthropology and once worked as a barman and doorman. He supports Southampton F.C. He is married with children and lives in London.
