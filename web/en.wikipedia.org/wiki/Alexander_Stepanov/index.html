Alexander Alexandrovich Stepanov (born 16 November 1950) is a Russian-American computer scientist best known as the designer of the C++ Standard Template Library (STL) and a leading advocate of generic programming.

Career

Stepanov studied mathematics at Moscow State University and emigrated to the United States in 1977. Working at General Electric, Bell Labs, Hewlett-Packard and later Silicon Graphics, he developed libraries of algorithms based on the idea that algorithms should be specified in terms of abstract requirements on types — the approach he called generic programming. With Meng Lee, he designed the STL, which was adopted into the C++ standard in 1994 and has influenced libraries in many other languages.

Writings

With Paul McJones, Stepanov wrote Elements of Programming (2009), a rigorous treatment of algorithms founded on algebraic structures. He also co-authored From Mathematics to Generic Programming (2015) with Daniel Rose. In 2016 he received an honorary doctorate from Aalto University.
