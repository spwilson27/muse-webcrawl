Katy, Texas

Katy, Texas
State: Texas
Counties: Harris, Fort Bend, Waller
Named for: MKT Railroad ("the Katy")

Katy is a city in the U.S. state of Texas, in the Greater Houston metropolitan area, spanning Harris, Fort Bend and Waller counties. The wider Katy area, including large master-planned communities, has grown rapidly since the late twentieth century.

History

The town was founded in the 1890s on the Missouri–Kansas–Texas Railroad — commonly called "the Katy" — from which it takes its name. It was long a centre for rice farming on the coastal prairie.

Growth

Suburban development along Interstate 10 and the Grand Parkway transformed the area, and the Katy Independent School District is now among the largest in Texas. Attractions include the Katy Mills mall and the historic rice dryers that remain landmarks of the old town.
