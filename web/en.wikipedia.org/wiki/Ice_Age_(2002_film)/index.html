Ice Age is a 2002 American computer-animated adventure comedy-drama film produced by Blue Sky Studios and distributed by 20th Century Fox. It was directed by Chris Wedge, co-directed by Carlos Saldanha, and stars the voices of Ray Romano, John Leguizamo, and Denis Leary.

Set during the Pleistocene ice age, the film follows Manny, a no-nonsense woolly mammoth; Sid, a wisecracking ground sloth; and Diego, a saber-toothed tiger with a hidden agenda, who find a human baby and set out to return it to its tribe. A largely silent subplot follows Scrat, a saber-toothed squirrel, in his endless pursuit of an acorn.

Reception and legacy

The film was a major commercial success, grossing over $383 million worldwide, and was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature. It launched the Ice Age franchise, one of the highest-grossing animated series of all time, followed by The Meltdown (2006), Dawn of the Dinosaurs (2009), Continental Drift (2012), and Collision Course (2016), as well as television specials and the spin-off The Ice Age Adventures of Buck Wild (2022).
