Julius Nepos

Julius Nepos (died 9 May 480) was a Roman emperor who ruled the Western Empire in 474–475. Appointed by the Eastern emperor Leo I, he deposed Glycerius and was recognised in Gaul, Hispania and Italy, but was himself overthrown by his general Orestes in 475 and fled to Dalmatia.

Reign and exile

From Salona, Nepos continued to be recognised by Constantinople as the legitimate western emperor even after Orestes' son Romulus Augustulus was deposed by Odoacer in 476; Odoacer even struck coins in Nepos's name. Nepos was murdered by his own soldiers in 480, after which Odoacer annexed Dalmatia. Some historians regard Nepos, rather than Romulus, as the last legitimate Western Roman emperor.
