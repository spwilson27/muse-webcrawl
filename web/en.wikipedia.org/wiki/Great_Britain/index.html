Great Britain

Great Britain is an island in the North Atlantic Ocean off the northwestern coast of continental Europe, separated from France by the English Channel. With an area of about 209,000 square kilometres it is the largest island in Europe and the ninth-largest in the world, and the third-most populous island, home to England, Scotland and Wales.

Together with the island of Ireland and many smaller islands it forms the British Isles. The name 'Great Britain' also refers politically to England, Scotland and Wales together, which with Northern Ireland constitute the United Kingdom. London, on the River Thames, is its largest city and the UK's capital.
