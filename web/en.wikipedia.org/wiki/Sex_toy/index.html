Sex toy

A sex toy is an object or device used primarily to facilitate human sexual pleasure, including masturbation and partnered sex. The category includes vibrators, dildos, butt plugs, masturbation sleeves, nipple clamps, sex dolls and many other devices. Sex toys are sold in most developed countries through specialist retailers and online shops, and form a substantial global industry.

Types

  • Vibrators — electrically powered devices providing vibration, from external "wand" massagers to insertable designs.
  • Dildos — non-vibrating phallic or non-phallic insertable objects, some fitted for use with a harness.
  • Anal toys — butt plugs, beads and prostate massagers, typically flanged for safety.
  • Masturbators — sleeves and cups simulating penetrative sex, sometimes modelled on performers.
  • Sex dolls and machines — life-size dolls and motorised devices.
  • BDSM equipment — restraints, impact implements and electro-stimulation devices.

Materials and safety

Sex toys are manufactured from silicone, glass, stainless steel, ABS plastic and other materials. Non-porous, body-safe materials are recommended because porous materials can harbour bacteria; toys should be cleaned according to manufacturer guidance, and barriers such as condoms used where appropriate. In most jurisdictions the industry is lightly regulated, so material quality varies.

History and legality

Artefacts interpreted as sex toys date to prehistory, and dildos are documented in many ancient cultures. Vibrators were developed in the 19th century, in part as medical devices for treating "hysteria". Some jurisdictions have at times banned the sale of sex toys — in the United States, Texas and Alabama restrictions were litigated into the 21st century — while in other countries sales are restricted by obscenity law.
