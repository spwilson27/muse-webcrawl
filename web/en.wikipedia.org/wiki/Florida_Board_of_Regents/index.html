Florida Board of Regents

The Florida Board of Regents was the governing body for the State University System of Florida from 1965 until 2001, when it was replaced by the Florida Board of Governors following a constitutional amendment.

History

The board was created to consolidate oversight of Florida's public universities, succeeding earlier control arrangements.

In 2000 voters approved restructuring championed by Governor Jeb Bush; the Board of Regents was abolished, individual university boards of trustees were created, and the Board of Governors assumed system-wide coordination.

Role

The board appointed university presidents, approved budgets and programmes, and set policy for institutions including the University of Florida and Florida State University.
