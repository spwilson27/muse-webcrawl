Emotion

Emotion
ExamplesJoy, anger, fear, sadness, disgust, surprise
ComponentsFeeling, physiology, behaviour, expression
[Image: Faces expressing the basic emotions in a grid of portraits]

An emotion is a complex psychological state involving subjective feeling, physiological arousal and expressive behaviour. Theories range from basic-emotions research (Ekman's six, recognised across cultures) to constructionist accounts in which the brain builds emotions from bodily signals and concepts.

Function

Emotions prioritise action — fear readies flight, anger confrontation, joy exploration — and glue societies through empathy, guilt and love (see Love). Emotional disorders (depression, anxiety) are major causes of disability; emotional intelligence and regulation are teachable skills now common in school curricula.
