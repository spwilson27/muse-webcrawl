Michelle Yeoh Choo Kheng (born 6 August 1962) is a Malaysian actress. Rising to fame in Hong Kong action films of the 1980s and 1990s, in which she performed many of her own stunts, she gained international recognition in the James Bond film Tomorrow Never Dies (1997) and Ang Lee's martial arts epic Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon (2000). For her performance in Everything Everywhere All at Once (2022) she became the first Asian performer to win the Academy Award for Best Actress.

Early life

Yeoh was born in Ipoh, Malaysia, into a Malaysian Chinese family of Hokkien and Cantonese ancestry. She studied dance from the age of four, later training at the Royal Academy of Dance in London, where an injury ended her ambition to become a prima ballerina. In 1983 she won the Miss Malaysia World pageant and represented Malaysia at Miss World.

Career

Cast in Hong Kong action films, Yeoh — credited early on as Michelle Khan — became known for performing her own stunts in films such as Yes, Madam (1985), Magnificent Warriors (1987) and Police Story 3: Supercop (1992), opposite Jackie Chan. After a brief retirement following her first marriage, she returned in the 1990s and broke into Western cinema as Wai Lin in Tomorrow Never Dies (1997), followed by acclaim as Yu Shu Lien in Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon (2000).

Subsequent roles included Memoirs of a Geisha (2005), Sunshine (2007), Aung San Suu Kyi in The Lady (2011), Eleanor Young in Crazy Rich Asians (2018) and Philippa Georgiou in Star Trek: Discovery (2017–2020). Her multiverse role as Evelyn Wang in Everything Everywhere All at Once earned her the Academy Award, Golden Globe and Screen Actors Guild Award for Best Actress.

Personal life and honours

Yeoh has served as a global ambassador for road-safety and development campaigns. She was awarded the Ordre des Arts et des Lettres and the Legion of Honour in France, and in 2024 received the Presidential Medal of Freedom from the United States. She married French motorsport executive Jean Todt in 2023.
