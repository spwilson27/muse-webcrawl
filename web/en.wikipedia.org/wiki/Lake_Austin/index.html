Lake Austin

Lake Austin is a reservoir on the Colorado River in Austin, Texas, in the United States. It was formed in 1939 by the construction of Tom Miller Dam by the Lower Colorado River Authority. Lake Austin is one of the seven Highland Lakes and is a popular site for boating, fishing, and other recreation.

Description

The lake stretches about 21 miles (34 km) through west Austin. Because discharges from Lake Travis upstream keep its level nearly constant, Lake Austin is sometimes called a "constant-level lake". Its shores are lined with waterfront homes, marinas, and restaurants.

Recreation

Popular activities include motorboating, water-skiing, and fishing for largemouth bass and catfish. Emma Long Metropolitan Park on the northern shore offers swimming, camping, and picnic areas.
