List of rivers of France

This is a list of the principal rivers of France, arranged by drainage basin. French rivers flow to the Atlantic Ocean, the English Channel, the North Sea (via the Meuse and Rhine), the Mediterranean Sea and — in a few cases — across borders as parts of larger systems.


The Loire, France's longest river, in the Loire Valley.

The Rhine forms much of France's eastern border for about 190 km, although only its left bank touches French territory (Alsace). The Moselle, a Rhine tributary, drains Lorraine past Metz and Thionville.
