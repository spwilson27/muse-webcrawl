History of Australia (1788–1850)

The history of Australia from 1788 to 1850 covers the first decades of British colonisation, beginning with the arrival of the First Fleet at Sydney Cove in January 1788 and the establishment of the penal colony of New South Wales.

Colonisation

Captain Arthur Phillip founded the settlement at Port Jackson. Convict transportation brought over 160,000 convicts to Australia before it ended, first in the eastern colonies and later in the west.

Indigenous peoples and expansion

Colonisation brought disease, dispossession, and frontier conflict with Aboriginal peoples, whose population fell sharply. New colonies were founded at Van Diemen's Land (1803), Moreton Bay, South Australia (1836), and Port Phillip, while free immigration and the pastoral industry expanded rapidly before the gold rushes of 1851.
