Bolton Wanderers Football Club is a professional association football club based in Bolton, Greater Manchester, England. A founding member of the Football League in 1888, the club won the FA Cup four times, most famously the 1923 "White Horse Final", the first at Wembley.

History

Founded in 1874 as Christ Church Football Club, the club played at Burnden Park for 102 years before moving to its current stadium in Horwich in 1997. Under Sam Allardyce in the 2000s Bolton qualified for the UEFA Cup. Financial crisis brought administration in 2019 and relegation to the fourth tier, followed by recovery under new ownership.

Honours

FA Cup winners 1923, 1926, 1929 and 1958; EFL Trophy winners 1989 and 2023. Club legend Nat Lofthouse scored 255 league goals. See also Bolton.
