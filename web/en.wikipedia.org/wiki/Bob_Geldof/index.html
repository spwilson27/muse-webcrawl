Bob Geldof

Robert Frederick Zenon Geldof (born 5 October 1951) is an Irish singer-songwriter, actor, author and political activist. He rose to fame as the lead singer of the Boomtown Rats, who achieved popularity as part of the punk rock movement. Geldof is widely recognised for his activism, especially his anti-poverty efforts concerning Africa. In 1984, he and Midge Ure founded the charity supergroup Band Aid to raise money for famine relief in Ethiopia. They went on to organise the charity super-concert Live Aid in 1985, the Live 8 concerts in 2005 and the charity single "Do They Know It's Christmas?"

Early life

Geldof was born in Dún Laoghaire, in the Republic of Ireland, to Robert and Evelyn Geldof. His father was of Belgian and Jewish descent; his mother was of Irish and French-Jewish descent. He was educated at Blackrock College, a Catholic, fee-paying public school.

Music career

After a period working as a music journalist in Canada, Geldof returned to Ireland in 1975 and became the lead singer of the pop-punk band the Boomtown Rats. The band found success with the number-one single "Rat Trap", the first UK number one single by an Irish band, and "I Don't Like Mondays", a UK and international hit written in response to a school shooting in San Diego.

Charity work

Disturbed by reports of famine in Ethiopia, Geldof co-wrote "Do They Know It's Christmas?" with Midge Ure and recorded it with the supergroup Band Aid in November 1984. The single became the biggest-selling in UK chart history at the time. On 13 July 1985, Geldof and Ure organised Live Aid, twin concerts held in London and Philadelphia, which raised over £150 million for famine relief. Geldof was nominated for the Nobel Peace Prize, and received an honorary knighthood in 1986. In 2005 he organised Live 8, a series of concerts coinciding with the G8 summit.

Personal life

Geldof married television presenter Paula Yates in 1986; they had three daughters before divorcing in 1996. Yates later died of a heroin overdose in 2000. Geldof subsequently married French actress Jeanne Marine in 2015.
