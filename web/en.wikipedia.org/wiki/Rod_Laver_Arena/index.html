Rod Laver Arena is the centre court of Melbourne Park and the principal venue of the Australian Open tennis tournament. Opened in 1988, when the tournament moved from Kooyong, it was one of the first major tennis stadiums with a retractable roof, allowing play to continue through Melbourne's summer heat and rain. It is named for Rod Laver, the Australian champion who twice won all four Grand Slam singles titles in a calendar year (1962 and 1969), and Laver traditionally presents — or is honoured at — the men's trophy ceremony.

History and use

The arena has staged the Australian Open finals since 1988, including the tournament's longest matches: the 2012 men's final between Novak Djokovic and Rafael Nadal, at five hours and 53 minutes the longest Grand Slam final in history, finished close to 1:40 a.m. Outside January the arena is a leading concert and indoor-sports venue, hosting basketball and netball among other events, and it formed part of Melbourne's 2006 Commonwealth Games facilities with a temporary swimming pool installed for that event.
