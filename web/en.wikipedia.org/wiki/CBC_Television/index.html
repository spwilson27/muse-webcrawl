CBC Television

Country: Canada
Owner: Canadian Broadcasting Corporation
Launched: 6 September 1952
Language: English

CBC Television (also known as CBC TV) is a Canadian English-language broadcast television network owned by the Canadian Broadcasting Corporation, the national public broadcaster. The main CBC News programme is The National, and the network's flagship entertainment programming includes dramas, comedies, sports and documentary series produced in Canada.

History

CBC Television began broadcasting on 6 September 1952 from CBFT in Montreal, followed two days later by CBLT in Toronto. It grew into the country's first national television network, connected coast-to-coast by microwave relay from 1958. The network carried Hockey Night in Canada from its earliest years until 2014, when the NHL rights passed to Rogers Media, though CBC continued to air the programme under a sub-licensing arrangement. CBC operates owned-and-operated stations in most major Canadian cities; since 2016 it has had no privately owned affiliates.

Programming

Notable CBC series include The National, The Fifth Estate, Marketplace, Schitt's Creek, Kim's Convenience, Anne with an E, Heartland, Murdoch Mysteries, Coronation Street (imported from ITV), and coverage of the Olympic Games. The network's French-language counterpart is Ici Radio-Canada Télé.
