Diego Dionisio Luna Alexander (born 29 December 1979) is a Mexican actor, director and producer. He is known for the film Y tu mamá también (2001), which launched him and childhood friend Gael García Bernal to international attention, and for playing Cassian Andor in the Star Wars film Rogue One (2016) and the Disney+ series Andor (2022–2025), for which he received a Golden Globe nomination.

Career

The son of set designer Alejandro Luna, Diego Luna began acting as a child in Mexican telenovelas and theatre. His international breakthrough came with Alfonso Cuarón's road film Y tu mamá también. He subsequently appeared in Hollywood productions including The Terminal (2004), Milk (2008) and Elysium (2013), while continuing to work in Spanish-language cinema. With García Bernal he co-founded the production company Canana Films and the Ambulante documentary festival. He played drug lord Miguel Ángel Félix Gallardo in the series Narcos: Mexico (2018–2021).

Directing

Luna has directed the feature films Abel (2010), Cesar Chávez (2014), about the farm-workers' leader, and Mr. Pig (2016), as well as documentary work.
