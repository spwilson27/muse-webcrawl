List of World Heritage Sites in Vietnam

The United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) World Heritage Sites are places of importance to cultural or natural heritage as described in the UNESCO World Heritage Convention, established in 1972. Vietnam accepted the convention on 19 October 1987. As of the mid-2020s, Vietnam has nine sites on the World Heritage List, plus a number of sites on the tentative list.

Descriptions

The Complex of Huế Monuments preserves the capital of the Nguyễn dynasty (1802–1945), including the Imperial City, royal tombs and temples along the Perfume River. Ha Long Bay, "the bay of descending dragons", contains some 1,600 limestone islands and islets in spectacular karst scenery. Hội An is an exceptionally well-preserved Southeast Asian trading port of the 15th–19th centuries. Mỹ Sơn preserves ruined Hindu temples of the Champa kingdom built between the 4th and 14th centuries. Phong Nha-Kẻ Bàng protects some of Asia's oldest karst mountains and enormous cave systems, including Sơn Đoòng, the world's largest cave passage. The Tràng An complex combines karst towers, flooded valleys and archaeological evidence of human occupation spanning 30,000 years.
