University of Nevada, Las Vegas

The University of Nevada, Las Vegas (UNLV) is a public land-grant research university in Paradise, Nevada, adjacent to the Las Vegas Strip. Founded in 1957 as a branch of the University of Nevada, it became independent in 1969 and enrolls around 30,000 students.

Academics

UNLV is known for hospitality administration through the William F. Harrah College of Hospitality, as well as law (Boyd School of Law), dental medicine, engineering and fine arts.

Athletics

The Runnin' Rebels men's basketball team won the NCAA championship in 1990. UNLV teams play in the Mountain West Conference, with football at Allegiant Stadium.
