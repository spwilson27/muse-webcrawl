Brothers of Italy (Italian: Fratelli d'Italia, FdI) is a national-conservative and right-wing populist political party in Italy, currently the country's largest party. Its leader, Giorgia Meloni, has served as Prime Minister of Italy since October 2022, heading the first government led by a party with roots in the post-fascist tradition.

History

The party was founded in December 2012 by Giorgia Meloni, Ignazio La Russa and Guido Crosetto, who split from Silvio Berlusconi's People of Freedom party. Its name derives from the opening words of the Italian national anthem. Many of its early members came from National Alliance, itself the successor of the Italian Social Movement (MSI).

After modest results in the 2013 and 2018 general elections, the party surged in opposition during the governments of Giuseppe Conte and Mario Draghi. In the 2022 general election it won about 26 per cent of the vote, becoming the largest party, and Meloni formed a coalition government with the League and Forza Italia.

Ideology

Brothers of Italy is generally described as national-conservative, sovereigntist and right-wing populist. It advocates a confederal Europe of nation states, restrictive immigration policies, support for the traditional family, and Atlanticist foreign policy, having strongly backed Ukraine following Russia's invasion in 2022. The party's symbol features the tricolour flame, inherited from the MSI.
