.308 Winchester

The .308 Winchester is a smokeless powder rimless bottlenecked rifle cartridge widely used for hunting, target shooting, military and law-enforcement applications. Introduced by Winchester in 1952, it is the commercial version of the military 7.62×51mm NATO round.

Performance

The .308 Winchester fires bullets typically of 150–180 grains at muzzle velocities around 2,600–2,800 ft/s. It is considered suitable for most big game in North America and is one of the most popular short-action rifle cartridges worldwide.

Use

Because of its accuracy and moderate recoil, the cartridge is used in precision rifle competition, police marksman roles and a vast range of hunting rifles.
