Bombardier Transportation
Founded: 1974
HQ: Berlin, Germany
Parent: Alstom (from 2021)
Products: Trains, trams, signalling

Bombardier Transportation was a Canadian-German rolling stock and rail transport manufacturer, headquartered in Berlin, and a division of Bombardier Inc. In January 2021 it was acquired by French rival Alstom for about €5.5 billion, creating one of the world's largest rail companies.

History

Bombardier entered rail in 1974, acquiring Montreal Locomotive Works and building cars for Montreal's metro. Through acquisitions — including BN Constructions, ANF, Adtranz (2001) — it became a global leader in locomotives, multiple units, trams (Flexity), metros (MOVIA), monorails, and signalling.

Products

Notable products include TRAXX locomotives, Talent and Twindexx multiple units, London Underground stock, Toronto Rocket subway cars, and propulsion for many high-speed trains. See Bombardier Inc., Rail transport.
