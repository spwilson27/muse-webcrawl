Stade Louis II

The Stade Louis II is a stadium in the Fontvieille district of Monaco. Named after Prince Louis II of Monaco, it is the home of AS Monaco FC football club and was the venue of the UEFA Super Cup from 1998 to 2012. It also hosts the Monaco Diamond League athletics meeting, the Herculis.

History

The original Stade Louis II opened in 1939. The present stadium, constructed on reclaimed land in Fontvieille, was inaugurated in 1985. It is recognisable by its nine arches at the far end of the ground. Beneath the pitch, the complex contains a multi-storey car park, an Olympic-sized swimming pool, squash and basketball facilities, and the offices of the Monegasque Olympic Committee and several sporting federations.

Events

The stadium hosted the UEFA Super Cup annually between 1998 and 2012. The Herculis international athletics meeting, part of the Diamond League, takes place there each summer; the venue's fast track has seen several world-class performances. Monaco's athletics facilities also held part of the 1987 World Master's Games and other events.
