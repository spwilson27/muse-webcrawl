Answers in Genesis

Answers in Genesis (AiG) is an American fundamentalist Christian apologetics parachurch organisation. It advocates young Earth creationism on the basis of its literal, historical-grammatical interpretation of the Book of Genesis and the Bible as a whole. The organisation is led by Ken Ham, an Australian-born former science teacher.

Activities

AiG operates the Creation Museum (opened 2007) and the Ark Encounter theme park in Williamstown, Kentucky (opened 2016), featuring a full-scale model of Noah's Ark. It publishes books, curricula, the Answers magazine, and runs a large website presenting creationist responses to evolution and an old Earth.

Views and reception

The organisation rejects evolution and holds that the universe and Earth were created about 6,000 years ago and that Noah's flood explains most of the geological record. The scientific community regards its claims as pseudoscience. In 2014 Ken Ham debated science educator Bill Nye at the Creation Museum, an event widely covered in the media.
