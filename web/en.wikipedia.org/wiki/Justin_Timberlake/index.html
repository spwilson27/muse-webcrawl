Justin Randall Timberlake (born 31 January 1981) is an American singer, songwriter, record producer and actor. Dubbed the "Prince of Pop", he is one of the best-selling music artists of all time, with estimated sales of over 117 million records worldwide.

Early career and NSYNC

Born in Memphis, Tennessee, Timberlake appeared on Star Search and Disney's The All-New Mickey Mouse Club alongside Britney Spears, Christina Aguilera and Ryan Gosling. In 1995 he co-founded the boy band NSYNC, which became one of the best-selling boy bands ever with albums such as No Strings Attached (2000), before going on hiatus in 2002.

Solo career

His debut solo album Justified (2002), made with The Neptunes and Timbaland, produced the hits "Like I Love You", "Cry Me a River" and "Rock Your Body". FutureSex/LoveSounds (2006) yielded three consecutive number-one singles — "SexyBack", "My Love" and "What Goes Around... Comes Around" — and established him as a dominant pop artist. After a musical hiatus focused on acting, he returned with The 20/20 Experience (2013) and its sequel, followed by Man of the Woods (2018) and Everything I Thought It Was (2024). He headlined the Super Bowl LII halftime show in 2018; his role in the 2004 Super Bowl XXXVIII halftime controversy with Janet Jackson remains a much-discussed episode.

Acting and other ventures

Timberlake's acting credits include Alpha Dog (2006), The Social Network (2010, as Sean Parker), Friends with Benefits (2011) and voice work as Branch in the Trolls franchise, whose 2016 song "Can't Stop the Feeling!" topped charts in 17 countries and earned an Academy Award nomination. He has won ten Grammy Awards and four Primetime Emmy Awards, including for hosting Saturday Night Live.

He married actress Jessica Biel in 2012; they have two sons. In June 2024 he was arrested for driving while intoxicated in Sag Harbor, New York, and later pleaded guilty to a reduced charge of driving while ability impaired.
