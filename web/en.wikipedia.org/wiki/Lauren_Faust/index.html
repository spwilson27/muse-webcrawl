Lauren Faust

Lauren Faust, creator of My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic

Lauren J. Faust (born July 25, 1974) is an American animator, writer, director, and producer. She is best known for developing the animated series My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic (2010) for Hasbro, which became a major cultural phenomenon with an unexpectedly large adult fanbase, and for her work on The Powerpuff Girls and Foster's Home for Imaginary Friends.

Career

Faust studied animation at the California Institute of the Arts and began her career as a layout artist on feature films including The Iron Giant (1999). She joined Cartoon Network's The Powerpuff Girls as a storyboard artist and writer, and later served as supervising producer and story editor on Foster's Home for Imaginary Friends, created by her husband, Craig McCracken. She received several Annie Award nominations and won an Emmy Award for her work on Foster's.

My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic

Hasbro hired Faust to develop a new incarnation of its My Little Pony franchise. The series premiered on The Hub in October 2010 and was praised for its characterisation, humour, and cross-generational appeal. Faust stepped down after the first season but remained involved as a consulting producer for the second. She later co-created Super Best Friends Forever for DC Nation and developed the 2019 reboot DC Super Hero Girls.
