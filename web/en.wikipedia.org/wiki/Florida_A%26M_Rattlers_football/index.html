Florida A&M Rattlers football

The Florida A&M Rattlers football team represents Florida A&M University in American football, competing in NCAA Division I Football Championship Subdivision as a member of the Southwestern Athletic Conference (SWAC).

History

The Rattlers have a storied history among historically black colleges and universities, winning multiple black college national championships, most famously the undefeated 1978 team that won the inaugural NCAA Division I-AA national championship.

Legendary coach Jake Gaither led the programme from 1945 to 1969, compiling a 204–36–4 record.

Rivalries and home ground

The team's signature rivalry is the Florida Classic against Bethune–Cookman, played annually in Orlando.

Home games are played at Bragg Memorial Stadium in Tallahassee. The Marching 101 band is closely associated with the team's game-day tradition.
