Poison Ivy (character)

Poison Ivy (Dr. Pamela Lillian Isley) is a character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, primarily in stories featuring Batman. Created by writer Robert Kanigher and artist Carmine Infantino, she first appeared in Batman #181 (June 1966). A botanist turned eco-terrorist, she uses plant toxins, pheromones and a supernatural connection to plant life — the Green — in her crusade to protect nature at any cost, making her one of Batman's most enduring adversaries and, in later stories, an antihero.

Fictional character biography

Pamela Isley was a brilliant but shy Gotham City botanist. In most versions of her origin, her mentor (often Jason Woodrue, the Floronic Man) experimented on her with plant toxins, transforming her physiology: her skin took on plant-like properties, she became immune to all poisons and diseases, and she gained the ability to control plant growth and secrete toxins and mind-influencing pheromones. Concluding that plants matter more than human life, she embarked on a career of eco-terrorism.

Characterisation

Ivy is frequently portrayed as a femme fatale who seduces and manipulates with pheromones. Since the 2010s her relationship with Harley Quinn has been depicted as a central, romantic partnership, notably in the Harley Quinn animated series. Writers have increasingly framed her as an environmental extremist antiheroine whose goals — saving the planet — are sympathetic even when her methods are murderous.

In other media

Poison Ivy was portrayed by Uma Thurman in the film Batman & Robin (1997), voiced by Diane Pershing in Batman: The Animated Series, and played by Clare Foley, Maggie Geha and Peyton List at different ages in the television series Gotham. She is a playable character in the Injustice fighting games and appears in the Batman: Arkham video game series.
