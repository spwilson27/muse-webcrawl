The Liga Portugal 2, also known as the Segunda Liga, is the second-highest division of the Portuguese football league system, administered by Liga Portugal. It sits below the Primeira Liga and above Liga 3.

History

The division was created in 1990 as the Segunda Divisão de Honra, replacing the old second tier structure. Over the years it has been known by several sponsored names, including Liga de Honra, Segunda Liga, Ledman LigaPro, and Liga Portugal 2 SABSEG. Reserve teams of top-flight clubs, such as Benfica B and Porto B, were admitted from 2012–13 and are ineligible for promotion.

Format

Eighteen clubs play each other home and away over a season. The top two teams are promoted to the Primeira Liga, with a promotion play-off involving the third-placed team in some seasons, while the lowest teams are relegated to Liga 3, with a relegation play-off for another club. Paços de Ferreira is among the most successful clubs in the division's history, having won the title several times.
