Gonad

A gonad is the organ that makes gametes. The gonads in males are the testes, which produce sperm; the gonads in females are the ovaries, which produce ova (eggs).

Gonads also function as endocrine glands: the testes secrete androgens such as testosterone, and the ovaries secrete estrogens and progesterone, which regulate sexual development and reproduction. Their activity is controlled by gonadotropins (LH and FSH) from the pituitary gland under the influence of GnRH from the hypothalamus.

Development

In human embryos, the gonads develop from the gonadal ridges on the posterior abdominal wall. The presence or absence of the SRY gene on the Y chromosome directs differentiation into testes or ovaries respectively.
