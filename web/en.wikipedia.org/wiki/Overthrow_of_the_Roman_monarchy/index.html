Overthrow of the Roman monarchy

The overthrow of the Roman monarchy was an event in ancient Rome traditionally dated to 509 BC, in which the monarchy was abolished and the Roman Republic established. According to tradition, Lucius Tarquinius Superbus, the last king, was expelled following outrage over the rape and suicide of Lucretia.

Led by Lucius Junius Brutus and Lucius Tarquinius Collatinus, who became the first consuls, Rome replaced kingship with annually elected magistrates.
