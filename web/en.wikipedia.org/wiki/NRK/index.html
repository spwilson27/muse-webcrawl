NRK

NRK (Norwegian Broadcasting Corporation; Norwegian: Norsk rikskringkasting AS) is the Norwegian government-owned radio and television public broadcasting company, and the largest media organisation in Norway. Founded in 1933, it is a member of the European Broadcasting Union.

Services

NRK operates three national television channels (NRK1, NRK2 and NRK3/NRK Super) and numerous radio channels, including NRK P1, P2 and P3, as well as the streaming service NRK TV and the children's platform NRK Super.

Funding

NRK was historically funded by a licence fee; since 2020 it has been financed through taxation, with funding set by the Norwegian parliament. It broadcasts in Norwegian Bokmål and Nynorsk and provides programming in Sámi languages.
