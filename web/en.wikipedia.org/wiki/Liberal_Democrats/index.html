Liberal Democrats

Liberal Democrats may refer to several political parties and movements around the world, most of them espousing liberalism:

United Kingdom

  • Liberal Democrats — a liberal political party in the United Kingdom, formed in 1988 by the merger of the Liberal Party and the Social Democratic Party (SDP). Often abbreviated "Lib Dems".

Other countries

  • Liberal Democratic Party — parties of this name exist in Japan (long the governing party), Russia, Australia (minor party) and elsewhere.
  • Liberal Democrats (Australia) — now the Libertarian Party, a minor Australian party founded in 2001.
  • Liberal Democrats (Belgium/Flanders) — Open Flemish Liberals and Democrats (Open Vld).
  • Liberal'nye Demokraty and various liberal-democratic parties in Europe, Africa and Asia, many of them members of Liberal International or the Alliance of Liberals and Democrats for Europe (ALDE).
