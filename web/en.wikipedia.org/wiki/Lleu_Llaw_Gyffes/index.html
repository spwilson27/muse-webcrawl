Lleu Llaw Gyffes

Welsh mythology
Mother: Arianrhod
Guardian: Gwydion

Lleu Llaw Gyffes ("the fair-haired one with the skilful hand") is a hero of Welsh mythology, protagonist of the latter half of the Fourth Branch of the Mabinogi (Math fab Mathonwy), and cognate with the Irish god Lugh and the pan-Celtic Lugus.

Story

Born to Arianrhod in a magical mishap, Lleu was raised by his uncle Gwydion, who tricked Arianrhod into naming and arming the boy despite her curses, and with Math made him a wife of flowers, Blodeuwedd. She betrayed Lleu to her lover Gronw Pebr, who speared him under the impossible conditions of his death. Lleu flew away as a wounded eagle and was found and healed by Gwydion, then killed Gronw with the same spear, through a stone.

Interpretation

Parallels in name and skill ("many-skilled" Lugh) suggest Lleu preserves an ancient Celtic deity, and place-names such as Dinas Dinlle preserve his memory. See Lugh.
