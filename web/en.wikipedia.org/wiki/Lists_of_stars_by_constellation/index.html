Lists of stars by constellation

There are 88 constellations recognised by the International Astronomical Union (IAU), whose boundaries were formally adopted in 1928. Each constellation has an associated list of its notable stars, usually organised by Bayer designation and apparent magnitude. The brightest star in each constellation is conventionally designated Alpha, following Johann Bayer's Uranometria (1603), though there are many exceptions.

Organisation of the lists

Star lists typically record the star's name and Bayer or Flamsteed designation, its right ascension and declination, apparent magnitude, spectral type, and distance. Named stars are relatively few; most catalogue entries use designations from the Henry Draper Catalogue (HD), the Hipparcos catalogue, or variable-star designations.
