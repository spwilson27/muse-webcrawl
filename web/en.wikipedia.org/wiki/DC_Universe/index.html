DC Universe

The DC Universe (DCU) is an American media franchise and shared universe based on characters from DC Comics, created by James Gunn and Peter Safran and produced by DC Studios. It is a "soft reboot" of the preceding DC Extended Universe (DCEU), retaining some cast members and story elements while discarding others.

History

After becoming co-heads of DC Studios in October 2022, Gunn and Safran announced an eight-to-ten-year plan structured in chapters, the first titled "Chapter One: Gods and Monsters". The franchise officially began with the animated Max series Creature Commandos, which premiered in December 2024. The first film, Superman, written and directed by Gunn and starring David Corenswet as Superman, was released in July 2025.

Films and series

Announced Chapter One projects include the films Supergirl: Woman of Tomorrow, The Authority, The Brave and the Bold, Swamp Thing, and Clayface, and series including Peacemaker season 2 (carried over from the DCEU), Lanterns, Waller, Paradise Lost, and Booster Gold. Consistent with Gunn's approach, actors are intended to portray the same characters across film, television, animation, and video games.

Elseworlds

DC stories told outside the DCU continuity, such as The Batman saga and the Joker films, are released under the DC Elseworlds banner and are not part of the shared universe.
