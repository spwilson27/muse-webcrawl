Sleepy Hollow is a 1999 American gothic supernatural horror film directed by Tim Burton. It is a loose adaptation of Washington Irving's 1820 short story "The Legend of Sleepy Hollow". The film stars Johnny Depp as Ichabod Crane, a New York City police constable sent to the village of Sleepy Hollow in 1799 to investigate a series of beheadings attributed to the legendary Headless Horseman, and Christina Ricci as Katrina Van Tassel.

Plot

Crane, a rationalist detective, applies forensic methods to the murders but confronts a genuinely supernatural killer: the ghost of a Hessian mercenary (Christopher Walken in flashback) whose skull was stolen from his grave. The investigation exposes a conspiracy led by Lady Van Tassel (Miranda Richardson), who controls the Horseman to gain the Van Garrett fortune. Crane returns the skull, breaking her control, and departs with Katrina.

Reception

The film was a commercial success and praised for its atmospheric Hammer-horror-inspired production design, which won the Academy Award for Best Art Direction. Depp's performance and Burton's visual style were widely noted, though some critics found the plot convoluted.
