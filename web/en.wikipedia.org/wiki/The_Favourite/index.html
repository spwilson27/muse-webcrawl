The Favourite is a 2018 period black comedy film directed by Yorgos Lanthimos and written by Deborah Davis and Tony McNamara. Set in early 18th-century Great Britain during the War of the Spanish Succession, it stars Olivia Colman as Queen Anne, Rachel Weisz as Sarah Churchill, Duchess of Marlborough, and Emma Stone as Abigail Hill.

Plot

The frail, capricious Queen Anne relies on her intimate friend Sarah Churchill, who effectively governs through her influence and supports funding the war against France. Sarah's impoverished cousin Abigail arrives seeking employment and, through flattery, wit, and ruthlessness, supplants Sarah as the queen's favourite, allying with the Tory leader Robert Harley. The rivalry descends into poisoning and blackmail; though Abigail triumphs, the final scene shows her degraded by her position, squeezing the queen's sore legs as Anne grips her hair.

Reception and awards

The film premiered at the 75th Venice International Film Festival, winning the Grand Jury Prize and the Volpi Cup for Colman. It received ten Academy Award nominations, with Colman winning Best Actress, and was widely acclaimed for its performances, anachronistic dialogue, and Robbie Ryan's fisheye-lensed cinematography. It grossed about $95 million worldwide.
