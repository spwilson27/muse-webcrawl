Christian views on marriage

Christian views on marriage regard marriage as a sacred covenant ordained by God, based on Genesis 2 and Jesus' teaching quoting "the two shall become one flesh". Traditions differ on sacramentality, divorce, and remarriage.

By tradition

The Roman Catholic Church teaches marriage between a baptized man and woman is a sacrament and indissoluble, though annulment may declare a marriage invalid from the start. Eastern Orthodoxy calls it a mystery and permits divorce and remarriage penitentially in limited cases. Most Protestant churches treat marriage as a covenant or ordinance, and many permit divorce and remarriage on biblical grounds such as adultery or desertion.

Purposes

Commonly cited purposes include companionship, procreation, mutual sanctification, and reflecting Christ's relationship with the Church (Ephesians 5).

Contemporary debates

Denominations differ sharply over same-sex marriage, interfaith marriage, and gender roles (complementarian vs egalitarian views).
