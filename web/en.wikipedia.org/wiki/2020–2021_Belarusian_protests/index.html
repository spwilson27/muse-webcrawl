2020–2021 Belarusian protests

The 2020–2021 Belarusian protests were mass demonstrations against President Alexander Lukashenko following the disputed 9 August 2020 presidential election, officially won by Lukashenko with about 80% against opposition candidate Sviatlana Tsikhanouskaya.


Date: 24 May 2020 – 2021
Location: Belarus, mainly Minsk
Causes: disputed election, authoritarianism, economic stagnation

Protests

Hundreds of thousands marched in Minsk on successive Sundays, using the historic white-red-white flag. Workers struck and women in white formed solidarity chains. Security forces detained thousands; credible reports documented beatings and torture in detention, notably at Okrestina prison.

Outcome

Tsikhanouskaya fled to Lithuania; opposition leaders were jailed or exiled. With Russian support Lukashenko retained power, and protests were suppressed by 2021 amid mass arrests, closed media and civil-society raids. Western states imposed sanctions and did not recognise the election result.
