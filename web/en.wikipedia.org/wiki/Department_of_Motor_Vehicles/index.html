Department of Motor Vehicles

A Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) is a state-level government agency in the United States that administers vehicle registration and driver licensing. The precise name and duties vary by state: some states have a separate agency for licensing, and in some states the DMV is a division of a larger department such as a Department of Transportation or Department of Public Safety. Similar agencies exist in other countries under different names.

Services

  • Issuing and renewing driver's licenses and state identification cards
  • Registering and titling motor vehicles, and issuing license plates
  • Administering written and road tests for new drivers
  • Maintaining driving records and enforcing license suspensions
  • Collecting vehicle-related taxes and fees

Cultural reputation

DMV offices are a byword in American culture for long lines and bureaucratic slowness, a reputation frequently satirized in film and television. In response, many states have moved services online, introduced appointment systems, and installed self-service kiosks.
