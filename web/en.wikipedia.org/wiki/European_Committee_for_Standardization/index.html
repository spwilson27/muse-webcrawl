The European Committee for Standardization (CEN, from French Comité Européen de Normalisation) is a public standards organisation whose mission is to foster the economy of the European Union in global trading by developing European Standards (ENs). It is one of three European Standardisation Organisations, alongside CENELEC (electrotechnical) and ETSI (telecommunications).

Membership and process

CEN brings together the national standardisation bodies of 34 countries, including all EU member states, EFTA countries, the United Kingdom, Turkey, North Macedonia and Serbia. Standards are drafted by technical committees with national delegations, then adopted by weighted national vote; members must implement each EN identically as a national standard and withdraw conflicting ones.

Role

Harmonised European standards support EU legislation under the "New Approach", giving manufacturers a presumption of conformity with essential legal requirements.
