ESPN+

Type: Sports streaming service
Launched: 12 April 2018
Owner: ESPN Inc. (The Walt Disney Company / Hearst Communications)
Area served: United States

ESPN+ (pronounced "ESPN Plus") is an American over-the-top subscription video streaming service available in the United States, owned by ESPN Inc., a joint venture between The Walt Disney Company (which owns a controlling majority stake) and Hearst Communications. Launched on April 12, 2018, the service is built on technology from Disney's BAMTech streaming platform and is also available bundled with Disney+ and Hulu.

Content

ESPN+ carries live events not shown on ESPN's linear networks: a large slate of college sports, Major League Soccer (via MLS Season Pass's predecessor arrangement), NHL games (from 2021, including out-of-market games via NHL Power Play), Bundesliga, La Liga, the FA Cup, UFC Fight Nights and pay-per-view events (ESPN+ became the exclusive U.S. home of UFC PPVs from 2019 until the Paramount deal beginning in 2026), Top Rank boxing, PGA Tour Live golf, tennis Grand Slams, and NHL, MLB, and international soccer archives. It also hosts original programming such as the documentary series Detail and Peyton's Places, the daily studio show ESPN FC, and the full 30 for 30 library.

The service is complementary to ESPN's cable channels: marquee events televised on ESPN, ESPN2, or ABC generally require a television subscription (via ESPN's authenticated streaming), though an ESPN streaming service launched in 2025 ("ESPN Unlimited" / the ESPN direct-to-consumer app) combines both, with ESPN+ content folded into it.

Subscribers

ESPN+ reached 1 million subscribers within its first five months, 6.6 million by early 2020, and about 25 million by 2022–2023, after which growth plateaued and reporting was eventually folded into Disney's broader segment results. Pricing rose progressively from $4.99/month at launch to $10.99/month and beyond.
