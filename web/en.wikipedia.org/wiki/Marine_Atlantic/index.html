Marine Atlantic Inc. is a Canadian federal Crown corporation that operates ferry services between the Island of Newfoundland and the province of Nova Scotia, a constitutionally mandated link under the Terms of Union of 1949.

Routes

The corporation runs a year-round service between North Sydney, Nova Scotia, and Port aux Basques, Newfoundland, and a seasonal service to Argentia, Newfoundland. The Port aux Basques crossing is about 177 km and takes roughly six to eight hours.

Fleet

Its vessels have included the MV Blue Puttees, MV Highlanders, MV Atlantic Vision and the MV Leif Ericson. In the 2020s the corporation moved to charter new dual-fuel RoPax ferries, beginning with the MV Ala'suinu, which entered service in 2024.

History

Marine Atlantic was created in 1986 to take over the ferry operations of Canadian National Railways in Atlantic Canada. Its head office is in St. John's, with operations based in Port aux Basques and North Sydney.
