List of Intel Pentium 4 microprocessors

The Pentium 4 is a line of single-core x86 microprocessors made by Intel, introduced in November 2000 and shipped through August 2008. All Pentium 4 processors are based on the NetBurst microarchitecture, with cores codenamed Willamette, Northwood, Prescott, and Cedar Mill. The line was succeeded by the Pentium D and, ultimately, the Core 2 family.

Cedar Mill (65 nm)

The final Pentium 4 core, Cedar Mill (6x1 and 6x3 models, 3.0–3.6 GHz, 2 MB L2), was a die shrink of the Prescott 6xx series released in January 2006, with reduced power consumption. Mobile versions (Mobile Pentium 4 and Pentium 4-M) and the later Pentium D dual-core parts are listed in separate sections of the full article.
