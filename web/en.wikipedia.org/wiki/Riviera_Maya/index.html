The Riviera Maya is a tourism and resort district in the Mexican state of Quintana Roo, running along the Caribbean coastline of the Yucatán Peninsula from Puerto Morelos in the north to the Sian Ka'an Biosphere Reserve south of Tulum. Originally called the Cancún–Tulum corridor, the region was rebranded in the late 1990s to market its distinct character of smaller resort towns, Maya archaeological sites and natural attractions. It is served principally by Cancún International Airport and Federal Highway 307.

Geography and attractions

The coast is fringed by the Mesoamerican Barrier Reef System, the second-largest barrier reef in the world, which supports snorkelling and diving tourism. Inland, the porous limestone of the Yucatán shelf carries underground rivers and flooded sinkholes known as cenotes, many open for swimming and cave diving; the Sac Actún system is among the longest underwater cave systems known. Archaeological highlights include the clifftop Maya city of Tulum, the great inland city of Cobá, and the caleta of Xel-Há, now an eco-park. The Sian Ka'an reserve, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, protects tropical forest, wetlands and reef.

History

The region formed part of the pre-Columbian Maya world; Tulum flourished in the Postclassic period and was among the first Maya sites seen by Spanish expeditions in 1518. After the Caste War of Yucatán the coast remained sparsely populated, its economy based on fishing, copra and chicle. Large-scale development began after the creation of Cancún in the 1970s and accelerated from the 1990s as all-inclusive resorts spread south from Playa del Carmen, then a small ferry port for Cozumel. The Tren Maya railway, opened in stages from 2023, added stations along the corridor.

Environment

Development pressures include strain on freshwater aquifers, sewage treatment, loss of mangrove, and periodic mass strandings of sargassum seaweed on beaches since the 2010s. Conservation programmes protect nesting sea turtles at Akumal and Xcacel, and the reef is partly within national park and biosphere reserve designations.
