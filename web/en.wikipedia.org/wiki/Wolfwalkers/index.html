Wolfwalkers

Wolfwalkers is a 2020 animated fantasy adventure film directed by Tomm Moore and Ross Stewart. Produced by the Irish studio Cartoon Saloon with Mélusine Productions, it is the third instalment in Moore's "Irish Folklore Trilogy", following The Secret of Kells (2009) and Song of the Sea (2014). The film premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival and was released on Apple TV+.

Plot

In 1650s Kilkenny, Ireland, during the Cromwellian conquest, young English apprentice hunter Robyn Goodfellowe befriends Mebh, a wild girl who is a "wolfwalker" — a person whose spirit leaves her sleeping body as a wolf. Together they seek Mebh's missing mother while Robyn's father Bill, ordered by the Lord Protector to exterminate the wolves threatening the town, is torn between duty and his daughter, who herself becomes a wolfwalker.

Reception

The film received universal critical acclaim for its hand-drawn animation and storytelling, and was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature, as well as winning numerous Annie Awards and other honours.
