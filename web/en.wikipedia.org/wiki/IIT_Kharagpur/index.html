The Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur (IIT Kharagpur or IIT KGP) is a public institute of technology and research university in Kharagpur, West Bengal, India. Founded in 1951, it was the first Indian Institute of Technology to be established and is recognized as an Institute of National Importance.

History

The institute began on the site of the Hijli Detention Camp, a former British detention centre, in a symbolic gesture linking India's freedom struggle with its technological future. Its first classes were held in the Shaheed Bhavan building in August 1951.

Campus and academics

With the largest campus of any IIT at about 2,100 acres, IIT Kharagpur hosts numerous departments and schools spanning engineering, science, management (VGSoM), law (RGSoIPL), and medicine, and enrolls over ten thousand students.
