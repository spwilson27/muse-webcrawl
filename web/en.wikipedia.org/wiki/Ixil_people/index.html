Ixil people

The Ixil are a Maya ethnic group native to the Cuchumatanes highlands of El Quiché department in Guatemala. They number roughly 100,000–140,000 and are concentrated in the "Ixil Triangle" of the municipalities of Nebaj, San Juan Cotzal, and San Gaspar Chajul, speaking the Ixil language.

History

The Ixil region, rich in jade and salt, was conquered by the Spanish in the 1530s only after stubborn resistance. During the Guatemalan Civil War (1960–1996) the Ixil area was a stronghold of the Guerrilla Army of the Poor, and the army's counterinsurgency campaigns of the early 1980s devastated Ixil villages. The UN-backed Historical Clarification Commission concluded that state forces committed acts of genocide against the Ixil Maya between 1981 and 1983.

Trials

In 2013 former head of state Efraín Ríos Montt was convicted of genocide and crimes against humanity against the Ixil — the first such conviction of a former head of state in his own country — though the verdict was annulled on procedural grounds days later.
