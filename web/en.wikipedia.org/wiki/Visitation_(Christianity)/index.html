Visitation (Christianity)

In Christianity, the Visitation is the visit of the Virgin Mary to her cousin Elizabeth, as described in the Gospel of Luke (Luke 1:39–56). Both women were pregnant: Mary with Jesus, and Elizabeth with John the Baptist.

Biblical account

After the Annunciation, Mary travelled to a town in the hill country of Judea to visit Elizabeth. At Mary's greeting, the child in Elizabeth's womb leapt, and Elizabeth, filled with the Holy Spirit, hailed Mary as "mother of my Lord". Mary responded with the hymn known as the Magnificat: "My soul magnifies the Lord…"

Feast and devotion

The Feast of the Visitation is celebrated on 31 May in the Roman Catholic Church (formerly 2 July) and in some other traditions. The episode is the second Joyful Mystery of the Rosary and a frequent subject in Christian art, often showing the embrace of the two expectant mothers.
