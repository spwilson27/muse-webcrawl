The Heckler & Koch HK417 is a 7.62×51mm NATO battle rifle and designated marksman rifle designed by Heckler & Koch. It is the larger-calibre counterpart of the 5.56mm HK416, using the same short-stroke gas piston system and AR-15-style controls.

Variants and service

The HK417 is produced in assaulter, recce and sniper configurations with different barrel lengths. It has been adopted by numerous military and police forces; in German service a variant serves as the G28 designated marksman rifle, and the US Army selected a version as the M110A1 compact semi-automatic sniper system. The civilian semi-automatic version is marketed as the MR308/MR762.
