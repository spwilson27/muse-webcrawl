Mazinger Z
Created by: Go Nagai
Manga: 1972–1974
Anime: 1972–1974 (92 episodes)
Genre: Super robot / mecha

Mazinger Z (Japanese: マジンガーZ) is a Japanese manga and anime series created by Go Nagai. It is credited with defining the super robot genre and with introducing the concept of a giant robot piloted by a human from a cockpit inside the robot itself. The story follows teenager Koji Kabuto, who pilots the mighty robot Mazinger Z — built by his grandfather from the fictional Super-Alloy Z — against the mechanical beasts of the villainous Dr. Hell.

Story

Archaeologist Dr. Hell, having discovered giant ancient robots on the island of Bardos, seeks world domination. Professor Juzo Kabuto builds Mazinger Z from Japanium-derived Super-Alloy Z to stop him; after Juzo is mortally wounded by Dr. Hell's henchwoman Baron Ashura, his grandson Koji takes the controls, aided by Sayaka Yumi in the robot Aphrodite A and later Great Mazinger.

Impact

The series was a major hit in Japan and internationally, especially in Spain and Latin America, and spawned sequels including Great Mazinger and Grendizer, as well as the 2017 film Mazinger Z: Infinity. Mazinger Z's weapons — Rocket Punch and Breast Fire — became iconic.
