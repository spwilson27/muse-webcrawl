Joe Burrow

Born: Joseph Lee Burrow, 10 December 1996, Ames, Iowa, U.S.

Position: Quarterback

Team: Cincinnati Bengals

College: Ohio State (2015–2017); LSU (2018–2019)

NFL draft: 2020, 1st overall

Joseph Lee Burrow (born December 10, 1996) is an American football quarterback for the Cincinnati Bengals of the National Football League (NFL). He played college football for the Ohio State Buckeyes and the LSU Tigers, with whom he won the Heisman Trophy and the 2020 College Football Playoff National Championship in one of the most celebrated seasons in college football history.

College career

After three seasons as a backup at Ohio State, Burrow transferred to LSU in 2018. In 2019 he set NCAA single-season records with 60 touchdown passes and 5,671 passing yards while leading the Tigers to a 15–0 record and the national title.

NFL career

Selected first overall by Cincinnati in the 2020 NFL draft, Burrow's rookie season was cut short by a serious knee injury. He returned in 2021 to lead the Bengals to Super Bowl LVI, which they lost narrowly to the Los Angeles Rams, and won the NFL Comeback Player of the Year Award. In 2023 he signed a contract extension that briefly made him the highest-paid player in NFL history, before a wrist injury ended his season. He returned in 2024 to lead the league in passing yards and touchdowns and win Comeback Player of the Year again.
