PRhyme


Artists: Royce da 5'9" and DJ Premier. Released: December 9, 2014. Label: Bad Half Entertainment.

PRhyme is the debut studio album by the American hip hop duo of the same name, consisting of rapper Royce da 5'9" and producer DJ Premier. It was released on December 9, 2014, by Bad Half Entertainment.

Background

The project was built entirely from samples of the music of composer Adrian Younge, whose retro-soul recordings Premier chopped and reworked — a constraint Premier had not previously applied across a whole album. Royce and Premier had collaborated since "Boom" (2000), and PRhyme formalized the partnership as a group.

Track listing and guests

The nine-track album features guest appearances from Killer Mike and Ab-Soul ("Downtown"), Mac Miller and Common ("Dat Sound Good"), Jay Electronica ("To Me, to You"), ScHoolboy Q ("Underground Kings"), and Slaughterhouse ("Microphone Preem"). The single "Courtesy" preceded the release.

Reception and sequel

PRhyme was praised for Premier's cohesive production and Royce's sharp, reflective writing, debuting in the top ten of the Billboard Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums chart. A sequel, PRhyme 2, again built from a single sample source — the works of producer Antman Wonder — followed in March 2018.
