Student activity

Student activities are extracurricular activities organised for students at schools, colleges and universities, outside the regular academic curriculum. They include student clubs and societies, student government, campus media, fraternities and sororities, performing arts, community service, and intramural and intercollegiate athletics.

Organisation

At many institutions, student activities are coordinated by a students' union or student affairs office, which recognises clubs, allocates funding (often from a student activity fee charged to all enrolled students), and provides facilities such as student centres. Participation is generally voluntary, in contrast to co-curricular activities that are formally linked to coursework.

Effects

Educational research associates involvement in student activities with higher retention and graduation rates, greater satisfaction with the institution, and the development of leadership and social skills — a relationship formalised in Alexander Astin's theory of student involvement.
