Nasal cavity

The nasal cavity is the large, air-filled space above and behind the nose, forming the uppermost part of the respiratory tract. It is divided into left and right halves by the nasal septum.

Structure

Each half contains three shelf-like projections, the nasal conchae (turbinates), which increase the surface area of the mucosa. The paranasal sinuses open into the cavity, and the nasolacrimal duct drains tears into it.

Function

The cavity warms, moistens and filters inspired air, and its roof contains the olfactory epithelium responsible for the sense of smell. It also contributes to the resonance of the voice.
