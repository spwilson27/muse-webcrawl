Prince Bernhard of Lippe-Biesterfeld

Born: 29 June 1911, Jena, German Empire
Died: 1 December 2004, Utrecht, Netherlands
Spouse: Juliana of the Netherlands
Issue: Beatrix, Irene, Margriet, Christina

Prince Bernhard of Lippe-Biesterfeld (29 June 1911 – 1 December 2004) was a German-born Dutch prince who was the consort of Queen Juliana of the Netherlands. Born Bernhard Leopold Frederik Everhard Julius Coert Karel Godfried Pieter of Lippe-Biesterfeld in Jena, he married Princess Juliana in 1937 and became a central figure of Dutch public life for more than six decades.

Second World War

Despite his German origins and brief membership in Nazi organisations in the 1930s, Bernhard broke with Germany during the Second World War, serving with the Dutch government-in-exile in London, flying with the Royal Air Force, and acting as commander of the Dutch armed forces at the end of the war. He was present at the German surrender negotiations at Wageningen in May 1945.

Public roles

Bernhard co-founded the Bilderberg Group in 1954 and served as the first president of the World Wildlife Fund (WWF) from 1961 to 1976, helping to found WWF Netherlands. An avid pilot and horseman, he was inspector general of the Dutch armed forces.

Lockheed scandal

In 1976, the Lockheed bribery scandals revealed that Bernhard had accepted more than one million dollars in bribes from the Lockheed Corporation. The affair seriously damaged his reputation; he resigned his military and business posts, and a criminal prosecution was avoided after Queen Juliana threatened to abdicate. He remained a popular, if controversial, figure until his death in 2004.
