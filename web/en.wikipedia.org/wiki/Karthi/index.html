Karthi

Born: Karthik Sivakumar, 25 May 1977, Madras (now Chennai), Tamil Nadu, India
Occupation: Actor
Years active: 2004–present
Spouse: Ranjani (m. 2011)

Karthik Sivakumar (born 25 May 1977), better known by his stage name Karthi, is an Indian actor who works in Tamil cinema. He has won three Filmfare Awards South, an Edison Award, a SIIMA Award and a Tamil Nadu State Film Award. The younger son of veteran actor Sivakumar and younger brother of actor Suriya, Karthi initially assisted director Mani Ratnam before making his acting debut.

Early life and education

Karthi was born in Madras (now Chennai) to actor Sivakumar and Lakshmi. He completed his schooling in Chennai and graduated in mechanical engineering from Crescent Engineering College, then earned a master's degree in industrial engineering from Binghamton University in New York. After working briefly as an engineering consultant in the United States, he returned to India and assisted Mani Ratnam on Aayutha Ezhuthu (2004), where he also made an uncredited appearance.

Career

Karthi's debut as a lead actor came with Ameer Sultan's Paruthiveeran (2007), in which his performance as a brash village youth won widespread acclaim and the Filmfare Award for Best Actor – Tamil. He consolidated his position with commercially successful films such as Aayirathil Oruvan (2010), Paiyaa (2010), Naan Mahaan Alla (2010), Siruthai (2011) and Madras (2014).

A career resurgence followed with acclaimed performances in Theeran Adhigaaram Ondru (2017), Kadaikutty Singam (2018) and Kaithi (2019), in which he played an ex-convict protecting police officers through one night. He later appeared in the historical epic Ponniyin Selvan films (2022–2023) as Vanthiyathevan, and in Sardar (2022), Japan (2023) and Meiyazhagan (2024), drawing praise for his versatility across action, rural drama and character roles.

Personal life

Karthi married Ranjani in 2011; the couple have a daughter and a son. He is active in social causes through the Makkal Nala Mandram fan welfare organisation, which he reorganised as a charitable trust.
