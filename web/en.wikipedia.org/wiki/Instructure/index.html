Instructure

Instructure, Inc.
Founded: 2008
Headquarters: Salt Lake City, Utah, United States
Products: Canvas LMS
Parent: KKR (from 2024)

Instructure, Inc. is an American educational technology company based in Salt Lake City, Utah. It is the developer of Canvas, a web-based learning management system (LMS) widely used by schools, universities and businesses, and of the Mastery assessment products.

History

The company was founded in 2008 by two graduate students at Brigham Young University, Brian Whitmer and Devlin Daley. Canvas was launched in 2011 and gained rapid adoption in higher education, overtaking established competitors at many institutions. Instructure listed on the New York Stock Exchange in 2015, was taken private by Thoma Bravo in 2020 for about $2 billion, relisted in 2021, and in 2024 agreed to be acquired by investment funds managed by KKR in a deal valuing it at about $4.8 billion.

Products

Canvas LMS provides course management, assignments, grading and mobile apps. The Canvas learning platform also includes Studio for video, Catalog for course listings, Impact for adoption analytics, and Mastery for standards-based assessment, following the acquisition of MasteryConnect.
