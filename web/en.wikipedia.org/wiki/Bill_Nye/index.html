Bill Nye

William Sanford Nye (born November 27, 1955) is an American science communicator, television presenter, and engineer. He is best known as the host of the science education television show Bill Nye the Science Guy (1993–1999) and as a science communicator in popular media.

Bill Nye
Born: 27 November 1955, Washington, D.C.
Education: Cornell University (BS, mechanical engineering)
Occupation: Science communicator, engineer, presenter
Known for: Bill Nye the Science Guy

Early life and engineering

Nye studied mechanical engineering at Cornell, where he took astronomy classes from Carl Sagan. He worked at Boeing in Seattle on aircraft systems.

Television

After comedy work on Almost Live!, his Science Guy persona led to the PBS/Disney show that won numerous Emmy Awards and made science accessible to children. Later series include Bill Nye Saves the World (Netflix) and The End Is Nye.

Advocacy

Nye advocates action on climate change and science education, debated creationist Ken Ham in 2014, served as CEO of The Planetary Society, and co-developed the LightSail solar sailing spacecraft. He has written books including Undeniable and Unstoppable.
