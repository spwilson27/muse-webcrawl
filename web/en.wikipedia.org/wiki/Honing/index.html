Honing

Honing is an abrasive machining process that produces a precision surface on metal using a rotating tool fitted with abrasive stones, most commonly to finish the interior of cylinders. A hone expands against the bore while rotating and reciprocating, creating a characteristic crosshatch pattern.

Applications

Engine cylinder bores, hydraulic cylinders, gears and gun barrels are honed to exact size, roundness and surface finish; the crosshatch retains lubricating oil. Related hand honing maintains knife and razor edges on a strop-like steel or stone, technically realigning rather than removing metal.

Process

Parameters include stone grit, pressure, speed ratio (which sets the crosshatch angle, typically around 45°), and honing oil. Plateau honing leaves a smoother bearing surface for piston rings.
