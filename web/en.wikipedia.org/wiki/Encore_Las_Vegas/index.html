Encore Las Vegas is a luxury resort and casino located on the Las Vegas Strip in Paradise, Nevada. It is owned and operated by Wynn Resorts and is the sister property to Wynn Las Vegas, to which it is connected. The resort opened on December 22, 2008.

Facilities

The 48-storey bronze-glass tower contains 2,034 all-suite accommodations, a casino of approximately 80,000 square feet, several restaurants (including Sinatra and Wing Lei), nightclubs and dayclubs (XS Nightclub and Encore Beach Club), a spa, retail space and convention facilities. Encore and its sister property have regularly received Forbes Five-Star ratings for their hotel, spa and restaurant offerings.

History

Encore was developed at a cost of about $2.3 billion on land adjacent to Wynn Las Vegas, on the former site area of the Desert Inn golf course. Steve Wynn positioned it as a more intimate, European-inspired companion to the original resort. A second Encore property, Encore Boston Harbor, opened in Massachusetts in 2019.
