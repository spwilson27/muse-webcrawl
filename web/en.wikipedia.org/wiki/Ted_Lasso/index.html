Ted Lasso is an American sports comedy-drama television series developed by Jason Sudeikis, Bill Lawrence, Brendan Hunt, and Joe Kelly. It is based on a character Sudeikis first portrayed in a series of promotional shorts for NBC Sports' coverage of the Premier League. The series follows Ted Lasso, an American college football coach who is unexpectedly recruited to coach the fictional English Premier League football club AFC Richmond, despite having no experience of association football. The club's owner, Rebecca Welton, hires him secretly hoping he will fail and ruin the team, an act of revenge against her ex-husband, but his folksy optimism gradually wins over the club and its supporters.

Premise

Lasso's coaching philosophy centres on kindness, empathy, and believing in people over tactics — summed up in the handwritten "BELIEVE" sign he tapes above the dressing-room door. Over three seasons the series explores the mental health of players and staff, the redemption of antagonistic characters such as striker Jamie Tartt and kit-man-turned-coach Nate Shelley, whose betrayal and defection to West Ham United drives the third season, and Richmond's sporting arc from relegation to a Premier League title challenge. The series concluded with its third season in May 2023, although a fourth season was later announced to be in development.

Reception

The series was widely praised for its performances, writing, and optimistic tone, particularly during the COVID-19 pandemic. Its first season was nominated for 20 Primetime Emmy Awards — the most nominations for a first-season comedy — and won Outstanding Comedy Series, with acting awards for Sudeikis, Waddingham, and Goldstein. The second season also won Outstanding Comedy Series. The third season received a more mixed critical response but remained popular with audiences. Filming took place in London, with Richmond's Nelson Road stadium portrayed by Selhurst Park, the home of Crystal Palace.
