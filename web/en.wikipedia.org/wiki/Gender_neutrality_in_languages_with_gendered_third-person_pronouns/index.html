Gender neutrality in languages with gendered third-person pronouns

Gender neutrality in languages with gendered third-person pronouns concerns how speakers of languages such as English, German, French or the Scandinavian languages refer to people without specifying gender, where the traditional pronoun system distinguishes masculine and feminine.

Strategies

Approaches include the singular they in English; newly coined pronouns such as Swedish hen (officially recognised in 2015), German proposals, and French iel; and restructuring sentences to avoid pronouns. These developments form part of wider gender-neutral language reform.
