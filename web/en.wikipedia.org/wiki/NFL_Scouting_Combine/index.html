NFL Scouting Combine

The NFL Scouting Combine is a week-long showcase, held annually in late February or early March, at which college football players perform physical and mental tests in front of National Football League coaches, general managers, and scouts. Since 1987 it has been held in Indianapolis, Indiana, most recently at Lucas Oil Stadium.

History

The combine grew out of separate scouting camps run by organisations such as National Football Scouting, BLESTO and Quadra Scouting. In 1985 the camps were merged into a single event in New Orleans, and the event settled in Indianapolis in 1987, where it has remained ever since.

Tests and drills

Athletes perform a standard battery of drills: the 40-yard dash, bench press (225-pound repetitions), vertical jump, broad jump, three-cone drill, and the 20-yard and 60-yard shuttle runs. Players also undergo medical examinations, interviews with teams, and cognitive testing.

Participation

Attendance is by invitation only, with roughly 330 of the top draft-eligible college players invited each year. Performance at the combine can significantly raise or lower a player's draft stock.
