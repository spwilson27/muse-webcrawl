List of volcanoes in Russia

Most of Russia's volcanoes are concentrated on the Kamchatka Peninsula in the Russian Far East, one of the most volcanically active regions on Earth, and in the Kuril Islands chain extending toward Japan. The Volcanoes of Kamchatka are a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Russia has around 40 volcanoes that have erupted in historical times.

Kuril Islands

  • Sarychev Peak (Matua Island) — dramatic 2009 eruption photographed from the ISS.
  • Alaid (Atlasov Island) — highest Kuril volcano, 2,285 m.
  • Ebeko (Paramushir Island) — frequently active near Severo-Kurilsk.
  • Chikurachki — highest volcano on Paramushir.

Elsewhere

Older, extinct volcanic centers exist in the Caucasus (Mount Elbrus and Mount Kazbek, both dormant stratovolcanoes), Siberia, and the Baikal region.
