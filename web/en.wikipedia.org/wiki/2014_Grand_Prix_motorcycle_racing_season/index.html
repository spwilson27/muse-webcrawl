2014 Grand Prix motorcycle racing season

2014 FIM Road Racing World Championship
Races: 18
MotoGP champion: Marc Márquez (Honda)
Moto2 champion: Esteve Rabat (Kalex)
Moto3 champion: Álex Márquez (Honda)

The 2014 Grand Prix motorcycle racing season was the 66th F.I.M. Road Racing World Championship season. It comprised 18 Grands Prix across the MotoGP, Moto2 and Moto3 classes, beginning in Qatar in March and ending in Valencia in November.

MotoGP

Defending champion Marc Márquez of Repsol Honda dominated the season, winning the first ten races consecutively and ultimately 13 of 18 rounds — a premier-class record — to retain the world championship with 362 points. Valentino Rossi finished second for Yamaha ahead of his teammate Jorge Lorenzo. New "Open class" regulations replaced the previous CRT category, and the Argentine Grand Prix returned to the calendar at Termas de Río Hondo.

Moto2 and Moto3

In Moto2, Esteve "Tito" Rabat won the championship with seven victories. In Moto3, Álex Márquez — younger brother of Marc — secured the title at the final round in Valencia, making the Márquez brothers the first siblings to win Grand Prix world titles in the same season.
