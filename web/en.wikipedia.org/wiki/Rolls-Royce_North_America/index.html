Rolls-Royce North America

Rolls-Royce North America Inc. is a subsidiary of the British engineering company Rolls-Royce plc, headquartered in Reston, Virginia. It oversees the group's operations in the United States and Canada, which employ several thousand people across aerospace, defence, marine, and energy businesses.

History

Rolls-Royce's North American presence expanded greatly with the 1995 acquisition of the Allison Engine Company of Indianapolis, Indiana, a historic manufacturer of aircraft engines founded in 1915. The Indianapolis site remains the company's largest US manufacturing and engineering centre, producing engines such as the AE 3007 and AE 2100 families and supporting US military programmes including the V-22 Osprey's AE 1107C engine.

Operations

The subsidiary supplies gas turbines and services to the US Air Force, Navy, and Army, builds marine propulsion systems, and conducts research in advanced materials and hybrid-electric propulsion. Its government business is structured to comply with US security requirements for foreign-owned defence contractors.
