Methacrylate

Methacrylates are derivatives of methacrylic acid, most commonly the esters of methacrylic acid. The parent species, methyl methacrylate (MMA), is a major industrial chemical used in the production of poly(methyl methacrylate) (PMMA), the transparent plastic sold under trade names such as Plexiglas, Perspex and Lucite.

Chemistry

Methacrylic acid has the formula CH₂=C(CH₃)CO₂H; its esters combine a reactive vinyl group with an ester group, allowing them to polymerise readily by free-radical polymerisation. The α-methyl group distinguishes methacrylates from acrylates and gives their polymers higher glass-transition temperatures and greater rigidity.

Uses

Beyond PMMA sheet and moulding resins, methacrylate monomers and polymers are used in paints and coatings, adhesives, dental materials, bone cement, contact lenses and nail products. Hydroxyethyl methacrylate (HEMA) is a key monomer for soft contact lenses.
