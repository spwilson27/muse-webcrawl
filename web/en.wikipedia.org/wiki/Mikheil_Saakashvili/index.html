Mikheil Saakashvili (born 21 December 1967) is a Georgian and formerly Ukrainian politician. A US-educated lawyer, he led Georgia's peaceful Rose Revolution of 2003 and served two terms as President of Georgia (2004–2013), pursuing radical anti-corruption, market and pro-Western reforms, a course that culminated in the 2008 Russo-Georgian War. After leaving office he moved to Ukraine, served as governor of Odesa Oblast, fell out with Kyiv, and was stripped of Ukrainian citizenship; he returned to Georgia in 2021 and was imprisoned there.

Rise and presidency

Saakashvili studied law in Kyiv and at Columbia University, worked for a New York law firm, and returned to Georgia in 1995, entering parliament under President Eduard Shevardnadze before becoming minister of justice. Resigning in protest at corruption, he founded the United National Movement and, after disputed elections in November 2003, led the Rose Revolution that forced Shevardnadze's resignation. Elected president with overwhelming support in January 2004, he rebuilt the police and military, slashed bureaucracy, attracted foreign investment and sought NATO and EU integration.

Russo-Georgian War and later Georgia years

Tensions over the separatist regions of South Ossetia and Abkhazia erupted in August 2008 into a five-day war with Russia, after which Moscow recognised both regions as independent. Saakashvili's popularity declined amid accusations of authoritarian tendencies; his party lost the 2012 parliamentary elections, and he left Georgia at the end of his term in 2013. Georgian courts later convicted him in absentia of abuse of power.

Ukraine and return

Granted Ukrainian citizenship, Saakashvili was appointed governor of Odesa Oblast in 2015 by President Petro Poroshenko, resigning in 2016 amid feuds over reform. He was stripped of citizenship in 2017, dramatically re-entered Ukraine, was deported in 2018, and returned after Volodymyr Zelensky restored his citizenship in 2019. In October 2021 he secretly returned to Georgia, was arrested, and has since been imprisoned amid contested health and legal disputes.
