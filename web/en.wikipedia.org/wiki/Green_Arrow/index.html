Green Arrow

Green Arrow is a superhero who appears in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by Mort Weisinger and designed by George Papp, he first appeared in More Fun Comics No. 73 in November 1941. His real name is Oliver Jonas Queen, a wealthy businessman, former mayor of Star City, and vigilante.

Green Arrow is an archer who invents trick arrows with various special functions, such as glue, explosive, time bomb, fire extinguisher, flash, tear gas, cryonic, boomerang and boxing-glove arrows. Originally the character was modeled on Robin Hood and functioned as a "backup" feature; in his classic adventures he was assisted by his sidekick Speedy (Roy Harper) and used an Arrowcar and Arrowcave much in the manner of Batman's equipment.

Reinvention

In the late 1960s, writer Denny O'Neil and artist Neal Adams radically reinvented the character, stripping him of his fortune and teaming him with Green Lantern in the acclaimed Green Lantern/Green Arrow series (1970–1972), which addressed racism, overpopulation and drug abuse — most famously in the story in which Speedy is revealed to be addicted to heroin. Green Arrow became a vocal left-leaning crusader for social justice and one of DC's most politically outspoken heroes. Mike Grell's Longbow Hunters (1987) and subsequent ongoing series made him a grittier urban hunter.

In other media

The character was prominently adapted for television in Smallville (played by Justin Hartley) and in the CW series Arrow (2012–2020), played by Stephen Amell, which launched the shared "Arrowverse" of DC television series.
