List of fried rice dishes

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Fried rice
OriginChina (Sui dynasty era)
Main ingredientCooked rice stir-fried in a wok

Fried rice is a dish of cooked rice that has been stir-fried in a wok or frying pan and is usually mixed with other ingredients such as eggs, vegetables, seafood, or meat. Originating in China, it has spread throughout Asia and beyond, with countless regional varieties.

Asian varieties

DishRegionDescription
Yangzhou fried riceChina (Jiangsu)The classic Cantonese-style standard: rice with char siu pork, shrimp, egg, scallions and peas.
Fujian fried riceChina (Fujian/Hong Kong)Fried rice topped with a thick gravy of seafood, chicken and mushrooms.
Hokkien fried riceChina/TaiwanMustard greens, pork and dried shrimp feature prominently.
Nasi gorengIndonesia / MalaysiaSweetened with kecap manis; served with fried egg, prawns and krupuk crackers.
Nasi goreng pattayaMalaysiaFried rice wrapped in a thin omelette.
Khao padThailandThai fried rice with jasmine rice, fish sauce, lime and cucumber.
Bokkeum-bapKoreaKorean fried rice; kimchi bokkeum-bap (with kimchi) is the most famous version.
Chāhan (yakimeshi)JapanJapanese fried rice derived from Chinese cooking, typically with egg, negi and pork.
SinangagPhilippinesGarlic fried rice, a breakfast staple served with eggs and cured meats (silog meals).
Cơm chiênVietnamVietnamese fried rice, often with Chinese sausage (lạp xưởng).
Biryani-adjacent fried ricesSouth AsiaSpiced rice dishes; note biryani itself is layered and steamed rather than wok-fried.

Other varieties

  • Arroz chaufa — Peruvian-Chinese (chifa) fried rice with soy sauce, scallions and meats.
  • Arroz frito — Cuban and Spanish adaptations of Chinese fried rice.
  • Hawaiian fried rice — with Spam, Portuguese sausage and pineapple in some versions.
  • Pineapple fried rice — Thai-style rice served in a hollowed pineapple, with cashews and raisins.
  • Special fried rice — a British-Chinese takeaway staple with mixed meats and egg.
