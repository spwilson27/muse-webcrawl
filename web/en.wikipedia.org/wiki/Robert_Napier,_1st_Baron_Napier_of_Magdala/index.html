Robert Cornelis Napier, 1st Baron Napier of Magdala GCB GCSI (6 December 1810 – 14 January 1890) was a British Indian Army officer. An engineer by training, he served in the Anglo-Sikh Wars, helped design and build the Ganges Canal, defended Lucknow during the Indian Mutiny of 1857, and commanded the Second China War expedition of 1860 alongside General Hope Grant.

Expedition to Abyssinia

Napier is best known for the 1868 Expedition to Abyssinia, a vast logistical operation to rescue hostages held by Emperor Tewodros II. Landing at Zula and marching 400 miles through mountainous country with 13,000 soldiers and some 40,000 followers and animals, his force stormed the fortress of Magdala; Tewodros took his own life and the hostages were freed. Napier was raised to the peerage as Baron Napier of Magdala. He later served as Commander-in-Chief in India (1870–1876) and Governor of Gibraltar.
