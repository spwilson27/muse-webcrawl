Damn Yankees

Damn Yankees is a 1955 musical comedy with book by George Abbott and Douglass Wallop, music and lyrics by Richard Adler and Jerry Ross, retelling the Faust legend in 1950s baseball.


Premiere: 5 May 1955, Broadway
Music: Adler and Ross

Plot

Senators fan Joe Boyd sells his soul to Mr. Applegate to become young slugger Joe Hardy and beat the New York Yankees, aided and tempted by Lola, before outwitting the devil via an escape clause.

Productions

The original ran 1,019 performances and won seven Tonys. Gwen Verdon starred as Lola, choreographed by Bob Fosse. Songs include "(You Gotta Have) Heart" and "Whatever Lola Wants". A film followed in 1958 and a revival in 1994.
