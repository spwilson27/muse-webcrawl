Type rating

A type rating is a certification granted by an aviation regulator that permits a licensed pilot to fly a specific make and model of aircraft. Regulators such as the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) and the European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA) require type ratings for large aircraft, turbojet-powered aeroplanes and certain other categories.

Training

Obtaining a type rating involves ground school on the aircraft's systems, full flight simulator sessions, and a checkride with an examiner. Some aircraft families share a common type rating — for example the Airbus A320 family — allowing pilots to fly several variants after differences training. Airlines typically sponsor type ratings for new hires, sometimes with training bonds.
