Handheld projector

A handheld projector (also known as a pocket projector, mobile projector, pico projector or mini beamer) is an image projector in a handheld device. It was developed as a computer display device for compact portable devices such as mobile phones, personal digital assistants, and digital cameras.

These projectors use laser or LED light sources and technologies such as DLP, LCoS, or laser beam steering to project images many times larger than the device itself, though brightness and resolution are limited compared with full-size projectors.

History

Pico projectors emerged in the mid-2000s as standalone accessories and as modules embedded in mobile phones. Improvements in LED efficiency and battery capacity made brighter, higher-resolution devices practical in the 2010s.

Applications

Uses include impromptu business presentations, sharing photos and video, gaming, and wearable displays. Built-in projectors allow a small screen image to be projected onto a wall for group viewing.
