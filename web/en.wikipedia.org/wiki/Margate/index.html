Margate

Margate is a seaside town in the district of Thanet in Kent, England. It lies on the north-eastern coast of Kent, on the North Sea, about 15 miles (24 km) east-north-east of Canterbury. Historically a leading English seaside resort for more than 250 years, the town includes the districts of Cliftonville, Garlinge, Palm Bay and Westbrook. Its population was around 61,000 at the 2011 census.

History

Margate's name derives from "Meregate", a gate or gap in the cliffs where a pool or mere stood. The town was recorded in 1264 and developed as a limb of the Cinque Ports through nearby Dover. In the eighteenth century Margate became one of England's first fashionable sea-bathing resorts; bathing machines were in use by the 1730s–1750s, and visitors arrived from London by sailing hoy and later by steamboat and railway.

The railway reached Margate in 1846, and the Victorian era brought grand hotels, a pier, and winter gardens. The artist J. M. W. Turner boarded at a house on Marine Terrace in the 1830s and declared the Thanet skies "the loveliest in all Europe".

Like many British resorts, Margate declined in the later twentieth century as foreign package holidays grew. The Dreamland amusement park, famous for its Grade II* listed Scenic Railway rollercoaster (1920), closed in 2003 but reopened in 2015 after a long campaign.

Renaissance

The opening of the Turner Contemporary gallery on the seafront in April 2011 is widely credited with spurring a cultural regeneration of the town, attracting artists, independent shops, and visitors; Tracey Emin, who grew up in Margate, has strong connections with the town. The Old Town area near the harbour is now known for galleries, vintage shops, and cafés.

Attractions

Attractions include Margate Main Sands, the Harbour Arm, the Shell Grotto (a subterranean passageway covered in shell mosaics, discovered in 1835), the Margate Caves, Tudor House, and the Theatre Royal (1787), one of the oldest theatres in England. The Winter Gardens (1911) is a major entertainment venue.

Transport

Margate railway station offers services to London St Pancras, Victoria and Charing Cross via the Kent coast line. The town is served by the A28 and A299 Thanet Way roads.
