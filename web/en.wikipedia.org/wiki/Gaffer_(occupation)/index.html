Gaffer (occupation)

Occupation typeFilm and television crew
DepartmentElectrical / lighting
Works withCinematographer; grip department

A gaffer is the head electrician on a film or television production, responsible for the design, layout, and execution of the lighting plan under the direction of the director of photography. Gaffers oversee the electrical department, including the best boy electric and set lighting technicians.

Role

Working from the cinematographer's intent, the gaffer decides which lamps, positions, gels, and diffusion to use, manages power distribution and rigging safety, and shapes light throughout the shoot. The gaffer collaborates closely with the key grip, whose department handles the non-electrical lighting modifiers such as flags, nets, and reflectors.

Terminology

The term is British in origin, from slang for a foreman or old man; in British film units "gaffer" survives as the standard title, while in theatre the equivalent role is usually the chief electrician or master electrician.
