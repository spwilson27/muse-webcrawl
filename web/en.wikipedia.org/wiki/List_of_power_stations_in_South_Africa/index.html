This is a list of power stations in South Africa. Most electricity is generated by the state utility Eskom, whose fleet is dominated by large coal-fired stations in Mpumalanga, close to the country's coalfields. South Africa also operates Africa's only nuclear power station, Koeberg near Cape Town, the Gariep and Vanderkloof hydroelectric schemes on the Orange River, and pumped-storage schemes such as Ingula. Persistent breakdowns at ageing coal plants have caused widespread load-shedding since 2007.
