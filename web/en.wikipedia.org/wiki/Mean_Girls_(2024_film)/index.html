Mean Girls is a 2024 American teen musical comedy film directed by Samantha Jayne and Arturo Perez Jr. from a screenplay by Tina Fey. It is a film adaptation of the Broadway musical of the same name, itself based on Fey's 2004 film Mean Girls and Rosalind Wiseman's 2002 book Queen Bees and Wannabes.

Plot

Sixteen-year-old Cady Heron, raised in Kenya by her research-scientist mother, returns to the United States and enrolls at North Shore High School. Befriended by outsiders Janis 'Imi'ike and Damian Hubbard, she is also invited to join "the Plastics"—queen bee Regina George and her followers Gretchen Wieners and Karen Shetty. At Janis's urging, Cady infiltrates the clique to sabotage Regina, but along the way she falls for Regina's ex-boyfriend Aaron Samuels, betrays her real friends, and becomes a "Plastic" herself. After the infamous Burn Book is made public and chaos erupts, Cady takes responsibility, makes amends, and is crowned Spring Fling Queen, breaking the crown and sharing it.

Production and release

The film was originally developed for streaming on Paramount+ before being upgraded to a theatrical release. Its marketing initially concealed its nature as a musical, billing it as "a new twist from Tina Fey." It opened at number one at the U.S. box office on Martin Luther King Jr. weekend 2024 and grossed about $105 million worldwide. Reviews were mixed, with praise for Rapp's performance and criticism that the film hewed closely to its predecessors.
