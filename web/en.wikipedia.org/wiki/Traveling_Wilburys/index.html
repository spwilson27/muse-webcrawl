Traveling Wilburys

Origin: Los Angeles, California, US

Genres: Rock, folk rock, country rock

Years active: 1988–1991

Members: George Harrison, Bob Dylan, Tom Petty, Jeff Lynne, Roy Orbison

The Traveling Wilburys were a British-American supergroup consisting of Bob Dylan, George Harrison, Jeff Lynne, Roy Orbison and Tom Petty. Originating from an idea discussed by Harrison and Lynne during the sessions for Harrison's comeback album Cloud Nine, the band formed in April 1988 after the five musicians united in Santa Monica, California, to record a bonus track for Harrison's next European single.

History

The song recorded for the single, "Handle with Care", was judged too good to be used merely as a B-side, and the group reconvened to record a full album. Traveling Wilburys Vol. 1, released in October 1988, was a commercial and critical success, winning the Grammy Award for Best Rock Performance by a Duo or Group with Vocal. The members adopted tongue-in-cheek pseudonyms as the fictional Wilbury brothers.

Roy Orbison died of a heart attack in December 1988, shortly after the first album's release. The remaining four continued as a quartet and released a second album, deliberately misnumbered Traveling Wilburys Vol. 3, in October 1990. The group never toured and gradually wound down by 1991, though their catalogue was successfully reissued in 2007 as The Traveling Wilburys Collection, which reached number one in several countries.

Discography

Traveling Wilburys Vol. 1 (1988); Traveling Wilburys Vol. 3 (1990). Well-known songs include "Handle with Care", "End of the Line", "Not Alone Any More", "Tweeter and the Monkey Man" and "Wilbury Twist".
