As-Suwayda Governorate

Country: Syria
Capital: As-Suwayda
Area: 5,550 km²
Region: Jabal al-Druze / Hauran

The basalt plateau of Jabal al-Druze

As-Suwayda Governorate (Arabic: السويداء) is one of the fourteen governorates of Syria, located in the far south of the country, bordering Jordan. Its capital is the city of As-Suwayda. The governorate covers about 5,550 km² and encompasses most of the Jabal al-Druze, a rugged volcanic plateau also known as Jabal al-Arab.

Demographics

The population is overwhelmingly Druze, making the governorate the heartland of Syria's Druze community, with Christian and Sunni Muslim minorities. The region has a long tradition of local autonomy and was the centre of the Great Syrian Revolt of 1925–27 led by Sultan al-Atrash.

Districts

The governorate is divided into three districts (manatiq): As-Suwayda, Salkhad and Shahba. Its towns include Shahba (ancient Philippopolis, birthplace of the Roman emperor Philip the Arab) and Qanawat, noted for Roman ruins. During the Syrian civil war, the governorate remained largely under government control while local Druze militias guarded its neutrality; from 2023 it saw sustained anti-government protests, and after the fall of the Assad regime in December 2024 its status became a focus of negotiation with Syria's new authorities.
