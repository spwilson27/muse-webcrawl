List of IEEE awards

The Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) confers a wide range of awards recognising achievement in engineering and related fields. Its highest honour is the IEEE Medal of Honor. IEEE-level awards are distinct from the honours of its technical societies. This list covers the principal corporate awards.
