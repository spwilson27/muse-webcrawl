The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 1

The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 1 is a 2014 American dystopian action film directed by Francis Lawrence, the third instalment in The Hunger Games film series and the first half of the adaptation of Suzanne Collins's novel Mockingjay.

DirectorFrancis Lawrence
Based onMockingjay by Suzanne Collins
StarringJennifer Lawrence, Josh Hutcherson, Liam Hemsworth, Julianne Moore, Philip Seymour Hoffman
Release date21 November 2014

Plot

After the destruction of District 12, Katniss Everdeen shelters in District 13 and reluctantly becomes the "Mockingjay", the propaganda symbol of the rebellion against President Snow's Capitol, while Peeta is held captive and used against her.

Reception

The film grossed over $755 million worldwide. Critics praised the performances and political themes but criticised the decision to split the novel into two films, leaving Part 1 feeling like a prelude.
