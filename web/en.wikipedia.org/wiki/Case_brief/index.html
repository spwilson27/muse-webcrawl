Case brief

A case brief is a short written summary of a judicial opinion, prepared as a study and reference aid, especially by law students learning to read cases. It distils a court decision, often many pages long, into its essential components.

Structure

A typical brief contains the case citation, the material facts, the procedural history, the issue presented, the holding, the court's reasoning (ratio decidendi), the judgement or disposition, and any concurring or dissenting opinions. Commercial case briefs are published for standard law-school casebooks, though students are usually encouraged to brief cases themselves as a learning exercise.
