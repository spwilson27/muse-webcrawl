Died11 July 1994, Monterey, California, U.S.

Gary Arlen Kildall (19 May 1942 – 11 July 1994) was an American computer scientist and microcomputer entrepreneur who created the CP/M operating system and founded Digital Research, Inc. (DRI). During the late 1970s and early 1980s CP/M was the dominant operating system for microcomputers, and Kildall is regarded as one of the pioneers of the personal computer revolution.

Early life and education

Kildall was born in Seattle, where his family ran a navigation school. He studied at the University of Washington, earning a doctorate in computer science in 1972 with research on code optimisation and data-flow analysis. He then taught at the Naval Postgraduate School in Monterey, California.

CP/M and Digital Research

Working with Intel's new microprocessors, Kildall wrote the PL/M programming language and, in 1974, created CP/M (Control Program for Microcomputers), the first commercial operating system for microcomputers, which introduced the influential BIOS concept separating hardware-dependent code from the rest of the system. He and his wife Dorothy McEwen founded Digital Research in 1976 to market it. CP/M was adopted by dozens of manufacturers and accumulated a large software library.

The IBM episode and later work

In 1980 IBM approached Digital Research about licensing CP/M for its forthcoming personal computer. The negotiations famously failed — popular legend blames Kildall's absence to fly his private plane, though accounts differ — and IBM instead adopted Microsoft's 86-DOS, marketed as PC DOS, which displaced CP/M on the IBM PC. Digital Research later produced DR-DOS, the GEM graphical environment, and the multi-user MP/M. DRI was sold to Novell in 1991.

Death and recognition

Kildall died in Monterey on 11 July 1994, aged 52, from injuries sustained days earlier. In 1995 the Software Publishers Association recognised his contributions, and a plaque in Pacific Grove, California, marks Digital Research's former headquarters as the site where CP/M was developed.
