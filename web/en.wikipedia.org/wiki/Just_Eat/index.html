Just Eat

Company information
Type: Subsidiary
Industry: Online food ordering
Founded: 2001, Kolding, Denmark
Parent: Just Eat Takeaway.com

Just Eat is an online food order and delivery platform. Founded in Denmark in 2001, it expanded first to the United Kingdom in 2006, which became its largest market, and then to many other countries, connecting customers with local takeaway restaurants through its website and apps.

History

The company listed on the London Stock Exchange in 2014 and grew through acquisitions of rivals and national operators. In 2020 Just Eat merged with the Dutch company Takeaway.com to form Just Eat Takeaway.com, one of the world's largest food-delivery groups. The Just Eat brand continues to operate in the United Kingdom, Ireland, Denmark and several other markets, known in Britain for advertising campaigns featuring the slogan "Did somebody say Just Eat".

Business model

Just Eat historically operated mainly as a marketplace, taking commission on orders while restaurants delivered the food themselves; it later built its own courier networks in several markets to compete with delivery-first rivals.
