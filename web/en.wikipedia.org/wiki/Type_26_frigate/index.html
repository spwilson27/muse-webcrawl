Type 26 frigate

The Type 26 frigate, also known as the City class, is a class of frigates being built for the United Kingdom's Royal Navy by BAE Systems, with variants selected by Australia (the Hunter class) and Canada (the River-class destroyer). Designed primarily for anti-submarine warfare, the ships displace around 6,900 tonnes and are about 150 metres long.

Design

The class features an acoustically quiet hull, a mission bay for boats and containers, a flight deck for a Wildcat or Merlin helicopter, Sea Ceptor air-defence missiles, and a Mk 41 vertical launching system for land-attack weapons. The eight Royal Navy ships are named after cities, beginning with HMS Glasgow, and are being built on the Clyde to replace the Type 23 frigates.
