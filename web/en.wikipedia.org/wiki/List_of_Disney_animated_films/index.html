List of Disney animated films

This is a list of animated films produced by Walt Disney Animation Studios, the flagship animation studio of the Walt Disney Company, forming the official "Disney Animated Canon". The studio has produced more than 60 feature films beginning with Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937).

Other Disney animation

Separate lists cover Pixar films (beginning with Toy Story, 1995), direct-to-video sequels from Disneytoon Studios, and films of Disney Television Animation. Disney-distributed works by Studio Ghibli and other studios are not part of the canon.
