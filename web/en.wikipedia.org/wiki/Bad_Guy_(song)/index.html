"Bad Guy" is a song by American singer Billie Eilish and the fifth single from her debut studio album, When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go? (2019). Eilish and her brother Finneas O'Connell co-wrote the song, with the latter producing it and Eilish providing additional production.

Composition

The song is an electropop and dance-pop track built around a minimalist production featuring a deep bass line, a snapped beat, and Eilish's deadpan, half-whispered vocals. Lyrically, Eilish teases a lover about his tough-guy posturing while suggesting she is the "bad guy" of the relationship. The track shifts tempo near the end into a darker trap breakdown.

Commercial performance

"Bad Guy" topped the charts in more than 20 countries, including Australia, Canada, and New Zealand. In the United States it reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100 in August 2019, dethroning Lil Nas X's "Old Town Road" after its record 19-week run, and making Eilish the first artist born in the 2000s to top the chart.

Accolades

At the 62nd Annual Grammy Awards in 2020, "Bad Guy" won Record of the Year and Song of the Year. A remix featuring Justin Bieber was released in July 2019.
