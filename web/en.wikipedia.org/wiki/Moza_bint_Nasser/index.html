Overview

Moza bint Nasser Al-Missned (born 8 August 1959) is a Qatari royal, the consort of Sheikh Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani, the former Emir of Qatar, and the mother of the current Emir, Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani. Unlike many royal consorts in the region she has maintained a highly visible public role, and she is regarded as one of the most influential women in the Middle East, known internationally for her work in education and social development — and for her distinctive, much-photographed style.

Work

Sheikha Moza is chairperson of the Qatar Foundation for Education, Science and Community Development, which she co-founded in 1995 and which built Education City in Doha, hosting branch campuses of universities including Georgetown, Carnegie Mellon and Weill Cornell. In 2003 UNESCO appointed her a Special Envoy for Basic and Higher Education. She founded the World Innovation Summit for Education (WISE) and the Education Above All foundation, whose Educate A Child programme has aimed to enrol millions of out-of-school children. She also created the Silatech initiative to promote youth employment in the Arab world, and served as vice chair of Qatar's Supreme Council of Health and of the Supreme Education Council.

She holds a degree in sociology from Qatar University (1986) and honorary doctorates from several universities. Her son Tamim's accession in 2013, when Sheikh Hamad abdicated, made her one of the few women in the Gulf to be the mother of a reigning monarch.
