Crescent

A crescent is a shape symbolising the lunar phase in the first quarter (a "sickle moon") or by extension any curved shape reminiscent of it. The crescent is one of the oldest symbols in human history and has been used in heraldry, religion, and national flags.

Astronomy

A crescent moon occurs when the Moon is positioned so that less than half of its visible disc is illuminated by the Sun, shortly after new moon (waxing crescent) or shortly before it (waning crescent). Venus and Mercury also show crescent phases through a telescope.

Symbolism

The crescent and star became strongly associated with the Ottoman Empire and, by extension, with Islam, appearing on the flags of countries including Turkey, Algeria, Pakistan, and Malaysia. Crescent forms also appear in earlier Byzantine, Sasanian, and ancient Near Eastern iconography.

Other uses

The term describes many crescent-shaped things: the French croissant pastry (from the French word for crescent), fertile regions such as the Fertile Crescent of the Near East, and curved geographical features and streets.
