Steamboat Willie is a 1928 American animated short film directed by Walt Disney and Ub Iwerks, produced by Walt Disney Studio and released by Celebrity Productions. It stars Mickey Mouse — in his public debut — along with Minnie Mouse and the gruff riverboat captain Pete. The cartoon is celebrated as the first fully synchronized sound cartoon, its soundtrack carefully matched to the on-screen action, and it launched Mickey Mouse to stardom. November 18, 1928, the date of its premiere at New York's Colony Theater, is recognized as Mickey's official birthday.

Plot

Mickey is a deckhand aboard the steamboat Podunk, whistling "Steamboat Bill" at the wheel while Captain Pete scowls. The boat picks up livestock at a dock, and Minnie Mouse boards, dropping her sheet music. Animals aboard swallow the music; Mickey and Minnie improvise a riotous musical performance — the melody "Turkey in the Straw" — using the animals and cargo as instruments, until Pete banishes Mickey to peeling potatoes in the galley.

Production and significance

Disney had already completed two silent Mickey cartoons, Plane Crazy and The Gallopin' Gaucho, but they had failed to find a distributor. Inspired by the success of the early sound film The Jazz Singer (1927), Disney put synchronized sound at the center of the third cartoon; Iwerks did most of the animation, and Disney himself provided the squeaky voices. The film was an immediate sensation, made Disney's studio the leader in animation, and was selected for the U.S. National Film Registry in 1998.

Copyright and public domain

Steamboat Willie became a famous case study in copyright term extension: Disney lobbied for extensions, including the 1998 Sonny Bono Copyright Term Extension Act, as the film's copyright approached expiry. The 1928 film nevertheless entered the U.S. public domain on January 1, 2024, allowing anyone to use this early version of Mickey Mouse — though Disney's trademark rights in Mickey as a brand identifier remain in force. Within days of the film entering the public domain, independent creators had announced horror films and games featuring the Steamboat Willie version of the character.
