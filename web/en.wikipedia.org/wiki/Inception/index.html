Inception is a 2010 science fiction action film written and directed by Christopher Nolan, who also produced it with Emma Thomas, his wife. The film stars Leonardo DiCaprio as Dom Cobb, a professional thief who steals information by infiltrating the subconscious of his targets during dream states. Offered a chance to have his criminal history erased, Cobb is tasked with the near-impossible "inception": planting an idea inside a target's mind without the target realising it.

Plot

Cobb and his team enter layered dreams — dreams within dreams — aboard a trans-Pacific flight, targeting energy magnate Robert Fischer, whom they must persuade to dissolve his father's empire. As the team descends through successive dream levels, Cobb's projection of his dead wife Mal repeatedly endangers the mission, and the distinction between dream and reality, symbolised by Cobb's spinning-top totem, becomes increasingly uncertain. The film's ending, cutting away before the top is seen to fall, is famously ambiguous.

Reception and legacy

Inception grossed over $839 million worldwide, becoming one of the highest-grossing films of 2010. It won four Academy Awards, including Best Cinematography and Best Visual Effects, and was nominated for Best Picture and Best Original Screenplay. Critics praised its originality, structure and Hans Zimmer score; the film entered popular culture as a byword for nested, self-referential structures.
