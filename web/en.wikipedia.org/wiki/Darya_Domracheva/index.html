Darya Vladimirovna Domracheva (Belarusian: Дар'я Уладзіміраўна Домрачава; born 3 August 1986) is a retired Belarusian biathlete and coach. She is the most successful female biathlete in Olympic history in terms of gold medals won at a single Games, having won three golds at the 2014 Winter Olympics in Sochi, and added a fourth gold in the relay at Pyeongchang 2018. She received the title Hero of Belarus in 2014.

Career

Domracheva grew up in Nyagan, Siberia, where her parents worked as architects, and began biathlon there before the family returned to Minsk. She initially competed for Russia as a junior before switching to Belarus. Her World Cup breakthrough came in the 2008–09 season. In 2014–15 she won the overall Biathlon World Cup crystal globe. She took a break from competition in 2016–17 following the birth of her daughter, returned for the 2017–18 season, and announced her retirement in June 2018 because of family circumstances.

Personal life

Domracheva is married to Norwegian biathlon legend Ole Einar Bjørndalen; they have a daughter, Xenia. After retirement she and Bjørndalen worked as coaches of the Chinese national biathlon team ahead of the Beijing 2022 Winter Olympics.
