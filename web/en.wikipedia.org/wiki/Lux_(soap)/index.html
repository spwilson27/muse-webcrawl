Lux is a global brand of personal care products developed by Unilever. The range includes beauty soaps, shower gels, bath additives, hair shampoos and conditioners. Lux is one of the world's best-known soap brands and has been marketed in more than 100 countries.

History

Lux was introduced in 1899 by Lever Brothers as "Sunlight Flakes", a laundry soap sold in flakes rather than as a cake. In 1900 the name was changed to Lux, derived from the Latin word for light and suggesting "luxury". In 1924 Lux became the first mass-market toilet soap to be launched worldwide, positioned as an affordable beauty soap comparable to fine French soaps.

From the late 1920s the brand became famous for its advertising campaigns featuring Hollywood film stars, with the slogan that nine out of ten film stars used Lux soap. Endorsements by actresses became a hallmark of the brand for decades, and Lux extended the star-endorsement strategy across Asia, the Middle East and Latin America, using local film and television celebrities.

Products

The Lux range centres on fragranced beauty soap bars, but in many markets also includes liquid hand soap, body wash, bath foam and hair care lines. Formulations and fragrances are adapted to regional markets while retaining the brand's positioning around fragrance, soft skin and affordable glamour.
