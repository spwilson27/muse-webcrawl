Kannada cinema, also known as Sandalwood, is the segment of Indian cinema dedicated to the production of motion pictures in the Kannada language, widely spoken in Karnataka. Based primarily in Bengaluru, the industry produces around 200 films a year.

History

The first Kannada talkie, Sati Sulochana, was released in 1934. The industry grew around figures such as Dr. Rajkumar, whose films — including Bedara Kannappa (1954) — defined its golden age, alongside directors like Puttanna Kanagal and Girish Kasaravalli, leaders of the parallel-cinema movement. In the 21st century, films such as K.G.F: Chapter 1 (2018) and its sequel, and Kantara (2022), achieved pan-Indian and international success, transforming the industry's reach.

Recognition

Kannada films and artists have won numerous National Film Awards, and Bengaluru hosts the Karnataka Chalanachitra Academy and the annual Bangalore International Film Festival.
