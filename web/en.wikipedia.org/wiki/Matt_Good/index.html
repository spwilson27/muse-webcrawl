Matthew Aaron Good (born February 11, 1984) is an American musician, singer and record producer. He is best known as the lead guitarist, keyboardist and backing vocalist of the post-hardcore band From First to Last, which he co-founded in Tampa, Florida, in 1999 (originally as First Too Last). After Sonny Moore (Skrillex) left the band, Good also took on lead vocal duties.

Career

With From First to Last, Good recorded albums including Dear Diary, My Teen Angst Has a Body Count (2004), Heroine (2006) and Dead Trees (2015). The band went on hiatus in 2010; in the interim Good formed the supergroup Destroy Rebuild Until God Shows (D.R.U.G.S.) with Craig Owens and others, releasing a self-titled album in 2011, and pursued electronic production as one half of the duo Kit Fysto with AJ Calderon. From First to Last reunited, and in 2017 released the single "Make War" with Moore returning.

Production work

Good has worked extensively as a producer and songwriter for other artists in the alternative and metalcore scenes, with credits for bands including Asking Alexandria, The Word Alive, Sleeping with Sirens, Memphis May Fire, Ice Nine Kills and Hollywood Undead. He is known for blending electronic production with heavy guitar-driven rock.
