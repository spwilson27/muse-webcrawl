Military animal

Military animals are animals employed by armed forces for warfare and other combat-related activities, as well as for transport, detection, communication and ceremonial duties. Species used have included horses, camels, elephants, dogs, pigeons and dolphins.

Uses

Horses formed cavalry and hauled artillery and supplies for thousands of years; elephants served as mounts and shock troops in South and Southeast Asia. In modern forces, military working dogs detect explosives and patrol, carrier pigeons delivered messages into the twentieth century, and marine mammals such as bottlenose dolphins and sea lions have been trained for mine detection and harbour security. Animals have also been used as weapons or weapon carriers in experimental programmes, and as mascots.

Welfare and recognition

Many countries now treat service animals as personnel, providing veterinary care and retirement. The Dickin Medal, instituted in Britain in 1943, honours animals displaying gallantry in wartime.
