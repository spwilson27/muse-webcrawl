Brandon Paak Anderson (born February 8, 1986), known professionally as Anderson .Paak (pronounced "Anderson Pack"), is an American singer, rapper, songwriter, record producer, and drummer. Renowned for performing live while drumming, he rose to prominence with the albums Venice (2014) and Malibu (2016), and in 2021 formed the duo Silk Sonic with Bruno Mars, whose single "Leave the Door Open" won multiple Grammy Awards including Record and Song of the Year.

Early life

Anderson was born in Oxnard, California, to an African-American father and a Korean-born mother. He began drumming at church as a child and performed in his family's farming community; after a period of homelessness with his young family, he worked as assistant to Shafiq Husayn of Sa-Ra and rebuilt his career in Los Angeles, releasing music first under the name Breezy Lovejoy.

Career

Signing with Dr. Dre's Aftermath Entertainment, Anderson contributed prominently to Dre's Compton (2015) and released the Grammy-nominated Malibu in 2016. Subsequent albums include Oxnard (2018), Ventura (2019), which won the Grammy for Best R&B Album, and An Evening with Silk Sonic (2021) with Bruno Mars. He also leads the band the Free Nationals and founded the label Apeshit Inc. His 2024 collaboration Why Lawd? with producer Knxwledge, as the duo NxWorries, continued his run of acclaimed releases.

Musical style

Anderson's music blends soul, funk, R&B, and West Coast hip hop, and he cites Stevie Wonder, D'Angelo, and Dr. Dre among his influences. The ".Paak" name, and the dot in it, he says, stands for "detail" — a reminder of his Korean surname Paak and of attention to craft.
