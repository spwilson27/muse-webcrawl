Bachchan family

The Bachchan family is a prominent Indian film family associated with Bollywood, headed by the poet Harivansh Rai Bachchan and dominated by his son, the actor Amitabh Bachchan, often described as the most influential actor in the history of Indian cinema.

Members

The family traces its roots to Uttar Pradesh. Harivansh Rai Bachchan (1907–2003), who adopted the pen name "Bachchan", was a celebrated Hindi poet of the Chhayavaad movement; his wife Teji Bachchan was a social activist. Their elder son, Amitabh Bachchan (born 1942), became Bollywood's defining superstar in the 1970s with films such as Zanjeer, Sholay and Deewaar.

Amitabh married actress Jaya Bhaduri in 1973. Their daughter Shweta Bachchan Nanda is a writer and columnist, married into the Kapoor-linked Nanda family. Their son Abhishek Bachchan (born 1976) is an actor married to actress and former Miss World Aishwarya Rai; their daughter Aaradhya was born in 2011. Amitabh's younger brother Ajitabh Bachchan is a businessman, and Ajitabh's daughter Namrata is a photographer and poet.

Influence

The Bachchans are frequently called the "first family of Bollywood". Amitabh has also hosted the long-running game show Kaun Banega Crorepati, and family members are among India's most followed public figures, with their Mumbai homes, Jalsa and Prateeksha, attracting crowds of fans every Sunday.
