STV (TV channel)

STV is a Scottish free-to-air television channel serving the majority of Scotland. It is owned by STV Group and consists of the two ITV network franchises for Central and Northern Scotland formerly known as Scottish Television and Grampian Television. The channel reaches about 3.2 million viewers each month and broadcasts a mixture of network programming from ITV alongside Scottish news, current affairs, and entertainment.

History

Scottish Television began broadcasting to Central Scotland on 31 August 1957, and Grampian Television to the north-east in 1961. The two companies came under common ownership when SMG plc acquired Grampian in 1997. In 2006, both stations were rebranded under the unified name STV, and the company later renamed itself STV Group. A local channel, STV2, operated from 2017 to 2018 before closing.

Programming

STV's flagship news programmes are STV News at Six, produced separately for the Central and North regions (with further sub-regional opt-outs), and the current affairs programme Scotland Tonight. The channel is available on Freeview, satellite, cable, and through the STV Player streaming service, which also serves as the catch-up platform for ITV content in Scotland (and later hosted BritBox-originated material in some regions).
