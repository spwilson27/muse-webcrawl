List of general topology topics

The List of general topology topics is an outline of concepts in general topology (point-set topology), the branch of mathematics concerned with topological spaces and continuous functions — the abstract foundations of notions such as closeness, convergence, compactness, and connectedness.

Basic concepts

  • Topological space and open sets
  • Closed set, closure, interior, boundary
  • Basis and subbasis
  • Continuous function and homeomorphism
  • Subspace, product, and quotient topologies
  • Metric space

Properties and axioms

  • Separation axioms: T0, T1, Hausdorff (T2), regular, normal
  • Countability axioms: first-countable, second-countable, separable, Lindelöf
  • Compactness and paracompactness; Heine–Borel theorem
  • Connectedness and path-connectedness
  • Convergence: sequences, nets, filters

Major theorems and constructions

  • Tychonoff's theorem
  • Urysohn's lemma and the Tietze extension theorem
  • Baire category theorem
  • Stone–Čech compactification
  • One-point compactification and completion
  • Initial and final topologies; function spaces
