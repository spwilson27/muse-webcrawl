Stéphane Peterhansel (born 6 August 1965) is a French rally driver and the most successful competitor in the history of the Dakar Rally, with a record fourteen overall victories across the motorcycle and car categories.

Career

Peterhansel began as a trials and enduro rider and won the Dakar on a Yamaha motorcycle six times between 1991 and 1998. Switching to cars, he won with Mitsubishi in 2004, 2005, and 2007, then with the X-raid Mini team in 2012 and 2013, and with Peugeot in 2016 and 2017. His eighth car victory came with Mini in 2021 in Saudi Arabia, thirty years after his first win. He later joined Audi's hybrid RS Q e-tron programme.
