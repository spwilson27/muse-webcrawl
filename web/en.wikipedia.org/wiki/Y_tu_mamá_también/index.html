Y tu mamá también

Directed by: Alfonso Cuarón
Written by: Alfonso & Carlos Cuarón
Starring: Maribel Verdú, Gael García Bernal, Diego Luna
Release: 2001
Language: Spanish

Y tu mamá también (Spanish for "And Your Mother Too") is a 2001 Mexican coming-of-age road film directed by Alfonso Cuarón and co-written with his brother Carlos Cuarón. It stars Maribel Verdú, Gael García Bernal, and Diego Luna.

Plot

Two teenage friends from different social classes in Mexico City — wealthy Tenoch and working-class Julio — persuade Luisa, the older Spanish wife of Tenoch's cousin, to join them on a road trip to a beach they invent on the spot, "Boca del Cielo" (Heaven's Mouth). Along the drive through rural Mexico, an omniscient narrator periodically freezes the action to reveal the characters' hidden truths and the social and economic realities passing by the car windows. The journey becomes a sexual and emotional awakening for the boys, and a farewell for Luisa, who is privately facing terminal illness.

Reception and legacy

The film was a major international success and a landmark of the "Nuevo Cine Mexicano." It was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay and won the Best Screenplay award at Venice. Its frank treatment of class, politics, and sexuality was controversial in Mexico, where its adults-only rating restricted its audience. It launched or consolidated the international careers of its director and two young stars.
