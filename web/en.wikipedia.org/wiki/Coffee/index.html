Coffee is a beverage prepared from roasted coffee beans — the seeds of berries of Coffea plants. Darkly coloured, bitter and slightly acidic, it is one of the world's most popular drinks, valued for the stimulant caffeine.

History and production

Coffee drinking is documented in 15th-century Yemen and spread through the Ottoman world to Europe's coffeehouses, hubs of news and ideas. Brazil, Vietnam and Colombia lead production; insect pollinators help yields, and climate change threatens suitable growing zones — a sustainability concern from Geneva trade desks to forest farms.
