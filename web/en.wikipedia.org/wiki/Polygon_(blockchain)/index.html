Polygon (blockchain)

Polygon
Formerly: Matic Network
Native token: POL (formerly MATIC)
Launched: 2017 (as Matic)
Type: Ethereum scaling platform

Polygon is a blockchain platform and framework for building and connecting Ethereum-compatible blockchain networks. Founded in India in 2017 by Jaynti Kanani, Sandeep Nailwal, and Anurag Arjun as the Matic Network, it rebranded to Polygon in 2021 while retaining its token symbol MATIC at the time.

Technology

Polygon's best-known component is the Polygon PoS chain, a proof-of-stake sidechain/commit chain that runs in parallel to Ethereum, periodically publishing checkpoints to the Ethereum mainnet. It offers much lower transaction fees and faster confirmation than Ethereum layer 1 while remaining EVM-compatible, so existing Ethereum applications can be deployed with minimal changes.

The platform later expanded into a suite of scaling technologies, including Polygon zkEVM, a zero-knowledge rollup compatible with the Ethereum Virtual Machine, and the AggLayer (aggregation layer) intended to unify liquidity across multiple chains. In 2024 the MATIC token was migrated 1:1 to a new token, POL, designed to secure and coordinate the growing family of Polygon chains.

Adoption

Polygon has been used for decentralized finance (DeFi), non-fungible tokens (NFTs), and blockchain games, and has hosted enterprise and consumer projects, including NFT programs associated with brands such as Starbucks, Reddit, Nike, and Meta. Its low fees made it a common choice for high-volume minting and microtransactions.
