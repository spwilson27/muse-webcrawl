Bicycle racing

Governing bodyUCI
DisciplinesRoad, track, mountain bike, cyclo-cross, BMX
Olympic sportSince 1896

Bicycle racing is a competitive sport in which riders race bicycles in a variety of disciplines. It has been part of the Olympic Games since the first modern Games in 1896 and is governed internationally by the Union Cycliste Internationale (UCI).

Disciplines

Road racing includes one-day classics, stage races such as the Tour de France, Giro d'Italia and Vuelta a España, and time trials. Other disciplines include track racing on velodromes, cross-country and downhill mountain biking, cyclo-cross on mixed winter courses, BMX racing and freestyle, and gravel racing.

Format and tactics

Road races are shaped by drafting: riders shelter in a peloton to save energy, form breakaways, and use team tactics to position a leader for sprints or mountain finishes.
