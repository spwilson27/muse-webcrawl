Ben Lexcen AM (born Robert Clyde Miller; 19 March 1936 – 1 May 1988) was an Australian yachtsman and marine architect, designer of the winged-keel yacht Australia II, which won the America's Cup in 1983.

Early life

Born in Boggabri, New South Wales, Miller left school early, worked as a railway apprentice and in aircraft engineering, and developed an interest in sailing and yacht design.

Australia II

Lexcen designed Australia II for Alan Bond's 1983 America's Cup challenge. Its revolutionary winged keel, kept secret under a skirt, helped defeat Dennis Conner's Liberty and end 132 years of United States dominance.

Legacy

Lexcen changed his name by deed poll in 1977. He died in 1988. The Lexcen sports scholarship and other memorials honour his contribution to Australian sailing.
