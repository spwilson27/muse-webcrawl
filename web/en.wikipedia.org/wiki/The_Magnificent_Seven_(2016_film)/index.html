The Magnificent Seven (2016 film)

Directed by: Antoine Fuqua
Starring: Denzel Washington, Chris Pratt, Ethan Hawke
Released: 2016
Box office: $162 million

The Magnificent Seven is a 2016 American Western action film directed by Antoine Fuqua and written by Nic Pizzolatto and Richard Wenk. It is a remake of the 1960 Western of the same name, which itself was a reimagining of Akira Kurosawa's Seven Samurai (1954).

Plot

In 1879, the mining town of Rose Creek is terrorized by the ruthless industrialist Bartholomew Bogue (Peter Sarsgaard). A widow, Emma Cullen, hires bounty hunter Sam Chisolm (Denzel Washington), who assembles six other gunfighters — gambler Josh Faraday (Chris Pratt), sharpshooter Goodnight Robicheaux (Ethan Hawke), tracker Jack Horne, assassin Billy Rocks, outlaw Vasquez, and Comanche warrior Red Harvest. The seven train the townspeople and face Bogue's army in a climactic battle.

Reception

The film opened the 2016 Toronto International Film Festival and grossed $162 million worldwide. Reviews were mixed to positive, praising the cast and action while noting the remake's familiarity. It was the final film scored by composer James Horner, completed posthumously by Simon Franglen.
