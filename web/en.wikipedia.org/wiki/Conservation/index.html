Conservation biology

Conservation biology
DefinitionScientific study and practice of protecting biodiversity
ToolsProtected areas, habitat restoration, captive breeding, law
[Image: A ranger releasing a rehabilitated bird into a forest clearing]

Conservation in biology is the protection of species, habitats and ecosystems from excessive rates of extinction and erosion of interactions. It responds to the biodiversity crisis — habitat loss, overexploitation, invasive species, pollution and climate change driving species like those in the dog and forest pages' wild relatives toward extinction.

Practice

Tools include protected areas (national parks, reserves), the IUCN Red List, treaties such as CITES, captive breeding and rewilding. Conservation intersects with Sustainability, indigenous stewardship (Tibetan-protected snow leopard habitat among them) and the satellite monitoring of habitats.
