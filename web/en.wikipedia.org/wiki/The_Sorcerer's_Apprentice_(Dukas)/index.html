The Sorcerer's Apprentice (Dukas)

Composer: Paul Dukas
Composed: 1896–1897
Subtitle: Scherzo after a ballad by Goethe

The Sorcerer's Apprentice (French: L'Apprenti sorcier) is a symphonic poem by the French composer Paul Dukas, completed in 1897 and subtitled "Scherzo after a ballad by Goethe." It is by far Dukas's most famous work.

Music and story

Based on Johann Wolfgang von Goethe's 1797 ballad Der Zauberlehrling, the piece depicts a lazy apprentice who, left alone, enchants a broom to fetch water for him. Unable to stop the spell, he watches the broom multiply after he splits it with an axe, until the returning sorcerer restores order. Dukas's vivid orchestration — including the broom's theme in the bassoons and a prominent glockenspiel — precisely follows the narrative, making it a textbook example of program music.

Fantasia

The work reached its widest audience through Walt Disney's 1940 animated film Fantasia, in which Mickey Mouse plays the apprentice in a segment conducted by Leopold Stokowski. The sequence introduced the music to generations of listeners and fixed its imagery in popular culture; it was reprised in Fantasia 2000.
