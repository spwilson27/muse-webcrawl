Glan-llyn

Glan-llyn is an outdoor education and activity centre on the southern shore of Llyn Tegid (Bala Lake) near Bala, Gwynedd, in north Wales. Operated by the Urdd Gobaith Cymru (the Welsh League of Youth), it is one of the organisation's residential centres, offering Welsh-medium outdoor activities and courses primarily aimed at young people. The name is Welsh for "lakeside".

History and activities

The Urdd established its first permanent camp at Glan-llyn in 1950, on the shore of Wales's largest natural lake, and the site developed into a year-round residential centre. Activities offered include sailing, canoeing, kayaking, windsurfing and other water sports on Llyn Tegid, together with climbing, hill-walking in the surrounding mountains of Snowdonia (Eryri), and team-building courses. The centre accommodates school groups, youth groups and other visitors, and all activities are conducted through the medium of Welsh, in keeping with the Urdd's aim of promoting the language among young people.

Glan-llyn is one of three main Urdd residential centres, alongside Llangrannog in Ceredigion and Cardiff.
