Pacific Park

Pacific Park
Location: Santa Monica Pier, Santa Monica, California
Opened: 25 May 1996
Attractions: Pacific Wheel, West Coaster

Pacific Park is an ocean-front amusement park located on the Santa Monica Pier in Santa Monica, California. Opened on 25 May 1996, it is a small, admission-free park whose rides are sold individually.

Attractions

The park's best-known ride is the Pacific Wheel, a 130-foot Ferris wheel lit by hundreds of thousands of LED lights and described as the world's only solar-powered Ferris wheel. Its other signature ride is the West Coaster, a steel roller coaster that circles the park. In all the park has around a dozen rides, plus midway games and food outlets.

In popular culture

Set against the Pacific Ocean at the western end of historic Route 66, Pacific Park and its illuminated wheel are a frequent backdrop in films, television programmes and music videos, and one of the most photographed amusement parks in the United States.
