Love is a complex set of emotions and behaviours characterised by intimacy, passion and commitment, directed at people, activities or ideals. Cultures distinguish romantic eros, friendship philia, family storge and selfless agape — distinctions made by the Greeks and used from Plato's Symposium onward.

Psychology and biology

Attachment theory describes bonding styles formed in infancy; neuroscience implicates dopamine, oxytocin and serotonin in attraction and pair-bonding. Love underlies marriage customs worldwide and much of art — from Petrarch's sonnets to the pop songs streamed in this crawl's music pages.
