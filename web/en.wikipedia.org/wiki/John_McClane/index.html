John McClane

John McClane is a fictional character and the protagonist of the Die Hard film franchise. Portrayed by Bruce Willis, McClane is a New York City police detective who repeatedly finds himself in the middle of large-scale terrorist attacks and heists, which he thwarts through improvisation and sheer stubbornness.

Character

McClane was created by screenwriter Jeb Stuart, based on the character Detective Joe Leland from Roderick Thorp's 1979 novel Nothing Lasts Forever. Unlike the invulnerable action heroes of the 1980s, McClane is portrayed as an ordinary, vulnerable man — barefoot, bleeding, and complaining throughout Die Hard (1988). His catchphrase is "Yippee-ki-yay, motherfucker."

Films

In Die Hard (1988), McClane single-handedly battles Hans Gruber's gang in Los Angeles's Nakatomi Plaza on Christmas Eve. He returned in Die Hard 2 (1990), Die Hard with a Vengeance (1995), Live Free or Die Hard (2007), and A Good Day to Die Hard (2013). McClane is married (later separated) from Holly Gennero and has two children, Lucy and John Jr.

Reception

McClane is widely regarded as one of the greatest action heroes in film history, credited with reshaping the action genre toward reluctant, everyman protagonists.
