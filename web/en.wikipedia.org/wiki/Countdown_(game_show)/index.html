Countdown (game show)

Countdown
Channel 4 game show
First broadcast: 2 November 1982
Based on: Des chiffres et des lettres

Countdown is a British game show involving word and number puzzles that has been broadcast on Channel 4 since the channel's launch day, 2 November 1982. It was the first programme aired on Channel 4 and is one of the longest-running game shows in the world.

Format

Two contestants compete in letters rounds, choosing vowels and consonants to form the longest possible word; numbers rounds, using arithmetic to reach a target from six random numbers; and a final "Countdown Conundrum", a nine-letter anagram. It was hosted for many years by Richard Whiteley with Carol Vorderman as arithmetician; later presenters include Des Lynam, Jeff Stelling, Nick Hewer and Anne Robinson.
