"Putin's Palace" (Russian: Дворец Путина) is the popular name for a large palace complex on the Black Sea coast near Gelendzhik in Krasnodar Krai, Russia. The complex became widely known in January 2021 after the release of an investigation by Alexei Navalny's Anti-Corruption Foundation (FBK), which alleged that the residence had been built for President Vladimir Putin through an elaborate corruption scheme, at a cost estimated at around 100 billion rubles (about US$1.4 billion).

The investigation

The FBK film presented drone footage, architectural plans, photographs and financial documents said to show the palace's luxurious features, including elaborate interiors and underground facilities. The film was viewed tens of millions of times within days and the phrase "a palace for Putin" featured in protests across Russia.

Response

Putin denied that he or his family owned the palace. Businessman Arkady Rotenberg subsequently claimed he was the beneficiary of the complex and that it was being developed as an aparthotel.
