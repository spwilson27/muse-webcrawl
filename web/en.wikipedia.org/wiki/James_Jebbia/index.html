James Jebbia (born 22 July 1963) is an English-American fashion designer and businessman best known as the founder of Supreme, the New York skateboarding and streetwear brand founded in 1994 that became one of the most influential labels in global youth culture.

Early life

Jebbia was born in the United States to an American father in the U.S. Air Force and an English mother, a teacher, and spent much of his childhood in Crawley, West Sussex, England, after his parents separated. He moved to New York City at the age of 19, where he worked in retail, including at the SoHo clothing store Parachute, and sold goods from a stall on Canal Street.

Career

In the late 1980s Jebbia opened Union NYC, a store importing British street fashion, and worked closely with Shawn Stüssy, managing the New York store of the Stüssy brand. In April 1994 he opened the first Supreme store on Lafayette Street in downtown Manhattan, designed with open space so skateboarders could ride in. The store combined skate hardware and clothing with a stark aesthetic and the red box logo with the Supreme name in Futura Heavy Oblique.

Under Jebbia's direction Supreme developed the "drop" model of weekly limited releases and collaborations with artists and brands from Louis Vuitton to Nike, generating intense demand and resale culture. In 2017 the private equity firm Carlyle Group acquired a stake valuing the company at about $1 billion; in 2020 VF Corporation acquired Supreme for $2.1 billion, and in 2024 it was sold on to EssilorLuxottica. Jebbia won the CFDA Menswear Designer of the Year award in 2018.
