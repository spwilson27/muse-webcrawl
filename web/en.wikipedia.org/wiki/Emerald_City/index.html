The Emerald City is the capital city of the fictional Land of Oz in L. Frank Baum's Oz books and in the 1939 film The Wizard of Oz. It lies at the end of the yellow brick road, at the exact centre of Oz, where the four countries of the land meet.

Description

In The Wonderful Wizard of Oz (1900), visitors to the city are required to wear green-tinted spectacles, supposedly to protect their eyes from the city's brilliance — though the Wizard privately admits the glasses make the ordinary city appear green. In later books the city is genuinely splendid, built of green marble, studded with emeralds, and surrounded by a high wall. Its palace is the seat of Princess Ozma after her restoration to the throne.

In other media

The 1939 MGM film depicted the Emerald City as an Art Deco fantasy of green towers, reached through fields of poppies; its image has become iconic in popular culture. The city figures in Gregory Maguire's novel Wicked and its stage and screen adaptations as a glittering but politically corrupt capital. "Emerald City" is also a long-standing nickname for Seattle, Washington.
