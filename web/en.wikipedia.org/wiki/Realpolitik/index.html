Realpolitik

Realpolitik ("politics of reality") is diplomacy and statecraft based primarily on considerations of power and practical circumstances rather than ideology, ethics or moral principles. The term was coined by Ludwig von Rochau in his 1853 Principles of Realpolitik in the context of German liberal nationalism.

Coined1853, Ludwig von Rochau
Associated withBismarck, Kissinger

Practice

Most famously associated with Otto von Bismarck, whose flexible alliances pursued Prussian and German interests, Realpolitik also describes Niccolò Machiavelli's earlier thought and, in the 20th century, Henry Kissinger's détente and opening to China under President Nixon.

Criticism

Critics argue it can justify amorality and short-termism; defenders see it as honest recognition of power realities. It is related to political realism in international relations. Contrast with idealist or values-based approaches; see also Ostpolitik.
