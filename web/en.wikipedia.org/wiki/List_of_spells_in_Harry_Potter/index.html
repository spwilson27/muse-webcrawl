List of spells in Harry Potter

The Harry Potter novels by J. K. Rowling feature a large repertoire of spells, usually cast with a wand and spoken incantations derived largely from Latin. This list collects the principal spells named in the books and films.


Most spells in the series are cast with a wand and a spoken incantation.

Unforgivable Curses

The Killing Curse (Avada Kedavra), the Cruciatus Curse (Crucio) and the Imperius Curse (Imperio) are classified as Unforgivable: their use on another human carries a life sentence in Azkaban, except when authorised (for example, for Aurors during wartime).
