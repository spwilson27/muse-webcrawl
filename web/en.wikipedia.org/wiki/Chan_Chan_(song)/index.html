Chan Chan (song)

"Chan Chan" is a Cuban son composition by Compay Segundo, written in 1984 and first recorded in 1987. It became internationally famous as the opening track of the Buena Vista Social Club album (1997) and film (1999).

Lyrics

The song tells of Juanica and Chan Chan sifting sand on the beach at Alto Cedro, on the way to Marcané and Cueto – places in eastern Cuba named in the refrain "De Alto Cedro voy para Marcané / Llego a Cueto, voy para Mayarí". Its simple four-chord son structure made it a signature tune of the Cuban music revival.
