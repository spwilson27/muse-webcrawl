DTS (company)

DTS, Inc.
Founded: 1990 as Digital Theater Systems
Headquarters: Calabasas, California
Industry: Audio technology
Parent: Xperi (from 2016)

DTS, Inc. is an American audio technology company best known for the DTS family of multichannel audio codecs used in cinemas, home theatre, Blu-ray discs and streaming.

History

Founded as Digital Theater Systems, the company debuted its digital surround sound in cinemas with Steven Spielberg's Jurassic Park in 1993, offering an alternative to Dolby Digital with audio stored on CD-ROMs synchronised to the film. DTS soundtracks later appeared on DVDs and became one of two major formats on Blu-ray.

Products

Its technologies include DTS-HD Master Audio, the object-based DTS:X, DTS-HD and virtual surround products such as DTS Virtual:X. DTS was acquired by Tessera Technologies in 2016 and became part of Xperi.
