Charlie Hebdo shooting

Date7 January 2015
LocationParis, France
Deaths12
PerpetratorsSaïd and Chérif Kouachi

The Charlie Hebdo shooting was a terrorist attack on 7 January 2015 on the Paris offices of the French satirical weekly newspaper Charlie Hebdo. Two gunmen, brothers Saïd and Chérif Kouachi, killed 12 people, including editor Stéphane "Charb" Charbonnier and several well-known cartoonists, and wounded 11 others.

Background

The magazine had been targeted before for publishing cartoons of the Prophet Muhammad; its offices were firebombed in 2011. The attackers identified themselves with al-Qaeda in the Arabian Peninsula, which later claimed responsibility.

Aftermath

Related attacks over the following two days killed five more people. On 11 January 2015, millions marched in Paris and other cities in defence of free expression under the slogan "Je suis Charlie". The Kouachi brothers were killed by police on 9 January.
