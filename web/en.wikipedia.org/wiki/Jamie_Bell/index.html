Andrew James Matfin Bell (born 14 March 1986) is an English actor. He rose to fame as a teenager in the title role of Billy Elliot (2000), for which he won the BAFTA Award for Best Actor in a Leading Role, becoming one of the youngest winners of the award. He has since built a varied career in independent and mainstream films, and played Bernie Taupin in the Elton John biopic Rocketman (2019).

Early life

Bell was born in Billingham, County Durham, in a family with a strong dance tradition: his grandmother, mother, aunt and sister were all dancers. He began ballet lessons at the age of three and attended the Northfield School. His performance in a school production led him toward acting.

Career

Chosen from some 2,000 boys, Bell played Billy Elliot, a Durham miner's son who discovers ballet during the 1984–85 miners' strike. The film was a critical and commercial success. He went on to roles in Deathwatch (2002), Undertow (2004), Peter Jackson's King Kong (2005), Flags of Our Fathers (2006), Jumper (2008) and Defiance (2008). He voiced Tintin in Steven Spielberg's The Adventures of Tintin (2011) and starred in Snowpiercer (2013), Filth (2013) and as Ben Grimm / the Thing in Fantastic Four (2015).

On television he starred as Abraham Woodhull in the AMC spy drama Turn: Washington's Spies (2014–2017). His portrayal of Bernie Taupin in Rocketman (2019) earned widespread praise, and he received further acclaim for All of Us Strangers (2023), in which he played the father of Andrew Scott's character.

Personal life

Bell has a son from his marriage to actress Evan Rachel Wood (2013–2014). He married actress Kate Mara, his Fantastic Four co-star, in 2017; they have two children.
