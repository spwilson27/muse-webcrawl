Anything Goes

Music and lyrics: Cole Porter
Book: P. G. Wodehouse, Guy Bolton, Howard Lindsay, Russel Crouse
Premiere: 21 November 1934, Alvin Theatre, New York

Anything Goes is a musical with music and lyrics by Cole Porter. The original book was a collaborative effort by P. G. Wodehouse and Guy Bolton, heavily revised by the team of Howard Lindsay and Russel Crouse. The story concerns madcap antics aboard an ocean liner bound from New York to London.

Plot

Billy Crocker, a stowaway aboard the SS American, is in love with heiress Hope Harcourt, who is engaged to Lord Evelyn Oakleigh. Nightclub singer Reno Sweeney and Public Enemy Number 13, "Moonface" Martin, aid Billy in his quest to win Hope. The ensuing complications involve disguises, mistaken identities, and a shipboard wedding.

Productions

The musical opened on Broadway in 1934 starring Ethel Merman as Reno Sweeney and was a great success, running for 420 performances. It has been revived many times, including the 1987 Lincoln Center production starring Patti LuPone and the 2011 Broadway revival starring Sutton Foster, which won the Tony Award for Best Revival of a Musical. The show was adapted into films in 1936 and 1956.
