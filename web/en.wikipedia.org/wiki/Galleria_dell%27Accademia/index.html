Galleria dell'Accademia

LocationVia Ricasoli 58–60, Florence, Italy
Established1784
TypeArt museum
Best known forMichelangelo's David

The Galleria dell'Accademia is an art museum in Florence, Italy, founded in 1784 by Grand Duke Pietro Leopoldo of Tuscany as a gallery for students of the adjacent Academy of Fine Arts. It is best known as the home of Michelangelo's marble statue of David.

Collections

David was moved to the Accademia from Piazza della Signoria in 1873 and is displayed in a purpose-built tribune, together with Michelangelo's unfinished Prigioni (Slaves) and his Saint Matthew. The museum also holds important collections of Florentine Gothic and Renaissance painting, works by Botticelli, Ghirlandaio, and Pontormo, the original plaster model for Giambologna's Rape of the Sabines, and a notable department of historical musical instruments, including violins by Stradivari.

Visitor information

The Accademia is one of Italy's most visited museums, and advance booking is generally recommended.
