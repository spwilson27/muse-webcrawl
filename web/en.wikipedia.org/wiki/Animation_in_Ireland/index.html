Animation in Ireland

Animation in Ireland refers to the production of animated films and television in Ireland. Although the industry was small for much of the twentieth century, it expanded from the 1980s onward through independent studios, international service work and internationally acclaimed feature films.

Development

Early Irish animation included commercials and short films, while larger-scale production grew with studios serving overseas clients. The establishment of animation courses and public support helped create a professional base in Dublin and other cities. Irish studios became known for hand-drawn features as well as children's television.

Notable works

Kilkenny-based Cartoon Saloon brought Irish animation wide attention with The Secret of Kells (2009), Song of the Sea (2014), The Breadwinner (2017) and Wolfwalkers (2020), several of which received Academy Award nominations. Other Irish companies produce preschool series, comedies and co-productions for global broadcasters and streaming services.
