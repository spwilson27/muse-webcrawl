Hall & Oates were an American pop rock duo formed in Philadelphia in 1970, consisting of Daryl Hall and John Oates. Blending rock, rhythm and blues and soul influences, they became the most commercially successful musical duo of all time, with reported sales of over 80 million records worldwide.

Career

The duo first charted in the mid-1970s with songs such as "Sara Smile" and "Rich Girl" (their first US number one, in 1977). Their greatest success came in the early 1980s with a string of hits including "Kiss on My List", "Private Eyes", "I Can't Go for That (No Can Do)", "Maneater" and "Out of Touch" — six Billboard Hot 100 number-one singles in total. Albums such as Voices (1980), Private Eyes (1981) and H2O (1982) defined their polished, synth-inflected sound.

Hall & Oates were inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2014 and continued touring together into the 2020s. In 2023–2024 a legal dispute between the two over the sale of a stake in their joint venture became public, and both indicated that the partnership had effectively ended.
