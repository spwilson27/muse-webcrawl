Downtime

Downtime is a term used to refer to periods when a system is unavailable. Downtime or outage duration refers to a period of time that a system fails to provide or perform its primary function. Reliability, availability, recovery, and unavailability are related concepts. The unavailability is the proportion of a time span that a system is unavailable or offline. This is usually a result of the system failing to function because of an unplanned event, or because of routine maintenance (a planned event in contrast).

The term is commonly applied to networks and servers. Common reasons for unplanned network or service outages include system failures (such as crashes), physical damage to equipment (such as fire or water damage), or cyber attacks (such as distributed denial-of-service attacks or ransomware). A planned outage is typically the result of a change that requires an outage in order to implement, such as upgrading software, changing networking configuration, or performing preventative maintenance on hardware at a facility.

Types

Industry standards for the term and its use vary. In computing, "downtime" generally refers to a period during which a computer system, network, or application is not operational and cannot be used, whether due to failure, maintenance, or other causes. In manufacturing, downtime refers to periods when production equipment is not operating, whether planned (such as for changeovers, maintenance, or breaks) or unplanned (such as breakdowns or material shortages).

Effects

Outages caused by system failures can have a significant effect on the users of computer systems. For example, businesses can suffer substantial financial losses when e-commerce services go down, and online services may lose user trust. Many organisations measure and report availability using figures such as "five nines" (99.999% uptime), which allows only a few minutes of downtime per year. Service-level agreements (SLAs) often specify maximum permitted downtime and financial penalties for exceeding it.

Planned downtime

Planned downtime is downtime specifically scheduled in advance, usually for maintenance. Maintenance windows are often scheduled during periods of low demand, such as overnight or on weekends, to minimise user impact. Users are typically notified in advance of scheduled downtime.
