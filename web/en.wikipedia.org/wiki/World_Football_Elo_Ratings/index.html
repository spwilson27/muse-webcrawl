The World Football Elo Ratings are a ranking system for men's national association football teams, adapted from the Elo rating system originally devised for chess by Arpad Elo. Published at eloratings.net, the ratings are widely used by analysts as an alternative to the official FIFA World Rankings.

Method

Ratings are updated after every international match. The change in a team's rating depends on the result, the margin of victory, and the strength of the opponent: defeating a stronger team earns more points than defeating a weaker one, with the expected result computed from the pre-match rating difference. A weighting factor accounts for the importance of the tournament. Historical ratings can be computed back to the first international in 1872, allowing comparisons of teams across eras.

P = Pbefore + K · (W − We)

where K is a weight index adjusted for goal difference, W the actual result (1 win, 0.5 draw, 0 loss) and We the expected result.

Comparison with FIFA rankings

Unlike the FIFA rankings, which historically used a fixed window of results, the Elo method responds to every match and never fully discards older results, simply discounting them through subsequent games. In 2018 FIFA itself adopted an Elo-based formula for the FIFA World Rankings.
