List of waterfalls by flow rate

Inga Falls, among the largest waterfalls by discharge

This list ranks waterfalls by their mean annual water discharge (flow rate), measured in cubic metres per second. The largest waterfalls by flow are generally not the tallest; many are rapids or cataracts on very large rivers, such as the Congo and the Amazon system. Flow rates vary seasonally, and some falls, notably Khone Phapheng and Guaíra, have been altered or submerged by dam construction.
