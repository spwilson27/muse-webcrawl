UCI Mountain Bike World Cup

The UCI Mountain Bike World Cup is a multi-round series of mountain bike races organized by the Union Cycliste Internationale (UCI), the world governing body for cycling. The series is the premier season-long competition in mountain biking and features the disciplines of cross-country Olympic (XCO), cross-country short track (XCC), downhill (DHI), and endurance formats such as cross-country marathon and enduro.

OrganizerUnion Cycliste Internationale (UCI)

History

A cross-country World Cup series began in 1989, and a separate downhill World Cup followed in 1993. Over the years the series has visited venues across Europe, North America, and beyond, with long-running stops such as Mont-Sainte-Anne in Canada, Fort William in Scotland, and Nové Město na Moravě in the Czech Republic. In 2023 the UCI consolidated its mountain bike series under a single organizer and brand, adding enduro and marathon racing alongside cross-country and downhill.

Format

Riders accumulate points at each round toward overall World Cup titles awarded at the end of the season in each discipline and category. Cross-country racing is held on laps of technical off-road circuits, while downhill is a timed solo descent of a steep, rough course. Elite, under-23, and junior categories are contested, and the overall winners wear distinctive leader's jerseys during the season.
