The Italian Job (2003 film)

Directed by: F. Gary Gray
Starring: Mark Wahlberg, Charlize Theron, Edward Norton, Jason Statham, Seth Green, Mos Def, Donald Sutherland
Released: May 30, 2003
Box office: $176.1 million

The Italian Job is a 2003 American heist action film directed by F. Gary Gray. A remake of the 1969 British film of the same name, it stars Mark Wahlberg as Charlie Croker, leader of a crew of thieves who plan to steal back gold from a former ally who double-crossed them, in a caper famed for its chase sequences featuring Mini Coopers through Los Angeles.

Plot

In Venice, Charlie Croker and mentor John Bridger (Donald Sutherland) lead a crew that steals $35 million in gold bars. Crew member Steve (Edward Norton) betrays them, seizing the gold and killing Bridger. A year later, Croker reassembles the team — explosives expert Left Ear, computer hacker Napster, getaway driver Handsome Rob, and Bridger's daughter Stella (Charlize Theron), a professional safe-cracker — to steal the gold from Steve's Los Angeles mansion. After Steve moves the gold by armored truck, the crew hacks the city's traffic grid, detonates the street beneath the truck, and escapes in three Mini Coopers through subway tunnels and storm drains, outrunning Steve's helicopter before he is arrested by a Ukrainian mobster whose cousin he had killed.

Production and reception

BMW, maker of the revived Mini, supplied dozens of cars for the production, including specially built electric Minis for filming in Los Angeles subway tunnels. The film received generally positive reviews and grossed $176 million worldwide on a $60 million budget; it became the highest-grossing film produced by Paramount Pictures in 2003. Plans for a sequel, long titled The Brazilian Job, were never realized.
