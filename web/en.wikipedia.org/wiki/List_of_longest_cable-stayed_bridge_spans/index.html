List of longest cable-stayed bridge spans

This is a list of the longest cable-stayed bridge spans in the world, ranked by the length of the main span. The longest since 2012 is the Russky Bridge in Vladivostok, Russia, at 1,104 metres — the only cable-stayed bridge with a span over one kilometre. China holds most of the remaining top places.

Notes

Multi-span cable-stayed bridges complicate rankings: the Jiaxing–Shaoxing Sea Bridge and the Millau Viaduct have several cable-stayed spans, none individually at the top of the list. The Changtai Yangtze River Bridge in China, completed in the mid-2020s with a 1,176 m main span, was expected to take the record from the Russky Bridge.
