Raising and lowering indices

In mathematics and mathematical physics, raising and lowering indices are operations on tensors which convert a contravariant index into a covariant one (lowering), or vice versa (raising). The conversion is done by contracting with the metric tensor gμν or its inverse gμν.

Definition

Given a vector vμ, the corresponding covector is vν = gνμvμ; conversely, vμ = gμνvν, using Einstein summation notation. The operations apply to tensors of any rank, index by index. They feature prominently in general relativity, where the Minkowski or spacetime metric converts between vector and covector descriptions — for example, the electromagnetic field tensor is obtained by raising one index of its covariant form.
