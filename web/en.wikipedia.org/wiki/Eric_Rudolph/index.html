Eric Robert Rudolph (born 19 September 1966), also known as the Olympic Park Bomber, is an American domestic terrorist convicted of a series of bombings across the southern United States between 1996 and 1998, which killed two people and injured over 200 others.

Bombings

The first attack took place at Centennial Olympic Park in Atlanta on 27 July 1996, during the Summer Olympics, killing one spectator and injuring 111; a cameraman died of a heart attack at the scene. Rudolph later bombed gay nightclubs and abortion clinics in the Atlanta area and in Birmingham, Alabama, where an off-duty police officer working as a security guard was killed in January 1998.

Fugitive years and capture

Rudolph became one of the FBI's Ten Most Wanted Fugitives and evaded capture for more than five years in the Appalachian wilderness of western North Carolina. He was arrested in Murphy, North Carolina, on 31 May 2003 by a police officer who found him behind a grocery store.

Conviction

In 2005, as part of a plea agreement that spared him the death penalty, Rudolph pleaded guilty to all charges and was sentenced to four consecutive life terms without the possibility of parole. He is held at the ADX Florence supermax prison in Colorado.
