Court of the Lord Lyon


Arms of the Lord Lyon

The Court of the Lord Lyon, also known as the Lyon Court, is a standing court of law in Scotland with jurisdiction over heraldry and pedigrees. The court, which is part of the Scottish legal system, is headed by the Lord Lyon King of Arms, who must be legally qualified, as he has criminal jurisdiction in heraldic matters, and the court is fully integrated into the Scottish legal system, including having a Procurator Fiscal.

Jurisdiction

The Lyon Court maintains the Public Register of All Arms and Bearings in Scotland, established in 1672, in which all grants and matriculations of coats of arms are recorded. Uniquely among heraldic authorities, the bearing of arms in Scotland is a matter of law: the unauthorised use of arms is a criminal offence, and the court may impose fines and order the seizure or destruction of objects bearing unlawful arms.

Officers

Assisting the Lord Lyon are the heralds and pursuivants of Scotland, the Lyon Clerk and Keeper of the Records, and the Procurator Fiscal to the Court, who investigates complaints of usurped arms.
