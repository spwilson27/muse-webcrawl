Francisco de Paula Santander

Born2 April 1792, Villa del Rosario, Viceroyalty of New Granada
Died6 May 1840, Bogotá, Colombia
OfficePresident of New Granada (1832–1837)

Francisco José de Paula Santander y Omaña (2 April 1792 – 6 May 1840) was a Colombian military and political leader who served as Vice President of Gran Colombia (1821–1827) and later as President of the Republic of New Granada (1832–1837).

Independence wars

Santander organised the patriot army in Casanare that made possible Bolívar's crossing of the Andes and the victory at Boyacá in 1819. As vice president governing in Bolívar's absence, he built the institutions of the new republic, earning the title "the Man of Laws".

Later career

His legalism and centralism clashed with Bolívar's ambitions; implicated by association in the 1828 attempt on Bolívar's life, he was exiled. After Gran Colombia dissolved, he returned to become president of New Granada, promoting education and administrative reform.
