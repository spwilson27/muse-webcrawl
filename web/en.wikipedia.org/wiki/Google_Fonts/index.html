Google Fonts is a computer font and web font service owned by Google. Launched in 2010 as Google Web Fonts, it provides a library of free and open-source font families that can be embedded in websites through a content delivery network, or downloaded for local use. The service hosts well over a thousand font families covering Latin and many other writing systems, and its API is one of the most widely used font delivery systems on the web.

History

Google Web Fonts launched in 2010 with a small catalogue of open-source typefaces. The service was renamed Google Fonts in 2016 alongside a redesigned browsing interface. In 2020 the site added support for variable fonts as a first-class feature, and the catalogue icon was updated. Google also commissions typefaces, notably the Noto family, which aims to support all languages and scripts encoded in Unicode, and the Roboto family used across Google's products.

Use

Web developers include fonts by linking a stylesheet from Google's servers or by using the CSS API, after which the fonts are delivered as files in formats such as WOFF2, subsetted by character range to reduce download size. Fonts can also be downloaded from the Google Fonts website or from the project's GitHub repository and self-hosted. Most fonts in the library are released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, with some under the Apache License 2.0, permitting free use in commercial and personal projects.

Privacy considerations

Because fonts are fetched from Google's servers, requests reveal the visitor's IP address to Google. This has attracted legal and privacy scrutiny: in January 2022 a Munich court ruled that embedding Google Fonts in a way that transmitted a visitor's IP address without consent violated the GDPR. As a result, some website operators choose to self-host the font files instead.

Popular typefaces

Among the most used families in the library are Roboto, Open Sans, Lato, Montserrat, Oswald, Poppins and Noto Sans. Google Fonts also distributes a large icon font derived from the Material Symbols icon set.
