dinnerladies (stylised in lower case) is a British television sitcom created, written by and starring Victoria Wood. It was broadcast on BBC One from 1998 to 2000, running for two series of 16 episodes. Set almost entirely in the canteen of a fictional factory in Manchester (HWD Components), it follows the catering team — headed by manager Bren Furlong (Wood) — and the gentle comedy of their working days, romances, family troubles and banter over the serving counter.

Production and style

Unlike most sitcoms, dinnerladies was filmed for much of its run in sequence as a continuous real-time piece: each episode largely plays out in the canteen over the course of one shift, with few cuts away, giving it the character of a stage play and allowing unusually long, naturalistic scenes. Wood deliberately avoided broad catchphrases and plot-driven farce, building humour from character and dialogue; characters include the careworn Bren, her mother Petula (Julie Walters), anxious Twinkle (Maxine Peake), dignified Dolly (Thelma Barlow), sex-obsessed Jean (Anne Reid), dreamy Anita (Shobna Gulati), and the caretaker Stan (Duncan Preston), with Bren's slow-burn romance with canteen manager Tony (Andrew Dunn) threading through both series.

Reception

The series won the British Comedy Award for best new TV comedy and is frequently ranked among the greatest British sitcoms — fourth in the BBC's 2004 poll Britain's Best Sitcom among more recent programmes and consistently near the top of critics' lists. Its 1999 Christmas special and the final episode, in which Bren's future is resolved, drew large audiences.
