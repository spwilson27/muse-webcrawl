King of Prussia (shopping mall)

King of Prussia is a shopping mall located in the community of King of Prussia in Upper Merion Township, Montgomery County, Pennsylvania, northwest of Philadelphia. Owned by Simon Property Group, it is one of the largest shopping malls in the United States, with roughly 2.9 million square feet of retail space and more than 450 stores.

History

The mall opened in 1963 as an open-air plaza and grew through successive expansions, including the enclosed Court at King of Prussia. A major 2016 expansion connected the Plaza and Court sections. Anchors have included Nordstrom, Bloomingdale's, Macy's, Neiman Marcus and Primark, and the complex is a major retail destination for the Philadelphia metropolitan area.
