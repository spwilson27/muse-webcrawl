Quartier des spectacles

The Quartier des spectacles is an arts and entertainment district located in the downtown area of Montreal, Quebec, Canada. It is an area of nearly one square kilometre that contains more than 80 cultural venues, including 30 performance halls with almost 28,000 seats for all types of shows, as well as eight public squares and some 40 festivals.

The district is bounded roughly by René Lévesque Boulevard, Sherbrooke Street, and Saint-Hubert, Saint-Urbain and City Councillors streets. It is home to major institutions such as the Place des Arts, the Musée d'art contemporain de Montréal, the Théâtre du Nouveau Monde, the Club Soda, and the Monument-National.

Development

The Quartier des spectacles project was launched in the early 2000s by the City of Montreal and the provincial and federal governments to revitalize the eastern part of downtown and to consolidate Montreal's reputation as a festival city. A signature element is the Quartier's lighting plan, which uses red dot projections and coordinated illumination to identify venues and guide visitors. The Place des Festivals, a large public square inaugurated in 2009, hosts outdoor events for festivals such as the Montreal International Jazz Festival and Just for Laughs.
