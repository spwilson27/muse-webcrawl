Stihl

Stihl (German: [ʃtiːl]) is a German manufacturer of chainsaws and other handheld power equipment, including blowers and trimmers. Its headquarters are in Waiblingen, Baden-Württemberg, Germany. Stihl was founded in 1926 by Andreas Stihl, an important innovator in early chainsaw production.

Andreas Stihl designed and hand-built the first chainsaws in the 1920s; his early electric and two-man petrol saws made forestry work far less labour-intensive. The company grew after the Second World War, patenting an all-position diaphragm carburettor that allowed saws to be used at any angle. Stihl claims to be the world's best-selling chainsaw brand and the only chainsaw manufacturer to make its own saw chains and guide bars.

The Stihl Group develops, manufactures and distributes power tools for forestry, agriculture, landscape maintenance and the construction industry, as well as garden tools under the Viking brand (renamed Stihl in 2019). Products are sold through a network of specialist dealers rather than big-box stores. The company remains family-owned, and its United States subsidiary, Stihl Inc., is headquartered in Virginia Beach, Virginia.
