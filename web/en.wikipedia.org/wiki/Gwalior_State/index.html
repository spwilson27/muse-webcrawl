Gwalior State

Gwalior State was a princely state within the Maratha Confederacy and later the British Raj, ruled by the Scindia dynasty. One of the five premier Maratha states, it was among the largest and most prestigious princely states of India, entitled to a 21-gun salute.

History

The state grew from the campaigns of Ranoji Scindia, a Maratha commander who established himself at Ujjain in the 1730s. His descendant Mahadaji Scindia (r. 1768–1794) made Gwalior a dominant power in northern India, building a European-trained army and effectively controlling the Mughal emperor at Delhi. After defeat in the Third Anglo-Maratha War (1817–18), the state accepted British paramountcy.

Later rule and accession

Nineteenth- and twentieth-century maharajas such as Jivajirao Scindia developed the state with railways, industries and the palatial Jai Vilas Palace at Lashkar. In 1948 the last maharaja acceded to the Dominion of India, and Gwalior State was merged into the new state of Madhya Bharat, with Jivajirao as its Rajpramukh.
