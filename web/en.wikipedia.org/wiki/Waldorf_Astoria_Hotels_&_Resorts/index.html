Waldorf Astoria Hotels & Resorts is a luxury hotel and resort brand of Hilton Worldwide. The brand descends from the Waldorf Astoria New York, the landmark Park Avenue hotel formed by merging William Waldorf Astor's Waldorf Hotel (1893) with his cousin John Jacob Astor IV's Astoria Hotel (1897); the current Art Deco building opened in 1931.

The Waldorf Astoria New York, flagship of the brand, opened on Park Avenue in 1931.

The brand

Hilton launched Waldorf Astoria as its top-tier luxury flag in 2006, converting historic properties such as the Arizona Biltmore and La Quinta Resort. The collection has grown to several dozen hotels and resorts in destinations including Amsterdam, Beverly Hills, Dubai, Jerusalem, Shanghai and the Maldives.

The original hotel

The New York flagship pioneered room service and the Waldorf salad, and for decades served as a diplomatic and social hub; every U.S. president from Herbert Hoover to Barack Obama stayed there. It closed in 2017 for a major conversion by owner Anbang Insurance Group into condominiums plus a smaller hotel, reopening in the mid-2020s.
