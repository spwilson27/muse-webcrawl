List of rivers of Russia

Russia has roughly 100,000 rivers, including some of the longest and largest on Earth. The great Siberian rivers — the Ob, Yenisei and Lena — flow north across the West Siberian Plain and Siberia to the Arctic Ocean, while the Volga, the longest river in Europe, drains European Russia into the Caspian Sea.
