President of Mozambique

The President of the Republic of Mozambique is the head of state and head of government of Mozambique, and commander-in-chief of the armed forces. The office was created at independence from Portugal on 25 June 1975. The president is directly elected for a five-year term and, under the 2004 constitution, may serve at most two consecutive terms.

List of presidents

  • Samora Machel (1975–1986) — leader of FRELIMO and first president of the People's Republic of Mozambique; died in an air crash near Mbuzini, South Africa, in October 1986.
  • Joaquim Chissano (1986–2005) — oversaw the end of the civil war (1992 Rome peace accords), the 1990 multi-party constitution and the first democratic elections in 1994; re-elected in 1999.
  • Armando Guebuza (2005–2015) — served two terms; his presidency coincided with the offshore natural-gas boom and later the "hidden debts" scandal.
  • Filipe Nyusi (2015–2025) — served two terms; signed the 2019 Maputo peace agreement with RENAMO and faced the insurgency in Cabo Delgado.
  • Daniel Chapo (from January 2025) — FRELIMO candidate who won the disputed October 2024 election and was inaugurated on 15 January 2025 amid widespread protests.

Powers

The president appoints the prime minister and cabinet, commands the armed forces, conducts foreign relations and can dissolve the Assembly of the Republic in defined circumstances. All presidents to date have come from FRELIMO, the liberation movement that has governed Mozambique continuously since independence.
