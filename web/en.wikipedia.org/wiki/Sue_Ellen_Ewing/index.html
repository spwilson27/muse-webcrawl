Sue Ellen Ewing is a fictional character in the CBS primetime soap opera Dallas (1978–1991) and its 2012 TNT revival. Portrayed by Linda Gray, she is the wife of the scheming oil baron J.R. Ewing and one of the show's most prominent characters, evolving from a marginal figure in the first season into a central, critically acclaimed role.

Characterisation

Sue Ellen is a former Miss Texas, pushed into marriage by her ambitious mother. Trapped in an unhappy marriage to the unfaithful J.R., she becomes an alcoholic, a storyline that unfolds across the series: repeated battles with drinking, stays in sanatoriums, affairs (including with her husband's rival Cliff Barnes and with rodeo cowboy Dusty Farlow), and a gradual emergence as a successful businesswoman in her own right, running a lingerie company and later a film production venture.

The "Who shot J.R.?" storyline

Sue Ellen was among the suspects in the famous 1980 "Who shot J.R.?" mystery. Initially arrested after the shooting, she was cleared when it emerged that, drunk, she had given the gun to her sister Kristin Shepard, the actual shooter.

Reception

Linda Gray's performance was widely praised, earning her an Emmy Award nomination for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Drama Series in 1981 and two Golden Globe nominations. The character is often cited as a ground-breaking portrayal of alcoholism and female resilience on American television. Gray reprised the role in the 2012 revival series, in which Sue Ellen, now sober, is a powerful figure in Texas business and politics who runs for governor.
