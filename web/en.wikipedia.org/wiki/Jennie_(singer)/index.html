Jennie, member of Blackpink and solo artist.

Kim Jennie (Korean: 김제니; born January 16, 1996), known mononymously as Jennie, is a South Korean singer, rapper, and actress. Born in Seoul and educated in New Zealand, she joined YG Entertainment as a trainee in 2010 and debuted in 2016 as a member of the girl group Blackpink, which became one of the best-selling female groups of all time. She was the first Blackpink member to release solo music, with the single "Solo" (2018).

Early life

Jennie was born in Seoul and at age nine moved to Auckland, New Zealand, where she lived with a homestay family and attended ACG Parnell College. Her years abroad made her fluent in English. Originally planning to study in the United States, she instead auditioned for YG Entertainment at fourteen and returned to South Korea as a trainee, training for nearly six years before debut. During training she appeared in labelmate G-Dragon's music video "That XX" and featured on tracks by G-Dragon, Lee Hi, and Seungri.

Career with Blackpink

Blackpink debuted on August 8, 2016, with the single album Square One. Jennie serves as the group's main rapper and a lead vocalist. With Blackpink she released the record-breaking albums The Album (2020) and Born Pink (2022), headlined Coachella in 2023 — the first Korean act to do so — and embarked on the Born Pink World Tour, the highest-grossing concert tour by a girl group.

Solo career and acting

Her debut single "Solo" topped charts in South Korea and made her the first Korean female soloist to surpass a billion YouTube views for a music video. In 2023 she released the special single "You & Me" and made her acting debut under the name Jennie Ruby Jane in the HBO series The Idol. In late 2023 she founded her own label, Odd Atelier, for her solo activities while renewing her group contract with YG, and in 2024 signed with Columbia Records. Her debut studio album, Ruby (2025), included the singles "Mantra", "Love Hangover", and "Like Jennie" and was supported by the Ruby Experience concert run. Jennie is also a global ambassador for Chanel and one of the most-followed Korean celebrities on social media.
