Corus Entertainment

[Image: Corus Entertainment]

Corus Entertainment Inc. is a Canadian mass media and broadcasting company formed in 1999 as a spin-off of Shaw Communications. Headquartered at Corus Quay in Toronto, Ontario, it operates the Global Television Network, numerous specialty television channels, radio stations, and the animation studio Nelvana.

History

Corus was created in 1999 by JR Shaw as a separate, publicly traded company holding Shaw's radio and television assets. Over the following years it acquired specialty channels including YTV, Treehouse TV, Teletoon and W Network, as well as the Nelvana animation studio in 2000.

In 2016 Corus acquired Shaw Media from Shaw Communications for C$2.65 billion, bringing the Global television network and a large portfolio of specialty channels under its control. The acquisition made Corus one of Canada's largest media companies.

Assets

Corus's holdings include the Global Television Network, specialty channels such as Food Network Canada, HGTV Canada, History, Showcase, Slice and National Geographic Canada, the Global News division, radio stations in major markets including CKNW in Vancouver and CFIQ in Toronto, and the Nelvana animation studio. It also operates streaming services including StackTV through Amazon Channels.

Challenges

In the 2020s Corus faced declining advertising revenue, heavy debt from the Shaw Media acquisition, and the loss of key programming rights. In 2024 the company announced cost-cutting measures and job reductions after Warner Bros. Discovery moved rights to several of its lifestyle brands to Rogers Communications.
