Palm Treo

The Palm Treo was a line of smartphones originally developed by Handspring and later produced by Palm, Inc. The Treo 180 appeared in 2002, and the Treo 600 (2003) and Treo 650 (2004) became popular business devices combining a phone, PDA, email, and a thumb keyboard.

Features

Most Treos ran Palm OS, while some models such as the Treo 700w ran Windows Mobile. They featured QWERTY keyboards, touchscreens operated with a stylus, expandable storage, and early mobile web and messaging capabilities.

End of the line

The Treo Pro (2008) was among the last models; Palm replaced the line with webOS devices such as the Palm Pre and Centro successors.
