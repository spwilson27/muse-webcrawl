Center for a Stateless Society
Abbreviation: C4SS
Founded: 2006
Type: anarchist think tank / media centre
Orientation: left-wing market anarchism

The Center for a Stateless Society (C4SS) is an anarchist think tank and media centre founded in 2006, associated with the Molinari Institute in Auburn, Alabama. It advocates a stateless society based on free markets as understood by mutualist and individualist anarchist traditions, an orientation often called left-wing market anarchism.

Activities

C4SS publishes commentary, studies and translations of classical anarchist texts, and its fellows and writers have included figures such as Roderick T. Long, Kevin Carson and Gary Chartier. It promotes concepts such as "freed markets" — markets freed from state-granted privilege — and counter-economics, and it participated in founding the Alliance of the Libertarian Left.
