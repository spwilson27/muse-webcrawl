Affirmed (February 21, 1975 – January 12, 2001) was an American champion Thoroughbred racehorse who won the 1978 U.S. Triple Crown — the eleventh winner of the series — and is the most recent horse to sweep the Kentucky Derby, Preakness Stakes, and Belmont Stakes until American Pharoah repeated the feat in 2015. He was named American Horse of the Year in both 1978 and 1979.

Rivalry with Alydar

Affirmed's career is defined by his rivalry with Alydar, to whom he was closely related in age and ability. The two met ten times, with Affirmed winning seven. Their duel in the 1978 Belmont Stakes — a stretch-long head-to-head battle — is considered one of the greatest races in American turf history; Affirmed won by a head to complete the Triple Crown.

Career

Trained by Laz Barrera for Harbor View Farm and ridden by eighteen-year-old Steve Cauthen, Affirmed compiled a record of 22 wins, 5 seconds, and 1 third in 29 starts, earning $2,393,818. His major victories included the Santa Anita Derby, Hollywood Derby, Woodward Stakes (twice), Jockey Club Gold Cup, and the Marlboro Cup Invitational Handicap over Seattle Slew, the 1977 Triple Crown winner, in one of the decade's most anticipated match races.

Legacy

Affirmed was inducted into the National Museum of Racing and Hall of Fame in 1980. Blood-Horse magazine ranked him ninth among the top 100 U.S. racehorses of the 20th century. He died in 2001 at Jonabell Farm in Kentucky, where he had stood at stud; among his notable offspring was Flawlessly.
