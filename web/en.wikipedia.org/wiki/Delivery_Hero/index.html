Delivery Hero SE is a German multinational online food-delivery company based in Berlin. Founded in 2011 by Niklas Östberg and others, it operates in dozens of countries across Europe, Asia, the Middle East and Latin America under brands including foodpanda, Glovo, Talabat and Woowa Brothers (Baemin) in South Korea.

History

The company grew rapidly through acquisitions of local delivery platforms and went public on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange in 2017. It has since been a constituent of the DAX index. Delivery Hero focuses on food delivery, quick commerce ("Dmarts" dark stores) and subscription services.
