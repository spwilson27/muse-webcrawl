List of rivers of South Africa

Rivers of South Africa
Longest riverOrange River (2,200 km)
Major basinsOrange, Limpopo, Vaal, Tugela, Olifants
DrainageAtlantic Ocean and Indian Ocean

This is a list of rivers of South Africa. South Africa's rivers drain either westward into the Atlantic Ocean or eastward into the Indian Ocean. The longest and largest by discharge is the Orange River, which forms much of the northern border with Namibia. Most South African rivers are seasonal or intermittent in the arid interior, while the eastern escarpment rivers flow year-round.

Other notable rivers

Other rivers include the Caledon, the Sundays River, the Gamtoos, the Berg River, the Crocodile River, the Marico, the Mgeni, the Mhlathuze, the White and Black Umfolozi rivers, the Blood River of KwaZulu-Natal fame, and the Kei River, which gave its name to the former Transkei and Ciskei territories.
