Pfizer

Pfizer Inc. is an American multinational pharmaceutical and biotechnology corporation headquartered at The Spiral in Manhattan, New York City. It is one of the world's largest pharmaceutical companies.

Founded1849, Brooklyn, New York
FoundersCharles Pfizer, Charles Erhart
IndustryPharmaceuticals, biotechnology
ProductsComirnaty, Paxlovid, Lipitor, Viagra

History

Founded in 1849, Pfizer grew supplying citric acid, penicillin in WWII, and blockbuster drugs including Lipitor (atorvastatin) and Viagra (sildenafil). Major acquisitions include Warner-Lambert, Pharmacia and Wyeth.

COVID-19

With BioNTech, Pfizer developed Comirnaty, the first mRNA COVID-19 vaccine authorised in the West (2020), and the antiviral Paxlovid. See Drug development and FDA.
