Space Weather Prediction Center

The Space Weather Prediction Center (SWPC), formerly the Space Environment Center, is a laboratory and service center of the US National Weather Service, part of NOAA, located in Boulder, Colorado.

Space Weather Prediction Center
HeadquartersBoulder, Colorado, U.S.
ParentNOAA / National Weather Service
RoleSpace weather forecasts, watches, warnings

Mission

SWPC monitors the Sun and near-Earth environment continuously, issuing forecasts and alerts for solar flares, geomagnetic storms (G-scale), solar radiation storms (S-scale) and radio blackouts (R-scale) that affect satellites, aviation, power grids, GPS and communications.

Data

It uses spacecraft including DSCOVR at the L1 point for solar wind monitoring, GOES satellites for X-ray flux and particle measurements, and ground magnetometer networks.

History

Its origins lie in wartime radio propagation research; it has operated in Boulder since the 1940s under various names, becoming SWPC in 2007.
