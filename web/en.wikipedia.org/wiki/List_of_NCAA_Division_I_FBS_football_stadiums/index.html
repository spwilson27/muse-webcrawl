List of NCAA Division I FBS football stadiums

The following is a list of stadiums used by schools in the NCAA Division I Football Bowl Subdivision (FBS), the highest level of college football in the United States. More than 130 schools field FBS teams. The largest stadium is Michigan Stadium, with a capacity above 107,000, and several FBS stadiums seat more than 100,000 spectators — among the largest sports venues in the world.
