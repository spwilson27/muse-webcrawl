List of most-visited art museums

This list ranks the world's art museums by annual visitor numbers, based on surveys published by The Art Newspaper and the Association of Leading Visitor Attractions. Attendance fell dramatically worldwide in 2020–2021 because of the COVID-19 pandemic, then recovered substantially in 2022–2023.

Trends

The Louvre has been the world's most-visited art museum for most of the past two decades and caps daily admissions. Asian museums, particularly in South Korea and China, have climbed the rankings, while blockbuster exhibitions continue to drive peaks at major European and American institutions.
