Parallel Lines is the third studio album by the American rock band Blondie, released on 23 September 1978 by Chrysalis Records. Produced by Mike Chapman, it was the band's commercial breakthrough, reaching number one in the United Kingdom and number six on the US Billboard 200.

Background

Blondie formed in New York City in 1974 around singer Debbie Harry and guitarist Chris Stein. After two albums that sold modestly, the band enlisted Australian producer Mike Chapman, known for his work with The Sweet and Suzi Quatro, to give the group a more polished pop sound.

Singles and reception

The album produced the international hit "Heart of Glass", a disco-inflected track that topped charts in the US and UK, as well as "One Way or Another", "Hanging on the Telephone" (a cover of The Nerves) and "Sunday Girl". Critics praised the record's blend of punk energy, 1960s girl-group melody and new wave sheen; it is frequently ranked among the greatest albums of all time and was inducted into the Grammy Hall of Fame.

Track listing
