Sven Kramer

Sven Kramer (born 23 April 1986) is a retired Dutch long track speed skater who specialised in the long distances, winning the gold medal in the men's 5000 metres at the 2010, 2014 and 2018 Winter Olympics. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest speed skaters of all time.

Career

Kramer won a record nine World Allround Championships and ten European Allround titles. At the 2010 Vancouver Olympics he won the 5000 m but was famously disqualified in the 10,000 m after an incorrect lane change directed by his coach. He went on to dominate long-distance skating for more than a decade and set world records in the 5000 m and 10,000 m.

Later life

Kramer retired after the 2022 season and moved into coaching and sports management in the Netherlands.
