Timeline of space exploration


Spaceflight milestones since 1957

This timeline of space exploration records the major firsts and landmark missions in humanity's exploration of space, from the first artificial satellite to interplanetary probes and crewed stations.

Milestones

DateEvent
4 October 1957Sputnik 1 (USSR) becomes the first artificial satellite.
12 April 1961Yuri Gagarin becomes the first human in space aboard Vostok 1.
20 July 1969Apollo 11: Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin land on the Moon.
1971Salyut 1, the first space station, is launched.
1977Voyager 1 and Voyager 2 launch on their grand tour of the outer planets.
1981First Space Shuttle flight (Columbia, STS-1).
1990Hubble Space Telescope deployed.
1998First modules of the International Space Station launched.
2004Cassini–Huygens reaches Saturn; the Huygens probe lands on Titan in 2005.
2012Voyager 1 enters interstellar space; Curiosity rover lands on Mars.
2015New Horizons flies by Pluto.
2021James Webb Space Telescope launched; Perseverance rover and Ingenuity helicopter operate on Mars.
