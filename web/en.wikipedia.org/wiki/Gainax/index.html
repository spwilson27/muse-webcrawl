Gainax Co., Ltd. was a Japanese anime studio famous for works such as Neon Genesis Evangelion, Royal Space Force: The Wings of Honnêamise, Nadia: The Secret of Blue Water, FLCL, and Tengen Toppa Gurren Lagann.

History

The studio grew out of the amateur group Daicon Film, whose spectacular opening animations for the Daicon III and IV science fiction conventions (1981 and 1983) drew wide attention. Incorporated as Gainax in 1984, it debuted the ambitious feature Royal Space Force in 1987. Neon Genesis Evangelion (1995) became a cultural phenomenon despite notorious budget and schedule pressures.

Later years

Many senior staff left to found or join studios such as Khara and Trigger. After years of financial and legal troubles, Gainax filed for bankruptcy in 2024, ending operations after roughly four decades; its trademarks and works were transferred to Khara.
