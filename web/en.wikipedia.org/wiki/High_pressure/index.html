High pressure

High pressure commonly refers to an area where atmospheric pressure at the surface is greater than in surrounding regions — an anticyclone. High-pressure systems are associated with descending air, clear skies and settled weather.

Meteorology

In the Northern Hemisphere winds circulate clockwise around a high (counterclockwise in the Southern Hemisphere) due to the Coriolis effect. Subtropical highs such as the Azores High shape climate belts and ocean gyres; winter continental highs can bring frost and fog.

Other uses

In physics and engineering, "high pressure" denotes pressures far above ambient, studied with diamond-anvil cells and used in processes from food preservation (high-pressure processing) to synthesis of superhard materials.
