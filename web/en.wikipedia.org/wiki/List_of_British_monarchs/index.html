List of British monarchs

There have been 13 monarchs of the Kingdom of Great Britain and the United Kingdom since the union of England and Scotland in 1707, and twelve monarchs of Great Britain before the Irish union of 1801. The separate kingdoms of England and Scotland each had their own monarchs before 1707; England's conventional line begins with Æthelstan, first king of a united England in 927.
