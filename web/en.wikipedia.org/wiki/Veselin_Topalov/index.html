Veselin Aleksandrov Topalov (born 15 March 1975) is a Bulgarian chess grandmaster and a former FIDE World Chess Champion. Known for his aggressive, dynamic style and willingness to sacrifice material for the initiative, he was one of the world's leading players for roughly two decades.

Veselin Topalov at the chessboard.

Career

Topalov learned chess from his father and rose quickly, winning the World Under-14 Championship in 1989 and earning the grandmaster title in 1992. He broke through to the elite with strong results in the mid-1990s, including celebrated wins over Garry Kasparov and Anatoly Karpov, and a famous victory over Kasparov at Linares in 1999 involving a rook sacrifice.

In 2005 Topalov won the FIDE World Chess Championship tournament at San Luis, Argentina, with a dominant score, becoming world champion. In 2006 he lost the reunification match against Vladimir Kramnik in Elista — a match overshadowed by the "Toiletgate" controversy over cheating allegations. He won the 2008–2010 Candidates cycle Challenger match against Gata Kamsky but lost the 2010 world championship match to Viswanathan Anand in Sofia.

Later years

Topalov remained a top-ten player into the 2010s, winning the 2011 Linares tournament and reaching a peak Elo rating of 2820 in 2015, among the highest ever recorded. He represented Bulgaria at numerous Chess Olympiads, winning an individual gold medal on board one at the 2014 Olympiad in Tromsø.
