Thor is the Norse god of thunder, storms and the protection of humankind, the foremost defender of the gods against the giants. Red-bearded and immense in strength, he wields the short-handled hammer Mjölnir, wears a belt that doubles his strength, and drives a chariot pulled by two goats. Of all the Norse gods he was the most widely worshipped, his hammer worn as a protective amulet across the Viking world.

Myths and fate

Famous tales include Thor's fishing for the World Serpent Jörmungandr, his journey to the giant Útgarða-Loki, where illusions trick him, and the theft of his hammer by the giant Thrym, recovered when Thor disguises himself as the bride. At Ragnarök Thor will kill the World Serpent and take nine steps before falling to its venom.
