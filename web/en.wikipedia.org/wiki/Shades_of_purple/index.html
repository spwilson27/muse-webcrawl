Shades of purple

Purple is the colour between blue and red on the visible spectrum. Shades of purple range from pale lavender to deep, near-black aubergine. In common usage, "purple" covers both spectral violet-adjacent hues and non-spectral mixtures of red and blue light.

Cultural associations

Purple has long been associated with royalty, wealth and power, because Tyrian purple dye was extraordinarily expensive to produce. It is also linked with spirituality, mourning in some cultures, and — in modern politics — with centrism or mixed allegiance, being a blend of red and blue.
