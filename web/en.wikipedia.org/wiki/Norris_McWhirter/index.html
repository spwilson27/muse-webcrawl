Norris McWhirter

Norris Dewar McWhirter (12 August 1925 – 19 April 2004) was a British author, political activist and sports journalist. With his twin brother Ross McWhirter, he co-founded The Guinness Book of Records (now Guinness World Records) in 1955.

Life

The McWhirter twins studied at Marlborough College and Oxford, served in the Royal Naval Volunteer Reserve during the Second World War, and afterwards ran a sports facts and figures agency. Commissioned by Sir Hugh Beaver of Guinness, they compiled the first edition of the record book, published in 1955 and an immediate bestseller.

Norris was a commentator on the BBC television series Record Breakers. Politically conservative, he co-founded the National Association for Freedom (later The Freedom Association) in 1975. His brother Ross was assassinated by the Provisional IRA in 1975. Norris McWhirter remained editor of the record book until 1986 and continued writing and campaigning until his death in 2004.
