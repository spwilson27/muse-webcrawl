Savoy Theatre

The Savoy Theatre is a West End theatre in the Strand in the City of Westminster, London. It opened on 10 October 1881, built by impresario Richard D'Oyly Carte to present the comic operas of Gilbert and Sullivan, which came to be known as the Savoy operas.

Savoy Theatre
Location: Strand, London WC2
Opened: 10 October 1881
Built by: Richard D'Oyly Carte
Capacity: ~1,150

Innovations

The Savoy was the first public building in the world to be lit entirely by electric light (incandescent lamps by Joseph Swan), and introduced innovations such as numbered seating and queueing for tickets.

History

After the Gilbert and Sullivan era, the theatre hosted opera, Shakespeare and modern drama. It was rebuilt in Art Deco style in 1929 to designs associated with Frank Tugwell and Basil Ionides. A fire in 1990 led to restoration; it reopened in 1993. In recent decades it has hosted long-running musicals.
