In Utero

In Utero
Artist: Nirvana
Released: 13 September 1993
Producer: Steve Albini
Label: DGC
Preceded by: Nevermind (1991)

In Utero is the third and final studio album by the American rock band Nirvana, released on 13 September 1993 by DGC Records. After the unexpected mass success of Nevermind, Kurt Cobain sought a rawer, less polished sound, recording with Steve Albini at Pachyderm Studio in Minnesota in February 1993.

Recording and release

The sessions were fast and largely live, with Albini refusing royalties and taking a flat fee. Rumours that DGC considered the result uncommercial led Scott Litt to remix the singles 'Heart-Shaped Box' and 'All Apologies'. The album title — a phrase Cobain used in journals — replaced the working title 'I Hate Myself and I Want to Die'. Its cover, designed by Cobain and art director Robert Fisher, shows a winged anatomical manikin.

Reception and legacy

In Utero debuted at number one on the Billboard 200 and has sold over 15 million copies worldwide. Singles included 'Heart-Shaped Box', 'Rape Me' and 'All Apologies'; the band's MTV Unplugged performance followed that November. Critics praised its abrasive yet melodic songs about sickness, fame and ambivalence toward stardom; it is routinely ranked among the greatest albums of the 1990s. Cobain died in April 1994, making it the band's last studio statement. It was inducted into the Library of Congress National Recording Registry in 2024 (2023 class).
