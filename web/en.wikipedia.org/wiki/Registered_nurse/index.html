Registered nurse

A registered nurse (RN) is a nurse who has graduated from a nursing programme and met the requirements of a licensing body to obtain a nursing licence. Registered nurses provide patient care, educate patients and the public about health conditions, and give advice and emotional support to patients and their families.

Education and licensing

Entry to the profession typically requires an Associate Degree in Nursing, a Bachelor of Science in Nursing, or a hospital diploma, followed by passing a licensing examination — in the United States and Canada, the NCLEX-RN. Registered nurses work in hospitals, clinics, schools, community settings and many other environments, and may specialise in fields such as critical care, paediatrics, oncology or public health. Advanced practice registered nurses, including nurse practitioners, hold graduate degrees and expanded scopes of practice.
