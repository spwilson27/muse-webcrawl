Plumbing

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Plumbing is any system that conveys fluids for a wide range of applications. Plumbing uses pipes, valves, plumbing fixtures, tanks and other apparatuses to convey fluids. Heating and cooling, waste removal and potable water delivery are among the most common uses for plumbing, but it is not limited to those applications. The word derives from the Latin plumbum for lead, as the first effective pipes used in the Roman era were lead pipes; in Roman times a worker in lead was a plumbarius, the origin of the word "plumber".

Systems

A plumbing system in a building comprises three main parts:

  • An adequate potable water supply system, delivering water under pressure to fixtures;
  • A drainage system, carrying wastewater and sewage away by gravity, protected by water-filled traps (such as the U-bend) that block sewer gases;
  • Fixtures — sinks, baths, showers, toilets and taps — that use water and discharge it to the drains, together with a vent system that equalises air pressure in the drain pipes.

Materials

Traditional materials included lead, clay and cast iron. Modern supply piping is most often copper, or plastics such as PVC, CPVC and cross-linked polyethylene (PEX), chosen for corrosion resistance and ease of jointing. Drainage commonly uses PVC or ABS plastic, cast iron in larger and noise-sensitive installations.

History

Sophisticated plumbing existed in the Indus Valley Civilisation, in Minoan Crete — where the palace of Knossos had flushing latrines — and above all in ancient Rome, whose aqueducts, lead water pipes and the great sewer Cloaca Maxima set standards unmatched until the nineteenth century. Modern sanitary plumbing developed with the germ theory of disease, municipal waterworks, and building codes; public-health gains from safe water and sewerage are often credited with major increases in urban life expectancy.

Regulation

Installation and repair are typically regulated trades: plumbers are licensed in most jurisdictions, and work must comply with plumbing codes covering pipe sizing, venting, backflow prevention and fixture standards.
