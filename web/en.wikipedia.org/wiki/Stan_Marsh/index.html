Stanley "Stan" Marsh is one of the four main characters of the American animated television series South Park. He is voiced by series co-creator Trey Parker, on whom the character is loosely based. Stan is a third- and later fourth-grade student living in the fictional Colorado town of South Park, and is typically depicted wearing a blue hat with a red puffball, a brown jacket and red gloves.

Stan Marsh, one of the four central boys in South Park

Character

Stan is generally portrayed as the most level-headed and morally grounded of the four boys, often serving as the voice of reason and delivering the episode's moral in the form of a summary speech beginning "You know, I learned something today". He is best friends with Kyle Broflovski — their greeting pairing of "Oh my God, they killed Kenny!" and "You bastards!" is one of the show's signature running jokes in its early seasons. Stan's family includes his father Randy, a geologist who became a prominent comic figure in later seasons, his mother Sharon, and his older sister Shelley.

Role in the series

Along with Kyle, Cartman and Kenny, Stan has been central to the series since its 1997 debut. Storylines frequently place him in conflict with adult absurdity in the town, and several episodes explore his depression, his on-off relationship with Wendy Testaburger, and his views on environmental and social issues.
