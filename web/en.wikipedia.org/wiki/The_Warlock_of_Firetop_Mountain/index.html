The Warlock of Firetop Mountain is a single-player adventure gamebook written by Steve Jackson and Ian Livingstone, illustrated by Russ Nicholson, and published by Puffin Books in 1982. It was the first book in the Fighting Fantasy series and launched the gamebook phenomenon of the 1980s.

Gameplay and story

The reader plays a lone adventurer who enters the labyrinth beneath Firetop Mountain seeking the treasure of the evil warlock Zagor. The story is divided into 400 numbered paragraphs; at the end of each passage the reader chooses among options that direct them to other paragraphs. Combat and tests of fortune are resolved with two six-sided dice, tracked on an adventure sheet recording the hero's Skill, Stamina, and Luck scores. A notorious feature is the maze section near the end, and the final challenge of finding the correct combination of keys to open Zagor's treasure chest after defeating him.

Impact

The book was an immediate bestseller and, together with its successors, sold in the millions, introducing a generation of children to fantasy role-playing concepts. It has been reprinted many times, adapted into a board game, a video game, and other media, and a sequel, Return to Firetop Mountain, was written by Livingstone for the series' tenth anniversary in 1992. The book's success established Jackson and Livingstone — co-founders of Games Workshop — as central figures in British gaming culture.
