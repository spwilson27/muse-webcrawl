Michael Gregg Wilson CBE (born 20 January 1942) is an American film producer and screenwriter, best known as the long-time producer, with his half-sister Barbara Broccoli, of the James Bond film series through Eon Productions.

Career

Wilson, stepson of Bond producer Albert R. "Cubby" Broccoli, joined Eon in the 1970s, first working in legal and production capacities. He became executive producer on Moonraker (1979) and co-wrote several Bond screenplays in the 1980s with Richard Maibaum, including For Your Eyes Only, Octopussy, A View to a Kill, The Living Daylights and Licence to Kill. From GoldenEye (1995) onward he produced the films jointly with Barbara Broccoli, overseeing the Pierce Brosnan and Daniel Craig eras through No Time to Die (2021).

Wilson is also known for cameo appearances in many of the Bond films, and for his large collection of historical photography and his role as a trustee of photographic archives. He and Broccoli received the Irving G. Thalberg Memorial Award at the 2024 Governors Awards for their stewardship of the Bond franchise. In 2025 Eon announced that creative control of the franchise would pass to Amazon MGM Studios, with Wilson and Broccoli retaining co-ownership.
