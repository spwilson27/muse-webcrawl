X-Force is a fictional superhero team appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, most often in association with the X-Men. Created by writer Fabian Nicieza and artist Rob Liefeld, the team first appeared in The New Mutants #100 (1991) and soon received its own series, whose first issue became one of the best-selling comics of all time.

Concept

X-Force was formed when the time-travelling soldier Cable reorganised the young New Mutants into a more militant strike force, willing to take a proactive and sometimes lethal approach that set it apart from Professor Xavier's more idealistic X-Men. Early members included Domino, Shatterstar, Warpath, Boom-Boom, Cannonball and Feral.

Later incarnations

The concept has been repeatedly reinvented. A satirical, media-obsessed version appeared in Peter Milligan and Mike Allred's run, which evolved into X-Statix. In 2008 a covert black-ops team led by Wolverine was formed to handle threats by any means necessary, while later series have cast X-Force as the intelligence and special-operations arm of the mutant nation of Krakoa, headed by Beast with Sage handling analysis.

In other media

Members of X-Force, including Cable and Domino, featured prominently in the film Deadpool 2 (2018), in which a hastily assembled version of the team is introduced with comically disastrous results.
