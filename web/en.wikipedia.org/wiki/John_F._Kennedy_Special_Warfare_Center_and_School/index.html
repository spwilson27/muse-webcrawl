John F. Kennedy Special Warfare Center and School

The United States Army John F. Kennedy Special Warfare Center and School (SWCS), located at Fort Liberty (formerly Fort Bragg), North Carolina, is the US Army's center for training and doctrine for Army special operations forces — principally the Special Forces (Green Berets), Civil Affairs, and Psychological Operations.

History

Founded in 1950 as the Psychological Warfare School and reorganised as the Special Warfare School in 1956, the institution was renamed in 1964 for President John F. Kennedy, a champion of unconventional warfare who had authorised the Green Berets' headgear in 1961.

Training

SWCS runs the Special Forces Qualification Course (the "Q Course"), including the culminating exercise Robin Sage in the fictional country of Pineland, as well as sniper, military freefall, combat diver (at Key West), survival (SERE), language, and civil affairs and PSYOP qualification courses. It trains thousands of US and allied soldiers each year.
