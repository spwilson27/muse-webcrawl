Comerica Incorporated is an American financial services company headquartered in Dallas, Texas. Its principal subsidiary, Comerica Bank, operates banking centres in Texas, Michigan, Arizona, California, and Florida, with a focus on commercial banking alongside retail and wealth management services.

History

The company traces its origins to the Detroit Savings Fund Institute, founded in Detroit in 1849. Through a long series of mergers, notably with Manufacturers National Bank in 1992, it became Comerica, taking its name from "Commerce America". The corporate headquarters moved from Detroit to Dallas in 2007, while Michigan remained its largest market.

Operations

Comerica's business segments comprise the Commercial Bank, the Retail Bank, and Wealth Management. Its name is also associated with Comerica Park, home of the Detroit Tigers baseball team, under a naming-rights agreement dating to the park's opening in 2000.
