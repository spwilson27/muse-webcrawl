Lakeport, California

Lakeport is an incorporated city and the county seat of Lake County, California, United States. It lies on the western shore of Clear Lake, the largest natural freshwater lake entirely within California.

History

The area was inhabited by Pomo peoples for millennia. The town was founded in the 1860s as Forbestown and later renamed Lakeport. Historically an agricultural centre known for pears and walnuts, it is now a hub for tourism on Clear Lake.

Geography

Lakeport sits at an elevation of about 413 metres. Its population was 5,026 at the 2020 census.
