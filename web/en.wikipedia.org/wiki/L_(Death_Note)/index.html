L (Death Note)

L Lawliet (エル・ローライト), known mononymously as L, is a fictional character in the manga series Death Note, created by writer Tsugumi Ohba and artist Takeshi Obata. He is an enigmatic, highly intelligent consulting detective who takes on the challenge of capturing the mass murderer known as Kira, the story's protagonist, Light Yagami.

Character

L is the world's greatest detective, having solved numerous difficult cases while concealing his identity behind a computer screen and the single letter "L", communicating through his assistant Watari. Raised at Wammy's House, an orphanage for gifted children in Winchester, England, he is gaunt and dishevelled, with dark circles under his eyes, and displays eccentric habits: he crouches rather than sits in chairs, holds objects delicately by the fingertips, consumes large quantities of sweets, and continually voices percentage estimates of his suspicions.

Role in the story

L narrows the Kira investigation to Japan and quickly identifies Light Yagami as his prime suspect, enrolling at the same university and eventually joining the Japanese Task Force investigation alongside him. Their cat-and-mouse conflict drives the first half of the series. L is ultimately killed by the shinigami Rem, who is manipulated by Light into writing L's real name in her Death Note to protect Misa Amane. His investigation is continued by his designated successors, Near and Mello.

Reception

L is consistently ranked among the most popular characters in Death Note and in anime generally, praised for his intellect, mannerisms, and rivalry with Light. He has been portrayed by Kenichi Matsuyama in the Japanese live-action films and by Lakeith Stanfield in the 2017 American adaptation.
