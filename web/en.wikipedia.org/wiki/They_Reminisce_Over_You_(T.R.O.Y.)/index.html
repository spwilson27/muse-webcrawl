They Reminisce Over You (T.R.O.Y.)

"They Reminisce Over You (T.R.O.Y.)"
Artist: Pete Rock & CL Smooth
Album: Mecca and the Soul Brother
Released: 1992
Genre: Hip hop

"They Reminisce Over You (T.R.O.Y.)" is a song by American hip hop duo Pete Rock & CL Smooth, released in 1992 as a single from their debut album Mecca and the Soul Brother. It is widely regarded as one of the greatest hip hop songs of all time.

Background

The song was written in memory of Troy Dixon, a close friend of the duo (known as "Trouble T Roy" of Heavy D & the Boyz), who died in 1990 after a fall during a concert tour. CL Smooth's lyrics reflect on family, childhood, and loss.

Production

Pete Rock's production is celebrated for its layered sample of the saxophone and bass line from "Today" by Tom Scott and the California Dreamers, combined with a vocal sample, creating the song's signature warm, melancholic groove.

Legacy

The song frequently appears on critics' lists of the best rap tracks ever recorded and has been sampled and referenced by numerous later artists. In 2012 a dispute over Lupe Fiasco's use of the same source sample in "Around My Way (Freedom Ain't Free)" drew renewed attention to the original.
