Preferred gender pronoun


Pronoun badges at a conference

A preferred gender pronoun, or simply a person's pronouns, is the set of pronouns that an individual prefers others to use in order to reflect that person's gender identity. Common sets in English include he/him, she/her and the singular they/them; some people prefer neopronouns such as xe/xem, or no pronouns at all.

Practice

Declaring one's pronouns — in introductions, email signatures, name badges and online profiles — became increasingly common in the 2010s as a way of normalising the sharing of gender-related information and reducing misgendering of transgender and non-binary people.

Debate

The practice has attracted debate over compelled speech, grammar and workplace policy in several countries, while style guides including those of major news agencies have adopted singular they for people who use it.
