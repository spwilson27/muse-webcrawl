Mario Fortino Alfonso Moreno Reyes (12 August 1911 – 20 April 1993), known as Cantinflas, was a Mexican comedian, actor and filmmaker considered the most widely accomplished Mexican comedic actor of his time and a pioneer of Mexican cinema. Charlie Chaplin called him the greatest comedian alive. His character — a poor, trousers-falling-down pelado who confounded authority with torrents of circular, evasive talk — gave Spanish the verb cantinflear, recognised by the Royal Spanish Academy.

Career

Moreno developed the Cantinflas persona in Mexico City's carpa (tent theatre) circuit. His film breakthrough came with Ahí está el detalle (1940), and dozens of comedies followed during the Golden Age of Mexican cinema. Internationally he played Passepartout opposite David Niven in Around the World in 80 Days (1956), winning a Golden Globe. He was also a labour leader in the Mexican actors' union and a noted philanthropist.

Legacy

Cantinflas remains a cultural icon across the Spanish-speaking world; his verbal style — saying much while saying nothing — is still invoked in politics, and his likeness appears in murals, statues and a 2014 biographical film.
