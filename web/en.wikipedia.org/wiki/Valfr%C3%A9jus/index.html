Valfréjus

Valfréjus is a ski resort in the Maurienne valley of the French Alps, in the commune of Modane, Savoie. Developed in the 1980s, it sits at about 1,550 metres above sea level and is known as a compact, family-oriented resort near the Italian border and the Fréjus Tunnel.

Ski area

The resort offers roughly 65 kilometres of pistes, served by gondolas and chairlifts rising to Punta Bagna at about 2,737 metres. Slopes run largely through larch forest, and the area is noted for off-piste and ski touring possibilities toward the Thabor massif.

Access

Valfréjus is reached from Modane, which has a station on the Paris–Turin railway through the Fréjus Rail Tunnel, and by motorway through the Fréjus Road Tunnel from Italy.
