The Alexander S. Onassis Public Benefit Foundation is a charitable foundation created under the will of the Greek shipping magnate Aristotle Onassis in memory of his son Alexander, who died in a plane crash in 1973 at the age of 24. Established in 1975 and based in Vaduz, Liechtenstein — with all its activities planned and administered from Athens — the foundation receives a share (40 percent) of the profits of the Onassis business empire and devotes them to public benefit projects in Greece and abroad.

Activities

The foundation works in culture, education, health, social solidarity and the environment. Its flagship cultural venue is the Onassis Stegi (Onassis Cultural Centre) in Athens, opened in 2010, hosting theatre, dance, music, visual arts and digital art. It funds extensive scholarship programmes for Greek and foreign students and researchers, supports cardiac surgery (the Onassis Cardiac Surgery Centre in Athens opened in 1993), and established affiliate foundations abroad, including Onassis USA in New York.

Prizes

The foundation awards international prizes in fields such as finance (the Onassis Prize in Finance), shipping and international law, in cooperation with academic institutions including the Bayes Business School in London.
