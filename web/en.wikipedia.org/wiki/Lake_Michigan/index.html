Lake Michigan is one of the five Great Lakes of North America. It is the second-largest of the Great Lakes by volume and the third-largest by surface area, after Lake Superior and Lake Huron. It is the only Great Lake located entirely within the United States, bordered by the states of Michigan, Wisconsin, Illinois and Indiana. Hydrologically it forms a single lake with Lake Huron, joined at the Straits of Mackinac.

The word "Michigan" originally referred to the lake itself and is believed to come from the Ojibwa word michigami, meaning "great water". Some 12 million people live along Lake Michigan's shores, chiefly in the Chicago and Milwaukee metropolitan areas.

Geography

The lake is about 494 km (307 mi) long and up to 190 km (118 mi) wide. Its main arms are Green Bay in the northwest and Grand Traverse Bay in the northeast. Islands include Beaver Island, the Manitou Islands and the Fox Islands in the north. The lake's southern basin is ringed by some of the largest freshwater dunes in the world, preserved in Indiana Dunes National Park and Sleeping Bear Dunes National Lakeshore.

History

Indigenous peoples, including the Hopewell tradition cultures, lived around the lake for millennia. French explorer Jean Nicolet reached Green Bay in 1634, among the first Europeans to see the lake. The lake ports grew with the fur trade, then with lumber, grain and iron shipping in the 19th century. Car ferries crossed the lake for over a century; the SS Badger still carries passengers between Ludington, Michigan, and Manitowoc, Wisconsin.

Environment and use

Lake Michigan is the water supply for Chicago and many suburbs; the Chicago diversion reverses the Chicago River away from the lake under a decree limiting withdrawal. Fisheries target lake trout, whitefish, perch and stocked salmon. Invasive mussels have clarified the water while disrupting the food web, and beach closings due to E. coli are a recurring summer issue in some locations. The shores support major tourism, especially along Michigan's west coast and Wisconsin's Door Peninsula.
