List of Solar System objects

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Solar System contains the Sun and everything bound to it by gravity: eight planets, their moons, dwarf planets, and millions of smaller bodies. This article lists Solar System objects — by orbit, by size and in related lists — including the planets and the largest moons, asteroids and trans-Neptunian objects. See also the Solar System overview article.

Contents1. By size
2. By orbit

By size

The largest objects are the Sun, then the gas giants Jupiter and Saturn, the ice giants Uranus and Neptune, Earth, Venus, Mars, and the moons Ganymede and Titan — both larger than the planet Mercury. The largest dwarf planets include Pluto, Eris, Haumea and Makemake.

By orbit

Lists are also organised by orbital region: inner planets, the asteroid belt (including Ceres), Jupiter's trojans, centaurs, the {W('Kuiper_belt')} and scattered disc, and comets. Separate lists catalog planets' natural satellites, minor planets by number, and the tallest mountains and largest craters in the Solar System.
