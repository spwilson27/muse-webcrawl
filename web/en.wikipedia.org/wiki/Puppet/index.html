Puppet

A puppet is an inanimate figure manipulated by a person, the puppeteer, to perform in theatre, film, television or ritual. Puppetry is an ancient art found in cultures worldwide.

Types

Major forms include glove or hand puppets; rod puppets; marionettes, controlled by strings from above; shadow puppets, flat figures casting shadows on a screen, as in Indonesian wayang; bunraku, large puppets operated visibly by three puppeteers in Japan; ventriloquist figures; and modern screen puppets such as Jim Henson's Muppets, built of foam and fleece and performed for camera.

History

Puppets appear in ancient Egypt, Greece, India and China, in medieval European fairs with characters such as Punch and Judy, and in religious and folk traditions. Today puppetry is used in entertainment, education and therapy.
