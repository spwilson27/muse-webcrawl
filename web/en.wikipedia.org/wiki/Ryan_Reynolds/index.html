Ryan Rodney Reynolds (born October 23, 1976) is a Canadian-American actor, producer, and businessman. He began his career starring in the Canadian teen soap opera Hillside (1991–1993) and had minor roles before landing the lead role on the sitcom Two Guys and a Girl (1998–2001). Reynolds then starred in a range of films, including the comedies National Lampoon's Van Wilder (2002), Waiting... (2005), and The Proposal (2009), and the drama Buried (2010).

His commercial success came with the superhero films Deadpool (2016) and Deadpool 2 (2018), in which he played the title character; the former set numerous records at the time of its release. He continued the role in the Marvel Cinematic Universe film Deadpool & Wolverine (2024), which became one of the highest-grossing films of all time. Reynolds has also starred in the action film Free Guy (2021) and voiced Pikachu in Pokémon Detective Pikachu (2019).

Early life

Reynolds was born in Vancouver, British Columbia, the youngest of four sons. His father was a Royal Canadian Mounted Police officer and later a food wholesaler; his mother worked in retail sales. He is of Irish and Scottish descent and was raised Roman Catholic. He graduated from Kitsilano Secondary School in 1994.

Business ventures

Outside acting, Reynolds has built a notable business career. He acquired an ownership stake in Aviation American Gin in 2018, which was sold to Diageo in 2020 in a deal worth up to $610 million, and held a stake in Mint Mobile, which was acquired by T-Mobile in 2023. Through his production and marketing company Maximum Effort, he has produced many of his films and advertising campaigns. In 2020, Reynolds and actor Rob McElhenney purchased the Welsh football club Wrexham A.F.C., whose progress was documented in the series Welcome to Wrexham.

Personal life

Reynolds married actress Scarlett Johansson in 2008; they divorced in 2011. He married actress Blake Lively in 2012, and they have four children. He became an American citizen in 2018. He has spoken publicly about his lifelong struggle with anxiety.
