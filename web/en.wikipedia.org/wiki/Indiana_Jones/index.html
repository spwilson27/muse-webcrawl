Indiana Jones

Indiana Jones is an American media franchise created by George Lucas, based on the adventures of the fictional archaeologist Dr. Henry "Indiana" Jones Jr., played by Harrison Ford. The franchise began with the film Raiders of the Lost Ark (1981), directed by Steven Spielberg, and grew into one of the most successful adventure series in cinema history.

Films

The film series comprises Raiders of the Lost Ark (1981), Indiana Jones and the Temple of Doom (1984), Indiana Jones and the Last Crusade (1989), Indiana Jones and the Kingdom of the Crystal Skull (2008) and Indiana Jones and the Dial of Destiny (2023). The films follow Jones racing rivals — often Nazis — to recover legendary artefacts such as the Ark of the Covenant and the Holy Grail.

Other media

The franchise also includes the television series The Young Indiana Jones Chronicles (1992–1993), novels, comics, theme park attractions and video games, from the graphic adventure Fate of Atlantis to Indiana Jones and the Great Circle (2024). John Williams composed the iconic "Raiders March".
