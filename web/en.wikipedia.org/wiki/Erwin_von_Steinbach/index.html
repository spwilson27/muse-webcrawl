Erwin von Steinbach

Erwin von Steinbach (c. 1244 – 17 January 1318) was a German architect and master mason best known as the designer of the west facade of Strasbourg Cathedral, one of the masterpieces of Gothic architecture.

Career

Erwin began work at Strasbourg around 1277, drawing on the design of Notre-Dame de Paris while developing the distinctive openwork and rose window of the facade. After his death, his sons and successors continued work on the cathedral according to his plans. A street in Strasbourg and the reputation celebrated by Goethe in his essay "On German Architecture" (1772) commemorate his genius.
