Shoreditch

Shoreditch is a district in the East End of London, within the London Borough of Hackney. It lies immediately north of the City of London, bordered by Hackney Road, Old Street, and Great Eastern Street. Once a working-class area of furniture makers and weavers, it has since the 1990s become a center for the creative and technology industries.

History

The name may derive from "Shore's Ditch" or from Jane Shore, mistress of Edward IV. Shoreditch was the site of two of England's first playhouses, The Theatre (1576) and the Curtain Theatre (1577), where some of Shakespeare's early plays were staged. Huguenot silk weavers settled nearby in Spitalfields in the 17th century. In the 19th century the area became associated with the furniture trade and with poverty documented by social reformers.

Modern Shoreditch

From the 1990s, artists, galleries, and bars transformed Shoreditch into a fashionable nightlife and arts district. The cluster of technology startups around Old Street roundabout became known as "Silicon Roundabout." Gentrification has raised rents and displaced some long-standing communities. Transport links include Shoreditch High Street station on the London Overground (opened 2010), Old Street, and Liverpool Street nearby.
