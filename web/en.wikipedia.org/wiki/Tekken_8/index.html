Tekken 8 (Japanese: 鉄拳8) is a 2024 fighting game developed by Bandai Namco Studios and Arika and published by Bandai Namco Entertainment. It is the eighth main instalment — and tenth overall entry — in the Tekken series, and was released for PlayStation 5, Windows, and Xbox Series X/S on 26 January 2024. Built on Unreal Engine 5, it is the first mainline entry developed exclusively for modern hardware.

Gameplay

Tekken 8 retains the series' 3D, four-button fighting system but shifts emphasis toward aggressive play. Its central new mechanic is the Heat system: once per round a fighter can activate Heat to gain enhanced moves, chip damage on block, and access to a powerful Heat Smash. The Rage system from Tekken 7 returns in simplified form as Rage Arts. The game introduced a "Special Style" simplified control scheme aimed at newcomers, alongside returning modes such as Arcade Quest — a single-player mode in which a custom avatar tours arcades and learns the game's systems — plus character episodes, Tekken Ball, and the cinematic Story Mode, "The Dark Awakens". Online play uses rollback netcode and supports cross-platform matchmaking.

Plot

The story continues the Mishima saga. Six months after the events of Tekken 7, Jin Kazama, having learned to control his Devil Gene with the help of his mother Jun Kazama's influence, confronts his father Kazuya Mishima, who has seized control of G Corporation and plunged the world into war. Jin rallies allies including Lars Alexandersson, Alisa Bosconovitch, and Ling Xiaoyu, while the revived Jun plays a central role in the final confrontation, which culminates in a father–son battle that resolves the long-running feud between Jin and Kazuya.

Roster

The base game launched with 32 playable characters, mixing veterans such as Paul Phoenix, Marshall Law, Nina Williams, King, Hwoarang, and Yoshimitsu with newcomers Azucena Ortiz, a Peruvian MMA fighter and coffee ambassador; Victor Chevalier, a French UN special forces founder voiced by Vincent Cassel; and Reina, a mysterious fighter connected to the Mishima line. Additional characters, including Eddy Gordo, Lidia Sobieska, Heihachi Mishima, and guest character Clive Rosfield from Final Fantasy XVI, were added through downloadable content seasons.

Reception

The game received widespread critical acclaim for its visuals, content-rich single-player offerings, and accessible yet deep gameplay, and it sold over two million copies within its first month. It won Best Fighting Game at The Game Awards 2024.
