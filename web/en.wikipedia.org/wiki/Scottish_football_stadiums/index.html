List of football stadiums in Scotland

Scotland's football stadiums range from Hampden Park, the national stadium in Glasgow, to small community grounds used by clubs in the Highland and Lowland leagues. The three largest club grounds — Celtic Park, Ibrox Stadium and Hampden itself — are all in Glasgow and are among the largest stadiums in the United Kingdom. Following the Taylor Report and Scottish regulations of the 1990s, senior grounds became all-seater, later supplemented by licensing requirements of the Scottish Professional Football League.

History

Many Scottish grounds date from the late Victorian football boom; Hampden Park was for decades among the largest stadiums in the world and hosted record crowds, including 149,415 for Scotland v England in 1937, still a European record attendance. Stadium disasters at Ibrox in 1902 and 1971 spurred successive safety reforms, culminating in all-seater requirements and modern safety certification administered by local authorities under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act.
