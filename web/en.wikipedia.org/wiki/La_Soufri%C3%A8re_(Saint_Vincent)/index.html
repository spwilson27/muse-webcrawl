La Soufrière (Saint Vincent)

La Soufrière
Elevation: 1,234 m (4,049 ft)
Type: Stratovolcano
Location: Saint Vincent, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines

La Soufrière is an active stratovolcano on the Caribbean island of Saint Vincent in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines. At 1,234 metres it is the island's highest peak. It is one of the most closely monitored volcanoes in the Caribbean.

Eruptive history

Major eruptions occurred in 1718, 1812, 1902, 1971, 1979, and 2020–21. The 1902 eruption, on the eve of the eruption of Mount Pelée on Martinique, killed around 1,600 people. The 1979 eruption prompted the evacuation of some 20,000 people but caused no deaths. In December 2020 the volcano entered a new eruptive phase; explosive eruptions beginning 9 April 2021 displaced about 20,000 residents and blanketed the island in ash, but again caused no direct fatalities.

Crater lake

Between eruptions the crater held a lake, famously containing a growing lava dome island after the 1971 effusive eruption.
