Central Committee of the Chinese Communist Party

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Central Committee of the Chinese Communist Party, officially the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China, is the highest organ of the Chinese Communist Party (CCP) between national congresses. According to the party's constitution, it carries out the resolutions of the National Congress, directs the party's work, and represents the party externally. It consists of full members and alternate members, elected for five-year terms by the National Congress.

The Central Committee is large — the 20th Central Committee, elected in 2022, has 205 full members and 171 alternate members — and meets in plenary sessions (plenums) at least once a year. In practice, day-to-day authority rests with bodies it elects: the Politburo, the Politburo Standing Committee, and the General Secretary, who convenes its meetings.

Functions

At its first plenum after a National Congress, the Central Committee elects the Politburo, the Politburo Standing Committee, the General Secretary, and the Secretariat, and approves the leadership of the Central Military Commission and the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection. Subsequent plenums address major policy questions; for example, third plenums traditionally focus on economic reform, and the 1978 Third Plenary Session of the 11th Central Committee launched the reform and opening up era under Deng Xiaoping.

Membership

Members are typically senior party, state, military and provincial officials. Alternate members attend plenums without voting rights and are elevated to full membership when vacancies arise. Members are nominally elected by the National Congress from a list of candidates modestly larger than the number of seats, in a process managed by the outgoing leadership.

Departments

The Central Committee works through functional departments, including the Organization Department (personnel), the Publicity Department (propaganda), the United Front Work Department, the International Department, and the General Office, which handles the committee's daily administration.
