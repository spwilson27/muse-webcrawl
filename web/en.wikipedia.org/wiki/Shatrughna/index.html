Shatrughna

Shatrughna (Sanskrit: शत्रुघ्न, romanized: Śatrughna) is a figure in the Hindu epic Ramayana. He is the youngest son of King Dasharatha of Ayodhya and his wife Sumitra, the twin brother of Lakshmana, and the younger half-brother of Rama and Bharata.

Role in the Ramayana

Shatrughna was especially devoted to Bharata, as Lakshmana was to Rama. He married Shrutakirti, a cousin of Sita. When Bharata ruled Ayodhya as regent during Rama's exile, Shatrughna assisted him in administering the kingdom.

In the later book of the epic, Shatrughna is sent by Rama to slay the demon Lavanasura, who ruled at Madhuvana (Mathura). He defeated the demon and founded or repopulated the city of Mathura, later dividing his kingdom between his sons. According to tradition, Shatrughna entered the Sarayu river alongside Rama at the end of Rama's reign.
