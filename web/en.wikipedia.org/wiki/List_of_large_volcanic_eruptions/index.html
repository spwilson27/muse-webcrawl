List of large volcanic eruptions

This is a list of large volcanic eruptions, ranked using the Volcanic Explosivity Index (VEI), which is based mainly on the volume of erupted material. The largest eruptions in recorded history reached VEI 7; prehistoric caldera-forming "supereruptions" reached VEI 8, ejecting more than 1,000 km³ of material.
