Astronomy & Astrophysics

Astronomy & Astrophysics (A&A) is a monthly peer-reviewed scientific journal covering theoretical, observational, and instrumental astronomy and astrophysics. It is one of the major astronomy journals in the world and is owned by the European Southern Observatory's member states through a board of directors representing the sponsoring countries; it is published by EDP Sciences.

History

Astronomy & Astrophysics was created in 1969 through the merger of several national European journals, including the French Annales d'Astrophysique, the Bulletin of the Astronomical Institutes of the Netherlands, the German Zeitschrift für Astrophysik, and Scandinavian and other titles. The merger reflected a deliberate effort to give European astronomy a single strong international journal. A separate Astronomy and Astrophysics Supplement Series, devoted to tabular data and catalogues, was published until 2001, when it was folded into the main journal in electronic form.

Scope and structure

The journal is organised into sections covering subjects such as astrophysical processes, cosmology, extragalactic astronomy, galactic astronomy, stellar structure and evolution, solar and interplanetary physics, planetary systems, and astronomical instrumentation. It publishes research articles, letters to the editor for rapid communication, and research notes. Articles are frequently accompanied by online-only supplementary material including large catalogues.

Publishing model

A&A is unusual among major journals in being owned by its community of sponsoring countries rather than by a commercial publisher. Authors from sponsoring countries historically published without page charges, supported by subscriptions. The journal moved to an open-access model in the 2020s under agreements negotiated with EDP Sciences.
