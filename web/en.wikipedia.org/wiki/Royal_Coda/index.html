Royal Coda

OriginSacramento, California, US
GenresPost-hardcore, progressive rock, dance-punk
Years active2016–present
LabelsBlue Swan Records, Equal Vision Records

Royal Coda is an American post-hardcore supergroup formed in Sacramento, California in 2016. The band features vocalist Kurt Travis, formerly of Dance Gavin Dance, alongside guitarist Sergio Medina and drummer Joseph Arrington, both known for their work in Stolas and Sianvar.

History

The group released its self-titled debut album, Royal Coda, in April 2018 through Blue Swan Records. The record blended progressive rock instrumentation with soulful, R&B-influenced vocals. A second album, Compassion, followed in 2019, continuing the band's intricate guitar work and shifting time signatures. Bassist Steffen Gotsch and guitarist Will Swan of Dance Gavin Dance have also been associated with the project's recordings.

Musical style

Royal Coda's sound combines math-rock guitar tapping, funk rhythms and melodic post-hardcore, frequently compared to the "Swancore" scene centred on Blue Swan Records.
