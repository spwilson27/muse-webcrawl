Castello Orsini-Odescalchi

Castello Orsini-Odescalchi is a castle in Bracciano, Italy, on the southern shore of Lake Bracciano about 30 km northwest of Rome. Built by the Orsini family from 1470 under Napoleone Orsini, it is one of the largest and best-preserved Renaissance castles in Italy.

History

The fortress passed to the Odescalchi family in 1696. Its frescoed halls hosted popes and kings, and it remains privately owned by the Odescalchi, operating as a museum and prestigious wedding venue — notably for celebrity weddings that drew international press attention.

Architecture

Pentagonal walls with massive round towers surround courtyards and apartments decorated with frescoes by Antoniazzo Romano and the Zuccari school. See Bracciano and Orsini family.
