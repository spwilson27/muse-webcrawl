Duke of Wellington is a title in the Peerage of the United Kingdom, created in 1814 for Arthur Wellesley, the victor of the Peninsular War. A year later he defeated Napoleon at the Battle of Waterloo (18 June 1815). The family seat is Stratfield Saye House in Hampshire, purchased with funds voted by Parliament; the London residence is Apsley House, known as "No. 1, London".

History

The 1st Duke later served twice as Prime Minister. The Wellesley family also holds the Spanish dukedom of Ciudad Rodrigo and titles in the Netherlands and Portugal granted for the Duke's victories. The dukedom descends in the male line to the present day.

The current holder is Charles Wellesley, 9th Duke of Wellington. The heir apparent is Arthur Wellesley, Earl of Mornington.
