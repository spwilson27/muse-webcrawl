Kashta

Kashta was an 8th-century BCE king of the Kingdom of Kush in Nubia (present-day Sudan). He expanded Kushite influence into Upper Egypt and laid the foundations for his son Piye's conquest of Egypt, which established the 25th Dynasty.

Reign

Kashta ruled from Napata near Gebel Barkal. He peacefully extended control to Elephantine and Thebes, where his daughter Amenirdis I was installed as heiress to the Divine Worshipper of Amun, giving Kush religious legitimacy in Upper Egypt.

Family and burial

His wife was Pebatjma; his children included kings Piye and Shabaka. Kashta was buried in a pyramid at El-Kurru, the Kushite royal cemetery.
