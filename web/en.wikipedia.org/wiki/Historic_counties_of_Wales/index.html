Historic counties of Wales

The historic counties of Wales are the thirteen ancient counties established by the Laws in Wales Acts 1535 and 1542: Anglesey, Brecknockshire, Caernarvonshire, Carmarthenshire, Cardiganshire, Denbighshire, Flintshire, Glamorgan, Merionethshire, Monmouthshire, Montgomeryshire, Pembrokeshire, and Radnorshire.

Use

They were administrative counties until 1974, when they were replaced by eight new counties. The historic counties remain used for cultural and ceremonial purposes and as the basis of the preserved counties of Wales, though boundaries differ in places.
