"Truth Hurts" is a song by American singer and rapper Lizzo. Originally released as a standalone single in September 2017, it became a sleeper hit two years later after going viral on TikTok and appearing in the film Someone Great; it was then added to the deluxe edition of Lizzo's third studio album, Cuz I Love You (2019). The song reached number one on the US Billboard Hot 100, spending seven weeks at the top.

Composition

"Truth Hurts" is a bouncy hip hop track built around a bright piano loop. Its opening line — "I just took a DNA test, turns out I'm 100% that bitch" — adapted a viral tweet by singer Mina Lioness, and became one of the most quoted lyrics of 2019. The song's theme of confident self-worth after a breakup anchored Lizzo's breakthrough era of body-positive pop.

Commercial performance

At seven weeks atop the Hot 100, "Truth Hurts" tied the record for the longest-running number-one single by a female rapper at the time. It won the Grammy Award for Best Pop Solo Performance at the 62nd Grammy Awards in 2020 and was certified multi-platinum in several countries.

Authorship dispute

The song was the subject of a public authorship controversy: songwriters Justin and Jeremiah Raisen claimed they had co-written its key line in a 2017 session, while Lizzo maintained the line derived from the Mina Lioness tweet, whom she later credited and shared proceeds with after litigation and public discussion.
