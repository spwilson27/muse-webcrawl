Sichuan Giant Panda Sanctuaries
LocationSichuan, China
Area924,500 ha
UNESCO World Heritage SiteInscribed 2006 (natural)

The Sichuan Giant Panda Sanctuaries, located in southwest Sichuan province, China, are home to more than 30% of the world's population of the rare giant panda. The sanctuaries cover 924,500 hectares across seven nature reserves and nine scenic parks in the Qionglai and Jiajin Mountains.

Description

The reserves include Wolong, Mount Siguniang, and the Jiajin Mountains. In addition to giant pandas, the region shelters red pandas, snow leopards, and clouded leopards, and is botanically among the richest sites outside tropical rainforests, with between 5,000 and 6,000 plant species.

Conservation

Inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2006, the sanctuaries support captive breeding centres, most famously at Wolong, that have contributed to panda conservation and reintroduction research.
