List of parks and gardens in Paris

Paris contains more than 400 parks and gardens covering roughly 3,000 hectares, including two great historic woods, the Bois de Boulogne and the Bois de Vincennes. Many of the best-known gardens date from the 17th century or from Baron Haussmann's renovation under Napoleon III.

History

The oldest garden in Paris is the Jardin des Plantes, founded as a royal medicinal herb garden in 1626. The Tuileries and Luxembourg gardens followed in the 17th century. Under Napoleon III and Haussmann, the Bois de Boulogne and Bois de Vincennes were landscaped in the English style and many neighbourhood squares were created.
