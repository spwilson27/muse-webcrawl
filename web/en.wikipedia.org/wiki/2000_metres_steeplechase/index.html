2000 metres steeplechase

The 2000 metres steeplechase is a track and field event combining middle-distance running with barrier and water jump obstacles. It is a shorter version of the standard 3000 metres steeplechase, featuring 18 barriers and 5 water jumps (or variations depending on the track layout and whether the water jump is inside or outside the track bend).

Overview

The event is not part of the Olympic or World Championship programme, where the 3000 metres steeplechase is standard, but it is contested at some international meetings and is used for youth and developmental competition. World Athletics recognises world records in the event, though they are classified as world bests in some periods of ratification history.

Records

As of the mid-2020s, the men's world best stood to France's Mahiedine Mekhissi-Benabbad, who ran 5:10.68 in Reims in 2010, while the women's mark was set by Gesa Felicitas Krause of Germany with 5:52.80 in Berlin in 2019. Kenyan and Ethiopian athletes, dominant in the longer event, have also recorded many of the fastest times at this distance.
