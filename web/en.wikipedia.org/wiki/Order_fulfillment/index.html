Order fulfillment

Order fulfillment is the complete process of receiving, processing and delivering customer orders. In its broadest sense it runs from the point of sale enquiry to delivery of the product, encompassing order entry, inventory management, picking, packing, shipping and returns handling.

Process

A typical fulfillment workflow begins when an order is received and checked against available stock. Items are picked from warehouse locations, packed and labelled, then handed to a carrier for delivery. Modern operations use warehouse management systems, barcode or RFID scanning, and automation to raise speed and accuracy.

Models

Companies may fulfil orders in-house, outsource to third-party logistics providers (3PLs) that run fulfillment centres, or use drop shipping, in which the supplier ships directly to the customer. Fast, accurate fulfillment became a competitive priority with the growth of e-commerce.
