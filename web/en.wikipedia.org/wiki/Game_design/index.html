Game design

Game design is the process of creating the content and rules of a game in the pre-production stage, and the gameplay, environment, storyline and characters in the production stage. The game designer, like a film director, is the visionary who invents and oversees the game's concepts.

Overview

Game design applies to video games, board games, card games, dice games, role-playing games and sports. The designer decides what a player can do, what challenges they face, how the system responds, and what makes the experience meaningful. A widely used formal framework is the MDA framework — Mechanics, Dynamics, Aesthetics — which analyses how rules generate emergent behaviour and how that behaviour produces emotional responses in players.

Elements

  • Mechanics — the rules and procedures: movement, resources, chance, victory conditions.
  • Systems and balance — how mechanics interact, and tuning of difficulty, economies and progression.
  • Level design — constructing the spaces and encounters in which play happens.
  • Narrative design — integrating story, characters and world, including environmental storytelling.
  • User experience — feedback, onboarding and tutorials, and the "game feel" of moment-to-moment play.
  • Play-testing and iteration — the discipline's core method: build, test with real players, revise.

Process

Design typically begins with a concept and a prototype — sometimes paper, sometimes code — to test the "core loop" of moment-to-moment activity. Decisions are recorded in a game design document (GDD), though modern practice favours living documents and rapid iteration. Designers draw on psychology — notably flow theory, the state of absorption produced when challenge matches skill — and on the theory of fun articulated by designers such as Raph Koster.

History

Though games are ancient, game design as a profession dates mainly from the 1970s, with the rise of commercial video games and tabletop role-playing games. Influential designers include Shigeru Miyamoto (Super Mario Bros., The Legend of Zelda), Sid Meier (Civilization), Will Wright (SimCity, The Sims) and Richard Garfield (Magic: The Gathering).
