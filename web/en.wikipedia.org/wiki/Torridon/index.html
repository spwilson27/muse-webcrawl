Torridon

TorridonCountry: Scotland
Council area: Highland
Region: Wester Ross

Torridon is a small village in Wester Ross in the Northwest Highlands of Scotland, and also the name of the surrounding area of mountains and sea lochs between Loch Maree and Loch Torridon.

Geography

The Torridon Hills are formed of ancient Torridonian sandstone resting on Lewisian gneiss, among the oldest rocks in Britain. Peaks such as Liathach, Beinn Eighe and Beinn Alligin are celebrated among hillwalkers and climbers for their steep terraced profiles.

Conservation

Much of the estate is owned by the National Trust for Scotland, and Beinn Eighe was designated Britain's first National Nature Reserve in 1951.

The village

The village of Torridon sits at the head of Upper Loch Torridon and serves walkers, with a youth hostel, inn and visitor centre. The single-track A896 road links it to Kinlochewe and Shieldaig.
