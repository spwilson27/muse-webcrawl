Guy Maurice Marie Louise Verhofstadt (born 11 April 1953) is a Belgian politician who served as Prime Minister of Belgium from 1999 to 2008 and as a Member of the European Parliament from 2009 to 2024, where he led the Alliance of Liberals and Democrats for Europe (ALDE) group.

Verhofstadt in the European Parliament

Career

A lawyer from Ghent, Verhofstadt entered politics with the Flemish liberals, becoming party president at a young age and earning the nickname "Baby Thatcher" for his free-market manifestos of the 1980s. He served as deputy prime minister and budget minister in the 1980s before leading two "purple" coalition governments of liberals, socialists and greens from 1999, overseeing economic modernisation, Belgium's adoption of the euro-era budgets, and liberal social legislation including the legalisation of euthanasia and same-sex marriage.

In the European Parliament, Verhofstadt became one of the most prominent advocates of deeper European integration and a federal Europe, and served as the Parliament's Brexit coordinator during the United Kingdom's withdrawal from the European Union. He is the author of several books on European politics and co-founded the Spinelli Group of federalist MEPs.
