Persona 5 Strikers, released in Japan as Persona 5 Scramble: The Phantom Strikers, is an action role-playing game developed by Omega Force and P-Studio and published by Atlus. It is a crossover between Koei Tecmo's Dynasty Warriors series and Atlus's Persona series, and a direct sequel to the 2016 role-playing game Persona 5.

Story and gameplay

Set six months after the events of Persona 5, the game follows the Phantom Thieves of Hearts on a summer road trip across Japan, investigating a series of mysterious incidents in which people's desires are stolen through distorted cognitive worlds called Jails, aided by a sentient artificial intelligence named Sophia and a police officer, Zenkichi Hasegawa. In contrast to the turn-based battles of the main series, combat takes place in real time with hack-and-slash mechanics against large groups of Shadows, while retaining series elements such as Persona summoning, the fusion system, and turn-based pause for skill selection.

Reception

The game received generally favourable reviews, with critics praising its faithful recreation of the world and characters of Persona 5 and its story as a worthy sequel, though some criticised repetitive combat. It shipped over 1.5 million copies worldwide across all platforms.
