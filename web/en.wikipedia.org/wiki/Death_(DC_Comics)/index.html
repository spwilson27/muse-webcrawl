Death (DC Comics)

Death is a fictional character appearing in comic books published by DC Comics, created by writer Neil Gaiman and artist Mike Dringenberg. She first appeared in The Sandman vol. 2, #8 (August 1989). Death is one of the Endless, a family of seven anthropomorphic personifications of fundamental metaphysical concepts, and is the elder sister of Dream (Morpheus).

Character

Unlike most grim depictions of death, Gaiman's Death is a cheerful, compassionate young woman, typically dressed in black goth fashion with a silver ankh pendant and an Eye of Horus marking around her right eye. She guides the souls of the dead and visits every living being twice: at birth and at death. She is consistently portrayed as one of the most powerful and kindest of the Endless.

In other media

Death has appeared in spin-off miniseries including Death: The High Cost of Living (1993) and Death: The Time of Your Life (1996), and is portrayed by Kirby Howell-Baptiste in the Netflix adaptation of The Sandman (2022).
