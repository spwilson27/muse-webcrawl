Time Warner

Time Warner Inc. was an American multinational mass media and entertainment conglomerate headquartered in New York City. It was formed in 1990 by the merger of Time Inc. and Warner Communications, and at its height was one of the world's largest media companies, with holdings including Warner Bros., HBO, CNN, Turner Broadcasting, and Time Warner Cable.

History

In 2000 the company merged with AOL to form AOL Time Warner, a deal widely regarded as one of the most disastrous in corporate history; the AOL name was dropped in 2003, and AOL and Time Warner Cable were later spun off (both in 2009), as was Time Inc. in 2014.

Acquisition

AT&T announced its acquisition of Time Warner in October 2016; after a federal antitrust trial, the deal closed in June 2018 for about $85 billion, and the company was renamed WarnerMedia. AT&T later spun WarnerMedia off to merge with Discovery, forming Warner Bros. Discovery in 2022.
