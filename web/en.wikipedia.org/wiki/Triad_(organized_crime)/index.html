Triad (organized crime)

A triad (Chinese: 三合會, "Three Harmonies Society") is a transnational organized crime syndicate originating in China and based in Hong Kong, Taiwan, mainland China, and Chinese diaspora communities worldwide.

History

Triads trace their origins to secret societies of the Qing dynasty, such as the Heaven and Earth Society (Tiandihui), which mixed resistance to the Qing with mutual aid and criminal enterprise. The name "triad" was coined by British colonial authorities in Hong Kong from the societies' triangular symbols and the union of heaven, earth, and man.

Organization and activities

Major groups have included the Sun Yee On, 14K, and Wo Shing Wo. Triads traditionally used elaborate initiation rituals and a numeric rank system (such as 489 for the "dragon head" leader and 426 for enforcers). Their activities include drug trafficking (historically heroin), extortion, gambling, loan-sharking, smuggling, counterfeiting, and human trafficking, though modern triads are typically looser networks of criminal entrepreneurs than rigid hierarchies.
