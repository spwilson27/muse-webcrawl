John Dykstra

John Charles Dykstra, ASC (born 3 June 1947) is an American special effects artist, pioneer in the use of computers in filmmaking, and best known as the original special effects supervisor for Star Wars (1977), for which he developed the revolutionary Dykstraflex motion control camera system.

Early career

Dykstra studied industrial design at California State University, Long Beach. He worked with Douglas Trumbull on Silent Running (1972) before being recruited by George Lucas to head the visual effects team for Star Wars.

Dykstraflex and ILM

At the newly founded Industrial Light & Magic, Dykstra led the development of the Dykstraflex, a computer-controlled camera system that allowed repeatable, complex camera moves around miniature models. The system enabled the dynamic space battles of Star Wars and won Dykstra an Academy Award for Best Visual Effects.

Later work

Dykstra went on to supervise effects for Battlestar Galactica, Star Trek: The Motion Picture, Firefox, Spider-Man and Spider-Man 2 (for which he won a second Academy Award), X-Men: First Class, and Once Upon a Time in Hollywood.
