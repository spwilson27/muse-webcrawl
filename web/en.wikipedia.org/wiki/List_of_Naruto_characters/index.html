List of Naruto characters

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Naruto manga and anime series, created by Masashi Kishimoto, features an extensive cast of characters in a fictional world of ninja villages. Most are ninja from the Hidden Leaf Village (Konohagakure), where the protagonist Naruto Uzumaki — host of the Nine-Tailed Fox, Kurama — seeks to become Hokage.

Hokage

  • Hashirama Senju — First Hokage, co-founder of the Leaf, famed for Wood Release.
  • Tobirama Senju — Second Hokage, creator of many jutsu including the Flying Thunder God and Reanimation.
  • Hiruzen Sarutobi — Third Hokage, "the Professor", teacher of the Sannin.
  • Minato Namikaze — Fourth Hokage, the "Yellow Flash", Naruto's father, who sealed Kurama into his son.
  • Tsunade — Fifth Hokage, one of the Sannin and the greatest medical ninja.
  • Kakashi Hatake — Sixth Hokage.
  • Naruto Uzumaki — Seventh Hokage.

The Sannin and mentors

  • Jiraiya — The "Toad Sage", one of the legendary Sannin, Naruto's godfather and mentor; killed by Pain.
  • Tsunade — Sannin and Fifth Hokage (see above).
  • Orochimaru — Sannin who defected to pursue immortality and all jutsu; a major antagonist of Part I.
