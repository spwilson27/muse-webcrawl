1. FSV Mainz 05

1. Fußball- und Sportverein Mainz 05 e. V., commonly known as Mainz 05 or simply Mainz, is a German sports club founded in 1905 and based in Mainz, Rhineland-Palatinate. Its football team plays in the Bundesliga, the top tier of German football, and hosts home matches at the Mewa Arena.

History

Mainz spent most of its history in the regional and second divisions. The club's modern era began under manager Jürgen Klopp, who led Mainz to its first Bundesliga promotion in 2004. After relegation in 2007, the club returned to the top flight in 2009 under Jørn Andersen and Thomas Tuchel, and has since been a largely consistent Bundesliga side, qualifying for the UEFA Europa League on several occasions, including a group-stage appearance in the 2016–17 season.

The club is nicknamed die Nullfünfer (the 05ers) and is known in Germany as a Karnevalsverein ("carnival club") because Mainz is a centre of the Rhineland carnival tradition. Its principal rivalry is with Eintracht Frankfurt, contested in the Rhine-Main derby.

Stadium

Mainz played for most of its history at the Stadion am Bruchweg before moving in 2011 to the newly built arena now known as the Mewa Arena, with a capacity of about 33,000.
