Roussillon wine

Roussillon wine is produced in the Roussillon region of southern France, in the Pyrénées-Orientales department near the Spanish border and the Mediterranean. Together with neighbouring Languedoc it forms the Languedoc-Roussillon wine region.

History and climate

Viticulture dates to Greek colonists. The hot, dry, sunny Mediterranean climate, swept by the Tramontane wind, favours ripe grapes and low disease pressure.

Wines

Roussillon is especially known for vins doux naturels — fortified sweet wines such as Banyuls, Maury and Rivesaltes — made mainly from Grenache. Dry wines are produced under appellations including Côtes du Roussillon and Collioure, from Grenache, Syrah, Mourvèdre, Carignan and white varieties such as Grenache blanc and Macabeu.
