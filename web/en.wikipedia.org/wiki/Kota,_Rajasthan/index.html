Kota is a city in the southeastern part of the Indian state of Rajasthan, situated on the banks of the Chambal River. It is the third most populous city of Rajasthan and a major centre for industry and education.

History

Kota was once part of Bundi state; in 1624 it became the capital of the separate princely state of Kotah under the Hada Chauhan Rajputs. The Maharao's palace and the city's gardens date largely from this era.

Education hub

Since the 1990s Kota has become famous across India for its coaching institutes preparing students for engineering and medical entrance examinations such as the JEE and NEET, drawing hundreds of thousands of students each year.

Industry and landmarks

The city is known for Kota stone, a fine-grained limestone used in construction, and for chemical and power industries. Landmarks include the Chambal Garden, Seven Wonders Park and the Kota Barrage.
