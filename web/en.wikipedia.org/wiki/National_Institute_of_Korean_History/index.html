National Institute of Korean History

Founded: 1946
Location: Gwacheon, South Korea
Type: Government research institute
Parent: Ministry of Education

The National Institute of Korean History (NIKH; Korean: 국사편찬위원회) is a South Korean government body responsible for researching, compiling and publishing the history of Korea.

History and functions

Founded in 1946, shortly after liberation from Japanese rule, the institute collects historical records, publishes source compilations such as the Joseon Wangjo Sillok (Annals of the Joseon Dynasty) in accessible editions, and operates digital archives including the Korean History Database.

Textbook controversy

The institute was at the centre of a major political controversy in 2015–2017, when the government tasked it with producing a single state-authored history textbook for schools; the plan provoked strong opposition from historians and the public and was scrapped after a change of government.
