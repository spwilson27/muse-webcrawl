Silent Hill

Silent Hill is a Japanese horror media franchise created by Konami. Centred on a series of survival horror video games, it is set largely in the fictional American town of Silent Hill, a place shrouded in fog and periodically overtaken by a hellish "Otherworld," where the protagonists' psyches and guilt shape the monsters they face.

Games

The first Silent Hill was released for the PlayStation in 1999, developed by Team Silent. Harry Mason searches the town for his missing adopted daughter Cheryl, uncovering the activities of a cult, the Order. Silent Hill 2 (2001), widely considered the series' peak and one of the greatest video games ever made, follows James Sunderland, drawn to the town by a letter from his deceased wife Mary, and introduced the iconic monster Pyramid Head. Silent Hill 3 (2003) continued the cult storyline; Silent Hill 4: The Room (2004) was the last Team Silent entry. Later Western-developed titles included Homecoming, Downpour, and the acclaimed but delisted playable teaser P.T. (2014). A remake of Silent Hill 2 by Bloober Team was released in 2024.

Themes and style

The series is distinguished by psychological horror rather than jump scares: industrial and organic decay, radio static that signals nearby monsters, music and sound design by Akira Yamaoka, and multiple endings depending on the player's actions. The town itself is said to be influenced by Centralia, Pennsylvania, a real town emptied by an underground mine fire.

Films

Christophe Gans directed a film adaptation in 2006, followed by Silent Hill: Revelation (2012); a further film, Return to Silent Hill, has been announced.
