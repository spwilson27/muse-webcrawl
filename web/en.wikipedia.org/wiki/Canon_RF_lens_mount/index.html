Canon RF lens mount

Specifications
Introduced: 2018
Throat diameter: 54 mm
Flange focal distance: 20 mm
Predecessor: Canon EF mount

The Canon RF lens mount is an interchangeable-lens mount developed by Canon for its full-frame mirrorless cameras. Introduced in September 2018 with the Canon EOS R, it succeeded the EF mount used on Canon's SLR and DSLR cameras since 1987.

Design

The RF mount retains the EF mount's 54 mm throat diameter but reduces the flange focal distance from 44 mm to 20 mm, allowing more compact bodies and greater freedom in optical design, including very wide apertures such as the RF 28–70mm f/2L USM and RF 50mm f/1.2L USM. The mount uses a 12-pin electronic connection (up from 8 on EF), enabling faster communication between lens and body, a customisable control ring on many lenses, and improved in-body image stabilisation coordination.

Compatibility

EF and EF-S lenses can be used on RF bodies with Canon's EF–EOS R mount adapters, which preserve autofocus and aperture control; variants include a control-ring adapter and a drop-in filter adapter. RF lenses cannot be used on EF bodies. Canon also produces RF-S lenses designed for its APS-C mirrorless bodies such as the EOS R7 and R10, introduced from 2022.

Third-party lenses

For several years Canon restricted autofocus RF lenses by third-party manufacturers, leading some makers to withdraw products; from 2024 Sigma and Tamron began releasing licensed RF-S lenses. Manual-focus RF lenses from various makers have been available throughout.
