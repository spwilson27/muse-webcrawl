Wolverhampton Wanderers F.C.

Wolverhampton Wanderers Football Club, commonly known as Wolves, is a professional football club based in Wolverhampton, England. Founded in 1877 as St Luke's, the club plays at Molineux Stadium and competes in the Premier League.

History

Wolves were founder members of the Football League in 1888 and won the FA Cup in 1893 and 1908. Under manager Stan Cullis in the 1950s the club enjoyed its most successful era, winning the league title in 1953–54, 1957–58 and 1958–59 and the FA Cup in 1949 and 1960. Famous floodlit friendlies against European sides such as Honvéd helped inspire the creation of the European Cup.

Later years

The club won the League Cup in 1974 and 1980 but suffered financial collapse and successive relegations in the 1980s. Wolves returned to the top flight in 2003 and again in 2009. After relegation to League One in 2013, the club recovered, and under Chinese owners Fosun and manager Nuno Espírito Santo won the Championship in 2018 and reached the Europa League quarter-finals in 2020.

Colours and identity

Wolves' traditional colours are old gold shirts with black shorts. The club badge features a wolf's head. Their main rivals are West Bromwich Albion, with whom they contest the Black Country derby.

Honours

Major honours include three First Division titles, four FA Cups, two League Cups, and the 1972 UEFA Cup runners-up finish.
