Seminole County is a county located in the central portion of the U.S. state of Florida. As of the 2020 census, its population was 484,271, making it one of the most densely populated counties in Florida. Its county seat and largest city is Sanford. The county is part of the Orlando–Kissimmee–Sanford Metropolitan Statistical Area.

History

Seminole County was created on April 25, 1913, out of the northern portion of Orange County. It was named for the Seminole people who historically lived in the region, including during the Seminole Wars of the 19th century. The St. Johns River and Lake Monroe made Sanford an important river port in the steamboat era, and the area became a centre of citrus production before suburban growth transformed it in the late 20th century.

Geography and communities

The county covers about 345 square miles, including lakes and wetlands such as Lake Jesup and the Econlockhatchee River. Incorporated cities include Altamonte Springs, Casselberry, Lake Mary, Longwood, Oviedo, Sanford and Winter Springs. The county is known for its highly rated public schools and its suburban office parks along the Interstate 4 corridor.

Economy

Major employers include financial services, healthcare and technology firms, with several corporate offices concentrated in Lake Mary and Heathrow. The Orlando Sanford International Airport lies within the county.
