The Creatures were an English band formed in 1981 by Siouxsie Sioux and Budgie, the singer and drummer of Siouxsie and the Banshees. As a side project during a Banshees hiatus, the duo explored a stripped-down, percussion-heavy sound quite different from their main band.

Music

Their debut EP Wild Things (1981) was followed by the album Feast (1983), recorded partly in Hawaii and featuring the top-20 single "Miss the Girl". The second album, Boomerang (1989), recorded in Spain, blended flamenco, mariachi, and blues influences and is often regarded as their finest work. Later releases included Anima Animus (1999) and Hái! (2003), the latter drawing on Budgie's collaboration with taiko drumming.

Legacy

The Creatures allowed Siouxsie and Budgie to experiment with voice and rhythm free of guitar-led arrangements, and the project continued intermittently until their split in 2005.
