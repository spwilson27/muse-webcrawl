List of airliner shootdown incidents

Military shootdowns of misidentified aircraft

The KAL 007 and Iran Air 655 incidents remain the most notorious cases of state military forces destroying civilian airliners. KAL 007 led the United States to make GPS available for civilian use, while Iran Air 655 resulted in a settlement at the International Court of Justice and an ex gratia payment by the United States. The destruction of MH17 led to criminal prosecutions in the Netherlands and reforms in conflict-zone flight planning.

Attacks with man-portable air-defense systems (MANPADS) have also been attempted or carried out against airliners, including the 2002 Mombasa attacks (a missed Arkia 757) and the 2003 DHL Baghdad incident, in which an A300 was hit but landed safely.
