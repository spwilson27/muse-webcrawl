Marcham is a village and civil parish in the Vale of White Horse district of Oxfordshire, England, about 2 miles (3.2 km) west of Abingdon-on-Thames and near the River Ock, a tributary of the Thames. Historically in Berkshire until the 1974 boundary changes transferred the Vale to Oxfordshire, the parish includes the hamlets of Cothill and Gozzards Ford. The 2011 census recorded a population of 2,423.

History and archaeology

The Marcham area has produced evidence of settlement from the Iron Age and Roman periods. Excavations by the University of Oxford at Trendles Field, just north of the village, in the 1990s and 2000s revealed an Iron Age and Romano-British ritual landscape, including a rare Romano-Celtic temple and an amphitheatre-like structure of a type unusual in Roman Britain — a major research excavation that ran for over a decade. Marcham itself is recorded in Anglo-Saxon charters, and its name is generally thought to derive from Old English for "horse marsh" or a marsh where mare's-tail grew. The parish church of All Saints is a Grade II* listed building with a medieval west tower; part of its fabric dates from the thirteenth century, and it houses a celebrated set of pre-Reformation and later monuments.

The village today

Marcham has a primary school, a village shop and post office, pubs, a sports and social club, and a village hall (the Marcham Centre, opened in 2020, replacing the old institute). The parish is known for Denman College, the national residential college of the Women's Institute, which was based at Marcham Park from 1947 until its closure in 2020. The A415 road passes through the village, linking Abingdon with Kingston Bagpuize and the A420 to Oxford and Swindon. Nearby attractions include the gardens of Cothill and the countryside of the Vale of White Horse, with the Berkshire Downs rising to the south.

Governance

Marcham civil parish council administers local services, with higher tiers provided by Vale of White Horse District Council and Oxfordshire County Council. The village lies in the Wantage parliamentary constituency.
