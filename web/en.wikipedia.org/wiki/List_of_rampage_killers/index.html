List of rampage killers

A rampage killer is a person who kills a number of people in a single episode, in a public place, with little or no cooling-off period. This list collects the deadliest recorded rampage killings worldwide, as documented in the full article, which is organised by region and number of victims and includes mass shootings, vehicle attacks, arson and other methods. It excludes acts of war, state violence and organised terrorism, which are listed elsewhere.


Memorials at the sites of mass killings have become common sites of public mourning.

Overview

The deadliest rampages by a lone offender include the 2011 Norway attacks (77 killed, combining the Oslo bombing and the Utøya shooting), the 2017 Las Vegas shooting (60 killed), the 2019 Christchurch mosque shootings in New Zealand (51 killed), the 2016 Nice truck attack in France (86 killed), and the 1982 Woo Bum-kon spree in South Korea (56 killed). Earlier cases include the 1927 Bath School disaster in the United States and the 1996 Port Arthur massacre in Australia, which led to sweeping changes in Australian gun law.

Classification

Researchers distinguish rampage killers from serial killers (multiple episodes separated by cooling-off periods) and from spree killers in some typologies, although definitions vary between criminologists and between the FBI and other agencies. Common study themes include the offenders' social isolation, grievance-fuelled thinking, planning behaviour, and the role of media coverage in possible copycat attacks.
