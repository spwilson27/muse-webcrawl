Hard Rock Cafe

Hard Rock Cafe, Inc. is an American multinational chain of theme restaurants founded in 1971 in London, England, by Americans Isaac Tigrett and Peter Morton. The cafe covers 874 square metres (9,410 sq ft) and its decor is famously filled with rock and roll memorabilia, including guitars, costumes, and platinum records donated by musicians. The chain is owned by the Seminole Tribe of Florida through its subsidiary Seminole Hard Rock.

Industry: Restaurants, casinos, hotels

Founded: 14 June 1971, London, England

Founders: Isaac Tigrett, Peter Morton

Headquarters: Davie, Florida, United States

Owner: Seminole Tribe of Florida

History

The first Hard Rock Cafe opened at Old Park Lane in Hyde Park, London, in a building that had previously been a Rolls-Royce showroom. The founders sought to bring American-style burgers and casual dining to London. The memorabilia collection began in 1979 when guitarist Eric Clapton donated a red Fender Lead II guitar to reserve his favourite bar stool; Pete Townshend of The Who responded by donating one of his guitars, and the collection grew into one of the largest assemblages of rock memorabilia in the world, with tens of thousands of items rotated among locations.

The chain expanded internationally during the 1980s and 1990s, opening cafes, hotels, and casinos in cities across the globe. In December 2006, the Seminole Tribe of Florida purchased Hard Rock International from the Rank Group for approximately $965 million, in one of the largest acquisitions of a global hospitality brand by a Native American tribe.

Memorabilia

Hard Rock's collection includes more than 80,000 pieces, among them John Lennon's handwritten lyrics, Jim Morrison's leather pants, Madonna's bustier, and Buddy Holly's glasses. Items are catalogued and displayed in cafes, hotels, and casinos worldwide, with pieces periodically rotated between venues.

Hard Rock Hotels and Casinos

Beyond the cafes, the brand operates hotels and casino resorts, including the Seminole Hard Rock Hotel & Casino in Hollywood, Florida, notable for its guitar-shaped hotel tower that opened in 2019, and the Seminole Hard Rock Hotel & Casino Tampa. The former Hard Rock Hotel and Casino in Las Vegas closed in 2020 and was rebranded as Virgin Hotels Las Vegas; the brand later acquired The Mirage on the Las Vegas Strip in 2022.
