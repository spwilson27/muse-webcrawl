Levi Strauss & Co.
Founded: 1853, San Francisco
Founder: Levi Strauss
Known for: Levi's denim jeans

Levi Strauss & Co. is an American clothing company known worldwide for its Levi's brand of denim jeans. It was founded in 1853 when German immigrant Levi Strauss arrived in San Francisco, California, during the Gold Rush, initially running a dry goods business.

History

In 1873, Strauss and tailor Jacob Davis patented work trousers reinforced with copper rivets at stress points — the garment that became blue jeans. The company's "501" jeans, named after their lot number, became an enduring global staple. Levi Strauss & Co. later expanded into other casual clothing and acquired brands including Dockers and Denizen.

Company

The company is headquartered in San Francisco and sells products in more than 100 countries. It returned to public markets with an NYSE listing in 2019. Levi's has also become known for sustainability initiatives such as water-saving finishes and second-hand resale programmes.
