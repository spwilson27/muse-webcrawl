Del Vasto

Del Vasto is a noble Italian family originating in Piedmont, descendants of the Aleramici dynasty, whose members ruled marquisates including Saluzzo, Savona, Busca and Ceva in the Middle Ages.

Origins

The family descends from Boniface del Vasto (died about 1125), whose sons founded several marquis lines in Liguria and Piedmont.

Branches

Branches included the Marquises of Saluzzo, the most prominent, who ruled until the 16th century.

Legacy

Though the main lines became extinct, the del Vasto name remains in Italian toponyms and historiography of medieval Piedmont.
