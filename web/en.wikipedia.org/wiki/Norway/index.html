Norway

[Image: A Norwegian fjord with a small village at the water's edge]
A Norwegian fjord with a small village at the water's edge

Norway, officially the Kingdom of Norway, is a Nordic country in Northern Europe occupying the western portion of the Scandinavian Peninsula. It is famous for its deeply indented coastline of fjords — among the longest coastlines in the world — as well as mountains and glaciers. Its capital is Oslo and its population is about 5.5 million.

Norway also includes the Arctic archipelago of Svalbard and claims Antarctic territory. Oil and gas discovered in the North Sea from 1969 transformed Norway into one of the world's richest countries; much of the revenue is saved in the Government Pension Fund Global, the world's largest sovereign wealth fund, worth well over a trillion dollars. Norway is a founding member of NATO but has twice voted against joining the EU. The northern lights, midnight sun, Viking heritage and winter sports are national signatures.
