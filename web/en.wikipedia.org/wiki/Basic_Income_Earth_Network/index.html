The Basic Income Earth Network (BIEN) is an international network of academics and activists interested in the idea of a basic income — a periodic cash payment unconditionally delivered to all members of a community on an individual basis, without means test or work requirement.

History

BIEN was founded in 1986 in Louvain-la-Neuve, Belgium, as the Basic Income European Network. As interest in basic income grew outside Europe, the organisation expanded its remit and in 2004 adopted its present name, becoming the Basic Income Earth Network while retaining the acronym BIEN (which also matches the French and Spanish word for "good" or "well").

Activities

BIEN holds international congresses bringing together researchers, policymakers and campaigners; its recent congresses have been hosted on several continents. It recognises national and regional affiliate networks around the world and publishes news and research summaries through its website and bulletin. Philosophers associated with its founding and growth include Philippe Van Parijs, whose work helped popularise basic income in academic debate.
