Rugby union positions

In the game of rugby union, there are 15 players on each team, comprising eight forwards and seven backs. Each player wears a number from 1 to 15 corresponding to their position; substitutes are numbered 16 to 23. Positions are standardised under the laws of World Rugby, though players' roles vary with tactics and playing style.

Terminology

Names vary regionally: in New Zealand the fly-half is the "first five-eighth" and the inside centre the "second five-eighth"; "stand-off" and "out-half" are used in parts of the British Isles and Ireland. Utility backs are players able to cover several positions, and specialist replacements — a front-row "bomb squad" of impact forwards — became a prominent tactic in modern international rugby.
