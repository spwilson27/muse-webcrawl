The Forerunners are a fictional ancient alien civilisation in the Halo science-fiction franchise, created by Bungie and developed by 343 Industries. Once the dominant civilisation of the Milky Way galaxy, the Forerunners assumed the "Mantle of Responsibility" — a philosophy of stewardship over all life — before being driven to extinction roughly 100,000 years before the events of the games.

History in the fiction

According to the series' expanded lore, the Forerunners rose to power after rebelling against their creators, the Precursors, millions of years in the past. Their ecumene spanned millions of worlds and was organised into rates, including Builders, Miners, Lifeworkers and Warrior-Servants. When the parasitic Flood threatened to consume all sentient life, the Forerunners waged a desperate three-century war, culminating in the construction and firing of the Halo Array — a network of ring-shaped superweapons whose activation sterilised the galaxy, destroying the Flood's food supply and the Forerunners themselves, after samples of life had been preserved and later reseeded by automated systems under the Librarian's Conservation Measure.

Remnants and technology

The games take place among the Forerunners' surviving megastructures: the Halo installations, the Ark foundry outside the galaxy, shield worlds such as Requiem, and artefacts including monitors (such as 343 Guilty Spark), Sentinels and the Promethean constructs of Halo 4. Humanity is identified in the fiction as the Forerunners' designated "Reclaimers", able to operate their technology, a plot element central to the Covenant's religion and the Human–Covenant War.

Development

Forerunner architecture — vast geometric structures of angular metal set into natural landscapes — became a signature visual element of the series, and later media, including Greg Bear's Forerunner Saga novels, expanded their history in detail.
