Devolver Digital, Inc. is an American video game publisher based in Austin, Texas, specialising in the publishing of independent games. Founded in 2009 by former executives of Gathering of Developers and Gamecock Media Group, the company rose to prominence with the Serious Sam series and the breakout hit Hotline Miami (2012).

History

Devolver was founded in 2009 by Mike Wilson, Harry Miller, Rick Stults, Nigel Lowrie, and Graeme Struthers. Its first titles were high-definition remakes of Serious Sam games. The company built a reputation for championing distinctive indie titles, publishing successes such as Hotline Miami, The Talos Principle, Enter the Gungeon, Gris, Fall Guys (before its move to Epic Games), Loop Hero, Inscryption, and Cult of the Lamb. In November 2021 the company went public on the London Stock Exchange's AIM market.

Marketing and persona

Devolver is famous for its irreverent marketing, fronted by the fictional executive Nina Struthers, and for its satirical E3 press conferences beginning in 2017, which lampooned industry trends such as loot boxes and microtransactions while still showcasing games. The company also founded the film distribution arm Devolver Films.
