BBC Radio Wales
First air date: 13 November 1978
Format: News, music, talk, sport
Language: English
Owner: BBC Cymru Wales
Headquarters: Cardiff

BBC Radio Wales is a Welsh national radio network owned and operated by BBC Cymru Wales. It broadcasts English-language programming across Wales on FM, DAB and online, with news, sport, music and entertainment for Welsh audiences.

History

The station launched on 13 November 1978, replacing the Welsh opt-out of BBC Radio 4. It was created alongside BBC Radio Cymru, which broadcasts in Welsh, as part of an expansion of BBC services for Wales.

Programming

Programmes include breakfast and drivetime news shows, Country Focus, comedy and music strands, and extensive live sport under the Radio Wales Sport banner covering Welsh football, rugby and Cardiff City, Swansea City, Newport County and Wrexham.
