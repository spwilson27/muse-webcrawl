Yamaguchi esterification

The Yamaguchi esterification is a chemical reaction of an aliphatic carboxylic acid and 2,4,6-trichlorobenzoyl chloride that forms a mixed anhydride, which upon reaction with an alcohol in the presence of a stoichiometric amount of DMAP produces the corresponding ester.

Overview

The method was reported by Masaru Yamaguchi and co-workers in 1979. It is especially powerful for macrolactonisation—the formation of large-ring lactones in natural product total synthesis—because it proceeds under mild conditions with good tolerance of sensitive functionality, and has been used in syntheses of macrolide antibiotics such as erythromycin derivatives.
