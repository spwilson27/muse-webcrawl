Jessica Lange

Jessica Phyllis Lange (born April 20, 1949) is an American actress. Prolific in film, television, and theatre, she is the recipient of numerous accolades, including two Academy Awards, three Primetime Emmy Awards, and a Tony Award, making her one of the few performers to achieve the Triple Crown of Acting.

Career

Lange made her film debut in Dino De Laurentiis's King Kong (1976). In 1982 she became the first performer in 40 years to receive two Oscar nominations in the same year, winning Best Supporting Actress for Tootsie and being nominated for Best Actress for Frances. She won the Academy Award for Best Actress for Blue Sky (1994), and earned acclaim for All That Jazz (1979), The Postman Always Rings Twice (1981), Sweet Dreams (1985), and Music Box (1989).

Later work

From 2011 she reached a new audience starring in Ryan Murphy's anthology series American Horror Story, winning two Emmy Awards, and later played Joan Crawford in Feud: Bette and Joan (2017). On stage she won the Tony Award for Best Actress for Long Day's Journey into Night (2016). Lange is also a published photographer and a Goodwill Ambassador for UNICEF.
