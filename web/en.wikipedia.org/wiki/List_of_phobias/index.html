List of phobias

A phobia is an anxiety disorder characterised by an intense, irrational fear of a specific object, situation or activity. Many phobias have standardised names formed from Greek or Latin roots with the suffix -phobia. This list gives the most commonly recognised names; clinically, specific phobias are grouped as animal, natural-environment, blood-injection-injury, situational and other types, plus social phobia (social anxiety disorder) and agoraphobia.

Notes

Long lists of Greek-derived names exist for almost any conceivable fear, but most are rarely used clinically; diagnosis rests on the pattern of fear and avoidance rather than the name. The jocular word hippopotomonstrosesquippedaliophobia (fear of long words) is deliberately self-referential and not a clinical term.
