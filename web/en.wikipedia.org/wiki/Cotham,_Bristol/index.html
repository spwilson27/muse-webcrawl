Cotham, Bristol

Cotham
Suburb of Bristol, England
Unitary authority: Bristol
Ceremonial county: Bristol

Cotham is an affluent suburb and electoral ward in the north of Bristol, England, lying between Bishopston, Redland and Kingsdown. It is characterised by large Victorian and Edwardian houses, many now converted into flats and student accommodation.

Features

Cotham is home to Cotham School, a secondary academy, and lies close to the University of Bristol. Cotham Hill and nearby Whiteladies Road provide shops, cafés and restaurants. The area has a number of listed buildings and conservation areas protecting its historic streetscape.
