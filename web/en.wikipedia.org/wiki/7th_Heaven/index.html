7th Heaven is an American family drama television series created by Brenda Hampton. It premiered on The WB on August 26, 1996, and concluded on The CW on May 13, 2007, after eleven seasons and 243 episodes, making it the longest-running series produced by Aaron Spelling and one of the longest-running family dramas in American television history.

Premise

The series follows the Reverend Eric Camden, a Protestant minister, and his wife Annie as they raise their seven children — Matt, Mary, Lucy, Simon, Ruthie, and twins Sam and David — in the fictional town of Glenoak, California. Episodes typically centered on moral lessons and family problems, from teenage pregnancy and peer pressure to grief and faith.

Reception

The show was The WB's most-watched series at its peak, drawing over 12 million viewers for a 1999 episode. It launched the career of Jessica Biel, who played Mary Camden. The series concluded in 2007 after a brief reprieve: it had been cancelled after season ten but was revived by the newly formed CW network for a final season.
