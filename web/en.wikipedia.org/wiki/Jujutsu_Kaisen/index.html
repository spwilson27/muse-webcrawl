Jujutsu Kaisen

AuthorGege Akutami
PublisherShueisha
MagazineWeekly Shōnen Jump
Original run2018–2024
GenresAction, dark fantasy, supernatural

Jujutsu Kaisen is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Gege Akutami. It was serialized in Shueisha's Weekly Shōnen Jump from March 2018 to September 2024, with its chapters collected in 30 tankōbon volumes.

Plot

The story follows high school student Yuji Itadori, who swallows a cursed object — the finger of the powerful Curse Ryomen Sukuna — and becomes his vessel. Enrolled at Tokyo Jujutsu High School, Yuji trains as a jujutsu sorcerer alongside Megumi Fushiguro and Nobara Kugisaki under the teacher Satoru Gojo, fighting malignant beings known as Curses born from human negative emotions.

Media

An anime television series adaptation by MAPPA premiered in 2020, followed by a second season in 2023. The prequel film Jujutsu Kaisen 0 (2021) was a major box-office success. The franchise also includes light novels, video games and stage plays.

Reception

The manga has over 100 million copies in circulation, making it one of the best-selling manga series of all time. Both the manga and anime have received widespread critical acclaim and numerous awards.
