La Liste

La Liste is a restaurant ranking and guide that publishes an annual list of the 1,000 best restaurants in the world. Launched in France in December 2015 with the support of the French Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the French Tourism Development Agency, it was created under the impetus of diplomat Philippe Faure as a French-based alternative to the British-published World's 50 Best Restaurants ranking.

Methodology

Unlike rankings based on a jury of voters, La Liste aggregates scores from hundreds of guidebooks, review publications, and crowdsourced review websites. Its algorithm standardises scores from sources including national guides (such as Michelin and Gault & Millau), newspaper critics, and online platforms, weighted by the reliability of each source, and converts them into a score out of 100 for each restaurant. The compilers describe the result as a "meta-ranking" of more than 600 sources covering some 30,000 restaurants in over 200 countries.

Rankings

The first edition, published in 2015, placed Benoît Violier's Restaurant de l'Hôtel de Ville in Crissier, Switzerland, at number one. Subsequent top-ranked restaurants have included Guy Savoy in Paris, Le Bernardin in New York, and other establishments in France, Japan, and the United States, with several restaurants sometimes sharing the top score. La Liste has since expanded into additional products, including hotel rankings and lists of the world's best pastry shops and affordable restaurants.

Reception

The launch of La Liste drew international attention as an explicit French response to perceived biases in other rankings, though its organisers present it as complementary to existing guides. Its methodology has been both praised for transparency and criticised for favouring establishments covered by traditional printed guides.
