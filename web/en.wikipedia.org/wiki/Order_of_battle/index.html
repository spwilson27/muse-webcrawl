Order of battle

An order of battle of an armed force is the identification, structure, personnel, and command hierarchy of its units and formations. It lists the units participating in a campaign or battle, their commanders, strengths and organisation, and is a standard product of military intelligence as well as a basic reference for military historians.

Historical use

Originally the term described the order in which units were drawn up for battle. In modern usage it covers an opponent's entire force structure: divisions, brigades, battalions, ships or squadrons, together with their equipment and readiness. Histories of campaigns conventionally include orders of battle in appendices.

Intelligence

Maintaining an accurate enemy order of battle — often abbreviated ORBAT — is a core task of intelligence staffs, drawing on reconnaissance, signals intelligence and captured documents. Deception operations historically aimed to falsify it, most famously the phantom formations of the Second World War.
