The North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) is a military and political alliance founded in 1949 by North American and European states to provide collective security, principally against the Soviet Union. Its Article 5 declares an attack on one member an attack on all; it has been invoked only once, after the 11 September 2001 attacks on the United States.

Enlargement

After the Cold War, NATO admitted most former Warsaw Pact states and, following Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine (see the revolution's successor state's war in the seed crawl's current-events coverage), Finland (2023) and Sweden (2024) joined, bringing membership to 32. Headquarters are in Brussels, Belgium, near Geneva's international district in neighbouring Switzerland.
