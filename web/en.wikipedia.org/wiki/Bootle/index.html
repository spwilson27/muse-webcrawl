Bootle

Bootle is a town in the Metropolitan Borough of Sefton, Merseyside, England, lying on the River Mersey estuary about 4 miles (6.4 km) north of Liverpool city centre. Historically in Lancashire, it grew from a small seaside resort for Liverpool merchants into a major dockland town as the Port of Liverpool expanded northwards in the 19th century.

Bootle's docks, including Canada Dock and Gladstone Dock, handled emigrant, timber and container traffic, and the town was heavily bombed during the Liverpool Blitz of 1941 — it was among the most heavily damaged county boroughs in Britain. Its population is around 50,000. Bootle is home to Bootle F.C. and to the headquarters functions of several government agencies, and the town is served by stations on the Merseyrail Northern line.
