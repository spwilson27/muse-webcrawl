List of FA Cup finals

The FA Cup is an annual knockout football competition in English football, organized by the Football Association. First held in 1871–72, it is the world's oldest national football competition. This article lists the FA Cup finals: the winning and runner-up clubs, scores, and venues for each year. The final has traditionally been played at Wembley Stadium in London.

History

The first final, in 1872, saw Wanderers defeat Royal Engineers 1–0 at Kennington Oval. Finals were held at various venues before Wembley's first final in 1923. Arsenal are the most successful club in the competition's history, with 14 wins, followed by Manchester United. The list notes famous finals such as the 1953 "Matthews Final" (Blackpool 4–3 Bolton Wanderers) and the first final decided by a penalty shootout, in 2005.
