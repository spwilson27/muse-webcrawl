Volkswagen Touareg (third generation)

The Volkswagen Touareg is a mid-size luxury crossover SUV produced by German automaker Volkswagen since 2002 at the Volkswagen Bratislava Plant. The vehicle is named after the nomadic Tuareg people of the Sahara Desert.

Development

The first-generation Touareg was developed jointly with the Porsche Cayenne and the Audi Q7, sharing the Volkswagen Group PL71 platform, but the Touareg was distinguished by serious off-road hardware including low-range gearing and available air suspension. Engines ranged from a 3.2-litre V6 to a 6.0-litre W12 and a V10 TDI diesel famous for towing a Boeing 747 in a publicity demonstration.

Later generations

The second generation (2010) added hybrid power and shed weight; the third generation (2018), built on the MLB Evo platform alongside the Audi Q7 and Bentley Bentayga, was not sold in North America, where the Atlas took its place. A plug-in hybrid Touareg R became the flagship of the range.
