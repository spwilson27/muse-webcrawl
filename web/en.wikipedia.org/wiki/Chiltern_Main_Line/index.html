Chiltern Main Line

The Chiltern Main Line is a railway line in England linking London Marylebone to Birmingham Snow Hill, via High Wycombe, Banbury, and Leamington Spa. It is operated principally by Chiltern Railways.

History

The line was built jointly by the Great Western Railway and the Great Central Railway, opening in stages between 1906 and 1910 as part of the last main line built to London (the Great Central Main Line). It was threatened with closure in the 20th century but survived and was comprehensively modernised under British Rail's Total Route Modernisation project in the 1990s.

Services and upgrades

Since privatisation, Chiltern Railways has upgraded the line under the Evergreen programme, raising line speeds, redoubling single-track sections, and, in 2015, opening a new link from Bicester to Oxford, extended to Oxford station in 2016. Services connect London with the Chiltern Hills, Oxfordshire, Warwickshire, and the West Midlands.
