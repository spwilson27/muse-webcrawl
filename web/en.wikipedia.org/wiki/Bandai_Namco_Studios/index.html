Bandai Namco Studios

Bandai Namco Studios Inc. is a Japanese video game developer headquartered in Shinagawa, Tokyo. It is the principal in-house development studio of the publisher Bandai Namco Entertainment, itself part of the Bandai Namco Holdings group. The studio was formed in 2012 by consolidating internal development teams, and it works both on the group's own franchises — including Tekken, Tales, Ace Combat and Pac-Man — and on contract development for other publishers, notably Nintendo.

History

Bandai Namco Games established the studio in April 2012, gathering roughly 1,000 developers from its internal teams into a dedicated development company. The move followed the 2005 merger of Bandai and Namco that created the Bandai Namco group. Subsidiary studios were later added in Vancouver, Singapore and Malaysia to support production.

Notable works

  • Tekken 7 (2015) and Tekken 8 (2024)
  • Tales of Berseria (2016) and Tales of Arise (2021)
  • Ace Combat 7: Skies Unknown (2019)
  • Scarlet Nexus (2021)
  • Co-development support on Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS and Wii U and Super Smash Bros. Ultimate
  • Pokkén Tournament (2015)
