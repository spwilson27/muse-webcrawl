List of oil fields

This is a list of oil fields — accumulations of crude oil beneath the Earth's surface large enough to be extracted commercially. "Giant" fields are those with ultimate recoverable reserves of more than 500 million barrels; the largest of all, Saudi Arabia's Ghawar, has produced more oil than any other field in history. Fields are listed with their location and approximate size.

Notes

Reserve figures for national oil companies are often unaudited and politically sensitive; published numbers vary widely, particularly for the Middle East. Unconventional accumulations such as the Orinoco Belt and Canada's Athabasca oil sands contain enormous resources but are developed field-by-field through mining and in-situ projects rather than as single conventional fields.
