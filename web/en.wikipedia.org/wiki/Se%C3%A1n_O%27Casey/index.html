Seán O'Casey

Seán O'Casey
Born: John Casey, 30 March 1880, Dublin, Ireland
Died: 18 September 1964, Torquay, England
Occupation: Playwright
Notable works: Juno and the Paycock, The Plough and the Stars

Seán O'Casey (born John Casey; 30 March 1880 – 18 September 1964) was an Irish dramatist and memoirist, one of the major figures of twentieth-century Irish theatre. Raised in Dublin tenements, he worked as a labourer and was active in trade union and nationalist politics before turning to playwriting.

Works

His Dublin trilogy — The Shadow of a Gunman (1923), Juno and the Paycock (1924) and The Plough and the Stars (1926) — set working-class tenement life against the Irish revolutionary period and was staged at the Abbey Theatre; the last provoked riots for its unheroic portrayal of the Easter Rising. After the Abbey rejected The Silver Tassie (1928), O'Casey settled permanently in England. He also wrote the autobiographical series I Knock at the Door and later plays such as Cock-a-Doodle Dandy.
