Harold and Maude

Harold and Maude is a 1971 American romantic black comedy-drama film directed by Hal Ashby, written by Colin Higgins, and starring Ruth Gordon and Bud Cort. It tells the story of Harold, a young man obsessed with death, who falls in love with Maude, a free-spirited 79-year-old woman who teaches him to embrace life.

Plot

Harold Chasen, the son of a wealthy widow, stages elaborate fake suicides to shock his mother and attends strangers' funerals for amusement. At one funeral he meets Maude, who steals cars, champions trees, and lives life with joyful defiance. As their unconventional romance deepens, Maude reveals her past as a survivor of the Holocaust, and on her 80th birthday she carries out her long-planned decision to die, leaving Harold transformed.

Reception and legacy

The film was a box-office disappointment on release but grew into a celebrated cult classic. Its soundtrack by Cat Stevens, including "If You Want to Sing Out, Sing Out", is closely associated with the film. It is frequently ranked among the greatest comedies of all time.
