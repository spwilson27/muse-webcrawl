List of mass shootings in the United States

Mass shootings are a significant and widely debated phenomenon in the United States. Definitions vary: the Gun Violence Archive counts incidents in which four or more people are shot, while federal definitions of "mass killing" focus on fatalities. By broad definitions, the United States experiences hundreds of mass shootings per year — more than any other developed country.

Frequency and policy debate

Tracking organisations recorded more than 600 mass shootings (by the four-or-more-shot definition) in several recent years. Mass shootings are central to the American gun debate, prompting measures such as the Bipartisan Safer Communities Act of 2022, state-level assault-weapon and red-flag laws, and ongoing litigation over the scope of the Second Amendment.
