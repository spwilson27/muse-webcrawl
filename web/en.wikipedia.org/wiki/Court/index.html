Court

Court
DefinitionTribunal with authority to adjudicate disputes and administer justice
LevelsTrial courts, appellate courts, supreme/constitutional courts
[Image: A courtroom with a judge's bench, flags and wood panelling]

A court is any person or institution with authority to judge or adjudicate legal disputes and administer justice in civil, criminal and administrative law. Courts range from local magistrates to supreme courts that can strike down laws (judges presiding, sometimes with a jury).

Systems

Adversarial systems (common law) pit parties before a neutral judge; inquisitorial systems (civil law) give judges an investigating role. International courts — the ICJ and ICC — extend adjudication between and beyond states, with uneven enforcement.
