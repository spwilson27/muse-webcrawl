Fandom (website)

Fandom is a wiki hosting service and domain operated by Fandom, Inc., a for-profit company founded in 2004 by Wikipedia co-founder Jimmy Wales and Angela Beesley Starling. Originally launched as Wikicities and renamed Wikia in 2006, the service took the Fandom name in 2016. It hosts hundreds of thousands of community-run wikis devoted to video games, films, television series, books and other topics of fan interest, written in a similar collaborative style to Wikipedia but supported by advertising.

History

Wikicities launched in October 2004 as a commercial sister project to the non-profit Wikipedia, offering free wiki hosting for communities. It rebranded as Wikia in 2006 and expanded rapidly, acquiring popular fan wikis and launching official wikis in partnership with entertainment companies. In 2016 the main domain became fandom.com. The company also acquired properties such as the gaming site Curse and its Gamepedia wikis in 2018–2019, migrating Gamepedia wikis onto the Fandom platform, and in 2022 it acquired several entertainment and gaming media brands including GameSpot, Metacritic and TV Guide.

Criticism

Fandom has attracted criticism from wiki communities over heavy advertising, including autoplaying videos, changes imposed on wiki designs, and the mandatory migration to the Unified Community Platform. Some communities have "forked" their wikis to independent hosting or rival services in response to these policies.
