Insular Government of the Philippine Islands

Insular Government of the Philippine Islands
Period: 1901–1935
Capital: Manila
Status: Unincorporated territory of the United States
Succeeded by: Commonwealth of the Philippines

The Insular Government of the Philippine Islands (Spanish: Gobierno Insular de las Islas Filipinas) was the civil government established by the United States in the Philippine Islands in 1901, succeeding the United States Military Government. It administered the archipelago as an unincorporated territory during the American colonial period.

Headed by a Governor-General appointed by the U.S. President, the government progressively introduced elected institutions, including the Philippine Assembly (1907) and, under the Jones Law of 1916, an elected Senate. It was replaced in 1935 by the Commonwealth of the Philippines, a transitional government leading toward independence in 1946.
