Hydrangeaceae

Hydrangeaceae is a family of flowering plants in the order Cornales, comprising shrubs, small trees, and some lianas, native mainly to Asia and North America with a few species elsewhere. The family includes about 17 genera and roughly 190 species in modern circumscriptions.

Genera

Well-known genera include Hydrangea (hydrangeas), Philadelphus (mock-oranges), Deutzia, Kirengeshoma, and Schizophragma. Many are popular ornamental garden plants grown for their showy inflorescences.

Characteristics

Members typically have opposite leaves, flowers in clusters, and capsular or berry-like fruits. The family has at times been placed within Saxifragaceae or treated with subfamily rank (Hydrangeoideae), but APG classifications recognise it as a distinct family in Cornales.

References: APG IV classification; Flora of North America.
