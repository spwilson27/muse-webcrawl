Bradley Walsh

Bradley Walsh
Born: 4 June 1960, Watford, Hertfordshire
Occupation: Television presenter, comedian, actor, singer, former footballer
Walsh is best known as host of The Chase

Bradley John Walsh (born 4 June 1960) is an English television presenter, comedian, actor, singer and former professional footballer. He is best known as the host of the ITV game show The Chase, which he has presented since 2009, and for playing Danny Baldwin in Coronation Street and Ronnie Brooks in Law & Order: UK.

Early life and football career

Walsh was born in Watford, Hertfordshire, and grew up in Leavesden. As a young man he was a professional footballer with Brentford, though injuries ended his playing career; he also had loan spells with clubs including Barnet and Dunstable Town.

Television career

Walsh moved into entertainment as a comedian and presenter, hosting the game show Wheel of Fortune in the 1990s. His acting breakthrough came in 2004 when he joined Coronation Street as factory boss Danny Baldwin, a role he played until 2006. From 2018 to 2022 he played Graham O'Brien, a companion of the Thirteenth Doctor in Doctor Who. He also presents the travel series Bradley Walsh & Son: Breaking Dad with his son Barney, and co-hosted the BBC revival of Gladiators from 2024.

Music

In 2016 Walsh released the album Chasing Dreams, a collection of standards that became the best-selling debut album by a British artist that year; a follow-up, When You're Smiling, appeared in 2017.
