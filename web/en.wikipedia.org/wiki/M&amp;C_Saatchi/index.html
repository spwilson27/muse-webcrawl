M&C Saatchi is an international advertising agency network founded in 1995 in London by Maurice Saatchi, Charles Saatchi and their partners, after the brothers were ousted from the board of Saatchi & Saatchi, the agency they had founded in 1970. The new agency quickly won major clients, several of which followed the brothers from their former company.

History

The Saatchi brothers' departure from Saatchi & Saatchi in 1994–95 followed shareholder discontent over the company's expansion. M&C Saatchi opened with backing and a roster of clients including British Airways and the Conservative Party, and grew into a network with offices in many countries. The group has been publicly listed and operates under a model of independent partner agencies sharing the brand.

Work

The agency is known for the philosophy of "Brutal Simplicity of Thought" and for campaigns across retail, travel, government and public-health communication.
