Constituencies of Namibia

Namibia is divided into 14 regions, which are subdivided into 121 constituencies. The constituencies are the country's electoral districts: each elects one councillor to its regional council, and constituencies are also used for elections to the National Assembly.

Administration

Constituencies were first defined after independence in 1990 and their number and boundaries have been adjusted by successive delimitation commissions. A constituency councillor represents the constituency on the regional council, and regional councils each elect three members to the National Council, the upper house of Parliament.
