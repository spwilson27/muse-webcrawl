Acme thread

The Acme thread form is a trapezoidal screw thread profile developed for power transmission applications such as lead screws in lathes and other machine tools. It has a 29° included thread angle and flat crests and roots.

Acme threads are stronger and easier to manufacture than square threads while allowing the use of split nuts that can be disengaged and adjusted for wear. Standardised series include general-purpose Acme and the shorter stub Acme form.

Because they are designed for transmitting power and motion rather than fastening, Acme threads are commonly found in vises, jacks, valve stems and CNC machine axes, often paired with bronze or polymer nuts.
