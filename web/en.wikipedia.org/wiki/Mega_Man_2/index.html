Mega Man 2

Developer: Capcom
Release: 1988 (Japan), 1989 (North America)
Platform: Nintendo Entertainment System
Genre: Action, platform

Mega Man 2 (known in Japan as Rockman 2: The Mystery of Dr. Wily) is an action-platform game developed and published by Capcom for the Nintendo Entertainment System. It is the second game in the original Mega Man series.

Gameplay

The villain Dr. Wily returns with eight new Robot Masters — Metal Man, Air Man, Bubble Man, Quick Man, Crash Man, Flash Man, Heat Man and Wood Man — whose stages can be tackled in any order. Defeating each grants Mega Man its weapon, which is strong against another boss in a rock-paper-scissors chain. The game added the Energy Tank, support Items and a password system, and its soundtrack, including the famous Dr. Wily stage theme, is widely celebrated.

Reception and legacy

Produced largely in the development team's spare time after the first game's modest sales, Mega Man 2 became the best-selling entry of the original series and is frequently ranked among the greatest video games of all time.
