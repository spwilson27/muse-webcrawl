Blackboard Learn (previously the Blackboard Learning System) is a web-based learning management system (LMS) developed by Blackboard Inc., used by schools, universities, businesses and government agencies to deliver course content, assignments, discussions, assessments and grades online.

History

The product grew out of CourseInfo, an online education platform founded in 1997 by Stephen Gilfus and Daniel Cane at Cornell University; the company merged into Blackboard Inc. the same year. Blackboard Learn became the dominant LMS in North American higher education in the 2000s. In 2015 Blackboard announced "Learn Ultra", a redesigned, responsive interface with a modernised "Ultra Course View"; institutions could run Ultra Base Navigation with Original courses, then migrate courses to Ultra view. Blackboard merged with Anthology in 2021, and Learn remains the combined company's flagship LMS, competing with Instructure Canvas, D2L Brightspace and Moodle.

Features

Blackboard Learn provides tools for course content organisation, online assignments and quizzes, discussion boards, group work, a grade centre, learning analytics, accessibility checking (via Ally), plagiarism detection integrations such as SafeAssign, and virtual classroom sessions (Blackboard Collaborate). It is delivered primarily as cloud-hosted software as a service.
