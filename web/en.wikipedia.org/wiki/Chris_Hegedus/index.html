Chris Hegedus
Born: April 23, 1952, United States
Occupation: Documentary filmmaker
Spouse/collaborator: D. A. Pennebaker

Chris Hegedus (born 1952) is an American documentary filmmaker, best known for her long collaboration with D. A. Pennebaker at Pennebaker Hegedus Films.

Career

Hegedus began as a camera operator and editor. With Pennebaker she co-directed The War Room (1993), following Bill Clinton's 1992 presidential campaign and strategists James Carville and George Stephanopoulos; the film was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature. Other works include Startup.com (2001), about the dot-com boom and bust, Elaine Stritch: At Liberty (2004), Kings of Pastry (2009), and Unlocking the Cage (2016), about animal-rights lawyer Steven Wise.

Style

Her work follows the direct cinema / cinéma vérité tradition of Pennebaker: observational filming with minimal narration, capturing events as they unfold.
