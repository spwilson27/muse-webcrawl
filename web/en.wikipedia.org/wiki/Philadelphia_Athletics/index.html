Philadelphia Athletics

The Philadelphia Athletics were a Major League Baseball team based in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, from 1901 to 1954. A charter member of the American League, the club was managed for its entire Philadelphia existence by Connie Mack. After the 1954 season the franchise moved to Kansas City, and later to Oakland, where it became the Oakland Athletics.

History

The Athletics won nine American League pennants and five World Series championships in Philadelphia. Mack built two dynasties: the early-1910s club of the "00,000 infield" and Home Run Baker, and the 1929–1931 team featuring Jimmie Foxx, Mickey Cochrane, and Lefty Grove that beat the Chicago Cubs and St. Louis Cardinals in consecutive Series.

Ballparks

The team played at Columbia Park from 1901 to 1908 and then at Shibe Park (later Connie Mack Stadium), which it shared with the Phillies from 1938 onward.

Departure

Declining attendance and finances led to the sale and relocation of the club to Kansas City after the 1954 season, ending 54 seasons in Philadelphia.
