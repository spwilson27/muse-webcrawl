List of business schools

A business school is a university-level institution that confers degrees in business administration and management, most notably the Master of Business Administration (MBA). This article lists notable business schools by region. Many of the best-known schools are accredited by one of the "triple crown" bodies — AACSB, EQUIS, and AMBA — and are ranked annually by publications such as the Financial Times and The Economist.
