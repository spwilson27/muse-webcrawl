Orem is a city in Utah County, Utah, United States, situated on the eastern shore of Utah Lake beneath the Wasatch Mountains. It forms part of the Provo–Orem metropolitan area and borders Provo to the south. The city takes its name from Walter C. Orem, a railway builder, and adopted the motto "Family City USA".

History

The area was first settled by Mormon pioneers in the late nineteenth century as an agricultural benchland community of orchards and farms. It incorporated in 1919 and grew rapidly after the Second World War, becoming largely suburban.

Education and economy

Orem is home to Utah Valley University, one of the largest public universities in the state. The city is part of the "Silicon Slopes" technology corridor and hosts software and technology companies alongside retail centres such as University Place.
