List of storms on the Great Lakes

The Great Lakes of North America are notorious for powerful autumn storms, often called "November witches," which have caused many shipwrecks. The combination of cold Arctic air over relatively warm lake water can generate hurricane-force winds and waves exceeding 35 feet (11 m).

Causes

Great Lakes storms typically form when strong low-pressure systems track across the region in late autumn. Lake-effect enhancement, long fetch distances, and rapidly falling pressures ("bomb cyclones") can produce conditions comparable to hurricanes, though the storms are extratropical.
