The governor of New York is the head of government of the U.S. state of New York and the commander-in-chief of the state's military forces. New York has had 57 governors (counting repeat terms separately, more). Four New York governors went on to become President of the United States: Martin Van Buren, Grover Cleveland, Theodore Roosevelt, and Franklin D. Roosevelt.
