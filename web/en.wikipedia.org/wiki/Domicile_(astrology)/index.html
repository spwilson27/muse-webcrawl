Domicile (astrology)

In astrology, a domicile is the zodiac sign over which a planet has rulership. It is one of the essential dignities of a planet. The planet is considered to be strengthened when placed in its own domicile, as if at home in its own sign.

Traditional assignments

In the traditional scheme derived from Ptolemy's Tetrabiblos, the Sun rules Leo and the Moon rules Cancer. The remaining five visible planets each rule two signs: Mercury rules Gemini and Virgo; Venus rules Taurus and Libra; Mars rules Aries and (traditionally) Scorpio; Jupiter rules Sagittarius and (traditionally) Pisces; Saturn rules Capricorn and (traditionally) Aquarius.

Modern astrologers often assign the trans-Saturnian planets as co-rulers: Uranus for Aquarius, Neptune for Pisces and Pluto for Scorpio. The opposite of domicile is detriment — a planet placed in the sign opposite its domicile is considered weakened. Domicile is distinct from exaltation, another essential dignity.
