Williams Grand Prix Engineering Limited, competing as Williams Racing, is a British Formula One team and constructor. It was founded in 1977 by Frank Williams and engineer Patrick Head, and is based at Grove, Oxfordshire. One of the most successful teams in Formula One history, Williams has won nine Constructors' Championships and seven Drivers' Championships, and remains one of only a handful of independent teams to have dominated the sport.

History

After an earlier venture run on a shoestring, Frank Williams Racing Cars, Frank Williams founded Williams Grand Prix Engineering in 1977. The team's breakthrough came in 1979, when Clay Regazzoni won the British Grand Prix in the FW07, and in 1980 Alan Jones took the team's first Drivers' and Constructors' titles. Keke Rosberg won the 1982 championship.

The Honda-powered cars of the mid-1980s, driven by Nigel Mansell and Nelson Piquet, won constructors' titles in 1986 and 1987, Piquet taking the 1987 drivers' crown. After a leaner period, the Renault-powered cars designed by Patrick Head and Adrian Newey dominated the early 1990s: Mansell won the championship in 1992 with the technically advanced, active-suspension FW14B, and Alain Prost followed in 1993.

The team's history was marked by tragedy on 1 May 1994, when Ayrton Senna was killed in a crash at Imola driving the Williams FW16. Williams nonetheless won the constructors' title in 1994, and again in 1996 with Damon Hill and in 1997 with Jacques Villeneuve — its most recent championships.

Later years

Partnerships with BMW (2000–2005) brought race wins but no titles, and after the manufacturer's departure the team declined, enduring years at the back of the grid. In 2020 the Williams family sold the team to the US investment firm Dorilton Capital, ending more than four decades of family ownership; Claire Williams stepped down after the Italian Grand Prix that year. Under team principal James Vowles from 2023, the team has pursued a rebuilding programme.

Records

Williams has won more than 110 Grands Prix, ranking behind only Ferrari and McLaren in Formula One history, and is one of the sport's iconic independent constructors.
