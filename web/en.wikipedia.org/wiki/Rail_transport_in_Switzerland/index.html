Rail transport in Switzerland
Main operatorSwiss Federal Railways (SBB CFF FFS)
Network lengthAbout 5,300 km
GaugesStandard gauge; metre and narrow gauges
ElectrificationAlmost 100%

Rail transport in Switzerland is among the densest and most heavily used in the world: the Swiss travel more kilometres by rail per person than any other nation. The network integrates long-distance, regional, suburban and mountain railways under a coordinated national timetable.

Network and operators

Swiss Federal Railways (SBB) operates most standard-gauge lines, alongside companies such as BLS. Narrow-gauge railways — including the Rhaetian Railway, whose Albula and Bernina lines are UNESCO World Heritage sites — serve Alpine regions. Major infrastructure includes the Gotthard Base Tunnel (57 km, opened 2016), the world's longest railway tunnel.

Taktfahrplan

Since 1982 Switzerland has used a regular-interval clock-face timetable (Taktfahrplan), expanded by the Rail 2000 programme, so that trains arrive and depart at the same minutes each hour with coordinated connections, supported by integrated ticketing such as the GA Travelcard.
