Howmet Aerospace Inc. is an American aerospace company headquartered in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. It is a leading global provider of advanced engineered solutions for the aerospace and transportation industries, producing jet engine components, aerospace fastening systems, forged aluminum wheels, and engineered structures.

History

Howmet Aerospace traces its origins to Alcoa Inc. In 2016, Alcoa split into two companies: Alcoa Corporation, which retained the bauxite, alumina, and aluminum businesses, and Arconic Inc., which took over the engineered products and rolled products businesses. On April 1, 2020, Arconic itself split into two independent public companies: Howmet Aerospace, comprising the Engine Products, Fastening Systems, Engineered Structures, and Forged Wheels segments, and Arconic Corporation, comprising the rolled products business.

The name "Howmet" derives from the Howmet Corporation, a historic manufacturer of precision investment castings for jet engines that had been acquired by Alcoa in the 1990s. The company's Howmet Castings operations are known for producing superalloy turbine blades and other critical hot-section components for aircraft engines.

Operations

Howmet Aerospace's principal product lines include:

  • Engine Products — investment-cast turbine airfoils, rings, disks, and other components for jet engines and industrial gas turbines.
  • Fastening Systems — aerospace fasteners and installation systems used in airframes and engines.
  • Engineered Structures — titanium and aluminum structural components for aerospace applications.
  • Forged Wheels — forged aluminum wheels for commercial trucks and other heavy vehicles.

The company's customers include major aircraft and engine manufacturers such as Boeing, Airbus, GE Aerospace, Rolls-Royce, and Pratt & Whitney, as well as defense contractors and commercial vehicle manufacturers.
