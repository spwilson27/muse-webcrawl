Citizen journalism

[Image: Bystanders filming a breaking event on their phones]

Citizen journalism is the gathering and sharing of news by ordinary people rather than professional journalists — eyewitness photos and video, live streams, and local reporting published via social media and blogs. It rose with camera phones and platforms in the 2000s, from the 2004 tsunami and London bombings coverage to protest movements worldwide.

Role and limits

Citizen media often provides the first and only record of events, and professional newsrooms built verification desks to authenticate it. Its weaknesses — rumour, manipulation, lack of context — and the safety risks to contributors are central debates.
