Kaaterskill Falls is a two-stage waterfall on Spruce Creek in the Catskill Mountains of eastern New York, near the hamlet of Haines Falls in Greene County. With a total drop of about 260 feet (79 m), it is one of the tallest waterfalls in New York State.

History and culture

The falls became a celebrated subject of nineteenth-century American art and literature: Thomas Cole and other Hudson River School painters depicted them, and Washington Irving's "Rip Van Winkle" and William Cullen Bryant's poetry drew on the surrounding Kaaterskill Clove. The site lay near the Catskill Mountain House and other grand resort hotels of the era.

Access and safety

The falls are reached by trails from NY Route 23A and Laurel House Road, with a viewing platform completed in 2016. The area has a history of accidents and fatalities among visitors who leave the trail, prompting fencing, ranger patrols, and safety campaigns.
