Gloucester Road is a major road in Bristol, England, running north from the city centre through Stokes Croft, Bishopston and Horfield toward Filton and the M5 motorway, largely following the route of the A38. It is widely noted for having one of the longest stretches of independent shops of any street in the United Kingdom.

Character

The road is lined with independent cafés, bakeries, greengrocers, bookshops, pubs and restaurants, and has been celebrated as a stronghold of independent retail resisting chain-store dominance; local surveys have counted well over a hundred independent businesses along its length. The upper part of the road passes the Memorial Stadium, home of Bristol Rovers F.C., and the former Horfield Prison. Banksy's street art and the nearby Stokes Croft area contribute to the street's bohemian reputation.

History

The road developed in the nineteenth century as Bristol expanded into the suburbs of Bishopston and Horfield, and it was once served by trams. It takes its name from its direction toward the city of Gloucester.
