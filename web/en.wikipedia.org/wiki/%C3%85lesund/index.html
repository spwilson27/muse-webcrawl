Ålesund

Ålesund (Norwegian pronunciation: [ˈôːləsʉn]) is a town and municipality in Møre og Romsdal county, Norway. It is part of the traditional district of Sunnmøre and has a population of roughly 50,000 (2024). The town is known for its concentration of Art Nouveau (Jugendstil) architecture.

History

On the night of 23 January 1904, a devastating fire destroyed most of the town, which was then largely built of wood. Kaiser Wilhelm II of Germany, who often vacationed in the area, sent aid, and the town was rebuilt in stone, brick and mortar in the Art Nouveau style within a few years.

Economy and sights

Ålesund is a major fishing port and a gateway to the Geirangerfjord. The Aksla viewpoint, reached by 418 steps from the town park, offers a panoramic view over the archipelago.
