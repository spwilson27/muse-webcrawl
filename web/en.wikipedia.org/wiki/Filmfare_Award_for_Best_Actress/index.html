Filmfare Award for Best Actress

The Filmfare Award for Best Actress is given by Filmfare as part of its annual Filmfare Awards for Hindi films, to recognise the female performer who has delivered an outstanding performance in a leading role.

History

The award was first given in 1954, with Meena Kumari winning for Baiju Bawra. Nutan and Kajol share the record for most wins in the category, with five each. The awards are voted on by both the public and a committee of experts.

Notable winners

Multiple winners include Meena Kumari, Madhuri Dixit, Jaya Bachchan, Shabana Azmi, Vidya Balan, and Alia Bhatt. The category has been a leading indicator of leading-actress prominence in Bollywood.
