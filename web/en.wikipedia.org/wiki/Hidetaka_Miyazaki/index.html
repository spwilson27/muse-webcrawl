Hidetaka Miyazaki (born September 19, 1974) is a Japanese video game designer and executive, president of FromSoftware, known for creating the 'Soulsborne' action role-playing games.

Career

After studying social sciences at Keio University and working at Oracle, Miyazaki joined FromSoftware in 2004, inspired by the game Ico. He directed Demon's Souls (2009), a project considered a likely failure whose difficult, atmospheric design became a cult success, followed by Dark Souls (2011), Bloodborne (2015), Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice (2019) and Elden Ring (2022), which sold tens of millions and won Game of the Year awards.

Design philosophy

His games feature high difficulty balanced by a sense of achievement, environmental and item-based storytelling drawn from his childhood reading of partially understood English fantasy books, and interconnected world design.

Recognition

Miyazaki received the Lifetime Achievement Award at the Golden Joystick Awards (2018) and was named one of Time's 100 most influential people in 2023.
