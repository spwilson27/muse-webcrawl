President

President
DefinitionHead of state (and often government) of a republic
ExamplesUnited States, France, Germany (ceremonial), India (ceremonial)
[Image: A presidential podium with a national seal before flags]

A president is a common title for the head of state or chief executive of a republic. Presidential systems (US, much of Latin America) give the president executive power independent of the legislature; semi-presidential (France) and parliamentary republics (Germany, India) split roles with a prime minister.

The office

Washington set the republican template: elected, term-limited, civilian. Presidencies concentrate symbolism and crisis power in one person — the design choice their constitutions forever revisit, especially where presidents strain term limits (monarchy by another route, critics say).
