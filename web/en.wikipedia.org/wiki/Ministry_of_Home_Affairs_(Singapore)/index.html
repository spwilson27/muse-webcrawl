The Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) is a ministry of the Government of Singapore responsible for public safety, civil defence, and immigration. It oversees the departments and statutory boards collectively known as the Home Team, which work together to keep Singapore safe and secure.

Home Team agencies

Agencies under the ministry include the Singapore Police Force, the Singapore Civil Defence Force, the Immigration and Checkpoints Authority, the Singapore Prison Service, the Central Narcotics Bureau, and the Internal Security Department. Statutory boards under MHA include the Home Team Science and Technology Agency (HTX), the Casino Regulatory Authority (Gambling Regulatory Authority), the Singapore Corporation of Rehabilitative Enterprises (Yellow Ribbon Singapore), and the Home Team Academy.

History

The ministry was set up in 1959 when Singapore attained self-government, with responsibilities drawn from the colonial administration. Since independence in 1965 it has developed the Home Team concept, formalised in 1995, to integrate the work of its departments. The ministry administers key legislation such as the Internal Security Act and the Misuse of Drugs Act and coordinates national resilience and emergency preparedness.
