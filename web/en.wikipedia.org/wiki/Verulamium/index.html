Verulamium

Verulamium was a town in Roman Britain, situated near present-day St Albans in Hertfordshire. It was one of the largest Roman settlements in Britain.

History

Originally a Catuvellauni settlement, it became a municipium around AD 50. It was sacked during Boudica's revolt in AD 60/61, rebuilt in stone, and flourished with a theatre, forum, and city walls.

Legacy

St Alban, Britain's first Christian martyr, was associated with the town. Excavated remains are preserved in Verulamium Park and the Verulamium Museum.
