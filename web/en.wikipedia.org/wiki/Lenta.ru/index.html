Lenta.ru

Lenta.ru is a Russian-language online newspaper based in Moscow, founded in 1999. It was for many years one of the most popular news websites in Russia, known for its fast, round-the-clock coverage of Russian and international news.

History

Lenta.ru was founded in 1999 by Anton Nosik and Gleb Pavlovsky's Foundation for Effective Politics, quickly becoming a leading source of online news for Russian readers. Journalist Galina Timchenko served as editor-in-chief from 2004.

2014 editorial change

In March 2014, amid the annexation of Crimea, owner Alexander Mamut dismissed Timchenko and replaced her with a more Kremlin-friendly editor. Dozens of staff resigned in protest; Timchenko and many colleagues went on to found the independent outlet Meduza in Latvia.

Present day

Lenta.ru continues to operate as a major mainstream Russian news portal, though its editorial line shifted markedly after 2014 toward alignment with state positions.
