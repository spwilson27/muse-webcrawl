National Council for Scientific and Technological Development (CNPq)

CNPq
Portuguese: Conselho Nacional de Desenvolvimento Científico e Tecnológico
Founded: 1951
Headquarters: Brasília, Brazil
Parent: Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation

The National Council for Scientific and Technological Development (CNPq, Portuguese: Conselho Nacional de Desenvolvimento Científico e Tecnológico) is a Brazilian federal agency under the Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation, responsible for funding and promoting scientific and technological research in Brazil.

Activities

Founded in 1951 — originally as the National Research Council — CNPq provides research grants, scholarships from undergraduate to postdoctoral level, and funding for research groups and infrastructure. It maintains the Lattes Platform, a national database of researchers' curricula vitae that has become a standard reference in Brazilian academia.

Role in Brazilian science

Together with CAPES and state research foundations such as FAPESP, CNPq forms the backbone of Brazil's public research funding system, supporting tens of thousands of researchers across all fields of knowledge.
