Circa Resort & Casino is a casino hotel in downtown Las Vegas, Nevada, at the west end of the Fremont Street Experience. Developed by brothers Derek and Greg Stevens, it opened in October 2020 as the first entirely new casino resort built downtown since 1980, and its 35-story tower is the tallest building in the downtown area.

Features

Circa is noted for the world's largest sportsbook, a three-story facility with a giant high-definition screen; Stadium Swim, a year-round rooftop pool amphitheatre with six pools facing a 40-foot screen; and the world's largest keno board. The casino incorporates the restored "Vegas Vickie" neon cowgirl sign in its lobby. The resort is adults-oriented, originally operating a 21-and-over policy for the property.

Site

The resort occupies the block formerly home to the Las Vegas Club, Mermaids Casino, and the Glitter Gulch strip club, demolished in 2016–2017 for the project.
