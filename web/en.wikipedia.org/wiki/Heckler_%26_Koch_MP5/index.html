The Heckler & Koch MP5 (Maschinenpistole 5) is a 9×19mm submachine gun developed in the 1960s by Heckler & Koch. Using the roller-delayed blowback system of the G3 rifle and firing from a closed bolt, it became renowned for accuracy and reliability.

Variants and use

The family includes the compact MP5K, the integrally suppressed MP5SD, and fixed- and retractable-stock variants. It was adopted by military and law-enforcement units in over 40 countries; its use by the British SAS during the 1980 Iranian Embassy siege brought it worldwide prominence. Although increasingly replaced by carbines, the MP5 remains widely used by police and special forces.
