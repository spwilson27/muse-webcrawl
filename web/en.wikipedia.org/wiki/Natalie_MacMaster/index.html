Natalie MacMaster

Natalie Ann MacMaster CM ONS (born June 13, 1972) is a Canadian fiddler from Troy, Inverness County, Nova Scotia, who plays Cape Breton fiddle music. One of the most celebrated performers of the genre, she has released numerous albums, won Juno Awards, and was made a Member of the Order of Canada. She is married to fellow fiddler Donnell Leahy of the Leahy family band.

Life and career

MacMaster began playing the fiddle at age nine, influenced by her uncle, the noted Cape Breton fiddler Buddy MacMaster, and released her debut album Four on the Floor at sixteen. Her albums Fit as a Fiddle (1993), No Boundaries (1997), In My Hands (1999), and Blueprint (2003) brought her international attention, blending traditional Cape Breton style with step dancing and contemporary arrangements. She has performed with artists including Yo-Yo Ma, Alison Krauss, and the Chieftains, and at events such as the Celtic Colours festival in Cape Breton.

With her husband Donnell Leahy, she recorded the duo album One (2015) and tours with the family project Vishten/Leahy family shows; the couple's children also perform. MacMaster was appointed to the Order of Nova Scotia and the Order of Canada, and has received honorary doctorates from Canadian universities.
