Fortnite Battle Royale

Fortnite Battle Royale is a free-to-play battle royale video game developed and published by Epic Games. It was released as an early access title for Windows, macOS, PlayStation 4, and Xbox One in September 2017, with versions for iOS, Nintendo Switch, and Android following in 2018. It is a companion game to Fortnite: Save the World, a cooperative survival game.

Gameplay

Up to 100 players are dropped from a flying "Battle Bus" onto an island, where they scavenge for weapons and resources while fighting to be the last player or team alive. A shrinking storm circle steadily reduces the playable area, forcing encounters and damaging players caught outside it. Distinctively, players can harvest materials — wood, brick, and metal — and build structures such as walls, ramps, and forts for defense and mobility. The last surviving player or squad earns a "Victory Royale".

Development and release

Epic developed the mode rapidly following the success of PlayerUnknown's Battlegrounds, releasing it within two months of the launch of Save the World. The game is organized into chapters and seasons, each introducing map changes, new mechanics, and a battle pass of cosmetic rewards. Revenue comes from microtransactions for cosmetic items purchased with the in-game currency, V-Bucks.

Reception and impact

Fortnite Battle Royale became a major cultural phenomenon, drawing more than 125 million players in its first year and hundreds of millions of registered accounts thereafter. It is credited with popularizing the battle royale genre and the live-service seasonal model, and its in-game concerts and cross-media events attracted wide mainstream attention. Epic also established competitive play, including the Fortnite World Cup in 2019.
