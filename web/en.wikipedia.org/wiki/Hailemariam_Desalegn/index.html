Hailemariam Desalegn Boshe (born 19 July 1965) is an Ethiopian politician who served as Prime Minister of Ethiopia from 2012 until his resignation in 2018. He succeeded the long-serving prime minister Meles Zenawi, initially in an acting capacity following Meles's death in August 2012, and was confirmed in office the following month.

Early life and career

Hailemariam studied civil engineering at Addis Ababa University and later earned a master's degree in sanitation engineering in Finland. He worked as a lecturer and dean at Arba Minch University before entering politics. He served as president of the Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region from 2001 to 2006, then as deputy prime minister and minister of foreign affairs from 2010.

Premiership

As head of the ruling EPRDF coalition, Hailemariam oversaw continued economic growth but also faced widespread protests in the Oromia and Amhara regions beginning in 2015, driven by grievances over land, representation and human rights, to which the government responded with states of emergency. In February 2018, amid continuing unrest, he announced his resignation as prime minister and party chairman, becoming the first Ethiopian leader to step down in this manner; he was succeeded by Abiy Ahmed in April 2018.
