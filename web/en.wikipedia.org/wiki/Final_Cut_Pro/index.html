Final Cut Pro is a professional non-linear video editing application developed by Apple for macOS. It is widely used by independent filmmakers, broadcasters, and online video creators.

History

Final Cut was originally developed by Macromedia; after acquiring the team and product, Apple released Final Cut Pro in 1999. Successive versions through Final Cut Pro 7 (2009) made it a mainstay of film and television post-production, used on features including The Social Network and The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo. In 2011 Apple replaced it with the rewritten Final Cut Pro X (later renamed Final Cut Pro), introducing a trackless "Magnetic Timeline", background rendering, and a redesigned media organisation model — a launch that was controversial among professionals because of removed features and the absence of backward project compatibility, though many capabilities were restored in later updates. A version for iPad was released in 2023.

Features

Final Cut Pro supports high-resolution workflows including 4K, 8K, ProRes and HDR video, multicam editing, advanced colour grading, 360-degree video, and extensive third-party plug-in support through FxPlug. It is sold for a one-time licence fee on the Mac App Store.
