List of Iranian films

This is a list of films produced in Iran, organized by decade. Iranian cinema has been internationally acclaimed since the 1960s and especially after the Iranian New Wave, with directors such as Abbas Kiarostami, Asghar Farhadi, Majid Majidi, Jafar Panahi and Mohsen Makhmalbaf winning major festival awards.
