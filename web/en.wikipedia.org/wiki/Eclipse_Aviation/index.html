Eclipse Aviation Corporation was the Albuquerque, New Mexico-based manufacturer of the Eclipse 500 very light jet (VLJ). The company pioneered the VLJ category, aiming to produce twin-engine jets in large numbers at a price far below that of traditional business jets, and to enable a new generation of air taxi services.

History

Eclipse Aviation was founded in 1998 by Vern Raburn, a former Microsoft executive. The company developed friction stir welding techniques to speed production and selected the Pratt & Whitney Canada PW610F engine for the Eclipse 500 after difficulties with the original Williams International engine. The Eclipse 500 received FAA certification in 2006, and deliveries began, but production was hampered by supplier problems, quality issues, and persistent cash shortages.

Bankruptcy and successor

The company ceased operations in November 2008 and filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection; in March 2009 the case was converted to Chapter 7 liquidation. Its assets were acquired by Eclipse Aerospace, which later merged with Kestrel Aircraft to form ONE Aviation, resuming support and production of the design as the Eclipse 550.
