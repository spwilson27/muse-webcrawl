This is an incomplete list of mountains by elevation above sea level. All of the 14 mountains in the world higher than 8,000 metres (the eight-thousanders) are in the Himalaya and Karakoram ranges of Asia. The highest peak outside Asia is Aconcagua in the Andes of Argentina.
