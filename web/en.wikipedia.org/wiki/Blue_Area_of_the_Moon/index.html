Blue Area of the Moon

The Blue Area of the Moon is a fictional location in Marvel Comics. It is a region on Earth's Moon, located in the crater Copernicus area as depicted in the comics, containing the ruins of an ancient city built by the alien Kree millions of years ago. The Blue Area has an artificial atmosphere of breathable air and Earth-like gravity, allowing characters to survive there without spacesuits. It was created for the Skrull–Kree War era stories and first appeared in Fantastic Four #98 (1970).

Fictional history

Within Marvel continuity, the Kree constructed the city as an outpost and site of a contest between the Kree and the Cotati. The Blue Area later became a battleground in conflicts involving the Fantastic Four, the Inhumans and the Avengers. It was also the site of the Watcher Uatu's citadel. The Blue Area is notable as the location of the apparent death of Jean Grey's Phoenix in the X-Men's "Dark Phoenix Saga" (1980), where the Phoenix, judged a threat to the universe, took her own life on the lunar surface there.
