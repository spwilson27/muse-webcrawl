MODAF

MODAF viewpoints organize architecture descriptions

The Ministry of Defence Architecture Framework (MODAF) is an enterprise architecture framework developed by the United Kingdom's Ministry of Defence to support the planning and management of defence capabilities, particularly network-enabled capability. It is closely related to the United States Department of Defense Architecture Framework (DoDAF), from which it was adapted.

Overview

MODAF defines a set of standardized viewpoints and views for describing architectures from different perspectives: Strategic (StV), Operational (OV), Systems (SV), Technical Standards (TV), Acquisition (AcV) and Service-Oriented (SOV) viewpoints, along with the All Views (AV) that provide context. Version 1.2 of MODAF defined 47 views. Underpinning the framework is the MODAF Meta-Model (M3), which defines the data elements and relationships so that architectures produced by different teams can be integrated and exchanged.

Development and succession

MODAF was released in the mid-2000s and went through versions 1.0 to 1.2.4. The UK MoD subsequently moved, together with NATO partners, toward adoption of the NATO Architecture Framework (NAF), whose version 4 is based on the Unified Architecture Framework (UAF) — a convergence of MODAF, DoDAF and related frameworks maintained by the Object Management Group.
