The Grinch is a 2018 American animated Christmas comedy film produced by Illumination and distributed by Universal Pictures. Directed by Scott Mosier and Yarrow Cheney, it is the third screen adaptation of Dr. Seuss's 1957 book How the Grinch Stole Christmas!, following the 1966 television special and the 2000 live-action film. The film stars the voice of Benedict Cumberbatch as the title character, with Pharrell Williams as the narrator.

Plot

The Grinch, a cynical green creature living alone on Mount Crumpit with his dog Max, is driven to distraction by the Whos of Whoville's preparations for an ever-larger Christmas celebration. Determined to stop Christmas from coming, he disguises himself as Santa Claus and, with a reluctant Max posing as a reindeer, steals every present, tree and decoration in the town. A chance encounter with the kind-hearted Cindy Lou Who, who wants only to ask Santa to help her overworked single mother, begins his change of heart. Hearing the Whos sing on Christmas morning despite their losses, the Grinch realises Christmas "doesn't come from a store", returns everything, and is welcomed to the Whos' feast.

Reception

The film grossed $526 million worldwide against a $75 million budget, becoming the highest-grossing Christmas film of all time at its release and Illumination's third-highest-grossing film up to that point. Reviews were mixed: critics praised the animation and Cumberbatch's performance while finding the adaptation unnecessary and less witty than the source. Tyler, the Creator recorded new versions of "You're a Mean One, Mr. Grinch" and an original song, "I Am the Grinch", for the soundtrack.
