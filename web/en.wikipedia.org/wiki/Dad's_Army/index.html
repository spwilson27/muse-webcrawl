Dad's Army

Dad's Army is a British television sitcom about the Home Guard during the Second World War. Written by Jimmy Perry and David Croft, it was broadcast on BBC1 from 1968 to 1977, running for nine series and 80 episodes, plus radio adaptations, a stage show, and two feature films (1971 and 2016).

Premise

The series is set in the fictional seaside town of Walmington-on-Sea and follows a platoon of Local Defence Volunteers — mostly men too old or otherwise ineligible for regular military service. The platoon is commanded by the pompous bank manager Captain Mainwaring (Arthur Lowe), assisted by the suave Sergeant Wilson (John Le Mesurier), the excitable Lance Corporal Jones (Clive Dunn), and privates including the black-marketeer Walker, the elderly Godfrey, the "stupid boy" Pike, and the doom-laden Frazer.

Legacy

Regularly voted among Britain's best-loved sitcoms — it ranked first in the 2004 BBC poll Britain's Best Sitcom — the show produced famous catchphrases including "Don't panic!", "They don't like it up 'em", and "You stupid boy". Its theme tune, "Who Do You Think You Are Kidding, Mr Hitler?", won an Ivor Novello Award.
