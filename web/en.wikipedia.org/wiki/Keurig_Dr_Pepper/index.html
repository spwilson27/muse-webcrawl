Keurig Dr Pepper Inc. is a North American beverage company formed in 2018 by the merger of Keurig Green Mountain, the maker of the Keurig single-serve coffee brewing system, with the Dr Pepper Snapple Group. It is the third-largest beverage company in North America, with a portfolio spanning soft drinks, coffee systems, juices and mixers.

History

Keurig, founded in Massachusetts in 1992, built its business around the K-Cup pod system for single-cup coffee. Green Mountain Coffee Roasters acquired Keurig in 2006 and took its name in 2014; the company was taken private in 2016 by JAB Holding Company in a deal worth about $13.9 billion. Dr Pepper Snapple Group, headquartered in Plano, Texas, traced its origins to Dr Pepper, created in Waco, Texas in 1885, and to Snapple and the soft-drink bottling operations spun out of Cadbury in 2008.

The merger closed on July 9, 2018, with Dr Pepper Snapple shareholders receiving a special dividend and a stake in the combined company, which took the name Keurig Dr Pepper and dual headquarters in Burlington, Massachusetts and Frisco, Texas. The company has since made further acquisitions, including stakes in energy-drink and ready-to-drink coffee brands, and in 2025 announced a plan to acquire JDE Peet's and subsequently split its coffee and beverage businesses.

Products

The company's brands include Dr Pepper, Keurig brewers and K-Cup pods, Green Mountain Coffee Roasters, 7 Up (in North America), Canada Dry, A&W, Sunkist soda, Snapple, Hawaiian Punch, Mott's and Clamato.
