Zinedine Zidane

Born: 23 June 1972, Marseille, France
Position: Attacking midfielder
Clubs: Cannes, Bordeaux, Juventus, Real Madrid
France: 108 caps

Zinedine Yazid Zidane (born 23 June 1972), popularly known as Zizou, is a French former professional footballer who played as an attacking midfielder, and later a manager. Widely regarded as one of the greatest players of all time, he was known for elegance, vision, passing and technique.

Club career

After Cannes and Bordeaux, Zidane joined Juventus in 1996, winning Serie A titles. In 2001 he moved to Real Madrid for a then-world-record fee, winning La Liga and the 2002 UEFA Champions League, in which he scored a famous left-foot volley in the final against Bayer Leverkusen.

International career

Zidane scored twice in France's 1998 World Cup final win over Brazil and led France to Euro 2000 victory. He retired after the 2006 World Cup, where France reached the final and he was sent off in extra time for headbutting Marco Materazzi.

Managerial career

As Real Madrid head coach (2016–2018, 2019–2021) he won three consecutive Champions League titles and two La Liga titles.
