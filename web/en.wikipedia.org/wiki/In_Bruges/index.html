In Bruges

Directed by: Martin McDonagh

Starring: Colin Farrell, Brendan Gleeson, Ralph Fiennes

Released: 2008

Running time: 107 minutes

In Bruges is a 2008 black comedy-drama crime film written and directed by Martin McDonagh in his feature-length directorial debut. It stars Colin Farrell and Brendan Gleeson as two London-based Irish hitmen hiding out in Bruges, Belgium, after a job gone wrong, and Ralph Fiennes as their ruthless boss.

Plot

After accidentally killing a young boy during his first hit, rookie hitman Ray is sent with his partner Ken to lie low in Bruges and await instructions. Ken embraces sightseeing in the medieval city, while Ray, consumed by guilt, despises it. Their boss Harry eventually orders Ken to kill Ray for the botched job; Ken cannot bring himself to do it, and the confrontation that follows draws Harry himself to Bruges for a violent reckoning.

Production

McDonagh wrote the script after a short visit to Bruges left him torn between admiration for its beauty and boredom. The film was shot on location in Bruges in early 2007, and McDonagh later adapted elements of the story from his unrealised stage ideas. The score was composed by Carter Burwell.

Reception

The film premiered at the Sundance Film Festival in January 2008 and was widely praised for its script, dark humour, and performances. Farrell won the Golden Globe Award for Best Actor in a Comedy or Musical, and McDonagh was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay. In Bruges has since acquired a strong cult following and is frequently cited among the best films of the 2000s.
