Markos Vafeiadis

Markos Vafeiadis
Born: 1906, Tosya, Ottoman Empire
Died: 22 February 1992, Athens, Greece
Known for: Commanding the Democratic Army of Greece (DSE)

Markos Vafeiadis (1906 – 22 February 1992) was a leading figure of the Communist Party of Greece (KKE) and the commander of the Democratic Army of Greece (DSE) during the Greek Civil War (1946–1949).

Early life

Born in Tosya in Anatolia to a Greek family, Vafeiadis came to Greece as a refugee after the Greco-Turkish War and worked as a tobacco worker in Thessaloniki and Kavala, becoming active in the communist movement and labour unions.

Civil war and later life

During the Axis occupation he was a guerrilla in the ELAS resistance. In 1946 he took command of the newly formed DSE, but clashed with KKE general secretary Nikos Zachariadis over strategy — Vafeiadis favoured guerrilla warfare, Zachariadis a conventional army — and was removed from command in 1948 and later expelled from the party. After the communist defeat he lived in exile in the Soviet Union; he returned to Greece in 1983 and was eventually reconciled with the KKE.
