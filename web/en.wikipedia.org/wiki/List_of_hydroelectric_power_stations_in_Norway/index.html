List of hydroelectric power stations in Norway

Hydroelectric power in Norway supplies the overwhelming majority of the country's electricity — historically around 90 per cent of production — from more than 1,600 hydroelectric power stations, exploiting the country's mountainous terrain, high rainfall and numerous reservoirs.

Norway's reservoir storage capacity — roughly 86 TWh — is the largest in Europe and amounts to about half of the continent's total reservoir capacity, allowing Norwegian power to balance wind and solar generation in neighbouring countries through interconnectors. Major operators include Statkraft, the state-owned utility that is Europe's largest producer of renewable energy.
