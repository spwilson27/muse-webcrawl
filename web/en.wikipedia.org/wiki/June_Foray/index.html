June Foray (born June Lucille Forer; 18 September 1917 – 26 July 2017) was an American voice actress, best known as the voice of Rocky the Flying Squirrel and Natasha Fatale in The Rocky and Bullwinkle Show. Chuck Jones famously remarked that "June Foray is not the female Mel Blanc; Mel Blanc was the male June Foray."

Career

Foray began on radio in her teens and moved to Los Angeles, becoming a regular in radio comedy and drama. From the 1940s she worked for Walt Disney, Warner Bros., Hanna-Barbera, Jay Ward and others. For Warner Bros. she voiced Granny (the owner of Tweety) and the mischievous Witch Hazel; at MGM and elsewhere she voiced countless incidental characters.

Her signature roles came with Jay Ward's Rocky and His Friends (1959), where she voiced the earnest Rocket J. Squirrel, villainess Natasha Fatale, and numerous others — nearly every female character in the series. She was Cindy Lou Who in the 1966 television special How the Grinch Stole Christmas! and later voiced characters in The Smurfs, DuckTales (as Ma Beagle and Magica De Spell) and The Powerpuff Girls.

Advocacy and honours

Foray was a tireless advocate for animation: she helped found ASIFA-Hollywood, created the Annie Awards, and successfully championed the creation of the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature. She received a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame in 2000 and, at 95, won a Daytime Emmy Award in 2013 for her role as Mrs. Cauldron on The Garfield Show, becoming the oldest Emmy nominee and winner at the time.

She died on 26 July 2017, two months short of her 100th birthday.
