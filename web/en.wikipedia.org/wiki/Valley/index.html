Valley

[Image: A green river valley between forested hills]
A green river valley between forested hills

A valley is a low area of land between hills or mountains, typically with a river or stream flowing through it. Valleys are most commonly formed by erosion of the land by rivers over a long period, or by glacial action, which carves deep U-shaped valleys. Rift valleys form where tectonic plates pull apart.

Famous valleys include the Nile Valley, the Great Rift Valley of East Africa, California's Central Valley, the Yosemite Valley, the Rhine Valley and the Kashmir Valley. Valleys have often been centres of settlement and agriculture because of their water, fertile soil and shelter.
