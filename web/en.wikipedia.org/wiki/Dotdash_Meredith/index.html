Dotdash Meredith

Dotdash Meredith is an American digital media and print publishing company based in Des Moines, Iowa, and New York City. It was formed in 2021 when Dotdash, owned by IAC, acquired Meredith Corporation's magazine and digital businesses for approximately $2.7 billion. Meredith, founded in 1902 by Edwin Thomas Meredith, had been one of the largest magazine publishers in the United States, with titles including Better Homes and Gardens, People, Southern Living, and Real Simple.

Brands

The combined company publishes brands such as People, Better Homes and Gardens, Food & Wine, Travel + Leisure, Entertainment Weekly (digital-only after 2022), InStyle, and websites including The Spruce, Verywell, Investopedia, Serious Eats, and Allrecipes. It describes itself as the largest digital and print publisher in America.
