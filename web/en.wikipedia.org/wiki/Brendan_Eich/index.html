Brendan Eich

Born: 4 July 1961, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania

Known for: Creating JavaScript; co-founding Mozilla; Brave browser

Brendan Eich is an American computer programmer and technology executive best known as the creator of the JavaScript programming language. While at Netscape in 1995, he developed JavaScript (originally "Mocha", then "LiveScript") in about ten days.

Career

Eich co-founded the Mozilla project in 1998 and served as chief architect and later CTO of Mozilla. In March 2014 he became Mozilla's CEO but resigned after eleven days amid controversy over a past political donation. In 2015 he founded Brave Software, maker of the privacy-focused Brave browser, and launched the Basic Attention Token.
