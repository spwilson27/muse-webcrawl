MetLife Stadium is a multi-purpose stadium at the Meadowlands Sports Complex in East Rutherford, New Jersey, near New York City. Opened in 2010, it replaced the original Giants Stadium and is the home of both the New York Giants and the New York Jets of the National Football League — one of only two stadiums shared by two NFL teams. It is among the largest stadiums in the NFL, with a capacity of 82,500.

History

The stadium was built privately by the Giants and Jets for about $1.6 billion, making it one of the most expensive stadiums ever constructed. It opened as New Meadowlands Stadium before MetLife purchased the naming rights in 2011. The two teams alternate the stadium's colour lighting scheme: blue for the Giants and green for the Jets.

Major events

MetLife Stadium hosted Super Bowl XLVIII in February 2014, the first Super Bowl played outdoors in a cold-weather city, in which the Seattle Seahawks defeated the Denver Broncos. It has also hosted WrestleMania 29 and 35, the 2016 Copa América Centenario final, and major concerts. The stadium is scheduled to host the final of the 2026 FIFA World Cup.
