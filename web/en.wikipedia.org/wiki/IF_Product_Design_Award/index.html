iF Product Design Award

The iF Product Design Award was introduced in 1954 and is annually conferred by the iF International Forum Design GmbH, based in Hannover, Germany. It is one of the world's oldest and most prestigious design awards, alongside the Red Dot Award and the IDEA awards.

History

The iF (Industrie Forum Design) was founded in 1953, shortly after the end of the Second World War, to promote and support industrial design in Germany. The first awards were presented at the Hannover Fair in 1954. Over the decades the competition grew into an international event attracting thousands of entries from dozens of countries each year.

Categories and judging

Today the iF Design Award covers disciplines including product design, packaging, communication design, interior architecture, professional concept, service design, and architecture. Entries are judged by an international jury of design experts on criteria such as idea, form, function, differentiation, and impact. The best entries each year receive the iF Gold Award.

Recognition

Winning products may carry the iF label, which is widely used in marketing as a mark of design quality. The award is particularly prominent in consumer electronics, automotive, and industrial equipment industries.
