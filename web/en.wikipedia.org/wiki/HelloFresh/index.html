HelloFresh SE is a German publicly traded meal-kit company headquartered in Berlin. It is generally regarded as the largest meal-kit provider in the world, operating in numerous countries across Europe, North America and Australasia. Subscribers choose recipes online and receive weekly boxes containing pre-portioned ingredients and recipe cards.

History

HelloFresh was founded in November 2011 by Dominik Richter, Thomas Griesel and Jessica Nilsson, and was initially backed by Rocket Internet, which also funded its rapid international expansion. The company rolled out across Europe and launched in the United States in 2013. In November 2017 HelloFresh completed its initial public offering on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange, raising about €318 million, shortly before its American rival Blue Apron's own IPO difficulties.

Business model and expansion

The company sells subscription meal plans (typically two to six recipes per week for two or four people) sourced through regional fulfilment centres. In 2018 it acquired the US organic meal-kit company Green Chef, and in 2020 it bought Factor75, a prepared-meals business later branded Factor. Demand surged during the COVID-19 pandemic, pushing the company to sustained profitability and entry into Germany's MDAX and later DAX index discussions.

Criticism

HelloFresh has faced criticism over subscription cancellation practices, packaging waste, and labour conditions at some fulfilment sites, alongside industry-wide questions about the environmental footprint of meal kits compared with conventional grocery shopping.
