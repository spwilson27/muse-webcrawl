Up in Smoke is a 1978 American comedy film directed by Lou Adler and starring Cheech Marin and Tommy Chong, the comedy duo Cheech & Chong. It was the duo's first feature film and is widely regarded as the first of the "stoner comedy" genre.

Plot

Unemployed drummer Pedro de Pacas (Marin) and pot-smoking hippie Anthony "Man" Stoner (Chong) meet by chance and embark on a series of misadventures in search of marijuana. Deported to Mexico, they unwittingly agree to drive a van back to the United States — a van constructed entirely out of marijuana ("fiberweed") — while being pursued by the inept narcotics officer Sergeant Stedenko and his team. The film culminates in a battle of the bands at the Roxy Theatre in Los Angeles.

Reception and legacy

Made on a modest budget, the film was a major commercial success, grossing over $100 million worldwide, and helped define counterculture comedy of the late 1970s. It spawned a series of sequels and follow-ups, including Cheech and Chong's Next Movie (1980) and Nice Dreams (1981).
