2018 Commonwealth Games

The 2018 Commonwealth Games, officially the XXI Commonwealth Games and branded Gold Coast 2018, were held on the Gold Coast, Queensland, Australia, from 4 to 15 April 2018. It was the fifth time Australia had hosted the Commonwealth Games. Around 4,400 athletes from 71 Commonwealth nations and territories competed in 19 sports and 275 events.

Highlights

Gold Coast 2018 was the first major multi-sport event to achieve gender equality in medal events, with an equal number of events for men and women, and featured the largest integrated para-sport programme in the Games' history. Beach volleyball made its Commonwealth debut, and women's rugby sevens was contested for the first time. Hosts Australia dominated the medal table, while Vanuatu won its first-ever Commonwealth medals. The Games also saw controversy when a number of athletes and officials failed to depart Australia after the event.
