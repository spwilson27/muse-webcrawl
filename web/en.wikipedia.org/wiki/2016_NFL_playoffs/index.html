2016 NFL playoffs

The 2016 NFL playoffs began on Saturday, January 7, 2017, following the 2016 regular season, and concluded with Super Bowl LI on February 5, 2017, at NRG Stadium in Houston, Texas. By NFL convention the playoffs are named for the year in which they begin, even though most games are played in the following calendar year.

Participants

The AFC was led by the top-seeded New England Patriots (14–2), followed by the Kansas City Chiefs, Pittsburgh Steelers, Houston Texans, Oakland Raiders and Miami Dolphins. In the NFC, the Dallas Cowboys (13–3) held the top seed, followed by the Atlanta Falcons, Seattle Seahawks, Green Bay Packers, New York Giants and Detroit Lions.

Notable games

The wild card round saw home teams sweep: Oakland lost to Houston with rookie Connor Cook making his first career start; Seattle beat Detroit; Pittsburgh defeated Miami; and Green Bay beat the Giants behind a Hail Mary touchdown by Aaron Rodgers. In the divisional round, Green Bay upset Dallas 34–31 on a last-second field goal after a long sideline completion, while Atlanta, New England and Pittsburgh also advanced. The conference championships went to the Falcons (44–21 over Green Bay) and the Patriots (36–17 over Pittsburgh).

Super Bowl LI

In Super Bowl LI, the Patriots overcame a 28–3 third-quarter deficit to defeat the Falcons 34–28 in the first Super Bowl to go to overtime — the largest comeback in Super Bowl history. Tom Brady was named MVP.
