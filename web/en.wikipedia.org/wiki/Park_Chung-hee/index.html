Park Chung-hee

Park Chung-hee (박정희)
Born: 14 November 1917, Gumi, Korea (under Japanese rule)
Died: 26 October 1979 (aged 61), Seoul, South Korea
President of South Korea: 1962/1963 – 1979
Spouse: Yuk Young-soo
Children: including Park Geun-hye
Cause of death: Assassination

Park Chung-hee (Korean: 박정희; 14 November 1917 – 26 October 1979) was a South Korean politician and army general who led South Korea from 1961 until his assassination in 1979. He came to power in the May 16 coup of 1961, headed the military junta (the Supreme Council for National Reconstruction), and was elected president in 1963, ruling through the Third and Fourth Republics.

Economic policy

Park's government pursued aggressive state-directed industrialisation through Five-Year Plans, export promotion, and support for the great family conglomerates (chaebol). Per-capita income rose dramatically during his rule — the period associated with the "Miracle on the Han River" — and projects such as the Pohang steel mill (POSCO) and the Gyeongbu Expressway became symbols of the era. The New Village Movement (Saemaul Undong) sought to modernise the countryside.

Authoritarian rule

Park's rule was authoritarian. In October 1972 he declared martial law, dissolved the National Assembly, and promulgated the Yushin Constitution, which granted him sweeping powers and effectively life tenure. His regime suppressed dissent through the Korean Central Intelligence Agency and emergency decrees; critics, including future president Kim Dae-jung (kidnapped from Tokyo in 1973), were persecuted. Normalisation of relations with Japan (1965) and the dispatch of South Korean troops to the Vietnam War were major foreign-policy decisions.

Assassination and legacy

On 26 October 1979, Park was shot dead by Kim Jae-gyu, the director of the KCIA, during a dinner at a safe house in Seoul, amid growing unrest (the Bu-Ma Democratic Protests). His legacy remains deeply contested in South Korea: he is credited with laying the foundations of the country's economic rise, and condemned for dictatorship and human-rights abuses. His daughter Park Geun-hye served as president from 2013 until her impeachment and removal in 2017.
