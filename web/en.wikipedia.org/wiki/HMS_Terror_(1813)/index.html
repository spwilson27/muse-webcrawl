HMS Terror (1813)

HMS Terror was a specialised warship and a bomb vessel built in 1813. Converted for polar exploration, she took part in expeditions to both poles, including James Clark Ross's Antarctic voyage (1839–1843) — Mount Terror in Antarctica is named for her.

Franklin expedition

In 1845, Terror and HMS Erebus sailed under Sir John Franklin in search of the Northwest Passage. Both ships became icebound in the Canadian Arctic and were lost with all hands. Terror's wreck was located in 2016 in Terror Bay, off King William Island, remarkably well preserved.
