Overview

The Casbah is the citadel and old quarter of Algiers, Algeria — a historic medina of palaces, mosques, Ottoman-era houses and steep lanes descending the hill to the bay. The word casbah (qasba) refers to the citadel at its summit, built in the 16th century under Ottoman regency.

History

Algiers grew on the site of Phoenician and Roman Icosium and was refounded in the 10th century. The Casbah was a stronghold of the FLN during the Battle of Algiers (1956–57), depicted in the film The Battle of Algiers. Decay, overcrowding and earthquakes threaten its fabric despite UNESCO listing; notable buildings include the Ketchaoua Mosque and the Dar Aziza palace.
