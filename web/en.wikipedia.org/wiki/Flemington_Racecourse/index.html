Flemington Racecourse
LocationFlemington, Melbourne, Victoria, Australia
Opened1840
Owned byVictoria Racing Club
Notable racesMelbourne Cup, Victoria Derby

Flemington Racecourse is a major horse racing venue in Melbourne, Australia. It is most notable for hosting the Melbourne Cup, which is Australia's richest horse race. The racecourse is situated on low alluvial flats beside the Maribyrnong River, in the inner-western suburb of Flemington.

History

Racing has been held at the site since 1840, and the first Melbourne Cup was run there in 1861. The Victoria Racing Club, founded in 1864, administers the course. Flemington has a circumference of about 2,310 metres and a famous 1,200-metre straight known as the "Straight Six." The course and its rose gardens are heritage-listed, and the Melbourne Cup Carnival each November draws crowds exceeding 100,000 on Cup Day.

Transport

The course is served by its own railway station on the Flemington Racecourse line, which operates on race days, and by tram services along Epsom Road and Mount Alexander Road.
