Tanumshede

Tanumshede is a locality in Tanum Municipality, Västra Götaland County, Sweden, with a population of just over 1,600. It serves as the municipal seat and is best known internationally for the Bronze Age rock carvings of the surrounding Tanum plain.

Rock carvings

The Rock Carvings in Tanum were inscribed on UNESCO's World Heritage List in 1994. Spread across the area around Tanumshede are some 1,500 known carving sites with thousands of images from the Nordic Bronze Age (c. 1800–500 BCE), depicting ships, warriors, ploughing scenes, animals, sun symbols and ritual processions. Notable panels include Vitlycke — which features the famous "Bridal Couple" — Aspeberget, Litsleby and Fossum.

The locality

The Vitlycke Museum in Tanumshede serves as the visitor and research centre for the World Heritage site. The town itself is a small service centre for the agricultural and tourist economy of northern Bohuslän, connected by the E6 motorway and the Bohus Line railway between Gothenburg and Strömstad.
