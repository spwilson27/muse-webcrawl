Luke Spencer

First appearance: 1978
Portrayed by: Anthony Geary
Series: General Hospital
Spouse: Laura Spencer (divorced), Tracy Quartermaine (divorced)

Lucas Lorenzo "Luke" Spencer is a fictional character on the American television soap opera General Hospital, played by Anthony Geary from his introduction in 1978 until Geary's retirement in 2015 (with a brief 2017 appearance). One half of the supercouple "Luke and Laura", Luke is among the most famous characters in daytime television history.

Character history

Luke arrived in Port Charles as a small-time mob-connected street hustler working at the Campus Disco. Hired by Frank Smith to kill Senator Mitch Williams, he fell in love with Laura Webber; their romance — controversially beginning with a 1979 storyline in which Luke raped Laura at the disco, later reframed by the show and long debated — propelled General Hospital to record ratings. The couple's on-screen wedding on 16 November 1981 was watched by some 30 million viewers, the largest audience in American soap history, with Elizabeth Taylor guest-starring as Helena Cassadine.

Luke and Laura's adventures took the show into action-adventure territory: foiling the Cassadines' Ice Princess weather machine (1981), the Aztec treasure, and repeated battles with the Cassadine family. Luke went on to become mayor of Port Charles, club owner (Luke's, later the Haunted Star) and a perpetual schemer with a wandering eye, later marrying Tracy Quartermaine. Anthony Geary won a record eight Daytime Emmy Awards for the role. The character was killed off-screen in a cable-car accident revealed in 2022, and memorialised in a special episode.

Legacy

Luke Spencer transformed the soap antihero and, with Laura, defined the "supercouple" phenomenon of the 1980s.
