Ng Mui (Chinese: 五枚) is a semi-legendary Chinese martial artist said to have been a Buddhist nun of the Shaolin Monastery and one of the Five Elders who survived the monastery's destruction during the Qing dynasty. In the most famous tradition, she is the creator of the martial art that became Wing Chun.

Legend

Accounts — transmitted orally through Wing Chun lineages rather than historical records — say Ng Mui, inspired by watching a crane fight a snake (or fox), devised a compact, economical fighting system suited to a smaller person. She taught it to a young woman named Yim Wing-chun, who used it to defeat a local bully, and the art took the student's name.

Historical status

No contemporary evidence confirms Ng Mui's existence; historians treat her as a founding figure of lineage legend, comparable to other Shaolin survivor myths of the period.
