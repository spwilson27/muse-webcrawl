Techlash

Techlash is a portmanteau of "technology" and "backlash" describing the growing public, political, and media hostility toward large technology companies — particularly the major American firms such as Google, Apple, Facebook (Meta), and Amazon — that intensified in the late 2010s.

Causes

The backlash was driven by concerns over data privacy scandals such as Cambridge Analytica, the spread of misinformation and its role in elections, market concentration and antitrust issues, tax avoidance, content moderation disputes, the gig economy's labor practices, and the perceived social harms of social media.

Responses

Governments responded with investigations, fines, and new regulation, including the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Digital Markets Act. The term was named a word of the year by Oxford Dictionaries in 2018, reflecting the concept's broad currency.
