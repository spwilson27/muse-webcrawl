SES S.A. is a Luxembourgish satellite telecommunications company headquartered at the Château de Betzdorf in Betzdorf, Luxembourg. It is one of the world's largest satellite operators, providing satellite communication services to broadcasters, content providers, internet service providers, governments and institutions.

History

The company was founded in 1985 as Société Européenne des Satellites with the backing of the Luxembourg government. Its first satellite, Astra 1A, was launched in 1988 and began delivering television channels to homes across Europe from the 19.2° East orbital position, helping to pioneer direct-to-home satellite television. SES expanded globally through acquisitions, notably GE Americom (which became SES Americom) and New Skies Satellites, and built up the O3b Networks medium Earth orbit constellation, later followed by the high-throughput O3b mPOWER system.

In 2024 SES agreed to acquire its rival Intelsat in a deal valued at about $3.1 billion, aimed at creating a stronger competitor in a market reshaped by low Earth orbit constellations. SES operates a fleet of dozens of geostationary and medium Earth orbit satellites.
