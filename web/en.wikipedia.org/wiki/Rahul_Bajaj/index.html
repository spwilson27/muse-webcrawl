Rahul Bajaj

Rahul Bajaj (10 June 1938 – 12 February 2022) was an Indian billionaire businessman and philanthropist. He was the chairman emeritus of the Bajaj Group and led Bajaj Auto for nearly five decades, building it into one of the world's largest manufacturers of motorcycles and three-wheelers.

Life and career

Born in Calcutta into the Bajaj industrialist family, he was educated at St. Stephen's College, Delhi, and Harvard Business School. He took over the reins of Bajaj Auto in 1968 and oversaw the success of the Bajaj Chetak scooter, a symbol of India's middle class in the licence-raj era. He served two terms in the Rajya Sabha, the upper house of India's Parliament, and was known for speaking candidly on economic policy.

Honours

Bajaj received the Padma Bhushan, India's third-highest civilian award, in 2001. He died in Pune in February 2022.
