Chalcophile element

Chalcophile elements are those that prefer to bond with sulfur and other chalcogens rather than oxygen, in the Goldschmidt classification of elements. They include sulfur, selenium, tellurium, and metals such as copper, zinc, lead, silver, mercury, cadmium and arsenic.

Because of their affinity for sulfide, chalcophile elements are concentrated in sulfide ores and were depleted from the Earth's crust into the core and sulfide phases during planetary differentiation. They are less abundant in silicate crust than lithophile elements.
