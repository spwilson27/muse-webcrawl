GeoGebra

Developer: Markus Hohenwarter et al.

Initial release: 2001

Written in: Java, HTML5

Type: Interactive geometry software

License: Freeware (with source-available components)

GeoGebra is an interactive geometry, algebra, statistics and calculus application, intended for learning and teaching mathematics and science from primary school to university level. GeoGebra is available on multiple platforms, with apps for desktops (Windows, macOS and Linux), tablets (Android, iPad and Windows Store) and web.

History

GeoGebra was created by Markus Hohenwarter as part of his master's thesis at the University of Salzburg in Austria in 2001. Following successful crowdfunding campaigns, the company behind GeoGebra expanded its offering to include additional apps and services aimed at schools and teachers.

Features

GeoGebra brings together geometry, algebra, spreadsheets, graphing, statistics and calculus in a single package. Points, vectors, segments, lines, polygons and conic sections can be constructed dynamically, and all objects can be changed dynamically afterwards. Equations and coordinates can be entered directly, and the software can deal with variables for numbers, vectors and points. The GeoGebra Materials platform hosts millions of free learning resources created by teachers and students worldwide.

Licensing

GeoGebra is freely available for non-commercial use. Parts of the software are released under open licenses, while materials created with it may be shared under Creative Commons licenses through the GeoGebra Materials platform.
