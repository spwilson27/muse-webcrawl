Ryohgo Narita (成田 良悟, Narita Ryōgo, born May 30, 1980) is a Japanese light novel author best known for the series Baccano! and Durarara!!. His works are characterized by very large ensemble casts, interlocking non-linear storylines, and urban settings in which the supernatural collides with everyday life and organized crime.

Career

Narita made his debut in 2003 with Baccano!, which won the Gold Prize at the 9th Dengeki Novel Prize. Set largely in Prohibition-era America, the series follows alchemists who attained immortality in 1711 and the tangled conflicts that follow them across centuries. In 2004 he began Durarara!!, set in Ikebukuro, Tokyo, featuring a headless rider (Celty Sturluson), superhumanly strong bartender Shizuo Heiwajima, and information broker Izaya Orihara. Both series were adapted into popular anime television series by the studio Brain's Base.

Narita's other works include Vamp!, Etsusa Bridge, and the Ketsuekiba no Tabi ("Dead Mount Death Play," as manga writer), and he is the author of Fate/strange Fake in Type-Moon's Fate franchise. He frequently describes his writing style as piling up characters and letting their collisions generate the plot.
