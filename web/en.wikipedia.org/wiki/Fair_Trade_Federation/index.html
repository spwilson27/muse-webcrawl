Fair Trade Federation

The Fair Trade Federation (FTF) is a North American trade association of organisations that are fully committed to fair trade. Founded in 1994 as the North American Alternative Trade Organization (NAATO), it changed its name to the Fair Trade Federation in 1995. Unlike product-certification systems, the FTF evaluates the entire organisation: members are screened against nine principles covering fair wages, safe working conditions, long-term trading relationships, environmental stewardship, transparency, and respect for cultural identity.

Membership and principles

Members include retailers, wholesalers and producers' partners in the United States and Canada, selling handicrafts, textiles, coffee, food and other goods sourced largely from artisans and farmers in the developing world. The federation's nine principles require members to create opportunities for economically disadvantaged producers, pay promptly and fairly, ensure safe and empowering working conditions, support capacity building, promote fair trade, respect the environment, and maintain transparency and accountability.

Activities

The FTF holds an annual conference, provides education and advocacy on fair trade, and operates within the broader global movement alongside bodies such as the World Fair Trade Organization (WFTO), of which it has been closely affiliated. It celebrated World Fair Trade Day and campaigns for trade policies benefiting marginalised producers.
