John Wick is a fictional character and the titular protagonist of the John Wick film franchise, portrayed by Keanu Reeves. Wick is a legendary retired hitman, known in the criminal underworld as the "Baba Yaga", who is forced back into violent action after gangsters steal his car and kill the puppy left to him by his late wife, Helen.

Story

In the first film (2014), directed by Chad Stahelski, Wick's rampage against the Tarasov crime family draws him back into the world governed by the High Table, with its Continental hotels, gold-coin currency and strict rules. The sequels — John Wick: Chapter 2 (2017), Chapter 3 – Parabellum (2019) and Chapter 4 (2023) — expand the mythology as Wick fights a growing bounty on his head and seeks a path to freedom, culminating in a duel in Paris at the end of the fourth film, after which he is believed dead; a grave bearing his name appears beside Helen's.

The character is noted for the franchise's distinctive "gun fu" action choreography, blending firearms with judo and jiu-jitsu, and for Reeves's extensive weapons and driving training. Wick has appeared across the franchise's spin-offs, comics and video games.
