Sikorsky Aircraft

Sikorsky Aircraft is an American aircraft manufacturer based in Stratford, Connecticut. It was established by aviation pioneer Igor Sikorsky in 1923 and was among the first companies to manufacture helicopters for civilian and military use.

History

Sikorsky developed the VS-300 in 1939, the first viable American helicopter using a single main rotor configuration that became standard worldwide. The company became a subsidiary of United Technologies in 1929 and was sold to Lockheed Martin in 2015.

Products

Well-known products include the UH-60 Black Hawk, SH-60 Seahawk, CH-53 family, S-76 and S-92 civilian helicopters, and earlier flying boats such as the S-42.
