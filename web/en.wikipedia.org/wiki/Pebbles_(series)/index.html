Pebbles (series)

LabelForced Exposure / various reissue labels
GenreGarage rock, psychedelic rock, proto-punk
First volume1978 (BFD Records)
CuratorGreg Shaw (associated)

The Pebbles compilations collected obscure 1960s American garage-rock singles.

Pebbles is an extensive series of compilation albums of 1960s American garage rock and psychedelic music, issued on LP and later CD. The series was inspired by Lenny Kaye's landmark 1972 double album Nuggets: Original Artyfacts from the First Psychedelic Era. The first Pebbles volume appeared in 1978, drawing on the collection and enthusiasm of Greg Shaw of Bomp! Records, and the series eventually ran to dozens of volumes, including regional and international sub-series.

Content and influence

Most tracks on Pebbles were originally released as singles in small regional pressings and had never been reissued. The series championed raw, energetic "garage" sounds—as heard in bands such as the Seeds and the Standells alongside far more obscure acts—and helped define the canon of garage rock revivalism. Some early volumes were criticised for bootleg or semi-official status, but the series became a vital document for collectors and musicians in the garage revival and punk scenes.

Legacy

Pebbles spawned countless imitator series (such as Back from the Grave and Garage Punk Unknowns) and kept obscure 1960s music in circulation for new generations.
