The World Fair Trade Organization (WFTO) is a global network of organisations representing the fair trade supply chain, including producer organisations, exporters, importers, wholesalers, retailers and support organisations. It was founded in 1989 as the International Federation of Alternative Trade (IFAT) and adopted its present name in 2009. Its headquarters are in Culemborg, Netherlands.

Guarantee System

The WFTO operates a Guarantee System by which member organisations are assessed against its ten Principles of Fair Trade, covering fair prices, fair trading conditions, capacity building, no child or forced labour, gender equity, safe working conditions and respect for the environment. Verified members may use the WFTO Guaranteed Fair Trade label on their products. Unlike commodity certification systems such as Fairtrade International's, the WFTO system verifies the whole organisation rather than individual product lines.

World Fair Trade Day

The WFTO and its members promote World Fair Trade Day, held annually on the second Saturday of May, with events raising awareness of fair trade among consumers and policymakers.
