Sunshine 60

Sunshine 60 in Ikebukuro, Tokyo

Sunshine 60 (サンシャイン60) is a 60-storey mixed-use skyscraper in Ikebukuro, Toshima, Tokyo, Japan. Completed in 1978 and standing 239.7 metres (786 ft) tall, it was briefly the tallest building in Asia and remained the tallest in Japan until 1985. The tower forms the centrepiece of the Sunshine City complex, a "city within a city" containing an aquarium, planetarium, museum, shopping centre and hotels. Its name refers to its 60 floors above ground.

Construction and features

Sunshine City was built on the site of the former Sugamo Prison, where Japanese war criminals — including wartime prime minister Hideki Tojo — were executed after the Second World War. When it opened, Sunshine 60's elevators were among the fastest in the world, travelling at 600 metres per minute. The building's 60th-floor observation deck, later relaunched as the SKY CIRCUS Sunshine 60 Observatory with virtual-reality attractions, offers panoramic views across Tokyo. The complex receives tens of millions of visitors a year and houses the Sunshine Aquarium, one of Japan's oldest rooftop aquariums.
