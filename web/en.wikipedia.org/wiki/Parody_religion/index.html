Parody religion

A parody religion, or mock religion, is a belief system that challenges the spiritual convictions of others using humor, satire, or burlesque. Often created to achieve a specific purpose related to another religion, a parody religion can be a parody of several religions, sects, gurus, or cults, or even a parody of itself based on the concept of religion.

One approach of parody religions is to persuade followers of other faiths that their beliefs and traditions can be seen in absurd or surreal light, if taken literally. Another approach is to use the legal status of religion to gain rights and privileges usually reserved for genuine religious bodies, as a form of social or legal protest.

Examples

Well-known examples include the Church of the Flying Spaghetti Monster (Pastafarianism), founded in 2005 by Bobby Henderson to protest the teaching of intelligent design in Kansas public schools; the Church of the SubGenius; Discordianism, centered on the goddess Eris; the Invisible Pink Unicorn; and the Church of Euthanasia. Dudeism, inspired by the film The Big Lebowski, occupies a boundary between parody and sincere philosophy.

Legal status

Parody religions have occasionally achieved official recognition: adherents have won the right in some jurisdictions to wear religious headgear, such as colanders, in driver's license photographs, and Pastafarian ministers have officiated weddings in New Zealand. Courts and legislatures continue to debate where the line between sincere belief and satire lies for purposes of religious-freedom law.
