The President of Iran (Persian: رئیس‌جمهور ایران) is the head of government of the Islamic Republic of Iran and the highest-ranking official in the country after the Supreme Leader. The president is elected by universal suffrage for a four-year term and may serve a maximum of two consecutive terms. The office was established after the Iranian Revolution of 1979 and the adoption of the Constitution of the Islamic Republic.

Role within the system

Iran's political system combines republican institutions with clerical oversight. The president is responsible for implementing the constitution in matters not directly assigned to the Supreme Leader, heads the executive branch, appoints ministers (subject to parliamentary approval), signs treaties and legislation, and chairs the Supreme National Security Council. However, the Supreme Leader controls the armed forces, judiciary, and state broadcasting, and appoints the heads of major foundations, making the presidency a powerful but subordinate office.

Elections and eligibility

Candidates must be approved by the Guardian Council, which vets them for loyalty to the Islamic system and religious credentials. Candidates must be Iranian-born citizens of Iranian origin, Muslim, and, in the council's interpretation of the constitution's requirement of rejal (political-religious figures), historically men have dominated approved candidate lists. Elections are typically held every four years; turnout and the range of permitted candidates are closely watched as measures of the system's openness.

History

The first president, Abolhassan Banisadr, was impeached in 1981. His successor, Mohammad-Ali Rajai, was assassinated weeks after taking office. Ali Khamenei served two terms (1981–1989) before becoming Supreme Leader. Subsequent presidents include Akbar Hashemi Rafsanjani (1989–1997), the reformist Mohammad Khatami (1997–2005), Mahmoud Ahmadinejad (2005–2013), Hassan Rouhani (2013–2021), and Ebrahim Raisi (2021–2024), who died in a helicopter crash in May 2024. Masoud Pezeshkian, a reformist-leaning former health minister, won the snap election that followed and took office in July 2024.

Administration

The Presidential Administration is based in the Pasteur neighbourhood of Tehran. The president is assisted by several vice presidents, including the First Vice President, who manages cabinet business and succeeds in the event of the president's death or incapacity pending a new election within fifty days.
