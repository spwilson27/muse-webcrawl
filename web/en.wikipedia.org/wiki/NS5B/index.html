NS5B

NS5B (non-structural protein 5B) is an RNA-dependent RNA polymerase encoded by the hepatitis C virus (HCV). It is essential for replication of the viral RNA genome.

Structure and function

NS5B has the characteristic polymerase "right hand" structure of fingers, palm and thumb domains. Anchored to intracellular membranes by a C-terminal tail, it copies the viral positive-sense RNA genome through a negative-sense intermediate.

Drug target

Because human cells have no equivalent enzyme, NS5B is a prime target for direct-acting antivirals. Nucleoside inhibitors such as sofosbuvir act as chain terminators, while non-nucleoside inhibitors bind allosteric pockets; these drugs helped make chronic hepatitis C curable.
