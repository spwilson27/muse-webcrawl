So You Think You Can Dance

So You Think You Can Dance is an American television reality program and dance competition airing on Fox. In the series, contestants perform dances drawn from a variety of styles — including contemporary, hip-hop, jazz, ballroom and ballet — before a panel of judges, with audience voting determining eliminations until a winner, billed as "America's Favorite Dancer", is crowned.

Format

The show was created by Simon Fuller and Nigel Lythgoe and premiered on 20 July 2005. Open auditions are held in cities across the United States, followed by callbacks in Las Vegas (later seasons used an "Academy" format). Finalists perform solos, duets and group routines, usually in styles assigned by draw and choreographed by guest choreographers such as Mia Michaels, Travis Wall and Sonya Tayeh.

The series was hosted by Lauren Sánchez in its first season and by Cat Deeley from the second season onward, while Lythgoe served as a permanent judge alongside rotating judges including Mary Murphy, who became known for her enthusiastic "hot tamale train" praise.

Reception and franchise

The program has won multiple Emmy Awards, principally for choreography and lighting design. Its success launched an international franchise with versions in dozens of countries, including the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia and India.
