Ard Schenk

Ard Schenk, Dutch speed skating champion of the early 1970s.

Adrianus "Ard" Schenk (born 16 September 1944) is a former Dutch speed skater who is regarded as one of the greatest allround skaters in the sport's history. Between 1970 and 1972 he won three consecutive World Allround Championships and three European Allround Championships, and at the 1972 Winter Olympics in Sapporo he won three gold medals (1500 m, 5000 m and 10,000 m), becoming one of the stars of those Games.

Career

Schenk, a physiotherapist from Anna Paulowna in North Holland, rose to prominence in the late 1960s alongside fellow Dutchman Kees Verkerk. In the 1970–71 and 1971–72 seasons he dominated international allround skating, setting multiple world records, including over 1500 m and 10,000 m. He was awarded the Oscar Mathisen Award (the "Oscar statuette" of speed skating) three times, in 1970, 1971 and 1972.

In 1973 Schenk turned professional, joining a short-lived professional skating circuit, which ended his eligibility for Olympic and ISU competition. After retiring he remained involved in Dutch sport as a coach, commentator and administrator.
