Adam Matthew Vinatieri (born December 28, 1972) is an American former professional football placekicker who played in the National Football League (NFL) for 24 seasons with the New England Patriots and Indianapolis Colts. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest kickers of all time and is the NFL's all-time leading scorer.

[Image: Adam Vinatieri kicking a field goal]

Career

Vinatieri played college football at South Dakota State and began his professional career with the Amsterdam Admirals of NFL Europe before signing with the Patriots in 1996. He became famous for clutch postseason kicks: his game-tying and game-winning field goals in the blizzard of the 2001 AFC Divisional Playoff against Oakland (the "Tuck Rule" game), and his last-second game-winning kicks in Super Bowls XXXVI and XXXVIII for New England. Signing with Indianapolis in 2006, he won a fourth championship in Super Bowl XLI.

Records

Vinatieri surpassed Morten Andersen in 2018 to become the NFL's all-time leading scorer, finishing with 2,673 points. He also holds records for the most field goals made in a career (599) and the most consecutive field goals made (44). He retired after the 2019 season, at age 47 one of the oldest players in NFL history.
