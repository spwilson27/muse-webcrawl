Loi Krathong
Observed: Thailand, Laos
Date: Full moon of 12th lunar month

Loi Krathong is a Thai festival celebrated annually throughout Thailand and nearby regions on the evening of the full moon of the twelfth month in the traditional Thai lunar calendar, usually in November.

Traditions

Participants float a krathong, a small decorated raft traditionally made from banana trunk and leaves with flowers, incense and a candle, on rivers and lakes to honour the water goddess Phra Mae Khongkha and release misfortune.

Related festivals

In northern Thailand it coincides with Yi Peng, famous for thousands of sky lanterns released into the night sky.
