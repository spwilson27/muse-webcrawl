Charlie and the Great Glass Elevator is a children's book by British author Roald Dahl, published in 1972. It is the sequel to Charlie and the Chocolate Factory (1964) and continues the adventures of Charlie Bucket and Willy Wonka.

Plot

The story picks up as Charlie, his parents, his four grandparents and Willy Wonka soar in the Great Glass Elevator, which overshoots into orbit around Earth. They dock with a space hotel, encounter the shape-shifting Vermicious Knids, and later return to the chocolate factory, where a mishap with Wonka-Vite ages the grandparents and a dose of Vita-Wonk makes them vanish into Minusland before being restored.
