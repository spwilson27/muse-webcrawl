The CONCACAF W Gold Cup is an international women's association football competition organized by CONCACAF, the governing body for North America, Central America, and the Caribbean. It is the flagship championship tournament for women's national teams in the region, distinct from the CONCACAF W Championship, which serves as the World Cup qualifying tournament.

The inaugural W Gold Cup was held in the United States in 2024.

Inaugural edition

The tournament was announced as part of CONCACAF's expanded women's competition calendar and was first played from February to March 2024 in the United States, with matches in Carson, San Diego, Houston, and Los Angeles. Twelve teams took part: eight from CONCACAF and four invited guests from South America's CONMEBOL (Brazil, Colombia, Argentina, and Paraguay).

The United States won the inaugural title, defeating Brazil 1–0 in the final at Snapdragon Stadium in San Diego, with Lindsey Horan scoring the only goal. Qualification for CONCACAF teams ran through the 2023 Road to CONCACAF W Gold Cup, a league-style competition that also fed into the region's new women's nations league structure.

Format

The tournament uses a group stage of three groups of four, followed by quarter-finals, semi-finals, and a final. It is planned to be held every two years as part of the region's women's football calendar.
