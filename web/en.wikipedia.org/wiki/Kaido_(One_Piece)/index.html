Kaido (One Piece)

Kaido is a fictional character in the Japanese manga series One Piece, created by Eiichiro Oda. Known as "Kaido of the Beasts" and regarded in-universe as the "Strongest Creature in the World", he is the Governor-General of the Beasts Pirates and one of the Four Emperors who rule over the New World. He serves as the primary antagonist of the Wano Country arc.

Character

Kaido is a towering, muscular man with a wild mane of black hair, a horned head and a long moustache, with a scale tattoo of a dragon on his left arm. Having eaten the Uo Uo no Mi, Model: Seiryu, a Mythical Zoan-type Devil Fruit, he can transform into a gigantic azure eastern dragon. Renowned for his near-indestructibility — legend says he has survived execution and suicide attempts countless times, and that "in a one-on-one fight, always bet on Kaido" — he is a melancholic, bored warrior who dreams of starting the greatest war the world has ever seen.

Story role

Formerly an apprentice aboard the Rocks Pirates alongside Whitebeard and Big Mom, Kaido later seized control of Wano Country in alliance with the shogun Kurozumi Orochi, ruling it tyrannically for over two decades. He is defeated after an extended battle on Onigashima by Monkey D. Luffy, whose awakened Gear 5 form finally prevails.

Reception

Kaido has been a popular antagonist in fan polls, noted for his overwhelming menace and his tragic backstory of disillusionment with the World Government.
