Xi Jinping Thought
Full name: Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era
Introduced: 19th Party Congress, October 2017
Enshrined: CCP constitution (2017), PRC constitution (2018)

Xi Jinping Thought, formally Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, is an ideological doctrine of the Chinese Communist Party (CCP) associated with Xi Jinping, General Secretary of the CCP since 2012. It was incorporated into the constitution of the Chinese Communist Party at the 19th National Congress in October 2017 and into the state constitution of the People's Republic of China in March 2018, making Xi the first leader since Mao Zedong to have his eponymous ideology enshrined in the party constitution while still in office.

Content

The doctrine is commonly summarized in "eight clarifies" and "fourteen upholds" presented in Xi's report to the 19th Congress. Central themes include upholding the absolute leadership of the Party over all work, people-centered development, comprehensively deepening reform and the rule of law, a new development philosophy, national rejuvenation through the "Chinese Dream," building a strong military, and constructing a "community with a shared future for mankind" in foreign policy. Later elaborations include the "two establishes" affirming Xi's core status.

Dissemination

Xi Jinping Thought is studied throughout the party, government, military, and education system, including through the multi-volume Xi Jinping: The Governance of China, study sessions, and the "Xuexi Qiangguo" study app. In 2021 the doctrine's historical status was further elevated in the party's third historical resolution.
