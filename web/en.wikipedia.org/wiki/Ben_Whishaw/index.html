Benjamin John Whishaw (born 14 October 1980) is an English actor. He has received various accolades, including three British Academy Television Awards and a Primetime Emmy Award, and is known for performances on stage and screen ranging from Shakespearean leads to the voice of Paddington Bear.

Early life

Whishaw was born in Clifton, Bedfordshire, the son of Linda, a cosmetics worker, and Jose Whishaw, who worked in information technology. He has a fraternal twin brother, James. He attended Samuel Whitbread Community College and later trained at the Royal Academy of Dramatic Art, graduating in 2003.

Career

Whishaw first attracted wide attention in 2004 playing the title role in Trevor Nunn's production of Hamlet at the Old Vic, a performance praised for its youthful intensity. On screen he played Sebastian Flyte in the 2008 film adaptation of Brideshead Revisited and the poet John Keats in Jane Campion's Bright Star (2009).

He became internationally familiar as Q, the quartermaster of MI6, in the James Bond films Skyfall (2012), Spectre (2015) and No Time to Die (2021). Since 2014 he has voiced Paddington Bear in the films Paddington and Paddington 2.

On television, Whishaw won the BAFTA Award for Best Actor for playing Richard II in The Hollow Crown (2012). He played Norman Scott in the miniseries A Very English Scandal (2018), winning the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Limited Series, and starred as Adam Kay in the medical comedy-drama This Is Going to Hurt (2022).

Personal life

Whishaw entered a civil partnership with Australian composer Mark Bradshaw in 2012. He is known for keeping his private life out of the public eye.
