Stade Toulousain

Stade Toulousain (French pronunciation: [stad tuluzɛ̃]), sometimes referred to as Toulouse, is a French professional rugby union club based in Toulouse in south-western France. Founded in 1907, the club is the most successful in European rugby history, with a record number of French league titles and European Cup victories.

History

The club was formed in 1907 from the merger of two student sports clubs in Toulouse. It won its first French championship in 1912, beginning a long tradition of success. The club's red and black colours are traditionally said to derive from the ceremonial dress of the capitouls, the historic municipal magistrates of Toulouse.

Under coach Guy Novès, who led the team from 1993 to 2015, Toulouse became the dominant force in the new professional era, winning the inaugural European Cup (Heineken Cup) in 1996 with a victory over Cardiff in extra time. Further European titles followed in 2003, 2005 and 2010, and the club won a record fifth European crown — the first team to reach that mark — in 2021, adding a sixth in 2024 by defeating Leinster in the final.

Domestically, Stade Toulousain has won the Bouclier de Brennus, the trophy of the French championship, more times than any other club, passing twenty titles in the early 2020s. The club is renowned for its fluent, attacking style of play and for its academy, which has produced generations of French internationals, including Fabien Pelous, the club's most-capped player, and Antoine Dupont, widely regarded as one of the finest scrum-halves of the professional era.

Stadium

The club plays its home matches at the Stade Ernest-Wallon, which holds about 19,500 spectators. For especially large fixtures, including European knockout matches and local derbies, the club sometimes moves to the larger Stadium de Toulouse.

Honours

  • French champions (Top 14): numerous titles, a competition record, beginning in 1912
  • European Rugby Champions Cup: winners 1996, 2003, 2005, 2010, 2021, 2024
  • Challenge Yves du Manoir: several titles
