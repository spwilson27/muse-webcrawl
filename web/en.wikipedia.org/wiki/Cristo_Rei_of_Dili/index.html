Cristo Rei of Dili

Location: Fatucama, east of Dili, East Timor
Height: 27 m (89 ft) statue and globe
Completed: 1996

Cristo Rei of Dili (Christ the King of Dili) is a 27-metre-high statue of Jesus Christ standing on a globe at Fatucama, on a headland east of Dili, the capital of East Timor. It is one of the country's principal landmarks.

History

The statue was a gift from the Indonesian government of President Suharto and was presented in 1996 as a monument to mark the twentieth anniversary of East Timor's integration into Indonesia, the statue's height of 27 metres corresponding to East Timor's status as Indonesia's 27th province. It was inaugurated by Suharto in October 1996. East Timorese attitudes toward the monument were mixed during the occupation, but after independence in 2002 the statue was retained and became a popular destination for residents and visitors.

The statue faces west toward the sea with arms outstretched. It is reached by a stairway of about 500 steps lined with representations of the Stations of the Cross, climbing the headland above Cristo Rei beach and Backside Beach.
