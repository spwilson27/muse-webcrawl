Nouvelle-Aquitaine

Nouvelle-Aquitaine is the largest administrative region in France, created in 2016 from the former regions of Aquitaine, Limousin and Poitou-Charentes. Its capital is Bordeaux.

Geography

Spanning 84,036 km² in southwestern France, it has an Atlantic coastline including the Côte d'Argent, the Pyrenees in the south, and renowned wine regions around Bordeaux, Cognac and Bergerac. Population is about six million.

Economy

Agriculture, wine, aerospace, tourism and the ports of Bordeaux and La Rochelle are major sectors.
