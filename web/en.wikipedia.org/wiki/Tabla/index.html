Tabla

The tabla is a pair of hand drums used in Hindustani classical music and in popular and devotional music of the Indian subcontinent. It consists of a smaller drum, the dayan, played with the dominant hand, and a larger metal or clay drum, the bayan.

Construction and technique

Each drum has a head of goat skin with a central black spot of iron filings and starch called the syahi, which gives the tabla its distinctive bell-like tones. Players produce a large vocabulary of strokes, named by syllables such as na, tin, ge, and ke, that are organized into rhythmic cycles called tala.
