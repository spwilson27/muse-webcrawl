Republic of the Rif

The Republic of the Rif (Arabic: جمهورية الريف) was a confederate republic in the Rif region of northern Morocco that existed from 1921 to 1926. It was founded by Abd el-Krim, leader of the Berber tribes of the Rif, after a crushing victory over the Spanish army at the Battle of Annual in July 1921, during the Rif War.

History

The republic was proclaimed on 18 September 1921, with Abd el-Krim as its president and Ajdir as its capital. It established a government with ministries, a national army and its own currency project, and sought international recognition, which it never obtained. Spain, having lost much of its Moroccan protectorate, returned with French assistance; a joint Franco-Spanish offensive from 1925, including the Alhucemas landing, defeated the Rifian forces. Abd el-Krim surrendered in May 1926 and was exiled to Réunion; the republic was dissolved and its territory reincorporated into Spanish Morocco.

Legacy

The Republic of the Rif is remembered as an early anti-colonial state and Abd el-Krim's guerrilla methods influenced later independence movements and theorists of guerrilla warfare.
