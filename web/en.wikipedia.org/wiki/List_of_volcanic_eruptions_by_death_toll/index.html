List of volcanic eruptions by death toll

Volcanic eruptions rank among the deadliest natural disasters. The largest death tolls usually result not from lava but from pyroclastic flows, tsunamis generated by flank collapse or caldera formation, lahars (volcanic mudflows), and famine and disease caused by ash fall and climate effects. The deadliest eruption in recorded history was Mount Tambora (Indonesia) in 1815, which killed at least 71,000 people, many through the subsequent "Year Without a Summer" famine.
