Creative Technology Ltd. is a Singaporean multinational technology company known for consumer electronics, especially PC audio. Its Sound Blaster sound cards became the de facto standard for PC audio in the 1990s.

History

Founded in 1981 by Sim Wong Hoo, the company grew from a computer repair shop into a global brand. The original Sound Blaster (1989) brought affordable digital audio and speech to IBM-compatible PCs. Creative later launched the Zen and Nomad MP3 players and the ZiiSound and Sound BlasterX lines.

Products

Creative's product range includes sound cards, gaming headsets, portable speakers, webcams and the Super X-Fi headphone holography technology, which won awards at CES 2019.
