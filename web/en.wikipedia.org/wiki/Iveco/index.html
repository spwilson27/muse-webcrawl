Iveco

Iveco (Industrial Vehicles Corporation) is an Italian multinational manufacturer of commercial vehicles headquartered in Turin. It produces trucks, buses, and vans under brands including Iveco, Iveco Bus, Heuliez Bus, and Astra, as well as defence vehicles and powertrains through FPT Industrial.

History

Iveco was formed in 1975 by the merger of the truck divisions of Fiat, OM, and Lancia Veicoli Speciali in Italy, Unic in France, and Magirus-Deutz in Germany. It later acquired Ford's UK truck operations, Spain's Pegaso, and Australia's International Trucks business.

Recent developments

After decades within Fiat and later CNH Industrial, Iveco Group was spun off and listed separately in 2022. The company has invested in electric and hydrogen trucks, including a joint venture with Nikola, and in 2025 Tata Motors agreed to acquire Iveco's commercial vehicle business.
