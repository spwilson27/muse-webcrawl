BMW (German: Bayerische Motoren Werke AG, lit. "Bavarian Motor Works") is a German multinational manufacturer of luxury vehicles and motorcycles headquartered in Munich, Bavaria. The company was founded in 1916 as an aircraft engine manufacturer, producing engines during World War I and World War II. BMW is known for its performance-oriented automobiles, marketed under the slogan "The Ultimate Driving Machine" and the German Freude am Fahren ("sheer driving pleasure").

Automobiles are marketed under the brands BMW, Mini and Rolls-Royce, and motorcycles are marketed under the brand BMW Motorrad. BMW is one of the best-selling luxury automakers in the world and owns and produces the Mini marque and is the parent company of Rolls-Royce Motor Cars.

History

BMW originated in 1916 from the renaming of Rapp Motorenwerke, an aircraft engine manufacturer. After the Treaty of Versailles prohibited German aircraft production, BMW shifted to motorcycle production (from 1923) and automobiles (from 1928–29, beginning with licensed production of the Austin Seven as the BMW Dixi). During the Second World War, BMW produced aircraft engines such as the BMW 801 radial engine, using forced labour — a period the company has since publicly acknowledged and investigated.

After the war, BMW came close to being taken over by Daimler-Benz in 1959, but investment from the Quandt family secured its independence. The "Neue Klasse" sedans of the 1960s established BMW's sporting image, followed by the numbered model ranges — the 3 Series, 5 Series and 7 Series — that still define its lineup. BMW acquired the Rover Group in 1994 (selling most of it in 2000 while retaining Mini) and the rights to the Rolls-Royce marque in 1998.

Products

BMW's core model range consists of the numbered passenger-car series (1 through 8 Series), the X range of SUVs, the Z roadsters, the high-performance M models, and the BMW i sub-brand for electric and electrified vehicles, which began with the i3 and i8. BMW Motorrad produces motorcycles across sport, touring, adventure and urban segments. BMW has a long motorsport heritage, including Formula One (as an engine supplier and, from 2006 to 2009, with its own team), touring car racing and the Isle of Man TT.

Logo

The circular BMW emblem, often interpreted as a stylised aircraft propeller against the sky, derives from the blue and white colours of the Bavarian flag arranged in alternating quadrants; the propeller interpretation was promoted by later advertising. The company's slogan in Germany is Freude am Fahren, rendered in English markets as "Sheer Driving Pleasure" or "The Ultimate Driving Machine".
