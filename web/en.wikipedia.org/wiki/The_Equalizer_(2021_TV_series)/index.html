The Equalizer (2021 TV series)

The Equalizer is an American crime drama television series that premiered on CBS on February 7, 2021. It is a reboot of the 1980s series of the same name and stars Queen Latifah as Robyn McCall, a former CIA operative who uses her skills to help people with nowhere else to turn.

Premise

Robyn McCall, an enigmatic single mother in New York City with a mysterious background as a former CIA operative, works as a vigilante for justice under the name "The Equalizer", aiding ordinary people who have been failed by the system. She is assisted by ex-sniper turned bar owner Melody "Mel" Bayani, hacker Harry Keshegian, and her Aunt Vi, while NYPD detective Marcus Dante alternates between pursuing her and reluctantly working with her.

Production

The series premiered immediately following Super Bowl LV, drawing a large audience, and became a reliable ratings performer for CBS in the Sunday evening slot. Chris Noth departed the series in early 2022 following allegations of sexual assault; his character was written out. CBS cancelled the series in May 2025 after five seasons, with the fifth-season finale serving as the series finale.
