List of dams and reservoirs in South Africa

The following is a list of dams and reservoirs in South Africa. South Africa is a semi-arid country, and its network of more than 500 registered large dams is essential for water supply, irrigation, hydroelectric power, and flood control. Many of the largest dams are on the Orange River system.

Management

Most large dams are owned by the Department of Water and Sanitation and operated in conjunction with water boards and irrigation schemes. The Lesotho Highlands Water Project, a joint scheme with Lesotho, transfers water from the Orange River headwaters to the Vaal system supplying Gauteng.
