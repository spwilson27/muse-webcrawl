Novello Theatre

The Novello Theatre is a West End theatre on Aldwych, in the City of Westminster, London.

History

Designed by W. G. R. Sprague, it opened on 22 May 1905 as the Waldorf Theatre, twin to the Aldwych Theatre. It was renamed the Strand Theatre, then the Whitney, and in 2005 was extensively refurbished and renamed in honour of composer Ivor Novello, who had lived in a flat above it. The theatre seats about 1,105 and is owned by Delfont Mackintosh Theatres.

Productions

Long runs have included Mamma Mia!, which transferred here in 2012.
