Dr. Moira MacTaggert is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, most closely associated with the X-Men. Created by writer Chris Claremont and artist Dave Cockrum, she first appeared in The Uncanny X-Men #96 (1975). A Scottish geneticist and one of the world's leading experts on mutants, she is a longtime ally, former fiancée and intellectual equal of Professor Charles Xavier, founder of the Mutant Research Centre on Muir Island, Scotland.

Publication history and character

Introduced as Xavier's housekeeper at his Westchester school, Moira was soon revealed as a Nobel Prize-winning geneticist who had loved Xavier in their youth at Oxford before marrying the abusive politician Joseph MacTaggert — the father of her son Kevin, the reality-warping mutant Proteus. Across the Claremont era she was the X-Men's physician, scientist and moral confidante; her cloning research restored Xavier's ability to walk after an accident, and she developed a treatment for the mutant Legacy Virus that had killed her friend Illyana Rasputin. The strain of the virus she isolated infected her — the first prominent human case — and she died in an attack on Muir Island by the shapeshifter Mystique in 2001, her final data helping find the cure.

The 2019 retcon: Moira X

In Jonathan Hickman's House of X and Powers of X (2019), Moira was retconned as a mutant herself: her power is reincarnation — on death she restarts her own life in the womb with all memories intact, a power that resets the timeline. She had lived ten lives, variously allying with Xavier, Magneto and Apocalypse, and her accumulated knowledge underpinned the founding of the mutant nation of Krakoa. Intended as Krakoa's hidden third founder, she was later revealed as embittered: depowered and expelled after attempting to "cure" mutation, she became a machine-allied enemy of mutantkind, uploading her mind into robotic form and fighting the Krakoan nation during the Fall of X storyline, before dying (in robot form) in the era's conclusion.

In other media

Moira MacTaggert appears in the X-Men film series: played briefly by Olivia Williams in X-Men: The Last Stand (2006) and, as a CIA agent rather than a scientist, by Rose Byrne in X-Men: First Class (2011) and X-Men: Apocalypse (2016). She also appeared in X-Men: The Animated Series.
