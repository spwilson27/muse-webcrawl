2020 Republican Party presidential primaries

2020 Republican primaries
Contests held: 3 February – 11 August 2020
Nominee: Donald Trump
Running mate: Mike Pence
Delegates won: 2,443 of 2,550
Challengers: Bill Weld, Joe Walsh, Mark Sanford (all withdrew)

The 2020 Republican Party presidential primaries and caucuses were a series of electoral contests to select delegates to the 2020 Republican National Convention, which nominated the party's candidate for the 2020 United States presidential election. Incumbent President Donald Trump was renominated with minimal opposition.

Background

Trump filed for re-election on the day of his inauguration in 2017 and enjoyed overwhelming support within the party. Several state Republican parties cancelled their primaries or caucuses, citing Trump's incumbency. Challenges were mounted by former Massachusetts governor Bill Weld, former congressman Joe Walsh, and former South Carolina governor Mark Sanford, none of whom gained significant traction; Walsh and Sanford withdrew in late 2019/early 2020 and Weld ended his campaign in March 2020.

Results

Trump won every contest, securing the delegates needed for the nomination by 17 March 2020. He and Vice President Mike Pence were formally renominated at the Republican National Convention in August 2020. In the general election they were defeated by Joe Biden and Kamala Harris.
