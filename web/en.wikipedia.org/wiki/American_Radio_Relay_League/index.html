American Radio Relay League

The American Radio Relay League (ARRL) is the national association for amateur radio in the United States. Founded in 1914 by Hiram Percy Maxim and Clarence D. Tuska, it represents licensed radio amateurs before regulators, promotes technical experimentation and supports emergency communications.

Activities

ARRL publishes the monthly magazine QST, handbooks and licensing study materials that have introduced generations of operators to radio. It sponsors contests, awards such as DX Century Club and Worked All States, and events including Field Day, in which clubs operate portable stations and practise emergency readiness. Local affiliated clubs and volunteer examiners form much of its grassroots structure.

Advocacy and service

The league advocates for amateur spectrum allocations at the Federal Communications Commission and international conferences. Through the Amateur Radio Emergency Service and partnerships with served agencies, members provide backup communications during disasters. ARRL headquarters is in Newington, Connecticut, where the historic W1AW station transmits bulletins and code practice.
