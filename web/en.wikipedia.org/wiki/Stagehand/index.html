Stagehand

A stagehand is a person who works backstage or behind the scenes in theatres, film, television and concerts, setting up and moving scenery, lighting, sound equipment and props before, during and between performances.

Roles

Stagehands may specialise as grips (scenery and set construction), flymen (operating the fly system of ropes and counterweights), props assistants, or followspot operators. In larger productions a stage manager and deck chief coordinate the crew during performances.

Unions

In the United States, many professional stagehands are represented by the International Alliance of Theatrical Stage Employees (IATSE). Training combines apprenticeships with certifications in rigging and workplace safety.
