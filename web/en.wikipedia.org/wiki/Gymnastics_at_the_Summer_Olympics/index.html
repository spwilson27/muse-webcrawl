Gymnastics at the Summer Olympics
Disciplines: Artistic, Rhythmic, Trampoline
Artistic since: 1896 (men), 1928 (women)
Rhythmic since: 1984
Trampoline since: 2000

Gymnastics has been contested at the Summer Olympic Games since the first modern Games in Athens in 1896. The programme now comprises three disciplines: artistic gymnastics, rhythmic gymnastics, and trampoline gymnastics.

Artistic gymnastics

Men have competed since 1896 and women since 1928. Events include team and individual all-around competitions and apparatus finals: floor, pommel horse, rings, vault, parallel bars and horizontal bar for men; vault, uneven bars, balance beam and floor for women. Legendary champions include Larisa Latynina, Simone Biles, and Kōhei Uchimura.

Rhythmic and trampoline

Rhythmic gymnastics, for women, joined in 1984 with all-around and group events using rope, hoop, ball, clubs, and ribbon. Trampoline events for men and women were added in 2000.

Scoring

Since 2006, artistic gymnastics uses an open-ended Code of Points combining a difficulty (D) score with an execution (E) score, replacing the famous "perfect 10" system under which Nadia Comăneci scored the first perfect 10 in 1976.
