Dora Milaje

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Dora Milaje are a fictional organisation appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. They are the all-female special forces and royal guard of Wakanda, the bodyguards of the Black Panther, recruited from Wakanda's tribes and regarded as among the finest fighters in the Marvel Universe. Their name is commonly translated as "the adored ones".

Publication history

The Dora Milaje were introduced by writer Christopher Priest and artist Mark Texeira in Black Panther vol. 3 #1 (1998). Priest drew in part on the historical women warriors associated with the Fon kingdom of Dahomey. Prominent members include Okoye, who serves as their general, Aneka, Ayo, and Nakia. In the comics they wear shaven heads and distinctive red uniforms, and traditionally served ceremonially as wives-in-training to the king — a role later writers treated as an archaic custom the Panther abolished or ignored.

In other media

The Dora Milaje reached a wide audience through the Marvel Cinematic Universe, beginning with Black Panther (2018), where Danai Gurira plays their leader Okoye and Florence Kasumba plays Ayo. Their precision spear choreography and the Okoye character became standout elements of the films, and the group has appeared in subsequent MCU productions including Avengers: Infinity War, Black Panther: Wakanda Forever, and the Falcon and the Winter Soldier series.
