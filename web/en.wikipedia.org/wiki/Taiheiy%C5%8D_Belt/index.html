Taiheiyō Belt

The Taiheiyō Belt (Japanese: 太平洋ベルト, "Pacific Belt") is a megalopolis in Japan extending about 1,200 km along the Pacific coast of Honshu from the Kantō region through Nagoya and Kansai to northern Kyushu.

Overview

Formed during Japan's postwar industrialization, the belt contains the country's largest metropolitan areas and much of its manufacturing industry and population. The earlier concept of the Tōkaidō corridor forms its core.
