Michael Otsuka (born 1964) is a philosopher specializing in political philosophy and normative ethics. He is known for his work on left-libertarianism, expounded in his book Libertarianism without Inequality (2003), which defends a robust right of self-ownership combined with an egalitarian distribution of worldly resources.

Career

Otsuka studied at Yale and Oxford, taught at UCLA and University College London, and was Professor of Philosophy at the London School of Economics before moving to Rutgers University. His research also addresses the ethics of risk and harm, the permissibility of killing, intergenerational justice, and pension policy, on which he has advised public bodies in the United Kingdom.
