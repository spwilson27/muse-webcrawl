Neymar

Neymar da Silva Santos Júnior
Born: 5 February 1992, Mogi das Cruzes, Brazil
Position: Forward / attacking midfielder
Clubs: Santos, Barcelona, Paris Saint-Germain, Al-Hilal, Santos
Brazil caps: 100+, goals: record scorer for Brazil

Neymar da Silva Santos Júnior (born 5 February 1992), known as Neymar, is a Brazilian professional footballer who plays as a forward. Widely regarded as one of the most skilful players of his generation, he is Brazil's all-time top goalscorer.

Club career

Neymar rose at Santos, winning the Copa Libertadores in 2011. He joined Barcelona in 2013, forming the "MSN" attack with Lionel Messi and Luis Suárez, winning La Liga twice and the UEFA Champions League in 2015 as part of a treble. In 2017 he moved to Paris Saint-Germain for €222 million, still the world record transfer fee, winning multiple Ligue 1 titles. Injuries limited his later PSG years. He joined Saudi club Al-Hilal in 2023, suffered a serious knee injury, and returned to Santos in 2025.

International career

Neymar won Olympic silver in 2012 and gold at Rio 2016, and the 2013 Confederations Cup. He starred at the 2014 World Cup before injury in the quarter-final, and played in 2018 and 2022. In 2023 he surpassed Pelé as Brazil's official top scorer.

Style

Known for dribbling, flair, tricks, playmaking and finishing, Neymar is also a prominent — and sometimes controversial — commercial and media figure.
