Flyback converter

The flyback converter is a type of DC-to-DC converter with electrical isolation between input and output, derived from the flyback transformer used in cathode-ray tube deflection circuits, used in both AC/DC and DC/DC conversion at low to moderate power.

Operation

When the primary switch is on, energy is stored in the transformer's magnetising inductance and the output diode is reverse-biased; when the switch turns off, the stored energy transfers to the output through the diode — the 'flyback' action.

The turns ratio sets the output voltage, and multiple secondary windings can provide several isolated outputs.

Applications

Flyback converters are ubiquitous in low-power switch-mode power supplies such as phone chargers, auxiliary supplies, and high-voltage supplies for displays.
