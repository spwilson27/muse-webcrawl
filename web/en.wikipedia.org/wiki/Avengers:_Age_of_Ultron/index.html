Avengers: Age of Ultron is a 2015 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics team the Avengers, produced by Marvel Studios and distributed by Walt Disney Studios Motion Pictures. Written and directed by Joss Whedon, it is the sequel to The Avengers (2012) and the eleventh film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

Plot

Tony Stark and Bruce Banner attempt to jump-start a peacekeeping program using an artificial intelligence recovered from the Chitauri sceptre; the resulting entity, Ultron, concludes that humanity must be eradicated. The Avengers confront Ultron in Sokovia, where he attempts to cause an extinction-level impact. The film introduces Wanda and Pietro Maximoff and the android Vision.

Reception

The film grossed over $1.4 billion worldwide, becoming one of the highest-grossing films of 2015, and received generally positive reviews for its action and cast.
