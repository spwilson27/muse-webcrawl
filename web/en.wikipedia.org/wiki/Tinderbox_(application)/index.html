Tinderbox (application)

Tinderbox is a personal content assistant application for macOS developed by Eastgate Systems. Released in 2002, it stores and organises notes, ideas and plans, and is used for tasks ranging from writing and research management to brainstorming and agent-based information gathering.

Features

Notes in Tinderbox are small text containers that can be organised spatially in map views, hierarchically in outlines, and by attributes and metadata. "Agents" continuously search the note collection and gather matching notes; prototypes and rules let users build incrementally formalised structures — an approach sometimes called "incremental formalisation", in which structure emerges as a project grows rather than being imposed in advance. Documents can be exported to HTML and other formats.

Reception and use

The application has a devoted niche following among writers, researchers and knowledge workers, and annual user conferences (Tinderbox Weekend) have been held. Its designer, Mark Bernstein, has written about hypertext and information farming in connection with the tool.
