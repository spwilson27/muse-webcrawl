Lady Charlotte Guest

Lady Charlotte Guest
Born: 19 May 1812, Uffington, Lincolnshire
Died: 15 January 1895
Known for: First English translation of the Mabinogion

Lady Charlotte Guest (1812–1895), née Bertie, was an English aristocrat, businesswoman and translator best known for producing the first English translation of the Mabinogion, the medieval Welsh prose tales.

Life

In 1833 she married Josiah John Guest, owner of the Dowlais Ironworks in Merthyr Tydfil, the largest ironworks in the world. She learned Welsh, promoted Welsh culture and education, and after her husband's death in 1852 ran the ironworks herself — a remarkable role for a woman of her era — handling labour disputes and modernising the business.

The Mabinogion

Her translation, published in parts from 1838 to 1849 with scholarly notes, introduced the Four Branches of the Mabinogi and the Arthurian Welsh tales to a wide audience and remains influential. She later married Charles Schreiber and collected ceramics and fans, collections now in the British Museum and V&A. See Mabinogion.
