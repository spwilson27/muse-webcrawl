List of highest-grossing animated films

This is a list of the highest-grossing animated films of all time by worldwide box-office gross, unadjusted for inflation. In 2025 the Chinese film Ne Zha 2 became the highest-grossing animated film in history, the first non-Hollywood production to pass $2 billion. Most of the other top titles are from Walt Disney Animation Studios, Pixar, Illumination and DreamWorks Animation.
