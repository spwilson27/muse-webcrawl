The Encyclopedia of Korean Culture (Korean: 한국민족문화대백과사전) is a major Korean-language encyclopedia covering the history, culture, and society of Korea. It is produced by the Academy of Korean Studies and is freely available online.

History

The project was conceived as a comprehensive national encyclopedia documenting Korean heritage. The print edition was published in the 1990s in dozens of volumes, and the work was subsequently digitised and expanded as an online resource, becoming one of the most widely used reference works for Korean studies.

Content

Entries cover Korean history from prehistory to the present, traditional customs, folklore, religion, literature, arts, geography, and biographies of notable figures. The online edition includes photographs, illustrations, and multimedia supplements, and is frequently updated.
