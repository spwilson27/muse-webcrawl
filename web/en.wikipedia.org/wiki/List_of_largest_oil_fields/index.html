List of largest oil fields

The world's largest oil fields are concentrated in the Middle East, which holds several "supergiant" fields — those with more than 5 billion barrels of ultimately recoverable oil. The largest of all is the Ghawar field in Saudi Arabia, by far the biggest conventional oil field ever discovered; it has produced more oil than any other field and historically supplied a significant share of Saudi production. Reserves figures are estimates and are often politically sensitive, so values given by different sources vary.

Unconventional resources

If unconventional deposits are counted, the largest accumulations are far bigger still: Canada's Athabasca oil sands contain on the order of 165 billion barrels of proven reserves (from ~1.7 trillion barrels of bitumen in place), and Venezuela's Orinoco Belt extra-heavy oil is credited with roughly 235–300 billion barrels of technically recoverable reserves, which underpinned Venezuela's claim to the world's largest proved reserves. The Green River Formation oil shale in the United States contains an enormous resource in place, though it has never been commercially produced at scale.
