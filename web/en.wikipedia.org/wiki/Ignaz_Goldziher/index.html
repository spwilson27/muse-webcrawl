Ignaz Goldziher

Ignaz Goldziher (22 June 1850 – 13 November 1921), often credited as Ignác (Yitzhaq Yehuda) Goldziher, was a Hungarian scholar of Islam. He is widely regarded as one of the founders of the modern academic study of Islam in Europe.

Life and career

Born in Székesfehérvár, Goldziher studied at Budapest, Berlin, Leipzig and Leiden, and travelled in Syria, Palestine and Egypt. Despite his eminence, academic appointments were long delayed by the antisemitism of the era; he became a full professor at the University of Budapest only in 1905.

Work

His Muhammedanische Studien (1889–1890) revolutionised the study of hadith, arguing that many traditions attributed to Muhammad reflect the debates of later generations. His Vorlesungen über den Islam (1910) remains a landmark survey of Islamic doctrine and law.
