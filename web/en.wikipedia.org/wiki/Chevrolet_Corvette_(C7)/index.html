Chevrolet Corvette (C7)

The Chevrolet Corvette (C7) is the seventh generation of the Corvette sports car manufactured by American automobile manufacturer Chevrolet. It was introduced for the 2014 model year as the first to bear the Stingray name since the 1976 third-generation model. Production ended with the 2019 model year, after which the C7 was succeeded by the mid-engine C8 Corvette.

Overview

The C7 was unveiled at the North American International Auto Show in Detroit on 13 January 2013. It retained the front-engine, rear-wheel-drive layout of all previous Corvettes and featured an all-aluminium frame, extensive use of carbon fibre (including the bonnet and removable roof panel), and a new 6.2-litre LT1 V8 engine with direct injection, variable valve timing and cylinder deactivation, producing 455 horsepower (339 kW) in standard form.

Transmission choices were a seven-speed manual — the first offered in its class — or a six-speed automatic, replaced for 2015 by an eight-speed automatic. The interior was extensively redesigned with higher-quality materials after criticism of earlier generations.

Models

  • Stingray — the base model, powered by the 455–460 hp LT1 V8. The Z51 performance package added dry-sump lubrication, larger wheels, an electronic limited-slip differential and aerodynamic upgrades.
  • Z06 (2015–2019) — powered by the supercharged 6.2-litre LT4 V8 producing 650 hp (485 kW) and 650 lb⋅ft (881 N⋅m) of torque, making it the most powerful Corvette built to that time.
  • Grand Sport (2017–2019) — combined the Stingray's naturally aspirated LT1 engine with the Z06's wide body, chassis and aerodynamic components.
  • ZR1 (2019) — the flagship, powered by the supercharged LT5 V8 producing 755 hp (563 kW), with a top speed of 212 mph (341 km/h). It was the most powerful and fastest production Corvette ever built at the time.

Production

All C7 Corvettes were built at GM's Bowling Green Assembly Plant in Kentucky. The final C7 — a black Z06 coupé — was auctioned for charity in June 2019, selling for $2.7 million, and the last car was produced in November 2019.
