Chahamanas of Shakambhari

The Chahamanas of Shakambhari (IAST: Cāhamāna), also known as the Chauhans of Sambhar or Chauhans of Ajmer, were a Rajput dynasty that ruled parts of present-day Rajasthan and neighbouring areas in India between the 6th and 12th centuries CE. Their capital was Shakambhari (present-day Sambhar).

History

The dynasty was founded by Simharaja in the 10th century. Notable rulers include Arnoraja, Vigraharaja IV, who expanded the kingdom and was a patron of literature, and Prithviraja III (Prithviraj Chauhan), famous for his conflicts with Muhammad of Ghor. Prithviraja was defeated at the Second Battle of Tarain in 1192, marking a turning point in North Indian history.

The Chahamanas built temples, forts such as Ajmer's Taragarh, and the Anasagar Lake.
