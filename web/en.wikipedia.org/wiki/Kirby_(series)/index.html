Kirby is a Japanese video game series developed by HAL Laboratory and published by Nintendo. The series centres on Kirby, a small pink spherical creature who inhabits the planet Pop Star and defends it from threats such as King Dedede and Meta Knight. Kirby's signature ability is to inhale enemies and objects, either spitting them out as projectiles or, in most games from Kirby's Adventure (1993) onward, copying their powers.

Gameplay

Most Kirby games are side-scrolling platformers designed to be approachable for beginners — Kirby can fly by inflating himself — while hiding considerable depth for experienced players. Alongside the main platformers, the series includes spin-offs such as the racing game Kirby Air Ride, the pinball game Kirby's Pinball Land and the puzzle game Kirby's Star Stacker. Kirby and the Forgotten Land (2022) moved the main series into full 3D.

Development and reception

Kirby was created by Masahiro Sakurai, then a young designer at HAL, as a character simple enough for anyone to draw. The character's placeholder circle design became final; his pink colour was chosen by Sakurai, though Shigeru Miyamoto had imagined him yellow, and his colour in the first game's Western box art appeared white. The series has sold more than 50 million copies worldwide, and Kirby has appeared as a fighter in every Super Smash Bros. game.
