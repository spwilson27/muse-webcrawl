Raymond "Ray" Palmer is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Gardner Fox and artist Gil Kane, he first appeared in Showcase #34 (October 1961) as the Silver Age version of the Atom, a hero able to shrink to subatomic sizes.

Fictional biography

Palmer is a physics professor at Ivy University who discovers how to use matter from a white dwarf star, compressed into a lens, to shrink objects. After an accident leaves him able to control his size and weight naturally, he becomes the Atom, a long-standing member of the Justice League of America. His marriage to Jean Loring and her later breakdown became central to the storyline of Identity Crisis (2004).

Powers and abilities

Palmer can reduce his size down to subatomic levels while retaining his full mass and strength, ride air currents and telephone signals, and enter miniature worlds. He is also a brilliant physicist.

In other media

The character was portrayed by Brandon Routh in the Arrowverse television franchise, including Legends of Tomorrow.
