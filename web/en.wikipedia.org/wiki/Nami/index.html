Nami is a fictional character in the Japanese manga series One Piece and its franchise, created by Eiichiro Oda. She is the navigator of the Straw Hat Pirates and one of the first crew members to join protagonist Monkey D. Luffy. Nicknamed "Cat Burglar" Nami, she is an orphan raised in Cocoyasi Village in the East Blue, and dreams of drawing a complete map of the world.

Character

Nami is introduced as a thief who steals only from pirates, having been forced into service of the fish-man pirate Arlong, who occupied her village and murdered her adoptive mother, the Marine Bellemere. Working to buy back her village's freedom for 100 million berries, she joins Luffy's crew while secretly still bound to Arlong; after Arlong betrays their deal, Luffy defeats him and Nami joins the Straw Hats in earnest — the emotional climax of the Arlong Park arc.

Abilities

Nami has an innate talent for navigation and meteorology, sensing changes in weather with her body. In combat she wields the Clima-Tact, a staff engineered by Usopp (later upgraded with Weatheria technology and the thundercloud homie Zeus) that manipulates weather phenomena such as lightning, mirages, and gusts. She also manages the crew's finances and is notorious for her love of money and tangerines, the latter a memento of Bellemere's grove; she bears a tattoo on her left shoulder derived from her sister Nojiko's and her earlier Arlong Pirates mark.

Reception

Nami consistently ranks among the most popular characters in the series' fan polls and is portrayed by Emily Rudd in Netflix's live-action adaptation of One Piece.
