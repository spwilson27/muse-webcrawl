Vince McMahon

Vincent Kennedy McMahon (born August 24, 1945) is an American businessman and former professional wrestling promoter. He is best known as the co-founder of the modern WWE, the world's largest professional wrestling promotion, which he transformed from a regional company into a global entertainment enterprise.

Career

McMahon was born in Pinehurst, North Carolina, and met his father, promoter Vincent J. McMahon, at the age of 12. He began working for his father's Capitol Wrestling Corporation (later the World Wide Wrestling Federation) as a ring announcer and commentator. In 1982 he purchased the company and, as chairman of the renamed World Wrestling Federation (WWF, later WWE), broke with the industry's territorial system, signing stars such as Hulk Hogan and promoting nationally televised spectacles including the first WrestleMania in 1985, which launched the "Rock 'n' Wrestling" boom.

During the 1990s "Monday Night War" with rival WCW, McMahon adopted the on-screen persona of "Mr. McMahon", a tyrannical boss who feuded with star Steve Austin, a centrepiece of the company's successful Attitude Era. In 2001 WWE bought WCW, ending the war. McMahon later merged WWE with the UFC's parent company to form TKO Group Holdings in 2023, serving as executive chairman; he resigned from TKO's board in January 2024 amid litigation, ending his decades of control of the company.

Other ventures

Beyond wrestling, McMahon launched the World Bodybuilding Federation and the XFL American football league (2001, and again in 2020). He is a member of the WWE Hall of Fame (as part of the McMahon family business legacy) and has a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame.
