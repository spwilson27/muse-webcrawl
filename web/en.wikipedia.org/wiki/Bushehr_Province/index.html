Bushehr Province

Placeholder image: the Bushehr coastline on the Persian Gulf.

Bushehr Province (Persian: استان بوشهر) is one of the 31 provinces of Iran. Its capital is the city of Bushehr. The province lies in the south of the country along the coast of the Persian Gulf, bordering Khuzestan Province to the northwest, Kohgiluyeh and Boyer-Ahmad and Fars provinces to the north and northeast, and Hormozgan Province to the east.

Geography and climate

The province consists of a narrow coastal plain and the foothills of the Zagros Mountains inland. The climate is hot and humid along the coast, with long summers; the maritime influence of the Persian Gulf shapes local fishing and trade. Major ports include Bushehr itself and the energy hub of Asaluyeh near the South Pars gas field.

Economy

Bushehr is best known internationally for the Bushehr Nuclear Power Plant, Iran's first commercial nuclear reactor, built with Russian assistance near the capital. The province is also a centre of date cultivation, fishing and shipbuilding, and hosts major petrochemical facilities linked to the South Pars/North Dome gas condensate field, one of the largest natural gas fields in the world.

History

The region's settlements date to the Elamite and Achaemenid eras. Bushehr grew into an important trading port in the 18th and 19th centuries, at times serving as the principal port of Persia and hosting a British residency during the colonial rivalry over the Gulf.
