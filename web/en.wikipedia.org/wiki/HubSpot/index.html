HubSpot

HubSpot, Inc. is an American developer and marketer of software products for inbound marketing, sales, and customer service. It was founded by Brian Halligan and Dharmesh Shah in 2006 and is headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts.

History

Halligan and Shah, who met at MIT, popularised the concept of "inbound marketing" — attracting customers through content and search rather than interruptive advertising. HubSpot raised venture funding from firms including Sequoia Capital and went public on the New York Stock Exchange in 2014 under the ticker HUBS. Yamini Rangan became chief executive in 2021, succeeding Halligan.

Products

HubSpot's platform is built around a free customer relationship management (CRM) system, with paid "hubs" for marketing automation, sales enablement, customer service ticketing, content management, and operations. The company also runs HubSpot Academy, a widely used free training and certification programme, and the annual INBOUND conference in Boston.
