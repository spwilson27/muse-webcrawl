Ushguli

Ushguli

Location: Svaneti, Georgia

Elevation: approx. 2,100 m

Status: UNESCO World Heritage Site (part of Upper Svaneti, 1996)

Ushguli is a community of four villages — Zhibiani, Chvibiani, Murqmeli and Chazhashi — in the Mestia municipality of Svaneti, Georgia. Situated at the head of the Enguri gorge at around 2,100 metres above sea level, it is often described as one of the highest continuously inhabited settlements in Europe.

Setting

The villages lie in the shadow of Mount Shkhara, at 5,193 m the highest peak in Georgia. The community is snowbound for much of the winter, when the road to Mestia may be impassable, and life retains many medieval features shaped by the isolation of the Caucasus highlands.

Heritage

Ushguli is famous for its medieval defensive tower houses, built by Svan families between the 9th and 12th centuries, and for the 12th-century Lamaria church with its medieval frescoes. Together with the rest of Upper Svaneti, the villages were inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1996 for their preserved medieval landscape.
