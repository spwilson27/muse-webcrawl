List of earthquakes in China

China has a long recorded history of destructive earthquakes, owing to the collision of the Indian Plate with the Eurasian Plate and the resulting fault systems across the country's west and north. Chinese historical records of earthquakes extend back nearly 4,000 years.

The 1556 Shaanxi earthquake is the deadliest earthquake — and one of the deadliest natural disasters — in recorded history; many victims lived in yaodong cave dwellings carved into loess cliffs, which collapsed. The 2008 Sichuan earthquake devastated Wenchuan County and surrounding areas and prompted national reforms in building standards and disaster response.
