David Lipman

David J. Lipman is an American biologist and computational biologist best known as the long-serving director of the National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI), a division of the U.S. National Library of Medicine at the National Institutes of Health, which he led from its founding in 1989 until 2017.

Career

Lipman was a co-developer, with Stephen Altschul and colleagues, of the BLAST (Basic Local Alignment Search Tool) program for comparing biological sequences, one of the most widely used tools in bioinformatics. At NCBI he oversaw resources including GenBank, PubMed, and PubMed Central, championing open access to scientific literature and data.

Honors

He is a member of the National Academy of Medicine and the National Academy of Sciences, and received the ISCB Senior Scientist Award for his contributions to computational biology.
