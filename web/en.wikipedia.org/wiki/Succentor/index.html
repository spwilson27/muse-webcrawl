Succentor

The succentor (from Latin succentor, "one who sings in answer") is a member of a cathedral or collegiate church chapter who assists and deputises for the precentor in directing the music and liturgy of the choir. The succentor is responsible for the singing on one side of the choir (traditionally the cantoris side, opposite the decani side led by the precentor) and for maintaining the chant when the precentor is absent.

Role and history

In medieval cathedral foundations, the succentor was often also the master of the choristers or head of the song school, training the choirboys in plainsong. In some cathedrals the office survives today, sometimes combined with the position of organist or assistant organist. The corresponding deputy in other musical traditions differs; in Anglican practice the succentor is typically a minor canon or a lay clerk appointed by the chapter.
