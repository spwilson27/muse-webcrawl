Cérès is a commune in the Loire department in the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region of central France. It is a small rural village situated in the rolling countryside east of the Loire plain, surrounded by farmland and pasture.

Geography

The commune lies in the canton of Charlieu, in the arrondissement of Roanne. The local landscape is characterised by bocage — small fields divided by hedgerows — typical of the Charolais-Brionnais fringe to the north of the department.

Administration

Like other French communes, Cérès is administered by an elected municipal council and a mayor. Services for its few hundred residents are largely shared with neighbouring communes through intercommunal structures.
