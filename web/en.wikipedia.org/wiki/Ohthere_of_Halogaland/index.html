Ohthere of Hålogaland

Ohthere of Hålogaland (Norwegian: Ottar fra Hålogaland) was a Viking Age seafarer known only from an account of his travels that he gave to King Alfred (r. 871–99) of the Anglo-Saxon kingdom of Wessex in about 890 AD. His account was incorporated into an Anglo-Saxon adaptation of Orosius' Histories against the Pagans.

Ohthere described voyages north around the North Cape to the White Sea, and south to Hedeby in Denmark. He described his home in Hålogaland, the northernmost inhabited part of Norway, his wealth in reindeer, and tribute paid by the Sami (Finnas). His account is a key source for early Scandinavian geography and trade.
