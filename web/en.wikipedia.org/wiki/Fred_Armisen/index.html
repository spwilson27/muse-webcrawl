Fred Armisen

Fereydun Robert "Fred" Armisen (born December 4, 1966) is an American actor, comedian, writer, producer and musician. He is best known as a cast member of Saturday Night Live (2002–2013) and as the co-creator and co-star, with Carrie Brownstein, of the sketch comedy series Portlandia (2011–2018).

Born: December 4, 1966, Hattiesburg, Mississippi, U.S.
Occupations: Actor, comedian, writer, musician
Years active: 1984–present
Known for: Saturday Night Live; Portlandia

Early life and music

Armisen was born in Hattiesburg, Mississippi, to a Venezuelan mother and a father of German and Korean descent, and grew up on Long Island, New York. Inspired by punk rock, he became a drummer, playing in the Chicago post-hardcore band Trenchmouth in the late 1980s and early 1990s and later performing with the Blue Man Group. His comedy career began with short films made while posing as a journalist at music festivals, which led to work on HBO's Reverb and the cult comedy showcase circuit.

Saturday Night Live

Armisen joined Saturday Night Live in 2002 and remained for eleven seasons, becoming known for musical and character-based impressions including Barack Obama (2008–2012), Prince, Mahmoud Ahmadinejad and Randy Newman, and recurring characters such as the politically correct Garth (of "Garth and Kat"), roadie Ian Rubbish and producer Nicholas Fehn. From 2014 to 2022 he was the bandleader and drummer of the 8G Band on Late Night with Seth Meyers.

Portlandia and other work

Portlandia, created with Carrie Brownstein and Jonathan Krisel for IFC, satirised the hipster culture of Portland, Oregon, across eight seasons and won Peabody and Writers Guild awards. Armisen co-created and stars in the mockumentary series Documentary Now! (2015– ) with Bill Hader, and co-created the Spanish-language comedy Los Espookys (2019–2022). He has appeared in films including Anchorman, EuroTrip and The Dictator, voiced characters in animated series, and played Uncle Fester in Netflix's Wednesday (2022).
