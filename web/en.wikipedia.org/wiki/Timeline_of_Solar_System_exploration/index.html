Timeline of Solar System exploration

This is a timeline of Solar System exploration ordering events by date of spacecraft launch or encounter. It includes the principal robotic missions — flybys, orbiters, landers, rovers, and sample returns — as well as the crewed Apollo programme that landed astronauts on the Moon between 1969 and 1972.

Overview

Exploration began with a rapid sequence of Soviet and American firsts during the Space Race. The 1970s opened the outer Solar System, and the Voyager probes exploited a rare planetary alignment in 1977. From the 1990s, long-lived orbiters and rovers transformed Mars and the gas giants into thoroughly studied worlds, while new spacefaring nations — Europe, Japan, China, and India — joined the effort. The most distant active missions, the Voyagers, continue to return data from interstellar space more than four decades after launch.
