The Texas Heart Institute (THI) is a nonprofit cardiology and cardiovascular surgery research and education institution located in the Texas Medical Center in Houston, Texas. It was founded in 1962 by cardiovascular surgeon Denton A. Cooley.

History

Cooley founded the institute at St. Luke's Episcopal Hospital to advance research and training in heart surgery. In 1968 Cooley performed the first successful heart transplant in the United States, and in 1969 he implanted the first total artificial heart in a human patient at the institute. THI became closely associated with rival surgeon Michael DeBakey of nearby Baylor College of Medicine, a celebrated feud in American medicine.

Research and education

The institute conducts research in areas including stem cell therapy, regenerative medicine, ventricular assist devices, electrophysiology, and minimally invasive surgery, and it runs fellowship programs that have trained thousands of cardiologists and surgeons. Its clinical partner is Baylor St. Luke's Medical Center, and in 2022 THI formed an integrated academic affiliation with the University of Texas Health Science Center at Houston.
