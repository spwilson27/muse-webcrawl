Gwangju

Gwangju (Korean: 광주, "city of light") is South Korea's sixth-largest metropolis and a designated Metropolitan City, completely surrounded by South Jeolla Province. It has a population of roughly 1.4 million and is a centre of administration, education and culture for the Honam region.

The city is internationally known for the Gwangju Uprising of May 1980, when citizens rose against the military junta following martial law; hundreds were killed when troops retook the city, and the event became a landmark in South Korea's democratisation, commemorated at the May 18th National Cemetery. Gwangju hosts the Gwangju Biennale, Asia's oldest contemporary art biennale (founded 1995), the Kimchi Culture Festival, and is known for its cuisine. It was host city for events of the 2002 FIFA World Cup and the 2015 Summer Universiade.
