Jefferson nickel

The Jefferson nickel is a five-cent coin struck by the United States Mint since 1938, when it replaced the Buffalo nickel. Designed by Felix Schlag, its obverse depicts President Thomas Jefferson and its reverse his Virginia home, Monticello.

Value5 U.S. cents
First minted1938
ObverseThomas Jefferson
ReverseMonticello

History

During World War II, nickels were struck in a copper-silver-manganese alloy (1942–1945) to save nickel for the war effort, marked with a large mint mark above Monticello. In 2004–2005 the Westward Journey series issued commemorative reverses marking the Lewis and Clark bicentennial, and a new forward-facing Jefferson portrait by Joe Fitzgerald debuted in 2006.
