MotoE

MotoE, officially the FIM Enel MotoE World Championship (formerly a World Cup), is a class of motorcycle racing that uses only electric motorcycles. The series runs as a support category at selected rounds of the MotoGP World Championship and is sanctioned by the FIM, with Dorna Sports as promoter.

History

MotoE held its inaugural season in 2019 with a shortened calendar after a fire at Jerez destroyed the entire fleet of bikes before the season began. The first championship was won by Matteo Ferrari. From 2023 the series gained full World Championship status.

Motorcycles

The series is a single-make formula. From 2019 to 2022, Energica supplied the Ego Corsa motorcycle. From 2023, Ducati became the sole supplier with the V21L prototype-derived machine, producing around 110 kW (150 hp) with a top speed of roughly 275 km/h. Races are short, typically around seven to eight laps, reflecting battery capacity.

Champions

Champions have included Matteo Ferrari (2019), Jordi Torres (2020, 2021), Dominique Aegerter (2022), and Mattia Casadei (2023).
