Severus Snape

Severus Snape is a fictional character in J. K. Rowling's Harry Potter series. A half-blood wizard, he is the Potions master at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, later Defence Against the Dark Arts professor and finally headmaster. Initially portrayed as a villainous and bullying teacher hostile to Harry Potter, Snape is progressively revealed to be a double agent whose actions were motivated by his lifelong love for Harry's mother, Lily Evans.

Character biography

Snape grew up in Spinner's End, near the Evans family, and befriended Lily Evans before they attended Hogwarts, where he was sorted into Slytherin. At school he was bullied by James Potter and Sirius Black, and excelled in Potions, annotating his textbook with his own inventions under the name "the Half-Blood Prince". After an irrevocable falling-out with Lily, he joined the Death Eaters, and overheard part of the prophecy that led Lord Voldemort to target the Potters. Horrified, Snape begged Albus Dumbledore to protect Lily, and thereafter served Dumbledore as a spy.

Throughout the series Snape protects Harry while treating him with contempt born of his resemblance to James. In Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince he kills Dumbledore — an act later revealed to have been prearranged between them. In Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows he is killed by Voldemort's snake Nagini, and with his last moments gives Harry memories revealing his loyalty and the truth about Harry's destiny. Harry later names his second son Albus Severus Potter in his honour.

Reception

Snape is widely regarded as one of the most complex and popular characters in the series; readers voted him their favourite character in a 2011 Bloomsbury poll. In the film adaptations he was portrayed by Alan Rickman, whose performance was critically acclaimed.
