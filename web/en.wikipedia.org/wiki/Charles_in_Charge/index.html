Charles in Charge is an American sitcom starring Scott Baio as Charles, a young college student who works as a live-in babysitter and housekeeper in exchange for room and board. The series aired on CBS in 1984–85 and then in first-run syndication from 1987 to 1990.

Premise

In the first season Charles worked for the Pembroke family; when the show returned in syndication, he worked for the Powell family, with his best friend Buddy (Willie Aames) providing comic relief. The cast changes included Nicole Eggert and Josie Davis as the Powell children.
