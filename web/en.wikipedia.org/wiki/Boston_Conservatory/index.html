The Boston Conservatory, now Boston Conservatory at Berklee, is a private performing arts conservatory in Boston, Massachusetts. Founded in 1867, it is one of the oldest conservatories of its kind in the United States and is particularly noted for its musical theater and contemporary dance programs.

History

The conservatory was founded by Julius Eichberg, a German-born violinist and composer, in 1867. It was one of the first conservatories to admit women and African Americans. In 2016 it merged with Berklee College of Music, while retaining its own name, programs, and Fenway campus.

Programs

The school offers undergraduate and graduate degrees through divisions of music (including classical performance, composition, and contemporary classical music), dance — whose contemporary dance program is among the oldest in the nation — and theater, including a widely recognized B.F.A. in musical theater. Students present hundreds of public performances each year.

Notable alumni

Alumni include Katharine McPhee, Jennifer Simard, and many performers on Broadway and in major dance and opera companies.
