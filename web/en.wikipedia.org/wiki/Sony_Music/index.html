Sony Music

Sony Music Entertainment (SME), commonly known as Sony Music, is an American multinational music company owned by Sony Group Corporation. It is the second-largest of the "Big Three" record companies, behind Universal Music Group and ahead of Warner Music Group. Its music publishing division, Sony Music Publishing, is the largest music publisher in the world.

History

The company's roots trace back to the American Record Corporation (1929) and CBS Records, which Sony acquired in 1988 for US$2 billion, renaming it Sony Music Entertainment in 1991. From 2004 to 2008, Sony operated a joint venture with Bertelsmann, Sony BMG, before buying out Bertelsmann's stake. In 2019, Sony's music businesses were consolidated under the Sony Music Group.

Labels and artists

Sony Music operates flagship labels including Columbia Records, RCA Records, Epic Records, and Arista Records. Its roster has included Michael Jackson, Beyoncé, Adele, Bob Dylan, Bruce Springsteen, Harry Styles, and many of the best-selling recording artists in history. Sony Music Publishing administers the catalogues of songwriters including, historically, the Beatles' Northern Songs catalogue through its interest in ATV Music.

Related companies

Sony Music Entertainment Japan (SMEJ) operates independently of the US company and reports directly to Sony Group in Tokyo; see Sony Music Entertainment Japan. Sony Music is separate from Sony Interactive Entertainment, the PlayStation company.
