The 1936 NFL Championship Game was the fourth championship game of the National Football League, played on 13 December 1936. The Western Division champion Green Bay Packers defeated the Eastern Division champion Boston Redskins 21–6.

Background

The game was scheduled for Boston, but Redskins owner George Preston Marshall, angered by poor home attendance, moved it to the neutral Polo Grounds in New York. It was the first NFL title game played at a neutral site. Marshall moved the franchise to Washington the following year.

Game summary

Don Hutson opened the scoring with a touchdown catch from Arnie Herber, and Green Bay controlled the game behind its passing attack and defence. The championship was the Packers' fourth league title and their first won in a championship game rather than the standings.
