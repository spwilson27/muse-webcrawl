Star Wars: The Last Jedi

Star Wars: The Last Jedi (also known as Star Wars: Episode VIII – The Last Jedi) is a 2017 American epic space opera film written and directed by Rian Johnson and produced by Lucasfilm. It is the second instalment of the Star Wars sequel trilogy, following The Force Awakens (2015).

Plot

Rey seeks the guidance of the exiled Luke Skywalker on Ahch-To while the Resistance, led by General Leia Organa, flees the pursuing First Order. As the Resistance's fuel runs low, Rey confronts Kylo Ren and Supreme Leader Snoke, while Luke ultimately projects himself across the galaxy to confront Ren on Crait, buying the survivors time to escape.

Cast and reception

The ensemble cast includes Mark Hamill, Carrie Fisher (in her final film role), Adam Driver, Daisy Ridley, John Boyega, Oscar Isaac, Andy Serkis, and Kelly Marie Tran. The film grossed over $1.3 billion worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing film of 2017. Critics praised its direction, visuals, and performances, while audience reactions were notably divided.
