Cellular agriculture

Cellular agriculture focuses on the production of agricultural products from cell cultures using a combination of biotechnology, tissue engineering, molecular biology, and synthetic biology, as an alternative to conventional animal agriculture.

Approaches

Two main approaches exist: tissue engineering, used for cultured meat grown from animal cells on scaffolds in bioreactors; and precision fermentation, in which engineered yeast or bacteria produce specific proteins such as milk whey, casein, egg white or heme.

Development and regulation

The first cultured beef burger was presented by Mark Post's team in 2013. Singapore became the first country to approve sale of cultured chicken (2020), followed by approvals in the United States (2023). Supporters cite lower land use and emissions and animal-welfare benefits; challenges include cost, scaling bioreactors, energy use and consumer acceptance.
