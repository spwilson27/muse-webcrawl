Storfjorden (Sunnmøre)

Storfjorden (English: "the big fjord") is a fjord in the Sunnmøre region of Møre og Romsdal county, Norway. At about 110 kilometres (68 mi) long, it is the fifth longest fjord in Norway. The fjord runs from Hareidlandet island in the west, near the town of Ålesund, eastwards and inwards to the village of Geiranger at the head of its innermost branch, the Geirangerfjorden.

Geography

The fjord system cuts through the municipalities of Hareid, Ulstein, Ørsta, Sula, Ålesund, Sykkylven, Stranda, and Fjord. The main fjord has several branches, including the Sulafjorden, Vartdalsfjorden, Ørstafjorden, Sykkylvsfjorden, Norddalsfjorden, Tafjorden, Sunnylvsfjorden, and the famous Geirangerfjorden, the last two of which form part of the West Norwegian Fjords UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Transport

Several ferry crossings traverse the fjord, connecting the road network of Sunnmøre, and cruise ships travel its length to Geiranger during the summer season. A fixed-link subsea tunnel and bridge project, discussed under names such as the Storfjord bridge and tunnel connections, has long been proposed to replace some crossings.
