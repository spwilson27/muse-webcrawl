J. Allen Hynek

Josef Allen Hynek (May 1, 1910 – April 27, 1986) was an American astronomer, professor, and ufologist. He is perhaps best remembered for his UFO research; he worked as scientific advisor to the U.S. Air Force's Project Sign, Project Grudge and Project Blue Book.

Career

Hynek earned his PhD in astrophysics from the University of Chicago in 1935 and taught at Ohio State University and Northwestern University, where he chaired the astronomy department.

UFO studies

Initially sceptical, Hynek came to believe some UFO reports deserved serious study. He devised the "Close Encounter" classification system, later popularised by the film Close Encounters of the Third Kind, for which he was a technical advisor. In 1973 he founded the Center for UFO Studies (CUFOS).
