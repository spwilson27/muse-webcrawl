The BlackBerry Q10 is a smartphone manufactured by BlackBerry Limited (then Research In Motion). It was unveiled on January 30, 2013 as one of the two launch devices for the BlackBerry 10 operating system, alongside the touchscreen BlackBerry Z10, and was released in the spring of 2013.

Design and hardware

The Q10 was the first BlackBerry 10 device to feature the brand's signature physical QWERTY keyboard, combined with a square 3.1-inch 720 × 720 Super AMOLED touchscreen. It is powered by a dual-core Qualcomm Snapdragon S4 Plus processor (a TI OMAP variant in some models), with 2 GB of RAM and 16 GB of internal storage expandable by microSD. The phone has an 8-megapixel rear camera, a 2-megapixel front camera, a removable 2100 mAh battery, NFC, and LTE connectivity in most variants.

Reception

Reviewers praised the keyboard as the best physical smartphone keyboard available and the build quality, but criticised the small app selection of the new platform and the learning curve of its gesture-based interface. The device was aimed at loyal BlackBerry users attached to hardware keyboards; its design was later developed into the BlackBerry Classic.
