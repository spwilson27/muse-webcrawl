Riot Games, Inc. is an American video game developer, publisher and esports tournament organizer based in Los Angeles, California. The company was founded in September 2006 by Brandon Beck and Marc Merrill to develop League of Legends, released in 2009, which became one of the most-played PC games in the world. Riot has since expanded into other genres and media, most notably the tactical first-person shooter Valorant (2020), the auto battler Teamfight Tactics (2019), the digital card game Legends of Runeterra (2020), and the mobile/console title League of Legends: Wild Rift (2020).

Chinese technology conglomerate Tencent acquired a majority stake in Riot in 2011 and full ownership in December 2015. Riot operates numerous offices worldwide and runs major international esports leagues for its titles, including the League of Legends World Championship and the Valorant Champions Tour. The company also produces music and animated media set in its fictional universes, such as the animated series Arcane (2021).

History

Riot Games was founded in 2006 by Brandon Beck and Marc Merrill, friends from the University of Southern California who were inspired by the Warcraft III mod Defense of the Ancients. Riot's first title, League of Legends, was announced in October 2008 and released on October 27, 2009. The game grew rapidly through its free-to-play model, and by the mid-2010s had tens of millions of monthly players.

In February 2011, Tencent bought a 93% stake in Riot for approximately $400 million, and in December 2015 it acquired the remaining equity. In October 2019, during its tenth-anniversary celebration, Riot announced a slate of new projects beyond League of Legends, ending its long-standing single-game focus.

Games

Riot's catalogue centers on the League of Legends universe of Runeterra, including the MOBA itself, Teamfight Tactics, Legends of Runeterra, and Wild Rift. Its other major franchise is Valorant, a 5v5 character-based tactical shooter released in June 2020. Riot also publishes third-party titles through its Riot Forge label and has games in development, including a fighting game and an MMO set in Runeterra.

Esports

Riot organizes professional leagues and international tournaments for League of Legends and Valorant. The League of Legends World Championship, held annually since 2011, is among the most-watched esports events in the world. Regional leagues operate in North America, Europe, Korea, China, and other territories.

Controversies

In 2018, reporting on Riot's workplace culture led to a class-action gender discrimination lawsuit, which the company settled for $100 million in 2021. Riot subsequently committed to changes in hiring, pay equity, and workplace policies.
