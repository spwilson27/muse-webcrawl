Radha

Radha is a Hindu goddess and the chief consort of the god Krishna. She is worshipped as the goddess of love, tenderness, compassion, and devotion, and is regarded in many traditions as the supreme goddess and the internal potency (hladini shakti) of Krishna.

Tradition

Radha's story is told in the Bhagavata Purana, Gita Govinda, and other Vaishnava texts. Born in Barsana and raised in Vrindavan, her selfless love for Krishna is the model of bhakti in Gaudiya Vaishnavism and other Krishnaite movements. Festivals such as Radhashtami and Holi celebrate her.

Worship

Radha is worshipped together with Krishna as Radha-Krishna in temples across India and worldwide, notably in Vrindavan, Barsana, and ISKCON temples.
