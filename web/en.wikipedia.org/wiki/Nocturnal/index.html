Nocturnal

Nocturnal may refer to:

Science and nature

  • Nocturnality, animal behaviour characterised by activity during the night and sleeping during the day.
  • Nocturnal (astronomy), or nocturlabe, an instrument for determining time from the positions of stars at night.
  • Nocturnal emission, an ejaculation occurring during sleep.
  • Nocturnal enuresis, bedwetting during sleep.
  • Nocturnal hypoglycemia, low blood sugar during the night.
  • Nocturnal animals, see Nocturnality.

Arts and entertainment

  • Nocturnal, a 2007 album by the American deathcore band The Black Dahlia Murder.
  • Nocturnal, a 2010 novel by Scott Sigler.
  • "Nocturnal", a song in various albums and by various artists.
  • Nocturnal Animals, a 2016 film directed by Tom Ford.

Religion

  • Nocturns, a division of the canonical hours of prayer said during the night, particularly within Matins.
  • Nocturnal Adoration Society, a Catholic organisation promoting night-time eucharistic adoration.
