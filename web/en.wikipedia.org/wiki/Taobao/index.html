Taobao

Taobao (Chinese: 淘宝网; pinyin: Táobǎo wǎng) is a Chinese online shopping platform operated by Alibaba Group. Founded in May 2003 by Jack Ma's Alibaba, it is one of the world's largest e-commerce websites, providing a marketplace primarily for consumer-to-consumer (C2C) and small-business retail. Its name means "search for treasure."

History

Taobao was launched in 2003 as Alibaba's answer to eBay's expansion into China through EachNet. Offering free listings to sellers, localised features and the escrow-based Alipay payment service (introduced in 2004) to build buyer trust, Taobao rapidly overtook eBay in China; eBay withdrew its main Chinese site in 2006. In 2008 Alibaba launched Taobao Mall — later renamed Tmall — as a separate business-to-consumer platform for brand retailers.

Features

Sellers on Taobao range from individuals to large merchants, offering goods from clothing and electronics to services. The platform integrates instant messaging (AliWangWang), user reviews, and extensive livestream commerce, in which hosts sell products in real time. Taobao is a central venue of the annual Singles' Day (11 November) shopping festival, the world's largest retail event by sales. The platform has also spawned buying-agent services that let overseas customers purchase Taobao goods.
