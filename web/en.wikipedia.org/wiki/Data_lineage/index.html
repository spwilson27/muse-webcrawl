Data lineage

Data lineage is the record of data's origins, movements, transformations, and uses over its lifecycle. It shows where data comes from, how it changes, and where it goes, supporting governance, compliance, debugging, and impact analysis.


Field: Data management, data governance

Concepts

Lineage is represented as a directed graph of datasets, processes, and systems, at both business (high-level) and technical (column-level) granularity. It includes provenance metadata such as source systems, transformation logic, timestamps, and owners.

Uses

Organisations use lineage for regulatory compliance (e.g. GDPR, BCBS 239), root-cause analysis of data quality issues, assessing the impact of schema changes, and building trust in analytics and machine-learning pipelines. Modern data catalogues and open standards such as OpenLineage automate lineage capture from ETL and query engines.
