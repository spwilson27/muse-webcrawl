Jean Danjou


Captain Jean Danjou

Jean Danjou (15 April 1828 – 30 April 1863) was a decorated French army captain of the Foreign Legion, famous for commanding the Legion's stand at the Battle of Camarón in Mexico.

Battle of Camarón

On 30 April 1863, during the French intervention in Mexico, Danjou led a company of 62 legionnaires escorting a convoy. Attacked by a far larger Mexican force near the village of Camarón, the legionnaires made a last stand in a hacienda; Danjou was killed early in the fighting, and his men fought nearly to the last.

Legacy

Camarón became the Foreign Legion's defining battle honour, commemorated every 30 April as Camerone Day. Danjou's wooden hand, lost in an earlier explosion, is the Legion's most prized relic and is paraded annually. See also French Foreign Legion.
