Happier Than Ever is the second studio album by American singer-songwriter Billie Eilish, released on July 30, 2021, by Darkroom and Interscope Records. Eilish co-wrote the album with its producer, her brother Finneas O'Connell.

A downtempo pop record, the album deals with the struggles of fame, self-reflection, and a past abusive relationship. It was promoted by the singles "My Future", "Therefore I Am", "Your Power", "Lost Cause", "NDA", and the title track.

Reception

The album received widespread critical acclaim and topped charts in numerous countries, debuting at number one on the US Billboard 200. Its title track was praised for its shift from a soft ballad into a rock-driven climax and became a signature live performance.

Track listing

Tracks include "Getting Older", "I Didn't Change My Number", "Billie Bossa Nova", "Oxytocin", "Halley's Comet", "Not My Responsibility", "Overheated", "Everybody Dies", and "Male Fantasy".
