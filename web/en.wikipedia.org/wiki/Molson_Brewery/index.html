Molson Brewery

Molson Brewery is a Canadian multinational brewing company headquartered in Montreal, Quebec. Founded in 1786 by John Molson, it is the oldest brewery in North America and one of the oldest companies in Canada. Molson merged with Coors in 2005 to form Molson Coors, one of the world's largest brewing companies, but the Molson name and its brands continue in Canada.

History

John Molson, an English immigrant, bought a small brewery on the St Lawrence River in Montreal in 1786. The business prospered and the Molson family expanded into steamships, banking (Molson's Bank) and railways; Molson money also helped found McGill University and the Montreal General Hospital. In the 20th century Molson grew through acquisitions, including the Sick's and Carling O'Keefe breweries, to become Canada's largest brewer. The family also owned the Montreal Canadiens hockey team from 1957 to 1971, and the Molson name appeared on the arena and the team's sponsorship.

Brands and merger

Molson's flagship brands include Molson Canadian (launched 1959), Molson Export, Molson Dry, Rickard's and Creemore Springs. In 2005 Molson merged with the American brewer Adolph Coors Company to create Molson Coors Brewing Company, headquartered jointly in Montreal and Denver; in 2016 Molson Coors acquired full control of the Miller brands after the SABMiller takeover by AB InBev. The historic Montreal brewery site on Notre-Dame Street was replaced by a new brewery in Longueuil, opened in 2021.
