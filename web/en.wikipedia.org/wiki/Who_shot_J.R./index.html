Who shot J.R.?

"Who shot J.R.?" is an advertising catchphrase and cultural phenomenon arising from the American television series Dallas. It refers to the cliffhanger ending of the third-season finale, "A House Divided" (first broadcast 21 March 1980), in which oil tycoon J. R. Ewing is shot by an unseen assailant, leaving viewers to speculate for eight months about the shooter's identity.

The cliffhanger

In "A House Divided", J. R. — played by Larry Hagman — is working late in his office at Ewing Oil when a gunshot rings out; he collapses, and the camera lingers on the fallen tycoon without revealing the shooter. The episode ended the season, and the identity of the gunman became one of the most discussed questions in television history. The phrase "Who shot J.R.?" appeared on T-shirts, in newspaper headlines, and in betting pools around the world.

The resolution

The mystery was resolved in the fourth-season episode "Who Done It", broadcast on 21 November 1980. The shooter was revealed to be Kristin Shepard (Mary Crosby), J. R.'s sister-in-law and former mistress, who had shot him in a drunken rage after he refused to marry her. An estimated 83 million viewers in the United States watched the resolution, making it one of the most-watched television episodes of all time.

Cultural impact

"Who shot J.R.?" is credited with popularising the season-ending cliffhanger as a television convention and demonstrating the global reach of American prime-time drama. The storyline has been parodied and referenced in countless shows, and the phrase entered the language as shorthand for any eagerly anticipated mystery.
