Lalisa Manobal (born Pranpriya Manobal; 27 March 1997), known mononymously as Lisa, is a Thai rapper, singer and dancer based in South Korea. She is a member of the South Korean girl group Blackpink, which debuted under YG Entertainment in August 2016.

Lisa performing with Blackpink.

Life and career

Lisa was born in Buriram Province, Thailand, and was raised in Bangkok. In 2010 she was the only applicant accepted at YG Entertainment's auditions in Thailand and moved to South Korea in 2011, becoming the label's first non-ethnically Korean artist. She debuted with Blackpink in 2016 as the group's main dancer and lead rapper, and the group went on to become one of the best-selling girl groups of all time.

Solo work

Lisa made her solo debut with the single album Lalisa in September 2021; its lead single of the same name broke records for the most-viewed music video by a solo artist in 24 hours, and the follow-up "Money" became a global viral hit. In 2024 she founded her own management company, Lloud, and released singles including "Rockstar", "New Woman" (with Rosalía) and "Moonlit Floor", followed by her debut studio album Alter Ego (2025). She also made her acting debut in the third season of the HBO series The White Lotus. Lisa is one of the most-followed K-pop artists on social media and a global ambassador for brands such as Celine and Bulgari.
