Adverbial
Grammar: Syntactic function / phrase type
Realised by: Adverbs, prepositional phrases, clauses
Function: Circumstantial modification of a clause

An adverbial is a word, phrase, or clause that functions as a modifier of a clause or sentence, providing circumstantial information about time, place, manner, cause, purpose, or condition.

Forms

Adverbials may be single adverbs (She left early), prepositional phrases (She left in the morning), noun phrases (She left last week), or adverbial clauses introduced by subordinating conjunctions (She left because it was late).

Adjuncts and complements

Grammarians distinguish optional adverbials (adjuncts) from adverbial complements that are required by a verb, such as the place phrase in He put the book on the table.
