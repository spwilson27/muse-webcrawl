The Republic Aviation Corporation was an American aircraft manufacturer based in Farmingdale, Long Island, New York. Originally known as the Seversky Aircraft Company, the company was responsible for the design and production of many important military aircraft, including its most famous products: the P-47 Thunderbolt fighter, one of the most successful high-altitude fighters of World War II, and the F-105 Thunderchief, the largest single-seat, single-engine combat aircraft in history.

History

The company was founded in 1931 by Alexander P. de Seversky, a Russian émigré and former Imperial Russian Navy pilot. After Seversky was removed from control in 1939, the firm was reorganized as Republic Aviation, with designer Alexander Kartveli responsible for its celebrated line of fighter aircraft. During World War II the Farmingdale plant built more than 15,000 P-47 Thunderbolts.

Post-war products included the F-84 Thunderjet and its swept-wing derivative the F-84F Thunderstreak, the RF-84F Thunderflash reconnaissance aircraft, and the F-105 Thunderchief, which bore the brunt of U.S. strike operations early in the Vietnam War. Republic also built the A-10 Thunderbolt II's predecessor studies and contributed to early space programs. In 1965 the company was acquired by Fairchild Hiller, becoming the Republic Aviation Division of Fairchild.
