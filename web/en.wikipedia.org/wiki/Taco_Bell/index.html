Taco Bell is an American multinational chain of fast food restaurants founded in 1962 by Glen Bell in Downey, California. Based in Irvine, California, Taco Bell is a subsidiary of Yum! Brands, Inc., and serves a variety of Mexican-inspired foods, including tacos, burritos, quesadillas, nachos, and specialty items. With more than 8,000 restaurants across the United States and dozens of other countries, it is the largest Mexican-style fast food chain in the world.

History

Glen Bell began in the restaurant business with a hot dog stand in San Bernardino, California, in 1948. Watching long lines at a Mexican restaurant across the street, he reverse-engineered its taco recipe and developed a method for pre-frying taco shells to speed service. He opened a series of taco stands under names including Taco-Tia and El Taco before founding the first restaurant bearing his own name, Taco Bell, in Downey in 1962. Franchising began in 1964, and the chain grew rapidly across the American Southwest and beyond.

PepsiCo acquired Taco Bell in 1978, and Bell sold his remaining shares. Under PepsiCo the chain expanded nationally and internationally and absorbed competitors and adjacent concepts. In 1997 PepsiCo spun off its restaurant division as Tricon Global Restaurants, renamed Yum! Brands in 2002, grouping Taco Bell with KFC and Pizza Hut.

Menu and marketing

Taco Bell's menu centers on seasoned ground beef, chicken, and steak in tacos, burritos, and combination items, alongside vegetarian and value offerings. Signature items include the Crunchwrap Supreme (2005), Doritos Locos Tacos (2012) — a co-branded product with a Doritos-flavored shell that became one of the most successful fast-food launches ever — the Mexican Pizza, and Mountain Dew Baja Blast, a drink exclusive to the chain for many years. Taco Bell has a strong late-night trade ("fourth meal") and a marketing voice aimed at younger customers, known for irreverent campaigns and extensive social media engagement. The chain's bell logo derives from the founder's surname.

The company has also received attention for its "Taco Bell weddings" at its Las Vegas flagship Cantina restaurant and for promotional tie-ins such as the promise of a free taco for everyone in America when a base was stolen during the World Series ("Steal a Base, Steal a Taco").
