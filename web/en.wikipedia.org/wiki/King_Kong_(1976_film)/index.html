King Kong (1976 film)

Directed by: John Guillermin
Produced by: Dino De Laurentiis
Starring: Jeff Bridges, Jessica Lange, Charles Grodin
Released: 1976

King Kong is a 1976 American monster adventure film produced by Dino De Laurentiis and directed by John Guillermin. A remake of the 1933 film of the same name, it stars Jeff Bridges, Charles Grodin, and Jessica Lange in her film debut.

Plot

Fred Wilson, an executive of the Petrox oil corporation, leads an expedition to an uncharted island in the Indian Ocean, believed to hold a vast oil deposit. Stowaway palaeontologist Jack Prescott and rescued actress Dwan join the voyage. On the island the party encounters a colossal ape, Kong, worshipped by the islanders. Kong is captured and transported to New York City as a promotional spectacle, escapes, and is eventually shot down from the twin towers of the World Trade Center, updating the Empire State Building climax of the original.

Production

The film was a major production employing a giant mechanical ape built under Carlo Rambaldi, alongside Rick Baker in an ape suit. John Barry composed the score. The film was a commercial success and won an Academy Award for Best Visual Effects (a special achievement award), though critical reaction was mixed.

Legacy

A sequel, King Kong Lives, followed in 1986. The 1976 film is one of three major King Kong remakes and adaptations of the original story, alongside Peter Jackson's 2005 film.
