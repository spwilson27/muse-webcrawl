Carolinas Campaign

DateJanuary – April 1865
LocationSouth Carolina and North Carolina
ResultUnion victory

The Carolinas Campaign (January 1 – April 26, 1865) was the final campaign of the American Civil War in the Carolinas theatre. Major General William Tecumseh Sherman advanced north from Savannah, Georgia, through South and North Carolina, against the Confederate forces of General Joseph E. Johnston.

Course of the campaign

Sherman's armies crossed into South Carolina in early 1865, captured Columbia, the state capital, and pushed into North Carolina, fighting the battles of Averasborough and Bentonville in March. After the fall of Richmond and Lee's surrender at Appomattox, Johnston surrendered his army to Sherman near Durham, North Carolina, on April 26, 1865 — the largest surrender of Confederate forces in the war.
