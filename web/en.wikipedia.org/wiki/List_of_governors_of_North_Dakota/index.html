List of governors of North Dakota

The governor of North Dakota is the head of government of the U.S. state of North Dakota. The governor is elected to a four-year term. The current officeholder is Kelly Armstrong, who took office on 15 December 2024. This article lists the governors of North Dakota since statehood in 1889.

The North Dakota State Capitol in Bismarck, seat of the governor's office.
