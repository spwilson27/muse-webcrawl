Hunedoara County

Hunedoara County (Romanian: Județul Hunedoara) is a county (județ) of Romania, in the historical region of Transylvania, with its capital city at Deva. It covers an area of about 7,063 km² and had a population of roughly 360,000 at the 2021 census.

Geography

The county lies in western Romania, spanning the Mureș River valley and parts of the Southern Carpathians, including the Retezat Mountains and Retezat National Park. Other towns include Hunedoara, Petroșani, Lupeni, Orăștie, Brad, and Hațeg.

History and economy

The area is rich in history: it contains the Dacian fortresses of the Orăștie Mountains, including Sarmizegetusa Regia, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and the medieval Corvin Castle. The Jiu Valley was a major coal-mining region, and Hunedoara was a centre of steel production during the communist era, industries that declined sharply after 1989.
