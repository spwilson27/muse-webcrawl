List of Formula One Grands Prix

This is a complete list of Formula One Grands Prix which have been part of the FIA World Championship since its inception in 1950. By the mid-2020s more than 1,100 World Championship races had been held under over 50 different Grand Prix titles in more than 30 countries.

The British Grand Prix at Silverstone, host of the first World Championship race in 1950.

The British and Italian Grands Prix are the only events to have featured in every championship season. The Indianapolis 500 counted towards the championship from 1950 to 1960 although few Formula One regulars contested it. Recent decades have added races in the Middle East and Asia, and new American events in Miami (2022) and Las Vegas (2023). Defunct events include the Argentine, South African, Mexican (in its earlier eras), Caesars Palace, Dallas, Detroit, European, Luxembourg, Pacific, San Marino and Pescara Grands Prix—the last, in 1957, being run on the longest circuit ever used in the championship.
