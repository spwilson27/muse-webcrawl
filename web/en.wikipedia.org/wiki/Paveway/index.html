Paveway

Paveway is a series of laser-guided bomb kits produced by Raytheon (originally Texas Instruments) that convert unguided bombs into precision-guided munitions. The name is a registered trademark of Raytheon.

ManufacturerRaytheon
TypeLaser-guided bomb kit
Introduced1968 (Paveway I)

Design

A Paveway kit adds a computer-control group with a semi-active laser seeker at the nose and an air foil group of fins at the tail to a standard bomb body, such as the Mark 80 series. The weapon homes on laser energy reflected from a target designated by the launching aircraft, another aircraft or ground forces.

Generations

Paveway I (first used in Vietnam) was followed by Paveway II with fixed plastic wings and improved electronics, Paveway III with proportional guidance and larger wings for low-altitude delivery, and the GPS/INS-aided Enhanced Paveway series.
