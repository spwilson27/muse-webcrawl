Peace of Constance

The Peace of Constance was a treaty signed on 25 June 1183 between the Holy Roman Emperor Frederick Barbarossa and the cities of the Lombard League, ending their long conflict in northern Italy.

Background

Frederick's campaigns to assert imperial authority in Italy were resisted by the Lombard League, whose forces defeated him at the Battle of Legnano in 1176. The preliminary Peace of Venice (1177) led to negotiations at Constance.

Terms

The emperor recognised the Lombard cities' right to elect their own consuls, build fortifications, maintain the League and exercise regalian rights within their territories, in return for formal recognition of imperial overlordship and certain payments. The settlement effectively confirmed the autonomy of the northern Italian communes.
