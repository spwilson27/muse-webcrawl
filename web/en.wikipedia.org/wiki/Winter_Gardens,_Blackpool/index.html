Winter Gardens, Blackpool
Location: Church Street, Blackpool, England
Opened: 1878
Venues: Opera House, Empress Ballroom, Pavilion, Olympia
Status: Grade II* listed

The Winter Gardens, Blackpool is a large entertainment complex in Blackpool, Lancashire, England, occupying a 4.9-acre town-centre site. Opened in 1878, it has grown into a collection of interlinked halls, theatres and ballrooms that have hosted variety shows, opera, political party conferences, dances and sporting events for more than a century.

History and components

The original gardens and pavilion opened in 1878; the grand Empress Ballroom followed in 1896, famed for its ornate plasterwork and one of the largest dance floors in Britain, and the Opera House — the present theatre dating from 1939, designed by Charles Deacon and seating nearly 3,000 — is among the largest stages in the United Kingdom. Frank Matcham designed the earlier Opera House (1889) and the Victoria Street foyer. Other spaces include the Spanish Hall, the Renaissance Hall and the Olympia exhibition hall.

Events

The Winter Gardens long served as a principal venue for the annual conferences of Britain's major political parties, especially the Conservative Party. It hosts the Blackpool Dance Festival, the world's foremost ballroom dancing competition, held in the Empress Ballroom since 1920; the World Matchplay darts tournament; and the Rebellion punk festival. It is Grade II* listed, and in 2018 a new conference and exhibition centre was added to the complex.
