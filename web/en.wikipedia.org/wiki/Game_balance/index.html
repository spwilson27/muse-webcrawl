Game balance

Game balance is the aspect of game design concerned with ensuring that a game's systems, options, and challenges are fair and provide interesting choices for players. A balanced game is one in which player skill, rather than the selection of an overpowered strategy, character, or item, is the primary determinant of success. Balance applies to video games, board games, card games, and role-playing games alike.

Concepts

Designers generally seek a state in which no single strategy is dominant — a situation sometimes described through the concept of intransitivity, as in rock–paper–scissors, where each option is strong against some and weak against others. A strategy that defeats all others is called a dominant strategy and is generally considered a failure of balance. Community slang describes overpowered elements as "OP" or "broken," and underpowered ones as weak or unusable.

Methods

Balance is achieved through mathematical modeling, simulation, playtesting, and telemetry from live games. In multiplayer online games, developers frequently adjust values in patches — weakening ("nerfing") or strengthening ("buffing") elements in response to win-rate statistics and community feedback. Single-player games balance difficulty curves so that challenge rises in step with player skill and character power.

Difficulty and fairness

In player-versus-environment games, balance concerns the relationship between player capabilities and opposition. In player-versus-player games, it concerns symmetry or calibrated asymmetry between sides, maps, and character rosters. Perfect balance is rarely attainable or even desirable; designers often aim for a dynamic metagame in which different strategies cycle in popularity.

Criticism

Some designers and players argue that an excessive focus on balance can homogenize games and remove interesting extremes, noting that many beloved games contain famously unbalanced elements. Others contend that in competitive and esports contexts, rigorous balance is essential to legitimacy.
