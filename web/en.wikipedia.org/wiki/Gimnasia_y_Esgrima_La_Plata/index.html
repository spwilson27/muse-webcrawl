Club de Gimnasia y Esgrima La Plata is an Argentine multi-sports club based in La Plata, Buenos Aires Province, founded in 1887. Its football team, nicknamed El Lobo ("The Wolf") for its stadium's location in the city's forest (El Bosque), plays in the Primera División.

History

Gimnasia won the amateur championship in 1929 and the Copa Centenario de la AFA in 1993–94, its first official AFA tournament title. The club is also famous for its passionate rivalry with Estudiantes de La Plata in the Clásico platense. Diego Maradona managed the team from 2019 until his death in 2020.

Other sports

The club fields teams in basketball, volleyball, gymnastics, fencing, and other sports, reflecting its "gymnastics and fencing" name.
