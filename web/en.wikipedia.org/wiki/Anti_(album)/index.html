Anti is the eighth studio album by Barbadian singer Rihanna, released on 28 January 2016 through Roc Nation and Westbury Road. It marked a departure from the dance-pop of her earlier albums toward a moodier, more experimental sound.

Content

The album was led by the single "Work", featuring Drake, which topped charts worldwide. Other tracks include "Needed Me", "Kiss It Better" and "Love on the Brain". The cover artwork, by Roy Nachum, depicts a childhood photograph of Rihanna overlaid with a gold crown and a poem in Braille.

Reception

Anti debuted at number one on the US Billboard 200 and received generally favourable reviews, praised for its emotional honesty and artistic risk-taking. It is frequently ranked among Rihanna's finest works.
