Gin pole

A gin pole is a simple supported pole used to lift loads. It consists of a vertical or near-vertical pole held in place by guy-wires, with a pulley or block and tackle at the top through which a hoisting rope passes. The name is thought to derive from "engine".

Uses

Gin poles are used to raise antenna towers, masts, and tower sections during construction and maintenance, to lift heavy equipment where a crane is impractical, and in logging and rigging work. Tower climbers use gin poles to hoist sections and antennas to the top of communications towers.

Safety

Because the pole relies entirely on its guying and footing, correct rigging and load limits are critical; gin pole failures under side loads have caused serious accidents in tower work.
