Canadian Opera Company

The Canadian Opera Company (COC) is an opera company in Toronto, Ontario, and the largest opera producer in Canada. Founded in 1950, it performs at the Four Seasons Centre for the Performing Arts, which opened in 2006.

Founded: 1950
Location: Toronto, Ontario
Venue: Four Seasons Centre for the Performing Arts

History

The company grew from the Opera Festival Company and was renamed the Canadian Opera Company in 1977. It has commissioned Canadian works and toured internationally.

Productions

Each season typically includes six productions ranging from standard repertoire by Mozart, Verdi and Puccini to contemporary opera, sung in the original language with English surtitles.
