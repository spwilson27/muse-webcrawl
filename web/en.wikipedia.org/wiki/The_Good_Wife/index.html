The Good Wife is an American legal and political drama television series created by Robert and Michelle King. It aired on CBS from 2009 to 2016 and stars Julianna Margulies as Alicia Florrick.

Premise

Alicia, the wife of Cook County State's Attorney Peter Florrick, returns to work as a junior litigator at the Chicago firm Stern, Lockhart & Gardner after her husband is jailed following a sex and corruption scandal. Over seven seasons the series follows Alicia's rise from junior associate to named partner and political candidate, her complicated relationships with firm partner Will Gardner and investigator Kalinda Sharma, and the machinery of Chicago politics. The show was noted for storylines drawn from contemporary technology and current events — among network dramas it was an early and sophisticated explorer of social media, surveillance, Bitcoin and online privacy.

Reception and legacy

The series won numerous awards, including a Peabody Award and Emmy Awards for Margulies and for guest and supporting performances. Its fifth season is frequently cited among the finest seasons of American television. The franchise continued with the spin-offs The Good Fight (2017–2022) and Elsbeth (2024–).
