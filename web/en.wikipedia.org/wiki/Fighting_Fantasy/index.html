Fighting Fantasy

Fighting Fantasy is a series of single-player role-playing gamebooks created by British authors Steve Jackson and Ian Livingstone, first published by Puffin Books in 1982. The reader plays the hero of the story, making choices that direct them between numbered paragraphs, and battles creatures using a simple dice-based combat system involving Skill, Stamina and Luck attributes.

History

The series began with The Warlock of Firetop Mountain (1982), written by Jackson and Livingstone together, and rapidly became a phenomenon, selling millions of copies worldwide. Dozens of titles followed, including The Citadel of Chaos, Deathtrap Dungeon, City of Thieves and the four-part Sorcery! series by Steve Jackson. Many books are set on the fantasy world of Titan, in particular the continent of Allansia. After a decline in the 1990s, the series was relaunched by Wizard Books in 2002 and later by Scholastic, with new titles alongside reissues.

Legacy

Fighting Fantasy popularised the gamebook format and introduced many readers to fantasy role-playing. It has been adapted into board games, video games, apps and a multi-player role-playing game (Advanced Fighting Fantasy).
