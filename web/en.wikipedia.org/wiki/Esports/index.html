Esports

Esports (also known as electronic sports, e-sports, or eSports) is a form of competition using video games. Esports often takes the form of organised, multiplayer video game competitions, particularly between professional players, individually or as teams. Major titles include League of Legends, Counter-Strike 2, Dota 2, Valorant and StarCraft II, played in leagues and arena tournaments watched by hundreds of millions online.

History

Competitive gaming grew from arcade high-score contests and 1990s LAN tournaments (Quake, StarCraft) into South Korea's televised professional leagues in the 2000s. Streaming on Twitch from the 2010s globalised audiences; the League of Legends World Championship and Dota's International — with prize pools once exceeding $40 million — fill stadiums.

Industry

Professional organisations sign salaried players, coaches and analysts; revenue comes from media rights, sponsorship, publisher support and merchandise, totalling over a billion dollars annually. Esports was a full medal event at the 2022 Asian Games (held 2023), and the IOC has launched Olympic Esports events. Issues include match-fixing, doping (e.g. stimulants), player burnout and short careers.
