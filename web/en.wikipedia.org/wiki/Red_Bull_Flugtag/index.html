Red Bull Flugtag

Red Bull Flugtag (German for "flight day") is an event organized by Red Bull in which competitors attempt to fly home-made, human-powered flying machines, size-limited to around 10 metres (33 ft) and usually not exceeding 300 kilograms (660 lb). The flying machines are usually launched off a pier about 9 metres (30 ft) high into the sea or a body of water. Most competitors enter for the entertainment value, and the flying machines rarely fly at all.

Format

Teams of up to five people design and build their craft in advance. On the day, the team pushes the craft (with one pilot aboard) off the end of the flight deck. Entries are judged on three criteria: the distance flown, the creativity of the craft, and a short performance given by the team before launch.

History

The first Flugtag took place in Vienna, Austria, in 1992. Since then, events have been held in more than 40 countries around the world, drawing large crowds. The world record distance, set at an event in Long Beach, California, in 2013, is 78.64 metres (258 ft), achieved by a team called "The Chicken Whisperers".

The name is a play on words: while Flugtag means "flight day" or "air show", the trademarked spelling suggests the English "flug tag".
