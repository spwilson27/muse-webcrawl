Canadian passport

The Canadian passport is the passport issued to citizens of Canada for international travel. The regular passport has a navy-blue cover bearing the Royal Arms of Canada and is issued by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada through the Passport Program. Since 2013 all new Canadian passports have been electronic passports (ePassports) containing a chip with the holder's information.

History

Canadian passports were first issued in the nineteenth century in the form of letters of request. Booklet passports appeared after the First World War, and the distinctive blue cover was introduced in 1921. A redesigned passport with new artwork and security features was unveiled in 2023, though the design proved controversial for removing some historical imagery.

Use and visa access

Canadian citizens can enter many countries without a prior visa; the Canadian passport consistently ranks among the world's more powerful travel documents, with visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to around 185 destinations. The passport's bilingual text, in English and French, includes a request in the name of the Crown for the bearer's safe passage.

Types

In addition to the regular passport, Canada issues diplomatic, special (official), temporary and emergency travel documents, distinguished by cover colour.
