The Jersey Shore

The Jersey Shore is the coastal region of the U.S. state of New Jersey, stretching along the Atlantic Ocean from the New York metropolitan area to the Philadelphia metropolitan area, roughly 141 miles of oceanfront.

Tourism

The region is a major summer destination known for its beaches, boardwalks, amusement piers, and shore towns such as Asbury Park, Atlantic City, Wildwood, and Cape May. Atlantic City is notable for its casinos and historic boardwalk, one of the oldest in the United States.

History

Resort development began in the nineteenth century with rail access from the cities. The shore was heavily damaged by Hurricane Sandy in 2012 and has since been substantially rebuilt. The name was also popularised by the MTV reality series Jersey Shore. See also New Jersey.
