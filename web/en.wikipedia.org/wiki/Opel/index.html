Opel is a German automobile manufacturer headquartered in Rüsselsheim. Adam Opel founded the company in 1862 as a sewing-machine maker; it later became Germany's largest bicycle manufacturer before producing its first automobile in 1899. The firm's emblem is the Blitz (lightning bolt) through a circle.

Ownership history

General Motors acquired Opel in 1929 (fully by 1931), and it served as GM's core European brand for nearly 90 years — cars were also sold as Vauxhall in the United Kingdom. In 2017 GM sold Opel to France's Groupe PSA, which in 2021 merged into Stellantis.

Models

Well-known models include the Kadett, Rekord, Manta, Corsa, Astra, Insignia and the electric Ampera. Opel announced plans to sell only electric vehicles in Europe from 2028.
