Trix

Trix is an American brand of breakfast cereal made by General Mills, introduced in 1954. It is a sweetened, fruit-flavoured cereal consisting of small corn-and-rice based pieces. The cereal is marketed primarily to children and is famous for its mascot, the Trix Rabbit, and its long-running slogan "Silly rabbit! Trix are for kids!"

History

General Mills launched Trix in 1954 as a sugar-coated version of its Kix cereal, with flavours originally described as raspberry red, lemony lemon and orangey orange. The fruit-shaped pieces were replaced in 1991 by round "fruit" balls, then by actual fruit-shaped pieces in the 2000s, before returning to classic fruit shapes in 2018 after a consumer campaign. In 2017–2018 General Mills brought back "Classic Trix" with artificial colours and flavours alongside a version coloured with natural ingredients.

The Trix Rabbit

The Trix Rabbit, created in 1959, is a cartoon rabbit perpetually scheming to obtain a bowl of Trix, only to be foiled by children who chant the slogan. In promotional "elections" held in 1976 and 1980, children voted overwhelmingly to let the rabbit finally have some Trix, and he was shown enjoying a bowl in the resulting advertisements.

Flavours and related products

Flavours have included Wildberry and Watermelon variants, and the cereal has been sold in yogurt (Trix Yogurt) and other spin-off forms. The slogan was briefly reversed in a 2020s advertising campaign aimed at adults.
