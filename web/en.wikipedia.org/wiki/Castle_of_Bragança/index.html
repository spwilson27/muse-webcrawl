Castle of Bragança

The Castle of Bragança (Portuguese: Castelo de Bragança) is a medieval castle in Bragança, northeastern Portugal, on the northeastern frontier with Spain. Its well-preserved citadel encloses the old town, a distinctive pillory, and the Domus Municipalis, a rare Romanesque civic building.

History

A fortress on the site was refounded by Sancho I in 1187 and granted a charter; the great keep was built under Afonso III and Dinis in the 13th century, and walls were strengthened during the Restoration War in the 17th century. It is a classified national monument housing a military museum.
