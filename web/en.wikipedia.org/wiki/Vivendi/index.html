Vivendi

Vivendi SE is a French investment company headquartered in Paris, focused on content, media and entertainment. Founded in 1853 as the Compagnie Générale des Eaux, a water utility, it transformed in the 1990s and 2000s into a media conglomerate before refocusing as a holding company around assets including the Canal+ group, Havas, Prisma Media and stakes in companies such as Lagardère and Universal Music Group.

History

The Compagnie Générale des Eaux was created by imperial decree of Napoleon III on 14 December 1853 to supply water to Lyon and Paris. Over the following century it diversified into waste management, energy, transport and construction. Under Guy Dejouany and then Jean-Marie Messier, who took over in 1996, the company bought into pay television (Canal+), telecoms (SFR/Cegetel) and, in 2000, Seagram's entertainment assets including Universal Studios and Universal Music — renaming itself Vivendi Universal.

The expansion left the group deeply indebted, and in 2002 Messier was forced out amid one of France's largest-ever corporate losses. His successor Jean-René Fourtou sold off much of the empire, including the water business (which became Veolia), and the group shortened its name back to Vivendi in 2006.

Refocusing under Bolloré

Industrialist Vincent Bolloré became the group's largest shareholder and chairman in 2014. Vivendi sold its telecoms assets, took control of the advertising group Havas (2017), bought the publisher Editis (later sold), and in 2021 listed Universal Music Group in Amsterdam, distributing most of its stake to shareholders. In 2023 it completed the takeover of the Lagardère group, adding publishing (Hachette) and travel retail. In December 2024 Vivendi split into four separately listed companies: Canal+, Havas, Louis Hachette Group, and a slimmed-down Vivendi holding.

Activities

Through its spin-offs and holdings, the Vivendi sphere spans pay television and streaming (Canal+), advertising and communications (Havas), magazine publishing (Prisma Media), book publishing (Hachette via Lagardère), live entertainment and ticketing, and video games through a stake in Gameloft, which it acquired in 2016.
