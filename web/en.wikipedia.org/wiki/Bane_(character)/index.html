Bane (character)

Bane is a fictional supervillain appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Chuck Dixon and artist Graham Nolan, he first appeared in Batman: Vengeance of Bane #1 (1993). Raised in the Peña Duro prison on the island of Santa Prisca, Bane is a brilliant strategist and tactician whose strength is enhanced by the super-steroid Venom. He is best known as the villain who broke Batman's back in the Knightfall storyline.

Fictional character biography

Bane was born to serve the life sentence of his father, a revolutionary who had fled the island nation of Santa Prisca. Raised in the brutal Peña Duro prison, he educated himself from books, trained his body, and survived experimental administration of the drug Venom, which killed other test subjects. Escaping to Gotham City, he studied Batman, reasoned out his secret identity, and — after exhausting the Dark Knight by engineering a mass escape from Arkham Asylum — fought and defeated him, breaking his back over his knee.

Batman's replacement, Jean-Paul Valley, later defeated Bane with superior technology and forced him into Venom withdrawal. Bane has since alternated between villain and antihero, serving with the Secret Six and the Suicide Squad and, in some storylines, seeking to destroy the drug cartels that created Venom.

In other media

Bane was played by Robert Swenson in Batman & Robin (1997) and by Tom Hardy in The Dark Knight Rises (2012). He has appeared in numerous animated series, including Batman: The Animated Series, and in video games such as the Batman: Arkham series.
