Tribalism

Tribalism is the state of being organised by, or advocating for, tribes or tribal lifestyles, and in modern usage a strong loyalty to one's own social group — in-group — often accompanied by hostility or bias toward out-groups.

Anthropology

In anthropology a tribe is a human social group bound by kinship, shared culture and territory, intermediate between a band and a chiefdom or state. Scholars debate whether tribes are primordial units or products of interaction with states.

Political tribalism

In contemporary politics, tribalism describes partisan or identity-based polarisation in which group loyalty overrides evidence or shared norms. Related concepts include identity politics, nepotism and ethnocentrism.

Criticism

The term is criticised as pejorative when applied to non-Western societies, implying primitiveness.
