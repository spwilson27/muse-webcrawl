GPUOpen

GPUOpen
Developer: AMD
Announced: 15 December 2015
Launched: 26 January 2016
Type: Software development initiative
License: MIT (for most libraries)

GPUOpen is a middleware software suite originally developed by AMD's Radeon Technologies Group that offers advanced visual effects for computer games. It was announced on 15 December 2015 and released on 26 January 2016. GPUOpen provides open-source libraries, tools, and SDKs for graphics and compute development, positioned as an open alternative to proprietary middleware such as NVIDIA GameWorks.

Rationale

AMD stated that GPUOpen was created to bring the console development model — where developers can access hardware at a low level — to the PC, and to make effects code fully open source so that developers could inspect, modify and optimise it for their own titles. Components are hosted publicly, principally on GitHub, under permissive licences.

Components

Notable GPUOpen technologies include FidelityFX, a suite of image-quality effects including FidelityFX Super Resolution (FSR), an open spatial and later temporal upscaling technology; TressFX, a hair and fur rendering library first used in Tomb Raider (2013); geometry and shadow libraries such as GeometryFX and ShadowFX; and tools including the Radeon GPU Profiler and the Compressonator texture-compression tool. GPUOpen also covers sample code and documentation for DirectX 12 and Vulkan development.
