Ijma

Ijma (Arabic: إجماع, ijmāʿ) is an Arabic term for the consensus of Muslim scholars, regarded in Sunni jurisprudence as one of the sources of Islamic law (fiqh), after the Quran and the Sunnah.

Authority

Sunni jurists ground the authority of consensus in Quranic verses and in a hadith stating that the community would not agree upon an error. A ruling established by consensus is considered certain and binding, and cannot be contradicted by later individual reasoning.

Forms

Consensus may be explicit, when every qualified scholar states an opinion, or tacit, when some speak and others remain silent. Jurists differ over whose consensus counts — the Companions of Muhammad, scholars of a city such as Medina, or all scholars of an age.
