Big Woods (Arkansas)

LocationEastern Arkansas, United States
HabitatBottomland hardwood forest and wetlands

The Big Woods of Arkansas is one of the largest remaining tracts of bottomland hardwood forest in the Mississippi Alluvial Plain, centred on the Cache and White river basins of eastern Arkansas.

Ecology

The forest of bald cypress, tupelo and oak, threaded with bayous and sloughs, supports migratory waterfowl, black bears and many bird species. Protected areas include the Cache River and White River National Wildlife Refuges.

Ivory-billed woodpecker

The Big Woods drew worldwide attention in 2004–2005, when a reported rediscovery of the ivory-billed woodpecker — long thought extinct — was announced from the Cache River area, though later searches failed to confirm a surviving population conclusively.
