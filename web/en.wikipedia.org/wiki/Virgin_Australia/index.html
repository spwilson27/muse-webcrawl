Virgin Australia

Virgin Australia is an Australian airline based in Brisbane, Queensland. It is the second-largest domestic airline in Australia after Qantas, operating scheduled services to more than 40 destinations across Australia and a small number of international routes. Its frequent-flyer programme is Velocity Frequent Flyer.

History

The airline was launched in 2000 as Virgin Blue, a low-cost carrier founded by the British Virgin Group with businessman Brett Godfrey, starting with two leased Boeing 737s flying between Brisbane and Sydney. It grew rapidly after the collapse of Ansett Australia in 2001. Under chief executive John Borghetti from 2010, it rebranded as Virgin Australia (2011), moved upmarket to compete directly with Qantas, introduced business class and wide-body services, and acquired the regional carrier Skywest and a stake in Tigerair Australia.

Heavy losses and the COVID-19 pandemic pushed the airline into voluntary administration in April 2020. It was sold to Bain Capital later that year and relaunched as a mid-market "hybrid" carrier focused on domestic routes with a simplified Boeing 737 fleet. In 2025 Qatar Airways acquired a 25 per cent stake, and Virgin Australia returned to the Australian Securities Exchange.
