London King's Cross railway station

King's Cross is a major London railway terminus, opened in 1852 by the Great Northern Railway. It is the southern terminus of the East Coast Main Line to Edinburgh and a gateway to Yorkshire, North East England and Scotland.

King's Cross
Location: Camden, London
Opened: 1852
Architect: Lewis Cubitt
Platforms: 12 (0-11)
Operator: Network Rail

History

Designed by Lewis Cubitt with two great arched train sheds, the station took its name from a former monument to George IV. A major redevelopment completed for the 2012 Olympics added the Western Concourse designed by John McAslan + Partners.

Services and connections

Operators include LNER, Hull Trains, Grand Central and Great Northern / Thameslink. Below is King's Cross St Pancras, the largest interchange on the London Underground. The fictional Platform 9¾ from Harry Potter is marked in the concourse.
