Sextuple

The term sextuple means sixfold — consisting of six parts, multiplied by six, or a group or set of six. It is formed on the same pattern as double (2), triple (3), quadruple (4) and quintuple (5), using the Latin root for six.

Uses

  • Multiple birth: "sextuplets" are six offspring born at one birth. Human sextuplets are extremely rare, historically occurring either spontaneously or in connection with fertility treatment; well-documented sets include the Dilley sextuplets (born 1993 in Indiana, the first surviving set in the United States) and others widely covered in the media.
  • Sport: a "sextuple" in association football denotes a team winning six trophies in a single season or calendar year. FC Barcelona achieved this in 2009 and Bayern Munich in 2020, winning their domestic league, domestic cup, super cup, the UEFA Champions League, the UEFA Super Cup and the FIFA Club World Cup.
  • Music: a sextuplet is a group of six notes played in the time normally occupied by four or another number, a form of tuplet.
  • Mathematics and computing: a sextuple is an ordered list of six elements, a special case of a tuple.
