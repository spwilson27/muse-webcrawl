Black Desert Online

DeveloperPearl Abyss
GenreMMORPG
Released2014 (Korea); 2016 (North America/Europe)
PlatformsWindows, PlayStation, Xbox, mobile (Black Desert Mobile)

Black Desert Online is a sandbox-oriented massively multiplayer online role-playing game developed by Korean studio Pearl Abyss. It is noted for its highly detailed character creator, action-based combat, and seamless open world.

Gameplay

Players choose from many classes and explore a world divided between the republic of Calpheon and the kingdom of Valencia, whose rivalry over "black stones" drives the setting. Combat uses real-time aiming and combos rather than tab-targeting. Beyond combat, the game offers life skills such as fishing, trading, farming, sailing, and horse training, plus large-scale node and siege wars between guilds.

Development and reception

The game launched in South Korea in 2014 and in the West in 2016, and was later remastered with improved graphics and audio. A console version and a separate mobile game followed. It has sold tens of millions of copies and remains Pearl Abyss's flagship title.
