Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant
Location: near Pripyat, Ukraine
Reactors: 4 × RBMK-1000
Status: Decommissioned

The Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant is a decommissioned nuclear power station near the city of Pripyat in northern Ukraine. It consisted of four RBMK-1000 reactors. On 26 April 1986, reactor No. 4 was destroyed in the Chernobyl disaster, the worst accident in the history of nuclear power.

History

Construction began in the 1970s; the first reactor was commissioned in 1977. After the 1986 explosion and fire, a concrete "sarcophagus" was built over the ruined reactor, replaced in 2016–2019 by the New Safe Confinement, a giant steel arch slid into place over the site. The remaining reactors continued operating for years; the last, unit 3, shut down in 2000.

Decommissioning

Decommissioning and waste management at the site are ongoing, with the fuel and contaminated structures being gradually dismantled under international oversight.
