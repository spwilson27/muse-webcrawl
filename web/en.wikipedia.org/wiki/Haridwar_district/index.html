Haridwar district is a district in the state of Uttarakhand in India. Haridwar is the headquarter of the district. It is one of the most populous districts of Uttarakhand and is an important Hindu pilgrimage centre, as the river Ganges emerges here from the Himalayan foothills onto the Indo-Gangetic plain.

Geography

The district covers an area of about 2,360 square kilometres in the southwestern part of Uttarakhand. It is bordered by the districts of Dehradun and Pauri Garhwal and by the state of Uttar Pradesh. The Ganges and its tributaries drain the district, and part of Rajaji National Park lies within its boundaries.

Demographics

According to the 2011 census, Haridwar district has a population of 1,890,422, making it the most populous district of Uttarakhand. Hinduism is the majority religion, followed by Islam, with significant communities of Sikhs and others. Hindi is the most widely spoken language.

Economy and pilgrimage

The cities of Haridwar and Roorkee are the district's principal urban centres. Haridwar hosts the Kumbh Mela, one of the largest religious gatherings in the world, and the annual Kanwar Yatra. Roorkee is known for the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee and for a large industrial estate.
