Farm stay

A farm stay (or farmstay) is any type of accommodation on a working farm. Some farm stays are a form of agritourism in which guests stay in farmhouse rooms or converted barns, cottages, or cabins, and may observe or participate in farm activities such as feeding animals, collecting eggs, gardening, or harvesting produce.

Concept

Farm stays give farmers an additional source of income and give visitors, often urban families, direct experience of rural life. The term is used particularly in Australia and New Zealand, where farm stays are a well-established part of the tourism industry, and in Italy, where farm accommodation is known as agriturismo and is regulated by law. Related forms include ranch stays and dude ranches in North America, and "WWOOFing," in which volunteers exchange labour for food and lodging on organic farms through the organization World Wide Opportunities on Organic Farms.

Activities

Typical farm stay activities include animal feeding and care, horse riding, milking, cheese making, farm tours, and meals prepared from farm produce. Some farm stays are primarily bed-and-breakfast accommodation in a rural setting, while others are working stays in which guests take part in daily chores.
