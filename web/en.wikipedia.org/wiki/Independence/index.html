Independence

Independence
DefinitionCondition of a nation free from external control; self-governance
WaveDecolonisation after 1945 reshaped the world map
[Image: A new national flag being raised at an independence ceremony]

Independence is the condition of a nation, country or state in which its residents exercise self-government, free of external control. Independence is usually won by declaration, war or negotiated transfer — the United States' Declaration (1776, Thomas Jefferson) is the template document.

Decolonisation

After 1945, independence movements ended the European empires in Asia and Africa within a generation, creating most of today's states — and many of its border disputes. Independence days are the commonest national holidays on Earth.
