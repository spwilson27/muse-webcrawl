The World Radiocommunication Conference (WRC) is an international treaty conference organised by the International Telecommunication Union (ITU) to review and, as necessary, revise the Radio Regulations — the international treaty governing the use of the radio-frequency spectrum and the geostationary-satellite and non-geostationary-satellite orbits. Conferences are held every three to four years under the ITU Radiocommunication Sector (ITU-R).

Work of the conference

Delegates from ITU member states negotiate allocations of frequency bands to radiocommunication services — such as mobile broadband (IMT), broadcasting, satellite, aeronautical and maritime services, and amateur radio — and update the technical and procedural rules for their use. Decisions are recorded in the Final Acts of the conference and require consensus among participating administrations, since they amend treaty text.

Recent conferences

WRC-15 was held in Geneva, Switzerland, and WRC-19 in Sharm el-Sheikh, Egypt, identifying additional spectrum for 5G mobile services including millimetre-wave bands. WRC-23 was held in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, in late 2023, addressing among other topics the future use of the upper 6 GHz band and spectrum for satellite and aeronautical services.
