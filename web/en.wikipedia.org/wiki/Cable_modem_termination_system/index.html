Cable modem termination system

A cable modem termination system (CMTS) is a piece of equipment, typically in a cable television company's headend, used to provide high-speed data services, such as cable Internet or Voice over Internet Protocol, to cable subscribers. A CMTS provides many of the same functions provided by the DSLAM in a DSL system.

Function

The CMTS takes traffic from a group of subscribers on one side and routes it to the Internet on the other. It modulates downstream data onto radio-frequency channels of the hybrid fiber-coaxial (HFC) network and demodulates upstream bursts from customers' cable modems, operating under the DOCSIS standard. The CMTS also performs traffic management, encryption, IP address assignment support and quality-of-service scheduling.

Architectures

Traditional integrated CMTS units combine RF and routing functions in one chassis. Modular and distributed architectures (M-CMTS, Remote PHY and Remote MACPHY) move parts of the system toward the network edge to increase capacity, a trend driven by DOCSIS 3.1 and 4.0 upgrades.
