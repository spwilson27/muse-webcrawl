Jonathan Monroe Craig (born 26 March 1986) is a Canadian-American singer known for his soul-influenced vocals in post-hardcore bands, principally as a former lead vocalist of Dance Gavin Dance and Emarosa, and as frontman of Slaves.

Career

Raised partly in Canada and in Washington state, Craig sang in local bands before joining Sacramento's Dance Gavin Dance in 2005, fronting their debut album Whatever I Say Is Royal Ocean (2006) and Downtown Battle Mountain (2007). He left in 2007 and formed Emarosa, recording Relativity (2008) and Emarosa (2010), before being dismissed in 2011. He briefly rejoined Dance Gavin Dance for Downtown Battle Mountain II (2011) and toured with the supergroup Isles & Glaciers. In 2014 he formed the band Slaves, releasing Through Art We Are All Equals and subsequent albums; the group later rebranded as Rain City Drive, with Craig departing in 2024. His solo debut, A Dream Is a Question You Don't Have to Answer, appeared in 2009.

Personal life and controversies

Craig's career has been repeatedly disrupted by struggles with addiction, which he has discussed publicly, and by controversies including a 2011 arrest connected to a social-media scam involving a laptop sale, for which he apologised. Allegations of misconduct also led to festival and tour removals at various points. He has entered treatment on several occasions and has continued to record and perform as a solo artist.
