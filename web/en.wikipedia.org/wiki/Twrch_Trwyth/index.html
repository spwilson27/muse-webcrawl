Twrch Trwyth

Twrch Trwyth (Welsh pronunciation roughly "toorkh troo-ith") is an enchanted boar in Welsh mythology, most famously hunted in the medieval prose tale Culhwch and Olwen. Once a king, he was transformed into a monstrous boar for his sins, with a comb, razor and scissors lodged between his ears — treasures the giant Ysbaddaden demands before his daughter Olwen may marry Culhwch.

SpeciesEnchanted boar
OriginWelsh mythology

The hunt

King Arthur and his war-band pursue Twrch Trwyth from Ireland across South Wales. The chase, a tour of Welsh place-lore, costs many lives; Mabon ap Modron and other heroes aid the hunt. At the River Severn the treasures are snatched from between the boar's ears, and Twrch Trwyth is driven into the sea toward Cornwall, never to be seen again.

Significance

The episode is among the oldest Arthurian material, likely reflecting older Celtic boar-hunt myths comparable to Irish and Norse traditions. Twrch Trwyth's offspring, seven young boars, accompany him.
