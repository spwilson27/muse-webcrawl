MusicBrainz

TypeOnline music encyclopedia
Launched2000
OperatorMetaBrainz Foundation

MusicBrainz is a project that aims to create a collaborative music database and encyclopedia. Founded by Robert Kaye in 2000, it collects music metadata — information about artists, recordings, releases and the relationships between them — and makes it available to the public under open licenses.

Overview

MusicBrainz data is entered and maintained by a global community of volunteer editors. Each entity in the database, such as an artist, release group, release, recording or work, is assigned a permanent MusicBrainz Identifier (MBID). The database covers millions of artists and releases, and its data is used by music tagging software, streaming services and other projects.

Software and related projects

The MetaBrainz Foundation, the non-profit organisation behind MusicBrainz, develops the Picard tagger, which uses acoustic fingerprinting to identify audio files and apply MusicBrainz metadata. Related MetaBrainz projects include ListenBrainz, which records users' listening history, and BookBrainz, a similar database for literary works.
