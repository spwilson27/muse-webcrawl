Hard to Be a God (Russian: Трудно быть богом) is a 1964 science fiction novel by Soviet writers Arkady and Boris Strugatsky, set in their Noon Universe. It is one of the most famous works of Soviet science fiction.

Plot

Anton, an operative from a future utopian Earth, is sent undercover as the nobleman Don Rumata to a medieval kingdom on another planet. Earth observers are forbidden to interfere in the planet's bloody history, and Rumata must watch a rising reign of terror crush learning and culture while unable to act openly — the burden of being, in effect, a god who cannot intervene.

Adaptations

The novel has been adapted to film twice: a 1989 West German–Soviet production directed by Peter Fleischmann, and Aleksei German's long-delayed version, completed shortly before his death and released in 2013 to critical acclaim. A 2008 video game was also based on the novel.
