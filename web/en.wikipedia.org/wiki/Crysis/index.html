Crysis

Crysis is a first-person shooter video game developed by Crytek and published by Electronic Arts, released for Microsoft Windows in November 2007. It is the first game in the Crysis series. The game is based in a future where a massive ancient alien structure has been discovered beneath a mountain on an island near the Philippines, and the player controls a special-forces soldier equipped with an advanced "Nanosuit".

Gameplay

The player takes the role of Jake Dunn ("Nomad"), a US Army Delta Force soldier sent to the fictional Lingshan Islands in the East Philippine Sea after North Korean forces seize the area where archaeologists have uncovered an alien artifact. The Nanosuit provides four modes — armor, strength, speed and cloak — that allow players to approach sandbox-style encounters in different ways. Open-ended level design lets players combine stealth, vehicle use and direct assault.

Development and reception

Built on CryEngine 2, Crysis was widely praised for its graphics, which far exceeded most contemporary games and made it a benchmark for PC hardware, giving rise to the phrase "But can it run Crysis?" It received critical acclaim for its visuals, gameplay flexibility and physics, though its system requirements were notoriously demanding and its later alien-focused levels more divisive. It sold over three million copies and spawned the standalone expansion Crysis Warhead (2008), sequels Crysis 2 (2011) and Crysis 3 (2013), and a remastered edition in 2020.
