Amazon Prime Video

Type: Video on demand streaming service
Owner: Amazon
Launched: September 7, 2006 (as Amazon Unbox)
Headquarters: Seattle, Washington, U.S.

Amazon Prime Video, commonly known as Prime Video, is an American subscription video on-demand streaming service owned by Amazon. It offers television series and films for rent or purchase, and original content produced by Amazon MGM Studios, as a standalone service or as part of the Amazon Prime subscription.

History

The service launched in 2006 as Amazon Unbox, a download service, and later became Amazon Video on Demand and then Amazon Instant Video. In 2011 it was bundled with Amazon Prime memberships in the United States. The service expanded internationally in 2016, launching in most countries worldwide, and adopted the Prime Video name.

Original programming

Prime Video's original series include Transparent, The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel, The Boys, Fleabag (in some territories), Invincible, Reacher, Fallout and the high-budget fantasy series The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power. The service also holds sports rights, notably Thursday Night Football of the NFL in the United States.

Features

Prime Video supports add-on "Channels" for third-party subscription services, rentals and purchases through its store, and 4K HDR streaming on supported devices. An advertising-supported tier was introduced for subscribers in 2024, with an ad-free option for an additional fee.
