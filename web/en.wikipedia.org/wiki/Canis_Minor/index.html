Canis Minor

Canis Minor is a small constellation in the northern celestial hemisphere. Its name is Latin for 'lesser dog', in contrast to Canis Major, the 'greater dog'; both are traditionally pictured as following Orion the hunter. It was one of Ptolemy's 48 constellations.

Stars

Canis Minor has only two bright stars. Procyon, the eighth-brightest star in the night sky, is a binary system 11.5 light-years away; its name means 'before the dog'. The other, Gomeisa (Beta), is a blue-white star about 162 light-years away.

Mythology

Greek myth variously identified the lesser dog with Maera, dog of Icarius, or with the Teumessian fox. In other cultures Procyon formed part of different asterisms.

Deep-sky objects

The constellation lies away from the galactic plane and has few notable deep-sky objects, mainly faint galaxies.
