Munster Koach

The Munster Koach is the family car in the 1960s television sitcom The Munsters. Built by the noted customiser George Barris, the Koach was constructed from three Ford Model T bodies, stretched to a length of about 18 feet, and finished as a blend of hot rod, hearse and Victorian carriage, with brass radiator and lamps, blood-red velvet interior and a powerful Ford V8 engine.

History

Producer Tom Daniel is credited with the original design sketch. Barris' shop built the car in roughly three weeks for the show's 1964 debut. It featured in more than twenty episodes and in the feature film Munster, Go Home! (1966). In the series' fiction, the Koach was assembled by Herman Munster from parts at the "Komoko Body Shop" — a hearse, a hot rod and assorted carriage hardware.

DRAG-U-LA

The Koach's famous stablemate is DRAG-U-LA, a dragster built from a real coffin, created for Grandpa in the episode "Hot Rod Herman" and later displayed alongside the Koach. The original Koach survives in museum and private collections and remains one of the most recognisable television cars ever built.
