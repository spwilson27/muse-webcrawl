Schengen Information System

TypeEU law-enforcement and border database
Operatoreu-LISA
Current versionSIS (renewed 2023)

The Schengen Information System (SIS) is a governmental database maintained by the European Commission and operated by the agency eu-LISA, used by Schengen countries to maintain and distribute information on individuals and property of interest for border control, law enforcement, and public security.

Contents

The system holds alerts on wanted or missing persons, persons sought for judicial procedures, third-country nationals to be refused entry or stay, and objects such as stolen vehicles, firearms, and identity documents. The second-generation SIS II, launched in 2013, added biometric data such as fingerprints and photographs; a renewed SIS entered operation in 2023 with expanded alert categories.

Access

Competent national authorities — police, border guards, customs, and visa and vehicle-registration authorities — can query the system, with data protection supervision by national authorities and the European Data Protection Supervisor.
