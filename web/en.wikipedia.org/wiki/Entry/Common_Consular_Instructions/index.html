Common Consular Instructions

TypeSchengen visa policy instrument
Superseded byEU Visa Code (2010)

The Common Consular Instructions (CCI) were instructions addressed to the diplomatic missions and consular posts of the Schengen states, setting out common procedures for examining visa applications and issuing uniform Schengen visas.

Content

The Instructions specified the types of visas (airport transit, transit, short-stay, and limited territorial validity visas), the documents applicants had to present, the consultation procedures between member states, and the format of the uniform visa sticker.

Replacement

With the entry into force of the EU Visa Code in 2010, the Common Consular Instructions were repealed and their content largely incorporated into the directly applicable regulation, increasing transparency for applicants.
