The European Border and Coast Guard Agency, commonly known as Frontex, is an agency of the European Union headquartered in Warsaw, Poland. It coordinates border control efforts across the EU's external borders and, since reforms in 2016 and 2019, operates a standing corps of border guards.

History

Frontex was established in 2004 as the European Agency for the Management of Operational Cooperation at the External Borders. Its mandate expanded substantially during and after the 2015 European migrant crisis; the 2019 regulation provided for a standing corps of 10,000 operational staff and a greatly increased budget.

Activities

The agency conducts risk analysis, coordinates joint operations and rapid border interventions, supports return operations of third-country nationals, and works with neighbouring non-EU countries. It has faced criticism and legal challenges over alleged involvement in pushbacks of migrants at sea, which it denies.
