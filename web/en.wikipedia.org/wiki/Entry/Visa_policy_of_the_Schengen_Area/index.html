Visa policy of the Schengen Area

Governed byEU Visa Code (Regulation (EC) No 810/2009)
Uniform visaShort-stay, up to 90 days in any 180-day period

The visa policy of the Schengen Area is an element within the wider area of freedom, security and justice policy of the European Union. It applies to the Schengen Area and to other EU member states except Ireland. The uniform Schengen visa allows its holder to travel throughout the Schengen Area for short stays.

Visa exemption and requirement

Nationals of countries listed in Annex II of the EU visa regulation may enter visa-free for up to 90 days in any 180-day period, while nationals of Annex I countries must obtain a visa in advance from the consulate of their main destination country. The Schengen visa is issued as a uniform sticker and may be valid for one, two, or multiple entries.

Visa Code

Procedures are set by the EU Visa Code, in force since 2010, which harmonised application forms, fees, processing times, and appeal rights. Long-stay visas and residence permits remain matters of national law.
