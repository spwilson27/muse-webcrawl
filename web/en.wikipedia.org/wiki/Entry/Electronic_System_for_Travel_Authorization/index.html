Electronic System for Travel Authorization

AgencyU.S. Customs and Border Protection
Launched2008 (mandatory 2009)
Applies toVisa Waiver Program travellers

The Electronic System for Travel Authorization (ESTA) is an automated system that determines the eligibility of visitors to travel to the United States under the Visa Waiver Program. It is operated by U.S. Customs and Border Protection, part of the Department of Homeland Security.

Operation

Travellers from Visa Waiver Program countries must submit an online application, providing biographical and passport information, at least 72 hours before travel. An approved ESTA is generally valid for two years or until the passport expires, and permits multiple entries for stays of up to 90 days. The system cross-checks applicant data against law-enforcement databases.

Notes

An approved ESTA is not a visa and does not guarantee admission; the final decision rests with the border officer on arrival.
