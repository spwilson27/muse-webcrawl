Entry/Eu-LISA

In the context of the European Union's Entry/Exit System (EES), eu-LISA — the European Union Agency for the Operational Management of Large-Scale IT Systems in the Area of Freedom, Security and Justice — is the agency responsible for developing and operating the central information technology systems behind the Union's border management, including the entry and exit recording of third-country nationals crossing the external borders of the Schengen Area.

Role in the entry systems

eu-LISA manages the large-scale databases underpinning EU home affairs: the second-generation Schengen Information System (SIS II), the Visa Information System (VIS) and the Eurodac asylum fingerprint database. Its mandate was expanded to develop and operate the Entry/Exit System established by Regulation (EU) 2017/2226, which electronically records the name, travel document and biometric data (facial image and fingerprints) of third-country nationals entering and leaving the Schengen Area, replacing manual passport stamping.

The agency is likewise responsible for the technical development of the European Travel Information and Authorisation System (ETIAS) and for interoperability components linking the systems, so that border guards and law-enforcement authorities can query several databases through a single European search portal.

Organisation

eu-LISA was established by Regulation (EU) No 1077/2011 and became operational on 1 December 2012. Its seat is in Tallinn, Estonia; its operational and technical centre is in Strasbourg, France, with a backup site in Sankt Johann im Pongau, Austria.
