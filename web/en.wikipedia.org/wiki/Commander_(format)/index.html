Commander is a series of supplementary product lines and a casual multiplayer format for the collectible card game Magic: The Gathering. In Commander, each player builds a 100-card singleton deck (no duplicates except basic lands) around a legendary creature or planeswalker — the "commander" — which sits in a special zone, the command zone, and can be cast repeatedly at increasing cost.

Rules

All cards in a deck must match the commander's colour identity. Players start with 40 life, draw seven-card hands with generous mulligans, and usually play in free-for-all pods of four. A player who takes 21 or more combat damage from a single commander loses. The commander "tax" adds {2} to its casting cost for each previous cast from the command zone. Games are typically long, social and political, with emphasis on spectacle and self-expression over competition.

History

The format originated as "Elder Dragon Highlander", a fan format created by judges in Alaska and popularized by Sheldon Menery and the Commander Rules Committee. Wizards of the Coast officially adopted it in 2011 with the release of preconstructed Commander decks, which became annual products. Commander grew to become by many measures the most popular way to play Magic, spawning dedicated products, the spin-off Brawl format, and a vast deck-building culture. In 2024, management of the format was transferred from the independent Rules Committee to Wizards of the Coast, followed by the introduction of a bracket system for matching deck power levels in casual play.
