Economy of Zimbabwe

CurrencyZiG (from 2024); US dollar widely used
Main sectorsAgriculture, mining, tourism

The economy of Zimbabwe is based on agriculture (tobacco, maize, cotton), mining (gold, platinum, lithium, chrome, diamonds), manufacturing and tourism (Victoria Falls).

History

At independence in 1980 Zimbabwe had a diversified economy and was a regional breadbasket. ESAP structural adjustment in the 1990s, fast-track land reform from 2000 under ZANU–PF, fiscal deficits and money printing produced economic collapse and hyperinflation peaking in 2008, abandonment of the Zimbabwe dollar and dollarisation in 2009.

Recent

Subsequent currencies (bond notes, RTGS dollar, ZiG gold-backed unit launched 2024), power shortages, informalisation and remittances shape the economy; lithium and gold mining have grown.
