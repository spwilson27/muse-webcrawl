Wilma Flintstone

First appearance: "The Flagstones" pilot (1959) / The Flintstones (1960)
Voiced by: Jean Vander Pyl (original series)
Spouse: Fred Flintstone
Children: Pebbles Flintstone

Wilma Flintstone (née Slaghoople, though her maiden name is given variously in the series) is a fictional character in the Hanna-Barbera animated television series The Flintstones (1960–1966). She is the red-haired wife of Fred Flintstone and mother of Pebbles, living in the Stone Age town of Bedrock.

Character

Wilma is typically portrayed as the sensible, level-headed partner in the Flintstone household, frequently rescuing Fred from the consequences of his schemes alongside her friend and neighbour Betty Rubble. She wears a white one-shouldered tunic and a necklace of round stones, with her red hair in a bun. Her household runs on animal-powered Stone Age "appliances", a running gag of the series.

Appearances

Voiced by Jean Vander Pyl in the original series, Wilma appeared in subsequent Flintstones spin-offs, specials, and the 1966 film The Man Called Flintstone; she was played by Rosie O'Donnell in the 1994 live-action film The Flintstones and by Kristen Wiig (voice) in later productions.

Cultural impact

As one of the first animated sitcom wives, Wilma became an enduring pop-culture figure, appearing in advertising, merchandise, and references across decades of television.
