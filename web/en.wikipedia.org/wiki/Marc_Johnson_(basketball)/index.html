Marc Johnson (basketball)

Born: November 15, 1979, Hempstead, New York
Nationality: American
Listed height: 6 ft 6 in (1.98 m)
Position: Guard / forward
College: UConn

Marc Johnson (born November 15, 1979) is an American former professional basketball player. He played college basketball at the University of Connecticut (UConn), where he was a member of the Huskies team that won the 1999 NCAA championship.

Career

Johnson attended St. Dominic High School on Long Island before joining UConn. Though a reserve for much of his college career, he contributed to the Huskies' 1999 title run under coach Jim Calhoun. After college he pursued a professional career overseas, playing in leagues in Europe, before moving into coaching and player development work.
