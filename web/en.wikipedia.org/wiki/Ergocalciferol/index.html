Ergocalciferol

Other names: Vitamin D2
Formula: C28H44O
Molar mass: 396.66 g/mol
Source: Ergosterol (fungal/plant)

Ergocalciferol, also known as vitamin D2 and nonspecifically as calciferol, is a type of vitamin D found in food and used as a dietary supplement. As a supplement it is used to prevent and treat vitamin D deficiency, including rickets. It is also used for familial hypophosphatemia, hypoparathyroidism, and Fanconi syndrome. Ergocalciferol is produced by ultraviolet irradiation of ergosterol, a sterol found in fungi and some plants, and is commonly used to fortify foods.

Comparison with vitamin D3

Vitamin D supplements such as ergocalciferol (D2) and cholecalciferol (D3) both raise blood levels of calcifediol, the main circulating form of vitamin D. Evidence suggests D3 may raise levels somewhat more effectively or for longer at equal doses, though the clinical significance of the difference is debated, and major guidelines treat both as acceptable for correcting deficiency.

Safety

Excessive doses over long periods can cause hypercalcaemia, with symptoms including nausea, weakness, and kidney problems. Ergocalciferol is on the World Health Organization's List of Essential Medicines.
