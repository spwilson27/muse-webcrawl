Larry Kehres

Larry Kehres (born September 7, 1949) is a former American football coach and college athletics administrator. He served as the head football coach at the University of Mount Union in Alliance, Ohio, from 1986 to 2012, compiling a record of 332–24–3 and winning eleven NCAA Division III national championships, the most by any head coach in college football at the time of his retirement.

Career

A graduate of Mount Union, where he played quarterback, Kehres became the Purple Raiders' head coach in 1986 and built the programme into the dominant force in Division III: his teams won the Stagg Bowl in 1993, 1996, 1997, 1998, 2000, 2001, 2002, 2005, 2006, 2008 and 2012, and mounted winning streaks of 54 and 55 games. His career winning percentage of .929 is among the highest in college football history. Kehres stepped down as head coach after the 2012 season — handing the job to his son, Vince Kehres — and continued as Mount Union's athletic director until 2020. He was inducted into the College Football Hall of Fame in 2017.
