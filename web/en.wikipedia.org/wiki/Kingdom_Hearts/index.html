Kingdom Hearts

Developer: Square Enix

Director: Tetsuya Nomura

First release: Kingdom Hearts, 2002

Genre: Action role-playing

Kingdom Hearts is a series of action role-playing games developed and published by Square Enix, created in collaboration with Disney. The series is a crossover between Square Enix's Final Fantasy franchise and Disney animated properties, following the young hero Sora as he travels between worlds drawn from Disney films, accompanied by Donald Duck and Goofy.

Gameplay and story

Players control Sora, wielder of the Keyblade — a key-shaped weapon able to unlock any lock and combat the darkness. The games combine real-time action combat with role-playing elements and visits to worlds based on films such as Aladdin, The Lion King, Hercules and Pirates of the Caribbean. Recurring antagonists include the Heartless, the Nobodies and Organization XIII, and the saga's central villain Xehanort. The main entries are Kingdom Hearts (2002), Kingdom Hearts II (2005) and Kingdom Hearts III (2019), connected by numerous spin-off and remastered titles.

Music and reception

The series' music was composed principally by Yoko Shimomura, with theme songs "Hikari"/"Simple and Clean", "Passion"/"Sanctuary" and "Don't Think Twice" by Hikaru Utada. Known for its intricate — and often criticised as convoluted — storyline, the franchise has sold more than 38 million copies worldwide and is among the most successful crossover series in gaming.
