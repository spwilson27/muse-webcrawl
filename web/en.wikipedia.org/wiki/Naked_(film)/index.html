Naked (film)

Naked is a 1993 British black comedy-drama film written and directed by Mike Leigh, starring David Thewlis as Johnny, a homeless, unemployed drifter who flees Manchester for London and embarks on a nocturnal odyssey through the city.

Plot

After stealing a car, Johnny stays with his ex-girlfriend Louise and her flatmate Sophie, then wanders London's streets, engaging strangers — a night security guard, a homeless couple, a poster girl — in bleak, philosophical encounters, while the landlord Jeremy pursues his own predatory course.

Reception

Thewlis won the Best Actor award at the Cannes Film Festival and Leigh won Best Director. The film is widely regarded as one of the finest British films of the 1990s.
