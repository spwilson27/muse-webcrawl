List of Florida state parks

The Florida State Parks system, administered by the Florida Department of Environmental Protection's Division of Recreation and Parks, comprises more than 170 state parks, trails, and historic sites covering over 800,000 acres. Florida's was the first state park system to win the National Recreation and Park Association's Gold Medal for excellence three times, and it has since won a fourth. The first parks were established in 1935.

Selected parks

  • Bahia Honda State Park — in the Florida Keys, noted for its beaches.
  • Blue Spring State Park — a winter refuge for manatees on the St. Johns River.
  • Edward Ball Wakulla Springs State Park — home of one of the world's largest and deepest freshwater springs.
  • Florida Caverns State Park — the only Florida state park with air-filled cave tours.
  • Fort Foster State Historic Site and Fort Mose Historic State Park — historic fort sites.
  • Gasparilla Island State Park, Honeymoon Island State Park, Caladesi Island State Park — Gulf Coast barrier island parks.
  • Hugh Taylor Birch State Park — urban park in Fort Lauderdale.
  • Ichetucknee Springs State Park — famous for tubing on the Ichetucknee River.
  • John Pennekamp Coral Reef State Park — the first undersea park in the United States.
  • Jonathan Dickinson State Park — the largest state park in southeast Florida.
  • Little Talbot Island State Park and Anastasia State Park — Atlantic coast parks near Jacksonville and St. Augustine.
  • Manatee Springs State Park, Fanning Springs State Park, Rainbow Springs State Park — first-magnitude springs parks.
  • Myakka River State Park — one of Florida's oldest and largest parks.
  • Oleta River State Park — the state's largest urban park, in Miami.
  • Ravine Gardens State Park — a ravine created by water flowing from the St. Johns River.
  • Silver Springs State Park — historic attraction with glass-bottom boats.
  • St. Andrews State Park, Topsail Hill Preserve State Park, Grayton Beach State Park — Panhandle beach parks.
  • Weeki Wachee Springs State Park — known for its underwater mermaid performances.

The full list is organised alphabetically and includes state preserves, recreation areas, botanical sites, historic sites, and the state's rail trails and greenways, such as the Marjorie Harris Carr Cross Florida Greenway.
