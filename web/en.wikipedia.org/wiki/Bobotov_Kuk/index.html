Bobotov Kuk

Bobotov Kuk (Montenegrin: Боботов Кук) is a peak in northern Montenegro, rising to 2,523 metres (8,278 ft). It is the highest summit of the Durmitor mountain range and was long considered the highest point in Montenegro, until surveys showed Zla Kolata on the Albanian border to be slightly higher.

Geography

Bobotov Kuk lies within Durmitor National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site near the town of Žabljak. The peak overlooks glacial lakes known locally as "mountain eyes", including Crno Jezero (Black Lake), and commands views across the Tara River Canyon. Its slopes are predominantly limestone, sculpted by glaciation.

Climbing

The summit is a popular mountaineering objective, usually approached from Žabljak or from the Sedlo pass. The standard route is a marked hiking and scrambling ascent requiring no technical climbing in summer conditions, though snow can persist into early summer.
