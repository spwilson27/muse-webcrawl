Akashi (明石市, Akashi-shi) is a city in southern Hyōgo Prefecture, Japan, on the coast of the Seto Inland Sea facing Awaji Island. It is known for the Akashi Kaikyō Bridge — the longest suspension bridge in the world at its completion — which links the city to Awaji Island, and for Akashiyaki, a local dumpling dish regarded as a forerunner of takoyaki.

Geography and landmarks

The Japan Standard Time Meridian at 135° east longitude passes through the city, marked by the Akashi Municipal Planetarium, the oldest operating planetarium in Japan. The ruins of Akashi Castle, built in 1619, stand in Akashi Park.

History

Akashi developed as a castle town and port. The modern city was incorporated in 1919. The Great Hanshin earthquake of 1995, whose epicentre lay in the Akashi Strait, caused damage in the region.
