Courtauld Gallery

The Courtauld Gallery is an art museum in Somerset House, on the Strand in London. It houses the art collection of the Courtauld Institute of Art, a self-governing college of the University of London specialising in the history of art. The collection is especially famous for its Impressionist and Post-Impressionist paintings.

Collection

Textile magnate Samuel Courtauld assembled much of the core collection and founded the Institute in 1932. Highlights include Manet's A Bar at the Folies-Bergère, Cézanne's The Card Players and Lac d'Annecy, Van Gogh's Self-Portrait with Bandaged Ear, major works by Renoir, Degas, Monet, Gauguin and Seurat, and Roger Fry's bequest of early 20th-century British art. The Princes Gate Collection added Old Master works, including paintings by Rubens and Botticelli. The gallery reopened in 2021 after a major redevelopment, "Courtauld Connects," which redisplayed the collection and restored the building's Great Room.
