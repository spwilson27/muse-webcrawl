Bus lane

A bus lane, or bus-only lane, is a traffic lane restricted to buses (and often bicycles, taxis or emergency vehicles) on certain days and times, with the intention of moving more people more efficiently and speeding up public transport that would otherwise be held up by traffic congestion.

Types

Bus lanes may operate full-time or only during peak hours, with-flow or contraflow to other traffic. They are often marked with special paint (commonly red), road studs, signs and signals, and may be enforced by cameras. A fully separated running way for buses is termed a busway, as used in bus rapid transit systems.

Effectiveness

Studies generally find bus lanes improve bus journey times and reliability, and can increase ridership, though benefits depend on enforcement and whether general traffic capacity removed is offset by modal shift.
