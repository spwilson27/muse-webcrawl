Eurovision Song Contest 2009

Eurovision Song Contest 2009
Dates12, 14 and 16 May 2009
Host cityMoscow, Russia
VenueOlimpiyskiy Indoor Arena
WinnerNorway — "Fairytale", Alexander Rybak
Participants42 countries

The Eurovision Song Contest 2009 was the 54th edition of the contest, held in Moscow, Russia, following Dima Bilan's victory in 2008 with "Believe". Forty-two countries participated.

Result

Norway's Alexander Rybak won with the song "Fairytale", scoring 387 points — then the highest total under that voting system — with a record margin over runner-up Iceland ("Is It True?" by Yohanna). Azerbaijan finished third. Rybak's song became a major European hit.

Changes

2009 saw the reintroduction of national juries in the final, combined 50/50 with televoting, in response to concerns about bloc and diaspora voting. Georgia withdrew after its entry was judged political, and Slovakia returned after an 11-year absence.
