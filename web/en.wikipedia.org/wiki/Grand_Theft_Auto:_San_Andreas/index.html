Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas

Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas is a 2004 action-adventure game developed by Rockstar North and published by Rockstar Games. It is the fifth main entry in the Grand Theft Auto series, following Grand Theft Auto: Vice City (2002), and was released for PlayStation 2 in October 2004, with later versions for Windows, Xbox, mobile platforms, and others.

Gameplay and setting

The game is set in the fictional state of San Andreas, based on California and Nevada, encompassing the cities of Los Santos (Los Angeles), San Fierro (San Francisco), and Las Venturas (Las Vegas), together with countryside and desert. The player controls Carl "CJ" Johnson, who returns home in 1992 and becomes involved in gang warfare and a corrupt police conspiracy. The open world allows driving, flying, swimming, and a wide range of side activities, and introduced character customisation of body type, clothing, and skills.

Reception and legacy

San Andreas received universal acclaim for its scale, story, and music, and became the best-selling PlayStation 2 game of all time, with over 27.5 million copies sold across all platforms. It was at the centre of the "Hot Coffee" controversy in 2005, when a disabled sex minigame was found in the game files, prompting a temporary Adults Only rating and re-releases with the content removed. A remastered version was included in Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy – The Definitive Edition (2021).
