Singapore Prison Service

The Singapore Prison Service (SPS) is a government agency under Singapore's Ministry of Home Affairs responsible for the custody, rehabilitation and reintegration of offenders. Its officers are known by the service's motto as "Captains of Lives".

History and structure

Prisons in Singapore trace their origins to the colonial era; the historic Changi Prison was built in 1936. Most of the country's penal institutions are now consolidated in the Changi Prison Complex, which opened in stages from 2000.

Rehabilitation

SPS works with the Yellow Ribbon Project and community partners to support inmates' education, work skills and aftercare, aiming to reduce reoffending. Singapore retains capital punishment and judicial caning for certain offences, which prison officers administer under law.
