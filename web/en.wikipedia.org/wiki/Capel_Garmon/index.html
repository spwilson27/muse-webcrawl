Capel Garmon

Capel Garmon is a village and community in Conwy County Borough, north Wales. It lies in the Conwy valley near Betws-y-Coed and Llanrwst, in a rural area of farmland and woodland below the Carneddau mountains.

History

The village is known for the Capel Garmon burial chamber, a Neolithic chambered tomb dating to around 3000 BC, and for the Capel Garmon Firedog, an ornate Iron Age firedog discovered in 1852 and now held by Amgueddfa Cymru – Museum Wales. The firedog, decorated with bull's-head terminals, is considered a masterpiece of Celtic metalwork.

Geography

Capel Garmon stands on elevated ground east of the River Conwy. The parish church is dedicated to St Garmon. The surrounding landscape includes the Fairy Glen gorge on the nearby River Conwy.
