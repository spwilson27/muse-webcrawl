Weta Workshop

Wētā Workshop
Founded: 1987 (as RT Effects)
Founders: Richard Taylor and Tania Rodger
Headquarters: Miramar, Wellington, New Zealand
Industry: special effects, props, design

Weta Workshop (stylised Wētā Workshop) is a New Zealand special effects and prop company based in Miramar, Wellington. Founded in 1987 by Richard Taylor and Tania Rodger as RT Effects, it gained worldwide fame for its work on Peter Jackson's The Lord of the Rings film trilogy.

Work

The company designs and fabricates creatures, prosthetics, armour, weapons, miniatures ("bigatures"), costumes and animatronics. For The Lord of the Rings (2001–2003) it produced tens of thousands of handcrafted items, sharing Academy Awards for makeup and visual effects. Major credits include King Kong (2005), The Chronicles of Narnia, Avatar (2009) and its sequels, District 9, The Hobbit trilogy, Mad Max: Fury Road and Blade Runner 2049. Its sister company Weta FX (formerly Weta Digital) handles digital effects.

Other activities

Weta Workshop also produces collectible statues, children's television such as Thunderbirds Are Go, museum exhibitions — including the scale Gallipoli exhibition at Te Papa with its celebrated giant figures — and the Weta Cave visitor attraction in Miramar.
