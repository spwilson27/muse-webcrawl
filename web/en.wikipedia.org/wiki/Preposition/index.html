Preposition

A preposition is a word or group of words used before a noun, pronoun, or noun phrase to show direction, time, place, location, spatial relationships, or to introduce an object. Examples in English include in, on, at, by, with, under, and between. Together with its object, a preposition forms a prepositional phrase, which usually functions as an adjective or adverb.

Types

Prepositions may be single words (simple prepositions) or multiword expressions (complex prepositions) such as in front of and because of. In many languages, prepositions govern a particular grammatical case of their object.

Postpositions and other adpositions

Languages in which the relational word follows its object, such as Japanese and Turkish, are said to use postpositions. Prepositions and postpositions are grouped together as adpositions.

Usage questions

Traditional prescriptive grammar discouraged ending a sentence with a preposition in English, though the construction is natural and widely attested; modern usage guides generally accept it.
