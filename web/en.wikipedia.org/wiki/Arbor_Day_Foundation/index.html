The Arbor Day Foundation is an American nonprofit membership organisation dedicated to planting trees. Founded in 1972 — the centennial of the first Arbor Day, the tree-planting holiday created in Nebraska by J. Sterling Morton in 1872 — it is among the largest nonprofit conservation groups devoted to trees.

Programmes

The Foundation runs the Tree City USA programme (with the US Forest Service and state foresters), recognising communities that maintain urban forestry standards, as well as Tree Campus USA for universities, large-scale reforestation partnerships in national forests, and the Rain Forest Rescue programme. It distributes millions of trees to members and partners annually and promotes Arbor Day observance, held nationally on the last Friday in April.

Arbor Day Farm

Its headquarters campus near Nebraska City includes Arbor Day Farm and the historic Arbor Lodge, Morton's estate, operated as educational attractions.
