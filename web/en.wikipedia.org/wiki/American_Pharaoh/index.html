American Pharoah (foaled 2 February 2012) is an American Thoroughbred racehorse who became the 12th winner of the American Triple Crown in 2015, the first since Affirmed in 1978, ending a 37-year drought. Trained by Bob Baffert and ridden by Victor Espinoza in his Triple Crown races, he was owned and bred by Zayat Stables. (His name contains a misspelling of "Pharaoh" that resulted from a naming contest entry.)

Racing career

American Pharoah was the champion two-year-old colt of 2014. In 2015 he won the Kentucky Derby and the Preakness Stakes (in a downpour) before winning the Belmont Stakes wire-to-wire to complete the Triple Crown. After a shock defeat by Keen Ice in the Travers Stakes, he ended his career by winning the Breeders' Cup Classic at Keeneland, becoming the first horse to complete the "Grand Slam" of the Triple Crown plus the Classic.

Stud career

He was retired to Coolmore's Ashford Stud in Kentucky, where his breeding rights had been sold for a reported sum in the tens of millions of dollars. He has sired stakes winners in North America, Europe, Japan and Australia.
