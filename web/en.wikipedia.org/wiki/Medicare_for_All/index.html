Medicare for All

Medicare for All is a proposal to expand the United States Medicare program to cover all residents under a single-payer national health insurance system, replacing most private insurance.

Proposals

Bills introduced by Senator Bernie Sanders and Representatives Pramila Jayapal and Debbie Dingell would provide comprehensive coverage — including dental, vision, hearing, and long-term care — without premiums, deductibles, or copayments, funded through new taxes.

Debate

Supporters cite lower administrative costs and universal coverage; critics cite the large increase in federal spending, disruption to employer-based insurance, and tax increases. Polling and cost estimates (e.g., from the Mercatus Center and CBO discussions) are widely contested.
