Shopaholic

Shopaholic is an informal term for a person with a compulsive tendency to shop, often linked to oniomania (compulsive buying disorder), a behavioural pattern characterised by an irresistible urge to purchase items, frequently beyond need or means, followed by feelings of guilt or distress.

In popular culture

The word was popularised by Sophie Kinsella's bestselling Shopaholic novel series, beginning with The Secret Dreamworld of a Shopaholic (2000), whose heroine Rebecca Bloomwood struggles with shopping-driven debt. The series was adapted into the 2009 film Confessions of a Shopaholic.

Treatment

Compulsive buying is commonly addressed through cognitive behavioural therapy, financial counselling, and support groups such as Debtors Anonymous.
