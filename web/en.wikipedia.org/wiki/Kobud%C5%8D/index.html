Kobudō

Kobudō (古武道, "old martial way") is a collective term for Japanese traditional techniques for the use of armour, blades, firearms, and techniques related to combat and horse riding. In modern usage it often refers to ancient martial arts (koryū), and in Okinawa specifically to the weapons systems of Okinawan martial arts.

Okinawan kobudō

Okinawan kobudō is a Japanese martial art known for its weapons training, using implements derived from farming and fishing tools such as the bō (staff), sai, tonfa, nunchaku, and kama. It developed under the weapons restrictions imposed on Okinawa and is frequently practised alongside karate.
