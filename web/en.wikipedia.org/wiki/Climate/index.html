Climate

Climate
DefinitionLong-term pattern of weather in an area (30+ years typical)
SystemsTropical, dry, temperate, continental, polar
[Image: A world map of climate zones in bands of colour]

climate is the long-term pattern of temperature, humidity, wind and precipitation in a region, averaged over decades — as the saying goes, climate is what you expect, weather is what you get. The Köppen system classifies climates from tropical rainforest to polar ice cap.

Climate change

Earth's climate shifts naturally (ice ages) and is now changing rapidly from human greenhouse gas emissions — the subject of the IPCC reports and the sustainability response. Regional climates determine farming (which of Rice, Wheat, Corn grows where), architecture and disease ranges.
