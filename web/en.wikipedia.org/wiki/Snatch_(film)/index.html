Snatch (film)

Directed by: Guy Ritchie
Written by: Guy Ritchie
Starring: Jason Statham, Brad Pitt, Stephen Graham, Alan Ford, Benicio del Toro, Dennis Farina, Vinnie Jones
Released: 2000
Running time: 104 minutes

Snatch is a 2000 British crime comedy film written and directed by Guy Ritchie, featuring an ensemble cast. Set in the London criminal underworld, the film interweaves two plots: the hunt for a stolen 84-carat diamond and the misadventures of small-time boxing promoters drawn into the world of a ruthless crime boss.

Plot

Franky "Four-Fingers" (Benicio del Toro), a jewel thief with a gambling addiction, steals a huge diamond in Antwerp and brings it to London, intending to sell it through Doug "The Head" Denovitz to New York jeweller "Cousin Avi" (Dennis Farina). Meanwhile, unlicensed boxing promoters Turkish (Jason Statham) and Tommy (Stephen Graham) fall foul of gangster Brick Top (Alan Ford) after their fighter is knocked out before a fixed bout by Irish Traveller bare-knuckle boxer Mickey O'Neil (Brad Pitt), whose thickly accented speech is famously incomprehensible to the other characters. The diamond passes through the hands of pawnbrokers Sol and Vinny, hitman "Bullet-Tooth Tony" (Vinnie Jones), and a dog that swallows it, before the storylines collide in a violent finale.

Style and reception

The film shares the fast-cutting, darkly comic style of Ritchie's earlier Lock, Stock and Two Smoking Barrels (1998), with interlocking storylines, voice-over narration, and an eclectic soundtrack. Snatch was a commercial success, grossing over $83 million against a modest budget, and received generally positive reviews praising its pace, dialogue, and performances — particularly Pitt's turn as Mickey. It has since become a cult favourite and was adapted into a television series of the same name (2017–2018).
