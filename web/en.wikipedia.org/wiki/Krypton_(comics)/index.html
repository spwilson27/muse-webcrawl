Krypton is a fictional planet appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, and the native world of Superman. Created by writer Jerry Siegel and artist Joe Shuster, it was first referred to in Action Comics #1 (1938) and first depicted in Superman #1 (1939). Krypton was destroyed in a natural cataclysm shortly after the infant Kal-El was rocketed to Earth, making him the planet's best-known survivor.

Description

Krypton orbited a red sun named Rao, and its inhabitants, Kryptonians, were a technologically advanced, emotionally restrained people who appear identical to humans. Under a yellow sun, Kryptonians develop extraordinary powers — the source of Superman's abilities on Earth. Kryptonian civilization varied across incarnations: the Golden Age version was a utopia of science; the Silver Age added elaborate lore, including the bottled city of Kandor, the Phantom Zone and the sunstone crystal technology; John Byrne's 1986 reboot The Man of Steel recast Krypton as a cold, sterile world where children were gestated in birthing matrices.

Destruction

The exact cause of Krypton's destruction differs by version: tectonic instability and a chain reaction in the planet's radioactive core (which produced the mineral kryptonite), the machinations of the alien Brainiac, or the acts of the criminal Rogol Zaar in later continuity. In nearly all versions, the scientist Jor-El foresaw the disaster but was disbelieved by the Kryptonian Science Council, and could save only his infant son.

Survivors

Besides Kal-El, notable survivors in various continuities include his cousin Kara Zor-El (Supergirl), the criminals of the Phantom Zone led by General Zod, the inhabitants of the shrunken city of Kandor and of Argo City, and the dog Krypto.

Kryptonite

Debris from the planet, irradiated by its destruction, formed kryptonite, a mineral whose green variety robs Superman of his powers and can kill him; other colours produce different effects in the classic comics. Kryptonite was introduced in the 1943 radio series and entered the comics in 1949.

In other media

Krypton's crystalline design in the 1978 film Superman defined its screen image for decades. The planet is the setting of the prequel television series Krypton (2018–19), centred on Superman's grandfather Seg-El, and features in Man of Steel (2013) and the Arrowverse series Supergirl.
