Greater Anglia

Greater Anglia (legal name Greater Anglia Limited) is a British train operating company owned as a joint venture by Transport UK Group (formerly Abellio) and Mitsui & Co. It operates the East Anglia franchise, providing inter-city, commuter and local train services from London Liverpool Street to destinations in the East of England.

Services

Greater Anglia operates services on the Great Eastern Main Line to Ipswich and Norwich, the West Anglia lines to Cambridge and Stansted Airport, and numerous branch lines in Essex, Suffolk, Norfolk, Cambridgeshire and Hertfordshire, including the Bittern, Breckland, East Suffolk, Felixstowe, Gainsborough, Mayflower, Sunshine Coast and Wherry lines. Intercity services between London and Norwich are marketed under the Greater Anglia brand, and the Stansted Express airport service is also operated by the company.

History

The company began operating in February 2012, when Abellio took over the franchise from National Express East Anglia. In 2016 the franchise was re-let to Abellio for a term running to 2025, with Mitsui taking a 40% stake in 2017. The new franchise committed to replacing the entire train fleet.

Rolling stock

Between 2019 and 2022 Greater Anglia replaced its entire fleet with new trains built by Stadler and Bombardier/Alstom: Stadler FLIRT bi-mode and electric multiple units (Classes 745 and 755) on intercity, regional and Stansted services, and Alstom Aventra Class 720 units on commuter routes. The wholesale fleet replacement, including bi-mode trains able to run on diesel on non-electrified branch lines, was one of the largest fleet renewals on the British railway network.
