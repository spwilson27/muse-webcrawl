The United States Air Force (USAF) is the air service branch of the United States Armed Forces, and is one of the eight uniformed services of the United States. It is the youngest branch of the U.S. military, having been formed as a separate service on 18 September 1947 under the National Security Act of 1947. Its predecessor organizations include the Aeronautical Division of the Signal Corps (1907), the Army Air Forces (1941–1947), and the Army Air Corps.

The Air Force's core missions are air superiority, global integrated intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance, rapid global mobility, global strike, and command and control. It operates a large fleet of manned and unmanned aircraft, intercontinental ballistic missiles (in cooperation with U.S. Strategic Command), and military satellites.

History

American military aviation began in 1907 with the Aeronautical Division of the U.S. Army Signal Corps. During the Second World War the United States Army Air Forces became the largest air arm in the world, conducting strategic bombing campaigns in Europe and the Pacific. The demonstrated importance of independent air power led to the creation of a separate Air Force in 1947, with Stuart Symington as its first Secretary and General Carl Spaatz as its first Chief of Staff.

During the Cold War the USAF built Strategic Air Command into a nuclear deterrent force of bombers and ICBMs, fought in the Korean and Vietnam Wars, and later played decisive roles in the Gulf War (1991), the Kosovo campaign, and operations in Afghanistan and Iraq. The service has since emphasized stealth aircraft such as the F-117, B-2, F-22, and F-35, remotely piloted aircraft, and space and cyber capabilities — the latter two partly transferred to the United States Space Force after its establishment in 2019.

Organization

The Department of the Air Force, headed by the Secretary of the Air Force, administers both the Air Force and the Space Force. Major commands include Air Combat Command, Air Mobility Command, Air Force Global Strike Command, Air Education and Training Command, Air Force Materiel Command, Air Force Reserve Command, and the overseas commands Pacific Air Forces and United States Air Forces in Europe – Air Forces Africa.

Aircraft

The inventory includes fighters (F-15, F-16, F-22, F-35), bombers (B-52, B-1, B-2, with the B-21 in development), tankers (KC-135, KC-46), transports (C-5, C-17, C-130), helicopters, trainers, and unmanned systems such as the MQ-9 Reaper and RQ-4 Global Hawk.
