Kenji Fukaya

Kenji Fukaya (Japanese: 深谷賢治, born March 12, 1959) is a Japanese mathematician known for his work in symplectic geometry and Riemannian geometry. He is a professor at Stony Brook University and a member of the Simons Center for Geometry and Physics, and previously taught at Kyoto University.

Work

Fukaya is best known for the Fukaya category, an A-infinity category constructed from Lagrangian submanifolds of a symplectic manifold, which plays a central role in homological mirror symmetry. He also contributed to the theory of Gromov–Hausdorff convergence of Riemannian manifolds and to Floer homology.

Honours

He has received the Japan Academy Prize and was a plenary speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians in 1990. He is a member of the Japan Academy.
