Raúl Modesto Castro Ruz (born 3 June 1931) is a Cuban retired politician and general who served as First Secretary of the Communist Party of Cuba from 2011 to 2021, and as President of the Council of State and Council of Ministers of Cuba from 2008 to 2018. The younger brother of Fidel Castro, he was a key commander in the Cuban Revolution.

Revolutionary career

Castro participated in the 1953 attack on the Moncada Barracks and was imprisoned, then exiled to Mexico, where he helped organize the 26th of July Movement's return aboard the yacht Granma in 1956. As a rebel commander in the Sierra Maestra and the Second Eastern Front, he played a major military role in the overthrow of the Batista regime.

Minister and successor

From 1959 until 2008 he served as Minister of the Revolutionary Armed Forces, becoming the world's longest-serving defence minister. He formally succeeded Fidel as president in February 2008.

Presidency

His tenure saw limited market-oriented economic reforms, expansion of self-employment, and the historic restoration of diplomatic relations with the United States announced in December 2014. He stepped down as president in 2018 in favour of Miguel Díaz-Canel and retired as party leader in 2021.
