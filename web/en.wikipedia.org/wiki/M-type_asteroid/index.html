M-type asteroid

M-type asteroids are asteroids of partially known composition; they are moderately bright (albedo 0.1–0.2) and thought to contain relatively high concentrations of metal, principally nickel–iron. They are the presumed source of iron meteorites, and may represent the exposed metallic cores of differentiated planetesimals whose rocky mantles were stripped by collisions.

Characteristics

M-type asteroids occupy the middle asteroid belt. Their spectra are generally featureless and slightly reddish, similar to those of iron–nickel alloys, though some members formerly classed as M-type have been reclassified. The largest M-type asteroid is 16 Psyche, the target of NASA's Psyche mission, launched in 2023. Other notable examples include 21 Lutetia (visited by the Rosetta spacecraft) and 216 Kleopatra, a metal-rich body with a "dog-bone" shape and two moons.
