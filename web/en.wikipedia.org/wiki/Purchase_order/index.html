Purchase order

A purchase order (PO) is a document issued by a buyer to a seller, indicating the types, quantities and agreed prices of products or services that the buyer wishes to purchase. Sending a purchase order to a supplier constitutes a legal offer to buy; once accepted by the seller, it usually forms a binding contract. Purchase orders are a standard instrument of procurement in business and government, providing documentation, budgetary control and an audit trail for spending.

Contents

A typical purchase order includes a unique PO number, the date of issue, the names and addresses of buyer and seller, a description of each item ordered with quantities and unit prices, delivery date and location, payment terms, and the total value of the order. The PO number is quoted on subsequent documents — the delivery note, the seller's invoice and the payment record — so that all can be matched.

Process

The purchase-order cycle generally begins with a purchase requisition raised internally by the department needing goods or services. After approval, the purchasing department issues a PO to the chosen supplier. The supplier acknowledges the order, manufactures or picks the goods, and delivers them with a packing list. On receipt, the buyer checks the delivery against the PO (goods receipt), and when the invoice arrives, accounts payable performs a three-way match between the purchase order, the goods-receipt record and the invoice before authorising payment.

Types and advantages

Variants include blanket purchase orders, which commit to buying goods or services over a period with deliveries scheduled as needed, and standing orders for recurring supplies. Using purchase orders helps organisations control expenditure (spending is approved before commitment), prevents duplicate or unauthorised purchases, simplifies receiving and invoice verification, and creates records for accounting and audit. Modern ERP and e-procurement systems generate, approve and transmit purchase orders electronically, sometimes using standard formats such as EDI documents.
