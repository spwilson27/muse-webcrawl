Dalton Highway

The James W. Dalton Highway, usually shortened to the Dalton Highway (Alaska Route 11), is a 414-mile (666 km) road in Alaska running from the Elliott Highway near Livengood, north of Fairbanks, to Deadhorse near the Arctic Ocean and the Prudhoe Bay oil fields. Built in 1974 as a supply road for the Trans-Alaska Pipeline System, it parallels the pipeline for most of its length.

Route

The highway crosses the Yukon River, passes the Arctic Circle, and climbs over Atigun Pass (4,739 ft) in the Brooks Range before descending to the North Slope coastal plain. Services are extremely sparse — fuel and lodging are available only at Coldfoot, roughly halfway, and at Deadhorse — and the road is notorious for steep grades, loose gravel, roaming wildlife and long stretches without mobile coverage. It gained wide recognition through the television series Ice Road Truckers.

Access

The highway was restricted to permitted traffic until 1981 and fully opened to the public to Deadhorse in 1994, though the oilfield area beyond remains restricted, with Arctic Ocean access via guided tours.
