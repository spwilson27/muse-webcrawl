Sid and Nancy

Sid and Nancy (also known as Sid & Nancy: Love Kills) is a 1986 British biographical drama film directed by Alex Cox, about the life of Sex Pistols bassist Sid Vicious and his destructive relationship with girlfriend Nancy Spungen. It stars Gary Oldman as Vicious and Chloe Webb as Spungen, with a screenplay by Cox and Abbe Wool.

Plot and reception

The film depicts Vicious's rise with the Pistols, the band's chaotic 1978 American tour and breakup, and the couple's heroin addiction in New York, ending with Spungen's stabbing death at the Chelsea Hotel in October 1978 and Vicious's fatal overdose in February 1979. Oldman's performance was widely acclaimed and launched his film career, though John Lydon and others criticized the film's accuracy; it remains a cult classic of punk cinema.

Legacy

The film is credited with helping shape the popular myth of the Sid and Nancy story. It premiered at the Cannes Film Festival and appears on numerous lists of the best British films and music biopics.
