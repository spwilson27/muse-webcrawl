Black Widow (Natalia Alianovna Romanova; Russian: Наталья Альяновна Романова) is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by Stan Lee, Don Rico and Don Heck, she debuted in Tales of Suspense #52 (1964) as a Soviet spy and antagonist of Iron Man, before defecting to the United States, becoming an agent of S.H.I.E.L.D. and a long-serving member of the Avengers.

Fictional biography

Born in Stalingrad in the Soviet Union, Natasha was orphaned as a child and raised in the covert "Red Room" programme, where she was trained in espionage and combat alongside other young women given the codename "Black Widow", with false memories implanted of a career as a ballerina. A variant of the super-soldier programme slowed her aging and enhanced her physiology. After missions against Iron Man, she defected, partnered with Hawkeye and Daredevil, led the Los Angeles-based Champions, and served repeatedly with the Avengers, at one point chairing the team.

Abilities and equipment

Black Widow is a master martial artist, marksman, acrobat and intelligence operative fluent in numerous languages. Her signature equipment includes the "Widow's Bite" — bracelets that discharge powerful electrostatic blasts — a grappling line and an array of spy gadgets. She has no conventional superpowers beyond enhanced resistance to aging and disease in most portrayals.

In other media

Scarlett Johansson portrayed Natasha Romanoff in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, beginning with Iron Man 2 (2010) and culminating in the solo film Black Widow (2021), making the character one of the franchise's most prominent heroes.
