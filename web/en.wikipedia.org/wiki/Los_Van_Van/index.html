Los Van Van

Los Van Van
Origin: Havana, Cuba
Formed: 1969
Founder: Juan Formell
Genres: Songo, salsa, timba

Los Van Van is a Cuban dance band founded in 1969 by bassist, composer and arranger Juan Formell. Widely regarded as one of the most important groups in modern Cuban popular music, the band is nicknamed "the Rolling Stones of salsa" (el tren de la música cubana — "the train of Cuban music") for its longevity and popularity.

Style and history

Formell, formerly of Orquesta Revé, created Los Van Van by fusing the charanga format — French flute and violins — with electric guitars, synthesisers, drum kit and a rhythm section drawing on rock and funk. The result was a new rhythm called songo, which underlies what later became known as timba. Signature songs from the band's decades-long catalogue include "La Habana no aguanta más", "Sandunguera", "Muévete" and "Esto te pone la cabeza mala".

Recognition

Los Van Van won the Grammy Award for Best Salsa Album in 2000 for Llegó... Van Van (Van Van Is Here) and have won multiple Latin Grammy Awards. After Juan Formell's death in 2014, the band continued under the direction of his son Samuel Formell.
