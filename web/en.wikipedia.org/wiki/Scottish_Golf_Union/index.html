Scottish Golf Union

Founded: 1920
Dissolved: 2015 (merged into Scottish Golf)
Sport: Golf
Jurisdiction: Scotland
Role: Governing body for men's amateur golf

The Scottish Golf Union (SGU) was the governing body for men's amateur golf in Scotland from 1920 until 2015. Based for many years at St Andrews, the "Home of Golf", it represented hundreds of affiliated clubs and tens of thousands of registered male golfers, administered the handicapping system in Scotland, and ran national amateur championships.

History and role

The SGU was founded in 1920 by Scottish clubs seeking a unified body for amateur competition and standardised rules in cooperation with The R&A. It organised flagship events including the Scottish Amateur Championship (first played in 1922) and the Scottish Open Amateur Stroke Play Championship, and selected Scottish national teams for the Home Internationals and the Eisenhower Trophy. The corresponding women's body was the Scottish Ladies' Golfing Association (SLGA, founded 1904).

Merger

After several years of negotiation, the SGU and SLGA voted to amalgamate, creating a single governing body, Scottish Golf Limited, which came into existence on 1 October 2015. The unified body took over handicapping, championships, player development, and club support for all golfers in Scotland.
