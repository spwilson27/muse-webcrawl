Reno Air Races

The Reno Air Races, officially the National Championship Air Races, were a multi-day air racing event held each September at Reno Stead Airport in Nevada from 1964 to 2023. Multiple classes of aircraft — including Unlimited, Jet, Sport, T-6, Formula One and Biplane — raced head-to-head around pylons at low altitude, with the Unlimited class exceeding 500 mph.

The event was founded by Bill Stead and became the world's fastest motorsport. A fatal accident in 2011 killed pilot Jimmy Leeward and ten spectators, prompting safety changes. The event held its final Reno edition in 2023 and moved to Roswell, New Mexico, from 2025.
