Deutsche Telekom AG is a German telecommunications company headquartered in Bonn. It is the largest telecommunications provider in Europe by revenue and one of the largest in the world, operating fixed and mobile networks in Germany, across Europe, and in the United States through its majority stake in T-Mobile US. The company is recognisable by its magenta "T" branding.

History

Deutsche Telekom was created in 1995 when the telecommunications arm of the state-owned Deutsche Bundespost was spun off as part of Germany's postal reforms, and it was partially privatised with a public offering in 1996 — then the largest in European history. The German federal government remains a significant shareholder.

Operations

The group's activities include German fixed and mobile services under the Telekom brand, European subsidiaries such as those in Poland, Greece, Hungary and the Netherlands, the IT services unit T-Systems, and T-Mobile US, which became one of the largest American mobile carriers following its merger with Sprint in 2020.
