Districts of Syria

The 14 governorates of Syria, or muḥāfaẓāt, are divided into 65 districts, or manāṭiq (singular: minṭaqah), each named after its largest city or administrative centre. The districts are in turn divided into 284 subdistricts, or nawāḥī.

Overview

The district system forms the second tier of Syrian local administration. District capitals share the name of their district in nearly all cases (for example, Douma District is administered from the city of Douma), with a few exceptions such as Mount Simeon District, administered from Aleppo. The capital city of Damascus forms its own governorate and is not part of any district of the surrounding Rif Dimashq Governorate.
