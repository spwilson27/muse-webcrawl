MD Anderson Cancer Center

The University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center is a comprehensive cancer center in Houston, Texas, and one of the largest and most highly regarded cancer hospitals in the world. Founded in 1941, it is a component of the University of Texas System and is located in the Texas Medical Center, the largest medical complex in the world. Its mission is summarised by its slogan, "Making Cancer History".

History

The center was created by the Texas Legislature in 1941 as part of the University of Texas, funded partly by the M. D. Anderson Foundation established by the cotton magnate and banker Monroe Dunaway Anderson. The institution first operated in temporary quarters — including converted barracks and the estate of a Houston businessman — before moving to its present site in the Texas Medical Center in the 1950s. It was one of the first three comprehensive cancer centers designated under the US National Cancer Act of 1971.

Care and research

MD Anderson employs more than 20,000 people and cares for well over 100,000 patients a year, drawn from every US state and many countries. It runs one of the largest clinical trials programmes of any cancer center, with thousands of patients enrolled in studies of new therapies. Research strengths include immunotherapy — the institution was central to the development of immune checkpoint inhibitors associated with Nobel laureate James P. Allison, whose work on CTLA-4 blockade was carried out there — as well as genomics, targeted therapy and cancer prevention. In 2012 the center launched its "Moon Shots" programme, an initiative modelled on the Apollo programme to accelerate progress against selected cancers.

Education

The center operates the MD Anderson Cancer Center UTHealth Graduate School of Biomedical Sciences jointly with UTHealth Houston, trains large numbers of clinical and research fellows and residents, and runs a school of health professions offering degrees in laboratory and imaging sciences.

Rankings

MD Anderson has frequently been ranked the number one hospital for cancer care in the U.S. News & World Report "Best Hospitals" survey, occupying the top position in most years since the rankings began.
