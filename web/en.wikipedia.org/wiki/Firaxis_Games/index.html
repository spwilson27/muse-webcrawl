Firaxis Games, Inc. is an American video game developer based in Hunt Valley, Maryland. Founded in 1996 by Sid Meier, Jeff Briggs and Brian Reynolds after they left MicroProse, the studio is best known for the Civilization series of turn-based strategy games and for the modern XCOM series. Since 2005 the studio has been a subsidiary of 2K, itself part of Take-Two Interactive.

History

Firaxis was formed on May 1, 1996, following the departure of Meier, Briggs and Reynolds from MicroProse, the studio behind the original Civilization (1991). The new company's name was coined as a contraction of "fiery" and "axis", suggesting creativity and productivity. Its early releases included Sid Meier's Gettysburg! (1997), the science-fiction strategy game Sid Meier's Alpha Centauri (1999), and Sid Meier's Civilization III (2001).

In November 2005, Take-Two Interactive acquired Firaxis for a reported US$26.7 million, placing it under the newly formed 2K label. The studio went on to develop Civilization IV (2005), Civilization V (2010) and Civilization VI (2016), as well as the spin-off Civilization: Beyond Earth (2014). In 2012 Firaxis revived the X-COM franchise with the turn-based tactics game XCOM: Enemy Unknown, followed by XCOM 2 (2016) and the spin-off XCOM: Chimera Squad (2020).

In December 2022 the studio released Marvel's Midnight Suns, a tactical role-playing game developed with Marvel Games. In 2023, long-time studio head Steve Martin departed and Heather Hazen became studio chief; Sid Meier remained as director of creative development. A seventh mainline Civilization title, Civilization VII, was announced in 2024 and released in February 2025.
