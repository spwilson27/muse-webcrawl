World Athletics

World Athletics, formerly known as the International Association of Athletics Federations (IAAF), is the international governing body for the sport of athletics, covering track and field, cross country running, road running, race walking, mountain running and ultra running.

History

The federation was founded on 17 July 1912 in Stockholm by representatives of 17 national federations, shortly after the Stockholm Olympic Games. It was known as the International Amateur Athletic Federation until 2001, then the IAAF, before adopting the name World Athletics in 2019. Its headquarters are in Monaco.

Role

World Athletics sets competition rules, ratifies world records, accredits member federations (more than 200), organises the World Athletics Championships (first held in 1983) and circuits such as the Diamond League, and runs anti-doping and integrity programmes through the Athletics Integrity Unit, founded in 2017.

Presidents have included Lord Burghley, Adriaan Paulen, Primo Nebiolo, Lamine Diack and, since 2015, Sebastian Coe.
