The All American Futurity is an American Quarter Horse race held annually at Ruidoso Downs Race Track in Ruidoso Downs, New Mexico. Run over 440 yards (a quarter of a mile) on Labor Day, it is open to two-year-old Quarter Horses and is the richest race in Quarter Horse racing, with a purse of about $3 million.

History

The race was first run in 1959 and was won by Hugh Huntley's mare Galobar. It quickly became the premier event for two-year-old Quarter Horses, the culmination of a series of time trials in which hundreds of horses compete for ten places in the final. Winning the Futurity has made the careers of famous horses such as Easy Jet (1969), Dash For Cash (1975) and Refrigerator (1990).

Together with the All American Derby for three-year-olds and the Champion of Champions, it is one of the cornerstones of the Quarter Horse racing calendar. In 2024 the race was temporarily relocated because of flooding and wildfires affecting the Ruidoso area.

Records

  • Speed record: just over 20.3 seconds for 440 yards, set by multiple horses in the modern era.
  • Notable winners include Special Effort (1981), the only horse to win the Ruidoso "Triple Crown" of futurities (Kansas, Rainbow and All American Futurities) as a two-year-old.
