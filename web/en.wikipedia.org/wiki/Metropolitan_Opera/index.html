Metropolitan Opera

The Metropolitan Opera (the Met) is an American opera company based in New York City, resident at the Metropolitan Opera House at Lincoln Center.

Metropolitan Opera
Founded1883

Music DirectorYannick Nézet-Séguin

History

Founded in 1883, the Met first performed on Broadway at 39th Street, moving to Lincoln Center in 1966. It has premiered many works and hosted singers from Caruso to Callas.

Broadcasts

Radio broadcasts began in 1931; the Met Opera on Demand and Live in HD cinema transmissions since 2006 reach global audiences.
