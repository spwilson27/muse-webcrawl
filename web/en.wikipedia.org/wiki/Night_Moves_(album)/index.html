Night Moves (album)

Night Moves is the ninth studio album by American rock singer-songwriter Bob Seger, released on October 22, 1976, by Capitol Records. It was Seger's first album credited to Bob Seger & the Silver Bullet Band, although the band appears on only part of the record; several songs were recorded with the Muscle Shoals Rhythm Section. The album was Seger's commercial breakthrough in the United States.

Night Moves

Artist: Bob Seger & the Silver Bullet Band

Released: 22 October 1976

Genre: Rock, heartland rock

Label: Capitol

Peak position: No. 8 (US Billboard 200)

Background and recording

After years of regional success in Michigan, Seger recorded much of the album at Muscle Shoals Sound Studio in Alabama, with additional sessions in Detroit and Toronto. The title track, a nostalgic reflection on adolescent romance in the 1960s, was recorded in Toronto with local musicians after the scheduled sessions had ended.

Reception and legacy

Night Moves reached number eight on the Billboard 200 and was certified six times platinum in the United States. The title track, "Mainstreet" and "Rock and Roll Never Forgets" became enduring radio staples. The album is frequently listed among the greatest rock albums of the 1970s, and the song "Night Moves" was named by the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame as one of the songs that shaped rock and roll.

Track listing

All tracks written by Bob Seger: "Rock and Roll Never Forgets", "Night Moves", "The Fire Down Below", "Sunburst", "Sunspot Baby", "Mainstreet", "Come to Poppa", "Ship of Fools", "Mary Lou".
