Bloomberg News

Bloomberg News
Founded1990
FoundersMichael Bloomberg; Matthew Winkler
ParentBloomberg L.P.
[Image: The Bloomberg Tower in New York, with the terminal floor]

Bloomberg News is a financial news agency founded in 1990 by Michael Bloomberg and Matthew Winkler, originally to supply news to Bloomberg Terminal subscribers. It grew into one of the world's largest news organisations, with thousands of journalists, and powers Bloomberg Television, radio and the consumer site bloomberg.com.

Model

Bloomberg's news is financed by the terminal business, which gives it unusual resources and a markets-first news agenda: prices, deals and central banks. It launched Bloomberg Businessweek (acquired 2009) and Bloomberg Opinion among other products.

Reach

Its reporting is syndicated to newspapers worldwide; the agency is known for speed on market-moving news and for its data-driven coverage of finance and technology.
