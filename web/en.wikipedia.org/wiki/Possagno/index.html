Possagno

Country: Italy
Region: Veneto
Province: Treviso
Population: about 2,200

Possagno is a comune (municipality) in the province of Treviso, in the Italian region of Veneto, at the foot of Monte Grappa, about 60 km northwest of Venice. It is best known as the birthplace of the neoclassical sculptor Antonio Canova (1757–1822).

Main sights

The Tempio Canoviano, a grand neoclassical church designed by Canova himself and built after his death with his financial bequest, dominates the town; Canova is buried there, his tomb inside a pyramidal monument he originally designed for another patron. The Museo Canova (Gipsoteca) preserves the sculptor's birthplace, studio and a celebrated collection of his plaster models and sketches, including the models for Psyche Revived by Cupid's Kiss and The Three Graces.

History

The area saw heavy fighting in the First World War, lying near the Piave front and Monte Grappa. Possagno's identity remains closely tied to Canova, whose works made the small town a destination for art historians and admirers of neoclassicism.
