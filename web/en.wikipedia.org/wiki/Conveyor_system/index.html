Conveyor system

A conveyor system is a common piece of mechanical handling equipment that moves materials from one location to another. Conveyors are especially useful in applications involving the transport of heavy or bulky materials, and they allow quick and efficient transport of a wide variety of materials, making them very popular in the material handling and packaging industries. They also have popular consumer applications, as supermarkets and airports use conveyors to move items or baggage to customers.

Types

There are many varieties of conveyor systems. Belt conveyors consist of two or more pulleys with a continuous loop of material — the conveyor belt — rotating about them. Roller conveyors use a series of rollers to move items by gravity or power. Chain conveyors tow or carry products on chains. Other types include overhead conveyors, bucket conveyors for vertical bulk transport, pneumatic conveyors, screw or auger conveyors, and flexible conveyors based on articulated plastic chains.

Applications

Conveyor systems are used in mining and bulk handling, food processing, bottling and canning plants, manufacturing assembly lines, warehouse automation, e-commerce distribution centres, and airport baggage handling. Specialized food-grade designs use washable belts and stainless steel construction.

Controls and safety

Modern installations are coordinated by programmable logic controllers with photo-eye sensors, barcode readers, and diverters for sortation. Safety standards require emergency stop devices, guarding of nip points, and lockout procedures for maintenance.
