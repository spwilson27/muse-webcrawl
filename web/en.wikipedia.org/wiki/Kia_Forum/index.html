The Kia Forum, known simply as the Forum, is a multi-purpose indoor arena in Inglewood, California. Opened in 1967, it was the home of the Los Angeles Lakers and Los Angeles Kings until 1999 and is one of the best-known concert venues in the United States.

History

Built by Jack Kent Cooke, the circular arena hosted the Lakers' "Showtime" era and the Kings' Wayne Gretzky years. After the teams moved to the Staples Center, the Forum was renovated and reopened in 2014 as a concert-focused venue. Kia acquired the naming rights in 2022.
