Bernard Malamud
Born: 26 April 1914, Brooklyn
Died: 18 March 1986
Notable work: The Fixer

Bernard Malamud (26 April 1914 – 18 March 1986) was an American novelist and short story writer, regarded as one of the best known American Jewish authors of the 20th century.

Works

His baseball novel The Natural (1952) was adapted into a popular film. The Assistant (1957), The Fixer (1966)—which won the Pulitzer Prize and National Book Award—and collections such as The Magic Barrel (1958) explore moral struggle among ordinary, often Jewish-American characters.

Style

Malamud's prose combines realism with allegory and fable-like compression; his recurring theme is that suffering can teach compassion and responsibility.
