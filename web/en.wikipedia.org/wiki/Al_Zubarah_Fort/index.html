Al Zubarah Fort is a historic military fort located in the ancient town of Zubarah in the Al Shamal municipality of Qatar, on the northwestern coast of the Qatar Peninsula about 105 km from Doha. Built in 1938 under Sheikh Abdullah bin Jassim Al Thani, it stands near the ruins of the 18th-century pearling and trading town of Al Zubarah.

Description

The fort is a square structure with thick walls of coral rock and limestone rendered in mud plaster, three circular corner towers and one rectangular tower, and a crenellated parapet. It served as a coast guard station and police post protecting Qatar's northern coast.

Heritage

Restored in the 1980s, the fort now houses a museum and visitor centre for the Al Zubarah Archaeological Site, inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2013 as an outstanding example of a Gulf pearling town.
