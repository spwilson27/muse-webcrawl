Fonda family

OriginNetherlands; settled in New Netherland / New York
Notable membersHenry Fonda, Jane Fonda, Peter Fonda, Bridget Fonda
OccupationActing

The Fonda family is an American family prominent in film and theatre across three generations of the 20th and 21st centuries. Of Dutch descent, the family traces its American roots to settlers in New Netherland in the 17th century; the town of Fonda, New York, is named for an ancestor.

Members

Henry Fonda (1905–1982), the patriarch, was one of Hollywood's defining leading men, acclaimed for roles in The Grapes of Wrath (1940), 12 Angry Men (1957) and Once Upon a Time in the West (1968), and won the Academy Award for Best Actor for On Golden Pond (1981).

His children continued the tradition: Jane Fonda (born 1937), actress, activist and fitness entrepreneur, won Academy Awards for Klute (1971) and Coming Home (1978); Peter Fonda (1940–2019) co-wrote and starred in the counterculture landmark Easy Rider (1969) and was Oscar-nominated for Ulee's Gold (1997). Peter's daughter Bridget Fonda (born 1964) starred in films such as Single White Female (1992) before retiring from acting. Jane's son Troy Garity is also an actor.
