Porn Star Martini

The Porn Star Martini is a passion fruit-flavoured cocktail made with vanilla vodka, passion fruit liqueur, passion fruit juice and lime juice, traditionally served with a shot glass of prosecco on the side. Despite its name it is not a true martini. It became one of the most popular cocktails in the United Kingdom during the 2010s.

History

The drink was created in 2002 by bartender Douglas Ankrah at the Townhouse bar in Knightsbridge, London. Ankrah originally called it the "Maverick Martini" after a club in Cape Town; the provocative later name, he explained, was meant to evoke something bold, sexy and playful rather than to be taken literally. It is also closely associated with Ankrah's LAB bar in Soho.

Preparation

The cocktail is typically made by shaking vanilla vodka, Passoã (or another passion fruit liqueur), passion fruit juice or purée, vanilla sugar syrup and lime juice with ice, then straining into a chilled coupe or martini glass. It is garnished with half a fresh passion fruit, and accompanied by a small shot of chilled prosecco, which the drinker may sip alternately or pour over the drink.

Popularity

Industry surveys have repeatedly listed the Porn Star Martini among the best-selling cocktails in British bars, and it was widely reported as the most searched-for cocktail recipe in the UK in the late 2010s. Some venues have renamed it, for example to "Passion Star Martini", to avoid the original name.
