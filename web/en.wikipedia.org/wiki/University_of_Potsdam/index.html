University of Potsdam

The University of Potsdam (German: Universität Potsdam) is a public university in Potsdam, the capital of the German state of Brandenburg. Founded in 1991, shortly after German reunification, it is the largest university in Brandenburg, with roughly 22,000 students.

Campuses

The university occupies three main sites in Potsdam: the Am Neuen Palais campus, located in buildings adjoining Sanssouci Park; the Golm campus, a science hub shared with several Max Planck and Fraunhofer institutes; and the Griebnitzsee campus near the Berlin border, home to law, economics, and social sciences and the Hasso Plattner Institute for digital engineering, which is affiliated with the university.

Academics

The university has five faculties — Law; Arts and Humanities; Human Sciences; Economics and Social Sciences; and Science — plus the Digital Engineering Faculty jointly run with the Hasso Plattner Institute. It is particularly known for cognitive sciences, linguistics, geosciences, and public administration studies.
