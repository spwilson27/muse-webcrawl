List of accidents involving sports teams

This is a list of notable accidents, mostly aviation disasters, in which members of professional or national sports teams were killed or injured while travelling.

Aviation accidents

DateTeamIncident
4 May 1949Torino FC (football)Superga air disaster: the aircraft carrying the "Grande Torino" team crashed near Turin, killing all 31 aboard.
6 February 1958Manchester United (football)Munich air disaster: eight players among 23 killed when the team's flight crashed on take-off from Munich.
16 July 1960Denmark Olympic football teamEight players killed in a crash on take-off from Copenhagen.
3 April 1961Green Cross (Chilean football)Team lost in a crash in the Andes; 24 killed.
29 September 1977University of Evansville basketballTeam plane crashed shortly after take-off in Indiana.
14 November 1970Marshall University footballSouthern Airways Flight 932 crashed near Huntington, West Virginia, killing all 75 aboard including 37 players.
13 October 1972Old Christians rugby (Uruguay)Uruguayan Air Force Flight 571 crashed in the Andes; 16 of 45 survived 72 days.
11 August 1979Pakhtakor Tashkent (football)Entire squad among 178 killed in the Dniprodzerzhynsk mid-air collision.
8 December 1987Alianza Lima (football)Team plane crashed into the sea near Lima, killing 43.
28 April 1993Zambia national football teamMilitary aircraft crashed off Gabon, killing all 30 aboard.
7 September 2011Lokomotiv Yaroslavl (ice hockey)Yak-Service Flight 9633 crashed on take-off at Yaroslavl; 44 killed, nearly the entire team.
28 November 2016Chapecoense (football)LaMia Flight 2933 crashed near Medellín, Colombia; 71 killed including 19 players.
