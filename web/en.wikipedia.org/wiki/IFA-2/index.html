IFA-2 (Interconnexion France-Angleterre 2) is a 1,000 MW high-voltage direct current (HVDC) submarine power cable between France and the United Kingdom, running beneath the English Channel. It links Fareham in Hampshire, England, with Tourbeville in Normandy, France.

History

The project was developed by National Grid and Réseau de Transport d'Électricité (RTE). Construction began in 2018, and the interconnector entered commercial operation in early 2021, following the earlier 2,000 MW IFA link commissioned in 1986.

Route

The cable makes landfall at Chilling, near Warsash in Hampshire, connecting to a converter station at Daedalus, near Lee-on-the-Solent. On the French side it lands near Tourbeville and connects to the Tourbeville substation.
