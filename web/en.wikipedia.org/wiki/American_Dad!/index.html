American Dad!

Genre: Animated sitcom
Created by: Seth MacFarlane, Mike Barker, Matt Weitzman
First aired: 6 February 2005
Networks: Fox (2005–2014), TBS (2014–present)

American Dad! is an American animated sitcom created by Seth MacFarlane, Mike Barker and Matt Weitzman for Fox, premiering in 2005 and moving to TBS in 2014. It centres on the Smith family of Langley Falls, Virginia: Stan Smith, a hyper-patriotic CIA agent; his wife Francine; their children Hayley and Steve; Roger, a flamboyant alien living in their attic; and Klaus, a goldfish containing the brain of an East German Olympic skier.

Premise and style

Unlike MacFarlane's Family Guy, American Dad! relies less on cutaway gags and more on character-driven storylines and surreal escalation, frequently built around Roger's countless disguises and personas, which became a signature running device. Early seasons satirised post-9/11 American conservatism through Stan's rigidity, but the show gradually became an absurdist ensemble comedy noted for its dense plotting and voice acting.

Reception

The series has run for more than twenty seasons and 350 episodes, earning Emmy nominations and a devoted following; critics have praised its evolution from political satire into one of the most inventive adult animated comedies on television, with Roger (voiced by MacFarlane) frequently singled out as its breakout character.
