Le Plateau-Mont-Royal

Le Plateau-Mont-Royal is a borough of Montreal, Quebec, Canada, located on the eastern slope of Mount Royal. It is known for its colourful Victorian rowhouses with exterior staircases, its francophone cultural life, and neighbourhoods such as the Mile End, Saint-Louis Square area and Laurier Avenue.

Historically a working-class francophone district, the Plateau gentrified from the 1960s onward, attracting artists, writers and students; it has often been described as one of North America's most creative neighbourhoods. Landmarks include La Fontaine Park, Mount Royal Park (shared with neighbouring boroughs), and Saint-Jean-Baptiste Church.

The borough had a population of roughly 105,000 at the 2021 census and is among the densest in Canada.
