British naval forces in the Falklands War

[Image placeholder: British naval forces in the Falklands War]

The British naval forces in the Falklands War formed the bulk of the task force sent by the United Kingdom to retake the Falkland Islands after Argentina's invasion on 2 April 1982. Under the overall naval command of Rear Admiral Sandy Woodward, the force centred on the aircraft carriers HMS Hermes and HMS Invincible with their Sea Harrier fighters.

Composition and operations

The task force included destroyers, frigates, nuclear and diesel submarines, amphibious ships (HMS Fearless and Intrepid), and a large fleet of requisitioned merchant vessels ("ships taken up from trade") including the liners Queen Elizabeth 2 and Canberra. Key naval episodes included the sinking of the Argentine cruiser ARA General Belgrano by HMS Conqueror, the loss of HMS Sheffield, Ardent, Antelope and Coventry to air attack, and the amphibious landings at San Carlos Water in May 1982 that enabled the land campaign ending in Argentine surrender on 14 June.
