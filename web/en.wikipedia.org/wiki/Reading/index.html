Reading

Reading
DefinitionProcess of decoding written language to extract meaning
MilestonesAlphabetic scripts, printing press, universal literacy campaigns
[Image: A child reading a picture book by lamplight, absorbed]

Reading is the process of taking in the sense or meaning of symbols, often letters — the decoding of written language into thought. It is the gateway skill of formal education and the quiet technology behind libraries (Library) and the e-book alike.

Reading and the brain

Learning to read rewires visual and language areas of the Brain; literacy transformed societies from writing's invention to the printing press (wider crawl) and mass schooling. Screen reading, audiobooks and declining deep-reading habits are active research and policy topics.
