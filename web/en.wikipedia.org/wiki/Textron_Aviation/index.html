Textron Aviation Inc. is an American aviation manufacturer and the general aviation business unit of the Textron conglomerate. It was formed in March 2014 following Textron's acquisition of the Beechcraft Corporation, combining it with the Cessna Aircraft Company into a single company that retains both the Cessna and Beechcraft brands.

Products

Textron Aviation produces the Cessna line of piston singles (such as the Skyhawk 172, Skylane, and Stationair), Caravan turboprops, and the Citation family of business jets, including the Citation Latitude, Longitude, CJ series, and Mustang. Under the Beechcraft brand it builds the King Air turboprops, the Baron and Bonanza piston aircraft, and the T-6 Texan II military trainer. The company also provides parts, service, and support through a global service center network.

History

Cessna (founded 1927) and Beechcraft (founded 1932) were both pillars of Wichita's "Air Capital" aviation cluster. Textron acquired Cessna in 1992; its purchase of Beechcraft out of bankruptcy proceedings led to the creation of Textron Aviation in 2014, which has since introduced new types such as the Cessna SkyCourier utility twin and the Beechcraft Denali single-engine turboprop.
