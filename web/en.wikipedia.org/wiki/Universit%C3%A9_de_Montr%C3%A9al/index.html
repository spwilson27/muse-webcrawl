The Université de Montréal (UdeM) is a French-language public research university in Montreal, Quebec, Canada. Founded in 1878 as a branch of Université Laval, it became independent in 1919 and is now one of Canada's largest universities, with more than 65,000 students including its affiliated schools.

Campus

The main campus sits on the northern slope of Mount Royal and is dominated by the art deco Roger Gaudry Building, whose tower is a Montreal landmark. A second campus, MIL, opened in the Outremont district in 2019 on a former rail yard.

Academics and research

UdeM is a major research institution, noted for work in artificial intelligence through Mila, the Quebec AI institute associated with professor Yoshua Bengio, as well as in health sciences, law, and the natural sciences. Its affiliated schools HEC Montréal and Polytechnique Montréal are separately administered but award UdeM degrees in many programs.
