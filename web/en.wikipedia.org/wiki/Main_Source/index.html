Main Source was an American-Canadian hip hop group based in New York City and Toronto, composed of Toronto-born DJs and producers K-Cut and Sir Scratch, and Queens-based rapper and producer Large Professor. Their debut album, Breaking Atoms (1991), is regarded as a hip hop classic and featured the first recorded appearance of Nas on the track "Live at the Barbeque".

History

The group formed in the late 1980s. Signed to Wild Pitch Records, they released their debut single "Think"/"Watch Roger Do His Thing" in 1990. Breaking Atoms, released on July 23, 1991, received critical acclaim for Large Professor's sample-based production and socially conscious lyrics, with songs such as "Just Hangin' Out", "Looking at the Front Door" and "Peace Is Not the Word to Play". The album earned five mics in The Source magazine's review section in retrospective assessments of the era's landmark records.

Large Professor left the group after Breaking Atoms owing to business disagreements. He was replaced by Queens rapper Mikey D, formerly of Mikey D & The L.A. Posse. The reconstituted group released a second album, Fuck What You Think, in 1994, before disbanding. Large Professor went on to a successful solo and production career, while K-Cut and Sir Scratch remained active in production.
