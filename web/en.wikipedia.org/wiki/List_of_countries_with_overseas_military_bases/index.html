List of countries with overseas military bases

A number of states maintain military bases outside their own territory. The United States operates by far the largest network, with personnel stationed in dozens of countries. Other states with significant overseas basing include the United Kingdom, France, Russia, China, Turkey and India.

History

Overseas basing expanded greatly during the Second World War and the Cold War, when the United States and Soviet Union ringed each other with bases. After 1991 the U.S. network contracted in Europe but expanded in the Middle East and Asia-Pacific. China's first overseas base at Djibouti — a city that also hosts American, French, Japanese and Italian facilities — marked its emergence as a basing power.
