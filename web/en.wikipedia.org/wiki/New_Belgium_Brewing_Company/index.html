New Belgium Brewing Company is a nationally distributed brewery in the United States owned by Kirin/Lion. Founded in 1991 in Fort Collins, Colorado, by Jeff Lebesch and Kim Jordan, it grew out of Lebesch's home-brewing hobby, inspired by a bicycle trip through Belgium's brewing villages.

History and products

The brewery's flagship beer, Fat Tire Amber Ale, named after Lebesch's Belgian bike trip, became one of the best-known craft beers in the United States; the Voodoo Ranger IPA line later became a major seller. New Belgium built a second brewery in Asheville, North Carolina, in 2016. For many years the company was entirely employee-owned through an employee stock ownership plan (ESOP) and was a prominent certified B Corporation.

In 2019, New Belgium was acquired by Lion, a subsidiary of Japan's Kirin Holdings, ending its employee ownership. In 2023 the Fat Tire recipe was reformulated as "Fat Tire Ale" in a widely publicized relaunch.
