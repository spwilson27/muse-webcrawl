Museum ship

A museum ship, also called a memorial ship, is a ship that has been preserved and converted into a museum open to the public for educational or memorial purposes.

Examples include HMS Victory in Portsmouth, USS Constitution in Boston, the Cutty Sark in Greenwich, USS Midway in San Diego, USS Intrepid in New York, and the Vasa in Stockholm — a salvaged 17th-century warship.

Preservation

Keeping large vessels afloat is expensive: hull corrosion, dry-docking and staffing are major challenges, and some museum ships are kept in permanent dry berths or embedded in concrete. Some, such as Constitution, remain in commission and occasionally sail.
