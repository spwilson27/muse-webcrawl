Codrington Library

The Codrington Library is the library of All Souls College, University of Oxford. It is named after Christopher Codrington (1668–1710), a fellow of the college and governor of the Leeward Islands, whose bequest of books and money funded its construction.

Building and collections

The library interior, some 60 metres long, was fitted out over the 18th century and retains its historic bookcases and a sundial by Christopher Wren on the exterior. The library holds strong collections in law and history, and its funding from Codrington's Barbados sugar plantations has made it part of the university's discussions about benefactions linked to slavery.
