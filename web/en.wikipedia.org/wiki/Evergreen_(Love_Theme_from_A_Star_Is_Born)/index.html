Evergreen (Love Theme from A Star Is Born)

"Evergreen", performed by Barbra Streisand in the 1976 film A Star Is Born.

"Evergreen" (also called "Love Theme from A Star Is Born") is a song performed by American singer Barbra Streisand for the 1976 film A Star Is Born, in which she starred opposite Kris Kristofferson. The music was composed by Streisand herself, with lyrics by Paul Williams. It was released as a single from the film's soundtrack album in 1976.

Reception and chart performance

The song became one of Streisand's biggest hits. It reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100 in March 1977, where it remained for three weeks, and was her second number-one single in the United States after "The Way We Were". It also topped the adult contemporary chart and charted internationally, reaching the top ten in several countries.

Awards

"Evergreen" won the Academy Award for Best Original Song at the 49th Academy Awards in 1977, making Streisand the first woman to win the award as a composer. It also won the Golden Globe Award for Best Original Song and the Grammy Award for Song of the Year at the 20th Grammy Awards in 1978, where Streisand additionally won Best Female Pop Vocal Performance for the recording.

Other versions

Williams recorded his own version of the song, and an alternate take with Spanish-language lyrics ("Tema de Amor de Nace una Estrella") was recorded by Streisand for some releases. The song has since been covered by numerous artists and remains a standard in Streisand's concert repertoire.
