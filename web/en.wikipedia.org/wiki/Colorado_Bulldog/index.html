Colorado Bulldog

Colorado Bulldog
Type: Cocktail
Base spirit: Vodka
Ingredients: Vodka, coffee liqueur, cream or milk, cola
Served: On the rocks

The Colorado Bulldog is a cocktail made with vodka, coffee liqueur, cream (or milk), and a splash of cola. It is closely related to the White Russian, differing by the addition of cola and usually being served in a larger glass over ice.

Preparation

A common recipe combines one part vodka and one part coffee liqueur, such as Kahlúa, in a highball or rocks glass filled with ice, adds cream or milk, and tops the drink with cola, which produces a light fizz and slight foaming as the soda meets the dairy. Proportions vary widely between bars.

Relation to other drinks

The drink belongs to a family of vodka–coffee–cream cocktails that includes the White Russian, the Black Russian (without cream), and the Mudslide. The origin of the Colorado Bulldog's name and its association with Colorado are unclear; it gained popularity in American bars in the late twentieth century.
