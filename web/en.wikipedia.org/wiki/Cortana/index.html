Cortana

[Image: Cortana]

Cortana is a virtual assistant developed by Microsoft that uses the Bing search engine to perform tasks such as setting reminders and answering questions for the user. Cortana was first demonstrated at the Microsoft BUILD Developer Conference in April 2014 and was first released as part of Windows Phone 8.1.

History

Cortana was named after the fictional artificial intelligence character in the Halo video game series, who is voiced by Jen Taylor; Taylor also provided the voice for the assistant in several markets. The assistant was integrated into Windows 10 in 2015 and made available on Android, iOS, Xbox One and other platforms.

Features

Cortana could set reminders based on time, places or people, track packages and flights, send emails and texts, manage calendars, and answer natural-language questions using information from Bing. It also integrated with Microsoft services such as Outlook and, through skills, with third-party applications.

Deprecation

Beginning in 2020, Microsoft reduced Cortana's role as a general-purpose assistant, removing it from the Windows taskbar and ending support for the mobile apps. In 2023 Microsoft announced the end of support for the standalone Cortana app in Windows, as the company shifted its AI efforts toward Windows Copilot and Microsoft 365 Copilot.
