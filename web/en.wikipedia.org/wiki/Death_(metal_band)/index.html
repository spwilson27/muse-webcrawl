Death (metal band)

OriginOrlando, Florida, United States
Years active1983–2001
GenreDeath metal, technical death metal
Key figureChuck Schuldiner

Death was an American death metal band formed in Orlando, Florida, in 1983 by guitarist and vocalist Chuck Schuldiner. Widely regarded as one of the founding and most influential acts of the death metal genre, the band evolved from a raw, aggressive sound toward increasingly technical and progressive music across seven studio albums.

History

Originally called Mantas, the group renamed itself Death and released the landmark debut Scream Bloody Gore in 1987, followed by Leprosy (1988) and Spiritual Healing (1990). With Human (1991), Individual Thought Patterns (1993), Symbolic (1995) and The Sound of Perseverance (1998), Schuldiner worked with rotating line-ups of virtuoso musicians and shifted toward technical, melodic composition.

Dissolution and legacy

Schuldiner placed Death on hold to pursue his band Control Denied and died of a brain tumour in December 2001, aged 34, after which Death ceased. The band's influence on extreme metal is immense, and the genre itself is commonly said to take its name in part from the band.
