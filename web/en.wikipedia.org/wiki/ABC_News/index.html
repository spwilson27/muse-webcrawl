ABC News is the news division of the American Broadcasting Company (ABC), owned by Disney. Its programmes include World News Tonight, Good Morning America, Nightline, This Week and 20/20, and its anchors have included Peter Jennings, who fronted World News Tonight from 1983 until his death in 2005.

History

ABC was long the third of the three networks in news resources, but World News Tonight became the top-rated evening newscast in the Jennings era. Nightline grew out of the Iran hostage crisis coverage of 1979–80 under Ted Koppel.

Digital

ABC News runs abcnews.go.com, the ABC News Live streaming channel and ABC Audio; the data-journalism site FiveThirtyEight was part of the division until its closure in 2025.
