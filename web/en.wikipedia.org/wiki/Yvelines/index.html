Yvelines

Yvelines is a department in the Île-de-France region of north-central France. Its prefecture is Versailles, home of the famous Palace of Versailles. It is the westernmost department of the Paris greater metropolitan area.

History

The department was created on 1 January 1968 from the western part of the former Seine-et-Oise department, when Paris-area departments were reorganised. Its name derives from the Yveline forest / water-source region.

Geography

Yvelines is traversed by the Seine in the north and includes the Rambouillet forest in the south. Communes range from dense suburbs such as Saint-Germain-en-Laye and Sartrouville to rural villages. It borders Val-d'Oise, Hauts-de-Seine, Essonne, Eure-et-Loir, and Eure.

Economy and landmarks

Major employers cluster in Saint-Quentin-en-Yvelines and along the Seine (automotive plants at Poissy and Flins). Landmarks include Versailles, the Château de Saint-Germain-en-Laye, and Thoiry zoo.
