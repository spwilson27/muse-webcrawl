Outward Bound

Outward Bound is an international network of outdoor education organisations that run adventure-based courses designed to develop character, leadership and teamwork. It was founded in 1941 in Aberdyfi, Wales, by the educator Kurt Hahn and the shipping magnate Lawrence Holt.

The first school trained young merchant seamen in survival skills during the Second World War; its name comes from the nautical term for a ship leaving harbour. Hahn's philosophy emphasised experiential learning, physical challenge and service to the community.

Today Outward Bound schools operate in more than 30 countries, offering wilderness expeditions, sailing voyages and urban programmes for young people, schools, veterans and corporate groups. Its motto is "To serve, to strive and not to yield."
