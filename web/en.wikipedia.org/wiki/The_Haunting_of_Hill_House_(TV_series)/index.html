The Haunting of Hill House is an American supernatural horror drama television series created and directed by Mike Flanagan for Netflix. Loosely based on Shirley Jackson's 1959 novel of the same name, it premiered on October 12, 2018, as the first entry in The Haunting anthology series.

Plot

The series alternates between two timelines. In 1992, Hugh and Olivia Crain and their five children move into Hill House to renovate and sell it, but mounting paranormal events culminate in a catastrophic night that drives them from the house. Twenty-six years later, the estranged siblings — author Steven, mortician Shirley, psychologist Theo, and twins Nell and Luke — are drawn back together by tragedy and forced to confront what happened. Ghosts such as the Bent-Neck Lady embody the family's grief and trauma, and the sixth episode, "Two Storms", built around extended single-take sequences, was singled out for acclaim.

Reception

The series received widespread critical praise for its acting, direction and emotional depth, and strong audience response. A second, standalone season, The Haunting of Bly Manor, based on Henry James's The Turn of the Screw, followed in 2020. Jackson's novel had previously been filmed as The Haunting (1963).
