Mutsamudu

CountryComoros
IslandAnjouan
Founded1482

Mutsamudu is the second-largest city in the Comoros. Founded in 1482, it is the capital and largest city of the island of Anjouan and also the former home of former Comorian president Ahmed Abdallah Mohamed Sambi. It consists of an ancient medina with stone houses and a citadel.

Geography and features

The city lies on the northwest coast of Anjouan. Its historic centre features narrow streets and buildings influenced by Swahili and Arab architecture, including the old palace area and the Shirashi mosque tradition of the island. Above the town stands an old citadel built to defend against Malagasy slave raiders. The deep-water port of Mutsamudu is the most important port of the Comoros archipelago, handling much of the country's trade.

Climate

Mutsamudu has a tropical climate with a hot, rainy season from roughly November to April and a cooler, drier season during the rest of the year.
