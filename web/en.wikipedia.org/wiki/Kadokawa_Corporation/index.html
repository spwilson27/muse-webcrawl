Kadokawa Corporation (株式会社KADOKAWA) is a Japanese media conglomerate and one of Japan's largest publishers, headquartered in Fujimi, Chiyoda, Tokyo. Founded in 1945 by poet Gen'yoshi Kadokawa as Kadokawa Shoten, it publishes books, manga, magazines, and light novels, and produces anime and films.

History

Under Gen'yoshi's son Haruki Kadokawa, the company expanded aggressively into film in the 1970s with the slogan "read, then watch — or watch, then read." After corporate restructuring, numerous publishing imprints — including ASCII Media Works, Enterbrain (publisher of Famitsu), Media Factory, and Fujimi Shobo — were consolidated under Kadokawa. In 2014 Kadokawa merged with Dwango, operator of the video platform Niconico, forming Kadokawa Dwango before reverting to the Kadokawa name in 2019.

Business

Kadokawa is a major force in anime production and the light-novel market, with franchises including Sword Art Online, Re:Zero, Neon Genesis Evangelion (manga/publishing), and The Melancholy of Haruhi Suzumiya. It holds a majority stake in video game developer FromSoftware (Elden Ring, Dark Souls). In 2024 Sony became Kadokawa's largest shareholder with a roughly 10% stake under a strategic alliance, after reports of a possible acquisition.
