Vikings–Packers rivalry

The Vikings–Packers rivalry is a National Football League (NFL) rivalry between the Minnesota Vikings and the Green Bay Packers. The teams first met in 1961, the Vikings' inaugural season, and have played twice annually since as members of the same division — the NFC North (formerly the NFC Central). It is considered one of the most intense rivalries in the NFL, fueled by the proximity of Minnesota and Wisconsin and decades of battles for division titles.

History

Green Bay dominated the early years under Vince Lombardi, while Minnesota's "Purple People Eaters" teams of the late 1960s and 1970s reversed the balance. The rivalry produced many memorable moments, including Antonio Freeman's improbable overtime catch on Monday Night Football in 2000 — prompting Al Michaels' famous "He did what?" call — and Randy Moss's infamous "moon" celebration at Lambeau Field during the Vikings' wild-card playoff win in January 2005.

Brett Favre

The rivalry took a dramatic turn in 2009 when quarterback Brett Favre, who had led Green Bay for 16 seasons and won a Super Bowl there, signed with Minnesota after a season with the New York Jets. Favre led the Vikings to a division title and victories in both meetings with the Packers that year, intensifying the feud considerably.

Overall

The Packers lead the all-time series, which has included two postseason meetings (split 1–1). The teams have combined for numerous division championships, and their games regularly feature some of the league's most hostile road crowds.
