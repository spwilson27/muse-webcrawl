Beaumont is a city in the U.S. state of Texas. It is the county seat of Jefferson County, on the Neches River about 85 miles (137 km) east of Houston. With a population of 115,282 at the 2020 census, Beaumont is one of the largest cities in the Golden Triangle, a major industrial area on the Texas Gulf Coast formed with Port Arthur and Orange. The city forms part of the Beaumont–Port Arthur metropolitan statistical area.

History

Beaumont was founded in 1835 on land purchased by Joseph Pulsifer and was incorporated in 1838. The early economy rested on lumber, rice farming, and cattle raising, served by the port on the Neches River and later by railroads. The city's destiny changed on January 10, 1901, when the Lucas Gusher blew in at Spindletop, a salt dome south of the city. The well produced an estimated 100,000 barrels of oil per day at its peak, launching the modern petroleum industry and transforming Beaumont into a boomtown; companies founded or expanded in its wake included Texaco and Gulf Oil.

Economy

Petrochemicals and refining dominate Beaumont's economy. The Port of Beaumont is one of the busiest in the United States by tonnage and is a major military shipping port. ExxonMobil operates a large refinery and chemical complex in the city. Lamar University, founded in 1923, is a major educational institution and employer, and the city serves as a regional center for health care, retail, and shipping.

Geography and climate

Beaumont sits on the flat coastal plain of Southeast Texas, just inland from the Gulf of Mexico, at an elevation of about 16 feet. The Neches River forms the city's eastern boundary. The climate is humid subtropical, with hot, humid summers and mild winters; the area is prone to hurricanes and flooding, having been struck by Hurricane Rita (2005) and inundated by Hurricane Harvey (2017), which caused catastrophic flooding in the region.

Demographics

At the 2020 census, Beaumont's population was 115,282. The city has a large African American community, comprising roughly half the population, alongside significant white and growing Hispanic populations. Beaumont is part of the historical heartland of Cajun and Creole culture in Texas, reflected in its cuisine and music traditions.
