Chicago Fire (TV series)

Chicago Fire is an American drama television series created by Michael Brandt and Derek Haas, with Dick Wolf as an executive producer. It is the first instalment of Wolf Entertainment's Chicago franchise, which includes Chicago P.D., Chicago Med and Chicago Justice. The series follows the professional and personal lives of firefighters, rescue personnel and paramedics of the Chicago Fire Department at the fictional Firehouse 51. It premiered on NBC on October 10, 2012.

Premise

The series centres on the firefighters of Engine Company 51, Truck Company 81, Rescue Squad Company 3, Battalion 25 and Ambulance 61, based at Firehouse 51. Led for most of the series by Battalion Chief Wallace Boden, the crews respond to fires, rescues and medical emergencies across Chicago while dealing with personal struggles, relationships and losses. Prominent long-running characters include Matthew Casey, Kelly Severide, Gabriela Dawson, Sylvie Brett and Christopher Herrmann.

Production

The series is filmed in Chicago. Exterior shots of Firehouse 51 use a real Chicago fire station, Engine 18 on the Near West Side, while interior scenes are filmed at Cinespace Chicago Film Studios. Real Chicago firefighters have appeared as extras, and the show consults with the fire department for technical accuracy.

Reception and franchise

Chicago Fire has been a ratings staple for NBC's Wednesday night lineup and spawned a shared franchise collectively branded "One Chicago". Its success led to the police procedural Chicago P.D. (2014), the medical drama Chicago Med (2015) and the short-lived legal drama Chicago Justice (2017), with frequent crossover episodes between the series.
