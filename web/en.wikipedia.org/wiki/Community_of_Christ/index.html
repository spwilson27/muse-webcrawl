Community of Christ

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Community of Christ, known from 1872 to 2001 as the Reorganized Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints (RLDS Church), is an international Christian denomination headquartered in Independence, Missouri, United States. It is the second-largest church in the Latter Day Saint movement, tracing its origins to the church founded by Joseph Smith in 1830, and it reports membership of roughly a quarter of a million in more than fifty countries.

History

After the death of Joseph Smith in 1844, the Latter Day Saint movement divided. Groups of Saints who did not follow Brigham Young westward eventually "reorganized" in 1860 under the leadership of Smith's son, Joseph Smith III, who served as president-prophet for over fifty years. The church's headquarters were later established at Independence, Missouri, site of the Temple Lot. Presidents descended from Joseph Smith led the church until 1996, when Wallace B. Smith designated W. Grant McMurray, the first president not of Smith descent.

Beliefs and practices

Community of Christ uses the Bible and the Book of Mormon as scripture and also accepts the Doctrine and Covenants, which continues to receive additions presented by the church's prophet-president. It is Trinitarian in its statement of faith, practises baptism by immersion for those aged eight and older, and ordains women to priesthood offices — a change made in 1984 that prompted a schism by some traditionalist members. The church has become known for a peace-and-justice orientation, and its best-known building is the Independence Temple, dedicated in 1994 "to the pursuit of peace", recognisable by its spiral shell-like spire. The church's world headquarters also include the historic Auditorium in Independence and Graceland University in Lamoni, Iowa, which it sponsors.

Name change

The church adopted the name Community of Christ in 2001, seeking a name that expressed its mission and identity and distinguished it from the larger Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints (LDS Church), with which it is not affiliated.
