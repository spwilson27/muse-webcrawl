BHIM (Bharat Interface for Money) is a mobile payment application developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) and based on the Unified Payments Interface (UPI). It was launched by Prime Minister Narendra Modi on 30 December 2016 at a Digi-Dhan Mela event in New Delhi, as part of the push towards digital payments that followed the 2016 demonetisation. The app is named in honour of B. R. Ambedkar, the jurist and economist who chaired the drafting committee of the Indian Constitution.

Features

BHIM lets users send and request money instantly between bank accounts using a virtual payment address, a mobile number, or a QR code, without sharing account details. A single app can be linked to several bank accounts, and transfers settle immediately at any hour. The app has been offered in numerous Indian languages, and related services include BHIM Aadhaar Pay, which allows merchants to accept payments authenticated by a customer's fingerprint, and USSD-based access for phones without internet connections.

Development and adoption

As the reference UPI application built by the platform's own operator, BHIM helped popularise UPI in its early years, though third-party apps such as PhonePe and Google Pay later took the larger shares of transaction volume. Version 2.0, released in 2019, added features including donation and IPO options and expanded language support. UPI itself grew into one of the world's largest real-time payment systems, processing billions of transactions per month.
