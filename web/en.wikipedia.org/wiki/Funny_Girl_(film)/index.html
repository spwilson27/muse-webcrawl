Funny Girl is a 1968 American biographical musical film directed by William Wyler, based on the 1964 Broadway musical of the same name. It stars Barbra Streisand, reprising her stage role, as the comedian and Ziegfeld Follies star Fanny Brice, and Omar Sharif as her husband, gambler Nick Arnstein.

Plot and production

The film traces Brice's rise from the Lower East Side to Broadway stardom and the rise and collapse of her marriage to Arnstein. Songs by Jule Styne and Bob Merrill include "People", "Don't Rain on My Parade" and "I'd Rather Be Blue over You". The film was a major commercial success and Columbia Pictures' top earner of its year.

Reception

Streisand won the Academy Award for Best Actress (in a famous tie with Katharine Hepburn for The Lion in Winter) for her film debut. The film received eight Oscar nominations and was followed by a sequel, Funny Lady (1975). In 2016 it was selected for preservation in the United States National Film Registry.
