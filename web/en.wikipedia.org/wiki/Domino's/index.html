Domino's Pizza, Inc., trading as Domino's, is an American multinational pizza restaurant chain. Founded in 1960, it is one of the largest pizza companies in the world by sales and store count, operating a heavily franchised network of more than 20,000 stores in over 90 markets.

History

In 1960 Tom and James Monaghan bought DomiNick's, a small pizza shop in Ypsilanti, Michigan. James traded his half of the business to Tom for a Volkswagen Beetle within a year. By 1965 Tom Monaghan had three stores and adopted the name Domino's Pizza; the three dots on the logo originally represented those three stores. The company's promise of delivery within 30 minutes, introduced in the 1970s, became famous (and was later softened amid safety concerns). Bain Capital bought the company from Monaghan in 1998, and Domino's went public on the New York Stock Exchange in 2004.

Business and technology

Domino's has positioned itself as a technology company as much as a pizza chain: a majority of orders in many markets arrive through digital channels, supported by the Pizza Tracker, voice and app ordering, and experiments with GPS delivery tracking, drones and autonomous vehicles. In 2013 the UK and Ireland business introduced the "Domino's" branding without "Pizza", later adopted globally. India, served through master franchisee Jubilant FoodWorks, is Domino's largest market outside the United States by store count.
