Omnicom Group Inc. is an American global media, marketing and corporate communications holding company headquartered in New York City. It is one of the world's largest advertising groups, employing around 70,000 people in more than 100 countries. Its networks include the creative agency networks BBDO, DDB and TBWA, the media network Omnicom Media Group (OMG) and the Diversified Agency Services group.

History

Omnicom was formed in 1986 from the merger of the BBDO, Doyle Dane Bernbach (DDB) and Needham Harper agency groups — a pioneering "big bang" merger of holding companies. Under long-serving CEO John Wren (from 1997) it grew by acquisition into one of the "big four" agency groups alongside WPP, Publicis and Interpublic. In 2013 Omnicom and Publicis announced a merger of equals that was abandoned in 2014. In 2024 Omnicom agreed to acquire Interpublic Group in a deal designed to create the world's largest advertising company.

Operations

Omnicom's agencies provide advertising, strategic media planning and buying, digital marketing, public relations, customer relationship management, branding and specialist communications for thousands of clients worldwide.
