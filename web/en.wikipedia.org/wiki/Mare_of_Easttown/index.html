Mare of Easttown is an American crime drama limited series created by Brad Ingelsby and broadcast by HBO in 2021. Kate Winslet stars as Mare Sheehan, a police detective in the fictional Delaware County suburb of Easttown, Pennsylvania, who investigates the murder of a young mother while dealing with the aftermath of her son's suicide and a custody battle over her grandson.

Plot

Mare is under pressure for failing to solve the disappearance of a local girl a year earlier. When teenage mother Erin McMenamin is found shot dead, Mare is paired with county detective Colin Zabel (Evan Peters). The investigation exposes secrets across the close-knit community, and the killer is ultimately revealed within Mare's own circle of family and friends.

Reception

The series was widely acclaimed, particularly for Winslet's performance and its authentic depiction of the Philadelphia suburbs' Delco accent and culture. It won four Primetime Emmy Awards, including Outstanding Lead Actress for Winslet, Supporting Actress for Julianne Nicholson and Supporting Actor for Peters.
