Rangers Football Club is a professional football club in Glasgow, Scotland, playing in the Scottish Premiership. Founded in 1872 by four teenage boys, Rangers is one of the two giants of Scottish football, its rivalry with Celtic — the Old Firm derby — being one of the most famous in world football. The club plays at Ibrox Stadium in the Govan district of Glasgow.

History

Rangers were founder members of the Scottish Football League in 1890 and won the first championship, shared with Dumbarton. The club went on to win a world-record haul of league titles — 55 Scottish championships — along with dozens of Scottish Cups and League Cups. In Europe, Rangers won the European Cup Winners' Cup in 1972, defeating Dynamo Moscow in Barcelona, and reached the UEFA Cup final in 2008 and the Europa League final in 2022.

Liquidation and return

In 2012, financial collapse forced the club's holding company into administration and then liquidation; a new company acquired the club's assets, and Rangers were admitted to the fourth tier of Scottish football. The club climbed back through the divisions, returning to the top flight in 2016 and winning its 55th league title, unbeaten, in 2021 under Steven Gerrard. Rangers' honours total remains among the largest of any club in world football.
