Roadshow

A roadshow is a travelling presentation or exhibition that tours multiple locations. The term is used in several fields, including entertainment, business and finance.

Entertainment

In theatre and film, a roadshow originally meant a touring production. In mid-20th-century cinema, the roadshow theatrical release was a prestige, limited-engagement presentation of a major film with reserved seating and an intermission. Travelling shows such as the Antiques Roadshow television format later popularised the term for appraisal events visiting town after town.

Business and finance

In finance, a roadshow is a series of meetings in which executives of a company issuing securities present their investment case to potential institutional investors in different cities, typically ahead of an initial public offering (IPO). In marketing, companies run product roadshows — mobile demonstrations and exhibitions — to reach customers and distributors across a region.

Other uses

Political campaigns, technology launches and educational programmes also use roadshows as a format: a self-contained presentation that can be set up quickly in successive venues.
