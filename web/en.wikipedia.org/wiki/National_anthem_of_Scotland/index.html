National anthem of Scotland

Scotland has no official national anthem. A number of songs are used as unofficial anthems, most commonly "Flower of Scotland", "Scotland the Brave" and "Scots Wha Hae".

Current position

At most international sporting events Scotland uses "Flower of Scotland" as its anthem. Written by Roy Williamson of The Corries in 1965, it commemorates the victory of Robert the Bruce over Edward II at Bannockburn in 1314. It was adopted by the Scottish Rugby Union in 1990 and by the Scotland football team in 1997.

"God Save the King" is the anthem of the United Kingdom as a whole. In 2006 the Scottish Parliament debated a motion on an official anthem but took no decision. Other candidates include "A Man's a Man for A' That" by Robert Burns.
