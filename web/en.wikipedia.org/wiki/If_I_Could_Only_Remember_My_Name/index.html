If I Could Only Remember My Name

If I Could Only Remember My Name is the debut solo album by American singer-songwriter David Crosby, released on February 22, 1971, by Atlantic Records. Recorded during the height of Crosby, Stills, Nash & Young's fame, it features an extraordinary ensemble drawn from the San Francisco and Los Angeles rock scenes — members of the Grateful Dead, Jefferson Airplane, Santana, and Crosby's bandmates Graham Nash, Neil Young, and Joni Mitchell. Initially dismissed by many critics, the album has since been reappraised as a masterpiece of atmospheric folk-rock.

Background and recording

Crosby recorded the album while grieving the death of his girlfriend Christine Hinton in a 1969 car accident. Sessions at Wally Heider Studios brought together an informal collective sometimes called the Planet Earth Rock and Roll Orchestra. Jerry Garcia contributed pedal steel guitar and played on several tracks, Phil Lesh played bass, Bill Kreutzmann and Mickey Hart drummed, Grace Slick and Paul Kantner sang and played, and Joni Mitchell added vocals. Much of the music grew from loose, improvisational jamming shaped around Crosby's alternate guitar tunings and wordless vocal harmonies.

Music

Tracks include the chant-like "Music Is Love," the elegiac "Cowboy Movie" — a thinly veiled allegory of CSNY's internal conflicts featuring Garcia's guitar — "Tamalpais High (At About 3)," the harmonically dense "Laughing," the a cappella-inflected "I'd Swear There Was Somebody Here," recorded as a requiem for Hinton, and the closing, hymn-like "Silence Is" fragments. The album's sound is defined by layered vocals, open tunings, and a drifting, melancholic atmosphere.

Reception and legacy

Upon release the album reached the top 20 of the Billboard 200 but received hostile reviews, including a famous dismissal in Rolling Stone. Over subsequent decades its reputation rose sharply: it has appeared in numerous greatest-albums lists, Mojo and other publications have hailed it as a lost classic, and it strongly influenced later folk and "freak folk" artists. Crosby did not release another solo studio album until Oh Yes I Can in 1989.
