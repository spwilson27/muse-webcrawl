List of ethnic groups in China

The People's Republic of China officially recognises 56 ethnic groups (minzu), comprising the Han Chinese majority and 55 minority groups. The Han make up roughly 91 per cent of the population; the recognised minorities together number over 125 million people.

Other recognised groups include the Kazakh, Dai, Li, Lisu, She, Lahu, Va (Wa), Shui, Naxi, Qiang, Tu, Xibe, Kyrgyz, Daur, Jingpo, Salar, Blang, Maonan, Tajik, Pumi, Achang, Nu, Ewenki, Jino, De'ang, Uzbek, Russian, Yugur, Bonan, Monba, Oroqen, Derung, Hezhen, Gaoshan (Taiwanese indigenous peoples), Lhoba and Tatar. The smallest recognised group, the Lhoba, numbers only a few thousand.
