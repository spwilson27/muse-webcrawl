Phantasy Star is a role-playing video game developed and published by Sega for the Master System. Released in Japan in December 1987 and internationally in 1988, it is the first entry in the Phantasy Star series and one of the pioneering console role-playing games.

Story and gameplay

The game is set in the Algol planetary system, whose worlds are ruled by the tyrant King Lassic. The heroine, Alis Landale, swears revenge after her brother Nero is killed by Lassic's robot police, and journeys across the three planets — Palma, Motavia and Dezoris — gathering allies including the musk-cat Myau, the warrior Odin and the esper Noah. The game blends science fiction and fantasy, and features turn-based combat, first-person 3D maze dungeons that were graphically advanced for their time, and vehicles for crossing planetary terrain. It was notable for having a female protagonist, still rare in the genre, and for its large cartridge and battery-backed save system.

Legacy

Phantasy Star was a critical and commercial success and launched a series of sequels for the Genesis/Mega Drive, culminating in Phantasy Star IV: The End of the Millennium (1993), and later the influential online spin-offs beginning with Phantasy Star Online (2000). The original is frequently listed among the greatest video games of all time and has been re-released in numerous compilations and on modern platforms.
