Genstat

Genstat (General Statistics) is a comprehensive statistical software package developed originally at Rothamsted Research in the United Kingdom and now produced by VSN International. It is particularly noted for its strength in the design and analysis of experiments, especially in agriculture and the biological sciences.

History

Genstat originated in the 1960s through the work of John Nelder and colleagues at Rothamsted Experimental Station. Nelder also led the development of the related GLIM system for generalized linear models; the generalized linear model framework itself was formalized by Nelder and Robert Wedderburn in 1972. Genstat's own command language gives expert users fine control over analyses, complemented by graphical menus for routine work.

Features

The package covers data management, linear and generalized linear mixed models, analysis of variance for complex designs, REML estimation, multivariate analysis, time series, spatial statistics, and graphics. It is widely used by research institutes and universities for analyzing field trials and laboratory experiments, and a free discovery edition targets teaching and small datasets.
