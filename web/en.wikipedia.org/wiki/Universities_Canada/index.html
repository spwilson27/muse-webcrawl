Universities Canada

Universities Canada (French: Universités Canada) is the national association representing Canadian universities. Founded in 1911 as the Association of Universities and Colleges of Canada, it adopted its present name in 2015.

Role

The organization advocates for member institutions with the federal government, promotes research and international mobility, administers scholarship programs, and provides a forum for university presidents. Membership includes nearly 100 universities and university-level colleges across Canada.

History

It grew out of a 1911 conference of Canadian universities and played roles in wartime mobilization, postwar expansion of higher education and the development of national research funding.
