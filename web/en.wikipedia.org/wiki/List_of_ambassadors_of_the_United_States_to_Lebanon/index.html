List of ambassadors of the United States to Lebanon

The United States recognized Lebanon in 1944 and established a legation in Beirut, raised to an embassy in 1952. The ambassador is the chief of mission at the U.S. Embassy in Awkar, north of Beirut. The mission was closed between 1989 and 1990 during the Lebanese Civil War, and Ambassador John McCarthy was evacuated; relations were later restored.
