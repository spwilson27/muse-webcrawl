Rason

Rason (Korean: 라선특별시) is a North Korean special city and ice-free port on the country's northeast coast, in North Hamgyong Province. It was formed by the merger of the neighbouring cities of Rajin and Chŏngjin's Sonbong district — the name combines the first syllables of Rajin and Sonbong. It borders China's Jilin province and Russia's Primorsky Krai.

Special economic zone

In 1991 the area was designated the Rajin-Sonbong Economic Special Zone, North Korea's first special economic zone, intended to attract foreign investment with its ice-free deep-water port and its position at the junction of the Chinese, Russian, and North Korean borders. Development has been limited but has included a Chinese-built road and pier facilities at Rajin port, a Russian rail link and coal terminal, casinos and hotels catering to Chinese visitors, and reported experiments with markets and mobile phone coverage.

Geography and transport

Rason lies on the Sea of Japan (East Sea of Korea). The port of Rajin remains ice-free year-round, a rarity on Korea's east coast. A railway connects Tumangang, on the Russian border, to the Trans-Siberian network via Khasan, and Chinese investors have upgraded road links to Hunchun in Jilin.
