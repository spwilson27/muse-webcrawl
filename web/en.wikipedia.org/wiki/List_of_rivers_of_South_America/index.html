List of rivers of South America

South America is drained by three giant systems: the Amazon, the largest river in the world by discharge; the Orinoco; and the Río de la Plata system, formed by the Paraná and Paraguay rivers. Remarkably, the Amazon and Orinoco are connected by the natural Casiquiare canal.
