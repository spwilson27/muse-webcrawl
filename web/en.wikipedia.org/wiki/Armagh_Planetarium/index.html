Armagh Planetarium

Armagh Planetarium is a planetarium and astronomy education centre in Armagh, Northern Ireland, located close to the Armagh Observatory. Together the two institutions form Armagh Observatory and Planetarium, which is funded by the Department for Communities.

History

The planetarium was founded by Dr Eric Lindsay, director of the observatory, and opened in 1968. It is the oldest operating planetarium in Ireland. The building houses a digital theatre, exhibition areas displaying meteorites, models of spacecraft and interactive displays about the Solar System and beyond.

Activities

The centre runs school programmes, public shows and special events, including telescope evenings and exhibitions on space exploration. It sits within the observatory's landscaped grounds, which include an astropark with scale models of the Solar System and a human orrery.
