Mors Kochanski

Mors Kochanski (November 10, 1940 – December 5, 2019) was a Canadian bushcraft and wilderness survival instructor, naturalist and author. He acquired an international reputation for his lifelong work in outdoor education and wilderness living skills, particularly in the boreal forest of northern Canada.

Life and work

Kochanski was born in Saskatchewan and raised in Prince Albert, of Polish immigrant parentage. He briefly studied at university, intending to become a naval architect, before devoting himself to outdoor education. He taught for decades through the University of Alberta, the Devonian Botanic Garden, and his own courses, and contributed to outdoor programming for military, cadet and search-and-rescue organisations.

Bushcraft

His 1987 book Northern Bush Craft (expanded from the earlier Bush Craft, 1977) became a classic of the field, covering knife use, firecraft, shelter, cordage, edible and medicinal plants, and winter travel in the northern forest. Kochanski popularised the axiom "the more you know, the less you carry", emphasising knowledge and skill over heavy equipment. He also promoted the try stick as a teaching tool for knife skills and the "super shelter" — a plastic-sheet shelter warmed by a long fire.

Kochanski influenced later survival instructors internationally and advised television productions; he died in 2019 at the age of 79.
