Reno–Tahoe International Airport

Reno–Tahoe International Airport (IATA: RNO) is a public and military airport located three miles southeast of downtown Reno, Nevada, United States. It is the second-busiest commercial airport in Nevada after Harry Reid International Airport in Las Vegas, and serves the Reno–Sparks area and the Lake Tahoe region.

The airport, operated by the Reno-Tahoe Airport Authority, has three runways and two concourses. It was built in 1929 and named Hubbard Field; it also hosts the Nevada Air National Guard's 152nd Airlift Wing. Airlines including Southwest, American, Delta and United offer flights to major western and central US hubs.
