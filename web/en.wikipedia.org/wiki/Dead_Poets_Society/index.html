Dead Poets Society
Directed by: Peter Weir
Written by: Tom Schulman
Starring: Robin Williams, Robert Sean Leonard, Ethan Hawke
Released: 1989
Runtime: 128 minutes

Dead Poets Society is a 1989 American coming-of-age drama film directed by Peter Weir and written by Tom Schulman. Robin Williams stars as John Keating, an unorthodox English teacher at an elite, tradition-bound boys' preparatory school in Vermont in 1959, who inspires his students through poetry to think for themselves and "seize the day" (carpe diem).

Plot

At Welton Academy, Keating encourages his students to revive the "Dead Poets Society", a secret club meeting in a cave to read poetry. The students — including shy Todd Anderson (Ethan Hawke) and Neil Perry (Robert Sean Leonard), who defies his domineering father to act in a school play — are transformed, but Neil's suicide after his father forbids him to act leads the school to scapegoat Keating, who is dismissed. In the famous final scene, the students stand on their desks to salute him with Whitman's line "O Captain! My Captain!".

Reception

The film was a commercial and critical success, grossing over $235 million worldwide. Schulman's screenplay won the Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay, and the film was nominated for Best Picture, Best Director and Best Actor. It has remained culturally influential, popularising the carpe diem theme for a generation of viewers, though some critics have questioned its sentimentality and its portrayal of Keating's teaching.
