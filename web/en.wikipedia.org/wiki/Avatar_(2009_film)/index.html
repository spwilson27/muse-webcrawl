Avatar (2009 film)

Avatar is a 2009 epic science fiction film written and directed by James Cameron, starring Sam Worthington, Zoe Saldaña, Stephen Lang, Michelle Rodriguez and Sigourney Weaver. Set in 2154 on Pandora, a lush habitable moon of a gas giant in the Alpha Centauri system, it follows a paraplegic Marine, Jake Sully, who operates a genetically engineered Na'vi body — an "avatar" — and is torn between his mission for a mining corporation and the indigenous Na'vi people. Avatar is the highest-grossing film of all time, with a worldwide gross of over $2.9 billion including reissues.

Plot

On resource-depleted Earth, the RDA corporation mines the valuable mineral unobtanium on Pandora, whose atmosphere is toxic to humans. Jake Sully replaces his deceased twin brother in the Avatar Program, remotely piloting a Na'vi hybrid body. Accepted into the Omatikaya clan through the chief's daughter Neytiri, Jake learns their ways and falls in love with her. When the RDA destroys the clan's Hometree to reach the unobtanium beneath it, Jake turns against the corporation and leads the Na'vi clans in a victorious defence of the sacred Tree of Souls; at the end he permanently transfers his consciousness into his avatar.

Production

Cameron wrote an 80-page treatment in 1994 but waited until visual-effects technology could realise his vision. Filming combined live action with performance capture at Weta Digital in New Zealand; Cameron co-developed a new generation of 3D cameras and virtual-camera techniques that let him view the digital world while directing actors. The Na'vi language was constructed by linguist Paul Frommer.

Reception and legacy

The film was praised for its groundbreaking 3D spectacle and visual effects, and it won three Academy Awards (Best Cinematography, Best Production Design, Best Visual Effects) from nine nominations. It surpassed Cameron's own Titanic as the highest-grossing film ever and reclaimed the record after a 2021 re-release in China briefly displaced Avengers: Endgame. Commentators discussed its environmental message and its echoes of colonialism and earlier stories. A series of sequels followed, beginning with Avatar: The Way of Water (2022).
