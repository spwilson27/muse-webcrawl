Flores is the capital of the Petén Department in Guatemala's northern lowlands. The older part of the city is located on an island on Lake Petén Itzá, connected to the mainland suburbs of Santa Elena and San Benito by a causeway. It is the main gateway for visitors to the Maya ruins of Tikal.

History

The island was the site of Nojpetén (Tayasal), capital of the Itza Maya kingdom — the last independent Maya state, which did not fall to the Spanish until 1697, more than 150 years after the conquest of the rest of Guatemala. The Spanish founded the modern town on the site and named it Flores in honour of Cirilo Flores.

Tourism

Colourful colonial-style streets, lakeside restaurants and hotels make Flores a popular base for trips to Tikal, Yaxhá and other Petén archaeological sites. Mundo Maya International Airport serves the city.
