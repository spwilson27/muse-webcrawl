Cork Airport

Cork Airport (Irish: Aerfort Chorcaí) (IATA: ORK, ICAO: EICK) is the second-largest of the three principal international airports in Ireland, after Dublin and ahead of Shannon. It is located 6.5 km (4.0 mi) south of Cork city centre in an area known as Ballygarvan. The airport is owned and operated by DAA.

History

Plans for an airport in Cork began in the 1950s, and Cork Airport was officially opened on 16 October 1961. A new terminal building opened in 2006, significantly increasing capacity. The airport serves destinations across Ireland, the United Kingdom and continental Europe, with airlines including Aer Lingus and Ryanair, and handles roughly two and a half to three million passengers a year in recent years.

Facilities

The airport has a single passenger terminal and two asphalt runways. It serves the wider Munster region as a gateway for tourism and business.
