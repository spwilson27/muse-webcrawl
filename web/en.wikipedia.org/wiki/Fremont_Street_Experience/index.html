The Fremont Street Experience (FSE) is a pedestrian mall and attraction occupying the westernmost five blocks of Fremont Street in downtown Las Vegas, Nevada, including the area known for decades as "Glitter Gulch". Its centrepiece is the Viva Vision canopy, a barrel-vaulted LED display stretching about 1,375 feet (419 metres) above the street, on which light-and-sound shows are presented nightly.

History

Fremont Street was Las Vegas's original casino district, home to landmark properties such as Binion's Horseshoe, the Golden Nugget, the Pioneer Club (with its neon cowboy "Vegas Vic") and the Fremont Hotel. As resort development shifted to the Las Vegas Strip, downtown declined. The Fremont Street Experience — a partnership of downtown casino owners with the city — closed the street to traffic and covered it with a light canopy, opening on 14 December 1995 at a cost of roughly US$70 million, in an effort to draw visitors back downtown.

The original canopy, lit by millions of bulbs, was replaced in 2004 by the LED-based Viva Vision screen, which was comprehensively upgraded in a renovation completed for New Year's Eve 2019 with a much brighter, higher-resolution display of tens of millions of LEDs. Free concerts are held on stages at either end of the mall, and the SlotZilla zip line (opened 2014) carries riders beneath the canopy, including a longer "zoomline" launching from a 114-foot tower styled as a giant slot machine.
