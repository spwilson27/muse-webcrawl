University: University of Colorado Boulder
Conference: Big 12
Location: Boulder, Colorado
Mascots: Ralphie (live buffalo), Chip

The Colorado Buffaloes are the intercollegiate athletic teams representing the University of Colorado Boulder. They compete in NCAA Division I, primarily in the Big 12 Conference, which Colorado rejoined in 2024 after a period in the Pac-12 (2011–2024) and earlier Big 12 / Big Eight membership. The school fields varsity teams in sports including football, men's and women's basketball, cross country, skiing, track, volleyball, soccer and lacrosse.

Football

Colorado football plays at Folsom Field and won a share of the national championship in 1990 under coach Bill McCartney. The live buffalo mascot Ralphie traditionally runs the field before games. Colorado revived national attention under coach Deion Sanders from 2023, with stars including Shedeur Sanders and Travis Hunter, who won the Heisman Trophy in 2024.

Other sports

Colorado is a powerhouse in skiing and cross country, with numerous NCAA team titles, and its men's and women's basketball teams play at the CU Events Center.
