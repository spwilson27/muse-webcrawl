National parks of Wales

Wales has three national parks, covering about 20% of its land area: Snowdonia (Eryri, designated 1951), Pembrokeshire Coast (1952), and Brecon Beacons (Bannau Brycheiniog, 1957).

Parks

Snowdonia (2,170 km²) includes Yr Wyddfa (Snowdon), the highest mountain in Wales at 1,085 m. Pembrokeshire Coast is the only predominantly coastal national park in the UK. Brecon Beacons includes Pen y Fan (886 m) and was renamed Bannau Brycheiniog in 2023. The parks are managed by individual park authorities for conservation and public enjoyment.
