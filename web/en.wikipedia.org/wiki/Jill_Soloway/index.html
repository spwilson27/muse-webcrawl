Jill Soloway

Jill Soloway (born September 26, 1965) is an American television creator, writer and director. They are best known as the creator of the Amazon series Transparent (2014–2019), about a Los Angeles family whose parent comes out as transgender, and for their work on Six Feet Under and United States of Tara. Soloway is nonbinary and uses they/them pronouns.

Jill Soloway, creator of Transparent.

Career

Soloway began in Chicago theatre and comedy before moving to television writing, joining the staff of Six Feet Under in its later seasons. They co-created United States of Tara (2009–2011) with Diablo Cody. Transparent, inspired in part by their own parent's coming out, won multiple Emmy and Golden Globe awards, including Outstanding Directing for a Comedy Series for Soloway, and was praised for bringing transgender stories and performers to mainstream television.

Other work

Soloway directed the feature film Afternoon Delight (2013), which won the directing award at the Sundance Film Festival. Their memoir, She Wants It: Desire, Power, and Toppling the Patriarchy (2018), discusses their career, gender identity and the #MeToo movement. They have also been an advocate for inclusive hiring practices in the entertainment industry.
