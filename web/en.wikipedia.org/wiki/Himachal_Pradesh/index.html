Himachal Pradesh

Himachal Pradesh is a state in northern India, situated in the western Himalayas. Its capital is Shimla, and Dharamshala is its winter capital. The name, coined by Acharya Diwakar Datt Sharma, means "snow-laden province" in Sanskrit.

Geography

Covering 55,673 km², the mountainous state ranges from the Shivalik hills to the Greater Himalayas, with rivers including the Chenab, Ravi, Beas, Sutlej, and Yamuna. It is known for hill stations such as Manali, Kullu, Dalhousie, and Chamba.

Economy

Hydroelectric power, tourism, horticulture — especially apples — and agriculture are mainstays. Himachal is one of India's most literate and prosperous states per capita.

History

Formed after independence from princely states and Punjab hill areas, it became a full state on 25 January 1971.
