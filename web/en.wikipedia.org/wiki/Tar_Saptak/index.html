MovementPrayogvad (experimentalism) in Hindi poetry

Tar Saptak (Hindi: तार सप्तक, "High Octave" / "Stringed Saptak") is a landmark anthology of Hindi poetry published in 1943, edited by Agyeya (Sachchidananda Hirananda Vatsyayan). It collected the work of seven young poets and launched the experimentalist movement known as Prayogvad in modern Hindi literature.

Poets included

The anthology featured Gajanan Madhav Muktibodh, Nemichandra Jain, Bharat Bhushan Agrawal, Prabhakar Machwe, Girija Kumar Mathur, Ramvilas Sharma, and Agyeya himself. Their poems broke with the romantic Chhayavad tradition, engaging urban life, psychology, and intellectual complexity in freer forms.

Legacy

Two further sequels, Dusra Saptak (1951) and Teesra Saptak (1959), each presenting seven more poets, extended the movement, which evolved into the Nai Kavita ("New Poetry") of post-independence Hindi letters.
