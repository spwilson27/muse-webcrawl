Broken Bells is an American indie rock duo formed in Los Angeles in 2009, consisting of artist-producer Brian Burton, better known as Danger Mouse, and James Mercer, lead vocalist and guitarist of the Shins. The project pairs Mercer's melodic songwriting and falsetto with Burton's atmospheric, sample-informed production, producing a sound often described as spacey, melancholic indie pop.

Background and debut

Mercer and Burton met at the Roskilde Festival in Denmark in 2004, where they discovered a mutual admiration, and began collaborating in Los Angeles in 2008, writing in secret without a record deal. Their self-titled debut album, Broken Bells, was released in March 2010 to positive reviews, debuting in the US top ten; the single "The High Road" topped the Billboard Alternative Songs chart. The album's artwork, by artist Jacob Escobedo, features the duo's signature pink orb motif. An EP, Meyrin Fields, followed in 2011.

Later work

The duo's second album, After the Disco (2014), extended the first album's themes of loneliness and aftermath into a conceptual loose narrative, supported by a short film and extensive touring with a full live band. After a hiatus during which Mercer recorded with the Shins and Burton worked as a producer, Broken Bells returned with the single "Shelter" in 2018 and released their third album, Into the Blue, in October 2022, drawing on influences from 1960s psychedelia and soul. The duo characterise the project as an ongoing collaboration rather than a side project, with Mercer contributing most lyrics and Burton shaping arrangements and production.
