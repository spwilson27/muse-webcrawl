Rand Miller

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Rand Miller is an American video game designer and the co-founder, with his brother Robyn Miller, of Cyan (later Cyan Worlds). He is best known as the co-creator of Myst (1993), the best-selling computer game of the 1990s, and for playing the character Atrus in the Myst series.

Career

The Miller brothers founded Cyan in 1987 and first produced children's games such as The Manhole. Their breakthrough came with Myst, a graphical adventure game whose richly detailed pre-rendered worlds and minimal interface helped popularise the CD-ROM format. Its sequel Riven (1997) followed, and Rand Miller took a leading role in expanding the series with games including Uru: Ages Beyond Myst, Myst V: End of Ages and later the crowd-funded games Obduction (2016) and Firmament (2023).

Atrus and writing

Miller portrayed Atrus, the writer of linking books who connects the player to the Ages of Myst, in the series' live-action video sequences. He also co-authored the Myst novels, including The Book of Atrus, The Book of Ti'ana and The Book of D'ni, which developed the history of the D'ni civilisation central to the games' fiction.
