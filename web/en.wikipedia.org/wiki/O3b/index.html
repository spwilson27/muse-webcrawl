O3b

TypeSatellite constellation (medium Earth orbit)
OperatorSES (O3b Networks)
First launches2013
SuccessorO3b mPOWER

O3b is a satellite constellation in medium Earth orbit (MEO) operated by SES, providing high-throughput, low-latency broadband connectivity to remote and underserved areas. The name "O3b" stands for "Other three billion", referring to the billions of people without reliable broadband access when the project was founded by Greg Wyler in 2007.

System

The first four O3b satellites were launched in June 2013, and the constellation grew to about twenty satellites orbiting at roughly 8,000 km altitude along the equator. Operating in the Ka band, the satellites' equatorial MEO orbit gives much lower latency (around 150 milliseconds or less) than traditional geostationary satellites, at the cost of covering latitudes only up to about ±50 degrees. Customers include telecommunications operators, internet service providers, cruise lines and other maritime users, energy companies, and governments and militaries; O3b services notably connected remote Pacific island nations and were used to restore connectivity after natural disasters and cable cuts.

O3b mPOWER

The follow-on system, O3b mPOWER, consists of more powerful second-generation satellites built by Boeing, with thousands of electronically steerable beams per satellite. The first mPOWER satellites were launched in December 2022, and the system entered service in 2024, offering higher capacities, flexible bandwidth allocation and wider coverage while remaining in medium Earth orbit.
