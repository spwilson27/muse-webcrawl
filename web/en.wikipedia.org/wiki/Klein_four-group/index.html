Klein four-group

The Klein four-group (German: Vierergruppe), denoted V or K4, is the smallest non-cyclic group, of order four. It is abelian, and every non-identity element has order two.

Structure

The group consists of an identity e and three elements a, b, c with ab = c, ac = b and bc = a. It is isomorphic to the direct product Z2 × Z2 and to the symmetry group of a rectangle (flips about its two axes plus the half-turn). Named for Felix Klein, it appears as the Galois group of a biquadratic field extension and as the kernel of the sign map on the alternating group A4, where it is a normal subgroup.
