Riichi Mahjong

Riichi Mahjong (Japanese: リーチ麻雀), also known as Japanese Mahjong, is a four-player variant of mahjong widely played in Japan and, increasingly, internationally. It is distinguished by the riichi declaration, the use of dora bonus tiles, and the furiten rule.

Players4 (variants: 3)
Tiles136 (including red fives)
OriginJapan

Gameplay

Players build a hand of fourteen tiles aiming to complete a winning pattern of four sets (sequences or triplets) and a pair, or one of a few special hands. A hand must contain at least one yaku (scoring pattern) to be declared a win.

Riichi declaration

A player with a closed (concealed) hand one tile away from completion may declare riichi, betting 1,000 points and committing to discard without changing the hand. Riichi itself is a yaku and grants access to further bonuses such as ippatsu and hidden dora.

Scoring

Hands are scored by han (doubles) and fu (base points), with payments differing between the dealer (East) and non-dealers, and between self-drawn (tsumo) and discard (ron) wins. Games typically use a starting score of 25,000 points.
