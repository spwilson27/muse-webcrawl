The 2007 Stanley Cup Finals was the championship series of the National Hockey League's 2006–07 season, contested between the Western Conference champion Anaheim Ducks and the Eastern Conference champion Ottawa Senators. The Ducks won the best-of-seven series four games to one, capturing the first Stanley Cup in franchise history and becoming the first team from California to win the Cup.

The series

Anaheim's physical style and the dominant defence pairing of Scott Niedermayer and Chris Pronger — both former Norris Trophy winners — shut down Ottawa's high-scoring top line of Daniel Alfredsson, Jason Spezza and Dany Heatley. The Ducks won the opening two games at home, split the two games in Ottawa, and clinched the Cup with a 6–2 victory in Game 5, in which Travis Moen scored twice, including the winning goal after a notorious own-goal mix-up by the Senators. Scott Niedermayer was awarded the Conn Smythe Trophy as playoff MVP, and he and his brother Rob became the first brothers to win the Cup together since the 1980s.

Ottawa's appearance was the first by a Canadian-based team in the Final since 2004 and the first in the modern Senators' history; the franchise would not return to the final for many years.
