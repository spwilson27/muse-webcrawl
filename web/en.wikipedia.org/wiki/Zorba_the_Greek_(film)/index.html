Zorba the Greek (film)

Zorba the Greek

Directed by: Michael Cacoyannis

Released: 1964

Starring: Anthony Quinn, Alan Bates, Irene Papas, Lila Kedrova

Zorba the Greek is a 1964 drama film directed by Michael Cacoyannis, adapted from the 1946 novel by Nikos Kazantzakis. Anthony Quinn stars as Alexis Zorba, a boisterous, life-loving Cretan peasant who befriends a reserved English writer, played by Alan Bates.

Plot

The writer Basil travels to Crete to reopen an inherited lignite mine. Zorba's zest for life, and the tragedies that befall the village widow and Basil's landlady Madame Hortense, teach him to embrace life — culminating in the famous beach dance after the mine scheme collapses.

Reception

The film won three Academy Awards, including Best Supporting Actress for Lila Kedrova, and its score by Mikis Theodorakis, including "Zorba's Dance", popularised the sirtaki worldwide.
