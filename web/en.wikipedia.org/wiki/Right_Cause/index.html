Right Cause

Right Cause (Russian: Правое дело, Pravoye Delo) was a liberal-conservative political party in Russia, formed in November 2008 through the merger of the Union of Right Forces (SPS), Civilian Power, and the Democratic Party of Russia. It positioned itself as a pro-market, pro-democracy party supportive of free enterprise and the rule of law.

History

The party's founding co-chairs included Boris Titov, Georgy Bovt, and Leonid Gozman. In June 2011 the billionaire Mikhail Prokhorov became party leader and set an ambitious electoral program, but he was ousted in an internal coup three months later amid allegations of Kremlin interference, an episode that drew wide attention. After poor results in the 2011 Duma election, the party shifted toward a more nationalist-conservative profile under later leadership.

In 2016 Right Cause was reorganized and re-founded as the Party of Growth (Партия Роста), led by Boris Titov, effectively ending Right Cause as a separate organization.
