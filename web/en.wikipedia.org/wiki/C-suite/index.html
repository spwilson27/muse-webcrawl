C-suite
Also called: C-level executives
Examples: CEO, CFO, COO, CTO, CIO, CMO

The C-suite, or C-level, is a term used to describe the senior executive team of a corporation, whose job titles typically begin with the letter C, for "chief". The term derives from the chief executive officer (CEO) and the cluster of chief officers who report to that role or to the board of directors.

Common roles

Core C-suite roles include the chief executive officer (CEO), chief financial officer (CFO), chief operating officer (COO), chief information officer (CIO), chief technology officer (CTO) and chief marketing officer (CMO). Newer additions include chief data officer, chief sustainability officer and chief experience officer.

Usage

The term became widespread in business journalism in the 2000s as executive titles proliferated. Critics note "title inflation" as companies create new chief roles; the collective bargaining term is also used for the executive office suite on the top floor of a headquarters.
