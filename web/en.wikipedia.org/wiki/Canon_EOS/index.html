Canon EOS

Canon EOS (Electro-Optical System) is a series of autofocus single-lens reflex (SLR) and mirrorless cameras produced by Canon Inc. since 1987. The name is also that of the goddess Eos in Greek mythology.

EF and RF mounts

EOS film and digital SLRs use the Canon EF lens mount, introduced with the EOS 650 in 1987, notable for its fully electronic connection between body and lens and built-in autofocus motors. Compact-system EOS M cameras used the EF-M mount from 2012, and full-frame mirrorless EOS R cameras introduced the RF mount in 2018.

Product lines

The range spans entry-level models (such as the Rebel / Kiss / xx00D series), enthusiast bodies, and professional series including the EOS-1D and EOS 5D lines. The EOS 5D Mark II (2008) was widely credited with sparking the DSLR video revolution in filmmaking.

Cinema EOS

Canon also markets Cinema EOS digital cinema cameras sharing the EOS branding and lens mounts, used in film and television production.
