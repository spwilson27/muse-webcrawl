Nielsen Holdings plc is a global data, measurement and analytics company best known for the Nielsen ratings of television audiences. Founded by Arthur C. Nielsen in 1923, the company grew from radio and television audience research into consumer measurement across more than 100 countries.

History

Arthur Nielsen began by measuring radio audiences in the 1930s and launched television ratings in 1950, which became the industry standard for US network programming decisions. The company passed through ownership by Dun & Bradstreet and VNU and was listed on the New York Stock Exchange from 2011. In 2021 it sold its consumer intelligence arm (NielsenIQ) to Advent International, and in 2022 a consortium led by Elliott Investment Management and Brookfield Business Partners took Nielsen private in a deal valued at about 6 billion.

Operations

Nielsen's audience measurement division tracks television, streaming, radio (via Nielsen Audio) and other media, while its Gracenote unit supplies entertainment metadata.
