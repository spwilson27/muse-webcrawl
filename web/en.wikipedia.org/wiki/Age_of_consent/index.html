Age of consent

Age of consent
Typical range worldwide: 14–18 years
Related: Age of majority, statutory rape laws

The age of consent is the age at which a person is considered to be legally competent to consent to sexual acts. Consequently, an adult who engages in sexual activity with a person younger than the age of consent is generally committing a criminal offence such as statutory rape, regardless of apparent consent.

Variation

Ages of consent vary by jurisdiction, commonly between 14 and 18. Many places provide close-in-age exemptions, higher ages where one party is in a position of trust or authority, and separate rules related to marriage. Historically ages were much lower and were raised during 19th- and 20th-century reform campaigns.

Related concepts

The age of consent is distinct from the age of majority, age of criminal responsibility, marriageable age, and ages for voting or alcohol. Debates continue about balancing protection of minors in adolescence against criminalisation of peer relationships.
