AT&T Stadium is a retractable roof stadium in Arlington, Texas, United States. It serves as the home of the Dallas Cowboys of the National Football League (NFL) and was completed on May 27, 2009. It is also the home of the Cotton Bowl Classic, the Big 12 Championship Game, and the Southwestern Athletic Conference championship.

Design

The stadium is famous for its enormous high-definition video board, suspended from the ceiling over the centre of the field, which was the world's largest at the time of opening. Its retractable roof and huge end-zone doors are among the largest of their kind. The stadium holds the all-time NFL regular season attendance record, with 105,121 spectators at a 2009 game.

Events

AT&T Stadium hosted Super Bowl XLV in 2011, the 2014 NCAA Men's Final Four, and numerous concerts, boxing matches, and soccer games. It is scheduled to be a venue for the 2026 FIFA World Cup.
