London Grand Prix

The London Stadium, host venue of the event in its later years

The London Grand Prix, currently known for sponsorship reasons as the London Athletics Meet, is an annual athletics event held in London, England. It is one of the meetings of the Diamond League, the top tier of international one-day track and field competitions.

History

The event has a long history under various names. From 1953 onwards it was associated with Crystal Palace National Sports Centre, which hosted the meeting for most of the following decades. As a Diamond League meeting it attracted the world's leading athletes, and in 2011 Usain Bolt's appearances made it one of the best-attended meetings on the circuit.

In 2013 the meeting moved to the Olympic Stadium in Stratford (later renamed the London Stadium) as the "Anniversary Games", marking one year since the London 2012 Olympic Games. It has since been staged at the London Stadium, including a memorable 2019 edition featuring a fast men's 5000 metres. In 2023 the rebranded London Athletics Meet drew a sell-out crowd of some 50,000, the largest one-day athletics attendance in the world that year.

Events

The programme typically includes sprints, middle- and long-distance races, hurdles, jumps and throws, together with para-athletics events. Diamond League scoring events vary by year according to the league's rotation.
