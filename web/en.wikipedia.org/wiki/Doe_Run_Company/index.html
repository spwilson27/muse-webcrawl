Doe Run Company

The Doe Run Company is an American natural resources company, and one of the largest lead producers in the Western Hemisphere, mining, milling and smelting lead, zinc and copper. It is headquartered in St. Louis, Missouri, and operates mines and mills in the Viburnum Trend of southeast Missouri, one of the world's richest lead deposits. Doe Run is owned by the Renco Group. The company has been involved in notable environmental controversies, including lead contamination in Herculaneum, Missouri, where its smelter operated until its closure in 2013, and at La Oroya in Peru through a former subsidiary.
