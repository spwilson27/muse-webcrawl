Project Zero

Project Zero is a team of security analysts employed by Google who are tasked with finding zero-day vulnerabilities. It was announced on 15 July 2014. The team researches vulnerabilities in widely used software and hardware — from any vendor, not only Google's — and reports them to the responsible vendor before public disclosure.

Disclosure policy

Project Zero generally gives vendors 90 days to fix a reported vulnerability before publishing details, with a shorter deadline (7 days) for vulnerabilities known to be under active exploitation. The policy is intended to reduce the window in which users are exposed to unpatched, publicly known flaws, and has influenced industry-wide disclosure norms.

Notable findings

The team has disclosed significant vulnerabilities across operating systems, browsers, and mobile platforms, and maintains a public issue tracker documenting its reports, including its research on the prevalence of zero-day exploitation in the wild.
