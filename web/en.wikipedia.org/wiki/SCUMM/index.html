SCUMM

SCUMM
Full name: Script Creation Utility for Maniac Mansion
Developer: Lucasfilm Games / LucasArts
Designers: Ron Gilbert, Aric Wilmunder
First used: Maniac Mansion (1987)

SCUMM (Script Creation Utility for Maniac Mansion) is a video game engine developed at Lucasfilm Games, later LucasArts, that powered most of the studio's celebrated graphic adventure games. It was created by Ron Gilbert and Aric Wilmunder for Maniac Mansion (1987) and evolved through many versions over roughly a decade.

Design

SCUMM combines an interpreted scripting language with a verb-based point-and-click interface: players combine on-screen verbs ("Walk to", "Pick up", "Use", "Talk to") with objects and characters. Its data-driven design let designers build rooms, puzzles and dialogue without depending heavily on programmers, enabling the witty, puzzle-driven adventures for which LucasArts became famous.

Games

SCUMM games include Zak McKracken and the Alien Mindbenders, Indiana Jones and the Last Crusade, Loom, The Secret of Monkey Island, Monkey Island 2: LeChuck's Revenge, Day of the Tentacle, Sam & Max Hit the Road, Full Throttle and The Curse of Monkey Island. The fan-made ScummVM interpreter keeps the games playable on modern systems.
