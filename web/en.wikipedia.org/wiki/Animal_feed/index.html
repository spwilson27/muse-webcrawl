Animal feed

Animal feed is food given to domestic animals, especially livestock, to support maintenance, growth, reproduction, milk production, eggs, work or health. Feed may be forage, grains, oilseed meals, by-products of food processing, minerals, vitamins and manufactured compound feeds.

Types

Ruminants such as cattle and sheep digest large amounts of pasture, hay and silage with microbial fermentation. Pigs and poultry are usually fed concentrated rations based on cereals and protein meals. Aquaculture feeds may contain fishmeal, plant proteins, oils and micronutrients, while pet foods are formulated for companion animals.

Nutrition and safety

Feed formulation balances energy, protein, amino acids, fibre, minerals and water for a species and production stage. Contamination by mycotoxins, pathogens, heavy metals or prohibited substances is a major regulatory concern because it can affect animal health and enter human food chains.
