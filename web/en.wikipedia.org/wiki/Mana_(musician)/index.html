Mana (Japanese: 魔名) is a Japanese musician, songwriter, producer and fashion designer, best known as the leader and guitarist of the visual kei rock band Malice Mizer and the founder of the symphonic metal project Moi dix Mois. One of the most influential figures in the visual kei movement, he is credited with popularising Gothic Lolita fashion through his clothing label Moi-même-Moitié.

Career

Mana began his career in punk and metal bands in Hiroshima before co-founding Malice Mizer in 1992 with guitarist Közi. As the band's leader and principal composer, he crafted its elaborate theatrical image, blending European classical influences with rock. Malice Mizer became one of the defining visual kei acts of the 1990s before going on indefinite hiatus in 2001 following the death of drummer Kami.

In 2002 Mana launched Moi dix Mois, a solo project with hired vocalists and musicians, releasing albums such as Dix Infernal (2003) and touring Europe, where the band developed a substantial following. He also runs the independent label Midi:Nette, founded in 1994.

Fashion and persona

In 1999 Mana founded the fashion brand Moi-même-Moitié, whose "Elegant Gothic Lolita" and "Elegant Gothic Aristocrat" lines helped define Gothic Lolita style internationally. In public he famously maintains a silent persona, rarely speaking and historically letting bandmates or written text communicate for him, and is celebrated for his cross-dressing stage image.
