The Armenian Revolutionary Federation (Armenian: Հայ Յեղափոխական Դաշնակցութիւն, Hay Heghapokhakan Dashnaktsutyun), commonly called the Dashnaktsutyun or ARF, is an Armenian nationalist and socialist political party founded in 1890 in Tiflis (Tbilisi) by Christapor Mikaelian, Stepan Zorian, and Simon Zavarian.

History

The ARF organized Armenian self-defense and revolutionary activity in the Ottoman and Russian empires, aiming initially at reform and autonomy for Ottoman Armenians. During and after the Armenian genocide it played a leading role in the First Republic of Armenia (1918–1920), supplying its leading statesmen, before the republic's Sovietization drove the party into exile. In the diaspora it built an extensive network of parties, schools, churches, and youth organizations in Lebanon, Syria, Iran, France, the United States, and elsewhere.

Modern role

After Armenia regained independence in 1991 the ARF re-established itself there, serving in coalition governments at times and in opposition at others; it was briefly banned in the mid-1990s. The party advocates recognition of the Armenian genocide, support for Armenians of Nagorno-Karabakh (Artsakh), and social-democratic policies, and holds seats in the National Assembly, often through electoral alliances.
