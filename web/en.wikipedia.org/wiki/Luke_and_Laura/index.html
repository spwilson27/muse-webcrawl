Luke and Laura are fictional characters and a supercouple from the American daytime drama General Hospital. Luke Spencer was played by Anthony Geary and Laura Webber by Genie Francis. The pairing became the most famous couple in soap opera history and is credited with transforming the genre in the early 1980s.

Luke and Laura's 1981 wedding drew 30 million viewers

Storyline

Luke, a small-time mob-connected club manager, and Laura, a young woman married to Scotty Baldwin, were thrown together in adventure storylines orchestrated by producer Gloria Monty, including the 1981 "Ice Princess" plot to stop the Cassadine family's weather machine. The pair became a cultural phenomenon: their wedding on 16 and 17 November 1981 was watched by around 30 million viewers, the highest-rated hour in American soap opera history, and was attended in-story by guest star Elizabeth Taylor as Helena Cassadine.

Controversy and legacy

The couple's origin story — in which Luke raped Laura at the Campus Disco in 1979 before the two fell in love — was controversial even at the time and has been reassessed in later decades, including by the show itself, which revisited the assault in 1998. Despite this, Luke and Laura are widely regarded as the archetypal soap supercouple, credited with drawing younger and male viewers to daytime television and inspiring the supercouple phenomenon of the 1980s.
