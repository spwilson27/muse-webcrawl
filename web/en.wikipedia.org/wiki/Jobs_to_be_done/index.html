Jobs to be done

Jobs to be done (JTBD) is a framework in marketing and product development holding that customers "hire" products and services to get a job done — to make progress in a particular circumstance. It was popularised by Harvard Business School professor Clayton Christensen.

Concepts

The framework shifts focus from product attributes and customer demographics to the underlying task or progress sought, which may have functional, emotional and social dimensions. A classic illustration is the milkshake study, in which morning commuters "hired" milkshakes to make a long drive less boring and keep them full until lunch.

Application

Practitioners use job statements of the form "When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [outcome]", and techniques such as "switch" interviews with recent buyers to uncover the circumstances of purchase and abandonment.
