Shakti (band)

Origin: United States / India
Genres: Jazz fusion, world music, Indian classical
Years active: 1973–1978; 1997–present (as Remember Shakti and revived Shakti)
Key members: John McLaughlin, L. Shankar, Zakir Hussain, Vikku Vinayakram, Ramnad Raghavan

Shakti is a fusion band formed by English guitarist John McLaughlin in the early 1970s, bringing together jazz improvisation with South and North Indian classical music. "Shakti" is a Sanskrit word meaning creative energy or divine feminine power.

History

After leaving the Mahavishnu Orchestra, McLaughlin — a devotee of Sri Chinmoy and a serious student of Indian music — formed Shakti with violinist L. Shankar, tabla player Zakir Hussain, ghatam player Vikku Vinayakram, and mridangam player Ramnad Raghavan. The acoustic group recorded three albums for Columbia: Shakti with John McLaughlin (1976), A Handful of Beauty (1976), and Natural Elements (1977), plus a celebrated live recording from Southampton College in 1975.

The band's music combined dizzyingly fast unison lines, complex Indian tala rhythmic cycles, konnakol vocal percussion, and modal improvisation, played with a virtuosity that influenced generations of fusion and world-music performers. McLaughlin played a specially built acoustic guitar with sympathetic and drone strings.

Remember Shakti and revival

In 1997 McLaughlin revived the concept as Remember Shakti, with Zakir Hussain and new members including mandolinist U. Srinivas, vocalist Shankar Mahadevan, and kanjira player V. Selvaganesh. A later incarnation under the Shakti name released This Moment in 2023, which won the Grammy Award for Best Global Music Album.
