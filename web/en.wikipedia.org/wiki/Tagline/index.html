Tagline

In entertainment, a tagline is a short text which serves to clarify a thought for, or is designed with a form of dramatic effect. Many films and television programmes have a catchphrase or tagline that is used in trailers, posters, and other promotional material to capture the theme or tone of the work.

Usage

Taglines are a form of branding: a memorable phrase repeated in association with a product, company, film, or show. Famous film taglines include "In space no one can hear you scream" for Alien (1979) and "Who ya gonna call?" for Ghostbusters (1984). In advertising, similar phrases are called slogans or straplines when they endure across campaigns. On the web, a tagline may also appear beneath a site's name to summarize its purpose.
