Justice League (TV series)

Television series
Genre: Superhero, action, adventure
Developed by: Bruce Timm
Network: Cartoon Network
Original run: 2001–2004 (as Justice League); continued as Justice League Unlimited, 2004–2006

Justice League is an American animated television series that aired on Cartoon Network from 2001 to 2004. Produced by Warner Bros. Animation and developed by Bruce Timm, it is set in the shared DC Animated Universe and follows the superhero team of Superman, Batman, Wonder Woman, the Flash (Wally West), Green Lantern (John Stewart), Hawkgirl and the Martian Manhunter.

Overview

The series favoured multi-part, serialised storylines — unusual for American children's animation — including the Thanagarian invasion arc that closed its second season. It was followed by Justice League Unlimited (2004–2006), which expanded the League's roster to dozens of DC Comics heroes and introduced season-long arcs involving the Cadmus organisation and the Legion of Doom.

Reception

Both series are widely regarded as among the finest animated superhero adaptations, praised for their mature storytelling, voice cast — including Kevin Conroy as Batman and George Newbern as Superman — and faithful treatment of the DC universe.
