Tehran Province (Persian: استان تهران) is one of the 31 provinces of Iran. It covers an area of 18,814 square kilometres and is located to the north of the central plateau of Iran. Its capital is the city of Tehran, which is also the national capital.

Geography

The province lies at the foot of the Alborz mountain range, which forms its northern boundary and includes Mount Damavand, the highest peak in Iran, near its northeastern edge. The province has a cold semi-arid and mountainous climate varying by altitude.

Administrative divisions

Tehran Province is divided into 16 counties, including Tehran, Eslamshahr, Shahriar, Qods, Malard, Varamin, Rey (Shahr-e Rey), Pakdasht, Damavand, and Firuzkuh. It is the most populous and most densely populated province of Iran, containing the Tehran metropolitan area.

History

The province's territory contains ancient settlements around Rey, one of Iran's oldest cities. In 2010, Alborz Province was split off from Tehran Province to its west, reducing its area.
