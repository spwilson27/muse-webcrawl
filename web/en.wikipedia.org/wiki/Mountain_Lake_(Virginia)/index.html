Mountain Lake (Virginia)

Mountain Lake

Location: Giles County, Virginia, United States

Coordinates: 37.3556° N, 80.5364° W

Surface elevation: approx. 1,181 m (3,875 ft)

Surface area: approx. 50 acres (20 ha) at full level

Basin: One of only two natural freshwater lakes in Virginia

Mountain Lake is a freshwater lake in Giles County, in the southwestern part of the U.S. state of Virginia. Situated on Salt Pond Mountain near the town of Pembroke, it is one of only two natural freshwater lakes in Virginia (the other being Lake Drummond in the Great Dismal Swamp). The lake and the surrounding land are protected as part of the Mountain Lake Conservancy, and the nearby Mountain Lake Lodge is famous as the filming location for the 1987 film Dirty Dancing.

Formation and hydrology

Mountain Lake occupies a basin at about 3,875 feet (1,181 m) above sea level. Geologists believe the lake formed when a large rockslide dammed the valley of a mountain stream several thousand years ago. The lake has no surface outlet; water drains through fractures in the lakebed into an underground system, which makes the lake level highly variable. Historical records document repeated episodes in which the lake has shrunk dramatically or nearly dried up, alternating with periods when it refilled.

Beginning in 2008 the water level fell sharply, and by 2020 the lake had receded to a fraction of its former size — at its low point only a small pond remained in the deepest basin. Geologists attributed the drawdown to drought conditions combined with shifts in the fractured bedrock plumbing beneath the lake. The lake level has fluctuated in subsequent years.

Mountain Lake Lodge

A resort has stood at the lake since the 19th century; the present stone Mountain Lake Lodge was built in the 1930s. The property gained international fame as "Kellerman's Resort," the fictional Catskills setting of Dirty Dancing (1987), starring Patrick Swayze and Jennifer Grey. The University of Virginia's Mountain Lake Biological Station, founded in 1930, is also located nearby and hosts research and courses in field biology.
