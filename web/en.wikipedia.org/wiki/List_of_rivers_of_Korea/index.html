List of rivers of Korea

The rivers of the Korean Peninsula mostly rise in the mountainous east and flow west or south across gentler plains, so the longest rivers drain into the Yellow Sea and the Korea Strait. The longest river entirely associated with Korea is the Yalu (Amnok), which forms the border with China.

Because the peninsula's mountain spine (the Taebaek range) lies close to the east coast, east-flowing rivers are short and steep, while western rivers are long, slow and historically navigable far inland.
