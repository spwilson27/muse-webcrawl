War Memorial Opera House

The War Memorial Opera House is an opera house in San Francisco, California, located on the western side of Van Ness Avenue across from the west side of the San Francisco City Hall. It is part of the San Francisco War Memorial and Performing Arts Center. It has been the home of the San Francisco Opera since opening night in 1932.

History

Designed in the Beaux-Arts style by Arthur Brown Jr., co-designer of City Hall, the building was dedicated as a memorial to San Franciscans who died in the First World War. In 1945 it hosted the signing conference sessions of the United Nations Charter, and the 1951 Treaty of San Francisco was signed in the adjacent Herbst Theatre. The building was damaged in the 1989 Loma Prieta earthquake and underwent a seismic retrofit completed in 1997.

Tenants

Besides the San Francisco Opera, the venue is home to the San Francisco Ballet, which performs there for its regular season.
