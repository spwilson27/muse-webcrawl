Sommelier

A sommelier (French: [sɔməlje]) is a trained wine professional, normally working in fine restaurants, who specialises in all aspects of wine service as well as wine and food pairing. The modern sommelier's responsibilities typically include developing the wine list, purchasing and cellar management, staff training, and advising diners at the table. The word derives from the French for the officer in charge of provisions and pack animals (somme, pack load) in a royal or noble household; by the 19th century it had narrowed to the keeper of the wine cellar.

A sommelier at work; the tastevin worn around the neck is a traditional emblem of the profession.

Role

Beyond table service — presenting, opening, decanting, and pouring wine — sommeliers curate the cellar, negotiate with suppliers, set markups, and increasingly oversee other beverages including sake, beer, spirits, and non-alcoholic pairings. In larger establishments a head sommelier directs a team. The traditional symbol of the role is the tastevin, a shallow silver tasting cup worn on a ribbon or chain.

Training and certification

The best-known certifying body is the Court of Master Sommeliers, founded in London in 1969 (first examinations 1969; the first Master Sommelier diploma awarded outside exam format to Michael Broadbent). Its four levels are Introductory, Certified, Advanced, and Master Sommelier. The Master Sommelier examination — covering theory, blind tasting of six wines, and practical service — is famously difficult, with a pass rate of only a few percent; since the diploma's creation only a few hundred people worldwide have earned the title. The Wine & Spirit Education Trust (WSET) offers a parallel educational ladder up to the Master of Wine, a separate and equally prestigious qualification administered by the Institute of Masters of Wine. The profession gained popular attention through the documentary film SOMM (2012) and its sequels.

Competitions

The Association de la Sommellerie Internationale organises the "Best Sommelier of the World" contest every three years or so; winners include Gérard Basset (2010, the only person to hold both Master Sommelier and Master of Wine titles along with other honours), Paolo Basso (2013), Arvid Rosengren (2016), Marc Almert (2019), and Raimonds Tomsons (2023).
