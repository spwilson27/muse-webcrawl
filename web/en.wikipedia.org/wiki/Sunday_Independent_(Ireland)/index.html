Sunday Independent (Ireland)

The Sunday Independent, Ireland's best-selling Sunday newspaper

The Sunday Independent is an Irish Sunday newspaper broadsheet published in Dublin by Independent News & Media (now Mediahuis Ireland). Founded in 1906, it has for decades been Ireland's highest-selling Sunday newspaper, known for its opinion columnists, extensive sports coverage and frequently controversial commentary on Irish politics and society.

History

The Sunday Independent was launched in 1906 as the Sunday companion of William Martin Murphy's Irish Independent. Under long-serving editors it grew into a dominant voice of middle Ireland, mixing news with combative opinion writing. The paper carried work by celebrated columnists and, in the 1990s, famously — and controversially — campaigned on issues from divorce referendums to the Northern Ireland peace process.

Its reporting produced notable controversies, including the 1996 killing of crime reporter Veronica Guerin (who wrote for the paper), and legal disputes over stories concerning business figures. As part of Independent News & Media, the paper passed through the era of Denis O'Brien's controlling stake before the group was acquired by the Belgian-Dutch Mediahuis in 2019. Like other print titles, its circulation has declined with the shift to digital, though it long remained Ireland's best-selling Sunday paper, with readership concentrated in the over-35 market.

Content

Editions combine news, a large opinion and comment section, business coverage, lifestyle and magazine supplements, and one of the country's biggest Sunday sports sections covering Gaelic games, rugby and soccer.
