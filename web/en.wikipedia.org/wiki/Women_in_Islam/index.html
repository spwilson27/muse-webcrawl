Women in Islam

The experiences of women in Islam vary widely across countries, cultures and legal traditions, shaped by interpretations of the Qur'an and hadith, local custom, and modern state law.

Scriptural and historical background

The Qur'an addresses women directly, granting rights to inheritance, property and divorce that were notable in 7th-century Arabia, while classical jurisprudence developed detailed rules on marriage, family and dress. Women such as Khadija and Aisha played prominent roles in early Islamic history.

Contemporary debates

Current discussions cover education, employment, political participation, veiling, family law reform, and Islamic feminism. Practices differ greatly between and within Muslim-majority countries, and between Muslim communities worldwide.
