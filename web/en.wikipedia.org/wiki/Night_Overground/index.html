Night Overground is the overnight service pattern of London Overground, operated by Transport for London, providing 24-hour trains on Friday and Saturday nights on parts of the network, principally the East London line.

Night Overground

Night Overground is the name given to the overnight services operated on the London Overground network in London, England, as part of Transport for London's Night Tube-style weekend programme. Services run through the night on Fridays and Saturdays (that is, the nights of Friday/Saturday and Saturday/Sunday).

History

Night Overground began on the East London line in December 2017, with trains running all night between Dalston Junction and New Cross Gate. The service was extended south to Crystal Palace and West Croydon, and in later years overnight running was added on further sections of the network as part of the wider expansion of 24-hour weekend services in London, complementing the Night Tube on the London Underground, which had launched in August 2016.

Services

Overnight trains generally run at intervals of about 15–30 minutes on the served routes, connecting inner London districts such as Hackney, Whitechapel, Shoreditch, Canada Water, Peckham and Sydenham. The services use the same Class 710 and Class 378 electric multiple units as daytime operations, and standard Oyster and contactless fares apply. The night services are intended to support London's night-time economy and workers travelling outside conventional hours.
