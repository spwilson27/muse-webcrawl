It's Garry Shandling's Show

It's Garry Shandling's Show is an American sitcom created by Garry Shandling and Alan Zweibel that ran on Showtime from 1986 to 1990. It was one of the first original series produced for cable television and is remembered for its metafictional style, in which Shandling played himself and frequently broke the fourth wall.

Created byGarry Shandling, Alan Zweibel
StarringGarry Shandling, Geoffrey Blake, Molly Cheek, Scott Nemes
NetworkShowtime
Original run1986 – 1990 (72 episodes)

Premise

Shandling plays a neurotic stand-up comedian living in Sherman Oaks, California, fully aware that he is the star of a television show. The theme song was famously self-referential ("This is the theme to Garry's show..."), and plot lines often revolved around the machinery of sitcom making itself.

Legacy

The series influenced later self-aware comedies and preceded Shandling's acclaimed HBO series The Larry Sanders Show. Episodes were later rerun on the Fox network in the United States.
