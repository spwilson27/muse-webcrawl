Administrative divisions of Armenia

Armenia is divided into ten provinces called marzer (singular marz), plus the capital city Yerevan, which has the status of a community (hamaynk) with special administration. The provinces are Aragatsotn, Ararat, Armavir, Gegharkunik, Kotayk, Lori, Shirak, Syunik, Tavush and Vayots Dzor.

Below the provincial level, Armenia is organised into communities (hamaynkner), which following a series of consolidations in the 2010s and 2020s were merged from several hundred municipalities into a much smaller number of enlarged communities, each comprising one or more settlements.

Provincial governors (marzpets) are appointed by the central government, while community heads and councils are elected. Yerevan's mayor is elected by the city council, in office since the first such election in 2009.
