Infamous (film)

Infamous is a 2006 American drama film written and directed by Douglas McGrath. It dramatises Truman Capote's research for his true-crime book In Cold Blood (1966), following the author as he travels to Kansas to investigate the 1959 Clutter family murders and forms a complex attachment to one of the killers, Perry Smith.

Cast

Toby Jones stars as Truman Capote, with Sandra Bullock as his friend Nelle Harper Lee, Daniel Craig as Perry Smith, Jeff Daniels as investigator Alvin Dewey, and supporting performances by Sigourney Weaver, Hope Davis, Isabella Rossellini, Peter Bogdanovich and Gwyneth Paltrow (as singer Kitty Dean in the opening scene).

Reception

The film premiered at the Venice Film Festival and was released in October 2006. It arrived a year after Bennett Miller's Capote (2005), which covered the same events and won Philip Seymour Hoffman an Academy Award. Infamous was often overshadowed commercially, but critics praised Toby Jones's uncanny portrayal of Capote, and the film has since been reappraised favourably for its lighter, more social portrait of the writer.
