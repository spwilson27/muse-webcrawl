International Color Consortium

The International Color Consortium (ICC) was formed in 1993 by eight industry vendors for the purpose of creating, promoting and encouraging the standardization of an architecture and components for an open, vendor-neutral, cross-platform color management system. The result of this activity was the ICC profile specification.

ICC profiles

ICC profiles describe the color attributes of a particular device or viewing requirement, enabling consistent color reproduction between different devices and operating systems. Version 4 of the specification was published as ISO 15076-1:2005; in 2023 the ICC introduced iccMAX (ICC.2) as a more flexible successor framework.

Membership

Founding members included Adobe, Agfa, Apple, Kodak, Microsoft, Silicon Graphics, Sun Microsystems, and Taligent. The ICC is headquartered in Reston, Virginia, United States.
