Larry (cat)

Larry
Chief Mouser to the Cabinet Office
In office: 2011 – present
Born: c. January 2007
Residence: 10 Downing Street, London

Larry (born c. January 2007) is a British domestic tabby cat who has served as Chief Mouser to the Cabinet Office at 10 Downing Street since 2011. Officially employed by the Cabinet Office, Larry is the first Downing Street cat to hold the title in a formal capacity with a published job description; his duties are described as including "greeting guests to the house, inspecting security defences and testing antique furniture for napping quality".

Early life and appointment

Larry was a stray, taken in by Battersea Dogs & Cats Home in London. He was adopted in February 2011, during the premiership of David Cameron, after reports of a rat being seen near the door of Number 10 during live television broadcasts. He was recommended by Battersea staff for his strong predatory drive, though his early performance was publicly questioned after a slow start to his mousing and an incident in which he was photographed sleeping while on duty.

Tenure

Larry has remained at Downing Street through the governments of David Cameron, Theresa May, Boris Johnson, Liz Truss and Rishi Sunak, and continued in post under Keir Starmer, becoming a fixture of British political imagery. His tenure has been marked by reported territorial disputes with other government cats—notably Palmerston, the Foreign Office cat (retired 2020), with whom he had several public altercations—and by his habit of occupying the doorstep during press events. He is cared for by Downing Street staff and funded through a staff kitty rather than public money.

Larry has an unofficial social media presence with a large following, and his health and longevity are periodically the subject of press coverage; in September 2022 he marked becoming the first Chief Mouser to serve under three monarchs' reigns' transitions, remaining in place during the change of prime minister and monarch that month.
