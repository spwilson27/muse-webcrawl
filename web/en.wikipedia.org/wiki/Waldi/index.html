Waldi

Waldi was the official mascot of the 1972 Summer Olympic Games in Munich, West Germany. A dachshund, Waldi was the first official mascot in the history of the Olympic Games.

Design

Waldi was designed under the direction of Otl Aicher, the graphic designer responsible for the Munich Games' visual identity. The dachshund, a popular Bavarian breed noted for endurance, tenacity and agility, was rendered in a striped pattern using the pastel colours of the 1972 Games — light blue, green, orange and yellow — deliberately avoiding the red and black associated with earlier German regimes. Waldi's head and tail were light blue, with the body divided into coloured stripes. A living dachshund, Cherie von Birkenhof, served as the model.

Legacy

The marathon course at the Munich Games was laid out in a shape recalling Waldi's outline, with runners entering the Olympic Stadium through the mouth. Waldi appeared on posters, toys and merchandise, and its success established the Olympic mascot as a permanent feature of the Games; mascots have been produced for every Olympics since.
