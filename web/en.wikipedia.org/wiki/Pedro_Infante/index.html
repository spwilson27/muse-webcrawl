Pedro Infante

Born: 18 November 1917, Mazatlán, Sinaloa, Mexico
Died: 15 April 1957, Mérida, Yucatán, Mexico
Occupations: Actor, singer

Pedro Infante Cruz (18 November 1917 – 15 April 1957) was a Mexican actor and singer, one of the most popular stars of the Golden Age of Mexican cinema. An icon of Mexican popular culture, he embodied the charro and the working-class everyman in dozens of films and recorded hundreds of songs, particularly rancheras.

Career

Born in Mazatlán and raised in modest circumstances, Infante worked as a carpenter before turning to music and radio. His film breakthrough came in the 1940s with films such as Los tres huastecos (1948), in which he played three brothers, and Ismael Rodríguez's urban melodramas Nosotros los pobres (1948) and Ustedes los ricos, in which his character Pepe el Toro became a beloved figure. He won the Silver Bear for Best Actor at the Berlin International Film Festival for Tizoc (1957).

Death and legacy

Infante died at age 39 when the cargo plane he was piloting crashed in Mérida, Yucatán. His death caused national mourning, and his birthday and death anniversary are still commemorated by crowds of fans; the saying "Pedro Infante no ha muerto" ("Pedro Infante is not dead") reflects his enduring place in Mexican culture.
