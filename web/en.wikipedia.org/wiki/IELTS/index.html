IELTS

The International English Language Testing System (IELTS) is an international standardised test of English language proficiency for non-native English language speakers. It is jointly managed by the British Council, IDP: IELTS Australia and Cambridge Assessment English, and was established in 1989. IELTS is one of the major English-language tests in the world and is accepted by most Australian, British, Canadian, European, Irish and New Zealand academic institutions, by over 3,000 academic institutions in the United States, and by various professional organisations. Several million tests are taken each year.

Structure

The test has four sections—Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking—taken on the same day or within a short window. Two versions exist: the Academic version, for those applying to higher education or professional registration, and the General Training version, for secondary education, work, or migration purposes.

Scoring

BandSkill level
9Expert user

Candidates receive a band score from 1 to 9 for each section and an overall band score; results are generally valid for two years.
