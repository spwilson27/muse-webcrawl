Oito Batutas

Os Oito Batutas ("The Eight Batons" or "The Eight Maestros") was a pioneering Brazilian musical ensemble formed in Rio de Janeiro in 1919. Led by the flautist Pixinguinha and including guitarist Donga, the group played a central role in popularizing choro and early samba in Brazil and abroad.

Career

The group was assembled to perform in the lobby of the Cinema Palais in Rio. In 1922 the Oito Batutas travelled to Paris, where they performed for several months to great acclaim, absorbing influences from jazz and the fox-trot that they carried back to Brazilian popular music. Their success helped legitimize Afro-Brazilian musical styles among Brazil's elites.

Legacy

With arrangements largely by Pixinguinha, the ensemble's repertoire mixed maxixe, choro, samba, and cateretê. The Oito Batutas are regarded as a foundational group in the history of Brazilian popular music.
