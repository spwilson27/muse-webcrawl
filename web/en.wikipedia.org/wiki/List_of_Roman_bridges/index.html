List of Roman bridges

The Romans were the first civilization to build large, permanent bridges, typically of stone with semicircular arches, many of which survive today. Roman bridges carried roads, and sometimes aqueducts, across the rivers of Europe, North Africa and the Near East. The following are among the best known surviving or historically recorded examples.

Roman bridge engineering pioneered the use of the semicircular arch, cofferdams for river foundations, and pozzolanic concrete. Many medieval and modern bridges stand on Roman piers.
