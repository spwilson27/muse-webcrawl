NCAA Division I Wrestling Championships

Sport: Collegiate wrestling
Founded: 1928
Weight classes: 10
Most team titles: Iowa Hawkeyes

The NCAA Division I Wrestling Championships is an annual wrestling tournament for male college students in institutions from the National Collegiate Athletic Association's Division I. Held each March, it crowns individual national champions in ten weight classes and a team champion based on accumulated points.

Format

About 330 wrestlers qualify through conference tournaments and at-large selections. The three-day double-elimination tournament seeds 33 wrestlers per weight class. Wrestlers who place in the top eight of their class earn All-American honours.

History and team race

The first championships were held in 1928. Oklahoma State won the earliest titles, while the Iowa Hawkeyes under coach Dan Gable became the dominant programme from the late 1970s. In recent decades Penn State, coached by Cael Sanderson, has won the majority of team titles, with Iowa and Oklahoma State remaining the other most successful programmes overall.
