The Bothy Band

The Bothy Band were an Irish traditional music group formed in 1974, widely credited with revolutionising the sound of Irish folk music by combining driving rhythm — guitar, bouzouki and piano — with virtuoso fiddle, flute and uilleann pipes playing.

History

The classic line-up featured Paddy Keenan (uilleann pipes), Matt Molloy (flute), Tommy Peoples and later Kevin Burke (fiddle), Mícheál Ó Domhnaill (guitar, vocals), Tríona Ní Dhomhnaill (vocals, clavinet) and Dónal Lunny (bouzouki, guitar). They released three studio albums — The Bothy Band (1975), Old Hag You Have Killed Me (1976) and Out of the Wind – Into the Sun (1977) — plus the live album After Hours (1979), before disbanding in 1979.

Legacy

Despite their short career, the Bothy Band's energetic ensemble style became the template for modern Irish traditional groups, and its members went on to influential careers with The Chieftains, Planxty, Nightnoise and others. Reunion concerts have taken place in recent years.
