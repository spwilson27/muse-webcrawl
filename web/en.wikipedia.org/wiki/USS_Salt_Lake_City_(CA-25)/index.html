USS Salt Lake City (CA-25)

USS Salt Lake City (CA-25) was a Pensacola-class heavy cruiser of the United States Navy, launched in 1929 and commissioned in December that year. Nicknamed the "Swayback Maru" by her crew because of her distinctive profile, she saw extensive combat in the Pacific during World War II.

Service history

Salt Lake City was patrolling near Hawaii during the attack on Pearl Harbor and joined the first American carrier raids of the war in early 1942. She fought in the Aleutian Islands campaign, notably at the Battle of the Komandorski Islands in March 1943, where she was badly damaged in one of the longest naval gunnery duels of the war. After repairs she supported operations at Tarawa, the Marshall Islands, the Philippines, Iwo Jima and Okinawa.

Fate

After the war the cruiser was used as a target ship in the Operation Crossroads atomic bomb tests at Bikini Atoll in 1946, surviving both blasts. She was sunk as a target off southern California in May 1948. She earned 11 battle stars and a Navy Unit Commendation.
