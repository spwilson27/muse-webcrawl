Puntland


The coast of Puntland near the Horn of Africa

Puntland (Somali: Puntland; Arabic: بونتلاند), officially the Puntland State of Somalia, is an autonomous territory in the northeastern Horn of Africa and a federal member state of Somalia. Its capital is Garoowe, in the Nugaal region, and its largest city and commercial centre is Bosaso, on the Gulf of Aden coast. The name derives from the ancient Land of Punt, recorded in Egyptian sources.

History

Puntland was established on 1 August 1998 at a constitutional conference in Garoowe, following the collapse of the Somali central government in 1991. Unlike Somaliland to the west, Puntland has not sought international recognition as an independent state; its stated aim is to participate in a future federal Somalia. Its first president was Abdullahi Yusuf Ahmed, who later became president of Somalia's Transitional Federal Government.

Geography

Puntland occupies the northeastern third of Somalia, stretching from the Golis Mountains to the Indian Ocean coast at the tip of the Horn, including Cape Guardafui. It borders Somaliland and the disputed Sool and Sanaag regions to the west and Ethiopia to the south.

Economy and security

Livestock exports through Bosaso, frankincense and fishing are pillars of the economy. Puntland became known internationally for maritime piracy off its coast in the 2000s, which later declined sharply; the Puntland Maritime Police Force has also fought al-Shabaab and Islamic State affiliates in the Golis and Al-Miskad mountains.
