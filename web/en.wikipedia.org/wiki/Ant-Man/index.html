Ant-Man is the name of several fictional superheroes appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. The original Ant-Man was Hank Pym, a scientist who discovered a means of shrinking to the size of an insect using what he called Pym Particles, and who later also operated as Giant-Man, Goliath and Yellowjacket. The second Ant-Man, Scott Lang, is a reformed thief who inherited the suit and technology.

Publication history

Hank Pym first appeared as a scientist in a stand-alone story in Tales to Astonish #27 (January 1962) and returned as the superhero Ant-Man in issue #35. Pym was a founding member of the Avengers. Scott Lang debuted in Avengers #181 (1979) and took up the Ant-Man role in Marvel Premiere #47. A third, morally ambiguous Ant-Man, Eric O'Grady, was introduced in 2006.

Powers and abilities

Using Pym Particles contained in a suit and a cybernetic helmet, Ant-Man can shrink to sub-microscopic size or, by reversing the process, grow to many times his normal height, retaining roughly human strength at reduced size. The helmet allows communication with ants and other insects, which the hero uses as mounts, scouts and allies.

In other media

Scott Lang, played by Paul Rudd, is the Ant-Man of the Marvel Cinematic Universe, appearing in Ant-Man (2015), Ant-Man and the Wasp (2018), Avengers: Endgame (2019) and Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania (2023), with Michael Douglas as Hank Pym and Evangeline Lilly as the Wasp.
