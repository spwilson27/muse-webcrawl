Palestinian refugees

Palestinian refugees are citizens of Mandatory Palestine, and their descendants, who fled or were expelled from their country during the 1948 Palestine war (in what Palestinians call the Nakba) and during the Six-Day War of 1967. Most Palestinian refugees live in or near refugee camps in Jordan, Lebanon, Syria, the West Bank, and the Gaza Strip, and are registered with the United Nations Relief and Works Agency (UNRWA), which counted about 5.9 million registered refugees in the early 2020s.

Origins

During the 1948 war, more than 700,000 Palestinian Arabs fled or were expelled from their homes in the territory that became Israel. The causes remain debated by historians, encompassing flight during fighting, direct expulsions (notably in Lydda and Ramle), and fear of massacres. In the 1967 war a further roughly 280,000–325,000 Palestinians were displaced, many for a second time.

UNRWA and the camps

The UN General Assembly established UNRWA in December 1949 to provide relief, education, and health services. Fifty-eight recognised camps house a large share of the registered population. Unusually among refugee populations, Palestinian refugee status is inherited by descendants under UNRWA's definition, pending a political settlement.

Status in host countries

Jordan has granted citizenship to most Palestinian refugees within its borders. In Lebanon, refugees face significant legal restrictions on employment and property, and most remain stateless. Refugees in Syria had broad civil rights before the civil war displaced many again.

Right of return

Palestinians claim a right of return grounded in UN General Assembly Resolution 194 (1948), which resolved that refugees wishing to return and live at peace should be permitted to do so, or receive compensation. Israel rejects a mass return, arguing it would end Israel's character as a Jewish state, and maintains the issue must be settled by negotiation. The refugee question is one of the core final-status issues of the Israeli–Palestinian conflict.
