Killer Frost

Killer Frost is a name used by several supervillains and anti-heroines appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, all possessing cryokinetic powers — the ability to absorb heat and project intense cold and ice. The mantle originated with Crystal Frost, an enemy of Firestorm who debuted in Firestorm #3 (1978). She was succeeded by Louise Lincoln, and later by Caitlin Snow, the best-known modern incarnation, popularised by The Flash television series.

Incarnations

Crystal Frost

A brilliant scientist whose unrequited love for her professor, Martin Stein, curdled into hatred of men, Crystal Frost was transformed by an accident in a thermafrost chamber. As Killer Frost she battled Firestorm until her death, her powers literally consuming her.

Louise Lincoln

Frost's friend and colleague Louise Lincoln recreated the accident to gain the same powers, becoming a colder, more ruthless Killer Frost and a recurring member of villain teams including the Secret Society and the Suicide Squad.

Caitlin Snow

Introduced in the New 52 as a S.T.A.R. Labs scientist transformed by an encounter with a H.I.V.E. engine, Caitlin Snow is a more sympathetic figure who must absorb heat to survive but struggles against her killer instincts; versions of her have served with the Justice League of America. In The CW's The Flash (played by Danielle Panabaker), Caitlin Snow and her alter ego Frost are central characters.

Powers

Killer Frost absorbs thermal energy from her surroundings and converts it into cold, projecting blasts of ice, creating ice constructs and freezing victims solid; the Frost and Lincoln versions must feed on the heat of living beings, making their kiss lethal.
