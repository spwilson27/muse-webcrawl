ʿAAd (Arabic: عاد, also spelled Aad) is a village in the Akkar District of the Akkar Governorate in northern Lebanon, near the border with Syria. It is one of the many small rural settlements of the Akkar plain and its surrounding hills, where the economy is based largely on agriculture.

Geography

The village lies in the far north of Lebanon, in a region of farmland and low hills watered by seasonal streams flowing toward the Nahr al-Kabir river, which forms part of the Lebanese–Syrian frontier. Like other villages of the Akkar, it is connected by local roads to the district centre of Halba and to the coastal city of Tripoli.
