National Government (1931)

The National Government was a coalition government formed in the United Kingdom in August 1931 during the Great Depression. It was initially formed by Labour prime minister Ramsay MacDonald together with the Conservative Party and the Liberal Party to deal with a financial crisis and a run on the pound.

History

MacDonald's Labour cabinet split over proposed cuts to unemployment benefit, and he instead formed the National Government, which led to his expulsion from the Labour Party. The coalition won a landslide victory in the October 1931 general election. Although MacDonald remained prime minister until 1935, the government was dominated by the Conservatives under Stanley Baldwin; later National Governments under Baldwin and Neville Chamberlain continued until 1940, when Winston Churchill formed a wartime coalition.
