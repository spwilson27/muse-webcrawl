Red Digital Cinema

Red Digital Cinema Camera Company is an American company that manufactures professional digital cinema cameras. Founded in 2005 by Jim Jannard, the founder of Oakley, the company is headquartered in Irvine, California.

History

The company's first camera, the Red One, was announced in 2006 and released in 2007. It recorded 4K resolution images to hard drives or flash media using the proprietary Redcode Raw codec, offering image quality competitive with 35 mm film at a far lower price than established digital cinema cameras. The camera was used on productions such as Wanted and District 9.

Cameras

Subsequent camera systems include the Epic and Scarlet lines, the DSMC2 family with sensors such as Monstro 8K VV and Helium 8K S35, and the Komodo, a compact 6K camera released in 2020. The V-Raptor, launched in 2021, records 8K at up to 120 frames per second. RED cameras have been widely adopted in feature films, television and streaming productions.

In 2024, Nikon Corporation announced the acquisition of RED, making it a wholly owned subsidiary.
