Billie Eilish Pirate Baird O'Connell (born December 18, 2001) is an American singer and songwriter. She first gained public attention in 2015 with her debut single "Ocean Eyes", written and produced by her brother, Finneas O'Connell, with whom she collaborates on music and live shows. Her debut studio album, When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go? (2019), debuted atop the US Billboard 200 and included the hit single "Bad Guy", her first number-one in the United States.

Early life

Eilish was born in Los Angeles, California, to actress and musician Maggie Baird and actor Patrick O'Connell, both of whom are of Irish and Scottish descent. She was homeschooled and joined the Los Angeles Children's Chorus at age eight. She began writing songs at eleven and recorded music with her brother in his bedroom studio.

Career

Eilish released her debut extended play, Don't Smile at Me, in 2017. Her debut album followed in 2019 to critical acclaim. At the 2020 Grammy Awards she became the youngest person and the first woman to win all four main categories — Best New Artist, Record of the Year, Album of the Year, and Song of the Year — in a single year. Her later albums include Happier Than Ever (2021) and Hit Me Hard and Soft (2024). She also co-wrote and performed "No Time to Die" for the James Bond film of the same name, and "What Was I Made For?" for the film Barbie (2023), both of which won the Academy Award for Best Original Song, making her the youngest person to win two Academy Awards.

Artistry and public image

Eilish is known for her soft, whispered vocal style, dark and minimalist production, and music dealing with themes such as depression, climate change, and body image. She has been noted for her distinctive fashion sense, including oversized clothing. She has used her platform to advocate for climate action, voting rights, and animal rights, and she follows a vegan lifestyle.

Accolades

Among many honours, Eilish has received multiple Grammy Awards, two Academy Awards, two Golden Globe Awards, and numerous American Music Awards, Brit Awards, and MTV Video Music Awards, and has set several Guinness World Records.
