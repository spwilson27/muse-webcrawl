Sik Sik Yuen

Sik Sik Yuen (Chinese: 嗇色園) is a Hong Kong Taoist charitable organisation founded in 1921. It administers the famous Wong Tai Sin Temple in the Wong Tai Sin district of Kowloon, dedicated to the deity Wong Tai Sin, renowned for "answering whatever is requested".

Services

Beyond religious activities, the Yuen operates schools, elderly care homes, clinics providing Western and traditional Chinese medicine, and other social welfare and cultural services in Hong Kong, funded largely by donations at the temple. Its motto promotes the three teachings — Taoism, Buddhism and Confucianism — and the relief of suffering.
