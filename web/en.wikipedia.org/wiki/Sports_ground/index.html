Sports ground


A typical multi-use sports ground.

A sports ground is an outdoor area, venue, or facility set aside for playing sports. The term is broad: it covers everything from a village recreation ground with a cricket square and football pitch to the former home grounds of professional clubs. In British usage, "ground" is also the traditional word for the stadium of a football or cricket club, as in "county ground".

Types

Sports grounds typically include one or more playing surfaces — grass, artificial turf, clay, or hard courts — together with supporting facilities such as a pavilion or clubhouse, changing rooms, floodlights, and spectator areas ranging from a boundary rope to covered stands. Multi-sport grounds host different codes across seasons, for example cricket in summer and football in winter.

Community role

Local sports grounds, often owned by councils, parishes, schools, or sports clubs, provide the base of grassroots participation. In the United Kingdom, many are protected as playing fields, and their sale for development is subject to planning controls and consultation with bodies such as Sport England.
