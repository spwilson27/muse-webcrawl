The Memorial Tournament is a PGA Tour golf tournament founded and hosted by Jack Nicklaus. It is played each year in late May or early June at Muirfield Village Golf Club in Dublin, Ohio, a course designed by Nicklaus.

History

First played in 1976, the tournament was conceived by Nicklaus as a prestigious event honouring distinguished figures in golf; each year an honoree is inducted into the Captains Club. The limited invitational field makes it one of the more exclusive regular events on the PGA Tour schedule. Tiger Woods has won the tournament a record five times.

Course

Muirfield Village, named after Muirfield in Scotland, has also hosted the Ryder Cup (1987), the Solheim Cup and the Presidents Cup, and is noted for its amphitheatre-style finishing holes.
