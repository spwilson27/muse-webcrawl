2016 Chicago Cubs season

The 2016 Chicago Cubs season was the 145th season of the Chicago Cubs franchise and their 101st at Wrigley Field. Managed by Joe Maddon, the Cubs finished the regular season with a major league-best 103–58 record, won the National League Central, and went on to win the 2016 World Series over the Cleveland Indians — the franchise's first championship in 108 years, ending the famous "Curse of the Billy Goat."

2016 Chicago Cubs

League: National League
Division: Central (1st)
Record: 103–58 (.640)
Manager: Joe Maddon
Ballpark: Wrigley Field
Result: World Series champions

Regular season

The Cubs started the season strongly and never relinquished their division lead, clinching the NL Central on 15 September. Third baseman Kris Bryant was named National League MVP, while pitchers Jon Lester and Kyle Hendricks finished second and third in Cy Young voting; Hendricks led the majors with a 2.13 ERA. First baseman Anthony Rizzo anchored the lineup alongside Bryant.

Postseason

Chicago defeated the San Francisco Giants in the Division Series and the Los Angeles Dodgers in the NLCS to reach their first World Series since 1945. Facing Cleveland, the Cubs fell behind 3–1 in the series before winning three straight, capped by a dramatic 8–7 victory in ten innings in Game 7 after a rain delay in Cleveland. Ben Zobrist, whose RBI double in the tenth gave Chicago the lead, was named World Series MVP.

Legacy

The championship ended the longest title drought in North American professional sports and drew an estimated five million people to the victory parade and rally in Chicago.
