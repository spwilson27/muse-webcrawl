Gigabyte Technology

Gigabyte Technology (stylised as GIGABYTE) is a Taiwanese manufacturer and distributor of computer hardware, founded in 1986 and headquartered in Xindian District, New Taipei City.

Gigabyte is best known for motherboards and graphics cards, and also produces laptops (including under its AORUS gaming brand), desktop computers, PC components, monitors, servers and peripherals. It is one of the world's largest motherboard manufacturers, competing with ASUS, MSI and ASRock.

Brands

AORUS is Gigabyte's premium gaming brand, launched in 2014, covering motherboards, graphics cards, laptops and peripherals. The company has also marketed products under the AERO line for creators.
