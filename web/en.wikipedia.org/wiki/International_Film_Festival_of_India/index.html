International Film Festival of India

The International Film Festival of India (IFFI), founded in 1952, is one of the most significant film festivals in Asia. Held annually in the state of Goa, it is conducted jointly by the National Film Development Corporation and the Government of Goa.

History

The first edition was held in Bombay (now Mumbai) in 1952 under the patronage of Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru. The festival travelled to various Indian cities before finding a permanent venue in Goa in 2004.

Awards

The festival's top competition award is the Golden Peacock for Best Film. Other honours include the Silver Peacock awards for best director, actor and actress, and the Satyajit Ray Lifetime Achievement Award.

Organisation

IFFI is accredited by the International Federation of Film Producers Associations (FIAPF) as a competitive feature film festival.
