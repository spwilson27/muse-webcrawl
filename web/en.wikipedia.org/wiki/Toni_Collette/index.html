Toni Collette

Occupation: Actress, singer
Years active: 1990–present

Toni Collette (born 1 November 1972) is an Australian actress and singer known for her versatility in comedy and drama. She received an Academy Award nomination for The Sixth Sense (1999) and won an Emmy Award for the series United States of Tara.

Early life and breakthrough

Collette was born in Sydney and trained at the National Institute of Dramatic Art before leaving to pursue stage work. Her breakthrough came with the title role in the comedy Muriel's Wedding (1994), for which she gained weight and won international acclaim.

Career

Her films include Emma (1996), Velvet Goldmine (1998), About a Boy (2002), Little Miss Sunshine (2006) and the horror film Hereditary (2018), the latter winning her wide critical praise. On stage she has appeared on Broadway in The Wild Party.

Music

Collette has also sung professionally, performing with the band Toni Collette & the Finish and contributing songs to film soundtracks.
