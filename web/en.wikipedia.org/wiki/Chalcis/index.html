Chalcis

Chalcis (Greek: Χαλκίδα, Chalkida) is the chief town of the island of Euboea in Greece, situated on the Euripus Strait at its narrowest point. It is the capital of the regional unit of Euboea.

History

Ancient Chalcis was a major Ionian city, a great colonising power in the 8th–7th centuries BCE, founding settlements in Sicily, Italy and Chalcidice, which bears its name. Aristotle died here in 322 BCE. The modern city is known for the tidal phenomenon of the Euripus and its bridges connecting Euboea to the mainland.
