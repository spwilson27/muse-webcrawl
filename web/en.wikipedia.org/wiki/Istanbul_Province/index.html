Istanbul Province

Istanbul Province (Turkish: İstanbul ili) is a province of Turkey, located in the Marmara Region. It covers an area of about 5,461 square kilometres and has a population of roughly 16 million, making it by far the most populous province in the country. Its capital is the city of Istanbul, Turkey's largest city and economic centre.

Geography

The province straddles the Bosphorus strait, which divides it between Europe (Thrace) to the west and Asia (Anatolia) to the east. Istanbul Province is bordered by Tekirdağ Province to the west, Kocaeli Province to the east, the Black Sea to the north, and the Sea of Marmara to the south. The Bosphorus, the Golden Horn, and the Sea of Marmara shoreline shape much of its terrain, which also includes the Belgrade Forest and the Princes' Islands.

Administration

Since 2004 the boundaries of the Istanbul Metropolitan Municipality have coincided with those of the province. The province is divided into 39 districts, 25 of them on the European side and 14 on the Asian side. The Istanbul governorship (valilik) administers provincial affairs on behalf of the central government.

History

The province grew out of the Ottoman capital's administrative hinterland. After the founding of the Republic of Turkey in 1923, Istanbul lost its status as capital to Ankara but remained the country's commercial and cultural heart. Rapid industrialisation and migration from the rest of Turkey drove explosive growth in the second half of the 20th century, transforming the province into a megacity region.
