Táncház

Táncház (Hungarian for "dance house") is a Hungarian folk dance and music movement that began in Budapest in 1972, in which participants learn traditional dances socially, accompanied by live folk bands playing village music, principally from Transylvania. Led by musicians such as Ferenc Sebő, Béla Halmos and the band Muzsikás, the movement emphasised learning directly from elderly village tradition-bearers rather than staged choreography.

Recognition

The táncház method of teaching and transmitting intangible cultural heritage was selected by UNESCO for its Register of Good Safeguarding Practices in 2011. Dance houses remain a fixture of Hungarian cultural life, and the model has inspired folk-dance revival movements in other countries.
