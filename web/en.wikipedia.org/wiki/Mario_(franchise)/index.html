Mario is a Japanese media franchise created by game designer Shigeru Miyamoto for Nintendo. Centred on the fictional Italian plumber Mario and his adventures in the Mushroom Kingdom, usually rescuing Princess Peach from the villain Bowser, the franchise began with the arcade game Donkey Kong (1981) and is the best-selling video game franchise of all time, with more than 830 million copies sold across all its titles. Mario himself serves as Nintendo's mascot and one of the most recognisable fictional characters in the world.

History

Mario first appeared as "Jumpman" in Donkey Kong (1981) and in Mario Bros. (1983) alongside his brother Luigi. The franchise was defined by Super Mario Bros. (1985) for the Nintendo Entertainment System, which established the side-scrolling platform formula and helped revive the home console industry. Landmark titles followed, including Super Mario Bros. 3 (1988), Super Mario World (1990), Super Mario 64 (1996), a pioneering 3D platformer, Super Mario Galaxy (2007) and Super Mario Odyssey (2017).

Sub-series and spin-offs

Beyond the main platform games, the franchise spans the kart-racing series Mario Kart (whose 2014 entry Mario Kart 8 is among the best-selling games ever made), the Mario Party board-game series, role-playing games (Super Mario RPG, Paper Mario, Mario & Luigi), sports titles (tennis, golf, football), the Luigi's Mansion and Wario spin-offs, Super Mario Maker level-creation games, and educational and puzzle titles such as Dr. Mario.

Other media

The franchise extends to animated television series, manga, merchandise on an enormous scale, theme-park lands (Super Nintendo World, opened in Japan in 2021 and in Hollywood in 2023), and films: the poorly received live-action Super Mario Bros. (1993) and the animated The Super Mario Bros. Movie (2023), which grossed over $1.3 billion worldwide. Charles Martinet voiced Mario in games from the mid-1990s until 2023, when he became a "Mario Ambassador" and Kevin Afghani took over the role.
