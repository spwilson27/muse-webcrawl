The Colorado Buffaloes football team represents the University of Colorado Boulder in college football at the NCAA Division I FBS level. The Buffaloes play their home games at Folsom Field in Boulder and compete in the Big 12 Conference, which they rejoined in 2024 after a spell in the Pac-12. Before each home game, a live buffalo, Ralphie, leads the team onto the field in one of college football's most famous traditions.

History

Colorado fielded its first team in 1890. The program's peak came under coach Bill McCartney, whose 1990 team shared the national championship, defeating Notre Dame in the Orange Bowl. Running back Rashaan Salaam won the Heisman Trophy in 1994. Other notable seasons include the 1971 team, which finished third nationally, and the 2001 Big 12 championship team. In 2023 the program drew national attention with the hiring of head coach Deion Sanders, nicknamed "Coach Prime".

Rivalries

Colorado's historic rivalries include the Rocky Mountain Showdown with Colorado State and a long-running series with Nebraska, renewed after both schools' conference moves. The Buffaloes also developed rivalries with Utah in the Pac-12 era.
