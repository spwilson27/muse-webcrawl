Marathon is a series of science fiction first-person shooter video games developed and published by Bungie for the Apple Macintosh in the 1990s. The original trilogy consists of Marathon (1994), Marathon 2: Durandal (1995), and Marathon Infinity (1996). The games are noted for their unusually rich storytelling for the genre, delivered largely through computer terminals, and for features such as free mouse-look and extensive multiplayer modes.

Story

The player is a security officer aboard the colony ship UESC Marathon orbiting Tau Ceti IV in 2794, when the alien Pfhor attack. Guided — and manipulated — by the ship's AIs Leela, the rampant Durandal, and Tycho, the player fights the Pfhor and their enslaved S'pht across the trilogy, in a plot dealing with artificial intelligence "rampancy", alien ruins, and time travel.

Development and legacy

Released when the Macintosh had few flagship games, Marathon filled the role that Doom played on PC and introduced or popularised concepts including dual-wielding, rocket jumping in multiplayer ("SPNKR jumping"), and networked voice chat. Bungie later released the trilogy's source code; the open-source Aleph One engine keeps the games playable on modern systems. Bungie went on to create Halo, which shares themes and imagery with Marathon. In 2023 Bungie announced a new Marathon title, a PvP extraction shooter rebooting the franchise.
