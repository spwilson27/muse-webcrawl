Jeonju World Cup Stadium

Jeonju World Cup Stadium is a football stadium in Jeonju, South Korea, home of the K League club Jeonbuk Hyundai Motors. It has a capacity of around 42,000 spectators.

History

Opened in November 2001, the stadium was built for the 2002 FIFA World Cup, during which it hosted three matches, including Mexico's win over the United States in the round of 16. Its roof is designed to resemble the sound of the city.

Use

Since the World Cup it has served as Jeonbuk Hyundai Motors' home ground and hosted international matches and concerts. See also 2002 FIFA World Cup.
