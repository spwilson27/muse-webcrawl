National archives

National archives are central archives maintained by countries to preserve their national documentary heritage, government records of enduring value, and historical materials. They typically hold primary source documents including treaties, legislation, census records, maps, photographs and audiovisual material.

Functions

National archives acquire, preserve, catalogue and make accessible records. Examples include The National Archives in the United Kingdom (formed 2003), the National Archives and Records Administration (NARA) in the United States (established 1934), the Archives nationales in France (1794), and national archives in Canada, Australia, India and many other states.

Many have digitised large collections for online access, and play a role in freedom of information, records management standards and historical research.
