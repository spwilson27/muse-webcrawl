Snowstorm

A snowstorm is a weather event in which snow is the main form of precipitation, often accompanied by strong winds and reduced visibility.

A snowstorm is a storm in which snow falls heavily over a region, frequently driven by strong winds. When wind speeds reach sustained levels of 35 mph (56 km/h) or greater, with visibility reduced to less than a quarter of a mile for at least three hours, the event is classified as a blizzard in the United States and Canada. Lesser events may simply be described as snowstorms, snow squalls, or flurries depending on intensity and duration.

Types

Snowstorms take several forms. Lake-effect snow occurs when cold air passes over the relatively warm waters of a large lake, picking up moisture that falls as intense, localized snow bands downwind. Nor'easters are powerful cyclonic storms along the East Coast of North America that can produce blizzard conditions over a wide area. Alberta clippers are fast-moving low-pressure systems that sweep southeastward from Canada, typically bringing light to moderate snow and sharply colder air. Mountain regions experience orographic snowfall, enhanced where moist air is forced to rise over high terrain.

Impacts

Heavy snow disrupts transportation, closes roads and airports, downs power lines, and can collapse roofs under accumulated weight. Snowstorms in populated areas also strain emergency services and can lead to carbon monoxide poisoning when heating vents are blocked or generators are used indoors. Historically notable storms include the Great Blizzard of 1888 in the northeastern United States, the 1977 Buffalo blizzard, and the January 2016 United States blizzard, which set snowfall records in several major cities.

Measurement

Snowfall is measured with rulers inserted into accumulated snow on a flat surface, while snow depth and liquid-equivalent water content are recorded at weather stations. Forecasters use snowfall-rate and accumulation forecasts, and in the United States the National Weather Service issues winter storm watches and warnings, including specific blizzard warnings when criteria are met.
