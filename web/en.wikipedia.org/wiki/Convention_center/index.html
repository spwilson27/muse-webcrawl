A convention center (American English; convention centre or conference centre in British English) is a large building designed to hold a convention, where individuals and groups gather to promote and share common interests.

Characteristics

Convention centers typically offer a large exhibition hall (often 10,000 m² or more) subdivisible into smaller spaces, ballrooms, and meeting rooms. Hotels are frequently attached or nearby.

Types of events

They host trade fairs, industry conferences, political party conventions, academic conferences, and fan conventions such as Comic-Con.

Notable centers

  • McCormick Place, Chicago — the largest in North America
  • Hannover Messegelände, Germany — the world's largest exhibition ground
  • Las Vegas Convention Center
  • ExCeL London

Economics

Cities subsidise convention centers to attract visitor spending, though economists debate whether the promised economic returns materialise.
