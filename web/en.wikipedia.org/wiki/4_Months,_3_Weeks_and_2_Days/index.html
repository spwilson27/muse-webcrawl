4 Months, 3 Weeks and 2 Days

Poster for Cristian Mungiu's Palme d'Or-winning drama.

4 Months, 3 Weeks and 2 Days (Romanian: 4 luni, 3 săptămâni și 2 zile) is a 2007 Romanian drama film written and directed by Cristian Mungiu. Set in Bucharest in 1987, during the final years of Nicolae Ceaușescu's communist regime, it follows two university students, Otilia (Anamaria Marinca) and Găbița (Laura Vasiliu), roommates who arrange an illegal abortion for Găbița, whose pregnancy gives the film its title.

Plot

Otilia books a hotel room and meets the abortionist, "Mr. Bebe" (Vlad Ivanov), who, finding they have less money than agreed and that the pregnancy is further along than claimed — raising the legal danger to himself — demands sex from both women as payment. The procedure is completed, and Otilia must dispose of the fetus while attending a family dinner at her boyfriend's parents' home. The film ends with the two women silently resolved never to speak of the day again.

Reception and legacy

The film premiered at the 2007 Cannes Film Festival, where it won the Palme d'Or, the first Romanian film to do so. Shot largely in long, unbroken takes in a muted, realist style, it is regarded as a landmark of the Romanian New Wave and as a powerful depiction of life under Decree 770, the regime's draconian ban on abortion and contraception. It was widely acclaimed by critics, though it was controversially not nominated for the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film.
