Mabey Compact 200

The Mabey Compact 200 is a modular steel panel bridge system produced by the British engineering company Mabey. It is a development of the wartime Bailey bridge concept, using prefabricated steel panels that can be transported by truck or container and assembled rapidly on site with a minimum of heavy equipment.

Design and use

The system can be configured as single or multi-span crossings, with single or double lanes, and can carry heavy highway or military vehicle loads. Because the components are standardised and interchangeable, Compact 200 bridges are widely used for emergency and disaster relief, temporary works crossings during construction, and permanent rural infrastructure in developing countries. They can be launched from one bank without work in the gap, an advantage inherited from the Bailey bridge design.

Background

Mabey, founded in the United Kingdom, built its business on acquiring surplus Bailey bridge stocks and later manufacturing its own panel bridge systems, of which the Compact 200 became one of the most widely exported models, used by armed forces, aid agencies, and road authorities in dozens of countries.
