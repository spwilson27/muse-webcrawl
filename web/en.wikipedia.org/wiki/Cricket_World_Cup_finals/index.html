List of Cricket World Cup finals

The Cricket World Cup trophy, contested in the tournament final.

The Cricket World Cup is the international championship of One Day International cricket, organised by the International Cricket Council (ICC) and held roughly every four years. The tournament concludes with a single final match between the two semi-final winners.

Australia is the most successful team in the tournament's history, having won the title six times. India and the West Indies have each won twice, while Pakistan, Sri Lanka and England have each won once. The 2019 final between England and New Zealand is widely regarded as one of the greatest one-day matches ever played: the scores finished level, and England won on boundary count after a tied Super Over.
