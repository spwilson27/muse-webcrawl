List of regions of Japan

Japan is traditionally divided into eight regions (地方, chihō), running from north to south: Hokkaidō, Tōhoku, Kantō, Chūbu, Kansai, Chūgoku, Shikoku and Kyūshū. The regions are not official administrative units — government is organised through 47 prefectures — but they are widely used for geography, weather forecasts, business organisation and cultural identity. Okinawa Prefecture is often grouped with Kyūshū or treated as its own (Ryukyu) region.

Subdivisions

Chūbu is often subdivided into Hokuriku (the Sea of Japan coast), Kōshin'etsu and Tōkai (the Pacific side). Chūgoku divides into San'in (north) and San'yō (south). The Kantō and Kansai regions anchor the country's two great metropolitan areas, Greater Tokyo and Keihanshin (Osaka–Kobe–Kyoto).
