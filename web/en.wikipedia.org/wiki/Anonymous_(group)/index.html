Anonymous is a decentralised international activist and hacktivist collective and movement primarily known for its various cyberattacks against several governments, government institutions and agencies, corporations, and the Church of Scientology. Its participants, often called "Anons", commonly wear Guy Fawkes masks popularised by the graphic novel and film V for Vendetta.

Anonymous originated in 2003 on the imageboard 4chan as a shared identity used by users posting without a name. It has no leadership, membership list or formal structure; anyone may act under the name, which has made the collective both resilient and unpredictable. Its actions have ranged from pranks and protest campaigns to distributed denial-of-service attacks and data breaches.

History

Early years and Project Chanology

In its early form, Anonymous was associated with coordinated pranks and raids organised on 4chan's /b/ board. The collective gained wider public attention in 2008 with Project Chanology, a campaign against the Church of Scientology launched after the Church attempted to remove a leaked promotional video featuring Tom Cruise from the internet. The campaign combined online attacks with street protests in which participants wore Guy Fawkes masks.

Activism and hacktivism

In 2010 Anonymous launched Operation Payback in support of file-sharing sites, and later supported WikiLeaks by attacking the websites of payment companies that had cut off donations to the organisation. Campaigns followed in support of the Arab Spring uprisings, Occupy Wall Street, and various regional protest movements. Offshoot groups such as LulzSec carried out high-profile breaches in 2011 before disbanding amid arrests.

Later activity

Anonymous-branded operations have continued intermittently, including campaigns against extremist organisations, government surveillance, and, in 2022, Russian state websites and media following the invasion of Ukraine. Because the name is open to anyone, the authenticity and coordination of individual operations vary widely.

Characteristics

Anonymous is frequently described as an "internet gathering" rather than an organisation. Participants communicate through imageboards, chat channels and social media. The collective's iconography includes the Guy Fawkes mask and a logo depicting a suited figure with a question mark in place of a head, overlaid on a globe. Its slogans include "We are Legion", a reference to the biblical Legion, and "Knowledge is free".

Arrests and criticism

Numerous participants have been arrested in the United States, United Kingdom, Netherlands, Spain and other countries in connection with attacks attributed to Anonymous. Critics have accused the collective of vigilantism and of harming bystanders through indiscriminate data leaks, while supporters describe it as a form of digital protest and civil disobedience.
