Adafruit Industries

Adafruit Industries is an American open-source hardware company based in New York City, founded in 2005 by engineer Limor Fried (known online as "Ladyada"). The company designs and manufactures electronic components, development boards, tools and kits for hobbyists, educators and makers, and sells them through its online store.

Products and community

Adafruit is known for its tutorials and open documentation, the CircuitPython programming environment for microcontrollers, the Feather and Trinket development-board lines, NeoPixel addressable LEDs, and wearable electronics kits such as FLORA and Gemma. It publishes extensive learning guides and video content aimed at making electronics accessible, and is a prominent participant in the open-source hardware and maker movements.
