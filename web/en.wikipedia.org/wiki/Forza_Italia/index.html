Forza Italia (FI; lit. "Forward Italy" or "Let's Go Italy") is a centre-right political party in Italy, whose ideology includes elements of liberal conservatism, Christian democracy, liberalism and populism. FI is a member of the European People's Party. Silvio Berlusconi was the party's leader and president until his death in 2023, and has been succeeded by Antonio Tajani.

History

The party was founded in December 1993 and won its first general election soon after, in March 1994, just three months later. It was the main party of three coalitions: the Pole of Freedoms (1994), the Pole for Freedoms (1996) and the House of Freedoms (2001 and 2006). In 2008 the party merged with National Alliance and several minor parties to form The People of Freedom (PdL), an enlarged single party of the centre-right, but in 2013 the party was revived under its old name by Berlusconi after a split within the PdL.

Ideology

The party's ideology is a mix of liberal conservatism, Christian democracy, liberalism and populism. On social issues the party has combined Catholic values with liberal positions on some matters, while advocating lower taxes, deregulation and a strong Atlanticist foreign policy within the European Union.
