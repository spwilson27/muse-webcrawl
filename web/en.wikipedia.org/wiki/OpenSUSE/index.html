openSUSE

openSUSE is a free and open-source Linux distribution developed by the openSUSE project and sponsored principally by SUSE. It descends from SUSE Linux, which began in Germany in 1994, and shares much technology with the commercial SUSE Linux Enterprise distribution. Its mascot is a green chameleon named Geeko.

Editions

The project offers two main editions: Leap, a regular-release distribution aligned with SUSE Linux Enterprise sources, and Tumbleweed, a rolling-release distribution continuously updated from the Factory development branch after automated testing with openQA. openSUSE is known for the YaST configuration tool, the Zypper package manager, the Open Build Service and the Btrfs/Snapper snapshot-and-rollback setup.

History

SUSE was acquired by Novell in 2003, which opened development to the community as the openSUSE project in 2005. MicroOS and other container-focused variants extend the family for servers and immutable desktops.
