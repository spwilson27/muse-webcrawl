Orcs Must Die!

Orcs Must Die!
Developer: Robot Entertainment
Release: 2011
Genre: Tower defense, action
Platforms: Xbox 360, Microsoft Windows

Orcs Must Die! is a tower-defense action video game developed and published by Robot Entertainment, released in 2011 for Xbox Live Arcade and Microsoft Windows. The player takes the role of a War Mage defending magical rifts against waves of invading orcs and other monsters.

Gameplay

Unlike purely strategic tower-defense games, the player character fights on the battlefield in third person, wielding weapons and spells while placing traps — spike traps, arrow walls, spring traps and tar pits — along the enemies' path. Success is rated with skulls that can be spent upgrading traps between levels.

Reception and sequels

The game was praised for blending action with strategy and was followed by Orcs Must Die! 2 (2012), which added cooperative play, the free-to-play Orcs Must Die! Unchained, and Orcs Must Die! 3 (2020).
