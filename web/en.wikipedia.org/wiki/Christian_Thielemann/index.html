Christian Thielemann (born 1 April 1959) is a German conductor, regarded as a leading interpreter of the German Romantic repertoire, especially the operas of Richard Wagner and the symphonies of Anton Bruckner. He has held chief positions with the Deutsche Oper Berlin, the Munich Philharmonic, and the Staatskapelle Dresden, and conducted the Vienna Philharmonic's New Year's Concert in 2019 and 2024. In 2024 he succeeded Daniel Barenboim as general music director of the Berlin State Opera.

Early life and training

Thielemann was born in Berlin and studied viola and composition at the Hochschule für Musik in the city. He worked as a répétiteur and assistant at houses including the Deutsche Oper Berlin, and served as an assistant to Herbert von Karajan and Daniel Barenboim — formative influences on his monumental, tradition-conscious conducting style. Early appointments included Düsseldorf and Nuremberg, where he became, at 29, one of Germany's youngest general music directors.

Career

Deutsche Oper Berlin and Munich

Thielemann was general music director of the Deutsche Oper Berlin from 1997 to 2004, resigning amid disputes over funding. He then led the Munich Philharmonic (2004–2011), where his tenure also ended in disagreement with the city administration over concert planning, though his Bruckner performances with the orchestra were widely praised.

Bayreuth and Dresden

Thielemann made his Bayreuth Festival debut in 2000 and became the festival's most regular conductor of the following two decades, serving as its music director from 2015 to 2020; he led celebrated productions of Tristan und Isolde, Lohengrin, and the Ring cycle. From 2012 to 2024 he was chief conductor of the Staatskapelle Dresden, taking the orchestra on extensive tours and recording cycles of Bruckner, Schumann, and Beethoven. Dresden's management decided in 2021 not to extend his contract beyond 2024, a decision that drew protest from the orchestra's musicians.

Vienna and Berlin

A close relationship with the Vienna Philharmonic brought acclaimed recordings of the Beethoven and Bruckner symphonies and two New Year's Concerts (2019 and 2024). In 2023 it was announced that Thielemann would succeed Daniel Barenboim as general music director of the Staatsoper Unter den Linden in Berlin, beginning in September 2024.

Style and reputation

Thielemann is noted for slow tempos, a dark, burnished orchestral sound, and fidelity to the German performance tradition descending from Wilhelm Furtwängler. His repertoire centres on Wagner, Strauss, Bruckner, Brahms, and Beethoven, and he rarely performs music written after the mid-20th century. He has also attracted controversy over outspoken remarks about German cultural politics.
