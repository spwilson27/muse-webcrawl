List of counties in Vermont

The U.S. state of Vermont has 14 counties. Reflecting Vermont's New England town-based governance, county government is weak: counties principally provide courts, sheriffs, and a county seat town ("shire town"). Nine of Vermont's counties were created before statehood in 1791, and five after. Populations below are approximate, from the 2020 census era.

Etymology notes

County names reflect a mix of English place names (e.g., Rutland, Windsor), Revolutionary-era figures (Chittenden, named for Governor Thomas Chittenden), geographic features (Grand Isle), and older colonial counties from New York's claims to the territory.
