This is a list of proper names of stars — traditional names, many of Arabic, Greek or Latin origin, that have been formally approved by the International Astronomical Union (IAU) through its Working Group on Star Names (WGSN), established in 2016. Approved names include Sirius, Vega, Betelgeuse and Polaris; new names have also been assigned through the IAU's NameExoWorlds campaigns.
