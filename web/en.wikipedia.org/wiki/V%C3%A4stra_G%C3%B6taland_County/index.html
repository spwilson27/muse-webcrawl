Västra Götaland County

Västra Götaland County

Country: Sweden

Capital: Gothenburg (seat of county administration)

Formed: 1998

Population: approx. 1.7 million

Västra Götaland County (Swedish: Västra Götalands län) is a county on the western coast of Sweden. It was created on 1 January 1998 by merging the former counties of Gothenburg and Bohus, Älvsborg and Skaraborg. With about 1.7 million inhabitants it is the second most populous county in Sweden.

Geography

The county borders Norway and stretches from the Kattegat coast and the Gothenburg archipelago inland to Sweden's two largest lakes, Vänern and Vättern. Gothenburg, Sweden's second-largest city and largest port, is the administrative capital; Vänersborg is the seat of the regional council.

Administration

The county contains 49 municipalities, the most of any Swedish county. The Västra Götaland Regional Council is responsible for public transport (Västtrafik), healthcare and regional development, while the County Administrative Board represents the national government in the county.
