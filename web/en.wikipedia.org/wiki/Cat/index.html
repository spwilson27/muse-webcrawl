The cat is a small domesticated carnivorous mammal, descended from the African wildcat, that has lived with humans for roughly 10,000 years — since the first farming villages of the Near East, where stored grain (see Wheat) drew rodents and the cats that hunt them.

Behaviour and culture

Cats are solitary hunters with acute night vision, hearing and balance, famous for independence yet capable of strong bonds with people. Venerated in ancient Egypt (a theme of Egyptology), demonised in medieval Europe, they are now among the world's most popular pets — and, as hunters of wildlife, an ecological concern on islands and continents alike.
