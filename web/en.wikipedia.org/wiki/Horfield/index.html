Horfield

Horfield is a suburb and ward of Bristol, England, situated in the north of the city between Filton and Bishopston. It is centred on the northern stretch of Gloucester Road, one of the city's main shopping and nightlife streets.

Features

Horfield is a largely Victorian and Edwardian residential area of terraced housing. Notable features include Horfield Common, one of Bristol's larger green spaces, and the Memorial Stadium, home of Bristol Rovers Football Club. The area is served by the parish church of St Edmund and lies close to the former Horfield Prison and the old Filton airfield area to the north.
