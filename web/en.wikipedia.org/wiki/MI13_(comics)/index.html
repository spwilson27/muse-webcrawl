MI13 (comics)

MI13 and its agents in Marvel Comics

MI13, sometimes written MI:13, is a fictional British intelligence agency appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Charged with defending the United Kingdom from supernatural, extraterrestrial and superhuman threats — the "weird happenings" remit — it deals with matters involving magic, vampires, fairies and alien incursions.

Publication history

The organization grew out of earlier fictional British agencies in Marvel continuity, including STRIKE and the Department of Paranormal Activities associated with the team Excalibur. MI13 came to prominence under Pete Wisdom, who appeared as its field leader in the series New Excalibur and then headlined Captain Britain and MI13 (2008–2009), written by Paul Cornell with art by Leonard Kirk. That series, launched during the Skrull Secret Invasion, was critically acclaimed and nominated for a Hugo Award.

Members and storylines

Key agents and allies include Pete Wisdom, Captain Britain (Brian Braddock), the vampire Spitfire, the Black Knight (Dane Whitman), Blade, Faiza Hussain (who wields the sword Excalibur and later took the Captain Britain mantle), and John the Skrull. Major storylines involved the invasion of the Skrulls, Dracula's assault on Britain from his base on the Moon in "Vampire State", and the agency's uneasy relationship with other Marvel intelligence organizations such as S.H.I.E.L.D. and S.W.O.R.D.
