London Luton Airport (IATA: LTN, ICAO: EGGW) is an international airport located in Luton, Bedfordshire, England, about 29 miles (47 km) north of Central London. It is one of six international airports serving the London region, alongside Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted, City and Southend. The airport is a major base for low-cost carriers, particularly easyJet (which has its head office at the airport), Wizz Air, Ryanair and TUI Airways.

History

The airport opened on 16 July 1938 as Luton Municipal Airport. During the Second World War it was used by the Royal Air Force as a fighter station. In the post-war decades it became a popular departure point for package holiday charter flights; the 1970s television advertisement featuring Lorraine Chase and the slogan of Campari helped make its name famous in popular culture. The airport was renamed London Luton Airport in 1990 to emphasise its position within the London airport system. A major expansion of the terminal was completed in 2018, and passenger numbers reached over 18 million in 2019.

Facilities and transport

The airport has a single passenger terminal and one runway, 07/25, of 2,160 metres. It is served by Luton Airport Parkway railway station on the Midland Main Line, connected to the terminal by the Luton DART automated people mover, which opened in 2023 and replaced a shuttle bus service. Road access is via the M1 motorway (junction 10).
