Vanguard-class submarine

The Vanguard class is a class of nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarines (SSBNs) in service with the Royal Navy. The class comprises four boats — HMS Vanguard, Victorious, Vigilant and Vengeance — built by Vickers (now BAE Systems Submarine Solutions) at Barrow-in-Furness between 1986 and 1999. They carry the Trident II D5 submarine-launched ballistic missile and provide the United Kingdom's continuous at-sea nuclear deterrent, based at HMNB Clyde (Faslane).

Design

Displacing nearly 16,000 tonnes submerged, the Vanguard class is powered by a Rolls-Royce PWR2 pressurised water reactor driving a pump-jet propulsor, giving a submerged speed of over 25 knots. Each boat has 16 missile tubes for Trident II D5 missiles, each capable of carrying multiple warheads; under policy the boats deploy with reduced loadings. The class introduced new sonar, command and habitability standards, and each submarine operates with two crews ("port" and "starboard") to sustain continuous deterrent patrols.

Service

HMS Vanguard entered service in 1993 and the class took over the deterrent role from the Resolution class in the mid-1990s. Since 1969 the Royal Navy has maintained an unbroken continuous at-sea deterrent. Long overhaul and refuelling periods at Devonport have extended the class's life; the boats are to be replaced from the early 2030s by the Dreadnought class.
