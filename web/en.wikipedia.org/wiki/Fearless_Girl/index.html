Fearless Girl

Artist: Kristen Visbal
Year: 2017
Medium: Bronze sculpture
Location: Broad Street, facing the New York Stock Exchange, New York City

Fearless Girl is a bronze sculpture by Kristen Visbal, commissioned by the asset management firm State Street Global Advisors (SSGA) and installed in Manhattan's Financial District in March 2017. Depicting a young girl in a defiant pose — feet planted, hands on hips, chin up — it was initially placed facing the famous Charging Bull statue, becoming an instant global symbol in debates about gender diversity in corporate leadership.

History

The statue was installed on the eve of International Women's Day, 7 March 2017, as the centrepiece of an SSGA campaign pressing companies in which it invested to add more women to their boards; a plaque read "Know the power of women in leadership. SHE makes a difference," the final word being the ticker symbol of SSGA's gender-diversity exchange-traded fund. Originally permitted for one week in Bowling Green park, the statue's stay was repeatedly extended amid great public enthusiasm and large crowds. Its juxtaposition with Charging Bull drew a strong protest from that statue's sculptor, Arturo Di Modica, who argued that Fearless Girl transformed his work's meaning into an advertising stunt.

Relocation and replicas

In December 2018 Fearless Girl was moved a short distance to its current position on Broad Street, facing the New York Stock Exchange, where it received a long-term permit. Authorised replicas have since been installed in other cities, including Oslo, Melbourne, and London (in Paternoster Square near the London Stock Exchange). The statue won major advertising industry awards, including top honours at the Cannes Lions festival, and its image has been widely used by campaigners for women's representation in business and politics — as well as criticised as an example of corporate "femvertising", a criticism sharpened when State Street paid a settlement in 2017 over allegations it had underpaid female and minority employees.
