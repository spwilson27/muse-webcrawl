Netherlands Maritime Special Operations Forces

The Netherlands Maritime Special Operations Forces (NLMARSOF, also simply MARSOF) is the special forces unit of the Netherlands Marine Corps, part of the Royal Netherlands Navy. It was formally established in 2013 by merging the earlier Mountain Leader reconnaissance troop, the Unit Interventie Mariniers (UIM) and the frogman (combat diver) platoon into a single maritime special operations command.

Organisation

NLMARSOF consists of three squadrons: C Squadron, specialised in conventional special operations and reconnaissance including mountain and arctic warfare; M Squadron, the maritime counter-terrorism element, heir to the UIM and tasked with large-scale interventions such as offshore installations and shipping; and T Squadron, responsible for training and support. Operators complete a demanding selection and training pipeline, including combat diving, parachuting and mountain leader courses.

Operations

MARSOF personnel have deployed on counter-piracy operations off Somalia, with special operations task groups in Afghanistan and Mali, and in support of NATO missions. The unit works closely with allied maritime special forces such as the British SBS and German Kampfschwimmer.
