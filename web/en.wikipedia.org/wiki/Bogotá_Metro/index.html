Bogotá Metro

Bogotá Metro
Locale: Bogotá, Colombia
Transit type: Rapid transit (elevated metro)
Lines: 1 (under construction), more planned
Status: Under construction since 2020

The Bogotá Metro (Spanish: Metro de Bogotá) is a rapid transit project under construction in Bogotá, Colombia. A metro for the capital had been proposed since the 1940s; construction of the first, elevated line began in 2020, with a Chinese-led consortium, Metro Línea 1, building the line.

Line 1

The first line runs about 24 km from the southwest of the city (Bosa) to Calle 72 in the northeast, with 16 stations, entirely on viaduct. It is designed to interchange with the TransMilenio bus rapid transit system.

History

Numerous studies and failed tenders marked the project's long gestation. The contract was awarded in 2019 and ground was broken in October 2020. A second line, partly underground, has also been tendered. See also Bogotá.
