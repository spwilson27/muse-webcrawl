Battle of Rorke's Drift
Date: 22–23 January 1879
Location: Natal, South Africa
Part of: Anglo-Zulu War

The Battle of Rorke's Drift was an engagement in the Anglo-Zulu War fought on 22 and 23 January 1879. About 150 British and colonial troops under Lieutenants John Chard and Gonville Bromhead defended the mission station at Rorke's Drift against repeated attacks by 3,000 to 4,000 Zulu warriors.

Background

Hours earlier, a Zulu army had destroyed a British column at Isandlwana. Survivors warned the garrison, which improvised defences from mealie bags and biscuit boxes.

The battle

Attacks continued through the night, setting the hospital ablaze. The defenders held a shrinking perimeter until the Zulu withdrew at dawn. Eleven Victoria Crosses were awarded, the most for a single action by one regiment.

Aftermath

The defence became famous in Britain, offsetting news of Isandlwana, and was dramatised in the 1964 film Zulu.
