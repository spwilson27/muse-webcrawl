Image Comics

Founded: 1992

Founders: Erik Larsen, Jim Lee, Rob Liefeld, Todd McFarlane, Whilce Portacio, Marc Silvestri, Jim Valentino

Headquarters: Portland, Oregon

Image Comics is an American comic book publisher founded in 1992 by a group of high-profile artists who left Marvel Comics over disputes about creator ownership of their work. It is the third-largest comic book publisher in the United States by market share, and is distinctive for publishing creator-owned titles: Image itself owns no intellectual property except its name and logo, with creators retaining rights to their characters.

History

The seven founders — Erik Larsen, Jim Lee, Rob Liefeld, Todd McFarlane, Whilce Portacio, Marc Silvestri and Jim Valentino — launched the company with series including McFarlane's Spawn, Liefeld's Youngblood, Lee's WildC.A.T.s, Larsen's The Savage Dragon and Silvestri's Cyberforce. The books were commercial sensations: Spawn #1 sold about 1.7 million copies, and Spawn became one of the longest-running creator-owned series in American comics. Jim Lee's studio later left to become WildStorm under DC Comics in 1998.

Later development

Under publisher Eric Stephenson, Image broadened in the 2010s beyond superheroes into acclaimed genre and literary titles, including The Walking Dead by Robert Kirkman (whose success as a television series transformed the company), Saga by Brian K. Vaughan and Fiona Staples, Invincible, Chew, East of West and Monstress. Image has repeatedly ranked as the third-largest US comics publisher behind Marvel and DC and has won numerous Eisner and Harvey Awards.
