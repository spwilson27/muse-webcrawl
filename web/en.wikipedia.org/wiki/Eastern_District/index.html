Eastern District

Eastern District may refer to several places and administrative divisions around the world.

Places

  • Eastern District, Hong Kong — one of the 18 districts of Hong Kong, located on the north-eastern part of Hong Kong Island, covering areas including North Point, Quarry Bay, Shau Kei Wan and Chai Wan. It is among the most populous districts of the territory, with a population of over half a million.
  • Eastern District, Samoa — a former district of Western Samoa on the island of Upolu.
  • Eastern District, American Samoa — one of the three primary administrative subdivisions of American Samoa, comprising the eastern portion of the island of Tutuila together with Aunu'u Island. It includes the capital, Pago Pago, and has a population of roughly 23,000.
  • Eastern District, Taichung — a district of Taichung, Taiwan.

Courts

In the United States, "Eastern District" is the short name of several federal judicial districts, including the Eastern District of New York, the Eastern District of Virginia (famed for its speedy "rocket docket"), the Eastern District of Texas, the Eastern District of California, the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, and the Eastern District of Michigan, among others.
