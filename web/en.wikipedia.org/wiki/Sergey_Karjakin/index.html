Sergey Alexandrovich Karjakin (born 12 January 1990 in Simferopol, Ukrainian SSR) is a Russian (formerly Ukrainian) chess grandmaster. A child prodigy, he became a grandmaster at the age of 12 years and 7 months — for many years the youngest in history — earning the nickname "Minister of Defence" for his exceptional resourcefulness in difficult positions.

Career

Karjakin won the World Under-12 Championship in 2001 and gained attention as a second to Ruslan Ponomariov during the 2002 FIDE World Championship. He became the youngest player to qualify for a Candidates Tournament through the 2007 World Cup cycle, won the elite Norway Chess tournament in 2013 and 2014, and triumphed in the 2015 Chess World Cup. In March 2016 he won the Candidates Tournament in Moscow, earning the right to challenge Magnus Carlsen for the World Chess Championship in New York that November; after a 6–6 tie in classical games, he lost the rapid tiebreaks. He also won the World Rapid Championship (2012) and World Blitz Championship (2016).

Change of federation and later activity

Born in Crimea, Karjakin switched from the Ukrainian to the Russian chess federation in 2009 and became a Russian citizen. In 2022, following statements about the Russian invasion of Ukraine, the FIDE Ethics and Disciplinary Commission barred him from FIDE-rated events for six months; he subsequently entered Russian politics, becoming a senator in 2024.
