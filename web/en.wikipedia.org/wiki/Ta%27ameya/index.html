Ta'ameya

Ta'ameya (Egyptian Arabic: طعمية) is an Egyptian fritter made from fava beans, regarded as the Egyptian counterpart of falafel, which elsewhere in the Levant is typically made from chickpeas or a mixture of chickpeas and fava beans.

Preparation

Soaked fava beans are ground with herbs such as parsley, coriander, and leek, seasoned, formed into patties or balls, sometimes coated in sesame seeds or stuffed with onions and tomatoes, and deep-fried until crisp.
