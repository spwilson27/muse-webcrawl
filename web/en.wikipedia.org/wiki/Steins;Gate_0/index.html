Steins;Gate 0 is a visual novel video game developed by 5pb. (now Mages Inc.), part of the Science Adventure series. It is a sequel — more precisely a "midquel" — to Steins;Gate (2009), expanding on the events of that game's ending. It was released in Japan in December 2015 for PlayStation 3, PlayStation 4, and PlayStation Vita, followed by releases on Windows, Xbox One, and Nintendo Switch, with English localization by PQube and Spike Chunsoft.

Story

The story follows Rintaro Okabe in the Beta world line, where he failed to save Kurisu Makise and, traumatized and guilt-ridden, has abandoned his mad-scientist persona and his research into time travel. At a university seminar he encounters Maho Hiyajo and Alexis Leskinen, colleagues of Kurisu, who present Amadeus, an artificial intelligence system built from Kurisu's digitized memories. Okabe becomes a tester for Amadeus, speaking with a simulation of Kurisu, while rival powers pursue Kurisu's thesis on time machines and the world line converges toward the Third World War of 2036. The narrative branches between story routes determined by whether Okabe answers or ignores calls from Amadeus.

Gameplay and adaptation

Like its predecessor, the game is largely read as text with occasional player choices; Steins;Gate 0 replaces the original's phone-trigger system with the RINE messaging app and Amadeus call choices. The game received generally positive reviews for its darker, more melancholic story. Ephemeral light novels and a manga preceded and accompanied the game, and a 23-episode anime television adaptation by White Fox aired from April to September 2018.
