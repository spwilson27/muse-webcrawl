The Open Group

The Open Group is a global consortium that develops vendor-neutral technology standards and certifications. It was formed in 1996 by the merger of X/Open and the Open Software Foundation, two rival bodies of the Unix wars era.

Role and standards

The Open Group owns the UNIX trademark and administers the Single UNIX Specification, against which operating systems are certified as "UNIX" products. Its best-known publications are TOGAF (The Open Group Architecture Framework), the ArchiMate modelling language, the Open Agile Architecture standard, and the FACE and SOSA technical standards for military avionics and sensor systems. It also maintains the Open Trusted Technology Provider Standard for supply-chain security.

Organisation

The consortium is funded by member organisations, which include major technology vendors, government agencies and large IT users. It operates forums and work groups devoted to architecture, security, trusted technology and industry verticals, and runs professional certification programs such as TOGAF certification and the Open Certified Architect (Open CA) credential.
