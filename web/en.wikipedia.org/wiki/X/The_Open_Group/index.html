The Open Group

The Open Group is a global consortium that enables the achievement of business objectives through technology standards and open source initiatives, with more than 900 member organisations. It was formed in 1996 by the merger of X/Open and the Open Software Foundation. The Open Group owns the UNIX trademark and publishes the Single UNIX Specification.

Standards and activities

The consortium is best known for TOGAF, its enterprise architecture framework, and for ArchiMate, an enterprise modelling language. It also maintains the Single UNIX Specification and certification programmes (via the Austin Group jointly with the IEEE), the Open Trusted Technology Provider Standard (O-TTPS) for supply-chain security, the FACE and SOSA consortia for avionics and sensor systems, and the Open Footprint Forum for environmental data.

Certification

The Open Group runs certification schemes for products (UNIX systems, TOGAF tools), professionals (TOGAF, ArchiMate, IT specialists) and conformance with its standards. Its governing structure includes forums and work groups open to members, and offices in locations including San Francisco, Reading (UK), São Paulo, Mumbai and Shanghai.
