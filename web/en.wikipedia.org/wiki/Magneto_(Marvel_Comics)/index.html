Magneto (real name Erik Lehnsherr, born Max Eisenhardt) is a character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, created by writer Stan Lee and artist Jack Kirby. He first appeared in The X-Men #1 (1963) as the archenemy of the X-Men, a powerful mutant with the ability to generate and control magnetic fields. A Holocaust survivor whose family was murdered in the camps, Magneto is driven by the conviction that mutants must protect themselves from human persecution by any means necessary — a philosophy that repeatedly brings him into conflict with his old friend Charles Xavier, who dreams of peaceful coexistence.

Publication history

Magneto debuted as a straightforward villain leading the Brotherhood of Evil Mutants. Over subsequent decades, particularly under writer Chris Claremont in the 1970s and 1980s, the character was given a tragic backstory as a survivor of Auschwitz, transforming him into one of comics' most complex figures — by turns terrorist, antihero, and occasional ally or leader of the X-Men themselves.

Fictional character biography

Max Eisenhardt survived the Sonderkommando at Auschwitz, where his emerging powers could not save his family. After the war he settled in Israel and befriended Charles Xavier, with whom he debated mutantkind's future. His turn to violence followed the death of his daughter and attacks by fearful humans. As Magneto, he founded the Brotherhood of Mutants, seized military bases, and later ruled the mutant nation of Genosha and helped found the mutant state of Krakoa. His iconic helmet shields him from telepathic intrusion.

Powers and abilities

Magneto is classified as an Omega-level mutant. He can manipulate the electromagnetic force on a planetary scale: moving metal objects of enormous mass, generating force fields, flying, and even affecting the iron in living bodies. His intellect is also formidable; he has constructed advanced technology, including orbital bases and devices to alter mutant genetics.

In other media

The character was portrayed by Ian McKellen in the X-Men film series beginning in 2000, and by Michael Fassbender as the younger Magneto beginning with X-Men: First Class (2011).
