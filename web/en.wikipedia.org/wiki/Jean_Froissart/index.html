Jean Froissart

Jean Froissart (c. 1337 – c. 1405) was a French-speaking medieval author and court historian from the Low Countries, best known for his Chronicles.

Chronicles

Froissart's Chronicles cover events in western Europe from about 1322 to 1400, with a focus on the Hundred Years' War between England and France. Based on his own travels and interviews with nobles and knights, they are a major source for the age of chivalry, though coloured by his aristocratic sympathies.

Life

Born in Valenciennes, Froissart served Queen Philippa of Hainault, consort of Edward III of England, and later other patrons. He also wrote poetry, including the romance Méliador. See also Hundred Years' War.
