Internet Explorer

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Internet Explorer
Released16 August 1995
DeveloperMicrosoft
Discontinued15 June 2022 (most Windows versions)
SuccessorMicrosoft Edge
[Image: The Internet Explorer logo: a blue lowercase e with a gold ring orbiting it]

Internet Explorer (formerly Microsoft Internet Explorer, commonly abbreviated IE) is a discontinued series of graphical web browsers developed by Microsoft, part of the Windows operating system line from 1995 to 2022.

IE began as licensed code from Spyglass's Mosaic, and Microsoft pursued Netscape with relentless iteration and a decisive weapon: it was bundled free with Windows. Versions 4 and 5 won the first browser war, and at its 2002–2003 peak IE held about 95% of the browser market. That dominance — and the bundling that achieved it — was central to the United States v. Microsoft antitrust case.

Victory bred stagnation: after IE6 (2001) development nearly stopped for five years, security holes multiplied, and web developers despaired of its non-standard rendering, which 'quirks mode' preserved for a generation of sites. Firefox (2004) and then Google Chrome (2008) dismantled its share; Microsoft retired the brand in favour of Microsoft Edge, and on 15 June 2022 Internet Explorer was disabled on mainstream Windows, ending a 27-year run — mourned ironically by the developers who had cursed it, many in South Korea and in corporate intranets where it lingered longest.
