Chantilly cream

Chantilly cream (French: crème Chantilly) is sweetened whipped cream, lightly flavoured with vanilla, named after the Château de Chantilly. It is traditionally associated with the chef François Vatel in the 17th century.

Preparation

Cold heavy cream is whipped with icing sugar and vanilla until soft peaks form. It is served with fruit, pastries, hot chocolate and desserts, and used as a filling for cream puffs and cakes. Overwhipping can turn it grainy and towards butter.
