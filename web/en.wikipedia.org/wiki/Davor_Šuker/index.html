Davor Šuker

Davor Šuker (born 1 January 1968) is a Croatian former professional footballer and the former president of the Croatian Football Federation, a post he held from 2012 to 2021. Playing as a striker, he won the Golden Boot at the 1998 FIFA World Cup, where Croatia finished third in its first appearance at the tournament.

Club career

Šuker began his career at Osijek and Dinamo Zagreb before moving to Spain, where he starred for Sevilla and then Real Madrid, winning La Liga and the UEFA Champions League in 1998. He later played for Arsenal, West Ham United, Bayer Leverkusen, and 1860 Munich.

International career

He is Croatia's all-time top scorer with 45 goals in 69 appearances, having earlier played for Yugoslavia. After retirement he founded a football academy in Zagreb bearing his name.
