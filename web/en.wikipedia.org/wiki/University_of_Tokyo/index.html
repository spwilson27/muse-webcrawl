University of Tokyo

The University of Tokyo (Japanese: 東京大学, Tōkyō daigaku; abbreviated UTokyo or Tōdai) is a public research university in Bunkyō, Tokyo, Japan. Founded in 1877 as the first Imperial University, it is widely regarded as Japan's most prestigious university.

Structure

UTokyo comprises ten faculties and fifteen graduate schools, with campuses at Hongo, Komaba and Kashiwa, and enrolls about 28,000 students. Undergraduates spend their first two years at Komaba in the College of Arts and Sciences before specializing.

Notable people

Alumni and faculty include numerous Japanese prime ministers and Nobel laureates, among them physicist Leo Esaki and chemist Ei-ichi Negishi. The Yasuda Auditorium and Akamon gate are campus landmarks.
