The Tall Ships Races

The Tall Ships Races are annual sailing events for sail training "tall ships" (sailing vessels). They are organised by Sail Training International (STI) and involve races and cruises in company between ports, mainly in European waters. A central aim of the races is to promote international friendship and understanding among young people: at least half of each vessel's crew must be between 15 and 25 years old.

History

The first race was held in 1956, from Torbay in Devon to Lisbon, with twenty large sailing vessels taking part. It was intended as a farewell race for the last of the great commercial sailing ships, but its success led to a series of events held in different seas, including the Baltic, the Mediterranean and transatlantic crossings. From 1973 the races were sponsored for many years by Cutty Sark whisky and were known as the Cutty Sark Tall Ships Races.

Format

Each year's event typically consists of two or more race legs separated by a "cruise in company," during which the fleet sails together without racing. Host ports organise festivals, parades of sail and crew activities. Vessels are divided into classes by rig and size, and results are calculated on corrected time using a handicap system.
