The Imperial War Museum (IWM) is a British national museum organisation with branches at five locations in England, three of which are in London. Founded in 1917 during the First World War, it records and interprets the experiences of people who have lived, fought and died in conflicts involving Britain and the Commonwealth from the First World War to the present day.

Collections and branches

The museum's main branch, IWM London, is housed in the former Bethlem Royal Hospital building in Lambeth and holds major galleries on the two world wars and the Holocaust. Other branches include IWM Duxford, a historic airfield in Cambridgeshire; the cruiser HMS Belfast on the Thames; the Churchill War Rooms in Whitehall; and IWM North in Trafford, Greater Manchester, designed by Daniel Libeskind. The museum holds vast collections of documents, photographs, film, sound recordings, art and artefacts.
