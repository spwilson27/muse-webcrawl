Q is a fictional character appearing in the Star Trek franchise, portrayed by John de Lancie. He is a member of the Q Continuum, a race of extra-dimensional beings possessing near-omnipotent power over time, space and matter, who nevertheless display mischievous, capricious personalities. Q made his debut in "Encounter at Farpoint", the pilot episode of Star Trek: The Next Generation (1987), and went on to appear across The Next Generation, Deep Space Nine, Voyager, Lower Decks and Picard.

Character

In his first appearance Q puts humanity on trial for its savagery, an arc that bookends The Next Generation: he returns in the series finale "All Good Things..." to conclude the trial. In between he repeatedly tests, taunts and occasionally helps Captain Jean-Luc Picard and the crew of the Enterprise — most consequentially in "Q Who", where he hurls the ship across the galaxy into first contact with the Borg. Other members of the Continuum share the name Q, and episodes of Voyager explored its internal politics, including a Q civil war. Star Trek: Picard season two (2022) featured Q as the dying instigator of a time-travel crisis, ending his long relationship with Picard.

Reception

Q is consistently ranked among the most popular recurring characters in Star Trek, praised for de Lancie's performance and for the philosophical sparring between Q's cosmic cynicism and Picard's humanism.
