Earth sign

In Western astrology, the twelve signs of the zodiac are grouped by the four classical elements—fire, earth, air and water—each group being called a triplicity. The earth signs are Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn. In astrological interpretation, earth signs are associated with practicality, stability, material concerns, sensuality and a grounded, methodical temperament, reflecting the qualities of the element earth.

The earth triplicity: Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn, spaced 120° apart on the zodiac.

Astrological interpretation

Astrologers describe earth-sign emphasis in a birth chart as indicating concern with the tangible world: work, resources, the body and long-term security. Earth signs are said to be compatible in temperament with the water signs, and to complement the other earth signs. The system of triplicities dates to Hellenistic astrology, where signs were also assigned to day and night sects and to the four humours—earth corresponding to the melancholic temperament.
