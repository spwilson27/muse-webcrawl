John R. Adler Jr. (born 1954) is an American neurosurgeon and professor at Stanford University. He is best known as the inventor of the CyberKnife, a frameless robotic radiosurgery system used to treat tumours throughout the body.

Career

Adler conceived the CyberKnife while training with Lars Leksell, inventor of the Gamma Knife, in Sweden. Unlike earlier radiosurgery devices, the CyberKnife uses a compact linear accelerator mounted on a robotic arm combined with real-time image guidance, allowing treatment without a rigid frame fixed to the patient's skull. He founded the company Accuray in 1990 to commercialise the system; the first patient was treated at Stanford in 1994.

Other work

Adler has served as a professor of neurosurgery at Stanford School of Medicine and later founded the medical technology company Zap Surgical Systems. He is also the founder of the Cureus medical journal.
