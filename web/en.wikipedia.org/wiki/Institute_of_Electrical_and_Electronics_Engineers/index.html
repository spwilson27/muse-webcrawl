Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

IEEE is the world's largest technical professional organisation.

The Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) is an American 501(c)(3) charitable professional association for electronics engineering, electrical engineering, and related disciplines. Founded in 1963 from the merger of the American Institute of Electrical Engineers (1884) and the Institute of Radio Engineers (1912), it has more than 460,000 members in over 190 countries and describes itself as "the world's largest technical professional organization dedicated to advancing technology for the benefit of humanity."

Activities

IEEE publishes about a third of the world's literature in electrical engineering and computer science through its Xplore digital library, sponsors more than 2,000 conferences annually, and organises 39 technical societies (including the Computer Society, the largest). Its Standards Association produces ubiquitous specifications such as IEEE 802.11 (Wi-Fi), 802.3 (Ethernet), and the 754 floating-point standard.

Honours

The IEEE Medal of Honor, established in 1917, is among the highest awards in engineering; other honours include the Edison Medal and the Computer Society's Computer Pioneer Award. Membership grades run from Student through Member and Senior Member to Fellow, held by a small percentage of members for extraordinary accomplishments.
