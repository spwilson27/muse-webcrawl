Exhibition Road

Exhibition Road is a street in South Kensington, London, in the Royal Borough of Kensington and Chelsea. It is a major cultural corridor, home to several of London's most famous museums and academic institutions, an area sometimes nicknamed "Albertopolis" after Prince Albert, consort of Queen Victoria.

Institutions on or near the road include the Victoria and Albert Museum, the Natural History Museum, the Science Museum, Imperial College London, the Royal College of Music, and the Royal Albert Hall nearby. The road takes its name from the Great Exhibition of 1851, profits from which were used to develop the district.

In 2012 the street was remodelled as a shared-space scheme, removing kerbs and barriers to give pedestrians priority alongside vehicles.
