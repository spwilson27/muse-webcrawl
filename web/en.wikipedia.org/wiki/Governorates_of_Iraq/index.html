Governorates of Iraq

CountryIraq
Number19 governorates
Arabicمحافظات (muḥāfaẓāt)

Iraq is divided into 19 governorates (Arabic: muḥāfaẓāt, singular muḥāfaẓa), also known as provinces. The governorates are the country's first-level administrative subdivisions. Before 1976 they were called liwa. Four of the northern governorates form the autonomous Kurdistan Region.

List

The governorates are: Al Anbar, Babylon (Babil), Baghdad, Basra, Dhi Qar, Al-Qadisiyyah, Diyala, Dohuk, Erbil, Halabja, Karbala, Kirkuk, Maysan, Muthanna, Najaf, Nineveh (Ninawa), Saladin, Sulaymaniyah, and Wasit. Halabja, in the Kurdistan Region, became the 19th governorate after recognition processes in 2014–2025.

Baghdad Governorate, containing the capital, is the most populous, while Al Anbar in the west is the largest by area. Each governorate is headed by a governor and has an elected provincial council under Iraq's post-2003 decentralization framework.

Kurdistan Region

The governorates of Erbil, Sulaymaniyah, Dohuk, and Halabja are administered by the Kurdistan Regional Government, which enjoys constitutionally recognized autonomy. Disputed territories, including Kirkuk and parts of Nineveh, Diyala, and Saladin, are claimed both by the federal government and the Kurdistan Region.
