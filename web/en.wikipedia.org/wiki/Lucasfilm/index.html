Lucasfilm Ltd. LLC is an American film and television production company founded by filmmaker George Lucas in 1971. It is best known for creating and producing the Star Wars and Indiana Jones franchises, and for its pioneering work in special effects, sound, and digital filmmaking technology.

History

Lucas founded the company in San Rafael, California, before the release of American Graffiti (1973). The success of Star Wars (1977) transformed Lucasfilm into a major independent studio. Its subsidiaries included Industrial Light & Magic (ILM), founded in 1975 to create the effects for Star Wars, Skywalker Sound, LucasArts for video games, and THX, the cinema sound standard. The company was based for many years at Skywalker Ranch in Marin County before moving its San Francisco operations to the Letterman Digital Arts Center in the Presidio in 2005.

Acquisition by Disney

In October 2012, the Walt Disney Company acquired Lucasfilm for approximately $4.05 billion in cash and stock. Kathleen Kennedy became president, and Lucasfilm went on to produce the Star Wars sequel trilogy (2015–2019), standalone films such as Rogue One (2016), and streaming series including The Mandalorian for Disney+.

Selected productions

Major Lucasfilm productions include the nine-episode Skywalker Saga, the Indiana Jones films, Willow (1988), Labyrinth (1986), and television series such as The Young Indiana Jones Chronicles, Star Wars: The Clone Wars, Andor, and Ahsoka.
