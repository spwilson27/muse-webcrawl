Hellblazer

Hellblazer (also known as John Constantine, Hellblazer) is an American contemporary horror comic book series published by DC Comics. It originally ran under the DC Comics imprint and later under Vertigo. The character of John Constantine first appeared in Alan Moore's Swamp Thing run before starring in his own title, launched in January 1988 with writer Jamie Delano and artist John Ridgway.

Overview

The series follows John Constantine, a cynical working-class occult detective and con man from Liverpool. Unlike conventional superheroes, Constantine relies on trickery, negotiation, and manipulation rather than direct magical combat, and stories frequently end with Pyrrhic victories and the deaths of his friends — a recurring theme often summarised by his "long trenchcoat, short life expectancy" reputation.

Publication history

The original series ran for 300 issues, from 1988 to 2013, making it one of Vertigo's longest-running titles. Notable writers included Jamie Delano, Garth Ennis (whose run introduced the "Dangerous Habits" storyline, in which Constantine is diagnosed with terminal lung cancer), Neil Gaiman, Warren Ellis, Brian Azzarello, Mike Carey, and Peter Milligan. Ennis's "Dangerous Habits" later served as the basis for the 2005 film Constantine, starring Keanu Reeves.

Legacy

Hellblazer helped define the Vertigo line's mature, literary approach to comics and remains influential in the urban-fantasy and horror genres.
