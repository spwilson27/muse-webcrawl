James Murphy (musician)

Born: February 4, 1970, Princeton Junction, New Jersey, U.S.

Occupation: Musician, producer, DJ

Bands: LCD Soundsystem

James Murphy (born February 4, 1970) is an American musician, record producer, and DJ, best known as the founder and frontman of the electronic dance-rock band LCD Soundsystem and as co-founder of the record label DFA Records.

Career

After playing drums in various punk and indie bands, Murphy founded DFA Records in 2001 with Tim Goldsworthy. Under the name LCD Soundsystem, he released the singles "Losing My Edge" (2002) and "Daft Punk Is Playing at My House" (2005), followed by acclaimed albums Sound of Silver (2007) and This Is Happening (2010).

LCD Soundsystem

LCD Soundsystem announced its retirement with a farewell concert at Madison Square Garden in 2011, documented in the film Shut Up and Play the Hits. The band reunited in 2016, releasing the album American Dream in 2017, which won the Grammy Award for Best Dance Recording for "Tonite". Murphy is also known as a DJ and as a producer for artists including the Yeah Yeah Yeahs, Arcade Fire, and David Bowie.
