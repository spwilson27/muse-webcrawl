Adrian Newey

Adrian Newey
Born: 26 December 1958, Colchester, England
Occupation: Motorsport engineer
Known for: Formula One aerodynamic design
Teams: Williams, McLaren, Red Bull Racing, Aston Martin

Adrian Newey (born 1958) is a British engineer widely regarded as one of the most successful Formula One designers in history. His cars have won numerous Constructors' and Drivers' championships across three different teams.

Career

Newey studied aeronautics at the University of Southampton and began his motorsport career in the early 1980s. At Williams in the 1990s his cars, including the FW14B, dominated with active suspension and advanced aerodynamics. He moved to McLaren in 1997, then joined Red Bull Racing in 2006, where his designs powered Sebastian Vettel's four consecutive titles (2010–2013) and later Max Verstappen's championships. In 2024 he announced he would leave Red Bull and join Aston Martin as Managing Technical Partner.

Design approach

Newey is known for an intuitive, whole-car aerodynamic philosophy and for integrating chassis and aerodynamic design rather than treating them separately.
