Operation Reindeer

Operation Reindeer (German: Unternehmen Rentier) was a German operation during World War II intended to secure the nickel mines around Petsamo in Finland, in the event of a renewed war between Finland and the Soviet Union. It was a part of Operation Silver Fox.

Background

The Petsamo region, ceded to Finland after the Treaty of Tartu in 1920, contained nickel deposits at Kolosjoki that were strategically important for German war industry. After the Winter War of 1939–40, Germany feared that the Soviet Union might seize the mines in a renewed attack on Finland. Plans were prepared for German mountain troops stationed in Norway to occupy the area pre-emptively.

The operation

The operation was executed at the start of Operation Barbarossa on 22 June 1941. Units of Mountain Corps Norway under Eduard Dietl crossed from Norway into Finnish territory and occupied the Petsamo area without resistance, securing the mines and the Arctic port of Liinahamari. From these positions, the same troops then advanced toward Murmansk as part of Operation Platinum Fox.
