AirPods Max are wireless Bluetooth over-ear headphones designed by Apple, and released on December 15, 2020. They are the highest-end model in Apple's AirPods line, sitting above the entry-level AirPods and mid-range AirPods Pro, and feature active noise cancellation, transparency mode, spatial audio, and a stainless-steel and aluminium design with a knit-mesh canopy headband.

Design and features

Each ear cup houses an Apple H1 chip and a 40 mm dynamic driver, with nine microphones used for noise cancellation and voice pickup. Controls include a Digital Crown, adapted from the Apple Watch, for volume and playback, and a noise-control button toggling between noise cancellation and transparency. Computational audio adapts equalization to the fit of the ear cushions, and spatial audio with head tracking creates a surround effect for supported video content.

AirPods Max weigh about 385 grams, noticeably heavier than most competing headphones, and ship with a "Smart Case" that puts them into an ultralow-power state but was widely criticized for its appearance and limited protection. A refreshed version with a USB-C connector and new colours was released in September 2024.

Reception

Reviews praised the sound quality, noise cancellation, and build quality, while criticizing the high price, weight, and case design. Condensation accumulating inside the ear cups during extended use was reported by some users.
