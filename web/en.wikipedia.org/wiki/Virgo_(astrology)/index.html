Virgo (astrology)

Virgo (♍︎) is the sixth astrological sign in the zodiac. It spans the 150th–180th degree of the zodiac. Under the tropical zodiac, the Sun transits this sign on average between August 23 and September 22. Virgo is one of the three earth signs, along with Taurus and Capricorn, and is ruled in modern astrology by the planet Mercury. Its symbol is the maiden, often linked to Astraea, the Greek goddess of justice and innocence who, in mythology, was the last immortal to leave Earth at the end of the Golden Age.

Mythology

The figure of Virgo is most commonly identified with Astraea, daughter of Astraeus and Eos, who carried the scales of justice and lived among mortals during the Golden Age. As humanity grew corrupt through the Silver, Bronze, and Iron Ages, Astraea withdrew first to the hills and finally to the heavens, where she became the constellation Virgo, with her scales forming neighbouring Libra. Other traditions associate the maiden with Demeter, the harvest goddess, or her daughter Persephone; the brightest star in the constellation, Spica, is named for the ear of grain the maiden holds.

Traits in astrology

In popular astrology, Virgo is characterised as analytical, methodical, and service-oriented. As a mutable earth sign, it is associated with practicality, attention to detail, and a drive toward improvement and craftsmanship. Negative attributions in the tradition include perfectionism, over-criticism, and worry. Astrologers associate the sign with health, daily routines, and skilled work.

Compatibility

Virgo is traditionally said to be compatible with the other earth signs, Taurus and Capricorn, and with the water signs Cancer and Scorpio. Its opposite sign is Pisces.
