Violet Crumble is an Australian chocolate bar. It consists of a brittle honeycomb toffee centre coated in compound chocolate. It was created by Abel Hoadley and first produced in Melbourne, Australia, in 1913. The bar is famous for its tagline, "It's the way it shatters that matters," and for its distinctive purple wrapper. Production is now carried out by South Australian company Menz Confectionery, which purchased the brand from Nestlé in 2018 (with production transferred in 2019).

History

Abel Hoadley, founder of Hoadley's Chocolates, created the Violet Crumble in 1913. Hoadley's wife's favourite flower was the violet, and he named the bar accordingly, colouring the wrapper purple in her honour. The honeycomb centre was difficult to make because it absorbed moisture and became chewy; Hoadley solved the problem by coating the honeycomb in chocolate and wrapping it to keep it dry, giving the bar its characteristic "crumble."

The bar became one of Australia's most popular confectionery items. Hoadley's was later acquired by Rowntree's, which in turn was acquired by Nestlé in 1988. For many years Violet Crumble was produced at Nestlé's Campbellfield factory in Melbourne.

Change of ownership

In January 2018, Nestlé announced it had sold the Violet Crumble brand to Adelaide-based Robern Menz (now Menz Confectionery). Production moved to Menz's factory in Glynde, South Australia, returning the brand to Australian ownership after decades. The first South Australian–made Violet Crumble bars reached shelves in October 2018, accompanied by new product variants, including bite-sized "Violet Crumble Nuggets," cubes, and plant-based versions.

Description

The Violet Crumble bar weighs 50 grams in its standard form. Its centre is a light, crisp, shattering honeycomb (similar to cinder toffee), coated in a thin layer of compound milk chocolate. Consumers traditionally test the bar's freshness by its shattering crunch. The bar has a long-standing rivalry with the similar Crunchie bar produced by Cadbury; Australians commonly debate which honeycomb bar is superior, the key difference being the texture of the honeycomb—Crunchie's being denser and chewier.

In popular culture

Violet Crumble is referenced in Australian popular culture as an icon of Australian confectionery, alongside products such as Vegemite and Tim Tams. Limited-edition flavours, including caramel and dark chocolate variants, have been released periodically.
