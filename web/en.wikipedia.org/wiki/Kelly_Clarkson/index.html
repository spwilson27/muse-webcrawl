Kelly Clarkson

Kelly Brianne Clarkson (born April 24, 1982) is an American singer, songwriter and television personality. She rose to fame as the winner of the first season of American Idol in 2002 and has since become one of the best-selling music artists worldwide, noted for her powerful vocals and crossover success in pop, rock and country.

Career

After winning American Idol, Clarkson's debut single "A Moment Like This" topped the Billboard Hot 100. Her debut album Thankful (2003) was followed by the multi-platinum Breakaway (2004), whose singles "Since U Been Gone", "Behind These Hazel Eyes" and "Because of You" became pop staples and earned her two Grammy Awards. Later albums include My December (2007), All I Ever Wanted (2009, featuring the number-one single "My Life Would Suck Without You"), Stronger (2011, whose title track won a Grammy for its album), Piece by Piece (2015), Meaning of Life (2017) and Chemistry (2023). In total she has won three Grammy Awards among many honours.

Television

Clarkson served as a coach on the American version of The Voice for multiple seasons, winning several times, and since 2019 has hosted the syndicated daytime talk programme The Kelly Clarkson Show, which has won numerous Daytime Emmy Awards, including for her hosting.
