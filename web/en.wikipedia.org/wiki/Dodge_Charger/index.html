Dodge Charger

The Dodge Charger is a model of automobile marketed by Dodge over several generations. First introduced for 1966 as a mid-size fastback, the Charger became one of the defining American muscle cars, especially the 1968–1970 models with their full-width hidden-headlight grille.

History

The nameplate has been used on subcompact hatchbacks (1980s), full-size sedans, and concept cars. The 1969 Charger Daytona and its Plymouth sibling were built for NASCAR with a distinctive nose cone and high rear wing. The Charger was famously featured as the "General Lee" in the television series The Dukes of Hazzard and as Dominic Toretto's car in the Fast & Furious films.

Dodge revived the Charger in 2006 as a four-door sedan sharing the LX platform with the Chrysler 300, offered with Hemi V8 engines in R/T, SRT, and the 707-horsepower Hellcat variants. In 2024–2025 the nameplate moved to the new STLA Large platform with electric (Charger Daytona) and twin-turbo six-cylinder versions.
