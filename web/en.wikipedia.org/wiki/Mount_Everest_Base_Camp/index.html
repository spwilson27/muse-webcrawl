Everest Base Camp

LocationsNepal (south, 5,364 m); Tibet (north, 5,150 m)
PurposeStaging for Everest expeditions
[Image: Tents of South Base Camp on the Khumbu Glacier]
Tents of South Base Camp on the Khumbu Glacier

Everest Base Camp refers to the two base camps on opposite sides of Mount Everest: the South Base Camp in Nepal (5,364 m) and the North Base Camp in Tibet (5,150 m), staging points for climbs of the world's highest mountain.

The south camp and the trek

Nepal's base camp sits on the Khumbu Glacier below the Icefall; in the spring season it becomes a tent city of expeditions. Reaching it is itself one of the world's great treks — from Lukla through Namche Bazaar and the Sherpa villages of Sagarmatha National Park, with the viewpoint of Kala Patthar (5,545 m) giving trekkers their classic Everest panorama. The 2015 Nepal earthquake triggered an avalanche from Pumori that killed more than twenty people at the camp. The Tibetan camp is reached by road across the plateau to Rongbuk Monastery.
