Masters of Horror is an American horror anthology television series created by director Mick Garris for Showtime. Each hour-long episode was directed by a celebrated horror filmmaker, including John Carpenter, Dario Argento, Tobe Hooper, and Takashi Miike.

Series

The series ran for two seasons (2005–2007) with 26 episodes. It grew out of informal dinners among horror directors who dubbed themselves the 'masters of horror.' Notable episodes include Carpenter's 'Cigarette Burns' and Miike's 'Imprint', the latter deemed too disturbing to air and released only on DVD.

Legacy

After leaving Showtime the concept continued briefly as NBC's Fear Itself (2008). The series is credited with showcasing auteur-driven horror on television before the genre's later anthology revival.
