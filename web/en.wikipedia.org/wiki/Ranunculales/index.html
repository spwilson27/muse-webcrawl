Ranunculales

Ranunculales is an order of flowering plants in the eudicots. It is the earliest-diverging (most basal) lineage of the eudicots in modern classifications such as the APG IV system. The order contains roughly 4,450 species in about 165 genera.

Families

The APG system recognises seven families: Ranunculaceae (buttercup family, the largest, with about 2,500 species), Papaveraceae (poppy family), Berberidaceae (barberry family), Menispermaceae (moonseed family), Circaeasteraceae, Eupteleaceae and Lardizabalaceae.

Significance

The order includes many ornamentals (poppies, delphiniums, clematis, anemones) and important medicinal and toxic plants: the opium poppy (Papaver somniferum), goldenseal, and various sources of alkaloids such as morphine, berberine and curare-type compounds. Because of its basal position among eudicots, Ranunculales is important in studies of early angiosperm evolution.
