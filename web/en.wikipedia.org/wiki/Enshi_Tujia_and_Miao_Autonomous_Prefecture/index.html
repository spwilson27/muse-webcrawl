Enshi Tujia and Miao Autonomous Prefecture

Enshi Tujia and Miao Autonomous Prefecture (Chinese: 恩施土家族苗族自治州; pinyin: Ēnshī Tǔjiāzú Miáozú Zìzhìzhōu) is an autonomous prefecture in the mountainous southwestern corner of Hubei province, People's Republic of China. It is the only autonomous prefecture in Hubei, and is named for its substantial Tujia and Miao ethnic minority populations. Its seat is Enshi City.

Geography

The prefecture is dominated by the Wuling Mountains and dramatic karst scenery, including the Enshi Grand Canyon and the Tenglong Cave system near Lichuan, one of the largest karst caves in China. The Qing River flows through the prefecture. Enshi is noted as a rich source of selenium in its soils and is sometimes called a "selenium capital."

Administration

Enshi administers two county-level cities (Enshi and Lichuan) and six counties (Xianfeng, Laifeng, Badong, Xuan'en, Hefeng and Jianshi). The prefecture was established in 1983, replacing the former Enshi Prefecture; it had earlier been known as the Exi Tujia and Miao Autonomous Prefecture.

Transport

The prefecture is served by Enshi Xujiaping Airport and by the Shanghai–Wuhan–Chengdu passenger railway, whose Yichang–Wanzhou section crosses exceptionally difficult terrain with numerous bridges and tunnels, including the Qingjiang Bridge.
