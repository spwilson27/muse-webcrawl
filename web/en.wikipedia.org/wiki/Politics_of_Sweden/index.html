Politics of Sweden

The politics of Sweden take place in a framework of a parliamentary representative democratic constitutional monarchy. The Riksdag, a unicameral parliament with 349 members, holds legislative power; the government, headed by the Prime Minister, exercises executive power. The monarch, King Carl XVI Gustaf, is head of state with a largely ceremonial role under the 1974 Instrument of Government.

Political parties

The Swedish Social Democratic Party dominated Swedish politics for much of the 20th century. Other major parties include the Moderate Party, the Sweden Democrats, the Centre Party, the Left Party, the Christian Democrats, the Liberals, and the Green Party.

Elections

General elections are held every four years on the second Sunday of September, simultaneously for the Riksdag, county councils, and municipal councils, using proportional representation with a 4 percent national threshold.
