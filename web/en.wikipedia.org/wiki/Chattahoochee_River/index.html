Chattahoochee River

Length430 mi (690 km)
SourceBlue Ridge Mountains, Georgia
MouthApalachicola River, Florida

The Chattahoochee River is a river in the southeastern United States flowing 430 miles (690 km) from the Blue Ridge Mountains of north Georgia southwestward through Atlanta's metropolitan area, along the Alabama–Georgia border, and into Florida, where it joins the Flint River to form the Apalachicola River.

Course and use

The river is impounded by a series of dams, including Buford Dam, which forms Lake Lanier, Atlanta's principal water source. Water allocation among Georgia, Alabama and Florida along the Apalachicola–Chattahoochee–Flint basin has been the subject of a long-running legal dispute known as the "tri-state water wars".

Recreation

The Chattahoochee River National Recreation Area preserves parkland along the river near Atlanta, and whitewater courses at Columbus, Georgia, are among the longest urban whitewater runs in the world.
