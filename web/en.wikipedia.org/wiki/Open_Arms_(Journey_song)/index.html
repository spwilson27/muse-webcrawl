"Open Arms" is a song by American rock band Journey. It was released as a single from their 1981 album, Escape. Co-written by band members Steve Perry and Jonathan Cain, the song is a power ballad whose lyrics attempt to renew a drifting relationship. It is one of the band's most recognizable radio hits and their biggest US Billboard Hot 100 hit, reaching number two in February 1982 and holding that position for six weeks.

Background

The song was originally written by Jonathan Cain when he was a member of The Babys, but lead singer John Waite rejected it as "too syrupy". Cain later brought the song to Journey. Steve Perry helped finish the lyrics, but guitarist Neal Schon and drummer Steve Smith were initially unimpressed, considering it too much of a ballad for the band's rock sound. The song was nevertheless recorded for Escape and became the album's third single.

Reception

"Open Arms" reached number two on the Billboard Hot 100, kept from the top spot by "Centerfold" by The J. Geils Band and "I Love Rock 'n' Roll" by Joan Jett and the Blackhearts. It has been certified platinum in the United States. Mariah Carey covered the song on her 1995 album Daydream, and it has been covered by many other artists.
