Port of Savannah

Container cranes at the Garden City Terminal, Port of Savannah.

The Port of Savannah is a major U.S. seaport located in Savannah, Georgia. It is operated by the Georgia Ports Authority and is one of the busiest container ports in the United States, consistently ranking among the top four by container volume and serving as the largest single-terminal container facility in North America at its Garden City Terminal.

Facilities

The port's principal facility is the Garden City Terminal, a single large terminal on the Savannah River with multiple berths and ship-to-shore cranes, extensive on-terminal rail connections, and direct access to interstate highways. The Ocean Terminal handles break-bulk and roll-on/roll-off cargo.

Savannah Harbor Expansion Project

The Savannah Harbor Expansion Project (SHEP), completed in 2022, deepened the shipping channel to 47 feet, allowing the port to serve fully loaded neo-Panamax container ships that transit the expanded Panama Canal. The project included environmental features such as a fish passage at the New Savannah Bluff Lock and Dam and oxygen injection systems for the river.

Economic role

The port is a major gateway for imports destined for the U.S. Southeast and Midwest and for exports of agricultural and forest products. Its growth has been driven by efficient single-terminal operations, dual rail service, and a large surrounding distribution-centre network.
