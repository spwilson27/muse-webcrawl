Master of Orion

Master of Orion
Developer: Simtex
Publisher: MicroProse
Released: 1993
Genre: Turn-based strategy, 4X

Master of Orion is a turn-based, 4X science fiction strategy video game developed by Simtex and published by MicroProse in 1993 for MS-DOS. The player leads one of several alien races to conquer the galaxy through exploration, colonisation, research, diplomacy, and space combat, aiming to be elected ruler by the Galactic Council or to conquer all rivals.

Gameplay

Players manage colonies with sliders allocating production to ships, defences, factories, ecology, and research across six technology fields. Ship design is customisable. Races such as the Psilons, Humans, Klackons, and the robotic Meklar each have distinct bonuses.

Legacy

The game helped define the 4X genre — the term itself was coined in connection with it — and spawned sequels Master of Orion II: Battle at Antares (1996), Master of Orion III (2003), and a 2016 reboot, Master of Orion: Conquer the Stars.
