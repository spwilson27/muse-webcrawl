Niland, California

Niland is a census-designated place in Imperial County, California, United States, located on the northeastern shore of the Salton Sea, about 47 miles (76 km) southeast of Indio. Its population was around 1,000 at recent censuses.

History

The community was originally known as Old Beach and later Imperial Junction; the name Niland — from "Nile land", a promotional reference to the fertile farming of the Imperial Valley — was adopted in 1914. The Southern Pacific Railroad passed through the settlement.

Geography and nearby sites

Niland lies near the Salton Sea's shrinking shoreline in the Sonoran Desert, in an area of agriculture, geothermal activity and mud pots. It is the nearest town to Slab City, a free off-grid encampment on a former military base a few miles east, and to Salvation Mountain, the folk-art environment created by Leonard Knight.
