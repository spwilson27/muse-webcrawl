Yelü Dashi

Reign1124–1143
DynastyWestern Liao (Qara Khitai)
Born1087
Died1143

Yelü Dashi (耶律大石, 1087–1143) was a Khitan prince of the Liao dynasty and the founder of the Qara Khitai (Western Liao) empire in Central Asia. A descendant of the Liao imperial clan, he was unusual among Khitan nobles for holding a jinshi degree from the Chinese examination system.

Flight west and founding of Qara Khitai

As the Jurchen Jin dynasty destroyed the Liao in the 1120s, Yelü Dashi escaped westward with a band of followers rather than submit. Moving through Mongolia into Central Asia, he gathered support among local peoples, defeated rivals, and by the 1130s had established a new empire centred at Balasagun (in present-day Kyrgyzstan), taking the title Gurkhan ("universal khan").

Battle of Qatwan and legacy

In 1141 his forces decisively defeated the Seljuk sultan Ahmad Sanjar at the Battle of Qatwan near Samarkand, making Qara Khitai the dominant power in Central Asia. Reports of this victory, garbled in transmission, helped give rise in Europe to the legend of Prester John. Yelü Dashi died in 1143; the dynasty he founded endured until the Mongol conquest in 1218.
