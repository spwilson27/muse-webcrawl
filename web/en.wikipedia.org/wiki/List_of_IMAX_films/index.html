List of IMAX films

IMAX is a proprietary system of high-resolution cameras, film formats, projectors and theatres known for their very large screens. This article lists films released in IMAX, including documentary shorts made especially for IMAX theatres and Hollywood features presented in the format, some of which were filmed wholly or partly with IMAX cameras.

IMAX documentaries

The first IMAX film, Tiger Child, premiered at Expo 70 in Osaka, Japan. Early classics of the format include To Fly! (1976), Grand Canyon: The Hidden Secrets (1984), The Dream Is Alive (1985, about the Space Shuttle), Chronos (1985), Everest (1998), the highest-grossing IMAX documentary of its era, Space Station 3D (2002), Deep Sea 3D (2006), Hubble 3D (2010) and A Beautiful Planet (2016).

Feature films in IMAX

Hollywood features were initially shown in IMAX through the DMR (Digital Media Remastering) process, beginning with the 2002 re-release of Apollo 13. Christopher Nolan's The Dark Knight (2008) was the first major feature to include sequences filmed with IMAX 70mm cameras; Nolan later shot The Dark Knight Rises, Interstellar, Dunkirk, Tenet and Oppenheimer extensively in the format. Other landmarks include Avatar (2009), Mission: Impossible – Ghost Protocol (2011), Gravity (2013), Avengers: Infinity War and Endgame (the first films shot entirely with IMAX digital cameras), Dune (2021), Top Gun: Maverick (2022) and Dune: Part Two (2024). The "Filmed for IMAX" programme certifies cameras and workflows for the format.

IMAX Enhanced and home formats

Since 2018, the IMAX Enhanced programme has brought expanded-aspect-ratio presentations of selected titles to home video and streaming.
