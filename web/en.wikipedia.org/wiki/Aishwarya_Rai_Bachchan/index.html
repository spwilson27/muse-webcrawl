Aishwarya Rai Bachchan

Aishwarya Rai Bachchan (née Rai; born 1 November 1973) is an Indian actress who works primarily in Hindi and Tamil films. She won the Miss World 1994 pageant and went on to become one of the most popular and influential celebrities in India, receiving numerous accolades including two Filmfare Awards from eleven nominations. In 2009 she was honoured with the Padma Shri by the Government of India.

Early life and modelling

Rai was born in Mangalore, Karnataka, into a Tulu-speaking family; her father was an army biologist. The family moved to Mumbai, where she studied at Jai Hind College and later enrolled in architecture before leaving to pursue modelling. She gained fame through a Pepsi commercial and won the Miss India runner-up title before being crowned Miss World in 1994.

Film career

Rai made her acting debut in Mani Ratnam's Tamil film Iruvar (1997) and her Hindi debut in Aur Pyaar Ho Gaya the same year. She achieved major success with Hum Dil De Chuke Sanam (1999) and Taal (1999), and played Paro in Sanjay Leela Bhansali's Devdas (2002), then India's most expensive film, which premiered at Cannes. She appeared in international productions such as Bride and Prejudice (2004) and The Pink Panther 2 (2009), and in acclaimed Indian films including Jodhaa Akbar (2008), Enthiran (2010) and Ae Dil Hai Mushkil (2016). She is a regular presence at the Cannes Film Festival and a long-time brand ambassador for L'Oréal.

Personal life

In 2007 she married actor Abhishek Bachchan, son of Amitabh Bachchan; their daughter was born in 2011. She has supported eye-donation and other charitable causes, and served as a UNAIDS goodwill ambassador.
