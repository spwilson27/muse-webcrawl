Enga language

Enga is a language of the East New Guinea Highlands stock spoken by the Enga people of Enga Province in Papua New Guinea. With several hundred thousand speakers, it is the most widely spoken indigenous language in Papua New Guinea by number of native speakers.

Classification and dialects

Enga belongs to the Engan family within the Trans–New Guinea languages. Major dialect groupings include Kyaka, Ipili, and Mai, which some classify as separate languages. The language has a complex verb morphology and a small phoneme inventory typical of the region. Missionary linguists produced written materials and translations in Enga during the twentieth century.
