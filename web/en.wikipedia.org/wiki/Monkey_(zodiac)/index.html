The Monkey (猴) is the ninth of the 12-year cycle of animals which appear in the Chinese zodiac related to the Chinese calendar. The Year of the Monkey is associated with the Earthly Branch symbol 申.

Basic astrology elements

According to Chinese astrology, people born in the Year of the Monkey are said to be witty, intelligent, curious and inventive, but also potentially mischievous and restless. The Monkey's fixed element is Metal, and its polarity is Yang. In the cycle of Earthly Branches, the Monkey corresponds to Shēn (申), associated with the hours 3pm to 5pm.

Cultural references

The Monkey holds a prominent place in Chinese folklore through the figure of Sun Wukong, the Monkey King of the 16th-century novel Journey to the West. Monkey years are traditionally regarded as auspicious for innovation and change.
