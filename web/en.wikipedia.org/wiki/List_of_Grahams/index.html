List of Grahams

Grahams are hills in Scotland between 600 and 762 metres high (1,969 and 2,500 feet) with a minimum prominence of 150 metres. The list was compiled by Fiona Graham and published in 1992 by Alan Dawson in The Relative Hills of Britain. The list originally used the imperial band of 2,000 to 2,499 feet; following metric resurvey the definition was adjusted in 2022 to 600–762 metres.
