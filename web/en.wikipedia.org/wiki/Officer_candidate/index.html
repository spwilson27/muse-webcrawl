Officer candidate

Officer candidate or officer aspirant (OA) is a rank in some militaries of the world that is an appointment held by military personnel during their training to become officers. In many countries, officer candidates are senior non-commissioned personnel or trainees who have passed selection and are undergoing officer training.

In the United States, officer candidates attend Officer Candidate School or equivalent programmes; in the United Kingdom, officer cadets train at institutions such as the Royal Military Academy Sandhurst, Britannia Royal Naval College, and Royal Air Force College Cranwell. Upon successful completion, candidates are commissioned.
