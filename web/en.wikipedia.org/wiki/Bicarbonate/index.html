Bicarbonate

FormulaHCO₃⁻
NameHydrogen carbonate
Conjugate acidCarbonic acid

In inorganic chemistry, bicarbonate (systematically hydrogen carbonate) is an anion with the formula HCO₃⁻. It is the conjugate base of carbonic acid and the conjugate acid of the carbonate ion, and plays a central role in the pH buffering system of blood.

Role in physiology

The bicarbonate buffer system maintains blood pH near 7.4. Carbon dioxide from respiration dissolves in blood and equilibrates with carbonic acid and bicarbonate, while the kidneys regulate bicarbonate reabsorption and excretion.

Compounds and uses

Sodium bicarbonate (baking soda) is widely used in cooking, cleaning and medicine; potassium bicarbonate is used as a fungicide and leavening agent. Bicarbonate is also the dominant form in which carbon dioxide is transported in seawater, linking it to ocean chemistry and the carbon cycle.
