Solar power in Australia

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Solar power in Australia is a major contributor to the country's electricity supply. Australia has the highest solar radiation of any continent, and correspondingly high rates of rooftop photovoltaic adoption: around one in three Australian homes has rooftop solar panels, among the highest per-capita rates in the world.

Growth

Generous feed-in tariffs, falling panel prices, the Small-scale Renewable Energy Scheme and abundant sunshine drove rapid uptake from the 2010s. Large-scale solar farms followed, concentrated in Queensland, New South Wales and Victoria.

Capacity

Combined rooftop and utility-scale solar regularly supplies a substantial share of midday demand in the National Electricity Market, occasionally exceeding half of demand in some states, and has reshaped the grid's 'duck curve' of net demand.

Challenges

Integration challenges include minimum-demand events, the need for storage and transmission, and curtailment of midday generation, addressed in part by batteries and projects such as proposed solar export cables.
