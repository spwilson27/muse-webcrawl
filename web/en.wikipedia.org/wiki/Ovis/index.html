Ovis

Ovis is a genus of mammals, part of the Caprinae subfamily of the ruminant family Bovidae. Its member species are known collectively as sheep. The domestic sheep (Ovis aries) is descended from the wild mouflon.

Species include the argali, bighorn sheep, Dall sheep, mouflon, snow sheep, and urial, inhabiting mountainous regions of Europe, Asia, and North America.
