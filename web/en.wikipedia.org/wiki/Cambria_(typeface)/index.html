Cambria (typeface)

Cambria is a transitional serif typeface commissioned by Microsoft and distributed with Windows and Office. It was designed by Dutch type designer Jelle Bosma in 2004 and is part of the ClearType Font Collection, designed to read well on screen and in print at small sizes.

CategorySerif
DesignerJelle Bosma
Commissioned byMicrosoft
Date released2007 (with Windows Vista / Office 2007)

Design

Cambria has sturdy letterforms with relatively large x-height, even spacing and proportions, and strong serifs suited to low-resolution rendering. It ships in regular, italic, bold, and bold italic styles, and includes extensive character support for Latin, Greek, and Cyrillic scripts. Cambria Math is a variant specialised for mathematical typesetting, used as the standard font for equations in Microsoft Office since 2007.

Usage

Cambria served as the default serif font in Microsoft Office from 2007 and became widely used for documents, academic papers, and typeset mathematics.
