Range (instrument)

ContextMetrology, measurement technology
DefinitionSet of values between the lowest and highest measurable quantities
Related termsSpan, full scale, dynamic range

The range of a measuring instrument is the set of values of the measured quantity for which the instrument is intended to function within specified limits of error. It is bounded by the minimum and maximum values the instrument can measure reliably; the difference between these limits is called the span.

Examples

A thermometer with a range of −20 °C to 100 °C has a span of 120 K. A measuring device may have several switchable ranges — for example, a multimeter measuring voltage from millivolts to hundreds of volts — and modern instruments often select the appropriate range automatically (autoranging).

Related concepts

Operating a device beyond its range can produce saturation, clipping or damage. The useful measuring range should be distinguished from the wider damage limits and from the dynamic range, which expresses the ratio between the largest and smallest measurable signals.
