DICE (legally EA Digital Illusions CE AB) is a Swedish video game developer based in Stockholm, owned by Electronic Arts. It is best known for the Battlefield series of first-person shooters and for creating the Frostbite game engine, as well as for Mirror's Edge and the Star Wars Battlefront games.

History

DICE grew out of the Amiga demoscene group The Silents; its founders — including Fredrik Liliegren, Olof Gustafsson, Andreas Axelsson and Markus Nyström — began with pinball games for the Amiga (Pinball Dreams, Pinball Fantasies and Pinball Illusions) in the early 1990s, then moved into racing games and other genres. The breakthrough came with Battlefield 1942 (2002), whose large-scale combined-arms multiplayer became the studio's signature. Electronic Arts, initially a minority investor and publisher, acquired DICE fully in 2006.

Games and technology

The studio's Frostbite engine, debuting with Battlefield: Bad Company (2008), became EA's standard technology across many franchises. DICE's major releases include successive Battlefield titles (from 1942 through Battlefield V and Battlefield 2042), the parkour action game Mirror's Edge (2008) and its reboot Mirror's Edge Catalyst (2016), and Star Wars Battlefront (2015) and Battlefront II (2017). A Los Angeles studio, DICE LA, was later rebranded Ripple Effect Studios. DICE is one of Sweden's most prominent game developers and a multi-time winner of industry awards for audio and technology.
