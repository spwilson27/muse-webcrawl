P&O Ferries is a British shipping company that operates ferries from the United Kingdom to Ireland and continental Europe (France, the Netherlands and Belgium). The company was created in 2002 through the merger of P&O's ferry operations; its heritage descends from the Peninsular and Oriental Steam Navigation Company founded in 1822. It is owned by DP World.

Routes

Key routes have included Dover–Calais (the busiest ferry route in the world), Hull–Rotterdam, Larne–Cairnryan, and Dublin–Liverpool. The company's fleet of roll-on/roll-off passenger ferries carries cars, freight and foot passengers.

2022 controversy

In March 2022, P&O Ferries dismissed about 800 UK-based seafarers without notice and replaced them with lower-paid agency staff, provoking widespread political and public condemnation in Britain and scrutiny of maritime employment law. The company acknowledged it had not followed consultation requirements.
