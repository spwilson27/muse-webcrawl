Tokyo International Forum
Location: Marunouchi, Tokyo
Architect: Rafael Viñoly
Opened: 1997

The Tokyo International Forum (Japanese: 東京国際フォーラム) is a multi-purpose exhibition and convention centre in Marunouchi, Chiyoda, Tokyo, Japan. It stands on the site of the former Tokyo City Hall, between Tokyo Station and Yūrakuchō Station.

Architecture

Designed by Uruguayan-American architect Rafael Viñoly and completed in 1996, the complex is noted for its main Glass Hall, a boat-shaped glass-and-steel atrium about 207 metres long whose dramatic trusses resemble the hull of a ship. The design won praise as one of the most striking public buildings of 1990s Japan.

Facilities and events

The Forum contains eight halls, including Hall A, one of the largest concert and theatre venues in Japan with about 5,000 seats, plus exhibition space, restaurants, and shops. It hosts concerts, musicals, conferences, and antique markets. During the Tokyo 2020 Olympic Games, it served as the venue for weightlifting.
