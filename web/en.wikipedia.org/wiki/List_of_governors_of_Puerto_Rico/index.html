List of governors of Puerto Rico

The governor of Puerto Rico is the head of government of the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico. Puerto Rico was governed by Spanish-appointed captains general and governors from the early 1500s until 1898, then by U.S. military and presidentially appointed governors until 1949, when Luis Muñoz Marín became the first popularly elected governor. The residence is La Fortaleza in San Juan, the oldest executive mansion in continuous use in the Americas.
