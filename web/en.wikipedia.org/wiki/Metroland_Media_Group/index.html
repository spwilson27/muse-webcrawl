Metroland Media Group is a Canadian media company and a subsidiary of Torstar, publishing community newspapers and operating local news websites primarily in Ontario. At its height it published more than a hundred community newspapers — including titles such as the Mississauga News, Brampton Guardian, Scarborough Mirror, and YorkRegion.com brands — reaching millions of readers in the Greater Toronto Area and southern Ontario, alongside flyer distribution and local digital services.

History

Metroland grew out of the community newspaper operations assembled by Torstar in the postwar decades, publishing free-distribution papers in Toronto's suburbs. It expanded into digital local news and marketing services in the 2000s and 2010s. Following Torstar's acquisition by Nordstar in 2020, the company faced the industry-wide decline in print advertising.

2023 restructuring

In September 2023, Metroland sought creditor protection under the Companies' Creditors Arrangement Act, ended the print publication of its dozens of community newspapers (converting them to digital-only), and cut about 600 jobs — roughly two-thirds of its workforce — one of the largest restructurings in Canadian newspaper history. Its community websites continued to publish online.
