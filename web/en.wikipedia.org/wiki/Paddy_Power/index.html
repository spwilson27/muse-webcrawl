Paddy Power

Paddy Power is an Irish gambling company founded in 1988 in Dublin, Ireland. It operates a chain of licensed betting shops in Ireland and the United Kingdom, together with online sports betting, casino, poker and bingo services. The company is part of Flutter Entertainment.

History

Paddy Power was formed in 1988 by the merger of the betting shop chains of Stewart Kenny, David Power and John Corcoran. The name derives from David Power's son, Patrick "Paddy" Power. The company floated on the Irish and London stock exchanges in 2000.

In 2016 Paddy Power merged with the online betting exchange Betfair to form Paddy Power Betfair, which later renamed itself Flutter Entertainment. The Paddy Power brand continues to be used for its retail shops and much of its UK and Irish online business.

Marketing

The company is known for irreverent, humour-led advertising and publicity stunts, as well as for offering novelty bets and "money-back specials." Some campaigns have drawn complaints and regulatory rulings, and the brand's provocative style has been both commercially successful and controversial.
