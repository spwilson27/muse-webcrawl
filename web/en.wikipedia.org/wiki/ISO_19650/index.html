ISO 19650

ISO 19650 is an international standard for managing information over the whole life cycle of a built asset using building information modelling (BIM). It contains all the principles and high-level requirements for information management previously contained in the British standards BS 1192 and PAS 1192-2, which it superseded. The standard was first published in December 2018.

Parts

The series includes Part 1 (concepts and principles), Part 2 (delivery phase of the assets), Part 3 (operational phase of the assets), Part 4 (information exchange) and Part 5 (security-minded approach to information management). ISO 19650-2:2018 is largely based on PAS 1192-2, reflecting the UK's BIM Level 2 programme.

Key concepts

The standard defines roles for appointing parties, lead appointed parties and appointed parties, and documents such as exchange information requirements (EIR), BIM execution plans (BEP), and master and task information delivery plans. Central to the approach is the common data environment (CDE), through which information containers progress through work-in-progress, shared, published and archived states, and the concept of a defined level of information need.

Adoption

ISO 19650 has been adopted as a national standard in many countries, including as BS EN ISO 19650 in the United Kingdom, and underpins government BIM mandates for public construction projects in numerous jurisdictions.
