Emilio Azcárraga Jean

Emilio Fernando Azcárraga Jean (born 21 February 1968) is a Mexican businessman who served as chairman and chief executive officer of Grupo Televisa, the largest mass media company in the Spanish-speaking world. He is the son of Emilio Azcárraga Milmo and grandson of Emilio Azcárraga Vidaurreta, the company's founder.

Career at Televisa

Azcárraga Jean took over leadership of Televisa in 1997, at age 29, following the death of his father. Under his tenure the company restructured its finances, expanded into cable and telecommunications, and in 2021 combined its content and media assets with Univision Communications to create TelevisaUnivision, the largest Spanish-language content company.

In October 2024, Azcárraga Jean took a leave of absence as executive chairman of Grupo Televisa amid a United States Department of Justice investigation related to FIFA business dealings; he remained chairman of Club América and the Estadio Azteca-related businesses. He is also owner of Club América, one of Mexico's most popular football teams.
