Brill Publishers

Brill
Founded: 1683
Headquarters: Leiden, Netherlands
Type: academic publisher

Brill (Dutch: Uitgeverij Brill), also known as E. J. Brill, Koninklijke Brill and Brill Nijhoff, is a Dutch international academic publishing company founded in 1683 in Leiden, Netherlands. The company publishes journals, books and reference works in the humanities and social sciences, international law and selected scientific fields.

History

The firm traces its origins to the bookseller Jordaan Luchtmans, who began trading in 1683. The Brill name came from Evert Jan Brill, who took over the business in the 19th century. Brill became known for scholarly works on the Middle East, Asia and classical studies, and for its typefounding tradition, which produced the Brill typeface.

Publishing programme

Brill publishes hundreds of journals and thousands of books, including the Encyclopaedia of Islam and other major reference works. In 2024 Brill combined with the German publisher De Gruyter to form De Gruyter Brill.
