Century Theatres is an American chain of movie theatres headquartered in San Rafael, California. Founded in 1941, the chain operated primarily in the western United States, particularly in California, Nevada, Utah, Arizona, Colorado and Washington.

History

Century Theatres grew from a small group of Northern California cinemas into one of the largest regional exhibitors in the United States. The chain became known for large multiplexes with stadium seating and, in later years, premium large-format screens marketed under the XD brand, featuring large screens and enhanced sound systems.

In 2006, Century Theatres was acquired by Cinemark Theatres in a deal valued at roughly $1 billion in cash and stock, creating one of the largest motion picture exhibition companies in the world. Cinemark retained the Century name at many locations, and the brand continues to operate as part of Cinemark's circuit, with most new premium locations branded as Century XD theatres.

Features

Century locations typically offer reserved seating, recliner seats in renovated auditoriums, digital projection, and Cinemark's XD premium format. The chain participates in Cinemark's Movie Club subscription programme and loyalty schemes.
