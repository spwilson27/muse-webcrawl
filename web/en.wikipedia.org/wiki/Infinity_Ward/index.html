Industry: Video games

Infinity Ward, Inc. is an American video game developer based in Woodland Hills, Los Angeles, California. Founded in 2002 by former members of 2015, Inc. — the studio behind Medal of Honor: Allied Assault — Infinity Ward created the Call of Duty franchise for publisher Activision and remains one of its principal development studios.

History

The studio's first title, Call of Duty (2003), was a World War II first-person shooter that won numerous Game of the Year awards. Infinity Ward followed with Call of Duty 2 (2005), a launch title for the Xbox 360, and then shifted the series to a contemporary setting with Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare (2007), widely credited with redefining the military shooter genre and online multiplayer design. Its sequel, Modern Warfare 2 (2009), became one of the best-selling games of its era.

Later work

After a widely publicized 2010 dispute in which founders Jason West and Vince Zampella were dismissed by Activision (they went on to form Respawn Entertainment), Infinity Ward continued the series with Modern Warfare 3 (2011, co-developed with Sledgehammer Games), Ghosts (2013), Infinite Warfare (2016), and the rebooted Modern Warfare (2019) and its sequels. The studio also led development of the battle-royale mode Warzone.
