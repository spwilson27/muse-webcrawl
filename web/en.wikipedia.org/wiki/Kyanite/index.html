Kyanite

FormulaAl2SiO5
Crystal systemTriclinic
ColourBlue, white, grey, green

Kyanite is a blue silicate mineral, found in aluminium-rich metamorphic pegmatites and sedimentary rock. It is a polymorph of andalusite and sillimanite, sharing the chemical composition Al2SiO5 but forming at higher pressures.

Properties

Kyanite is anisotropic: its hardness varies with direction, about 4.5 parallel to its length and 6.5–7 across it. Bladed blue crystals are typical, and the mineral serves geologists as an index mineral indicating high-pressure regional metamorphism. Industrially, kyanite is used as a refractory in ceramics, spark plugs, and foundry moulds.
