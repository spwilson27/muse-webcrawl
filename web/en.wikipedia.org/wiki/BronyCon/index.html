BronyCon

BronyCon was an annual fan convention for bronies, the adult and teenage fans of the animated television series My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic. It grew from small fan meetups in New York City in 2011 into one of the largest My Little Pony conventions in the world.

History

The first events were held in New York in 2011 and drew a few hundred attendees. As the fandom expanded, BronyCon moved to venues in New Jersey and then, from 2013, to the Baltimore Convention Center in Maryland. Attendance peaked at more than ten thousand people in 2015. Programming included panels with voice actors and show staff, concerts, charity auctions, cosplay, art and vendor halls, and fan-made music and animation.

Final convention and legacy

Organizers announced that 2019 would be the final BronyCon, citing declining attendance as the show approached its end. The last event was held in Baltimore in August 2019 and was framed as a celebration of the fandom's creative community. Smaller regional pony conventions continued afterward.
