General Zod

General Zod, Kryptonian military leader and enemy of Superman.

General Zod (full name Dru-Zod) is a fictional supervillain appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, commonly in association with Superman. Created by writer Robert Bernstein and artist George Papp, the character first appeared in Adventure Comics #283 (April 1961). A megalomaniacal Kryptonian general, Zod was exiled to the Phantom Zone for his crimes and, upon escaping, repeatedly attempts to conquer Earth and destroy Superman, the son of his jailer Jor-El.

Fictional character biography

Dru-Zod served as a high-ranking general in Krypton's military guild and a colleague of Jor-El. Ambitious and ruthless, he attempted to seize control of Krypton, creating an army of clones of himself and Jor-El (the Bizarro prototypes); for these crimes he was banished to the Phantom Zone for forty years. Surviving Krypton's destruction in the Zone, Zod was eventually released and gained superhuman powers under Earth's yellow sun, equal to Superman's. He is typically accompanied by fellow exiles such as Faora and Non, and commands absolute loyalty from his followers—demanding that others "kneel before Zod".

In other media

The character achieved wide fame through Terence Stamp's portrayal in Superman (1978) and Superman II (1980), where his demand that Superman kneel became iconic. Michael Shannon played Zod as the principal antagonist of Man of Steel (2013) and reprised the role in The Flash (2023). Zod has also appeared in animated series, the television series Smallville and Krypton, and numerous video games including Injustice.

Powers and abilities

As a Kryptonian under a yellow sun, Zod possesses superhuman strength, speed, invulnerability, flight, heat vision, freeze breath, and enhanced senses, weakened only by kryptonite and red-sun radiation. Unlike Superman, he couples these powers with military training and tactical ruthlessness.
