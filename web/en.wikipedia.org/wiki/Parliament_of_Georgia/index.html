Parliament of Georgia

The Parliament of Georgia (Georgian: Sakartvelos Parlamenti) is the supreme legislative body of Georgia. It is unicameral and consists of 150 members (120 from the 2024 election onward under a fully proportional system), elected for four-year terms.

History

Georgia's first parliament convened in the Democratic Republic of Georgia in 1919. After independence from the Soviet Union in 1991, a new parliament was established under the 1995 constitution. The legislature sat in Kutaisi from 2012 to 2019 before returning to the capital, Tbilisi.

Powers

Parliament passes laws, approves the budget, ratifies treaties, elects the President of Georgia (since constitutional reforms, by an electoral college), and confirms the Prime Minister and government. The Chairperson of Parliament presides over its sessions.

Elections

Following constitutional amendments, parliamentary elections are held under proportional representation with party lists, replacing the former mixed system.
