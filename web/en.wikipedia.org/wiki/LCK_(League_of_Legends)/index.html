League of Legends Champions Korea (LCK) is the top-level professional League of Legends competition in South Korea. Run by Riot Games Korea, the league comprises ten franchised teams and is widely regarded as one of the strongest regional leagues in the game's history; Korean teams won five consecutive World Championships from 2013 to 2017.

History

Professional Korean League of Legends began in 2012 with OnGameNet's The Champions tournament, won by MiG Blaze and Azubu Frost. The competition was restructured as the LCK for 2015, with production shared between OnGameNet and SPOTV before Riot Games took broadcasting in-house. In 2018 the league moved into LoL Park, a dedicated arena in Seoul's Jongno district. A franchise system replaced relegation in 2021, with ten partner teams paying buy-ins reportedly around 10 billion won.

Format

The season is divided into Spring and Summer splits. Each split consists of a double round-robin regular season of best-of-three matches, followed by playoffs for the top teams. The Spring champion (and, from 2025, the LCK Cup winner) qualifies for the Mid-Season Invitational or First Stand tournament, while the Summer results determine South Korea's seeds at the World Championship through the playoffs and regional qualifier ("gauntlet").

Teams and dynasties

The league's most famous organisation is T1 (formerly SK Telecom T1), home of star mid laner Faker and winner of multiple domestic and world titles. Other historic champions include Samsung Galaxy, KT Rolster, Gen.G, DWG KIA (Dplus KIA), and Hanwha Life Esports.
