Tiger Leaping Gorge

Tiger Leaping Gorge (Chinese: 虎跳峡; pinyin: Hǔtiào Xiá) is a scenic canyon on the Jinsha River, the upper course of the Yangtze, in Yunnan Province, China, about 60 kilometres north of Lijiang. Around 15 kilometres long, the gorge lies between Jade Dragon Snow Mountain and Haba Snow Mountain, where the river drops through rapids between cliffs rising some 3,000 metres above it, making it one of the deepest river gorges in the world.

Name and legend

According to legend, a tiger fleeing a hunter leapt across the river at its narrowest point — about 25 metres — landing on a rock in midstream, giving the gorge its name.

Tourism

The gorge is a popular trekking destination, with a high trail running along the eastern side past Naxi villages and guesthouses, and it forms part of the Three Parallel Rivers of Yunnan Protected Areas UNESCO World Heritage Site.
