Newark Bay

Newark Bay is a tidal bay at the confluence of the Passaic and Hackensack rivers in northeastern New Jersey, in the New York metropolitan area. It is connected to Upper New York Bay by the Kill Van Kull and to Raritan Bay via the Arthur Kill.

Geography

The bay is approximately 9 km (5.5 miles) long and 1–2 km wide. The cities of Newark and Elizabeth lie on its western shore, Bayonne on the east, and Kearny and Harrison along the rivers feeding it. Shooters Island, largely a bird sanctuary, lies near its southern end.

Port and industry

Newark Bay's shores host Port Newark–Elizabeth Marine Terminal, one of the busiest container ports in the United States and the largest on the East Coast by volume in many years. The bay is crossed by the Newark Bay Bridge (I-78), the Lehigh Valley Railroad Bridge and the PATH and rail lines serving the port.
