Canada Research Chair

The Canada Research Chairs program is a Canadian government initiative to attract and retain leading researchers at Canadian universities. Established in 2000, it funds research professorships in engineering, natural sciences, health sciences, humanities and social sciences.

Established: 2000
Country: Canada
Administered by: Tri-agency programs

Tiers

Tier 1 Chairs, for outstanding researchers acknowledged as world leaders, are tenable for seven years and renewable. Tier 2 Chairs, for exceptional emerging scholars, are tenable for five years and renewable once.

Allocation

Chairs are allocated to universities roughly in proportion to their share of federal research funding, and institutions nominate candidates who undergo peer review.
