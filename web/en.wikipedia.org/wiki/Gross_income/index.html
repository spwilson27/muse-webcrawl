Gross income

Gross income is an individual's total income before taxes and other deductions, or a company's total revenue less the cost of goods sold. It is the starting point from which taxes and net income are computed.

Individual gross income

For individuals, gross income includes wages, salaries, tips, interest, dividends, rental income, business income, alimony received under older agreements, pensions and other receipts. In the United States federal tax system, gross income is defined broadly by the Internal Revenue Code as "all income from whatever source derived," subject to statutory exclusions such as gifts, inheritances, municipal bond interest and certain employee benefits. Adjusted gross income (AGI) is gross income minus specific adjustments, and taxable income is AGI minus deductions and exemptions.

Business gross income

For a business, gross income (often expressed as gross profit) equals total sales revenue minus the direct cost of goods sold. It does not deduct operating expenses, interest or taxes; subtracting those yields net income. Gross margin — gross income divided by revenue — is a widely used measure of profitability.
