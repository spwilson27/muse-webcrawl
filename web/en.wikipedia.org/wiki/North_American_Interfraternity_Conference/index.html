North American Interfraternity Conference

The North American Interfraternity Conference (NIC) is an association of collegiate men's fraternities founded in 1909 at a meeting in New York City. It serves as a trade association and advocacy body for its member fraternities across the United States and Canada.

NIC
Founded: 1909
Type: Trade association
Members: 50+ men's fraternities
Headquarters: Indianapolis, Indiana

Role

The NIC sets standards for member fraternities, provides educational programming and insurance resources, advocates for students' freedom of association rights, and runs anti-hazing and health-and-safety initiatives. Its member organisations collectively initiate hundreds of thousands of undergraduate men.

History

Originally called the National Interfraternity Conference, the body reorganised in the early 2000s and adopted its present name in 2008. In 2018–2019 several large fraternities withdrew or threatened withdrawal over governance and dues disputes, leading to reforms that refocused the NIC on advocacy and support of campus Interfraternity Councils.
