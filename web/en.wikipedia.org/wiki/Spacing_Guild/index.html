Spacing Guild

The Spacing Guild is a fictional organisation in Frank Herbert's Dune universe. Together with the Imperial House Corrino and the Landsraad, it forms one of the three great powers of the Imperium. The Guild holds a monopoly on interstellar travel and banking: its enormous heighliner starships, piloted by mutated Guild Navigators who use the spice melange to gain limited prescience, are the only safe means of faster-than-light travel across the known universe.

Role in the novels

Because Navigators consume vast quantities of spice, the Guild is utterly dependent on Arrakis, the only source of melange, and this dependence shapes its politics: it prizes the uninterrupted flow of spice above any allegiance. In Dune (1965), the Guild's fear that Paul Atreides might disrupt spice production drives its pressure on the Emperor. Paul exploits the monopoly's weakness by threatening to destroy the spice cycle, forcing the Guild to back his ascension. In later novels the Guild's power wanes as Ixian navigation machines and, eventually, no-ships break its monopoly.

Navigators

Guild Navigators are humans mutated by lifelong immersion in spice gas: elongated, with webbed limbs and small heads, they live in tanks aboard heighliners. Their spice-induced prescience lets them "see" safe paths through foldspace, steering ships powered by the Holtzman effect. A senior Navigator, Edric, appears as a conspirator in Dune Messiah.
