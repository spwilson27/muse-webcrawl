Annie Lennox

Annie Lennox (born 25 December 1954) is a Scottish singer-songwriter and political activist. She rose to fame as one half of Eurythmics with Dave Stewart, whose 1983 single "Sweet Dreams (Are Made of This)" became an international hit. Her distinctive contralto voice and androgynous visual style made her one of the most recognisable British pop artists of the 1980s and 1990s.

Career

Before Eurythmics, Lennox performed with The Tourists. With Eurythmics she recorded albums including Sweet Dreams (Are Made of This), Touch, Be Yourself Tonight and Revenge. Her solo debut Diva (1992) included "Why" and "Walking on Broken Glass"; later albums include Medusa, Bare and Songs of Mass Destruction.

Activism and recognition

Lennox has campaigned on HIV/AIDS, women's rights and poverty, founding the SING campaign and supporting organisations such as Amnesty International and Greenpeace. She has won Brit Awards, Grammy Awards, a Golden Globe and an Academy Award for "Into the West" from The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King.
