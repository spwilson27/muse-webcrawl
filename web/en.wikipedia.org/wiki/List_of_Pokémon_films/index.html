List of Pokémon films

The List of Pokémon films covers the animated feature films based on the Pokémon media franchise, produced principally by OLM and distributed in Japan by Toho. Beginning with Pokémon: The First Movie in 1998, the series produced a new film nearly every year for over two decades, most of them following Ash Ketchum and Pikachu on adventures alongside legendary Pokémon.

Later series

Subsequent films accompanied the Advanced Generation, Diamond and Pearl, Black and White, XY, and Sun and Moon anime series. Pokémon: Lucario and the Mystery of Mew (2005) and the Diamond and Pearl trilogy are frequently singled out by fans. Starting with Pokémon the Movie: I Choose You! (2017), the films moved into an alternate continuity retelling Ash's journey. Pokémon the Movie: Secrets of the Jungle (2020) was the 23rd film, featuring the mythical Pokémon Zarude.

Live-action

In 2019 Legendary Pictures released Pokémon Detective Pikachu, the franchise's first live-action film, starring Ryan Reynolds as the voice of Pikachu. It grossed over $450 million worldwide.
