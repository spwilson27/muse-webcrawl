Fish

The freshwater whitefishes are fish of the subfamily Coregoninae in the salmon family Salmonidae, including the genera Coregonus (true whitefishes, ciscoes, and inconnu), Prosopium (round whitefishes), and Stenodus. They inhabit cold lakes and rivers of northern North America, Europe, and Asia, and include the lake whitefish (Coregonus clupeaformis), a major commercial species of the North American Great Lakes, prized for its mild white flesh and roe. The taxonomy of the group is notoriously complex because populations in different lakes have diverged rapidly since the last glaciation, producing many local forms.

The name "whitefish" is also a fisheries term for various marine demersal species such as cod and haddock — see white fish.

Places

Whitefish, Montana is a resort town in Flathead County, Montana, United States, near Glacier National Park and noted for the Whitefish Mountain Resort ski area. Whitefish Point, on Michigan's Upper Peninsula along Lake Superior, is known for the Great Lakes Shipwreck Museum and its lighthouse.
