Siargao is a tear-drop shaped island in the Philippine Sea, in the province of Surigao del Norte in Mindanao, Philippines. It is internationally known as the surfing capital of the Philippines, centred on the Cloud 9 break near the town of General Luna.

Geography

The island covers about 437 square kilometres and contains the largest mangrove forest reserves in Mindanao. Nearby attractions include the Sugba Lagoon, the Magpupungko rock pools, and the islets of Naked, Daku, and Guyam.

Surfing

Cloud 9 is a powerful reef break producing thick, hollow tubes, best surfed from September to December. It hosts international competitions, including a World Surf League qualifying event. Siargao was devastated by Typhoon Odette (Rai) in December 2021 and has since largely rebuilt.
