ARRL

The American Radio Relay League (ARRL) is the national association for amateur radio in the United States. Founded in 1914 by Hiram Percy Maxim and Clarence D. Tuska, it is headquartered in Newington, Connecticut, and represents the interests of US amateur radio operators to the FCC and international bodies.

Activities

The ARRL publishes the monthly magazine QST (since 1915), organizes contests such as Field Day and the ARRL DX Contest, coordinates volunteer examiner licensing sessions, and operates the Logbook of the World (LoTW) confirmation service. Its headquarters station, W1AW, transmits bulletins and Morse code practice. The league also coordinates emergency communications through the Amateur Radio Emergency Service (ARES).

History

The "relay" in its name refers to early members relaying messages across distances by shortwave. ARRL advocacy helped preserve amateur allocations through successive international radio conferences, and the organization is the US member society of the International Amateur Radio Union (IARU).
