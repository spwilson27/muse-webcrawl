Shebitku

Shebitku
Pharaoh of Egypt, 25th (Kushite) Dynasty
Reign: c. 714/713–705 BCE
Predecessor: Shabaka
Successor: Taharqa

Shebitku (also Shabataka) was a Kushite king of Napata and the third pharaoh of the Twenty-fifth Dynasty of Egypt, reigning roughly 714/713–705 BCE. He succeeded his uncle (or father) Shabaka and was succeeded by Taharqa.

Reign

Shebitku confronted the expansion of Assyria under Sennacherib. Egyptian-Kushite forces supported the revolt of Hezekiah of Judah; the account in the Hebrew Bible of the destruction of Sennacherib's army (701 BCE) credits an Egyptian-Kushite force under "Tirhakah" (Taharqa). Inscriptions record Shebitku's building work at Thebes, where the Divine Wife of Amun, Amenirdis I, served under him. His pyramid tomb is at el-Kurru in Nubia.
