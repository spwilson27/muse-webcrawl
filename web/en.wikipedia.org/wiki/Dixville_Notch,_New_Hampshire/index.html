Dixville Notch, New Hampshire

Dixville Notch is an unincorporated community in Dixville township, Coös County, New Hampshire, United States. The village takes its name from the mountain pass (a "notch" in New England usage) through which New Hampshire Route 26 runs, connecting the Connecticut River valley with the Androscoggin River drainage near Errol.

Midnight voting tradition

Dixville Notch is best known nationally for its tradition of voting in the first minutes of election day in United States presidential elections and in the New Hampshire primary. Under a practice begun in 1960, all eligible voters in the tiny community gather at the Balsams Grand Resort Hotel at midnight, cast their ballots, and the polls are closed once every registered voter has voted — results that are then widely reported as the first in the nation, alongside the similar tradition at Hart's Location. New Hampshire law allows polling places to close once all registered voters have cast ballots.

The village's results have often been treated by the media as a curiosity and a miniature bellwether; in 2016 Hillary Clinton won the Dixville Notch vote, while in 2020 Joe Biden swept all five votes cast, and in 2024 Kamala Harris and Donald Trump tied three votes apiece.

The Balsams

The dominant landmark of the village is The Balsams Grand Resort Hotel, a grand hotel founded in the nineteenth century and opened in its present form in 1866 (the Hale House) and enlarged under industrialist Henry Hale in the early twentieth century. The resort closed in 2011 and its redevelopment has been the subject of long-running plans and debate in the North Country.

Geography

Dixville Notch (the pass, elevation about 1,871 feet / 570 m) is flanked by Dixville Peak and Cave Mountain, with dramatic cliffs above the highway. Nearby is Lake Gloriette, an artificial lake in front of the Balsams along Route 26.
