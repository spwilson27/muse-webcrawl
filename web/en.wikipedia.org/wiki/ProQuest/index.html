ProQuest LLC is an Ann Arbor, Michigan-based global information-content and technology company, founded in 1938 as University Microfilms. ProQuest is known for its applications and information services for libraries, providing access to dissertations, theses, ebooks, newspapers, periodicals, historical collections, governmental archives, and aggregated databases of full-text articles. Its content is commonly accessed through library subscriptions.

History

Eugene Power founded University Microfilms in 1938 to preserve works from the British Museum on microfilm. The company became the principal repository for doctoral dissertations in North America, publishing Dissertation Abstracts and later the ProQuest Dissertations and Theses database. Over subsequent decades it expanded into periodical indexes, historical newspaper archives, and electronic reference products, and changed its name to ProQuest in 2001. ProQuest acquired several other information businesses, including CSA, Chadwyck-Healey, and Ex Libris (2015). In 2021 ProQuest was acquired by Clarivate in a deal valued at about $5.3 billion.

Products

Major products include the ProQuest Central multi-disciplinary database, ProQuest Dissertations & Theses Global, historical newspaper collections (such as the Historical New York Times), Ebook Central, and library systems from Ex Libris such as the Alma library services platform.
