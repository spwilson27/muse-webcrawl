Pokémon (anime)

This article covers the Pokémon animated franchise as a whole. For the main television series, see Pokémon (TV series).

The Pokémon anime is the body of animated works based on the Pokémon media franchise created by Satoshi Tajiri. It began with the television series in 1997 and expanded to include theatrical films, television specials, short films, and web series, becoming one of the most recognisable anime properties worldwide.

Television series

The long-running TV series is divided into successive series corresponding to the main video game generations, each set in a new region. Most followed Ash Ketchum and Pikachu from 1997 until 2023; Pokémon Horizons: The Series then introduced new lead characters. The series has aired in more than 190 countries and territories.

Films

Beginning with Pokémon: The First Movie (1998), featuring the legendary Pokémon Mewtwo, animated Pokémon films were released annually for over two decades. The franchise also produced the live-action/CGI film Pokémon Detective Pikachu (2019).

Influence

The anime popularised Pokémon globally and shaped the broader international boom in Japanese animation and children's media franchises in the late 1990s and 2000s.
