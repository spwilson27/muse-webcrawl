European Green Deal

The European Green Deal, approved in 2020, is a set of policy initiatives by the European Commission with the overarching aim of making the European Union climate neutral by 2050. Announced by Commission President Ursula von der Leyen in December 2019, it has been described as Europe's "man on the Moon moment".

Key elements

The deal includes the European Climate Law, which writes the 2050 target and an intermediate 55% emission reduction target for 2030 into law; the "Fit for 55" legislative package; the Circular Economy Action Plan; the Biodiversity Strategy for 2030; and the Just Transition Mechanism to support regions most affected by the move away from fossil fuels.
