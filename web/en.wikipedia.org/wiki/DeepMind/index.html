Google DeepMind is a British-American artificial intelligence research laboratory, formed in April 2023 by the merger of DeepMind with Google's Brain team. Founded in London in 2010 by Demis Hassabis, Shane Legg, and Mustafa Suleyman, DeepMind was acquired by Google in 2014 for a reported £400 million or more. The company's stated mission is to "solve intelligence" and use it to advance science and benefit humanity.

Milestones

  • Atari DQN (2013–2015) — a single deep reinforcement-learning agent learned to play dozens of Atari 2600 games from raw pixels.
  • AlphaGo (2016) — defeated Lee Sedol, one of the world's strongest Go players, 4–1 in Seoul; its successor AlphaZero mastered Go, chess, and shogi by self-play alone.
  • AlphaFold (2020) — AlphaFold 2 effectively solved the protein structure prediction problem at CASP14; DeepMind released predicted structures for over 200 million proteins. Hassabis and John Jumper shared half of the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for this work.
  • Gemini (2023– ) — Google's flagship multimodal large language model family, developed by the merged Google DeepMind organisation.

Other research

DeepMind systems have controlled plasma in a tokamak, speeded matrix-multiplication algorithms (AlphaTensor), discovered faster sorting routines (AlphaDev), forecast weather (GraphCast), and predicted the structures of materials (GNoME). Its work on AI safety and ethics has included the short-lived independent ethics board envisaged at acquisition and later internal responsible-AI teams.
