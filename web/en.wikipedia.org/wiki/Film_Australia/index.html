Film Australia

Film Australia was an Australian Government film production company based in Sydney. It traced its origins to the Commonwealth Film Unit, established in 1946, and was formally constituted as Film Australia in 1973. Its Sydney studios at Lindfield produced documentaries, educational films, television programmes, and features promoting Australia at home and abroad.

History

The Commonwealth Film Unit, later the Australian Film, Television and Radio School predecessor body known as Film Division, produced thousands of information films for government departments. Renamed Film Australia in 1973, the organisation became a producer of acclaimed documentary series and drama. In 1988 it was restructured as a government-owned company, Film Australia Limited.

Closure

In 2008, Film Australia was merged with the Australian Film Commission and the Film Finance Corporation to form Screen Australia, ending its independent existence. Its extensive film archive, dating back decades, documents Australian life and is preserved as part of the national audiovisual collection.
