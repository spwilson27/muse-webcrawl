Moon Jae-in (Korean: 문재인; born 24 January 1953) is a South Korean politician and former human rights lawyer who served as the 12th president of South Korea from 2017 to 2022. Prior to his presidency, he served as senior secretary for civil affairs and chief of staff to President Roh Moo-hyun, member of the National Assembly, and leader of the Democratic Party of Korea.

Early life and career

Moon was born in Geoje to North Korean refugee parents who had been evacuated during the Korean War. He studied law at Kyung Hee University, was arrested for protesting against the Yushin Constitution, and later passed the bar exam. As a lawyer in Busan, he worked alongside Roh Moo-hyun and became known for human rights cases.

Presidency

Moon won the 2017 presidential election following the impeachment of Park Geun-hye. His presidency was marked by diplomatic engagement with North Korea, including three inter-Korean summits in 2018 and meetings between Kim Jong Un and U.S. President Donald Trump. Domestically, his administration pursued income-led growth policies, raised the minimum wage, expanded welfare, and managed South Korea's widely praised early response to the COVID-19 pandemic.
