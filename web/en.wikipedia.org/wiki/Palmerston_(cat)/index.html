Palmerston (cat)

Palmerston is a domestic cat who served as Chief Mouser of the Foreign & Commonwealth Office (FCO) in London from 2016 to 2020. A black-and-white bicolor cat, he was adopted from Battersea Dogs & Cats Home and named after the nineteenth-century foreign secretary and prime minister Viscount Palmerston.

Career

Palmerston arrived at the Foreign Office in April 2016. His role continued the long Whitehall tradition of government "chief mousers", most famously embodied by Larry of 10 Downing Street. The two cats were occasionally reported to skirmish on Downing Street, incidents eagerly covered by the British press, and Palmerston was once filmed being ejected from Number 10 by a police officer.

Retirement

In August 2020 the Foreign Office announced that Palmerston would "retire" to the countryside home of a member of staff who had cared for him during lockdown, jokingly publishing a resignation letter signed with a paw print. His departure was widely reported, and he was credited with catching at least several dozen mice during his tenure.
