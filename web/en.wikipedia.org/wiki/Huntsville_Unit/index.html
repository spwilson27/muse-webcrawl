Huntsville Unit

The Huntsville Unit (HV), nicknamed the Walls Unit, is a Texas state prison located in Huntsville, Texas, United States. Opened in 1849, it is the oldest state prison in Texas and is operated by the Texas Department of Criminal Justice.

Executions

The Huntsville Unit houses the State of Texas execution chamber, the most active in the United States. Since the resumption of capital punishment in Texas in 1982, executions by lethal injection have been carried out there; before 1924, executions took place by hanging at county level, and from 1924 to 1964 the prison's electric chair, nicknamed "Old Sparky", was used. The prison's nickname derives from its high red-brick walls.

Other functions

The unit also serves as a regional release centre for inmates leaving the Texas prison system, and the Texas Prison Museum operates nearby. The Captain Joe Byrd Cemetery in Huntsville holds the burials of unclaimed executed and deceased inmates.
