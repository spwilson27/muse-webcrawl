Komi (Go)

Komi (Japanese: コミ, 込み) is a scoring bonus in the board game Go, awarded to the second player (White) to compensate for Black's first-move advantage. In modern rules it is usually a fractional number of points, such as 6.5 or 7.5, the half point serving to prevent drawn games.

History and values

Komi was introduced in Japanese professional Go in the twentieth century as even games without compensation proved to favour Black. Its value has crept upward over the decades — from 4.5 or 5.5 points in earlier eras to 6.5 in Japan and 7.5 in China and Korea — as statistics and, more recently, artificial intelligence analysis suggested Black's advantage was larger than once assumed. AI engines estimate the advantage at roughly 7 points on a 19×19 board under Chinese scoring.

Variations

Different rulesets use different komi: Chinese and AGA rules use 7.5, Japanese and Korean rules use 6.5, and some systems have used integer komi with draws possible. Komi may also be negative or enlarged in handicap games.
