Roger W. Smith OBE (born 1970) is a British independent watchmaker based on the Isle of Man. A protégé of the master horologist George Daniels, Smith is one of very few watchmakers who build a watch entirely by hand using traditional methods, and is regarded as the principal heir to the English handmade watch tradition.

Roger W. Smith at work on the Isle of Man.

Career

Smith trained at the Manchester School of Horology and spent seven years making, on his own, a pocket watch to Daniels' standards of complete self-reliance — the so-called "Daniels Method", in which a single maker crafts every component. His first attempt was rejected by Daniels; his second, completed after a further five years, was accepted. Smith moved to the Isle of Man to work alongside Daniels, collaborating on the Millennium series of watches for Omega.

He established his own studio, Roger W Smith Ltd, in 2001. His watches, produced in very small numbers each year — around ten to fifteen pieces — include the Series 1 through Series 5 models, featuring his refinement of Daniels' co-axial escapement and hand-finished cases, dials and movements. Smith was awarded the OBE for services to British watchmaking.
