The Sambadrome Marquês de Sapucaí (Portuguese: Sambódromo da Marquês de Sapucaí) is a purpose-built parade ground in downtown Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, created to host the annual Rio Carnival samba school parades.

Design and history

Designed by architect Oscar Niemeyer and inaugurated in 1984, the venue consists of a 700-metre parade avenue flanked by spectator stands, ending in a large square dominated by a distinctive arch. It is named after the street along which it was built. The top samba schools of the Special Group parade here on Carnival nights, judged on costume, music, choreography and floats.

Other uses

Outside Carnival the Sambadrome hosts concerts and other events; during the 2016 Summer Olympics it staged the archery competition and part of the marathon course.
