William Malone (born 1953) is an American horror filmmaker. He is best known for directing the 1999 remake House on Haunted Hill and the cult science-fiction horror film Scared to Death (1981). Malone is also noted as a major collector of memorabilia from the 1956 film Forbidden Planet, having owned the original Robby the Robot prop.

Early life and career

Malone was born in Lansing, Michigan. As a teenager he made amateur monster movies, and at nineteen he moved to Los Angeles, where he worked at the Don Post Studios mask factory, making Halloween masks. His experience with makeup and props led him into the film industry.

Films

Malone's feature directorial debut, Scared to Death (also released as The Aberdeen Experiment), was made for a very low budget and developed a cult following. He went on to direct Creature (1985), a science-fiction horror film set aboard a spacecraft, and episodes of television series including Tales from the Crypt, Freddy's Nightmares, and Masters of Horror, for which he directed the controversial episode "The Fair Haired Child" (2006).

His biggest commercial success was the Dark Castle Entertainment production House on Haunted Hill (1999), a remake of William Castle's 1959 film, which opened at number one at the US box office. He later directed FeardotCom (2002) and Parasomnia (2008).

Forbidden Planet collection

A lifelong science fiction fan, Malone assembled one of the largest private collections of Forbidden Planet memorabilia, including Robby the Robot, which he sold at auction in 2017 for a then-record price for a movie prop.
