Address

An address is a collection of information, presented in a mostly fixed format, used to give the location of a building, apartment, plot of land or other structure, or of a person or organisation for the purpose of delivering mail and locating the place. Addresses generally include a street number and name, the settlement, a district or state, and a postal code, though formats vary widely between countries.

Formats and systems

National postal authorities standardise address formats; the Universal Postal Union coordinates international addressing, and many countries maintain official address databases (such as the UK Postcode Address File or the US ZIP Code system). Addressing systems differ structurally — for example, Japan's system is based on nested administrative areas and city blocks rather than streets, while much of rural Ireland historically used townlands. Precise geocoding systems and what3words-style identifiers supplement conventional addresses in some applications.
