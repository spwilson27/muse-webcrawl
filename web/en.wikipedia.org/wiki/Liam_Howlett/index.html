Liam Howlett

Born: Liam Paul Paris Howlett, 21 August 1971, Braintree, Essex, England

Occupation: Musician, songwriter, DJ, record producer

Known for: Founder and songwriter of the Prodigy

Liam Paul Paris Howlett (born 21 August 1971) is an English musician, songwriter, DJ, and record producer, best known as the founder, main songwriter, and keyboardist of the electronic dance music band the Prodigy. He formed the group in Braintree, Essex, in 1990 after meeting dancer Keith Flint and DJ Leeroy Thornhill on the rave scene.

Career

Influenced first by hip hop and then by the UK rave scene, Howlett produced the Prodigy's early EPs and the debut album Experience (1992). Music for the Jilted Generation (1994) and The Fat of the Land (1997) — featuring the number-one singles "Firestarter" and "Breathe" — made the band a global act, with Howlett as its sole songwriter and studio architect. He has written and produced all of the group's subsequent albums, including Always Outnumbered, Never Outgunned (2004), Invaders Must Die (2009), The Day Is My Enemy (2015), and No Tourists (2018).

Other work and personal life

Howlett released the DJ mix album The Dirtchamber Sessions Volume One in 1999 under the name "Liam Prodigy". He is married to Natalie Appleton of All Saints, with whom he has a son. Following Keith Flint's death in 2019, Howlett returned the Prodigy to touring in 2022 and has continued producing new material.
