Southern Jaguars
University: Southern University
Stadium: A. W. Mumford Stadium, Baton Rouge, Louisiana

Head coach history includes Ace Mumford, Pete Richardson

The Southern Jaguars football programme represents Southern University and A&M College in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, in NCAA Division I FCS football as a member of the Southwestern Athletic Conference (SWAC).

History

The programme's golden era came under coach Ace Mumford (1936–1961), for whom the stadium is named, and reached a modern peak under Pete Richardson (1993–2009), who won four Black college national championships and five SWAC titles. The Jaguars play their arch-rivals Grambling State in the annual Bayou Classic in New Orleans, one of the most celebrated events in HBCU sport, and face Jackson State in the BoomBox Classic.

Culture

The Human Jukebox marching band, which performs with the team at the Bayou Classic, is regarded as one of the finest show bands in the United States and a central part of the game-day spectacle.
