2025

2025 was a year in which continuing wars, a change of administration in the United States, advances in artificial intelligence and major sporting and cultural events dominated headlines.

Overview

Year pages on Wikipedia list events by month and topic — international politics, disasters, elections, science, sport, arts and deaths of notable people — with links to detailed articles. This summary records only the broad remembered shape of the year rather than a dated chronicle, as specific day-by-day events are not reliably recalled in order.
