Link (The Legend of Zelda)

Link is a fictional character and the protagonist of Nintendo's video game franchise The Legend of Zelda, created by Japanese game designers Shigeru Miyamoto and Takashi Tezuka. He first appeared in The Legend of Zelda (1986) and has featured in nearly every subsequent entry in the series.

Character

Link is traditionally a left-handed (in most titles) Hylian youth from the kingdom of Hyrule, chosen by the goddess Hylia to wield the Master Sword and oppose the forces of evil, most often the Demon King Ganon. He is usually depicted wearing a green tunic and cap, though Breath of the Wild made a blue Champion's Tunic his signature look. Link rarely speaks on screen, communicating through gestures and player-selected responses.

Concept

Miyamoto named him Link because he links the player to the game world. Across the series' split timelines, multiple incarnations of Link appear, each a distinct hero of his own era. He is one of the most recognizable characters in gaming and also appears in the Super Smash Bros. series.
