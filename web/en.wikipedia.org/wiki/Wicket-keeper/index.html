Wicket-keeper

The wicket-keeper in cricket is the fielder on the fielding side who stands behind the wicket at the striker's end, wearing gloves and pads. The role is a specialist position governed by Law 27 of the Laws of Cricket.

Role

The keeper's primary duties are to take deliveries that pass the striker, effect stumpings when a batter leaves the crease, take catches off edges, and assist in run outs. A keeper may also stand up to the stumps for slower bowlers or back for fast bowlers.

Equipment and laws

The wicket-keeper is the only fielder permitted gloves and external leg guards. If the keeper's gloves or person collect the ball in front of the wicket improperly, penalties can apply. A substitute may not keep wicket without the umpires' consent under modern playing conditions.

Keeper-batters

Modern cricket values keepers who also bat strongly, such as Adam Gilchrist, MS Dhoni, Kumar Sangakkara and Jos Buttler. Records for most dismissals are led by Mark Boucher in Test cricket. Notable pure keepers include Godfrey Evans, Wally Grout and Sarah Taylor.
