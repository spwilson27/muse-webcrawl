Rockstar Games, Inc. is an American video game publisher based in New York City. Established in December 1998 as a subsidiary of Take-Two Interactive, the company is best known for the Grand Theft Auto series, and also publishes the Red Dead, Max Payne, Manhunt, Midnight Club and Bully franchises.

The Rockstar Games logo.

History

Rockstar was founded by British video game producers Sam and Dan Houser, Terry Donovan, Jamie King and Gary Foreman, initially working with BMG Interactive before its acquisition by Take-Two. The label gathered studios including DMA Design, renamed Rockstar North, which developed Grand Theft Auto III (2001), a landmark in open-world game design. Successive Grand Theft Auto titles became among the best-selling video games of all time; Grand Theft Auto V (2013) alone has sold over 200 million copies.

Studios

Rockstar operates studios around the world, among them Rockstar North in Edinburgh, Rockstar Leeds, Rockstar Lincoln, Rockstar London, Rockstar New England, Rockstar San Diego, Rockstar Toronto, Rockstar India and Rockstar Australia. Together they develop the company's major titles collaboratively.
