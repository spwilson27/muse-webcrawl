Believe is the twenty-second studio album by American singer Cher, released on 22 October 1998 by WEA and Warner Bros. Records. A dance-pop album that marked a decisive turn toward electronic dance music, Believe revived Cher's commercial fortunes and became the best-selling album of her career, with sales of more than 11 million copies worldwide.

Singles

The title track, "Believe", was a worldwide number-one hit in 23 countries and the best-selling single of 1999 in the UK. Its heavily processed vocals popularised the conspicuous use of Auto-Tune pitch correction as a creative effect — widely dubbed the "Cher effect". It made Cher, at 52, the oldest female artist to top the UK chart at the time, and won the Grammy Award for Best Dance Recording. Further singles included "Strong Enough", "All or Nothing" and "Dov'è l'amore".

Reception and legacy

Critics praised the album's reinvention of Cher as a dance artist. Believe was nominated for the Grammy Award for Best Pop Album, and its title song's vocal production became a landmark in pop recording, influencing the pervasive Auto-Tune sound of 2000s and 2010s pop and hip hop. The album was supported by the Do You Believe? tour (1999–2000).
