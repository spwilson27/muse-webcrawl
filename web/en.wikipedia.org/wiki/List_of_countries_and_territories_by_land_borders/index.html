Longest borders

The longest international land border is that between Canada and the United States, at 8,891 km including the Alaska segment. Other very long borders include Kazakhstan–Russia (about 7,600 km), Chile–Argentina (about 6,700 km), and Mongolia's borders with China and Russia. Island states such as Japan, Australia, and the United Kingdom (one land border, with Ireland) sit at the other extreme.

Landlocked countries

Forty-four countries are landlocked. Only two, Liechtenstein and Uzbekistan, are doubly landlocked — surrounded entirely by landlocked states. The country with the longest total land border is China, followed by Russia, whose borders span Europe and Asia.
