The Marshall Mathers LP 2

Artist: Eminem
Released: 5 November 2013
Labels: Aftermath, Interscope, Shady
Producers: Dr. Dre, Rick Rubin (executive), Eminem and others
Genre: Hip hop

The Marshall Mathers LP 2 is the eighth studio album by the American rapper Eminem, released on 5 November 2013 by Aftermath Entertainment, Interscope Records and Shady Records. Conceived as a sequel and "revisitation" of his landmark album The Marshall Mathers LP (2000), it was executive-produced by Dr. Dre and Rick Rubin and recorded between 2012 and 2013.

Background and music

Eminem announced the album during the 2013 MTV Video Music Awards. The cover shows the boarded-up house at 19946 Dresden Street in Detroit where he grew up — the same house depicted on the original LP's cover; the house burned and was demolished shortly after the new cover photograph was taken. Musically the album mixes the original record's themes and characters with Rubin-influenced rock sampling: "Berzerk" samples Billy Squier's "The Stroke", and "Rhyme or Reason" samples the Zombies' "Time of the Season".

Track listing and singles

Singles included "Berzerk", "Survival", "Rap God" — noted for a verse in which Eminem raps 1560 words across the song, including a celebrated rapid-fire section — and "The Monster" featuring Rihanna, which reached number one in many countries, as well as "Headlights", an apology to his mother, featuring Nate Ruess. Other guests include Kendrick Lamar, Skylar Grey and Jamie N Commons. The album contains skits echoing the 2000 record, and its closing track follows on from the narrative of "Stan" through the perspective of Stan's brother Matthew ("Bad Guy").

Reception

The album debuted at number one on the Billboard 200 with 792,000 copies sold in its first week, and won the Grammy Award for Best Rap Album. Reviews were generally favourable, praising Eminem's technical rapping while some critics found the revival of his Slim Shady persona's shock value strained.
