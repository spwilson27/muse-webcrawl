Remake

A remake is a film, television series, video game, or similar work that is based on and retells the story of an earlier production in the same medium. The term has a variety of connotations, from broadly acceptable reinterpretations to "shot-for-shot" copies. Remakes are distinct from sequels, which continue a story, and from reboots, which restart a franchise with a new continuity.

Film

In cinema, remakes are made for many reasons: the original may have been in another language (as with many Hollywood versions of European or Asian films), may have used outdated technology, or may have a proven story that studios hope will succeed again with a modern audience and cast. Examples include The Magnificent Seven (1960), a remake of Akira Kurosawa's Seven Samurai; Martin Scorsese's Cape Fear (1991); and the many remakes of A Star Is Born.

Video games

In video games, a remake rebuilds a game from the ground up with new code, graphics and sometimes altered design, while a remaster updates an existing game's presentation without rebuilding it. Remakes such as Resident Evil 2 (2019) and Final Fantasy VII Remake (2020) have been major commercial successes.

Criticism

Remakes are often criticised as evidence of creative exhaustion in the entertainment industry, though some are praised for improving on or reinterpreting their sources.
