Calvert family

The Calvert family was an English Catholic family that held the title Baron Baltimore and founded the proprietary colony of Maryland in North America. The family's arms — black and gold pales with a bend counterchanged — survive in the flag of Maryland.

History

George Calvert, 1st Baron Baltimore (c. 1580–1632), a former Secretary of State to James I, obtained a charter for Maryland shortly before his death. His son Cecilius Calvert, 2nd Baron Baltimore, issued the charter's instructions and sent the first settlers aboard the Ark and the Dove in 1634. His younger son Leonard Calvert became the colony's first governor.

Proprietary rule

The Calverts governed Maryland as proprietors for most of the period until the American Revolution, promoting religious toleration (notably the Maryland Toleration Act of 1649) while navigating conflicts with Protestant settlers and Parliament. The last proprietor, Frederick Calvert, 6th Baron Baltimore, died in 1771.
