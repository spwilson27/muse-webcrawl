Arecibo message

The Arecibo message is an interstellar radio message carrying basic information about humanity and Earth that was sent to the globular cluster Messier 13 in 1974.

Arecibo message
Sent16 November 1974
FromArecibo Telescope, Puerto Rico
TargetMessier 13
Length1,679 bits

Design

Designed by Frank Drake with Carl Sagan and others, the 1,679-bit message — the product of primes 23 and 73 — arranges into a pictogram when arranged as 73 rows by 23 columns. It encodes numbers 1–10, atomic numbers of elements in DNA, DNA structure, a human figure, the Solar System, and the Arecibo telescope itself.

Transmission

It was broadcast at 2,380 MHz during a ceremony marking remodelling of the Arecibo Telescope. It will take about 25,000 years to reach M13. The transmission was more a demonstration of technology than a serious attempt at communication.

Legacy

The message inspired later interstellar messages and public responses, including a "reply" hoax crop formation in 2001 near Chilbolton.
