Empress Matilda (c. 1102–1167), also called Maud, was the daughter and designated heir of Henry I of England. Widowed from Emperor Henry V in 1125, she married Geoffrey of Anjou, but on Henry I's death in 1135 her cousin Stephen of Blois seized the throne.

The Anarchy

Matilda landed in England in 1139, beginning the civil war known as the Anarchy. She captured Stephen at Lincoln in 1141 and was styled "Lady of the English," but Londoners drove her out before coronation, and the capture of her half-brother Robert of Gloucester forced Stephen's release. Famous episodes include her escape from Oxford Castle across the frozen Thames in winter 1142. She left England in 1148.

Legacy

From Normandy Matilda supported her son Henry's claim; after the Treaty of Wallingford (1153), Henry succeeded as Henry II in 1154, founding the Plantagenet dynasty. She acted as his adviser in Rouen until her death in 1167.
