North Dakota State University

North Dakota State University (NDSU) is a public land-grant research university in Fargo, North Dakota. Founded in 1890 as the North Dakota Agricultural College, it is the state's flagship agricultural and applied sciences institution and part of the North Dakota University System.

NDSU
Established: 1890
Location: Fargo, North Dakota
Nickname: Bison
Students: ~11,000–12,000

Academics and research

NDSU offers more than 100 undergraduate and dozens of graduate programmes across agriculture, engineering, health professions, business, and the arts and sciences. It is classified among "R1: Doctoral Universities — Very high research activity," with strengths in agriculture, materials science, and coatings and polymers.

Athletics

The Bison compete in NCAA Division I. The football programme, playing at the Fargodome, became an FCS dynasty, winning nine national championships in eleven seasons from 2011 to 2021.
