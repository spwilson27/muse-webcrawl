Secondary audio program

Secondary audio program (SAP) is a feature of the MTS (multichannel television sound) system used in analogue North American television broadcasting. It provides an additional monaural audio channel carried alongside the main stereo programme audio.

Uses

SAP is most commonly used for an alternative-language soundtrack — in the United States typically Spanish-language audio for English-language programmes — or for Descriptive Video Service (DVS) audio descriptions for blind and visually impaired viewers, in which a narrator describes on-screen action. It has also carried unrelated services such as weather radio or reading services. Viewers select SAP through a television's audio menu. In digital television, equivalent functions are provided by separate audio streams within the ATSC standard.
