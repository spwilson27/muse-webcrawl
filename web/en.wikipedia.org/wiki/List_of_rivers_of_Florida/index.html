List of rivers of Florida

Florida has more than 1,700 streams and rivers. Because the peninsula is low and flat, most Florida rivers are slow-moving, and many are fed by the state's numerous first-magnitude springs. The longest river entirely in Florida is the St. Johns, which flows north — one of the few major North American rivers to do so.
