List of largest optical telescopes historically

This list traces the succession of the world's largest optical telescopes from the first refractors of the early seventeenth century to the giant reflectors of the modern era. For most of history the record was held by a single instrument until a larger one superseded it; aperture grew from a few centimetres to more than ten metres.

Notes

The Yerkes 40-inch remains the largest refracting telescope ever successfully used for astronomy, as lens size is limited by sagging under the glass's own weight. Segmented and multi-mirror designs have dominated since the 1990s; the forthcoming Extremely Large Telescope (39.3 m, Chile) is expected to take the record when completed.
