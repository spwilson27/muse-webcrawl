TORRO scale

The TORRO tornado intensity scale (or T-Scale) is a scale measuring tornado intensity between T0 and T11, developed by Terence Meaden of the Tornado and Storm Research Organisation (TORRO) in the United Kingdom.

Scale

The scale relates wind speed to expected damage, ranging from T0 (light damage, 39–72 km/h) to T11 (super tornado, above 480 km/h). It is subdivided more finely than the Fujita scale and is widely used in Europe.
