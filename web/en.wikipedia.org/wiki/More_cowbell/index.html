More cowbell

"More cowbell" is a comedy sketch from a 2000 episode of the NBC television series Saturday Night Live. In the sketch, record producer "The Bruce Dickinson" (played by Christopher Walken) repeatedly demands that the fictional cowbell player Gene Frenkle (played by Will Ferrell) play his instrument louder during a recording session by the band Blue Öyster Cult of their song "(Don't Fear) The Reaper".

The sketch

The sketch first aired on April 8, 2000. It portrays the band recording "(Don't Fear) The Reaper" in 1976, with Frenkle's enthusiastic and distracting cowbell playing continually interrupting takes. Dickinson, however, insists he has "a fever, and the only prescription is more cowbell". The sketch also featured Chris Kattan, Chris Parnell, Horatio Sanz, and Jimmy Fallon as members of the band.

Cultural impact

"More cowbell" became a widely quoted catchphrase and an enduring piece of American popular culture, frequently listed among the greatest Saturday Night Live sketches of all time. The phrase has been used in everyday speech to suggest enthusiastically adding more of something, and has appeared on merchandise, in political commentary, and in tributes to the real Blue Öyster Cult, whose actual recording of the song does feature a cowbell.
