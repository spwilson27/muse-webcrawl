Mahendra Singh Dhoni (born 7 July 1981) is an Indian professional cricketer who plays as a right-handed batter and a wicket-keeper. Widely regarded as one of the most prolific wicket-keeper-batsmen and captains, he represented the Indian cricket team and was its captain from 2007 to 2017 in limited-overs formats and from 2008 to 2014 in Test cricket. Playing as a right-handed middle-order batter, he is also India's most successful captain in international cricket, having led the team to win the 2007 ICC World Twenty20, the 2011 Cricket World Cup, and the 2013 ICC Champions Trophy — the only captain to win all three major ICC white-ball trophies.

Early life

Dhoni was born in Ranchi, in present-day Jharkhand, to a family originally from Uttarakhand. He excelled at school in badminton and football before being sent to fill in as a cricket wicket-keeper, which revealed his aptitude. Before cricket became a career, he worked as a Travelling Ticket Examiner (TTE) for Indian Railways at Kharagpur station from 2001 to 2003 — a period later dramatised in his 2016 biographical film M.S. Dhoni: The Untold Story.

International career

Dhoni debuted in One Day Internationals against Bangladesh in December 2004 and in Tests a year later against Sri Lanka. He announced himself with a blistering 148 against Pakistan in Visakhapatnam and 183 not out against Sri Lanka at Jaipur, then the highest score by a wicket-keeper in ODIs. Appointed captain for the inaugural World Twenty20 in 2007, he led a young Indian side to the title, and succeeded Rahul Dravid and Anil Kumble as ODI and Test captain. Under his leadership India reached the No. 1 Test ranking in 2009 and won the 2011 World Cup on home soil, Dhoni sealing the final against Sri Lanka at Mumbai with a six — one of the most replayed shots in cricket history. He retired from Test cricket in December 2014, gave up the limited-overs captaincy in January 2017, and announced his international retirement on 15 August 2020. In 538 international matches he scored over 17,000 runs and effected more than 800 dismissals as wicket-keeper.

IPL career

Dhoni was the most expensive player in the first Indian Premier League auction in 2008, bought by the Chennai Super Kings, whom he has captained for most of their existence, winning five IPL titles (2010, 2011, 2018, 2021, 2023). After two seasons with Rising Pune Supergiant during Chennai's suspension, he returned in 2018. His finishing ability, calm under pressure, and trademark "helicopter shot" made him among the league's defining figures; Chennai fans honour him with the title Thala ("leader").

Honours

Dhoni has received India's highest sporting honour, the Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna (2008), along with the Padma Shri (2009) and Padma Bhushan (2018). He holds an honorary rank of lieutenant colonel in the Territorial Army and was inducted into the ICC Cricket Hall of Fame in 2025.
