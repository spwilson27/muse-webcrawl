Districts of Mozambique

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The provinces of Mozambique are divided into 154 districts. The districts are further divided into administrative posts (postos administrativos) and localities. The district is the principal tier of local administration below the province; district governments are headed by administrators appointed by the central government, though Mozambique has debated decentralisation reforms for decades. The capital, Maputo, has the status of a province and is divided into municipal districts.

Administration

Districts are the focus of Mozambique's development planning: district development plans and a decentralised district fund (introduced in the mid-2000s) channel resources to local priorities such as agriculture, water and small infrastructure. District administrators are appointed by the President, while municipal councils in cities and towns are elected. The total number of districts has grown over time as new districts have been created by subdivision — most recently a round of creations in the 2010s and 2020s that brought the total to 154.
