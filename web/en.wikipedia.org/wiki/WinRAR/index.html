WinRAR is a trialware file archiver utility for Windows, developed by Eugene Roshal of win.rar GmbH. First released in 1995, it creates and views archives in the proprietary RAR format and unpacks many other formats, including ZIP, 7z, TAR and ISO.

Features

WinRAR supports strong AES encryption, multi-volume archives, self-extracting archives, recovery records that can repair damaged archives, and integration with the Windows shell. Command-line versions (RAR) are available for macOS, Linux and FreeBSD, and a free variant is offered for Android.

Licensing and popularity

WinRAR is distributed as shareware with a 40-day trial; famously, the program continues to function after the trial expires, with only reminder prompts — a lenient policy widely credited for its enormous installed base, estimated in the hundreds of millions of users. Its "please purchase" reminder dialogues have become an internet cultural staple.
