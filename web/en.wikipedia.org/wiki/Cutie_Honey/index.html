Cutie Honey (Japanese: キューティーハニー) is a Japanese media franchise created by manga artist Go Nagai. It debuted simultaneously in 1973 as a manga in Weekly Shōnen Champion and as an anime television series. The heroine, Honey Kisaragi, is an android girl built by Professor Kisaragi who can transform into multiple forms — most famously the pink-haired swordswoman Cutie Honey — using the Atmospheric Element Condenser within her body, to battle the criminal organisation Panther Claw, led by Sister Jill and the demon Panther Zora, who murdered her creator.

Influence and adaptations

Cutie Honey is credited as a pioneering work of the magical girl genre, introducing a transforming heroine who actively fought enemies, and it helped popularise transformation sequences in anime. The franchise has been revived repeatedly, including the OVA New Cutie Honey (1994), the series Cutie Honey Flash (1997), Hideaki Anno's live-action film Cutie Honey (2004) and the companion anime Re: Cutie Honey, and Cutie Honey Universe (2018), produced for Go Nagai's 50th anniversary as an artist.
