The Club of Rome is a non-profit, informal organization of intellectuals and business leaders whose goal is to stimulate debate on global challenges. It was founded in 1968 at the Accademia dei Lincei in Rome, Italy, by Italian industrialist Aurelio Peccei and Scottish scientist Alexander King.

The Limits to Growth

The Club gained worldwide attention with its first report, The Limits to Growth (1972), written by Donella Meadows, Dennis Meadows, Jørgen Randers and William Behrens. Using the World3 computer model, the report simulated interactions between population, industrial production, food, resources and pollution, and concluded that continued exponential growth on a finite planet could lead to overshoot and decline during the 21st century. The book sold millions of copies and remains one of the best-selling environmental books of all time.

Later work

Subsequent reports addressed the global employment crisis, the digital age, and planetary stewardship. More recent publications include Earth for All (2022), proposing transformations in food, energy, poverty and inequality to keep humanity within planetary boundaries.
