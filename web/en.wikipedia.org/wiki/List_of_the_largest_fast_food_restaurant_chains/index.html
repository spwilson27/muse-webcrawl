List of the largest fast food restaurant chains

The following is a list of the largest fast food restaurant chains in the world, ranked by number of locations. Fast food chains typically operate through franchising, and several of the largest — including Subway and McDonald's — have more than 35,000 outlets worldwide. Counts vary by source and year as chains open and close outlets.

By revenue

By system-wide sales, McDonald's is the world's largest fast food chain, with annual system sales exceeding US$120 billion, followed by Starbucks and Chick-fil-A (which, despite far fewer stores and closing on Sundays, ranks among the top three in the United States by sales). Yum! Brands (KFC, Pizza Hut, Taco Bell) is the largest restaurant company by number of units under one corporate parent.
