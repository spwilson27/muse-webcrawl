The Addams Family is a fictional family created by American cartoonist Charles Addams. They are an eccentric, wealthy clan who delight in the macabre and are seemingly unaware or unconcerned that other people find them bizarre or frightening. The family first appeared as single-panel cartoons in The New Yorker beginning in 1938.

Characters

The household is headed by the passionate Gomez Addams and his serene, pale wife Morticia. Their children are the solemn Wednesday and the mischievous Pugsley. The family also includes Uncle Fester, Grandmama, the hulking butler Lurch, and Thing, a disembodied hand. Gomez and Morticia are famously devoted to one another, and the family — despite its ghoulish tastes — is depicted as close-knit and loving.

Adaptations

The family was popularised by the 1964–1966 television sitcom, whose theme song with its finger snaps became iconic. Later adaptations include the films The Addams Family (1991) and Addams Family Values (1993), a Broadway musical (2010), animated films (2019, 2021), and the Netflix series Wednesday (2022), which became one of the platform's most-watched shows.
