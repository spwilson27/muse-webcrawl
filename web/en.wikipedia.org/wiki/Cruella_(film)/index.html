Cruella (film)

Cruella is a 2021 American crime comedy film directed by Craig Gillespie with a screenplay by Dana Fox and Tony McNamara. Produced by Walt Disney Pictures, it is a live-action prequel and reboot of sorts to the One Hundred and One Dalmatians franchise, being based on the character Cruella de Vil, introduced in Dodie Smith's 1956 novel and Disney's 1961 animated film.

Plot

In 1970s London, young Estella — a clever, creative girl with distinctive black-and-white hair — dreams of becoming a fashion designer. After her mother is killed by Dalmatians belonging to the formidable Baroness von Hellman, Estella falls in with young thieves Jasper and Horace. A decade later, working incognito for the Baroness, Estella discovers the truth about her mother's death and reinvents herself as the flamboyant, vengeful designer persona "Cruella", waging a guerrilla fashion war against her employer — and eventually learning a secret about her own parentage.

Production and release

The film features an extensive soundtrack of 1960s–70s rock and pop, and costume design by Jenny Beavan, who won the Academy Award for Best Costume Design. Cruella premiered at the El Capitan Theatre in May 2021 and was released simultaneously in theaters and on Disney+ with Premier Access. It received generally positive reviews, particularly for the performances, costumes and production design. A sequel is in development, with Stone set to return.
