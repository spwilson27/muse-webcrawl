ItalDesign

Founded: 1968, Turin, Italy
Founders: Giorgetto Giugiaro, Aldo Mantovani
Headquarters: Moncalieri, Italy
Parent: Automobili Lamborghini (Volkswagen Group)

ItalDesign Giugiaro S.p.A., trading as ItalDesign, is an Italian design and engineering company founded in 1968 by Giorgetto Giugiaro and Aldo Mantovani. Based in Moncalieri near Turin, it provides styling, engineering and prototyping services, chiefly to the automotive industry.

Notable designs

Giugiaro's studio styled many production cars, including the Volkswagen Golf Mk1, Fiat Panda and Uno, Lotus Esprit, Lancia Delta, BMW M1, Maserati Bora, DeLorean DMC-12 and the Alfa Romeo Brera. Its concept cars, such as the 1988 Aztec and later Zerouno (2017), led to a small-series production arm, Italdesign Automobili Speciali.

Ownership

In 2010 Volkswagen Group, through Lamborghini, acquired a majority stake of 90.1%; the Giugiaro family sold its remaining shares in 2015, when Giorgetto and Fabrizio Giugiaro left the company.
