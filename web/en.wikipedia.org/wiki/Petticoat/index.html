Petticoat

A petticoat or underskirt is an article of clothing, a type of undergarment worn under a skirt or dress. Petticoats add fullness, shape, warmth, and modesty, and protect outer garments.

History

The word derives from Middle English pety cote, "small coat". From the 16th century onward, stiffened and quilted petticoats shaped fashionable silhouettes; in the mid-19th century they were supplemented and partly replaced by the crinoline and bustle. Decorative petticoats were sometimes meant to be glimpsed beneath raised skirts, trimmed with lace or embroidery.

Modern use

In the 20th century, slips and half-slips took over most everyday functions, while full, stiffened petticoats survive in bridal wear, square dancing, historical reenactment, and the 1950s-revival and Lolita fashions, where net and organza petticoats give skirts their bell shape.
