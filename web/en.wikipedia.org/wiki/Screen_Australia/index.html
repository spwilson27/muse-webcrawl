Screen Australia
Formed: 1 July 2008
Type: Australian Government agency
Purpose: Funding and support of Australian screen production

Screen Australia is the Australian Government's key funding body for the Australian screen production industry. It was formed on 1 July 2008 under the Screen Australia Act 2008 through the merger of the Australian Film Commission, Film Australia and the Film Finance Corporation Australia.

Functions

The agency provides development and production funding for feature films, television drama, documentaries and online content; supports Indigenous screen storytelling through its Indigenous Department; administers parts of the Producer Offset tax incentive; and promotes Australian screen culture through research, marketing and industry development programs.
