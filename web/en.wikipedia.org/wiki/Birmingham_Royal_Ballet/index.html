Birmingham Royal Ballet

Birmingham Royal Ballet in performance at its home theatre, the Birmingham Hippodrome

Birmingham Royal Ballet (BRB) is one of the United Kingdom's leading classical ballet companies, based at the Birmingham Hippodrome in Birmingham, England. The company is internationally recognised for its classical repertory, its productions of the nineteenth-century classics, and the ballets created by its founder choreographer Frederick Ashton and founding artistic director Peter Wright.

History

The company's origins lie in the Sadler's Wells Ballet, founded in London in association with Ninette de Valois's Vic-Wells Ballet. After the main company moved to the Royal Opera House in 1946 and became The Royal Ballet, the Sadler's Wells company continued as a touring ensemble, later known as the Sadler's Wells Royal Ballet. In 1990 the company relocated to Birmingham, taking its present name and becoming resident at the Birmingham Hippodrome, a move championed by artistic director Peter Wright.

Repertory

BRB performs full-length classics including Swan Lake, The Sleeping Beauty, The Nutcracker, Giselle, Coppélia and Romeo and Juliet. Peter Wright's production of The Nutcracker, created for the company's Birmingham opening in 1990, remains a signature work. The repertory also includes ballets by Ashton, Kenneth MacMillan, George Balanchine and David Bintley, who served as artistic director from 1995 to 2019.

Directors and touring

Cuban dancer Carlos Acosta became artistic director in 2020. The company tours extensively in the UK and internationally, performs with its own orchestra, the Royal Ballet Sinfonia, and runs education and outreach programmes across the West Midlands. Its associated school is Elmhurst Ballet School.
