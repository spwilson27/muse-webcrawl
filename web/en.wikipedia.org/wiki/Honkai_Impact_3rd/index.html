Honkai Impact 3rd

Honkai Impact 3rd is a free-to-play 3D action role-playing game developed and published by miHoYo (HoYoverse). It was released for mobile devices in China in October 2016 and globally in 2017–2018, with later ports to PC and a Steam release. Set in a world beset by the Honkai, a corrupting force that produces monsters, zombies, and godlike beings called Herrschers, the game follows the Valkyries of the organization Schicksal and the crew of the battleship Hyperion, led in the story by the protagonist Kiana Kaslana.

Gameplay

Players control a team of three Valkyries in real-time hack-and-slash combat, freely switching between characters whose types form a rock-paper-scissors system (Mecha, Biologic, Psychic, later joined by Imaginary and Quantum). Battles emphasize dodging to trigger time-slow effects, QTE switch-in attacks, and ultimate skills. Characters, weapons, and "Stigmata" equipment sets are obtained largely through a gacha system. Alongside the main story chapters, the game offers open-world areas, roguelike modes such as Elysian Realm, co-op raids, and frequent limited-time events.

Story and media

The game's narrative, spanning dozens of chapters, an open-world sequel storyline ("A Post-Honkai Odyssey"), and the "Part 2" relaunch in 2024 set on Mars, is supplemented by official manga, visual novels, and animated shorts—including widely praised cinematics such as "Final Lesson." Its character songs and concerts have also been popular. Honkai Impact 3rd established miHoYo's "Honkai" setting, later revisited in Honkai: Star Rail (2023), and its success funded the development lessons that led to Genshin Impact.

Reception

The game was praised for its console-quality graphics on mobile, fluid combat, and emotionally driven storytelling, though criticized for gacha monetization and a steep learning curve for new players due to years of accumulated systems. It has grossed well over a billion dollars worldwide and maintains an active global community.
