Gumdrop

Gumdrops are a type of gummy candy, typically brightly coloured, cone- or dome-shaped pieces of gelatin (or pectin) confectionery coated in granulated sugar. They are flavoured with fruit and spice flavours and are a traditional ingredient in baking and cake decoration, as well as a popular sweet in their own right, especially around Christmas, where they are used to decorate gingerbread houses.

Spice-flavoured gumdrops — in flavours such as clove, anise, allspice and spearmint — are sold as "spice drops". The candy's name is also used generically for similar soft gum confections. The term "gumdrop" gained a second life in the space age: the conical shape of the Apollo command module led to its nickname, and NASA's early spacesuit pressure garment components were also compared to gumdrops in popular reporting.
