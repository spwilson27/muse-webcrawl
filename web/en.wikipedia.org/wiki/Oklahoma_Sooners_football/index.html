Oklahoma Sooners football

Stadium: Gaylord Family Oklahoma Memorial Stadium
Location: Norman, Oklahoma
Conference: Southeastern Conference (SEC)
Colors: Crimson and cream

The Oklahoma Sooners football team represents the University of Oklahoma in college football in the NCAA Division I Football Bowl Subdivision. The Sooners play their home games at Gaylord Family Oklahoma Memorial Stadium in Norman, Oklahoma. One of the most storied programs in college football history, Oklahoma claims seven national championships and has produced numerous Heisman Trophy winners.

History

The program began in 1895. Under coach Bennie Owen in the early twentieth century the Sooners became a regional power. Bud Wilkinson, who coached from 1947 to 1963, built a dynasty that included three national championships and a record 47-game winning streak that still stands as the longest in major college football. Barry Switzer coached from 1973 to 1988, winning three more national titles with the wishbone offense. Bob Stoops restored the program in the 2000s, winning the 2000 national championship.

Rivalries

Oklahoma's principal rivals include the Texas Longhorns, met annually in the Red River Rivalry game in Dallas, and the Oklahoma State Cowboys in the Bedlam Series. The Sooners also have a historic rivalry with Nebraska.

Conference history

The Sooners were long-time members of the Big 12 Conference (and its predecessors). Oklahoma and Texas moved to the Southeastern Conference (SEC) effective in 2024.

Honors

Oklahoma Heisman Trophy winners include Billy Vessels, Steve Owens, Billy Sims, Jason White, Sam Bradford, Baker Mayfield and Kyler Murray. The program has won dozens of conference championships and has appeared in numerous major bowl games.
