Casualty (TV series)

Casualty is a British medical drama television series first broadcast on BBC One on 6 September 1986. Set in the accident and emergency department of the fictional Holby City Hospital, it is the longest-running primetime medical drama series in the world.

Overview

Created by Jeremy Brock and Paul Unwin partly in response to pressures on the National Health Service, Casualty follows the professional and personal lives of emergency department staff — consultants, nurses, paramedics and receptionists — alongside self-contained stories of patients injured in accidents, often shown in a short opening sequence.

Production and legacy

The series was filmed in Bristol for its first 25 years before production moved to the Roath Lock studios in Cardiff in 2011. It has launched or featured many well-known British actors and spun off the sister series Holby City (1999–2022) and HolbyBlue. Long-serving characters such as nurse Charlie Fairhead, played by Derek Thompson from the first episode until 2024, became fixtures of British television.
