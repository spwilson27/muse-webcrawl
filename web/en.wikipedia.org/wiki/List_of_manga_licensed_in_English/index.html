List of manga licensed in English

English-language manga
Major licensors include Viz Media, Kodansha USA, Yen Press and Seven Seas.

This is a list of notable manga series that have been licensed for an official English-language release, principally in North America and the United Kingdom. English-language manga publishing expanded rapidly in the 2000s, led by publishers such as Viz Media, Tokyopop (until 2011), Del Rey Manga, Kodansha USA, Yen Press, Dark Horse Manga and Seven Seas Entertainment.
