Dulquer Salmaan (born 28 July 1983) is an Indian actor, playback singer and film producer who predominantly works in Malayalam films, in addition to Tamil, Telugu and Hindi films. The son of veteran actor Mammootty, he is a recipient of several awards including five Filmfare Awards South and a Kerala State Film Award.

Career

Salmaan made his acting debut with the Malayalam crime film Second Show (2012) and rose to prominence with Ustad Hotel (2012). He won the Kerala State Film Award for Best Actor for his performance in Charlie (2015). His notable Malayalam films include Bangalore Days (2014), Kali (2016), Kammatipaadam (2016), Parava (2017) and Kurup (2021), in which he played the fugitive criminal Sukumara Kurup.

In Tamil cinema he starred in Vaayai Moodi Pesavum (2014), Mani Ratnam's O Kadhal Kanmani (2015), and Hey Sinamika (2022). His Telugu film Mahanati (2018), in which he portrayed Gemini Ganesan, was a major success, and Sita Ramam (2022) brought him wide acclaim across India. His Hindi films include Karwaan (2018) and The Zoya Factor (2019). He also owns the production company Wayfarer Films and ventures in the automobile and restaurant businesses.
