The glans penis (commonly called the head of the penis) is the rounded, sensitive bulbous structure at the distal end of the human penis, formed by the expansion of the corpus spongiosum. The urethra opens at its tip through the external urethral orifice (meatus).

Anatomy

The glans is rimmed by the corona and joined to the shaft at the neck; in uncircumcised males it is normally covered, wholly or partly, by the foreskin (prepuce), to which it is attached ventrally by the frenulum. It is richly supplied with sensory nerve endings.

Development

The glans develops from the genital tubercle and is homologous to the clitoral glans in females.
