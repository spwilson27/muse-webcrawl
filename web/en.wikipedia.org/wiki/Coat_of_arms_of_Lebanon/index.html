Coat of arms of Lebanon

The coat of arms of Lebanon consists of a red shield with a white diagonal band (bend sinister) bearing a green cedar tree, the national symbol of Lebanon. The design closely mirrors the national flag, which shows a cedar on a white stripe between two horizontal red stripes.

Symbolism

The cedar of Lebanon is an ancient symbol of the country, mentioned in the Bible and associated with holiness, eternity and peace. Red is traditionally said to represent sacrifice and white purity and peace.
