Konark Sun Temple

Konark Sun Temple
Location: Konark, Odisha, India
Built: c. 1250 CE
Architectural style: Kalinga

Konark Sun Temple is a 13th-century CE Hindu Sun temple at Konark about 35 kilometres northeast of Puri city on the coastline in Puri district, Odisha, India. The temple is attributed to king Narasimhadeva I of the Eastern Ganga dynasty about 1250 CE.

Design

Dedicated to the Hindu Sun god Surya, the temple complex was designed in the shape of a gigantic chariot with elaborately carved stone wheels, pillars and walls. A major part of the structure is now in ruins.

Recognition

The temple is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and is one of the most famous Brahman sanctuaries in India.
