Parachute cord

Parachute cord (also paracord or 550 cord when referring to type III paracord) is a lightweight nylon kernmantle rope originally used in the suspension lines of parachutes. It is now used as a general-purpose utility cord by military personnel and civilians alike, and is a staple of survival kits.

Construction

Paracord consists of an outer woven nylon sheath (the mantle) surrounding several two- or three-ply inner yarns (the core or kern). Type III paracord, the most common military type, has a minimum breaking strength of 550 pounds (about 2,450 newtons), hence the nickname "550 cord".

Uses

Beyond parachutes, paracord is used for lanyards, shoelaces, tent guy-lines, tourniquets, and improvised repairs. The inner strands can be removed and used as fishing line or sewing thread. Decorative uses include woven bracelets, belts, and key fobs.
