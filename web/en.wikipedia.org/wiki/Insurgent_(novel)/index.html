Insurgent (novel)

Insurgent
Author: Veronica Roth
Published: May 1, 2012
Genre: Dystopian, young adult
Preceded by: Divergent
Followed by: Allegiant

Insurgent is a 2012 dystopian science fiction novel by American author Veronica Roth, the second book in the Divergent trilogy, following Divergent (2011) and preceding Allegiant (2013).

The novel continues the story of Beatrice "Tris" Prior in a post-apocalyptic Chicago divided into factions based on personality virtues. As war breaks out between the factions after the Erudite simulation attack, Tris grapples with grief, guilt, and her identity as a Divergent while uncovering secrets about her society.

A film adaptation, The Divergent Series: Insurgent, was released in March 2015.
