Will Ferrell
Born: 16 July 1967, Irvine, California, U.S.
Occupation: Actor, comedian, producer, writer
Years active: 1991–present

John William Ferrell (born 16 July 1967) is an American actor, comedian, producer, and writer. He rose to fame as a cast member of the NBC sketch comedy series Saturday Night Live (1995–2002), where he was one of the show's breakout performers, known for impressions of George W. Bush and Alex Trebek and characters such as cheerleader Craig Buchanan.

Film career

Ferrell became a leading comic film star of the 2000s with Old School (2003), Elf (2003), Anchorman: The Legend of Ron Burgundy (2004), Talladega Nights (2006), Blades of Glory (2007), Step Brothers (2008), and The Other Guys (2010). He co-founded the production company Gary Sanchez Productions with Adam McKay and the comedy website Funny or Die. He has also taken acclaimed dramatic and voice roles, including in Stranger than Fiction (2006), The Lego Movie (2014), and Barbie (2023).

Other ventures

Ferrell is a part-owner of Los Angeles FC of Major League Soccer and is known for charity work, including participation in marathons and the cancer charity Schwaggle/Cancer for College.
