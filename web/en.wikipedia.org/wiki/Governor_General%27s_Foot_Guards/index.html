The Governor General's Foot Guards (GGFG) is a Canadian Army Primary Reserve infantry regiment based in Ottawa, Ontario. Raised in 1872, it is Canada's senior reserve infantry regiment and one of three household regiments in Canada. Together with the Canadian Grenadier Guards, it supplies the soldiers of the Ceremonial Guard that mounts the Changing of the Guard on Parliament Hill and guards Rideau Hall each summer.

History

The regiment was organised as the 1st Battalion, Governor General's Foot Guards in 1872. Its members served in the North-West Rebellion, the Boer War, and both World Wars; during the Second World War the regiment mobilised as an armoured unit, the 21st Canadian Armoured Regiment (GGFG), landing in Normandy in 1944. Since 1945, members have served on operations in Afghanistan and elsewhere, and the regiment provides aid during domestic emergencies.

Role and dress

The Foot Guards wear the scarlet tunic and bearskin cap modelled on the British Foot Guards, with the plume of the regiment worn on the left side of the bearskin. Their motto is Civitas et Princeps cura nostra ("Our country and ruler are our concern").
