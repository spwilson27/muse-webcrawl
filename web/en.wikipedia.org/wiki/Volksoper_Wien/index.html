Volksoper Wien

The Volksoper Wien (Vienna People's Opera) is an opera house in Vienna, Austria, and the city's second major opera company after the Vienna State Opera. Located on the Währinger Straße in the 9th district, it presents operetta, opera, musicals, and ballet, all performed in German.

History

The theatre opened in 1898 as the Kaiser-Jubiläums-Stadttheater, built to mark the golden jubilee of Emperor Franz Joseph I. It initially staged spoken drama; from 1903 it turned to opera and operetta, adopting the name Volksoper. The house premiered works such as Tosca's Vienna premiere and championed Viennese operetta by Lehár, Kálmán, and Johann Strauss II.

Today

The Volksoper seats about 1,300 with standing room for several hundred more and gives roughly 300 performances a year. Its repertoire emphasizes accessible productions in German translation, classic operetta, family performances, and contemporary musical theatre.
