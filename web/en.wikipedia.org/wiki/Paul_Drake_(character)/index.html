Paul Drake (character)

Paul Drake is a fictional private detective in the Perry Mason novels and adaptations created by Erle Stanley Gardner. He runs the Drake Detective Agency and is the trusted investigator of defence attorney Perry Mason.

Created byErle Stanley Gardner
Portrayed byWilliam Hopper (TV series)
OccupationPrivate investigator

Character

Drake is tall, easy-going and wisecracking, fond of the greeting "Hi, beautiful" addressed to Mason's secretary Della Street. While Mason argues in court, Drake gathers evidence, tails suspects and locates witnesses, often at personal risk.

In adaptations

In the CBS television series (1957–1966), Drake was played by William Hopper. In the HBO series Perry Mason (2020–2023), a reimagined Paul Drake — a Black patrolman turned investigator in 1930s Los Angeles — was played by Chris Chalk.
