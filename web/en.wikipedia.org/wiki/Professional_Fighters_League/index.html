Professional Fighters League

The Professional Fighters League (PFL) is an American mixed martial arts promotion founded in 2017 by Donn Davis and launched in 2018 from the assets of the former World Series of Fighting. It is distinguished from other promotions by its season format: fighters compete in a regular season, playoffs and championship, modelled on mainstream team sports leagues, with $1 million prizes for each weight-class champion.

Format

In the original format, fighters earn points in two regular-season bouts — three for a win plus bonus points for finishes by round — with the top fighters advancing to single-elimination playoffs and a championship event, traditionally held on New Year's Eve. Weight classes have included heavyweight, light heavyweight, welterweight, lightweight, featherweight and women's divisions. Notable champions include Kayla Harrison, Lance Palmer, Ray Cooper III and Larissa Pacheco.

Growth

The PFL has raised substantial venture funding, introduced a "SmartCage" with real-time fighter analytics, signed crossover attractions such as boxer Claressa Shields and former UFC champion Francis Ngannou, and in 2023 acquired rival promotion Bellator MMA. It also operates regional leagues including PFL Europe and PFL MENA.
