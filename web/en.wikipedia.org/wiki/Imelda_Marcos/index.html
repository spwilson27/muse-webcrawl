Imelda Marcos

Imelda Romualdez Marcos (born 2 July 1929) is a Filipino politician and former first lady who was married to Ferdinand Marcos, the 10th president of the Philippines. She was First Lady from 1965 to 1986.

First Lady

Known for her 'edifice complex' building projects such as the Cultural Center of the Philippines, she also served as Governor of Metro Manila and Minister of Human Settlements during martial law. She became notorious for extravagance, symbolised by a collection of about 1,000–3,000 pairs of shoes found after the family fled in 1986.

Exile and return

After the People Power Revolution she lived in exile in Hawaii, faced numerous corruption cases, returned to the Philippines in 1991, and later served three terms in the House of Representatives (2010–2019). In 2018 she was convicted of graft, a verdict under appeal. The Marcos family returned to power when her son became president in 2022.
