Beartooth is an American rock band from Columbus, Ohio, formed in 2012 by vocalist and multi-instrumentalist Caleb Shomo, formerly of Attack Attack!. The band is known for blending metalcore and hardcore punk with melodic hard rock, and for lyrics addressing Shomo's struggles with depression and anxiety.

History

Shomo began writing Beartooth material while still in Attack Attack!, initially as a side project. After leaving that band, he signed Beartooth to Red Bull Records and released the EP Sick in 2013. The debut studio album Disgusting followed in June 2014 and was recorded almost entirely by Shomo himself.

Later albums

The second album, Aggressive (2016), debuted in the US top 30. Disease (2018) continued the band's introspective themes, and Below (2021) adopted a heavier, darker sound written during lockdown. The fifth album, The Surface (2023), marked a shift toward more hopeful subject matter and produced the rock radio hits "Might Love Myself" and "I Was Alive". Beartooth has toured extensively as a headliner and appeared at major rock and metal festivals worldwide.
