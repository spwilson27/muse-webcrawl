Gainbridge Fieldhouse

Location125 South Pennsylvania Street, Indianapolis, Indiana
Opened6 November 1999
Capacity17,923 (basketball)
TenantsIndiana Pacers (NBA); Indiana Fever (WNBA)
Former namesConseco Fieldhouse; Bankers Life Fieldhouse

Gainbridge Fieldhouse is a multi-purpose arena in downtown Indianapolis, Indiana, serving as the home of the Indiana Pacers of the NBA and the Indiana Fever of the WNBA. Opened in 1999 as Conseco Fieldhouse, it replaced Market Square Arena.

Design

Designed by Ellerbe Becket, the fieldhouse deliberately evokes Indiana's historic high-school and college gymnasiums, with a retro fieldhouse aesthetic, extensive use of brick and limestone, and memorabilia celebrating Indiana basketball. It is frequently praised as one of the finest arenas in the NBA.

Events

Beyond basketball, the venue has hosted the Big Ten basketball tournaments, NCAA tournament games, the 2012 NCAA Women's Final Four, professional wrestling, and concerts. A major renovation programme in the early 2020s modernised concourses and added an outdoor plaza.
