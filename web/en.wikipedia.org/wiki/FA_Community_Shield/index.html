The FA Community Shield (formerly the FA Charity Shield) is an annual English football match organised by the Football Association. It is contested at Wembley Stadium between the champions of the previous Premier League season and the holders of the FA Cup. If the same team won both, the Premier League runners-up take part.

History

The match descends from the Sheriff of London Charity Shield and was first played in its FA form in 1908, when Manchester United faced Queens Park Rangers. It traditionally opens the English football season in early August. Proceeds were historically distributed to charities; following a Charity Commission review, the match was rebranded as the Community Shield in 2002.

Status

The Shield is generally regarded as a prestigious friendly or "glorified friendly" rather than a major trophy, though it is officially a competitive fixture. Matches drawn after 90 minutes are decided by a penalty shoot-out, with no extra time.

Records

Manchester United have won the Shield outright more times than any other club. The highest-scoring match was the 1911 edition, in which Manchester United beat Swindon Town 8–4.
