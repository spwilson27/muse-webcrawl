Leigh and Atherton is a constituency in Greater Manchester represented in the House of Commons of the United Kingdom. It was created by the 2023 periodic review of Westminster constituencies and was first contested at the 2024 general election. The seat centres on the towns of Leigh and Atherton in the Metropolitan Borough of Wigan.

Boundaries

The constituency comprises the Wigan borough wards of Leigh North, Leigh South, Leigh West, Atherton North, Atherton South and Lidderdale, Atherleigh, Tyldesley and Mosley Common, and Astley, together with Golborne and Lowton West from the former Leigh seat. It succeeds the abolished Leigh constituency, which had existed since 1885 and had been represented for many years by Andy Burnham before his election as Mayor of Greater Manchester in 2017.

History and elections

The predecessor Leigh seat was a Labour stronghold for most of the 20th century, but was won by the Conservatives in 2019 amid a broader realignment of "red wall" seats. At the 2024 general election, the new Leigh and Atherton seat was won by Jo Platt of the Labour Party, who had previously represented Leigh from 2017 to 2019, defeating the incumbent Conservative MP. Platt's victory restored the area to Labour representation under the revised boundaries.
