Skydeck commonly refers to the observation deck on the 103rd floor of the Willis Tower (formerly Sears Tower) in Chicago, Illinois. Officially branded Skydeck Chicago, it is the highest observation deck in the United States, at 1,353 feet (412 m) above the ground, and one of Chicago's most popular tourist attractions.

History

The observation deck opened with the tower in 1974. In 2009 the deck was renovated and relaunched with The Ledge, a series of retractable glass boxes extending 4.3 feet (1.3 m) from the facade, allowing visitors to stand on glass floors directly above Wacker Drive. A major renovation completed in 2021 added a two-story interactive museum experience on the lower levels covering Chicago history and culture.

Features

On clear days visibility can reach up to 50 miles across four states (Illinois, Indiana, Wisconsin, and Michigan). Attractions include high-speed elevators with video presentations, interactive exhibits, and the glass Ledge boxes, each built from three layers of half-inch glass.

Other uses

"Skydeck" is also used generically for rooftop observation areas and appears in other contexts, including as the former name of an observation area at other towers; this article concerns the Willis Tower attraction.
