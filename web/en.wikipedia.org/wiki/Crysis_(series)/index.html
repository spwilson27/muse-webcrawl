Crysis (series)

Crysis is a series of first-person shooter video games developed by Crytek and published by Electronic Arts. The series centres on soldiers equipped with the Nanosuit, an advanced powered exoskeleton granting enhanced strength, speed, armour and cloaking abilities, in conflicts involving North Korean forces and the alien Ceph.

Games

The first game, Crysis (2007), was set on the fictional Lingshan Islands and was famed for its demanding graphics, becoming a benchmark for PC hardware. It was followed by the expansion Crysis Warhead (2008), the console-oriented sequel Crysis 2 (2011), set in a ruined New York City, and Crysis 3 (2013), set in an overgrown, domed New York. A remastered trilogy was released in 2021, and a fourth main instalment, Crysis 4, was announced in 2022.

Gameplay and legacy

The series is known for sandbox-style combat in open environments, destructible scenery, and the tactical flexibility conferred by the Nanosuit's modes. The original game's system requirements gave rise to the enduring meme "But can it run Crysis?" as a test of gaming hardware. See also Crysis 2 and Crytek.
