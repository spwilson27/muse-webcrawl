Mills Mansion

Mills Mansion, formally the Mills Mansion State Historic Site and now part of Staatsburgh State Historic Site in Staatsburg, Dutchess County, New York, is a Beaux-Arts country house on the Hudson River built for financier Ogden Mills and his wife Ruth Livingston Mills.

History

The estate originated with a Greek Revival house of the 1830s owned by the Livingston family. In 1895–1896 the architect Stanford White of McKim, Mead & White remodelled and greatly enlarged it into a 79-room mansion with lavish Gilded Age interiors. The Mills family donated the house and grounds to New York State in 1938.

Today

The mansion is preserved as a museum illustrating the lifestyle of Hudson Valley landed gentry, with period furnishings, and the surrounding grounds form part of Mills Memorial State Park along the Hudson.
