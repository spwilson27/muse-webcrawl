Barasoain Church

Barasoain Church (formally the Our Lady of Mount Carmel Parish) is a Roman Catholic church in Malolos, Bulacan, Philippines. It is one of the most historically significant churches in the country, having hosted the First Philippine Congress in 1898, which ratified the Malolos Constitution and proclaimed the First Philippine Republic.

LocationMalolos, Bulacan, Philippines
DenominationRoman Catholic
Founded1885 (current stone church)
Historic eventMalolos Congress, 1898

History

Founded by Augustinian missionaries, the parish went through several earlier structures destroyed by fire and earthquake. The present stone church was completed in the late 19th century. From September 1898 it served as the seat of the revolutionary Congress led by Emilio Aguinaldo, and the church convent briefly housed government offices.

Architecture

The church displays a restrained Baroque style with a three-storey bell tower. A museum in the convent, run by the National Historical Commission, exhibits artefacts of the First Republic.

Recognition

The church appears on Philippine currency and stamps and was declared a National Landmark; it remains an active parish and a site of national commemoration.
