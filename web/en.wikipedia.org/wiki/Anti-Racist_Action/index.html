Anti-Racist Action (ARA) is a decentralised network of anti-fascist and anti-racist activists, founded in Minneapolis, Minnesota, in 1987 by members of the anti-racist skinhead scene.

History and activity

ARA grew out of the Baldies, a Minneapolis anti-racist skinhead crew, and spread into a network of chapters across North America. Chapters combined community organising, education and direct confrontation of neo-Nazi and white supremacist groups, monitoring far-right activity and opposing racist events.

Principles

The network operated on shared points of unity, including opposing racism and fascism, supporting direct action, and refusing reliance on police or courts to stop fascist organising.
