MileagePlus

MileagePlus is the frequent-flyer program of United Airlines. Launched as Mileage Plus in 1981, a week after American Airlines' pioneering AAdvantage program, it became one of the world's largest airline loyalty programs, with members earning miles for flights on United and its Star Alliance partners, redeemable for award travel, upgrades, and other rewards.

History

United created the program in May 1981 in response to AAdvantage. Following United's merger with Continental Airlines, the two carriers combined their programs in 2012 under the MileagePlus name (using Continental OnePass's systems and account structure). In 2020 United raised $6.8 billion in financing backed by the program, revealing that MileagePlus had been appraised at roughly $21.9 billion — a striking indication of the economics of modern loyalty programs.

Earning and redemption

Since 2015, miles for United flights have been earned based on ticket price rather than distance flown (base members earning 5 miles per dollar, with multipliers for elite members). Award pricing moved to a fully dynamic model in 2019, removing published award charts. Non-flight earning comes through co-branded Chase credit cards, hotel and car partners, shopping portals, and dining programs.

Premier status

Elite tiers — Premier Silver, Gold, Platinum, and 1K — are earned through Premier Qualifying Flights and Points (PQF/PQP), and confer upgrades, bonus miles, lounge access (on international itineraries), extra baggage, and priority services. The invitation-only Global Services tier sits above 1K for United's highest-value customers.
