Ilex

Ilex is a genus of over 570 species of flowering plants in the family Aquifoliaceae, commonly known as hollies. It is one of the most widely distributed woody plant genera, found in temperate and tropical regions worldwide.

Description

Species range from small shrubs to trees. Many are evergreen with glossy, often spiny leaves and small white flowers; the fruit is a small berry-like drupe, usually red, eaten by birds but toxic to humans. Hollies are dioecious — male and female flowers grow on separate plants.

Uses

Several species are ornamentals associated with Christmas decoration, notably European holly (Ilex aquifolium) and American holly (I. opaca). Yerba mate (I. paraguariensis) is brewed as a caffeinated drink in South America.
