The Climatic Research Unit (CRU) is a research centre at the University of East Anglia in Norwich, England, founded in 1972. It is one of the leading institutions studying natural and anthropogenic climate change.

Work

The CRU produces widely used datasets, including the CRUTEM land temperature record and, with the Met Office Hadley Centre, the HadCRUT global temperature series. Its researchers also work on dendroclimatology, reconstructing past climates from tree rings.

History

The unit was founded by climatologist Hubert Lamb, its first director, who had argued for the importance of historical climate records. In 2009 the unit was at the centre of controversy after emails were stolen and published; multiple independent inquiries subsequently exonerated the scientists of wrongdoing.
