Energy Community

The Energy Community, also referred to in the past as the Energy Community of South East Europe, is an international organisation established between the European Union (EU) and a number of third countries in order to extend the EU internal energy market to Southeast Europe and beyond. It was founded by the Treaty establishing the Energy Community, signed in Athens in October 2005 and entering into force on 1 July 2006.

Objectives

The aim of the Energy Community is to create an integrated pan-European energy market by extending the EU's internal energy market rules and principles to countries in Southeast Europe, the Black Sea region and beyond, on the basis of a legally binding framework. Contracting parties commit to implementing relevant EU energy legislation (the acquis communautaire on energy) covering electricity, gas, environment, competition, renewables, energy efficiency, oil and statistics.

Members

The contracting parties include Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo, North Macedonia, Georgia, Moldova, Montenegro, Serbia and Ukraine, together with the European Union. Several other countries participate as observers. The organisation's Secretariat is headquartered in Vienna, Austria.

Institutions

The Energy Community's institutions include the Ministerial Council, the Permanent High Level Group, the Energy Community Regulatory Board, and technical fora, supported by the Secretariat, which monitors implementation of the treaty obligations in the contracting parties.
