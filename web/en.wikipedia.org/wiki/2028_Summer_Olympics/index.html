The 2028 Summer Olympics, officially the Games of the XXXIV Olympiad and commonly known as Los Angeles 2028 or LA28, are a forthcoming international multi-sport event scheduled to take place from 14 to 30 July 2028 in Los Angeles, California, United States.

Host selection

Los Angeles was awarded the Games on 13 September 2017 at the 131st IOC Session in Lima, Peru, as part of an unusual double award in which Paris received the 2024 Games. Los Angeles had previously hosted the Summer Olympics in 1932 and 1984, and will become the third city, after London and Paris, to host three Summer Games.

Venues

The LA28 plan relies almost entirely on existing or temporary venues. The opening ceremony is planned to be shared between the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum and SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, and swimming is scheduled for SoFi Stadium — the largest swimming venue in Olympic history. Other venues include the Crypto.com Arena, the Rose Bowl, and Long Beach for rowing, canoe sprint and sailing-adjacent events.

Sports programme

Five sports were added by the organising committee: baseball/softball, cricket, flag football, lacrosse and squash. Cricket returns for the first time since 1900, while flag football and squash make their Olympic debuts.

Budget and legacy

The organising committee's budget has been estimated at around $6.9 billion, funded privately through sponsorships, broadcast rights and ticket sales. The city agreed to act as a financial backstop. No new permanent venues are planned, in line with the IOC's Agenda 2020 sustainability goals.
