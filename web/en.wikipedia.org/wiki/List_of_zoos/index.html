List of zoos

This is a list of notable zoos and zoological gardens around the world. The oldest zoo in continuous operation is the Tiergarten Schönbrunn in Vienna, founded in 1752; the oldest scientific zoo is London Zoo, opened to scientists in 1828 and to the public in 1847.

Selected notable zoos by region

Europe

  • London Zoo (United Kingdom, 1828)
  • Berlin Zoological Garden (Germany, 1844) — among the largest species collections in the world
  • Tiergarten Schönbrunn (Vienna, Austria, 1752)
  • Jardin des Plantes / Ménagerie (Paris, France, 1794)
  • Artis (Amsterdam, Netherlands, 1838)
  • Prague Zoo (Czech Republic, 1931)
  • Chester Zoo (United Kingdom, 1931)

North America

  • San Diego Zoo (California, 1916)
  • Bronx Zoo (New York, 1899)
  • Smithsonian National Zoological Park (Washington, D.C., 1889)
  • Philadelphia Zoo (chartered 1859, opened 1874)
  • Toronto Zoo (Canada, 1974)
  • Omaha's Henry Doorly Zoo and Aquarium (Nebraska, 1894)

Asia and Oceania

  • Singapore Zoo (1973)
  • Ueno Zoo (Tokyo, Japan, 1882)
  • Beijing Zoo (China, 1906)
  • Taronga Zoo (Sydney, Australia, 1916)
  • Melbourne Zoo (Australia, 1862)

Africa and South America

  • Johannesburg Zoo (South Africa, 1904)
  • Giza Zoo (Egypt, 1891)
  • Buenos Aires Zoo (Argentina, 1888; now an ecopark)
  • São Paulo Zoo (Brazil, 1958)
