Mogadishu

Mogadishu, locally known as Xamar or Hamar, is the capital and most populous city of Somalia, with a population estimated at 2.5 million or more. It is located in the Banaadir region on the Indian Ocean coast of the Horn of Africa.

Founded by Arab and Persian traders by the 10th century, Mogadishu was a great Swahili-coast trading city visited by Ibn Battuta and Zheng He, later an Italian colonial capital (Mogadiscio). It was known as the 'White Pearl of the Indian Ocean' before civil war from 1991 reduced much of it to ruins — including the 1993 Battle of Mogadishu ('Black Hawk Down'). Despite repeated al-Shabaab attacks (notably the October 2017 truck bombing that killed more than 500), the city has rebuilt substantially with Turkish and Gulf investment, reopening embassies, hotels and its port and airport.
