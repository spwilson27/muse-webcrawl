Pentane

Pentane is an organic compound with the formula C5H12 — an alkane with five carbon atoms. The term usually refers to the straight-chain isomer n-pentane; there are three structural isomers: n-pentane, isopentane (methylbutane), and neopentane (dimethylpropane).

Properties

n-Pentane is a colorless, highly flammable liquid with a boiling point of about 36.1 °C and a density of 0.626 g/mL. It is one of the most volatile liquid alkanes at room temperature and is nearly insoluble in water but mixes with most organic solvents.

Uses

Pentanes are components of some fuels and are used as laboratory solvents, in liquid chromatography, and as blowing agents in the production of polystyrene foam — a use that has been scrutinized because pentane is a volatile organic compound contributing to photochemical smog. n-Pentane is also used as the working fluid in some geothermal binary-cycle power plants.
