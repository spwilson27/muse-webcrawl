Recycling codes

The ASTM International Resin Identification Coding System, often abbreviated RIC, is a set of symbols appearing on plastic products that identify the plastic resin out of which the product is made. It was developed in 1988 by the Society of the Plastics Industry (now the Plastics Industry Association) in the United States.

Codes

The system comprises seven codes: 1 PET (polyethylene terephthalate), 2 HDPE (high-density polyethylene), 3 PVC (polyvinyl chloride), 4 LDPE (low-density polyethylene), 5 PP (polypropylene), 6 PS (polystyrene), and 7 other plastics.

Use and criticism

The codes help recycling facilities sort plastics, but the numbers do not indicate whether an item is recyclable in a given locality. Because the symbol resembles the universal recycling symbol, consumers often misinterpret it, which has led some jurisdictions to require changes to the labelling.
