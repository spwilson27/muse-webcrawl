Horton Hears a Who! (also known as Dr. Seuss' Horton Hears a Who!) is a 2008 American animated adventure comedy film produced by Blue Sky Studios and distributed by 20th Century Fox. The film is the fourth screen adaptation of the 1954 book of the same name by Dr. Seuss, following the 1970 television special, and the first fully computer-animated feature film adaptation of a Dr. Seuss work. It was directed by Jimmy Hayward and Steve Martino from a screenplay by Cinco Paul and Ken Daurio, and stars the voices of Jim Carrey and Steve Carell as Horton the Elephant and Mayor Ned McDodd, respectively.

Plot

In the Jungle of Nool, Horton the Elephant hears a faint cry coming from a speck of dust floating in the air. The speck turns out to be the microscopic city of Whoville, home to the Whos, led by Mayor Ned McDodd. Horton vows to protect the Whos, declaring that "a person's a person, no matter how small." The other animals of the jungle, led by the Sour Kangaroo, ridicule Horton and believe he has lost his mind; she hires a vulture named Vlad to destroy the speck. After several attempts, the animals conspire to rope and cage Horton and boil the speck in a vat of beezlenut oil. Horton urges the Whos to make noise so they can be heard, and with contributions from every citizen of Whoville — including the Mayor's son JoJo, whose improvised instrument makes the final sound needed — the Whos break through, proving their existence. The animals relent, and Whoville is saved.

Reception

The film premiered on March 3, 2008, and was released in the United States on March 14, 2008. It received generally positive reviews from critics, who praised its faithfulness to the source material and its animation, and was a commercial success, grossing $297 million worldwide against a budget of $85 million.
