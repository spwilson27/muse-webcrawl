Jason Bateman

Jason Bateman
Born: 14 January 1969, Rye, New York, U.S.
Occupation: Actor, director, producer

Jason Kent Bateman (born January 14, 1969) is an American actor, director and producer. He began his career as a child actor on Little House on the Prairie and Silver Spoons in the early 1980s.

Career

Bateman's career revived with his starring role as Michael Bluth in the sitcom Arrested Development (2003–2019), for which he won a Golden Globe Award. He went on to star in films including Juno, Hancock, Up in the Air, Horrible Bosses and Game Night, and directed films such as Bad Words and The Family Fang.

From 2017 to 2022 he starred as Marty Byrde in the Netflix crime drama Ozark, winning a Primetime Emmy Award for directing and a Screen Actors Guild Award for his acting. His sister is actress Justine Bateman.
