ArchiCAD

ArchiCAD (stylized as Archicad) is a building information modeling (BIM) computer-aided design software for architects, developed by the Hungarian company Graphisoft. First released in 1984 for the Apple Macintosh, it is recognized as one of the earliest BIM applications and pioneered the "Virtual Building" concept.

Developer: Graphisoft (Nemetschek Group)
Initial release: 1984
Type: CAD / BIM software
Platforms: Windows, macOS

Features

Archicad allows architects to design buildings in 2D and 3D simultaneously: plans, sections, elevations, and schedules are generated from a single 3D model. Features include parametric building elements (walls, slabs, roofs, stairs), teamwork collaboration through BIMcloud, rendering, and interoperability via IFC and other formats.

History

Graphisoft was founded in 1982, and its Radar CH software, the predecessor of ArchiCAD, was created for the Apple Lisa and Macintosh. The company later became part of Germany's Nemetschek Group.
