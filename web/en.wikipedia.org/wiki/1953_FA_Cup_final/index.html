1953 FA Cup final

DateMay 2, 1953
VenueWembley Stadium, London
MatchBlackpool 4–3 Bolton Wanderers
Attendance100,000
Remembered asThe Matthews Final

The 1953 FA Cup final was played at Wembley Stadium on May 2, 1953, between Blackpool and Bolton Wanderers. Blackpool won 4–3 after trailing 3–1 late in the second half. The match is universally known as the Matthews Final because 38-year-old winger Stanley Matthews finally won an FA Cup winner's medal after defeats in the 1948 and 1951 finals.

Match summary

Bolton led through Nat Lofthouse and, after Stan Mortensen equalised, goals by Bobby Langton and Eric Bell restored and extended their advantage. Bell was injured and, in an era before substitutes, Bolton were effectively reduced in strength. Blackpool surged forward; Mortensen completed a hat-trick, still unique in a Wembley FA Cup final, and Bill Perry scored the winning goal in injury time from a Matthews pass.

Significance

The final became one of the defining spectacles of post-war English football. Televised to a growing audience, it combined a dramatic comeback, an ageing star's redemption and a hat-trick by Mortensen, whose achievement is often overshadowed by the Matthews label. The game also highlighted the cruelty of the no-substitute rule for injured players.
