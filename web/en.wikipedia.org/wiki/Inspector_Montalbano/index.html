Inspector Montalbano

Inspector Montalbano (Italian: Il commissario Montalbano) is an Italian police procedural television series based on the novels by Andrea Camilleri, starring Luca Zingaretti as Salvo Montalbano, a police chief in the fictional Sicilian town of Vigàta.

Overview

Produced by Rai from 1999, the series was filmed around Ragusa, Scicli and Punta Secca in southeastern Sicily. Montalbano — food-loving, morally driven, often clashing with bureaucracy and the Mafia — solves murders with deputies Mimì Augello, Giuseppe Fazio and the comic Catarella. A prequel, Young Montalbano, aired in 2012–2015.

Reception

The series became one of Italy's most successful TV exports, broadcast in dozens of countries, and boosted tourism to the filming locations, known as "Montalbano places."
