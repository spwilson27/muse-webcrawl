Cantabrian Wars

The Cantabrian Wars (29-19 BC) were a series of campaigns by the Roman Empire against the Cantabri and Astures of northern Hispania, the final stage of the Roman conquest of the Iberian Peninsula.

Date: 29-19 BC
Location: Northern Hispania
Result: Roman victory

Campaigns

Emperor Augustus personally campaigned in 26-25 BC, and his general Agrippa completed the subjugation in 19 BC after fierce guerrilla resistance in the mountainous terrain.

Aftermath

The region was incorporated into Hispania Tarraconensis, and gold mining and Roman roads transformed the area.
