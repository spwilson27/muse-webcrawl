Krakoa

Krakoa is a fictional living island appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Len Wein and artist Dave Cockrum, it first appeared in Giant-Size X-Men #1 (May 1975) as an antagonist that captured the original X-Men, prompting the formation of a new team. Krakoa later became the sovereign mutant nation-state in the relaunched X-Men line beginning with House of X and Powers of X (2019), written by Jonathan Hickman.

Fictional history

Krakoa was once a small Pacific island mutated into a sentient, hungry ecosystem by nuclear weapons testing; it survived by feeding on mutant energy. In its debut story it captured the original X-Men, and Professor X assembled a new international team to rescue them, after which Krakoa was hurled into space by Polaris. A fragment left behind grew into a new Krakoa that later hosted the Jean Grey School. In 2019's House of X, Professor X, Magneto, and Moira MacTaggert established Krakoa as the homeland of mutantkind: a sovereign nation recognised in exchange for three miracle drugs given to humanity, connected worldwide by portal gates, governed by a Quiet Council, and able to resurrect dead mutants through the combined powers of the Five. The "Krakoan era" that followed redefined the X-Men franchise, introducing Krakoan language, laws (including "make more mutants"), and the Hellfire Gala, before concluding with the Fall of X storyline in 2024.
