Canini

Canini is a taxonomic tribe within the subfamily Caninae, containing the wolf-like canids such as wolves, coyotes, jackals, domestic dogs and related South American and extinct species.

Rank: Tribe
Family: Canidae
Subfamily: Caninae

Taxonomy

Canini includes the genera Canis, Cuon and Lycaon in the subtribe Canina, and numerous South American genera such as Chrysocyon, Cerdocyon and Speothos in the subtribe Cerdocyonina.

Evolution

The tribe originated in North America in the Miocene and later dispersed to other continents.
