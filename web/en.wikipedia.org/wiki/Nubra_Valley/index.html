Nubra Valley

The Nubra Valley is a high-altitude desert valley in Ladakh, India, formed by the Shyok and Nubra (Siachen) rivers, about 150 km north of Leh across the Khardung La pass.

Features

Known for sand dunes at Hunder, double-humped Bactrian camels, Diskit Monastery with its large Maitreya statue, and hot springs at Panamik. It was historically on a Silk Road trade route to Yarkand and Kashgar.
