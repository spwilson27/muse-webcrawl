Douglas Craig Emhoff (born October 13, 1964) is an American lawyer who served as the second gentleman of the United States from 2021 to 2025, as the husband of Vice President Kamala Harris. He is the first person to hold the title of second gentleman, and the first Jewish spouse of a U.S. vice president.

Early life and education

Emhoff was born in Brooklyn, New York, to Jewish parents, and grew up in New Jersey and later in the Los Angeles area. He earned a bachelor's degree from California State University, Northridge, and a Juris Doctor from the USC Gould School of Law.

Legal career

Emhoff built a career as an entertainment and intellectual-property litigator in Los Angeles, working at firms including Pillsbury Winthrop Shaw Pittman, Venable, and DLA Piper, where he was a partner. He took a leave of absence from DLA Piper in 2020 during Harris's vice-presidential campaign and left the firm before the inauguration. In 2021 he joined the faculty of Georgetown University Law Center as a distinguished visitor and fellow, teaching courses on entertainment law.

Second Gentleman

As second gentleman, Emhoff focused on combating antisemitism, promoting access to justice and gender equity, and representing the United States abroad. Following the October 2023 Hamas attack on Israel and a rise in antisemitic incidents, he became one of the administration's most visible voices on the issue. In 2023 President Biden appointed him to the board of the United States Holocaust Memorial Council. After Harris became the Democratic presidential nominee in 2024, Emhoff campaigned extensively on her behalf.

Personal life

Emhoff has two children, Cole and Ella, from his first marriage to film producer Kerstin Emhoff. He married Kamala Harris in 2014. His stepchildren affectionately call him and Harris by nicknames — he is "Doug", and Harris is known to them as "Momala".
