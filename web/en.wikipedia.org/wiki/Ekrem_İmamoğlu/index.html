Ekrem İmamoğlu

Ekrem İmamoğlu
Born: 4 June 1970, Trabzon, Turkey
Party: Republican People's Party (CHP)
Office: Mayor of Istanbul (2019–2025)

Ekrem İmamoğlu (born 4 June 1970) is a Turkish politician of the Republican People's Party (CHP) who served as the Mayor of Istanbul from 2019 until his suspension and arrest in 2025. He rose to national prominence by defeating the governing AKP's candidate in the 2019 Istanbul mayoral elections, seen as a major symbolic blow to President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan.

Early life and career

İmamoğlu was born in Trabzon and studied business administration at Istanbul University. He worked in his family's construction business and served as a director of the football club Trabzonspor before entering politics. In 2014 he was elected mayor of Istanbul's Beylikdüzü district.

Mayor of Istanbul

In the March 2019 local elections İmamoğlu narrowly defeated former prime minister Binali Yıldırım. The result was controversially annulled by the Supreme Election Council, but İmamoğlu won the re-run in June 2019 by a much larger margin, with the slogan "Everything will be very beautiful" (Her şey çok güzel olacak). He was re-elected in March 2024 with an increased share of the vote. As mayor he pursued an inclusive, service-oriented administration and became one of the most popular opposition figures in Turkey and a leading prospective presidential candidate.

Legal cases and arrest

İmamoğlu faced a series of prosecutions, including a 2022 conviction for "insulting" election officials, which carried a political ban that remained under appeal. In March 2025, a day after Istanbul University annulled his university diploma — a qualification required to run for president — he was detained on corruption and terrorism-related charges, triggering nationwide protests; the CHP nevertheless selected him as its presidential candidate while he was in custody.
