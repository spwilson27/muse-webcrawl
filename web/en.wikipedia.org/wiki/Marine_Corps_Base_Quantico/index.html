Marine Corps Base Quantico

Marine Corps Base Quantico is a United States Marine Corps installation located near Triangle, Virginia, covering nearly 55,148 acres across three counties beside the Potomac River. Nicknamed the “Crossroads of the Marine Corps”, it is the Corps’ principal site for officer training and combat development.

History

The base was established in 1917 as Marine Barracks, Quantico, when the Marine Corps leased land to train troops deploying to France in the First World War. The government purchased the site outright in 1918. The concepts of modern amphibious warfare were developed and tested here between the wars.

Tenants and facilities

Quantico hosts Officer Candidates School, The Basic School for newly commissioned officers, and Marine Corps University. It is also home to the FBI Academy, the headquarters of the Federal Bureau of Investigation’s training division, and the Drug Enforcement Administration’s training academy. The National Museum of the Marine Corps opened adjacent to the base in 2006, and Quantico National Cemetery lies nearby.
