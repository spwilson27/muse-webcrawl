Blue Apron Holdings, Inc. is an American meal kit company founded in 2012. It delivers boxes of pre-portioned ingredients together with recipe cards that allow subscribers to cook complete meals at home. The name refers to the blue aprons worn by apprentice chefs in France. Blue Apron was a pioneer of the meal-kit subscription model in the United States and one of the largest companies in the sector.

History

The company was founded by Matt Salzberg, Ilia Papas and chef Matt Wadiak, and grew rapidly on venture capital funding, reaching a valuation of around $2 billion in 2015. It went public on the New York Stock Exchange in June 2017, in the first IPO of a US meal-kit company, but its shares fell sharply amid rising customer acquisition costs and competition from rivals such as HelloFresh and from Amazon's entry into groceries. Blue Apron struggled with fulfilment problems at a new New Jersey facility, declining subscriber numbers, layoffs and repeated management changes. In 2023 the company sold its operational infrastructure to FreshRealm and was acquired by Wonder Group, transitioning to an asset-light model in which fresh meal production is outsourced while Blue Apron continues as a consumer brand.
