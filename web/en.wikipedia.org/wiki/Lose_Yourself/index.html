"Lose Yourself" is a song by American rapper Eminem, written and recorded for the soundtrack of the 2002 film 8 Mile, in which Eminem starred. Released as a single in October 2002, it became his first number-one single on the Billboard Hot 100, remaining at the top for twelve weeks.

Composition and themes

Built around a hard guitar-driven beat produced by Eminem with Jeff Bass and Luis Resto, the song follows the film's protagonist, Jimmy "B-Rabbit" Smith Jr., capturing the pressure of seizing a single opportunity — summed up in the famous hook, "You only get one shot." Its dense internal rhymes and escalating delivery are widely cited in discussions of rap technique.

Reception and legacy

"Lose Yourself" won the Academy Award for Best Original Song at the 75th Academy Awards, the first rap song to receive the honour, as well as Grammy Awards for Best Rap Song and Best Male Rap Solo Performance. It is routinely ranked among the greatest hip hop songs of all time and became one of Eminem's signature recordings, certified diamond in the United States.
