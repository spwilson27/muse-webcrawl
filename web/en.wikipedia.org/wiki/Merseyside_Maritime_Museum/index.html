The Merseyside Maritime Museum is a museum based in the city of Liverpool, Merseyside, England. It is part of National Museums Liverpool and is situated in the Royal Albert Dock on the city's historic waterfront. The museum opened in 1980 and expanded in 1984, telling the story of Liverpool's role as one of the world's great ports.

Collections and exhibitions

The museum's galleries explore the development of the Port of Liverpool, emigration from the city to the New World, and Liverpool's connections with famous ocean liners. Exhibitions have covered the sinking of the RMS Titanic and RMS Lusitania, both of which had strong Liverpool links, as well as the Battle of the Atlantic during the Second World War. The museum also houses the International Slavery Museum on its upper floors, opened in 2007, which examines Liverpool's involvement in the transatlantic slave trade.

Historic vessels

Historic vessels associated with the museum and berthed in the dock include the pilot boat Edmund Gardner and the steam tug Daniel Adamson, reflecting the working heritage of the River Mersey.
