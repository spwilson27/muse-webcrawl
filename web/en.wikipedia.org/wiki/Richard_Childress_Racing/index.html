Richard Childress Racing

Richard Childress Racing (RCR)
Owner: Richard Childress
Base: Welcome, North Carolina, USA
Series: NASCAR Cup Series, Xfinity Series
Founded: 1969

Richard Childress Racing (RCR) is an American professional stock car racing team that currently competes in the NASCAR Cup Series and the NASCAR Xfinity Series. The team is based in Welcome, North Carolina, and is owned and operated by former driver Richard Childress.

History

Childress, who began racing as an independent driver in 1969, retired from driving in 1981 to concentrate on team ownership. RCR rose to prominence with Dale Earnhardt, who won six of his seven Cup Series championships with the team (1986, 1987, 1990, 1991, 1993 and 1994) driving the iconic black No. 3 Goodwrench Chevrolet. Childress also won the 1998 Daytona 500 with Earnhardt — the driver's only victory in the event after twenty attempts.

After Earnhardt

Following Earnhardt's death at the 2001 Daytona 500, Kevin Harvick took over the ride, renumbered 29, winning in only his third start. RCR later fielded drivers including Jeff Burton, Clint Bowyer, Ryan Newman, Austin Dillon — Childress's grandson, who revived the No. 3 in the Cup Series and won the 2018 Daytona 500 — and Tyler Reddick. The team has also won multiple championships in the Xfinity and Truck series.

Facilities

RCR's campus in Welcome, North Carolina, includes race shops, an engine programme through ECR Engines, and the Richard Childress Racing Museum, housed in the team's original shop.
