Police

Police
DefinitionConstituted body empowered to enforce law, protect life and property
First modern forceLondon Metropolitan Police, 1829 (Peel)
[Image: Police officers on a city street beside a marked patrol car]

The police are a constituted body of persons empowered by a state to enforce the law, ensure public safety and order, and prevent, detect and investigate criminal activity. Sir Robert Peel's Metropolitan Police (London, 1829) founded the modern model of civilian policing by consent.

Debates

Policing spans community patrol to paramilitary units; accountability, use of force, racial disparities and reform or abolition movements are live controversies, intensified by smartphone video and the social media pages of this crawl. Most crime-solving remains unglamorous: witnesses, forensics, paperwork.
