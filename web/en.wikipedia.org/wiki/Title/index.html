Title

A title is one or more words used to identify a work, a person, or a legal right. The term is used in several distinct senses:

Works

The title of a book, film, song or other creative work is its name, typically chosen by the creator to identify the work and hint at its content or theme. Works may also have subtitles, working titles used during production, and different titles in translation.

Persons

A title can denote a person's rank, office, profession or honour — for example, titles of nobility (duke, countess), academic titles (doctor, professor), religious titles, and courtesy titles. In many contexts titles precede the name.

Law

In property law, title is the legal right to own and use property, often evidenced by a deed or registration. A "title" may also be the name of a statute or a major division of a legal code, such as the titles of the United States Code.
