SAE J1772

SAE J1772
Type: Electric vehicle charging connector
Standard body: SAE International
Region: North America, Japan

SAE J1772, formally SAE Surface Vehicle Recommended Practice J1772: Electric Vehicle Conductive Charge Coupler, is a North American standard for electrical connectors for electric vehicles maintained by SAE International. It defines the common five-pin connector, often simply called the J1772 or "J-plug", used for Level 1 and Level 2 AC charging.

Design

The circular connector has five pins: two AC power pins (L1 and neutral/L2), a protective earth, a proximity detection pin, and a control pilot pin carrying a 1 kHz square wave by which the charging station and vehicle negotiate the available current. The design incorporates safety interlocks so the connector is not live when disconnected, and a latch that prevents drive-off while plugged in.

Use

The connector was adopted by virtually every automaker selling plug-in vehicles in North America and Japan before the industry shift toward the Tesla-developed NACS (SAE J3400) connector in the mid-2020s. A DC fast-charging extension, the Combined Charging System (CCS Combo 1), adds two large DC pins below the J1772 inlet.
