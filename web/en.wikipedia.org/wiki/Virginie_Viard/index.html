Virginie Viard (born 1962) is a French fashion designer who served as artistic director of Chanel from 2019 to 2024. A longtime collaborator of Karl Lagerfeld, she was his closest deputy for more than three decades before succeeding him following his death in February 2019.

Career

Viard studied fashion design in Lyon and began her career as an assistant to costume designer Dominique Borg. In 1987 she joined Chanel as an intern in embroidery, quickly becoming Lagerfeld's principal assistant and coordinator of the haute couture studio. She followed Lagerfeld to Chloé during his tenure there in the 1990s before returning to Chanel, where she became director of the fashion creation studio, overseeing haute couture, ready-to-wear and accessories collections.

Chanel artistic director

On Lagerfeld's death in February 2019, Chanel named Viard artistic director of fashion collections — the first woman to lead the house's creative direction since founder Gabrielle "Coco" Chanel herself. Her tenure emphasised wearability and continuity with the house codes, softer silhouettes and a more relaxed interpretation of the Chanel suit. Reception from critics was mixed, though the brand's revenues grew strongly during her tenure. In June 2024 Chanel announced her departure after some thirty years with the house; she was succeeded by Matthieu Blazy, whose appointment was announced in December 2024.
