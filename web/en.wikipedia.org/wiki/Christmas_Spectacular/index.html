Christmas Spectacular

The Christmas Spectacular Starring the Radio City Rockettes is an annual musical holiday stage show presented at Radio City Music Hall in New York City. One of New York's most enduring Christmas traditions, the 90-minute show features the precision dance company the Rockettes, Santa Claus, and signature numbers including the "Parade of the Wooden Soldiers" and the "Living Nativity".

History

The show began in 1933, a year after Radio City Music Hall opened. Producer Leon Leonidoff and choreographer Russell Markert, founder of the Rockettes, created a short holiday pageant to accompany film screenings; the "Parade of the Wooden Soldiers", with the dancers collapsing in slow motion like falling dominoes, and the "Living Nativity" both date from that first production and have been performed ever since. In 1979 the show was expanded into a full-length production detached from film presentations, and it has since been revised periodically, adding numbers such as "New York at Christmas", a double-decker bus tour sequence, and "Rag Dolls", while stage technology has grown to include LED screens, projection mapping and flying effects.

Production

During the holiday season the production typically plays multiple performances daily — up to five on peak days — from November to early January, before audiences totalling more than one million people per season. The cast includes the Rockettes, an ensemble of singers and dancers, a live orchestra, and animals (camels, sheep and a donkey) for the Nativity scene, which are famously walked through midtown Manhattan. Touring productions have also played in other American cities and at venues such as the Grand Ole Opry House in Nashville.

Together with the Rockefeller Center Christmas Tree, the show is regarded as a quintessential New York City holiday institution, drawing generations of families since the Great Depression.

Signature numbers

  • "Parade of the Wooden Soldiers"
  • "The Twelve Days of Christmas" (tap number)
  • "New York at Christmas"
  • "Here Comes Santa Claus"
  • "Living Nativity"
  • "Joy to the World" (finale)
