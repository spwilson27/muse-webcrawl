Rocket Raccoon

Rocket Raccoon is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Bill Mantlo and artist Keith Giffen, the character first appeared in Marvel Preview #7 (1976). Rocket is an intelligent, anthropomorphic raccoon — a genetically engineered creature from the planet Halfworld — who is an expert tactician and marksman and the partner of Groot.

Publication history

Rocket starred in his own 1985 limited series and appeared as a supporting character in series such as Quasar and Annihilation. He became a founding member of the modern Guardians of the Galaxy team formed in 2008 by Dan Abnett and Andy Lanning.

In other media

In the Marvel Cinematic Universe, Rocket is voiced by Bradley Cooper (with Sean Gunn providing motion capture), appearing from Guardians of the Galaxy (2014) onward in the trilogy, the Avengers films, and Thor: Love and Thunder. His tragic origin as the experiment "89P13" of the High Evolutionary is explored in Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3 (2023).
