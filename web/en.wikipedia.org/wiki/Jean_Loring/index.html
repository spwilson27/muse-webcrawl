Jean Loring is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Gardner Fox and artist Murphy Anderson, she first appeared in Atom #1 (1962) as an attorney and the girlfriend — later wife — of Ray Palmer, the superhero known as the Atom.

Fictional character biography

Jean Loring was a successful lawyer in Ivy Town and long-time romantic partner of Ray Palmer. The two eventually married, though the marriage ended in divorce. In the 2004 storyline Identity Crisis, a mentally unstable Jean was revealed as the murderer of Sue Dibny, wife of the Elongated Man, in a desperate scheme to win Ray back; the controversial story had a major impact on the DC Universe.

Subsequently, Jean was possessed by the spectre of Eclipso and became a villain, before being imprisoned in the events surrounding Countdown and Blackest Night. Versions of the character have appeared in other media, including the television series Arrow.
