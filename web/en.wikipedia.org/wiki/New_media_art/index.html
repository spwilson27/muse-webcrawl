New media art

New media art comprises artworks created with new media technologies — digital art, computer graphics, virtual and interactive art, video art, internet art, robotics and biotechnology art.

New media art
Media: Digital, interactive, networked, virtual
Pioneers: Nam June Paik, Roy Ascott, Lynn Hershman Leeson

History

Roots lie in Dada, Fluxus and conceptual art, and in 1960s video art (Paik) and early computer art. From the 1990s, the web enabled net.art; festivals such as Ars Electronica and institutions including ZKM Karlsruhe championed the field.

Themes and preservation

Interactivity, participation, networks, artificial intelligence and data are central themes. Conservation is challenging because hardware and software become obsolete, prompting emulation and documentation strategies. See Digital art, Internet art.
