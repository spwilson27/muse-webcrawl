Tawahi

Tawahi is a district of Aden Governorate in southern Yemen, located on the western side of the Aden peninsula near the port. It is one of the oldest residential areas of the city of Aden and historically served the harbour community.

Overview

Tawahi developed during the period when Aden was a British protectorate and coaling station, becoming a commercial and residential quarter close to Steamer Point, where passenger ships called. The district contains markets, mosques and colonial-era buildings reflecting its maritime past.

Recent history

Like the rest of Aden, Tawahi was affected by the Yemeni civil war that began in 2015, when fighting reached the city and the district changed hands during the battle for Aden.
