Embraer Phenom 300

Manufacturer: Embraer
First flight: April 29, 2008
Introduction: 2009
Status: In production, in service

The Embraer EMB-505 Phenom 300 is a light business jet designed and produced by the Brazilian aerospace manufacturer Embraer. Certified for single-pilot operation, it can carry up to 11 occupants and was the most-delivered light jet in the world for more than a decade, as well as the most-delivered business jet overall in several years.

Development

The Phenom 300 was developed from the smaller Phenom 100 very light jet, with a stretched fuselage, larger cabin, and more powerful Pratt & Whitney Canada PW535E1 turbofans. It first flew in April 2008 and received certification in December 2009.

Phenom 300E

The enhanced Phenom 300E, introduced in 2018 and updated in 2020, added redesigned seats, updated avionics based on the Garmin G3000 with autothrottle, and improved performance, with a range of about 2,010 nautical miles.

Operators

The type is popular with fractional ownership and charter companies, including NetJets and Flexjet, and with private owners.
