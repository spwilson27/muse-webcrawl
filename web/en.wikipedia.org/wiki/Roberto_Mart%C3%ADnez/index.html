Roberto Martínez Montoliú (born 13 July 1973) is a Spanish football manager and former player, head coach of the Portugal national team. As a player he became part of the "Three Amigos" of Spanish signings at Wigan Athletic in 1995 and spent most of his career in British football.

Managerial career

Martínez began managing at Swansea City, winning League One in 2008 with a possession-based style, then moved to Wigan Athletic, where his team famously won the 2013 FA Cup against Manchester City days before being relegated from the Premier League. At Everton he recorded the club's record Premier League points total in his first season. He coached Belgium's "golden generation" to third place at the 2018 World Cup and to the top of the FIFA rankings, and took charge of Portugal in 2023, winning the UEFA Nations League in 2025.
