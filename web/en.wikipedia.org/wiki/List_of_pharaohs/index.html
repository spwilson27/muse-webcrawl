List of pharaohs

The pharaohs were the monarchs of ancient Egypt from the unification of Upper and Lower Egypt around 3100 BC until the Roman annexation in 30 BC. This list follows the conventional chronology, organised by the dynasties of Manetho. Dates before the New Kingdom are approximate.
