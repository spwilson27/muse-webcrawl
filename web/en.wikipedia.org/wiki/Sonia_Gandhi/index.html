Sonia Gandhi

Sonia Gandhi (née Maino; born 9 December 1946) is an Indian politician of Italian origin. She is the longest-serving president of the Indian National Congress, having led the party from 1998 to 2017 and again as interim president from 2019 to 2022. The widow of former Prime Minister Rajiv Gandhi, she led the Congress-led United Progressive Alliance (UPA) to victory in the 2004 and 2009 general elections, though she declined the prime ministership in 2004 in favour of Manmohan Singh. A member of the Nehru–Gandhi family, she has been a Member of Parliament representing Rae Bareli and, from 2024, a member of the Rajya Sabha.

Early life and family

Sonia Maino was born in Lusiana, in the Veneto region of Italy. While studying English at a language school in Cambridge, England, she met Rajiv Gandhi, then a student at the University of Cambridge. They married in 1968 and she moved to India, becoming an Indian citizen in 1983. After the assassination of her mother-in-law, Prime Minister Indira Gandhi, in 1984, Rajiv became prime minister; he was assassinated in 1991.

Political career

For several years after her husband's death Sonia Gandhi stayed out of politics, but in 1998 she agreed to lead a demoralised Congress party. She was elected to the Lok Sabha from Amethi in 1999 and became Leader of the Opposition. In 2004 the UPA's surprise victory made her India's most powerful politician; her decision to refuse the premiership and install Manmohan Singh was widely noted. Under her leadership the UPA governments enacted the rural employment guarantee (MGNREGA), the Right to Information Act and the National Food Security Act. After Congress's heavy defeats in 2014 and 2019, her son Rahul Gandhi and later Mallikarjun Kharge assumed the party presidency.
