Dangerous Liaisons (film)

Directed by: Stephen Frears
Screenplay: Christopher Hampton
Starring: Glenn Close, John Malkovich, Michelle Pfeiffer
Released: 1988
Source: Les Liaisons dangereuses by Choderlos de Laclos

Dangerous Liaisons is a 1988 American period romantic drama film directed by Stephen Frears, adapted by Christopher Hampton from his own stage play, which was based on the 1782 French epistolary novel Les Liaisons dangereuses by Pierre Choderlos de Laclos. It stars Glenn Close as the Marquise de Merteuil and John Malkovich as the Vicomte de Valmont, two decadent aristocrats who wager over seduction and ruin.

Plot

Merteuil challenges her former lover Valmont to corrupt the convent-educated Cécile de Volanges; when he initially refuses in favour of seducing the virtuous Madame de Tourvel (Michelle Pfeiffer), their games of manipulation spiral into genuine love, betrayal and tragedy, ending in duels, disgrace and death amid pre-Revolutionary Parisian society.

Reception

The film was a critical and commercial success, nominated for seven Academy Awards and winning three — Best Adapted Screenplay, Best Art Direction and Best Costume Design. Close and Pfeiffer received widespread acclaim. It is frequently ranked among the finest literary adaptations of its era, and competed with Miloš Forman's Valmont (1989), adapted from the same novel.
