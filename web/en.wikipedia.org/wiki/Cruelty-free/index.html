Cruelty-free

Cruelty-free is a label for products or activities that do not harm or kill animals anywhere in the world. It is mainly used for cosmetics and household products that have not been tested on animals.

Definition and certification

Definitions vary: a finished product may not be tested on animals while its ingredients were. Certification programmes include the Leaping Bunny programme (Cruelty Free International / Coalition for Consumer Information on Cosmetics) and PETA's Beauty Without Bunnies, which audit supply chains.

Regulation

The European Union banned animal testing for cosmetics and the sale of animal-tested cosmetics (completed 2013); similar bans exist in India, Israel, Norway, Australia and several US states. Some markets have required animal tests for imported cosmetics, complicating global claims.
