The Bertelsmann Stiftung is an independent foundation under private law, headquartered in Gütersloh, Germany. Founded in 1977 by Reinhard Mohn, the majority shareholder of the media conglomerate Bertelsmann, it is one of Germany's largest foundations and operates as a think tank producing research and policy proposals in areas including education, democracy, health, economic policy and social affairs.

Work

The foundation describes itself as a promoter of "reform and social change" and works exclusively on an operational basis, developing and implementing its own projects rather than awarding grants. Its best-known products include country and transformation indices, studies on education and the labour market, and recommendations on governance and European policy.

Criticism

The foundation's close ties to the Bertelsmann media group and its influence on public policy debate in Germany have attracted criticism from observers who question the democratic legitimacy of its agenda-setting role.
