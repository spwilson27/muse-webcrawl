2019 Ukrainian presidential election

The 2019 Ukrainian presidential election was held on 31 March and 21 April 2019. With no candidate winning a majority in the first round, a run-off took place between comedian and political newcomer Volodymyr Zelenskyy and incumbent president Petro Poroshenko. Zelenskyy won the second round with 73.22 percent of the vote, the largest margin in Ukrainian presidential history.

Election summary

First round: 31 March 2019
Run-off: 21 April 2019
Winner: Volodymyr Zelenskyy (73.22%)
Runner-up: Petro Poroshenko (24.45%)
Candidates: 39 (first round)
Turnout (run-off): 62.07%

Campaign

Zelenskyy, best known for playing a fictional Ukrainian president in the television series Servant of the People, announced his candidacy on New Year's Eve 2018 and campaigned largely through social media on an anti-corruption platform. Poroshenko ran on the slogan "Army, Language, Faith", emphasising national security amid the war in Donbas. In the first round Zelenskyy took 30.24 percent and Poroshenko 15.95 percent, with former prime minister Yulia Tymoshenko third.

Run-off and result

The campaign's second phase was marked by an unprecedented public debate between the candidates at Kyiv's Olimpiyskiy Stadium on 19 April. In the run-off on 21 April, Zelenskyy won every oblast except Lviv. He was inaugurated on 20 May 2019 and immediately dissolved parliament, calling snap elections that his Servant of the People party won in July.
