Uru (Marvel Comics)

Uru is a fictional metal that appears in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. It is the material from which Thor's hammer, Mjolnir, is forged. Uru is said to come from the first moon in the universe and to be mined in the realm of Nidavellir by the Dwarves.

Properties

Uru is highly durable and has the unusual property of holding enchantments extremely well; it absorbs and stores magical energy like a sponge, which is why Odin's enchantments upon Mjolnir persist. Its appearance resembles stone more than metal, and weapons forged from it, such as Mjolnir and the axe Stormbreaker, rank among the most powerful in the Marvel Universe. Working uru requires the heat of a star and the skill of the Dwarven smiths of Nidavellir.

Notable objects

  • Mjolnir, the hammer of Thor
  • Stormbreaker, forged by Eitri
  • The Destroyer armour
  • Odinsword and other Asgardian artifacts

In other media

Uru appears in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, where Mjolnir and Stormbreaker are forged at Nidavellir using the heat of a dying star.
