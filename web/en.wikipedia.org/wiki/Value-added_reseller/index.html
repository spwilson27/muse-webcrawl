Value-added reseller

A value-added reseller (VAR) is a company that adds features or services to an existing product and then resells it — usually to end users — as an integrated product or complete turn-key solution. The term is common in the information technology industry.

Business model

VARs typically purchase hardware or software from original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) and vendors, then add their own services: consulting, system design, custom configuration or software development, installation, training, and ongoing support and maintenance. Examples range from small firms building PCs with bundled software, to large systems integrators delivering enterprise resource planning implementations.

Unlike plain distributors, who simply move products through the supply chain, a VAR's margin comes from the value of its additions rather than volume alone. Vendors operate partner programmes that authorise and certify VARs, sometimes granting territorial or vertical-market exclusivity.

Related concepts

The model is closely related to that of the systems integrator and the managed service provider; some VARs have shifted toward recurring-revenue services and cloud solutions as hardware margins have thinned.
