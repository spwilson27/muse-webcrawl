ISO 50001

TitleEnergy management systems — Requirements with guidance for use
First published2011
Latest version2018
PublisherISO

ISO 50001 is an international standard for energy management systems (EnMS), published by the International Organization for Standardization. It specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining and improving an energy management system, enabling organizations to follow a systematic approach to achieving continual improvement of energy performance, including energy efficiency, energy use and energy consumption.

Approach

The standard is built on the Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle common to ISO management system standards. Organizations establish an energy policy, conduct an energy review, set energy performance indicators and baselines, and implement action plans to reduce energy consumption. The 2018 revision aligned the standard with the Annex SL high-level structure used by ISO 9001 and ISO 14001.

Certification

Organizations can obtain third-party certification to ISO 50001, and tens of thousands of certificates have been issued worldwide, notably in energy-intensive manufacturing sectors.
