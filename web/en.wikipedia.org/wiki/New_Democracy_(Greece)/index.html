New Democracy (Greek: Νέα Δημοκρατία, Néa Dimokratía, ND) is a liberal-conservative political party in Greece. Founded in 1974 by Konstantinos Karamanlis after the fall of the military junta, it has been one of the country's two dominant parties, alternating in government with the socialist PASOK for much of the post-1974 era.

New Democracy is one of Greece's two major parties.

History

Karamanlis led the party to victory in the first free elections of 1974 and oversaw the restoration of democracy, a new constitution and Greece's accession to the European Economic Community in 1981. The party later governed under Konstantinos Mitsotakis (1990–1993), Kostas Karamanlis (2004–2009) and Antonis Samaras (2012–2015), during the Greek government-debt crisis.

Recent years

Under Kyriakos Mitsotakis, elected leader in 2016, New Democracy won the 2019 election and formed a majority government, and won again in 2023 with an outright majority, pursuing economic liberalisation, digital reform and closer European and transatlantic alignment.
