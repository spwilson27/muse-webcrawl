Melinda Dillon

Melinda Rose Dillon (October 13, 1939 – January 9, 2023) was an American actress. She received two Academy Award nominations for Best Supporting Actress, for her roles as Jillian Guiler in Close Encounters of the Third Kind (1977) and Teresa Perrone in Absence of Malice (1981). She is also beloved by audiences as Mother Parker in the holiday classic A Christmas Story (1983).

Early life and stage career

Dillon was born in Hope, Arkansas, and raised largely in Cullman, Alabama, in a military family. She began her career in Chicago, working with the improvisational comedy troupe The Second City, and was nominated for a Tony Award for her performance as Honey in the original Broadway production of Edward Albee's Who's Afraid of Virginia Woolf? (1963).

Film career

Her film debut came in The April Fools (1969). After a notable turn as Woody Guthrie's wife in Bound for Glory (1976), she achieved wide recognition as the mother of an abducted child in Steven Spielberg's Close Encounters of the Third Kind. Subsequent credits include Slap Shot (1977), The Prince of the City (1981), Harry and the Hendersons (1987), The Prince of the City, Magnolia (1999), and Reign Over Me (2007). She also appeared in television productions such as the 2001 adaptation of The Song of the Lark.

Personal life and death

Dillon married character actor Richard Libertini in 1963; they had one son and divorced in 1978. A notably private figure who rarely gave interviews in her later years, she died in Los Angeles on January 9, 2023, at the age of 83.
