List of busiest container ports

This is a list of the world's busiest container ports, ranked by total annual container throughput measured in twenty-foot equivalent units (TEUs). East Asian ports, and Chinese ports in particular, dominate the top of the list: the Port of Shanghai has been the world's busiest container port since 2010, when it overtook the Port of Singapore.

The growth of Chinese manufacturing exports drove the rapid rise of ports such as Shanghai, Shenzhen, and Ningbo-Zhoushan from the 1990s onward. Transhipment hubs such as Singapore and Busan handle large volumes of containers transferred between vessels, while gateway ports such as Rotterdam and Los Angeles primarily serve their national and continental hinterlands.
