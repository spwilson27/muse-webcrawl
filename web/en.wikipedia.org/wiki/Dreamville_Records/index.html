Dreamville Records is an American record label founded by American rapper J. Cole and his manager Ibrahim Hamad in 2007. The label is distributed through Interscope Records and is known for its collaborative compilation albums and for hosting the Dreamville Festival in Raleigh, North Carolina.

History

J. Cole established Dreamville during his early career, and the label gained prominence alongside his commercial success in the 2010s. Its roster has included Bas, Ari Lennox, JID, EarthGang, Cozz, Lute and Omen. The label's compilation Revenge of the Dreamers III (2019), recorded during widely publicised invitation-only sessions in Atlanta, debuted at number one on the Billboard 200 and earned a Grammy nomination.

Dreamville Festival

Since 2019 the label has staged the Dreamville Festival in Raleigh's Dorothea Dix Park, a two-day event drawing tens of thousands of attendees, with its final edition held in 2025.
