List of oldest universities in continuous operation

The University of Bologna (1088), the oldest university in the Western world

This is a list of the oldest universities in continuous operation in the world. A university is typically a medieval institution of higher learning organised as a corporation of masters and students (universitas magistrorum et scholarium). The oldest such institution is the University of Bologna, founded in 1088; still older centres of learning, such as al-Qarawiyyin in Fez (859) and al-Azhar in Cairo (972), began as mosque schools and acquired university status later.

Older institutions

The University of al-Qarawiyyin in Fez, Morocco, founded as a mosque in 859, is recognised by UNESCO and Guinness World Records as the oldest existing educational institution; Nalanda in India (5th century AD) was an ancient monastic university but ceased operation around 1200. Plato's Academy and the schools of Constantinople also predate the medieval universities but did not operate continuously into the modern era.
