San-X Co., Ltd. (サンエックス株式会社, San'ekkusu Kabushikigaisha) is a Japanese company specializing in the creation and marketing of kawaii (cute) fictional characters. Founded in 1932, the company produces stationery, plush toys, and a wide range of character goods sold throughout Japan and internationally. San-X is best known as the creator of Rilakkuma, the relaxed bear character introduced in 2003, as well as Sumikko Gurashi, Tarepanda, and Jinbei-san.

History

The company was founded in October 1932 as Chida Handler Co., a stationery wholesaler. It later adopted the name San-X, a play on the Japanese word san (three) and the letter X, suggesting unlimited possibilities. Over the decades the firm shifted from general stationery toward original character design, becoming one of Japan's leading character-goods companies alongside Sanrio.

Characters

San-X characters are often described as slightly melancholic, shy, or quirky compared with the cheerful mascots of rival firms — a quality sometimes called "healing" (iyashikei). Notable creations include:

  • Tarepanda (1995) — a lazy panda that moves by rolling, sparking a national craze in the late 1990s.
  • Rilakkuma (2003) — a bear in a zippered costume who loves relaxing; one of the most successful Japanese characters of the 2000s, later featured in the Netflix stop-motion series Rilakkuma and Kaoru (2019).
  • Sumikko Gurashi (2012) — a group of anxious characters who prefer to sit in corners; adapted into successful animated films.
  • Jinbei-san — a gentle whale shark character.
  • Sentimental Circus, Kamio-adjacent lines and numerous seasonal characters.
