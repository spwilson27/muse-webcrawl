National Express Coaches

ParentMobico Group (formerly National Express Group)
Founded1972
HeadquartersBirmingham, England
Area servedUnited Kingdom

National Express Coaches is the largest operator of scheduled long-distance coach services in the United Kingdom. It runs a nationwide network linking hundreds of towns and cities, major airports including Heathrow, Gatwick and Stansted, and large events, with London Victoria Coach Station as its principal hub.

History

The brand was created in 1972 by the state-owned National Bus Company to unify the express coach services of its regional subsidiaries under one name and livery. The business was privatised in the late 1980s through a management buyout, and the group later expanded into buses, trains and overseas transport. Most services are not operated by National Express itself but by franchised local coach companies running to its schedules, standards and livery, while National Express handles marketing, ticketing and the network timetable.

Services

Coaches are typically white with red and blue branding, offer reserved seating, air conditioning, Wi-Fi and power sockets, and are wheelchair-accessible. Fares are sold yield-managed, like airline tickets, with cheap advance "funfares" historically as low as £1. The company competes with rail and with rival networks such as Megabus and FlixBus, and carries millions of passengers a year on routes such as London–Birmingham, London–Manchester and airport shuttles across the country.
