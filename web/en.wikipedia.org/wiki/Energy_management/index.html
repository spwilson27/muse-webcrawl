Energy management

Key standardISO 50001

Energy management is the practice of planning, monitoring, and optimising the procurement and consumption of energy in buildings, industry, and organisations in order to reduce cost, conserve resources, and cut greenhouse-gas emissions.

Practices

Effective energy management typically begins with metering and energy auditing to establish a baseline, followed by operational changes, maintenance improvements, and capital projects such as efficient lighting, HVAC upgrades, and process optimisation. Energy management systems — both organisational frameworks, standardised by ISO 50001, and building automation software — provide for continual monitoring and improvement.

In buildings and industry

In commercial buildings, energy management addresses heating, cooling, ventilation, and lighting loads, often through a building management system. In industry, it focuses on process heat, motors, compressed air, and steam systems. Demand-side management and load shifting also fall under the discipline, as organisations respond to time-of-use tariffs and grid constraints.
