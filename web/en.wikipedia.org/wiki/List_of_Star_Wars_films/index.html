List of Star Wars films

The Star Wars franchise created by George Lucas centers on a series of films produced by Lucasfilm. The saga began with Star Wars (1977, later retitled Episode IV – A New Hope) and grew into the "Skywalker Saga" of nine episodic films organized into three trilogies, supplemented by standalone anthology films.
