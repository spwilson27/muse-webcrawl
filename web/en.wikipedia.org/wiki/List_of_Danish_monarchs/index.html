List of Danish monarchs

This is a list of Danish monarchs, that is, the kings and queen regnant of Denmark. It includes the Viking-age kings whose reigns are partly legendary, the medieval kings who united the Danish realm, the monarchs of the Kalmar Union, and the modern constitutional monarchs of the House of Glücksburg. The Danish monarchy is among the oldest continuing in Europe, conventionally dated to Gorm the Old in the tenth century.

Roskilde Cathedral, burial place of most Danish monarchs since the fifteenth century.

Early and Viking-age kings

Semi-legendary kings such as Sigurd Hring and Ragnar Lodbrok dominate the sagas. The first historically secure kings include Gorm the Old (reigned c. 936–958) and his son Harald Bluetooth, who claimed on the Jelling stones to have "won all of Denmark and Norway and made the Danes Christian." Harald's son Sweyn Forkbeard conquered England in 1013, and Sweyn's son Cnut the Great ruled a North Sea empire comprising Denmark, England and Norway.

Modern monarchs

The absolute monarchy ended with the constitution of 1849 under Frederick VII. The present House of Glücksburg succeeded in 1863 with Christian IX, "the father-in-law of Europe." Margrethe II reigned from 1972 until her abdication on 14 January 2024, when she was succeeded by her son Frederik X, the current king.
