Captain Zapp Brannigan is a fictional character in the animated television series Futurama, voiced by Billy West. An officer of the Democratic Order of Planets (DOOP) and captain of the flagship Nimbus, he is portrayed as a pompous, incompetent, and lecherous glory-seeker whose military successes result from recklessness and the sacrifice of his troops, in contrast to his public image as a decorated hero.

Zapp Brannigan, self-styled hero of the DOOP fleet.

Character

Brannigan is a parody of Captain James T. Kirk of Star Trek, blended with the public persona of William Shatner. His signature traits include malapropisms ("cham-paggin"), grandiose self-narration, and a velour uniform worn without underwear. His long-suffering lieutenant, the amphibious alien Kif Kroker, carries out his orders with open contempt. Brannigan's obsessive pursuit of Leela — who despises him after a single pitying encounter — is a recurring plot device, and his catastrophic decisions (destroying DOOP headquarters, losing wars against pacifists) are invariably covered up or rewarded.

Creation

The role was written for Phil Hartman, who died in 1998 before production; Billy West took over, incorporating an impression of Hartman alongside Shatner's cadence. The character appears throughout the original run, the direct-to-video films, and the revived series.
