Porto Santo Stefano
Country: Italy
Region: Tuscany
Comune: Monte Argentario

Porto Santo Stefano is a seaport town on the Monte Argentario promontory in southern Tuscany, Italy, and the largest town and administrative centre of the comune of Monte Argentario. It lies on the north-western side of the promontory, connected to the mainland near Orbetello.

The town grew up around a fishing port and was fortified under Spanish rule as part of the State of the Presidi; the Spanish Fortress (Fortezza Spagnola), built in the seventeenth century, dominates the old town and now houses exhibitions on the area's maritime history. Porto Santo Stefano was heavily bombed in the Second World War and rebuilt afterwards.

Tourism and transport

Today the town is a popular resort and sailing centre, with ferries to the islands of Giglio and Giannutri in the Tuscan Archipelago. In January 2012 the town's rescue services responded to the Costa Concordia disaster off nearby Giglio Island.
