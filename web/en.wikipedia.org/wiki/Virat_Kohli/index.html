Virat Kohli (born 5 November 1988) is an Indian international cricketer who plays for the India national cricket team and Royal Challengers Bengaluru in the IPL. A right-handed batsman, he is regarded as one of the greatest batsmen of all time, particularly in ODI cricket, in which he holds the record for most centuries — passing Sachin Tendulkar's mark of 49 with his 50th ODI hundred in the 2023 World Cup semi-final.

Kohli captained India to the Under-19 World Cup title in 2008 and debuted for the senior side the same year. He was a member of India's 2011 World Cup-winning squad and became Test captain in 2014 and all-format captain in 2017, leading India to a historic first Test series win in Australia in 2018–19 and to the top of the Test rankings. Famed for his chases in limited-overs cricket, his fitness regime and his intense on-field persona, Kohli retired from T20 internationals after India's 2024 T20 World Cup triumph and from Test cricket in May 2025, remaining an ODI player and the IPL's all-time leading run-scorer. He is one of the most followed people in the world on social media.
