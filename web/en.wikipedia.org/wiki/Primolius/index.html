Primolius

Primolius is a genus of macaw parrots in the family Psittacidae, native to South America. It contains three small macaw species formerly placed in Ara or Propyrrhura.

Species

The three species are the blue-headed macaw (Primolius couloni), the golden-collared or yellow-collared macaw (P. auricollis), and the Illiger's or blue-winged macaw (P. maracana). They are smaller than the large Ara macaws and mostly green in plumage. Several are kept in aviculture, and the blue-headed macaw is considered vulnerable due to habitat loss and trapping.
