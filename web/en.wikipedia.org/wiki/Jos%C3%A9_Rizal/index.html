José Protacio Rizal Mercado y Alonso Realonda (1861–1896) was a Filipino nationalist, writer, and polymath regarded as the national hero of the Philippines. His novels exposed the abuses of Spanish colonial rule and the friarocracy.

Life and works

Rizal studied medicine in Madrid and became an ophthalmologist. In 1887 he published Noli Me Tángere, followed by El filibusterismo (1891), both banned in the Philippines. A leading figure of the Propaganda Movement, he founded the civic organization La Liga Filipina, whose creation led to his exile to Dapitan. During the Philippine Revolution, which he did not endorse in its premature form, he was arrested, tried for rebellion, and executed by firing squad in Manila on December 30, 1896.

Legacy

Rizal's martyrdom galvanized the independence movement; Rizal Day is a national holiday in the Philippines.
