List of National League pennant winners

The List of National League pennant winners records the champions of Major League Baseball's National League for every season since the league's founding in 1876. The pennant winner advances to the World Series to face the American League champion. Before 1969 the pennant went to the team with the best regular-season record; since then it has been decided by the National League Championship Series.

Early years

The Chicago White Stockings won the first National League pennant in 1876. The Boston Beaneaters dominated the 1890s, while the New York Giants and Chicago Cubs were the powers of the dead-ball era. The Brooklyn Dodgers and St. Louis Cardinals defined the 1940s, and the "Shot Heard 'Round the World" decided the 1951 pennant in a playoff.

Most pennants

The Los Angeles/Brooklyn Dodgers and the San Francisco/New York Giants are the most successful National League franchises by pennant count, each with more than twenty, followed by the St. Louis Cardinals.
