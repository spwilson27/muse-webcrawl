The Secret Life of Pets is a 2016 American animated comedy film produced by Illumination Entertainment and distributed by Universal Pictures. Directed by Chris Renaud and co-directed by Yarrow Cheney, it was written by Brian Lynch and the writing team of Cinco Paul and Ken Daurio. The film imagines what household pets do while their owners are away at work or school.

Plot

Max, a pampered Jack Russell Terrier living in a Manhattan apartment building, is devastated when his owner Katie adopts Duke, a huge Newfoundland mix from the pound. After the two become lost in the city and are caught by animal control, they are rescued by Snowball, a deranged white rabbit who leads "The Flushed Pets", a gang of abandoned animals bent on revenge against humans. Max and Duke must set aside their rivalry, traverse New York City — with help from Gidget, a Pomeranian who loves Max — and return home before Katie does.

Reception

The film received generally positive reviews for its animation and humour and was a major box-office success, grossing $875 million worldwide against a $75 million budget, making it one of the highest-grossing animated films of 2016. A sequel, The Secret Life of Pets 2, was released in 2019, with Patton Oswalt replacing Louis C.K. as the voice of Max.
