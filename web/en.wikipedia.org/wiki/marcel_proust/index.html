Marcel Proust
Born: 10 July 1871, Paris
Died: 18 November 1922, Paris
Notable work: In Search of Lost Time

Valentin Louis Georges Eugène Marcel Proust (10 July 1871 – 18 November 1922) was a French novelist, literary critic, and essayist who wrote the monumental novel À la recherche du temps perdu (In Search of Lost Time), published in seven volumes between 1913 and 1927.

Life

Proust was born in the Auteuil quarter of Paris, the son of a prominent pathologist. Frail and asthmatic from childhood, he increasingly withdrew from society to write, famously working at night in a cork-lined room.

In Search of Lost Time

The novel, one of the longest ever written, explores memory, time, and desire through the experiences of its unnamed narrator. The episode of the madeleine dipped in tea, which triggers a flood of involuntary memory, is among the most famous scenes in literature.
