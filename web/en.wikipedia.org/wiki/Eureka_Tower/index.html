Eureka Tower

Image: Eureka Tower, Melbourne (placeholder)

Eureka Tower is a 297.3-metre (975 ft) skyscraper located in the Southbank precinct of Melbourne, Victoria, Australia. Construction began in 2002 and the building was officially opened in October 2006. On completion it was the tallest residential building in the world, and it remained Melbourne's tallest building until the completion of Australia 108.

Design

Designed by Fender Katsalidis Architects, the tower's name and design commemorate the Eureka Stockade, the 1854 gold-rush rebellion at Ballarat. The building's gold crown and red stripe symbolise the gold and the bloodshed of the uprising, while its blue glass and white horizontal lines evoke the Eureka flag. The tower contains 556 apartments across 91 storeys, served by some of the fastest lifts in the Southern Hemisphere.

Skydeck

The Eureka Skydeck on the 88th floor, 285 metres up, features "The Edge", a glass cube that projects three metres out from the building with visitors inside. The observation deck offers panoramic views across Melbourne and Port Phillip Bay.
