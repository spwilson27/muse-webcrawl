Olympia London International Horse Show

The Olympia London International Horse Show, known in recent years as the London International Horse Show, is a major equestrian event held annually in London shortly before Christmas. Founded in 1907, it is one of the oldest and most prestigious horse shows in the United Kingdom, combining international show jumping, dressage, and driving competitions with displays and entertainment.

History and venue

The show was first held at the Olympia exhibition centre in Kensington, London, in 1907, and after interruptions for the two world wars became a fixture of the British sporting calendar. In 2021 the show moved to the ExCeL London exhibition centre. The event traditionally includes a FEI World Cup qualifier in show jumping and a dressage World Cup leg.

Programme

Alongside elite competition, the show is famous for its festive displays, which have included the Shetland Pony Grand National, the Dog Agility competitions, the King's Troop Royal Horse Artillery musical drive, and a traditional Christmas finale. The Puissance high-jump competition, featuring the famous red wall, is a longstanding highlight.
