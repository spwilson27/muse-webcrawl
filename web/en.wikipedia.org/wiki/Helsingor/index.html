Helsingor

Helsingor is the unaccented (anglicised/ASCII) spelling of Helsingør, the Danish port city at the northern entrance to the Øresund, and also the Swedish name-form (Swedish: Helsingör) of the same city. This spelling is used in English-language and international contexts where the Danish letter ø cannot be reproduced.

The city

Helsingør, known in English classically as Elsinore, is famous for Kronborg Castle, the setting of Shakespeare's Hamlet, and historically for collecting the Sound Dues from shipping. Ferries run across the strait to Helsingborg, Sweden.
