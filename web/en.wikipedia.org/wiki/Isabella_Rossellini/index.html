Isabella Fiorella Elettra Giovanna Rossellini (born 18 June 1952) is an Italian actress, model and filmmaker. The daughter of director Roberto Rossellini and actress Ingrid Bergman, she was for 14 years the face of Lancôme cosmetics and achieved film prominence in David Lynch's Blue Velvet (1986).

Early life and modelling

Raised in Rome and Paris, she moved to New York, worked as a translator and journalist, and began modelling in her late twenties, appearing on many Vogue covers photographed by Richard Avedon and others. Her Lancôme contract, begun in 1982 and ended amid ageism controversy in the 1990s, was famously renewed in 2016 when she was in her sixties.

Film career

Early roles included White Nights (1985); as the tortured singer Dorothy Vallens in Blue Velvet she won critical acclaim, followed by Wild at Heart, Death Becomes Her, Fearless, Immortal Beloved and Two Lovers. She received a Golden Globe nomination for Crime of the Century (1996). Later work includes Green Porno (2008), her whimsical short-film series on animal reproduction which she wrote and performed, and recent acclaimed roles in La Chimera (2023) and Conclave (2024), earning an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actress for the latter.

Other work

Rossellini holds a master's degree in animal behaviour, runs an organic farm on Long Island, serves on conservation boards, and has written memoirs including Some of Me and My Chickens and I.
