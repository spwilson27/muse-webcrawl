ASME Y14.1

ASME Y14.1
Title: Decimal Inch Drawing Sheet Size and Format
Publisher: ASME (American Society of Mechanical Engineers)
Series: Y14 engineering drawing standards

ASME Y14.1 is a standard of the ASME Y14 series on engineering drawing practices, titled Decimal Inch Drawing Sheet Size and Format. It establishes the sizes and formats used for engineering drawing sheets in decimal inch units.

Content

The standard defines the standard US drawing sheet sizes (commonly designated A through E, and roll sizes), together with requirements for sheet margins, borders, zoning, and the location and content of title blocks and revision blocks. A companion standard, Y14.1M, covers metric drawing sheet size and format.

Context

The Y14 series as a whole governs how engineering drawings are created and interpreted; its best-known member is ASME Y14.5, covering dimensioning and tolerancing. Y14.1's sheet formats underpin the consistent exchange of drawings across industry.
