List of Christian denominations

A Christian denomination is a distinct religious body within Christianity, identified by traits such as a name, history, organisation and doctrine. This list of Christian denominations groups the major traditions and their principal branches; estimates of the total number of distinct denominations worldwide run into the tens of thousands.

Catholicism

The Catholic Church, the largest Christian communion with over 1.3 billion baptised members, comprises the Latin Church and 23 Eastern Catholic Churches in full communion with the Pope, including the Ukrainian Greek Catholic Church, the Maronite Church and the Syro-Malabar Church. Independent Catholic movements include the Old Catholic churches of the Union of Utrecht and various sedevacantist groups.

Eastern Christianity

The Eastern Orthodox Church includes the autocephalous churches of Constantinople, Alexandria, Antioch, Jerusalem, Russia, Serbia, Romania, Bulgaria, Georgia, Greece and others. The Oriental Orthodox communion includes the Coptic, Armenian Apostolic, Syriac Orthodox, Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo, Eritrean and Malankara churches. The Church of the East tradition survives in the Assyrian Church of the East and the Ancient Church of the East.

Protestantism

Major Protestant families include the Lutheran churches (grouped largely in the Lutheran World Federation), the Reformed/Calvinist tradition (Presbyterian and Congregational churches), Anglicanism (the Church of England and the worldwide Anglican Communion), Baptists, Methodists (including the United Methodist Church), Pentecostals (such as the Assemblies of God), Adventists (notably the Seventh-day Adventist Church), Anabaptists (Mennonites, Amish, Hutterites), Quakers, the Moravian Church, the Salvation Army and numerous evangelical and non-denominational churches.

Restorationism and other movements

The Latter Day Saint movement, founded by Joseph Smith, includes The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and the Community of Christ. Other restorationist groups include Jehovah's Witnesses, the Iglesia ni Cristo, Christian Science and Unitarian Universalist churches with Christian roots.
