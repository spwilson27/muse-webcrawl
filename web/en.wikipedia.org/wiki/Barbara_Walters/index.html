Barbara Walters

Barbara Walters
Born25 September 1929, Boston
Died30 December 2022, New York City
OccupationBroadcast journalist (ABC News)
[Image: Walters on the set of one of her interview specials]

Barbara Walters (1929–2022) was an American broadcast journalist who broke television's gender barriers: the first woman to co-host a network evening newscast (ABC Evening News, 1976) and a pioneer of the celebrity and newsmaker interview, fronting 20/20 and creating The View (1997).

Interviews

Walters' specials — with presidents, world leaders and stars — defined the confessional television interview; her interviews with Monica Lewinsky (1999) and others drew enormous audiences. She retired from daily television in 2014.
