Ensenada is a municipality in the Mexican state of Baja California, with its seat at the port city of Ensenada on the Pacific coast, about 125 kilometres south of the United States border. Before the creation of San Quintín Municipality in 2020, Ensenada was the largest municipality in Mexico by area; it remains one of the largest, covering a large part of the central Baja California peninsula.

Geography and communities

The municipality includes the city of Ensenada, the Valle de Guadalupe wine region — Mexico's principal wine-producing area — the agricultural valleys around Maneadero, and stretches of both Pacific coastline and desert interior. The offshore Isla Todos Santos, known for big-wave surfing, lies in the bay. Communities include the municipal seat and delegations such as Maneadero, El Sauzal and Francisco Zarco.

Economy

The economy rests on the deep-water port (a major stop for cruise ships and a container and fishing port), commercial fishing and fish processing, agriculture in the irrigated valleys, wine production and tourism in Valle de Guadalupe, and off-road racing: the Baja 500 and the finish of the Baja 1000 are associated with Ensenada. The municipality had a population of 443,807 at the 2020 census.
