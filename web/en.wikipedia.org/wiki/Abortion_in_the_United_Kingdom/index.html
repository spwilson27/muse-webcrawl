Abortion in the United Kingdom

Abortion in the United Kingdom is regulated principally by the Abortion Act 1967 in England, Scotland and Wales, which permits abortion up to 24 weeks of pregnancy where two registered medical practitioners certify that one of the statutory grounds is met. Northern Ireland's law was liberalised in 2019–2020, replacing the near-total prohibition that had previously applied there.

Legal framework

Abortion remains technically an offence under the Offences Against the Person Act 1861, but the Abortion Act 1967 provides a defence where the statutory conditions are satisfied. The most commonly used ground (Ground C) is that the pregnancy has not exceeded 24 weeks and that continuing it would involve greater risk to the physical or mental health of the pregnant woman than termination. There is no time limit where there is a substantial risk of serious fetal abnormality (Ground E) or a grave risk to the woman's life. The 1967 Act never extended to Northern Ireland; following votes at Westminster during the suspension of the Northern Ireland Assembly, abortion there was decriminalised in October 2019 and a regulatory framework followed in 2020.

Provision

Most abortions in England and Wales are funded by the NHS and carried out by independent sector providers such as BPAS and MSI Reproductive Choices. Early medical abortion is the dominant method; since 2020, "pills by post" telemedicine for early medical abortion has been permanently permitted in England and Wales following a temporary COVID-era measure.

Statistics

Government statistics recorded approximately 251,000 abortions for residents of England and Wales in 2022, the highest number since records began, with an age-standardised rate of about 20 per 1,000 women aged 15–44. Scotland records roughly 16,000–18,000 terminations per year.

Political debate

Abortion has generally been treated as a matter of conscience in Parliament, with free votes. Periodic debates concern the 24-week limit, decriminalisation proposals that would remove abortion from criminal law entirely, and buffer zones around clinics, which were legislated for in England and Wales in 2023 and in Scotland in 2024.
