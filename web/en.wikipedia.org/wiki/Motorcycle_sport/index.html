Motorcycle sport

Grand Prix motorcycle racing

Motorcycle sport encompasses all sporting aspects of motorcycling. The various disciplines are not all races, and several involve tests of skill, endurance, or observed control rather than outright speed. Most motorcycle sport worldwide is governed by the Fédération Internationale de Motocyclisme (FIM).

Road racing

Road racing takes place on paved circuits or closed public roads. Its premier class is Grand Prix motorcycle racing (MotoGP, with Moto2 and Moto3 support classes), alongside the Superbike World Championship for production-based machines. Traditional road races on public roads include the Isle of Man TT and the North West 200.

Off-road disciplines

Off-road sports include motocross and its indoor variant supercross, enduro, cross-country rallies such as the Dakar Rally, motorcycle trials, in which riders negotiate obstacles without touching the ground, and flat track racing on oval dirt circuits.

Track and other disciplines

Motorcycle speedway is raced on short dirt ovals with bikes that have no brakes, deliberately sliding through corners. Other disciplines include drag racing, hill climbing, ice racing, and land speed record attempts.
