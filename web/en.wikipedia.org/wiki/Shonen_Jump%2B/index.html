Shonen Jump+

PublisherShueisha
Launched22 September 2014
TypeDigital manga magazine/app

Shonen Jump+ (少年ジャンプ+, stylised Shōnen Jump+) is a Japanese online manga magazine and mobile application operated by Shueisha, launched in September 2014 as a digital companion to Weekly Shōnen Jump. It serialises original manga, spin-offs of Jump series, and digital editions of the print magazine's content.

Content and hits

The platform mixes free rotating chapters with new serialisations, and has produced major hits of its own, including Spy × Family, Kaiju No. 8, Dandadan, Chainsaw Man Part 2, and Hell's Paradise: Jigokuraku. Several Jump+ series have received anime adaptations and rank among the best-selling manga of their years, marking a shift of hit-making power from print to digital-first publication.

Manga Plus and global reach

Most Jump+ series are released internationally, often simultaneously with Japan, through Shueisha's Manga Plus service in English and other languages. The platform also runs newcomer contests and one-shot programmes that feed new creators into the Jump line.
