Nissan 350Z

The Nissan 350Z (known in Japan as the Nissan Fairlady Z, model code Z33) is a two-seat sports car produced by Nissan from 2002 to 2009. It revived the Z-car line after the 300ZX's withdrawal from many markets, and was conceived under design chief Ajay Panchal's team as an affordable, front-mid-engine, rear-wheel-drive coupé.

Specifications

The 350Z used the 3.5-litre VQ35DE V6 (later the higher-revving VQ35HR from 2007), producing roughly 287–306 hp depending on year and market, with a six-speed manual or five-speed automatic transmission. A roadster version followed in 2004, and the track-focused Nismo 350Z in 2007.

Reception

Priced aggressively (from about $26,000 in the US at launch), the 350Z was a commercial and critical success, winning numerous comparison tests and becoming a staple of drifting and tuning culture. It was succeeded by the 370Z in 2009. See also Nissan Fairlady Z and Nissan Z.
