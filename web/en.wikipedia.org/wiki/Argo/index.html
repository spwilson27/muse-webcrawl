Argo

In Greek mythology, the Argo was the ship on which Jason and the Argonauts sailed from Iolcos to Colchis to retrieve the Golden Fleece.

Myth

The ship was built by Argus and guided by the goddess Athena. It had a special piece of timber in its prow from the sacred forest of Dodona, which could speak and render prophecies. The crew, the Argonauts, included Heracles, Orpheus, Castor and Pollux, and Atalanta in some versions.

Voyage

Adventures included the Lemnian women, the Harpies and Phineus, the Symplegades (clashing rocks), and arrival in Colchis, where with Medea's help Jason obtained the Golden Fleece.

Aftermath

After the journey the Argo was consecrated to Poseidon in the Isthmus of Corinth. Later, Jason died when a rotting piece of the ship fell on him. The constellation Argo Navis commemorated the ship, later divided into Carina, Puppis and Vela.
