Hylotelephium

Hylotelephium is a genus of flowering plants in the stonecrop family, Crassulaceae. Its roughly 33 species are succulent perennials native to Asia, Europe, and North America. They were formerly included in the genus Sedum and are still widely known to gardeners as sedums or stonecrops.

Description

Hylotelephium species have fleshy, water-storing leaves on upright stems and produce dense, flat-topped clusters of small star-shaped flowers, usually pink, white, or red, in late summer and autumn. The best known is the showy stonecrop, Hylotelephium spectabile (syn. Sedum spectabile), and its hybrid Hylotelephium 'Herbstfreude' (Autumn Joy), among the most popular border perennials. Orpine, Hylotelephium telephium, gives the genus its name.

Cultivation

These plants are valued for drought tolerance, late-season flowers attractive to bees and butterflies, and seed heads that persist into winter. They require full sun and well-drained soil and are easily propagated by division or stem cuttings.
