2019 Rugby World Cup

2019 Rugby World Cup
Host: Japan
Dates: 20 September – 2 November 2019
Teams: 20
Matches: 45 (3 cancelled)
Champions: South Africa (3rd title)
Runners-up: England
Third place: New Zealand

The 2019 Rugby World Cup was the ninth edition of the Rugby World Cup, held in Japan from 20 September to 2 November 2019. It was the first World Cup held in Asia and the first held outside the traditional heartlands of the sport.

Tournament

Twenty teams competed across twelve venues. Hosts Japan produced memorable wins over Ireland and Scotland to top their pool and reach the quarter-finals for the first time, before losing to South Africa. Typhoon Hagibis forced the cancellation of three pool matches — the first cancellations in tournament history. England upset two-time defending champions New Zealand in the semi-finals, while South Africa edged Wales.

Final

In the final at Yokohama, South Africa defeated England 32–12, with tries from Makazole Mapimpi and Cheslin Kolbe and 22 points from Handré Pollard. The Springboks, captained by Siya Kolisi, lifted the Webb Ellis Cup for the third time, equalling New Zealand's record.
