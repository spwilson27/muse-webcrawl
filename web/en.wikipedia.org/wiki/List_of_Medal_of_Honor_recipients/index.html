List of Medal of Honor recipients

The Medal of Honor was created during the American Civil War and is the highest military decoration presented by the United States government to a member of the armed forces. Recipients must have distinguished themselves at the risk of their own life above and beyond the call of duty in action against an enemy of the United States. More than 3,500 Medals of Honor have been awarded, to over 3,400 individuals; 19 people have received the medal twice.

Double recipients

Nineteen men have received two Medals of Honor, including Thomas Custer (brother of George Armstrong Custer), Marine Corps legends Dan Daly and Smedley Butler, and five Marines who received both the Army and Navy versions for the same action in World War I.

Other notable facts

  • The youngest recipient was Willie Johnston, a drummer boy aged about 13 in the Civil War.
  • Charles Lindbergh received the medal as a civilian for his 1927 transatlantic flight.
  • The medal was revoked from 911 recipients by a 1916–17 review board; several awards were later restored, including those of Mary Walker and William F. Cody.
  • Theodore Roosevelt was awarded the medal posthumously in 2001 for his charge up San Juan Hill; his son Theodore Roosevelt Jr. received it for Utah Beach on D-Day — one of only two father-son pairs.
