Uranus

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun, a pale cyan ice giant discovered by William Herschel in 1781 — the first planet found with a telescope. Its axis is tilted by about 98 degrees, so it effectively rolls around the Sun on its side, probably after an ancient collision. Uranus is the coldest planetary atmosphere recorded (as low as −224 °C) despite a relatively featureless appearance.

Contents1. Exploration and moons

Exploration and moons

Voyager 2 (1986) is the only spacecraft visit. Uranus has rings and 28+ known moons named after Shakespearean and Pope characters — Miranda, Ariel, Umbriel, Titania and Oberon largest. A dedicated Uranus orbiter has been ranked a top priority for a future flagship mission.
