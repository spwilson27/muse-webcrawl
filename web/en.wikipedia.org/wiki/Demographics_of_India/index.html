Demographics of India

Population density of India by district

India is the most populous country in the world, having overtaken China in 2023 according to United Nations estimates, with a population of more than 1.4 billion — roughly a sixth of humanity. The demographics of India are remarkable for their diversity of languages, religions and ethnic groups.

Population characteristics

India's population is relatively young, with a median age under 30. About two-thirds of Indians live in rural areas, though urbanization is rapid and cities such as Mumbai, Delhi and Bangalore count tens of millions of inhabitants. The 2011 census recorded a population of 1.21 billion and a literacy rate of about 73%, with wide variation between states — Kerala achieves near-universal literacy while some northern states lag considerably.

Religion and language

According to the 2011 census, about 79.8% of the population is Hindu, 14.2% Muslim, with Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Jains and others making up the remainder. India has 22 constitutionally recognized languages; Hindi is the most widely spoken first language (about 44%), followed by Bengali, Marathi, Telugu and Tamil. English serves as an associate official language and a lingua franca of government and commerce.

Trends

Fertility has fallen to around replacement level nationally, though it remains above replacement in states such as Bihar and Uttar Pradesh and well below it in the south, raising questions about future political representation and inter-state migration.
