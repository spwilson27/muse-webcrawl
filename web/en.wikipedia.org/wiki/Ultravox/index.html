Ultravox were a British new wave band formed in London in April 1974 as Tiger Lily. Led first by John Foxx and later by Midge Ure, they became one of the most successful British electronic bands of the early 1980s, best known for the 1981 hit single "Vienna".

Ultravox during their reunion tour, 2009.

History

John Foxx era (1974–1979)

Signed to Island Records, the band (briefly styled Ultravox!) released three albums — Ultravox! (1977), Ha!-Ha!-Ha! (1977), and Systems of Romance (1978) — blending punk energy with synthesisers, glam and Kraftwerk-inspired electronics. Despite critical respect and influence on later synth-pop, commercial success eluded them; Foxx left for a solo career in 1979.

Midge Ure era (1979–1988)

Guitarist and singer Midge Ure, recruited after working with keyboardist Billy Currie in Visage, fronted a re-formed Ultravox that signed to Chrysalis. The album Vienna (1980) produced the title single, which reached No. 2 in the UK in early 1981 and became one of the defining songs of the New Romantic era. Further hits followed with albums Rage in Eden (1981), Quartet (1982, produced by George Martin), and Lament (1984), including singles such as "Hymn", "Visions in Blue", and "Dancing with Tears in My Eyes". The band split in 1988.

Later activity

A Currie-led version recorded two albums in the 1990s without chart success. The classic Ure–Currie–Cross–Cann line-up reunited in 2008, touring and releasing the album Brilliant in 2012, before activity ceased in 2017; bassist Chris Cross died in 2024.
