Jazz à Vienne

Image placeholder: Jazz à Vienne

Jazz à Vienne is a jazz festival held annually in Vienne, in the Isère department of south-eastern France, since 1981. It is one of the largest and best-known jazz festivals in Europe, lasting about two weeks at the end of June and beginning of July and drawing audiences in the hundreds of thousands.

History and venue

The festival's main stage is the ancient Roman theatre of Vienne (Théâtre Antique), a first-century venue carved into the hillside overlooking the Rhône, with a capacity of about 7,500. The festival was founded in 1981 and has hosted many of the leading names in jazz, blues, soul, and world music. Concerts also take place at other venues around the town, including the Jardin de Cybèle, which hosts free afternoon performances, and late-night club sessions.

Programme

Jazz à Vienne has featured artists such as Miles Davis, Ella Fitzgerald, Dizzy Gillespie, Herbie Hancock, and many contemporary performers across jazz and related genres. The festival also runs the RéZZo Focal Jazz à Vienne springboard competition for emerging groups and an academy for young musicians.
