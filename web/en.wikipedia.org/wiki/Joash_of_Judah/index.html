Joash of Judah

Joash
Reignc. 836–796 BCE
PredecessorAthaliah
SuccessorAmaziah
FatherAhaziah of Judah

Joash (also Jehoash), was the eighth king of Judah, and the only surviving son of Ahaziah after the massacre of the royal family ordered by his grandmother Athaliah. As an infant he was hidden in the Temple of Solomon by his aunt Jehosheba for six years, while Athaliah reigned.

In the seventh year, the high priest Jehoiada staged a coup, crowned the seven-year-old Joash, and had Athaliah executed. Guided by Jehoiada, Joash restored worship of Yahweh, destroyed the temple of Baal, and organised repairs to the Jerusalem Temple funded by a chest placed at its entrance.

Later reign and death

After Jehoiada's death, the biblical account says Joash turned to idolatry and ordered the stoning of Jehoiada's son Zechariah, who had protested. He later paid tribute to Hazael of Aram-Damascus from Temple treasures and was assassinated by his own officials at Beth Millo. He was succeeded by his son Amaziah.
