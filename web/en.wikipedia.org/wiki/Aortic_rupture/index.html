Aortic rupture
Specialty: Emergency medicine, vascular surgery
Causes: Ruptured aneurysm, trauma, dissection
Prognosis: Often fatal without immediate surgery

Aortic rupture is the rupture or break of the aorta, the largest artery in the body. It is distinct from aortic dissection, though dissection can progress to rupture.

Causes and presentation

Most commonly rupture of an aortic aneurysm, or blunt traumatic injury (e.g. rapid deceleration). Presents with severe chest/abdominal/back pain, hypotension, shock, and rapid blood loss into thoracic or abdominal cavity or retroperitoneum.

Treatment

Immediate resuscitation, permissive hypotension, massive transfusion, and emergency open or endovascular repair. Mortality exceeds 80% for free rupture before reaching hospital in many series.
