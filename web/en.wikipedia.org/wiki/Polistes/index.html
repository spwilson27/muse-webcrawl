Polistes

Polistes
Family: Vespidae
Subfamily: Polistinae
Common name: Paper wasps

Polistes is a cosmopolitan genus of social wasps and the most common type of paper wasp in North America. The genus contains more than 200 species. They build nests of a papery material made from chewed plant fibres, typically hanging by a single stalk under eaves and branches.

Behaviour

Colonies are founded in spring by one or more overwintered queens (foundresses). Polistes colonies are relatively small, usually with fewer than 100 workers, and dominance hierarchies among foundresses determine who lays most eggs. Unlike yellowjackets, Polistes wasps are generally not aggressive away from the nest.

Notable species

Polistes carolina and Polistes dominula (the European paper wasp) are widespread and frequently encountered around human dwellings.
