Queen Mathilde of Belgium

Queen Mathilde

Born: 20 January 1973, Uccle, Belgium
Spouse: Philippe of Belgium
Consort since: 21 July 2013

Mathilde (born Mathilde Marie Christine Ghislaine d'Udekem d'Acoz, 20 January 1973) is Queen of the Belgians as the wife of King Philippe, who ascended the throne on 21 July 2013 following the abdication of King Albert II.

Early life and career

She was born in Uccle into the Belgian nobility, daughter of Count Patrick d'Udekem d'Acoz. She studied speech therapy at the Institut libre Marie Haps in Brussels and worked as a speech therapist, and later earned a master's degree in psychology from the Université catholique de Louvain.

Marriage and role

She married Prince Philippe, Duke of Brabant, on 4 December 1999 in Brussels. The couple have four children: Princess Elisabeth, Duchess of Brabant; Prince Gabriel; Prince Emmanuel; and Princess Eléonore. As queen, Mathilde focuses on issues including child welfare, mental health, education, and sustainable development, and serves as an advocate for the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.
