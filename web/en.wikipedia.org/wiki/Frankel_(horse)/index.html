Frankel (horse)

Frankel (foaled 11 February 2008) is a retired champion British Thoroughbred racehorse and an active sire. Frankel was unbeaten in his 14-race career and was the highest-rated racehorse in the world from May 2011. He was trained by Henry Cecil, ridden in all his races by Tom Queally, and owned by Khalid Abdullah of Juddmonte Farms, who named him after the American trainer Bobby Frankel.

Racing career

Frankel won all five of his races as a two-year-old, culminating in the Dewhurst Stakes. At three he won the 2000 Guineas by six lengths in one of the most celebrated performances in the race's history, and went on to win the St James's Palace Stakes, the Sussex Stakes (the "Duel on the Downs" against Canford Cliffs), the Queen Elizabeth II Stakes, and, at four, the Lockinge Stakes, Queen Anne Stakes, a second Sussex Stakes, the International Stakes at York — his first race over ten furlongs — and finally the Champion Stakes at Ascot in October 2012, after which he was retired to stud.

Assessment and stud career

Timeform rated Frankel 147, the highest rating the organisation had ever awarded, and the World Thoroughbred Rankings gave him 140. As a stallion at Banstead Manor Stud, he has sired numerous Group 1 winners including Cracksman, Adayar, Hurricane Lane, and Inspiral, quickly establishing himself as one of the world's leading sires.
