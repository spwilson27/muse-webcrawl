Beta testing

Beta testing is a method of software testing in which a version of the software, known as a beta version, is released to a limited audience outside the programming team. It is one of the stages of the software release life cycle, coming after alpha testing and before the release candidate and general availability stages.

Purpose

The goal of beta testing is to expose the software to realistic usage, diverse hardware and operating conditions, and user behaviour that internal testing cannot replicate. Testers report defects and provide feedback on features and usability, which developers use to decide whether the product is ready for release.

Practices

Beta programmes are managed through feedback channels, automated crash reporting and staged rollouts. In consumer software, "beta" versions may be distributed through app-store testing programmes; in games, early access programmes combine beta testing with commercial release.
