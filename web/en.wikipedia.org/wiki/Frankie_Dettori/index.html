Lanfranco "Frankie" Dettori (born 15 December 1970) is an Italian jockey who was based in the United Kingdom for most of his career and is one of the most successful and popular riders in the history of horse racing. He is famed for his flamboyant personality, his trademark flying dismount, and for riding all seven winners on a single card at Ascot in 1996 — the "Magnificent Seven."

Career

Dettori was born in Milan, the son of Gianfranco Dettori, a champion Italian jockey. He left school at 13 to become a stable boy and apprentice, riding his first winner in Turin in 1986. Moving to Britain, he became apprentice to trainer Luca Cumani in Newmarket and quickly rose to prominence, becoming British Champion Jockey in 1994, 1995, and 2004.

On 28 September 1996, at the Festival of British Racing at Ascot, Dettori rode the winner of all seven races on the card — an achievement estimated to have cost bookmakers tens of millions of pounds and one of the most famous feats in racing history.

Dettori was retained rider for the Godolphin operation for eighteen years until their split in 2012, after which he rode as a freelancer, notably for trainer John Gosden. His big-race victories include six wins in the Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe — most memorably on the great filly Enable in 2017 and 2018 — and two Epsom Derbys, on Authorized (2007) and Golden Horn (2015).

Later years

In 2012 Dettori served a six-month ban after testing positive for a prohibited substance in France. He announced in late 2022 that he would retire at the end of 2023, then reversed the decision and relocated to California to continue riding in the United States, where he has continued to win major stakes races.
