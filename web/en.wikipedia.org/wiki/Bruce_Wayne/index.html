Bruce Wayne is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, commonly in association with the superhero Batman: Bruce Wayne is Batman's secret identity. Created by artist Bob Kane and writer Bill Finger, he first appeared in Detective Comics #27 in 1939.

Fictional biography

Bruce Wayne is the wealthy owner of Wayne Enterprises and the heir of the Wayne family of Gotham City. As a child he witnessed the murder of his parents, Dr. Thomas Wayne and Martha Wayne, in a street robbery — the defining trauma of his life. He swore an oath to wage war on criminals and spent years travelling the world, training his body and mind in martial arts, detective work, science and criminology. Inspired to strike fear into criminals by the image of a bat, he adopted the costumed identity of Batman, operating from the Batcave beneath Wayne Manor with the aid of his butler Alfred Pennyworth and allies including Robin and Commissioner James Gordon.

Characterisation

In public, Wayne cultivates the image of a carefree billionaire playboy and philanthropist, funding good causes through the Wayne Foundation, in order to deflect suspicion from his night-time activities. The character is defined by his refusal to kill or use firearms, his obsessive discipline and his dual identity.
