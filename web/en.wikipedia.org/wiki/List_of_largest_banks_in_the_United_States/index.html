List of largest banks in the United States

The List of largest banks in the United States ranks American banking institutions by total assets. The industry is highly concentrated: the four largest banks — JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, Citigroup, and Wells Fargo — each hold trillions of dollars in assets and are designated global systemically important banks. Figures are based on Federal Reserve statistical releases of large commercial banks.

Structure of the industry

The United States has roughly four thousand FDIC-insured commercial banks, down from more than 14,000 in the 1980s, owing to decades of consolidation following interstate banking deregulation. Regional bank failures in 2023 — Silicon Valley Bank, Signature Bank, and First Republic — prompted renewed scrutiny of mid-sized institutions and deposit insurance.
