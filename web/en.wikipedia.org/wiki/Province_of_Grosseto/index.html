Province of Grosseto

The Province of Grosseto (Italian: Provincia di Grosseto) is a province in the Tuscany region of Italy, the largest province in the region by area. Its capital is the city of Grosseto.

Geography

The province covers the Maremma coastal plain, the Argentario promontory, hills including the Colline Metallifere, and the slopes of Monte Amiata. It includes part of the Tuscan Archipelago, such as Giglio Island. Tourism, agriculture and wine production (Morellino di Scansano) are important to its economy.
