American Hustle is a 2013 American black comedy crime film directed by David O. Russell, loosely inspired by the FBI's Abscam operation of the late 1970s and early 1980s. Christian Bale and Amy Adams star as con artists Irving Rosenfeld and Sydney Prosser, who are forced by FBI agent Richie DiMaso (Bradley Cooper) to run an elaborate sting targeting corrupt politicians, including Camden mayor Carmine Polito (Jeremy Renner). Jennifer Lawrence plays Irving's unpredictable wife, Rosalyn.

Plot

Caught running a loan scam, Irving and Sydney agree to help DiMaso entrap other criminals. The operation expands from petty fraud to bribing public officials with money from a fictitious Arab sheik, entangling a well-meaning mayor and, dangerously, the Mafia. Relationships fracture as schemes layer upon schemes, until Irving negotiates a way out that limits the damage to his friends.

Reception

The film was a critical and commercial success, praised for its performances and direction. It received ten Academy Award nominations, including Best Picture, Best Director, and all four acting categories, though it won none. It won three Golden Globe Awards, including Best Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy, and the BAFTA for Best Original Screenplay.
