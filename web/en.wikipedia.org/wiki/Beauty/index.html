Beauty

Beauty
FieldAesthetics / philosophy of art
Related conceptsSublime, taste, aesthetic judgement
[Image: A classical marble statue of Aphrodite, the Greek ideal of beauty]

Beauty is a property or quality of objects, persons, places and ideas that provides pleasure to the senses or mind. Whether beauty is objective (in the object), subjective (in the eye of the beholder), or relational has been debated since antiquity: Plato made Beauty itself one of the eternal Forms, medieval thinkers listed it among the transcendentals with truth and goodness, and Hume and Kant analysed it as a judgement of taste claiming universal agreement without concepts.

Theories

Classical accounts tie beauty to symmetry, proportion and unity-in-variety; evolutionary psychologists to signals of health and fitness; cultural critics to power and convention. Beauty standards demonstrably vary across eras and cultures — and demonstrably converge in some respects (faces, landscapes), keeping the philosophical question alive in Aesthetics and everyday life alike.
