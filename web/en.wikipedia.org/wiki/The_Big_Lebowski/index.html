The Big Lebowski

The Big Lebowski is a 1998 American crime comedy film written, produced and directed by Joel Coen, with Ethan Coen also credited. It stars Jeff Bridges as Jeffrey "The Dude" Lebowski, a laid-back Los Angeles slacker and avid bowler who becomes entangled in a kidnapping plot after being mistaken for a millionaire of the same name.

Plot

Two thugs urinate on the Dude's rug while seeking money owed to millionaire Jeffrey Lebowski. Seeking compensation, the Dude is drawn into a scheme involving the millionaire's young wife, a ransom delivery, nihilists, and his bowling partners Walter Sobchak and Donny Kerabatsos. The convoluted case unravels with the revelation that the kidnapping was staged.

Legacy

A modest box-office performer on release, the film became a major cult classic, inspiring the annual Lebowski Fest, the joke religion Dudeism, and induction into the National Film Registry in 2014. The spin-off The Jesus Rolls was released in 2019.
