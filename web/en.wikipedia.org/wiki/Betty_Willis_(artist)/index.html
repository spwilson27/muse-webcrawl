Betty Willis (May 20, 1923 – April 19, 2015) was an American graphic designer best known for designing the iconic "Welcome to Fabulous Las Vegas" sign in 1959.

Early life and career

Willis was born in Overton, Nevada. After studying art in California, she returned to Las Vegas and worked as a commercial artist, eventually joining the sign company Western Neon.

The sign

In 1959, Western Neon was commissioned by Clark County to build a welcome sign for the south end of the Las Vegas Strip. Willis designed a horizontal diamond shape evocative of Googie architecture, with red neon lettering reading "Welcome to Fabulous Las Vegas," individual white neon circles containing blue stars, and a red starburst on top representing happiness. The reverse side reads "Drive Carefully" and "Come Back Soon." Willis never copyrighted the design, calling it her gift to the city; the image entered the public domain and became one of the most reproduced symbols of Las Vegas.

Later life and legacy

Willis continued working in sign design into her later years. The sign was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 2009. She died at her home in Overton on April 19, 2015, at the age of 91.
