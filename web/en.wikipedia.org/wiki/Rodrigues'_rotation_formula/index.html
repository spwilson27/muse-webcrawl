Rodrigues' rotation formula

In three-dimensional geometry, Rodrigues' rotation formula, named after Olinde Rodrigues, gives an efficient method for rotating a vector about an axis.

If v is a vector in ℝ³ and k is a unit vector along the rotation axis, and θ is the angle of rotation (by the right-hand rule), then the rotated vector is:

vrot = v cosθ + (k × v) sinθ + k (k · v)(1 − cosθ).

The formula follows from decomposing v into components parallel and perpendicular to k. In matrix form it produces the rotation matrix R = I + (sinθ)K + (1 − cosθ)K², where K is the cross-product matrix of k, and is equivalent to the matrix exponential exp(θK). It is widely used in computer graphics, robotics and orbital mechanics, and relates closely to quaternions and the axis–angle representation.
