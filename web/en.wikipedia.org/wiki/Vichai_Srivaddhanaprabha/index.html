Vichai Srivaddhanaprabha

Vichai Srivaddhanaprabha (born Vichai Raksriaksorn; 4 April 1958 – 27 October 2018) was a Thai billionaire businessman, founder of the duty-free retailer King Power, and owner of Leicester City Football Club.

Career

Vichai founded King Power in 1989, which grew into Thailand's dominant duty-free business operating at the country's major airports. In 2010 his consortium bought Leicester City; under his ownership, and with the club's debt converted to equity, Leicester won the Championship in 2014 and then, at odds of 5000–1, the 2015–16 Premier League title — one of the most celebrated upsets in sporting history. He also bought Belgian club OH Leuven in 2017 and was a keen polo player.

Death

On 27 October 2018, the helicopter carrying Vichai crashed shortly after taking off from the King Power Stadium following a match, killing him and the four others on board. The outpouring of grief in Leicester was immense; a memorial garden and a statue of Vichai now stand at the stadium, and his son Aiyawatt succeeded him as chairman.
