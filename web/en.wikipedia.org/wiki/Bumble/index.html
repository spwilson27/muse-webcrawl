Bumble

Bumble is an online dating and networking application launched in 2014. In heterosexual matches, it requires women users to make the first contact with matched male users, while in same-sex matches either person can send a message first. The app was founded by Whitney Wolfe Herd, a co-founder of Tinder, together with Andrey Andreev, founder of Badoo.

Features

Users swipe right to like or left to pass on profiles. A match occurs when two users have liked each other; in different-sex matches the woman has 24 hours to send the first message before the match expires. Beyond dating, Bumble offers modes for finding friends (Bumble BFF) and professional networking (Bumble Bizz).

Company

Bumble Inc., headquartered in Austin, Texas, completed an initial public offering on the Nasdaq in February 2021. The company also owns the dating apps Badoo and Fruitz. Wolfe Herd became, at the time of the IPO, one of the youngest self-made female billionaires.
