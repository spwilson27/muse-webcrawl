Life expectancy

Life expectancy
DefinitionAverage period a person may expect to live
Global averageAbout 73 years (recent UN/WHO estimates)
[Image: An elderly couple walking in a park holding hands, autumn light]

Life expectancy is a statistical measure of the average time an organism is expected to live, based on year of birth, current age and demographic factors. Global life expectancy rose from about 47 years in 1950 to roughly 73 before the pandemic knocked it back — public health's proudest graph.

Gaps

It ranges from the mid-80s (Japan, Switzerland) to the mid-50s in the poorest countries, and varies by income, sex and race within countries. Retirement ages, pensions and the old-age care systems all key off this number.
