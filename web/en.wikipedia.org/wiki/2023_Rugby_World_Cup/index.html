The 2023 Rugby World Cup was the tenth men's Rugby World Cup, held in France from 8 September to 28 October 2023. Defending champions South Africa retained the title, defeating New Zealand 12–11 in the final at the Stade de France in Saint-Denis to become the first team to win the tournament four times.

Tournament

Twenty teams competed in nine stadiums across France, with Chile making their World Cup debut. The pool stage produced notable upsets, including Fiji's victory over Australia, which contributed to the Wallabies' first-ever pool-stage elimination, and Portugal's first World Cup win, against Fiji.

Knockout stage

South Africa won three consecutive knockout matches by a single point: against hosts France in the quarter-finals, England in the semi-finals, and New Zealand in the final. In the final, New Zealand captain Sam Cane was sent off in the first half — the first red card in a Rugby World Cup final — while Handré Pollard kicked all of South Africa's points.

Bronze final

England defeated Argentina 26–23 in the third-place play-off.
