Qdoba is an American chain of fast casual restaurants serving Mexican-style cuisine, known for burritos, tacos, quesadillas and its signature three-cheese queso. It was founded in Denver, Colorado in 1995 by Anthony Miller and Robert Hauser under the name Zuma, and later renamed Z-Teca and then Qdoba Mexican Grill.

History

The chain was acquired by Jack in the Box in 2003 and expanded rapidly through franchising. In 2018 Jack in the Box sold Qdoba to funds managed by Apollo Global Management for about $305 million; in 2022 it was sold to Butterfly Equity. The chain competes most directly with Chipotle Mexican Grill.
