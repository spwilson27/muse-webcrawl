14K (十四K) is a triad group based in Hong Kong and active internationally. Founded in Guangzhou in 1945 by former Kuomintang general Kot Siu-wong, it is traditionally regarded as one of the largest triad societies, with numerous semi-autonomous factions.

History

The society originated among Kuomintang loyalists after the Chinese Civil War; the "14" is variously explained as referring to a Guangzhou address (14 Po Wah Road) and the "K" to "karat", symbolising the purity of its members' loyalty. After 1949 many members fled to Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan.

Activities

14K factions have been implicated in drug trafficking (notably heroin and methamphetamine), illegal gambling, loan-sharking, extortion and human smuggling, with a presence in Chinatowns across North America, Europe and Southeast Asia. Its loose structure contrasts with the more centralised Sun Yee On triad, its principal rival.
