The Ohio State University (Ohio State or OSU) is a public land-grant research university in Columbus, Ohio, United States. Founded in 1870 as the state's land-grant university under the Morrill Act of 1862, it is one of the largest universities in the United States by enrollment and a flagship institution of the University System of Ohio.

History

The university was chartered in 1870 as the Ohio Agricultural and Mechanical College and opened in 1873 on a farm north of Columbus; it adopted its present name in 1878. Under presidents including William Oxley Thompson the university broadened beyond agriculture into a comprehensive research institution. In the 20th century it grew into a major centre for research, medicine — through the Wexner Medical Center — and athletics.

Academics and campus

Ohio State offers more than 200 undergraduate majors across 15 colleges, including highly ranked programmes in business (Fisher College), engineering, law (Moritz College), medicine, and political science. The Columbus campus is organised around the Oval, a large green at its historic centre, and includes the Thompson Library, the Ohio Union, and Ohio Stadium. Regional campuses are located in Lima, Mansfield, Marion, Newark, and Wooster.
