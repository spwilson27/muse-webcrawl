Antisocial personality disorder

ASPD
Specialty: Psychiatry
Symptoms: Persistent disregard for rights of others, deceit, impulsivity
Onset: By age 15 as conduct disorder
ICD-10: F60.2

Antisocial personality disorder (ASPD) is a personality disorder characterised by a pervasive pattern of disregard for and violation of the rights of others, beginning in childhood or early adolescence and continuing into adulthood.

Diagnosis

DSM-5 criteria include failure to conform to social norms, deceitfulness, impulsivity, irritability and aggressiveness, reckless disregard for safety, consistent irresponsibility, and lack of remorse. Diagnosis requires age 18+ and evidence of conduct disorder before 15.

Causes and prevalence

Genetic and environmental factors, childhood adversity, and neurobiological differences contribute. Prevalence estimated 0.2–3.3% in general population, higher in prison populations.

Treatment

No medication is specifically approved; psychotherapy, including mentalization-based and schema approaches, shows limited evidence. Management focuses on comorbid conditions and risk reduction.
