Alejandro Fernández Abarca (born 24 April 1971) is a Mexican singer, the son of the legendary ranchera singer Vicente Fernández. Nicknamed El Potrillo ("The Little Colt"), he is one of the best-selling Latin music artists, with tens of millions of records sold, and has won multiple Latin Grammy Awards.

Early life and career

Born in Guadalajara, Fernández first performed on stage as a child alongside his father. He studied architecture before devoting himself to music, releasing his debut album in 1992. Early recordings focused on traditional mariachi and ranchera music, continuing his father's legacy.

Pop crossover

With the album Me estoy enamorando (1997), produced by Emilio Estefan, Fernández crossed over into Latin pop ballads and reached number one on the Billboard Latin charts, greatly expanding his audience while continuing to record ranchera albums such as México – Madrid: En directo y sin escalas. He has collaborated with artists including Gloria Estefan, Beyoncé, Christina Aguilera and Plácido Domingo.

Acting and legacy

Fernández has also acted in films, including a role portraying Emiliano Zapata in Zapata: El sueño de un héroe (2004). He has a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame and continues the Fernández musical dynasty alongside his children, several of whom are also singers.
