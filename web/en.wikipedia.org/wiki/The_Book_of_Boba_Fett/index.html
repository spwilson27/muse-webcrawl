The Book of Boba Fett

Created by: Jon Favreau
Starring: Temuera Morrison, Ming-Na Wen
Episodes: 7
Original release: December 29, 2021 – February 9, 2022 (Disney+)

The Book of Boba Fett is an American space Western television series created by Jon Favreau for the streaming service Disney+. It is part of the Star Wars franchise and a spin-off of The Mandalorian, following bounty hunter Boba Fett and mercenary Fennec Shand as they attempt to take control of the criminal territory on Tatooine formerly ruled by Jabba the Hutt.

Premise

Having survived the sarlacc pit, Boba Fett (Temuera Morrison) is rescued and adopted by a tribe of Tusken Raiders. In the present timeline, Fett seizes Jabba's palace and throne in Mos Espa and, with Fennec Shand (Ming-Na Wen), tries to rule the underworld "with respect" rather than fear — facing challenges from the Hutt twins, a Tusken massacre's aftermath, and ultimately the Pyke Syndicate. Several episodes feature the Mandalorian Din Djarin, Grogu, Luke Skywalker, and Ahsoka Tano, bridging the story toward the third season of The Mandalorian.

Production

The series was announced in a post-credits scene of The Mandalorian's second-season finale in December 2020. Robert Rodriguez, who directed Fett's return episode, executive produced and directed several episodes, alongside Favreau, Dave Filoni, Kathleen Kennedy, and Colin Wilson; Bryce Dallas Howard, Steph Green, and Kevin Tancharoen also directed. Ludwig Göransson composed the main theme, with Joseph Shirley writing the score.

Reception

The series received generally positive reviews for Morrison's performance, its action, and its connections to the wider saga, though some critics found the flashback structure and Fett's characterization uneven. The finale's guest appearances drew particular attention.
