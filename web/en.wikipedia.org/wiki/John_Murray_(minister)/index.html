John Murray (10 December 1741 – 3 September 1815) was a British-born minister who is regarded as one of the founders of American Universalism. He preached the doctrine of universal salvation — that God would ultimately save all people — and helped establish the first Universalist congregations in the United States.

Ministry

Originally a Calvinist Methodist associated with George Whitefield's movement in England, Murray was influenced by the Universalist writings of James Relly. He emigrated to America in 1770 and preached universal salvation in New Jersey and New England, often facing hostility. In 1779 he became pastor of the first Universalist church in Gloucester, Massachusetts.

Legacy

Murray served as a chaplain during the American Revolutionary War and in 1793 became minister of the Universalist society in Boston. He is often called the "father of American Universalism".
