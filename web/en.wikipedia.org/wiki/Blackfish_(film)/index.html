Blackfish is a 2013 American documentary film directed by Gabriela Cowperthwaite. It concerns Tilikum, an orca held by SeaWorld and involved in the deaths of three people, and examines the consequences of keeping orcas in captivity. The film premiered at the 2013 Sundance Film Festival and was released theatrically by Magnolia Pictures; it was broadcast by CNN Films.

Synopsis

The documentary focuses on the death of senior SeaWorld trainer Dawn Brancheau, who was killed by Tilikum in 2010, and argues that the stresses of captivity contributed to the orca's aggression. It features interviews with former SeaWorld trainers describing capture operations, incidents in which captive orcas injured or killed people, and the separation of calves from their mothers.

Impact

The film had a substantial public and commercial impact, sometimes called the "Blackfish effect": SeaWorld's attendance and share price declined, corporate partnerships were dropped, and proposed legislation in California sought to ban orca captivity for entertainment. In 2016 SeaWorld announced the end of its orca breeding programme and the phasing out of theatrical orca shows. SeaWorld has disputed the film's accuracy, calling it propaganda.
