Colorado

Colorado is a state in the western United States, known for encompassing most of the Southern Rocky Mountains. It is the eighth-largest state by area and the 21st-most populous, with about 5.9 million residents. Its capital and largest city is Denver.

Colorado has the highest average elevation of any US state, with 58 mountain peaks above 14,000 feet ('fourteeners') including Mount Elbert, and the Continental Divide running through it; the Colorado, Rio Grande, Arkansas and South Platte rivers rise here. Its economy spans mining, agriculture, defence, technology (the Boulder-Denver corridor) and tourism — ski resorts such as Aspen, Vail and Telluride, and Rocky Mountain, Mesa Verde and Great Sand Dunes national parks. Colorado was part of the Louisiana Purchase and the Mexican cession; the 1858 Pike's Peak Gold Rush founded Denver.
