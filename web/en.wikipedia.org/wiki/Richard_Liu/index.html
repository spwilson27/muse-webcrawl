Richard Liu

Richard Liu Qiangdong (born March 10, 1973) is a Chinese billionaire entrepreneur who founded JD.com, one of China's largest e-commerce companies, and served as its chairman and chief executive officer.

Early life

Liu was born in Suqian, Jiangsu, to a family of modest means. He studied sociology at Renmin University of China in Beijing, graduating in 1996, and taught himself computer programming. He later earned an EMBA from China Europe International Business School.

JD.com

In 1998 Liu founded a small shop selling magneto-optical products in Beijing's Zhongguancun district, named Jingdong after himself and his then girlfriend. After the SARS outbreak in 2003 closed his physical stores, he moved the business online, launching what became JD.com in 2004. The company grew into a major retailer known for its owned logistics network, fast delivery, and emphasis on authentic goods. JD listed on the NASDAQ in 2014 and in Hong Kong in 2020.

Later developments

Liu stepped down as CEO in 2022, succeeded by Xu Ran later, while remaining chairman for a time. In 2018 he was arrested in Minneapolis on suspicion of sexual misconduct; prosecutors declined to charge him, and a civil case was settled in 2022. He is married to Zhang Zetian.
