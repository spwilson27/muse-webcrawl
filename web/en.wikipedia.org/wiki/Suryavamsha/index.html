Suryavamsha

Suryavamsha (Sanskrit: सूर्यवंश, "Solar dynasty") is a legendary Indian dynasty said to descend from Surya, the Hindu sun god, through his son Vaivasvata Manu and Manu's son Ikshvaku. Together with the lunar Chandravamsha, it is one of the two great lineages of classical Indian royal genealogy.

Legendary kings

The dynasty's line, as given in the Ramayana, Mahabharata and Puranas, runs from Ikshvaku through kings such as Sagara, Bhagiratha — who is said to have brought the Ganges down to earth — and Raghu, to Rama, the hero of the Ramayana, who belonged to this line and is also called Suryavamshi. Kalidasa's Sanskrit epic Raghuvamsha ("Dynasty of Raghu") celebrates the line.

Historical claimants

Numerous historical royal houses claimed Suryavamsha descent to legitimise their rule, including the Chalukyas, the Sisodia Rajputs of Mewar, and various dynasties of Odisha and Nepal. The kings of Thailand also trace the Chakri dynasty's lineage symbolically to this solar line through the Ayutthaya tradition.
