Francisco Solano López

Born24 July 1827, Asunción, Paraguay
Died1 March 1870, Cerro Corá, Paraguay
OfficePresident of Paraguay (1862–1870)

Francisco Solano López Carrillo (24 July 1827 – 1 March 1870) was the second president of Paraguay, succeeding his father Carlos Antonio López in 1862. He led Paraguay into the Paraguayan War (1864–1870) against the Triple Alliance of Brazil, Argentina and Uruguay.

Paraguayan War

Ambitious to make Paraguay a regional power, López intervened in Uruguay's civil war, seized Brazilian territory and invaded Argentina, provoking the alliance against him. The war devastated Paraguay: most of its male population perished, and López increasingly resorted to conscription of children and purges of alleged conspirators.

Death

Refusing surrender, López was killed by Brazilian troops at the Battle of Cerro Corá on 1 March 1870, reportedly declaring "I die with my country". He remains a polarising figure — national hero to many Paraguayans, reckless tyrant to his critics.
