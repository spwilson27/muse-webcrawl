Meydan Racecourse

Location: Meydan City, Dubai, United Arab Emirates

Owned by: Dubai Racing Club

Date opened: 27 March 2010

Course type: Flat racing; left-handed

Notable race: Dubai World Cup

Meydan Racecourse is a racecourse in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. The grandstand is over a mile in length and can accommodate more than 60,000 spectators. Meydan opened on 27 March 2010, replacing Nad Al Sheba Racecourse, which occupied the same site.

The complex includes the Meydan Hotel, a five-star trackside hotel with most rooms overlooking the course, a museum, a gallery, an IMAX cinema, and the headquarters of the Dubai Racing Club. The development is part of the Meydan City project championed by Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, ruler of Dubai and founder of the Godolphin racing stable.

The course

Meydan features a 1,750-metre (about 8.75-furlong) all-weather Tapeta synthetic track inside a 2,400-metre (12-furlong) turf course. Racing is conducted left-handed. In 2014–15 the Tapeta surface was replaced with a conventional dirt track to align with the preferences of American dirt horses contesting the Dubai World Cup meeting.

Major races

Meydan hosts the Dubai World Cup Night series each March, culminating in the Dubai World Cup, one of the world's richest horse races, inaugurated in 1996 at Nad Al Sheba and won at Meydan's opening meeting by Gloria de Campeão. The card also includes the Dubai Sheema Classic and Dubai Turf (formerly Dubai Duty Free) on grass, the Dubai Golden Shaheen sprint, and the UAE Derby. The racing season runs from November to March, including the Dubai World Cup Carnival (now the Dubai Racing Carnival).

Records and notable winners

Thunder Snow is the only horse to win the Dubai World Cup twice, in 2018 and 2019. Arrogate's come-from-behind victory in 2017 is widely considered one of the great performances in the race's history. The racecourse also hosts concerts and other events, and its distinctive crescent-shaped roof is fitted with solar panels and a titanium facade.
