Il Mare (Korean: 시월애; "love across time", released internationally as Il Mare, "The Sea" in Italian) is a 2000 South Korean fantasy romance film directed by Lee Hyun-seung, starring Jun Ji-hyun and Lee Jung-jae.

Plot

Eun-joo, a voice actress, moves out of a lakeside house named "Il Mare" and leaves a note in the mailbox asking the next tenant to forward her mail. The letter is received by Sung-hyun, an architecture student who moved into the house two years earlier, in 1997. The two begin a correspondence through the mailbox, which connects their two times, and gradually fall in love despite being separated by two years. They attempt to arrange a meeting across time, but a tragedy intervenes, and Eun-joo desperately writes to the past to change events.

Reception and remake

The film was modestly received on release but grew in reputation as a classic of the Korean melodrama and a forerunner of the Korean Wave's international popularity. It was remade in Hollywood as The Lake House (2006), starring Keanu Reeves and Sandra Bullock.
