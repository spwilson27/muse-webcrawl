List of Doges of Venice

The Doge of Venice was the chief magistrate and leader of the Republic of Venice between 697 and 1797. The office lasted for more than a thousand years, and tradition counts some 120 doges, from Paoluccio Anafesto to Ludovico Manin, who abdicated when Napoleon abolished the Republic.

Early doges (697–900)

  • Paoluccio Anafesto (697–717) — traditionally the first doge
  • Marcello Tegalliano (717–726)
  • Orso Ipato (726–737)
  • Teodato Ipato (742–755)
  • Galla Gaulo (755–756)
  • Domenico Monegario (756–764)
  • Maurizio Galbaio (764–787)
  • Giovanni Galbaio (787–804)
  • Obelerio Antenoreo (804–811)
  • Agnello Participazio (811–827)
  • Giustiniano Participazio (827–829)
  • Giovanni I Participazio (829–836)
  • Pietro Tradonico (836–864)
  • Orso I Participazio (864–881)
  • Giovanni II Participazio (881–887)
  • Pietro I Candiano (887)
  • Pietro Tribuno (888–912)

High medieval doges (900–1200)

  • Orso II Participazio (912–932)
  • Pietro II Candiano (932–939)
  • Pietro IV Candiano (959–976)
  • Pietro Orseolo I (976–978)
  • Pietro Orseolo II (991–1009)
  • Vitale Faliero (1084–1095) — first to style himself Doge of Venice, Dalmatia and Croatia
  • Vitale I Michiel (1096–1102)
  • Enrico Dandolo (1192–1205) — blind doge who diverted the Fourth Crusade to Constantinople

Later medieval and Renaissance doges (1200–1600)

  • Pietro Ziani (1205–1229)
  • Jacopo Tiepolo (1229–1249)
  • Marino Faliero (1354–1355) — executed for treason; his portrait in the Doge's Palace is blacked out
  • Andrea Dandolo (1343–1354)
  • Antonio Venier (1382–1400)
  • Francesco Foscari (1423–1457) — longest-serving doge, deposed
  • Leonardo Loredan (1501–1521)
  • Andrea Gritti (1523–1538)
  • Sebastiano Venier (1577–1578) — victor of Lepanto
  • Pasquale Cicogna (1585–1595)

Final doges (1600–1797)

  • Leonardo Donato (1606–1612)
  • Francesco Contarini (1623–1624)
  • Francesco Morosini (1688–1694)
  • Giovanni Pesaro (1658–1659)
  • Silvestro Valier (1694–1700)
  • Paolo Renier (1779–1789)
  • Ludovico Manin (1789–1797) — the last doge; abdicated 12 May 1797
