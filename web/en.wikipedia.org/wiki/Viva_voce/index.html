Viva voce

Viva voce, from Latin "by live voice", commonly shortened to viva, refers to oral examination or oral testimony. In academia it most often denotes the oral defence of a doctoral thesis.

Doctoral viva

In the United Kingdom and many Commonwealth systems, a PhD candidate defends their dissertation in a closed oral examination before internal and external examiners, who question the candidate on the thesis, its methods and its contribution. Outcomes range from pass with no corrections, through minor or major corrections, to referral for resubmission or failure. In much of continental Europe and the United States (where the analogue is the "dissertation defense") the defence may be public or semi-public.

Other uses

In law and parliaments, viva voce describes oral evidence or a voice vote, as opposed to written testimony or a recorded ballot.
