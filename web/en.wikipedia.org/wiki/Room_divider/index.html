Room divider

A room divider is a screen or piece of furniture placed in a way that divides a room into separate areas. Room dividers are used by interior designers and architects as a means of dividing space and creating privacy, and are frequently portable, non-structural elements rather than permanent walls.

Types

Common forms include folding screens (such as the Chinese pingfeng and the Japanese byōbu), hanging curtains and bead curtains, bookshelf and shelving units, plants, and sliding partitions such as Japanese shōji and fusuma. In offices, cubicle partitions and movable acoustic panels serve as room dividers, while lofts and studio apartments use them to separate sleeping and living areas.

History

Folding screens originated in China, where they were used from at least the Han dynasty as windbreaks and status furnishings, and spread through East Asia before becoming fashionable in European interiors from the 17th century onward.
