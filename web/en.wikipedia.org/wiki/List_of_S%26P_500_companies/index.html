List of S&P 500 companies

The S&P 500 is a stock market index maintained by S&P Dow Jones Indices that measures the stock performance of 500 large companies listed on stock exchanges in the United States. The list of selected companies is not a definitive top-500 ranking by any single metric; it is determined by the S&P Index Committee based on criteria including market capitalization, liquidity, domicile, public float, and sector representation. The index accounts for roughly 80% of the total market capitalization of U.S. equities.

Sectors

Constituents are classified under the Global Industry Classification Standard (GICS) into eleven sectors: Information Technology, Health Care, Financials, Consumer Discretionary, Communication Services, Industrials, Consumer Staples, Energy, Utilities, Real Estate, and Materials.
