Holcomb, Kansas

Holcomb is a city in Finney County, Kansas, United States, located just west of Garden City along the Arkansas River. It had a population of about 2,200 at the 2020 census.

History

Holcomb is best known as the site of the 1959 murders of the Clutter family, who lived on a farm outside the town. The case was the subject of Truman Capote's 1966 non-fiction novel In Cold Blood, which brought national attention to the community. The town is served by the Holcomb school district and lies near a large coal-fired power plant, the Holcomb Station.
