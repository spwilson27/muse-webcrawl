Blood and soil

Blood and soil (German: Blut und Boden) is a nationalist slogan expressing a concept of ethnicity based on descent ("blood") and homeland territory ("soil"), closely associated with the ideology of Nazi Germany.

Origins

The phrase appeared in late 19th-century German nationalist writing and was popularised by Richard Walther Darré, the Nazi minister of agriculture, who promoted the ideal of a rural, racially defined peasantry as the foundation of the nation.

Ideological role

The concept underpinned Nazi agrarian and settlement policies, including the drive for Lebensraum in Eastern Europe and hereditary farm laws binding land to "racially pure" families. The slogan has since been taken up by far-right movements elsewhere, including at the 2017 Unite the Right rally in Charlottesville, Virginia.
