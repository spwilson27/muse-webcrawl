List of tallest buildings in Chicago

Chicago, Illinois, the third-largest city in the United States, is the birthplace of the skyscraper and home to one of the world's most celebrated skylines. The city has more than 1,300 high-rise buildings, including several that have held the title of tallest building in the world.

History

Chicago's first skyscraper, the Home Insurance Building (1885), is widely considered the first building supported by a steel frame. After the Great Chicago Fire of 1871, the city rebuilt rapidly, and the Chicago School of architecture pioneered steel-frame construction. The Willis Tower was the world's tallest building from 1974 until 1998.
