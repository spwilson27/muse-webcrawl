Batman: Arkham is a series of action-adventure video games based on the DC Comics character Batman, developed principally by Rocksteady Studios. The series is acclaimed for its "Freeflow" melee combat, stealth "Predator" sequences and exploration, and for performances by Kevin Conroy (Batman) and Mark Hamill (the Joker).

Games

The main series comprises Batman: Arkham Asylum (2009), set in the asylum on Arkham Island; Batman: Arkham City (2011), which moves the action to a walled-off district of Gotham; the prequel Batman: Arkham Origins (2013) by WB Games Montréal; and Batman: Arkham Knight (2015), which adds the Batmobile. Spin-offs include the VR title Batman: Arkham VR (2016) and Batman: Arkham Shadow (2024).

Reception

The series is widely regarded as among the finest superhero game franchises, with Arkham Asylum and Arkham City winning numerous Game of the Year awards and holding a Guinness World Record as the most critically acclaimed superhero game series.
