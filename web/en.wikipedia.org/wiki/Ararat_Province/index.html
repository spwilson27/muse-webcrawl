Ararat Province

Ararat Province (Armenian: Արարատի մարզ) is a province (marz) of Armenia, located in the south of the country on the Ararat plain, bordering Turkey to the west and south and Azerbaijan's Nakhchivan exclave to the southeast. Its capital and largest city is Artashat.

Geography and economy

The province occupies part of the fertile Ararat plain between the Araxes River and the Gegham mountains. It is one of Armenia's principal agricultural regions, known for vineyards, orchards, vegetables and wine and brandy production. Mount Ararat, across the border in Turkey, dominates the landscape and is visible from much of the province. Historical sites include the Khor Virap monastery, traditionally associated with Gregory the Illuminator, and the ruins of the ancient Armenian capital of Artashat and the Hellenistic-era city of Dvin.
