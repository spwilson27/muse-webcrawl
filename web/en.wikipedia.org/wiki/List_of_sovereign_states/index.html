List of sovereign states

The following is a list of sovereign states of the world. It contains 195 entries: the 193 member states of the United Nations and the two observer states, the Holy See (Vatican City) and Palestine. A further section of the article covers other states with partial or no international recognition.


A political map of the world's sovereign states.

Largest and smallest states

By area, the largest sovereign states are Russia (17.1 million km²), Canada, China, the United States and Brazil; the smallest are Vatican City (0.49 km²), Monaco, Nauru, Tuvalu and San Marino. By population, the largest are India (which surpassed China in 2023), China, the United States, Indonesia and Pakistan.

States with limited recognition

The article also lists entities such as Kosovo, Taiwan (Republic of China), Northern Cyprus, Somaliland, Transnistria, Abkhazia, South Ossetia and Western Sahara (Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic), whose sovereignty claims are recognised by some but not all UN member states.
