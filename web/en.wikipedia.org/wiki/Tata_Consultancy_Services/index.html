Tata Consultancy Services

TCS House, Mumbai — headquarters of Tata Consultancy Services

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is an Indian multinational information technology services and consulting company headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra. A part of the Tata Group, it is one of the largest private-sector employers in India and among the world's largest IT services firms by revenue and market capitalisation, operating in more than 50 countries with a workforce numbering in the hundreds of thousands.

History

TCS was founded in 1968 as a division of Tata Sons, initially providing punched-card and computer services to sister Tata companies; F. C. Kohli, often called the father of the Indian software industry, was its first general manager. Early milestones included work for international clients in the 1970s, the development of banking software, and a highly successful initial public offering in 2004 — then India's largest. Its banking platform, TCS BaNCS, became one of the world's most widely deployed core-banking systems.

Services

The company provides IT services, consulting, business process services, software products and platforms across industries such as banking and financial services, retail, manufacturing, life sciences, telecommunications and utilities. Offerings include application development and maintenance, cloud migration, data and artificial intelligence services, cybersecurity, and digital engineering. TCS is consistently ranked among the most valuable IT services brands globally.

Operations and sponsorships

TCS is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange and National Stock Exchange of India. It is a prominent sponsor of long-distance running events worldwide, including the New York City, London, Amsterdam and Mumbai marathons, and sponsors the TCS Pace Sticks and various technology-in-sport initiatives.
