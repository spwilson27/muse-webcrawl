"Let It Go" is a song from Disney's 2013 animated feature film Frozen, written by husband-and-wife songwriting team Kristen Anderson-Lopez and Robert Lopez and performed in the film by Idina Menzel in her role as Queen Elsa. It became a worldwide cultural phenomenon and one of the best-known movie songs of its decade.

Context and composition

In the film, Elsa, having fled her kingdom after her ice powers are exposed, sings the song as she embraces her abilities and builds an ice palace in the mountains — a celebration of self-acceptance and release from concealment ("Conceal, don't feel"). The songwriters have said the number reshaped the story, prompting revisions that recast Elsa from villain to sympathetic heroine. A pop version by Demi Lovato plays over the end credits.

Reception and impact

"Let It Go" won the Academy Award for Best Original Song and the Grammy Award for Best Song Written for Visual Media, reached the top five of the Billboard Hot 100, and was certified multi-platinum; foreign-language versions were released in dozens of languages, and the song's ubiquity — and children's constant replays of it — became a running pop-culture joke. It remains the signature number of the Frozen franchise and of the stage musical adaptation.
