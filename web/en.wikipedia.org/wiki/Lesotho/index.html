Lesotho

Lesotho, officially the Kingdom of Lesotho, is a landlocked country in Southern Africa entirely surrounded by South Africa — one of only three enclaved countries in the world. It is the only independent state in the world entirely above 1,000 metres in elevation; its lowest point (1,400 metres) is the highest 'low point' of any country. Its population is about 2.3 million and its capital is Maseru.

Lesotho was founded by King Moshoeshoe I in the 1820s, who gathered his Sotho people in mountain strongholds (Thaba Bosiu) against the Zulu wars and Boer expansion, then sought British protection. The Lesotho Highlands Water Project delivers water to South Africa's Gauteng province and generates royalties and power. Textile factories supplying US brands, diamond mining (Letseng, source of giant gems) and migrant labour to South African mines drive the economy; blankets and the mokorotlo hat are national symbols.
