Kingdom of Italy (Napoleonic)

The Kingdom of Italy (Italian: Regno d'Italia) was a French client state in northern Italy from 1805 to 1814. It was formed from the Italian Republic, with Napoleon Bonaparte as King of Italy and his stepson Eugène de Beauharnais as viceroy. Its capital was Milan.

The kingdom grew by annexations including Venetia, the Marches, and South Tyrol, reaching about 155,000 km² and some 6 million inhabitants by 1810. It introduced the Napoleonic Code, modern administration, conscription and infrastructure, and fielded Italian troops who served in Napoleon's campaigns including the invasion of Russia in 1812.

Fall

After Napoleon's defeats in 1813-1814, Eugène defended Italy but the kingdom collapsed with the Treaty of Fontainebleau and the Congress of Vienna restored Austrian rule in Lombardy-Venetia.
