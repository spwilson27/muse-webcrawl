BeWelcome

Type: Hospitality exchange service
Launched: February 2007
Operator: BeVolunteer (non-profit)
Membership: More than 200,000 members

BeWelcome is a hospitality exchange network through which members offer free accommodation to travelling guests and arrange meetings and activities. It was launched in February 2007 by volunteers who had left the Hospitality Club, and it is run by the non-profit association BeVolunteer, registered in France. It is notable as one of the largest non-commercial, volunteer-run and open-source hospitality networks.

History

BeWelcome grew out of dissatisfaction with the centralised, closed management of Hospitality Club. Its founders committed the new project to democratic, transparent governance and open-source software. In 2012 many members of Couchsurfing joined or returned to BeWelcome after Couchsurfing converted to a for-profit company, and membership passed 100,000 in the following years.

Organisation

The platform's code (the BW Rox software) is open source. Decisions are taken by the elected board of BeVolunteer and by member votes at an annual general assembly. Members create profiles stating whether they can host, show guests around, or simply meet up; trust is supported by a comment system describing past exchanges. Use of the service is free, funded by donations.
