BBC Radio 5 Live
First air date: 28 March 1994
Format: News, sport, talk
Owner: BBC
Sister station: BBC Radio 5 Sports Extra
Headquarters: MediaCityUK, Salford

BBC Radio 5 Live is a British national radio station owned and operated by the BBC. It broadcasts rolling news, live sport and phone-in discussion, principally on DAB, digital television and online, following the closure of its former medium-wave frequencies in the 2020s for most transmitters.

History

The station launched on 28 March 1994 as BBC Radio 5 Live, replacing the original Radio 5. Its format combined the news coverage developed during the Gulf War on Radio 4 FM with live sports rights including Premier League football, Test cricket (shared), Formula One and major tournaments.

Programming

Flagship shows include 5 Live Breakfast, 606 football phone-in, and live commentary strands. The station moved from London to MediaCityUK in Salford in 2011 as part of the BBC's north of England relocation.
