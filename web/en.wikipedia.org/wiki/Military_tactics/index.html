Military tactics

Military tactics encompasses the techniques for implementing military engagements, including the disposition and manoeuvre of units on a battlefield, the use of weapons and terrain, and the coordination of arms in combat. Tactics is the level of war between strategy — the planning and conduct of campaigns — and individual techniques such as marksmanship. In modern doctrine, tactics concerns units up to roughly division size, while the operational level links tactical actions to strategic aims.

Concepts

Recurring tactical concepts across history include concentration of force at a decisive point, mass, economy of force, surprise, security, and unity of command. Common forms of manoeuvre include frontal attack, flanking maneuver and envelopment (single, double, and vertical by airborne forces), penetration, turning movement, and infiltration. Defensive tactics employ defense in depth, strongpoints, reverse slopes, reserves and counterattacks.

Historical development

Ancient armies relied on formations such as the Greek phalanx, the Roman legion with its manipular flexibility, and combined cavalry-and-archer systems of steppe peoples. Hannibal's double envelopment at Cannae (216 BCE) became a canonical study. Gunpowder progressively transformed tactics: linear formations of the 17th–18th centuries gave way to Napoleonic columns and corps organization, then to rifled firepower, trenches and artillery in the First World War. In response came infiltration tactics, tanks, and the combined-arms doctrines of the Second World War, exemplified by Germany's early-war "Blitzkrieg" operations. Airpower, helicopters (air assault), precision-guided munitions, electronic warfare and drones have continued the evolution in the late 20th and 21st centuries.

Combined arms

Modern tactics emphasize combined arms — integrating infantry, armor, artillery, engineers, air defense, aviation and signals so that the strength of each compensates for the weaknesses of others. Small-unit tactics (fire and movement, bounding overwatch, ambushes, urban warfare drills) form the tactical foundation, adapted by armies through doctrine, training and wargaming.
