Bilgeboard

TypeSailing vessel appendage
Used onScows and shallow-draft sailboats

A bilgeboard is a retractable board mounted in the bilge on each side of a sailing hull, used to provide lateral resistance against leeway in place of a single centreboard or daggerboard.

Operation

Bilgeboards pivot down into the water from housings inside the hull. When sailing, the leeward board is lowered (often at a slight angle, generating lift to windward) while the windward board is raised to reduce drag. The arrangement keeps the cockpit and cabin free of a centreboard trunk.

Use

Bilgeboards are characteristic of racing scows on North American inland lakes and are found on some shallow-draft cruising designs, where twin boards also let the boat sit upright when dried out on the bottom.
