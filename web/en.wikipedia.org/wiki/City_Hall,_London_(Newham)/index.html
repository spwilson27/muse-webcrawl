City Hall, London (Newham)

City Hall, London

Location: Kamal Chunchie Way, Royal Victoria Dock, Newham, London
Opened: 2012 (as The Crystal); GLA headquarters from 2022
Architect: WilkinsonEyre (building designed for Siemens)

City Hall is the headquarters of the Greater London Authority (GLA), the regional government for Greater London. Since January 2022 it has been housed in The Crystal, at Royal Victoria Dock in Newham, East London. It replaced the earlier City Hall building on the South Bank in Southwark, which the GLA occupied from 2002 to 2021.

The Crystal

The Crystal was designed by WilkinsonEyre and opened in 2012 as a sustainable-cities exhibition and conference centre developed by Siemens. The angular glass building, shaped to evoke a crystal, was intended as a showcase for green building technology, using ground-source heat pumps, solar panels, and rainwater recycling. Siemens sold the building to the GLA in 2019.

Move from Southwark

In 2020 the Mayor of London, Sadiq Khan, announced that the GLA would leave the Southwark City Hall — owned by a Kuwaiti investment company and rented at high cost — and move to The Crystal, which the GLA already owned, to save money. The London Assembly and the Mayor's Office relocated in early 2022. The building was renamed City Hall, and the street in front was renamed Kamal Chunchie Way, after the Ceylonese-born community worker and resident of Newham.
