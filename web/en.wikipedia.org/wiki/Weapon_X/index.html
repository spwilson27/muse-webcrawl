Weapon X

PublisherMarvel Comics
First appearanceThe Incredible Hulk #180 (1974, as an organization behind Wolverine)
TypeFictional clandestine genetics program

Weapon X is a fictional clandestine government genetic research facility in Marvel Comics appearing in American comic books. The project is a division of the larger Weapon Plus program and is most famous for producing the mutant Wolverine, whose skeleton was bonded with the indestructible metal adamantium and whose memories were systematically erased and reprogrammed. The letter "X" is the Roman numeral ten, denoting the tenth program in the Weapon Plus series.

Fictional history

Weapon Plus was established in the aftermath of World War II to create super-soldiers, beginning with the program that produced Captain America (Weapon I). Weapon X, run jointly with elements of the Canadian government as Department K, experimented on mutants. Its best-known subject, James "Logan" Howlett, was kidnapped, subjected to the adamantium bonding process, and trained as an assassin before escaping, his past reduced to fragments — a storyline famously depicted in Barry Windsor-Smith's 1991 "Weapon X" serial in Marvel Comics Presents.

Other subjects and adaptations

The program also produced or modified subjects including Sabretooth, Deadpool (through the related Workshop), and Laura Kinney (X-23), a clone of Wolverine. Weapon X has appeared extensively in other media, including the X-Men film series, where it is associated with Colonel William Stryker and the Alkali Lake facility, and in the film Logan (2017) through its successor organization.
