Biometrics

Biometrics are body measurements and calculations related to human characteristics and features. Biometric authentication (or realistic authentication) is used in computer science as a form of identification and access control.

Characteristics

Biometric identifiers include fingerprints, facial features, iris and retina patterns, voice, hand geometry, gait, DNA and behavioural traits such as keystroke dynamics. A good trait is universal, distinctive, permanent and collectable.

Systems

Systems operate in verification (1:1, confirming a claimed identity) or identification (1:N, searching a database) mode. Performance is measured by false match and false non-match rates. Templates are stored and compared, ideally with liveness / presentation-attack detection.

Applications and concerns

Uses include passports, India's Aadhaar system, border control, phone unlocking and law enforcement. Concerns include privacy, surveillance, bias and error rates across demographics, data breaches — biometric traits cannot be changed like passwords — and consent.
