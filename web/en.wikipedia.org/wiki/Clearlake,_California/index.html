Clearlake is a city in Lake County, California, United States. Situated on the southeast shore of Clear Lake, the largest natural freshwater lake entirely within California, the city was incorporated in 1980.

History

The area around Clear Lake has been inhabited by Indigenous peoples, including the Pomo, for thousands of years. Settlers arrived in the late 19th century, and the community grew around agriculture and lakeside resorts. The city took its name from the lake, which early settlers found notably clear.

Geography

Clearlake lies at the southern end of the lake in a region of volcanic geology, near Mount Konocti, an extinct volcano that forms a prominent landmark on the lake's western shore.
