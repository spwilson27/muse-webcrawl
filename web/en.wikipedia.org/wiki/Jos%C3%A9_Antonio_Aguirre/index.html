José Antonio Aguirre y Lecube (1904–1960) was a Basque politician and the first lehendakari (president) of the Basque Government. A leading figure of the Basque Nationalist Party (PNV), he presided over the autonomous Basque government created during the Spanish Civil War.

Career

Aguirre became mayor of Getxo while still in his twenties and was elected to the Spanish Cortes. When the Statute of Autonomy of the Basque Country was approved in October 1936, he took the oath as lehendakari under the Tree of Gernika. After the fall of Bilbao in 1937, he directed the Basque government in exile, first from Catalonia and then from abroad, and during World War II he travelled in the Americas before settling in Paris, where he led the government-in-exile until his death.
