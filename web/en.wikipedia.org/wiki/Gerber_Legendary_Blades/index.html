Gerber Legendary Blades is an American manufacturer of knives, multi-tools and other outdoor and survival equipment, based in Portland, Oregon. Founded in 1939 by Joseph R. Gerber, it is one of the largest makers of knives and tools for the United States military.

History

The company began when Joseph Gerber, who ran an advertising firm, sent handcrafted carving knife sets as holiday gifts in 1939; the sets proved so popular that he founded a cutlery business. Gerber grew with a reputation for quality kitchen and hunting knives designed with makers such as Al Mar, and in 1966 moved to its current Tigard/Portland-area headquarters. In 1987 the Finnish company Fiskars acquired Gerber. Well-known products include the Mark II combat knife (1966), the LMF II infantry knife, the Multi-Plier multi-tools, and the Bear Grylls survival line.
