E (musical note)

E
Pitch class4
Enharmonic equivalentsF♭, D𝄪
Position in C major scaleThird degree (mediant)
SolfègeMi

E is the fourth note and the tenth semitone of the solfège scale (mi). It lies a whole tone above D and a semitone below F, and is enharmonically equivalent to F♭ (F-flat) and D𝄪 (D double-sharp). In the equal-tempered scale, E above middle C (E4) has a frequency of about 329.63 Hz when A4 is tuned to 440 Hz.

Scales

E is the tonic of the E major and E natural minor scales. The scale of E major contains four sharps (F♯, C♯, G♯ and D♯), while E minor is the relative minor of G major and has one sharp in its key signature (F♯). On the guitar in standard tuning, the first and sixth strings are both tuned to E, making the note central to guitar music; the lowest string of the double bass and the highest open string of the violin are also E-related reference pitches in their respective tunings.
