Tyndall Air Force Base

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Tyndall Air Force Base is a United States Air Force base located 12 miles (19 km) east of Panama City, Florida, on the Gulf of Mexico coast. It is named in honour of World War I pilot 1st Lt. Frank Benjamin Tyndall. The base is the home of the 325th Fighter Wing, part of Air Combat Command.

History

The base was established in 1941 as a flexible gunnery school for the Army Air Forces. During the Cold War it hosted interceptor squadrons and weapons training under Air Defense Command. In the 21st century it became the principal training base for the F-22 Raptor.

Hurricane Michael

On 10 October 2018, Hurricane Michael made landfall near the base as a Category 5 storm, causing catastrophic damage to nearly every building on the installation. The base was subsequently rebuilt as the "Installation of the Future", and F-35A Lightning II squadrons were assigned to replace the training mission.

Units

In addition to the 325th Fighter Wing, Tyndall hosts the 1st Air Force (Air Forces Northern), the headquarters responsible for the air defence of the continental United States, and the 53rd Weapons Evaluation Group.
