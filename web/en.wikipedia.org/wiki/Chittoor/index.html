Chittoor

Country: India

State: Andhra Pradesh

District: Chittoor district

Languages: Telugu, Tamil, Urdu

Chittoor is a city and district headquarters in the Chittoor district of the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh. It lies in the Poini River valley in the southern part of the state, near the border with Tamil Nadu, roughly 130 km west of Chennai and 170 km east of Bengaluru.

Geography and economy

The city sits amid the low hills of the Eastern Ghats' southern reaches and has a hot semi-arid climate. Chittoor is a regional market centre for agriculture — particularly mangoes, groundnuts, sugarcane and tamarind — and for dairy production; the surrounding district is also known for granite quarrying and silk weaving in nearby towns. The Chittoor mango and the region's milk shed supply major South Indian cities.

Transport and education

National Highway 69 and NH 40 pass through the city, and Chittoor railway station lies on the Katpadi–Renigunta line. The district hosts institutions including Sri Venkateswara University colleges in nearby Tirupati, and Chittoor itself has government colleges and professional institutions in engineering, pharmacy and agriculture.

Nearby places

The wider district is home to the pilgrimage city of Tirupati with the Sri Venkateswara Temple at Tirumala, the Kanipakam Vinayaka temple, Horsley Hills, and the Kaundinya Wildlife Sanctuary.
