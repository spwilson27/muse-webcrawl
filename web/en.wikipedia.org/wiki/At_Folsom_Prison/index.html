At Folsom Prison

Artist:Johnny Cash
Released:6 May 1968
Recorded:13 January 1968
Venue:Folsom State Prison, California
Genre:Country, outlaw country
Label:Columbia

At Folsom Prison is the first live album by American singer-songwriter Johnny Cash, released by Columbia Records on May 6, 1968. It was recorded during two concerts held on January 13 that year at Folsom State Prison in California.

The album consists of songs performed with a small band, including June Carter, and features Cash's signature song "Folsom Prison Blues". Despite the reluctance of Columbia executives, who considered the project risky, the album was a commercial and critical success, revitalizing Cash's career and reaching number one on the country charts.

Background

Cash had long sympathized with prisoners and had performed at prisons since the 1950s. The concert, championed by producer Bob Johnston, was recorded in two shows on the same day; most of the released album came from the morning performance.

Legacy

At Folsom Prison is routinely ranked among the greatest albums of all time. It was certified triple platinum in the United States and was selected for preservation in the National Recording Registry. Cash followed it with another prison album, At San Quentin (1969).
