Disney Infinity

Disney Infinity
Developer: Avalanche Software
Publisher: Disney Interactive Studios
First release: 2013
Genre: Action-adventure, toys-to-life

Disney Infinity was a toys-to-life action-adventure video game series developed by Avalanche Software and published by Disney Interactive Studios. Players placed physical figurines of Disney and Pixar characters on a peripheral base to bring them into the game, where they could play through themed "Play Sets" or build their own worlds in the sandbox "Toy Box" mode.

Games

  • Disney Infinity (2013) — launched with characters from Pirates of the Caribbean, Monsters University, and The Incredibles.
  • Disney Infinity: Marvel Super Heroes (2.0, 2014) — added Marvel characters and an expanded Toy Box.
  • Disney Infinity 3.0 (2015) — added Star Wars content.

Discontinuation

Disney discontinued the series in May 2016 as part of its exit from self-published console games, closing Avalanche Software (later reopened under Warner Bros.). A PC "Gold Edition" collection was subsequently released without the toys-to-life requirement.
