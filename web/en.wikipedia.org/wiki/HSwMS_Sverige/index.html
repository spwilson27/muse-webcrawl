HSwMS Sverige

HSwMS Sverige was the lead ship of the Sverige-class coastal defence ships of the Swedish Navy. Funded partly by a public subscription campaign that raised over 15 million kronor after the "F-boat" debate of 1911–12, she was launched in 1915 and commissioned in 1917. With four 283 mm guns and a speed of over 22 knots, she was the largest and most powerful warship ever operated by Sweden at the time.

Launched3 May 1915

FateScrapped 1958–59

History

The ship originated in Sweden's 1911 naval programme and the political controversy over funding (the "F-båt" question), which contributed to the fall of the government; a national collection organised by students and the public ultimately financed her construction at Götaverken. Sverige served as flagship through the interwar period and both world wars, receiving anti-aircraft and fire-control upgrades, and was struck from the navy list in 1953.
