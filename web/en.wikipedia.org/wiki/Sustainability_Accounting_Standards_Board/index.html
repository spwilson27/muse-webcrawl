Sustainability Accounting Standards Board

The Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB) was a non-profit organisation founded in 2011 that set standards for the disclosure of financially material sustainability information by companies to investors. SASB published standards for 77 industries, identifying for each a small set of environmental, social and governance topics most likely to affect financial performance.

Approach

Unlike broad sustainability reporting frameworks, SASB emphasised financial materiality and industry specificity, aiming to make sustainability disclosure useful in capital markets and comparable to financial reporting. Its standards were developed through evidence-based research and public consultation.

Consolidation into the ISSB

In 2021 SASB merged with the International Integrated Reporting Council to form the Value Reporting Foundation, and in 2022 the foundation was consolidated into the IFRS Foundation, whose new International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB) took over responsibility for the SASB standards. The ISSB's first standards, IFRS S1 and S2 (2023), build on SASB's industry-based approach.
