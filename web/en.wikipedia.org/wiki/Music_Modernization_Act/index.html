Music Modernization Act

The Orrin G. Hatch–Bob Goodlatte Music Modernization Act, known as the Music Modernization Act (MMA), is a United States law enacted on 11 October 2018 that modernises copyright rules for music in the streaming era. It combined three bills: the Music Modernization Act, the CLASSICS Act and the AMP Act.

Provisions

Title I created a blanket mechanical licence for streaming services and a non-profit Mechanical Licensing Collective (the MLC) to administer it and distribute royalties to songwriters and publishers. Title II (CLASSICS) extended federal remedies to pre-1972 sound recordings. Title III (AMP) allowed producers and engineers to receive certain royalties directly.

The act was widely supported across the music industry as the most significant reform of music copyright in decades.
