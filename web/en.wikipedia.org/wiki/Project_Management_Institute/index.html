Project Management Institute

The Project Management Institute (PMI) is a U.S.-based not-for-profit professional organization for project management, founded in 1969. It is the world's largest association for the project management profession, with several hundred thousand members and certification holders in more than 180 countries, organized through local chapters.

Standards and publications

PMI publishes A Guide to the Project Management Body of Knowledge (the PMBOK Guide), the most widely used standard for project management practice, first issued in 1996 and regularly revised; the seventh edition (2021) reorganized the standard around principles and performance domains. PMI also publishes practice standards, the Project Management Journal, and industry research such as the annual Pulse of the Profession reports.

Certifications

PMI administers professional credentials, most prominently the Project Management Professional (PMP) certification, launched in 1984 and held by well over a million people worldwide. Other certifications include the Certified Associate in Project Management (CAPM), Program Management Professional (PgMP), and the PMI Agile Certified Practitioner (PMI-ACP). Credential holders must maintain certification through continuing professional development units.

Activities

PMI holds conferences, provides training and academic accreditation through its Global Accreditation Center, and advocates for the profession. Its headquarters are in Newtown Square, Pennsylvania.
