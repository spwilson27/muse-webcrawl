Oblasts of Ukraine

An oblast (Ukrainian: область) is the primary administrative division of Ukraine. Ukraine is divided into 24 oblasts, most of which are named after their administrative centres. Together with the Autonomous Republic of Crimea and the two cities with special status, Kyiv and Sevastopol, they form the first-level subdivisions of the country.

Overview

Each oblast is governed by an oblast council (oblasna rada) and a state administration headed by a governor appointed by the President of Ukraine. Oblasts are subdivided into raions (districts); a 2020 reform consolidated these into 136 enlarged raions. Most oblasts are named after their capital cities — for example Lviv Oblast, Odesa Oblast and Kharkiv Oblast — with a few exceptions such as Zakarpattia Oblast and Kirovohrad Oblast (whose capital is Kropyvnytskyi) and Volyn Oblast (capital Lutsk).

List

The 24 oblasts are: Cherkasy, Chernihiv, Chernivtsi, Dnipropetrovsk, Donetsk, Ivano-Frankivsk, Kharkiv, Kherson, Khmelnytskyi, Kyiv, Kirovohrad, Luhansk, Lviv, Mykolaiv, Odesa, Poltava, Rivne, Sumy, Ternopil, Vinnytsia, Volyn, Zakarpattia, Zaporizhzhia and Zhytomyr.
