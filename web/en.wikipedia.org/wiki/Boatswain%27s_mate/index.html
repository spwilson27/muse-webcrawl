Boatswain's mate

[Image placeholder: Boatswain's mate]

Boatswain's mate is a job classification (rating) in several navies. In the United States Navy, boatswain's mates (BM) are responsible for the exterior surfaces of ships, deck equipment, small boat operations, navigation duties, and the supervision of deck force personnel; it is among the oldest ratings in the U.S. Navy, dating to 1794.

Duties

Boatswain's mates maintain hull and superstructure surfaces, operate and maintain deck machinery such as cranes, winches and boats, handle mooring and anchoring evolutions, and serve as members of underway replenishment and damage-control parties. Aboard smaller vessels they may also stand watch as helmsmen and lookouts.

Other navies

Similar roles exist in coast guards and Commonwealth navies, where the boatswain (bosun) is the senior deck department sailor responsible to the commanding officer for the working of the ship's boats, rigging and ground tackle.
