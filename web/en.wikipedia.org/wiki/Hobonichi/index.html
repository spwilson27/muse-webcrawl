Hobonichi Co., Ltd. (Japanese: 株式会社ほぼ日, Kabushiki-gaisha Hobonichi) is a Japanese company run by copywriter Shigesato Itoi. It is best known outside Japan for its day-per-page yearly planner, the Hobonichi Techo, and within Japan for its website, Hobo Nikkan Itoi Shinbun ("Almost Daily Itoi News"), founded in 1998, which publishes essays, interviews, and columns.

Hobonichi Techo

The Hobonichi Techo was first released in 2001 as a planner sold through the website. The book features a page for each day of the year, printed on thin Tomoe River paper, with a layout that opens flat, daily quotes selected from the website's articles, and a wide range of interchangeable covers sold separately. Variants include the Original (A6), the Cousin (A5), the Planner (an English-language A6 edition introduced in 2013), the Weeks (a slim weekly planner), and the five-year journal (5-Year Techo).

The planner has a devoted international following among journal-keeping and stationery enthusiasts, and annual sales have surpassed one million copies across the range. English-language editions and an official English online store have expanded its popularity outside Japan.

Other activities

Hobonichi also sells stationery and lifestyle goods, runs the Hobonichi School, and organises events. The company was listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange's JASDAQ market in March 2017.
