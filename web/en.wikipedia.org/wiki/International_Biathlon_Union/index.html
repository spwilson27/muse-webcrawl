International Biathlon Union

The International Biathlon Union (IBU) is the international governing body of biathlon. It was founded on 2 July 1993 in London, when the biathlon commission of the UIPMB (Union Internationale de Pentathlon Moderne et Biathlon) separated to form its own federation. Its headquarters are in Anif near Salzburg, Austria.

Competitions

The IBU organises the premier biathlon competitions, including the Biathlon World Cup, the Biathlon World Championships, and the IBU Cup circuit. Biathlon events at the Winter Olympic Games are run under IBU rules.

Governance

The IBU comprises more than 60 national federations. Following doping scandals, the federation undertook governance reforms in the late 2010s, creating an independent Biathlon Integrity Unit (BIU) in 2019.
