Derwent Reservoir, Derbyshire

Derwent Reservoir is the middle of three reservoirs in the Upper Derwent Valley in the Peak District of Derbyshire, England, lying between Ladybower Reservoir downstream and Howden Reservoir upstream.

History

The reservoir was built between 1902 and 1916 by the Derwent Valley Water Board. During the Second World War, the Derwent dam was used by RAF 617 Squadron for low-level flying practice ahead of the Dambusters raid on the Ruhr dams in 1943, because of its similarity to the German targets.

Geography

The River Derwent flows through the reservoir, which supplies water to cities including Derby, Leicester, Nottingham and Sheffield.
