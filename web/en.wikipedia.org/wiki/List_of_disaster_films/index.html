List of disaster films

This is a list of disaster films, a genre centred on an impending or ongoing disaster — natural, accidental or deliberately caused — and the efforts of characters to survive it. The genre peaked commercially in the 1970s and again in the 1990s.

The 1970s cycle — including The Poseidon Adventure, Earthquake and The Towering Inferno — was defined by producer Irwin Allen and all-star ensemble casts. The 1990s revival was driven by advances in computer-generated imagery, exemplified by Twister and Independence Day.
