Revelstoke Dam

The Revelstoke Dam is a hydroelectric dam and generating station on the Columbia River in British Columbia, Canada, about 5 km north of the city of Revelstoke. Completed in 1984 by BC Hydro, it is one of the largest hydroelectric developments in the province. The concrete gravity dam is about 175 metres high and impounds Lake Revelstoke, a narrow reservoir extending some 130 km north to the tailrace of the Mica Dam.

Generating station

The Revelstoke Generating Station initially housed four generating units; a fifth unit was added in 2010, bringing total installed capacity to about 2,480 MW, with provision for a sixth unit. The station operates largely as a run-of-river plant with limited storage, coordinating with the upstream Mica Dam's regulated flows. Power is transmitted south to the main grid of British Columbia. A visitor centre near the dam presents the history of the Columbia River power developments and the impact of the projects on the region and its Indigenous peoples.
