List of fjords

Fjords
A fjord is a long, narrow, deep inlet of the sea between high cliffs, formed by glacial erosion.

A fjord is a long, narrow inlet with steep sides, created by glacial erosion. Fjords are found mainly in Norway, Chile, New Zealand, Canada, Greenland, Alaska and Iceland. The longest fjord in the world is the Scoresby Sound in Greenland at about 350 km (220 mi); the Sognefjord in Norway is the longest in Europe at 205 km (127 mi).
