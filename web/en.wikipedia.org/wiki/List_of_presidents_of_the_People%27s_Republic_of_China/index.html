List of presidents of the People's Republic of China

The President of the People's Republic of China is the ceremonial head of state of China. Real power rests with the General Secretary of the Communist Party and the Chairman of the Central Military Commission; since 1993 the same person has usually held all three posts.

The office

The presidency was created by the 1954 constitution, abolished in 1975, and restored in 1982. The president is elected by the National People's Congress; a 2018 amendment removed the two-term limit. Mao and Liu held the office under the title "Chairman of the People's Republic of China".
