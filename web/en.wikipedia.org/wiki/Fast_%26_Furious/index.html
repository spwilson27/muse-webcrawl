Fast & Furious is an American media franchise centered on a series of action films concerned with street racing, heists, spies and family. The franchise also includes short films, a television series, toys, live shows, video games and theme park attractions.

The first film, The Fast and the Furious, was released in 2001 and starred Paul Walker as undercover officer Brian O'Conner and Vin Diesel as street racer Dominic Toretto. The series evolved from street-racing plots into elaborate global heist and espionage adventures. Following Walker's death in 2013, his character was retired in Furious 7 (2015).

Films

The main series comprises ten films from The Fast and the Furious (2001) through Fast X (2023), plus the spin-off Hobbs & Shaw (2019) starring Dwayne Johnson and Jason Statham. The franchise has grossed over $7 billion worldwide, making it one of the highest-grossing film franchises of all time.
