List of female governors in the United States

As of the mid-2020s, 49 women have served as governor of a U.S. state (counting repeat officeholders in different states once). The first two women to serve as state governors took office in January 1925: Nellie Tayloe Ross of Wyoming, elected in her own right, and Miriam "Ma" Ferguson of Texas. Arizona has had the most female governors of any state, with five. In January 2025 a record 14 women served simultaneously as state governors.
