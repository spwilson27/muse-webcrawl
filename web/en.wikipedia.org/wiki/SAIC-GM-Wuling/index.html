SAIC-GM-Wuling Automobile (SGMW) is a Chinese automobile manufacturing joint venture between SAIC Motor, General Motors and Guangxi Automobile Group (formerly Wuling Motors), established in 2002 and headquartered in Liuzhou, Guangxi.

Products

The venture is best known for its Wuling brand minivans and small commercial vehicles, long among China's best-selling vehicles, and for the Baojun brand launched in 2010. Its Wuling Hongguang Mini EV, launched in 2020, became one of the world's top-selling electric cars, popularising ultra-affordable city EVs.

Operations

SGMW operates major plants in Liuzhou and Qingdao and an overseas plant in Indonesia. It has been one of China's highest-volume automakers, with annual sales exceeding two million vehicles in strong years.
