Bard was a conversational generative artificial intelligence chatbot developed by Google. Initially based on the LaMDA family of large language models and later on PaLM 2 and Gemini, Bard was released in March 2023 in response to the rapid rise of OpenAI's ChatGPT. In February 2024, Google unified its AI products under the Gemini brand, and Bard was renamed Gemini.

Background

Google had researched large language models for years, publishing the transformer architecture in 2017 and the conversational model LaMDA in 2021. After ChatGPT's launch in November 2022 drew wide attention, Google issued an internal "code red" and accelerated work on a public product. CEO Sundar Pichai announced Bard on 6 February 2023, describing it as an "experimental conversational AI service".

Launch and rollout

Bard opened to a waitlist in the United States and United Kingdom on 21 March 2023. Its announcement was marred by an error in a promotional demonstration, in which Bard incorrectly stated that the James Webb Space Telescope had taken the first pictures of an exoplanet; the mistake coincided with a sharp fall in Alphabet's share price. In May 2023 Bard moved from LaMDA to the more capable PaLM 2 model, the waitlist was dropped, and availability expanded to more than 180 countries. Later updates added image generation, coding assistance, integration with Google services, and support in over 40 languages.

Rebranding to Gemini

In December 2023 Google introduced the Gemini family of multimodal models, and Bard was upgraded to run on Gemini Pro. On 8 February 2024 the Bard name was retired and the chatbot became Gemini, aligning the product with the underlying model family. A paid tier, Gemini Advanced, launched at the same time as part of Google One.

Reception

Early reviews found Bard competent at summarisation and drafting but more prone to factual inaccuracies and less capable at coding than rival systems. Commentators noted that the launch reflected intensifying competition in generative AI between Google and Microsoft-backed OpenAI.
