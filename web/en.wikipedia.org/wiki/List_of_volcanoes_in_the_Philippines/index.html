List of volcanoes in the Philippines

The Philippines has more than 50 volcanoes, of which about two dozen are classified as active by the Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology (PHIVOLCS). The archipelago lies along the Pacific "Ring of Fire", where the Philippine Sea Plate and the Eurasian Plate converge. The 1991 eruption of Mount Pinatubo was the second-largest volcanic eruption of the 20th century.

Potentially active and inactive volcanoes

Potentially active volcanoes include Mount Arayat, Mount Banahaw and Mount Makiling, while many others — such as Mount Apo, the highest peak in the country at 2,954 m — are classified as inactive.
