Kinsey Institute

The Kinsey Institute for Research in Sex, Gender, and Reproduction is a research institute at Indiana University in Bloomington, Indiana, founded in 1947 by entomologist Alfred Kinsey. It was established to continue and house Kinsey's pioneering interview-based research into human sexual behaviour.

Research and collections

The institute became famous through the Kinsey Reports — Sexual Behavior in the Human Male (1948) and Sexual Behavior in the Human Female (1953) — and the Kinsey scale of sexual orientation. It maintains major library, archive and art collections on sexuality, and conducts contemporary research on sexual health, relationships and gender.
