UPS Airlines Flight 6 was a scheduled international cargo flight from Dubai, United Arab Emirates, to Cologne, Germany. On 3 September 2010, the Boeing 747-400 freighter operating the flight crashed shortly after departure following an in-flight fire in the cargo hold, killing both crew members, Captain Doug Lampe and First Officer Matthew Bell.

Accident

About 22 minutes after takeoff, the crew reported a fire warning and smoke in the cockpit and declared an emergency, attempting to return to Dubai. The fire burned through flight-control cables and disabled the captain's oxygen supply; dense smoke made the instruments and the view outside nearly impossible to see. The first officer, flying with a relief captain relaying instructions by radio from air traffic control after communications difficulties, could not maintain control, and the aircraft crashed in an unpopulated area near a military camp southwest of the airport.

Investigation and aftermath

The investigation by the UAE General Civil Aviation Authority concluded that the fire originated in a cargo container holding large quantities of lithium batteries, which were not declared as hazardous material. The accident, together with the earlier loss of Asiana Airlines Flight 991, drove major regulatory changes restricting the carriage of bulk lithium battery shipments on cargo aircraft and spurred work on fire-resistant cargo containers and improved detection and suppression standards.
