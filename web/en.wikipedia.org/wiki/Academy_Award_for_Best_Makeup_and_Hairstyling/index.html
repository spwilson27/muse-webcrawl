Academy Award for Best Makeup and Hairstyling

The Academy Award for Best Makeup and Hairstyling is an Oscar presented annually by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences for outstanding achievement in film makeup and hairstyling. Before becoming a competitive category in 1981, exceptional makeup work had been recognised with Honorary Awards, notably for The 7 Faces of Dr. Lao (1964) and Planet of the Apes (1968).

History

The first competitive award went to Rick Baker for his groundbreaking transformation effects in An American Werewolf in London. Baker went on to win a record seven Oscars in the category from eleven nominations. The category was renamed from Best Makeup to Best Makeup and Hairstyling in 2012. A shortlist "bake-off" process, in which the Makeup Artists and Hairstylists Branch reviews reels from shortlisted films, determines the nominees; the number of nominees was expanded from three to five in 2019.

Notable winners

  • The Elephant Man (1981 ceremony, honorary recognition debate)
  • Amadeus (1984)
  • Beetlejuice (1988)
  • Driving Miss Daisy (1989)
  • Mrs. Doubtfire (1993)
  • The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (2003)
  • La Vie en Rose (2007)
  • The Iron Lady (2011)
  • Darkest Hour (2017)
