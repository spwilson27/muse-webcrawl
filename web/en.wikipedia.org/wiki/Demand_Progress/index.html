Demand Progress

Demand Progress mobilizes supporters online for Internet-freedom campaigns

Demand Progress is an American Internet-activism organization and political action committee founded in 2010 by Internet activist Aaron Swartz and former congressional staffer David Segal. The group campaigns for progressive policies with an emphasis on Internet freedom, civil liberties, government reform and opposing concentrated corporate power.

Campaigns

Demand Progress first gained prominence in 2010 with its campaign against the Combating Online Infringement and Counterfeits Act (COICA), and it was a leading participant in the mass online protests against the Stop Online Piracy Act (SOPA) and the PROTECT IP Act in 2011–2012, which culminated in the January 2012 Internet blackout. The organization has also campaigned on net neutrality, mass surveillance and anti-corruption measures.

Organization

The group operates an online organizing network with millions of members and runs both a 501(c)(4) advocacy arm and an affiliated political action committee, the Demand Progress PAC.
