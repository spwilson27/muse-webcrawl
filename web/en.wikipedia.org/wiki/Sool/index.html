Sool

Sool (Somali: Sool) is an administrative region in northern Somalia. Its capital is Las Anod. The region is part of the territory claimed by the self-declared Republic of Somaliland, while many local clans, predominantly the Dhulbahante, have at various times aligned with Puntland or sought a separate administration; control of the region has been contested, and in 2023 fighting around Las Anod led to Somaliland forces withdrawing from the city.

Geography

Sool borders Sanaag to the north, Togdheer to the west, Ethiopia to the south, and the Nugal and Bari regions to the east. Much of the region is semi-arid plateau, part of the wider Haud grazing lands.

History

The region was a centre of the Dervish movement led by Mohammed Abdullah Hassan in the early 20th century, with its capital at Taleh. After the Somali civil war, authority in Sool shifted between Somaliland and Puntland, reflecting the allegiances of its clan elders. Local movements such as Khatumo State have sought recognition as a federal member state of Somalia.

Districts

Districts of Sool include Las Anod, Aynabo, Taleh, Hudun, and Bo'ame.
