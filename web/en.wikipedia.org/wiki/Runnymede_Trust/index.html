Runnymede Trust

The Runnymede Trust is an independent race equality think tank based in London, United Kingdom. It was founded in 1968 with the aim of promoting a multicultural Britain through research, public education and policy work.

Work

Runnymede publishes research on ethnic inequalities in education, employment, health, housing and the criminal justice system. Its best-known publication is the 1997 report The Future of Multi-Ethnic Britain, produced by the Commission on the Future of Multi-Ethnic Britain chaired by Bhikhu Parekh, which attracted wide public debate. The Trust also coined or popularised discussion of the term "Islamophobia" through its 1997 report Islamophobia: A Challenge for Us All.
