The Russian Olympic Committee (ROC) competed at the 2022 Winter Olympics in Beijing, China, from 4 to 20 February 2022. Because of sanctions imposed for state-sponsored doping and the cover-up of data from the Moscow anti-doping laboratory, Russian athletes competed under the ROC name and flag rather than those of Russia, and Tchaikovsky's Piano Concerto No. 1 replaced the Russian anthem for gold medallists.

Results

The ROC team finished second in total medals with 32, including six golds. Cross-country skier Alexander Bolshunov won three gold medals, and the ROC men's ice hockey team reached the final, losing to Finland. Figure skating was marred by the doping case of Kamila Valieva, who tested positive for trimetazidine in a sample given in December 2021; the team event medals were withheld pending legal proceedings.

Context

Beijing 2022 was the second consecutive Winter Games at which Russian athletes competed under a neutral designation, following "Olympic Athletes from Russia" at Pyeongchang 2018.
