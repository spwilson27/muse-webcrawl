IEEE Spectrum

Flagship magazine of the IEEE

A cover of IEEE Spectrum

IEEE Spectrum is the flagship magazine and website of the IEEE, the world's largest professional association for electrical and electronics engineers and allied disciplines. Published monthly since 1964 (descending from earlier institute journals reaching back to the nineteenth century), it covers technology, engineering and science news and features for a broad professional readership.

Content and reputation

The magazine is known for accessible, deeply reported features on computing, energy, robotics, telecommunications and biomedical engineering, along with annual special features such as its " Top Tech" round-ups and salary surveys of the profession. Its journalism has won numerous National Magazine Awards and other honours unusual for an association publication.

Audience and editions

IEEE Spectrum is distributed to IEEE members worldwide and has regional and language editions; its website publishes daily reporting independent of the print issue.
