Chocolate cake

Chocolate cake
Type: Cake
Main ingredients: Chocolate or cocoa powder, flour, sugar, eggs, butter
Variations: Devil's food, Black Forest, molten lava cake, Sachertorte

Chocolate cake is a cake flavoured with melted chocolate, cocoa powder, or both. It is one of the most popular cake types worldwide, served at birthdays, weddings and celebrations.

History

Chocolate drinks reached Europe from the Americas in the 16th century, but solid chocolate cakes only became practical after the Dutch process for cocoa powder (1828) and cheaper chocolate production in the 19th century. An early American recipe appeared in Eliza Leslie's 1847 cookbook. By the early 20th century chocolate layer cakes were standard in American baking; the German chocolate cake (named for Samuel German, not Germany) dates to 1957 newspaper publication in Texas.

Varieties

Devil's food cake is a rich, dark, moist variant; Black Forest cake (Schwarzwälder Kirschtorte) layers chocolate sponge with cherries and cream; molten chocolate cake has a liquid centre; flourless chocolate cake relies on eggs and chocolate; the Austrian Sachertorte is coated in apricot jam and dark icing.

Preparation

Typical methods cream butter and sugar, add eggs and melted chocolate or cocoa, then flour and leavening, and bake in round pans before frosting with ganache, buttercream or fudge icing.
