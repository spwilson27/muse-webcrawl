Stickhandling

Stickhandling is a technique in ice hockey and related stick sports in which a player controls the puck (or ball) with the blade of the stick, moving it rapidly from side to side to maintain possession, deceive opponents, and prepare passes or shots.

Technique

Effective stickhandling relies on soft hands, rolling the wrists, keeping the head up, and controlling the puck with the full length of the blade. Players practise with drills using balls, pucks, and obstacles to develop touch at speed and in tight spaces.

Deception

Advanced stickhandling includes dekes — feints such as toe drags, between-the-legs moves, and the lacrosse-style scoop popularised in highlight reels. In floorball, field hockey, and bandy, analogous skills are central to attacking play.
