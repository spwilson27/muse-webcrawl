The Leftovers is an American supernatural mystery drama television series created by Damon Lindelof and Tom Perrotta, based on Perrotta's 2011 novel of the same name. The series premiered on HBO on June 29, 2014, and concluded on June 4, 2017, after three seasons and 28 episodes.

Premise

The series begins three years after the "Sudden Departure", an unexplained global event in which 2% of the world's population—140 million people—simultaneously disappeared. The story focuses on Kevin Garvey (Justin Theroux), a police chief in the fictional town of Mapleton, New York, and his family, as well as Nora Durst (Carrie Coon), a woman who lost her entire family in the Departure. The first season largely follows the novel's storyline, while the second and third seasons venture into original territory, relocating the narrative to the fictional town of Jarden, Texas, and later to Australia.

Cast and characters

The ensemble cast includes Justin Theroux as Kevin Garvey, Amy Brenneman as Laurie Garvey, Christopher Eccleston as Matt Jamison, Liv Tyler as Megan "Meg" Abbott, Chris Zylka as Tom Garvey, Margaret Qualley as Jill Garvey, Carrie Coon as Nora Durst, Emily Meade as Aimee, Amanda Warren as Lucy Warburton, Ann Dowd as Patti Levin, Michael Gaston as Dean, Max and Charlie Carver as Adam and Scott Frost, Annie Q. as Christine, Janel Moloney as Mary Jamison, Regina King as Erika Murphy, Kevin Carroll as John Murphy, and Scott Glenn as Kevin Garvey Sr.

Production

HBO acquired the rights to Perrotta's novel before its publication. Lindelof, co-creator of Lost, served as showrunner. The pilot was directed by Peter Berg. Music for the series was composed by Max Richter, whose score—including the piece "The Leftovers (Main Title Theme)" and recurring use of "November"—received widespread praise.

Reception

The first season received generally positive reviews, though some critics found its tone relentlessly bleak. The second and third seasons were met with widespread critical acclaim; the third season holds a near-unanimous positive rating on review aggregators, and the series finale, "The Book of Nora", is frequently cited among the greatest television episodes of all time. The series has appeared on numerous critics' lists of the best television shows of the 2010s.

Episodes

Season 1 (2014) comprises 10 episodes set primarily in Mapleton. Season 2 (2015) comprises 10 episodes set in Jarden, Texas, a town from which no one departed. Season 3 (2017) comprises 8 episodes, largely set in Australia in the lead-up to the seventh anniversary of the Sudden Departure.
