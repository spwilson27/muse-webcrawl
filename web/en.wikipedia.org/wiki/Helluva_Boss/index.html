Helluva Boss

Helluva Boss is an American adult animated web series created by Vivienne Medrano. Set in the same Hell as her series Hazbin Hotel, it follows the employees of I.M.P. (Immediate Murder Professionals), an assassination company run by the imp Blitzo, whose workers use a stolen grimoire belonging to the demon prince Stolas to travel to the living world and carry out hits for damned clients. The pilot premiered on YouTube on 25 November 2019, and the series proper began on 31 October 2020.

Characters

The main cast includes Blitzo (voiced by Brandon Rogers), the chaotic founder of I.M.P.; weapons expert Moxxie (Richard Horvitz) and his wife Millie (Vivian Nixon), the company's cheerful bruiser; hellhound receptionist Loona; and the owl-like Goetia prince Stolas (Bryce Pinkham), whose affair with Blitzo gives I.M.P. access to Earth. Episodes mix workplace comedy with an ongoing serialized storyline about Stolas's marriage and Blitzo's past.

Production and reception

The series is produced by SpindleHorse Toons and released independently on Medrano's YouTube channel, funded partly through merchandise and Patreon. Episodes feature original musical numbers and have accumulated hundreds of millions of views; critics and audiences have praised the animation, voice acting and emotional storytelling. In 2025 it was announced that the series would also stream on Amazon Prime Video, with earlier seasons arriving on the platform alongside new episodes.
