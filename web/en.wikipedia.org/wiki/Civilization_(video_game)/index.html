Civilization (video game)

Sid Meier's Civilization is a turn-based strategy video game released in 1991 by MicroProse, designed by Sid Meier and Bruce Shelley. The player guides a civilisation from 4000 BC to the near future, competing with rival civilisations to build an empire that "stands the test of time".

Gameplay

Starting with a single settler unit, the player founds cities, explores a randomly generated map, researches technologies along a branching tech tree, builds wonders of the world, manages diplomacy and wages war. Victory comes by conquering the world, or by being the first to launch a spaceship to Alpha Centauri. The game popularised the "just one more turn" style of play and helped define the 4X genre.

Legacy

Civilization is widely regarded as one of the most influential video games ever made. It launched a long-running series with sequels from Civilization II (1996) through Civilization VII (2025), as well as spin-offs and imitators. A running joke in the series portrays the pacifist Mahatma Gandhi as peculiarly fond of nuclear weapons, an artefact of an integer-underflow bug in the original game's aggression values.
