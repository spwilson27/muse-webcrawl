The Eurovision Song Contest 2015 was the 60th edition of the annual Eurovision Song Contest. It took place in Vienna, Austria, following the country's victory at the 2014 contest with the song "Rise Like a Phoenix" by Conchita Wurst. Hosted by the European Broadcasting Union (EBU) and Österreichischer Rundfunk (ORF), the contest was held at the Wiener Stadthalle, with two semi-finals on 19 and 21 May and the final on 23 May 2015.

Format and participants

Forty countries participated, including Australia, which was invited to compete as a special guest to mark the contest's 60th anniversary. The shows were presented by Mirjam Weichselbraun, Alice Tumler, and Arabella Kiesbauer, with Conchita Wurst as green-room host. ORF designed the stage as a giant eye-shaped portal with a kinetic sculpture of spheres.

Sweden won with 365 points, its sixth victory. Notably, the United Kingdom, France, Spain, and host Austria scored poorly; Austria and Germany both finished with zero points, Austria being the first host country to score none.
