David Heinemeier Hansson

David Heinemeier Hansson (born 15 October 1979), known as DHH, is a Danish programmer, writer, and racing driver. He is the creator of the Ruby on Rails web application framework and a co-founder and co-owner of the software company 37signals (now Basecamp), makers of Basecamp and the HEY email service. He has co-authored several business books, including Rework and Remote: Office Not Required.

Career

Hansson extracted Ruby on Rails in 2004 from his work on the project management tool Basecamp at 37signals; the open-source framework won him the Google/O'Reilly Hacker of the Year award in 2005 and shaped a generation of web development with its "convention over configuration" philosophy. With Jason Fried he has written bestselling books arguing for calm, bootstrapped, remote-friendly companies. In 2020 the company launched HEY, an email service that attracted attention for its public dispute with Apple over App Store rules.

Motorsport

As a racing driver, Hansson has competed in the FIA World Endurance Championship and the 24 Hours of Le Mans, winning the GTE Am class at Le Mans in 2014 with Aston Martin Racing.
