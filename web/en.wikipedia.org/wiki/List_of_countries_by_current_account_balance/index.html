List of countries by current account balance

This is a list of countries by current account balance, based on data from the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and the World Bank. The current account is one of the two main components of a country's balance of payments, the other being the capital account. It records the net flow of goods, services, primary income and secondary income (transfers) between a country and the rest of the world.

A current account surplus means a country exports more than it imports in the broad sense, and is therefore a net lender to the rest of the world; a deficit means the country is a net borrower. Persistent large surpluses and deficits are sometimes described as global imbalances.

Overview

Historically, the largest surplus countries have included export-oriented economies such as China, Germany, Japan and the oil-exporting states of the Persian Gulf, while the United States has run the world's largest current account deficit in absolute terms for several decades. Measured as a percentage of GDP, small financial centres and commodity exporters often record the most extreme values.
