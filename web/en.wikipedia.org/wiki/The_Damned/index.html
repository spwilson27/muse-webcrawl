The Damned are an English rock band formed in London in 1976 by Dave Vanian, Brian James, Captain Sensible, and Rat Scabies. They were the first English punk band to release a single — "New Rose", in October 1976 — and the first to release a studio album, Damned Damned Damned (1977), as well as the first English punk act to tour the United States.

Career

After a chaotic early period and a brief breakup, the band re-formed and developed a darker, more melodic sound on Machine Gun Etiquette (1979) and The Black Album (1980). Their gothic image, spearheaded by Vanian's vampiric stage persona, made them pioneers of gothic rock. In the mid-1980s they achieved mainstream success with the singles "Eloise" (a UK No. 3 hit in 1986) and "Alone Again Or".

Legacy

Unlike many of their punk contemporaries, the Damned have continued recording and touring for decades, and are recognised for bridging punk, goth, and psychedelic rock.
