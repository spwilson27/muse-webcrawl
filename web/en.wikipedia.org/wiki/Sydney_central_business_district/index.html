Sydney central business district

The Sydney central business district (CBD) is the main commercial centre of Sydney, New South Wales, Australia. It extends from Circular Quay in the north to around Central railway station in the south, and from Darling Harbour in the west to Hyde Park and the Domain in the east.

Geography and landmarks

The CBD contains Australia's densest concentration of skyscrapers, including Sydney Tower, and heritage landmarks such as the Queen Victoria Building, Sydney Town Hall, the State Library of New South Wales and the government precinct along Macquarie Street. The Rocks, site of the first European settlement, lies at its north-western edge.

Economy and transport

The district is Australia's financial capital, home to the Reserve Bank and the headquarters of major banks and firms. It is served by heavy rail, the Sydney Metro, light rail, buses and ferries from Circular Quay, and has been progressively pedestrianised around George Street.
