Brony (plural: bronies), a portmanteau of "bro" and "pony", is a term for a member of the brony fandom — the adult fandom of the animated television series My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic, which premiered in 2010. Although the show was produced for young girls and their parents, it attracted a large following of older viewers, especially adult men, who appreciated its writing, characters, music, and Flash animation.

History

The fandom began on the imageboard 4chan in late 2010 and rapidly spread across forums, YouTube, and fan-art sites such as DeviantArt. Fans produced an enormous volume of fan fiction, music, art, and animations, and organized conventions including BronyCon in New York and later Baltimore, which drew over 10,000 attendees at its peak before concluding in 2019.

Culture and reception

The phenomenon attracted media attention and academic study as an example of participatory fan culture and "new sincerity". Hasbro, the show's owner, generally embraced the unexpected audience. The fandom also engaged in substantial charity work, and a documentary, Bronies: The Extremely Unexpected Adult Fans of My Little Pony, was released in 2012.
