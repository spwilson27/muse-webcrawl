Dust collector

A dust collector is a system used to enhance the quality of air released from industrial and commercial processes by collecting dust and other impurities from air or gas. Designed to handle high-volume dust loads, a dust collector consists of a blower, dust filter, a filter-cleaning system, and a dust receptacle or removal system. It differs from domestic air purifiers in capacity and robustness.

Types

Common types include baghouses, which draw air through fabric filter bags that are cleaned by shaking or pulses of compressed air; cartridge collectors, using pleated filter media; cyclone separators, which spin particles out of the airstream by centrifugal force; wet scrubbers; and electrostatic precipitators, which charge and capture particles on collector plates.

Applications

Dust collection is essential in woodworking, metalworking, pharmaceutical and cement plants, both to protect workers' health and to reduce the risk of combustible dust explosions, which regulators address through standards such as NFPA 652 in the United States.
