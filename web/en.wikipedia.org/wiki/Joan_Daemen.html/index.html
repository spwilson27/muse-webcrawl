Joan Daemen

Joan Daemen (born 1965) is a Belgian cryptographer known for co-designing the Rijndael cipher, which was selected as the Advanced Encryption Standard (AES), and the Keccak hash function, standardized as SHA-3.

Career

Daemen studied at KU Leuven, where he received his PhD in 1995, and has worked for STMicroelectronics and Radboud University in Nijmegen. With Vincent Rijmen he designed Rijndael, chosen by NIST in 2000 to become AES.

Keccak

With Guido Bertoni, Michaël Peeters and Gilles Van Assche, Daemen developed the Keccak sponge construction, which won the NIST SHA-3 competition in 2012 and was standardized in 2015.
