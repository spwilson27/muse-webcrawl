Bridge (card game)

Bridge

Players: 4 (two partnerships)

Cards: 52

Type: Trick-taking game

Contract bridge, usually simply called bridge, is a trick-taking card game played by four players in two competing partnerships with a standard 52-card deck. It evolved from whist in the late nineteenth century and became one of the world's most popular card games.

Play

The game consists of an auction (bidding) phase, in which partnerships bid for the contract — the number of tricks they undertake to win with a named trump suit or no trumps — followed by play of the cards, in which the declarer's partner's hand (the dummy) is laid face up. Scoring rewards fulfilled contracts and penalises defeated ones; duplicate bridge, in which the same deals are played at multiple tables, is the standard competitive form.

Popularity

Bridge is governed internationally by the World Bridge Federation. Famous devotees have included Warren Buffett and Bill Gates.
