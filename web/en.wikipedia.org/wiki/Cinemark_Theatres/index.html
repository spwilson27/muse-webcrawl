Cinemark Theatres is an American movie theatre chain that started operations in 1984. It is owned by Cinemark Holdings, Inc., headquartered in Plano, Texas. Cinemark operates theatres under several brands, including Cinemark, Century Theatres, Tinseltown, CinéArts and Rave Cinemas.

History

Cinemark was founded by Lee Roy Mitchell in 1984, opening its first theatres in Texas. Through the 1990s and 2000s the company expanded across the United States and into Latin America, where it operates in countries including Mexico, Brazil, Argentina and Colombia. In 2006 Cinemark acquired Century Theatres, adding more than 300 locations.

The company completed an initial public offering in 2007 and trades on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol CNK. Cinemark is typically ranked among the three largest motion picture exhibitors in the United States, alongside AMC Theatres and Regal Cinemas.

Formats and brands

Cinemark operates the large-format Cinemark XD (Extreme Digital Cinema) premium screens, and has introduced luxury concepts such as recliner seating, reserved seating and dine-in locations. Its Movie Club subscription programme, launched in 2017, was among the first subscription offerings from a major US exhibitor.
