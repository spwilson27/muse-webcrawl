The president of Hungary (Hungarian: Magyarország köztársasági elnöke) is the head of state of Hungary. The office has a largely ceremonial and representational role, with executive power exercised by the government led by the prime minister.

Election

The president is elected by the National Assembly for a term of five years and may serve a maximum of two terms. Candidates must be Hungarian citizens over 35, nominated by at least 40 members of parliament.

Role and powers

The president signs laws into force but may return a law to parliament for reconsideration or refer it to the Constitutional Court. The president formally appoints the prime minister, ministers, judges, and ambassadors, sets election dates, and represents Hungary internationally. Tamás Sulyok became president in 2024.
