Medical University of Vienna

Established: 1365 (as part of the University of Vienna); independent 2004
Location: Vienna, Austria
Students: about 8,000

The Medical University of Vienna (German: Medizinische Universität Wien, MedUni Wien) is one of the largest medical universities in Europe and the largest medical training institution in the German-speaking world. It became an independent university in 2004, having been the medical faculty of the University of Vienna, founded in 1365.

History

The Vienna Medical School was a world centre of medicine in the 18th and 19th centuries, associated with figures such as Gerard van Swieten, Ignaz Semmelweis, Theodor Billroth, Karl Landsteiner and Julius Wagner-Jauregg; several of its researchers won Nobel Prizes. The university works closely with the Vienna General Hospital (AKH), one of Europe's largest hospitals.

Organisation

The university is organised into dozens of departments and clinical institutes, with degree programmes in human medicine and dentistry and extensive doctoral and research activity in fields from immunology to medical imaging.
