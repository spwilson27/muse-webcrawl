Inhumans

The Inhumans are a superhuman race of superheroes appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. They were created by writer Stan Lee and artist Jack Kirby, and first appeared in Fantastic Four #45 (December 1965).

Their home, the city of Attilan, is frequently referred to in stories as the Great Refuge. The Inhumans are descended from an ancient experiment by the alien Kree on early humans: the Kree spliced alien DNA into a group of prehistoric humans, hoping to create super-soldiers for their war against the Skrulls. The experiment was abandoned, and the subjects flourished in isolation, developing advanced technology and a rigid caste society.

Terrigenesis

Inhuman abilities are activated through exposure to the Terrigen Mist, a mutagenic vapour released from Terrigen Crystals, in a ceremony called Terrigenesis. Each Inhuman who undergoes the process emerges with a unique power, though the transformation can also produce severe physical mutations, and Inhuman society traditionally assigns social roles based on the outcome.

Royal Family

The Inhumans are ruled by a royal family whose most prominent members include Black Bolt (Blackagar Boltagon), their silent king whose voice can unleash devastating sonic energy; Medusa, his wife and interpreter, whose hair is prehensile and immensely strong; Crystal, who manipulates the classical elements; Gorgon, whose hooves generate seismic shockwaves; Karnak, who can perceive the weak point in any structure or opponent; Triton, an amphibious scout; and Lockjaw, an enormous bulldog-like creature who can teleport.

In other media

The Inhumans have appeared in animated series and were featured in the Marvel Cinematic Universe: Inhumans were introduced in the television series Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. (2015), and a dedicated series, Marvel's Inhumans, premiered in 2017 on ABC after an IMAX preview of its first episodes. Black Bolt later appeared in the film Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness (2022) as a member of the Illuminati of Earth-838.
