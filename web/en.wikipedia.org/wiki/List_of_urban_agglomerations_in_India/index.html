List of urban agglomerations in India

An urban agglomeration in India, as defined by the Census of India, is a continuous urban spread consisting of a town and its adjoining outgrowths, or two or more physically contiguous towns. At the 2011 Census there were 53 urban agglomerations with populations over one million, up from 35 in 2001.

India's urban structure is highly concentrated: the three largest agglomerations — Mumbai, Delhi and Kolkata — each exceed 14 million people, and together the million-plus cities house a large share of the country's urban population. Census towns and statutory towns within these agglomerations are administered separately even where they form one continuous built-up area.
