The African Queen (film)

The African Queen
Director: John Huston
Year: 1951
Starring: Humphrey Bogart, Katharine Hepburn
Based on: C. S. Forester novel (1935)

The African Queen is a 1951 British-American adventure film directed by John Huston, adapted from C. S. Forester's novel. During the First World War in German East Africa, missionary Rose Sayer (Katharine Hepburn) persuades gin-soaked riverboat captain Charlie Allnut (Humphrey Bogart) to attack a German gunboat using his launch, the African Queen.

Production

Filmed on location in Uganda and the Congo in Technicolor, the shoot was famously arduous. Bogart won the Academy Award for Best Actor — his only Oscar.

Legacy

Selected for the US National Film Registry; ranked highly on AFI lists.
