Eidos Interactive Limited was a British video game publisher based in London. Founded from the earlier publisher Domark, it became one of the largest game publishers of the late 1990s, best known for the Tomb Raider series and its heroine Lara Croft, developed by Core Design.

Eidos was the publisher of Tomb Raider and Hitman.

History

The company took shape in the mid-1990s through the merger of Domark, Simis and Big Red Software and the acquisition of Core Design and U.S. Gold. The runaway success of Tomb Raider (1996) made Eidos a major public company, and it expanded rapidly, acquiring studios including Crystal Dynamics and IO Interactive. Major franchises included Hitman, Deus Ex, Thief, Legacy of Kain, Commandos and Championship Manager.

Decline and acquisition

Over-expansion, uneven releases and accounting troubles weakened the company in the early 2000s. After a bidding contest, Eidos was acquired by SCi Entertainment in 2005; the combined group later adopted the Eidos name. In 2009 the company was bought by Japanese publisher Square Enix, becoming Square Enix Europe; the Eidos brand survived in studio names such as Eidos-Montréal, developer of later Deus Ex and Thief titles.
