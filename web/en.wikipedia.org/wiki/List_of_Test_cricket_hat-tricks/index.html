List of Test cricket hat-tricks

A hat-trick occurs in cricket when a bowler dismisses three batsmen with consecutive deliveries. Hat-tricks are exceptionally rare in Test cricket: since the first by Australia's Fred Spofforth in 1879, fewer than 50 have been taken in over 2,500 Test matches. Only four bowlers — Hugh Trumble, Jimmy Matthews, Wasim Akram and Stuart Broad — have taken two Test hat-tricks; Matthews' two came in the same match in 1912, a unique feat.
