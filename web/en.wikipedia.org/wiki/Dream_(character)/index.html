Dream, also known as Morpheus and other names, is a fictional character and the protagonist of the comic book series The Sandman, written by Neil Gaiman and published by DC Comics under its Vertigo imprint. He is one of the seven Endless, anthropomorphic personifications of fundamental metaphysical concepts, and is the lord of dreams and stories, ruler of the realm called the Dreaming.

Character overview

Dream is typically depicted as a tall, pale man with wild dark hair and a sombre, proud demeanour. The series begins with his capture in 1916 by occultists who had intended to imprison his sister Death; he escapes in 1988 and must rebuild his realm and recover his tools of office — his helm, his pouch of sand and his dreamstone.

The Endless

His siblings are Destiny, Death, Destruction, Desire, Despair and Delirium. Across the series, Dream's pride and rigid adherence to his responsibilities bring him into conflict with gods, demons, mortals and his own family, and he gradually — and tragically — changes.

In other media

Dream is played by Tom Sturridge in the Netflix television adaptation of The Sandman, which premiered in 2022.
