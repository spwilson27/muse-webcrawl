The Croatia national football team represents Croatia in men's international football matches. It is controlled by the Croatian Football Federation (HNS), the governing body for football in Croatia. The team's distinctive kit features a red-and-white checkerboard pattern derived from the Croatian coat of arms, and the team is nicknamed Vatreni ("the Blazers").

History

Croatia played unofficial matches in the 1940s as a wartime state and as part of Yugoslavia, whose national team Croatian players helped power for decades. Following Croatia's independence, the team was re-established in 1990 and admitted to UEFA and FIFA in the early 1990s. Croatia's first major tournament was UEFA Euro 1996, where it reached the quarter-finals.

The 1998 breakthrough

At the 1998 FIFA World Cup in France, Croatia's first World Cup, the team stunned the football world by finishing third, defeating Germany 3–0 in the quarter-finals before losing narrowly to hosts France in the semi-finals. Striker Davor Šuker won the Golden Boot with six goals.

2018 World Cup final

Led by coach Zlatko Dalić and captain Luka Modrić, Croatia reached the final of the 2018 World Cup in Russia — the smallest nation by population to reach a World Cup final since Uruguay in 1950. Croatia won all its group matches and survived three consecutive knockout matches that went to extra time, beating England 2–1 in the semi-final before losing 4–2 to France in the final. Modrić won the tournament's Golden Ball and later the 2018 Ballon d'Or. Croatia followed this with a third-place finish at the 2022 World Cup in Qatar and reached the final of the 2023 UEFA Nations League, losing to Spain on penalties.
