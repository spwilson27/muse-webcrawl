Turk (rapper)

Turk
Birth name: Tab Virgil Jr.
Born: New Orleans, Louisiana, US
Group: Hot Boys (Cash Money Records)

Tab Virgil Jr., better known by his stage name Turk (or Young Turk), is an American rapper from New Orleans, Louisiana. He rose to prominence in the late 1990s as a member of the Hot Boys, the Cash Money Records group that also included Lil Wayne, Juvenile and B.G.

Career

Turk appeared on the Hot Boys albums Get It How U Live! (1997) and Guerrilla Warfare (1999), the latter reaching number one on the Billboard Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums chart. His debut solo album, Young & Thuggin', was released in 2001 and reached the top 10 of the Billboard 200. Subsequent solo albums included Raw & Uncut (2003) and Still a Hot Boy (2005).

Legal issues

Turk's career was interrupted by an extended prison sentence arising from a 2004 shooting incident involving a police officer in Memphis, Tennessee. Released in 2012, he subsequently resumed recording and performed reunion shows with his former Hot Boys bandmates.
