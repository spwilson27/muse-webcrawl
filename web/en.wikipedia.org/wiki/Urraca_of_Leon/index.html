Urraca of León

Urraca of León

Reign: 1109–1126

Born: c. 1079, León

Died: 8 March 1126, Saldaña

Spouses: Raymond of Burgundy; Alfonso I of Aragon

Urraca (c. 1079 – 8 March 1126), called the Temeraria ("the Reckless"), was Queen of León, Castile and Galicia from 1109 until her death. She was the daughter and successor of Alfonso VI and one of the first women in European history to reign in her own right over a major kingdom.

Reign

Urraca inherited the throne after the death of her half-brother Sancho at the Battle of Uclés in 1108 and her father the following year. Her marriage to Alfonso I of Aragon, arranged to unite the Christian kingdoms, collapsed into open warfare between the spouses. Rebellions in Galicia and conflicts with her sister Teresa of Portugal further tested her rule.

Succession

Urraca championed the rights of her son by Raymond of Burgundy, whom she had crowned Alfonso VII in Santiago de Compostela in 1111. Despite the turmoil of her reign, she defended her kingdom against the Almoravids and preserved it for her successor, dying in 1126.
