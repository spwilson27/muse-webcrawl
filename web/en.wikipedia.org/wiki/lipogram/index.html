Lipogram

A lipogram (from the Ancient Greek lipográmmatos, "leaving out a letter") is a kind of constrained writing or word game consisting of writing paragraphs or longer works in which a particular letter or group of letters is avoided.

Examples

Ernest Vincent Wright's 1939 novel Gadsby is written entirely without the letter "e". Georges Perec's 1969 novel La Disparition (translated into English as A Void) also omits the letter "e", the most common letter in both French and English.

History

Lipograms date back to ancient Greek authors such as Lasus of Hermione, who wrote hymns avoiding the letter sigma.
