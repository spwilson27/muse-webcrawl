Kokemäki (Swedish: Kumo) is a town and municipality of Finland in the Satakunta region, situated on the banks of the river Kokemäenjoki.

History

The Kokemäki area has been inhabited since the Stone Age; the Iron Age Teljä settlement mentioned in early sources is associated with the river valley. The municipality was founded in 1869, and Kokemäki received town status in 1977. The medieval church of St. Henry's chapel in nearby Kakkulainen, linked to the legend of Bishop Henry, is a site of historical interest in the region.

Economy and transport

Agriculture and food processing dominate the local economy. The Tampere–Pori railway passes through the town.
