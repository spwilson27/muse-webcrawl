Virgin Galactic

Virgin Galactic is an American spaceflight company founded in 2004 by Richard Branson's Virgin Group. It develops commercial spacecraft to provide suborbital spaceflights to paying space tourists, flying from Spaceport America in New Mexico. The company is publicly traded on the New York Stock Exchange.

System and history

Virgin Galactic's system evolved from SpaceShipOne, the Ansari X Prize-winning craft designed by Burt Rutan's Scaled Composites. Its second-generation vehicle pairs the carrier aircraft VMS Eve with the rocket-powered SpaceShipTwo spaceplane, which is released at altitude, fires its hybrid rocket motor to climb above 80 kilometres, and glides back to a runway landing, giving passengers several minutes of weightlessness. The programme suffered a major setback in 2014 when the first SpaceShipTwo, VSS Enterprise, broke up in flight, killing co-pilot Michael Alsbury.

The replacement vehicle, VSS Unity, made its first spaceflight in December 2018. In July 2021 Unity flew a full crew including Branson himself, and regular commercial service began in 2023 with monthly flights for researchers and private astronauts. Unity was retired in mid-2024 after its seventh commercial flight, as the company focused resources on its next-generation Delta-class spaceplanes, expected to enter service around 2026 with higher flight rates and six seats.
