Real World Records

Founded: 1989
Founder: Peter Gabriel
Genre: World music
Location: Box, Wiltshire, England

Real World Records is a British record label specialising in world music. It was founded in 1989 by musician Peter Gabriel, growing out of the success of the WOMAD (World of Music, Arts and Dance) festival, and is based at Real World Studios in Box, Wiltshire.

History

Gabriel established the label to provide a platform for artists from around the world whose music was receiving acclaim at WOMAD festivals but who lacked access to high-quality recording facilities and international distribution. The label's launch releases in 1989 included albums by Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, the Guo Brothers, and Tabu Ley Rochereau.

Real World Records releases are known for their high production values and distinctive visual presentation, with early artwork frequently designed by the graphic studio Assorted Images under Malcolm Garrett. The label's catalogue spans music from Africa, Asia, Latin America, and Europe, and it has released recordings by artists including Sheila Chandra, Afro Celt Sound System, Papa Wemba, Yungchen Lhamo, and Martyn Bennett.

Real World Studios

The label is closely associated with Real World Studios, a residential recording complex built in a converted mill near Bath. Its flagship "Big Room" is a large collaborative recording space designed so musicians can work facing one another rather than separated by glass.
