Alfred Nobel

Alfred Nobel (1833–1896) was a Swedish chemist, engineer and inventor, most famous for inventing dynamite and for bequeathing his fortune to establish the Nobel Prizes.

The prizes

Nobel held hundreds of patents and built an industrial empire in armaments and explosives. His 1895 will directed most of his estate to prizes in physics, chemistry, medicine, literature and peace, first awarded in 1901.
