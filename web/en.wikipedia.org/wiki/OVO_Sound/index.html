OVO Sound is a Canadian record label founded in 2012 by the rapper Drake, his producer Noah "40" Shebib, and manager Oliver El-Khatib. The name derives from Drake's October's Very Own (OVO) brand — Drake was born in October — whose owl logo became one of the most recognisable marks in popular music and streetwear.

History

The label grew out of the October's Very Own blog and collective Drake and his team had run since the late 2000s, and was formally launched in 2012 with a distribution deal through Warner Bros. Records. Its first major signing was the singer PartyNextDoor, followed by the duo Majid Jordan, the R&B duo dvsn, Roy Woods, Plaza, Baka Not Nice, and later acts such as Smiley and Naïka. The label also ran the OVO Sound Radio programme, first on Beats 1/Apple Music and later on Sirius XM, which Drake used to premiere much of his own music.

OVO Fest and brand

Beyond recordings, OVO Sound is closely tied to the annual OVO Fest in Toronto, launched in 2010, which became famous for surprise superstar guests. The OVO name extends to a successful clothing line (in collaboration with Canada Goose, the Toronto Raptors, and others), flagship stores in Toronto, New York, Los Angeles and London, and Drake's broader business ventures.
