Exceptional Lie algebra

An exceptional Lie algebra is a simple Lie algebra whose Dynkin diagram belongs to one of the five exceptional types: G2, F4, E6, E7, and E8. Unlike the four infinite classical families (A, B, C, D) arising from matrix groups, the exceptionals do not fit into a uniform pattern and are deeply connected with the octonions.

Dimensions

  • G2: dimension 14 — the automorphism group of the octonions.
  • F4: dimension 52.
  • E6: dimension 78.
  • E7: dimension 133.
  • E8: dimension 248 — the largest exceptional algebra, whose root lattice is the E8 lattice.

Constructions

The exceptional algebras can be constructed using Jordan algebras and the Freudenthal–Tits magic square, which produces G2, F4, E6, E7, and E8 from pairs of division algebras. E8 appears prominently in string theory (the E8 × E8 heterotic string) and in the 2007 "exceptionally simple theory of everything" proposal of Garrett Lisi.
