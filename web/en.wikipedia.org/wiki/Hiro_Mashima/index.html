Hiro Mashima

Hiro Mashima (真島 ヒロ, born May 3, 1977) is a Japanese manga artist best known as the creator of Fairy Tail and Rave Master. His series are published in Kodansha's Weekly Shōnen Magazine and have been among the magazine's most successful titles, with wide international circulation and anime adaptations.

Career

Born in Nagano, Mashima was influenced by Akira Toriyama's Dragon Ball and aspired to draw manga from childhood. He won a Kodansha newcomers' prize for the one-shot "Magician" and debuted professionally in 1998. His first long series, Rave Master (1999–2005), ran 35 volumes. Fairy Tail (2006–2017) became a global hit, following the wizard guild of the title, and spawned anime series, films, and spin-offs written or supervised by Mashima. In 2018 he launched the science-fantasy series Edens Zero, which concluded in 2024; he subsequently began the sequel/spin-off Fairy Tail: 100 Years Quest in story duties and started new work including the farming-game-based Farmagia designs.

Style and output

Mashima is noted for a prodigious work pace — often producing weekly chapters partly drawn by a small team under his close direction — and for a clean, dynamic style influenced by Toriyama and Eiichiro Oda, who was a fellow assistant-era contemporary. He frequently engages fans online and incorporates their feedback.
