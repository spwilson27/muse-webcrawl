Manx Grand Prix

Location: Isle of Man
Course: Snaefell Mountain Course, 37.73 miles
First held: 1923 (as the Manx Amateur Road Races)
Classes: Senior, Junior, Lightweight, Classic and others

The Manx Grand Prix is an annual motorcycle road racing event held on the Isle of Man over the Snaefell Mountain Course, traditionally in late August and early September. It is widely regarded as a proving ground for riders aspiring to compete in the Isle of Man TT.

History

The event began in 1923 as the Manx Amateur Road Races, created for amateur competitors excluded from the professional TT. It took the name Manx Grand Prix in 1930. Except for wartime interruptions and cancellations such as the 2001 foot-and-mouth outbreak and the COVID-19 pandemic years, it has run annually ever since.

Races

The meeting includes races for modern machinery — Senior, Junior (Supersport), and Lightweight (Supertwin) — as well as a Newcomers race for first-time competitors on the Mountain Course. Since 2013, the associated Classic TT (later rebranded as part of the Manx Grand Prix programme) has featured historic racing motorcycles.

Notable riders

Many future TT stars, including multiple-race winners, first learned the Mountain Course at the Manx Grand Prix. Winning a Manx GP "replica" trophy is a traditional milestone on the way to a professional road racing career.
