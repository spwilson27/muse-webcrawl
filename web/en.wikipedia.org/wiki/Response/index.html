Response

Response may refer to a reaction or answer to a stimulus, an action, or a request. In general usage, a response is something done, spoken, or written as a reaction to something else.

In psychology and biology

In psychology, a response is any behaviour that results from a stimulus, forming the second half of the stimulus–response model central to behaviourism. In biology, a response is a change in an organism or part of an organism brought about by a stimulus, such as tropisms in plants or reflexes in animals.

In computing

In computing, a response is the output returned by a system, server, or program following a request; for example, an HTTP response carries a status code and content from a web server to a client. In electronics, the response of a circuit describes how its output varies with its input over time or frequency.

Other uses

In music, a responsory is a chant sung in alternation between a soloist and a choir; in call and response, a phrase is answered by another phrase. In liturgy, a response is a verse or sentence spoken by the congregation after a reading or prayer. Emergency response refers to the actions of police, fire, and medical services after an incident.
