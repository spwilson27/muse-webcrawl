Qingpu District

Qingpu District (simplified Chinese: 青浦区; traditional Chinese: 青浦區; pinyin: Qīngpǔ Qū) is a suburban district of Shanghai Municipality, China, bordering Jiangsu Province to the west. It covers about 676 km² and had a population of roughly 1.27 million at the 2020 census.

Geography and sights

The district surrounds Dianshan Lake, Shanghai's largest natural freshwater lake and the source of the Huangpu River. Its best-known attraction is Zhujiajiao, a Ming- and Qing-era water town of canals and stone bridges. The Oriental Land park and the Fangta and Qinglong pagodas are also in the district.

Transport and development

Qingpu is served by Line 17 of the Shanghai Metro, opened in 2017, and lies along the G50 Shanghai–Chongqing Expressway. The Hongqiao transport and business hub lies just east of the district.
