Regina Pats

City: Regina, Saskatchewan
League: Western Hockey League
Founded: 1917
Arena: Brandt Centre
Memorial Cups: 1925, 1928, 1930, 1974

The Regina Pats are a Canadian major junior ice hockey team based in Regina, Saskatchewan, playing in the Western Hockey League (WHL). Founded in 1917, they are the oldest continuously operating major junior hockey franchise in the world, and take their name from Princess Patricia's Canadian Light Infantry, a regiment raised in the First World War.

History

The Pats won the Memorial Cup as Canadian junior champions in 1925, 1928, 1930 and 1974. The club hosted the tournament for its centennial season in 2018. Notable alumni include many NHL players, and in 2023 the Pats featured Connor Bedard, the first overall pick in that year's NHL entry draft, whose record-breaking junior career drew nationwide attention to the team.

Arena and identity

The team plays at the Brandt Centre and wears red, white and blue. Its longevity and community role make it one of the signature institutions of Regina sporting life.
