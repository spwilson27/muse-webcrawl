Four Heavenly Kings (Cantopop)

The Four Heavenly Kings (四大天王) is the collective name for the four biggest male stars of Cantopop in Hong Kong during the 1990s: Jacky Cheung, Andy Lau, Aaron Kwok, and Leon Lai. The title, borrowed from the Buddhist guardian deities, was coined by the Hong Kong media around 1992 and the four dominated the popular music awards and charts of the decade across the Chinese-speaking world.

Members

  • Jacky Cheung (張學友) — famed as the "God of Songs," among the best-selling music artists worldwide.
  • Andy Lau (劉德華) — a prolific singer and one of Hong Kong's most successful film actors.
  • Aaron Kwok (郭富城) — known for dance-pop hits and later acclaimed acting roles.
  • Leon Lai (黎明) — a major idol of the early 1990s, later prominent in film and philanthropy.

Their dominance was so complete that Hong Kong music awards of the era are often described as a contest among the four. Their influence extended into Taiwan, mainland China, and overseas Chinese communities, defining the golden age of Cantopop before its relative decline in the 2000s.
