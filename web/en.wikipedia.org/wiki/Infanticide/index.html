Infanticide

Definition: The intentional killing of an infant

Related terms: Neonaticide (killing within 24 hours of birth), filicide

Infanticide is the intentional killing of an infant, generally understood as a child under one year of age. It has been practised in many cultures historically, often linked to poverty, illegitimacy stigma, sex selection, or infant disability, and is now criminalized virtually everywhere.

History

In ancient Greece and Rome, unwanted infants could be exposed; child sacrifice involving infants is attested in some ancient cultures. Many societies later developed alternatives such as baby hatches and foundling hospitals.

Law

Some jurisdictions, such as England and Wales under the Infanticide Act 1938, treat infanticide by a mother whose mental balance was disturbed by childbirth as a distinct offence, an alternative to murder. In biology, infanticide also describes killing of young by adults of the same species, observed in lions, primates, and other animals.
