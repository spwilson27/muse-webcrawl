The Prison Reform Trust (PRT) is a United Kingdom charity that campaigns for a just, humane and effective penal system. Founded in 1981, it works to reduce imprisonment, improve conditions for prisoners and their families, and promote evidence-based criminal justice policy in England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

Work

The Trust publishes research and briefings, including its long-running Bromley Briefings Prison Factfile, a compendium of statistics on the prison system. It runs advice and information services for prisoners and families, supports prisoner engagement through programmes such as the Prisoner Policy Network, and campaigns on issues including overcrowding, the Imprisonment for Public Protection (IPP) sentence, women's imprisonment, and the treatment of people with learning disabilities in the criminal justice system.

Position

PRT argues that prison should be reserved for serious and violent offenders, that short sentences are ineffective, and that community alternatives should be expanded. It works alongside other penal reform organisations such as the Howard League for Penal Reform, while maintaining a distinct focus on practical policy research and prisoner consultation.
