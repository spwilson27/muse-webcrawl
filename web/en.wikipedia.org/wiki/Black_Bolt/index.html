Black Bolt (Blackagar Boltagon) is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby, the character first appeared in Fantastic Four #45 (December 1965). He is the king of the Inhumans, a race of superhumans hidden in the city of Attilan, and the husband of Medusa.

Powers and characterisation

Black Bolt's Terrigen-mutated body channels ambient electrons into a quasi-sonic voice of immense destructive power: even a whisper can level mountains, and a shout has been depicted as capable of devastating entire armies and cities. Consequently he lives in near-total silence, communicating through sign language, expression and intermediaries, principally his wife Medusa, who interprets for him. A tuning-fork-shaped antenna on his forehead helps him channel and control his power. He also possesses superhuman strength, durability and flight.

Storylines and media

As ruler of Attilan — successively located in the Himalayas, the Moon's Blue Area and elsewhere — Black Bolt has confronted his mad brother Maximus, led the Inhumans through events such as War of Kings and Infinity, and served as a member of the secretive Illuminati alongside Iron Man, Doctor Strange and Mister Fantastic. Anson Mount portrayed the character in the television series Inhumans (2017) and as an alternate-universe variant in the film Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness (2022).
