War Machine

War Machine (James Rupert "Rhodey" Rhodes) is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by David Michelinie, Bob Layton, and John Byrne Jr., the character first appeared in Iron Man #118 (1979) as a supporting character, a U.S. Marine and friend of Tony Stark, and later assumed the War Machine identity in Iron Man #282 (1992).

Fictional biography

Rhodes, a helicopter pilot from Philadelphia, met Tony Stark during the Vietnam War and later became Stark's personal pilot and chief engineer at Stark International. When Stark's alcoholism incapacitated him, Rhodes took over as Iron Man for a time. He later received his own suit—the Variable Threat Response Battle Suit, a heavily armed grey variant of the Iron Man armour bristling with guns and missiles—and became known as War Machine, fighting alongside the Avengers and leading the Fifty State Initiative team in later storylines.

In other media

James Rhodes is played in the Marvel Cinematic Universe by Terrence Howard in Iron Man (2008) and by Don Cheadle from Iron Man 2 (2010) onward, appearing in numerous Avengers films. In the MCU the character later uses the "Iron Patriot" armour and was announced to headline the film Armor Wars.
