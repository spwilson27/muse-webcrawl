Dilbert

Dilbert is an American comic strip written and illustrated by Scott Adams, first published on April 16, 1989. It is known for its satirical office humour about a white-collar, micromanaged office featuring engineer Dilbert as the title character.

Characters and setting

The strip is set in the offices of a technology company and follows Dilbert, a competent engineer surrounded by incompetent management, epitomised by the Pointy-Haired Boss. Regular characters include Wally, a perennial slacker; Alice, a hard-working and short-tempered engineer; Asok, a naive intern; and Dilbert's pet Dogbert, a megalomaniacal talking dog. The humour targets corporate bureaucracy, meaningless jargon, downsizing and flawed products.

Publication and media

Dilbert grew from Adams's doodles while working at Pacific Bell and was at its peak syndicated in thousands of newspapers worldwide. It spawned bestselling books such as The Dilbert Principle (1996), an animated television series (1999–2000), merchandise and a widely read website. In 2023, after controversial remarks by Adams, many newspapers dropped the strip, which continued as the webcomic Dilbert Reborn.
