Panoz is an American manufacturer of sports cars and racing cars founded in 1989 by Dan Panoz, son of pharmaceutical magnate Don Panoz. Based in Braselton, Georgia, the company became known for front-engined sports cars such as the Roadster, Esperante, and the striking Panoz LMP and GTR-1 racing machines that competed at the 24 Hours of Le Mans and in the American Le Mans Series.

History

Dan Panoz founded the Panoz Auto Development Company in 1989 after acquiring the rights to a chassis design from the Irish firm Thompson Motor Company. The company's first car, the Panoz Roadster, entered production in 1992; an aluminium-intensive evolution, the AIV Roadster, followed in 1997. The Esperante, a more refined grand tourer, debuted in 2000.

Panoz expanded into motorsport management as well: Don Panoz founded the American Le Mans Series in 1999, and the family acquired circuits including Road Atlanta and Sebring International Raceway interests. Panoz racing cars achieved class success internationally, and the Esperante GT-LM won its class at Le Mans in 2006. In 2014 the company introduced the Esperante Spyder, celebrating 25 years of production, and in 2017 unveiled the Avezzano.
