Villa Park

Villa Park is a football stadium in Aston, Birmingham, England, with a seating capacity of about 42,600. It has been the home ground of Aston Villa Football Club since 1897. The stadium is one of the most historic in English football and has hosted more FA Cup semi-finals than any other ground.

History

Aston Villa moved to the Aston Lower Grounds — later renamed Villa Park — in 1897, renting the site, which had previously been a Victorian amusement park with a cycling track. The ground was progressively rebuilt in the twentieth century; the famous Holte End terrace, once among the largest behind-goal stands in Britain, was converted to seating in the 1990s. The stadium has four main stands: the Holte End, the Trinity Road Stand, the North Stand, and the Doug Ellis Stand.

Major events

Villa Park hosted matches at the 1966 FIFA World Cup and UEFA Euro 1996, as well as the last-ever European Cup Winners' Cup final in 1999 between Lazio and Mallorca. It has staged England international matches, rugby league and rugby union fixtures — including games at the 2015 Rugby World Cup — and open-air concerts. The ground is also a traditional venue for FA Cup semi-finals, a role it held for many decades before semi-finals moved to Wembley Stadium.
