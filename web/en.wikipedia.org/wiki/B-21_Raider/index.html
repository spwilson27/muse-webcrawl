Northrop Grumman B-21 Raider

The Northrop Grumman B-21 Raider is an American strategic bomber under development for the United States Air Force (USAF) by Northrop Grumman. A long-range, stealth, heavy bomber, it is designed to be capable of delivering both conventional and thermonuclear weapons. The B-21 is expected to replace the B-1 Lancer and B-2 Spirit in USAF service.

Development

The B-21 was developed under the Long Range Strike Bomber (LRS-B) program. Northrop Grumman won the development contract in October 2015. The aircraft was publicly unveiled on 2 December 2022 at Plant 42 in Palmdale, California. Its name honours the Doolittle Raiders of 1942; the "21" denotes the first bomber of the 21st century. The first aircraft, named Cerberus, made its maiden flight on 10 November 2023 from Palmdale to Edwards Air Force Base.

Design

The B-21 is a flying wing, smaller than the B-2, with deeply buried engines and simplified trailing-edge geometry optimised for very low observability across a broad range of radar frequencies. It has an open-systems architecture to permit rapid upgrades, and is designed to be optionally crewed, though it is expected to operate with a crew in normal service. Details of its range, payload, and speed remain classified.
