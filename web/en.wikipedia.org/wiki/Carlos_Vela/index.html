Carlos Alberto Vela Garrido (born 1 March 1989) is a Mexican professional footballer who plays as a forward. Known for his elegant left foot, vision, and finishing, Vela starred for Real Sociedad in Spain's La Liga and became the first and emblematic captain of Los Angeles FC in Major League Soccer.

Club career

Vela emerged from the youth system of Guadalajara (Chivas) and starred at the 2005 FIFA U-17 World Championship in Peru, where Mexico won the title and he finished as top scorer, winning the Golden Boot. Arsenal signed him in 2005, but work-permit restrictions led to loan spells in Spain with Salamanca and Osasuna, and later with West Bromwich Albion in England. A loan to Real Sociedad in 2011 became permanent the following year; in San Sebastián he developed into one of La Liga's finest attackers, helping the club qualify for the UEFA Champions League in 2013.

In 2017 Vela was announced as the first designated player signing of expansion club Los Angeles FC. As captain, he led LAFC to the Supporters' Shield in 2019, setting an MLS single-season record with 34 goals and winning the league's Most Valuable Player award, and he captained the club to its first MLS Cup title in 2022.

International career

Vela debuted for the Mexico national team in 2007 and played at the 2010 and 2018 FIFA World Cups. His relationship with the national setup was periodically strained: he declined call-ups for several years, including for the 2014 World Cup, before returning in late 2014. He won the CONCACAF Gold Cup with Mexico in 2009 and 2015.
