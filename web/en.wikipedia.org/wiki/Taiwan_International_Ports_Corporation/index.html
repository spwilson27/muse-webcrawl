Taiwan International Ports Corporation

Taiwan International Ports Corporation (TIPC; 臺灣港務股份有限公司) is a state-owned enterprise of Taiwan responsible for the operation and management of the island's international commercial ports. It was established on 1 March 2012 under a reform that separated port administration ("harbour bureau" functions, which passed to the Maritime and Port Bureau) from commercial port operations.

Ports

TIPC operates the international ports of Keelung, Taichung, Kaohsiung, Hualien, Taipei, Suao, and Anping, through branch offices, together with subsidiaries engaged in logistics, warehousing, and free-trade zone operations. The Port of Kaohsiung, its largest operation, is Taiwan's principal container port. The corporation is wholly owned by the Ministry of Transportation and Communications.
