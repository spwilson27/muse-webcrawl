Enfield F.C.

Enfield Football Club is a football club traditionally based in Enfield, Greater London, England. Founded in 1893 as Enfield Spartans, the club became one of the most successful amateur sides in England during the 1960s, 1970s and 1980s, winning the FA Amateur Cup twice (1967 and 1970) and the Isthmian League numerous times, and later won the FA Trophy in 1988 and the Football Conference (now National League) championship twice in the 1980s.

Enfield playing at their former home, Southbury Road.

History

The club played at Southbury Road for most of the twentieth century. The ground was sold in 1999, beginning years of ground-sharing and financial difficulty. In 2001 supporters formed a breakaway club, Enfield Town, one of the first fan-owned clubs in England. The original club was wound up in 2007 over unpaid debts, and a successor club, Enfield 1893, was formed, later reverting to the Enfield FC name.

Honours

Major honours include the Football Conference championship (1982–83 and 1985–86), the FA Trophy (1987–88) and the FA Amateur Cup (1966–67 and 1969–70).
