Crazy


Single by Gnarls Barkley (2006)

"Crazy" is a song by the American duo Gnarls Barkley — producer Danger Mouse and singer CeeLo Green — released in 2006 as the lead single from their debut album St. Elsewhere. Built around a sample from "Nel cimitero di Tucson" by Gian Franco Reverberi and Gian Piero Reverberi, from the 1968 spaghetti western Django, Prepare a Coffin, the song combines soul, funk, and electronic production beneath Green's vocal musing on sanity and fame.

Chart success

Leaked online before release, "Crazy" became the first song to top the UK Singles Chart purely on download sales, spending nine consecutive weeks at number one in 2006 — the longest run of the decade — before being deliberately withdrawn from sale by the band. It reached number two on the Billboard Hot 100 in the United States and topped charts in many countries.

Reception

The song won the Grammy Award for Best Urban/Alternative Performance in 2007, and was named the best song of 2006 by Rolling Stone, which later ranked it among the greatest songs of all time. It has been covered by numerous artists and widely used in film and television.
