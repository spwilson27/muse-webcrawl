Museum of Aviation (Warner Robins)

LocationWarner Robins, Georgia, United States
TypeAviation museum
Established1984

The Museum of Aviation is the second-largest aerospace museum of the United States Air Force. It is located in Warner Robins, Georgia, adjacent to Robins Air Force Base. The museum opened in 1984 and is operated with support from the Museum of Aviation Foundation.

Collection

The museum's collection includes more than 85 historic aircraft displayed across four exhibit buildings and outdoor grounds covering roughly 51 acres. Aircraft on display include the SR-71 Blackbird that set a world speed record, a U-2 spy plane, F-15 Eagle and F-16 Fighting Falcon fighters, B-29 and B-52 bombers, C-130 Hercules transports, and numerous helicopters and missiles.

Exhibits and programs

In addition to its aircraft, the museum hosts exhibits on the history of Robins Air Force Base, the United States Air Force, and Georgia aviation heritage. Its education programs include the National STEM Academy, which offers classes and camps in science, technology, engineering and mathematics for students and teachers.
