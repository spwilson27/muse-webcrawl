Embrapa
Full name: Empresa Brasileira de Pesquisa Agropecuária
Founded: 1973
Headquarters: Brasília, Brazil
Type: state-owned agricultural research corporation

Embrapa, the Brazilian Agricultural Research Corporation (Empresa Brasileira de Pesquisa Agropecuária), is a state-owned research company affiliated with Brazil's Ministry of Agriculture. Founded in 1973, it is widely credited with transforming Brazil into one of the world's largest agricultural producers and exporters.

Work

Embrapa coordinates a national network of dozens of specialised research centres devoted to crops, livestock, regions, and themes such as soils, genetic resources, and agroforestry. Its scientists adapted soybeans to tropical latitudes, developed methods to farm the acidic soils of the Cerrado savanna with lime and phosphorus, bred cattle and pastures suited to the tropics, and advanced no-till farming and crop–livestock–forest integration systems.

Impact

Research from Embrapa helped turn the once-marginal Cerrado into a major grain-producing region and underpinned Brazil's rise as a leading exporter of soy, beef, poultry, coffee, and sugar. The corporation also works on family farming, biological pest control, and drought-resistant crops for the semi-arid Northeast, and runs international cooperation programmes, especially with Africa.
