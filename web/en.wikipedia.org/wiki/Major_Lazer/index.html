Major Lazer

Major Lazer is an American electronic dance music and moombahton group, founded in 2008 by producer Diplo and DJ Switch, with Switch leaving in 2011. The project has been led by Diplo alongside Walshy Fire and, since 2019, Ape Drums (who replaced Jillionaire). The group is named after its fictional namesake character, a one-armed Jamaican zombie war commando, and its music fuses reggae and dancehall with electronic styles including moombahton, house and trap, presented with a comic-book, cartoon aesthetic.

History

The debut album Guns Don't Kill People... Lazers Do (2009), recorded in Jamaica, leaned heavily on dancehall. The second album Free the Universe (2013) broadened the sound with guest vocalists, and the third, Peace Is the Mission (2015), produced the group's biggest hit, "Lean On" — a collaboration with DJ Snake and Danish singer MØ — which became one of the most-streamed songs of all time. The follow-up single "Cold Water" (2016), featuring Justin Bieber and MØ, debuted at number two on the Billboard Hot 100. The album Music Is the Weapon followed in 2020.

Other media

An animated television series, Major Lazer, premiered on FXX in 2015, with the character voiced by Adewale Akinnuoye-Agbaje. The group is also known for its large-scale live shows and for Diplo's related cartoon mythology surrounding the Major Lazer character.
