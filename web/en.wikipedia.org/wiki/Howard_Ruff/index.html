Howard Ruff

Born1930
Died2016
OccupationFinancial adviser, author, newsletter publisher

Howard J. Ruff (1930–2016) was an American financial adviser and writer, best known for his 1978 bestseller How to Prosper During the Coming Bad Years and for the long-running investment newsletter The Ruff Times.

Views

Ruff warned through the 1970s of inflation, currency debasement and economic crisis, advocating investment in gold, silver and other hard assets and personal preparedness such as food storage. His book was one of the best-selling financial titles of its era, and he became a prominent figure in the survivalist and hard-money movements.

Career

Before finance, Ruff worked as a singer and corporate executive. He founded Ruff Research and ran for the U.S. Senate in Utah. Though some of his predicted catastrophes did not materialise on schedule, his newsletter continued for decades with a large subscriber base.
