River Ribble

The River Ribble is a river in Northern England, rising near the Ribblehead Viaduct in North Yorkshire and flowing south-west for about 121 km (75 mi) through Lancashire — including Settle, Clitheroe, and Preston — to the Irish Sea near Lytham.

Course and history

The Ribble flows through Ribblesdale and the Forest of Bowland, passing beneath Pendle Hill, and becomes tidal below Preston, where its docks were once among England's largest. Its broad estuary, with extensive salt marshes, is an internationally important site for wintering wildfowl and wading birds. In 1840 a hoard of Viking silver, the Cuerdale Hoard, was found on its bank near Preston.
