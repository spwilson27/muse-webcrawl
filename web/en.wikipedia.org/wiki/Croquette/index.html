Croquette

A croquette is a deep-fried food consisting of a thick binder combined with a filling, breaded and fried. The name comes from the French croquer, "to crunch". Croquettes appear in many cuisines, typically as a side dish, snack, or fast food.

Varieties

Common forms include Spanish croquetas made with béchamel and jamón; Japanese korokke of mashed potato and minced meat; Dutch kroketten with ragout filling, famously sold from vending walls; and potato croquettes served across Europe and the Americas. Fillings range from ham, chicken, and salt cod to cheese and vegetables.

Preparation

The cooled filling is shaped into cylinders or ovals, coated in flour, egg, and breadcrumbs, and deep-fried until crisp and golden.
