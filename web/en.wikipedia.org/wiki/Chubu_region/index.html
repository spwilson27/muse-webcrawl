Chubu region

The Chubu region (中部地方, Chūbu-chihō) is the central region of Honshu, Japan's main island, situated between Kanto and Kansai. It comprises nine prefectures: Niigata, Toyama, Ishikawa, Fukui, Yamanashi, Nagano, Gifu, Shizuoka, and Aichi, with Nagoya as its largest city.

Chubu region
Island: Honshu
Prefectures: 9
Largest city: Nagoya
Subregions: Hokuriku, Koshi, Tokai

Subregions and geography

Chubu divides into Hokuriku (on the Sea of Japan, heavy snow), the central highlands (Koshin'etsu/Tosan, including the Japanese Alps and Mount Fuji on its edge), and Tokai (on the Pacific, industrial heartland around Nagoya, home to Toyota). The region is a major manufacturing and agricultural area.
