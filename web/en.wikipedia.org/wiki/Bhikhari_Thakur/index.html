Bhikhari Thakur

Born18 December 1887, Kutubpur, Saran district, Bengal Presidency (now Bihar)
Died10 July 1971 (aged 83)
OccupationPlaywright, folk theatre artist, poet, social reformer
Known forFounder of modern Bhojpuri theatre; the play Bidesiya

Bhikhari Thakur (18 December 1887 – 10 July 1971) was an Indian playwright, lyricist, actor and folk theatre artist in the Bhojpuri language. Often called the "Shakespeare of Bhojpuri literature", he is regarded as the founder of modern Bhojpuri theatre and used his plays to attack social evils such as child marriage, dowry, caste discrimination and the plight of migrant labourers.

Life and work

Born into a barber (Nai) family in the village of Kutubpur in present-day Bihar, Thakur had little formal schooling and worked as a barber and migrant labourer in Kolkata before forming his own theatre troupe. His travelling company performed across Bihar, Bengal and eastern Uttar Pradesh for decades.

Major works

His most famous play, Bidesiya ("The Foreigner"), portrays a migrant worker who leaves his wife for Kolkata and the suffering that follows; it gave its name to an entire genre of Bhojpuri folk drama. Other works include Beti Bechwa (against the sale of daughters in marriage), Ganga Snan and Bhai Birodh. His songs and plays remain central to Bhojpuri cultural identity.
