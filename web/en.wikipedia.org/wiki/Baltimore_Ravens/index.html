Baltimore Ravens

The Ravens at M&T Bank Stadium

The Baltimore Ravens are a professional American football team based in Baltimore, Maryland. They compete in the National Football League (NFL) as a member of the American Football Conference (AFC) North division, and play their home games at M&T Bank Stadium. The team's name references Edgar Allan Poe's poem "The Raven"; Poe lived and is buried in Baltimore.

History

The franchise was established in 1996 when Cleveland Browns owner Art Modell relocated his team to Baltimore; under the settlement with the city of Cleveland, the Browns' name, colours and history remained in Cleveland, and the Ravens were technically an expansion team. General manager Ozzie Newsome built a formidable roster, and in the 2000 season, behind a record-setting defense led by linebacker Ray Lewis, the Ravens won Super Bowl XXXV, defeating the New York Giants. Under head coach John Harbaugh (hired 2008) and quarterback Joe Flacco, Baltimore won Super Bowl XLVII over the San Francisco 49ers in February 2013. In 2018 the team drafted quarterback Lamar Jackson, who won the NFL Most Valuable Player award in 2019 and 2023, leading one of the league's most dynamic rushing offences. The Ravens are known for consistently strong defense, special teams, and one of the NFL's most stable organisations, owned by Steve Bisciotti since 2004.
