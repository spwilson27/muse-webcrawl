Kingdom of Tungning

The Kingdom of Tungning (1661-1683) was a Han Chinese regime founded by the Ming loyalist Koxinga (Zheng Chenggong) in southwestern Taiwan after he expelled the Dutch East India Company. Based at Tainan, it claimed to continue the Ming dynasty against the Manchu Qing.

Koxinga captured Fort Zeelandia in 1662 after a nine-month siege. He died the same year and was succeeded by his son Zheng Jing, who ruled for almost two decades, developing trade, agriculture and administration, and intervened in mainland China during the Revolt of the Three Feudatories. After Zheng Jing's death a succession struggle weakened the kingdom, and Qing admiral Shi Lang defeated its fleet at Penghu in 1683. Zheng Keshuang surrendered, and Taiwan was incorporated into the Qing Empire as part of Fujian province.
