Mount Khustup

Mount Khustup (Armenian: Խուստուփ) is a mountain in Syunik Province of southern Armenia, in the Bargushat range of the Zangezur Mountains. Its summit reaches about 3,206 metres (10,518 ft) above sea level, making it one of the most prominent peaks in the region.

Geography and significance

Khustup rises near the town of Kapan and is visible from much of the surrounding area. The Voghji River originates on its northern slopes. The mountain is a popular destination for hiking and mountaineering in Armenia.

Khustup is closely associated with the Armenian military commander Garegin Nzhdeh, whose partisan forces operated in the region in the early 1920s. By his wish, his remains were buried on the slope of the mountain, and the site remains a place of pilgrimage for many Armenians.
