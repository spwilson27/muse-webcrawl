Shein

Shein (styled SHEIN; pronounced "she-in") is a Chinese-Singaporean online fast-fashion retailer founded in Nanjing, China, in 2008 by Chris Xu (Xu Yangtian) and headquartered in Singapore since 2021. Selling inexpensive clothing directly to consumers in more than 150 countries through its website and app, Shein became the world's largest fashion retailer by market share in the early 2020s, surpassing established fast-fashion brands. It confidentially filed for a United States IPO in 2023 and later pursued a London listing.

Business model

Shein pioneered an ultra-fast, data-driven "on-demand" model: it tests thousands of new styles in tiny initial batches (often 100–200 pieces) produced by a network of thousands of small contract factories concentrated in Guangzhou's Panyu district ("Shein village"), then rapidly reorders items that sell. A gamified app, influencer marketing, and low prices target young consumers, especially Generation Z. The company expanded beyond its own label with a marketplace for third-party sellers and acquired brands including the UK retailer Missguided (2023), taking a stake in the Forever 21 operator SPARC Group the same year.

Criticism and controversy

Shein has faced sustained criticism over labour conditions in its supply chain (a Swiss NGO reported very long working hours, paid per piece), environmental impact and textile waste from ultra-fast fashion, product safety findings (lead and other chemicals detected in some garments), alleged intellectual-property infringement by independent designers (including a 2023 RICO lawsuit filed by designers in the US), tax treatment under the US de minimis exemption for low-value imports, and allegations — which the company denies — regarding cotton from Xinjiang. Lawmakers in the US, EU, and France have proposed measures on customs exemptions and fast-fashion advertising.

History

The company began as ZZKKO selling wedding dresses online, rebranded as Sheinside, and shortened its name to Shein in 2015 as it shifted to general womenswear and vertically integrated design and supply. Its valuation peaked at about US$100 billion in an April 2022 funding round before being marked down in later rounds.
