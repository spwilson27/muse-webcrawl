Max Verstappen

Max Emilian Verstappen
Born: September 30, 1997, Hasselt, Belgium
Nationality: Dutch
Team: Red Bull Racing
Titles: Formula One World Champion 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

Max Emilian Verstappen (born 30 September 1997) is a Dutch racing driver who competes in Formula One for Red Bull Racing. He won the Formula One World Drivers' Championship in 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2024, and is regarded among the most talented drivers of his generation.

Early career

The son of former Formula One driver Jos Verstappen and karting champion Sophie Kumpen, Verstappen excelled in karting and single-seaters before debuting with Toro Rosso in 2015 at age 17 — the youngest driver in Formula One history, which prompted the FIA to set a minimum age of 18.

Red Bull Racing

Promoted to Red Bull Racing mid-season in 2016, he won on his debut at the Spanish Grand Prix, becoming the youngest Grand Prix winner ever. After years as a frontrunner, he beat Lewis Hamilton to the 2021 title in a controversial season finale at Abu Dhabi, then dominated 2022 and especially 2023, when he won 19 of 22 races and set a record of 10 consecutive victories. He secured a fourth consecutive title in 2024.

Driving style

Verstappen is known for aggressive racecraft, exceptional wet-weather skill, and defensive driving that has at times drawn scrutiny from stewards and rivals.
