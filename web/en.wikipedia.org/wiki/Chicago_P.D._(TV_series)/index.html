Chicago P.D. is an American police procedural drama television series created by Dick Wolf and Matt Olmstead as a spin-off of Chicago Fire and the second series of the Chicago franchise. It premiered on NBC on 8 January 2014. The show follows the uniformed patrol officers and the Intelligence Unit of the 21st District of the Chicago Police Department as they pursue perpetrators of the city's major street crimes.

Premise

The series centres on the Intelligence Unit, led by Sergeant Hank Voight (Jason Beghe), a morally grey commander willing to bend the rules to close cases, and his detectives — among them Jay Halstead (Jesse Lee Soffer), Hailey Upton (Tracy Spiridakos), Kevin Atwater (LaRoyce Hawkins) and Kim Burgess (Marina Squerciati). Uniformed patrol storylines interweave with intelligence operations, and the unit's cases frequently connect to events unfolding in Chicago Fire and Chicago Med.

Reception and crossovers

Chicago P.D. has been renewed through numerous seasons and regularly ranks among NBC's strongest Wednesday-night performers, anchoring the network's One Chicago block alongside the other franchise series. Annual multi-show crossover events feature the unit working major investigations in tandem with Firehouse 51 and the staff of Gaffney Chicago Medical Center.
