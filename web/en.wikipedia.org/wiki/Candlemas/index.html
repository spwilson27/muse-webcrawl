Candlemas

Candlemas, also known as the Feast of the Presentation of Jesus Christ or the Feast of the Purification of the Blessed Virgin Mary, is a Christian feast day celebrated on 2 February, commemorating the presentation of Jesus at the Temple.

Date: 2 February
Observances: Blessing of candles, church services

Traditions

Candles are blessed and carried in procession, symbolising Christ as the light of the world. In some cultures Candlemas marks the end of the Christmas season and is associated with weather lore, related to Groundhog Day.
