Country: China

The People's Liberation Army (PLA; Chinese: 中国人民解放军) is the military of the People's Republic of China and the principal armed wing of the Chinese Communist Party (CCP). It is the world's largest military force by active personnel. The PLA has five service branches: the Ground Force, the Navy, the Air Force, the Rocket Force and the Strategic Support Force.

History

Founded on 1 August 1927 during the Nanchang uprising as the Red Army, the PLA fought the Chinese Civil War against the Kuomintang, surviving the Long March of 1934–35, and resisted Japan during the Second Sino-Japanese War. Renamed the People's Liberation Army in 1946, it defeated the Nationalists and completed the founding of the PRC in 1949. It later fought in the Korean War (as the People's Volunteer Army), the Sino-Indian War of 1962 and the Sino-Vietnamese War of 1979.

Modernisation

Since the 1990s the PLA has undergone rapid modernisation, shifting from a mass infantry army to a technology-intensive force with aircraft carriers, stealth aircraft, anti-ship ballistic missiles and expanding cyber and space capabilities. Reforms under Xi Jinping reorganised the force into theatre commands oriented towards joint operations. Under China's system, the party commands the gun: the PLA answers to the Central Military Commission of the CCP rather than to the state alone.
