DistroWatch

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia


The DistroWatch homepage

DistroWatch is a website that provides news, popularity rankings, and other general information about various Linux distributions as well as other free software and open source Unix-like operating systems, such as OpenSolaris, MINIX, and BSD distributions. It was founded on 31 May 2001 and is maintained by Ladislav Bodnar.

Features

The site maintains extensive information pages for each distribution, including package versions, download links, reviews, and screenshots. Its best-known feature is the Page Hit Ranking, which tracks the number of visits to each distribution's page on the site and is often cited as a rough indicator of public interest in distributions, although it is not a measure of actual usage or installations.

DistroWatch also publishes a weekly newsletter, DistroWatch Weekly, covering distribution releases and related open-source news.
