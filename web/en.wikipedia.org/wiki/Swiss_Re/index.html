Swiss Re Ltd is a Swiss reinsurance company based in Zurich, and one of the world's largest reinsurers. It provides insurance-based capital solutions and risk management to insurance companies and other clients through its Property & Casualty Reinsurance, Life & Health Reinsurance, and Corporate Solutions business units.

History

Swiss Re was founded on 19 December 1863 by the Helvetia insurance company, Schweizerische Kreditanstalt, and the Basler Handelsbank, following the great fire of Glarus in 1861, which had exposed the inadequacy of local insurance reserves. The company expanded internationally over the following decades, opening offices in New York and London.

Operations

Swiss Re's sigma research unit publishes widely cited studies of world insurance markets. The group operates in more than 80 offices worldwide. Its London offices occupy 30 St Mary Axe, known as "The Gherkin," a landmark building it commissioned and later sold. The company suffered heavy losses during the 2008 financial crisis from credit default swap exposures and required a capital injection from Berkshire Hathaway, subsequently repaid.
