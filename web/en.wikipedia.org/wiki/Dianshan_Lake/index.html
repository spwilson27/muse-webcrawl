Dianshan Lake

Dianshan Lake (Chinese: 淀山湖; pinyin: Diànshān Hú) is a freshwater lake in the Qingpu District of Shanghai and partly in Kunshan, Jiangsu, in eastern China. Covering roughly 62 square kilometres, it is the largest lake within the Shanghai municipality and the source of the Huangpu River.

Geography

The shallow lake lies west of central Shanghai and is connected through a network of waterways to Lake Tai. Its shores include the ancient water town of Zhujiajiao, a popular tourist destination known for canals and Ming- and Qing-dynasty bridges.

Use

Dianshan Lake is an important source of drinking water for Shanghai and a site for aquaculture, recreation, rowing and sailing. Surrounding wetlands have been developed as parks and resorts, including the Shanghai Grand View Garden area nearby.
