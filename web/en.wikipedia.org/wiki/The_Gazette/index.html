The Gazette, often called the Montreal Gazette, is a daily English-language newspaper published in Montreal, Quebec, Canada. Founded on 3 June 1778 by printer Fleury Mesplet, it is one of the oldest newspapers in North America still in publication.

History

The paper began as a French-language journal and over its long history appeared in French, English, and bilingual editions before becoming English-only. It served Montreal's anglophone community through the nineteenth and twentieth centuries as the city's dominant English daily, competing with papers such as the Montreal Star (closed 1979) and the Montreal Daily News. In 2014 the paper formally dropped "Montreal" from its masthead to become simply The Gazette. It is owned by Postmedia Network and is the only English-language daily broadsheet remaining in Montreal.
