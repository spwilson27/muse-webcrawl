Rocket Internet

Rocket Internet SE is a German internet company headquartered in Berlin. Founded in 2007 by the Samwer brothers — Marc, Oliver and Alexander — it builds and invests in online businesses, frequently by replicating proven e-commerce models in emerging markets, a "startup studio" or clone-factory approach that made it one of Europe's most prominent internet firms.

History

Rocket-backed companies included the fashion retailers Zalando and Zalora, food delivery services such as Foodpanda and HelloFresh, and marketplaces including Lazada and Jumia. The company listed on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange in October 2014 in one of Germany's largest technology IPOs of the period. Its portfolio companies went on to their own listings, notably Zalando, HelloFresh, Delivery Hero, Jumia and Global Fashion Group. In 2020 Rocket Internet announced a delisting, buying back its shares and returning to private ownership as a Samwer family investment vehicle.

Criticism

The company's practice of copying successful American business models for other markets, and its aggressive management culture, attracted sustained criticism, though its defenders argue it brought e-commerce infrastructure and employment to underserved markets.
