1Malaysia Development Berhad scandal

TypeCorruption and money-laundering scandal
Amount~US$4.5 billion allegedly misappropriated
Key figuresNajib Razak, Jho Low

The 1Malaysia Development Berhad scandal, often called the 1MDB scandal, is a global corruption and money-laundering conspiracy in which about US$4.5 billion was allegedly misappropriated from 1Malaysia Development Berhad, a Malaysian state-owned investment fund founded by Prime Minister Najib Razak.

Allegations

Investigators alleged that fugitive financier Jho Low orchestrated the diversion of fund money through shell companies and offshore accounts to buy luxury real estate, a superyacht, artwork, and to finance the film The Wolf of Wall Street. Hundreds of millions of dollars were traced to accounts linked to Najib.

Investigations and fallout

The scandal was investigated in Malaysia, the United States, Switzerland, Singapore, and elsewhere. Goldman Sachs, which raised billions for the fund, pleaded guilty and paid billions in penalties. Najib lost the 2018 election, was tried in Malaysia, and in 2020 was convicted of abuse of power, money laundering, and criminal breach of trust in the SRC International case, receiving a 12-year sentence (later partially pardoned). Jho Low remains a fugitive.
