Slender Man

The Slender Man (also spelled Slenderman) is a fictional supernatural character that originated as a creepypasta Internet meme created by Something Awful forum user Eric Knudsen (also known as "Victor Surge") in 2009. He is depicted as a thin, unnaturally tall humanoid with a featureless white head and face, wearing a black suit, sometimes with tentacle-like appendages sprouting from his back. Stories depict him stalking, abducting, or traumatizing people, often children.

The character spread virally through collaborative online fiction, fan art, and video, becoming one of the best-known examples of digital folklore. The web series Marble Hornets (2009–2014) popularized him in found-footage form, and the 2012 indie game Slender: The Eight Pages became a viral hit. A studio film, Slender Man, was released in 2018 to negative reviews.

2014 stabbing

In 2014, two 12-year-old girls in Waukesha, Wisconsin, lured a classmate into the woods and stabbed her 19 times, later telling police they did it to appease the Slender Man. The victim survived. The case triggered a moral panic about online horror fiction; the perpetrators were tried as adults and committed to mental institutions. The incident is examined in the 2016 documentary Beware the Slenderman.

Analysis

Folklorists study Slender Man as a case of "ostension" and emergent digital legend-making: a character with no single canonical text, continuously re-authored by a community, and blurring the line between fiction and belief.
