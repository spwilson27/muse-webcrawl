Houston Independent School District

The Houston Independent School District (HISD) is the largest public school system in Texas and the eighth-largest in the United States. Headquartered in the Hattie Mae White Educational Support Center in Houston, it serves a large portion of the city of Houston and several surrounding municipalities.

Overview

HISD educates roughly 190,000 students across more than 270 campuses, including elementary, middle, and high schools as well as charter and magnet programs. Well-known campuses include the High School for the Performing and Visual Arts (Kinder HSPVA), DeBakey High School for Health Professions, and Carnegie Vanguard High School.

History

The district traces its origins to Houston's public schools of the 19th century and was formally established in the 1920s. It desegregated gradually after Brown v. Board of Education. In 2023, the Texas Education Agency took over the district's administration, appointing a board of managers and a superintendent following years of low ratings at one high school and state investigations.

Governance

Before the state takeover, the district was governed by an elected nine-member board of trustees. The appointed board of managers assumed governance in June 2023 for a period expected to last several years.
