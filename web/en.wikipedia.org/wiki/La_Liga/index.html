La Liga (officially LaLiga EA Sports for sponsorship) is the top division of the Spanish football league system, contested by 20 teams since 1997. Founded in 1929, it is one of Europe's "big five" leagues and has been ranked the top league in Europe for much of the 21st century by UEFA coefficients, driven by the dominance of its clubs in the UEFA Champions League.

The league has been dominated by two clubs: Real Madrid (36 titles) and FC Barcelona (28), whose meeting — El Clásico — is one of the most watched club matches in world sport; Atlético Madrid (11 titles) is the only other regular challenger. The Messi–Cristiano Ronaldo era (2009–2018), when both played in Spain, set scoring records widely considered untouchable: Messi's 474 La Liga goals and 50-goal season (2011–12) headline them. Only nine clubs have ever won the title.
