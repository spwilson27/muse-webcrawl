Simmons (electronic drum company)

Simmons is an electronic drum brand founded in England by Dave Simmons. The company pioneered commercial electronic percussion in the late 1970s and became iconic in the 1980s with its SDS series drum synthesisers and distinctive hexagonal drum pads.

History

The SDS-5 (SDSV), released in 1981, was the first widely available electronic drum kit and was used by many pop and new wave acts of the era. Later products included the SDS7, SDS9 and the portable SDS1000. The original company declined in the late 1980s; the Simmons name was later revived for a line of electronic drum kits sold in the United States.
