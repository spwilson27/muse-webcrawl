Fox Animation Studios was an American animation production company owned by 20th Century Fox. It was founded in 1994 by former Disney and Sullivan Bluth animators Don Bluth and Gary Goldman, and was based in Phoenix, Arizona, where Fox built a new studio facility to house it.

Productions

The studio's first feature, Anastasia (1997), was a commercial and critical success, grossing over 39 million worldwide and demonstrating that traditional animation could still compete during Disney's Renaissance era. A direct-to-video spin-off, Bartok the Magnificent, followed in 1999.

Closure

Its second theatrical feature, the science-fiction film Titan A.E. (2000), was a major box-office failure. Fox closed the studio in June 2000, before the film's release had concluded, laying off roughly 300 employees. Fox's later theatrical animation was produced chiefly by Blue Sky Studios.
