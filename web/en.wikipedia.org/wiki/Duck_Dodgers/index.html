Duck Dodgers

Duck Dodgers is an American animated television series produced by Warner Bros. Animation, based on the 1953 theatrical short Duck Dodgers in the 24½th Century. It aired on Cartoon Network from 2003 to 2005, running for three seasons and 39 episodes. Daffy Duck stars as the egotistical space hero Duck Dodgers, with Porky Pig as his faithful Eager Young Space Cadet, defending Earth against Marvin the Martian and other cosmic threats.

Premise

Frozen in the 21st century and thawed in the 24th, Dodgers talks his way into a captaincy with the Galactic Protectorate through a falsified résumé. Despite cowardice and incompetence, he repeatedly saves the day, usually thanks to the Cadet. Recurring characters include the Martian Queen Tyr'ahnee, Commander X-2 (Marvin the Martian), and Dr. I.Q. Hi.

Reception

The series was praised for its witty writing and affectionate parodies of science fiction franchises such as Star Trek and Star Wars, and was nominated for Annie Awards, winning for music.
