Hush (Dr. Thomas "Tommy" Elliot) is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, commonly as an adversary of the superhero Batman. Created by writer Jeph Loeb and artist Jim Lee, the character first appeared in Batman #609 (January 2003) as part of the twelve-issue storyline "Batman: Hush".

A childhood friend of Bruce Wayne, Tommy Elliot was a brilliant surgeon who grew to resent Wayne. As Hush — his face wrapped in bandages — he orchestrated a year-long conspiracy against Batman, manipulating a gallery of Gotham City villains including the Riddler, Poison Ivy, Killer Croc, and Clayface.

Fictional character biography

Thomas Elliot was born into one of Gotham City's wealthiest families and befriended the young Bruce Wayne, whom he taught strategy through games. Unlike Bruce, Tommy despised his parents; he cut the brake lines of their car in an attempt to inherit the family fortune. His father was killed, but his mother survived thanks to surgery performed by Dr. Thomas Wayne, leaving Tommy to resent both his mother and the Waynes. His mother later died of cancer, and Elliot inherited the fortune.

Elliot became a renowned surgeon. Learning from Edward Nygma (the Riddler) that Bruce Wayne was Batman, Elliot faked his own death — shooting himself and disguising Clayface as his corpse — and returned as the bandaged figure Hush, manipulating Batman's enemies in an elaborate plot to destroy his former friend.

In later storylines, notably Batman: Heart of Hush (2008), Elliot returned seeking revenge, targeting Catwoman. He later used plastic surgery to make himself Bruce Wayne's double, scheming to steal the Wayne fortune before being imprisoned and eventually escaping.

In other media

Hush has appeared in animated films including Batman: Hush (2019), the video game Batman: Arkham City (2011) — where his identity-theft subplot is introduced — and Batman: Arkham Knight (2015). A young Tommy Elliot appears in the television series Gotham, and the character's storyline was adapted in The CW's Batwoman.
