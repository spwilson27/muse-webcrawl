Iron Man is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. The character was co-created by writer and editor Stan Lee, developed by scripter Larry Lieber, and designed by artists Don Heck and Jack Kirby, first appearing in Tales of Suspense #39 in 1963.

Fictional biography

Tony Stark is a billionaire industrialist and genius engineer who inherited Stark Industries, a weapons manufacturer. While demonstrating new technology abroad, Stark is captured by enemy forces and suffers a severe chest injury from shrapnel threatening his heart. With fellow captive physicist Ho Yinsen, he builds a powered suit of armour fitted with an arc reactor that keeps the shrapnel at bay and allows his escape. Returning home, Stark renounces weapons manufacture and refines the armour, becoming Iron Man — a founding member of the Avengers.

Powers and character

Stark has no innate superpowers; his suits grant flight, superhuman strength, repulsor weaponry and advanced computing, powered by the arc reactor in his chest. Storylines have explored his alcoholism (notably "Demon in a Bottle", 1979), his role in the superhero Civil War, and his self-sacrificing death in the film continuity.

In other media

Portrayed by Robert Downey Jr. in the Marvel Cinematic Universe beginning with Iron Man (2008), the character became one of the most recognisable superheroes in the world and anchored the MCU's first decade.
