Alcohol (drug)

Alcohol (drug)
ChemicalEthanol
FormsBeer, wine, spirits
EffectsRelaxation, disinhibition; dependence and organ damage with misuse
[Image: Glasses of beer, wine and spirits on a bar counter]

Alcohol, specifically ethanol, is a psychoactive drug produced by fermentation of sugars by yeast — the basis of Beer, Wine and distilled spirits, consumed by humans for thousands of years (nearly as long as Bread has been baked). It depresses the central nervous system, lowering inhibition and impairing coordination and judgment.

Harms and regulation

Alcohol contributes to liver cirrhosis, cancers, accidents and addiction (alcoholism), and is among the most harmful drugs overall by combined harm to users and others. States regulate it by taxation, licensing, drink-driving laws and, in some places and eras (US Prohibition, in the wider crawl), outright bans.
