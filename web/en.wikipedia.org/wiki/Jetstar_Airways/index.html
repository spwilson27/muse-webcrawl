Jetstar Airways Pty Ltd, operating as Jetstar, is an Australian low-cost airline headquartered in Melbourne, Victoria. A wholly owned subsidiary of the Qantas Group, it was established in 2003 as Qantas's response to the low-cost carrier Virgin Blue (now Virgin Australia). Jetstar operates an extensive domestic network within Australia and New Zealand and international services across Asia and the Pacific.

History

Jetstar launched domestic services on 25 May 2004 with a fleet of Boeing 717s inherited from Impulse Airlines, soon replacing them with Airbus A320s. Long-haul operations began in 2006 with Airbus A330s, later replaced by Boeing 787 Dreamliners, serving destinations such as Honolulu, Bali, Tokyo and Seoul. The Jetstar brand was extended through part-owned affiliates including Jetstar Asia (Singapore), Jetstar Pacific (Vietnam) and Jetstar Japan; Jetstar Asia was merged into Scoot's Singapore operations' successor arrangements in 2025, when Qantas wound down the Singapore-based airline.

Fleet and network

The fleet is centred on the Airbus A320 family, increasingly the A321neo and long-range A321LR/XLR variants, plus Boeing 787-8s for long-haul routes. Major bases include Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane, Gold Coast, Perth and Auckland.
