Monte Rosa

Monte Rosa (Italian: Massiccio del Monte Rosa; German: Monte Rosa-Massiv) is a mountain massif in the eastern part of the Pennine Alps, on the border between Italy (Piedmont and Aosta Valley) and Switzerland (Valais). Its highest peak, the Dufourspitze at 4,634 metres (15,203 ft), is the highest point in Switzerland and the second-highest mountain in the Alps and western Europe, after Mont Blanc.

The massif is a large group of peaks, most over 4,000 metres, including the Zumsteinspitze, Signalkuppe (on which stands the Margherita Hut, the highest building in Europe), Parrotspitze, Ludwigshöhe, and the Lyskamm. It is surrounded by some of the largest glaciers in the Alps, including the Gorner Glacier. The name "Rosa" derives not from the Italian word for "pink" but from a local dialect term related to "glacier". The Dufourspitze was first climbed in 1855 and is named after the Swiss general and cartographer Guillaume-Henri Dufour.
