The SBF 120 (Société des Bourses Françaises 120 Index) is a benchmark stock market index of the Paris Bourse, operated by Euronext. It is composed of the 120 most actively traded and largest capitalisation stocks listed in Paris, and serves as the underlying index for most French equity index funds and structured products tracking the broad French market.

Composition

The SBF 120 combines the constituents of three sub-indices: the CAC 40 (the 40 largest and most liquid stocks), the CAC Next 20 (the next 20 candidates for promotion to the CAC 40) and the CAC Mid 60 (60 mid-cap companies). Membership is reviewed quarterly by Euronext's index steering committee, based on free-float market capitalisation and trading volume (velocity).

Calculation

The index is calculated in real time during Paris trading hours (09:00–17:30 CET) and is weighted by free-float-adjusted market capitalisation, with a capping factor applied so that no single constituent exceeds a set share of the index. It is available in price-return and total-return (dividends reinvested) versions.
