Arconic Corporation is an American industrial company specialising in lightweight metals engineering and manufacturing, producing aluminium sheet, plate, and extrusions as well as engineered products for the aerospace, automotive, and construction markets.

History

Arconic was created on 1 November 2016 when Alcoa split into two companies: Alcoa Corporation, retaining mining and smelting, and Arconic, taking the engineered products businesses. On 1 April 2020 Arconic itself split, with its forged and engineered structures businesses becoming Howmet Aerospace while Arconic retained the rolled products operations.

Products

The company produces aluminium rolled products, architectural systems, and transportation components, supplying airframe structures, automotive body sheet, and building façades.
