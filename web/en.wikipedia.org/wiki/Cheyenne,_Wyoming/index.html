Cheyenne, Wyoming

Cheyenne
State capital of Wyoming
Founded: 1867
Population: about 65,000

Cheyenne is the capital and most populous city of the U.S. state of Wyoming, and the county seat of Laramie County. It is located in the southeastern corner of the state, near the borders with Colorado and Nebraska.

History

Cheyenne was founded in 1867 as a Union Pacific Railroad town and named for the Cheyenne Native American people. It became the territorial and then state capital. Its frontier heritage is celebrated each July with Cheyenne Frontier Days, a large rodeo and western festival dating to 1897.

Geography and economy

Situated on the high plains at about 1,850 metres elevation, the city is a transport and government centre; F. E. Warren Air Force Base lies on its western edge.
