The New Teen Titans

The New Teen Titans is an American comic book series published by DC Comics, launched in 1980 by writer Marv Wolfman and artist George Pérez. Reviving the Teen Titans concept, it became one of DC's best-selling and most acclaimed titles of the 1980s.

Team and stories

The roster combined returning members Robin (Dick Grayson), Wonder Girl (Donna Troy), Kid Flash, Beast Boy (as Changeling), and Cyborg with new characters Raven and Starfire. Famous story arcs include "The Judas Contract," which introduced the mercenary Deathstroke's infiltration of the team through Terra, and tales featuring the demon Trigon and the organization H.I.V.E.

Legacy

The series' character-driven storytelling and long-form arcs influenced later superhero comics, and its version of the team underlies subsequent Teen Titans adaptations in animation and television.
