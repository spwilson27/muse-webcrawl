Aero Vodochody

Aero Vodochody Aerospace a.s. is a Czech aircraft manufacturer based at Vodochody, north of Prague. Founded in 1919, it is one of the oldest aircraft companies in the world and is best known for its military jet trainers.

History

The company built fighters and reconnaissance aircraft in the interwar years, licence-produced designs during the Second World War and under communism, and from the 1960s specialised in jet trainers: the L-29 Delfín, the first jet trainer designed in the Eastern Bloc; the L-39 Albatros, of which nearly 3,000 were built; and the L-159 ALCA light combat aircraft. It also produced the S-76 Sikorsky helicopter fuselages and components for other manufacturers as an aerostructures supplier.

Recent activity

After privatisation and a period under Boeing and later the Penta investment group, Aero developed the L-39NG, a modernised new-build version of the Albatros with a Williams engine and glass cockpit, which first flew in 2018.
