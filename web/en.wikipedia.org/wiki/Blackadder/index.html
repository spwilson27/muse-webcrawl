Blackadder

Genre: Sitcom, historical comedy
Created by: Richard Curtis, Rowan Atkinson
Starring: Rowan Atkinson, Tony Robinson
Country: United Kingdom
No. of series: 4
Original release: 1983–1989

Blackadder is a series of four period British sitcoms, plus several one-off instalments, originally broadcast by BBC One between 1983 and 1989. All television episodes starred Rowan Atkinson as the antihero Edmund Blackadder and Tony Robinson as his dogsbody, Baldrick.

Series overview

Each series is set in a different historical period, with Blackadder's character evolving from a dim-witted prince in The Black Adder (1983), through a cunning Elizabethan courtier in Blackadder II (1986), a scheming butler in Blackadder the Third (1987), to a cynical army captain in Blackadder Goes Forth (1989), set in the trenches of the First World War.

Recurring themes

A running gag is Baldrick's announcement "I have a cunning plan", usually preceding a ludicrous scheme. The final episode of Blackadder Goes Forth, "Goodbyeee", is widely praised for its poignant ending in which the main characters go over the top.

Legacy

Blackadder is regularly voted among the best British sitcoms of all time. A one-off special, Blackadder's Christmas Carol, aired in 1988, and Blackadder: Back & Forth was made for the Millennium Dome in 1999.
