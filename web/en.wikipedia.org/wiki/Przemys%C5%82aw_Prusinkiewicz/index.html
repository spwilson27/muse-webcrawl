Przemysław Prusinkiewicz

Field: Computer graphics, computational botany
Institution: University of Calgary
Known for: L-systems; The Algorithmic Beauty of Plants (1990)

Przemysław (Przemek) Prusinkiewicz is a Polish-Canadian computer scientist, professor emeritus at the University of Calgary, known for applying formal language theory to the modelling of plants. His work brought Aristid Lindenmayer's L-systems into computer graphics.

Work

With Aristid Lindenmayer, Prusinkiewicz co-authored The Algorithmic Beauty of Plants (1990), the landmark book showing how L-systems — parallel rewriting grammars — can generate realistic models of plant structure, branching patterns, inflorescences, and phyllotaxis. He later extended this research into developmental models of plant growth, virtual laboratory software (L-studio), and related work on seashells and ornament generation.

Recognition

His plant images became iconic in computer graphics, and he received awards from the computer graphics community for contributions to the modelling of natural phenomena.
