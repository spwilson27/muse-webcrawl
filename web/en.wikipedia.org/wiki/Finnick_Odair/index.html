Finnick Odair

Finnick Odair is a fictional character in The Hunger Games trilogy by American author Suzanne Collins. He is a victor of the 65th Hunger Games from District 4, the fishing district of the nation of Panem, and later becomes a key ally of Katniss Everdeen during the Quarter Quell and the rebellion against the Capitol. In the film adaptations, Finnick is portrayed by actor Sam Claflin.

Character biography

Finnick won the 65th Hunger Games at the age of fourteen, making him one of the youngest victors in Panem's history. Gifted a trident by a sponsor — reportedly one of the most expensive gifts ever given in the arena — he used it together with a net to trap and kill other tributes, in the style of a gladiator. After his victory he became a Capitol celebrity, famous for his beauty and charm. It is later revealed that President Snow forced the desirable Finnick into prostitution, selling his body to wealthy Capitol citizens, and that Finnick used his position to gather secrets from his clients.

Role in the series

In Catching Fire, Finnick is reaped for the 75th Hunger Games, the third Quarter Quell, alongside the elderly victor Mags, who volunteers in place of Annie Cresta, Finnick's beloved. In the arena he becomes one of Katniss Everdeen's principal allies, saving Peeta Mellark's life and carrying Mags until her death. He is extracted from the arena by the rebels and taken to District 13.

In Mockingjay, a traumatised Finnick works with the rebellion, proposes publicly to Annie and marries her in District 13. During a televised broadcast he exposes the secrets he learned in the Capitol, including Snow's use of poison. Finnick joins the squad that infiltrates the Capitol, where he is killed by muttations in the sewers beneath the city while buying time for the others to escape. Annie later gives birth to their son.

Reception

Finnick is frequently cited as one of the most popular characters in the series, noted for the contrast between his flirtatious public persona and his backstory of exploitation and devotion to Annie. Sam Claflin's portrayal in The Hunger Games: Catching Fire (2013) and the two Mockingjay films was widely praised.
