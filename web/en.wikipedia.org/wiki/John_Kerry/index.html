John Kerry

John Forbes Kerry (born December 11, 1943) is an American politician and diplomat who served as the 68th United States Secretary of State from 2013 to 2017 and was the Democratic nominee for president in 2004, losing narrowly to incumbent George W. Bush.

Early life and Vietnam

Born in Aurora, Colorado, and educated at Yale, Kerry enlisted in the US Navy and commanded a Swift boat in Vietnam, earning a Silver Star, a Bronze Star, and three Purple Hearts. On returning home he became a prominent spokesman for Vietnam Veterans Against the War, testifying before the Senate in 1971.

Senate and 2004 campaign

Kerry represented Massachusetts in the US Senate from 1985 to 2013, chairing the Foreign Relations Committee. His 2004 presidential campaign was shadowed by attacks on his war record by the "Swift Boat Veterans for Truth," and he lost the Electoral College 251–286 despite winning 48.3% of the popular vote.

Secretary of State and later work

As President Barack Obama's secretary of state, Kerry negotiated the 2015 Iran nuclear deal (JCPOA) and helped achieve the Paris Agreement on climate change. From 2021 to 2024 he served as the first US Special Presidential Envoy for Climate under President Joe Biden.
