Ivalice

Ivalice (イヴァリース, Ivarīsu) is a fictional world created by Yasumi Matsuno for a series of video games published by Square (now Square Enix). It first appeared in Final Fantasy Tactics (1997) and later served as the setting of Vagrant Story, Final Fantasy Tactics Advance, Final Fantasy XII, and related titles, collectively associated with the Ivalice Alliance compilation.

Setting

Although presented differently across games, Ivalice is generally a medieval-style world of kingdoms, airships, and magic, populated by humans and races such as the bangaa, viera, moogles, and nu mou in the later games. Recurring elements include the Zodiac Braves legend, auracite, and the influence of the Church of Glabados in the Tactics storyline.

Games

  • Final Fantasy Tactics (1997)
  • Vagrant Story (2000)
  • Final Fantasy Tactics Advance (2003)
  • Final Fantasy XII (2006)
  • Final Fantasy Tactics A2: Grimoire of the Rift (2007)

Reception

The setting is noted for its mature political themes, intricate plots, and distinctive art direction under Akihiko Yoshida and Hiroshi Minagawa.
