Wiley (musician)

Richard Kylea Cowie Jr. (born 19 January 1979), better known by his stage name Wiley, is a British grime MC, rapper, songwriter, DJ and record producer from Bow, East London. He is widely regarded as the founding father of grime music, sometimes nicknamed the "Godfather of Grime".

Career

Wiley began on pirate radio, performing with crews such as the Ladbroke Grove-based Pay As U Go Cartel before founding the Roll Deep entourage. His early white-label releases, including "Eskimo" (2002), defined the cold, minimal "eski beat" sound from which grime took its original name. His debut album, Treddin' on Thin Ice (2004), is considered a landmark of the genre.

He later achieved mainstream UK chart success with singles including "Wearing My Rolex" (2008) and "Heatwave" (2012), which reached number one. His 2017 album Godfather was critically acclaimed and won the NME Godlike Genius Award era of recognition; he was appointed MBE in 2018 for services to music. Wiley mentored or influenced a generation of grime artists, including Dizzee Rascal, Skepta, and Stormzy.
