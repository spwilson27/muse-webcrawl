Highlands Ranch is a census-designated place (CDP) and large master-planned community in Douglas County, Colorado, United States, forming part of the Denver metropolitan area. With a population of 103,444 at the 2020 census, it is one of the most populous unincorporated communities in the United States.

History

The land was used for ranching from the 19th century; its centrepiece, the Highlands Ranch Mansion, was built over decades beginning in the 1890s. Development of the planned community began in 1981 under Mission Viejo Company and later Shea Homes, on roughly 22,000 acres south of Denver. The community is governed through a metropolitan district and the Highlands Ranch Community Association, which operates recreation centres, trails and open space, while municipal services are provided by Douglas County.

Character

Highlands Ranch is a predominantly residential suburb of single-family homes, schools, retail centres and office parks, with extensive parks and trail systems and views of the Rocky Mountain foothills. It consistently ranks among the wealthiest and most highly educated communities in Colorado, and its rapid growth made it a noted example of late 20th-century American master-planned suburban development.
