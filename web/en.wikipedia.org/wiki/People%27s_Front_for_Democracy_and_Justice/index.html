People's Front for Democracy and Justice

People's Front for Democracy and Justice

Abbreviation: PFDJ

Founded: 16 February 1994

Headquarters: Asmara, Eritrea

Leader: Isaias Afwerki

Predecessor: Eritrean People's Liberation Front

The People's Front for Democracy and Justice (PFDJ; Tigrinya: ህዝባዊ ግንባር ንደሞክራስን ፍትሕን) is the founding and sole legal political party of Eritrea. It is headed by Isaias Afwerki, the country's president since independence.

History

The PFDJ was established at the third congress of the Eritrean People's Liberation Front (EPLF) in February 1994, renaming the wartime liberation movement as a peacetime political front after Eritrea's independence from Ethiopia in 1993. The EPLF had waged a 30-year war of independence, culminating in the capture of Asmara in 1991 and a UN-supervised referendum in 1993.

Rule

The PFDJ governs Eritrea as a one-party state. National elections scheduled under the 1997 constitution have never been held, and the constitution itself has not been implemented. The party oversees indefinite national service for citizens and exerts deep influence over the military, economy and media; independent media were shut down in 2001 and opposition figures detained. Supporters credit the PFDJ with achieving and defending independence; critics, including the United Nations and human rights organisations, have accused its government of widespread abuses.
