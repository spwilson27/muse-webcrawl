Nathan Evans (singer)

Nathan Evans

Born: 19 December 1994, Airdrie, Scotland

Genres: Folk, sea shanty, pop

Occupation: Singer-songwriter

Years active: 2020–present

Known for: "Wellerman"

Nathan Evans (born 19 December 1994) is a Scottish singer-songwriter from Airdrie, North Lanarkshire. He rose to fame in late 2020 and early 2021 when his TikTok performances of sea shanties went viral, sparking the "ShantyTok" trend during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Career

Evans worked as a postal worker for Royal Mail before his music career. He began posting covers on TikTok in 2020. His rendition of the 19th-century New Zealand whaling song "Wellerman" went viral in January 2021, leading to a record deal with Polydor Records. A remixed version with producers 220 Kid and Billen Ted reached number one on the UK Singles Chart and topped charts in several other countries.

He released the albums Blood, Sweat and Tears (2022) and 1994 (2024), the latter named for his birth year and reaching number one in Scotland.

Discography

Singles include "Wellerman" (2021), "Ring Ding (A Scotsman's Story)", "Told You So", and "Heather on the Hill" (2024).
