MacBook Air

The MacBook Air is a line of laptop computers developed and manufactured by Apple Inc. since 2008. It features a thin, light aluminum enclosure and was promoted as the world's thinnest notebook when Steve Jobs pulled the original model out of a manila envelope at the Macworld Conference & Expo in January 2008.

History

The first MacBook Air used a custom Intel Core 2 Duo processor and a 1.8-inch hard drive borrowed from the iPod. A major redesign in 2010 introduced flash storage, an 11.6-inch model, and the wedge-shaped unibody design that defined the line for a decade. In 2020 the MacBook Air became one of the first Macs to ship with Apple's own silicon, the Apple M1 chip, followed by the redesigned M2 model in 2022 and an M3 model in 2024.

Design

The Air is Apple's lightest and thinnest notebook line, aimed at consumers and students. Current models use a fanless design, a backlit Magic Keyboard, Touch ID, and a Liquid Retina display, and are offered in 13-inch and 15-inch sizes.
