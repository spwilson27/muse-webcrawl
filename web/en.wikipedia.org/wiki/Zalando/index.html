Zalando

Zalando SE

Founded: 2008, Berlin, Germany

Founders: Robert Gentz, David Schneider

Industry: E-commerce (fashion)

Zalando SE is a German online retailer of shoes, fashion and beauty products, headquartered in Berlin. Founded in 2008 by Robert Gentz and David Schneider with backing from Rocket Internet, it grew from a shoe shop into one of Europe's largest fashion platforms.

History

The company initially sold footwear online in Germany, modelled on the American retailer Zappos, and expanded across Europe from 2009 onward. Zalando listed on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange in 2014 and later shifted from pure retail toward a platform model connecting brands and partner retailers.

Operations

Zalando serves millions of active customers across more than twenty European markets, operating its own logistics network and the Zalando Lounge off-price club and Zalon styling services.
