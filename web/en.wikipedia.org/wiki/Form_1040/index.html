Form 1040

Form 1040
Official name: U.S. Individual Income Tax Return
Issued by: Internal Revenue Service
First issued: 1913

Form 1040, officially the U.S. Individual Income Tax Return, is the standard form used by individuals to file their annual income tax returns with the Internal Revenue Service (IRS). Taxpayers report income, claim deductions and credits, and calculate the tax owed or refund due.

History

The first Form 1040 was issued in 1913 following ratification of the Sixteenth Amendment, which authorised a federal income tax. The original form was three pages with a page of instructions. Over the twentieth century the form and its schedules grew considerably more complex. A major redesign for tax year 2018 shrank the main form to postcard size and moved many items to new numbered schedules, though later revisions restored some lines.

Variants and schedules

Related forms have included the simplified 1040A and the short 1040EZ (discontinued after 2017), and Form 1040-SR for seniors. Income and adjustments are reported on schedules such as Schedule A (itemised deductions), Schedule C (business income) and Schedule D (capital gains). Most returns are now filed electronically.
