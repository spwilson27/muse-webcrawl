Martin Fourcade (born 4 September 1988) is a retired French biathlete and non-commissioned officer. He is one of the most successful biathletes in history, winning five Olympic gold medals and thirteen World Championship titles, and a record seven consecutive overall World Cup globes.

Career

Fourcade dominated men's biathlon through the 2010s, winning the overall World Cup title every season from 2011–12 to 2017–18. At the 2018 Pyeongchang Winter Olympics he won three gold medals. He announced his retirement in March 2020, ending his career with a victory in Kontiolahti.

Later work

After retiring he became involved in sports administration and environmental advocacy, serving on the IOC Athletes' Commission and supporting the Paris 2024 organising efforts. In 2025 it was confirmed he would receive a sixth Olympic title after the reallocation of a 2010 relay medal following a doping disqualification.
