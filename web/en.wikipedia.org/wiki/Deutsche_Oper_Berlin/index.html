Deutsche Oper Berlin

The Deutsche Oper Berlin is an opera company and opera house located in the Charlottenburg district of Berlin, Germany. It is the second largest of Berlin's three opera houses, with seating for about 1,850.

History

The house opened in 1912 as the Deutsches Opernhaus. The original building was destroyed in an air raid in 1943; the present building, designed by Fritz Bornemann, opened in 1961. It served as West Berlin's principal opera house during the city's division.
