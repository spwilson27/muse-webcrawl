Qatar National Library

Qatar National Library (QNL) is the national library of Qatar, located in Education City, Doha. Designed by Rem Koolhaas and OMA, the diamond-shaped building opened officially on 16 April 2018, having opened to the public in November 2017.

Collections

The library holds over one million books and provides access to extensive digital resources. Its Heritage Library contains rare manuscripts, maps, and documents relating to Arab and Islamic history and the Gulf region. The Qatar Digital Library, developed with the British Library, digitises archival material on the Gulf.

Services

QNL functions as national, research, and public library, hosting exhibitions, lectures, children's services, and innovation spaces with 3D printing and music studios.
