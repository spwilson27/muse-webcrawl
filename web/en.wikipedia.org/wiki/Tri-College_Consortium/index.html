Tri-College Consortium

The Tri-College Consortium (also known as the Tri-Co) is an academic consortium of three liberal arts colleges in the Philadelphia suburbs of Pennsylvania: Bryn Mawr College, Haverford College, and Swarthmore College.

Cooperation

The three colleges, all founded in the 19th century with Quaker roots, share a course registration system that lets students take classes at any of the three campuses, a common library catalogue (Tripod), and shuttle services between the campuses. Together they enroll roughly 5,000 students.

Bryn Mawr and Haverford maintain an especially close "Bi-College" relationship, including joint departments and a shared newspaper. The consortium also operates cooperative programs in areas such as languages and peace and conflict studies. See Swarthmore College and Haverford College.
