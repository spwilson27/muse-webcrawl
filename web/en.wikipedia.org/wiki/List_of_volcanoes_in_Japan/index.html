List of volcanoes in Japan

Japan has 111 active volcanoes as designated by the Japan Meteorological Agency — about a tenth of the world's total — because the archipelago sits at the junction of several tectonic plates along the Pacific Ring of Fire. Around 50 are continuously monitored. Major eruptions in recorded history include the 1707 Hōei eruption of Mount Fuji, the 1914 eruption of Sakurajima, the 1991–1995 eruption of Mount Unzen, and the 2014 eruption of Mount Ontake, which killed 63 hikers.

Volcanic activity has shaped Japanese culture and geography: hot springs (onsen) cluster around volcanic areas, and volcanoes such as Fuji, Asama and Sakurajima feature prominently in art and religion. The Japan Meteorological Agency maintains a five-level volcanic alert system for inhabited areas.
