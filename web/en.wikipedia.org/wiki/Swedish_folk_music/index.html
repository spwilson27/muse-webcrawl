Swedish folk music

Swedish folk music encompasses the traditional music of Sweden, dominated by dance music played on the fiddle. Its best-known forms are the polska, a dance in triple time, and related couple dances such as the hambo, along with herding music, ballads and ceremonial tunes.

Spelmän and traditions

Folk musicians, called spelmän, traditionally played at weddings and dances, and many tunes are associated with legendary local fiddlers. The province of Dalarna is especially famed for its rich fiddling tradition and large midsummer celebrations.

Instruments

Besides the fiddle, distinctive instruments include the nyckelharpa (keyed fiddle), historically centred on Uppland, herding instruments such as the cow horn and wooden lur, and later the accordion and clarinet.

Revival

A strong folk music revival from the 1970s onwards institutionalised the tradition through spelmansstämmor (fiddlers' gatherings), folk high schools and professional ensembles, and Sweden's folk scene remains one of Europe's most vigorous.
