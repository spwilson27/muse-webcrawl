Mass Effect 2 is a 2010 action role-playing video game developed by BioWare and published by Electronic Arts. It is the second game in the Mass Effect series and the sequel to Mass Effect (2007). The game was released for Microsoft Windows and Xbox 360 in January 2010 and for PlayStation 3 in 2011.

Plot

Set two years after the first game, the story follows Commander Shepard, who is killed in an ambush by the mysterious Collectors and resurrected by the human-supremacist organisation Cerberus. Working for Cerberus's leader, the Illusive Man, Shepard must assemble a team of specialists, earn their loyalty, and lead them through the Omega-4 relay on a "suicide mission" to stop the Collectors, who are abducting entire human colonies on behalf of the Reapers. The survival of Shepard and each squadmate depends on the player's choices, ship upgrades, and performance in the final mission.

Gameplay

Mass Effect 2 streamlines its predecessor's systems: combat plays as a cover-based third-person shooter, character classes are more distinct, and players can import a save file from the first game so earlier decisions carry over. Dialogue, moral choices (Paragon and Renegade), and squad loyalty missions remain central.

Reception

The game received universal acclaim for its characters, writing, voice acting, and improved combat, and it won numerous Game of the Year awards. It is frequently cited among the greatest video games of all time. A sequel, Mass Effect 3, followed in 2012, and the game was remastered in the Mass Effect Legendary Edition in 2021.
