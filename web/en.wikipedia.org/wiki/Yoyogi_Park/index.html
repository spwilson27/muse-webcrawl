Yoyogi Park (代々木公園, Yoyogi kōen) is a park in Yoyogikamizonochō, Shibuya, Tokyo, Japan. It is one of Tokyo's largest city parks, featuring wide lawns, ponds, and forested areas. It is a popular gathering place, particularly on Sundays, for Japanese rock music fans, jugglers, comedians, martial arts groups, cosplayers, and other subculture and hobby groups.

History

The site of Yoyogi Park was where Captain Yoshitoshi Tokugawa made the first powered aircraft flight in Japan on 19 December 1910. It later became an army parade ground. After the Second World War it was the site of the Washington Heights housing complex for United States military personnel. In 1964 the area was used for the main Olympic village for the Tokyo Olympics and the Yoyogi National Gymnasium. In 1967 most of the area north of the gymnasium complex was turned into Yoyogi Park.

Today the park stands adjacent to Harajuku Station and Meiji Shrine in Shibuya. It is well known for its ginkgo tree forest and for gatherings of rockabilly dancers near the Harajuku entrance on Sundays. The park was intended as a venue for the 2020 Summer Olympics, hosting handball events at the neighboring gymnasium.
