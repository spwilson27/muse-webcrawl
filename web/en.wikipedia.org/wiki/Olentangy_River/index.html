Olentangy River

Length97 mi (156 km)
SourceNear Galion, Ohio
MouthScioto River, Columbus, Ohio

The Olentangy River is a 97-mile-long (156 km) tributary of the Scioto River in north-central Ohio, United States. It rises near Galion in Crawford County and flows generally south through Delaware County and the city of Columbus, joining the Scioto River in downtown Columbus.

Name and history

The river's name derives from a Delaware (Lenape) word meaning "river of red face paint," referring to the vermilion clay found along its banks. The Ohio State University campus lies along the river in Columbus, and the riverfront forms part of the city's park system.

Ecology

A 22-mile stretch through Delaware County is designated an Ohio State Scenic River. Removal of low-head dams in Columbus in the 2010s restored free-flowing conditions and improved habitat for fish and mussels.
