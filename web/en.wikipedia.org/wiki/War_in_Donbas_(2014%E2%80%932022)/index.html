War in Donbas (2014–2022)

The war in Donbas (2014–2022) was the armed conflict between Ukraine and Russian-backed separatists in Donetsk and Luhansk oblasts that ran from April 2014 until Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine on 24 February 2022.

Phases

The conflict began with the seizure of administration buildings in spring 2014 and escalated into open warfare during the summer, including the battles for Sloviansk, Kramatorsk and Donetsk Airport. After the Minsk agreements, the war settled into static trench warfare punctuated by shelling and local offensives, with the contact line running through towns such as Avdiivka, Marinka and Shchastia.

Casualties and displacement

The United Nations estimated total deaths at over 14,200 by the end of 2021, including combatants on both sides and at least 3,400 civilians. Around 1.5 million people were registered as internally displaced at the conflict's peak.

Aftermath

Russia's recognition of the self-proclaimed republics on 21 February 2022 and the invasion three days later transformed the regional war into the largest European conflict since the Second World War.
