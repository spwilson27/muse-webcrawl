Jan Długosz

Jan Długosz (1 December 1415 – 19 May 1480), also known as Johannes Longinus, was a Polish chronicler, diplomat and clergyman. His twelve-volume Annales seu cronicae incliti regni Poloniae (Annals or Chronicles of the Famous Kingdom of Poland) is the greatest Polish historical work of the Middle Ages.

Life

Długosz entered the service of Zbigniew Oleśnicki, Bishop of Kraków, and later became secretary to King Casimir IV Jagiellon. He served on numerous diplomatic missions across Europe and was Archbishop-elect of Lwów shortly before his death.

Annals

The Annals, written in Latin between 1455 and 1480, cover Polish history from legendary times to 1480, incorporating European and crusading history. Despite some legendary material, they remain a foundational source for medieval Poland, notably including a detailed account of the Battle of Grunwald (1410).
