List of Batman enemies

Batman has a large cast of adversaries — his "rogues gallery" — widely regarded as one of the most distinctive in comics. It ranges from mobsters and costumed criminals to superhuman threats, many of them confined in Arkham Asylum. This list covers the most prominent recurring enemies.

Other notable enemies

Additional recurring foes include Hugo Strange, Deadshot, Firefly, Killer Moth, the Ventriloquist and Scarface, Black Mask, Hush (Thomas Elliot), Talia al Ghul, Lady Shiva, Deathstroke (also a Titans foe), Victor Zsasz, Solomon Grundy, the Court of Owls, Calendar Man, Kite Man, Professor Pyg, Mr. Bloom, and crime families such as the Falcones and Maronis.
