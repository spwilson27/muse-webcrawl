Silver Linings Playbook

Silver Linings Playbook is a 2012 American romantic comedy-drama film written and directed by David O. Russell. An adaptation of Matthew Quick's 2008 novel The Silver Linings Playbook, the film stars Bradley Cooper and Jennifer Lawrence, with Robert De Niro, Jacki Weaver, Chris Tucker, and Julia Stiles in supporting roles. It tells the story of Pat Solitano (Cooper), a man with bipolar disorder who, after a stay in a psychiatric facility, moves back in with his parents and meets Tiffany Maxwell (Lawrence), a young widow who offers to help him reconcile with his estranged wife if he will partner her in a dance competition.

Plot

After eight months in a mental health facility for assaulting his wife's lover, Pat Solitano is released into the care of his parents in suburban Philadelphia. Determined to win back his wife Nikki despite a restraining order, he insists on reading the books on her teaching syllabus and on maintaining an optimistic philosophy of finding "silver linings". Through friends he meets Tiffany, the widowed sister-in-law of his friend Ronnie, who has her own history of depression and compulsive behaviour. Tiffany offers to deliver a letter to Nikki if Pat will be her partner in an upcoming dance contest. As they train, Pat's obsession with the Philadelphia Eagles — shared by his father, a superstitious bookmaker — leads to a climactic wager that combines the Eagles' result with the couple's dance score. Pat gradually recognises his feelings for Tiffany, and the two begin a relationship.

Release and reception

The film premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival on September 8, 2012, where it won the People's Choice Award, and was released in the United States by The Weinstein Company in November 2012. It received widespread critical acclaim for Russell's direction and screenplay and for the performances of Cooper, Lawrence and De Niro, and was a substantial commercial success, grossing over $236 million worldwide against a $21 million budget.

Accolades

Silver Linings Playbook received eight Academy Award nominations, including Best Picture, Best Director and Best Adapted Screenplay, and became the first film since Reds (1981) to be nominated in all four acting categories. Jennifer Lawrence won the Academy Award for Best Actress. The film also won four Independent Spirit Awards, including Best Film.
