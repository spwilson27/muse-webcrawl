Oneworld (stylised as oneworld) is a global airline alliance founded on 1 February 1999. Its member airlines include Alaska Airlines, American Airlines, British Airways, Cathay Pacific, Finnair, Iberia, Japan Airlines, Malaysia Airlines, Qantas, Qatar Airways, Royal Air Maroc, Royal Jordanian, and SriLankan Airlines, together with a number of affiliated carriers.

As one of the three largest airline alliances, alongside Star Alliance and SkyTeam, Oneworld member airlines serve more than a thousand destinations worldwide and coordinate frequent-flyer programmes, lounge access, codeshares, and through fares for their passengers.

History

The alliance was announced in September 1998 by founding members American Airlines, British Airways, Canadian Airlines, Cathay Pacific, and Qantas, and began operations on 1 February 1999. It was the third major alliance to launch, after Star Alliance (1997) and shortly before SkyTeam (2000). Canadian Airlines left in 2000 following its acquisition by Air Canada, a Star Alliance member.

Membership has expanded and shifted over the years: Aer Lingus departed in 2007 (later partly reintegrating through a joint business), Malév and Mexicana ceased operating, S7 Airlines' membership was suspended in 2022, and Royal Air Maroc joined in 2020 as the alliance's first African full member. Hawaiian Airlines has been announced as a future member following its acquisition by Alaska Air Group.

Member benefits

Oneworld offers reciprocal frequent-flyer earning and redemption across members, tier status recognition (Emerald, Sapphire, and Ruby) with benefits such as lounge access, priority check-in and boarding, and the oneworld Explorer round-the-world fare product.
