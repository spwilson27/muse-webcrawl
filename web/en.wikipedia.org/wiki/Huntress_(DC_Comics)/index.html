Huntress (DC Comics)

The Huntress is the name of several fictional characters appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. The best-known versions are Helena Wayne, the daughter of Batman and Catwoman of Earth-Two, created by Paul Levitz, Joe Staton, and Bob Layton and first appearing in All Star Comics #69 and DC Super Stars #17 (1977); and Helena Bertinelli, a Gotham City vigilante from a Mafia family, introduced in The Huntress #1 (1989), created by Joey Cavalieri and Joe Staton. An earlier Golden Age Huntress, Paula Brooks, was a villain who later used the name Tigress.

Helena Bertinelli as the Huntress, armed with her crossbow.

Helena Bertinelli

Helena Rosa Bertinelli was born into the Bertinelli crime family, once among Gotham's most powerful Mafia dynasties. At age eight she witnessed the murder of her entire family and was raised in Sicily by relatives, training in combat and marksmanship. Returning to Gotham as the Huntress, she waged a violent war on organised crime, frequently clashing with Batman over her willingness to kill. She later became a core member of the Birds of Prey, worked with the Question, and in the New 52 continuity was reinvented as Helena Wayne, while Bertinelli returned in the Grayson series as a spy of the organisation Spyral before resuming the Huntress identity in DC Rebirth.

Helena Wayne

On Earth-Two, Helena Wayne was the daughter of Bruce Wayne and Selina Kyle, raised as a hero and a lawyer. After her mother's death she adopted the Huntress identity, joining the Justice Society of America and Infinity, Inc. She died during Crisis on Infinite Earths (1985) and was erased from continuity, but the character was revived in the 2012 series Worlds' Finest as a refugee from Earth 2 operating alongside Power Girl.

In other media

The Huntress has appeared in animated series including Justice League Unlimited and Batman: The Brave and the Bold, in the television series Birds of Prey (2002) and Arrow, and in the film Birds of Prey (2020), played by Mary Elizabeth Winstead.
