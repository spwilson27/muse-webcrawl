List of equestrian statues

An equestrian statue is a statue of a rider mounted on a horse, a genre that has symbolised military and political authority since antiquity. Famous surviving ancient examples include the statue of Marcus Aurelius in Rome. This list collects notable equestrian statues by country.

An often-repeated urban legend claims that the position of the horse's hooves indicates how the rider died (rearing = died in battle, one hoof raised = died of wounds, all hooves down = died of natural causes). The claim is a myth and does not hold for most statues, including those at Gettysburg, where it is most often repeated.
