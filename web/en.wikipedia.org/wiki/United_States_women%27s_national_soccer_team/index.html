The United States women's national soccer team (USWNT) represents the United States in international women's soccer competitions. It is the most successful team in the history of the FIFA Women's World Cup, having won the tournament four times, and has also won multiple Olympic gold medals.

History

The team played its first international match in 1985 and won the inaugural Women's World Cup in China in 1991. The 1999 World Cup, hosted in the United States, became a landmark moment for women's sports, culminating in a sold-out final at the Rose Bowl where Brandi Chastain converted the winning penalty kick against China.

Recent era

Back-to-back World Cup titles in 2015 and 2019, led by players such as Carli Lloyd, Megan Rapinoe, and Alex Morgan, cemented the team's dominance. In 2022 the team reached a landmark equal-pay agreement with U.S. Soccer. After a disappointing round-of-16 exit at the 2023 World Cup, the team rebounded to win gold at the 2024 Paris Olympics under coach Emma Hayes.

Honours

The USWNT has won four World Cups, five Olympic gold medals, and numerous CONCACAF titles, and has spent long periods ranked first in the FIFA Women's World Rankings.
