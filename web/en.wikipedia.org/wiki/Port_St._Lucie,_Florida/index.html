Port St. Lucie, Florida

Port St. Lucie is a city in St. Lucie County, Florida, United States, on the Atlantic coast in the region known as the Treasure Coast. It is one of the largest cities in Florida by population and was among the fastest-growing cities in the United States in the early 21st century.

History

The area was largely undeveloped ranch and citrus land until the 1950s, when the General Development Corporation purchased some 40,000 acres and began building a planned community. The city was incorporated in 1961. Growth accelerated dramatically in the 2000s housing boom, briefly stalling in the Great Recession before resuming; the population exceeded 200,000 in the 2020 census.

Features

Port St. Lucie is the spring training home of the New York Mets at Clover Park. The city is largely residential, organized around master-planned communities such as Tradition, and includes the Port St. Lucie Botanical Gardens and stretches of the St. Lucie River. The PGA Village golf complex is also located there.
