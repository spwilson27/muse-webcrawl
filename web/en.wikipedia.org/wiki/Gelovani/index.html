Gelovani

Gelovani is a Georgian surname. The best-known bearer is Mikheil Gelovani (1893–1956), a Georgian and Soviet film actor celebrated for his portrayals of Joseph Stalin in numerous Soviet propaganda films, including The Great Dawn (1938), The Defence of Tsaritsyn (1942), The Vow (1946) and The Fall of Berlin (1949).

Mikheil Gelovani

Gelovani was born in Lasuria, in the Kutaisi Governorate of the Russian Empire. He began acting in Georgian theatre and cinema in the 1920s before becoming the screen embodiment of Stalin, a role that dominated his later career. He received the Stalin Prize several times (1941, 1942, 1946, 1947) and was named a People's Artist of the USSR in 1950.
