Water dog

A water dog is a type of gundog bred to flush and retrieve game from water.

Water dog is a type of gundog bred to flush game from cover and to retrieve birds shot over water. Water dogs are typically medium-sized, active dogs with dense, often curly or wavy coats that protect them in cold water, and many are noted for their swimming ability and intelligence.

History

Dogs have assisted fishermen and hunters around water for centuries. On the Portuguese coast, the Portuguese Water Dog worked aboard fishing boats, herding fish into nets, retrieving lost tackle and carrying messages between boats. Similar regional types developed in Spain, France (the Barbet), Ireland and the Netherlands. Many modern retriever and spaniel breeds descend in part from these old water dogs, and the poodle — now often seen as a companion breed — originated as a German water retriever, its distinctive clip designed to aid swimming.

Breeds

Breeds commonly classed as water dogs include the Portuguese Water Dog, Spanish Water Dog, Barbet, Irish Water Spaniel, American Water Spaniel, Wetterhoun, Lagotto Romagnolo (originally a water retriever before becoming a truffle dog) and the standard poodle.
