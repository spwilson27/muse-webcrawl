Luc Paul Maurice Besson (born 18 March 1959) is a French film director, producer, and screenwriter. A leading figure of the Cinéma du look movement, he is known for visually stylish action and science-fiction films and for founding the production company EuropaCorp.

Career

Besson grew up around the Mediterranean, where his parents worked as scuba-diving instructors; an accident at 17 ended his own diving ambitions and turned him to film. After early work including Le Dernier Combat (1983), he broke through with Subway (1985), The Big Blue (1988), and La Femme Nikita (1990). Léon: The Professional (1994) and The Fifth Element (1997) brought him international success; the latter became one of the highest-grossing French films of its era. Through EuropaCorp he wrote and produced the Taxi, Transporter, and Taken franchises, and directed Lucy (2014) and Valerian and the City of a Thousand Planets (2017).

Style

Besson's films favour striking visual design, operatic emotion, and youthful outsiders. He frequently collaborates with composer Éric Serra and cinematographer Thierry Arbogast, and often writes the scripts he directs.

Personal life

Besson has been married several times, including to actresses Anne Parillaud, Maïwenn, and Milla Jovovich, and to producer Virginie Silla.
