Minnesota Vikings

U.S. Bank Stadium, home of the Vikings since 2016

The Minnesota Vikings are a professional American football team based in Minneapolis, Minnesota. The Vikings compete in the National Football League (NFL) as a member of the National Football Conference (NFC) North division. Founded in 1960, the franchise plays its home games at U.S. Bank Stadium.

History

Under coach Bud Grant, the Vikings of the 1970s — led by quarterback Fran Tarkenton and the "Purple People Eaters" defensive line — reached four Super Bowls (IV, VIII, IX and XI) but lost all four. The team won the 1969 NFL Championship before the AFL–NFL merger. Notable later eras include the high-scoring 1998 team featuring Randy Moss and Cris Carter, and the 2017 season highlighted by the "Minneapolis Miracle" playoff play.

Identity

The team's colours are purple and gold, its horned-helmet logo is among the most recognisable in the NFL, and fans celebrate with the "Skol" chant. The Vikings' mascot is Viktor the Viking.
