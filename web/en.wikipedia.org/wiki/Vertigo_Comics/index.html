Vertigo Comics (also simply Vertigo) is an imprint of the American comic book publisher DC Comics, created in 1993 to publish comics with more adult content — graphic violence, sexuality and mature themes — that could not appear under the Comics Code Authority restrictions of DC's main line. Its founding editor was Karen Berger.

History

Vertigo grew out of DC's late-1980s "British Invasion" of writers, consolidating existing mature-readers titles such as The Sandman by Neil Gaiman, Hellblazer, Swamp Thing, Doom Patrol and Shade, the Changing Man. The imprint went on to publish acclaimed series including Preacher, Y: The Last Man, Fables, Transmetropolitan, 100 Bullets, DMZ, Lucifer and Scalped, many of them creator-owned and noted for literary ambitions and long-form storytelling.

Closure and revival

DC retired the Vertigo imprint at the end of 2019 as part of a reorganisation of its publishing lines, folding mature titles into the DC Black Label imprint from January 2020. In 2024, DC announced the revival of Vertigo as its home for creator-owned and adult-oriented series.
