Choir of King's College, Cambridge

The Choir of King's College, Cambridge is an English choir of men and boys that sings daily services in King's College Chapel, Cambridge. Founded in the 15th century under the college statutes of Henry VI, it is one of the world's most celebrated choral ensembles.

Composition

The choir consists of boy choristers educated at King's College School and adult choral scholars and lay clerks from the university. It is led by the Director of Music.

Broadcasts and recordings

Its Festival of Nine Lessons and Carols, begun in 1918 and broadcast by the BBC since 1928, is a Christmas Eve institution heard by millions worldwide. The choir has made numerous recordings of Renaissance, Baroque and contemporary repertoire.
