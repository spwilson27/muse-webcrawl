Atonement is a 2007 romantic war drama film directed by Joe Wright and based on Ian McEwan's 2001 novel of the same name. It stars James McAvoy as Robbie Turner and Keira Knightley as Cecilia Tallis, with Saoirse Ronan as Cecilia's younger sister Briony, whose false accusation destroys the lovers' lives.

Plot

In 1935 England, 13-year-old Briony Tallis misinterprets a series of encounters between her sister Cecilia and Robbie Turner, the housekeeper's son, and accuses Robbie of a crime he did not commit. Five years later Robbie, released from prison to serve in the army, is caught in the retreat to Dunkirk while Cecilia works as a nurse in London. Decades later, an elderly Briony, now a novelist, reveals the story's framing: her book is her attempt at atonement, granting the lovers in fiction the happiness she denied them in life.

Production

The film is noted for a celebrated five-minute Steadicam tracking shot along the beach at Dunkirk, showing thousands of soldiers awaiting evacuation. Locations included Stokesay Court in Shropshire as the Tallis house and the beaches of Redcar standing in for Dunkirk.

Reception

Atonement opened the Venice Film Festival and was nominated for seven Academy Awards, winning Best Original Score for Dario Marianelli. It also won the Golden Globe for Best Motion Picture – Drama and the BAFTA for Best Film.
