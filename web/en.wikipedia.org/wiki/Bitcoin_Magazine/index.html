Bitcoin Magazine is a publication covering Bitcoin and digital currencies. One of the first news magazines devoted to cryptocurrency, it began publishing in 2012 and is among the oldest and most established sources of news, information and commentary on Bitcoin and blockchain technology.

History

The magazine was co-founded in 2012 by Vitalik Buterin — who later went on to co-create Ethereum — together with Mihai Alisie and other early Bitcoin enthusiasts. Its first print issue appeared in May 2012 and was notable as one of the first publications that could itself be purchased with bitcoin. The operation later came under the ownership of BTC Inc (BTC Media), based in Nashville, Tennessee, which also organises the annual Bitcoin Conference, among the largest gatherings of the Bitcoin industry.

Content and activities

Bitcoin Magazine publishes news reporting, technical explainers, market analysis and opinion pieces advocating Bitcoin adoption. Beyond publishing, its parent company runs conferences and other media ventures aimed at the Bitcoin ecosystem. The magazine has also released collectible print editions, including issues distributed at its conferences.
