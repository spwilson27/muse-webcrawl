University of Sierra Leone

The University of Sierra Leone is a public university in Sierra Leone, established in 1972. It is composed of constituent colleges, principally Fourah Bay College, Njala University College (until Njala became a separate university in 2005), the College of Medicine and Allied Health Sciences (COMAHS), and the Institute of Public Administration and Management (IPAM).

History

The university's oldest constituent, Fourah Bay College, was founded in 1827 by the Church Missionary Society to train teachers and missionaries, making it the oldest university-level institution in West Africa. Fourah Bay was affiliated with Durham University in England from 1876 to 1967, and earned a reputation as the "Athens of West Africa" for its role in educating leaders across Anglophone West Africa.

Structure

Under the Universities Act 2021, the constituent colleges gained greater autonomy while remaining part of the university system. Fourah Bay College, on Mount Aureol in Freetown, offers humanities, sciences, engineering, and law; IPAM specializes in business and public administration; COMAHS trains doctors, nurses, and pharmacists.
