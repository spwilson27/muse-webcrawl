Experian

Experian plc is a multinational data analytics and consumer credit reporting company headquartered in Dublin, Ireland, and listed on the London Stock Exchange as a constituent of the FTSE 100 Index. Together with Equifax and TransUnion, it is one of the "Big Three" credit-reporting agencies. Experian collects and aggregates information on more than one billion people and businesses, including hundreds of millions of credit histories.

Operations

The company sells decision analytics, marketing data, and credit reports and scores to businesses, and offers consumers credit monitoring, identity theft protection, and free credit reports. Its largest market is the United States, where it maintains one of the three nationwide consumer credit bureaus. Experian's history runs through TRW Information Systems, acquired in 1996 by Bain Capital and Thomas H. Lee Partners and renamed Experian; it later belonged to the British retailer GUS plc before demerging in 2006.

Controversies

Experian has been involved in several data security incidents, including a 2015 breach at its subsidiary exposing data of about 15 million T-Mobile applicants, and has faced regulatory fines and lawsuits over inaccurate reports and inadequate dispute handling in several countries.
