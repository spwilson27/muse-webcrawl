Horry County, South Carolina

Horry County
County seat: Conway
Largest city: Myrtle Beach
Founded: 1801 (as Kingston County, renamed 1810)
Named for: Peter Horry
Population (2020): about 352,000
Area: 1,255 sq mi (largest county in SC by land area east of the Mississippi comparisons aside, it is the state's largest)

Horry County (pronounced OH-ree) is the easternmost county in the U.S. state of South Carolina, bordering North Carolina and the Atlantic Ocean. Its county seat is Conway, and its largest city is Myrtle Beach. The county forms the core of the Grand Strand resort region and has been among the fastest-growing counties in the United States.

History

The county was created in 1801 as Kingston County and renamed in 1810 for Revolutionary War general Peter Horry. Long isolated by rivers and swamps — locally nicknamed the "Independent Republic of Horry" — its economy rested on tobacco, timber, and naval stores until tourism transformed the coast after the 1930s.

Geography and communities

The Waccamaw River and the Intracoastal Waterway cross the county. Communities include Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Conway, Surfside Beach, Garden City, Loris, Aynor, and Little River. The county is served by Coastal Carolina University in Conway and Myrtle Beach International Airport. Tourism draws well over 15 million visitors in peak years to the Grand Strand's beaches, golf courses, and attractions.
