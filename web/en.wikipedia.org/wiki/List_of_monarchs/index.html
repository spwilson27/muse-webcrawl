List of monarchs

This article provides an overview of lists of monarchs — kings, queens, emperors, sultans and other sovereign rulers — past and present, organised by region and state. There are currently more than 40 sovereign monarchies in the world, ranging from absolute monarchies such as Saudi Arabia and Eswatini to constitutional monarchies such as the United Kingdom, Japan and Spain.

Lists by region

Wikipedia maintains detailed monarch lists for most historical states, including lists of British, French, Spanish, Ottoman, Chinese (emperors by dynasty), Japanese, Roman and Byzantine emperors, popes, and pharaohs of Egypt, as well as lists of the longest-reigning and shortest-reigning monarchs.

Longest-reigning monarchs

The longest verified reign of any monarch belongs to Louis XIV of France (72 years, 110 days, 1643–1715). Queen Elizabeth II reigned for 70 years and 214 days, the longest of any British monarch. Sobhuza II of Swaziland is often credited with an even longer reign (about 82 years) beginning in infancy, though the exact status of his early rule is debated.
