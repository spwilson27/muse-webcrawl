Luke Cage, born Carl Lucas and also known as Power Man, is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created in 1972, he was one of the first African-American superheroes to headline his own comic book series.

Fictional biography

Growing up in Harlem, New York, Lucas was framed for a crime by his former friend Willis Stryker and imprisoned. In prison he volunteered for an experimental cell-regeneration procedure, which a racist guard sabotaged; the accident left Lucas with superhuman strength and steel-hard, bulletproof skin. Escaping and clearing his name, he adopted the name Luke Cage and set up a "Hero for Hire" business, later partnering with martial artist Danny Rand (Iron Fist) in the team Heroes for Hire. Cage later married Jessica Jones, with whom he has a daughter, and has served as a member and leader of the Avengers.

In other media

Mike Colter portrayed Luke Cage in the Marvel Cinematic Universe Netflix series Jessica Jones (2015), Luke Cage (2016–2018), and The Defenders (2017), bringing the character to a wide audience.
