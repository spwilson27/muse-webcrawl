Altamira

The term Altamira may refer to several places and subjects. This page lists the principal articles.

Cave of Altamira

The most famous bearer of the name is the Cave of Altamira near Santillana del Mar in Cantabria, Spain. Discovered in 1868 and recognised in 1879 by Marcelino Sanz de Sautuola and his young daughter María, it contains celebrated Upper Palaeolithic paintings — notably the polychrome bison of the Great Ceiling — executed mainly during the Magdalenian period, roughly 15,000 years ago, with some art dating back more than 30,000 years. Initially dismissed as a forgery, the paintings were accepted as authentic by the early 20th century and transformed understanding of prehistoric art. The cave is a UNESCO World Heritage Site; to protect the fragile paintings, public access is strictly limited and most visitors see the full-scale replica in the adjacent National Museum and Research Centre of Altamira.

Places

  • Altamira, Tamaulipas — a port city and municipality in the Mexican state of Tamaulipas, part of the Tampico metropolitan area and one of Mexico's major industrial ports.
  • Altamira, Pará — a municipality in the Brazilian state of Pará, one of the largest municipalities in the world by area, located on the Xingu River near the Belo Monte Dam.
  • Altamira, Huila — a town and municipality in the Huila Department of Colombia.
  • Altamira, Caracas — a neighbourhood in the Chacao municipality of Caracas, Venezuela, centred on Plaza Altamira (Plaza Francia).
  • Altamira — a neighbourhood of Bilbao, Spain.

Other uses

  • Altamira (publisher) and other companies named for the cave or places above.
  • Altamira — a surname of Spanish origin.
