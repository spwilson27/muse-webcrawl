The T-84 is a Ukrainian main battle tank built on the design of the Soviet T-80, specifically the diesel-engined T-80UD. It was developed by the Kharkiv Morozov Machine Building Design Bureau and manufactured by the Malyshev Factory in Kharkiv.

Design

The T-84 features a welded turret in place of the T-80UD's cast turret, a Ukrainian-built 125 mm KBA-3 gun, Nozh explosive reactive armour, and the Shtora countermeasures system in some variants. Its most advanced version is the T-84 Oplot-M (BM Oplot), adopted by the Ukrainian Ground Forces and exported to Thailand. The export-oriented T-84-120 Yatagan, fitted with a 120 mm NATO-standard gun, was developed for foreign customers.

Service

Small numbers serve with Ukraine, and the type saw use in the war in Donbas and the Russo-Ukrainian War. Thailand received 49 Oplot-T tanks under a contract signed in 2011.
