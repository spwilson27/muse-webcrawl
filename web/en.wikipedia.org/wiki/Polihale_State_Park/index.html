Polihale State Park

Polihale State Park
Location: Kauai, Hawaii, United States
Area: 140 acres

Polihale State Park is a remote wild beach park on the western side of the Hawaiian island of Kauai. It is the most western publicly accessible area in Hawaii, although not the westernmost point. The park is remote and accessible only via a poorly marked, dirt sugar cane road, making a four-wheel drive vehicle preferable.

The park covers about 140 acres and includes a long stretch of white sand beach backed by the cliffs marking the start of the Napali Coast. Queen's Pond offers relatively calmer swimming when conditions permit; strong currents make much of the beach hazardous. Facilities include campsites, picnic areas and restrooms, with views of the forbidden island of Niihau on clear days.

Polihale means "house bosom" in Hawaiian, and in Native Hawaiian tradition the area is associated with the realm of the dead.
