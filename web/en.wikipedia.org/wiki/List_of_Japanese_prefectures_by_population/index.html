List of Japanese prefectures by population

Japan is divided into 47 prefectures. The following is a list of Japanese prefectures by population, based on the 2020 census (total population about 126 million). Tokyo is by far the most populous; the least populous prefecture, Tottori, has under 600,000 residents.

Rounding out the lower half are Yamaguchi, Ehime, Nagasaki, Nara, Aomori, Iwate, Shiga, Ōita, Yamagata, Miyazaki, Akita, Toyama, Saga, Kagawa, Yamanashi, Wakayama, Saga and Ishikawa, followed by Fukui, Tokushima, Kōchi and Shimane; Tottori (553,407) ranks 47th, the smallest.

Trends

Population is concentrating in the Greater Tokyo Area (Tokyo, Kanagawa, Saitama and Chiba — together over 37 million), while most rural prefectures have declined since the national population peaked in 2008.
