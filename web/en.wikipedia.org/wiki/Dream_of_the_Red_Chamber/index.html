Dream of the Red Chamber

Dream of the Red Chamber
Chinese: 紅樓夢 (Hónglóu Mèng)
Author: Cao Xueqin (first 80 chapters); Cheng Weiyuan & Gao E (last 40)
Published: 1791 (print)
Also known as: The Story of the Stone

Dream of the Red Chamber, also called The Story of the Stone, is a Chinese novel composed in the mid-18th century and generally recognised as the pinnacle of Chinese fiction. It is one of China's Four Great Classical Novels.

Plot

Frametold by a sentient stone, the novel chronicles the rise and fall of the wealthy Jia clan through the love triangle of Jia Baoyu with his cousins Lin Daiyu, frail and poetic, and Xue Baochai, sensible and capable, and portrays in detail the lives of the women of the household in the Grand View Garden.

Themes and study

Blending realism, romance, and Buddhist and Taoist reflections on illusion and desire, the work is semi-autobiographical of author Cao Xueqin's family decline. Its study, 'Redology', is a scholarly field unto itself, with the lost original ending a famous puzzle.
