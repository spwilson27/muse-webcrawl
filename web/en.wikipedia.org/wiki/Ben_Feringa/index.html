Bernard Lucas Feringa (born 18 May 1951), often called Ben Feringa, is a Dutch chemist specialising in molecular nanotechnology and homogeneous catalysis, and a Nobel Prize laureate.

Life and career

Feringa was born in Barger-Compascuum, Netherlands, studied chemistry at the University of Groningen, and became professor there in 1988.

Research

He is best known for work on molecular switches and motors. In 1999 his group reported the first light-driven molecular motor, a molecule that rotates unidirectionally when illuminated.

Honours

He shared the 2016 Nobel Prize in Chemistry with Jean-Pierre Sauvage and J. Fraser Stoddart for the design and synthesis of molecular machines.
