Science Based Targets initiative


Founded: 2015
Partners: CDP, UN Global Compact, WRI, WWF
Purpose: Corporate climate target validation

The Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) is a global body enabling businesses and financial institutions to set greenhouse gas emissions reduction targets in line with climate science. It was founded in 2015 as a collaboration between CDP, the United Nations Global Compact, the World Resources Institute (WRI), and the World Wide Fund for Nature (WWF), in the run-up to the Paris Agreement.

Function

The SBTi defines and promotes best practice in emissions reductions and net-zero targets, provides technical assistance and resources to companies, and independently validates targets against criteria aligned with limiting global warming to 1.5 °C above pre-industrial levels. In 2021 it launched the Corporate Net-Zero Standard, the first framework for corporate net-zero target setting in line with climate science. Thousands of companies worldwide have committed to or set validated science-based targets.

The initiative has occasionally faced scrutiny, including debate over its validation of targets relying on carbon offsets and environmental attribute certificates, and governance restructuring in 2023–2024 as it became a standalone organisation with a standard-setting arm.
