Chin Peng

Born: 21 October 1924, Sitiawan, Perak, British Malaya

Died: 16 September 2013 (aged 88), Bangkok, Thailand

Known for: Secretary-general of the Malayan Communist Party; leader of the Malayan National Liberation Army during the Malayan Emergency

Chin Peng (born Ong Boon Hua; 21 October 1924 – 16 September 2013) was a Malayan communist politician and guerrilla leader. As secretary-general of the Malayan Communist Party (MCP), he led the party's armed wing, the Malayan National Liberation Army, during the Malayan Emergency (1948–1960), the guerrilla war against British colonial rule and later the independent Federation of Malaya.

Early life and wartime resistance

Ong Boon Hua was born in Sitiawan, Perak, to a Chinese immigrant family. Drawn to anti-imperialist politics as a teenager, he joined the MCP in 1940. During the Japanese occupation of Malaya, he became a leading figure in the Malayan Peoples' Anti-Japanese Army (MPAJA), which cooperated with the British Force 136. He served as the MPAJA's liaison with the British and was later awarded the Burma Star and mentioned in connection with honours he declined after the war.

The Emergency

After the war, Chin Peng — his best-known alias — succeeded the assassinated party leader Lai Teck as secretary-general in 1947. Following the murder of three European planters in June 1948 and the British declaration of a state of emergency, the MCP retreated to the jungle and waged an insurgency. The campaign ultimately failed; the Emergency was formally ended in 1960, and Chin Peng withdrew to the Thai border area and later to China.

Later life

In 1989 the MCP signed the Hat Yai peace agreement with the governments of Malaysia and Thailand, formally ending the armed struggle. Chin Peng sought to return to Malaysia, but his applications were rejected by the courts, and he remained in exile in Thailand. His memoir, My Side of History, was published in 2003. He died in Bangkok in 2013, and controversy continued in Malaysia over whether his remains could be brought home.
