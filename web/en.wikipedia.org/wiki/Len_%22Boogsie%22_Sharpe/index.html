Lennox "Boogsie" Sharpe (born 28 October 1953) is a Trinidadian steelpan composer, arranger and player, widely regarded as one of the greatest arrangers in the history of the steelband movement. He is the founder and longtime musical director of Phase II Pan Groove of Port of Spain.

Career

Sharpe grew up in St. James, Port of Spain, immersed in panyard culture. He began arranging as a teenager and in 1972 co-founded Phase II Pan Groove, the first steelband to have its players perform exclusively original compositions at the Panorama competition. With Phase II he won numerous Panorama titles, beginning in 1987.

Compositions

Unlike most arrangers, who arrange calypsos written by others, Sharpe composes the band's Panorama tunes himself, including celebrated works such as "This Feeling Nice", "Woman on the Bass", "Pan Rising" and "Birthday Party". His style blends jazz harmony and classical influences with calypso rhythm.

Recognition

Sharpe has received national honors in Trinidad and Tobago, including the Hummingbird Medal, and an honorary doctorate from the University of the West Indies. He is frequently cited as a national cultural icon.
