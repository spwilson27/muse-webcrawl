List of countries and dependencies by area

This is a list of countries and dependencies by area, ranked by total area in square kilometres. The list includes all 193 member states of the United Nations, the two observer states (the Holy See and Palestine), and numerous dependent territories. Total area usually includes land area and inland waters (lakes, reservoirs, rivers), and sometimes coastal and territorial waters. The figures are generally taken from the United Nations Statistics Division and the CIA World Factbook.

The largest country in the world by total area is Russia, which spans eastern Europe and northern Asia and accounts for roughly one-eighth of the world's inhabited land area. The smallest widely recognised sovereign state is Vatican City, at less than half a square kilometre.
