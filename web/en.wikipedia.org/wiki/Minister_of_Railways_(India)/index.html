Formation1947

The Minister of Railways is the head of the Ministry of Railways in the Government of India and is responsible for rail transport in the country. The ministry oversees Indian Railways, one of the world's largest railway networks and employers, through the Railway Board.

History

The first railway minister of independent India was John Matthai (1947–1948). For decades the railway minister presented a separate Railway Budget in Parliament each year — a practice dating from the 1920s — until the railway budget was merged with the Union Budget in 2017. The portfolio has traditionally been politically significant, often allotted to leaders of coalition partners, and figures such as Lal Bahadur Shastri, Madhu Dandavate, Nitish Kumar, Lalu Prasad Yadav, Mamata Banerjee and Suresh Prabhu have held the post.

Responsibilities

The minister sets policy for railway operations, expansion, electrification, safety and modernisation, and supervises the public sector undertakings under the ministry, including container, construction and catering corporations.
