Koei Tecmo Holdings Co., Ltd. is a Japanese video game and amusement company, created in 2009 by the merger of Koei and Tecmo. The company is headquartered in Yokohama, Japan, and operates development divisions including Omega Force, Team Ninja, Gust, and Ruby Party.

History

Koei was founded in 1978 by Kō Shibusawa and was known for historical simulation games such as Nobunaga's Ambition and Romance of the Three Kingdoms, as well as the Dynasty Warriors action series developed by Omega Force. Tecmo, founded in 1967, was known for series such as Ninja Gaiden, Dead or Alive, Fatal Frame, and Monster Rancher. The two companies announced merger plans in 2008 and completed the combination on April 1, 2009, initially under the name Tecmo Koei Holdings; the name was changed to Koei Tecmo Holdings in 2014.

Divisions and franchises

Omega Force is best known for the "Warriors" (Musou) hack-and-slash franchise, including Dynasty Warriors and Samurai Warriors, as well as licensed collaborations such as Hyrule Warriors and Fire Emblem Warriors. Team Ninja develops the Dead or Alive and Ninja Gaiden series and created the Nioh action role-playing games. Gust is known for the Atelier series of alchemy-themed role-playing games. Ruby Party specializes in otome games, notably the Angelique series.
