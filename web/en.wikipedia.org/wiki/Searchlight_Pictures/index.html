Searchlight Pictures

The Searchlight Pictures production logo.

Searchlight Pictures (formerly Fox Searchlight Pictures) is an American film production and distribution company owned by Walt Disney Studios, part of the Walt Disney Company. Founded in 1994 as the speciality division of 20th Century Fox, it specialises in independent and arthouse films, and its releases have won numerous Academy Awards, including Best Picture winners Slumdog Millionaire, 12 Years a Slave, Birdman, The Shape of Water and Nomadland.

Formerly: Fox Searchlight Pictures (1994–2020)

Founded: 1994; Los Angeles, California

Parent: Walt Disney Studios (since 2019)

Products: Motion pictures

Notable films: The Full Monty, Black Swan, The Grand Budapest Hotel, The Favourite, Poor Things

History

Fox Searchlight was established in 1994 to give 20th Century Fox a presence in the speciality-film market dominated by Miramax. Early successes included The Full Monty (1997) and Boys Don't Cry (1999). Through the 2000s and 2010s it became the industry's most decorated specialty label, releasing films by Danny Boyle, Wes Anderson, Alexander Payne, Guillermo del Toro and Yorgos Lanthimos.

Disney era

Disney acquired the studio in its 2019 purchase of 21st Century Fox. In January 2020 "Fox" was dropped from the name, and the label continued producing and acquiring independent films, increasingly premiering some titles on streaming services such as Hulu. Its 2023 release Poor Things won four Academy Awards.
