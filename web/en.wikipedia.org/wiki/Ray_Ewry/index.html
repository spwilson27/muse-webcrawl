Ray Ewry

Raymond Clarence Ewry (October 14, 1873 – September 29, 1937) was an American track and field athlete who dominated the standing jump events at the early Olympic Games. He won eight Olympic gold medals in the standing high jump, standing long jump, and standing triple jump between 1900 and 1908 — a record for individual events that stood for a century — plus two further golds at the 1906 Intercalated Games.

Early life

Ewry contracted polio as a child and was told he might never walk without a wheelchair. He devised his own programme of leg exercises to rebuild his strength, and went on to study engineering at Purdue University, where his jumping exercises became competitive athletics.

Olympic career

At Paris 1900, St. Louis 1904, and London 1908, Ewry swept the standing jumps, setting world records including 1.675 m in the standing high jump and 3.48 m in the standing long jump. The standing events were dropped from the Olympics after 1912. His tally of eight individual Olympic gold medals was unmatched until Michael Phelps surpassed it in 2008.
