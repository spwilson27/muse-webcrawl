Nahnmwarki


Nahnmwarki
Type: Paramount chiefly title
Region: Madolenihmw, Pohnpei, Federated States of Micronesia

The Nahnmwarki is the paramount chief, or king, of Madolenihmw, one of the traditional chiefdoms (wehi) of the island of Pohnpei in the Federated States of Micronesia. The title heads the highest-ranking of Pohnpei's traditional polities and remains a position of significant ceremonial and cultural authority today.

Origins

According to Pohnpeian oral history, the Nahnmwarki system arose after the overthrow of the Saudeleur Dynasty — the rulers of the stone city of Nan Madol — by the culture hero Isokelekel and his companions. Isokelekel reorganized Pohnpei into autonomous chiefdoms under nahnmwarki (paramount chiefs) and nahnken (talking chiefs or orator chiefs), establishing a dual title system in which the nahnken serves as executive and spokesman for the sacred nahnmwarki.

Role and structure

The Nahnmwarki is regarded as sacred and is treated with elaborate ritual deference, including distinctive forms of speech (the "language of respect" in Pohnpeian) and ceremonial service. Below the nahnmwarki stands a hierarchy of title-holders organized in title lines, with land tenure, feasts (kamadipw), and first-fruits offerings (nohpwei) flowing through the system. The Nahnmwarki of Madolenihmw, whose line of paramount titles includes honorific names associated with Nan Madol, is traditionally considered the senior chief of Pohnpei.

Modern era

Despite colonial administrations (Spanish, German, Japanese, and American) and the modern FSM state government of Pohnpei, the Nahnmwarki system persists, and the title retains influence over land matters, custom, and ceremonial life.
