Disenchantment (TV series)

Disenchantment is an American animated fantasy sitcom created by Matt Groening for Netflix. The series is Groening's first production to appear exclusively on a streaming platform; he previously created The Simpsons and Futurama. Set in the medieval fantasy kingdom of Dreamland, it follows Bean, a rebellious alcoholic princess, her naive elf companion Elfo, and her personal demon Luci.

Premise

Princess Tiabeanie "Bean" of Dreamland rejects an arranged marriage and adventures with Elfo, an exiled elf, and Luci, a demon sent to corrupt her. Story arcs involve her mother Queen Dagmar, King Zøg, the neighbouring kingdom of Steamland, prophecies, and Bean's developing magical powers.

Production and release

Produced by The ULULU Company and Rough Draft Studios, the show premiered 17 August 2018. Fifty episodes were released in five parts of ten episodes, the final part arriving 1 September 2023.

Reception

Early reviews were mixed, praising the animation and voice cast but finding the humour uneven; later parts were received more warmly for their serialised storytelling.
