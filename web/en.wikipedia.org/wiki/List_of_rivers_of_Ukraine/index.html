List of rivers of Ukraine

Ukraine has more than 60,000 rivers and streams, most of which drain southward into the Black Sea and the Sea of Azov. The largest river system is that of the Dnieper (Dnipro), which crosses the country from north to south and divides it into Right-Bank and Left-Bank Ukraine.

Most western Ukrainian rivers belong to the Danube and Vistula basins, while the Prypiat and its tributaries drain Polesia in the north. Six large reservoirs on the Dnieper, built during the Soviet era, supply hydroelectric power and irrigation; the destruction of the Kakhovka Dam in June 2023 emptied the Kakhovka Reservoir and caused widespread flooding downstream.
