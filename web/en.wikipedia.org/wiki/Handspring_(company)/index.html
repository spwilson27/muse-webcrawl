Handspring, Inc. was an American electronics company founded in 1998 by Jeff Hawkins, Donna Dubinsky, and Ed Colligan, the founders of Palm, Inc. The company was best known for the Visor line of personal digital assistants and the Treo line of smartphones.

History

Handspring licensed the Palm OS and launched the Visor in 1999, competing directly with Palm's own devices. The Visor's distinguishing feature was the Springboard expansion slot, which accepted modules such as cameras, music players, and modems.

Treo

In 2002 Handspring introduced the Treo line, combining a mobile phone with a PDA. The Treo 600, released in 2003 with a built-in keyboard and camera, was a notable success. That year Palm acquired Handspring, and the Treo line continued under the Palm brand.
