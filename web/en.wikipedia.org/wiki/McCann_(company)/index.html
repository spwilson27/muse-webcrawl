McCann (company)

McCann
Formerly: McCann Erickson
Industry: Advertising, marketing
Founded: 1902 (as H. K. McCann Co.); 1912 (McCann Erickson merger lineage)
Headquarters: New York City, United States
Parent: Interpublic Group (McCann Worldgroup)

McCann, formerly McCann Erickson, is an American global advertising agency network, part of McCann Worldgroup within the Interpublic Group of Companies. It is one of the world's largest agency networks, with offices in well over one hundred countries.

History

The agency's roots lie in Harrison King McCann's H. K. McCann Company, founded in 1912 as one of the first agencies to open as an independent business rather than as a department of a publisher. In 1930 H. K. McCann merged with Alfred Erickson's agency to form McCann Erickson. Under Marion Harper Jr., who became president in 1948, the agency expanded aggressively abroad and pioneered the full-service, integrated holding structure that led to the formation of Interpublic in the early 1960s.

The agency's philosophy was summed up in its enduring motto, "Truth Well Told", adopted in 1912.

Notable work

McCann is known for campaigns including Coca-Cola's "It's the Real Thing", the enduring Christmas work for Coca-Cola, L'Oréal's "Because You're Worth It" (originated by Ilon Specht at McCann in 1971), and Mastercard's "Priceless" campaign, launched in 1997 and one of the longest-running platforms in advertising. In 2017 McCann New York's "Fearless Girl" statue, installed facing Wall Street's Charging Bull for State Street Global Advisors, became one of the most awarded works in Cannes Lions history.
