Mumbai Metropolitan Region

The Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR) is the metropolitan area centred on Mumbai in Maharashtra, India, encompassing Mumbai city and its satellite towns across parts of the Mumbai, Thane, Palghar and Raigad districts. With a population exceeding 20 million over an area of roughly 6,300 km², it is one of the largest urban agglomerations in the world.

The region is planned by the Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority (MMRDA), established in 1975. Its constituent municipal corporations include Greater Mumbai, Thane, Kalyan-Dombivli, Navi Mumbai, Ulhasnagar, Bhiwandi-Nizampur, Vasai-Virar and Mira-Bhayandar, among others. The region is connected by the Mumbai Suburban Railway, national highways, the Mumbai Metro, and the ports of Mumbai and Jawaharlal Nehru Port (Nhava Sheva).
