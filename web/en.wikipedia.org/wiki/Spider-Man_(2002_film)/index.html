Spider-Man is a 2002 American superhero film directed by Sam Raimi, based on the Marvel Comics character of the same name. It stars Tobey Maguire as Peter Parker, a shy high-school student who gains spider-like abilities after being bitten by a genetically engineered spider, alongside Kirsten Dunst as Mary Jane Watson, Willem Dafoe as Norman Osborn / Green Goblin, and James Franco as Harry Osborn.

Plot

After the spider bite gives him superhuman strength, agility and a "spider-sense", Peter Parker initially uses his powers for personal gain. When his Uncle Ben is murdered by a burglar Peter had allowed to escape, he learns that "with great power comes great responsibility" and becomes the hero Spider-Man, eventually confronting the Green Goblin.

Reception and legacy

The film was a major critical and commercial success, grossing over $820 million worldwide and becoming the first film to earn $100 million in a single weekend. It is credited, along with X-Men (2000), with launching the modern superhero film boom. It was followed by two sequels, Spider-Man 2 (2004) and Spider-Man 3 (2007), and Maguire later reprised the role in Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021).
