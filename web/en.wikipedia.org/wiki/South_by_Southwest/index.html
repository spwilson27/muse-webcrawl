South by Southwest (SXSW)
LocationAustin, Texas, United States
Founded1987
TimingMid-March, annually

South by Southwest (abbreviated SXSW) is an annual conglomeration of parallel film, interactive media, and music festivals and conferences that take place in mid-March in Austin, Texas, United States. Beginning in 1987 as a regional music festival, it has grown into one of the world's largest and most influential events for the creative and technology industries.

Components

The event comprises several overlapping tracks: the SXSW Conference, with keynotes and panels across technology, culture and business; SXSW Interactive, long a launchpad for technology companies (Twitter gained early prominence there in 2007, and Foursquare launched in 2009); the SXSW Film & TV Festival, a significant venue for independent film premieres; and the SXSW Music Festival, one of the largest of its kind, with thousands of acts performing in venues across the city. Comedy, gaming and education (SXSW EDU) have their own strands.

History and impact

SXSW was founded by staff of the Austin Chronicle and modelled partly on New York's New Music Seminar; its name plays on Hitchcock's North by Northwest. Attendance grew from about 700 registrants in 1987 to tens of thousands of official participants, plus large numbers of unofficial events. The festival delivers a major economic boost to Austin and has spawned international editions, including SXSW Sydney (from 2023) and SXSW London (from 2025). The 2020 edition was cancelled because of the COVID-19 pandemic, the first cancellation in the event's history, and editions were held online or in hybrid form before returning in person.
