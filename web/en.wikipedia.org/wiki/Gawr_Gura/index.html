Gawr Gura was an English-speaking virtual YouTuber (VTuber) affiliated with Hololive English, under the Japanese agency Cover Corporation. A member of the first English generation, "Myth", alongside Mori Calliope, Takanashi Kiara, Ninomae Ina'nis and Watson Amelia, she debuted on 13 September 2020 and rapidly became the most-subscribed VTuber on YouTube, surpassing Kizuna AI in 2021 and reaching over 4 million subscribers.

Persona and content

Gura was presented as a descendant of the Lost City of Atlantis who came to the surface world because it was "boring down there". Her design features shark motifs, including a shark-tooth hoodie and a tail, and her greeting "a" — the single letter spoken at the start of her debut stream — became an enduring meme among her fans, who are known as the Gawr Gura "Chumbuds" and collectively the shrimp fandom.

Her streams centred on gaming, karaoke singing and chatting. She released several original songs, most notably "Reflect" (2021), and performed at Hololive concerts. In 2023 she sang "Tokyo Wabi-Sabi Lullaby" and voiced a character appearance connected to the anime event circuit; she also famously performed at a Los Angeles Dodgers game hologram event in 2024 as part of Hololive's collaboration.

Graduation

On 15 April 2025, Cover Corporation announced that Gawr Gura would graduate (retire the character) on 1 May 2025, citing disagreements with management and company direction. Her final stream and graduation concert drew exceptionally large live audiences, and she remains one of the best-known VTubers of the medium's global expansion era.
