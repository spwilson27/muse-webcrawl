Bossier City, Louisiana

Bossier City is a city in Bossier Parish in the northwestern corner of Louisiana, United States. It lies on the east bank of the Red River, directly across from its larger twin city of Shreveport. The population was about 62,700 at the 2020 census. The city is part of the Shreveport–Bossier City metropolitan area.

History

The area was originally known as Bennett's Bluff and later as Cane's Landing, a cotton-shipping point on the Red River. The city was incorporated in 1907 and named for Pierre Bossier, a 19th-century Creole general and Louisiana politician. Growth followed the arrival of Barksdale Air Force Base in the 1930s, established on land east of the city.

Economy

Barksdale Air Force Base, headquarters of the Eighth Air Force and home to the B-52 Stratofortress fleet, is the area's largest employer. Since the 1990s, riverboat casinos on the Red River — including Margaritaville, Horseshoe, and Boomtown — and the Louisiana Boardwalk shopping district have made Bossier City a regional gambling and retail centre.

Geography and climate

The city covers about 44 square miles on flat terrain east of the Red River floodplain. It has a humid subtropical climate with hot, humid summers and mild winters, typical of the Ark-La-Tex region.
