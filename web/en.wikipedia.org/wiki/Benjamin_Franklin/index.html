Benjamin Franklin

Benjamin Franklin
Born17 January 1706, Boston
Died17 April 1790, Philadelphia
Known forPrinter, scientist (electricity), inventor, diplomat, Founding Father
[Image: Benjamin Franklin with long hair and spectacles, in an engraving style]

Benjamin Franklin was an American polymath: a leading writer, printer, political philosopher, politician, postmaster, scientist, inventor and diplomat. As a scientist he was a major figure of the Enlightenment for his discoveries concerning electricity (the lightning rod, and terminology like battery and charge).

Statesman

Franklin helped draft the Declaration of Independence, secured the French alliance that won the Revolutionary War (working in Paris alongside the era of the French Revolution), and at 81 was the Constitutional Convention's elder sage. His Autobiography and Poor Richard's Almanack made him America's first self-made celebrity.
