Palm Beach County, Florida

Palm Beach County is a county in the southeastern part of Florida, United States. Its county seat is West Palm Beach. With a population of about 1.49 million in the 2020 census, it is the third-most populous county in Florida and the 26th-most populous in the United States. It is the largest county in Florida by land area west of the Atlantic coastal ridge, extending west to Lake Okeechobee.

History

Palm Beach County was created in 1909 from the northern part of Dade County. It was named after its first settled community, Palm Beach. Henry Flagler's railroad and resort development in the 1890s spurred settlement. The western part of the county, around Lake Okeechobee, developed into a major agricultural region — the Glades communities of Belle Glade and Pahokee became centers of sugar cane and winter vegetable production, part of the Everglades Agricultural Area.

Geography

The county stretches from the Atlantic Ocean westward to the eastern shore of Lake Okeechobee. Its eastern strip contains the densely populated Gold Coast municipalities, including Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Boynton Beach, Lake Worth Beach, West Palm Beach, Palm Beach, Jupiter, and Palm Beach Gardens. Major protected areas include the Arthur R. Marshall Loxahatchee National Wildlife Refuge and parts of Everglades conservation areas.

Economy and transportation

Tourism, agriculture, construction, and technology are major sectors. Palm Beach International Airport serves the county, along with Interstate 95, Florida's Turnpike, Tri-Rail commuter rail, and Brightline passenger rail.
