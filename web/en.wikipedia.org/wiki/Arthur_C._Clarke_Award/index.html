Arthur C. Clarke Award

The Arthur C. Clarke Award is a British award given for the best science fiction novel first published in the United Kingdom during the previous year.

Arthur C. Clarke Award
CountryUnited Kingdom
First awarded1987
First winnerThe Handmaid's Tale by Margaret Atwood

History

The award was established with a grant from Arthur C. Clarke and first presented in 1987. The winner receives an engraved bookend and a cash prize whose amount, for many years, equalled the year (e.g. £2001 in 2001).

Winners

Notable winners include Margaret Atwood, Geoff Ryman, Pat Cadigan, China Miéville (three times), Ian R. MacLeod, M. John Harrison, Lauren Beukes, Ann Leckie (Ancillary Justice), Emily St. John Mandel? — note Mandel was shortlisted, and recent winners include Adrian Tchaikovsky and Ned Beauman (Venomous Lumpsucker, 2023) and Martin MacInnes (In Ascension, 2024).
