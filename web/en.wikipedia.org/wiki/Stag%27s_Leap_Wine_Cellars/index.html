Stag's Leap Wine Cellars is a winery in the Napa Valley of California, founded in 1970 by Warren Winiarski. It achieved international fame when its 1973 Cabernet Sauvignon won first place among red wines at the Judgment of Paris tasting in 1976, a landmark result for California wine.

History

Winiarski, a former University of Chicago lecturer, purchased the ranch in the Stags Leap District and produced his first vintage in 1972. The 1973 Cabernet, made by winemaker Mike Grgich, shocked the French judging panel in Paris.

Legacy

A bottle of the 1973 vintage is held by the Smithsonian Institution. The winery's estate vineyards, S.L.V. and Fay, remain benchmarks for Napa Cabernet Sauvignon.
