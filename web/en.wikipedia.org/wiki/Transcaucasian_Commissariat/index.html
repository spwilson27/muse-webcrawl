Transcaucasian Commissariat

The Transcaucasian Commissariat was the first government of an independent Transcaucasia, established in Tiflis on 28 November 1917 (15 November in the Old Style calendar), after the October Revolution in Petrograd. It replaced the Special Transcaucasian Committee (Ozakom) and was formed by the Georgian, Armenian, and Azerbaijani national councils, which refused to accept the authority of the Bolshevik Council of People's Commissars.

History

Chaired by the Georgian Menshevik Evgeni Gegechkori, the Commissariat functioned as a provisional government for the region covering the former Caucasus Viceroyalty. It faced the collapse of the Russian Caucasus Army in the war against the Ottoman Empire, and negotiated the Armistice of Erzincan with the Ottomans in December 1917. It convened the Transcaucasian Sejm in February 1918 as a regional legislature. Under Ottoman military pressure, the Sejm proclaimed the Transcaucasian Democratic Federative Republic on 22 April 1918, replacing the Commissariat; the federation itself dissolved within weeks into separate Georgian, Armenian, and Azerbaijani republics.
