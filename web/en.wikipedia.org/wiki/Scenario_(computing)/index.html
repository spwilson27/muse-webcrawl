Scenario (computing)

In computing, a scenario is a narrative describing how a system might be used, or how events might unfold, told from the perspective of its users or stakeholders. Scenarios are widely used in requirements engineering, human–computer interaction, and software testing to explore and communicate the behaviour of a system before or during its development.

Usage

In requirements engineering, a scenario describes a single specific instance of a use case: a concrete sequence of actions and interactions between actors and the system. Scenario-based design, developed by researchers such as John M. Carroll, uses stories about people and activities to guide the design of interactive systems, keeping attention on users' goals rather than on system functions.

Testing

In software testing, scenario testing uses plausible stories of system use to design test cases that exercise realistic combinations of features, which can reveal faults that more atomistic tests miss. In systems analysis and military planning, scenario planning explores alternative futures to test the robustness of designs and strategies.
