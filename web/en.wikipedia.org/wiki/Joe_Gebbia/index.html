Joseph "Joe" Gebbia Jr. (born August 21, 1981) is an American designer and Internet entrepreneur. He is a co-founder of the vacation rental company Airbnb, having founded it with Brian Chesky and Nathan Blecharczyk in 2008, and served as its chief product officer. A graduate of the Rhode Island School of Design, Gebbia brought a design-led approach to the company's development and became a prominent advocate of design thinking in technology businesses.

Career

Gebbia and Chesky, roommates in San Francisco, launched Airbnb after renting out air mattresses in their apartment during a 2007 design conference. The company grew into one of the world's largest accommodation platforms; Gebbia was a key public face of its design philosophy and served on its board. In 2022 he stepped back from his full-time role at Airbnb, remaining a board member and adviser, and co-founded Samara, a housing company developing modular backyard homes. He has been involved with Tesla as a board member since 2022 and has pursued interests in philanthropy, architecture, and art; in 2025 he took a role related to government design and digital services in the United States.

Philanthropy

Gebbia signed the Giving Pledge in 2016, committing a majority of his wealth to philanthropy, and has supported causes including refugee housing, education, and design.
