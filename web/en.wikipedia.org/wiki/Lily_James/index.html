Lily Chloe Ninette James (born 5 April 1989) is an English actress. She studied acting at the Guildhall School of Music and Drama in London and began her career in the British television series Just William (2010). Following a supporting role in the period drama series Downton Abbey (2012–2015), James had her film breakthrough playing the title role in the fantasy film Cinderella (2015).

She went on to star as Natasha Rostova in the television adaptation of War & Peace (2016), and in films including Baby Driver (2017), Darkest Hour (2017), The Guernsey Literary and Potato Peel Pie Society (2018), the musical Mamma Mia! Here We Go Again (2018), Yesterday (2019) and Rebecca (2020). Her portrayal of Pamela Anderson in the biographical miniseries Pam & Tommy (2022) earned nominations for a Primetime Emmy Award and a Golden Globe Award.

Early life

James was born in Esher, Surrey, the daughter of Ninette Mantle, an actress, and James "Jamie" Thomson, a musician. She attended ArtsEd in Tring and graduated from the Guildhall School of Music and Drama in 2010. She adopted her father's first name as her stage surname after his death in 2008.

Career

After early theatre work at the Young Vic and the Orange Tree Theatre, James was cast as Lady Rose MacClare in Downton Abbey, becoming a series regular. Kenneth Branagh's Cinderella made her an international star. She subsequently alternated between period drama and contemporary roles, appeared on stage in Romeo and Juliet at the Garrick Theatre, and starred in The Iron Claw (2023).

Personal life

James has been involved in charitable work including campaigns for girls' education. She lives in London.
