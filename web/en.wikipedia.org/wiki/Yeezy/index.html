Yeezy

TypeFashion and footwear brand
Created byKanye West (Ye)
PartnersNike (2009–2013), Adidas (2013–2022)

Yeezy is a fashion brand founded by the American artist and designer Kanye West, legally known as Ye. It is best known for its sneaker collaborations, first with Nike (the Air Yeezy, 2009) and then on a much larger scale with Adidas beginning in 2015.

Adidas partnership

The Adidas Yeezy line launched with the Yeezy Boost 750 in February 2015, followed by the hugely popular Yeezy Boost 350 and its V2 revision, as well as the 500, 700, and Foam Runner models and Yeezy Season clothing collections. Built on limited releases and resale-driven hype, the line became one of the most valuable sneaker franchises in the world, generating over a billion dollars in annual revenue at its peak.

Termination

In October 2022, Adidas terminated the partnership after a series of antisemitic remarks by West, writing off remaining inventory and later selling it off with portions of proceeds directed to anti-hate organisations. Yeezy also had a short-lived collaboration with Gap (Yeezy Gap, 2020–2022).
