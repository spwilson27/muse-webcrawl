Indoctrination

Indoctrination is the process of inculcating a person with ideas, attitudes, cognitive strategies or professional methodologies. Typically the term carries a pejorative connotation implying that the indoctrinated person is expected to accept the teachings without critical examination or questioning.

Meaning and debate

Philosophers and educators debate where teaching ends and indoctrination begins. Some define indoctrination by its method (suppressing critical inquiry), others by its content (doctrines held to be non-negotiable), and others by the intention or outcome — producing unshakeable belief. The distinction matters in discussions of religious education, political propaganda, military training and schooling.

Applications

The term is frequently applied to totalitarian regimes' education and propaganda systems, to cults, and — often as an accusation — to mainstream schooling and media. Related concepts include brainwashing, socialisation and propaganda, though these are not exact synonyms.
