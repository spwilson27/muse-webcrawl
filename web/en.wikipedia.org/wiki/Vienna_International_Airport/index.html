Vienna International Airport

Vienna International Airport (German: Flughafen Wien-Schwechat; IATA: VIE, ICAO: LOWW) is the international airport of Vienna, Austria, located in Schwechat, 18 km southeast of central Vienna. It is the hub of Austrian Airlines and a base for low-cost carriers including Ryanair/Lauda and Wizz Air.

History

Originally built as a military airfield in 1938, it became a civil airport after the Second World War under British occupation. Successive expansions added Terminal 2 (1960), Terminal 1A, and the Skylink terminal (opened 2012 as Terminal 3). It handled around 32 million passengers in 2019, a record, and recovered strongly after the pandemic.

Facilities

The airport has two runways and three terminals. The City Airport Train (CAT) links it to Wien Mitte in 16 minutes. A third runway has been debated for decades amid environmental litigation.
