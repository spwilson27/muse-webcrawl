Vila Belmiro

Estádio Urbano Caldeira, better known as Vila Belmiro, is a football stadium in the Vila Belmiro neighbourhood of Santos, São Paulo, Brazil. It has been the home of Santos FC since 1916 and currently holds roughly 16,000 spectators, making it one of Brazil's smaller top-flight grounds but one of its most storied.

History

Inaugurated on 12 October 1916, the stadium hosted Santos through the Pelé era, when the club was among the world's finest; Pelé's memorial and museum displays are kept there, and his funeral wake was held at the stadium in December 2022–January 2023. Its compact, steep stands create an intense atmosphere nicknamed the "Alçapão" (trap).

Future

Santos has long planned a new arena on the site; redevelopment proposals for a modern ~30,000-seat stadium have advanced in the 2020s.
