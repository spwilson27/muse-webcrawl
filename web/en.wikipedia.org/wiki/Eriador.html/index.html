Eriador

Eriador is a large region in the north-west of Middle-earth in the fictional works of J. R. R. Tolkien. Bounded by the Blue Mountains (Ered Luin) to the west, the Misty Mountains to the east, and the river Gwathló to the south, it contains the Shire, Bree, Rivendell, and the ruined kingdoms of Arnor.

History

In the Second Age, Eriador was largely forested and inhabited by Edain-related peoples; much of its woodland was felled by the Númenóreans for shipbuilding. In the Third Age, after the fall of Arnor, the region became sparsely populated wilderness, watched over by the Rangers of the North. Following the War of the Ring, Eriador was repopulated under the Reunited Kingdom of King Elessar.
