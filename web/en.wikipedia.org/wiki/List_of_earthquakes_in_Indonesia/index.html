List of earthquakes in Indonesia

Indonesia is in a very active seismic zone, also active volcanic zone: Pacific Ring of Fire and Alpide belt. This is a list of earthquakes in Indonesia.

Indonesia lies at the junction of several tectonic plates, producing frequent earthquakes and tsunamis.

Background

Indonesia lies on the Pacific Ring of Fire where several tectonic plates — the Indo-Australian Plate, the Pacific Plate, the Philippine Sea Plate and the Eurasian Plate — converge. The subduction of oceanic plates beneath the archipelago has produced some of the most powerful earthquakes ever recorded, including the 2004 Indian Ocean earthquake off Sumatra, which triggered a devastating tsunami that killed more than 227,000 people around the Indian Ocean rim.
