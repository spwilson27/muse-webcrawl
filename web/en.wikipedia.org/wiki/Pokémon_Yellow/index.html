Pokémon Yellow Version: Special Pikachu Edition, often known simply as Pokémon Yellow, is a role-playing video game developed by Game Freak and published by Nintendo for the Game Boy. It is an enhanced version of Pokémon Red and Blue and is part of the first generation of the Pokémon video game series. It was first released in Japan on 12 September 1998, and in Australia and North America in 1999 and Europe in 2000.

Gameplay

Like Red and Blue, Yellow is set in the fictional Kanto region and follows a young trainer on a quest to capture and train Pokémon and become the Pokémon League Champion. Reflecting the popularity of the Pokémon anime, the player begins the game not with a choice of three starter Pokémon but with a Pikachu, which follows the player character around on the overworld screen outside its Poké Ball — a first for the series. The Pikachu has a visible mood that changes in response to events, and the player can check its happiness. Elements from the anime were incorporated throughout: Jessie and James of Team Rocket appear as characters, some Pokémon locations and availability were altered, and all 151 Pokémon could be presented in enhanced colour palettes on the Game Boy Color, with updated sprites.

Development and release

Yellow was developed as a tie-in to the anime and to bridge the gap before Pokémon Gold and Silver. It supported the Game Boy Printer accessory, allowing players to print Pokédex entries, and included the surfing Pikachu mini-game, accessible under special conditions.

Reception and legacy

The game received positive reviews and was a major commercial success, selling over 14 million copies worldwide and ranking among the best-selling Game Boy games. Critics praised its colour presentation and anime touches while noting its similarity to Red and Blue. It was re-released on the Nintendo 3DS Virtual Console in 2016 for the series' 20th anniversary, and remade as Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee! for Nintendo Switch in 2018.
