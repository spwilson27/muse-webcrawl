The Superior Spider-Man is a superhero comic book series that was published by Marvel Comics from January 2013 to September 2014. It was written by Dan Slott, with art by Ryan Stegman, Humberto Ramos, and Giuseppe Camuncoli. The series replaced The Amazing Spider-Man, following the events of the "Dying Wish" storyline in which Doctor Octopus swapped minds with Peter Parker.

Premise

In the storyline, the dying Otto Octavius transfers his consciousness into the body of Peter Parker, leaving Parker's mind to die in Octavius's failing body. Influenced by Parker's memories and his own ego, Octavius vows to become a better — "superior" — Spider-Man and hero than Parker ever was. His methods, however, are more ruthless and calculating: he uses an army of small spider-bots to patrol New York, employs henchmen, and applies lethal or near-lethal force, straining his relationships with Parker's friends and fellow heroes.

Reception and legacy

The series was a strong seller and initially controversial among fans, but it was praised for its exploration of heroism and identity. It concluded with the return of Peter Parker's mind in time for the relaunch of The Amazing Spider-Man. The concept later returned in spin-off series and a 2022 revival, and Octavius's time as Spider-Man has been revisited in animated and other media.
