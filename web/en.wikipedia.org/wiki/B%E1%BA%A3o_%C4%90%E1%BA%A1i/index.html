Bảo Đại (born Nguyễn Phúc Vĩnh Thụy; 22 October 1913 – 30 July 1997) was the last emperor of Vietnam, the 13th ruler of the Nguyễn dynasty. Educated in France, he ascended the throne in 1926 and reigned under the French protectorate over Annam.

Abdication and return

In March 1945 he proclaimed an Empire of Vietnam under Japanese sponsorship; in August 1945 he abdicated in favour of Hồ Chí Minh's Democratic Republic of Vietnam, famously preferring to be a citizen of a free country rather than emperor of an enslaved one. The French brought him back in 1949 as chief of state of the anti-communist State of Vietnam.

Final years

He was deposed in a 1955 referendum by his prime minister Ngô Đình Diệm, who proclaimed the Republic of Vietnam. Bảo Đại spent the rest of his life in exile in France, dying in Paris in 1997.
