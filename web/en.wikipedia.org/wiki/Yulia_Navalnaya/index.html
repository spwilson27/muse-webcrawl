Yulia Navalnaya

Yulia Borisovna Navalnaya (Юлия Борисовна Навальная)
Born: 24 July 1976, Moscow, Russian SFSR, Soviet Union
Occupation: Economist, public figure
Spouse: Alexei Navalny (m. 2000; died 2024)

Yulia Borisovna Navalnaya (née Abrosimova; born 24 July 1976) is a Russian public figure and economist, the widow of Russian opposition leader Alexei Navalny. She has been described in the media as the "first lady" of the Russian opposition. After her husband's death in a Russian prison in February 2024, she announced that she would continue his work.

Background and role

Navalnaya graduated from the Plekhanov Russian University of Economics and worked in banking. She met Alexei Navalny in 1998 and married him in 2000; the couple had two children. She frequently appeared alongside him at trials, rallies, and in Anti-Corruption Foundation materials, and she played a key role in securing his medical evacuation to Berlin after his poisoning with a Novichok agent in August 2020.

Following Alexei Navalny's death on 16 February 2024, Navalnaya addressed the Munich Security Conference and vowed to hold the Russian leadership accountable. She became chair of the Anti-Corruption Foundation and of the board of the Human Rights Foundation in 2024, and has urged Russians to protest and support political prisoners. The European Parliament awarded her and, posthumously, her husband recognition; she received the Magnitsky Human Rights Award and other honors.
