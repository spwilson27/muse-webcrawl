Benjamin Madley

Benjamin Madley is an American historian specializing in Native American history and the history of genocide. He is a professor of history at the University of California, Los Angeles, where he also works with the American Indian Studies Center.

Scholarship

Madley earned his doctorate at Yale University. His best-known book, An American Genocide: The United States and the California Indian Catastrophe, 1846–1873 (Yale University Press, 2016), documents the mass killing of California Indians after U.S. annexation, arguing from state and federal records that the campaign constituted genocide supported by public funding and policy. The book received wide scholarly and public attention, including the Los Angeles Times Book Prize for history, and contributed to California's official apologies and truth-and-healing initiatives toward Native peoples.

Other work

Madley has written on the German colonization of South West Africa and has edited and contributed to studies of indigenous peoples and colonial violence in comparative perspective. He has also served as an expert and consultant in matters involving California Indian history.
