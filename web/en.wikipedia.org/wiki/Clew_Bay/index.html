Clew Bay

Clew Bay (Irish: Cuan Mó) is a natural ocean bay in County Mayo, Ireland. It is traditionally said to contain 365 islands — one for every day of the year — which are in fact partially submerged drumlins formed during the last ice age. The largest island, Clare Island, guards the entrance to the bay and was the stronghold of the 16th-century pirate queen Grace O'Malley (Gráinne Mhaol).

Geography

The towns of Westport and Newport lie at the eastern end of the bay. Overlooking the bay from the south is Croagh Patrick, Ireland's holy mountain, climbed by pilgrims each year on Reek Sunday. John Lennon once owned Dorinish Island in the bay, which he bought in 1967.

Economy and nature

The bay supports salmon and shellfish aquaculture and is designated a Special Area of Conservation for its marine habitats, including maerl beds and the drumlin islands that host breeding seabirds.
