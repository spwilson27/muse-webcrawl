Workflow

A workflow is a defined sequence of tasks, steps or processes through which a piece of work passes from initiation to completion, involving people, information and tools in a repeatable pattern.

Concepts

Workflows may be sequential, parallel, state-driven or rules-driven. Workflow management systems model, execute and monitor processes, routing tasks and documents between participants according to procedural rules — a field formalised by the Workflow Management Coalition from 1993.

Applications

Workflows structure business process management, scientific data pipelines, publishing, software CI/CD, and healthcare. Diagrams such as BPMN flowcharts visualise decision points, approvals and hand-offs, helping organisations automate routine work and audit compliance.
