St Martin, Jersey

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

St Martin (Jèrriais: Saint Martîn) is one of the twelve parishes of Jersey in the Channel Islands. Located in the north-east of the island, it had a population of about 3,950 at the 2021 census.

Geography

The parish includes the village of St Martin and the harbour and village of Gorey, overlooked by Mont Orgueil Castle, one of Jersey's principal historic sites, which guards the coast facing France. Rozel Bay lies on its northern coast. The parish is notable for its dolmens and for the megalithic sites at Faldouet.

Administration

Like all Jersey parishes, St Martin is administered by a Constable and Procureurs du Bien Public, with the parish church of St Martin at its centre; its honorary police are among the oldest such forces. The parish gives its name to electoral districts electing Deputies to the States Assembly.
