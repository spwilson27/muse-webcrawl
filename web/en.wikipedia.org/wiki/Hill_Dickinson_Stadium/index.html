Hill Dickinson Stadium

The Hill Dickinson Stadium is a football stadium at Bramley-Moore Dock in Vauxhall, Liverpool, England. It is the home ground of Premier League club Everton F.C., who moved there from Goodison Park at the start of the 2025–26 season. The stadium has a capacity of 52,769.

Development

Everton had explored leaving Goodison Park, their home since 1892, for decades before confirming the Bramley-Moore Dock site within the Liverpool Waters development in 2017. Planning permission was granted in 2021 despite the site's location within the then Liverpool Maritime Mercantile City World Heritage Site — a factor in UNESCO's decision to remove Liverpool from the World Heritage list that year. Construction by Laing O'Rourke began in August 2021, including infilling the historic dock, and the stadium was completed in late 2024 at a reported cost of around £750 million, substantially more than the original £500 million estimate.

Design and naming

Designed by architect Dan Meis with BDP Pattern, the stadium combines a brick, steel and glass façade reflecting the dock's maritime heritage with a steep single-tier south stand for some 13,000 fans. In June 2025 the Liverpool-based commercial law firm Hill Dickinson acquired the naming rights.

Events

The stadium hosted test events in early 2025, including an under-18s friendly, before Everton's senior team moved in for the 2025–26 season. It is a scheduled venue for UEFA Euro 2028 and has also hosted rugby league fixtures.
