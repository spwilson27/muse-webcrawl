The Flemish Parliament (Dutch: Vlaams Parlement) constitutes the legislative power in Flanders for matters falling within the competence of the Flemish Community and the Flemish Region. It has 124 members: 118 elected in the Flemish Region and six elected by Dutch-speaking voters in the Brussels-Capital Region.

History

The body originated in 1971 as the Cultural Council for the Dutch-Speaking Cultural Community. Following Belgium's state reforms it became the Flemish Council, and in 1996 formally the Flemish Parliament, moving into its own building in Brussels, a former post office on the Leuvenseweg.

Competences

The parliament legislates on education, culture, language use, healthcare policy, environment, transport and economic policy within Flanders, and elects and oversees the Flemish Government, headed by the Minister-President of Flanders.
