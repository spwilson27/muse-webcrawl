The National Broadcasting Company (NBC) is an American commercial broadcast television and radio network serving as the flagship property of the NBC Entertainment division of NBCUniversal, a subsidiary of Comcast. Founded in 1926 by the Radio Corporation of America, NBC is the first major broadcast network in the United States and one of the "Big Three" American television networks, alongside CBS and ABC. Its headquarters are at 30 Rockefeller Plaza in New York City, and it is sometimes referred to as the "Peacock Network" after its iconic peacock logo, created in 1956 to promote colour broadcasts.

NBC's headquarters at 30 Rockefeller Plaza in New York, home of Studio 8H.

History

Radio

NBC began as a radio network formed by RCA from stations including WEAF (New York) and WJZ (Newark/New York), launching on 15 November 1926. It soon operated two parallel networks, the "Red" and "Blue" networks. Under pressure from the Federal Communications Commission, RCA divested the Blue Network in 1943; it became the American Broadcasting Company (ABC). NBC Radio's classical music programming under Arturo Toscanini and the NBC Symphony Orchestra gave the network cultural prestige, and its owned stations pioneered network news.

Television

NBC began experimental television broadcasts in the 1930s and launched regular service with the opening of the 1939 New York World's Fair. Commercial television began in 1941. Under executive Sylvester "Pat" Weaver, NBC created landmark formats including Today (1952) and The Tonight Show (1954). NBC was the first network to broadcast in colour across its schedule, driven by parent RCA's colour-television business, and introduced the peacock in 1956.

Later twentieth century

General Electric acquired RCA in 1986 and thus NBC. In the 1980s and 1990s the network dominated with "Must See TV" Thursday nights — The Cosby Show, Cheers, Seinfeld, Frasier, Friends, and ER — under entertainment president Brandon Tartikoff and his successors. NBC acquired Telemundo in 2001 and the Bravo cable channel in 2002.

NBCUniversal era

In 2004 NBC merged with Vivendi Universal Entertainment to form NBCUniversal. Comcast acquired a controlling stake in 2011 and full ownership in 2013. In 2020 NBCUniversal launched the streaming service Peacock, which became a central outlet for NBC programming and sports. NBC has long held the American rights to the Olympic Games and broadcasts Sunday Night Football, the highest-rated US primetime programme of the 2010s.

Programming

Notable NBC programmes past and present include Saturday Night Live (broadcast from Studio 8H since 1975), The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon, Today, NBC Nightly News, Meet the Press (the longest-running programme in American television history), Law & Order and its spin-offs, The Office, 30 Rock, Parks and Recreation, This Is Us, and the Chicago franchise. The network operates through more than 200 affiliated stations reaching nearly all US television households.

Related divisions

NBC News produces network newscasts and operates with MSNBC and CNBC in the NBCUniversal News Group. NBC Sports covers the Olympics, NFL, Premier League, and golf. NBC Entertainment develops primetime, late-night, and scripted programming, while the owned television stations group operates NBC's owned-and-operated stations in major markets.
