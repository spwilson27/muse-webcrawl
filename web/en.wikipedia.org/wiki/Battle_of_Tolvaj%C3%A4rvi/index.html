Battle of Tolvajärvi
Date: 12 December 1939
Location: Near Ilomantsi, Finland
Part of: Winter War

The Battle of Tolvajärvi was fought on 12 December 1939 during the Winter War between Finland and the Soviet Union. Finnish forces under Colonel Paavo Talvela counterattacked the Soviet 139th Rifle Division around Lake Tolvajärvi in Ladoga Karelia.

Background

After the Soviet invasion of 30 November 1939, Soviet troops advanced toward the road junction at Tolvajärvi, threatening the flank of the Finnish IV Corps.

The battle

Talvela's group attacked across frozen lakes and through forest, enveloping the Soviet division and forcing its retreat with heavy losses.

Significance

It was Finland's first major offensive victory of the war and lifted national morale.
