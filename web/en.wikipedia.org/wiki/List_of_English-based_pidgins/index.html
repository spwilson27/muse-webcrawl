List of English-based pidgins

English-based pidgins are contact languages with English as their lexifier language. Many arose through trade, colonisation and plantation labour in the Atlantic and Pacific worlds. When a pidgin becomes the native language of a community it becomes a creole. This list covers both pidgins and English-based creole languages.
