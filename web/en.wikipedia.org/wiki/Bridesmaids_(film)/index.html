Bridesmaids is a 2011 American comedy film directed by Paul Feig, written by Annie Mumolo and Kristen Wiig, and produced by Judd Apatow. The plot centres on Annie (Wiig), who suffers a series of misfortunes after being asked to serve as maid of honour for her best friend Lillian (Maya Rudolph), and her rivalry with Lillian's wealthy, polished new friend Helen (Rose Byrne).

Reception and impact

The film was a critical and commercial success, grossing over $300 million worldwide against a $32.5 million budget. Melissa McCarthy was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress, and Wiig and Mumolo were nominated for Best Original Screenplay. Bridesmaids is frequently credited with demonstrating the box-office viability of female-led R-rated comedies and revitalising the careers of its ensemble cast.
