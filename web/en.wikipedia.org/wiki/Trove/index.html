Trove

Trove is an Australian online library database aggregator and digital library service hosted by the National Library of Australia. Launched in 2009, it provides a single search point across the catalogues of Australian libraries, museums, archives and other collecting organisations, and gives free access to digitised resources including books, journals, maps, music, photographs and — most notably — millions of pages of digitised Australian newspapers.

Newspapers

Trove's digitised newspaper collection, built largely through the Australian Newspapers Digitisation Program, allows full-text searching of historic titles from every state and territory, many dating back to the early 1800s. Members of the public can correct the optical character recognition text, and the service is heavily used by historians, genealogists and journalists.
