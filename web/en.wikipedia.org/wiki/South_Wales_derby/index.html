South Wales derby

The South Wales derby is a football rivalry between Cardiff City and Swansea City, the two largest clubs in Wales, whose cities lie about 40 miles apart along the M4 corridor. It is regarded as one of the fiercest derbies in British football.

History

The clubs first met competitively in 1912, shortly after Swansea Town (later City) was founded. Matches were often violent in the 1980s and 1990s, leading to away-fan bans and "bubble trip" restrictions requiring supporters to travel on official coaches. The rivalry intensified when both clubs reached the Premier League era: in the 2013–14 season they met in the top flight for the first time, each winning their home game.

Notable matches

Memorable meetings include Cardiff's 5–0 Welsh Cup win in 1995, Swansea's 3–2 victory in 2009 with a last-minute penalty, and the 2019–20 and 2021–22 seasons in which Swansea completed the first league "doubles" in the fixture's history.
