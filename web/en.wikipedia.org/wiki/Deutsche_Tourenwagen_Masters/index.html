Deutsche Tourenwagen Masters

The Deutsche Tourenwagen Masters (DTM) is a sports car racing series sanctioned by ADAC and based in Germany, with races held across Europe. Its predecessor was the original Deutsche Tourenwagen Meisterschaft, held from 1984 to 1996.

History

The series was revived in 2000 as the Deutsche Tourenwagen Masters, dominated for two decades by Audi, BMW and Mercedes-Benz. From 2021 the championship adopted GT3-based regulations (DTM Trophy support series aside), opening the field to a wider range of manufacturers.
