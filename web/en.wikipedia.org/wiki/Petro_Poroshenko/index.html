Petro Poroshenko

Petro Oleksiyovych Poroshenko (born 26 September 1965) is a Ukrainian businessman and politician who served as the fifth President of Ukraine from 2014 to 2019. A billionaire confectionery magnate, he founded the Roshen chocolate company, earning the nickname the "Chocolate King".

Presidency

Poroshenko was elected in May 2014, shortly after the Revolution of Dignity and Russia's annexation of Crimea, and led Ukraine during the war in Donbas, signing the Minsk agreements. His administration pursued association with the European Union, anti-corruption reforms, and military rebuilding, and secured autocephaly for the Orthodox Church of Ukraine. He lost the 2019 election to Volodymyr Zelenskyy.

Later career

Poroshenko leads the European Solidarity party in the Verkhovna Rada. After Russia's full-scale invasion in 2022 he remained a prominent, sometimes critical, opposition figure; he also faced treason charges brought in late 2021, which he described as politically motivated.
