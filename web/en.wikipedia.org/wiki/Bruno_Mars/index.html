Peter Gene Hernandez (born 8 October 1985), known professionally as Bruno Mars, is an American singer, songwriter and record producer. Known for his stage performances, retro showmanship and wide vocal range, he is one of the best-selling music artists of all time, with over 130 million records sold worldwide.

Life and career

Mars was born in Honolulu, Hawaii, into a musical family and performed from childhood as a young Elvis Presley impersonator. After moving to Los Angeles, he first found success as a songwriter with the production team the Smeezingtons, co-writing hits such as Flo Rida's "Right Round" and Travie McCoy's "Billionaire" (on which he also sang). His debut album, Doo-Wops & Hooligans (2010), produced the number-one singles "Just the Way You Are" and "Grenade".

His second album, Unorthodox Jukebox (2012), included "Locked Out of Heaven" and "When I Was Your Man". In 2014 he was featured on Mark Ronson's "Uptown Funk", one of the best-selling singles of all time. His third album, the funk- and R&B-driven 24K Magic (2016), swept the major categories at the 2018 Grammy Awards, winning Album of the Year. In 2021 he released An Evening with Silk Sonic, a collaboration with Anderson .Paak as the duo Silk Sonic, whose single "Leave the Door Open" also won major Grammys including Record and Song of the Year.

Artistry

Mars draws on Michael Jackson, Elvis Presley, James Brown and Prince, blending pop with funk, soul, R&B, reggae and rock, accompanied by tightly choreographed live shows with his band, the Hooligans.
