DoDAF

The Department of Defense Architecture Framework (DoDAF) is an architecture framework for the United States Department of Defense (DoD) that provides visualization infrastructure for specific stakeholders' concerns through viewpoints organized by various views. These views are artifacts for visualizing and understanding the often larger and more complex issues of a problem.

History

DoDAF evolved from the C4ISR Architecture Framework of the 1990s. Version 1.0 was published in 2003, version 1.5 in 2007, and DoDAF 2.0 in 2009, which shifted emphasis to a data-centric approach built around the DoDAF Meta Model (DM2).

Viewpoints

DoDAF 2.0 organizes its models into eight viewpoints: All Viewpoint (AV), Capability Viewpoint (CV), Data and Information Viewpoint (DIV), Operational Viewpoint (OV), Project Viewpoint (PV), Services Viewpoint (SvcV), Standards Viewpoint (StdV), and Systems Viewpoint (SV). Each viewpoint contains defined models, such as the OV-1 high-level operational concept graphic and the SV-1 systems interface description.
