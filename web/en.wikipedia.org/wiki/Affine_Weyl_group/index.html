Affine Weyl group

Affine Weyl group
Field: Mathematics (group theory, Lie theory)
Type: Coxeter group
Structure: Semidirect product of a Weyl group with a translation lattice

In mathematics, an affine Weyl group is the Weyl group of an affine root system, equivalently the group generated by reflections in the affine hyperplanes associated with a root system. It arises naturally in the theory of Lie algebras and affine Lie algebras.

Construction

The affine Weyl group can be written as a semidirect product of the ordinary (finite) Weyl group with the lattice of translations by the coroot lattice. It is a Coxeter group whose generators are the reflections in the walls of an alcove, and its elements tile the ambient Euclidean space by alcoves.

Applications

Affine Weyl groups appear in the representation theory of p-adic groups, in the geometry of affine Grassmannians, and in the combinatorics of crystals and Macdonald polynomials.
