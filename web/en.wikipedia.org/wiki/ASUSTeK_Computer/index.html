ASUSTeK Computer Inc. (stylised as ASUSTeK and doing business as ASUS) is a Taiwanese multinational computer and phone hardware and electronics manufacturer headquartered in Beitou District, Taipei, Taiwan. Its products include desktop computers, laptops, netbooks, mobile phones, networking equipment, monitors, Wi-Fi routers, projectors, motherboards, graphics cards, optical storage, multimedia products, peripherals, and wearables.

History

Asus was founded in Taipei in 1989 by T.H. Tung, Ted Hsu, Wayne Hsieh and M.T. Liao, all of whom had previously worked at Acer as hardware engineers. The company's name derives from Pegasus, the winged horse of Greek mythology, using the last four letters of the word to give the company a high position in alphabetical listings.

Products

Asus is one of the world's largest PC vendors and the world's largest manufacturer of motherboards. Its Republic of Gamers (ROG) brand targets gaming hardware, while the ZenFone and ROG Phone lines cover smartphones. The company gained wide attention in 2007–2008 with the Eee PC, which pioneered the netbook category.
