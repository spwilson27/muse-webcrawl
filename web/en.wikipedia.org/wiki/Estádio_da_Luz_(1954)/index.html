Estádio da Luz (1954)

The Estádio da Luz was a multi-purpose stadium in Lisbon, Portugal, used mostly for football matches. Opened on 1 December 1954, it was the home ground of S.L. Benfica for nearly five decades until its demolition in 2002, after which a new stadium of the same name was built nearby for UEFA Euro 2004.

History

Built largely with cement, reflecting Portugal's post-war economy, the original Luz was at its peak one of the largest stadiums in Europe, with an official capacity that reached around 120,000. Its vast third tier, completed in 1985, made it briefly the largest stadium in Europe by capacity.

Notable matches

The stadium hosted the 1962 European Cup Final, in which Benfica defeated Real Madrid 5–3, and the 1992 European Cup Winners' Cup Final. It was also one of the venues of the 1991 FIFA World Youth Championship, whose final drew a crowd of over 120,000.

Demolition

Benfica played its last match at the old stadium in 2002 before demolition; the new Estádio da Luz opened in October 2003 on an adjacent site.
