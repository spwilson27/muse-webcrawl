Jeff Cheeger

Born: December 1, 1943, Brooklyn, New York, U.S.
Fields: Mathematics
Institutions: Stony Brook University, New York University (Courant Institute)
Known for: Cheeger constant, Cheeger–Gromoll soul theorem, Cheeger–Müller theorem, Riemannian geometry

Jeff Cheeger (born December 1, 1943, in Brooklyn, New York) is an American mathematician known for his contributions to differential geometry, Riemannian geometry, and geometric analysis. His work has been influential in linking geometry with topology, analysis, and probability.

Education and career

Cheeger received his bachelor's degree from Harvard University in 1964 and his PhD from Princeton University in 1967, under the supervision of Salomon Bochner and James Simons. He taught at Stony Brook University and later joined the Courant Institute of New York University, where he is a Silver Professor of Mathematics.

Work

Cheeger introduced the Cheeger constant (also called the isoperimetric constant) of a Riemannian manifold, a quantity relating the volume of a hypersurface dividing the manifold to the volumes it separates; it gives bounds on the first eigenvalue of the Laplace operator. With Detlef Gromoll he proved the soul theorem, describing the structure of complete manifolds of nonnegative sectional curvature. With Mikhael Gromov he developed the theory of collapse of Riemannian manifolds with bounded curvature. The Cheeger–Müller theorem, proved independently by Cheeger and Werner Müller, established the equality of analytic and Reidemeister torsion. He also proved the Cheeger–Simons differential character theory and, with Bruce Kleiner, results on differentiability of Lipschitz functions on metric measure spaces, extending Rademacher's theorem.

Awards

Cheeger is a member of the National Academy of Sciences and a fellow of the American Academy of Arts and Sciences. He received the Oswald Veblen Prize in Geometry in 2001 and the Steele Prize for Lifetime Achievement in 2019. In 2021 he shared the Shaw Prize in Mathematical Sciences with Jean-Michel Bismut.
