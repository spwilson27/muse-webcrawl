Chronology of Jesus

The chronology of Jesus aims to establish a timeline for the life of Jesus of Nazareth from the Gospels and other historical evidence. There is no single agreed chronology, but scholars generally place his birth between 6 and 4 BC and his crucifixion in AD 30 or 33.

Birth

Matthew and Luke place the birth in the reign of Herod the Great, who died in 4 BC; Luke also links it to the census of Quirinius (AD 6), a discrepancy widely discussed. The traditional date of 25 December and the Anno Domini era devised by Dionysius Exiguus (AD 525) are not considered historically exact.

Ministry and crucifixion

Jesus's baptism by John the Baptist is often dated around AD 27–29, inferred from Luke's statement that John began preaching in the 15th year of Tiberius. His public ministry lasted one to three years in the different Gospel accounts. The crucifixion under Pontius Pilate (prefect AD 26–36) is usually dated to Friday 7 April AD 30 or Friday 3 April AD 33, dates matching the Passover timing in the Gospels.
