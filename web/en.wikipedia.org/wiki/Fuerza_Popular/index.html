Fuerza Popular (English: Popular Force) is a political party in Peru. Founded in 2010 as Fuerza 2011, it is the principal political vehicle of Fujimorism and is led by Keiko Fujimori, daughter of former president Alberto Fujimori.

History

The party was created to support Keiko Fujimori's presidential candidacy in the 2011 general election, in which she reached the runoff and lost narrowly to Ollanta Humala. She ran again in 2016, winning the first round and the largest bloc in Congress — an outright congressional majority — but lost the runoff to Pedro Pablo Kuczynski by a very small margin. From Congress, Fuerza Popular exercised enormous influence over Peruvian politics during a period of intense institutional conflict, contributing to the resignation of Kuczynski in 2018.

From 2018, Keiko Fujimori faced prosecution over allegations of money laundering and illegal campaign financing linked to the Odebrecht scandal, which she denies; she spent periods in pre-trial detention. She ran for president a third time in 2021, losing the runoff to Pedro Castillo by roughly 44,000 votes, and contested the result. In subsequent elections the party's congressional representation declined sharply from its 2016 peak, though it remains a significant force on the Peruvian right.
