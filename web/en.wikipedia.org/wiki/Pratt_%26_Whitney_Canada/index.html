Pratt & Whitney Canada (PWC) is a Canadian aircraft engine manufacturer and a subsidiary of the American engine maker Pratt & Whitney. Founded in Montreal in 1928 as a service centre for Pratt & Whitney engines, it became a design authority in its own right and is now the group's centre for small and medium aircraft engines.

Products

The company's most famous product is the PT6 turboprop, introduced in 1963 and one of the most popular turboprops ever built, powering aircraft such as the Beechcraft King Air and the de Havilland Canada Twin Otter. Other families include the PW100 regional turboprops, the PW300 and PW500 turbofans for business jets, and the PW800 series.

Operations

Headquartered in Longueuil, Quebec, PWC engines power aircraft in more than 200 countries, and the company supports a large global service network. It employs several thousand people, mainly in the Montreal area.
