Great American Ball Park

Great American Ball Park is a baseball stadium in Cincinnati, Ohio, United States. It is the ballpark of the Cincinnati Reds of Major League Baseball. It opened on March 31, 2003, replacing Cinergy Field (formerly Riverfront Stadium), the Reds' home field from 1970 to 2002. The stadium, named for the Great American Insurance Group, has a seating capacity of about 42,000.

Features

The ballpark stands on the Ohio River, and views from the stands include the river, the bridges to Kentucky, and the Cincinnati skyline. Two smokestacks beyond the centre-field wall, modelled on the riverboats that once plied the Ohio, emit steam and fireworks when Reds players hit home runs. The "Power Stacks" are a signature feature of the park.

Other features include "The Gap", a 35-foot break in the stands between home plate and third base that frames a view of downtown; the Cincinnati Reds Hall of Fame and Museum adjacent to the park; and a rose garden marking the spot where Pete Rose's record-breaking 4,192nd hit landed in the old stadium.

Events

The stadium hosted the 2015 Major League Baseball All-Star Game. It has also hosted concerts and other sporting events. See also Cincinnati and list of Major League Baseball stadiums.
