Act East policy

The Act East policy is a foreign-policy initiative of the Government of India, announced by Prime Minister Narendra Modi at the East Asia Summit in Myanmar in November 2014. It upgraded the earlier "Look East" policy (begun in the early 1990s under P. V. Narasimha Rao) from an economic orientation to a more active strategic, political, cultural and connectivity engagement with Southeast and East Asia.

Scope

The policy promotes trade and investment with ASEAN states, infrastructure connectivity through India's Northeast (including the Kaladan Multi-Modal Transit Transport Project and the India–Myanmar–Thailand Trilateral Highway), defence cooperation, and people-to-people and cultural links. Its geographic scope extends beyond ASEAN to Japan, South Korea, Australia and the Pacific island states, within the broader Indo-Pacific framework.
