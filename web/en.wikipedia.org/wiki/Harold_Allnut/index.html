Harold Allnut is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. A gifted mechanic and engineer with a hunchback and a form of mutism, Harold is best known as the behind-the-scenes technician of the Batman family: he builds and maintains the Batmobile, the Batsuit and the gadgets of the Batcave, though he rarely interacts with the wider world outside the cave.

Publication history

Harold first appeared in The Question #33 (1989), created by writer Denny O'Neil and artist Joe Quesada, as a henchman of the Penguin. He subsequently came under Batman's protection and was given a workshop and living quarters adjoining the Batcave, a status quo maintained through the 1990s storylines including Knightfall, during which his engineering supported Jean-Paul Valley's tenure as Batman.

Fictional biography

Harold is mute and communicates in writing, and he is a brilliant self-taught inventor able to repair nearly any device. He briefly ran afoul of the Bat-family when the villain Hush manipulated him, promising surgery to cure his conditions; Harold was killed in Batman: Gotham Knights during the Hush aftermath storyline (2004), dying in Batman's arms after revealing he had always known he was being used and had tried to warn Bruce Wayne. In later continuities, including the post-Rebirth era, versions of Harold have reappeared as part of the extended Batcave support cast.
