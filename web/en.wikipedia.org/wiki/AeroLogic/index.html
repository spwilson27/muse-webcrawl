AeroLogic GmbH is a German cargo airline based at Leipzig/Halle Airport. It is a 50/50 joint venture between DHL Express and Lufthansa Cargo, operating long-haul scheduled freighter services for its two parents with a fleet of Boeing 777F aircraft.

History

AeroLogic was founded in 2007 (initially under the working framework agreed by DHL and Lufthansa Cargo in 2004) and began flight operations in June 2009. The model was designed to share capacity: on weekdays the aircraft primarily fly for DHL's express network and at weekends for Lufthansa Cargo's general freight network. Leipzig/Halle, DHL's main European hub, serves as the home base.

Operations and fleet

The airline flies an all-Boeing 777F fleet — the type's long range and payload suit intercontinental routes between Europe, Asia and North America, with destinations including Frankfurt, Hong Kong, Singapore, Shanghai, Seoul, Chicago, Cincinnati and New York. The fleet has grown steadily from the first eight aircraft to more than twenty 777Fs, registered in Germany (D-AALx series) and crewed under German licences.
