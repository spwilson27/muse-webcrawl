Halloween Horror Nights is an annual Halloween-themed special event held at Universal Studios theme parks in Orlando, Hollywood, Japan, and Singapore. Widely regarded as one of the world's premier haunted attractions, it features elaborately themed haunted houses, outdoor "scare zones" staffed by costumed scareactors, and live shows, frequently built around major horror film and television franchises.

History

The event began at Universal Studios Florida in 1991 as a three-night event called "Fright Nights" before being renamed Halloween Horror Nights the following year. Over the decades it expanded into a weeks-long autumn season and an industry of its own, drawing on intellectual properties such as The Walking Dead, Stranger Things, Halloween, and The Texas Chain Saw Massacre, alongside original "icons" — recurring host characters from Orlando's mythology, including Jack the Clown and the Director.

Format

Each year the event offers a set of new haunted houses (typically around ten in Orlando), scare zones, and stage shows, with most regular park attractions also operating. Because of its intense content, it is marketed to teens and adults and is not recommended for children under 13. The event was cancelled in 2020 due to the COVID-19 pandemic — though Orlando staged a modified daytime offering — and returned in full in 2021.
