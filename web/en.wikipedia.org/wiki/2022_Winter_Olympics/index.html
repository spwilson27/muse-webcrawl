The 2022 Winter Olympics, officially the XXIV Olympic Winter Games, were held in Beijing, China, from 4 to 20 February 2022. Beijing became the first city to host both the Summer and Winter Olympic Games, having staged the 2008 Summer Olympics. The Games were held under strict COVID-19 countermeasures in a "closed loop" system that separated participants from the Chinese public, and were preceded by diplomatic boycotts by several Western governments over China's human rights record. Norway topped the medal table for the second consecutive Winter Games, winning a record 16 gold medals.

Host selection and venues

Beijing was elected in 2015, defeating Almaty, Kazakhstan, by 44 votes to 40 after several European bids withdrew. Venues were arranged in three clusters: Beijing itself (reusing 2008 venues such as the "Bird's Nest" stadium and converting the "Water Cube" into the "Ice Cube" for curling), Yanqing for alpine skiing and sliding sports, and Zhangjiakou for most snow events, linked by a new high-speed railway. Artificial snow was used extensively at the mountain venues.

The Games

About 2,900 athletes from 91 nations competed in 109 events, including seven new ones such as women's monobob, big air freestyle skiing and mixed team events. American-born freestyle skier Eileen Gu, competing for China, won two golds and a silver and became one of the Games' most prominent figures. China's team achieved its best-ever Winter Olympic result with nine golds. A major controversy arose when Russian figure skater Kamila Valieva, 15, was permitted to compete despite a positive test for the banned substance trimetazidine; the team event medals were left unawarded pending investigation, and the affair shadowed the closing days of the Games.

COVID-19 and boycotts

All participants lived within a sealed bubble with daily testing; foreign spectators were barred and domestic ticket sales cancelled. The United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia and others staged a diplomatic boycott — sending athletes but no officials — citing the treatment of Uyghurs in Xinjiang and other human-rights concerns. Russia competed as the "Russian Olympic Committee" under sanctions for state-sponsored doping.

Medal table

Norway finished first with 16 gold, 8 silver and 13 bronze medals, followed by Germany and China. The Games closed on 20 February 2022, days before Russia's invasion of Ukraine, which led to Russian and Belarusian athletes being excluded from most international sport.
