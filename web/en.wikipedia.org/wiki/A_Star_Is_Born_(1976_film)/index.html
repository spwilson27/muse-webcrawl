A Star Is Born is a 1976 American musical romantic drama film directed by Frank Pierson, starring Barbra Streisand as Esther Hoffman and Kris Kristofferson as John Norman Howard. It is the second remake of the 1937 film of the same name, relocating the story from Hollywood acting to the rock music world: a self-destructive rock star whose career is collapsing discovers and falls in love with a talented young singer whose stardom eclipses his own.

Plot

John Norman Howard, a rock idol beset by drugs and drink, hears Esther singing in a bar and champions her career. They marry, but as Esther's star rises his unreliability sabotages both his own comeback and her tour. Determined not to drag her down, he dies in a deliberate car crash, and Esther closes the film with a triumphant, grief-stricken concert performance.

Music and reception

The film's signature song, "Evergreen (Love Theme from A Star Is Born)", written by Streisand and Paul Williams, won the Academy Award for Best Original Song and reached number one on the charts; the soundtrack album was also a major hit. Although reviews were mixed, the film was a commercial success, grossing about $80 million in North America. The story was remade again in 2018 with Bradley Cooper and Lady Gaga.
