Timeline of volcanism on Earth

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This timeline of volcanism on Earth lists notable volcanic eruptions, from the largest prehistoric events recorded in the geological record to major eruptions of the historical era. Eruption sizes are commonly compared using the Volcanic Explosivity Index (VEI), a logarithmic scale based chiefly on the volume of erupted material.

Historical eruptions

The eruption of Mount Vesuvius in 79 AD destroyed Pompeii and Herculaneum. The 1815 eruption of Mount Tambora in Indonesia, the largest of the historical era, ejected over 150 km³ of material and caused the "Year Without a Summer" in 1816. Krakatoa's 1883 eruption was heard thousands of kilometres away and generated devastating tsunamis. In 1980 Mount St. Helens erupted laterally in the deadliest and most economically destructive volcanic event in United States history. The 1991 eruption of Mount Pinatubo measurably cooled global climate for several years, and Iceland's Eyjafjallajökull eruption in 2010 closed European airspace for days because of its ash plume.

Measurement

For prehistoric events, volumes are estimated from mapped deposits of ignimbrite and ash. Sulfur emissions recorded in polar ice cores also allow ancient eruptions, such as the 1257 Samalas eruption, to be identified and dated precisely.
