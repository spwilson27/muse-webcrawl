Ryuichi Sakamoto (坂本 龍一, January 17, 1952 – March 28, 2023) was a Japanese composer, pianist, and producer, widely regarded as one of the most influential musicians of his generation. As a member of Yellow Magic Orchestra (YMO) with Haruomi Hosono and Yukihiro Takahashi, he helped pioneer electronic music, synth-pop, and techno.

Career

Trained in classical composition at Tokyo University of the Arts, Sakamoto worked as a session musician and arranger in the 1970s before YMO's 1978 debut made the trio international stars. His solo work ranged from the experimental electronic album Thousand Knives (1978) and the landmark B-2 Unit (1980) to the accessible pop of Neo Geo (1987) and later introspective piano music.

As a film composer, Sakamoto won the Academy Award for Best Original Score for The Last Emperor (1987, with David Byrne and Cong Su), having also acted in the film. His scores include Merry Christmas, Mr. Lawrence (1983), in which he co-starred with David Bowie, The Sheltering Sky (1990), Little Buddha (1993), The Revenant (2015), and Call Me by Your Name (2017, contributing songs). His late solo albums async (2017) and 12 (2023) reflected on mortality after his cancer diagnoses.

Activism and death

Sakamoto was a prominent environmental and anti-nuclear activist, co-founding the more trees forest-conservation project and opposing nuclear power after the 2011 Fukushima disaster. Diagnosed with throat cancer in 2014 and later rectal cancer, he died in Tokyo on March 28, 2023, at age 71. His final concert film and recordings were released posthumously to wide acclaim.
