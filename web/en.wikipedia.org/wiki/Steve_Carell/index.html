Steve Carell

Steven John Carell (born August 16, 1962) is an American actor and comedian. He rose to prominence as a correspondent on The Daily Show with Jon Stewart (1999–2005) and became a television star playing Michael Scott in the American version of The Office (2005–2011, 2013), a role that won him a Golden Globe Award and made him one of the defining comic actors of his generation. He later earned acclaim for dramatic film work, including an Academy Award nomination for Foxcatcher (2014), and voiced Gru in the Despicable Me franchise.

Early life

Carell was born in Concord, Massachusetts, and raised in Acton, the youngest of four brothers. His father, Edwin, was an electrical engineer, and his mother, Harriet, a psychiatric nurse. He attended Denison University in Ohio, where he performed with an improvisational comedy troupe and worked as a student radio disc jockey. After moving to Chicago, he joined The Second City, where he performed alongside Stephen Colbert; in 1991 he appeared in a small role in Curly Sue and later briefly on The Dana Carvey Show (1996).

Career

As a correspondent on The Daily Show from 1999 to 2005, Carell built a national profile. Film breakthroughs came in quick succession: Bruce Almighty (2003), Anchorman (2004) as Brick Tamland, and The 40-Year-Old Virgin (2005), which he co-wrote with Judd Apatow and which established him as a film lead. Simultaneously, The Office became a cultural phenomenon; Carell left the series in 2011 after seven seasons. He balanced comedy (Little Miss Sunshine, Get Smart, Crazy, Stupid, Love, The Big Short, Battle of the Sexes) with voice work as Gru in Despicable Me (2010) and its sequels, among the highest-grossing animated films of all time.

Carell's transformative dramatic performance as John du Pont in Foxcatcher (2014) earned Academy Award and BAFTA nominations. He played Bobby Riggs in Battle of the Sexes (2017), Donald Rumsfeld in Vice (2018), and starred in Beautiful Boy (2018) and Welcome to Marwen (2018). On television he co-created and starred in Space Force (2020–2022) and received praise for The Morning Show (2019) and the limited series The Patient (2022). In 2024 he made his Broadway debut in Uncle Vanya.

Personal life

Carell married actress Nancy Walls, a fellow Second City performer and former Saturday Night Live cast member, in 1995; they have two children. He co-owns the Marshfield Hills General Store in Massachusetts and is known for a notably low-profile private life relative to his fame.
