The world football transfer record is the highest fee ever paid by one association football club to another for the transfer of a player's registration. The current record was set in August 2017, when Paris Saint-Germain activated the €222 million (£198 million) release clause in Neymar's contract with Barcelona, more than doubling the previous record.

History

The first fee of £1,000 was paid in 1893, when Aston Villa signed Willie Groves from West Bromwich Albion. The record passed £100,000 in 1928 (David Jack, Bolton to Arsenal), £1 million in 1979 (Trevor Francis, Birmingham City to Nottingham Forest), and £10 million in 1992 (Jean-Pierre Papin, Marseille to Milan). The record climbed steeply after the Bosman ruling: Ronaldo, Zinedine Zidane, Kaká, Cristiano Ronaldo, Gareth Bale and Paul Pogba successively held it before Neymar's move.

Record progression highlights

Notable steps include Denílson's £21.5 million move to Real Betis in 1998, Hernán Crespo and Luís Figo in 2000, Zidane's €77.5 million in 2001, Cristiano Ronaldo's £80 million in 2009, Bale's €100.8 million in 2013 and Pogba's €105 million in 2016.
