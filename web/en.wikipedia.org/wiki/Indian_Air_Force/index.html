Indian Air Force

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Indian Air Force is among the largest air arms in the world.

The Indian Air Force (IAF) is the air arm of the Indian Armed Forces. Its primary mission is to secure Indian airspace and to conduct aerial warfare during armed conflict. It was officially established on 8 October 1932, which is marked annually as Air Force Day, and became the "Royal Indian Air Force" in 1945 before the prefix was dropped in 1950.

History

The IAF flew in the Second World War, chiefly in Burma against Japan. Since independence it has fought in the wars of 1947–48, 1965, 1971, and Kargil (1999, Operation Safed Sagar), and conducted the 2019 Balakot air strike. It also performs humanitarian airlift and disaster relief, from the 2004 tsunami to flood evacuations.

Organisation and fleet

The force is organised into five operational commands (Western, Eastern, Central, Southern, South Western) plus Training and Maintenance Commands. Its combat fleet includes the Dassault Rafale, Sukhoi Su-30MKI, HAL Tejas, Mirage 2000, and MiG-29, supported by C-17 and C-130J transports, Apache and Chinook helicopters, and indigenous systems such as the Akash missile and Netra AEW&C. The President of India is Supreme Commander.
