Joséphine Bonaparte (born Marie Josèphe Rose Tascher de La Pagerie; 1763–1814) was the first wife of Napoleon Bonaparte and the first Empress of the French. Born in Martinique, she married Alexandre de Beauharnais, who was guillotined during the Reign of Terror; she herself was imprisoned and narrowly escaped execution.

Empress

She married Napoleon in 1796 and was crowned empress alongside him in 1804. Unable to provide an heir, she consented to a divorce in 1810, after which Napoleon married Marie Louise of Austria. Joséphine retired to the Château de Malmaison, famous for its gardens and rose collection, where she died in 1814. Her children by her first marriage, Eugène and Hortense, founded lines that entered several European royal houses.
