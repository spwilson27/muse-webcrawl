Alexander Johan Hjalmar Skarsgård (born 25 August 1976) is a Swedish actor. He first gained international attention as the vampire Eric Northman in the HBO series True Blood (2008–2014), and won an Emmy Award and a Golden Globe for his performance as the abusive husband Perry Wright in Big Little Lies (2017–2019).

Early life

Skarsgård was born in Stockholm, the eldest son of actor Stellan Skarsgård. He acted as a child — starring in Åke and His World (1984) — but disliked the attention and quit acting for several years. He served as a teenager in the Swedish Navy's anti-terrorist unit, later studying in England and New York before returning to acting in Sweden.

Career

After Swedish film roles and a small part in Zoolander (2001), his breakthrough came with the HBO miniseries Generation Kill (2008), followed by True Blood. His film roles include Melancholia (2011), The Legend of Tarzan (2016), in which he played the title role, and Robert Eggers's Viking revenge film The Northman (2022), which he also produced. He played the tech billionaire Lukas Matsson in the final seasons of Succession and the title role in the series Murderbot (2025).
