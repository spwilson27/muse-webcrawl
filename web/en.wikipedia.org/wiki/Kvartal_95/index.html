Kvartal 95

Kvartal 95 is a Ukrainian production company founded in 1997, and also the name of the comedy troupe from which it grew. Based in Kryvyi Rih and later Kyiv, the troupe takes its name from Kvartal 95, a neighbourhood of Kryvyi Rih. It became one of Ukraine's most popular comedy acts before entering television and film production.

Kvartal 95 Founded: 1997
Headquarters: Kyiv, Ukraine
Industry: Entertainment, television
Key people: Volodymyr Zelenskyy (co-founder)

History

The troupe was formed by Volodymyr Zelenskyy and others from Kryvyi Rih to compete in KVN, the popular post-Soviet comedy competition. After success in KVN's major league, the team launched its own television show, Evening Kvartal (Vechirniy Kvartal), in 2005, which became a prime-time satirical revue lampooning Ukrainian politics.

Productions

The studio produced numerous comedy series and films, most notably Servant of the People (2015–2019), in which Zelenskyy played a schoolteacher who unexpectedly becomes President of Ukraine. The show's popularity helped propel Zelenskyy's real-life political campaign; he was elected President of Ukraine in 2019.

After 2019

Following Zelenskyy's election, several studio figures entered government service, and the company continued producing entertainment programming, including during the Russian invasion of Ukraine that began in 2022.
