Megalomania

Megalomania is a mental state characterised by delusions of grandeur, power, wealth or omnipotence, and an inflated sense of self-importance. The term derives from the Greek megas (great) and mania (madness).

Clinical context

Grandiose delusions occur in several conditions, including the manic phase of bipolar disorder, schizophrenia and narcissistic personality disorder, where grandiosity is a recognised feature. In modern diagnostic manuals megalomania is not a standalone diagnosis but rather a symptom described as grandiose delusion or delusions of grandeur.

Popular usage

Outside psychiatry, the word is used loosely for rulers, executives and others seen as obsessed with power or monumental self-aggrandisement. Historically, psychiatrists such as Emil Kraepelin discussed megalomaniac delusions in the context of paranoia and general paralysis of the insane.
