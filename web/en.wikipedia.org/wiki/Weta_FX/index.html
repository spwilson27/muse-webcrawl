Wētā FX

Wētā FX, formerly known as Weta Digital, is a New Zealand digital visual effects and animation company based in Wellington. Founded in 1993 by Peter Jackson, Richard Taylor, and Jamie Selkirk to produce the effects for Heavenly Creatures, it achieved worldwide prominence with The Lord of the Rings trilogy and has won multiple Academy Awards for visual effects.

History and work

The company developed groundbreaking techniques for The Lord of the Rings, including the MASSIVE crowd-simulation software and the digital character Gollum. Later landmarks include King Kong (2005), Avatar (2009) and Avatar: The Way of Water (2022), the Planet of the Apes reboot series, Avengers: Infinity War and Endgame, The Jungle Book, and Dune. Weta Digital was renamed Wētā FX in 2021 after selling its technology division to Unity Software; in 2023, Unity sold the business back to a group including Peter Jackson.

Awards

Wētā FX has won Academy Awards for Best Visual Effects for The Fellowship of the Ring, The Two Towers, The Return of the King, King Kong, Avatar, The Jungle Book, and Avatar: The Way of Water, among numerous BAFTA and VES awards.
