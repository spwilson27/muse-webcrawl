Moi dix Mois

Moi dix Mois ("Me ten months" in French) is a Japanese visual kei gothic metal band and solo project of guitarist and composer Mana, formerly of the influential visual kei band Malice Mizer. Founded in 2002, the project combines classical music, harpsichord and pipe organ textures with heavy metal, performed under a dark, elegant aesthetic drawn from European gothic and aristocratic imagery. Mana releases the music through his own label, Midi:Nette.

History

After Malice Mizer went on indefinite hiatus in 2001 following the death of drummer Kami and the departure of vocalist Gackt and later Klaha, Mana announced Moi dix Mois on 19 March 2002 — his birthday, and a date laden with the project's numerology of "10" (dix). The debut album Dix Infernal appeared in 2003, followed by the European release and tours that made the project one of the most visible acts of the 2000s visual kei wave abroad.

Subsequent albums include Nocturnal Opera (2004), Beyond the Gate (2006), Dixanadu (2007), D+Sect (2010) and the compilation Reprise (2012), which re-recorded material including a song originally written for Malice Mizer. Vocalists have included Juka (2002–2005) and Seth (from 2006); the supporting line-up performs under stage names such as Sugiya (bass), Hayato (drums) and Ryux (guitar). Mana himself plays guitar and keyboards and composes all music and lyrics, never singing and rarely speaking on stage, where an announcer or the vocalist addresses the audience.

Style and image

Moi dix Mois' sound fuses twin-guitar metal with harpsichord arpeggios, church organ and operatic or harsh vocals, with texts in Japanese, Latin phrases and French titles. Mana's fashion label Moi-même-Moitié, founded in 1999, supplies the project's wardrobe and helped popularise the "Elegant Gothic Lolita" and "Elegant Gothic Aristocrat" styles internationally.
