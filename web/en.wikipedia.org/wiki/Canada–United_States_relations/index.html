Canada–United States relations

Canada–United States relations encompass trade, defence, environmental, cultural and diplomatic ties between Canada and the United States, two countries sharing the world's longest international land border at 8,891 kilometres.

Border length: 8,891 km
Key agreements: USMCA, NORAD, Boundary Waters Treaty

History

Relations evolved from conflict during the American Revolution and War of 1812 to close cooperation in the twentieth century, including both world wars and the Cold War alliance through NATO and NORAD.

Trade

The two countries are among each other's largest trading partners. The Canada–United States–Mexico Agreement (CUSMA/USMCA), in force since 2020, succeeded NAFTA as the main free-trade framework.

Defence

NORAD, established in 1958, jointly provides aerospace warning and control for North America. Canada and the United States also cooperate extensively on border security.
