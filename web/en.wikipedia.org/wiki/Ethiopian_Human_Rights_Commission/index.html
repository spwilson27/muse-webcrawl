The Ethiopian Human Rights Commission (EHRC; Amharic: የኢትዮጵያ ሰብዓዊ መብቶች ኮሚሽን) is Ethiopia's national human rights institution, established by law to promote and protect human rights under the Ethiopian constitution and the international treaties Ethiopia has ratified. It investigates complaints, monitors places of detention, conducts inquiries into violations, and reports to the House of Peoples' Representatives. Following reforms in 2020 its mandate and independence were strengthened, and it gained accreditation with the Global Alliance of National Human Rights Institutions.

History

The Commission was created by Proclamation No. 210/2000 under Article 55(14) of the 1995 constitution and began operating in 2004. For much of its early history it was criticised by civil-society groups as weak and reluctant to confront the government. The political opening after 2018 changed its role: lawyer and former prisoner of conscience Daniel Bekele became Chief Commissioner in 2019, and Proclamation No. 1224/2020 amended its establishing law to broaden its powers, including unannounced visits to detention facilities and public reporting.

Work and investigations

During the Tigray War the EHRC investigated massacres and abuses, including a joint investigation with the UN Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights published in November 2021, which found that all parties had committed violations, some potentially amounting to war crimes and crimes against humanity. The Commission has also reported on massacres in Oromia and Amhara regions, on conditions of detention, and on abuses during states of emergency, sometimes disputing official accounts. Its findings have been alternately welcomed and contested by the federal government and regional actors.

Structure

The EHRC is led by a Chief Commissioner appointed by the House of Peoples' Representatives, with commissioners responsible for thematic areas such as the rights of women, children, persons with disabilities, and civil and political rights. It maintains branch offices across Ethiopia's regions.
