Ra's al Ghul is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, commonly as an adversary of the superhero Batman. Created by editor Julius Schwartz, writer Dennis O'Neil and artist Neal Adams, he first appeared in Batman #232 (June 1971). His name, from Arabic, translates as "Head of the Ghoul" or "Head of the Demon".

Ra's al Ghul is the leader of the League of Assassins and the father of Talia al Ghul and Nyssa Raatko, and the maternal grandfather of Damian Wayne. He is an international, centuries-old eco-terrorist who believes that humanity is a blight on the Earth and seeks to restore balance to the environment by drastically reducing the human population. He sustains his life through the restorative Lazarus Pits, which grant him near-immortality.

Fictional character biography

Born centuries ago (his exact age and origin vary by telling) to a tribe in the Arabian desert, Ra's discovered a Lazarus Pit and used it to prolong his life. Over the centuries he amassed wealth, knowledge and a global organisation dedicated to his vision of environmental balance achieved through the destruction of much of mankind. Recognising Batman as a worthy successor, Ra's repeatedly sought to recruit him as his heir, ideally through marriage to Talia, while clashing with him when Batman refused to embrace murder.

In other media

The character has appeared in Batman: The Animated Series (voiced by David Warner), as the principal antagonist portrayed by Liam Neeson in Christopher Nolan's Batman Begins (2005), and in the television series Arrow, played by Matt Nable, as well as numerous animated films and video games including the Batman: Arkham series.
