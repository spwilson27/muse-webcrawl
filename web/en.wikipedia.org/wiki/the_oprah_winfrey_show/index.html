The Oprah Winfrey Show, often referred to as The Oprah Show or simply Oprah, is an American daytime syndicated talk show that aired nationally for 25 seasons from September 8, 1986, to May 25, 2011, in Chicago, Illinois. Produced and hosted by Oprah Winfrey, it remains the highest-rated daytime talk show in American television history.

History

The show originated from Winfrey's role as host of the low-rated half-hour morning talk show AM Chicago, which she took over in 1984 and quickly brought to first place in local ratings. The programme was relaunched nationally in 1986 by King World Productions. It was produced by Winfrey's company, Harpo Productions, and taped at Harpo Studios in Chicago's Near West Side.

Format and influence

The show mixed celebrity interviews, self-improvement segments, book discussions and philanthropic initiatives. Its Oprah's Book Club segment repeatedly propelled selected titles to bestseller status — a phenomenon known as the "Oprah effect". Notable giveaways and interviews, including the 1993 Michael Jackson interview and the 2004 car giveaway, became cultural events. Winfrey ended the show in 2011 to launch the Oprah Winfrey Network (OWN). See also Oprah Winfrey.
