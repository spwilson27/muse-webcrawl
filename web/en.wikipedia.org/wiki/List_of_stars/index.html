List of stars

This is a list of notable individual stars — stars with their own Wikipedia articles — including the Sun, the brightest stars in the night sky, the nearest stars, and stars notable for size, mass, variability or hosting exoplanets.


Stars vary enormously in mass, size, colour and luminosity.

Other notable stars

  • Polaris — the current north pole star.
  • UY Scuti and VY Canis Majoris — red supergiants/hypergiants among the largest known stars.
  • Eta Carinae — a massive luminous blue variable system.
  • TRAPPIST-1 — ultra-cool dwarf with seven Earth-sized planets.
  • Tabby's Star (KIC 8462852) — noted for unusual dimming.
  • 51 Pegasi — first Sun-like star found to host an exoplanet.
  • R136a1 — among the most massive and luminous stars known.
