String (structure)

In structural engineering, a string (or stringer) is a longitudinal supporting member running between trusses, beams or girders. Stringers carry loads from decking, planks or panels and transfer them to the transverse supporting structure.

Applications

In bridges, stringers are the longitudinal beams that support the deck and rest on transverse floor beams, which in turn are carried by the main trusses or girders. In aircraft fuselages, stringers are the longitudinal stiffeners riveted to the skin, working with frames to resist bending and buckling. In stair construction, the inclined supporting members are called stringers (or strings), into which the treads and risers are set. Timber pallets likewise use stringers as the longitudinal boards between deck boards.
