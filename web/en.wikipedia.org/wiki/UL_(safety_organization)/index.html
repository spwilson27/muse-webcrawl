UL (safety organization)

UL LLC, formerly Underwriters Laboratories, is a global safety science company and product certification organization headquartered in Northbrook, Illinois, United States. Founded in 1894 by electrical engineer William Henry Merrill, it grew out of work performed for insurers at the World's Columbian Exposition in Chicago, where Merrill was hired to assess fire risks in the fair's electrical installations.

History

Merrill established the Underwriters' Electrical Bureau in 1894 under the National Board of Fire Underwriters, testing electrical devices for safety. The bureau became Underwriters Laboratories in 1901. Over the twentieth century UL expanded from electrical testing into fire protection, hazardous materials, water quality, and many other fields, and its UL Mark became a widely recognized sign that a product had been tested against published safety standards.

Activities

UL develops standards through UL Standards & Engagement, tests and certifies products, and provides advisory, auditing, and software services. Its certification marks appear on billions of products worldwide. In 2012 the organization restructured, with Underwriters Laboratories becoming a nonprofit standards body and UL LLC operating as a for-profit subsidiary; the group rebranded as UL Solutions and became a publicly traded company in 2024.
