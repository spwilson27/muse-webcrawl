Newfoundland Power

Newfoundland Power Inc. is the primary distributor of electricity on the island portion of Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada. Headquartered in St. John's, it serves approximately 270,000 customers — about 87 per cent of all electricity consumers in the province — across roughly 155,000 square kilometres.

Role

Newfoundland Power is an investor-owned utility regulated by the Newfoundland and Labrador Board of Commissioners of Public Utilities. It purchases most of its power from the provincial Crown corporation Newfoundland and Labrador Hydro and operates its own network of small hydroelectric generating stations, along with the distribution lines, substations and customer services in its service area. The other main distributor, serving Labrador and parts of the island, is Newfoundland and Labrador Hydro itself.

Ownership and history

The company traces its origins to the St. John's street railway and lighting enterprises of the late 19th century; it was incorporated in 1924 as the Newfoundland Light & Power Co. Since 1987 it has been a wholly owned subsidiary of Fortis Inc., the St. John's-based utility holding company — the first Fortis acquisition and the foundation of what became one of North America's largest investor-owned utility groups.
