Nikka Whisky Distilling

The Nikka Whisky Distilling Co. Ltd. (ニッカウヰスキー株式会社) is a Japanese producer of whisky and other beverages headquartered in Tokyo. It was founded in 1934 by Masataka Taketsuru, widely regarded as the father of Japanese whisky, and is now a subsidiary of Asahi Group Holdings.

History

Taketsuru, who had studied whisky-making in Scotland and helped build Japan's first whisky distillery at Yamazaki for Suntory, left to found his own company, originally Dai Nippon Kaju, in Yoichi, Hokkaido — a location chosen for its resemblance to Scotland's climate. Its first whisky was released in 1940.

Distilleries and products

Nikka operates the Yoichi Distillery (single malts, coal-fired pot stills) and the Miyagikyo Distillery in Sendai (established 1969, lighter steam-heated malts), plus the Coffey stills at Miyagikyo producing grain whisky. Well-known bottlings include Nikka From The Barrel, Coffey Grain, Taketsuru Pure Malt and Yoichi and Miyagikyo single malts.
