Attack Attack! (American band)

Attack Attack! is an American metalcore band from Columbus, Ohio, formed in 2007. The band's fusion of metalcore with electronic dance music and Auto-Tuned clean vocals made them the most prominent — and polarising — exponents of the "crabcore" style, named after the deep crouching stance the members adopted in the video for their song "Stick Stickly".

History

The band formed in Westerville, Ohio, from members of local acts and signed to Rise Records in 2008 after posting demos on MySpace. Their debut album, Someday Came Suddenly (2008), combined breakdown-driven metalcore with trance synthesisers and featured the single "Stick Stickly", whose video went viral in 2009 and spawned the mocking "crabcore" label — which the band partly embraced.

Personnel churn was continual: original unclean vocalist Austin Carlile was dismissed in 2008 and went on to front Of Mice & Men; keyboardist and clean vocalist Caleb Shomo took over lead vocals for the self-titled second album (2010), which reached the US top 30, and the more conventionally metallic This Means War (2012). Clean vocalist Johnny Franck's 2010 departure, announced partly on religious grounds, was also widely reported. Shomo left in 2012 citing depression and health problems, later achieving greater success with his band Beartooth. Attack Attack! announced their break-up and a farewell tour in 2013.

Reunion

In October 2020 the band reunited with a line-up including original drummer Andrew Wetzel and released the single "All My Life", followed by the EP Long Time, No Sea (2021) and further singles, touring again while moving towards a more electronic metal sound.

Style and legacy

Attack Attack! were central to the "electronicore" wave of the late 2000s and early 2010s. Critics frequently derided the combination of deathcore vocals, pop choruses and rave breakdowns, but the band's commercial success and influence on a generation of Warped Tour metalcore acts are widely acknowledged.
