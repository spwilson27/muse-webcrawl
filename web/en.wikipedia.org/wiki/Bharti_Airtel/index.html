Bharti Airtel Limited, commonly known as Airtel, is an Indian multinational telecommunications company based in New Delhi. It operates in India and in 14 African countries through its subsidiary Airtel Africa. Airtel is India's second-largest mobile network operator and one of the largest mobile operators in the world by subscriber numbers, with more than 550 million customers across its operations.

History

The company was founded in 1995 by Sunil Bharti Mittal as Bharti Tele-Ventures, after Bharti won a license to build a cellular network in Delhi. The Airtel brand launched its mobile services in Delhi in 1995. Bharti pioneered the outsourcing of network operations and IT — concluding landmark deals with Ericsson, Nokia, and IBM — and a low-cost "minutes factory" model that made mobile telephony affordable to mass-market customers in India.

In 2010, Bharti acquired the African operations of Kuwait's Zain Group for about US$10.7 billion, one of the largest cross-border deals by an Indian company, expanding into 15 African countries. Airtel Africa was separately listed in London in 2019.

Operations

Airtel provides 4G and 5G mobile services across India, having launched Airtel 5G Plus beginning in October 2022. Its businesses include mobile voice and data, fixed-line and broadband (Airtel Xstream Fiber), direct-to-home television (Airtel Digital TV), enterprise connectivity (Airtel Business), and digital services including the Wynk music app, Airtel Thanks, and Airtel Payments Bank. In India its chief competitor is Reliance Jio; the two companies constitute a virtual duopoly in Indian mobile telephony after the consolidation that followed Jio's 2016 entry.
