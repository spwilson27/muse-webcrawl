Carry On Cleo

Carry On Cleo is a 1964 British comedy film, the tenth in the Carry On series. Directed by Gerald Thomas and written by Talbot Rothwell, it parodies Shakespeare's Antony and Cleopatra, the Burton-Taylor epic Cleopatra (1963), and sword-and-sandal films generally. It reused sets and costumes left at Pinewood from Cleopatra.

Plot

In prehistoric Britain, cavemen Hengist Pod and Horsa are captured by Romans and taken to Rome, where Hengist becomes bodyguard to Julius Caesar. Mark Antony falls for Cleopatra, Queen of Egypt, played by Amanda Barrie, while Sid James plays Antony and Kenneth Williams plays a neurotic Caesar fearing assassination — delivering the famous line "Infamy! Infamy! They've all got it in for me!" voted among the funniest film one-liners.

Cast

Sid James as Mark Antony, Kenneth Williams as Julius Caesar, Kenneth Connor as Hengist Pod, Charles Hawtrey as Seneca, Joan Sims as Calpurnia, Jim Dale as Horsa and Amanda Barrie as Cleopatra.
