Newfoundland and Labrador Hydro (NL Hydro) is a Canadian Crown corporation and the primary power generator for the province of Newfoundland and Labrador. A subsidiary of the provincial energy corporation (now Energy NL, formerly Nalcor Energy), it operates most of the province's large hydroelectric stations and the high-voltage transmission grid.

Operations

NL Hydro's largest asset is the 5,428 MW Churchill Falls generating station in Labrador, one of the largest underground powerhouses in the world, most of whose output is sold to Hydro-Québec under a long-term contract signed in 1969. On the island, its main stations include Bay d'Espoir, Hinds Lake and Cat Arm, supplemented by the Holyrood thermal station.

Muskrat Falls

Through Nalcor, NL Hydro developed the Muskrat Falls project on the lower Churchill River, an 824 MW station whose cost overruns became one of the largest public-policy controversies in the province's history.
