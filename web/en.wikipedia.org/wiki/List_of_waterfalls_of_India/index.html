List of waterfalls of India

Jog Falls, Karnataka, at monsoon flow

India has hundreds of waterfalls, concentrated in the Western Ghats, the Himalayas, and the plateaus of central and northeastern India. Most reach peak flow during the monsoon season (June to September). The tallest waterfall in India is Kunchikal Falls in Karnataka, with a height of about 455 metres (1,493 ft).
