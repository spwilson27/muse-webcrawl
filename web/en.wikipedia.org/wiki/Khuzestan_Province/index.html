Khuzestan Province

Country: Iran
Capital: Ahvaz
Known for: Oil industry, Karun River, ancient Elam

Khuzestan Province is one of the 31 provinces of Iran, located in the southwest of the country, bordering Iraq and the Persian Gulf. Its capital is Ahvaz. The province roughly corresponds to ancient Elam and is the heartland of Iran's petroleum industry.

History

Elam, one of the earliest civilisations of the region, flourished here from the 3rd millennium BCE, with its capital at Susa. The ancient ziggurat of Chogha Zanbil and the ruins of Susa are major archaeological sites. The port city of Abadan became the centre of Iran's oil refining after oil was struck at Masjed Soleyman in 1908.

Geography and people

The province ranges from the Zagros foothills in the north to hot, flat plains in the south, crossed by the Karun, Iran's largest river. It has a diverse population of Persians, Arabs, Lurs and Bakhtiari, and experiences some of the highest summer temperatures on Earth.
