Stateira II

Stateira II (also called Barsine; died 323 BC) was a Persian princess, the eldest daughter of Darius III of Persia, who became a wife of Alexander the Great.

Life

Stateira was captured with her mother, grandmother Sisygambis, and sisters after Alexander's victory at the Battle of Issus in 333 BC. Alexander treated the royal captives honourably. In 324 BC he married Stateira at the mass weddings in Susa, as part of his policy of uniting Macedonian and Persian elites; her sister Drypetis married Hephaestion at the same ceremony.

Death

After Alexander's death in Babylon in 323 BC, Stateira was murdered, according to Plutarch, on the orders of Roxana, Alexander's first wife, to eliminate a rival and her unborn potential heir.
