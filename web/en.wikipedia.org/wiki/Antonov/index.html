Antonov

Founded: 31 May 1946
Founder: Oleg Antonov
Headquarters: Kyiv, Ukraine
Industry: Aerospace and defence

Antonov (Ukrainian: Державне підприємство «Антонов») is a Ukrainian aircraft manufacturing and services company. Antonov's particular expertise is in the fields of very large aeroplanes and aeroplanes using unprepared runways. Antonov (model prefix An-) has built a total of approximately 22,000 aircraft, and thousands of its planes are operating in the former Soviet Union and in developing countries.

History

The company was founded in 1946 in Novosibirsk as a top-secret Soviet research and design bureau (OKB-153), headed by Oleg Antonov. In 1952 the bureau moved to Kyiv, Ukrainian SSR. Its first great success was the An-2 biplane, a rugged utility aircraft produced in enormous numbers. Later designs included the An-12 and An-22 transports, the An-124 Ruslan strategic airlifter, and the An-225 Mriya, the largest and heaviest aeroplane ever built, originally designed to carry the Buran space shuttle.

After Ukrainian independence, Antonov continued as a state enterprise, developing the An-148 and An-158 regional jets, the An-178 military transport, and the An-188 project. The sole completed An-225 was destroyed during the Battle of Antonov Airport in February 2022, in the opening phase of the Russian invasion of Ukraine; Antonov has stated its intention to rebuild the aircraft.

Notable aircraft

  • An-2 — single-engine biplane utility aircraft
  • An-124 Ruslan — heavy strategic airlifter
  • An-225 Mriya — largest cargo aircraft ever built
  • An-148/An-158 — regional jet family
