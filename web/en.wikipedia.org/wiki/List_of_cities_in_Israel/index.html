List of cities in Israel

This is a list of cities in Israel — municipalities granted city status by the Israeli Ministry of the Interior. Population figures are approximate, based on Israel Central Bureau of Statistics estimates. Jerusalem's status is internationally disputed; East Jerusalem is considered occupied Palestinian territory by the United Nations, while Israel claims the whole city as its capital.

Other cities

Further cities include Acre, Afula, Arad, Ariel, Dimona, Eilat, Kiryat Shmona, Lod, Ma'alot-Tarshiha, Nahariya, Nazareth Illit (Nof HaGalil), Ofakim, Or Yehuda, Qiryat Gat, Ra'anana, Ramla, Safed (Tzfat), Sderot, Tamra, Tayibe, Tiberias, Tira, Umm al-Fahm and Yokneam Illit. Mixed Jewish-Arab cities include Haifa, Lod, Ramla, Acre and Tel Aviv-Jaffa.
