Fininvest S.p.A. is an Italian holding company controlled by the Berlusconi family. Founded in 1978 by Silvio Berlusconi in Milan, it became the vehicle through which he built a business empire spanning television broadcasting, publishing, advertising, insurance, and football.

History

Berlusconi established Fininvest in 1978 to consolidate his real-estate and media ventures. Through the 1980s the group assembled Italy's first national private television network, acquiring Canale 5, Italia 1, and Rete 4, which later formed the core of Mediaset. The group also controlled the Mondadori publishing house and the AC Milan football club, which Berlusconi owned from 1986 until its sale in 2017.

Holdings

Major holdings have included Mediaset (now MFE – MediaForEurope), Mondadori Group, Banca Mediolanum, and the theatre and cinema producer Medusa Film. After Berlusconi's death in June 2023, control of the company passed to his children, with his eldest daughter Marina Berlusconi serving as chairwoman.
