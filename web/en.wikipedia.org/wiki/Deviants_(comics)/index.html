Deviants (comics)

The Deviants, also known as Homo descendantus, are a fictional race of humanoids appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by Jack Kirby, they first appeared in The Eternals #1 (July 1976).

Within the Marvel Universe, the Deviants were created a million years ago by the cosmic Celestials, who experimented on early humans. In contrast to the stable and beautiful Eternals, every Deviant is genetically unstable: each is a unique mutant, and many appear monstrous to human eyes, a trait called "the Deviant syndrome". Deviant society, centred on the sunken city of Lemuria, has historically been ruled by figures such as Kro and Brother Tode, and tends toward war with both the Eternals and humanity.

The Deviants appear in the Marvel Cinematic Universe film Eternals (2021) as the Eternals' ancient enemies.
