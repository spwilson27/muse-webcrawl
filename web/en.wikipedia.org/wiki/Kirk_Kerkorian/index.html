Kirk Kerkorian

Kirk Kerkorian (June 6, 1917 - June 15, 2015) was an American businessman of Armenian descent, investor and philanthropist known as the 'king of Las Vegas'. Through Tracinda Corporation he built and sold major resorts including the International Hotel, MGM Grand, and was a controlling figure in MGM Resorts and Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer film studio, which he bought and sold three times.

A former boxer and RAF Ferry Command pilot, Kerkorian made fortunes in charter airlines, real estate and auto investments, at times holding large stakes in Chrysler, GM and Ford. His Lincy Foundation donated hundreds of millions to Armenia and Armenian causes, especially after the 1988 earthquake.
