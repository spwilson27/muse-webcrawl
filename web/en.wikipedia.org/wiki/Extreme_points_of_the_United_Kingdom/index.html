Extreme points of the United Kingdom

This is a list of the extreme points of the United Kingdom: the locations that are the furthest north, south, east, and west, and the highest and lowest, in the UK.

United Kingdom overall

The northernmost point is Out Stack in the Shetland Islands; the southernmost is Pednathise Head in the Isles of Scilly (with Bishop Rock the southernmost point of England's building-bearing land). The easternmost point is Lowestoft Ness, Suffolk, and the westernmost is Rockall, claimed by the UK, with Corrachadh Mòr in Scotland the westernmost point of mainland Great Britain. The highest point is Ben Nevis (1,345 m) in Scotland, and the lowest is Holme Fen in Cambridgeshire, approximately 3 m below sea level.
