Puskás Aréna

Puskás Aréna is a football stadium in Budapest, Hungary. Completed in 2019 on the site of the former Ferenc Puskás Stadium, it is the home ground of the Hungary national football team and the largest stadium in the country, with a capacity of around 67,000 spectators.

History

The arena is named after Ferenc Puskás, the legendary Hungarian footballer and captain of the "Mighty Magyars" of the 1950s. The old stadium on the site, opened in 1953 as the People's Stadium, was demolished in 2016 to make way for the new arena. Construction began in 2017, and the stadium was inaugurated on 15 November 2019 with a friendly match between Hungary and Uruguay.

Major events

Puskás Aréna was one of the host venues of UEFA Euro 2020, staging group matches and a round-of-16 tie. It also hosted the 2020 UEFA Super Cup and the 2023 UEFA Europa League Final. It regularly hosts the final of the Hungarian Cup.

Design

The stadium features a distinctive façade with a mesh-like outer skin and a roof covering all seats. Its design references elements of the old stadium, including the main entrance building, which was preserved.
