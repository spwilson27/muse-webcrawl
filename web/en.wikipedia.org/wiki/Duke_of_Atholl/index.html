Duke of Atholl is a title in the Peerage of Scotland, created in 1703 for John Murray, 2nd Marquess of Atholl. The dukes, chiefs of Clan Murray, have their seat at Blair Castle in Perthshire.

History

The Murray family acquired the earldom of Atholl in the 17th century. The dukedom was granted by Queen Anne in recognition of the 2nd Marquess's support for the Revolution Settlement. The dukes historically held the hereditary right to maintain the Atholl Highlanders, the only legal private army in Europe, raised in 1777 and still maintained as a ceremonial unit.

The title passed through various branches of the family. Since 1996 the dukedom has been held by a South African branch of the Murrays; the current holder is Bruce Murray, 12th Duke of Atholl. The heir apparent is Michael Bruce John Murray, Marquess of Tullibardine.
