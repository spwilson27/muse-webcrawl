European political party

A European political party, formally a political party at European level, is an organization recognized by the European Union which is composed primarily of national political parties and individual citizens, and which follows a political program in EU affairs. They are distinct from political groups in the European Parliament, though closely linked.

Regulation and funding

Since 2004 European parties have been eligible for public funding from the EU budget, regulated notably by Regulation (EU) No 1141/2014, which established the Authority for European Political Parties and European Political Foundations for registration and oversight.

Major parties

  • European People's Party (centre-right)
  • Party of European Socialists (centre-left)
  • Alliance of Liberals and Democrats for Europe Party
  • European Green Party
  • European Conservatives and Reformists Party
  • Party of the European Left
  • European Free Alliance

Role

European parties coordinate national member parties, adopt common manifestos for European elections, and nominate lead candidates (Spitzenkandidaten) for President of the European Commission.
