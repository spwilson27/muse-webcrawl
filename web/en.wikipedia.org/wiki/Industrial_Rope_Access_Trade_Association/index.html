Industrial Rope Access Trade Association (IRATA)

Founded: late 1980s, United Kingdom

Purpose: Rope access safety standards and technician certification

The Industrial Rope Access Trade Association (IRATA International) is the global trade association for the work-at-height rope access industry, formed in the United Kingdom in the late 1980s initially to serve the offshore oil and gas industry.

Role

IRATA maintains a widely adopted code of practice and a three-level technician certification scheme (Levels 1–3), covering training, work experience, and supervision requirements. Its member companies perform inspection, maintenance, and construction work on structures such as offshore platforms, bridges, towers, and building façades.

Safety record

IRATA publishes annual work and safety analysis reports; rope access under its system is noted for low incident rates compared with many other access methods.
