Toll road

A toll road, also called a turnpike or tollway, is a road for which a fee is charged for passage. Tolls fund construction, maintenance and operation, historically collected at toll booths and increasingly by electronic toll collection and automatic number-plate recognition.

Turnpike trusts built many British roads in the 18th and 19th centuries, and tolled motorways are common in many countries. Modern systems include open road tolling without booths and congestion pricing schemes in cities such as Singapore, London and Stockholm.
