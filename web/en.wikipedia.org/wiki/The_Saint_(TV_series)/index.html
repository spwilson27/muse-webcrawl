Overview

The Saint is a British mystery thriller television series that aired on ITV from 1962 to 1969. It stars Roger Moore as Simon Templar, the charming, roguish adventurer created by Leslie Charteris in a long-running series of books beginning in 1928. Templar, known as 'the Saint' — a modern-day Robin Hood — travels the world righting wrongs, outwitting criminals, and occasionally bending the law himself.

The series ran for six series and 118 episodes, the first 71 in black and white and the remainder in colour. Moore often broke the fourth wall in early episodes, addressing the audience directly, and a halo would appear above his head when his name was mentioned.

Production and legacy

Produced by ITC Entertainment, The Saint was one of the longest-running adventure series of its era and was exported widely, achieving major success in the United States. Moore's performance made him an international star and helped prepare the way for his later casting as James Bond. The character returned in later television series and a 1997 feature film starring Val Kilmer.
