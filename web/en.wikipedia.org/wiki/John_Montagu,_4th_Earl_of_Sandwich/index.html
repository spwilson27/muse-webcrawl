John Montagu, 4th Earl of Sandwich (November 13, 1718 – April 30, 1792) was a British statesman who served three times as First Lord of the Admiralty, including throughout most of the American Revolutionary War. He is chiefly remembered today because the food item known as the sandwich is named after him.

Career

Educated at Eton and Trinity College, Cambridge, Sandwich entered politics young and held a succession of offices: First Lord of the Admiralty, Secretary of State for the Northern Department, and Postmaster General. As head of the Admiralty during the American war he was criticised for keeping too much of the fleet in home waters and for alleged corruption in naval appointments, though historians credit him with reforms in dockyard administration and support for exploration.

The sandwich

According to a popular story, Sandwich asked for meat served between slices of bread so that he could eat without leaving the gaming table — or, in the more charitable version, his desk — and the snack took his name. He was also a patron of Captain James Cook, who named the Sandwich Islands (Hawaii) after him.
