Denver International Airport

IATADEN
Opened1995
LocationDenver, Colorado, United States

Denver International Airport (IATA: DEN) is the largest airport in the United States by land area, covering about 33,500 acres (52 square miles), and one of the busiest in the world by passenger traffic. It opened in 1995, replacing Stapleton International Airport.

Features

The airport is recognizable by its white tensile-fabric roof, designed to evoke the snow-capped Rocky Mountains. It is a hub for United Airlines and Frontier Airlines and a major base for Southwest Airlines. Its size and unusual art installations, including the "Blue Mustang" sculpture, have made it a frequent subject of conspiracy theories.
