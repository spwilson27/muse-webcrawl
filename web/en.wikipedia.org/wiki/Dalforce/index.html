Dalforce

Dalforce, officially the Singapore Overseas Chinese Anti-Japanese Volunteer Army, was an irregular military unit raised in Singapore shortly before the Japanese invasion in December 1941. It was organised by British officer John Dalley — from whom the name derives — through the Chinese Liaison Committee, drawing volunteers largely from Singapore's Chinese community, including both Kuomintang and communist supporters.

Numbering roughly 1,250–4,000 men with minimal training and equipment, Dalforce companies fought alongside regular troops during the defence of Singapore in February 1942, notably in the northwest sector. After the fall of Singapore many members were executed in reprisals or joined guerrilla forces in Malaya.
