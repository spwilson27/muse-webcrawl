Alençon is a commune in the Orne department in Normandy, north-western France, and the prefecture of the department. It lies on the river Sarthe at the southern edge of Normandy, near the border with the Pays de la Loire.

History

The town developed in the Middle Ages as a fortified settlement and later as a centre of the lace trade. Its needle lace, the celebrated point d'Alençon, brought international fame from the 17th century onward. Alençon is also known as the birthplace of Saint Thérèse of Lisieux, born there in 1873.

Sights

Landmarks include the Gothic Notre-Dame church, the Château des Ducs, the 18th-century town hall, and the Maison d'Ozé. A museum of fine arts and lace preserves the town's lacemaking heritage.
