Edmund Allenby, 1st Viscount Allenby

Field Marshal Edmund Henry Hynman Allenby, 1st Viscount Allenby (23 April 1861 – 14 May 1936) was a British soldier and administrator, best known for commanding the Egyptian Expeditionary Force that captured Palestine and Syria from the Ottoman Empire in World War I.

Edmund Allenby
Born: 1861, Brackenhurst, England
Died: 1936, London
Commands: Third Army, Egyptian Expeditionary Force
Later: High Commissioner of Egypt 1919–1925

Early career

Allenby served in the Boer War and on the Western Front, commanding the Third Army at Arras in 1917 before being sent to Egypt.

Palestine campaign

He won at Beersheba and Gaza, entered Jerusalem on foot on 11 December 1917 out of respect for the holy city, and destroyed Ottoman armies at Megiddo (September 1918), one of history's decisive cavalry operations, working alongside T. E. Lawrence and Arab forces.

Egypt

As High Commissioner he recommended Egyptian independence, granted in 1922 in limited form. He retired in 1925.
