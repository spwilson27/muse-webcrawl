MITRE ATT&CK

MITRE ATT&CK (Adversarial Tactics, Techniques, and Common Knowledge) is a knowledge base of cyber adversary behaviour, developed and maintained by the MITRE Corporation. It catalogues the tactics and techniques used by threat actors across the stages of an intrusion.

MITRE ATT&CK
Developer: MITRE Corporation
Released: 2013
Type: Cybersecurity knowledge base
Matrices: Enterprise, Mobile, ICS

Structure

The framework is organised as a matrix: columns represent tactics (the adversary's goals, such as initial access, persistence, privilege escalation and exfiltration), and each tactic contains techniques and sub-techniques describing how the goal is achieved, with documented procedure examples from real intrusions.

Usage

Security teams use ATT&CK for threat intelligence, detection engineering, red-teaming and measuring defensive coverage. It is freely available and widely adopted across the cybersecurity industry.
