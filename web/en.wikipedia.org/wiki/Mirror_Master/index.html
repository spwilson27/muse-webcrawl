Mirror Master

Mirror Master is the name of several supervillains appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, principally as enemies of the Flash. The character wields mirror-based technology that allows him to travel through reflective surfaces, trap people inside mirrors, create duplicates, and enter the "Mirror World". Mirror Master is a core member of the Rogues, the loose fraternity of Central City villains.

Sam Scudder

The original Mirror Master, Sam Scudder, debuted in The Flash #105 (1959), created by John Broome and Carmine Infantino. A convict who discovered how to trap images in mirrors, Scudder built an arsenal of trick mirrors. He died during Crisis on Infinite Earths.

Evan McCulloch

The second Mirror Master, Evan McCulloch, a Scottish orphan turned mercenary, inherited Scudder's equipment from a shadowy consortium and debuted in Animal Man #8 (1989). Written prominently by Grant Morrison and later by Geoff Johns, McCulloch's version is more ruthless and remains the best-known incarnation, featuring heavily in The Flash comics and in animated and live-action adaptations.
