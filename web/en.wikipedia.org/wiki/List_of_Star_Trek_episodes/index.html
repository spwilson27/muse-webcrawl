List of Star Trek episodes

Star Trek is an American science fiction media franchise created by Gene Roddenberry. Beginning with The Original Series in 1966, the franchise has grown to include numerous television series and more than 900 episodes in total. Dedicated episode lists exist for each series; this article provides an overview with notable episodes.
