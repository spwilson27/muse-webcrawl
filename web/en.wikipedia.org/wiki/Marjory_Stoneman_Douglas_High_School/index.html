Marjory Stoneman Douglas High School (MSD) is a public high school in Parkland, Florida, operated by Broward County Public Schools. Opened in 1990 and named for the Everglades conservationist Marjory Stoneman Douglas, it serves Parkland and portions of Coral Springs and enrolled roughly 3,200 students.

History

On 14 February 2018 the school was the site of a mass shooting in which a former student killed 17 people — 14 students and three staff members — and injured 17 others, one of the deadliest school shootings in United States history. Survivors founded the advocacy group Never Again MSD and helped organise the March for Our Lives, spurring Florida legislation on school safety and firearms. The freshman building where most victims died was demolished in 2024.

Academics

The school is highly rated academically, with an acclaimed drama programme — whose students wrote the song "Shine" after the shooting — and championship debate and athletics teams.
