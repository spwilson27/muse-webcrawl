Caeleb Remel Dressel (born August 16, 1996) is an American competitive swimmer who specialises in freestyle, butterfly, and individual medley events. He is a nine-time Olympic gold medalist and holds world records in several events, and is widely regarded as one of the greatest sprint swimmers in history.

Career

Dressel first gained attention at the University of Florida under coach Gregg Troy, where he won multiple NCAA titles and set American records in the 50-yard freestyle, becoming the first swimmer to break 18 seconds in that event. He made his Olympic debut at Rio 2016, winning two gold medals in relays.

At the 2017 World Championships in Budapest, Dressel won seven gold medals, tying Michael Phelps's record for a single world championships. He won six more golds at the 2019 Worlds in Gwangju. At the Tokyo 2020 Olympics he won five gold medals, including the individual 50 m and 100 m freestyle and 100 m butterfly titles — setting a world record of 49.45 in the butterfly — and was one of the stars of the Games.

After stepping away from competition for a period in 2022–23 for his mental health, Dressel returned to win two further relay golds and a silver at the Paris 2024 Olympics. His world records in the 100 m butterfly (long course) and short-course events, and his explosive starts and underwater dolphin kicks, have defined modern sprint swimming technique.
