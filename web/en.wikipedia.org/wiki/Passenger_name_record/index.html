Passenger name record

A passenger name record (PNR) is a record in the database of a computer reservation system (CRS) containing the itinerary and details for a passenger, or a group of passengers travelling together. The concept was introduced by airlines and is now also used for hotels, trains, car rentals and tours.

Contents

A PNR typically includes the passenger's name, contact details, itinerary with flight numbers and dates, ticketing information and fare, payment details, and special service requests such as meals or assistance. It is identified by a record locator, usually a six-character code given to the traveller as their booking reference.

Privacy and government use

PNR data is shared between airlines, alliances and global distribution systems such as Amadeus, Sabre and Travelport. Since the 2000s, governments — including the United States, the European Union and Canada — have required airlines to transfer PNR data for security and border purposes, prompting agreements and court rulings balancing security against data-protection rights.
