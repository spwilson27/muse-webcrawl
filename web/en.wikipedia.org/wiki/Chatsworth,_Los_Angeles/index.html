Chatsworth, Los Angeles

Named forChatsworth House, England

Chatsworth is a suburban neighbourhood in the northwestern San Fernando Valley region of Los Angeles, California, United States. It is known for its rocky hills, historic movie ranches, and equestrian character.

History

The area was home to the Fernandeño Tataviam Band of Mission Indians before Spanish colonization. In the 20th century, Chatsworth's rugged scenery made it a popular filming location for Westerns, notably at the Iverson Movie Ranch; scenes from many classic films and television shows were shot among its sandstone formations, including the Santa Susana Mountains.

Landmarks

Landmarks include Stoney Point Park, popular with climbers; the Chatsworth Reservoir area; and the old stagecoach trail through Santa Susana Pass.
