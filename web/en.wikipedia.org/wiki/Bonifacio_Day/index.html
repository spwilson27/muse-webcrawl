Bonifacio Day

Bonifacio Day is a regular national public holiday in the Philippines observed annually on 30 November. It commemorates the birth of Andrés Bonifacio (1863–1897), the founder of the Katipunan and one of the country's national heroes, who led the start of the Philippine Revolution against Spanish colonial rule in 1896.

The holiday was established by Philippine Legislature Act No. 2946 in 1921 and is celebrated with wreath-laying ceremonies, parades and speeches, principally at the Bonifacio Monument in Caloocan and at his shrine in Manila. Unlike Rizal Day, which marks its hero's death on 30 December, Bonifacio Day falls on its hero's birthday.
