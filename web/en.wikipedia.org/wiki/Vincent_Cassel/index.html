Vincent Cassel (French: [vɛ̃sɑ̃ kasɛl]; born Vincent Crochon, 23 November 1966) is a French actor. He first achieved recognition for his performance as a troubled French Jewish youth in Mathieu Kassovitz's 1995 film La Haine (Hate), for which he received a César Award nomination. He garnered wide international acclaim with English-speaking audiences for his performances in Ocean's Twelve (2004) and Ocean's Thirteen (2007), as well as Black Swan (2010). He also gained recognition for his roles in the films Eastern Promises (2007), Mesrine (2008), in which he portrayed French gangster Jacques Mesrine, and Jason Bourne (2016). Cassel was engaged to model and actress Tina Kunakey.

Early life

Cassel was born in Paris, France, to Jean-Pierre Cassel (born Jean-Pierre Crochon), an actor, and Sabine Litique, a journalist. His brother, Mathias, is the frontman of the hip-hop group Assassin, and his sister, Cécile Cassel, is an actress. Cassel's father was French and Jewish; his mother is French Roman Catholic. He was educated at the Cours Florent acting school and spent time in New York, where he studied at the Actors Institute and trained as a juggler and acrobat at a circus school in France.

Career

Cassel's breakthrough role came in 1995 with La Haine, directed by Mathieu Kassovitz. In the film, he played Vinz, a young Jewish man in the Paris suburbs caught up in rioting following police brutality. The film was a critical and commercial success, and Cassel received César Award nominations for Best Actor and Most Promising Actor.

In the late 1990s and 2000s, Cassel alternated between French and international productions. He appeared in L'Appartement (1996), Dobermann (1997), Elizabeth (1998), The Messenger: The Story of Joan of Arc (1999), Brotherhood of the Wolf (2001), Irréversible (2002), directed by Gaspar Noé, and Ocean's Twelve (2004), in which he played the master thief François Toulour, reprising the role in Ocean's Thirteen (2007).

In 2008, Cassel starred in the two-part biographical crime film Mesrine, portraying notorious French gangster Jacques Mesrine. His performance earned him the César Award for Best Actor. He followed this with roles in Black Swan (2010), playing the demanding ballet director Thomas Leroy, A Dangerous Method (2011), Trance (2013), Child 44 (2015), and Jason Bourne (2016). In 2020, he joined the third season of the HBO series Westworld. He also provided the French voice of Monsieur Hood in Shrek and voiced characters in other animated films.

Personal life

Cassel married Italian actress Monica Bellucci in 1999, after meeting her on the set of L'Appartement. They have two daughters, Deva (born 2004) and Léonie (born 2010). The couple announced their separation in August 2013. In 2018, Cassel married French model Tina Kunakey; they have a daughter, Amazonie, born in 2019. Cassel is fluent in French, English, Portuguese, and Italian, and has lived in Brazil, where he became a naturalized citizen.
