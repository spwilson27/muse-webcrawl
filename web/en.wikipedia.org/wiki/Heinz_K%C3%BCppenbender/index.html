Heinz Küppenbender

Born23 February 1901
Died4 July 1988
OccupationEngineer, camera designer
Known forDesign of the Contax camera system

Heinz Küppenbender (1901–1988) was a German engineer and one of the most influential camera designers of the 20th century. As chief designer at Zeiss Ikon in Dresden, he led the development of the Contax rangefinder camera system launched in 1932, which rivalled Leica and introduced innovations such as a combined rangefinder-viewfinder and a wide range of interchangeable lenses.

Career

Küppenbender had earlier worked at Ernemann and joined Zeiss Ikon on its formation. After the Second World War he moved to the re-established Zeiss Ikon in Stuttgart, where he was involved in the post-war Contax and later Contarex systems. His designs helped define the professional 35mm camera market for decades.
