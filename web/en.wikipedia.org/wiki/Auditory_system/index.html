Auditory system

The auditory system is the sensory system for the sense of hearing. It includes both the peripheral structures — the outer, middle and inner ear — and the central auditory pathways and areas of the brain that process sound. Sound is collected by the pinna, funnelled through the ear canal to the eardrum, transmitted and amplified by the three ossicles of the middle ear, and converted into neural signals by the hair cells of the cochlea in the inner ear.

Peripheral auditory system

Outer ear

The pinna (auricle) and ear canal collect sound and provide directional cues; the canal resonates at frequencies important for speech. The tympanic membrane (eardrum) vibrates in response to sound pressure.

Middle ear

The ossicles — malleus, incus and stapes, the smallest bones in the human body — transfer vibration from the eardrum to the oval window of the cochlea, acting as an impedance-matching lever system that compensates for the difference between air and the fluid of the inner ear. The Eustachian tube equalises pressure across the eardrum.

Inner ear

Within the snail-shaped cochlea, the basilar membrane vibrates at different places for different frequencies (tonotopy). Inner hair cells transduce this mechanical motion into electrical signals; outer hair cells act as a "cochlear amplifier". Signals leave via the auditory division of the vestibulocochlear nerve.

Central auditory system

Auditory information ascends through the cochlear nuclei, superior olivary complex (important for sound localisation using interaural time and level differences), lateral lemniscus, inferior colliculus and medial geniculate body of the thalamus, reaching the primary auditory cortex in the temporal lobe, where pitch, timbre and complex sounds such as speech are analysed.

Disorders

Disorders of the auditory system include conductive and sensorineural hearing loss, tinnitus, presbycusis (age-related hearing loss), otitis media and central auditory processing disorders. Treatments range from hearing aids to cochlear implants.
