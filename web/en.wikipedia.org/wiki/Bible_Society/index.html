Bible society

TypeNon-profit organisation
PurposeBible translation, publication and distribution
Umbrella bodyUnited Bible Societies

A Bible society is a non-profit organisation devoted to translating, publishing and distributing the Bible at affordable prices, usually working across denominational lines. Bible societies exist in many countries and cooperate through the United Bible Societies fellowship, founded in 1946.

History

The modern movement began with the British and Foreign Bible Society, founded in London in 1804, followed by societies in Europe and the American Bible Society in 1816. Their founding principle was to circulate the scriptures "without note or comment".

Work

Bible societies translate the Bible into new languages, revise existing translations, print and distribute copies, and run literacy and scripture-engagement programmes. Many employ translation consultants who support local translator teams worldwide.
