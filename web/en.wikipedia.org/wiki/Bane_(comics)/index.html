Bane (comics)

Bane is the name of several characters appearing in comic books — principally the DC Comics supervillain who is an adversary of Batman. This article indexes the comics-related figures who have used the name and the publications in which they appear. For the character's biography and media appearances in depth, see the main article on the character.

The DC Comics character

The best-known Bane debuted in the one-shot Batman: Vengeance of Bane (1993), written by Chuck Dixon with art by Graham Nolan, as the origin chapter of the Knightfall saga. Editor Dennis O'Neil sought a villain who combined the intelligence of Ra's al Ghul with overwhelming physical power; the character's design — a masked luchador-like strongman with tubes feeding the drug Venom to his brain — became iconic. See Bane (character).

Other characters named Bane

  • Dorrance's son / Antonio Diego — the live-action film version of the character in Batman & Robin (1997), a serial killer transformed by Venom experiments.
  • Bane (The Dark Knight Rises) — the 2012 film incarnation, an excommunicated member of the League of Shadows, portrayed without the Venom apparatus of the comics.
  • Bane of the Post — unrelated usages of the word "bane" in older comic anthologies.

Publication history

Following Knightfall (1993–1994), Bane starred in the sequel one-shot Vengeance of Bane II: The Redemption (1995), featured in Bane of the Demon (1998), and became a regular in ensemble titles including Secret Six (2008–2011), written by Gail Simone, which recast him as a grimly honourable antihero.
