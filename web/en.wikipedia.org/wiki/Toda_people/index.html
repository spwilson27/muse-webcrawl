Toda people

The Toda people are a Dravidian ethnic group who live in the Nilgiri Mountains of Tamil Nadu, India. Traditionally pastoralists herding buffalo, they numbered only about 2,000 in the early 21st century. Their language, Toda, is a Dravidian language.

Toda society is known for its barrel-vaulted houses, sacred buffalo dairies functioning as temples, and distinctive embroidered cloaks called pithukuli. The community historically practised fraternal polyandry. The Nilgiri landscape they inhabit is now a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve.

Culture

Buffalo herding and dairying remain central ritually, though many Toda now farm. Their embroidery, featuring red and black patterns on white cloth, received a Geographical Indication tag.
