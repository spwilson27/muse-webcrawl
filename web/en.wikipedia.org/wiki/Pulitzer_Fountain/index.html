Pulitzer Fountain

Pulitzer Fountain is an outdoor fountain located in Grand Army Plaza in Manhattan, New York City, at Fifth Avenue and 59th Street, in front of the Plaza Hotel. It was funded by a bequest from newspaper publisher Joseph Pulitzer and completed in 1916.

Design

The fountain was designed by architect Thomas Hastings, with a bronze statue of Pomona, Roman goddess of abundance, sculpted by Karl Bitter, standing atop the tiered granite basins. Pulitzer had admired the Place de la Concorde fountains in Paris and left $50,000 for a similar fountain. The plaza and fountain were restored in the late 1980s with funding associated with the adjacent hotel.
