A nightclub is a club that is open at night, usually for drinking, dancing and entertainment. Nightclubs often feature a dance floor, a DJ booth, and areas for socialising and bars serving alcohol. Many are distinguished from pubs and bars by elements such as cover charges, door policies, dress codes and music-centred entertainment.

History

The modern nightclub descends from 19th-century Parisian cabarets and American saloons and dance halls. During Prohibition in the United States, illegal speakeasies flourished. The discothèque format — recorded music played by a disc jockey rather than a live band — emerged in occupied Paris and spread after the Second World War. The 1970s disco era, centred on venues such as New York's Studio 54, made the DJ and the dance floor the heart of club culture; in the 1980s and 1990s, house and techno scenes in Chicago, Detroit, London and Berlin (exemplified by clubs like the Hacienda and Berghain) reshaped global popular music.

Music and culture

Club culture is closely tied to electronic dance music genres — disco, house, techno, trance, drum and bass — and to resident DJs who build reputations for long sets. Lighting designers, VJs and sound systems (such as the famous rigs at Ministry of Sound) are integral to the experience. Superclubs in Ibiza and Las Vegas headline touring DJs, while underground venues emphasise community and door selection.

Business and regulation

Nightclubs operate under liquor licensing, noise and closing-time regulations that vary widely by jurisdiction. Many earn most revenue from alcohol sales and table ("bottle") service. The sector has faced pressure from rising rents, licensing costs, changing drinking habits and, in 2020–21, pandemic closures; industry bodies in the UK and elsewhere report a long-term decline in club numbers alongside growth in festivals and day parties.

Safety

Notable disasters, such as the Station nightclub fire (2003) and the Kiss nightclub fire (2013), led to stricter fire codes, capacity limits and pyrotechnics rules for venues.
