London Luton Airport (IATA: LTN, ICAO: EGGW), previously called Luton International Airport, is an international airport located 2.7 km (1.7 mi) east of the centre of Luton in Bedfordshire, England, and about 46 km (29 mi) north of Central London. It is the fourth-largest airport serving the London area, after Heathrow, Gatwick and Stansted, and one of the UK's principal bases for low-cost airlines, above all easyJet, which has its headquarters at the airport, and Wizz Air, for which Luton is the largest UK base.

History

The airport opened on 16 July 1938 as a municipal aerodrome and served during the Second World War as an RAF station, home to No. 264 Squadron's night fighters among others. After the war it became a base for charter and inclusive-tour operators, and in the 1960s and 1970s it was notable for the holiday airlines Court Line and Britannia Airways. The airport was made famous to a wider British audience by the 1970s Campari television advertisement featuring Lorraine Chase ("Nah, Luton Airport!"). Growth accelerated with the low-cost revolution of the 1990s; easyJet began flying from Luton in 1995. A major redevelopment completed in 2018 expanded the terminal, and the Luton DART automated shuttle linking the terminal with Luton Airport Parkway railway station opened in 2023.

Facilities and operations

Luton has a single terminal and one runway (07/25), which is relatively short at 2,162 m, limiting the airport largely to short- and medium-haul operations by narrow-body aircraft. The airport is owned by Luton Borough Council through Luton Rising (formerly London Luton Airport Limited) and operated under concession by London Luton Airport Operations Ltd. It operates 24 hours a day, though night flights are restricted. Planning consent has been granted for expansion intended to raise capacity substantially, with a second terminal proposed.

Ground transport

The airport is close to junction 10 of the M1 motorway. Luton Airport Parkway station, about a mile from the terminal and now connected by the DART shuttle, offers trains to London St Pancras in as little as around 25 minutes on fast services.
