Buck-Tick (stylised as BUCK-TICK) is a Japanese rock band formed in Fujioka, Gunma, in 1983. The classic line-up consisted of vocalist Atsushi Sakurai, guitarists Hisashi Imai and Hidehiko Hoshino, bassist Yutaka Higuchi and drummer Toll Yagami. One of the founders of the visual kei movement, the band has maintained an unusually stable line-up for four decades and has released more than twenty studio albums.

History

Buck-Tick made their major-label debut in 1987 and rose to prominence with albums such as Seventh Heaven (1988), Taboo (1989) and Aku no Hana (1990), whose title track became their first number-one single. Over subsequent decades the band continually reinvented its sound, incorporating gothic rock, industrial music, electronica and dark wave on albums including Darker Than Darkness (1993), Sexy Stream Liner (1997), Mona Lisa Overdrive (2003) and Abracadabra (2020). Vocalist Atsushi Sakurai died of a brain-stem haemorrhage in October 2023 after collapsing on stage; the remaining members announced their intention to continue the band.

Legacy

Buck-Tick are widely credited with shaping visual kei and Japanese gothic rock, influencing generations of Japanese musicians with their dark aesthetics and Imai's experimental guitar work.
