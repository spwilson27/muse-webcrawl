Iveco Group

Iveco Group N.V.
Type: Public
Industry: Automotive
Founded: 1 January 2022 (spin-off)
Headquarters: Turin, Italy

Iveco Group N.V. is a multinational corporation specialised in commercial and specialty vehicles, powertrain, and related financial services. The group was formed through a spin-off from CNH Industrial, completed on 1 January 2022, and is listed on Euronext Milan.

Brands

The group's brands include IVECO (trucks and commercial vehicles), FPT Industrial (powertrain), IVECO BUS and HEULIEZ (buses), IDV (defence vehicles), ASTRA (quarry and construction trucks), MAGIRUS (firefighting vehicles), and IVECO CAPITAL (financial services).

History

The IVECO business traces its origins to the 1975 merger of the commercial vehicle operations of Fiat and several European manufacturers. In 2025, Tata Motors agreed to acquire Iveco Group's commercial vehicles business, while the defence business (IDV and ASTRA) was agreed to be sold to Leonardo.
