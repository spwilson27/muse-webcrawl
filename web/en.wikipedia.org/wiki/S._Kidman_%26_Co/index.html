S. Kidman & Co

Founded1899
FounderSidney Kidman
IndustryCattle, pastoral agriculture
HeadquartersAdelaide, South Australia

S. Kidman & Co is one of Australia's largest beef producers and pastoral companies, founded in 1899 by Sidney Kidman, the famous "Cattle King". The company historically controlled vast outback stations forming a chain from the Channel Country to markets in the south, allowing cattle to be moved between properties according to drought conditions.

Holdings

At its peak the Kidman empire covered more than 3 percent of Australia's land area. Its stations have included Anna Creek Station in South Australia, the world's largest cattle station. In 2016, after a high-profile sale process reviewed on national-interest grounds, a majority stake was acquired by Hancock Prospecting, Gina Rinehart's company, with a minority Chinese partner later reduced in the final structure.

Legacy

Kidman's strategy of holding a network of stations along reliable watercourses remains a landmark in Australian pastoral history.
