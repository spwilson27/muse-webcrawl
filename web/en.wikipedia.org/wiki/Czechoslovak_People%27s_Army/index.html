Czechoslovak People's Army

Czechoslovak People's Army
Active: 1954–1990
Country: Czechoslovakia
Allegiance: Communist Party of Czechoslovakia
Branch: Army, Air Force, Air Defence

The Czechoslovak People's Army (Czech: Československá lidová armáda, ČSLA) was the armed forces of communist Czechoslovakia from 1954 to 1990, during the period of the Czechoslovak Socialist Republic.

History

After the communist takeover of 1948, the armed forces were reorganised on the Soviet model and renamed in 1954. Czechoslovakia was a founding member of the Warsaw Pact in 1955, and the ČSLA was integrated into Pact planning, though it controversially did not take part in the 1968 invasion of its own country — that was carried out by other Pact members against it.

Dissolution

Following the Velvet Revolution of 1989, the army was depoliticised and its name reverted to the Czechoslovak Army in 1990. On the dissolution of Czechoslovakia in 1993 it split into the Army of the Czech Republic and the Armed Forces of the Slovak Republic.
