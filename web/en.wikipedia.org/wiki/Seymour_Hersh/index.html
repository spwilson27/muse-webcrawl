Seymour Hersh

Seymour Hersh
Born8 April 1937, Chicago
OccupationInvestigative journalist
[Image: Hersh speaking at a journalism conference]

Seymour Hersh (born 1937) is an American investigative journalist who exposed the My Lai massacre in 1969 (Pulitzer Prize, 1970), reported on domestic CIA spying, and revealed the Abu Ghraib prison abuses in 2004 in The New Yorker. His later reporting — relying on unnamed sources, including disputed accounts of the Bin Laden raid and the Nord Stream sabotage — has been both influential and controversial.

Method

Hersh's career is the modern archetype of the lone investigative reporter cultivating intelligence-community sources; editors and critics have debated the verifiability of his single-source claims, especially in his Substack era.
