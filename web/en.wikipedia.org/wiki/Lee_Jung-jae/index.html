Lee Jung-jae (born 15 December 1972) is a South Korean actor and businessman. One of South Korea's most successful actors, he first rose to prominence in the 1990s with the television drama Sandglass (1995) and the film An Affair (1998), and achieved leading-man status with the romance Il Mare (2000), later remade in Hollywood as The Lake House. He gained worldwide fame as Seong Gi-hun, the protagonist of the Netflix survival drama Squid Game (2021–2025).

Career

Lee was discovered while working in a café and began as a fashion model before turning to acting. After a string of film roles in the 2000s, he enjoyed a commercial resurgence with the heist film The Thieves (2012), the crime drama New World (2013), and the period action film Assassination (2015), several of which rank among the highest-grossing Korean films of their time. He co-founded the management company Artist Company with fellow actor Jung Woo-sung.

Squid Game and international recognition

For Squid Game, Lee won the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama Series in 2022 — the first Asian actor and first actor in a non-English-language series to do so — along with a Screen Actors Guild Award. He subsequently joined the Star Wars franchise as Jedi Master Sol in the Disney+ series The Acolyte (2024).

Directorial debut

Lee made his feature directorial debut with the espionage thriller Hunt (2022), which he also wrote and starred in; it premiered at the Cannes Film Festival and was a box-office success in South Korea.
