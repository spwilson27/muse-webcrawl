Grand Theft Auto Online

Grand Theft Auto Online is an online multiplayer action-adventure game developed by Rockstar North and published by Rockstar Games. It was released on 1 October 2013 for PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360, two weeks after the launch of Grand Theft Auto V, and has since been released for PlayStation 4, Windows, Xbox One, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X/S.

Gameplay

The game is set in the fictional state of San Andreas, shared with Grand Theft Auto V, and supports up to 30 players in a persistent open world. Players create a custom character and undertake jobs, races, adversary modes, cooperative heists, and criminal enterprises such as motorcycle clubs, biker businesses, nightclubs, and agencies. Structured content is delivered through regular free updates, funded in part by Shark Card microtransactions.

Reception and longevity

Launch was hampered by severe server and connectivity problems, but the game became one of the most profitable entertainment products of all time, generating billions of dollars in revenue and sustaining a large player base for more than a decade. Major updates have included The Doomsday Heist, The Diamond Casino & Resort, The Cayo Perico Heist, and Los Santos Drug Wars. A standalone version of Online was released for ninth-generation consoles in 2022.
