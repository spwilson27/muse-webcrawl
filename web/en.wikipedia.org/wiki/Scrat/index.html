Scrat

Scrat is a fictional sabre-toothed squirrel and a central comic character in Blue Sky Studios' Ice Age franchise. Voiced (through squeaks and screams) by director Chris Wedge, Scrat is perpetually obsessed with collecting acorns, repeatedly trying to bury one in the ground — an act that, through slapstick misfortune, tends to trigger cracking ice sheets, volcanic eruptions and continental drift.

Character

Scrat appears in largely wordless sequences that bookend and intercut the main plots of the films. His single-minded pursuit of his acorn serves as a running gag and, in the franchise's cosmology, as a tongue-in-cheek explanation for major geological events: in Ice Age: Continental Drift (2012) his chase literally splits the continents, and in Collision Course (2016) it propels him into space and rearranges the Solar System.

Appearances

Scrat debuted in Ice Age (2002) and returned in every sequel, as well as in the short films Gone Nutty (2002), No Time for Nuts (2006), Scrat: Spaced Out (2016) and the Disney+ series Ice Age: Scrat Tales (2022), in which he discovers he has a son, Baby Scrat. A female counterpart, Scratte, was introduced in Ice Age: Dawn of the Dinosaurs (2009).

Reception

Despite minimal screen time in the early films, Scrat became the franchise's mascot and one of its most popular characters, credited with much of the series' marketing identity.
