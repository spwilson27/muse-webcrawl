John Edward Walsh Jr. (born 26 December 1945) is an American television personality, investigative journalist and victims' rights advocate. He became nationally known after the 1981 abduction and murder of his six-year-old son, Adam Walsh, a crime that transformed him into a campaigner for missing children.

Advocacy

Walsh and his wife Revé helped found the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children in 1984 and campaigned for legislation including the Missing Children's Assistance Act and, later, the Adam Walsh Child Protection and Safety Act of 2006, named for his son. The case remained unsolved until 2008, when authorities named the late Ottis Toole as Adam's killer. The 1983 television film Adam dramatised the family's story.

Television career

From 1988 to 2013 Walsh hosted America's Most Wanted, a programme that profiled fugitives and missing persons and which was credited with aiding the capture of more than a thousand fugitives. He later hosted The Hunt with John Walsh on CNN and In Pursuit with John Walsh on Investigation Discovery, and returned as co-host of a revived America's Most Wanted in 2024 alongside his son Callahan.
