Central Florida Tourism Oversight District

The Central Florida Tourism Oversight District (CFTOD) is the governing jurisdiction and special taxing district for the land of the Walt Disney World Resort in Bay Lake and Lake Buena Vista, Florida. It was created in February 2023 by the Florida Legislature, replacing the Reedy Creek Improvement District, which had governed the area since 1967.

TypeSpecial taxing district
Established27 February 2023

AreaAbout 25,000 acres (39 sq mi)

Background

The district was created after a dispute between Florida Governor Ron DeSantis and the Walt Disney Company, which had publicly opposed the state's Parental Rights in Education Act in 2022. The new law transferred appointment of the district's board of supervisors from landowners — effectively Disney — to the governor of Florida, and renamed the district.

Functions

Like its predecessor, the district provides services normally supplied by local government, including fire protection, emergency medical services, water and wastewater utilities, roads, drainage, and building regulation, funded largely through taxes levied on the land within it, overwhelmingly Walt Disney World.
