Marlene Dietrich

Marie Magdalene "Marlene" Dietrich (27 December 1901 – 6 May 1992) was a German-American actress and singer whose career spanned from the 1910s to the 1980s, famous for The Blue Angel (1930) and her Hollywood films with Josef von Sternberg.

Born27 Dec 1901, Berlin, Germany
Died6 May 1992, Paris, France
OccupationActress, singer

Career

Dietrich's role as Lola Lola in The Blue Angel made her a star; in Hollywood she made Morocco, Shanghai Express, Destry Rides Again, Witness for the Prosecution, and Judgment at Nuremberg. Her androgynous style and contralto voice, notably "Lili Marleen", defined her image.

War and later life

She became a US citizen, refused Nazi overtures, and entertained Allied troops extensively in WWII. Later she performed worldwide as a cabaret singer.
