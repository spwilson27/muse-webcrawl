ESPN

ESPN (originally an initialism for Entertainment and Sports Programming Network) is an American international basic cable sports channel owned by ESPN Inc., a joint venture between The Walt Disney Company (80%) and Hearst Communications (20%). The company was founded in 1979 by Bill Rasmussen along with his son Scott Rasmussen and Ed Eagan. ESPN broadcasts primarily from studio facilities located in Bristol, Connecticut.

Programming

The network's flagship program is SportsCenter, a daily sports news television program that has aired since ESPN's launch on September 7, 1979. ESPN and its sister networks (ESPN2, ESPNU, ESPN Deportes, the SEC Network and ACC Network, among others) hold rights to major properties including Monday Night Football (NFL), the NBA, Major League Baseball, college football and the College Football Playoff, and numerous other sports.

Expansion and streaming

ESPN operates international channels and digital services; ESPN.com launched in 1995, and the subscription streaming service ESPN+ launched in 2018. The brand has been described by many as one of the most valuable in sports media, though the network has faced subscriber declines with cord-cutting and has undergone several rounds of layoffs and restructuring. In 2025 ESPN launched a direct-to-consumer version of its full linear network within the ESPN app.
