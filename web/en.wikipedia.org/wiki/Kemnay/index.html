Kemnay (Scottish Gaelic: Ceann a' Mhaigh) is a village in the Garioch area of Aberdeenshire, Scotland, situated on the River Don about 5 miles southwest of Inverurie and 16 miles northwest of Aberdeen.

History and granite

Kemnay is closely associated with granite quarrying: Kemnay Quarry, opened in the 19th century, supplied stone for major buildings and monuments worldwide, including work in London and overseas, and employed generations of villagers. The village grew around the quarry, the parish church and its position on the Don.

Today

Now largely a commuter village for Aberdeen, Kemnay has a secondary school (Kemnay Academy), golf course and local amenities, while nearby Castle Fraser and the Don riverside walks draw visitors.
