The A2 is a motorway in Switzerland forming the country's principal north–south transalpine axis. It runs about 295 kilometres from Basel at the German border through Lucerne and the Gotthard massif to Chiasso at the Italian border, where it continues as Italy's A9 toward Milan. Most of its length forms part of European route E35, linking Amsterdam with Rome.

Route

From Basel the A2 passes through the Belchen Tunnel beneath the Jura Mountains, then via Sissach and the Härkingen interchange (where it meets the A1) to Lucerne. South of Lucerne it hugs Lake Lucerne through the Seelisberg Tunnel, crosses into the canton of Uri at the Reuss valley, and climbs to Göschenen, the northern portal of the Gotthard Road Tunnel. Emerging at Airolo in Ticino, the road descends through the Leventina valley past Bellinzona and Lugano to the Italian border at Chiasso.

Gotthard Road Tunnel

The route's centrepiece, the Gotthard Road Tunnel, opened in 1980 and at 16.9 km (10.5 mi) is one of the world's longest road tunnels. It operates as a single bidirectional bore, making it a notorious bottleneck at holiday periods; a second tube was approved by Swiss voters in 2016 to allow renovation of the original.
