Testing effect

Testing effect
Field: Cognitive psychology / education
Also called: Retrieval practice effect
Key researchers: Roediger & Karpicke (2006)

The testing effect is the finding that long-term memory is enhanced by actively retrieving information — as in taking a practice test — compared with simply restudying it. It is also called retrieval practice.

Evidence

In Roediger and Karpicke's 2006 experiments, students who were tested on prose passages recalled far more after a week than students who restudied, even without feedback. Meta-analyses confirm robust benefits across ages and materials.

Mechanisms

Proposed explanations include elaborative retrieval, strengthening of retrieval routes, and transfer-appropriate processing. It underlies spaced flashcard systems like Anki.
