A Pravasi Bharatiya Divas convention

Pravasi Bharatiya Divas (Non-Resident Indian Day) is a celebratory day observed in India to mark the contribution of the overseas Indian community to the development of India. It is held on 9 January, commemorating the return of Mahatma Gandhi from South Africa to Mumbai on 9 January 1915.

Convention

Since 2003 a large convention has been organised, first annually and later biennially, hosted by Indian cities including New Delhi, Mumbai, Indore and Bhubaneswar. The event features plenary sessions with government ministers and diaspora leaders, and the presentation of the Pravasi Bharatiya Samman awards to distinguished members of the diaspora.
