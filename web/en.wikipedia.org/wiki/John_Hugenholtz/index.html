Johannes Bernhardus Theodorus "Hans" Hugenholtz (31 October 1914 – 25 March 1995), known in English as John Hugenholtz, was a Dutch designer of motor racing circuits. He was a central figure in Dutch motorsport and designed several circuits used by Formula One.

Career

Hugenholtz was involved in the creation of Circuit Zandvoort in the Netherlands and later designed the Suzuka Circuit in Japan, built in 1962 as a Honda test track. He is also credited with the designs of the Jarama circuit in Spain and work on circuits at Zolder in Belgium, Hockenheim in Germany, and Ontario Motor Speedway in the United States.

Legacy

The hairpin at Zandvoort known as the Hugenholtzbocht (Hugenholtz Corner) is named after him. His son, Hans Hugenholtz Jr., became a racing driver.
