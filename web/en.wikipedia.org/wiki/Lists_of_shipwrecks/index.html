Lists of shipwrecks

The wreck of a vessel on a rocky shoreline

A shipwreck is the remains of a ship destroyed at sea or the event of its destruction, by sinking, running aground, fire, or enemy action. This article indexes the lists of shipwrecks maintained by year, location, and category. It is estimated that there are more than three million shipwrecks worldwide.
