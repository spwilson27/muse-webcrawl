Phosgene

Phosgene is an organic chemical compound with the formula COCl2. It is a toxic, colourless gas; in low concentrations its odour resembles freshly cut hay or grass. It is a valuable industrial building block for the production of polyurethanes and polycarbonates, usually generated and consumed in situ because of its hazards.

Phosgene was used as a chemical weapon during the First World War, where it was responsible for the majority of chemical-weapons deaths, acting as a choking agent that damages the lungs. It is listed under the Chemical Weapons Convention, while remaining an important industrial reagent handled under strict controls.
