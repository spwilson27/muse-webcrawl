Mega Man (character)

Mega Man, known in Japan as Rockman (ロックマン), is the protagonist of the Mega Man video game franchise created by Capcom. Originally a lab assistant robot named Rock, built by Dr Thomas Light, he was converted into a fighting robot to battle the mad scientist Dr Wily and his Robot Masters. Mega Man first appeared in the 1987 Nintendo Entertainment System game Mega Man and has become one of the most recognisable video game characters, as well as Capcom's mascot alongside Ryu.

Character design and abilities

Mega Man is a small android boy with light-blue and cyan armour, a rounded helmet, and his signature weapon, the Mega Buster, an arm cannon that fires charged plasma shots. His Variable Weapons System lets him copy the special weapon of each defeated Robot Master, using it (often with a colour change) for the rest of his mission. He is aided by his robotic dog Rush, his sister Roll, and his "brother" Proto Man, and he follows a strong moral code against harming humans.

Appearances

The character headlines the original "classic" Mega Man series of platform games and has appeared in more than fifty games, spin-offs, and cameos, including the fighting games Marvel vs. Capcom and Nintendo's Super Smash Bros. series. Successor incarnations of the character appear in the Mega Man X, Legends, Battle Network, and Star Force series. Mega Man has starred in animated television series (including the 1994 Ruby-Spears cartoon and 2018's Mega Man: Fully Charged), manga, and an Archie Comics series.

Reception

Mega Man is consistently ranked among the most popular video game heroes, praised for his simple, appealing design by artist Keiji Inafune and for the weapon-copying gameplay he anchors. The notoriously poor North American box art of the first game, depicting a middle-aged man in ill-fitting armour, has itself become a famous in-joke, later referenced in games as "Bad Box Art Mega Man".
