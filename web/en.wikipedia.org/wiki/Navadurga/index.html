Navadurga

Navadurga (Sanskrit: नवदुर्गा, "nine Durgas") are the nine manifestations of the Hindu goddess Durga, worshipped especially during the festival of Navaratri, each on a successive night.

The nine forms

The forms are Shailaputri, Brahmacharini, Chandraghanta, Kushmanda, Skandamata, Katyayani, Kalaratri, Mahagauri, and Siddhidhatri. Each represents a stage of the goddess's life and a particular aspect of strength, devotion and protection. Temples dedicated to the nine forms are found across India and Nepal, including in Varanasi.
