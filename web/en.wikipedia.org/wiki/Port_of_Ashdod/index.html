Port of Ashdod

Port of Ashdod
Location: Ashdod, Israel
Opened: 1965
Operated by: Ashdod Port Company
Type: Deep-water seaport

The Port of Ashdod (Hebrew: נמל אשדוד) is one of Israel's two main cargo ports, located in the city of Ashdod on the Mediterranean coast, about 40 km south of Tel Aviv. Opened in 1965, it handles a large share of Israel's imports and exports, including containers, bulk cargo, vehicles, and passenger traffic.

The port was built to relieve the older Port of Haifa and the shallow port of Jaffa, and it grew with the new city of Ashdod, which was founded in 1956 partly to serve the port project. A major expansion, the Eitan Port, was inaugurated in 2005, adding modern container terminals. A new privately operated facility, the South Port of Ashdod, opened in 2021 adjacent to the existing port.

Operations

Ashdod is a major gateway for cargo to and from central and southern Israel and a hub for cruise ships and ferries. The port has periodically been affected by labour disputes and by congestion, prompting government reforms aimed at increasing competition among Israeli ports.
