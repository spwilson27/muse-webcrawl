Minor League Baseball

A Minor League Baseball game

Minor League Baseball (MiLB) is a hierarchy of professional baseball leagues in the Americas that compete at levels below Major League Baseball (MLB). The system serves primarily as a development pathway for players, umpires and executives, with most teams affiliated to an MLB club.

Structure

Affiliated leagues are classified, from highest to lowest, as Triple-A, Double-A, High-A and Single-A, with rookie-level leagues played at spring training complexes in the Arizona Complex League and Florida Complex League, plus the Dominican Summer League. Each MLB organisation fields affiliates at each level, holding the rights to the players assigned to them.

History

Minor leagues date to the nineteenth century; the modern farm system was pioneered by Branch Rickey with the St. Louis Cardinals in the 1920s and 1930s. A major reorganisation in 2021, led by MLB, reduced the number of affiliated teams to 120 and restructured the leagues under MLB control.
