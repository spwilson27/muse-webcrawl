Coffer

A coffer (from Latin cophinus, a basket) is a chest for storing valuables such as money, jewels or documents, typically strongly made and lockable. By extension, "coffers" commonly denotes the treasury or funds of an organisation or state.

Related uses

In architecture, a coffer is a sunken panel in a ceiling, vault or dome, as in the Pantheon in Rome; such panels reduce weight and decorate the surface. In civil engineering, a cofferdam is a temporary watertight enclosure used for construction in water — a related term derived from the same root idea of a box.
