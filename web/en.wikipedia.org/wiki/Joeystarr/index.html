Joeystarr

Born: Didier Morville, 27 October 1967, Saint-Denis, France

Occupation: Rapper, actor, producer

Associated acts: Suprême NTM

Joeystarr (born Didier Morville, 27 October 1967), also spelled Joey Starr, is a French rapper, record producer and actor from Saint-Denis, in the northern suburbs of Paris. He co-founded the hardcore rap group Suprême NTM in 1989 with Kool Shen.

Career

NTM became one of the most important groups in French hip hop, known for confrontational songs about police violence and life in the banlieues, including "Police" and "Seine-Saint-Denis Style". The group also repeatedly clashed with authorities, receiving fines and bans for anti-police lyrics. After NTM's split, Joeystarr released the solo albums Gare au jaguar (2006) and Egomaniac (2011); the group reunited for acclaimed tours in 2008 and 2018–2019.

Acting

As an actor he has appeared in films including La Haine (in a minor role), Polisse (2011), for which he received a César nomination, and Le Bal des actrices. His gravelly voice and screen presence have made him a regular presence in French cinema and television.
