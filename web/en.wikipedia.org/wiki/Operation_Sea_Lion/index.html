Operation Sea Lion

Operation Sea Lion (German: Unternehmen Seelöwe) was Nazi Germany's planned invasion of the United Kingdom during the Second World War, prepared in 1940 after the fall of France. Hitler issued Directive No. 16 on 16 July 1940 ordering preparations for a landing across a broad front on England's south coast.

The plan required the Luftwaffe to gain air superiority over the Royal Air Force — the objective of the Battle of Britain — and the Kriegsmarine to clear minefields and protect a fleet largely of improvised river barges. German commanders doubted its feasibility given British naval strength.

After the Luftwaffe failed to defeat Fighter Command, the operation was postponed indefinitely on 17 September 1940 and effectively abandoned as Germany turned east toward the invasion of the Soviet Union. Historians generally consider that, had it been attempted, Sea Lion would have faced severe logistical and naval difficulties.
