The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS) is an American nonprofit organization funded by auto insurance companies, founded in 1959 and based in Arlington, Virginia. It works to reduce deaths, injuries, and property damage from motor vehicle crashes through research, consumer information, and advocacy.

Crash testing

The IIHS conducts its own crash tests at its Vehicle Research Center in Ruckersville, Virginia, including moderate overlap front, small overlap front (driver and passenger side), side impact, roof strength, and head restraint tests. It also evaluates headlights and front crash prevention systems such as automatic emergency braking and pedestrian detection.

Ratings

Vehicles receive ratings of Good, Acceptable, Marginal, or Poor. The IIHS awards Top Safety Pick and Top Safety Pick+ designations to the best performers; award criteria have been progressively tightened over the years, which manufacturers credit with driving improvements such as stronger small-overlap crash performance and better headlights.

Research

The affiliated Highway Loss Data Institute (HLDI) publishes insurance loss statistics by vehicle model. IIHS research covers driver behavior, alcohol-impaired driving, teen drivers, speed, red-light cameras, and roadway design.
