Renold plc

Renold plc is a British engineering company and a leading international manufacturer of industrial chains and related power transmission products. The company is based in Wythenshawe, Manchester, England, and is listed on the London Stock Exchange.

The company was founded in 1879 by Hans Renold, a Swiss-born engineer who had settled in Manchester. Renold is credited with inventing the bush roller chain in 1880, a design that became the basis of the modern roller chain used in bicycles, motorcycles, and industrial machinery worldwide.

History

Hans Renold established his business in 1879 with a small loan, purchasing an existing chain-making business in Salford. His patent for the bush roller chain in 1880 transformed chain performance and durability. In 1885, Renold granted free use of his bicycle chain patent to the bicycle industry, helping to standardise the design. The company expanded into roller chains for industrial applications and, in the early twentieth century, merged interests with Coventry Chain and other manufacturers. Later acquisitions included the Holroyd gear business.

Operations

Renold operates in two principal divisions: Renold Chain, which produces transmission, conveyor and leaf chains, and Torques and Couplings products. The group has manufacturing and sales operations across Europe, the Americas, Australasia and Asia, supplying industries including materials handling, food processing, mining, and agriculture.
