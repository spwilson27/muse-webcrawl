Corvette Stingray (concept car)

The Corvette Stingray concept car is a concept car created by Chevrolet to celebrate the 50th anniversary of the Stingray nameplate. It debuted at the 2009 Chicago Auto Show, blending design cues from the original 1959 Sting Ray racer and the 1963 Sting Ray coupe — including a split rear window, side "gills", scissor doors and a clamshell hood — with a futuristic interior featuring interactive touch-screen controls, a rear-view camera in place of mirrors, and a hybrid drivetrain concept.

In media

The car gained wide exposure playing the character Sideswipe in the film Transformers: Revenge of the Fallen (2009), and appeared again in Transformers: Dark of the Moon (2011) — in the latter as a convertible version. Although Chevrolet stated the concept was not intended for production, several of its styling ideas influenced the eventual seventh-generation Corvette Stingray (C7) launched in 2014.
