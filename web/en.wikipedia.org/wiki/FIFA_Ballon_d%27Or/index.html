FIFA Ballon d'Or

Presented byFIFA and France Football
First awarded2010
Last awarded2015

The FIFA Ballon d'Or was an annual association football award presented by FIFA between 2010 and 2015 to the world's best-performing male player. It was created by merging France Football's Ballon d'Or with the FIFA World Player of the Year award under a partnership between the two organisations.

History

The combined award was voted on by national team coaches and captains together with international journalists. Lionel Messi won the first three editions (2010–2012) and again in 2015; Cristiano Ronaldo won in 2013 and 2014. A parallel FIFA Women's World Player award was presented at the same gala.

Aftermath

When the partnership expired in 2016, the awards separated again: France Football revived the standalone Ballon d'Or, while FIFA created The Best FIFA Men's Player award.
