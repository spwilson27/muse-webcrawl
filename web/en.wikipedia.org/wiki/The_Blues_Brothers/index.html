The Blues Brothers

The Blues Brothers is a 1980 American musical action comedy film directed by John Landis, starring John Belushi as "Joliet" Jake Blues and Dan Aykroyd as Elwood Blues. Developed from a recurring Saturday Night Live musical sketch, it features performances by Aretha Franklin, James Brown, Ray Charles, Cab Calloway and John Lee Hooker.

Plot

Released from prison, Jake Blues and his brother Elwood learn that the Catholic orphanage where they grew up will be closed unless $5,000 in back taxes is paid. On a "mission from God", they reunite their R&B band to raise the money, pursued across Illinois by police, neo-Nazis, a country band and a vengeful mystery woman.

Music and legacy

The soundtrack album was a major hit and helped revive interest in blues, soul and R&B. The film became a cult classic and was selected for the National Film Registry in 2020. A sequel, Blues Brothers 2000, was released in 1998.
