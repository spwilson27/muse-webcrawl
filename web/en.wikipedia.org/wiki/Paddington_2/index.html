Paddington 2 is a 2017 live-action/animated family comedy film directed by Paul King and written by King, Simon Farnaby and Jon Croker. A sequel to Paddington (2014), it is based on the stories of Paddington Bear, created by Michael Bond. Ben Whishaw voices Paddington, with Hugh Bonneville, Sally Hawkins, Brendan Gleeson, Julie Walters, Jim Broadbent, Peter Capaldi and Hugh Grant.

Plot

Now settled with the Brown family in Windsor Gardens, Paddington works odd jobs to save for a rare pop-up book of London in Samuel Gruber's antique shop, hoping to send it to his Aunt Lucy in Peru for her 100th birthday. The book is stolen by Phoenix Buchanan, a washed-up actor and master of disguise, who uses it to hunt for a hidden treasure. Paddington is wrongly convicted of the theft and sent to prison, where he befriends the inmates — including the gruff prison cook Knuckles McGinty — by spreading the influence of marmalade. The Browns work to clear his name, and Paddington escapes prison in time to help stop Buchanan and rescue the treasure; the book is finally sent to Aunt Lucy.

Production

Development of a sequel began before the release of the first film. Filming took place in London and at Warner Bros. Studios, Leavesden. Hugh Grant's character, Phoenix Buchanan, was written with him in mind, and the film was dedicated to Michael Bond, who died in June 2017, shortly before release.

Reception

Paddington 2 premiered on 5 November 2017 and was released in the United Kingdom on 10 November. It grossed $227.9 million worldwide. The film received widespread critical acclaim; on the review aggregator Rotten Tomatoes it held a 100% approval rating for an extended period, making it one of the best-reviewed films on the site. Critics praised its warmth, visual inventiveness and Grant's performance. It was nominated for three BAFTA Awards, including Outstanding British Film.
