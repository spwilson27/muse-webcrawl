West Ham United Football Club is a professional football club based in Stratford, East London, England. Founded in 1895 as Thames Ironworks, the club reformed as West Ham United in 1900 and competes in the Premier League.

History

West Ham played at the Boleyn Ground (Upton Park) from 1904 until 2016, when the club moved to the London Stadium, built for the 2012 Olympics. The club won the FA Cup in 1964, 1975 and 1980 and the European Cup Winners' Cup in 1965. Three West Ham players — captain Bobby Moore, Geoff Hurst and Martin Peters — were key members of England's 1966 World Cup-winning team.

Recent years

Under manager David Moyes, West Ham won the UEFA Europa Conference League in 2023, defeating Fiorentina in the final in Prague — the club's first major trophy in 43 years.
