Phoenix Cinema

The Phoenix Cinema is a historic independent cinema in East Finchley, London. Opened in 1912 as the East Finchley Picturedrome, it is one of the oldest continuously operating cinemas in the United Kingdom.

Location52 High Road, East Finchley, London
Opened1912
TypeIndependent cinema

History

The building is noted for its Art Deco auditorium, created in a 1938 refurbishment, with a single screen and seating for about 260. Threatened with demolition in the 1980s, it was saved by a local campaign and is now run by a charitable trust.

Programme

The Phoenix shows arthouse, foreign-language, and classic films alongside mainstream releases, and hosts film courses and community events.
