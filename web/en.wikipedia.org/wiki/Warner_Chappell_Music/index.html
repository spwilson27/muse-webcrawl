Warner Chappell Music, Inc. is the music publishing arm of Warner Music Group and one of the largest music publishers in the world, administering copyrights in more than a million compositions by songwriters across nearly every genre. Its roots lie in the London firm Chappell & Co., founded in 1811.

Warner Chappell Music logo

History

Chappell & Co. began as a London music shop and publisher, expanding into piano manufacture and concert promotion, and built a celebrated catalogue of British musical theatre and light music. In 1987, Warner Communications acquired Chappell and merged it with Warner Bros. Music, creating Warner/Chappell Music, for many years led by chairman Les Bider. The company subsequently passed through the Time Warner breakup into the independent Warner Music Group.

Activities

Warner Chappell signs and develops songwriters, licenses compositions for film, television, advertising, and streaming, and collects performance and mechanical royalties worldwide. Its roster and catalogue have included works associated with artists and writers such as Radiohead, Bruno Mars, Lizzo, and George Gershwin, as well as the Warner Bros. film score catalogues. For decades the company owned the rights to "Happy Birthday to You", until a 2015 U.S. court ruling held its copyright claim invalid.
