David Pratt (assassin)

Born1 October 1908, Scotland
Died1 October 1961, Kamfers Dam asylum, South Africa

David Beresford Pratt (1 October 1908 – 1 October 1961) was a South African businessman who attempted to assassinate Prime Minister Hendrik Verwoerd, the architect of apartheid, on 9 April 1960.

The shooting

Pratt, a wealthy farmer and businessman of British descent from the Magaliesberg area, shot Verwoerd twice in the head at point-blank range with a .22 pistol as the prime minister opened the Rand Easter Show in Johannesburg, days after the Sharpeville massacre. Verwoerd remarkably survived and recovered. Pratt said he had shot "the epitome of apartheid" and claimed he wanted to kill apartheid itself rather than the man.

Aftermath

The court found Pratt suffered from epilepsy and mental illness; he was declared a State President's patient and confined to mental institutions rather than tried. He died by suicide on his 53rd birthday while in custody at an asylum near Kimberley. Verwoerd was later assassinated in 1966 by Dimitri Tsafendas.
