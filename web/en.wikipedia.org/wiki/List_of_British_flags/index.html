List of British flags

This is a list of flags that have been used in the United Kingdom, British colonies and the former British Empire. The national flag of the United Kingdom is the Union Jack (Union Flag), combining the crosses of St George (England), St Andrew (Scotland) and St Patrick (Ireland).

National flags

FlagUse
Union JackNational flag of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland (adopted 1801).
Flag of England (Cross of St George)Red cross on a white field; national flag of England.
Flag of Scotland (Saltire)White diagonal cross on a blue field; national flag of Scotland.
Flag of Wales (Y Ddraig Goch)A red dragon on a green and white field; national flag of Wales.
Northern IrelandHas no official national flag; the Ulster Banner was used by the former Government of Northern Ireland (1953–1973).

Ensigns

FlagUse
Red EnsignCivil ensign, flown by British merchant ships; the basis of many Commonwealth and colonial flags.
Blue EnsignFlown by government vessels and used, defaced, by many government departments and overseas territories.
White EnsignEnsign of the Royal Navy, also flown by the Royal Yacht Squadron.
RAF EnsignSky-blue ensign with the Union Flag in the canton and the RAF roundel in the fly.
Civil Air EnsignFlown at civil airports and on British civil aircraft.

Royal standards

  • Royal Standard of the United Kingdom — the banner of the monarch, quartering the arms of England, Scotland and Ireland; a distinct version is used in Scotland.
  • Standards are also borne by other members of the Royal Family, derived from the royal arms with heraldic labels of difference.

Crown Dependencies

  • Isle of Man — a red flag bearing the triskelion of three armoured legs.
  • Jersey — a red saltire on white with the arms of Jersey and the Plantagenet crown.
  • Guernsey — a red cross with a gold cross within it, on white.
