List of United States secretaries of state

The United States secretary of state is the head of the United States Department of State and the principal foreign affairs adviser to the president. The secretary is the highest-ranking member of the Cabinet and fourth in the presidential line of succession. Thomas Jefferson was the first secretary of state, appointed by George Washington in 1790.

Six secretaries of state later became president: Jefferson, Madison, Monroe, John Quincy Adams, Van Buren and Buchanan. Madeleine Albright was the first woman to hold the office; Colin Powell the first African American; Condoleezza Rice the first African American woman.
