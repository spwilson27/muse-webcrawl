Paul Samuel Feig (born September 17, 1962) is an American film director, producer, screenwriter, and actor. He is best known for creating the cult television series Freaks and Geeks (1999–2000) and for directing hit comedy films including Bridesmaids (2011), The Heat (2013), and Spy (2015).

Career

Born in Royal Oak, Michigan, Feig began as a stand-up comedian and actor before moving into writing and directing. He directed episodes of series such as Arrested Development, The Office, and Weeds. His later films include the all-female Ghostbusters reboot (2016), the thriller A Simple Favor (2018), the romantic comedy Last Christmas (2019), and The School for Good and Evil (2022).

Feig frequently collaborates with Melissa McCarthy, and is known on set for his trademark three-piece suits. See also Freaks and Geeks and Bridesmaids.
