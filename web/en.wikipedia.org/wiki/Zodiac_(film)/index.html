Zodiac is a 2007 American mystery crime thriller film directed by David Fincher from a screenplay by James Vanderbilt, based on the nonfiction books by San Francisco Chronicle cartoonist Robert Graysmith about the hunt for the Zodiac Killer, who terrorised Northern California in the late 1960s and early 1970s. It stars Jake Gyllenhaal as Graysmith, Mark Ruffalo as inspector Dave Toschi, and Robert Downey Jr. as reporter Paul Avery.

Plot and approach

Rather than a conventional whodunit, the film is a meticulous procedural tracing the killer's murders and taunting letters, the police investigation across multiple jurisdictions, and the growing obsession of Graysmith, whose research eventually centres on suspect Arthur Leigh Allen. The case remains officially unsolved, and the film ends without resolution — a deliberate reflection of its theme of obsession.

Reception and legacy

Zodiac underperformed commercially on release but was named by many critics among the best films of 2007, and its reputation has grown steadily; it is now frequently ranked among the finest films of the 21st century and of Fincher's career. It was among the first major features shot largely on digital cameras (the Thomson Viper), enabling Fincher's famously exacting, many-take precision.
