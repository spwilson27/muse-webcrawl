1993 Cannes Film Festival

Dates13–24 May 1993
LocationCannes, France
Jury presidentLouis Malle
Palme d'OrFarewell My Concubine; The Piano

The 1993 Cannes Film Festival was held from 13 to 24 May 1993. For only the second time in the festival's history, the Palme d'Or was shared: by Chen Kaige's Farewell My Concubine and Jane Campion's The Piano.

Highlights

Campion became the first woman to win the Palme d'Or. The Piano also earned Holly Hunter the Best Actress award. Mike Leigh won Best Director for Naked, whose star David Thewlis won Best Actor. Other films in competition included Falling Down, The Puppetmaster, and Much Ado About Nothing.

Significance

The joint Palme made 1993 one of the most celebrated editions of the festival, with both winning films going on to wide international acclaim.
