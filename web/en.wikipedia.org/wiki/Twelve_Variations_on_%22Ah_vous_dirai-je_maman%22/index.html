Twelve Variations on "Ah vous dirai-je maman"

The Twelve Variations on "Ah vous dirai-je maman", K. 265/300e, is a piano composition by Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, composed when he was around 25 years old and published in Vienna in 1785. It consists of a theme and twelve variations for solo piano.

The theme

The melody is the French folk song "Ah! vous dirai-je, maman", best known in the English-speaking world as the tune of "Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star", "Baa, Baa, Black Sheep" and the "Alphabet Song". Mozart's set explores the simple melody through increasingly virtuosic figuration, changes of mode and character, including a slow minor-key variation and a brilliant finale.

Dating

Long believed to have been written in Paris in 1778, paper and handwriting studies have led scholars to date the work to Vienna around 1781–82.
