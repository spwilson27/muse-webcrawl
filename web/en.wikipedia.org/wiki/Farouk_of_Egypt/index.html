Farouk of Egypt

King of Egypt and the Sudan
Reign: 28 April 1936 – 26 July 1952
Predecessor: Fuad I
Successor: Fuad II
Born: 11 February 1920, Cairo
Died: 18 March 1965, Rome

Farouk I (11 February 1920 – 18 March 1965) was the tenth ruler of Egypt from the Muhammad Ali dynasty and the penultimate King of Egypt and the Sudan, reigning from 1936 until his abdication in 1952 following the Egyptian revolution of that year.

Early life and accession

Farouk was the only son of King Fuad I and his second wife, Nazli Sabri. He was educated in Egypt and briefly at the Royal Military Academy, Woolwich, in England. He succeeded his father on 28 April 1936 at the age of sixteen; a regency council governed until he assumed full powers at his coronation in July 1937. Initially popular, the young king was seen by many Egyptians as a nationalist counterweight to British influence, which remained dominant under the Anglo-Egyptian Treaty of 1936.

Reign

During the Second World War, Egypt was a battleground and a vast Allied base. In the Abdeen Palace incident of February 1942, British forces surrounded the palace and compelled Farouk to appoint a pro-British government under Mustafa el-Nahhas, a humiliation that damaged the monarchy's prestige. After the war, corruption, the lavish lifestyle of the king, and Egypt's defeat in the 1948 Arab–Israeli War steadily eroded support for the regime. Farouk married Narriman Sadek in 1951, and their son, the future Fuad II, was born in January 1952, days before the Cairo Fire riots.

Overthrow and exile

On 23 July 1952 the Free Officers Movement led by Mohamed Naguib and Gamal Abdel Nasser seized power in a coup. Farouk abdicated on 26 July in favour of his infant son and sailed from Alexandria aboard the royal yacht. The monarchy was abolished the following year and Egypt declared a republic. Farouk lived in exile in Italy, renowned for his appetites and collections; much of his vast accumulation of coins, watches, and other treasures was auctioned by the Egyptian state in 1954. He died in Rome in 1965 after collapsing at a restaurant table.
