Eleni Kounalakis (née Tsakopoulos; born March 3, 1966) is an American politician, businesswoman, and former diplomat serving as the 50th lieutenant governor of California since 2019. A member of the Democratic Party, she is the first woman elected to the office. Kounalakis previously served as the United States ambassador to Hungary from 2010 to 2013 under President Barack Obama.

Early life and business career

Kounalakis was born in Sacramento to Greek immigrant parents; her father, Angelo Tsakopoulos, founded the Sacramento land-development firm AKT Development. She earned a bachelor's degree from Dartmouth College and an MBA from the Haas School of Business at the University of California, Berkeley. Before entering public service, she spent nearly two decades working in the family real-estate development business, including as president of AKT Development, and was active in Democratic politics and philanthropy, serving multiple times as a delegate to Democratic National Conventions.

Ambassador to Hungary

Nominated by President Obama in 2009 and sworn in on January 11, 2010, Kounalakis served as ambassador to Hungary until July 2013. Her memoir, Madam Ambassador: Three Years of Diplomacy, Dinner Parties and a Demonstration in Budapest (2015), recounts her tenure, including her experience of political change under the government of Viktor Orbán.

Lieutenant Governor of California

Kounalakis announced her candidacy for lieutenant governor in 2017 and won the November 2018 general election, taking office on January 7, 2019 alongside Governor Gavin Newsom. She was re-elected in 2022. As lieutenant governor she sits on the boards of the University of California and California State University systems, chairs the California Commission for Economic Development, and has worked on housing affordability, homelessness, and trade missions. She later announced a campaign for governor of California.
