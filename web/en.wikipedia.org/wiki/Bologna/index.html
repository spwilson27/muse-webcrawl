Bologna

Bologna
Region: Emilia-Romagna, Italy
Population: ~390,000 (city)
Known for: University of Bologna (founded 1088)

Bologna is the capital and largest city of the Emilia-Romagna region in northern Italy. It is home to the University of Bologna, founded in 1088 and the oldest university in continuous operation in the world, and is renowned for its medieval towers, extensive porticoes and cuisine.

History

Founded by the Etruscans as Felsina and later the Roman colony of Bononia, Bologna became a free commune in the Middle Ages and a centre of legal study. Its skyline was once filled with perhaps 180 family towers; the Asinelli and Garisenda towers remain its symbols.

Culture and cuisine

The city's porticoes were inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2021. Bologna gives its name to ragù alla bolognese, served with tagliatelle, and to mortadella. Nicknames include "La Dotta" (the learned), "La Grassa" (the fat) and "La Rossa" (the red). See also Emilia-Romagna.
