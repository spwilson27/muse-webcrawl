87North Productions is an American film and television production company founded in 2019 by director and former stuntman David Leitch and producer Kelly McCormick. The company specializes in action films, drawing on Leitch's background in stunt coordination and second-unit direction, and grew out of the creative team behind the John Wick franchise.

Background

Leitch, a co-founder of the stunt design company 87eleven Action Design, formed 87North to develop star-driven action films with an emphasis on practical stunt work. The company's name echoes the latitude-inspired branding of the earlier 87eleven venture.
