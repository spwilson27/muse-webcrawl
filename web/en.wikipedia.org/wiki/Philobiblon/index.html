Philobiblon

The Philobiblon is a Latin prose work traditionally attributed to the English bishop and statesman Richard de Bury (1287–1345), completed in 1344. It is one of the earliest and best-known treatises on the love of books — bibliophily — and on the care and collecting of libraries.

AuthorRichard de Bury (attributed)
LanguageLatin
Date1344

Contents

Across twenty chapters, the Philobiblon praises the wisdom stored in books, laments their neglect and destruction in war, defends the collecting of a great library, and sets out plans for lending books to scholars — De Bury intended his collection to form the basis of a library at Durham College, Oxford.

Legacy

The work remained in manuscript until its first printing at Cologne in 1473 and has been translated many times; it is regarded as a foundational text of librarianship and book collecting.
