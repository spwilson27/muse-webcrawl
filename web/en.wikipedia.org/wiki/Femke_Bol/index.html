Femke Bol

Born23 February 2000, Amersfoort, Netherlands
SportTrack and field
Events400 m hurdles, 400 m

Femke Bol (born 23 February 2000) is a Dutch track and field athlete who specialises in the 400 metres hurdles and the flat 400 metres. She is one of the fastest women in history over the hurdles, holding the European record, and won the gold medal in the mixed 4 × 400 metres relay at the 2024 Paris Olympics with a celebrated final-leg comeback.

Career

Bol emerged internationally as a teenager, winning European junior titles before breaking through at senior level in 2020. At the Tokyo Olympics in 2021 she took bronze in the 400 m hurdles behind Sydney McLaughlin and Dalilah Muhammad, setting a European record. In February 2023 she broke the long-standing world indoor 400 m record, running 49.26 seconds, and that summer became world champion in the 400 m hurdles in Budapest, also anchoring the Dutch women's 4 × 400 m relay to gold. In March 2024 she lowered her own world indoor record to 49.17.

Personal bests

Her outdoor 400 m hurdles best of 50.95 seconds, set in 2024, made her only the second woman ever to break 51 seconds. She is also a multiple European champion indoors and outdoors and a central figure in the strong Dutch relay squads of the 2020s.
