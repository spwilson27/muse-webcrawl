X Corp. is an American technology company established by Elon Musk in 2023 as the successor to Twitter, Inc., and the operator of the social networking service X (formerly Twitter). The company was formed as part of Musk's acquisition of Twitter, completed in October 2022 for about $44 billion.

History

X Corp. was incorporated in Nevada on March 9, 2023. In April 2023, Twitter, Inc. ceased to exist as a separate entity after merging into X Corp., which became the operator of the platform and a wholly owned subsidiary of X Holdings Corp. In July 2023, Musk announced the rebranding of Twitter to X, retiring the bird logo in favor of a stylized X. In March 2025, Musk announced that his artificial intelligence company xAI had acquired X Corp. in an all-stock transaction valuing the social media company at about $33 billion.

Operations

The company operates the X platform, which it has been extending beyond microblogging toward Musk's stated ambition of an "everything app," including longer-form video, payments ambitions, and the integration of the Grok chatbot developed by xAI. Its headquarters moved from San Francisco to Texas following the acquisition and rebrand.
