List of poker hands

In poker, players form sets of five playing cards, called hands, according to the rules of the game. Each hand has a rank, which is compared against the ranks of other hands participating in the showdown to decide who wins the pot. In high games, such as Texas hold 'em and seven-card stud, the highest-ranking hands win; in low games, the lowest ranks win. This article lists the standard five-card hand categories from highest to lowest, with their probabilities in a five-card draw from a 52-card deck.

Tie-breaking

When two hands fall in the same category, the ranks of the individual cards decide the winner: for example, a pair of aces beats a pair of kings, and a full house is compared first by the rank of its three of a kind. Aces may count low in a "wheel" straight (5-4-3-2-A), which is the lowest straight. Suits are not ranked in most standard games, so identical hands in different suits tie and split the pot.
