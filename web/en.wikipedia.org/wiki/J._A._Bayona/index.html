Notable worksThe Orphanage, The Impossible, A Monster Calls, Society of the Snow

Juan Antonio García Bayona (born 9 May 1975), known professionally as J. A. Bayona, is a Spanish film director. He first gained recognition with his feature debut, the horror film The Orphanage (2007), presented by Guillermo del Toro and a major critical and commercial success in Spain.

His English-language debut, The Impossible (2012), dramatised a family's survival of the 2004 Indian Ocean tsunami and earned Naomi Watts an Academy Award nomination for Best Actress. He followed it with the fantasy drama A Monster Calls (2016), adapted from Patrick Ness's novel.

In 2018 Bayona directed Jurassic World: Fallen Kingdom, the fifth instalment of the Jurassic Park franchise, which grossed over $1.3 billion worldwide. He subsequently directed the first two episodes of Amazon's The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power (2022).

His 2023 film Society of the Snow, about the 1972 Andes flight disaster, premiered at the Venice Film Festival, won twelve Goya Awards including Best Film and Best Director, and was nominated for the Academy Award for Best International Feature Film.

Style

Bayona's work frequently blends genre spectacle — horror, disaster and fantasy — with intimate emotional stories of families and children facing loss. He began his career directing music videos and studied at the Cinema and Audiovisual School of Catalonia (ESCAC).
