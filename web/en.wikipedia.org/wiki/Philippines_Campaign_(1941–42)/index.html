Philippines Campaign (1941–42)

The Philippines Campaign (Filipino: Kampanya sa Pilipinas), also known as the Battle of the Philippines, was the invasion of the Commonwealth of the Philippines by the Empire of Japan and the defence of the islands by United States and Filipino forces during the Pacific War. It lasted from 8 December 1941 to 8 May 1942.

Japanese forces landed on Luzon and other islands shortly after the attack on Pearl Harbor. Defenders under General Douglas MacArthur withdrew to the Bataan Peninsula and the island fortress of Corregidor. Bataan surrendered on 9 April 1942, followed by the Bataan Death March; Corregidor fell on 6 May. The defeat led to the Japanese occupation of the Philippines until 1944–45.
