Arsène Lupin III is a fictional character created by the Japanese manga artist Monkey Punch, and the protagonist of the Lupin the Third franchise, which began as a manga in 1967. The grandson of Maurice Leblanc's gentleman thief Arsène Lupin, Lupin III is presented as the world's most wanted thief, perpetually pursued by ICPO Inspector Koichi Zenigata.


Lupin III, the gentleman thief

Character

Lupin is brilliant, cheerful and lecherous, announcing his thefts in advance with calling cards and escaping through ingenuity and gadgetry. He is usually accompanied by the marksman Daisuke Jigen and the swordsman Goemon Ishikawa XIII, and alternately aided and betrayed by the opportunist Fujiko Mine.

Media

The franchise spans several television anime series (beginning in 1971), numerous television specials, and films including Hayao Miyazaki's feature directorial debut The Castle of Cagliostro (1979), widely regarded as a classic of anime cinema. Lupin's character design — most famously his coloured jackets, which change between series — has made him an enduring icon of Japanese popular culture.

Namesake

Monkey Punch used the Lupin name without the Leblanc estate's permission; after years of dispute, the character is credited in some overseas releases simply as "Lupin" or under alternate names, though the franchise now uses the full name internationally.
