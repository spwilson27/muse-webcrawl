Novak Djokovic (born 22 May 1987) is a Serbian professional tennis player. He has been ranked world No. 1 for a record 428 weeks and has won 24 Grand Slam men's singles titles — the most in history — including a record ten Australian Open titles. By most statistical measures he is considered the greatest tennis player of all time.

The third member of the "Big Three" alongside Roger Federer and Rafael Nadal, Djokovic emerged with the 2008 Australian Open, then dominated the 2010s: his 2011 season (70–6, three majors) and his 2015 season are ranked among the finest in the sport's history. He has completed the career Grand Slam three times over, held all four majors simultaneously in 2015–16, and won every Masters 1000 event at least twice. In 2023 he won his 23rd and 24th majors (Paris and New York), passing Nadal, and in August 2024, at 37, he defeated Carlos Alcaraz to win Olympic gold in Paris — completing the career "Golden Slam" and the last major prize missing from his résumé.

Playing style

Djokovic is famed for the best return of serve and two-handed backhand in the game, extraordinary flexibility and defensive retrieval, and mental resilience in deciding sets. His rivalry with Nadal produced 60 meetings, the most in the Open Era men's game.
