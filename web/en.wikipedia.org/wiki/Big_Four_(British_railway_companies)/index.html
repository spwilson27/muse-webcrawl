Big Four (British railway companies)

Period1923–1947
CompaniesGWR, LMS, LNER, SR
Succeeded byBritish Railways

The Big Four were the four largest railway companies in the United Kingdom, created by the Railways Act 1921 (the "Grouping") and operating from 1923 until nationalisation in 1948.

The companies

They were the Great Western Railway (GWR), the London, Midland and Scottish Railway (LMS), the London and North Eastern Railway (LNER) and the Southern Railway (SR). Nearly all of Britain's pre-grouping companies were merged into one of the four.

Legacy

The Big Four era produced famous locomotives and expresses such as the LNER's Flying Scotsman and Mallard. On 1 January 1948 the four were nationalised into British Railways.
