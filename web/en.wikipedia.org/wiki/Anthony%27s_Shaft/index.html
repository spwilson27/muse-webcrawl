Anthony's Shaft (in Romanian, Puțul Aninoasa or similar local usage) is a historic coal-mining shaft in the Jiu Valley mining district of Hunedoara County, Romania, associated with the Aninoasa mine near the town of Aninoasa.

Background

The Jiu Valley developed in the later 19th and early 20th centuries into Romania's principal coalfield, and its deep shafts were named and organised as production units of the valley's mining companies. Mining declined sharply after 1989 as the industry contracted.

Note

Anthony's Shaft is one of numerous named shafts of the Jiu Valley coalfield; documentation of individual shafts is sparse and largely local.
