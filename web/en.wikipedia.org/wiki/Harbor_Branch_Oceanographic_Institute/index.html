Harbor Branch Oceanographic Institute (HBOI or Harbor Branch) is a non-profit oceanographic institution now part of Florida Atlantic University, located on a campus along the Indian River Lagoon in Fort Pierce, Florida, United States.

History

The institute was founded in 1971 by J. Seward Johnson Sr. in collaboration with engineer and aquanaut Edwin Link. It became known for its Johnson Sea Link manned submersibles, used in deep-sea research and in recovery operations following the Space Shuttle Challenger disaster. In 2007 Harbor Branch merged with Florida Atlantic University.

Research

Research areas include marine ecosystem health, aquaculture, marine mammal rescue and rehabilitation, ocean exploration, and marine biomedical research, including the search for compounds from marine organisms with pharmaceutical potential.
