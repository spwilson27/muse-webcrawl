Jacksonville, Florida

Jacksonville is the most populous city in Florida and the largest city by area in the contiguous United States as of 2020. It is the seat of Duval County, with which the city government consolidated in 1968, giving Jacksonville its great size.

Geography

Jacksonville is in Northeast Florida on the banks of the St. Johns River, about 25 miles south of the Georgia state line and near the Atlantic coast. Its port, JAXPORT, is a major deepwater port.

History

The area was inhabited by the Timucua people; French Fort Caroline (1564) was one of the earliest European settlements in the present-day US. The city, founded in 1822, was named after Andrew Jackson. The Great Fire of 1901 destroyed much of downtown, which was rebuilt; Jacksonville later became a centre for banking, insurance, logistics and the military, with major naval stations nearby.

Economy and sports

The city is home to the Jacksonville Jaguars of the NFL and hosts the annual Florida–Georgia college football game.
