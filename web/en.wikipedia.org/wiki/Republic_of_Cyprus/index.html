The Republic of Cyprus is an island country in the eastern Mediterranean Sea, south of Turkey and west of Syria and Lebanon. It is the third largest and third most populous island in the Mediterranean. Its capital and largest city is Nicosia. Cyprus gained independence from the United Kingdom in 1960 and joined the European Union in 2004, adopting the euro in 2008.

Division

Since 1974 the island has been divided following a coup d'état by Greek Cypriot nationalists seeking union with Greece and the subsequent Turkish military intervention. The northern third is administered by the self-declared Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus, recognized only by Turkey, while a UN buffer zone (the Green Line) separates the two areas. Two British Sovereign Base Areas, Akrotiri and Dhekelia, remain under UK control.

Economy

The economy of the government-controlled area is dominated by services, particularly tourism, shipping, financial services, and real estate, with significant offshore natural gas discoveries in its exclusive economic zone.
