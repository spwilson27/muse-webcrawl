Table football

Table football, known as foosball in North America, is a table-top game loosely based on association football. Players manipulate rods mounted with miniature player figures to strike a ball into the opponent's goal.

History

Patents for table football games appeared in Europe in the 1890s and 1920s, and the game became especially popular in Germany, France, and the United States. Competitive play is governed internationally by the International Table Soccer Federation.
