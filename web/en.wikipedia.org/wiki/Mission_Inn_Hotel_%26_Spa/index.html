The Mission Inn Hotel & Spa is a historic hotel in downtown Riverside, California. Occupying an entire city block, it is famous for its extravagant Mission Revival and Spanish Revival architecture — an eccentric assemblage of cloisters, rotundas, flying buttresses, catacombs, towers, and a chapel-like wedding venue, built up over decades by innkeeper Frank Augustus Miller.

History

The inn began in 1876 as a modest adobe boarding house run by the Miller family. Frank Miller expanded it continuously from 1902 into the 1930s, adding wings designed by architects including G. Stanley Wilson and filling them with art, bells, and antiques collected around the world. U.S. presidents, including Theodore Roosevelt and Richard Nixon — who was married there — stayed at the inn. After decades of decline and closure, the hotel was restored and reopened in 1992; it is a National Historic Landmark and hosts the popular Festival of Lights each holiday season.
