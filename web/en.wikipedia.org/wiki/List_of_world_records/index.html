List of world records

A world record is the best global and most important performance that has ever been recorded and officially verified in a specific skill, sport, or other kind of activity. This article serves as an index of notable world records across fields; most records are catalogued by Guinness World Records, while sporting records are governed by their respective federations.

Sport

  • Athletics: 100 m — 9.58 s, Usain Bolt (2009); marathon — 2:00:35, Kelvin Kiptum (2023); men's long jump — 8.95 m, Mike Powell (1991); women's 100 m — 10.49 s, Florence Griffith Joyner (1988).
  • Swimming: numerous records held by Michael Phelps and others; Phelps also holds the record for most Olympic gold medals (23).
  • Weightlifting: super-heavyweight totals exceeding 490 kg by Lasha Talakhadze.
  • Cycling: hour record beyond 56 km.

Human extremes

  • Tallest man: Robert Wadlow, 2.72 m (8 ft 11 in).
  • Tallest living man: Sultan Kösen, 2.51 m.
  • Longest-lived person: Jeanne Calment, 122 years 164 days.
  • Deepest freedive and other endurance records.

Science and nature

  • Hottest recorded air temperature: 56.7 °C (134 °F), Death Valley, California, 1913.
  • Deepest point on Earth: Challenger Deep, ~10,935 m below sea level.
  • Fastest land animal: cheetah, up to ~98 km/h in bursts.
  • Largest known organism by area: Armillaria fungus colonies and the Pando aspen clone.

Engineering and construction

  • Tallest building: Burj Khalifa, Dubai, 828 m.
  • Longest bridge: Danyang–Kunshan Grand Bridge, China, 164.8 km.
  • Fastest production car speeds exceeding 490 km/h (Bugatti Chiron Super Sport 300+).
  • Largest passenger aircraft: Airbus A380 by capacity.
