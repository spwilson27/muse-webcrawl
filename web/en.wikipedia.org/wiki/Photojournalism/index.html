Photojournalism

[Image: A photojournalist's cameras and a contact sheet of news frames]

Photojournalism is journalism told through photographs — the work of photographers who document news events for publication. It matured with the halftone press, the Leica-era picture magazines (Life, from 1936) and the great agencies and cooperatives such as Magnum Photos (founded 1947).

Landmarks and ethics

Iconic images — from the Spanish Civil War and Vietnam to the present — have shaped public memory and policy debates. The field's ethics centre on truthfulness: staging and heavy manipulation are prohibited by agency and contest rules, a norm tested by digital tools and AI-generated imagery.
