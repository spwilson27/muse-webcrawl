Touch World Cup

The Touch World Cup is the premier international tournament for the sport of touch (touch football), organised by the Federation of International Touch (FIT) and held every four years. It features national teams across men's, women's, and mixed divisions, as well as age-group ("masters") categories.

History

The first Touch World Cup was held on the Gold Coast, Australia, in 1988, with a handful of nations. Subsequent tournaments have been staged in New Zealand, Hawaii (United States), Japan, South Africa, Scotland, Australia, and Malaysia (Putrajaya, 2019), where a record twenty-eight nations competed. The 2024 edition was hosted in Nottingham, England. Australia and New Zealand have dominated the open divisions throughout the tournament's history, reflecting the sport's Antipodean roots, with Australia winning the great majority of gold medals across categories.

Format

Teams are drawn into pools followed by knockout rounds, with matches played under FIT rules over 40 minutes. Alongside the open divisions, competitions are held in categories such as Mixed Open, Men's and Women's 30s, 35s, 40s and beyond, making the event one of the largest participation tournaments in any football code.
