The Naked Gun is an American comedy media franchise created by David Zucker, Jim Abrahams, and Jerry Zucker (ZAZ), centred on the incompetent but unflappable police lieutenant Frank Drebin, played by Leslie Nielsen. The franchise began with the short-lived 1982 television series Police Squad! and achieved far greater success on film.

Films

The first film, The Naked Gun: From the Files of Police Squad! (1988), follows Drebin as he uncovers a plot to assassinate Queen Elizabeth II during her visit to Los Angeles, masterminded by businessman Vincent Ludwig (Ricardo Montalbán). Built on a dense stream of sight gags, puns, and parodies of police procedurals, the film was a critical and commercial hit and is regularly ranked among the funniest films ever made.

Two sequels followed: The Naked Gun 2½: The Smell of Fear (1991), in which Drebin foils a plot by energy-industry interests, and Naked Gun 33⅓: The Final Insult (1994), which sends Drebin undercover and climaxes at the Academy Awards ceremony. A new film in the franchise starring Liam Neeson as Frank Drebin Jr. was released in 2025.

Principal characters

Frank Drebin (Leslie Nielsen) is a detective sergeant, later lieutenant, of Police Squad who solves his cases largely by accident while remaining serenely unaware of the chaos around him. He is supported by Captain Ed Hocken (George Kennedy) and the accident-prone Detective Nordberg (O. J. Simpson), and in the first film romances Jane Spencer (Priscilla Presley).

Style and legacy

The films extended the joke-a-minute parody style ZAZ had pioneered in Airplane! (1980), relying on Nielsen's deadpan delivery against absurd visual comedy playing out in both foreground and background. Nielsen's performance redefined his career, transforming him from a dramatic actor into one of screen comedy's most beloved figures.
