List of fjords of Norway

Norway is famous for its fjords — long, narrow sea inlets carved by glaciers, flanked by steep cliffs. The Norwegian coastline, including fjords and islands, is one of the longest in the world.

Other notable fjords

  • Geirangerfjorden — a branch of Storfjorden and, with Nærøyfjorden, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
  • Nærøyfjorden — a narrow branch of Sognefjorden, as little as 250 m wide in places.
  • Lysefjord — in Rogaland, known for the Pulpit Rock (Preikestolen) and Kjerag cliffs.
  • Trollfjord — a short, narrow fjord in Nordland near Lofoten.
  • Altafjorden, Kvæfjorden, Malangen — major fjords of the north.
  • Romsdalsfjorden — by Molde and Åndalsnes in Møre og Romsdal.

The Sognefjorden, nicknamed the "King of the fjords", reaches depths of over 1,300 metres and is the largest fjord in Norway and one of the largest in the world.
