Photo album

A photo album is a book or collection in which photographs are stored, mounted, and preserved, often arranged to document family life, travel, or special events. Albums became common with the spread of amateur photography in the late 19th century.

History

Victorian carte de visite and cabinet card albums were status objects displayed in parlours. The introduction of the Kodak Brownie in 1900 turned album-keeping into a mass pastime; 20th-century formats included corner-mounted prints, magnetic ("sticky") albums, and slip-in pocket albums.

Digital albums

With digital photography, albums have largely migrated online: photo-sharing services and social media provide virtual albums, while printed photobooks are generated from digital files. Archivists note that acid-free materials are essential for the long-term preservation of physical prints.
