Infiniti (インフィニティ, Infiniti) is the luxury vehicle division of the Japanese automaker Nissan. Nissan launched the Infiniti brand in North America in November 1989, at nearly the same time Toyota and Honda introduced their Lexus and Acura luxury brands. The first models were the Q45 full-size luxury sedan and the M30 coupe, sold through a dedicated dealer network with an emphasis on customer service.

History

Through the 1990s the range expanded with the G20, J30 and QX4. In the 2000s the G35 coupe and sedan — rebadged versions of the Japanese Nissan Skyline — became the brand's defining products and won wide critical praise. A new naming strategy introduced in 2014 renamed all cars with the letter Q and all SUVs/crossovers with QX, followed by a number denoting the model's place in the hierarchy.

Markets and motorsport

Infiniti expanded beyond North America into the Middle East, Asia and Europe, though it withdrew from the Western European market in 2020 to concentrate on North America and China. The brand has been involved in motorsport as a sponsor and technical partner, including a prominent partnership with the Red Bull Racing Formula One team during its championship-winning years in the early 2010s, and co-developed hybrid powertrain technology with the Renault–Nissan alliance.
