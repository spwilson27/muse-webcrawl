The Mexican Pacific League (Spanish: Liga Mexicana del Pacífico, LMP), known for sponsorship reasons as the Liga ARCO Mexicana del Pacífico, is a ten-team professional baseball winter league based in northwestern Mexico. Founded in 1945 as the Liga de la Costa del Pacífico, the league's season runs from October to January, and its champion represents Mexico at the Caribbean Series.

Teams

The league's clubs include the Tomateros de Culiacán, Naranjeros de Hermosillo, Yaquis de Obregón, Venados de Mazatlán, Charros de Jalisco, Águilas de Mexicali, Cañeros de Los Mochis, Mayos de Navojoa, Algodoneros de Guasave, and Sultanes de Monterrey, who joined to give the league year-round representation alongside the summer Mexican League.

Format

The season is divided into two halves, with a points system determining playoff seeding. Eight teams qualify for the January playoffs, which culminate in a best-of-seven championship series. Rosters mix Mexican veterans, prospects, and a limited number of foreign players, and the league is classified at a level comparable to Triple-A winter ball.
