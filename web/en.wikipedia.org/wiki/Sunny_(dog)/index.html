Sunny (dog)

Sunny (born March 2012) is a pet dog of the Obama family, the 44th presidential family of the United States. Sunny is a female Portuguese Water Dog, the same breed as the Obamas' older dog, Bo. She joined the First Family at the White House in August 2013.

Background

Sunny was born in Michigan in March 2012 and was officially unveiled by the White House on 19 August 2013. The Obamas chose a Portuguese Water Dog in part because the breed is considered relatively hypoallergenic, which suited Malia Obama's allergies — the same consideration that had led to Bo's selection in 2009. To mark Sunny's arrival, the Obamas made a donation to the Washington Humane Society.

White House life

Like Bo, Sunny appeared at official events, holiday celebrations, and visits with wounded service members and children. She was known for a more energetic temperament than Bo and was occasionally reported to have had minor mishaps with guests. After the Obamas left the White House in 2017, both dogs remained with the family; Bo died in May 2021.
