Rafael Advanced Defense Systems Ltd. (Hebrew: רפאל – מערכות לחימה מתקדמות) is an Israeli state-owned defense technology company. Founded in 1948 as Israel's national R&D defense laboratory, it develops and produces weapons, military, and defense technologies for the Israel Defense Forces and for export customers worldwide.

History

Rafael began as the Science Corps of the newly formed IDF and grew into the Armament Development Authority, known by the Hebrew acronym RAFAEL (from "Authority for the Development of Armaments"). It was incorporated as a state-owned limited company in 2002. Along with Israel Aerospace Industries and Elbit Systems, it is one of Israel's three largest defense firms.

Products

Rafael is best known for air and missile defense: the Iron Dome short-range rocket interception system (developed with IAI and deployed from 2011), and David's Sling (with Raytheon) for medium-range threats. Its Spike family of electro-optical anti-tank guided missiles is used by dozens of countries. Other products include the Trophy active protection system for armoured vehicles, the Python and Derby air-to-air missiles, Litening targeting pods, Derby, and the C-Dome naval point-defense system.
