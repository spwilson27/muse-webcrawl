Legal writing

Legal writing involves the analysis of fact patterns and presentation of arguments in documents such as legal memoranda and briefs. One form of legal writing involves drafting a balanced analysis of a legal problem or issue.

Categories

Legal writing can be divided into predictive writing (memoranda analysing likely outcomes), persuasive writing (briefs and motions intended to convince a court), and legal drafting (contracts, statutes, and other instruments that create legal rights and obligations).

Style

Legal writing is often criticised for its use of archaic phrases, Latin terms, and long sentences. The plain English movement has encouraged lawyers to write more clearly and concisely for clients and the public.
