Joseph Mathunjwa

Joseph Mathunjwa is a South African trade union leader, best known as the president of the Association of Mineworkers and Construction Union (AMCU), which he founded in 1998 and which became a major rival to the National Union of Mineworkers in the platinum and gold sectors.

AMCU and Marikana

Mathunjwa led AMCU during the 2012 Marikana strike at Lonmin's platinum mine, during which 34 striking miners were shot dead by police on 16 August 2012. He was widely reported to have tried to persuade rock-drill operators to lay down their weapons shortly before the shooting.

Later strikes

Under Mathunjwa, AMCU led the five-month platinum strike of 2014, one of the longest in South African history, which ended with a wage settlement.
