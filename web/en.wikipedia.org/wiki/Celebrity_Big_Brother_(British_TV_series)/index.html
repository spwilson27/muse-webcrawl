Celebrity Big Brother (British TV series)

Celebrity Big Brother is a British television reality game show based on the Dutch show Big Brother, created by producer John de Mol in 1997. The celebrity version features famous housemates living together in a custom-built house, isolated from the outside world, while being continuously watched by television cameras. Housemates nominate each other for eviction, and the public votes to evict; the last remaining housemate wins.

History

The first series aired on Channel 4 in 2001 as a short Comic Relief special and was won by comedian Jack Dee. The show ran on Channel 4 until 2010, producing notable series including the fifth series in 2007, which drew international attention and a race row involving Shilpa Shetty, who went on to win. Channel 5 revived the programme in 2011, airing two series a year for much of the decade; winners in this era included Julian Clary, Rylan Clark, and Charlotte Crosby. Channel 5 ended the show in 2018. ITV acquired the format and relaunched Celebrity Big Brother in March 2024, with David Potts winning the first ITV series.

Format

Celebrity series are shorter than the civilian version, typically lasting around three weeks. Housemates compete in tasks for a shopping budget, nominate in the Diary Room, and face public votes. The show is presented from a studio beside the house, with live eviction shows and a spin-off discussion programme — Big Brother's Bit on the Side on Channel 5 and Late & Live on ITV.

Reception

The programme has been one of the most talked-about reality formats in Britain, generating record viewing figures in its Channel 4 years and frequent controversy over housemate behaviour, voting, and the ethics of the format. The 2007 series prompted tens of thousands of complaints to Ofcom and debate in Parliament, but also significantly raised the show's international profile.
