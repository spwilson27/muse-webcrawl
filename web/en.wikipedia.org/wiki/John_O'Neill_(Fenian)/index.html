John O'Neill (Fenian)

John O'Neill (March 9, 1834 – January 7, 1878) was an Irish-born American soldier and Fenian leader. A veteran of the American Civil War, he is best known for leading the Fenian raids into Canada in 1866, 1870 and 1871, attempts by Irish-American members of the Fenian Brotherhood to seize Canadian territory in order to pressure Britain into granting Irish independence.

Early life and Civil War service

O'Neill was born in Drumgallon, County Monaghan, Ireland, and emigrated to the United States in 1848. He served in the United States Army, fought in the Civil War, and rose to the rank of lieutenant colonel, gaining a reputation as a capable cavalry officer.

Fenian raids

After the war O'Neill joined the Fenian Brotherhood and became the leading advocate of its "Canada strategy." In June 1866 he led a force across the Niagara River, winning a skirmish at the Battle of Ridgeway before withdrawing in the face of approaching Canadian and British forces and the loss of American supply support. He led further incursions in 1870 and 1871; the latter, into Manitoba, ended with his arrest by United States troops.

Later life

O'Neill later promoted Irish colonisation schemes in Nebraska, where the town of O'Neill bears his name. He died in Omaha in 1878.
