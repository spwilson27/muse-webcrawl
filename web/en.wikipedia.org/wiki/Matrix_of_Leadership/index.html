Matrix of Leadership

The Matrix of Leadership is a fictional artefact in the Transformers franchise. An ancient and powerful relic of the planet Cybertron, it is traditionally carried by the leader of the Autobots — usually Optimus Prime — within a compartment in his chest. The Matrix embodies the wisdom of the past Primes and is said to hold a connection to Primus, the creator god of the Transformers.

Original series

The Matrix was introduced in The Transformers: The Movie (1986). As Optimus Prime dies, he passes the Matrix to Ultra Magnus; it is later claimed by the young Autobot Hot Rod, who, upon opening it at the climax, is transformed into Rodimus Prime. The Matrix's energy destroys the planet-eating Unicron, establishing the "light our darkest hour" prophecy as a central piece of franchise lore.

Attributes

Within the fiction, the Matrix can bestow life on new Transformers, confer knowledge and visions on its bearer, and serve as a weapon against Unicron, to whom it is anathema. Only a bearer deemed worthy can open it; others, such as the Decepticon Scourge, are corrupted or harmed by its power.

Later appearances

The Matrix has appeared in numerous subsequent continuities, including Beast Wars, the live-action film series beginning in 2007 — where it features as the key to the Star Harvester in Revenge of the Fallen — and the comics published by IDW. Its design, a spherical core within an ornate housing with twin handles, is one of the franchise's most recognisable props.
