River Ant

The River Ant is a river in Norfolk, England, one of the principal tributaries of the River Bure within the Norfolk Broads. It rises near Honing and flows south for about 27 km (17 mi), passing North Walsham and through Barton Broad before joining the Bure at Ant Mouth near Ludham Bridge.

Navigation

The lower river is navigable as part of the Broads network. The North Walsham and Dilham Canal, opened in 1826, extended navigation from the Ant toward North Walsham. Notable features along the river include the large windpump at Turf Fen and How Hill, an Edwardian house now an environmental study centre.
