Fred Flintstone

Frederick "Fred" Flintstone is the main character of the animated sitcom The Flintstones, which aired on ABC from 1960 to 1966 and became a landmark of prime-time animation. A Stone Age everyman living in the town of Bedrock, Fred works as a bronto-crane operator at the Slate Rock and Gravel Company and is famous for his catchphrase "Yabba dabba doo!"

Created by: William Hanna and Joseph Barbera
Voiced by: Alan Reed (1960–1977); later Henry Corden, Jeff Bergman
Spouse: Wilma Flintstone
Children: Pebbles Flintstone
Best friend: Barney Rubble

Character

Fred is blustery, loud, short-tempered and devoted to get-rich-quick and status-boosting schemes that usually collapse, but he is also warm-hearted and devoted to his family. He is married to Wilma, with whom he has a daughter, Pebbles, and a pet dinosaur, Dino. His neighbours and best friends are Barney and Betty Rubble; Fred and Barney are fellow members of the Loyal Order of Water Buffaloes lodge. His hobbies include bowling, golf, poker and lounging in the pool, and his favourite food is the bronto burger.

Origins and portrayal

Hanna-Barbera modelled Fred partly on Ralph Kramden of The Honeymooners. Alan Reed provided his voice throughout the original series and its early spin-offs, improvising the famous "yabba dabba doo" in place of the scripted "yahoo". The character has since appeared in numerous spin-off series, television specials, and two live-action films — played by John Goodman in The Flintstones (1994) and Mark Addy in The Flintstones in Viva Rock Vegas (2000) — as well as decades of commercials for Fruity Pebbles and Cocoa Pebbles cereals and Flintstones chewable vitamins.

Legacy

Fred Flintstone is one of the most recognisable cartoon characters in the world; TV Guide ranked him among the greatest television cartoon characters, and the show's Stone Age take on suburban consumer life remains a template for animated sitcoms.
