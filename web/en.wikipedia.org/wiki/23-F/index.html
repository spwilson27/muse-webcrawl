23-F

23-F was a failed coup d'état in Spain on 23 February 1981. Civil Guard Lieutenant Colonel Antonio Tejero led armed officers into the Congress of Deputies during the vote to invest Leopoldo Calvo-Sotelo as prime minister, taking deputies hostage.


Date: 23–24 February 1981
Location: Madrid and Valencia, Spain
Outcome: Coup failed; plotters imprisoned

Events

Tejero fired shots into the ceiling of Congress while General Jaime Milans del Bosch declared a state of emergency in Valencia and deployed tanks. King Juan Carlos I, after securing the loyalty of most military commanders, appeared on television in uniform in the early hours of 24 February and denounced the coup. The plotters surrendered that morning.

Significance

The failure of 23-F consolidated Spain's young democracy after the transition from Francoism. Tejero, Milans del Bosch and Alfonso Armada received long prison sentences.
