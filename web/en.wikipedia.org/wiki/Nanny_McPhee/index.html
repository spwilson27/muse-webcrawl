Nanny McPhee

Nanny McPhee is a 2005 comedy-drama fantasy film directed by Kirk Jones, based on the Nurse Matilda books by Christianna Brand. The screenplay was written by Emma Thompson, who also stars in the title role, alongside Colin Firth, Thomas Sangster, Kelly Macdonald, and Angela Lansbury. A sequel, Nanny McPhee and the Big Bang, was released in 2010.

Plot

In Victorian England, widowed undertaker Cedric Brown (Colin Firth) is at his wits' end: his seven unruly children have driven away seventeen nannies. A mysterious voice advises him to seek out Nanny McPhee (Emma Thompson), a stern, warty, snaggle-toothed woman with magical powers, who arrives announcing she can help. Her motto is: "When you need me but do not want me, then I must stay. When you want me but no longer need me, then I have to go." As the children learn each of her five lessons, one of her unsightly features disappears. Meanwhile Cedric must remarry to keep the financial support of his formidable Aunt Adelaide (Angela Lansbury), and the children scheme first against a dreadful prospective stepmother and then to bring about their father's marriage to the kind scullery maid Evangeline (Kelly Macdonald).

Reception

The film was a commercial success, grossing over $120 million worldwide, and received generally positive reviews for Thompson's performance and screenplay and its colourful production design.
