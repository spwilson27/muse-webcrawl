Jack Dorsey

Jack Patrick Dorsey (born November 19, 1976) is an American entrepreneur and software designer, best known as a co-founder of the social network Twitter (now X) and of the financial technology company Block, Inc. (formerly Square). He served two separate terms as Twitter's chief executive, from 2006 to 2008 and again from 2015 to 2021.

Early life

Dorsey grew up in St. Louis, Missouri, where he became interested in dispatch routing software and wrote open-source dispatch programs as a teenager. He studied at the Missouri University of Science and Technology and later at New York University, leaving before graduating to move to California. Working at a podcasting company, Odeo, he developed an idea for sharing short status updates; on March 21, 2006 he sent the first tweet: "just setting up my twttr".

Twitter

Dorsey was Twitter's first CEO, but was replaced by co-founder Evan Williams in 2008 amid concerns about his management, remaining chairman. He returned as executive chairman in 2011 and became interim, then permanent, CEO in 2015 while simultaneously running Square — an unusual dual role. His second tenure saw the character limit doubled to 280, experiments with algorithmic timelines, and intensifying controversy over content moderation, including the permanent suspension of President Donald Trump in January 2021. Dorsey stepped down in November 2021, handing the role to Parag Agrawal, and left the board in 2022 after Elon Musk's acquisition of the company.

Square and Block

In 2009 Dorsey co-founded Square with Jim McKelvey, launching a small card reader that let small businesses accept card payments through a phone. The company, renamed Block, Inc. in 2021, grew to include Cash App, the Tidal music service, and Bitcoin-related ventures; Dorsey is a prominent advocate of Bitcoin and later of decentralised social networking through the Bluesky project, which he helped initiate.

Philanthropy and views

In April 2020 Dorsey announced he would move about $1 billion of his equity — roughly 28% of his wealth — to a fund called Start Small, beginning with COVID-19 relief. Known for minimalist habits, fasting, and meditation retreats, he has been a polarising figure in debates about free expression online.
