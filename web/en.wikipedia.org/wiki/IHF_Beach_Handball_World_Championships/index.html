The IHF Beach Handball World Championships are the official world championships for men's and women's national beach handball teams, organized by the International Handball Federation. The tournament was first held in 2004 in El Gouna, Egypt.

Format

The championships are held every two years and feature separate men's and women's tournaments. Matches are played on sand courts, and each match consists of two 10-minute sets; if each team wins one set, a shoot-out decides the winner. Brazil has been among the most successful nations in the men's competition, while countries such as Croatia, Norway and Spain have also won titles.

History

After the inaugural 2004 edition, further championships were staged in Rio de Janeiro, Cádiz, Antalya, Muscat, Recife, Budapest, Kazan and Heraklion, reflecting the global spread of the beach variant of handball.
