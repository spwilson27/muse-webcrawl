Torstar Corporation is a Canadian mass media company best known as the publisher of the Toronto Star, Canada's largest-circulation daily newspaper. The company's origins lie in the Toronto Star itself, founded in 1892; the modern corporation grew out of Toronto Star Limited, incorporated in 1958 when the newspaper was transferred to a charitable ownership structure under the Atkinson Foundation principles established by Joseph E. Atkinson.

Holdings

At its peak Torstar owned a portfolio of daily and community newspapers across Ontario, including the Hamilton Spectator, Waterloo Region Record, St. Catharines Standard, The Peterborough Examiner and the Metroland Media Group chain of community papers. It also held interests in digital properties, the Toronto Star's thestar.com, VerticalScope, and book publisher Harlequin Enterprises (sold to HarperCollins in 2014 for about C$455 million).

Privatization

After years of declining print advertising revenue, Torstar agreed in May 2020 to be taken private by NordStar Capital, an investment firm controlled by Paul Rivett and Jordan Bitove, in a deal valued at about C$52 million (later raised to C$60 million). The buyout ended the voting-trust structure that had governed the Star for decades and sparked an acrimonious dispute between the two NordStar partners, resolved in 2022 with Bitove taking control of the newspaper assets.
