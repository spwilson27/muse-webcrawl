Cluemaster (Arthur Brown) is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, commonly as an adversary of the superhero Batman. Created by writer Gardner Fox and artist Carmine Infantino, he first appeared in Detective Comics #351 (May 1966).

Fictional character biography

Arthur Brown was the host of a television game show who turned to crime, leaving clues to his crimes in imitation of the Riddler — but, in a twist, sometimes omitting or falsifying the clue so that Batman could not simply follow the trail. He is the father of Stephanie Brown, who became the vigilante Spoiler (and later Robin and Batgirl) partly to foil his criminal schemes and "spoil" his clues.

Cluemaster later joined the makeshift "Misfits" team of minor villains, worked with the Suicide Squad, and was seemingly killed during a mission, though he later resurfaced. He has also appeared in animated series and other media featuring Batman.

Powers and abilities

Cluemaster has no superhuman powers. He relies on planning, henchmen, and a costume fitted with pellets containing glue, flares, smoke and incapacitating agents.
