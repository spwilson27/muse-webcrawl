Les Trois Vallées

Les Trois Vallées (French for "The Three Valleys") is a vast linked ski area in the Tarentaise Valley of the French Alps, in the Savoie department. It is frequently described as the largest ski area in the world, with around 600 kilometres of interconnected pistes.

LocationSavoie, French Alps

Highest lift3,230 m (Val Thorens)

The valleys

The area joins the valleys of Saint-Bon (Courchevel), Allues (Méribel) and Belleville (Val Thorens, Les Menuires), with a fourth valley, Maurienne, reached via Orelle. Resorts are linked by an extensive lift network allowing skiers to travel between valleys without removing their skis.

Resorts

Val Thorens, at 2,300 m, is the highest ski resort in Europe and opens from late November into May. Courchevel is known for luxury hotels and hosted events during the 1992 Winter Olympics period; Méribel sits at the heart of the area and hosted Olympic ice hockey and women's alpine skiing in 1992.
