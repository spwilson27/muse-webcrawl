Inch

Inch
Unit system: Imperial / US customary
Unit of: Length
Symbol: in, ″
1 in = 25.4 mm (exactly)
= 1/12 ft = 1/36 yd

The inch (symbol: in or ″) is a unit of length in the British imperial and United States customary systems of measurement. It is equal to exactly 25.4 millimetres under the international yard and pound agreement of 1959. There are 12 inches in a foot and 36 inches in a yard.

History

The English word "inch" derives from the Latin uncia, meaning one-twelfth part. Traditional definitions included the width of a human thumb and the length of three dry barleycorns laid end to end — the latter written into English statute in the early 14th century. Inch standards varied between countries until 20th-century international agreements fixed the yard, and hence the inch, in metric terms.

Usage

The inch remains in everyday use in the United States for most measurements, and in the United Kingdom and Commonwealth countries for some applications, such as road signs in miles, human height, wheel and tyre sizes, and display screen sizes worldwide. Fractions of an inch are commonly expressed in binary fractions (halves, quarters, eighths) in machining and carpentry, or in decimal "thousandths of an inch" (mils) in engineering.
