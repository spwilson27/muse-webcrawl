Alfa Romeo Automobiles S.p.A. is an Italian premium car manufacturer and a subsidiary of Stellantis. The company was founded on 24 June 1910 in Milan, Italy, as A.L.F.A. (Anonima Lombarda Fabbrica Automobili); the name "Romeo" was added after Nicola Romeo took control in 1915.

History

Alfa Romeo built its reputation in motor racing, winning the first Automobile World Championship in 1925 and dominating early Grand Prix racing. Enzo Ferrari ran the company's racing team, Scuderia Ferrari, before founding his own marque. The brand won the first two Formula One World Championships, in 1950 and 1951, with Giuseppe Farina and Juan Manuel Fangio driving the Alfetta 158/159.

Cars and identity

Road cars such as the Giulietta, Giulia and Spider established Alfa Romeo's image of stylish, sporting Italian design, centred on the Cuore Sportivo ("sporting heart") slogan and the distinctive shield-shaped scudetto grille. After decades under state ownership and then Fiat, the brand became part of Stellantis in 2021, with recent models including the Giulia saloon, Stelvio SUV and Tonale compact SUV.
