P.A.R.T.S.

P.A.R.T.S.
Type: Dance school
Established: 1995
Location: Brussels, Belgium
Founder: Anne Teresa De Keersmaeker

P.A.R.T.S. (Performing Arts Research and Training Studios) is an international school for contemporary dance and choreography based in Brussels, Belgium. It was founded in 1995 by the choreographer Anne Teresa De Keersmaeker, artistic director of the dance company Rosas.

History

The school was created to meet a growing need for structured professional training in contemporary dance in Belgium and Europe. From its beginnings it has been closely linked with Rosas and with the broader Brussels dance scene, and it shares facilities and artistic networks with the company.

Training

P.A.R.T.S. offers a multi-year programme combining daily technical training in contemporary dance and ballet with composition, improvisation, repertoire, theory and research. Students work with guest teachers and choreographers from around the world, and the programme culminates in student creations and public performances. Admission is by audition, and the student body is highly international.

Influence

Graduates of P.A.R.T.S. have gone on to found companies and to work as dancers and choreographers across Europe and beyond, and the school is regarded as one of the leading training institutions for contemporary dance.
