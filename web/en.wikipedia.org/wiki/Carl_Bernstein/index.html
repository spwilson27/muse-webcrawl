[Image: Bernstein at a typewriter in the Post's newsroom]

Carl Bernstein (born 1944) is an American investigative journalist who, with Bob Woodward at the Washington Post, reported the Watergate scandal in 1972–74. Their book All the President's Men and its 1976 film adaptation (Bernstein played by Dustin Hoffman) fixed the image of the crusading reporter in popular culture.

Later career

Bernstein left the Post in 1977, worked in television and magazines, and wrote books including a biography of Hillary Clinton; he remains a commentator on American politics and press freedom.
