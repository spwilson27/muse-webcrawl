List of muscles of the human body

The human body contains more than 600 named skeletal muscles, together accounting for roughly 40 per cent of body mass. This list organises the major muscles by region of the body. Each muscle attaches between bones (or other structures) by tendons and produces movement when it contracts.

Muscles of the head and neck

MuscleMain action
FrontalisRaises the eyebrows; wrinkles the forehead
Orbicularis oculiCloses the eyelids
Orbicularis orisCloses and protrudes the lips
Zygomaticus majorRaises the corners of the mouth (smiling)
MasseterElevates the jaw in chewing
TemporalisElevates and retracts the jaw
BuccinatorCompresses the cheek
SternocleidomastoidRotates and flexes the neck
Trapezius (upper part)Elevates and rotates the scapula and extends the neck
ScalenesElevate the first and second ribs; flex the neck

Muscles of the trunk

MuscleMain action
Pectoralis majorAdducts and medially rotates the arm
Pectoralis minorStabilises and depresses the scapula
Serratus anteriorProtracts the scapula ("boxer's muscle")
Rectus abdominisFlexes the trunk ("six-pack" muscle)
External obliqueRotates and flexes the trunk
Latissimus dorsiExtends, adducts and medially rotates the arm
Erector spinaeExtends the vertebral column
DiaphragmPrincipal muscle of inspiration
IntercostalsElevate and depress the ribs in breathing

Muscles of the upper limb

MuscleMain action
DeltoidAbducts the arm
Biceps brachiiFlexes the elbow; supinates the forearm
Triceps brachiiExtends the elbow
BrachialisFlexes the elbow
Supraspinatus, infraspinatus, teres minor, subscapularisRotator cuff — stabilise the shoulder joint
Flexor carpi radialis / ulnarisFlex the wrist
Extensor digitorumExtends the fingers
Thenar musclesMove the thumb (opposition)

Muscles of the lower limb

MuscleMain action
Gluteus maximusExtends the hip; largest muscle by volume
Gluteus mediusAbducts and stabilises the hip
IliopsoasPrincipal hip flexor
SartoriusFlexes hip and knee; longest muscle in the body
Quadriceps femoris (rectus femoris, vastus lateralis, vastus medialis, vastus intermedius)Extends the knee
Hamstrings (biceps femoris, semitendinosus, semimembranosus)Flex the knee; extend the hip
Adductor magnusAdducts the thigh
GastrocnemiusPlantar-flexes the foot (calf)
SoleusPlantar-flexes the foot
Tibialis anteriorDorsiflexes and inverts the foot
