List of generating stations in Canada

This is a list of generating stations in Canada, grouped by province and type. Canada generates the majority of its electricity from hydroelectric sources, followed by nuclear, natural gas, wind and coal.

The Robert-Bourassa generating station in Quebec is Canada's largest power station.

Fossil fuel and renewables

Large gas-fired stations include the Greenfield Energy Centre and Goreway in Ontario, while the Sundance and Genesee stations in Alberta historically anchored coal generation and have been converting to natural gas. Wind power is concentrated in Ontario, Quebec and Alberta, and solar generation is concentrated in Ontario.
