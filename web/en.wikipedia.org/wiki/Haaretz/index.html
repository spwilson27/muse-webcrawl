Haaretz (Hebrew: 'The Land [of Israel]') is an Israeli daily newspaper founded in 1918 in Jerusalem, Israel's oldest daily still in print. Published in Hebrew and English by the Haaretz Group (historically the Schocken family), it is known for liberal, critical journalism and influential opinion and culture pages, with a small but elite readership.

Position

Haaretz's editorial line is dovish on the conflict, secular and civil-libertarian; its columnists are fixtures of Israeli debate. Its English edition, produced with a former International Herald Tribune partnership tradition, is the main window for foreign readers into Israel's liberal press.
