NAM Atlântico (A140) is a multipurpose amphibious helicopter carrier and the flagship of the Brazilian Navy. Built in the United Kingdom as HMS Ocean, a landing platform helicopter (LPH), she was commissioned into the Royal Navy in 1998 and served as Britain's helicopter carrier and amphibious assault ship for two decades.

Service in Brazil

Brazil purchased the ship in 2018 as HMS Ocean was decommissioned, and she was commissioned into the Brazilian Navy in June 2018 as PHM Atlântico. In 2020 her designation was changed from helicopter carrier (PHM) to multipurpose aircraft carrier (NAM, Navio Aeródromo Multipropósito), recognising her ability to operate fixed-wing drones and tilt-rotor aircraft concepts. Displacing around 21,500 tonnes, she can carry a large complement of helicopters, marines, vehicles, and landing craft, and has supported humanitarian and disaster-relief operations.
