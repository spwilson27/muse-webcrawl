Kitzbuhel

Kitzbuhel (in standard spelling Kitzbühel) is a medieval town in Tyrol, Austria, district capital of Kitzbühel district, renowned as a ski resort. Population about 8,300. It lies in the Kitzbühel Alps between the Hahnenkamm and Kitzbüheler Horn mountains.

The town hosts the annual Hahnenkamm races, among the most famous and demanding downhill races on the World Cup circuit on the Streif course, and the Austrian Open tennis tournament historically. Its well-preserved old town, mining history in silver and copper, and summer hiking and golf make it a year-round resort.
