News Corporation, commonly known as News Corp, is an American mass media and publishing company founded by Rupert Murdoch. The name has been borne by two successive conglomerates: the original News Corporation (1980–2013), a media empire spanning film, television and newspapers, and the present News Corp, created in 2013 when the original split into a publishing company and the entertainment-focused 21st Century Fox.

News Corp publishes newspapers in the US, UK and Australia.

The original company

Murdoch built News Corporation from a single Adelaide newspaper inherited in the 1950s into a global empire owning, among many assets, The Sun and The Times in Britain, the New York Post and The Wall Street Journal (via Dow Jones) in the United States, major Australian newspapers, the 20th Century Fox film studio, Fox television networks and stakes in satellite broadcasters. The company was damaged in 2011 by the British phone-hacking scandal, which closed the News of the World newspaper.

The current company

Today's News Corp retains the publishing assets: Dow Jones & Company and The Wall Street Journal, the New York Post, News UK, News Corp Australia, book publisher HarperCollins, and digital real-estate services including Realtor.com. It remains controlled by the Murdoch family through voting shares.
