Myeongdong

CountrySouth Korea
CitySeoul
DistrictJung District

Myeongdong (Korean: 명동) is a neighbourhood in Jung District, Seoul, South Korea. It is one of Seoul's best-known shopping and tourism districts, famous for its fashion and cosmetics shops, street food stalls and dense crowds, and it is also a centre of Korean Catholicism.

Features

The district is home to flagship stores of Korean and international beauty brands, department stores such as Lotte and Shinsegae nearby, and numerous restaurants and street vendors. Landmarks include Myeongdong Cathedral, completed in 1898, the first Gothic-style church in Korea and a symbol of the Korean pro-democracy movement. Myeongdong is served by Myeongdong Station on Seoul Subway Line 4 and Euljiro stations nearby.

History

The area's name derives from Myeongryebang, a residential district of the Joseon era. It developed into a commercial centre during the Japanese colonial period and grew after the Korean War into the country's premier retail and financial district, a role it partly retains despite competition from Gangnam and other districts.
