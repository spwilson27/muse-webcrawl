Defense contractor

A defense contractor is a business that provides products or services to a government's military or intelligence agencies: weapons systems, aircraft, ships, electronics, logistics and private security services.

Industry

The largest contractors include Lockheed Martin, RTX (Raytheon), Boeing, Northrop Grumman, General Dynamics and BAE Systems, measured by arms revenue. The industry consolidated sharply after the Cold War - US Secretary William Perry's 1993 "Last Supper" encouraged mergers.

Criticism

Critics cite cost overruns, revolving-door employment between industry and the Pentagon, and lobbying influence - concerns encapsulated in Eisenhower's warning about the military-industrial complex. Private military companies such as those used in Iraq and Afghanistan raise accountability questions.
