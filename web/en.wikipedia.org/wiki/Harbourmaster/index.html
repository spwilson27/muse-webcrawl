Harbourmaster

A harbourmaster (or harbormaster) is an official responsible for enforcing the regulations of a particular harbour or port, in order to ensure the safety of navigation and the security and operation of the port facilities.

Duties

Duties typically include supervising vessel movements and berthing, enforcing port bylaws, coordinating pilotage and dredging, responding to pollution and emergencies, and investigating marine incidents within port limits. In many jurisdictions the harbourmaster holds statutory powers under harbour or merchant shipping legislation.

Organisations

Professional bodies include the International Harbour Masters' Association (IHMA) and national associations such as the UK Harbour Masters' Association.
