Stakhanovite movement

The Stakhanovite movement was a mass campaign in the Soviet Union encouraging workers to exceed production quotas, named after the coal miner Alexei Stakhanov, who in August 1935 reportedly hewed 14 times his quota of coal in a single shift in the Donbas.

History

Promoted by the Communist Party from late 1935, the movement rewarded record-breaking "Stakhanovites" with higher pay, housing and honours, and tied the campaign to socialist emulation and the fulfilment of the Five-Year Plans.

Criticism

Records were often staged with extensive support teams, disrupted normal production, and created resentment among ordinary workers. Nonetheless, the movement became a lasting symbol of Soviet labour propaganda and heroic industrialisation.
