The Boys (TV series)


The superhero team the Seven, as marketed by Vought International

The Boys is an American superhero drama television series developed by Eric Kripke for Amazon Prime Video. Based on the comic book of the same name by Garth Ennis and Darick Robertson, it follows the eponymous team of vigilantes as they combat "supes" — superpowered individuals who are marketed and controlled by the powerful corporation Vought International and who abuse their abilities behind a facade of heroism.

Premise

After Hughie Campbell's girlfriend is killed by the speedster A-Train, Hughie is recruited by Billy Butcher into a black-ops team dedicated to bringing down corrupt superheroes. Their chief target is the Seven, Vought's premier superhero team, led by the psychopathic Homelander. The series satirises superhero media, celebrity culture, corporate power and American politics, and is noted for its graphic violence and dark humour.

Cast and production

The ensemble cast includes Karl Urban as Billy Butcher, Jack Quaid as Hughie Campbell, Antony Starr as Homelander, Erin Moriarty as Starlight/Annie January, and Dominique McElligott as Queen Maeve. The series premiered on 26 July 2019; further seasons followed in 2020, 2022 and 2024, with a fifth and final season announced. It has received critical acclaim and multiple Emmy nominations.

Franchise

The series spawned the animated anthology The Boys Presents: Diabolical (2022) and the spin-off Gen V (2023), set at a university for young supes.
