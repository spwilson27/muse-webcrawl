AEG Presents

AEG Presents
Industry: Live entertainment
Parent: Anschutz Entertainment Group
Known for: Coachella, BST Hyde Park

AEG Presents is the live entertainment division of the Anschutz Entertainment Group (AEG) and one of the world's largest presenters of live music and entertainment events. It promotes concerts, tours and festivals and operates venues in North America, Europe and Asia.

Festivals and tours

Through its Goldenvoice subsidiary, AEG Presents produces the Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival, Stagecoach and other festivals, and promotes BST Hyde Park in London and numerous global concert tours.
