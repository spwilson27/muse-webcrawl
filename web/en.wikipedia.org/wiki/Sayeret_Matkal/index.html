Sayeret Matkal

Sayeret Matkal (Hebrew: סיירת מטכ"ל, "General Staff Reconnaissance Unit"), often simply "The Unit", is the prime special forces unit of the Israel Defense Forces (IDF), modelled on the British SAS and directly subordinate to the IDF Military Intelligence Directorate (Aman).

History

Founded in 1957 by Avraham Arnan, the unit specialises in deep reconnaissance, intelligence collection and counter-terrorism/hostage rescue outside Israel's borders.

Notable operations

Operations attributed to the unit include Operation Entebbe (1976), the rescue of hostages from the hijacked Air France flight in Uganda, in which its commander Yonatan Netanyahu was killed; the 1972 Sabena Flight 571 rescue; and the 1973 Operation Spring of Youth in Beirut. Former members include prime ministers Benjamin Netanyahu and Ehud Barak.
