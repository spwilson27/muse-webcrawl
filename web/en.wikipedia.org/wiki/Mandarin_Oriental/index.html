Mandarin Oriental is a luxury hotel brand operated by the Mandarin Oriental Hotel Group, a member of the Jardine Matheson Group. The brand is known for combining Asian-inspired service traditions with luxury accommodation in major cities and resort destinations around the world.

History

The group's origins lie in two hotels: The Mandarin, which opened in Hong Kong in 1963 and quickly gained a reputation as one of Asia's finest hotels, and The Oriental in Bangkok, a celebrated hotel founded in the 19th century. The two businesses were combined in 1974 under the name Mandarin Oriental, and the combined company expanded internationally, opening hotels in Asia, Europe and the Americas.

The group's emblem, an eleven-bladed fan, was adopted as a symbol evoking its Asian heritage, and each hotel displays a unique fan designed to reflect its location.

Properties

Mandarin Oriental operates several dozen hotels and resorts, as well as branded residences, in destinations including Hong Kong, Bangkok, Singapore, Tokyo, London, Paris, New York and Dubai. The company has pursued a strategy of managing and investing in a relatively small portfolio of high-end properties rather than mass-market expansion, and is dual-listed in Singapore and Bermuda within the Jardines group.
