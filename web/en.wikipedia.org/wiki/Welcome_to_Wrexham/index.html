Genre: Sports documentary

Welcome to Wrexham is a sports documentary television series following Wrexham A.F.C., the Welsh professional football club purchased in 2020 by actors Ryan Reynolds and Rob McElhenney. Produced by Boardwalk Pictures, it premiered on FX in August 2022.

Premise

The series chronicles the actors' unlikely ownership of one of the world's oldest football clubs, then playing in the fifth-tier National League, and interweaves the club's matches with the lives of players, staff and supporters in the working-class town of Wrexham. Season two covers the club's dramatic 2022–23 season, in which Wrexham won the National League title and promotion back to the English Football League after 15 years; later seasons document further promotions into League One and beyond.

Reception and impact

The show has won multiple Primetime Emmy Awards, including for Outstanding Unstructured Reality Program, and has been credited with dramatically raising the club's global profile, tourism to Wrexham and interest in lower-league football. Critics have praised its focus on the community as much as on its celebrity owners.
