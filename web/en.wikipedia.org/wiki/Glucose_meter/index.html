Glucose meter

A glucose meter is a medical device for determining the approximate concentration of glucose in the blood. Also known as a glucometer, it is a key element of home blood glucose monitoring by people with diabetes mellitus or hypoglycemia.

A small drop of blood, obtained by pricking the skin with a lancet, is placed on a disposable test strip that the meter reads. Most meters use enzyme electrodes (glucose oxidase or glucose dehydrogenase) that generate a current proportional to glucose concentration.

Accuracy and use

International standard ISO 15197 sets accuracy requirements for meters. Results are expressed in mg/dL in the United States and mmol/L in much of the rest of the world. Continuous glucose monitors (CGMs) increasingly supplement or replace fingerstick testing for many patients.
