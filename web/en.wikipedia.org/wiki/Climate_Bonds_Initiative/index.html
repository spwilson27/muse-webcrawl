Climate Bonds Initiative

The Climate Bonds Initiative (CBI) is an international, investor-focused not-for-profit organisation working to mobilise the largest capital market of all — the bond market — for climate change solutions. Founded in 2009 and based in London, it promotes investment in projects and assets necessary for a rapid transition to a low-carbon and climate-resilient economy.

Climate Bonds Standard

CBI administers the Climate Bonds Standard and Certification Scheme, a labelling scheme for bonds and loans, loosely modelled on the Fair Trade label for coffee, which certifies that the proceeds are used to finance assets consistent with the Paris Agreement goals. Sector-specific criteria cover areas such as renewable energy, low-carbon buildings, transport, and water infrastructure.

Market development

The organisation publishes market data on green bonds and provides policy advice and capacity building to governments and market participants. The green bond market it helped catalyse grew from a niche when CBI was founded to annual issuance in the hundreds of billions of dollars in the 2020s.
