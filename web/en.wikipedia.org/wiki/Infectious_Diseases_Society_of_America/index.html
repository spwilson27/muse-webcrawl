Infectious Diseases Society of America (IDSA)

Founded: 1963

Headquarters: Arlington, Virginia, United States

Membership: Over 13,000 physicians, scientists, and health professionals

The Infectious Diseases Society of America (IDSA) is a professional medical association representing physicians, scientists, and other health professionals who specialize in infectious diseases, based in Arlington, Virginia.

Activities

IDSA publishes clinical practice guidelines widely used in treating infections, publishes the journals Clinical Infectious Diseases, The Journal of Infectious Diseases, and Open Forum Infectious Diseases, and holds the annual IDWeek conference jointly with partner societies.

Advocacy

The society advocates for antimicrobial stewardship, vaccine policy, research funding, and public-health preparedness, and its Lyme disease guidelines have been a notable subject of public controversy.
