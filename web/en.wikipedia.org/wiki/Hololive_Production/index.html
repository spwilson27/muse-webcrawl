Hololive Production

Hololive Production (Japanese: ホロライブプロダクション, stylized in lowercase) is a virtual YouTuber (VTuber) talent agency owned by the Japanese technology entertainment company Cover Corporation. The agency manages a large roster of streamers who perform using anime-style digital avatars, broadcasting live gameplay, singing, chatting streams, and original music primarily on YouTube and other platforms.

History

Cover Corporation was founded in 2016 by Motoaki Tanigo. Its first virtual talent, Tokino Sora, debuted in September 2017, and the Hololive brand was formally organized the following year. The roster expanded through successive "generations" of Japanese talents, and the agency launched international branches: Hololive Indonesia (2020), Hololive English (2020), whose debut generation "Myth" became a breakout success with Western audiences, and later the English "Promise" and "Justice" generations, as well as the male-focused Holostars branch and its English counterpart Holostars English.

Activities

Hololive talents stream individually and collaborate in group broadcasts, concerts, and events. The agency produces original songs, animated shorts, and large-scale live concerts using 3D models, including the annual "Hololive Super Expo" convention and festival concerts. Its performers have amassed tens of millions of subscribers collectively; notable talents include Gawr Gura, Usada Pekora, Shirakami Fubuki, Mori Calliope, and Houshou Marine. In 2023–2024, Hololive talents held some of the largest subscriber counts of any VTubers in the world, and the agency became one of the most commercially prominent examples of the VTuber model of virtual entertainment.
