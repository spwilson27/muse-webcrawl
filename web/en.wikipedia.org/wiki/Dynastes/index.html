Dynastes

Dynastes
Family: Scarabaeidae (rhinoceros beetles)
Range: southern United States to South America

Dynastes is a genus of large scarab beetles, in the subfamily Dynastinae (rhinoceros beetles), native to the Americas from the southern United States through Central America to South America. Males bear long horns on the head and thorax used in combat over food and mates.

Species

The best-known species is the Hercules beetle, Dynastes hercules, among the longest beetles in the world, with males reaching up to 17 cm including the horn. Other recognised species include D. tityus (the eastern Hercules beetle of the United States), D. neptunus, D. maya, D. hyllus, D. grantii (western Hercules beetle), D. bleuzeni, and D. satanas of Bolivia.

Biology

Larvae develop in decaying wood and leaf litter over one to two years before pupating; adults feed on sap and fruit and are strong fliers attracted to lights. Large Dynastes are popular in the insect-keeping hobby and are renowned for their strength, able to lift many times their own body mass.
