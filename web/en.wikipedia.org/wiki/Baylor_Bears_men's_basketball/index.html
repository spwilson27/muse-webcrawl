Baylor Bears men's basketball

The Baylor Bears men's basketball team represents Baylor University in Waco, Texas. The Bears compete in NCAA Division I as a member of the Big 12 Conference. The programme won the NCAA national championship in 2021.

History

Baylor began playing men's basketball in 1907 and reached the NCAA Final Four in 1948 and 1950, finishing as national runner-up in 1948. The programme then spent decades in relative obscurity, and in 2003 it was devastated by scandal: player Patrick Dennehy was murdered by teammate Carlton Dotson, and subsequent investigations revealed extensive rules violations under coach Dave Bliss, leading to severe NCAA sanctions including the cancellation of the team's non-conference schedule in 2005–06.

Scott Drew, hired in 2003 from Valparaiso, undertook one of the most notable rebuilding projects in college basketball history. Baylor returned to the NCAA Tournament in 2008 and reached the Elite Eight in 2010 and 2012. The Bears won the NIT in 2013.

National championship

In the 2020–21 season, Baylor opened 18–0, won its first conference regular-season title since 1950, and advanced to the Final Four. In the national championship game on 5 April 2021, the Bears defeated previously unbeaten Gonzaga 86–70, led by Jared Butler, Davion Mitchell, and MaCio Teague, securing the school's first men's basketball national title. The team moved from the Ferrell Center to the new Foster Pavilion in 2024.
