Keke Rosberg

Keijo Erik Rosberg (born 6 December 1948), best known as "Keke", is a Finnish former racing driver and the winner of the 1982 Formula One World Championship. He was the first Finnish driver to compete regularly in Formula One and to win the championship. He is the father of Nico Rosberg, the 2016 Formula One World Champion.

Career

Rosberg made his Formula One debut in 1978 and drove for Theodore, ATS, Wolf, Fittipaldi, and finally Williams, where he found success. In the chaotic 1982 season, marked by tragedy and technical upheaval, he won only one race — the Swiss Grand Prix at Dijon — but scored consistently enough to take the title with Williams.

Later life

After retiring from Formula One at the end of 1986, Rosberg raced in sports cars and touring cars, founded his own team in DTM, and managed drivers including Mika Häkkinen and his son Nico.
