Pan's Labyrinth (Spanish: El laberinto del fauno, lit. "The Labyrinth of the Faun") is a 2006 dark fantasy film written and directed by Guillermo del Toro. Set in Spain in 1944, in the aftermath of the Spanish Civil War, it interweaves the story of Ofelia, the young stepdaughter of a sadistic Falangist captain, with a fairy-tale underworld in which a faun sets her three tasks to prove she is the reincarnated Princess Moanna.

Plot

Ofelia travels with her pregnant mother to a rural mill where Captain Vidal hunts anti-Franco guerrillas. Guided by a fairy, she meets the Faun, completes perilous tasks — including evading the child-eating Pale Man — and ultimately refuses to spill her baby brother's blood, sacrificing herself and, in the closing vision, taking her place in the underworld kingdom.

Reception

The film premiered at the 2006 Cannes Film Festival to a long standing ovation and won three Academy Awards (Cinematography, Art Direction, and Makeup) from six nominations. It is widely regarded as one of the greatest fantasy films ever made and a landmark of Spanish-language cinema.
