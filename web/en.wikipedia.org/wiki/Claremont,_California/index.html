Claremont, California

Claremont is a suburban city in Los Angeles County, California, United States, located on the eastern edge of the county, in the Pomona Valley at the foothills of the San Gabriel Mountains, approximately 30 miles (48 km) east of downtown Los Angeles. The population was about 37,000 at the 2020 census. Claremont is known as the home of the Claremont Colleges, a consortium of seven independent institutions of higher education, and for its tree-lined streets, which have earned it the nickname "the City of Trees and PhDs".

History

The area was originally home to the Tongva people. Claremont was founded in 1887, when the Santa Fe Railway built a station on its line through the Pomona Valley; the town was named after Claremont, New Hampshire. Pomona College, founded the same year and initially located in Pomona, moved to Claremont in 1889 and became the nucleus of a cluster of colleges modelled in part on Oxford and Cambridge, an idea advanced by president James Blaisdell. Citrus groves covered much of the town in the early twentieth century; after their decline, residential neighbourhoods replaced them. The city was incorporated in 1907.

The Claremont Colleges

The Claremont Colleges — Pomona College, Scripps College, Claremont McKenna College, Harvey Mudd College, and Pitzer College, together with Claremont Graduate University and Keck Graduate Institute — share facilities and a central library system while retaining separate identities, in a grouping often compared to the Oxbridge collegiate model. The consortium enrolls more than 8,000 students and is a dominant presence in the city's economy and culture. Also in Claremont are the Rancho Santa Ana Botanic Garden (California Botanic Garden), devoted to native plants, and the Folk Music Center founded by Ben Harper's family.

Geography and community

Claremont borders Upland and Montclair in San Bernardino County to the east, Pomona to the south, La Verne to the west, and San Antonio Heights to the north. The city's downtown "Village" district retains many historic buildings and hosts a large weekly farmers' market. Claremont has repeatedly been recognised for its urban forest and sustainability measures, and in 2007 it was named one of the best places to live in the United States by Money magazine. The city is served by the Metrolink San Bernardino Line and by Interstate 10 and State Route 210.
