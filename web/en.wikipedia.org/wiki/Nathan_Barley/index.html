Nathan Barley

Created by: Charlie Brooker, Chris Morris
Starring: Nicholas Burns
Network: Channel 4
Original run: 2005

Nathan Barley is a British television sitcom created by Charlie Brooker and Chris Morris, broadcast on Channel 4 in 2005. It satirises the shallow, self-deluded media hipsters of east London, centred on the title character, a web designer and self-styled "self-facilitating media node".

Background

The character originated in Brooker's satirical TV listings website TVGoHome, where Nathan Barley's fictional programme listings mocked the pretensions of dot-com-era media culture. The television series expanded this into a full sitcom set around the fictional magazine Sugar Ape (stylised "SugarAPE") and the offices of a trend-chasing production company.

Plot and themes

Nathan (Nicholas Burns) is a talentless but supremely confident Hoxton trendsetter whose website, trashbat.co.ck, epitomises meaningless digital hype. The series contrasts him with Dan Ashcroft, a despairing journalist whose angry article about "the idiots" becomes, to his horror, the hipsters' manifesto. The show skewered media jargon, vacuous cool and the emerging culture of online self-promotion, and has been praised as prescient about social media.
