Back to the Future Part III is a 1990 American science fiction Western film written and directed by Robert Zemeckis, and the third and final instalment of the Back to the Future trilogy. It stars Michael J. Fox, Christopher Lloyd, Mary Steenburgen, Thomas F. Wilson and Lea Thompson. The film continues immediately from Part II (1989), with which it was filmed back-to-back.

Plot

Stranded in 1955, Marty McFly learns that Dr. Emmett "Doc" Brown, sent back to 1885, was shot and killed by Buford "Mad Dog" Tannen only days after writing a letter instructing that the DeLorean be destroyed. Marty travels to 1885 to save him, arriving in a Hill Valley of the Wild West. Doc, now working as a blacksmith, has fallen in love with schoolteacher Clara Clayton, whose death in a ravine Marty has already prevented, altering the timeline. With no petrol for the damaged DeLorean, the pair plan to push it to 88 mph with a steam locomotive. Marty defeats Buford in a gunfight standoff — refusing the bait of being called "chicken" — and the train heist succeeds: Marty returns to 1985 as the DeLorean is destroyed by a modern freight train. Doc, remaining in the past with Clara, later visits Marty in a steam-powered time-travelling locomotive with his family, advising that the future is unwritten.

Production and reception

Filming took place largely in California and Arizona (Monument Valley), with a full Western town built near Sonora. ZZ Top, who performed the theme "Doubleback", appear as the band at the town festival. The film received generally positive reviews, praised as a warm and satisfying conclusion to the trilogy, and grossed about $245 million worldwide against a budget of about $40 million — less than its predecessors but a commercial success.
