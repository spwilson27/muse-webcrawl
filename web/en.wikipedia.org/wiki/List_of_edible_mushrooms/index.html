List of edible mushrooms

This is a list of edible mushrooms — fungi whose fruiting bodies are consumed as food. Edible mushrooms are cultivated commercially or collected from the wild in many cultures; a small number are prized delicacies, while many others are staples.

Safety

Many edible species have toxic look-alikes; for example, chanterelles can be confused with the poisonous jack-o'-lantern mushroom. Wild mushrooms should only be eaten when identified with certainty, and some species, such as raw morels, are toxic unless cooked.
