The series made its star, Volodymyr Zelenskyy, president of Ukraine in fiction—and then in reality.

Servant of the People (Ukrainian: Слуга народу, Sluha narodu) is a Ukrainian political satire television series created and produced by Volodymyr Zelenskyy and his studio Kvartal 95. Zelenskyy stars as Vasyl Petrovych Holoborodko, an honest, unassuming history teacher whose secretly recorded classroom rant against corruption goes viral, leading the public to crowdfund his candidacy and elect him President of Ukraine.

Plot

As president, Holoborodko—a modest man who continues to live in his parents' apartment and cycle to work—battles the entrenched oligarchs, corrupt officials, and absurd bureaucracy of Ukrainian politics, attempting reforms while navigating the machinations of his scheming prime minister and the influential families who truly run the country. Three seasons and a feature film, Servant of the People 2 (2016), were produced between 2015 and 2019.

From fiction to reality

The series became a political phenomenon. In 2017–2018, Zelenskyy's team registered a real political party named Servant of the People after the show. Zelenskyy announced his own presidential candidacy on New Year's Eve 2018 and won the 2019 Ukrainian presidential election in a landslide, taking roughly 73% of the vote in the second round against incumbent Petro Poroshenko. His party then won a parliamentary majority—the first in Ukraine's history—in snap elections that year. Following Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, the series gained a large international audience via streaming services such as Netflix, where it was widely watched as audiences sought to understand Zelenskyy's background.
