Dr. Hannibal Lecter is a fictional character created by novelist Thomas Harris. A brilliant forensic psychiatrist and cannibalistic serial killer, he first appeared as a supporting character in the novel Red Dragon (1981) and became central in The Silence of the Lambs (1988), Hannibal (1999) and the prequel Hannibal Rising (2006).

Character

Of Lithuanian aristocratic descent, Lecter is depicted as highly intelligent, cultured and courteous, with refined tastes in art, music and food — traits that contrast with the murders and cannibalism that earned him the nickname "Hannibal the Cannibal". Captured by FBI profiler Will Graham, he is held in the Baltimore State Hospital for the Criminally Insane, from where he assists investigators Graham and Clarice Starling in catching other killers.

Portrayals and reception

Anthony Hopkins won the Academy Award for Best Actor for his portrayal in the film The Silence of the Lambs (1991), and reprised the role in Hannibal (2001) and Red Dragon (2002). The character was ranked the greatest movie villain of all time by the American Film Institute. Brian Cox played the role (as "Lecktor") in Manhunter (1986), and Mads Mikkelsen portrayed Lecter in the television series Hannibal (2013–2015), to wide critical acclaim.
