Confucius was a Chinese philosopher and politician of the Spring and Autumn period traditionally considered the paragon of Chinese sages. His philosophy, Confucianism, emphasises personal and governmental morality, correctness of social relationships, justice, kindness and sincerity.

Influence

Confucius taught that society coheres through ritual, filial piety and the cultivation of virtue (ren) in rulers and subjects alike. His thought shaped Chinese, Korean, Japanese and Vietnamese civilisation for two millennia — civil-service examinations tested his classics (see School) — and remains central to East Asian culture.
