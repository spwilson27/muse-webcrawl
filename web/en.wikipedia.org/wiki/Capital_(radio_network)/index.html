Capital is a network of twelve independent contemporary hit radio stations in the United Kingdom, broadcasting a mix of local and networked programming, owned and operated by Global. It launched nationally on 3 January 2011, when the former Galaxy Network and several local stations were rebranded under the name of London's Capital London (originally Capital Radio), which began broadcasting in 1973 as one of Britain's first legal commercial radio stations.

Programming

The network carries Capital Breakfast — hosted from London by Roman Kemp for many years — and networked daytime and evening shows alongside local drivetime programmes. Signature events include the Summertime Ball at Wembley Stadium and the Jingle Bell Ball, which feature major international pop acts.

History

Capital Radio won the London general entertainment licence and went on air on 16 October 1973, days after LBC. Over subsequent decades its owners built a group that merged into GCap Media and was acquired by Global in 2008. Ofcom's relaxation of localness rules enabled the 2011 national rebrand, making Capital a direct competitor to BBC Radio 1 for young listeners.

Sister stations

Global also operates Capital Xtra and the brand extension Capital Dance, launched in 2020, alongside the Heart, Smooth and LBC networks.
