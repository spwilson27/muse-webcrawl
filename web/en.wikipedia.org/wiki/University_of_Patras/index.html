The University of Patras (Greek: Πανεπιστήμιο Πατρών) is a public university in Patras, Greece. Founded on 11 November 1964, it is the third-largest university in the country by student population and is situated on a large self-contained campus at Rio, about 7 km northeast of the city of Patras, near the Rio–Antirrio Bridge.

History

The university was established as part of a national plan to expand higher education beyond Athens and Thessaloniki. It began with schools of natural sciences, engineering, and medicine, and later absorbed the University of Western Greece in 2019, expanding its departments into Agrinio and other cities of the region.

Organization

The university comprises schools of Natural Sciences, Engineering, Health Sciences, Humanities and Social Sciences, Economics and Business, and Agricultural Sciences, with more than 30 departments. Its teaching hospital, the University General Hospital of Patras, is a major referral center for western Greece.

Research and rankings

The University of Patras is regularly ranked among the top Greek universities in international league tables and is noted internationally for engineering, computer science, and materials research. Its emblem depicts Saint Andrew, patron saint of Patras, whose crucifixion cross (the saltire) the city claims as a relic.
