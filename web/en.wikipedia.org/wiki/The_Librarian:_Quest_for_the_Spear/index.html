The Librarian: Quest for the Spear is a 2004 American made-for-television fantasy-adventure film directed by Peter Winther and starring Noah Wyle as Flynn Carsen. It is the first film in The Librarian franchise, and premiered on TNT on December 5, 2004.

Plot

Flynn Carsen, a perpetual student in his thirties with 22 academic degrees, is forced out of university and applies for a job at the Metropolitan Public Library. He is hired by the eccentric Judson (Bob Newhart) and Charlene (Jane Curtin) to a secret position: the Librarian, guardian of a vast hidden collection of magical and historical artifacts—including Excalibur, the Holy Grail, Pandora's box, and the Ark of the Covenant—stored beneath the library. When one of three pieces of the Spear of Destiny is stolen by the villainous Serpent Brotherhood, led by Edward Wilde (Kyle MacLachlan), Flynn is dispatched around the globe, aided by bodyguard Nicole Noone (Sonya Walger), to recover the remaining pieces in the Amazon rainforest and the Himalayas before the Brotherhood can assemble the spear and gain its power.

Sequels and series

The film's success led to two sequels: The Librarian: Return to King Solomon's Mines (2006) and The Librarian: Curse of the Judas Chalice (2008), and a television series, The Librarians (2014–2018), in which Wyle reprised his role in a recurring capacity.

Reception

The film drew strong ratings for TNT—over 7 million viewers—and received mixed-to-positive reviews, with praise for its lighthearted, Indiana Jones-style adventure tone. It was nominated for a Saturn Award for Best Television Presentation.
