Polygonatum
Family: Asparagaceae
Common name: Solomon's seal
Species: ~70

Polygonatum, commonly known as Solomon's seal, is a genus of flowering plants in the asparagus family, Asparagaceae, comprising roughly 70 species found across temperate regions of the Northern Hemisphere.

Description

Plants grow from creeping rhizomes that bear circular scars said to resemble a seal stamp. Arching stems carry alternate or whorled leaves, with small white or greenish bell-shaped flowers hanging beneath the leaf axils, followed by red or blue-black berries.

Uses

Several species, such as Polygonatum multiflorum and Polygonatum odoratum, are grown as shade-tolerant garden plants. The rhizomes (known as yuzhu) are used in traditional Chinese medicine and as food.
