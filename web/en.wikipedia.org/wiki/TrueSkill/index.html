TrueSkill

TrueSkill is a Bayesian skill-rating system developed by Microsoft Research and used for matchmaking on Xbox Live. Unlike the Elo rating system, which represents a player's skill by a single number, TrueSkill models each player's skill as a Gaussian distribution characterised by a mean (μ) and a standard deviation (σ) expressing the system's uncertainty about that skill.

How it works

New players start with μ = 25 and σ = 25/3. After each game, the system updates the distributions using Bayesian inference over a factor graph, adjusting means according to the outcome and shrinking σ as more evidence accumulates. A player's displayed rank is a conservative estimate, μ − 3σ. TrueSkill generalises naturally to multiplayer and team games, handling free-for-all matches and arbitrary team compositions that defeat pairwise systems such as Elo. It also predicts draw probabilities and can optimise matchmaking by pairing players whose skills are uncertain and close.

Successors

Microsoft later introduced TrueSkill 2, which incorporates additional in-game performance signals and player experience, and published the system in use across titles such as Halo, Gears of War, and Forza.
