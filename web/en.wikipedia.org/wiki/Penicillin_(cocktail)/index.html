Penicillin (cocktail)

The Penicillin is a whisky-based cocktail created in 2005 by Australian bartender Sam Ross at the Milk & Honey bar in New York City. It is regarded as a modern classic of the craft cocktail movement.

The drink is made with blended Scotch whisky, fresh lemon juice, and a honey-ginger syrup, shaken with ice and served over ice in a rocks glass, with a float of peaty single malt Scotch (typically from Islay) on top and a garnish of candied ginger. The combination of smoke, citrus, honey, and ginger gives the cocktail its distinctive flavour; the name is a playful reference to the drink's supposed restorative, "medicinal" qualities.

The Penicillin is an International Bartenders Association (IBA) official cocktail in the "new era drinks" category.
