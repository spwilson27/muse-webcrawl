Hack and slash

Hack and slash is a genre or style of gameplay that emphasises combat, originally used for tabletop role-playing games in which adventuring focused on killing monsters rather than role-playing. The term was later applied to video games featuring fast-paced melee combat against waves of enemies.

Video games

In video games, hack-and-slash titles range from arcade beat 'em ups such as Golden Axe to action role-playing games like the Diablo series and character-action games such as Devil May Cry and God of War, combining combos, weapon upgrades and boss fights.
