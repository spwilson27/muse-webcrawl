Tourism in the Republic of Ireland

Tourism in IrelandVisitors: about 11 million annually (pre-2020 peak)
Key attractions: Dublin, Cliffs of Moher, Ring of Kerry, Giant's Causeway (NI)

Tourism in the Republic of Ireland is one of the largest contributors to the Irish economy, employing hundreds of thousands of people and drawing visitors principally from Britain, the United States, Germany and France.

Attractions

Dublin offers literary heritage, Trinity College and the Book of Kells, and the Guinness Storehouse, consistently among the country's most visited paid attractions. Elsewhere, the Wild Atlantic Way touring route, the Cliffs of Moher, Killarney National Park and the Ring of Kerry showcase Ireland's coastal scenery.

Heritage and culture

Historic sites such as Newgrange, the Rock of Cashel and Kilmainham Gaol, together with traditional music, festivals and the Irish diaspora's ancestral tourism, form a central part of the visitor offering.

Promotion

Tourism Ireland markets the island of Ireland overseas, while Fáilte Ireland develops tourism within the Republic. The industry recovered strongly after the COVID-19 pandemic.
