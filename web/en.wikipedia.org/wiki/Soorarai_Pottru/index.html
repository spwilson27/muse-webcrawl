Soorarai Pottru

Soorarai Pottru (transl. Praise the Brave) is a 2020 Indian Tamil-language drama film directed by Sudha Kongara and produced by Suriya and Guneet Monga. The film stars Suriya, Aparna Balamurali and Paresh Rawal. It is partly inspired by events from the life of G. R. Gopinath, founder of the low-cost airline Air Deccan, as described in his memoir Simply Fly: A Deccan Odyssey.

Plot

Nedumaaran Rajangam ("Maara"), a former Indian Air Force captain from a poor village background, dreams of founding a low-cost airline so that ordinary people can afford to fly. Facing obstruction from the aviation establishment and the ruthless airline magnate Paresh Goswami, Maara perseveres with the support of his wife Sundari ("Bommi"), who runs her own bakery business. After years of setbacks, his venture finally takes off.

Release and reception

Originally planned for a theatrical release, the film premiered on Amazon Prime Video on 12 November 2020, during the COVID-19 pandemic. It received widespread critical acclaim for its direction, screenplay and performances, particularly those of Suriya and Aparna Balamurali. The music was composed by G. V. Prakash Kumar.

Accolades

At the 68th National Film Awards the film won five awards, including Best Feature Film, Best Actor (Suriya), Best Actress (Aparna Balamurali), Best Screenplay and Best Music Direction (background score). It was among the Indian films considered for selection at the Academy Awards and was later remade in Hindi as Sarfira (2024).
