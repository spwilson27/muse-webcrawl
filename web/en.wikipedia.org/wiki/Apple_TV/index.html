Apple TV

Apple TV is a digital media player and microconsole developed and marketed by Apple Inc. It is a small network appliance that plays media content from streaming services, local networks, and Apple devices via AirPlay, outputting to a connected television.

Developer: Apple Inc.
Type: Digital media player / microconsole
Introduced: 2007
Operating system: tvOS

History

The first-generation Apple TV was introduced in 2007 and included a hard drive for storing media synced from iTunes. Later generations shifted to a streaming-focused design. The fourth generation, released in 2015, introduced tvOS, an App Store, and a touch-based Siri Remote. The Apple TV 4K models added support for 4K resolution, HDR formats such as Dolby Vision, and more powerful chips.

Features

Apple TV runs apps for video streaming, music, games, and fitness, integrates with the Apple TV app and Apple TV+ service, and supports HomeKit smart-home control, Apple Arcade, SharePlay, and spatial audio with compatible AirPods.
