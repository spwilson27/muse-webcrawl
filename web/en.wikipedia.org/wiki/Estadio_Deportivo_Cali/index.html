The Estadio Deportivo Cali, also known as the Estadio de Palmaseca, is a football stadium in Palmira, Colombia, in the Cali metropolitan area. It is the home ground of Deportivo Cali and was, on its completion, the largest stadium in Colombia.

Construction and opening

Deportivo Cali had long played at the Estadio Olímpico Pascual Guerrero, owned by the city of Cali. Seeking a ground of their own, the club built the Palmaseca stadium on land on the road between Cali and Palmira; construction began in 2007 and the stadium was inaugurated on 21 February 2010. Club president Humberto Arias and the team's independence were central themes of the project.

Design

Because of local environmental rules limiting building height in the sugarcane-growing area, much of the bowl is sunk below ground level, with the lower tiers in an excavated basin. The stadium originally held around 52,000 spectators, later reduced to roughly 42,000 as seating was installed throughout. It was among the first Colombian stadiums to include extensive hospitality suites and on-site parking.
