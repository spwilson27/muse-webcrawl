Breakthrough Starshot
Announced: 12 April 2016
Founders: Yuri Milner, Stephen Hawking, Mark Zuckerberg
Target: Alpha Centauri, 4.37 ly
Budget: $100 million research

Breakthrough Starshot is a research and engineering project by the Breakthrough Initiatives aiming to develop a proof-of-concept fleet of gram-scale interstellar probes ("StarChips") propelled by ground-based laser-pushed lightsails to about 20% of the speed of light, reaching Alpha Centauri in about 20 years.

Concept

Each nanocraft combines a centimetre-scale chip (camera, photon thrusters, power, communications) with a metre-scale lightsail a few hundred atoms thick, accelerated by a phased laser array of up to 100 GW. The target system includes Proxima Centauri b, an Earth-size planet in the habitable zone.

Challenges

Sail survival, laser focusing over millions of kilometres, interstellar dust collisions, and communicating back across 4 light-years with a tiny laser remain major unsolved problems. See Interstellar travel, Solar sail.
