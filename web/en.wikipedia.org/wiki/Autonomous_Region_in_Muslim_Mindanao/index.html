Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (ARMM)

Country: Philippines
Created: 1989
Dissolved: 2019
Succeeded by: Bangsamoro Autonomous Region

The Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (ARMM) was an autonomous region of the Philippines, located in the Mindanao island group. Created in 1989 under Republic Act No. 6734 following a plebiscite, it comprised provinces with predominantly Muslim populations: Lanao del Sur, Maguindanao, Basilan, Sulu and Tawi-Tawi, and later the city of Marawi and others at various times.

History

The region was the product of peace negotiations between the Philippine government and the Moro National Liberation Front, culminating in the 1996 Final Peace Agreement. Its powers and territory were expanded in 2001.

Dissolution

Following the ratification of the Bangsamoro Organic Law, ARMM was abolished in 2019 and replaced by the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM), which also incorporated areas that voted to join in a 2019 plebiscite.
