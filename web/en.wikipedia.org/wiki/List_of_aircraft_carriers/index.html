List of aircraft carriers

This is a list of aircraft carriers of the world's navies. Carriers in current service range from large nuclear-powered fleet carriers (supercarriers) to light carriers operating STOVL aircraft or helicopters.

Historical notes

The first full-length flat-deck carrier was HMS Argus (1918). Carriers were decisive in the Second World War, notably at Pearl Harbor, Midway and in the Battle of the Philippine Sea. Famous retired or lost carriers include HMS Ark Royal, USS Enterprise (CV-6), USS Lexington, USS Yorktown, and the Japanese carriers sunk at Midway — Akagi, Kaga, Sōryū and Hiryū. The United States also operates nine amphibious assault ships (LHA/LHD) that function as light carriers.
