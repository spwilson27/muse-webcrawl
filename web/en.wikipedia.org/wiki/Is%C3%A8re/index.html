Country: France

Department number: 38

Isère is a department in the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region of southeastern France. It takes its name from the river Isère, a left tributary of the Rhône. Its prefecture is Grenoble, and it is one of the most populous and geographically varied departments in France, spanning lowland plains, the foothills of the Alps, and high mountain terrain.

Geography

The department includes parts of the Chartreuse, Belledonne, and Écrins mountain ranges, and major ski resorts such as Alpe d'Huez and Les Deux Alpes. The Isère river flows through Grenoble and across the department toward the Rhône.

History

The department was created during the French Revolution on 4 March 1790. The area has a long industrial and scientific tradition; Grenoble is a major center for research in physics, microelectronics, and computer science.

Tourism

Isère attracts visitors for alpine sports, including stages of the Tour de France that climb Alpe d'Huez, and for historic sites such as the Chartreuse monastery and the Bastille fortress overlooking Grenoble.
