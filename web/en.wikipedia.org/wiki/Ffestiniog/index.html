Ffestiniog is a town and community in Gwynedd, Wales, situated at the top of the Vale of Ffestiniog on the edge of the Snowdonia National Park. It is known historically for its connection with the slate industry and the Ffestiniog Railway.

History

The town grew substantially in the 19th century with the development of slate quarrying in the area, particularly around the neighbouring village of Blaenau Ffestiniog, which became one of the major centres of the Welsh slate industry. The Ffestiniog Railway, opened in 1836, carried slate from the quarries down to the harbour at Porthmadog.

Ffestiniog is home to the Ffestiniog pumped-storage hydroelectric power station, commissioned in 1963, which uses the Llyn Stwlan reservoir. The station was the first major pumped-storage scheme in the United Kingdom.

Language and culture

Ffestiniog is a strongly Welsh-speaking community; a large majority of residents speak Welsh. The town hosts cultural events and is a gateway to walks in the Moelwyn mountains.
