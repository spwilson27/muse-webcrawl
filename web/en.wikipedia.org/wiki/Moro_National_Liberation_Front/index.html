The Moro National Liberation Front (MNLF) is a political organisation in the Philippines that was founded in 1972 to seek independence or autonomy for the Moro (Muslim Filipino) peoples of Mindanao, Palawan, and the Sulu Archipelago, a homeland they call Bangsamoro.

History

The MNLF, led by former university professor Nur Misuari, launched an armed rebellion against the government of Ferdinand Marcos following the declaration of martial law and in the wake of the 1968 Jabidah massacre. The Tripoli Agreement of 1976, brokered by Libya, promised autonomy but broke down, and fighting continued. A splinter group formed the Moro Islamic Liberation Front (MILF) in the late 1970s.

Peace agreement

In 1996 the MNLF signed a Final Peace Agreement with the government of Fidel V. Ramos, and Misuari became governor of the Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (ARMM). The MNLF was recognised by the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation as the legitimate representative of the Bangsamoro people. Discontented factions clashed with the government again, notably in the 2013 Zamboanga City crisis. The Bangsamoro Autonomous Region created in 2019 is led principally by the MILF.
