Graph

Graph may refer to several related concepts, listed below.

Mathematics and computing

  • Graph (discrete mathematics) — a structure made of vertices (nodes) connected by edges; the subject of graph theory, originating in Euler's 1736 solution of the Seven Bridges of Königsberg problem.
  • Graph of a function — the set of ordered pairs (x, f(x)) of a function, typically drawn as a curve or surface in a coordinate system.
  • Graph theory — the branch of mathematics studying graphs, paths, colouring, trees and networks.
  • Graph (abstract data type) — in computer science, a data structure representing networks, used in routing, social-network and dependency analysis.
  • Graph database — a database that uses graph structures with nodes, edges and properties to represent and store data.
  • Conceptual graph, scene graph, signal-flow graph and graph rewriting — further specialised structures.

Data display

  • Chart — sometimes informally called a graph: a graphical representation of data, including bar graphs, line graphs, pie charts and scatter plots.
  • Graph paper — paper printed with a regular grid for plotting.

Other uses

  • Graph (linguistics) — a unit of a writing system, or the smallest written unit; related to the grapheme.
  • -graph- — a Greek-derived combining form meaning "written" or "drawn", as in photograph, telegraph and autograph.
  • Graph, in commerce-records usage, part of the word's appearance in product and company names.
