List of Pokémon episodes

Pokémon, known in Japan as Pocket Monsters, is a Japanese anime television series produced by OLM, based on the Pokémon video game series by Nintendo and Game Freak. The series follows the adventures of Pokémon trainers — principally Ash Ketchum (Satoshi in Japan) for its first 25 seasons — as they travel, battle, and catch Pokémon. It premiered on TV Tokyo on April 1, 1997, and has produced well over 1,200 episodes.

Ash and Pikachu headlined the series for more than 25 years.

Notable episodes

The first episode, "Pokémon, I Choose You!", aired April 1, 1997, and introduced Ash and Pikachu. The episode "Dennō Senshi Porygon" (December 1997) caused photosensitive seizures in hundreds of Japanese viewers due to flashing red and blue lights, leading to a four-month hiatus and new broadcasting guidelines; it has never been re-aired. Ash's story concluded in 2023 with the mini-series "Aim to Be a Pokémon Master", after he won the World Coronation Series in Pokémon Ultimate Journeys.

Films and specials

The series has been accompanied by more than twenty animated feature films, numerous Pikachu shorts, television specials, and side-story series such as Pokémon Chronicles, Pokémon Origins, and Pokémon Evolutions.
