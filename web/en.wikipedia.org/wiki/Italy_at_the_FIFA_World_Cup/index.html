Italy at the FIFA World Cup

Italy is one of the most successful national teams in the FIFA World Cup, having won the tournament four times: in 1934 and 1938 under coach Vittorio Pozzo, in 1982 under Enzo Bearzot, and in 2006 under Marcello Lippi. Italy also finished as runners-up in 1970 and 1994.

Record

Italy first entered in 1934, as hosts, defeating Czechoslovakia in the final, and retained the title in France in 1938. The 1982 triumph in Spain was led by Paolo Rossi, the tournament's top scorer. In 2006 Italy beat France on penalties in the Berlin final, a match remembered for Zinedine Zidane's dismissal. Remarkably, after 2006 Italy failed to progress beyond the group stage in 2010 and 2014 and failed to qualify at all for the 2018 and 2022 tournaments.

Notable players

Record World Cup appearances and goals involve players such as Paolo Maldini, Fabio Cannavaro, Gianluigi Buffon and Roberto Baggio.
