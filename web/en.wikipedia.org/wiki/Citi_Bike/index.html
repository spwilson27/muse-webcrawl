Citi Bike

Citi Bike is a privately owned public bicycle sharing system serving New York City and Jersey City, New Jersey. Named after lead sponsor Citigroup, it began operations on 27 May 2013 and has grown into one of the largest bike-share systems in the world, with tens of thousands of bicycles and more than two thousand docking stations.

History

Citi Bike launched in Manhattan and Brooklyn in 2013 after delays caused by software problems and Hurricane Sandy. The system was initially operated by Alta Bicycle Share; after financial difficulties it was acquired by Bikeshare Holdings in 2014 and later by the rideshare company Lyft in 2018. Successive expansion phases brought stations to all five boroughs and to Jersey City (2015), and pedal-assist electric bikes were introduced from 2019.

Usage

Riders unlock bikes with an app, key fob or kiosk and must return them to a dock within a set time (30–45 minutes depending on membership) to avoid extra fees. Annual memberships, single rides and day passes are offered; the fleet includes classic pedal bikes and e-bikes, and ridership peaks in the summer months.
