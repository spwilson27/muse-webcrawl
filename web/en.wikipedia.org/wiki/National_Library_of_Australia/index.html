National Library of Australia
Established: 1960 (National Library Act)
Location: Parkes, Canberra, Australian Capital Territory
Collection: Millions of items, including Australian publications under legal deposit

The National Library of Australia (NLA) is the country's largest reference library and its national library. Operating under the National Library Act 1960, it is responsible for collecting, preserving, and providing access to Australia's documentary heritage, including copies of works published in Australia under legal deposit.

Collections and services

The Library's building, opened in 1968 on the shores of Lake Burley Griffin in Canberra, houses extensive collections of books, manuscripts, maps, photographs, newspapers, and oral histories. Its digital services include Trove, a widely used discovery platform aggregating digitised newspapers and other content from Australian libraries and archives, and the Australian Web Archive.
