Rakuten Kobo

Rakuten Kobo Inc., known as Kobo, is a Canadian company that sells e-books, audiobooks, and e-readers. Founded in Toronto in 2009 as a spin-off of the Indigo Books & Music retail chain, Kobo was acquired by the Japanese e-commerce company Rakuten in 2011 for approximately US$315 million and thereafter operated as Rakuten Kobo.

History

Kobo began as a cloud-based e-reading service and launched its first e-reader, the Kobo eReader, in 2010. The company positioned itself as an international alternative to Amazon's Kindle, partnering with local booksellers and retailers — including the American Booksellers Association, WHSmith in the United Kingdom, and FNAC — to distribute its devices and storefronts in many countries.

Products

Kobo's hardware line includes the entry-level Kobo Nia, the waterproof Kobo Libra and Kobo Sage, and the Kobo Elipsa, which supports stylus note-taking on a large E Ink display. Its e-book store carries millions of titles, and the Kobo Plus subscription offers unlimited access to a catalogue of e-books and audiobooks in participating markets.
