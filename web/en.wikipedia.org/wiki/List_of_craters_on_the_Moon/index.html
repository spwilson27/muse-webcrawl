List of craters on the Moon

The Moon's surface is covered with impact craters formed by collisions with asteroids and comets. More than 9,000 craters have been catalogued and named by the International Astronomical Union; most are named after deceased scientists, scholars, artists, and explorers. The following is a selection of the largest and most notable named lunar craters.

Satellite craters are designated by the name of the parent crater followed by a capital letter (for example, Copernicus A). The South Pole–Aitken basin, at roughly 2,500 km across, is the largest known impact structure on the Moon but is classified as a basin rather than a crater.
