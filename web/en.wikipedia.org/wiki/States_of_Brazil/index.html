States of Brazil

The Federative Republic of Brazil is a federation composed of 26 states (estados) and the Federal District (Distrito Federal), which hosts the capital, Brasília. The states are grouped into five geographic regions: North, Northeast, Central-West, Southeast, and South.

List

States include Acre, Alagoas, Amapá, Amazonas, Bahia, Ceará, Espírito Santo, Goiás, Maranhão, Mato Grosso, Mato Grosso do Sul, Minas Gerais, Pará, Paraíba, Paraná, Pernambuco, Piauí, Rio de Janeiro, Rio Grande do Norte, Rio Grande do Sul, Rondônia, Roraima, Santa Catarina, São Paulo, Sergipe and Tocantins (the newest, created in 1988).

Government

Each state has a governor and a legislative assembly, its own constitution within federal limits, and state courts. Municipalities form the third level of the federation.

History

The states descend from Portuguese captaincies and imperial provinces; the republic of 1889 converted provinces into states. Boundaries and new states were added through the 20th century.
