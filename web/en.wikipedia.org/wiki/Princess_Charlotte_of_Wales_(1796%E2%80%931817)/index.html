Princess Charlotte of Wales (1796–1817)

Princess Charlotte of Wales
Born: 7 January 1796, Carlton House, London
Died: 6 November 1817, Claremont House, Surrey
Spouse: Prince Leopold of Saxe-Coburg-Saalfeld
Father: George, Prince Regent (later George IV)

Princess Charlotte Augusta of Wales (7 January 1796 – 6 November 1817) was the only child of the Prince Regent (the future George IV) and Caroline of Brunswick. As George III's only legitimate grandchild for much of her life, she was second in line to the British throne and expected eventually to become queen.

Life

Charlotte's parents separated shortly after her birth and detested each other; she was raised largely apart from both. After breaking an engagement to William, Prince of Orange, she married Prince Leopold of Saxe-Coburg-Saalfeld in May 1816, a happy union.

Death and consequences

On 5 November 1817, after a labour lasting some fifty hours, Charlotte delivered a stillborn son and died the following morning, probably from postpartum haemorrhage. Her death plunged Britain into public mourning and left the succession uncertain, prompting a scramble among George III's sons to marry and produce heirs — the race that ultimately led to the birth of Queen Victoria.
