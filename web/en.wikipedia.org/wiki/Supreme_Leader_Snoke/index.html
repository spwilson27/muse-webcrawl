Supreme Leader Snoke is a fictional character in the Star Wars franchise. He appears in the sequel trilogy films The Force Awakens (2015) and The Last Jedi (2017), portrayed through motion capture and voiced by Andy Serkis. Snoke is the tyrannical Supreme Leader of the First Order, a powerful user of the dark side of the Force who corrupted Ben Solo into Kylo Ren.

Snoke on his throne aboard the Supremacy

Appearances

In The Force Awakens, Snoke appears only as a giant hologram, directing General Hux and Kylo Ren in the use of Starkiller Base. In The Last Jedi, he appears in person aboard his flagship, the Supremacy, where he tortures Rey and orders Kylo Ren to kill her; instead, Ren uses the Force to ignite a lightsaber beside Snoke's throne, cutting him in half and seizing the title of Supreme Leader himself.

Origins

The Rise of Skywalker (2019) reveals that Snoke was an artificial "strandcast" created by the resurrected Emperor Palpatine — a puppet through whom Palpatine secretly controlled the First Order; tanks of Snoke clones are seen in the Emperor's citadel on Exegol. Novelisations describe Snoke's origins in the Unknown Regions and his role in the fall of the New Republic era.

Reception

The character's sudden death and initially unexplained background were subjects of extensive fan speculation and debate, with some critics praising the subversion and others finding the character underdeveloped.
