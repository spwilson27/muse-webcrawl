Hardcourt bike polo

Hardcourt bike polo is a variant of traditional bicycle polo played on hard surfaces such as street hockey courts and car parks, rather than on grass. It emerged in the late 1990s and early 2000s among bicycle messengers and urban cyclists in the United States.

Gameplay

Teams of three players ride fixed-gear or single-speed bicycles and use mallets to hit a small ball toward the opposing goal. Players may not put a foot down ("dabbing"); a player who does must "tap out" at mid-court before rejoining play. Goals typically must be scored with the narrow end of the mallet ("shot"), not the wide side ("shuffle").

Competitions

The first World Hardcourt Bike Polo Championship was held in Toronto in 2009. Regional championships are held in North America, Europe and Australasia, coordinated in part by the North American Hardcourt Bike Polo Association.
