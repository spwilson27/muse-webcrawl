Baking

Baking
DefinitionCooking food by prolonged dry heat in an oven
StaplesBread, cakes, pastries, pies
[Image: Golden loaves and pastries cooling on a bakery rack]

Baking is a method of preparing food that uses prolonged dry heat, normally in an oven. Bread is the oldest baked staple, followed by cakes, cookies, pies and pastries; the baker is among the oldest specialised trades in any city.

Technique

Baking is chemistry made delicious: gluten development, leavening by yeast or baking powder, and precise ratios matter more than in most cooking — hence its reputation as the exact branch. Home baking surged during the pandemic sourdough craze, when starter cultures became household pets.
