Gobelins Manufactory

The Gobelins Manufactory (French: Manufacture des Gobelins) is a historic tapestry factory in Paris, France. It is located at 42 avenue des Gobelins, near Les Gobelins station on the 13th arrondissement. It was originally established on the site as a medieval dyeing business by the family Gobelin.

In 1662 the workshops were purchased by Jean-Baptiste Colbert on behalf of Louis XIV and reorganised as the Manufacture royale des meubles de la Couronne, a royal manufactory producing tapestries, furniture and other luxuries for the French court under the artistic direction of Charles Le Brun. Famous series woven there include the History of the King and the Elements, from designs by Le Brun and later artists such as François Boucher.

The manufactory still operates today under the French Ministry of Culture, weaving tapestries after historic and contemporary designs for French state buildings, using traditional high-warp (haute lisse) techniques.
