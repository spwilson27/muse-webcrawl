The Late Show with Stephen Colbert is an American late-night news and political satire talk show hosted by Stephen Colbert, which premiered on CBS on 8 September 2015. Produced by Spartina Productions and CBS Television Studios, it is the second iteration of CBS's Late Show franchise. Colbert succeeded David Letterman, who had hosted the Late Show with David Letterman from 1993 to 2015.

Format

The show is taped at the Ed Sullivan Theater in New York City, the historic home of The Ed Sullivan Show and of Letterman's programme. Each episode typically opens with a monologue heavy on political satire—particularly coverage of American politics—followed by comedy segments, a musical performance in many episodes, and interviews with celebrity, political, and cultural guests. Jon Batiste served as bandleader with his band Stay Human from the show's debut until 2022.

Background

Colbert came to the show from Comedy Central's The Colbert Report (2005–2014), on which he had played a fictional conservative pundit persona also named Stephen Colbert. On The Late Show he hosts as himself. After an initial period behind competitors in the ratings, the programme found its footing during the 2016 presidential election and its aftermath, with its sharply political monologues drawing large audiences; it became the most-watched late-night programme in the United States from 2017 onwards, frequently leading total viewership.

Notable moments

The show has produced many widely circulated segments, including coverage of election nights, interviews with figures such as Barack Obama and Prince Harry, and musical performances and reunions. During the COVID-19 pandemic the show was produced from Colbert's home and later from a smaller studio space before returning to the Ed Sullivan Theater with an audience in June 2021. In July 2025, CBS announced the show would end in May 2026, citing financial considerations, a decision that drew wide public and industry reaction.

Accolades

The programme has been nominated for numerous Primetime Emmy Awards, including Outstanding Variety Talk Series, and has won awards for interactive programming and writing.
