Final Crisis is a 2008–2009 crossover storyline published by DC Comics, centred on a seven-issue limited series written by Grant Morrison and drawn primarily by J. G. Jones. It is the culmination of DC's "Crisis" saga that began with Crisis on Infinite Earths (1985) and continued through Infinite Crisis (2005).

Plot

The story depicts the day evil won: Darkseid, reborn after the events of Death of the New Gods, conquers Earth by disseminating the Anti-Life Equation, which strips humanity of free will. As the fabric of the Multiverse decays, the heroes of Earth — including Superman, who journeys beyond the Multiverse to confront the vampire Monitor Mandrakk — fight to restore reality. Key events include the death of the Martian Manhunter, the apparent death of Batman, and Barry Allen's return as the Flash.

Publication and tie-ins

The core series was accompanied by tie-in limited series including Final Crisis: Superman Beyond, Final Crisis: Legion of 3 Worlds, Final Crisis: Revelations, Final Crisis: Rogues' Revenge, and Batman issues depicting Batman's fate. Morrison's story drew heavily on Jack Kirby's Fourth World mythology.

Reception

The series received mixed-to-positive reviews: its ambition and art were praised, while its dense, compressed storytelling divided readers. It was nominated for an Eisner Award for best limited series.
