Parliament of Trinidad and Tobago

TypeBicameral
HousesSenate; House of Representatives
Seats31 senators; 41 representatives
LocationRed House, Port of Spain

The Parliament of Trinidad and Tobago is the bicameral legislature of the twin-island republic. It consists of the President of the Republic together with a Senate of 31 appointed members and a House of Representatives of 41 elected members, following the Westminster model.

Composition

Senators are appointed by the President: sixteen on the advice of the Prime Minister, six on the advice of the Leader of the Opposition, and nine independent senators at the President's discretion. Members of the House are elected by first-past-the-post in single-member constituencies, two of which are in Tobago.

History

The parliament met at the Red House in Port of Spain, which was stormed during the 1990 attempted coup. After two decades meeting at temporary quarters while the Red House was restored, parliament returned to the building in 2020.
