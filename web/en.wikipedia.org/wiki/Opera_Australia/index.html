Opera Australia

Formation1956 (as Australian Elizabethan Theatre Trust Opera Company)
TypeOpera company
LocationThe Opera Centre, Surry Hills, Sydney, Australia
Artistic DirectorJo Davies (from 2025)

Opera Australia is the principal opera company in Australia. Based in Sydney, its performance season at the Sydney Opera House accompanied by the Opera Australia Orchestra runs for approximately eight months of the year, with the remainder of its time spent in the Arts Centre Melbourne, where it is accompanied by Orchestra Victoria. In 2004, the company gave 226 performances in its subscription seasons in Sydney and Melbourne, attended by more than 294,000 people.

History

The company traces its origins to the Australian Elizabethan Theatre Trust Opera Company, founded in 1956, which staged its first season in 1957 with four operas by Mozart, performed in all capital cities. In 1970 it became the Australian Opera, and after the opening of the Sydney Opera House in 1973 the company established its home there. In 1996, the Australian Opera merged with the Victoria State Opera to form Opera Australia.

Repertoire and events

Opera Australia performs standard repertoire alongside Australian works, and since 2012 has staged Handa Opera on Sydney Harbour, an annual outdoor production on a floating stage. The company also tours nationally and presents musicals in partnership with commercial producers.
