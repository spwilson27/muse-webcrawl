Type 055 destroyer

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Type 055 destroyer (NATO designation: Renhai-class cruiser) is a class of stealth guided-missile destroyers built for China's People's Liberation Army Navy. It is among the largest surface combatants in the world, displacing around 12,000–13,000 tonnes.

Design

The class features an integrated mast, a low radar cross-section hull form, and a 112-cell vertical launching system able to fire surface-to-air missiles, anti-ship cruise missiles, land-attack missiles and anti-submarine weapons. Sensors include a dual-band phased-array radar suite for air defence and fleet command roles.

Service

The lead ship, Nanchang, was launched in 2017 and commissioned in January 2020. Eight ships of the first batch entered service, serving as escorts for China's aircraft carrier groups, and a further batch has been under construction.

Role

Western analysts generally classify the Type 055 as a cruiser because of its size, sensors and command facilities; China classifies it as a destroyer. It is regarded as one of the most capable multi-role surface combatants in service.
