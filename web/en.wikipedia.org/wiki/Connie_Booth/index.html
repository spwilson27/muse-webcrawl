Connie Booth

Booth co-wrote and starred in the sitcom Fawlty Towers.

Connie Booth (born 31 January 1944) is an American actress and writer. She is best known for co-writing and co-starring with her then-husband John Cleese in the British sitcom Fawlty Towers (1975–1979), in which she played the chambermaid and waitress Polly Sherman.

Booth was born in Indianapolis, Indiana, and moved to New York, where she studied acting and worked as a waitress. After meeting Cleese, she moved to London and appeared in Monty Python productions, including And Now for Something Completely Different (1971) and Monty Python and the Holy Grail (1975), in which she played a woman accused of being a witch. Her other acting credits include the film The Return of the Musketeers (1989) and appearances in British television drama.

Booth and Cleese married in 1968 and divorced in 1978; they have a daughter, Cynthia. Booth later trained as a psychotherapist and worked in that profession in London for many years, having retired from acting in the 1990s. In 2000 she married theatre critic John Lahr.
