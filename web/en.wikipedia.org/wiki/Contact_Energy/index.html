Contact Energy

Contact Energy Limited is a New Zealand electricity generator and retailer. It is one of the country's largest energy companies, operating geothermal, hydroelectric and thermal power stations and supplying electricity, natural gas and LPG to retail and wholesale customers. Contact is listed on the New Zealand Exchange (NZX: CEN).

History

Contact Energy was established in 1996 as a state-owned enterprise when the Electricity Corporation of New Zealand (ECNZ) was split, with Contact taking over a portfolio of generation assets. It was privatised in 1999. The Australian company Origin Energy acquired a majority stake in 2004 and held it until 2015, when Origin sold its shareholding, returning Contact to widely held ownership.

Generation assets

Contact operates a diversified portfolio of generation plant across New Zealand, including:

  • Geothermal stations in the Taupō Volcanic Zone, including Wairakei, Ohaaki, Te Mihi, Poihipi Road and Te Huka
  • The Clyde and Roxburgh hydroelectric stations on the Clutha River
  • Gas-fired generation including Stratford and the Ahuriri gas storage facility

The company has invested in new geothermal development, including the Tauhara power station near Taupō, one of the largest geothermal plants built in New Zealand in recent decades.

Retail

Contact retails electricity and gas to several hundred thousand residential and business customers and has been a significant competitor in the deregulated New Zealand electricity market alongside companies such as Meridian Energy, Genesis Energy and Mercury NZ.
