Kim Ji-soo (Korean: 김지수; born January 3, 1995), known mononymously as Jisoo, is a South Korean singer and actress. She is a member of the South Korean girl group Blackpink, formed by YG Entertainment, which debuted in August 2016 and became one of the most commercially successful girl groups of all time.

Career

Jisoo joined YG Entertainment as a trainee in 2011 and appeared in music videos and advertisements before debuting with Blackpink in 2016 with the single album Square One. With the group she has recorded record-breaking albums and tours and performed at festivals including Coachella. As an actress, she made her television debut with a cameo in The Producers (2015) and played her first leading role in the JTBC series Snowdrop (2021–22). She made her solo music debut in March 2023 with the single album Me, whose lead single "Flower" was a major commercial success; in 2024 she founded her own label, Blissoo, and in 2025 released the EP Amortage.

Public image

Jisoo is a global ambassador for fashion and beauty houses including Dior and Cartier, and is frequently cited among the most influential figures in the Korean entertainment industry's global reach.
