Sustainable Development Goal 7

Sustainable Development Goal 7 (SDG 7 or Global Goal 7) concerns "affordable, reliable, sustainable and modern energy for all" and is one of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals established by the United Nations in 2015. Its official wording is: "Ensure access to affordable, reliable, sustainable and modern energy for all."

Targets

SDG 7 has five targets: universal access to affordable and reliable modern energy services by 2030; a substantial increase in the share of renewable energy in the global mix; doubling the global rate of improvement in energy efficiency; enhanced international cooperation to facilitate access to clean energy research, technology, and investment; and expanded infrastructure and upgraded technology for supplying modern and sustainable energy services, particularly in developing countries.

Progress

The share of the world's population with access to electricity has risen steadily, exceeding 90 per cent, but hundreds of millions of people — mainly in sub-Saharan Africa — remain without power, and billions rely on polluting fuels for cooking. Renewables have grown rapidly, led by solar and wind, yet tracking reports by the IEA, IRENA, the World Bank, and WHO conclude that progress, while real, is still insufficient to meet the 2030 targets for access, efficiency, and clean cooking.
