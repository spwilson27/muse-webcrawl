Anthony's Shaft

Anthony's Shaft is a former lead-mining shaft in Nidderdale, North Yorkshire, England, near the village of Greenhow Hill. It is notable as the entrance associated with the Stump Cross Caverns area and the extensive lead workings of the Greenhow Hill orefield, one of the historic centres of lead mining in the Yorkshire Dales.

History

Lead was mined on Greenhow Hill for centuries, with intensive working during the 18th and 19th centuries. Shafts such as Anthony's Shaft were sunk to reach the lead veins (rakes) that cut through the Carboniferous limestone and gritstone of the area. Ore was hauled to the surface, dressed on the hillside, and smelted at local mills.

Setting

The shaft stands in a landscape of spoil heaps, hushes and ruined dressing floors characteristic of the Greenhow mining field, close to Coldstones Quarry and the show cave of Stump Cross Caverns. The site forms part of the wider industrial heritage of Nidderdale, an Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty.
