Bhojpuri cinema

IndustryBhojpuri cinema (Bhojiwood)
LanguageBhojpuri
RegionBihar, eastern Uttar Pradesh, Nepal Terai

Bhojpuri cinema is the Indian film industry producing films in the Bhojpuri language, spoken across Bihar, eastern Uttar Pradesh and the Nepalese Terai. It serves one of the largest regional-language audiences in India and the Bhojpuri diaspora.

History

The first Bhojpuri film, Ganga Maiyya Tohe Piyari Chadhaibo, was released in 1963. The industry grew slowly until a revival in the 2000s, when hits such as Sasura Bada Paisawala (2003) demonstrated the commercial potential of Bhojpuri-language features for rural and small-town audiences.

Themes and audience

Bhojpuri films typically feature rural settings, family melodrama, folk music and devotional themes. Music videos and film songs form a major part of the wider Bhojpuri entertainment economy, with stars such as Manoj Tiwari, Ravi Kishan, Dinesh Lal Yadav and Pawan Singh drawing large followings.

Production centres

Although rooted in the Bhojpuri-speaking region, much production is based in Mumbai, with shoots also taking place in Bihar and Uttar Pradesh.
