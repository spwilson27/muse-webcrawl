L3Harris Technologies, Inc. is an American technology and defense contractor formed in June 2019 by the merger of L3 Technologies and Harris Corporation — at the time described as the largest all-stock merger in the defense sector. It is among the largest defense contractors in the United States.

Business

The company supplies communications systems, avionics, electronic warfare equipment, night-vision devices, space payloads, and intelligence and surveillance systems to military and civil customers. In 2023 it acquired Aerojet Rocketdyne, expanding its position in missile propulsion and rocket motors.
