Psylocke is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, commonly in association with the X-Men. The character was created by writer Chris Claremont and artist Herb Trimpe, and first appeared as Elizabeth "Betsy" Braddock in Captain Britain #8 (1976), as the twin sister of the superhero Captain Britain (Brian Braddock).

Betsy Braddock was originally a supporting character and charter pilot who developed precognitive and telepathic mutant powers. She later joined the X-Men under the codename Psylocke. In a famous and controversial 1989 storyline, her mind was placed in the body of the Japanese assassin Kwannon, giving the character a new appearance and martial-arts skills; she became known for manifesting a "psychic knife", described as the focused totality of her telepathic powers.

Publication history

After appearing in British Marvel UK titles, Braddock was brought into the American X-Men books in New Mutants Annual #2 (1986). She became a member of the X-Men during Chris Claremont's run on The Uncanny X-Men, and the body-swap storyline in Acts of Vengeance (1989) transformed her into one of the team's most recognisable members of the 1990s.

For nearly three decades Betsy remained in Kwannon's body while Kwannon briefly took the name Revanche. In 2018–2019 storylines the two women were restored to their original bodies: Betsy Braddock subsequently became the new Captain Britain and led Excalibur, while Kwannon assumed the Psylocke identity herself, retaining the codename and the psychic knife.

Powers and abilities

Psylocke is a powerful telepath and telekinetic. She can read and project thoughts, create telepathic illusions, and form weapons of psychic energy, most famously her psychic knife, which can disrupt a victim's nervous system. In Kwannon's body she also possesses the trained skills of a ninja assassin, including mastery of katana combat and stealth.

In other media

Psylocke has appeared in animated X-Men series, video games such as the Marvel vs. Capcom series, and the films X-Men: The Last Stand (2006) and X-Men: Apocalypse (2016), in which she was played by Olivia Munn.
