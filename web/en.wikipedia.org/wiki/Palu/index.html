Palu

Palu is a chartered city on the west coast of Sulawesi island, Indonesia, and the capital of Central Sulawesi province. It lies at the mouth of the Palu River, at the head of a long, narrow bay. The city's population was about 373,000 at the 2020 census.

Geography

Palu sits in one of Indonesia's driest areas, sheltered by surrounding mountains in a rain shadow, and straddles the Palu-Koro Fault, an active strike-slip fault running through the city and along the valley to the southeast. The surrounding regencies are known for ancient megalithic statues in the Bada and Besoa valleys.

2018 earthquake and tsunami

On 28 September 2018 a magnitude 7.5 earthquake struck near Palu, triggering a tsunami in Palu Bay and catastrophic soil liquefaction in neighbourhoods such as Balaroa and Petobo, where entire districts sank into flowing mud. More than 4,000 people died in the disaster, making it the deadliest earthquake of 2018. Reconstruction, including new housing and a rebuilt bridge across the bay, continued for years afterwards.

Economy

Palu is a regional trading port for copra, rattan, and agricultural produce from Central Sulawesi's interior, and hosts the Mutiara SIS Al-Jufrie Airport.
