Asian handicap

An Asian handicap betting coupon

The Asian handicap is a form of sports betting, originating in Indonesia and popularised across Asia, in which a virtual handicap expressed in goals or points is applied to level the contest between two mismatched teams. Its defining feature is that it eliminates the possibility of a drawn outcome on the bet, reducing the wager to two possible results; stakes are returned ("pushed") when the handicap-adjusted result is level.

How it works

The stronger team "gives" a handicap and the weaker team "receives" it. Handicaps are set in whole goals, half goals, or quarter goals. Half-goal handicaps (e.g. −0.5/+0.5) remove any possibility of a push. Quarter-goal handicaps (e.g. −0.25, −0.75) split the stake between the two adjacent half-goal lines, so a bet can be half won and half pushed, or half lost and half pushed.

−0.5Favourite wins by any margin
−1.0Favourite wins by 2+ goals (win by exactly 1 = stake returned)
−1.5Favourite wins by 2+ goals
−0.25Favourite wins; a draw loses half the stake
−0.75Favourite wins by 2+ goals; win by exactly 1 wins half the stake

Advantages and spread

Because a draw cannot lose the whole bet, Asian handicaps usually offer lower bookmaker margins and prices closer to even money than traditional 1X2 markets. The format spread from Asian bookmakers into Europe in the late 1990s and 2000s, helped by internet betting, and is now standard on major football betting markets, with equivalents applied to other sports such as basketball and ice hockey.
