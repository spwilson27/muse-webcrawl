Spring (season)

Spring, also known as springtime, is one of the four temperate seasons, succeeding winter and preceding summer. There are various technical definitions of spring, but local usage varies according to climate, cultures, and customs. In the Northern Hemisphere it is generally associated with the months of March, April, and May; in the Southern Hemisphere, with September, October, and November.

Astronomical and meteorological spring

Astronomically, spring begins at the March equinox, when day and night are roughly equal in length, and ends at the June solstice. Meteorologists define spring as the three-month period of March through May in the Northern Hemisphere for statistical convenience.

Natural phenomena

During spring, Earth's axis tilts increasingly toward the Sun, days lengthen, and temperatures rise. Plants bud and flower, many animals breed or return from migration, and snowmelt swells rivers. The season is culturally associated with renewal and rebirth, celebrated in festivals such as Easter, Passover, and Nowruz.
