The Avengers are a team of superheroes appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, created by writer-editor Stan Lee and artist/co-plotter Jack Kirby. The team made its debut in The Avengers #1 (cover-dated September 1963). Labeled "Earth's Mightiest Heroes", the team was initially formed by Iron Man, Thor, the Hulk, Ant-Man and the Wasp, brought together when Loki manipulated the Hulk; Captain America joined in issue #4 after being revived from suspended animation in ice.

History

The Avengers have featured a rotating roster throughout their history, with virtually every major Marvel hero serving at some point. Major story arcs include the "Kree–Skrull War", "Avengers Disassembled", "Civil War", "Secret Invasion" and the events leading to crossover titles such as New Avengers and Mighty Avengers. Their battle cry is "Avengers Assemble!"

In other media

The team anchors the Marvel Cinematic Universe, beginning with Marvel's The Avengers (2012), followed by Age of Ultron (2015), Infinity War (2018) and Endgame (2019), among the highest-grossing films of all time.
