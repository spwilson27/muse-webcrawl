Daredevil (Marvel Comics series)

Daredevil is an American comic book series published by Marvel Comics featuring the superhero Daredevil (Matt Murdock), a blind lawyer from New York's Hell's Kitchen whose other senses are heightened to superhuman levels. The character was created by writer-editor Stan Lee and artist Bill Everett, with input from Jack Kirby.

Publication history

The series debuted with Daredevil #1 in April 1964. Daredevil originally wore a yellow-and-red costume before adopting his iconic all-red suit, designed by Wally Wood. Early runs by Lee, Gene Colan and others established Murdock's supporting cast, including Foggy Nelson and Karen Page, and villains such as the Owl and Stilt-Man.

Frank Miller era and beyond

The series was transformed in the early 1980s when writer-artist Frank Miller took over, introducing the assassin Elektra and the crime lord Kingpin as Daredevil's nemesis, and telling darker, noir-influenced stories such as "Born Again" (with David Mazzucchelli). Subsequent acclaimed runs include those by Ann Nocenti, Kevin Smith and Joe Quesada ("Guardian Devil"), Brian Michael Bendis and Alex Maleev, Ed Brubaker, Mark Waid, Charles Soule and Chip Zdarsky. Daredevil storylines have been adapted into film and a popular television series.
