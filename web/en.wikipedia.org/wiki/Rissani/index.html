Rissani

Rissani (Arabic: الريصاني) is a town in eastern Morocco, in the Errachidia Province of the Drâa-Tafilalet region, near the Algerian border and the sand dunes of Erg Chebbi. It lies close to the site of the ancient city of Sijilmassa.

History

Rissani stands near the ruins of Sijilmassa, a medieval trading city and northern terminus of trans-Saharan caravan routes. The Alaouite dynasty, Morocco's ruling house, traces its origins to the Tafilalet oasis around Rissani, and the town hosts the mausoleum of Moulay Ali Cherif, founder of the dynasty.

Today

The town is known for its traditional souk, held several days a week, its fortified villages (ksars), and its role as a gateway for visitors travelling to Merzouga and the dunes of the Sahara.
