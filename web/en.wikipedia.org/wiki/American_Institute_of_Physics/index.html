American Institute of Physics

The American Institute of Physics (AIP) is a nonprofit federation of scientific societies in the physical sciences. Founded in 1931 to advance the physical sciences during the funding crisis of the Great Depression, AIP serves its member societies — including the American Physical Society, the Optical Society (Optica), and the American Astronomical Society — and the wider physics community through publishing, history, and policy programs.

Activities

AIP Publishing, a wholly owned subsidiary, publishes scientific journals including The Journal of Chemical Physics, Applied Physics Letters, and the Journal of Applied Physics, as well as Physics Today, AIP's flagship magazine, founded in 1948. Through Scitation, AIP hosts the scholarly literature of its member societies.

History and archives

The Niels Bohr Library & Archives at AIP's headquarters in College Park, Maryland, is one of the world's foremost collections on the history of modern physics, preserving oral histories, photographs, and manuscripts. AIP also produces statistical research on physics education and employment, science news services such as Inside Science, and the annual Science Writing Awards, and it administers prizes including the Andrew Gemant Award.
