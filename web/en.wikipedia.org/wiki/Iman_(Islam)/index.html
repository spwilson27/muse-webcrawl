Iman (Islam)

Iman (Arabic: إيمان, romanized: ʾīmān) in Islamic theology denotes faith or belief, most often belief in the six articles of faith. A believer is called a mu'min.

The six articles

According to the Hadith of Gabriel, iman comprises belief in: (1) God (Allah), (2) His angels, (3) His revealed books, (4) His messengers, (5) the Day of Judgement, and (6) divine decree (qadar), both its good and its hardship.

Relation to Islam and ihsan

In the same hadith, iman is distinguished from islam (outward submission, expressed in the five pillars) and ihsan (excellence in worship). Theologians debated whether faith consists of confession, assent of the heart and deeds, and whether it can increase and decrease.

References: Qur'an; Sahih Muslim, Hadith of Gabriel; classical theological works.
