List of U.S. state birds

This is a list of official U.S. state birds. Most states adopted their birds in the early to mid twentieth century, often following campaigns by Audubon societies and schoolchildren. The northern cardinal is the most common choice, serving as state bird of seven states, while the western meadowlark is the state bird of six.
