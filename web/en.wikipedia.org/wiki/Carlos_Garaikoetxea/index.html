Carlos Garaikoetxea Urriza (born 2 June 1938) is a Spanish Basque politician who served as the first Lehendakari (president) of the Basque Government after the restoration of democracy, holding office from 1980 to 1985. A member of the Basque Nationalist Party (PNV/EAJ) for most of his career, he later broke with it and founded the Eusko Alkartasuna party.

Career

Born in Pamplona, Navarre, Garaikoetxea trained as a lawyer and economist and became involved in Basque politics during Spain's transition to democracy. After the Statute of Autonomy of the Basque Country was approved in 1979, he led the PNV to victory in the first Basque regional elections of 1980 and became Lehendakari, overseeing the establishment of the autonomous institutions, the Basque police (Ertzaintza) and education system.

Internal disputes with the PNV leadership under Xabier Arzalluz led to Garaikoetxea's resignation in 1985; in 1986 he founded Eusko Alkartasuna (Basque Solidarity), a social-democratic Basque nationalist party, and later served as a Member of the European Parliament.
