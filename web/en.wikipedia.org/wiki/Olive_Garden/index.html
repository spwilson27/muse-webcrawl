Olive Garden is an American casual dining restaurant chain specializing in Italian-American cuisine, including pasta dishes, steaks, and salads. It is the largest chain of Italian-themed full-service restaurants in the United States and the flagship brand of Darden Restaurants.

History

The first Olive Garden opened in Orlando, Florida, in 1982, developed by General Mills' restaurant division. Darden Restaurants was spun off from General Mills in 1995. The chain is known for its unlimited breadsticks, salad, and soup promotions and the slogan "When you're here, you're family."

Menu

Signature items include fettuccine Alfredo, chicken parmigiana, lasagna, and the "Tour of Italy" combination plate. The company operates a cooking school facility in Tuscany, Italy, used for training chefs and developing recipes.
