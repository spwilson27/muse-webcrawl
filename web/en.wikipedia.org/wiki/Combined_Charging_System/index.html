Combined Charging System (CCS)

Charging modesAC (Type 1 or Type 2) and DC fast charging
DC powerUp to 350 kW (specification up to 500 kW)

The Combined Charging System (CCS) is a standard for charging electric vehicles. It uses Combo 1 and Combo 2 connectors to provide power at up to 500 kilowatts (kW), and is designed to support both alternating current (AC) charging and direct current (DC) fast charging through a single vehicle inlet. The upper part of the connector is a standard Type 1 (in North America) or Type 2 (in Europe) AC connector, extended with two large DC pins below.

History

CCS was developed by the Charging Interface Initiative (CharIN), founded by German and international automakers, and was first demonstrated in 2012. It became the mandated DC fast-charging standard in the European Union under the Alternative Fuels Infrastructure Directive, and was long the dominant standard on non-Tesla vehicles in North America, in competition with CHAdeMO and Tesla's proprietary connector (later opened as the North American Charging Standard, NACS).

Communication

For DC charging, CCS uses power-line communication (HomePlug Green PHY) between vehicle and charger, governed by standards including DIN SPEC 70121 and ISO 15118. ISO 15118 also enables "Plug & Charge", which authenticates and bills the driver automatically when the cable is connected.
