Overview

Kampong Ayer is a cluster of traditional stilt villages on the Brunei River in Bandar Seri Begawan, the capital of Brunei. Often called the "Venice of the East", it is considered the world's largest water village, historically home to a large share of the capital's population, connected by boardwalks and water taxis.

Today

The settlement predates the sultanate's land palaces — Brunei's court itself was water-based for centuries, described by Magellan's chronicler Pigafetta in 1521. Modern government housing programmes have drawn many residents ashore, but villages such as Tamoi maintain schools, mosques and fire services on stilts.
