Off-network syndication

Off-network syndication (also called second-run syndication) is the licensing of a television programme that was originally run on a broadcast network or, in some cases, a cable network that is typically already in production and completed to independent stations and channels for reruns.

Historically, a series needed around 100 episodes—typically four or five seasons—to be viable for daily stripping in syndication. Successful off-network series such as sitcom reruns have generated billions in revenue for studios. The market has changed with the rise of cable channels and streaming services acquiring rerun rights.
