Sainsbury's

J Sainsbury plc
Founded: 1869, London
Headquarters: London, England
Industry: Retail
Products: Supermarkets, convenience stores
Subsidiaries: Argos, Nectar, Habitat

J Sainsbury plc, trading as Sainsbury's, is a British supermarket chain and the second-largest retailer of groceries in the United Kingdom, with a market share of around 15%. Founded in 1869 by John James Sainsbury with a shop in Drury Lane, London, the company operates more than 1,400 supermarkets and convenience stores across the UK.

A Sainsbury's supermarket, one of the UK's leading grocery retailers.

History

Sainsbury's began as a retailer of fresh foods and later expanded into packaged groceries. It pioneered self-service shopping in Britain, opening its first self-service store in Croydon in 1950. The company floated on the London Stock Exchange in 1973 in what was then the largest flotation ever held there. For much of the twentieth century Sainsbury's was the UK's market leader before being overtaken by Tesco in 1995.

Operations

Alongside its supermarkets, the group owns the catalogue retailer Argos (acquired in 2016), the Nectar loyalty programme, Sainsbury's Bank, and the Tu clothing brand. Own-label ranges include "by Sainsbury's" and the premium "Taste the Difference" line. The company's slogan "Live Well for Less" was used from 2011.
