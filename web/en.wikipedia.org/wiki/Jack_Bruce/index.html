Jack Bruce

John Symon Asher Bruce (14 May 1943 – 25 October 2014) was a Scottish musician, singer, songwriter and composer. He gained popularity as the primary lead vocalist and bassist of the rock supergroup Cream.

Career

Born in Bishopbriggs, Scotland, Bruce studied cello and composition before playing in blues and jazz groups including the Graham Bond Organisation. With Eric Clapton and Ginger Baker he formed Cream in 1966, co-writing songs such as "Sunshine of Your Love", "White Room" and "I Feel Free" with lyricist Pete Brown.

Solo work

His solo albums include Songs for a Tailor (1969) and Harmony Row (1971). He collaborated widely across rock, jazz and blues until his death in 2014.
