Temple Entry Proclamation

The Temple Entry Proclamation was issued on 12 November 1936 by Maharaja Chithira Thirunal Balarama Varma of the princely state of Travancore (in present-day Kerala, India). It abolished the ban on so-called "untouchable" castes entering Hindu temples in the state, making Travancore the first region in India to throw open state-controlled temples to all Hindus.

Background

The proclamation followed decades of reform movements and agitations such as the Vaikom Satyagraha (1924–25). The Maharaja, advised by Diwan C. P. Ramaswami Iyer, acted despite conservative opposition. Mahatma Gandhi hailed the proclamation as a "miracle of the modern age".

Legacy

The measure is regarded as a landmark in Indian social reform and helped set the stage for temple-entry provisions in independent India.
