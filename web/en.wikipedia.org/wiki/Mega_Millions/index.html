Mega Millions

Mega Millions (originally known as The Big Game in 1996 and renamed The Big Game Mega Millions six years later) is an American multi-jurisdictional lottery game offered in 45 states, the District of Columbia and the U.S. Virgin Islands. Drawings are held on Tuesday and Friday evenings in Atlanta, Georgia.

Format

Players pick five numbers from 1 to 70 and one Mega Ball number from 1 to 24; matching all six wins the jackpot, which starts at a set minimum and rolls over until won. Changes over the years — including the 2017 matrix change and a 2025 redesign raising the ticket price to $5 with a built-in multiplier — were designed to produce larger jackpots more quickly.

Record jackpots

Mega Millions has produced several of the largest lottery prizes in history, including a $1.537 billion jackpot won in South Carolina in October 2018 and a $1.602 billion jackpot won in Florida in August 2023. Together with Powerball it dominates the American jackpot-lottery market.
