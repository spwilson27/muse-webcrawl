WNBA Finals

WNBA Finals
League: Women's National Basketball Association
First held: 1997
Format: Best-of series between conference champions

The WNBA Finals are the championship series of the Women's National Basketball Association (WNBA), played between the champions of the league's playoffs to determine the season champion.

History

The first championship in 1997 was a single game, won by the Houston Comets, who went on to win the first four titles (1997–2000) behind Cynthia Cooper, Sheryl Swoopes and Tina Thompson. The series expanded over time: a best-of-three format gave way to best-of-five from 2005, and a best-of-seven format was adopted beginning with the 2025 season.

Notable champions

Four championships each have been won by the Houston Comets, Minnesota Lynx and Seattle Storm, while teams such as the Las Vegas Aces, Los Angeles Sparks, Phoenix Mercury, Chicago Sky and New York Liberty have each won titles. The Finals MVP award is presented to the series' outstanding player. See also WNBA.
