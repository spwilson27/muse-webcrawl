Stephan Bonnar

Stephan Patrick Bonnar (April 4, 1977 – December 22, 2022), nicknamed "The American Psycho", was an American mixed martial artist who competed in the light heavyweight division of the Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC) and Bellator MMA.

The Ultimate Fighter

Bonnar rose to fame on the first season of The Ultimate Fighter in 2005. In the live finale he lost a unanimous decision to Forrest Griffin in a back-and-forth classic that is widely credited with saving the UFC and launching the modern MMA boom; both men were awarded contracts, and the bout was later inducted into the UFC Hall of Fame's fight wing.

Later career

Bonnar fought many of the era's top light heavyweights, including Rashad Evans, Lyoto Machida, Jon Jones and Anderson Silva, against whom he headlined UFC 153 in 2012. He retired with a professional record of 15 wins and 9 losses, later working as an analyst and in professional wrestling. He died in December 2022, aged 45.
