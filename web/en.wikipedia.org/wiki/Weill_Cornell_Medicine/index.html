Weill Cornell Medicine is the biomedical research unit and medical school of Cornell University, located on the Upper East Side of Manhattan in New York City. It comprises the Weill Cornell Medical College and the Weill Cornell Graduate School of Medical Sciences. Its primary teaching hospital affiliate is NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital.

History

The school was founded in 1898 as Cornell University Medical College. In 1927 it became affiliated with New York Hospital, and the joint medical centre on York Avenue opened in 1932. It was renamed in 1998 for benefactor Sanford I. Weill, former chairman of Citigroup, whose gifts to the institution were among the largest in American medical education.

Education and research

Weill Cornell confers the M.D. degree as well as M.D.-Ph.D. degrees through the Tri-Institutional MD-PhD Program run jointly with Rockefeller University and the Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center. It also operates a branch campus in Doha, Qatar - Weill Cornell Medicine - Qatar - established in 2001, the first American medical school campus outside the United States.
