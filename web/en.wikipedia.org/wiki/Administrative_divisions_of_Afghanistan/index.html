Administrative divisions of Afghanistan

The administrative divisions of Afghanistan consist of 34 provinces (velayat), which are subdivided into hundreds of districts, and beneath them subdistricts and villages.

Structure

Each province is headed by a governor. Provinces include Kabul, Herat, Kandahar, Balkh, Nangarhar, Badakhshan and Helmand among others. Districts number roughly 400 (their count changes as districts are split), each led by a district governor. The capital city Kabul forms its own province and municipality.

Regions

For statistical and planning purposes provinces are often grouped into regions — Central, North, Northeast, West, South, Southeast and East — though these have no administrative status.
