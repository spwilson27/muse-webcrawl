American Statistical Association

The American Statistical Association (ASA) is the main professional organization for statisticians and related professionals in the United States. Founded in Boston, Massachusetts on 27 November 1839, it is the second-oldest continuously operating professional association in the country. The ASA states its mission as promoting the proper application of statistics, supporting statistical education, and advancing the profession.

Activities

The ASA publishes several journals, including the Journal of the American Statistical Association (JASA), one of the field's leading journals, The American Statistician, and the Journal of Computational and Graphical Statistics (with partner societies). It organizes the Joint Statistical Meetings (JSM), one of the largest statistical conferences in the world, together with other societies such as the Institute of Mathematical Statistics and international associations.

The association issues influential guidance on statistical practice, including its 2016 statement on p-values and its ethical guidelines for statistical practitioners. It confers fellowship on distinguished members and runs accreditation for professional statisticians (PStat). The ASA also promotes statistics education through curricula guidelines (the GAISE reports) and public outreach such as "This is Statistics".
