La Arrolladora Banda El Limón

La Arrolladora Banda El Limón de René Camacho is a Mexican banda group from El Limón de los Peraza, near Mazatlán, Sinaloa. Founded by clarinetist René Camacho, the group descends from the Banda El Limón founded in the 1960s; after a split in the mid-1990s, Camacho's faction adopted the name "La Arrolladora" ("The Overwhelming One").

La Arrolladora Banda El Limón performing

The banda is one of the most successful acts in regional Mexican music, known for a large brass-and-clarinet ensemble line-up of around 20 musicians and vocalists. Its popular recordings include songs such as "La Llamada de Mi Ex", "En los Puritos Huesos", "De Ti Exclusivo", "Ya Es Muy Tarde", "El Ruido de Tus Zapatos", "Entre Beso y Beso", and "El Final de Nuestra Historia". The group has won multiple Latin Grammy Awards, including Best Banda Album, and Billboard Latin Music Awards, and has toured extensively in Mexico and the United States.

History

René Camacho joined Banda El Limón as a young clarinetist and eventually became its musical director. When the original band divided in 1997, Camacho formed La Arrolladora Banda El Limón, which rose to prominence in the 2000s with a succession of chart-topping albums on the Fonovisa/Disa labels. Vocalists who have fronted the group include Jorge Medina, whose departure in 2017 after two decades led to a new line-up of singers. The banda's concerts fill arenas and fairgrounds, and its singles are staples of regional Mexican radio in both Mexico and the US.
