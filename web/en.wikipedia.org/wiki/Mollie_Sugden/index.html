Mollie Sugden (born Mary Isobel Sugden; 21 July 1922 – 1 July 2009) was an English actress best known for playing the saleswoman Mrs Betty Slocombe in the BBC sitcom Are You Being Served? (1972–1985) and its sequel Grace & Favour (1992–1993). With her brightly coloured hair and catchphrases concerning her 'pussy' (her cat Tiddles), Mrs Slocombe became one of British television's most recognisable comedy characters.

Early life and career

Sugden was born in Keighley in the West Riding of Yorkshire and trained at the Guildhall School of Music and Drama. She spent years in repertory theatre and appeared in television series including Hugh and I, Z-Cars, and The Liver Birds, in which she played Mrs Hutchinson from 1971 to 1979.

She was cast as Mrs Slocombe when Are You Being Served? began in 1972, remaining for the whole of its thirteen-year run and in the 1977 film version.

Later work

Sugden starred with her Are You Being Served? colleagues in the sequel Grace & Favour, and appeared in series such as That's My Boy and My Husband and I. She continued to work in theatre and made guest appearances into the 2000s.

She was married to actor William Moore, whom she met in repertory theatre, from 1958 until his death in 2000; they had twin sons. Sugden died in Guildford in 2009 after a long illness.
