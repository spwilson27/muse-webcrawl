Greggs plc is a British bakery chain specialising in savoury products such as sausage rolls, sandwiches, and sweet items including doughnuts and vanilla slices. Founded by John Gregg as a delivery business in Tyneside in 1939, it opened its first shop in Gosforth, Newcastle upon Tyne, in 1951. It is the largest bakery chain in the United Kingdom, with more than 2,400 outlets.

History

Greggs expanded by acquiring regional bakery chains across England, Scotland, and Wales during the late 20th century. In the 2010s it repositioned itself as a "food-on-the-go" retailer rather than a traditional bakery, extending opening hours and adding evening delivery.

Products and popularity

The sausage roll is its best-known product; in January 2019 Greggs launched a vegan sausage roll made with Quorn, which attracted national media attention and sold out in many stores. The company sells over 100 million sausage rolls a year.

Operations

Greggs operates its own bakeries and supply chain, and a value-led pricing strategy targeting high-street and travel locations. It is listed on the London Stock Exchange.
