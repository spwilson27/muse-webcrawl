Adelaide-class frigate

The Adelaide class is a class of guided-missile frigates constructed in the United States and Australia for service with the Royal Australian Navy. The class is based on the United States Navy's Oliver Hazard Perry-class frigates, but modified to Australian requirements. The first four vessels were built in the United States, while the final two were constructed in Australia. All six entered service from 1980 onwards and were progressively withdrawn, the last (HMAS Melbourne) decommissioning in 2019.

Ships

The class comprised HMAS Adelaide, Canberra, Sydney, Darwin, Melbourne and Newcastle. The ships served in the Persian Gulf during the 1990–91 Gulf War and in subsequent Middle East deployments, border protection operations, and the 1999 INTERFET deployment to East Timor. From 2004 the four surviving ships underwent the FFG Upgrade (SEA 1390), receiving the Evolved Sea Sparrow Missile in an eight-cell vertical launch system, improved radars and combat system upgrades — a project noted for significant cost growth and delay. Adelaide and Canberra were sunk as dive wrecks after decommissioning; Melbourne and Newcastle were sold to Chile in 2020.
