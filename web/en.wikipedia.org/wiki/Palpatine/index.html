Palpatine

Sheev Palpatine is a fictional character in the Star Wars franchise. He is the main antagonist of the Skywalker saga, created by George Lucas and portrayed by Ian McDiarmid in the feature films. Palpatine is a Dark Lord of the Sith who, under his Sith name Darth Sidious, secretly engineers the fall of the Galactic Republic and the Jedi Order while publicly serving as a senator from Naboo, then Supreme Chancellor, and finally Emperor of the Galactic Empire.

Character overview

Publicly, Palpatine presents himself as a humble, avuncular servant of the Republic. In secret, as Darth Sidious, he is a master manipulator trained by Darth Plagueis, who orchestrates the Invasion of Naboo, the Clone Wars, and Order 66 — the purge of the Jedi — in a decades-long grand plan to seize absolute power. As Emperor, he rules the galaxy through fear, with Darth Vader as his enforcer.

Appearances

Palpatine first appeared as a hologram in The Empire Strikes Back (1980) and in person in Return of the Jedi (1983), where he is thrown to his death by Vader aboard the second Death Star. The prequel trilogy (1999–2005) chronicles his rise from senator to Emperor. McDiarmid returned for The Rise of Skywalker (2019), in which Palpatine is revealed to have survived through cloning and Sith rituals on the hidden world Exegol, secretly guiding the First Order through his puppet Supreme Leader Snoke, before his final destruction by his granddaughter Rey.

Reception and legacy

Palpatine is widely regarded as one of the greatest film villains of all time, noted for McDiarmid's theatrical performance and the character's embodiment of political corruption and the seduction of power. His line deliveries and the "tragedy of Darth Plagueis the Wise" scene have become prominent in popular culture.
