Minor third

In music theory, a minor third is a musical interval encompassing three half steps, or semitones. Staff position-wise, it is an interval spanning three letter names, such as C to E-flat. It is qualified as minor because it is the smaller of the two thirds: a minor third spans three semitones, while a major third spans four.

Tuning

In equal temperament a minor third is equal to 300 cents, slightly narrower (by about 16 cents) than the just minor third of frequency ratio 6:5, which is about 316 cents. In just intonation the 6:5 minor third is a consonant interval frequently used as the basis of the minor triad.

In harmony

The minor third is the defining interval of the minor chord: a minor triad consists of a root, a minor third above it, and a perfect fifth. Minor thirds, along with major thirds, are considered among the most pleasant and expressive intervals in Western music, often associated with melancholy or tender character.
