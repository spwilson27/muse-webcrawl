IEEE 1028

IEEE Std 1028, the IEEE Standard for Software Reviews and Audits, is a standard published by the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers that defines systematic examination processes for software products. The standard specifies common procedures and content for five types of review and audit: management reviews, technical reviews, inspections, walk-throughs and audits.

Scope

For each review type, IEEE 1028 defines entry and exit criteria, required procedures, and the roles and responsibilities of participants such as the review leader, recorder, reader and author. Inspections are the most formal type, following the tradition of Fagan inspections, with the goal of detecting anomalies in software work products; audits, by contrast, provide an independent evaluation of compliance with specifications, standards and contractual agreements.

History

The standard was first published in 1988 and revised in 1997 and 2008. Elements of IEEE 1028 have been incorporated into the joint ISO/IEC/IEEE 29119 and 20246 families of standards on software testing and reviews.
