Superman II is a 1980 superhero film directed by Richard Lester, with substantial material shot by original director Richard Donner before he was replaced. It is the sequel to Superman (1978) and stars Christopher Reeve as Superman.

Plot

Three Kryptonian criminals led by General Zod, imprisoned in the Phantom Zone, are freed and come to Earth with powers equal to Superman's. Meanwhile, Superman gives up his powers to live as a mortal with Lois Lane, and must find a way to restore them to confront Zod and the villain Lex Luthor, who seeks to profit from the invasion.

Production and the Donner Cut

Donner had filmed much of the sequel concurrently with the first film but was fired after disagreements with the producers; Lester reshot many scenes. In 2006, Superman II: The Richard Donner Cut was released, restoring much of Donner's footage and his original ending.
