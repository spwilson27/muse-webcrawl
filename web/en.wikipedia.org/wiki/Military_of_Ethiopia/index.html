Military of Ethiopia

Troops of the Ethiopian National Defense Force (ENDF).

The military of Ethiopia, formally the Ethiopian National Defense Force (ENDF), is the armed force of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia. It consists of an army and an air force; Ethiopia is landlocked and maintains no navy since Eritrea's independence in 1993 (a naval force has been planned for re-establishment since 2018). The ENDF is among the largest and most battle-experienced militaries in Africa.

History

Ethiopia's imperial army famously defeated Italy at the Battle of Adwa in 1896, preserving Ethiopian independence during the Scramble for Africa. Emperor Haile Selassie modernized the forces with foreign assistance; Ethiopian troops fought in the Korean War as part of the Kagnew Battalion. Italy occupied Ethiopia from 1936 to 1941 before liberation by Ethiopian patriots and Allied forces.

After the 1974 revolution, the Derg regime built one of sub-Saharan Africa's largest armies with Soviet and Cuban support, fighting the Ogaden War (1977–1978) against Somalia and a long war against Eritrean and Tigrayan insurgents. The Derg's army collapsed in 1991 and was replaced by the forces of the EPRDF.

Recent conflicts

Ethiopia fought a border war with Eritrea from 1998 to 2000, intervened in Somalia from 2006, and contributed heavily to African Union peacekeeping. From November 2020 to November 2022 the ENDF fought the Tigray War against the Tigray People's Liberation Front, one of the deadliest conflicts of the 21st century, ended by the Pretoria agreement. The force has since been engaged against Fano militias in Amhara and the Oromo Liberation Army.
