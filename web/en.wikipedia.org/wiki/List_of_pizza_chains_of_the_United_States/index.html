This is a list of pizza chains of the United States. Pizza is one of the most popular foods in the United States, and the market is dominated by large national chains — Domino's, Pizza Hut, Little Caesars and Papa John's — alongside regional chains and fast-casual concepts. Domino's overtook Pizza Hut as the largest pizza seller in the world by retail sales in 2017.
