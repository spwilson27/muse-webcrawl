Cholet

Cholet is a commune in the Maine-et-Loire department in western France, with a population of roughly 55,000. It lies near the border of the historical Vendée region.

History

Cholet was a centre of the textile industry, famous for its handkerchiefs, and was heavily affected by the Wars of the Vendée during the French Revolution, when the Battle of Cholet was fought nearby in 1793.

Economy and sport

Modern industries include rubber manufacturing and food processing. The town is home to Cholet Basket, a leading French basketball club, and the Cholet-Pays de Loire cycling race.
