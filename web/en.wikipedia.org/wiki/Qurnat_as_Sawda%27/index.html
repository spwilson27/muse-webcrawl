Qurnat as Sawda' (Arabic: القرنة السوداء) is the highest point in Lebanon and the Levant, rising to 3,088 metres (10,131 ft) above sea level. It is located in the Bsharri District of the North Governorate, within the Mount Lebanon range, near the ski resort of The Cedars.

Geography

The summit forms part of a broad, rounded massif whose name means "the black peak" in Arabic. Its slopes are covered with snow for much of the year, and the surrounding high plateau is one of the coldest and most sparsely vegetated landscapes in the country. A small weather station and military installations have been sited near the top.

Climate and access

The peak is accessible by rough tracks from the Cedars area, and in summer hikers cross its plateau on routes linking Bsharri with the Bekaa Valley. Winters bring heavy snowfall and severe winds, making the summit substantially colder than Lebanon's coastal cities.
