European Professional Club Rugby

Image: European club rugby (placeholder)

European Professional Club Rugby (EPCR) is the governing body and organiser of the two leading club rugby union tournaments in Europe: the European Rugby Champions Cup and the EPCR Challenge Cup. Based in Lausanne, Switzerland, it was founded in 2014 to replace European Rugby Cup Limited (ERC), which had run the Heineken Cup since 1995.

Background

EPCR was created after a dispute between ERC and the English and French clubs over competition format, qualification, and commercial rights. The new body brought the leagues and unions of England, France, Ireland, Scotland, Wales, and Italy into a shared governance structure. From the 2022–23 season, South African teams from the United Rugby Championship also entered the competitions.

Competitions

The Champions Cup features 24 leading clubs, while the Challenge Cup serves as the second-tier tournament. Finals are staged at major stadiums across Europe, and the competitions are broadcast widely, with sponsorship including Investec, which became the Champions Cup's title sponsor in 2023.
