Global Media & Entertainment Limited, trading as Global, is a British media and entertainment group founded in 2007 by Ashley Tabor-King. It is the owner of the largest commercial radio group in the United Kingdom and one of Europe's largest outdoor advertising companies.

Radio brands

Global's radio portfolio includes Capital, Heart, Classic FM, LBC, Smooth, Radio X, Capital XTRA and Gold, reaching tens of millions of listeners weekly. Its Global Player app streams its stations and a catalogue of podcasts.

Outdoor advertising

Through acquisitions including Exterion Media and Primesight in 2018, Global became a major operator of outdoor advertising, managing advertising on London Underground, National Rail stations, bus shelters and roadside billboards across the UK.
