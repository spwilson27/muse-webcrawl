My Melody

My Melody (Japanese: マイメロディ, Mai Merodi), frequently shortened to My Melo, is a fictional character produced by the Japanese company Sanrio. She is a white rabbit who wears a pink (originally red) hood resembling a bonnet, and is one of Sanrio's most enduring characters after Hello Kitty, with whom she is close friends.

Character profile

My Melody was born in the forest of Mariland. Her favourite food is almond pound cake, and her hobby is baking cookies with her mother. She wears a hood handmade by her grandmother — the design was originally inspired by Little Red Riding Hood, and early merchandise presented her in red. Her best friend is a mouse named Flat. In later media she is paired with Kuromi, a tomboyish rival in a black hood who debuted in 2005 and became a popular character in her own right.

History

The character debuted in 1975 on stationery and gift merchandise. Like other Sanrio characters, My Melody appears on an enormous range of licensed products, from school supplies to household goods. She has starred or co-starred in several anime productions, including Onegai My Melody (2005–2009), in which Kuromi and Baku escape the Dream World to cause mischief among humans, and the stop-motion Netflix series My Melody & Kuromi (2025), released for the character's 50th anniversary.

Popularity

My Melody regularly places near the top of Sanrio's annual Character Awards popularity rankings, and her design is a staple of Japanese kawaii culture. The visual contrast between the sweet, pink My Melody and the punk-styled Kuromi has driven a large shared merchandising line.
