Stem

Stem may refer to several different things:

Science and nature

  • Plant stem — the structural axis of a vascular plant, supporting leaves, flowers and fruits and transporting water and nutrients.
  • Stem cell — an undifferentiated cell capable of giving rise to specialised cell types.
  • Brain stem — the posterior part of the brain connecting the cerebrum with the spinal cord.

Language and music

  • Word stem — in linguistics, the part of a word to which affixes are added.
  • Stem (music) — the vertical line attached to a note head; also the separated audio tracks (stems) used in music production and remixing.

Other uses

  • STEM — an acronym for science, technology, engineering and mathematics education.
  • Stem (ship) — the forwardmost part of a vessel's bow.
  • Stem (bicycle) — the component connecting the handlebars to the fork.
