Cyber Monday


Online shopping during the Cyber Monday sales (placeholder image)

Observed: Monday after Thanksgiving (United States and elsewhere)

Cyber Monday is a marketing term for the Monday after the Thanksgiving holiday in the United States, created by retailers to encourage online shopping. It falls on the Monday following Black Friday.

History

The term was coined in 2005 by the National Retail Federation's Shop.org division, based on research showing that the Monday after Thanksgiving was consistently one of the biggest online shopping days of the year — attributed at the time to consumers shopping from fast work internet connections. Originally an American phenomenon centered on electronics and e-commerce deals, Cyber Monday has spread internationally and has frequently exceeded Black Friday in U.S. online sales; in recent years U.S. Cyber Monday spending has topped ten billion dollars. Retailers have extended promotions into a broader "Cyber Week."
