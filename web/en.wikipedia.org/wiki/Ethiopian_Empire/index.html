Ethiopian Empire

The Ethiopian Empire, historically also known as Abyssinia, was a kingdom that spanned a geographical area in the current states of Ethiopia and Eritrea. It existed, with interruptions, from about 1270, when the Solomonic dynasty was founded, until 1974, when the monarchy was overthrown in a coup by the Derg.

History

Yekuno Amlak founded the Solomonic dynasty around 1270, claiming descent from the biblical Solomon and the Queen of Sheba. Under Amda Seyon I the empire expanded in the 14th century. Ethiopia resisted the Adal Sultanate in the 16th century with Portuguese help, endured the fragmented Era of the Princes (Zemene Mesafint, 1769–1855), and was reunified by Tewodros II.

Menelik II defeated Italy at the Battle of Adwa in 1896, preserving independence during the Scramble for Africa, and modernised the state from his new capital Addis Ababa. Italy occupied Ethiopia from 1936 to 1941 under Mussolini; Haile Selassie was restored after the East African campaign. Growing discontent, famine and student protest preceded the 1974 revolution.
