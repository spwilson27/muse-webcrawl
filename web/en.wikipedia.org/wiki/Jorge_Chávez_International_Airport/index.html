Jorge Chávez International Airport

Jorge Chávez International Airport (IATA: LIM, ICAO: SPJC) is the main international airport serving Lima, Peru, located in Callao, about 10 km from the historic centre of Lima. It is named after the Peruvian aviator Jorge Chávez (1887–1910), the first person to fly across the Alps.

Opened in 1960 and inaugurated in its modern form in 1965, the airport is the hub of LATAM Perú and a major South American connecting hub. A new terminal and second runway, built under concession by Lima Airport Partners, opened in 2025. It is repeatedly ranked among South America's best airports.
