Gilmore Girls

Gilmore Girls is an American comedy-drama television series created by Amy Sherman-Palladino. It ran for seven seasons from 2000 to 2007, first on The WB and finally on The CW, and was revived in 2016 as the four-part Netflix miniseries Gilmore Girls: A Year in the Life.

Premise

The series centres on Lorelai Gilmore (Lauren Graham), a quick-witted single mother, and her teenage daughter Rory (Alexis Bledel), in the fictional Connecticut town of Stars Hollow. Known for its rapid-fire dialogue, literary and pop-culture references, and coffee-fuelled humour, the show explores family, friendship, ambition, and romance across three generations of Gilmore women.

Reception

The series received critical acclaim, won an Emmy Award, and became a lasting cultural phenomenon, with its revival drawing large audiences. It is frequently cited among the greatest television shows of all time.
