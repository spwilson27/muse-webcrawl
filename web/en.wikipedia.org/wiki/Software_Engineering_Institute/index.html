Software Engineering Institute
Founded: 1984
Location: Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania
Operated by: Carnegie Mellon University

The Software Engineering Institute (SEI) is a United States federally funded research and development centre sponsored by the Department of Defense and operated by Carnegie Mellon University in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. It was established in 1984 to advance software engineering practices.

Work

The SEI is best known for the Capability Maturity Model (CMM) and its successor CMMI, frameworks for assessing and improving organisational processes. Its CERT Division, founded in 1988 as the Computer Emergency Response Team coordination centre, pioneered cybersecurity incident response.

Research areas

Current work includes software architecture, artificial intelligence engineering, cybersecurity, and the acquisition of software-intensive systems.
