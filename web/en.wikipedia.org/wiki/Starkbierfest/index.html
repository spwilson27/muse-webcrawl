Starkbierfest

Starkbierfest is a strong beer festival held in Munich, Germany, during Lent, sometimes described as the city's "fifth season". Breweries serve Starkbier — strong, dark doppelbock lagers such as Paulaner's Salvator — originally brewed by monks as "liquid bread" to sustain them while fasting.

History

The tradition traces to the Paulaner monks, who received permission to brew their strong Salvator beer in the 17th century and later served it publicly. The modern festival at the Paulaner brewery on the Nockherberg is known for the ceremonial tapping of the first keg and a satirical political cabaret (the Politiker-Derblecken) mocking public figures.

Beers

Festival beers typically exceed 7% alcohol by volume and are served in one-litre stoneware steins (Keferloher).
