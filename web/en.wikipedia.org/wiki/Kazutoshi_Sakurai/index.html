Kazutoshi Sakurai (桜井 和寿, Sakurai Kazutoshi, born 8 March 1970) is a Japanese musician, singer and songwriter. He is the lead vocalist, guitarist and principal songwriter of the rock band Mr. Children, one of the best-selling musical acts in Japanese history, with tens of millions of records sold since their major-label debut in 1992.

Career

Sakurai formed the band that became Mr. Children with schoolmates in the late 1980s. His songwriting — combining rock, pop and folk influences with introspective lyrics — drove hits such as "Innocent World", "Tomorrow Never Knows", "Hana" and "Sign". He is also a member of the charity supergroup Bank Band, founded with producer Takeshi Kobayashi, which organises the ap bank fes to raise funds for environmental and disaster-relief causes.

Style and influence

Sakurai is noted for his high, emotive tenor voice and for lyrics dealing with love, mortality and social observation. He has been repeatedly ranked among Japan's most influential vocalists and songwriters.
