Kettle and Stony Point First Nation

The Kettle and Stony Point First Nation (Ojibwe: Wabaakwaamiing Nezihche-ziibi) is an Anishinaabe (Ojibwe, Odawa, and Potawatomi) First Nations band government in Ontario, Canada. Its territory comprises the Kettle Point 44 and Stony Point reserves in Lambton County on the shores of Lake Huron and the Ipperwash area.

History

The Stony Point reserve was expropriated by the Canadian government in 1942 under the War Measures Act to build the Ipperwash military camp, with promises the land would be returned after the war. In 1995, during protests over the occupation, protester Dudley George was shot and killed by an Ontario Provincial Police officer — an event known as the Ipperwash Crisis, which led to a public inquiry.

Ipperwash return

The former Camp Ipperwash lands were formally returned to the First Nation in 2016 following decades of advocacy, negotiations, and cleanup of the site.
