The Mask of Zorro

Directed by: Martin Campbell
Starring: Antonio Banderas, Anthony Hopkins, Catherine Zeta-Jones
Released: 1998
Box office: $250 million

The Mask of Zorro is a 1998 American swashbuckler film directed by Martin Campbell, based on the character Zorro created by Johnston McCulley. Anthony Hopkins plays the aging Don Diego de la Vega, the original Zorro, who trains a young thief, Alejandro Murrieta (Antonio Banderas), to succeed him and take revenge on their common enemy, Don Rafael Montero.

Plot

Twenty years after Montero had de la Vega imprisoned and raised his daughter Elena (Catherine Zeta-Jones) as his own, Montero returns to California with a plot to buy the land with gold secretly mined by exploited laborers. The freed de la Vega moulds the reckless Alejandro into a gentleman bandit; together the two Zorros confront Montero and the brutal Captain Love.

Reception

The film was a critical and commercial success, praised for its swordplay, humor, and old-fashioned adventure spirit, and it launched Catherine Zeta-Jones to stardom in Hollywood. A sequel, The Legend of Zorro, followed in 2005.
