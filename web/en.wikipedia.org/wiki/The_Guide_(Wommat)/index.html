"The Guide" is a jazz composition associated with tenor saxophonist Ike Quebec, recorded during his celebrated run of sessions for Blue Note Records around 1961–1962. The parenthetical designation distinguishes the recording within discographies of Quebec's soulful hard bop output of the period.

Background

Ike Quebec (1918–1963) was a key tenor saxophonist of the swing-to-bebop transition who found a late-career home at Blue Note, where Alfred Lion used him as a talent scout and house musician. His blues-drenched, big-toned playing anchored a series of jukebox singles and albums such as Blue & Sentimental (1961).

Style

Quebec's recordings of the era blend swinging rhythm-and-blues phrasing with bop harmony, typically featuring organ or piano accompaniment and a relaxed, behind-the-beat groove. "The Guide" belongs to this body of work, which has been widely reissued in Blue Note collections.
