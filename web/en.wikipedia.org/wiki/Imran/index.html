Imran

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Imran (Arabic: عمران, ʿImrān) is an Arabic male given name and, in the Quran, the name of a family mentioned prominently in Islamic tradition. The third chapter (surah) of the Quran is named Al Imran, "The Family of Imran," after this family.

In the Quran

In Islamic tradition, Imran is identified as the father of Maryam (Mary, the mother of Isa, Jesus) and thus the maternal grandfather of Jesus. Maryam's mother, the wife of Imran, is said to have dedicated her unborn child to the service of God; Maryam was subsequently raised under the care of the prophet Zakariya (Zechariah). The Quran presents the family of Imran as chosen by God "above the worlds" (Quran 3:33), alongside the families of Adam, Noah, and Abraham.

Some scholars note a correspondence between the Quranic Imran and the biblical Amram, father of Moses, Aaron, and Miriam, and have debated whether the Quranic figure is a distinct person or a conflation; Muslim commentators generally treat Mary's father Imran as a separate, later figure of the same name.

As a given name

Imran is a common given name among Muslims worldwide, including in the Middle East, South Asia, and Southeast Asia. Notable bearers include the Pakistani cricketer and politician Imran Khan.
