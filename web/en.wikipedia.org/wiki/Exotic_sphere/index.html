Exotic sphere

An exotic sphere is a differentiable manifold that is homeomorphic but not diffeomorphic to the standard Euclidean n-sphere — topologically a sphere, but with a different smooth structure.

Exotic sphere
Field: Differential topology
First example: John Milnor, 1956 (7-sphere)

History

John Milnor shocked topologists in 1956 by constructing exotic 7-spheres, using fibre bundles over S4, earning work related to his Fields Medal. Michel Kervaire and Milnor classified exotic spheres in many dimensions via groups of homotopy spheres.

Examples and classification

There are 28 oriented smooth structures on the 7-sphere. No exotic spheres exist in dimensions 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6; dimension 4 remains a famous open case tied to the smooth Poincaré conjecture. Exotic spheres arise in surgery theory and have applications in theoretical physics.
