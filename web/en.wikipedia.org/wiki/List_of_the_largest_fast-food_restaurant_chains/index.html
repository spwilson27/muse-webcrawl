List of the largest fast-food restaurant chains

This is a list of the largest fast-food restaurant chains in the world, ranked by number of locations. Counts change constantly with openings, closures and franchise reporting, and are approximate for the mid-2020s. The Chinese chain Mixue Ice Cream & Tea overtook McDonald's in location count in the mid-2020s, driven by rapid expansion in China and Southeast Asia.
