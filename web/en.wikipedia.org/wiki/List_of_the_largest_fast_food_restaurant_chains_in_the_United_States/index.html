List of the largest fast food restaurant chains in the United States

This is a list of the largest fast food restaurant chains in the United States, ranked by the number of locations and by sales. The American fast food industry began with chains such as White Castle (founded 1921) and expanded rapidly after the Second World War with franchising.

By sales

By revenue, McDonald's is by far the largest fast food chain in the United States, followed by Starbucks, Chick-fil-A and Taco Bell. Chick-fil-A achieves notably high sales per restaurant despite closing on Sundays.
