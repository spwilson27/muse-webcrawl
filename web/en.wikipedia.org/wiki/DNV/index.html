DNV

DNV (formerly DNV GL and Det Norske Veritas) is an international accredited registrar and classification society headquartered in Høvik, Norway. Founded in 1864 in Oslo, the company employs around 15,000 people in more than 100 countries.

DNV provides classification, technical assurance, software and independent expert advisory services to the maritime, oil and gas, and energy industries. It also provides certification services to customers across many industries, including food and beverage, healthcare and automotive, and is one of the world's largest certification bodies.

History

Det Norske Veritas was founded to inspect and appraise Norwegian merchant ships. In 2013 it merged with Germanischer Lloyd to form DNV GL, reverting to the name DNV in 2021. The DNV Foundation (Stiftelsen Det Norske Veritas) is the sole owner, and profits are reinvested in research and development.

Activities

Its research arm publishes widely cited industry outlooks such as the Energy Transition Outlook. DNV rules for ship classification are among the most used worldwide.
