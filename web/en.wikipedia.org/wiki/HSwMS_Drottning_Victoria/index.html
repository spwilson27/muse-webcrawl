HSwMS Drottning Victoria

HSwMS Drottning Victoria was a Sverige-class coastal defence ship of the Swedish Navy, named after Queen Victoria of Sweden. Launched in 1917 and commissioned in 1921, she was the second of three sister ships that formed the backbone of Sweden's fleet through both world wars, mounting four 283 mm (11.1 in) guns. She served until 1957 and was scrapped in 1959.

Service

Built at Götaverken in Gothenburg, Drottning Victoria was modernised in the 1930s with new fire control and anti-aircraft armament. During the Second World War she served with the Coastal Fleet maintaining Swedish neutrality patrols. After the war the ageing ship was used for training, and in the 1950s served as a target and accommodation vessel before being sold for scrap.
