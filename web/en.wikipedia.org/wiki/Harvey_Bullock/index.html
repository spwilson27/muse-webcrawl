Harvey Bullock is a fictional police detective appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, commonly in association with Batman. A slovenly, hard-drinking, rough-edged Gotham City Police Department detective with flexible ethics but a bedrock loyalty to Commissioner James Gordon, Bullock was created by Archie Goodwin and Howard Chaykin and first appeared in Detective Comics #441 (1974).

Fictional biography

Bullock was introduced as an apparently corrupt officer planted by the mayor to sabotage Gordon, but the character softened in later portrayals into a gruff veteran whose brutality and shortcuts mask a genuine, if cynical, commitment to the city. He became a central supporting player in the Gotham Central ensemble and in Batman: The Animated Series (1992), where his suspicion of Batman was a running theme. Frequently partnered with Renee Montoya, Bullock has been depicted resigning in disgrace after killing a suspect and later returning to the force. In the television series Gotham (2014–2019) he was played by Donal Logue as the partner of a young Jim Gordon.

Characterisation

Bullock embodies the old-school street cop of noir fiction: fedora, crumpled raincoat, doughnuts and threats, contrasted with Gordon's rectitude and Batman's absolutism. Modern writers use him to explore the compromises policing demands in Gotham and as comic relief grounded in genuine pathos.
