SAP Center at San Jose (originally known as the San Jose Arena and commonly called "the Shark Tank") is an indoor arena located in San Jose, California. It is the home of the San Jose Sharks of the National Hockey League and the San Jose Barracuda of the American Hockey League.

History

The arena opened in 1993, two years after the Sharks began play at the Cow Palace in Daly City. Naming rights were held by Compaq and then Hewlett-Packard, during which the building was known as HP Pavilion, before SAP acquired the rights in 2013. The arena has hosted major events including NHL All-Star Games, the 2019 Stanley Cup Final home games for the Sharks' 2016 and 2019 playoff runs, NCAA basketball tournament games, figure skating championships, and numerous concerts.

Features

The building seats 17,562 spectators for hockey. It is known for the Sharks' distinctive entrance, in which players skate onto the ice through a large shark head with glowing eyes during pregame introductions. The arena sits near Diridon Station, San Jose's main transit hub, and has been the subject of long-term negotiations between the Sharks and the city over renovations and the impact of nearby development.
