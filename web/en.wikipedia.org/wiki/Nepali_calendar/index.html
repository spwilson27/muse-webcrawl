Nepali calendar

The Nepali calendar (Nepali: ‎नेपाली पात्रो) is the official solar calendar of Nepal, based on the Bikram Sambat era. It runs approximately 56.7 years ahead of the Gregorian calendar, so that, for example, the Nepali year 2081 began in mid-April 2024.

Months

The year has twelve months whose lengths vary from 29 to 32 days and are fixed by official committee rather than by a simple arithmetic rule:

  • Baisakh, Jestha, Ashadh, Shrawan, Bhadra, Ashwin
  • Kartik, Mangsir, Poush, Magh, Falgun, Chaitra

The new year (Naya Barsha) falls on 1 Baisakh, in mid-April. Because month lengths are irregular and set in advance by the government's calendar decision committee, printed calendars and conversion tables are widely used.

Eras and other calendars

The Bikram Sambat era is traditionally dated from 57 BCE and attributed to King Vikramaditya. Nepal also recognises other calendars in cultural and religious life, including the lunar Nepal Sambat (revived as a national calendar), the Gregorian calendar for international business, and various ethnic and religious calendars.
