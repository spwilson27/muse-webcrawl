José Ángel Gutiérrez


José Ángel Gutiérrez
Born: 25 October 1944, Crystal City, Texas, U.S.
Occupation: Attorney, professor, political activist
Known for: Co-founder of MAYO; founder of La Raza Unida Party

José Ángel Gutiérrez (born 25 October 1944) is an American attorney, professor and Chicano civil rights activist. He was a co-founder of the Mexican American Youth Organization (MAYO) and a founding leader of La Raza Unida Party in Texas.

Activism

Growing up in Crystal City, Texas, Gutiérrez experienced the segregation faced by Mexican Americans. While at Texas A&I University he helped organise students, and in 1967 he co-founded MAYO to press for educational and political reform. In 1970 he helped launch La Raza Unida Party, which won local elections in Crystal City and other South Texas communities, including school board victories that followed the Crystal City school walkouts of 1969.

Later career

Gutiérrez earned advanced degrees in political science and a law degree, served as a county judge in Zavala County, and became a professor at the University of Texas at Arlington, where he founded the Center for Mexican American Studies. His papers and oral histories are an important record of the Chicano movement.
