Unicron is a fictional character and the principal antagonist in the Transformers franchise. He is a planet-sized robotic being who devours planets to sustain himself and is regarded as the embodiment of chaos and evil in Transformers mythology. Unicron is the eternal arch-enemy of his twin brother, Primus, the creator god of the Transformers.

Unicron made his debut in the 1986 animated film The Transformers: The Movie, where he was voiced by Orson Welles in the actor's final film role. In the film, Unicron transforms the damaged Megatron into Galvatron and tasks him with destroying the Autobot Matrix of Leadership.

Character biography

In most continuities, Unicron and Primus were created as twin cosmic entities. While Primus created the thirteen original Transformers and the planet Cybertron, Unicron wandered the universe consuming worlds. Unicron can transform from a mechanical planet into an enormous robot, and his presence is a threat to all life in the universe.

In other media

Unicron has appeared in numerous Transformers television series, comic books, and films, including Transformers: Armada, Transformers: Prime, and Transformers: The Last Knight (2017), in which it is revealed that Unicron is the planet Earth itself.
