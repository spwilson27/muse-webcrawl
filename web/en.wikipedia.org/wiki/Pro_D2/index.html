Pro D2

The Pro D2 is the second tier of French professional rugby union, below the Top 14. It is organised by the Ligue Nationale de Rugby (LNR) and contested by 16 clubs in a home-and-away league season followed by play-offs.

Format

Each team plays the others twice during the regular season. The top six advance to the play-off phase: the first- and second-placed teams receive byes to the semi-finals, while teams ranked third to sixth contest barrage matches. The play-off winner is promoted to the Top 14, and the losing finalist traditionally plays an access match against the 13th-placed Top 14 side for the second promotion place. The bottom two clubs are relegated to the Nationale, the semi-professional third division.

History

A second division of French rugby existed in various forms through the twentieth century; the professional Pro D2 name dates from the restructuring of the French leagues in 2000. The competition is notable for strong regional clubs — historically including Béziers, Agen, Perpignan, Bayonne and Biarritz — and for passionate local support, with several teams having won French championships before the professional era.
