Balenciaga SA (Spanish: [balenˈθjaɣa]) is a high fashion house founded in 1919 by the Spanish couturier Cristóbal Balenciaga in San Sebastián, Spain, and based in Paris, France. Balenciaga produces ready-to-wear, shoes, handbags, and accessories, and licenses its name and branding to Coty for fragrances. The house is owned by the French luxury group Kering.

Cristóbal Balenciaga was renowned as a couturier of uncompromising standards and was described as "the master of us all" by Christian Dior. His sculptural silhouettes — the balloon dress, the baby doll dress, the sack dress, and the trapeze line — reshaped women's fashion in the post-war era.

History

Cristóbal Balenciaga opened his first boutique in San Sebastián in 1919, later expanding to Madrid and Barcelona. The Spanish Civil War forced him to close his Spanish stores and move to Paris, where he opened his couture house on Avenue George V in 1937. He retired in 1968 and the house closed; he died in 1972.

The rights to the Balenciaga name were acquired in the 1980s, and the house was relaunched with ready-to-wear collections. Michel Goma and later Josephus Thimister designed for the house in the 1990s, before Nicolas Ghesquière became creative director in 1997. Ghesquière revitalised the brand over fifteen years, making it one of the most influential and commercially successful labels of its period.

Recent decades

Ghesquière departed in 2012 and was succeeded by Alexander Wang (2012–2015). In 2015 Demna Gvasalia, co-founder of Vetements, became artistic director; under Demna the house became known for oversized streetwear-inflected silhouettes, the Triple S sneaker, the Hourglass bag, and highly publicised runway spectacles. Demna departed in 2025, when Pierpaolo Piccioli, formerly of Valentino, was appointed creative director.

In November 2022 the brand faced a major public controversy over two advertising campaigns, one featuring children with teddy-bear bags styled with bondage accessories, prompting widespread criticism and apologies from the company.

Products

  • Ready-to-wear for women and men, and haute couture (revived in 2021, fifty-three years after Cristóbal Balenciaga's last couture show).
  • Leather goods, including the City (Motorcycle) bag, Hourglass, and Rodeo lines.
  • Footwear, notably the Triple S, Speed Trainer, and Defender sneakers.
  • Fragrances under licence, beginning historically with Le Dix (1947).
