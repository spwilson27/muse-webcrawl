Greatest hits album

Greatest hits compilations are among the best-selling albums of all time.

A greatest hits album, also known as a best of album or a catalogue album, is a type of compilation album that collects popular and commercially successful songs by a particular artist or band. While it is typically supported by the artist, it can also be created by the artist's record label without their express approval, in order to generate sales, being typically regarded as a marketing tool. They are typically produced after an artist has had enough successful songs to fill a compilation.

History

The format emerged in the 1950s as record labels began repackaging hit singles onto long-playing records. Johnny Mathis's Johnny's Greatest Hits (1958) is often credited as an early landmark of the genre and remained on the Billboard album chart for years. As the album era developed, greatest hits packages became a standard part of major artists' catalogues, issued at career milestones, at the end of recording contracts, or during the lucrative holiday sales season.

Content and marketing

Greatest hits albums usually consist of an artist's most successful singles, though they may also include album tracks, new recordings, remixes, or previously unreleased material intended to attract existing fans as well as casual listeners. A common practice is to include one or two new songs, which may themselves be released as singles. Because they gather an artist's best-known material, compilations are frequently among the biggest sellers in a catalogue: collections such as the Eagles' Their Greatest Hits (1971–1975) and ABBA's Gold: Greatest Hits are among the best-selling albums of all time worldwide.

Criticism

Compilation albums have sometimes been criticised as purely commercial exercises, particularly when released without an artist's involvement or when an artist's catalogue is repackaged repeatedly in slightly different configurations. In the streaming era, label-curated playlists have partly assumed the role once played by physical greatest hits releases, though official compilations continue to be issued.
