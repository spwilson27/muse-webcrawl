Nougat

Nougat is a family of confections made with sugar or honey, roasted nuts (almonds, walnuts, pistachios or hazelnuts), whipped egg whites, and sometimes candied fruit.

Varieties

White nougat includes the French nougat de Montélimar and Spanish turrón; brown nougat (nougatine) is made without egg whites and has a firmer, crunchier texture. Nougat is a common filling in chocolate bars worldwide.

History

Similar sweets have ancient roots in the Middle East and Mediterranean, described in medieval Arabic and Persian cookbooks and spread to southern Europe.
