Scientific and Technological Advanced Research Laboratories, usually shortened to S.T.A.R. Labs, is a fictional scientific research and development facility organization appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. It first appeared in Superman vol. 1 #246 in 1971 and was created by writer Cary Bates and artist Rich Buckler.

Description

S.T.A.R. Labs is depicted as one of the largest and most technologically advanced scientific organizations in the DC Universe, with laboratories in numerous cities including Metropolis, Central City, Gotham City, and other locations around the world. The organization studies superhuman phenomena, alien technology, and advanced weaponry, and its scientists frequently assist superheroes such as Superman and the Flash.

In many storylines, S.T.A.R. Labs serves as a hub for the treatment and study of metahumans — individuals gifted with superhuman abilities. Its facilities are also a recurring source of conflict, as its experiments sometimes go awry or its technology is stolen by villains.

In other media

S.T.A.R. Labs appears prominently in The Flash television series (2014–2023), where the Central City facility's particle accelerator explosion gives Barry Allen his superhuman speed and creates numerous metahumans. In the series, the facility is run by Harrison Wells (Eobard Thawne in disguise) and staffed by Caitlin Snow and Cisco Ramon. The organization has also appeared in the animated series Young Justice and in the film Justice League (2017), where its labs store a Mother Box.
