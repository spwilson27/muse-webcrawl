Royal New Zealand Navy

The Royal New Zealand Navy (RNZN; Māori: Te Taua Moana o Aotearoa) is the maritime arm of the New Zealand Defence Force. It was formally established on 1 October 1941.

History

New Zealand naval forces served in both World Wars, notably the cruiser HMNZS Achilles at the Battle of the River Plate in 1939. After 1941 the RNZN operated as an independent service, later serving in Korea, the Malayan Emergency and peacekeeping operations.

Fleet

Based at Devonport Naval Base in Auckland, the fleet includes Anzac-class frigates HMNZS Te Kaha and Te Mana, the replenishment ship HMNZS Aotearoa, the multi-role vessel HMNZS Canterbury, and offshore and inshore patrol vessels.
