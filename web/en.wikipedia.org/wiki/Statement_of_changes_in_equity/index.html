Statement of changes in equity

The statement of changes in equity is one of the primary financial statements, reconciling the equity of a company at the beginning of a period with its equity at the end, and explaining every movement between the two.

Contents

The statement shows the effects of profit or loss, other comprehensive income, share issues and buybacks, dividends, transfers to and from reserves, and corrections of prior-period errors, broken down by component of equity such as share capital, share premium, revaluation reserve, and retained earnings.

Requirements

IAS 1 under IFRS requires a statement of changes in equity in a complete set of financial statements; US GAAP permits a similar statement of shareholders' equity. It complements the balance sheet, income statement, and cash flow statement.
