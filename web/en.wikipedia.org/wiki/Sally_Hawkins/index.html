Sally Cecilia Hawkins (born 27 April 1976) is an English actress. She began her career on stage and screen in the late 1990s and gained critical acclaim for her collaborations with director Mike Leigh, including Vera Drake (2004) and the comedy-drama Happy-Go-Lucky (2008), for which she won the Golden Globe Award for Best Actress in a Musical or Comedy and the Silver Bear for Best Actress at Berlin.

Hawkins received Academy Award nominations for her performance as the sister of Cate Blanchett's character in Woody Allen's Blue Jasmine (2013) and for her leading role as a mute cleaner who falls in love with an amphibious creature in Guillermo del Toro's fantasy drama The Shape of Water (2017). She is also widely known for playing Mrs Brown in the Paddington films (2014–2024).

Early life

Hawkins was born in Dulwich, London, the daughter of Jacqui and Colin Hawkins, authors and illustrators of children's books. She was raised in Blackheath and attended James Allen's Girls' School in Dulwich. Dyslexic, she was drawn to acting through school productions and graduated from the Royal Academy of Dramatic Art in 1998.

Career

Breakthrough

After early roles in All or Nothing (2002) and Vera Drake (2004) for Mike Leigh, and television work including the BBC adaptations Tipping the Velvet (2002) and Fingersmith (2005), Hawkins won international recognition as the irrepressibly cheerful Poppy in Happy-Go-Lucky (2008).

Further acclaim

She earned a BAFTA nomination for Made in Dagenham (2010) and an Oscar nomination for Best Supporting Actress for Blue Jasmine (2013). Her portrayal of Elisa Esposito in The Shape of Water (2017) brought a second Oscar nomination, for Best Actress. Subsequent work includes Maudie (2016), the Paddington sequels, Spencer (2021), The Lost King (2022), and Wonka (2023).

Personal life

Hawkins has spoken about living with lupus and being dyslexic, and is notably private about her personal life. She has been an ambassador for various charitable causes.
