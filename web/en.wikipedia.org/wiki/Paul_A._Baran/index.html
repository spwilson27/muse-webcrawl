Paul A. Baran

Paul Alexander Baran (25 August 1909 – 26 March 1964) was a Russian-born American Marxist economist, long a professor at Stanford University. He is known for his work on monopoly capitalism and underdevelopment.

Life

Born in Mykolaiv in the Russian Empire, Baran was educated in Germany, worked in the Soviet Union and in German banking, emigrated to the United States before the Second World War, and earned a doctorate from Harvard. He worked for US government agencies during the war before joining Stanford in 1948, where he remained despite political pressure during the McCarthy era.

Work

His book The Political Economy of Growth (1957) developed the concept of economic surplus and influenced dependency theory. With Paul Sweezy he co-authored Monopoly Capital, published posthumously in 1966, a landmark of American Marxist economics associated with Monthly Review.
