Heavy equipment

Heavy equipment, also known as heavy machinery or plant, refers to heavy-duty vehicles and machinery specially designed for construction, earthwork, mining, forestry, agriculture and other industrial tasks. Common examples include excavators, bulldozers, wheel loaders, cranes, dump trucks, graders, pavers and backhoes.

Types

Earthmoving equipment moves soil and rock; lifting equipment such as tower and mobile cranes raises materials; compaction equipment prepares ground surfaces; and material-handling machines move bulk goods. Machines are typically diesel-powered with hydraulic systems driving their working implements.

Industry

Major global manufacturers include Caterpillar, Komatsu, Deere & Company, Volvo Construction Equipment, Hitachi Construction Machinery, Liebherr and Sany. Equipment is sold, leased or rented, and increasingly fitted with telematics, GPS machine guidance and semi-autonomous features.

Safety and operation

Operation generally requires training and, in many jurisdictions, licensing. Major hazards include rollovers, struck-by incidents and contact with overhead power lines.
