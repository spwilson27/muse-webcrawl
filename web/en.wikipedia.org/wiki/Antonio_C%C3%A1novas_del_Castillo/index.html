Antonio Cánovas del Castillo (1828–1897) was a Spanish politician and historian, and the chief architect of the political system of the Restoration of the Bourbon monarchy in Spain. He served six times as Prime Minister between 1874 and 1897.

The Restoration system

Cánovas engineered the return of Alfonso XII in 1874 and drafted the Constitution of 1876. He designed the turno pacífico, the managed alternation in power between his Conservative Party and the Liberal Party of Práxedes Mateo Sagasta, intended to stabilise Spain after decades of coups and civil wars.

Death

He was assassinated in 1897 by the Italian anarchist Michele Angiolillo, in revenge for the repression of anarchists in Barcelona. His death came on the eve of the Spanish–American War of 1898.
