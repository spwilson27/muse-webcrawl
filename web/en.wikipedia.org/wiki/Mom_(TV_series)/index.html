Mom is an American television sitcom created by Chuck Lorre, Eddie Gorodetsky, and Gemma Baker that aired on CBS from September 23, 2013, to May 13, 2021. Set in Napa, California, it follows dysfunctional mother–daughter duo Bonnie and Christy Plunkett, who, having battled addiction, attempt to pull their lives together by attending Alcoholics Anonymous meetings.

Premise

Christy Plunkett (Anna Faris) is a single mother, newly sober, working as a waitress while attending law school later in the series. Her estranged mother Bonnie (Allison Janney), also a recovering addict, re-enters her life, and much of the comedy and drama arises from their fraught relationship and from their circle of friends in recovery. Although a sitcom, the series addressed serious subjects including addiction, relapse, domestic violence, cancer, and death, and was praised for its balance of humour and realistic treatment of recovery.

Reception and awards

The series received a positive response from critics, particularly for its writing and performances. Allison Janney won two consecutive Primetime Emmy Awards for Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Comedy Series for her role, in 2014 and 2015, and the show received numerous other nominations and awards during its run. Faris departed the series after the seventh season, and the eighth season centred on Bonnie and the remaining ensemble before the show concluded in 2021.
