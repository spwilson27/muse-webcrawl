Armed Forces Recreation Center

The Edelweiss Lodge and Resort in Garmisch, Germany

The Armed Forces Recreation Centers (AFRCs) are a group of resort hotels operated by the United States Department of Defense for members of the U.S. armed forces, their families, and other authorised patrons. The resorts provide affordable vacation accommodation and recreational facilities at desirable locations, operated as self-supporting activities within the military's Morale, Welfare and Recreation programs, principally under the U.S. Army's Family and MWR command.

Locations

  • Shades of Green — at Walt Disney World Resort, Florida, serving the Orlando area since the Army acquired the property in 1994.
  • Hale Koa Hotel — on Waikiki Beach, Honolulu, Hawaii, opened in 1975.
  • Edelweiss Lodge and Resort — in Garmisch-Partenkirchen, Germany, opened in 2004, serving U.S. forces in Europe.
  • Dragon Hill Lodge — in Seoul, South Korea, on the Yongsan Garrison, opened in 1990.

Historically the Army also operated recreation hotels in the Bavarian Alps around Berchtesgaden and Garmisch dating from the immediate post-Second World War occupation, including properties that were eventually returned to German control.

Eligibility and operation

Rooms are allocated by category of patron — active-duty personnel receive priority, followed by reservists, retirees, DoD civilians and sponsored guests — and room rates are scaled by rank and pay grade, so junior service members pay less. The resorts offer restaurants, tours, ticket sales and recreation programs typical of commercial resorts, tailored to their locations.
