The Estadio Monumental Antonio Vespucio Liberti, officially Estadio Mâs Monumental for sponsorship reasons and popularly known simply as El Monumental, is a stadium in the Belgrano district of Buenos Aires, Argentina. It is the home ground of Club Atlético River Plate and the largest stadium in Argentina and in South America.

History

The stadium was inaugurated on 26 May 1938 with a friendly match between River Plate and Uruguayan club Peñarol. It is named after Antonio Vespucio Liberti, the club president who drove its construction. The ground hosted nine matches of the 1978 FIFA World Cup, including the final, in which Argentina defeated the Netherlands 3–1 after extra time.

A major renovation completed in 2023 lowered the pitch, removed the athletics track and added new lower-tier seating, raising capacity above 83,000 and making the Monumental the largest stadium on the continent. The venue has also staged numerous Copa Libertadores finals, the 2015 and 2024 finals among them, and is the usual home of the Argentina national football team.

Other events

Beyond football, the Monumental is one of Argentina's principal concert venues and has hosted performances by international acts as well as rugby union internationals and other sporting events.
