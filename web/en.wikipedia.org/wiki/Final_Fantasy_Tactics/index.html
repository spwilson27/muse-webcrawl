Final Fantasy Tactics is a 1997 tactical role-playing game developed and published by Square for the PlayStation. Directed by Yasumi Matsuno, it is a spin-off of the Final Fantasy series and the first entry set in the world of Ivalice, combining the series' job system with grid-based, turn-based battles on isometric maps.

Gameplay

Battles take place on three-dimensional isometric fields where units move and act according to speed and charge-time mechanics. Characters can be developed through an extensive job system with over twenty classes — including knight, archer, black mage, summoner, and ninja — learning abilities from one job that can be combined with others. Height, facing, terrain, and a zodiac compatibility system affect combat.

Plot

The story follows Ramza Beoulve, a young noble who becomes a mercenary during the War of the Lions, a succession conflict in the kingdom of Ivalice following the Fifty Years' War. Ramza uncovers a conspiracy involving the Church of Glabados and the Lucavi demons while his childhood friend Delita rises through manipulation and betrayal.

Reception and legacy

The game was acclaimed for its deep customisation, mature political narrative, and Hitoshi Sakimoto's score, and has sold over two million copies. An enhanced remake, Final Fantasy Tactics: The War of the Lions, was released for PlayStation Portable in 2007 and later for mobile platforms.
