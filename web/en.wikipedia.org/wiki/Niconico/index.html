Niconico (Japanese: ニコニコ), formerly Nico Nico Douga, is a Japanese video-sharing website managed by Dwango, a subsidiary of Kadokawa. Launched in December 2006, it is known for its distinctive feature of scrolling viewer comments (danmaku-style overlays) that appear directly over the video in real time.

Features and culture

Niconico became a centre of Japanese internet culture, nurturing Vocaloid music, fan-made animations, game commentary (jikkyō), and the "utattemita" (sung-it-myself) cover scene. It hosts live broadcasts (Niconama), and its annual Chōkaigi festival celebrates user-generated content.

History

The service initially relied on YouTube as its video source before building its own infrastructure. Though overtaken by YouTube in Japan, it remains influential. A major cyberattack in June 2024 forced a prolonged shutdown of Kadokawa's services, after which Niconico relaunched in a rebuilt form.
