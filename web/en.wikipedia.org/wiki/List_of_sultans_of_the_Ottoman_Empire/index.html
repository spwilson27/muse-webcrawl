List of sultans of the Ottoman Empire

The Ottoman Empire was ruled by sultans of the House of Osman from its founding around 1299 until the abolition of the sultanate on 1 November 1922. In all there were 36 sultans. From 1517, beginning with Selim I, the sultans also held the title of Caliph of Islam. After a civil war (the Ottoman Interregnum, 1402–1413) following Timur's defeat of Bayezid I at Ankara, the empire was reunified by Mehmed I.
