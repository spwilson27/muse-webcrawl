Mariska Magdolna Hargitay (born January 23, 1964) is an American actress, producer, and director. The daughter of actress Jayne Mansfield and bodybuilder Mickey Hargitay, she is best known for starring as Olivia Benson on the NBC drama series Law & Order: Special Victims Unit (1999–present), for which she has received a Primetime Emmy Award and a Golden Globe Award. Benson is the longest-running character in a live-action primetime television series in American history, and Hargitay is among the highest-paid actresses on television.

Early life

Hargitay was born in Santa Monica, California, the daughter of former Miss Universe and actress Jayne Mansfield and Hungarian-born former Mr. Universe Mickey Hargitay. In June 1967, when she was three years old, she and two of her brothers survived the car crash that killed their mother. Hargitay was raised by her father and his third wife, Ellen. She attended the University of California, Los Angeles, studying theatre, and was crowned Miss Beverly Hills USA in 1982.

Career

Hargitay made her film debut in the horror comedy Ghoulies (1984). Early roles included the series Downtown (1986–87) and Falcon Crest, guest appearances on Seinfeld and Ellen, a recurring role on ER as Cynthia Hooper (1997–98), and the film Leaving Las Vegas (1995).

In 1999 she was cast as Detective (later Captain) Olivia Benson in Law & Order: Special Victims Unit, the first spin-off of Dick Wolf's Law & Order franchise. She won the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Drama Series in 2006 and the Golden Globe Award for Best Actress in a Television Drama in 2005. She has also directed episodes of the series and serves as an executive producer. Her portrayal of a detective specialising in sex crimes led her to train as a rape crisis counsellor.

Philanthropy

Inspired by letters from viewers who disclosed their own experiences of sexual assault, Hargitay founded the Joyful Heart Foundation in 2004, which supports survivors of sexual assault, domestic violence and child abuse. She has campaigned prominently to end the backlog of untested rape kits in the United States, testifying before Congress and producing the documentary I Am Evidence (2017).

Personal life

Hargitay married actor Peter Hermann, whom she met on the set of SVU, in 2004. They have three children, one biological son and two adopted children.
