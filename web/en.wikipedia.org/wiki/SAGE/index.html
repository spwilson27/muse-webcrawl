SAGE

SAGE may refer to several things. The best-known is the Semi-Automatic Ground Environment, the United States' Cold War air-defence computer network.

Semi-Automatic Ground Environment

The Semi-Automatic Ground Environment was a system of large computers and associated networking equipment, developed by MIT's Lincoln Laboratory and built largely by IBM, that coordinated NORAD's radar data to produce a single image of US airspace for air defence from the late 1950s into the 1980s. At its heart was the AN/FSQ-7 computer, the largest computer system ever built, with duplex vacuum-tube machines installed in hardened direction centres across North America. SAGE pioneered many technologies, including real-time computing, magnetic-core memory, CRT displays with light guns, and modem-based data communication.

Other uses

  • SAGE Publishing, an academic publishing company founded in 1965.
  • SageMath (formerly Sage), a free open-source mathematics software system.
  • SAGE (Stratospheric Aerosol and Gas Experiment), a series of NASA Earth-observation instruments.
  • SAGE Group, the British enterprise software company (Sage Group plc).
