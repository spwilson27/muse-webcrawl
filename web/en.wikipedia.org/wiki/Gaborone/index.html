Gaborone

Gaborone is the capital and largest city of Botswana, with about 250,000 residents. It is located in the southeastern corner of Botswana, near the South African border, between the Kgale and Oodi hills, on the Notwane River.

Gaborone was founded in the 1960s as a planned capital, chosen for its water supply and neutrality among Botswana's tribes, ahead of independence in 1966 (replacing Mafeking, which lay in South Africa). It is named after Chief Gaborone of the Batlokwa. The city hosts the government enclave, the National Assembly, the headquarters of the Southern African Development Community (SADC), the University of Botswana and the Three Dikgosi Monument. Diamonds (the Debswana company) underpin its economy; the Gaborone Dam supplies its water.
