LGBTQ

LGBTQ
MeaningLesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, and queer (or questioning)
Usage since1990s–2000s, widespread in the 2010s
Related formsLGBT, LGBTQIA+, LGBTQ+, LGBT+

LGBTQ is an extended form of the initialism LGBT, standing for lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, and queer (the Q is also read as "questioning"). It functions as an umbrella term for sexual and gender minorities and their communities, movements and cultures.

Usage

The addition of Q reflected the reclamation of "queer" as an inclusive umbrella word by activists and academics from the early 1990s (e.g., Queer Nation and queer theory), and a wish to include people whose identities are fluid or not captured by the four original letters. Style guides and institutions increasingly adopted LGBTQ in the 2010s; in 2016 the Associated Press and major LGBTQ media organisations used it as a standard form.

Debates

Discussion continues over letter order, further additions (I for intersex, A for asexual, 2S for Two-Spirit, a plus sign), and whether ever-longer initialisms aid or hinder clarity. Some writers use "LGBTQ+" to signal openness. Others retain LGBT, or use specific terms relevant to the subject at hand.
