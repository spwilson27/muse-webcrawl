Masters of the Air

Masters of the Air is an American war drama miniseries created by John Shiban and John Orloff for Apple TV+. It is based on the 2007 book of the same name by Donald L. Miller and follows the actions of the 100th Bomb Group ("the Bloody Hundredth"), a B-17 Flying Fortress heavy bomber unit of the United States Army Air Forces during World War II. It is a companion piece to Band of Brothers (2001) and The Pacific (2010).

Premise

The series portrays the daylight bombing campaign over Nazi-occupied Europe from 1943 to 1945, centred on Majors Gale "Buck" Cleven and John "Bucky" Egan and the crews of the 100th Bomb Group based at RAF Thorpe Abbotts in England. It depicts missions over Berlin, the Regensburg and Schweinfurt raids, and the experiences of airmen shot down and held as prisoners of war, as well as the Tuskegee Airmen of the 332nd Fighter Group in the final episode.

Production

Originally developed at HBO, the series moved to Apple in 2019. With a reported production budget of around $250 million, it was filmed in England beginning in 2021, using large-scale sets and LED volume stages for the aerial sequences. It premiered on 26 January 2024 to generally positive reviews, with praise for its production values and performances.
