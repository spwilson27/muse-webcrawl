Midpoint
Field: Geometry
Definition: Point equidistant from both endpoints of a segment

In geometry, the midpoint is the middle point of a line segment. It is equidistant from both endpoints, and it is the centroid of the segment and of the endpoints themselves.

Formula

For a segment with endpoints (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) in the plane, the midpoint is ((x1 + x2)/2, (y1 + y2)/2) — the average of the coordinates. The definition extends to three dimensions and to n-dimensional space by averaging each coordinate.

Construction

The midpoint can be constructed with compass and straightedge by drawing two circles of equal radius centred on the endpoints; the line joining their intersections (the perpendicular bisector) crosses the segment at its midpoint.
