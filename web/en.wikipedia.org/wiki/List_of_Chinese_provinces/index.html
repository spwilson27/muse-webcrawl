Provinces of China

This article lists the province-level administrative divisions of China. The People's Republic of China administers 34 province-level divisions: 23 provinces, 5 autonomous regions, 4 direct-controlled municipalities, and 2 special administrative regions. (The count of 23 provinces includes Taiwan, which the PRC claims but does not administer; the Republic of China administers Taiwan and small parts of Fujian.)

Other province-level divisions

The autonomous regions are Guangxi, Inner Mongolia, Ningxia, Xinjiang, and Tibet (Xizang). The municipalities are Beijing, Tianjin, Shanghai, and Chongqing. The special administrative regions are Hong Kong and Macau.
