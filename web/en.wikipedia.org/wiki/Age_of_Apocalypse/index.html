Age of Apocalypse

The Age of Apocalypse is a 1995–1996 comic book crossover storyline published by Marvel Comics across its X-Men titles. It depicted an alternate timeline in which the mutant villain Apocalypse conquered much of the world after Professor Charles Xavier was accidentally killed before forming the X-Men.

Plot

In the preceding storyline "Legion Quest", Legion travelled back in time to kill Magneto but accidentally killed his own father, Professor X. The resulting timeline, designated Earth-295, diverged: Magneto, honouring Xavier's dream, founded the X-Men, while the ancient mutant Apocalypse awoke early and launched a campaign of conquest and genetic culling. The time-travelling Bishop, the only person who remembered the original timeline, worked with Magneto's X-Men to reach the M'Kraan Crystal and restore history. The storyline temporarily replaced all X-Men titles with renamed books (such as Astonishing X-Men, Amazing X-Men, Weapon X and Factor X) showing the altered versions of familiar characters.

Legacy

The storyline was a major commercial success and the dystopian timeline has been revisited repeatedly, including a 2005 tenth-anniversary sequel and later appearances in Uncanny X-Force and Secret Wars. Several characters from the reality, including X-Man (Nate Grey), Dark Beast and Sugar Man, crossed into the main Marvel continuity.
