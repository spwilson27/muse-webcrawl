Nas

Nasir bin Olu Dara Jones (born September 14, 1973), known mononymously as Nas, is an American rapper. Raised in the Queensbridge Houses in Queens, New York, he is widely regarded as one of the greatest lyricists in hip hop. His debut album Illmatic (1994), recorded while he was still a teenager, is consistently ranked among the greatest albums of all time in any genre.

Early life and Illmatic

Nas is the son of jazz musician Olu Dara and postal worker Fannie Ann Jones. Dropping out of school in the eighth grade, he educated himself and began rapping as "Nasty Nas". He gained attention with appearances on Main Source's "Live at the Barbeque" (1991) and MC Serch's "Back to the Grill" before signing with Columbia Records. Illmatic, produced by DJ Premier, Large Professor, Pete Rock, Q-Tip and L.E.S., contained only ten tracks, including "N.Y. State of Mind" and "The World Is Yours"; though modest commercially on release, it was hailed by critics and later canonised as a landmark of East Coast hip hop.

Commercial success and feud with Jay-Z

His second album, It Was Written (1996), debuted at number one on the Billboard 200 and produced the hit "If I Ruled the World (Imagine That)" with Lauryn Hill. Subsequent albums included I Am… and Nastradamus (both 1999). In 2001 Nas released Stillmatic, widely seen as a return to form, amid a celebrated feud with Jay-Z that produced the diss tracks "Ether" and "Takeover" and is remembered as one of hip hop's defining rivalries; the two reconciled on stage in 2005.

Later career

Nas's later work includes God's Son (2002), Hip Hop Is Dead (2006), the untitled 2008 album, Life Is Good (2012) and Nasir (2018). Beginning in 2020 he released a trilogy of albums with producer Hit-Boy — King's Disease, King's Disease II and King's Disease III — and two Magic albums. King's Disease won the Grammy Award for Best Rap Album, his first Grammy from fourteen nominations. Outside music, Nas co-founded the venture firm QueensBridge Venture Partners, an early investor in companies such as Ring and PillPack, and has been involved in record labels, publishing and film.
