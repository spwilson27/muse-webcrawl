British Empire

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

[Image: A world map with the territories of the British Empire shaded pink/red]

The British Empire comprised the colonies, dominions, protectorates and territories ruled or administered by the United Kingdom from the late 16th to the 20th century. At its height in the early 20th century it was the largest empire in history, ruling about a quarter of the world's land area and population — 'the empire on which the sun never sets'.

Contents1. Rise and fall

Rise and fall

Beginning with overseas colonies and trading companies such as the East India Company, the empire expanded through trade, war and settlement: India, Canada, Australia, large parts of Africa and Asia. After World War II, decolonisation rapidly dissolved it (India 1947, African independence around 1960, Hong Kong handover 1997), leaving the Commonwealth of Nations and 14 British Overseas Territories.
