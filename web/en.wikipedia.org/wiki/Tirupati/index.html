Tirupati

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Tirupati is a city in the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh, at the foot of the Seshachalam Hills. It is one of the most important pilgrimage centres in Hinduism, serving as the gateway to the hill shrine of Tirumala, the Sri Venkateswara Temple dedicated to the deity Venkateswara, a form of Vishnu.

The temple

The Venkateswara Temple atop the seven hills of Tirumala is among the most visited religious sites in the world, drawing tens of millions of pilgrims annually, and among the wealthiest temples, receiving vast donations in cash, gold and hair offerings from devotees who have their heads tonsured. The temple is administered by the Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams (TTD), which also runs hospitals, schools and the famous distribution of the Tirupati laddu, a consecrated sweet granted geographical-indication status. The annual Brahmotsavam festival attracts hundreds of thousands of visitors.

The city

Tirupati city below the hills has a population of several hundred thousand and serves as a regional transport, education and healthcare hub, with institutions such as Sri Venkateswara University. It has its own airport and major railway station, and other notable temples nearby include those at Srikalahasti, Kanipakam and Tiruchanur. The nearby Sri Venkateswara National Park covers part of the forested hills.
