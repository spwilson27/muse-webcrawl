List of cities by population

The United Nations uses three definitions for what constitutes a city, which are inconsistently applied around the world: the population of the city proper, the urban area, and the metropolitan area. The table below uses the urban area (contiguous built-up area) concept, which produces comparable figures across countries. By this measure, Tokyo is the largest city in the world, followed by Delhi and Shanghai.

Largest cities proper (administrative boundaries)

By the population within administrative city limits, the largest municipalities include Chongqing in China (whose municipality covers a largely rural area of over 30 million people), Shanghai (about 29 million), Beijing (about 21 million), Chengdu, Tianjin, Guangzhou, and Istanbul. Because municipal boundaries vary enormously in the area they cover, city-proper figures are generally considered less comparable than urban-area figures.
