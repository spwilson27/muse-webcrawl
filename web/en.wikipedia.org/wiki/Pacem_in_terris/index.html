Pacem in terris
Author: Pope John XXIII
Date: 11 April 1963
Type: Papal encyclical — "Peace on Earth"

Pacem in terris ("Peace on Earth") is a papal encyclical by Pope John XXIII, issued on 11 April 1963, weeks before his death, amid Cold War tensions after the Cuban Missile Crisis.

Content

Unusually addressed to "all men of good will", not only Catholics, it grounds peace in truth, justice, charity, and freedom; catalogues human rights (life, worship, emigration, political participation) and corresponding duties; calls for disarmament, an end to the arms race, and a strengthened United Nations and world public authority.

Reception

It was acclaimed worldwide, influencing Catholic social teaching, Vatican II's Dignitatis humanae, and human-rights discourse, and remains a landmark of modern papal diplomacy.
