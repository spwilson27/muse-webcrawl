S.L. Benfica

Sport Lisboa e Benfica (commonly known as Benfica) is a Portuguese professional football club based in Lisbon. Founded on 28 February 1904 as Sport Lisboa, it merged with Grupo Sport Benfica in 1908. Benfica is one of the "Big Three" clubs in Portugal that have never been relegated from the Primeira Liga, alongside rivals Sporting CP and FC Porto, and is the most successful club in Portuguese football by trophies won.

History

Benfica was founded by a group of young men including Cosme Damião, the club's driving force for its first decades. The club grew into Portugal's dominant side in the 1960s under Hungarian coach Béla Guttmann, winning the European Cup in 1961 (beating Barcelona) and 1962 (beating Real Madrid) with a side led by Eusébio, the Mozambican-born striker regarded as one of the greatest players of all time. After the 1962 final, Guttmann, denied a bonus, supposedly cursed the club never to win another European trophy for 100 years; Benfica have since lost numerous European finals, fuelling the legend of the "Guttmann curse".

The original Estádio da Luz opened in 1954 and was once the largest stadium in Europe. Its replacement, built for UEFA Euro 2004, hosted the 2014 and 2020 Champions League finals (the latter as part of the COVID-rescheduled tournament).

Honours

Benfica hold the record for Portuguese league championships (38 Primeira Liga titles, as of the 2023 title), and have won the Taça de Portugal a record 26 times, the Taça da Liga eight times, and the European Cup twice (1961, 1962). The club was ranked among Europe's elite historically, being named by FIFA among the great clubs of the 20th century.

Support and other sports

Benfica has one of the largest paying memberships of any sports club in the world—its member (sócio) count, over 250,000, set a Guinness World Record in 2006. The club's eagle mascot, Vitória, flies around the stadium before home matches. As a multi-sport club, Benfica also fields notable teams in basketball, handball, volleyball, futsal, roller hockey and athletics, and its women's football team has become dominant in Portugal since its formation in 2018.
