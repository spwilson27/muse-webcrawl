Santa Catarina

Country: Brazil
Capital: Florianópolis
Largest city: Joinville
Area: about 95,700 km²
Population: about 7.6 million (2022)

Santa Catarina (Portuguese pronunciation: [ˈsɐ̃tɐ kataˈɾinɐ]) is one of the 27 federative units of Brazil. It is located in the centre of the country's Southern region. It is bordered to the north by the state of Paraná, to the south by the state of Rio Grande do Sul, to the east by the Atlantic Ocean, and to the west by the Argentine province of Misiones.

Geography

The state capital is Florianópolis, largely situated on Santa Catarina Island. The state has a varied landscape of Atlantic beaches, islands, and bays along the coast, rising to high plateaus and the Serra do Mar and Serra Geral ranges inland, where frosts and occasional snow occur in winter at high elevations, as at São Joaquim.

Demographics and culture

Santa Catarina received heavy European immigration in the 19th century, especially Germans, Italians, and Azorean Portuguese, whose influence remains visible in towns such as Blumenau — famous for one of the world's largest Oktoberfest celebrations — Pomerode, and Joinville. The state consistently ranks among Brazil's highest in life expectancy, literacy, and human development indices.

Economy

The economy is diversified, with industry (textiles, machinery, appliances), agriculture and agro-industry (pork and poultry processing), fishing, and tourism centred on beach resorts such as Balneário Camboriú and Florianópolis.
