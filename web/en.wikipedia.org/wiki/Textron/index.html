Textron

Textron Inc. is an American industrial conglomerate based in Providence, Rhode Island. Its subsidiaries include Bell Textron (helicopters), Textron Aviation (Cessna and Beechcraft aircraft), Arctic Cat, E-Z-GO and Jacobsen. It was a pioneer of the modern multi-industry conglomerate form.

History

Royal Little founded the company in 1923 in the textile business — the name Textron combines "textiles" and "Lustron", a synthetic fibre. After wartime parachute production, Little diversified aggressively in the 1950s into unrelated industries, making Textron a model conglomerate studied in business schools. It later shed many non-core lines to focus on aircraft, helicopters, defense systems, finance and industrial products.

Business segments

Textron Aviation produces Cessna and Beechcraft piston, turboprop and Citation business jets. Bell produces commercial and military helicopters, including the V-22 Osprey tiltrotor in partnership with Boeing. Textron Systems supplies unmanned systems and armored vehicles, and the industrial segment makes E-Z-GO golf cars and specialised vehicles.
