John Wright (January 1568 – 8 November 1605) was a member of the group of English Catholics who planned the failed Gunpowder Plot of 1605, an attempt to assassinate King James I and blow up the House of Lords during the State Opening of Parliament.

Involvement in the plot

Wright and his younger brother Christopher were among the earliest recruits to the conspiracy led by Robert Catesby. A skilled swordsman educated at St Peter's School in York, Wright was considered one of the most devoted of the plotters. He attended school with Guy Fawkes.

Death

After the plot's discovery, Wright fled London with the other conspirators. He was killed on 8 November 1605 in the shootout with the Sheriff of Worcestershire's men at Holbeche House in Staffordshire, alongside Catesby and Thomas Percy.
