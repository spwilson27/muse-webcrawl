Drew Barrymore

Born 22 February 1975, Culver City, California, U.S.
Occupations Actress, producer, talk-show host, businesswoman
Years active 1978–present

Drew Blythe Barrymore (born February 22, 1975) is an American actress, producer and talk-show host. A member of the Barrymore family of actors, she achieved fame as a child actress in Steven Spielberg's E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial (1982) and went on to a long career in film and television.

Career

After a highly publicised troubled adolescence, Barrymore returned to prominence in the 1990s with films including Poison Ivy, Scream, Ever After and The Wedding Singer. She starred in and produced the Charlie's Angels films through her company Flower Films, and won acclaim — including a Golden Globe — for portraying Edith Bouvier Beale in HBO's Grey Gardens (2009). She later starred in the Netflix series Santa Clarita Diet and, from 2020, hosted the syndicated daytime talk programme The Drew Barrymore Show. Her memoir Little Girl Lost was published when she was 14.
