Lists of rivers

This article is an index of lists of rivers on Wikipedia, organised by continent, country, and other criteria. A river is a natural flowing watercourse, usually freshwater, flowing towards an ocean, sea, lake, or another river. The longest river systems in the world include the Nile (~6,650 km), the Amazon (~6,400 km, but the largest by discharge), and the Yangtze (~6,300 km).
