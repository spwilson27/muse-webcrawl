Comparative army enlisted ranks

Enlisted rank insignia vary widely between armies, though NATO codes allow rough equivalence.

This article presents a comparison of enlisted ranks used by the world's armies. Enlisted personnel — soldiers below the rank of commissioned officer, including non-commissioned officers (NCOs) — form the great majority of every army. Because rank titles and structures differ greatly between countries and languages, comparisons are approximate; within NATO, the STANAG 2116 rank codes (OR-1 to OR-9, "Other Ranks") provide a common framework for equivalence used in joint operations.

Structure

Typical enlisted hierarchies progress from junior ranks — private (OR-1/OR-2), lance corporal or private first class (OR-3) — through corporals (OR-4) and sergeants (OR-5/OR-6) to senior NCOs such as staff sergeants, sergeants first class, first sergeants, and warrant-grade ranks such as sergeant major (OR-9). Some armies insert distinct warrant officer grades; others, particularly those modelled on the Soviet system, historically used a flatter structure with ranks such as yefreytor, junior and senior sergeants, and starshina.
