National security

National security, or national defence, is the security and defence of a sovereign state, including its citizens, economy, and institutions, regarded as a duty of government. Originally focused on military defence against external attack, the concept has broadened to include economic security, energy security, environmental security, food security, cyber security and protection against terrorism and espionage.

Measures

Measures include armed forces, intelligence agencies, diplomacy, emergency management, resilience of critical infrastructure, and economic instruments such as strategic reserves and trade controls. Debates centre on the balance between security and civil liberties, proportionality, and international cooperation through alliances such as NATO.
