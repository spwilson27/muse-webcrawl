Platform supply vessel

A platform supply vessel alongside an offshore installation.

A platform supply vessel (PSV), a type of offshore support vessel, is a ship specially designed to supply offshore oil and gas platforms and other offshore installations.

Role

PSVs transport goods, personnel and fluids — including drilling mud, cement, fuel and water — between shore bases and offshore structures. They range from about 65 to 350 feet in length and feature large clear deck areas, below-deck tanks and dynamic positioning systems that hold the vessel stationary beside a platform in rough seas.

Other support vessels

Related types include anchor handling tug supply vessels (AHTS), construction support vessels, and standby/rescue vessels, which together service the offshore energy industry.
