ESATA

ESATA may refer to:

Computing

  • eSATA — External Serial ATA, the external variant of the Serial ATA computer bus interface, standardized in 2004 for connecting external storage devices such as hard disk drives and optical drives to a computer, using a shielded cable and a modified connector distinct from the internal SATA connector. Related variants include eSATAp (Power over eSATA), which combines eSATA, USB and power in a single port.
