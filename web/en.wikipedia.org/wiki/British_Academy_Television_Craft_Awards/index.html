British Academy Television Craft Awards

British Academy Television Craft Awards
Presented by: BAFTA
First awarded: 2000 (as a separate ceremony)
Country: United Kingdom

The British Academy Television Craft Awards are accolades presented by the British Academy of Film and Television Arts (BAFTA) honouring the behind-the-scenes craft and technical achievements of British television, in categories such as directing, writing, photography, editing, production design, costume and sound.

History

The craft categories were originally presented within the main British Academy Television Awards. In 2000 BAFTA established a separate ceremony to give fuller recognition to production crafts. The awards include categories for breakthrough talent and a special award for outstanding creative contribution.

Categories

Awards cover director (fiction, factual, multi-camera), writer (drama, comedy), editing, photography and lighting, original music, sound, titles and graphic identity, and emerging talent, among others.
