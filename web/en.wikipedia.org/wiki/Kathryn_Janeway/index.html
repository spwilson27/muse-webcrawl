Kathryn Janeway
First appearance: "Caretaker" (1995)
Portrayed by: Kate Mulgrew
Affiliation: Starfleet, United Federation of Planets
Rank: Captain (later Admiral)

Kathryn Janeway is a fictional character in the Star Trek franchise. She is the captain of the starship USS Voyager in the television series Star Trek: Voyager (1995–2001), played by Kate Mulgrew — the first woman to serve as the regular lead captain of a Star Trek series. Janeway later appears as an admiral in the film Star Trek: Nemesis (2002) and as a hologram and admiral in the animated series Star Trek: Prodigy.

Character

Born in Bloomington, Indiana, Janeway trained as a science officer before moving to command. In the series premiere, her ship is stranded in the Delta Quadrant, some 70,000 light-years from Earth, and she unifies her Starfleet crew with a group of Maquis rebels for the seven-year journey home. Janeway is portrayed as a decisive, scientifically minded commander with a love of coffee, holonovels and 19th-century literature, who frequently wrestles with how far Starfleet principles can bend in isolation.

Reception

The character has been praised as a landmark for women in science fiction television and frequently ranks among the most popular Star Trek captains. Mulgrew reprised the role in voice for Prodigy (2021–2024).
