List of largest shopping malls

This article lists the largest shopping malls in the world by gross leasable area (GLA). Asia dominates the top of the list, with many of the biggest centres built since 2000 in China, Malaysia, Thailand and the Philippines.

History

West Edmonton Mall held the title of world's largest mall from 1981 until 2004. The South China Mall, despite its size, struggled for years with very low occupancy after opening and was nicknamed a "dead mall" before later redevelopment improved tenancy.
