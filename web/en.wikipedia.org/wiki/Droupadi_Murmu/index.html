Droupadi Murmu

Droupadi Murmu
15th President of India (2022– )
Born: 20 June 1958, Uparbeda, Odisha
Party: Bharatiya Janata Party
Previous office: Governor of Jharkhand (2015–2021)

Droupadi Murmu (born 1958) is an Indian politician serving as the 15th and current President of India since 25 July 2022. She is the first person from a tribal community (the Santhal people) and the second woman, after Pratibha Patil, to hold the office, and the youngest to become president.

Early life and career

Born in Mayurbhanj district, Odisha, Murmu worked as a school teacher before entering politics, serving as a councillor, minister in the Odisha government (2000–2009), and MLA for Rairangpur.

Governorship and presidency

As Governor of Jharkhand (2015–2021) she was the state's first woman governor. Nominated by the ruling NDA coalition in 2022, she defeated Yashwant Sinha in the presidential election and took office that July.
