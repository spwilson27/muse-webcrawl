Thales Nederland


A naval radar built by Thales Nederland in Hengelo

Thales Nederland B.V. is a subsidiary of the French Thales Group based in the Netherlands. The company is principally involved in defence electronics, such as naval command and control systems, fire-control systems, sensors, and telecommunications equipment. Its headquarters are in Hengelo, Overijssel.

History

The company was founded in 1922 as NV Hazemeyer's Fabriek van Signaalapparaten (Hazemeyer Signal Apparatus Factory) in Hengelo, established to supply fire-control equipment to the Royal Netherlands Navy. Hazemeyer grew into Hollandse Signaalapparaten (Signaal), becoming one of the Netherlands' leading defence electronics firms, particularly noted for its naval radar and fire-control systems.

In 1956 the company was acquired by Philips, under which it operated as Hollandse Signaalapparaten B.V. In 1990, Philips sold the bulk of its defence activities to the French Thomson-CSF, which later became the Thales Group, and the company was renamed Thales Nederland.

Products

Thales Nederland is especially known for naval sensors and combat management systems, including the APAR multifunction radar, the SMART-L long-range air and surface surveillance radar, and the TACTICOS combat management system. Its equipment is fitted to warships of many navies worldwide, including the Royal Netherlands Navy's De Zeven Provinciën-class frigates.

Operations

The company employs roughly 2,000 people in the Netherlands, with principal sites in Hengelo, Huizen, and Delft. It exports the majority of its production and works closely with the Dutch "golden triangle" of government, knowledge institutes, and industry.
