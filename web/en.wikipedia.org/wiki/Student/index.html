Student

Student
DefinitionA person enrolled in an educational institution
LevelsPrimary, secondary, undergraduate, postgraduate
EtymologyLatin studere, "to devote oneself to"

A student is a person enrolled in a school or other educational institution. In the United Kingdom and most commonwealth countries, a "student" attends a secondary school or higher education institution, while pupils in primary school are usually called "pupils." In the United States, the term "student" is used for learners of all levels.

By level of education

Students progress through primary and secondary schooling to tertiary education. Undergraduates work toward a first degree, such as a bachelor's; postgraduates (graduate students in North America) pursue master's degrees and doctorates. Students may study full-time or part-time, in person or through distance and online learning, and adult learners returning to education are often called mature students.

Student life and politics

Students have a distinctive culture, including student unions, societies, sports, and traditions such as graduation ceremonies and, in some countries, student nightlife districts. Students have also been a significant political force: movements led or powered by students include the May 1968 protests in France, the anti-apartheid and anti–Vietnam War movements, and numerous pro-democracy movements. International students — those studying abroad — number in the millions and contribute significantly to host economies; the Erasmus programme facilitates exchange within Europe.
