Toray Industries

Toray Industries, Inc. (東レ株式会社) is a Japanese multinational corporation headquartered in Tokyo, specialising in advanced materials: synthetic fibres and textiles, performance chemicals, carbon-fibre composites, pharmaceuticals and water-treatment membranes. Founded in 1926 as Toyo Rayon, it is one of the world's largest producers of carbon fibre.

History and products

The company began with rayon production backed by Mitsui capital and technology licensed from Britain. After the Second World War it diversified into nylon (with DuPont technology), polyester film, and, from 1971, PAN-based carbon fibre under the Torayca brand. Torayca prepreg is the primary structural material of the Boeing 787 Dreamliner's airframe, Toray's largest single aerospace customer relationship. The company's Ultrasuede microfibre, Lumirror films and Romembra reverse-osmosis membranes are market leaders in their niches.

Scale

Toray operates in more than 25 countries with tens of thousands of employees and annual revenues on the order of ¥2.4 trillion. It sponsors the Pan Pacific Open tennis tournament and supplies materials for everything from Uniqlo clothing lines to desalination plants.
