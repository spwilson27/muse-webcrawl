Latakia Governorate

Latakia Governorate

Country: Syria
Capital: Latakia
Area: ~2,300 km²
Population: ~1.0–1.3 million (estimates vary)
Districts: Latakia, Jableh, Qardaha, Al-Haffah

Latakia Governorate (Arabic: محافظة اللاذقية) is one of the fourteen governorates of Syria, situated in the north-west of the country on the Mediterranean coast. It is the smallest governorate by area but one of the most densely populated. Its capital and largest city is Latakia, Syria's principal seaport, and other significant towns include Jableh, Qardaha, and Al-Haffah.

Geography

The governorate stretches from the Turkish border in the north to Tartus Governorate in the south, and eastward to Idlib and Hama governorates. A narrow coastal plain gives way to the Syrian Coastal Mountain Range (the Jabal an Nusayriyah), which reaches about 1,500 metres and catches heavy rainfall, making this the wettest and greenest part of Syria, with citrus groves, tobacco fields, olives, and forests. North of Latakia lies the famous archaeological site of Ugarit (Ras Shamra), where an early alphabet was discovered.

Demographics and history

The coastal mountains have for centuries been the heartland of Syria's Alawite community, and the governorate has a mixed population of Alawites, Sunni Muslims, and Christians. The ruling Assad family originated from Qardaha in the mountains east of Latakia. During the Syrian civil war the governorate was a government stronghold and comparatively less fought over than much of the country, though its northern countryside saw fighting and it received many displaced people; Russia established its Khmeimim air base south of the city in 2015. After the fall of the Assad regime in December 2024, the coast saw sectarian violence in March 2025 in which many Alawite civilians were killed.

Economy

The economy rests on the port of Latakia, agriculture (citrus fruit, tobacco, olives, apples), food processing, tourism to the beaches and mountain resorts such as Slunfeh and Kessab, and remittances. The Tishrin Dam and university — Tishrin University in Latakia city — are important institutions.
