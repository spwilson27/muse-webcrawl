Remington Arms Company, LLC was an American manufacturer of firearms and ammunition. Founded in 1816 by Eliphalet Remington in Ilion, New York, it was one of the oldest gun makers in the United States and, for much of its history, one of the largest.

History

The company began when Eliphalet Remington forged his first rifle barrel and grew through government contracts in the American Civil War and both World Wars. Well-known products include the Model 700 bolt-action rifle, the 870 pump-action shotgun, and the 1100 semi-automatic shotgun.

Bankruptcy and breakup

Burdened by debt and litigation — including lawsuits arising from the Sandy Hook Elementary School shooting, in which a Remington-made rifle was used — the company filed for bankruptcy in 2018 and again in 2020. Its assets were sold off in pieces: the ammunition business went to Vista Outdoor, and the firearms business at Ilion was acquired by a new company, RemArms.
