Timeline of the evolutionary history of life

This timeline of the evolutionary history of life outlines the major events in the development of life on Earth, from abiogenesis about 4 billion years ago to the present.

Hadean and Archaean

Time (Ga)Event
c. 4.0Earliest evidence of life; self-replicating molecules, LUCA
c. 3.5Stromatolites; photosynthetic cyanobacteria
c. 2.4Great Oxidation Event

Proterozoic

Eukaryotic cells arise through endosymbiosis (mitochondria, later chloroplasts) by c. 2.1 Ga; multicellular life and sexual reproduction evolve; the Ediacaran biota (635–541 Ma) are the first large complex organisms, following Snowball Earth glaciations.

Phanerozoic

The Cambrian explosion (c. 539 Ma) produced most animal phyla; life colonised land in the Ordovician–Devonian; forests, amphibians, reptiles, dinosaurs, mammals, birds and flowering plants followed. Five mass extinctions punctuate the record, most famously the end-Cretaceous event 66 Ma that cleared the way for mammalian—and ultimately human—dominance.
