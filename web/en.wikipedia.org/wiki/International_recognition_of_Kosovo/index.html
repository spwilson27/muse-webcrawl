International recognition of Kosovo

Recognition of Kosovo
Declaration of independence: 17 February 2008
UN member states recognising: about 100
Status: Partially recognised

Kosovo declared independence from Serbia on 17 February 2008. Its international recognition has been extensive but incomplete: around one hundred United Nations member states, including the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany and France, recognise Kosovo as an independent state, while Serbia, Russia, China and five European Union members, among others, do not.

Background

After the Kosovo War of 1998–99 the province was administered by the United Nations under Security Council Resolution 1244 while remaining formally part of Serbia. Negotiations on final status, led by Martti Ahtisaari, proposed supervised independence, which Serbia rejected. Kosovo's assembly then declared independence unilaterally.

Legal proceedings

In 2010 the International Court of Justice advised, in response to a question from the UN General Assembly, that Kosovo's declaration of independence did not violate international law. Serbia and Kosovo later entered an EU-facilitated dialogue, reaching the Brussels Agreement of 2013 on normalisation, though Kosovo's membership of the United Nations remains blocked.
