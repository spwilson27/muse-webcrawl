Ramón María Narváez

Ramón María Narváez y Campos, 1st Duke of Valencia (5 August 1799 – 23 April 1868), was a Spanish general and statesman who served repeatedly as Prime Minister of Spain during the reign of Isabella II. He was the dominant figure of the Moderate Party (Partido Moderado).

Career

Narváez made his name fighting the Carlists in the First Carlist War. After helping to overthrow the regent Espartero in 1843, he became prime minister in 1844 and oversaw the conservative Constitution of 1845, the creation of the Civil Guard, and centralising reforms of taxation and education. His governments alternated in power with Progressive administrations, and he returned to office several times, including in 1856–57, 1864–65, and 1866–68, governing with an increasingly authoritarian hand.

Known as El Espadón de Loja ("the Big Sword of Loja"), Narváez died in office in April 1868, only months before the Glorious Revolution deposed Isabella II.
