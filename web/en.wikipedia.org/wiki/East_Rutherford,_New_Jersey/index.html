East Rutherford is a borough in Bergen County, New Jersey, United States. As of the 2020 census its population was about 10,000. Although a small residential community, East Rutherford is nationally known as the home of the Meadowlands Sports Complex, including MetLife Stadium, the Meadowlands Racetrack and the American Dream shopping and entertainment complex.

History

The area was originally part of Bergen Township and then Union Township. The borough was formed as Boiling Springs Township in 1889 and renamed East Rutherford on March 23, 1894 (the "East" distinguishing it from Rutherford borough nearby). Its character changed dramatically with the development of the New Jersey Meadowlands: the Meadowlands Sports Complex opened in 1976 with the racetrack and Giants Stadium, bringing the New York Giants and later the New York Jets to the borough.

Sports and entertainment

MetLife Stadium, opened in 2010, is the home of both the New York Giants and the New York Jets of the NFL — one of only two stadiums shared by two NFL teams — and hosted Super Bowl XLVIII in 2014 and the 2014 and later matches of major international soccer tournaments, and is scheduled to host matches of the 2026 FIFA World Cup, including the final. The Meadowlands Racetrack hosts the Hambletonian, harness racing's premier event. The adjacent American Dream complex, which opened in stages beginning in 2019 after a famously long development history, includes an indoor theme park, water park and ski slope.

Geography and transport

The borough covers about 4 square miles in the Meadowlands along the Hackensack River, close to New York City. It is served by New Jersey Transit's Rutherford station (just over the border in Rutherford) and the Meadowlands station for events, and by Routes 3, 17 and 120.
