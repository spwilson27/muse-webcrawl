Jeremy Paxman

Jeremy Dickson Paxman (born 11 May 1950) is an English retired journalist, author, and television presenter. He is best known for presenting the BBC Two news programme Newsnight from 1989 to 2014 and the quiz show University Challenge from 1994 to 2023.

Early life and education

Paxman was born in Leeds and educated at Malvern College and St Catharine's College, Cambridge, where he read English and edited the student newspaper Varsity.

Career

He joined the BBC in 1972, reporting from Northern Ireland during the Troubles and working as a foreign correspondent before becoming a presenter of Panorama and the Six O'Clock News. On Newsnight he became famous for his combative interviewing style; in a celebrated 1997 interview he asked Home Secretary Michael Howard the same question twelve times. As host of University Challenge he became the longest-serving quizmaster in the show's history.

Books and later life

Paxman's books include The English (1998), The Political Animal (2002), On Royals (2006), Empire (2011), and Great Britain's Great War (2013). In 2021 he revealed he had been diagnosed with Parkinson's disease, and he presented the documentary Paxman: Putting Up with Parkinson's in 2022.
