List of X-planes

The X-planes are a series of experimental United States aircraft and rockets used to test and evaluate new technologies and aerodynamic concepts. Designations in the X-series are assigned by the U.S. Department of Defense and have run from the X-1 of 1946 to projects numbered into the X-60s. Some numbers were skipped, and a few designations were later applied to spacecraft or missiles.

The Bell X-1 broke the sound barrier on October 14, 1947, piloted by Chuck Yeager.

History

The series began with the Bell X-1, developed by the U.S. Air Force and the National Advisory Committee for Aeronautics (NACA), NASA's predecessor. X-planes have been operated by NASA, the Air Force, the Navy, and DARPA, and tested at facilities including Edwards Air Force Base and NASA's Armstrong Flight Research Center.
