List of bank failures in the United States (2008–present)

This is a list of bank failures in the United States from 2008 to the present, covering insured commercial banks closed by regulators and placed into Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC) receivership. The wave began with the 2007–2008 financial crisis, when failures rose from just 3 in 2007 to 25 in 2008, 140 in 2009, and a peak of 157 in 2010.

2008

The largest bank failure in American history occurred on September 25, 2008, when Washington Mutual of Seattle, with about $307 billion in assets, was seized and sold to JPMorgan Chase. IndyMac Bank of Pasadena, California, failed earlier that summer, and 25 banks failed in total during 2008.

2009–2013

Failures spread through community and regional banks burdened by commercial real estate losses, concentrated in Georgia, Florida, Illinois, and California. Colonial Bank (Alabama, 2009) and BankUnited (Florida, 2009) were among the largest. The count declined steadily: 92 in 2011, 51 in 2012, and 24 in 2013.

2014–2022

Failure numbers fell to single digits most years, and some years passed with no failures at all, reflecting post-crisis capital strengthening and supervision.

2023

The failures of Silicon Valley Bank (March 10, 2023) and Signature Bank (March 12, 2023), followed by First Republic Bank (May 1, 2023), were the second-, third-, and fourth-largest in U.S. history, prompting emergency measures including protection of all depositors at the two March banks and sales arranged by the FDIC.
