Tangled

Tangled is a 2010 American animated musical fantasy film produced by Walt Disney Animation Studios and released by Walt Disney Pictures. Loosely based on the German fairy tale "Rapunzel" by the Brothers Grimm, it is the 50th Disney animated feature film. Directed by Nathan Greno and Byron Howard, it features the voices of Mandy Moore, Zachary Levi, and Donna Murphy, with songs by Alan Menken and Glenn Slater.

Plot

A drop of sunlight falls to Earth and grows into a magical golden flower with healing powers, guarded by the vain Mother Gothel. When the queen of Corona falls ill in childbirth, the flower is used to heal her, and her daughter Rapunzel is born with glowing golden hair. Gothel kidnaps the infant and raises her in a hidden tower, using her hair to stay young. Each year the king and queen release floating lanterns on Rapunzel's birthday. At eighteen, Rapunzel persuades the wanted thief Flynn Rider to take her to see the lanterns; adventure, romance, and the truth of her identity follow.

Production

The film spent six years in production and cost an estimated $260 million, making it one of the most expensive animated films ever made. Animators developed new software to render Rapunzel's 70 feet of hair, and the film blended computer animation with a painterly look inspired by classical oil painting.

Reception and legacy

Tangled received positive reviews and was a commercial success. Its song "I See the Light" was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Original Song. A sequel short, Tangled Ever After (2012), and the television series Rapunzel's Tangled Adventure (2017–2020) followed.
