Žydrūnas Savickas (born 15 July 1975) is a Lithuanian former professional strongman and powerlifter. Widely considered the greatest strongman of all time, he is the only athlete to have won every major strongman competition of the modern era.

Career

Savickas began competing in strength sports as a teenager and won Lithuania's strongest man contest repeatedly from the late 1990s. He won the Arnold Strongman Classic eight times (2003–2008, 2014, 2016), a record for the event, and claimed the World's Strongest Man title four times: 2009, 2010, 2012, and 2014.

He also won the IFSA Strongman World Championships twice, the Strongman Champions League overall title, and numerous Giants Live and Fortissimus events. After injuries in the mid-2000s he returned to dominate the sport for another decade.

Records

Savickas set multiple world records in the log lift, raising the mark repeatedly to over 220 kg, and held records in events including the axle press, the hummer tyre deadlift, and the giant barbell squat. In powerlifting he totalled over 1,100 kg in competition early in his career.

Personal life

Savickas was born in Biržai, Lithuania. He later served as a city councillor in Vilnius. He is known by the nickname "Big Z".
