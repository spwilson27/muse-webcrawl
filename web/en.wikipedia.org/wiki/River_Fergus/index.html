River Fergus

The River Fergus (Irish: An Forghas) is a river in County Clare, Ireland. It rises in the Burren lowlands near Lough Fergus and flows roughly 60 km south through Corofin and Ennis — the county town — before entering the Shannon Estuary near Clarecastle.

Features

The Fergus drains a limestone catchment dotted with turloughs (seasonal lakes) and is known for brown trout fishing. In Ennis the river passes through the town centre beneath several bridges and beside the site of Ennis Friary; the lower, tidal reach below Clarecastle was historically navigable and supported the port trade of the town. Islands in the estuary include Inishmore and the Fergus estuary mudflats, an important habitat for wintering wildfowl and waders within the Shannon Estuary SPA.

Flooding of Ennis by the Fergus, as in November 2009, led to a major flood relief scheme for the town completed in the 2010s and 2020s.
