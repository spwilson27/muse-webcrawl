Equestrian at the 2008 Summer Olympics

Equestrian competitions at the 2008 Summer Olympics in Beijing were held from 9 to 21 August at the Hong Kong Sports Institute and Olympic Equestrian Venue — the first time since 1956 that the equestrian events of an Olympic Games were held in a different jurisdiction from the host city, because Hong Kong offered established equine disease-free status and quarantine facilities. Six events were contested: dressage, eventing and jumping, each with individual and team competitions.

Notes

Germany was the most successful nation, winning both dressage team and eventing titles. Anky van Grunsven's individual dressage gold was her third consecutive individual title. Several horses tested positive for prohibited substances at the Games, including cases involving capsaicin, which led to the disqualification of riders in the jumping competition and, after appeals, the reallocation of the team jumping bronze medal to Norway.
