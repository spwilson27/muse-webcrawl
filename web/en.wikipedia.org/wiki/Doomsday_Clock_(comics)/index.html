Doomsday Clock (comics)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Doomsday Clock is a twelve-issue American comic book limited series published by DC Comics from 2017 to 2019, written by Geoff Johns with art by Gary Frank and colour by Brad Anderson. It is a sequel to Watchmen (1986–87) by Alan Moore and Dave Gibbons, bringing that series' characters — notably Doctor Manhattan and Rorschach — into the main DC Universe to confront Superman.

Plot

Seven years after the events of Watchmen, as the world teeters on the edge of nuclear war following the exposure of Ozymandias's hoax, a dying Ozymandias recruits a new Rorschach and other fugitives to find Doctor Manhattan, who has fled to the DC Universe. There, Manhattan's experiments on its timeline explain the alterations of the New 52 era; the story culminates in his confrontation with Superman and a restoration of hope and history to the DC Universe, tying together threads from DC Rebirth.

Title and themes

The title refers to the Doomsday Clock of the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists, echoed in Watchmen's recurring clock imagery. Johns described the story as being about hope versus cynicism in superhero fiction. The series received generally positive reviews but was noted for repeated scheduling delays.
