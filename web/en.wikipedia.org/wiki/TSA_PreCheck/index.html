TSA PreCheck

TSA PreCheck (branded as TSA Pre✓) is a Trusted Traveler program initiated in October 2011 and administered by the U.S. Transportation Security Administration that allows selected members to receive expedited screening at more than 200 U.S. airports. In PreCheck lanes, passengers may keep on shoes, belts and light jackets, and leave laptops and compliant liquids in their carry-on bags.

Enrollment

U.S. citizens and lawful permanent residents apply online, then visit an enrollment center for fingerprinting and identity verification, paying a fee of around $78–85 for five years of membership. Approved travellers receive a Known Traveler Number, which is added to airline reservations so that the PreCheck indicator appears on their boarding passes. Members of Global Entry and other trusted traveller programmes receive PreCheck eligibility automatically.

Availability

The program is available to passengers travelling on dozens of participating airlines. TSA reports that the great majority of PreCheck passengers wait less than ten minutes in security lines.
