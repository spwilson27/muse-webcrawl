Mike Mignola
Born: 16 September 1960, Berkeley, California
Known for: Hellboy, B.P.R.D.
Style: Heavy shadows, minimal line work

Michael Mignola (born 16 September 1960) is an American comics artist and writer, best known for creating Hellboy for Dark Horse Comics, along with its expanded universe of spin-offs including B.P.R.D., Abe Sapien, Lobster Johnson and Witchfinder.

Career

Mignola began at Marvel Comics in the 1980s as an inker and then penciller, drawing titles such as Rocket Raccoon, Alpha Flight and the graphic novel Batman: Gotham by Gaslight (1989) for DC. In 1994 he launched Hellboy: Seed of Destruction, written initially with John Byrne, at Dark Horse. His distinctive style — bold silhouettes, deep blacks and spare, sculptural compositions — became highly influential.

Other work

Mignola worked as a production designer on films including Bram Stoker's Dracula and Disney's Atlantis: The Lost Empire, and collaborated closely with Guillermo del Toro on the Hellboy films (2004, 2008). His later projects include Baltimore, Joe Golem and Mr. Higgins Comes Home, often with co-writer Christopher Golden.
