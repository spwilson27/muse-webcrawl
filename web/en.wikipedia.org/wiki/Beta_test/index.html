Beta test

A beta test is a phase of software testing in which a pre-release version of a product is given to a limited group of real users outside the development organisation, so that defects, usability problems and compatibility issues can be found under real-world conditions before general release.

Types

Beta tests may be closed, restricted to an invited group of testers, or open (public beta), available to anyone who wishes to participate. Feedback is collected through bug reports, surveys and automated telemetry. The beta phase traditionally follows alpha testing, which is performed internally or by a small group closely connected to the developers.

Modern usage

With continuous deployment and web-based software, perpetual betas have become common: products such as web services may remain labelled "beta" for long periods while in general use. The term has spread beyond software to hardware and consumer product trials.
