Museo Casa Enzo Ferrari

The Museo Casa Enzo Ferrari (also known as the Museo Enzo Ferrari) is a museum in Modena, Italy, dedicated to the life and work of Enzo Ferrari, the founder of the Ferrari sports car marque. The museum complex comprises two separate buildings: the former house and workshop of Enzo Ferrari's father, and a new exhibition building designed by the architect Jan Kaplický of Future Systems, with its striking yellow aluminium roof in the shape of a car bonnet air intake. The museum opened on 10 March 2012.

Exhibitions

The main exhibition building houses a changing display of Ferrari road and racing cars, alongside multimedia presentations on Enzo Ferrari's life. The restored workshop of Enzo's father, Alfredo Ferrari, contains the Museum of Ferrari Engines (Museo dei Motori Ferrari), displaying engines from the company's history. Together with the Museo Ferrari in nearby Maranello, it forms the twin-centre of Ferrari museums in Emilia-Romagna.

Architecture

The new pavilion covers some 6,000 square metres beneath a low, curved roof punctured by vent-like openings, finished in the yellow of the city of Modena and of Ferrari's badge. The design was the last major project of Jan Kaplický, completed after his death by his practice Future Systems with Shiro Studio.
