Bracket (tournament)

A bracket or tournament bracket is a tree diagram that represents the series of games played during a knockout tournament. Different knockout tournament formats result in different brackets; the simplest and most common is the single-elimination bracket, in which the loser of each match is immediately eliminated and the winners advance through rounds — such as the round of 16, quarter-finals, semi-finals, and final — until a champion is determined.

Seeding

Competitors are usually placed in the bracket according to seeding, arranged so that the strongest entrants cannot meet until the later rounds. In a standard seeded bracket with 2n entrants, the first seed plays the last seed, the second seed plays the second-to-last, and so on. When the number of entrants is not a power of two, some top seeds receive byes into the second round.

Uses

Brackets are ubiquitous in sports playoffs, including the NCAA basketball tournament ("March Madness"), where predicting the full bracket has become a popular pastime. They are also used in esports, chess and other board-game tournaments, game shows, and even non-sporting popularity contests.

Types

Besides single-elimination brackets, there are double-elimination brackets, in which a competitor must lose twice to be eliminated and losers drop into a second bracket, and various repechage formats that give early losers a route back into medal contention.
