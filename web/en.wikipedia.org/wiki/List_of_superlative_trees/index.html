List of superlative trees

The following is a list of superlative trees in the world — trees that hold records for height, volume, age, girth, or other extremes. Measurements of record trees are frequently contested, and ages of clonal organisms are estimated rather than directly counted.

Stoutest

The Árbol del Tule, a Montezuma cypress (Taxodium mucronatum) in Santa María del Tule, Oaxaca, Mexico, has the stoutest trunk of any living tree, with a circumference of about 42 m (137.8 ft). Sacred fig and baobab species also feature among the widest-crowned and largest-canopied trees.
