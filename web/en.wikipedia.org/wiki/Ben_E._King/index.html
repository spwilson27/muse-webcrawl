Ben E. King (born Benjamin Earl Nelson, September 28, 1938 – April 30, 2015) was an American soul and R&B singer and record producer. He rose to fame as one of the principal lead singers of the vocal group the Drifters, singing lead on hits including "There Goes My Baby", "This Magic Moment" and "Save the Last Dance for Me", before a successful solo career crowned by the 1961 classic "Stand by Me", one of the most enduring songs of the 20th century.

Career

Nelson was born in Henderson, North Carolina, and grew up in Harlem, New York, singing in church choirs and doo-wop groups. In 1958 he joined the Five Crowns, who were hired wholesale by manager George Treadwell to become the new Drifters. With the Drifters, Nelson co-wrote and sang lead on "There Goes My Baby" (1959), a pioneering record in its use of strings, and sang on "Save the Last Dance for Me", recorded just before his departure in 1960 after a pay dispute.

As a solo artist for Atco Records, billed as Ben E. King, he released "Spanish Harlem" (1960) and "Stand by Me" (1961), the latter written with Jerry Leiber and Mike Stoller and built on a bass line derived from a gospel standard. "Stand by Me" reached the US top five twice — in 1961 and again in 1986, when it became the theme of the Rob Reiner film of the same name and topped the UK chart in 1987. Other solo hits included "Amor", "Don't Play That Song (You Lied)" and "I (Who Have Nothing)". In 1975 he returned to the upper reaches of the charts with the funk/disco hit "Supernatural Thing".

Legacy

King was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 1988 as a member of the Drifters. "Stand by Me" has been recorded by hundreds of artists, was named one of the Songs of the Century by the Recording Industry Association of America, and in 2015 King's recording was inducted into the Library of Congress's National Recording Registry — weeks before his death from coronary artery disease on 30 April 2015, aged 76. He had remained an active performer and philanthropist, founding the Stand By Me Foundation for youth education.
