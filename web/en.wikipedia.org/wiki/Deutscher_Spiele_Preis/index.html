Deutscher Spiele Preis

The Deutscher Spiele Preis (German Game Prize) is an award for board and card games, voted on by the German games community and presented annually at the Spiel fair in Essen since 1990.

Voting

Unlike the Spiel des Jahres jury award, the Deutscher Spiele Preis is determined by open votes from players, retailers, and journalists, and tends to favour more complex "gamers' games". Early winners include Hoity Toity (1990) and The Settlers of Catan (1995).
