Infosys Limited is an Indian multinational information technology company that provides business consulting, information technology and outsourcing services. Founded in Pune in 1981 by N. R. Narayana Murthy and six co-founders with an initial capital of about US$250, it is headquartered in Bengaluru and is one of India's largest private-sector employers, with a workforce of more than 300,000 people.

History

Infosys grew rapidly in the 1990s on the global delivery model — performing software work in India for clients in North America and Europe — and in 1993 became one of the first Indian companies to list on an Indian stock exchange following economic liberalisation; in 1999 it became the first Indian company listed on the NASDAQ. Its employee stock option programme and emphasis on corporate governance made it a bellwether of the Indian IT industry.

Services and campuses

The company offers application development and maintenance, cloud and digital transformation services, artificial intelligence, engineering services and business process outsourcing. Its training centre in Mysuru is among the largest corporate universities in the world, capable of training many thousands of new recruits at a time. Salil Parekh has served as chief executive officer and managing director since 2018.
