Bruce Timm

Bruce Walter Timm (born February 5, 1961) is an American animator, writer, producer, and character designer. He is best known as co-creator, with Eric Radomski, of Batman: The Animated Series and as a central architect of the DC Animated Universe.

Career

Timm began in animation in the 1980s, working on shows such as He-Man and the Masters of the Universe, The Real Ghostbusters, and Tiny Toon Adventures. His streamlined, angular character designs—rooted in classic comics and Art Deco influences—defined the look of Batman: The Animated Series (1992–1995). He notably redesigned Harley Quinn, a character created for the show with writer Paul Dini.

DC Animated Universe and later work

With Dini and others, Timm went on to produce Superman: The Animated Series, Batman Beyond, Justice League, and Justice League Unlimited. He later produced many DC direct-to-video animated films and served as executive producer on projects including Batman: Caped Crusader. His distinctive style has strongly influenced modern superhero animation and comics art.
