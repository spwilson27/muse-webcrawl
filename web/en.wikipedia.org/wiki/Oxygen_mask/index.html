Oxygen mask

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

An oxygen mask is a medical device used to transfer breathing oxygen gas from a storage tank or supply to the lungs. Oxygen masks may cover only the nose and mouth (oral nasal mask) or the entire face (full-face mask). They may be made of plastic, silicone, or rubber. In certain circumstances, oxygen may be delivered via a nasal cannula instead of a mask.

Medical use

Oxygen therapy is indicated for patients with hypoxemia, respiratory distress, carbon monoxide poisoning and many other conditions. Simple masks deliver oxygen concentrations of about 40–60%; partial rebreather and non-rebreather masks, which include a reservoir bag, can deliver higher concentrations approaching 90% or more. Venturi masks mix oxygen with room air through calibrated ports to deliver precise concentrations.

Aviation and other uses

Aircrew and passengers use oxygen masks at high altitude or in cabin depressurisation; military aviators wear mask-and-helmet assemblies integrated with communications and, in some aircraft, pressure breathing. Firefighters, divers' emergency systems and spacecraft also employ related designs.
