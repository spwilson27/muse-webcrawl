CATL

Full name: Contemporary Amperex Technology Co. Limited
Founded: 2011
Founder: Robin Zeng
Headquarters: Ningde, Fujian, China
Industry: Batteries, energy storage

Contemporary Amperex Technology Co. Limited (CATL) is a Chinese battery manufacturer founded in 2011. It is the world's largest maker of lithium-ion batteries for electric vehicles and energy storage systems, supplying major automakers worldwide.

History

CATL grew out of the battery business of TDK subsidiary ATL. After its 2018 listing on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange, the company expanded rapidly with the global EV market, building plants in China and overseas, including in Germany and Hungary, and holding roughly a third of the global EV battery market.

Technology

The company produces lithium iron phosphate (LFP) and nickel-manganese-cobalt (NMC) cells, and has promoted cell-to-pack designs, the Qilin battery, and fast-charging Shenxing batteries.
