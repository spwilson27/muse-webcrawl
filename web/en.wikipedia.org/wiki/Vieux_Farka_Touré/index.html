Vieux Farka Touré

Vieux Farka Touré (born 1981) is a Malian singer and guitarist, son of the renowned desert-blues musician Ali Farka Touré. Born in Niafunké, he initially played percussion and calabash before taking up guitar despite his father's wish that he avoid the musician's life; Ali relented and contributed to his son's debut.

Career

His self-titled debut album (2006) featured a duet with his father, among Ali's last recordings. Subsequent albums — Fondo (2009), The Secret (2011, produced by Eric Krasno), Mon Pays (2013) and Samba (2017) — blended Songhai tradition with rock, funk and reggae. He performed at the opening concert of the 2010 FIFA World Cup in South Africa and collaborated with Dave Matthews, Derek Trucks and Khruangbin (Ali, 2022, a tribute reworking his father's songs).

Style

Nicknamed "the Hendrix of the Sahara", he is noted for virtuosic, effects-laden guitar work rooted in Malian grooves.
