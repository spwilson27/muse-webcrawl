Scary Movie is a 2000 American comedy slasher-parody film directed by Keenen Ivory Wayans. It parodies numerous horror and teen films, principally Scream and I Know What You Did Last Summer, with additional spoofs of The Sixth Sense, The Blair Witch Project, The Matrix, and others.

Plot

A group of teenagers — including Cindy Campbell and her friends — are stalked by a masked killer a year after they accidentally killed a man and dumped his body. The plot follows Scream's structure through a series of sketches lampooning famous scenes, culminating in a party-house showdown and a twist ending riffing on The Usual Suspects.

Reception and sequels

Made for about $19 million, the film grossed roughly $278 million worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing entry of the franchise and reviving the mainstream parody film. It launched Anna Faris's career and spawned four sequels (2001, 2003, 2006, 2013), though critical reception was mixed, praising the energy of the spoof while noting its reliance on gross-out humour.
