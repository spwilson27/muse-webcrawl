Tennis Channel

Country: United States
Launched: 15 May 2003
Owner: Sinclair Broadcast Group
Headquarters: Santa Monica, California

The Tennis Channel is an American sports-oriented digital cable and satellite television network owned by the Sinclair Broadcast Group. It is devoted to the sport of tennis and related racquet sports, carrying live matches, news, instruction, documentaries and original programming. It was the first single-sport cable channel in the United States devoted to tennis.

History

The network was founded in 2001 by Steve Bellamy and launched on 15 May 2003. Early investors included former players and tennis figures, and the channel built its schedule around tournament rights, classic matches and instructional series. Under chief executive Ken Solomon the network expanded its live-event coverage and distribution.

In 2016 Sinclair Broadcast Group acquired Tennis Channel for about $350 million. Sinclair integrated the network with its other sports properties, and in 2017 it launched Tennis Channel Plus, a direct-to-consumer streaming service carrying thousands of additional live matches not shown on the linear channel.

Programming and rights

Tennis Channel holds American cable rights to the French Open (Roland Garros) — sublicensing finals and other matches to broadcast partners at times — and carries extensive coverage of the ATP Tour and WTA Tour, Davis Cup and Billie Jean King Cup ties, and Laver Cup. Its on-air team has included commentators and analysts such as Mary Carillo, Jim Courier, Martina Navratilova, Lindsay Davenport, Paul Annacone, Tracy Austin and Prakash Amritraj.
