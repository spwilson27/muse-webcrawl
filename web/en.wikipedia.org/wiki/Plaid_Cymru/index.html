Plaid Cymru (Welsh: [plaid ˈkəmri]; officially Plaid Cymru – the Party of Wales) is a Welsh nationalist and social democratic political party in Wales, dedicated to securing independence for Wales from the United Kingdom. It campaigns for the promotion of the Welsh language and culture, and sits in both the Senedd (Welsh Parliament) and the UK Parliament at Westminster.

Plaid Cymru supporters, whose party colour is green, at a rally in Wales.

History

The party was founded in 1925 at the National Eisteddfod in Pwllheli as Plaid Genedlaethol Cymru (the National Party of Wales), largely to defend the Welsh language. Its early leaders included Saunders Lewis, who in 1936 took part in the burning of an RAF bombing school on the Llŷn Peninsula — an act of protest that made the party nationally known. Under Gwynfor Evans, elected president in 1945, the party grew steadily; Evans won the Carmarthen by-election in 1966, becoming Plaid Cymru's first MP, a breakthrough for Welsh nationalism.

The party won two seats in the February 1974 general election and campaigned for devolution. In the 1997 referendum Plaid supported the Yes campaign that created the National Assembly for Wales. In the first Assembly elections of 1999 the party won 17 of 60 seats, becoming the official opposition. After the 2007 election it entered government for the first time, in the "One Wales" coalition with Labour under deputy first minister Ieuan Wyn Jones. Subsequent leaders have included Leanne Wood (2012–2018), Adam Price (2018–2023) and Rhun ap Iorwerth (from 2023). In 2021 Plaid Cymru signed a cooperation agreement with the Labour Welsh Government covering areas such as free school meals and council tax reform.

Policies

Plaid Cymru's central aim is Welsh independence, including membership of the European Union for an independent Wales. It also advocates public ownership, action on climate change, the expansion of Welsh-medium education, and official status and promotion for the Welsh language. The party is affiliated at European level with the European Free Alliance.
