Mahesh Bhatt

Mahesh Bhatt (born 20 September 1948) is an Indian film director, producer and screenwriter known for his work in Hindi cinema. He is regarded as one of the notable filmmakers of Indian parallel and mainstream cinema, and is the head of the production company Vishesh Films along with his brother Mukesh Bhatt.

Career

Bhatt began his career assisting director Raj Khosla and made his directorial debut with Manzilein Aur Bhi Hain (1974). His breakthrough came with the semi-autobiographical Arth (1982), followed by the acclaimed Saaransh (1984), which was India's entry for the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film. His other notable films include Naam (1986), Daddy (1989), Aashiqui (1990), Sadak (1991), Dil Hai Ke Manta Nahin (1991), Hum Hain Rahi Pyar Ke (1993), and Zakhm (1998), for which he received the National Film Award for Best Feature Film on National Integration.

In the 2000s, through Vishesh Films, he produced and mentored a series of successful commercial films including Raaz, Murder, Gangster, and Jannat, launching the careers of several actors and directors.

Personal life

Bhatt is the son of filmmaker Nanabhai Bhatt. He is the father of actress Alia Bhatt and filmmaker Pooja Bhatt, and the uncle of actor Emraan Hashmi. He is known for his outspoken views on social and political issues and has authored works including the autobiographical A Taste of Life.
