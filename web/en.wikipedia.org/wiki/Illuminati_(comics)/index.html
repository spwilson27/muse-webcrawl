Illuminati (comics)

The Illuminati are a fictional secret society and group of superheroes appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. The characters joined forces and secretly work behind the scenes of the main Marvel Universe. The Illuminati were created by writer Brian Michael Bendis and artist Steve McNiven, and first appeared in New Avengers #7 (July 2005).

The group was established after the Kree–Skrull War, in a retcon story written by Bendis. Its founding members were Iron Man (Tony Stark), Mister Fantastic (Reed Richards), Doctor Strange, Professor X (Charles Xavier), Namor the Sub-Mariner, and Black Bolt, king of the Inhumans. Each member represented a different segment of the superhero community, and the group met in secret to share information and shape strategy against large-scale threats.

Publication history

The Illuminati featured prominently in the build-up to the "Civil War" storyline and in the 2007 miniseries New Avengers: Illuminati, also by Bendis. Their decision to exile the Hulk from Earth led directly to the "Planet Hulk" and "World War Hulk" storylines. During Jonathan Hickman's run on the Avengers titles, the group reassembled and played a central role in the "Infinity" and Secret Wars (2015) events, confronting the incursions that destroyed parallel universes.

Membership

Membership has shifted over time, at various points including Captain America, Black Panther (who initially refused to join), Hank Pym, Beast, Amadeus Cho, Captain Britain, and others. The group famously gathered the Infinity Gems and divided them among its members for safekeeping.

In other media

The Illuminati appeared in the Marvel Cinematic Universe film Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness (2022), depicted as a council of heroes on Earth-838 including Professor X, Captain Carter, Captain Marvel (Maria Rambeau), Black Bolt, Mister Fantastic and Karl Mordo.
