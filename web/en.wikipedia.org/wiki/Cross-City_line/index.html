The Cross-City line is a suburban railway line in the West Midlands of England, running from Lichfield in the north through Birmingham New Street to Redditch and Bromsgrove in the south. It is one of the most heavily used commuter routes in the region.

History

Through services began in 1978, linking the formerly separate Lichfield and Redditch branches via a restored cross-city route through Birmingham, with several new stations. The line was electrified in the early 1990s, and the southern terminus was extended from Redditch to Bromsgrove in 2018.

Services

Trains run at frequent intervals through the central section, serving stations such as Sutton Coldfield, Birmingham New Street, the University of Birmingham station (University) and Longbridge. Services are operated by West Midlands Railway.
