Govia Thameslink Railway (GTR) is a British train operating company owned by Govia, a joint venture between the Go-Ahead Group (65%) and Keolis (35%). It operates the Thameslink, Southern and Great Northern (TSGN) rail contract, the largest rail franchise in the United Kingdom by passenger numbers, under the brands Thameslink, Southern, Great Northern and Gatwick Express.

History

The Department for Transport awarded the TSGN franchise to Govia in May 2014. Services began on 14 September 2014 with the Thameslink and Great Northern routes transferred from First Capital Connect; the Southern and Gatwick Express operations were incorporated in July 2015. The combined franchise created the UK's largest train operator, running roughly one in five of all passenger journeys in Britain at its peak.

The franchise was heavily affected by the introduction of a new timetable in May 2018, which caused widespread cancellations and disruption and led to emergency timetable changes, government intervention and compensation arrangements for passengers. GTR's contract was repeatedly extended and converted to a national rail contract structure following the emergency measures introduced during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Services

Thameslink services run north–south through central London via London Bridge and St Pancras, linking Bedford, Luton, Peterborough and Cambridge with Gatwick Airport, Brighton and stations in Kent and Surrey. Southern operates services from London Victoria and London Bridge to the south coast, including Gatwick Express airport services between Victoria and Gatwick Airport. Great Northern runs trains from London King's Cross and Moorgate to Hertfordshire, Cambridgeshire and King's Lynn.

Fleet

GTR operates one of the largest fleets in Britain, including Class 387, Class 700 (Thameslink Programme trains built by Siemens), Class 717 and Class 377 Electrostar multiple units, as well as Class 455 and diesel Class 171 units on non-electrified Southern routes.
