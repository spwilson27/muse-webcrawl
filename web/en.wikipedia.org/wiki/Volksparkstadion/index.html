Volksparkstadion

Volksparkstadion
Location: Bahrenfeld, Hamburg, Germany
Capacity: 57,000
Opened: 1953 (original); 2000 (rebuild)
Tenants: Hamburger SV

The Volksparkstadion is a football stadium located in Bahrenfeld, Hamburg, Germany. It is the home ground of Hamburger SV and has a capacity of about 57,000 spectators. The stadium lies adjacent to the Barclays Arena within the Altona Volkspark from which it takes its name.

History

The original Volksparkstadion opened in 1953 on the site of earlier grounds used by HSV. It hosted matches at the 1974 FIFA World Cup and UEFA Euro 1988. Between 1998 and 2000 the stadium was almost entirely rebuilt: the old bowl was demolished and a new arena, rotated 90 degrees from the original orientation, was constructed with a roof covering all seats.

Sponsorship names

Under sponsorship arrangements the stadium has at various times been named the AOL Arena, HSH Nordbank Arena and Imtech Arena, reverting to the traditional Volksparkstadion name in 2015 after HSV supporters and the club bought back the naming rights.

Major events

The rebuilt stadium hosted games at the 2006 FIFA World Cup and the 2010 UEFA Europa League Final. It was also one of the venues for UEFA Euro 2024. Besides football, it regularly hosts large open-air concerts.
