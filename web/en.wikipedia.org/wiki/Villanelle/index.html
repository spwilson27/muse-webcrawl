Villanelle

A villanelle is a nineteen-line poetic form consisting of five tercets followed by a quatrain, with two refrains and only two rhymes throughout.

Form

The first and third lines of the opening tercet repeat alternately as refrains. Famous examples include Dylan Thomas's "Do not go gentle into that good night" and Elizabeth Bishop's "One Art".

History

Originating from Italian and French pastoral songs, the fixed form was established in France in the 19th century.
