Highways in Croatia

Highways in Croatia
Motorways: autoceste (A1–A11)
Total motorway length: over 1,300 km
Operator: Hrvatske autoceste (HAC) and concessionaires

Highways in Croatia are divided into motorways (autoceste, singular autocesta) and expressways (brze ceste). Motorways are designated with the letter A followed by a number, and form a network connecting Zagreb with Split, Rijeka, Osijek, Varaždin and the coast.

Network

The principal route is the A1 motorway from Zagreb via Karlovac and the Sveti Rok tunnel to Split and Ploče, serving the Dalmatian coast. The A3 runs east–west across Slavonia, the A4 north-east to Hungary, the A6 to Rijeka, and the A7 along the northern Adriatic. The Pelješac Bridge, opened in 2022, connects the Dubrovnik exclave to the rest of Croatia by road.

Tolls

Most motorways operate a closed toll system based on distance travelled, with electronic toll collection (ENC) available.
