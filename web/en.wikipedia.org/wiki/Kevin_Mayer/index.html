Kevin Mayer

Kevin Mayer (born 10 February 1992) is a French athlete specialising in the combined events. He is the world record holder in the decathlon, having scored 9,126 points at the Décastar meeting in Talence in September 2018, surpassing the previous mark of Ashton Eaton. Mayer is a two-time Olympic silver medallist (Rio 2016 and Tokyo 2020) and a two-time world champion in the decathlon (2017 and 2022).

Career

Mayer won the world junior title in 2010 and steadily rose through the senior ranks. At the 2016 Rio Olympics he pushed Eaton closely to take silver with 8,834 points. He won his first world title in London in 2017 and added a second in Eugene in 2022. Indoors, he won the world indoor heptathlon title in 2018 and held the European indoor heptathlon record.

World record

At Talence on 15–16 September 2018, Mayer compiled 9,126 points across the ten events — including a 10.55 s 100 metres, a 7.80 m long jump, and a 5.45 m pole vault — becoming the first man to exceed 9,100 points and only the third to break 9,000.
