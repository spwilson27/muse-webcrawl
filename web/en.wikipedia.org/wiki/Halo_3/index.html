Halo 3

Halo 3 is a 2007 first-person shooter video game developed by Bungie and published by Microsoft Game Studios for the Xbox 360. It is the third main instalment in the Halo series and concludes the original trilogy's story arc.

Plot

The game continues the war between humanity's UNSC, the alien Covenant, and the parasitic Flood. The Master Chief returns to Earth to stop the Prophet of Truth from activating the Halo array via the Ark, aided by the Arbiter and Sergeant Johnson; Cortana's fate forms a central thread, ending with the Chief entering cryosleep aboard the drifting Forward Unto Dawn.

Gameplay

Halo 3 added equipment items, new vehicles and weapons, four-player online co-operative campaign play, the Forge map editor, and saved-film Theatre mode — features that became series hallmarks.

Reception and sales

Released on 25 September 2007, Halo 3 grossed more than US$170 million on its first day and sold over 14 million copies. It received widespread critical acclaim and became a landmark Xbox 360 title. It was later included in Halo: The Master Chief Collection.
