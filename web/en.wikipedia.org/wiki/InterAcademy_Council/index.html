InterAcademy Council
Founded: 2000
Type: Association of national science academies
Successor role: folded into the InterAcademy Partnership (2016)

The InterAcademy Council (IAC) was an international organisation of national academies of science, engineering and medicine, established in 2000 to mobilise the world's scientific academies to provide independent expert advice to governments and international bodies.

Work

The IAC produced peer-reviewed studies on issues including energy transitions (Lighting the Way, 2007), scientific capacity building in Africa, and responsible conduct in research (Responsible Conduct in the Global Research Enterprise, 2012, with IAP). In 2010 it conducted an independent review of the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC), recommending reforms to its management and conflict-of-interest policies, which the IPCC adopted.

Reorganisation

In 2016 the IAC's functions were brought together with those of IAP and other academy networks under the umbrella of the InterAcademy Partnership.
