Italy national football team results

This page collects the match results of the Italy national football team, one of the most successful national sides in world football. Italy played its first international on 15 May 1910 in Milan, defeating France 6–2. The team, known as the Azzurri, has won the FIFA World Cup four times (1934, 1938, 1982 and 2006) and the UEFA European Championship twice (1968 and 2020).

Records

Italy's most-capped player is Gianluigi Buffon with 176 appearances; the top scorer is Luigi Riva with 35 goals. The team's longest unbeaten run, a world record of 37 matches between 2018 and 2021, ended with defeat by Spain in the UEFA Nations League semi-final.
