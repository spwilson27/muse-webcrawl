Aquamarine (gem)

Aquamarine is a blue-to-cyan variety of the mineral beryl, coloured by trace amounts of iron. Its name comes from the Latin aqua marina, "sea water", referring to its colour. It is the birthstone for March and a popular gemstone in jewellery.

Mineral: Beryl
Colour: Blue, blue-green, cyan
Hardness: 7.5–8 (Mohs)
Birthstone: March

Sources

Aquamarine is found in granite pegmatites. Important sources include Brazil (the historical source of many large stones), Pakistan, Afghanistan, Russia, Nigeria, Madagascar, and the United States. The largest cut aquamarine, the Dom Pedro, was cut from a Brazilian crystal and weighs over 10,000 carats.

Properties and treatment

Aquamarine is relatively hard and durable, with a vitreous lustre. Much commercial aquamarine is heat-treated to remove greenish or yellowish tones and enhance the blue colour; this treatment is stable and widely accepted in the trade.
