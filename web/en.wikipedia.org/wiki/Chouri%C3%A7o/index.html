Chouriço

Chouriço is a Portuguese pork sausage seasoned with paprika, garlic and wine, closely related to Spanish chorizo but typically smokier and often made with a coarser texture. It is a staple of Portuguese cuisine at home and across the Portuguese diaspora.

Varieties and use

Regional varieties include chouriço de carne, the blood sausage chouriço de sangue, and the leaner paio. Chouriço is grilled, added to stews and soups such as caldo verde, and famously flamed in alcohol as chouriço à bombeiro. In former Portuguese territories such as Goa, local versions incorporate vinegar and hot spices.
