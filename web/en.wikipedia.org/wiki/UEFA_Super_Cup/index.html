The UEFA Super Cup is an annual football match organised by UEFA and contested by the winners of the two main European club competitions: the UEFA Champions League and the UEFA Europa League. The competition's official name was originally the European Super Cup; it was renamed the UEFA Super Cup in 1995.

History

The idea for the competition came from Dutch journalist Anton Witkamp in the early 1970s, when Dutch football dominated Europe. The first edition, played in 1973 (for the 1972 title), saw Ajax defeat Rangers. Originally a two-legged tie between the European Cup and European Cup Winners' Cup holders, it became a single match in 1998, held for many years at the Stade Louis II in Monaco before rotating among European stadiums from 2013 onward. Since the Cup Winners' Cup was abolished in 1999, the second participant has been the UEFA Cup / Europa League winner.

Format

The Super Cup is a single 90-minute match played at a neutral venue in August, at the start of the European club season. If scores are level at the end of normal time, the match goes directly to a penalty shoot-out, with no extra time.

Records

Real Madrid is the most successful club in the competition's history with six titles, followed by Barcelona and AC Milan with five each. Italian, Spanish, and English clubs have dominated the list of winners.
