DTS:X

DTS:X is a multi-dimensional audio (MDA) object-based audio codec developed by DTS, first announced in January 2015. Like its competitor Dolby Atmos, DTS:X allows sound engineers to place individual sounds as objects in a three-dimensional space rather than assigning them to fixed channels.

At home, DTS:X is delivered over HDMI to AV receivers that render the objects to whatever speaker layout is present, up to configurations such as 7.1.4. The codec is included on many Ultra HD Blu-ray and Blu-ray titles. DTS:X Pro extends support to up to 32 speaker outputs, and DTS Virtual:X can simulate height effects from fewer speakers.

History

DTS acquired SRS Labs in 2012, whose MDA platform formed the basis of DTS:X. The format launched in cinemas and home theatre products in 2015–2016.
