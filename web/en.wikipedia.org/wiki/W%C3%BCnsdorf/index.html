Wünsdorf

Wünsdorf
Country: Germany
State: Brandenburg
Town: Zossen
Known for: Former Soviet military headquarters

Wünsdorf is a district of the town of Zossen in the Teltow-Fläming district of Brandenburg, Germany, about 40 km south of Berlin. It is known for its long military history and for the vast abandoned Soviet garrison complex sometimes nicknamed "Little Moscow".

History

The area became a major Imperial German army training ground before the First World War and housed a prisoner-of-war camp known for its "Half Moon Camp" for Muslim prisoners. During the Second World War it hosted headquarters facilities of the Wehrmacht's high command, including the Maybach bunker complexes. After 1945 Wünsdorf became the headquarters of the Soviet Group of Forces in Germany; at its peak tens of thousands of Soviet soldiers and dependents lived there, with a daily train to Moscow. The last Russian troops withdrew in 1994.

After reunification

Parts of the site have been converted into a "book town" (Bücherstadt Wünsdorf, founded 1998) with antiquarian bookshops, housing and museums about the site's military past.
