DHL Group, formerly Deutsche Post DHL Group, is a German multinational package delivery and supply chain management company headquartered in Bonn. It is the world's largest logistics company, particularly in sea and air mail, and operates the German national postal service under the Deutsche Post brand and the international express, freight and supply chain businesses under the DHL brand.

History

The company emerged from the privatisation of the German federal postal authority Deutsche Bundespost in the 1990s. Deutsche Post acquired the international express operator DHL — founded in San Francisco in 1969 by Adrian Dalsey, Larry Hillblom and Robert Lynn — in stages between 1998 and 2002. In 2023 the group changed its name from Deutsche Post DHL Group to DHL Group to reflect the international weight of the DHL businesses, while Deutsche Post remains the brand for postal and parcel services in Germany.

Divisions

The group is organised into divisions covering express international parcels, global freight forwarding, supply chain contract logistics, e-commerce solutions, and the domestic German post and parcel business. It operates in more than 220 countries and territories.
