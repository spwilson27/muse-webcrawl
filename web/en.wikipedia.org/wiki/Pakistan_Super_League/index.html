The Pakistan Super League (PSL) is a men's Twenty20 cricket league contested by six city-based franchise teams representing six cities of Pakistan. The league was founded by the Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB) in 2015 and its first season was played in 2016. Rather than operating as an association of independently owned teams, the league is a single entity in which each franchise is controlled by investors.

The commercial rights to the initial franchises were sold for US$93 million for a span of 10 years in December 2015. The league usually takes place in February and March of each year, and is Pakistan's premier domestic Twenty20 competition.

History

The PCB formally announced the launch of the PSL in September 2015. The inaugural season, held in the United Arab Emirates in February 2016 because of security concerns at home, featured five teams and was won by Islamabad United. A sixth team, Multan Sultans, joined for the 2018 season. Gradually more matches were staged in Pakistan, and from 2020 the entire tournament has been played on home soil, marking the full return of top-level franchise cricket to the country.

Format

Each team plays every other team twice in a double round-robin league stage. The top four teams advance to the playoffs, which follow the Page playoff system used by other major T20 leagues, culminating in a final. Players are acquired through a draft system, with squads divided into categories (Platinum, Diamond, Gold, Silver and Emerging), and each squad includes a quota of overseas players.

Results

Islamabad United and Lahore Qalandars are among the most successful sides; Islamabad won the inaugural 2016 title and again in 2018, while Lahore won back-to-back titles in 2022 and 2023. Peshawar Zalmi won in 2017, Quetta Gladiators in 2019, Karachi Kings in 2020 and Multan Sultans in 2021.
