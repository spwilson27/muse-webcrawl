Medication

A medication (also called a medicament, medicine, pharmaceutical drug, or simply a drug) is a substance used to diagnose, cure, treat, or prevent disease. Drug therapy (pharmacotherapy) is a central part of medicine.

Medication
TypesPrescription, over-the-counter
RoutesOral, intravenous, topical, inhaled

Classification

Medications are classified by chemical structure, mechanism of action, and therapeutic use (the ATC system). Examples include analgesics, antibiotics, antivirals, vaccines, and chemotherapy agents.

Regulation and safety

Agencies such as the U.S. Food and Drug Administration and the European Medicines Agency approve drugs after clinical trials. Adverse drug reactions, interactions, and adherence are major safety concerns. Historical practice is covered in Medicine in the medieval Islamic world.
