Morrison is a home rule municipality in Jefferson County, Colorado, United States, located in the foothills west of Denver. The population was about 430 at the 2020 census. The town is named after George Morrison, a stonemason and builder who settled in the area in the 1870s and quarried its distinctive red sandstone.

Geography and attractions

Morrison sits along Bear Creek at the mouth of a canyon, beneath dramatic sandstone hogback formations. It is best known as the gateway to Red Rocks Amphitheatre and Red Rocks Park, and as the site where the first two Triceratops fossils and other dinosaur remains were excavated in 1877 from the nearby Morrison Formation, a rock unit named after the town. Dinosaur Ridge, with exposed dinosaur tracks, lies just east of town.
