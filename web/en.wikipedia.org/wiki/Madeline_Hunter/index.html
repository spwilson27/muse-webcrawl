Madeline Hunter

Madeline Hunter is an American author of historical romance novels, best known for her medieval and Regency-set romances. Her books have appeared on the New York Times and USA Today bestseller lists, and she has twice won the RITA Award, the highest award given in romance fiction.

Career

Hunter published her first novel, By Arrangement, in 2000. Before writing fiction full-time she worked in academia, holding a doctorate in art history. Her works include series such as the Seducer novels, the Rothwell series, and the Fairbourne Quartet, and stand-alone novels including The Sinner and By Possession.

Awards

She won the Romance Writers of America's RITA Award for Best Novel with Strong Romantic Elements for The Charmer (2004) and for Best Historical Romance for Sinful in Satin (2011), and has been nominated for the award multiple times.
