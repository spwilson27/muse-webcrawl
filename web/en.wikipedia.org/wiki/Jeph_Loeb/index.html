Joseph "Jeph" Loeb III (born 29 January 1958) is an American film and television writer, producer and comic book writer. Loeb was a producer and writer on the TV series Smallville and Lost, as well as a writer and co-executive producer on the NBC series Heroes. From 2010 to 2019 he was executive vice president of Marvel Television, overseeing series including Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D., Daredevil and Jessica Jones.

Comics career

Loeb is known for his collaborations with artist Tim Sale, including Batman: The Long Halloween, Batman: Dark Victory, Superman for All Seasons and the Marvel "colour" books Daredevil: Yellow, Spider-Man: Blue, Hulk: Gray and Captain America: White. With artist Jim Lee he produced Batman: Hush; with Ed McGuinness, Superman/Batman. His work has won multiple Eisner Awards.

Film and television

Loeb's early film credits include the screenplays for Teen Wolf (1985) and Commando (1985). As head of Marvel Television he oversaw the studio's live-action and animated output for a decade, until the division was folded into Marvel Studios in 2019.

Personal life

Loeb's son Sam died of bone cancer in 2005 at the age of 17; the Superman/Batman issue "Sam's Story", published in tribute, raised money for a scholarship in his name. Sam Loeb had himself written an issue of Superman/Batman before his death.
