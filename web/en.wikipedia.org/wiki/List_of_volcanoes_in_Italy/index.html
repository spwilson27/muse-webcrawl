List of volcanoes in Italy

Italy is one of the few European countries with active volcanoes, a consequence of the collision between the African and Eurasian plates. Italian volcanism is concentrated in the south — Campania, the Aeolian Islands, and Sicily — and includes some of the most closely monitored and historically significant volcanoes in the world.

Monitoring

Italian volcanoes are monitored by the National Institute of Geophysics and Volcanology (INGV), with observatories in Naples (Vesuvius Observatory, founded 1841, the oldest in the world), Catania, and Rome. Civil protection plans exist for the roughly 700,000 people in the Vesuvius "red zone."
