Microsoft 365 is a subscription service and product family developed by Microsoft that provides productivity software and related cloud services. Launched on 28 June 2011 as Office 365, it rebranded to its current name on 21 April 2020.

Services

Plans combine desktop and web versions of Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and OneNote with cloud services including Exchange Online email, SharePoint and OneDrive storage, and the Teams collaboration platform. Enterprise plans add device management, security, and compliance tools. In 2023 Microsoft integrated its Copilot AI assistant across the suite.

History

Office 365 began as a consolidation of Microsoft's earlier business online services, introducing a per-user subscription model alongside the traditional perpetual licences of Microsoft Office. Consumer subscriptions followed in 2013. The 2020 rebranding reflected the suite's expansion beyond Office applications into personal productivity, safety, and creativity services.

Reception and scale

Microsoft 365 grew into one of the world's largest software-as-a-service businesses, with tens of millions of consumer subscribers and hundreds of millions of commercial seats, though it faces continuing competition from Google Workspace.
