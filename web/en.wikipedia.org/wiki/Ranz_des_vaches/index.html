Ranz des vaches

Ranz des vaches is the traditional Swiss melody played on alphorn and sung by herdsmen to call cattle and during milking. Its name comes from French for 'rows of cows'. The melodies, varying by canton, are among the oldest examples of Swiss folk music.

Cultural significance

The ranz des vaches was famously said to provoke intense nostalgia (mal du pays) in Swiss mercenaries abroad, and Jean-Jacques Rousseau claimed its performance was forbidden among Swiss troops on pain of death. Composers including Beethoven, Liszt, and Rossini quoted such melodies; Rossini used a ranz des vaches in the William Tell Overture.
