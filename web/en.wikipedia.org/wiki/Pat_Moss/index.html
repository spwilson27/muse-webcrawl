Patricia Ann "Pat" Moss-Carlsson (née Moss; 27 December 1934 – 14 October 2008) was one of the most successful British female rally drivers of the 1950s and 1960s. She was the younger sister of Formula One driver Stirling Moss.

Career

Moss began competing in the mid-1950s and quickly became a leading figure in international rallying. She won the European Ladies' Rally Championship several times and achieved notable outright results, including winning the 1960 Liège–Rome–Liège Rally in an Austin-Healey 3000 — an event considered one of the toughest of its era — and the 1962 Tulip Rally in a Mini Cooper.

She married Swedish rally driver Erik Carlsson in 1963. After retiring from regular competition in the late 1960s, she remained a respected figure in historic rallying. Her career helped pave the way for later women competitors in motorsport.
