Minister of Home Affairs (India)

North Block, New Delhi, seat of the Ministry of Home Affairs

The Minister of Home Affairs (or Home Minister) is the head of the Ministry of Home Affairs of the Government of India. One of the senior-most offices in the Union Cabinet, the home minister is responsible for internal security, domestic policy, the Indian Police Service, central armed police forces, and relations between the Union and the states.

Minister of Home Affairs
IncumbentAmit Shah (since 31 May 2019)
AppointerPresident of India on the advice of the Prime Minister
Formation15 August 1947
First holderVallabhbhai Patel

History

The first home minister of independent India was Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, who also served as deputy prime minister and was instrumental in the integration of the princely states. The office has since been held by figures including Gulzarilal Nanda, Lal Krishna Advani, Shivraj Patil, P. Chidambaram, Rajnath Singh and Amit Shah.

Responsibilities

The ministry oversees internal security, border management, Centre–state relations, official language policy, and the administration of union territories. The home minister is a key member of the Cabinet Committee on Security.
