Day of Vengeance

Day of Vengeance
Publisher: DC Comics
Schedule: Monthly (limited series)
Publication date: 2005
Main character(s): Spectre, Ragman, Enchantress, Blue Devil

Day of Vengeance is a six-issue DC Comics limited series published in 2005, written by Bill Willingham with art principally by Justiniano and Walden Wong. It served as one of the lead-in miniseries to the Infinite Crisis crossover event, and told of the Spectre, driven mad and unbound from a human host, embarking on a campaign to destroy all magic in the DC Universe.

Plot

After Jean Loring manipulates the demon Eclipso into corrupting the Spectre, the Spectre begins systematically killing magical beings and destroying places of magic, including the Rock of Eternity above Gotham City. The wizard Shazam is killed fighting him. In response, a loose alliance of mystical characters — Ragman, Enchantress, Blue Devil, Nightmaster, Nightshade and Detective Chimp — forms the group that would become Shadowpact, and fights to contain the catastrophe. The series ends with the debris of the Rock of Eternity, and a resurrected Captain Marvel, setting in motion events continued in Infinite Crisis and the ongoing Shadowpact series.

Collected editions

The miniseries was collected in a trade paperback together with related material. A follow-up one-shot, Day of Vengeance: Infinite Crisis Special, bridged the series and its sequel events.
