BayArena

The BayArena is a football stadium in Leverkusen, North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany, and the home ground of Bundesliga club Bayer 04 Leverkusen. The stadium has a capacity of around 30,210 spectators and is owned by the Bayer pharmaceutical company, the club's long-standing sponsor.

History

The stadium opened in 1958 as the Ulrich-Haberland-Stadion, named after a former chairman of Bayer AG. Originally a modest ground with a capacity of about 20,000, it was repeatedly modernised as Bayer Leverkusen rose through the German leagues. In 1986 the ground hosted matches during West Germany's preparations connected with major tournaments, and extensive rebuilding in the 1990s converted it into an all-seater stadium.

In 1998 the stadium was renamed BayArena following a sponsorship and modernisation programme. A further expansion completed in 2009 raised capacity above 30,000 and added a distinctive roof structure, a hotel integrated into the stadium complex, and improved hospitality facilities.

Events

Besides Leverkusen's Bundesliga and European fixtures, the BayArena has hosted several Germany national team matches and was a training venue during the 2006 FIFA World Cup held in Germany. Its relatively compact size compared with other Bundesliga grounds gives it a reputation as an intimate, steep-sided arena.
