Middle East Technical University

Middle East Technical University (METU; Turkish: Orta Doğu Teknik Üniversitesi, ODTÜ) is a public technical university in Ankara, Turkey. Founded in 1956, it teaches in English and emphasises research and education in engineering and the natural sciences.

History and campus

METU was established under acting director Charles Abrams and first rector Mustafa Kemal Kurdaş, initially to train architects and engineers for Turkey and the Middle East. Its large forested campus south of Ankara, around Lake Eymir, includes the university's science and technology museum and a prominent steppe-forestation programme that won the Aga Khan Award for Architecture in 1995.

Academics

The university has faculties of architecture, engineering, education, economic and administrative sciences, and arts and sciences, serving tens of thousands of students. Its Northern Cyprus Campus opened in 2003. METU consistently ranks among Turkey's leading research universities.
