Travelling overwatch

Travelling overwatch (or traveling overwatch) is a military tactical movement technique in which one element moves while another element remains in a position of readiness to provide suppressive or covering fire if the moving element encounters the enemy.

Description

In travelling overwatch, the lead element advances continuously at a steady pace while the trailing overwatch element follows at a distance that maintains visual contact and fields of fire, positioning itself — often by moving from one vantage point to another — so that it can engage threats at any moment. The technique is used when contact with the enemy is expected but not certain, trading speed for security.

Comparison with related techniques

It is intermediate between travelling, where all elements move simultaneously when enemy contact is unlikely, and bounding overwatch, where the moving and overwatch elements alternate short bounds and contact is expected imminently. Bounding overwatch is the slowest and most secure; travelling is the fastest and least secure. The concept generalizes from infantry fire teams and squads up through armored formations, where tanks or infantry fighting vehicles overwatch one another across open terrain.
