Mohanlal Viswanathan (born 21 May 1960), known mononymously as Mohanlal, is an Indian actor, producer and singer who works predominantly in Malayalam cinema, with notable appearances in Tamil, Telugu, Hindi and Kannada films. One of the most celebrated actors in Indian cinema, his career spans more than four decades and 350-plus films; critics and fans frequently describe him as one of the finest actors India has produced, famed for the naturalness and range of his performances. He is affectionately called Lalettan by fans in Kerala.

Career

Mohanlal's first film, Thiranottam (1978), was released only decades later; his screen debut came as the villain in Manjil Virinja Pookkal (1980). By the mid-1980s he was Malayalam cinema's biggest star, moving from villain and comic roles to complex leads in films such as T. P. Balagopalan M.A. (1986), Rajavinte Makan (1986), Iruvar's predecessors in the classic era — the late 1980s and 1990s brought a stream of landmark performances: Thoovanathumbikal (1987), Chithram (1988), Kireedam (1989), Bharatham (1991, National Film Award for Best Actor), Vanaprastham (1999, a second Best Actor award), and commercial blockbusters such as Aaraam Thampuran (1997) and Narasimham (2000). In Tamil cinema he starred in Mani Ratnam's Iruvar (1997); in Hindi in Company (2002); in Telugu in Janatha Garage (2016).

Later milestones include Drishyam (2013), one of the most acclaimed Indian thrillers and the first Malayalam film to gross ₹50 crore, its sequel Drishyam 2 (2021), the action blockbuster Pulimurugan (2016), and Lucifer (2019), directed by Prithviraj Sukumaran. In 2025 his films L2: Empuraan and Thudarum became the highest-grossing Malayalam films of their time.

Honours and other work

Mohanlal has won five National Film Awards (including Best Actor twice and a Special Jury Award), nine Kerala State Film Awards and numerous Filmfare Awards South. The Government of India awarded him the Padma Shri in 2001 and the Padma Bhushan in 2019. He holds the honorary rank of lieutenant colonel in the Territorial Army, runs production and distribution ventures, has served as president of the Association of Malayalam Movie Artists (AMMA) from 2018, and co-owns the Indian Super League club Kerala Blasters' early ventures and other businesses. In 2025 he was announced as the recipient of the Dadasaheb Phalke Award, Indian cinema's highest honour, for 2023.
