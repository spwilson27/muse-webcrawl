BioWare is a Canadian video game developer based in Edmonton, Alberta, known for story-driven role-playing games. It was founded in February 1995 by newly graduated medical doctors Ray Muzyka, Greg Zeschuk and Augustine Yip.

History

BioWare's breakthrough was Baldur's Gate (1998), developed with the Infinity Engine and credited with revitalising the computer role-playing game, followed by Baldur's Gate II: Shadows of Amn (2000) and Neverwinter Nights (2002). Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic (2003) won wide acclaim and numerous Game of the Year awards.

In 2007 Electronic Arts acquired BioWare (with Pandemic Studios) in a deal valued at about $860 million. Under EA, BioWare created the Mass Effect trilogy (2007–2012) and the Dragon Age series (beginning 2009), and developed the MMO Star Wars: The Old Republic (2011) from its Austin studio. Later releases included Mass Effect: Andromeda (2017), the online action game Anthem (2019), Mass Effect Legendary Edition (2021) and Dragon Age: The Veilguard (2024).

Legacy

BioWare became renowned for branching narratives, player choice, companion characters and moral decision systems. Muzyka and Zeschuk both left the company in 2012. The studio received a star on Canada's Walk of Fame in 2010.
