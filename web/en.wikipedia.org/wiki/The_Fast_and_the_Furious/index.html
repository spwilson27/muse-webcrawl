The Fast and the Furious

Directed by: Rob Cohen
Starring: Paul Walker, Vin Diesel, Michelle Rodriguez, Jordana Brewster
Released: 22 June 2001
Box office: $207.3 million

The Fast and the Furious is a 2001 American action film directed by Rob Cohen, from a screenplay by Gary Scott Thompson, Erik Bergquist and David Ayer. It is the first installment in the Fast & Furious franchise. The film stars Paul Walker as Brian O'Conner, an undercover police officer sent to infiltrate a street-racing crew led by Dominic Toretto (Vin Diesel), who is suspected of hijacking trucks carrying electronic equipment.

Plot

Brian O'Conner, an LAPD officer working with the FBI, infiltrates the Los Angeles street-racing scene to investigate a series of audacious heists in which gangs in black Honda Civics hijack semi-trucks. He befriends mechanic and racer Dominic Toretto, falls in love with Dom's sister Mia, and earns Dom's trust by helping him escape arrest after an illegal race. Torn between duty and loyalty, Brian ultimately lets Dom escape in his own car after the crew's final heist goes wrong, ending his police career.

Production and reception

The film was inspired by Ken Li's 1998 Vibe magazine article "Racer X" about street racing in New York City, and its title was licensed from Roger Corman's 1955 film of the same name. Made on a budget of about $38 million, it grossed $207 million worldwide. Critics compared its plot to Point Break but praised its racing sequences and the chemistry of its leads; its success launched one of the highest-grossing film franchises of all time, with notable installments including Furious 7 (2015).
