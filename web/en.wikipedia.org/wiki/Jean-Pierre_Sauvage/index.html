Jean-Pierre Sauvage

Jean-Pierre Sauvage (born 21 October 1944) is a French coordination chemist who shared the Nobel Prize in Chemistry in 2016 with Fraser Stoddart and Ben Feringa for the design and synthesis of molecular machines.

Born21 October 1944, Paris, France
FieldsCoordination chemistry, molecular machines
AwardsNobel Prize in Chemistry (2016)

Work

Working under Jean-Marie Lehn, Sauvage developed methods using copper templates to synthesize catenanes—mechanically interlocked rings of molecules—and later rotaxanes that could act as molecular switches and muscles, foundational steps toward molecular machines.

Career

He spent his career at the University of Strasbourg and the CNRS, and is a member of the French Academy of Sciences.
