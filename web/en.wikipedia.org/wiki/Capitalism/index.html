Capitalism

Capitalism
FeaturesPrivate ownership, markets, wage labour, profit
Key textAdam Smith, The Wealth of Nations (1776)
[Image: A bustling stock-exchange trading floor under electronic ticker boards]

Capitalism is an economic system based on private ownership of the means of production and their operation for profit, with prices set largely by markets. Adam Smith's The Wealth of Nations (1776) gave it an early defence: competition and the 'invisible hand' align private gain with public benefit.

Varieties and criticism

Capitalist economies range from laissez-faire to welfare-state and state-directed models; they drove industrialisation through the steam engine and fossil fuels, generated enormous wealth and innovation, and are criticised for inequality, instability and environmental harm — the counterpoint to communism and a subject of sustainability debates.
