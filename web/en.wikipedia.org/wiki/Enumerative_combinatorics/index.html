Enumerative combinatorics

FieldCombinatorics
Key toolsGenerating functions, recurrence relations

Enumerative combinatorics is an area of combinatorics that deals with the number of ways that certain patterns can be formed. Two examples of this type of problem are counting combinations and counting permutations.

Overview

The central goal is to count the number of elements of a finite set, often by finding a closed formula or a generating function. Important techniques include the principle of inclusion–exclusion, recurrence relations, and exponential generating functions. Famous enumerative sequences include the Fibonacci numbers, the Catalan numbers, and the Bell numbers.

Twelvefold way

The twelvefold way provides a unified framework for counting functions between two finite sets, organised by whether the functions are injective, surjective, or unrestricted, and whether the sets' elements are considered distinguishable.
