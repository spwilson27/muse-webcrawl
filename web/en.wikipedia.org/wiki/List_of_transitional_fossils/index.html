List of transitional fossils

A transitional fossil is a fossilised organism showing traits intermediate between an ancestral group and its descendant group — key evidence for evolution. This list collects the most famous examples, grouped by the transition they document. In modern cladistic terms all such forms are better described as showing a mosaic of ancestral and derived features rather than being literal "missing links".

TransitionKey fossilsNotes
Fish → tetrapodsTiktaalik, Panderichthys, Acanthostega, Ichthyostega, VentastegaTiktaalik (Devonian, ~375 Ma) had fins with wrist-like bones; Acanthostega had eight digits.
Amphibians → amniotesSeymouria, CasineriaSeymouria combines amphibian and reptile features.
Land mammals → whalesPakicetus, Ambulocetus, Rodhocetus, Maiacetus, Basilosaurus, IndohyusA classic sequence from wolf-sized shore-dwellers to fully aquatic whales; Basilosaurus retained tiny hind limbs.
Dinosaurs → birdsArchaeopteryx, Anchiornis, Microraptor, Confuciusornis, AurornisArchaeopteryx (Late Jurassic, ~150 Ma) has flight feathers plus teeth and a long bony tail.
Synapsids → mammalsDimetrodon (a stem form), Thrinaxodon, Probainognathus, Morganucodonta, RepenomamusCynodonts show the jaw joint and ear bones evolving toward the mammalian condition.
Early primates → humansAustralopithecus (including "Lucy"), Homo habilis, Homo erectus, Ardipithecus, SahelanthropusBipedalism precedes large brains in the hominin record.
Stem turtlesOdontochelys, Proganochelys, EunotosaurusOdontochelys had a plastron but no carapace, and teeth.
Land mammals → pinnipedsPuijila, Enaliarctos, PotamotheriumPuijila was an otter-like walking swimmer of the Arctic Miocene.
Manatee relativesPezosirenA four-legged, walking early sirenian from Eocene Jamaica.
HorsesHyracotherium ("Eohippus"), Mesohippus, Merychippus, PliohippusClassic series showing toe reduction and tooth change with grassland spread.
