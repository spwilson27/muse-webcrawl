Four Continents Figure Skating Championships

The Four Continents Figure Skating Championships (4CC) is an annual figure skating competition. The International Skating Union established it in 1999 to provide skaters from non-European countries with a championship comparable to the long-established European Championships.

History

The name refers to the four continents represented: Africa, the Americas, Asia and Oceania (the ISU's usage grouping Africa, Asia, America and Oceania). Skaters compete in men's singles, women's singles, pair skating and ice dance. The first championships were held in Halifax, Canada, in 1999. The competition is generally held in January or February and, in Olympic years, serves as a key preparation event.

Qualification

Each ISU member nation outside Europe may enter up to three skaters or teams per discipline, provided they have achieved the minimum technical element scores required by the ISU. Prize money is awarded at the event, and medals are given in the overall result and in the short and free segments ("small medals").
