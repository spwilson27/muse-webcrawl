Red Frogs

Red Frogs Australia is a support network and harm-reduction program for young people, best known for its presence at Schoolies week celebrations in Australia. Volunteers, identifiable by their red shirts, provide practical assistance, hydration, and a sober, friendly presence to school leavers, and take their name from the red frog lollies they hand out.

History

The program began in 1997 on the Gold Coast, founded by Andy Gourley, who, while working as a skate park chaplain, began visiting hotel rooms during Schoolies with a bag of Allen's Red Frogs to build rapport with partying teenagers and offer help. The idea grew into a large volunteer network coordinated through local churches.

Activities

Red Frogs teams patrol party areas, staff hydration stations and "chill-out" zones, walk young people safely back to accommodation, cook pancakes in hotel rooms, and assist those affected by alcohol or distress. The network has expanded beyond Schoolies to university orientation weeks, festivals, and sporting events, and has operated internationally, including in New Zealand, Canada, South Africa, and the United Kingdom.
