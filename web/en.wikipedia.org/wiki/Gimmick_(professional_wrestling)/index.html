Gimmick (professional wrestling)

In professional wrestling, a gimmick is the in-ring persona, character, or trait assigned to a performer. Gimmicks shape a wrestler's costume, mannerisms, catchphrases, entrance music, and storylines, and they are central to how audiences connect with characters as heroes (faces) or villains (heels).

Types

Gimmicks range from exaggerated real personalities to occupational characters (plumbers, clowns, undertakers), supernatural figures such as The Undertaker, and masked personas in the lucha libre tradition. A "gimmick change" or repackaging can revive a performer's career.

Related usage

The word is also used for specialty matches ("gimmick matches") such as steel cage or ladder matches, and for foreign objects ("gimmicks") used as weapons in a bout.
