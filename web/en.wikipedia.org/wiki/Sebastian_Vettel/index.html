Sebastian Vettel

Sebastian Vettel (born 3 July 1987) is a German former racing driver who competed in Formula One from 2007 to 2022. He won four consecutive World Drivers' Championships with Red Bull Racing from 2010 to 2013, and ranks among the most successful drivers in the sport's history with 53 race victories.

Career

Vettel, from Heppenheim, progressed through karting and junior formulae with the support of Red Bull. He made his Formula One debut with BMW Sauber in 2007 and later that year joined Toro Rosso, with whom he won the 2008 Italian Grand Prix—becoming at the time the youngest race winner in the sport's history.

Red Bull years

Promoted to Red Bull Racing in 2009, Vettel finished runner-up that season before winning four consecutive titles (2010–2013), becoming the youngest world champion in 2010. His 2013 season included a record-equalling nine consecutive race wins.

Ferrari and Aston Martin

Vettel joined Ferrari in 2015, winning 14 races and twice finishing championship runner-up to Lewis Hamilton, but no title. He moved to Aston Martin for 2021 and retired at the end of 2022, having become known also for environmental advocacy.
