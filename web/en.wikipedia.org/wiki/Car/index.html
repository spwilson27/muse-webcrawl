A car, or automobile, is a wheeled motor vehicle used for transportation, typically seating one to eight people and running on roads with four wheels powered by an internal combustion engine or electric motor. Cars reshaped cities, suburbs, industry and culture more than perhaps any other machine.

History and transition

Karl Benz's 1885 Patent-Motorwagen began the industry; Ford's assembly line made cars mass property. Safety (belts, airbags, crumple zones), emissions rules and now electrification and driver assistance define the modern car. Electric vehicles passed a tenth of new sales in many markets in the 2020s, charged from the battery ecosystem.
