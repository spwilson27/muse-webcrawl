Oder-Spree is a Kreis (district) in the eastern part of Brandenburg, Germany. It takes its name from the two rivers that cross it, the Oder — which forms the border with Poland to the east — and the Spree. Neighbouring districts include Märkisch-Oderland, the city of Frankfurt (Oder), and the districts of Spree-Neiße, Dahme-Spreewald and the state of Berlin.

History

The district was established in 1993 by joining the old districts of Beeskow, Eisenhüttenstadt, Fürstenwalde and the district-free city of Eisenhüttenstadt in the course of the Brandenburg district reform.

Geography

The district includes the Scharmützelsee and Schwielochsee lake regions, popular recreation areas southeast of Berlin, and stretches of the Oder-Spree Canal, which links the two rivers. The Tesla Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg at Grünheide, opened in 2022, is a major recent industrial development in the district.
