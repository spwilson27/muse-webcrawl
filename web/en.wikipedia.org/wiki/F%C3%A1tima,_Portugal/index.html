Fátima is a city and civil parish in the municipality of Ourém, in the district of Santarém, Portugal. It is internationally famous for the Sanctuary of Fátima, a major Catholic pilgrimage site commemorating reported apparitions of the Virgin Mary in 1917.

The apparitions

Three shepherd children—Lúcia dos Santos and her cousins Francisco and Jacinta Marto—reported that Mary appeared to them on the 13th day of six consecutive months in 1917 at the Cova da Iria. The events culminated on 13 October 1917 with the so-called Miracle of the Sun, witnessed by a large crowd. The Church recognised the apparitions as worthy of belief in 1930; Francisco and Jacinta were canonised in 2017.

The sanctuary

The Sanctuary includes the Basilica of Our Lady of the Rosary (1928–1953), the Chapel of the Apparitions built on the site of the appearances, and the modern Basilica of the Holy Trinity (2007), one of the largest churches in the world. Millions of pilgrims visit each year, especially on 12–13 May and 12–13 October.

Name

The name Fátima derives, by local tradition, from a Moorish princess named after Fatimah, daughter of Muhammad, who converted to Christianity during the Reconquista.
