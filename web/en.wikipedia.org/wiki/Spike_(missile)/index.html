Spike (missile)

Spike
Type: Anti-tank guided missile family
Place of origin: Israel
Manufacturer: Rafael Advanced Defense Systems
In service: 1997–present
Guidance: electro-optical / infrared, "fire and forget"

Spike is a family of Israeli anti-tank guided missiles (ATGMs) developed and manufactured by Rafael Advanced Defense Systems. The missiles use electro-optical guidance with a fibre-optic or radio data link, allowing fire-and-forget operation, lock-on before or after launch, and mid-flight target updates by the operator.

Variants

  • Spike-SR — short range, disposable launcher, effective to about 1.5 km.
  • Spike-MR — medium range, about 2.5–4 km, man-portable.
  • Spike-LR / LR2 — long range, about 4–5.5 km, fired from tripods, vehicles and boats.
  • Spike-ER / ER2 — extended range, up to 8–16 km, used from vehicles, helicopters and naval mounts.
  • Spike NLOS — non-line-of-sight variant with ranges up to 25–32 km, guided into targets beyond visual range.

Service

Spike missiles have been exported widely and are used by dozens of armies, including many NATO members in Europe, as well as forces in Asia and Latin America. The EuroSpike consortium produces the missile under licence in Germany for European customers.
