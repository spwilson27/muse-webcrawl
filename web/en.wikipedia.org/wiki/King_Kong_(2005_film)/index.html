King Kong (2005 film)

Directed by: Peter Jackson
Starring: Naomi Watts, Jack Black, Adrien Brody, Andy Serkis
Released: 2005

King Kong is a 2005 epic monster adventure film co-written, produced, and directed by Peter Jackson. A remake of the 1933 film of the same name, it stars Naomi Watts, Jack Black, and Adrien Brody, with Andy Serkis providing motion capture for Kong.

Plot

In 1933 New York, ambitious filmmaker Carl Denham tricks actress Ann Darrow and playwright Jack Driscoll into sailing to the mysterious Skull Island. There the crew encounters hostile islanders, giant prehistoric creatures, and Kong, a 25-foot gorilla who becomes attached to Ann. Kong is captured and brought to New York, where he escapes during his Broadway debut, searches for Ann, and makes his famous last stand atop the Empire State Building before falling to his death.

Production

Jackson, a lifelong admirer of the original, shot the film mainly in New Zealand with extensive digital effects by Weta Digital, recreating 1930s New York and a lush, dangerous Skull Island. The film runs over three hours and includes celebrated sequences such as Kong's fight with three Vastatosaurus rex dinosaurs.

Reception

The film grossed over $550 million worldwide and won three Academy Awards, for sound editing, sound mixing, and visual effects. It was widely praised as a loving and spectacular homage to the 1933 classic.
