The Chicago franchise (also called One Chicago by NBC) is a media franchise of American television series created by Dick Wolf, following the professional and personal lives of first responders in Chicago, Illinois. The shows share a universe and frequently cross over with one another and with the Law & Order and FBI franchises.

Series

Chicago Fire premiered in 2012 and follows the firefighters, rescue specialists and paramedics of Firehouse 51. Chicago P.D. (2014) follows the officers of the Chicago Police Department's 21st District Intelligence Unit. Chicago Med (2015) follows the doctors, nurses and staff of the fictional Gaffney Chicago Medical Center. Chicago Justice (2017), following prosecutors and investigators, was cancelled after one season. The four programmes are produced by Wolf Entertainment in association with Universal Television.

Crossovers

Annual multi-series crossover events unite the shows in shared storylines, such as a massive city-wide emergency tackled jointly by firefighters, police and medical staff. Characters recur across series, and smaller crossovers occur throughout each season, making the Chicago universe one of the most interconnected in American network television.
