Debitage

Debitage is the collective material remaining after reducing a stone core in lithic reduction: flakes, chips, shatter and debris produced by knapping stone tools in archaeology.

Analysis

Archaeologists study debitage size, cortex, platform preparation and raw material to reconstruct reduction sequences, tool production locations, skill, mobility and trade. Debitage analysis, including mass analysis and refitting, can reveal activity areas even where finished tools were carried away.
