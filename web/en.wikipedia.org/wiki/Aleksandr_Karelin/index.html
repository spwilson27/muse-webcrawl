Aleksandr Aleksandrovich Karelin (born 19 September 1967) is a Russian former Greco-Roman wrestler, widely regarded as the greatest practitioner of the sport in history. Nicknamed the "Russian Bear" and "Alexander the Great", he dominated the super-heavyweight division for over a decade, remaining undefeated in international competition for thirteen years.

Career

Born in Novosibirsk, Siberia, Karelin took up wrestling as a teenager and rose rapidly through the Soviet system. He won Olympic gold at Seoul 1988, Barcelona 1992 (for the Unified Team) and Atlanta 1996 (for Russia), and nine consecutive world championship titles between 1989 and 1999. His signature move, the Karelin lift — a reverse body lift applied to a prone opponent — was spectacular for a wrestler of his size (1.91 m, around 130 kg).

The Sydney upset

At the Sydney 2000 Olympics, Karelin was sensationally defeated 1–0 in the final by the American Rulon Gardner, one of the biggest upsets in Olympic history. It was only the second loss of Karelin's career; he retired afterwards with a record of 887 wins and 2 defeats.

Later life

After wrestling, Karelin entered politics, serving as a deputy in the Russian State Duma with United Russia from 1999, and later becoming a senator representing Novosibirsk Oblast. He holds a doctorate in physical education and is a Hero of the Russian Federation.
