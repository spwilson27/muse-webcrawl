Vice President of Indonesia

The Vice Presidential Palace in Gambir, Jakarta.

The Vice President of the Republic of Indonesia (Indonesian: Wakil Presiden Republik Indonesia) is the second-highest officer in the executive branch of the Indonesian government and the first in the presidential line of succession. The vice president is elected as a running mate together with the president in a direct national election for a five-year term, renewable once.

Duties

Under the 1945 Constitution, the vice president assists the president and succeeds to the presidency in case of the president's death, resignation, removal, or incapacity until the end of the term. The vice president may also be assigned specific portfolios by the president; historically these have included poverty reduction, economic coordination, and interfaith affairs.

History of the office

The first vice president was Mohammad Hatta, who served alongside Sukarno from 1945 to 1956 and is remembered as the co-proclaimer of Indonesian independence. The office remained vacant for long stretches of the Sukarno and early Suharto eras. Later occupants have included Adam Malik, Umar Wirahadikusumah, Sudharmono, Try Sutrisno, B. J. Habibie (who succeeded Suharto in 1998), Megawati Sukarnoputri (who succeeded Abdurrahman Wahid in 2001), Hamzah Haz, Jusuf Kalla, Boediono, and Ma'ruf Amin. Since 20 October 2024 the vice president is Gibran Rakabuming Raka, serving under President Prabowo Subianto.

Election

Since 2004 the president and vice president have been elected directly as a joint ticket by popular vote, requiring an absolute majority with regional distribution thresholds, with a runoff between the top two tickets if no ticket achieves the required majority in the first round.
