Arctic Slope Regional Corporation

The Arctic Slope Regional Corporation (ASRC) is one of thirteen Alaska Native regional corporations established under the Alaska Native Claims Settlement Act of 1971 (ANCSA). It represents the Iñupiat people of Alaska's North Slope and is headquartered in Utqiaġvik (formerly Barrow), the northernmost community in the United States.

Founded1972 (under ANCSA)
HeadquartersUtqiaġvik, Alaska, U.S.
Area servedNorth Slope Borough region
ShareholdersRoughly 13,000–14,000 Iñupiat shareholders

Overview

Under ANCSA, ASRC received title to approximately 5 million acres of land on the North Slope, together with monetary settlement funds. It has grown into one of the largest Alaska-owned companies, with operations in energy support services, petroleum refining and marketing, construction, government services and resource development, and it is a significant employer across the region. ASRC advocates for the interests of its Iñupiat shareholders, including subsistence rights and responsible development of North Slope resources.
