History of Guyana

The history of Guyana covers the Indigenous peoples of the Guiana coast, Dutch and British plantation colonies built on enslaved and indentured labour, and independence in 1966.

Colonial period

The Dutch established trading posts and colonies such as Essequibo and Demerara in the 17th century. Britain took control in the early 19th century, uniting the colonies as British Guiana in 1831. After abolition, indentured workers arrived from India, Portugal, and China.

Independence

Guyana became independent on 26 May 1966 and a republic in 1970. Politics was dominated for decades by Forbes Burnham's PNC and its socialist "co-operative republic", followed by economic liberalisation and, more recently, major offshore oil production beginning in 2019.
