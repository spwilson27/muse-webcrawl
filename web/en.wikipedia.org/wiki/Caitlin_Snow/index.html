Dr. Caitlin Snow is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. She is the third character to take up the mantle of Killer Frost, succeeding Crystal Frost and Louise Lincoln, though later incarnations — principally the Arrowverse television version — have recharacterised her as a hero who struggles against, and eventually separates from, a colder alter ego, going by the name Frost.

Publication history and biography

Introduced in the New 52 relaunch, Caitlin Snow was a brilliant young scientist at S.T.A.R. Labs' Outpost #72 in the Arctic, sent to work on a thermodynamic engine. When H.I.V.E. agents attacked, the engine's coolant system ruptured; the accident fused her body with ice, transforming her into a heat vampire who must absorb warmth to survive — killing in the process — and setting her against Firestorm, whose nuclear heat she craved. Later comics softened the character into an anti-hero and Justice League member.

In other media

Danielle Panabaker portrayed Caitlin Snow as a main character throughout The Flash (2014–2023) in the Arrowverse, where Snow is the kind-hearted bioengineer of Team Flash whose repressed cryokinetic powers emerge as the dissociated personality "Killer Frost," later simply Frost, eventually becoming a distinct person in her own right. The character's popularity in this form has fed back into the comics.
