Instax

Instax
Brand: Fujifilm
Introduced: 1998
Type: Instant film and cameras

Instax (stylised as instax) is a brand of instant still cameras and instant films marketed by Fujifilm. First launched in 1998 as the Instax Mini system, it grew into a family of analogue and hybrid digital instant cameras whose prints develop within minutes after exposure.

History

Fujifilm had produced instant films since the early 1980s, compatible at times with Polaroid systems. After Polaroid's decline, Fujifilm expanded its own Instax line worldwide. The colourful Instax Mini cameras became especially popular in Japan and then internationally in the 2010s, driven by demand for tangible photographs and social use at events.

Film formats

Instax Mini film produces credit-card-sized images. Instax Wide gives larger prints, and Instax Square uses a square format. Hybrid models, such as the Mini Evo, let users preview and select images on a screen before printing, and can also print images from smartphones via companion printers such as the Instax Link series.

Cameras

Models have included the Mini 8, 9, 11 and 12, the Wide 300, the Square SQ series, and hybrid cameras combining digital sensors with instant printing. Many special editions and colour variants have been released.
