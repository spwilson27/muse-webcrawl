Spoleto Festival USA

Founded1977
FounderGian Carlo Menotti
LocationCharleston, South Carolina, U.S.
Duration17 days, late May to early June

Spoleto Festival USA is a performing arts festival held annually in Charleston, South Carolina. Founded in 1977 by the Italian-American composer Gian Carlo Menotti, together with Christopher Keene and others, it was created as the American counterpart to Menotti's Festival dei Due Mondi (Festival of Two Worlds) in Spoleto, Italy. Charleston was chosen for its historic architecture, intimate scale and abundance of theatres, churches and outdoor spaces suitable as venues.

For 17 days each spring the festival fills the city with performances of opera, theatre, dance, chamber and orchestral music, jazz and other forms—typically more than 150 performances in a season. The festival has a distinguished record of premieres and early presentations of major artists; landmark productions have included the American premieres of operas and the early work of companies and performers who went on to international careers. Notable productions have included The Rise and Fall of the City of Mahagonny, Tennessee Williams' The Notebook of Trigorin, and numerous works commissioned by the festival.

Piccolo Spoleto

Running concurrently, Piccolo Spoleto—organized by the City of Charleston's Office of Cultural Affairs—focuses on local and regional artists, with an emphasis on community and family programming, much of it free. Together the two festivals are a defining feature of Charleston's cultural calendar and a major contributor to its tourism economy.
