Bloomfield Road is a football stadium in Blackpool, Lancashire, England. It has been the home ground of Blackpool Football Club since 1901, when the club moved from Raikes Hall Gardens; the ground itself first hosted football in 1899. It takes its name from the road beside it.

History

The ground was developed piecemeal over the twentieth century, with large open terraces at both ends and a record attendance of 38,098 for a match against Wolverhampton Wanderers in 1955. Blackpool's finest era came in the 1950s with Stanley Matthews and the 1953 FA Cup Final victory. Following the Taylor Report the ground fell into disrepair under the Oyston ownership, and its capacity was reduced severely. Redevelopment began in 2000 with new stands on the north and west sides; the South Stand (Jimmy Armfield Stand) opened in 2010, and a temporary East Stand hosted supporters during the club's 2010–11 Premier League season. A permanent East Stand remains a long-standing ambition.

The stadium today

Bloomfield Road has four stands: the Sir Stanley Matthews West Stand, the Mortensen Kop (North Stand), the Jimmy Armfield South Stand and a temporary East Stand. Its current capacity is around 16,600. The ground also houses a statue of Jimmy Armfield and a memorial to the club's 1953 FA Cup-winning side.
