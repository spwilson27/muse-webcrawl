List of international cricket hat-tricks

A hat-trick in cricket occurs when a bowler takes three wickets with three consecutive deliveries. Hat-tricks are rare in all formats of international cricket, and rarer still to involve the first three balls of a spell or an innings.

T20 International hat-tricks (selected)

Brett Lee took the first T20I hat-trick, against Bangladesh in 2007. Lasith Malinga, Rashid Khan, Curtis Campher (four wickets in four balls at the 2021 T20 World Cup) and Jason Holder are among the bowlers to have achieved the feat in the shortest format.
