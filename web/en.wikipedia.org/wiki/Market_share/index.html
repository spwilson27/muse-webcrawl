Market share

Market share is the percentage of a market (in units or revenue) accounted for by a particular firm or product. It is calculated as the company's sales over a period divided by total sales of the industry over the same period, multiplied by 100.

Significance

Increasing market share is a common business objective because larger share is associated with economies of scale, greater bargaining power with suppliers and distributors, and often higher profitability. Market share figures depend heavily on how the relevant market is defined.

Measurement

Share can be measured by unit volume or by value; the two can differ significantly where competitors' prices differ. Analysts also track share of the "served available market" and relative share — a firm's share divided by that of its largest competitor.
