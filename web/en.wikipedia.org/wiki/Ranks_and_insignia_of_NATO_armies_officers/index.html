Ranks and insignia of NATO armies officers

The North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) maintains a standard ranking scale, laid down in STANAG 2116, so that the officer ranks of member states' armies can be compared. Ranks are assigned codes from OF-1 (junior officers) to OF-10 (the highest, generally reserved for wartime supreme commanders or heads of state).

Use

The system allows officers of different nations serving in combined NATO commands to know their relative seniority. Insignia vary by country — stars, pips, crowns, stripes and sleeve lace are all used — but the underlying rank equivalence is standardised. Similar scales exist for naval and air force officers and for other ranks (OR-1 to OR-9).
