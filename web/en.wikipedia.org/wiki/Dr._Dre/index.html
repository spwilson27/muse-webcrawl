Andre Romell Young (born February 18, 1965), known professionally as Dr. Dre, is an American rapper, record producer, record executive and entrepreneur. He is regarded as one of the most influential figures in hip hop, credited with popularising West Coast G-funk and launching the careers of Snoop Dogg, Eminem and 50 Cent.


Dr. Dre, producer and co-founder of Beats Electronics.

Career

Dre began as a DJ and member of the World Class Wreckin' Cru before co-founding N.W.A, whose album Straight Outta Compton (1988) made him a star producer. After leaving Ruthless Records he co-founded Death Row Records and released his solo debut The Chronic (1992), a landmark of G-funk. In 1996 he founded Aftermath Entertainment, signing Eminem (1998) and 50 Cent, and released 2001 (1999); a third album, Compton, followed in 2015.

Business ventures

In 2006 Dre co-founded Beats Electronics with Jimmy Iovine; Apple acquired the company in 2014 for about $3 billion, making Dre one of the wealthiest figures in music. He headlined the Super Bowl LVI halftime show in 2022, and the Grammy Awards named their Global Impact Award after him in 2023.
