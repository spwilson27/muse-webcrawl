The World's 50 Best Restaurants is an annual list and awards ceremony, first published in 2002 by the British magazine Restaurant and now organised by William Reed. The ranking is based on votes from an "Academy" of over a thousand chefs, restaurateurs, food writers, and travelling gourmets, and has become one of the most influential — and debated — measures of prestige in fine dining.

History

The list began as a feature in Restaurant magazine compiled with modest ambitions, but quickly grew into a gala event rivalling the Michelin Guide in publicity. Ferran Adrià's El Bulli in Spain headed the list a record five times (2002, 2006–2009), and René Redzepi's Noma in Copenhagen matched that feat (2010–2012, 2014, 2021). Other number ones include The French Laundry, The Fat Duck, El Celler de Can Roca, Osteria Francescana, Mirazur, Geranium, and Central in Lima, which topped the 2023 list.

Methodology

The world is divided into voting regions, each chaired by an Academy chair who selects panellists. Voters name ten restaurants where they have eaten in the previous 18 months, ranked in preference order; at least four choices must be outside their home region. There are no fixed criteria and no requirement that a restaurant meet any standard beyond the voter's judgement. Since 2019, former number-one restaurants are retired into a separate "Best of the Best" category and are no longer eligible for the main list.

Regional lists and related awards

The brand has expanded into Asia's 50 Best Restaurants (since 2013), Latin America's 50 Best (since 2013), the Middle East & North Africa's 50 Best, and North America's 50 Best, alongside the World's 50 Best Bars. Individual awards include World's Best Female Chef and the One To Watch award.

Criticism

Critics have charged the list with Eurocentrism, susceptibility to lobbying and free-meal campaigning, and a bias toward expensive tasting-menu formats; French commentators in particular launched the rival La Liste in 2015. Supporters counter that it recognises chefs Michelin overlooks and has helped make gastronomy a global conversation.
