Family history

Family history is a microhistorical field and a subdiscipline of history and genealogy that studies the composition and history of families across time and space, using censuses, parish records, wills, oral histories, and photographs.

Genealogy vs family history

Genealogy traces lines of descent and builds family trees; family history places those families in social, economic and cultural context — occupations, migration, household structure, and daily life.

Sources and methods

Researchers use civil registration, church records, military files, newspapers, and increasingly DNA testing and online platforms such as Ancestry.com. Historical demography (e.g. the Cambridge Group, Louis Henry) pioneered family reconstitution.

Significance

Family history is one of the most popular hobbies worldwide, and medical family history helps assess inherited disease risk. See archives and heraldry.
