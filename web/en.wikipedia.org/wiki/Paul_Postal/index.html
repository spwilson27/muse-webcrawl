Paul Postal

Paul Martin Postal (born November 10, 1936) is an American linguist known for his work in generative grammar and syntactic theory, and for his sharp critiques of linguistic methodology.

BornNovember 10, 1936
FieldsLinguistics, syntax
Known forRelational grammar; critique of construction grammar

Career

Postal earned his PhD at MIT and worked for many years at IBM's Thomas J. Watson Research Center. With George Lakoff and John Robert Ross he helped develop generative semantics in the late 1960s. He later became a leading figure, with David Perlmutter, in relational grammar.

Selected works

His books include On Raising (1974), Arc Pair Grammar (with Perlmutter, 1983) and Skeatical Linguistic Essays (2004), a collection attacking fashionable but, in his view, methodologically weak approaches in linguistics.
