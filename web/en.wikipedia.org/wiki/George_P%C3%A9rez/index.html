George Pérez (9 June 1954 – 6 May 2022) was an American comic book artist and writer, celebrated for his detailed, dynamic artwork on superhero team books for Marvel Comics and DC Comics.

Career

Born in the Bronx to Puerto Rican parents, Pérez began at Marvel in the 1970s on titles such as The Avengers and Fantastic Four. At DC he co-created The New Teen Titans (1980) with writer Marv Wolfman, one of the era's best-selling series, and illustrated the landmark crossover Crisis on Infinite Earths (1985). He relaunched Wonder Woman as writer-artist in 1987, reshaping the character's mythology.

Later work and legacy

Pérez returned to Marvel for a celebrated Avengers run with Kurt Busiek and drew the intercompany crossover JLA/Avengers (2003). He retired in 2019 due to ill health and died of pancreatic cancer in 2022. His intricate crowd scenes and storytelling made him one of the most influential superhero artists of his generation.
