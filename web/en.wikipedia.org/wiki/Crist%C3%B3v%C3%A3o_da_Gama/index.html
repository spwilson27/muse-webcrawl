Cristóvão da Gama

Bornc. 1516, Évora, Portugal
Died29 August 1542, Wofla, Ethiopia
FatherVasco da Gama

Cristóvão da Gama (c. 1516 – 29 August 1542) was a Portuguese explorer and soldier, the youngest son of Vasco da Gama. He is best known for leading a small Portuguese expeditionary force that aided the Ethiopian Empire against the invading Adal Sultanate.

Ethiopian–Adal war

In 1541 Gama landed at Massawa with about 400 musketeers in response to Ethiopian emperor Gelawdewos's appeal for help against the forces of Imam Ahmad ibn Ibrahim al-Ghazi. The Portuguese won early engagements but were overwhelmed at the Battle of Wofla in August 1542, where Gama was captured.

Death and legacy

Refusing to convert to Islam, Gama was executed on the Imam's orders. His brother Estêvão da Gama's threat of retaliation and the later death of the Imam at Wayna Daga in 1543 helped turn the war. Gama's story became one of the celebrated episodes of Portuguese expansion in the East.
