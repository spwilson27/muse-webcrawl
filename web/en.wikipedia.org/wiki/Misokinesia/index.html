Misokinesia

Misokinesia is a condition characterised by a strong negative emotional or physiological response to seeing another person's small, repetitive movements, such as fidgeting, foot-tapping, or leg-jiggling. The term means "hatred of movement" and is related to misophonia, which involves sensitivity to specific sounds.

Research

Research published in 2021 by Sumeet Jaswal and Todd Handy at the University of British Columbia found that roughly one-third of participants reported some degree of misokinesia sensitivity. Responses can include anxiety, irritation, anger, and difficulty concentrating. The phenomenon had received little scientific attention before that study despite anecdotal reports.
