Officer Candidate School

Officer Candidate School (OCS) is a type of military school in the United States that trains, assesses, and evaluates prospective commissioned officers. Each branch of the US Armed Forces operates its own OCS: the Army at Fort Moore (formerly Fort Benning), Georgia; the Navy at Naval Station Newport, Rhode Island; the Marine Corps at Quantico, Virginia; the Air Force through Officer Training School at Maxwell AFB, Alabama; and the Coast Guard at the Coast Guard Academy in New London, Connecticut.

Candidates are typically college graduates, either civilians or enlisted service members, who complete an intensive course of leadership, academics, and physical training—commonly 10 to 17 weeks—before commissioning as ensigns or second lieutenants.
