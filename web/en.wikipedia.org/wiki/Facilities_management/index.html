Facilities management or facility management (FM) is a professional management discipline focused on the efficient and effective delivery of support services for the organisations that it serves. The International Organization for Standardization (ISO) defines facility management as the "organizational function which integrates people, place and process within the built environment with the purpose of improving the quality of life of people and the productivity of the core business."

Scope

Facilities management is generally divided into two categories. "Hard FM" covers physical building services such as heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC), electrical and mechanical maintenance, plumbing, fire safety systems and structural repairs. "Soft FM" covers services that make the workplace more pleasant or secure, including cleaning, catering, security, reception, mail handling, waste management and grounds maintenance.

Roles and responsibilities

A facilities manager is responsible for ensuring that buildings and their services meet the needs of the people who work in them. Duties include managing budgets, negotiating contracts with service providers, ensuring compliance with health and safety legislation, coordinating building maintenance and repairs, space planning, and managing moves and refurbishments. In larger organisations the function may be led by a head of facilities or director of real estate and workplace services.

Delivery models

FM services may be delivered in-house, outsourced to a single specialist provider, or bundled under a total facilities management contract with one integrator. Many organisations use computer-aided facility management (CAFM) or integrated workplace management systems (IWMS) to track assets, work orders, and space utilisation. Building information modeling (BIM) data is increasingly handed over to FM teams to support maintenance across the building life cycle.

Standards and profession

The ISO 41000 family of standards, published from 2017, established an international vocabulary and management-system framework for the discipline, with ISO 41001 setting requirements for facility management systems. Professional bodies include the International Facility Management Association (IFMA), founded in 1980, and the Institute of Workplace and Facilities Management (IWFM) in the United Kingdom.
