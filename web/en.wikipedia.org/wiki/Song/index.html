Song


A song is a musical composition for the human voice, usually with lyrics.

A song is a musical composition intended to be performed by the human voice, typically with lyrics. Songs exist in every known culture and range from folk songs passed down orally to composed art songs and commercial popular songs.

Forms

Songs commonly combine melody, harmony, rhythm, and text. Recurring structures include strophic form (the same music for each verse), verse–chorus form in popular music, and through-composed songs in the art-song tradition. Genres of song include hymns, lullabies, work songs, ballads, and the German Lied.

History

Song is among the oldest forms of music-making. Written traditions include the songs of troubadours and minnesingers in the Middle Ages, Renaissance madrigals and lute songs, and the 19th-century art song of Schubert and others. Recording technology in the 20th century made the popular song a central commercial art form worldwide.
