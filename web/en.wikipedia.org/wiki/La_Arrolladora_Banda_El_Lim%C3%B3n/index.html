La Arrolladora Banda El Limón

La Arrolladora Banda El Limón is a Mexican banda ensemble from El Limón de los Peraza, Sinaloa. Founded by René Camacho, the group emerged from the earlier Banda El Limón and became one of the most successful acts in regional Mexican music, known for its large brass-and-woodwind sound and romantic ballads alongside traditional rancheras and corridos.

History

René Camacho founded Banda El Limón in the 1960s. After a dispute with Salvador Lizárraga, who continued with La Original Banda El Limón, Camacho formed La Arrolladora Banda El Limón in the late 1990s. The band went on to win multiple Latin Grammy Awards, including for Best Banda Album.

Style

The ensemble features a large line-up of clarinets, trumpets, trombones, tuba, and percussion, with vocalists performing songs of love and heartbreak that have made songs such as "Ya es muy tarde" and "El ruido de tus zapatos" major hits.
