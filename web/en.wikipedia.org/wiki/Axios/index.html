Axios

Axios
Founded2016
FoundersJim VandeHei; Mike Allen; Roy Schwartz
OwnerCox Enterprises (from 2022)
[Image: The Axios logo and its Smart Brevity story format]

Axios is an American digital news company founded in 2016 by Politico co-founders Jim VandeHei and Mike Allen with Roy Schwartz, launched in 2017. It popularised 'Smart Brevity' — short, bullet-pointed stories built around a 'Why it matters' line — and a newsletter-first model spanning politics, business, technology and local news.

Growth

Axios expanded into local newsletters in dozens of US cities, podcasts and an HBO television show, and sold to Cox Enterprises in 2022 in a deal valuing it at about $525 million, as remembered.
