Regional Mexican


Performers of mariachi and banda, two regional Mexican styles.

Regional Mexican is an umbrella term and radio/music-industry format in the United States and Mexico encompassing the various regional styles of Mexican country and folk music. It groups together genres that share roots in the music of rural Mexico but differ in instrumentation and region.

Subgenres

  • Mariachi — violins, trumpets, and guitars from Jalisco, associated with songs such as rancheras.
  • Banda — large brass ensembles from Sinaloa.
  • Norteño — accordion and bajo sexto from northern Mexico and Texas, including conjunto.
  • Duranguense, grupero, tierra caliente, and sierreño.
  • Corridos — narrative ballads, including modern corridos tumbados that fuse the form with hip-hop, popularised in the 2020s by artists such as Peso Pluma and Natanael Cano.

Industry significance

Regional Mexican is consistently the largest Spanish-language radio format in the United States. The 2020s saw the genre achieve unprecedented global streaming success, with corridos tumbados and sierreño acts reaching the top of international charts.
