Xenomorph

The Xenomorph (also known as Xenomorph XX121) is a fictional endoparasitoid extraterrestrial species and the titular antagonist of the Alien film series. The species made its debut in the film Alien (1979) and reappeared in the sequels Aliens (1986), Alien 3 (1992), and Alien Resurrection (1997).

Xenomorph
First appearance: Alien (1979)
Created by: Dan O'Bannon, Ronald Shusett, H. R. Giger
Portrayed by: Bolaji Badejo (1979)

Concept and creation

The creature was designed by Swiss artist H. R. Giger, whose biomechanical aesthetic combined organic and mechanical forms. Its life cycle — egg, facehugger, chestburster, adult — was devised by screenwriters Dan O'Bannon and Ronald Shusett.

Characteristics

Xenomorphs have an elongated cylindrical skull, a pharyngeal jaw, acidic blood, and a segmented tail tipped with a blade. They exhibit a eusocial structure with queens, drones, and warriors, analogous to ants and termites. The adult takes on traits of its host through a process sometimes called DNA reflex.

Life cycle

A queen lays eggs containing facehuggers, which implant an embryo in a host. The embryo erupts from the host's chest, killing it, and rapidly matures into an adult.

Cultural impact

The Xenomorph is widely regarded as one of cinema's greatest monsters and has appeared in games, comics, and the crossover Alien vs. Predator franchise.
