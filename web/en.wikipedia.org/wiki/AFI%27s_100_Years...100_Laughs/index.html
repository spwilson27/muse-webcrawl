AFI's 100 Years...100 Laughs

AFI's 100 Years...100 Laughs
Presented by: American Film Institute
Year: 2000
Part of: AFI 100 Years... series
Number 1 film: Some Like It Hot (1959)

AFI's 100 Years...100 Laughs is a list of the 100 funniest American films, presented by the American Film Institute (AFI) in 2000 as part of its AFI 100 Years... series celebrating the centennial of American cinema. The list was unveiled in a CBS television special hosted by Drew Barrymore.

The list

Some Like It Hot (1959), directed by Billy Wilder, was ranked number one. Wilder was the most represented director on the list. The top ten also included Tootsie, Dr. Strangelove, Annie Hall, Duck Soup, Blazing Saddles, M*A*S*H, It Happened One Night, The Graduate and Airplane!

Selection

The hundred films were chosen from a ballot of 500 nominated comedies distributed to a jury of filmmakers, critics, historians and other members of the film community, who judged each film by its total comedic impact and legacy.
