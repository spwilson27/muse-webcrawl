Reno Air Races

First held1964
ClassesUnlimited, Jet, Sport, T-6, Formula One, Biplane, STOL Drag

The Reno Air Races, officially the National Championship Air Races, were a multi-day air racing event held each September at Reno Stead Airport in Reno, Nevada, from 1964 to 2023. Aircraft raced head-to-head around closed pylon courses at speeds exceeding 500 mph in the Unlimited class, dominated by modified Second World War fighters such as the P-51 Mustang and Hawker Sea Fury.

History

Bill Stead organized the first races in 1964 at a small dirt strip, reviving the tradition of the pre-war Cleveland National Air Races. The event grew into the largest air racing meeting in the world, drawing hundreds of thousands of spectators and incorporating airshow performances, including military demonstration teams.

2011 accident and final years

In 2011 the modified P-51 "The Galloping Ghost" crashed into the crowd, killing the pilot and ten spectators — the deadliest air racing accident in U.S. history — leading to major safety changes. Organizers announced that 2023 would be the final edition at Stead Airport, citing growth of the surrounding community, with plans to relocate the championship to a new venue.
