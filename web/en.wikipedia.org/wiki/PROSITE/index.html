PROSITE

PROSITE is a database of protein families, domains, and functional sites, created in 1988 by Amos Bairoch and maintained by the SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics. It documents biologically significant sequence patterns and profiles.

Content

Entries describe patterns written as regular expressions and, later, generalised profiles built from multiple alignments. Each entry is accompanied by documentation explaining the family or site and its biological role.

Use

Researchers scan new protein sequences against PROSITE to infer function and family membership; the resource is integrated into InterPro and the UniProt annotation pipeline.
