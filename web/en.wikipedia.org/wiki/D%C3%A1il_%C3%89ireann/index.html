Dáil Éireann

Dáil Éireann
Type: Lower house of the Oireachtas
Seats: 158 (increased to 174 after the 2024 election)
Meeting place: Leinster House, Dublin
Presiding officer: Ceann Comhairle

Dáil Éireann is the lower house of the Oireachtas, the legislature of Ireland. It is directly elected at least once every five years under the single transferable vote form of proportional representation.

Composition and powers

Members, known as Teachtaí Dála (TDs), are elected from multi-seat constituencies. The Dáil is the dominant house: it nominates the Taoiseach (head of government), approves the government, and controls finance legislation. The Seanad (upper house) can delay but generally not block most bills.

History

The first Dáil assembled in Dublin in January 1919, when Sinn Féin deputies elected to Westminster declared an Irish Republic. The modern Dáil operates under the 1937 Constitution of Ireland and sits in Leinster House.
