An American Werewolf in London

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

An American Werewolf in London is a 1981 comedy horror film written and directed by John Landis. It stars David Naughton, Jenny Agutter, and Griffin Dunne. The film tells the story of two American backpackers in England who are attacked by a werewolf; one is killed, and the survivor begins transforming under the full moon.

Directed by: John Landis
Starring: David Naughton, Jenny Agutter, Griffin Dunne
Released: 1981
Budget: about $5.8 million
Box office: about $62 million

Plot

David Kessler and Jack Goodman are attacked on the Yorkshire moors after leaving a pub called The Slaughtered Lamb. Jack is killed; David wakes in a London hospital, haunted by visions of his undead friend, who warns that David is now a werewolf and must take his own life to end the curse and free the victims' souls. Under the full moon David transforms and goes on a rampage through London, culminating in a chaotic finale in Piccadilly Circus.

Reception and legacy

The film was a commercial and critical success, praised for balancing horror and dark comedy. Rick Baker's groundbreaking transformation sequence won the first Academy Award for Best Makeup ever presented. The film spawned a 1997 sequel, An American Werewolf in Paris, and remains a landmark of practical effects and horror cinema.
