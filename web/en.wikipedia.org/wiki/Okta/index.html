Okta

Founded2009

IndustryIdentity and access management software

Okta, Inc. is an American identity and access management company headquartered in San Francisco, California. It provides cloud-based software that helps organizations manage and secure user authentication into applications, websites, and devices through single sign-on, multi-factor authentication, and lifecycle management.

History

Okta was founded in 2009 by Todd McKinnon and Frederic Kerrest, who had previously worked together at Salesforce. The company went public on the Nasdaq in 2017. In 2021 Okta acquired Auth0, a competing identity platform, in a deal valued at about $6.5 billion.

Products

Its Workforce Identity and Customer Identity (CIAM) clouds offer single sign-on, adaptive MFA, universal directory, API access management, and identity governance to thousands of enterprise customers.
