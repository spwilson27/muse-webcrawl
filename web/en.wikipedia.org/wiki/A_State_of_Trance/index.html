A State of Trance (ASOT) is a Dutch trance music radio show hosted by DJ and producer Armin van Buuren (with Ruben de Ronde as a regular co-host). First aired in May 2001 on Dutch radio, the weekly two-hour program features new trance and progressive releases, guest mixes, and recurring segments such as the Tune of the Week and the ASOT Radio Classic. It claims a listenership of tens of millions across dozens of countries via syndication and streaming.

Milestones and events

Every 50th episode—and later each 100-episode milestone—has been celebrated with large arena events and world tours; the 500th, 600th, and 1000th episodes drew multi-city tours and festival-scale productions. The 1,000th episode aired in January 2021. The brand also issued annual compilation albums ("A State of Trance" year mixes) and, in the 2010s, ASOT festival editions in Utrecht and elsewhere.

Significance

ASOT is the longest-running and most influential radio program in trance music, serving as a central promotional platform for the genre and a launchpad for new artists. Its weekly tracklists are closely followed by fans, and the show's "Tune of the Year" listener vote is a notable annual fixture of the scene.
