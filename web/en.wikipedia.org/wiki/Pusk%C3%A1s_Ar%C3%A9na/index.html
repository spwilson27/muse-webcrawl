The Puskás Aréna is a football stadium in the 14th district (Zugló) of Budapest, Hungary. Built on the site of the former Ferenc Puskás Stadium, it is named after Hungary's most famous footballer, Ferenc Puskás. Construction began in 2017 and the stadium opened on 15 November 2019 with a friendly match between Hungary and Uruguay.

Tournaments

The stadium was a venue for UEFA Euro 2020, hosting group-stage matches and a round-of-16 game, and was notable as the only venue of the tournament to operate at full capacity. It also hosted the 2023 UEFA Europa League final and was selected to host the 2026 UEFA Champions League final.

Design

Designed by architect György Skardelli, the arena retains elements recalling the old stadium, including the distinctive tower building, and is the largest stadium in Hungary.
