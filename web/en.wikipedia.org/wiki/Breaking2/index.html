Breaking2

Breaking2
Organiser: Nike
Date: 6 May 2017
Venue: Autodromo Nazionale Monza, Italy
Best time: Eliud Kipchoge, 2:00:25
Kipchoge during the Breaking2 attempt, Monza, 2017

Breaking2 was a project by Nike to break the two-hour barrier for the marathon. It culminated on 6 May 2017 in an exhibition event at the Autodromo Nazionale Monza motor racing circuit in Italy, where Kenyan runner Eliud Kipchoge completed the marathon distance in 2:00:25 — the fastest marathon-distance time ever recorded at that point, though not eligible as a world record. The other selected athletes, Zersenay Tadese and Lelisa Desisa, finished in 2:06:51 and 2:14:10 respectively.

The attempt

The event was engineered to optimise every variable: a rotating team of pacemakers ran in an arrow formation to shield the athletes from wind, drinks were delivered by moped to avoid breaking stride, and the runners wore Nike's Zoom Vaporfly Elite shoes with a carbon-fibre plate. Kipchoge's time improved the then world record (2:02:57 by Dennis Kimetto) by more than two minutes, but because of the in-and-out pacemakers and other assistance it did not count for record purposes. Two years later, in the Ineos 1:59 Challenge in Vienna, Kipchoge went further, running 1:59:40.2 in a similarly assisted event.
