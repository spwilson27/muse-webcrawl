DXCC

DXCC stands for DX Century Club, an amateur radio award administered by the American Radio Relay League (ARRL). It is granted to operators who can prove two-way contact (a "QSO") with amateur stations in at least 100 distinct "DXCC entities" and who have received confirmation, traditionally by QSL cards and nowadays also via Logbook of The World (LoTW).

Entities

The DXCC List contains roughly 340 current entities. An entity is usually a country, but geographically or politically separate territories — such as islands, overseas possessions, or separated enclaves — may count separately under the DXCC rules. Deleted entities, which no longer meet the criteria, still count toward an operator's lifetime totals.

Awards

Beyond the basic mixed-mode award, DXCC is issued for single modes (CW, phone, digital) and single bands; the DXCC Honor Roll recognises those who have confirmed nearly all current entities, and the ultimate achievement, #1 Honor Roll, requires all of them. "DXpeditions" — trips mounted specifically to activate rare entities — are a central part of the DXCC culture.
