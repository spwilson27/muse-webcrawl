Erika Leonard, known by her pen name E. L. James (born Erika Mitchell, 7 March 1963), is a British author. She wrote the bestselling erotic romance trilogy Fifty Shades of Grey, Fifty Shades Darker and Fifty Shades Freed, along with companion novels told from the male protagonist's perspective and the standalone romances The Mister (2019) and The Missus (2023).

Career

James worked as a television executive before writing fiction. Writing under the username "Snowqueens Icedragon", she first published what became Fifty Shades of Grey as Twilight fan fiction titled Master of the Universe. It was released as an e-book and print-on-demand title in 2011 by a small Australian publisher, then acquired by Vintage Books in 2012.

Fifty Shades

The trilogy follows the relationship between graduate Anastasia Steele and wealthy businessman Christian Grey, whose preferences include bondage and discipline. The books became a publishing phenomenon, selling more than 150 million copies worldwide and topping bestseller lists in numerous countries, and were adapted into a film trilogy released between 2015 and 2018. The series received mixed-to-negative critical reviews for its prose and its depiction of relationships, but is credited with expanding the mainstream market for erotic fiction by women and with demonstrating the commercial power of fan-fiction communities and e-book word of mouth.

Personal life

James lives in London with her husband, screenwriter Niall Leonard, and their two sons. In 2012 Time magazine named her one of its 100 most influential people in the world.
