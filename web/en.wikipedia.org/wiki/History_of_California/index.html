History of California

The history of California spans thousands of years of Indigenous habitation, Spanish and Mexican rule, and statehood in the United States from 1850 to the present.

Early and colonial periods

Indigenous peoples lived in California for millennia before European contact. Spanish colonisation began in 1769 with missions and presidios along the coast; California passed to Mexico in 1821.

American statehood and growth

The United States acquired California in 1848 after the Mexican–American War. The Gold Rush of 1849 brought a population explosion, and California entered the Union in 1850. Railroads, agriculture, oil, Hollywood, and later the technology industry shaped its growth into the most populous US state.
