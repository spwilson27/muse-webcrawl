Thought for the Day is a daily religious and ethical reflection broadcast on BBC Radio 4's Today programme each weekday and Saturday morning. Lasting about three minutes, it offers a faith perspective on current events and contemporary life.

History

The slot descends from Lift Up Your Hearts, which began on the BBC Home Service in 1939 and featured speakers from Christian denominations. It took the name Thought for the Day in 1970. Speakers have expanded to include representatives of the world's major faiths — among them Jonathan Sacks, Rowan Williams, and Indarjit Singh — writing and presenting in rotation.

Debate

The segment is periodically criticised by secular and humanist groups for excluding non-religious contributors, and the BBC has defended it as a distinctively religious slot within a wider range of programming, pointing to alternatives such as Radio 4 Extra's Thought for the Day counterparts and humanist contributions in other programmes and nations' slots such as BBC Scotland's Thought for the Day alternatives.
