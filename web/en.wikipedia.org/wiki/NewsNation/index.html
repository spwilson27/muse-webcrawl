NewsNation is an American subscription television network owned by the Nexstar Media Group. The channel launched in its current news format on March 1, 2021, having previously been the entertainment network WGN America, itself a successor to the national superstation feed of Chicago's WGN-TV. NewsNation was built around a prime-time national newscast that debuted on September 1, 2020, marketed as a fact-based, unbiased alternative to the opinion-driven programming of the established cable news channels.

History

Nexstar, the largest owner of local television stations in the United States, announced in early 2020 that WGN America would launch a nightly three-hour prime-time newscast produced from WGN-TV's Chicago studios, drawing on the newsrooms of Nexstar's local stations nationwide. The programme, originally titled NewsNation Prime, premiered on September 1, 2020. On March 1, 2021, the entire channel was rebranded as NewsNation, and the news schedule expanded.

Programming

The schedule grew from prime time into mornings, daytime and weekends, with programmes including Morning in America, NewsNation Live, The Hill, Elizabeth Vargas Reports, Dan Abrams Live, Banfield and Cuomo, hosted by Chris Cuomo. In September 2023 the network expanded to a 24-hour news schedule on weekdays, adding syndicated and news programming in overnight hours. Despite critical attention, NewsNation's ratings remain well below those of Fox News, MSNBC and CNN.
