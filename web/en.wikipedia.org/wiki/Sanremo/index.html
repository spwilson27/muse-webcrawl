Sanremo (Italian: [sanˈrɛːmo]; Ligurian: Sanrémmo), historically also written San Remo, is a city and comune on the Mediterranean coast of Liguria in northwestern Italy, in the province of Imperia. Founded in Roman times, it is a famous resort on the Italian Riviera, celebrated for its mild climate, its flower-growing industry — for which it is called the "City of Flowers" (Città dei Fiori) — and for two internationally known events: the Sanremo Music Festival and the Milan–San Remo cycle race.

Geography and climate

Sanremo lies on a wide bay between Capo Nero and Capo Verde, sheltered by hills that give it an exceptionally mild microclimate for its latitude, with average January temperatures comparable to much more southerly locations. This climate made it a fashionable winter resort for European aristocracy in the 19th and early 20th centuries — visitors included Empress Maria Alexandrovna of Russia, to whom the Russian Orthodox church in the town is a memorial, and Alfred Nobel, who lived and died at Villa Nobel in 1896.

Culture and events

The Sanremo Music Festival (Festival della canzone italiana), held annually since 1951 at the Teatro Ariston, is Italy's most important popular song contest and the inspiration for the Eurovision Song Contest; its winner often represents Italy at Eurovision. In March, the Milan–San Remo one-day cycling race, the longest of professional cycling's five "Monuments" at around 300 km, traditionally finishes on the Via Roma after the climbs of the Cipressa and the Poggio. The town is also famous for its casino (opened 1905), its flower market supplying much of Italy's cut-flower trade, and Rallye Sanremo, long a round of the World Rally Championship.

Old town

The medieval old town, La Pigna ("the pine cone"), climbs a steep hill in a maze of covered lanes, arches and small squares, crowned by the 12th-century Cathedral of San Siro and the gardens of the Queen Elena park. Below it, the modern town centres on Corso Matteotti, with the Ariston theatre and shopping streets, and the port, Porto Sole and the old harbour.
