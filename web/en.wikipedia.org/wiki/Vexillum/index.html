Vexillum

The vexillum (plural vexilla) was a flag-like standard used by the Roman military: a square piece of cloth suspended from a horizontal crossbar attached to a spear or staff, in contrast to the solid eagle standard (aquila) of a legion. A detachment serving away from its parent unit was itself called a vexillatio, from the flag under which it served.

Uses

Vexilla bore the name and emblem of the legion or unit, often with "SPQR" and images such as the legion's zodiac sign. They marked rallying points in battle and were carried by a standard-bearer (the vexillarius). Red vexilla were also used as signal flags in naval and battlefield communication. The study of flags takes its name — vexillology — from this object, and military colours and guidons descend partly from the form.
