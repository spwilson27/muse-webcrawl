Verse

Verse is a form of writing arranged in lines, typically metrical, as distinct from prose. The term is also used for a single line of poetry, a stanza, or a short numbered division of a chapter in scripture such as the Bible.

Poetic verse

Verse composition is governed by metre — patterns of stressed and unstressed syllables — in traditions such as the classical hexameter, English iambic pentameter and the syllabic metres of French and Japanese poetry. Blank verse is unrhymed verse in a regular metre, used by Shakespeare and Milton; free verse abandons regular metre and rhyme while retaining lineation.

Other senses

In popular music, a verse is a section of a song with the same melody but changing lyrics, alternating with the chorus. In scripture, verses are the numbered subdivisions of chapters, a system standardised in the 16th century.
