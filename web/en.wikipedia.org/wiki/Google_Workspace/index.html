Google Workspace

[Image: Google Workspace]

Google Workspace is a collection of cloud computing, productivity and collaboration tools, software and products developed and marketed by Google. It consists of Gmail, Contacts, Calendar, Meet and Chat for communication; Drive for storage; and the Google Docs Editors suite for content creation.

History

The service was first launched in February 2006 as Gmail for Your Domain, became Google Apps, and was rebranded as G Suite in 2016. On 6 October 2020 it was renamed Google Workspace, with redesigned icons and deeper integration between applications. In 2021 Google announced that Workspace would be available to all users with a Google account, including consumers.

Editions

Workspace is offered in tiers such as Business Starter, Business Standard and Business Plus, as well as Enterprise editions and special versions for education and nonprofits. Paid plans add storage, administrative controls, and enhanced security features.
