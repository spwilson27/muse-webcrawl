Disney+ is an American subscription video on-demand over-the-top streaming service owned and operated by the Walt Disney Company through its Disney Entertainment division. Launched on 12 November 2019 in the United States, Canada and the Netherlands, it distributes films and television series produced by Walt Disney Studios and Walt Disney Television, with dedicated content hubs for Disney, Pixar, Marvel, Star Wars, National Geographic, and — in most international markets — the general-entertainment Star brand.

History

Disney announced the service after ending its distribution deal with Netflix, building the platform on technology from BAMTech, in which it had acquired a majority stake. Disney+ reached 10 million subscribers on its first day and more than 100 million within about sixteen months, helped by hit originals such as The Mandalorian, the first live-action Star Wars series, and Marvel Studios series including WandaVision and Loki. An advertising-supported tier launched in late 2022, and Hulu content was progressively integrated into the service in the United States.

Content

The library centres on Disney's back catalogue, including animated classics, alongside Pixar films, the Marvel Cinematic Universe and the Star Wars saga. Original programming spans series, films and documentaries; some theatrical releases, such as Mulan (2020), premiered on the service via a Premier Access surcharge during the COVID-19 pandemic.
