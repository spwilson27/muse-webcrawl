Ostan

An ostan (Persian: استان, romanised ostān; plural استان‌ها ostānhā) is a first-level administrative subdivision of Iran, equivalent to a province. As of 2014, Iran is divided into 31 ostans. Each ostan is governed from a local centre, usually the largest local city, which is referred to as the capital (مرکز, markaz) of that province.

Administration

Each ostan is headed by a governor-general (استاندار, ostāndār), appointed by the Minister of the Interior subject to approval of the cabinet. The ostans are subdivided into counties called shahrestan (شهرستان), which are in turn divided into districts (bakhsh) and rural districts (dehestan).

History

Iran (Persia) was divided into provinces for much of its imperial history, but the modern system derives from reforms of the 19th and 20th centuries. The 1937 territorial divisions law divided the country into ten provinces; the number has since grown through successive subdivisions to 31. The most recent ostan created was Alborz Province, separated from Tehran Province in 2010 with Karaj as its capital.

Largest provinces

By area, the largest ostans include Kerman, Sistan and Baluchestan, South Khorasan, Fars, Razavi Khorasan and Isfahan. By population, Tehran Province is the largest, followed by Razavi Khorasan, Isfahan, Fars and Khuzestan.
