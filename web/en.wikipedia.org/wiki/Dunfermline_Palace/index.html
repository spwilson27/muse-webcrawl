Dunfermline Palace

Dunfermline Palace is a ruined former Scottish royal palace in Dunfermline, Fife. It occupies a site adjacent to Dunfermline Abbey and was a favoured residence of many Scottish monarchs. Charles I and Elizabeth of Bohemia were born here, making it the birthplace of the last British monarch born in Scotland until the modern era.

History

Dunfermline was a royal centre from the reign of Malcolm III and his queen, Saint Margaret. The palace developed from the abbey's guest house, linked to the abbey church by a gatehouse, the Pends. James VI granted the palace to his queen, Anne of Denmark; their children Elizabeth (1596), Charles (1600) and Robert were born here. After the Union of the Crowns in 1603 the palace fell into disuse.

Remains

The surviving ruins include the great south wall of the refectory range overlooking the Firth of Forth, the kitchen, and the palace gateway. The site is managed together with Dunfermline Abbey by Historic Environment Scotland.
