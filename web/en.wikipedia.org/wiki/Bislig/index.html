Bislig

Bislig, officially the City of Bislig, is a 3rd-class component city in the province of Surigao del Sur, Philippines. According to the 2020 census, it has a population of about 99,000 people. It has sometimes been called the "Booming City on the Bay".

Geography and economy

Bislig lies on the eastern coast of Mindanao facing Bislig Bay and the Pacific Ocean. Its economy historically rested on logging and paper production: the Paper Industries Corporation of the Philippines (PICOP) operated one of the largest paper mills in Southeast Asia in the city from the 1960s. Agriculture, fishing, and commerce are now leading sectors.

Tourism

The city's best-known attraction is Tinuy-an Falls, a wide, three-tiered waterfall on the Bislig River often called the "Little Niagara of the Philippines". The nearby Enchanted River in Hinatuan and the beaches of Lawigan are also popular with visitors.
