Evan Clark Williams (born 31 March 1972) is an American technology entrepreneur who co-founded several major Internet companies. He created the blogging service Blogger, co-founded Twitter, and later founded the publishing platform Medium.

Career

Williams grew up on a farm in Nebraska and briefly attended the University of Nebraska before moving into technology work. In 1999 his company Pyra Labs launched Blogger, one of the earliest tools for publishing personal weblogs, which helped popularise the term "blogger". Google acquired Blogger in 2003, and Williams left the following year.

In 2006 he co-founded Obvious Corporation and, within it, the microblogging service Twitter, alongside Jack Dorsey, Biz Stone, and Noah Glass. Williams served as Twitter's chief executive from 2008 until 2010, when he stepped down and Dorsey later returned as CEO. In 2012 he launched Medium, an online publishing platform intended to encourage longer-form writing.
