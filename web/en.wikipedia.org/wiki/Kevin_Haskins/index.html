Kevin Haskins (born Kevin Michael Dompe, July 19, 1960) is an English drummer best known as a founding member of the gothic rock band Bauhaus, and subsequently of Tones on Tail and Love and Rockets. His inventive drumming — blending dub, funk and tribal rhythms with rock — helped define the sound of post-punk and gothic rock. His older brother is bassist David J, with whom he has played in all three bands.

Career

Haskins formed Bauhaus in Northampton in 1978 with his brother David J, guitarist Daniel Ash and singer Peter Murphy. The band's 1979 debut single "Bela Lugosi's Dead" became a gothic rock anthem, and albums such as In the Flat Field (1980) and Mask (1981) established their reputation before the group split in 1983. Haskins and Ash then formed Tones on Tail, and in 1985 Haskins, Ash and David J reunited (without Murphy) as Love and Rockets, who achieved international success with the 1989 hit "So Alive".

Bauhaus reunited for tours in 1998 and 2005–2008 and released the album Go Away White in 2008. In 2017 Haskins formed Poptone with Daniel Ash, playing material from Bauhaus, Tones on Tail and Love and Rockets. Haskins has also composed music for film, television and video games.
