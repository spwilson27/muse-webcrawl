SPC Global

SPC Global Limited, trading as SPC (originally the Shepparton Preserving Company and later SPC Ardmona), is an Australian food company based in Shepparton, Victoria. Founded in 1917 by a group of fruit growers in the Goulburn Valley, it is Australia's largest processor of packaged fruit and vegetables, known for brands including SPC, Ardmona, Goulburn Valley, IXL and Taylors. Since 2024 the company has traded as SPC Global following a merger with the Original Juice Company and Nature One Dairy.

History

The Shepparton Preserving Company was established in 1917 when Goulburn Valley fruit growers formed a cooperative cannery to preserve surplus peaches, pears and apricots. The company grew into a major exporter and in 2002 merged with Ardmona to form SPC Ardmona. Coca-Cola Amatil acquired the business in 2005 for about $750 million.

In the 2010s SPC Ardmona came under severe pressure from cheap imports and supermarket pricing. In 2013 Woolworths' decision to source home-brand tomatoes elsewhere and the federal government's rejection of a request for $25 million in assistance drew national attention; in 2014 the Victorian government provided support and Woolworths signed a new supply deal after a public campaign. Coca-Cola Amatil sold SPC in 2019 to Shepparton Partners Collective for about $40 million, with plans to expand into new categories and export markets.

Recent developments

Under private ownership, SPC launched new products and acquired the Kuisine frozen meals business. In 2024, SPC merged with the Original Juice Company and Singapore-founded Nature One Dairy to form SPC Global, which listed on the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX) in late 2024 through a reverse takeover, creating a diversified food and beverage group with combined annual revenue reported in the hundreds of millions of dollars.
