Halo (franchise)

Halo is a military science fiction media franchise, originally developed by Bungie and currently managed and developed by Halo Studios (formerly 343 Industries), part of Xbox Game Studios. The series launched in 2001 with the first-person shooter video game Halo: Combat Evolved and its tie-in novel The Fall of Reach. The latest release, Halo Infinite, was released in 2021. The franchise is one of the best-selling video game series of all time, with more than 81 million copies sold.

Master Chief, a Spartan supersoldier, is the face of the Halo franchise.

Overview

The series is centered on an interstellar war between humanity and a theocratic alliance of alien races known as the Covenant. The Covenant, led by its religious leaders called the Prophets, worship an ancient civilisation known as the Forerunners, who perished while defeating the parasitic Flood. The games follow the Master Chief John-117, one of a group of supersoldiers codenamed Spartans, and his artificial intelligence companion Cortana. The term "Halo" refers to the Halo Array — a group of massive, ring-shaped superweapons created by the Forerunners to destroy all sentient life in the galaxy in order to starve the Flood.

Games

The main series comprises Halo: Combat Evolved (2001), Halo 2 (2004), Halo 3 (2007), Halo 4 (2012), Halo 5: Guardians (2015), and Halo Infinite (2021). Spin-offs include the prequel Halo: Reach (2010), the side story Halo 3: ODST (2009), the real-time strategy games Halo Wars (2009) and Halo Wars 2 (2017), and various mobile and arcade titles. Bungie developed the series until 2010, after which responsibility passed to 343 Industries, renamed Halo Studios in 2024, which also oversaw Halo: The Master Chief Collection, a compilation of the main games.

Other media

The franchise has expanded into dozens of novels, beginning with Eric Nylund's The Fall of Reach, graphic novels and comics, reference books, animated features such as Halo Legends, and the live-action web series Halo 4: Forward Unto Dawn. A television series produced by Showtime and Paramount+ premiered in 2022 and ran for two seasons.

Impact

Halo is often credited with popularising the first-person shooter on games consoles, establishing features such as regenerating health, a two-weapon limit, and integrated online multiplayer via Xbox Live as genre standards. The games have been lauded for their storytelling, multiplayer design, and music by Martin O'Donnell and Michael Salvatori. The franchise has grossed billions of dollars and become a flagship property for the Xbox brand.
