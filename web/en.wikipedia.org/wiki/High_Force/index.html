High Force is a waterfall on the River Tees near Middleton-in-Teesdale, County Durham, in the North Pennines Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty. The river plunges about 21 metres over the hard dolerite of the Whin Sill into a plunge pool below.

Geology and history

The falls were formed where the Tees crosses the resistant igneous Whin Sill; a second, abandoned channel records an earlier course. J. M. W. Turner painted the falls, and they have been a tourist attraction since Victorian times.
