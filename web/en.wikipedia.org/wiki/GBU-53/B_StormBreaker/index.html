GBU-53/B StormBreaker

GBU-53/B StormBreaker
Type: Small-diameter precision-guided glide bomb
Manufacturer: Raytheon (RTX)
Mass: approx. 93 kg (204 lb)
Users: United States Air Force and Navy

The GBU-53/B StormBreaker, formerly known as the Small Diameter Bomb II (SDB II), is an American air-launched precision-guided glide bomb developed by Raytheon. It is designed to strike stationary and moving targets at stand-off ranges of more than 40 nautical miles (74 km), in all weather, day or night.

Design

The StormBreaker uses a tri-mode seeker combining millimeter-wave radar, imaging infrared, and semi-active laser guidance, supplemented by GPS/INS navigation, allowing it to engage targets through smoke, dust, fog, and rain and to receive in-flight target updates via a two-way datalink. Its small 93 kg size lets strike aircraft such as the F-15E and F-35 carry many weapons — up to 24 on a dedicated rack in the case of the F-15E — and its compact warhead reduces collateral damage.

Service

The weapon entered US Air Force service on the F-15E Strike Eagle beginning in 2020, with integration on Navy and Marine Corps F-35s and other platforms following.
