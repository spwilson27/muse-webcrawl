London Waterloo station

London Waterloo station
Location: Lambeth, London
Managed by: Network Rail

London Waterloo is a central London railway terminus and Underground station complex in the London Borough of Lambeth. It is one of Britain's busiest stations, serving destinations in southwest London, Surrey, Hampshire, Berkshire and Dorset.

History

Waterloo opened in 1848 for the London and South Western Railway and was rebuilt in grand Edwardian style, completed in 1922. The former Eurostar terminal, Waterloo International, closed in 2007 when services moved to St Pancras.

Layout

The station has a large arched trainshed, a famous four-faced clock meeting point, and connections to the Northern, Bakerloo, Jubilee and Waterloo & City lines.
