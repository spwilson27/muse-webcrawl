Shinsengumi

The Shinsengumi (新選組, "Newly Selected Corps") was a special police force organised by the Tokugawa shogunate government in Japan in 1863 to protect representatives of the shogunate in Kyoto and combat anti-shogunate activities during the Bakumatsu period. Recruited largely from masterless swordsmen (rōnin), it became famous — and feared — under its commander Kondō Isami and vice-commander Hijikata Toshizō for its strict internal code, enforced by death, and its light-blue haori jackets with the character for "sincerity" (makoto) on the banner.

History

The corps originated as the Rōshigumi, a body of rōnin assembled in Edo in 1863 to guard the shogun Iemochi's procession to Kyoto. On arrival, most returned to Edo with their patron, but the swordsmen around Kondō Isami and Serizawa Kamo remained in Kyoto, receiving official status from the Aizu domain, which was charged with security in the capital. The internal rivalry with Serizawa ended with his assassination by Kondō's faction in October 1863. The Shinsengumi's reputation was cemented by the Ikedaya incident of July 1864, when it raided an inn and foiled a Chōshū plot against Kyoto. By 1865 it numbered some 200 men, organised in squads under captains including Okita Sōji, Nagakura Shinpachi, Saitō Hajime, and Tōdō Heisuke.

Final years

After the shogunate's defeat at the Battle of Toba–Fushimi in January 1868, the Shinsengumi retreated with loyalist forces. Kondō was captured and executed by the new government army in April 1868; Hijikata continued fighting with the Ezo Republic forces and was killed at the Battle of Hakodate in June 1869, an event commonly treated as the end of the corps and of the samurai era it symbolised. The Shinsengumi has since become a staple of Japanese popular culture, portrayed heroically in novels, films, television dramas, manga, and anime — notably Shimazaki Tōson's contemporary Shiba Ryōtarō's novel Moeyo Ken, the film When the Last Sword Is Drawn, the NHK Taiga drama Shinsengumi!, and the manga Rurouni Kenshin and Gintama.
