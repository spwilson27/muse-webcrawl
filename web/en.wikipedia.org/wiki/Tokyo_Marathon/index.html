Tokyo Marathon

The Tokyo Marathon (東京マラソン) is an annual marathon held in Tokyo, Japan, and one of the seven World Marathon Majors. Established in 2007, it is generally run in late February or early March and attracts around 37,000 participants, making it one of the largest mass-participation marathons in Asia.

History

The event replaced earlier elite races in the city, notably the Tokyo International Marathon, and was notable from its first edition for opening the full marathon to amateur runners on a large scale. In 2013 it joined the World Marathon Majors series alongside Boston, London, Berlin, Chicago, and New York City. The 2020 edition was restricted to elite runners because of the COVID-19 pandemic.

Course

The course starts at the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building in Shinjuku and passes districts including Iidabashi, Nihonbashi, Asakusa, Ginza, and Shinagawa before finishing near Tokyo Station at Gyoko-dori. Course records have been set by runners including Dickson Chumba, Wilson Kipsang, Brigid Kosgei, and Sutume Asefa Kebede; Ethiopia's Deresa Geleta and others have won recent editions in times around 2:03–2:05 for men and 2:15–2:19 for women.
