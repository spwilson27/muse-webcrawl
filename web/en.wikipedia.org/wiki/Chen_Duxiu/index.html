Chen Duxiu (1879-1942) was a Chinese intellectual and political leader, a leading figure of the New Culture and May Fourth movements and co-founder of the Chinese Communist Party.

New Culture

As dean at Peking University and editor of New Youth (Xin Qingnian), founded in 1915, Chen championed science and democracy against Confucian tradition and promoted vernacular Chinese literature.

Communist Party

With Li Dazhao he helped found the CCP in 1921 and served as its first general secretary. Blamed for the failure of the alliance with the Kuomintang after the 1927 Shanghai massacre, he was removed, expelled in 1929, and later led a Trotskyist opposition group.

Later life

Imprisoned by the Nationalist government 1932-37, Chen spent his last years in Sichuan in poverty, working on linguistics and writing, his historical reputation partly rehabilitated in later decades.
