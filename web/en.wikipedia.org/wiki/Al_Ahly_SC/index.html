Al Ahly Sporting Club (Arabic: النادي الأهلي للرياضة البدنية), commonly known as Al Ahly, is an Egyptian professional sports club based in Cairo, Egypt. The club is mainly known for its professional football team which currently plays in the Egyptian Premier League, the highest tier in the Egyptian football league system. The club is renowned for its consistent success both domestically and continentally, and is the most decorated club in African football.

History

Al Ahly was founded on 24 April 1907 by Omar Lotfy Bey and a group of Egyptian students and notables, at a time when most sporting clubs in Egypt were restricted to foreigners; its name, meaning "The National", reflected its founding purpose. The club's first chairman was the Englishman Mitchell-Innes, though Egyptian leadership soon followed. Al Ahly won the first Egyptian league championship in 1948–49 and went on to dominate Egyptian football.

Honours

The club has won a record number of Egyptian Premier League titles (more than 40), a record number of Egypt Cups, and a record 12 CAF Champions League titles, most recently extending its continental dominance in the 2020s. In 2000 the Confederation of African Football named Al Ahly the African Club of the Century. Al Ahly has also finished third at the FIFA Club World Cup on several occasions. Its fiercest rivalry is with Zamalek SC, contested in the Cairo derby, one of the most intense derbies in world football.

Other sports

Although best known for football, Al Ahly is a multi-sport club with successful teams in handball, basketball, volleyball and other sports, and operates branches in Gezira, Nasr City, Sheikh Zayed and New Cairo.
