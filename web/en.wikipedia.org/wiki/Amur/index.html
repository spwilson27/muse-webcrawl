Amur

The Amur River, known in China as the Heilong Jiang ('Black Dragon River'), is the world's tenth-longest river, flowing about 2,824 kilometres and forming much of the border between the Russian Far East and northeastern China (Manchuria). With its tributary the Argun, it reaches 4,444 kilometres.

The Amur basin is a meeting ground of taiga, steppe and temperate forest, home to Amur tigers and leopards. The river marked the Sino-Russian frontier after the 1858-60 treaties by which Russia annexed the left bank; border clashes occurred in 1969 (on the Ussuri tributary). Khabarovsk and Blagoveshchensk/Heihe face each other across it; in 2022 the first road bridge between Russia and China over the Amur opened at Blagoveshchensk.
