Korean FA Cup

The Korean FA Cup trophy

The Korean FA Cup (Korean: 코리아컵), known as the Korea Cup, is an annual football knockout cup competition in South Korea, organised by the Korea Football Association (KFA). Founded in 1996, it is open to professional clubs of the K League, semi-professional and amateur teams, and university sides, making it the country's principal national cup tournament. The winner qualifies for the AFC Champions League.

History

Before the FA Cup, South Korea's main cup competition was the National Football Championship, held from 1946 to 2000. The modern FA Cup was launched in 1996 following the professionalisation of Korean football, and from 2016 to 2023 it carried the sponsorship name of KEB Hana Bank. It was rebranded the Korea Cup in 2024.

Format

The competition is a single-elimination tournament. Lower-division and university teams enter in the early rounds, with K League 1 clubs joining in later rounds. Finals were traditionally played as a single match, though two-legged finals were used between 2007 and 2015.

Records

The most successful clubs include Pohang Steelers, Jeonbuk Hyundai Motors, Suwon Samsung Bluewings and FC Seoul. Pohang won the 2023 edition, while Jeonbuk claimed the 2022 title after defeating FC Seoul in the final.
