Jack (hero)

Jack is an archetypal Cornish and English hero and stock character appearing in multiple legends, fairy tales and nursery rhymes, generally portrayed as a young adult.

Character

Unlike the trickster Jack of Appalachian tales in some respects, the English Jack is typically brave, quick-witted and lucky, often defeating giants, witches and other adversaries. His tales are associated especially with Cornwall.

Tales

Famous Jack stories include "Jack and the Beanstalk", "Jack the Giant Killer", "Jack and His Golden Snuff-Box" and "Jack and His Master".
