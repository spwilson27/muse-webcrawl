Haast's eagle

Haast's eagle (Hieraaetus moorei) was a species of eagle that lived in the South Island of New Zealand and is the largest eagle known to have existed. With a mass of roughly 10–15 kg and a wingspan of up to about 3 metres, it preyed on flightless moa.

Extinction

After Polynesian settlement, its moa prey were hunted to extinction, and Haast's eagle itself died out around 1400 AD. The species was first described by Julius von Haast in 1871. It may correspond to the pouakai of Māori legend.
