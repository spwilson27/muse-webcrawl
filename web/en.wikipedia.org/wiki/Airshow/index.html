Airshow

An airshow (or air show) is a public event where aircraft are exhibited, often including aerobatic flying displays, military demonstrations, and static ground displays. Airshows are held at airfields and air bases and range from small local fly-ins to major international events such as the Paris Air Show, EAA AirVenture Oshkosh, and the Royal International Air Tattoo.

Displays

Flying displays typically feature aerobatics, formation teams such as the Red Arrows and the Blue Angels, historic warbirds, parachuting, and demonstrations of current military aircraft. Trade-oriented shows center on industry exhibitions and aircraft sales, while public shows emphasize entertainment and recruitment.

Safety

Airshows are governed by strict rules separating display flying from spectators, minimum altitudes and distances, and display authorization for pilots. Serious accidents — including the 1952 Farnborough crash, the 1988 Ramstein disaster, and the 2015 Shoreham crash — led to progressively tighter regulation, particularly of high-energy maneuvers over or toward crowds.
