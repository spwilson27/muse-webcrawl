Governorates of Syria

Syria is divided into fourteen governorates (Arabic: muḥāfaẓāt, singular muḥāfaẓah), the country's first-level administrative divisions. Each governorate is headed by a governor appointed by the president, and is subdivided into districts (mintaqah) and subdistricts (nahiyah).

List of governorates

  • Aleppo Governorate — capital Aleppo; the most populous governorate
  • Rif Dimashq Governorate — capital Douma; surrounds Damascus
  • Damascus Governorate — capital Damascus
  • Homs Governorate — capital Homs; the largest by area
  • Hama Governorate — capital Hama
  • Latakia Governorate — capital Latakia
  • Idlib Governorate — capital Idlib
  • Al-Hasakah Governorate — capital Al-Hasakah
  • Deir ez-Zor Governorate — capital Deir ez-Zor
  • Tartus Governorate — capital Tartus
  • Raqqa Governorate — capital Raqqa
  • Daraa Governorate — capital Daraa
  • As-Suwayda Governorate — capital As-Suwayda
  • Quneitra Governorate — capital Quneitra; includes part of the Golan Heights

Administration

Governors are responsible for administration, health, education, agriculture and public services within their governorates, and preside over elected provincial councils. The Quneitra Governorate is largely under Israeli occupation in the Golan Heights, and the Syrian civil war from 2011 left several governorates divided between government, opposition and Kurdish-led administrations.
