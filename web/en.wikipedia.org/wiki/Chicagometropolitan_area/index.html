The Chicago metropolitan area, often called Chicagoland, is the metropolitan area associated with the city of Chicago in the U.S. state of Illinois. It spans parts of northeastern Illinois, northwest Indiana and southeast Wisconsin, and is the third-most populous metropolitan area in the United States.

Geography

The area is centered on Cook County and the city of Chicago on the southwestern shore of Lake Michigan, extending into the "collar counties" of DuPage, Lake, Kane, McHenry and Will in Illinois, Lake and Porter counties in Indiana, and Kenosha County in Wisconsin.

Economy

The region is a major center for finance, manufacturing, transportation and logistics, anchored by O'Hare International Airport, the Chicago Board of Trade and a dense network of rail and highway corridors.
