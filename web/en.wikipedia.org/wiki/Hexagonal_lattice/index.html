Hexagonal lattice

The hexagonal lattice (also called the triangular lattice) is one of the five two-dimensional Bravais lattice types. Its points are arranged at the vertices of a regular triangular tiling, equivalently at the centres of a hexagonal tiling, with sixfold rotational symmetry.

Geometry

Primitive vectors can be taken at 120° to one another with equal length. The lattice has the densest circle packing in the plane, with packing density π/(2√3) ≈ 0.9069. Its dual is the honeycomb lattice, the structure of graphene, with two atoms per unit cell.

Occurrences

Hexagonal lattices appear in crystallography (the hexagonal crystal family), in the atomic structure of graphite, and in optimal sampling and cellular network layouts.

References: Ashcroft & Mermin, Solid State Physics; International Tables for Crystallography.
