Desiccator

Desiccators are sealable enclosures containing desiccants used for preserving moisture-sensitive items such as cobalt chloride paper for another use. A common use for desiccators is to protect chemicals which are hygroscopic or which react with water from humidity.

Construction and use

A laboratory desiccator is typically a heavy glass vessel with a ground-glass lid sealed with vacuum grease. A perforated plate holds the samples above a bed of desiccant such as silica gel, calcium chloride, or anhydrous calcium sulfate. Vacuum desiccators can be evacuated to further reduce moisture and speed drying of crucibles after ignition.
