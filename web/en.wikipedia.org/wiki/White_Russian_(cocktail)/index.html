White Russian (cocktail)

Type: Cocktail
Ingredients: Vodka, coffee liqueur, cream
Served: On the rocks in an old fashioned glass

A White Russian is a cocktail made with vodka, coffee liqueur (such as Kahlúa) and cream, served over ice in an old fashioned glass. It is the cream-topped descendant of the Black Russian, which appeared around 1949; the addition of cream — and the name White Russian — dates to the 1960s. Neither drink is of Russian origin; the name refers simply to the vodka.

Cultural popularity

By the 1980s the drink had acquired a slightly dated image, but it was revived enormously by the 1998 Coen brothers film The Big Lebowski, in which it is the constant drink of the protagonist, "the Dude". Numerous variants exist, including the White Cuban (rum), the Blind Russian (with Irish cream), and the Anna Kournikova (with skimmed milk).
