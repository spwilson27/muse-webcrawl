Neustadt an der Weinstraße

Neustadt an der Weinstraße is a town in Rhineland-Palatinate, Germany. With a population of around 53,000, it is the largest town on the German Wine Route (Deutsche Weinstraße) and a centre of the Palatinate wine-growing region.

Geography

The town lies on the eastern edge of the Palatinate Forest, at the mouth of the Speyerbach valley, west of the Rhine plain. Its nine wine villages — including Hambach, Mußbach and Gimmeldingen — are incorporated districts.

Culture

Neustadt hosts the German Wine Harvest Festival, at which the German Wine Queen is traditionally crowned. Above the town stands Hambach Castle, a symbol of the German democracy movement as the site of the Hambach Festival of 1832.
