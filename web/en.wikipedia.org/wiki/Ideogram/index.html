Ideogram
TypeGraphic symbol representing an idea or concept
Related termsPictogram, logogram, logograph

Mathematical notation and traffic signs are conventional ideograms.

An ideogram or ideograph (from Greek idea, "idea", and graphein, "to write") is a graphic symbol that represents an idea or concept, independent of any particular words or language. Strictly, an ideogram directly encodes a meaning, whereas a logogram represents a specific word or morpheme — the distinction is blurred in practice and the terms are often used interchangeably.

Examples

Many familiar symbols are ideographic: Arabic numerals (4 means the same number in any language), mathematical symbols such as + and =, currency signs, and many traffic and information signs. Pictograms — simplified depictions of objects — often function ideographically when they convey a prohibition or instruction rather than merely naming a thing (e.g. a crossed-out cigarette meaning "no smoking").

In writing systems

No full writing system consists of pure ideograms; every writing system combines meaning-bearing elements with phonetic ones. Egyptian hieroglyphs, Sumerian cuneiform, and Chinese characters all originated partly in pictographic and ideographic signs but developed rebus and phonetic uses early. Chinese characters (kanji/hanzi) are often popularly called "ideographs", but modern characters overwhelmingly represent morphemes with phonetic as well as semantic components, and are more accurately described as logographic.

Ideography as theory

In linguistics and semiotics, the idea of a universal ideographic writing — a script readable across languages — has long appealed to philosophers. Leibniz's project of a "characteristica universalis", a symbolic calculus for reasoning, was conceived in similar terms. Modern constructed writing systems such as Blissymbols (developed by Charles Bliss in 1949 and later used in augmentative and alternative communication) attempt the same goal: Blissymbols comprises some 4,500 characters combinable into new meanings and has been used successfully with people who have severe speech difficulties.
