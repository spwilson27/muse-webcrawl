José Antonio Domínguez Bandera (born 10 August 1960), known professionally as Antonio Banderas, is a Spanish actor, director, and producer. He first gained fame in the films of Pedro Almodóvar in the 1980s, then became an international star in Hollywood in the 1990s, and later won wide critical acclaim — including the Best Actor award at Cannes and an Academy Award nomination — for Pain and Glory (2019).

Banderas, one of Spain's most internationally recognised actors.

Early life and Spanish career

Born in Málaga, Banderas initially wanted to be a professional footballer until a broken foot ended that ambition; he turned to acting, working in theatre and moving to Madrid during La Movida Madrileña. Almodóvar cast him in Labyrinth of Passion (1982), beginning a collaboration that continued through Matador (1986), Women on the Verge of a Nervous Breakdown (1988), and Tie Me Up! Tie Me Down! (1989).

Hollywood and later work

His English-language breakthrough came with The Mambo Kings (1992), followed by Philadelphia (1993), Interview with the Vampire (1994), Robert Rodriguez's Desperado (1995), Evita (1996), and The Mask of Zorro (1998). He directed Crazy in Alabama (1999) and starred on Broadway in Nine (2003), earning a Tony nomination. His voice role as Puss in Boots in the Shrek franchise led to two spin-off films. Reuniting with Almodóvar for The Skin I Live In (2011) and Pain and Glory (2019) brought his most decorated performances; he also played Pablo Picasso in the series Genius (2018). Banderas founded the Teatro del Soho in his native Málaga.
