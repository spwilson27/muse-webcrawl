Weight plate

Weight plate
TypeWeight training equipment
Used withBarbell, dumbbell, weight machines
Standard bore50 mm (Olympic); 25–28 mm (standard)

A weight plate is a flat, heavy object, usually made of cast iron, that is used in combination with barbells or dumbbells to produce a bar with a desired total weight for the purpose of physical exercise. Plates are secured to the bar with collars to prevent them sliding off during use.

Two general categories exist: "standard" plates, which have a centre bore of roughly 25–28 mm and are used with ordinary dumbbell handles and non-Olympic barbells, and "Olympic" plates, which have a 50 mm (2 inch) bore and fit Olympic barbells. Olympic plates are the kind used in competitive weightlifting and powerlifting.

Bumper plates

Bumper plates are Olympic plates made of dense rubber (often with a steel hub) designed to be dropped safely on a platform when a lift is missed or completed. They are made to a standard diameter (450 mm for full-size plates) so that the bar rests at a consistent height regardless of the load, and lighter training plates of the same diameter are sometimes made of rigid plastic.

Standard weights and colours

Under International Weightlifting Federation rules, competition plates are colour-coded by mass:

In pound-based gyms, common plate denominations are 2.5, 5, 10, 25, 35 and 45 lb. Fractional or "change" plates of 2.5 kg and below allow small increments in loading.
