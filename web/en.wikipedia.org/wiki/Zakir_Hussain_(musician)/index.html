Zakir Hussain (musician)

Ustad Zakir Hussain (9 March 1951 – 15 December 2024) was an Indian tabla player, composer, percussionist, music producer and film actor. He was widely regarded as one of the greatest tabla players of all time and was the eldest son of tabla player Ustad Alla Rakha.

Career

Hussain was a child prodigy who began touring as a professional musician in his teens. He played on numerous recordings and accompanied many of India's leading classical musicians, and he became a major figure in bringing the tabla to international audiences. He was a founding member of the fusion group Shakti with John McLaughlin, and collaborated with artists including George Harrison, Van Morrison, Yo-Yo Ma, Béla Fleck and Edgar Meyer.

Awards

He received the Padma Shri in 1988, the Padma Bhushan in 2002, and the Padma Vibhushan in 2023, three of India's highest civilian honours. He won multiple Grammy Awards, including three at the 2024 ceremony for his work with Shakti and with Fleck and Meyer. He died in San Francisco in December 2024, aged 73.
