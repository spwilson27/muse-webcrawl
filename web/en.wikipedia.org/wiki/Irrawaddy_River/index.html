Irrawaddy River

The Irrawaddy River (Ayeyarwady) is Myanmar's largest river and most important commercial waterway, flowing about 2,170 kilometres from the confluence of the N'mai and Mali rivers in Kachin State south through the centre of the country to the Andaman Sea, where it divides into a nine-armed delta.

The Irrawaddy basin is Myanmar's heartland: the ancient capitals of Bagan, Ava, Amarapura and Mandalay all stood on or near its banks, and Rudyard Kipling's poem 'Mandalay' immortalised the 'road to Mandalay' by river steamer. The river is home to the endangered Irrawaddy dolphin. The suspended Myitsone Dam, a huge Chinese-backed project at the river's source, became a focus of national protest and remains a political flashpoint.
