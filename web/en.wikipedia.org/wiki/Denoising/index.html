Denoising

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Denoising is the process of removing noise from a signal, image, audio recording, or other data, in order to recover the underlying "clean" content as faithfully as possible. It is a fundamental problem in signal processing, with applications in photography, astronomy, medical imaging, audio restoration, seismology, and communications. Because some noise is always mixed with the signal, denoising necessarily trades noise removal against the loss or distortion of genuine detail.

Image denoising

Noise in digital images arises from sensor electronics, low light, and high ISO settings, and appears as random variations in brightness or colour. Classical methods include linear smoothing filters, median filtering (effective against salt-and-pepper noise), anisotropic diffusion, wavelet thresholding, and total variation minimisation. The non-local means algorithm (2005) averages pixels with similar neighbourhoods anywhere in the image, and the block-matching and 3D filtering method (BM3D, 2007) long stood as a benchmark. Since the 2010s, deep learning methods — convolutional networks trained on pairs of noisy and clean images, or even on noisy data alone — have surpassed classical approaches, and are now built into smartphone cameras and raw converters.

Audio denoising

In audio, denoising (noise reduction) removes hiss, hum, clicks, and background noise from recordings and live speech. Techniques include spectral gating and subtraction, Wiener filtering, and modern neural-network systems used in teleconferencing to isolate voices from environments. Historical consumer systems such as Dolby noise reduction worked by companding the signal during recording and playback.

Other domains

Denoising is also applied to one-dimensional signals such as electrocardiograms, to video (where temporal information aids the process), to 3D geometry meshes, and — in a modern usage — in diffusion models for generative AI, which are trained to reverse a gradual noising process and thus learn to denoise random noise into coherent images.
