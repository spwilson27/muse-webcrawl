A General History of the Pyrates

A General History of the Pyrates
Author: Captain Charles Johnson (attributed)
Published: 1724, London
Subject: Golden Age piracy

A General History of the Robberies and Murders of the most notorious Pyrates, commonly A General History of the Pyrates, is a 1724 book published in London containing biographies of contemporary pirates of the Golden Age. Authorship is attributed to Captain Charles Johnson, sometimes linked speculatively to Daniel Defoe.

Contents

The first volume covers Blackbeard, Stede Bonnet, Calico Jack, Anne Bonny and Mary Read, among others; a second volume appeared in 1728. It introduced iconic details such as Blackbeard's smoking beard and the Jolly Roger descriptions that shaped modern pirate imagery.

Influence

Despite mixing fact and invention, it remains a primary source for historians and influenced Robert Louis Stevenson and J. M. Barrie.
