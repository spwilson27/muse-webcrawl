Ripley's Believe It or Not!

Ripley's Believe It or Not! is an American franchise created by Robert Ripley, which deals in bizarre events and items so strange and unusual that readers might question the claims. Originally a newspaper panel, the features began in 1918 as a sports cartoon in The New York Globe before expanding to general oddities from Ripley's world travels.

History

The panel was initially called "Champs and Chumps" and later renamed "Believe It or Not!" in 1919. Distributed by King Features Syndicate, it became one of the most widely read newspaper features in the world. After Ripley's death in 1949, the panel continued under other cartoonists and researchers. Ripley also opened "Odditorium" museums displaying curiosities, beginning at the 1933 Chicago World's Fair.

Media and attractions

The franchise has included radio and television series (including a 1980s version hosted by Jack Palance, a 2000s version hosted by Dean Cain, and a 2019 Travel Channel series hosted by Bruce Campbell), annual books of oddities, and Ripley's museums, aquariums, and attractions operated worldwide by the Jim Pattison Group, which owns Ripley Entertainment.
