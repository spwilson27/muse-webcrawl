National Security Council (United Kingdom)

The National Security Council (NSC) of the United Kingdom is a Cabinet committee established in May 2010 by Prime Minister David Cameron. It coordinates the government's work on national security, foreign policy, defence, international relations and development, intelligence and resilience.

Membership and structure

The council is chaired by the Prime Minister and includes senior ministers such as the Foreign Secretary, Home Secretary, Defence Secretary and Chancellor of the Exchequer, together with the heads of the intelligence agencies as required. It is supported by the National Security Adviser and a National Security Secretariat in the Cabinet Office.

Role

The NSC oversees strategic defence and security reviews and meets weekly when Parliament is sitting, more often in crises.
