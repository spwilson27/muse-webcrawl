Chuvashia, officially the Chuvash Republic, is a federal subject (republic) of Russia in the middle Volga region of European Russia, on the right bank of the Volga River between Nizhny Novgorod and Kazan. It is the homeland of the Chuvash people, a Turkic ethnic group and the republic's titular nationality.

Geography and demography

The republic covers about 18,300 km² of forest-steppe and mixed forest along the Volga and its tributary the Sura. Its capital and largest city is Cheboksary, a Volga port and industrial centre. Of roughly 1.2 million inhabitants, about two-thirds are ethnic Chuvash, with Russians the largest minority; the population is predominantly Orthodox Christian, with a Muslim minority.

History and culture

The Chuvash descend in part from the Volga Bulgars, whose medieval state dominated the region before the Mongol invasions; their language, Chuvash, is the sole surviving member of the Oghur branch of Turkic. The territory came under Russian rule after Ivan the Terrible's conquest of Kazan in 1552. A Chuvash Autonomous Oblast was created in 1920, becoming an Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic in 1925 and a republic within Russia after 1991. Chuvash in the Cyrillic alphabet was standardised in the 19th century by the educator Ivan Yakovlev; the republic is also noted in Russia for hops production and brewing.
