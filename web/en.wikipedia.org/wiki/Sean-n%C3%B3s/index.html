Sean-nós

Sean-nós (Irish for "old style") refers to a family of unaccompanied traditional arts from Ireland, most notably sean-nós singing and sean-nós dancing. The singing is highly ornamented, free in rhythm, and performed solo, usually in the Irish language.

Singing

Sean-nós songs are often long narrative ballads or laments, delivered with melismatic ornamentation, nasal timbre and subtle variations between verses. The tradition is strongest in the Gaeltacht regions of Connemara, Donegal and West Cork; celebrated singers include Joe Heaney and Darach Ó Catháin.

Dancing

Sean-nós dance is a low-to-the-ground, improvised style of percussive step dancing, with relaxed upper body and intricate footwork, quite distinct from the competitive form of Irish dance. It is traditionally performed on a small space such as a table, a door, or half a barrel.

Competitions

Both arts feature prominently at the annual Oireachtas na Gaeilge festival and the Fleadh Cheoil competitions, which helped preserve and spread the styles.
