Political correctness

Political correctness (adjectivally politically correct, commonly abbreviated PC) is a term used to describe language, policies, or measures that are intended to avoid offense or disadvantage to members of particular groups in society.

History

The phrase appeared sporadically in earlier centuries, then was used within left-wing circles in the mid-20th century, sometimes self-critically or ironically. In the late 1980s and early 1990s it became prominent in conservative criticism of academia and public institutions in the United States, and entered mainstream political debate.

Debate

Supporters describe the underlying practices as basic courtesy and inclusive language; critics argue that political correctness can amount to censorship or enforced conformity. The term is most often used pejoratively in contemporary usage.
