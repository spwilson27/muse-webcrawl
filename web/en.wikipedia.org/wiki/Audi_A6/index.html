The Audi A6 is an executive car manufactured by the German automaker Audi since 1994. It succeeded the Audi 100 and competes with the BMW 5 Series and Mercedes-Benz E-Class.

Generations

The A6 has been produced in successive generations designated C4 through C8, offered as a four-door saloon and a five-door Avant estate, with front-wheel drive or quattro all-wheel drive. Engines range from four-cylinder units to V8s; performance variants include the S6 and the high-performance RS 6 Avant. Allroad quattro versions feature raised suspension and rugged styling.

Recognition

The A6 has been a consistent strong seller for Audi, particularly in Europe and China, where a long-wheelbase A6L is produced. The C6 generation won the World Car of the Year award in 2005.
