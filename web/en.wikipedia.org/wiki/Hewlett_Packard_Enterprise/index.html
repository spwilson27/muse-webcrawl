Hewlett Packard Enterprise Company (HPE) is an American multinational information technology company based in Spring, Texas. HPE was created on November 1, 2015, when the original Hewlett-Packard company split into two: HP Inc., which retained the personal computer and printing businesses, and HPE, which took over the enterprise products and services businesses.

History

The split, announced in 2014 and executed under chief executive Meg Whitman, was intended to let each company focus on its own markets. HPE subsequently spun off and merged much of its enterprise services arm with Computer Sciences Corporation to form DXC Technology (2017), and merged its software business into Micro Focus (2017). Antonio Neri became president and CEO in 2018 and led the company's pivot toward an edge-to-cloud, as-a-service strategy under the HPE GreenLake brand.

Products and businesses

HPE's portfolio includes ProLiant and Synergy servers, high-performance computing systems descended from Cray (acquired in 2019), Alletra and Primera storage, Aruba networking (acquired in 2015), and the GreenLake hybrid cloud platform. In January 2024 HPE announced an agreement to acquire Juniper Networks for about $14 billion, a transaction completed in July 2025 after antitrust litigation with the U.S. Department of Justice was settled.
