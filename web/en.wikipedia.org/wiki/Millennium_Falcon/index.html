Millennium Falcon

The Millennium Falcon is a fictional starship in the Star Wars franchise. Designed by Ralph McQuarrie and Joe Johnston, the modified YT-1300 Corellian light freighter first appeared in Star Wars (1977) and is famously piloted by the smuggler Han Solo and his first mate, the Wookiee Chewbacca.

Description

The Falcon is a heavily modified YT-1300 light freighter manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation. Despite its dilapidated appearance — Solo's boast that "she may not look like much, but she's got it where it counts" — the ship is one of the fastest in the galaxy, capable of attaining 0.5 past lightspeed thanks to extensive modifications to its hyperdrive. It is armed with quad laser cannons and concussion missiles and fitted with smuggling compartments beneath its deck plates.

History in the films

Before falling into Solo's hands, the ship belonged to Lando Calrissian, who lost it to Solo in a game of sabacc. In A New Hope (1977), Solo and Chewbacca are hired to carry Luke Skywalker, Obi-Wan Kenobi and the droids R2-D2 and C-3PO to Alderaan, drawing the ship into the Rebel Alliance's struggle against the Galactic Empire. The Falcon plays a key role in the destruction of both Death Stars: in Return of the Jedi (1983), Calrissian pilots it into the second Death Star's reactor shaft. The ship returns in the sequel trilogy, beginning with The Force Awakens (2015), and its early history under Calrissian is depicted in Solo: A Star Wars Story (2018).

Cultural impact

The Millennium Falcon is one of the most recognizable spacecraft in cinema. Full-scale replicas have been built as attractions at Disney theme parks, where the ship anchors the Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge lands. The ship's distinctive saucer shape, offset cockpit and forward mandibles have made it an enduring icon of science-fiction design.
