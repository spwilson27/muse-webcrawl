Battle of Rezang La

Battle of Rezang La
Date: 18 November 1962
Location: Rezang La, Ladakh
Part of: Sino-Indian War

The Battle of Rezang La was fought on 18 November 1962 during the Sino-Indian War, at a mountain pass south of Chushul in Ladakh at an altitude of about 5,500 metres. C Company of the Indian Army's 13 Kumaon Regiment, led by Major Shaitan Singh, held the position against repeated Chinese People's Liberation Army attacks.

Background

Rezang La overlooked the Chushul airstrip, a vital supply point. The Indian position lacked artillery support in direct range and was isolated in extreme cold.

The battle

Chinese forces attacked in waves before dawn. The Kumaonis fought with rifles, light machine guns and mortars, repelling successive assaults. Of about 120 men, 114 were killed, including Major Singh, who was posthumously awarded the Param Vir Chakra.

Legacy

The battle is remembered in India as one of the great last stands, commemorated by a memorial at Chushul and retellings in literature and film.
