Ashton Eaton

Born: January 21, 1988, Portland, Oregon, U.S.
Sport: Track and field
Event: Decathlon, heptathlon
Personal best: Decathlon 9,045 points (former world record)

Eaton, widely regarded as one of the greatest multi-event athletes of all time.

Ashton James Eaton (born January 21, 1988) is a retired American decathlete and two-time Olympic champion. He won gold medals in the decathlon at the 2012 London Olympics and the 2016 Rio de Janeiro Olympics, and held the world record in both the decathlon (9,045 points) and the indoor heptathlon (6,645 points).

Early life and college career

Eaton was born in Portland, Oregon, and raised in Bend, Oregon. A versatile athlete in high school, he attended the University of Oregon, where he won multiple NCAA titles in the decathlon and heptathlon for the Oregon Ducks and received The Bowerman award in 2010 as the nation's top collegiate track and field athlete.

Professional career

Eaton broke the heptathlon world record three times between 2010 and 2012. At the 2012 U.S. Olympic Trials in Eugene, Oregon — held a century after Jim Thorpe's decathlon triumph — he set a new decathlon world record of 8,934 points, surpassing Roman Šebrle's mark. He went on to win the Olympic title in London that summer.

At the 2015 World Championships in Beijing, Eaton improved his own world record to 9,045 points, breaking the championship record despite the gruelling ten-event schedule. He successfully defended his Olympic crown at Rio 2016 with 8,893 points, becoming only the third man in history to win consecutive Olympic decathlon titles.

Retirement and personal life

Eaton announced his retirement from athletics in January 2017. He is married to Canadian multi-event athlete Brianne Theisen-Eaton, an Olympic bronze medallist in the heptathlon; the couple announced their retirements on the same day. Eaton later pursued studies and work in engineering and technology.
