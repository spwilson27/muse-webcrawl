Megabus (Europe)

Megabus is the brand name for a low-cost intercity bus service operated in Europe by the Stagecoach Group. Launched in the United Kingdom in 2003, the service expanded across the European continent from 2012, offering long-distance journeys between major cities at fares advertised from as little as £1 or €1 plus a booking fee.

History

Megabus was launched in the UK in August 2003 by Stagecoach, initially serving routes from London to cities including Oxford, Manchester, Liverpool and Birmingham. The brand proved successful and was extended to North America in 2006. Continental European services began in 2012, with routes linking Paris, Brussels, Amsterdam, Cologne and other cities. In 2017 Stagecoach sold its Megabus continental European retail operations to the German competitor Flixbus, while continuing to operate some services.

Services

Megabus coaches typically offer reserved seating, power sockets, free Wi-Fi on many routes and onboard toilets. The distinctive blue livery features the character "Sid", a portly cartoon driver, on the side of the vehicles. Booking is primarily online, with dynamic pricing similar to low-cost airlines.
