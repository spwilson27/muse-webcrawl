Melissa Ann McCarthy (born August 26, 1970) is an American actress, comedian, producer, and writer. She gained recognition for her role as Sookie St. James on the television series Gilmore Girls (2000–2007) and achieved a breakthrough with her Academy Award-nominated performance in the comedy film Bridesmaids (2011).

Career

McCarthy starred as Molly Flynn on the sitcom Mike & Molly (2010–2016), winning a Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Comedy Series in 2011. Her successful film comedies include Identity Thief (2013), The Heat (2013), Tammy (2014), Spy (2015) and The Boss (2016). She received widespread critical acclaim and a second Academy Award nomination for portraying writer Lee Israel in the biographical drama Can You Ever Forgive Me? (2018).

Production and business

With her husband Ben Falcone, McCarthy co-founded the production company On the Day Productions. She has repeatedly been ranked among the world's highest-paid actresses by Forbes.
