Riverside, California

County: Riverside County
State: California
Incorporated: October 11, 1883
Population: approx. 317,000 (2020 census)
Known for: Birthplace of the California citrus industry

Riverside is a city in and the county seat of Riverside County, California, United States, located in the Inland Empire metropolitan area. It takes its name from its location beside the Santa Ana River. With a population of about 317,000 at the 2020 census, it is the most populous city in the Inland Empire and one of the largest cities in California.

History

Riverside was founded in 1870 by John W. North, a former judge and abolitionist, together with associates from a silk-growing colony. The city's destiny was transformed in 1873 when Eliza Tibbets received two Brazilian navel orange trees from the United States Department of Agriculture. The Washington navel orange thrived in Riverside's climate, and the seedless, sweet fruit proved ideal for shipping. One of the original "parent" trees still survives at the corner of Magnolia and Arlington avenues. By the 1880s Riverside's citrus groves had made it, per capita, one of the wealthiest cities in the United States.

The Mission Inn, begun in 1876 by Frank Miller and expanded over decades into an eccentric, cathedral-like complex of wings, rotundas, and chapels, became a landmark hotel that hosted presidents and celebrities.

Geography and climate

Riverside lies about 55 miles east of Los Angeles, in the valley of the Santa Ana River. Mount Rubidoux, a small granite hill within the city, is crowned by a cross and open-air amphitheater used for Easter sunrise services since 1909. The climate is semi-arid, with hot, dry summers and mild winters; the city is also affected by Santa Ana winds blowing from the desert passes.

Education and economy

The University of California, Riverside, founded in 1954, grew out of a citrus experiment station established in 1907 and remains a major research institution. The city began as an agricultural center and is now a hub for logistics, education, health care, and government services for the Inland Empire.
