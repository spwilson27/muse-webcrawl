Silicon Valley is an American comedy television series created by Mike Judge, John Altschuler and Dave Krinsky that aired on HBO from 2014 to 2019. It parodies the culture of technology startups in California's Silicon Valley.

Premise

The series follows Richard Hendricks (Thomas Middleditch), a programmer who develops a revolutionary lossless compression algorithm and founds the startup Pied Piper. With friends and housemates including Erlich Bachman, Dinesh, Gilfoyle and Jared, he struggles against the giant company Hooli and its CEO Gavin Belson, venture capitalists, and competitors.

Reception

The show was praised for its accurate, insider satire of tech culture — co-creator Mike Judge had worked as an engineer in Silicon Valley — and won two Primetime Emmy Awards among many nominations.
