List of Japanese prefectures by area

Japan consists of 47 prefectures (todōfuken), forming the first level of jurisdiction and administrative division. This article lists the prefectures ranked by land area. By far the largest is Hokkaido, the northernmost main island, which alone accounts for roughly 22 percent of the country's total land area of about 377,975 square kilometres. The smallest is Kagawa Prefecture on Shikoku.
