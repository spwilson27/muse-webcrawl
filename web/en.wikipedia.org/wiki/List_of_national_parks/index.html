List of national parks

A national park is a protected area set aside by a national government for conservation and public enjoyment. The modern concept began with Yellowstone in the United States in 1872. Today there are more than 6,500 national parks worldwide, and the IUCN counts protected areas of all types in the hundreds of thousands.

National parks by country

Separate lists exist for most countries, including the United States (63 congressionally designated national parks), Canada (48), India (106), China, the United Kingdom (15), Australia (more than 500 areas carry the name), and many others. Yellowstone's model influenced park systems globally through the 20th century.
