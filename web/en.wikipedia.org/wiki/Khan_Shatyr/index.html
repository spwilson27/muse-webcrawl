Overview

Khan Shatyr ("Royal Marquee") is a giant transparent tent in Astana, Kazakhstan — a shopping and entertainment centre opened on 6 July 2010, the 70th birthday of President Nursultan Nazarbayev. Designed by Norman Foster's practice, it is 150 metres tall (on a 200-metre elliptical base) and is among the largest tensile structures in the world.

Features

The tent's ETFE skin spans a cable net held by a central tripod mast, maintaining a summer-like internal climate despite Astana's winters of −35 °C. Inside are shops, restaurants, a monorail, a river ride and an indoor beach resort (the Sky Beach Club) with sand imported from the Maldives, heated year-round. Khan Shatyr anchors the new monumental axis of the capital with the Bayterek tower and the Ak Orda palace.
