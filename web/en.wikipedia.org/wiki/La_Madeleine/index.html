La Madeleine

La Madeleine may refer to:

  • La Madeleine, Paris — the Church of Sainte Marie-Madeleine, a Roman Catholic church in the 8th arrondissement of Paris, famous for its neoclassical temple form.
  • La Madeleine, Nord — a commune in the Nord department of northern France, adjoining Lille.
  • Madeleine (cake) — sometimes styled "la madeleine," a small French sponge cake.

The Paris church

The Madeleine Church in Paris was designed as a temple to the glory of Napoleon's army. Construction began in 1764 to an earlier design, was halted during the French Revolution, and was restarted by Napoleon in 1806 under the architect Pierre-Alexandre Vignon, who modelled it on the Maison Carrée at Nîmes. Surrounded by 52 Corinthian columns, the windowless building was consecrated as a church dedicated to Mary Magdalene in 1842. It stands at the north end of the rue Royale, facing the Place de la Concorde and the Palais Bourbon across the Seine, and is a noted venue for classical concerts and society weddings and funerals.
