Jessica Hynes

Jessica Hynes (née Stevenson; born 30 October 1972) is an English actress, director, and writer. She is best known as the co-creator, co-writer, and star of the British sitcom Spaced (1999–2001).

Career

Hynes co-created Spaced with Simon Pegg and director Edgar Wright, playing Daisy Steiner. Her other television work includes The Royle Family, in which she played Cheryl Carroll, Doctor Who, Twenty Twelve and its sequel W1A, in which she played PR consultant Siobhan Sharpe, and Russell T Davies's Years and Years (2019). In film she appeared in Shaun of the Dead (2004), Son of Rambow (2007), Burke & Hare (2010), and Paddington 2 (2017).

Awards

She won the British Comedy Award for Best TV Comedy Actress and was nominated for a BAFTA for Spaced. In 2019 she won the BAFTA TV Award for Best Female Comedy Performance for There She Goes, in which she played the mother of a severely learning-disabled girl, and used her acceptance speech to protest cuts to special-needs education funding.
