CBS Corporation

Founded: 2005 (split from Viacom)
Defunct: 2019 (merged into ViacomCBS)
Headquarters: New York City, US
Industry: Mass media

The CBS Corporation was an American mass media corporation that existed from 2006 to 2019. It was created when Viacom split into two companies, with CBS Corporation inheriting the CBS television network, radio, publishing and outdoor advertising businesses.

Assets

Its holdings included the CBS television network, The CW (jointly with Warner Bros.), Showtime Networks, Simon & Schuster publishers, CBS Studios and a large group of local television and radio stations. The radio division was spun off to Entercom in 2017.

Merger

In December 2019 CBS Corporation merged with Viacom to form ViacomCBS (later Paramount Global), reuniting the companies after thirteen years apart.
