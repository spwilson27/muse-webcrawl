Shoplifting

Shoplifting is the theft of goods from an open retail establishment, typically by concealing items on one's person or in a bag and leaving the store without paying. In many jurisdictions the offence is legally classified as larceny or retail theft.

Methods and prevention

Common methods include concealment in clothing or strollers, price-tag switching, and "booster bags" lined to defeat sensor tags. Retailers counter shoplifting with electronic article surveillance (EAS) tags, CCTV, mirrors, locked display cases, store detectives, and civil recovery demands.

Impact

Shrinkage from shoplifting costs retailers billions of dollars annually worldwide, contributing to higher prices and, in organised retail crime cases, large-scale resale operations prosecuted as organised theft.
