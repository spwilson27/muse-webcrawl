Google Meet is a video communication service developed by Google. It is one of two apps that constitute the replacement for Google Hangouts, the other being Google Chat. It replaced the consumer-facing Google Duo on November 1, 2022, after the Duo mobile app was renamed Meet and the original Meet app was phased out.

History

Google Meet was launched in March 2017 as an enterprise video conferencing service. During the COVID-19 pandemic, use of the service grew rapidly, and in April 2020 Google made it free to all users with a Google account. Between January and April 2020, Meet's daily usage grew by a factor of 30.

Features

Meet supports one-on-one and group video calls, screen sharing, live captions, in-meeting chat, reactions, polls, breakout rooms, and meeting recording for Workspace customers. Calls can be joined from a web browser without installing software, or through mobile apps. In 2023 and 2024, Google added AI-powered features including automatic note-taking and background effects generated by AI.
