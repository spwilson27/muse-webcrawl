Hybe Corporation (Korean: 하이브, stylized as HYBE Corporation) is a South Korean multinational entertainment company founded in 2005 by Bang Si-hyuk as Big Hit Entertainment. It operates as a record label, talent agency, music production and publishing company, and platform business. Hybe is best known as the home of the boy group BTS, whose global success transformed the once-small agency into the largest entertainment company in South Korea by market capitalization.

History

Big Hit Entertainment was founded in 2005 and initially managed ballad groups and hip-hop acts with modest results, nearly going bankrupt around 2007. It debuted BTS in 2013; the group's worldwide breakthrough in the late 2010s generated record profits and allowed Big Hit to expand aggressively. In 2019 it acquired Source Music (home of GFriend), and in 2020 it acquired a majority stake in Pledis Entertainment (Seventeen, NU'EST). The company went public on the Korea Exchange in October 2020 in one of the year's largest Korean IPOs.

In March 2021, Big Hit Entertainment rebranded as Hybe Corporation, restructuring into a label division, a solutions division, and a platforms division built around the fan-community app Weverse. Hybe expanded internationally through Hybe America, acquiring Scooter Braun's Ithaca Holdings in 2021, and later the Atlanta label Quality Control. It launched new labels including ADOR (which debuted NewJeans in 2022) and, with CJ ENM, Belift Lab (Enhypen, Illit). A widely publicized corporate dispute between Hybe and ADOR's leadership erupted in 2024. In 2025 Hybe sold its stake in former rival SM Entertainment, which it had accumulated during a contested takeover battle in 2023.

Artists and business

Hybe's labels manage acts including BTS and its members' solo projects, Tomorrow X Together, Seventeen, Enhypen, Le Sserafim, NewJeans, BoyNextDoor, and &Team, as well as international acts under Hybe America. Beyond music, Hybe operates Weverse, sells merchandise and licensed content, produces games and original story content, and has invested in artificial intelligence and voice technology.
