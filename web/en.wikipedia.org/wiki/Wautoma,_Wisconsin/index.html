Wautoma is a city in Waushara County, Wisconsin, United States. The population was 2,209 at the 2020 census. It is the county seat of Waushara County. The city is located mostly within the boundaries of the Town of Wautoma and the Town of Dakota.

History

Wautoma was incorporated in 1939. The name is believed to derive from a Native American chief or from a Potawatomi word meaning "dark sky" or "walk far away." The city developed as a market and service center for the agricultural surroundings of the county and later became a gateway community for tourism in central Wisconsin, situated on the old stagecoach route between Berlin and Stevens Point.

Geography

Wautoma is located at approximately 44°04′N 89°17′W in central Wisconsin. According to the United States Census Bureau, the city has a total area of about 2.72 square miles, of which 2.68 square miles are land and 0.04 square miles are water. The White River flows through the area.

Demographics

As of the 2010 census, there were 2,218 people, 945 households, and 523 families residing in the city. The population has remained roughly stable around 2,200 into the 2020 census.

Education

Wautoma is served by the Wautoma Area School District, which operates Wautoma High School, Parkside Middle School, and Redgranite and Riverview elementary schools.

Recreation

The nearby lakes and the Mecan and White rivers draw visitors for fishing, camping, and boating. The community hosts events such as the Waushara County Fair.
