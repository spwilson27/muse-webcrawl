Musée Condé
LocationChantilly, France
Established1897
CollectionPaintings, manuscripts, drawings

The Musée Condé is a French museum located in the Château de Chantilly, about 50 kilometres north of Paris. It houses one of France's finest collections of paintings, second only to the Louvre in old master works, along with illuminated manuscripts and drawings.

Collection

Highlights include the Très Riches Heures du Duc de Berry, one of the most famous illuminated manuscripts in the world, and works by Raphael, Poussin, Watteau, and Ingres. The collection was bequeathed to the Institut de France by Henri d'Orléans, Duke of Aumale, in 1884, on condition that the arrangement of works never be altered.

Library

The museum library holds some 13,000 volumes and 1,500 manuscripts, including the Hours of Jeanne d'Évreux and the Ingeborg Psalter.
