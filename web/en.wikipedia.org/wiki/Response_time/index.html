Response time of a request

Response time is the total time a system takes to react to a request for service — from the moment the request is submitted until the completed result is returned. In interactive computing it is a key measure of performance and usability.

In computing

For a user-interface action, response time is the delay between the input and the visible feedback. Classic usability guidelines hold that delays under about 0.1 seconds feel instantaneous, under 1 second keep the user's flow of thought, and beyond 10 seconds risk losing attention. In transaction-processing systems, response time combines service time with waiting (queueing) time, and degrades sharply as utilisation approaches capacity.

Other uses

The term is also used for display pixel response time (the time a pixel takes to change colour, affecting motion blur), for emergency services (time from call to arrival), and for sensors and control systems reacting to a stimulus.
