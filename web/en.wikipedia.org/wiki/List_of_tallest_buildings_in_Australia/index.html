List of tallest buildings in Australia

This article lists the tallest buildings in Australia by height and number of floors.

Australia's tallest buildings are concentrated in its major east-coast cities, particularly Gold Coast, Melbourne, Sydney and Brisbane. The Gold Coast's Q1 tower in Surfers Paradise has been measured as Australia's tallest building to its spire since its completion in 2005, while Melbourne's Eureka Tower and the more recent Australia 108 have surpassed it to the top of the roof or occupy the highest floors.

Criteria

Rankings are generally based on the standards of the Council on Tall Buildings and Urban Habitat (CTBUH), which distinguishes between height to architectural top (including spires but excluding antennas), highest occupied floor, and height to the tip.
