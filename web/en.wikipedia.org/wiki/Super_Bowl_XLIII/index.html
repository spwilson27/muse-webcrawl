Super Bowl XLIII was the championship game of the National Football League for the 2008 season, played on February 1, 2009, at Raymond James Stadium in Tampa, Florida. The Pittsburgh Steelers defeated the Arizona Cardinals 27–23 to win their record sixth Super Bowl title.

Game summary

The game is remembered for several dramatic plays. Steelers linebacker James Harrison returned an interception 100 yards for a touchdown at the end of the first half, at the time the longest play in Super Bowl history. In the fourth quarter, Cardinals receiver Larry Fitzgerald scored on a 64-yard catch-and-run to give Arizona a 23–20 lead, but Steelers quarterback Ben Roethlisberger drove Pittsburgh downfield, and Santonio Holmes made a toe-tapping touchdown catch in the corner of the end zone with 35 seconds remaining.

Entertainment

Jennifer Hudson sang the national anthem, and Bruce Springsteen and the E Street Band performed at halftime.
