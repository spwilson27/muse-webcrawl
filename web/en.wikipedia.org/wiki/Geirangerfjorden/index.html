Geirangerfjorden

Geirangerfjorden is a fjord in the Sunnmøre region of Møre og Romsdal county, Norway. The 15-kilometre-long fjord is a branch of the Storfjorden and is listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site together with Nærøyfjord.

The fjord is one of Norway's most visited tourist attractions. Its steep sides feature abandoned farms and famous waterfalls, including the Seven Sisters (De syv søstrene), the Suitor (Friaren) and the Bridal Veil (Brudesløret).

The village of Geiranger lies at the head of the fjord. The fjord is threatened by a potential rockslide from the mountain Åkerneset, which could generate a destructive tsunami, a scenario dramatised in the 2015 film The Wave.
