USS Enterprise (CV-6)

USS Enterprise (CV-6) was a Yorktown-class aircraft carrier of the United States Navy, commissioned in 1938. She was the most decorated U.S. Navy ship of World War II, earning 20 battle stars, and took part in more major actions against Imperial Japan than any other U.S. warship. Nicknamed the "Big E", she was one of only three American carriers commissioned before the war to survive it.

Wartime service

Enterprise was at sea returning from delivering aircraft to Wake Island on 7 December 1941, escaping the attack on Pearl Harbor. Her aircraft fought at the Marshall Islands raids, the Doolittle Raid escort, and decisively at the Battle of Midway, where her dive bombers helped sink the Japanese carriers Akagi and Kaga. She went on to fight at Guadalcanal, the Eastern Solomons, Santa Cruz, the Philippine Sea, and Leyte Gulf, and in 1945 supported the invasions of Iwo Jima and Okinawa, where she was knocked out of the war by a kamikaze strike in May 1945.

Postwar

After the war Enterprise helped bring troops home in Operation Magic Carpet. Despite public campaigns to preserve her as a memorial, she was sold for scrap in 1958 and broken up by 1960. Her name was passed to the world's first nuclear-powered aircraft carrier, USS Enterprise (CVN-65).
