Manos Hatzidakis

Manos Hatzidakis (1925 – 1994) was a Greek composer, theorist of Greek music, and conductor, regarded as one of the greatest Greek composers of the 20th century. Born in Xanthi, he studied in Athens and rose to prominence after the Second World War.

Work

Hatzidakis won the Academy Award for Best Original Song in 1960 for "Never on Sunday" from the film of the same name. He brought rebetiko music to wider respectability with his 1949 lecture and arrangements, composed the cycles Gioconda's Smile and Magnus Eroticus, and wrote extensively for theatre and film, collaborating with Mikis Theodorakis and Nana Mouskouri, who popularised many of his songs. He later founded the Orchestra of Colours.
