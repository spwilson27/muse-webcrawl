Kate Mulgrew

Katherine Kiernan Maria Mulgrew (born 29 April 1955) is an American actress and author. She is best known for playing Captain Kathryn Janeway in Star Trek: Voyager (1995–2001) — the first woman to lead a Star Trek series as a regular captain — and Galina "Red" Reznikov in the Netflix series Orange Is the New Black (2013–2019).

Early life and career

Mulgrew was born in Dubuque, Iowa, and studied at NYU's Tisch School of the Arts and the Stella Adler Conservatory. She rose to prominence as Mary Ryan in the soap opera Ryan's Hope (1975–78) and starred as Kate Columbo in the short-lived Mrs. Columbo (1979). Film roles included Throw Momma from the Train (1987) and Camp Nowhere (1994).

Later work

After Voyager, Mulgrew appeared in NTSF:SD:SUV::, Warehouse 13 and Mr. Mercedes, and returned to voice Janeway in Star Trek: Prodigy (2021–2024). Her portrayal of Red earned her a Critics' Choice Award and an Emmy nomination. She won an Obie Award for her one-woman stage performance as Katharine Hepburn in Tea at Five. Her memoirs are Born with Teeth (2015) and How to Forget (2019).
