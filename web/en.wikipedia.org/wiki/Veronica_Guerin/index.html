Veronica Guerin

Veronica Guerin (5 July 1959 – 26 June 1996) was an Irish investigative journalist who wrote for the Sunday Independent. Her reporting on Dublin's organised crime and drug trade made her a national figure; she was shot dead by drug traffickers in 1996, an event that shocked Ireland and led to major anti-crime legislation.

Career

Guerin began in public relations, having played football and basketball for Ireland, before moving into journalism. She gained prominence with interviews and exposés of criminal figures, naming the methods of drug gangs while Irish libel law constrained naming individuals. She survived being shot in the leg in 1995 and received the International Press Freedom Award from the Committee to Protect Journalists.

Murder and legacy

On 26 June 1996, while stopped at traffic lights on the Naas Road outside Dublin, Guerin was shot by a gunman on a motorcycle. Her murder prompted the creation of the Criminal Assets Bureau to seize criminals' assets. Brian Meehan was convicted of the murder; drug lord John Gilligan was convicted of drug trafficking though acquitted of the murder itself. Her story was dramatised in the 2003 film Veronica Guerin, starring Cate Blanchett.
