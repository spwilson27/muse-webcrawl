Setsuko Hara

Setsuko Hara
Born: 17 June 1920, Yokohama, Japan
Died: 5 September 2015, Kanagawa
Occupation: Actress (1935–1963)

Setsuko Hara (born Masae Aida) was a Japanese actress, one of the most celebrated stars of Japanese cinema, best known for her collaborations with Yasujirō Ozu, including Late Spring (1949), Early Summer (1951) and Tokyo Story (1953), in which she played characters named Noriko.

Career

Debuting in 1935, she became a wartime star, then an emblem of post-war Japanese womanhood in films by Ozu, Mikio Naruse and Akira Kurosawa (No Regrets for Our Youth, The Idiot).

Retirement

She retired abruptly in 1963, following Ozu's death, and lived in seclusion in Kamakura for over fifty years, refusing interviews — a disappearance often likened to Greta Garbo's.
