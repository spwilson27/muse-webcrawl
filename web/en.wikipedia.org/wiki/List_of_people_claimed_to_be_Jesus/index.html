List of people claimed to be Jesus

This is a list of people claimed to be Jesus — individuals who claimed, or whose followers claimed for them, that they were the reincarnation, return or embodiment of Jesus of Nazareth. Such claims have appeared throughout Christian history and around the world, often founding new religious movements.

17th–19th centuries

NameDatesNotes
Jacob Frank1726–1791Polish Jewish religious leader; his followers believed him the reincarnation of the Messiah, and he claimed continuity with Jesus and Sabbatai Zevi.
Ann Lee ("Mother Ann")1736–1784Founder of the Shakers; her followers regarded her as the female manifestation of Christ's second coming.
Richard Brothers1757–1824British religious figure who claimed divine status as "Prince of the Hebrews".
John Nichols Thom1799–1838English claimant who styled himself the Messiah; killed at the Battle of Bossenden Wood.
Bahá'u'lláh1817–1892Founder of the Baháʼí Faith; Baháʼís believe him the return of Christ in the glory of the Father, among other manifestations.
Hong Xiuquan1814–1864Leader of the Taiping Rebellion; claimed to be the younger brother of Jesus Christ.

20th–21st centuries

NameDatesNotes
Haile Selassie I1892–1975Emperor of Ethiopia; regarded as God incarnate by Rastafari, though he himself remained an Ethiopian Orthodox Christian.
Krishna Venta1911–1958Founder of the Fountain of the World cult in California; claimed to be Christ.
Sun Myung Moon1920–2012Founder of the Unification Church; his followers regarded him and his wife as fulfilling the mission of Jesus.
Jim Jones1931–1978Founder of the Peoples Temple; at times claimed to be a manifestation of Jesus and Buddha.
Yahweh ben Yahweh1935–2007Founder of the Nation of Yahweh in the United States.
David Koresh1959–1993Leader of the Branch Davidians; claimed to be the final prophet and Lamb of God.
Sergey Torop ("Vissarion")born 1961Russian founder of the Church of the Last Testament; claims to be the reincarnation of Jesus.
Apollo Quiboloyborn 1950Filipino pastor who calls himself the "Appointed Son of God".
Alan John Miller ("AJ")born 1962Australian founder of the Divine Truth movement; claims to be Jesus returned.
Mitsuo Matayoshi ("Jesus Matayoshi")1944–2018Japanese eccentric politician who called himself "the only God Matayoshi Jesus".
Inri Cristoborn 1948Brazilian claimant who styles himself the reincarnation of Jesus and leads a small church in Brasília.
José Luis de Jesús Miranda1946–2013Puerto Rican founder of Growing in Grace; claimed to be both Jesus Christ returned and the Antichrist.
