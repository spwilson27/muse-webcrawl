World Car Awards

The World Car Awards, whose top honour is the World Car of the Year (WCOTY), is an annual set of automotive awards voted on by an international jury of motoring journalists. Launched in 2004 and administered from Toronto, Canada, the winners are traditionally announced at the New York International Auto Show.

Categories

Alongside the overall World Car of the Year, awards are given for World Luxury Car, World Performance Car, World Urban Car, World Car Design of the Year, and—from 2022—World Electric Vehicle of the Year, plus a World Car Person of the Year. Eligible cars must be sold in at least two major markets on at least two continents during the award period.

Notable winners

Early overall winners included the Audi A6 (2005), BMW 3 Series (2006) and Volkswagen Golf (2009, 2013). Electric models have featured increasingly: the Hyundai Ioniq 5 swept three categories in 2022, and the Hyundai Ioniq 6 repeated the feat in 2023; the Kia EV9 won the overall title in 2024.
