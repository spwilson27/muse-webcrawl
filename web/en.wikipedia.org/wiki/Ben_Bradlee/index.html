Ben Bradlee

Ben Bradlee
Born26 August 1921, Boston
Died21 October 2014
OccupationNewspaper editor
[Image: Bradlee in the Post newsroom during the Watergate years]

Ben Bradlee (1921–2014) was the executive editor of the Washington Post from 1968 to 1991 who, with publisher Katharine Graham, steered the paper through the Pentagon Papers and Watergate, transforming it into a national institution. His swashbuckling newsroom style ('Bradlee's Post') was portrayed by Jason Robards in All the President's Men.

Career

A Boston Brahmin and wartime naval officer turned reporter, Bradlee had been Newsweek's Washington bureau chief and a friend of President Kennedy before the Post. Under him the paper won many Pulitzers and created the Style section; he retired as executive editor in 1991.
