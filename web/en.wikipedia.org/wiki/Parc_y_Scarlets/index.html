Parc y Scarlets is a rugby union stadium in Llanelli, Carmarthenshire, Wales. It is the home ground of the Scarlets, one of Wales's four professional regional rugby teams. The stadium opened in November 2008, replacing the club's historic home at Stradey Park.

Facilities

The stadium has a capacity of just under 15,000, with a main stand incorporating hospitality suites, a museum celebrating the history of Llanelli rugby, and a large supporters' barn. An indoor training barn and community facilities adjoin the ground.

History

The first match at Parc y Scarlets was played on 15 November 2008 against Munster. The ground has hosted Wales women's internationals, football matches, and concerts, and the adjoining retail park developments helped fund its construction.
