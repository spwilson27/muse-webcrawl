Need to know

Access to sensitive information is limited to those with a demonstrated need to know.

The term "need to know", when used by government and military organizations and their contractors, describes the restriction of data which is considered very sensitive. Under need-to-know restrictions, even if an individual has all the necessary official approvals (such as a security clearance) to access certain information, they should not be given access to it — or should not be read in on it — unless they have a specific need to know; that is, access to the information must be necessary for them to conduct their official duties.

Principles

Need to know is a core element of compartmentalization: sensitive programs are divided into compartments so that no single person has access to more information than their task requires. Together with the principle of least privilege in computer security, it limits the damage from espionage, accidental disclosure, or insider threats, because a compromised individual can only reveal the compartments to which they were read in.

In practice, access typically requires both a security clearance at or above the classification level of the material and a formal determination that the person needs the information for their work. Holders of clearances are instructed not to discuss classified matters with colleagues who lack a need to know, even if those colleagues hold the same clearance level.

Use in information security

The same concept is applied in corporate and information-security settings, where it is combined with role-based access control, data classification, and separation of duties. It also features in privacy and data-protection practice, where personal data should be accessible only to staff who need it for a defined purpose.

Criticism

Strict application of need-to-know has been criticized for impeding information sharing between agencies — a concern highlighted after the September 11 attacks, which prompted moves toward a "responsibility to provide" information alongside need-to-know controls.
