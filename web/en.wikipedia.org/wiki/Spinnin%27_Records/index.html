Spinnin' Records

Spinnin' Records
Founded: 1999, Hilversum, Netherlands
Founders: Eelko van Kooten, Roger de Graaf
Genre: electronic dance music
Parent: Warner Music Group (since 2017)

Spinnin' Records is a Dutch electronic music record label founded in 1999 by Eelko van Kooten and Roger de Graaf. It is one of the world's best-known dance music labels, recognised for releasing big room house, electro house, future house and other EDM styles, and for its large online following built through YouTube and social media promotion.

History

The label began by licensing tracks and building compilations before signing its own artists. During the global EDM boom of the early 2010s, Spinnin' became a hit-making label with releases such as Martin Garrix's "Animals" (2013) and tracks by artists including Tiësto, Don Diablo, Oliver Heldens, DVBBS, and KSHMR. In September 2017 the label was acquired by Warner Music Group in a deal reported at over $100 million.

Sublabels and activities

Spinnin' operates numerous sublabels, including DOORN Records (run by Sander van Doorn), Musical Freedom (founded by Tiësto), Heldeep Records (Oliver Heldens), Spinnin' Deep, and Dharma Worldwide (KSHMR). It also runs the Spinnin' Talent Pool platform, through which unsigned producers can submit demos and gain exposure.
