Shuri is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Reginald Hudlin and artist John Romita Jr., she first appeared in Black Panther vol. 4 #2 (2005). Shuri is the princess of the fictional African nation of Wakanda, the younger sister of T'Challa, and one of the smartest people in the Marvel Universe, serving as Wakanda's chief scientist and technologist. She has herself taken up the mantle of the Black Panther.

Publication history and biography

Introduced as a headstrong princess eager to challenge for the title of Black Panther, Shuri trained rigorously in combat and, after T'Challa was left comatose by Doctor Doom, she passed the trials and became the Black Panther and Queen of Wakanda, advised by a past-life spiritual journey in the Djalia, the plane of Wakandan memory. In later storylines she sacrificed her life to hold back Thanos's forces, was resurrected, and gained mystical "Ancient Future" powers connecting her to Wakanda's history, including the ability to transform into a flock of birds and animate stone.

In other media

Letitia Wright portrays Shuri in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, debuting in Black Panther (2018) as the brilliant inventor of Wakanda's vibranium technology who aids her brother with remotely piloted vehicles and her vibranium gauntlets. She appears in Avengers: Infinity War (2018) and Avengers: Endgame (2019), and in Black Panther: Wakanda Forever (2022) she takes on the mantle of the Black Panther following T'Challa's death. The MCU version of the character was widely praised and credited with inspiring interest in STEM among young viewers.
