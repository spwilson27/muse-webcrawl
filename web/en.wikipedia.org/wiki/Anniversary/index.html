Anniversary

An anniversary is the date on which an event took place or an institution was founded in a previous year, and may also be the commemoration or celebration of that event. The word derives from the Latin anniversarius, meaning "returning yearly", formed from annus (year) and versus (turned).

Types

Anniversaries are kept for births (birthdays), weddings, deaths, and historical events such as national independence days. Milestone anniversaries are often given traditional names and gifts: the 25th wedding anniversary is silver, the 50th is golden, the 60th diamond, and the 75th is sometimes also diamond. Centenaries mark 100 years, bicentenaries 200, and a millennium 1,000 years.

Symbols and names

In the Commonwealth realms, significant royal anniversaries are marked as jubilees: a silver jubilee marks 25 years of a monarch's reign, a golden jubilee 50 years, and a platinum jubilee 70 years. Latin-derived terms are also used: a quincentenary is 500 years, and terms such as sesquicentennial denote 150 years.

Observance

Anniversaries may be marked by private gifts and gatherings, or by public ceremonies, commemorative coins and stamps, concerts and fireworks. Companies and institutions commonly celebrate founding anniversaries with special branding and events.
