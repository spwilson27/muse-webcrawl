Petco Park

Location: 100 Park Blvd, San Diego, California

Opened: April 8, 2004

Capacity: Baseball: ~39,860 (approx.)

Tenants: San Diego Padres (MLB)

Surface: Grass

Petco Park is an open-air baseball stadium in San Diego, California, United States. It is the home of the San Diego Padres of Major League Baseball (MLB). The ballpark opened in 2004 and is located in downtown San Diego's East Village neighborhood, adjacent to the historic Gaslamp Quarter.

History

The stadium was built as part of a larger downtown redevelopment project, following a public vote (Proposition C) in 1998 that approved public financing for a new ballpark district. Construction began in 2000 but was delayed by litigation, beginning in earnest in 2002. It replaced Qualcomm Stadium (formerly Jack Murphy Stadium) in Mission Valley, where the Padres had played since their founding in 1969. The Padres played their first game at Petco Park on April 8, 2004, against the San Francisco Giants.

The ballpark is named after the pet-supply retailer Petco, which purchased naming rights. Petco Park hosted games of the 2006 World Baseball Classic, including the semifinals and final, and the 2016 MLB All-Star Game.

Design and features

Designed by the architecture firm Populous (then HOK Sport) with input from Antoine Predock's studio, the park is noted for its sandstone and stucco exterior reflecting regional architecture, its open design with views of San Diego Bay and the downtown skyline, and the incorporation of the historic Western Metal Supply Co. building — a brick warehouse dating to 1909 — into the left field foul pole area. The building houses suites, a restaurant, and rooftop seating, and its corner defines the left field foul pole.

Beyond the outfield is Park at the Park, a grassy berm area outside the outfield fence where fans can watch games on a large video screen for a small fee; it functions as a neighborhood park on non-game days. The stadium is widely regarded as a pitcher-friendly park owing to its deep outfield dimensions and marine-layer air, though the fences were moved in and lowered before the 2015 season to increase offense.

Events

In addition to Padres games, Petco Park has hosted concerts, soccer matches, rugby sevens, golf events (a nine-hole course laid out inside the stadium), and the annual Holiday Bowl parade events nearby. It has also hosted college basketball and, in 2016, the World Baseball Classic's second-round pool play returned.
