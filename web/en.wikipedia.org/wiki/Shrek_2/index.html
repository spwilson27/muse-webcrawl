Shrek 2 is a 2004 American animated comedy film produced by DreamWorks Animation and the sequel to Shrek (2001). Loosely based on the picture book by William Steig, it follows the ogre Shrek as he travels with his new wife Princess Fiona to meet her parents in the kingdom of Far Far Away — and confronts a plot by the Fairy Godmother and Prince Charming to undo the marriage. It introduced the character Puss in Boots, voiced by Antonio Banderas.

Plot

Shrek and Fiona return from their honeymoon to an invitation from Fiona's parents, King Harold and Queen Lillian. The meeting goes badly: Harold, who had promised the Fairy Godmother that her son Prince Charming would marry Fiona, conspires with her to eliminate Shrek. Warned by Donkey and the swashbuckling Puss in Boots — initially hired as an assassin but won over to Shrek's side — Shrek steals a "Happily Ever After" potion that transforms him into a handsome human and Fiona back into a human princess. In the climax, the heroes storm the royal ball with the help of a giant gingerbread man, the Fairy Godmother is destroyed by her own reflected spell, and Harold — revealed to have been a frog transformed by an earlier potion — restores the couple, who choose to remain ogres.

Reception and legacy

Shrek 2 premiered at the 2004 Cannes Film Festival and became the highest-grossing animated film of all time on its release, earning about $935 million worldwide, a record it held until Toy Story 3 (2010). It was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature. Critics praised its wit, pop-culture parody of Hollywood (Far Far Away as a fairy-tale Beverly Hills) and its climactic use of the song "Holding Out for a Hero". The film spawned further sequels and the Puss in Boots spin-off series.
