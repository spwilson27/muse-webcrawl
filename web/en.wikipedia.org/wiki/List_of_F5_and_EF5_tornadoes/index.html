List of F5 and EF5 tornadoes

This is a list of tornadoes rated F5 on the Fujita scale or EF5 on the Enhanced Fujita scale, the highest rating for tornado intensity, corresponding to estimated winds exceeding 200 mph (322 km/h) and "incredible" damage. Since 1950, only a few dozen tornadoes worldwide have officially received this rating, the great majority of them in the United States.

Rating history

The Fujita scale was introduced in 1971 and applied retroactively to United States tornadoes from 1950 onward by Ted Fujita and the Storm Prediction Center. The Enhanced Fujita scale replaced it operationally in the United States on 1 February 2007 and in Canada in 2013. Ratings are based on damage indicators and degrees of damage rather than direct wind measurement.

Outside the United States

The only officially rated F5/EF5 tornadoes outside the United States in the modern record occurred in Canada (Elie, Manitoba, 2007 — Canada's first F5) and in Europe, where historical events such as the 1764 Woldegk tornado in Germany have been retrospectively assessed at F5 intensity.
