Duncan Zowie Haywood Jones (born 30 May 1971) is a British film director, producer and screenwriter. He is the son of the musician David Bowie and the model and actress Angie Bowie.

Career

Jones began his career directing commercials. His feature debut, the science fiction film Moon (2009), starring Sam Rockwell, won the BAFTA Award for Outstanding Debut by a British Writer, Director or Producer. He followed it with the thriller Source Code (2011), starring Jake Gyllenhaal.

He directed the big-budget video game adaptation Warcraft (2016), which grossed over $430 million worldwide, largely in China, and the Netflix science fiction film Mute (2018), set in a future Berlin and linked thematically to Moon. In 2024 he released the animated film Rogue Trooper, based on the 2000 AD comic character.
