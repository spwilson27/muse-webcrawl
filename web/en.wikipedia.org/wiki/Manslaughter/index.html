Manslaughter

Manslaughter is a common law legal term for homicide considered by law as less culpable than murder. The distinction typically turns on the absence of malice aforethought or intent to kill.

CategoryCriminal homicide
TypesVoluntary, involuntary

Voluntary manslaughter

Voluntary manslaughter is an intentional killing committed in the heat of passion in response to provocation, or under diminished responsibility / diminished capacity in some jurisdictions.

Involuntary manslaughter

Involuntary manslaughter is an unintentional killing resulting from criminal negligence, recklessness, or an unlawful act not amounting to a felony. Vehicular manslaughter is a common statutory form.
