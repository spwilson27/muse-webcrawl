Atom transfer radical polymerization

ATRP
Full name: Atom transfer radical polymerization
Type: Controlled radical polymerization
Discovered: 1995

Atom transfer radical polymerization (ATRP) is a controlled radical polymerization method discovered independently in 1995 by Krzysztof Matyjaszewski and by Mitsuo Sawamoto. It uses a transition metal complex, typically copper, to reversibly activate alkyl halide chain ends.

Mechanism

ATRP establishes an equilibrium between dormant chains capped with halogen and a low concentration of active radicals, mediated by Cu(I)/Cu(II) complexes with ligands. This gives polymers with predetermined molecular weight, narrow dispersity and preserved end functionality for block copolymers.

Variants

Variants include ARGET, ICAR, SARA and photo-ATRP, which reduce catalyst amounts to parts-per-million levels.
