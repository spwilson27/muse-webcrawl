W1A

W1A is a British comedy television series that satirises the management of the BBC. Written and directed by John Morton, it is a follow-up to the earlier series Twenty Twelve and stars Hugh Bonneville as Ian Fletcher, who becomes the BBC's Head of Values. It aired on BBC Two and BBC Four from 2014 to 2017, with a lockdown reunion special in 2020.

Premise

The title refers to the postcode of Broadcasting House, the BBC's London headquarters. The series follows Ian Fletcher and his colleagues through endless meetings, rebrands and initiatives, laced with management jargon; characters such as brand consultant Siobhan Sharpe (Jessica Hynes) and the hapless intern Will Humphries became widely quoted. David Tennant provides the deadpan narration.

Reception

W1A was praised for the accuracy of its satire of corporate bureaucracy, winning praise from critics and reportedly from BBC staff themselves. Bonneville later reprised the Ian Fletcher role for a sequel series set at FIFA, Twenty Twenty Six.
