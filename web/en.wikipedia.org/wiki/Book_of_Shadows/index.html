Book of Shadows
Type: Wiccan ritual book / grimoire
Originator: Gerald Gardner, 1940s–50s

A Book of Shadows is a book of religious and magical texts in the Neopagan religion of Wicca, containing rituals, spells, laws and lore copied by initiates.

History

Gerald Gardner, founder of modern Wicca, introduced the Book in the late 1940s/early 1950s for his Bricket Wood coven, claiming an ancient lineage; scholars trace its composition to Gardner and High Priestess Doreen Valiente, who rewrote much of it, drawing on sources including the Key of Solomon and Aleister Crowley. Traditionally each initiate hand-copies the book, which is kept secret within the coven.

Modern use

In eclectic Wicca, a Book of Shadows (or grimoire) is often a personal journal of practice. Published versions and popular culture (e.g. Charmed) broadened the term. See Gerald Gardner, Grimoire.
