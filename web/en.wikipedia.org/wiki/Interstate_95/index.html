Interstate 95 (I-95) is the main north–south Interstate Highway on the East Coast of the United States, running from Miami, Florida, to the Canadian border at Houlton, Maine. At about 1,919 miles (3,089 km), it is the longest north–south Interstate and passes through more states than any other Interstate.

Route

I-95 serves most major East Coast metropolitan areas, including Miami, Jacksonville, Savannah, Richmond, Washington, D.C., Baltimore, Philadelphia, New York City, New Haven and Boston. Much of its route parallels U.S. Route 1 and the Atlantic coastline.

History

The highway was built in segments from the 1950s onward, incorporating toll roads such as the Florida Turnpike sections and the New Jersey Turnpike. A long-standing gap in New Jersey was closed in 2018 with the completion of the interchange with the Pennsylvania Turnpike.
