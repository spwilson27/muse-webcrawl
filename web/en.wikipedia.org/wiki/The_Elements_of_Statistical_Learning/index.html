The Elements of Statistical Learning

The Elements of Statistical Learning: Data Mining, Inference, and Prediction is a widely used textbook on statistical learning by Trevor Hastie, Robert Tibshirani, and Jerome Friedman of Stanford University. First published in 2001, with a second edition in 2009, it is a standard reference for graduate courses in statistics and machine learning.

Contents

The book covers supervised learning in depth: linear methods for regression and classification, basis expansions and regularisation, kernel smoothing, model assessment and selection, additive models and trees, boosting and ensemble methods, neural networks, support vector machines, prototype methods, and unsupervised learning techniques such as clustering and principal components. It is known for its extensive use of colour graphics and worked examples on real data sets.

Reception and related works

The full text has been made freely available as a PDF from the authors' university website, contributing to its enormous citation count and influence on the popularisation of statistical learning. A more applied companion text, An Introduction to Statistical Learning (2013), by Gareth James, Daniela Witten, Hastie and Tibshirani, presents much of the same material with labs in R (and later Python) aimed at a broader audience.
