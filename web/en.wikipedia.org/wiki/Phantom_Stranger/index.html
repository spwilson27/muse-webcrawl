Phantom Stranger

Publisher: DC Comics

First appearance: Phantom Stranger #1 (August–September 1952)

Created by: John Broome, Carmine Infantino

The Phantom Stranger is a fictional superhero and occult figure published by DC Comics. Created by writer John Broome and artist Carmine Infantino, he first appeared in Phantom Stranger #1 (1952). Clad in a dark suit, cape, fedora, and a gold medallion, with eyes shadowed and white hair, the Stranger is an enigmatic agent of the supernatural in the DC Universe whose true nature and origin are deliberately unexplained.

Publication history

The character's original 1950s series ran six issues, in which he debunked apparently supernatural frauds (or, ambiguously, genuine occult threats). He was revived in 1969 in a second series under the Vertigo-era approach to horror, often working alongside the blind parapsychologist Dr. Terry Thirteen, who persisted in trying to prove the Stranger a fraud. The Stranger became a recurring figure in DC's magical landscape, serving as a member of the Sentinels of Magic and, in the New 52 continuity (2011), being reintroduced as leader of the "Trinity of Sin" alongside Pandora and the Question in Trinity of Sin: The Phantom Stranger.

Origin

DC has traditionally kept the Stranger's origin a mystery. A 1990 issue of Secret Origins presented four possible origins without confirming any: that he was a fallen angel who sided with neither Heaven nor Hell in Lucifer's rebellion; that he was the Wandering Jew; that he was a human granted power by cosmic forces in a time loop; or that he was an agent of the Lords of Order. In the New 52 continuity, he was identified as Judas Iscariot, condemned to walk the earth wearing the thirty pieces of silver as a medallion — an identification later continuity has treated variably.

Powers and role

The Stranger possesses vast but ill-defined magical powers, near-immortality, and uncanny knowledge of occult matters; he typically serves as an adviser and guide — appearing to warn heroes of supernatural crises, sometimes restrained by cosmic rules from intervening directly. He has been a member of the Justice League in some continuities and a recurring ally of characters such as the Spectre, Zatanna, and John Constantine.
