Kristen Carroll Wiig (born 1973) is an American actress, comedian and writer. She was a cast member of Saturday Night Live from 2005 to 2012, known for characters such as the Target lady and Gilly.

Career

Wiig trained with The Groundlings in Los Angeles before joining SNL. She co-wrote and starred in the comedy Bridesmaids (2011), earning an Academy Award nomination for Best Original Screenplay. Her film roles include Adventureland, The Secret Life of Walter Mitty, The Martian and Wonder Woman 1984.

Voice work

She voices Lucy Wilde in the Despicable Me franchise and Ruffnut in the How to Train Your Dragon films, and co-created the Apple TV+ series Palm Royale.
