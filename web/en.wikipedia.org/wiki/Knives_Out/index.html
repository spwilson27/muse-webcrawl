Knives Out

Knives Out
Directed by: Rian Johnson
Released: 2019
Starring: Daniel Craig, Chris Evans, Ana de Armas

Knives Out is a 2019 American murder mystery film written and directed by Rian Johnson. It follows detective Benoit Blanc (Daniel Craig) investigating the death of wealthy novelist Harlan Thrombey, found dead after his 85th birthday party at his Massachusetts mansion.

Plot

Blanc, assisted by nurse Marta Cabrera, untangles the lies of Thrombey's greedy family. The film plays with whodunit conventions: the apparent solution emerges early, then is overturned as the true plot — involving grandson Ransom's tampering with Harlan's medication — is revealed.

Reception and sequels

The film was a critical and commercial success, grossing over $300 million worldwide and earning Johnson an Academy Award nomination for Best Original Screenplay. Sequels Glass Onion: A Knives Out Mystery (2022) and a third Blanc film followed on Netflix.
