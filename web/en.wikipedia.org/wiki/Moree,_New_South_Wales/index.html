Moree, New South Wales

Moree is a town in Moree Plains Shire in northern New South Wales, Australia, located on the Mehi River. It is a major agricultural centre on the black soil plains, known for cotton, wheat, pecan nuts and olives, and for its artesian hot spring baths fed by the Great Artesian Basin.

Moree is a major railway junction where lines branch toward Mungindi, Walgett and Narrabri, and lies at the junction of the Newell and Gwydir highways. The town's population is around 7,000–9,000, with a significant Aboriginal (Kamilaroi/Gamilaraay) community. The name is believed to derive from an Aboriginal word, sometimes translated as "rising sun" or "long spring".
