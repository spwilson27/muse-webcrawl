List of current monarchs of sovereign states

This is a list of current monarchs of sovereign states. As of the mid-2020s there are 43 sovereign states with a monarch as head of state, of which 15 are Commonwealth realms sharing King Charles III as head of state. Monarchies range from absolute monarchies such as Saudi Arabia, Oman, Brunei and Eswatini, to constitutional monarchies such as the United Kingdom, Spain, the Netherlands, Japan and Thailand, in which the monarch's role is largely ceremonial.
