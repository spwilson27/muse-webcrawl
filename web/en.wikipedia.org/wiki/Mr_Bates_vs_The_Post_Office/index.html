Mr Bates vs The Post Office is a four-part British television drama series broadcast on ITV in January 2024. Written by Gwyneth Hughes and based on the real-life British Post Office scandal, it tells the story of Alan Bates and the campaign by subpostmasters who were wrongly prosecuted, bankrupted, and ruined after faults in the Post Office's Horizon computer system made it appear that money was missing from their branch accounts.

Toby Jones stars as Alan Bates, the former subpostmaster of Craig-y-Don, Llandudno, who founded the Justice for Subpostmasters Alliance and led the group litigation against the Post Office. The cast also includes Monica Dolan, Julie Hesmondhalgh, Alex Jennings as a senior Post Office executive figure, and Will Mellor.

Impact

The series had an extraordinary public and political impact. Broadcast to audiences of over nine million, it propelled the long-running scandal to the top of the news agenda; within days, the UK government announced legislation to quash the convictions of hundreds of subpostmasters en masse, enacted as the Post Office (Horizon System) Offences Act 2024, along with an additional compensation scheme. Former Post Office chief executive Paula Vennells returned her CBE following a public petition that gathered more than a million signatures.

The drama won widespread critical acclaim and multiple awards, and is widely cited as an example of television drama directly changing public policy.
