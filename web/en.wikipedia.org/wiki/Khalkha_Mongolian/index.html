Khalkha Mongolian

Native to: Mongolia
Speakers: ~3 million
Family: Mongolic
Writing: Cyrillic; traditional Mongolian script

Khalkha Mongolian is the de facto official language variety of Mongolia and the most widely spoken and best-known dialect of the Mongolian language. It is named after the Khalkha, the largest subgroup of Mongols, and forms the basis of the standard written Mongolian used in Mongolia.

Features

Khalkha is distinguished phonologically by its treatment of historical *s and by palatalised consonants. Like other Mongolian varieties it is agglutinative, follows vowel harmony, and uses subject–object–verb word order.

Writing systems

Since the 1940s Khalkha has been written in an adapted Cyrillic alphabet in Mongolia; the classical vertical Mongolian script, still official in Inner Mongolia, has been promoted again in Mongolia since the 1990s and is taught in schools.
