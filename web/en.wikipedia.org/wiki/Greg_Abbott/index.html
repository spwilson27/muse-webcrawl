Greg Abbott

Gregory Wayne Abbott
Born November 13, 1957, Wichita Falls, Texas
48th Governor of Texas (2015–present)
Party: Republican

Gregory Wayne Abbott (born November 13, 1957) is an American politician, attorney, and jurist serving as the 48th governor of Texas since 2015. A member of the Republican Party, he served as the 50th attorney general of Texas from 2002 to 2015 and as a justice of the Texas Supreme Court from 1996 to 2001.

Early life and accident

Abbott was born in Wichita Falls and raised in Duncanville. He graduated from the University of Texas at Austin and earned his law degree from Vanderbilt University Law School. In 1984, while jogging, Abbott was struck by a falling oak tree, leaving him paralyzed from the waist down; he uses a wheelchair. The accident and settlement shaped his later advocacy on tort reform.

Legal and judicial career

Governor George W. Bush appointed Abbott to the Texas Supreme Court in 1995; he was twice elected to the court. In 2002 he was elected attorney general, succeeding John Cornyn, and served three terms, frequently suing the federal government during the administration of Barack Obama.

Governor of Texas

Abbott won the 2014 gubernatorial election, defeating Wendy Davis, and was re-elected in 2018 and 2022. His governorship has included responses to Hurricane Harvey (2017), the 2021 Texas power crisis, border security operations such as Operation Lone Star, and signature legislation on abortion (the Texas Heartbeat Act of 2021), gun rights, and voting. In 2023 he signed a school voucher programme into law after earlier attempts stalled.
