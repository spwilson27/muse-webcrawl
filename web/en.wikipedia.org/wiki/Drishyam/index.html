Drishyam

Directed by Jeethu Joseph
Written by Jeethu Joseph
Starring Mohanlal, Meena
Release date 19 December 2013
Language Malayalam

Drishyam (transl. "Visual") is a 2013 Indian Malayalam-language crime thriller film written and directed by Jeethu Joseph and starring Mohanlal and Meena. It tells the story of Georgekutty, a cable-TV operator in a Kerala village whose family becomes entangled in the disappearance of a police officer's son, and who uses his encyclopaedic knowledge of films to construct an alibi.

Reception and remakes

The film was a critical and commercial landmark — one of the highest-grossing Malayalam films of its time — praised for its screenplay and Mohanlal's performance. It was remade in numerous languages, including Tamil (Papanasam), Telugu, Hindi (Drishyam, 2015, starring Ajay Devgn) and Kannada, and was the first Malayalam film remade in Chinese (Sheep Without a Shepherd). A sequel, Drishyam 2, followed in 2021. The story was widely discussed for its thematic echoes of the Japanese novel The Devotion of Suspect X, though Jeethu Joseph has denied it as a source.
