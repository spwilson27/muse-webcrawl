Breydon Water

Breydon Water is a large expanse of sheltered estuary at the gateway to the Norfolk Broads in eastern England, near Great Yarmouth. It is formed where the rivers Yare, Bure, and Waveney meet before flowing out to the North Sea through Yarmouth harbour.

Wildlife

The intertidal mudflats of Breydon Water are an important site for wintering and passage waterbirds, including avocets, lapwings, golden plovers, and Bewick's swans. The estuary is protected as a nature reserve, Ramsar site, and Site of Special Scientific Interest, managed by the RSPB.

History

The Roman fort of Gariannonum (Burgh Castle) overlooks the estuary, which in Roman times was part of a much larger inlet of the sea.
