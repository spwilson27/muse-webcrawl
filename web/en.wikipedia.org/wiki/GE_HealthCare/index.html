GE HealthCare

GE HealthCare Technologies Inc. is an American healthcare company that produces medical technology with emphasis on medical imaging equipment, patient monitoring, and diagnostics. It was spun off from General Electric (GE) in January 2023 and is headquartered in Chicago, Illinois.

History

GE's healthcare business traces its roots to the Victor X-Ray Corporation, acquired in 1926, and grew into one of the world's largest makers of CT, MRI, ultrasound, and X-ray systems. As part of the breakup of the GE conglomerate announced in 2021, GE HealthCare became a standalone public company listed on Nasdaq under the ticker GEHC in January 2023. Peter Arduini serves as president and CEO.

Business

The company reports segments in Imaging, Ultrasound, Patient Care Solutions, and Pharmaceutical Diagnostics. Its products include the Revolution CT scanner family, SIGNA MRI systems, LOGIQ and Vivid ultrasound, CARESCAPE patient monitors, and contrast agents and radiopharmaceuticals such as Omnipaque and DaTscan. It employs roughly 50,000 people and sells in more than 160 countries, with annual revenues on the order of $19–20 billion.
