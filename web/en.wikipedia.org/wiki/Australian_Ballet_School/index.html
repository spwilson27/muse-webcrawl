The Australian Ballet School

The Australian Ballet School is the national ballet training institution of Australia, founded in 1964 by Dame Peggy van Praagh, the founding artistic director of The Australian Ballet. It is based at The Australian Ballet Centre in Southbank, Melbourne.

Training

The school offers an eight-level programme for students aged roughly 8 to 19, combining classical ballet technique with academic education delivered in partnership with schools in Melbourne. Most graduates join The Australian Ballet, of which it is the primary feeder school, or other Australian and international companies.

History

The school opened in 1964, two years after The Australian Ballet itself was founded. Notable directors include Margaret Scott (the founding director of the school programme in its early decades) and Gailene Stock; Lisa Pavane, a former principal artist, became director in 2015.

The school runs audition tours nationally and an Interstate Training Program for younger students.
