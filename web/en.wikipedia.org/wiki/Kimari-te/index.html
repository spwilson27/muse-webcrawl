Kimari-te

Kimarite (Japanese: 決まり手, kimari-te, "deciding hand") are the winning techniques used in sumo wrestling. The Japan Sumo Association recognises an official list of techniques — currently 82 basic kimarite, plus wins by non-technique (such as disqualification or a wrestler stepping out without a technique).

Common techniques

The most frequent kimarite include yorikiri (force out by pushing an opponent from the ring while holding the mawashi), oshidashi (push out), hatakikomi (slap down), uwatenage (outer-arm throw) and shitate-nage (inner-arm throw). Rare techniques award a wrestler extra prize recognition and are announced to the crowd.
