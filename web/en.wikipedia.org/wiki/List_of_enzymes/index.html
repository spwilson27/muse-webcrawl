List of enzymes

This is a list of enzymes, organised by the Enzyme Commission (EC) classification. Enzymes are biological catalysts, almost all of them proteins, that accelerate chemical reactions in living organisms. The EC system groups enzymes into seven classes according to the reaction they catalyse.

EC 1: Oxidoreductases

  • Alcohol dehydrogenase — oxidises alcohols
  • Lactate dehydrogenase — interconverts pyruvate and lactate
  • Cytochrome c oxidase — terminal enzyme of the respiratory chain
  • Glucose oxidase — oxidises glucose to hydrogen peroxide and gluconic acid

EC 2: Transferases

  • Hexokinase — phosphorylates glucose in glycolysis
  • Alanine transaminase — transfers amino groups
  • DNA methyltransferase — adds methyl groups to DNA

EC 3: Hydrolases

  • Amylase — hydrolyses starch
  • Lipase — hydrolyses fats
  • Pepsin and trypsin — proteases of digestion
  • Lactase — hydrolyses lactose

EC 4: Lyases

  • Pyruvate decarboxylase — removes carbon dioxide from pyruvate
  • Fumarase — hydrates/dehydrates fumarate and malate

EC 5: Isomerases

  • Triose phosphate isomerase — interconverts glycolytic intermediates
  • Topoisomerase — changes DNA topology

EC 6: Ligases

  • DNA ligase — joins DNA strands, essential in replication and repair
  • Pyruvate carboxylase — joins pyruvate and carbon dioxide

EC 7: Translocases

  • ATP synthase components and membrane transporters classified since 2018 under this class
