Star Wars: The Force Awakens

Star Wars: The Force Awakens (also known as Star Wars: Episode VII – The Force Awakens) is a 2015 American epic space opera film produced, co-written, and directed by J. J. Abrams. The sequel to Return of the Jedi (1983), it is the seventh film in the "Skywalker Saga". Set thirty years after Return of the Jedi, the film follows Rey, Finn, Poe Dameron, and Han Solo's search for Luke Skywalker and their fight in the Resistance against the First Order, led by Kylo Ren and Supreme Leader Snoke.

Plot

Thirty years after the defeat of the Galactic Empire, Luke Skywalker, the last Jedi, has vanished. From the Empire's ashes has arisen the First Order, which seeks to destroy the New Republic; the Republic-backed Resistance, led by General Leia Organa, opposes it. Resistance pilot Poe Dameron obtains a map fragment showing Luke's location and hides it in his droid BB-8 on the desert planet Jakku. When the First Order attacks, stormtrooper Finn defects and helps Poe escape, then teams up with scavenger Rey and BB-8. Reuniting with Han Solo and Chewbacca aboard the Millennium Falcon, the group joins the Resistance's assault on Starkiller Base. Han confronts his son — Kylo Ren, born Ben Solo — and is killed. Rey, strong in the Force, battles Kylo Ren as the base is destroyed, then journeys to the ocean planet Ahch-To, where she finds Luke and offers him his old lightsaber.

Production

The Walt Disney Company acquired Lucasfilm in October 2012 for $4.05 billion and announced a new trilogy. Abrams was hired to direct in January 2013; Michael Arndt wrote the initial screenplay before Abrams and Lawrence Kasdan took over. Principal photography began in April 2014 at Pinewood Studios and on location in Abu Dhabi and Iceland. John Williams returned to compose the score.

Release and reception

The film premiered in Los Angeles on December 14, 2015, and was released in the United States on December 18. It received positive reviews and grossed over $2 billion worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing film of 2015 and, at the time, the third-highest-grossing film of all time. It was nominated for five Academy Awards. Two sequels followed: The Last Jedi (2017) and The Rise of Skywalker (2019).
