Lex Luthor

Alexander Joseph "Lex" Luthor is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by Jerry Siegel and Joe Shuster, he first appeared in Action Comics #23 (1940) and is the archenemy of Superman.

Lex Luthor
First appearanceAction Comics #23 (1940)
Created byJerry Siegel, Joe Shuster
AffiliationLexCorp; Legion of Doom; Injustice League
AbilitiesGenius intellect; powered warsuit

Characterisation

Originally a mad scientist, Luthor was reinvented in the 1980s as a ruthless billionaire industrialist, head of LexCorp in Metropolis, driven by ego and a belief that Superman is a threat to humanity. He has served as President of the United States in the comics and been portrayed on screen by Gene Hackman, Kevin Spacey and Jesse Eisenberg.
