Brian Lara

Brian Charles Lara (born 2 May 1969) is a Trinidadian former West Indies cricketer, widely regarded as one of the greatest batsmen of all time, nicknamed 'The Prince of Port of Spain'.

Career

Lara debuted for the West Indies in 1990 and captained the side in several spells. In 1994 he scored 375 against England, breaking Garfield Sobers' Test record, then 501 not out for Warwickshire, the highest first-class score. In 2004 he reclaimed the Test record with 400 not out against England in Antigua.

Statistics and legacy

He played 131 Tests scoring 11,953 runs with 34 centuries, and 299 ODIs scoring 10,405 runs. He retired in 2007 after the World Cup in the Caribbean. The Brian Lara Cricket Academy stadium in Trinidad is named for him. Contemporaries include Viv Richards of the preceding generation.
