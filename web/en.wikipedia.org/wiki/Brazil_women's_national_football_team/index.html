Brazil women's national football team

The Brazil women's national football team (Portuguese: Seleção Brasileira de Futebol Feminino) represents Brazil in international women's association football and is governed by the Brazilian Football Confederation (CBF). Nicknamed As Canarinhas ("the Female Canaries"), the team has participated in every FIFA Women's World Cup since the inaugural tournament in 1991, finishing runners-up in 2007, and has won the Copa América Femenina a record number of times. Its most famous player, Marta, is widely regarded as one of the greatest footballers of all time and is the all-time top scorer in World Cup history, men's or women's.

History

Women's football was formally banned in Brazil from 1941 until 1979, which hampered the sport's development. The national team played its first official international in 1986 and took part in a FIFA invitational tournament in China in 1988. Brazil competed at the first Women's World Cup in 1991 and reached the semi-finals in 1999, finishing third. At the 2004 Athens Olympics the team won silver, and its golden generation peaked at the 2007 World Cup in China, where a celebrated 4–0 semi-final victory over the United States took Brazil to the final, lost 2–0 to Germany. Brazil took another Olympic silver at Beijing 2008 and further silver at the Paris 2024 Games.

Copa América

Brazil is the dominant force in South American women's football, winning the Copa América Femenina in 1991, 1995, 1998, 2003, 2010, 2014, 2018, and 2022, with Argentina's 2006 victory the only interruption of its title run.

Notable players

Marta Vieira da Silva, six times named FIFA World Player of the Year, scored a record 17 World Cup goals across six tournaments. Other landmarks include midfielder Formiga, who played at seven World Cups and seven Olympic Games; forward Cristiane, the Olympics' all-time top scorer; and Sissi, star of the 1999 team. Despite the team's success, players have repeatedly campaigned for better pay, investment, and professional structures within Brazilian women's football.
