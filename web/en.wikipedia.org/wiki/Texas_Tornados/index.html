Texas Tornados

Origin: San Antonio, Texas, United States

Genres: Tejano, Tex-Mex, country, rock and roll, conjunto

Years active: 1989–present (with interruptions)

Members (classic lineup): Doug Sahm, Augie Meyers, Freddy Fender, Flaco Jiménez

The Texas Tornados is a Tex-Mex supergroup formed in 1989, bringing together Doug Sahm and Augie Meyers (of the Sir Douglas Quintet), Tejano accordionist Flaco Jiménez, and country singer Freddy Fender. The band blends conjunto, country, blues, and rock and roll, singing in both English and Spanish.

Career

Their self-titled debut album (1990) included the hit "(Hey Baby) Que Paso" and the single "Who Were You Thinkin' Of". The Tornados won the Grammy Award for Best Mexican-American Performance for "Soy de San Luis" from the album Zone of Our Own (1991). Subsequent albums include Hangin' on by a Thread (1992), 4 Aces (1996), and Live from the Limo, Vol. 1 (1999).

Later years

Doug Sahm died in 1999 and Freddy Fender in 2006. The group later reconvened with Shawn Sahm (Doug's son) and released Está Bueno (2010), which included some of Fender's final recordings. The Tornados remain emblematic of the San Antonio Tex-Mex sound.
