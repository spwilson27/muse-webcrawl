Rhône wine

Rhône wine region
CountryFrance
Main grapesSyrah, Grenache, Mourvèdre, Viognier
Key appellationsCôte-Rôtie, Hermitage, Châteauneuf-du-Pape, Côtes du Rhône

Rhône wine is produced in the Rhône Valley of south-eastern France, along the Rhône river from Lyon to the Mediterranean. The region divides into the Northern Rhône, dominated by Syrah, and the larger Southern Rhône, known for blends led by Grenache.

Northern Rhône

The steep granite slopes of the north produce the appellations Côte-Rôtie, Condrieu, Hermitage, Cornas, and Saint-Joseph. Reds here are almost exclusively Syrah; whites include Viognier, Marsanne, and Roussanne.

Southern Rhône

The south, with its Mediterranean climate and famous galets roulés (rounded stones), produces Châteauneuf-du-Pape, Gigondas, Vacqueyras, and the high-volume Côtes du Rhône appellations, typically blends of Grenache, Syrah, and Mourvèdre ("GSM"). Rhône varieties have been widely planted in Australia and California.
