List of best-selling albums

This is a list of the world's best-selling albums of recorded music. To appear on the list, an album must have sold at least 20 million copies worldwide, with figures supported by reliable sources. The best-selling album of all time is Michael Jackson's Thriller (1982), with claimed sales of around 70 million copies.
