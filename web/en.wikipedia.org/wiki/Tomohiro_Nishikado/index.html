Tomohiro Nishikado

Tomohiro Nishikado (born March 31, 1944) is a Japanese video game developer best known as the creator of Space Invaders (1978), the landmark arcade game that launched the shoot-'em-up genre and helped ignite the golden age of arcade video games.

Career

Nishikado joined Taito in 1969 and worked on electromechanical and early video games including Speed Race (1974), one of the earliest racing games with scrolling graphics. For Space Invaders he designed custom hardware based on the Intel 8080 because contemporary computers were not powerful enough; the game's accelerating aliens were an accidental result of the hardware rendering faster as sprites were destroyed, which Nishikado kept as a difficulty feature.

Impact

Space Invaders was a phenomenon in Japan and worldwide, quadrupling production of 100-yen coins in Japan and becoming the first blockbuster arcade title. Nishikado later founded the developer Dreams and continued consulting in the industry; he is credited with foundational contributions to game design and Japanese pop culture.
