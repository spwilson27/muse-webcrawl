ENTSO-E
European Network of Transmission System Operators for Electricity
Founded: 2008, Brussels

The European Network of Transmission System Operators for Electricity (ENTSO-E) represents transmission system operators (TSOs) from across Europe. Established under the EU's Third Energy Package and operational from 2009, it promotes a secure, integrated European electricity market and transmission system.

Role

ENTSO-E develops network codes and guidelines, prepares the Ten-Year Network Development Plan, coordinates system operation and adequacy assessments, and publishes transparency data on generation, load and cross-border flows. Its member TSOs operate the synchronous grid of continental Europe, one of the world's largest interconnected power systems.

Members and events

Membership spans EU states and several non-EU countries whose grids are synchronised or associated. After Russia's 2022 invasion of Ukraine, the Ukrainian and Moldovan grids were emergency-synchronised with continental Europe in March 2022, and the Baltic states disconnected from the Russian-Belarusian BRELL ring and joined the continental system in February 2025.
