Kawasaki Heavy Industries is one of Japan's major heavy-industry conglomerates.

Kawasaki Heavy Industries Ltd. (川崎重工業株式会社, Kawasaki Jūkōgyō Kabushiki-gaisha) is a Japanese public multinational corporation headquartered in Kobe, Hyōgo Prefecture. It manufactures ships, railway rolling stock, aircraft and aerospace systems, motorcycles, industrial robots, gas turbines and energy plant equipment.

History

The company traces its origins to 1878, when Kawasaki Shōzō founded the Kawasaki Tsukiji Shipyard in Tokyo. Incorporated in 1896 as Kawasaki Dockyard Co., Ltd., the firm grew with Japanese naval expansion, building warships, submarines and merchant vessels. In the twentieth century it diversified into steel structures, rolling stock, aircraft and, after the Second World War, motorcycles and consumer products.

Business divisions

  • Aerospace Systems — military and civil aircraft, helicopters, aero engines and space systems.
  • Energy Solution & Marine Engineering — ships, submarines, gas turbines, industrial plants.
  • Precision Machinery & Robot — hydraulic equipment and industrial robots.
  • Rolling Stock — trains including Shinkansen vehicles and overseas transit cars.
  • Motorcycle & Engine — Kawasaki motorcycles, personal watercraft and utility vehicles (spun off in 2021 as Kawasaki Motors, Ltd.).

Kawasaki is a member of the Mizuho keiretsu and is listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange.
