James Mark Burnett (born 17 July 1960) is a British-American television producer who is widely credited with pioneering modern reality television. He created or produced Survivor, The Apprentice, Shark Tank, The Voice and Are You Smarter than a 5th Grader?

Early life

Born in London, Burnett served as a paratrooper in the British Army, including in the Falklands War, before emigrating to the United States in 1982, where he worked jobs including as a nanny and selling T-shirts on Venice Beach.

Career

After success in adventure racing with Eco-Challenge, Burnett adapted the Swedish format Expedition Robinson into the American Survivor (2000), a ratings phenomenon. The Apprentice (2004), hosted by Donald Trump, further cemented his reputation. He served as chairman of MGM Television from 2015 to 2022.

Faith-based productions

With his wife Roma Downey he produced the History channel miniseries The Bible (2013) and the film Son of God (2014). Burnett has won multiple Emmy Awards and was named one of Time magazine's 100 most influential people.
