Company Shops

Company Shops was the name of a railroad community in North Carolina, United States, that developed around the repair shops of the North Carolina Railroad. The settlement grew into the modern city of Burlington, North Carolina.

History

In the 1850s, the North Carolina Railroad chose a central location in Alamance County for its maintenance and repair shops. The community that grew around the facility was known simply as "Company Shops." A post office by that name was established in 1859.

Becoming Burlington

When the railroad moved its shops elsewhere in the 1880s, local leaders sought incorporation under a new name. In 1893 the town officially changed its name from Company Shops to Burlington.

Legacy

The name survives locally in references to the historic district and the restored train station area of downtown Burlington.
