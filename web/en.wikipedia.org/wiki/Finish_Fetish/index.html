Finish Fetish

Finish Fetish, also known as the "look of LA", is a style of art associated with a group of Los Angeles artists in the 1960s. The movement is characterised by slick, polished, industrially fabricated surfaces inspired by Southern California car and surf culture.

Artists

Artists associated with Finish Fetish include Billy Al Bengston, Larry Bell, Robert Irwin, Craig Kauffman, John McCracken, and Ken Price. Their works employed materials such as resin, lacquer, acrylic plastic, and polished metal.

Legacy

Closely related to the Light and Space movement, Finish Fetish art contrasted with the rough, assemblage aesthetics of East Coast Pop art and helped define a distinct West Coast minimalism.
