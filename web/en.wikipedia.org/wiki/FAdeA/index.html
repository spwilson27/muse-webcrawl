FAdeA (Fábrica Argentina de Aviones "Brigadier San Martín" S.A.) is Argentina's principal aircraft manufacturer, based in Córdoba. Founded in 1927 as the Fábrica Militar de Aviones (FMA), it was the first major aircraft factory in Latin America.

History

The FMA built licence-produced European designs before developing indigenous aircraft, notably the Pulqui series of early jet fighters designed with the involvement of the German engineer Kurt Tank after the Second World War. The factory later produced the IA-58 Pucará counter-insurgency aircraft and the IA-63 Pampa jet trainer. Briefly privatised under Lockheed Martin in the 1990s, it returned to state ownership in 2009 under its present name.

Products

FAdeA maintains and modernises aircraft for the Argentine Air Force, manufactures the Pampa trainer and Pucará, and performs maintenance and component work for commercial and military customers.
