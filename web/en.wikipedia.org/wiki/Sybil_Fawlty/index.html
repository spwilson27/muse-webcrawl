Sybil Fawlty is a fictional character in the BBC sitcom Fawlty Towers, played by Prunella Scales. She is the wife of hotelier Basil Fawlty and co-proprietor of the Torquay hotel that gives the series its name. Where Basil is frantic and incompetent, Sybil is the more capable manager of the two — though she often prefers gossiping on the telephone to doing any work herself.

Character

Sybil is recognisable by her distinctive, high-pitched laugh and her withering summons of "Basil!", deployed whenever her husband's schemes unravel. She is frequently shown chatting to her friend Audrey on the phone ("I know...") while Basil struggles around her. Their marriage is portrayed as one of mutual exasperation: Basil refers to her with a string of unflattering nicknames, usually out of her earshot.

Role in the series

Across the twelve episodes (1975 and 1979), Sybil serves as the voice of practical authority, rescuing situations Basil has made worse and intimidating him into line. In "The Anniversary," her (mistaken) belief that Basil has forgotten their wedding anniversary drives the plot. Prunella Scales's performance is widely regarded as one of British sitcom's finest supporting turns.
