Captain America: Civil War

Captain America: Civil War is a 2016 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics character Captain America, produced by Marvel Studios and directed by Anthony and Joe Russo. It is the sequel to Captain America: The First Avenger (2011) and Captain America: The Winter Soldier (2014), and the 13th film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

Captain America: Civil War
Directors: Anthony and Joe Russo
Starring: Chris Evans, Robert Downey Jr., Scarlett Johansson
Release: 2016
Box office: approx. $1.15 billion

Plot

After collateral damage from the Avengers' missions, the United Nations' Sokovia Accords propose oversight of the team. Steve Rogers opposes the accords while Tony Stark supports them, splitting the Avengers. The conflict deepens over Bucky Barnes, framed for a bombing by Helmut Zemo, who seeks to destroy the Avengers from within by revealing that Barnes killed Stark's parents.

Reception

The film grossed over $1.15 billion worldwide and was praised for its ensemble cast, action, and themes. It introduced Spider-Man (Tom Holland) and Black Panther (Chadwick Boseman) to the MCU.
