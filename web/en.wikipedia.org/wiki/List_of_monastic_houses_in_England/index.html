List of monastic houses in England

This is a list of monastic houses in England: abbeys, priories, friaries, and other religious houses founded before the Dissolution of the Monasteries under Henry VIII (1536–1541), together with later refoundations.

Notable houses

  • Fountains Abbey (Yorkshire) — Cistercian, founded 1132; among the best-preserved ruins in England.
  • Rievaulx Abbey — Cistercian, founded 1132.
  • Glastonbury Abbey — Benedictine, associated with Arthurian legend.
  • Canterbury (St Augustine's and Christ Church) — from the Gregorian mission of 597.
  • Westminster Abbey — Benedictine; refounded and still a royal peculiar.
  • Lindisfarne Priory — founded 635 by St Aidan; raided by Vikings in 793.
  • Tintern, Kirkstall, Byland, Jervaulx — further major Cistercian houses.
  • Battle Abbey — founded by William the Conqueror on the site of Hastings.

Orders

Houses belonged to orders including the Benedictines, Cistercians, Cluniacs, Augustinian canons, Premonstratensians, Carthusians, and the mendicant friars (Franciscans, Dominicans, Carmelites, Augustinian friars).
