List of piers in the United Kingdom

At their Victorian and Edwardian peak, around 100 pleasure piers stood around the coast of the United Kingdom; roughly half survive today. The earliest, Ryde Pier on the Isle of Wight (opened 1814), was built as a landing stage, while later piers combined promenading, entertainment pavilions, theatres and steamer berths. Many were designed by the engineer Eugenius Birch, who built fourteen piers including those at Margate, Aberystwyth and Brighton (the West Pier).

The National Piers Society, founded in 1979, campaigns for the preservation of piers and awards an annual Pier of the Year prize. Several piers have been restored with Heritage Lottery funding, including Hastings Pier, which reopened in 2016 and won the Stirling Prize for architecture in 2017.
