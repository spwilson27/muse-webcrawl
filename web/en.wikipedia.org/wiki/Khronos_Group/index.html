Khronos Group

The Khronos Group, Inc. is an American non-profit member-funded industry consortium based in Beaverton, Oregon, that develops royalty-free open standards for graphics, computing and media on a wide range of platforms and devices. Founded in 2000, it has more than 150 member companies.

Standards

Khronos is best known for OpenGL and its successors and relatives: Vulkan (a low-overhead graphics and compute API released in 2016), OpenCL for heterogeneous computing, OpenGL ES for embedded systems, WebGL for 3D graphics in browsers, glTF for 3D asset transmission, OpenXR for virtual and augmented reality, SYCL for single-source C++ heterogeneous programming, and OpenVX for computer vision acceleration.

Structure

Members participate in working groups that draft specifications; conformance test suites and an adopters programme underpin certification, and the group's standards are generally royalty-free for implementers. Khronos assumed stewardship of OpenGL from the Architecture Review Board in 2006.
