List of governors-general of India

The Governor-General of India was the representative of the British monarch (and originally of the East India Company) and the head of the administration in British India. The office was created in 1773 as Governor-General of the Presidency of Fort William (Bengal); the Charter Act of 1833 made its holder Governor-General of India. After 1858 the officeholder was also styled Viceroy, representing the Crown directly. The last holder under British rule was Lord Mountbatten; after independence the Dominion of India kept a Governor-General until 1950, when India became a republic with a President.
