Purity ring

A purity ring, also called a promise ring, chastity ring or abstinence ring, is a ring worn as a symbol of a pledge to practise sexual abstinence until marriage. The practice is associated mainly with evangelical Christian groups in the United States.

History

The rings spread in the 1990s through abstinence education movements such as True Love Waits and the Silver Ring Thing (later Unaltered), often given at purity balls or pledge ceremonies, sometimes inscribed with phrases such as "True Love Waits". Some celebrities publicised wearing them in the 2000s, and the practice has been both promoted by supporters and criticised by researchers and former wearers who question the effectiveness and framing of abstinence pledges.
