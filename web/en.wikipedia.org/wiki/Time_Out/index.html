Time Out

Time Out is a global media and hospitality brand that publishes city guides and listings for events, restaurants, bars, film, theatre and nightlife. It began in London in 1968, founded by Tony Elliott, initially as a small listings magazine.

History

Time Out London became an influential guide to the city's culture. Editions expanded to New York (1995) and dozens of other cities worldwide. Print editions were suspended in many cities during the COVID-19 pandemic, with some titles continuing online only. In London the magazine became free in 2012.

Time Out Market

The company launched food halls under the Time Out Market name, beginning in Lisbon in 2014, curating restaurants and bars from each city under one roof, with further markets in cities including New York, Dubai and Barcelona.
