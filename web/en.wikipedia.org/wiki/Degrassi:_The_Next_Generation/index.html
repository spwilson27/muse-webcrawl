Degrassi: The Next Generation

Genre: Teen drama

Created by: Linda Schuyler; Yan Moore

Country: Canada

Seasons: 14

Episodes: 385

Original release: 2001 – 2015

Degrassi: The Next Generation is a Canadian teen drama television series and the fourth series in the Degrassi franchise, following The Kids of Degrassi Street, Degrassi Junior High, and Degrassi High. It premiered on CTV in October 2001 and concluded in 2015 after fourteen seasons; from season ten it aired on MuchMusic in Canada and TeenNick in the United States. The series was succeeded by Degrassi: Next Class on Netflix.

Series overview

Set at the fictional Degrassi Community School in Toronto, the series follows successive cohorts of students confronting issues including bullying, sexuality, mental illness, pregnancy, substance abuse, and school violence, continuing the franchise's reputation for addressing difficult subjects realistically. The first seasons featured a new generation connected to the original series: Emma Nelson, the daughter of Spike from Degrassi Junior High, whose generation included characters played by Miriam McDonald, Cassie Steele, Stacey Farber, Aubrey Graham (later known as the rapper Drake), and Lauren Collins. As cast members graduated, the show rotated in new classes of students, eventually becoming a telenovela-style daily series during the summer months under its later title, simply Degrassi.

Reception and legacy

The show won numerous Gemini Awards and international honours, and was praised for its frank storylines and for launching several acting careers. It remains one of Canada's most successful television exports.
