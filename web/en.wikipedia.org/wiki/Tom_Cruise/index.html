Thomas Cruise Mapother IV (born July 3, 1962), known as Tom Cruise, is an American actor and producer. One of the world's highest-paid actors, he has received various accolades, including an honorary Palme d'Or and three Golden Globe Awards, in addition to nominations for four Academy Awards. His films have grossed over $12 billion worldwide, making him one of the highest-grossing box-office stars of all time. He is known for performing his own stunts, increasingly elaborate in the Mission: Impossible franchise.

Career

Cruise broke through with leading roles in Risky Business (1983) and Top Gun (1986). Critical acclaim followed for The Color of Money (1986), Rain Man (1988) and Born on the Fourth of July (1989), for which he received his first Oscar nomination. In the 1990s he starred in hits including A Few Good Men (1992), Jerry Maguire (1996) and Magnolia (1999), and began the Mission: Impossible series (1996– ), which he produces through Cruise/Wagner Productions. Top Gun: Maverick (2022) became his highest-grossing film, earning over $1.4 billion.

Personal life

Cruise is an outspoken advocate of the Church of Scientology, which he joined around 1990 and credits with helping him overcome dyslexia. His marriages to Mimi Rogers, Nicole Kidman and Katie Holmes drew extensive media attention, as did a famous 2005 appearance on The Oprah Winfrey Show and his public criticism of psychiatry. In 2025 he was announced as a recipient of an Honorary Academy Award for his contributions to cinema.
