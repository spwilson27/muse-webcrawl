Wilco is an American rock band based in Chicago, Illinois. The band was formed in 1994 by singer-songwriter Jeff Tweedy and bassist John Stirratt from the remaining members of the alt-country group Uncle Tupelo, following that band's breakup. Taking their name from the voice procedure phrase "will comply", Wilco evolved from alt-country beginnings into one of the most critically acclaimed American rock bands of their era, blending rock, folk, country, and experimental and avant-garde elements.

Yankee Hotel Foxtrot and breakthrough

The band's fourth album, Yankee Hotel Foxtrot (recorded 2000–2001), was rejected by their label, Reprise Records; Wilco acquired the rights, streamed the album free on their website, and eventually released it in 2002 on Nonesuch Records to widespread acclaim — it is frequently ranked among the greatest albums of its decade. The ordeal was documented in Sam Jones's film I Am Trying to Break Your Heart. Its follow-up, A Ghost Is Born (2004), won the Grammy Award for Best Alternative Music Album.

Later work

With a stabilised line-up including guitarist Nels Cline and drummer Glenn Kotche, Wilco released Sky Blue Sky (2007), Wilco (The Album) (2009), The Whole Love (2011), Star Wars (2015), Schmilco (2016), Ode to Joy (2019), Cruel Country (2022), and Cousin (2023), among others, and founded their own label, dBpm Records. The band also recorded the Mermaid Avenue albums of unfinished Woody Guthrie lyrics with Billy Bragg.
