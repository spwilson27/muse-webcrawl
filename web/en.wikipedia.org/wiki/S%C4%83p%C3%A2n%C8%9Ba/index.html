Săpânța

CountryRomania
CountyMaramureș
Known forMerry Cemetery; Săpânța-Peri Monastery

Săpânța is a commune in Maramureș County, Romania, near the Tisza river and the Ukrainian border. It is internationally known for its Merry Cemetery (Cimitirul Vesel), where bright blue wooden crosses are painted with scenes from the deceased's life and humorous or candid epitaphs in verse.

Merry Cemetery

The tradition began in the 1930s with local craftsman Stan Ioan Pătraș, who carved the first crosses with poems. His apprentice Dumitru Pop Tincu continued the work. The cemetery's vivid "Săpânța blue" crosses have made it a major tourist attraction and a UNESCO-associated cultural landmark in Maramureș.

Săpânța-Peri Monastery

The commune also contains the Săpânța-Peri Monastery, whose wooden church tower reaches about 78 metres, among the tallest wooden church structures in the world, rebuilt on the site of a historic monastery destroyed in the 18th century.
