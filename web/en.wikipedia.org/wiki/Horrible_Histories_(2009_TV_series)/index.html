Horrible Histories (2009 TV series)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The cast of Horrible Histories

Horrible Histories is a British children's live-action historical and musical sketch comedy television series, based on the book series of the same name by Terry Deary. It was produced by Lion Television with Citrus Television for CBBC and first aired in 2009. The original run comprised five series (2009–2013), followed by a reboot with a new cast from 2015.

Format

Each episode presents sketches, songs, cartoons, and fake advertisements covering historical eras presented in the books, including the Rotten Romans, Terrible Tudors, Vile Victorians, Woeful Second World War, Gorgeous Georgians, and Groovy Greeks. Recurring features include "Stupid Deaths", in which the Grim Reaper interviews historical figures about their demise; fake news bulletins; and pastiches of popular songs and television formats. A puppet rat, Rattus Rattus, links segments and reassures viewers that the (mostly) gory details are true. Songs became a signature element, including the "Monarchs' Song" and historical parodies that later won a strong adult following online.

Cast and reception

The core troupe of the original run — Mathew Baynton, Simon Farnaby, Martha Howe-Douglas, Jim Howick, Laurence Rickard, and Ben Willbond — wrote and performed much of the material and later went on to create Yonderland, Bill, and the BBC sitcom Ghosts. The series won numerous awards, including multiple BAFTA Children's Awards, and was praised for combining genuine historical accuracy with anarchic humour appealing to adults as well as children.
