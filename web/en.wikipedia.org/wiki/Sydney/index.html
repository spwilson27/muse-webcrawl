Sydney

The Sydney Opera House and harbour

Sydney is the capital city of New South Wales and the most populous city in Australia, with about 5.3 million residents in Greater Sydney. It is located on Australia's east coast around Sydney Harbour (Port Jackson), considered one of the world's great natural harbours.

Sydney was founded in 1788 as a British penal colony — the first European settlement in Australia — on land inhabited for tens of thousands of years by Aboriginal peoples including the Eora. The Sydney Opera House, with its sail-like shells designed by Jørn Utzon and opened in 1973, and the Sydney Harbour Bridge ('the Coathanger', 1932) are national icons. Sydney hosted the 2000 Olympic Games, and its beaches — Bondi, Manly — and mild climate define its outdoor lifestyle.
