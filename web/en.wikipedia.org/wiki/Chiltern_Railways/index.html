Chiltern Railways is a British train operating company that runs passenger services principally from London Marylebone along the Chiltern Main Line to Birmingham and the London to Aylesbury Line via Amersham. It began operating in July 1996 as one of the first privatised rail franchises, under a management buyout team led by Adrian Shooter, and became notable for long-term investment commitments unusual in the franchising era.

History

The franchise was awarded for an initial seven years, then renegotiated in 2002 into a 20-year deal in exchange for substantial infrastructure investment, including redoubling sections of single track between Princes Risborough and Banbury, new platforms at Birmingham Moor Street, and the Evergreen programme of upgrades. In 2015 Chiltern opened a new route between London and Oxford, the first new rail link between London and a major city in over a century, built partly over a rebuilt chord at Bicester. Services to Oxford Parkway began in October 2015 and to Oxford station in December 2016.

Services and fleet

Chiltern operates fast services between Marylebone and Birmingham Moor Street and Snow Hill competing with the West Coast Main Line, plus commuter services to Aylesbury, High Wycombe, Banbury, Leamington Spa and Stratford-upon-Avon, and services north of Birmingham to Kidderminster. Its diesel fleet comprises Class 165 and Class 168 Turbos, Class 172 units, and locomotive-hauled sets with Class 68 locomotives. The company consistently ranked among the best-performing operators in passenger satisfaction surveys.
