Missile

Missile
DefinitionGuided, self-propelled weapon (distinct from unguided rockets)
RangesFrom man-portable to intercontinental ballistic (ICBM)
[Image: A ballistic missile launching from a silo in a cloud of smoke]

A missile is a guided weapons system that delivers a warhead by rocket or jet engine and steers itself in flight. Types include surface-to-air, anti-tank, cruise missiles that fly like pilotless aircraft, and ballistic missiles that arc through space — ICBMs tipped with nuclear warheads anchored Cold War deterrence.

Guidance

Guidance methods evolved from wire and radio to inertial, GPS, radar and infrared homing — the anti-tank and air-defence missiles of 2020s wars showcase cheap precision. Missile defence and proliferation are central to modern strategy alongside radar networks.
