Culture of Kerala

The culture of Kerala has developed over millennia in the southwestern Indian state of Kerala, influenced by its geography between the Western Ghats and the Arabian Sea, by maritime trade, and by Hindu, Christian, Muslim and Jewish communities living side by side.

Performing arts

Kathakali, a story-play with elaborate costume and makeup; Mohiniyattam dance; Theyyam ritual performance; Kalaripayattu martial art; and Carnatic and Sopana music are emblematic. Temple and church festivals feature caparisoned elephants and percussion ensembles such as Panchavadyam.

Language and literature

Malayalam is the official language, with a rich literary tradition and a major film industry (Mollywood). Onam, the harvest festival centred on the legend of Mahabali, is celebrated by all communities, with the sadhya feast served on banana leaves.

Cuisine and dress

Rice, coconut, seafood and spices dominate; traditional dress includes the mundu and Kasavu sari with gold border.
