The Australian Ballet School

The Australian Ballet School is the national ballet training institution of Australia and the official school of The Australian Ballet. Located in Melbourne, Victoria, it was founded in 1964 by Dame Margaret Scott.

Established1964
LocationSouthbank, Melbourne, Australia
FounderMargaret Scott
AffiliationThe Australian Ballet

Training

The school offers an eight-year programme combining classical ballet training with academic education. The majority of dancers in The Australian Ballet are graduates of the school, and alumni dance with companies worldwide.

Facilities

The school is based at the Australian Ballet Centre in Southbank, sharing facilities with the parent company, and performs annual productions showcasing its senior students.
