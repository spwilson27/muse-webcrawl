Weekly Shōnen Jump (Japanese: 週刊少年ジャンプ) is a weekly shōnen manga anthology published in Japan by Shueisha under the Jump line of magazines. Launched in 1968, it is one of the longest-running and best-selling manga magazines in the world, and the flagship home of many of the most popular manga series ever made, including Dragon Ball, One Piece, Naruto, Slam Dunk, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Bleach, Demon Slayer, and Jujutsu Kaisen.

History

Shueisha launched Shōnen Jump in 1968 as a fortnightly magazine; it became weekly the following year. Circulation climbed through the 1980s and peaked during the magazine's so-called "Golden Age" in the mid-1990s at over 6.5 million copies per week. Print circulation has since declined with the rise of digital distribution, but the magazine remains the centre of the Jump franchise, now complemented by the Shonen Jump+ digital app.

Editorial system

Series in the magazine are ranked by weekly reader surveys (the famous popularity polls printed as postcards), and consistently low-ranking series are cancelled, sometimes within a few months. The magazine's long-standing editorial motto is "friendship, effort, victory" (友情・努力・勝利), themes said to underpin its biggest hits. New artists are scouted through contests such as the Tezuka Award.

International editions

Viz Media published a North American edition from 2003 to 2012, then a digital Weekly Shonen Jump until 2018, after which chapters moved to the Shonen Jump digital vault with simultaneous English publication alongside Japan.
