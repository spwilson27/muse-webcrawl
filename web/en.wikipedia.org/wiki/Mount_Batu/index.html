Mount Batu

Mount Batu
Elevationabout 4,600 m
LocationBale Zone, Oromia Region, Ethiopia
RangeBale Mountains

Mount Batu is one of the highest peaks in Ethiopia, rising to roughly 4,600 metres (about 15,100 feet) in the Bale Mountains of the Oromia Region in the southeastern part of the country. It is generally ranked as the second-highest mountain entirely within Ethiopia, after Ras Dashen in the Simien Mountains, and the highest peak of the Bale massif.

Geography

The mountain forms part of the Sanetti Plateau, a vast Afro-alpine highland in Bale Mountains National Park characterised by moorland, glacial valleys, and giant lobelia and groundsels. The plateau, which lies mostly above 4,000 metres and is crossed by one of Africa's highest all-weather roads, supports exceptional wildlife, including the Ethiopian wolf — the world's rarest canid — and the mountain nyala. The peak's name, batu, means "stone" in the Oromo language, reflecting its bare rocky summit cone.
