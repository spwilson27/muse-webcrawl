Chess Olympiad

The Chess Olympiad is a biennial chess tournament in which teams from countries around the world compete. Organised by FIDE, it has been held regularly since 1927 (with an unofficial event in 1924) and comprises an open event and a women's event. Teams of four players (plus reserves) play matches against other national teams.

History

The first official Olympiad was held in London in 1927. The Soviet Union dominated the event from its debut in 1952 until the 1990s. Since then, countries such as Ukraine, Armenia, China, the United States and India have won the open title.

Format

The tournament is played as a Swiss-system team event over 11 rounds, with medals awarded to the top teams and to individual performers on each board.
