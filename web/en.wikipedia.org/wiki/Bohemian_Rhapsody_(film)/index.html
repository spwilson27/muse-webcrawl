Bohemian Rhapsody (film)

Bohemian Rhapsody is a 2018 biographical musical drama film directed by Bryan Singer from a screenplay by Anthony McCarten. The film follows the life of Freddie Mercury, the lead singer of the British rock band Queen, culminating in their performance at Live Aid at Wembley Stadium in 1985. It stars Rami Malek as Mercury.

Production and release

The film had a troubled production: Singer was fired partway through filming and replaced by Dexter Fletcher, though Singer retained sole director credit under DGA rules. Released in late 2018, it became the highest-grossing musical biopic ever, earning over $900 million worldwide.

Reception and awards

Reviews were mixed — critics praised Malek's performance but faulted the film's handling of chronology and Mercury's life — yet it won four Academy Awards, including Best Actor for Malek, and the Golden Globe for Best Motion Picture – Drama.
