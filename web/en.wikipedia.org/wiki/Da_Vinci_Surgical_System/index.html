Da Vinci Surgical System

The da Vinci Surgical System is a robotic surgical system made by Intuitive Surgical. Approved by the US Food and Drug Administration in 2000, it is used for minimally invasive procedures including prostatectomies, cardiac valve repair, hysterectomy and other gynaecological and urological surgery.

The system consists of a surgeon's console, where the operator views a magnified three-dimensional image and manipulates master controls; a patient-side cart with robotic arms holding instruments and an endoscopic camera; and a vision cart. The wristed instruments translate the surgeon's hand movements into smaller, tremor-filtered motions inside the body.

Generations and criticism

Successive models include the da Vinci S, Si, Xi and the single-port SP, plus the da Vinci 5 launched in 2024. Debate continues over cost-effectiveness versus conventional laparoscopy for some procedures, and early years saw reports of complications and questions about training and credentialing.
