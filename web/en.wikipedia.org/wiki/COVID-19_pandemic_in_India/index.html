COVID-19 pandemic in India
First case: 30 January 2020 (Kerala)
Major waves: 2020; Delta wave, spring 2021; Omicron, early 2022
Vaccines: Covishield, Covaxin

The COVID-19 pandemic in India began on 30 January 2020, when the first case was confirmed in Kerala in a student returning from Wuhan. India subsequently experienced one of the largest outbreaks in the world by confirmed case numbers.

First wave and lockdown

On 24 March 2020 the government imposed one of the world's strictest nationwide lockdowns, lasting in phases for over two months and triggering a mass migration of informal workers from cities to villages. Cases peaked in September 2020 before declining.

Delta wave

A devastating second wave in April–May 2021, driven by the Delta variant — first identified in India — overwhelmed hospitals, with acute shortages of oxygen and beds; official deaths rose sharply and excess-mortality studies estimated the true toll far higher. A mass vaccination campaign followed, administering over two billion doses by 2023.
