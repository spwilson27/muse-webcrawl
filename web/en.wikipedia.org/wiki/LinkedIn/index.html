LinkedIn is a business- and employment-oriented social networking service, mainly used for professional networking, careers and job seeking. Founded by Reid Hoffman and team members from PayPal and SocialNet, it launched in 2003 and was acquired by Microsoft in 2016 for about US$26.2 billion, as widely reported.

Members build a profile resembling a CV, connect with colleagues, follow companies, apply for jobs and publish posts and articles. Recruiters use it heavily for hiring; revenue comes from premium subscriptions, advertising and talent solutions.
