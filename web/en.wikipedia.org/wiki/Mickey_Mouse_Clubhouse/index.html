Mickey Mouse Clubhouse is an American children's animated television series produced by Disney Television Animation. It aired from 5 May 2006 to 6 November 2016, aimed at preschoolers, and featured Mickey Mouse and his friends solving everyday problems through basic maths, shapes and problem-solving.

Format

In each episode, Mickey greets viewers at the Clubhouse and presents a problem or adventure; the group summons Toodles, a flying computer who provides up to four "Mouseketools" to be used during the story. Mickey speaks directly to the audience, encouraging children to count, identify shapes and answer questions. Regular characters include Minnie Mouse, Donald Duck, Daisy Duck, Goofy and Pluto, with Pete appearing frequently as a friendly antagonist.

Music and legacy

The series features the songs "The Mickey Mouse Clubhouse Theme" and "Hot Dog!", written and performed by They Might Be Giants; the latter became a widespread internet meme. The show was succeeded in style by Mickey and the Roadster Racers (2017), and a revival, Mickey Mouse Clubhouse+, premiered in 2025.
