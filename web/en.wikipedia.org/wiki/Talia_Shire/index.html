Talia Shire (born Talia Rose Coppola; 25 April 1946) is an American actress best known for her roles as Connie Corleone in The Godfather films and as Adrian Balboa in the Rocky series. She received Academy Award nominations for both signature roles: Best Supporting Actress for The Godfather Part II (1974) and Best Actress for Rocky (1976).

Life and career

Shire was born in Lake Success, New York, into the Coppola family: her father Carmine Coppola was a composer and arranger, and her brother is director Francis Ford Coppola. She took the surname Shire from her first husband, composer David Shire. Her brother cast her as Connie, the sister of the Corleone brothers, in The Godfather (1972), a role she reprised in both sequels.

In 1976 she was cast as Adrian Pennino, the shy pet-shop clerk who marries Sylvester Stallone's Rocky Balboa, in Rocky. The character — and her cry of "Win, Rocky, win!" — became an audience favourite, and Shire played Adrian in five films, the character's death from cancer framing the story of Rocky Balboa (2006), in which she appeared in flashback. Her other film work includes Old Boyfriends (1979), Rad (1986), New York Stories (1989) and I ♥ Huckabees (2004). Her son by her second husband, producer Jack Schwartzman, is actor Jason Schwartzman.
