Penedès (wine region)

CountrySpain (Catalonia)
DesignationDenominació d'Origen Penedès
Key grapesXarel·lo, Macabeu, Parellada, Tempranillo
Famous forCava (classic Penedès), still wines

Penedès is a Catalan wine region in north-east Spain, between Barcelona and Tarragona, and one of the country's most important and historic wine-producing areas. The Denominació d'Origen Penedès protects its still wines, while most of its sparkling wine is sold as Cava or under the newer Clàssic Penedès designation.

Geography

The region divides into three zones: the coastal Baix Penedès, the central plain of the Alt Penedès around Vilafranca del Penedès, and higher vineyards in the mountains. The Mediterranean climate, moderated by altitude, suits both white and red varieties.

Wines

Penedès is the heartland of Cava, centred on Sant Sadurní d'Anoia, where firms such as Freixenet and Codorníu built a global sparkling-wine industry from the native grapes Macabeu, Xarel·lo and Parellada. The region also produces respected dry whites — especially from Xarel·lo — and reds from Tempranillo (locally Ull de Llebre), Garnacha and international varieties, pioneered by producers such as Torres.
