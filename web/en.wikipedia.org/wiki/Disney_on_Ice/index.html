Disney on Ice

Disney on Ice, originally Walt Disney's World on Ice, is a touring ice show produced by Feld Entertainment's Ice Follies and Holiday on Ice division under agreement with The Walt Disney Company. Aimed primarily at children, the shows feature figure skaters dressed as Disney cartoon characters in performances that each derive their music and plot from elements collected from various Disney films and properties; the stars of the show are credited as the Disney characters themselves.

History

The series began in 1981, when Feld Entertainment — owners of the Ice Follies and Holiday on Ice tours — negotiated with Disney to produce shows featuring Disney characters on ice. The first production toured internationally. Over the following decades, multiple touring units were created so that several different productions could play simultaneously in different parts of the world.

Productions

Touring productions have included revues built around classic characters such as Mickey Mouse and Minnie Mouse, as well as themed shows adapting films including The Lion King, Toy Story, Finding Nemo, Frozen, Moana and Encanto, and princess-themed revues. Long-running productions have included titles such as 100 Years of Magic, Let's Celebrate!, Treasure Trove, Passport to Adventure, Follow Your Heart, Reach for the Stars, Mickey's Search Party and Into the Magic. Productions rotate regularly, with new shows debuting and older ones retiring as Disney's film catalogue evolves.

Format

Performances typically run about two hours including an intermission and combine ensemble production numbers, pairs and solo skating, elaborate costumes and props, and pyrotechnic and flying effects. Shows are staged in arenas fitted with temporary ice surfaces.
