Disney Live!

Disney Live! is a series of touring stage shows produced by Feld Entertainment under license from The Walt Disney Company, featuring costumed performers portraying Disney characters in arena and theatre productions aimed primarily at young children and families. The shows combine songs and storylines from Disney films and television series with dance, puppetry, and audience participation, and tour extensively in North America and internationally.

Productions

The franchise began in 2004 with Disney Live! Mickey's Magic Show, a magic-themed production starring Mickey Mouse alongside illusionists. Subsequent productions have included Playhouse Disney Live! (later Disney Junior Live on Tour!), built around Disney Junior characters such as Mickey Mouse Clubhouse, Jake and the Never Land Pirates, and Doc McStuffins; Mickey's Music Festival; Mickey and Minnie's Doorway to Magic (2015); and Disney Live! Mickey and Minnie's Doorway to Magic variants touring abroad. Productions typically run about 90 minutes with an intermission and play mid-size arenas and performing-arts centres.

Relationship to other Disney stage brands

Disney Live! complements Feld Entertainment's long-running Disney On Ice skating shows, which target a similar family audience with large-scale arena spectacle, and Disney's own theatrical arm, which produces Broadway-style stage musicals. The Live! shows are designed for younger children, with familiar television characters, simple plots, and frequent invitations for the audience to sing and dance along.
