Jason Peter "Jay" Garrick is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. He is the first character known as the Flash. Created by writer Gardner Fox and artist Harry Lampert, he first appeared in Flash Comics #1 (cover-dated January 1940). He is distinguished by his winged metal helmet, modelled on that of the Roman god Mercury.

Fictional character biography

Jay Garrick was a college student who gained superhuman speed after inhaling hard water vapours during a laboratory accident. Adopting the identity of the Flash, he fought crime in Keystone City and became a founding member of the Justice Society of America, the first superhero team. After superhero comics declined in popularity, Garrick was retired in the early 1950s.

Revival

In the landmark story "Flash of Two Worlds!" (The Flash #123, 1961), Garrick was revealed to live on a parallel world, Earth-Two, and met his Silver Age counterpart Barry Allen. The meeting launched the DC Multiverse concept, and Garrick remained a mentor figure to later Flashes including Wally West and Bart Allen. Following the Flashpoint storyline and subsequent reboots, Garrick was reintroduced in various forms, eventually restored to continuity as an elder statesman of the Justice Society.

In other media

Garrick has appeared in animated series such as Justice League and in live action in The Flash television series, where he is portrayed by John Wesley Shipp.
