Oil campaign of World War II

The oil campaign of World War II was an Allied strategic bombing campaign against the petroleum, oil, and lubrication (POL) supply of Nazi Germany, conducted by the Royal Air Force and US Army Air Forces from 1940 to 1945. Targets included synthetic fuel plants, refineries such as Ploiești in Romania, oil fields, and storage depots.

The campaign intensified from May 1944 and is credited with causing severe fuel shortages that curtailed Luftwaffe training and operations, limited German mobility, and contributed significantly to the Allied victory.
