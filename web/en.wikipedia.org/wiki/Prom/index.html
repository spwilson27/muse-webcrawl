Prom

A prom, short for promenade dance, is a formal dance party for high school students, typically held near the end of the senior year. Proms are a major rite of passage in the United States and Canada and have spread, with variations, to other countries.

Customs

Students traditionally attend in formal attire—gowns and tuxedos—often in couples or groups. Customs include exchanging corsages and boutonnieres, arriving in rented limousines, professional photographs, and the crowning of a prom king and queen chosen by vote. Many schools hold separate junior and senior proms, and after-prom parties are often organized to provide supervised celebration.

History

Proms evolved in the late 19th and early 20th centuries from formal class banquets and promenade dances at American colleges and high schools, becoming widespread by the mid-20th century. They have been extensively depicted in popular culture, in films and television about high school life.
