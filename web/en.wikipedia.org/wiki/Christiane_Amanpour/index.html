Christiane Amanpour

Christiane Amanpour
Born12 January 1958, London
OccupationJournalist, CNN chief international anchor
[Image: Amanpour reporting from a conflict zone in the 1990s]

Christiane Amanpour (born 1958) is a British-Iranian journalist, CNN's chief international anchor, famed for war reporting from the Gulf War (1991) and the Bosnian war, and for her interview programme Amanpour on CNN International and PBS. Her insistence on being 'truthful, not neutral' summarises her approach to reporting atrocities.

Career

Amanpour joined CNN in 1983 and rose through its foreign desk; her Sarajevo reporting made her one of the best-known correspondents of her generation. She has interviewed most major world leaders and also presented ABC's This Week (2010–11).
