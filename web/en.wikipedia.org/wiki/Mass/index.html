Mass is a property of a physical body: a measure of its resistance to acceleration (inertia) and of the strength of its gravitational attraction to other bodies. The SI unit is the kilogram, since 2019 defined via the Planck constant rather than a physical artefact.

Mass and energy

In relativity, mass and energy are equivalent (E = mc²), explaining the energy of fission and of stars fusing hydrogen. Most of the visible universe's mass is ordinary matter; the discrepancy in galactic motion led to the hypothesis of dark matter's sibling puzzle, dark matter, sought by telescopes such as Webb.
