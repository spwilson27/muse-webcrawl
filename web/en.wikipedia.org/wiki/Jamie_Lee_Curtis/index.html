Jamie Lee Curtis (born November 22, 1958) is an American actress, producer and children's author. The daughter of actors Tony Curtis and Janet Leigh, she made her film debut as Laurie Strode in John Carpenter's horror film Halloween (1978), a role that established her as a "scream queen" and which she reprised in several sequels through Halloween Ends (2022).

Career

Curtis successfully moved beyond horror with performances in Trading Places (1983), A Fish Called Wanda (1988), True Lies (1994), which won her a Golden Globe, and the television series Anything but Love, which also won her a Golden Globe. Later successes include Freaky Friday (2003), Knives Out (2019) and Everything Everywhere All at Once (2022), for which she won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress.

Other work

She is the author of a number of bestselling children's picture books, beginning with When I Was Little: A Four-Year-Old's Memoir of Her Youth (1993), and is a blogger and activist, including advocacy for children's hospitals and addiction recovery.
