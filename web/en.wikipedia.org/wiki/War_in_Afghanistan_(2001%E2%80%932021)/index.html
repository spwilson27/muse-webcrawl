War in Afghanistan (2001–2021)

War in Afghanistan
Date: 7 October 2001 – 30 August 2021
Location: Afghanistan
Result: Taliban victory; fall of the Islamic Republic of Afghanistan

The War in Afghanistan was a conflict that began in October 2001 when the United States and its allies, responding to the September 11 attacks, invaded Afghanistan to dismantle al-Qaeda and remove the Taliban regime that harboured it. It became the longest war in American history.

Course of the war

The initial campaign quickly toppled the Taliban government, and an internationally backed Islamic Republic of Afghanistan was established. However, the Taliban regrouped and waged a prolonged insurgency against Afghan forces and the NATO-led coalition, which peaked at more than 130,000 foreign troops. The US surge of 2009–2011 was followed by a transition to Afghan-led security.

End of the war

The 2020 Doha Agreement between the United States and the Taliban set a timetable for withdrawal. As international forces withdrew in 2021 the Taliban launched a rapid offensive, capturing Kabul on 15 August 2021; the final US evacuation from Kabul airport ended on 30 August 2021. See also Taliban.
