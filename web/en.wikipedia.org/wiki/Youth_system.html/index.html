Youth system

Image: Youth system

A youth system is a youth investment program within an organisation, usually a professional sports club, designed to develop and nurture young talent with the intention that they will eventually progress to the senior or first team.

In association football

Youth systems in football are often called academies. Clubs scout children from a young age, provide coaching, education and facilities, and may register players under contract once they reach a certain age. Notable academies include La Masia at FC Barcelona and the Ajax Youth Academy.

Other sports

Similar development structures exist in ice hockey, baseball (minor league systems) and other team sports, though formats vary widely by country and governing body.
