John Belushi

John Adam Belushi (1949–1982) was an American comedian and actor, one of the original cast members of Saturday Night Live and co-star of the films National Lampoon's Animal House (1978) and The Blues Brothers (1980).

Career

Born in Chicago to Albanian immigrant parents, Belushi honed his comedy at The Second City before joining Saturday Night Live in 1975, where characters such as the Samurai Futaba made him famous. His turn as Bluto in Animal House made him a major film star.

Death and legacy

Belushi struggled with drug addiction and died of an overdose in Los Angeles in 1982, aged 33. His younger brother Jim Belushi also became a comedian and actor.
