Len Sharpe

Len Sharpe may refer to several people. This article is a general overview page for the name; for the Trinidadian steelpan arranger and composer best known by the nickname "Boogsie", see the dedicated article.

People named Len Sharpe

  • Len "Boogsie" Sharpe (born 1953) — Trinidadian composer, arranger and steelpan player, long associated with the Phase II Pan Groove steel orchestra and widely regarded as one of the most innovative arrangers in Panorama history.
  • Other sportspeople and musicians of the same name have appeared in regional records; because of the prominence of the steelpan arranger, the unqualified name is most often associated with him.
