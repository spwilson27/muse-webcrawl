The 2013–14 Premier League was the 22nd season of the Premier League, the top flight of English football. Manchester City won the title on the final day of the season, finishing two points ahead of Liverpool.

Title race

The season produced one of the most dramatic title races in Premier League history. Liverpool, powered by the goals of Luis Suárez and Daniel Sturridge, led late in the season, but a home defeat to Chelsea — remembered for Steven Gerrard's slip — and a 3–3 draw at Crystal Palace after leading 3–0 allowed Manchester City to overtake them. City won their final five matches to secure the championship under Manuel Pellegrini.

Managerial changes

The season began with new managers at all three of the previous season's top clubs: David Moyes succeeded Sir Alex Ferguson at Manchester United, José Mourinho returned to Chelsea, and Pellegrini took over at City. Moyes was sacked in April with United finishing seventh.

Records

Luis Suárez won the Golden Boot with 31 goals despite missing the first five games of the season through suspension. Goal-line technology was used in the Premier League for the first time.
