"At Last" is a song written by Mack Gordon and Harry Warren for the 1941 musical film Sun Valley Serenade. First recorded by Glenn Miller and his Orchestra, with vocals by Ray Eberle, the song became a standard, and its 1960 recording by Etta James — arranged by Riley Hampton with a sweeping string introduction — is regarded as her signature song and was inducted into the Grammy Hall of Fame in 1999.

History

The song was performed in Sun Valley Serenade by Glenn Miller's orchestra and reprised in the 1942 film Orchestra Wives; Miller's recording reached the upper charts in 1942. Etta James recorded her version for her 1960 debut album At Last! on Argo Records, a subsidiary of Chess Records. Though released as the B-side of "All I Could Do Was Cry", her lush, bluesy ballad became her signature performance and a standard at weddings and inaugurations — Beyoncé performed it for Barack and Michelle Obama's first dance at the 2009 inaugural ball.

Other versions and honours

The song has been recorded by artists including Nat King Cole, Celine Dion, Christina Aguilera, and Beyoncé, who played Etta James in the 2008 film Cadillac Records. Etta James's recording was placed on the Library of Congress's National Recording Registry recognition circuit via the Grammy Hall of Fame, and in 2021 Rolling Stone included it among the greatest songs of all time.
