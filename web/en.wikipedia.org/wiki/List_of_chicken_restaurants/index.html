List of chicken restaurants

This is a list of notable restaurant chains whose menus centre on chicken, particularly fried chicken and grilled chicken dishes. Chicken is one of the largest segments of the global fast-food industry.

Major chains

  • KFC (United States, founded 1952) — the largest chicken restaurant chain in the world, famous for its "11 herbs and spices" recipe developed by Colonel Harland Sanders; more than 25,000 outlets in over 145 countries.
  • Popeyes (United States, 1972) — Louisiana-style spicy fried chicken; its 2019 chicken sandwich launch drew national attention.
  • Chick-fil-A (United States, 1967) — known for chicken sandwiches and customer service; one of the largest US chains by sales per restaurant.
  • Jollibee (Philippines, 1978) — Chickenjoy fried chicken; flagship brand of Jollibee Foods Corporation.
  • Nando's (South Africa, 1987) — flame-grilled peri-peri chicken with Mozambican-Portuguese roots.
  • Church's Chicken / Texas Chicken (United States, 1952)
  • Bojangles (United States, 1977) — Cajun-seasoned chicken and biscuits, concentrated in the Southeast.
  • Zaxby's (United States, 1990) — chicken fingers and wings.
  • Raising Cane's (United States, 1996) — chicken-finger-focused chain.
  • Wingstop (United States, 1994) — chicken wings.
  • Bonchon (South Korea) — Korean fried chicken.
  • Pollo Campero (Guatemala, 1971)
  • Mary Brown's (Canada, 1969)
  • El Pollo Loco (Mexico/United States) — flame-grilled chicken.
  • Buffalo Wild Wings (United States, 1982) — sports-bar chain centred on wings.

Defunct chains

  • Kenny Rogers Roasters (rotisserie chicken; US restaurants largely closed, brand continues in Asia)
  • Chicken Delight (1960s US chain, "Don't cook tonight, call Chicken Delight")
  • Pioneer Chicken (Los Angeles chain, mostly defunct)
