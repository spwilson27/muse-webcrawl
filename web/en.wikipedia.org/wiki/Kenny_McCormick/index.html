Kenny McCormick

Kenneth "Kenny" McCormick
Series: South Park
Voiced by: Matt Stone
First appearance: 1992 shorts / series 1997

Kenneth "Kenny" McCormick is one of the four main characters of the animated television series South Park, created by Trey Parker and Matt Stone and voiced by Stone. He is recognisable by his orange parka, whose hood muffles his speech.

Character

Kenny comes from the poorest family in South Park. In the show's early seasons he died in nearly every episode — prompting the catchphrase "Oh my God, they killed Kenny!" — only to return unexplained in the next. Later seasons explained his resurrections as a supernatural curse tied to his parents' involvement with a Cthulhu cult. Kenny also appears as the masked vigilante Mysterion.

Cultural impact

Kenny's repeated deaths became one of the defining running gags of early South Park and of 1990s popular culture.
