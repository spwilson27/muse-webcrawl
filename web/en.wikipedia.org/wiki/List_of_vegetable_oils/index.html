List of vegetable oils

Vegetable oils are triglycerides extracted from plants — from seeds, nuts, fruit, leaves or other parts. They are used in cooking, as fuel and biodiesel feedstock, in cosmetics and soaps, and industrially in paints, lubricants and linoleum. Global production is dominated by palm oil and soybean oil.

Major commodity oils

  • Palm oil — from the fruit of the oil palm (Elaeis guineensis); the world's most produced vegetable oil.
  • Soybean oil — pressed from soybeans; widely used in food processing.
  • Rapeseed oil / canola — from Brassica napus; low in saturated fat.
  • Sunflower oil — from sunflower seeds.
  • Peanut (groundnut) oil — valued for a high smoke point.
  • Cottonseed oil, corn oil, safflower oil, sesame oil.

Fruit oils

  • Olive oil — pressed from olives; central to Mediterranean cuisine.
  • Coconut oil — from the kernel of the coconut; high in saturated fat.
  • Avocado oil, palm kernel oil, cocoa butter.

Nut and specialty oils

  • Almond oil, walnut oil, hazelnut oil, macadamia oil.
  • Flaxseed (linseed) oil — a drying oil used in paints and wood finishes, as well as a food supplement.
  • Castor oil — from castor beans; industrial and medicinal uses.
  • Jojoba oil — technically a liquid wax ester, used in cosmetics.
  • Argan oil, neem oil, tea tree oil (an essential oil), grapeseed oil, rice bran oil.
