François Achille Bazaine

Died23 September 1888, Madrid, Spain
RankMarshal of France
CommandsArmy of the Rhine

François Achille Bazaine (13 February 1811 – 23 September 1888) was a French general and Marshal of France who served in Algeria, the Crimea, Italy and Mexico, where he commanded the French expeditionary force after 1863.

Franco-Prussian War

In 1870 Bazaine took command of the Army of the Rhine. After defeats at Borny, Mars-la-Tour and Gravelotte, he allowed his army of some 180,000 men to be shut up in Metz, and on 27 October 1870 he surrendered the fortress to the Prussians — the largest capitulation of French troops in the war.

Trial and exile

Accused of treason, Bazaine was court-martialled in 1873 and sentenced to death, commuted to twenty years' imprisonment. He escaped from the fortress of Sainte-Marguerite in 1874 and lived the rest of his life in exile in Spain, dying in Madrid.
