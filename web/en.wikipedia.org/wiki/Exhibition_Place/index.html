Exhibition Place

Exhibition Place is a publicly owned mixed-use district in Toronto, Ontario, Canada, located by the shoreline of Lake Ontario, just west of downtown Toronto. The 197-acre (80 ha) site includes exhibit, trade, and banquet centres, theatre and music facilities, monuments, parkland, sports facilities, and a number of civic, provincial, and national historic sites.

The site is home to the annual Canadian National Exhibition (CNE), held for several weeks each summer. Other major venues on the grounds include the Enercare Centre, the Better Living Centre, BMO Field, and the Coca-Cola Coliseum. Buildings of note include the Bandshell, the Princess Margaret Fountain, and the Princes' Gates, opened in 1927.

The lands were originally part of the garrison grounds of Fort York, and were set aside for exhibition purposes in the late 19th century. The Toronto Industrial Exhibition was first held there in 1879 and evolved into the CNE. The grounds also hosted the 2015 Pan American Games events and remain an important year-round event venue.
