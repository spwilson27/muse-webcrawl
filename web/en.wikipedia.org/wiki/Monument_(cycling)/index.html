Monument (cycling)

The five Monuments

The Monuments are five classic cycle races generally considered to be the oldest, hardest, most prestigious and most difficult one-day events in men's road cycling. They each have long histories and specific individual characteristics and are part of the UCI World Tour. Each race is contested over an exceptionally long distance — typically well over 240 kilometres — in a single day.

Characteristics

Milan–San Remo is the longest professional one-day race (nearly 300 km) and usually ends in a sprint. The Tour of Flanders and Paris–Roubaix are famous for their cobbled roads and steep cobbled climbs. Liège–Bastogne–Liège is the oldest of the five and a race for climbers in the Ardennes. The Giro di Lombardia closes the season in the hills around Lake Como.

Records

Eddy Merckx holds the record for Monument victories with 19 wins across the five races. Only three riders — Merckx, Roger De Vlaeminck, and Rik Van Looy, all Belgian — have won all five Monuments during their careers. In women's cycling, Milan–San Remo Women was added in 2025, extending the concept of the Monuments to the women's calendar.
