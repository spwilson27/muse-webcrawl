Severe thunderstorm warning
Issued by: National Weather Service (USA) and counterparts
Criteria (US): winds ≥ 58 mph or hail ≥ 1 inch

A severe thunderstorm warning is an alert issued by weather services when a thunderstorm meeting severe criteria is imminent or occurring in the warned area. In the United States, the National Weather Service issues the warning when radar or spotters indicate winds of at least 58 mph (93 km/h) or hail at least one inch in diameter.

Warning vs watch

A warning means severe weather is happening or about to happen and people should take shelter; a severe thunderstorm watch, by contrast, means conditions are favourable over a broader area and period. Warnings are typically issued for counties or polygon areas for 30 to 60 minutes.

Dissemination

Warnings are broadcast via NOAA Weather Radio, the Emergency Alert System, wireless emergency alerts to mobile phones, and media outlets.
