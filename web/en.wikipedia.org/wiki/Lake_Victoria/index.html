Lake Victoria

Lake Victoria is Africa's largest lake by area and the world's largest tropical lake, with a surface of about 68,800 square kilometres. It is shared by Tanzania, Uganda and Kenya and is the principal reservoir of the Nile, whose main outflow at Jinja, Uganda, begins the White Nile.

The lake supports one of the world's largest inland fisheries, but the introduction of the Nile perch in the 1950s drove many of its hundreds of endemic cichlid fish species to extinction in a famous ecological disaster. Its namesake is Queen Victoria; the lake was reached by John Hanning Speke in 1858.
