International Affairs (journal)

International Affairs is a peer-reviewed academic journal of international relations. It was established in 1922 (as the Journal of the British Institute of International Affairs, later the Journal of the Royal Institute of International Affairs) and is published by Oxford University Press on behalf of Chatham House (the Royal Institute of International Affairs) in London.

Scope

The journal publishes research and analysis on international politics, foreign policy, global governance, security, and political economy, aimed at both academic and policy audiences. It issues six numbers per year and also publishes special issues and a blog.

History

The first issue appeared in 1922 for members of the British Institute of International Affairs. The journal took its present title in 1944. Notable former editors include Toynbee-era scholars and, in later decades, editors drawn from British IR academia. International Affairs is consistently ranked among the leading journals in international relations.
