BBC News

BBC News is the BBC's news-gathering and broadcasting division, the largest broadcast news operation of its kind, providing the BBC's television, radio and online news in the UK and, via the World Service and international channels, worldwide.

Output

Its output includes flagship bulletins, the BBC News channel, Radio 4 and 5 Live, regional news, and one of the world's most-visited news websites, funded mainly by the UK licence fee with commercial income for international services.
