University of Tehran

The University of Tehran (UT) is Iran's oldest and most prominent public university, established in 1934 by decree of Reza Shah on the initiative of education minister Ali-Asghar Hekmat. It is nicknamed the 'Mother University' of Iran.

History and structure

UT brought together existing schools of medicine, law, engineering and letters. It now comprises many colleges and faculties with tens of thousands of students, plus institutes and research centers across Tehran and other campuses.

Symbolism

The university's main gate, designed in the 1960s, is a national symbol of higher education. Its Friday prayer venue and student movements have given the campus a significant place in Iran's modern political history.
