Hasbro, Inc. is an American multinational toy manufacturing and entertainment holding company founded on December 6, 1923 by Henry, Helal and Herman Hassenfeld as Hassenfeld Brothers in Providence, Rhode Island. Hasbro owns the trademarks and products of Kenner, Parker Brothers, and Milton Bradley, among others, and since 1999 has owned Wizards of the Coast, publisher of Dungeons & Dragons and Magic: The Gathering.

History

Founding and early years

The Hassenfeld brothers, Polish-Jewish immigrants, began by selling textile remnants, then moved into pencil cases and school supplies. In the 1940s the company expanded into toys, producing modelling compound similar to Play-Doh and toy doctor and nurse kits. The name was shortened to Hasbro in 1968.

G.I. Joe and growth

In 1964 Hasbro launched G.I. Joe, coining the term "action figure" for the poseable doll marketed to boys. The 1980s brought major successes with Transformers and My Little Pony, developed in partnership with Marvel and supported by animated television series.

Acquisitions

Hasbro acquired the Milton Bradley Company in 1984 and Parker Brothers in 1991, giving it classic board games such as Monopoly, Clue and Risk. It bought Kenner Products in 1991, obtaining brands including Tonka, Nerf and Play-Doh, and acquired Wizards of the Coast in 1999. In 2019 Hasbro bought Entertainment One (eOne), adding Peppa Pig and PJ Masks, though it later sold eOne's film and television business to Lionsgate in 2023.

Products and brands

Hasbro's portfolio includes action figures, dolls, games, puzzles, pre-school toys and licensed products. Its "franchise brands" have included Transformers, My Little Pony, Nerf, Monopoly, Play-Doh, Magic: The Gathering and Dungeons & Dragons. The company also produces toys under license for properties such as Marvel, Star Wars and Disney.

Hasbro Studios and media

Hasbro has developed its brands into films and television, notably the live-action Transformers and G.I. Joe film series produced with Paramount Pictures, and animated series produced by its studios.
