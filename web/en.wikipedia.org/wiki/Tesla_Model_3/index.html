The Tesla Model 3 is a battery electric mid-size sedan manufactured by Tesla, Inc. Unveiled on 31 March 2016, it was designed as the company's first mass-market electric vehicle, with a promised starting price of US$35,000. Production began in July 2017 at the Fremont factory in California, followed from late 2019 by Gigafactory Shanghai in China.

History

Tesla received approximately 400,000 reservations in the months after the unveiling. The production ramp in 2017–2018 — described by CEO Elon Musk as "production hell" — involved extensive manual work and a temporary assembly line in a tent structure (GA4) before volumes stabilised. In 2018 the Model 3 became the best-selling plug-in electric car in the United States and, later, the first electric car to surpass one million global sales (2021). A short-lived $35,000 Standard Range version was sold in limited numbers, largely as an off-menu item.

Design

The Model 3 has a minimalist interior dominated by a single 15-inch central touchscreen through which nearly all vehicle functions are controlled; there is no conventional instrument cluster. Variants have included Standard Range Plus, Long Range and Performance versions, with single rear-motor or dual-motor all-wheel-drive configurations, and lithium iron phosphate (LFP) or nickel-based battery chemistries depending on market and period.

2024 refresh ("Highland")

A substantial facelift, codenamed "Highland", was unveiled in September 2023 for cars built in Shanghai and later Fremont. It brought revised front and rear styling, improved aerodynamics and range, a quieter cabin with new materials, an 8-inch rear display, ventilated front seats, and the removal of the steering-column stalks in favour of wheel buttons and screen-based gear selection. A new Performance version followed in 2024.

Safety

The Model 3 earned five-star overall ratings in NHTSA testing and top marks from Euro NCAP and the IIHS, though some driver-assistance features (Autopilot and "Full Self-Driving") have been the subject of regulatory investigations and recalls over driver attention and system behaviour.
