Washlet

Washlet (ウォシュレット, Woshuretto) is a registered trademark of the Japanese company TOTO Ltd. for its line of toilet seats with bidet functions. Launched in 1980, the Washlet pioneered the modern electronic bidet seat and became ubiquitous in Japan, where such seats are installed in the large majority of households, hotels and public facilities.

Features

A typical Washlet combines a heated seat with a self-cleaning nozzle that sprays warm water for cleansing, adjustable in pressure, temperature and position. Common features include warm-air drying, deodorising filtration, automatic lid opening and flushing, and, in public versions, sound-masking functions. Controls are mounted on a side panel or a wall remote.

Spread and influence

The product grew from TOTO's adaptation of an American medical bidet seat design and, aided by a famous 1982 television advertisement, transformed Japanese bathroom culture. Washlets have since spread to hotels, airports and homes worldwide, and the name is often used generically in Japan for any bidet toilet seat.
