Supporters' Shield


Awarded for: Best regular-season record in Major League Soccer
First awarded: 1996 (retroactively); trophy created 1999
Most wins: LA Galaxy and D.C. United

The Supporters' Shield is an annual award given to the Major League Soccer (MLS) team with the best regular-season record, as determined by the league's points system. Conceived by supporters to honour league-wide consistency in a system otherwise crowned by the MLS Cup playoffs, it was first physically presented in 1999, with earlier winners recognised retroactively.

History and significance

The idea was proposed by supporter Sam Pierron and the trophy funded by donations from fans of all clubs. Winning the Shield has carried benefits including a berth in the CONCACAF Champions Cup/Champions League and hosting rights through the playoffs.

Notable winners

D.C. United and the LA Galaxy have been among the most frequent winners. Los Angeles FC set a then-record points total in 2019, and several clubs have won the Shield and the MLS Cup in the same season, a feat known as part of "the double".
