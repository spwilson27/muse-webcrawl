Dehradun

Dehradun is the capital and most populous city of the Indian state of Uttarakhand, located in the Doon Valley in the foothills of the Himalayas.

Geography

It lies between the Ganges and Yamuna river systems, at about 640 metres elevation, with a climate cooler than the plains. The Rajaji National Park is nearby.

Education

Dehradun is known for institutions such as the Indian Military Academy, Forest Research Institute, Doon School and the Survey of India headquarters.

History

Tradition links its name to Guru Ram Rai's camp (dera) in the dun (valley). It became Uttarakhand's interim capital in 2000 and later permanent capital.
