List of islands by population

This is a list of islands by population. The most populous island in the world is Java in Indonesia, home to more than 150 million people — over half of Indonesia's population. A small number of islands hold a disproportionate share of humanity: Java, Honshu, and Great Britain together account for several hundred million people.
