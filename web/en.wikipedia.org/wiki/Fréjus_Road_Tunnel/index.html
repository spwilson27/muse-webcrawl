Fréjus Road Tunnel

The Fréjus Road Tunnel is a toll tunnel that carries the European route E70 through the Alps between Modane in France and Bardonecchia in Italy, roughly parallel to the older Fréjus Rail Tunnel. At 12.87 kilometres long, it was among the longest road tunnels in the world when it opened in 1980.

Fréjus Road Tunnel
Length: 12.87 km
Opened: 12 July 1980
Route: E70 (A43 / A32)
Connects: Modane (France) – Bardonecchia (Italy)

History

Construction began in 1974 and the tunnel opened to traffic on 12 July 1980, providing a year-round road link that avoided the seasonal Fréjus and Mont Cenis passes. It is a single bore carrying two-way traffic, operated jointly by French and Italian companies (SFTRF and SITAF).

Safety

After the Mont Blanc Tunnel fire of 1999, safety in the Fréjus tunnel was extensively reviewed and upgraded, with improved ventilation, refuges and monitoring. A second parallel safety/rescue gallery was subsequently built and plans developed to convert it into a second traffic tube. A serious fire in 2005, caused by a truck, killed two Slovenian truck drivers and closed the tunnel for several weeks, prompting further traffic restrictions on dangerous goods.
