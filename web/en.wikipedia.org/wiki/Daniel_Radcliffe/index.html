Daniel Radcliffe

Daniel Jacob Radcliffe (born 23 July 1989) is an English actor. He rose to fame at age twelve as the title character in the Harry Potter film series, in which he starred in all eight films from Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone (2001) to Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 2 (2011).

Early life and Harry Potter

Radcliffe was born in Fulham, London, the only child of literary agent Alan Radcliffe and casting agent Marcia Gresham. He made his acting debut in the BBC's David Copperfield (1999) before being cast as Harry Potter after an exhaustive search, becoming one of the world's highest-paid child actors. Growing up on screen alongside Emma Watson and Rupert Grint, he received wide praise for the darker later instalments.

Stage and later career

Determined to diversify, Radcliffe appeared naked on stage in Peter Shaffer's Equus (2007) in London and on Broadway, and sang and danced as J. Pierrepont Finch in How to Succeed in Business Without Really Trying (2011). His film roles after Potter included the horror film The Woman in Black (2012), Allen Ginsberg in Kill Your Darlings (2013), Swiss Army Man (2016), Now You See Me 2 (2016), Jungle (2017), Guns Akimbo (2019) and "Weird Al" Yankovic in Weird: The Al Yankovic Story (2022). On television he starred in A Young Doctor's Notebook and the anthology comedy Miracle Workers (2019–2023). In 2024 he won the Tony Award for Best Featured Actor in a Musical for the Broadway revival of Stephen Sondheim's Merrily We Roll Along.

Personal life

Radcliffe has spoken about having dyspraxia and about his past alcohol dependence during the final Potter years; he has been sober since 2010. He is a supporter of The Trevor Project and LGBTQ rights. He lives in New York with his partner, actress Erin Darke, with whom he has a son.
