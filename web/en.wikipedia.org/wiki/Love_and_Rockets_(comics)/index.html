Love and Rockets (comics)

Love and Rockets (often abbreviated L&R) is a comic book series by the Hernandez brothers — Mario, Gilbert and Jaime Hernandez, collectively known as Los Bros Hernandez. Published by Fantagraphics Books since 1982, it became a flagship title of the alternative comics movement and is regarded as one of the most acclaimed comics series ever published.

Content

The series comprises several long-running storylines. Gilbert's "Palomar" stories (also called Heartbreak Soup) are set in a fictional Central American village and chronicle the lives of its inhabitants, notably the bathhouse keeper Luba, over decades, in a mode often compared to magical realism. Jaime's "Locas" stories follow Maggie Chascarrillo and Hopey Glass, young women in the Latino punk scene of a fictional California barrio, following their friendship and on-off romance from youth into middle age with unusual fidelity to the ageing of its characters. Mario contributed stories less frequently.

Legacy

Love and Rockets won numerous Eisner and Harvey Awards, and its influence on alternative and independent comics — in its long-form serial storytelling, its Latino characters and settings, and its blend of realism with science-fiction and soap-opera elements — has been widely acknowledged.
