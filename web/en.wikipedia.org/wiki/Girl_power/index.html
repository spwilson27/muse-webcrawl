Girl power

Girl power is a slogan that encourages and celebrates women's empowerment, independence, confidence, and strength. It entered mainstream culture in the 1990s and is closely associated with third-wave feminism and popular music.

Origins

The phrase circulated in punk and zine culture in the early 1990s, notably in the riot grrrl movement; the band Bikini Kill published a zine titled Girl Power in 1991. The Spice Girls popularised the slogan worldwide from 1996 onward as part of their image and marketing, framing it as friendship, ambition, and self-belief for young women.

Criticism

Commentators have debated whether "girl power" represents genuine feminist empowerment or a commercialised, individualistic dilution of it, and the word "girl" itself has been criticised as infantilising. Nevertheless, the phrase remains a widely used shorthand for female confidence and solidarity.
