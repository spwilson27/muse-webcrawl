List of castles in Scotland

Scotland is home to an estimated 2,000–3,000 castles, ranging from medieval strongholds and tower houses to grand stately homes built or remodeled in the Scots Baronial style. Many are ruins, while others remain clan seats, hotels, or visitor attractions cared for by Historic Environment Scotland and the National Trust for Scotland.

Eilean Donan Castle is one of Scotland's most photographed castles.
