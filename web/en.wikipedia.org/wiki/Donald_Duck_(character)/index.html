Donald Duck

Donald Duck is an American cartoon character created by Walt Disney. A white duck wearing a sailor suit, cap and black or red bow tie but no trousers, he first appeared in The Wise Little Hen in 1934 and became one of Disney's most enduring stars, known for his semi-intelligible voice and excitable, irascible temperament.

Characteristics

Donald speaks in a distinctive duck-like voice originated by Clarence Nash. His temper, ambition and frequent bad luck drive many stories, while his nephews Huey, Dewey and Louie and uncle Scrooge McDuck anchor his extended family.

History

Donald rose through Disney shorts in the 1930s and 1940s, wartime propaganda films, and an extensive comics tradition led by Carl Barks, who developed Duckburg and much of Donald's world.

Legacy

Donald remains Finland's and Scandinavia's favourite Disney character in comics form, has a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame, and appears across film, television, video games and theme parks.
