BBC Sport

BBC Sport is the sports division of the BBC, providing national sports coverage for BBC television, radio and online in the United Kingdom. It is one of the world's best-known sports broadcasters, holding rights at various times to football, the Olympic Games, Wimbledon tennis, Formula One, snooker, darts, athletics and rugby.

Television and radio

Flagship programmes include Match of the Day (football highlights, since 1964), Grandstand (1958–2007), Sports Personality of the Year and extensive live coverage of Wimbledon and the Olympics. BBC Radio 5 Live provides live commentary and sports news.

Online

The BBC Sport website is among the most visited sports sites in the UK, offering live text commentary, results, fixtures and video. Since 2012 the department has been based at MediaCityUK in Salford.
