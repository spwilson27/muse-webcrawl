Evanston, Illinois

Evanston is a city in Cook County, Illinois, United States, on the shore of Lake Michigan just north of Chicago. Settled in the 1830s and incorporated in 1863, it had a population of about 78,000 at the 2020 census. It is home to Northwestern University.

History

Northwestern was founded in 1851, and its charter's temperance provision made Evanston dry until 1972. The city was a center of the temperance movement, home to Frances Willard and the Woman's Christian Temperance Union.

Geography and neighborhoods

Evanston is served by CTA and Metra lines, has beaches on Lake Michigan including Lighthouse Beach near the Grosse Point Lighthouse (1873), and features the Charles Gates Dawes House.
