The Speed Force is a fictional cosmic energy source in the DC Comics universe. It is the source of the powers of the Flash and other speedsters, and is described as a semi-sentient dimension of energy based on velocity and motion. The concept was introduced in the 1990s during writer Mark Waid's run on The Flash, beginning with the storyline "Terminal Velocity".

Description

Speedsters draw their super-speed from the Speed Force, which grants them abilities including running at or beyond light speed, vibrating through solid objects, time travel, and lending or stealing speed from others. The Speed Force also acts as a kind of afterlife or trap for speedsters: those who run too fast may be absorbed into it. Its opposite is the Negative Speed Force, used by the Reverse-Flash.

In other media

The Speed Force appears prominently in the television series The Flash (2014–2023), where it is personified by familiar figures, and in the 2023 film The Flash. It has become a central element of Flash mythology across comics, animation and live-action adaptations.
