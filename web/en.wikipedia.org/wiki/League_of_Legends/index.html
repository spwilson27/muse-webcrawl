League of Legends

League of Legends (LoL) is a 2009 multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) video game developed and published by Riot Games. Inspired by Defense of the Ancients, a custom map for Warcraft III, two teams of five players battle to destroy the opposing team's base structure, the Nexus, on the map Summoner's Rift. Each player controls a "champion" with unique abilities, choosing from a roster of more than 170 champions. The game is free-to-play, monetised through cosmetic customisation, and is one of the most-played PC games in the world; Riot has reported monthly player counts around 150 million in recent years.

Gameplay

In the standard mode, two teams of five occupy opposite corners of Summoner's Rift, a map divided by a river into three lanes (top, middle, bottom) and an intervening jungle. Players select champions filling roles such as top laner, jungler, mid laner, attack damage carry (ADC), and support. Over a match lasting typically 25–45 minutes, champions gain experience and gold by killing computer-controlled minions, monsters, and enemy champions, buying items to grow stronger until one team destroys the enemy Nexus, defended by turrets and inhibitors. Other permanent and rotating modes include ARAM (single-lane 5v5), the autobattler spin-off Teamfight Tactics, and the mobile/console version League of Legends: Wild Rift.

Development and growth

Riot Games was founded in 2006 by Brandon Beck and Marc Merrill; key designers included Steve "Guinsoo" Feak and later Steve "Morello" Mescon, veterans of the DotA mod scene. After release, the game grew explosively through the 2010s, becoming a cultural phenomenon in South Korea's PC bang culture and China's internet cafés. Riot continuously updates the game with new champions, balance patches, visual updates, and an expanding fictional universe, Runeterra, which also underlies the animated series Arcane (2021–2024).

Esports

League of Legends is a leading esport, organised into regional leagues — including Korea's LCK, China's LPL, and the Americas' and EMEA's competitions — culminating in the annual World Championship ("Worlds"). T1 and its star player Faker (Lee Sang-hyeok), who won Worlds in 2013, 2015, 2016, 2023, and 2024, are the most successful team and player in the game's history. The 2023 and 2024 finals set global viewership records for esports, with peak audiences in the tens of millions (over 6.9 million peak viewers excluding Chinese platforms for the 2024 final).

Reception

The game has been praised for its depth, accessibility, and longevity, while criticism has long targeted player toxicity and balance churn. It has won numerous awards and is credited with establishing the free-to-play MOBA as a dominant genre.
