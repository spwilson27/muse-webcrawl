Captain Cold (real name Leonard Snart) is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by John Broome and Carmine Infantino, he first appeared in Showcase #8 (1957) as an enemy of the Flash. He is the leader of the Rogues, the loose collective of Flash villains based in Central City and Keystone City.

Fictional character biography

Raised by an abusive father, Leonard Snart became a career criminal. Seeking a way to counter the Flash's speed, he stole and modified a cyclotron-based device, discovering by accident a freezing effect: his "cold gun" emits a beam that lowers temperatures to absolute zero, creating ice and cold fields that can slow or stop even the Flash. Unlike villains such as Mr. Freeze, Cold has no metahuman powers of his own; his effectiveness comes from his weapon, his tactical mind and his leadership.

Cold enforces a strict code among the Rogues: no unnecessary killing, no drugs, and loyalty to the group. His younger sister Lisa Snart became the villain Golden Glider. During DC's New 52 relaunch, Cold temporarily gained inherent cryokinetic powers and a more heroic role, joining the Justice League in the wake of the Forever Evil storyline.

In other media

Leonard Snart was portrayed by Wentworth Miller in The CW's Arrowverse series The Flash and Legends of Tomorrow, becoming one of the franchise's most popular recurring characters.
