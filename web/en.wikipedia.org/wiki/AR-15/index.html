AR-15

ArmaLite AR-15
Type: Rifle
Designer: Eugene Stoner
Designed: 1956
Manufacturer: ArmaLite, Colt

The ArmaLite AR-15 is a gas-operated assault rifle manufactured in the United States between 1959 and 1964. Designed by Eugene Stoner, it was adopted in modified form by the U.S. military as the M16 rifle.

History

ArmaLite developed the AR-15 using lightweight materials and the .223 Remington cartridge. ArmaLite sold the design to Colt's Manufacturing Company in 1959. Colt marketed semi-automatic versions to civilians and the selective-fire version became the basis for the M16 during the Vietnam War.

Design

The rifle uses a direct impingement gas system, rotating bolt, straight-line recoil design, aluminum alloy receivers and synthetic furniture, making it notably light for its era.
