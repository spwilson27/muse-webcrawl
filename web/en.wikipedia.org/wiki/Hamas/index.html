Hamas (Arabic: حماس, romanized: Ḥamās), officially the Islamic Resistance Movement (حركة المقاومة الإسلامية, Ḥarakat al-Muqāwamah al-ʾIslāmiyyah), is a Palestinian nationalist Sunni Islamist political organisation with a military wing, the Al-Qassam Brigades. It has governed the Israeli-occupied Gaza Strip since 2007. The name is an acronym of its Arabic name and also echoes the Arabic word for "zeal".

Hamas was founded in 1987, during the First Intifada, as an offshoot of the Egyptian Muslim Brotherhood in Palestine, by imam Sheikh Ahmed Yassin and others. Its 1988 charter called for the destruction of Israel and the establishment of an Islamic state in its place; a revised policy document issued in 2017 softened its language, accepting a Palestinian state within the 1967 borders as a formula of national consensus while continuing to reject recognition of Israel.

History

Hamas grew out of Islamic charitable and educational networks (the da'wa) run by Yassin in Gaza from the 1970s. During the 1990s peace process it carried out suicide bombings against Israeli civilians and soldiers, and it fought an on-and-off rivalry with the secular Fatah movement. In the 2006 Palestinian legislative elections Hamas won a majority of seats, leading to a short-lived unity government, the dismissal of the Hamas-led cabinet by President Mahmoud Abbas, and the 2007 Battle of Gaza, after which Hamas took sole control of the Gaza Strip.

Governance of Gaza and wars with Israel

Since 2007 Hamas has administered Gaza under an Israeli and Egyptian blockade, and has fought several wars with Israel, including in 2008–09, 2012, 2014, and 2021. On 7 October 2023, Hamas-led militants launched a large-scale attack on southern Israel, killing about 1,200 people and taking roughly 250 hostages; Israel responded with a prolonged military campaign in Gaza that devastated the territory and killed tens of thousands of Palestinians, and assassinated much of Hamas's senior leadership, including Ismail Haniyeh and Yahya Sinwar, during 2024.

Designation

Hamas, or in some cases only its military wing, is designated a terrorist organisation by Israel, the United States, the European Union, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Japan, and others; other states regard it as a legitimate resistance movement or political party.
