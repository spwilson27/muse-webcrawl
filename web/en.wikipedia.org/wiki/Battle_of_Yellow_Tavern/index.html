Battle of Yellow Tavern
Date: 11 May 1864
Location: Henrico County, Virginia
Part of: American Civil War

The Battle of Yellow Tavern was fought on 11 May 1864 during the American Civil War. Union cavalry under Major General Philip Sheridan defeated Confederate cavalry under Major General J.E.B. Stuart in a raid toward Richmond, Virginia.

Background

During the Overland Campaign, Sheridan took the Union cavalry on a raid to threaten Richmond and draw out Stuart's horsemen.

The battle

At Yellow Tavern, north of Richmond, Sheridan's larger force overwhelmed the Confederates. Stuart was mortally wounded by a Union trooper and died the next day in Richmond.

Significance

The Confederacy lost its most famous cavalry commander, and Union cavalry asserted its growing superiority.
