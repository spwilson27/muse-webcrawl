McLaren

McLaren Group
Industry: Automotive, motorsport, technology
Founded: 1963 (Bruce McLaren Motor Racing); group formed 1985
Headquarters: McLaren Technology Centre, Woking, Surrey, England
Founder: Bruce McLaren

McLaren Group Limited is a British company based at the McLaren Technology Centre in Woking, Surrey, England. The group is best known for its Formula One team, McLaren Racing, one of the most successful in the sport's history, and for McLaren Automotive, the manufacturer of high-performance road cars such as the 720S, Artura and the P1 hybrid hypercar.

History

New Zealand racing driver Bruce McLaren founded Bruce McLaren Motor Racing in 1963 and entered Formula One in 1966. After his death in a testing accident at Goodwood in 1970, the team was carried on under Teddy Mayer and then, from 1980, Ron Dennis, whose Project Four organisation merged with the team to create the modern McLaren. The Dennis era brought dominant seasons with Niki Lauda, Alain Prost and Ayrton Senna, the near-perfect 1988 campaign of the Honda-powered MP4/4, and later titles with Mika Häkkinen and Lewis Hamilton.

Road cars

The company's road-car ambitions began with the Gordon Murray-designed McLaren F1 of 1992, long the world's fastest production car. McLaren Automotive was relaunched as a full manufacturer in 2010 with the MP4-12C and has since produced a range of supercars organised into Sports, Super and Ultimate Series, all built around carbon-fibre tubs at the McLaren Production Centre.

Racing beyond Formula One

McLaren has also competed in Can-Am, where it dominated the late 1960s, won the Indianapolis 500 in the 1970s, and in the modern era has fielded teams in IndyCar, Formula E, Extreme E and esports through McLaren Racing, alongside an endurance programme in GT racing.
