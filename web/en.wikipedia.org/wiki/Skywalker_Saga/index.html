Skywalker Saga

The Skywalker Saga is a collection of nine epic space-opera films in the Star Wars franchise, telling the story of the Skywalker family across three trilogies. The original trilogy (1977–1983) follows Luke Skywalker; the prequel trilogy (1999–2005) follows his father Anakin Skywalker; and the sequel trilogy (2015–2019) follows Rey, Ben Solo and the legacy of the family.

Films

The saga comprises A New Hope, The Empire Strikes Back, Return of the Jedi, The Phantom Menace, Attack of the Clones, Revenge of the Sith, The Force Awakens, The Last Jedi and The Rise of Skywalker. Created by George Lucas, the films are among the highest-grossing in cinema history and concluded their main storyline in 2019.

Themes

Recurring themes include the conflict between the light and dark sides of the Force, redemption, hope, and the hero's journey drawn from mythology.
