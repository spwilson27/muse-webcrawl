World Taekwondo Grand Prix

Sport: Taekwondo
Founded: 2013
Organiser: World Taekwondo
Motto: "World's Best Taekwondo"

The World Taekwondo Grand Prix is a series of international taekwondo tournaments organised by World Taekwondo (WT), first held in 2013. The Grand Prix circuit brings together the highest-ranked competitors in each Olympic weight category and serves as one of the principal qualification pathways for the Olympic Games.

History

The Grand Prix was launched in 2013, with the inaugural event staged in Manchester, England. The series was created to give taekwondo a regular, high-profile world tour comparable to the Grand Slam and Grand Prix circuits in other Olympic sports. Each season typically comprises several Grand Prix stops in different host cities, followed by a Grand Prix Final.

Format

Competition is held in the eight Olympic weight classes (four men's and four women's). Only the top-ranked athletes in the WT world rankings — generally the top 32 in each division — receive invitations, making the Grand Prix one of the most selective events on the calendar. Points earned at Grand Prix events count toward the WT Olympic rankings.

Editions

Host cities have included Manchester, Suzhou, Astana, Mexico City, Moscow, Rabat, London, Rome, Paris, Taiyuan and Riyadh. The Grand Prix Final, contested by the very top seeds, has regularly been held at the end of the season, with the winner in each category earning a direct quota place for their National Olympic Committee at the following Olympic Games.
