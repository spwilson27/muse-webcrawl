Integrated education

Integrated education refers to schooling that brings together children from different religious, ethnic, or social backgrounds in the same schools, rather than educating them separately. The term is used most prominently in Northern Ireland, where schools have historically been divided along Catholic and Protestant lines.

The first planned integrated school in Northern Ireland, Lagan College, opened in 1981. Integrated schools aim for a roughly balanced intake of pupils from both main traditions, as well as others, and promote respect for differing identities. The Northern Ireland Council for Integrated Education (NICIE) supports the sector.

The term also overlaps with inclusive education and mixed schooling in other contexts.
