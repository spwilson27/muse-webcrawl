List of aircraft carriers of the United States Navy

The United States Navy has operated aircraft carriers since 1922, when USS Langley (CV-1) was commissioned. Carriers became the capital ships of the fleet during World War II. Today the Navy operates eleven nuclear-powered supercarriers of the Nimitz and Gerald R. Ford classes. Hull classification symbols include CV, CVN (nuclear), CVL (light), and CVE (escort).

Escort carriers

The Navy operated more than 120 escort carriers (CVE) during World War II, including the Long Island, Bogue, Sangamon, Casablanca (50 ships), and Commencement Bay classes, used for convoy escort, antisubmarine warfare, and amphibious support.
