Eagle Scout

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Eagle Scout medal.

Eagle Scout is the highest rank attainable in the Scouts BSA programme of the Boy Scouts of America (BSA). Since its introduction in 1911, more than two and a half million young men and women have earned the rank — roughly 4–6% of all Scouts. The first Eagle Scout was Arthur Rose Eldred, in 1912. Requirements include earning at least 21 merit badges, demonstrating leadership in a position of responsibility, and planning and carrying out a substantial community service project (the Eagle Scout Service Project), followed by a board of review. The rank must normally be completed before the Scout's 18th birthday. Girls became eligible when Scouts BSA opened to them in February 2019; the inaugural class of female Eagle Scouts was recognised in 2021.

Requirements

The current requirements include: being active in the troop for at least six months as a Life Scout; earning 21 merit badges, including specified required ones such as First Aid, Citizenship in the Community, Nation and World, Communication, Cooking, Personal Fitness, Camping, Family Life, and Personal Management; serving in a troop leadership position; completing the service project; writing a statement of ambitions and listing leadership positions and honours; and passing a board of review. Further distinctions — Eagle Palms — are awarded for merit badges earned beyond the minimum.

Recognition and notable Eagles

Eagle Scouts receive a medal, a square-knot insignia for adults, and a certificate; the National Eagle Scout Association supports the community of alumni. Eagle Scout is widely recognised by colleges, employers and the US military, where it can qualify an enlistee for advanced rank. Famous Eagle Scouts include US President Gerald Ford, astronaut Neil Armstrong (a Distinguished Eagle Scout), film director Steven Spielberg, and numerous senators, governors and business leaders. The Distinguished Eagle Scout Award honours adult Eagles for national-level achievement at least 25 years after earning the rank.
