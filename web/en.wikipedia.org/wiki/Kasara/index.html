Kasara is a town in Thane district in the Indian state of Maharashtra. It lies on the Mumbai–Nashik corridor at the foot of the Thal Ghat (also called the Kasara Ghat), the steep mountain pass by which the Central Railway line climbs from the Konkan coastal lowlands onto the Deccan plateau.

Transport

Kasara railway station is the terminal station of the North-East section of Mumbai's suburban railway (Central line), roughly 120 kilometres from Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus. Long-distance trains stop here to attach or detach banker locomotives for the climb through the Thal Ghat, whose gradients are among the steepest on Indian Railways. The old and new alignments of the ghat section, with their tunnels and viaducts, are notable engineering works.

Geography and tourism

Surrounded by the Sahyadri hills, Kasara is a gateway to Igatpuri and to trekking destinations in the Western Ghats. The monsoon transforms the ghat section with waterfalls and mist, making the train journey a popular excursion from Mumbai. The nearby Vaitarna river valley supplies reservoirs important to Mumbai's water system.
