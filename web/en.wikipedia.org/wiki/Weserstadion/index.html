Weserstadion

The Weserstadion is a football stadium in Bremen, Germany, situated on the north bank of the River Weser from which it takes its name. It is the home ground of SV Werder Bremen of the Bundesliga and has a capacity of roughly 42,100 spectators for league matches.

History

Football has been played on the site since 1909, and the modern stadium took shape after the Second World War. Reconstruction and expansions over the decades enclosed the bowl and added roofs; a major redevelopment completed in 2011 brought the stands closer to the pitch by removing the athletics track, added a distinctive solar-cell facade, and set the floodlight masts among the most recognizable in German football. Through sponsorship the stadium has also been known as the Wohninvest Weserstadion since 2019.

Events

The stadium hosts Werder Bremen's home fixtures, including matches in the Nordderby against Hamburger SV, as well as occasional German national team games and concerts. Its riverside setting allows some fans to arrive by boat, and the grounds include the Wuseum, Werder Bremen's club museum.
