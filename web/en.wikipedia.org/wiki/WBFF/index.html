WBFF

WBFF (channel 45) is a television station in Baltimore, Maryland, United States, affiliated with the Fox network and owned by Sinclair Broadcast Group. It is the flagship station of the Sinclair group, which is headquartered in nearby Hunt Valley. WBFF's studios are located on West 41st Street in Baltimore's Hampden neighborhood, and its transmitter is in the Woodberry district.

History

WBFF signed on the air on April 11, 1971, as an independent station owned by Chesapeake Television, the forerunner of Sinclair Broadcast Group, founded by Julian Sinclair Smith. It became a charter affiliate of the Fox Broadcasting Company when the network launched in October 1986. The station broadcasts a large volume of local news, including a long-running morning show, and has frequently been among the highest-rated Fox affiliates in the United States.

Sister stations and programming

WBFF is a sister station to WUTB (channel 24), Baltimore's MyNetworkTV affiliate, which Sinclair operates under a local marketing agreement. In addition to Fox network programming, WBFF airs syndicated shows and, since 2021, has served as one of the Baltimore homes of network programming displaced by its extensive newscasts.
