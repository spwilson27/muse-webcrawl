Drawing

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
[Image: A silverpoint and chalk study of hands and drapery in the manner of a Renaissance master]

Drawing is a form of visual art in which an artist uses instruments to mark paper or another two-dimensional surface. Instruments include graphite pencils, pen and ink, brushes, charcoal, chalk, pastels, crayons and markers; in the digital era drawing is also done with styluses on tablets. Drawing is often exploratory, the basis of preparatory studies for paintings, sculpture and architecture, but has long been practised as a finished art in its own right.

Renaissance masters such as Leonardo da Vinci and Albrecht Dürer raised drawing to a central intellectual practice — Leonardo's notebooks join anatomy, engineering and composition studies — and the medium remains fundamental to art education, illustration, comics and design.
