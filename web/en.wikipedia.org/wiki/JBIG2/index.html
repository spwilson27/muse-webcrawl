JBIG2

JBIG2 is an image compression standard for bi-level images, developed by the Joint Bi-level Image Experts Group. It is suitable for both lossless and lossy compression, typically achieving far smaller files than CCITT Group 4 for scanned text.

Overview

JBIG2 encodes pages by segmenting them into regions of text, halftone and generic content, using pattern matching and substitution for repeated symbols such as letters. It is widely used in PDF files for scanned documents.

Controversy

Lossy JBIG2 substitution in some photocopiers was found in 2013 to alter digits in scanned documents, raising concerns for archival use.
