List of Victoria Cross recipients

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Victoria Cross (VC) is the highest and most prestigious award of the British honours system, awarded for gallantry "in the presence of the enemy" to members of the British Armed Forces and, historically, to forces of Commonwealth countries. It was introduced by Queen Victoria on 29 January 1856, initially to honour acts of valour during the Crimean War. The metal traditionally used for the crosses is said to come from cannons captured at the Siege of Sevastopol. In total, the medal has been awarded 1,358 times to 1,355 individual recipients; only three men have won it twice (the VC and Bar).

Other notable facts

  • The youngest recipient was Hospital Apprentice Andrew Fitzgibbon, aged 15 during the Taku Forts action in 1860; the oldest was Lieutenant William Raynor, aged 61, at Delhi in 1857.
  • The most awards for a single action were the 11 given for the defence of Rorke's Drift (1879), and 7 for the second relief of Lucknow and for Lancashire Landing at Gallipoli.
  • Father-and-son and brother pairs of recipients exist; four father-son pairs and three pairs of brothers have received the medal.
  • The Victoria Cross for Australia, Victoria Cross for New Zealand and Victoria Cross of Canada are separate national awards instituted in the late 20th century to the same standard.
  • The American unknown soldier, the Canadian unknown soldier and other allied unknown warriors were honoured with the VC collectively.
