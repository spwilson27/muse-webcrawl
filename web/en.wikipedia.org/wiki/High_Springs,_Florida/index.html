High Springs is a city in Alachua County, Florida, United States, north-west of Gainesville. It grew as a railroad town in the 1880s and was first incorporated as Santaffey before adopting its present name, referring to a spring on a hill.

Attractions

The town is a gateway for divers and swimmers visiting the freshwater springs of the Santa Fe River, including Ginnie Springs, Poe Springs and Blue Springs, and is known for antique shops and cave-diving outfitters.
