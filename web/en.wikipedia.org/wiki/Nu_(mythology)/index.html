Nu (mythology)

Nu (also Nun) is the personification of the primordial watery abyss in ancient Egyptian religion, from which the world and gods arose.

Mythology

In the Ogdoad cosmology of Hermopolis, Nun and his female counterpart Naunet represented the inert waters of chaos existing before creation. The first land and the creator god Atum/Ra emerged from Nun, and each night the sun was believed to travel through or be reborn from these waters. Nun was depicted as a man with water ripples or a frog-headed figure.
