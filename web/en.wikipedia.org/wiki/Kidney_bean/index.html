Kidney bean

The kidney bean is a variety of the common bean, Phaseolus vulgaris, named for its kidney-like shape and colour. Red kidney beans are a staple ingredient in dishes such as chili con carne and the Louisiana Creole dish red beans and rice.

Nutrition and safety

Kidney beans are rich in protein, fibre, iron and folate. Raw or undercooked red kidney beans contain high levels of the lectin phytohaemagglutinin, which is destroyed by thorough boiling; beans should be boiled vigorously for at least ten minutes before eating.
