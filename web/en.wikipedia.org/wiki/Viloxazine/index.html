Viloxazine

Viloxazine, sold under the brand name Qelbree among others, is a medication used to treat attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) in children and adults. It is a non-stimulant selective norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor (NRI). The extended-release formulation was approved in the United States in 2021 for children and adolescents, with adult approval following in 2022.

History

Viloxazine was originally marketed in Europe from the 1970s as an antidepressant under the name Vivalan, but was withdrawn from those markets in the early 2000s for commercial reasons. It was later reformulated as an extended-release capsule by Supernus Pharmaceuticals and repurposed for ADHD.

Pharmacology and use

Viloxazine increases synaptic norepinephrine by blocking its transporter; it also has activity at certain serotonin receptors. As a non-stimulant, it lacks the abuse potential of amphetamine and methylphenidate products, but may take several weeks to reach full effect. Common side effects include somnolence, decreased appetite, fatigue, nausea, and insomnia. Like other ADHD and antidepressant medications, it carries a boxed warning in the US regarding increased risk of suicidal thoughts and behaviors in children and adolescents.
