The University of São Paulo (Universidade de São Paulo, USP) is the largest and one of the most prestigious public universities in Brazil. Created in 1934 by bringing together existing professional schools with a new Faculty of Philosophy, Sciences and Letters, it is funded by the state of São Paulo and charges no tuition.

Scale and research

USP has campuses in São Paulo city — centred on the Cidade Universitária Armando de Salles Oliveira — and in several other cities of the state, including Ribeirão Preto, São Carlos, and Piracicaba. It produces a large share of Brazil's doctoral graduates and scientific publications and hosts major teaching hospitals and museums.

Admissions

Entrance is through the highly competitive vestibular examinations administered by FUVEST. Alumni include numerous Brazilian presidents, ministers, and leading scientists and artists.
