Mary Poppins Returns is a 2018 American musical fantasy film directed by Rob Marshall, with a screenplay written by David Magee and a story by Magee, Marshall, and John DeLuca. Loosely based on the book series Mary Poppins by P. L. Travers, the film is a sequel to the 1964 film Mary Poppins. It stars Emily Blunt as Mary Poppins, with Lin-Manuel Miranda, Ben Whishaw, Emily Mortimer, Julie Walters, Angela Lansbury, Colin Firth, Meryl Streep, and Dick Van Dyke in supporting roles.

Plot

Set in 1930s London, some twenty years after the original film, the story follows Michael Banks, now a widowed father of three facing the repossession of the family home at 17 Cherry Tree Lane during the Great Slump. When the children's kite pulls Mary Poppins back down from the sky, she resumes her post and, together with lamplighter Jack, takes the family on a series of magical adventures — including an animated undersea bath sequence, a visit to her cousin Topsy, and a race to Big Ben to turn back time — while Michael seeks the bank shares certificate that would save the house.

Production

Walt Disney Pictures announced the film in September 2015, with Rob Marshall directing. Principal photography took place at Shepperton Studios from February to July 2017. The film combined live action with hand-drawn animation in the style of the original, employing veteran animators. Dick Van Dyke, who played Bert in 1964, returned as Mr. Dawes Jr.

Reception

The film premiered at the Dolby Theatre on 29 November 2018 and grossed $349.5 million worldwide. It received generally positive reviews and four Academy Award nominations, including Best Original Score and Best Original Song for "The Place Where Lost Things Go".
