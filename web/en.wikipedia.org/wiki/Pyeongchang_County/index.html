Pyeongchang County

Country: South Korea
Province: Gangwon
Population: roughly 40,000
Known for: 2018 Winter Olympics

Pyeongchang County (Korean: 평창군, Pyeongchang-gun) is a county in Gangwon Province, South Korea, located in the Taebaek Mountains about 180 km east of Seoul. Mountainous and sparsely populated — around 84% of its area is mountainous — it is one of the coldest and snowiest inhabited regions of South Korea and a centre for winter sports and mountain tourism.

2018 Winter Olympics

After unsuccessful bids for 2010 and 2014, Pyeongchang was awarded the 2018 Winter Olympics and Winter Paralympics. The Games centred on the Alpensia Resort cluster (ski jumping, sliding events, the main stadium) and the Yongpyong ski resort, with ice events held in nearby Gangneung. To avoid confusion with Pyongyang, organizers stylized the name as "PyeongChang". A new high-speed rail line connected the region to Seoul for the Games.

Tourism and culture

Attractions include the Buddhist temples Woljeongsa and Sangwonsa on Mount Odae, the annual Pyeongchang Trout Festival, and ski resorts such as Yongpyong and Phoenix Pyeongchang. The county also hosted the 2024 Winter Youth Olympics jointly with Gangneung.
