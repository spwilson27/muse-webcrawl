Sony α (Alpha)
Manufacturer: Sony
Introduced: 2006
Mounts: A-mount (discontinued), E-mount
Product type: Digital cameras

Sony α (pronounced "Alpha") is a line of digital cameras produced by Sony. The brand originated in 2006 when Sony acquired the camera business of Konica Minolta and launched its first digital single-lens reflex camera, the α100, using the Minolta A-mount system.

A-mount era

Early α cameras were conventional DSLRs and later translucent-mirror (SLT) models using the A-mount, with in-body image stabilisation inherited from Konica Minolta's technology. Models ranged from entry-level bodies to the full-frame α99 series.

E-mount and mirrorless

In 2010 Sony introduced the E-mount with the NEX series of mirrorless interchangeable-lens cameras. The full-frame α7 series, launched in 2013, helped establish mirrorless cameras as professional tools. Subsequent lines include the high-resolution α7R series, the video-oriented α7S series, the sports-focused α9, and the APS-C α6000 series. Sony gradually discontinued A-mount products, completing the transition to E-mount.

Lenses

The E-mount lens system includes Sony's premium G Master (GM) lenses, G lenses, and Zeiss-branded optics, alongside a large third-party ecosystem.
