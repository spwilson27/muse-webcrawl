Scissor lift

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

A scissor lift is a type of aerial work platform that can usually only move vertically. The mechanism used to achieve this is the use of linked, folding supports in a criss-cross "X" pattern, known as a pantograph (or scissor mechanism). The upward motion is achieved by the application of pressure to the outside of the lowest set of supports, elongating the crossing pattern and propelling the work platform vertically. The platform may also have an extending "bridge" to allow closer access to the work area.

Design

The contraction of the scissor action can be hydraulic, pneumatic, or mechanical (via a leadscrew or rack and pinion system). Depending on the power system employed, the lift may need no more than simple hand operation to descend, since gravity does the work on the way down. Scissor lifts are classified by their working environment and power source: electric slab lifts, with non-marking tyres, are used indoors on level surfaces for construction and maintenance; rough-terrain lifts have larger tyres, higher ground clearance, and often diesel or dual-fuel engines for outdoor sites. Platform heights typically range from about 6 to 18 metres.

Uses

Scissor lifts are used wherever safe, temporary access to high areas is required: building maintenance, window cleaning, warehouse stock picking, sign installation, film production, and construction. Because they lift vertically and have relatively large platforms, they are well suited to work requiring room for tools and materials, but they offer less reach and flexibility than boom lifts, which can move both vertically and horizontally.

Safety

Safe operation requires level ground, adherence to load limits, guardrails and fall-arrest anchorages on the platform, and attention to overhead hazards such as power lines. Stability depends on keeping the centre of gravity within the wheelbase; some large models use outriggers. Standards such as ANSI MH 29.1 and the EN 1570 series govern the design and safe use of scissor lifts and lifting tables.
