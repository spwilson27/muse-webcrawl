Tri Nations Series

The Tri Nations Series was an annual international rugby union competition held between the national teams of Australia (the Wallabies), New Zealand (the All Blacks), and South Africa (the Springboks). It ran from 1996 to 2011.

History

The tournament was created with the advent of professional rugby and the formation of SANZAR in 1995. New Zealand dominated the competition, winning ten titles; South Africa and Australia each won three. Within the series, the Bledisloe Cup (Australia vs New Zealand) and the Mandela Challenge Plate (Australia vs South Africa) were contested.

Successor

In 2012 Argentina joined the competition, which was renamed The Rugby Championship. See The Rugby Championship and rugby union.
