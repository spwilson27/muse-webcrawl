Added sugar

Added sugars are sugar carbohydrates added to food and beverages during processing or preparation, as distinct from sugars naturally occurring in foods such as fruit and milk.

Definition

The term includes sucrose, high-fructose corn syrup, honey, molasses, fruit juice concentrates and many other sweeteners listed on ingredient labels under dozens of names. Nutrition labelling rules in the United States require added sugars to be declared separately on the Nutrition Facts label.

Health effects

High intake of added sugar is associated with obesity, type 2 diabetes, cardiovascular disease, dental caries and fatty liver disease. The World Health Organization recommends limiting free sugars to less than 10% of total energy intake.

Sources

Sugar-sweetened beverages are the largest source in many diets, followed by desserts, snacks, breakfast cereals and sauces.
