RAAF Base Edinburgh is a Royal Australian Air Force base located in Edinburgh, 25 km north of Adelaide, South Australia. It opened in 1954 and hosts Australia's maritime patrol and response force, as well as significant elements of the Australian Defence Force's intelligence and electronic warfare capabilities.

History

The base was established near the Salisbury Weapons Research Establishment to support long-range weapons testing at Woomera. It became the home of the RAAF's Lockheed P-3 Orion maritime patrol aircraft, operated by No. 92 Wing, which have since been replaced by the Boeing P-8A Poseidon and MQ-4C Triton.

Units

Resident units include No. 92 Wing with its Poseidon squadrons, No. 462 Squadron, and the Australian Army's 1st Armoured Regiment, which relocated portions of its strength to the Edinburgh Defence Precinct.
