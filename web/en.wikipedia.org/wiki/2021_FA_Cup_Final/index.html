The 2021 FA Cup Final was the 140th final of the FA Cup, the world's oldest football cup competition, played at Wembley Stadium in London on 15 May 2021 between Chelsea and Leicester City. Leicester won 1–0 to lift the FA Cup for the first time in their history, having lost each of their previous four finals, the most recent in 1969.

Match

Played before a crowd of 21,000 — then the largest attendance at an English football match since the start of the COVID-19 pandemic, as part of the government's pilot events programme — the final was goalless at half-time. In the 63rd minute, Leicester midfielder Youri Tielemans collected a pass from Luke Thomas and struck a powerful shot into the top corner from about 25 yards, a goal widely considered among the finest in FA Cup final history.

Chelsea pressed for an equaliser: former Leicester player Ben Chilwell had a late effort ruled out by VAR for a marginal offside in the build-up, and Leicester goalkeeper Kasper Schmeichel made two outstanding saves, denying Chilwell and Mason Mount, to preserve the lead. Captain Schmeichel lifted the trophy jointly with club captain Wes Morgan.

Road to the final

Chelsea, managed by Thomas Tuchel, had beaten Manchester City in the semi-final, while Brendan Rodgers's Leicester defeated Southampton. It was Chelsea's second consecutive FA Cup final defeat, having lost to Arsenal in 2020, though they won the Champions League two weeks later.
