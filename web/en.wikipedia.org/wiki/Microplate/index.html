Microplate

A microplate, also known as a microtiter plate or multiwell plate, is a flat plate with multiple wells used as small test tubes. It has become a standard tool in analytical research and clinical diagnostic testing laboratories.

Formats

Common formats have 6, 12, 24, 48, 96, 384 or 1536 wells arranged in a rectangular matrix with standardised footprint and spacing, allowing automated handling and reading by microplate readers.

Uses

Microplates are used in enzyme-linked immunosorbent assays (ELISA), cell culture, high-throughput screening, PCR preparation and spectrophotometric measurements.
