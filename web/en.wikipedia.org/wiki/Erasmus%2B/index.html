Erasmus+

TypeEU education and mobility programme
Established2014 (successor to Erasmus, 1987)
Administered byEuropean Commission

Erasmus+ is the European Union's programme to support education, training, youth and sport in Europe. It began on 1 January 2014, combining the earlier Erasmus student exchange scheme with other programmes covering schools, vocational training, adult education, and sport.

Background

The original Erasmus programme, named after the Dutch humanist Desiderius Erasmus of Rotterdam, was established in 1987 and enabled university students to study or take a traineeship abroad for between three months and a year. Millions of students have participated.

Structure

Erasmus+ is organised around key actions covering mobility of individuals, cooperation for innovation and exchange of good practices, and support for policy reform. It operates in programme countries across Europe and, for some actions, partner countries worldwide.
