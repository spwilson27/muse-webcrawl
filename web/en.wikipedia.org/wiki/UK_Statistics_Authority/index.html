UK Statistics Authority

The UK Statistics Authority is a non-ministerial government department of the United Kingdom responsible for oversight of the national statistical system. Established under the Statistics and Registration Service Act 2007 and operating from 1 April 2008, its statutory objective is to promote and safeguard the production and publication of official statistics that serve the public good.

Functions

The Authority has two principal functions. First, it oversees the Office for National Statistics (ONS), the UK's largest producer of official statistics, covering the economy, population and society. Second, through its regulatory arm — the Office for Statistics Regulation — it monitors and publicly assesses the quality, trustworthiness and value of official statistics across more than 200 producing bodies, and can designate statistics as "accredited official statistics" (formerly National Statistics) when they meet the standards of the Code of Practice for Statistics.

Governance

The Authority reports directly to the UK Parliament rather than to a minister, in order to protect statistical independence. It is led by a board chaired by a non-executive chair; the National Statistician is a member of the board and the Authority's principal adviser, also heading the Government Statistical Service. The Authority has intervened publicly when statistics have been misused in political debate or when pre-release practices threatened trust in official figures.
