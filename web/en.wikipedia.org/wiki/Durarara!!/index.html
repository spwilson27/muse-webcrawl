Durarara!!

Durarara!! (Japanese: デュラララ!!), often shortened to DRRR!!, is a Japanese light novel series written by Ryohgo Narita and illustrated by Suzuhito Yasuda. Set in Ikebukuro, Tokyo, it follows an ensemble cast — including a headless Irish dullahan working as an underground courier, a schoolboy founder of an anonymous online gang, and a bartender-clad strongman — whose lives intertwine amid gang wars and supernatural events.

Media

The novels were published by ASCII Media Works under the Dengeki Bunko imprint from 2004 to 2014, comprising thirteen volumes; a sequel series, Durarara!! SH, followed. An anime television adaptation by Brain's Base aired in 2010, with a second season (Durarara!!×2) in 2015–2016. Manga adaptations and video games have also been produced. Yen Press publishes the novels in English.

Story

Mikado Ryūgamine moves to Ikebukuro at the invitation of his childhood friend Masaomi Kida, who warns him about the district's dangerous figures: the superhumanly strong Shizuo Heiwajima, the information broker Izaya Orihara, and the mysterious gang known as the Dollars. Mikado soon encounters Celty Sturluson, a dullahan searching for her stolen head, and is drawn into the city's hidden conflicts.
