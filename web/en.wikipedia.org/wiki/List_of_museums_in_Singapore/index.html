Singapore has a museum scene concentrated in the civic district and around Marina Bay. The National Heritage Board manages the national museums, including the National Museum of Singapore, the Asian Civilisations Museum and the National Gallery Singapore, which opened in 2015 in the former Supreme Court and City Hall buildings.
