Shaggy Rogers

Norville "Shaggy" Rogers is a fictional character in the Scooby-Doo franchise created by Hanna-Barbera. He is the owner and best friend of the Great Dane Scooby-Doo, and a member of the teenage mystery-solving gang Mystery, Inc., alongside Fred Jones, Daphne Blake and Velma Dinkley. Shaggy debuted in Scooby-Doo, Where Are You! in 1969.

Character

Shaggy is depicted as a lanky, laid-back teenager with shaggy hair and a small goatee, usually dressed in a green V-neck shirt and brown bell-bottoms. His defining traits are his cowardice and his enormous appetite: he and Scooby would rather eat than investigate, and can usually be persuaded into danger only by the offer of Scooby Snacks. His speech is peppered with "Zoinks!" and the filler word "like". Despite his fearfulness, Shaggy is a skilled runner, gymnast and ventriloquist in various episodes, and his cowardice is often played against accidental heroics.

Portrayals

Shaggy was voiced for decades by Casey Kasem, whose beatnik-inspired delivery defined the character. Later voice actors include Billy West, Scott Innes and Matthew Lillard, who also played Shaggy in the live-action films Scooby-Doo (2002) and Scooby-Doo 2: Monsters Unleashed (2004) before taking over the animated role. Will Forte voiced the character in the 2020 animated film Scoob!

Cultural impact

Shaggy is one of the most recognisable characters in American animation. In 2019, an internet meme depicting "Ultra Instinct Shaggy" as an omnipotent being became so widespread that it was referenced by Warner Bros. and included as an easter egg in the video game MultiVersus.
