Post Office Limited

A branch post office in the United Kingdom.

Post Office Limited, commonly known as the Post Office, is the United Kingdom's retail post office company. It operates a network of more than 11,500 branches across the country, the great majority of which are run by independent postmasters or retail partners under agency arrangements, with a smaller number of directly managed ("Crown") branches. The company is wholly owned by the UK Government through the Department for Business and Trade.

History

The Post Office was historically part of the General Post Office and later the Post Office Corporation. Under the Postal Services Act 2000 it became a public limited company, Post Office Ltd, a subsidiary of the Royal Mail Group. The Postal Services Act 2011 provided for the separation of the Post Office from Royal Mail, which took effect in April 2012, leaving Post Office Limited as an independent company in public ownership while Royal Mail was subsequently privatised.

Services

Branches provide mail and parcel services on behalf of Royal Mail and Parcelforce, government services such as passport check-and-send and driving licence renewals, banking services for customers of major UK banks under the Banking Framework, bill payments, foreign currency, and Post Office-branded financial and insurance products. The network is regarded as a piece of social infrastructure, particularly in rural areas, and receives a government subsidy to maintain uneconomic branches.

Horizon scandal

Between 1999 and 2015 the Post Office prosecuted hundreds of subpostmasters for theft, fraud and false accounting on the basis of data from the Horizon computer system supplied by Fujitsu. Many were convicted, imprisoned or ruined, while others were forced to repay money that shortfalls in the system appeared to show missing. Following group litigation and Court of Appeal rulings in 2019 and 2021, dozens of convictions were quashed, and the affair was recognised as one of the most widespread miscarriages of justice in British legal history. A statutory public inquiry was established, and dramatisation of the scandal in the 2024 television series Mr Bates vs The Post Office led to legislation exonerating all affected subpostmasters in England and Wales.
