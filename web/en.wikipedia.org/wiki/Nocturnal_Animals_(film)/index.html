Nocturnal Animals is a 2016 American neo-noir psychological thriller film written, produced, and directed by Tom Ford in his second feature, based on the 1993 novel Tony and Susan by Austin Wright. The film stars Amy Adams and Jake Gyllenhaal, with Michael Shannon, Aaron Taylor-Johnson, Isla Fisher, and Armie Hammer in supporting roles.

Plot

Los Angeles art gallery owner Susan Morrow receives the manuscript of a novel, Nocturnal Animals, written by her ex-husband Edward Sheffield, whom she has not seen in nineteen years. The film interweaves three strands: Susan's present-day life and troubled second marriage; flashbacks to her courtship with Edward and the betrayal that ended it; and the story within the novel, in which Tony Hastings (played by Gyllenhaal, who also plays Edward) sees his wife and daughter murdered by a gang led by Ray Marcus during a night drive in West Texas, and seeks justice with the help of detective Bobby Andes (Shannon). Reading the violent manuscript, Susan interprets it as Edward's veiled reckoning with their past, and arranges to meet him — but he never arrives.

Reception

The film premiered at the 73rd Venice International Film Festival, where it won the Grand Jury Prize. It was widely praised for its performances and visual design. Michael Shannon was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor, and Aaron Taylor-Johnson won the Golden Globe for Best Supporting Actor. The film received nine BAFTA nominations.
