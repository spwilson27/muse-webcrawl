ASC Pty Ltd, formerly the Australian Submarine Corporation, is an Australian government-owned shipbuilding company based at Osborne in Adelaide, South Australia. It is best known for designing and building the six Collins-class submarines for the Royal Australian Navy and for maintaining the fleet at Osborne and at Henderson, Western Australia.

History

ASC was incorporated in 1985 by a consortium including the Swedish submarine designer Kockums, to compete for the contract to build Australia's new submarines. The Collins class — large, advanced conventionally powered boats — was built at a purpose-built facility at Osborne between 1990 and 2003. The project suffered early technical problems, famously including excessive noise and combat-system issues, but the class was progressively remediated and is regarded as highly capable. The Commonwealth acquired full ownership of ASC in 2000.

Other work

As part of the Air Warfare Destroyer Alliance with Raytheon Australia and the Defence Materiel Organisation, ASC built blocks and consolidated the three Hobart-class destroyers at Osborne, delivered between 2017 and 2020. ASC also performs deep maintenance and life-of-type extension work for the Collins fleet.

AUKUS and the future

Under the AUKUS agreement, Australia is to acquire conventionally armed, nuclear-powered submarines. ASC, together with BAE Systems, was selected in 2024 to build the SSN-AUKUS submarines at Osborne, and ASC is also to sustain the future fleet at Henderson, reshaping the company into the centrepiece of Australia's submarine enterprise.
