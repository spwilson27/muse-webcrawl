Culiacán

Culiacán, officially Culiacán Rosales, is the capital and largest city of the Mexican state of Sinaloa. It lies in the north-west of Mexico at the confluence of the Tamazula and Humaya rivers, which form the Culiacán River. With around a million inhabitants in its metropolitan area, it is a major agricultural and commercial centre.

History

The city was founded in 1531 by the Spanish conquistador Nuño Beltrán de Guzmán as San Miguel de Culiacán. The surrounding valleys became one of Mexico's most productive agricultural regions, especially for tomatoes and other vegetables exported to the United States.

Modern city

Culiacán is home to the Autonomous University of Sinaloa and the Tomateros baseball team. The city has been prominently associated with the Sinaloa Cartel, and episodes such as the 2019 "Culiacanazo" — battles following the attempted arrest of Ovidio Guzmán — drew international attention.
