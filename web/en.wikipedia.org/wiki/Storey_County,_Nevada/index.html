Storey County, Nevada

County seat: Virginia City
Founded: 1861
Population: approx. 4,100 (2020)

Storey County is a county in western Nevada, one of the state's original counties (1861), named for Captain Edward Farris Storey, killed in the Pyramid Lake War. Its seat is historic Virginia City.

History

The county sat atop the Comstock Lode, the richest silver deposit in American history, discovered in 1859. Virginia City boomed into one of the richest cities in the West, funding much of San Francisco's growth.

Modern era

Today the county hosts the Tahoe Reno Industrial Center, one of the world's largest industrial parks, home to Tesla's Gigafactory Nevada and facilities of Google, Apple, and Switch. Storey County is also one of the few Nevada counties where licensed brothels operate, including the Mustang Ranch.
