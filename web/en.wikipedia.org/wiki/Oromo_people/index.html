The Oromo people (pronounced /ˈɒrəmoʊ/; Oromo: Oromoo, Amharic: ኦሮሞ) are a Cushitic ethnic group and nation native to the Oromia region of Ethiopia and parts of Northern Kenya. They speak the Oromo language (Afaan Oromoo), the most widely spoken Cushitic language, as a mother tongue. The Oromo are the largest single ethnic group in Ethiopia, constituting roughly a third of the country's population, and one of the largest ethnic groups in Africa.

History

The Oromo homeland is generally placed in the highlands of southern Ethiopia and northern Kenya. In the 16th century, great movements of Oromo pastoralists (sometimes called the "Oromo migrations") transformed the demographic and political map of the Horn of Africa, bringing Oromo groups into contact and conflict with the Christian Ethiopian kingdom and the Adal Sultanate. Oromo groups organised themselves under the gadaa system, an indigenous democratic socio-political order based on age-grade classes, which UNESCO has inscribed on its list of Intangible Cultural Heritage. In the late 19th century most Oromo lands were incorporated into the Ethiopian Empire during the conquests of Menelik II.

Culture and society

Traditional Oromo religion, Waaqeffanna, centred on the sky god Waaqa; today the Oromo are predominantly Muslim and Christian in roughly comparable proportions, with Waaqeffanna still practised. The annual Irreecha festival of thanksgiving, held at Lake Hora Arsadi in Bishoftu and in Addis Ababa, draws millions of participants. Oromo culture places high value on cattle pastoralism in the lowland groups (such as the Borana) and on mixed agriculture among highland groups; coffee has deep cultural and economic importance. Since the 1990s the Oromo language has been written in the Latin-based Qubee alphabet and used in regional administration and education.

Politics

Oromo nationalism and grievances over political and economic marginalisation have shaped modern Ethiopian politics. The Oromo Liberation Front was founded in 1973; after 1991 the Oromo People's Democratic Organization participated in the federal ruling coalition. Mass Oromo protests from 2014 to 2018 helped bring Abiy Ahmed — of mixed Oromo-Amhara parentage — to the premiership in 2018, though conflict between federal forces and the Oromo Liberation Army has continued.
