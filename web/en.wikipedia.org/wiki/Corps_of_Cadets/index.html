Corps of Cadets


Image placeholder: Corps of Cadets

A corps of cadets is the student military body at a military academy or a university with a military training program. Members, called cadets, live and train in a structured military environment while pursuing academic studies, preparing for commissioning as officers or for civilian leadership roles.

United States

In the United States, notable corps of cadets include those at the United States Military Academy at West Point, the Virginia Military Institute, The Citadel, Texas A&M University, Virginia Tech, and Norwich University. Senior military colleges maintain corps as a central part of campus life, with cadets wearing uniforms, observing rank structure, and participating in drills and ceremonies.

Organisation

Corps are typically organised into companies, battalions, and regiments or brigades, led by cadet officers under the supervision of active-duty or retired military staff. Daily life follows a schedule of reveille, formations, inspections, and physical training alongside academic classes.
