WNBA Most Valuable Player Award

The Women's National Basketball Association Most Valuable Player Award (WNBA MVP) is an annual award given since the league's inaugural season in 1997 to the best-performing player of the regular season, as voted by a panel of sportswriters and broadcasters.

SportBasketball
LeagueWNBA
First awarded1997 (Cynthia Cooper)

History

Cynthia Cooper of the Houston Comets won the first two MVP awards in 1997 and 1998. Three players have won the award three times: Sheryl Swoopes, Lisa Leslie and Lauren Jackson. A'ja Wilson won her third MVP in 2024 with the first unanimous vote in over two decades, and won a record fourth award in 2025. Winners are typically announced during the playoffs, and the award is distinct from the WNBA Finals MVP.
