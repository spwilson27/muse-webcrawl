Sydney Trains

Sydney Trains is the state-owned operator of the suburban and interurban passenger rail network serving Sydney and surrounding regions in New South Wales, Australia. It commenced operations on 1 July 2013, succeeding CityRail, and is an agency of Transport for NSW. The network is famous for its double-decker electric multiple units.

Network

Sydney Trains operates nine suburban lines (T1 North Shore & Western, T2 Inner West & Leppington, T3 Liverpool & Inner West, T4 Eastern Suburbs & Illawarra, T5 Cumberland, T6 Carlingford (converted to light rail), T7 Olympic Park, T8 Airport & South and T9 Northern) plus intercity services to the Blue Mountains, Central Coast and Newcastle, Illawarra and South Coast, Southern Highlands and Hunter regions, covering more than 160 stations and over 800 km of track. Central station is the hub of the network. Rolling stock includes Tangara, Waratah (Series A and B), Millennium and Oscar (H set) trains. Passengers tap on and off with the Opal card, and patronage before the pandemic exceeded a million trips per weekday.
