List of films based on video games

This is a list of live-action and animated films based on video games, including theatrical releases and direct-to-video productions. The genre began in earnest with Super Mario Bros. (1993), the first major Hollywood adaptation of a game. Video game films were long notorious for poor critical reception, but several recent adaptations — notably The Super Mario Bros. Movie (2023) and the Sonic the Hedgehog series — have been major box-office successes.
