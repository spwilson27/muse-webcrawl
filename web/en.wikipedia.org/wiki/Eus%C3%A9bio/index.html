Eusébio da Silva Ferreira (1942–2014), nicknamed the "Black Panther" or the "Black Pearl", was a Portuguese footballer who played as a striker. He is considered one of the greatest players of all time and Benfica's greatest-ever player, scoring 473 goals in 440 competitive matches for the club.

Career

Born in Maputo, Mozambique, Eusébio joined Benfica in 1961 and helped the club win the European Cup in 1962, scoring twice in the final against Real Madrid. He won the Ballon d'Or in 1965 and was the top scorer at the 1966 World Cup, where Portugal finished third.

Legacy

Eusébio won 11 league titles with Benfica and finished his career in North America and Mexico. His death in 2014 prompted national mourning in Portugal; he was interred in the National Pantheon in 2015.
