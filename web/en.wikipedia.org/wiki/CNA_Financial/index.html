CNA Financial

Industry: Insurance
Founded: 1897
Headquarters: Chicago, Illinois, U.S.
Parent: Loews Corporation
Website: cna.com

CNA Financial Corporation is an American financial corporation based in Chicago, Illinois. Its principal subsidiary, Continental Casualty Company, was founded in 1897. CNA is one of the largest commercial property and casualty insurers in the United States, providing insurance products and services to businesses and professionals.

History

Continental Casualty was founded in Detroit, Michigan, in 1897 as a provider of accident and health insurance to railroad workers. The company moved to Chicago and expanded into casualty insurance lines. The modern CNA Financial Corporation was formed through mergers, including the 1963 combination of Continental Casualty and National Fire Insurance Company of Hartford, and the 1974 merger that created CNA Financial as a holding company.

Loews Corporation acquired control of CNA in the 1970s and owns the large majority of its shares. In 2018, CNA moved its headquarters to a new tower, CNA Center, at 151 North Franklin Street in the Chicago Loop.

Operations

CNA writes commercial property and casualty insurance, including professional liability, management liability, surety bonds, and warranty products, through its Commercial, Specialty, and International segments. It is the seventh-largest commercial insurer in the United States by net premiums written.
