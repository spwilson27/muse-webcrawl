NMB48

NMB48 (read "N.M.B. Forty-eight") is a Japanese idol girl group based in Namba, Osaka. Produced by Yasushi Akimoto, it is a sister group of AKB48 and takes its name from the Namba district (abbreviated NMB). The group performs at the NMB48 Theater in the Yes-Namba Building and debuted in 2010–2011.

History

First-generation auditions were held in 2010, and the group made its stage debut on 1 January 2011 at the NMB48 Theater. Its debut single, "Zetsumetsu Kurokami Shōjo" (2011), topped the Oricon chart. The members are divided into teams (historically Team N, Team M and Team BII, along with trainees). Former captain Sayaka Yamamoto was the group's most prominent member until her graduation in 2018; other notable members have included Miyuki Watanabe, Nana Yamada, Miru Shiroma and Yuuka Kato.

Style and activities

Like its sister groups, NMB48 follows the "idols you can meet" concept: daily theater performances, handshake events and fan elections. Reflecting its Osaka base, the group emphasises comedy and lively talk, drawing on the Kansai region's manzai traditions. NMB48 members also appear in AKB48's singles and general elections.

Discography notes

The group's singles include "Oh My God!", "Kitagawa Kenji", "Kamonegix", "Don't Look Back!" and "Durian Shōnen". The group has also released studio albums and tours nationally.
