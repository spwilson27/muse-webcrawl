List of top international men's football goal scorers

This is a list of the top international goal scorers in men's association football, counting goals scored in full ("A") international matches for a national team. Cristiano Ronaldo of Portugal holds the all-time record, having passed Ali Daei's long-standing mark of 109 goals in 2021.
