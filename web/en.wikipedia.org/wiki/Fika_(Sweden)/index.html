Fika (Sweden)

Fika is a Swedish concept, a state of mind, an attitude and an important part of Swedish culture. It means making time for friends and colleagues to share a cup of coffee (or tea) and a little something to eat. Fika cannot be translated, it literally means a coffee and cake break and is almost a ritual in Swedish society.

Description

Fika is a social concept in Swedish culture with the basic meaning "to have coffee", often accompanied with pastries, cookies or pie. A fika can take place in a café, at work, as a break involving colleagues, or at home, and can take place at any time of day. Typically, fika also involves eating something sweet, such as cinnamon buns (kanelbulle), cake, or cookies.

Etymology

The word "fika" is believed to originate from a 19th-century slang inversion of the syllables of the Swedish word kaffi (coffee).

Workplace fika

Many Swedish workplaces have morning and afternoon fika breaks built into the schedule, considered important for team bonding and productivity.
