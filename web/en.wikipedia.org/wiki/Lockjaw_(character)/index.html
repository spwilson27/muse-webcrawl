Lockjaw (character)

Lockjaw (character)
Species: Inhuman dog
Abilities: Teleportation, superhuman strength

Lockjaw is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. He is a giant bulldog-like Inhuman who serves as the teleporting companion and protector of the Inhuman Royal Family, most often associated with Crystal and Medusa.

('History', 'Created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby, Lockjaw first appeared in Fantastic Four in the 1960s during the introduction of the Inhumans of Attilan.')

('Powers and abilities', 'Lockjaw can teleport himself and passengers across vast interstellar and interdimensional distances, track scents across dimensions, and possesses great strength and durability. Despite his animal appearance, his intelligence has long been a subject of debate among the Inhumans.')
