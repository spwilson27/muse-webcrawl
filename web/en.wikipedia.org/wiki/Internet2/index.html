Internet2

Internet2 is a not-for-profit consortium of universities, research institutions, government agencies and industry partners in the United States that operates an advanced networking infrastructure for research and education.

History

Internet2 was founded in 1996 by 34 university researchers seeking a network beyond the capacity of the commercial internet for research applications. Its first backbone, built with partners, was known as Abilene.

Network

The Internet2 Network provides high-capacity connectivity — backbone links reaching hundreds of gigabits per second — linking hundreds of universities and research centres, and interconnecting with similar research networks worldwide, such as GÉANT in Europe.

Activities

Beyond connectivity, the consortium supports identity and access management services such as InCommon, cloud services, and communities in areas including telemedicine, distance learning and large-scale scientific data transfer.
