Prinsjesdag

Prinsjesdag (Dutch for "Little Princes' Day") is the day on which the reigning monarch of the Netherlands addresses a joint session of the States General in The Hague, delivering the Speech from the Throne (Troonrede) that sets out the government's policy plans for the coming parliamentary year. It is held every year on the third Tuesday in September, marking the opening of the Dutch parliamentary season.

Ceremony

The monarch travels in state procession from Noordeinde Palace to the Ridderzaal (Hall of Knights) at the Binnenhof, traditionally in the Golden Coach (and, during its restoration, the Glass Coach), escorted by military units in ceremonial dress. Members of the Senate and House of Representatives, ministers and dignitaries assemble in the Ridderzaal, where the president of the joint session leads the traditional cheer of "Long live the King!" after the speech.

Budget presentation

On the same afternoon, the Minister of Finance presents the national budget (Miljoenennota) and budget memoranda to the House of Representatives, ceremonially carried in a special briefcase (het koffertje). The following days are devoted to the general political debate on the government's plans.

History

The name dates from the 18th century, when the birthdays of the young stadtholder princes of Orange were celebrated with public festivities; it later attached to the constitutional ceremony of opening the States General, fixed on the third Tuesday of September in 1887.
