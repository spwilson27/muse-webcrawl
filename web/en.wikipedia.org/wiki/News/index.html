News

[Image: A wall of television screens in a newsroom showing different channels]

News is freshly significant information about current events, gathered and presented by journalists and news organisations through media including print, broadcasting, and digital platforms. What counts as news is shaped by news values such as timeliness, impact, proximity, prominence and human interest.

News values

Editors traditionally select stories by criteria systematised by Galtung and Ruge (1965): negativity, frequency, unambiguity, meaningfulness, consonance, unexpectedness, continuity, elite people and nations, and personalisation — a framework still taught in journalism schools.

Forms

News ranges from breaking coverage and wire copy to explainers, live blogs and newsletters. The 24-hour cycle and social media have compressed the time between event and report toward zero, with verification becoming a central newsroom discipline.
