Demoiselle

Demoiselle is a French word meaning "young lady" or "damsel", the diminutive of dame. It appears in English and in scientific and common names in a number of distinct senses.

Uses

As an honorific, demoiselle historically corresponded to "Miss", a title for an unmarried woman, and in older French usage could denote a woman of noble birth who was not titled in her own right.

In zoology, the name is applied to several animals noted for their graceful appearance: the demoiselle crane (Grus virgo), a small crane of Central Eurasia; the damselfishes of the genus Chromis and relatives, often called demoiselles; and the damselflies (suborder Zygoptera), whose French name is demoiselles. Demoiselle is also the name given to the numidian crane in older literature.

In aviation, the Demoiselle was a pioneering series of ultra-light aircraft designed by the Brazilian-French aviation pioneer Alberto Santos-Dumont in 1907–1909. The Santos-Dumont Demoiselle, particularly the No. 20 and No. 21 models, was among the first aircraft produced in series and is often described as a forerunner of the modern ultralight aeroplane.

In other contexts, "Demoiselle" appears in the name of the demoiselle wines of Bordeaux, in heraldry, and as a term for the small decorative structures and paving features in French garden design.
