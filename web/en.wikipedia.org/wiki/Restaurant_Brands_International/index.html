Restaurant Brands International Inc. (RBI) is a Canadian-American multinational fast food holding company. It was formed in 2014 by the merger of the American fast food chain Burger King and the Canadian coffeehouse and restaurant chain Tim Hortons in a deal valued at about US$11.4 billion, and is majority-owned by the Brazilian investment firm 3G Capital. It is one of the world's largest quick-service restaurant companies, with tens of thousands of restaurants in more than 100 countries.

History

After acquiring Burger King in 2010, 3G Capital merged it with Tim Hortons in 2014, creating RBI with headquarters in Toronto. In 2017 RBI acquired Popeyes Louisiana Kitchen for about US$1.8 billion, and in 2021 it acquired Firehouse Subs for about US$1.0 billion. RBI operates an almost entirely franchised model, earning revenue from royalties, rent, and franchise fees rather than from operating most restaurants itself.
