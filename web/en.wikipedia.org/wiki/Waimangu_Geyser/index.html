Waimangu Geyser

Waimangu Geyser
Location: Waimangu Volcanic Rift Valley, New Zealand
Active: 1900–1904
Height: up to ~460 m (record)

The Waimangu Geyser, in the Waimangu Volcanic Rift Valley near Rotorua, New Zealand, was the most powerful geyser ever recorded. Active from 1900 to 1904, it erupted periodically to heights reportedly reaching 460 metres (1,500 ft).

History

The geyser formed in the valley created by the 1886 eruption of Mount Tarawera. Its eruptions threw black mud and rocks high into the air, drawing tourists despite the danger; in 1903 an eruption killed four visitors, including guide Joseph Warbrick. Activity ceased in 1904 after a landslide changed the local water table.

Today

The Waimangu valley remains an active geothermal area with hot springs, the Frying Pan Lake and Inferno Crater, managed as a scenic reserve and visitor attraction.
