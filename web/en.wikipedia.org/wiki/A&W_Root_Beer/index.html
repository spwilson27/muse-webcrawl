A&W Root Beer is an American brand of root beer founded in 1919 by Roy W. Allen, who opened a roadside root beer stand in Lodi, California. Allen partnered with Frank Wright in 1922 — the "A&W" initials combine their surnames — and the business grew into A&W Restaurants, one of the earliest franchised restaurant chains in the United States, famous for serving root beer in frosted glass mugs and for carhop service.

History

The beverage and the restaurants expanded together across the US, and the brand entered Canada in 1956, where the restaurant chain became especially enduring and is now separately owned. The packaged soft drink was introduced for retail sale in the early 1970s and became one of the best-selling root beers in the United States. Ownership of the drinks brand passed through several companies and it is now marketed by Keurig Dr Pepper. A&W's mascot, Rooty the Great American Root Bear, appeared from 1974.

Product

A&W Root Beer is a caffeine-free, sweetened carbonated drink flavoured in the traditional root beer style (historically sassafras-based, now using artificial and natural flavourings). Diet/Zero Sugar versions are also sold.
