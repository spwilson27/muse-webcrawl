José Mujica


José Alberto Mujica Cordano
Born: 20 May 1935, Montevideo, Uruguay
Died: 13 May 2025 (aged 89), Montevideo
Office: 40th President of Uruguay (2010–2015)
Political party: Broad Front (MPP)

José Alberto Mujica Cordano (20 May 1935 – 13 May 2025) was a Uruguayan politician, former revolutionary and farmer who served as the 40th president of Uruguay from 2010 to 2015.

Early life and imprisonment

In the 1960s Mujica joined the Tupamaros guerrilla movement. Captured several times, he spent nearly 15 years in prison under Uruguay's military dictatorship, much of it in harsh solitary confinement. He was released in 1985 when democracy was restored.

Political career

Mujica helped found the Movement of Popular Participation (MPP) within the Broad Front coalition, served as a deputy and senator, and was Minister of Livestock, Agriculture and Fisheries from 2005 to 2008. He won the 2009 presidential election and took office on 1 March 2010.

Presidency and reputation

His government legalised abortion, same-sex marriage and a regulated cannabis market, and expanded renewable energy. Mujica gained worldwide attention for his austere lifestyle: he lived on his small farm, drove an old Volkswagen Beetle and donated most of his presidential salary to charity, leading some media to call him "the world's poorest president". He later served again as a senator before retiring from politics in 2020.
