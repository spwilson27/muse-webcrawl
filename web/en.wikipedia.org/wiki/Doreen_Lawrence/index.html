Doreen Delceita Lawrence, Baroness Lawrence of Clarendon, OBE (née Graham; born 24 October 1952), is a British Jamaican campaigner and the mother of Stephen Lawrence, a black British teenager who was murdered in a racist attack in south-east London in 1993.


Doreen Lawrence, campaigner and life peer.

Campaign

After the failure of the initial police investigation into her son's murder, Lawrence and her husband Neville campaigned for years for justice. Their campaign led to the Macpherson Report (1999), which concluded that the Metropolitan Police was "institutionally racist" and prompted major reforms in policing and the abolition of the double jeopardy rule in serious cases. Two of Stephen's killers were finally convicted in 2012.

Later work

Lawrence founded the Stephen Lawrence Charitable Trust (now Blueprint for All) in 1998 to support young people from disadvantaged backgrounds. She was appointed OBE in 2003 and made a life peer in 2013, sitting in the House of Lords as Baroness Lawrence of Clarendon. In 2020 she led a Labour Party review into the disproportionate impact of COVID-19 on Black, Asian and minority ethnic communities.
