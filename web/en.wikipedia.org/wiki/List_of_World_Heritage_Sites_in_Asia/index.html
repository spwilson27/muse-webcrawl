List of World Heritage Sites in Asia

UNESCO designates World Heritage Sites of outstanding cultural or natural value. Asia hosts several hundred inscribed sites. China and India are among the countries with the most sites worldwide. This list is organized by UNESCO's regional groupings (Arab States, Asia-Pacific) and gives representative sites per country.
