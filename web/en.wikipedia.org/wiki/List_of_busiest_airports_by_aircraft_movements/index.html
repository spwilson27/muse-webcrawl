List of busiest airports by aircraft movements

An aircraft movement is a takeoff or a landing. Airports with the most movements tend to be large hubs with extensive general aviation or flight training activity in addition to airline operations. The figures below reflect typical recent (post-pandemic recovery) years and are approximate.

General aviation

Counting general aviation, some of the world's busiest fields by movements are training and reliever airports such as Van Nuys Airport (California) and Deer Valley Airport (Phoenix, Arizona), which can exceed 400,000–500,000 movements per year, dominated by flight training circuits. During the annual EAA AirVenture fly-in, Wittman Regional Airport in Oshkosh, Wisconsin, becomes the busiest control tower in the world for one week.
