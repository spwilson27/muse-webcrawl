Feld Entertainment

Type: Private

Industry: Live entertainment

Founded: 1967

Headquarters: Ellenton, Florida, United States

Feld Entertainment Inc. is an American live show production company which owns a number of traveling circuses and other live entertainment properties. The company is best known as the former owner and producer of Ringling Bros. and Barnum & Bailey Circus, which toured from 1967 under Feld ownership until its initial closure in 2017, and was later revived without animal acts.

History

The company was founded by Irvin Feld, who with his brother Israel Feld bought the Ringling Bros. and Barnum & Bailey Circus in 1967. Irvin Feld's son Kenneth Feld later became chief executive of the company. Under the Feld family, the circus business expanded into ice shows and arena spectaculars.

Productions

Feld Entertainment produces and promotes a wide range of live touring productions, including Disney On Ice, Monster Jam, Marvel Universe Live, Sesame Street Live, and Supercross events. Its productions are staged in dozens of countries and are seen by tens of millions of spectators each year.
