Oliver Wyman is an American management consulting firm. Founded in New York City in 1984 by former Booz Allen Hamilton partners Alex Oliver and Bill Wyman, it has grown into one of the largest strategy consulting firms in the world, with particular strength in financial services. It is a subsidiary of Marsh McLennan (formerly Marsh & McLennan Companies), operating alongside sister businesses such as Mercer and Guy Carpenter.

History

Oliver, Wyman & Company built its early reputation advising banks and insurers on strategy, risk, and operations. In 2003 it was acquired by Marsh & McLennan and combined with the parent's other consulting assets; in 2007 the firm unified several acquired units — including Mercer Management Consulting and Mercer Oliver Wyman — under the single Oliver Wyman name. Subsequent growth included acquisitions in energy, digital, and design consulting. The firm today maintains offices in more than 30 countries across the Americas, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and the Middle East.

Practice areas

The firm's work spans financial services (its largest sector), as well as transportation, energy, healthcare, retail, and the public sector. Oliver Wyman is particularly known for its annual risk and stress-testing work with central banks and regulators, and for research publications such as its joint World Economic Forum reports on climate and industry transformation. Together with McKinsey, Bain, and BCG, it is frequently listed among the most prestigious employers in management consulting.
