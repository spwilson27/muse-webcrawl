Wanderlust

Wanderlust is a strong desire to wander, travel, and explore the world. The term originates from the German words wandern ("to hike or roam") and Lust ("desire, pleasure"). In German, the word has a slightly different connotation, evoking a pleasurable experience of walking in nature rather than simply a urge to travel abroad.

Etymology and usage

The word entered English in the early 20th century as a loanword from German. In the modern sense, wanderlust describes not mere tourism but an intrinsic impulse toward exploration and new experiences. It is frequently used in travel writing and advertising, and has been the title of numerous songs, films, magazines, and festivals.

Related concepts

Related notions include dromomania, an outdated psychiatric diagnosis of an uncontrollable impulse to wander, and the German concept of Fernweh ("far-sickness"), the longing for distant places, sometimes considered the opposite of homesickness.
