Seti I

Seti I
Pharaoh of Egypt, 19th Dynasty
Reign: c. 1294/1290–1279 BCE
Predecessor: Ramesses I
Successor: Ramesses II

Menmaatre Seti I was the second pharaoh of the Nineteenth Dynasty of Egypt, father of Ramesses II, and son of Ramesses I. His reign (c. 1290–1279 BCE) restored Egyptian power after the Amarna period.

Reign

Seti campaigned in Canaan, Syria, and Libya, retaking territory lost under Akhenaten and fighting the Hittites. He built extensively: the magnificent temple at Abydos with its king list, a mortuary temple at Thebes, and additions at Karnak including the Great Hypostyle Hall begun under him.

Tomb

His tomb, KV17 in the Valley of the Kings, is among the longest, deepest and most finely decorated. His well-preserved mummy is now in Cairo.
