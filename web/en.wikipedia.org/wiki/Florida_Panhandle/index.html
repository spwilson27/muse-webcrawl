Florida Panhandle

The Florida Panhandle (also known as West Florida and Northwest Florida) is the northwestern part of the U.S. state of Florida. It is a strip of land roughly 200 miles (320 km) long and 50 to 100 miles (80 to 161 km) wide, lying between Alabama on the north and the west, Georgia on the north, and the Gulf of Mexico to the south. Its eastern boundary is arbitrarily defined at the Apalachicola River.

Geography and cities

Major population centres include Pensacola, Panama City, Tallahassee (the state capital), Destin, Fort Walton Beach and Panama City Beach. The region is known for white-sand Gulf beaches along the "Emerald Coast", several large military installations including Eglin Air Force Base and Naval Air Station Pensacola, and a culture more closely aligned with the Deep South than peninsular Florida.

Time zone

Most of the Panhandle observes the Central Time Zone, unlike the rest of Florida, which is on Eastern Time; the Apalachicola River roughly forms the dividing line.
