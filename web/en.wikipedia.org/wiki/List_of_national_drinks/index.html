National drinks
ScopeAlcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages associated with countries

A national drink is a distinct beverage that is strongly associated with a particular country, and can be part of its national identity and self-image. National drinks fall into two broad categories: alcoholic drinks such as whisky in Scotland or tequila in Mexico, and non-alcoholic drinks such as tea in China and Britain or coffee in Brazil and Ethiopia. Some are officially designated by law; others are recognised by custom and widespread consumption.
