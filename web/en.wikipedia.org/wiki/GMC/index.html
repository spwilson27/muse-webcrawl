GMC

GMC is a division of the American automobile manufacturer General Motors that primarily sells trucks, SUVs, vans and crossovers. GMC vehicles are positioned as premium counterparts to Chevrolet trucks and are sold mainly in North America and the Middle East.

GMC
Parent: General Motors
Founded: 1911 (as GM Truck Company)
Products: Trucks, SUVs, vans
Slogan: "We Are Professional Grade"

History

The brand traces to the Rapid Motor Vehicle Company, acquired by GM in 1909, and the General Motors Truck Company formed in 1911. GMC built heavy trucks, buses (including the famous Greyhound coaches), and military vehicles such as the DUKW amphibious truck of the Second World War.

Models

Current and notable models include the Sierra full-size pickup, the Canyon mid-size pickup, the Yukon and Acadia SUVs, the Savana van, the all-electric Hummer EV, and the luxury Denali sub-line, introduced in 1999, which accounts for a large share of sales.
