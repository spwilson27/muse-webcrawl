Wisin & Yandel were a Puerto Rican reggaeton duo consisting of Wisin (Juan Luis Morera) and Yandel (Llandel Veguilla), billed as "El Dúo de la Historia" (The Duo of History). Among the genre's best-selling acts, they helped bring reggaeton from Puerto Rico to a global pop audience.

Career

The duo debuted in the late 1990s and broke through with Pa'l Mundo (2005), featuring "Rakata" and "Noche de Sexo". Subsequent albums Los Extraterrestres (2007), La Revolución (2009), and Los Vaqueros: El Regreso (2011) consolidated their success; they won the Grammy Award for Best Latin Urban Album and multiple Latin Grammy and Billboard Latin Music Awards. Their record 2009 performance at Puerto Rico's José Miguel Agrelot Coliseum set attendance marks. After a five-year split devoted to solo careers, they reunited in 2018 for Los Campeones del Pueblo and closed their career with La Última Misión (2022) and a record-breaking world tour.
