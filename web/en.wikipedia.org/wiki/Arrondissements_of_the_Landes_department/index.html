Arrondissements of the Landes department

The two arrondissements of the Landes department, in the Nouvelle-Aquitaine region of southwestern France.

The two arrondissements of the Landes department are sub-divisions of the department in the Nouvelle-Aquitaine region of France. Arrondissements are administered by a subprefect and serve as electoral and administrative districts; each is divided into cantons and communes.

History

The department of Landes was created in 1790 during the French Revolution. The arrondissement system was established nationally in 1800. Landes originally also included an arrondissement of Saint-Sever, which was suppressed in the 1926 reorganisation of French arrondissements, leaving the present two.
