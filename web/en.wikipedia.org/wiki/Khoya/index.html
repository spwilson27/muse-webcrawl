Khoya

Khoya, also called khoa or mawa, is a dairy product made by slowly simmering milk in an open pan until most of its water evaporates, leaving concentrated milk solids. It is a base ingredient in many South Asian sweets.

Preparation and varieties

Khoya is made from whole milk reduced over gentle heat with constant stirring. Varieties include hard (batti), soft (chikna) and granular (danedar) khoya, differing in moisture and used for different recipes.

Uses

Khoya forms the base of mithai such as gulab jamun, barfi, peda and kalakand, and is also used in savoury dishes and as a stuffing for breads and vegetables.
