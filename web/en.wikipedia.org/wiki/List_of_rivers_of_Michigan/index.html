List of rivers of Michigan

This is a list of rivers in the U.S. state of Michigan. Michigan is bordered by four of the five Great Lakes and contains more than 51,000 kilometres (about 36,000 miles) of rivers and streams across its Upper and Lower Peninsulas.

By drainage basin

Rivers in the eastern Lower Peninsula drain to Lake Huron, Lake St. Clair and Lake Erie, including the Huron, Raisin, Saginaw, Shiawassee, Cass and Clinton rivers. Western rivers such as the Grand, Kalamazoo, Pere Marquette and White drain to Lake Michigan. In the Upper Peninsula, the Ontonagon, Tahquamenon, Manistique and Escanaba rivers drain to Lakes Superior and Michigan.
