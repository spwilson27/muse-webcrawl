Book collecting

Book collecting is the collecting of books, including seeking, acquiring, organizing, cataloging, displaying, storing, and maintaining whatever books are of interest to a given collector. It ranges from casual accumulation to serious antiquarian collecting of rare and first editions.

History

Collecting dates to ancient libraries such as Alexandria. In the Renaissance, humanists and nobles built libraries. The 19th century saw bibliomania and famous sales; the 20th century developed the modern rare-book trade.

Collecting areas

Collectors focus on first editions, signed copies, fine bindings, illustrated books, incunabula, miniature books, or a single author or subject. Condition, provenance and edition points determine value.

Care

Books should be stored upright, away from light, heat and humidity, handled with clean hands, and protected from pests.
