Degrassi: Next Class

Degrassi: Next Class is a Canadian teen drama television series created by Linda Schuyler and Yan Moore. It is the fifth series in the Degrassi franchise, following Degrassi: The Next Generation. The series premiered on Family Channel's F2N block in Canada and was released internationally on Netflix in January 2016. Four seasons of ten episodes each were produced before the series was cancelled in 2019.

Premise

Like its predecessors, the series follows a group of students at the fictional Degrassi Community School in Toronto as they confront issues including sexuality, mental health, race, religion, online culture, consent and substance use. The shorter, ten-episode seasons were structured around season-long arcs and social-media-driven storylines, reflecting the producers' aim to address the experiences of Generation Z. Several characters carried over from the later seasons of The Next Generation, while a new cohort of younger students formed the core cast.

Production and reception

The series was produced by Epitome Pictures (DHX Studios Toronto) and filmed in Toronto. It retained executive producer Stephen Stohn and co-creator Linda Schuyler from earlier incarnations. Critics praised the show's frank treatment of topics such as abortion, radicalisation and gender identity, and it received nominations at the Canadian Screen Awards. Netflix did not renew the series after its fourth season, released in July 2017; the cancellation was confirmed in March 2019.
