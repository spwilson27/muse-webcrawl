Rama II is a science fiction novel by Gentry Lee and Arthur C. Clarke, published in 1989. It is a sequel to Clarke's classic Rendezvous with Rama (1973), written nearly two decades later with Lee, who contributed most of the prose.

Plot

Seventy years after the first Rama spacecraft passed through the Solar System, a second cylindrical vessel arrives. Humanity, now with a standing plan for such an event, dispatches the spacecraft Newton with a crew of twelve cosmonauts (the Newton expedition) to intercept and explore it. The novel follows the crew's personal conflicts, political intrigues back on Earth, and their encounters with biots — the robotic creatures of Rama — and an octospider, one of the vessel's enigmatic inhabitants.

Reception and sequels

The book differs in tone from its predecessor, concentrating more on character development and melodrama. It was followed by The Garden of Rama (1991) and Rama Revealed (1993), completing the series.
