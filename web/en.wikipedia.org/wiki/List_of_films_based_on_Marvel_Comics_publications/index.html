List of films based on Marvel Comics publications

The following is a list of films based on Marvel Comics publications. Marvel properties have been adapted to film since the 1940s serial Captain America, but the modern era began with Blade (1998) and X-Men (2000). The Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU), launched with Iron Man (2008), became the highest-grossing film franchise of all time.

Animated films

Animated adaptations include Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018), which won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature, and its sequels, as well as the direct-to-video Marvel Animated Features line.
