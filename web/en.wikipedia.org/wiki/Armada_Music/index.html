Armada Music

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Armada Music is one of the world's largest independent dance music labels.

Armada Music is a Dutch independent record label specialising in electronic dance music. It was founded in 2003 by Armin van Buuren, Maykel Piron, and David Lewis—the name is an acronym formed from the first two letters of each founder's name (ARmin, MAykel, DAivid). Headquartered in Amsterdam, the label is widely regarded as one of the largest independent dance music labels in the world.

Sub-labels and artists

Armada operates a large family of sub-labels, including Armind, A State of Trance, Armada Trice, Armada Deep, Armada Chill, WAKE YOUR MIND, and In My Opinion, covering trance, progressive house, deep house, and other EDM styles. Artists associated with the label include Armin van Buuren, Andrew Rayel, Dash Berlin, W&W, Cosmic Gate, Aly & Fila, Ferry Corsten, Lost Frequencies, and Loud Luxury.

Activities

Beyond releasing records, Armada runs the long-running A State of Trance brand with Armin van Buuren, organises events and festival stages, and manages publishing and artist management activities. In 2023, Armada launched an investment arm (BEAT Music Fund) to acquire music catalogues, acquiring rights including those of Kevin Saunderson and other dance music catalogues.
