Proper No. Twelve

Proper No. Twelve is a brand of Irish whiskey launched in 2018 by mixed martial artist Conor McGregor through his company Eire Born Spirits. The name refers to Crumlin, the Dublin neighbourhood where McGregor grew up, which lies in the Dublin 12 postcode district, and to his nickname "The Notorious" persona built around doing things "properly." The whiskey is a blended Irish whiskey, triple-distilled and aged in bourbon casks, produced at the Old Bushmills Distillery in County Antrim.

A bottle of Proper No. Twelve

History

McGregor had previously promoted a whiskey under the name "Notorious," but withdrew it after a trademark dispute with an existing brewer. Proper No. Twelve was launched in Ireland and the United States in September 2018, with McGregor stating that a portion of proceeds — announced as five dollars per case — would be donated to first-responder organisations. The brand expanded rapidly into other markets on the strength of McGregor's celebrity, and at launch the company reported that demand temporarily outstripped supply.

Sale of majority stake

In 2021, Becle, the Mexican drinks company that owns the Jose Cuervo tequila brand and already held a minority stake, acquired majority control of Eire Born Spirits in a deal reported to value the business at more than $600 million; McGregor and his team retained an interest and McGregor continued to promote the brand. In 2024 it was reported that McGregor's remaining stake had been bought out, ending his ownership while he remained under contract for promotional appearances for a period.

Reception

Reviews from whiskey critics were generally mixed to negative, with many describing the spirit as an unremarkable blend marketed successfully on its founder's fame, though some conceded it was a drinkable entry-level Irish whiskey. Commercially it has nonetheless been regarded as one of the most successful celebrity spirits launches, credited with introducing new younger drinkers to the Irish whiskey category.
