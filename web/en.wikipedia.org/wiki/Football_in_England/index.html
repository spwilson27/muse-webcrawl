Association football is the most popular sport in England, where the modern game was codified. The Football Association, founded in London in 1863, published the first Laws of the Game, and England played Scotland in the first official international match in 1872.

League system

England has the world's oldest league competition: the Football League, founded in 1888. The current pyramid is headed by the Premier League (founded 1992), followed by the EFL Championship, League One and League Two, and a deep structure of semi-professional and amateur non-League divisions with promotion and relegation between levels. The FA Cup, first played in 1871–72, is the oldest national football competition in the world.

National teams and honours

The England men's team won the FIFA World Cup on home soil in 1966 and reached the Euro final in 2020 (played 2021) and again in 2024. The women's team won the UEFA Women's Euro in 2022 and again in 2025, transforming the popularity of women's football in the country. English clubs have won the European Cup/Champions League many times, most famously Manchester United, Liverpool, Chelsea, Nottingham Forest, Aston Villa and Manchester City.
