Optimus Prime

Optimus Prime

Voiced by: Peter Cullen
Function: Autobot leader
Alternate mode: Semi-truck (cab)

Optimus Prime is a fictional character and the protagonist of the Transformers franchise. He is the leader of the Autobots, a faction of sentient, transforming robots from the planet Cybertron, and is characterised by his strong moral character, selflessness and dedication to protecting all life, particularly humanity, against the Decepticons led by Megatron.

Character

In most continuities, Optimus was originally known as Orion Pax, a humble Cybertronian who was chosen by the Matrix of Leadership to become a Prime. He transforms into a red and blue semi-trailer truck cab, and in the original Generation 1 depiction carries an ion blaster and an energon axe, with his trailer and the small vehicle Roller forming part of his unit.

Portrayals

Optimus Prime has been voiced since 1984, in the original series and in the live-action films, by Peter Cullen, whose performance — modelled partly on his brother Larry, a Vietnam veteran — defined the character's grave, compassionate tone. The character died in the 1986 animated film Transformers: The Movie, a decision that provoked a strong reaction from young fans, and he was soon resurrected in the television series.

Legacy

Optimus Prime is one of the most recognisable characters of 1980s popular culture and a central figure of the multibillion-dollar Transformers toy and media franchise. His surname "Prime" and his catchphrases ("Autobots, roll out!", "Freedom is the right of all sentient beings") are widely quoted.
