Dodi Fayed

Emad El-Din Mohamed Abdel Mena'em Fayed (15 April 1955 – 31 August 1997), known as Dodi Fayed, was an Egyptian film producer and the son of billionaire Mohamed Al-Fayed, the former owner of Harrods department store and Fulham Football Club.

Life and death

Fayed worked as a film producer through Allied Stars, with credits including Chariots of Fire (1981), Breaking Glass, F/X, and Hook. In the summer of 1997 his relationship with Diana, Princess of Wales, attracted intense media attention. On 31 August 1997, Fayed and Diana were killed in a car crash in the Pont de l'Alma tunnel in Paris, along with their driver Henri Paul; bodyguard Trevor Rees-Jones was the sole survivor.

A memorial to Fayed and Diana was erected by his father at Harrods in London.
