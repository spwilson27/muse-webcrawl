Kirin Brewery Company

Kirin Brewery Company, Limited Industry: Beverage / brewing
Founded: 1885 (as Japan Brewery Company); 1907 as Kirin Brewery
Headquarters: Nakano, Tokyo, Japan
Parent: Kirin Holdings
Known products: Kirin Ichiban, Kirin Lager

The Kirin Brewery Company, Limited (麒麟麦酒株式会社) is one of Japan's largest brewing companies and the core beer business of Kirin Holdings. Its name and logo derive from the kirin, a mythical hooved creature of East Asian folklore said to appear at the birth of a sage.

History

The company descends from the Spring Valley Brewery founded in Yokohama in 1870 by the Norwegian-American brewer William Copeland, one of the earliest breweries in Japan. Reorganised as the Japan Brewery Company in 1885 with support from Thomas Blake Glover, it began marketing Kirin Beer in 1888. In 1907 the business was spun off as the independent Kirin Brewery Company.

For much of the post-war period Kirin held a dominant share — at times close to 60 per cent — of the Japanese beer market. Its flagship Kirin Lager, one of Japan's oldest beer brands, was challenged from 1987 by Asahi's Super Dry, and Kirin responded with Kirin Ichiban Shibori ("first press"), brewed only from the first wort, launched in 1990 and now its best-known brand internationally.

Business

Within the Kirin Group, the brewery operates breweries across Japan and has expanded overseas, holding stakes in brewers such as Lion in Australia and New Zealand and the San Miguel Brewery in the Philippines, alongside craft beer acquisitions in the United States. The wider Kirin Holdings group also includes soft drinks (Kirin Beverage), pharmaceuticals and health science businesses.
