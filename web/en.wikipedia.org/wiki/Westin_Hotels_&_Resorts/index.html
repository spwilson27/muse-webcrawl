Westin Hotels & Resorts is an American upscale hotel chain owned by Marriott International. As of recent counts it operates more than 225 hotels and resorts in nearly 40 countries and territories.

History

The company was founded in 1930 when two rival hoteliers in Washington state merged their interests as Western Hotels. The chain adopted the name "Westin" — a contraction of "Western International" — in 1981, and its former flagship properties include Seattle's Westin Building. It was acquired by Starwood Hotels and Resorts in 1998 and passed to Marriott when Marriott acquired Starwood in 2016.

Brand

Westin markets itself around wellness, under the slogan of helping guests "rise" refreshed: signature features include the "Heavenly Bed" (introduced in 1999 and credited with starting the hotel industry's luxury-bedding race), Heavenly Bath fixtures, in-room fitness gear partnerships and the SuperFoodsRx menu. Its hotels typically serve business travellers and resort guests in the upper-upscale segment.
