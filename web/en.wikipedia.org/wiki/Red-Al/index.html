Red-Al

Red-Al (a trade name) is the common name for sodium bis(2-methoxyethoxy)aluminium hydride, also known as SMEAH. It is a reducing agent used in organic synthesis, typically supplied as a solution in toluene.

Reactivity

Red-Al behaves similarly to lithium aluminium hydride (LiAlH4), reducing esters, carboxylic acids, amides, nitriles, and epoxides to the corresponding alcohols or amines. It is often preferred because it is soluble in aromatic hydrocarbons, thermally stable, non-pyrophoric in solution, and tolerates higher temperatures than LiAlH4.

Applications

The reagent is used in laboratory and industrial reductions, including the partial reduction of amides and nitriles and the reductive cleavage of ethers. It was developed in Czechoslovakia in the 1960s by researchers at the Institute of Chemical Process Fundamentals.
