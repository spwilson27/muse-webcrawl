Anil Dhirubhai Ambani (born 4 June 1959) is an Indian businessman. He is the chairman and managing director of the Reliance Group (also known as Reliance ADA Group), which was formed following the demerger of his father's Reliance Industries in 2005–2006. He leads a number of listed corporations including Reliance Capital, Reliance Infrastructure, Reliance Power and Reliance Communications.

Early life and education

Ambani is the younger son of Dhirubhai Ambani, founder of Reliance Industries, and Kokilaben Ambani. He attended the University of Mumbai, where he earned a Bachelor of Science degree, and holds an MBA from the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania.

Career

Ambani joined Reliance in 1983 and was closely involved in the company's capital market initiatives. Following the split of the Reliance empire between him and his elder brother Mukesh Ambani, he took control of the group's telecom, financial services, entertainment, power and infrastructure businesses. In 2008, Reliance Power's initial public offering was the largest in India's history at the time. Ambani served as a member of the Rajya Sabha, the upper house of the Indian Parliament, from 2004 to 2006.

Later business difficulties

From the early 2010s, several of Ambani's companies accumulated heavy debts. Reliance Communications entered insolvency proceedings in 2019, and in 2020 Ambani told a London court that his net worth was effectively zero amid a dispute over loans from Chinese banks.
