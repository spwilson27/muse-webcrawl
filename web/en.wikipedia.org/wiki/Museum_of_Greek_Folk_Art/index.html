Museum of Greek Folk Art

LocationPlaka, Athens, Greece
TypeFolk art museum
Established1918

The Museum of Greek Folk Art is a museum in Athens, Greece, dedicated to modern Greek folk art and folk culture from the mid-17th century to the present day. It was founded in 1918 as the Museum of Greek Handicrafts and is now known as the Museum of Modern Greek Culture.

Collections

The museum's collections include embroidery, weaving, traditional costumes from across Greece, jewellery, wood carving, pottery and ceramics, metalwork, shadow theatre figures and paintings. Among its notable holdings are works by the folk painter Theofilos Hatzimihail and a collection of Karagiozis shadow puppets.

Buildings

The museum's exhibitions have historically been spread across several sites in the Plaka district of Athens, including the Mosque of Tzistarakis in Monastiraki Square, the Bath House of the Winds, and annexes at 22 Panos Street. A consolidated museum complex was developed in the Monastiraki area to bring the collections together.
