Earth-One

Earth-One (also Earth-1) is a fictional universe in comic books published by DC Comics. It was the primary setting for DC's Silver Age stories, introduced when the company began distinguishing between the continuity of its Golden Age characters (retroactively assigned to Earth-Two) and the newer versions who debuted in the late 1950s and 1960s.

The concept was established in the story "Flash of Two Worlds!" in The Flash #123 (1961), in which Barry Allen met his Golden Age counterpart Jay Garrick. The idea was expanded in Justice League of America #21–22 (1963), "Crisis on Earth-One!", the first team-up between the Justice League of Earth-One and the Justice Society of Earth-Two.

History

Earth-One was home to the Silver Age versions of Superman, Batman, Wonder Woman, the Barry Allen Flash, Hal Jordan's Green Lantern, and the Justice League of America. Its history diverged from Earth-Two's in numerous details: its heroes generally debuted later, and some characters existed only in one universe.

Earth-One ceased to exist as a separate continuity in the 1985–86 limited series Crisis on Infinite Earths, in which the surviving parallel Earths were merged into a single universe. The name "Earth-1" was later reused in various reboots, including the post-Infinite Crisis multiverse and the Earth One graphic novel line launched in 2010.
