The athletics competitions at the 2012 Summer Olympics in London took place between 3 and 12 August 2012 at the Olympic Stadium in Stratford, with the marathons and race walking events held on a course around The Mall in central London. A total of 47 events were contested—24 for men and 23 for women—by more than 2,200 athletes from 201 nations.

Highlights

The meeting is remembered above all for "Super Saturday" on 4 August, when British athletes won three gold medals in the stadium in one evening: Jessica Ennis in the heptathlon, Greg Rutherford in the long jump, and Mo Farah in the 10,000 metres. Farah went on to complete a distance double by winning the 5,000 metres a week later.

Jamaica's Usain Bolt successfully defended his 100 metres and 200 metres titles, becoming the first man to retain both Olympic sprint crowns, and anchored Jamaica's 4 × 100 metres relay team to a world record of 36.84 seconds in the final event of the programme. Kenya's David Rudisha produced one of the greatest 800 metres runs in history, breaking his own world record with 1:40.91 in the final. The United States topped the medal table with nine golds and 29 medals overall.

Records and controversies

World records were also set by Jamaica's men's sprint relay team and the United States women's 4 × 100 metres relay team (40.82). In subsequent years, a number of results—especially in women's middle-distance and field events—were annulled following doping investigations and retests of stored samples, leading to the reallocation of several medals, most notably in the women's 1500 metres.

Medal summary

The United States finished first in the athletics medal table, followed by Russia (whose tally was later reduced by doping disqualifications), Jamaica, and Kenya. Host nation Great Britain won four gold medals, its best athletics performance of the modern era to that point. First-time Olympic athletics medallists included Grenada, whose Kirani James won the men's 400 metres, giving the country its first-ever Olympic medal.
