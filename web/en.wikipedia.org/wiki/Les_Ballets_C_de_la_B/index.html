Les Ballets C de la B

Les Ballets C de la B (originally Les Ballets Contemporains de la Belgique) was a Belgian contemporary dance company based in Ghent, founded in 1984 by the choreographer Alain Platel. It became one of Europe's most influential dance-theatre companies before winding up its activities in the early 2020s.

Style and productions

The company's work mixed dance, theatre, live music and performers recruited from outside conventional dance training — circus artists, children, and people with disabilities — in collages Platel called "bastard dance". Landmark productions included Bonjour Madame (1993), Iets op Bach (1998), Wolf (2003), vsprs (2006), pitié! (2008), Gardenia (2010), tauberbach (2014) and Requiem pour L. (2018). Other choreographers associated with the company included Sidi Larbi Cherkaoui (Foi, Rien de Rien), Koen Augustijnen and Ted Stoffer.

Organisation

Les Ballets C de la B operated as a platform structure in which several choreographers created work under one roof, supported by the Flemish authorities. Its productions toured worldwide for nearly four decades, and Platel's approach — raw, musical and political — strongly influenced European dance theatre.
