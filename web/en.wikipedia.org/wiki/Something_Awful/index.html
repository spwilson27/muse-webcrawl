Something Awful

Something Awful (SA) is an American comedy website and online forum hosting a variety of content, including blog entries, forums, feature articles, digitally edited pictures, and humorous media reviews. It was created by Richard "Lowtax" Kyanka in 1999 and has been credited with originating or popularising a number of staples of Internet culture, including the Let's Play video format.

History

Kyanka launched the site in 1999 after being fired from PlanetQuake. To fund it, SA famously charged a one-time fee (long priced at $9.95) for forum accounts — a paywall widely mocked at the time that nonetheless fostered a distinctive, heavily moderated community whose members call themselves "goons". After Kyanka's legal and financial troubles, he sold the site in 2020; he died in November 2021.

Influence

Something Awful helped seed early web culture: the "Photoshop Phriday" features, the All Your Base-era humour, the popularisation of Let's Play (begun as screenshot-narrated threads by forum users around 2006–07), and the Slender Man character (created in a 2009 SA Photoshop thread). Several influential sites and communities, including early 4chan users, drew from SA's culture. Its Let's Play Archive preserves thousands of completed threads.

Community and ventures

SA-affiliated projects included the comedy troupe behind "Doom House", the Flash Tub and Front Page features, and charity drives by forum members. The forums remain active decades after launch, an unusual longevity for a pay-to-post community.
