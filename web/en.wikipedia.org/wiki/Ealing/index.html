Ealing
Area of London, England
Borough: London Borough of Ealing
Known as: "Queen of the Suburbs"

Ealing is a district in West London, England, the administrative centre of the London Borough of Ealing. Historically a village in Middlesex, it grew rapidly after the arrival of the Great Western Railway and was dubbed the "Queen of the Suburbs" for its leafy, semi-rural character.

History

A settlement at Ealing is recorded in the 12th century. The opening of Ealing Broadway station in 1838 and later tram and Underground services transformed it into a prosperous Victorian suburb. The borough became part of Greater London in 1965.

Culture

Ealing Studios, founded in 1902, is the world's oldest continuously working film studio site and gave its name to the celebrated Ealing comedies of the 1940s and 1950s, including Kind Hearts and Coronets and The Ladykillers. The district also hosts the Ealing Jazz Festival and summer festivals in Walpole Park.
