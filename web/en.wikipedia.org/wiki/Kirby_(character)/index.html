Kirby (character)

Kirby is the protagonist of Nintendo's Kirby video game series, created by Masahiro Sakurai and developed by HAL Laboratory. A small, round, pink creature from Planet Popstar in Dream Land, Kirby debuted in Kirby's Dream Land (1992) for the Game Boy.

Abilities

Kirby's signature abilities are inhaling enemies and objects, floating by inflating himself with air, and — from Kirby's Adventure (1993) onward — Copy Abilities that let him mimic the powers of swallowed foes. He defends Dream Land against foes such as King Dedede and Meta Knight.

Design

Originally a placeholder sprite, Kirby's simple spherical design was retained; his pink colour was settled after some disagreement, his first western appearance showing him white. He also appears as a fighter in the Super Smash Bros. series.
