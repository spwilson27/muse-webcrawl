The I Threes were a Jamaican reggae vocal trio formed in 1974 to support Bob Marley & The Wailers after Peter Tosh and Bunny Wailer left the group. Its members were Rita Marley (Bob Marley's wife), Marcia Griffiths and Judy Mowatt.

Career

Griffiths had already scored a hit with "Young, Gifted and Black" (with Bob Andy) and Mowatt had been a member of the Gaylettes. Together their close three-part harmonies became an integral part of the Wailers' sound on albums such as Natty Dread, Rastaman Vibration, Exodus and Uprising, and on stage throughout Marley's career until his death in 1981. Their first recording with Marley was on "Jah Live" (1975) era sessions and the Natty Dread album.

The trio also recorded in their own right, releasing the album Beginning in 1986, and each member pursued a successful solo career — Griffiths becoming known as the "Queen of Reggae". The I Threes have reunited for tribute performances in later years.
