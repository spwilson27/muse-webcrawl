Communism is a political and economic ideology advocating a classless, stateless society with common ownership of the means of production — summarised as 'from each according to his ability, to each according to his needs'. Marx and Engels's Communist Manifesto (1848) founded its modern form.

History

After the Russian Revolution of 1917, communist states arose across Eastern Europe and Asia under leaders including Joseph Stalin; the Cold War pitted them against the capitalist West led by the US and its NATO allies. Most collapsed in 1989–91; China, Vietnam, Cuba, Laos and North Korea remain nominally communist under one-party rule.
