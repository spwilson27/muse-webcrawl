Shadowpact

The Shadowpact is a fictional team of supernatural heroes in the DC Comics universe, created by writer Bill Willingham. The group first appeared in Day of Vengeance #1 (2005), a tie-in to the Infinite Crisis event, and went on to star in its own ongoing series, Shadowpact, which ran for 25 issues from 2006 to 2008.

History

The Shadowpact was formed when the Spectre, driven mad and convinced that magic itself was evil, began systematically destroying magical beings and places, including the Rock of Eternity above Gotham City. A disparate group of mystics gathered at the Oblivion Bar — an interdimensional tavern frequented by magic users — and resolved to oppose the Spectre and restore the balance of magic. Though they could not defeat the Spectre directly, their efforts bought time for other forces to intervene, and the group formally banded together afterward to police supernatural threats, naming themselves after the impromptu alliance.

The team's base of operations remained the Oblivion Bar, whose owner, Eddie Deacon, served as a supporting character. Many of the members were characters Willingham revived from DC's supernatural back catalogue and retooled for modern stories, including the talking chimpanzee Detective Chimp, who served the team as a strategist.

Members

  • Blue Devil — Dan Cassidy, a stuntman bonded to a demonic suit of armor.
  • Detective Chimp — Bobo T. Chimpanzee, a hyper-intelligent chimpanzee detective.
  • Enchantress — June Moone, a sorceress who shares her body with a powerful and unstable magical entity.
  • Nightmaster — Jim Rook, wielder of the mystical Sword of Night, and owner of the Oblivion Bar's earthly entrance.
  • Nightshade — Eve Eden, able to create creatures from living shadow.
  • Ragman — Rory Regan, whose patchwork suit absorbs the souls of the wicked.
  • Zauriel — an angel of the Eagle Host who chose to remain on Earth.
  • Warlock's Daughter — Laura Fell, a young witch who joined during the ongoing series.

In other media

Several Shadowpact members have appeared individually in live-action and animated DC adaptations, although the team itself, as constituted in the comics, has not headlined a screen adaptation.
