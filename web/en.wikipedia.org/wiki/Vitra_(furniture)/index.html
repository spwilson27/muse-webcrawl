Vitra (furniture)

Vitra is a Swiss family-owned furniture company founded in 1950 by Willi and Erika Fehlbaum in Birsfelden, near Basel. Vitra is known for manufacturing the works of major modern and contemporary designers — above all Charles and Ray Eames, whose furniture it has produced for the European market under licence from Herman Miller since 1957 — and for its campus at Weil am Rhein, Germany, a landmark collection of contemporary architecture.

Products and designers

Under Rolf Fehlbaum, Vitra expanded from licensing Eames and Herman Miller designs to commissioning original work from designers including George Nelson, Verner Panton (the Panton Chair, 1967, the first single-piece plastic chair), Antonio Citterio, Jasper Morrison, Ronan and Erwan Bouroullec, Konstantin Grcic, and Hella Jongerius. Its catalogue spans office chairs, home furniture, and iconic objects such as the Eames Lounge Chair.

Vitra Campus and Design Museum

The Weil am Rhein campus contains buildings by Frank Gehry, Zaha Hadid (her first built work, the fire station), Tadao Ando, Álvaro Siza, Herzog & de Meuron (the VitraHaus, 2010), SANAA, and Renzo Piano, among others. The Vitra Design Museum, founded in 1989 in a Gehry-designed building, mounts major exhibitions on design and holds one of the world's largest collections of modern furniture, including the estates of Charles and Ray Eames.
