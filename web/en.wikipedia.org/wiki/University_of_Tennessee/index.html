University of Tennessee

The University of Tennessee, Knoxville (UT or UTK) is a public land-grant research university in Knoxville, Tennessee. Founded in 1794 as Blount College, it is the flagship campus of the University of Tennessee system and one of the oldest public universities in the United States.

History

The institution became Tennessee's land-grant university under the Morrill Act of 1862 and adopted the name University of Tennessee in 1879. Its campus, centred on "the Hill" around Ayres Hall, grew substantially in the 20th century; it also operates joint research programmes with nearby Oak Ridge National Laboratory, including the Bredesen Center for graduate energy science.

Academics and athletics

UT enrols more than 36,000 students across 11 colleges and is recognised for programmes in supply chain management, nuclear engineering, and business. Its athletic teams, the Volunteers (and Lady Vols for women's teams), are famous for their orange and white colours and checkerboard end zones; Neyland Stadium, home of the football team, is one of the largest stadiums in the United States, with a capacity of over 100,000.
