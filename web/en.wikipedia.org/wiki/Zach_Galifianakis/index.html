Zach Galifianakis

Born: October 1, 1969, Wilkesboro, North Carolina, U.S.

Occupation: Comedian, actor, writer

Zachary Knight Galifianakis (born October 1, 1969) is an American comedian and actor, known for his deadpan stand-up style and for playing Alan Garner in The Hangover film trilogy (2009–2013).

Career

Galifianakis began performing stand-up in New York in the 1990s and appeared on Comedy Central Presents in 2001. He gained a wider audience hosting the Funny or Die talk show parody Between Two Ferns with Zach Galifianakis (2008–2018), whose interviewees included Barack Obama and Hillary Clinton. The series was adapted into a feature film in 2019.

Other work

He co-created and starred in the FX comedy-drama Baskets (2016–2019), voicing roles in animated films including Puss in Boots (2011) and The Lego Batman Movie (2017), where he voiced the Joker.
