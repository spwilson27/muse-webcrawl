Roy Plomley

Roy Plomley (20 January 1914 – 28 May 1985) was a British radio broadcaster, producer and playwright, best known as the creator and presenter of BBC Radio's Desert Island Discs.

Career

Plomley devised Desert Island Discs in 1941 and presented its first broadcast in January 1942. He chaired the programme for more than 40 years, interviewing hundreds of castaways. He also hosted the 1950s game show that became Take Your Pick on radio, and acted and wrote for stage and radio. He was appointed OBE in 1975.
