Raipur

Raipur is the capital city of the Indian state of Chhattisgarh. It became the state capital when Chhattisgarh was formed from Madhya Pradesh on 1 November 2000. The city is a major commercial and industrial hub of central India.

Geography and economy

Raipur lies near the centre of the Chhattisgarh plain, often called the "rice bowl of India", with the Mahanadi River to its east. The surrounding region is rich in coal, iron ore and limestone, and the city hosts steel, cement and alloy industries, wholesale grain and produce markets, and the planned new capital area of Naya Raipur (Atal Nagar) to its southeast. Swami Vivekananda Airport serves the city.

Demographics

The population of the city proper was about 1 million at the 2011 census, with over 1.8 million in the urban agglomeration; Hindi and Chhattisgarhi are the main languages.
