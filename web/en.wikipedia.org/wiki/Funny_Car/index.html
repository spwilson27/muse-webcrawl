Funny Car

Funny Car is a type of drag racing vehicle and a specific racing class in organized drag racing. Funny cars are characterized by having a tilt-up fiberglass or carbon fiber automobile body over a custom-fabricated chassis, giving them an appearance vaguely approximating manufacturers' showroom models. They are powered by supercharged, nitromethane-burning engines producing around 11,000 horsepower.

The class originated in the mid-1960s in the United States. In NHRA competition, Funny Cars race over 1,000 feet, reaching speeds exceeding 330 mph in under four seconds. Famous drivers include John Force, Don Prudhomme, and Ron Capps. The name derives from the cars' odd, "funny" appearance compared with stock vehicles, with the wheelbase altered for traction.
