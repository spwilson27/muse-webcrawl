Cotai is a 5.6-square-kilometre area of reclaimed land in Macau, China, situated between the islands of Taipa and Coloane, which were previously separated by Seac Pai Van Bay. Its name is a portmanteau of the two islands' names. Cotai is home to the Cotai Strip, a concentration of large integrated casino resorts modelled on the Las Vegas Strip.

Development

The land was reclaimed mainly in the 2000s to provide room for Macau's expanding gaming industry after the liberalisation of casino licences in 2002. The Venetian Macao, opened in 2007 by Las Vegas Sands, was the anchor project and for a time the largest casino building in the world. It was followed by City of Dreams (2009), Galaxy Macau (2011), Sands Cotai Central (2012, now The Londoner Macao), Studio City (2015), The Parisian Macao and Wynn Palace (2016), MGM Cotai (2018) and Grand Lisboa Palace (2021).

Transport and facilities

Cotai is crossed by the Lotus Bridge to Hengqin Island in mainland China and served by the Taipa line of the Macau Light Rapid Transit, opened in 2019. The area also contains the Macau East Asian Games Dome, the campus of the Macau University of Science and Technology, and the Cotai Water Jet ferry terminal at nearby Taipa.
