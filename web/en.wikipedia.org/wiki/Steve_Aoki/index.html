Steve Aoki

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Steven Hiroyuki Aoki (born November 30, 1977) is an American DJ, record producer, music executive, and entrepreneur. He is the founder of the record label Dim Mak Records, which has released music by acts including The Chainsmokers, Bloc Party, and Blood Brothers. Known for his relentless touring schedule and for throwing cakes at fans during performances, Aoki has ranked consistently among the world's highest-earning and most popular electronic dance music DJs.

Early life

Aoki was born in Miami, Florida, and raised in Newport Beach, California. He is the son of Hiroaki Aoki, the Japanese-born wrestler and founder of the Benihana restaurant chain, and the younger brother of model and actress Devon Aoki. He attended the University of California, Santa Barbara, graduating with degrees in sociology and feminist studies, and played in hardcore punk bands while organizing shows in his college living room.

Career

Aoki founded Dim Mak in 1996 while still at university, naming the label after a move of his childhood hero, Bruce Lee. In the 2000s he became a prominent Los Angeles club DJ, known for remixes of artists such as Drake, Michael Jackson, and Linkin Park. His debut studio album, Wonderland (2012), was nominated for a Grammy Award for Best Dance/Electronica Album. Subsequent albums in the Neon Future series featured guest appearances from will.i.am, Fall Out Boy, and others. His collaboration "Just Hold On" with Louis Tomlinson became a UK number-one single in 2016, and "Waste It on Me" (2018), featuring BTS, was a major streaming hit.

Live performances and ventures

Aoki is famed for playing upwards of 200 shows a year and for stage rituals including cake-throwing, champagne spraying, and crowd-surfing on inflatable rafts. Beyond music he has founded the Aoki Foundation, which supports research into brain science and regenerative medicine, and has pursued interests in fashion, NFTs, and longevity science.
