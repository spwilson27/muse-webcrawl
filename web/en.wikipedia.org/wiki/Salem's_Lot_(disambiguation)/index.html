Salem's Lot (disambiguation)

'Salem's Lot is a 1975 horror novel by Stephen King, about a writer who returns to the fictional Maine town of Jerusalem's Lot (Salem's Lot) and discovers its inhabitants are becoming vampires.

'Salem's Lot, Stephen King's 1975 vampire novel, and its adaptations.

Salem's Lot may also refer to adaptations of the novel:

  • Salem's Lot (1979), a television miniseries directed by Tobe Hooper, also released in a shortened theatrical film version.
  • Salem's Lot (2004), a television miniseries starring Rob Lowe.
  • Salem's Lot (2024), a feature film adaptation released on Max, directed by Gary Dauberman.
  • A Return to Salem's Lot (1987), a film sequel to the 1979 miniseries.
  • Salem's Lot, radio dramatisations of the novel produced by the BBC.
