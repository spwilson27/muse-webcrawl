Heliosphere

TypeStellar bubble / magnetosphere-like region
Created bySolar wind from the Sun
BoundaryHeliopause

The heliosphere is the magnetosphere-like bubble in space created by the solar wind from the Sun, encompassing the Solar System and shielding it from much interstellar cosmic radiation.

Structure

The solar wind flows outward supersonically until the termination shock, where it slows abruptly — crossed by Voyager 1 in 2004 and Voyager 2 in 2007. Beyond lies the heliosheath, then the heliopause, the boundary with the interstellar medium, crossed by Voyager 1 in 2012 at about 121 AU and Voyager 2 in 2018. A bow wave may precede the heliosphere as the Sun moves through the local interstellar cloud.

Shape

Long pictured as comet-like with a long tail, recent models and IBEX and Cassini data suggest rounder, bubble- or croissant-like shapes, an active research question.

Exploration

Pioneer, Voyager, Ulysses, IBEX and IMAP missions study its plasma, magnetic field and energetic neutral atoms.
