List of American comics creators

This is a list of notable American comics creators — writers, artists, pencillers, inkers, and cartoonists who have worked in American comic books and comic strips.

Selected creators

  • Will Eisner — The Spirit; pioneer of the graphic novel
  • Jack Kirby — co-creator of Captain America, the Fantastic Four, the X-Men, the Hulk, and the New Gods
  • Stan Lee — Marvel Comics writer and editor; co-creator of Spider-Man and many others
  • Steve Ditko — co-creator of Spider-Man and Doctor Strange
  • Bob Kane and Bill Finger — creators of Batman
  • Jerry Siegel and Joe Shuster — creators of Superman
  • Charles M. Schulz — Peanuts
  • Winsor McCay — Little Nemo
  • Art Spiegelman — Maus
  • Robert Crumb — underground comix pioneer
  • Frank Miller — The Dark Knight Returns, Sin City
  • Alan Moore (British-born, worked extensively in American comics) — Watchmen
  • Neil Gaiman (British-born) — The Sandman
  • Alison Bechdel — Fun Home
  • Lynda Barry, Chris Ware, Daniel Clowes, Harvey Kurtzman, Carl Barks, John Stanley, Gil Kane, Wally Wood, Neal Adams, Jim Steranko, Bernie Wrightson, George Pérez, John Byrne, David Mazzucchelli
