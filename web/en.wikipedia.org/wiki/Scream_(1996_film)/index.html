Scream (1996 film)

Scream is a 1996 American slasher film directed by Wes Craven and written by Kevin Williamson. It stars David Arquette, Neve Campbell, Courteney Cox, Matthew Lillard, Rose McGowan, Skeet Ulrich, Jamie Kennedy, and Drew Barrymore. Set in the fictional town of Woodsboro, California, the film follows high school student Sidney Prescott and her friends, who are targeted by a masked killer known as Ghostface.

Williamson's script combined genuine scares with satire of the clichés of the slasher genre, whose characters are aware of horror-movie conventions. The film was a major commercial and critical success, grossing $173 million worldwide, and is credited with revitalising the horror genre in the 1990s.

Plot

After student Casey Becker is murdered by a caller who quizzes her on horror films, a killing spree unfolds around Sidney, who is still grieving her mother's murder a year earlier. Reporters including Gale Weathers converge on Woodsboro as Sidney and her friends try to identify the killer, culminating in a house-party finale where the killers — her boyfriend Billy Loomis and his accomplice Stu Macher — are revealed.

Legacy

Scream spawned a franchise of sequels and a television series, and its Ghostface mask became an enduring pop-culture icon.
