Clear Lake, Iowa

Clear Lake is a city in Cerro Gordo County, Iowa, United States. It lies on the eastern shore of the lake of the same name, one of the largest natural lakes in Iowa, and had a population of roughly 7,700 at the 2020 census. The city is a popular summer resort destination known for boating, fishing, and beaches.

History

The area around Clear Lake was settled by European Americans in the early 1850s, and the town was platted in 1857. It developed as a resort community in the late nineteenth century, served by railways bringing visitors from across the Midwest.

"The Day the Music Died"

On 3 February 1959, rock and roll musicians Buddy Holly, Ritchie Valens, and J. P. "The Big Bopper" Richardson were killed, along with pilot Roger Peterson, when their chartered plane crashed in a cornfield near Clear Lake shortly after takeoff. They had just performed at the Surf Ballroom in Clear Lake, a venue that still operates and hosts an annual Winter Dance Party commemorating the event. A memorial stands at the crash site, marked by a large pair of horn-rimmed glasses in tribute to Holly.

Geography

Clear Lake (the lake) covers about 3,600 acres and is a glacial lake. The city lies along Interstate 35 and U.S. Route 18, west of Mason City. McIntosh Woods State Park and Clear Lake State Park provide public access to the shore.
