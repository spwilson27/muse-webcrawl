Letterkenny

Letterkenny is a Canadian television sitcom created by Jared Keeso and Jacob Tierney, which premiered in 2016 on Crave and concluded in 2023 after twelve seasons. Set in the fictional small rural Ontario town of Letterkenny, the series is known for its rapid-fire, pun- and slang-heavy dialogue and its affectionate, unsentimental portrait of small-town life.

Premise

The show follows siblings Wayne and Katy, who run a small farm and produce stand, along with their friends Daryl and "Squirrelly" Dan. The recurring refrain of the series is that "there are 5,000 people in Letterkenny; these are their problems." Storylines revolve around the town's overlapping social groups: the farmers ("hicks"), the hockey players, the drug-dealing "skids", the local Christians, the gym-goers, and members of the nearby First Nation reserve, whose rivalries and friendships drive the comedy.

History and reception

Letterkenny grew out of the YouTube web series Letterkenny Problems (2013), whose short sketches showcasing the title characters' deadpan verbal sparring attracted a wide audience. The television adaptation became a flagship success for Crave and, after distribution on Hulu, gained a large following in the United States. It won multiple Canadian Screen Awards, including Best Comedy Series. A spin-off, Shoresy, following the foul-mouthed hockey character of the same name, premiered in 2022.
