Antistatic device

An antistatic device is any device that reduces, dampens or otherwise inhibits the build-up and discharge of static electricity, which can damage sensitive electronic components or ignite flammable liquids and gases. Such devices form part of an electrostatic discharge (ESD) protected area in electronics manufacturing and servicing.


[Hero image placeholder: antistatic wrist strap and grounding cord]

Types

  • Antistatic wrist straps — worn by technicians, draining charge through a high-resistance (typically 1 MΩ) cord to ground.
  • Antistatic mats and flooring — dissipative surfaces that equalise potentials at workbenches.
  • Antistatic bags — metallised shielding bags that protect components from ESD in storage and transport.
  • Ionizers — blowers or bars that flood an area with positive and negative ions to neutralise charge on insulators.
  • Antistatic garments, gloves and footwear — conductive-fibre clothing used in cleanrooms and assembly plants.

Principles

Devices work by grounding, dissipative materials (surface resistivities between about 10⁵ and 10¹² ohms per square), or air ionisation. Standards such as ANSI/ESD S20.20 govern their use in industry.
