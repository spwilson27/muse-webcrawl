Westin Hotels & Resorts
Founded: 1930 (as Western Hotels)
Parent: Marriott International
Industry: Hospitality

Westin Hotels & Resorts is an American upscale hotel chain owned by Marriott International. Founded in 1930 in the Pacific Northwest as Western Hotels, the company became Western International Hotels and adopted the name Westin in 1981.

Brand

Westin markets itself around wellness, introducing the "Heavenly Bed" in 1999, which was widely credited with starting the hotel industry's "bed wars", along with fitness partnerships and the "Eat Well, Move Well, Sleep Well" pillars. The brand operates several hundred hotels and resorts in dozens of countries, typically in city centres and resort destinations.

Ownership history

Westin passed through owners including United Airlines and Aoki Corporation before being acquired by Starwood Hotels and Resorts in the 1990s; it became part of Marriott International following Marriott's acquisition of Starwood in 2016. See also Marriott International.
