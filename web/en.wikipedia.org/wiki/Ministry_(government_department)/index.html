Ministry (government department)

Ministry
Government department headed by a minister

A ministry is a governmental organisation, headed by a minister, that manages a specific sector of public administration. Ministries are common in parliamentary and many presidential systems; equivalent bodies elsewhere are called departments, secretariats, or offices — as with the departments of the United States federal government or the "secretary of state" departments of the United Kingdom.

Organisation

A ministry is typically led politically by a minister, a member of the cabinet, and administered by senior civil servants such as a permanent secretary or director-general. Common ministries include foreign affairs, finance, defence, interior, justice, health, and education. The number, names, and portfolios of ministries change as governments reorganise — a reshuffle of responsibilities sometimes called changes to the machinery of government.
