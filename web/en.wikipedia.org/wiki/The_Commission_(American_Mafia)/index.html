The Commission is the governing body of the American Mafia, formed in 1931 by Charles "Lucky" Luciano following the Castellammarese War. It replaced the old "boss of bosses" arrangement with a board of directors composed of the bosses of New York's Five Families and other powerful families, notably Chicago and Buffalo.

Role

The Commission was intended to settle disputes between families, approve new bosses and major initiatives, regulate rackets, and authorise murders of made members. Its creation formalised cooperation among crime families and prohibited the unauthorised killing of bosses.

History

Major setbacks came with the Apalachin meeting raid of 1957, which exposed the organisation to national attention, and the Mafia Commission Trial of 1985–1986, in which federal prosecutors used RICO charges to convict the heads of the Five Families, effectively decapitating the Commission.
