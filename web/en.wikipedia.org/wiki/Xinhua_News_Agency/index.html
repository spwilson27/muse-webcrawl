Xinhua News Agency

Xinhua News Agency
Founded1931
HeadquartersBeijing
StatusOfficial state agency
[Image: Xinhua's headquarters in Beijing]

Xinhua News Agency is the official state news agency of the People's Republic of China, founded in 1931 as the Red China News Agency and renamed Xinhua ('New China') in 1937. One of the world's largest agencies by bureau count, it is the authoritative channel for Chinese government announcements and a central instrument of China's external communication.

Role

Xinhua, overseen by the State Council, publishes in many languages, runs the Xinhuanet web portal and the China Xinhua News app, and supplies content to media worldwide. Its reporting follows the Chinese Communist Party's line on sensitive topics; Western governments and researchers have characterised parts of its output as state messaging or propaganda, a description Beijing rejects.
