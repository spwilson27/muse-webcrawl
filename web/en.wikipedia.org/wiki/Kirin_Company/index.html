Kirin Company, Limited is a Japanese beverage company and the operating arm of Kirin Holdings, one of Japan's "big four" brewers alongside Asahi, Suntory, and Sapporo. Its flagship products include Kirin Lager, one of the oldest beer brands in Japan, and Kirin Ichiban Shibori, a lager made using only the first press of the wort.

History

The company traces its origins to the Spring Valley Brewery founded in Yokohama in 1870 by the Norwegian-American brewer William Copeland. Reorganised as the Japan Brewery Company in 1885 with Mitsubishi backing, it launched Kirin beer in 1888, named after the kirin, a mythical hooved creature of Chinese folklore that still adorns its labels. In 1907 the business was incorporated as Kirin Brewery Company. A 2007 restructuring created the Kirin Holdings structure, and in 2013 the domestic beverage operations were integrated under Kirin Company.

Operations

Beyond beer, Kirin Company sells soft drinks (including the long-running Gogo no Kōcha afternoon tea brand), bottled water, and dairy products through its Koiwai subsidiary, and has overseas operations including Lion in Australia and New Zealand and a stake in Myanmar Brewery.
