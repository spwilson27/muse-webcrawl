Kongsberg Defence & Aerospace

Parent: Kongsberg Gruppen

Headquarters: Kongsberg, Norway

Products: Missile systems, air defence, remote weapon stations

Kongsberg Defence & Aerospace (KDA) is a business area of the Norwegian technology group Kongsberg Gruppen and one of Norway's largest defence contractors, supplying command and control systems, weapons control systems, missiles, and communications to armed forces worldwide.

Products

Its best-known systems include the NASAMS air-defence system (developed with Raytheon), the Naval Strike Missile (NSM) and its air-launched variant the Joint Strike Missile (JSM), and the PROTECTOR family of remote weapon stations, widely exported.

History

The business descends from the Kongsberg Weapons Factory, founded in 1814. It became part of Kongsberg Gruppen when the state-owned group was restructured in the late 1980s.
