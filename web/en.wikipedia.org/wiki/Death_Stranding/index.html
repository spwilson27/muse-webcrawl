Death Stranding

Death Stranding is a 2019 action game developed by Kojima Productions and directed by Hideo Kojima. It was Kojima's first game after his departure from Konami.

The game is set in a post-apocalyptic United States ravaged by a cataclysmic event known as the Death Stranding, which unleashed spectral creatures called BTs (Beached Things) into the world. Players control Sam Porter Bridges, a porter tasked with delivering cargo and reconnecting isolated settlements via the Chiral Network.

Gameplay

Players traverse open terrain on foot and by vehicle, managing balance and cargo weight while avoiding BTs and hostile human factions. An asynchronous multiplayer system lets players encounter structures and supplies left by others.

Reception

The game received generally favourable reviews, with praise for its originality, performances (including Norman Reedus and Mads Mikkelsen) and visuals, though its slow pace divided critics. A sequel, Death Stranding 2: On the Beach, was released in 2025.
