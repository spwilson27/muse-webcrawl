A centered hexagonal number, or hex number, is a centered figurate number that represents a hexagon with a dot in the centre and all other dots surrounding the centre in concentric hexagonal layers. The nth centered hexagonal number is given by the formula 3n(n − 1) + 1, producing the sequence 1, 7, 19, 37, 61, 91, 127, 169, …

Properties

Each centered hexagonal number is one more than six times a triangular number, and equivalently the difference between consecutive centered hexagonal numbers is a multiple of six: 6, 12, 18, 24, … The sum of the first n centered hexagonal numbers is the nth cube, n³, a striking identity provable geometrically by dissecting a cube of dots into hexagonal shells. Centered hexagonal numbers that are prime (7, 19, 37, 61, 127, …) are sometimes called hex primes. They also count the points of a hexagonal lattice within a hexagon-shaped region, which makes them appear in crystallography, in the arrangement of cells in beehives, and in board games played on hex grids such as the arrangement of tiles at maximum packing radius.
