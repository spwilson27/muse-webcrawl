Fez (video game)

Fez
Developer: Polytron Corporation
Designer: Phil Fish
Composer: Disasterpeace (Rich Vreeland)
Released: 13 April 2012 (Xbox 360)
Genre: Puzzle-platform

Fez is a puzzle-platform video game developed by Phil Fish and Shawn McGrath under the name Polytron Corporation and released in 2012. The player controls Gomez, a two-dimensional character living in a 2D world, who receives a magical fez that reveals the world to be three-dimensional. The central mechanic is rotating the world in 90-degree steps between four 2D views, revealing new paths and platforms.

Gameplay

There are no enemies, bosses, or deaths in Fez; the goal is exploration and puzzle-solving. Gomez collects cube fragments to restore the Hexahedron, a cosmic object whose shattering threatens his world. Beyond its platforming surface, the game conceals a second layer of puzzles involving a fictional alphabet, a numeral system, QR codes, and button-input "anti-cubes", many of which were solved collaboratively by players online. Completing the game unlocks a New Game+ mode with a first-person view.

Development

Fez had a famously long and troubled development, becoming a symbol of independent game development. Announced in 2007, it won the Excellence in Visual Art award at the 2008 Independent Games Festival, but disputes between Fish and his original business partner, a switch of publishers, and repeated redesigns delayed release for five years. The development was chronicled in the 2012 documentary Indie Game: The Movie. On release the game won the IGF's Seumas McNally Grand Prize and received strong reviews, though a save-corrupting patch controversy and Fish's outspoken public persona kept it in the news; a planned sequel, Fez II, was cancelled in 2013.

Legacy

Fez has been ported to many platforms, including PlayStation, Nintendo Switch, and iOS, and is credited with helping popularise the modern wave of independent games. Its soundtrack by Disasterpeace, built from 1980s synthesiser textures, is frequently cited among the best video game scores of its era.
