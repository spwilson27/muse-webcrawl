Miles Gonzalo Morales is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Brian Michael Bendis and artist Sara Pichelli, he first appeared in Ultimate Fallout #4 (August 2011) in the Ultimate Marvel imprint, succeeding Peter Parker as Spider-Man in that universe after Parker's death. A teenager of Black American and Puerto Rican descent from Brooklyn, Morales later crossed into Marvel's mainstream continuity following the Secret Wars storyline.

Fictional biography

Miles, the son of Jefferson Davis (later renamed Jefferson Morales) and Rio Morales, gained spider powers after being bitten by a genetically engineered spider stolen by his uncle Aaron Davis, the criminal Prowler. In addition to Peter Parker's powers he can camouflage himself and deliver a "venom strike" bio-electric shock. Reluctant at first, he took up the mantle after witnessing Peter Parker's death, and was eventually mentored by the resurrected Parker's counterpart and other heroes.

In other media

Morales is the protagonist of the animated films Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018), which won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature, and its sequel Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse (2023), voiced by Shameik Moore. He headlines the video games Marvel's Spider-Man: Miles Morales (2020) and appears in Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (2023), voiced by Nadji Jeter. His creation was widely discussed as a milestone for diversity in superhero comics.
