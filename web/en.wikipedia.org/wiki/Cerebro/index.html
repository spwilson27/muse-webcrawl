Cerebro (Spanish and Portuguese for "brain"; originally styled "Cyberno" in prototype form) is a fictional device appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, most commonly in association with the X-Men. Created by writer Stan Lee and artist Jack Kirby, it first appeared in The X-Men #7 (September 1964).

Description

Cerebro is a supercomputer built by Professor Charles Xavier to detect and locate mutants by their distinctive brainwave patterns, which differ from those of baseline humans. Used with a telepath such as Xavier or Emma Frost, the device amplifies the user's telepathy to a global range, allowing them to find mutants anywhere on Earth and, in some stories, to harm or kill them if driven to destructive power levels. The system is typically housed in a spherical chamber in the X-Mansion (Xavier's School for Gifted Youngsters), accessed by a walkway, with the user wearing a distinctive helmet.

Versions and storylines

Successive versions include Cerebra, an upgraded system at the Jean Grey School, and a portable helmet-based unit. In one storyline, Cerebro gained sentience and became a villain; in the House of X era, Cerebro — backed up by Xavier — continuously records the minds of all mutants, enabling their resurrection on the island nation of Krakoa. Cerebro features prominently in the X-Men films, where its chamber and helmet are iconic set pieces.
