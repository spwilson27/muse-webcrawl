Sole proprietorship

A sole proprietorship, also called a sole tradership or simply a proprietorship, is a type of business entity owned and run by one individual, in which there is no legal distinction between the owner and the business. The sole proprietor is entitled to all profits and is personally responsible for all of the business's debts, losses, and liabilities. It is the simplest and most common form of business organization in many countries, including the United States, where most small businesses start out as sole proprietorships.

Characteristics

  • Formation — no formal creation is usually required beyond obtaining any necessary licenses and permits; operating under a different name typically requires registering a "doing business as" (DBA) or fictitious name.
  • Unlimited liability — creditors can pursue the owner's personal assets, the form's chief disadvantage.
  • Taxation — the business is not taxed separately; income "passes through" to the owner's personal return (in the U.S., reported on Schedule C), though the owner pays self-employment tax.
  • Control and dissolution — the owner has complete control, and the business generally ends when the owner dies or chooses to close it.

Advantages and disadvantages

Advantages include minimal start-up cost and paperwork, full control, and simple tax filing. Disadvantages include unlimited personal liability, difficulty raising capital (no stock can be issued, and loans depend on the owner's credit), limited life, and the burden on a single person to perform or hire all functions. Many growing businesses convert to an LLC or corporation to gain liability protection and easier access to capital, at the cost of greater formality.

International equivalents

Analogous forms exist worldwide: the sole trader in the United Kingdom, Australia, and New Zealand; the entreprise individuelle in France; the Einzelunternehmen in Germany; and the kōjin jigyō (individual enterprise) in Japan. Rules vary, but the absence of a separate legal personality is the common feature.
