EasyGroup (styled as easyGroup) is the private investment and brand-licensing company of the Greek-Cypriot entrepreneur Sir Stelios Haji-Ioannou. It owns the "easy" brand and licenses it to a family of businesses — most famously the airline easyJet — which apply a low-cost, no-frills business model, a distinctive bright-orange livery and a lowercase "easy" prefix across travel, leisure and consumer services.

History

Haji-Ioannou founded easyJet in 1995, adapting the Southwest Airlines low-cost model to Europe with ticketless, direct internet sales and unbundled fares. The airline floated on the London Stock Exchange in 2000, reducing the founder's stake while easyGroup retained ownership of the brand itself, licensed back to the airline under a brand licence agreement — an arrangement that has periodically produced disputes between Haji-Ioannou and the airline's board over strategy, dividends and the terms of the licence.

Brand ventures

Ventures launched or licensed under the easy brand have included:

  • easyJet — one of Europe's largest low-cost airlines (separately listed; brand licensee).
  • easyBus — low-cost airport and intercity coach services.
  • easyHotel — budget hotels (listed in London in 2014).
  • easyCar — car rental and car-sharing brokerage.
  • easyCruise — a budget cruise venture (later sold).
  • easyInternetcafé, easyMoney, easyGym, easyStorage, easyPizza and other extensions of the brand into retail and services.

Some ventures have been short-lived, consistent with Haji-Ioannou's strategy of testing the brand in new markets at low cost and exiting those that do not scale.

Brand disputes

EasyGroup vigorously defends the "easy" mark, bringing actions against unrelated businesses using "easy"-prefixed names, with mixed results in the courts. Its 2010 settlement with easyJet clarified the airline's rights to use the brand for its core business and ancillary services. The group is also associated with the Stelios Philanthropic Foundation and the Stelios Awards for disabled entrepreneurs.
