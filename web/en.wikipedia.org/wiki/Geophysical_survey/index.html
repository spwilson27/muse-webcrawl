Geophysical survey

Geophysical survey is the systematic collection of geophysical data for spatial studies. It involves measuring physical properties of the Earth — such as gravity, magnetism, electrical resistivity, seismic wave velocity and electromagnetic response — in order to infer subsurface structure without direct excavation or drilling.

Methods

Survey methods include seismic reflection and refraction, gravity surveys, magnetic surveys, electrical resistivity tomography, ground-penetrating radar and electromagnetic induction. Surveys may be conducted on the ground, from aircraft or drones (airborne geophysics), from ships, or in boreholes.

Applications

Geophysical surveys are used in mineral and hydrocarbon exploration, groundwater investigation, engineering site studies, environmental assessment and archaeology, where techniques such as magnetometry and resistivity can reveal buried structures without disturbing them.
