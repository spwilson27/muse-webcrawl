Expedia Group

Expedia Group, Inc. is an American travel technology company headquartered in Seattle, Washington. It operates online travel agencies and metasearch engines including Expedia.com, Hotels.com, Vrbo, Travelocity, Orbitz, Hotwire, Wotif, and the metasearch site Trivago.

Expedia began in 1996 as a division of Microsoft and was spun off in a 1999 initial public offering. The company grew through acquisitions, including Travelocity in 2015, Orbitz Worldwide in 2015, and HomeAway (the parent of Vrbo) in 2015. It was renamed from Expedia, Inc. to Expedia Group in 2018 to reflect its portfolio of brands.

The company is one of the world's largest online travel groups, competing principally with Booking Holdings.
