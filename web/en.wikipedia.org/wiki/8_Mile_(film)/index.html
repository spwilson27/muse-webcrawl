8 Mile (film)

8 Mile

Director: Curtis Hanson

Writer: Scott Silver

Starring: Eminem, Kim Basinger, Brittany Murphy, Mekhi Phifer

Release date: 8 November 2002

Running time: 111 minutes

Box office: $242.9 million

8 Mile is a 2002 American drama film directed by Curtis Hanson and written by Scott Silver. It stars rapper Eminem in his film debut, alongside Kim Basinger, Brittany Murphy, and Mekhi Phifer. The film is a semi-autobiographical account of Eminem's early life in Detroit, Michigan.

Plot

In 1995 Detroit, Jimmy "B-Rabbit" Smith Jr. is a young white rapper from a poor background, living in a trailer park with his alcoholic mother south of 8 Mile Road, the road dividing the predominantly Black city of Detroit from its wealthier, predominantly white northern suburbs. Working a menial job at a metal-stamping plant, Jimmy aspires to succeed in hip hop, a largely Black art form, and competes in freestyle rap battles at a local club called the Shelter, hosted by his friend Future. After choking in an early battle, Jimmy gradually builds confidence and ultimately defeats the champion Papa Doc, pre-empting his opponent's insults by rapping about his own hardships first.

Reception

The film was a commercial success, grossing about $243 million worldwide against a $41 million budget, and Eminem's performance was widely praised. The song "Lose Yourself," written for the film, won the Academy Award for Best Original Song, the first rap song to do so.
