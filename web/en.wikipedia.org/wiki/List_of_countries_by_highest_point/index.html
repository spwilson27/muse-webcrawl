List of countries by highest point

This is a list of countries by their highest point above sea level. The ranking is headed by Nepal and China, which share Mount Everest at 8,849 metres. At the other end are low-lying island states whose highest ground is only a few metres above the sea.

Lowest high points

The Maldives has the lowest maximum elevation of any country, about 2.4 metres, followed by other atoll nations such as Tuvalu (about 4.6 m) and the Marshall Islands. These figures underlie those states' vulnerability to sea-level rise.

Notes

Elevations vary slightly between surveys and national measurement conventions; Everest's official height was jointly announced by Nepal and China in 2020 as 8,848.86 m.
