KLRU (channel 18) is a PBS member television station in Austin, Texas, United States. It is the flagship station of public broadcasting in Central Texas and is best known nationally as the home of the long-running music performance series Austin City Limits.

History

The station signed on in 1979, licensed to the Capital of Texas Public Telecommunications Council. From its studios in Austin it has served the region with educational, cultural and public-affairs programming for more than four decades.

Programming

Alongside the PBS national schedule, KLRU produces local programmes on Texas politics, arts and community affairs. Its most famous production, Austin City Limits, began in 1975 on the station's predecessor arrangements and became the longest-running music series in American television history, showcasing performers from Willie Nelson to contemporary acts.

Digital television

KLRU broadcasts multiple digital subchannels, including its main PBS service and additional educational and cultural channels.
