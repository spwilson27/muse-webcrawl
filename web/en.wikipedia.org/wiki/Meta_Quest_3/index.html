Meta Quest 3 is a mixed reality headset developed by Meta Platforms' Reality Labs, announced on June 1, 2023, and released on October 10, 2023. It is the successor to the Meta Quest 2, positioned above the budget Meta Quest 3S released in 2024.

Hardware

The Quest 3 is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon XR2 Gen 2, offering roughly double the GPU performance of the Quest 2's chip, with 8 GB of RAM and 128 GB or 512 GB of storage. Pancake lenses make its front profile about 40% slimmer than the Quest 2, and its displays provide 2064×2208 resolution per eye with a roughly 110° horizontal field of view. Two RGB cameras and a depth sensor enable full-colour video passthrough for mixed reality, in contrast to the Quest 2's monochrome passthrough. Its Touch Plus controllers dropped the tracking rings of earlier designs, using a hybrid of camera tracking and internal sensors, and the headset supports controller-free hand tracking.

Software and reception

The Quest 3 runs Meta Horizon OS and is backward compatible with the Quest 2 software library, with many titles receiving graphical enhancements. It supports Wi-Fi 6E, Quest Link and Air Link for PC VR. Reviewers praised its passthrough quality relative to its price, its performance gain, and its comfort improvements, while noting a battery life of roughly two hours and a launch library of exclusive mixed-reality content that was still small. It competed with devices such as the Pico 4 and, at the high end, Apple's Vision Pro (2024), which it undercut substantially in price.
