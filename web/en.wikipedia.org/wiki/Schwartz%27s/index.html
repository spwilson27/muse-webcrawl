Schwartz's, officially the Schwartz's Montreal Hebrew Delicatessen, is a Jewish delicatessen restaurant in Montreal, Quebec, Canada, founded in 1928 by Reuben Schwartz, a Jewish immigrant from Romania. Located on Saint-Laurent Boulevard ("the Main"), it is famous for its Montreal-style smoked meat sandwiches and is one of the city's oldest and most celebrated delicatessens.

History

Schwartz's built its reputation on smoked meat prepared in-house with a spice blend kept secret, served on rye bread with mustard, alongside pickles, coleslaw, and cherry cola. The narrow dining room, with its communal tables and counter, changed little over the decades. In 2012 the restaurant was purchased by a consortium including René Angélil and Celine Dion, amid public pledges to preserve its character and recipes; a neighbouring take-out annex was added.

Cultural significance

The deli has inspired a book, a stage musical (Schwartz's: The Musical), and countless "smoked meat wars" comparisons with rivals such as Lester's. Long queues outside the door are a fixture of the boulevard.
