Sociedad Anónima Deportiva América S.A., commonly known as América de Cali, is a Colombian professional football club based in Cali. Founded in 1927, it is one of the most successful and popular clubs in Colombian football, traditionally wearing red shirts and nicknamed Los Diablos Rojos (The Red Devils).

History

América won its first league title in 1979 and went on to dominate Colombian football in the 1980s, winning five consecutive championships from 1982 to 1986. The club reached the Copa Libertadores final four times (1985, 1986, 1987 and 1996) without winning the trophy, a record of near misses in South American football. The club spent five seasons in the second division after relegation in 2011, returning to the top flight in 2016, and won further league titles in 2019 and 2020.

Rivalries and stadium

The club's fiercest rivalry is with Deportivo Cali, contested in the Clásico Vallecaucano. América plays its home matches at the Estadio Olímpico Pascual Guerrero, which it shares with its city rival.
