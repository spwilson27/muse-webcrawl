The Two Popes

The Two Popes is a 2019 biographical drama film directed by Fernando Meirelles and written by Anthony McCarten, adapted from McCarten's play The Pope. It stars Anthony Hopkins as Pope Benedict XVI and Jonathan Pryce as Cardinal Jorge Mario Bergoglio, the future Pope Francis.

Plot

Frustrated with the direction of the Catholic Church under Benedict XVI, Argentine Cardinal Bergoglio travels to Rome in 2012 to request permission to retire. Instead, the introspective Benedict — facing scandal and his own doubts — summons him to a series of conversations at the Vatican and Castel Gandolfo. The two men debate tradition and reform, confess their failings (including Bergoglio's conduct during Argentina's Dirty War), and gradually form an unlikely friendship, leading to Benedict's historic resignation in 2013 and Bergoglio's election as his successor.

Reception

Premiering at the Telluride Film Festival, the film was released by Netflix to critical acclaim, with praise for both lead performances. Hopkins, Pryce and McCarten received Academy Award nominations.
