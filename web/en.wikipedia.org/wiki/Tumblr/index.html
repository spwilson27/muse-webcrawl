Tumblr

Tumblr is an American microblogging and social networking website founded by David Karp in 2007. Users post multimedia and short-form content to their own blogs ("tumblelogs") and follow other users; reblogging is the platform's central sharing mechanism.

History

Tumblr grew rapidly, hosting tens of millions of blogs by the early 2010s. Yahoo acquired it in 2013 for about $1.1 billion. Verizon Media, Yahoo's later owner, sold Tumblr in 2019 to Automattic, the company behind WordPress.com, for a reported sum far below the original purchase price.

Culture and policy

The site became known for fandom communities, art, humour blogs and social-justice discourse, and for a distinctive aesthetic culture. A December 2018 ban on adult content, imposed after the app was briefly removed from Apple's App Store, led to a significant decline in traffic. The policy was partially relaxed in 2022.
