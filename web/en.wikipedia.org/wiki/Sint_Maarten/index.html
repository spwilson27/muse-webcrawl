Sint Maarten

Sint Maarten is a constituent country of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in the Caribbean. It encompasses the southern 40 percent of the island of Saint Martin; the northern part is the French collectivity of Saint Martin. Its capital is Philipsburg.

History and status

The island was divided between France and the Netherlands by the Treaty of Concordia in 1648. Sint Maarten was part of the Netherlands Antilles until that federation was dissolved on 10 October 2010, when it became a separate country within the Kingdom, with its own parliament and government while the Netherlands retains defence and foreign affairs.

Geography and economy

The economy is dominated by tourism, served by Princess Juliana International Airport, famous for aircraft landing low over Maho Beach. The island was devastated by Hurricane Irma in September 2017 and underwent a long recovery. English and Dutch are official languages, and the currency is the Netherlands Antillean guilder.
