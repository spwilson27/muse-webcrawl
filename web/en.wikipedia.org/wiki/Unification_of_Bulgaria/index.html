Unification of Bulgaria
Date6 September 1885 (O.S.)
LocationPlovdiv, Eastern Rumelia
ResultUnion of the Principality of Bulgaria and Eastern Rumelia

The Unification of Bulgaria was the act of unification of the Principality of Bulgaria and the province of Eastern Rumelia, proclaimed on 6 September 1885 in Plovdiv. The two territories had been separated by the Congress of Berlin in 1878, which left the autonomous Bulgarian principality north of the Balkan Mountains and created the Ottoman province of Eastern Rumelia to the south.

The unification was prepared by the Bulgarian Secret Central Revolutionary Committee and carried out with the support of the Bulgarian prince Alexander of Battenberg. It violated the Treaty of Berlin and provoked an international crisis.

Aftermath

Serbia, dissatisfied with the shift in the Balkan balance of power, declared war on Bulgaria in November 1885. Bulgarian forces defeated the Serbian army at the Battle of Slivnitsa, and the unification was subsequently recognized by the Great Powers. The Treaty of Bucharest (1886) confirmed the union, with Prince Alexander recognized as Governor-General of Eastern Rumelia. Unification Day, 6 September, is a national holiday in Bulgaria.
