Everybody Knows This Is Nowhere

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Studio album by Neil Young with Crazy Horse
Released:May 14, 1969
Recorded:January–March 1969
Genre:Folk rock, country rock, hard rock
Label:Reprise

Everybody Knows This Is Nowhere is the second studio album by Canadian-American musician Neil Young, released in May 1969 on Reprise Records. It is his first album with the band Crazy Horse, with whom he would collaborate throughout his career. The album includes three of Young's most enduring songs — "Cinnamon Girl", "Down by the River" and "Cowgirl in the Sand" — all written while Young was ill in bed with a high fever, according to Young himself.

Background and recording

After the dissolution of Buffalo Springfield and an underperforming solo debut, Young recruited the Rockets — Danny Whitten (guitar), Billy Talbot (bass) and Ralph Molina (drums) — and renamed them Crazy Horse. The sessions, produced by Young and David Briggs, favoured raw, live-in-the-studio performances, including the extended guitar interplay between Young and Whitten on the album's two long closing tracks. Whitten's harmony singing and rhythm guitar became central to the band's sound until his death in 1972.

Reception and legacy

The album reached number 34 on the US Billboard 200 and was eventually certified platinum. It is widely regarded as a landmark of folk rock and country rock and a foundational text for later alternative and indie rock guitar bands. Rolling Stone has repeatedly placed it among the greatest albums of all time, and its ragged, improvisational sound — a deliberate contrast to the polished production of Young's debut — established the template Young would revisit with Crazy Horse for decades.

Track listing

Side one: "Cinnamon Girl", "Everybody Knows This Is Nowhere", "Round & Round (It Won't Be Long)", "Down by the River". Side two: "The Losing End (When You're On)", "Running Dry (Requiem for the Rockets)", "Cowgirl in the Sand". All songs written by Neil Young.
