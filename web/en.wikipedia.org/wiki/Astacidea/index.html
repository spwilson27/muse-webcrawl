Astacidea is an infraorder of decapod crustaceans, comprising the true lobsters, the freshwater crayfish and the reef lobsters. Members of the group are distinguished by their large chelae (claws) on the first pair of walking legs; about 780 species are known, spread across marine and freshwater habitats worldwide.

Classification

The infraorder is divided into superfamilies including Astacoidea (northern-hemisphere crayfish), Parastacoidea (southern-hemisphere crayfish), Nephropoidea (clawed lobsters) and Enoplometopoidea (reef lobsters).

Ecology and uses

Astacideans are mostly bottom-dwelling scavengers and predators. Several species are important food animals, and some crayfish have become invasive when introduced outside their native ranges.
