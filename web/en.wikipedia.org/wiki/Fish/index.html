A fish is an aquatic, anamniotic, gill-bearing vertebrate animal with fins. Fish evolved over 500 million years ago and now number more than 30,000 species — sharks and rays, bony fish from seahorses to tuna, and jawless lampreys and hagfish.

Humans and fish

Fisheries and aquaculture supply a major share of humanity's animal protein — think of rice-and-fish diets across Asia — but overfishing has depleted many stocks; management, marine reserves and sustainable aquaculture are responses. Fish breathe by extracting oxygen dissolved in water, a very different medium from the air of birds in the wider crawl.
