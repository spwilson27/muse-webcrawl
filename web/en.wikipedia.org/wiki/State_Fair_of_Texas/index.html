State Fair of Texas

Location: Fair Park, Dallas, Texas, United States
First held: 1886
Duration: 24 days (late September to mid/late October)
Attendance: over 2 million annually
Mascot/symbol: Big Tex (55-foot cowboy figure)

The State Fair of Texas is an annual state fair held in Dallas, Texas, at the historic Fair Park, running for 24 days each autumn from late September. First organised in 1886, it is one of the largest state fairs in the United States by attendance, regularly drawing more than two million visitors, and claims to be the longest-running fair of its kind. Its symbol is Big Tex, a towering cowboy figure erected in 1952 who greets fairgoers with "Howdy, folks!"

Attractions

The fair combines agricultural and livestock exhibitions with a vast Midway of rides — including the 212-foot Texas Star, among the tallest Ferris wheels in North America — concerts, parades, creative arts competitions, and the Cotton Bowl stadium in Fair Park, which hosts the annual Red River Rivalry football game between the University of Texas and the University of Oklahoma, as well as the Grambling–Prairie View game. Since 2005 the fair has held the Big Tex Choice Awards, a competition for innovative fried foods; celebrated winners and finalists include fried butter, fried beer and fried Coca-Cola. Fletcher's Original Corny Dogs, introduced at the fair in 1942, remain an iconic concession. The fair also hosts a major auto show, a longstanding attraction since 1913.

History

The fair began in 1886 under the Dallas State Fair & Exposition and, after a merger and a destructive fire, was reorganised in 1904, when the city of Dallas acquired the site as Fair Park. The grounds contain one of the largest collections of Art Deco exhibition architecture in the United States, built largely for the 1936 Texas Centennial Exposition. Big Tex burned in an electrical fire in 2012 and was rebuilt in time for the 2013 fair. The fair was cancelled in 2020 because of the COVID-19 pandemic — replaced by a drive-through event — and returned in 2021.
