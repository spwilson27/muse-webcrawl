Encanto is a 2021 American animated musical fantasy film produced by Walt Disney Animation Studios. Set in the mountains of Colombia, it tells the story of the Madrigals, a multigenerational family whose members each receive a magical "gift" — super-strength, healing through food, weather control, prophecy — from a miraculous candle, except for the ordinary youngest daughter, Mirabel.

Plot

Fleeing violence, Alma Madrigal loses her husband Pedro but gains a miracle: an enchanted house, the Casita, in a hidden valley (the Encanto). Decades later the magic begins to fade, cracks appear in the house, and Mirabel — who received no gift — investigates, uncovering the family's buried grief and the ostracised uncle Bruno, whose visions were blamed for misfortunes. In reconciling with Alma and acknowledging the pressure the miracle placed on everyone, the family rebuilds the house and the magic returns.

Music and reception

Lin-Manuel Miranda's songs blend Colombian musical styles; "We Don't Talk About Bruno" became an unexpected viral hit that topped the Billboard Hot 100, while "Dos Oruguitas" was nominated for an Academy Award. The film won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature and was praised for its portrayal of Colombian culture and family dynamics.
