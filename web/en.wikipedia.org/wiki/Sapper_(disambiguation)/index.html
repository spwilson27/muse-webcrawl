Sapper (disambiguation)

Sapper most commonly denotes a combat engineer soldier. The word derives from the French sapeur, one who digs sapes — trenches dug toward enemy fortifications in siege warfare. It may also refer to the following.

Military

  • Sapper — a soldier in a combat engineer role: building fortifications and bridges, laying and clearing minefields, demolitions, and breaching obstacles. "Sapper" is a formal rank-equivalent title in the British Army's Royal Engineers and in several Commonwealth armies, equivalent to private.
  • Sapper Tab — a United States Army qualification tab awarded to graduates of the Sapper Leader Course at Fort Leonard Wood.

People

  • "Sapper" — the pen name of Herman Cyril McNeile (1888–1937), British author of the Bulldog Drummond adventure novels.

Arts and fiction

  • Sapper — a fictional character (Sapper Morton) in the film Blade Runner 2049 (2017), played by Dave Bautista.
  • The Sapper — various fictional and gaming uses, including engineer-type character classes and the player-built sentry-demolishing device ("sapper") in the game Team Fortress 2.

Other uses

  • Sapper Hill — a settlement in the Falkland Islands, near Stanley, named for Royal Engineers stationed there.
  • Sapper — in slang and computing, occasionally used for tools or scripts that drain or undermine resources; also a historical term in mining for an excavator of saps (tunnels).
