Casey Affleck (born Caleb Casey McGuire Affleck-Boldt; August 12, 1975) is an American actor and filmmaker. He is the younger brother of actor Ben Affleck. Acclaimed for his naturalistic performances, he received the Academy Award for Best Actor for his role as a grieving janitor in Manchester by the Sea (2016).

Career

Affleck grew up in Cambridge, Massachusetts. After early television work he appeared in Gus Van Sant's To Die For (1995) and his brother's Good Will Hunting (1997), followed by roles in the Ocean's trilogy (2001–2007) as Virgil Malloy. His breakthrough came in 2007: he starred in his brother's directorial debut Gone Baby Gone and earned an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actor as Robert Ford in The Assassination of Jesse James by the Coward Robert Ford.

He directed the mockumentary I'm Still Here (2010), starring Joaquin Phoenix. His portrayal of Lee Chandler in Kenneth Lonergan's Manchester by the Sea (2016) won the Academy Award, BAFTA, and Golden Globe for Best Actor. Later films include A Ghost Story (2017), The Old Man & the Gun (2018), Light of My Life (2019, which he also directed), The World to Come (2020), and Oppenheimer (2023), in which he played Boris Pash.

Personal life

Affleck was married to actress Summer Phoenix, with whom he has two sons; they divorced in 2017. In 2010 two former colleagues filed civil lawsuits alleging misconduct during the filming of I'm Still Here; both suits were settled out of court, and Affleck has denied wrongdoing while later acknowledging that the set's atmosphere was unprofessional.
