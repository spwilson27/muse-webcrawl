Haus der Geschichte

The Haus der Geschichte (officially Haus der Geschichte der Bundesrepublik Deutschland, "House of the History of the Federal Republic of Germany") is a museum in Bonn, Germany, dedicated to German history from 1945 to the present. It opened in 1994 and is one of the most visited museums in Germany. Its permanent exhibition, "Our History: Germany since 1945," traces the division of Germany, the two German states, reunification, and the years since.

Collections and exhibitions

The museum holds more than 75,000 objects, including everyday items, political artefacts, vehicles, and a piece of the Berlin Wall. Notable exhibits include Chancellor Konrad Adenauer's official Mercedes-Benz, a cinema from the 1950s, and sections devoted to migration, the Cold War, and popular culture. Temporary exhibitions address current historical themes.

Foundation

The museum is run by the Stiftung Haus der Geschichte der Bundesrepublik Deutschland, a foundation under public law that also operates the Zeitgeschichtliches Forum in Leipzig, the Tränenpalast in Berlin, and the Museum in der Kulturbrauerei. Admission to the Bonn museum is free.
