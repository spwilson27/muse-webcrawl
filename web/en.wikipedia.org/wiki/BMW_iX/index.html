BMW iX

BMW iX (I20)
Manufacturer: BMW
Production: 2021–present
Class: Mid-size luxury crossover SUV
Powertrain: Battery electric, dual motor
Range: up to approx. 630 km (WLTP, xDrive50)

The BMW iX is a battery electric mid-size luxury crossover SUV manufactured by BMW since 2021. Developed from the Vision iNEXT concept, it is BMW's flagship electric SUV, built on a dedicated EV architecture with an aluminium spaceframe and carbon-fibre-reinforced plastic body elements.

Variants

Models include the xDrive40, xDrive50 (523 hp) and the M60 performance version with over 600 hp. Large batteries of up to about 105 kWh usable provide long range, and the minimalist interior introduced BMW's Curved Display and hexagonal steering wheel.

Position

The iX competes with the Audi Q8 e-tron, Mercedes-Benz EQS SUV and Tesla Model X, and showcased BMW's fifth-generation eDrive technology later used across its EV range.
