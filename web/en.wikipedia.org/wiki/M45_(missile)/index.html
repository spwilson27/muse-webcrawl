M45 (missile)

The M45 SLBM is a French submarine-launched ballistic missile, the fourth generation of the force de dissuasion's sea-based nuclear deterrent. It armed the Triomphant-class ballistic missile submarines of the French Navy.

M45
Type: Submarine-launched ballistic missile
Origin: France
Operator: French Navy
Range: approx. 6,000 km
Warhead: TN 75 MIRV (up to 6)

Development

Developed as an improvement over the M4 missile, the M45 entered service in 1996 with the commissioning of Le Triomphant. It carried up to six TN 75 warheads with penetration aids and had improved accuracy and range.

Replacement

From about 2010 the M45 was progressively replaced by the longer-ranged M51 missile aboard the Triomphant-class boats.
