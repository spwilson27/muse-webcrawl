East Bay Ray

East Bay Ray (born Raymond Pepperell, November 17, 1958), is an American musician best known as the guitarist for the San Francisco Bay Area hardcore punk band Dead Kennedys.

East Bay Ray
Born: Raymond Pepperell, 17 November 1958
Instrument: Guitar
Associated act: Dead Kennedys

Career

Pepperell co-founded Dead Kennedys in 1978 with Jello Biafra, Klaus Flouride and Ted. His surf- and psychedelic-influenced guitar style, using echo and twang, defined songs such as 'Holiday in Cambodia' and 'California Über Alles'.

Later work

After the band split in 1986, he was involved in lengthy litigation over royalties and the Alternative Tentacles catalogue. He has toured with the reformed Dead Kennedys (without Biafra) since 2001.
