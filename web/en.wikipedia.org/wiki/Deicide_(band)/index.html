Deicide (band)

OriginTampa, Florida, U.S.
GenresDeath metal
Years active1987–present
Key memberGlen Benton (vocals, bass)

Deicide is an American death metal band formed in Tampa, Florida, in 1987 by drummer/composer Steve Asheim and guitarist brothers Eric and Brian Hoffman as Carnage, soon renamed Amon and then Deicide in 1989. Fronted by vocalist and bassist Glen Benton, the band is known for its blasphemous, anti-Christian lyrics and imagery, and is one of the best-selling death metal acts of all time.

History

The band's self-titled debut album appeared in 1990, followed by Legion (1992), widely regarded as a landmark of the genre. Albums such as Once Upon the Cross (1995) and Serpents of the Light (1997) cemented their reputation. The Hoffman brothers left in 2004, after which the band continued with rotating guitarists, releasing albums including The Stench of Redemption (2006), To Hell with God (2011), In the Minds of Evil (2013), Overtures of Blasphemy (2018), and Banished by Sin (2024).

Controversy

Deicide has attracted controversy throughout its career: Benton's inverted cross branded into his forehead, protests by religious and animal-rights groups, and concert bans in several countries. The band has nonetheless remained a fixture of the death metal scene, based in the genre's Florida heartland alongside Morbid Angel, Death, and Obituary.
