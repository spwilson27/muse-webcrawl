The Battle of Stalingrad was a major battle on the Eastern Front of the Second World War, in which Nazi Germany and its Axis allies fought the Soviet Union for control of the city of Stalingrad on the Volga River. Fought from the summer of 1942 to 2 February 1943, it is regarded as the bloodiest battle in modern warfare and the decisive turning point of the war in Europe: the German 6th Army was encircled and destroyed, and Germany never regained the initiative in the east.

The German advance

In the summer offensive of 1942 (Case Blue), German forces drove toward the Caucasus oil fields and the Volga. The 6th Army under General Friedrich Paulus reached Stalingrad in August; massed Luftwaffe bombing killed tens of thousands of civilians and reduced the city to rubble — rubble that defenders of Vasily Chuikov's 62nd Army turned into strongpoints. For months the battle degenerated into savage close-quarters fighting for individual buildings, factories and the Mamayev Kurgan hill, immortalised in episodes such as the defence of Pavlov's House.

Operation Uranus

While German forces bled in the city, Soviet commanders Zhukov and Vasilevsky secretly massed over a million men on the flanks, where the line was held by weaker Romanian armies. On 19 November 1942 Operation Uranus struck north and south of the city; the two pincers met at Kalach four days later, trapping some 250,000–300,000 Axis troops in the Stalingrad pocket (the Kessel). Hitler forbade a breakout, and Göring's promised airlift delivered only a fraction of the supplies needed. A relief attempt (Operation Winter Storm) in December failed.

Surrender

Starving and frozen, the pocket shrank under Soviet assaults in January 1943. Paulus, promoted to field marshal on 30 January in a hint that he should kill himself rather than surrender, capitulated the next day; the last northern pocket surrendered on 2 February 1943. Some 91,000 Germans were taken prisoner, of whom only about 5,000–6,000 survived captivity. The city — renamed Volgograd in 1961 — is commemorated by the vast Motherland Calls statue on Mamayev Kurgan.
