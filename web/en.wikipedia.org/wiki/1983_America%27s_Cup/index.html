The 1983 America's Cup was the 25th edition of the America's Cup yacht race, held off Newport, Rhode Island. The Australian challenger Australia II, skippered by John Bertrand, defeated the American defender Liberty, skippered by Dennis Conner, four races to three.

Significance

The victory ended the New York Yacht Club's 132-year winning streak — the longest in sporting history — dating back to the first race in 1851. Australia II featured a revolutionary winged keel designed by Ben Lexcen, which gave her a decisive edge in light winds and manoeuvrability.

The series

Liberty led 3–1 before Australia II won three straight races. In the deciding seventh race, Australia II overcame a 57-second deficit at the final mark to win by 41 seconds, sparking nationwide celebrations in Australia.
