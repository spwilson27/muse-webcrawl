Overwatch 2

Overwatch 2 is a hero shooter video game developed and published by Blizzard Entertainment. Released in early access on 4 October 2022 as a free-to-play title, it replaced the original Overwatch (2016), whose servers were shut down the day before launch.

Gameplay

The game pits two teams of five players against each other, each player choosing from a roster of heroes divided into Tank, Damage and Support roles, each with unique abilities. The shift from six to five players per team, with a single tank, was the principal structural change from the original. Modes include Push, Control, Escort, Hybrid and Flashpoint, across competitive and casual playlists.

Development and reception

Announced at BlizzCon 2019 alongside a planned cooperative PvE mode, the game launched with PvP first; the large-scale PvE "hero mode" was cancelled in 2023, with story missions released in smaller paid packages instead. The move to a free-to-play model with battle passes and an in-game shop replaced the original's loot boxes and drew mixed reactions, while the core gameplay was generally praised.
